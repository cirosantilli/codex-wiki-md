# Existence of a time-series spectral density

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral density of a stationary process](spectral-density-of-a-stationary-process.md)

A [weakly stationary process](weakly-stationary-process.md) has a [time-series spectral density](spectral-density-of-a-stationary-process.md) precisely when its [spectral measure of a stationary time series](spectral-measure-of-a-stationary-time-series.md) is [absolutely continuous with respect to](absolute-continuity-of-measures.md) [Lebesgue measure](lebesgue-measure.md). Absolute summability of its [autocovariances](autocovariance.md) is sufficient and yields a continuous Fourier-series density. It is not necessary for existence; for a general integrable density, [Fejér sums](fejer-sum.md) give an $L^1$ recovery formula.
