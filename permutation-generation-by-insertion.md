# Permutation generation by insertion

↑ **Parent:** [Permutation generation](permutation-generation.md)

Generate every [permutation](permutation.md) of a finite [list](list-abstract-data-type.md) by recursively permuting its tail and inserting its head into each possible position. For $n$ distinct input elements, every shorter [permutation](permutation.md) has $n$ insertion positions, giving $n(n-1)!=n!$ results. Removing the distinguished head reconstructs the unique shorter [permutation](permutation.md) and insertion position.
