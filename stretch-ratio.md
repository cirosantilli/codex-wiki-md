# Stretch ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Deformation gradient](deformation-gradient.md)

The ratio of current to reference lengths of an infinitesimal material line element. Along an [elastic rod](elastic-rod.md), it is $\alpha=ds/dS=|d\mathbf r/dS|>0$. A [principal stretch](principal-stretch.md) is a [stretch ratio](stretch-ratio.md) along a principal direction of the [stretch tensor](stretch-tensor.md).
