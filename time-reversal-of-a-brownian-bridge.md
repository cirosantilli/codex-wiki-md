# Time reversal of a Brownian bridge

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian bridge](brownian-bridge.md)

The [Brownian bridge covariance kernel](brownian-bridge-covariance-kernel.md) is invariant under reversing both times, and its mean is zero. The two centered [Gaussian processes](gaussian-process.md) therefore have equal finite-dimensional laws. Equality on countably many rational coordinates transfers the event of continuity at zero to the reversed endpoint. Interior continuity then extends the rational-time limit to all times. This proves the terminal continuity of the [stochastic integral representation of a Brownian bridge](stochastic-integral-representation-of-a-brownian-bridge.md).
