# Triangulation

↑ **Parent:** [Simplicial complex](simplicial-complex.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triangulation_(topology))

A triangulation of a [topological space](topological-space.md) is a [homeomorphism](homeomorphism.md) from the realization of a [simplicial complex](simplicial-complex.md) to that space. In [three-manifold](3-manifold.md) topology, thickening its one-skeleton and the dual one-skeleton constructs a [Heegaard splitting](heegaard-splitting.md).
