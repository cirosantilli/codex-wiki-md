# Wealth-variable Legendre dual

↑ **Parent:** [Utility duality with martingale deflators](utility-duality-with-martingale-deflators.md)

For increasing strictly [concave](concave-function.md) wealth value $v$, the dual $J(z)=\sup_x[v(x)-xz]$ is convex. At an interior optimum $z=v^\prime(x)$, $J^\prime=-x$, $J^{\prime\prime}=-1/v^{\prime\prime}>0$, and $v=J-zJ^\prime$. This sign convention is the negative of the [concave Legendre dual](concave-legendre-dual.md). It can turn the optimized portfolio term of a [Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman equation](hamilton-jacobi-bellman-equation.md) into a linear second derivative.
