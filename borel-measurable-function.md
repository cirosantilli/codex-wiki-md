# Borel measurable function

↑ **Parent:** [Measurable function](measurable-function.md)

For [topological spaces](topological-space.md) $X,Y$, a [Borel measurable function](borel-measurable-function.md) $f:X\to Y$ is a [measurable function](measurable-function.md) for the [Borel sigma-algebras](borel-sigma-algebra.md) on both spaces. Equivalently, the [preimage](preimage.md) of every open [set](set-split.md) in $Y$ is a [Borel set](borel-set.md) in $X$. With a different source sigma-algebra $\mathcal A$, the phrase measurable into $Y$ with its Borel sigma-algebra means $f^{-1}(B)\in\mathcal A$ for every Borel $B\subseteq Y$; specifying the source is essential.
