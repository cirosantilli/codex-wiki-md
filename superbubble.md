# Superbubble

↑ **Parent:** [Interstellar medium](interstellar-medium.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superbubble)

A superbubble is an expanding cavity of hot gas driven by the combined energy input of stellar winds and multiple [supernova](supernova.md) explosions. Constant mechanical power and negligible radiative losses lead to a radius proportional to $t^{3/5}$ in a uniform ambient medium.
