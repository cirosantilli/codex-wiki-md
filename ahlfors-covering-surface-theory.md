# Ahlfors covering surface theory

↑ **Parent:** [Nevanlinna theory](nevanlinna-theory.md)

This theory compares the area, boundary length and [Euler characteristic](euler-characteristic.md) of a surface mapped holomorphically to another surface, using the pulled-back metric. It extends covering-degree and [Riemann-Hurwitz formula](riemann-hurwitz-formula.md) estimates when some boundary maps into the interior of the target. The [Ahlfors second fundamental theorem](ahlfors-second-fundamental-theorem.md) and [Ahlfors five islands theorem](ahlfors-five-islands-theorem.md) are important consequences.
