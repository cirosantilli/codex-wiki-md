# Volcanic loading

↑ **Parent:** [Isostasy](isostasy.md)

Addition of erupted or intruded [rock](rock-geology.md) as a gravitational load on a planetary surface or interior. Deposited thickness, [density](density.md), geometry and emplacement history control [isostatic adjustment](isostatic-adjustment.md) and [lithospheric flexure](lithospheric-flexure.md). Thermal and compositional changes in the source can supply additional effects distinct from the weight of the deposited material.
