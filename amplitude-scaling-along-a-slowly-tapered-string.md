# Amplitude scaling along a slowly tapered string

↑ **Parent:** [Characteristic impedance of a stretched string](characteristic-impedance-of-a-stretched-string.md)

A static lossless taper preserves [frequency](frequency.md). If [characteristic impedance](characteristic-impedance.md) varies slowly on the local [wavelength](wavelength.md) scale, reflection is small and mean [power](power.md) $Z\omega^2A^2/2$ is conserved. At fixed [tension](tension.md) this gives the displayed amplitude law, while [wave speed](wave-speed.md) and [wavelength](wavelength.md) scale as $\rho^{-1/2}$. A long section containing an abrupt step is not automatically adiabatic.
