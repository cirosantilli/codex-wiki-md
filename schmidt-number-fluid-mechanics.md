# Schmidt number (fluid mechanics)

↑ **Parent:** [Passive scalar](passive-scalar.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schmidt_number)

The molecular Schmidt number is the ratio of [kinematic viscosity](kinematic-viscosity.md) to scalar [diffusivity](diffusion-coefficient.md). A large value allows a [passive scalar](passive-scalar.md) to develop structure below the viscous smoothing scale of the [velocity field](velocity-field.md). It is distinct from the [turbulent Schmidt number](turbulent-schmidt-number.md), which compares effective turbulent transport coefficients, and from the [Schmidt number](schmidt-number.md) used for quantum entanglement.
