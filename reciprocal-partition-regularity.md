# Reciprocal partition regularity

↑ **Parent:** [Partition regular matrix](partition-regular-matrix.md)

If a [rational matrix](rational-matrix.md) $A$ is a [partition regular matrix](partition-regular-matrix.md), every [finite coloring](finite-coloring.md) of the [positive integers](positive-integer.md) admits [monochromatic](monochromatic-set.md) $y_1,\ldots,y_n$ such that

$$
A(1/y_1,\ldots,1/y_n)^{\mathsf T}=0.
$$

Choose a [compactness bound for partition regularity](compactness-bound-for-partition-regularity.md) $T$ for the number of colors, put $S=\operatorname{lcm}(1,\ldots,T)$, and pull back the coloring by $t\mapsto S/t$ on $[T]$. A [monochromatic](monochromatic-set.md) positive solution $x$ of $Ax=0$ gives $y_i=S/x_i$; then $(1/y_i)=x_i/S$. The [least common multiple](least-common-multiple.md) guarantees that every $y_i$ is a [positive integer](positive-integer.md).
