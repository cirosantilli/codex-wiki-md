# Linear friction coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Linear drag](linear-drag.md)

The dimensional linear friction coefficient relates [drag force](drag-physics.md) and [velocity](velocity.md), $F_d=-\zeta v$, and has units of [force](force.md) divided by speed. It differs from the dimensionless aerodynamic [drag coefficient](drag-coefficient.md). For an isolated sphere in [Stokes flow](stokes-flow-split.md), the [Stokes drag law](stokes-s-law.md) gives $\zeta=6\pi\eta a$.
