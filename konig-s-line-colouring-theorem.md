<h1 id="konig-s-line-colouring-theorem">Kőnig's line colouring theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Edge chromatic number](edge-chromatic-number.md)

For every finite [bipartite graph](bipartite-graph-split.md), the [edge chromatic number](edge-chromatic-number.md) equals its [maximum degree](maximum-degree.md). Repeatedly remove a [matching covering maximum-degree vertices in a bipartite graph](matching-covering-maximum-degree-vertices-in-a-bipartite-graph.md): each removal reduces the [maximum degree](maximum-degree.md) by at least one. The removed [matchings in a graph](matching-graph-theory-split.md) are colour classes of a [proper edge colouring](proper-edge-coloring.md). The reverse bound follows from the distinct colours required at a [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) of [maximum degree](maximum-degree.md).
