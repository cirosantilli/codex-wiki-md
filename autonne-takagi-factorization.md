# Autonne-Takagi factorization

↑ **Parent:** [Singular value decomposition](singular-value-decomposition.md)

Every complex [symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md) admits a unitary congruence decomposition with a [unitary matrix](unitary-matrix.md) $U$ and nonnegative diagonal entries $s_j$ equal to its [singular values](singular-value.md). This differs from unitary similarity diagonalization. It reduces a [multimode squeezed vacuum](multimode-squeezed-vacuum.md) to independent oscillator squeezing factors.
