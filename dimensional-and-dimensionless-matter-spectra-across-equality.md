# Dimensional and dimensionless matter spectra across equality

↑ **Parent:** [Dimensionless cosmological power spectrum](dimensionless-cosmological-power-spectrum.md)

The dimensionless spectrum is $\mathscr P(k)=k^3P(k)/(2\pi^2)$. If it rises as $k^4$ below equality and as $\ln^2k$ above equality, then the dimensional spectrum rises as $k$ and falls as $k^{-3}\ln^2k$. Thus the dimensional spectrum has a turnover while the dimensionless spectrum only bends to a much slower rise. A schematic transition must smoothly join the two asymptotic regimes.
