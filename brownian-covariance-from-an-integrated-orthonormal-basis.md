# Brownian covariance from an integrated orthonormal basis

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian covariance kernel](brownian-covariance-kernel.md)

For a complete [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) of $L^2(\mathbb R_+)$, the [Parseval identity for a Hilbertian basis](parseval-identity-for-a-hilbertian-basis.md) identifies the sum of the feature products with $\langle1_{[0,s]},1_{[0,t]}\rangle=\min(s,t)$. The [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) gives absolute convergence.
