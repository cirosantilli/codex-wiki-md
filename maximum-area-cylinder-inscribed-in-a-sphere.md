# Maximum-area cylinder inscribed in a sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrange multiplier](lagrange-multiplier.md)

For a closed right [circular cylinder](circular-cylinder.md) with radius $r>0$, height $h>0$, and total [surface area](surface-area.md) $A=2\pi r(r+h)$, inscription in a [sphere](sphere.md) of radius $R$ gives $r^2+h^2/4=R^2$. The [Lagrange multiplier](lagrange-multiplier.md) equations imply $h^2+2rh-4r^2=0$, so the positive aspect ratio is $h/r=\sqrt5-1$. The dimensions are $r^2=R^2(5+\sqrt5)/10$ and $h^2=2R^2(5-\sqrt5)/5$.

There is also an [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) proof of global maximality. Set $w=h/2$, so the [constraint](constraint-mechanics.md) is $r^2+w^2=R^2$, and write $A/(2\pi)=r^2+2rw$. This [quadratic form](quadratic-form-split.md) has [symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md) $\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$ with largest [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) $(1+\sqrt5)/2$, the [golden ratio](golden-ratio.md). Its maximizing [eigenvector](eigenvector.md) lies in the positive quadrant, yielding the displayed area and aspect ratio.
