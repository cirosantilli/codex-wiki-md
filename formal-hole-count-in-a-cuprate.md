# Formal hole count in a cuprate

↑ **Parent:** [Cuprate superconductor](cuprate-superconductor.md)

For a chosen homogeneous composition $\mathrm{La}_{2-x}\mathrm{Ba}_x\mathrm{CuO}_{4-\delta}$, assign formal [oxidation states](oxidation-state.md) $+3$ to [lanthanum](lanthanum.md), $+2$ to [barium](barium.md) and $-2$ to [oxygen](oxygen.md). If $v$ is the mean formal [copper](copper.md) [oxidation state](oxidation-state.md), [charge neutrality](charge-neutrality.md) requires

$$
3(2-x)+2x+v-2(4-\delta)=0,
\qquad v=2+x-2\delta.
$$

The excess formal positive charge per [copper](copper.md) relative to $+2$ is therefore $p_{\mathrm{formal}}=x-2\delta$. For stipulated $x=0.12$ and $\delta=0.01$, it is $0.10$; at $\delta=0.06$, it is zero. These are bookkeeping examples, not measurements of mobile carriers. The symbol $x$ here belongs to this declared formula, not the original mixed-phase recipe. A whole-pellet elemental ratio cannot simply be assigned to each constituent phase.

[Charge neutrality](charge-neutrality.md) alone does not locate holes. The lecture reviews [oxygen](oxygen.md)-related carrier evidence and charge-$2e$ pairing from the [Josephson effect](josephson-effect.md) and [fluxoid and flux quantization in a superconductor](fluxoid-and-flux-quantization-in-a-superconductor.md). Pair charge does not establish [phonon](phonon.md)-mediated [BCS theory](bcs-theory.md) for [cuprate superconductors](cuprate-superconductor.md).[Part 2, pp.444–446, 450; pairing, carriers and mechanism](perovskite-type-oxides-the-new-approach-to-high-tc-superconductivity-by-johannes-georg-bednorz-and-karl-alexander-muller.md)
