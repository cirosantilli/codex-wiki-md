# Repunit

↑ **Parent:** [Repdigit](repdigit.md)

A [repdigit](repdigit.md) whose digits are all 1 in a specified [positional notation](positional-notation.md) of base $b$. Its value is the [geometric series](geometric-series.md) $R_b(k)=1+b+\cdots+b^{k-1}=(b^k-1)/(b-1)$. In base ten, appending one digit gives $R_{10}(k+1)=10R_{10}(k)+1$, so its [remainder](remainder.md) [modulo](modulo-operation.md) $n$ can be computed by a [recurrence relation](recurrence-relation.md) without constructing the full [integer](integer-split.md).
