# Completed Epstein zeta function

↑ **Parent:** [Epstein zeta function](epstein-zeta-function.md)

The completion $\mathcal E_\Lambda(s)=\pi^{-s}\Gamma(s)E_\Lambda(s)$ satisfies $\mathcal E_\Lambda(s)=m(\Lambda)^{-1}\mathcal E_{\Lambda^\vee}(n/2-s)$. Its simple poles at zero and $n/2$ have residues $-1$ and $m(\Lambda)^{-1}$. The [pole-subtracted theta integral for an Epstein zeta function](pole-subtracted-theta-integral-for-an-epstein-zeta-function.md) gives both the continuation and this [dual lattice](dual-lattice.md) symmetry.
