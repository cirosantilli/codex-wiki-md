# Paschen-Back transition-channel count

↑ **Parent:** [Paschen-Back effect](paschen-back-effect.md)

For a one-electron P-to-S transition with [nuclear spin](nuclear-spin.md) zero, ten electric-dipole sublevel channels have finite strength at weak nonzero field. Six retain finite strength in the complete orbital-spin decoupling limit. Channel count is distinct from spectral [frequency](frequency.md) count: retaining first-order residual [spin-orbit interaction](spin-orbit-interaction.md) [energy](energy-split.md) gives five distinct frequencies, with two central channels degenerate.
