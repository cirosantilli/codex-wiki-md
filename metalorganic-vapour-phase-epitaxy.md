# Metalorganic vapour-phase epitaxy

↑ **Parent:** [Epitaxy](epitaxy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metalorganic_vapour-phase_epitaxy)

Epitaxial growth using volatile metal-organic precursors delivered in the gas phase. Precursor decomposition and surface incorporation grow a crystalline compound layer or nanostructure; [temperature](temperature-split.md) and [mass](mass.md) transport influence composition.
