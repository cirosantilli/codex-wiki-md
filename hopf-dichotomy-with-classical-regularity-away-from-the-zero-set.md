# Hopf dichotomy with classical regularity away from the zero set

↑ **Parent:** [Hopf lemma](hopf-lemma.md)

Let $v\leq0$ be $C^1$ on a connected domain and $C^2$ away from its zero set, with $\Delta v+v=0$ wherever $v<0$. If negative and zero points coexist, an interior [Euclidean ball](euclidean-ball.md) in $\{v<0\}$ touches its zero set. On that [Euclidean ball](euclidean-ball.md) $\Delta v=-v>0$, so the [Hopf boundary point lemma](hopf-lemma.md) forces a nonzero derivative at an interior maximum, contrary to $Dv=0$. Thus $v$ is identically zero or strictly negative. Mere [Lipschitz continuity](lipschitz-continuity.md) does not suffice: $-\max\{\sin x_1,0\}$ on $(-1,1)^n$ has both negative and zero regions.
