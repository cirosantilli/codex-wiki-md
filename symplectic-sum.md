# Symplectic sum

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic submanifold](symplectic-submanifold.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symplectic_sum)

A symplectic sum removes tubular neighborhoods of symplectic submanifolds whose normal bundles have opposite [Euler classes](euler-class-of-a-vector-bundle.md) and glues their boundaries by a fiber-reversing identification.
