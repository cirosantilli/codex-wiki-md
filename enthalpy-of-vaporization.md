# Enthalpy of vaporization

↑ **Parent:** [Vaporization](vaporization.md)

The molar [enthalpy](enthalpy-split.md) difference between [vapor](vapor.md) and [liquid](liquid.md) at specified conditions, evaluated on coexistence when it is a [latent heat](latent-heat.md). Treating it as constant and treating the [vapor](vapor.md) as ideal gives $\ln(P_2/P_1)=(\Delta H_{\rm vap}/R)(1/T_1-1/T_2)$.
