# Hilltop inflation

↑ **Parent:** [Slow-roll approximation](slow-roll-approximation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hilltop_inflation)

Hilltop inflation begins near a local maximum of the inflaton potential. For

$$
V(\phi)=\Lambda^4-\frac12m^2\phi^2,
$$

a positive field rolls away from the maximum at $\phi=0$.
