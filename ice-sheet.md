# Ice sheet

↑ **Parent:** [Glaciology](glaciology.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ice_sheet)

An ice sheet is a large body of glacier ice covering land. Its mass budget combines accumulation, surface ablation, basal melting and discharge. Changes of floating [ice shelves](ice-shelf.md) can influence discharge of grounded ice through changes in restraint, even though shelf melt itself has only a small direct sea-level contribution.
