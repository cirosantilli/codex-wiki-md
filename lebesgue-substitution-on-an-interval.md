# Lebesgue substitution on an interval

↑ **Parent:** [Change of variables formula](change-of-variables-formula.md)

Let $\phi:I\to J$ be a $C^1$ [diffeomorphism](diffeomorphism.md) between open [intervals](interval-mathematics.md), which may be unbounded. The [change of variables formula](change-of-variables-formula.md) holds for every nonnegative [Lebesgue measurable function](lebesgue-measurable-function.md) $f:J\to[0,\infty]$, allowing infinite integrals. Applying it to $|f|$ also proves equivalence of [Lebesgue integrability](integrability.md) of $f$ and of the transformed integrand; for an integrable signed or complex $f$ the same identity then holds by splitting into positive and negative, or real and imaginary, parts. The absolute value of the [derivative](derivative.md) is essential for a decreasing substitution such as $\phi(x)=1/x$. Endpoints can be omitted because they are [null sets](null-set.md).
