# Engineering dimensions of relativistic fields

↑ **Parent:** [Mass dimension](mass-dimension.md)

Canonical free scalar and gauge-vector kinetic terms contain two derivatives and two fields, whereas the Dirac kinetic term contains one derivative and two spinor fields. Matching their [mass dimension](mass-dimension.md) to $d$ in $d$ spacetime dimensions gives the displayed engineering dimensions. These are the dimensions used in [power counting](power-counting-in-quantum-field-theory.md); interacting scaling dimensions can additionally contain anomalous contributions.
