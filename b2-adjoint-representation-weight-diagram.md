# B2 adjoint representation weight diagram

↑ **Parent:** [B2 root system](b2-root-system.md)

For simple [roots](root-of-a-root-system.md) $(1,0),(-1,1)$, [Dynkin labels](dynkin-label.md) of a Euclidean weight $(x,y)$ are $(2x,-x+y)$. The highest root $(1,1)$ gives the [Adjoint representation](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md), of dimension ten. Its eight nonzero weights are the eight [roots](root-of-a-root-system.md) of $B_2$; the zero [weight](weight-representation-theory.md) has multiplicity two. The nine distinct label pairs are $(2,0),(0,1),(-2,2),(2,-1),(0,0),(-2,1),(2,-2),(0,-1),(-2,0)$.
