# Fujita vanishing

↑ **Parent:** [Serre vanishing](serre-vanishing.md)

Given a [coherent sheaf](coherent-sheaf.md) $\mathcal F$ and an [ample Cartier divisor](ample-cartier-divisor.md) $A$ on a [projective scheme](projective-scheme.md), one integer $a_0$ ensures $H^i(X,\mathcal F(aA+N))=0$ for all $i>0$, all $a\geq a_0$, and every [nef divisor](nef-line-bundle.md) $N$. The uniformity in $N$ is stronger than [Serre vanishing](serre-vanishing.md). [Fujino's note on Fujita vanishing](https://www.math.kyoto-u.ac.jp/~fujino/fujita-vani3.pdf) states the theorem for arbitrary projective schemes over a field.
