# Cyclic imitation chain

↑ **Parent:** [Density-dependent Markov jump process](density-dependent-markov-jump-process.md)

In a population of $N$ playing [rock paper scissors](rock-paper-scissors.md), let losers copy winners. With pair-selection events of rate $(N-1)/2$, the proportion process has jumps $(e_1-e_3)/N$, $(e_2-e_1)/N$, $(e_3-e_2)/N$ at rates $Nx_1x_3$, $Nx_2x_1$, $Nx_3x_2$. Its [fluid limit](fluid-limit.md) obeys $\dot x_i=x_i(x_{i-1}-x_{i+1})$, with indices interpreted cyclically. The [product of numbers](product-of-numbers.md) $x_1x_2x_3$ is conserved in this [ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md), whereas the finite chain eventually reaches a monochromatic absorbing state.
