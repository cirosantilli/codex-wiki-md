# Vortex-induced force on a plane wall

↑ **Parent:** [Line vortex](line-vortex.md)

A vortex of circulation $\Gamma$ distance $d$ from a plane wall has an opposite-sign image across the wall. The wall velocity has magnitude $|\Gamma|d/[\pi(d^2+y^2)]$, giving a pressure reduction $\rho\Gamma^2d^2/[2\pi^2(d^2+y^2)^2]$ by the [Bernoulli equation](bernoulli-equation.md). Integrating this deficit gives the displayed force per unit width, directed from the wall toward the fluid and vortex. The uniform ambient pressure is subtracted to isolate the finite vortex-induced force.
