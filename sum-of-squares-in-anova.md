# Sum of squares in ANOVA

↑ **Parent:** [Analysis of variance](analysis-of-variance.md)

For an [orthogonal projection matrix](orthogonal-projection-matrix.md) $P$, a [sum of squares in ANOVA](sum-of-squares-in-anova.md) is $Y^TPY=\|PY\|^2$. Mutually orthogonal projections yield an additive decomposition of total squared response magnitude. Removing the grand mean gives the corrected total.
