# Coefficient of static friction

↑ **Parent:** [Static friction](static-friction.md)

The [static friction](static-friction.md) force at impending sliding has magnitude $\mu_s$ times the normal contact [force](force.md). In a simple grain model this coefficient represents resistance to downstream motion; the model excludes lift and detailed contact torques.
