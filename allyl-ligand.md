# Allyl ligand

↑ **Parent:** [Ligand in coordination chemistry](ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md)

An [allyl ligand](allyl-ligand.md) can bind as $\eta^3$ $\mathrm{C_3H_5^-}$, donating four [electrons](electron.md) in ionic [electron counting](electron-counting.md). Protonation can release an alkene and make coordination sites available.
