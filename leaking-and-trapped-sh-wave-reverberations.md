# Leaking and trapped SH-wave reverberations

↑ **Parent:** [SH-wave travel times in a crustal waveguide](sh-wave-travel-times-in-a-crustal-waveguide.md)

For subcritical incidence, a reflected [SH-wave](sh-wave.md) loses [energy](energy-split.md) into the faster substrate. Postcritical incidence has an evanescent substrate tail and no outgoing normal [energy flux](energy-flux.md) in the ideal lossless model. Repeated [reflections](reflection-mathematics.md) can then interfere to form [Love waves](love-wave.md). Individual reflected rays are contributions to the field; normal-mode [frequencies](frequency.md) require the reflection-phase condition and are not supplied by a geometrical travel-time curve alone.
