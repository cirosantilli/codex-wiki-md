# Local sound-speed estimate for ring-flow stability

↑ **Parent:** [Current bottleneck in an inhomogeneous condensate](current-bottleneck-in-an-inhomogeneous-condensate.md)

Combining zero-current bulk densities with [quantized circulation](quantized-circulation.md) gives an approximate winding bound. If equal halves have densities $\rho_0(1-a)$ and $\rho_0(1+a)$, $0<a<1$, the more restrictive local condition is $n^2<(mR^2\mu_0/\hbar^2)(1-a)/(1+a)^2$. A self-consistent finite-current calculation must also allow kinetic pressure to change those densities.
