# Duhem problem

↑ **Parent:** [Scientific confirmation](scientific-confirmation.md)

From $T\land A\Rightarrow E$ and $\neg E$, infer only $\neg T\lor\neg A$. A failed prediction therefore does not logically isolate failure of the target $T$ from failure of [auxiliary hypotheses](auxiliary-hypothesis.md) $A$. Independent calibration and varied experiments can discriminate packages; the logical ambiguity does not imply that every package is equally supported.[part II, chapter VI, section 2](the-aim-and-structure-of-physical-theory-by-pierre-duhem.md)
