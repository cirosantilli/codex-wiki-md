# Sharply three-transitive on a projective line

↑ **Parent:** [Projective general linear group action on the projective line](projective-general-linear-group-action-on-the-projective-line.md)

For any field $F$, a [projective linear group](projective-linear-group.md) element is uniquely determined by its images of three distinct points of the [projective line](projective-line.md), and any ordered distinct target triple is attainable. Given representatives $v_z,v_x$ forming a [basis](basis.md) and $v_y=A v_z+B v_x$, the [matrix](matrix.md) with columns $A v_z,B v_x$ sends $(0,1,\infty)$ to $(x,y,z)$. Distinctness forces $A,B\ne0$. The [matrices](matrix.md) realizing one projective map differ by a nonzero scalar; over $\mathbb F_p$ there are $p-1$ such [matrices](matrix.md).
