# Photoreceptor sampling

↑ **Parent:** [Photoreceptor](photoreceptor.md)

Spatial and temporal sampling of [light](light.md) by a finite receptor array. In a regular one-dimensional point-sampling model with spacing $d$, frequencies $f$ and $f+k/d$ give identical sinusoidal samples because the phase difference at sample $n$ is $2\pi kn$. Restricting a real signal below the [Nyquist frequency](nyquist-frequency.md) $1/(2d)$ removes that basic ambiguity. Real retinal mosaics are irregular, two-dimensional and optically filtered.
