# Commutative-monoid enrichment

↑ **Parent:** [Enriched category](enriched-category.md)

A commutative-monoid enrichment gives each hom-set a [commutative monoid](commutative-monoid.md) structure, with zero and addition, so that composition distributes over addition in both variables and annihilates zero. This is called a semi-additive structure in some conventions, including Cambridge Part III paper 119 of 2017. Other conventions reserve [semi-additive category](semi-additive-category.md) for categories with this enrichment and finite [biproducts](biproduct.md). A one-object category arising from a [semiring](semiring.md) has the enrichment without generally having those finite products.
