# H2RG detector

↑ **Parent:** [Hybrid infrared detector](hybrid-infrared-detector.md)

The HAWAII-2RG architecture has a $2048\times2048$ grid with an 18-micrometre pitch. For infrared work its readout circuit is normally bonded to a [mercury cadmium telluride](mercury-cadmium-telluride.md) absorber. Reference pixels track electronic offsets and an independently addressed guide window can be read alongside the science array.
