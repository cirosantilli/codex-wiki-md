# Polynomial hull

↑ **Parent:** [Complex analysis](complex-analysis-split.md)

For a compact set $K\subset\mathbb C^n$, its polynomial hull is the set displayed, where $p$ ranges over all complex [multivariate polynomials](multivariate-polynomial.md). It contains $K$. In one complex dimension, bounded complementary components are filled in: the [maximum modulus principle](maximum-modulus-principle.md) bounds every [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) inside those components by its boundary values. Polynomial approximation and separation distinguish points outside the hull.
