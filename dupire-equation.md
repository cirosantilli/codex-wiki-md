# Dupire equation

↑ **Parent:** [Local volatility model](local-volatility-model.md)

For a non-dividend-paying stock and constant interest rate $r$, discounted call prices satisfy $C_T=\tfrac12K^2\sigma(T,K)^2C_{KK}-rKC_K$. Strike differentiation recovers the discounted terminal density as $C_{KK}$; differentiating the discounted payoff identity supplies the maturity derivative. [Put-call parity](put-call-parity.md) implies the same equation for put prices.
