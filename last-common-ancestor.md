# Last common ancestor

↑ **Parent:** [Phylogenetics](phylogenetics.md)

The most recent ancestor shared by the stated descendant lineages. In population history it is important to distinguish a shared ancestral population from the common ancestor of copies at a particular [genetic locus](genetic-locus.md).
