# Hilbert cube

↑ **Parent:** [Ramsey theory](ramsey-theory-split.md)

A Hilbert $n$-cube in the [positive integers](positive-integer.md) is a set

$$
\left\{a+\sum_{i\in I}x_i:I\subseteq[n]\right\},
$$

where $a,x_1,\ldots,x_n$ are positive integers.
