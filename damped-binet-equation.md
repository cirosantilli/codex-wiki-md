# Damped Binet equation

↑ **Parent:** [Binet equation](binet-equation.md)

An attractive [inverse-square force](inverse-square-force.md) together with radial drag $-k\dot r/r^2$ preserves [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) $h=r^2\dot\theta$ but modifies the [Binet equation](binet-equation.md) for $u=1/r$ by a first derivative. For $k/|h|<2$ and $h\ne0$, the reciprocal radius is a decaying oscillation about $p/h^2$. Forward angular distance $\operatorname{sgn}(h)(\theta-\theta_0)$ makes its damping sign explicit for either orbital orientation.
