# Self-similar starting plume

↑ **Parent:** [Starting plume](starting-plume.md)

For constant kinematic [buoyancy flux](buoyancy-flux.md) $f_0$ from a point source, [dimensional analysis](dimensional-analysis.md) gives head height and radius proportional to $f_0^{1/4}t^{3/4}$, head velocity proportional to $f_0^{1/4}t^{-1/4}$, and head [reduced gravity](reduced-gravity-split.md) proportional to $f_0^{1/4}t^{-5/4}$. Dimensionless coefficients require the chosen [fluid entrainment](fluid-entrainment.md) and head closures.
