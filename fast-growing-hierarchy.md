# Fast-growing hierarchy

↑ **Parent:** [Ordinal arithmetic](ordinal-arithmetic.md)

A fast-growing hierarchy indexes number-theoretic [functions](function-split.md) by an effective system of [ordinal](ordinal.md) notations. Successor stages iterate the preceding function, while limit stages diagonalize along a chosen [fundamental sequence of a limit ordinal](fundamental-sequence-of-a-limit-ordinal.md). These choices are part of the definition. With the standard [Cantor normal form](cantor-normal-form.md) sequences below [epsilon zero](epsilon-zero.md), each fixed $F_\alpha$ for $\alpha<\varepsilon_0$ is provably total in [Peano arithmetic](peano-arithmetic.md), but the uniform endpoint $F_{\varepsilon_0}$ is not. Every function provably total in [Peano arithmetic](peano-arithmetic.md) is eventually bounded by some lower level. These classification results are proved in [https://epub.ub.uni-muenchen.de/3843/1/3843.pdf](https://epub.ub.uni-muenchen.de/3843/1/3843.pdf) .
