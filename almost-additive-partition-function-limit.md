# Almost-additive partition-function limit

↑ **Parent:** [Thermodynamic formalism](thermodynamic-formalism.md)

The sequences $a_n+D$ and $a_n-D$ are respectively subadditive and superadditive. Apply [Fekete's lemma](fekete-s-lemma.md) to the first and its negative to the second; their normalized difference is $2D/n$, so their limits coincide. For full-shift weighted orbit sums, summable variations of the potential bound the energy change at a concatenation boundary, giving this estimate for the logarithm of the [partition function](canonical-partition-function.md). A fixed initial symbol or fixed remote tail changes only a bounded normalization and not the limit.
