# Total internal reflection

↑ **Parent:** [Snell law for electromagnetic waves](snell-law-for-electromagnetic-waves.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Total_internal_reflection)

Total internal reflection occurs when a wave travels from higher to lower [refractive index](refractive-index.md) above the critical incidence angle, leaving no propagating transmitted plane wave normal to the interface.
