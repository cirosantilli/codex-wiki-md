# Plancherel theorem for locally compact abelian groups

↑ **Parent:** [Plancherel theorem](plancherel-theorem.md)

For $h\in C_c(G)$, apply [Fourier inversion on a locally compact abelian group](fourier-inversion-on-a-locally-compact-abelian-group.md) at the identity to $h*h^*$, whose transform is $|\widehat h|^2$. This proves the displayed norm identity on $C_c(G)$. Density and completeness give a unique linear isometric extension to all $L^2(G)$. On $L^1\cap L^2$ it agrees almost everywhere with the integral [Fourier transform on a locally compact abelian group](fourier-transform-on-a-locally-compact-abelian-group.md).
