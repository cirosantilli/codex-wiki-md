# Bromine monoxide

↑ **Parent:** [Atmospheric chemistry](atmospheric-chemistry.md)

The [radical](radical-chemistry.md) $\mathrm{BrO}$ participates in catalytic atmospheric [ozone](ozone.md) destruction. Its measured abundance is only one partition of the total [bromine](bromine.md) inventory; precursor delivery, [photodissociation](photodissociation.md) and reservoir reactions determine the relation between the two.
