# Piecewise-deterministic Markov process

↑ **Parent:** [Markov process](markov-process-split.md)

A [Markov process](markov-process-split.md) following a deterministic [flow of a vector field](flow-of-a-vector-field.md) between random jumps, with jump [hazard function](hazard-function.md) $\alpha(X_{t-})$ and destination [transition kernel](markov-kernel.md) $K_t(X_{t-},dy)$. The integrated rate along the flowing path determines the next jump time. Retaining instead the holding times of a different piecewise-constant process need not yield a [Markov process](markov-process-split.md) in the flowing state. Assume [nonexplosion](nonexplosion-of-a-continuous-time-markov-chain.md) when describing paths at all finite times.
