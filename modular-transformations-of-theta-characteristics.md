# Modular transformations of theta characteristics

↑ **Parent:** [Theta function with characteristics](theta-function-with-characteristics.md)

Translation of tau by one gives $\theta[\alpha,\beta](z,\tau+1)=e^{-\pi i\alpha(\alpha-1)}\theta[\alpha,\beta+\alpha-1/2](z,\tau)$. Gaussian [Poisson summation](poisson-summation-formula.md) gives $\theta[\alpha,\beta](z/\tau,-1/\tau)=\sqrt{-i\tau}\,e^{\pi iz^2/\tau+2\pi i\alpha\beta}\theta[\beta,-\alpha](z,\tau)$. The square root is the holomorphic branch positive at tau=i. These formulas keep the characteristic phase, which is lost if one states only the transformation of unshifted theta constants.
