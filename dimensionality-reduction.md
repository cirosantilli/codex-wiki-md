# Dimensionality reduction

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical learning](statistical-learning-split.md)

Representing data using fewer coordinates. A method must specify which properties it tries to preserve, such as reconstruction, local distances, prediction or temporal structure. Preserving most total [variance](variance-split.md) need not preserve the information required by a task.
