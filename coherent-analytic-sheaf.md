# Coherent analytic sheaf

↑ **Parent:** [Sheaf of modules](sheaf-of-modules.md)

On a [complex manifold](complex-manifold.md), a sheaf of modules over the [sheaf of holomorphic functions](structure-sheaf-of-a-complex-manifold.md) is coherent analytic if it is locally finitely generated and the kernel of every morphism $\mathcal O^r\to\mathcal F$ is locally finitely generated. The structure sheaf and locally free finite-rank sheaves are examples. [Cartan theorem B](cartan-theorem-b.md) gives vanishing of their higher [sheaf cohomology](sheaf-cohomology.md) on a [Stein manifold](stein-manifold.md).
