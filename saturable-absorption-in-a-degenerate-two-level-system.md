# Saturable absorption in a degenerate two-level system

↑ **Parent:** [Absorption cross section](absorption-cross-section.md)

For a closed [two-level system](two-level-system.md) with total population $N$, excited-state lifetime $\tau$ and statistical sublevel populations, let $W_a=\sigma_{12}I/(\hbar\omega)$. The steady [rate equation](rate-equation.md) $0=W_a[N_1-(g_1/g_2)N_2]-N_2/\tau$ gives [absorption coefficient](absorption-coefficient.md) $\kappa=N\sigma_{12}/(1+I/I_{s,a})$. For a uniform slab of thickness $L$ and transmission $T=I_{\rm out}/I_{\rm in}$, integrating $dI/dz=-\kappa I$ proves $\ln T+I_{\rm in}(T-1)/I_{s,a}=-N\sigma_{12}L$. Resolved sublevel [optical pumping](optical-pumping.md) requires a more detailed model.
