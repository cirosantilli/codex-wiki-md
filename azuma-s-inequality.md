<h1 id="azuma-s-inequality">Azuma's inequality</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Martingale](martingale-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azuma's_inequality)

If a [martingale](martingale-split.md) has increments with $|M_i-M_{i-1}|\leq c_i$ almost surely for deterministic $c_i$, then each one-sided deviation of magnitude $t>0$ from $M_0$ has [probability](probability.md) at most $\exp(-t^2/(2\sum_i c_i^2))$. If all $c_i$ vanish, the [martingale](martingale-split.md) is constant. The inequality gives concentration from bounded increments without requiring independent increments.
