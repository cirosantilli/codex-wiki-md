# Parallel lines as a quotient vector space

↑ **Parent:** [Quotient vector space](quotient-vector-space.md)

For fixed $u\ne0$, the parallel [affine lines](affine-line.md) $l_x=x+\operatorname{span}(u)$ are precisely the cosets of the indicated [quotient vector space](quotient-vector-space.md). Changing representatives adds a multiple of $u$, so $l_x+l_y=l_{x+y}$ and $a l_x=l_{ax}$ are well-defined. The zero is the whole line $l_0$, not a single point. If $(u,b_1,b_2)$ is a [basis](basis.md), the two line classes $l_{b_1},l_{b_2}$ form a [basis](basis.md) of the quotient by spanning and independence modulo $\operatorname{span}(u)$.
