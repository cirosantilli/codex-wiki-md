# Lower ramification numbering

↑ **Parent:** [Ramification group](ramification-group.md)

For all real $s\geq0$, this definition uses the normalized integral valuation on $L$. Equivalently $G_s=G_{\lceil s\rceil}$. The group $G_0$ is the [inertia group](inertia-group.md) and $G_1$ is the [wild inertia group](wild-inertia-group.md).
