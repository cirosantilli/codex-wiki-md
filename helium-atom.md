# Helium atom

↑ **Parent:** [Helium](helium.md)

A neutral [helium atom](helium-atom.md) contains two [electrons](electron.md) bound to a [nucleus](atomic-nucleus-split.md) of charge $+2e$. Even with a fixed [nucleus](atomic-nucleus-split.md) and nonrelativistic kinetics, the repulsion $e^2/(4\pi\epsilon_0r_{12})$ prevents exact separation into two hydrogenic one-electron problems. Its ground configuration is $1s^2$ with total [spin](spin.md) zero, while distinct occupied orbitals allow both [singlet helium states](singlet-helium-state.md) and [triplet helium states](triplet-helium-state.md).
