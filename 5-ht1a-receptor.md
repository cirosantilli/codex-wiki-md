# 5-HT1A receptor

↑ **Parent:** [Serotonin receptor](serotonin-receptor.md)

A [serotonin receptor](serotonin-receptor.md) that can act as a raphe autoreceptor or a [receptor](receptor-biochemistry.md) on non-serotonergic target cells. Developmental and acute functions must be distinguished.
