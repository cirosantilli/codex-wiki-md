# Localization length

↑ **Parent:** [Localized eigenstate](localized-eigenstate.md)

The [localization length](localization-length.md) $\xi$ measures the decay scale of a [localized eigenstate](localized-eigenstate.md)'s [wave amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) envelope. Here $a$ is the site spacing and $n_0$ its centre. With this amplitude convention, probability decays on half that scale:

$$
|\psi_n|\sim e^{-|n-n_0|a/\xi},\qquad |\psi_n|^2\sim e^{-2|n-n_0|a/\xi}.
$$

Individual random tails fluctuate; this notation describes their typical envelope, not exact equality at every site. A [localization length](localization-length.md) much greater than a finite sample's [length](length.md) makes a state look extended inside that sample. The [participation ratio](participation-ratio.md) below is an effective site count, not an independent fitted value of $\xi$.
