# Conormal module of the diagonal

↑ **Parent:** [Kähler differential](kahler-differential.md)

For a commutative $k$-algebra $A$, let $I$ be the kernel of multiplication $\mu:A\otimes_k A\to A$. The formula $a\mapsto[1\otimes a-a\otimes1]$ is a [derivation of an algebra](derivation-of-an-algebra.md) into the [quotient module](quotient-module.md) $I/I^2$, with $A$ acting through the first factor. It induces an isomorphism from the [module of Kähler differentials](kahler-differential.md). An inverse is induced by $q(x\otimes y)=x\,dy$: the identity $q(rs)=\mu(r)q(s)+\mu(s)q(r)$ shows $q(I^2)=0$. For $z=\sum_i x_i\otimes y_i\in I$, the equality $\sum_i x_i y_i=0$ gives $[z]=\sum_i x_i[1\otimes y_i-y_i\otimes1]$, proving that the two maps are inverse. For an [affine variety](affine-algebraic-set.md), $I$ is the defining ideal of its [diagonal morphism](diagonal-morphism.md).
