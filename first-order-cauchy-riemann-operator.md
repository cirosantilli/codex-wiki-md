# First-order Cauchy-Riemann operator

↑ **Parent:** [Cauchy-Riemann equations](cauchy-riemann-equations.md)

The first-order Cauchy-Riemann operator on two real variables is $\partial_{\bar z}=\tfrac12(\partial_{x_1}+i\partial_{x_2})$. It is a scalar complex [elliptic differential operator](elliptic-differential-operator.md), since its [principal symbol](principal-symbol-of-a-partial-differential-equation.md) has nonzero modulus at every nonzero real frequency.
