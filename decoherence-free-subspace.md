# Decoherence-free subspace

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum error correction](quantum-error-correction-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decoherence-free_subspaces)

A subspace on which every operator in a specified noise model acts as a common scalar, so the encoded [density operator](density-matrix-split.md) is preserved without syndrome-dependent recovery. For collective $z$ rotations, the span of $|01\rangle$ and $|10\rangle$ has zero total $Z$ eigenvalue and is invariant. Protection depends on the collective-noise assumption; independent local rotations do not generally preserve its logical phase.
