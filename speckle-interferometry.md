# Speckle interferometry

↑ **Parent:** [Astronomical seeing](astronomical-seeing.md)

Calibrated Fourier statistics of short-exposure [speckle patterns](speckle-pattern.md) recover information suppressed by a long seeing-blurred exposure. A power spectrum constrains the modulus, while additional phase-sensitive statistics such as the [bispectrum](bispectrum.md) are needed for image reconstruction. The exposure must adequately freeze turbulence and the source must supply enough [photons](photon.md); noise and calibrator mismatch limit the reconstruction.
