# Consistency of a negated-consistency extension

↑ **Parent:** [Formal consistency statement](formal-consistency-statement.md)

For adequate arithmetic [provability predicates](arithmetical-provability-predicate.md), the [arithmetized deduction theorem](arithmetized-deduction-theorem.md) identifies inconsistency of $S+\neg\operatorname{Con}_S$ with $S$-provability of $\operatorname{Con}_S$. Formalized second incompleteness gives $\Box_S\operatorname{Con}_S\to\Box_S\bot$. Contraposition yields the displayed conditional. This is an internal theorem and does not license removal of its [consistency](consistent-first-order-theory.md) premise.
