# Equivalence of connected double covers of the torus

↑ **Parent:** [Integral linear automorphism of the torus](integral-linear-automorphism-of-the-torus.md)

Any two connected degree-two coverings of the torus fit into a commuting square with homeomorphisms of their total and base spaces. They correspond to index-two subgroups of $\mathbb Z^2$, which are kernels of the three nonzero maps $\mathbb Z^2\to\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z$. Reduction modulo two shows that $GL_2(\mathbb Z)$ acts transitively on these subgroups; the [classification of connected covering spaces](classification-of-connected-covering-spaces.md) and the [lifting criterion for a covering space](lifting-criterion-for-a-covering-space.md) then supply the homeomorphism of total spaces.
