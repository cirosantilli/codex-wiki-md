# Solubility product

↑ **Parent:** [Precipitation (chemistry)](precipitation-chemistry.md)

The [solubility product](solubility-product.md) is the [equilibrium constant](equilibrium-constant.md) for dissolution of a specified [solid](solid.md) into its dissolved [ions](ion.md). For a pure bulk AB [solid](solid.md) at the chosen [temperature](temperature-split.md), [pressure](pressure-split.md) and [standard states](standard-state.md),

$$
\mathrm{AB}(s)\rightleftharpoons\mathrm A^+(aq)+\mathrm B^-(aq),\qquad K_{\mathrm{sp}}=a_{\mathrm A}a_{\mathrm B}.
$$

The pure bulk [solid](solid.md)'s [thermodynamic activity](thermodynamic-activity.md) is one in this convention. Coexisting [solid](solid.md) and [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md) at [chemical equilibrium](chemical-equilibrium.md) give this equality. If all available [solid](solid.md) dissolves, the final [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md) may instead be undersaturated; do not impose equality merely because a salt was initially added.[Chemistry 2e, §15.1, The Solubility Product: saturated solution with excess solid and dissolution equilibrium](chemistry-2e-by-paul-flowers-and-others/15-1-precipitation-and-dissolution.md)

Using the [thermodynamic activity](thermodynamic-activity.md) definition $a_i=\gamma_i c_i/c^\circ$, with [activity coefficients](activity-coefficient.md) $\gamma_i$ and reference [molar concentration](molar-concentration.md) $c^\circ$, gives the independent conversion

$$
K_{\mathrm{sp}}=\gamma_{\mathrm A}\gamma_{\mathrm B}\frac{c_{\mathrm A}c_{\mathrm B}}{(c^\circ)^2},\qquad K_{\mathrm{sp},c}=\frac{K_{\mathrm{sp}}(c^\circ)^2}{\gamma_{\mathrm A}\gamma_{\mathrm B}}.
$$

Thus $K_{\mathrm{sp}}$ is [dimensionless](dimensionless-quantity.md), while the conditional concentration form $K_{\mathrm{sp},c}$ has units $\mathrm M^2$ for this one-to-one model. Treating $K_{\mathrm{sp},c}$ as constant requires a justified fixed or approximately fixed [activity coefficient](activity-coefficient.md) product. Changing [ionic strength](ionic-strength.md) can change that product even at fixed [temperature](temperature-split.md); changing [temperature](temperature-split.md) can change the [equilibrium constant](equilibrium-constant.md) itself. Specify the [solid](solid.md) [thermodynamic phase](thermodynamic-phase.md) too: different [crystal polymorphs](crystal-polymorph.md) need not have the same [solubility product](solubility-product.md).
