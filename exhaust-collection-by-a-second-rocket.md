# Exhaust collection by a second rocket

↑ **Parent:** [Rocket equation](rocket-equation.md)

Two initially stationary rockets exchange a fuel load $M$: A ejects it with constant rearward relative [speed](speed.md) $u_0$ and B captures all of it. A has final [velocity](velocity.md) $v_{Af}=u_0\ln[(m_A+M)/m_A]$. Since final [momentum](momentum-split.md) is zero, $(m_B+M)v_{Bf}=-m_Av_{Af}$. No elastic-capture assumption is needed. With negligible flight time, transferred [mass](mass.md) $q$ gives the same balance with instantaneous [masses](mass.md) $m_A+M-q$ and $m_B+q$; their opposite [speeds](speed.md) are equal when those [masses](mass.md) agree. Final complete collection is a condition on the model, not a guarantee that every ballistic exhaust trajectory intercepts B.
