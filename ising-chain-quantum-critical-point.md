# Ising-chain quantum critical point

↑ **Parent:** [Transverse-field Ising model](transverse-field-ising-model.md)

For positive ferromagnetic exchange and nonnegative transverse field, the [Ising-chain quasiparticle dispersion](ising-chain-quasiparticle-dispersion.md) closes its gap at $g=1$, with $\epsilon_k\sim2J|k|$. This is the [quantum phase transition](quantum-phase-transition.md) between the model's ordered and field-polarized regimes. The exact dispersion identifies the gapless scale; computing its order parameter additionally requires ground-state correlations.
