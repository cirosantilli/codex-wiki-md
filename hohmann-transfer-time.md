# Hohmann transfer time

↑ **Parent:** [Hohmann transfer orbit](hohmann-transfer-orbit.md)

A [Hohmann transfer orbit](hohmann-transfer-orbit.md) between coplanar [circular Kepler orbits](circular-kepler-orbit.md) of radii $r_1,r_2$ traverses half an [elliptic orbit](elliptic-orbit.md) of [semi-major axis](semi-major-axis.md) $a=(r_1+r_2)/2$. [Kepler's third law](kepler-s-third-law.md) therefore gives $t=\pi\sqrt{a^3/(GM)}$. Relative to the initial [orbital period](orbital-period.md) $T_1$, $t=(T_1/2)[(r_1+r_2)/(2r_1)]^{3/2}$. Radii must be measured from the attracting centre, rather than from its surface.
