# Ramanujan differential relations

↑ **Parent:** [Eisenstein series](eisenstein-series.md)

For normalized [Eisenstein series](eisenstein-series.md) $E_2,E_4,E_6$ and $D=q\frac{d}{dq}$, the relations are $DE_2=(E_2^2-E_4)/12$, $DE_4=(E_2E_4-E_6)/3$, and $DE_6=(E_2E_6-E_4^2)/2$. They turn derivatives of these series into polynomial expressions in the same series.
