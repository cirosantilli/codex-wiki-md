# Market price of risk

↑ **Parent:** [Local martingale deflator](local-martingale-deflator.md)

In a one-factor diffusion market, the market price of risk is the excess [drift](drift-coefficient.md) per unit [spot volatility](spot-volatility.md). It is the coefficient in the [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) part of a [local martingale deflator](local-martingale-deflator.md), $dY_t=-Y_t(r_tdt+\lambda_tdW_t)$.
