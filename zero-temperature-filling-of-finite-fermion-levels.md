# Zero-temperature filling of finite fermion levels

↑ **Parent:** [Pauli exclusion principle](pauli-exclusion-principle.md)

For $M$ complete [energy eigenstates](energy-eigenstate.md) of a one-particle [Quantum Hamiltonian](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) for identical noninteracting [fermions](fermion.md), order their energies $\epsilon_1\le\cdots\le\epsilon_M$. At fixed particle number $0\le N\le M$, the [Quantum Hamiltonian](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) and the particle-number constraint are

$$
H=\sum_{\alpha=1}^M\epsilon_\alpha n_\alpha,\qquad \sum_{\alpha=1}^Mn_\alpha=N,\qquad n_\alpha\in\{0,1\}.
$$

The [ground state](ground-state.md) occupies the $N$ lowest complete [one-particle states](one-particle-state.md). A short minimization argument suffices: moving an occupant from a higher energy to an empty lower energy decreases the total energy. This filling describes the [ground state](ground-state.md) at [temperature](temperature.md) $T=0$; exclusion by itself also applies to excited [quantum states](quantum-state.md) and at positive [temperature](temperature.md). The underlying thermal description is the [Fermi-Dirac distribution](fermi-dirac-distribution.md).[Sections 3.6–3.6.2, occupation restriction, spin multiplicity and zero-temperature filling](statistical-physics-by-david-tong/3-quantum-gases.md)

If the last occupied energy belongs to a group of $g$ [one-particle states](one-particle-state.md) at the same energy and $q$ of them must be occupied, with all lower states filled, the [ground state](ground-state.md) subspace has [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) $\binom gq$. Exclusion does not select a particular [basis](basis.md) or a particular [quantum superposition](quantum-superposition.md) in that subspace. The factor of two for an [Electron](electron.md) [spatial orbital](spatial-orbital.md) assumes both [spin](spin.md) projections are included; a model restricted to one [spin](spin.md) projection has only one state per [spatial orbital](spatial-orbital.md). A [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) can split the [degenerate energy levels](degenerate-energy-levels.md), and interactions can change the lowest-energy [electron configuration](electron-configuration.md). Thus this finite noninteracting filling rule is not a complete prescription for interacting [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md).
