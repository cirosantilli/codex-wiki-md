# Normal isoperimetric extremal

↑ **Parent:** [Isoperimetric constraint](isoperimetric-constraint.md)

A constrained [extremal](extremal.md) is normal when the constraint [functional](functional.md) has a nonzero [first variation](first-variation.md) in some allowed direction. The [implicit function theorem](implicit-function-theorem.md) then permits a compensating variation, yielding a multiplier equation with objective coefficient normalized to one. The [Euler-Lagrange equation](euler-lagrange-equation.md) applies to the objective integrand minus the multiplier times the constraint integrand.
