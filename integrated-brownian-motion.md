# Integrated Brownian motion

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md)

The ordinary time integral of standard [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) is a centered [Gaussian process](gaussian-process.md) with continuously differentiable paths and derivative $B_t$. For $0\leq s\leq t$, its [covariance](covariance.md) is $\mathbb E(I_sI_t)=s^2(3t-s)/6$, obtained by integrating the Brownian [covariance](covariance.md) $\min(u,v)$. Consequently $I_t$ has [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md) $N(0,t^3/3)$. This time integral is distinct from the [Itô integral](ito-integral.md) with respect to [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md).
