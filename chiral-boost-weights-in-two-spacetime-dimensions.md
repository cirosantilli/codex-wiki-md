# Chiral boost weights in two spacetime dimensions

↑ **Parent:** [Dirac field in two spacetime dimensions](dirac-field-in-two-spacetime-dimensions.md)

For the coordinate [Lorentz boost](lorentz-boost.md) $t'=t\cosh\alpha+x\sinh\alpha$, $x'=t\sinh\alpha+x\cosh\alpha$, the spinor convention $\psi'(x')=S\psi(x)$ gives $S=\exp(\alpha\gamma^5/2)$. In the [Dirac field in two spacetime dimensions](dirac-field-in-two-spacetime-dimensions.md) basis, this yields the displayed weights. The derivatives $\partial_t+\partial_x$ and $\partial_t-\partial_x$ scale by $e^{-\alpha}$ and $e^\alpha$, respectively, so the two [kinetic terms](kinetic-term.md) are invariant. Opposite weights cancel in the [Dirac mass term](dirac-mass-term.md). Reversing the coordinate boost convention reverses both exponents.
