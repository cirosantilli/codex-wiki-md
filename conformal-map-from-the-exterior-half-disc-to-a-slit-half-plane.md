# Conformal map from the exterior half-disc to a slit half-plane

↑ **Parent:** [Conformal map](conformal-map.md)

The [rational function](rational-function.md) $g(z)=2z/(1-z^2)$ is a [conformal](conformal-map.md) bijection from $\{z:\operatorname{Im}z>0,\ |z|>1\}$ onto $\{w:\operatorname{Im}w>0\}\setminus\{iy:y\geq1\}$. For a proof, the [Cayley transform between the half-plane and disk](cayley-transform-between-the-half-plane-and-disk.md) $f(z)=(z-i)/(z+i)$ maps the source onto the right half of the [unit disc](unit-disc.md). Squaring maps this half-disc bijectively onto $\mathbb D\setminus(-1,0]$. Finally $T(s)=i(1-s)/(1+s)$ maps the disc to the [upper half-plane](upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md) and $(-1,0]$ to the excluded ray. The composition $T(f(z)^2)$ simplifies to $g(z)$. Each factor is [holomorphic](holomorphic-function-split.md) with nonzero derivative and bijective on its indicated domain.
