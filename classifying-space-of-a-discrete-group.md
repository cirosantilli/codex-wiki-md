# Classifying space of a discrete group

↑ **Parent:** [Classifying space](classifying-space.md)

Give a discrete group $G$ a contractible free [CW complex](cw-complex.md) $EG$. The quotient $BG=EG/G$ has [universal cover](universal-cover.md) $EG$, so it has [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md) $G$ and no higher [homotopy groups](homotopy-group.md). It is thus an [Eilenberg–MacLane space](eilenberg-maclane-space.md) $K(G,1)$, even when $G$ is nonabelian. The cohomology of $BG$ with the appropriate local coefficients computes [group cohomology](group-cohomology.md).
