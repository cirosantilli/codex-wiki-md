# Translational magnetic damping of a square wire loop

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic braking](magnetic-braking.md)

A square wire loop partly in a uniform field has linear drag $B^2a^2v/R$, giving velocity-relaxation time $\tau_t=mR/(B^2a^2)$. For uniform wire of mass [density](density.md) $\rho_m$ and resistivity $\rho_e$, $\tau_t=16\rho_m\rho_e/B^2$, independent of wire dimensions.
