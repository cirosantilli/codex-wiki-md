# Mean-field magnetic-susceptibility amplitude ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic susceptibility](magnetic-susceptibility.md)

For a scalar quartic [Landau free energy](landau-free-energy.md) with $a=a_0t$ and positive quartic coefficient, the zero-field susceptibility is $1/(a_0t)$ above the transition and $1/(2a_0|t|)$ within a selected ordered phase below it. Their singular amplitudes have ratio two. Below the transition this is a one-sided pure-phase response: the globally minimizing [magnetization](magnetization.md) jumps between opposite phases when the field crosses zero. The mean-field ratio is not asserted to be exact in every fluctuation-dominated system.
