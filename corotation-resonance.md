# Corotation resonance

↑ **Parent:** [Orbital resonance](orbital-resonance.md)

A rotating gravitational pattern resonates with a [stellar orbit](stellar-orbit.md) when their azimuthal [frequencies](frequency.md) agree. In [action-angle variables](action-angle-variables.md), this is the $k_R=0$ resonance of an azimuthal harmonic. Its torque changes azimuthal [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) without changing [radial action](radial-action.md) to leading order, allowing [stellar radial migration](stellar-radial-migration.md) with little heating. The circular-orbit location is the [corotation radius](corotation-radius.md).
