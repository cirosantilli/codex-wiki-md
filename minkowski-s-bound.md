<h1 id="minkowski-s-bound">Minkowski's bound</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal class group](ideal-class-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minkowski's_bound)

If a number field of degree $n$ and signature $(r_1,r_2)$ has discriminant $d_K$, every ideal class contains an integral ideal $I$ with

$$
N(I)\leq
\left(\frac4\pi\right)^{r_2}
\frac{n!}{n^n}\sqrt{|d_K|}.
$$
