# Capacitance of an isolated conducting sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Capacitance](capacitance.md)

An isolated conducting sphere of radius $R$ in a medium of [permittivity](permittivity.md) $\epsilon$ has surface [electrostatic potential](electric-potential.md) $V=Q/(4\pi\epsilon R)$ relative to infinity. Thus its [capacitance](capacitance.md) is $C=Q/V=4\pi\epsilon R$.
