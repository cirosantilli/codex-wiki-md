# Pyrazine

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md)

A planar six-membered [aromatic ring](aromatic-ring.md) with nitrogen at opposite ring sites. Its molecular [point group](point-group.md) is the [D2h point group](d2h-point-group.md). The six ring $p_z$ [atomic orbitals](atomic-orbital.md) form a six-[electron](electron.md) [conjugated system](conjugated-system.md); the in-plane nitrogen lone pairs are outside this $\pi$ basis.
