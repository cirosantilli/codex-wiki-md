# Dirichlet realization of an elliptic operator

↑ **Parent:** [Elliptic boundary value problem](elliptic-boundary-value-problem-split.md)

A smooth uniformly elliptic differential expression with symmetric Dirichlet [bilinear form](bilinear-form.md) has an unbounded [self-adjoint operator](self-adjoint-operator.md) realization $A_D$ on $L^2(U)$, whose domain is $H^2(U)\cap H_0^1(U)$ on a smooth bounded domain. If the form is strictly positive, the [Lax-Milgram theorem](lax-milgram-theorem.md) and [elliptic regularity](elliptic-regularity.md) make $A_D^{-1}$ a bounded map from $L^2$ to $H^2\cap H_0^1$. Its action on $L^2$ is [compact](compact-space.md) by the [Rellich-Kondrachov compactness theorem](rellich-kondrachov-theorem.md) and self-adjoint by symmetry of the form. The [spectral theorem for compact Hermitian operators](spectral-theorem-for-compact-hermitian-operators.md) therefore gives an [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) of smooth Dirichlet [eigenfunctions](eigenfunction.md) with positive [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) tending to infinity.
