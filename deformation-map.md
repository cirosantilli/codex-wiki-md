# Deformation map

↑ **Parent:** [Deformation (mechanics)](deformation-mechanics.md)

A deformation map sends each particle's [reference configuration](reference-configuration.md) position to its [current configuration](current-configuration.md) position. Its spatial derivative with respect to the [Lagrangian coordinates](lagrangian-coordinate.md) is the [deformation gradient](deformation-gradient.md).
