# Electroweak unification history

↑ **Parent:** [Electroweak interaction](electroweak-interaction.md)

[Sheldon Lee Glashow](sheldon-lee-glashow.md)'s 1979 lecture, Towards a Unified Theory: Threads in a Tapestry, recalls his 1961 $SU(2)\times U(1)$ construction: a [photon](photon.md), a massive neutral intermediary and a mixing angle. It also stresses its unresolved mass problem: symmetry-breaking masses had been inserted, rather than generated by the [Higgs mechanism](higgs-mechanism.md). Establishing the gauge structure and explaining the [gauge boson](gauge-boson.md) masses were complementary steps.[Authored Nobel-lecture scan, 8 December 1979, printed pp. 497–499, “The weak and electromagnetic interactions”](towards-a-unified-theory-threads-in-a-tapestry-by-sheldon-lee-glashow.md)

[Steven Weinberg](steven-weinberg.md)'s A Model of Leptons (1967) builds a spontaneously broken [gauge theory](gauge-theory.md) for the electron-type [leptons](lepton.md), using a scalar doublet to give masses while retaining a massless [photon](photon.md). It derives neutral and charged couplings and discusses [neutrino](neutrino.md)-[electron](electron.md) scattering. Its closing question about [renormalizability](renormalizable-quantum-field-theory.md) is explicitly unresolved; the paper is neither a proof of that property nor a complete treatment of [quarks](quark.md).[A Model of Leptons, Physical Review Letters 19 (1967), pp. 1264–1266; opening scope, Eqs. (4)–(8), closing renormalizability question and footnote 1](a-model-of-leptons-by-steven-weinberg.md)

[Abdus Salam](abdus-salam.md)'s 1979 lecture, Gauge Unification of Fundamental Forces, describes his and [Steven Weinberg](steven-weinberg.md)'s completion of the spontaneously broken $SU(2)\times U(1)$ formulation for [leptons](lepton.md). He separates that construction from the subsequent establishment of [renormalizability](renormalizable-quantum-field-theory.md). Its role is to connect the mass mechanism with predictive neutral and charged couplings, not to identify a photographed event. The [Gargamelle](gargamelle.md) evidence subsequently tested the predicted [neutral current](neutral-current.md).[Salam's authored Nobel lecture, 8 December 1979, §II, printed pp. 519–522, especially p. 521; university-hosted collected scan](gauge-unification-of-fundamental-forces-by-abdus-salam.md)
