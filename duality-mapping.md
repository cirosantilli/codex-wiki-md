# Duality mapping

↑ **Parent:** [Banach space](banach-space-split.md)

The [duality mapping](duality-mapping.md) takes a vector to its supporting dual functionals for half its squared [norm](norm.md). Equivalently $p\in\mathcal J(x)$ when $\|p\|_* =\|x\|$ and $\langle x,p\rangle=\|x\|^2$. This follows from the dual-norm inequality and equality in the conjugate relation for squared norms. In a [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md), the [Riesz representation theorem](riesz-representation-theorem.md) identifies it with the identity; in a general [Banach space](banach-space-split.md) it can be set-valued.
