# Uniformly vanishing functions can retain a nonzero integral

↑ **Parent:** [Uniform convergence](uniform-convergence.md)

On the half-line, $\sup f_n=1/(en)\to0$, but $\int_0^\infty f_n=1$. The change of variable $x=ny$ shows why: the decreasing amplitude is spread over an increasing interval. [Uniform convergence](uniform-convergence.md) controls integrals on finite-measure domains; it does not alone control total mass on an infinite-measure domain.
