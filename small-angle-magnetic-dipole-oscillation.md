# Small-angle magnetic dipole oscillation

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic dipole](magnetic-dipole.md)

A [magnetic dipole](magnetic-dipole.md) of fixed magnitude $m_B$, free to rotate about an axis with [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) $J$, has [potential energy](potential-energy.md) $-m_BB\cos\theta$ in a uniform [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md). Near stable alignment, $J\ddot\theta=-m_BB\theta$, so its small-angle period is $2\pi\sqrt{J/(m_BB)}$. This neglects damping, bearing friction and any other restoring [torque](torque.md).
