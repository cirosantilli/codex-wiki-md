# Kohn anomaly

↑ **Parent:** [Electron-phonon interaction](electron-phonon-interaction.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kohn_anomaly)

A feature in phonon dispersion caused by a nonanalytic wavevector dependence of electronic screening. In a quasi-one-dimensional conductor, enhanced response near $2k_F$ can soften a lattice mode and signal an approaching [Peierls transition](peierls-transition.md).
