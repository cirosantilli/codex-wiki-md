# Orthogonal coordinate reduction of a subspace

↑ **Parent:** [QR decomposition](qr-decomposition.md)

For a full-column-rank $n\times k$ matrix $V$, extend the thin [QR decomposition](qr-decomposition.md) $V=Q_1R_1$ to an [orthogonal matrix](orthogonal-matrix.md) $Q=(Q_1\ Q_2)$. Then

$$
Q^TV=\begin{pmatrix}R_1\\0\end{pmatrix},
$$

so $Q^T$ maps the column space of $V$ onto the first $k$ coordinate directions. Successive [Householder transformations](householder-transformation.md) construct the same reduction without first forming a full orthogonal basis.
