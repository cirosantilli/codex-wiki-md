# Discrete-indiscrete adjunction

↑ **Parent:** [Adjoint functors](adjoint-functors.md)

Discretization $D$ and indiscretization $J$ retain underlying sets. Every function $DX\to Y$ is continuous, and every function $X\to JY$ is continuous, giving the [adjunction](adjoint-functors.md). Its induced [monad](monad.md) and [comonad](comonad.md) are idempotent, since repeated [discrete topology](discrete-space.md) or [indiscrete topology](indiscrete-topology.md) construction changes nothing.
