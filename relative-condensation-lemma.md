# Relative condensation lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Relative constructible hierarchy](relative-constructible-hierarchy.md)

If $\alpha$ is a nonzero [limit ordinal](limit-ordinal.md) and $Y\prec L_\alpha(X)$ with $X\cup\{X\}\subseteq Y$, the [Mostowski collapse theorem](mostowski-collapse-theorem.md) sends $Y$ to $L_\beta(X)$ for some nonzero limit $\beta\leq\alpha$ and fixes $X$ pointwise. The base is fixed by transitivity and inclusion of all its members. Transfer the [relative constructible level recognition](relative-constructible-level-recognition.md) formula first by elementarity and then through the collapse isomorphism to identify its image. If $\rho=X\cap\operatorname{Ord}$, ordinal heights are $\rho+\alpha$ and $\rho+\beta$, giving the bound. Having only $X\in Y$ does not guarantee this base-fixing version.
