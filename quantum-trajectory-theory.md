# Quantum Trajectory Theory

↑ **Parent:** [Lindblad equation](lindblad-equation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_Trajectory_Theory)

A quantum-trajectory unravelling represents a [Lindblad equation](lindblad-equation.md) as an ensemble of stochastic pure-state histories. In one jump unravelling, a state evolves between jumps under the non-Hermitian effective Hamiltonian $H_{\rm eff}=H-\frac i2\sum_\alpha L_\alpha^\dagger L_\alpha$, while a jump of type $\alpha$ sends it to a normalized multiple of $L_\alpha|\psi\rangle$.
