# Diamagnetic levitation in an inverse-height field

↑ **Parent:** [Diamagnetism](diamagnetism.md)

A small weakly susceptible [sphere](sphere.md) has induced [magnetic energy](magnetic-energy.md) $-\chi VB^2/(2\mu_0)$ and vertical [force](force-split.md) $\chi VBB'/\mu_0$. In field magnitude $B=B_0r_0/z$, balancing [gravity](gravity.md) gives the title height for $\chi<0$. The vertical [force](force-split.md) varies as $z^{-3}$, giving a restoring derivative at that equilibrium. Three-dimensional trapping cannot be established from an axial field magnitude alone; lateral field geometry and Maxwell constraints also matter.
