# Paramagnetism

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetization](magnetization.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paramagnetism)

In a linear isotropic regime, a paramagnetic material has positive [magnetic susceptibility](magnetic-susceptibility.md): its induced [magnetization](magnetization.md) is aligned with the applied [magnetic field intensity](magnetic-field-intensity.md). [Molecular oxygen](molecular-oxygen.md) supplies an example discussed through [molecular orbital theory](molecular-orbital-theory.md) in the [covalent bond](covalent-bond.md) article.
