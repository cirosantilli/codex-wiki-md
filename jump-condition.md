# Jump condition

↑ **Parent:** [Differential equation](differential-equation-split.md)

A jump condition relates the one-sided limits of a solution or its derivatives across a discontinuity or singular forcing. Integrating a [differential equation](differential-equation-split.md) across a shrinking interval containing a [Dirac delta](dirac-delta-function.md) source determines the permitted jump. A [jump condition for an impulse](jump-condition-for-an-impulse.md) and [Rankine-Hugoniot condition](rankine-hugoniot-conditions.md) are specific instances with different governing equations.
