# Zero sound

↑ **Parent:** [Fermi liquid](fermi-liquid-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zero_sound)

[Zero sound](zero-sound.md) is a collisionless collective [Fermi surface](fermi-surface.md) deformation sustained by [Landau quasiparticle](landau-quasiparticle.md) interactions.[Opening and section 1, pp.101–103](oscillations-in-a-fermi-liquid-by-lev-davidovich-landau.md)

A useful analogy is a crowd whose members respond to their neighbors' density without repeatedly bumping into them: the restoring interaction can remain effective when collisions cannot relax the distribution within one cycle. [Zero sound](zero-sound.md) is collective; it is not a single [Landau quasiparticle](landau-quasiparticle.md) outrunning every other excitation.
