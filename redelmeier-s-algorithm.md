<h1 id="redelmeier-s-algorithm">Redelmeier's algorithm</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Algorithm](algorithm-split.md)

An [algorithm](algorithm-split.md) for enumerating [polyominoes](polyomino.md) by maintaining included cells, an ordered frontier of untried cells, and excluded cells. Exploring inclusion and exclusion in a consistent order prevents different growth histories from counting the same rooted shape repeatedly.
