# Confluent hypergeometric function of the first kind

↑ **Parent:** [Confluent hypergeometric function](confluent-hypergeometric-function.md)

For $b$ not a nonpositive integer, the regular solution normalized by $M(a,b,0)=1$ has the [power series](power-series.md)

$$
M(a,b,z)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{(a)_n}{(b)_n}\frac{z^n}{n!}.
$$

Here $(a)_n$ is the [rising factorial](rising-factorial.md). Substitution into the [Kummer differential equation](kummer-differential-equation.md) proves the coefficient recurrence. This normalization agrees with [NIST's definitions](https://dlmf.nist.gov/13.2).
