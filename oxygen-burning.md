# Oxygen burning

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar nuclear fusion](stellar-nuclear-fusion.md)

Oxygen fusion in sufficiently hot evolved stellar cores has several reaction channels, including the displayed silicon-plus-alpha channel and channels producing phosphorus and protons. Its ashes feed [silicon burning](silicon-burning.md), while [stellar neutrino energy loss](stellar-neutrino-energy-loss.md) strongly affects the evolution timescale.
