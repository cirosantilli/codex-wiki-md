# Finite-mass Marangoni spreading on a liquid film

↑ **Parent:** [Thin-film equations with insoluble surfactant](thin-film-equations-with-insoluble-surfactant.md)

A localized amount $M=\int C\,dx$ of [insoluble surfactant](insoluble-surfactant.md) spreads over a planar film of depth scale $h_0$. Neglect gravity, capillarity and diffusion. Then $C\sim M/\ell$ and [Marangoni stress](marangoni-effect.md) drives $u_s\sim AMh_0/(\mu\ell^2)$, giving $\ell\sim(AMh_0t/\mu)^{1/3}$. The [thin-film equations with insoluble surfactant](thin-film-equations-with-insoluble-surfactant.md) admit a [similarity solution](similarity-solution.md) in which the film height and concentration depend on $x/\ell$, conserving total [surfactant](surfactant.md) and fluid volume relative to the undisturbed layer.
