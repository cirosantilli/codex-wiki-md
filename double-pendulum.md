# Double pendulum

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrangian mechanics](lagrangian-mechanics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Double_pendulum)

A [double pendulum](double-pendulum.md) consists of two pendula coupled because the second pivot moves with the first [mass](mass.md). It has two [degrees of freedom](degree-of-freedom.md): even when the upper pivot is fixed, moving the upper arm carries the lower pivot along. This makes the [double pendulum](double-pendulum.md) a compact example of how [mechanical constraints](constraint-mechanics.md) couple motion, and how [Lagrangian mechanics](lagrangian-mechanics-split.md) converts geometry into dynamics.

Use two equal [point masses](point-mass.md) $m>0$ and two massless rigid rods of equal [length](length.md) $\ell>0$, with joints that allow free rotation in one vertical [plane](plane.md). The upper pivot is fixed; [gravity](gravity.md) is uniform, with [gravitational acceleration](gravitational-acceleration.md) $g>0$. Neglect joint [friction](friction.md) and [drag force](drag-physics.md). Both [angles](angle.md) $\theta_1,\theta_2$ are measured from the downward vertical, positive toward the right, in [radians](radian.md). In particular, $\theta_2$ is an absolute [angle](angle.md), not the bend between the rods: that bend is $\theta_2-\theta_1$.

Dashed vertical lines are the separate references for $\theta_1$ and $\theta_2$; the lower [position vector](position-vector.md) is the sum of the two rod [vectors](vector.md). The drawn [angles](angle.md) illustrate geometry, rather than the [small-angle approximation](small-angle-approximation.md).

The [double pendulum Lagrangian](double-pendulum-lagrangian.md) below gives the exact dynamics of this ideal model. Near the downward [stable equilibrium](stable-equilibrium.md), [normal modes of a double pendulum](normal-modes-of-a-double-pendulum.md) replace the coupled motion with two independent [harmonic oscillators](simple-harmonic-motion.md). Their fixed [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) ratios explain which releases make the rods swing together and which make them swing in opposite directions.
