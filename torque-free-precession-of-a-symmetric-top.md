# Torque-free precession of a symmetric top

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetric top](symmetric-top.md)

For an axially symmetric [rigid body](rigid-body-dynamics-split.md) with $I_1=I_2=I_\perp$ and $I_3=I_\parallel$, no external [torque](torque.md) means that [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) $\mathbf L$ is fixed in an [inertial frame](inertial-frame.md). The axial [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md) $\omega_3$ is constant by [Euler equations for a torque-free rigid body](euler-equations-for-a-torque-free-rigid-body.md), and the symmetry axis obeys

$$
\dot{\mathbf e}_3=\frac{\mathbf L}{I_\perp}\times\mathbf e_3.
$$

Its angle to $\mathbf L$ is constant and it precesses in space at $L/I_\perp$. The transverse angular-velocity components instead rotate in body coordinates at signed rate $(I_\parallel-I_\perp)\omega_3/I_\perp$. These are different frame descriptions and must not be substituted for one another.
