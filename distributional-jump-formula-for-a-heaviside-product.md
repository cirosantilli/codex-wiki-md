# Distributional jump formula for a Heaviside product

↑ **Parent:** [Distributional derivative of the Heaviside step function](distributional-derivative-of-the-heaviside-step-function.md)

For a [smooth function](smooth-function.md) $u$, the [distributional derivative](distributional-derivative.md) of the product of $u$ with the [Heaviside function](heaviside-step-function.md) is $D(Hu)=Hu'+u(0)\delta_0$. Induction gives the displayed formula. It records all boundary sources, including derivatives of the [Dirac delta distribution](dirac-delta-function.md), and determines which initial derivatives must vanish in a [retarded fundamental solution](retarded-fundamental-solution.md).
