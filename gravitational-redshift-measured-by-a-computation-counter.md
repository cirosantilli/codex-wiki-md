# Gravitational redshift measured by a computation counter

↑ **Parent:** [Gravitational redshift between static Schwarzschild observers](gravitational-redshift-between-static-schwarzschild-observers.md)

Two identical stationary devices counting operations at the same rate per [proper time](proper-time.md) act as [clocks](clock.md). If the upper one sends start and finish light signals over a fixed path to the lower one in a stationary [metric](metric.md), both propagation delays are identical in [coordinate time](coordinate-time.md) and cancel from the interval comparison. The lower elapsed [proper time](proper-time.md) divided by the upper one is $\sqrt{g_{tt}(r_2)/g_{tt}(r_1)}$. In the weak field the relative lag is $g\Delta h/c^2$, giving the displayed operation deficit. A rate of $10^{12}$ operations per second, a week and a height difference of ten metres give a deficit of roughly $660$ operations. This observable tests the radial [clock](clock.md)-rate factor; it does not independently determine the radial spatial [metric](metric.md) coefficient because the common signal delay cancels.
