# GNSS

↑ **Parent:** [Geodesy](geodesy.md)

Global navigation satellite systems can measure station position time series in a stated terrestrial [reference frame](reference-frame.md). Vertical and horizontal secular or transient motions require treatment of station offsets, atmospheric delays, loading and reference-frame uncertainty.
