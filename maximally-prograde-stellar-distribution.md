# Maximally prograde stellar distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Streaming velocity](streaming-velocity.md)

For a nonrotating axisymmetric [galactic distribution function](galactic-distribution-function.md) even in $L_z$, reverse every retrograde azimuthal [velocity](velocity.md). The resulting distribution is $F_+(E,L_z)=2F(E,L_z)\Theta(L_z)$: it preserves density and even [velocity](velocity.md) moments but makes the [streaming velocity](streaming-velocity.md) $\langle|v_\phi|\rangle$. Dependence on conserved $E,L_z$ preserves stationarity. A centered [Gaussian distribution](normal-distribution.md) of width $\sigma$ becomes a [half-normal distribution](half-normal-distribution.md), with mean $\sqrt{2/\pi}\sigma$.
