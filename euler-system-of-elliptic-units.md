# Euler system of elliptic units

↑ **Parent:** [Elliptic unit](elliptic-unit.md)

Elliptic-unit distribution gives norm-compatible units indexed by auxiliary squarefree ray moduli, with norm factor $1-\operatorname{Frob}_{\mathfrak q}^{-1}$ at a newly added prime. Kolyvagin derivative operators turn these units into classes with controlled valuations, and suitable auxiliary primes chosen using [Chebotarev density theorem](chebotarev-density-theorem.md) bound class-group characteristic ideals. The construction supplies the difficult divisibility in the CM [Iwasawa main conjecture](main-conjecture-of-iwasawa-theory.md).
