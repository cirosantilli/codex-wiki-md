# Failure of cancellation for connected sums of surfaces

↑ **Parent:** [Connected sum of surfaces](connected-sum-of-surfaces.md)

The [torus](torus.md) and [Klein bottle](klein-bottle.md) are not homeomorphic because only the former is [orientable](orientable-surface.md). Adding a [projective plane](projective-plane.md) to either by [connected sum of surfaces](connected-sum-of-surfaces.md) makes a [nonorientable surface](non-orientable-surface.md) with [Euler characteristic](euler-characteristic.md) minus one. The [classification of compact surfaces](classification-of-compact-surfaces.md) identifies both with three projective planes. Thus connected-sum cancellation can fail even for closed connected surfaces.
