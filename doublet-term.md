# Doublet term

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic term](atomic-term.md)

An [atomic term](atomic-term.md) with total [spin](spin.md) one half and spin multiplicity two. A doublet S term has only J=1/2, while a doublet with nonzero L can have J=L-1/2 and J=L+1/2. Spin multiplicity therefore need not equal the number of resolved [spectral lines](spectral-line.md).
