# Total decay width

↑ **Parent:** [Decay width](decay-width.md)

The sum of the [partial decay widths](partial-decay-width.md) over mutually exclusive final channels. It determines the unstable state's [mean lifetime](mean-lifetime-physics.md) through $\tau=\hbar/\Gamma_{\mathrm{tot}}$. A new open channel increases the total width even when an existing channel's partial width remains unchanged.
