# Heated particle-laden layer

↑ **Parent:** [Suspension (chemistry)](suspension-chemistry.md)

A dilute [particle suspension](suspension-chemistry.md) can supply both excess [mass density](density.md) and heat. To first order its [equation of state](equation-of-state.md) is $\rho/\rho_0=1+\gamma\phi-\theta$, where $\phi$ is the [particle volume fraction](particle-volume-fraction.md) and $\theta$ is fractional [thermal expansion](thermal-expansion.md). [Particle deposition flux](particle-deposition-flux.md) reduces $\phi$, while particle heating increases $\theta$. The competition can reverse the [reduced gravity](reduced-gravity-split.md) and create [unstable density stratification](unstable-density-stratification.md).
