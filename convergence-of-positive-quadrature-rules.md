# Convergence of positive quadrature rules

↑ **Parent:** [Quadrature rule](quadrature-rule.md)

If each $(n+1)$-node quadrature rule is exact through degree $n$ and has positive weights, then it converges on every continuous function. The [Weierstrass approximation theorem](weierstrass-approximation-theorem.md) reduces the error to the uniform polynomial-approximation error, while exactness on constants controls the sum of the weights.
