# Sherrington-Kirkpatrick model

↑ **Parent:** [Spin glass](spin-glass.md)

The [Sherrington-Kirkpatrick model](sherrington-kirkpatrick-model.md) couples every distinct pair of [Ising spins](ising-spin-variable.md) using independent standard [normal random variables](gaussian-random-variable.md) $z_{ij}$ for $i<j$, with $J_{ji}=J_{ij}$. Here $J_0$ and $\mathcal J$ have units of [energy](energy-split.md). [David Sherrington](david-sherrington.md) and [Scott Kirkpatrick](scott-kirkpatrick.md) introduced this scaling in 1975; their original replica-symmetric calculation gave an unphysical negative low-temperature [entropy](entropy-split.md). The model and that initial proposed solution must be distinguished.[pp. 1793 and 1795, coupling scaling (2), replica-symmetric assumption and acknowledged negative entropy](solvable-model-of-a-spin-glass-by-david-sherrington-and-scott-kirkpatrick.md)
