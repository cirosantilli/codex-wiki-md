# Variational costate gradient for Hamiltonian quantum control

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum optimal control](quantum-optimal-control.md)

For $J=\langle\psi(T)|A|\psi(T)\rangle-(\lambda/2)\int_0^Tf^2dt$ and $i\hbar\dot\psi=(H_0+fH_1)\psi$, enforce the state equation with $-2\operatorname{Re}\int\langle\chi|\dot\psi+iH\psi/\hbar\rangle dt$. Variation gives the same [Schrödinger equation](schrodinger-equation.md) for the [costate](costate.md), propagated backward from $\chi(T)=A\psi(T)$. Control variation gives the displayed real [gradient](gradient.md). Forward state propagation, backward [costate](costate.md) propagation, and a constrained [gradient](gradient.md) update form [direct-adjoint looping](direct-adjoint-looping.md). These are necessary stationarity equations, not a guarantee of a global optimum.
