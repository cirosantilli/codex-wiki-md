# Coherent-state overlap

↑ **Parent:** [Coherent state](coherent-state.md)

Expand both states in the [Fock basis](fock-basis.md) and sum the exponential series to obtain the displayed overlap. In particular $\langle\alpha|-\alpha\rangle=e^{-2|\alpha|^2}$. Distinct [coherent states](coherent-state.md) are nonorthogonal at finite separation; dropping this overlap can give incorrect normalization and small-amplitude limits.
