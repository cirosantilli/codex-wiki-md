# Sinusoidal Cole-Hopf solution for negative-flux Burgers flow

↑ **Parent:** [Cole-Hopf transformation](cole-hopf-transformation.md)

For $q_Z-qq_\theta=\delta q_{\theta\theta}$ and initial data $q=\sin\theta$, the [Cole-Hopf transformation](cole-hopf-transformation.md) $q=2\delta\psi_\theta/\psi$ gives $\psi(\theta,0)=\exp[-\cos\theta/(2\delta)]$. The [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md) of this exponential and the [heat equation](heat-equation.md) give the displayed exact positive diffusion solution. Its first harmonic gives $q\sim4\delta[I_1(a)/I_0(a)]e^{-\delta Z}\sin\theta$. The [large-argument asymptotic expansion of a modified Bessel function](large-argument-asymptotic-expansion-of-a-modified-bessel-function.md) makes the ratio tend to one as $\delta\to0$.
