# Boolean cofactor

↑ **Parent:** [Boolean function](boolean-function.md)

The [Boolean function](boolean-function.md) obtained by assigning a fixed [Boolean](boolean-data-type.md) value $b$ to a chosen [Boolean variable](boolean-variable.md) $x$ in $f$. The original [Boolean function](boolean-function.md) satisfies $f=(\neg x\land f\vert_{x=0})\lor(x\land f\vert_{x=1})$.
