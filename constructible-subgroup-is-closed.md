# Constructible subgroup is closed

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic group](algebraic-group.md)

Let $S$ be constructible in an [algebraic group](algebraic-group.md), and $C=\overline S$. The closure is a subgroup. Choose a dense open $U\subseteq C$ contained in $S$. For every $c\in C$, the dense opens $U,cU$ intersect, giving $u=cv$ for $u,v\in S$. Hence $c=uv^{-1}\in S$ and $S=C$. The [Chevalley constructibility theorem](chevalley-constructibility-theorem.md) therefore proves closedness of homomorphism images.
