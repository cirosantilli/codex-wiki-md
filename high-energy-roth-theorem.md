# High-energy Roth theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Roth theorem on three-term arithmetic progressions](roth-theorem-on-three-term-arithmetic-progressions.md)

For fixed $\theta>0$, a sufficiently large finite set of [integers](integer.md) with [additive energy](additive-energy.md) at least $\theta|A|^3$ has a nonconstant three-term [arithmetic progression](arithmetic-progression.md). The [Balog-Szemerédi-Gowers theorem](balog-szemeredi-gowers-theorem.md) and [Ruzsa modelling lemma](ruzsa-modelling-lemma.md) reduce the problem to the [Roth theorem on three-term arithmetic progressions](roth-theorem-on-three-term-arithmetic-progressions.md) in a dense cyclic model.
