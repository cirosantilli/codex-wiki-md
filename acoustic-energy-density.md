# Acoustic energy density

↑ **Parent:** [Acoustic energy conservation](acoustic-energy-conservation.md)

For a linear acoustic perturbation of a uniform fluid,

$$
E=\frac12\rho_0|\mathbf u|^2
+\frac{p'^2}{2\rho_0c_0^2}
$$

is the sum of kinetic and compressional energy densities. Together with the [acoustic energy flux](acoustic-energy-flux.md) $\mathbf I=p'\mathbf u$, it satisfies $E_t+\nabla\mathbin\cdot\mathbf I=0$.
