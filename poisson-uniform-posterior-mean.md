# Poisson-uniform posterior mean

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson mixture](poisson-mixture.md)

For a uniform [prior distribution](prior-probability.md) on $(l,u)$ with $0<l<u$, conditionally independent [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md) observations have sufficient count $s=\sum_i x_i$. Their [Bayesian posterior](bayesian-posterior.md) density is proportional to $t^s e^{-nt}$ on that bounded interval. The displayed [posterior mean](posterior-mean.md) is the [Bayes estimator under squared error loss](bayes-estimator-under-squared-error-loss.md), and is generally not the affine [Bühlmann credibility estimate](buhlmann-credibility-premium.md).
