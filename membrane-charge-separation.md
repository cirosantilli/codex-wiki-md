# Membrane charge separation

↑ **Parent:** [Membrane potential](membrane-potential-split.md)

A [membrane potential](membrane-potential-split.md) can coexist with near [electroneutrality](electroneutrality.md) in the bulk [cytosol](cytosol.md) and external [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md). The small imbalance producing the voltage lies near the [cell membrane](cell-membrane-split.md); most positive and negative [electric charges](electric-charge-split.md) balance in the bulk.[Molecular Biology of the Cell, 4th edition by Bruce Alberts and others; The Resting Potential Decays Only Slowly When the Na+-K+ Pump Is Stopped; Figure 11-22: minute charge redistribution and interfacial excess](molecular-biology-of-the-cell-4th-edition-by-bruce-alberts-and-others/ion-channels-and-the-electrical-properties-of-membranes.md)

For a separate original [capacitor](capacitor.md) model, choose a total [membrane capacitance](membrane-capacitance.md) $C=5\,\mathrm{pF}$ and $V_m=-70\,\mathrm{mV}$. These are stipulated parameters, not measured properties of a particular [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md), and this voltage is not asserted to equal the preceding $E_{\mathrm K}$. Model the separated [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) relative to an uncharged reference by

$$
Q_{\mathrm{inside}}=CV_m=(5\times10^{-12})(-70\times10^{-3})\,\mathrm C=-3.5\times10^{-13}\,\mathrm C,\qquad Q_{\mathrm{outside}}=-Q_{\mathrm{inside}}.
$$

Using the [elementary charge](elementary-charge.md) $e=1.602176634\times10^{-19}\,\mathrm C$, this represents

$$
N_{\mathrm{excess}}=\frac{|Q_{\mathrm{inside}}|}{e}\approx2.18\times10^6
$$

excess monovalent negative-charge equivalents inside. These can represent a deficit of positive [ions](ion.md), rather than the arrival of that many negative [ions](ion.md).

Independently choose an internal reservoir [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) $\mathcal V=1\,\mathrm{pL}=10^{-12}\,\mathrm L$ containing $140\,\mathrm{mM}=0.140\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$ [potassium](potassium.md), with balancing negative [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md). With the [Avogadro constant](avogadro-constant.md) $N_A=6.02214076\times10^{23}\,\mathrm{mol^{-1}}$, its [potassium](potassium.md) inventory is

$$
N_{\mathrm K}=c\mathcal V N_A\approx8.43\times10^{10},\qquad
\frac{N_{\mathrm{excess}}}{N_{\mathrm K}}\approx2.59\times10^{-5}=0.00259\%.
$$

Thus, if charging were entirely due to outward monovalent [potassium](potassium.md) transport, about one in $38\,600$ of these [potassium](potassium.md) [ions](ion.md) would suffice. This is a comparison with the chosen [potassium](potassium.md) inventory, not a fraction of all charged particles or a measurement of spatial [charge density](charge-density.md). No shape, surface area or thickness has been inferred from the chosen [capacitance](capacitance.md) and [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md). The calculation illustrates why substantial voltage does not require a strongly charged bulk [cytosol](cytosol.md).
