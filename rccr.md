# RccR

↑ **Parent:** [Transcription factor](transcription-factor.md)

[RccR](rccr.md) is a metabolite-responsive transcriptional regulator of central carbon genes in studied Pseudomonas species. In [Pseudomonas aeruginosa](pseudomonas-aeruginosa.md), [KDPG](kdpg.md) binding and repression of shunt-associated genes connect carbon state to [enzyme](enzyme-split.md) expression.
