# Oscillating minimizing sequence with no minimizer

↑ **Parent:** [Minimizing sequence](minimizing-sequence.md)

A [minimizing sequence](minimizing-sequence.md) can converge uniformly while its [derivatives](derivative.md) keep oscillating between distinct energy wells. For the [functional](functional.md) $I(u)=\int_0^1[u^4+(u'^4-16)^4]$, with zero endpoint values and [Lipschitz](lipschitz-continuity.md) admissible functions, the triangular waves displayed above have slopes $\pm2$ [almost everywhere](almost-everywhere.md) and height $1/N$. Direct integration gives $I(u_N)=1/(5N^4)$. Nonnegativity therefore makes the [infimum](infimum.md) zero. Attainment would require both $u=0$ and $|u'|=2$ [almost everywhere](almost-everywhere.md), an impossibility. The uniform limit is zero but its energy is $16^4$; thus the energy fails to be [lower semicontinuous](lower-semicontinuity.md) for this convergence. The example explains why control of [function](function-split.md) values alone cannot replace suitable [convexity](convex-function.md) in the [derivative](derivative.md) variable in the [direct method in the calculus of variations](direct-method-in-the-calculus-of-variations.md).
