# Endofunctor

↑ **Parent:** [Functor](functor.md)

An endofunctor is a [functor](functor.md) from a [category](category-split.md) to itself. A [monad](monad.md) adds a unit and multiplication to an endofunctor, satisfying the unit and associativity laws.
