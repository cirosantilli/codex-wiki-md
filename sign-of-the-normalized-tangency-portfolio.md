# Sign of the normalized tangency portfolio

↑ **Parent:** [Market portfolio](market-portfolio.md)

Assume a positive definite [covariance matrix](covariance-matrix.md) and nonzero excess-mean vector $d=\mu-r\mathbf1$. The [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) gives $|d^Tx|\leq\sqrt{d^T\Sigma^{-1}d}\sqrt{x^T\Sigma x}$, with the positive-premium efficient direction $x=cz$, $c>0$. If $s=\mathbf1^Tz\ne0$, its normalized risky portfolio has [Sharpe ratio](sharpe-ratio.md) $\operatorname{sgn}(s)\sqrt{d^T\Sigma^{-1}d}$. Thus negative normalization reverses the efficient orientation. If $s=0$, the efficient risky direction is self-funded and cannot be normalized to a unit risky budget.
