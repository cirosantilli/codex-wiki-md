# Curvatures of a parametrized surface of revolution

↑ **Parent:** [First fundamental form of a surface of revolution](first-fundamental-form-of-a-surface-of-revolution.md)

Put $q=(f'^2+g'^2)^{1/2}$ for the profile parametrization

$$
X(u,v)=(f(v)\cos u,f(v)\sin u,g(v)).
$$

With unit normal $N=(-g'\cos u,-g'\sin u,f')/q$, the [principal curvatures](principal-curvature.md) are

$$
k_1=\frac{g'}{fq},
\qquad
k_2=\frac{f'g''-g'f''}{q^3}.
$$

Consequently $H=(k_1+k_2)/2$ and $K=k_1k_2$.
