# Grand-canonical Hamiltonian of a Bose field

↑ **Parent:** [Weakly interacting Bose gas](weakly-interacting-bose-gas.md)

For a repulsive contact-interacting [Bose-Einstein condensate](bose-einstein-condensate.md), the classical [Hamiltonian density](hamiltonian-density.md) generating evolution in a frame rotating at the [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) is $\mathcal K=\hbar^2|\nabla\psi|^2/(2M)-\mu|\psi|^2+g|\psi|^4/2$. Its integral is the [grand canonical ensemble](grand-canonical-ensemble.md) generator $H-\mu N$, not the physical energy $H$ alone. For $g,\mu>0$ the uniform [mean-field theory](mean-field-theory.md) minima satisfy $|\psi_0|^2=\mu/g$, with a freely chosen constant phase. In the physical frame, the corresponding field has time factor $e^{-i\mu t/\hbar}$.
