<h1 id="3-3-dimethylphthalide">3,3-Dimethylphthalide</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Lactone](lactone.md)

A benzene-fused five-membered [lactone](lactone.md) containing a gem-dimethyl-substituted saturated ring [carbon](carbon.md). It can result from double methyl addition to one [carbonyl](carbonyl-group.md) of [dimethyl phthalate](dimethyl-phthalate.md) followed by ring closure.
