# Heat kernel with constant absorption

↑ **Parent:** [Heat kernel](heat-kernel.md)

The displayed [fundamental solution](fundamental-solution-of-a-linear-differential-operator.md) solves $u_t=u_{xx}-cu$ on the real line with a [Dirac delta](dirac-delta-function.md) at the initial time. It is the ordinary [heat kernel](heat-kernel.md) multiplied by $e^{-ct}$, because that factor removes the constant reaction term. Its [integral](integral-split.md) is $e^{-ct}$ and it converges to the [Dirac delta](dirac-delta-function.md) as $t\downarrow0$. Convolution with initial data gives the corresponding solution. For $c>0$ the total mass decays by absorption; for $c<0$ it grows instead.
