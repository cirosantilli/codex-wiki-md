# Cache hierarchy

↑ **Parent:** [Cache (computing)](cache-computing.md)

Several levels of [memory cache](cache-computing.md), typically combining a small low-[latency](latency-engineering.md) first level with larger later levels. Each level reduces traffic reaching the next; local and global [cache miss rates](cache-miss-rate.md) must be distinguished.
