# Fringe stopping

↑ **Parent:** [Complex visibility](complex-visibility.md)

Electronic compensation of the time-dependent reference [geometric delay](geometric-delay.md) multiplies each [complex visibility](complex-visibility.md) by an opposite reference phase. A stationary [point source](point-source.md) at the [phase centre](phase-centre.md) then has constant corrected phase despite Earth rotation. [antenna](antenna-radio.md) pointing alone does not perform this correction. Off-axis sources retain the relative fringe evolution that encodes their angular offsets.
