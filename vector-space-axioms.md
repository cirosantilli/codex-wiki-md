# Vector space axioms

↑ **Parent:** [Vector space](vector-space-split.md)

Addition makes $V$ an [Abelian group](abelian-group.md): for all $u,v,w\in V$, $(u+v)+w=u+(v+w)$ and $u+v=v+u$, there is a [zero vector](zero-vector.md) $0$, and every $v$ has an additive inverse $-v$. [Scalar multiplication](scalar-multiplication.md) satisfies, for $a,b\in\mathbb F$,

$$
1v=v,\qquad (ab)v=a(bv),\qquad a(u+v)=au+av,\qquad(a+b)v=av+bv.
$$

Both operations take values in $V$. These requirements make manipulating [linear combinations](linear-combination.md) independent of how sums are grouped or expanded.
