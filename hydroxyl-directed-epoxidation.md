# Hydroxyl-directed epoxidation

↑ **Parent:** [Epoxidation](epoxidation.md)

In [hydroxyl-directed epoxidation](hydroxyl-directed-epoxidation.md), [hydrogen bonding](hydrogen-bond.md) between an allylic hydroxyl and the oxidant favors [oxygen](oxygen.md) delivery from a particular [alkene](alkene.md) face. This influences [diastereoselectivity](diastereoselectivity.md) without requiring an unchanged preference under every solvent or protecting-group condition.
