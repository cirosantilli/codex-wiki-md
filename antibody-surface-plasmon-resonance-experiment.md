# Antibody surface plasmon resonance experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Antibody affinity](antibody-affinity.md)

A concrete comparison used a Biacore T100 with a CM5 chip bearing the target [receptor](receptor-biochemistry.md); unused reactive surface groups were blocked. At $25\,{}^{\circ}\mathrm{C}$ in a [pH](ph.md) 7.4 [buffer solution](buffer-solution.md), intact [IgG](immunoglobulin-g.md) and its [Fab fragment](fragment-antigen-binding.md) were injected for $180\,\mathrm s$ at $5\,\mu\mathrm L/\mathrm{min}$; dissociation was monitored for $3600\,\mathrm s$. The optical response tracks changes in [refractive index](refractive-index.md) near the surface, rather than counting occupied [paratopes](paratope.md) directly. A reference surface and blank injections corrected background; association and dissociation responses were fitted with a 1:1 model. The reported intact-[IgG](immunoglobulin-g.md) detachment was slower than that of the [Fab fragment](fragment-antigen-binding.md). [Kaur and others, Materials and Methods, Surface plasmon resonance; Table 1, whole IgG and Fab rows](antibody-unfolding-and-metastable-state-binding-in-force-spectroscopy-and-recognition-imaging-by-parminder-kaur-and-others.md)

This comparison supports format-dependent attachment, not an intrinsic single-site [antibody affinity](antibody-affinity.md) for intact [IgG](immunoglobulin-g.md). The reference and blanks address background, not two-arm [avidity](avidity.md), surface accessibility or delivery by [diffusion](diffusion.md). The paper's fitted 1:1 constants alone cannot distinguish those mechanisms. Its [Fab fragment](fragment-antigen-binding.md) comparison therefore motivates specifying the binding format before interpreting a [dissociation constant](dissociation-constant.md); a good fit does not establish a unique molecular model.
