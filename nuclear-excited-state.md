# Nuclear excited state

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic nucleus](atomic-nucleus-split.md)

A [nuclear excited state](nuclear-excited-state.md) lies above the same [nucleus](atomic-nucleus-split.md)'s [ground state](ground-state.md) in internal [energy](energy-split.md). [spin](spin.md), [parity](parity.md) and approximate [isospin](isospin.md) help classify it. Single-particle rearrangements, broken pairs and collective deformation/vibration give different excitation patterns and can mix. Below particle-emission thresholds, [gamma decay](gamma-decay.md) and [internal conversion](internal-conversion.md) are important de-excitation routes; an [excitation energy](excitation-energy.md) is not necessarily the [energy](energy-split.md) of every emitted [gamma ray](gamma-ray.md) in a cascade.
