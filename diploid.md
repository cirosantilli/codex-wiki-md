# Diploid

↑ **Parent:** [Ploidy](ploidy.md)

Diploid [biological cells](biological-cell.md) have two sets of [chromosomes](chromosome.md). In the ordinary one-locus inheritance model, a [diploid](diploid.md) [genotype](genotype.md) therefore contains two [allele](allele.md) copies at each [genetic locus](genetic-locus.md).
