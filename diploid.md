# Diploid

↑ **Parent:** [Ploidy](ploidy.md)

Diploid [biological cells](biological-cell-split.md) have two sets of [chromosomes](chromosome.md). In the ordinary one-locus inheritance model, a [diploid](diploid.md) [genotype](genotype.md) therefore contains two [allele](allele.md) copies at each [genetic locus](genetic-locus.md).

Two [homologous chromosomes](homologous-chromosome.md) of a given type are distinct inherited members of the two sets; the two [sister chromatids](sister-chromatids.md) of one replicated [chromosome](chromosome.md) are its copied members. In the stipulated $2n=6$ example, a [diploid](diploid.md) [G2 phase](g2-phase.md) [cell nucleus](cell-nucleus.md) has six replicated [chromosomes](chromosome.md), not twelve sets or twelve independently counted [chromosomes](chromosome.md).
