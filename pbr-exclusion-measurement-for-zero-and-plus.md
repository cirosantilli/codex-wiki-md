# PBR exclusion measurement for zero and plus

↑ **Parent:** [Pusey-Barrett-Rudolph theorem](pusey-barrett-rudolph-theorem.md)

The product preparations $00,0+,+0,++$ are excluded respectively by the orthonormal vectors $(01+10)/\sqrt2$, $(00-01+10+11)/2$, $(00+01-10+11)/2$ and $(00-11)/\sqrt2$, written in the computational basis. Each vector has zero inner product with its labelled preparation. This [projective measurement](projective-measurement.md) gives [antidistinguishable quantum states](antidistinguishable-quantum-states.md) and drives the two-copy [PBR theorem](pusey-barrett-rudolph-theorem.md) contradiction.
