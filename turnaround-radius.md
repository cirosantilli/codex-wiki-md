# Turnaround radius

↑ **Parent:** [Spherical secondary infall](spherical-secondary-infall.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turnaround_radius)

The turnaround radius of a shell is its maximum physical radius before collapse. At a given cosmic time, the currently turning shell defines a natural similarity length.
