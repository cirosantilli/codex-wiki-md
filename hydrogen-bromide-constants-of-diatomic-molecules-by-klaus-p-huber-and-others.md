# Hydrogen bromide: Constants of diatomic molecules by Klaus P. Huber and others

🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by Gerhard H. Herzberg](work-by-gerhard-h-herzberg.md), [Work by Klaus P. Huber](work-by-klaus-p-huber.md)

By [Klaus P. Huber](klaus-p-huber.md) and [Gerhard H. Herzberg](gerhard-h-herzberg.md). NIST Chemistry WebBook compilation of diatomic constants.

[https://webbook.nist.gov/cgi/cbook.cgi?ID=C10035106&Mask=1000](https://webbook.nist.gov/cgi/cbook.cgi?ID=C10035106&Mask=1000)

Cited row: ground $X\,{}^1\Sigma^+$ state of [hydrogen bromide](hydrogen-bromide.md), $\omega_e=2648.975\ \mathrm{cm^{-1}}$ and $\omega_ex_e=45.2175\ \mathrm{cm^{-1}}$. The isotope designation in this table is H81Br. These constants characterize low vibrational levels; an exact global [Morse potential](morse-potential.md) is a further modelling assumption.
