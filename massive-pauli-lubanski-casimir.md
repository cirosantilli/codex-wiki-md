# Massive Pauli-Lubanski Casimir

↑ **Parent:** [Pauli-Lubanski pseudovector](pauli-lubanski-pseudovector.md)

For a massive positive-energy [unitary irreducible representation](unitary-irreducible-representation.md) of the [Poincaré group](poincare-group.md) with signature $(+---)$, choose a rest frame. The [Pauli-Lubanski pseudovector](pauli-lubanski-pseudovector.md) has zero time component and spatial components $mJ_i$, up to a common sign convention. Its square is therefore $-m^2\mathbf J^2$. The [SU(2)](su-2-group.md) little-group representation has [spin](spin.md) $s$ and $\mathbf J^2=s(s+1)$, proving the invariant value. The mass and spin label the [massive induced representation of the Poincare double cover](massive-induced-representation-of-the-poincare-double-cover.md).
