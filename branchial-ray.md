# Branchial ray

↑ **Parent:** [Branchial arch](branchial-arch.md)

A cartilaginous projection supporting the [gill](gill.md) apparatus in living cartilaginous fishes. It is not the same structure as a bony-fish [gill](gill.md) raker or a dermal [fin](fin.md) ray.
