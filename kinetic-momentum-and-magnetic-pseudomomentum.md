# Kinetic momentum and magnetic pseudomomentum

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetric gauge](symmetric-gauge.md)

For charge $e$, define

$$
\pi=p-eA,\qquad K=p+eA.
$$

In the symmetric gauge,

$$
[K_i,\pi_j]=0,
\qquad
[K_i,K_j]=-ie\hbar\varepsilon_{ijk}B_k.
$$
