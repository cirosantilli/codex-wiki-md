# Actin cable

↑ **Parent:** [Actin filament](actin-filament.md)

A bundle of [actin filaments](actin-filament.md) that can support directed [intracellular transport](intracellular-transport.md). Absence of a visible cable does not exclude sparse short [actin filaments](actin-filament.md) below an imaging threshold.
