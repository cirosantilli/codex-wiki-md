# Additive energy of a frequency graph

↑ **Parent:** [Additive energy](additive-energy.md)

For a set of shifts $H$ and a map $\theta$ into a [circle group](circle-group.md), this [additive energy](additive-energy.md) counts quadruples whose shift sums and frequency sums both agree. It is the [additive energy](additive-energy.md) of the graph of $\theta$ in the product [abelian group](abelian-group.md).
