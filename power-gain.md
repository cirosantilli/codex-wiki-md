# Power gain

↑ **Parent:** [Electronic amplifier](electronic-amplifier.md)

The ratio of [signal](signal.md) [power](power.md) delivered to a specified output load to [signal](signal.md) [power](power.md) accepted at a specified input, with $P_{\rm in}>0$. An [amplifier](amplifier.md) draws additional [energy](energy-split.md) from its supply, so a [power gain](power-gain.md) above one does not create [energy](energy-split.md).
