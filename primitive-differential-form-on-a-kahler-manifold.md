<h1 id="primitive-differential-form-on-a-kahler-manifold">Primitive differential form on a Kähler manifold</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Lefschetz operator of a Kähler manifold](lefschetz-operator-of-a-kahler-manifold.md)

For degree $k\leq n$, a form is primitive if $\Lambda\alpha=0$, equivalently $L^{n-k+1}\alpha=0$. In middle degree $k=n$ this is equivalent to $L\alpha=0$. On a compact [Kähler manifold](kahler-manifold.md), the same definitions apply to cohomology via [harmonic differential forms](harmonic-differential-form.md).
