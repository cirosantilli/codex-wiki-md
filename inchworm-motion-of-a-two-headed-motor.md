# Inchworm motion of a two-headed motor

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular motor](molecular-motor.md)

In inchworm motion the same head remains in front as the two heads advance without exchanging order. Ideal fixed label positions then advance by one centre-of-mass step per cycle, contrasting with the unequal alternating displacements possible in [hand-over-hand motion of a two-headed motor](hand-over-hand-motion-of-a-two-headed-motor.md).
