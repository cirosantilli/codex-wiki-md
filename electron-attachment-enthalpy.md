# Electron attachment enthalpy

↑ **Parent:** [Electron affinity](electron-affinity.md)

An [electron attachment enthalpy](electron-attachment-enthalpy.md) is the signed molar [enthalpy](enthalpy.md) change for a stated attachment reaction,

$$
X(g)+e^-(g)\longrightarrow X^-(g).
$$

For favourable attachment such as [chlorine](chlorine.md)'s first attachment, the [electron attachment enthalpy](electron-attachment-enthalpy.md) is negative. Its leading isolated-ground-state [energy](energy.md) contribution is $-N_{\rm A}A_{\rm ea}$, where $N_{\rm A}$ is the [Avogadro constant](avogadro-constant.md). At finite [temperature](temperature.md), consistent thermal populations, translational contributions and the $pV$ term in [enthalpy](enthalpy.md) must also be included, together with the same gaseous-[Electron](electron.md) reference used in the corresponding [ionization energy](ionization-energy.md) term. Consequently a measured spectroscopic [electron affinity](electron-affinity.md) is not automatically an exact $298.15\,\mathrm K$ attachment [enthalpy](enthalpy.md) with a minus sign. A [Born-Haber cycle](born-haber-cycle.md) must use compatible [enthalpies](enthalpy.md) throughout.
