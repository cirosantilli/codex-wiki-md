# Dielectric breakdown

↑ **Parent:** [Dielectric](dielectric.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dielectric_breakdown)

[Dielectric breakdown](dielectric-breakdown.md) is failure of an insulating regime under a sufficiently strong [electric field](electric-field-split.md), allowing substantial [electric current](electric-current.md) through the material. For example, an [electron](electron.md) freed from a [chemical atom](atom-physics.md) or [molecule](molecule-split.md) can become a mobile [charge carrier](charge-carrier.md). The small-field linear [electric susceptibility](electric-susceptibility.md) model does not describe this conducting state.[§8.5, dielectric breakdown paragraph immediately before the definition of dielectric strength](university-physics-volume-2-by-samuel-j-ling-and-others/8-5-molecular-model-of-a-dielectric.md)
