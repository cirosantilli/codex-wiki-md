<h1 id="iteration-of-a-one-fixed-point-mobius-transformation">Iteration of a one-fixed-point Möbius transformation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Möbius conjugacy normal forms](mobius-conjugacy-normal-forms.md)

A nonidentity [Möbius transformation](mobius-transformation.md) with only one [fixed point](fixed-point.md) is conjugate to a nonzero translation of the [Riemann sphere](riemann-sphere.md). Its translation iterates are $z+nb$, tending to infinity in the topology of the [Riemann sphere](riemann-sphere.md) for finite $z$, with infinity itself fixed. Conjugating back proves that every point tends to the unique [fixed point](fixed-point.md). This is convergence on the [sphere](sphere.md); if the [fixed point](fixed-point.md) is finite, intermediate iterates can still pass through infinity.
