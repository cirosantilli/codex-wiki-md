# Peano existence theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peano_existence_theorem)

A continuous finite-dimensional [vector field](vector-field.md) has a local solution through every initial point. The theorem asserts existence without uniqueness; local [Lipschitz continuity](lipschitz-continuity.md) supplies uniqueness through the [Picard-Lindelöf theorem](picard-lindelof-theorem.md).
