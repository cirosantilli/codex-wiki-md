# AGB superwind

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar wind](stellar-wind.md)

An episode of particularly strong [mass loss](mass-loss-astrophysics.md) near the end of [Asymptotic giant branch](asymptotic-giant-branch.md) evolution, capable of removing the remaining envelope rapidly compared with its prior evolution. Radiation acting on dust can transfer momentum to the gas; pulsations help lift material into cooler dust-forming layers. Envelope removal terminates sustained [hydrogen-burning shell](hydrogen-burning-shell.md) and [helium-burning shell](helium-burning-shell.md) activity.
