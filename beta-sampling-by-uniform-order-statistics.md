# Beta sampling by uniform order statistics

↑ **Parent:** [Beta distribution](beta-distribution.md)

For positive integers $\alpha,\beta$, the $\alpha$th [order statistic](order-statistic.md) from $\alpha+\beta-1$ independent [uniform distributions](continuous-uniform-distribution.md) on $(0,1)$ has [Beta distribution](beta-distribution.md) $\operatorname{Beta}(\alpha,\beta)$. Selecting the observations below and above a value $u$ gives its [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) proportional to $u^{\alpha-1}(1-u)^{\beta-1}$, with normalizing factor $(\alpha+\beta-1)!/((\alpha-1)!(\beta-1)!)$.
