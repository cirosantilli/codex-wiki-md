# NMR motional correlation time

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear magnetic resonance](nuclear-magnetic-resonance-split.md)

The decay timescale of the local magnetic-field fluctuations that relax a nuclear [spin](spin.md). Its relation to a tracer jump waiting time depends on geometry and the microscopic stochastic model.
