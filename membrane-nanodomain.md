# Membrane nanodomain

↑ **Parent:** [Cell membrane](cell-membrane-split.md)

A [membrane nanodomain](membrane-nanodomain.md) is a small region with locally differing composition or molecular organization. Its size, lifetime and physical origin require measurement; detergent resistance alone does not demonstrate a pre-existing domain in a living membrane.
