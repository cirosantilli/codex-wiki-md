# Nondimensionalization

↑ **Parent:** [Dimensional analysis](dimensional-analysis.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nondimensionalization)

Rescaling dimensional quantities by chosen characteristic scales produces [dimensionless variables](dimensionless-variable.md). The remaining dimensionless ratios identify independent parameters and possible asymptotic regimes.

In the [simple pendulum](simple-pendulum.md) equation, choose the time scale $t_* =\sqrt{\ell/g}$ and put $\tau=t/t_*$. The [chain rule](chain-rule.md) gives $\ddot\theta=(g/\ell)d^2\theta/d\tau^2$, so the equation becomes $d^2\theta/d\tau^2+\sin\theta=0$. This removes $\ell$ and $g$ from the equation, while the [initial conditions](initial-condition.md) still include angular [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) and dimensionless initial angular speed. A solution can therefore be calculated once and rescaled to many physical lengths and gravity values with the same dimensionless initial data.
