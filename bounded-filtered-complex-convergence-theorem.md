# Bounded filtered-complex convergence theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Convergence of a spectral sequence](convergence-of-a-spectral-sequence.md)

A finite filtration of a [cochain complex](cochain-complex.md) produces a convergent [spectral sequence](spectral-sequence.md). The induced abutment filtration is the image of $H^n(F^pC)\to H^n(C)$. Finiteness in each degree suffices; a bounded range of cochain degrees is not required.
