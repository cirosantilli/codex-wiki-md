# Photon absorption recoil correction

↑ **Parent:** [Electronic transition](electronic-transition.md)

An initially stationary isolated [chemical atom](atom-physics.md) of [rest mass](invariant-mass.md) $M$ absorbing a single [photon](photon.md) must recoil. If the internal excitation changes its [rest mass](invariant-mass.md) by $\Delta M=\Delta E/c^2$, [conservation of energy](conservation-of-energy.md) and [conservation of momentum](momentum-conservation.md) give $Mc^2+h\nu=\sqrt{(Mc^2+\Delta E)^2+(h\nu)^2}$ and hence the exact formula in the title. The additional positive [energy](energy-split.md) supplies recoil. An external body sharing the [momentum](momentum-split.md), or an initially moving [chemical atom](atom-physics.md), changes this laboratory resonance condition.
