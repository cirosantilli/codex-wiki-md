# Generalized eigenfunction in the forward-operator metric

↑ **Parent:** [Convex positively one-homogeneous functional](convex-positively-one-homogeneous-functional.md)

A nonzero $u$ satisfying this relation, with $Ku\ne0$, generalizes the [eigenfunction of an absolutely one-homogeneous functional](eigenfunction-of-an-absolutely-one-homogeneous-functional.md) to the quadratic geometry induced by the forward map. Pairing with $u$ yields $\lambda=J(u)/\|Ku\|^2$. With unit data norm it is a [generalized singular vector](generalized-singular-vector.md). If $\lambda>0$, $0\leq\alpha\lambda\leq1$ and the exact data are $Ku$, the vector $(1-\alpha\lambda)u$ minimizes the quadratic [variational regularization](variational-regularization.md) objective. Substitute its raywise [subgradient](subgradient.md) into the [subgradient optimality condition](subgradient-optimality-condition.md). Every minimizing branch has the same predicted data, and injectivity of $K$ gives equality of vectors. The [Bregman distance](bregman-divergence.md) along this ray is zero even though the reconstruction is shrunk. For nonnegative $J$ the branch continues as $(1-\alpha\lambda)_+u$; nonnegativity supplies $0\in\partial J(0)$ for the threshold argument.

Background on these generalized singular vectors: [Benning and Burger, Ground States and Singular Vectors of Convex Variational Regularization Methods](https://arxiv.org/abs/1211.2057).
