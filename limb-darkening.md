# Limb darkening

↑ **Parent:** [Specific intensity](specific-intensity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limb_darkening)

Limb darkening is the decrease of emergent [specific intensity](specific-intensity.md) toward the apparent edge of a stellar disc. A transit depth then depends on the intensity sampled by the planet as well as its projected area; the uniform-disc area ratio is only an approximation.
