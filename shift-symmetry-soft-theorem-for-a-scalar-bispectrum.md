# Shift-symmetry soft theorem for a scalar bispectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Primordial bispectrum](primordial-bispectrum.md)

If a scalar vacuum is invariant under an exact [scalar-field shift symmetry](scalar-field-shift-symmetry.md), the Ward identity makes the leading soft bispectrum vanish:

$$
\lim_{q\to0}\frac{B_\phi(q,k,|\mathbf k+\mathbf q|)}{P_\phi(q)}=0.
$$
