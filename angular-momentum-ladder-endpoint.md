# Angular momentum ladder endpoint

↑ **Parent:** [Angular momentum ladder operator](angular-momentum-ladder-operator.md)

The norm of a raised or lowered [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) state must be nonnegative. At the upper or lower endpoint, the relevant [ladder operator](ladder-operator.md) annihilates the state; the zero vector is not an [eigenvector](eigenvector.md).
