# Doppler shift for a uniformly accelerated receiver

↑ **Parent:** [Rindler horizon](rindler-horizon.md)

In units $c=1$, a receiver on the [hyperbolic motion](hyperbolic-motion.md) worldline $t=a^{-1}\sinh(a\tau)$, $x=a^{-1}\cosh(a\tau)$ has retarded [null coordinate](null-coordinate.md) $p=t-x=-a^{-1}e^{-a\tau}$. A right-moving light ray emitted at $(t_0,L)$ has $p=t_0-L$, so it meets the receiver only if $t_0<L$. The [photon](photon.md) wave covector is proportional to $(1,-1)$, and contraction with the receiver's [four-velocity](four-velocity.md) gives its measured [frequency](frequency.md) as $\nu_0(\dot t-\dot x)=\nu_0e^{-a\tau}$. The intersection condition gives the second displayed equality. Emission at $t_0=L$ lies on the [Rindler horizon](rindler-horizon.md) and never reaches the receiver at finite [proper time](proper-time.md); $L$ is the supremum of receivable emission times, not an attained latest time.
