# Intersection pairing on the Picard group of a surface

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic surface](algebraic-surface.md)

On a smooth projective surface, two [line bundles](line-bundle.md) are represented by divisors $D$ and $E$, and their intersection is the degree of the zero-cycle obtained after moving them into proper position. It is a symmetric bilinear form on the [Picard group](picard-group.md), and $D\cdot E$ equals the degree of $\mathcal O_X(D)$ restricted to $E$ when $E$ is an integral curve not contained in $D$.
