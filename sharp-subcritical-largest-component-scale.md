# Sharp subcritical largest-component scale

↑ **Parent:** [Subcritical component bound for a binomial random graph](subcritical-component-bound-for-a-binomial-random-graph.md)

For fixed $0<\lambda<1$ in $G(n,\lambda/n)$, the [largest component of a graph](largest-component-of-a-graph.md) has the displayed order [with high probability](with-high-probability.md). A dominating [Galton-Watson process](galton-watson-process.md) gives the tail bound $\mathbb P(T\geq j)\leq e^{-(\lambda-1-\log\lambda)(j-1)}$ by the exponential [Markov inequality](markov-inequality.md). For the lower bound, the [tree-component expectation in the Erdős-Rényi model](tree-component-expectation-in-the-erdos-renyi-model.md) diverges at $j=\lfloor(1-\eta)\log n/(\lambda-1-\log\lambda)\rfloor$. The disjoint-set covariance factor and the exclusion of overlaps give relative [variance](variance-split.md) tending to zero, so the [second moment method](second-moment-method.md) supplies a [tree component](tree-component.md) of that order.
