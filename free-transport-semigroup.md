# Free-transport semigroup

↑ **Parent:** [Free transport equation](free-transport-equation.md)

For $1\leq p<\infty$, these operators are isometries on the phase-space [Lp space](lp-space.md), with $U_tU_s=U_{t+s}$. They form a [strongly continuous semigroup](c0-semigroup.md) for $t\geq0$ and extend to a group for all real times. Strong continuity follows by smooth compactly supported approximation and measure preservation. They preserve the [essential supremum](essential-supremum.md) as well, but strong continuity is not claimed on the whole $L^\infty$ space.
