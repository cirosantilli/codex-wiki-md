# Thom isomorphism in complex cobordism

↑ **Parent:** [Complex cobordism](complex-cobordism.md)

For a rank-$r$ real bundle with chosen stable complex normal orientation, the complex-cobordism [Thom class](thom-class.md) gives the displayed isomorphism by $a\mapsto\pi^*a\,u_\xi$. Stabilizing by a trivial complex bundle suspends the Thom space and its class compatibly. The class of an actual complex rank-$n$ bundle has degree $2n$.
