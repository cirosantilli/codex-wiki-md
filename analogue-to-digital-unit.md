# Analogue-to-digital unit

↑ **Parent:** [Photodetector](photodetector.md)

An analogue-to-digital unit is a unit of a digitized [photodetector](photodetector.md) output after electronic conversion. Its relation to collected [Electrons](electron.md) is set by the [detector conversion gain](detector-conversion-gain.md); an additive electronic offset must be removed before interpreting a count as a [light](light.md) signal.
