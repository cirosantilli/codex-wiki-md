# Joule heating

↑ **Parent:** [Ohm's law](ohm-s-law.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joule_heating)

[Joule heating](joule-heating.md) is the dissipative conversion of electrical [work](work.md) into [internal energy](internal-energy-split.md) in an [electrical conductor](electrical-conductor.md). In a metal, the [electric field](electric-field-split.md) supplies [energy](energy-split.md) to [charge carriers](charge-carrier.md), which transfer it to the material. This makes an immersed [resistor](resistor.md) a useful calibrated heater and makes [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) a source of losses in wiring. Wind-driven [electric currents](electric-current.md) are a proposed source of [heating of an inflated giant planet](heating-of-an-inflated-giant-planet.md).

For a stationary, purely resistive [Ohmic conductor](ohmic-conductor.md), use the [passive sign convention](passive-sign-convention.md): $V$ is the terminal [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) from the entry to the exit of positive [electric current](electric-current.md) $I$. The absorbed electrical [power](power.md) and [Ohm's law](ohm-s-law.md) give

$$
P=VI=I^2R=\frac{V^2}{R}\geq0,\qquad R>0.
$$

The last two expressions assume $V=IR$ at the relevant material [temperature](temperature-split.md). For constant [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) and [electric current](electric-current.md) over a duration $\Delta t$, the dissipated [energy](energy-split.md) is $E_{\rm diss}=I^2R\Delta t$; otherwise use $E_{\rm diss}=\int V(t)I(t)\,dt$. In [SI](international-system-of-units.md), $\mathrm{V\,A}=\mathrm W=\mathrm{J/s}$.[§9.5, Power Dissipated by a Resistor and Summary of Relationships: combining terminal power with Ohm's law](university-physics-volume-2-by-samuel-j-ling-and-others.md)

Absorbed electrical [power](power.md) $VI$ is more general than [Joule heating](joule-heating.md): a [capacitor](capacitor.md) can store [energy](energy-split.md), a [battery](electric-battery.md) can change its chemical [energy](energy-split.md), and an [electrical circuit](electrical-circuit-split.md) can deliver mechanical [work](work.md) or emit [light](light.md). Even a heated [resistor](resistor.md) need not show an indefinitely rising [temperature](temperature-split.md): in a thermal [steady state](steady-state.md), its dissipated [power](power.md) leaves by [heat transfer](heat-transfer.md). With the heater inside a [calorimeter](calorimeter.md) boundary, electrical input crosses as [work](work.md); the subsequent [heat transfer](heat-transfer.md) to the room is a different term in the [first law of thermodynamics](first-law-of-thermodynamics-split.md).
