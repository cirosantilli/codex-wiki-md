# Joule heating

↑ **Parent:** [Ohm's law](ohm-s-law.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joule_heating)

Joule heating converts electrical work into internal [energy](energy.md). A resistor dissipates $P=IV=I^2R$; in an ohmic continuum the local rate per volume is $\mathbf j\cdot\mathbf E=\eta j^2$, with resistivity $\eta$. Wind-driven currents are a proposed source of [heating of an inflated giant planet](heating-of-an-inflated-giant-planet.md).
