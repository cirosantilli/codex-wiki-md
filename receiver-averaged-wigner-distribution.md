# Receiver-averaged Wigner distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Wigner distribution](wigner-distribution.md)

For receiver-dependent [Green functions](green-s-function.md) $g_{\boldsymbol\zeta}(\mathbf z)$ and [aperture](aperture.md) weight $a(\boldsymbol\zeta)$, define $\mathcal W_A(\mathbf Z,\mathbf p)=\int a(\boldsymbol\zeta)W[g_{\boldsymbol\zeta},g_{\boldsymbol\zeta}](\mathbf Z,\mathbf p)\,d\boldsymbol\zeta$. Its inverse separation [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) is the kernel of the [time reversal operator](time-reversal-operator.md) on the source plane.
