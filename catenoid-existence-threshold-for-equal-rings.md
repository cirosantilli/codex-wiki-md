# Catenoid existence threshold for equal rings

↑ **Parent:** [Catenoid](catenoid.md)

An axisymmetric smooth [connected](connected-space.md) soap film spanning equal radius-$R$ rings at heights $\pm H$ has stationary profile $r=k^{-1}\cosh(kz)$, $k>0$. The boundary equation is $R/H=\cosh u/u$, $u=kH$. Its unique minimum occurs at $u\tanh u=1$, because the [derivative](derivative.md) numerator is $u\sinh u-\cosh u$. If $A$ is this root, the minimum is $\cosh A/A=\sinh A$. There are no catenoids below that ratio, one at equality, and two above it. Existence of stationary [connected](connected-space.md) films does not assert global minimality against pinching or disconnected competitors.
