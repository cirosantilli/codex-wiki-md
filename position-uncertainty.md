# Position uncertainty

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum uncertainty](quantum-uncertainty.md)

The position uncertainty of a [quantum state](quantum-state.md) is the [standard deviation](standard-deviation.md) of its measured [position](position.md), provided the second moment is finite. It is computed from the [position operator](position-operator.md) with the [Born rule](born-rule.md). An oscillator [coherent state](coherent-state.md) has $\Delta x=\sqrt{\hbar/(2m\omega)}$ independently of its mean [position](position.md); translating the state changes the mean without changing this [variance](variance-split.md).
