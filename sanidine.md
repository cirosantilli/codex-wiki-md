# Sanidine

↑ **Parent:** [Silicate mineral](silicate-mineral.md)

A high-temperature potassium feldspar [mineral](mineral.md), with idealized composition $\mathrm{KAlSi_3O_8}$. Substitution on its K and Al sites changes the [thermodynamic activity](thermodynamic-activity.md) of that [mineral endmember](mineral-endmember.md) and therefore the [chemical equilibrium](chemical-equilibrium.md) of reactions involving it.
