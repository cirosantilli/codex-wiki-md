# Length spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic surface](hyperbolic-surface.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Length_spectrum)

The unmarked length spectrum of a compact [hyperbolic surface](hyperbolic-surface.md) is the multiset of lengths of its closed [geodesics](geodesic.md), with a consistent convention for primitive curves and orientation. The marked version labels lengths by free homotopy classes. The [Selberg trace formula](selberg-trace-formula.md) relates the unmarked multiset to the [Laplace-Beltrami operator](laplace-beltrami-operator.md) [spectrum](spectrum-functional-analysis.md).
