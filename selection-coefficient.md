# Selection coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Population genetics](population-genetics-split.md)

A parameter measuring a relative reproductive advantage or disadvantage under a specified convention. For a [haploid](haploid.md) variant with relative [genotype fitness](genotype-fitness.md) $1+s$, positive $s$ is advantageous; other conventions require translation before comparison.
