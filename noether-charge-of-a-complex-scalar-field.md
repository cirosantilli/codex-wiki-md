# Noether charge of a complex scalar field

↑ **Parent:** [Global phase symmetry of a complex scalar field](global-phase-symmetry-of-a-complex-scalar-field.md)

For the [global phase symmetry of a complex scalar field](global-phase-symmetry-of-a-complex-scalar-field.md) oriented as $\delta\psi=-i\varepsilon\psi$, the [Noether current](noether-current.md) is $j^\mu=i(\psi^*\partial^\mu\psi-\psi\partial^\mu\psi^*)$. The [Euler-Lagrange field equations](euler-lagrange-field-equation.md) $\Box\psi+V'(|\psi|^2)\psi=0$ and its [complex conjugate](complex-conjugate.md) give $\partial_\mu j^\mu=i(\psi^*\Box\psi-\psi\Box\psi^*)=0$ for a real differentiable potential. Integrating $j^0$ gives $Q$ when boundary flux vanishes. A charged [scalar](scalar.md) coupled to electromagnetism has [electric charge](electric-charge.md) $qQ$ with the chosen charge normalization; without that physical identification it is an internal charge.
