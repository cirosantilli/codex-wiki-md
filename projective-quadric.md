# Projective quadric

↑ **Parent:** [Quadric surface](quadric-surface.md)

A projective quadric is the zero set of a nonzero homogeneous [quadratic polynomial](quadratic-polynomial.md) in [projective space](projective-space-split.md). Over a [field](field.md) of [characteristic](characteristic-of-a-field.md) different from two, represent it by a symmetric matrix $B$, up to a nonzero scalar. The gradient at a representative $v$ is $2Bv$, so the singular locus is the projectivization of $\ker B$. Thus the quadric is nonsingular exactly when $B$ is invertible. In the complex projective plane, ranks three, two, and one give respectively a smooth [projective conic](projective-conic.md), two distinct [projective lines](projective-line.md), and a double [projective line](projective-line.md).
