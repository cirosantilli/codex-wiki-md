# Fully faithful adjoint criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Unit and counit of an adjunction](unit-and-counit-of-an-adjunction.md)

For $F\dashv G$, the right adjoint $G$ is [full and faithful](full-and-faithful-functor.md) exactly when the counit $FG\to1$ is an isomorphism. Dually, $F$ is full and faithful exactly when the unit $1\to GF$ is an isomorphism.
