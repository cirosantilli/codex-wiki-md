# Completeness of the Fourier sine basis

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier sine basis](fourier-sine-basis.md)

The normalized functions $\sqrt{2/L}\sin(n\pi x/L)$, $n\geq1$, have [completeness of an orthonormal system](completeness-of-an-orthonormal-system.md) in [L2 space](l2-space-is-a-hilbert-space.md) on $(0,L)$. If a function is orthogonal to every sine, extend it oddly to $(-L,L)$. All its [Fourier coefficients](fourier-coefficient.md) vanish: the cosine and constant ones by oddness, and the sine ones by assumption. Completeness of the [Fourier basis](fourier-basis.md) makes the extension zero almost everywhere. This proves the required dense-span property.
