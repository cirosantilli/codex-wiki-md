# Wiener theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Wiener measure](wiener-measure.md)

There exists a [probability measure](probability-measure.md) on $C_0([0,\infty),\mathbb R)$ under which the coordinate process is standard [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md), and its distribution there is unique. Construct consistent centered [Gaussian](normal-distribution.md) finite-dimensional laws with [covariance](covariance.md) $\min(s,t)$ and use the [Kolmogorov extension theorem](kolmogorov-extension-theorem.md). The fourth increment moment $3|t-s|^2$ supplies a continuous modification by the [Kolmogorov continuity theorem](kolmogorov-continuity-theorem.md). Its [independent increments](independent-increments.md) and [normal](normal-distribution.md) increment distributions are unchanged. Uniqueness follows because coordinate evaluations at rational times generate the [Borel sigma-algebra](borel-sigma-algebra.md) of continuous-path space.
