# Middle-down proteomics

↑ **Parent:** [Proteomics](proteomics.md)

Analysis of relatively large [protein](protein-split.md) fragments to preserve linkage between sequence changes or modifications that separate into different [peptides](peptide.md) in [bottom-up proteomics](bottom-up-proteomics.md). It generally preserves less complete linkage than intact-chain analysis.
