# Covalent two-site singlet

↑ **Parent:** [Two-site Hubbard dimer](two-site-hubbard-dimer.md)

For orthonormal orbitals $a,b$, the symmetric spatial state $C=(|ab\rangle+|ba\rangle)/\sqrt2$ multiplied by the antisymmetric [spin](spin.md) singlet has one [electron](electron.md) on each center and no [double occupancy](double-occupancy.md). The full state remains antisymmetric under [fermion](fermion.md) exchange.
