# Commute time identity

↑ **Parent:** [Effective resistance](effective-resistance.md)

For a random walk on a finite [electrical network](electrical-network.md),

$$
\mathbb E_aT_z+\mathbb E_zT_a
=2\left(\sum_{e\in E}c(e)\right)R_{\mathrm{eff}}(a,z).
$$
