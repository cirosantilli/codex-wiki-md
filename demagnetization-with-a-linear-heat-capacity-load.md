# Demagnetization with a linear-heat-capacity load

↑ **Parent:** [Adiabatic demagnetization](adiabatic-demagnetization.md)

Let $N$ independent spin-$J$ moments and a load with [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) $C_L=Mk_BT/\theta$ remain in equilibrium during reversible isolated demagnetization. Then $S_L(T)=S_L(0)+Mk_BT/\theta$. Fully removing the field raises each spin [entropy](entropy-split.md) by its initial [magnetic entropy deficit](magnetic-entropy-deficit.md). Total [entropy](entropy-split.md) conservation gives $N\Delta s_{\rm mag}=Mk_B(T_i-T_f)/\theta$, yielding the title result. The formula assumes positive final [temperature](temperature-split.md), validity of the load law throughout the interval, negligible additional [entropy](entropy-split.md) contributions and an attainable equilibrated low-field endpoint. Interactions require an actual many-body [entropy](entropy-split.md) instead of the independent-spin expression.
