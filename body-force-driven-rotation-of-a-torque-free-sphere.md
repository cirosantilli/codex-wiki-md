# Body-force-driven rotation of a torque-free sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Lorentz reciprocal theorem for Stokes flow](lorentz-reciprocal-theorem-for-stokes-flow.md)

Use a [rotlet](rotlet.md) as the auxiliary [Stokes flow](stokes-flow-split.md), with a rigid sphere at its center. Its exterior-domain boundary torque is the imposed auxiliary couple. In the [Lorentz reciprocal theorem](lorentz-reciprocal-theorem-for-stokes-flow.md), the actual sphere's zero torque removes the other surface term, while the volume term is the rotlet dotted with the [body force](body-force.md). Varying the auxiliary couple gives the displayed angular velocity, assuming the integrals and the far boundary limit converge.
