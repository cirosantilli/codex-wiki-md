# Outside-out patch

↑ **Parent:** [Patch clamp](patch-clamp.md)

An [outside-out patch](outside-out-patch.md) is an excised membrane patch whose extracellular side faces the bath. It can form on withdrawal after [whole-cell recording](whole-cell-recording.md), with the intracellular face exposed to pipette solution. The orientation determines which side a bath-applied substance reaches.
