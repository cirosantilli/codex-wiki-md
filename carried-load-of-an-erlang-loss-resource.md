# Carried load of an Erlang loss resource

↑ **Parent:** [Erlang loss formula](erlang-loss-formula.md)

For offered load $a$ and capacity $C\geq1$, the carried load equals the mean occupancy:

$$
m_C(a)=a[1-E(a,C)].
$$

Differentiating the truncated-Poisson equilibrium weights gives $m_C'(a)=\operatorname{Var}_a(N)/a>0$ for $a>0$. The carried load increases from zero to $C$. This monotonicity supplies the strictly increasing term in the [convex potential for the Erlang fixed point](convex-potential-for-the-erlang-fixed-point.md).
