<h1 id="pelczynski-decomposition-method">Pełczyński decomposition method</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Complemented subspace](complemented-subspace.md)

For a [Banach space](banach-space-split.md) $E$ satisfying $E\cong(\bigoplus_{n\ge1}E)_p$, a [complemented subspace](complemented-subspace.md) $W$ is absorbed by $E$: write $E\cong W\oplus V$, expand the countable sum, and absorb one additional copy of $W$ into the countably many $W$ summands. Hence $E\oplus W\cong E$. If $Z$ is complemented in $E$ and contains a complemented copy of $E$, write $Z\cong E\oplus W$ with $W$ complemented in $E$; the preceding argument gives $Z\cong E$. The decompositions are [Banach space isomorphisms](banach-space-isomorphism.md), not merely algebraic decompositions.
