# Horizonless static vacuum rigidity

↑ **Parent:** [Static vacuum conformal spatial metric](static-vacuum-conformal-spatial-metric.md)

On a complete nonsingular spatial quotient with no inner boundary and standard [asymptotic flatness](asymptotically-flat-spacetime.md), a bounded [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md) tending to zero on all ends is zero. The [static vacuum conformal spatial metric](static-vacuum-conformal-spatial-metric.md) is then [Ricci flat](ricci-flat-manifold.md); [three-dimensional curvature from the Ricci tensor](three-dimensional-curvature-from-the-ricci-tensor.md) makes it flat. Completeness and an ordinary Euclidean asymptotic end exclude nontrivial flat quotients, yielding [Minkowski spacetime](minkowski-spacetime.md) with the standard global time coordinate.
