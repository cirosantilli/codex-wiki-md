# Covariate

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical model](statistical-model-split.md)

A covariate is an observed explanatory variable used to model the [conditional distribution](conditional-distribution.md) of a response. A vector of covariates can enter a [linear predictor](linear-predictor.md), a treatment-effect adjustment or a [hazard multiplier](hazard-multiplier.md). Conditioning on a covariate does not itself establish that its effect is causal.
