# Absolute value of an operator

↑ **Parent:** [Polar decomposition of a bounded operator](polar-decomposition-of-a-bounded-operator.md)

The absolute value of a [linear operator](linear-operator.md) $L$ is the [positive square root of an operator](positive-square-root-of-an-operator.md) $L^\dagger L$. Its [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are the [singular values](singular-value.md) of $L$, its [trace](matrix-trace.md) is the [trace norm](trace-norm.md) of $L$, and it appears in the [polar decomposition of a bounded operator](polar-decomposition-of-a-bounded-operator.md) $L=U|L|$.
