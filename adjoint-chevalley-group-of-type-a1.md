# Adjoint Chevalley group of type A1

↑ **Parent:** [Adjoint Chevalley group](adjoint-chevalley-group.md)

On the [sl2 Lie algebra](sl2-lie-algebra.md) with basis $e=E_{12},h=E_{11}-E_{22},f=E_{21}$, the integral adjoint root polynomials are

$$
\begin{aligned}
x_+(t)e&=e,&x_+(t)h&=h-2te,&x_+(t)f&=f+th-t^2e,\\
x_-(s)f&=f,&x_-(s)h&=h+2sf,&x_-(s)e&=e-sh-s^2f.
\end{aligned}
$$

These are conjugation by the upper and lower elementary matrices of the [special linear group](special-linear-group.md). Those matrices generate $SL_2(K)$, and a matrix commuting with both $E_{12}$ and $E_{21}$ is scalar. Thus the kernel is precisely $\{aI:a^2=1\}$ over every field, even in characteristic two, and the image is the displayed quotient.
