# Coherent-state electric-field variance

↑ **Parent:** [Optical quadrature](optical-quadrature.md)

For one optical mode, $E=E_0(a+a^\dagger)$ and $[a,a^\dagger]=1$. A [coherent state](coherent-state.md) obeys $a|\alpha\rangle=\alpha|\alpha\rangle$, so [normal ordering](normal-ordering.md) gives $\langle E^2\rangle=E_0^2[(\alpha+\alpha^*)^2+1]$. Subtracting the squared mean leaves $E_0^2$, independent of the coherent displacement. The extra one is the [canonical commutation relation](canonical-commutation-relation.md), not noise from a random coherent amplitude. A rotated [optical quadrature](optical-quadrature.md) has the same coherent-state variance with the same amplitude normalization.
