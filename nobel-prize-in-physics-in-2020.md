# Nobel Prize in Physics in 2020

↑ **Parent:** [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics-split.md)

One half went to [Roger Penrose](roger-penrose.md) for showing that [black hole](black-hole-split.md) formation is a robust prediction of [general relativity](general-relativity-split.md). One quarter each went to [Reinhard Genzel](reinhard-genzel.md) and [Andrea Ghez](andrea-m-ghez.md) for discovering a supermassive compact object at the centre of the [Milky Way](milky-way.md).[Individual official summary: Penrose one half for robust relativistic black-hole formation; Genzel and Ghez one quarter each for the Galactic-centre compact object.](the-nobel-prize-in-physics-2020-by-nobel-prize-outreach.md)
