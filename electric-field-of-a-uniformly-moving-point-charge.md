# Electric field of a uniformly moving point charge

↑ **Parent:** [Liénard-Wiechert field](lienard-wiechert-field.md)

Let $\mathbf R$ join the instantaneous position of charge q to the field point, with constant $\boldsymbol\beta=\mathbf v/c$. Transforming its rest-frame Coulomb field gives

$$
\mathbf E=\frac{q(1-\beta^2)\mathbf R}{4\pi\epsilon_0[R^2-|\boldsymbol\beta\times\mathbf R|^2]^{3/2}},\qquad
\mathbf B=\mathbf v\times\mathbf E/c^2.
$$

The [electric field](electric-field-split.md) is radial from the current position, reduced along the [velocity](velocity.md) and enhanced transversely. This present-position form is compatible with the causal retarded solution specifically because motion is uniform. There is no acceleration-dependent radiation field, although the moving charge has a [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) and transports electromagnetic [energy](energy-split.md).
