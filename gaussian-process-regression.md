# Gaussian process regression

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian process](gaussian-process-split.md)

Gaussian process regression estimates an unknown [function](function-split.md) by assigning its values a [Gaussian process](gaussian-process-split.md) law, then conditioning on observed values, often with independent [Gaussian noise](gaussian-noise.md). The [covariance function](covariance-function.md) expresses the proposed relation between nearby inputs; the [conditional multivariate normal distribution](conditional-multivariate-normal-distribution.md) gives the resulting [Gaussian process regression posterior](gaussian-process-regression-posterior.md). Its conditional [mean](expected-value-split.md) provides a prediction and its conditional [variance](variance-split.md) quantifies uncertainty under the chosen model. The [index set](index-set.md) can represent spatial locations or other inputs as well as time.
