# Nonsymmetric matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix](matrix.md)

A nonsymmetric matrix is a square [matrix](matrix.md) unequal to its [transpose](transpose.md). Its right [eigenvectors](eigenvector.md) need not be [orthogonal](orthogonal-vectors.md), and it need not be a [diagonalizable matrix](diagonalizable-matrix.md). For instance, $A=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&2\end{pmatrix}$ has [eigenvectors](eigenvector.md) $(1,0)^T$ and $(1,1)^T$ for its distinct [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md). A starting [vector](vector.md) $(1,0)^T$ has nonzero [inner product](inner-product-split.md) with $(1,1)^T$ but zero coefficient in that direction in the [eigenbasis](eigenbasis.md). This is why [power iteration](power-iteration.md) and [inverse iteration](inverse-iteration.md) require a nonzero spectral component rather than an ordinary nonorthogonality test in the general case.
