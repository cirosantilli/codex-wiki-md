# Discrete mathematics, probability and continuous mathematics examination paper by the University of Oxford (2013)

↑ **Parent:** [Computer science bibliography](computer-science-bibliography-split.md)

The nine-page [University of Oxford](university-of-oxford-split.md) Computer Science Prelims paper dated 20 June 2013. The public [released examination archive](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/past_exam_papers_for_1st_year_of/response/1398416/attach/2/Fakour%20Zip%20Final%201.zip?cookie_passthrough=1) contains the original scan as `4223-1.pdf`. Printed pages 2–4 cover [discrete mathematics](discrete-mathematics.md), pages 5–6 cover [calculus](calculus-split.md) and [ordinary differential equations](ordinary-differential-equation.md), and pages 7–9 cover [probability theory](probability-theory-split.md). Question 8(c)(i) introduces the two-roll model without asking for a separate calculation; Question 8(c)(v) allows the trivial independence of an impossible [event](event.md) unless the values are restricted to the two marginal supports.
