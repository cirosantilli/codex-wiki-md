# Thermal covariance of an elastic filament

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic filament](elastic-filament.md)

For a positive quadratic bending operator $K$ with a real [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) of [eigenfunctions](eigenfunction.md), the [equipartition theorem](equipartition-theorem.md) gives $C(x,y)=k_BT\sum_nW_n(x)W_n(y)/\mu_n$, where $KW_n=\mu_nW_n$. This is the inverse-operator [Green function](green-s-function.md) multiplied by [Boltzmann constant](boltzmann-constant.md) and [temperature](temperature.md). Unconstrained [zero-energy filament modes](zero-energy-filament-mode.md) prevent a normalizable [canonical ensemble](canonical-ensemble.md), and negative modes signal an unstable quadratic model.

For both ends clamped and $K=A\partial_x^4$, the diagonal is $C(x,x)=k_BT x^3(L-x)^3/(3AL^3)$. This differs from a clamped-free tip [variance](variance-split.md). The center [variance](variance-split.md) is $k_BT L^3/(192A)$.
