# Tau antineutrino

↑ **Parent:** [Tau neutrino](tau-neutrino.md)

The [antiparticle](antiparticle.md) associated with the tau-flavour [neutrino](neutrino.md). A [tau lepton](tau-particle.md) of positive charge emits it in an ordinary [charged current](charged-current.md) decay; a negative [tau lepton](tau-particle.md) instead emits a [tau neutrino](tau-neutrino.md).
