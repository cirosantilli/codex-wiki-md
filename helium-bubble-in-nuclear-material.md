# Helium bubble in nuclear material

↑ **Parent:** [Helium accumulation in nuclear materials](helium-accumulation-in-nuclear-materials.md)

A helium-containing cavity in an irradiated material. [helium](helium.md) helps stabilise small cavities and can accumulate at [grain boundaries](grain-boundary.md), contributing to embrittlement.
