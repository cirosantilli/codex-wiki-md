# Filter gain

↑ **Parent:** [Linear filter of a stationary time series](linear-filter-of-a-stationary-time-series.md)

For an absolutely summable [linear filter of a stationary time series](linear-filter-of-a-stationary-time-series.md), the [filter gain](filter-gain.md) is the modulus of its frequency response $a(\lambda)=\sum_r a_re^{ir\lambda}$. It multiplies a sinusoidal amplitude, while its square multiplies the [spectral density of a stationary process](spectral-density-of-a-stationary-process.md). A zero removes the corresponding frequency; the [filter gain](filter-gain.md) of a composition is the product of the two [filter gains](filter-gain.md).
