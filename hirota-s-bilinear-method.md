<h1 id="hirota-s-bilinear-method">Hirota's bilinear method</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Integrable systems](integrable-systems-split.md)

An exact-solution method that transforms a nonlinear [partial differential equation](partial-differential-equation-split.md) into equations bilinear in auxiliary [Hirota tau functions](hirota-tau-function.md). Finite exponential expansions of the [Hirota tau functions](hirota-tau-function.md) produce [soliton](soliton.md) families. The auxiliary exponential coefficients need not all be positive.
