# Lower ramification filtration of a prime-power cyclotomic extension

↑ **Parent:** [Ramification group](ramification-group.md)

For $L=\mathbb Q_p(\zeta_{p^n})$, the [uniformiser](uniformizer.md) $\zeta_{p^n}-1$ and the [uniformizer criterion for lower ramification groups](uniformizer-criterion-for-lower-ramification-groups.md) give the displayed [valuation](valuation.md) for $a\ne1$. Thus $G_0$ is the full unit group modulo $p^n$, and $G_i$ consists of units congruent to one modulo $p^j$ when $p^{j-1}\leq i\leq p^j-1$, $1\leq j<n$. All groups are trivial for $i\geq p^{n-1}$. The formula includes $p=2$, where the first congruence subgroup is already the full group.
