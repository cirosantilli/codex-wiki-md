# Incidence pseudoinverse decomposition

↑ **Parent:** [Oriented incidence matrix](oriented-incidence-matrix.md)

For a [connected graph](connected-graph.md), the [Moore-Penrose inverse](moore-penrose-inverse.md) of its [oriented incidence matrix](oriented-incidence-matrix.md) satisfies $D^\dagger D=I-\Pi_1$, because this product is the [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md) onto $(\ker D)^\perp$. Thus every signal decomposes into its constant mean and $D^\dagger D\theta$. Writing $L=\max_e\|(D^\dagger)_{\cdot,e}\|_2$, a [sub-Gaussian random vector](sub-gaussian-random-vector.md) noise gives a simultaneous bound on $\|(D^\dagger)^\top z\|_\infty$ with scale $L\sqrt{\log(m/\delta)}$.
