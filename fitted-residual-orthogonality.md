# Fitted-residual orthogonality

↑ **Parent:** [Hat matrix](hat-matrix.md)

For a full-rank [ordinary least squares](ordinary-least-squares.md) [design matrix](design-matrix.md), the [hat matrix](hat-matrix.md) $H$ and residual projection $G=I-H$ satisfy $HG=GH=0$. Hence [fitted values](fitted-values.md) and [regression residuals](regression-residual.md) are perpendicular as observed vectors. Under isotropic errors their cross-[covariance matrix](covariance-matrix.md) is zero, and under a [normal linear model](normal-linear-model.md) the two vectors are independent.
