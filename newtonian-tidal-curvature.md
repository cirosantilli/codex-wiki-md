# Newtonian tidal curvature

↑ **Parent:** [Newton–Cartan gravitational connection](newton-cartan-gravitational-connection.md)

Using $[\nabla_c,\nabla_d]V^a=R^a{}_{bcd}V^b$, the [Newton–Cartan gravitational connection](newton-cartan-gravitational-connection.md) has only the displayed Hessian components and their antisymmetric partners in the last two indices. The [geodesic deviation](geodesic-deviation.md) equation gives relative acceleration $-\partial_i\partial_j\Phi\,\delta x^j$. The [Poisson equation for Newtonian gravity](poisson-equation-for-newtonian-gravity.md) is therefore $R_{ab}=4\pi G\rho\,(dt)_a(dt)_b$. A spatially uniform gravitational acceleration has zero tidal curvature even if the connection coefficients do not vanish in the original coordinates.
