# Maximum entropy on a finite alphabet

↑ **Parent:** [Maximum entropy distribution on a finite set](maximum-entropy-distribution-on-a-finite-set.md)

An [information entropy](information-entropy.md) on at most $m$ outcomes is at most $\log_2m$, with equality for the uniform distribution on all $m$ outcomes. For the uniform reference $u$, the [Kullback-Leibler divergence](kullback-leibler-divergence.md) is $D(p\|u)=\log_2m-H(p)\geq0$, proving the bound.
