# Standard model (set theory)

↑ **Parent:** [Set theory](set-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standard_model_(set_theory))

A standard model of [set theory](set-theory-split.md) interprets membership by the ambient membership relation restricted to its domain. A [transitive model](transitive-model.md) additionally has a [transitive set](transitive-set.md) or class as its domain. Standardness alone does not impose transitivity.
