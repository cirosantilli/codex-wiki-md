# Kramers-Kronig relations

↑ **Parent:** [Linear response theory](linear-response-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kramers–Kronig_relations)

Causality constrains the real and imaginary parts of a frequency-dependent [linear response](linear-response-theory.md). For a dielectric with $\epsilon_r(\omega)\to1$ at large frequency, the $e^{-i\omega t}$ convention gives

$$
\epsilon_r'(\omega)-1=\frac2\pi\operatorname{P}\int_0^\infty\frac{\omega'\epsilon_r''(\omega')}{\omega'^2-\omega^2}\,d\omega'.
$$

The integral is a [Cauchy principal value](cauchy-principal-value.md). Absorption and [optical dispersion](dispersion-optics.md) therefore cannot be specified independently.
