# Exterior product

↑ **Parent:** [Exterior algebra](exterior-algebra.md)

The exterior product is the multiplication $\bigwedge^pV\otimes\bigwedge^qV\to\bigwedge^{p+q}V$ of the [exterior algebra](exterior-algebra.md). For [homogeneous elements of a graded algebra](homogeneous-element-of-a-graded-algebra.md), $u\wedge v=(-1)^{pq}v\wedge u$.
