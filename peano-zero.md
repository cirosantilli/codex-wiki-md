# Peano zero

↑ **Parent:** [Taylor expansion](taylor-expansion.md)

A Peano zero through order $r$ means that the [function](function-split.md) admits the zero [Taylor polynomial](taylor-polynomial.md) with remainder $o(|t|^r)$ at $x_0$. This does not assert the existence of ordinary iterated [derivatives](derivative.md) on a neighborhood. For example, multiplying $t^{r+1}$ by a bounded [continuous function](continuous-function.md) with [dense](dense-set.md) points of nondifferentiability preserves this estimate while destroying neighborhood [differentiability](differentiability.md).
