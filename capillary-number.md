# Capillary number

↑ **Parent:** [Surface tension](surface-tension.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capillary_number)

The capillary number compares viscous stresses with [surface tension](surface-tension.md). Its velocity and viscosity conventions must be specified; a porous-medium displacement can use the [Darcy velocity](darcy-velocity.md) and the viscosity of the displaced fluid.
