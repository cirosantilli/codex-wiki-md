# Right-acting BRST differential

↑ **Parent:** [BRST symmetry](brst-symmetry.md)

A [right-acting BRST differential](right-acting-brst-differential.md) obeys the right [graded Leibniz rule](graded-leibniz-rule.md). This convention permits $Q\phi=[c,\phi]$ together with $Qc=-[c,c]/2$. Using a left [graded Leibniz rule](graded-leibniz-rule.md) with those same two signs does not give a [nilpotent operator](nilpotent-linear-map.md); one of the signs must change.
