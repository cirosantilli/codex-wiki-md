# Inductive oscillation of a sliding rod on inclined rails

↑ **Parent:** [Motional electromotive force](motional-electromotive-force.md)

A vertical field linking a conducting loop on an incline contributes flux $Bl\cos\theta\,s$. For zero resistance and zero initial current, flux conservation gives $LI=Bl\cos\theta\,s$. The uphill magnetic [force](force-split.md) component then makes the downhill coordinate oscillate about $s_e=mgL\sin\theta/(B^2l^2\cos^2\theta)$. Release from $s=0$ first stops at $2s_e$, not at the force-free point $s_e$. The [energy](energy-split.md) is stored in the inductor, not dissipated.
