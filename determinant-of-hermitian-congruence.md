# Determinant of Hermitian congruence

↑ **Parent:** [Hermitian operator](hermitian-operator.md)

For a complex $n\times n$ matrix $B$, the real [linear map](linear-map.md) $A\mapsto BAB^*$ on the $n^2$-dimensional real [vector space](vector-space-split.md) of [Hermitian matrices](hermitian-operator.md) has [determinant](determinant.md) $|\det B|^{2n}$. A real [basis](basis.md) of [Hermitian matrices](hermitian-operator.md) is also a complex [basis](basis.md) of all matrices, so the matrix of this restriction equals the matrix of the complex-linear congruence map. Left and right multiplication separately have [determinants](determinant.md) $(\det B)^n$ and $(\overline{\det B})^n$.
