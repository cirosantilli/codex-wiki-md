# History of Euler characteristic

↑ **Parent:** [Euler characteristic](euler-characteristic.md)

[Leonhard Euler](leonhard-euler.md)'s Elementa doctrinae solidorum, or Elements of the doctrine of solids, appeared in 1758 in volume 4 of Novi Commentarii academiae scientiarum Petropolitanae, pp. 109–140. The repository record dates its composition to 1750. Composition and publication are distinct dates here.[Repository record for E230: author, English and Latin titles, written date 1750, published date 1758 and original journal citation](elementa-doctrinae-solidorum-by-leonhard-euler.md)

The modern [cell-count formula for Euler characteristic](cell-count-formula-for-euler-characteristic.md) puts polyhedral counting into an arbitrary-dimensional [CW complex](cw-complex.md). It also identifies what is being counted: the [Euler characteristic of a cube boundary and its interior](euler-characteristic-of-a-cube-boundary-and-its-interior.md) distinguishes a polyhedral surface from the filled solid using the missing three-dimensional term. The modern [homology](homology-split.md) formulation explains why this alternating arithmetic persists across different decompositions.[Algebraic Topology by Allen Hatcher, §2.2, printed pp. 146–147, Theorem 2.44: alternating cell counts, homology ranks and independence from the CW structure](algebraic-topology-by-allen-hatcher.md)
