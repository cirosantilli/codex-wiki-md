# Minigene splicing reporter

↑ **Parent:** [Biology laboratory technique](biology-laboratory-technique.md)

An engineered transcript containing a selected [RNA splicing](rna-splicing.md) region in a controlled expression context. Mutating a candidate [cis-regulatory element](cis-regulatory-element.md) tests local function, but an isolated construct can omit distant regulation or native [chromatin](chromatin.md) context.
