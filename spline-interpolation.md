# Spline interpolation

↑ **Parent:** [Spline approximation](spline-approximation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spline_interpolation)

Spline interpolation matches prescribed function values at distinct sites by an element of a finite-dimensional spline space. In a [B-spline](b-spline.md) basis its existence and uniqueness for all data are equivalent to invertibility of the [B-spline collocation matrix](b-spline-collocation-matrix.md). Distinct ordered sites satisfying the [Schoenberg–Whitney theorem](schoenberg-whitney-theorem.md) give this condition.
