# Real conic without real rational points

↑ **Parent:** [Projective plane curve](projective-plane-curve.md)

This smooth [projective plane curve](projective-plane-curve.md) has no real [rational points](rational-point.md): a sum of three real squares is zero only when all three coordinates vanish, which is forbidden in [projective space](projective-space-split.md). It is therefore not isomorphic over $\mathbb R$ to the [projective line](projective-line.md), which has rational points. Over $\mathbb C$ it becomes a nonsingular conic isomorphic to a projective line. Thus the ground field changes whether a geometric genus-zero curve admits a projective-line parametrization.
