# Steenrod algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Stable cohomology operation](stable-cohomology-operation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steenrod_algebra)

The Steenrod algebra organizes stable mod-$p$ [cohomology operations](cohomology-operation.md). At $p=2$ it is generated by the [Steenrod squares](steenrod-square.md); at an odd prime it is generated by the [Steenrod reduced powers](steenrod-reduced-power.md) and [Bockstein homomorphism](bockstein-homomorphism.md). Cohomology is a module over this algebra, and every continuous map induces a homomorphism preserving its action.
