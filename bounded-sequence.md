# Bounded sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Sequence](sequence.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bounded_sequence)

A sequence $(x_n)$ in a metric space is bounded when all its terms lie in some ball of finite radius.
