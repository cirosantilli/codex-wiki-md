# Orientation-map self-organization

↑ **Parent:** [Orientation selectivity](orientation-selectivity.md)

Correlation-based learning among spatially arranged ON and OFF inputs, with [synaptic competition](synaptic-competition.md) and lateral interactions, can select elongated [simple cell](simple-cell.md) receptive fields. Neighbor interactions coordinate preferred orientations across cortex, while competition allows different orientations to persist. Selectivity within one cell and a spatial map across many cells are separate predictions, and neither follows from unbounded [Hebbian learning](hebbian-learning.md) alone.
