# Periodic electrostatic Green function with one compact dimension

↑ **Parent:** [Kaluza-Klein theory](kaluza-klein-theory.md)

For the [Poisson equation](poisson-equation.md) $\nabla_4^2\phi=-4\pi Rq\,\delta^3(\mathbf x)\delta_{2\pi R}(z)$ on $\mathbb R^3\times S^1$, expand the periodic delta as $(2\pi R)^{-1}\sum_ne^{inz/R}$. The $n$th [Kaluza-Klein mode](kaluza-klein-mode.md) has radial potential $q e^{-|n|r/R}/(2\pi r)$, since $(\nabla_3^2-m^2)e^{-mr}/r=-4\pi\delta^3(\mathbf x)$. Summing the geometric series gives the displayed [Green function](green-s-function.md). At equal compact coordinate it reduces to $q\coth(r/(2R))/(2\pi r)$. Thus the interaction energy changes from $q_1q_2/(2\pi r)$ at large separation to $q_1q_2R/(\pi r^2)$ at small separation, with normalization fixed by the stated Poisson equation.
