# Nonpositively curved cube complex

↑ **Parent:** [Cubical complex](cubical-complex.md)

A cube complex is nonpositively curved when every vertex link is a [flag simplicial complex](clique-complex.md). Equivalently, its universal cover with the piecewise Euclidean metric is a [CAT(0) space](cat-0-space.md).
