# Zooplankton

↑ **Parent:** [Ecosystem](ecosystem.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zooplankton)

[Zooplankton](zooplankton.md) are drifting aquatic [consumers](consumer-food-chain.md), including small [animals](animal.md). In the illustrative [food web](food-web.md), the chosen [zooplankton](zooplankton.md) feed on [algae](algae.md) and serve as [prey](prey.md) for small [fish](fish.md); this assumed diet is not a claim about every kind of [zooplankton](zooplankton.md).
