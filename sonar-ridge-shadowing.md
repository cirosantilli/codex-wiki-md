# Sonar ridge shadowing

↑ **Parent:** [Sea-ice pressure ridge](sea-ice-pressure-ridge.md)

A [sonar](sonar.md) ridge-identification rule can miss a shallow peak near a deeper peak because the intervening valley fails the required separation criterion. This apparent shadowing suppresses short observed spacings even without an acoustic blind zone. Finite beam width and genuine geometric occlusion can impose additional limitations. The resulting minimum resolvable spacing need not be a universal physical exclusion distance.
