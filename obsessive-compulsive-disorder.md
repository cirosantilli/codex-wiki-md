# Obsessive-compulsive disorder

↑ **Parent:** [Mental disorder](mental-disorder.md)

A disorder involving [obsessions](obsession.md), [compulsions](compulsion.md) or both with clinically significant distress, time cost or impairment. [Compulsions](compulsion.md) may be overt or mental. Unwanted thoughts alone are insufficient to establish the disorder; their appraisal and associated responses matter.
