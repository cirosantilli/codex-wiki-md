# Kernel of a sum of positive operators

↑ **Parent:** [Positive operator](positive-operator.md)

For bounded [positive operators](positive-operator.md) $S,T$, membership $x\in\ker(S+T)$ gives $0=\langle Sx,x\rangle+\langle Tx,x\rangle$. Both terms are nonnegative, so both vanish. The [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality for a positive operator](cauchy-schwarz-inequality-for-a-positive-operator.md) makes $Sx=Tx=0$. The reverse inclusion is immediate. Commutativity is not required.
