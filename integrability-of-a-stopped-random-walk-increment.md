# Integrability of a stopped random-walk increment

↑ **Parent:** [Stopping time](stopping-time.md)

Let $X_n$ be integrable [independent and identically distributed random variables](independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md), and let $T\geq1$ be a [stopping time](stopping-time.md) for their [natural filtration](natural-filtration.md), with $\mathbb ET<\infty$. Since $\{T\geq n\}$ is measurable before $X_n$ is observed, [independence](independent-random-variables.md) and the [Tonelli theorem](tonelli-theorem.md) give $\mathbb E|X_T|\leq\sum_n\mathbb E[|X_n|\mathbf1_{\{T\geq n\}}]=\mathbb E|X_1|\mathbb ET$. The event $T=n$ need not be independent of $X_n$, so the selected increment can be biased.
