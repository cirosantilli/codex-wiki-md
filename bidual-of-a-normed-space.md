# Bidual of a normed space

↑ **Parent:** [Dual space](dual-space.md)

The bidual of a [normed vector space](normed-vector-space.md) $X$ is the continuous dual $X^{**}=(X^*)^*$. Evaluation defines the [canonical embedding into the bidual](canonical-embedding-into-the-bidual.md) $J_X(x)(f)=f(x)$. The [norm](norm.md) inequality gives $\|J_Xx\|\le\|x\|$, and a norming functional from the [Hahn-Banach theorem](hahn-banach-theorem.md) gives equality. Thus $J_X$ is isometric, and its image is dense in its closure, a [completion of a normed space](completion-of-a-normed-space.md).
