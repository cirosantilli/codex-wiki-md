# Dwarf nova

↑ **Parent:** [Cataclysmic variable](cataclysmic-variable.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dwarf_nova)

A [cataclysmic variable](cataclysmic-variable.md) with recurrent disk outbursts associated with the [hydrogen-ionization disk instability](hydrogen-ionization-disk-instability.md). Enhanced [accretion](accretion.md) powers the bright phase; the event does not require [thermonuclear runaway](thermonuclear-runaway.md) on the [white dwarf](white-dwarf.md).
