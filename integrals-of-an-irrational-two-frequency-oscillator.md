# Integrals of an irrational two-frequency oscillator

↑ **Parent:** [Integrable Hamiltonian system](integrable-hamiltonian-system.md)

On the regular set of two uncoupled oscillators with irrational frequency ratio, every continuous globally defined [integral of motion](integral-of-motion.md) is constant on each fixed-action [flat torus](flat-torus.md), because every orbit is dense there. A differentiable integral is consequently a function of the two actions, so its [differential of a smooth map](differential-of-a-smooth-map.md) lies in their span. Local angle combinations can still give extra local integrals; they fail to be single-valued on the torus.
