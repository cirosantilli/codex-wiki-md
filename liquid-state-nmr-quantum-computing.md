# Liquid-state NMR quantum computing

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum computing](quantum-computing.md)

[Liquid-state NMR quantum computing](liquid-state-nmr-quantum-computing.md) encodes [qubits](qubit.md) in distinguishable nuclear [spins](spin.md) of molecules in solution. Selective radiofrequency pulses implement one-spin control; [NMR scalar coupling](nmr-scalar-coupling.md) supplies entangling evolution. Under $H_J=(\pi\hbar J/2)Z_1Z_2$, evolution for $1/(2J)$ with $J>0$, followed by $-\pi/2$ rotations about each $z$ axis, produces a [Controlled-Z gate](controlled-z-gate.md) up to [global phase](global-phase.md). Ensemble detection and common pseudo-pure preparations make small demonstrations distinct from scalable pure-state initialization.
