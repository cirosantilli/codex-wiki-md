# Magnetic-susceptibility critical exponent

↑ **Parent:** [Critical exponent](critical-exponent.md)

The magnetic-susceptibility critical exponent is defined by the zero-field [magnetic susceptibility](magnetic-susceptibility.md) $\chi\sim|t|^{-\gamma}$. Separate amplitudes, and occasionally separate exponents, may occur on the two sides of the transition.
