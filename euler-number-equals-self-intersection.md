# Euler number equals self-intersection

↑ **Parent:** [Self-intersection formula](self-intersection-formula.md)

For an oriented closed surface smoothly embedded in an oriented four-manifold, a transverse section of the [normal bundle](normal-bundle.md) gives a homologous small push-off. Its signed zeros are exactly the signed intersections with the original surface. This identifies the evaluation of the [Euler class](euler-class-of-a-vector-bundle.md) with the [self-intersection number](self-intersection-number.md).
