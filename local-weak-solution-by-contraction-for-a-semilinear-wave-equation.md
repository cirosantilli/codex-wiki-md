# Local weak solution by contraction for a semilinear wave equation

↑ **Parent:** [Semilinear wave equation](semilinear-wave-equation.md)

Suppose the inhomogeneous linear wave equation has an energy estimate in a Banach solution space $X_T$, and its nonlinearity obeys

$$
\|F(w)\|_{L^2((0,T)\times U)}\leq C\sqrt T\,\|w\|_{X_T}^2,
$$

together with the corresponding locally Lipschitz estimate. For a sufficiently large closed ball and sufficiently small $T$, the map that solves the linear equation with source $F(w)$ is a contraction. The [contraction mapping theorem](contraction-mapping-theorem.md) then gives a local [weak solution](weak-solution.md).
