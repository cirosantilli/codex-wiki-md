# Kleinian group

↑ **Parent:** [Discrete subgroup](discrete-subgroup.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kleinian_group)

A discrete subgroup of $\operatorname{PSL}_2(\mathbb C)$, acting by [Möbius transformations](mobius-transformation.md) on the [Riemann sphere](riemann-sphere.md) or orientation-preserving isometries of [hyperbolic space](hyperbolic-space.md) $\mathbb H^3$. A group may be Kleinian even when some elements fix points; freeness and absence of torsion are additional conditions.
