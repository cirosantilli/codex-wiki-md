# Magnetic polarizability of a perfectly conducting sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic dipole moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md)

In the small-sphere oscillatory-field limit, [surface current density](surface-current-density.md) excludes the changing [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md). Adding a central magnetic dipole to a uniform applied field and imposing zero normal field at the sphere gives $\mathbf m=-2\pi a^3\mathbf H_{\rm inc}$. The minus sign expresses [diamagnetism](diamagnetism.md); it does not make this dipole's backward radiation cancel the electric contribution.[section 8.4, Example 8.4.3](electromagnetic-fields-and-energy-by-hermann-a-haus-and-james-r-melcher.md)
