# Inductor current continuity

↑ **Parent:** [Inductor](inductor.md)

For an ideal linear [inductor](inductor.md), $V_L=L\dot I_L$. Integrating shows that voltage with no impulse preserves current continuity at switching. This supplies an initial condition independently of [capacitor voltage continuity](capacitor-voltage-continuity.md); the two state variables must not be replaced by an assumed zero current derivative.
