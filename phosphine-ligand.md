# Phosphine ligand

↑ **Parent:** [Ligand in coordination chemistry](ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md)

A [phosphine ligand](phosphine-ligand.md) such as $\mathrm{PR_3}$ donates a phosphorus [lone pair](lone-pair.md). It can also accept [pi backbonding](pi-backbonding.md) into orbitals with substantial P–R antibonding character; phosphorus $3d$ orbitals need not be essential acceptors. Alkyl substitution generally increases sigma donation relative to electron-withdrawing substituents.
