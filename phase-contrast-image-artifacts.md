# Phase-contrast image artifacts

↑ **Parent:** [Phase-contrast microscopy](phase-contrast-microscopy.md)

A finite [phase plate](phase-plate.md) also intercepts diffracted light, altering low [spatial frequencies](spatial-frequency.md). Halos and shade-off can therefore mimic borders or obscure interiors.[Interpretation of Phase Contrast Images, halo and shade-off](introduction-to-phase-contrast-microscopy-by-nikon-instruments-inc.md) In the [coherent zero-mode model of phase contrast](coherent-zero-mode-model-of-phase-contrast.md), the separation is perfect by assumption, so that model cannot predict those artifacts.
