# Beamed gamma-ray optical depth

↑ **Parent:** [Gamma-ray compactness problem](gamma-ray-compactness-problem.md)

For an internally illuminated isotropic rest-frame blob with an unbroken [power-law spectrum](power-law-spectrum.md), use $R'\sim Dc\Delta t_{\rm obs}$, a corresponding-bin [luminosity](luminosity.md) boost $D^4$, and the [internal pair-production target energy in a relativistic blob](internal-pair-production-target-energy-in-a-relativistic-blob.md). At fixed observed gamma-ray [energy](energy-split.md),

$$
\tau'(D)\sim\tau_0D^{-6}\frac{F_{\log}(D^2\epsilon_{20})}{F_{\log}(\epsilon_{20})}
=\tau_0D^{-(4+2\alpha)}.
$$

Here $F_{\log}=\nu F_\nu$ and $\epsilon_{20}$ is the target [energy](energy-split.md) at $D=1$. The frequently used $D^{-6}$ is the $\alpha=1$ case, not a universal law for every [spectral energy distribution](spectral-energy-distribution.md). Geometry, spectral breaks and external illumination affect the result.
