# Quasicomponent

↑ **Parent:** [Connected component](connected-component.md)

The quasicomponent of a point is the intersection of all its clopen neighborhoods. Every connected set containing the point lies in its quasicomponent, since a [clopen set](clopen-set.md) cannot split a connected set. In a compact Hausdorff space, quasicomponents equal [connected components](connected-component.md) by the [Šura-Bura component lemma](sura-bura-component-lemma.md).
