# Turning tangent theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Signed curvature](signed-curvature.md)

The unit tangent of a simple regular closed plane curve traversed anticlockwise turns once. Consequently its integrated [signed curvature](signed-curvature.md) is $2\pi$; clockwise orientation gives $-2\pi$. Simplicity and one traversal are essential to this value.
