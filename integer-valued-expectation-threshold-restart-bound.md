# Integer-valued expectation threshold restart bound

↑ **Parent:** [Markov's inequality](markov-s-inequality.md)

Let $Z$ be a nonnegative integer-valued [random variable](random-variable-split.md) with $\mathbb EZ=A/q$, where $A\geq0$ and $q\geq1$ are [integers](integer-split.md). On $Z>A/q$, the integer $qZ$ is at least $A+1$. [Markov's inequality](markov-s-inequality.md) gives $\Pr(Z>A/q)\leq A/(A+1)$. Independent restarts therefore need at most $A+1$ attempts in [expected value](expected-value-split.md) to achieve $Z\leq\mathbb EZ$. The denominator bound on the [expected value](expected-value-split.md) matters for this runtime guarantee.
