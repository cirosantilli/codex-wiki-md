# Leading coefficient of an exponential alternant

↑ **Parent:** [Vandermonde determinant](vandermonde-determinant.md)

For distinct $\ell_i$ and $c_j$, expand the exponential entries in powers of $t$. Nonzero [determinant](determinant.md) terms first occur at the distinct powers $0,1,\ldots,m-1$. The coefficient is the product of their two [Vandermonde determinants](vandermonde-determinant.md) divided by the factorial product. Ratios of such alternants yield the [Weyl dimension formula](weyl-dimension-formula.md) by taking a torus [character](character-of-a-representation.md) to the identity.
