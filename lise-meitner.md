# Lise Meitner

A [physicist](physicist-split.md) who helped explain [nuclear fission](nuclear-fission.md). [Lise Meitner](lise-meitner.md) and [Otto Robert Frisch](otto-robert-frisch.md) interpreted the energy release associated with the chemical findings of [Otto Hahn](otto-hahn.md) and [Fritz Strassmann](fritz-strassmann.md). Following these contributions together distinguishes the identification of reaction products from their physical explanation.[Treffpunkt der Nobelpreisträger. Das Harnack-Haus in Berlin-Dahlem by Susanne Kiewitz; Susanne Kiewitz, final paragraphs: Hahn–Strassmann chemical findings, Meitner–Frisch interpretation and contributions to fission](treffpunkt-der-nobelpreistrager-das-harnack-haus-in-berlin-dahlem-by-susanne-kiewitz/lise-meitner-im-portrat.md)
