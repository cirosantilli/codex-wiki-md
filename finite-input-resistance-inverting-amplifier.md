# Finite-input-resistance inverting amplifier

↑ **Parent:** [Inverting amplifier](inverting-amplifier.md)

With the noninverting input grounded, a differential [input impedance](input-impedance.md) $R_{\rm in}$ draws current from the summing node. For zero [output impedance](output-impedance.md), $V_o=-Av$ and [Kirchhoff's current law](kirchhoff-node-law.md) gives $(v-V_i)/R_1+(v-V_o)/R_2+v/R_{\rm in}=0$. Eliminating $v$ yields the displayed [voltage gain](voltage-gain.md). The resistor-ratio result requires $|A|\gg1+R_2/R_1+R_2/R_{\rm in}$ as well as stable, unsaturated operation. The same expression applies to small-signal [phasors](phasor.md) with complex $A(f)$.
