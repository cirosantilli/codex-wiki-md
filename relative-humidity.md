# Relative humidity

↑ **Parent:** [Humidity](humidity.md)

[Relative humidity](relative-humidity.md) is the ratio of the [partial pressure](partial-pressure.md) of [water vapor](water-vapor.md) to its saturation [vapor pressure](vapor-pressure.md) at the same [temperature](temperature-split.md), usually expressed as a percentage.
