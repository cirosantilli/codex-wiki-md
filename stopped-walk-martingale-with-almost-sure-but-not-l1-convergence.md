# Stopped-walk martingale with almost sure but not L1 convergence

↑ **Parent:** [Stopped martingale](stopped-martingale.md)

For a [simple symmetric random walk](simple-symmetric-random-walk.md), the [infinite mean first passage of a simple symmetric random walk](infinite-mean-first-passage-of-a-simple-symmetric-random-walk.md) gives a finite almost sure first passage $T_1$ to $1$. The [stopped martingale](stopped-martingale.md) $M_n=1-S_{n\wedge T_1}$ is nonnegative and eventually zero almost surely. Nevertheless $\mathbb EM_n=1$ for every $n$, so it has [almost sure convergence](almost-sure-convergence.md) but no [convergence in L1](convergence-in-l1.md). It is therefore not [uniformly integrable](uniform-integrability.md). Before absorption the nearest-neighbour walk is at most zero, ensuring nonnegativity.
