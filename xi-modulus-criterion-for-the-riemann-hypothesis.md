# Xi modulus criterion for the Riemann hypothesis

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann hypothesis](riemann-hypothesis.md)

[Riemann hypothesis](riemann-hypothesis.md) is equivalent to monotonicity of $\sigma\mapsto|\xi(\sigma+it)|$ for every fixed $t$ on $\sigma\ge1/2$. Under [Riemann hypothesis](riemann-hypothesis.md) the [real logarithmic derivative of Riemann xi](real-logarithmic-derivative-of-riemann-xi.md) is positive for $\sigma>1/2$. Conversely a zero right of that line would force the nonnegative monotone modulus to vanish on an interval, contrary to the [identity theorem](identity-theorem.md). Functional symmetry rules out zeros to the left.
