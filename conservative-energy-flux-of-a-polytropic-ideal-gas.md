# Conservative energy flux of a polytropic ideal gas

↑ **Parent:** [Euler equations for an inviscid fluid](euler-equations-for-an-inviscid-fluid.md)

For an unmagnetized [ideal gas](ideal-gas.md) without gravity, the [pressure](pressure.md) equation gives internal-energy balance $\partial_te+\partial_x(u_xe)=-p\partial_xu_x$, where $e=p/(\gamma-1)$. The momentum equation gives kinetic-energy balance with source $-u_x\partial_xp$. Their sum is conservative with the displayed total-energy flux. Tangential [velocities](velocity.md) contribute to [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) even when all derivatives are in the normal direction. Smooth [polytropic flow](polytropic-flow.md) advects its [entropy](entropy.md) parameter; a physical shock need not preserve that parameter across the discontinuity.
