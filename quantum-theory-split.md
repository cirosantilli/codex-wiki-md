# Quantum theory

↑ **Parent:** [Branches of physics](branches-of-physics.md)

Quantum theory supplies a common language for [quantum mechanics](quantum-mechanics-split.md), [measurement in quantum mechanics](quantum-measurement-split.md), and [quantum information](quantum-information.md): prepare a [quantum state](quantum-state.md), transform it, and ask which experimental outcomes occur. In a finite-dimensional idealization, a [pure state](pure-state.md) is a normalized [vector](vector.md) of [complex numbers](complex-number.md). Its entries are [probability amplitudes](probability-amplitude.md); the [Born rule](born-rule.md) turns their squared magnitudes into [probabilities](probability.md). Between measurements, an isolated system evolves by a [unitary operator](unitary-operator.md), preserving its [norm](norm.md) and [inner products](inner-product-split.md). A [quantum circuit](quantum-circuit-split.md) makes this evolution explicit as a sequence of [quantum gates](quantum-logic-gate.md).

The extra information carried by [quantum phases](quantum-phase.md) is essential. Relative [quantum phases](quantum-phase.md) affect how [probability amplitudes](probability-amplitude.md) add when different contributions reach the same output. Their [interference](interference-wave-propagation.md) can enhance one outcome and cancel another. A [global phase](global-phase.md), multiplying the entire [quantum state](quantum-state.md) by the same complex factor of magnitude one, changes neither these relative phases nor observable predictions. The [Hadamard gate](hadamard-gate.md) below provides a small experiment in which this distinction becomes a deterministic change of output.

[Quantum coherence](quantum-coherence-in-a-specified-basis.md) describes this capacity to interfere relative to a specified [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md). In a [density matrix](density-matrix-split.md), it appears in off-diagonal entries; a diagonal [mixed state](mixed-state.md) retains the populations but lacks that basis coherence. Thus matching the [probabilities](probability.md) of one measurement need not match the predictions of a later experiment. Baumgratz, Cramer, and Plenio made this basis-dependent viewpoint precise in their resource framework for [quantum coherence](quantum-coherence-in-a-specified-basis.md). [Baumgratz, Cramer, and Plenio, Quantifying Coherence, 2014, pp. 1–2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1311.0275).

This makes [quantum information](quantum-information.md) an operational question: which distinctions can a preparation, an available [unitary operator](unitary-operator.md), and [measurement in quantum mechanics](quantum-measurement-split.md) reveal? [Amplitude amplification](amplitude-amplification.md) illustrates how carefully arranged [interference](interference-wave-propagation.md) converts a weakly represented answer into a likely observable result. Preserving the relevant [quantum coherence](quantum-coherence-in-a-specified-basis.md) throughout the computation is part of the physical task. [Watrous, IBM Quantum Learning, introduction to single systems](https://quantum.cloud.ibm.com/learning/en/courses/basics-of-quantum-information/single-systems/introduction).
