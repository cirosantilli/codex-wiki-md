# List-chromatic index

↑ **Parent:** [List edge coloring](list-edge-coloring.md)

The list-chromatic index is the least integer $q$ such that every assignment of at least $q$ allowed colors to each [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) admits a [list edge coloring](list-edge-coloring.md). Uniform identical lists show it is at least the [chromatic index](chromatic-index.md). The [Galvin theorem for bipartite list edge coloring](galvin-theorem-for-bipartite-list-edge-coloring.md) gives equality for [bipartite graphs](bipartite-graph.md).
