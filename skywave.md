# Skywave

↑ **Parent:** [Ionosphere](ionosphere.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skywave)

A skywave is a [radio wave](radio-wave.md) returned toward [Earth](earth.md) through interaction with the [ionosphere](ionosphere.md). In a smoothly varying [plasma](plasma-physics.md), the ray can bend continuously through [refraction](refraction.md) before returning: there need be no sharp material surface at the apparent point of reflection. Different [frequencies](frequency.md), launch directions and [wave polarizations](polarization-waves.md) can have different paths and losses.
