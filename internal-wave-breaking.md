# Internal-wave breaking

↑ **Parent:** [Internal wave](internal-wave.md)

Nonlinear disruption of an [internal gravity wave](internal-wave.md), for example when large displacement gradients overturn the stable density arrangement or strong shear becomes unstable. The resulting [turbulence](turbulence-split.md) dissipates wave energy and mixes the stratification, limiting ideal geometric focusing.
