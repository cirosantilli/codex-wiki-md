# Compact inverse and adjoint of a coercive elliptic operator

↑ **Parent:** [Lax-Milgram theorem](lax-milgram-theorem.md)

A coercive [zero-Dirichlet](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) weak form gives a [bounded](boundedness.md) solution map from $L^2$ into $H_0^1$. Composing with the compact [Sobolev embedding](compact-embedding.md) into $L^2$ gives the compact inverse $K$. Transposing the [bilinear form](bilinear-form-split.md) produces its Hilbert-space adjoint inverse $K^*$. The Fredholm compatibility condition for a shifted equation is [orthogonality](orthogonal-vectors.md) of $f$ to the homogeneous adjoint solutions. Indeed $(Kf,w)=(f,w)/\lambda$ when $K^*w=w/\lambda$, and the zero spectral parameter is excluded at resonance by coercivity.
