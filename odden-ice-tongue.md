# Odden ice tongue

↑ **Parent:** [Sea ice](sea-ice.md)

The Odden ice tongue is an intermittent extension of young [sea ice](sea-ice.md) into the Greenland Sea. Repeated formation and export of [frazil ice](frazil-ice.md) and [pancake ice](pancake-ice.md) can produce [brine rejection](brine-rejection.md) without a steadily thickening local ice cover, increasing the salinity of the water left behind.
