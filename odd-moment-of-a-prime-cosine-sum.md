# Odd moment of a prime cosine sum

↑ **Parent:** [Mean value of Dirichlet polynomials](mean-value-of-dirichlet-polynomials.md)

For odd $j$, expanding $(\sum_{p\le X}p^{-\sigma}\cos(t\log p))^j$ gives products with different numbers of [prime factors](prime-factor.md) on the two sides. [Unique prime factorization](fundamental-theorem-of-arithmetic.md) rules out every diagonal equality. The integral over $[T,2T]$ is therefore $O_j(X^j(\sum_{n\le X^j}n^{-\sigma})^2)$.
