# Proton relay

↑ **Parent:** [Proton transfer](proton-transfer.md)

A [proton relay](proton-relay.md) transfers a proton through a sequence of neighboring donors and acceptors, often including [water](water.md). The full catalytic sequence must regenerate the relay groups; a static hydrogen-bond diagram does not determine its direction or timing.
