# Charge symmetry of nucleon parton distributions

↑ **Parent:** [Parton distribution function](parton-distribution-function.md)

The approximate equality of [proton](proton.md) and [neutron](neutron-split.md) [parton distribution functions](parton-distribution-function.md) after interchanging [up quarks](up-quark.md) and [down quarks](down-quark.md), including their [antiquarks](antiquark.md). It follows from the corresponding [isospin](isospin.md) symmetry when electromagnetic and light-quark mass-difference effects are neglected. Isospin-singlet sea distributions are unchanged.
