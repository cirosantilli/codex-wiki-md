<h1 id="holstein-primakoff-transformation">Holstein–Primakoff transformation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Spin](spin.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holstein–Primakoff_transformation)

The Holstein–Primakoff transformation represents a spin-$S$ [operator algebra](operator-algebra.md) with a bosonic [creation operator](creation-operator.md) and [annihilation operator](annihilation-operator.md):

$$
S^+=\sqrt{2S}\sqrt{1-\frac{a^\dagger a}{2S}}\,a,\qquad S^-=\sqrt{2S}\,a^\dagger\sqrt{1-\frac{a^\dagger a}{2S}},\qquad S^z=S-a^\dagger a.
$$

The order of the square root and oscillator operator matters. On the physical [Fock states](fock-state.md) $|n\rangle$, $0\le n\le2S$, the raising and lowering matrix elements are $\sqrt{n(2S-n+1)}$ and $\sqrt{(n+1)(2S-n)}$. Their squared difference gives $[S^+,S^-]|n\rangle=2(S-n)|n\rangle$, proving the [spin commutation relations](spin-commutation-relations.md). Expanding the square root yields the [linear spin-wave approximation](linear-spin-wave-approximation.md) and its interaction corrections.
