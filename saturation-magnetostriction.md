# Saturation magnetostriction

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetostrictive strain](magnetostrictive-strain.md)

In the [saturated isotropic magnetostrictive strain](saturated-isotropic-magnetostrictive-strain.md) convention, [saturation magnetostriction](saturation-magnetostriction.md) is the longitudinal relative length change from a random-domain reference to alignment. A parallel-to-perpendicular length difference instead equals $3\lambda_s/2$ in that model.
