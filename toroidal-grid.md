# Toroidal grid

↑ **Parent:** [Grid graph](grid-graph.md)

A periodic rectangular grid whose opposite boundaries are identified. On an $m$ by $n$ grid, coordinates are taken [modulo](modulo-operation.md) $m$ and $n$, so each horizontal or vertical step wraps around at a boundary.
