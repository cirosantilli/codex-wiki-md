# Matter density

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmology](cosmology-split.md)

The matter density $\rho_m$ is the [energy density](energy-density.md) in nonrelativistic matter. In a homogeneous expanding universe, conservation of particle number and dilution of physical volume give $\rho_m\propto a^{-3}$.
