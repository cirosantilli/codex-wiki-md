# Residue-basis norm formula for an unramified extension

↑ **Parent:** [Unramified residue generator integral basis](unramified-residue-generator-integral-basis.md)

Let $L/K$ be a finite [unramified extension](unramified-extension.md) of complete fields with a [Non-Archimedean absolute value](non-archimedean-absolute-value.md), with separable residue extension and $[L:K]=[k_L:k_K]=n$. If $x$ is integral and its residue generates $k_L/k_K$, then $1,x,\ldots,x^{n-1}$ form a $K$-basis. Divide any nonzero combination by a coefficient of largest absolute value. Its remaining coefficients are integral, at least one is a unit, and the residue-basis independence makes the reduction nonzero. The displayed norm formula follows. In particular, the combination is integral exactly when every coefficient is integral, proving $\mathcal O_L=\mathcal O_K[x]$ without requiring a discrete valuation.
