# Rational interpolation

↑ **Parent:** [Interpolation](interpolation-split.md)

Rational interpolation seeks a [rational function](rational-function.md) $q/r$ with prescribed bounds on the [polynomial degrees](degree-of-a-polynomial.md) of its numerator and denominator and prescribed values at distinct [interpolation nodes](interpolation-node.md). A [rational interpolant](rational-interpolant.md) must have a well-defined value at each [interpolation node](interpolation-node.md). With a specified numerator-denominator representation, this requires $r(x_i)\ne0$ for every $i$. A normalization such as a [monic polynomial](monic-polynomial.md) denominator removes the common scaling freedom but does not guarantee existence or uniqueness. Multiplying by a zero denominator can create spurious solutions to the [rational interpolation linearization](rational-interpolation-linearization.md).
