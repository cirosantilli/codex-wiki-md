# Kodaira vanishing theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Positive holomorphic line bundle](positive-holomorphic-line-bundle.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kodaira_vanishing_theorem)

On a compact [Kähler manifold](kahler-manifold.md) $X$, if $P$ is a [positive holomorphic line bundle](positive-holomorphic-line-bundle.md), then $H^i(X,K_X\otimes P)=0$ for $i>0$. The canonical-bundle twist is part of the hypothesis and conclusion; positivity alone does not remove it on a general manifold. For a [complex torus](complex-torus.md), the [canonical bundle](canonical-bundle.md) is trivial, so higher cohomology of a positive line bundle vanishes without an additional twist.
