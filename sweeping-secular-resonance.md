# Sweeping secular resonance

↑ **Parent:** [Secular resonance](secular-resonance.md)

Changing planetary masses or other system parameters move the frequencies of [secular eigenmodes](secular-eigenmode.md). A particle can cross a [secular resonance](secular-resonance.md) when its free precession frequency meets a forcing frequency. Passage can change its [free eccentricity](proper-eccentricity.md) and mix modes; a single scalar phase replacement generally cannot describe the response.
