# Scalar-field cubic and quartic Feynman vertices

↑ **Parent:** [Feynman rule](feynman-rule.md)

With interaction density $\mu\phi^3/3!-\lambda\phi^4/4!$, the [Dyson series](dyson-series.md) contains $i\mathcal L_{\rm int}$ and hence cubic vertex $i\mu$ and quartic vertex $-i\lambda$. Wick contractions into the labelled slots cancel each factorial. Position integration gives the four-momentum delta function, and equivalent unlabelled contractions leave the [Feynman-diagram symmetry factor](feynman-diagram-symmetry-factor.md). The free line carries $i/(p^2-m^2+i0)$ in the mostly-minus convention.
