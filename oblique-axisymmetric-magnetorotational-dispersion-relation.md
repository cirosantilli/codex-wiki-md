# Oblique axisymmetric magnetorotational dispersion relation

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal magnetorotational dispersion relation](ideal-magnetorotational-dispersion-relation.md)

In the local incompressible weak-field limit, take an axisymmetric [wavevector](wavevector.md) $(k_r,0,k_z)$ and $\omega_A=\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf B/\sqrt{\mu_0\rho}$. Eliminating the pressure projects the radial force by $k_z^2/k^2$. This yields the displayed dispersion relation, where $\kappa$ is the [radial epicyclic frequency](radial-epicyclic-frequency.md). For a hydrodynamically stable rotation profile with outward-decreasing angular velocity, instability requires $0<\omega_A^2<-(k_z^2/k^2)d\Omega^2/d\ln r$. A purely toroidal field has no axisymmetric local field-line-bending frequency and therefore no ordinary weak-field MRI in this approximation.
