# Inertial motion

↑ **Parent:** [Inertial frame](inertial-frame.md)

Motion with zero [acceleration](acceleration.md) relative to the theory's inertial [affine connection](affine-connection.md). In an [inertial frame](inertial-frame.md), its spatial trajectory is uniform and straight. The geometric statement refers to the connection, not to a chosen [coordinate system](coordinate-system.md); force-free motion supplies its physical interpretation. In [general relativity](general-relativity-split.md) the corresponding freely falling trajectories are [timelike geodesics](timelike-geodesic.md), with local inertial coordinates rather than a global [inertial frame](inertial-frame.md) in a curved region.
