# Ionization-bounded nebula

↑ **Parent:** [Photoionization equilibrium](photoionization-equilibrium.md)

An ionization-bounded nebula contains enough absorbing material to trap the relevant ionizing photons, unlike a density-bounded region that ends before its ionization front. In dust-free hydrogen at steady [photoionization equilibrium](photoionization-equilibrium.md), the absorbed stellar photon rate equals the integrated [Case B recombination](case-b-recombination.md) rate. Escape, absorption by dust, and competition from other species must be accounted for before identifying the absorbed hydrogen rate with the entire stellar ionizing luminosity.
