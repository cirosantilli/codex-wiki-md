# Diagonal evaluation map

↑ **Parent:** [Topological embedding](topological-embedding.md)

A family of maps with a common domain determines the displayed map into the [Cartesian product](cartesian-product.md) of their codomains. It is injective exactly when the family separates points. For continuous coordinate maps it is continuous in the [product topology](product-topology.md). If inverse images of open sets under the coordinates generate the original topology, the inverse from the image is also continuous; thus the map is a [topological embedding](topological-embedding.md). A basis of such inverse images, as opposed to a subbasis, is a sufficient stronger hypothesis.
