# Tuple-generating dependency

↑ **Parent:** [First-order logic](first-order-logic.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuple-generating_dependency)

A function-free implication between conjunctions of positive [atomic formulas](atomic-formula.md), allowing existentially quantified witnesses in its conclusion. Standard [tuple-generating dependencies](tuple-generating-dependency.md) do not have disjunctive heads or negative constraints.
