# Punctured three-sphere

↑ **Parent:** [3-manifold](3-manifold.md)

A punctured three-sphere is $S^3$ with the interiors of finitely many disjoint [piecewise linear balls](piecewise-linear-ball.md) removed. Capping its spherical [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md) components with balls restores $S^3$. Removing another ball or gluing two such manifolds along one spherical [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md) component preserves this class.
