# Bogomolny monopole equations imply Yang-Mills-Higgs equations

↑ **Parent:** [Bogomolny-Prasad-Sommerfield monopole](bogomolny-prasad-sommerfield-monopole.md)

For zero scalar potential, the static field equations are $D_iD_i\Phi=0$ and $D_iF_{ij}=[\Phi,D_j\Phi]$. If $B_i=-D_i\Phi$, the [gauge-theory Bianchi identity](gauge-theory-bianchi-identity.md) gives the first equation. For the second,

$$
D_iF_{ij}=-\frac12\epsilon_{ijk}[F_{ik},\Phi]
=[B_j,\Phi]=[\Phi,D_j\Phi],
$$

using $F_{ik}=\epsilon_{ik\ell}B_\ell$ and $\epsilon_{ijk}\epsilon_{ik\ell}=-2\delta_{j\ell}$. Thus the first-order equations imply the full second-order equations locally, independently of the radial ansatz.
