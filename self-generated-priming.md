# Self-generated priming

↑ **Parent:** [Stimulus priming](stimulus-priming.md)

In the [SOP model](sop-model.md), a recent stimulus presentation leaves elements in secondary activation, making fewer elements available for primary activation on repetition. This supplies a transient response-decrement mechanism without requiring a new association.
