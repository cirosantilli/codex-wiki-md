# Laser rate equation

↑ **Parent:** [Laser](laser-split.md)

A [laser rate equation](laser-rate-equation.md) approximates coupled evolution of [population inversion](population-inversion.md) and intracavity [photon number](photon-number.md), including pumping, [spontaneous emission](spontaneous-emission.md), [stimulated emission](stimulated-emission.md) and [optical cavity](optical-cavity.md) losses. A simple model is $\dot S=[g(N)-1/\tau_c]S$, $\dot N=R_p-N/\tau_u-g(N)S$, where compatible normalizations absorb mode-volume factors. Large excess [optical gain](optical-gain.md) drives rapid field growth until inversion depletion reduces it below threshold.
