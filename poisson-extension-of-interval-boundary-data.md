# Poisson extension of interval boundary data

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson integral](poisson-integral.md)

For boundary value $c$ on $(-a,a)$ and zero outside, integrating the [Poisson kernel for the upper half-plane](poisson-kernel-for-the-upper-half-plane.md) gives the displayed bounded [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md) for $y>0$. It approaches the assigned values at boundary continuity points and the midpoint value $c/2$ at either jump. Changing the value at an isolated endpoint does not alter the integral. This is the unique bounded harmonic extension; pointwise decay in the vertical direction alone, without a spatial growth restriction, does not give uniqueness.
