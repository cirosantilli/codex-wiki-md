# Conditional concentration at a large-deviation threshold

↑ **Parent:** [Cramér's theorem](cramer-s-theorem.md)

For independent identically distributed real variables with everywhere finite [cumulant-generating function](cumulant-generating-function.md), let $\mu$ be the mean. If $a\geq\mu$ and $\mathbb P(X_1>a)>0$, then $\mathbb P(S_n/n\in[a,a+\varepsilon)\mid S_n/n\geq a)\to1$ for each $\varepsilon>0$. The [Legendre transform of a cumulant-generating function](legendre-transform-of-a-cumulant-generating-function.md) is finite at $a$, and a positive exponential parameter gives strictly greater cost at $a+\varepsilon$. A [Chernoff bound](chernoff-bound.md) for the farther tail divided by the [Cramér theorem](cramer-s-theorem.md) lower estimate at $a$ decays exponentially. The support condition both ensures positive conditioning probabilities and avoids a vacuous conditioning event.
