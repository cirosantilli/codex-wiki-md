# Nonincreasing Hubble rate for a canonical inflaton

↑ **Parent:** [First Hubble slow-roll parameter](first-hubble-slow-roll-parameter.md)

For flat expansion with a canonical scalar and no additional fluid, differentiating the [Friedmann equation](friedmann-equations.md) gives $\dot H=-4\pi G\dot\phi^2\leq0$. Expansion can still accelerate if $-\dot H/H^2<1$. Equality at an isolated turning point does not imply eternal de Sitter expansion.
