# Equal-mass circular binary

↑ **Parent:** [Two-body problem](two-body-problem.md)

Two equal point [masses](mass.md) can move around the same fixed circle of radius $a$ in diametrically opposite positions. Their stationary [centre of mass](center-of-mass.md) is the circle's center. A separation $2a$ gives gravitational [force](force.md) $Gm^2/(4a^2)$ on each [mass](mass.md), and [Newton's second law](newton-s-second-law.md) equates it to $ma\omega^2$. The common [angular speed](angular-speed.md) is therefore fixed by the displayed relation. Opposite tangential [velocities](velocity.md) produce the same sense of angular rotation.
