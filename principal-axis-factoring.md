# Principal axis factoring

↑ **Parent:** [Exploratory factor analysis](exploratory-factor-analysis.md)

A common-factor extraction method based on iteratively estimating shared variance and the associated [factor loadings](factor-loading.md). It differs from [principal component analysis](principal-component-analysis.md), whose components summarize total observed variation.
