# Boundary equilibrium of multiplicative price dynamics

↑ **Parent:** [Congestion control](congestion-control.md)

In $\dot\mu_j=\kappa_j\mu_j(y_j-q_j(\mu_j))$, a zero price remains zero even if resource demand exceeds supply. Such a boundary equilibrium need not minimize the [dual congestion potential](dual-congestion-potential.md); uniqueness of its positive minimizer does not imply uniqueness on the nonnegative orthant.
