# One-factor short-rate model

↑ **Parent:** [Short rate](short-rate.md)

A [one-factor short-rate model](one-factor-short-rate-model.md) specifies $dr_t=\mu(t,r_t)dt+\eta(t,r_t)dW_t$ with deterministic coefficient functions. All [zero-coupon bond](zero-coupon-bond.md) prices are functions of time, maturity and this single scalar state. The physical drift and pricing drift differ by $\eta\lambda$, where $\lambda$ is the [market price of risk](market-price-of-risk.md).
