<h1 id="path-and-moment-criteria-from-a-levy-triplet">Path and moment criteria from a Lévy triplet</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Lévy–Itô decomposition](levy-ito-decomposition.md)

For the convention in the [Lévy–Khintchine formula](levy-khintchine-formula.md), a Lévy process has almost surely differentiable paths exactly when $b=0$ and $K=0$, and continuous paths exactly when $K=0$. It is integrable exactly when $\int_{|x|>1}|x|K(dx)<\infty$, and it has a finite second moment exactly when $\int x^2K(dx)<\infty$.
