# Vertical modes of a horizontally constrained suspended bar

↑ **Parent:** [Generalized eigenvalue problem for small oscillations](generalized-eigenvalue-problem-for-small-oscillations.md)

A horizontal bar of [mass](mass.md) $M$ hangs from a [spring](spring.md) of incremental stiffness $\kappa_0>0$, and two equal masses $m$ hang separately from the bar through springs of stiffness $\kappa_1>0$. The bar remains horizontal and all motion is vertical. For physical displacements $q=(q_1,q_2,q_3)$ from the loaded [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md), the [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) and quadratic [potential energy](potential-energy.md) are

$$
T=\frac12[M\dot q_1^2+m\dot q_2^2+m\dot q_3^2],\qquad V=\frac12\kappa_0q_1^2+\frac12\kappa_1[(q_2-q_1)^2+(q_3-q_1)^2].
$$

[Gravity](gravity.md) determines the static extensions; its linear terms cancel at equilibrium. Exchange of the lower masses splits the [normal modes](normal-mode.md) into the antisymmetric mode $(0,1,-1)$ with $\omega^2=\kappa_1/m$, and two symmetric modes $(A,B,B)$. The latter obey $(\kappa_0+2\kappa_1-M\omega^2)A-2\kappa_1B=0$ and $-\kappa_1A+(\kappa_1-m\omega^2)B=0$. Their determinant gives the title quadratic in $\omega^2$, and $B/A=\kappa_1/(\kappa_1-m\omega^2)$. For $M=2,m=1,\kappa_0=4,\kappa_1=1$, their squared [angular frequencies](angular-frequency.md) are $2\pm\sqrt2$, with displacement ratios $(1,1\mp\sqrt2,1\mp\sqrt2)$ respectively. The lower symmetric mode moves all masses in phase; the higher one moves the lower pair against the bar.
