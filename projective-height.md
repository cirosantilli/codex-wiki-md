# Projective height

↑ **Parent:** [Absolute multiplicative Weil height](absolute-multiplicative-weil-height.md)

For a nonzero vector $\mathbf a=(a_0,\ldots,a_n)$ over a [number field](number-field.md) $K$, its absolute projective height is

$$
H_p(\mathbf a)=\prod_{v\in M_K}\max_i|a_i|_v^{d_v/[K:\mathbb Q]}.
$$

The [product formula](product-formula.md) makes this unchanged by multiplying all coordinates by the same nonzero scalar. The projective height of a [linear form](linear-functional.md) is the projective height of its coefficient vector.
