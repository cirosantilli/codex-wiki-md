# Harmonic approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Small oscillation](small-oscillation.md)

Near a [stable equilibrium](stable-equilibrium.md) $x_0$ with $V'(x_0)=0$ and $V''(x_0)>0$, the harmonic approximation truncates the [Taylor series](taylor-series.md) of the [potential energy](potential-energy.md) at quadratic order. With constant [mass](mass.md) $m$, [Newton's second law](newton-s-second-law.md) then gives a [harmonic oscillator](simple-harmonic-motion.md) with [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) $\omega=\sqrt{V''(x_0)/m}$. The approximation requires displacements small enough that the omitted terms are negligible; it does not describe a flat minimum where $V''(x_0)=0$.
