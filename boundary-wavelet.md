# Boundary wavelet

↑ **Parent:** [Interval-adapted wavelet basis](interval-adapted-wavelet-basis.md)

A boundary wavelet replaces a translated interior [wavelet](wavelet.md) whose [support of a function](support.md) meets a domain endpoint. Compatible finite boundary refinement matrices preserve orthogonality and localization across scales. If the coarse boundary spaces reproduce [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) of degree less than $q$, their detail-space [orthogonal complements](orthogonal-complement.md) have $q$ [vanishing moments](vanishing-moment.md).
