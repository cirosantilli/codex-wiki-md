# Center of an upper unitriangular group

↑ **Parent:** [Upper unitriangular group](upper-unitriangular-group.md)

The [upper unitriangular group](upper-unitriangular-group.md) is generated by adjacent [elementary transvection matrices](elementary-transvection-matrix.md) $I+sE_{i,i+1}$. Commuting a [matrix](matrix.md) $X$ with each such generator forces every entry above the diagonal to vanish except possibly its $(1,n)$ entry: comparing $XE_{i,i+1}$ with $E_{i,i+1}X$ kills the entries above position $(i,i)$ in column $i$ and to the right of position $(i+1,i+1)$ in row $i+1$. The surviving [matrices](matrix.md) $I+tE_{1n}$ do commute with the entire [group](group-split.md). In particular the [group center](center-of-a-group.md) has order $q$; it distinguishes $U_4(2)$ from $U_3(4)$ despite their common order $64$.
