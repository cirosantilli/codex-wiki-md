# Even lattice

↑ **Parent:** [Integral quadratic lattice](integral-quadratic-lattice.md)

Every vector in an even lattice has even integral norm. The [E8 lattice](e8-lattice.md) and the hyperbolic plane appearing in the [K3 intersection lattice](k3-intersection-lattice.md) are examples.
