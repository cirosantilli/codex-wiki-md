# Zero-shear lower wall in a translating and squeezing finite gap

↑ **Parent:** [Pressure gradient in a translating and squeezing finite gap](pressure-gradient-in-a-translating-and-squeezing-finite-gap.md)

The horizontal force on the lower wall vanishes when

$$
U=6V\frac{
\langle xh^{-2}\rangle
-\langle h^{-2}\rangle\langle xh^{-3}\rangle/
\langle h^{-3}\rangle}
{4\langle h^{-1}\rangle
-3\langle h^{-2}\rangle^2/\langle h^{-3}\rangle}.
$$
