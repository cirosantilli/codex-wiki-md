# Learning hedge in a binary-drift investment model

↑ **Parent:** [Intertemporal hedging demand](intertemporal-hedging-demand.md)

With the [binary Brownian drift filter](binary-brownian-drift-filter.md), a stock of observed excess drift $b(x)=\mu-r+\sigma a\tanh(ax)$ is driven by the same [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) as its information state $X$. The [Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman equation](hamilton-jacobi-bellman-equation.md) for [portfolio wealth](portfolio-wealth.md) $w$, dollar stock holding $\theta$ and value $J(w,x)$ includes $\sigma\theta J_{wx}$. Consequently $\theta^*=-[b(x)J_w+\sigma J_{wx}]/(\sigma^2J_{ww})$. For [CRRA utility](constant-relative-risk-aversion-utility.md), [homogeneity](homogeneity.md) gives $J=K(x)w^{1-R}/(1-R)$ and hence the displayed stock fraction. The second term is [intertemporal hedging demand](intertemporal-hedging-demand.md); replacing the unknown drift by its posterior mean while omitting this term does not generally give the optimal policy.
