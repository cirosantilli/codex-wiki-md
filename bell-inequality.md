# Bell inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Bell theorem](bell-theorem.md)

A [Bell inequality](bell-inequality.md) is a constraint on observable correlations satisfied by the specified class of local hidden-variable models. In finite-setting scenarios such constraints bound linear combinations of probabilities or expectation values. The [CHSH inequality](chsh-inequality.md) is one example, following from Bell factorization, measurement independence and bounded outcomes. A violation rules out the conjunction of the assumptions defining that model class; it does not alone identify a unique false premise.
