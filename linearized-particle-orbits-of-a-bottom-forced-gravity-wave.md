# Linearized particle orbits of a bottom-forced gravity wave

↑ **Parent:** [Surface gravity wave forced by a traveling bottom](surface-gravity-wave-forced-by-a-traveling-bottom.md)

For a [bottom-forced gravity wave](surface-gravity-wave-forced-by-a-traveling-bottom.md) with signed surface coefficient $b$, evaluate the first-order [velocity field](velocity-field.md) at a parcel's mean position $(\bar x,\bar y)$ and integrate, with $\Theta=k(\bar x-ct)$. The horizontal and vertical displacement coefficients are

$$
A=b\left[\frac g{kc^2}\cosh(k\bar y)+\sinh(k\bar y)\right],\qquad
B=b\left[\frac g{kc^2}\sinh(k\bar y)+\cosh(k\bar y)\right].
$$

Thus the [pathline](pathline.md) is an [ellipse](ellipse.md) with horizontal and vertical semiaxes $|A|,|B|$ whenever both are nonzero, and a line segment if one vanishes. The identity $A^2-B^2=b^2[(g/(kc^2))^2-1]$ determines which axis is longer. Also $B(0)=b$ and $B(-d)=a$, verifying the surface and bottom displacements. Closed first-order orbits do not rule out a higher-order mean drift.
