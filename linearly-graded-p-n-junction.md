# Linearly graded p-n junction

↑ **Parent:** [P-n junction](p-n-junction.md)

A [linearly graded p-n junction](linearly-graded-p-n-junction.md) has net ionized doping $N_D-N_A=\alpha x$ near the metallurgical junction. In the [depletion approximation](depletion-approximation.md), $\rho=e\alpha x$. With vanishing external field, neutrality places the depletion edges at $\pm w/2$ and integration of [Poisson equation](poisson-s-equation.md) gives

$$
E_x(x)=\frac{e\alpha}{2\varepsilon_s}\left(x^2-\frac{w^2}{4}\right).
$$

The field points from n to p and has largest magnitude $e\alpha w^2/(8\varepsilon_s)$ at $x=0$. The potential is cubic inside the layer.
