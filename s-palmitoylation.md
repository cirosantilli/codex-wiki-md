# S-palmitoylation

↑ **Parent:** [Protein](protein-split.md)

[S-palmitoylation](s-palmitoylation.md) attaches a palmitoyl group to a [cysteine](cysteine.md) side chain through a thioester linkage. Reversibility permits regulation of membrane association and trafficking; not every [G protein](g-protein.md) subunit uses the same acylation pattern.
