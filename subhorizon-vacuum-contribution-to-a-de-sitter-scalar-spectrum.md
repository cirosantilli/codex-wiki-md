# Subhorizon vacuum contribution to a de Sitter scalar spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Equal-time two-point function of a de Sitter scalar](equal-time-two-point-function-of-a-de-sitter-scalar.md)

The [equal-time two-point function of a de Sitter scalar](equal-time-two-point-function-of-a-de-sitter-scalar.md) has a contribution $P_{\rm vac}=1/(2ka^2)$, giving the displayed dimensionless [power spectrum](power-spectrum.md) per logarithmic [wavenumber](wavenumber.md). At fixed comoving [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) it decreases as $a^{-2}$; on short physical scales it reproduces the vacuum fluctuations of a [massless scalar field](massless-scalar-field.md) in [Minkowski spacetime](minkowski-spacetime.md). Its coincident integral is ultraviolet divergent, so the fixed-mode decrease cannot be interchanged with an unregulated integral over all modes.
