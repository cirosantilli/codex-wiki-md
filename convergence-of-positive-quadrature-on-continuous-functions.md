# Convergence of positive quadrature on continuous functions

↑ **Parent:** [Quadrature rule](quadrature-rule.md)

On a compact interval, positive [quadrature rule](quadrature-rule.md) weights whose sum is the interval length define uniformly bounded functionals on [continuous functions](continuous-function.md). If the rules eventually integrate every fixed [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) exactly, the [Weierstrass approximation theorem](weierstrass-approximation-theorem.md) proves convergence for every [continuous function](continuous-function.md).
