# Center equilibrium

↑ **Parent:** [Linear stability of a planar equilibrium](linear-stability-of-a-planar-equilibrium.md)

A center equilibrium of a planar [dynamical system](dynamical-system.md) is surrounded by closed trajectories. It is stable but not asymptotically stable.
