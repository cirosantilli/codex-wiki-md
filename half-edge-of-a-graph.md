# Half-edge of a graph

↑ **Parent:** [Configuration model](configuration-model.md)

A half-edge records an incidence of an [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) at a [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md). Two half-edges form each edge, including two incidences at the same vertex for a [self-loop](loop-graph-theory.md). In the [configuration model](configuration-model.md) these incidences are individually labelled before random pairing.
