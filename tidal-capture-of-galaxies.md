# Tidal capture of galaxies

↑ **Parent:** [Galaxy merger](galaxy-merger.md)

Tidal capture converts sufficient relative orbital energy into internal [tidal heating](tidal-heating.md) to leave two initially unbound [galaxies](galaxy-split.md) on a bound orbit. For identical spherical galaxies of mass $m_g$, a distant rapid flyby of [impact parameter](impact-parameter.md) $p$ and relative speed $v$ gives the approximate capture condition $pv<[(32/3)G^2m_g^2\langle r^2\rangle]^{1/4}$. This requires the [impulse approximation](impulse-approximation.md) and weak [gravitational focusing](gravitational-focusing.md); it is not a general criterion for subsequent coalescence.
