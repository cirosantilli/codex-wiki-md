# Metal-line cooling

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic line cooling](atomic-line-cooling.md)

Heavy elements provide many ionic transitions at temperatures where [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) and [helium](helium.md) have lost their most effective line emitters. [Metal-line cooling](metal-line-cooling.md) can therefore increase the plasma cooling function substantially and broaden its temperature features. Low-energy fine-structure transitions also permit cooling below the [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) atomic threshold when the relevant ions and [Electrons](electron.md) are present.
