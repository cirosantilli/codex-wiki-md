# Hermitian effect variational principle

↑ **Parent:** [Ky Fan maximum principle](ky-fan-maximum-principle.md)

For a finite-dimensional [Hermitian operator](hermitian-operator.md) $A$ with decreasing [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $\lambda_j$ and integer $0\leq r\leq d$, maximizing $\operatorname{Tr}(AB)$ over [positive contractions](positive-contraction.md) $B$ with [trace](matrix-trace.md) $r$ gives the sum of its leading $r$ [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md). In an [orthonormal eigenbasis](orthonormal-eigenbasis.md), the diagonal entries of $B$ lie in $[0,1]$ and sum to $r$. Moving their weight to the largest [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) can only increase the objective. An [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md) onto their [eigenvectors](eigenvector.md) attains the maximum.
