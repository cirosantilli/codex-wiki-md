# Beta-prime distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous probability distribution](continuous-probability-distribution-split.md)

For $a,b>0$, this [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) is obtained from a [Beta distribution](beta-distribution.md) variable $U$ by $X=U/(1-U)$. Indeed $u=x/(1+x)$ and $du/dx=(1+x)^{-2}$, and the [change of variables formula](change-of-variables-formula.md) gives the displayed density. In particular, the ratio of two [independent](independent-random-variables.md) equal-rate [exponential distributions](exponential-distribution.md) has $a=b=1$, density $(1+x)^{-2}$ and [cumulative distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md) $x/(1+x)$.
