# CPT completion of a supermultiplet

↑ **Parent:** [Supermultiplet](supermultiplet.md)

The [CPT theorem](cpt-theorem.md) reverses [helicity](helicity.md) and conjugates internal charges. If a [supermultiplet](supermultiplet.md) is not mapped into itself, a physical spectrum must also include its conjugate [supermultiplet](supermultiplet.md); this can double the irreducible state count.
