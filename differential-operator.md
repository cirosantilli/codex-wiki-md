# Differential operator

↑ **Parent:** [Analysis](analysis-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differential_operator)

A differential operator maps a [function](function-split.md) to an expression depending on finitely many of its [derivatives](derivative.md). A linear differential operator has the form $P=\sum_{|\alpha|\leq m}a_\alpha(x)\partial^\alpha$; its order is the largest derivative order with nonzero coefficient. The [Laplacian](laplacian.md) is a second-order example, and [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md) specify the domain when defining an operator on a [function space](function-space.md).
