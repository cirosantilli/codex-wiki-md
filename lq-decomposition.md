# LQ decomposition

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix decomposition](matrix-decomposition.md)

An [LQ decomposition](lq-decomposition.md) factors a real $m\times n$ matrix into a lower trapezoidal $m\times n$ matrix $L$ and an orthogonal $n\times n$ matrix $Q$. Transposing a [QR decomposition](qr-decomposition.md) of $A^T$ proves existence. Equivalently, right [Householder transformations](householder-transformation.md) clear the part of each row beyond its diagonal while preserving earlier zeros. This factorization is useful for underdetermined systems, complementing the role of [QR decomposition](qr-decomposition.md) in overdetermined systems.
