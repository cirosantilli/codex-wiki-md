# Radial vorticity conservation in a shearing sheet

↑ **Parent:** [Absolute-vorticity flux tensor](absolute-vorticity-flux-tensor.md)

When the only nonpotential force points radially, its [curl](curl.md) has no radial component. The radial [absolute vorticity](absolute-vorticity.md) therefore obeys a local flux [conservation law](conservation-law.md), although [vortex stretching](vortex-stretching.md) can change it along a fluid trajectory.
