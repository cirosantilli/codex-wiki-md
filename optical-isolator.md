# Optical isolator

↑ **Parent:** [Optical instrument](optical-instrument-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optical_isolator)

An [optical isolator](optical-isolator.md) transmits [light](light.md) preferentially in one direction. An ideal [Faraday effect](faraday-effect.md) device combines a $45^\circ$ rotator with [polarizers](polarizer.md) at $0^\circ$ and $45^\circ$: forward [light](light.md) passes, while backward [light](light.md) acquires another $45^\circ$ rotation and reaches the entrance [polarizer](polarizer.md) at $90^\circ$. See [Faraday rotation on a round trip](faraday-rotation-on-a-round-trip.md).
