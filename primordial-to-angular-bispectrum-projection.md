# Primordial-to-angular bispectrum projection

↑ **Parent:** [Reduced CMB bispectrum](reduced-cmb-bispectrum.md)

Linear cosmological transfer maps the [primordial bispectrum](primordial-bispectrum.md) to the [reduced CMB bispectrum](reduced-cmb-bispectrum.md). A Fourier representation of the momentum delta function and three [Rayleigh plane-wave expansions](rayleigh-plane-wave-expansion.md) separate the radial transfer integrals from a [Gaunt integral](gaunt-integral.md). The six angular factors and three Fourier measures give $(4\pi)^6/(2\pi)^9=(2/\pi)^3$. The observer-position phase cancels by momentum conservation; the remaining radial position is an auxiliary integration variable.
