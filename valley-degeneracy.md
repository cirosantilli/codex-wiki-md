# Valley degeneracy

↑ **Parent:** [Electronic valley](electronic-valley.md)

The number of symmetry-related equivalent [electronic valleys](electronic-valley.md) contributing to a specified low-[energy](energy-split.md) [energy band](energy-band.md). It multiplies the single-valley [density of states](density-of-states.md), independently of [spin degeneracy](spin-degeneracy.md). Equal electron and hole [effective masses](band-effective-mass.md) alone do not ensure equal total [density of states](density-of-states.md) values unless their [valley degeneracies](valley-degeneracy.md) and [spin degeneracies](spin-degeneracy.md) also agree.
