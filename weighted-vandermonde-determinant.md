# Weighted Vandermonde determinant

↑ **Parent:** [Vandermonde determinant](vandermonde-determinant.md)

For a nonnegative weight $w$ and [monic polynomials](monic-polynomial.md) $p_j$ of degree $j$, this [determinant](determinant.md) is $\prod_i\sqrt{w(x_i)}\prod_{i<j}(x_j-x_i)$. Its square is the product of the weights times the squared [Vandermonde determinant](vandermonde-determinant.md). For normalized weighted [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) it differs by the product of their norm factors.
