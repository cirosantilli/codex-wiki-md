# Integer surgery coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Dehn surgery on a framed link](dehn-surgery-on-a-framed-link.md)

Relative to the [meridian of a knot](meridian-of-a-knot.md) $\mu$ and [Seifert longitude](seifert-longitude.md) $\lambda$, an integer surgery coefficient $n$ means that the filling [meridian of a solid torus](meridian-of-a-solid-torus.md) is identified with $n\mu+\lambda$.
