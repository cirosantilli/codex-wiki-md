# Spectral radiant exitance

↑ **Parent:** [Radiant exitance](radiant-exitance.md)

Radiant [power](power.md) leaving a surface per surface [area](surface-area.md) and per unit [frequency](frequency.md) or [wavelength](wavelength.md). Isotropic cavity radiation obeys $M_\nu=cU_\nu/4$; the factor $1/4$ combines the outward hemisphere and projected area.
