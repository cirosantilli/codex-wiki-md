# Euler product for the Dedekind zeta function

↑ **Parent:** [Dedekind zeta function](dedekind-zeta-function.md)

Unique factorization of nonzero ideals and multiplicativity of the ideal norm give the formal identity

$$
\zeta_K(s)=\prod_{\mathfrak p}
\left(1-N(\mathfrak p)^{-s}\right)^{-1},
$$

where the product ranges over nonzero prime ideals.
