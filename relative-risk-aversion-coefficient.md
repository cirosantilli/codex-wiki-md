# Relative risk aversion coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Risk aversion](risk-aversion.md)

For positive wealth and increasing twice differentiable [utility function](utility-function-split.md), this dimensionless coefficient measures sensitivity to proportional wealth risk. If it is constant, integration gives $U'(W)=cW^{-\alpha}$ with $c>0$, and hence $U(W)=cW^{1-\alpha}/(1-\alpha)+d$ for $\alpha\ne1$ or $c\log W+d$ for $\alpha=1$. Positive, zero and negative coefficients correspond respectively to [risk aversion](risk-aversion.md), [risk neutrality](risk-neutrality.md) and [risk seeking](risk-seeking.md). The usual [CRRA utility](constant-relative-risk-aversion-utility.md) subclass takes $\alpha>0$.
