# Uniform-spanning-tree path representation of loop-erased random walk

↑ **Parent:** [Loop-erased random walk](loop-erased-random-walk.md)

On a finite connected unweighted [graph](graph-split.md), the [graph path](path-in-a-graph.md) between $x$ and $y$ in a [uniform spanning tree](uniform-spanning-tree.md) has the law of [loop-erased random walk](loop-erased-random-walk.md) from $x$ to $y$. Root [Wilson's algorithm](wilson-s-algorithm.md) at $y$ and process $x$ first: the first attached [graph path](path-in-a-graph.md) is exactly that [loop erasure](loop-erasure.md), and later attachments cannot change it. Changing the root to $x$ proves [reversibility of loop-erased random walk](reversibility-of-loop-erased-random-walk.md), even though chronological [loop erasure](loop-erasure.md) does not commute with reversing each individual random-walk trajectory.
