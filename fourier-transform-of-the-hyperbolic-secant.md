# Fourier transform of the hyperbolic secant

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md)

For real $k$, integrate $e^{-ikz}/\cosh z$ around a rectangle of height $\pi$. Its top-edge value is $-e^{\pi k}$ times the bottom-edge value, and the only enclosed [simple pole](simple-pole.md) is $i\pi/2$, with [residue](residue.md) $e^{\pi k/2}/i$. The vertical-edge integrals vanish as their real parts tend to positive and negative infinity. The [residue theorem](residue-theorem.md) gives $(1+e^{\pi k})\widetilde f(k)=2\pi e^{\pi k/2}$, proving the stated [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md) identity. This is an example of a function reproducing its shape under a [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md), up to rescaling.
