# Prime-to-characteristic torsion of an abelian variety

↑ **Parent:** [Abelian variety](abelian-variety-split.md)

If $N$ is invertible in the base [field](field.md), multiplication by $N$ on an [abelian variety](abelian-variety-split.md) is finite étale: its differential is $N$ times the identity, and properness turns its zero-dimensional fibres into finite fibres. The [Theorem of the Cube](theorem-of-the-cube.md) gives $[N]^*L\cong L^{N^2}$ for a symmetric [ample line bundle](ample-line-bundle.md). Top [intersection products](intersection-product-of-cartier-divisors.md) give degree $N^{2\dim A}$. For each prime power $\ell^r\mid N$, the kernels killed by $\ell^s$ have orders $\ell^{2s\dim A}$; the classification of [finite abelian groups](finite-abelian-group.md) forces $2\dim A$ cyclic factors of length $r$. Combining the prime powers gives the displayed group.
