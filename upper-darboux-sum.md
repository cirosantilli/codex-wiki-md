# Upper Darboux sum

↑ **Parent:** [Darboux sum](darboux-sum.md)

For a bounded function $f$ and a [partition of an interval](partition-of-an-interval.md) $P$, the upper Darboux sum multiplies the [supremum](supremum.md) of $f$ on each subinterval by its length and adds the results.
