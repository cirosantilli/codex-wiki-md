# Lie algebra representation homomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Lie algebra representation](lie-algebra-representation.md)

A linear map between [Lie algebra representations](lie-algebra-representation.md) is a representation homomorphism when it commutes with the action of every element. Its [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) and [image of a linear map](image-of-a-linear-map.md) are invariant subspaces. This is a map of modules, distinct from a [Lie algebra homomorphism](lie-algebra-homomorphism.md) between the acting algebras themselves.
