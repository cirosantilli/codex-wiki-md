# Friedel oscillation wavelength from carrier density

↑ **Parent:** [Friedel oscillations](friedel-oscillations.md)

A single isotropic three-dimensional valley with two equal [spin](spin.md) populations has $n=k_F^3/(3\pi^2)$. A density oscillation at [wavevector](wavevector.md) $2k_F$ therefore has the wavelength in the title. This depends on [carrier density](carrier-density.md), independently of effective mass and dielectric screening strength. Extra valley degeneracy, spin polarization or reduced dimensionality changes the state-counting relation.
