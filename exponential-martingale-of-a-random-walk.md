# Exponential martingale of a random walk

↑ **Parent:** [Random walk](random-walk.md)

For a [random walk](random-walk.md) with [independent and identically distributed random variables](independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md) as increments and finite [moment-generating function](moment-generating-function.md) at $\theta$, put $\kappa(\theta)=\log\mathbb E e^{\theta X_1}$. Then $e^{\theta S_n-n\kappa(\theta)}$ is a [martingale](martingale-split.md) by conditioning on the next independent increment. In particular, if $r>0$ and $\mathbb E r^{X_1}=1$, then $r^{S_n}$ is a [martingale](martingale-split.md). Stopping at a finite interval exit can make this process bounded, allowing the [dominated convergence theorem](dominated-convergence-theorem.md) to justify passage from [bounded stopping times](bounded-stopping-time.md) to the exit time.
