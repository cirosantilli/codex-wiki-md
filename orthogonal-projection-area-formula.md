# Orthogonal projection area formula

↑ **Parent:** [Surface area](surface-area.md)

For projection of a planar region with [unit normal](unit-normal.md) $\mathbf n$ onto a [plane](plane.md) with [unit normal](unit-normal.md) $\mathbf k$, the projected [area](surface-area.md) is $|\mathbf n\cdot\mathbf k|$ times the original [area](surface-area.md). Indeed, the signed projected [area](surface-area.md) of the [parallelogram](parallelogram.md) spanned by [tangent vectors](tangent-vector-split.md) $\mathbf u,\mathbf v$ is $(\mathbf u\times\mathbf v)\cdot\mathbf k$, while $\mathbf u\times\mathbf v$ is parallel to $\mathbf n$. Taking magnitudes proves the factor. The same pointwise formula applies to a [parametrized surface](parametrized-surface.md); adding projected patches counts multiplicity unless their images overlap only in sets of zero [area](surface-area.md).
