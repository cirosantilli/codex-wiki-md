# Affine chart of a variety

↑ **Parent:** [Affine open subscheme](affine-open-subscheme.md)

An open subset of an [algebraic variety](algebraic-variety.md) equipped with an identification with an [affine variety](affine-algebraic-set.md). Such charts make [quasi-coherent sheaves](quasi-coherent-sheaf.md) and regular maps accessible through their local [coordinate rings](coordinate-ring.md).
