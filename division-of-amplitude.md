# Division of amplitude

↑ **Parent:** [Interferometer](interferometer.md)

In [division of amplitude](division-of-amplitude.md), a partially reflecting interface sends parts of the same incident [wavefront](wavefront.md) into separate paths that are later recombined. A [Michelson interferometer](michelson-interferometer.md) is an example. The resulting [interference visibility](interferometric-visibility.md) depends on relative [optical path length](optical-path-length.md), [polarization of waves](polarization-waves.md), intensity balance and [optical coherence](optical-coherence.md).
