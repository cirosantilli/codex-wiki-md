# Archimedean ordered group

↑ **Parent:** [Linearly ordered group](linearly-ordered-group.md)

An [ordered group](partially-ordered-group-split.md) is Archimedean when $a\ge1$ and $a^n\le b$ for all positive [integers](integer.md) force $a=1$. In an additive [Abelian group](abelian-group.md) this excludes a positive element whose every integer multiple stays below one fixed bound. This generalizes the [Archimedean property](archimedean-property.md) of the [real numbers](real-number.md).
