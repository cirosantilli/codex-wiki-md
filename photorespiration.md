# Photorespiration

↑ **Parent:** [Photosynthesis](photosynthesis.md)

[Photorespiration](photorespiration.md) is the metabolic route processing products of Rubisco oxygenation and recovering part of their carbon. It consumes energy and releases some carbon dioxide; under particular conditions it also provides a sink for energy and reducing power.
