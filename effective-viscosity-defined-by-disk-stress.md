# Effective viscosity defined by disk stress

↑ **Parent:** [Vertically averaged viscous disk equations](vertically-averaged-viscous-disk-equations.md)

For cylindrical rotation independent of height, write $W_{r\phi}=\int T_{r\phi}dz$. Define the effective [kinematic viscosity](kinematic-viscosity.md) by $W_{r\phi}=\bar\nu\Sigma r\Omega'$, where $\Sigma$ is the [surface density of a disk](surface-density-of-a-disk.md). For Keplerian rotation, $\bar\nu=-2W_{r\phi}/(3\Sigma\Omega)$, and the outward [viscous torque in an accretion disk](viscous-torque-in-an-accretion-disk.md) is $-2\pi r^2W_{r\phi}=3\pi\bar\nu\Sigma\sqrt{GM r}$. A [Newtonian fluid stress tensor](newtonian-fluid-stress-tensor.md) gives the [density-weighted viscosity of a disk](density-weighted-viscosity-of-a-disk.md); the stress definition can also parameterize an effective turbulent or magnetic transport stress.
