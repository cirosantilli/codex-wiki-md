# Ambident nucleophile

↑ **Parent:** [Nucleophile](nucleophile.md)

An [ambident nucleophile](ambident-nucleophile.md) can form a bond through more than one atom. An [enolate](enolate.md) can attack through [carbon](carbon.md) or [oxygen](oxygen.md); these give distinct products, although its resonance contributors describe the same ion.
