# Newton identities in positive characteristic

↑ **Parent:** [Newton's identities](newton-s-identities.md)

The [polynomial identities](polynomial-identity.md) remain true in positive [characteristic of a field](characteristic-of-a-field.md), but recovering [elementary symmetric polynomials](elementary-symmetric-polynomial.md) by division can fail. In the [prime field](prime-field.md) $\mathbb F_2$ of [characteristic two](characteristic-two.md), the root lists $(0,0)$ and $(1,1)$ have the same power sum zero for every positive exponent: $0^k+0^k=0$ and $1^k+1^k=0$. Their [monic polynomials](monic-polynomial.md) differ, however: $z^2$ and $(z-1)^2=z^2+1$. Thus **even all positive power sums need not determine the polynomial** when its degree meets the characteristic. The relation $2e_2=e_1p_1-p_2$ becomes $0=0$ and supplies no value for $e_2$.
