# Decay-rate asymmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Decay width](decay-width.md)

The normalized difference of two [decay rates](decay-width.md). An asymmetry between different final states is not automatically a test of [CP violation](cp-violation.md); the physical meaning depends on which rates are compared.
