# Fifty Years of X-ray Diffraction, edited by Paul Peter Ewald

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)

Edited by [Paul Peter Ewald](paul-peter-ewald.md), credited as P. P. Ewald; the volume contains contributions by multiple authors. Published for the [International Union of Crystallography](international-union-of-crystallography.md) in 1962 by N.V.A. Oosthoek’s Uitgeversmaatschappij, Utrecht. The International Union of Crystallography hosts the 1999 digitization; neither editorial credit nor institutional issuance is whole-book authorship.

Official volume: [https://www.iucr.org/what-we-do/publications/books/Fifty-Years-of-X-ray-Diffraction](https://www.iucr.org/what-we-do/publications/books/Fifty-Years-of-X-ray-Diffraction)
