# Enhanced internal symmetry of free Dirac fields

↑ **Parent:** [Unitary flavour symmetry of degenerate Dirac fields](unitary-flavour-symmetry-of-degenerate-dirac-fields.md)

Without fixed gauge charges or interactions distinguishing particles from antiparticles, $N$ free [Dirac fields](dirac-field.md) give $2N$ left-handed [Weyl fields](weyl-field.md) after [charge conjugation](charge-conjugation.md) of the right-handed fields. Their massless kinetic term has [unitary group](unitary-group.md) $U(2N)$. Equal nonzero [Dirac masses](dirac-mass-term.md) have equal positive singular values and can be written as $2N$ equal [Majorana mass terms](majorana-mass-term.md); their preserving group is the [orthogonal group](orthogonal-group.md) $O(2N)$. Explicitly, in a basis with symmetric [mass matrix](mass-matrix.md) $m\mathbf1$, invariance requires $V^\dagger V=V^TV=\mathbf1$, hence real $V$. Restricting to transformations preserving a chosen common [fermion number](fermion-number.md) gives the usual $U(N)_L\times U(N)_R$ at zero [mass](mass.md) and $U(N)$ at nonzero [mass](mass.md).
