# Luminous flux

↑ **Parent:** [Photometry (optics)](photometry-optics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luminous_flux)

[Luminous flux](luminous-flux.md) is the visually weighted counterpart of [radiant flux](radiant-flux.md), measured in lumens. Its value depends on the [optical spectrum](optical-spectrum.md) and the specified visual response; it is distinct from [radiant flux](radiant-flux.md) in [watts](watt.md).[Radiometry and Photometry: Review for Vision Optics](osa-handbook-of-optics-volume-iii-visual-optics-and-vision/radiometry-and-photometry-review-for-vision-optics.md)
