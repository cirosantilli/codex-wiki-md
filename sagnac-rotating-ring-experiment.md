# Sagnac rotating-ring experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Sagnac interferometer](sagnac-interferometer.md)

In 1913 [Georges Sagnac](georges-sagnac.md) mounted a lamp, [collimator](collimator.md), [beam splitter](beam-splitter.md), four [mirrors](mirror.md) and [photographic plate](photographic-plate.md) on a horizontal rotating platform. The [mirrors](mirror.md) defined a closed polygon; the two beams traversed it in opposite senses. A slight [beam splitter](beam-splitter.md) adjustment produced visible [interference fringes](interference-fringe.md). A motor brought the platform to steady [rotation](rotation-mathematics.md), and switching the lamp exposed the plate. Comparing opposite senses of [rotation](rotation-mathematics.md) revealed a reversing displacement. Changing the [beam splitter](beam-splitter.md) adjustment supplied a further check against optical deformation. The report compared measured and calculated displacements. [Georges Sagnac](georges-sagnac.md) interpreted this as evidence for a [luminiferous aether](luminiferous-aether.md); the relativistic derivation below reproduces the delay without that hypothesis.[Georges Sagnac, 1913 second report, inspected in William Lonc's 2008 English translation, pp. 77–80, §§1–3: rotating apparatus, photographic procedure, reversal and deformation controls, comparison table, historical interpretation](regarding-the-proof-for-the-existence-of-a-luminiferous-ether-using-a-rotating-inteferometer-experiment-by-georges-sagnac.md)

The readout measures a relative [wave phase](phase-waves.md), not two stopwatch readings separated by an optical transit time. Reversal isolates a contribution odd in [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md): if a signed [interference fringe](interference-fringe.md) position is $q(\Omega)=q_0+k\Omega$ to leading order, then $[q(\Omega)-q(-\Omega)]/2=k\Omega$ cancels the fixed offset $q_0$. Changes of alignment or [temperature](temperature-split.md) correlated with reversal can survive this subtraction. Thus the experimental inference concerns a reproducible rotating-loop [phase difference](phase-difference.md), with controls for disturbances; it is not by itself a unique test of an underlying propagation hypothesis.
