# Geometric pair-number distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Particle-antiparticle two-mode squeezed vacuum](particle-antiparticle-two-mode-squeezed-vacuum.md)

The pair count of a two-mode squeezed [vacuum](vacuum.md) has $P(n)=(1-r)r^n$, where $r=|q|^2<1$. Its mean is $r/(1-r)$ and its [variance](variance-split.md) is $r/(1-r)^2$. Equal mode occupations follow from pair creation, not from independent [particle](particle-physics.md) and [antiparticle](antiparticle.md) draws.
