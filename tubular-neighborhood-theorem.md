# Tubular neighborhood theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Tubular neighborhood](tubular-neighborhood.md)

A smooth embedded [submanifold](submanifold.md) has a neighborhood diffeomorphic to a neighborhood of the [zero section](zero-section-of-a-vector-bundle.md) of its [normal bundle](normal-bundle.md), with the [zero section](zero-section-of-a-vector-bundle.md) identified with the [submanifold](submanifold.md). One may prescribe the normal-bundle splitting of the derivative along that section. Choose a [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md) making the splitting orthogonal and use its normal exponential map: its derivative there is the required [isomorphism](isomorphism.md), so the [inverse function theorem](inverse-function-theorem.md) supplies local [diffeomorphisms](diffeomorphism.md). A sufficiently small, possibly varying, fibre radius makes these fit into a [tubular neighborhood](tubular-neighborhood.md).
