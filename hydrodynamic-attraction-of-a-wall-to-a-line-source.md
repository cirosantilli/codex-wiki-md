# Hydrodynamic attraction of a wall to a line source

↑ **Parent:** [Line source](line-source.md)

In the upper [half-plane](half-plane.md), a [line source](line-source.md) of outward [volume flux](volumetric-flow-rate.md) $Q$ at height $a$ above an impermeable wall has an equal image source below it. On the wall $u=Qx/[\pi(x^2+a^2)]$ and the normal [velocity](velocity.md) vanishes. The steady [Bernoulli equation](bernoulli-equation.md) gives $p-p_\infty=-\rho Q^2x^2/[2\pi^2(x^2+a^2)^2]$. Since

$$
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{x^2}{(x^2+a^2)^2}\,dx=\frac\pi{2a},
$$

the ambient-relative [force per unit length](force-per-unit-length.md) pulls the wall toward the source with the displayed magnitude. The [torque](torque.md) about the source's projection onto the wall is zero in the symmetric [Cauchy principal value](cauchy-principal-value.md) sense. Individual one-sided moments diverge logarithmically. This planar result differs from the three-dimensional [hydrodynamic attraction of a plane wall to a point source](hydrodynamic-attraction-of-a-plane-wall-to-a-point-source.md).
