# Electric susceptibility

↑ **Parent:** [Dielectric](dielectric.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electric_susceptibility)

The [dimensionless quantity](dimensionless-quantity.md) relating induced [electric polarization](polarization-density.md) to the internal [electric field](electric-field.md) through $\mathbf P=\epsilon_0\chi_e\mathbf E$ in the static scalar model. It describes the material contribution; [relative permittivity](relative-permittivity.md) includes the [vacuum](vacuum.md) term as well, $\epsilon_r=1+\chi_e$. Zero [electric susceptibility](electric-susceptibility.md) gives $\epsilon_r=1$, not zero [permittivity](permittivity.md).
