# Syncope guidelines by the European Society of Cardiology (2018)

↑ **Parent:** [Clinical medicine bibliography](clinical-medicine-bibliography-split.md)

[https://academic.oup.com/eurheartj/article/39/21/1883/4939241](https://academic.oup.com/eurheartj/article/39/21/1883/4939241)

Primary society guideline. §3.1 defines [syncope](syncope.md) through transient loss of consciousness from cerebral [perfusion](perfusion.md) failure, with rapid onset, brief duration and spontaneous recovery. Cited for the distinction from the [cardiac affections of Aretaeus](cardiac-affections-of-aretaeus.md).
