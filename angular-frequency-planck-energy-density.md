# Angular-frequency Planck energy density

↑ **Parent:** [Black-body radiation](black-body-radiation.md)

The bulk [photon](photon.md) mode [density](density.md) per [volume](volume.md) per [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) is $\omega^2/(\pi^2c^3)$, including two transverse polarizations. Multiply it by the thermal oscillator [energy](energy-split.md) $\hbar\omega/(e^{\hbar\omega/(k_BT)}-1)$ to obtain the displayed spectrum. Its integral is $u=\pi^2k_B^4T^4/(15\hbar^3c^3)$. This is [volume](volume.md) [energy](energy-split.md) [density](density.md) rather than [radiance](radiance.md) or surface exitance; isotropic equilibrium radiation has outward black-surface flux $cu/4$. [zero-point energy](zero-point-energy.md) is excluded from this thermal spectrum.
