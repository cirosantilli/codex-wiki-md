# BCS quasiparticle

↑ **Parent:** [BCS quasiparticle spectrum](bcs-quasiparticle-spectrum.md)

An elementary excitation of the paired mean-field state whose [electron](electron.md) operator has mixed creation and annihilation components. Its positive excitation [energy](energy-split.md) is $E_k=\sqrt{\xi_k^2+|\Delta_k|^2}$. At zero [temperature](temperature-split.md) the [BCS ground state](bcs-ground-state.md) has zero occupation of these positive-energy modes, even though physical [electrons](electron.md) have a smooth nontrivial [momentum](momentum-split.md) occupation.
