# Brownian sphere-hitting probability in dimension three

↑ **Parent:** [Transience of Brownian motion in dimension at least three](transience-of-brownian-motion-in-dimension-at-least-three.md)

For standard three-dimensional [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) started at $x$ with $r<|x|<R$,

$$
\mathbb P_x(\tau_r<\tau_R)=\frac{|x|^{-1}-R^{-1}}{r^{-1}-R^{-1}}.
$$

The [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md) $x\mapsto1/|x|$ gives a bounded [stopped martingale](stopped-martingale.md) in the annulus, so the [optional stopping theorem](optional-sampling-theorem-for-a-supermartingale.md) gives the formula. Taking $R\to\infty$ yields $\mathbb P_x(\tau_r<\infty)=r/|x|$ for $|x|>r$. This limit uses path continuity on the finite interval before a hit, rather than assuming transience to prove transience.
