<h1 id="lubin-tate-change-of-series">Lubin–Tate change of series</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Lubin–Tate formal group](lubin-tate-formal-group.md)

If $f$ and $g$ are Lubin–Tate series for the same [uniformizer](uniformizer.md), there is a unique strict isomorphism $h:\mathcal F_f\to\mathcal F_g$ satisfying $h\circ f=g\circ h$ and $h(X)\equiv X\pmod{X^2}$.
