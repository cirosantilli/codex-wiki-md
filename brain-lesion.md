# Brain lesion

↑ **Parent:** [Brain](brain.md)

Damage to a region of the [brain](brain.md). An observed deficit can reflect local tissue loss, disconnected pathways or remote dysfunction; preserved performance can reflect redundant routes or compensation. Lesion extent and appropriate sensory and motor controls matter for [causal inference](causal-inference-split.md).
