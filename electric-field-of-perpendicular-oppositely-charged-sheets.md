# Electric field of perpendicular oppositely charged sheets

↑ **Parent:** [Electric field](electric-field-split.md)

Infinite sheets at $x=0$ and $y=0$ with [surface charge density](surface-charge-density.md) $+\sigma$ and $-\sigma$ have piecewise uniform [electric fields](electric-field-split.md). [Gauss's law](gauss-s-law.md) and [superposition](superposition-principle.md) give the displayed result outside the sheets. The [electric field lines](electric-field-line.md) bisect each quadrant and connect the positive sheet to the negative one. Its magnitude is $\sigma/(\sqrt2\epsilon_0)$.
