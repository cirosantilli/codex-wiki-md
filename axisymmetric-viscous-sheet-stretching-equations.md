# Axisymmetric viscous-sheet stretching equations

↑ **Parent:** [Viscous sheet](viscous-sheet.md)

For thickness $h(r,t)$, radial velocity $u(r,t)$ and external pressure $p_{\rm ext}$, the leading [Newtonian fluid stress tensor](newtonian-fluid-stress-tensor.md) and [incompressibility](incompressible-flow.md) give

$$
\sigma_{rr}=-p_{\rm ext}+2\mu(2u_r+u/r),\qquad
\sigma_{\theta\theta}=-p_{\rm ext}+2\mu(u_r+2u/r).
$$

Radial [force balance](force-balance.md) on an annular sector includes the inward projection of hoop [traction](traction.md) and external pressure on the sloping broad faces. Together with [conservation of mass](mass-conservation.md) it yields

$$
2\mu\left[\partial_r(2rhu_r+hu)-h(2u/r+u_r)\right]=rh\,\partial_rp_{\rm ext},\qquad
h_t+r^{-1}\partial_r(rhu)=0.
$$
