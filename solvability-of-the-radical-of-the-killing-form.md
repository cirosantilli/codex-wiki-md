# Solvability of the radical of the Killing form

↑ **Parent:** [Killing form](killing-form.md)

The radical $\mathfrak g^\perp=\{x:\kappa(x,\mathfrak g)=0\}$ of the Killing form is a solvable ideal. Invariance makes it an ideal, and the [Cartan solvability criterion](cartan-solvability-criterion.md) applied to its adjoint image proves solvability.
