# Tait coloring

↑ **Parent:** [Edge coloring](edge-coloring.md)

A proper three-color [edge coloring](edge-coloring.md) of a [cubic graph](cubic-graph.md). Label its colors by the nonzero elements of $\mathbb F_2^2$: the incident colors at each [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) then sum to zero. On a planar map without [bridges in a graph](bridge-graph-theory.md), integrating these labels on the [dual graph](dual-graph.md) gives a four-color face coloring. On a [torus](torus.md), a nonzero period can obstruct this integration.
