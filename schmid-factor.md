# Schmid factor

↑ **Parent:** [Schmid's law](schmid-s-law.md)

The orientation factor converting the magnitude of uniaxial [stress](stress.md) to [resolved shear stress](resolved-shear-stress.md). It lies between zero and $1/2$ because the slip-plane normal and slip direction are perpendicular.
