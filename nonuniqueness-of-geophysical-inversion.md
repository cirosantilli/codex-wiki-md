# Nonuniqueness of geophysical inversion

↑ **Parent:** [Inverse problem](inverse-problem-split.md)

Different subsurface models can produce the same measured field. A [gravity anomaly](gravity-anomaly.md), for example, does not uniquely separate [density](density.md), body shape and regional background. Geological constraints and complementary observations reduce this ambiguity; an inferred [density](density.md) should retain the model and reference-field assumptions used to obtain it.
