# Exponential divided difference

↑ **Parent:** [Divided difference](divided-difference.md)

The displayed integral is $(e^{t\beta}-e^{t\gamma})/(\beta-\gamma)$ for distinct exponents and $te^{t\gamma}$ when they coincide. The latter is the continuous limit, so a zero denominator is a removable singularity. This quantity appears in [variation-of-constants formula](variation-of-constants-formula.md) estimates of [matrix](matrix.md) evolution and avoids omitting an equal-exponent case.
