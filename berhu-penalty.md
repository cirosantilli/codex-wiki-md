# Berhu penalty

↑ **Parent:** [Lasso](lasso.md)

The Berhu, or reverse-Huber, penalty with threshold $\delta>0$ is

$$
\rho_\delta(t)=
\begin{cases}
|t|,&|t|\leq\delta,\\
(t^2+\delta^2)/(2\delta),&|t|>\delta.
\end{cases}
$$

It behaves like the [absolute value](absolute-value.md) near zero and quadratically for large coefficients.
