# Trade winds

↑ **Parent:** [Terrestrial atmosphere](terrestrial-atmosphere.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trade_winds)

Persistent low-latitude surface winds with a westward component, associated with the lower branch of the [Hadley cell](hadley-cell.md). Their weakening or reversal in the equatorial Pacific can excite [equatorial Kelvin waves](equatorial-kelvin-wave.md) that contribute to the development of [El Niño](el-nino.md).
