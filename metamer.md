# Metamer

↑ **Parent:** [Metamerism (color)](metamerism-color.md)

A stimulus belonging to a pair of physically different [optical spectra](optical-spectrum.md) that match for a specified observer and viewing condition. Such a match exhibits [metamerism](metamerism-color.md). Changing the observer, adaptation or illumination can break the match.
