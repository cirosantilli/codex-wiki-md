# Lie group

↑ **Parent:** [Lie theory](lie-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lie_group)

A Lie group is a [group](group-split.md) that is also a [smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md), with smooth multiplication and inversion. Its tangent space at the identity carries a natural [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) structure.
