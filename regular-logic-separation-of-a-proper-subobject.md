# Regular-logic separation of a proper subobject

↑ **Parent:** [Regular category](regular-category.md)

For a [small category](small-category.md) that is a [regular category](regular-category.md), use a sort for each object, an operation for each [morphism](morphism.md), and [regular theory](regular-theory.md) axioms describing [finite limits](finite-limit.md) and [regular epimorphisms](regular-epimorphism.md). Its models are [regular functors](regular-functor.md) into the [Category of sets](category-of-sets.md). For a proper [subobject](subobject.md) $m:S\hookrightarrow A$, the sequent asserting $\forall x:A\,\exists y:S,\ m(y)=x$ cannot be derivable, since its categorical interpretation would make $m$ a cover and hence invertible. The [chase completeness for regular logic](chase-completeness-for-regular-logic.md) supplies a model where it fails. It is essential to allow empty sorts.
