# Adiabatic wall

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal contact](thermal-contact.md)

An adiabatic wall prevents [heat transfer](heat-transfer.md). Two bodies at $300\,\mathrm K$ and $350\,\mathrm K$ can remain separated by a rigid adiabatic wall with no [heat transfer](heat-transfer.md), although they are not in [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium-split.md) with each other. An adiabatic boundary alone need not make a system an [isolated system](isolated-system.md): a moving insulated piston can still transmit [work](work.md). Isolation additionally excludes matter transfer and every [energy](energy-split.md) exchange.
