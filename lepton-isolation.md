# Lepton isolation

↑ **Parent:** [Particle isolation](particle-isolation.md)

Lepton [isolation](lepton-isolation.md) restricts additional track [momentum](momentum-split.md) or [calorimeter](calorimeter-particle-physics.md) [energy](energy-split.md) near a reconstructed lepton. It helps distinguish prompt leptons from [jets](jet-particle-physics.md) and heavy-hadron decays. Detector acceptance, nearby signal particles and collision pileup affect its efficiency; a [isolated](particle-isolation.md) [photon](photon.md) uses a related [isolation](particle-isolation.md) requirement rather than being a lepton.
