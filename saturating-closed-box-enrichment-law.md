# Saturating closed-box enrichment law

↑ **Parent:** [Closed-box model of galactic chemical evolution](closed-box-model-of-galactic-chemical-evolution.md)

A finite-metal-fraction variant of the [closed-box model of galactic chemical evolution](closed-box-model-of-galactic-chemical-evolution.md) uses effective [stellar yield](stellar-yield.md) $Y(1-Z_g)$, rather than constant yield. With pristine initial gas and $Y>0$, metal accounting gives $dZ_g/d\log(1/\mu)=Y(1-Z_g)$ and hence $Z_g=1-\mu^Y$. The mass-weighted mean [stellar metallicity](stellar-metallicity.md) is $[Y-(Y+1)\mu+\mu^{Y+1}]/[(Y+1)(1-\mu)]$. At final gas fraction $\mu_f$, the [metallicity distribution function](metallicity-distribution-function.md) is $(1-z)^{1/Y-1}/[Y(1-\mu_f)]$ on $0<z<1-\mu_f^Y$. For trace abundances, $Z_g\simeq-Y\log\mu$ and the usual [closed-box metallicity distribution](closed-box-metallicity-distribution.md) is recovered to leading order.
