# Toric ampleness criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Ample Cartier divisor](ample-cartier-divisor.md)

In the notation of the [toric basepoint-free criterion](toric-basepoint-free-criterion.md), the divisor is ample exactly when $\langle m_\sigma,v_\rho\rangle>-a_\rho$ for each ray outside $\sigma$. Equivalently, the normal fan of its divisor polytope is the original fan.
