# Ergodic component

↑ **Parent:** [Ergodic decomposition](ergodic-decomposition.md)

A conditional probability of an invariant [probability measure](probability-measure.md) given its [invariant sigma-algebra](invariant-sigma-algebra.md). In a standard Borel probability system, almost every such probability is invariant and [ergodic](ergodicity.md). Their integral recovers the original measure. The [countable-test proof of ergodicity of conditional components](countable-test-proof-of-ergodicity-of-conditional-components.md) explains why invariance alone is not the whole conclusion.
