# Collision

↑ **Parent:** [Collision mechanics](collision-mechanics.md)

A collision is an interaction between approaching bodies or particles that changes their motion. [Momentum conservation](momentum-conservation.md) and [conservation of energy](conservation-of-energy.md) constrain the outcomes; [inelastic collisions](inelastic-collision.md) can transfer mechanical energy into internal degrees of freedom.
