# Schneider-Lang theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Transcendence theory](transcendence-theory.md)

Let $f_1,\ldots,f_s$ be meromorphic functions with $f_j'\in K[f_1,\ldots,f_s]$ for a fixed [number field](number-field.md) $K$. Suppose $f_1,f_2$ are algebraically independent over $\mathbb C$ and have finite orders $\rho_1,\rho_2$. The set $S$ of points where every $f_j$ is finite and belongs to $K$ satisfies the displayed bound. The orders are growth orders for meromorphic functions. For $f_1=z$, $f_2=e^z$, the orders are zero and one and the functions are algebraically independent; an algebraic value of $e^\alpha$ at nonzero algebraic $\alpha$ would produce infinitely many such points $n\alpha$.
