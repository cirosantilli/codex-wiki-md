# Hilbert Nullstellensatz

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic geometry](algebraic-geometry-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hilbert_Nullstellensatz)

For an [algebraically closed field](algebraically-closed-field.md) $k$ and an [ideal](ideal.md) $I\subseteq k[x_1,\ldots,x_n]$, the strong Hilbert Nullstellensatz is

$$
I(V(I))=\sqrt I.
$$

Its weak form says that an ideal with empty [affine zero set](affine-algebraic-set.md) is the unit ideal. Equivalently, if finitely many [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) have no common zero, a polynomial combination of them equals one.
