# Lens bound on a reconstructed Fermi pocket

↑ **Parent:** [Rectangular-lattice Fermi-surface reconstruction](rectangular-lattice-fermi-surface-reconstruction.md)

In [rectangular-lattice Fermi-surface reconstruction](rectangular-lattice-fermi-surface-reconstruction.md), the zero-coupling closed orbit is the overlap lens of two equal circles of radius $k_F$ with centres separated by $2K$. Two circular sectors minus their triangular portions give

$$
A_{\max}=2k_F^2\arccos\frac{K}{k_F}-2K\sqrt{k_F^2-K^2}.
$$

At fixed [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md), the upper hybridized [energy band](energy-band.md) rises when the magnitude of the [Fourier coefficient](fourier-coefficient.md) increases. Its occupied pocket therefore shrinks, so this lens is an upper bound in the two-wave model. It is not a universal bound for arbitrary changes of [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) or additional [energy bands](energy-band.md).
