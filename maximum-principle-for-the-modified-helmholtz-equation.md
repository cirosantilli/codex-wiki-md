# Maximum principle for the modified Helmholtz equation

↑ **Parent:** [Maximum principle](maximum-principle.md)

Let $\Omega$ be a bounded domain, with $u\in C^2(\Omega)\cap C(\overline\Omega)$ real valued and $c>0$. At a positive interior maximum, $\Delta u\leq0$ but $cu>0$, contradicting the equation. A negative interior minimum gives the opposite contradiction. Thus any value with absolute magnitude exceeding the boundary maximum would be impossible, proving the displayed bound. In particular zero boundary data force $u=0$, and subtracting two solutions proves uniqueness for the corresponding inhomogeneous Dirichlet problem.
