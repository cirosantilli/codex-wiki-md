# Spline (mathematics)

↑ **Parent:** [Uniform approximation](uniform-approximation-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spline_(mathematics))

A spline is a [piecewise polynomial function](piecewise-polynomial-function.md) whose polynomial pieces obey specified smoothness conditions at their joins, called [spline knots](spline-knot.md). For example, a cubic spline is cubic on each interval and commonly has two continuous derivatives across interior knots. [Spline approximation](spline-approximation.md) and [spline interpolation](spline-interpolation.md) use these finite-dimensional function spaces to represent data or approximate functions.
