# Jackson kernel

↑ **Parent:** [Jackson-type estimate](jackson-type-estimate.md)

The normalized even kernel

$$
J_n(t)=\frac{3}{2\pi n(2n^2+1)}\left(\frac{\sin(nt/2)}{\sin(t/2)}\right)^4
$$

is nonnegative, has integral one, and is a [trigonometric polynomial](trigonometric-polynomial.md) of degree $2(n-1)$. Its mass is concentrated on a scale $1/n$: $J_n(t)\leq C\min(n,n^{-3}|t|^{-4})$ for $|t|\leq\pi$. This controls a weighted second moment and produces the [Jackson operator estimate](jackson-operator-estimate.md).
