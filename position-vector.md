# Position vector

↑ **Parent:** [Position](position.md)

A [position vector](position-vector.md) is the [vector](vector.md) from a chosen origin to a point. Its [time](time-in-physics.md) derivative is the [velocity](velocity.md); differences of [position vectors](position-vector.md) give [displacement](displacement.md).
