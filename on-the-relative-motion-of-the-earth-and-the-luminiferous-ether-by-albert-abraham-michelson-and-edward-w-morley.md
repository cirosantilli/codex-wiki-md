# On the Relative Motion of the Earth and the Luminiferous Ether by Albert Abraham Michelson and Edward W. Morley

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)

Morley](edward-w-morley.md) describes an [interferometer](interferometer.md) experiment constraining the orientation dependence of light propagation.
