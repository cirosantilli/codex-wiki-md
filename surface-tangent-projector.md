# Surface tangent projector

↑ **Parent:** [Surface covariant derivative](surface-covariant-derivative.md)

The orthogonal projector onto the tangent plane of a surface with unit [normal vector](normal-vector.md) $\mathbf n$. It removes the normal component of a vector and defines the [surface gradient](surface-gradient.md) $\nabla_s=P\nabla$.
