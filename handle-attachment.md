# Handle attachment

↑ **Parent:** [Topological manifold](topological-manifold.md)

Attaching an index-$k$ handle to an $n$-dimensional [manifold with boundary](manifold-with-boundary.md) glues $D^k\times D^{n-k}$ along $\partial D^k\times D^{n-k}$. The complementary $D^k\times\partial D^{n-k}$ replaces the attaching region in the boundary.
