# Spectral inverse theorem for the matrix-valued U2 quantity

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix-valued Gowers U2 quantity](matrix-valued-gowers-u2-quantity.md)

If $\|f(x)\|_{\mathrm{op}}\leq1$ and $\|f\|_{U^2}^4\geq cn>0$, select the [singular values](singular-value.md) $\lambda_p\geq\sqrt{c/2}$ of the [matrix Fourier blocks](matrix-fourier-block.md). Their weighted count $m=\sum_pd_{\rho_p}$ lies between $cn/2$ and $2n/c$. Scaling unit [right singular vectors](right-singular-vector.md) and their corresponding unit [left singular vectors](left-singular-vector.md) by $\sqrt{d_{\rho_p}}$ gives matrices $U(p),V(p)$ satisfying $\mathbb E_xf(x)U(p)\rho_p(x)^*=\lambda_pV(p)$ and [Hilbert-Schmidt inner product](hilbert-schmidt-inner-product.md) orthogonality within each chosen [irreducible representation](irreducible-representation.md). The [Schur averaging of rectangular matrices](schur-averaging-of-rectangular-matrices.md) then gives $\mathbb E_x\|UP(x)^*b\|_2^2=\|b\|_2^2$ for the concatenated $U$ and the corresponding [block diagonal matrix](block-diagonal-matrix.md) $P(x)$.
