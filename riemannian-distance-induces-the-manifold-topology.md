# Riemannian distance induces the manifold topology

↑ **Parent:** [Riemannian distance](riemannian-distance.md)

In a relatively [compact](compact-space.md) coordinate ball the [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md) norm is bounded above and below by positive multiples of the Euclidean norm. A coordinate segment gives the upper distance bound. A path leaving the ball has a positive lower length bound before its first exit; paths staying in it have the Euclidean displacement lower bound. Consequently distinct points have positive [Riemannian distance](riemannian-distance.md), and the resulting [metric topology](metric-topology.md) equals the manifold topology. Connectedness makes the distance finite because [smooth manifolds](smooth-manifold.md) are locally path [connected](connected-space.md).
