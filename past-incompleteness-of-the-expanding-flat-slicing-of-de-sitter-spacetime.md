# Past incompleteness of the expanding flat slicing of de Sitter spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [Timelike geodesic in the expanding flat slicing of de Sitter spacetime](timelike-geodesic-in-the-expanding-flat-slicing-of-de-sitter-spacetime.md)

The geodesic with nonzero conserved spatial momentum reaches $t=-\infty$ after the finite past proper time

$$
\tau_-=-\frac1{2H}\log\frac{\sqrt{1+v_0^2}+1}{\sqrt{1+v_0^2}-1}.
$$

This is incompleteness of the expanding coordinate patch, whose geodesics extend smoothly in global [de Sitter spacetime](de-sitter-spacetime.md); it is not a curvature singularity.
