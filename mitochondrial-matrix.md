# Mitochondrial matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Mitochondria](mitochondria.md)

The [mitochondrial matrix](mitochondrial-matrix.md) is the compartment enclosed by the [inner mitochondrial membrane](inner-mitochondrial-membrane.md), containing mitochondrial [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md), [ribosomes](ribosome.md) and many [enzymes](enzyme-split.md). Most [citric acid cycle](citric-acid-cycle.md) steps occur here; [succinate dehydrogenase](succinate-dehydrogenase.md) is membrane-associated. The [citric acid cycle](citric-acid-cycle.md) supplies [NADH](nadh.md) for the [mitochondrial respiratory chain](mitochondrial-respiratory-chain.md).[Chapter 14, The Mitochondrion Contains an Outer Membrane, an Inner Membrane, and Two Internal Compartments; Figure 14-8, matrix enzymes and inner-membrane succinate dehydrogenase](molecular-biology-of-the-cell-4th-edition-by-bruce-alberts-and-others.md)

Distinguish the location of a [chemical reaction](chemical-reaction-split.md) from the location of its detection. An external [oxygen](oxygen.md) [electrode](electrode.md) can report consumption by an intact preparation without entering the [mitochondrial matrix](mitochondrial-matrix.md). That signal does not locate every intermediate, establish [protein targeting](protein-targeting.md) or directly count newly formed [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md).
