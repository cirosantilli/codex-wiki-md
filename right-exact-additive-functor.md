# Right-exact additive functor

↑ **Parent:** [Additive functor](additive-functor.md)

An [additive functor](additive-functor.md) between [abelian categories](abelian-category.md) is [right exact functor](right-exact-additive-functor.md) when it sends every exact sequence $X\to Y\to Z\to0$ to an exact sequence $FX\to FY\to FZ\to0$. Equivalently, it preserves [cokernels in a category](cokernel-in-a-category.md), since additivity already preserves finite [biproducts](biproduct.md). This is the convention used in constructing [left derived functors](left-derived-functor.md). Left exactness preserves the initial part $0\to X\to Y\to Z$ instead. Contravariant Hom becomes a [right exact functor](right-exact-additive-functor.md) covariant [functor](functor.md) with target the opposite [abelian category](abelian-category.md).

An [additive functor](additive-functor.md) between [abelian categories](abelian-category.md) is [right exact](right-exact-additive-functor.md) when it preserves [categorical cokernels](cokernel-in-a-category.md), equivalently when it sends every exact sequence ending in zero to an exact sequence ending in zero. Additivity and preservation of [cokernels in a category](cokernel-in-a-category.md) imply preservation of finite [colimits](colimit.md). The additivity assumption is part of the ordinary [left derived functor](left-derived-functor.md) construction.
