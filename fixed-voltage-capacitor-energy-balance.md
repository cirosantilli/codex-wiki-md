# Fixed-voltage capacitor energy balance

↑ **Parent:** [Capacitor energy](capacitor-energy.md)

For a quasistatic change of [capacitance](capacitance.md) at fixed voltage, the [capacitor energy](capacitor-energy.md) is $U=CV^2/2$ and charge is $Q=CV$. Electrical work supplied to the field is $V\,dQ$, so mechanical work is $dU-V\,dQ=-V^2dC/2$. Increasing the separation of parallel plates makes $dC<0$: mechanical work is positive even though field energy decreases, because electrical energy returns to the source.
