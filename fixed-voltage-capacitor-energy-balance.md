# Fixed-voltage capacitor energy balance

↑ **Parent:** [Capacitor energy](capacitor-energy.md)

For a quasistatic change of [capacitance](capacitance.md) at fixed terminal [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md), the [capacitor energy](capacitor-energy.md) is $U=CV^2/2$ and plate [electric charge](electric-charge.md) is $Q=CV$. Electrical [work](work.md) supplied to the [electric field](electric-field.md) by the [battery](electric-battery.md) is $V\,dQ$, so mechanical [work](work.md) done on the [capacitor](capacitor.md) is $dW_{\rm mech}=dU-V\,dQ=-V^2dC/2$. Increasing the separation of a [parallel-plate capacitor](parallel-plate-capacitor.md) makes $dC<0$: mechanical [work](work.md) is positive even though [electrostatic energy](electrostatic-energy.md) decreases, because electrical [energy](energy.md) returns to the source. Conversely, when $dC>0$, the [battery](electric-battery.md) supplies $V^2dC$: half increases [capacitor energy](capacitor-energy.md) and half is available as mechanical [work](work.md) done by the [electric field](electric-field.md). Differentiating [capacitor energy](capacitor-energy.md) alone at fixed terminal [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) omits this exchange with the [battery](electric-battery.md).
