# Arithmetic decomposition

↑ **Parent:** [Arithmetic strategy](arithmetic-strategy.md)

Reexpressing a calculation through useful numerical relations, such as $8+5=8+2+3=13$. Decomposition can connect practiced facts with [conceptual knowledge](conceptual-knowledge.md) instead of requiring direct retrieval of every whole problem.
