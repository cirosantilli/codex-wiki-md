# Tangent bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Vector bundle](vector-bundle.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tangent_bundle)

The tangent bundle of a [smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md) $M$ is the [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) whose fiber over $p$ is the tangent space $T_pM$.
