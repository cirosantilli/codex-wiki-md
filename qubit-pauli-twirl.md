# Qubit Pauli twirl

↑ **Parent:** [Heisenberg-Weyl twirling channel](heisenberg-weyl-twirling-channel.md)

For any [qubit](qubit.md) operator $A$,

$$
\mathcal T(A)=\frac14(A+XAX+YAY+ZAZ)=\frac{\operatorname{Tr}A}{2}I.
$$

Expand $A$ in the [Pauli operator](pauli-operator.md) basis. The identity term is fixed, while every nonidentity coefficient has two positive and two negative contributions and cancels. Thus forgetting a uniformly random Pauli key sends every [density operator](density-matrix-split.md) to the [maximally mixed state](maximally-mixed-state.md). This is the dimension-two [Heisenberg-Weyl twirling channel](heisenberg-weyl-twirling-channel.md), since replacing $Y$ by $XZ$ changes only an irrelevant phase in the conjugation.
