# Ribonuclease P

↑ **Parent:** [Enzyme](enzyme-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ribonuclease_P)

An [enzyme](enzyme-split.md) that processes the $5'$ end of precursor [transfer RNA](transfer-rna.md). The bacterial [RNase P](ribonuclease-p.md) used in the discovery experiments contains catalytic [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) and a supporting [protein](protein-split.md); the [ribozyme](ribozyme.md) body gives the conditions under which its [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) component works alone. This description concerns those bacterial complexes, not every form of [RNase P](ribonuclease-p.md).
