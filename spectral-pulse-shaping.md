# Spectral pulse shaping

↑ **Parent:** [Optics](optics-split.md)

[Spectral pulse shaping](spectral-pulse-shaping.md) changes an optical pulse by applying a complex amplitude-and-phase mask $M(\omega)$ to its frequency components. The output in time is the inverse [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) of the masked spectrum. A passive device cannot amplify unavailable spectral components; finite bandwidth and spectral resolution constrain the realizable waveforms and time window.
