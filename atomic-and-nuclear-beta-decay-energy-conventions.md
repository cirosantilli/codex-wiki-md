# Atomic and nuclear beta-decay energy conventions

↑ **Parent:** [Beta decay](beta-decay.md)

For ground-to-ground beta-minus decay of bare [nuclei](atomic-nucleus-split.md), the nuclear [mass](mass.md) difference supplies the [electron](electron.md) rest [energy](energy-split.md) as well as the [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) shared among products. Neglecting recoil and [neutrino](neutrino.md) [mass](mass.md), $T_{\max}=Q_{\beta^-}$. For neutral atomic [masses](mass.md) the additional final atomic [electron](electron.md) cancels the emitted [electron](electron.md) rest-mass subtraction, giving $Q\simeq[M_{\rm atom}(i)-M_{\rm atom}(f)]c^2$ up to electron-binding corrections. A daughter excitation reduces the endpoint by its [excitation energy](excitation-energy.md).
