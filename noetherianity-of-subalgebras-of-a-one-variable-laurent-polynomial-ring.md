# Noetherianity of subalgebras of a one-variable Laurent polynomial ring

↑ **Parent:** [Laurent polynomial ring](laurent-polynomial-ring.md)

Every unital $k$-[subalgebra](subalgebra.md) $A\subseteq k[T,T^{-1}]$ is a [Noetherian ring](noetherian-ring.md). If $A\subseteq k[T]$ or $k[T^{-1}]$, a nonconstant element $f\in A$ makes that ambient [polynomial ring](polynomial-ring.md) a [finitely generated module](finitely-generated-module.md) over $k[f]$. Otherwise $A$ contains an $f$ with both positive and negative exponents, and both $T$ and $T^{-1}$ are [integral elements](integral-element.md) over $k[f]$. In either case $A$ is a [submodule](submodule.md) of a finite module over the [Noetherian ring](noetherian-ring.md) $k[f]$, and every [ideal](ideal.md) of $A$ is finitely generated over $k[f]$, hence over $A$.
