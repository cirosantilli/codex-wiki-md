# Energy per wavelength of a transverse string wave

↑ **Parent:** [Wave equation on a string](wave-equation-on-a-string.md)

For a small-slope [travelling wave](travelling-wave.md) $y=A\cos(kx-\omega t+\phi)$ on a uniform string with [linear mass density](linear-mass-density.md) $\mu$ and [tension](tension.md) $T$, the [wave equation on a string](wave-equation-on-a-string.md) requires $\omega^2=Tk^2/\mu$. Its [elastic energy of a small-slope string](elastic-energy-of-a-small-slope-string.md) and [kinetic energy density](kinetic-energy-density.md) are equal, giving total [energy density](energy-density.md) $u=\mu A^2\omega^2\sin^2(kx-\omega t+\phi)$. Its spatial average is $\mu A^2\omega^2/2$, and integrating over a full [wavelength](wavelength.md) $\lambda=2\pi/|k|$ gives the title formula. Omitting $\lambda$ produces an [energy density](energy-density.md), not an [energy](energy-split.md); units and a direct one-period [integral](integral-split.md) detect the mistake independently.
