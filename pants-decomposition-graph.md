# Pants decomposition graph

↑ **Parent:** [Pants decomposition](pants-decomposition.md)

The graph of a [pants decomposition](pants-decomposition.md) has one vertex per [pair of pants](pair-of-pants-mathematics.md) and one edge per glued cuff. It is a connected trivalent multigraph, possibly with loops. A closed surface of genus $g$ gives $2g-2$ vertices and $3g-3$ edges, so its first [Betti number](betti-number.md) is $g$. The graph records a decomposition rather than the homeomorphism class alone, and omits cuff lengths and twists.
