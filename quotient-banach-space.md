# Quotient Banach space

↑ **Parent:** [Banach space](banach-space-split.md)

For a closed [vector subspace](vector-subspace.md) $Y$ of a [Banach space](banach-space-split.md) $X$, the [quotient vector space](quotient-vector-space.md) $X/Y$ is complete with its [quotient norm](quotient-norm.md). Closedness makes the quotient seminorm a norm. Completeness follows by choosing representatives of a rapidly Cauchy subsequence whose successive differences have summable norms in $X$.
