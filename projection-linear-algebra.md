# Projection (linear algebra)

↑ **Parent:** [Linear operator](linear-operator.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Projection_(linear_algebra))

A [linear projection](projection-linear-algebra.md) is an [idempotent](idempotent.md) [linear operator](linear-operator.md): $P^2=P$. Every vector decomposes as $x=Px+(I-P)x$, into the [image of a linear map](image-of-a-linear-map.md) and the [kernel of a linear map](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md). Those two [vector subspaces](vector-subspace.md) form a [direct sum](direct-sum.md). An [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md) additionally uses the [orthogonal complement](orthogonal-complement.md) in an [inner product space](inner-product-space.md).
