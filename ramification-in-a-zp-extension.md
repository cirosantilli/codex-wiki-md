# Ramification in a Zp-extension

↑ **Parent:** [Zp-extension](zp-extension.md)

At least one finite prime ramifies in a [Zp-extension](zp-extension.md); otherwise the entire tower would lie in a finite [Hilbert class field](hilbert-class-field.md). Ramification can occur only over $p$: [local class field theory](local-class-field-theory.md) makes inertia away from $p$ an image of a unit group with finite maximal pro-$p$ quotient, whereas $\mathbb Z_p$ has no nontrivial finite subgroup. Nonzero inertia is open in $\mathbb Z_p$, so it becomes total after passing to a sufficiently high finite layer.
