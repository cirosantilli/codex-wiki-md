# Isolated mitochondria respiration experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Mitochondria](mitochondria.md)

[Respiratory control](respiratory-control.md) can be investigated outside an intact [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md) by supplying isolated [mitochondria](mitochondria.md) with [substrates](substrate-biochemistry.md) and [ADP](adenosine-diphosphate.md). This makes the boundary between [oxygen](oxygen.md) consumption and [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) formation experimentally accessible. The historical [mitochondrial oxygen electrode assay](mitochondrial-oxygen-electrode-assay.md) describes the 1955 experiment; the following authored protocol adds modern preparation and inhibitor controls.

In Functional Assessment of Isolated Mitochondria In Vitro (2009), [Ian R. Lanza](ian-r-lanza.md) and [K. Sreekumaran Nair](k-sreekumaran-nair.md) describe human skeletal-muscle biopsy mincing, cold [protease](protease.md) treatment, homogenization and [differential centrifugation](differential-centrifugation.md). A low-speed spin removes coarse material; subsequent spins collect and wash [mitochondria](mitochondria.md). A stirred, closed $2\,\mathrm{mL}$ Oxygraph-2k chamber at $37\,{}^\circ\mathrm{C}$ uses a calibrated polarographic [oxygen](oxygen.md) [electrode](electrode.md). Its signal gives dissolved [oxygen](oxygen.md) [concentration](concentration.md); the negative time [derivative](derivative.md) gives consumption. Instrument corrections include electrode consumption and [oxygen](oxygen.md) leakage.[Methods in Enzymology, volume 457: Mitochondrial Function, Part B; Tissue homogenization; Separation of mitochondria by differential centrifugation; Mitochondrial respiration, Overview; An oxygraph protocol for mitochondrial assessment, steps 1–2](methods-in-enzymology-volume-457-mitochondrial-function-part-b/functional-assessment-of-isolated-mitochondria-in-vitro.md)

Their additions include [glutamate](glutamate.md) plus [malate](malate.md), an [ADP](adenosine-diphosphate.md) pulse, then saturating [ADP](adenosine-diphosphate.md). Added [cytochrome c](cytochrome-c.md) tests outer-membrane damage: stimulation suggests loss of endogenous [cytochrome c](cytochrome-c.md). Later [oligomycin](oligomycin.md) inhibits [ATP synthase](atp-synthase.md); [FCCP](fccp.md) produces [respiratory uncoupling](respiratory-uncoupling.md); [antimycin A](antimycin-a.md) inhibits [respiratory complex III](respiratory-complex-iii.md) to estimate residual background. Figure 6 includes intervening [succinate dehydrogenase](succinate-dehydrogenase.md) and [respiratory complex I](respiratory-complex-i.md) interventions; the modeled traces below simplify that sequence. Isolation can damage or select [mitochondria](mitochondria.md), and saturating assay conditions differ from those in living [biological tissue](tissue-biology.md).[Methods in Enzymology, volume 457: Mitochondrial Function, Part B; Figure 3; An oxygraph protocol for mitochondrial assessment, steps 3–11 and Figure 6; Summary, nonphysiological assay conditions](methods-in-enzymology-volume-457-mitochondrial-function-part-b/functional-assessment-of-isolated-mitochondria-in-vitro.md)

The control logic matters more than a single steep slope. If [ADP](adenosine-diphosphate.md) accelerates consumption and a finite pulse is followed by slowing, demand is affecting respiratory flux. If [oligomycin](oligomycin.md) slows consumption, but subsequent [FCCP](fccp.md) accelerates it, the [electron transport chain](electron-transport-chain.md) can still turn over while [ATP synthase](atp-synthase.md) remains inhibited. That contrast separates restricted coupled turnover from a completely inactive chain. Failure of [FCCP](fccp.md) to restore a rate is less specific: [substrate](substrate-biochemistry.md) limitation, damage, low [oxygen](oxygen.md) or excessive uncoupler can also restrict respiration.

Neither the [cytochrome c](cytochrome-c.md) test alone nor a large [respiratory control ratio](respiratory-control-ratio.md) establishes every aspect of mitochondrial integrity. Likewise, [oxygen](oxygen.md) consumption is not a direct [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) assay: [respiratory uncoupling](respiratory-uncoupling.md) provides a concrete counterexample. The preparation, additions and normalization denominator determine what comparison the experiment can support.
