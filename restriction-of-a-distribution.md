# Restriction of a distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Distribution (mathematical analysis)](distribution-mathematical-analysis.md)

For [open sets](open-set.md) $V\subseteq U$ and $u\in\mathcal D'(U)$, define $\langle u|_V,\phi\rangle=\langle u,\widetilde\phi\rangle$, where $\widetilde\phi$ extends the [test function](test-function.md) $\phi\in\mathcal D(V)$ by zero. Because its [compact support](compact-support.md) lies inside $V$, this extension is smooth. Extension preserves convergence in the [space of test functions](space-of-test-functions.md), proving continuity of the restricted [distribution](distribution-mathematical-analysis.md). Successive restrictions agree with direct restriction.
