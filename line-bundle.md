# Line bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Locally free sheaf](locally-free-sheaf.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Line_bundle)

A line bundle on a [scheme](scheme.md) is a [locally free sheaf](locally-free-sheaf.md) of rank one. Its global sections can define a morphism to [projective space](projective-space-split.md) when they have no common zero.
