# Nondecreasing family of sets

↑ **Parent:** [Set](set-split.md)

A family of sets $(A_t)$ indexed by an [partially ordered set](partially-ordered-set.md) is nondecreasing when

$$
s\leq t\quad\Longrightarrow\quad A_s\subseteq A_t.
$$
