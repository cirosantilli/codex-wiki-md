# Nocebo effect

↑ **Parent:** [Placebo effect](placebo-effect.md)

Adverse change attributable to a negative [treatment](treatment.md) context or expectation. It can involve genuine discomfort and physiological modulation as well as reporting and attribution. Its presence does not exclude concurrent disease or a direct [adverse drug reaction](adverse-drug-reaction.md).[Science Translational Medicine 3, 70ra14; fixed remifentanil concentration, heat pain and expectation manipulation](the-effect-of-treatment-expectation-on-drug-efficacy-by-ulrike-bingel-and-others.md)
