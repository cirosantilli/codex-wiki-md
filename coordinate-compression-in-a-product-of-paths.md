# Coordinate compression in a product of paths

↑ **Parent:** [Vertex-isoperimetric inequality in a grid](vertex-isoperimetric-inequality-in-a-grid.md)

Coordinate compression in direction $i$ replaces every $i$-section of a subset of $[k]^d$ by the initial segment of the [simplicial order on a grid](simplicial-order-on-a-grid.md) having the same size. Assuming the vertex-isoperimetric theorem one dimension lower, this operation preserves cardinality and does not increase the [external vertex boundary](external-vertex-boundary.md).
