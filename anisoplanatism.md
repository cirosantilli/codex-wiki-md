# Anisoplanatism

↑ **Parent:** [Astronomical seeing](astronomical-seeing.md)

Anisoplanatism is the loss of correlation between [wavefront errors](wavefront-error.md) along different viewing directions. Correcting a [guide star](guide-star-adaptive-optics.md) therefore leaves a larger residual for a sufficiently separated target.
