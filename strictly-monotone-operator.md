# Strictly monotone operator

↑ **Parent:** [Monotone operator](monotone-operator.md)

A [strictly monotone operator](strictly-monotone-operator.md) $A:X\to X^*$ on a real [Banach space](banach-space-split.md) satisfies strict [operator monotonicity](operator-monotonicity.md) for every pair of distinct inputs. It is injective: $Au=Av$ would make the pairing zero and hence force $u=v$. In particular $Au=f$ has at most one solution for each $f\in X^*$.
