# Grounded-plate potential of a central charged sheet

↑ **Parent:** [Electric potential](electric-potential.md)

An ideal infinite sheet midway between parallel grounded plates has equal fields of magnitude $\sigma/(2\epsilon_0)$ on its two sides, directed from positive sheet charge toward the plates. Integrating over the half-separation d/2 gives the displayed [electric potential](electric-potential.md). Each plate carries induced inner-face [surface charge density](surface-charge-density.md) $-\sigma/2$. The potential is triangular between the plates, and the field jumps by $\sigma/\epsilon_0$ across the sheet. Edge fields are excluded.
