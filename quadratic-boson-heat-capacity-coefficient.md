# Quadratic-boson heat-capacity coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Low-temperature energy of gapless bosonic excitations](low-temperature-energy-of-gapless-bosonic-excitations.md)

One isotropic three-dimensional bosonic branch with dispersion $\epsilon=\alpha p^2$ and zero [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) has [density of states](density-of-states.md) per [volume](volume.md) $\sqrt\epsilon/(4\pi^2\hbar^3\alpha^{3/2})$. Integrating its Bose-Einstein [energy](energy-split.md) and differentiating [temperature](temperature-split.md) gives the title coefficient. Identical independent branches multiply it by their number. Thus a measured $T^{3/2}$ coefficient fixes branch multiplicity divided by $\alpha^{3/2}$, not a dispersion coefficient independent of that counting convention. A gap or low-dimensional spectrum changes the asymptotic law.
