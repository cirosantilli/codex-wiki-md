# Star forest

↑ **Parent:** [Forest](forest.md)

A [forest](forest.md) whose [connected components of a graph](component-graph-theory.md) are [star graphs](star-graph-theory.md). In an inclusion-minimal [edge cover](edge-cover.md), every [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) has at least one endpoint of [vertex degree](degree-graph-theory.md) one, forcing this structure with no [isolated vertex](isolated-vertex.md).
