# Admissible representation of a locally profinite group

↑ **Parent:** [Smooth representation of a locally profinite group](smooth-representation-of-a-locally-profinite-group.md)

An [admissible representation](admissible-representation-of-a-locally-profinite-group.md) is a [smooth representation](smooth-representation-of-a-locally-profinite-group.md) for which every [fixed-vector space](fixed-vector-space.md) $V^K$ for a [compact-open subgroup](compact-open-subgroup.md) has finite dimension. Admissibility can hold for an infinite-dimensional representation; it bounds each local fixed level, not their union.
