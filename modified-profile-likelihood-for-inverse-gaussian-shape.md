# Modified profile likelihood for inverse Gaussian shape

↑ **Parent:** [Modified profile likelihood](modified-profile-likelihood.md)

For independent [Inverse Gaussian distributions](inverse-gaussian-distribution.md) with shape $\psi$ and mean $\lambda$, the constrained and full nuisance [maximum-likelihood estimates](maximum-likelihood-estimator.md) both equal $\bar Y$, giving a unit sample-coordinate [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md). The nuisance [observed information](observed-fisher-information.md) there is $n\psi/\bar Y^3$. Hence subtracting half its logarithm from the [profile log-likelihood](profile-log-likelihood.md) replaces the coefficient $n/2$ of $\log\psi$ by $(n-1)/2$. For a nonconstant sample the modified fit is $(n-1)/(\sum_iY_i^{-1}-n/\bar Y)$.
