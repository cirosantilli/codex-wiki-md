# Reflection identity for Brownian exit from a half-disc

↑ **Parent:** [Harmonic measure](harmonic-measure.md)

For [planar Brownian motion](planar-brownian-motion.md) starting on the positive real axis inside a disk, let $T$ be circular exit and $S$ first contact with the imaginary axis. Reflecting the path after $S$ fixes the disk and swaps positive and negative circular exits. The [Strong Markov property](strong-markov-property.md) makes their probabilities equal on $S<T$. Therefore $\mathbb P(T<S)=\mathbb P(\operatorname{Re}B_T>0)-\mathbb P(\operatorname{Re}B_T<0)$.
