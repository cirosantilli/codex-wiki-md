# Bound charge

↑ **Parent:** [Bound charge and bound current](bound-charge-and-bound-current.md)

The effective [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) associated with [electric polarization](polarization-density.md) is bound charge. For [polarization density](polarization-density.md) $\mathbf P$, its volume [charge density](charge-density.md) is $\rho_{\mathrm b}=-\nabla\cdot\mathbf P$. At a boundary with unpolarized surroundings, its [surface charge density](surface-charge-density.md) is $\sigma_{\mathrm b}=\mathbf P\cdot\mathbf n$, where $\mathbf n$ is the outward [unit normal vector](unit-normal.md).
