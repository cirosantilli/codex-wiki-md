# Orthonormalization of a finite-field Hermitian form

↑ **Parent:** [Hermitian form over a quadratic finite field](hermitian-form-over-a-quadratic-finite-field.md)

A nondegenerate [Hermitian form over a quadratic finite field](hermitian-form-over-a-quadratic-finite-field.md) has an [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md). If all diagonal values vanished, evaluating $B(v+aw,v+aw)$ would give $\operatorname{Tr}(\bar a B(v,w))=0$ for every $a$, contradicting [quadratic finite-field norm and trace surjectivity](quadratic-finite-field-norm-and-trace-surjectivity.md) and nondegeneracy. Thus some $v$ has nonzero norm; rescaling using the [field norm](field-norm.md) makes it one. Its [orthogonal complement for a sesquilinear form](orthogonal-complement-for-a-sesquilinear-form.md) is nondegenerate, so induction completes the [basis](basis.md).
