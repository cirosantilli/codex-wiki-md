# Klein-Gordon field

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum field theory in curved spacetime](quantum-field-theory-in-curved-spacetime.md)

A free [Klein-Gordon field](klein-gordon-field.md) is a scalar obeying $(\Box-m^2-\xi R)\phi=0$ in the mostly-plus [metric signature](metric-signature.md). [Global hyperbolicity](globally-hyperbolic-spacetime.md) provides a well-defined initial-value problem. Quantization additionally needs a state or positive-norm mode splitting; the equation alone does not select particles.
