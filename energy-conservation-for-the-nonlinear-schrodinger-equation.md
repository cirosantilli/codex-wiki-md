<h1 id="energy-conservation-for-the-nonlinear-schrodinger-equation">Energy conservation for the nonlinear Schrödinger equation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Nonlinear Schrödinger blowup analysis](nonlinear-schrodinger-blowup-analysis.md)

For the focusing power equation $iu_t+\Delta u+|u|^{p-1}u=0$, the conserved energy is

$$
E(u)=\frac12\int|\nabla u|^2-
\frac1{p+1}\int|u|^{p+1}.
$$
