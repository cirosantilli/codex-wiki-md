# Nonabsoluteness of singular cardinalhood

↑ **Parent:** [Set-theoretic absoluteness](set-theoretic-absoluteness.md)

The statement that $\kappa$ is a [cardinal number](cardinal-number.md) with $\operatorname{cf}(\kappa)<\kappa$ is generally neither upward nor downward absolute. Upward absoluteness can fail when a larger model collapses the cardinal; downward absoluteness can fail when a larger model adds a short cofinal sequence to a cardinal that the smaller model regards as regular.
