# Kolmogorov-Smirnov statistic

↑ **Parent:** [Kolmogorov-Smirnov test](kolmogorov-smirnov-test.md)

The one-sample [Kolmogorov-Smirnov statistic](kolmogorov-smirnov-statistic.md) is the largest absolute discrepancy between the empirical and specified distribution functions. A common normalized version is $\sqrt nD_n$. For the uniform model with an unknown endpoint, deleting the observed maximum and rescaling the remaining observations by that maximum leaves conditionally independent standard uniform observations; the ordinary test then uses sample size $n-1$.
