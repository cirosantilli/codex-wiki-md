# Electric-field displacement of a quantum harmonic oscillator

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md)

For $H=p^2/(2m)+kx^2/2-qEx$, completing the square translates the well by $x_0=qE/k$ and shifts every [energy](energy-split.md) by $-q^2E^2/(2k)$. The [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) of the [ground state](ground-state.md) is translated without changing its width. Consequently the induced [electric dipole moment](electric-dipole-moment.md) is exactly linear in $E$, even when a low-order perturbative approximation to the state is inaccurate.
