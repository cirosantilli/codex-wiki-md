# Magnetic island

↑ **Parent:** [Cartesian magnetic flux function](cartesian-magnetic-flux-function.md)

A magnetic island in a planar [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) is a region of closed [magnetic field lines](magnetic-field-line.md) surrounding an extremum of the [Cartesian magnetic flux function](cartesian-magnetic-flux-function.md). A surrounding [separatrix](separatrix.md) can pass through a saddle of that function and separate the island from other flux regions. In ideal [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md), the flux-function value carried by each fluid particle and the area enclosed by each material contour are conserved.
