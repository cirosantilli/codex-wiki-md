# l2 sequence space

↑ **Parent:** [L-p sequence space](l-p-sequence-space.md)

The space $\ell^2$ consists of scalar sequences $x=(x_n)$ satisfying $\sum_n|x_n|^2<\infty$. With inner product $\langle x,y\rangle=\sum_nx_n\overline{y_n}$, it is a [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md).
