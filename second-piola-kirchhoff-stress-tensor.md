# Second Piola-Kirchhoff stress tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Piola-Kirchhoff stress tensors](piola-kirchhoff-stress-tensors.md)

The second tensor has two material indices and is symmetric when the [Cauchy stress tensor](cauchy-stress-tensor.md) is symmetric. The displayed relation uses the material-first [nominal stress tensor](nominal-stress-tensor.md) convention. In particular, $PF$ is generally not this tensor. These conventions agree with [A. F. Bower's definitions of stress measures](https://solidmechanics.org/Text/Chapter2_3/Chapter2_3.htm).
