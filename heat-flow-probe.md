# Heat flow probe

↑ **Parent:** [Heat flux density](heat-flux-density.md)

An instrument that combines subsurface temperature measurements with constraints on [thermal conductivity](thermal-conductivity.md) to estimate a local [heat flux](heat-flux-density.md). Multiple sensor depths help separate a stable [geothermal gradient](geothermal-gradient.md) from surface-temperature transients and installation disturbance. Conductive heat flow can differ from total heat transport where fluid circulation carries substantial [energy](energy-split.md).
