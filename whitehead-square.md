# Whitehead square

↑ **Parent:** [Whitehead product](whitehead-product.md)

The Whitehead square of a class $\alpha\in\pi_mY$ is $[\alpha,\alpha]\in\pi_{2m-1}Y$. For the identity class of an even-dimensional sphere, the [Hopf invariant](hopf-invariant.md) is two with the standard product-cell orientation: folding the two sphere summands makes the middle [cohomology class](cohomology-class.md) of the mapping cone pull back to $a+b$ on $S^m\times S^m$, and $(a+b)^2=2ab$. For odd $m$, graded symmetry makes the square two-torsion, hence rationally zero.
