# Functional Taylor expansion

↑ **Parent:** [Functional derivative](functional-derivative.md)

The first variation of a differentiable [action functional](action.md) gives its change under a small field deformation. With $E=\delta S/\delta\phi$, the change $\phi\mapsto\phi-\eta f$ generates $-\eta\int fE$ to first order. This is the basic cancellation behind the [equivalence theorem for field redefinitions](equivalence-theorem-for-field-redefinitions.md); the [functional Jacobian](functional-jacobian.md) and transformed operator insertions must also be treated consistently.
