# Square-root bound for increasing events

↑ **Parent:** [Harris-FKG inequality](harris-fkg-inequality.md)

For two [increasing events](increasing-event.md) of equal [probability](probability.md) $a$ under a [product measure](product-measure.md), positive association of their complements gives $1-P(A\cup B)\ge(1-a)^2$. Hence $a\ge1-\sqrt{1-P(A\cup B)}$. For $r$ equal-probability [increasing events](increasing-event.md), the same reasoning gives $a\ge1-(1-P(\bigcup_jA_j))^{1/r}$.
