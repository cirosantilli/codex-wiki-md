# Nuclear de-excitation

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear structure](nuclear-structure.md)

[Nuclear de-excitation](nuclear-de-excitation.md) is a transition from a higher to a lower [nuclear energy level](nuclear-energy-level.md). It can transfer energy to an emitted [gamma ray](gamma-ray.md) or to an atomic [electron](electron.md) through [internal conversion](internal-conversion.md), among other channels when thresholds permit.
