# Hunchback

↑ **Parent:** [Transcription factor](transcription-factor.md)

A [Drosophila](drosophila.md) [transcription factor](transcription-factor.md) supplied through maternal and zygotic expression. Posterior [Nanos](nanos.md)-dependent repression of the maternal [messenger RNA](messenger-rna.md) and anterior [Bicoid](bicoid.md)-dependent [transcription](transcription-biology.md) make distinct contributions.
