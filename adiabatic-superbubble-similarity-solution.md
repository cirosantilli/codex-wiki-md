# Adiabatic superbubble similarity solution

↑ **Parent:** [Superbubble](superbubble.md)

For constant injected power $\dot E$ and ambient [mass density](density.md) $\rho_1$, [dimensional analysis](dimensional-analysis.md) gives $R=\alpha(\dot E/\rho_1)^{1/5}t^{3/5}$. In a [similarity solution](similarity-solution.md), set $\eta=r/R$, $\rho=\rho_1f(\eta)$, $p=\rho_1\dot R^2g(\eta)$ and $u=\dot Rh(\eta)$. The strong-shock values on the inner side of $\eta=1$ are $f=(\gamma+1)/(\gamma-1)$ and $g=h=2/(\gamma+1)$.
