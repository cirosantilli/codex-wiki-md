# FRET donor

↑ **Parent:** [Förster resonance energy transfer](forster-resonance-energy-transfer.md)

A [FRET donor](fret-donor.md) supplies the initial [excited state](excited-state.md) [energy](energy-split.md). Its [fluorescence](fluorescence-split.md) provides both an emission channel and a reference [FRET donor lifetime](fret-donor-lifetime.md) when no [FRET acceptor](fret-acceptor.md) is present. Calling a label a [FRET donor](fret-donor.md) describes its role in a chosen pair, rather than a permanent classification of the [molecule](molecule-split.md).
