# Torsion subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Torsion element](torsion-element.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torsion_subgroup)

The torsion subgroup of an [abelian group](abelian-group.md) consists of all elements of finite order. For a finitely generated abelian group, it is the finite direct summand complementary to the free abelian part.
