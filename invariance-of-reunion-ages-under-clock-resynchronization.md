# Invariance of reunion ages under clock resynchronization

↑ **Parent:** [Twin paradox](twin-paradox.md)

Consider a change of distant-time convention $s=t+\kappa x/c$, where $\kappa$ is constant and $|\kappa|<1$. This changes time labels and simultaneity slices while leaving the [world lines](world-line.md) and the [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md) as geometric objects unchanged. In one spatial dimension,

$$
ds_{\rm spacetime}^2=-c^2ds^2+2\kappa c\,ds\,dx+(1-\kappa^2)dx^2.
$$

Substitute $dt=ds-\kappa\,dx/c$ into the [Minkowski interval](minkowski-interval.md) to obtain the formula. For a [timelike curve](timelike-curve.md), the [proper time](proper-time.md) calculated from this transformed [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md) equals the old value. At shared departure and reunion events with $x=0$, the labels also obey $s=t$. Thus a distant synchronization convention cannot alter the [twin paradox](twin-paradox.md)'s reunited [clock](clock.md) readings. It would be an error to change time labels while retaining the old diagonal metric components.
