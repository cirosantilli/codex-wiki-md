# Split gate

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum point contact](quantum-point-contact.md)

Two surface electrodes whose depletion regions define a narrow conducting opening in an underlying [two-dimensional electron gas](two-dimensional-electron-gas.md). Gate voltage changes the transverse confinement and channel transmission without physically etching the conducting channel to its electrical width.
