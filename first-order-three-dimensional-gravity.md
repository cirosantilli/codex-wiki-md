# First-order three-dimensional gravity

↑ **Parent:** [Einstein-Hilbert action](einstein-hilbert-action.md)

For a nondegenerate [orthonormal coframe in spacetime](orthonormal-coframe-in-spacetime.md) $E^a$ and independent antisymmetric Lorentz [connection 1-form](connection-1-form-split.md), the action $\int\epsilon_{abc}(R^{ab}\wedge E^c+\lambda E^a\wedge E^b\wedge E^c)$ has independent coframe and connection variations. Since $\delta R^{ab}=D\delta\omega^{ab}$, integration by parts yields a connection equation $\epsilon_{abc}T^c=0$. The three variations of the cubic coframe term give the coframe equation $\epsilon_{abc}(R^{ab}+3\lambda E^a\wedge E^b)=0$. In three dimensions these are exactly the equations displayed above. The solution has [constant sectional curvature](constant-sectional-curvature.md) $K=-3\lambda$. Omitting the factor of three changes the cosmological normalization.
