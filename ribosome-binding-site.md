# Ribosome-binding site

↑ **Parent:** [Translation (biology)](translation-biology.md)

A [ribosome-binding site](ribosome-binding-site.md) is a transcript region involved in recruiting or positioning a [ribosome](ribosome.md) for initiation. In many bacteria it includes a [Shine-Dalgarno sequence](shine-dalgarno-sequence.md), but accessibility and start-codon context matter and not all bacterial initiation uses that motif.
