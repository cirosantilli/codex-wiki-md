# Heat balance with volumetric heat generation

↑ **Parent:** [Heat equation](heat-equation.md)

Integrating [energy](energy-split.md) conservation over a fixed material volume gives storage equal to incoming conductive [heat flux](heat-flux-density.md) plus internally generated [heat](heat-split.md). Here $c_{\rm vol}$ is [volumetric heat capacity](volumetric-heat-capacity.md), with units $\mathrm{J\,m^{-3}\,K^{-1}}$. For a mass-specific [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md), the [density](density.md) factor must be included. Constant isotropic conductivity reduces the divergence to $k\nabla^2T$; variable conductivity must remain inside the divergence.
