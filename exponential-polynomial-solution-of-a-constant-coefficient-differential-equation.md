# Exponential polynomial solution of a constant-coefficient differential equation

↑ **Parent:** [Distributional regularity of a constant-coefficient ordinary differential equation](distributional-regularity-of-a-constant-coefficient-ordinary-differential-equation.md)

If a degree-$n$ characteristic [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) has distinct [roots of a polynomial](root-of-a-polynomial.md) $\lambda_j$ of multiplicities $m_j$, all homogeneous [distribution](distribution-mathematical-analysis.md) solutions are the displayed combinations. There are $\sum_jm_j=n$ independent coefficients. Repeated [characteristic roots of a constant-coefficient differential equation](characteristic-root-of-a-constant-coefficient-differential-equation.md) account for the polynomial factors multiplying exponentials; conjugate pairs give real sine and cosine combinations when real solutions are desired.
