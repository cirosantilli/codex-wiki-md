# Fundamental group of a surface

↑ **Parent:** [Fundamental group](fundamental-group.md)

The [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md) of a [surface](topological-surface.md). For a closed orientable genus-$g$ surface the [fundamental group of a closed orientable surface](fundamental-group-of-a-closed-orientable-surface.md) has $2g$ generators and one relation, the product of their paired commutators.
