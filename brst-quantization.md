# BRST quantization

↑ **Parent:** [BRST symmetry](brst-symmetry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BRST_quantization)

A gauge quantization prescription using a nilpotent [BRST charge](brst-charge.md) on the gauge-fixed state space. Physical states are represented by its [BRST cohomology](brst-cohomology.md), usually at [ghost number](ghost-number.md) zero. Exact states decouple under suitable invariant-measure, boundary and positivity assumptions. Nilpotence alone does not prove positivity of the quotient or select its physical ghost-number sector.
