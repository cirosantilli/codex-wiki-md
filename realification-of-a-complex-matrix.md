# Realification of a complex matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix](matrix.md)

For $A=P+iQ$ with real matrices $P,Q$, the same [linear map](linear-map.md) viewed over the reals has block matrix $\begin{pmatrix}P&-Q\\Q&P\end{pmatrix}$ in grouped real/imaginary coordinates. Interleaved coordinates merely permute the basis. Its complexification splits into the matrices $A$ and $\overline A$, giving the [realification determinant identity](realification-determinant-identity.md).
