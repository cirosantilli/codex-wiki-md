# Nazca Ridge

↑ **Parent:** [Nazca Plate](nazca-plate.md)

A prominent oceanic ridge on the [Nazca Plate](nazca-plate.md) entering the Peruvian [subduction zone](subduction-zone.md). Its association with variations in [slab dip](slab-dip.md) motivates tests using seismic geometry, plate reconstructions and the thermal and compositional contributions to [slab buoyancy](slab-buoyancy.md).
