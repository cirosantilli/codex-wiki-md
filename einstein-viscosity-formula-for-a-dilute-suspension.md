# Einstein viscosity formula for a dilute suspension

↑ **Parent:** [Extra dissipation due to a rigid inclusion](extra-dissipation-due-to-a-rigid-inclusion.md)

A dilute suspension of identical rigid spheres raises the [dynamic viscosity](dynamic-viscosity.md) by a fraction $5\phi/2$, where $\phi$ is the particle volume fraction. At imposed trace-free [rate-of-strain tensor](strain-rate-tensor.md) $E$, a sphere of radius $a$ adds [viscous dissipation](viscous-dissipation.md) $20\pi\mu a^3(E:E)/3$. Multiplication by the number density $3\phi/(4\pi a^3)$ gives $5\mu\phi E:E$, which added to $2\mu E:E$ proves the formula. This is the leading noninteracting-sphere term; [hydrodynamic interactions](hydrodynamic-interaction.md) enter at higher concentration.
