# Superhorizon conservation of single-field comoving curvature

↑ **Parent:** [Comoving curvature perturbation](comoving-curvature-perturbation.md)

For an adiabatic single-field attractor, gradient terms are negligible well outside the [Hubble radius](hubble-radius.md) and the growing comoving-curvature mode is constant. The separate-universe interpretation identifies it with the difference of local expansion histories. The conservation statement can fail for a non-attractor phase or an independent entropy mode.
