# Double-stranded RNA

↑ **Parent:** [Ribonucleic acid](ribonucleic-acid.md)

An [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) duplex formed by complementary strands or self-complementary segments. Purified duplex and single-strand controls distinguish a double-stranded trigger from a simple antisense effect in [RNA interference](rna-interference.md).
