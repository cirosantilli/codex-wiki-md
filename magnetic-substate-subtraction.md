# Magnetic-substate subtraction

↑ **Parent:** [Addition of angular momentum](addition-of-angular-momentum.md)

Let $d_M$ count states of fixed [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) projection $M$ in a finite rotational representation, and $n_J$ its spin-$J$ multiplicity. Every spin-$J$ [irreducible representation](irreducible-representation.md) contributes one state at each $M=-J,\ldots,J$. Thus $d_M=\sum_{J\geq M}n_J$ for nonnegative $M$, and subtraction gives $n_J=d_J-d_{J+1}$. Counting occupation states first incorporates [Bose-Einstein statistics](bose-einstein-statistics.md) before decomposing identical-boson angular states.
