# Radiation efficiency

↑ **Parent:** [Antenna gain](antenna-gain.md)

The fraction of accepted input power radiated by an [antenna](antenna-radio.md). With consistent feed definitions, [antenna gain](antenna-gain.md) is [radiation efficiency](radiation-efficiency.md) times [antenna directivity](antenna-directivity.md). Ohmic losses reduce the efficiency, while feed mismatch is usually tracked separately.
