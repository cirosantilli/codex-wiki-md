# Scaled Heisenberg lattice

↑ **Parent:** [Integer Heisenberg group](integer-heisenberg-group.md)

In the [real Heisenberg group](heisenberg-group.md) with multiplication $(x,y,z)(x',y',z')=(x+x',y+y',z+z'+xy')$, the displayed set is a discrete cocompact subgroup. Writing $a=(k,0,0)$, $b=(0,k,0)$, $c=(0,0,k)$ gives $[a,b]=c^k$ and $c$ central. Hence its [abelianization](abelianization.md) is $\mathbb Z^2\oplus\mathbb Z/k$. The right quotient maps to $\mathbb R^2/k\mathbb Z^2$ by its first two coordinates; each fibre is the central circle $\mathbb R/k\mathbb Z$.
