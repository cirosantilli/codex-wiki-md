<h1 id="the-spin-of-the-near-extreme-kerr-black-hole-grs-1915-plus-105-by-jeffrey-e-mcclintock-and-others">The Spin of the Near-Extreme Kerr Black Hole GRS 1915+105 by Jeffrey E. McClintock and others</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by Jeffrey E. McClintock](work-by-jeffrey-e-mcclintock.md)

The primary analysis fits thermal X-ray spectra with a relativistic disk model, using selected disk-dominated data and a [luminosity](luminosity.md) restriction to support the thin-disk assumption. Spectral hardening, inner-edge stress and independently constrained source parameters enter its interpretation. Its [black-hole spin](black-hole-spin.md) inference is conditional on the adopted accretion model and Kerr geometry.
