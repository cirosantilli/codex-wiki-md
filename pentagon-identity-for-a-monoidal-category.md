# Pentagon identity for a monoidal category

↑ **Parent:** [Monoidal category](monoidal-category.md)

For an [associator](associator.md) $a$ in a [monoidal category](monoidal-category.md), the two paths from $((W\otimes X)\otimes Y)\otimes Z$ to $W\otimes(X\otimes(Y\otimes Z))$ agree:

$$
a_{W,X,Y\otimes Z}a_{W\otimes X,Y,Z}=(1_W\otimes a_{X,Y,Z})a_{W,X\otimes Y,Z}(a_{W,X,Y}\otimes1_Z).
$$

This five-vertex diagram is the basic compatibility for reassociating four tensor factors. The [monoidal coherence theorem](monoidal-coherence-theorem.md) extends it to arbitrary finite tensor expressions.
