# Two-pair component of polarization-entangled down-conversion

↑ **Parent:** [SPDC pair state](spdc-pair-state.md)

For two collected spatial modes $a,b$, each with polarizations $H,V$, put $K^\dagger=a_H^\dagger b_V^\dagger+a_V^\dagger b_H^\dagger$. The one-pair state is a [Bell state](bell-state-split.md). The normalized two-pair component is

$$
|\Psi_2\rangle=\frac{|2H_a,2V_b\rangle+|H_aV_a,H_bV_b\rangle+|2V_a,2H_b\rangle}{\sqrt3}.
$$

This follows by expanding $(K^\dagger)^2$ and using the [bosonic creation operator](bosonic-creation-operator.md) identity $(a^\dagger)^n|0\rangle=\sqrt{n!}|n\rangle$. The three orthogonal normalized [Fock states](fock-state.md) have equal probability $1/3$. A tensor product of independently labelled pairs is a different multimode description; identifying its labels without renormalization changes its norm.
