# Ion-channel gating current

↑ **Parent:** [Voltage-gated ion channel](voltage-gated-ion-channel.md)

A transient electrical current associated with displacement of voltage-sensor charge during [ion channel](ion-channel.md) gating, distinct from current carried through an open permeation pathway. Its voltage dependence and changes following sensor mutations constrain the charge motions that couple [membrane potential](membrane-potential-split.md) to opening.
