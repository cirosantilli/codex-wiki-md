# Monovalent atom

↑ **Parent:** [Atom (Physics)](atom-physics.md)

An atom is monovalent in a specified bonding or conduction model when its relevant valence is one. In the simple metallic [free electron model](free-electron-model.md), one [monovalent atom](monovalent-atom.md) per primitive [unit cell](unit-cell.md) supplies one conduction [electron](electron.md) per cell. Counting the two [spin](spin.md) states then fills half of a single [energy band](energy-band.md) at zero [temperature](temperature-split.md).
