# Longitudinal capacitor field under a normal boost

↑ **Parent:** [Lorentz transformation of electromagnetic fields](lorentz-transformation-of-electromagnetic-fields.md)

For ideal oppositely charged parallel plates, the normal [electric field](electric-field-split.md) is $\sigma/\epsilon_0$. A boost along that normal leaves each transverse plate area unchanged. Invariance of total charge therefore preserves [surface charge density](surface-charge-density.md) and the normal [electric field](electric-field-split.md). The plate separation contracts, but the large-plate field does not depend on that separation. Including longitudinal contraction in a [voltage](voltage.md) argument while holding [voltage](voltage.md) fixed would impose an unsupported extra assumption.
