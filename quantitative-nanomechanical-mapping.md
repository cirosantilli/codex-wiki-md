# Quantitative nanomechanical mapping

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic force microscopy](atomic-force-microscopy.md)

Mapping of local mechanical response from calibrated [AFM force curves](afm-force-curve.md). Deformation, [adhesion](adhesion.md) and a model-dependent contact [elastic modulus](elastic-modulus.md) can be separated more directly than by interpreting oscillation phase alone. Tip radius, indentation depth, loading rate and substrate response constrain quantitative interpretation.
