# Ghost-gluon vertex

↑ **Parent:** [Faddeev-Popov ghost](faddeev-popov-ghost.md)

Expanding the ghost covariant derivative in [Yang-Mills theory](yang-mills-theory.md) produces an interaction between the [Faddeev-Popov ghost field](faddeev-popov-ghost.md), [Faddeev-Popov antighost field](faddeev-popov-antighost-field.md), and gauge boson. For the displayed kinetic convention, Fourier factors $e^{-ipx}$ and all momenta incoming give vertex $-g f^{abc}k_\mu$, where $k$ is the antighost momentum. The [structure constants](structure-constant.md) encode the [Adjoint representation](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md). Propagator, vertex, Fourier, and ghost-order conventions must be chosen consistently.
