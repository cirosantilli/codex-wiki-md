# Matching saturation

↑ **Parent:** [Matching (graph theory)](matching-graph-theory-split.md)

A [matching in a graph](matching-graph-theory-split.md) saturates a specified [vertex set](vertex-set.md) $X$ when every [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) of $X$ is incident to one selected [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md). Exactly one selected [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) meets each such [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md), since matching [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) have disjoint endpoints. [Vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) outside $X$ may remain unmatched. A [perfect matching](perfect-matching.md) saturates the entire [vertex set](vertex-set.md); [Hall's marriage theorem](hall-s-marriage-theorem.md) tests [matching saturation](matching-saturation.md) of a specified side in a finite [bipartite graph](bipartite-graph-split.md).
