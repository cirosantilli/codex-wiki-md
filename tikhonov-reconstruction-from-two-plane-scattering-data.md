# Tikhonov reconstruction from two-plane scattering data

↑ **Parent:** [Tikhonov regularization](tikhonov-regularization.md)

The two [Born approximation](born-approximation.md) measurement operators $A_\pm$ map a supported [scattering potential](scattering-potential.md) to fields on planes above and below it. The displayed [Tikhonov regularization](tikhonov-regularization.md) functional balances their residuals against a positive quadratic penalty. With an $L^2$ penalty, the unconstrained minimizer is $(A^*A+\alpha I)^{-1}A^*d$, where $A=(A_+,A_-)$. Regularization stabilizes estimation but cannot remove the nonuniqueness of [Born non-scattering contrasts at one incident direction](born-non-scattering-contrasts-at-one-incident-direction.md) without extra information.
