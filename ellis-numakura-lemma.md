<h1 id="ellis-numakura-lemma">Ellis–Numakura lemma</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Left-topological semigroup](left-topological-semigroup.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ellis–Numakura_lemma)

Every nonempty compact Hausdorff left-topological [semigroup](semigroup.md) contains an [idempotent](idempotent.md). Choose a minimal nonempty compact subsemigroup $K$. For $a\in K$, minimality gives $Ka=K$; the nonempty compact subsemigroup $\{x\in K:xa=a\}$ is therefore all of $K$, so $a^2=a$.
