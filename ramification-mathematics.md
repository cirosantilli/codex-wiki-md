# Ramification (mathematics)

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic geometry](algebraic-geometry-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramification_(mathematics))

Ramification is branching in a geometric or algebraic map, detected by multiplicity greater than one. For [number fields](number-field.md), [ramification of a prime](ramification-of-a-prime.md) means that some [prime ideal](prime-ideal.md) occurs with exponent greater than one in the extended prime ideal. For a map of smooth [algebraic curves](algebraic-curve.md), a local parameter pulls back to a parameter with order greater than one at a ramification point.
