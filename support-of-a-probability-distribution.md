# Support of a probability distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Probability distribution](probability-distribution.md)

For a [probability measure](probability-measure.md) on a [topological space](topological-space.md), its support consists of the points whose every open neighborhood has positive probability. For a continuous effort distribution supported on an interval, every open subinterval of that interval has positive probability.
