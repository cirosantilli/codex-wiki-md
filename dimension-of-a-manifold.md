# Dimension of a manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Topological manifold](topological-manifold.md)

A [topological manifold](topological-manifold.md) has dimension $n$ when its [manifold charts](manifold-chart.md) have coordinate domains open in $\mathbb R^n$. For a [smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md), each [tangent space](tangent-space.md) has [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) $n$, and a [tangent bundle](tangent-bundle.md) has [vector bundle rank](rank-of-a-vector-bundle.md) $n$.
