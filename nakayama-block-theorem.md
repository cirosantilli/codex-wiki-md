# Nakayama block theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Residue-content criterion for symmetric-group blocks](residue-content-criterion-for-symmetric-group-blocks.md)

At fixed degree $n$ in positive [characteristic](characteristic-of-a-field.md) $p$, two [Specht modules](specht-module.md) belong to the same [block of a group algebra](block-of-a-group-algebra.md) exactly when their labels have the same [core of a partition](core-of-a-partition.md) at modulus $p$. The simple labels in that block are precisely the [regular partitions](regular-partition.md) with that core. The theorem follows by combining the [residue-content criterion for symmetric-group blocks](residue-content-criterion-for-symmetric-group-blocks.md) with the [Jucys–Murphy description of the center of a symmetric-group algebra](jucys-murphy-description-of-the-center-of-a-symmetric-group-algebra.md).
