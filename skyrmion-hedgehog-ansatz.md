# Skyrmion hedgehog ansatz

↑ **Parent:** [Skyrmion](skyrmion.md)

The unit [Skyrmion](skyrmion.md) has a spherically symmetric ansatz with [Pauli matrices](pauli-matrices.md) $\boldsymbol\sigma$, $f(0)=\pi$ and $f(\infty)=0$. The [baryon number](baryon-number.md) is

$$
B=-\frac2\pi\int_0^\infty f'\sin^2f\,dr=1.
$$

The radial profile is obtained from the [Skyrme model](skyrme-model.md) variational equation. Spatial rotations and [isospin](isospin.md) rotations act on the same hedgehog orientation, leaving only three independent rotational [collective coordinates](collective-coordinate-of-a-soliton.md).
