# Conservativity of intuitionistic propositional logic over its implicational fragment

↑ **Parent:** [Implicational fragment of intuitionistic propositional logic](implicational-fragment-of-intuitionistic-propositional-logic.md)

If $\Gamma$ and $\varphi$ are implicational and $\Gamma\vdash_{\mathrm{IPC}}\varphi$, then $\Gamma\vdash_{\mathrm{IPC}(\to)}\varphi$. Soundness for Kripke semantics followed by [Kripke completeness of implicational intuitionistic logic](kripke-completeness-of-implicational-intuitionistic-logic.md) proves the claim.
