# Single-molecule absorption in a molecular crystal

↑ **Parent:** [Single-molecule experiment](single-molecule-experiment.md)

In 1989, [William E. Moerner](william-e-moerner.md) and [Lothar Kador](lothar-kador.md) reported individual [pentacene](pentacene.md) absorbers in [para-terphenyl](para-terphenyl.md) in [Optical detection and spectroscopy of single molecules in a solid by W. E. Moerner and L. Kador](optical-detection-and-spectroscopy-of-single-molecules-in-a-solid-by-w-e-moerner-and-l-kador.md) (pp. 2535–2538). Different local environments spread [photon absorption](photon-absorption.md) [frequencies](frequency.md); tuning into the sparse wings selected few resonant [molecules](molecule-split.md) despite many [molecules](molecule-split.md) in the illuminated volume.

Their tunable dye [laser](laser-split.md) focused through a $0.85$ [numerical aperture](numerical-aperture.md) lens onto a $100$–$200\,\mathrm{\mu m}$ crystal immersed in [superfluid](superfluid.md) [helium](helium.md) at $1.6\,\mathrm K$. [Frequency modulation](frequency-modulation.md) accompanied either a $5\,\mathrm{kHz}$ [electric field](electric-field-split.md) between coated sapphire disks, detected at $10\,\mathrm{kHz}$, or a $2\,\mathrm{MHz}$ [quartz](quartz.md) shear transducer. An avalanche [photodiode](photodiode.md) measured transmitted-light [photon absorption](photon-absorption.md), not [fluorescence](fluorescence-split.md). Double modulation suppressed residual [laser](laser-split.md) amplitude background.[pp. 2535–2537: sample, lens, dye laser, FM/Stark and FM/ultrasound apparatus and detection](optical-detection-and-spectroscopy-of-single-molecules-in-a-solid-by-w-e-moerner-and-l-kador.md)

Repeated scans gave the predicted modulated line shape. Blank crystals and off-resonance scans gave background; changing modulation [frequency](frequency.md) expanded and contracted the feature as predicted. Agreement between both perturbation methods supported the individual-absorber inference. The authors acknowledged out-of-focus [molecules](molecule-split.md) and the unlikely alternative of multiple absorbers with exactly coincident [frequencies](frequency.md).[Fig. 1(d–g), Fig. 2(b–g), and pp. 2537–2538: blanks, modulation-frequency changes, background and coincident-absorber qualification](optical-detection-and-spectroscopy-of-single-molecules-in-a-solid-by-w-e-moerner-and-l-kador.md)

This is evidence for a selected absorber in a prepared, known [chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md) and host. It does not establish arbitrary unknown [molecule](molecule-split.md) identity from one spectral feature, provide an atom-resolved photograph, or count every [molecule](molecule-split.md) in the crystal. A measurement sensitive to only a narrow excitation range cannot detect a [molecule](molecule-split.md) whose transition lies outside that range. This distinction separates the total particle inventory from the number responding to a particular probe.
