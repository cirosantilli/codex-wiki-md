<h1 id="gelfand-naimark-segal-construction">Gelfand–Naimark–Segal construction</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [State on a C-star algebra](state-on-a-c-star-algebra.md)

For a [state on a C-star algebra](state-on-a-c-star-algebra.md) $x$ on a unital algebra $A$, put $\langle[a],[b]\rangle=x(b^*a)$ on $A/N_x$, with the [inner product](inner-product-split.md) linear in its first argument. The [Cauchy–Schwarz inequality for positive C-star functionals](cauchy-schwarz-inequality-for-positive-c-star-functionals.md) makes the null [vectors](vector.md) pair to zero with every [vector](vector.md). Moreover $c^*c\leq\|c\|^21$ gives $x((ca)^*(ca))\leq\|c\|^2x(a^*a)$, so $N_x$ is a [left ideal](left-ideal.md) and $[a]\mapsto[ca]$ is well-defined and bounded by $\|c\|$. [Completion](hilbert-space-completion.md) gives a [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) $H_x$ and a star-representation $\pi_x(c)[a]=[ca]$. The adjoint identity follows from $x(b^*ca)=\langle[a],\pi_x(c^*)[b]\rangle$. The unit [vector](vector.md) $\xi_x=[1]$ is cyclic and recovers the state as $x(a)=\langle\pi_x(a)\xi_x,\xi_x\rangle$.
