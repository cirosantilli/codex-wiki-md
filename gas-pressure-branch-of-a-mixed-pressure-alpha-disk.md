# Gas-pressure branch of a mixed-pressure alpha disk

↑ **Parent:** [Vertically averaged alpha closure for a mixed-pressure layer](vertically-averaged-alpha-closure-for-a-mixed-pressure-layer.md)

For small positive radiation-to-gas ratio, constant [opacity](opacity.md), fixed alpha and molecular constants, eliminate $\lambda$ between $\lambda\propto H\Omega\Sigma$ and $\lambda\Sigma\propto H^7\Omega^6$. This gives $H^6\propto\Sigma^2\Omega^{-5}$ and $\bar\nu\propto\Sigma^{2/3}\Omega^{-2/3}$. [Keplerian rotation](keplerian-disk.md) supplies the displayed radial power. Since $\bar\nu\Sigma\propto\Sigma^{5/3}$, its derivative with respect to [surface density](surface-density-of-a-disk.md) is positive and local viscous perturbations diffuse rather than amplify.
