# Ventilated filling-box relaxation

↑ **Parent:** [Displacement ventilation](displacement-ventilation.md)

For total plume flux $P(h)$, fixed ceiling exhaust $F$, zero ambient [buoyancy](buoyancy.md), and uniform upper-layer [reduced gravity](reduced-gravity-split.md) $b$, the ideal two-layer balances are

$$
A\dot h=F-P(h),\qquad \frac{d}{dt}\bigl[A(H-h)b\bigr]=B-Fb.
$$

At $P(h_*)=F$ and $b_*=B/F$, [linearization](linearization.md) yields the decay times $\tau_h=A/P'(h_*)$ and $\tau_b=A(H-h_*)/F$. For $P(h)\propto h^{5/3}$, $\tau_h=3Ah_*/(5F)$. Upper-layer [buoyancy](buoyancy.md) relaxation usually dominates when $h_*\ll H$. Exact equilibrium is approached asymptotically; a fixed small relative tolerance takes order $\max(\tau_h,\tau_b)\log(1/\varepsilon)$ after the initial transient.
