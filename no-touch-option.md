# No-touch option

↑ **Parent:** [Barrier option](barrier-option.md)

A contract paying a fixed amount at expiry if specified barriers have not been touched. Its complement, with the same expiry payment convention, is a [one-touch option](one-touch-option.md). Paying upon first touch rather than at expiry changes the discounting and gives a different contract.
