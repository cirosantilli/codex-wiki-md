# Twisted half-smash product

↑ **Parent:** [Indexed prespectrum](indexed-prespectrum.md)

For compact $X$ over the space of [linear isometries](linear-isometry-of-hilbert-spaces.md) $U\to U\prime$, choose a [subordinate flag for a family of linear isometries](subordinate-flag-for-a-family-of-linear-isometries.md) $V_i,W_i$. The [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) $\xi_i$ has fiber $W_i\ominus f_x(V_i)$. Form $P(W_i)=\operatorname{Th}(\xi_i)\wedge E(V_i)$. Its structure maps use the orthogonal decomposition

$$
(W_j\ominus W_i)\oplus\xi_i\cong\xi_j\oplus f_x(V_j\ominus V_i)
$$

and the structure map of $E$. The [twisted half-smash product](twisted-half-smash-product.md) is the [spectrification](spectrification.md) $LP$, after extending from the cofinal flag. Unlike an arbitrary replacement by $X_+\wedge E$, this definition retains the specified isometry family at the point-set level.
