# Long exact sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Exact sequence](exact-sequence.md)

A long exact sequence is a sequence of [abelian groups](abelian-group.md) or [modules](module-mathematics.md) with arbitrarily many consecutive maps, for which the image of every map equals the kernel of the next. A [short exact sequence of cochain complexes](short-exact-sequence-of-cochain-complexes.md) yields a long exact sequence of their [cohomology](cohomology-split.md), with [connecting homomorphisms](connecting-homomorphism.md) increasing degree by one. The exact sequences of [relative cohomology](relative-cohomology.md) and the [Bockstein homomorphism](bockstein-homomorphism.md) are examples.
