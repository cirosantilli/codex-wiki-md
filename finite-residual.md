# Finite residual

↑ **Parent:** [Residually finite group](residually-finite-group.md)

The finite residual consists of all elements killed by every homomorphism to a finite group. A [group](group-split.md) is a [residually finite group](residually-finite-group.md) precisely when its finite residual is trivial. Intersecting all finite-index subgroups gives the same result: the [normal core](core-group-theory.md) of each such subgroup is normal of finite index and lies inside it.
