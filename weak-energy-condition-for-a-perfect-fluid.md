# Weak energy condition for a perfect fluid

↑ **Parent:** [Weak energy condition](weak-energy-condition.md)

For a [perfect fluid in general relativity](perfect-fluid-in-general-relativity.md) in [metric signature](metric-signature.md) $(-,+,+,+)$, the [weak energy condition](weak-energy-condition.md) is equivalent to $\rho\ge0$ and $\rho+p\ge0$. A normalized observer's [four-velocity](four-velocity.md) $W$ has [Lorentz factor](lorentz-factor.md) $\gamma=-u\cdot W\ge1$ relative to the fluid, and $T_{ab}W^aW^b=\rho+(\rho+p)(\gamma^2-1)$. The case $\gamma=1$ forces the first inequality and arbitrarily large $\gamma$ forces the second; conversely the same expression proves sufficiency.
