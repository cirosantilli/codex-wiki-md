# Hilbert proof system

↑ **Parent:** [Proof theory](proof-theory.md)

A [Hilbert proof system](hilbert-proof-system.md) specifies [axiom schemes](axiom.md) and [inference rules](inference-rule.md), commonly including [modus ponens](implication-elimination-rule.md). A [formal proof](formal-proof.md) is a finite [sequence](sequence.md) of [axiom instances](axiom.md), permitted assumptions and conclusions obtained from preceding lines by those rules. [Soundness](soundness-theorem-for-propositional-logic.md) and completeness compare this syntactic [derivability](formal-proof.md) with semantic validity or [semantic entailment](semantic-entailment.md). These properties concern the particular chosen [axioms](axiom.md) and rules.
