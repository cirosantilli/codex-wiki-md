# First-order stellar radiation force on a blackbody grain

↑ **Parent:** [Poynting–Robertson drag](poynting-robertson-drag.md)

For a [blackbody](blackbody.md) grain with [geometric cross-section](geometric-collision-cross-section.md) $\sigma$, [mass](mass.md) $m$ and stellar-frame [velocity](velocity.md) $\boldsymbol v$, define the [radiation-pressure coefficient](radiation-pressure-coefficient.md) $\beta=L_\star\sigma/(4\pi cGM_\star m)$. To first order in $v/c$, steady absorption and isotropic rest-frame reradiation give

$$
\boldsymbol F_{\rm rad}=\frac{m\beta GM_\star}{r^2}
\left[\left(1-\frac{\dot r}c\right)\widehat{\boldsymbol r}-\frac{\boldsymbol v}c\right].
$$

The relative incoming flux provides $1-\dot r/c$; [photon](photon.md) reradiation carries stellar-frame [momentum](momentum.md) proportional to $\boldsymbol v/c$. Subtracting static [radiation pressure](radiation-pressure.md) gives radial drag $-2m\beta GM_\star\dot r/(cr^2)$ and transverse drag $-m\beta GM_\star v_\theta/(cr^2)$. Non-blackbody grains require their actual [radiation-pressure efficiency](radiation-pressure-efficiency.md).
