# Auger electron

↑ **Parent:** [Auger effect](auger-effect.md)

An [electron](electron.md) ejected by the [Auger effect](auger-effect.md). Its [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) is determined mainly by the atomic levels involved, rather than directly by the [energy](energy-split.md) of the radiation that created the original vacancy.
