# Wavefront

↑ **Parent:** [Physical optics](physical-optics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wavefront)

A wavefront is a surface of constant [phase function](phase-function.md). A [plane wave](plane-wave.md) has planar wavefronts; variations in [refractive index](refractive-index.md) distort them.
