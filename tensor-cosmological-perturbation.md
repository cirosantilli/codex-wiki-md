# Tensor cosmological perturbation

↑ **Parent:** [Linear cosmological perturbation theory](linear-cosmological-perturbation-theory-split.md)

A tensor cosmological perturbation is the transverse, trace-free part of a metric perturbation about a [Friedmann-Lemaître-Robertson-Walker metric](friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md). In conformal coordinates, its spatial metric has the form $g_{ij}=a^2(\delta_{ij}+h_{ij})$, with $\partial_i h_{ij}=0$ and $h_{ii}=0$. Its two independent polarizations describe a [gravitational wave](gravitational-wave.md). [Neutrino tensor anisotropic stress](neutrino-tensor-anisotropic-stress.md) can damp its evolution through [neutrino free streaming](neutrino-free-streaming.md).
