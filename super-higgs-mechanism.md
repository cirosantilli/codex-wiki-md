# Super-Higgs mechanism

↑ **Parent:** [Supergravity](supergravity.md)

When local [supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md) is spontaneously broken, the [gravitino](gravitino.md) absorbs the [goldstino](goldstino.md) and becomes massive. The two [goldstino](goldstino.md) states provide its longitudinal spin-$1/2$ polarizations. With only global [supersymmetry breaking](supersymmetry-breaking.md), the [goldstino](goldstino.md) remains a physical massless particle. An ordinary [supersymmetric Higgs mechanism](supersymmetric-higgs-mechanism.md) instead makes an internal spin-one [gauge boson](gauge-boson.md) massive and can leave [supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md) unbroken.
