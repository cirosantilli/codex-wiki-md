# Maximal ideal space of a commutative Banach algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Character space of an algebra](character-space-of-an-algebra.md)

For a commutative complex unital [Banach algebra](banach-algebra-split.md), [maximal ideals](maximal-ideal.md) correspond bijectively to [characters of an algebra](character-of-an-algebra.md) by taking kernels. Give the maximal ideal space the [Gelfand topology](gelfand-topology.md) through this correspondence. It is a [compact Hausdorff space](compact-hausdorff-space.md), since the characters form a weak-star closed subset of the dual unit ball, which is compact by the [Banach-Alaoglu theorem](banach-alaoglu-theorem.md).
