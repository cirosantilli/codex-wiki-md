<h1 id="folner-condition-implies-amenability">Følner condition implies amenability</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Følner condition](folner-condition.md)

Normalized counting measures on a [Følner sequence](folner-sequence.md) have a convergent subnet in the compact product $[0,1]^{\mathcal P(G)}$. The limit is a left-invariant finitely additive probability measure, so every group satisfying the [Følner condition](folner-condition.md) is an [amenable group](amenable-group.md).
