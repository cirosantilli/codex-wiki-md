# Almost commutative algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Filtered algebra](filtered-algebra.md)

An almost commutative algebra over a [field](field.md) is an [algebra](algebra-split.md) with an exhaustive increasing nonnegative multiplicative [filtration](filtration-probability-theory.md) whose [associated graded ring](associated-graded-ring.md) is commutative and finitely generated as an algebra over the field. A common presentation uses finitely many degree-one generators, scalar degree-zero part, and [commutators](commutator.md) that lower degree. The finite-generation requirement is essential: an infinitely generated commutative [polynomial ring](polynomial-ring.md) has commutative associated graded algebra but need not be [Noetherian](noetherian-ring.md).
