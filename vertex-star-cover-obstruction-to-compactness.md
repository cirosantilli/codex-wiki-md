# Vertex-star cover obstruction to compactness

↑ **Parent:** [Open star in a simplicial complex](open-star-in-a-simplicial-complex.md)

The [topological realization](geometric-realization-of-a-simplicial-complex.md) of a [simplicial complex](simplicial-complex.md) with infinitely many [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) is not [compact](compact-space.md). Its vertex [open star in a simplicial complex](open-star-in-a-simplicial-complex.md) form an [open cover](open-cover.md), but a vertex belongs only to its own star. Thus every finite collection of vertex stars omits all the other vertices. The argument uses the [weak topology of a simplicial realization](weak-topology-of-a-simplicial-realization.md), in which a vertex star is [open](open-set.md) because its complement is the realization of the subcomplex of simplices missing that vertex. No local finiteness assumption is required.
