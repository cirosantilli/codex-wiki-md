# Forgotten symmetric function

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetric-function involution](symmetric-function-involution.md)

The coefficient of $m_\mu$ in $f_\lambda$ counts distinct rearrangements of the parts of $\lambda$ whose prefix-sum set contains the prefix sums of $\mu$. Indeed, [Hall inner product of symmetric functions](hall-inner-product-of-symmetric-functions.md) duality between $m_\lambda,h_\lambda$ turns this coefficient into $(-1)^{|\lambda|-\ell(\lambda)}$ times the coefficient of $h_\lambda$ in $e_\mu$. Expanding $E(t)=H(-t)^{-1}$ expresses each $e_{\mu_j}$ as a signed sum of compositions of $\mu_j$. Concatenating these compositions gives precisely the counted rearrangements, and every sign is $(-1)^{|\lambda|-\ell(\lambda)}$.
