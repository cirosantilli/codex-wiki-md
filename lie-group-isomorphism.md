# Lie group isomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Lie group homomorphism](lie-group-homomorphism.md)

An isomorphism of [Lie groups](lie-group.md) is a [group isomorphism](group-isomorphism.md) that is also a [diffeomorphism](diffeomorphism.md). Both the [homomorphism](homomorphism.md) and its inverse preserve the smooth structures. Its identity differential is a [Lie algebra isomorphism](lie-algebra-isomorphism.md), but an isomorphism of [Lie algebras](lie-algebra-split.md) determines a global [Lie group isomorphism](lie-group-isomorphism.md) without extra period data only for connected [simply connected](simply-connected-space.md) [groups](group-split.md).
