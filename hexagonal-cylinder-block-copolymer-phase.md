# Hexagonal cylinder block-copolymer phase

↑ **Parent:** [Microphase separation](microphase-separation.md)

A microdomain pattern of approximately parallel cylinders arranged on a two-dimensional hexagonal lattice in the other block's matrix. It is conventionally labelled H in many diblock [phase diagrams](phase-diagram.md). Cylinder orientation and defects are additional properties beyond the local morphology.
