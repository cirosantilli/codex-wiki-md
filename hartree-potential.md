# Hartree potential

↑ **Parent:** [Hartree equations](hartree-equations.md)

The local mean [Coulomb potential](coulomb-potential-energy.md) generated by the occupied [electron](electron.md) density. A separate positive background or external charge contribution must be included where appropriate. Hartree-Fock exchange is nonlocal and removes equal-orbital self-interaction from the total direct-plus-exchange result.
