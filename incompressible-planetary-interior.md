# Incompressible planetary interior

↑ **Parent:** [Exoplanet interior](exoplanet-interior.md)

An idealized self-gravitating interior with constant [mass density](density.md). Negligible surface pressure and spherical [hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) give $M=4\pi\rho R^3/3$ and the [uniform-density planetary pressure profile](uniform-density-planetary-pressure-profile.md). It is the $n=0$ [polytrope](polytrope.md) interpretation, rather than a literal substitution into $P=K\rho^{1+1/n}$. It approximates weak compression and misses real compositional stratification.
