# Zero-mean bounded-variation space

↑ **Parent:** [Function of bounded variation on a domain](function-of-bounded-variation-on-a-domain.md)

The zero-mean bounded-variation space is

$$
BV_0(\Omega)=\left\{u\in BV(\Omega):\int_\Omega u\,dx=0\right\}.
$$

On a bounded connected [Lipschitz domain](lipschitz-domain.md), removing the constant nullspace makes total variation coercive on this subspace by the [Poincaré inequality for total variation](poincare-inequality-for-total-variation.md).
