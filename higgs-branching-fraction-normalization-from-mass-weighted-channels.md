# Higgs branching-fraction normalization from mass-weighted channels

↑ **Parent:** [Higgs decay to a fermion pair](higgs-decay-to-a-fermion-pair.md)

At leading order, neglecting phase-space suppression and radiative corrections, [Higgs decay to a fermion pair](higgs-decay-to-a-fermion-pair.md) has partial [decay width](decay-width.md) proportional to $N_c^fm_f^2$, with $N_c=3$ for a quark and one for a lepton. A known sum of [branching fractions](branching-fraction.md) therefore fixes the proportionality between these weights and individual branching fractions without requiring the Higgs vacuum expectation value. The same normalization estimates a small additional fermionic channel if its weight is not included in the known sum.
