# Lagrangian torus

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrangian submanifold](lagrangian-submanifold.md)

A [Lagrangian submanifold](lagrangian-submanifold.md) diffeomorphic to an $n$-dimensional [flat torus](flat-torus.md) in a $2n$-dimensional [symplectic manifold](symplectic-manifold.md). Compact connected regular levels of a [completely integrable Hamiltonian system](integrable-hamiltonian-system.md) are examples, by the [Arnold-Liouville theorem](liouville-arnold-theorem.md).
