# Join of measurable partitions

↑ **Parent:** [Measurable partition](measurable-partition.md)

The join is the common refinement of two [measurable partitions](measurable-partition.md), with atoms given by their nonempty intersections. For a [measure-preserving transformation](measure-preserving-transformation.md), the block partition is $\xi_0^{n-1}=\bigvee_{j=0}^{n-1}T^{-j}\xi$.
