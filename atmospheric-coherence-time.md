# Atmospheric coherence time

↑ **Parent:** [Atmospheric turbulence](atmospheric-turbulence.md)

The timescale on which turbulent optical-path fluctuations decorrelate constrains short-exposure imaging and adaptive-optics control latency. In a frozen-flow approximation it scales as $r_0/v_{\rm eff}$, with an effective turbulence-weighted wind speed. It is not the temporal coherence time set by the optical [bandwidth](bandwidth-signal-processing.md).[§3.1, printed p.13, atmospheric coherence length and time](naco-user-manual-by-european-southern-observatory.md)
