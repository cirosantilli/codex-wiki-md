# Quantum Monte Carlo

↑ **Parent:** [Monte Carlo method](monte-carlo-method-split.md)

Stochastic methods for quantum many-particle expectation values or ground-state projection. Variational and diffusion approaches can include correlations beyond a single [Slater determinant](slater-determinant.md); [fermion](fermion.md) signs or nodal constraints and finite-system extrapolation require explicit control.
