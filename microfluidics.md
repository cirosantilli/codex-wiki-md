# Microfluidics

↑ **Parent:** [Fluid mechanics](fluid-mechanics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microfluidics)

[Microfluidics](microfluidics.md) controls and studies small fluid volumes in confined channels. In cellular assays it can impose reproducible flow and chemical fields, but channel geometry, wall interactions and flow-induced stresses can themselves alter behaviour.
