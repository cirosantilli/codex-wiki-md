# Hagfish

↑ **Parent:** [Cyclostome](cyclostome.md)

A jawless [vertebrate](vertebrate.md) with a strongly specialized feeding apparatus and slime-producing system. Soft-tissue [anatomy](anatomy.md) and secondary modifications make the ordinary [fossil record](fossil-record.md) difficult to interpret.
