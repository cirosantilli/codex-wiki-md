# Simplicial complex

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic topology](algebraic-topology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplicial_complex)

An abstract [simplicial complex](simplicial-complex.md) is a pair $K=(V,\Sigma)$, where $V$ is a [set](set-split.md) of [vertices of a simplicial complex](vertex-of-a-simplicial-complex.md) and $\Sigma$ is a collection of finite nonempty subsets, called [simplexes](simplex.md). Every singleton $\{v\}$ belongs to $\Sigma$, and every nonempty subset of a simplex is a simplex. Its [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) is the supremum of $|\sigma|-1$ over $\sigma\in\Sigma$. Some conventions also include an empty simplex; it contributes no points to the [geometric realization](geometric-realization-of-a-simplicial-complex.md).
