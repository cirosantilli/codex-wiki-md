# Reaction coordinate

↑ **Parent:** [Reaction mechanism](reaction-mechanism.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reaction_coordinate)

A [reaction coordinate](reaction-coordinate.md) parametrizes changes in molecular configuration along a [chemical reaction](chemical-reaction-split.md) path. It can involve a [bond length](bond-length.md), a [bond angle](bond-angle.md), or a combination of geometric changes. It is not elapsed time and is not the bulk [extent of reaction](extent-of-reaction.md): one describes configurations, while the other accounts for how much material has converted. The precise choice matters when interpreting an [energy profile](energy-profile-chemistry.md). See the [IUPAC Gold Book: reaction coordinate](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/R05168).
