# Galactic bulge

↑ **Parent:** [Galaxy](galaxy-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galactic_bulge)

A [galactic bulge](galactic-bulge.md) is a centrally concentrated [stellar population](stellar-population.md) in a [galaxy](galaxy-split.md), usually thicker than its disk. Its origin, rotation and random stellar motions can differ from those of the disk; bulge light is often described by a [Sérsic profile](sersic-profile.md).
