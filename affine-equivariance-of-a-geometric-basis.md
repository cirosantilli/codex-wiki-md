# Affine equivariance of a geometric basis

↑ **Parent:** [Computer aided geometric design](computer-aided-geometric-design.md)

A geometry representation with scalar [partition of unity](partition-of-unity.md) basis functions commutes with every [affine map](affine-map.md) of its [control points](control-point.md). For $T(x)=Ax+b$, linearity moves $A$ through the sum and the sum-to-one identity supplies the translation $b$. Positivity is needed for a [convex hull](convex-hull.md) property, but not for this equivariance. The same argument applies to linear [subdivision matrices](subdivision-matrix.md) whose rows sum to one. General [projective transformations](projective-linear-transformation.md) require homogeneous [control points](control-point.md) and rational dehomogenization rather than transforming Euclidean controls with unchanged weights.
