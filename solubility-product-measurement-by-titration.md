# Solubility product measurement by titration

↑ **Parent:** [Solubility product](solubility-product.md)

How can an invisible dissolved inventory be measured? The unsigned laboratory handout [Solubility Product Constant, Ksp, for Calcium Hydroxide by Acid-Base Titration](https://www.webassign.net/question_assets/ucscgencheml1/updated_labs2020/lab-10-procedure-2020.pdf), Procedure, starts with saturated [calcium hydroxide](calcium-hydroxide.md), supplied by the teacher or prepared from [calcium](calcium.md) and [water](water.md). Its apparatus includes filter paper, a funnel, stoppered flasks, a [volumetric pipette](volumetric-pipette.md), a volumetric flask and a [burette](burette.md). Two successive rounds of [filtration](filtration-chemistry.md) remove particles. The filtrate is stoppered to reduce [carbon dioxide](carbon-dioxide.md) exposure. A $10.00\,\mathrm{mL}$ [aliquot](aliquot.md) is diluted with deionized [water](water.md) and titrated with standardized [hydrochloric acid](hydrochloric-acid.md); [bromothymol blue](bromothymol-blue.md) gives a pale-green [titration endpoint](titration-endpoint.md). The readout is the difference between final and initial [burette](burette.md) readings; four consistent trials are required.[pp. 3–7, Procedure: gravity filtration steps 2–7, HCl preparation steps 8–14, and titrations steps 15–19](solubility-product-constant-ksp-for-calcium-hydroxide-by-acid-base-titration-university-of-california-santa-cruz.md)

Unlike AB, [calcium hydroxide](calcium-hydroxide.md) has one-to-two dissolution [stoichiometry](stoichiometry.md):

$$
\mathrm{Ca(OH)_2}(s)\rightleftharpoons\mathrm{Ca}^{2+}(aq)+2\mathrm{OH}^-(aq),\qquad
K_{\mathrm{sp},c}=[\mathrm{Ca}^{2+}][\mathrm{OH}^-]^2.
$$

For [molar solubility](molar-solubility.md) $s$ in the ideal model with no background [calcium](calcium.md) or [hydroxide](hydroxide.md), no additional [chemical reactions](chemical-reaction-split.md), and negligible [self-ionization of water](self-ionization-of-water.md), $[\mathrm{Ca}^{2+}]=s$ and $[\mathrm{OH}^-]=2s$. Hence $K_{\mathrm{sp},c}=4s^3$, with units $\mathrm M^3$; it is not the AB constant used in the figures. The [neutralization](neutralization.md) [chemical equation](chemical-equation.md) is $\mathrm H^++\mathrm{OH}^-\longrightarrow\mathrm H_2\mathrm O$. Let $C_H$ be the known [titrant](titrant.md) [molar concentration](molar-concentration.md), $v$ its delivered [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) at the [equivalence point](equivalence-point.md), and $V_a$ the original [aliquot](aliquot.md) [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md). Then the inventory calculation is

$$
h=[\mathrm{OH}^-]=\frac{C_Hv}{V_a},\qquad
s=\frac h2,\qquad K_{\mathrm{sp},c}=\frac{h^3}{2}.
$$

Dilution of the [aliquot](aliquot.md) changes its [concentration](concentration.md) during the [acid-base titration](acid-base-titration.md), but preserves its initial [amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md) of [hydroxide](hydroxide.md). Therefore $V_a$, rather than the diluted flask [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md), belongs in the denominator.

The handout identifies two biases: carried-over [calcium hydroxide](calcium-hydroxide.md) [solid](solid.md) dissolves during [acid-base titration](acid-base-titration.md), consuming extra [titrant](titrant.md); [carbon dioxide](carbon-dioxide.md) consumes [hydroxide](hydroxide.md) and forms [calcium carbonate](calcium-carbonate.md). Filtration and stoppering address these effects, while repeat trials test consistency. Its review questions also ask about dissolved ion pairs, which filtration cannot remove.[pp. 2–4, filtration and CO2 discussion; p. 9, Discussion Questions and Review Topics, ion pairs and dissolved CO2](solubility-product-constant-ksp-for-calcium-hydroxide-by-acid-base-titration-university-of-california-santa-cruz.md)

Further controls would compare a [water](water.md) blank, independently verify [titrant standardization](titrant-standardization.md), and record the saturation [temperature](temperature-split.md); these are proposed checks, not reported measurements. A [titration endpoint](titration-endpoint.md) estimates the [equivalence point](equivalence-point.md) and may introduce a systematic offset. Even perfect inventory measurement does not determine [activity coefficients](activity-coefficient.md). Recovering the [dimensionless](dimensionless-quantity.md) thermodynamic [solubility product](solubility-product.md) additionally requires

$$
K_{\mathrm{sp}}=\gamma_{\mathrm{Ca}}\gamma_{\mathrm{OH}}^2\frac{K_{\mathrm{sp},c}}{(c^\circ)^3}.
$$

This explains both the power and the limit of the experiment: [acid-base titration](acid-base-titration.md) measures a dissolved inventory, while the link from that inventory to a thermodynamic [equilibrium constant](equilibrium-constant.md) still depends on a chemical model.
