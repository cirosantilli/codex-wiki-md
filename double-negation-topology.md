# Double-negation topology

↑ **Parent:** [Grothendieck topology](grothendieck-topology.md)

The dense sieves form the double-negation [Grothendieck topology](grothendieck-topology.md). Its sheaf topos is a [Boolean topos](boolean-topos.md): the union of a subobject with its pseudocomplement is dense and thus becomes the whole object after sheafification. This need not be the [atomic topology](atomic-topology.md), which requires every nonempty sieve to be dense.
