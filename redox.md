# Redox

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical reaction](chemical-reaction.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Redox)

A [redox](redox.md) [chemical reaction](chemical-reaction.md) couples [oxidation](oxidation.md) and [reduction](reduction-chemistry.md). In the [electrode](electrode.md) examples here, [oxidation](oxidation.md) releases [Electrons](electron.md) and [reduction](reduction-chemistry.md) accepts them. See [Flowers et al., Chemistry 2e, §4.2: oxidation-reduction reactions](https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/4-2-classifying-chemical-reactions).
