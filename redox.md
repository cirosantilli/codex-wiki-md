# Redox

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical reaction](chemical-reaction-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Redox)

A [redox](redox.md) [chemical reaction](chemical-reaction-split.md) couples [oxidation](oxidation.md) and [reduction](reduction-chemistry.md). In the [electrode](electrode.md) examples here, [oxidation](oxidation.md) releases [electrons](electron.md) and [reduction](reduction-chemistry.md) accepts them. [§4.2, “Oxidation-Reduction Reactions”: paired electron loss and gain](chemistry-2e-by-paul-flowers-and-others.md)

[Redox](redox.md) can be recognized by changes in [oxidation state](oxidation-state.md): an increase records [oxidation](oxidation.md), a decrease [reduction](reduction-chemistry.md). These are formal [electron](electron.md) assignments, distinct from measured [partial atomic charges](partial-atomic-charge.md). [Half-reactions](half-reaction.md) expose the matching loss and gain, making [electric charge conservation](charge-conservation.md) visible.[§4.2, “Oxidation-Reduction Reactions”: oxidation-number definition and complementary processes](chemistry-2e-by-paul-flowers-and-others.md)

The [electron](electron.md) accounting need not describe free [electrons](electron.md) travelling between spatially separated [chemical species](chemical-species.md). For example, in $2\mathrm H_2+\mathrm O_2\longrightarrow2\mathrm H_2\mathrm O$, [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) changes from [oxidation state](oxidation-state.md) $0$ to $+1$ and [oxygen](oxygen.md) from $0$ to $-2$. Four [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) lose four formally assigned [electrons](electron.md) in total; two [oxygen](oxygen.md) [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) gain four. The [water](water.md) product has [covalent bonds](covalent-bond-split.md), and these assignments do not turn it into separated [ions](ion.md). See [ionic approximation of bonds](ionic-approximation-of-bonds.md) and [electron transfer and ionic character](electron-transfer-and-ionic-character.md).

By contrast, [neutralization](neutralization.md) can rearrange [chemical bonds](chemical-bond-split.md) without changing [oxidation states](oxidation-state.md):

$$
\mathrm H^+(aq)+\mathrm{OH}^-(aq)\longrightarrow\mathrm H_2\mathrm O(l).
$$

[Hydrogen](hydrogen.md) remains $+1$ and [oxygen](oxygen.md) remains $-2$. The aqueous [proton](proton.md) notation abbreviates its interaction with [water](water.md). This [acid-base chemistry](acid-base-chemistry-split.md) example conserves [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) but has no matching increase and decrease of [oxidation state](oxidation-state.md); movement of [ions](ion.md) or a change of [chemical bonds](chemical-bond-split.md) alone does not establish [redox](redox.md).
