# Access control

↑ **Parent:** [Computer security](computer-security.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Access_control)

Rules and mechanisms deciding which subjects may perform which operations on protected objects. [Discretionary access control](discretionary-access-control.md) permits designated owners to grant rights; the [Bell-LaPadula model](bell-lapadula-model.md) instead constrains information flow by classification.
