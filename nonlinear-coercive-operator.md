# Nonlinear coercive operator

↑ **Parent:** [Coercive operator](coercive-operator.md)

For a map $A:M\to X^*$ on a subset of a real [Banach space](banach-space-split.md), coercivity with respect to $u_0\in M$ means the displayed limit as $u\in M$ and $\|u-u_0\|\to\infty$. Here the brackets denote the [dual pairing](dual-pairing.md). This growth condition is useful in existence theorems for [monotone operators](monotone-operator.md). A linear Hilbert-space estimate $\langle Au,u\rangle\geq c\|u\|^2$, $c>0$, implies the condition at zero. Monotonicity alone does not: the zero operator is monotone and fails this growth condition on an unbounded space.
