# Von Mises conditions for extreme values

↑ **Parent:** [Maximum domain of attraction](maximum-domain-of-attraction.md)

For an absolutely continuous [distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md) with positive [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) near its [right endpoint of a distribution](right-endpoint-of-a-distribution.md), write $\eta=f/(1-F)$ for its [hazard function](hazard-function.md). The limits $t\eta(t)\to\alpha$ at an infinite endpoint or $(x_F-t)\eta(t)\to\alpha$ at a finite endpoint imply attraction to the [Fréchet distribution](frechet-distribution.md) or [negative Weibull distribution](negative-weibull-distribution.md), respectively. A continuously differentiable reciprocal [hazard function](hazard-function.md) with $(1/\eta)'\to0$ implies attraction to the [Gumbel distribution](gumbel-distribution.md), provided the [survival function](survival-function.md) tends to zero at the endpoint.
