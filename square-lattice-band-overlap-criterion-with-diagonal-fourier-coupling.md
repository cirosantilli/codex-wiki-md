# Square-lattice band-overlap criterion with diagonal Fourier coupling

↑ **Parent:** [Nearly-free electron model](nearly-free-electron-model.md)

For a divalent square [Bravais lattice](bravais-lattice.md), let $E_X=\hbar^2\pi^2/(2m_ea^2)$ and real [periodic potential](periodic-potential.md) coefficients $V_{10}<V_{11}<0$. The leading two-wave edge levels are $E_X\pm V_{10}$. The four-wave corner levels are $2E_X+V_{11}\pm2V_{10}$ and a doublet at $2E_X-V_{11}$. Comparing the lowest corner level with the upper edge level gives the displayed [indirect band gap](indirect-band-gap.md). The first spin-degenerate [energy band](energy-band.md) can hold the two [electrons](electron.md) per [primitive unit cell](primitive-unit-cell.md); it becomes isolated when $-E_X-V_{11}-3V_{10}>0$ in this truncation. This follows from resonant [degenerate perturbation theory](degenerate-perturbation-theory.md), and higher [plane waves](plane-wave.md) can shift the threshold.
