# Electromotive force

↑ **Parent:** [Electric current](electric-current.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electromotive_force)

[Electromotive force](electromotive-force.md) is the driving [work](work.md) per unit [electric charge](electric-charge.md) around an [electrical circuit](electrical-circuit.md), measured in [volts](volt.md), despite its name containing "force". For a stationary loop its electromagnetic contribution is $\mathcal E=\oint_C\mathbf E\cdot d\mathbf l$; for a moving [electrical conductor](electrical-conductor.md), include the [motional electromotive force](motional-electromotive-force.md). A nonzero closed-loop [line integral](line-integral.md) of the [electric field](electric-field.md) cannot be represented by a single-valued [electric potential](electric-potential.md) alone. An [electromotive force](electromotive-force.md) specifies the drive; the resulting [electric current](electric-current.md) also depends on the [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) and [self-inductance](self-inductance.md). [Feynman lectures, II.17.1: emf as a circuit integral](https://www.feynmanlectures.caltech.edu/II_17.html#Ch17-S1).
