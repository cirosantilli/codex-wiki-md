# First massive level of the open RNS string

↑ **Parent:** [Spinning string](spinning-string.md)

After the [GSO projection](gso-projection.md), the first massive open-string level has NS oscillator number $3/2$ and Ramond oscillator number $1$. Eight transverse directions give $8+64+\binom83=128$ NS states and $8\cdot8+8\cdot8=128$ R states. They form the massive Spin(9) representations $44+84$ and a gamma-traceless vector-spinor of dimension $9\cdot16-16=128$. The identical chiral oscillator count in a [closed string](closed-string.md) has the different mass normalization $\alpha'M^2=4$.
