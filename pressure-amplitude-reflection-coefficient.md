# Pressure-amplitude reflection coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Reflection coefficient](reflection-coefficient.md)

The reflected-to-incident compressional [pressure](pressure-split.md) or normal-stress amplitude ratio at a planar interface. For normal incidence between lossless homogeneous media, the displayed expression uses [acoustic impedances](acoustic-impedance.md) $Z_j=\rho_jV_{pj}$. The reflected [energy](energy-split.md) fraction is $r_p^2$, while a same-coordinate particle-velocity reflection coefficient has the opposite sign.
