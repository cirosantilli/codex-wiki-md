# One-loop Feynman diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Loop Feynman diagram](loop-feynman-diagram.md)

A [Feynman diagram](feynman-diagram.md) with one independent closed cycle and hence one independent [loop momentum](loop-momentum.md). An $n$-vertex polygon of [Dirac propagators](dirac-propagator.md) in four dimensions has [superficial degree of divergence](superficial-degree-of-divergence.md) $4-n$ before symmetry cancellations. For a [fermion loop](fermion-loop.md), the [closed fermion loop sign](closed-fermion-loop-sign.md) is separate from any [symmetry factor](feynman-diagram-symmetry-factor.md) or pairwise [charge conjugation](charge-conjugation.md) cancellation.
