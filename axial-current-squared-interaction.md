# Axial-current squared interaction

↑ **Parent:** [Axial current](axial-current.md)

Since the [chirality matrix](chirality-matrix.md) commutes with spinor Lorentz generators, $j_5^\mu=\bar\psi\gamma^\mu\gamma^5\psi$ transforms as a vector under proper [Lorentz transformations](lorentz-transformation.md) and as a [pseudovector](pseudovector.md) under [parity](parity.md). Contracting two copies gives the [Lorentz scalar](lorentz-scalar.md) $j_{5\mu}j_5^\mu$; the extra [parity](parity.md) signs cancel. [Lorentz invariance](lorentz-invariance.md) of this interaction does not require current conservation.
