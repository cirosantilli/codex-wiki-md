# Odd-dimensional real matrix with positive determinant has a positive eigenvalue

↑ **Parent:** [Eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md)

For a real [square matrix](square-matrix.md) $A$ of odd size, if $\det A>0$, then $A$ has a positive real [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md). Its [characteristic polynomial](characteristic-polynomial.md) $p(t)=\det(tI-A)$ is monic of odd degree, so $p(0)=-\det A<0$ and $p(t)\to+\infty$ as $t\to+\infty$. The [intermediate value theorem](intermediate-value-theorem.md) supplies a positive root. This proves the claim without assuming that all [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are real. The odd-size hypothesis is essential: $-I$ in even dimension has positive [determinant](determinant.md) and only the negative [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) $-1$.
