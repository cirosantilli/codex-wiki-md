# Dyadic interval

↑ **Parent:** [Dyadic partition](dyadic-partition.md)

A [dyadic interval](dyadic-interval.md) is one cell in a [dyadic partition](dyadic-partition.md). Its children are the left and right half intervals. Disjoint [dyadic intervals](dyadic-interval.md) at a fixed level produce orthogonal [indicator functions](indicator-function.md) and [independent](independent-random-variables.md) [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) increments.
