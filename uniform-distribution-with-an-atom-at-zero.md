# Uniform distribution with an atom at zero

↑ **Parent:** [Mixed probability distribution](mixed-probability-distribution.md)

For $0\leq q\leq1$ and $r>0$, this [mixed probability distribution](mixed-probability-distribution.md) has [probability mass](probability-mass.md) $1-q$ at zero and ordinary [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) $q/r$ on $(0,r)$. That ordinary density integrates to $q$, not to one; conditioned on $S>0$, the law is the [uniform distribution](continuous-uniform-distribution.md) on $(0,r)$. Its [cumulative distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md) is zero for $s<0$, $1-q+qs/r$ for $0\leq s<r$, and one for $s\geq r$.

For each positive integer $k$, integration gives $\mathbb E[S^k]=qr^k/(k+1)$. Thus the [expected value](expected-value-split.md) is $qr/2$ and the [variance](variance-split.md) is $qr^2/3-q^2r^2/4$. The nonzero atom contributes to normalization even though it contributes zero to positive moments. At $q=0$, conditioning on positive delay is undefined because the conditioning event has probability zero.
