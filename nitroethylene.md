# Nitroethylene

↑ **Parent:** [Alkene](alkene.md)

$\mathrm{CH_2{=}CHNO_2}$, an electron-poor [alkene](alkene.md) that can serve as a dienophile. Conversion of a resulting secondary nitro group to a [ketone](ketone.md) can connect a Diels–Alder reaction to a [Nef reaction](nef-reaction.md).
