# Tridentate ligand

↑ **Parent:** [Chelation](chelation.md)

A [coordination ligand](ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md) that coordinates one [metal](metal.md) through three donor atoms. A facial tridentate donor set occupies the vertices of one face of an octahedron.
