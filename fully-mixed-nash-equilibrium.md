# Fully mixed Nash equilibrium

↑ **Parent:** [Nash equilibrium](nash-equilibrium.md)

A [Nash equilibrium](nash-equilibrium.md) in which every player's [mixed strategy](mixed-strategy.md) is a [fully mixed strategy](fully-mixed-strategy.md). Every available action must therefore give the same maximal [expected payoff](expected-payoff.md) against the others' choices. These indifference equations, together with strictly positive [probabilities](probability.md) summing to one, suffice to verify such a [Nash equilibrium](nash-equilibrium.md).
