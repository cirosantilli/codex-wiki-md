# Hall scattering factor

↑ **Parent:** [Hall coefficient](hall-coefficient.md)

For one positive carrier population, define $r_H$ by $R_H=r_H/(ep)$ with $e>0$. Different statistical averages of energy-dependent scattering affect [hall conductivity](hall-conductivity.md) and longitudinal [electrical conductivity](electrical-conductivity.md), so $r_H$ need not be one. The measured [Hall mobility](hall-mobility.md) is $\mu_H=\sigma R_H=r_H\mu_{\rm drift}$. Multiple [charge carriers](charge-carrier.md) require a more general model even before such a correction is made.
