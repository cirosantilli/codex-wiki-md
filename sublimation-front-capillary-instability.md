# Sublimation-front capillary instability

↑ **Parent:** [Sublimation](sublimation.md)

For a locally linear ice temperature with positive gradient $G$ into the solid, a corrugated [sublimation](sublimation.md) front receives a perturbation in [heat flux](heat-flux-density.md) proportional to its horizontal wavenumber $\alpha$. The [Gibbs-Thomson effect](gibbs-thomson-relation.md) suppresses short waves, giving the displayed quasi-static growth rate. It requires $V/(\kappa\alpha)\ll1$ and $|\sigma|/(\kappa\alpha^2)\ll1$, as well as a perturbation depth short enough for the linear background approximation. Unstable modes have $0<\alpha<\sqrt{G/\Gamma}$.
