# Clamped-free beam under uniform load and endpoint force

↑ **Parent:** [Higher-order Euler-Lagrange equation](higher-order-euler-lagrange-equation.md)

For

$$
E[y]=\int_0^L\left(\frac A2(y'')^2+\rho gy\right)\,dx
$$

with the [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md) $y(0)=y'(0)=0$, $y''(L)=0$, and $-Ay'''(L)=F$, the [higher-order Euler-Lagrange equation](higher-order-euler-lagrange-equation.md) is

$$
Ay''''+\rho g=0.
$$

Its solution splits as

$$
y=y_0+y_F,
\qquad
y_0=-\frac{\rho g}{24A}x^2(6L^2-4Lx+x^2),
\qquad
y_F=\frac{F}{6A}x^2(3L-x).
$$
