# Root mean square magnetic field from beam power

↑ **Parent:** [Electromagnetic wave](electromagnetic-wave-split.md)

For a uniform plane-wave beam of cross section $A$, mean [intensity](intensity-physics.md) is $P/A=cB_{\rm rms}^2/\mu_0$. Hence $B_{\rm rms}=\sqrt{\mu_0P/(cA)}$. A sinusoidal peak [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) is $\sqrt2$ times the rms [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md). A [Gaussian beam](gaussian-beam.md) requires a stated waist or diameter convention before inferring its on-axis field from total [power](power.md).
