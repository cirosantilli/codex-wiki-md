# Rank-shifting automorphism construction of NFU

↑ **Parent:** [Type-shifting automorphism](type-shifting-automorphism.md)

In a model of [ZFC](zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory-with-choice.md), choose an external automorphism $j$ and an ordinal $\alpha$ with $j(\alpha)>\alpha$. Set $D_i=V_{j^i(\alpha)}$ for integer $i$, and declare the sets of type $i+1$ to be its objects that are subsets of $D_i$. Ordinary membership between successive types satisfies typed comprehension and set-extensionality. The automorphism shifts all types, yielding [NFU](new-foundations-with-urelements.md) by the type-zero collapse. A [Skolem hull](skolem-hull.md) of integer-indexed increasing ordinal indiscernibles supplies such a $j$ by shifting the indices. The model is externally ill-founded; a transitive well-founded universe cannot have such an automorphism.
