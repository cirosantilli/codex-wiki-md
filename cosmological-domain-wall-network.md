# Cosmological domain wall network

↑ **Parent:** [Barotropic equation of state](barotropic-equation-of-state.md)

An isotropic, noninteracting network of [domain walls](domain-wall.md) with energy density proportional to $a^{-1}$ has effective [equation-of-state parameter](equation-of-state-parameter.md) $w=-2/3$. This follows from [constant-equation-of-state density scaling](constant-equation-of-state-density-scaling.md) $\rho\propto a^{-3(1+w)}$.
