# Jacobi equation

↑ **Parent:** [Second variation](second-variation.md)

The Jacobi equation is the linearization of an [Euler-Lagrange equation](euler-lagrange-equation.md) about a stationary path. Its solutions describe infinitesimal one-parameter families of stationary paths.
