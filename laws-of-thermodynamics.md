# Laws of thermodynamics

↑ **Parent:** [Thermodynamics](thermodynamics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laws_of_thermodynamics)

The laws organize the equilibrium and process constraints of [thermodynamics](thermodynamics-split.md): the [zeroth law of thermodynamics](zeroth-law-of-thermodynamics.md) makes thermal equilibrium transitive, the [first law of thermodynamics](first-law-of-thermodynamics.md) expresses energy balance, the [Second law of thermodynamics](second-law-of-thermodynamics.md) restricts entropy changes, and the [Third law of thermodynamics](third-law-of-thermodynamics.md) describes the zero-temperature limit. Their analogy with horizon mechanics becomes quantitative through [Hawking temperature](hawking-temperature.md) and [Bekenstein-Hawking entropy](bekenstein-hawking-entropy.md).
