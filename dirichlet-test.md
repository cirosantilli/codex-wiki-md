# Dirichlet test

↑ **Parent:** [Convergent series](convergent-series.md)

If the partial sums of $\sum b_n$ are bounded and $a_n$ decreases to zero, then $\sum a_nb_n$ converges. The uniform version holds for functions $b_n(x)$ when their partial sums have one bound $M$ independent of $x$. Indeed partial sums starting at index $N$ have absolute value at most $2M$, and summation by parts bounds every weighted tail from $N$ to $K$ by $2Ma_N$. The [uniformly Cauchy sequence](uniformly-cauchy-sequence.md) criterion proves [uniform convergence](uniform-convergence.md). This justifies integrating a conditionally convergent [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md) on intervals staying away from its endpoint jump.
