# Four causes

↑ **Parent:** [Aristotelian natural philosophy](aristotelian-natural-philosophy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Four_causes)

[Aristotle](aristotle.md)'s four explanatory questions concern what something is made of, its form, the source of change and its end. The [final cause](final-cause.md) is the last of these; this is not a list of four modern interacting forces.[book II, chapter 3](physics-by-aristotle.md)
