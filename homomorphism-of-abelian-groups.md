# Homomorphism of abelian groups

↑ **Parent:** [Group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md)

A [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md) between [abelian groups](abelian-group.md), conventionally written additively. Its [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) and [image](image-of-a-function.md) are [subgroups](subgroup.md), and the additive formula implies preservation of zero, negation and every integer multiple. Maps on [Mordell-Weil groups](mordell-weil-group.md) induced by [isogenies of elliptic curves](isogeny-of-elliptic-curves.md) and by [good reduction](good-reduction-of-an-elliptic-curve.md) are examples.
