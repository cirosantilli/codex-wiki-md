# Mod-two intersection pairing of the Klein bottle

↑ **Parent:** [Klein bottle](klein-bottle.md)

A one-sided section loop and a two-sided fiber loop in the reflection [mapping torus](mapping-torus.md) have mod-two self-intersections one and zero and meet once. Their [intersection form](intersection-form.md) is $Q$. In the evaluation-dual [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) basis, the [Poincare duality pairing](poincare-duality-pairing.md) has matrix $Q^{-1}$, giving $t^2=0$ and $x^2=tx\ne0$.
