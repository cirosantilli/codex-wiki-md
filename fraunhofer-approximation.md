# Fraunhofer approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Fraunhofer diffraction](fraunhofer-diffraction.md)

The far-field approximation neglects quadratic variation of propagation phase across an aperture of characteristic size a at observation distance D. For two narrow slits separated by s, their path difference remains $s\sin\theta$ at a finite observation angle. The additional substitution $\sin\theta\simeq Y/D$ requires a small observation angle; it does not follow from the [Fraunhofer approximation](fraunhofer-approximation.md) itself.
