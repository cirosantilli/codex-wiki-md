# Nondegenerate stationary points of a planar harmonic function

↑ **Parent:** [Harmonic function](harmonic-function.md)

For a planar [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md), $u_{yy}=-u_{xx}$. Its [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) is therefore $\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\b&-a\end{pmatrix}$, with [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $\pm\sqrt{a^2+b^2}$. At a [stationary point](stationary-point.md) with nonzero [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) [determinant](determinant.md), both signs occur, so the point is a [saddle point](saddle-point.md). A zero determinant requires separate treatment and is not covered by this nondegenerate test. The result is the local quadratic counterpart of the [maximum principle](maximum-principle.md) for [harmonic functions](harmonic-function.md).
