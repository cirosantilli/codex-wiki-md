# Nonrelativistic quantum mechanics

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum mechanics](quantum-mechanics-split.md)

Nonrelativistic quantum mechanics describes systems at speeds small compared with the speed of light, usually with time as an external parameter and evolution governed by the [Schrödinger equation](schrodinger-equation.md).
