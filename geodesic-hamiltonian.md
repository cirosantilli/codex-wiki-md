# Geodesic Hamiltonian

↑ **Parent:** [Geodesic flow](geodesic-flow.md)

The metric defines this globally smooth [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) on the [cotangent bundle](cotangent-bundle.md). With $\omega=dx^i\wedge dp_i$ and $\iota_{X_H}\omega=dH$, its [Hamiltonian vector field](hamiltonian-vector-field.md) projects to affinely parametrized [geodesics](geodesic.md). The momentum is $p_i=g_{ij}\dot x^j$, and its conserved value is half the squared speed.
