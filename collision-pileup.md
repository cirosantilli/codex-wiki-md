# Collision pileup

↑ **Parent:** [Particle detection](particle-detection-split.md)

Additional collisions overlapping an event in the same beam crossing or detector response window. Extra tracks and [calorimeter](calorimeter.md) deposits affect particle-jet reconstruction, isolation and [missing transverse momentum](missing-transverse-momentum.md). Vertex association, subtraction and response [calibration](calibration-differential-geometry.md) reduce the effect but leave uncertainties that depend on collision conditions.
