# Transport vesicle

↑ **Parent:** [Organelle](organelle.md)

A [transport vesicle](transport-vesicle.md) is a membrane-bounded carrier that moves cargo between cellular compartments. Budding and fusion preserve the distinction between lumen-facing and [cytosol](cytosol.md)-facing membrane surfaces.
