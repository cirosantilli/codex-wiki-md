# NNS codon

↑ **Parent:** [Degenerate codon](degenerate-codon.md)

A [library](dna-library.md) [codon](codon.md) with any base at the first two positions and G or C at the third. Its 32 [codons](codon.md) represent the 20 standard [amino acids](amino-acid.md) unequally and include one [stop codon](stop-codon.md).
