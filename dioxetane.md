# Dioxetane

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical compound](chemical-compound.md)

A four-membered peroxide ring containing two adjacent [oxygen](oxygen.md) atoms and two [carbon](carbon.md) atoms. The parent 1,2-dioxetane has formula $\mathrm{C_2H_4O_2}$; its [ground state](ground-state.md) is normally described as a closed-shell singlet.
