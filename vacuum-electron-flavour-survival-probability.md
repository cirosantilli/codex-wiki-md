# Vacuum electron-flavour survival probability

↑ **Parent:** [Neutrino oscillation](neutrino-oscillation.md)

For coherent relativistic vacuum propagation and a [unitary](unitary-connection.md) [lepton mixing matrix](lepton-mixing-matrix.md), square $\sum_i|U_{ei}|^2\exp[-im_i^2L/(2E)]$ to obtain this [probability](probability.md). Only moduli of the mixing entries occur, so the ordinary three-state electron survival signal is independent of the intrinsic [CP](cp-symmetry.md) phase. Replacing two close fast phases by one is useful at short baselines but may fail over many periods.
