# Gas density

↑ **Parent:** [Density](density.md)

[Gas density](gas-density.md) is the [mass density](density.md) of a [gas](gas.md). For an [ideal gas](ideal-gas-split.md) sample of [amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md) $n$, mean [molar mass](molar-mass.md) $\overline M$ and [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) $V$, combine $m=n\overline M$ with $pV=nRT$ to obtain

$$
\rho=\frac{m}{V}=\frac{p\overline M}{RT}.
$$

Here $R$ is the [molar gas constant](molar-gas-constant.md). For a homogeneous [mixture](mixture-split.md) of [gases](gas.md), $\overline M=\sum_i x_iM_i$, where $x_i$ is the [mole fraction](mole-fraction.md) of component $i$. Equal [pressure](pressure-split.md) and [temperature](temperature-split.md) make a [gas density](gas-density.md) ratio equal to a mean-[molar mass](molar-mass.md) ratio within this model. [Argon detection in a binary gas mixture](argon-detection-in-a-binary-gas-mixture.md) gives a worked example and a [temperature](temperature-split.md) confounder.
