# Optically thin dust-mass estimator

↑ **Parent:** [Mass absorption coefficient](mass-absorption-coefficient.md)

For isothermal [optically thin](optically-thin-medium.md) dust at distance $D$, its measured spectral flux density determines its mass through $M_d=F_\nu D^2/[\kappa_\nu B_\nu(T)]$. Temperature and opacity uncertainty commonly dominate the inferred mass uncertainty.
