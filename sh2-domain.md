# SH2 domain

↑ **Parent:** [Protein domain](protein-domain.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SH2_domain)

An [SH2 domain](sh2-domain.md) is a [protein domain](protein-domain.md) that recognizes a [phosphotyrosine residue](phosphotyrosine-residue.md) together with surrounding sequence features. This selective binding recruits a [protein](protein-split.md) to a modified [receptor](receptor-biochemistry.md) or docking [protein](protein-split.md); it does not itself imply [catalysis](catalysis.md).[“Phosphorylated Tyrosines Serve as Docking Sites For Proteins With SH2 Domains”, Figure 15-53](molecular-biology-of-the-cell-4th-edition-by-bruce-alberts-and-others/signaling-through-enzyme-linked-cell-surface-receptors.md)
