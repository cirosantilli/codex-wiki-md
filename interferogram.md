# Interferogram

↑ **Parent:** [Interferometer](interferometer.md)

An [interferogram](interferogram.md) records detected [light intensity](light-intensity.md) produced by interference as a controlled position, phase or delay is varied. In [Fourier transform spectroscopy](fourier-transform-spectroscopy.md), the variable is the delay between two copies of the same field. Removing the constant background gives a correlation signal whose [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md) determines the source's spectral power density, subject to the measured delay window and instrumental response.
