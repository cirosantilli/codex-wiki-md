# Seismic lithosphere-asthenosphere boundary

↑ **Parent:** [Lithosphere](lithosphere.md)

A seismically inferred transition associated with the base of the [lithosphere](lithosphere.md), often identified by a decrease in [shear-wave velocity](shear-wave-velocity.md). Its depth and sharpness constrain combinations of [temperature](temperature-split.md), composition, hydration, [melt fraction](melt-fraction.md) and [seismic anisotropy](seismic-anisotropy.md). A sharp discontinuity inside a [craton](craton.md) can instead lie within its thick [lithospheric mantle](lithospheric-mantle.md).[Abstract and pp. 495–498, distinction between a sharp imaged interface and the possibly deeper cratonic boundary.](a-global-view-of-the-lithosphere-asthenosphere-boundary-by-catherine-a-rychert-and-peter-m-shearer.md)
