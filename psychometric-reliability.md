# Psychometric reliability

↑ **Parent:** [Psychometrics](psychometrics.md)

Consistency or precision of a psychological measure under specified conditions. Unreliable measures can weaken observed associations; high [reliability](psychometric-reliability.md) alone does not establish [construct validity](construct-validity.md).
