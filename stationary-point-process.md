# Stationary point process

↑ **Parent:** [Point process](point-process.md)

A [point process](point-process.md) on $\mathbb R^d$ is stationary if every [translation](translation-geometry.md) preserves its [probability law](probability-distribution-split.md). If its mean count in a unit cube is finite, its [intensity measure](intensity-measure-of-a-point-process.md) is $\mu(A)=\rho|A|$ for some $\rho\ge0$, where $|A|$ denotes [Lebesgue measure](lebesgue-measure.md). This follows because [stationarity](stationary-process.md) makes the mean [measure](measure-split.md) invariant under [translations](translation-geometry.md). A constant [intensity function of a point process](intensity-function-of-a-point-process.md) describes only these mean counts, and by itself asserts neither [stationarity](stationary-process.md) of the entire law nor [independence](independent-random-variables.md).[Section 8.1, Proposition 8.2, printed pp. 69-70: stationarity and constant intensity under a finite unit-cube mean](lectures-on-the-poisson-process-by-gunter-last-and-others.md)
