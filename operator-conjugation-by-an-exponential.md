# Operator conjugation by an exponential

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix exponential](matrix-exponential.md)

For finite [matrices](matrix.md), or formally on a common invariant domain where the expansion is meaningful, exponential conjugation is generated by the [commutator](commutator.md) map. Differentiating $F(s)=e^{sS}He^{-sS}$ gives $F^\prime=[S,F]$, and its Taylor series yields the nested-commutator expansion. An [anti-Hermitian](skew-hermitian-matrix.md) $S$ makes this a [unitary transformation](unitary-operator.md). For [perturbation theory](perturbation-theory.md) this may be used order by order without asserting convergence for arbitrary unbounded operators.
