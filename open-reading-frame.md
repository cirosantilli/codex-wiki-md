# Open reading frame

↑ **Parent:** [Reading frame](reading-frame.md)

A sequence interpreted in a particular [codon](codon.md) frame without an internal in-frame stop, with the boundary convention stated. Prediction of an [open reading frame](open-reading-frame.md) alone does not demonstrate [biological translation](translation-biology.md) or a stable functional product.
