# TGV divergence splitting

↑ **Parent:** [Total generalized variation](total-generalized-variation.md)

For a quadratic data term and divergence maps $A_1,A_2$, the dual constraint $A_1v\in D$ can be split through $\delta_D(A_1v)=\sup_w[\langle w,A_1v\rangle-\delta_D^*(w)]$. For a product of Euclidean row balls of radius $\beta$, its [support function](support-function.md) is $\delta_D^*(w)=\beta\sum_i\|w_i\|_2$. The resulting saddle coupling is $K(u,w)=A_2^*u-A_1^*w$. The [Chambolle–Pock algorithm](chambolle-pock-algorithm.md) then uses a row-ball projection, a quadratic [proximal operator](proximal-operator.md) and [radial soft thresholding](radial-soft-thresholding.md), with no projection onto an intersection involving a divergence operator.
