# Lie polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Free Lie algebra](free-lie-algebra.md)

A finite linear combination of iterated [Lie brackets](lie-bracket.md) in formal variables. It evaluates naturally in every [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md), and every [Lie algebra homomorphism](lie-algebra-homomorphism.md) preserves its evaluation. The homogeneous terms of the [Baker--Campbell--Hausdorff formula](baker-campbell-hausdorff-formula.md) are rational Lie polynomials; this permits the group law to be reconstructed from the bracket wherever the series converges.
