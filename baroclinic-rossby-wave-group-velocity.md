# Baroclinic Rossby-wave group velocity

↑ **Parent:** [Baroclinic Rossby wave](baroclinic-rossby-wave.md)

For fixed meridional wavenumber and deformation radius, differentiating $\omega=Uk-\beta k/(k^2+l^2+L_d^{-2})$ gives $c_{gx}=U+\beta(k^2-l^2-L_d^{-2})/(k^2+l^2+L_d^{-2})^2$. A localized packet uses this velocity rather than $\omega/k$. For $l=0$ its intrinsic sign changes at $|k|L_d=1$.
