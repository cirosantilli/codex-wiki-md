# Iwasawa theory

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic number theory](algebraic-number-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iwasawa_theory)

Iwasawa theory studies arithmetic objects in infinite towers of [number fields](number-field.md), often [Zp-extensions](zp-extension.md). Passing to inverse limits turns growing [ideal class groups](ideal-class-group.md), unit groups and [Galois groups](galois-group.md) into [modules](module-mathematics.md) over an [Iwasawa algebra](iwasawa-algebra.md). Their algebraic ranks and characteristic ideals organize the growth and relate it to [p-adic L-functions](p-adic-l-function.md).
