# O3 nonlinear sigma model

↑ **Parent:** [O(N) nonlinear sigma model](o-n-nonlinear-sigma-model.md)

In two spatial dimensions, a unit-vector field $\mathbf n:\mathbb R^2\to S^2$ has static energy $E=\int|\nabla\mathbf n|^2d^2x/2$. The sphere is also the [complex projective line](complex-projective-line.md), so its [stereographic projection](stereographic-projection.md) gives the equivalent complex field description. A constant value at spatial infinity compactifies a regular configuration to a map $S^2\to S^2$, whose [degree of a map between oriented manifolds](degree-of-a-map-between-oriented-manifolds.md) is its [topological charge](topological-charge.md).
