# Qubit stabilization by measurement feedback

↑ **Parent:** [Measurement-based quantum feedback](measurement-based-quantum-feedback.md)

An observed dissipative channel combined with direct feedback can create a modified [Lindblad operator](lindblad-operator.md) and a compensating [Quantum Hamiltonian](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md). Choosing those parameters can make a prescribed [pure state](pure-state.md) stationary and globally attractive. At exceptional parameter values, the dissipator can instead leave an entire family of states stationary, so algebraic target stationarity does not establish stabilization. Checking the [Affine Bloch equation](affine-bloch-equation.md) and its transverse [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) distinguishes these possibilities.
