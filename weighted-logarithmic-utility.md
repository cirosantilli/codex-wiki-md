# Weighted logarithmic utility

↑ **Parent:** [Proportional fairness](proportional-fairness.md)

The utility $\sum_rn_r\log x_r$ sums logarithmic utilities over individual flows. Its first-order condition gives [proportional fairness](proportional-fairness.md). Its negative is a [strictly convex function](strictly-convex-function.md) on active flow rates, giving uniqueness of those rates on a [convex set](convex-set.md) of feasible allocations.
