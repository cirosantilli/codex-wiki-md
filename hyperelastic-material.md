# Hyperelastic material

↑ **Parent:** [Finite elasticity](finite-elasticity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperelastic_material)

A hyperelastic material derives its elastic [stress](stress.md) from a [strain energy density](strain-energy-density.md). In reference-volume units its [first Piola-Kirchhoff stress tensor](first-piola-kirchhoff-stress-tensor.md) is the derivative of that density with respect to the [deformation gradient](deformation-gradient.md).
