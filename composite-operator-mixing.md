# Composite operator mixing

↑ **Parent:** [Composite operator](composite-operator.md)

Renormalization can relate bare operators to a matrix of renormalized operators, $\mathcal O_{i,B}=Z_{ij}\mathcal O_{j,R}$. The corresponding [anomalous dimension](anomalous-dimension.md) matrix acts on each insertion slot in the [Callan-Symanzik equation](callan-symanzik-equation.md).
