# Lagrangian Grassmannian

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrangian subspace](lagrangian-subspace.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lagrangian_Grassmannian)

The Lagrangian Grassmannian is the space of all [Lagrangian subspaces](lagrangian-subspace.md) of a symplectic vector space. The [unitary group](unitary-group.md) acts transitively on $\operatorname{LGr}(\mathbb R^{2n})$, and the stabilizer of $\mathbb R^n$ is the [orthogonal group](orthogonal-group.md) $O(n)$, giving

$$
\operatorname{LGr}(\mathbb R^{2n})\cong U(n)/O(n).
$$
