# Conjugates of a proper subgroup do not cover a finite group

↑ **Parent:** [Conjugation action](conjugation-action.md)

For a proper [subgroup](subgroup.md) $B$ of a [finite group](finite-group.md) $G$, the number $m$ of its conjugates is $[G:N_G(B)]\leq[G:B]$, because its [normalizer](normalizer.md) contains $B$. All conjugates contain the identity, so their union has size at most $1+m(|B|-1)\leq|G|-[G:B]+1<|G|$. Thus some element is not conjugate to any element of $B$. Finiteness is essential to this cardinal counting argument.
