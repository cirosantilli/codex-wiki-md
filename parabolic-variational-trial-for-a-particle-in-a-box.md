# Parabolic variational trial for a particle in a box

↑ **Parent:** [Infinite square well](infinite-square-well.md)

For an [infinite square well](infinite-square-well.md) of width $a$, the normalized trial $\widetilde\psi=\sqrt{30/a^5}\,x(a-x)$ obeys both [Dirichlet boundary conditions](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) and has no interior [nodes of a wavefunction](node-of-a-wavefunction.md). Its [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) is

$$
\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\frac{30}{a^5}\int_0^a(a-2x)^2\,dx
=\frac{5\hbar^2}{ma^2}.
$$

The ratio to the exact [ground-state energy](ground-state-energy.md) is $10/\pi^2=1.01321\ldots$. Its expansion in the normalized sine [eigenfunctions](eigenfunction.md) has coefficients $4\sqrt{60}/(\pi^3n^3)$ for odd $n$ and zero for even $n$. This supplies a concrete check of the [quantum variational principle](quantum-variational-principle.md) and shows that a small energy error can coexist with excited-state admixtures.
