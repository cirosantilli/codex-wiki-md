# Active mode locking

↑ **Parent:** [Mode locking](mode-locking.md)

An external periodic modulation of cavity loss, gain or phase fixes the relative phases of its [longitudinal cavity modes](longitudinal-cavity-mode.md). A transmission window produced with an [electro-optic modulator](electro-optic-modulator.md) at the pulse repetition frequency selects a circulating [optical pulse](optical-pulse.md). Modulation bandwidth and dispersion constrain the attainable pulse duration.
