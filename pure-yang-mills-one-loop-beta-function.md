# Pure Yang-Mills one-loop beta function

↑ **Parent:** [Charge renormalization](charge-renormalization.md)

For pure [Yang-Mills theory](yang-mills-theory.md) with a compact simple [gauge group](gauge-group.md) and canonically normalized [gauge field](gauge-field.md), the one-loop [renormalization-group beta function](beta-function-physics.md) is $\mu\,dg/d\mu=-11C_Ag^3/[3(4\pi)^2]+O(g^5)$, where $C_A>0$ is the [Casimir operator](casimir-element.md) eigenvalue in the [Adjoint representation](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md). This coefficient includes the gauge-field and [Faddeev-Popov ghost field](faddeev-popov-ghost.md) loops; it is the standard pure-gauge input when adding matter-loop contributions. Its negative sign gives [asymptotic freedom](asymptotic-freedom.md). Integrating the leading equation gives $g(\mu)^{-2}=g(\mu_0)^{-2}+11C_A\log(\mu/\mu_0)/[24\pi^2]$, while perturbation theory is valid. Combining this term with the [adjoint Dirac contribution to the Yang-Mills beta function](adjoint-dirac-contribution-to-the-yang-mills-beta-function.md) gives the [integer obstruction to one-loop marginality with adjoint Dirac fermions](integer-obstruction-to-one-loop-marginality-with-adjoint-dirac-fermions.md).
