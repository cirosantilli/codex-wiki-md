# L1 contraction of conditional expectation

↑ **Parent:** [Conditional expectation](conditional-expectation.md)

For an [integrable random variable](integrable-random-variable.md) $Y$, the [conditional expectation](conditional-expectation.md) satisfies $|\mathbb E[Y\mid\mathcal G]|\leq\mathbb E[|Y|\mid\mathcal G]$ [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md). Taking [expected values](expected-value.md) gives the [L1 contraction of conditional expectation](l1-contraction-of-conditional-expectation.md). Applying it to $Y_n-Y$ shows that [convergence in L1](convergence-in-l1.md) is preserved by [conditional expectation](conditional-expectation.md) with respect to any fixed [sigma-algebra](sigma-algebra.md).
