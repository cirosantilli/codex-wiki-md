# Dust-tail caustic

↑ **Parent:** [Steady dust-tail continuity equation](steady-dust-tail-continuity-equation.md)

A zero of the angular drift in a cold single-grain-size [dust tail](dust-tail.md) produces a formally divergent number density per unit angle. The divergence is integrable when the exact local angle-to-age map is retained. A spread of launch velocities or [radiation-pressure coefficients](radiation-pressure-coefficient.md) removes the singularity.
