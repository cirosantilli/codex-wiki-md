# Retinal bipolar cell

↑ **Parent:** [Neuron](neuron-split.md)

A retinal interneuron relaying [photoreceptor](photoreceptor.md) signals toward ganglion-cell circuits. ON and OFF types differ in how changes in [photoreceptor](photoreceptor.md) output affect their responses.
