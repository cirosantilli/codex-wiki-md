# Rubblising-to-dispersal collision-rate ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Rubblising collision](rubblising-collision.md)

For a common projectile [power-law size distribution](power-law-size-distribution.md), the rate of impacts exceeding a threshold $Q$ is proportional to $Q^{(1-\alpha)/3}$. Taking the strength-only threshold $Q_s=Q_aD^{-a}$ rather than the full [catastrophic disruption threshold](catastrophic-disruption-threshold.md) gives the displayed ratio. Here the strength-shattering rate counts all impacts above $Q_s$, including dispersive ones; subtracting $R_{
m cc}$ gives the bound-remnant-only [rubblising collision](rubblising-collision.md) rate. In a gravity-dominated cascade with $\alpha=3$, $a=1/2$, and $b=3/2$, it scales as $D^{4/3}$. Using the actual [strength-gravity disruption transition](strength-gravity-disruption-transition.md) minimum diameter gives coefficient $3^{-2/3}$ in the asymptotic rate ratio; treating that diameter as the equal-contribution crossover silently loses this factor.
