# Flattened power-law gravitational potential

↑ **Parent:** [Newtonian gravitational potential](newtonian-gravitational-potential.md)

A flattened power-law [Newtonian gravitational potential](newtonian-gravitational-potential.md) has equipotential spheroids $R^2+\lambda^2z^2=\text{constant}$. For $\Phi_0<0$ and $0<\beta<1$, its midplane [circular orbit](circular-orbit.md) angular frequency satisfies $\Omega^2=-\beta\Phi_0R_0^\beta R^{-\beta-2}$.
