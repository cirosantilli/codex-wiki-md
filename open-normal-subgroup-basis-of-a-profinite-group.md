# Open normal subgroup basis of a profinite group

↑ **Parent:** [Profinite group](profinite-group.md)

Every identity neighbourhood of a compact Hausdorff totally disconnected [topological group](topological-group-split.md) contains an open [normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md). Choose a [clopen set](clopen-set.md) $U$ containing the identity inside that neighbourhood. Compactness and continuity give a symmetric identity neighbourhood $V$ with $VU\subseteq U$, hence $VU=U$. The setwise stabilizer $H=\{g:gU=U\}$ is an open [subgroup](subgroup.md) contained in $U$. Its index is finite, so its [normal core](core-group-theory.md) is an open [normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md) contained in $U$. These subgroups recover the [profinite group](profinite-group.md) as the [inverse limit](inverse-limit.md) of its finite quotients.
