# Distinguishable scalar scattering by a shared mediator

↑ **Parent:** [Tree-level Feynman diagram](tree-level-feynman-diagram.md)

For three [real scalar fields](real-scalar-field.md) with [field interaction term](field-interaction-term.md) $\mathcal L_I=-\frac12(g_1\phi^2\Phi+g_2\psi^2\Phi)$ and mediator mass $M$, the connected [tree-level Feynman diagram](tree-level-feynman-diagram.md) for $\phi\psi\to\phi\psi$ has one exchanged $\Phi$ and [Mandelstam invariant](mandelstam-variables.md) $t=(p-p')^2$. Each vertex has a factor $-ig_j$: its two identical-field [Wick contractions](wick-contraction.md) cancel the factor $1/2$. Thus $i\mathcal M=(-ig_1)(-ig_2)i/(t-M^2+i0)$. There is no $s$- or $u$-channel at this order because the interaction has no mixed $\phi\psi\Phi$ vertex. Distinguishable external particles also require no exchange symmetrization.
