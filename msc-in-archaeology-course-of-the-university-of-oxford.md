# MSc in archaeology course of the University of Oxford

↑ **Parent:** [Master's course of the University of Oxford](master-s-course-of-the-university-of-oxford.md)

11 months full time. Included specifically for the officially named Environmental Archaeology and Landscape Archaeology streams, rather than as a general humanities expansion. These are streams within one MSc, not separate programmes. Expected start 10 October 2027; open for 2027-28 entry.

Official course page: [https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/graduate/courses/msc-archaeology](https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/graduate/courses/msc-archaeology)
