# Types realized in finite structures are principal

↑ **Parent:** [Principal formula of a type](principal-formula-of-a-type.md)

Fix a [tuple](finite-tuple.md) in a finite [first-order structure](first-order-structure.md). For every [tuple](finite-tuple.md) of a different [complete type](complete-type.md), choose a [logical formula](logical-formula.md) true at the first [tuple](finite-tuple.md) and false at the competitor. The finite [conjunction](conjunction-astronomy.md) selects only [tuples](finite-tuple.md) of the desired [partial type](partial-type.md) inside the [first-order structure](first-order-structure.md). Every implication from that [conjunction](conjunction-astronomy.md) to a member of the [partial type](partial-type.md) is true in the [first-order structure](first-order-structure.md) and hence belongs to its [complete theory](complete-theory.md). Thus the [conjunction](conjunction-astronomy.md) isolates the [partial type](partial-type.md), even in an infinite [first-order language](first-order-language.md).
