# Simultaneous deficiency and ramification for an exponential polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Nevanlinna deficiency](nevanlinna-deficiency.md)

The [rational composition law for the Nevanlinna characteristic](rational-composition-law-for-the-nevanlinna-characteristic.md) gives $T_f(r)=3r/\pi+O(1)$. Its zeros are precisely $2\pi i\mathbb Z$, all of [multiplicity](multiplicity-mathematics.md) two, so $N(r;0)=2r/\pi+O(\log r)$ and $\overline N(r;0)=r/\pi+O(\log r)$. Hence both its [Nevanlinna deficiency](nevanlinna-deficiency.md) and [Nevanlinna ramification index](nevanlinna-ramification-index.md) at zero equal one third.
