# Black-Scholes equation with continuous stock dividends

↑ **Parent:** [Black-Scholes equation](black-scholes-equation.md)

When the underlying [stock](stock.md) pays dividends at rate $\theta S_t$, its gain is $dS_t+\theta S_tdt$. Under the [risk-neutral measure](risk-neutral-measure.md) its ex-dividend drift is $(\rho-\theta)S_t$. A sufficiently smooth terminal-claim value obeys $p_t+(\rho-\theta)xp_x+\sigma^2x^2p_{xx}/2-\rho p=0$. These are dividends on the [stock](stock.md), not a source term paid by the claim itself.
