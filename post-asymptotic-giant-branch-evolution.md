# Post-asymptotic giant branch evolution

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar evolution](stellar-evolution.md)

After substantial envelope loss on the [Asymptotic giant branch](asymptotic-giant-branch.md), a low- or intermediate-mass star's exposed core heats while its radius shrinks, often at approximately constant [luminosity](luminosity.md) during continued shell burning. It may ionize the expelled envelope as a [planetary nebula](planetary-nebula.md). After the remaining nuclear fuel is depleted, its luminosity decreases and it joins a [white dwarf](white-dwarf.md) cooling track. Evolutionary tracks on the [Hertzsprung-Russell diagram](hertzsprung-russell-diagram.md) connect the central-star temperature and luminosity with core mass and elapsed time.
