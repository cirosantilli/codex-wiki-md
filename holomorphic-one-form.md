# Holomorphic one-form

↑ **Parent:** [Holomorphic differential form](holomorphic-differential-form.md)

A section $\omega$ of the [canonical bundle](canonical-bundle.md) of a [Riemann surface](riemann-surfaces.md), locally $\omega=\phi(z)\,dz$ with [holomorphic](complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md) $\phi$. A nonzero form on a [compact](compact-space.md) [genus](genus-of-a-surface.md)-$g$ surface has total [zero order](order-of-a-zero-of-a-differential.md) $2g-2$. Its integral supplies [flat coordinates](natural-coordinate-of-a-holomorphic-differential.md) away from zeros.
