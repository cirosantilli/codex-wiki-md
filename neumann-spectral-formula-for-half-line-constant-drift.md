# Neumann spectral formula for half-line constant drift

↑ **Parent:** [Fokas method](fokas-method.md)

The reflected [global relation](global-relation-for-a-linear-boundary-value-problem.md) expresses the unknown value trace in terms of the prescribed derivative trace. Multiplying the [half-line drift null contour identity](half-line-drift-null-contour-identity.md) by $(ik+\alpha)/(ik)$ removes the reflected time-dependent transform. Its pole at $k=0$ lies below the contour and is not crossed. The formula concerns the derivative $q_x(0,t)$, rather than the outward derivative $-q_x(0,t)$.
