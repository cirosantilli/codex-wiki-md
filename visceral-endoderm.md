# Visceral endoderm

↑ **Parent:** [Hypoblast](hypoblast.md)

An extraembryonic endodermal [epithelium](epithelium.md) associated with early mammalian development. It supplies signals and matrix and must be distinguished from [definitive endoderm](definitive-endoderm.md) formed through [gastrulation](gastrulation.md).
