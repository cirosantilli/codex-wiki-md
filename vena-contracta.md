# Vena contracta

↑ **Parent:** [Orifice](orifice.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vena_contracta)

The [vena contracta](vena-contracta.md) is the narrowest jet section after an opening. Its [area](surface-area.md) may be smaller than the opening area, so the jet [speed](speed.md) and [volumetric flow rate](volumetric-flow-rate.md) require separate comparisons with ideal flow.
