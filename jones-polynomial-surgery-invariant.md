# Jones polynomial surgery invariant

↑ **Parent:** [Kirby calculus](kirby-calculus.md)

For an m-component integral framed surgery [link](link.md), normalize its full colored bracket by the displayed expression, where $\sigma(L)$ is the signature of its linking [matrix](matrix.md). The [Kirby color handle-slide identity](kirby-color-handle-slide-identity.md) handles slides; $p_+p_-=\mathcal D^2$ cancels disjoint plus- or minus-one-framed unknots. [Kirby calculus](kirby-calculus.md) therefore makes this an oriented 3-manifold invariant. This convention has $\tau_r(S^3)=1$.
