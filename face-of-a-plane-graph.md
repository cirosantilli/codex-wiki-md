# Face of a plane graph

↑ **Parent:** [Plane graph](plane-graph.md)

A [face of a plane graph](face-of-a-plane-graph.md) is a [connected component](connected-component.md) of the [Euclidean plane](euclidean-plane.md) after removing the drawn [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) and [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md). Count the [outer face](outer-face.md) too. This is the plane counterpart of a [face of a planar map](face-of-a-planar-map.md) on the [sphere](sphere.md).[Planar Graphs; Bipartite Matching, slide lec 5f.6, including the outer face](planar-graphs-bipartite-matching-by-albert-r-meyer-6-042j-lecture-5f.md)
