# Finite axiomatizability with infinitely many scalar symbols

↑ **Parent:** [First-order theory](first-order-theory.md)

No finite [theory](first-order-theory.md) in the full language containing one [function symbol](function-symbol.md) for each [scalar](scalar-multiplication.md) of an infinite [field](field.md) can axiomatize exactly its nonzero [vector spaces](vector-space-split.md). It mentions only finitely many symbols; changing an unused [scalar](scalar-multiplication.md) function to violate preservation of zero leaves those finite sentences unchanged. Relative to the vector-space [axiom](axiom.md) schema, nonzeroness is instead the single [axiom](axiom.md) $\exists x(x\ne0)$. Absolute [finite axiomatizability](finite-axiomatizability.md) and finite additional axiomatizability must be distinguished.
