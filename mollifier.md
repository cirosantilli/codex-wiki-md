# Mollifier

↑ **Parent:** [Distribution theory](distribution-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mollifier)

A standard mollifier is a nonnegative [test function](test-function.md) $\rho$ with integral one. The rescaling $\rho_\varepsilon(x)=\varepsilon^{-n}\rho(x/\varepsilon)$ forms an approximation to the identity, and $u*\rho_\varepsilon$ is smooth wherever the convolution stays inside the domain.
