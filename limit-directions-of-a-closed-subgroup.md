# Limit directions of a closed subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Closed-subgroup theorem](closed-subgroup-theorem.md)

Let $H$ be a closed [subgroup](subgroup.md) of a [Lie group](lie-group.md) $G$, and let nonzero vectors $X_j$ in the ambient [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) $\mathfrak g$ tend to zero with $\exp X_j\in H$ and $X_j/\|X_j\|\to X$. For any real $t$, choose [integers](integer.md) $m_j$ with $m_j\|X_j\|\to t$. Then $(\exp X_j)^{m_j}=\exp(m_jX_j)\to\exp(tX)$. Closedness gives $\exp(tX)\in H$. [Compactness](compact-space.md) of the unit sphere supplies a limit direction from every sequence approaching the identity through nonidentity subgroup elements.
