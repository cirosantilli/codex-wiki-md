# Mirror coupling under an involution

↑ **Parent:** [Coalescing coupling](coalescing-coupling.md)

Suppose an [involution](involution.md) $R$ of a [Markov chain](markov-chain.md) satisfies $P(Rx,Ry)=P(x,y)$. Start one chain at $x$, set the other equal to its image under $R$ until the first [hitting time](first-passage-time.md) of the fixed-point set of $R$, and subsequently use identical transitions. This is a [coalescing coupling](coalescing-coupling.md), because the symmetry preserves each marginal [transition matrix](stochastic-matrix.md) and both chains coincide at that [hitting time](first-passage-time.md).
