# Composition of absolutely summable time-series filters

↑ **Parent:** [Linear filter of a stationary time series](linear-filter-of-a-stationary-time-series.md)

Two absolutely summable [linear filters of a stationary time series](linear-filter-of-a-stationary-time-series.md) compose by [convolution](convolution.md): $c_r=\sum_jb_ja_{r-j}$ and $\sum_r|c_r|\leq(\sum_r|a_r|)(\sum_j|b_j|)$. Absolute convergence in the space of square-integrable [random variables](random-variable-split.md) justifies exchanging the sums. The frequency responses multiply, giving the [spectral density transformation under a linear filter](spectral-density-transformation-under-a-linear-filter.md) twice.
