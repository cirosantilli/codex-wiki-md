# Heat-balance correction to a premelted-film growth model

↑ **Parent:** [Hydraulic control of frost heave](hydraulic-control-of-frost-heave.md)

Combine the full quasi-steady [Stefan condition](stefan-condition.md) above with $\delta T=T_mp_T/(\rho L)$, $a=(A/(6\pi p_T))^{1/3}$ and the [Darcy flow law](darcy-law.md) to obtain an implicit pressure-growth system. Even when latent heat is negligible, a conductivity ratio $K_s/K_l=r$ changes the leading molecular driving pressure by $r^{3/4}$. Thus a reduced equal-conductivity formula requires an explicit material and rate approximation.
