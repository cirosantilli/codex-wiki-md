# Horizontally integrated plume concentration

↑ **Parent:** [Passive scalar](passive-scalar.md)

The horizontally integrated plume concentration is the [integral](integral.md) of a local [concentration](concentration.md) $C$ across the plume. It measures scalar amount per unit vertical length and unit span, and its vertical [integral](integral.md) is total scalar amount per unit span. Unlike a local [concentration](concentration.md), its dimensions include one horizontal length. It must be distinguished from [horizontally averaged plume concentration](horizontally-averaged-plume-concentration.md) when a plume's width varies with height.
