# Tensor derivation

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor field](tensor-field.md)

A [tensor derivation](tensor-derivation.md) is a real-linear operation preserving every tensor type, obeying the tensor-product [Leibniz rule](leibniz-rule.md), and commuting with every [tensor contraction](tensor-contraction.md). A derivation $D$ of [smooth functions](smooth-function.md) and a real-linear operator on [vector fields](vector-field.md) satisfying $D(fY)=fDY+(Df)Y$ extend uniquely to a [tensor derivation](tensor-derivation.md). On a [differential one-form](one-form.md) it must satisfy

$$
(D\omega)(Y)=D(\omega(Y))-\omega(DY).
$$

This expression is linear over [smooth functions](smooth-function.md) in $Y$. In a local frame $De_a=A^b{}_ae_b$, the dual rule is $D\epsilon^a=-A^a{}_b\epsilon^b$. Apply the product rule to every coefficient and frame factor to define the extension; these dual signs cancel under contraction. Frame changes agree by differentiating the inverse [matrix](matrix.md). Cutoffs prove locality and hence uniqueness from local expansions.
