# Oxygen vacancy

↑ **Parent:** [Vacancy defect](vacancy-defect.md)

An unoccupied oxygen site in a reference [crystal structure](crystal-structure.md). Removing an oxide [ion](ion.md) gives an effective charge $+2e$ relative to the perfect lattice; the charge of the actual missing ion is $-2e$. A physical sample must satisfy overall [charge neutrality](charge-neutrality.md) through other defects, electronic carriers or changed cation valences.
