# Massless spectrum at the bosonic self-dual circle

↑ **Parent:** [Momentum and winding modes](momentum-and-winding-modes.md)

The [self-dual circle](self-dual-circle-compactification.md) mass equation and [closed-string level matching](closed-string-level-matching.md) exhaust the possibilities at level sum zero, one or two. Besides the usual neutral $(N,\widetilde N)=(1,1)$ states, charges $(n,w)=(\pm1,\pm1)$ supply singly excited chiral states, with the sector fixed by level matching. Ground-state charges $(\pm2,0)$ and $(0,\pm2)$ also become massless. Their compact energy cancels the uncompactified [tachyon](tachyon.md) mass. With $d$ physical transverse components, these families have $d^2+4d+4$ independent polarizations.
