# Stereoisomer

↑ **Parent:** [Stereochemistry](stereochemistry-split.md)

[Stereoisomers](stereoisomer.md) have the same [chemical bond](chemical-bond-split.md) connectivity but different spatial arrangements. A mirror pair of distinct [stereoisomers](stereoisomer.md) comprises [enantiomers](enantiomer.md); [diastereomers](diastereomer.md) have no mirror-pair relation.
