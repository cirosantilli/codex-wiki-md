# Elastic fluid

↑ **Parent:** [Gas](gas.md)

A historical physical name for a [gas](gas.md) or vapour, emphasizing its mobility and appreciable [compressibility](compressibility.md). It does not imply an exactly [ideal gas](ideal-gas-split.md) or prevent a vapour from undergoing [condensation](condensation.md).
