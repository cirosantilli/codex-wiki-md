# Angular distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum scattering](quantum-scattering-split.md)

The decay probability or scattering [cross section](cross-section-physics.md) per solid angle, relative to stated polar and azimuthal axes. Spin correlations and angular-momentum selection can make it anisotropic; changing the polar axis reverses terms odd in its direction cosine.
