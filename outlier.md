# Outlier

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical modelling](statistical-modelling-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outlier)

An outlier is an observation unusually far from the behavior expected under a statistical model. A large [regression residual](regression-residual.md) can identify a candidate, but data validation, [regression leverage](regression-leverage.md), and the possibility of model misspecification should be examined before excluding it. An outlier need not be an [influential observation](influential-observation.md).
