# Coprime discriminants imply linear disjointness

↑ **Parent:** [Field discriminant](field-discriminant.md)

Two finite [Galois extensions](finite-galois-extension.md) of the rational [number field](number-field.md) with coprime [field discriminants](field-discriminant.md) have trivial intersection. Any ramified prime in the intersection would ramify in both fields. The intersection is therefore everywhere unramified at finite primes and has [field discriminant](field-discriminant.md) of absolute value one. The [Minkowski lower bound for a number-field discriminant](minkowski-lower-bound-for-a-number-field-discriminant.md) excludes this in degree greater than one. The [Galois correspondence](galois-correspondence.md) then gives the product degree for their [compositum](field-compositum.md). The normality hypothesis is used in this last intersection-to-degree argument.
