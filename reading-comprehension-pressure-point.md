# Reading comprehension pressure point

↑ **Parent:** [Reading comprehension](reading-comprehension.md)

A component or task demand that limits successful understanding for a particular reader and text. Print access, [language](language.md) knowledge, inference and monitoring are candidate bottlenecks, not mutually exclusive [diagnoses](clinical-diagnosis.md).
