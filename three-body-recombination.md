# Three-body recombination

↑ **Parent:** [Ionization](ionization.md)

Three-body recombination captures an [Electron](electron.md) while a second free electron carries away energy, the inverse of direct electron-impact ionization. Its rate scales as the parent-ion density times the square of electron density. It is usually negligible compared with two-body [radiative recombination](radiative-recombination.md) and [dielectronic recombination](dielectronic-recombination.md) in the low-density solar corona, but matters in sufficiently dense plasmas.
