# Magnifying glass

↑ **Parent:** [Optical microscope](optical-microscope.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnifying_glass)

A [magnifying glass](magnifying-glass.md) uses a converging [optical lens](lens-optics.md) to present an upright enlarged [virtual image](virtual-image.md). For [focal length](focal-length.md) $f$ and eye [near point](near-point.md) distance $D$, the near-eye [angular magnification](angular-magnification.md) is $D/f$ with a relaxed eye, or $1+D/f$ with the [virtual image](virtual-image.md) at the [near point](near-point.md). These formulas assume negligible eyepiece-to-eye distance. With eye-to-lens distance $e$, object distance $u$, and the [virtual image](virtual-image.md) at the eye's [near point](near-point.md), the [angular magnification](angular-magnification.md) is $(D-e)/u$.
