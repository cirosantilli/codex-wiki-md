# Gamma-matrix trace

↑ **Parent:** [Spin sum](spin-sum.md)

A gamma-matrix trace is a [matrix trace](matrix-trace.md) of products of [gamma matrices](gamma-matrices.md). In four spacetime dimensions, $\operatorname{tr}(\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu)=4\eta^{\mu\nu}$ and $\operatorname{tr}(\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu\gamma^\rho\gamma^\sigma)=4(\eta^{\mu\nu}\eta^{\rho\sigma}-\eta^{\mu\rho}\eta^{\nu\sigma}+\eta^{\mu\sigma}\eta^{\nu\rho})$.
