# Acoustic phonon

↑ **Parent:** [Phonon](phonon.md)

An acoustic phonon is a quantum of vibration on an [acoustic phonon branch](acoustic-phonon-branch.md). At long wavelengths the atoms in each primitive cell move together and translational invariance makes the frequency approach zero. A three-dimensional crystal has three acoustic branches, corresponding to longitudinal and transverse polarizations. A linear small-wavevector dispersion has slope equal to the appropriate [sound speed](speed-of-sound.md).
