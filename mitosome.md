# Mitosome

↑ **Parent:** [Organelle](organelle.md)

A highly reduced mitochondrion-related [organelle](organelle.md) retaining selected metabolic functions rather than oxidative [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) production. Functions differ among lineages, including [iron-sulfur cluster](iron-sulfur-cluster.md) maturation in [Giardia](giardia.md) and [sulfate activation](sulfate-activation.md) in [Entamoeba histolytica](entamoeba-histolytica.md).[Abstract](mitochondrial-remnant-organelles-of-giardia-function-in-iron-sulphur-protein-maturation-by-jorge-tovar-and-others.md)
