# Moduli stabilization

↑ **Parent:** [Modulus of a string compactification](modulus-of-a-string-compactification.md)

Generating a potential with an acceptable stable minimum for previously undetermined compactification scalar fields. It can involve fluxes, branes and nonperturbative effects, with [supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md) and consistency restrictions. A plausible phenomenological model must determine or suitably control these fields rather than leave arbitrary massless scalars and varying couplings.
