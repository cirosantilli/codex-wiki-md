# Neomorphic mutation

↑ **Parent:** [Gain-of-function mutation](gain-of-function-mutation.md)

A [mutation](mutation.md) giving a qualitatively altered activity, such as a new substrate reaction. Mutant [IDH1](idh1.md) production of [2-hydroxyglutarate](2-hydroxyglutarate.md) provides an example.
