# Decay vertex

↑ **Parent:** [Particle detection](particle-detection-split.md)

The reconstructed location at which a [particle](particle-physics.md) decays, inferred from the trajectories of its decay products. Comparing two [decay vertices](decay-vertex.md) can determine a decay-time difference when the motion of the parent system is known.
