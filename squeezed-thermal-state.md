# Squeezed thermal state

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian quantum state](gaussian-quantum-state.md)

Applying a [single-mode squeeze operator](single-mode-squeeze-operator.md) to a thermal state gives the displayed principal optical-quadrature [variances](variance-split.md). Their uncertainty product exceeds the pure-state minimum when $n_{\rm th}>0$. Sufficient squeezing is needed to put the quiet [optical quadrature](optical-quadrature.md) below the vacuum reference.
