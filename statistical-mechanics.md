# Statistical mechanics

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical physics](statistical-physics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_mechanics)

Statistical mechanics connects microscopic states of a physical system to macroscopic thermal properties through probability distributions over those states. Its [microcanonical ensemble](microcanonical-ensemble.md), [canonical ensemble](canonical-ensemble.md) and [grand canonical ensemble](grand-canonical-ensemble.md) encode different constraints on exchanges with the surroundings.
