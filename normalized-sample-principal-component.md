# Normalized sample principal component

↑ **Parent:** [Sample principal component](sample-principal-component.md)

When $\Lambda_{ii}>0$, the normalized sample principal component $w_i=u_i/\sqrt{\Lambda_{ii}}$ has [Euclidean norm](euclidean-norm.md) one. The vectors $w_i$ form an [orthonormal set](orthonormal-set.md) spanning the [column space](column-space.md) of a full-column-rank centered design matrix.
