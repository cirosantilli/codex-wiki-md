# Rational square diffeomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Change of variables formula](change-of-variables-formula.md)

The displayed map is a [diffeomorphism](diffeomorphism.md) of the open unit square onto itself. For fixed $u$, the second coordinate decreases strictly from one to zero as $v$ increases from zero to one. Its inverse is $u=1-x$, $v=(1-y)/[1-(1-x)y]$, and its positive [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md) is $[1-(1-x)y]^2/x$. Composing it with $(t,w)\mapsto((1-t)/(1-wt),1-w)$ gives determinant $[1-(1-x)y][1-(1-x^2)y]^2/[x(1-y)]$. The reciprocal determinant therefore integrates to one over the target square by the [change of variables formula](change-of-variables-formula.md), despite boundary singularities.
