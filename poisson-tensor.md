# Poisson tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson manifold](poisson-manifold.md)

A [Poisson tensor](poisson-tensor.md) is a smooth bivector field defining $\{f,g\}=\pi(df,dg)$ whose bracket obeys the Jacobi identity. In coordinates, $\pi^{i\ell}\partial_\ell\pi^{jk}+\pi^{j\ell}\partial_\ell\pi^{ki}+\pi^{k\ell}\partial_\ell\pi^{ij}=0$. Its Hamiltonian distribution integrates into [symplectic leaves](symplectic-leaf.md), even though its rank can vary across the manifold. The tensor specifies a bracket without requiring a globally nondegenerate symplectic form.
