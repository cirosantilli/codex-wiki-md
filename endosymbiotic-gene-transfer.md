# Endosymbiotic gene transfer

↑ **Parent:** [Endosymbiotic theory](endosymbiotic-theory.md)

[Endosymbiotic gene transfer](endosymbiotic-gene-transfer.md) moves genetic material from an [endosymbiont](endosymbiont.md) or its descendant [organelle](organelle.md) into the host [genome](genome.md), notably the nuclear [genome](genome.md). A transferred [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) segment can remain silent. For a transferred [gene](gene.md) to replace an [organelle](organelle.md)-encoded function, nuclear [gene expression](gene-expression.md) must work and its [protein](protein-split.md) must reach the right compartment through [organelle protein import](organelle-protein-import.md). Transfer of [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) and transport of [proteins](protein-split.md) are distinct processes in opposite directions. [Introduction, p. 2869, and Discussion, p. 2875: transferred sequences and the additional targeting problem](experimental-reconstruction-of-functional-gene-transfer-from-the-tobacco-plastid-genome-to-the-nucleus-by-sandra-stegemann-and-ralph-bock.md)

[Gene](gene.md) loss and [endosymbiotic gene transfer](endosymbiotic-gene-transfer.md) are different: absence from an [organelle](organelle.md) [genome](genome.md) does not by itself show that a functional nuclear copy exists. Conversely, an imported [protein](protein-split.md) may derive from a host [gene](gene.md) rather than a transferred [gene](gene.md). The surviving [organelle](organelle.md) [genome](genome.md) is therefore a partial record, not a complete inventory of either ancestral [bacteria](bacteria.md) or the modern [organelle](organelle.md)'s machinery. [“How to tame your symbiont”: losses, transfers, host recruitment and repatriation of proteins](origin-and-evolution-of-plastids-and-photosynthesis-in-eukaryotes-by-geoffrey-i-mcfadden.md)
