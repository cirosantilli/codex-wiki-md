# Second Chern class of a tensor product of rank-two bundles

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor product of vector bundles](tensor-product-of-vector-bundles.md)

The [splitting principle for complex vector bundles](splitting-principle-for-complex-vector-bundles.md) makes pullback injective in integral [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) and splits both bundles into lines with [Chern roots](chern-root.md) $x_1,x_2$ and $y_1,y_2$. The tensor product has roots $x_i+y_j$. Expanding their six pairwise products gives the displayed formula. This integral computation does not divide by two, so it remains valid when the [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) has two-torsion. In particular, taking $F$ trivial gives $c_2(E\oplus E)=c_1(E)^2+2c_2(E)$.
