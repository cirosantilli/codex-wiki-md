# Single-channel recording

↑ **Parent:** [Patch clamp](patch-clamp.md)

[Single-channel recording](single-channel-recording.md) resolves individual [ion channel](ion-channel.md) opening events as changes in [electric current](electric-current.md). The distinction is resolution, not an absolute rule about configuration: rare, sufficiently large events can also be resolved during [whole-cell recording](whole-cell-recording.md). Simultaneously open identical [ion channels](ion-channel.md) can give successive current levels; indistinguishable amplitudes alone do not prove a unique channel identity.
