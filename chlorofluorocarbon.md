# Chlorofluorocarbon

↑ **Parent:** [Atmospheric chemistry](atmospheric-chemistry.md)

A compound composed of [carbon](carbon.md), [chlorine](chlorine.md) and [fluorine](fluorine.md), with no [hydrogen](hydrogen.md). Many such fully halogenated molecules resist lower-atmospheric attack but release reactive [chlorine](chlorine.md) after stratospheric [photodissociation](photodissociation.md), contributing to catalytic [ozone](ozone.md) destruction.
