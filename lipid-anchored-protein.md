# Lipid-anchored protein

↑ **Parent:** [Membrane protein](membrane-protein.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lipid-anchored_protein)

A [lipid-anchored protein](lipid-anchored-protein.md) attaches through a covalently linked [lipid](lipid.md). The anchor inserts into the [lipid bilayer](lipid-bilayer.md); the [polypeptide chain](polypeptide.md) need not cross it. This is a description of attachment rather than a synonym for [transmembrane protein](transmembrane-protein.md).[The Cell: A Molecular Approach, 2nd edition by Geoffrey M. Cooper; Cell Membranes, Membrane Proteins: covalently attached lipid anchors on either membrane face](the-cell-a-molecular-approach-2nd-edition-by-geoffrey-m-cooper/cell-membranes.md)
