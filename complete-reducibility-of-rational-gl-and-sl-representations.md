# Complete reducibility of rational GL and SL representations

↑ **Parent:** [Rational representation](rational-representation.md)

For finite-dimensional [rational representations](rational-representation.md) of complex $GL_m$ or $SL_m$, average a Hermitian [inner product](inner-product.md) over the [compact group](compact-group.md) $U(m)$ or $SU(m)$ using normalized [Haar measure](haar-measure.md). [Orthogonal complements](orthogonal-complement.md) become invariant under the compact group and its complexified [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md). The latter generates the complex group, giving invariant complements and hence complete reducibility. This conclusion does not hold for arbitrary [affine algebraic groups](linear-algebraic-group.md), such as the additive group.
