# Sedimentation

↑ **Parent:** [Suspension (chemistry)](suspension-chemistry-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sedimentation)

Sedimentation is separation of suspended particles through a body-force-driven relative motion. In the [Perrin sedimentation experiment](perrin-sedimentation-experiment.md), [diffusion](diffusion.md) balances gravitational settling to produce a stationary height profile.
