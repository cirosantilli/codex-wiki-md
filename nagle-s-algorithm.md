<h1 id="nagle-s-algorithm">Nagle's algorithm</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Silly window syndrome](silly-window-syndrome.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagle's_algorithm)

[Nagle's algorithm](nagle-s-algorithm.md) coalesces small writes while data are unacknowledged, permitting another send when a full [TCP segment](tcp-segment.md) can be formed or the outstanding data have been acknowledged. It reduces tiny transmissions but can add delay.
