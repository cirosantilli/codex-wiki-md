# Upwelling

↑ **Parent:** [Ocean circulation](ocean-circulation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upwelling)

Upward water motion bringing subsurface water toward the surface. For example, wind-driven surface divergence near the equator can be supplied by [upwelling](upwelling.md) of colder water from near the [thermocline](thermocline.md). A deeper [thermocline](thermocline.md) can reduce the cooling produced by a given rate of [upwelling](upwelling.md).
