# Group delay

↑ **Parent:** [Group refractive index](group-refractive-index.md)

The [group delay](group-delay.md) is the [frequency](frequency.md) derivative of an unwrapped transfer [wave phase](phase-waves.md), using the $e^{-i\omega t}$ convention. It predicts the leading delay of a narrow [wave envelope](envelope-waves.md) when the transfer [wave amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) and the delay vary little across its band; it is not generally a signal-front arrival time.
