# Hereditarily just-infinite profinite group

↑ **Parent:** [Just-infinite profinite group](just-infinite-profinite-group.md)

An infinite [profinite group](profinite-group.md) is [hereditarily just infinite](hereditarily-just-infinite-profinite-group.md) if every [open subgroup](open-subgroup.md) is a [just-infinite profinite group](just-infinite-profinite-group.md). To prove this, it suffices to show that every nonidentity element of every [open subgroup](open-subgroup.md) has open closed [normal closure](normal-closure.md): any nontrivial closed [normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md) then contains that [normal closure](normal-closure.md). The [Nottingham group](nottingham-group.md) is an example. In odd characteristic its depth-commutator formula constructs nonzero elements of each sufficiently high layer inside any such [normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md), and successive coefficient correction fills the entire tail.
