# Head-on quadrupole radiation from equal masses

↑ **Parent:** [Quadrupole formula](quadrupole-formula.md)

For two equal [point masses](point-mass.md) at $\pm z$ falling from rest at $\pm z_0$ under [Newtonian gravity](gravitational-acceleration.md), in [geometrized units](geometrized-units.md) one has $\ddot z=-m/(4z^2)$ and $\dot z=-\sqrt{m(1/z-1/z_0)/2}$. The only nonzero component of the [second mass moment tensor](second-mass-moment-tensor.md) is $I_{zz}=2mz^2$, and its third derivative is $-m^2\dot z/z^2$. The trace-free [mass quadrupole moment](mass-quadrupole-moment.md) and [quadrupole formula](quadrupole-formula.md) give the displayed power. From infinity, integrating down to $z=z_c$ gives $E=2\sqrt2\,m^{9/2}/(105z_c^{7/2})$. Relative to the total initial mass $2m$ the fraction is $\sqrt2(m/z_c)^{7/2}/105$. The weak-field slow-motion approximation must be distinguished from its extrapolation to a compact endpoint.
