# Recovery time of a stable equilibrium

↑ **Parent:** [Linear stability](linear-stability.md)

For a scalar [ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md) $N'=f(N)$ and an [equilibrium point](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) $N^*$ with $f'(N^*)<0$, the [linearization](linearization.md) gives $n'=f'(N^*)n$. Thus small perturbations decay like $e^{-t/\tau}$ with $\tau=-1/f'(N^*)$. This is a local exponential recovery time. In several dimensions, the slowest decaying stable [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) typically controls asymptotic recovery, subject to additional polynomial factors for nontrivial [Jordan blocks](jordan-block.md).
