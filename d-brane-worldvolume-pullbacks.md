# D-brane worldvolume pullbacks

↑ **Parent:** [Dirac-Born-Infeld action](dirac-born-infeld-action.md)

The brane embedding pulls the spacetime metric and two-form back to the [worldvolume](worldvolume.md). The [dilaton](dilaton.md) is evaluated at that embedding. If the quoted tension includes the constant-background factor $e^{-\Phi_0}$, use $\widetilde\Phi=\Phi(X)-\Phi_0$; otherwise use $\widetilde\Phi=\Phi(X)$ and a tension coefficient independent of that factor. These are normalization conventions for the same [Dirac-Born-Infeld action](dirac-born-infeld-action.md).
