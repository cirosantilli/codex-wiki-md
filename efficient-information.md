# Efficient information

↑ **Parent:** [Efficient score](efficient-score.md)

The efficient information for a scalar target [statistical parameter](statistical-parameter.md) is the squared [L2 norm](l2-norm.md) of its [efficient score](efficient-score.md). It cannot exceed the parametric [Fisher information](fisher-information-matrix.md), by the [Pythagorean theorem in an inner-product space](pythagorean-theorem-in-an-inner-product-space.md). It can vanish when first-order target variation can be reproduced by nuisance variation.
