# Intrinsic semiconductor

↑ **Parent:** [Semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md)

An [intrinsic semiconductor](intrinsic-semiconductor.md) has equilibrium mobile [electron](electron.md) and [electron hole](electron-hole.md) densities set predominantly by thermal excitation across its [band gap](band-gap.md), rather than by [semiconductor doping](semiconductor-doping.md). In the nondegenerate regime, charge neutrality gives $n=p=n_i$.
