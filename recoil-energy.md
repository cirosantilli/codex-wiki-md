# Recoil energy

↑ **Parent:** [Optical lattice](optical-lattice.md)

Recoil energy is the [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) gained by an initially stationary atom absorbing a photon of momentum $p_R$. In a standing-wave [optical lattice](optical-lattice.md) of spacing $d$, the photon wavenumber is $\pi/d$, giving $E_R=\hbar^2\pi^2/(2md^2)$.
