# Rat-liver acid-phosphatase latency experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Lysosomal enzyme latency](lysosomal-enzyme-latency.md)

In the 1951 study by [Jacques Berthet](jacques-berthet.md) and [Christian de Duve](christian-de-duve.md), [liver](liver.md) from fasted rats was immediately chilled in an ice-cold isotonic [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md). The tissue was generally pressed through wire mesh; the weighed pulp was homogenized in cold $0.25\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$ [sucrose](sucrose.md) using a glass [Potter-Elvehjem homogenizer](potter-elvehjem-homogenizer.md). Their [acid phosphatase](acid-phosphatase.md) test concerned activity toward added [glycerophosphate](glycerophosphate.md) at [pH](ph.md) $5$: the readout was [substrate](substrate-biochemistry.md) [hydrolysis](hydrolysis.md), rather than an image of a particle.[Berthet and de Duve (1951), Tissue fractionation studies. 1, printed p. 174, introduction and Methods: Fractionation of tissue constituents; inspected first-page text](tissue-fractionation-studies-1-the-existence-of-a-mitochondria-linked-enzymically-inactive-form-of-acid-phosphatase-in-rat-liver-tissue-by-jacques-berthet-and-christian-de-duve.md)

[Christian de Duve](christian-de-duve.md)'s retrospective describes successive [centrifugation](centrifugation.md) steps, separating [centrifugation pellets](centrifugation-pellet.md) and [supernatants](supernatant.md), and comparing [enzyme](enzyme-split.md) distributions across the recovered fractions. Membrane disruption exposed previously inaccessible [acid phosphatase](acid-phosphatase.md) activity, providing the crucial comparison with the membrane-preserving preparation. Joint fractionation patterns and release behavior of several [acid hydrolases](acid-hydrolase.md) supported a shared population of digestive particles. By 1955, this evidence supported the [lysosome](lysosome.md) concept.[Exploring Cells with a Centrifuge, printed pp. 152–153: preparation and differential fractionation; pp. 156–159, Figures 4–7: enzyme distributions, disruption and latency](exploring-cells-with-a-centrifuge-by-christian-de-duve.md)

The inference was biochemical: a barrier restricted access to active [enzymes](enzyme-split.md). Co-sedimentation could confuse distinct particles; a crude mitochondrial fraction did not establish residence inside [mitochondria](mitochondria.md). [Lysosomal enzyme latency](lysosomal-enzyme-latency.md) neither images an [organelle](organelle.md) nor determines its shape. [Electron microscopy](electron-microscopy.md) of enriched fractions supplied separate morphological evidence.[Printed pp. 153–157: mixed populations and distribution patterns; pp. 161–164: incomplete separation and subsequent electron microscopy of lysosome-rich fractions](exploring-cells-with-a-centrifuge-by-christian-de-duve.md)
