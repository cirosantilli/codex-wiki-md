# In-plane inertia isotropy of a square plate

↑ **Parent:** [Moment of inertia of a uniform square plate](moment-of-inertia-of-a-uniform-square-plate.md)

A uniform thin square of side $l$ and [mass](mass.md) $M$ has $I_z=Ml^2/6$ about the perpendicular central axis. Any central in-plane axis and its perpendicular in-plane partner have equal moments by $90^\circ$ rotational symmetry. The [perpendicular axis theorem](perpendicular-axis-theorem.md) says their sum is $I_z$, proving $I_\phi=Ml^2/12$ for every angle $\phi$. A non-square rectangle generally lacks this angle independence.
