# Electric force

↑ **Parent:** [Electric field](electric-field.md)

The [electric force](electric-force.md) on an [electric charge](electric-charge.md) $q$ is $\mathbf F_E=q\mathbf E$, using the [electric field](electric-field.md) of the other sources and excluding the charge's own self-field. Positive [electric charge](electric-charge.md) gives [force](force.md) along the [electric field](electric-field.md); negative [electric charge](electric-charge.md) reverses it. A field $\mathbf E=400\,\mathrm{N/C}\,\widehat{\mathbf x}$ therefore gives $\mathbf F_E=-8\times10^{-7}\,\mathrm N\,\widehat{\mathbf x}$ on $q=-2\,\mathrm{nC}$. This is the electric part of the [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md); the magnetic contribution is $q\mathbf v\times\mathbf B$. [OpenStax, University Physics II, §5.4: electric field and force](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/5-4-electric-field).
