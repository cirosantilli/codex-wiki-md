# Cosmic microwave background dipole

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmic microwave background anisotropy](cosmic-microwave-background-anisotropy-split.md)

The dipole is the $\ell=1$ angular component of [Cosmic microwave background anisotropy](cosmic-microwave-background-anisotropy-split.md). Its leading observed contribution is a [Doppler dipole](doppler-dipole.md) from observer motion, producing one hotter and one cooler hemisphere. Interpreting the whole measured dipole as motion is a physical model; an intrinsic dipole is a separate contribution.
