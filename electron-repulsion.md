# Electron repulsion

↑ **Parent:** [Electric charge](electric-charge-split.md)

[Electron repulsion](electron-repulsion.md) is the repulsive electrostatic interaction between [electrons](electron.md). In a metal complex it affects pairing costs and many-electron transition energies, so a measured absorption maximum is not always identical to a one-electron orbital splitting.
