# Massless quark Compton scattering

↑ **Parent:** [Compton scattering](compton-scattering.md)

The two electromagnetic [tree-level Feynman diagrams](tree-level-feynman-diagram.md) for $q\gamma\to q\gamma$ exchange a [quark](quark.md) in the $s$ and $u$ channels. Their sum obeys both photon [Ward identities](ward-identity.md). Averaging over the initial [quark](quark.md) [spin](spin.md), [color charge](color-charge.md) and two physical incoming [photon polarization vectors](photon-polarization-vector.md) gives $-2e^4Q_q^4(s/u+u/s)$ after final-state sums. The [color average](color-average.md) is one because the interaction preserves the single open [quark](quark.md) colour index. The interference trace is proportional to $(p_1+p_2)\cdot(p_1-p_4)=(s+t+u)/2=0$ for massless external states.
