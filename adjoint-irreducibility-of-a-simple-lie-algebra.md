# Adjoint irreducibility of a simple Lie algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Adjoint representation of a Lie algebra](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md)

Every invariant subspace of the [Adjoint representation of a Lie algebra](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md) is an ideal. Hence the adjoint representation of a [simple Lie algebra](simple-lie-algebra.md) is irreducible.
