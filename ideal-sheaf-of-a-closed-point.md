# Ideal sheaf of a closed point

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal sheaf of a closed subscheme](ideal-sheaf-of-a-closed-subscheme.md)

For a [closed point](closed-point.md) $P$ with residue field $k(P)$, evaluation gives a morphism from the [structure sheaf](structure-sheaf-of-a-scheme.md) onto the [skyscraper sheaf](skyscraper-sheaf.md) $k_P$. Its [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) is the ideal sheaf of $P$. On a [Noetherian scheme](noetherian-scheme.md) it is a [coherent ideal sheaf](coherent-ideal-sheaf.md).
