# Pathlevel solution of a rough differential equation

↑ **Parent:** [Rough differential equation](rough-differential-equation.md)

A pathlevel solution is the ordinary state path satisfying a local rough Taylor expansion with a remainder of order $\theta>1$ in a [control function for rough paths](control-function-for-rough-paths.md). It specifies the first level of the solution, rather than requiring the full lifted solution as part of the definition. With vector fields of regularity $\operatorname{Lip}^\gamma$, $\gamma>p$, the [Davie lemma](davie-discrete-sewing-lemma.md) bounds discrete Euler errors uniformly; compactness and passage to the limit give such a path.
