# Scintillator

↑ **Parent:** [Scintillation (physics)](scintillation-physics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scintillator)

A material that emits optical [photons](photon.md) after an ionizing particle deposits [energy](energy-split.md). Coupling it to [photomultipliers](photomultiplier.md) or other light sensors converts deposited energy into an electrical pulse.
