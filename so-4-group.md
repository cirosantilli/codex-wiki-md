<h1 id="so-4-group">SO(4) group</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Special orthogonal group](special-orthogonal-group.md)

The [SO(4) group](so-4-group.md) consists of real orthogonal four-by-four matrices of determinant one. It preserves orientation and the Euclidean metric. Its [Spin(4) double cover](spin-4-double-cover.md) identifies it with the quotient of two copies of [SU(2)](su-2-group.md) by their simultaneous central sign.
