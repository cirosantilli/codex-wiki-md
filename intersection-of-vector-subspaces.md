# Intersection of vector subspaces

↑ **Parent:** [Vector subspace](vector-subspace.md)

The intersection of any family of [vector subspaces](vector-subspace.md) of a common [vector space](vector-space-split.md) is the set of vectors belonging to every member of the family. It is again a vector subspace.
