# Geological formation

↑ **Parent:** [Stratigraphy](stratigraphy.md)

A mappable body of [rock](rock-geology.md) distinguished by its lithological characteristics and stratigraphic relations. Similar proportions of a few mineral categories do not uniquely identify a formation: different rock units can share those proportions, while sampling or alteration can vary within one unit.
