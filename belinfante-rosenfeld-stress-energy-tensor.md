# Belinfante-Rosenfeld stress-energy tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical stress-energy tensor](canonical-stress-energy-tensor.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belinfante–Rosenfeld_stress–energy_tensor)

The Belinfante-Rosenfeld construction adds the divergence of a spin-current superpotential to the [canonical stress-energy tensor](canonical-stress-energy-tensor.md), producing a symmetric conserved tensor with the same integrated four-momentum under suitable boundary conditions.
