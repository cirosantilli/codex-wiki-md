# Differencing

↑ **Parent:** [Time series](time-series-split.md)

The difference $\Delta X_t=X_t-X_{t-1}$ can remove a unit-root stochastic trend. A [seasonal difference operator](seasonal-difference-operator.md) instead subtracts an observation a full seasonal period earlier. Neither operation automatically removes a changing variance.
