# Two-stage linear gene-expression covariance

↑ **Parent:** [Intrinsic gene-expression noise](intrinsic-gene-expression-noise.md)

For linear RNA removal and protein production driven by independent additive white noises, the stationary RNA variance is $q_R/(2\gamma_R)$ and the protein variance is the displayed expression. Steady count birth-and-death intensities $q_R=2\gamma_R\langle R\rangle$, $q_P=2\gamma_P\langle P\rangle$ give the protein [Fano factor](fano-factor.md) $1+k_P/(\gamma_R+\gamma_P)$. This specializes arbitrary noise strengths; it is not an identity valid for every additive-noise model.
