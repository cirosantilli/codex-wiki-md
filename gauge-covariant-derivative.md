# Gauge covariant derivative

↑ **Parent:** [Gauge field](gauge-field.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gauge_covariant_derivative)

A gauge covariant derivative combines an ordinary derivative with a [gauge field](gauge-field.md) so that differentiating a field preserves its gauge-transformation law. In a matrix representation one common convention is $D_\mu=\partial_\mu-igA_\mu$.
