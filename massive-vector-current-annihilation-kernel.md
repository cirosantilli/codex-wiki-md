# Massive vector-current annihilation kernel

↑ **Parent:** [Electron-positron annihilation](electron-positron-annihilation.md)

For an initial [Dirac field](dirac-field.md) of mass $m$ annihilating into a distinct [Dirac field](dirac-field.md) of mass $M$ through a vector current, put $\Sigma=m^2+M^2$. With [Mandelstam variables](mandelstam-variables.md) satisfying $s+t+u=2\Sigma$, the [spin average](spin-average.md) of the two contracted vector currents is $2F$, where $F=(\Sigma-t)^2+(\Sigma-u)^2+2s\Sigma=t^2+u^2+4s\Sigma-2\Sigma^2$. The [gamma-matrix trace](gamma-matrix-trace.md) proof uses $\operatorname{tr}[(\not p_2-m)\gamma^\mu(\not p_1+m)\gamma^\nu]=4[p_2^\mu p_1^\nu+p_2^\nu p_1^\mu-(s/2)g^{\mu\nu}]$ and the analogous final tensor. Couplings, the mediator denominator and [color charge](color-charge.md) factors multiply this common kinematic expression.
