# Right coset

↑ **Parent:** [Coset](coset.md)

For a [subgroup](subgroup.md) $H$ of a [group](group-split.md) $G$ and $g\in G$, its right coset is $Hg=\{hg:h\in H\}$. The map $h\mapsto hg$ is a [bijection](bijection.md) from $H$ onto $Hg$, with inverse $x\mapsto xg^{-1}$; in particular all right cosets have the same [cardinality](cardinality.md) as $H$. Multiplication by subgroup elements is on the left in this expression because $g$ is the factor on the right.
