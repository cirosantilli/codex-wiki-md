# Virtual-reality delusion experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Virtual reality therapy](virtual-reality-therapy.md)

Freeman and colleagues compared graded virtual social [exposure](exposure-therapy.md) with threat testing that included [safety behavior](safety-behavior.md) reduction, assessing [belief](belief.md) conviction and later real-world [distress](distress.md). The brief small-sample experiment probes a combined causal manipulation rather than establishing durable effects of every component.[British Journal of Psychiatry 209, 62–67; 30 participants, graded social environments, safety reduction versus exposure and real-world assessment](virtual-reality-in-the-treatment-of-persecutory-delusions-randomised-controlled-experimental-study-testing-how-to-reduce-delusional-conviction-by-daniel-freeman-and-others.md)
