# Thiol Michael addition

↑ **Parent:** [Michael addition](michael-addition.md)

Conjugate addition of a thiol-derived [sulfur](sulfur.md) [nucleophile](nucleophile.md) to an activated alkene. The [sulfur](sulfur.md) substituent is installed at the beta [carbon](carbon.md) of an enone, not at its [carbonyl](carbonyl-group.md) [carbon](carbon.md).
