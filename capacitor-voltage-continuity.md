# Capacitor voltage continuity

↑ **Parent:** [Capacitor voltage](capacitor-voltage.md)

Integrating the current-voltage relation shows that a finite [electric current](electric-current.md) cannot change [capacitor voltage](capacitor-voltage.md) instantaneously. An imposed impulsive current can make it jump. A prescribed instantaneous short-circuit discharge is an idealization of this exceptional fast process, not a counterexample for finite current.
