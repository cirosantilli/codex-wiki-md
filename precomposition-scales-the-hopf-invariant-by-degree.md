# Precomposition scales the Hopf invariant by degree

↑ **Parent:** [Hopf invariant](hopf-invariant.md)

Let $\phi:S^{2q-1}\to S^q$ and precompose with a sphere self-map of [topological degree](topological-degree.md) $d$. The resulting map of mapping cones is the identity on the bottom $q$-cell and has degree $d$ on the top $2q$-cell. Pulling back the defining [cup product](cup-product.md) relation for the [Hopf invariant](hopf-invariant.md) multiplies its coefficient by $d$. In particular the element $d\eta\in\pi_3(S^2)$ has [Hopf invariant](hopf-invariant.md) $d$. Postcomposition by a degree-$d$ map of the target sphere instead multiplies the invariant by $d^2$.
