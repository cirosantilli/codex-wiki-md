# Pullback of a covector

↑ **Parent:** [Pullback of a covariant tensor](pullback-of-a-covariant-tensor.md)

The [pullback of a covector](pullback-of-a-covector.md) is the dual linear map to the [differential of a smooth map](differential-of-a-smooth-map.md). In coordinates its components are $(\phi^*\eta)_i=(\partial_i y^\alpha)\eta_\alpha$. The same rectangular Jacobian defines vector pushforward, with its other index contracted. No invertibility is needed.
