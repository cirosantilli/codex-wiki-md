# Photonic Bell test with efficient detection and random settings

↑ **Parent:** [Bell test](bell-test.md)

In 2015, [Marissa Giustina](marissa-giustina.md), [Anton Zeilinger](anton-zeilinger.md) and collaborators combined [spontaneous parametric down-conversion](spontaneous-parametric-down-conversion.md) with rapid random analyzer choices and efficient [transition-edge sensors](transition-edge-sensor.md). Local records included settings and detector signals; their [Bell inequality](bell-inequality.md) used detected and undetected trials, avoiding [fair sampling](fair-sampling.md). Locally fixed time slots prevented coincidence-based trial selection. Calibrated optical and electrical delays checked [spacelike separation](spacelike-separation.md) of each choice from the remote readout. The violation addressed detection and locality together. [Measurement independence](measurement-independence.md) still requires assumptions about the hidden state's origin; the experiment cannot exclude every possible common cause of source and settings.[Version 2, pp. 1-4, Figs. 1-2 and Eq. (1)](significant-loophole-free-test-of-bell-s-theorem-with-entangled-photons-by-marissa-giustina-and-others.md)
