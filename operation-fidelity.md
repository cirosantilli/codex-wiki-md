# Operation fidelity

↑ **Parent:** [Entanglement fidelity](entanglement-fidelity.md)

In the unsquared [quantum fidelity](fidelity-of-quantum-states.md) convention, operation fidelity compares a [purification of a density operator](purification-of-a-density-operator.md) with its image under a [quantum channel](quantum-channel.md) acting on the source subsystem:

$$
F_{\rm op}(\Lambda,\rho)=F\bigl(|\Psi_\rho\rangle\langle\Psi_\rho|,(\operatorname{id}\otimes\Lambda)(|\Psi_\rho\rangle\langle\Psi_\rho|)\bigr).
$$

Its square is [entanglement fidelity](entanglement-fidelity.md). Thus this convention is distinct from the squared quantity $F_e$; the two should not be installed as synonyms.
