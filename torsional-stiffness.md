# Torsional stiffness

↑ **Parent:** [Torsion spring](torsion-spring.md)

The derivative of an axial [torque](torque.md) with respect to relative end-rotation [angle](angle.md), evaluated at the operating configuration. In a linear [torsion spring](torsion-spring.md), $N=K_\varphi\varphi$.
