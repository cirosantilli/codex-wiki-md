# Planar lamina

↑ **Parent:** [Mass distribution](mass-distribution.md)

A planar lamina is an ideal [mass distribution](mass-distribution.md) confined to a [plane](plane.md), with [surface mass density](projected-surface-mass-density.md) $\sigma$ and infinitesimal [mass](mass.md) $dm=\sigma\,dA$. Its [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) about an axis is the [integral](integral-split.md) of squared perpendicular distance against this mass element. Three mutually perpendicular axes through one point, with two in the lamina's plane, obey the [perpendicular axis theorem](perpendicular-axis-theorem.md) even when the density is nonuniform.
