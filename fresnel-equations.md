# Fresnel equations

↑ **Parent:** [Dielectric-interface boundary conditions](dielectric-interface-boundary-conditions.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fresnel_equations)

The [Fresnel equations](fresnel-equations.md) give reflection and transmission amplitudes for both transverse-electric and transverse-magnetic [polarization of an electromagnetic wave](polarization-of-an-electromagnetic-wave.md) at a planar interface. They follow by applying [interface conditions for electromagnetic fields](interface-conditions-for-electromagnetic-fields.md) to incident, reflected and refracted [plane electromagnetic waves](plane-electromagnetic-wave.md).
