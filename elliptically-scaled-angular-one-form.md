# Elliptically scaled angular one-form

↑ **Parent:** [Nonexact angular one-form](nonexact-angular-one-form.md)

For real $a,b\ne0$, this [differential one-form](one-form.md) on the [punctured plane](punctured-complex-plane.md) is the [pullback of a differential form](pullback-of-a-differential-form.md) of the [nonexact angular one-form](nonexact-angular-one-form.md) under the invertible [linear transformation](linear-map.md) $(X,Y)=(ax,by)$. It is a [closed differential form](closed-differential-form.md) for every such pair. On a region admitting a continuous [complex argument](argument-complex-analysis.md), it has the local potential $f=\operatorname{Arg}(ax+iby)$. In a chart with $x\ne0$, this can be written $f=\arctan(by/(ax))+C$.

There is no globally single-valued potential on the whole [punctured plane](punctured-complex-plane.md). On the closed path $x=\cos t/a$, $y=\sin t/b$, $0\leq t\leq2\pi$, substitution gives $\omega_{a,b}=dt$, hence its [line integral](line-integral.md) is $2\pi\ne0$. This contradicts the zero integral around every closed path required of an [exact differential form](exact-differential-form.md). Around a positively oriented once-winding path the integral is $2\pi\operatorname{sgn}(ab)$. The local equation $\omega_{a,b}=0$ gives radial rays; away from a vertical ray its solutions as graphs are $y=Cx$ on intervals not crossing the excluded origin.
