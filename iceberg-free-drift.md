# Iceberg free drift

↑ **Parent:** [Iceberg](iceberg.md)

The free-drift velocity of an iceberg follows from air and water drag, the [Coriolis force](coriolis-force.md), pressure gradients, currents and other external forces. Large submerged area and mass make its wind response weak compared with that of a thin floe.
