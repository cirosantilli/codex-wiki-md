# Birefringent optical compensator

↑ **Parent:** [Birefringence](birefringence.md)

A known retarder inserted in a [polarization](polarization-waves.md) microscope to compare [phase](phase-waves.md) delay with an unknown specimen. Parallel slow directions add signed retardations; a slow direction paired with a fast one subtracts them. General relative angles require composing [polarization](polarization-waves.md) transformations rather than scalar addition.
