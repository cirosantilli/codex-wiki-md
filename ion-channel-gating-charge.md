# Ion-channel gating charge

↑ **Parent:** [Ion-channel gating current](ion-channel-gating-current.md)

The net displaced [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) associated with a change in channel conformation, inferred by integrating the [ion-channel gating current](ion-channel-gating-current.md) after subtracting unrelated currents. It is distinct from the [electric current](electric-current.md) itself and from charge carried through the open pore.
