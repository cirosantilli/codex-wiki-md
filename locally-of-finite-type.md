# Locally of finite type

↑ **Parent:** [Morphism of finite type](morphism-of-finite-type.md)

A [morphism of schemes](morphism-of-schemes.md) $f:X\to S$ is locally of finite type if, over every [affine open subscheme](affine-open-subscheme.md) $\operatorname{Spec}A\subseteq S$, its inverse image is covered by [affine schemes](affine-scheme.md) $\operatorname{Spec}B$ with $B$ a finitely generated $A$-[algebra](algebra-split.md). It is of [finite type](morphism-of-finite-type.md) exactly when it is also a [quasi-compact morphism](quasi-compact-morphism.md).
