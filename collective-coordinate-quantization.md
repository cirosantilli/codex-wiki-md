# Collective-coordinate quantization

↑ **Parent:** [Moduli-space approximation for solitons](moduli-space-approximation-for-solitons.md)

Quantizing a [moduli-space approximation](moduli-space-approximation-for-solitons.md) gives wavefunctions with measure $\sqrt{\det g}\,d^dq$ and, under the minimal scalar ordering, the [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) $-\hbar^2\Delta_g/2+V$, where $\Delta_g$ is the [Laplace-Beltrami operator](laplace-beltrami-operator.md). Global identifications, statistics and regularity supply additional restrictions on wavefunctions. Curvature-dependent ordering terms or quantum corrections are extra choices, not determined by the classical kinetic energy alone.
