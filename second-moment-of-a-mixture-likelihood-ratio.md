# Second moment of a mixture likelihood ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Chi-squared divergence](chi-squared-divergence.md)

If $L_j=dP_j/dQ$ and the nonnegative [mixture weights](mixture-weight.md) sum to one, the [likelihood ratio](likelihood-ratio.md) of the [mixture model](mixture-model.md) is $L=\sum_jw_jL_j$. Expanding $\mathbb E_QL^2-1$ proves the displayed [chi-squared divergence](chi-squared-divergence.md) identity. For [independent random variables](independent-random-variables.md) sampled from a fixed component, one-observation cross moments multiply across observations. The component is drawn once for the whole sample; redrawing it per observation gives a different [mixture model](mixture-model.md).
