# Two spin-one-half fermions on a ring

↑ **Parent:** [Indistinguishable particles](indistinguishable-particles.md)

For free [fermions](fermion.md) on a ring, each one-particle orbital has integer [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) label $l$ and two [spin](spin.md) states. Two particles occupy the $l=0$ orbital in a unique [singlet state](singlet-state.md) at zero kinetic [energy](energy-split.md). At the next distinct [energy](energy-split.md) $\hbar^2/(2mR^2)$, orbitals $0,+1$ or $0,-1$ can be combined with one [singlet state](singlet-state.md) or three [triplet state](spin-one-half-triplet-state.md) [spin](spin.md) states, giving total [quantum degeneracy](degenerate-energy-levels.md) eight. The lowest [triplet state](spin-one-half-triplet-state.md) has six states and its antisymmetric spatial factor vanishes when the particles coincide.
