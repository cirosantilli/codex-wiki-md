# Large family avoiding a high intersection

↑ **Parent:** [Extremal set theory](extremal-set-theory-split.md)

If a forbidden intersection size exceeds $n/2$, every subset of size at most $\lfloor n/2\rfloor$ avoids it, including on self-intersection. This [set family](set-family.md) contains at least half the [Boolean lattice](boolean-lattice.md). Consequently a fixed exponential bound $c^n$ with $c<2$ cannot hold uniformly as $n$ grows. Forbidden-intersection estimates below the middle rank therefore cannot simply be extrapolated to high intersections.
