# Effective action

↑ **Parent:** [Perturbative quantum field theory](perturbative-quantum-field-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Effective_action)

The quantum effective action is the [Legendre transform](convex-conjugate.md) of the generating functional of connected correlation functions. Its functional derivatives are the [one-particle-irreducible vertex functions](one-particle-irreducible-feynman-diagram.md).
