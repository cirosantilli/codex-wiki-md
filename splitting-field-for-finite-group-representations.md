# Splitting field for finite group representations

↑ **Parent:** [Group representation](group-representation.md)

A [field](field.md) $k$ is a splitting [field](field.md) for a finite [group](group-split.md) $G$ if every [simple module](irreducible-module.md) over its [group algebra](group-algebra.md) $kG$ is absolutely simple: it remains simple over every extension [field](field.md). Equivalently, the quotient by the [Jacobson radical](jacobson-radical.md) is a product of full [matrix algebras](matrix-algebra.md) over $k$. In [characteristic](characteristic-of-a-field.md) $p$, containing all $m$th [roots of unity](root-of-unity.md), where $|G|=p^a m$ and $p\nmid m$, is sufficient. This is the modular splitting-field theorem; it is substantially stronger than merely having [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) for one chosen group element.
