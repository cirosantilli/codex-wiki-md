# Second-countable space

↑ **Parent:** [Topological space](topological-space.md)

A second-countable [topological space](topological-space.md) has a countable collection of open sets such that every open set is a union of members of that collection. A space covered by countably many open [manifold charts](manifold-chart.md) is second countable because each coordinate domain has a countable basis of rational balls.
