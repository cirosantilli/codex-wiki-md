# Minjinia turgenensis

↑ **Parent:** [Placoderm](placoderm.md)

An Early [Devonian](devonian.md) Mongolian jawed [vertebrate](vertebrate.md) whose braincase contains a trabecular internal tissue interpreted as [endochondral bone](endochondral-bone.md). Implications for ancestral [bone](bone.md) depend on both tissue identity and phylogenetic placement.
