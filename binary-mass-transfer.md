# Binary mass transfer

↑ **Parent:** [Binary star](binary-star.md)

Transfer of [mass](mass.md) between components of a [binary star](binary-star.md), for example through [Roche lobe](roche-lobe.md) overflow or capture of a stellar wind. Orbital evolution depends on which component loses material and on the [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) carried away.
