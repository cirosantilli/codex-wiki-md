# Ring of integers of Q of square root two

↑ **Parent:** [Ring of integers of a quadratic field](ring-of-integers-of-a-quadratic-field.md)

Since $2\not\equiv1\pmod4$, the ring of integers of $\mathbb Q(\sqrt2)$ is $\mathbb Z[\sqrt2]$, with integral basis $1,\sqrt2$ and field discriminant eight.
