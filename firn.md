# Firn

↑ **Parent:** [Snow](snow.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Firn)

Firn is compacted, recrystallized old [snow](snow.md) that has not yet become dense glacier ice. Its [porosity](porosity.md) affects meltwater infiltration and [heat transfer](heat-transfer.md).
