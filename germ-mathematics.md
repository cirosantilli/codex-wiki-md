# Germ (mathematics)

↑ **Parent:** [Function](function-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germ_(mathematics))

A [germ](germ-mathematics.md) at a point is an [equivalence class](equivalence-class.md) of [functions](function-split.md) defined on a [neighbourhood](neighbourhood-mathematics.md) of the point: two representatives are equivalent if they agree on some smaller [neighbourhood](neighbourhood-mathematics.md). A [germ of a holomorphic function](germ-of-a-holomorphic-function.md) requires its representatives to be [holomorphic functions](holomorphic-function.md); smooth and continuous germs use their corresponding classes of [functions](function-split.md). The [germ](germ-mathematics.md) retains local behavior while forgetting the representative's values farther from the point.
