# Clause learning

↑ **Parent:** [SAT solver](sat-solver.md)

Deriving additional [Boolean clauses](clause-of-a-boolean-formula.md) implied by the original [Boolean formula](boolean-formula.md), usually after analyzing a conflict in search. A learned [Boolean clause](clause-of-a-boolean-formula.md) excludes a failed combination of choices on subsequent branches without removing any [satisfying assignment](satisfying-assignment.md).
