# State-price budget constraint

↑ **Parent:** [State-price density](state-price-density.md)

A [state-price density](state-price-density.md) converts terminal wealth and consumption into initial cost. For a nonnegative admissible [self-financing portfolio](self-financing-portfolio.md) with consumption, the deflated wealth plus cumulative deflated consumption is a [supermartingale](supermartingale.md), giving $\mathbb E[\zeta_TX]+\mathbb E\int_0^T\zeta_tc_tdt\leq w$. In a [complete market](complete-market.md), an integrable nonnegative terminal claim with full budget equality is replicable.
