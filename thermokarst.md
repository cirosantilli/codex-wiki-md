# Thermokarst

↑ **Parent:** [Permafrost](permafrost.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thermokarst)

Thermokarst is subsidence and related landscape change caused by thaw of ice-rich [permafrost](permafrost.md). Resulting lakes and saturated sediments can promote [methanogenesis](methanogenesis.md).
