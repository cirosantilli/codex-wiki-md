# Small-slope particle motion on a height graph

↑ **Parent:** [Newtonian mechanics](newtonian-mechanics.md)

A particle constrained to the graph $z=h(x,y)$ has [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) $m[|\dot{\mathbf r}|^2+(\nabla h\cdot\dot{\mathbf r})^2]/2$ and [potential energy](potential-energy.md) $mgh$. For a gently varying graph and velocities for which its geometric kinetic correction is higher order, the leading [Euler-Lagrange equations](euler-lagrange-equation.md) are the displayed planar gradient-force equations. Small slope alone at arbitrarily large speed or curvature does not justify discarding the normal inertial acceleration. For a radial height, the leading planar [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) is conserved, allowing an [effective potential](effective-potential.md) reduction.
