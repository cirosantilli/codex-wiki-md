# Opposite point charges have no linear restoring force at their midpoint

↑ **Parent:** [Coulomb's law](coulomb-s-law.md)

Place $+q$ at $-L$ and $-q$ at $+L$. For a nonzero [point charge](point-charge.md) $q_3$ between them, the signed force at $x=fL$ is $2k_eqq_3(1+f^2)/[L^2(1-f^2)^2]$. It is nonzero at $f=0$ and even in $f$, so its first nonconstant term is quadratic. The midpoint is not an [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) and cannot be treated as a harmonic restoring centre.
