# Surjective endomorphism of a Noetherian ring is an automorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Noetherian ring](noetherian-ring.md)

For a surjective [ring endomorphism](ring-endomorphism.md) $f$ of a [Noetherian ring](noetherian-ring.md), the ascending kernel chain $\ker f\subseteq\ker f^2\subseteq\cdots$ stabilizes. If $\ker f^m=\ker f^{m+1}$ and $f(a)=0$, choose $b$ with $f^m(b)=a$. Then $b\in\ker f^{m+1}=\ker f^m$, so $a=0$. Thus the [endomorphism](endomorphism.md) is a [ring automorphism](ring-automorphism.md).
