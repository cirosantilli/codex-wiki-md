# Naturality of the cap product

↑ **Parent:** [Cap product](cap-product.md)

For a [singular simplex](singular-simplex.md) $\sigma$ of dimension $n$ and a degree-$p$ [cochain](singular-cochain.md), use $\sigma\frown\alpha=\alpha(\sigma|[v_0,\ldots,v_p])\sigma|[v_p,\ldots,v_n]$, setting the result to zero if $p>n$. A [continuous map](continuous-map.md) $f$ commutes with both face restrictions, and the [pullback](pullback-category-theory.md) cochain evaluates by composing with $f$. Therefore $f_*(\sigma\frown f^*\alpha)=(f\circ\sigma)\frown\alpha$ already on chains. Passing to [homology](homology-split.md) and [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) proves the displayed [cap product](cap-product.md) identity.
