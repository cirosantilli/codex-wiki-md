# Effective connectivity in neuroscience

↑ **Parent:** [Neural circuit](neural-circuit.md)

A model-dependent or experimentally tested influence of one neural system on another. Perturbing one region and measuring a remote change strengthens a causal interaction claim, although the influence may be indirect.
