# Isothermal compressibility from chemical-potential response

↑ **Parent:** [Isothermal compressibility](isothermal-compressibility.md)

For a homogeneous extensive single-component [thermodynamic phase](thermodynamic-phase.md), the [Gibbs-Duhem equation](gibbs-duhem-equation.md) at fixed [temperature](temperature-split.md) gives $dp=n\,d\mu$. With [number density](number-density.md) $n=N/V$, fixed $N$ gives $dn=-(n/V)dV$. The [chain rule](chain-rule.md) therefore gives

$$
\kappa_T=\frac1n\left(\frac{\partial n}{\partial p}\right)_T
=\frac1{n^2}\left(\frac{\partial n}{\partial\mu}\right)_T.
$$

The [derivatives](derivative.md) are evaluated within the same smooth [equation of state](equation-of-state.md); an interface contribution or [phase coexistence](phase-coexistence.md) can invalidate this simple homogeneous derivation.
