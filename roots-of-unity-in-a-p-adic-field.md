# Roots of unity in a p-adic field

↑ **Parent:** [Local field](local-field.md)

The roots of unity in a finite extension $K/\mathbb Q_p$ form a finite group. Each element of their prime-to-$p$ part is a [Teichmuller representative](teichmuller-representative.md), while any $p$-power part lies among the [principal units](principal-unit.md).
