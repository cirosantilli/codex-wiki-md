# Meromorphic functions on the sphere are rational

↑ **Parent:** [Meromorphic function](meromorphic-function.md)

[Poles](pole.md) are isolated, including in the coordinate $w=1/z$ around infinity. [Compactness](compact-space.md) of the [Riemann sphere](riemann-sphere.md) therefore leaves only finitely many [poles](pole.md). Subtract the finite principal parts at finite [poles](pole.md) and the [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) principal part at infinity. The remainder is entire with a finite limit at infinity, hence bounded and constant by [Liouville's theorem](liouville-theorem.md). The original function is a sum of a [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) and finitely many partial fractions, so is rational.
