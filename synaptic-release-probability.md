# Synaptic release probability

↑ **Parent:** [Quantal neurotransmitter release](quantal-neurotransmitter-release.md)

[Synaptic release probability](synaptic-release-probability.md) is the [probability](probability.md) of release at a specified site during a specified opportunity, such as one presynaptic [action potential](action-potential.md). It depends on the site's state and conditions; it is not the [probability](probability.md) that the receiving [neuron](neuron.md) fires. In the example under [synaptic vesicle](synaptic-vesicle.md), $p$ refers to one available site, whereas $1-(1-p)^4$ refers to any of four [independent](independent-random-variables.md) sites releasing.
