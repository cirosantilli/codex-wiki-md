# Reverse fault

↑ **Parent:** [Reverse faulting](reverse-faulting.md)

A [fault](geological-fault.md) on which the [hanging wall](hanging-wall.md) moves up relative to the [footwall](footwall.md), accommodating shortening. A low-angle [reverse fault](reverse-fault.md) is a [thrust fault](thrust-fault.md). The [fault dip](fault-dip.md) is a geometric property and should be distinguished from the direction of [fault slip](fault-slip.md).
