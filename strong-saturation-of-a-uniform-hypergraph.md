# Strong saturation of a uniform hypergraph

↑ **Parent:** [Complete uniform hypergraph](complete-uniform-hypergraph.md)

An $r$-[uniform hypergraph](uniform-hypergraph.md) is strongly saturated with respect to a complete uniform hypergraph of order $r+t$ when every missing edge immediately completes such a clique. Existing cliques are allowed. All $r$-sets meeting a fixed $t$-set give an example: the witness for a missing edge is its union with that fixed set. It has $\binom nr-\binom{n-t}{r}$ edges, and the [exterior-power quotient bound for strong hypergraph saturation](exterior-power-quotient-bound-for-strong-hypergraph-saturation.md) proves optimality.
