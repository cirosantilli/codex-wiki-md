# Knight theorem for orthogonal martingales

↑ **Parent:** [Orthogonal continuous local martingales](orthogonal-continuous-local-martingales.md)

Let $M_1,\ldots,M_N$ be continuous [local martingales](local-martingale.md) starting at zero, with $\langle M_i,M_j\rangle=0$ for $i\ne j$ and $\langle M_j\rangle_\infty=\infty$ almost surely. The individual [Dambis-Dubins-Schwarz theorem](dambis-dubins-schwarz-theorem.md) time changes produce [independent](independent-random-variables.md) standard [Brownian motions](brownian-motion-split.md) $B_j$ with $M_j(t)=B_j(\langle M_j\rangle_t)$. Independence is between the [Brownian motions](brownian-motion-split.md); each may still depend on its own clock.
