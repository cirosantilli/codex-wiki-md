# Nuclear magneton

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear magnetic moment](nuclear-magnetic-moment.md)

The [nuclear magneton](nuclear-magneton.md) is the unit of [magnetic moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md)

$$
\mu_N=\frac{e\hbar}{2m_p},
$$

where $m_p$ is the [proton](proton.md) mass. For a [nuclear spin](nuclear-spin.md) operator $\mathbf I$, the [nuclear magnetic moment](nuclear-magnetic-moment.md) is $\boldsymbol\mu=g_I\mu_N\mathbf I/\hbar$. Consequently its [gyromagnetic ratio](gyromagnetic-ratio.md) is $\gamma_I=g_I\mu_N/\hbar$, and its [Larmor frequency](larmor-frequency.md) in a uniform [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) of magnitude $B$ is $|\gamma_I|B$. The [nuclear magneton](nuclear-magneton.md) is smaller than the [Bohr magneton](bohr-magneton.md) by the electron-to-proton mass ratio.
