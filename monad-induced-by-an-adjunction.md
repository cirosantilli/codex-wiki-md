# Monad induced by an adjunction

↑ **Parent:** [Monad](monad.md)

An adjunction $F\dashv G$ with unit $\eta$ and counit $\varepsilon$ induces the monad

$$
T=GF,\qquad \mu=G\varepsilon F,
$$

with unit $\eta$.
