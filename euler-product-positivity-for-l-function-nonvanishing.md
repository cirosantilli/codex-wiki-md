# Euler product positivity for L-function nonvanishing

↑ **Parent:** [Euler product](euler-product.md)

For $\sigma>1$, logarithmic expansion of the [Euler product](euler-product.md) reduces positivity to $3+4\cos\theta+\cos2\theta=2(1+\cos\theta)^2$. At [primes](prime-number.md) dividing the modulus the zeta contribution is positive by itself. If the last factor is bounded at the line-one point, a zero of the middle factor would outweigh the zeta [pole](pole.md) and force this product to zero. This proves the relevant [nonvanishing of Dirichlet L-functions on the line one](nonvanishing-of-dirichlet-l-functions-on-the-line-one.md).
