# Age of a closed dust universe

↑ **Parent:** [Age of a dust universe without a cosmological constant](age-of-a-dust-universe-without-a-cosmological-constant.md)

For an expanding [pressureless matter](pressureless-matter.md) universe with positive curvature, zero [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md) and present [scale factor](scale-factor-cosmology.md) one, set $u_0=\arccos(2/\Omega_0-1)\in(0,\pi)$. The [closed matter-dominated Friedmann solution](closed-matter-dominated-friedmann-solution.md) then gives the displayed [age of an FLRW universe](age-of-an-flrw-universe.md). Its flat limit is $2/(3H_0)$, despite the individually vanishing numerator and denominator. To see this, write $\delta=\Omega_0-1$; then $u_0=2\arctan\sqrt\delta$ and $u_0-\sin u_0=(4/3)\delta^{3/2}+O(\delta^{5/2})$.
