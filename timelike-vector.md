# Timelike vector

↑ **Parent:** [Lorentzian metric](lorentzian-metric.md)

A [timelike vector](timelike-vector.md) $v$ obeys $g(v,v)<0$ in [metric signature](metric-signature.md) $(-,+,+,+)$. Its orthogonal complement has positive-definite metric; the two timelike cones give the local future and past choices.
