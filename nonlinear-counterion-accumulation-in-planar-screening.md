# Nonlinear counterion accumulation in planar screening

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson-Boltzmann equation](poisson-boltzmann-equation.md)

A strong negative surface potential creates exponentially enhanced positive [counterion](counterion.md) [density](density.md) and exponentially suppressed negative [co-ion](co-ion.md) [density](density.md). The [co-ion](co-ion.md) population remains positive, unlike an extrapolated linear approximation. Finite ion size and [correlations](pearson-correlation-coefficient.md) eventually limit the applicability of point-ion [mean-field theory](mean-field-theory.md).
