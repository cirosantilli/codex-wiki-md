# Dynamo quenching

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamo action](dynamo-action.md)

[Dynamo quenching](dynamo-quenching.md) is the reduction or modification of magnetic-field generation by magnetic feedback on the generating flow and its correlations. The [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md) can change shear or turbulence, while [magnetic helicity](magnetic-helicity.md) affects mean-field regeneration. A phenomenological [scalar](scalar.md) example is $\alpha(B)=\alpha_0/(1+B^2/B_*^2)$. In the illustrative amplitude equation $\dot B=[c\alpha(B)-d]B$ with $c,d>0$, a growing field saturates at $B^2=B_*^2(c\alpha_0/d-1)$ when $c\alpha_0>d$. This demonstrates the negative feedback mechanism without claiming that algebraic quenching is a complete solar closure.
