# Clamped Hessian identity

↑ **Parent:** [Clamped second-order Sobolev space](clamped-second-order-sobolev-space.md)

For $u\in H_0^2(U)$, $\sum_{i,j}\|\partial_{ij}u\|_2^2=\|\Delta u\|_2^2$. Two [integrations by parts](integration-by-parts.md) prove this for compactly supported [test functions](test-function.md), and $H^2$ density extends it to the [clamped second-order Sobolev space](clamped-second-order-sobolev-space.md). Together with the [Poincare-Wirtinger inequality](poincare-wirtinger-inequality.md) applied to the mean-zero first derivatives and the zero-boundary [Poincaré inequality](poincare-inequality.md), it controls the full $H^2$ [norm](norm.md) by $\|\Delta u\|_2$. The boundary conditions are essential to this exact identity on bounded domains.
