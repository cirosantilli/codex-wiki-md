# Filtration (chemistry)

↑ **Parent:** [Solution (chemistry)](solution-chemistry.md)

[Filtration](filtration-chemistry.md) separates suspended [solid](solid.md) particles from a fluid using a porous barrier. Dissolved [ions](ion.md) can pass with the fluid; the separation does not establish the filtrate's composition. [Solubility product measurement by titration](solubility-product-measurement-by-titration.md) requires removal of undissolved [calcium hydroxide](calcium-hydroxide.md) before [acid-base titration](acid-base-titration.md).
