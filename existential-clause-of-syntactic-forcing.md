# Existential clause of syntactic forcing

↑ **Parent:** [Syntactic forcing relation](syntactic-forcing-relation.md)

For the usual recursive [syntactic forcing relation](syntactic-forcing-relation.md), $p\Vdash^*\exists x\,\varphi(x)$ means that conditions $q\leq p$ forcing $\varphi(\sigma)$ for some [forcing name](forcing-name.md) $\sigma$ are [dense below a forcing condition](dense-below-a-forcing-condition.md) $p$. This density clause proves the existential step of the [forcing theorem](forcing-theorem.md) from the truth lemma for each named instance. A [generic filter](generic-filter.md) containing $p$ meets that dense set after it is augmented by conditions incompatible with $p$.
