# Stationary position of a transposed distinguished book

↑ **Parent:** [Stationary distribution](stationary-distribution.md)

If a distinguished book is selected with [probability](probability.md) $p$ and each other book with [probability](probability.md) $q=(1-p)/(N-1)$, swapping the selected book with its left neighbor makes the distinguished position a [birth-death chain](birth-death-chain.md) with left rate $p$ and right rate $q$. Stationary [flow balance](flow-balance.md) gives successive probabilities in ratio $q/p$, yielding a truncated [geometric distribution](geometric-distribution.md); the uniform case occurs when all books are equally likely.
