# Radian

↑ **Parent:** [Angle](angle.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radian)

An [angle](angle.md) in [radians](radian.md) is the ratio of its subtended arc [length](length.md) to the radius of a [circle](circle.md). A full turn is $2\pi$ [radians](radian.md) and a [right angle](right-angle.md) is $\pi/2$ [radians](radian.md).
