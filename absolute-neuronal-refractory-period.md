# Absolute neuronal refractory period

↑ **Parent:** [Neuronal refractory period](neuronal-refractory-period.md)

During an [absolute neuronal refractory period](absolute-neuronal-refractory-period.md), the excitable region cannot initiate a second distinct regenerative [action potential](action-potential.md) under the physiological stimulation conditions being considered. Extensive [sodium](sodium.md) [ion-channel inactivation](ion-channel-inactivation.md) is central for a fast [sodium](sodium.md)-dependent [action potential](action-potential.md). “Cannot fire” concerns regeneration; an imposed pulse can still move [membrane potential](membrane-potential-split.md) or leave a recording artifact. Failure of just one fixed-amplitude test pulse does not establish an [absolute neuronal refractory period](absolute-neuronal-refractory-period.md): the same failure can occur in the [relative neuronal refractory period](relative-neuronal-refractory-period.md).

The boundary is an operational question about distinguishable [action potentials](action-potential.md) and the range of stimulation tested. A drawn vertical line is useful in the [phenomenological paired-pulse recovery](phenomenological-paired-pulse-recovery.md) model, where it is imposed explicitly; it should not be mistaken for a universal sharp biochemical switch.
