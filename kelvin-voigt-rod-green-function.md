# Kelvin-Voigt rod Green function

↑ **Parent:** [Kelvin-Voigt model](kelvin-voigt-model.md)

For a rod with unit mass density and elastic modulus, the [Kelvin-Voigt model](kelvin-voigt-model.md) gives $\phi_{tt}-\phi_{xx}-\gamma\phi_{txx}=0$, $\gamma>0$. For the operator with the opposite overall sign, $-\partial_t^2+\partial_x^2+\gamma\partial_t\partial_x^2$, the inverse under the $e^{-i\omega t}$ [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md) convention is the displayed expression. Its poles are $\omega_\pm=-i\gamma k^2/2\pm\sqrt{k^2-\gamma^2k^4/4}$. With $\Omega_k=\sqrt{k^2-\gamma^2k^4/4}$ real, the [retarded fundamental solution](retarded-fundamental-solution.md) in spatial Fourier variables is $\widetilde G_R(k,t)=-\Theta(t)e^{-\gamma k^2t/2}\sin(\Omega_kt)/\Omega_k$. Its initial derivative is $-1$, fixed by the leading coefficient $-\partial_t^2$. At a repeated root take the continuous limit; for imaginary $\Omega_k$ the sine becomes a hyperbolic sine.
