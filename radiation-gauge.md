# Radiation gauge

↑ **Parent:** [Coulomb gauge](coulomb-gauge.md)

For a free [electromagnetic field](electromagnetic-field.md) with suitable boundary conditions, combine [Coulomb gauge](coulomb-gauge.md) $\partial_iA_i=0$ with $A_0=0$. [Gauss's law](gauss-s-law.md) makes the second condition consistent in the source-free problem. The remaining field operator is spatially transverse and describes the two radiative [photon](photon.md) polarizations. A constrained scalar potential must be restored when charges are present.
