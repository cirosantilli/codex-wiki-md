# Ambolestes zhoui

↑ **Parent:** [Eutheria](eutheria.md)

An Early [Cretaceous](cretaceous.md) [eutherian](eutheria.md) from the Jehol biota. Its well-preserved anatomy informed reassessment of characters used to distinguish early [eutherians](eutheria.md) from metatherians.
