# Characteristic-function tightness bound

↑ **Parent:** [Lévy continuity theorem](levy-continuity-theorem.md)

For a [probability measure](probability-measure.md) on the real line with [characteristic function](characteristic-function.md) $\varphi$, averaging $1-\cos(tx)$ over $[-h,h]$ gives $1-\sin(hx)/(hx)$. This is at least $1/2$ when $|x|\geq2/h$. Integrating gives the displayed bound. Consequently pointwise convergence of [characteristic functions](characteristic-function.md) to a function continuous at zero implies [tightness of probability measures](uniform-tightness.md), by the [dominated convergence theorem](dominated-convergence-theorem.md) on $[-h,h]$ and a separate tail bound for the finitely many initial measures.
