# Isotropic subspace of a symplectic vector space

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic vector space](symplectic-vector-space.md)

A subspace $W$ of a [symplectic vector space](symplectic-vector-space.md) $(V,\Omega)$ is isotropic when $\Omega|_{W\times W}=0$. It has at most half the dimension of $V$; equality makes it a [Lagrangian subspace](lagrangian-subspace.md). This use of isotropic concerns an alternating [bilinear form](bilinear-form.md), rather than an [isotropic quadratic form](isotropic-quadratic-form.md).
