# Hybridoma technology

↑ **Parent:** [Antibody](antibody.md)

[Hybridoma technology](hybridoma-technology.md) combines an antibody-producing primary cell with an immortal cell line to establish continued production of a chosen antibody. Fusion-product survival and antigen specificity require separate selection and screening steps.
