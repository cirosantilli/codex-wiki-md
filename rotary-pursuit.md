# Rotary pursuit

↑ **Parent:** [Procedural memory](procedural-memory.md)

A motor-learning task in which a participant tries to keep a stylus on a moving target. Practice-related improvement and retention across sessions can assess skill learning separately from explicit recollection of the practice.
