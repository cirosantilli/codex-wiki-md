# Symmetric cyclotomic unit

↑ **Parent:** [Cyclotomic unit](cyclotomic-unit.md)

Half exponents mean the inverse of two in the odd-order root-of-unity group. For $p\nmid ab$, the ratio is a real global unit and is compatible under the norms in the p-power tower. Its [Coleman power series](coleman-power-series.md) is $(1+T)^{(b-a)/2}(1-(1+T)^a)/(1-(1+T)^b)$, with constant term $a/b$. Its [higher logarithmic derivatives](cyclotomic-higher-logarithmic-derivative.md) vanish at odd indices and equal $(a^k-b^k)B_k/k$ at even indices, using $B_1=-1/2$.
