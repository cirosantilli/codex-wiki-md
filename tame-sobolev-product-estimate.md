# Tame Sobolev product estimate

↑ **Parent:** [Sobolev algebra](sobolev-algebra.md)

For an integer $k\geq0$ and sufficiently regular bounded functions, $\|fg\|_{H^k}\leq C_k(\|f\|_\infty\|g\|_{H^k}+\|g\|_\infty\|f\|_{H^k})$. The [Leibniz rule](leibniz-rule.md) and derivative interpolation give the estimate. In a differentiated [semilinear wave equation](semilinear-wave-equation.md), this separates a controlled low-order coefficient from an arbitrarily high [Sobolev norm](sobolev-norm.md), allowing propagation of smoothness after a fixed base norm has been controlled.
