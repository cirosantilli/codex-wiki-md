# Parameter sensitivity of autorepressed expression

↑ **Parent:** [Negative autoregulation](negative-autoregulation.md)

At a [Hill equation](hill-equation.md) equilibrium with $s=(Y/K)^n$, differentiating $\beta=\alpha Y(1+s)$ gives the displayed logarithmic sensitivity. It approaches one without effective feedback and $1/(n+1)$ under strong repression. Threshold pinning in the [logic approximation for gene regulation](logic-approximation-for-gene-regulation.md) instead gives approximately zero production sensitivity while the threshold is held fixed.
