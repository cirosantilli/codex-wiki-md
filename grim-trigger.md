# Grim trigger

↑ **Parent:** [Iterated prisoner's dilemma](iterated-prisoner-s-dilemma.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grim_trigger)

A [grim trigger](grim-trigger.md) is a history-dependent [pure strategy](pure-strategy.md) in a [repeated game](repeated-game.md) that switches permanently to punishment after a trigger event. In the [iterated prisoner's dilemma](iterated-prisoner-s-dilemma.md) above, the trigger is any player's observed defection, and punishment is [always defect](always-defect.md). Unlike [Tit for tat](tit-for-tat.md), [grim trigger](grim-trigger.md) never restores cooperation after the opponent cooperates again. Whether mutual [grim trigger](grim-trigger.md) is a [subgame perfect equilibrium](subgame-perfect-equilibrium.md) depends on the stage [payoffs](payoff.md), [discount factor](discount-factor.md), and monitoring assumptions; it is not an unconditional property of the rule. See [Ozdaglar, lecture 15, slides 12–15](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-254-game-theory-with-engineering-applications-spring-2010/5a158a7558165d0e22b5b27fcfa01713_MIT6_254S10_lec15.pdf#page=12).
