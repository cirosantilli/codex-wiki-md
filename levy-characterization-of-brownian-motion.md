<h1 id="levy-characterization-of-brownian-motion">Lévy characterization of Brownian motion</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md)

A continuous local martingale $M$ with $M_0=0$ is standard Brownian motion exactly when its [quadratic variation](quadratic-variation.md) satisfies $[M]_t=t$.

The characterization asserts that a continuous [local martingale](local-martingale.md) starting at zero with quadratic variation $t$ is [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md).
