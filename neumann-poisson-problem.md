# Neumann Poisson problem

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson's equation](poisson-s-equation.md)

On a smooth bounded connected domain, the homogeneous Neumann problem seeks $u\in H^1(U)$ such that $\int\nabla u\cdot\nabla v=\int fv$ for every $v\in H^1(U)$. For $f\in L^2(U)$ it is solvable exactly when $\int f=0$, and the [weak solution](weak-solution.md) is unique up to constants. The [Poincare-Wirtinger inequality](poincare-wirtinger-inequality.md) and the [Riesz representation theorem](riesz-representation-theorem.md) give existence on the [mean-zero Sobolev space](mean-zero-sobolev-space.md). The test function one gives necessity of the compatibility condition.
