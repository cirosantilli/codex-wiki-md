# Minimal faithful permutation degree of the quaternion group

↑ **Parent:** [Quaternion group](quaternion-group.md)

The least size of a finite set on which the [quaternion group](quaternion-group.md) acts faithfully is eight. Every nontrivial [subgroup](subgroup.md) of $Q_8$ contains its central involution $-1$. In an action on fewer than eight points, the [orbit-stabilizer theorem](orbit-stabilizer-theorem.md) makes every [stabilizer subgroup](stabilizer-subgroup.md) nontrivial, so $-1$ fixes every point. This rules out a [faithful group action](faithful-group-action.md), even with several [group orbits](orbit-of-a-group-action.md). The regular action on eight points is faithful by [Cayley theorem](cayley-s-theorem.md).
