# Sum of two cubes

↑ **Parent:** [Factorization of polynomials](factorization-of-polynomials.md)

Replacing $b$ by $-b$ in the [difference of two cubes](difference-of-two-cubes.md) gives

$$
\boxed{a^3+b^3=(a+b)(a^2-ab+b^2).}
$$

For real cube roots $u=a^{1/3}$ and $v=b^{1/3}$, this simplifies $(a^{1/3}+b^{1/3})(a^{2/3}-(ab)^{1/3}+b^{2/3})$ to $a+b$. Specifying real cube roots matters when using such identities with negative real inputs; unconstrained [complex exponentiation](complex-exponentiation.md) has logarithm-branch choices.
