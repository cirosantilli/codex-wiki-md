# Causal Fourier resolvent of a point-driven fixed string

↑ **Parent:** [Wave equation](wave-equation-split.md)

For $y_{tt}-y_{xx}=F(t)\delta(x-x_0)$ with zero endpoint displacement, a [Fourier sine series](fourier-sine-series.md) gives $q_n''+k_n^2q_n=2F(t)\sin(k_nx_0)/L$. With the $e^{-i\omega t}$ [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md) convention, the causal inverse of this oscillator uses the displayed denominator. Its inverse transform is $H(t)\sin(k_nt)/k_n$, giving $q_n(t)=2\sin(k_nx_0)/(Lk_n)\int_{-\infty}^tF(s)\sin[k_n(t-s)]ds$ when the past integral exists. The plain denominator $k_n^2-\omega^2$ requires a pole prescription: its [Cauchy principal value](cauchy-principal-value.md) gives the time-symmetric kernel $\sin(k_n|t|)/(2k_n)$ instead. Homogeneous sine and cosine modes are additional freedom unless temporal data or causality are specified.
