<h1 id="elsasser-energy-invariant">Elsässer energy invariant</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Elsässer variable](elsasser-variable.md)

For smooth [ideal magnetohydrodynamics](ideal-magnetohydrodynamics.md) of constant [mass density](density.md), define $I_\pm=\int_V|\mathbf z^\pm|^2dV$. Taking the [dot product](dot-product.md) of the [Elsässer variable](elsasser-variable.md) equation with $2\mathbf z^\pm$ gives

$$
\partial_t|\mathbf z^\pm|^2+\nabla\cdot\left(|\mathbf z^\pm|^2\mathbf z^\mp+2\psi\mathbf z^\pm\right)=0.
$$

Therefore each integral is constant when its outward boundary flux vanishes, for example when both [velocity](velocity.md) and [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) are tangent to the fixed boundary, or for [periodic boundary conditions](periodic-boundary-conditions.md). The [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) plus [magnetic energy](magnetic-energy.md) is $\rho(I_++I_-)/4$, while the [cross-helicity](cross-helicity.md) is $\sqrt{\mu_0\rho}(I_+-I_-)/4$.
