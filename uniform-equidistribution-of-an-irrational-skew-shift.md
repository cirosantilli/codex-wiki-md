# Uniform equidistribution of an irrational skew shift

↑ **Parent:** [Irrational skew shift](irrational-skew-shift.md)

For an [irrational skew shift](irrational-skew-shift.md), the averages of every continuous function $g$ converge uniformly in the starting point to $\int g\,dm_2$, with $m_2$ normalized [Lebesgue measure](lebesgue-measure.md). Nonconstant [Fourier basis](fourier-basis.md) characters have either linear or quadratic phases. Linear phases are bounded [geometric series](geometric-series.md); for quadratic phases the [Van der Corput inequality for finite scalar sequences](van-der-corput-inequality-for-finite-scalar-sequences.md) reduces to linear correlations of irrational frequency, uniformly in the starting point. Approximation by [trigonometric polynomials](trigonometric-polynomial.md) proves the assertion and identifies every invariant [Borel probability measure](borel-probability-measure.md) as $m_2$.
