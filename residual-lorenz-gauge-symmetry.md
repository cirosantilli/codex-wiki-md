# Residual Lorenz gauge symmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Lorenz gauge condition](lorenz-gauge-condition.md)

An Abelian [gauge transformation](gauge-transformation.md) $A_a\mapsto A_a+\partial_a\lambda$ changes $\partial\cdot A$ by $\Box\lambda$. Consequently [gauge fixing](gauge-fixing.md) by a quadratic function of $\partial\cdot A$ retains transformations satisfying $\Box\lambda=0$, subject to the same [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md). Eliminating those residual transformations is part of specifying an inverse with a particular [Green function](green-s-function-split.md) prescription.
