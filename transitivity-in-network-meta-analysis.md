# Transitivity in network meta-analysis

↑ **Parent:** [Indirect treatment comparison](indirect-treatment-comparison.md)

Transitivity requires the studies of different treatment comparisons to be sufficiently comparable in distributions of [effect modifiers](effect-modifier.md), outcome definitions and other design features to support an [indirect treatment comparison](indirect-treatment-comparison.md) in one target population. For example, a treatment that works differently by disease severity cannot safely be compared indirectly across trials with systematically different severity distributions. Statistical consistency is an implication of suitable transitivity and modeling assumptions, not a substitute for assessing them.
