# Transverse stiffness of a stretched spring

↑ **Parent:** [Hooke's law](hooke-s-law.md)

A taut [spring](spring.md) of equilibrium [length](length.md) $L$ and initial [tension](tension.md) $T_0$ whose ends acquire a small relative transverse displacement $y$ tilts through $y/L$. Its leading transverse [force](force-split.md) is therefore $-T_0y/L$. The [length](length.md) change is quadratic in $y$, so using only incremental axial extension misses this first-order [force](force-split.md). A Hooke [spring](spring.md) with natural [length](length.md) $l_0$ has $T_0=k(L-l_0)$ and $k_\perp=k(1-l_0/L)$. The unstretched limit has no linear transverse stiffness under this geometry.
