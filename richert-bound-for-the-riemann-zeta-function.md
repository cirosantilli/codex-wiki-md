# Richert bound for the Riemann zeta function

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann zeta function](riemann-zeta-function.md)

For large positive $t$ and $0<\sigma\le1$, a fixed sufficiently large $C$ gives the displayed upper bound; to the right of one use $|\zeta(\sigma+it)|\ll\log^{2/3}t$. Its nonlinear dependence on $1-\sigma$ permits a wider [zero-free region of the Riemann zeta function](zero-free-region-of-the-riemann-zeta-function.md) through the [Landau zero-free-region theorem](landau-zero-free-region-theorem.md). Its proof uses estimates for [exponential sums](exponential-sum.md); its use as an upper-bound input is separate from a proof of a zero-free region.
