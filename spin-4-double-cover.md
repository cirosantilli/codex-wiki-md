<h1 id="spin-4-double-cover">Spin(4) double cover</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Spin group](spin-group.md)

Two [unit quaternions](unit-quaternion.md) act on Euclidean four-space by $q\mapsto aqb^{-1}$. This norm-preserving action maps onto the [SO(4) group](so-4-group.md). Its kernel is precisely $(1,1)$ and $(-1,-1)$: a kernel pair must have equal entries commuting with every [quaternion](quaternion.md). The two simply connected [SU(2)](su-2-group.md) factors therefore form the universal spin cover.
