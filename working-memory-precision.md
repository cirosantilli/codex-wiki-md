# Working memory precision

↑ **Parent:** [Working memory](working-memory.md)

The fidelity of a retained representation, assessed through the distribution of errors in continuous reports or a suitable behavioral model. Loss of precision is distinct from complete loss of an item; both can influence aggregate accuracy.
