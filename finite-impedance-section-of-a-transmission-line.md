# Finite impedance section of a transmission line

↑ **Parent:** [Transmission line](transmission-line.md)

A [length](length.md) $\ell$ with impedance ratio $\alpha$ between identical matched lossless lines has multiple internal reflections. Summing their amplitudes gives the displayed [power](power.md) transmission when the propagation [speed](speed.md) is the same in all sections. It is unity at half-wave resonances even though either individual interface reflects. Matched remote terminations remove external echoes but not this [frequency](frequency.md) dependence.
