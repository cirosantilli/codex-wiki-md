# Selectivity bound for a two-real-pole band-pass

↑ **Parent:** [Inverting two-pole band-pass filter](inverting-two-pole-band-pass-filter.md)

For a response proportional to $s/[(1+s\tau_1)(1+s\tau_2)]$ with positive time constants, its half-power width is $1/\tau_1+1/\tau_2$. Combining with the geometric-mean centre [frequency](frequency.md) gives the displayed [quality factor](q-factor.md); the [arithmetic-geometric mean inequality](arithmetic-geometric-mean-inequality.md) proves the bound. Equality requires matched time constants and gives half-power [frequencies](frequency.md) $(\sqrt2\pm1)\omega_0$. Adjusting gains does not make this topology a high-Q resonant filter.
