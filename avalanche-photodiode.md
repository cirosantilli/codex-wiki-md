# Avalanche photodiode

↑ **Parent:** [Photodiode](photodiode.md)

A [photodiode](photodiode.md) with a strong internal [electric field](electric-field-split.md) in which photogenerated charge carriers undergo [impact ionization](impact-ionization.md), amplifying the initial signal. Linear multiplication below breakdown and saturated single-event operation above breakdown are distinct operating regimes. A [single-photon avalanche diode](single-photon-avalanche-diode.md) uses the latter.
