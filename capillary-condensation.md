# Capillary condensation

↑ **Parent:** [Kelvin equation](kelvin-equation.md)

Condensation in a [wetting](wetting.md) confined geometry at a [vapor pressure](vapor-pressure.md) below planar-interface saturation. A concave [liquid](liquid.md) meniscus has lower equilibrium [vapor pressure](vapor-pressure.md) in the capillarity approximation. Gap size, [contact angle](contact-angle.md) and surface [adsorption](adsorption.md) affect the actual onset.
