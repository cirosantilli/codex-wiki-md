# Finite sl2 lowest-weight ladder

↑ **Parent:** [Sl2 triple](sl2-triple.md)

In a finite-dimensional representation of an [sl2 triple](sl2-triple.md), start with a nonzero vector $X_0$ satisfying $fX_0=0$ and $hX_0=-\lambda X_0$, and set $X_n=e^nX_0$. The [commutator](commutator.md) identities give the displayed weight and lowering formulas by induction. If $X_N\ne0$ and $X_{N+1}=0$, then $fX_{N+1}=(N+1)(\lambda-N)X_N$ forces $\lambda=N$, a nonnegative [integer](integer.md). The nonzero terminal vector is essential: choosing a later index after the ladder has already vanished gives no constraint. Applied to the [Adjoint representation](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md) of a [semisimple Lie algebra](semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md), this argument proves integrality of [Cartan integers](cartan-integer.md).
