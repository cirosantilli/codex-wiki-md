# Differentiating left-invariant matrix fields

↑ **Parent:** [Left-invariant vector field](left-invariant-vector-field.md)

On a [Matrix Lie group](matrix-lie-group.md), the [left-invariant vector field](left-invariant-vector-field.md) associated to a [tangent vector](tangent-vector.md) $B$ at the identity is $X_B(Q)=QB$. Its ambient derivative is $DX_B(Q)[H]=HB$. Therefore the [Lie bracket of vector fields](lie-bracket-of-vector-fields.md) is $[X_{B_1},X_{B_2}](Q)=Q(B_1B_2-B_2B_1)$, and the induced [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) bracket is the [commutator](commutator.md). This fixes the sign for left invariance; right-invariant fields induce the opposite sign when evaluated with the same matrix identification.
