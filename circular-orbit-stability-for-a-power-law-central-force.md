# Circular-orbit stability for a power-law central force

↑ **Parent:** [Circular orbit](circular-orbit.md)

For the radial [acceleration](acceleration.md) $-kr^n$ with $k>0$, a [circular orbit](circular-orbit.md) of radius $a$ has squared [specific angular momentum](specific-angular-momentum.md) $h^2=ka^{n+3}$. At fixed $h$, its radial [small oscillations](small-oscillation.md) have squared frequency $(n+3)ka^{n-1}$, so the orbit is stable for $n>-3$. This is a special case of the [effective potential stability criterion](effective-potential-stability-criterion.md).
