# Barotropic shearing-wave gravitational amplitude system

↑ **Parent:** [Shearing wave](shearing-wave.md)

A [shearing-wave ansatz](shearing-wave-ansatz.md) in a uniform inviscid isothermal [shearing sheet](shearing-sheet.md) removes background advection when the radial [wavevector](wavevector.md) grows as $k_x(t)=k_x(0)+Sk_yt$. The [razor-thin disk Poisson kernel](razor-thin-disk-poisson-kernel.md) reduces the pressure-plus-gravity amplitude to $[c_s^2-2\pi G\Sigma_0/k]\widetilde\Sigma'/\Sigma_0$. Continuity couples it to $ik_xv_x+ik_yv_y$, while the two momentum equations have rotational couplings $-2\Omega v_y$ and $(2\Omega-S)v_x$. The [linearized vortensity](linearized-vortensity.md) amplitude remains constant, because the barotropic [pressure](pressure.md) and gravitational forces have zero [curl](curl.md) and the background [vortensity](vortensity.md) is uniform.
