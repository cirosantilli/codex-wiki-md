# Offset uniformly charged spherical shells

↑ **Parent:** [Spherical shell theorem](spherical-shell-theorem.md)

Place a fixed shell of radius $a$ and charge $-q$ at $(0,a/2)$ and a fixed shell of radius $2a$ and charge $+q$ at $(0,-a/2)$. On the positive $x$ axis their centre distances coincide. The electrostatic [shell theorem](spherical-shell-theorem.md) makes the [electric field](electric-field-split.md) vanish until $x=\sqrt3a/2$, gives magnitude $k_eq/(x^2+a^2/4)$ until $x=\sqrt{15}a/2$, and gives $k_eqa/(x^2+a^2/4)^{3/2}$ thereafter. At each ideal charged surface the field has distinct one-sided limits. The net charge is zero but the [electric dipole moment](electric-dipole-moment.md) is $-qa\hat{\mathbf y}$.
