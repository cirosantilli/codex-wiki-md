# Posterior coin selection from repeated tosses

↑ **Parent:** [Bayes' theorem](bayes-theorem.md)

If a hidden coin is selected once with prior probability $\pi$ of coin $B$, repeated tosses are [independent](independent-random-variables.md) conditional on that coin. [Bayes' theorem](bayes-theorem.md) weights the prior by each all-heads likelihood. They need not be [independent](independent-random-variables.md) without conditioning on the chosen coin.
