# Isotropic elasticity on tensor components

↑ **Parent:** [Isotropic tensor](isotropic-tensor.md)

The rank-four law $c_{ijkl}=\alpha\delta_{ij}\delta_{kl}+\beta\delta_{ik}\delta_{jl}+\gamma\delta_{il}\delta_{jk}$ sends a strain array to $P_{ij}=\alpha\delta_{ij}T_{kk}+\beta T_{ij}+\gamma T_{ji}$. Applying the [scalar, symmetric-traceless and axial tensor decomposition](scalar-symmetric-traceless-and-axial-tensor-decomposition.md) gives the displayed independent actions on the three components. The inverse requires all relevant coefficients nonzero. Restricting to a [symmetric second-rank tensor](symmetric-second-rank-tensor.md) eliminates the axial part and gives $P_{ij}=\alpha\delta_{ij}T_{kk}+(\beta+\gamma)T_{ij}$. Without that symmetry restriction the two-parameter form is not generally valid.
