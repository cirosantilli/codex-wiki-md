<h1 id="squire-s-theorem">Squire's theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Orr-Sommerfeld equation](orr-sommerfeld-equation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Squire's_theorem)

For temporal normal-mode stability of a viscous parallel shear flow, any unstable three-dimensional mode maps by the [Squire transformation](squire-transformation.md) to an unstable two-dimensional mode at a no larger [Reynolds number](reynolds-number.md). Hence the first temporal modal instability occurs among two-dimensional disturbances. The theorem does not exclude three-dimensional [transient growth](transient-growth.md) or forced responses.
