# Theta oscillation

↑ **Parent:** [Neural oscillation](neural-oscillation.md)

A neural rhythm in the frequency range conventionally called theta. In rodent [hippocampal](hippocampus.md) experiments it provides a temporal reference for comparing the firing of local and remote cells. A matching rhythm does not alone identify the direction or necessity of communication.
