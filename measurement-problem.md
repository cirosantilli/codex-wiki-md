# Measurement problem

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum decoherence](quantum-decoherence.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Measurement_problem)

The quantum measurement problem asks how a unitary superposition of system and apparatus outcomes gives rise to one definite observed outcome. [Quantum decoherence](quantum-decoherence.md) explains the suppression of interference between outcome branches, but by itself does not select one branch.
