# Chiral superfield constrained by a nilpotent superfield

↑ **Parent:** [Nilpotent chiral superfield](nilpotent-chiral-superfield.md)

With $X^2=0$ and invertible $F_X$, the additional [chiral superfield](chiral-superfield.md) $Y=y+\sqrt2\theta\chi+\theta^2F_Y$ satisfying $XY=0$ has $y=(G\chi)/F_X-(GG)F_Y/(2F_X^2)$. Its [Weyl spinor](weyl-spinor.md) $\chi$ and [auxiliary field](auxiliary-field.md) $F_Y$ remain independent. This eliminates the independent scalar. It does not force $Y^2=0$.
