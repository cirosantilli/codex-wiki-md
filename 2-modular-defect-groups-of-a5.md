# 2-modular defect groups of A5

↑ **Parent:** [Defect group of a block](defect-group-of-a-block.md)

In characteristic $2$, a [Sylow subgroup](sylow-subgroup.md) $P$ of $A_5$ is a [Klein four-group](klein-four-group.md), and its [normalizer](normalizer.md) is $A_4$. Since $kA_4$ has one block, [Brauer first main theorem](brauer-first-main-theorem.md) gives a unique block of $kA_5$ with defect $P$. No order-two subgroup $D$ can be a [defect group of a block](defect-group-of-a-block.md): both $C_{A_5}(D)$ and $C_{A_5}(P)$ equal $P$, so the [Brauer morphisms](brauer-morphism.md) of a central block idempotent at $D$ and at $P$ are equal. A nonzero image is $1$, since a [group algebra of a p-group in characteristic p is local](group-algebra-of-a-p-group-in-characteristic-p-is-local.md), and consequently $D$ is not maximal among subgroups with nonzero Brauer image.
