# Bernoulli coordinate observable is not almost periodic

↑ **Parent:** [Bernoulli shift](bernoulli-shift.md)

For independent fair signs on a two-sided [Bernoulli shift](bernoulli-shift.md), the coordinate observable $f(y)=y_0$ has unit norm and pairwise orthogonal orbit values. Their pairwise squared distance is two, excluding a finite small net in global $L^2$. Its pullback along a [factor map](factor-map-between-measure-preserving-systems.md) can nevertheless be a [relatively almost periodic observable](relatively-almost-periodic-observable.md), since it is fiber-constant and takes only two values.
