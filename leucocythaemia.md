# Leucocythaemia

↑ **Parent:** [Leukaemia](leukaemia.md)

Historical term for an excess of white blood corpuscles, especially the persistent organ-associated disorders subsequently classified as [leukaemia](leukaemia.md). The old term does not make every raised white count malignant.
