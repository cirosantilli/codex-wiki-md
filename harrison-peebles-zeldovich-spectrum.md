# Harrison-Peebles-Zeldovich spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Matter power spectrum](matter-power-spectrum.md)

The Harrison-Peebles-Zeldovich spectrum is the scale-invariant primordial growing-mode spectrum: in a fixed [conformal time](conformal-time.md) slice before [cosmological horizon crossing](cosmological-horizon-crossing.md), the [matter power spectrum](matter-power-spectrum.md) is proportional to $\tau^4k$ in the usual growing-mode density convention. At horizon entry $\tau_H\sim k^{-1}$, its dimensionless mass [variance](variance-split.md) $k^3P(k,\tau_H)$ is independent of $k$. This statement fixes a spectral shape, not its amplitude. [Radiation domination](radiation-domination.md) subsequently suppresses small-scale density growth through the [Mészáros effect](meszaros-effect.md), producing the asymptotic [matter power spectrum](matter-power-spectrum.md) $P\propto k^{-3}\ln^2(k/k_{\rm eq})$ after [matter-radiation equality](matter-radiation-equality.md).
