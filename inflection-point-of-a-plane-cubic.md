# Inflection point of a plane cubic

↑ **Parent:** [Elliptic curve](elliptic-curve.md)

An inflection point of a smooth plane cubic is a point where its tangent line has intersection multiplicity three. Once one inflection point is chosen as the identity, the nine geometric inflection points are exactly the [3-torsion points](torsion-point-of-an-elliptic-curve.md).
