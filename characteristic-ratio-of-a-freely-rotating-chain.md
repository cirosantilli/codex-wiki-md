# Characteristic ratio of a freely rotating chain

↑ **Parent:** [Freely rotating chain](freely-rotating-chain.md)

For $N$ bonds of length $a$, fixed forward angle $\theta$ and uniform independent torsions, conditional averaging gives $\langle\mathbf a_i\cdot\mathbf a_j\rangle=a^2\alpha^{|j-i|}$. Consequently $\langle R^2\rangle=Na^2+2a^2\sum_{k=1}^{N-1}(N-k)\alpha^k$. Dividing by the independent-step result $Na^2$ and summing the [geometric series](geometric-series.md) gives the displayed $C_N$, for $\alpha\ne1$. For fixed $|\alpha|<1$, $C_N\to C_\infty=(1+\alpha)/(1-\alpha)$.

The angle is measured between forward vectors; a chemical internal bond angle is supplementary. At $\alpha=1$, the finite chain is a rigid rod with $C_N=N$, so taking the infinite-chain limit before the rigid limit would be invalid. The [Kuhn length of a freely rotating chain](kuhn-length-of-a-freely-rotating-chain.md) is asymptotically $aC_\infty$.
