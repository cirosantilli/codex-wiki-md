# Root-system finiteness lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Root system](root-system.md)

For roots $\alpha\ne\pm\beta$,

$$
\langle\alpha,\beta^\vee\rangle
\langle\beta,\alpha^\vee\rangle
=4\cos^2\theta\in\{0,1,2,3\},
$$

where $\theta$ is their angle. Integrality gives an integer, and nonparallelism makes it strictly smaller than four.
