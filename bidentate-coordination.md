# Bidentate coordination

↑ **Parent:** [Ligand in coordination chemistry](ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md)

In [bidentate coordination](bidentate-coordination.md), two donor atoms from one ligand bind the same metal. Attachment through both oxygens of [nitrite](nitrite.md) is distinct from choosing between its N and O monodentate modes.
