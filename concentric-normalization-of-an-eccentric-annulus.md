# Concentric normalization of an eccentric annulus

↑ **Parent:** [Conformal modulus of an annulus](conformal-modulus-of-an-annulus.md)

For an inner circle of real center $c$ and radius $d$ lying strictly inside the unit circle, choose the real root $|a|<1$ of $ca^2-(1+c^2-d^2)a+c=0$. When $c=0$, take $a=0$. The [automorphism of the unit disk](automorphism-of-the-unit-disk.md) $T_a$ sends the inner circle to a concentric circle. Indeed it sends the two real endpoints $c-d,c+d$ to opposite real numbers; preservation of circles and reflection symmetry then give an image centered at zero. Its radius is $r=T_a(c+d)\in(0,1)$.

The [conformal invariance of harmonic measure](conformal-invariance-of-harmonic-measure.md) and [harmonic measure of an annulus](harmonic-measure-of-an-annulus.md) give outer-boundary measure $\log(|T_a(z)|/r)/\log(1/r)$. For $c=d=2/5$, the quadratic gives $a=1/2$ and $r=1/2$, an explicit eccentric-annulus example.
