<h1 id="2-3-sigmatropic-rearrangement">2,3-sigmatropic rearrangement</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Organic chemistry](organic-chemistry.md)

A five-atom pericyclic rearrangement that shifts a sigma bond across adjacent two- and three-atom fragments. Allylic ammonium [ylides](ylide.md) transfer the terminal allyl carbon to the [ylide](ylide.md) carbon.
