# Magnetic field at the center of a hairpin wire

↑ **Parent:** [Biot-Savart law](biot-savart-law.md)

A semicircular current arc of radius $a$ connected tangentially to two parallel semi-infinite wires has the displayed [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) at its centre. The arc contributes $\mu_0I/(4a)$ and each straight segment $\mu_0I/(4\pi a)$ by the [Biot-Savart law](biot-savart-law.md). For the continuous hairpin current all three [magnetic fields](magnetic-field-split.md) have the same normal direction by the [right-hand rule](right-hand-rule.md); they add rather than cancel.
