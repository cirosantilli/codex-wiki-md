# Thin-lens equation

↑ **Parent:** [Thin lens](thin-lens.md)

The paraxial image relation for a [thin lens](thin-lens.md). With real-is-positive object and image distances $u$ and $v$ and focal length $f$, it is $1/f=1/u+1/v$. A virtual image has $v<0$ under this convention.
