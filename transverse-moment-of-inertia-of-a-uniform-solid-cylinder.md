# Transverse moment of inertia of a uniform solid cylinder

↑ **Parent:** [Moment of inertia of a uniform solid cylinder](moment-of-inertia-of-a-uniform-solid-cylinder.md)

For a uniform solid cylinder of length $l$, radius $r$ and [mass](mass.md) $m$, a thin circular slice at axial coordinate $z$ has transverse [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) $(z^2+r^2/4)dm$ about a diametral axis through the cylinder's midpoint. Integrating from $-l/2$ to $l/2$ gives $I_{\perp,G}=ml^2/12+mr^2/4$. The [parallel axis theorem](parallel-axis-theorem.md) gives $I_{\perp,\rm end}=ml^2/3+mr^2/4$ about a parallel axis through an end-face centre. The transverse and symmetry-axis [moments of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) coincide exactly when $l=\sqrt3r$.
