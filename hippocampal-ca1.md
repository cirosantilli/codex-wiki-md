# Hippocampal CA1

↑ **Parent:** [Hippocampus](hippocampus.md)

A [hippocampal](hippocampus.md) subfield receiving both [CA3](hippocampal-ca3.md) and entorhinal input and contributing to [hippocampal](hippocampus.md) output. [Heteroassociative memory](heteroassociative-memory.md) models assign learned transformations to its converging inputs and outputs, but a computational role requires more evidence than anatomical resemblance.
