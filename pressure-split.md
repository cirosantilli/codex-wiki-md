# Pressure

↑ **Parent:** [Thermodynamics](thermodynamics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pressure)

Pressure measures the compressive [normal force](normal-force.md) per unit [surface area](surface-area.md). For a uniformly loaded surface of [surface area](surface-area.md) $A$, the magnitude of the perpendicular [force](force-split.md) is $F_\perp=PA$; locally, $P=dF_\perp/dA$. Its [SI](international-system-of-units.md) unit is the pascal, $1\,\mathrm{Pa}=1\,\mathrm{N\,m^{-2}}$. A [force](force-split.md) parallel to the surface contributes shear [stress](stress.md), rather than [pressure](pressure-split.md). In a [gas](gas.md), the steady macroscopic [force](force-split.md) comes from many microscopic transfers of [momentum](momentum-split.md); [ideal gas pressure from wall collisions](ideal-gas-pressure-from-wall-collisions.md) makes this connection explicit. [OpenStax University Physics Volume 2, section 2.2: microscopic origin of pressure](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/2-2-pressure-temperature-and-rms-speed)

For a computed example, a uniform [pressure](pressure-split.md) of $100\,\mathrm{kPa}$ on a piston of [surface area](surface-area.md) $10\,\mathrm{cm^2}=10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^2}$ produces a perpendicular [force](force-split.md) of $100\,\mathrm N$. If the opposite face is exposed to $80\,\mathrm{kPa}$, the net [force](force-split.md) due to the two [pressures](pressure-split.md) is $(100-80)\times10^3\times10^{-3}=20\,\mathrm N$, directed toward the lower-[pressure](pressure-split.md) side. The [pressure](pressure-split.md) on either face and their difference answer different questions; [gauge pressure](gauge-pressure.md) records a difference from a specified reference, often [atmospheric pressure](atmospheric-pressure.md).

A scalar [pressure](pressure-split.md) describes a [gas](gas.md) in local [thermodynamic equilibrium](thermodynamic-equilibrium.md) with [isotropic](isotropy.md) random particle [velocities](velocity.md): every wall orientation has the same normal [force](force-split.md) per unit [surface area](surface-area.md). Without this [isotropy](isotropy.md), normal [stresses](stress.md) can depend on direction and a [pressure tensor](pressure-tensor.md) is needed. Thus random motion alone is insufficient; a preferred direction matters.

In [thermodynamics](thermodynamics-split.md), [pressure](pressure-split.md) and [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) are [thermodynamic conjugate variables](conjugate-variables-thermodynamics.md). For a [simple compressible system](simple-compressible-system.md), reversible expansion delivers [work](work.md) $\delta W=P\,dV$, and at fixed [entropy](entropy-split.md) $S$ and particle number $N$,

$$
P=-\left(\frac{\partial U}{\partial V}\right)_{S,N},
$$

where $U$ is [internal energy](internal-energy-split.md). A large [pressure](pressure-split.md) therefore means both a large wall [force](force-split.md) per unit [surface area](surface-area.md) and a large amount of expansion [work](work.md) per small increase in [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md).
