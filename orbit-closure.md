# Orbit closure

↑ **Parent:** [Topological dynamics](topological-dynamics.md)

The forward [orbit closure](orbit-closure.md) of $x$ under a [continuous map](continuous-map.md) $T$ is the [closure](closure-topology.md) of its forward [orbit](orbit-dynamical-system.md). It is a closed forward-invariant set. If $T$ is invertible, one may instead consider the two-sided [orbit closure](orbit-closure.md) using $n\in\mathbb Z$; the convention should be specified. For a [minimal point](minimal-point.md) of an invertible compact system the two closures agree.
