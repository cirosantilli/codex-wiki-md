# Oscillatory integral phase

↑ **Parent:** [Stationary phase method](stationary-phase-method.md)

In an integral $\int a(q)e^{i\lambda\beta(q)}dq$, the real-valued function $\beta$ is the phase. Its stationary points satisfy $\nabla\beta=0$; its [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) determines the local quadratic oscillation and the signature factor in [stationary phase method](stationary-phase-method.md). Degenerate stationary points and boundaries require separate analysis.
