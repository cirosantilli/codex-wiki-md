<h1 id="thevenin-s-theorem">Thévenin's theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical engineering](electrical-engineering-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thévenin's_theorem)

For a linear resistive [electrical circuit](electrical-circuit.md) with a well-defined two-terminal response, Thévenin's theorem replaces its terminal behavior by an ideal source of [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) $V_{\mathrm{oc}}$ in series with [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) $R_{\mathrm{th}}$. Here $I$ is the [electric current](electric-current.md) delivered to the external load. With independent sources only, calculate $R_{\mathrm{th}}$ by setting ideal voltage sources to zero (wires) and ideal current sources to zero (open connections). With dependent sources, retain them and determine the terminal ratio using a test source. The [Wheatstone bridge detector loading](wheatstone-bridge-detector-loading.md) calculation applies the independent-source version.
