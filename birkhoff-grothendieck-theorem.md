<h1 id="birkhoff-grothendieck-theorem">Birkhoff–Grothendieck theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vector bundle](vector-bundle.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birkhoff–Grothendieck_theorem)

Every [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) on the [projective line](projective-line.md) over a [field](field.md) splits as a [direct sum](direct-sum.md) of [line bundles](line-bundle.md). Choose the largest integer $a$ with $H^0(E(-a))\ne0$. A corresponding map $\mathcal O(a)\to E$ is saturated, since saturation otherwise increases its degree. Inductively split its quotient as $\bigoplus_i\mathcal O(b_i)$. Twisting the exact sequence by $-a-1$ and using $H^1(\mathcal O(-1))=0$ gives $b_i\leq a$. Hence all extension groups $H^1(\mathcal O(a-b_i))$ vanish, and the sequence splits. The multiset of degrees is determined by the dimensions of all twisted global-section spaces.
