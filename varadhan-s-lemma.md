<h1 id="varadhan-s-lemma">Varadhan's lemma</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Large deviation principle](large-deviation-principle.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Varadhan's_lemma)

If random elements satisfy a [large deviation principle](large-deviation-principle.md) at speed $a_n\to\infty$ with [rate function](rate-function.md) I, then a bounded continuous real function f satisfies the displayed exponential-integral identity. For the upper bound, partition its bounded range into finitely many closed intervals of width epsilon and apply the closed-set upper bound to their preimages. The largest exponential term controls the finite sum. For the lower bound, restrict to an open neighborhood where f is within epsilon of its value at a chosen point and use the open-set lower bound. Let epsilon tend to zero and optimize over the point. Boundedness avoids a separate exponential tail assumption.
