# Membrane time constant

↑ **Parent:** [Temporal summation](temporal-summation.md)

The [membrane time constant](membrane-time-constant.md) of a passive uniform compartment is its [membrane capacitance](membrane-capacitance.md) $C$ divided by its total fixed [electrical conductance](electrical-conductance.md) $G$. After input removal, voltage deviation from the relevant equilibrium undergoes [exponential decay](exponential-decay.md); in one [membrane time constant](membrane-time-constant.md) it falls to $e^{-1}$ of its initial value. For leak alone, $G=g_L$; while fixed [synaptic conductances](synaptic-conductance.md) are open, $G=g_L+\sum_jg_j$. Thus [shunting inhibition](shunting-inhibition.md) can both reduce voltage responses and shorten the integration window. This is a local passive timescale: a branching [dendrite](dendrite.md) can have several decay modes, and a time-varying [synaptic conductance](synaptic-conductance.md) need not admit one constant $\tau_m$.
