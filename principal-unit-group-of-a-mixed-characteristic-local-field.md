# Principal-unit group of a mixed-characteristic local field

↑ **Parent:** [Multiplicative group of a non-Archimedean local field](multiplicative-group-of-a-non-archimedean-local-field.md)

If $K/\mathbb Q_p$ is finite of degree $d$, then as a topological [abelian group](abelian-group.md)

$$
1+\mathfrak m_K\cong\mu_{p^a}(K)\times\mathbb Z_p^d,
$$

where $\mu_{p^a}(K)$ is the finite group of all [roots of unity](root-of-unity.md) in $K$ whose orders are [prime powers](prime-power.md) dividing a power of $p$.
