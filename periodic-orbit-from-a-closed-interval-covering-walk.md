# Periodic orbit from a closed interval-covering walk

↑ **Parent:** [Directed covering graph of an interval map](directed-covering-graph-of-an-interval-map.md)

Every closed walk

$$
J_0\longrightarrow J_1\longrightarrow\cdots\longrightarrow J_{n-1}\longrightarrow J_0
$$

in a [directed covering graph of an interval map](directed-covering-graph-of-an-interval-map.md) has a point $x\in J_0$ with $F^j(x)\in J_j$ and $F^n(x)=x$. This follows by successively pulling $J_0$ back through the covering relations and applying the [fixed-point property of a closed interval](fixed-point-property-of-a-closed-interval.md). If the itinerary has least period $n$ and avoids shared endpoints, $x$ lies on an $n$-cycle.
