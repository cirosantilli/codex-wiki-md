<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Expand the [anticommutators](../../../../anticommutator.md) using the relations in part (a). The terms with two creation or two [annihilation operators](../../../../annihilation-operator.md) vanish, leaving

$$
\boxed{\{\gamma_i^A,\gamma_j^A\}=2\delta_{ij},\qquad
\{\gamma_i^B,\gamma_j^B\}=2\delta_{ij},\qquad
\{\gamma_i^A,\gamma_j^B\}=0}.
$$

Each [Majorana fermion operator](../../../../majorana-fermion-operator.md) is [Hermitian](../../../../hermitian-operator.md) and squares to the identity. In particular, $(\gamma_i^A)^\dagger\gamma_i^A=1$, so this expression cannot distinguish an empty mode from an occupied one. A single Majorana operator is its own adjoint and does not supply distinct creation and [annihilation operators](../../../../annihilation-operator.md) for such an occupation.

An occupation can instead be assigned after [pairing Majorana operators into a fermionic mode](../../../../pairing-majorana-operators-into-a-fermionic-mode.md). For two distinct Majorana operators $\gamma_a,\gamma_b$,

$$
f=\frac{\gamma_a+i\gamma_b}{2},\qquad
\boxed{n_f=f^\dagger f=\frac{1+i\gamma_a\gamma_b}{2}}.
$$

This is a nontrivial [projection operator](../../../../projection-linear-algebra.md) with [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md) zero and one. Thus **occupation belongs to a chosen pair of Majorana operators, not to an individual Majorana operator**.
