<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For independent bosonic modes, the [canonical commutation relation](../../../../canonical-commutation-relation.md) is

$$
[b_i,b_j^\dagger]=\delta_{ij},\qquad [b_i,b_j]=[b_i^\dagger,b_j^\dagger]=0.
$$

For independent fermionic modes, the [canonical anticommutation relations](../../../../canonical-anticommutation-relations.md) are

$$
\{c_i,c_j^\dagger\}=\delta_{ij},\qquad \{c_i,c_j\}=\{c_i^\dagger,c_j^\dagger\}=0,
$$

where $[A,B]=AB-BA$ and $\{A,B\}=AB+BA$. The [creation operator](../../../../creation-operator.md) is the adjoint of the [annihilation operator](../../../../annihilation-operator.md). In either case the mode's [number operator](../../../../number-operator.md) is

$$
\boxed{n_i=a_i^\dagger a_i},
$$

with $a_i=b_i$ or $c_i$ as appropriate. It measures the mode's [occupation number](../../../../occupation-number.md). Its possible [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md) are

$$
\boxed{n_i=0,1,2,\ldots\ \text{for bosons};\qquad n_i=0,1\ \text{for fermions}}.
$$

For [fermions](../../../../fermion.md), $c_i^2=0$ implies $n_i^2=c_i^\dagger(1-c_i^\dagger c_i)c_i=n_i$, so the [number operator](../../../../number-operator.md) is a projection. This expresses the exclusion of double occupation of one fermionic mode. The total particle number is $N=\sum_i n_i$.
