<h1 id="1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The occupation formula from part (b) gives

$$
n_2=f_2^\dagger f_2=\frac{1+i\gamma_1^B\gamma_2^A}{2}.
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{H=t(2f_2^\dagger f_2-1)=t(2n_2-1)}.
$$

The independent [occupation numbers](../../../../occupation-number.md) $n_1,n_2$ are each zero or one. The [Hamiltonian](../../../../hamiltonian.md) is independent of $n_1$, so its four states have

$$
\boxed{\begin{array}{c|c|c}
n_2&E&\text{degeneracy}\\\hline
0&-t&2\\
1&+t&2
\end{array}}.
$$

Each energy admits $n_1=0$ or $n_1=1$; these states differ by occupancy of the zero-energy mode. The ground energy is $-|t|$ for either sign of $t$. These degeneracies refer to the full two-mode [fermionic Fock space](../../../../fermionic-fock-space.md). If one separately restricts to a fixed global [fermion parity](../../../../fermion-parity.md), only one state at each energy remains in that parity sector.
