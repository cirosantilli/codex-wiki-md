<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [scattering length](../../../../scattering-length-from-a-partial-wave-s-matrix.md) is $a_0=-\lim_{k\to0}\tan\delta_0/k$. Since $\sin(kR)\sim kR$ and $\cos(kR)\sim1$, the exact [phase shift](../../../../phase-shift.md) gives, away from the pole,

$$
\boxed{a_0=R\frac{g}{1+g},\qquad g=\frac{2mV_0R}{\hbar^2}}.
$$

Thus plot $a_0/R$ against $g$, so that both axes are [dimensionless](../../../../dimensionless-quantity.md).

<a id="2/d/image-delta-shell-scattering-length-versus-dimensionless-coupling-with-the-bound-state-threshold-at-g-minus-1"></a>
![](../../../../past-exam-of-the-theoretical-physics-ii-course-of-the-university-of-cambridge/2018-delta-shell-scattering-length.png)

**[Figure 1](#2/d/image-delta-shell-scattering-length-versus-dimensionless-coupling-with-the-bound-state-threshold-at-g-minus-1). Delta-shell scattering length versus dimensionless coupling, with the bound-state threshold at g = −1**.

For repulsion, $g>0$, the curve rises from zero to the horizontal asymptote $a_0/R=1$, staying between zero and one. For weak attraction, $-1<g<0$, the [scattering length](../../../../scattering-length-from-a-partial-wave-s-matrix.md) is negative and tends to $-\infty$ as $g\to-1^+$. For stronger attraction, $g<-1$, it is positive and greater than $R$, tending to $+\infty$ as $g\to-1^-$ and to $R$ as $g\to-\infty$. Its only zero is $g=0$.

The pole at $g=-1$ is the zero-energy threshold of an S-wave [bound state](../../../../bound-state.md). To see this directly, use $u\propto\sinh(\kappa r)$ inside and $u\propto e^{-\kappa r}$ outside for energy $-\hbar^2\kappa^2/(2m)$. The same derivative jump gives

$$
g=-\kappa R[1+\coth(\kappa R)]
=-\frac{2\kappa R}{1-e^{-2\kappa R}}.
$$

This approaches $-1$ as $\kappa\downarrow0$ and admits a positive $\kappa$ for $g<-1$. At the threshold the [scattering length](../../../../scattering-length-from-a-partial-wave-s-matrix.md) diverges; this is a [zero-energy scattering resonance](../../../../zero-energy-scattering-resonance.md), not a divergence of the finite-energy cross-section beyond its [partial-wave unitarity](../../../../partial-wave-unitarity.md) bound. These are the main features of the [scattering length of a delta-shell potential](../../../../scattering-length-of-a-delta-shell-potential.md).
