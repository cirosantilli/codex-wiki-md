<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a short-range potential, an incident [plane wave](../../../../plane-wave.md) of unit amplitude and wave vector $k\mathbf e_z$ has the large-distance [scattering wavefunction](../../../../scattering-wavefunction.md)

$$
\boxed{\psi(\mathbf r)=e^{ikz}+f(\theta,\phi)\frac{e^{ikr}}r+O(r^{-2})\qquad(r\to\infty)}.
$$

The coefficient $f$ is the [scattering amplitude](../../../../scattering-amplitude.md). For a central potential it depends only on the scattering angle $\theta$. The incident [probability current](../../../../probability-current.md) is $\hbar k/m$, while the scattered radial current is $(\hbar k/m)|f|^2/r^2$ to leading order. Their ratio gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{d\sigma}{d\Omega}=|f(\theta,\phi)|^2,\qquad
\sigma_{\mathrm{tot}}=\int |f(\theta,\phi)|^2\,d\Omega}.
$$

These are the [differential scattering cross-section](../../../../differential-scattering-cross-section.md) and total [cross section](../../../../cross-section-physics.md) for elastic scattering with the same incident and outgoing wave number.

The expression is _asymptotic_: it specifies the outgoing wave's leading $1/r$ term, not its finite-distance radial corrections or its form inside the interaction region. In particular, for angle-dependent $f$, the spherical term by itself has an angular-Laplacian contribution of order $r^{-3}$ to the free wave equation, which is cancelled by subleading terms in the exact solution.
