↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

With $\beta=(k_BT)^{-1}$, the normalized canonical [density operator](../../../../../density-matrix-split.md) is

$$
\boxed{\rho=\frac{e^{-\beta H}}Z,\qquad Z=\operatorname{Tr}e^{-\beta H}}.
$$

Its [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are the Boltzmann probabilities and its trace is one.
