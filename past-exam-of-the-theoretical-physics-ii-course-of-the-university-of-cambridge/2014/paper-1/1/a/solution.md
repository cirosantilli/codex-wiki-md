<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

An initially occupied, isolated instantaneous [energy eigenstate](../../../../../energy-eigenstate.md) follows its changing eigenspace, acquiring a [dynamical phase](../../../../../dynamical-phase.md) and generally a [Berry phase](../../../../../berry-phase.md). For smooth nondegenerate evolution, a useful local smallness condition is

$$
\boxed{\frac{\hbar|\langle m(t)|\dot H(t)|n(t)\rangle|}{|E_n(t)-E_m(t)|^2}\ll1\quad(m\ne n)}.
$$

A nonvanishing [spectral gap](../../../../../spectral-gap.md) and suitably smooth, slow driving throughout the evolution are needed; the local inequality alone is not a universal long-time error bound. Gap closure invalidates the simple nondegenerate approximation. A separated degenerate eigenspace may mix internally even when transitions out of it are suppressed.
