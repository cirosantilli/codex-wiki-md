<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Set $\vartheta_\alpha(t)=\hbar^{-1}\int^t E_\alpha(s)ds$. Differentiating the expansion produces $\dot a_\beta|\varphi_\beta\rangle+a_\beta|\dot\varphi_\beta\rangle-iE_\beta a_\beta|\varphi_\beta\rangle/\hbar$ for each term. The [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) term cancels $H|\Psi\rangle$ in the [Schrödinger equation](../../../../../schrodinger-equation.md). Projecting the remainder with $\langle\varphi_\alpha|$ and using [orthonormality](../../../../../orthonormal-set.md) yields

$$
\boxed{\dot a_\alpha=-\sum_\beta\langle\varphi_\alpha|\dot\varphi_\beta\rangle a_\beta e^{i(\vartheta_\alpha-\vartheta_\beta)}}.
$$

The diagonal term is purely imaginary and supplies the [geometric phase](../../../../../geometric-phase.md). Off-diagonal [instantaneous eigenstate couplings](../../../../../instantaneous-eigenstate-coupling.md) transfer population; differentiating the [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) equation gives $\langle m|\dot n\rangle=\langle m|\dot H|n\rangle/(E_n-E_m)$ when the levels are distinct.
