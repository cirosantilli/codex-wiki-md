<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

At large $r$, $|\mathbf r-\mathbf r_j|=r-\hat{\mathbf r}\cdot\mathbf r_j+O(r^{-1})$. Retain the displacement in the phase, but replace the denominator by $r$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{f(\hat{\mathbf r})=\frac1k\left[c_1e^{-ik\hat{\mathbf r}\cdot\mathbf r_1}+c_2e^{-ik\hat{\mathbf r}\cdot\mathbf r_2}\right]}.
$$

The [differential scattering cross-section](../../../../../differential-scattering-cross-section.md) is $|f|^2$ for [elastic scattering](../../../../../elastic-scattering.md) with this unit incident plane-wave normalization. [interference](../../../../../interference-wave-propagation.md) depends on outgoing direction and centre separation as well as the incoming phases already contained in $c_j$.
