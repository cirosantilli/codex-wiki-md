<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For an interaction of finite range $b$, higher [partial waves](../../../../../partial-wave.md) are suppressed at $kb\ll1$ by the [centrifugal barrier](../../../../../centrifugal-barrier.md), away from exceptional higher-wave resonances. Outside the potential, the zero-energy radial [S-wave scattering](../../../../../s-wave-quantum-scattering.md) equation is $u''=0$, where $\psi=u/r$. Its solution is proportional to $r-a$, so normalization of the constant term gives

$$
\boxed{\psi\simeq1-\frac ar}.
$$

The [scattering length](../../../../../scattering-length-from-a-partial-wave-s-matrix.md) $a$ is the extrapolated zero of $u(r)$. At nonzero small $k$, this form applies in the overlap region $b\ll r\ll k^{-1}$; it is not the far-field limit at fixed $k$. Equivalently, $k\cot\delta_0\to-1/a$ and $f_0\to-a$.
