<h1 id="5/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Put $C=\cos(\chi/2)$ and $S=\sin(\chi/2)$. Commutativity of the two [creation operators](../../../../../creation-operator.md) and the [binomial theorem](../../../../../binomial-theorem.md) give

$$
|\chi\rangle=\sum_{j=0}^N\sqrt{\binom Nj}\,C^{N-j}S^j|N-j,j\rangle.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{P(N_1=j)=\binom Nj(1-p)^{N-j}p^j,\quad p=S^2},
\qquad
\boxed{\overline N_1=Np,\quad\overline N_0=N(1-p)}.
$$

The state is a [fixed-number two-mode condensate](../../../../../fixed-number-two-mode-condensate.md) with binomial mode occupations; it is not a product of two independent Poisson-distributed Glauber [coherent states](../../../../../coherent-state.md).
