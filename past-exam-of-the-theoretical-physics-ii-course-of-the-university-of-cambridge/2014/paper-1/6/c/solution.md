<h1 id="6/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Set $P=a^\dagger b^\dagger$ and $Q=ab$. Independent [bosonic](../../../../../boson.md) [canonical commutation relations](../../../../../canonical-commutation-relation.md) give $[Q,P]=N_a+N_b+1=2K_3$, while $[K_3,P]=P$ and $[K_3,Q]=-Q$. Substituting $K_1=(P+Q)/2$ and $K_2=(P-Q)/(2i)$ yields

$$
[K_1,K_2]=-iK_3,\qquad[K_2,K_3]=iK_1,\qquad[K_3,K_1]=iK_2.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{[iK_1,iK_2]=iK_3,\quad[iK_2,iK_3]=-iK_1,\quad[iK_3,iK_1]=-iK_2},
$$

which matches the [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) [Lie algebra](../../../../../lie-algebra-split.md). This [boson-pair realization of SU(1,1)](../../../../../boson-pair-realization-of-su-1-1.md) differs from the number-conserving [Schwinger boson representation](../../../../../schwinger-boson-representation.md) of $SU(2)$.
