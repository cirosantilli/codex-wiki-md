<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The angular [action](../../../../../action.md) is $S=\int I\dot\theta^2ds/2$. In each winding sector, write $\theta=\theta_p+\xi$ with $\xi$ zero at both endpoints. The cross term vanishes and the Gaussian fluctuation [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md) is the free-particle prefactor with [mass](../../../../../mass.md) replaced by $I$. Thus

$$
\boxed{K_\theta(\Delta,T)=\sqrt{\frac{I}{2\pi i\hbar T}}\sum_{p\in\mathbb Z}\exp\left[\frac{iI(\Delta+2\pi p)^2}{2\hbar T}\right]}.
$$

Equivalently, multiply the line kernel for displacement $R(\Delta+2\pi p)$ by $R$ to change its measure from arc length to angle. The quadratic [action](../../../../../action.md) makes the classical-path sum exact, rather than merely a [stationary phase](../../../../../stationary-phase-method.md) approximation. Real-time sums are understood with the usual negative imaginary part of $T$ and its boundary limit.
