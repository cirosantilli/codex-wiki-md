<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [quantum propagator](../../../../../quantum-propagator.md) is the position-space kernel of the [time-evolution operator](../../../../../time-evolution-operator.md): $\Psi(x,t)=\int K(x,t|x',t')\Psi(x',t')dx'$. For a time-independent [Quantum Hamiltonian](../../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md), $U=e^{-iH(t-t')/\hbar}$. Inserting a complete orthonormal [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) eigenbasis gives

$$
\boxed{K(x,t|x',t')=\sum_\alpha\varphi_\alpha(x)\varphi_\alpha^*(x')e^{-iE_\alpha(t-t')/\hbar}}.
$$

A [continuous spectrum](../../../../../continuous-spectrum.md) requires the corresponding [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md). At equal times, completeness makes this kernel a [Dirac delta function](../../../../../dirac-delta-function.md).
