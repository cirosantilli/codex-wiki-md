<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Lift the circle to its real-valued angular covering coordinate. The free classical equation is $\ddot\theta=0$. For each [winding number](../../../../../winding-number.md) $p\in\mathbb Z$, the endpoint lift is $\theta+2\pi p$, and

$$
\boxed{\theta_p(s)=\theta'+\frac{\Delta+2\pi p}{T}(s-t'),\qquad
S_p=\frac{I(\Delta+2\pi p)^2}{2T}}.
$$

Positive and negative windings are distinct [classical paths](../../../../../classical-path.md) with the same physical endpoints. Reducing the angle modulo $2\pi$ returns their trajectories on the ring.
