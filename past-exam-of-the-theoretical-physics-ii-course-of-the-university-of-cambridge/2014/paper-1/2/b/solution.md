<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $I=mR^2$, $T=t-t'>0$, and $\Delta=\theta-\theta'$. Periodic angular [wave functions](../../../../../wave-function-split.md) have modes $e^{il\theta}/\sqrt{2\pi}$ with integer $l$ and [energies](../../../../../energy-split.md) $\hbar^2l^2/(2I)$. Normalizing the kernel with respect to $d\theta'$ gives

$$
\boxed{K_\theta(\Delta,T)=\frac1{2\pi}\sum_{l\in\mathbb Z}e^{il\Delta-i\hbar l^2T/(2I)}}.
$$

If the [integration](../../../../../integral-split.md) measure is arc length $R\,d\theta'$, the corresponding kernel is $K_\theta/R$. This measure choice accounts for the otherwise ambiguous [radius](../../../../../radius.md) prefactor.
