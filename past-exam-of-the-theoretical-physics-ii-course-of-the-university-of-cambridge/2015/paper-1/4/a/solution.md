<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For the canonical [quantum Gibbs state](../../../../../quantum-gibbs-state.md),

$$
\boxed{\rho=\frac{e^{-\beta H}}Z,\qquad
Z=\operatorname{Tr}e^{-\beta H},\qquad
\beta=\frac1{k_BT}.}
$$

If $H|n\rangle=E_n|n\rangle$, its spectral form is $\rho=Z^{-1}\sum_ne^{-\beta E_n}|n\rangle\langle n|$, with a sum over any degeneracy and an integral where a continuous spectrum needs one. It is Hermitian, positive and has unit [trace](../../../../../matrix-trace.md), and it commutes with $H$. The formula presumes a well-defined finite [partition function](../../../../../canonical-partition-function.md). At zero [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) a nondegenerate stable system approaches its ground-state projector; a degenerate ground space requires specifying the ensemble or preparation.
