<h1 id="5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For identical [fermions](../../../../../fermion.md), $(b_j^\dagger)^2=0$ and $b_2^\dagger b_1^\dagger=-b_1^\dagger b_2^\dagger$. Therefore

$$
a_1^\dagger a_2^\dagger|0\rangle
=\frac12(b_1^\dagger+b_2^\dagger)(b_1^\dagger-b_2^\dagger)|0\rangle
=-b_1^\dagger b_2^\dagger|0\rangle.
$$

The minus sign is a [global phase](../../../../../global-phase.md) for this output. **The only occupancy is $(n_1,n_2)=(1,1)$, with [probability](../../../../../probability.md) one.** Double occupancy of either specified single-particle mode is excluded; no extra internal states are present in this model.
