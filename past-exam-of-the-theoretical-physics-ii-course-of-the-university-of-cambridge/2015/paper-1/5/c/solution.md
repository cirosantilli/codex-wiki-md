<h1 id="5/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Bosonic [creation operators](../../../../../creation-operator.md) commute, so the cross terms cancel:

$$
a_1^\dagger a_2^\dagger|0\rangle
=\frac12[(b_1^\dagger)^2-(b_2^\dagger)^2]|0\rangle
=\frac{|2,0\rangle-|0,2\rangle}{\sqrt2}.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{P(2,0)=P(0,2)=\frac12,\qquad P(1,1)=0.}
$$

The square root of two in $(b^\dagger)^2|0\rangle=\sqrt2|2\rangle$ is essential. Destructive [interference](../../../../../interference-wave-propagation.md) removes the one-particle-per-output amplitude; the particles do not independently choose ports.

For [photons](../../../../../photon.md) this is the ideal [Hong-Ou-Mandel effect](../../../../../hong-ou-mandel-effect.md). Its laboratory test combines one [photon](../../../../../photon.md) from each input at a balanced [beam splitter](../../../../../beam-splitter.md) and scans relative arrival delay while measuring output coincidences. Spatial, [polarization](../../../../../polarization-waves.md) and spectral indistinguishability must be controlled; distinguishable [photons](../../../../../photon.md) restore a nonzero coincidence baseline. The original experiment used down-converted [photons](../../../../../photon.md), a translated [beam splitter](../../../../../beam-splitter.md) and coincidence counting, with an independently estimated accidental-coincidence background.[Physical Review Letters 59 (1987), pp. 2044–2046, figure 1 and equations (2), (9): setup, balanced splitting cancellation, delay scan and accidental subtraction](../../../../../measurement-of-subpicosecond-time-intervals-between-two-photons-by-interference-by-c-k-hong-and-others.md)
