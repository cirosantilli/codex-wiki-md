<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Choose orthonormal, flux-normalized incoming and outgoing channels and include all channels of this lossless scattering problem. Conservation of [probability](../../../../../probability.md) requires $\|\beta\|^2=\|\alpha\|^2$ for every input [vector](../../../../../vector.md). With $\beta=S\alpha$,

$$
\alpha^\dagger(S^\dagger S-I)\alpha=0\quad\text{for every }\alpha,
\qquad\boxed{S^\dagger S=I.}
$$

For a square channel [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) this also gives $SS^\dagger=I$. A submatrix obtained by omitting loss channels need not be unitary, but that is not the stated two-channel model.

The given real [Hadamard matrix](../../../../../hadamard-matrix.md) is symmetric, unitary and its own inverse. Hence

$$
a_1^\dagger=\frac{b_1^\dagger+b_2^\dagger}{\sqrt2},\qquad
a_2^\dagger=\frac{b_1^\dagger-b_2^\dagger}{\sqrt2}.
$$

This same transformation preserves the [canonical commutation relations](../../../../../canonical-commutation-relation.md) for [bosons](../../../../../boson.md) and anticommutation relations for [fermions](../../../../../fermion.md). To extract [probabilities](../../../../../probability.md) one must use normalized [number states](../../../../../number-state.md), including their factorial factors, even when the supplied input expression is unnormalized.
