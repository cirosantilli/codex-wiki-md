<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The defining condition is

$$
\boxed{SL(2,\mathbb C)=\{S\in M_2(\mathbb C):\det S=1\}.}
$$

The entries are complex and the [matrices](../../../../../matrix.md) are invertible; products and inverses retain [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) one. There is no [unitarity](../../../../../unitary-operator.md) condition $S^\dagger S=I$ in general. Four complex entries subject to one complex [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) equation give three complex, or six real, local group parameters.
