<h1 id="6/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [spinor](../../../../../spinor.md) transformation is $\chi'=S\chi$, with $S=\operatorname{diag}(e^{\kappa/2},e^{-\kappa/2})$. Its squared norm becomes

$$
\mathcal R=e^\kappa\cos^2(\theta/2)+e^{-\kappa}\sin^2(\theta/2)
=\cosh\kappa+\sinh\kappa\cos\theta.
$$

Dividing by $\sqrt{\mathcal R}$ isolates the new direction without discarding the physical scale factor in $X'$. The lower-to-upper component ratio gives

$$
\boxed{\tan\frac{\theta'}2=e^{-\kappa}\tan\frac\theta2,\qquad
\phi'=\phi\pmod{2\pi}.}
$$

Equivalently,

$$
\boxed{\cos\theta'=\frac{\cos\theta+\tanh\kappa}
{1+\tanh\kappa\cos\theta},\qquad
\sin\theta'=\frac{\sin\theta}{\mathcal R}.}
$$

This is [relativistic aberration](../../../../../relativistic-aberration.md) for the active positive-$z$ boost specified in the question. At the poles, [azimuth](../../../../../azimuth.md) is undefined rather than a measurable transformed variable. Under the literal [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) convention from (c), the actual [azimuth](../../../../../azimuth.md) $-\phi$ is likewise unchanged. The [time](../../../../../time-in-physics.md) coordinate is multiplied by $\mathcal R$; normalizing a [spinor](../../../../../spinor.md) for its direction must not erase that boost-induced change of null-vector scale.
