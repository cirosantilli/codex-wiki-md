<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [Pauli matrix multiplication law](../../../../../pauli-matrix-multiplication-law.md) gives

$$
(\mathbf a\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma)(\mathbf b\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma)
=(\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b)I+i(\mathbf a\times\mathbf b)\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma.
$$

For a [unit vector](../../../../../unit-vector.md) $\hat{\mathbf n}$, it follows that $(\hat{\mathbf n}\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma)^2=I$. In the exponential series the even powers therefore multiply $I$ and the odd powers multiply $\hat{\mathbf n}\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma$. Summing them separately,

$$
\boxed{e^{-i\theta\hat{\mathbf n}\cdot\mathbf S}
=I\cos(\theta/2)-i\hat{\mathbf n}\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma\sin(\theta/2).}
$$

Equivalently, the [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) of $\hat{\mathbf n}\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma$ are $\pm1$, so its two [spectral projectors](../../../../../spectral-projector.md) acquire phases $e^{\mp i\theta/2}$. The half-angle belongs to a [spinor](../../../../../spinor.md) [rotation](../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md), while the associated physical [spin](../../../../../spin.md) [vector](../../../../../vector.md) rotates through $\theta$.
