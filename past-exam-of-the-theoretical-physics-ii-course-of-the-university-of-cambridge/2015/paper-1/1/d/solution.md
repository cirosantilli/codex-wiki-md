For general [spin](../../../../../spin.md) $S$, use [dimensionless](../../../../../dimensionless-quantity.md) generators $S^j$ obeying $[S^i,S^j]=i\epsilon_{ijk}S^k$. The two exponentials represent the same group elements as for [spin](../../../../../spin.md) $1/2$, now in the $(2S+1)$-dimensional [representation](../../../../../group-representation.md) of $SU(2)$. A [representation](../../../../../group-representation.md) preserves products, so

$$
\boxed{e^{-i\Omega TS^z}e^{-i\Phi S^x}
=e^{-i\vartheta\hat{\mathbf n}\cdot\mathbf S}}
$$

with exactly the same axis and angle found in (c); after $N$ periods the angle is $N\vartheta$. One must not substitute higher-spin [matrices](../../../../../matrix.md) into the two-term Pauli exponential in (b), which used a property special to [spin](../../../../../spin.md) $1/2$.

The dimension and [action](../../../../../action.md) on [spin](../../../../../spin.md) states change, but the [spin-independent axis-angle composition](../../../../../spin-independent-axis-angle-composition.md) does not. In particular,

$$
\boxed{e^{-i2\pi\hat{\mathbf n}\cdot\mathbf S}=(-1)^{2S}I.}
$$

Integer-spin states return with positive sign and half-integer-spin states with negative sign. [spin](../../../../../spin.md) expectation [vectors](../../../../../vector.md) transform as spatial [vectors](../../../../../vector.md) in either case, while a general higher-spin state also carries higher multipole information.
