<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Use the [Dirac delta function](../../../../../dirac-delta-function.md) to set $p'=p+\alpha T$, and write $X=x-x'$. The remaining momentum-dependent [quantum phase](../../../../../quantum-phase.md) is

$$
pX-\alpha Tx'-\frac{T}{2m}\left(p+\frac{\alpha T}{2}\right)^2
-\frac{\alpha^2T^3}{24m}.
$$

Shifting the integration variable by $\alpha T/2$ reduces the integral to the free [Gaussian integral](../../../../../gaussian-integral.md). Therefore

$$
\boxed{K_\alpha=\theta(T)\sqrt{\frac{m}{2\pi i\hbar T}}
\exp\left\{\frac i\hbar\left[
\frac{mX^2}{2T}-\frac{\alpha T(x+x')}{2}-\frac{\alpha^2T^3}{24m}
\right]\right\}}.
$$

The [force](../../../../../force-split.md) is $-\alpha$, not $+\alpha$. Both the linear endpoint term and the cubic-time term disappear in the zero-force limit. The prefactor remains the free one.
