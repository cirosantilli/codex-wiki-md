<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For the [momentum-space propagator in a uniform force](../../../../../momentum-space-propagator-in-a-uniform-force.md), the stated Fourier convention makes $x$ act on [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) [wavefunctions](../../../../../wave-function-split.md) as $i\hbar\partial_p$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\left[i\hbar\partial_t-\frac{p^2}{2m}-i\alpha\hbar\partial_p\right]K_p
=i\hbar\delta(T)\delta(p-p')}.
$$

For $T>0$, the characteristic has $p(s)=p'-\alpha(s-t')$. Along it the amplitude acquires [quantum phase](../../../../../quantum-phase.md) $-\int_{t'}^t p(s)^2ds/(2m\hbar)$. [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) is translated without a Jacobian, giving

$$
\boxed{K_p=\theta(T)\delta(p-p'+\alpha T)
\exp\left[\frac{i(p^3-p'^3)}{6\alpha m\hbar}\right]}.
$$

The cubic expression is evaluated on the delta-function support. There it equals $-i[p'^2T-\alpha p'T^2+\alpha^2T^3/3]/(2m\hbar)$, making both its sign and its continuous $\alpha\to0$ limit clear. The jump at $T=0$ is the identity [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) kernel.
