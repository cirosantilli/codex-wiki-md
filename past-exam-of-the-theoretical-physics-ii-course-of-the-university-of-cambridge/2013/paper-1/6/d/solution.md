<h1 id="6/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The negative-kinetic-energy branch is interpreted as an [antiparticle](../../../../../antiparticle.md) channel. In the overlap $mc^2<E<V_0-mc^2$, particles can escape to the left and oppositely charged [antiparticles](../../../../../antiparticle.md) to the right. This interval exists only when $V_0>2mc^2$. The external electrostatic source supplies the [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) for pair creation; the reflected particle [flux](../../../../../flux.md) can exceed the incident one because the outgoing state need not contain only the incident particle.

**The conserved quantity is signed charge current, not a positive one-particle [probability](../../../../../probability.md) in both regions.** Negative $\mathcal T$ denotes rightward transport of opposite charge, so it is consistent with $\mathcal R>1$ and $\mathcal R+\mathcal T=1$. A quantized charged field with particle and [antiparticle](../../../../../antiparticle.md) creation operators gives the appropriate interpretation of this [Klein paradox](../../../../../klein-paradox.md). This bosonic result must not be replaced by a fermionic reflection law or interpreted as a failure of charge conservation.[Discussion after equation 4.155: pair creation and the oppositely signed transmitted current](../../../../../klein-paradox-for-spin-0-particles-by-douglas-m-gingrich.md)
