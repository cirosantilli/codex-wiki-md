<h1 id="5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The bosonic [commutator](../../../../../commutator.md) is important in $S_+S_-=\hbar^2n_\uparrow(n_\downarrow+1)$ and $S_-S_+=\hbar^2n_\downarrow(n_\uparrow+1)$. Therefore

$$
S^2=S_z^2+\frac{S_+S_-+S_-S_+}{2}
=\frac{\hbar^2}{4}\left[(n_\uparrow+n_\downarrow)^2+2(n_\uparrow+n_\downarrow)\right].
$$

On the $N$-particle sector,

$$
\boxed{S^2=\hbar^2\frac N2\left(\frac N2+1\right),\qquad s=\frac N2}.
$$

For example $N=0,1,2$ gives [spin](../../../../../spin.md) $0,\tfrac12,1$. The total [number operator](../../../../../number-operator.md) can be retained in the same identity before restricting to a sector.
