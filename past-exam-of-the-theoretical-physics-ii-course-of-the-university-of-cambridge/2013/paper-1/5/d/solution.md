<h1 id="5/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The defining matrix conditions are

$$
\boxed{SU(2)=\{U\in M_2(\mathbb C):U^\dagger U=I,\ \det U=1\}}.
$$

The group operation is matrix multiplication. Equivalently $U=a_0I-i\mathbf a\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma$ with real $a_0,\mathbf a$ and $a_0^2+|\mathbf a|^2=1$, so it has three real parameters and is a compact connected simply connected [Lie group](../../../../../lie-group.md). Its Hermitian [spin](../../../../../spin.md) generators obey the [commutator](../../../../../commutator.md) from part (a); the corresponding Lie-algebra matrices themselves are traceless and anti-Hermitian.
