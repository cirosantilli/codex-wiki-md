<h1 id="5/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Total symmetry makes a component depend only on its number $n_\uparrow$ of up indices. The possible values are $0,1,\ldots,N$, giving

$$
\boxed{\dim\operatorname{Sym}^N(\mathbb C^2)=N+1=2s+1}.
$$

A normalized basis is $|n_\uparrow,N-n_\uparrow\rangle$, with $S_z$ [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $\hbar(n_\uparrow-N/2)$. It is precisely one spin-$N/2$ multiplet. These are independent complex amplitudes before normalization and removal of a common [quantum phase](../../../../../quantum-phase.md), rather than $N+1$ independent real physical parameters.
