<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) [commutators](../../../../../commutator.md) used in the frame transformation are representation-independent. Therefore $H_R=\Delta S_z+\Omega_RS_x$ still holds. Rotating its axis onto $z$ gives $2s+1$ [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) and exact laboratory solutions

$$
\boxed{E_\mu=\hbar\mu\Omega,\quad
|\Psi_\mu(t)\rangle=e^{-i\mu\Omega t}R(t)|\mu\rangle_R,\quad
\mu=-s,-s+1,\ldots,s}.
$$

Now $R(T)=(-1)^{2s}I$, so $e^{i\Phi_\mu}=(-1)^{2s}e^{-i\mu\Omega T}$. In the adiabatic limit the [dynamical phase](../../../../../dynamical-phase.md) is $-\mu BT$ and

$$
\boxed{\gamma_\mu=-\mu\mathcal A\pmod{2\pi}}.
$$

Indeed $2\pi s+2\pi\mu\cos\theta$ and $-2\pi\mu(1-\cos\theta)$ differ by $2\pi(s+\mu)$, an integer multiple of $2\pi$. Integer [spins](../../../../../spin.md) return with a positive rotation sign and half-integer [spins](../../../../../spin.md) with a negative one; the [geometric phase](../../../../../geometric-phase.md) need not vanish for either.
