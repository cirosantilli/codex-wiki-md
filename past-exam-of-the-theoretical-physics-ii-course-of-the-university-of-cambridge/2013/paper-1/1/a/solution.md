<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $\Delta=\Omega_0-\omega$ and $R(t)=e^{-i\omega tS_z/\hbar}$. Differentiating $|\Psi\rangle=R|\Psi_R\rangle$ gives the rotating-frame [Hamiltonian operator](../../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) $R^\dagger HR-i\hbar R^\dagger\dot R$. The [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) [commutator](../../../../../commutator.md) yields $R^\dagger S_\pm R=e^{\pm i\omega t}S_\pm$, while $-i\hbar R^\dagger\dot R=-\omega S_z$. Thus the [rotating-frame Rabi Hamiltonian](../../../../../rotating-frame-rabi-hamiltonian.md) is

$$
\boxed{H_R=\Delta S_z+\Omega_RS_x
=\frac\hbar2\begin{pmatrix}\Delta&\Omega_R\\\Omega_R&-\Delta\end{pmatrix}},\qquad
i\hbar\partial_t|\Psi_R\rangle=H_R|\Psi_R\rangle.
$$

This transformation is exact for the circular drive; no [rotating-wave approximation](../../../../../rotating-wave-approximation.md) has been used.
