<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Choose the even-channel [scattering phase shift](../../../../scattering-phase-shift.md) convention $\varphi_{k,e}(x)=\pi^{-1/2}\cos(k|x|+\delta_e)$ for $k>0$. The [delta potential](../../../../delta-potential.md) derivative jump gives

$$
-2k\sin\delta_e=-2\kappa\cos\delta_e,
$$

so, choosing the branch that tends to zero as $k\to\infty$,

$$
\boxed{\delta_e(k)=\arctan(\kappa/k),\qquad \delta_o(k)=0.}
$$

An odd [scattering eigenfunction](../../../../scattering-wavefunction.md) is $\varphi_{k,o}(x)=\pi^{-1/2}\sin kx$: it vanishes at the interaction and its derivative is continuous, so it acquires no [scattering phase shift](../../../../scattering-phase-shift.md). These parity channels, with the [bound state](../../../../bound-state.md), give the continuum part of the [spectral resolution of a quantum propagator](../../../../spectral-resolution-of-a-quantum-propagator.md).
