<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Away from the [delta potential](../../../../delta-potential.md), the decaying [bound state](../../../../bound-state.md) obeys $E_0=-\hbar^2\kappa^2/(2m)$. Integrating the [Schrödinger equation](../../../../schrodinger-equation.md) across the attractive interaction gives

$$
\varphi'(0^+)-\varphi'(0^-)=-\frac{2mg}{\hbar^2}\varphi(0).
$$

For an even exponential, the left side is $-2\kappa\varphi(0)$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\kappa=\frac{mg}{\hbar^2},\qquad E_0=-\frac{mg^2}{2\hbar^2}.}
$$

The normalized [bound state in a delta potential](../../../../bound-state-in-a-delta-potential.md) is $\varphi_0(x)=\sqrt\kappa e^{-\kappa|x|}$, since $2\kappa\int_0^\infty e^{-2\kappa x}dx=1$. The single positive solution for $\kappa$ gives one [bound state](../../../../bound-state.md); an odd decaying wave cannot be both continuous and nonzero with this interaction.
