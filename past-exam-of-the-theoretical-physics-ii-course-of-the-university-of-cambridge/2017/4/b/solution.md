<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The incident [plane wave](../../../../plane-wave.md) carries [probability current](../../../../probability-current.md) $j_{\rm in}=\hbar k/m$. For the separated outgoing wave from the previous part,

$$
j_r^{\rm sc}=\frac\hbar m\operatorname{Im}(\psi_{\rm sc}^*\partial_r\psi_{\rm sc})
\sim\frac\hbar{mr}|f(\theta)|^2.
$$

The rate through a distant arc is $rj_r^{\rm sc}\,d\theta$. Dividing by incident flux defines the differential two-dimensional [scattering cross-section](../../../../scattering-cross-section.md),

$$
\boxed{\frac{d\lambda}{d\theta}=\frac{|f(\theta)|^2}{k},\qquad
\lambda=\frac1k\int_0^{2\pi}|f(\theta)|^2\,d\theta.}
$$

Because $f$ is [dimensionless](../../../../dimensionless-quantity.md), $\lambda$ has units of length, as appropriate for scattering in a plane.
