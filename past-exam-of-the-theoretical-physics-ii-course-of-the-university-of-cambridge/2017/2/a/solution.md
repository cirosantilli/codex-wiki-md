<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Complete the square in the [potential energy](../../../../potential-energy.md). With $x_F=F/(M\omega^2)$,

$$
H=\frac{p^2}{2M}+\frac{M\omega^2}{2}(x-x_F)^2-\frac{F^2}{2M\omega^2}.
$$

The [quantum harmonic oscillator](../../../../quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md) is translated without changing its level spacing. If $\varphi_n(x)$ is its normalized unforced [energy eigenstate](../../../../energy-eigenstate.md), then

$$
\boxed{\psi_n^{(F)}(x)=\varphi_n(x-x_F),\qquad
E_n^{(F)}=\hbar\omega\left(n+\frac12\right)-\frac{F^2}{2M\omega^2},\quad n=0,1,2,\ldots.}
$$

Equivalently, $|n;F\rangle=e^{-ix_Fp/\hbar}|n\rangle$. The [ground state](../../../../ground-state.md) [position](../../../../position.md) is centered at $x_F$, and its [position uncertainty](../../../../position-uncertainty.md) remains $\sqrt{\hbar/(2M\omega)}$.
