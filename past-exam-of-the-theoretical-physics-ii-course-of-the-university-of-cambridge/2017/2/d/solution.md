<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Specify the magnetic coupling before fixing its sign: let the [magnetic moment](../../../../magnetic-dipole-moment.md) be $\boldsymbol\mu=\gamma\mathbf S$, where $\gamma$ is a signed gyromagnetic ratio. The [Zeeman effect](../../../../zeeman-splitting.md) gives $H_Z=-\gamma\mathbf B\cdot\mathbf S$. For [spin one-half](../../../../spin-one-half.md), $\mathbf S=\hbar\boldsymbol\sigma/2$, so with $B=|\mathbf B|$ and $u=\beta\gamma\hbar B/2$,

$$
\boxed{\rho=\frac{e^{-\beta H_Z}}{\operatorname{Tr}e^{-\beta H_Z}}
=\frac12\left[I+\tanh u\,\hat{\mathbf B}\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma\right],\qquad
Z=2\cosh u.}
$$

This is the [quantum Gibbs state](../../../../quantum-gibbs-state.md). Its [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md) are $(1\pm\tanh u)/2$, so its [purity](../../../../purity-of-a-density-operator.md) is $(1+\tanh^2u)/2$. For nonzero level splitting it approaches a [pure state](../../../../pure-state.md) as $|u|\to\infty$: positive [inverse temperature](../../../../inverse-temperature.md) selects the lower [energy](../../../../energy-split.md), while negative [inverse temperature](../../../../inverse-temperature.md) selects the upper one. It is a [maximally mixed state](../../../../maximally-mixed-state.md) at $u=0$, including infinite [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) or zero magnetic splitting. At zero field the Gibbs state remains $I/2$; lowering [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) alone does not select a unique [pure state](../../../../pure-state.md) from that degeneracy.

[Negative temperature](../../../../negative-temperature.md) is meaningful for an internally equilibrated spin sector with a bounded [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) spectrum and [population inversion](../../../../population-inversion.md). It must be sufficiently isolated from unbounded motional degrees of freedom: the oscillator's unbounded spectrum, for instance, makes its partition sum diverge at negative [inverse temperature](../../../../inverse-temperature.md). The two-level spin partition sum itself stays finite for either sign.
