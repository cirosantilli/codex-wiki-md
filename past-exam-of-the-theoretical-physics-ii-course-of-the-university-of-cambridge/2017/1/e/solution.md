<h1 id="1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

For distinct positive integers $p,q$, use normalized box modes $\varphi_1=\sqrt{2/L}\sin(p\pi x/L)$ and $\varphi_2=\sqrt{2/L}\sin(q\pi x/L)$. Their [contact interaction](../../../../contact-interaction-quantum-mechanics.md) overlaps obey

$$
I_{1111}=I_{2222}=\frac{3}{2L},\qquad I_{1122}=\frac1L.
$$

Unlike traveling ring modes, these standing modes also allow pair conversion. For the first two box modes, or any distinct pair without a third-harmonic resonance, the [contact interaction between two box modes](../../../../contact-interaction-between-two-box-modes.md) is

$$
\boxed{H_{\rm int}=\frac{3g}{4L}\left[n_1(n_1-1)+n_2(n_2-1)\right]
+\frac{2g}{L}n_1n_2+\frac{g}{2L}\left(a_1^{\dagger2}a_2^2+a_2^{\dagger2}a_1^2\right).}
$$

The last two terms change an individual mode [occupation number](../../../../occupation-number.md) by two while conserving total [particle number](../../../../particle-number.md). Thus the [Hamiltonian operator](../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) is not diagonal in the [Fock state](../../../../fock-state.md) basis, and minimizing diagonal entries alone does not find its [ground state](../../../../ground-state.md). For example, at $N=2$ with no dispersion, $\left(|2,0\rangle-|0,2\rangle\right)/\sqrt2$ has the lowest [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) $g/(2L)$, below either unmixed endpoint's $3g/(2L)$.

The wording permits modes beyond the first two. For completeness, the identity $\sin^3u=(3\sin u-\sin3u)/4$ gives

$$
I_{1112}=-\frac{\delta_{q,3p}}{2L},\qquad I_{1222}=-\frac{\delta_{p,3q}}{2L}.
$$

When $q=3p$, add $-g(a_1^{\dagger2}a_1a_2+a_1^\dagger a_2^\dagger a_1^2)/(2L)$; when $p=3q$, add $-g(a_1^\dagger a_2^\dagger a_2^2+a_2^{\dagger2}a_1a_2)/(2L)$. These extra terms change an individual [occupation number](../../../../occupation-number.md) by one and require diagonalizing the corresponding fixed-$N$ matrix as well.
