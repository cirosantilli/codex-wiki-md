<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Add the one-particle [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) term $H_0=\epsilon_1n_1+\epsilon_2n_2$. It is diagonal in the same [Fock state](../../../../fock-state.md) basis as the [contact interaction between two ring modes](../../../../contact-interaction-between-two-ring-modes.md). For $N=2$, the ordered basis $(|2,0\rangle,|1,1\rangle,|0,2\rangle)$ gives

$$
\boxed{H=\begin{pmatrix}
2\epsilon_1+g/L&0&0\\
0&\epsilon_1+\epsilon_2+2g/L&0\\
0&0&2\epsilon_2+g/L
\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

For $g>0$, the middle [eigenvalue](../../../../eigenvalue.md) exceeds the mean of the two endpoint [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md) by $g/L$, so it cannot be the smallest. The [ground state](../../../../ground-state.md) is therefore **$|2,0\rangle$ if $\epsilon_1<\epsilon_2$, and $|0,2\rangle$ if $\epsilon_2<\epsilon_1$**, with

$$
\boxed{E_{\min}=2\min(\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2)+g/L.}
$$

If $\epsilon_1=\epsilon_2$, every normalized [linear combination](../../../../linear-combination.md) of the two endpoint [Fock states](../../../../fock-state.md) is a [ground state](../../../../ground-state.md). The same endpoint argument works for general fixed $N$, since the interaction contribution is a concave quadratic function of the mode [occupation number](../../../../occupation-number.md).
