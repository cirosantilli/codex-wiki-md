<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In [second quantization](../../../../second-quantization.md), expand the annihilating [quantized field](../../../../quantized-field.md) in a complete [orthonormal basis](../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) of [one-particle states](../../../../one-particle-state.md), $\hat\psi(x)=\sum_\alpha\varphi_\alpha(x)a_\alpha$. At equal times, the [canonical commutation relation](../../../../canonical-commutation-relation.md) for [bosons](../../../../boson.md) and the [canonical anticommutation relations](../../../../canonical-anticommutation-relations.md) for [fermions](../../../../fermion.md) are, respectively,

$$
[\hat\psi(x),\hat\psi^\dagger(x')]=\delta(x-x'),\qquad
[\hat\psi(x),\hat\psi(x')]=[\hat\psi^\dagger(x),\hat\psi^\dagger(x')]=0,
$$

and

$$
\{\hat\psi(x),\hat\psi^\dagger(x')\}=\delta(x-x'),\qquad
\{\hat\psi(x),\hat\psi(x')\}=\{\hat\psi^\dagger(x),\hat\psi^\dagger(x')\}=0.
$$

Here $\delta$ is a [Dirac delta distribution](../../../../dirac-delta-function.md). Multiplying by the basis [wave functions](../../../../wave-function-split.md) and integrating gives $[a_\alpha,a_\beta^\dagger]=\delta_{\alpha\beta}$ for [bosons](../../../../boson.md), or $\{a_\alpha,a_\beta^\dagger\}=\delta_{\alpha\beta}$ for [fermions](../../../../fermion.md); the two-annihilator and two-creator brackets vanish in the corresponding convention.

The mode [number operator](../../../../number-operator.md) and total [particle number](../../../../particle-number.md) operator are

$$
\boxed{n_\alpha=a_\alpha^\dagger a_\alpha,\qquad \hat N=\sum_\alpha n_\alpha.}
$$

The [bosonic occupation numbers](../../../../bosonic-occupation-number.md) are $0,1,2,\ldots$. For [fermions](../../../../fermion.md), $a_\alpha^2=0$ and the [canonical anticommutation relations](../../../../canonical-anticommutation-relations.md) imply $n_\alpha^2=n_\alpha$, so the [fermionic occupation numbers](../../../../fermionic-occupation-number.md) are only $0$ and $1$, as required by the [Pauli exclusion principle](../../../../pauli-exclusion-principle.md).

Keeping only two modes later is a projection: the projected field bracket is $\sum_{j=1}^2\varphi_j(x)\varphi_j^*(x')$, rather than the full [Dirac delta distribution](../../../../dirac-delta-function.md).
