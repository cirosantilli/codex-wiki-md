<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

At fixed [particle number](../../../../particle-number.md) $N$, write $n_1=n$ and $n_2=N-n$. The [contact interaction between two ring modes](../../../../contact-interaction-between-two-ring-modes.md) has [energy eigenvalues](../../../../energy-eigenvalue-split.md)

$$
E(n)=\frac{g}{2L}\left[N(N-1)+2n(N-n)\right],\qquad n=0,1,\ldots,N.
$$

For $g>0$, the nonnegative product $n(N-n)$ is minimized at the endpoints. Consequently

$$
\boxed{E_{\min}=\frac{gN(N-1)}{2L},\qquad
|\psi_{\min}\rangle=c_1|N,0\rangle+c_2|0,N\rangle,\quad |c_1|^2+|c_2|^2=1.}
$$

For $N\geq1$ this is a two-dimensional degenerate [ground state](../../../../ground-state.md) subspace. $N=0$ instead has the unique [Fock vacuum](../../../../fock-vacuum.md). In this projected model, putting all particles into one traveling mode avoids the extra exchange contribution paid when both distinct modes are occupied.

For the alternative coefficients, put $\alpha=\beta=A<0$ and $\gamma=0$. Then $E(n)=A[N(N-1)-2n(N-n)]$ is again minimized at $n=0,N$, with $E_{\min}=AN(N-1)$. Evenness of $N$ is unnecessary for this result; the balanced [Fock state](../../../../fock-state.md) maximizes this attractive-interaction [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) instead of minimizing it.
