<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Take periodic orbital [wavefunctions](../../../../../wave-function-split.md) on the ring, and write $H_0=-(\hbar^2/2I)\partial_\theta^2$. Between kicks the evolution over one period is

$$
U_T=e^{-iH_0T/\hbar}
=\exp\!\left(\frac{i\hbar T}{2I}\partial_\theta^2\right).
$$

Integrating through an infinitesimal kick gives

$$
U_\eta=\exp\!\left[-\frac i\hbar(-\hbar\eta\cos\theta)\right]
=e^{i\eta\cos\theta}.
$$

The free-motion contribution during the vanishing kick duration disappears in the ideal impulsive limit. The sign of the [phase](../../../../../phase-waves.md) follows from the negative kick potential.

Starting just after a kick, the [particle](../../../../../particle-physics.md) evolves freely first and is kicked at the end of the interval. Operators act right to left, so **one step is $U_\eta U_T$**, and

$$
\boxed{U(0^+\to NT^+)=(U_\eta U_T)^N.}
$$

The one-period product is the [Floquet operator](../../../../../floquet-operator.md) of this [quantum kicked rotor](../../../../../quantum-kicked-rotor.md). There is no additional kick at the initial endpoint $0^+$. Changing the stroboscopic convention to just before each kick would change the displayed order; it is not appropriate for these endpoints.
