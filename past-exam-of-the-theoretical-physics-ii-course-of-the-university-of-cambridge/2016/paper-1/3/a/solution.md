<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For one [spin](../../../../../spin.md) channel, let $X_s=a_s^\dagger b_s$ and $Y_s=X_s^\dagger$. The number-operator [commutators](../../../../../commutator.md) give

$$
[\Delta,X_s]=2X_s,\qquad[\Delta,Y_s]=-2Y_s.
$$

Moving a [boson](../../../../../boson.md) from $B$ to $A$ raises the number difference by two. On a number-difference [eigenstate](../../../../../eigenstate.md) $|d\rangle$, $X_s\mathcal E(\Delta)|d\rangle=\mathcal E(d)X_s|d\rangle$, while $\mathcal E(\Delta-2)X_s|d\rangle=\mathcal E(d)X_s|d\rangle$. Thus

$$
X_s\mathcal E(\Delta)=\mathcal E(\Delta-2)X_s,\qquad
Y_s\mathcal E(\Delta)=\mathcal E(\Delta+2)Y_s.
$$

Subtracting the reversed products yields

$$
\boxed{[X_s,\mathcal E(\Delta)]
=[\mathcal E(\Delta-2)-\mathcal E(\Delta)]X_s,}
$$



$$
\boxed{[Y_s,\mathcal E(\Delta)]
=[\mathcal E(\Delta+2)-\mathcal E(\Delta)]Y_s.}
$$

This [number-difference shift identity](../../../../../number-difference-shift-identity.md) fixes the ordering. The placement of the coefficient to the left matters: it acts on the state after the hop. This spectral argument applies to any function defined on the accessible number-difference [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md), without requiring a power-series expansion.
