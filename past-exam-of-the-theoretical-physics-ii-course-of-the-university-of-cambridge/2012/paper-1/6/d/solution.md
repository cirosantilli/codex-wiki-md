<h1 id="6/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Write $\kappa=\sqrt{2mV_0}/\hbar$ and $q=\kappa a$. At low [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md), the $S$ wave normally dominates. Under the additional stated near-resonance assumption $ka\ll|\delta_0|$, neglect $ka$ inside the cotangent of the matching condition and set $k'\simeq\kappa$:

$$
k\cot\delta_0\simeq\kappa\cot q,\qquad
\sin^2\delta_0\simeq
\frac{k^2}{k^2+\kappa^2\cot^2q}.
$$

Substitution in $\sigma_0=4\pi\sin^2\delta_0/k^2$ gives

$$
\boxed{\sigma_{\rm res}\simeq
\frac{2\pi\hbar^2}{m}\,
\frac1{E+V_0\cot^2(\sqrt{2mV_0}\,a/\hbar)}.}
$$

This is a [near-resonance square-well approximation](../../../../../near-resonance-square-well-approximation.md), not a uniform formula for every well radius. A [phase shift](../../../../../phase-shift.md) is defined modulo $\pi$; its representative must be chosen consistently when applying the comparison with $ka$.
