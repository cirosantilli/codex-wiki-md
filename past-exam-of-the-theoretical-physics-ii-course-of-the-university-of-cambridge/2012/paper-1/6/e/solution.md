<h1 id="6/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

For small fixed positive $E$, the stated approximate curve has maxima at

$$
\boxed{\kappa a=\left(n+\frac12\right)\pi,\qquad
\sigma_{\rm res,max}=\frac{2\pi\hbar^2}{mE}=\frac{4\pi}{k^2},
\quad n=0,1,\ldots.}
$$

Its formal minima are zero at $\kappa a=n\pi$. The peak height is the $S$-wave [unitarity](../../../../../unitary-operator.md) limit, and the peaks narrow as $E/V_0$ decreases.

<a id="6/e/image-square-well-resonance-limits"></a>
![](../../../../../past-exam-of-the-theoretical-physics-ii-course-of-the-university-of-cambridge/2012/paper-1-square-well-resonances.png)

**[Figure 1](#6/e/image-square-well-resonance-limits). Square-well resonance limits**.

To identify the bound-state threshold, let the bound-state [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) be $-\varepsilon$ with $\varepsilon>0$. The reduced radial wave is proportional to $\sin(Kr)$ inside and $e^{-\chi r}$ outside, with $K=\sqrt{2m(V_0-\varepsilon)}/\hbar$ and $\chi=\sqrt{2m\varepsilon}/\hbar$. Matching gives $K\cot(Ka)=-\chi$. As a new [bound state](../../../../../bound-state.md) emerges at $\varepsilon\downarrow0$,

$$
\boxed{\kappa\cot(\kappa a)=0
\ \Longrightarrow\
\kappa a=\left(n+\frac12\right)\pi.}
$$

Thus the limiting peak locations coincide with the appearance of successive **$S$-wave [bound states](../../../../../bound-state.md)**. At the exact threshold the zero-energy radial solution is not [square-integrable](../../../../../square-integrable-function.md); a [normalizable](../../../../../normalizable-wavefunction.md) [bound state](../../../../../bound-state.md) appears just beyond it. Finite-energy maxima shift slightly from the zero-energy [positions](../../../../../position.md).

The formal minima require care because the assumption used in part d fails there. Retaining $ka$ in the exact matching condition gives the [spherical square-well scattering length](../../../../../spherical-square-well-scattering-length.md)

$$
a_s=a-\frac{\tan(\kappa a)}{\kappa},\qquad
\sigma_0\longrightarrow4\pi a_s^2
$$

away from a threshold. At $\kappa a=n\pi$, $a_s=a$, so the true low-energy [S-wave scattering](../../../../../s-wave-quantum-scattering.md) contribution is $4\pi a^2$, not zero. Genuine leading-order zeros of that contribution instead satisfy $\tan q=q$, for example $q\simeq4.493409$ and $7.725252$. The original sketch compares the exact finite-energy $S$ wave with the requested approximation, making this nonuniformity visible. Dominance by the $S$ wave must be checked separately near a higher-partial-wave threshold. The scattering-length divergence and its bound-state interpretation are also derived in institutional lecture notes.[2017, §6.2.6, printed pp. 219–222: square-well matching, scattering length and threshold bound states](../../../../../topics-in-quantum-mechanics-by-david-tong.md)
