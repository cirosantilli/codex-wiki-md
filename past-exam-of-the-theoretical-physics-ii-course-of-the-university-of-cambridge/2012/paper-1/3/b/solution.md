<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The normalized initial [Gaussian wave packet](../../../../../gaussian-wave-packet.md) is real and even, so the integrands for $\langle x'\rangle_0$ and $\langle p'\rangle_0$ are odd. Its second [position](../../../../../position.md) moment is the elementary [Gaussian integral](../../../../../gaussian-integral.md) $\langle x'^2\rangle_0=1/(2\alpha)$. Since $\partial_x\Psi=-\alpha x\Psi$ and $\partial_x^2\Psi=(\alpha^2x^2-\alpha)\Psi$,

$$
\langle p'^2\rangle_0
=\hbar^2\left(\alpha-\alpha^2\langle x'^2\rangle_0\right)
=\frac{\hbar^2\alpha}{2}.
$$

Moreover $\langle x'p'\rangle_0=i\hbar\alpha\langle x'^2\rangle_0=i\hbar/2$ and $\langle p'x'\rangle_0=-i\hbar/2$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\langle x'\rangle_0=\langle p'\rangle_0
=\langle x'p'+p'x'\rangle_0=0,\qquad
\langle x'^2\rangle_0=\frac1{2\alpha},\quad
\langle p'^2\rangle_0=\frac{\hbar^2\alpha}{2}.}
$$
