<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Introduce the [dimensionless](../../../../../dimensionless-quantity.md) width $s=\hbar\alpha/(m\omega)>0$. Multiplying the [variances](../../../../../variance-split.md) gives

$$
\boxed{(\Delta x')_t^2(\Delta p')_t^2
=\frac{\hbar^2}{4}\left[
1+\frac14(s-s^{-1})^2\sin^2(2\omega t)\right]
\ge\frac{\hbar^2}{4}.}
$$

The minimum holds **at all times precisely when $\alpha=m\omega/\hbar$**, the oscillator ground-state width. For any other $\alpha$, it is still attained at the isolated quarter-period times $t=n\pi/(2\omega)$, including zero. At a fixed generic time, minimizing over $\alpha$ again selects $s=1$.

The excess product has a [covariance](../../../../../covariance.md) interpretation:

$$
C_{xp}(t)=\frac12\langle x'p'+p'x'\rangle_t
=\frac{\hbar}{4}(s-s^{-1})\sin2\omega t,\qquad
(\Delta x')^2(\Delta p')^2-C_{xp}^2=\frac{\hbar^2}{4}.
$$

Thus the [breathing Gaussian state in a harmonic oscillator](../../../../../breathing-gaussian-state-in-a-harmonic-oscillator.md) saturates the [Robertson-Schrödinger uncertainty relation](../../../../../robertson-schrodinger-uncertainty-relation.md) throughout, even when its position-momentum product exceeds the lower bound in the [Heisenberg uncertainty principle](../../../../../heisenberg-uncertainty-relation.md).
