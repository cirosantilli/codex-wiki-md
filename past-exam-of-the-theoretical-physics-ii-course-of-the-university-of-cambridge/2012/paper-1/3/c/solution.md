<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Square the operator solutions, retaining the symmetrized cross term rather than treating $x'(0)$ and $p'(0)$ as commuting variables. The initial symmetrized expectation vanishes, so

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
\langle x'^2\rangle_t
&=\frac{\cos^2\omega t}{2\alpha}
+\frac{\hbar^2\alpha}{2m^2\omega^2}\sin^2\omega t,\\
\langle p'^2\rangle_t
&=\frac{\hbar^2\alpha}{2}\cos^2\omega t
+\frac{m^2\omega^2}{2\alpha}\sin^2\omega t.
\end{aligned}}
$$

The means remain zero. These are also the [variances](../../../../../variance-split.md). Unless $\alpha=m\omega/\hbar$, the [Gaussian](../../../../../normal-distribution.md) state breathes: its [position](../../../../../position.md) and [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) widths exchange periodically while the mean [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) remains constant.
