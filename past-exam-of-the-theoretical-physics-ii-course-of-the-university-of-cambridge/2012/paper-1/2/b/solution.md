<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Each term is a [Hermitian](../../../../../hermitian-operator.md) rank-one [projection](../../../../../projection-linear-algebra.md) and $p_j$ is real, so $\rho^\dagger=\rho$. Using the rank-one [trace](../../../../../matrix-trace.md),

$$
\operatorname{Tr}\rho=\sum_jp_j\langle\psi_j|\psi_j\rangle
=\sum_jp_j=\boxed{1}.
$$

The remaining admissibility condition follows from $\langle v|\rho|v\rangle=\sum_jp_j|\langle\psi_j|v\rangle|^2\ge0$: **$\rho$ is [positive semidefinite](../../../../../positive-semidefinite-matrix.md)**.
