<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The given real symmetric [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) is [Hermitian](../../../../../hermitian-operator.md) and has [trace](../../../../../matrix-trace.md) one. A [spectral decomposition](../../../../../spectral-decomposition.md) supplies its positivity without relying on diagonal entries alone:

$$
\rho=\frac34|v_1\rangle\langle v_1|
+\frac14|v_2\rangle\langle v_2|,\qquad
v_1=\frac{(2,1,1)^{\mathsf T}}{\sqrt6},\quad
v_2=\frac{(0,1,-1)^{\mathsf T}}{\sqrt2}.
$$

The remaining normalized [eigenvector](../../../../../eigenvector.md) is $(-1,1,1)^{\mathsf T}/\sqrt3$ with [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) zero. Consequently the spectrum is **$\{3/4,1/4,0\}$**, every [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) is nonnegative, and the [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) is permissible. Its [purity](../../../../../purity-of-a-density-operator.md) is $(3/4)^2+(1/4)^2=\boxed{5/8}$, so it is **mixed**.
