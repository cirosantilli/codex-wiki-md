<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A normalized [pure state](../../../../../pure-state.md) has $\rho=|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|$, and therefore

$$
\boxed{\rho^2=\rho,\qquad\operatorname{Tr}\rho^2=1.}
$$

Conversely, diagonalize a permissible [density operator](../../../../../density-matrix-split.md). Its [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) obey $0\le\lambda_j\le1$ and $\sum_j\lambda_j=1$, so

$$
1-\operatorname{Tr}\rho^2
=\sum_j\lambda_j(1-\lambda_j)\ge0.
$$

Equality requires every [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) to be zero or one; exactly one is one. Thus the [idempotent matrix](../../../../../idempotent-matrix.md) condition and [purity](../../../../../purity-of-a-density-operator.md) one both characterize a [pure state](../../../../../pure-state.md). A [mixed state](../../../../../mixed-state.md) has at least two positive [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md), **$\rho^2\ne\rho$ and $\operatorname{Tr}\rho^2<1$**. In [dimension](../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) $d$ its [purity](../../../../../purity-of-a-density-operator.md) is at least $1/d$.
