An ensemble of normalized [quantum states](../../../../../quantum-state.md) with [probabilities](../../../../../probability.md) $p_j$ is represented by the [density operator](../../../../../density-matrix-split.md)

$$
\boxed{\rho=\sum_jp_j|\psi_j\rangle\langle\psi_j|,
\qquad p_j\ge0,\quad\sum_jp_j=1.}
$$

It determines every [observable](../../../../../observable.md) [expectation value](../../../../../expectation-value.md) through $\langle A\rangle=\operatorname{Tr}(\rho A)$. The ensemble decomposition need not be unique; the operator specifies the measurement statistics.
