<h1 id="2/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The original [Von Neumann entropy](../../../../../von-neumann-entropy-split.md) depends on its [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md), with $0\log0$ defined by continuity:

$$
\boxed{\frac{S_{\rm before}}{k_B}
=-\frac34\log\frac34-\frac14\log\frac14
=2\log2-\frac34\log3
\simeq0.562335.}
$$

After [rank-one dephasing](../../../../../rank-one-dephasing.md), the [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are the retained diagonal populations $1/2,1/4,1/4$, giving

$$
\boxed{\frac{S_{\rm after}}{k_B}=\frac32\log2\simeq1.039721,\qquad
\frac{\Delta S}{k_B}=\frac34\log3-\frac12\log2\simeq0.477386>0.}
$$

[dephasing](../../../../../dephasing-channel.md) removes coherences in the specified [basis](../../../../../basis.md), increasing the system's mixedness while preserving those measurement [probabilities](../../../../../probability.md). It cannot be a closed-system [unitary transformation](../../../../../unitary-operator.md), since the [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) change. The subsystem [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md) increase does not by itself assert an increase in the [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md) of a closed system plus its environment.
