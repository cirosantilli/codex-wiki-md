<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For $b>0$, the [exponential variational trial for a square-root half-line well](../../../../../exponential-variational-trial-for-a-square-root-half-line-well.md) satisfies the wall [boundary condition](../../../../../boundary-condition.md) and has norm $\int_0^\infty x^2e^{-2bx}dx=1/(4b^3)$. [integration by parts](../../../../../integration-by-parts.md) has no boundary term and gives

$$
\langle T\rangle
=\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}
\frac{\int_0^\infty(1-bx)^2e^{-2bx}dx}{1/(4b^3)}
=\frac{\hbar^2b^2}{2m}.
$$

The supplied [gamma integral](../../../../../gamma-integral.md) gives

$$
\langle V\rangle=\frac{15a}{16}\sqrt{\frac\pi2}\,b^{-1/2},\qquad
E(b)=\frac{\hbar^2b^2}{2m}
+\frac{15a}{16}\sqrt{\frac\pi2}\,b^{-1/2}.
$$

Setting $E'(b)=0$ yields the unique minimum,

$$
\boxed{b_*=\left(\frac{ma}{\hbar^2}\frac{15}{32}
\sqrt{\frac\pi2}\right)^{2/5}.}
$$

At the minimum $\langle V\rangle=4\langle T\rangle$, so the variational ground-state estimate is

$$
\boxed{E_{\rm var}=\frac{5\hbar^2b_*^2}{2m}
=1.876493\left(\frac{\hbar^2a^4}{2m}\right)^{1/5}.}
$$

The [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) diverges as $b\to0$ or $b\to\infty$, confirming that the stationary point minimizes the trial [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md).
