<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Write $E_*=(\hbar^2a^4/(2m))^{1/5}$. Then **$E_0^{\rm WKB}=1.811694E_*$ and $E_{\rm var}=1.876493E_*$**. The [variational method](../../../../../variational-method.md) guarantees $E_0\le E_{\rm var}$, since the trial satisfies the wall condition and has finite [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md). WKB gives no comparable upper-bound guarantee and is least controlled for the lowest state. The two estimates differ by about $3.6\%$ of the WKB value; their closeness is encouraging but does not prove that the exact [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) lies between them.
