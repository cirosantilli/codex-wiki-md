<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [half-line square-root potential well](../../../../../half-line-square-root-potential-well.md) is confining for $a>0$, which is assumed here. The hard wall imposes $\psi(0)=0$ and the smooth [turning point](../../../../../turning-point.md) is $x_{\rm t}=(E/a)^2$. With $u=a\sqrt x/E$, the WKB [action](../../../../../action.md) becomes

$$
\int_0^{x_{\rm t}}k(x)\,dx
=\frac{2\sqrt{2m}E^{5/2}}{\hbar a^2}
\int_0^1u(1-u)^{1/2}\,du
=\frac{8\sqrt{2m}E^{5/2}}{15\hbar a^2}.
$$

The [hard-wall and soft-turning-point WKB quantization](../../../../../hard-wall-and-soft-turning-point-wkb-quantization.md) condition therefore gives

$$
\boxed{E_n^{\rm WKB}
=\left[\frac{15\pi\hbar a^2}{8\sqrt{2m}}
\left(n+\frac34\right)\right]^{2/5},\qquad n=0,1,\ldots.}
$$

The coefficient $a$ here denotes the potential strength, not the [integration](../../../../../integral-split.md) limit in the generic quantization formula.

The [WKB approximation](../../../../../wkb-approximation.md) is reliable in the interior where $|k'|/k^2\ll1$, but fails near the soft [turning point](../../../../../turning-point.md) where $k\to0$; a connection formula supplies its phase. The hard wall also requires its [boundary condition](../../../../../boundary-condition.md), and $V'=a/(2\sqrt x)$ makes the local slow-variation condition fail sufficiently close to zero. Higher-energy states have a larger [action](../../../../../action.md) relative to $\hbar$, with smaller relative endpoint regions, so the leading WKB spectrum is generally more reliable at large $n$ than for the [ground state](../../../../../ground-state.md).
