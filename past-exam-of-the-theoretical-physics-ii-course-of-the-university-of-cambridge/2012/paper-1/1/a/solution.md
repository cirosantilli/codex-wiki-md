<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a differentiable family of invertible [linear operators](../../../../../linear-operator.md), differentiate the identity without changing the factor order:

$$
\frac{dA^{-1}}{d\lambda}A+A^{-1}\frac{dA}{d\lambda}=0.
$$

Multiplication on the right by $A^{-1}$ gives the [derivative of matrix inversion](../../../../../derivative-of-matrix-inversion.md),

$$
\boxed{\frac{dA^{-1}}{d\lambda}=-A^{-1}\frac{dA}{d\lambda}A^{-1}.}
$$

Commuting $A^{-1}$ past the [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) would generally be invalid.
