<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Take an [orthonormal basis](../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) $\{|j\rangle\}$ and insert its [completeness relation](../../../../../resolution-of-the-identity.md):

$$
\operatorname{Tr}(|u\rangle\langle v|)
=\sum_j\langle j|u\rangle\langle v|j\rangle
=\langle v|\left(\sum_j|j\rangle\langle j|\right)|u\rangle
=\boxed{\langle v|u\rangle}.
$$

No normalization or [orthogonality](../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) of $u,v$ is required. This is the [trace](../../../../../matrix-trace.md) of a [rank-one operator](../../../../../rank-one-operator.md); in a [Hilbert space](../../../../../hilbert-space-split.md) the sum converges by the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](../../../../../cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md).
