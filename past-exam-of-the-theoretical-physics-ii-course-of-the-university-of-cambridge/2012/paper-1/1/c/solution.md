<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

In a finite-dimensional [basis](../../../../../basis.md), interchange the two finite sums:

$$
\operatorname{Tr}(AB)=\sum_{j,k}A_{jk}B_{kj}
=\sum_{k,j}B_{kj}A_{jk}
=\boxed{\operatorname{Tr}(BA)}.
$$

This [cyclic property of the trace](../../../../../cyclic-property-of-the-trace.md) does not assert $AB=BA$ or permit arbitrary reordering of three factors. For infinite-dimensional [linear operators](../../../../../linear-operator.md), the [traces](../../../../../matrix-trace.md) and rearrangements need hypotheses, for example one [trace-class operator](../../../../../trace-class-operator.md) and one [bounded operator](../../../../../continuous-linear-operator.md); an arbitrary pair of unbounded [linear operators](../../../../../linear-operator.md) is not covered.
