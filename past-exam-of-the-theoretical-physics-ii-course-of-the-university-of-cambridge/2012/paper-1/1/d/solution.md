<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For real $\epsilon$, [unitarity](../../../../../unitary-operator.md) to first order gives

$$
I=U^\dagger U=(I-i\epsilon F^\dagger)(I+i\epsilon F)
=I+i\epsilon(F-F^\dagger)+O(\epsilon^2).
$$

Hence the generator of an [infinitesimal unitary transformation](../../../../../infinitesimal-unitary-transformation.md) obeys **$F=F^\dagger$**: it is [Hermitian](../../../../../hermitian-operator.md). More precisely, $U(\epsilon)=I+i\epsilon F+O(\epsilon^2)$ is a differentiable unitary family at the identity. The omitted second-order terms matter if $\epsilon$ is finite.
