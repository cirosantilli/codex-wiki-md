<h1 id="4/e/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [reduced density matrix](../../../../../reduced-density-matrix.md) has two [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) equal to $1/2$. Its [Von Neumann entropy](../../../../../von-neumann-entropy-split.md), which is the [entanglement entropy](../../../../../entanglement-entropy.md) of the bipartite [pure state](../../../../../pure-state.md), is

$$
\boxed{S_R=-\operatorname{Tr}(\rho_R\ln\rho_R)=-2\left(\frac12\ln\frac12\right)=\ln2.}
$$

This is one bit when base-two logarithms are used. It is the maximum [entanglement entropy](../../../../../entanglement-entropy.md) for a pair of two-dimensional systems, while the joint [Von Neumann entropy](../../../../../von-neumann-entropy-split.md) of the [singlet state](../../../../../singlet-state.md) remains zero.
