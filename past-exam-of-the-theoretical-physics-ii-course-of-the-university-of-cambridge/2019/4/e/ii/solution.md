<h1 id="4/e/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Taking the [partial trace](../../../../../partial-trace.md) over the left atom in the [singlet state](../../../../../singlet-state.md) gives the right-hand [reduced density matrix](../../../../../reduced-density-matrix.md)

$$
(\rho_R)_{ss'}=\sum_{a=\uparrow,\downarrow}\langle a,s|\rho_D|a,s'\rangle,
\qquad\boxed{\rho_R=\frac12\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix}=\frac I2.}
$$

Thus every measurement on the right stream alone has the same statistics as B or C. The joint [pure state](../../../../../pure-state.md) is compatible with locally [mixed states](../../../../../mixed-state.md); the correlations reside in the full [density operator](../../../../../density-matrix-split.md).
