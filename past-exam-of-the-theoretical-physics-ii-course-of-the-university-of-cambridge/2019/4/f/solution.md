<h1 id="4/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

Take the two magnetic moments along $z$ to be $\pm\mu$, so $H=-\mu B_z\sigma_z$ and the two [energies](../../../../energy-split.md) are $\mp\mu B_z$. The [quantum Gibbs state](../../../../quantum-gibbs-state.md) at inverse [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) $\beta=1/(k_BT)$ is

$$
\boxed{\rho_E=\frac{e^{\beta\mu B_z\sigma_z}}{2\cosh(\beta\mu B_z)}
=\frac1{2\cosh(\beta\mu B_z)}\begin{pmatrix}e^{\beta\mu B_z}&0\\0&e^{-\beta\mu B_z}\end{pmatrix}
=\frac{I+\tanh(\beta\mu B_z)\sigma_z}{2}.}
$$

Its [Bloch vector](../../../../bloch-vector.md) is $(0,0,\tanh\beta\mu B_z)$. At $B_z=0$, or in the high-temperature limit, it approaches $I/2$; at $T\to0^+$ with nonzero $\mu B_z$, it approaches the lower-energy [pure state](../../../../pure-state.md). Here $\mu$ is the magnitude or signed coefficient multiplying $\sigma_z$ in the magnetic [Hamiltonian](../../../../hamiltonian.md), rather than a moment with an additional unstated factor of two.
