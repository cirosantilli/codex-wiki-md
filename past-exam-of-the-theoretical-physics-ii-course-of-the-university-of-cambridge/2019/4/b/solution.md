<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Taking the [outer product](../../../../outer-product.md) for A and averaging the two [orthogonal projections](../../../../orthogonal-projection.md) for B gives

$$
\boxed{\rho_A=\frac12\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix}=\frac{I+\sigma_x}{2},\qquad
\rho_B=\frac12\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix}=\frac I2.}
$$

The off-diagonal entries in $\rho_A$ retain [quantum coherence](../../../../quantum-coherence-in-a-specified-basis.md) between the $z$-basis states. They are absent from B's classical [quantum state ensemble](../../../../quantum-state-ensemble.md). In particular, $\operatorname{Tr}\rho_A^2=1$ and $\operatorname{Tr}\rho_B^2=1/2$: A is a [pure state](../../../../pure-state.md), B a [mixed state](../../../../mixed-state.md).
