<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [quantum state ensemble](../../../../quantum-state-ensemble.md) of normalized states $|\psi_i\rangle$, with [probabilities](../../../../probability.md) $p_i\ge0$ and $\sum_i p_i=1$, has [density operator](../../../../density-matrix-split.md)

$$
\boxed{\rho=\sum_i p_i|\psi_i\rangle\langle\psi_i|.}
$$

It is a [positive semidefinite](../../../../positive-semidefinite-matrix.md) [Hermitian operator](../../../../hermitian-operator.md) with [trace](../../../../matrix-trace.md) one, and the [Born rule](../../../../born-rule.md) assigns a measurement outcome with projector $\Pi$ the [probability](../../../../probability.md) $\operatorname{Tr}(\rho\Pi)$.

A [pure state](../../../../pure-state.md) has $\rho=|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|$, hence $\rho^2=\rho$ and [purity of a density operator](../../../../purity-of-a-density-operator.md) $\operatorname{Tr}(\rho^2)=1$. Its only nonzero [eigenvalue](../../../../eigenvalue.md) is one. A [mixed state](../../../../mixed-state.md) has at least two nonzero [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md) and $\operatorname{Tr}(\rho^2)<1$. The [spectral theorem](../../../../spectral-theorem.md) proves the converse criteria, since the nonnegative [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md) sum to one. Different [quantum state ensembles](../../../../quantum-state-ensemble.md) may give the same [density operator](../../../../density-matrix-split.md); the mere presence of several terms in a preparation list does not make the state mixed if all positive-weight terms represent the same [quantum state ray](../../../../quantum-state-ray.md).
