<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [projective measurement](../../../../projective-measurement.md) probabilities are $P(\sigma_j=\pm1)=\operatorname{Tr}[\rho(I\pm\sigma_j)/2]$. Both [density operators](../../../../density-matrix-split.md) have zero $z$-component of their [Bloch vector](../../../../bloch-vector.md), so

$$
P_A(\sigma_z=\pm1)=P_B(\sigma_z=\pm1)=\frac12.
$$

Their full $\sigma_z$ outcome distributions, not merely their mean values, coincide. Along $x$, however,

$$
\boxed{P_A(\sigma_x=+1)=1,\quad P_A(\sigma_x=-1)=0;\qquad
P_B(\sigma_x=\pm1)=\frac12.}
$$

Thus repeated [spin measurement](../../../../spin-measurement.md) along $x$ distinguishes the preparations statistically. A single negative outcome excludes A, while a single positive outcome is compatible with either; under B the [probability](../../../../probability.md) of $N$ independent positive outcomes is $2^{-N}$.
