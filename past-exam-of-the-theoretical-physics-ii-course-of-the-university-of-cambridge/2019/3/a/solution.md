↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For [bosonic creation operators](../../../../bosonic-creation-operator.md) and [bosonic annihilation operators](../../../../bosonic-annihilation-operator.md), the [canonical commutation relations](../../../../canonical-commutation-relation.md) are

$$
[a_i,a_j^\dagger]=\delta_{ij},\qquad[a_i,a_j]=[a_i^\dagger,a_j^\dagger]=0.
$$

For [fermionic annihilation operators](../../../../fermionic-annihilation-operator.md) and their adjoints, the [canonical anticommutation relations](../../../../canonical-anticommutation-relations.md) are

$$
\{f_i,f_j^\dagger\}=\delta_{ij},\qquad\{f_i,f_j\}=\{f_i^\dagger,f_j^\dagger\}=0.
$$

The respective [number operators](../../../../number-operator.md) are $n_i=a_i^\dagger a_i$ and $n_i=f_i^\dagger f_i$. Their allowed [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md) are $\boxed{0,1,2,\ldots\text{ for bosons};\quad0,1\text{ for fermions}}$. In the fermionic case $n_i^2=n_i$, so its [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md) must satisfy $n^2=n$; this is the single-mode [Pauli exclusion principle](../../../../pauli-exclusion-principle.md).
