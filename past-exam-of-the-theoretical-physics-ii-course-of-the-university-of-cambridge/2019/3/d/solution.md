<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Using the consistently normalized [Jordan–Wigner transformation](../../../../jordan-wigner-transformation.md) from part (c), write $\sigma_1^x\sigma_2^x+\sigma_1^y\sigma_2^y=2(s_1^+s_2^-+s_1^-s_2^+)$. Since $f_1^\dagger P_1=f_1^\dagger$ and $f_1P_1=-f_1$, the parity string gives

$$
s_1^+s_2^-=f_1^\dagger f_2,\qquad s_1^-s_2^+=f_2^\dagger f_1.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{H=2h-2h(n_1+n_2)-2J(f_1^\dagger f_2+f_2^\dagger f_1).}
$$

The [orthogonal transformation](../../../../orthogonal-transformation.md) $b=(f_1+f_2)/\sqrt2$, $c=(f_1-f_2)/\sqrt2$ preserves the [canonical anticommutation relations](../../../../canonical-anticommutation-relations.md) and diagonalizes this bilinear [Hamiltonian](../../../../hamiltonian.md):

$$
\boxed{H=2h+(-2h-2J)b^\dagger b+(-2h+2J)c^\dagger c.}
$$

The [Fock vacuum](../../../../fock-vacuum.md) is $|\downarrow\downarrow\rangle$, with [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) $2h$. Occupying $b$ alone gives the symmetric opposite-spin state with [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) $-2J$; occupying $c$ alone gives the antisymmetric state with [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) $2J$; occupying both gives $|\uparrow\uparrow\rangle$, up to a [global phase](../../../../global-phase.md), with [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) $-2h$. This reproduces the entire spectrum in part (b).
