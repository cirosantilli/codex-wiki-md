<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In the ordered product basis $(|\uparrow\uparrow\rangle,|\uparrow\downarrow\rangle,|\downarrow\uparrow\rangle,|\downarrow\downarrow\rangle)$, the [two-spin XY Hamiltonian](../../../../two-spin-xy-hamiltonian.md) is

$$
\boxed{H=\begin{pmatrix}-2h&0&0&0\\0&0&-2J&0\\0&-2J&0&0\\0&0&0&2h\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

The transverse term exchanges opposite spins and leaves parallel spins unchanged. Diagonalizing its two-dimensional block gives the complete [orthonormal eigenbasis](../../../../orthonormal-eigenbasis.md)

$$
\boxed{\begin{array}{c|c}
\text{state}&\text{energy}\\\hline
|\uparrow\uparrow\rangle&-2h\\
(|\uparrow\downarrow\rangle+|\downarrow\uparrow\rangle)/\sqrt2&-2J\\
(|\uparrow\downarrow\rangle-|\downarrow\uparrow\rangle)/\sqrt2&2J\\
|\downarrow\downarrow\rangle&2h
\end{array}}
$$

The antisymmetric state is the [singlet state](../../../../singlet-state.md); the other three are [triplet states](../../../../spin-one-half-triplet-state.md). Accidental equality of [energies](../../../../energy-split.md) does not invalidate this [eigenbasis](../../../../eigenbasis.md).
