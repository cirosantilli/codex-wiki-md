<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Heisenberg equation of motion](../../../../heisenberg-equation-of-motion.md) and the [spin commutation relations](../../../../spin-commutation-relations.md) give, componentwise,

$$
\frac{dS_i}{dt}=\frac{i}{\hbar}[-\gamma B_jS_j,S_i]
=-\gamma\epsilon_{ijk}B_jS_k.
$$

Thus $\boxed{d\mathbf S/dt=\gamma\mathbf S\times\mathbf B}$. For constant [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) this is [Larmor precession](../../../../larmor-precession.md): the component along $\mathbf B$ is conserved and the transverse components rotate. In particular, for $\mathbf B=B\widehat{\mathbf z}$,

$$
S_x(t)=S_x(0)\cos\gamma Bt+S_y(0)\sin\gamma Bt,\quad
S_y(t)=S_y(0)\cos\gamma Bt-S_x(0)\sin\gamma Bt,\quad S_z(t)=S_z(0).
$$

The signed [angular velocity](../../../../angular-velocity.md) is $-\gamma\mathbf B$; the positive precession rate is $|\gamma B|$.
