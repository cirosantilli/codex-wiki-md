# Highest-weight classification of finite-dimensional semisimple Lie algebra modules

↑ **Parent:** [Highest-weight representation](highest-weight-representation.md)

For a complex [semisimple Lie algebra](semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md) and a choice of [positive roots](positive-root.md), every finite-dimensional [Irreducible Lie algebra representation](irreducible-lie-algebra-representation.md) has a unique [highest weight](highest-weight-of-a-representation.md), which is a [dominant integral weight](dominant-integral-weight.md). Conversely each dominant integral weight labels exactly one such irreducible module up to isomorphism. Its [highest-weight vector](highest-weight-vector.md) spans the highest-weight space and generates the module. This classification and [Weyl complete reducibility theorem](weyl-complete-reducibility-theorem.md) reduce decomposing any finite-dimensional module to its irreducible highest-weight summands.
