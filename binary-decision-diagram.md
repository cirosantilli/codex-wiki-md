# Binary decision diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Boolean function](boolean-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Binary_decision_diagram)

A [Directed acyclic graph](directed-acyclic-graph.md) representing a [Boolean function](boolean-function.md). A [decision node](decision-node.md) tests a [Boolean variable](boolean-variable.md) and has zero and one successors; evaluation follows the corresponding arrows until a [terminal node](terminal-node.md) returns a [Boolean](boolean-data-type.md) value. Sharing subgraphs can make a [binary decision diagram](binary-decision-diagram.md) much smaller than a [binary decision tree](binary-decision-tree.md).
