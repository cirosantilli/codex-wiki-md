# Projective Minkowski compactification

↑ **Parent:** [Minkowski conformal compactification](minkowski-conformal-compactification.md)

The Lorentzian real form of the [Klein quadric](klein-quadric.md) is the projectivized real null cone of signature $(2,4)$. Scaling a nonzero null vector makes its positive two-dimensional and negative four-dimensional parts both unit vectors. Real projectivization then identifies $(u,v)$ with $(-u,-v)$, yielding the displayed quotient. Its universal cover is $\mathbb R\times S^3$, and its fundamental group is infinite cyclic. An affine Minkowski chart omits a null cone at infinity. This projective compact space is distinct from an unwrapped causal cover used for a [Penrose diagram](penrose-diagram.md).
