# Classical ideal gas in a homogeneous soft trap

↑ **Parent:** [Classical ideal-gas partition function](classical-ideal-gas-partition-function.md)

For the potential $U=r^{2n}/V^{2n/3}$, rescaling $r=V^{1/3}(kT)^{1/(2n)}u$ in the classical one-particle [partition function](canonical-partition-function.md) gives the formula above. The parameter-conjugate force is $P=NkT/V$, the [internal energy](internal-energy.md) is $(3/2+3/(2n))NkT$, and $C_P-C_V=Nk$. The kinetic term contributes $3NkT/2$, while the potential term contributes $3NkT/(2n)$. For indistinguishable particles the $N!$ [factor](regression-factor.md) changes [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md) and [entropy](entropy.md) but not these force and heat-capacity formulas.
