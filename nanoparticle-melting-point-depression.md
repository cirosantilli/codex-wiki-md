# Nanoparticle melting-point depression

↑ **Parent:** [Gibbs--Thomson relation](gibbs-thomson-relation.md)

For the stipulated spherical solid–liquid capillarity model, $T_m(r)=T_m(\infty)[1-2\gamma/(r\Delta H_V)]$, where $\gamma$ is the interfacial [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md) and $\Delta H_V$ the [latent heat](latent-heat.md) density. It follows by matching $g_l-g_s\simeq\Delta H_V[T_m(\infty)-T]/T_m(\infty)$ to $2\gamma/r$. Support contacts, free surfaces, anisotropy, premelting and atomistic structure can modify this relation. A continuum extrapolation to negative [melting](melting.md) temperatures is not physical.
