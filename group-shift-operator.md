# Group shift operator

↑ **Parent:** [Hidden subgroup problem](hidden-subgroup-problem.md)

For a [finite abelian group](finite-abelian-group.md) $G$, $U(h)|g\rangle=|g+h\rangle$ defines the [regular representation](regular-representation.md) on the basis labelled by $G$. The [linear characters](linear-character.md) give the common [eigenbasis](eigenbasis.md)

$$
|v_\chi\rangle=|G|^{-1/2}\sum_g\overline{\chi(g)}|g\rangle,
\qquad U(h)|v_\chi\rangle=\chi(h)|v_\chi\rangle.
$$

[Character orthogonality](character-orthogonality.md) proves orthonormality, and changing variables by the [translation in a group](translation-in-a-group.md) proves the eigenvalue formula.
