# Bingham sliding law

↑ **Parent:** [Bingham plastic](bingham-plastic.md)

A thin Bingham layer of thickness $h$ between a stationary wall and a moving body gives $u_b=(h/\mu)(|\tau_b|-\tau_c)_+\operatorname{sgn}\tau_b$.
