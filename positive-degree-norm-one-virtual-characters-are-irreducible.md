# Positive-degree norm-one virtual characters are irreducible

↑ **Parent:** [Virtual character](virtual-character.md)

Write the [virtual character](virtual-character.md) as $\psi=\sum_\chi a_\chi\chi$ with integer coefficients and [irreducible characters](irreducible-character.md) $\chi$. [Character orthogonality](character-orthogonality.md) gives $\langle\psi,\psi\rangle=\sum_\chi a_\chi^2$. Norm one forces exactly one coefficient to be $1$ or $-1$, and positive degree selects the positive sign.
