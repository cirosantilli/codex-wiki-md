# Adiabatic pressure support during gravitational collapse

↑ **Parent:** [Astrophysical fluid dynamics](astrophysical-fluid-dynamics-split.md)

With fixed mass and homologous scale $L$, adiabatic [pressure](pressure.md) obeys $p\propto L^{-3\gamma}$. Its support relative to gravitational energy scales as $pL^3/|W_g|\propto L^{4-3\gamma}$. [Pressure](pressure.md) becomes relatively stronger on contraction for $\gamma>4/3$, weaker for $\gamma<4/3$ and has the same scaling at $\gamma=4/3$.
