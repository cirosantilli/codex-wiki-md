# Power-coordinate reduction of a singular Sturm-Liouville quotient

↑ **Parent:** [Sturm-Liouville theory](sturm-liouville-theory-split.md)

The singular equation $2xw''-w'+\lambda x^2w=0$ becomes $W''+(2\lambda/9)W=0$ under $z=x^{3/2}$, $w(x)=W(z)$. Its zero-endpoint finite-[energy](energy-split.md) quotient transforms as $2\int_0^1x^{-1/2}(w')^2dx/\int_0^1x^{1/2}w^2dx=(9/2)\int_0^1(W')^2dz/\int_0^1W^2dz$. The [Poincaré inequality](poincare-inequality.md) therefore proves the sharp minimum $9\pi^2/2$, attained by $w=\sin(\pi x^{3/2})$. This coordinate change turns an apparently singular weighted variational problem into the usual interval [energy](energy-split.md) problem. The admissible eigenfunction is $O(x^{3/2})$, making the integration-by-parts [boundary term](boundary-term.md) $wx^{-1/2}w'$ vanish and both [energy](energy-split.md) [integrals](integral-split.md) converge.
