# Herpolhode

↑ **Parent:** [Poinsot's ellipsoid](poinsot-s-ellipsoid.md)

The [herpolhode](herpolhode.md) is the contact curve traced on the fixed invariable plane in Poinsot's torque-free construction. It is distinct from the [polhode](polhode.md), which is described in body coordinates.
