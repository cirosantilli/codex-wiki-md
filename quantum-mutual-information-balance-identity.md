# Quantum mutual information balance identity

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum mutual information](quantum-mutual-information.md)

For any tripartite [density operator](density-matrix.md), expanding the three [quantum mutual information](quantum-mutual-information.md) terms in [Von Neumann entropy](von-neumann-entropy-split.md) gives $I(X:BD)=I(X:B)+I(XB:D)-I(B:D)$. This algebraic identity allows separate bounds on two information contributions, while [nonnegativity of quantum relative entropy](nonnegativity-of-quantum-relative-entropy.md) allows the last term to be dropped in an upper bound.
