# Weak composition of an integer

↑ **Parent:** [Composition of an integer](composition-of-an-integer.md)

A [weak composition of an integer](weak-composition-of-an-integer.md) $N\geq0$ into $k\geq1$ parts is an ordered [tuple](finite-tuple.md) of [nonnegative integers](natural-number.md) with [sum](sum.md) $N$. Zero parts retain their positions: $(0,2,3)$ and $(2,0,3)$ differ. Fixing the length $k$ is essential when applying [stars and bars](stars-and-bars-combinatorics.md), since inserting extra zero parts creates further [tuples](finite-tuple.md). [Gotti, Lecture 6, definition and discussion of weak compositions](https://math.mit.edu/~fgotti/docs/Courses/C.%20Combinatorial%20Analysis/6.%20Compositions/Compositions.pdf)
