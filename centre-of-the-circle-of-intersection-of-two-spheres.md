# Centre of the circle of intersection of two spheres

↑ **Parent:** [Sphere](sphere.md)

For two spheres with distinct centres $a,b$, radii $R,S>0$ and $d=|b-a|$, subtracting their equations gives a plane perpendicular to $b-a$. Its projection of $a$ is the displayed centre, and the circle radius squared is $R^2-[(d^2+R^2-S^2)/(2d)]^2$. A nondegenerate circle exists exactly when $|R-S|<d<R+S$; equality gives tangency. Coincident equal spheres are not a circle and are excluded by $d>0$.
