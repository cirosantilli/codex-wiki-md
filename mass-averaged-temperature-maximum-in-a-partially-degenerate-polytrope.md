# Mass-averaged temperature maximum in a partially degenerate polytrope

↑ **Parent:** [Contraction-limited central temperature](contraction-limited-central-temperature.md)

For a homologous [stellar polytrope](stellar-polytrope.md) of index $3/2$, take $P=K\rho^{5/3}+\mathcal R\rho T/\mu$ and zero surface pressure. The [stellar virial theorem](stellar-virial-theorem.md), with $\Omega=-6GM^2/(7R)$, gives

$$
\overline T_M=\frac{2\mu GM}{7\mathcal RR}-\frac{\mu A M^{2/3}}{3\mathcal RR^2},\qquad A=3K\int_0^1\left(\frac{3\rho}{4\pi\bar\rho}\right)^{2/3}dq.
$$

The normalized density profile fixes $A$ independently of mass and radius at fixed composition. Writing $\overline T_M=\alpha/R-\delta/R^2$ shows that its maximum is $\alpha^2/(4\delta)=3\mu G^2M^{4/3}/(49\mathcal RA)$, at $R=7A M^{-1/3}/(3G)$. Below this radius contraction increases the fraction of support provided by [electron degeneracy pressure](electron-degeneracy-pressure.md) and lowers the thermal temperature. A fixed normalized profile also fixes the ratio of central to mass-averaged temperature, giving a [hydrogen-burning minimum mass](hydrogen-burning-minimum-mass.md).
