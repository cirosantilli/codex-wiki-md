# Symmetry of the first-order monochromatic alpha tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Monochromatic small-Reynolds-number mean electromotive force](monochromatic-small-reynolds-number-mean-electromotive-force.md)

The first coefficient of the [alpha tensor](alpha-tensor.md) is $\alpha^{(1)}_{ij}=\epsilon_{ikl}\langle u_k\partial_j u_l\rangle$. Its contraction with the [Levi-Civita symbol](levi-civita-symbol.md) is

$$
\epsilon_{pij}\alpha^{(1)}_{ij}=\langle u_j\partial_j u_p-u_p\partial_j u_j\rangle=0,
$$

by [incompressibility](incompressible-flow.md) and vanishing averages of total derivatives. Every [antisymmetric second-rank tensor](antisymmetric-second-rank-tensor.md) in three dimensions is detected by this contraction, so the coefficient is a [symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md). The proof needs no [Fourier analysis](fourier-analysis-split.md).
