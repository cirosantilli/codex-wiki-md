# Modular representation ring

↑ **Parent:** [Modular representation theory](modular-representation-theory.md)

The modular representation ring is the [Grothendieck group](grothendieck-group.md) of finite-dimensional [group representations](group-representation.md), with relations $[V]=[U]+[W]$ for every [short exact sequence](short-exact-sequence.md) $0\to U\to V\to W\to0$, and multiplication $[V][W]=[V\otimes_k W]$ using the [tensor product of group representations](tensor-product-of-group-representations.md). The [Jordan–Hölder theorem](jordan-holder-theorem.md) makes the classes of [simple modules](irreducible-module.md) a free integral basis. Tensoring over a [field](field.md) is exact, so this multiplication is well defined. When $k$ is a [splitting field for finite group representations](splitting-field-for-finite-group-representations.md), [Brauer characters](brauer-character.md) identify $\mathbb C\otimes_{\mathbb Z}R_k(G)$ with the algebra of complex [class functions](class-function.md) on [p-regular elements](p-regular-element.md). The splitting hypothesis is essential: $\mathbb F_2C_3\cong\mathbb F_2\times\mathbb F_4$ has two [simple modules](irreducible-module.md), although $C_3$ has three $2$-regular conjugacy classes.
