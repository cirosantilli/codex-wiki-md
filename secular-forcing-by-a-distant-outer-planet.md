# Secular forcing by a distant outer planet

↑ **Parent:** [Laplace-Lagrange secular theory](laplace-lagrange-secular-theory.md)

For a test particle interior to a distant eccentric [planet](planet.md), write $\alpha=a/a_p\ll1$. The leading [Laplace coefficients](laplace-coefficient.md) are $b_{3/2}^{1}=3\alpha$ and $b_{3/2}^{2}=15\alpha^2/4$. Thus its [forced eccentricity](forced-eccentricity.md) is $Z_f=5\alpha e_p/4$ and its secular frequency is $A=(3/4)n_p(m_p/M)\alpha^{3/2}$. More distant inner particles have larger forced [orbital eccentricities](orbital-eccentricity.md) and faster secular precession, producing a growing gradient of [complex eccentricity](complex-eccentricity.md).
