# Coefficient of thermal expansion

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal expansion](thermal-expansion.md)

The fractional [mass density](density.md) decrease per [temperature](temperature.md) increase at fixed [pressure](pressure.md). In the [Boussinesq approximation](boussinesq-approximation.md), a [temperature](temperature.md) excess $\theta$ gives [reduced gravity](reduced-gravity-split.md) $g'=g\alpha_T\theta$. It converts a [heat](heat.md) budget into a buoyancy budget in [natural ventilation](natural-ventilation.md).
