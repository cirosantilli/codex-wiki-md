# Best-first search

↑ **Parent:** [Graph search](graph-search.md)

Maintain a frontier of partial states in a [priority queue](priority-queue.md) and expand the state with the best current priority. Correctness of a particular stopping rule depends on what the priority bounds. If every descendant has smaller priority than its ancestor, the frontier maximum also bounds the priority of every unseen descendant.
