# Darcy law

↑ **Parent:** [Porous-media flow](porous-media-flow-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darcy_law)

Darcy's law relates the [Darcy velocity](darcy-velocity.md), the fluid flux per unit total cross-sectional area relative to a solid skeleton, to the driving pressure gradient. With no body force, $\mathbf u=-(k/\mu)\nabla p$, where $k$ is [permeability of a porous medium](permeability-of-a-porous-medium.md) and $\mu$ is [dynamic viscosity](dynamic-viscosity.md). With gravity, the pressure gradient is replaced by $\nabla p-\rho\mathbf g$.
