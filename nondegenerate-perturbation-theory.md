# Nondegenerate perturbation theory

↑ **Parent:** [Time-independent perturbation theory](time-independent-perturbation-theory.md)

[Perturbation theory](perturbation-theory.md) for an isolated [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) with a one-dimensional [eigenspace](eigenspace.md). [First-order nondegenerate perturbation theory](first-order-nondegenerate-perturbation-theory.md) gives the diagonal perturbation expectation, while [second-order nondegenerate perturbation theory](second-order-nondegenerate-perturbation-theory.md) accounts for coupling to other unperturbed eigenstates through energy denominators. Symmetry can permit its use within one block even if another, uncoupled symmetry block has the same unperturbed eigenvalue.
