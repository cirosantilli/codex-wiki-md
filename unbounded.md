# Unbounded

↑ **Parent:** [Boundedness](boundedness.md)

Failure of [boundedness](boundedness.md): for every finite radius, some point lies outside the proposed bounding ball. A real-valued [function](function-split.md) is unbounded if its range is unbounded.
