# Statistical inference

↑ **Parent:** [Probability and statistics](probability-and-statistics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_inference)

[Statistical inference](statistical-inference-split.md) uses observed data and a [statistical model](statistical-model-split.md) to learn about unknown features of the process that generated them. In a [parametric statistical model](parametric-statistical-model.md), write $X\sim P_\theta$: the [probability distribution](probability-distribution-split.md) depends on an unknown [statistical parameter](statistical-parameter.md) $\theta$. An [estimand](estimand.md) specifies the target, such as a [mean](expected-value-split.md) or a difference of [means](expected-value-split.md). A [statistic](statistic.md) is a function of the observations; an [estimator](estimator.md) is a [statistic](statistic.md) used to estimate the target. Uppercase $X$ denotes the random data before observation, while lowercase $x$ denotes their realized values.

Three questions lead to different outputs. [Statistical estimation](statistical-estimation.md) asks which target value the observations suggest. A [confidence interval](confidence-interval.md) asks for a range produced by a procedure with calibrated [coverage probability](coverage-probability.md). A [hypothesis test](statistical-hypothesis-test-split.md) asks whether the observations depart sufficiently from a specified [null hypothesis](null-hypothesis.md) to enter its [rejection region](rejection-region.md). These outputs complement one another: an estimated [effect size](effect-size.md) describes magnitude, while a [standard error](standard-error.md) describes the [sampling distribution](sampling-distribution.md) of an [estimator](estimator.md). A [p-value](p-value.md) describes a tail [probability](probability.md) under the [null hypothesis](null-hypothesis.md).

The appeal of [statistical inference](statistical-inference-split.md) is that it turns variable observations into reproducible uncertainty statements. In the [normal mean confidence interval with known variance](normal-mean-confidence-interval-with-known-variance.md) below, a [sample mean](sample-mean.md) of $1.2$ has [standard error](standard-error.md) $0.5$: both the estimated shift and its precision matter. The calibration depends on the [statistical model](statistical-model-split.md), including [independence](independent-random-variables.md) and the specified [probability distribution](probability-distribution-split.md). Increasing [sample size](sample-size.md) improves precision under those assumptions; it does not repair a systematically misleading sampling procedure. [Bayesian inference](bayesian-statistics-split.md) provides another framework, using a [prior distribution](prior-probability.md) and a [likelihood function](likelihood-function.md) to obtain a [posterior distribution](bayesian-posterior.md).
