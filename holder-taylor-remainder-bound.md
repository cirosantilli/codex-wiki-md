<h1 id="holder-taylor-remainder-bound">Hölder-Taylor remainder bound</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Hölder space](holder-space.md)

Write $\alpha=r+\beta$ with an [integer](integer.md) $r\ge0$ and $0<\beta\le1$, using $r=\alpha-1$ at [integer](integer.md) $\alpha$. For $f\in C^{r,\beta}$, subtract its degree-$r$ [Taylor polynomial](taylor-polynomial.md) at $x_0$. For $r\ge1$, the [integral](integral.md) [Taylor remainder](taylor-remainder.md) with $f^{(r)}(t)-f^{(r)}(x_0)$ is bounded using the [Hölder seminorm](holder-seminorm.md); for $r=0$ this is exactly [Hölder continuity](holder-condition.md). The resulting bound is the displayed estimate. At [integer](integer.md) order, the convention is $C^{r,1}$; a $C^{r+1}$ [function](function-split.md) on a [compact](compact-space.md) [closed interval](closed-real-interval.md) also obeys this estimate.
