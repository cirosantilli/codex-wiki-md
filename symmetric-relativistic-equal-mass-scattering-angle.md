# Symmetric relativistic equal-mass scattering angle

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic collision](elastic-collision.md)

A particle of [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) $\alpha mc^2$ hitting an equal mass at rest, with equal and opposite outgoing angles $\theta$, obeys $\tan\theta=\sqrt{2/(\alpha+2)}$. Conservation of total energy gives equal final energies, and longitudinal [momentum conservation](momentum-conservation.md) fixes the angle.
