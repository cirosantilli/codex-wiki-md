# Complex integration contour

↑ **Parent:** [Contour integration](contour-integration.md)

A complex integration [contour](complex-integration-contour.md) is an oriented piecewise continuously differentiable [curve](curve.md) in the [complex plane](complex-plane.md). For a parametrization $\gamma(s)$, its [contour integral](contour-integral.md) is $\int_\Gamma f(z)\,dz=\int f(\gamma(s))\gamma'(s)\,ds$. Reversing orientation changes the sign. Unbounded [contours](complex-integration-contour.md) require a convergent improper or explicitly regularized limit.
