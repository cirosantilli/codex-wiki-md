# Hilbert space module over a von Neumann algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Von Neumann algebra](von-neumann-algebra.md)

Here a [Hilbert space module](hilbert-space-module-over-a-von-neumann-algebra.md) means a [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) with a [unital](unital-algebra.md) star-representation of a [Von Neumann algebra](von-neumann-algebra.md), rather than a Hilbert C-star module with an algebra-valued [inner product](inner-product.md). A closed [invariant subspace](invariant-subspace.md) is reducing because the algebra is closed under adjoints. Module equivalence means an intertwining unitary. The [polar decomposition of a bounded operator](polar-decomposition-of-a-bounded-operator.md) converts an intertwiner into a unitary between its initial and final support subspaces.
