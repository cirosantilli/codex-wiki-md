# Raman scattering

↑ **Parent:** [Scattering of light](scattering-of-light.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raman_scattering)

[Raman scattering](raman-scattering.md) is [inelastic scattering](inelastic-scattering.md) of [light](light.md) in which the outgoing [photon](photon.md) has a different [frequency](frequency.md) because matter gains or loses internal [energy](energy-split.md). [Molecular vibration](molecular-vibration.md) is a particularly useful recipient: a visible [photon](photon.md) can reveal an internal [energy](energy-split.md) spacing normally associated with [infrared](infrared.md) [frequencies](frequency.md). [Rayleigh scattering](rayleigh-scattering.md) supplies the much stronger, approximately unchanged-[frequency](frequency.md) reference. The distinction concerns the transferred [energy](energy-split.md), rather than merely a change of direction or the colour of the illuminating beam.

The elementary bookkeeping is [conservation of energy](conservation-of-energy.md):

$$
h\nu_0+E_i=h\nu_s+E_f,\qquad h(\nu_0-\nu_s)=E_f-E_i.
$$

Here $h$ is the [Planck constant](planck-constant.md), $\nu_0$ and $\nu_s$ are the incident and scattered [photon](photon.md) [frequencies](frequency.md), and $E_i,E_f$ are initial and final internal molecular [energies](energy-split.md). This expression neglects translational recoil and [Doppler broadening](doppler-broadening.md); those can matter in sufficiently precise gas measurements. A positive internal [energy](energy-split.md) change gives [Stokes Raman scattering](stokes-raman-scattering.md), and a negative one gives [Anti-Stokes Raman scattering](anti-stokes-raman-scattering.md). The [energy](energy-split.md) difference determines a possible line position, but it does not by itself establish that a transition is optically allowed or predict its strength.

The spontaneous molecular process developed here has a connection to the [Raman transition](raman-transition.md) used in [atomic measurement methods](atomic-measurement-methods.md): two [photon](photon.md) interactions transfer an internal [energy](energy-split.md) difference. Coherently driving that process with two applied [light](light.md) fields is a different experimental regime from collecting weak spontaneous [Raman scattering](raman-scattering.md) beside an illuminating beam.
