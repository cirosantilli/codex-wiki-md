# Wee1

↑ **Parent:** [Cell-cycle activation feedback](cell-cycle-activation-feedback.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wee1)

[Wee1](wee1.md) is a [protein kinase](protein-kinase.md) that inhibits [CDK1](cyclin-dependent-kinase-1.md) by adding a [phosphate](phosphate.md) group at an inhibitory site. Purified human [Wee1](wee1.md) phosphorylated [Cdc2](cyclin-dependent-kinase-1.md) at Tyr15 in the reported in vitro assay, with no Thr14 modification detected. A catalytic-site [mutation](mutation.md) abolished both the measured [protein kinase](protein-kinase.md) activity and the ability to inhibit division. This identifies a catalytic brake rather than merely a correlated [protein](protein-split.md) amount.[McGowan and Russell (1993), abstract: purified human Wee1, Tyr15 specificity, no detectable Thr14 phosphorylation, and Lys114 mutant control](human-wee1-kinase-inhibits-cell-division-by-phosphorylating-p34cdc2-exclusively-on-tyr15-by-clare-mcgowan-and-others.md)

Do not attribute all inhibitory [CDK1](cyclin-dependent-kinase-1.md) [phosphorylation](phosphorylation.md) to [Wee1](wee1.md): the [Xenopus laevis](xenopus-laevis.md) [Myt1](myt1.md) experiment separately identified activity toward both Thr14 and Tyr15. Nor does every [phosphorylation](phosphorylation.md) inhibit its target: activating Thr161 modification in the studied [Xenopus laevis](xenopus-laevis.md) [Cdc2](cyclin-dependent-kinase-1.md) has the opposite role. In [cell-cycle activation feedback](cell-cycle-activation-feedback.md), suppression of [Wee1](wee1.md) removes a brake on [CDK1](cyclin-dependent-kinase-1.md), reinforcing the transition without implying that the total amount of either [protein](protein-split.md) instantly disappears.
