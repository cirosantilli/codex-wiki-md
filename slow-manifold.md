# Slow manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Singular perturbation](singular-perturbation-split.md)

For a fast-slow system $\epsilon\dot x=f(x,y)$, $\dot y=g(x,y)$, the formal critical manifold is $f=0$. Where the fast [linearization](linearization.md) has [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) with strictly negative real parts, trajectories rapidly approach a nearby attracting [slow manifold](slow-manifold.md) and then evolve on the slow time. Near folds or loss of this spectral condition, simply imposing $f=0$ may fail.
