# Reciprocal three-dimensional Bessel strict local martingale

↑ **Parent:** [Bessel process](bessel-process.md)

For a three-dimensional [Bessel process](bessel-process.md) started at one, $R$ stays strictly positive and satisfies $dR=dW+R^{-1}dt$. The [Itô formula](ito-s-lemma.md) makes its reciprocal a [local martingale](local-martingale.md), but $\mathbb E[R_t^{-1}]=2\Phi(1/\sqrt t)-1<1$ for $t>0$. Thus the reciprocal is a [strict local martingale](strict-local-martingale.md). It can deflate the market with constant bank account and stock price $R$ without yielding a true pricing density for the bank account.
