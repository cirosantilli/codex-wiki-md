<h1 id="cubic-u-1-gauge-anomaly">Cubic U(1) gauge anomaly</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Gauge anomaly](gauge-anomaly.md)

In four spacetime dimensions, the pure local [gauge anomaly](gauge-anomaly.md) for a [circle group](circle-group.md) is proportional to the sum of cubed [U(1) charges](u-1-charge.md) over left-handed [Weyl fermions](weyl-spinor.md), counting a right-handed [Weyl fermion](weyl-spinor.md) through its left-handed charge conjugate. A consistent anomaly-free [gauge theory](gauge-theory.md) requires this sum to vanish, absent an additional anomaly-cancelling mechanism. Cancellation of the [mixed gauge-gravitational anomaly](mixed-gauge-gravitational-anomaly.md) additionally requires $\sum_aQ_a=0$. If the same spectrum is restricted to a [finite cyclic group](finite-cyclic-group.md), $q_a=Q_a\bmod n$ implies $\sum_aq_a^3=0\bmod n$ and, when imposed, $\sum_aq_a=0\bmod n$. These elementary consequences alone do not classify intrinsic discrete [gauge anomalies](gauge-anomaly.md) or guarantee an anomaly-free integer lift.
