# Frieze group

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean isometry](euclidean-isometry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frieze_group)

A [discrete subgroup](discrete-subgroup.md) of [Euclidean isometries](euclidean-isometry.md) is a frieze group when its [translation subgroup](translation-subgroup.md) is infinite cyclic and has finite index. Translations along a line alone give $\mathbb Z$; adding perpendicular mirrors gives the [infinite dihedral group](infinite-dihedral-group.md), while adding a mirror along the translation direction gives $\mathbb Z\times C_2$. These groups describe patterns periodic in one planar direction.
