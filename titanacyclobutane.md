# Titanacyclobutane

↑ **Parent:** [Organometallic compound](organometallic-compound.md)

A four-membered metallacycle containing [titanium](titanium.md) and three [carbon](carbon.md) atoms. Cycloreversion can exchange an [alkene](alkene.md) and a [titanium](titanium.md) alkylidene; other eliminations become available with suitable substituents.
