# Ideal centre of a horocycle

↑ **Parent:** [Horocycle](horocycle.md)

The ideal centre of a [horocycle](horocycle.md) is its point of tangency to the boundary at infinity; a horizontal [horocycle](horocycle.md) in the [upper half-plane model](poincare-half-plane-model.md) has centre infinity. Sending the centre to infinity by an [isometry](isometry.md) makes the [horocycle](horocycle.md) horizontal. Orthogonal [hyperbolic lines](hyperbolic-line.md) then become vertical, proving that a [hyperbolic line](hyperbolic-line.md) meets a [horocycle](horocycle.md) orthogonally exactly when it has the centre as an [ideal endpoint](ideal-endpoint.md).
