# Radial Dirichlet spectrum in three dimensions

↑ **Parent:** [Sturm-Liouville problem](sturm-liouville-problem.md)

For the radial [Laplace operator](laplace-operator.md) $L=-r^{-2}(r^2u')'$ on $0<r<R$, require a finite value at the origin and $u(R)=0$. The map $u\mapsto v=ru$ preserves the [norm](norm.md) from $L^2((0,R),r^2dr)$ to $L^2(0,R)$ and sends $L$ to $-d^2/dr^2$. The regular endpoint condition becomes $v(0)=0$. The [self-adjoint](self-adjoint-operator.md) realization therefore corresponds to the ordinary [Dirichlet boundary conditions](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) for the one-dimensional second [derivative](derivative.md). Its [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) and [eigenfunctions](eigenfunction.md) are displayed above, with $u_n(0)=n\pi/R$ understood by a [limit](limit-of-a-function.md). Their squared weighted [norm](norm.md) is $R/2$. Completeness and [orthogonality](orthogonal-vectors.md) follow from the [Fourier sine series](fourier-sine-series.md) on $(0,R)$.
