# Mutual contiguity

↑ **Parent:** [Contiguity of probability measures](contiguity-of-probability-measures.md)

Two sequences of probability laws are [mutually contiguous](mutual-contiguity.md) when [contiguity of probability measures](contiguity-of-probability-measures.md) holds in both directions. A positive mean-one limit of their [likelihood ratio](likelihood-ratio.md) is a standard sufficient criterion.
