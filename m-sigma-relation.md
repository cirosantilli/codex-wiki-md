# M-sigma relation

↑ **Parent:** [Supermassive black hole](supermassive-black-hole.md)

The M-sigma relation is an empirical correlation between [supermassive black hole](supermassive-black-hole.md) mass and the stellar [velocity dispersion](velocity-dispersion.md) of its host's spheroidal component. A commonly used rough parametrization has exponent near four, but the normalization, exponent, scatter, and selection effects depend on the observed sample. It is distinct from the [black-hole--bulge mass relation](black-hole-bulge-mass-relation.md).
