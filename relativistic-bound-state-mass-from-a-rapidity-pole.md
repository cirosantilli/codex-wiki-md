# Relativistic bound-state mass from a rapidity pole

↑ **Parent:** [Physical rapidity strip](physical-rapidity-strip.md)

At relative [rapidity](rapidity.md) $iu$, the analytically continued sum of the constituent [four-momentum](four-momentum.md) vectors is on the bound-state [mass shell](mass-shell.md) with the displayed [mass](mass.md). For equal constituents, [rapidities](rapidity.md) $\chi\pm iu/2$ sum to $2m_a\cos(u/2)(\cosh\chi,\sinh\chi)$. A [pole](pole.md) at $u=0$ is at threshold rather than a strictly [bound state](bound-state.md).
