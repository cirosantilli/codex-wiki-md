# Quiver representation morphism

↑ **Parent:** [Representation of a quiver](representation-of-a-quiver.md)

For [quiver representations](representation-of-a-quiver.md) $X=(X_i,f_\rho)$ and $Y=(Y_i,g_\rho)$, a morphism consists of maps $h_i:X_i\to Y_i$ satisfying $h_{t(\rho)}f_\rho=g_\rho h_{s(\rho)}$ for every arrow. Composition is vertexwise. These families are the kernel of the [extension complex of quiver representations](extension-complex-of-quiver-representations.md).
