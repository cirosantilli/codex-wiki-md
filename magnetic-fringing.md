# Magnetic fringing

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic circuit](magnetic-circuit.md)

[Magnetic fringing](magnetic-fringing.md) is the spreading of [magnetic field lines](magnetic-field-line.md) near an air gap or pole edge, so the gap's effective [magnetic flux](magnetic-flux.md) area exceeds its geometrical pole-face [area](surface-area.md). A gap much shorter than its transverse dimensions permits a leading uniform-field approximation. High core [magnetic permeability](permeability-electromagnetism.md) does not by itself justify neglecting [magnetic fringing](magnetic-fringing.md) for a wide gap.
