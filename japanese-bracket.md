# Japanese bracket

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md)

The Japanese bracket is a smooth positive frequency weight, comparable to $1+|\xi|$. It defines [Sobolev space](sobolev-space-split.md) norms and the growth estimates of a [symbol class](symbol-class.md) without singularities at zero frequency.
