# Complex cobordism

↑ **Parent:** [Cobordism](cobordism.md)

Complex cobordism is the [generalized cohomology theory](generalized-cohomology-theory.md) represented by the stable Thom spaces of universal complex vector bundles. Its geometric cycles use maps with chosen stable complex normal data. Its complex [Thom classes](thom-class.md) give [Chern classes in complex cobordism](chern-class-in-complex-cobordism.md), and proper oriented maps have a [Gysin map in complex cobordism](gysin-map-in-complex-cobordism.md).
