# Dark-matter direct detection

↑ **Parent:** [Dark matter](dark-matter.md)

A search for interactions of passing [dark matter](dark-matter.md) with a terrestrial target, for example [nuclear recoils](nuclear-recoil.md) from [elastic scattering](elastic-scattering.md). Predicting a count requires the local density and velocity distribution, interaction [cross section](cross-section-physics.md), target response and [detector acceptance](detector-acceptance.md). A background-compatible count gives a conditional interaction bound, rather than a measurement of the cosmological relic density.
