# W. Ernest Thomson

↑ **Parent:** [List of physicians](list-of-physicians.md)

Author of the 1894 physical taper comparison in [The True Position of Oxygen as a Restorative in Carbonic Acid Poisoning by W. Ernest Thomson](the-true-position-of-oxygen-as-a-restorative-in-carbonic-acid-poisoning-by-w-ernest-thomson.md). The author identity and career are corroborated by the [British Journal of Ophthalmology obituary, 1938, p. 59, identifying the thesis and its publication](https://bjo.bmj.com/content/bjophthalmol/22/1/59.full.pdf).
