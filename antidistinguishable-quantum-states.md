# Antidistinguishable quantum states

↑ **Parent:** [Measurement in quantum mechanics](quantum-measurement-split.md)

A finite family of [quantum states](quantum-state.md) is antidistinguishable if a [measurement in quantum mechanics](quantum-measurement-split.md) has labelled outcomes such that each outcome has zero probability for its corresponding state, while the outcomes form a complete [measurement in quantum mechanics](quantum-measurement-split.md). It can exclude a state without identifying which state was prepared. The [PBR exclusion measurement for zero and plus](pbr-exclusion-measurement-for-zero-and-plus.md) supplies a four-state [projective measurement](projective-measurement.md) example.
