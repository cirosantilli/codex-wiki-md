# Eisenstein ideal

↑ **Parent:** [Modular Hecke algebra](modular-hecke-algebra.md)

The Eisenstein ideal in a [modular Hecke algebra](modular-hecke-algebra.md) imposes the eigenvalue relations of an Eisenstein series, such as $T_\ell-(1+\ell)$ in an appropriate weight-two trivial-character setting. Congruences modulo this ideal connect [modular forms](modular-form.md) and class-field constructions in the [Mazur-Wiles theorem](mazur-wiles-theorem.md). Other weights and characters require adjusted eigenvalues.
