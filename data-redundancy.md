# Data redundancy

↑ **Parent:** [Computer science](computer-science-split.md)

Storing information that is also derivable from other stored information. A [hard link](hard-link.md) count summarizes [directory entries](directory-entry.md), while a [block allocation bitmap](block-allocation-bitmap.md) summarizes block ownership in a [file system](file-system-split.md). Such summaries accelerate common operations and permit consistency comparisons; replicated data can additionally assist recovery. Every update must maintain the redundant relationship, or establish a protocol for detecting and repairing divergence.
