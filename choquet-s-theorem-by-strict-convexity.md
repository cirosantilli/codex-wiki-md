<h1 id="choquet-s-theorem-by-strict-convexity">Choquet's theorem by strict convexity</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Choquet theorem](choquet-theorem.md)

Maximize the integral of a continuous strictly convex [function](function-split.md) among [probability measures](probability-measure.md) with a fixed [barycenter](barycenter.md). The [supporting measure lemma for affine upper envelopes](supporting-measure-lemma-for-affine-upper-envelopes.md) implies that the maximizing [measure](measure.md) has zero integral of the nonnegative envelope gap. Strict convexity makes this gap positive at every nonextreme point, so the [measure](measure.md) is concentrated on the extreme boundary. Metrizability makes that boundary Borel and supplies the continuous strictly convex [function](function-split.md).
