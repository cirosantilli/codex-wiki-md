# Graph blow-up

↑ **Parent:** [Graph](graph-split.md)

A blow-up replaces each vertex of a [graph](graph-split.md) by an [independent set](independent-set-graph-theory.md), and each original edge by all edges between the corresponding two sets. Replacing every vertex of $K_r$ by $t$ vertices gives the [balanced complete multipartite blow-up](balanced-complete-multipartite-blow-up.md) $K_r(t)$.
