# Null geodesic incompleteness

↑ **Parent:** [Null geodesic completeness](null-geodesic-completeness.md)

[Null geodesic incompleteness](null-geodesic-incompleteness.md) means that some [maximal geodesic](maximal-geodesic.md) with a [null tangent](null-vector.md) has a finite endpoint of its [affine parameter](affine-parameter.md). This can indicate a removable missing region or a genuine obstruction; it does not alone assert curvature divergence.
