# Lorenz number

↑ **Parent:** [Wiedemann-Franz law](wiedemann-franz-law.md)

The [Lorenz number](lorenz-number.md) is the ratio $L=K_e/(\sigma T)$. The Sommerfeld value $L_0=(\pi^2/3)(k_B/e)^2\simeq2.44\times10^{-8}\,\mathrm{W\,\Omega\,K^{-2}}$ applies under the assumptions of the [Wiedemann-Franz law](wiedemann-franz-law.md). The ratio must use electronic [thermal conductivity](thermal-conductivity.md) if lattice heat flow is appreciable.
