# Vanishing of a finitely generated module near a prime

↑ **Parent:** [Localization of a module](localization-of-a-module.md)

For a [finitely generated module](finitely-generated-module.md) $M=(m_1,\ldots,m_t)$ and a [prime ideal](prime-ideal.md) $P$, if $M_P=0$, choose $s_i\notin P$ annihilating $m_i$. Their product $f\notin P$ annihilates all of $M$. Conversely such an $f$ becomes a [unit](unit-in-a-ring.md) in $R_P$, forcing $M_P=0$. Thus

$$
\{P:M_P=0\}=\bigcup_{f\in\operatorname{Ann}_R(M)}D(f)
=\operatorname{Spec}R\setminus V(\operatorname{Ann}_R(M)).
$$

This identifies the [support of a module](support-of-a-module.md) $M$ with the [Zariski-closed set](zariski-closed-set.md) $V(\operatorname{Ann}_R(M))$. For $M=0$, use $f=1$. No [Noetherian](noetherian-ring.md) assumption on $R$ is required.
