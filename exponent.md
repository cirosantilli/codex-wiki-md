# Exponent

↑ **Parent:** [Exponentiation](exponentiation.md)

An exponent specifies the power in [exponentiation](exponentiation.md): in $a^b$, $a$ is the base and $b$ is the exponent. For a positive [integer](integer.md) exponent, $2^3=2\cdot2\cdot2=8$. For a positive [real number](real-number.md) base, a real exponent is defined by $a^b=\exp(b\log a)$ using the [exponential function](exponential-function.md) and [natural logarithm](natural-logarithm.md). The [Lp norm](lp-norm.md) uses its exponent $p$ to determine how strongly large [absolute values](absolute-value.md) affect the result.
