# Conformal invariance of harmonic measure

↑ **Parent:** [Harmonic measure](harmonic-measure.md)

For a [conformal map](conformal-map.md) extending to the relevant boundary sets, compose the target [harmonic measure](harmonic-measure.md) with the map. The planar [Laplacian](laplacian.md) transforms by the positive factor $|f'|^2$, so the composition is a [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md) with the correct boundary values. Uniqueness of the [Dirichlet problem](dirichlet-problem.md) gives the displayed identity on regular domains, including the smooth-circle domains in the [harmonic measure of an annulus](harmonic-measure-of-an-annulus.md).
