# Rayleigh monotonicity principle

↑ **Parent:** [Effective resistance](effective-resistance.md)

Increasing edge resistances, including deleting edges, cannot decrease [effective resistance](effective-resistance.md). Decreasing resistances or identifying vertices cannot increase [effective resistance](effective-resistance.md). These statements also follow from the [Thomson principle](thomson-principle.md).
