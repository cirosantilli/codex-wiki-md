# Independent record indicators

↑ **Parent:** [Independent relative ranks of a uniform random permutation](independent-relative-ranks-of-a-uniform-random-permutation.md)

The indicators $Y_i=\mathbf1_{\{R_i=1\}}$ of successively smaller records are [independent](independent-random-variables.md) and have [Bernoulli distributions](bernoulli-distribution.md) of parameters $1/i$. Successively larger records use $R_i=i$ and have the same laws. For $K_n=\sum_{i=1}^nY_i$, [expectation](expected-value.md) and [variance additivity for independent random variables](variance-additivity-for-independent-random-variables.md) give $E K_n=\sum_{i=1}^n1/i$ and $\operatorname{Var}K_n=\sum_{i=1}^n(1/i-1/i^2)$.
