# Hubble diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Type Ia supernova cosmology](type-ia-supernova-cosmology.md)

A Hubble diagram plots a distance indicator, such as standardized [supernova](supernova.md) apparent magnitude or [distance modulus](distance-modulus.md), against redshift. The low-redshift relation sets a distance-scale intercept, while curvature of the relation at larger redshift tests the expansion history through [luminosity distance](luminosity-distance.md). A common unknown absolute magnitude remains degenerate with the [Hubble constant](hubble-constant.md); the diagram's redshift-dependent shape can still constrain other cosmological parameters.
