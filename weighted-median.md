# Weighted median

↑ **Parent:** [Median](median.md)

A [median](median.md) of a weighted [probability distribution](probability-distribution-split.md). With positive weights summing to one, at most half the weight lies strictly below a weighted median and at most half lies strictly above it. The [lower weighted median](lower-weighted-median.md) chooses the first observed value whose cumulative weight reaches one half; the convention matters when the cumulative mass is exactly one half.
