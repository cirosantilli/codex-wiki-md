# Unitor

↑ **Parent:** [Monoidal category](monoidal-category.md)

The natural isomorphisms $\lambda_X:I\otimes X\to X$ and $\rho_X:X\otimes I\to X$ in a [monoidal category](monoidal-category.md). Together with the [associator](associator.md) they satisfy the triangle axiom.
