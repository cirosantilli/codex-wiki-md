# Parity as odd-mode number parity

↑ **Parent:** [Parity operator](parity-operator.md)

For a free intrinsic-even [real scalar field](real-scalar-field.md), momentum reflection $Rf(\mathbf p)=f(-\mathbf p)$ is a self-adjoint involution on the one-particle space. Its odd [orthogonal projector](orthogonal-projection.md) is $(1-R)/2$. The corresponding [second quantization](second-quantization.md) is $N_{\rm odd}=\tfrac12\int d^3p/(2\pi)^3\,a_{\mathbf p}^\dagger(a_{\mathbf p}-a_{-\mathbf p})$, with integration over all [momentum space](momentum-space.md). Conjugation by $e^{i\pi N_{\rm odd}}$ fixes even modes and reverses odd modes, hence sends $a_{\mathbf p}$ to $a_{-\mathbf p}$ and implements the [parity operator](parity-operator.md). Its square is one because $N_{\rm odd}$ has integer [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md). The equivalent positive expression is $N_{\rm odd}=\tfrac14\int d^3p/(2\pi)^3\,(a_{\mathbf p}^\dagger-a_{-\mathbf p}^\dagger)(a_{\mathbf p}-a_{-\mathbf p})$.
