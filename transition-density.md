# Transition density

↑ **Parent:** [Markov kernel](markov-kernel.md)

A [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) for the conditional future state of a [Markov process](markov-process-split.md) given its current state. Integrating $p_t(x,y)$ over a measurable region gives its [transition probability](transition-probability.md). A [transition kernel](markov-kernel.md) need not admit a density; discrete or partly atomic transition laws require a different reference measure or a separate atomic part.
