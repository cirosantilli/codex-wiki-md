# Equal-rate rocket burning and accretion

↑ **Parent:** [Rocket equation with stationary mass accretion](rocket-equation-with-stationary-mass-accretion.md)

If stationary mass arrives at the same constant time rate as fuel is ejected, total rocket mass stays fixed. The [rocket equation with stationary mass accretion](rocket-equation-with-stationary-mass-accretion.md) is then $M_0\dot v+\alpha v=\alpha u$. For initial rest, speed approaches the relative exhaust speed exponentially with relaxation time $M_0/\alpha$. This is the regular equal-rate limit of the unequal-rate mass-power formula.
