# Administrative censoring with uniform entry

↑ **Parent:** [Administrative censoring](administrative-censoring.md)

If entry is uniform on calendar interval $[\tau_a,\tau_b]$ and follow-up stops at $\tau_c$, the censoring duration is uniform on $[\tau_c-\tau_b,\tau_c-\tau_a]$. Its [hazard function](hazard-function.md) diverges at the longest possible follow-up. It is [independent censoring](independent-censoring.md) when entry date is independent of prognosis after the required conditioning.
