# Toomre parameter

↑ **Parent:** [Toomre's stability criterion](toomre-s-stability-criterion.md)

For a razor-thin fluid disk with [sound speed](speed-of-sound.md) or effective random speed $c$, [surface density](surface-density-of-a-disk.md) $\Sigma$ and radial epicyclic frequency $\kappa$, $Q=c\kappa/(\pi G\Sigma)$ compares pressure and rotational support with [self-gravity](self-gravity.md). The local axisymmetric fluid [Toomre stability criterion](toomre-s-stability-criterion.md) is $Q\geq1$. For a [Keplerian shearing sheet](keplerian-shearing-sheet.md), $\kappa=\Omega$. Collisionless stellar disks use a different numerical normalization; gas drag can permit secular growth even when this fluid parameter exceeds unity.
