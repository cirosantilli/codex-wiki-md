# Lifting a short modular progression to an integer progression

↑ **Parent:** [Arithmetic progression](arithmetic-progression.md)

If all residues of a modular [arithmetic progression](arithmetic-progression.md) have integer representatives in an interval of diameter less than half the modulus, the representatives form an integer [arithmetic progression](arithmetic-progression.md). Consecutive differences lie in an interval shorter than the modulus, so the common modular difference has a unique possible integer representative.
