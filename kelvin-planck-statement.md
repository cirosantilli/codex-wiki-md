# Kelvin-Planck statement

↑ **Parent:** [Second law of thermodynamics](second-law-of-thermodynamics-split.md)

No cyclic device can have as its sole effect the extraction of [heat](heat-split.md) from one reservoir and its complete conversion into [work](work.md). If the reservoir loses heat $Q>0$ at [temperature](temperature-split.md) $T$, its [entropy](entropy-split.md) change is $-Q/T$ while the cyclic device returns to its starting state. A sole-effect conversion would therefore reduce total entropy.
