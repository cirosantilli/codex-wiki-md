# Appraisal theory

↑ **Parent:** [Emotion](emotion.md)

A family of accounts in which evaluation of events in relation to goals, well-being and coping possibilities shapes [emotion](emotion.md). [Cognitive appraisal](cognitive-appraisal.md) can be fast and nonverbal. A theory requiring appraisal for every emotional episode must specify the appraisal process independently enough to permit a counterexample.
