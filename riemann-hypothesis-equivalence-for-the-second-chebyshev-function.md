# Riemann hypothesis equivalence for the second Chebyshev function

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann hypothesis](riemann-hypothesis.md)

The [Riemann hypothesis](riemann-hypothesis.md) is equivalent to

$$
\psi(x)=x+O_\epsilon(x^{1/2+\epsilon})
$$

for every $\epsilon>0$. In fact, the hypothesis and the [Riemann–von Mangoldt explicit formula](riemann-von-mangoldt-explicit-formula.md) give the stronger $O(x^{1/2}(\log x)^2)$ bound. Conversely, the stated error continues $-\zeta'/\zeta-s/(s-1)$ holomorphically to $\Re s>1/2$, excluding zeros there; the functional equation supplies the other half.
