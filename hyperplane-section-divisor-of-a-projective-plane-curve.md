# Hyperplane-section divisor of a projective plane curve

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperplane section](hyperplane-section.md)

If a [projective line](projective-line.md) $H$ does not contain the [projective plane curve](projective-plane-curve.md) $X$, its hyperplane-section divisor is

$$
[X\cap H]=\sum_{p\in X\cap H}I_p(X,H)p,
$$

where $I_p(X,H)$ is the [intersection multiplicity](intersection-multiplicity.md). The [Bézout theorem](bezout-s-theorem.md) gives $\deg[X\cap H]=(\deg X)(\deg H)=\deg X$.
