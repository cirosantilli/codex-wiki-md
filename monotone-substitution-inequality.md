# Monotone substitution inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Change of variables formula](change-of-variables-formula.md)

For a nondecreasing real function $z$ on an interval $I$ and nonnegative [measurable function](measurable-function.md) $h$,

$$
\int_I h(z(u))z^{\prime}(u)\,du\leq\int_{\mathbb R}h(s)\,ds.
$$

The pushforward of $z^{\prime}(u)\,du$ is dominated by [Lebesgue measure](lebesgue-measure.md): on each interval, the integral of the derivative is at most the increase of $z$. This handles jumps and singular parts without a smoothness assumption.
