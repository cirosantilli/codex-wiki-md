# Localized forcing determines WKB phase constants

↑ **Parent:** [WKB approximation](wkb-approximation.md)

For a slowly varying oscillator with rapidly decaying forcing, first construct the localized particular solution $u_p$. Apply the [initial conditions](initial-condition.md) to the residual $u_h$ to determine its [WKB approximation](wkb-approximation.md) amplitude and phase constants. Ignoring the early forcing because it later becomes small loses the permanent free oscillations it excites. In $u''+4e^{-2\varepsilon t}u=8e^{-2t}$, the particular expansion is $u_p=e^{-2t}[1+\varepsilon(t+1/2)]+O(\varepsilon^2)$, whose initial value shifts the first homogeneous cosine coefficient by $-\varepsilon/2$.
