<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The low-latitude regions of [Earth](../../../../earth.md) absorb more incoming solar [radiation](../../../../radiation.md) than they emit as outgoing [thermal radiation](../../../../thermal-radiation.md), whereas high latitudes have a net annual [radiation](../../../../radiation.md) deficit. The obliquity of solar incidence and the distribution of daylight create a strong latitude dependence of absorbed solar [energy](../../../../energy-split.md); emitted [thermal radiation](../../../../thermal-radiation.md) varies less strongly because terrestrial [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) contrasts are smaller. The [terrestrial atmosphere](../../../../terrestrial-atmosphere.md) and [ocean](../../../../ocean.md) therefore carry [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) from the tropical surplus toward the polar deficits. Otherwise the low latitudes would keep warming and the high latitudes cooling until a different local [radiative equilibrium](../../../../radiative-equilibrium.md) was reached.

For a steady, zonally averaged [energy balance](../../../../energy-balance.md), let $R(\phi)$ be net downward radiation at the top of the [terrestrial atmosphere](../../../../terrestrial-atmosphere.md) and $H(\phi)$ the total northward [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) transport across latitude $\phi$. A narrow spherical band and [conservation of energy](../../../../conservation-of-energy.md) give

$$
\frac{dH}{d\phi}=2\pi a^2\cos\phi\,R(\phi),\qquad
H(\phi)=2\pi a^2\int_{-\pi/2}^{\phi}R(\varphi)\cos\varphi\,d\varphi.
$$

The poleward direction is northward in the Northern Hemisphere and southward in the Southern Hemisphere. Seasonal [internal energy](../../../../internal-energy-split.md) storage modifies the instantaneous balance, but not the annual need for poleward redistribution.

Several circulation structures contribute in addition to the transient [eddies](../../../../eddy.md) produced by [baroclinic instability](../../../../baroclinic-instability.md) of the middle latitudes:

- The [Hadley cells](../../../../hadley-cell.md), mainly between the equator and about $30^\circ$ latitude, export [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) in their upper poleward branches and return air equatorward near the surface. The transported quantity includes [enthalpy](../../../../enthalpy-split.md), [gravitational potential energy](../../../../gravitational-energy.md) and [latent heat](../../../../latent-heat.md); the upper and lower branches have different thermal properties. The cross-equatorial [Hadley cell](../../../../hadley-cell.md) is especially important in the seasonal cycle.
- Quasi-stationary [Rossby waves](../../../../rossby-wave.md), associated with mountains and continental heating contrasts, carry [heat](../../../../heat-split.md) in the extratropical [terrestrial atmosphere](../../../../terrestrial-atmosphere.md). Their meridional [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) and [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) anomalies can have a poleward correlation. These are an additional time-mean contribution alongside travelling weather disturbances.
- The [ocean gyres](../../../../ocean-gyre.md) in tropical and subtropical basins redistribute warm water poleward in strong [western boundary currents](../../../../western-boundary-current.md) and return cooler water equatorward over broader regions. The thermal difference between the branches produces net [heat transport](../../../../heat-transport.md) despite little net meridional volume transport.
- The [oceanic overturning circulation](../../../../oceanic-overturning-circulation.md), including the [Atlantic meridional overturning circulation](../../../../atlantic-meridional-overturning-circulation.md), combines warm upper water moving poleward with colder deep return water. It transports substantial [heat](../../../../heat-split.md) through the tropical and subtropical Atlantic toward the northern high latitudes. Wind forcing, [buoyancy](../../../../buoyancy.md) exchange and mixing all help maintain this global circulation.

The [Brewer-Dobson circulation](../../../../brewer-dobson-circulation.md) additionally redistributes [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) in the [stratosphere](../../../../stratosphere.md), with tropical ascent and extratropical descent, although most total atmospheric [heat transport](../../../../heat-transport.md) occurs lower down. An [Eulerian mean](../../../../eulerian-mean-flow.md) middle-latitude [Ferrel cell](../../../../ferrel-cell.md) is thermally indirect and can oppose the poleward transport by [eddies](../../../../eddy.md); listing every meridional circulation as a direct poleward heat carrier would be incorrect.
