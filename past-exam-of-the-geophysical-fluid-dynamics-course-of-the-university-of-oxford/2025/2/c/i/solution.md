<h1 id="2/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Northern high-latitude cooling removes [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) from surface water and raises its [density](../../../../../density.md). If the surface water becomes denser than the water below, [convection](../../../../../convection-split.md) mixes it downward and forms dense water that can be exported in the deep southward branch of the [Atlantic meridional overturning circulation](../../../../../atlantic-meridional-overturning-circulation.md). This [water-mass transformation](../../../../../water-mass-transformation.md) helps maintain the contrast represented by $\alpha\Delta T$ in 2(a). Freshening can oppose it by reducing [density](../../../../../density.md) and increasing stable [stratification](../../../../../density-stratification.md). Local [convection](../../../../../convection-split.md) and the basin-integrated sinking [volume flux](../../../../../volumetric-flow-rate.md) are related but are not the same process: export and the return path also depend on the large-scale dynamics.
