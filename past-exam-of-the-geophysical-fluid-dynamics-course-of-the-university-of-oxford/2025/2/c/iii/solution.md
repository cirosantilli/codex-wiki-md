↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Southern Ocean westerly [wind stress](../../../../../wind-stress.md) drives northward surface [Ekman transport](../../../../../ekman-transport.md) in the Southern Hemisphere. Its divergence and the circumpolar geometry allow deep water to rise towards the surface along tilted [isopycnals](../../../../../isopycnal.md), providing an important return route for the deep branch of the [Atlantic meridional overturning circulation](../../../../../atlantic-meridional-overturning-circulation.md). Surface [heat fluxes](../../../../../heat-flux-density.md) and freshwater fluxes then transform the upwelled water before it enters shallower return pathways. The winds supply mechanical [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md); the overturning cannot be represented entirely by a local northern [density](../../../../../density.md) difference. [Eddy-induced overturning](../../../../../eddy-induced-overturning.md) opposes part of the wind-driven contribution, so the net [water-mass transformation](../../../../../water-mass-transformation.md) and transport need not increase in direct proportion to the [wind stress](../../../../../wind-stress.md).[Alfred Wegener Institute, Southern Ocean overturning circulation: wind-driven upwelling and its opposition by mesoscale eddies](https://www.awi.de/en/science/climate-sciences/physical-oceanography/main-research-focus/southern-ocean-overturning-circulation.html)
