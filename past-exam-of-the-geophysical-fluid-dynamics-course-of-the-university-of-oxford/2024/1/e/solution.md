↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

**Kelvin waves:** weakened [trade winds](../../../../trade-winds.md), or a westerly wind burst, excite a downwelling [equatorial Kelvin wave](../../../../equatorial-kelvin-wave.md). Its positive upper-layer thickness anomaly travels eastward and deepens the eastern Pacific [thermocline](../../../../thermocline.md). [Upwelling](../../../../upwelling.md) then entrains warmer water, favouring eastern Pacific surface warming and the development of [El Niño](../../../../el-nino.md). This is a propagating adjustment of the [thermocline](../../../../thermocline.md), rather than transport of an unchanged parcel of warm water across the entire basin.[Box 2, “Equatorial Waves”, and the adjoining discussion of eastern thermocline depression, printed page 358](../../../../el-nino-and-la-nina-causes-and-global-consequences-by-michael-j-mcphaden.md)

**Gravity waves:** changed tropical [convection](../../../../convection-split.md) and its [latent heat](../../../../latent-heat.md) release force atmospheric [internal gravity waves](../../../../internal-wave.md). Their rapid propagation spreads the associated [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md) and [buoyancy](../../../../buoyancy.md) disturbances, allowing the tropical [terrestrial atmosphere](../../../../terrestrial-atmosphere.md) to adjust to the shifted heating and contributing to the divergent circulation and surface wind response coupled to the ocean. This is one atmospheric adjustment mechanism; it does not assert that all wind variability during a particular [El Niño](../../../../el-nino.md) is caused by this wave family.

**Rossby waves:** wind forcing during [El Niño](../../../../el-nino.md) also launches westward [equatorial Rossby waves](../../../../equatorial-rossby-wave.md) with a shoaling [thermocline](../../../../thermocline.md) signal. Reflection at the western Pacific boundary can produce an eastward upwelling [equatorial Kelvin wave](../../../../equatorial-kelvin-wave.md), which shoals the eastern [thermocline](../../../../thermocline.md) and promotes cooling. This supplies a delayed negative feedback that can help terminate the event.[Printed pages 358–359, reflection of the western thermocline-elevation signal and the return of surface cooling](../../../../el-nino-and-la-nina-causes-and-global-consequences-by-michael-j-mcphaden.md) These examples describe mechanisms relevant to events such as 2023/24, rather than claiming an event-specific attribution from the exam data.
