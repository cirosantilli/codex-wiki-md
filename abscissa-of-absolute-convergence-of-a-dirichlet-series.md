# Abscissa of absolute convergence of a Dirichlet series

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet series](dirichlet-series.md)

[absolute convergence](absolute-convergence.md) of a [Dirichlet series](dirichlet-series.md) depends only on the [real part](real-part.md) of its variable. The set of real parts giving convergence is upward closed by the [comparison test](direct-comparison-test.md). If that set is nonempty and not all of the real line, its finite [infimum](infimum.md) is the abscissa: every larger real part gives convergence, and every smaller real part gives divergence. Boundary convergence is a separate question.
