# Repeated-measures analysis of variance

↑ **Parent:** [Analysis of variance](analysis-of-variance.md)

An [analysis of variance](analysis-of-variance.md) for a design containing repeated observations on the same experimental units, with an appropriate within-unit error structure. It differs from treating every recorded response as independent. The allocation and covariance assumptions, not just the factor count, determine the applicable tests.
