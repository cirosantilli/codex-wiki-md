# Vanishing of weight-two level-one modular forms

↑ **Parent:** [Valence formula for the modular group](valence-formula-for-the-modular-group.md)

A weight-two [modular form](modular-form.md) obeys $f(i)=i^2f(i)=-f(i)$, so it vanishes at $i$. The [valence formula for the modular group](valence-formula-for-the-modular-group.md) would then have left side at least $1/2$, but right side $2/12=1/6$. All orders are nonnegative by holomorphy, giving a contradiction for nonzero $f$.
