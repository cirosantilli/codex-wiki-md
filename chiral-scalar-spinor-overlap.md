# Chiral scalar spinor overlap

↑ **Parent:** [Chiral projector](chiral-projector.md)

A [Lorentz scalar](lorentz-scalar.md) Dirac bilinear connects opposite chiralities. In the stated on-shell normalization $\bar u_Ru_L=m_{\rm spinor}$, whereas $\bar u_Lu_L=0$. Such an overlap verifies the allowed Lorentz structure but does not determine an independent coefficient multiplying a different [mass](mass.md) operator.
