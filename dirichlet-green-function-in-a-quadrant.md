# Dirichlet Green function in a quadrant

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet Green function](dirichlet-green-function.md)

For the positive Laplacian convention $\Delta G=\delta$, the [method of images](method-of-images.md) in the first quadrant reflects an interior pole $(a,b)$ across each axis with negative sign and across both with positive sign:

$$
G_Q(x,y;a,b)=\frac1{2\pi}\left[\log|(x,y)-(a,b)|-\log|(x,y)-(-a,b)|-\log|(x,y)-(a,-b)|+\log|(x,y)-(-a,-b)|\right].
$$

The [Dirichlet Green function](dirichlet-green-function.md) vanishes on both axes. Its outward normal derivative on the horizontal axis is

$$
\partial_nG_Q(t,0;a,b)=\frac{4abt}{\pi[(t-a)^2+b^2][(t+a)^2+b^2]},
$$

and the vertical-axis kernel follows by exchange of coordinates. [Green second identity](green-second-identity.md) converts these derivatives into the quadrant [Poisson integral](poisson-integral.md) for boundary data.
