# COPI

↑ **Parent:** [Membrane transport](membrane-transport.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COPI)

[COPI](copi.md) is a coat system involved in retrieval toward the [endoplasmic reticulum](endoplasmic-reticulum.md) and traffic associated with the [Golgi apparatus](golgi-apparatus.md). Its coatomer assembly and ARF-family recruitment differ from [COPII](copii.md), while sharing membrane recruitment and cargo-selection principles.
