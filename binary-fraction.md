# Binary fraction

↑ **Parent:** [Binary expansion](binary-expansion.md)

A base-two positional [fraction](fraction.md) with a binary point separating nonnegative and negative powers of two. For example, $10.101_2=2+1/2+1/8=21/8$. A finite expansion is a [dyadic rational](dyadic-rational.md): with $F$ fractional places, its value is an [integer](integer-split.md) divided by $2^F$. Conversely, any nonnegative [reduced fraction](reduced-fraction.md) whose denominator is a power of two has a finite [binary expansion](binary-expansion.md). Leading [integer](integer-split.md) zeroes and trailing fractional zeroes do not change its value.
