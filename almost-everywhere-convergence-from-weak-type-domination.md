# Almost-everywhere convergence from weak-type domination

↑ **Parent:** [Weak type (1,1)](weak-type-1-1.md)

Suppose a family of [linear operators](linear-operator.md), including its limiting operator, is pointwise dominated by a [sublinear operator](sublinear-operator.md) of [weak type (1,1)](weak-type-1-1.md). Convergence [almost everywhere](almost-everywhere.md) on an $L^1$-dense subspace then extends to every $L^1$ input. Approximate the input by errors of [norm](norm.md) at most $2^{-2n}$. The weak estimate and the [Borel-Cantelli first lemma](borel-cantelli-first-lemma.md) make the corresponding domination errors tend to zero [almost everywhere](almost-everywhere.md). [Linearity](linearity.md) bounds the difference between the full and approximating convergence errors by twice that domination error. This argument does not need an uncountable supremum to be measurable.
