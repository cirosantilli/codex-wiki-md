# Delayed psychological distress

↑ **Parent:** [Psychological resilience trajectory](psychological-resilience-trajectory.md)

Increasing difficulty that becomes evident later rather than immediately after an event. Sparse follow-up or selective attrition can hide such patterns or make their apparent onset misleading.
