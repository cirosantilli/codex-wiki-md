# Mixed mathematics

↑ **Parent:** [Natural philosophy](natural-philosophy.md)

Early modern mathematical disciplines applied to physical or practical objects, including [astronomy](astronomy-split.md), [optics](optics-split.md) and [mechanics](mechanics.md). The social standing and explanatory claims of practitioners varied; this is not simply a synonym for the present administrative category of applied [mathematics](mathematics-split.md).
