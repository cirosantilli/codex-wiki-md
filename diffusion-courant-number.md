# Diffusion Courant number

↑ **Parent:** [Courant number](courant-number.md)

The diffusion [Courant number](courant-number.md) is $r=\nu k/d^2$ for diffusivity $\nu$, time step $k$ and spatial step $d$. Keeping $r$ fixed is a [parabolic mesh refinement](parabolic-mesh-refinement.md). For the [Forward Euler diffusion scheme](forward-euler-diffusion-scheme.md) it is bounded by $1/2$ on a uniform one-dimensional grid; the [Backward Euler diffusion scheme](backward-euler-diffusion-scheme.md) has no positive upper stability restriction.
