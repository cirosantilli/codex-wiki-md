# Hartree equations

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum mechanics](quantum-mechanics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hartree_equations)

The [Hartree equations](hartree-equations.md) are self-consistent one-particle equations obtained by approximating a many-body [wave function](wave-function-split.md) by a [tensor product](tensor-product.md) of orbital [wave functions](wave-function-split.md). Each orbital sees the potential averaged over the other particles' [probability densities](probability-density.md); attractive Newtonian mean-field variants include the [Gravitational Hartree equation](gravitational-hartree-equation.md).
