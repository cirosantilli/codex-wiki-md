# Unit-magnitude scalar diffusion has Brownian law

↑ **Parent:** [Lévy characterization of Brownian motion](levy-characterization-of-brownian-motion.md)

A [progressively measurable](progressive-measurability.md) real diffusion coefficient of absolute value one gives a [continuous local martingale](continuous-local-martingale.md) with [quadratic variation](quadratic-variation.md) $\int_0^t\sigma_s^2ds=t$. With zero initial value, [Lévy characterization of Brownian motion](levy-characterization-of-brownian-motion.md) makes the solution standard [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md). This applies to every [weak solution](weak-solution.md) of $dX_t=\operatorname{sgn}(X_t-a)dB_t$. The Brownian law does not imply that the solution equals the given driver or establish strong existence for a discontinuous coefficient.
