# Vector bundle endomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Vector bundle morphism](vector-bundle-morphism.md)

A smooth [vector bundle morphism](vector-bundle-morphism.md) from a [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) $E$ to itself, covering the identity on the base, is a [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) endomorphism. It is fibrewise linear and is equivalently a smooth section of $\operatorname{End}E$. In a local frame its matrix is smooth and changes by conjugation under changes of frame. An [almost complex structure](almost-complex-manifold.md) is a real endomorphism of the [tangent bundle](tangent-bundle.md) satisfying $J^2=-I$.
