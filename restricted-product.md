# Restricted product

↑ **Parent:** [Adele ring](adele-ring.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Restricted_product)

Given spaces $X_i$ with distinguished open subsets $U_i$, their restricted product consists of tuples $(x_i)$ for which $x_i\in U_i$ at all but finitely many indices.
