# Convective available potential energy

↑ **Parent:** [Buoyancy](buoyancy.md)

[Convective available potential energy](convective-available-potential-energy.md) is the positive [buoyancy](buoyancy.md) work per unit mass available to a rising [air parcel](air-parcel.md), $\mathrm{CAPE}=\int B\,dz$ over positive-buoyancy layers above free convection. In the ideal lossless parcel model $w_{\max}=\sqrt{2\,\mathrm{CAPE}}$. Negative-buoyancy work is treated separately as convective inhibition.
