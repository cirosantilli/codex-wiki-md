# Orthogonal shape and scale for a generalized Pareto distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Generalized Pareto distribution](generalized-pareto-distribution.md)

For the positive-shape [generalized Pareto distribution](generalized-pareto-distribution.md) with shape $\psi>0$ and scale $\sigma>0$, the expected [Fisher information matrix](fisher-information-matrix.md) for one observation is

$$
I(\psi,\sigma)=\frac1{1+2\psi}\begin{pmatrix}2/(1+\psi)&1/[\sigma(1+\psi)]\\1/[\sigma(1+\psi)]&1/\sigma^2\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The [orthogonalization equation for two statistical parameters](orthogonalization-equation-for-two-statistical-parameters.md) gives $\partial_\psi\sigma=-\sigma/(1+\psi)$ at fixed new nuisance coordinate. Thus $\eta=\sigma(1+\psi)$ is a global [interest-respecting reparametrization](interest-respecting-reparametrization.md). In coordinates $(\psi,\eta)$, the information is diagonal with entries $(1+\psi)^{-2}$ and $[\eta^2(1+2\psi)]^{-1}$.
