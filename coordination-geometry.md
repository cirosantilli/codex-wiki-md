# Coordination geometry

↑ **Parent:** [Coordination chemistry](coordination-chemistry.md)

The spatial arrangement of donor atoms around a central atom. [coordination number](coordination-number.md) counts neighbours but does not by itself distinguish geometries with the same number of donors.
