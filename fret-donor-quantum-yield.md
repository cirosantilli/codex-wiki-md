# FRET donor quantum yield

↑ **Parent:** [Förster distance](forster-distance.md)

The [FRET donor quantum yield](fret-donor-quantum-yield.md) is the emitted [fluorescence](fluorescence.md) [photon](photon.md) fraction per absorbed excitation [photon](photon.md), measured without a [FRET acceptor](fret-acceptor.md). In the constant-rate model used for [FRET efficiency](fret-efficiency.md), $Q_D=k_{\rm r}/(k_{\rm r}+k_{\rm nr})$. Changing the [FRET donor quantum yield](fret-donor-quantum-yield.md) changes the [Förster distance](forster-distance.md), even if label positions remain fixed.
