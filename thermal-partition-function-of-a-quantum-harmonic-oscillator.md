# Thermal partition function of a quantum harmonic oscillator

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator.md)

For a [quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator.md) with $\hbar=1$, $\omega>0$ and [inverse temperature](inverse-temperature.md) $\beta>0$, the [canonical partition function](canonical-partition-function.md) is $\sum_{r\ge0}e^{-\beta\omega(r+1/2)}$. The [geometric series](geometric-series.md) gives the displayed formula. Keeping the [zero-point energy](zero-point-energy.md) is essential to its prefactor.
