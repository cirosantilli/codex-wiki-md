# Polynya

↑ **Parent:** [Sea ice](sea-ice.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polynya)

A polynya is a persistent or recurrent area of open water or relatively thin ice within surrounding [sea ice](sea-ice.md). Wind can maintain it by transporting ice away; an oceanic [heat flux](heat-flux-density.md) can prevent freezing. Open-water width supplies an [open-water fetch](wave-fetch.md) over which wind waves can grow.
