# Adsorption confinement free energy

↑ **Parent:** [Adsorption](adsorption.md)

The translational [Helmholtz free energy](helmholtz-free-energy.md) cost of restricting a dilute [ideal gas](ideal-gas-split.md) from a volume $V$ to an [area](surface-area.md) $A$ at the same [temperature](temperature-split.md). For identical particle number and [mass](mass.md),

$$
F_{2D}-F_{3D}=-Nk_{\rm B}T\ln\frac{A/\Lambda^2}{V/\Lambda^3}
=Nk_{\rm B}T\ln\frac{V}{A\Lambda}.
$$

Here $\Lambda$ is the [thermal de Broglie wavelength](thermal-de-broglie-wavelength.md). This contribution is positive for $V/A\gg\Lambda$; a favourable binding [energy](energy-split.md) must be added separately to predict net [adsorption](adsorption.md).
