# Orthogonalization of a transplantation matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Transplantation theorem](transplantation-theorem.md)

Suppose an invertible real matrix $C$ intertwines symmetric orthogonal tile-reflection matrices: $CM_s=N_sC$. Then $C^TC$ commutes with every $M_s$, so its positive inverse square root does too. The [polar decomposition of an invertible real matrix](polar-decomposition-of-an-invertible-real-matrix.md) therefore gives an orthogonal matrix $O=C(C^TC)^{-1/2}$ satisfying $OM_s=N_sO$. On a disjoint union of congruent tiles, $O$ preserves both the $L^2$ [inner product](inner-product.md) and [Dirichlet energy](dirichlet-energy.md). The intertwining identities preserve the face trace conditions defining the assembled [Sobolev space](sobolev-space-split.md), so $O$ gives a [unitary equivalence](unitary-equivalence.md) of the assembled [Laplacians](laplacian.md).
