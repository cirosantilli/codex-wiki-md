# Mechanical equilibrium

↑ **Parent:** [Thermodynamic equilibrium](thermodynamic-equilibrium.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanical_equilibrium)

Mechanical equilibrium requires [force balance](force-balance.md) for the mechanical displacements that are allowed. Two uniform fluids separated by a freely moving, unloaded, frictionless piston with equal face [areas](surface-area.md) require equal [pressures](pressure-split.md). A supported piston can sustain a [pressure](pressure-split.md) difference, and a fluid in [gravity](gravity.md) instead obeys [hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md). For an upward height coordinate $z$, that balance is $dp/dz=-\rho g$, where $\rho$ is [mass density](density.md) and $g$ is the downward gravitational acceleration. Mechanical equilibrium alone does not establish [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium-split.md) or [chemical equilibrium](chemical-equilibrium.md).
