# Pointwise product of arithmetic functions

↑ **Parent:** [Arithmetic function](arithmetic-function-split.md)

The [pointwise product of arithmetic functions](pointwise-product-of-arithmetic-functions.md) multiplies two [arithmetic functions](arithmetic-function-split.md) at the same input: $(f\cdot g)(n)=f(n)g(n)$. Thus $(\mathbf1\cdot\mathbf1)(12)=1$ and $(\operatorname{id}\cdot\mathbf1)(12)=12$, using the [constant-one arithmetic function](constant-one-arithmetic-function.md) and the [identity arithmetic function](identity-arithmetic-function.md). These differ from the corresponding [Dirichlet convolution](dirichlet-convolution.md) values $6$ and $28$, because [Dirichlet convolution](dirichlet-convolution.md) sums over every positive [integer divisor](divisor.md).
