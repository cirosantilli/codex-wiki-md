# Odd-even comparison for Feigenbaum partition lengths

↑ **Parent:** [Feigenbaum geometric potential](feigenbaum-geometric-potential.md)

Odd-address intervals lie in the first image of the central restrictive interval, away from the critical point. The derivative of the [Feigenbaum map](feigenbaum-period-doubling-map.md) is bounded above and away from zero there, so the next even interval has comparable length by the [mean value theorem](mean-value-theorem.md). For the wraparound image, $g^{2^n}(\beta^n x)=\beta^ng(x)$ makes its length a fixed fraction $(1-\beta)/2$ of the central interval. Thus the odd and full partition length-power sums differ only by constant factors for each fixed real exponent, and have the same exponential growth rate.
