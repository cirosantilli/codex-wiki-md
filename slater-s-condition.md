<h1 id="slater-s-condition">Slater's condition</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Strong duality](strong-duality.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slater's_condition)

The Slater condition requires a feasible point at which every nonlinear convex inequality is strict and every affine equality holds. For a convex problem it implies [strong duality](strong-duality.md) and attainment of the dual optimum under standard finiteness assumptions.
