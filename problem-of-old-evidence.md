# Problem of old evidence

↑ **Parent:** [Bayesian confirmation](bayesian-confirmation.md)

Already accepted evidence can support a newly considered theory even though conditioning again on a fact of [probability](probability.md) one produces no increase in its [posterior probability](posterior-probability.md). Distinguish new learning, retrospective support and discovering a previously unrecognized theory–evidence connection.[chapter 3 of Theory and Evidence](why-i-am-not-a-bayesian-by-clark-glymour.md)
