# Quadratic-form ellipse area

↑ **Parent:** [Ellipse](ellipse.md)

For a two-dimensional [symmetric positive-definite matrix](symmetric-positive-definite-matrix.md) $M$ and $R>0$, the region $\mathbf x^TM\mathbf x\leq R$ is a filled [ellipse](ellipse.md) of [area](surface-area.md) $\pi R/\sqrt{\det M}$. By the [spectral theorem for real symmetric matrices](spectral-theorem-for-real-symmetric-matrices.md), an [orthogonal change of coordinates](orthogonal-change-of-coordinates.md) gives positive [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $\lambda_1,\lambda_2$ and semiaxes $\sqrt{R/\lambda_1}$ and $\sqrt{R/\lambda_2}$. Their product and the [area of an ellipse](area-of-an-ellipse.md) prove the formula. Translating the [center of an ellipse](center-of-an-ellipse.md) leaves the [area](surface-area.md) unchanged.
