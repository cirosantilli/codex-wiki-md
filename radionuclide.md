# Radionuclide

↑ **Parent:** [Radioactive decay](radioactive-decay.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radionuclide)

A radionuclide is a species of [atomic nucleus](atomic-nucleus-split.md) with an unstable nuclear state that undergoes [radioactive decay](radioactive-decay.md). A sample's expected surviving population decreases with its [nuclear decay constant](nuclear-decay-constant.md). [Carbon-14](carbon-14.md) is the [radioisotope](radionuclide.md) used in [radiocarbon dating](radiocarbon-dating.md).
