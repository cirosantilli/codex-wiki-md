# Discrete harmonic function

↑ **Parent:** [Harmonic function](harmonic-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discrete_harmonic_function)

A real function $h$ on the [vertices of a graph](vertex-graph-theory.md) of an unweighted [locally finite graph](locally-finite-graph.md) is a [discrete harmonic function](discrete-harmonic-function.md) at $u$ when $\deg(u)h(u)=\sum_{v\sim u}h(v)$, equivalently $Lh(u)=0$ for the [Graph Laplacian](laplacian-matrix.md). For positive degree this says its value is the average over its [graph neighbours](neighbour-of-a-vertex.md); at an isolated [vertex of a graph](vertex-graph-theory.md) the Laplacian condition is vacuous. On an [electrical network](electrical-network.md) the corresponding equation uses conductance weights and the [Weighted graph Laplacian](weighted-graph-laplacian.md). [Voltages](voltage.md) away from sources and sinks, and hitting [probabilities](probability.md) away from their prescribed boundaries, are [discrete harmonic functions](discrete-harmonic-function.md).
