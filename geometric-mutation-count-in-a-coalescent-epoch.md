# Geometric mutation count in a coalescent epoch

↑ **Parent:** [Mutation count on a neutral coalescent tree](mutation-count-on-a-neutral-coalescent-tree.md)

Integrating the [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md) [probability generating function](probability-generating-function.md) over the [exponential distribution](exponential-distribution.md) of the epoch length gives

$$
\mathbb E[s^{Y_j}]=\frac{j-1}{j-1+\theta(1-s)}.
$$

Thus $Y_j$ has a [geometric distribution](geometric-distribution.md) on $\{0,1,\ldots\}$, with [expectation](expected-value.md) $\theta/(j-1)$ and [variance](variance-split.md) $\theta/(j-1)+\theta^2/(j-1)^2$. The support convention matters: the count includes zero mutations.
