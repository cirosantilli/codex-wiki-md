# Vertex of a polytope

↑ **Parent:** [Convex polytope](convex-polytope.md)

A vertex of a [convex polytope](convex-polytope.md) is an [extreme point](extreme-point.md): it cannot be expressed as a nontrivial convex combination of two distinct points in that polytope. In a full-dimensional simple polytope of dimension $n$, a vertex lies on exactly $n$ incident [facets](facet.md).
