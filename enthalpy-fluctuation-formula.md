# Enthalpy fluctuation formula

↑ **Parent:** [Isothermal-isobaric ensemble](isothermal-isobaric-ensemble.md)

With temperature-independent microstate enthalpies and measure, differentiating the normalized weight $e^{-\beta H}/\Delta$ gives $\partial_\beta\langle H\rangle=-\operatorname{Var}(H)$. Since $d\beta/dT=-1/(k_BT^2)$, the displayed response follows. A stable finite-system equilibrium distribution therefore has nonnegative [heat capacity at constant pressure](heat-capacity-at-constant-pressure.md) in this setting.
