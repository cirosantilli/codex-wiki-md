# Frost heave

↑ **Parent:** [Premelting](premelting.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frost_heave)

Frost heave is displacement caused by segregated [ice](ice.md) growth supplied by liquid flow, rather than solely by the expansion of water on [freezing](freezing.md). A [premelting](premelting.md) film allows liquid access beneath the [ice](ice.md); [thermomolecular pressure](thermomolecular-pressure.md) draws supply against viscous resistance and load.
