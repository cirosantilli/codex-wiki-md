# Geodesic in the Poincare half-plane model

↑ **Parent:** [Poincaré half-plane model](poincare-half-plane-model.md)

The geodesics in the upper half-plane are vertical lines and semicircles centred on the boundary axis. Indeed, the length integrand $\sqrt{1+y'^2}/y$ has the [Beltrami identity](beltrami-identity.md)

$$
\frac1{y\sqrt{1+y'^2}}=\frac1R,
$$

which integrates to $(x-c)^2+y^2=R^2$. Swapping the coordinate axes gives circles centred on the vertical boundary for the metric $(dx^2+dy^2)/x^2$.
