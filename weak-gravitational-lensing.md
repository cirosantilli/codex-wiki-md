# Weak gravitational lensing

↑ **Parent:** [Gravitational lensing](gravitational-lensing-split.md)

When [gravitational lensing](gravitational-lensing-split.md) gives small angular distortions without generic multiple imaging, its deflection field changes source positions, magnification and shear. Angular derivatives of the lensing potential determine these distortions. Ensemble galaxy-shape distortions and [cosmic microwave background](cosmic-microwave-background-split.md) remapping probe the foreground gravitational field.
