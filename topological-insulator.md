# Topological insulator

↑ **Parent:** [Topological quantum matter](topological-quantum-matter-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topological_insulator)

A [topological insulator](topological-insulator.md) has an insulating bulk electronic spectrum and boundary electronic behavior constrained by a nontrivial bulk band topology and the relevant protecting symmetries. In common three-dimensional time-reversal-invariant examples, the conducting surface can be approximated at low [energy](energy-split.md) by a [Dirac cone](dirac-cone.md). The number of surface cones and the range of the linear approximation depend on the material; boundary conduction and thermal bulk [phonons](phonon.md) therefore need separate state counts.
