# Born rigidity

↑ **Parent:** [Special relativity](special-relativity-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Born_rigidity)

Motion preserving local proper distances measured on instantaneous comoving simultaneity slices. It constrains the acceleration of different points of a body, rather than describing an infinitely fast material signal. For rectilinear positive acceleration, the front of an extended Born-rigid body accelerates less strongly than its rear.
