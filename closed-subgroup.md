# Closed subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Topological group](topological-group-split.md)

A closed subgroup is a [subgroup](subgroup.md) which is closed in the ambient topology. It is a [topological group](topological-group-split.md) in its [subspace topology](subspace-topology.md), but need not be an [open subgroup](open-subgroup.md).
