# Closed subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Topological group](topological-group-split.md)

A [closed subgroup](closed-subgroup.md) is a [subgroup](subgroup.md) which is a [closed set](closed-set.md) in the ambient [topology](topology-split.md). It is a [topological group](topological-group-split.md) in its [subspace topology](subspace-topology.md), but need not be an [open subgroup](open-subgroup.md).

The additive [subgroup](subgroup.md) $\mathbb Z\subset\mathbb R$ of [integers](integer.md) is a [closed subgroup](closed-subgroup.md): every noninteger lies in an [open interval](open-interval.md) $(n,n+1)$ avoiding $\mathbb Z$. It is also a [discrete subgroup](discrete-subgroup.md), since $(n-1/2,n+1/2)\cap\mathbb Z=\{n\}$. Nevertheless it is not an [open subgroup](open-subgroup.md) of $(\mathbb R,+)$: every [open interval](open-interval.md) containing an [integer](integer.md) contains nonintegers too. Thus having a [discrete topology](discrete-space.md) internally says nothing about being an [open set](open-set.md) in the ambient [topology](topology-split.md).
