# Null effective potential in Schwarzschild spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [Equatorial null orbit in Schwarzschild spacetime](equatorial-null-orbit-in-schwarzschild-spacetime.md)

For an equatorial [null geodesic](null-geodesic.md) in [Schwarzschild spacetime](schwarzschild-spacetime-split.md), let $k=(1-2\mu/r)\,dt/d\lambda$ and $h=r^2d\phi/d\lambda$ be the conserved quantities for an [affine parameter](affine-parameter.md) $\lambda$. The null [metric](metric.md) condition gives $\dot r^2/2+V_{\rm eff}=c^2k^2/2$. For $h\ne0$, the [effective potential](effective-potential.md) is zero at the [Schwarzschild event horizon](schwarzschild-event-horizon.md), positive outside, and tends to zero at infinity. Its unique exterior maximum is at $r=3\mu$, with value $h^2/(54\mu^2)$ and second [derivative](derivative.md) $-h^2/(81\mu^4)$. This maximum gives the unstable [photon sphere](photon-sphere.md).

The [impact parameter](impact-parameter.md) of an incoming ray is $b=|h|/(ck)$ for $k>0$. Capture requires its radial energy to exceed the maximum, namely $b<3\sqrt3\mu$. At equality the incoming ray approaches the circular orbit asymptotically; it does not cross the horizon. For $h=0$, the potential is identically zero and the ingoing radial ray is captured.
