# Central average of representation matrices at squares

↑ **Parent:** [Frobenius-Schur indicator](frobenius-schur-indicator.md)

For a complex [irreducible representation](irreducible-representation.md) $D$ of a [finite group](finite-group.md) $G$, the sum $A=\sum_gD(g^2)$ commutes with every $D(h)$: conjugation sends its summands to $D((hgh^{-1})^2)$, merely permuting the summation index. [Schur's lemma](schur-s-lemma.md) gives $A=cI$. Taking the [trace](matrix-trace.md) yields $c\dim D=\sum_g\chi(g^2)=|G|\nu(D)$, proving the identity. Here $\nu(D)$ is the [Frobenius-Schur indicator](frobenius-schur-indicator.md). For a reducible representation the sum is still central in its image, but need not be one scalar on all irreducible constituents.
