# Logarithmic derivative

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann zeta function](riemann-zeta-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logarithmic_derivative)

The logarithmic derivative of a nonzero differentiable function $f$ is $f'/f$. For $\Re s>1$, the [Euler product](euler-product.md) for the [Riemann zeta function](riemann-zeta-function.md) gives

$$
-\frac{\zeta'(s)}{\zeta(s)}
=\sum_{n\geq1}\frac{\Lambda(n)}{n^s}.
$$
