# Chloroplast genome

↑ **Parent:** [Genome](genome.md)

The [genome](genome.md) carried within a [chloroplast](chloroplast.md). It retains some [photosynthesis](photosynthesis.md) and expression-machinery [genes](gene.md), while many components required for its expression are encoded in the nuclear [genome](genome.md).
