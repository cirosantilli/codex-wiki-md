# Stress contribution to energy transport in a moving capacitor

↑ **Parent:** [Electromagnetic stress-energy tensor](electromagnetic-stress-energy-tensor.md)

For a static rest-frame [electromagnetic stress-energy tensor](electromagnetic-stress-energy-tensor.md) with no [Poynting flux](poynting-flux.md), a [Lorentz transformation](lorentz-transformation.md) to a laboratory where it moves along $+x$ gives $u=\gamma^2(u_0+\beta^2T_*^{xx})$ and $S_x=\gamma^2v(u_0+T_*^{xx})$. Thus [energy](energy-split.md) flux includes stress as well as rest [energy density](energy-density.md). In an ideal [parallel-plate capacitor](parallel-plate-capacitor.md), $T_*^{xx}=+u_0$ for motion perpendicular to the [electric field](electric-field-split.md), but $T_*^{xx}=-u_0$ for motion parallel to it. The former has $S_x=2\gamma^2vu_0$; the latter has zero interior flux, with [work](work.md) at the moving plates transferring [energy](energy-split.md) at the boundaries. The field subsystem need not have an integrated [four-momentum](four-momentum.md) independent of simultaneity surface; include material support stresses for a closed system.
