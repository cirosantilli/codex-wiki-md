# Provability logic

↑ **Parent:** [Modal logic](modal-logic.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provability_logic)

[Provability logic](provability-logic.md) studies modal principles valid when $\Box A$ is interpreted by an [arithmetical provability predicate](arithmetical-provability-predicate.md). The distribution axiom corresponds to formalized [modus ponens rule](implication-elimination-rule.md), necessitation corresponds to verification of actual proofs, and the Löb axiom is the [Internal form of Löb's theorem](internal-form-of-lob-s-theorem.md). The standard system capturing these principles is [Gödel-Löb logic](godel-lob-logic.md).
