# Sampling fraction

↑ **Parent:** [Sample mean under simple random sampling](sample-mean-under-simple-random-sampling.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sampling_fraction)

The [sampling fraction](sampling-fraction.md) is the [sample size](sample-size.md) divided by the finite population size. In [simple random sampling](simple-random-sampling.md), it determines how much the [finite population correction](finite-population-correction.md) reduces the [variance](variance-split.md) relative to $S^2/n$.
