# Galactic disk heating

↑ **Parent:** [Galactic disk](galactic-disk.md)

[Galactic disk heating](galactic-disk-heating.md) increases the random velocities of [stars](star.md) through encounters with [molecular clouds](molecular-cloud.md), nonaxisymmetric gravitational disturbances, and other perturbations. Older [stellar populations](stellar-population.md) usually have larger [velocity dispersions](velocity-dispersion.md) and greater vertical thickness, although birth conditions and migration also affect the relation.
