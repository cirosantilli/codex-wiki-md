# Acceleration in cylindrical coordinates

↑ **Parent:** [Cylindrical coordinate system](cylindrical-coordinate-system.md)

Differentiate the [position vector](position-vector.md) $\mathbf r=r\mathbf e_r+z\mathbf e_z$ using $\dot{\mathbf e}_r=\dot\theta\mathbf e_\theta$, $\dot{\mathbf e}_\theta=-\dot\theta\mathbf e_r$, and fixed $\mathbf e_z$. The first [derivative](derivative.md) is the [velocity](velocity.md) $\dot r\mathbf e_r+r\dot\theta\mathbf e_\theta+\dot z\mathbf e_z$; differentiating once more gives the displayed [acceleration](acceleration.md). In particular, $r$ times the azimuthal [acceleration](acceleration.md) is $d(r^2\dot\theta)/dt$.
