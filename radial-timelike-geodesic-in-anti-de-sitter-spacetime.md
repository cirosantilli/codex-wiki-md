# Radial timelike geodesic in anti-de Sitter spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [Static coordinates on anti-de Sitter spacetime](static-coordinates-on-anti-de-sitter-spacetime.md)

In [static coordinates on anti-de Sitter spacetime](static-coordinates-on-anti-de-sitter-spacetime.md) with curvature radius $L$ and $c=1$, put $f=1+r^2/L^2$. The conserved [energy](energy-split.md) per unit rest [mass](mass.md) is $E=f\,dt/d\tau$. Timelike normalization for a radial [geodesic](geodesic.md) gives $(dr/d\tau)^2=E^2-1-r^2/L^2$, hence $d^2r/d\tau^2=-r/L^2$. An outward launch from the origin at [proper time](proper-time.md) $\tau_0$ gives the displayed harmonic solution until its first return, after [proper time](proper-time.md) $\pi L$, independently of $E>1$. Beyond that return, use a signed diameter coordinate or change the radial direction to keep the [areal radius](areal-radius.md) nonnegative. This is a coordinate realization of [Anti-de Sitter timelike geodesic refocusing](anti-de-sitter-timelike-geodesic-refocusing.md).
