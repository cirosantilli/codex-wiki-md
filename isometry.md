# Isometry

↑ **Parent:** [Riemannian geometry](riemannian-geometry-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isometry)

An isometry between [metric spaces](metric-space.md) preserves every distance. A Riemannian isometry preserves the Riemannian metric and therefore distances, angles, geodesics, and intrinsic curvature.
