# Circuit size

↑ **Parent:** [Boolean circuit](boolean-circuit.md)

The size of a [Boolean circuit](boolean-circuit.md) counts its gates, or in another common convention its wires. The convention must be fixed when stating quantitative bounds. In switching arguments, new clauses created from a [decision tree](decision-tree.md) are not separate original gates to be charged in the failure union bound.
