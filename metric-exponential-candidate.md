# Metric exponential candidate

↑ **Parent:** [Category of bounded metric spaces and non-expansive maps](category-of-bounded-metric-spaces-and-non-expansive-maps.md)

For bounded metric spaces $X,Y$, let $[X,Y]$ be the set of [non-expansive maps](non-expansive-map.md) and define

$$
\bar d(f,g)=\sup\{d_Y(fx,gy):d_X(x,y)<d_Y(fx,gy)\}.
$$

Whenever $\bar d$ satisfies the triangle inequality, it is a metric and makes $[X,-]$ right adjoint to $-\times X$ in $\mathbf{Met}_b$.
