# Unique smooth flex of a cuspidal cubic

↑ **Parent:** [Cuspidal cubic](cuspidal-cubic.md)

For $D=\{y^2z=x^3\}$, the determinant of the homogeneous [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) is $24xy^2$. Its intersections with $D$ are the cusp $[0:0:1]$ and the smooth point $[0:1:0]$. The [Hessian criterion for a flex](hessian-criterion-for-a-flex.md) therefore gives a unique smooth [flex](inflection-point-of-an-algebraic-plane-curve.md), with tangent $z=0$ and contact order three. Consequently every pair consisting of a triple line and a cuspidal cubic meeting only at a smooth point is projectively equivalent to $(3\{z=0\},D)$.
