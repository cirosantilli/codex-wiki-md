# Centrifugal strain of a thin elastic hoop

↑ **Parent:** [Young's modulus](young-s-modulus.md)

A small arc of a rotating thin hoop needs inward [force](force-split.md) per length $\rho A\omega^2r$. Its circumferential [tension](tension.md) supplies this through curvature, giving [tension](tension.md) $\rho A\omega^2r^2$ and tensile [stress](stress.md) $\rho\omega^2r^2$. [Hooke's law](hooke-s-law.md) converts that [stress](stress.md) to circumferential [strain](strain.md), which equals fractional radius change. Small [strain](strain.md), a slender cross-section and uniaxial [linear elasticity](linear-elasticity.md) are required; the cross-sectional area cancels at leading order.
