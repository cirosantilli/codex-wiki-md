# Complete quadrangle

↑ **Parent:** [Projective plane](projective-plane.md)

A complete quadrangle consists of four [projective points](projective-point.md) in the [projective plane](projective-plane.md), no three collinear, and all six joining [projective lines](projective-line.md). Its three diagonal points are the intersections of opposite joining lines. Over a [field](field.md) of characteristic different from two, the line joining two diagonal points cuts either side through the third diagonal point in a pair of [harmonic conjugate points](harmonic-conjugate-points.md). In a [projective frame](projective-frame.md) this follows by using the coordinate points and $[1:1:1]$ and computing the intersections.
