# Equational criterion for flatness

↑ **Parent:** [Flat module](flat-module.md)

For a [flat module](flat-module.md) $M$ over a [commutative ring](commutative-ring.md), every finite relation among elements of $M$ is a finite combination of relations among elements of the [ring](ring.md) itself, in the displayed sense. To obtain this, tensor the kernel of the linear map $A^r\to A$, $(u_i)\mapsto\sum_i a_iu_i$, with $M$. Flatness says that this tensor maps onto the kernel in $M^r$. Writing an element of that tensor as a finite sum gives the columns $b_{ij}$ and the elements $n_j$. The converse follows by applying this condition to relations in a [tensor product](tensor-product.md) to prove that tensoring preserves injections.
