# Uniform norm bound for B-spline orthogonal projection

↑ **Parent:** [Mixed-normalization spline Gram matrix](mixed-normalization-spline-gram-matrix.md)

Let nonnegative partition-normalized [B-splines](b-spline.md) $N_i$ form a [basis](basis.md), with $\sum_iN_i\le1$ on the integration interval and $d_i=\int N_i>0$. The row-normalized mixed [Gram matrix](gram-matrix.md) is $G_{ij}=\int N_iN_j/d_i$. The [normal equations](normal-equation.md) for the [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md) onto their [linear span](linear-span.md) become $Ga=b$, with $b_i=\int fN_i/d_i$ and $\|b\|_{\ell^\infty}\le\|f\|_\infty$. The [subpartition of unity for B-splines](subpartition-of-unity-for-b-splines.md) gives $\|\sum_i a_iN_i\|_\infty\le\|a\|_{\ell^\infty}$ and proves the bound. The ordinary symmetric Gram matrix omits this essential row normalization. The projection maps continuous functions into the spline space, not onto all continuous functions.
