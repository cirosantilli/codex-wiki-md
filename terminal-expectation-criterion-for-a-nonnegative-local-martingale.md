# Terminal expectation criterion for a nonnegative local martingale

↑ **Parent:** [Nonnegative local martingale](nonnegative-local-martingale.md)

A [nonnegative local martingale](nonnegative-local-martingale.md) is a [supermartingale](supermartingale.md). If its integrable terminal limit has the same expectation as its initial value, conditional [Fatou lemma](fatou-s-lemma.md) gives $\mathbb E[Z_\infty\mid\mathcal F_t]\leq Z_t$, and equality of expectations makes this an equality. It is therefore a [uniformly integrable martingale](uniformly-integrable-martingale.md).
