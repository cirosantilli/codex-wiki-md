# Quantum statistical mechanics

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical mechanics](statistical-mechanics.md)

[quantum statistical mechanics](quantum-statistical-mechanics.md) describes equilibrium and fluctuations with [density matrices](density-matrix-split.md) and [traces](matrix-trace.md) over a quantum [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md). The canonical equilibrium state is $e^{-\beta H}/Z$, while particle exchange introduces $H-\mu N$ in the [grand canonical ensemble](grand-canonical-ensemble.md). Noncommutativity and particle exchange distinguish it from a classical phase-space [distribution](distribution-mathematical-analysis.md).
