# Cubic field of discriminant minus 307

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic number theory](algebraic-number-theory-split.md)

The polynomial $X^3-5X+8$ is irreducible over $\mathbb Q$. For

$$
\beta=\frac{\alpha+\alpha^2}{2},
$$

one has

$$
\beta^3-5\beta^2+11\beta-12=0,
\qquad
\alpha=-4+3\beta-\beta^2.
$$

The field has ring of integers $\mathbb Z[\beta]$ and field discriminant $-307$.
