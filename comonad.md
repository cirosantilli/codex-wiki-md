# Comonad

↑ **Parent:** [Endofunctor](endofunctor.md)

A comonad is an [endofunctor](endofunctor.md) $G$ with [natural transformations](natural-transformation.md) $\epsilon:G\to1$ and $\delta:G\to G^2$ satisfying $G\epsilon\,\delta=\epsilon_G\delta=1_G$ and $G\delta\,\delta=\delta_G\delta$. It is the categorical dual of a [monad](monad.md). Its [coalgebras for a comonad](coalgebra-for-a-comonad.md) carry compatible maps into their image under $G$.
