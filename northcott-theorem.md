# Northcott theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Absolute multiplicative Weil height](absolute-multiplicative-weil-height.md)

There are only finitely many algebraic numbers of bounded [degree](field-extension.md) and bounded [Absolute multiplicative Weil height](absolute-multiplicative-weil-height.md). The finite set can be enumerated effectively from the bounded coefficients of their primitive minimal polynomials.

The degree and [Absolute multiplicative Weil height](absolute-multiplicative-weil-height.md) bounds are both essential.
