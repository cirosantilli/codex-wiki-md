# X0 2 modular curve

↑ **Parent:** [Modular curve](modular-curve.md)

The compact quotient for the [Gamma 0 congruence subgroup](gamma-0-congruence-subgroup.md) at level two has projective index three, two [modular cusps](cusp-of-a-modular-group.md) of [cusp widths](width-of-a-cusp.md) one and two, one order-two elliptic orbit and no order-three elliptic orbit. It has genus zero. It differs from the level-two principal-congruence curve for $\Gamma(2)$, which has three [modular cusps](cusp-of-a-modular-group.md).
