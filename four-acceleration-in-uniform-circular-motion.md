# Four-acceleration in uniform circular motion

↑ **Parent:** [Four-acceleration](four-acceleration.md)

For uniform [circular motion](circular-motion.md) in an inertial laboratory, the [Lorentz factor](lorentz-factor.md) is constant. With [metric signature](metric-signature.md) $+---$, [four-acceleration](four-acceleration.md) is $A^\mu=(0,\gamma^2\mathbf a)$ and [proper acceleration](proper-acceleration.md) has magnitude $\gamma^2v^2/r$. The instantaneous spatial [acceleration](acceleration.md) is perpendicular to [velocity](velocity.md), even though the particle has nonzero [proper acceleration](proper-acceleration.md).
