# Minimal-walk tree

↑ **Parent:** [Minimal walk along a club sequence](minimal-walk-along-a-club-sequence.md)

The tree of restrictions $\rho_\beta\upharpoonright\alpha$, ordered by extension. For a [club sequence](club-sequence.md) on $\omega_2$ with club order types at most $\omega_1$, the [Continuum hypothesis](continuum-hypothesis.md) bounds each level by $\aleph_1$. Trace injectivity and [Fodor lemma](fodor-lemma.md) exclude a [cofinal branch](cofinal-branch.md).
