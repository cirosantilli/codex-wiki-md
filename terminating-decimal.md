# Terminating decimal

↑ **Parent:** [Decimal representation](decimal-representation.md)

A [decimal representation](decimal-representation.md) with only finitely many nonzero fractional digits. A [reduced fraction](reduced-fraction.md) has this property exactly when its [denominator](denominator.md) has no [prime factors](prime-factor.md) other than two and five. The zero tail is distinguished from the genuine repeating block measured by [decimal period from multiplicative order](decimal-period-from-multiplicative-order.md).
