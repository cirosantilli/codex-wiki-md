# Stokes temperature-slaving operator

↑ **Parent:** [Infinite-Prandtl-number convection](infinite-prandtl-number-convection.md)

For a nonzero horizontal [Fourier mode](fourier-mode.md) $k$, solve $(D^2-k^2)^2w=Rk^2\theta$, $w=w''=0$, to obtain $w=\mathcal W_R\theta$. Under homogeneous [temperature](temperature.md) [Dirichlet boundary conditions](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md), the sine modes diagonalize this [self-adjoint operator](self-adjoint-operator.md), with [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $Rk^2/(k^2+n^2\pi^2)^2$. The [temperature](temperature.md) [linear operator](linear-operator.md) is $\mathcal L_R=D^2-k^2+\mathcal W_R$. At a marginal mode $g=\sin(n\pi z)$ it has $\mathcal W_Rg=(k^2+n^2\pi^2)g$.
