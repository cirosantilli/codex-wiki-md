# Critical point drying

↑ **Parent:** [Surface tension](surface-tension-split.md)

Drying through a fluid's [thermodynamic critical point](thermodynamic-critical-point.md) to avoid traversing an ordinary liquid-vapor coexistence interface. Removing the receding meniscus can protect compliant microstructures from [capillary force](capillary-force.md) during release. Solvent exchange and compatibility with the structure remain practical constraints.
