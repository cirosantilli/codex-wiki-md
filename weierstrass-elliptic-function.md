# Weierstrass elliptic function

↑ **Parent:** [Weierstrass functions](weierstrass-functions.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weierstrass_elliptic_function)

For a [period lattice](period-lattice.md) $\Lambda\subset\mathbb C$, the Weierstrass elliptic function is the normally convergent lattice sum

$$
\wp_\Lambda(z)=\frac1{z^2}+\sum_{\omega\in\Lambda\setminus\{0\}}\left(\frac1{(z-\omega)^2}-\frac1{\omega^2}\right).
$$

It is an even [elliptic function](elliptic-function.md) with a double [pole](pole.md) of principal part $(z-\omega)^{-2}$ and zero [residue](residue.md) at every point $\omega$ of the [period lattice](period-lattice.md).
