# Radical of an ideal

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal](ideal.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radical_of_an_ideal)

The radical of an [ideal](ideal.md) $I$ is

$$
\sqrt I=\{f:f^m\in I\text{ for some }m\geq1\}.
$$

An ideal equal to its radical is a radical ideal.
