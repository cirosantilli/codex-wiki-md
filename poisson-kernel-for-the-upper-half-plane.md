# Poisson kernel for the upper half-plane

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson kernel for the upper half-space](poisson-kernel-for-the-upper-half-space.md)

For the [complex upper half-plane](upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md), the Poisson kernel is

$$
P_y(x-v)=\frac1\pi\frac{y}{(x-v)^2+y^2}.
$$

It is the density of [harmonic measure](harmonic-measure.md) on the real boundary as viewed from $x+iy$.
