# Deep inelastic scattering

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum chromodynamics](quantum-chromodynamics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deep_inelastic_scattering)

Scattering that resolves the constituents of a [hadron](hadron.md) at large spacelike [momentum transfer](momentum-transfer.md), with an inclusive sum over final [hadron](hadron.md) states.
