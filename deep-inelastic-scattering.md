# Deep inelastic scattering

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum chromodynamics](quantum-chromodynamics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deep_inelastic_scattering)

Scattering that resolves the constituents of a [hadron](hadron.md) at large spacelike [momentum transfer](momentum-transfer.md), with an inclusive sum over final [hadron](hadron.md) states.

In [electron](electron.md) scattering from a [proton](proton.md), write the reaction as $e^-p\to e^-X$: the outgoing [electron](electron.md) is measured, while $X$ denotes all allowed final [hadron](hadron.md) states. Large [momentum transfer](momentum-transfer.md) supplies a short-distance probe; a large final [invariant mass](invariant-mass.md) separates the deep continuum from [elastic scattering](elastic-scattering.md) and individual [particle resonances](particle-resonance.md). The useful surprise is that an extended [proton](proton.md) can have a response characteristic of much smaller constituents. [Deep inelastic scattering](deep-inelastic-scattering.md) turns measurements outside the [proton](proton.md) into constraints on its internal [parton distribution functions](parton-distribution-function.md).

The [SLAC-MIT deep inelastic electron scattering experiment](slac-mit-deep-inelastic-electron-scattering-experiment.md) connects this definition to an actual measurement. Its evidence came from distributions of outgoing [electron](electron.md) [energies](energy-split.md) and [scattering angles](scattering-angle.md), rather than a photograph of a [quark](quark.md). [Bjorken scaling](bjorken-scaling.md) concerns [deep-inelastic structure functions](deep-inelastic-structure-function.md) after known scattering factors have been accounted for; it does not say that the raw [differential scattering cross-section](differential-scattering-cross-section.md) is constant.
