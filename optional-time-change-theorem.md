# Optional time-change theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Time change of a continuous process](time-change-of-a-continuous-process.md)

Let $M$ be a [continuous local martingale](continuous-local-martingale.md) and let $(\tau_s)$ be an increasing continuous family of finite [stopping times](stopping-time.md). Under the usual compatibility conditions, $M_{\tau_s}$ is a continuous local martingale for the time-changed filtration $\mathcal F_{\tau_s}$. A finite-variation process remains of finite variation after the same time change.
