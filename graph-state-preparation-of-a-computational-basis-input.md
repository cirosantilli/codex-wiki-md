# Graph-state preparation of a computational-basis input

↑ **Parent:** [One-bit teleportation](one-bit-teleportation.md)

A two-vertex [graph state](graph-state.md) starts with two $|+\rangle$ states joined by a [Controlled-Z gate](controlled-z-gate.md). Measuring the first vertex in the $X$ basis leaves the second in $X^sH|+\rangle=X^s|0\rangle$, where $s$ is the measurement outcome. Thus a known zero input can be supplied to a measurement wire using only a graph-state resource and a known [Pauli frame](pauli-frame.md). This preparation link precedes the links implementing the desired logical [J gate](j-gate-in-measurement-based-quantum-computation.md); later measurement angles and output-bit corrections absorb its byproduct.
