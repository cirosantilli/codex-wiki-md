# Inflated quotient projectives have trivial source

↑ **Parent:** [Trivial source module](trivial-source-module.md)

If $D$ is a normal [p-subgroup](p-subgroup.md) of $N$, any indecomposable [projective module](projective-module.md) of $k(N/D)$, inflated to $N$, is a summand of $k[N/D]=\operatorname{Ind}_D^Nk$. It has trivial source and vertex $D$. Its [Brauer quotient of a module](brauer-quotient-of-a-module.md) at $D$ is the entire module, since $D$ acts trivially and all proper-subgroup [relative traces](relative-trace.md) multiply by an index divisible by $p$. This prevents its vertex from being smaller than $D$.
