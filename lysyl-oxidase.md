# Lysyl oxidase

↑ **Parent:** [Enzyme](enzyme-split.md)

[lysyl oxidase](lysyl-oxidase.md) contributes to covalent cross-link formation in extracellular matrix proteins. Perturbing its activity can change matrix mechanics and chemistry together, which matters when interpreting mechanotransduction experiments.
