# Chromosomal crossover

↑ **Parent:** [Genetic recombination](genetic-recombination.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromosomal_crossover)

A chromosomal crossover is a reciprocal exchange of segments between nonsister [chromatids](chromatid.md) of [homologous chromosomes](homologous-chromosome.md) during [meiosis](meiosis.md). It can change the [haplotypes](haplotype.md) inherited at selected [genetic loci](genetic-locus.md); whether it does so depends on the transmitted [chromatid](chromatid.md) and interval.
