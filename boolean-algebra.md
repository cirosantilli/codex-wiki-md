# Boolean algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Distributive lattice](distributive-lattice.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boolean_algebra)

A Boolean algebra is a bounded [distributive lattice](distributive-lattice.md) in which every element has a complement. Its meet, join, and complement abstract conjunction, disjunction, and negation.
