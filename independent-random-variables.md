# Independent random variables

↑ **Parent:** [Random variable](random-variable-split.md)

Random variables $X_1,\ldots,X_n$ are independent when their generated sigma-algebras are [independent sigma-algebras](independent-sigma-algebras.md). Equivalently, their joint distribution is the product of their marginal distributions.
