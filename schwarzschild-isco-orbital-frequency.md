# Schwarzschild ISCO orbital frequency

↑ **Parent:** [Innermost stable circular orbit](innermost-stable-circular-orbit.md)

The Schwarzschild coordinate rotation [frequency](frequency.md) at the marginally stable circular orbit, measured per unit time of a stationary clock at infinity. It scales inversely with black-hole [mass](mass.md). A finite-radius static clock adds a gravitational time-dilation factor; a quasi-periodic brightness signal need not directly equal this orbital [frequency](frequency.md).
