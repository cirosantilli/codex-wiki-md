# Newton-Cotes closed quadrature

↑ **Parent:** [Quadrature rule](quadrature-rule.md)

Integrate the [Lagrange polynomial](lagrange-polynomial.md) through values at all equally spaced nodes $0,\ldots,s$, including both endpoints. The resulting [quadrature rule](quadrature-rule.md) is exact through degree $s$. For even $s$, the node [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) $\prod_{j=0}^s(t-j)$ is odd about $s/2$, so its integral vanishes and exactness extends through degree $s+1$. The weights need not remain positive as the number of nodes increases.
