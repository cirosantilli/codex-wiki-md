<h1 id="nanson-s-formula">Nanson's formula</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Deformation gradient](deformation-gradient.md)

For an orientation-preserving [deformation gradient](deformation-gradient.md) $F$, oriented area transforms by its cofactor matrix: $(Fa)\times(Fb)=(\det F)F^{-T}(a\times b)$. Taking the [dot product](dot-product.md) with $Fc$ proves this from the determinant identity for the scalar triple product. Applying it to tangent vectors of a surface proves [Nanson's formula](nanson-s-formula.md). Equating current and reference [tractions](traction.md) then gives the material-first [nominal stress tensor](nominal-stress-tensor.md) $P=JF^{-1}\sigma$ for symmetric [Cauchy stress tensor](cauchy-stress-tensor.md) $\sigma$.
