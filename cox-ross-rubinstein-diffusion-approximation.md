# Cox-Ross-Rubinstein diffusion approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Cox--Ross--Rubinstein model](cox-ross-rubinstein-model.md)

For mesh $h$, the risk-neutral up probability is $(e^{\rho h}-d)/(u-d)$. The log-price increment has mean $(\rho-\sigma^2/2)h+O(h^2)$ and variance $\sigma^2h+O(h^2)$. The [Donsker invariance principle](donsker-s-theorem.md) gives convergence of the log-price process to Brownian motion with this drift, hence of prices to the [Black-Scholes model](black-scholes-model.md). Payoff-price convergence additionally uses continuity at almost every limiting path and uniform integrability for unbounded payoffs.
