# Animal

↑ **Parent:** [Eukaryote](eukaryote.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animal)

[Animals](animal.md) are [multicellular organisms](multicellular-organism.md) with [eukaryotic](eukaryote.md) cellular organization. A representative [animal](animal.md) cell has a [cell membrane](cell-membrane.md), [cell nucleus](cell-nucleus.md) and [mitochondria](mitochondria.md), but no [cell wall](cell-wall.md) or [chloroplasts](chloroplast.md). A [neuron](neuron.md) illustrates how this common plan can acquire a specialized shape and signaling function. See [OpenStax Biology 2e, 4.3: animal cell organization](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/4-3-eukaryotic-cells).
