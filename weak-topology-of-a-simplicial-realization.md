# Weak topology of a simplicial realization

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric realization of a simplicial complex](geometric-realization-of-a-simplicial-complex.md)

Give the [geometric realization](geometric-realization-of-a-simplicial-complex.md) of an [abstract simplicial complex](simplicial-complex.md) the [quotient topology](quotient-topology.md) obtained by gluing the disjoint union of its standard [simplexes](simplex.md) along their labelled faces. A [subset](subset.md) $U$ is open exactly when $U\cap|\sigma|$ is open in every closed geometric [simplex](simplex.md) $|\sigma|$. This is the weak realization topology. It is the topology of its [CW complex](cw-complex.md) structure; the word weak here refers to the simplexwise test, rather than to the [weak topology](weak-topology-split.md) of a [topological vector space](topological-vector-space-split.md). A map out of the realization is [continuous](continuous-function.md) exactly when its restriction to every simplex is continuous.
