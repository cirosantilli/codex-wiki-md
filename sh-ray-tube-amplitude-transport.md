# SH ray-tube amplitude transport

↑ **Parent:** [SH-wave](sh-wave.md)

For a leading [wavefront](wavefront.md) singularity or discontinuity in a smooth isotropic [shear-horizontal wave](shear-horizontal-wave.md) field, the ray transport equation conserves the displayed quantity along a tube of [elastic rays](elastic-ray.md). Here $J$ is transverse tube width in two dimensions and area in three. Cylindrical spreading has $J\propto r$, so the leading [wave amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) is proportional to $r^{-1/2}$. This rule excludes the immediate source neighborhood and [caustics](wave-caustic.md).
