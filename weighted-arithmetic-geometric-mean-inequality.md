# Weighted arithmetic-geometric mean inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Arithmetic-geometric mean inequality](arithmetic-geometric-mean-inequality.md)

For nonnegative real numbers $a_i$ and positive weights $w_i$ with $\sum_iw_i=1$, the weighted [geometric mean](geometric-mean.md) does not exceed the weighted [arithmetic mean](arithmetic-mean.md):

$$
\prod_i a_i^{w_i}\leq\sum_iw_i a_i.
$$

For positive $a_i$, this follows from [Jensen's inequality](jensen-s-inequality.md) and the [concavity](concave-function.md) of the [natural logarithm](natural-logarithm.md); a [limit](limit-of-a-function.md) extends it to zero inputs. For two terms it gives $a^sb^{1-s}\leq sa+(1-s)b$ when $0<s<1$.
