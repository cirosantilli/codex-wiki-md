# Pullback of a Cartier divisor

↑ **Parent:** [Cartier divisor](cartier-divisor-split.md)

For a [morphism of varieties](morphism-of-algebraic-varieties.md) $f:X\to Y$, pull back local defining rational functions of a [Cartier divisor](cartier-divisor-split.md) $D$ whenever they remain nonzero on each component of $X$. Their ratios remain regular units, defining $f^*D$. Equivalently, pull back the associated [line bundle](line-bundle.md) and its rational section. A dominant morphism always permits this construction.
