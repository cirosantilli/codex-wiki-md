# Adiabat with linear heat capacity

↑ **Parent:** [Adiabatic process](adiabatic-process.md)

For a fixed-composition [ideal gas](ideal-gas-split.md), the reversible [adiabatic process](adiabatic-process.md) relation $c_p\,dT/T=R\,dp/p$ gives $A\ln(T/T_s)+B(T-T_s)=R\ln(p/p_s)$. Under [hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md), $gz=A(T_s-T)+B(T_s^2-T^2)/2$. Increasing positive $B$ at fixed surface conditions increases temperature at a given smaller pressure and raises the model's formal zero-temperature height. That finite-height mathematical boundary is not a physical absolute-zero prediction.
