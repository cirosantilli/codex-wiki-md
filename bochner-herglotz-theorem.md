<h1 id="bochner-herglotz-theorem">Bochner–Herglotz theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Bochner's theorem](bochner-s-theorem.md)

A [positive-definite function](positive-definite-function.md) $r$ on the discrete group $\mathbb Z$ is the sequence of [Fourier coefficients](fourier-coefficient.md) of a unique finite positive [Borel measure](borel-measure.md) on the circle, with total mass $r(0)$. Positivity makes the finite sums $\sum_{|n|<N}(1-|n|/N)r(n)e^{-2\pi int}$ nonnegative. Their measures converge weakly to the required measure; uniqueness follows from density of [trigonometric polynomials](trigonometric-polynomial.md) in continuous functions.
