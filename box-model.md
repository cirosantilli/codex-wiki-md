# Box model

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical model](mathematical-model.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Box_model)

A [box model](box-model.md) represents a system by finitely many internally uniform compartments. Balances of transported material and local sources give coupled [ordinary differential equations](ordinary-differential-equation.md). For a constituent's [concentration](concentration.md) $c_i$ in fixed box [volume](volume.md) $V_i$, the stored amount is $V_ic_i$; changing [volumes](volume.md) requires the balance of that product. A transport-only closed [box model](box-model.md) preserves total inventory, even when its transport [matrix](matrix.md) is nonsymmetric.
