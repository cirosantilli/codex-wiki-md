# Coexistence barrier of a cubic-quartic Landau transition

↑ **Parent:** [First-order transition in a cubic-quartic Landau potential](first-order-transition-in-a-cubic-quartic-landau-potential.md)

At coexistence, the [landau free energy](landau-free-energy.md) can factor as $F(\phi)=\lambda\phi^2(\phi-v)^2/4$. The minima at $0,v$ have equal [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md). Differentiating shows the intermediate maximum at $v/2$, with barrier height $\lambda v^4/64$. The barrier is a local free-energy barrier; predicting spatial [nucleation](nucleation.md) also requires interfacial or gradient information.
