# Generalized Stokes theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Exterior derivative](exterior-derivative.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generalized_Stokes_theorem)

If $X$ is an oriented $n$-manifold with boundary, $F:\partial X\hookrightarrow X$ is the inclusion, and $\omega$ is a compactly supported $(n-1)$-form, then

$$
\int_Xd\omega=\int_{\partial X}F^*\omega,
$$

where the boundary has the [outward-normal-first boundary orientation](outward-normal-first-boundary-orientation.md). A subordinate [partition of unity](partition-of-unity.md) reduces the theorem to the fundamental theorem of calculus in oriented coordinate half-spaces; local finiteness and compact support ensure that only finitely many terms contribute.
