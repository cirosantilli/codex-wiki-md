# Tubercle

↑ **Parent:** [Tuberculosis](tuberculosis.md)

A historical pathological name for a small nodular lesion. Chemical examination can characterize the material in a lesion without establishing its infectious cause. The word does not name one pure [chemical compound](chemical-compound.md).
