# Fluorescence dequenching

↑ **Parent:** [Quenching (fluorescence)](quenching-fluorescence.md)

An increase in [fluorescence](fluorescence-split.md) caused by reducing a quenching interaction. Dye dilution can reduce [Förster resonance energy transfer](forster-resonance-energy-transfer.md); the signal alone need not identify the process that diluted the dyes.
