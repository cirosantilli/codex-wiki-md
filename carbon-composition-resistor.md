# Carbon composition resistor

↑ **Parent:** [Resistor](resistor.md)

A [carbon composition resistor](carbon-composition-resistor.md) uses a bulk mixture of carbon and insulating binder. The conducting network sets its [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md). Its compact geometry has little winding [inductance](inductance.md), but composition and contact fluctuations can cause excess [electronic noise](electronic-noise.md) and changes of [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) with temperature and aging. It differs from a carbon-film [resistor](resistor.md).
