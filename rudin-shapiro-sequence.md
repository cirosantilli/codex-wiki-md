# Rudin-Shapiro sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Sequence](sequence.md)

For a [nonnegative integer](natural-number.md) $n$, let $r(n)$ count overlapping occurrences of $11$ in its [binary expansion](binary-expansion.md). The Rudin-Shapiro sign sequence is $b(n)=(-1)^{r(n)}$. Appending a [binary digit](binary-digit.md) gives $b(2n)=b(n)$ and $b(2n+1)=(-1)^n b(n)$.
