# Polarization of singly scattered sunlight

↑ **Parent:** [Rayleigh scattering](rayleigh-scattering.md)

In ideal single [Rayleigh scattering](rayleigh-scattering.md) of unpolarized incident [light](light.md) by an isotropic small scatterer, observed [intensities](intensity-physics.md) perpendicular and parallel to the [scattering plane](scattering-plane.md) are proportional to 1 and $\cos^2\chi$. Their normalized difference is $\mathcal P=\sin^2\chi/(1+\cos^2\chi)$. At a right-angle scattering geometry the output is fully linear and perpendicular to that plane. Multiple scattering, large particles and anisotropic scatterers change this ideal sky-polarization law.
