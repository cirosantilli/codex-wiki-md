# Charged sphere inside a neutral coaxial capacitor

↑ **Parent:** [Coaxial cylindrical capacitor](coaxial-cylindrical-capacitor.md)

An isolated [conducting sphere](conducting-sphere.md) of radius $R$ initially at potential $V_0$ has charge $Q=4\pi\epsilon_0RV_0$. Inserting it without contact into the hollow inner conductor of a long [coaxial cylindrical capacitor](coaxial-cylindrical-capacitor.md) conserves that charge. The neutral inner conductor acquires $-Q$ on its cavity wall and $+Q$ outside. With the outer conductor grounded, the annular [electric field](electric-field-split.md) is $Q/(2\pi\epsilon_0lr)$ and the inner potential is the displayed result. The original [electric potential](electric-potential.md) is not conserved. End effects are neglected; exact cavity fields are geometry-dependent.
