# Angular-frequency Einstein coefficients

↑ **Parent:** [Einstein coefficients](einstein-coefficients.md)

When $B_{12}$ and $B_{21}$ multiply [radiation energy density](radiation-energy-density.md) per unit [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md), they differ by a factor $2\pi$ from coefficients multiplying the density per ordinary frequency. For nondegenerate levels, [Boltzmann distribution](boltzmann-distribution.md) populations and [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium-split.md) give $B_{12}=B_{21}$ and the free-space ratio in the title. Specifically $B_\omega=2\pi B_\nu$ while $A$ is unchanged. Polarization restrictions and level degeneracy must be specified separately.
