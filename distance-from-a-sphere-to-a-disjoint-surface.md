# Distance from a sphere to a disjoint surface

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean distance](euclidean-distance.md)

If a [surface](topological-surface-split.md) lies outside a sphere centered at the origin, its [minimum distance](minimum-distance-of-a-code.md) to the sphere is its minimum radial distance minus the sphere radius. For each [surface](topological-surface-split.md) point, the [triangle inequality](triangle-inequality.md) gives that [lower bound](lower-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md), and the radial point on the sphere attains it. Thus minimizing the squared [norm](norm.md) on the [surface](topological-surface-split.md) suffices.
