# Pellet-cladding mechanical interaction

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear fuel cladding](nuclear-fuel-cladding.md)

Mechanical contact and load transfer between expanding or cracking fuel pellets and their enclosing [nuclear fuel cladding](nuclear-fuel-cladding.md). Local stress depends on clearance, fuel deformation and the cladding's evolving material properties.
