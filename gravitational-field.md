# Gravitational field

↑ **Parent:** [Gravity](gravity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gravitational_field)

In [Newtonian gravity](gravitational-acceleration.md), the gravitational field $\mathbf g$ specifies the [acceleration](acceleration.md) of a freely falling [test particle](test-particle.md) at each position. A [gravitational potential](newtonian-potential-of-a-point-mass.md) $\Phi$ gives $\mathbf g=-\nabla\Phi$, with the [gradient](gradient.md) taken in the spatial geometry of the model. In [general relativity](general-relativity-split.md), the [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md) describes gravitational effects, and [tidal forces](tidal-force.md) are encoded by [spacetime](spacetime.md) curvature.
