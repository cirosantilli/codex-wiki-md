# Resistor

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resistor)

A resistor is an [electrical circuit](electrical-circuit-split.md) component whose intended behavior is [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md). An ideal ohmic resistor obeys [Ohm's law](ohm-s-law.md), $V=IR$, and converts electrical [energy](energy-split.md) into heat at [power](power.md) $P=I^2R$ through [Joule heating](joule-heating.md). It provides the dissipative term in a [series RLC circuit](series-rlc-circuit.md).
