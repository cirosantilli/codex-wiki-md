# Closed cone of curves

↑ **Parent:** [Real numerical divisor classes](real-numerical-divisor-classes.md)

The closed cone of curves is the closure in $N_1(X)_{\mathbb R}$ of the [convex cone](convex-cone.md) generated by classes of integral curves. A [nef divisor](nef-line-bundle.md) pairs nonnegatively with this entire closed cone. Its boundary can contain limiting classes which are not represented by one curve.
