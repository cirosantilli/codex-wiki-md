# Colexicographic initial segment

↑ **Parent:** [Colexicographic order](colexicographic-order.md)

The first $a$ sets of a fixed size in [colexicographic order](colexicographic-order.md) form a [colexicographic initial segment](colexicographic-initial-segment.md). Its [lower shadow](lower-shadow.md) is again a [colexicographic initial segment](colexicographic-initial-segment.md) at the next smaller size.
