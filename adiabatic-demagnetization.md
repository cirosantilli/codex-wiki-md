# Adiabatic demagnetization

↑ **Parent:** [Temperature](temperature-split.md)

A cooling method that reduces an applied [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) while suppressing [heat transfer](heat-transfer.md) to or from the material. For a suitable material exhibiting [paramagnetism](paramagnetism.md), an approximately reversible field reduction can lower its [temperature](temperature-split.md) at approximately constant [entropy](entropy-split.md).

The preparation matters as much as the field reduction. First establish [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium-split.md) with a cold [heat bath](heat-bath.md), then raise the [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) while maintaining the bath [temperature](temperature-split.md): this is [magnetization](magnetization.md) in an [isothermal process](isothermal-process.md). The bath accepts [heat](heat-split.md) and the material loses [magnetic entropy](magnetic-entropy.md). Next open a [heat switch](heat-switch.md), thermally isolating the working material, and lower the [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md). If the sweep maintains internal [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium-split.md) with negligible [entropy production](entropy-production.md), this second leg is an [isentropic process](isentropic-process.md). An [adiabatic process](adiabatic-process.md) alone does not guarantee constant [entropy](entropy-split.md).

The attraction is a controllable [energy](energy-split.md) scale: changing the [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) changes the separation of magnetic [energy levels](energy-level.md), even across a thermally insulating gap. This lets an already cold solid become colder without needing a colder contacting [heat bath](heat-bath.md). A practical [refrigerator](refrigerator.md) also needs a load, a period of absorbing its [heat](heat-split.md), and a reset that rejects accumulated [entropy](entropy-split.md) to the bath. A single field sweep has finite cooling capacity.
