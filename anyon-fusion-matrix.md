# Anyon fusion matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Anyon fusion rule](anyon-fusion-rule.md)

A [matrix](matrix.md) encoding multiplication by charge $b$ in the [anyon fusion rule](anyon-fusion-rule.md). These [matrices](matrix.md) commute in a [braided monoidal category](braided-monoidal-category.md). On a [sphere](sphere.md), the [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) of the [anyon fusion space](anyon-fusion-space.md) with puncture charges $a_1,\ldots,a_n$ and total [vacuum anyon](vacuum-anyon.md) charge is $(N_{a_1}\cdots N_{a_n})_{11}$.
