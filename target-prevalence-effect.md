# Target prevalence effect

↑ **Parent:** [Visual search](visual-search.md)

Changes in search behavior and misses when targets become more or less frequent. A controlled artificial baggage-search study found more misses at low target prevalence, together with altered absent-decision times. The phenomenon motivates modeling decision criteria and stopping in addition to feature processing; laboratory rates are not automatically professional field rates.[Experiment 1 and Figure 2](rare-items-often-missed-in-visual-searches-by-jeremy-m-wolfe-and-others.md)
