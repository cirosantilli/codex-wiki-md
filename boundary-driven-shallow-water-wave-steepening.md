# Boundary-driven shallow-water wave steepening

↑ **Parent:** [Shallow water equations](shallow-water-equations.md)

For an outgoing [simple wave](simple-wave.md) into water initially at rest at depth $H$, the incoming [Riemann invariant](riemann-invariant.md) is $u-2\sqrt{gh}=-2\sqrt{gH}$. Thus $\sqrt{gh}=\sqrt{gH}+u/2$ and the outgoing [characteristic speed](characteristic-speed.md) is $v=\sqrt{gH}+3u/2$. A boundary characteristic emitted at time $s$ is $x=v(s)(t-s)$. Its envelope satisfies $t=s+v/v'$ and $x=v^2/v'$. At a zero-velocity onset with positive acceleration, the leading-edge intersection is the displayed distance. Later [characteristic curves](characteristic-curve.md) can form a shock earlier elsewhere in a sufficiently strong waveform.
