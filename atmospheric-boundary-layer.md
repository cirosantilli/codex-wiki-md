# Atmospheric boundary layer

↑ **Parent:** [Troposphere](troposphere.md)

The lowest part of the [terrestrial atmosphere](terrestrial-atmosphere.md) that responds directly to surface fluxes on relatively short timescales through [turbulence](turbulence-split.md). Its depth changes with heating, stability and weather; a sharp composition or [temperature](temperature-split.md) change can mark its top. A uniform-layer chemical budget converts a surface flux into a [volume](volume.md) tendency by dividing by layer depth.
