# Hoop stress

↑ **Parent:** [Thin-walled pressure vessel](thin-walled-pressure-vessel.md)

The circumferential normal [stress](stress.md) in a cylindrical or curved structure. In a thin cylindrical wall under internal pressure its membrane value is approximately $Pr/t$. It differs from axial and through-thickness stresses.
