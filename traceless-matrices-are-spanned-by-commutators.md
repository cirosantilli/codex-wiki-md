# Traceless matrices are spanned by commutators

↑ **Parent:** [Traceless matrix](traceless-matrix.md)

Every [commutator](commutator.md) of square [matrices](matrix.md) is traceless by cyclicity of the [trace](matrix-trace.md). Conversely, off-diagonal [matrix units](matrix-unit.md) and differences of diagonal units are themselves [commutators](commutator.md) and span all [traceless matrices](traceless-matrix.md). This proves equality with the linear span of commutators, without needing a theorem about representation as one commutator.
