# Proper rotation

↑ **Parent:** [Orthogonal matrix](orthogonal-matrix.md)

An orientation-preserving [orthogonal transformation](orthogonal-transformation.md). In three dimensions it has an invariant axis and acts as a planar rotation through angle $\theta$ perpendicular to that axis. Its [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are $1,e^{i\theta},e^{-i\theta}$, so $\operatorname{tr}R=1+2\cos\theta$. In higher dimensions a [proper rotation](proper-rotation.md) can involve several mutually orthogonal planes and need not fix an axis.
