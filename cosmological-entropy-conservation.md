# Cosmological entropy conservation

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmology](cosmology-split.md)

For an adiabatically expanding homogeneous universe, the [entropy density](entropy-density.md) satisfies

$$
\nabla_\mu(su^\mu)=0,
\qquad
\dot s+3Hs=0,
$$

so the entropy $sa^3$ in a comoving volume is constant.
