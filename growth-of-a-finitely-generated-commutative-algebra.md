# Growth of a finitely generated commutative algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Hilbert-Serre theorem](hilbert-serre-theorem.md)

Let a commutative $k$-algebra $R$ be generated by $x_1,\ldots,x_n$, and let $R_j$ be the span of words of total degree at most $j$ in those generators. The [associated graded ring](associated-graded-ring.md) of this filtration is a [standard graded algebra](standard-graded-algebra.md), and

$$
\dim_kR_j=\sum_{i=0}^j\dim_k(R_i/R_{i-1}).
$$

The [Hilbert-Serre theorem](hilbert-serre-theorem.md) therefore makes $\dim_kR_j$ eventually a polynomial in $j$. Its degree is $\dim R$; this follows by applying the Rees-ring deformation, whose special fibers are $R$ and the associated graded ring, so they have the same [Krull dimension](krull-dimension.md).
