<h1 id="poynting-robertson-decay-of-a-circumstellar-orbit">Poynting–Robertson decay of a circumstellar orbit</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Poynting–Robertson drag](poynting-robertson-drag.md)

With $A=\beta GM_\star/c$ constant, average the drag [work](work.md) $\dot\varepsilon=-A(2\dot r^2+v_\theta^2)/r^2$ over a bound [elliptic orbit](elliptic-orbit.md). Using $dt=r^2df/h$ gives the integral $\pi(2+3e^2)\mu^2/h^2$, where $\mu$ is the conservative central parameter. Combining with $\dot a=2a^2\dot\varepsilon/\mu$ gives

$$
\langle\dot a\rangle=-\frac A a\frac{2+3e^2}{(1-e^2)^{3/2}}.
$$

This is a weak-drag secular average. It also holds when the conservative parameter is $(1-\beta)GM_\star$, provided a bound [orbit](orbit-dynamical-system.md) exists; the conservative parameter cancels but the drag amplitude remains $A$. For a [circular Kepler orbit](circular-kepler-orbit.md), $a^2(t)=a_0^2-4At$.
