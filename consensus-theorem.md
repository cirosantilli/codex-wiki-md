# Consensus theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Boolean function](boolean-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consensus_theorem)

In [Boolean algebra](boolean-algebra.md), the product $yz$ is the consensus of $xy$ and $\bar xz$. If $yz=1$, either $x=1$ and $xy=1$, or $x=0$ and $\bar xz=1$, so adding the consensus does not change the [Boolean function](boolean-function.md). It can change a circuit's transient behavior: while $x$ switches, the two original product terms may briefly both be zero because of unequal [propagation delays](propagation-delay.md), but $yz$ remains one. Keeping the logically redundant consensus product can therefore remove a [static-one logic hazard](static-one-logic-hazard.md).
