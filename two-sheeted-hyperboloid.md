# Two-sheeted hyperboloid

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperboloid](hyperboloid.md)

This [regular surface](smooth-surface.md) has two connected components, $t=\pm\sqrt{1+x^2+y^2}$. The [symmetric-matrix model of the two-sheeted hyperboloid](symmetric-matrix-model-of-the-two-sheeted-hyperboloid.md) realizes them as the positive-definite and negative-definite symmetric determinant-one $2\times2$ matrices. Each component is diffeomorphic to $\mathbb R^2$; this differs from the connected [one-sheet hyperboloid](one-sheet-hyperboloid.md).
