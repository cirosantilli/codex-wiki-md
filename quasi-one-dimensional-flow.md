# Quasi-one-dimensional flow

↑ **Parent:** [Compressible flow](compressible-flow-split.md)

A model representing a passage by its varying cross-sectional area and approximately uniform section-averaged flow variables. It neglects detailed transverse [velocity](velocity.md) and thermodynamic structure. In a smooth lossless [nozzle](nozzle.md), conservation laws then connect area to [Mach number](mach-number.md); this model does not by itself determine the curved sonic surface of an arbitrary multidimensional passage.
