# Hardy inequality in Euclidean space

↑ **Parent:** [Sobolev inequality](sobolev-inequality.md)

This spatial form of [Hardy's inequality](hardy-s-inequality.md) controls a singular weight by [derivatives](derivative.md). For $d\geq3$ and $u\in H^1(\mathbb R^d)$,

$$
\int_{\mathbb R^d}\frac{|u(x)|^2}{|x|^2}\,dx
\leq\frac{4}{(d-2)^2}\int_{\mathbb R^d}|\nabla u(x)|^2\,dx.
$$
