# Photon gas

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical physics](statistical-physics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photon_gas)

A photon gas consists of [photons](photon.md) in [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium.md). Since photon number is not conserved when photons can be emitted and absorbed, the equilibrium [photon chemical potential](photon-chemical-potential.md) is zero. The [photon energy density](photon-energy-density.md) is $a_RT^4$, where $a_R=4\sigma/c$, and its [pressure](pressure.md) is one third of this [energy density](energy-density.md).
