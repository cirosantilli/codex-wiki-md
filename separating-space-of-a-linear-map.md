# Separating space of a linear map

↑ **Parent:** [Closed graph theorem](closed-graph-theorem.md)

For an everywhere-defined [linear map](linear-map.md) $T:E\to F$ between [Banach spaces](banach-space-split.md), its separating space measures the possible limiting defect in continuity at zero. It is a [closed linear subspace](closed-vector-subspace.md): linear combinations combine witnessing sequences, and diagonal choices with $\|x_j\|<1/j$ and $\|Tx_j-y_j\|<1/j$ handle a convergent sequence $y_j$ of defects. The [closed graph theorem](closed-graph-theorem.md) gives $T$ continuous exactly when $S(T)=\{0\}$, since a limit $(x_n,Tx_n)\to(x,y)$ has $y-Tx\in S(T)$.
