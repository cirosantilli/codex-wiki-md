# Hadamard product

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix](matrix.md)

The [matrix](matrix.md) with entries $(P\circ Q)_{ij}=P_{ij}Q_{ij}$. It differs from ordinary [matrix multiplication](matrix-multiplication.md). The [Schur product theorem](schur-product-theorem.md) states that it preserves [positive semidefiniteness](positive-semidefinite-matrix.md) when both factors are [positive semidefinite matrices](positive-semidefinite-matrix.md).
