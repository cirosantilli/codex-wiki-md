# Scalar field equation of motion

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar field theory](scalar-field-theory-split.md)

For a canonical real [scalar field](scalar-field.md) with [metric signature](metric-signature.md) $(-,+,+,+)$ and [scalar potential](scalar-potential.md) $V$, variation of its minimally coupled [action](action.md) gives the displayed equation. A homogeneous field in an [FLRW metric](friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md) therefore obeys $\ddot\varphi+3H\dot\varphi+V'(\varphi)=0$ in [natural units](natural-units.md). Its [energy density](energy-density.md) is $\dot\varphi^2/2+V$ and its [pressure](pressure-split.md) is $\dot\varphi^2/2-V$, which satisfy the [cosmological continuity equation](cosmological-continuity-equation.md).
