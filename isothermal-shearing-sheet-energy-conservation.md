# Isothermal shearing-sheet energy conservation

↑ **Parent:** [Shearing-sheet tidal potential](shearing-sheet-tidal-potential.md)

For constant [isothermal sound speed](isothermal-sound-speed.md), time-independent [shearing-sheet tidal potential](shearing-sheet-tidal-potential.md) $\Phi_t$, and inviscid flow, define $E=\Sigma u^2/2+c_s^2\Sigma\ln(\Sigma/\Sigma_{\rm ref})+\Sigma\Phi_t$. The [barotropic energy density](barotropic-energy-density.md) and [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) balances combine to give $\partial_tE+\nabla\cdot[(E+P)\mathbf u]=0$. [Coriolis acceleration](coriolis-acceleration.md) does no work. Integral conservation requires vanishing net boundary [energy flux](energy-flux.md).
