# CBL-interacting protein kinase

↑ **Parent:** [Protein kinase](protein-kinase.md)

A plant kinase recruited and regulated by a [calcineurin B-like protein](calcineurin-b-like-protein.md). The separate sensor and kinase provide a modular route from [calcium signaling](calcium-signaling.md) to regulation of transport and other targets.
