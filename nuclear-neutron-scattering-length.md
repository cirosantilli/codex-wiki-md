# Nuclear neutron scattering length

↑ **Parent:** [Neutron diffraction](neutron-diffraction.md)

The [nuclear neutron scattering length](nuclear-neutron-scattering-length.md) is the effective [length](length.md) coefficient for low-[energy](energy-split.md) nuclear [scattering amplitude](scattering-amplitude.md). For the elementary coherent calculation below, use a bound coherent coefficient $b_j$ for each site, and neglect absorption so that $b_j$ is real. This coefficient describes the [atomic nucleus](atomic-nucleus-split.md), including its [isotope](isotope.md) and [nuclear spin](nuclear-spin.md) dependence; it is not proportional to [atomic number](atomic-number.md). Replacing nuclear coefficients by electronic [magnetic moments](magnetic-dipole-moment.md) would describe a different scattering channel, with additional direction and spatial-distribution factors, as explained in [magnetic neutron diffraction](magnetic-neutron-diffraction.md).
