# Kinetochore ablation experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Spindle checkpoint](spindle-checkpoint.md)

In 1995, [Conly L. Rieder](conly-l-rieder.md), [Richard W. Cole](richard-w-cole.md), [Alexey Khodjakov](alexey-khodjakov.md) and [Greenfield Sluder](greenfield-sluder.md) tested whether an unattached [kinetochore](kinetochore.md) supplies the inhibitory signal delaying [anaphase](anaphase.md). Living PtK1 [biological cells](biological-cell-split.md) on glass coverslips were mounted in Rose chambers at $35$–$37\,{}^\circ\mathrm{C}$. An inverted microscope with a $60\times$ objective recorded [differential interference contrast microscopy](differential-interference-contrast-microscopy.md) video. A pulsed $532\,\mathrm{nm}$ [laser](laser-split.md) focused through that objective destroyed selected [chromosome](chromosome.md) regions. The readout was [chromosome](chromosome.md) movement and [anaphase](anaphase.md) onset before and after surgery.[pp. 941–942, Abstract and Materials and Methods: Cell Culture; Laser Microsurgery and Video-enhanced Light Microscopy](the-checkpoint-delaying-anaphase-in-response-to-chromosome-monoorientation-is-mediated-by-an-inhibitory-signal-produced-by-unattached-kinetochores-by-conly-l-rieder-and-others.md)

Destroying the last unattached [kinetochore](kinetochore.md) permitted [anaphase](anaphase.md) without that [chromosome](chromosome.md) aligning. Cutting [chromosome](chromosome.md) arms served as a damage control: progression still awaited attachment of the last naturally one-pole-attached [chromosome](chromosome.md). Correlative [electron microscopy](electron-microscopy.md) checked the absence of the targeted [kinetochore](kinetochore.md). Together these results located an inhibitory activity in or near the unattached [kinetochore](kinetochore.md), rather than establishing a requirement for alignment itself.[pp. 943–947, arm-cutting control, Table I, Figures 1, 5 and 6; destruction of the last unattached kinetochore and correlative electron microscopy](the-checkpoint-delaying-anaphase-in-response-to-chromosome-monoorientation-is-mediated-by-an-inhibitory-signal-produced-by-unattached-kinetochores-by-conly-l-rieder-and-others.md)

This intervention localized control; it did not identify every signaling [molecule](molecule-split.md) or guarantee faithful inheritance after damage. The cultured PtK1 preparation differs from the hypothetical $2n=4$ cartoon. Neither the cartoon nor this experiment establishes identical control in every [species](species.md).
