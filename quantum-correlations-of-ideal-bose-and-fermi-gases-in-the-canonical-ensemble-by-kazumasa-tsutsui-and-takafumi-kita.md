# Quantum Correlations of Ideal Bose and Fermi Gases in the Canonical Ensemble by Kazumasa Tsutsui and Takafumi Kita

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by Kazumasa Tsutsui](work-by-kazumasa-tsutsui.md), [Work by Takafumi Kita](work-by-takafumi-kita.md)

This primary paper distinguishes canonical ideal-gas reduced density matrices from the grand-canonical Wick factorization. It studies how particle statistics and ensemble choice affect one- and two-body correlations. Its date is later than the examination; it corroborates the state-assumption distinction rather than documenting what was available to candidates in 2013.
