# Nicotinic acetylcholine receptor

↑ **Parent:** [Ionotropic receptor](ionotropic-receptor.md)

A [nicotinic acetylcholine receptor](nicotinic-acetylcholine-receptor.md) is a pentameric cation-selective channel gated by acetylcholine. Extracellular binding interfaces communicate with a pore lined by M2 helices; transmitter recognition and ion selectivity are different properties.
