# Bounded-variation contraction under clipping

↑ **Parent:** [Function of bounded variation on a domain](function-of-bounded-variation-on-a-domain.md)

For $T(t)=\min(b,\max(a,t))$ and $u\in BV(\Omega)$, $T\circ u\in BV(\Omega)$ and $|D(T\circ u)|(\Omega)\le|Du|(\Omega)$. This is the contraction property of a one-Lipschitz scalar composition, obtainable by smooth approximation and [sequential lower semicontinuity](sequential-lower-semicontinuity.md). It proves range preservation for [total variation denoising](total-variation-denoising.md) when the data lie in $[a,b]$.
