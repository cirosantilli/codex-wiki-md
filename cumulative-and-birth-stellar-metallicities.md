# Cumulative and birth stellar metallicities

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar metallicity](stellar-metallicity.md)

In a well-mixed [closed-box model of galactic chemical evolution](closed-box-model-of-galactic-chemical-evolution.md), gas consumed into locked stellar mass supplies a natural cumulative mass coordinate $\delta$. The mean [stellar metallicity](stellar-metallicity.md) is $\overline Z_*(\delta)=\delta^{-1}\int_0^\delta Z_{\rm birth}(s)\,ds$, giving the displayed derivative relation. If the birth abundance increases linearly, $Z_{\rm birth}=b\delta$, its cumulative mean is half the current maximum. More generally the maximum of any nonnegative abundance distribution cannot lie below its mean. A fixed [initial mass function](initial-mass-function.md) and the same survival selection are required to identify this mass-weighted distribution with a star-count distribution.
