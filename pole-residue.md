# Pole residue

↑ **Parent:** [Self-energy](self-energy.md)

The coefficient of a simple one-particle pole in a [propagator](propagator.md) is its pole residue. If $G(p)=i/[p^2-m_0^2-\Sigma(p^2)]$ and $m$ is the [pole mass](pole-mass.md), then $Z_{\rm pole}=[1-\Sigma'(m^2)]^{-1}$. An [on-shell renormalization scheme](on-shell-renormalization-scheme.md) may impose unit residue by a finite or divergent [wave-function renormalization](wave-function-renormalization.md); mere [ultraviolet finiteness](ultraviolet-finiteness.md) does not force the residue to be one.
