# Translating-sphere acoustic thickness dipole

↑ **Parent:** [Acoustic thickness noise](acoustic-thickness-noise.md)

For a small rigid sphere translating with [velocity](velocity.md) $\mathbf V(t)$, the integrated thickness source $\rho_0\int\mathbf V\cdot\mathbf n_s\,dS$ vanishes. The first spatial moment is instead $\rho_0\int y_j\mathbf V\cdot\mathbf n_s\,dS=\rho_0\mathcal V V_j$, where $\mathcal V$ is the sphere volume. Expanding the [retarded time](retarded-time.md) across the compact source gives the displayed first nonzero thickness field. It is an [acoustic dipole](acoustic-dipole.md), at the same order as [acoustic loading noise](acoustic-loading-noise.md) for a translating sphere. The [added mass of a sphere](added-mass-of-a-sphere.md) supplies half this volume-mass coefficient through the loading term; their sum is $3\rho_0\mathcal V\mathbf n\cdot\ddot{\mathbf V}/(8\pi c_0^3r)$.
