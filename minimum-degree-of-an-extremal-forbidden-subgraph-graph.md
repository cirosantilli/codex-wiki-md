# Minimum degree of an extremal forbidden-subgraph graph

↑ **Parent:** [High-minimum-degree multipartite stability subgraph](high-minimum-degree-multipartite-stability-subgraph.md)

If a fixed [graph](graph-split.md) $F$ has [chromatic number](chromatic-number.md) $r+1$, every extremal $F$-free [graph](graph-split.md) $G$ on $n$ [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) has [minimum degree of a graph](minimum-degree-of-a-graph.md) $(1-1/r+o(1))n$. Use a [high-minimum-degree multipartite stability subgraph](high-minimum-degree-multipartite-stability-subgraph.md) $H$: replacing any [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) by one adjacent to all but one class of $H$ preserves $F$-freeness. Any supposed new copy of $F$ can replace the new [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) by an unused [common neighbour](common-neighbour.md) in the omitted class. Extremality therefore gives the lower bound on every [degree of a vertex](degree-graph-theory.md); the [Erdős-Stone theorem](erdos-stone-theorem.md) gives the upper bound on the average [degree of a vertex](degree-graph-theory.md).
