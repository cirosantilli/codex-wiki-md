# Upper-endpoint estimation in a truncated distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Truncated distribution](truncated-distribution.md)

For a positive continuous density $f_\theta$ with strictly increasing [cumulative distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md) $F_\theta$, observations from its upper-truncated law have [likelihood](likelihood-function.md) $\prod_i f_\theta(t_i)/F_\theta(M)^n$ on $M>t_{(n)}$. Holding $\theta$ fixed, decreasing $M$ toward $t_{(n)}$ increases the [likelihood](likelihood-function.md); taking a supremum over $\theta$ preserves nonincreasing dependence on $M$. Provided the nuisance fit is finite, the usual closed-support endpoint estimate is $t_{(n)}$. With the literal open support $t<M$, only a limiting supremum need exist. A sample maximum is not a validated bound for future observations, and support depending on a parameter prevents automatic application of [Wilks theorem](wilks-theorem.md).
