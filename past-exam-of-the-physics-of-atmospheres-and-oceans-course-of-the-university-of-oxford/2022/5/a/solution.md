<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The constants $R_a,R_b,R_d$ are effective reservoir capacities: stored [carbon dioxide](../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) [mass](../../../../mass.md) anomaly per unit effective concentration anomaly, in GtCO$_2$ per ppmv. Only $C_a$ is a literal atmospheric mole fraction; $C_b,C_d$ are scaled reservoir variables chosen to share its equilibrium value. The exchange coefficients $r,s$ are conductances, in GtCO$_2$ per ppmv per year: $r(C_a-C_b)$ is transfer from the [atmosphere](../../../../atmosphere.md) to the fast reservoir and $s(C_b-C_d)$ transfer from that reservoir to the deep [ocean](../../../../ocean.md). They are not by themselves inverse times; division by a reservoir capacity gives an inverse-time rate.

[Hydrostatic balance](../../../../hydrostatic-balance.md) estimates total atmospheric [mass](../../../../mass.md) as $M_{\rm air}=4\pi R_E^2\bar p/g$. One ppmv of a dilute [ideal gas](../../../../ideal-gas-split.md) is a mole fraction $10^{-6}$, so, with dry-air [molar mass](../../../../molar-mass.md) $M_{\rm mol,air}\simeq28.97$ g/mol and [carbon dioxide](../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) [molar mass](../../../../molar-mass.md) $44.01$ g/mol,

$$
R_a=\frac{4\pi(6.371\times10^6)^2(98500)}{9.81}
\frac{44.01}{28.97}\frac{10^{-6}}{10^{12}}
\simeq\boxed{7.8\,\mathrm{GtCO_2/ppmv}.}
$$

The final factor converts kilograms to gigatonnes. This is [carbon dioxide](../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) [mass](../../../../mass.md), not the smaller [carbon](../../../../carbon.md) [mass](../../../../mass.md).
