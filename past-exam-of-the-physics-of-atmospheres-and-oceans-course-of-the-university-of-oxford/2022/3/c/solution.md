<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let $1<S<S_c$ be the ambient [saturation ratio](../../../../saturation-ratio.md). There are two equilibrium radii: $r_-<r_c<r_+$. [Condensation](../../../../condensation.md) occurs if $S>S_{\rm eq}(r)$; [evaporation](../../../../evaporation.md) occurs if $S<S_{\rm eq}(r)$. Therefore droplets with $r<r_-$ grow towards $r_-$, while those with $r_-<r<r_c$ shrink towards $r_-$. The smaller equilibrium is stable.

Above $r_c$, droplets with $r_c<r<r_+$ still undergo [evaporation](../../../../evaporation.md) and ultimately approach $r_-$. Droplets with $r>r_+$ grow, and the larger equilibrium $r_+$ is unstable. Merely exceeding $r_c$ is therefore **not sufficient** for continued growth at subcritical ambient [atmospheric supersaturation](../../../../atmospheric-supersaturation.md). For example, if $(S-1)/(S_c-1)=0.6$, the two intersections have approximately $r_-/r_c=0.676$ and $r_+/r_c=2.349$; a droplet of radius $1.5r_c$ undergoes [evaporation](../../../../evaporation.md). If instead $S>S_c$, there is no equilibrium barrier in this approximation and droplets activate into growing [cloud droplets](../../../../cloud-droplet.md).

[Köhler theory](../../../../kohler-theory.md) explains activation of [cloud condensation nuclei](../../../../cloud-condensation-nucleus.md), rather than rapid production of [atmospheric precipitation](../../../../atmospheric-precipitation.md). [Diffusion-controlled droplet growth](../../../../diffusion-controlled-droplet-growth.md) is limited by [diffusion](../../../../diffusion.md) of [water](../../../../water.md) [vapor](../../../../vapor.md) and removal of released [latent heat](../../../../latent-heat.md); approximately $d(r^2)/dt$ is proportional to the ambient [atmospheric supersaturation](../../../../atmospheric-supersaturation.md). It slows in radius and tends to narrow a droplet size distribution. The available [water](../../../../water.md) [vapor](../../../../vapor.md), changing [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) and shared depletion of [atmospheric supersaturation](../../../../atmospheric-supersaturation.md) also invalidate unlimited growth at prescribed $S$. Formation of rain-sized drops normally requires [collision-coalescence](../../../../collision-coalescence.md), while colder [clouds](../../../../cloud.md) can additionally use [cloud ice nucleation versus ice growth](../../../../cloud-ice-nucleation-versus-ice-growth.md).
