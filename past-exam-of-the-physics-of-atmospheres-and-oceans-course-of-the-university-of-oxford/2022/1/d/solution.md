↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

No. Near the [liquid-gas critical point](../../../../liquid-gas-critical-point.md), the [mass densities](../../../../density.md) of [liquid](../../../../liquid.md) and [vapor](../../../../vapor.md) approach each other, so the neglected condensed-phase specific volume becomes comparable with the [vapor](../../../../vapor.md) specific volume. The [vapor](../../../../vapor.md) also ceases to obey the dilute [ideal gas law](../../../../ideal-gas-law.md). Although the [latent heat](../../../../latent-heat.md) tends to zero, the specific-volume difference tends to zero too: the exact [Clausius-Clapeyron relation](../../../../clausius-clapeyron-relation.md) contains their ratio. Setting only the numerator to zero therefore does not justify a vanishing physical [saturation pressure](../../../../vapor-pressure.md) slope. A realistic [equation of state](../../../../equation-of-state.md) and coexistence data are required near the surface; the dilute colder upper [atmosphere](../../../../atmosphere.md) can still be described approximately by the earlier expression.
