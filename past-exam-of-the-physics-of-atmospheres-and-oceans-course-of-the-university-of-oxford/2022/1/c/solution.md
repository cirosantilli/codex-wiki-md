<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For constant [gravitational acceleration](../../../../gravitational-acceleration.md), [hydrostatic balance](../../../../hydrostatic-balance.md) gives the [atmospheric column mass](../../../../atmospheric-column-mass.md) $m=p_{\rm sfc}/g$, with negligible [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md) at the top. For the same surface [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) $T_*<T_{\rm sfc}<T_c$, the variable [latent heat](../../../../latent-heat.md) gives a smaller integrated $\ln(p_{\rm sfc}/p_*)$, and hence

$$
\boxed{\frac{m_{\rm variable}}{m_{\rm fixed}}=
\exp\left\{K\left[T_c\left(\frac1{T_*}-\frac1{T_{\rm sfc}}\right)-\ln\frac{T_{\rm sfc}}{T_*}\right]
-\frac{L_*}{R_{\rm wv}}\left(\frac1{T_*}-\frac1{T_{\rm sfc}}\right)\right\}<1.}
$$

Multiplying by the same planetary surface area gives the same comparison for total [mass](../../../../mass.md). The models agree for a surface at or below the [triple point](../../../../triple-point.md), where they use the same [sublimation](../../../../sublimation.md) branch. The comparison assumes the same surface [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md), rather than the same surface [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md).
