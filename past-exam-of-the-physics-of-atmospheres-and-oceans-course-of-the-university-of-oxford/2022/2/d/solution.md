<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

At fixed $T_s$ and $a>0$, the outgoing [radiant flux](../../../../radiant-flux.md) scales as $F\propto\tau_\infty^{-2a}$. Increasing the [optical depth](../../../../optical-depth.md) shifts the effective emitting region to lower [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md) and lower [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md). A surface [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) cannot be inferred from emission alone: specify the absorbed stellar [radiant flux](../../../../radiant-flux.md) $F_0$ and impose [radiative equilibrium](../../../../radiative-equilibrium.md), $F=F_0$. If the no-[atmosphere](../../../../atmosphere.md) [blackbody](../../../../blackbody.md) has $T_0=(F_0/\sigma)^{1/4}$, then

$$
\boxed{\frac{T_s}{T_0}\sim
\left[\frac{a+1}{\Gamma(2a+1)}\right]^{1/4}\tau_\infty^{a/2}.}
$$

The vertical-ray approximation omits $a+1$ from this prefactor. For fixed $a>0$ and sufficiently large [optical depth](../../../../optical-depth.md), the surface is warmer than $T_0$: this is the thermal [greenhouse effect](../../../../greenhouse-effect.md) within the prescribed [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) profile. It does not by itself establish a self-consistent vertical heating or [convection](../../../../convection-split.md) balance.
