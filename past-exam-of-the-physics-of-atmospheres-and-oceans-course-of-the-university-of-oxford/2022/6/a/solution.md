<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use a shallow, homogeneous active upper layer of mean thickness $h_0$ and [mass density](../../../../density.md) $\rho_1$, over a much deeper, effectively stationary layer with [mass density](../../../../density.md) $\rho_2>\rho_1$. The [Boussinesq approximation](../../../../boussinesq-approximation.md) keeps the small density contrast only in [buoyancy](../../../../buoyancy.md), giving [reduced gravity](../../../../reduced-gravity-split.md) $g'=g(\rho_2-\rho_1)/\rho_0$. The [hydrostatic approximation](../../../../hydrostatic-approximation.md) and [shallow-water approximation](../../../../shallow-water-approximation.md) make horizontal [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) nearly depth independent and turn the interface slope into the horizontal force $-g'\nabla h$.

Linearize about rest and uniform $h_0$, with $|h-h_0|\ll h_0$, neglecting products of perturbations and [convective acceleration](../../../../convective-acceleration.md). The [f-plane](../../../../f-plane.md) approximation sets the [Coriolis parameter](../../../../coriolis-parameter.md) to constant $f_0$. Neglect wind forcing, [viscosity](../../../../dynamic-viscosity-split.md), entrainment and bottom drag. The two horizontal [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) equations then balance local [acceleration](../../../../acceleration.md), the [Coriolis force](../../../../coriolis-force.md) and the [pressure gradient](../../../../pressure-gradient.md). The signs $-f_0v$ and $+f_0u$ follow from eastward and northward coordinates.

The thickness equation is the linearized [conservation of mass](../../../../mass-conservation.md) law: horizontal [divergence](../../../../divergence.md) decreases $h$, while horizontal convergence increases it. Here $h$ is active-layer thickness, not total ocean depth, and $g'$ rather than $g$ gives the restoring force because the motion principally displaces the [thermocline](../../../../thermocline.md).
