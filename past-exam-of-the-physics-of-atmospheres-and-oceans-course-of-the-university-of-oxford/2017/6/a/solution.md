<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Weather-forecast errors arise from uncertainty in the initial atmospheric state, including [measurement noise](../../../../measurement-error.md), incomplete spatial coverage and imperfect [data assimilation](../../../../data-assimilation.md); from model error, including unresolved [clouds](../../../../cloud.md), [convection](../../../../convection-split.md) and turbulence, approximate physical parameterizations and imperfect boundary forcing; and from [discretization](../../../../discretization.md), [time stepping](../../../../numerical-integration-split.md) and [roundoff](../../../../round-off-error.md). [Sensitive dependence on initial conditions](../../../../butterfly-effect.md) amplifies suitable small errors through nonlinear dynamics, limiting deterministic predictability even with a good model. It is an amplification mechanism rather than a separate observational noise source. [Ensemble forecasts](../../../../ensemble-forecasting.md) sample some initial-state and model uncertainty, but their spread need not represent every systematic error.
