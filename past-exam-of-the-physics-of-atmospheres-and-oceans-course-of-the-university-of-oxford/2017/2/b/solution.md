<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Using the [adiabatic cloud droplet moments](../../../../adiabatic-cloud-droplet-moments.md), define $N=\int n(r)\,dr$ and the [mean volume radius](../../../../adiabatic-cloud-droplet-moments.md) by $r_v^3=N^{-1}\int r^3n(r)\,dr$. Summing the liquid mass of spherical [droplets](../../../../cloud-droplet.md) gives

$$
w=\frac{4\pi\rho_w}{3}\int r^3n(r)\,dr
=\frac{4\pi\rho_w}{3}Nr_v^3.
$$

Therefore, with the shallow-cloud [liquid-water content](../../../../liquid-water-content.md) $w=c_wh$,

$$
\boxed{r_v(h)=\left(\frac{3c_wh}{4\pi\rho_wN}\right)^{1/3}.}
$$

The growth is $h^{1/3}$ at fixed [number concentration](../../../../number-density.md), and the radius scales as $N^{-1/3}$ at fixed [water content](../../../../liquid-water-content.md). The dimensions are consistent: $c_w$ has units $\mathrm{kg\,m^{-4}}$, so $c_wh/(\rho_wN)$ has units $\mathrm{m^3}$. The formula is a third-moment radius; a monodisperse distribution is not required. Fixed $N$ per volume is the shallow-cloud approximation, not exact conservation of number per parcel volume during an arbitrarily deep expansion.
