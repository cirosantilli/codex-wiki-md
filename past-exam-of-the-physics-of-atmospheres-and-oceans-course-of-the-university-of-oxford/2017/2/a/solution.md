<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Below [cloud base](../../../../lifting-condensation-level.md), an initially unsaturated closed parcel expands as ambient [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md) falls. In the absence of heat exchange its [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) falls approximately at the [dry adiabatic lapse rate](../../../../dry-adiabatic-lapse-rate.md) $g/c_{p,d}$. Its vapour mixing ratio is conserved, but its saturation mixing ratio decreases strongly as it cools. Hence [relative humidity](../../../../relative-humidity.md) rises until the [lifting condensation level](../../../../lifting-condensation-level.md) is reached. The [density](../../../../density.md) normally decreases through expansion; the environmental profile determines the parcel's [buoyancy](../../../../buoyancy.md) and thus whether ascent can continue.

Near saturation, slight [atmospheric supersaturation](../../../../atmospheric-supersaturation.md) permits suitable [cloud condensation nuclei](../../../../cloud-condensation-nucleus.md) to activate. [Köhler theory](../../../../kohler-theory.md) describes the competition between curvature, which raises equilibrium [vapour pressure](../../../../vapor-pressure.md), and dissolved solute, which lowers it. Cooling generates [supersaturation](../../../../atmospheric-supersaturation.md), while [condensation](../../../../condensation.md) onto the new [droplets](../../../../cloud-droplet.md) consumes it. Their balance normally keeps in-cloud [relative humidity](../../../../relative-humidity.md) close to, but not identically at, saturation. The activated [droplet number](../../../../number-density.md) depends on the [aerosol](../../../../aerosol.md) population and the maximum [supersaturation](../../../../atmospheric-supersaturation.md) produced by the ascent.

Above [cloud base](../../../../lifting-condensation-level.md), [condensation](../../../../condensation.md) lowers the vapour content and raises the [liquid-water content](../../../../liquid-water-content.md). The [latent heat](../../../../latent-heat.md) released partly offsets expansion cooling, so the [moist adiabat](../../../../moist-adiabat.md) has a smaller lapse-rate magnitude than the dry one. In a dilute approximation,

$$
\Gamma_m\simeq\frac g{c_{p,d}}
\frac{1+L_vq_s/(R_dT)}{1+L_v^2q_s/(c_{p,d}R_vT^2)}.
$$

A closed adiabatic parcel conserves total water $q_t=q_v+q_l$ and approximately its [moist entropy](../../../../entropy-split.md); retaining condensate makes this a reversible-cloud idealization, rather than a precipitating pseudoadiabat. In a shallow [cloud](../../../../cloud.md), the saturation-mixing-ratio decrease and slow [density](../../../../density.md) variation yield nearly constant [condensation](../../../../condensation.md) per height, so [liquid-water content](../../../../liquid-water-content.md) is approximately linear above base. Once [activation](../../../../cloud-droplet-activation.md) is complete, assuming nearly fixed [droplet number](../../../../number-density.md) per volume gives increasing [droplet](../../../../cloud-droplet.md) size as condensate accumulates. [Entrainment](../../../../fluid-entrainment.md), continued [activation](../../../../cloud-droplet-activation.md), [collision-coalescence](../../../../collision-coalescence.md) and [atmospheric precipitation](../../../../atmospheric-precipitation.md) can all violate that simplified description; they are excluded from the stated retrieval model.
