<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Write $h=h_0+\eta$, with small interface displacement $\eta$ and horizontal variation scale $L$. The geostrophic pressure-Coriolis balance gives

$$
fU\sim g'\eta/L,\qquad U\sim\frac{g'\eta}{|f|L}.
$$

Subtracting the uniform reference state and the mass-conserving linear potential-energy term, the perturbation [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) densities per unit horizontal area are

$$
\mathrm{KE}\simeq\tfrac12\rho_1h_0U^2,\qquad
\mathrm{PE}\simeq\tfrac12\rho_1g'\eta^2.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{\frac{\mathrm{KE}}{\mathrm{PE}}\sim
\frac{g'h_0}{f^2L^2}=\left(\frac{L_R}{L}\right)^2,
\qquad L_R=\frac{\sqrt{g'h_0}}{|f|}.}
$$

The internal [deformation radius](../../../../deformation-radius.md) $L_R$ is the distance an internal [gravity wave](../../../../gravity-wave-split.md) travels in an inverse-Coriolis time. Balanced broad disturbances $L\gg L_R$ mainly store available [potential energy](../../../../potential-energy.md); balanced fine structures $L\ll L_R$ require relatively large [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md). This scale result presupposes small [Rossby number](../../../../rossby-number.md) $U/(|f|L)$; reducing the length at fixed displacement eventually violates [geostrophy](../../../../geostrophic-flow.md). It does not say an initially resting small-scale height perturbation already has that large [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md): such a perturbation undergoes [geostrophic adjustment](../../../../geostrophic-adjustment.md).
