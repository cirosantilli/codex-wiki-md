↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The first term describes [collision-coalescence](../../../../collision-coalescence.md): a collector sweeps area $\pi R^2$, overtakes small droplets at relative terminal speed $u_R-u_r$, encounters liquid [mass](../../../../mass.md) per volume $L$, and retains a fraction $E$ of that intercepted liquid. The second term describes diffusion-limited condensational growth, with vapour supersaturation $S-1$ and combined thermal and vapour-diffusion resistances $F_k+F_d$. Condensation is positive for $S>1$ and reverses to evaporation for $S<1$. The [liquid-water content](../../../../liquid-water-content.md) $L$ here is a local mass-per-volume quantity, not a vertically integrated [liquid-water path](../../../../liquid-water-path.md).
