<h1 id="4/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

A broad initial [cloud-droplet size distribution](../../../../cloud-droplet-size-distribution.md) can be seeded by giant or unusually soluble [cloud condensation nuclei](../../../../cloud-condensation-nucleus.md), producing a minority of larger droplets before gravitational collection becomes efficient. Variable supersaturation histories, entrainment and mixing can broaden the distribution relative to a perfectly homogeneous monodisperse model. Turbulent relative motion and preferential concentration can increase encounters, and rare successful collisions can create unusually rapid stochastic collectors. These mechanisms can work together; a single deterministic condensational law applied equally to identical droplets cannot create the initial differential fall speeds by itself. For warm rain, the subsequent amplification is liquid [collision-coalescence](../../../../collision-coalescence.md), distinct from ice-phase precipitation mechanisms.
