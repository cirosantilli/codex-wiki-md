<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Linearize about a uniform horizontal flow $U$ and a hydrostatic, vertically stratified basic state. The basic-flow [material derivative](../../../../material-derivative.md) is $D_0=\partial_t+U\partial_x$. The first two equations are the horizontal and vertical perturbation momentum balances: [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md) gradients accelerate the disturbance, while the vertical equation retains the [buoyancy](../../../../buoyancy.md) force $-g\rho'/\rho_0$ after the basic [hydrostatic balance](../../../../hydrostatic-balance.md) is subtracted.

The [Boussinesq approximation](../../../../boussinesq-approximation.md) neglects fractional [density](../../../../density.md) variations in [inertia](../../../../inertia.md) and enforces [incompressibility](../../../../incompressible-flow.md), $u_x+w_z=0$, but retains them in [buoyancy](../../../../buoyancy.md). Advecting the stable background [density](../../../../density.md) gradient gives $D_0\rho'+w\,d\bar\rho/dz=0$. With $N^2=-(g/\rho_0)d\bar\rho/dz>0$, this is the fourth supplied equation. A displaced parcel then has a restoring [buoyancy](../../../../buoyancy.md) tendency.

Small amplitude allows products of perturbations to be discarded; two-dimensional motion removes $y$ dependence and transverse flow. Rotation, viscosity, [diffusion](../../../../diffusion.md), diabatic heating and mean shear are neglected. $\rho_0$ is the reference inertial [density](../../../../density.md), whereas the small background [density](../../../../density.md) gradient still sets $N$. In a compressible real atmosphere the appropriate stability measure involves [potential temperature](../../../../potential-temperature.md); this Boussinesq system is a local idealization that filters acoustic waves rather than a full compressible atmospheric model.
