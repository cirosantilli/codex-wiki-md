<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Assume the atmosphere is transparent to incoming shortwave radiation, as required for the stated infrared-only radiative-equilibrium model. Global [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) balance gives

$$
\boxed{L_\infty=\frac{(1-\alpha)L_{\circledast}}4.}
$$

Put $D=\tau_\infty$ and $F=I_+-I_-$. [Radiative equilibrium](../../../../radiative-equilibrium.md) requires $dF/d\tau=0$, hence the two equations imply $I_++I_-=2\sigma T^4$. Substitution gives $dI_+/d\tau=dI_-/d\tau=-F/2$. At the top there is no incident infrared flux, $I_-(D)=0$, and $I_+(D)=F=L_\infty$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{I_+(\tau)=L_\infty\left[1+\frac{D-\tau}{2}\right],\qquad
I_-(\tau)=\frac{L_\infty}{2}(D-\tau).}
$$

The black ground supplies $I_+(0)=\sigma T_g^4$. Thus

$$
\boxed{L_\infty=\frac{2\sigma T_g^4}{2+\tau_\infty}.}
$$

The ground also satisfies $\sigma T_g^4=L_\infty+I_-(0)$, showing explicitly that its outgoing flux balances absorbed sunlight plus downward atmospheric radiation. In pure [radiative equilibrium](../../../../radiative-equilibrium.md) the air just above the ground need not have the ground's [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md): $\sigma T(0)^4=L_\infty(1+D)/2$, whereas $\sigma T_g^4=L_\infty(2+D)/2$. That discontinuity is relevant to surface-driven [convection](../../../../convection-split.md) in part (d).
