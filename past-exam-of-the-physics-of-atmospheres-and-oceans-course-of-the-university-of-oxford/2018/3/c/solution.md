<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Adding the two flux solutions gives

$$
\sigma T(\tau)^4=\frac{L_\infty}{2}(1+D-\tau).
$$

The supplied [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md)–optical-depth law gives $D-\tau=Dp/p_0$. With the exponential [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md) model $p=p_0e^{-z/H}$,

$$
\boxed{T(z)=T_\infty\left(1+ae^{-z/H}\right)^{1/4},\quad
T_\infty=\left(\frac{L_\infty}{2\sigma}\right)^{1/4},\quad a=\tau_\infty.}
$$

It approaches the finite atmospheric top [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) $T_\infty$ and has the lowermost-air value $T(0)=T_\infty(1+D)^{1/4}$. Its lapse rate is

$$
-\frac{dT}{dz}=\frac{T(z)}{4H}\frac{De^{-z/H}}{1+De^{-z/H}}.
$$

The stated approximate sign is appropriate physically: a nonisothermal [ideal gas](../../../../ideal-gas-split.md) does not have an exactly constant hydrostatic [scale height](../../../../scale-height.md) $R T/g$. Treating $H$ as a representative constant is an additional vertical-coordinate approximation. Algebraically the displayed profile is exact for the prescribed exponential [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md) and [two-stream](../../../../two-stream-approximation.md) equations.
