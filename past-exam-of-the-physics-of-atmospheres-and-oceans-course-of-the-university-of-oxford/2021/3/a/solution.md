<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Before [condensation](../../../../condensation.md), assume a closed parcel with fixed [atmospheric mixing ratio](../../../../atmospheric-mixing-ratio.md), constant [specific heat capacities](../../../../specific-heat-capacity.md), and negligible irreversible heating. The [first law of thermodynamics](../../../../first-law-of-thermodynamics-split.md) for a dry [adiabatic process](../../../../adiabatic-process.md) is

$$
c_p\,dT-\frac1\rho\,dp=0.
$$

The parcel remains in approximate [pressure equilibrium](../../../../pressure-equilibrium.md) with a hydrostatic environment. With the standard approximation that parcel and environmental [densities](../../../../density.md) are close, $dp/dz\simeq-\rho g$, hence

$$
\frac{dT}{dz}=-\frac g{c_p}=-\Gamma_d,\qquad
\boxed{T(z)=T_s-\Gamma_d z.}
$$

This is the [dry adiabatic lapse rate](../../../../dry-adiabatic-lapse-rate.md), about $9.8\,\mathrm{K\,km^{-1}}$ for air. More precisely the environmental [hydrostatic balance](../../../../hydrostatic-balance.md) gives $dT/dz=-g\rho_{\rm env}/(c_p\rho_{\rm parcel})$; the familiar constant [dry adiabatic lapse rate](../../../../dry-adiabatic-lapse-rate.md) uses their ratio close to one.
