<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [dew-point depression](../../../../dew-point-depression.md) is $6\,\mathrm K$, so

$$
\boxed{z_{\rm LCL}\simeq125\times6\,\mathrm m=750\,\mathrm m.}
$$

Keeping $T_{D,s}=276.15\,\mathrm K$, $H\simeq158.5\,\mathrm{km}$ and $c_p=1004\,\mathrm{J\,kg^{-1}\,K^{-1}}$ gives approximately $747\,\mathrm m$, consistent with the rounded estimate.

The real [cloud](../../../../cloud.md) base can differ because [entrainment](../../../../fluid-entrainment.md) changes the parcel's [atmospheric mixing ratio](../../../../atmospheric-mixing-ratio.md) and [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md), surface [humidity](../../../../humidity.md) varies, and the environmental [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md) profile need not match the assumed parcel profile. Finite [cloud-droplet activation](../../../../cloud-droplet-activation.md) requires some [atmospheric supersaturation](../../../../atmospheric-supersaturation.md), so the first visible [cloud droplets](../../../../cloud-droplet.md) need not occur exactly at the thermodynamic [lifting condensation level](../../../../lifting-condensation-level.md). A parcel must also actually be lifted that far; environmental [static stability](../../../../static-stability.md) can prevent this. The constant-[latent heat](../../../../latent-heat.md) and first-order [Taylor expansion](../../../../taylor-expansion.md) approximations produce smaller numerical differences.
