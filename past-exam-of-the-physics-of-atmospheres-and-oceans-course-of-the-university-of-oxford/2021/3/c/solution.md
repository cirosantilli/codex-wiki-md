<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

At the [lifting condensation level](../../../../lifting-condensation-level.md), the parcel [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) first equals its [dew point](../../../../dew-point.md). For $z/H\ll1$, the first-order [Taylor expansion](../../../../taylor-expansion.md) gives $T_D\simeq T_{D,s}(1-z/H)$. Equating this with the dry-[adiabatic process](../../../../adiabatic-process.md) result gives

$$
T_s-\Gamma_d z=T_{D,s}-\frac{T_{D,s}}H z,\qquad
\boxed{z_{\rm LCL}\simeq\frac{T_s-T_{D,s}}{\Gamma_d-T_{D,s}/H}.}
$$

Near terrestrial surface [temperatures](../../../../temperature-split.md), $T_{D,s}/H\simeq1.7$–$1.9\,\mathrm{K\,km^{-1}}$, whereas $\Gamma_d\simeq9.8\,\mathrm{K\,km^{-1}}$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{z_{\rm LCL}\simeq125\,\mathrm{m\,K^{-1}}(T_s-T_{D,s}).}
$$

Use absolute [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) for $T_{D,s}/H$. The [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) difference can be evaluated in [kelvins](../../../../kelvin.md) or degrees Celsius, whose increments agree. The coefficient is an approximate near-surface terrestrial value, not independent of composition or ambient conditions for all [atmospheres](../../../../atmosphere.md).
