<h1 id="6/d/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In the open-basin [Sverdrup balance](../../../../../sverdrup-balance.md) approximation,

$$
v_{\rm int}(y)=\frac{F_0}\beta\cos(\pi y/b),\qquad
\boxed{v_{\rm int,max}\simeq5.23\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}},\quad\text{northward}.}
$$

The maximum is at $y=0$. Evaluating the exact finite-friction solution instead gives its largest northward [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) at the eastern side of the equator, about $4.96\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$. Thus the requested scale is roughly $5\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ northward; the exact model also resolves variation across the open basin.
