<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [cloud](../../../../cloud.md) absorbs $e\sigma T_g^4$ from below and emits $e\sigma T_c^4$ from each face. It absorbs no stellar [radiation](../../../../radiation.md). Its net radiative heating per unit area is therefore

$$
\boxed{\mathcal R_c=e\sigma(T_g^4-2T_c^4)=e\sigma T_g^4(1-2q^4).}
$$

For $e>0$, a [cloud](../../../../cloud.md) with $q^4<1/2$ warms in the absence of other energy transfers; one with $q^4>1/2$ cools. For the thin [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md) thickness $\delta p$, its initial [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) tendency is approximately $g\mathcal R_c/(c_p\delta p)$ if its sensible [heat capacity](../../../../heat-capacity.md) dominates. [Latent heat](../../../../latent-heat.md) and changing condensate modify that conversion from heating to [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) change, but not the sign of the radiative input.

With $T_g$ fixed, pure [radiative equilibrium](../../../../radiative-equilibrium.md) requires

$$
\boxed{T_{c,\mathrm{rad}}=2^{-1/4}T_g.}
$$

This equals the ambient dry-[adiabatic process](../../../../adiabatic-process.md) [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) precisely when

$$
q^4=\frac12,\qquad \boxed{\frac{p_c}{p_s}=2^{-1/(4\kappa)}}.
$$

For ordinary dry air, $\kappa\simeq2/7$, so the ratio is about $0.545$. If $e=0$, the [cloud](../../../../cloud.md) exchanges no thermal [radiation](../../../../radiation.md) and its [radiation budget](../../../../radiation-budget.md) does not select any equilibrium [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md).
