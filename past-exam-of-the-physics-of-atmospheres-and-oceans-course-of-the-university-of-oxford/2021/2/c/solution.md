<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

At fixed ground [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md), warming the [cloud](../../../../cloud.md) increases its downward [radiant flux](../../../../radiant-flux.md) $e\sigma T_c^4$, giving a positive contribution to the ground's radiative heating. That is the immediate radiative effect. A self-consistent final answer must also remove the [convective heat transfer](../../../../convective-heat-transfer.md) used to pin the original [cloud](../../../../cloud.md) to the dry [adiabatic process](../../../../adiabatic-process.md).

For pure [radiative equilibrium](../../../../radiative-equilibrium.md) of both bodies, with no net nonradiative energy exchange, their two balances are

$$
\sigma T_g^4=Q+e\sigma T_c^4,\qquad
e\sigma T_g^4=2e\sigma T_c^4.
$$

For $e>0$ this gives

$$
\boxed{T_g=T_0(1-e/2)^{-1/4},\qquad T_c=T_0(2-e)^{-1/4}.}
$$

The $e\to0^+$ limit gives the selected limiting [cloud](../../../../cloud.md) [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md); at exactly $e=0$, only $T_g=T_0$ is fixed radiatively. The maximum [greenhouse effect](../../../../greenhouse-effect.md) at fixed $Q$ occurs at $e=1$:

$$
\boxed{T_g=2^{1/4}T_0\simeq1.189T_0.}
$$

The pinned dry-[adiabatic process](../../../../adiabatic-process.md) case instead has maximum factor $1/q=(p_s/p_c)^\kappa$. It exceeds $2^{1/4}$ for a high, cold [cloud](../../../../cloud.md) with $q<2^{-1/4}$, equals it at the level found in part b, and is smaller for a low, warm [cloud](../../../../cloud.md).

In particular, for $q^4<1/2$ the final purely radiative ground is **cooler** than the pinned solution, even though a warmer [cloud](../../../../cloud.md) increases downward [radiation](../../../../radiation.md) at fixed $T_g$. To see why these statements are compatible, let $H$ be the net upward nonradiative energy transfer from the ground to the [cloud](../../../../cloud.md) in the pinned state. Its [conservation of energy](../../../../conservation-of-energy.md) equation requires

$$
H=e\sigma(2T_c^4-T_g^4),\qquad
Q+e\sigma T_c^4-\sigma T_g^4=H.
$$

For the cold [cloud](../../../../cloud.md), $H<0$: maintaining it on the assumed dry [adiabatic process](../../../../adiabatic-process.md) requires removing its net radiative heating and returning energy elsewhere. Setting $H=0$ changes that energy redistribution. It is therefore incorrect to infer the final ground [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) by changing only the downward [radiant flux](../../../../radiant-flux.md). Indeed the final [cloud](../../../../cloud.md) is warmer than the pinned one for $q^4<1/2$ and $0<e<1$, while the final ground is cooler. At $e=1$ the [cloud](../../../../cloud.md) [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) is $T_0$ in both whole-column equilibria.

Fixed [cloud](../../../../cloud.md) properties are an artificial constraint. Warming a saturated [cloud](../../../../cloud.md) increases the [saturation pressure](../../../../vapor-pressure.md), tending to evaporate its [cloud droplets](../../../../cloud-droplet.md); the released [water vapor](../../../../water-vapor.md), changing [latent heat](../../../../latent-heat.md) budget and altered [buoyancy](../../../../buoyancy.md) can change its altitude and [emissivity](../../../../emissivity.md). Cooling favours [condensation](../../../../condensation.md) and potentially [atmospheric precipitation](../../../../atmospheric-precipitation.md). A warm perturbation that survives [evaporation](../../../../evaporation.md) can rise by [convection](../../../../convection-split.md), but neither a unique vertical displacement nor survival of the [cloud](../../../../cloud.md) follows without moisture supply and an environmental [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) profile. Finally, pure [radiative equilibrium](../../../../radiative-equilibrium.md) of the two bodies does not require the intervening transparent gas to remain on the original dry [adiabatic process](../../../../adiabatic-process.md); imposing that extra constraint is generally inconsistent except at the matching level in part b.
