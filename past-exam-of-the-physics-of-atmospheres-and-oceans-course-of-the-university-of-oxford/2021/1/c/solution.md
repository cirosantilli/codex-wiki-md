<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

At the interface the [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md) is continuous. Immediately below it the [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) is $T_s$ and mean [molar mass](../../../../molar-mass.md) is $M_d(1+ax)$; immediately above it the dry [hydrogen](../../../../hydrogen.md) has [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) $T_1$. The [ideal gas law](../../../../ideal-gas-law.md) therefore gives

$$
\rho_- =\frac{p_1M_d(1+ax)}{RT_s},\qquad
\rho_+=\frac{p_1M_d}{RT_1}.
$$

An infinitesimal interchange is unstable to [convection](../../../../convection-split.md) when the lower fluid is less dense than the upper fluid. Hence

$$
\boxed{\frac{T_s}{T_1}>1+a\frac{E(T_s)}{P(T_s)}.}
$$

Equality gives neutral interfacial [buoyancy](../../../../buoyancy.md); reversing the inequality gives a stable [density](../../../../density.md) jump. This is an interfacial criterion, not a claim that the whole dry upper [atmosphere](../../../../atmosphere.md) is stable. Its vertical [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) profile has not been specified. Also, $\mathrm{RH}(p_1)=p_1/P<1$, so infinitesimal displacement at the interface initially involves no [condensation](../../../../condensation.md) or release of [latent heat](../../../../latent-heat.md).

There are two competing effects. Increasing $T_s$ directly reduces the lower [density](../../../../density.md), favouring [convection](../../../../convection-split.md). Increasing the [mole fraction](../../../../mole-fraction.md) of heavy [water vapor](../../../../water-vapor.md) increases its mean [molar mass](../../../../molar-mass.md), opposing [convection](../../../../convection-split.md). With $x\ll1$, the criterion is approximately $T_s>T_1$. With $x\to1$, it tends to $T_s>\epsilon T_1\simeq9T_1$: substantial surface heating need not overcome the compositional stabilization in [hydrogen](../../../../hydrogen.md).

For a specified $P_d,\Delta p,T_1$, solve $T_s/T_1-1-ax(T_s)=0$ on the admissible branch to locate the transitions. Its [derivative](../../../../derivative.md) is $1/T_1-a x'(T_s)$, so there is no universal monotone trend: rapid growth of the [saturation pressure](../../../../vapor-pressure.md) can suppress an instability that was present at lower $T_s$. Without numerical parameter values, neither the number of crossings nor a unique interval of unstable [temperatures](../../../../temperature-split.md) can be asserted.
