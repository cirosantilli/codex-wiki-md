# Supplementary motor area

↑ **Parent:** [Frontal lobe](frontal-lobe.md)

A medial frontal motor region implicated in planning and sequencing actions, including self-initiated movement. It participates in interactions with [basal ganglia](basal-ganglia.md) and other motor systems.
