# Renormalizable chiral-superfield action

↑ **Parent:** [Supersymmetric action](supersymmetric-action.md)

In four spacetime dimensions, power-counting [renormalizability](renormalizable-quantum-field-theory.md) restricts an ungauged chiral action to a canonical quadratic [Kähler potential](kahler-potential.md), up to transformations, and a [superpotential](superpotential.md) of degree at most three. Terms of larger degree belong to an [effective field theory](effective-field-theory.md).
