# Modified Newtonian dynamics

↑ **Parent:** [Modified gravity](modified-gravity.md)

A low-acceleration modification associated with [Mordehai Milgrom](mordehai-milgrom.md). For an isolated spherical system, $g\mu(g/a_0)=g_N$, with $\mu(x)\to1$ at high $x$ and $\mu(x)\to x$ at low $x$. An isolated point-like baryonic distribution then gives $v_c^4=GM_ba_0$ and a flat asymptotic [galaxy rotation curve](galaxy-rotation-curve.md). The algebraic spherical relation is not a complete relativistic field theory and should not be assumed to hold vectorially for arbitrary [mass](mass.md) distributions.[Pages 365–370](a-modification-of-the-newtonian-dynamics-as-a-possible-alternative-to-the-hidden-mass-hypothesis-by-mordehai-milgrom.md)
