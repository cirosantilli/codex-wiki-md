# Normal modes of surface gravity waves in a rectangular tank

↑ **Parent:** [Surface gravity wave](surface-gravity-wave.md)

For a tank with side lengths $a,b$, depth $h$, impermeable walls and negligible [surface tension](surface-tension-split.md), the small-amplitude [surface gravity wave](surface-gravity-wave.md) [normal modes](normal-mode.md) have horizontal factors $\cos(m\pi x/a)\cos(n\pi y/b)$ and $k_{mn}^2=(m\pi/a)^2+(n\pi/b)^2$. The [velocity potential](velocity-potential.md) has vertical factor $\cosh(k_{mn}(z+h))$, satisfying the bottom [Neumann boundary condition](neumann-boundary-condition.md). Combining the [linearized free-surface boundary conditions](linearized-free-surface-boundary-conditions.md) gives $\omega_{mn}^2=gk_{mn}\tanh(k_{mn}h)$. The uniform $m=n=0$ [surface elevation](surface-elevation.md) is excluded when the total water volume is fixed. Taking one horizontal index zero gives the two-dimensional tank modes.

For $k_{mn}h\ll1$, the [shallow-water approximation](shallow-water-approximation.md) gives $\omega_{mn}\sim\sqrt{gh}\,k_{mn}$; for $k_{mn}h\gg1$, the [deep-water gravity wave](deep-water-gravity-wave.md) relation $\omega_{mn}^2\sim gk_{mn}$ follows. An [oscillating-wall forcing of a rectangular tank](oscillating-wall-forcing-of-a-rectangular-tank.md) couples to these same [normal modes](normal-mode.md) and becomes resonant at their natural frequencies.
