# Exponential of a Pauli vector

↑ **Parent:** [Pauli matrices](pauli-matrices.md)

For a real [unit vector](unit-vector.md) $\mathbf n$, the [Pauli matrix multiplication law](pauli-matrix-multiplication-law.md) gives $(\mathbf n\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma)^2=I$. Separating the even and odd powers in the [matrix exponential](matrix-exponential.md) proves the displayed identity. For [spin one-half](spin-one-half.md), the physical rotation through [angle](angle.md) $\theta$ has $a=\theta/2$.
