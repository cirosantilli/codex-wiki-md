# Periodic surface homeomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Nielsen–Thurston classification](nielsen-thurston-classification.md)

A periodic surface homeomorphism has a positive power equal to the identity. A mapping class is periodic when it has such a representative. Allowing motion of boundary circles is a different convention from requiring every [isotopy](isotopy.md) to fix the boundary pointwise.
