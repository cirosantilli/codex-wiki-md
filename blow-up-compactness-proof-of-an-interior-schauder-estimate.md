# Blow-up compactness proof of an interior Schauder estimate

↑ **Parent:** [Interior Schauder estimate](interior-schauder-estimate.md)

A compactness proof of the [interior Schauder estimate](interior-schauder-estimate.md) can magnify a pair of points where the [Hölder seminorm](holder-seminorm.md) of the [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) is nearly attained. If the proposed estimate fails, normalize that seminorm to one while lower norms and forcing tend to zero. The separation $r_j$ of the chosen pair tends to zero. Subtract the quadratic [Taylor polynomial](taylor-polynomial.md) and divide by $r_j^{2+\alpha}$ after rescaling space by $r_j$.

The resulting functions have uniformly bounded Hessian [Hölder seminorms](holder-seminorm.md) on expanding domains and vanishing Taylor data at zero. [Compact embedding of Hölder spaces](compact-embedding-of-holder-spaces.md) yields an entire [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md) whose second derivatives have finite global $\alpha$-Hölder seminorms, with $0<\alpha<1$. The [polynomial-growth Liouville theorem for harmonic functions](polynomial-growth-liouville-theorem-for-harmonic-functions.md) makes those derivatives constant, contradicting the normalized nonzero oscillation. The [Simon absorption lemma](simon-absorption-lemma.md) removes the residual small multiple of the larger-ball seminorm.
