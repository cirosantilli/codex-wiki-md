# Adjoining a root by an HNN extension

↑ **Parent:** [HNN extension](hnn-extension.md)

To adjoin an $n$th root of $g\in G$, let $m$ be the [order of a group element](order-of-a-group-element.md) $g$ and take a [cyclic group](cyclic-group.md) $C=\langle a\rangle$ of order $nm$ when $m$ is finite, or infinite order when $g$ has infinite order. In the [free product](free-product.md) $G*C$, $\langle a^n\rangle$ and $\langle g\rangle$ have the same order. The [HNN extension](hnn-extension.md) with $t^{-1}a^nt=g$ therefore embeds its base by [Britton's lemma](britton-s-lemma.md), and $h=t^{-1}at$ satisfies $h^n=g$. If $g=1$, take $C$ of order $n$ and identify the two trivial subgroups. This is an existence construction, not an algorithm for finding an unknown element order.
