# Compact elliptic spectral theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Compact resolvent](compact-resolvent.md)

On a [closed manifold](closed-manifold.md) with a [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md) the [positive Laplace-Beltrami operator](positive-laplace-beltrami-operator.md) is [self-adjoint](self-adjoint-operator.md) with [compact resolvent](compact-resolvent.md). It has a complete smooth [orthonormal eigenbasis](orthonormal-eigenbasis.md), finite-dimensional [eigenspaces](eigenspace.md), nonnegative [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) tending to infinity, and polynomial eigenvalue-counting bounds. [Elliptic regularity](elliptic-regularity.md) bounds derivatives of its [eigenfunctions](eigenfunction.md) polynomially in their [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md).
