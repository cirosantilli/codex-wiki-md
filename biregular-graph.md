# Biregular graph

↑ **Parent:** [Bipartite graph](bipartite-graph.md)

A biregular graph is a [bipartite graph](bipartite-graph.md) whose vertices have constant degree on each part separately. The two degree constants can differ. If its parts are $X,Y$ and its [edge density of a bipartite graph](edge-density-of-a-bipartite-graph.md) is $\gamma$, the two degrees are $\gamma|Y|$ and $\gamma|X|$. Thus its adjacency function has constant row and column averages $\gamma$.
