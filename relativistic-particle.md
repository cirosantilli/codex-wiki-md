# Relativistic particle

↑ **Parent:** [Special relativity](special-relativity-split.md)

A relativistic massive particle is described by a timelike worldline with proper-time four-velocity $u^\mu$. In signature $(+---)$ its normalization is $u^\mu u_\mu=c^2$ and its momentum is $p^\mu=mu^\mu$. The [relativistic charged-particle action](relativistic-charged-particle-action.md) adds electromagnetic coupling, whose variation gives the [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md).
