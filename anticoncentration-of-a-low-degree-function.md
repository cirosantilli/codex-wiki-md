# Anticoncentration of a low-degree function

↑ **Parent:** [Bonami lemma](bonami-lemma.md)

If a nonzero function on the [Boolean hypercube](boolean-hypercube.md) has degree at most $k$, then its support has measure at least $2^{-k}$. This sharp bound follows by induction on the dimension.
