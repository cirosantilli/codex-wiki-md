# Riemann integrability is closed under addition and maximum

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann integrability criterion](riemann-integrability-criterion.md)

For a common interval partition, the [oscillation of a function on an interval](oscillation-of-a-function-on-an-interval.md) satisfies $\operatorname{osc}_I(f+g)\le\operatorname{osc}_I f+\operatorname{osc}_I g$. Refining two good [Darboux sums](darboux-sum.md) therefore proves integrability of the sum. [Lipschitz composition preserves Riemann integrability](lipschitz-composition-preserves-riemann-integrability.md), so absolute values preserve it too. The identity $\max(f,g)=(f+g+|f-g|)/2$ then proves the assertion for the maximum.
