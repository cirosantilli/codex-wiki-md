# Magnetic energy balance in resistive magnetohydrodynamics

↑ **Parent:** [Resistive magnetohydrodynamics](resistive-magnetohydrodynamics-split.md)

For constant [electrical resistivity](electrical-resistivity.md) $\eta$ and [magnetic permeability](permeability-electromagnetism.md) $\mu_0$, let $E_B=\int_VB^2/(2\mu_0)\,dV$ and $\mathbf J=\nabla\times\mathbf B/\mu_0$. In a fixed volume with zero boundary energy flux, multiply the [resistive induction equation](resistive-induction-equation.md) by $\mathbf B/\mu_0$ and integrate by parts. This gives the displayed balance. The first term transfers [energy](energy-split.md) between the [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) and the fluid; the second is nonpositive and represents [Joule heating](joule-heating.md). The latter does not imply that the total [magnetic energy](magnetic-energy.md) must decrease if fluid motion continually supplies it.
