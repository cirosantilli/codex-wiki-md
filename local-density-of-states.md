# Local density of states

↑ **Parent:** [Density of states](density-of-states.md)

The [local density of states](local-density-of-states.md) resolves the [density of states](density-of-states.md) by [position](position.md) as well as [energy](energy-split.md). With a consistent counting of [spin](spin.md) states, its [integral](integral-split.md) over the specimen's [volume](volume.md) gives the total [density of states](density-of-states.md), $g(E)=\int\rho(\mathbf r,E)\,d^3\mathbf r$. It describes the spatial distribution of available states, rather than their occupation.
