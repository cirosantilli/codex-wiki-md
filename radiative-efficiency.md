# Radiative efficiency

↑ **Parent:** [Accretion luminosity](accretion-luminosity.md)

The [radiative efficiency](radiative-efficiency.md) is the escaping radiative power divided by an adopted accreted rest-mass [energy](energy-split.md) rate. Its meaning depends on where the [mass](mass.md) rate is measured and which emission is counted. For efficient Newtonian [accretion](accretion.md) onto a stellar surface it scales as $GM/(R_*c^2)$, whereas advection or outflows can reduce luminosity for the same supplied [mass](mass.md).
