# Phase slip

↑ **Parent:** [Phase oscillator](phase-oscillator.md)

An event changing the unwrapped relative phase by one cycle, usually $2\pi$, compared with a phase-locked state. [Thermally activated phase slips](thermally-activated-phase-slip.md) correspond to crossing neighboring barriers of a [tilted washboard potential](tilted-washboard-potential.md). Successive slips can produce long-term drift even while small fluctuations appear confined on shorter times.
