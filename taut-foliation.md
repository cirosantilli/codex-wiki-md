# Taut foliation

↑ **Parent:** [Foliation](foliation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taut_foliation)

A codimension-one [foliation](foliation.md) of a closed [three-manifold](3-manifold.md) is taut if every leaf meets a closed transverse curve. In particular, the fibers of a bundle over $S^1$ form a taut [foliation](foliation.md).
