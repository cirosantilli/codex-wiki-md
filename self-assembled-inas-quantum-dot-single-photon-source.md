# Self-assembled InAs quantum-dot single-photon source

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum dot](quantum-dot.md)

A [quantum dot](quantum-dot.md) of InAs embedded in [gallium arsenide](gallium-arsenide.md) confines injected [electrons](electron.md) and [electron holes](electron-hole.md). Strain-driven island formation supplies dots with lateral dimensions of order tens of nanometres and heights of a few nanometres. In an electrically driven implementation, a dot lies in a junction's intrinsic region. Selecting one dot spatially and one radiative line spectrally turns discrete [exciton](exciton.md) recombination into a [single-photon source](single-photon-source.md). Pulsed injection must avoid re-excitation during the same collection window.

The pioneering electrically driven device used an areal dot density around $5\times10^8\,\mathrm{cm^{-2}}$, a GaAs p-i-n structure and an aperture selecting individual-dot light.[Device description and figure 1, printed p.103](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1066790)
