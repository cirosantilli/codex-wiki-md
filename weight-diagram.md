# Weight diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Weight-space decomposition](weight-space-decomposition.md)

A diagram placing the [weights](weight-representation-theory.md) of a representation in the real span of its [weight lattice](weight-lattice.md), recording each [weight multiplicity](weight-multiplicity.md). Coincident weights must retain their multiplicity: for an [Adjoint representation](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md) the zero-weight multiplicity is the dimension of its [Cartan subalgebra](cartan-subalgebra.md).
