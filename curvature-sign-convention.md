# Curvature sign convention

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann curvature tensor](riemann-curvature-tensor.md)

Definitions of the [Riemann curvature tensor](riemann-curvature-tensor.md) can differ by an overall sign, independent of the [metric signature](metric-signature.md). Reversing that definition reverses the [Ricci tensor](ricci-tensor.md), [Ricci scalar](ricci-scalar.md) and [Einstein tensor](einstein-tensor.md), as well as the [curvature](curvature.md) side of the [geodesic deviation](geodesic-deviation.md) identity and the [Palatini identity](palatini-identity.md). A derivation must choose the contraction and [derivative](derivative.md) ordering explicitly before combining formulas from different conventions. Changing a [metric signature](metric-signature.md) alone is a separate operation.
