# Kakutani fixed-point theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Fixed-point theorem](fixed-point-theorem.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kakutani_fixed-point_theorem)

A nonempty [compact convex set](compact-convex-set.md) $K$ in a finite-dimensional space and an [upper hemicontinuous](upper-hemicontinuity.md) [set-valued mapping](set-valued-mapping.md) $F:K\rightrightarrows K$ with nonempty closed [convex](convex-function.md) values admit $x\in F(x)$. A closed graph suffices when the whole output space is [compact](compact-space.md).
