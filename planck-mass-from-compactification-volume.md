# Planck mass from compactification volume

↑ **Parent:** [Compactification (physics)](compactification-physics.md)

For an unwarped product metric with fixed compact volume $V_n$, integrating the higher-dimensional [Einstein-Hilbert action](einstein-hilbert-action.md) gives $M_{\rm Pl}^2=M_*^{n+2}V_n$ when the action coefficient is $M_D^{D-2}/2$. The [reduced Planck mass](reduced-planck-mass.md) convention must be distinguished from the unreduced one, and stabilizing the internal metric is a separate dynamical issue.
