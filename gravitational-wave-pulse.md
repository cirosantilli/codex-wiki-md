# Gravitational-wave pulse

↑ **Parent:** [Plane-fronted gravitational wave](plane-fronted-gravitational-wave.md)

A gravitational-wave pulse has curvature supported in a bounded interval of [retarded time](retarded-time.md). Spacetime can be flat both before and after the pulse even though freely falling bodies retain a displacement or relative velocity produced while the pulse passed.
