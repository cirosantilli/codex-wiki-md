# On the Gravitational Stability of a Disk of Stars by Alar Toomre

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by Alar Toomre](work-by-alar-toomre.md)

The primary calculation treats a collisionless stellar disk and obtains the critical radial [velocity dispersion](velocity-dispersion.md) $3.36G\Sigma/\kappa$. A fluid disk has a related but numerically different criterion involving $\pi G\Sigma/\kappa$. The distinction matters when a fluid [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation-split.md) is used to approximate stellar random motion.

[https://adsabs.harvard.edu/pdf/1964ApJ...139.1217T](https://adsabs.harvard.edu/pdf/1964ApJ...139.1217T)
