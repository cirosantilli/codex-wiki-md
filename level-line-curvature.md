# Level-line curvature

↑ **Parent:** [Curvature of a plane curve](curvature-of-a-plane-curve.md)

At a regular planar [level set](level-set.md) of a twice differentiable function, its oriented [curvature](curvature.md) with normal $\nabla u/|\nabla u|$ is $\kappa=(\Delta u-\nabla u^T(D^2u)\nabla u/|\nabla u|^2)/|\nabla u|$. Zero [curvature](curvature.md) at a point gives second-order contact with the tangent line, not a whole straight segment. For example the regular zero level line of $u(x,y)=y+x^3$ has zero [curvature](curvature.md) at the origin but is curved nearby.
