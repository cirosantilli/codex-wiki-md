# Magnetic flux concentration

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetohydrodynamics](magnetohydrodynamics.md)

Magnetic flux concentration redistributes existing [magnetic flux](magnetic-flux.md) into a smaller area. For a locally straight flux bundle with conserved flux, $B\mathcal A$ is constant, so decreasing area increases field strength. Converging convection, field-line stretching and [flux expulsion](flux-expulsion.md) can produce concentrations; finite [magnetic diffusivity](magnetic-diffusivity.md) opposes them and [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md) feedback limits the kinematic approximation. Redistribution alone is not evidence of net [dynamo action](dynamo-action.md).
