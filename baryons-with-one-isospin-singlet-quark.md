# Baryons with one isospin-singlet quark

↑ **Parent:** [Light-diquark spin-isospin correlation](light-diquark-spin-isospin-correlation.md)

For an [isospin](isospin.md) singlet [quark](quark.md) $h$ of charge $q_h$, the antisymmetric light-flavour pair gives an $I=0,J=1/2$ state with charge $q_h+1/3$. The symmetric light-flavour pairs $uu,\{ud\},dd$ give $I=1$ triplets of spins $1/2$ and $3/2$, with charges $q_h+4/3,q_h+1/3,q_h-2/3$. These follow from [light-diquark spin-isospin correlation](light-diquark-spin-isospin-correlation.md) and addition of constituent [electric charges](electric-charge.md), with zero [orbital angular momentum](orbital-angular-momentum.md).
