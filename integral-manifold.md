# Integral manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Integrable distribution](integrable-distribution.md)

An integral manifold of a rank-$r$ [smooth distribution](distribution-differential-geometry.md) $D$ is an immersed $r$-dimensional submanifold with $T_pN=D_p$ at every point. For an [integrable distribution](integrable-distribution.md), maximal connected integral manifolds form its leaves.
