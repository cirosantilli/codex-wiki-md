# Mutual induction

↑ **Parent:** [Electromagnetic induction](electromagnetic-induction.md)

[Mutual induction](mutual-induction.md) is [electromagnetic induction](electromagnetic-induction.md) in one [electrical circuit](electrical-circuit-split.md) associated with a changing [electric current](electric-current.md) in another. In a fixed linear arrangement, [mutual inductance](mutual-inductance.md) quantifies the coupling.
