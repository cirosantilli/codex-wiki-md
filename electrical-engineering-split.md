# Electrical engineering

↑ **Parent:** [Codex Wiki](split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_engineering)

Electrical engineering designs systems that generate, transmit, measure and control electrical [energy](energy-split.md) and information. An [electrical circuit](electrical-circuit-split.md) makes this concrete: [resistors](resistor.md), [capacitors](capacitor.md) and [inductors](inductor.md) turn [electric potential differences](electric-potential-difference.md) into predictable [electric currents](electric-current.md) and time-dependent responses. [Signal processing](signal-processing.md) then asks which information those responses preserve, suppress or reveal.

The connection is useful in both directions. A [series RLC circuit](series-rlc-circuit.md) selects a band of [frequencies](frequency.md) through its physical dynamics; a [moving average](moving-average.md) selects frequencies by arithmetic on recorded samples. Both can be studied through a [frequency response](frequency-response.md). The models differ, but the question is shared: what happens to each component of the [input](input-computer-science.md), and what does that do to the [output](output-computing.md) we care about?
