# Cyclic angular momentum in a rotational metric

↑ **Parent:** [Rotationally symmetric surface metric](rotationally-symmetric-surface-metric.md)

For the affinely parametrized [geodesic Lagrangian](geodesic-lagrangian.md) $\mathcal L=(\dot r^2+f(r)^2\dot\phi^2)/2$, the conserved cyclic momentum is $J=f(r)^2\dot\phi$. Its [Euler-Lagrange equations](euler-lagrange-equation.md) are $\ddot r-ff^{\prime}\dot\phi^2=0$ and $\ddot\phi+2f^{\prime}\dot r\dot\phi/f=0$.
