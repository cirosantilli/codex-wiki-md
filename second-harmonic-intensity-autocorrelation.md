# Second-harmonic intensity autocorrelation

↑ **Parent:** [Intensity autocorrelation](intensity-autocorrelation.md)

Overlap delayed replicas in a sufficiently thin phase-matched second-order crystal and collect their cross-generated sum-frequency signal. Its integrated [energy](energy-split.md) gives the displayed autocorrelation under undepleted, broadband-response assumptions. Temporal resolution depends on gate overlap, [group-velocity mismatch](group-velocity-mismatch.md) and spectral acceptance. Duration inference requires a pulse-shape assumption, and arbitrary temporal phase is not recovered.
