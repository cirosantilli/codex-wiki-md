# Work of a spatially gated myosin head

↑ **Parent:** [Spatially gated myosin attachment](spatially-gated-myosin-attachment.md)

For attached-state [elastic energy](elastic-energy.md) $U=\kappa(x-x_0)^2/2$ and a binding window much narrower than $x_0$, the expected assisting work before $x_0$ is $\kappa p_bx_0^2/2$. The detachment tail produces resisting work $-\kappa p_bv^2/k_{off}^2$. Mean [force](force-split.md) per head is $W/D$ for site spacing $D$. The detachment-time second moment, not the square of its mean, fixes the resisting term. These are idealized prescribed-velocity kinetics, not a complete muscle energy budget.
