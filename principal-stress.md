# Principal stress

↑ **Parent:** [Cauchy stress tensor](cauchy-stress-tensor.md)

The principal stresses are the [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) of the symmetric [Cauchy stress tensor](cauchy-stress-tensor.md). In their [orthonormal eigenbasis](orthonormal-eigenbasis.md), each [traction](traction.md) is normal to its corresponding principal plane.
