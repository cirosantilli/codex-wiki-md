# Damped Lyman-alpha system

↑ **Parent:** [Lyman-alpha absorption](lyman-alpha-absorption.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damped_Lyman-alpha_system)

A damped Lyman-alpha system has a sufficiently high neutral-hydrogen [column density](column-density.md) that the Lorentzian wings of its [Voigt profile](voigt-profile.md) are conspicuous. These wings come from the finite lifetime of the excited atomic state, not from unusually high gas [temperature](temperature.md). Damped systems usually trace substantial neutral reservoirs associated with [galaxies](galaxy-split.md).
