# Evanescent membrane response below cutoff

↑ **Parent:** [Equal-phase two-wave membrane modes](equal-phase-two-wave-membrane-modes.md)

Below the lowest strip cutoff, the axial [wavevector](wavevector.md) is imaginary. A localized harmonic drive produces a displacement decaying as $e^{-\kappa|z-z_0|}$ away from the source in the dominant lowest mode. Cross-sections still oscillate in time, but there is no far-field travelling axial wave. Several more rapidly decaying transverse modes can contribute near the source. For a symmetric central point drive, transverse modes vanishing at the centre are not excited.
