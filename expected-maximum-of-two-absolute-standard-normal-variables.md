# Expected maximum of two absolute standard normal variables

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian maximum](gaussian-maximum.md)

For two [independent](independent-random-variables.md) [standard normal random variables](standard-normal-random-variable.md), the maximum of their absolute values has distribution function $(2\Phi(x)-1)^2$ for $x\geq0$. Differentiation and [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md), using $\varphi'(x)=-x\varphi(x)$, give its expectation as $8\int_0^\infty\varphi(x)^2\,dx=2/\sqrt\pi$. This exact value can detect missing factors in two-sided Gaussian-maximum estimates.
