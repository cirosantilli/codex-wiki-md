# Critical gravity-tunnel settling time

↑ **Parent:** [Uniform-Earth chord oscillator](uniform-earth-chord-oscillator.md)

Adding a velocity-proportional [force](force-split.md) $-b\dot y$ produces a [damped harmonic oscillator](damped-harmonic-oscillator.md). The fastest non-oscillatory release from rest is critical [damping](damping.md), with the repeated-root solution in the title. Reaching fraction $\varepsilon$ of the initial displacement requires $(1+s)e^{-s}=\varepsilon$, $s=\omega_0t$. At one percent, $s=6.638352\ldots$, rather than $\log100$. Exact arrival at equilibrium takes infinite time; the tolerance reference must be stated.
