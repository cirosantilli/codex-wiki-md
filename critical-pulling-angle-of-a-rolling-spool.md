# Critical pulling angle of a rolling spool

↑ **Parent:** [Rolling without slipping](rolling-without-slipping.md)

For a spool of outer radius $R$, axle radius $r$, [mass](mass.md) $M$ and axial [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) $I$, a lower-tangent pull of [tension](tension.md) $T$ at upward angle $\theta$ produces horizontal [acceleration](acceleration.md) $a=TR(R\cos\theta-r)/(I+MR^2)$ while [rolling without slipping](rolling-without-slipping.md). This follows by combining $Ma=T\cos\theta+f$, $I\alpha=Tr+Rf$ and $a=-R\alpha$, with rightward translation and counterclockwise rotation positive. The [acceleration](acceleration.md) reverses at $\theta_c$; zero [acceleration](acceleration.md) means equilibrium only if initially at rest. At that angle the [static friction](static-friction.md) limit gives $T_{\max}=\mu Mg/(\cos\theta_c+\mu\sin\theta_c)$.
