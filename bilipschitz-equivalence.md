# Bilipschitz equivalence

↑ **Parent:** [Quasi-isometry](quasi-isometry.md)

A bilipschitz equivalence distorts all distances by multiplicative constants bounded away from zero and infinity. Every bilipschitz equivalence is a quasi-isometry.
