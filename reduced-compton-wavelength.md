# Reduced Compton wavelength

↑ **Parent:** [Compton wavelength](compton-wavelength.md)

The [Compton wavelength](compton-wavelength.md) divided by $2\pi$. A massive static [propagator](propagator.md) produces an exponential factor $e^{-r/\bar\lambda_C}$, making this the natural range scale for a [Yukawa potential](yukawa-potential.md). It is not a sharp geometric radius or a hard interaction cutoff.
