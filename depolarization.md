# Depolarization

↑ **Parent:** [Membrane potential](membrane-potential-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Depolarization)

For a negative neuronal [membrane potential](membrane-potential-split.md), [depolarization](depolarization.md) raises it toward zero or beyond, making the interior less negative relative to the exterior. This describes a voltage change, not necessarily increased [action potential](action-potential.md) generation; see the depolarizing inhibition example under [synaptic potential](synaptic-potential.md).
