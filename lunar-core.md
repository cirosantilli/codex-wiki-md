# Lunar core

↑ **Parent:** [Moon](moon.md)

The small metal-rich central region inferred from lunar [gravity](gravity.md), [rotation](rotation-mathematics.md) and seismic observations. A representative pre-2018 seismic model included a solid inner core, fluid outer core and surrounding partial-melt region; exact [radii](radius.md) depend on the inversion model.
