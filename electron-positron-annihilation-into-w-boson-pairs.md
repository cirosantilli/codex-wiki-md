# Electron-positron annihilation into W-boson pairs

↑ **Parent:** [Electron-positron annihilation](electron-positron-annihilation.md)

At tree level in the [Standard Model](standard-model-split.md), the diagrams are s-channel [photon](photon.md), [Z boson](z-boson.md) and [Higgs boson](higgs-boson.md) exchange, and t-channel [electron neutrino](electron-neutrino.md) exchange. The Higgs contribution couples to the [electron](electron.md) through its small [Yukawa coupling](yukawa-interaction.md). In a zero-electron-mass approximation that diagram vanishes, leaving the three gauge diagrams. The gauge contributions interfere; their sum has the high-energy behavior required by the electroweak gauge structure.
