<h1 id="layer-cake-extraction-of-folner-sets">Layer-cake extraction of Følner sets</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Følner sequence](folner-sequence.md)

For a finitely supported nonnegative function $\mu$ of sum one, let $F_t=\{x:\mu(x)>t\}$. Then $\int_0^\infty|F_t|\,dt=1$ and $\int_0^\infty|F_t\mathbin\triangle gF_t|\,dt=\|\mu-\lambda(g)\mu\|_1$. If the sum of these latter integrals over a finite set of translations is less than $\varepsilon$, some nonempty level set has the sum of its relative boundaries less than $\varepsilon$. This converts the [Reiter condition](reiter-condition.md) into finite [Følner sets](folner-set.md) without assuming that the [group](group-split.md) is finitely generated.
