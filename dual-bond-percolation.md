# Dual bond percolation

↑ **Parent:** [Planar dual graph](planar-dual-graph.md)

In planar [bond percolation](bond-percolation-split.md), declare a dual edge open exactly when the primal edge it crosses is closed. Independent primal parameter $p$ then gives independent dual parameter $1-p$.
