# Symmetric powers of the three-dimensional SU2 representation

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetric power](symmetric-power.md)

The three-dimensional irreducible [representation](group-representation.md) $V_2$ of [SU(2)](su-2-group.md) has weights $2,0,-2$. Thus the [character](character-of-a-representation.md) of its $n$th [symmetric power](symmetric-power.md) is $\sum_{a+b+c=n}t^{2(a-c)}$. Its weight $2\ell$ has multiplicity $\lfloor(n-|\ell|)/2\rfloor+1$ for $|\ell|\leq n$. Subtracting adjacent weight multiplicities gives one copy of each $V_{2n},V_{2n-4},\ldots$. The invariant subspace is one-dimensional for even $n$ and zero for odd $n$.
