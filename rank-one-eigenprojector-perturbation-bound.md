# Rank-one eigenprojector perturbation bound

↑ **Parent:** [Davis-Kahan curvature lemma](davis-kahan-curvature-lemma.md)

Let $\widehat P$ project onto a leading unit [eigenvector](eigenvector.md) of $H+W$, with $H$ as in the [Davis-Kahan curvature lemma](davis-kahan-curvature-lemma.md). Its maximizing [Rayleigh quotient](rayleigh-quotient.md) gives $\operatorname{tr}(H(P-\widehat P))\leq\operatorname{tr}(W(\widehat P-P))$. Duality of the [operator norm](operator-norm.md) and [trace norm](trace-norm.md), together with the rank-two bound $\|\widehat P-P\|_*\leq\sqrt2\|\widehat P-P\|_F$, proves the estimate. No gap condition on the perturbed matrix is needed.
