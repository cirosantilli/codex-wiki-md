# Self-duality of a second-order cone

↑ **Parent:** [Second-order cone](second-order-cone.md)

For $(u,s),(v,t)$ in the [second-order cone](second-order-cone.md), the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) gives $u\cdot v+st\ge-\|u\|\|v\|+st\ge0$. Thus the cone is contained in its [dual cone](dual-cone.md). Conversely, if $s<\|u\|$ and $u\ne0$, pairing $(u,s)$ with $(-u/\|u\|,1)$ is negative. If $u=0$ and $s<0$, use $(0,1)$. Every point outside the cone therefore lies outside its dual, proving that it is a [self-dual cone](self-dual-cone.md).
