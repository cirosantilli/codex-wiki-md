# Antibody specificity

↑ **Parent:** [Antibody](antibody.md)

[Antibody specificity](antibody-specificity.md) describes preferential recognition of selected [epitopes](epitope.md). It is established relative to tested alternatives and assay conditions, rather than inferred from a reagent's name. [Western blot antibody specificity controls](western-blot-antibody-specificity-control.md) distinguish recognition of the proposed [protein](protein-split.md) from retained label caused by other material.
