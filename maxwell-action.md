# Maxwell action

↑ **Parent:** [Maxwell Lagrangian](maxwell-lagrangian.md)

The source-free [Maxwell Lagrangian](maxwell-lagrangian.md) gives $S=-\tfrac14\int F_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu}\,d^4x$ in mostly-minus Lorentzian signature. Its [Euclidean action](euclidean-action.md) is the displayed positive quadratic functional of the [electromagnetic field tensor](electromagnetic-field-tensor.md). Variation, with boundary terms vanishing, yields $\partial_\mu F_{\mu\nu}=0$.
