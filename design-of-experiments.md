# Design of experiments

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical modelling](statistical-modelling-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Design_of_experiments)

[Design of experiments](design-of-experiments.md) chooses [experimental units](experimental-unit.md), [treatments](treatment.md), [replication](replication-in-experimental-design.md), [blocks in experimental design](blocks-in-experimental-design.md) and [randomization](randomization.md) so that scientifically useful [treatment contrasts](treatment-contrast.md) can be estimated with meaningful [standard errors](standard-error.md). Allocation determines which measurements supply independent treatment information.
