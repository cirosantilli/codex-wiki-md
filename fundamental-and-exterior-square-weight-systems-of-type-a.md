# Fundamental and exterior-square weight systems of type A

↑ **Parent:** [Weight system](weight-system.md)

For the [special unitary group](special-unitary-group.md) $SU(N)$, put $\varepsilon_i=e_i-N^{-1}\sum_je_j$. Its defining [representation](group-representation.md) has [weight system](weight-system.md) $\{\varepsilon_i\}$, and its [exterior square](exterior-square.md) has [weight system](weight-system.md) $\{\varepsilon_i+\varepsilon_j:i<j\}$; each has multiplicity one. Their [dual representations](dual-representation.md) negate the [weights of a representation](weight-of-a-representation.md). The [Dynkin label](dynkin-label.md) coordinates are $(\varepsilon_i)_r=\delta_{ir}-\delta_{i,r+1}$. These follow by evaluating diagonal traceless generators on coordinate vectors and their [exterior products](exterior-product.md).

Under the standard embedding $SU(N-1)\subset SU(N)$, the last defining [weight of a representation](weight-of-a-representation.md) restricts to zero and the others become the defining [weights of a representation](weight-of-a-representation.md) of $SU(N-1)$. Splitting pairs according to whether they contain the last index proves the [branching of a representation](branching-of-a-representation.md) $V_N\downarrow=V_{N-1}\oplus\mathbf1$ and $\Lambda^2V_N\downarrow=\Lambda^2V_{N-1}\oplus V_{N-1}$, including multiplicities.
