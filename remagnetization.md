# Remagnetization

↑ **Parent:** [Paleomagnetism](paleomagnetism.md)

Acquisition of a younger [remanent magnetization](remanent-magnetization.md) that partly or completely replaces an older magnetic record. Heating, chemical alteration or later mineral growth can cause it. A successful [continental reconstruction](continental-reconstruction.md) must connect the age of the measured magnetic component to the geological event being reconstructed.
