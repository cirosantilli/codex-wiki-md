# 2022

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the clinical social cognitive neuroscience course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Study answers to every option in the clinical [social cognition](../social-cognition.md) examination of 25 May 2022. Source release: [released examination PDF](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/experimental_psychology_past_exa/response/2757831/attach/3/New%20Compressed%20zipped%20Folder.zip?cookie_passthrough=1). The original examination required a choice of three; this resource covers all six without reproducing the statements.

Scientific claims use evidence available by the examination date. Diagnostic groups, individual cognitive profiles and causal mechanisms are distinguished; illustrative calculations are not experimental measurements.
