# Core-shell semiconductor nanocrystal

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum dot](quantum-dot.md)

A [nanocrystal](nanocrystal.md) with one semiconducting composition surrounded by another. [band offsets](band-offset.md), interface quality and shell thickness govern carrier confinement and coupling to external traps. [type-I band alignment](type-i-band-alignment.md) favors electron and hole localization together; [type-II band alignment](type-ii-band-alignment.md) can separate them. A larger shell [band gap](band-gap.md) does not by itself determine those offsets.
