# Pseudoscalar meson

↑ **Parent:** [Meson](meson.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pseudoscalar_meson)

A [meson](meson.md) with zero [spin angular momentum](spin.md) and negative [parity](parity.md). The [pion](pion.md) and [kaon](kaon.md) are familiar examples. Between two [pseudoscalar mesons](pseudoscalar-meson.md), [parity conservation](parity-conservation.md) in the strong interaction forces the [axial current](axial-current.md) matrix element to vanish, while the [vector current](vector-current.md) has [pseudoscalar-to-pseudoscalar form factors](pseudoscalar-to-pseudoscalar-form-factor.md). The weak decay as a whole need not conserve [parity](parity.md).
