# Wave conservation equation

↑ **Parent:** [Phase compatibility in geometrical optics](phase-compatibility-in-geometrical-optics.md)

For a smooth phase $\Theta$, define local [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) $k=\Theta_x$ and local [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) $\omega=-\Theta_t$. Equality of mixed derivatives gives $k_t+\omega_x=0$. Dividing by $2\pi$ expresses conservation of signed crest count with density $k/(2\pi)$ and flux $\omega/(2\pi)$. When a homogeneous local [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation-split.md) gives $\omega=\omega(k)$, the equation becomes $k_t+v_g(k)k_x=0$; its [characteristic curves](characteristic-curve.md) move at the [group velocity](group-velocity.md). This phase compatibility is distinct from energy or wave-action conservation.
