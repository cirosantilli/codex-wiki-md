# Magnetic electron lens

↑ **Parent:** [Electron lens](electron-lens.md)

A [magnetic electron lens](magnetic-electron-lens.md) uses a [solenoid](solenoid.md) around the beam axis; iron pole pieces concentrate its [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md).[Ruska, §§C and E, Fig. 4: iron enclosure and pole-piece lens](the-development-of-the-electron-microscope-and-of-electron-microscopy-by-ernst-ruska.md) A strictly axial trajectory in a perfectly uniform axial [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) experiences no [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md), since $\mathbf v\times\mathbf B=0$. Near the entrance, a radial field component turns an advancing [electron](electron.md) azimuthally; the resulting sideways [velocity](velocity.md) in the axial [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) also produces a radial [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md). This couples image rotation and focusing. Real trajectories bend smoothly, whereas a [thin lens](thin-lens.md) diagram compresses that bending into one effective plane.
