# Normality and equidistribution under integer multiplication

↑ **Parent:** [Normal number](normal-number.md)

A number $x$ is a [normal number](normal-number.md) in base $K$ exactly when $(T_K^nx)$ is an [equidistributed sequence](equidistributed-sequence.md). A word of length $r$ corresponds to a half-open interval of length $K^{-r}$; these intervals form arbitrarily fine grids, which approximate any interval from inside and outside.
