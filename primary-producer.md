# Primary producer

↑ **Parent:** [Ecosystem](ecosystem.md)

A [primary producer](primary-producer.md) builds organic [chemical compounds](chemical-compound.md) from inorganic sources. Photosynthetic [algae](algae.md), [plants](plant.md) and [cyanobacteria](cyanobacteria.md) use [light](light.md) [energy](energy.md) for this role through [photosynthesis](photosynthesis.md) and [carbon fixation](carbon-fixation.md). The role supplies new living material to a [food web](food-web.md); it does not mean that the [organism](organism.md) creates [energy](energy.md).
