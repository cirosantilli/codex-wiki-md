# Reactivity (nuclear engineering)

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear reactor](nuclear-reactor.md)

A dimensionless measure of deviation from critical neutron multiplication. Positive [reactivity](reactivity-nuclear-engineering.md) indicates a supercritical neutron balance; zero gives criticality. The population's actual time evolution also depends on delayed-neutron and feedback dynamics.
