# Bogomolny bound

↑ **Parent:** [Bogomolny equations](bogomolny-equations.md)

A Bogomolny bound rewrites a field-theory energy as a sum of nonnegative squares plus a topological term. Configurations satisfying the resulting first-order [Bogomolny equations](bogomolny-equations.md) saturate the bound.
