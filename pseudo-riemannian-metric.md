# Pseudo-Riemannian metric

↑ **Parent:** [Pseudo-Riemannian manifold](pseudo-riemannian-manifold.md)

A [pseudo-Riemannian metric](pseudo-riemannian-metric.md) is a smooth nondegenerate symmetric bilinear form on every tangent space, with locally constant signature. Its positive-definite special case is a [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md). Unlike a positive-definite metric, an indefinite metric admits nonzero null vectors. In either signature its [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md) is uniquely determined by torsion-freeness and metric compatibility.
