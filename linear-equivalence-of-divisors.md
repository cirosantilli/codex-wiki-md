# Linear equivalence of divisors

↑ **Parent:** [Divisor on an algebraic curve](divisor-on-an-algebraic-curve.md)

Two divisors $D$ and $E$ are linearly equivalent, written $D\sim E$, when $D-E$ is a [principal divisor](principal-divisor-on-an-algebraic-curve.md). Multiplication by a rational function whose divisor is $D-E$ gives an isomorphism between their spaces of sections, so $\ell(D)=\ell(E)$.
