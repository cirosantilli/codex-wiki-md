# Symplectic orthogonal complement

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic vector space](symplectic-vector-space.md)

The symplectic orthogonal complement of a subspace $W$ is $W^\omega=\{v:\omega(v,w)=0\text{ for every }w\in W\}$. The [nondegenerate bilinear form](nondegenerate-bilinear-form.md) gives $\dim W^\omega=\dim V-\dim W$. An [isotropic subspace of a symplectic vector space](isotropic-subspace-of-a-symplectic-vector-space.md) satisfies $W\subset W^\omega$; a [Lagrangian subspace](lagrangian-subspace.md) satisfies equality.
