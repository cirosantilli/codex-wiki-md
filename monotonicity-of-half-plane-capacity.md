# Monotonicity of half-plane capacity

↑ **Parent:** [Half-plane capacity](half-plane-capacity.md)

If compact H-hulls satisfy $A\subseteq C$, then

$$
\operatorname{hcap}(A)\leq\operatorname{hcap}(C).
$$

This follows from the [Brownian representation of half-plane capacity](brownian-representation-of-half-plane-capacity.md) or from composition of their mapping-out functions.
