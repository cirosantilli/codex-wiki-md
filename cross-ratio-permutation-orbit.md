# Cross-ratio permutation orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Cross-ratio](cross-ratio.md)

For the convention in [cross-ratio normalization of four projective points](cross-ratio-normalization-of-four-projective-points.md), permuting four distinct [projective points](projective-point.md) produces the six displayed rational expressions. Interchanging the first pair sends $\lambda$ to $1/\lambda$; interchanging the middle pair sends it to $1-\lambda$. The four permutations consisting of the identity and the three double transpositions form a [Klein four-group](klein-four-group.md) preserving the [cross-ratio](cross-ratio.md). The resulting six-element action gives every displayed expression.

For real $\lambda\notin\{0,1\}$, repeated values occur exactly for $\lambda\in\{-1,1/2,2\}$, and then the [orbit](orbit-dynamical-system.md) has size three. To prove this, a repetition gives a nonidentity stabilizer after composing one operation with the other's inverse. Its fixed-point equations give $-1$, $1/2$, $2$, or $\lambda^2-\lambda+1=0$, with the last case nonreal. With one point at infinity, $\lambda=(x_1-x_3)/(x_1-x_2)$; the three real exceptional values mean that one finite point is the midpoint of the other two. Thus these [harmonic conjugate points](harmonic-conjugate-points.md) configurations have three collinear equally spaced finite points in that affine coordinate.
