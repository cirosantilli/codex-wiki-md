# Design effect

↑ **Parent:** [Cluster sampling](cluster-sampling.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Design_effect)

The [design effect](design-effect.md) is a sampling [variance](variance-split.md) under a complex design divided by the corresponding simple-random-sampling [variance](variance-split.md). For independent equal-size clusters of size $m$ with exchangeable [intraclass correlation](intraclass-correlation-coefficient.md) $\rho$, a common mean-[estimation](statistical-estimation.md) approximation is $1+(m-1)\rho$. It expresses the loss of effective [sample size](sample-size.md) from positive correlation; finite-population and unequal-cluster-size designs require their own calculation.
