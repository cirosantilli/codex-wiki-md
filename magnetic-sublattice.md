# Magnetic sublattice

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic order](magnetic-order.md)

A [magnetic sublattice](magnetic-sublattice.md) groups sites with a common ordered [magnetic moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md) in a description of [magnetic order](magnetic-order.md). In the simplest two-sublattice description, assign [magnetizations](magnetization.md) $\mathbf M_A$ and $\mathbf M_B$ per total specimen [volume](volume.md), so that

$$
\mathbf M=\mathbf M_A+\mathbf M_B,\qquad
\mathbf L=\mathbf M_A-\mathbf M_B.
$$

Here $\mathbf L$ is one normalization of the [staggered magnetization](staggered-magnetization.md); conventions may include a factor $1/2$. Using the same total [volume](volume.md) for both contributions is essential. Equal [magnetic moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md) per site does not imply equal sublattice contributions if the site populations differ.

Ferromagnetism, antiferromagnetism, and ferrimagnetism: order and the net magnetic moment**. Original counting diagrams with six sites in equal [volumes](volume.md). Arrows are [magnetic moments](magnetic-dipole-moment.md), not trajectories. The stipulated site contributions are $+m_0,+m_0$ for [ferromagnetism](ferromagnetism.md), $+m_0,-m_0$ for [antiferromagnetism](antiferromagnetism.md), and $+2m_0,-m_0$ for [ferrimagnetism](ferrimagnetism.md). The two colors identify A and B sites. This schematic is not a measured material or the [crystal structure](crystal-structure.md) of [manganese(II) oxide](manganese-ii-oxide.md).

In this six-site example, three A sites and three B sites give total [magnetic moments](magnetic-dipole-moment.md) $6m_0$, $0$, and $3m_0$, respectively, along the chosen axis. The antiferromagnetic case has $\mathbf M=0$ but $\mathbf L=6m_0\widehat{\mathbf z}/V$. Cancellation of the sum leaves the alternating information intact.
