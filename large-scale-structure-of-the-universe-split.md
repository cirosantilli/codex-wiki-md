# Large-scale structure of the universe

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmology](cosmology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Large-scale_structure_of_the_universe)

The large-scale structure of the universe is the cosmic distribution of matter on scales where galaxies and halos form a web of clusters, filaments, sheets, and voids.
