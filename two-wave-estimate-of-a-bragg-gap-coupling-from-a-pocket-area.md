# Two-wave estimate of a Bragg-gap coupling from a pocket area

↑ **Parent:** [Rectangular-lattice Fermi-surface reconstruction](rectangular-lattice-fermi-surface-reconstruction.md)

Put $C=\hbar^2/(2m)$, $v=|V_G|$, $\mathbf G=(2K,0)$, and $k_x=K+q$. The upper [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) of the two coupled [plane waves](plane-wave.md) is

$$
E_+(q,k_y)=C(K^2+q^2+k_y^2)+\sqrt{4C^2K^2q^2+v^2}.
$$

At fixed [Fermi energy](fermi-energy.md) $Ck_F^2$, its closed [Fermi contour](fermi-contour.md) has

$$
y(q)^2=k_F^2-K^2-q^2-\sqrt{4K^2q^2+(v/C)^2},\qquad
q_m^2=k_F^2+K^2-\sqrt{4k_F^2K^2+(v/C)^2}.
$$

Thus $A(v)=4\int_0^{q_m}y(q)\,dq$ can be inverted numerically to estimate the [Bragg scattering](bragg-scattering.md) coupling. The pocket disappears at $v=C(k_F^2-K^2)$. The estimate assumes a fixed [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) and neglects coupling to other [plane waves](plane-wave.md); a measured pocket area alone does not determine all [Fourier coefficients](fourier-coefficient.md) of an arbitrary lattice potential.
