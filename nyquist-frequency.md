# Nyquist frequency

↑ **Parent:** [Nyquist–Shannon sampling theorem](nyquist-shannon-sampling-theorem.md)

For uniform samples at rate $f_s$, the frequency $f_s/2$ is the Nyquist frequency. A real baseband signal confined below this boundary can avoid spectral overlap when an appropriate [anti-aliasing filter](anti-aliasing-filter.md) is used.
