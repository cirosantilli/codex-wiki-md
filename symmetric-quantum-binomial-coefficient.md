# Symmetric quantum binomial coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian binomial coefficient](gaussian-binomial-coefficient.md)

The quotient $[n]_q[n-1]_q\cdots[n-k+1]_q/([k]_q\cdots[1]_q)$ is the displayed shifted [Gaussian binomial coefficient](gaussian-binomial-coefficient.md). It is the [formal character](formal-character-of-a-weight-module.md) of $\Lambda^kL_{n-1}$, where $L_{n-1}$ is the $n$-dimensional irreducible [sl2 Lie algebra](sl2-lie-algebra.md) representation. Indeed its exterior-power weights are sums of $k$ distinct terms of $n-1,n-3,\ldots,1-n$. [Parity unimodality of an sl2 character](parity-unimodality-of-an-sl2-character.md) proves unimodality after removing the intervening zero coefficients; equivalently, the ordinary Gaussian polynomial has unimodal coefficients.
