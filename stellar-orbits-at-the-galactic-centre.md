# Stellar orbits at the Galactic centre

↑ **Parent:** [Sagittarius A\*](sagittarius-a.md)

The useful idea is to weigh an unseen object through its effect on a visible tracer. A curved [stellar orbit](stellar-orbit.md) carries more information than a single fast-moving [star](star.md): its changing position and [velocity](velocity.md) constrain a common centre, a length scale and a clock. The [Galactic-centre stellar-orbit experiments](galactic-centre-stellar-orbit-experiments.md) combine [astrometry](astrometry.md) with [Doppler spectroscopy](doppler-spectroscopy.md); the [Galactic-centre point-mass orbit model](galactic-centre-point-mass-orbit-model.md) below shows how the two measurements complement one another.
