# Surface density contrast in an eccentric ring

↑ **Parent:** [Eccentric debris ring](eccentric-debris-ring.md)

A higher orbital line density at [apoapsis](apoapsis.md) does not always give a higher [surface density of a disk](surface-density-of-a-disk.md): the local ring width also changes. In a narrow [aligned eccentric ring](aligned-eccentric-ring.md), neglecting disc twist and keeping first-order [orbital eccentricity](orbital-eccentricity.md), $\Sigma_{\rm peri}\propto1+a\,de/da$ and $\Sigma_{\rm apo}\propto1-a\,de/da$. Constant eccentricity therefore gives constant surface density to this order.
