# Water molecule

↑ **Parent:** [Water](water.md)

A [molecule](molecule-split.md) containing two [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) and one [oxygen](oxygen.md) [chemical atom](atom-physics.md). An isolated ground-state [water molecule](water-molecule.md) has a bent equilibrium geometry; ordinary bulk [water](water.md) contains interacting [water molecules](water-molecule.md) rather than a collection of independent rigid objects.
