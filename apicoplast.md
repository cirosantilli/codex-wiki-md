# Apicoplast

↑ **Parent:** [Plastid](plastid.md)

An [apicoplast](apicoplast.md) is a reduced nonphotosynthetic plastid in many apicomplexan parasites. Retained biosynthetic outputs can make it essential after photosystems and photosynthetic carbon fixation are lost.
