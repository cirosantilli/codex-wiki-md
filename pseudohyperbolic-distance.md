# Pseudohyperbolic distance

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic metric](hyperbolic-metric.md)

On the [unit disc](unit-disc.md), the [pseudohyperbolic distance](pseudohyperbolic-distance.md) is

$$
\delta(z,w)=\left|\frac{z-w}{1-\overline wz}\right|.
$$

It is invariant under disc [Möbius transformations](mobius-transformation.md). For the curvature-minus-one [hyperbolic metric](hyperbolic-metric.md) with length element $2|dz|/(1-|z|^2)$, $\delta(z,w)=\tanh(\rho(z,w)/2)$ and $e^{-\rho(z,w)}=(1-\delta)/(1+\delta)$. The [Schwarz-Pick theorem](schwarz-pick-theorem.md) says that [holomorphic functions](holomorphic-function.md) from the disc into itself contract this distance. A [Blaschke factor](blaschke-factor.md) has modulus $\delta(z,a)$, so the distance also controls the size of a product with prescribed zeros.
