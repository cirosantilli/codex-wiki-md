# Tangent addition formula

↑ **Parent:** [Trigonometric function](trigonometric-function.md)

The tangent addition formula is

$$
\tan(A+B)=\frac{\tan A+\tan B}{1-\tan A\tan B}
$$

where both sides are defined.
