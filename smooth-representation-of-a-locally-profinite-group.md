# Smooth representation of a locally profinite group

↑ **Parent:** [Group representation](group-representation.md)

A complex [group representation](group-representation.md) $\pi:G\to\operatorname{GL}(V)$ is smooth when every vector is fixed by some [compact-open subgroup](compact-open-subgroup.md). Equivalently each vector's stabilizer is open. Smoothness is local constancy of orbit maps with the vector space regarded discretely, not ordinary continuity into a finite-dimensional complex topology.
