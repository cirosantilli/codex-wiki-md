# Atkinson cycle

↑ **Parent:** [Thermodynamic cycle](thermodynamic-cycle.md)

The ideal [Atkinson cycle](atkinson-cycle.md) considered here has reversible adiabatic compression, isochoric [heat](heat-split.md) addition, a longer reversible adiabatic expansion and isobaric [heat](heat-split.md) rejection. Its expansion ends at the initial [pressure](pressure-split.md) rather than at the initial volume. With constant ideal-gas [heat capacities](heat-capacity.md), $r=V_1/V_2$ and $r_p=p_3/p_2$, the [temperatures](temperature-split.md) are $T_2=T_1r^{\gamma-1}$, $T_3=r_pT_2$, and $T_{4'}=T_1r_p^{1/\gamma}$. This ideal cycle is distinguished from the detailed valve timing and losses of a practical engine.
