# Orientability of real projective space

↑ **Parent:** [Real projective space](real-projective-space.md)

The outward-normal-first [volume form](volume-form.md) on the unit sphere pulls back under the [antipodal map](antipodal-map.md) by $(-1)^{n+1}$. For $n\ge1$, the sphere is connected, and its orientation descends to the twofold quotient exactly when this [deck transformation](deck-transformation.md) preserves it. Thus [Real projective space](real-projective-space.md) is orientable precisely in odd positive dimensions; the zero-dimensional case is one point and is also orientable.
