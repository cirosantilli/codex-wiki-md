# Controls and limitations of a Foucault pendulum

↑ **Parent:** [Foucault pendulum](foucault-pendulum.md)

An unwanted [ellipse](ellipse.md) is an experimental warning: a finite-amplitude pendulum can develop additional orbit-axis [precession](precession.md) even without [Earth's rotation](earth-s-rotation.md). A symmetrical, firm support and careful launch reduce this effect.[Foucault's pendulum - the physics (and maths) explained, Complications: finite elliptical motion and asymmetries in support, launch, and drag](foucault-s-pendulum-the-physics-and-maths-explained-by-joe-wolfe.md) Distinguish that nonlinear effect from the tiny [ellipse](ellipse.md) already present in the [Exact linear Foucault pendulum trajectory](exact-linear-foucault-pendulum-trajectory.md). The linear calculation establishes a controlled approximation, not the complete motion of an arbitrary large swing.

Support [anisotropy](anisotropy.md) creates a second competing mechanism. As an illustrative modification, let the two horizontal restoring [angular frequencies](angular-frequency.md) be $\omega_x$ and $\omega_y$. Even with $\alpha=0$, a diagonal release from rest gives

$$
x=A_x\cos\omega_xt,\qquad y=A_y\cos\omega_yt.
$$

When $\omega_x\ne\omega_y$, the relative [phase](phase-waves.md) changes on the scale $1/|\omega_x-\omega_y|$: the trace changes shape without any terrestrial [Coriolis force](coriolis-force.md). It need not be uniform [precession](precession.md) of a [Foucault pendulum](foucault-pendulum.md). To cleanly resolve the terrestrial effect over times of order $1/|\alpha|$, a useful design target is $|\omega_x-\omega_y|\ll|\alpha|$. Near the equator that target becomes particularly demanding. Compare fast periods along perpendicular launch directions to look for this frequency splitting; repeat at several launch orientations to detect preferred suspension axes.

Other controls address distinct alternatives: shelter the apparatus from air currents, track the support against the building, measure both orbit axes, and compare early and late fitted directions as the [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) decays. A sustaining drive, if used in a different apparatus, must supply [mechanical energy](mechanical-energy.md) without imposing an azimuthal bias; an undriven run provides a useful comparison. A change in swing direction alone cannot exclude these effects. Agreement of the signed rate with independently known [geographical latitude](geographical-latitude.md) and [Earth's rotation](earth-s-rotation.md), together with stable repeat launches and small competing effects, is the inference the experiment is designed to support.
