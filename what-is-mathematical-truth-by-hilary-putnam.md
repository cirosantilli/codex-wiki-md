# What Is Mathematical Truth? by Hilary Putnam

↑ **Parent:** [Philosophy bibliography](philosophy-bibliography.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by Hilary Putnam](work-by-hilary-putnam.md)

[https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9780511625268.006](https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9780511625268.006)

Chapter 4 of Mathematics, Matter and Method, pp. 60–78; printed p. 73 gives the realist argument from scientific success. The chapter appeared in the 1975 collection; the linked publisher record is for the 1979 second edition and its later online publication. The similarly named short Historia Mathematica item is not silently substituted.
