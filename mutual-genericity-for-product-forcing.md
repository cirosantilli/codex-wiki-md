# Mutual genericity for product forcing

↑ **Parent:** [Product forcing](product-forcing.md)

If $H=G_0\times G_1$ is generic for [product forcing](product-forcing.md) over $M$, then $G_1$ is generic over $M[G_0]$. For a name $\dot D$ for a dense [subset](subset.md) of the second factor, take $p_0\in G_0$ forcing density. Ground-model pairs with first coordinate incompatible with $p_0$, or with first coordinate below $p_0$ forcing the second into $\dot D$, form a dense product [set](set-split.md). The [existential clause of syntactic forcing](existential-clause-of-syntactic-forcing.md) and the membership clause for a [canonical forcing name](canonical-forcing-name.md) give the density witnesses. The product filter meets this [set](set-split.md), cannot use the incompatible case, and hence its second projection meets $\dot D^{G_0}$.
