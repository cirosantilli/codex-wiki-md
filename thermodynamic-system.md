# Thermodynamic system

↑ **Parent:** [Thermodynamics](thermodynamics-split.md)

A thermodynamic system is the part of the physical world selected for an energy and matter balance. Its boundary determines which transfers count as [heat](heat.md), [work](work.md), or transported matter.
