# Thermodynamic system

↑ **Parent:** [Thermodynamics](thermodynamics-split.md)

A [thermodynamic system](thermodynamic-system.md) is the part of the physical world selected for an [energy](energy-split.md) and matter balance. Draw its boundary before classifying an interaction as [heat](heat-split.md), [work](work.md) or transported matter. The boundary may move: a sealed piston encloses a [closed system](closed-system.md) even while its [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) changes. An [isolated system](isolated-system.md) additionally excludes all external [energy](energy-split.md) transfers.[Thermodynamics and Propulsion, §1.2.2: system boundaries and closed systems](thermodynamics-and-propulsion.md)

Boundary choice changes the ledger. If an electrical heater warms [water](water.md), choosing only the [water](water.md) counts incoming [heat](heat-split.md); including the heater counts electrical [work](work.md) entering through its leads. The combined [internal energy](internal-energy-split.md) records storage, without separate stored categories for the two transfer mechanisms.[Thermodynamics and Propulsion, §1.3.4, Figure 1.14: resistor and water with alternative system boundaries](thermodynamics-and-propulsion.md)
