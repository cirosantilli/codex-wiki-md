# Stellar population

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar astrophysics](stellar-astrophysics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stellar_population)

A stellar population is an ensemble of [stars](star.md) described by its formation times, initial [masses](mass.md), composition and present [stellar evolution](stellar-evolution.md) states. Number fractions must be distinguished from mass-weighted fractions. Shared formation times can correlate the states of components of a [binary star](binary-star.md) even when their initial masses have [independence](independent-random-variables.md).
