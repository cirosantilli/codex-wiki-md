# Polarization in geometric quantization

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric quantization](geometric-quantization.md)

A polarization is an involutive complex rank-half distribution on which the [symplectic form](symplectic-form.md) vanishes. Polarized prequantum sections satisfy $\nabla_Xs=0$ for $X\in\mathcal F$. A real polarization is tangent to a [Lagrangian foliation](lagrangian-foliation.md); a complex [Kähler](kahler-manifold.md) polarization uses the antiholomorphic tangent spaces. Only observables whose [Hamiltonian flows](hamiltonian-flow.md) preserve the chosen distribution act directly on the polarized space. Singular leaves, [holonomy](holonomy.md) and changes of polarization are genuine quantization difficulties.
