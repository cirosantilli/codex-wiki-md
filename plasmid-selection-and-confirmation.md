# Plasmid selection and confirmation

↑ **Parent:** [Genetic transformation](genetic-transformation.md)

[Plasmid selection and confirmation](plasmid-selection-and-confirmation.md) separate recovering candidates from establishing what they contain. In an original idealized comparison, stipulate a susceptible recipient and a [selectable marker](selectable-marker.md) that permits growth under the chosen condition. The intended 6000-[base pair](base-pair.md) construction and an empty carrier retaining that marker can both yield [biological colonies](colony-biology.md). If removal of the 1500-[base pair](base-pair.md) [plasmid insert](plasmid-insert.md) followed by [DNA ligation](dna-ligation.md) joins its two boundaries without losing other material, the empty carrier is $6000-1500=4500$ [base pairs](base-pair.md). Selection for their shared [selectable marker](selectable-marker.md) cannot distinguish these candidates.

Use separate [scientific controls](scientific-control.md) for separate uncertainties. A recipient-only comparison tests background growth under selection. A known intact [plasmid](plasmid.md) tests whether recipient preparation, [genetic transformation](genetic-transformation.md) and recovery can yield selected carriers. A carrier-only construction comparison tests recovery in the absence of added [plasmid insert](plasmid-insert.md). Growth on supporting [cell culture medium](cell-culture-medium.md) tests viability under that supporting condition. These are analytical comparisons for the constructed example, not a list claimed to have been performed in the [1973 plasmid construction experiment](1973-plasmid-construction-experiment.md).

Recover candidate [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md), compare a complete [restriction digest](restriction-digest.md) with the stipulated pattern, and inspect relevant joins by [DNA sequencing](dna-sequencing.md). Each step narrows the claim: selection supports a growth [phenotype](phenotype.md); length comparison supports size and some structural features; [DNA sequencing](dna-sequencing.md) tests the letters sampled. Even a confirmed [nucleotide sequence](nucleic-acid-sequence.md) does not alone show the intended biological function. That requires an appropriate readout such as [functional complementation](functional-complementation.md), with its own [scientific controls](scientific-control.md) and recipient [genetic background](genetic-background.md).
