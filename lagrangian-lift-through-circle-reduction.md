# Lagrangian lift through circle reduction

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic reduction](symplectic-reduction.md)

At a regular free [circle group](circle-group.md) level of a [moment map](moment-map.md), the inverse image of a [Lagrangian submanifold](lagrangian-submanifold.md) of the reduced space is a [Lagrangian submanifold](lagrangian-submanifold.md) of the original space. Its restricted [symplectic form](symplectic-form.md) is the pullback of the reduced form and hence vanishes. Its dimension is one more than that of the reduced Lagrangian, exactly half the ambient dimension. It is a [circle bundle](circle-bundle.md) over the original Lagrangian.
