# Cross-situational word learning

↑ **Parent:** [Word learning](word-learning.md)

[Learning](learning.md) word–referent mappings by integrating information across individually ambiguous encounters. Recurrent co-occurrence can favour a mapping even when no single encounter uniquely identifies it.
