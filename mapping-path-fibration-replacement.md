# Mapping-path fibration replacement

↑ **Parent:** [Serre fibration](serre-fibration.md)

For a map $f:X\to B$, let $E_f$ consist of points of $X$ and paths starting at their images, with projection $p(x,\gamma)=\gamma(1)$. Appending segments of a base homotopy proves the [homotopy lifting property](homotopy-lifting-property.md), so $p$ is a [Serre fibration](serre-fibration.md). The constant-path inclusion $s:X\to E_f$ satisfies $ps=f$ and is a [homotopy equivalence](homotopy-equivalence.md): forget the path for an inverse, and shorten paths to prove the homotopy. The homotopy need not preserve the endpoint in $B$, so the statement concerns an equivalence of total spaces over a commuting triangle, not a fibrewise inverse. The fibre over $b_0$ is the [homotopy fibre](homotopy-fiber.md) of $f$.
