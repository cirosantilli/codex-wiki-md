# Fixed-beta brightness gain from emittance reduction

↑ **Parent:** [Beam brightness](beam-brightness.md)

[Photon](photon.md) source widths combine [electron](electron.md) and diffraction widths in quadrature. At fixed [optics](optics-split.md), reducing [electron](electron.md) emittance eventually has little effect when both radiation size and divergence dominate. This criterion differs from comparing emittances alone if the optical [beta function](beta-function.md) is not matched to the diffraction ellipse.
