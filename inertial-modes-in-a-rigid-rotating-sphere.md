# Inertial modes in a rigid rotating sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Inertial wave](inertial-wave.md)

In a uniform-density [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md) inside a smooth rigid sphere rotating at angular speed $\Omega$, a [normal mode](normal-mode.md) with phase $e^{i\sigma t+im\phi}$ has intrinsic frequency $s=\sigma+m\Omega$. For $s\ne0,\pm2\Omega$, eliminating [velocity](velocity.md) from the [linearized Euler equations](linearized-euler-equations.md) gives the displayed pressure equation, with $W=p'/\rho$. Impermeability gives $R W_R+(2m\Omega/s)W+(1-4\Omega^2/s^2)zW_z=0$ on the sphere. There is no condition $W=0$: the rigid wall supplies variable normal [pressure](pressure.md). These [inertial waves](inertial-wave.md) are restored by [Coriolis acceleration](coriolis-acceleration.md), and their pressure equation is hyperbolic for $|s|<2|\Omega|$.
