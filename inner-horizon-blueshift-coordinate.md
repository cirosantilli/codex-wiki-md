# Inner-horizon blueshift coordinate

↑ **Parent:** [Cauchy horizon](cauchy-horizon.md)

Near a nondegenerate outgoing inner [Cauchy horizon](cauchy-horizon.md), a regular null coordinate behaves as $V_-\propto-e^{-\kappa_-v}$, where $\kappa_->0$ is the magnitude of the inner-horizon [surface gravity](surface-gravity.md). Transforming stress components gives $T_{V_-V_-}\propto e^{2\kappa_-v}T_{vv}$. Even a small power-law tail can therefore cause an unbounded locally measured flux, motivating [mass inflation](mass-inflation.md).
