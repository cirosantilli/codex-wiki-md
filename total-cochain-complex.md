# Total cochain complex

↑ **Parent:** [Double cochain complex](double-cochain-complex.md)

The total differential is $d_h+d_v$ for anticommuting differentials. For a [double cochain complex](double-cochain-complex.md) bounded in both indices, both index filtrations are finite and have the same total [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) as abutment.
