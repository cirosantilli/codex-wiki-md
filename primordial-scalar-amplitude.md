# Primordial scalar amplitude

↑ **Parent:** [Scale-invariant inflationary power spectrum](scale-invariant-inflationary-power-spectrum.md)

At leading slow-roll order, the dimensionless primordial curvature-perturbation amplitude at horizon exit is

$$
A_s=\frac{H^2}{8\pi^2\epsilon_VM_{\rm Pl}^2}
=\frac{V}{24\pi^2M_{\rm Pl}^4\epsilon_V}.
$$
