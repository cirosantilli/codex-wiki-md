# Segmented mirror

↑ **Parent:** [Optical telescope](optical-telescope.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Segmented_mirror)

A segmented mirror assembles smaller mirrors into a large aperture. Sensors and actuators maintain position and figure; coherent imaging additionally requires [mirror segment phasing](mirror-segment-phasing.md).
