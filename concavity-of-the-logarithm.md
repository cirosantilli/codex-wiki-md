# Concavity of the logarithm

↑ **Parent:** [Logarithm](logarithm.md)

The [logarithm](logarithm.md) is [strictly concave](strictly-concave-function.md) on positive real numbers, as its second [derivative](derivative.md) is negative. Thus the [Jensen inequality](jensen-s-inequality.md) gives $\int\log f\,d\mu\le\log\int f\,d\mu$ for a [probability measure](probability-measure.md) and an integrable positive $f$, whenever the left side is defined. It also controls the negative logarithmic part of [convex](convex-function.md) combinations of strictly positive functions.
