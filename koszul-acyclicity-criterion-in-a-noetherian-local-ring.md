# Koszul acyclicity criterion in a Noetherian local ring

↑ **Parent:** [Koszul complex](koszul-complex.md)

Let $(R,\mathfrak m)$ be a [Noetherian local ring](noetherian-local-ring.md), let $M$ be nonzero and finitely generated, and let every $f_i$ lie in $\mathfrak m$. Then positive [Koszul homology](koszul-homology.md) vanishes exactly when $f$ is a [regular sequence on a module](regular-sequence-on-a-module.md) $M$. The [mapping cone](mapping-cone-homological-algebra.md) exact sequence proves the forward implication by induction; surjectivity of the last generator on earlier homology and the [Nakayama lemma](nakayama-lemma.md) prove the converse.
