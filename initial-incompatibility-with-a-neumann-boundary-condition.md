# Initial incompatibility with a Neumann boundary condition

↑ **Parent:** [Neumann boundary condition](neumann-boundary-condition.md)

Initial data whose normal [derivative](derivative.md) is nonzero at a wall cannot satisfy homogeneous [Neumann boundary conditions](neumann-boundary-condition.md) continuously all the way to the initial time. For example, $-\operatorname{erf}(ay)$ has wall [derivative](derivative.md) $-2ae^{-a^2}/\sqrt\pi$ at $y=\pm1$, which is nonzero for finite $a>0$. A parabolic [mild solution of an abstract Cauchy problem](mild-solution-of-an-abstract-cauchy-problem.md) can still take those initial data in an integral or square-integrable sense and satisfy the boundary condition for every positive time. An exactly compatible smooth profile is needed for a classical solution on the closed time interval.
