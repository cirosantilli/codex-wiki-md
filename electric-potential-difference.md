# Electric potential difference

↑ **Parent:** [Electric potential](electric-potential.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electric_potential_difference)

An electric potential difference compares the [electric potentials](electric-potential.md) at two points. In an [electrical circuit](electrical-circuit.md) it is the voltage across the terminals of a component. A vertex potential in an [electrical network](electrical-network.md) is specified relative to a reference vertex; terminal differences are independent of an additive change of reference.
