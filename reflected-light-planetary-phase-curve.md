# Reflected-light planetary phase curve

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric albedo](geometric-albedo.md)

The orbital variation of a planet's reflected stellar light as the visible illuminated area changes. In a simple spherical model, $F_p/F_*=A_g(R_p/a)^2\Phi(\alpha)$, where $\Phi(0)=1$ and $\alpha$ is the star-planet-observer angle. [Exoplanet clouds](exoplanet-cloud.md) can alter the [geometric albedo](geometric-albedo.md) and the angular scattering law; this observable differs from an [exoplanet thermal phase curve](exoplanet-thermal-phase-curve.md).
