# Veto player

↑ **Parent:** [Simple cooperative game](simple-cooperative-game.md)

A player belonging to every [winning coalition](winning-coalition.md). Giving all of the grand-[coalition](coalition-game-theory.md) value to a [veto player](veto-player.md) satisfies every [core](core-game-theory.md) constraint in a nontrivial [simple cooperative game](simple-cooperative-game.md). If there are no [veto players](veto-player.md), its [core of a simple cooperative game](core-of-a-simple-cooperative-game.md) is empty.
