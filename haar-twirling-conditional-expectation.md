# Haar twirling conditional expectation

↑ **Parent:** [Partial trace](partial-trace.md)

For an operator on a tensor-product [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md), averaging unitary conjugations on subsystem $B$ with normalized [Haar measure](haar-measure.md) gives $\mathcal E_A(O)=(\operatorname{Tr}_B O/d_B)\otimes I_B$. It fixes precisely the operators acting trivially on $B$ and is contractive in [operator norm](operator-norm.md). Writing $O-UOU^\dagger=[O,U]U^\dagger$ converts localization error into a [commutator](commutator.md) bound.
