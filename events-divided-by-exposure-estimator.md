# Events divided by exposure estimator

↑ **Parent:** [Constant hazard survival model](constant-hazard-survival-model.md)

For independent exponentially distributed failure times subject to independent [right censoring](right-censoring.md), let $d$ be observed failures and $E=\sum_i x_i$ total follow-up exposure. The parameter-dependent [likelihood](likelihood-function.md) is $\theta^d e^{-\theta E}$, giving [maximum-likelihood estimator](maximum-likelihood-estimator.md) $\widehat\theta=d/E$. The fitted individual [cumulative hazards](cumulative-hazard-function.md) sum to $d$, but this estimating identity alone does not establish that a constant hazard model fits.
