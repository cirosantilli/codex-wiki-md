# Simple coroot

↑ **Parent:** [Coroot](coroot.md)

For chosen [simple roots](simple-root.md), the corresponding [coroots](coroot.md) are the simple coroots. They form the simple system of the [dual root system](dual-root-system.md). The [fundamental weights](fundamental-weight.md) are defined by $\langle\omega_j,\alpha_i^\vee\rangle=\delta_{ij}$, so pairings with simple coroots record coordinates in the fundamental-weight [basis](basis.md).
