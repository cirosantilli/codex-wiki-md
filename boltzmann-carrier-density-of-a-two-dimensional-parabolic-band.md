# Boltzmann carrier density of a two-dimensional parabolic band

↑ **Parent:** [Two-dimensional electron gas](two-dimensional-electron-gas.md)

State counting with total degeneracy $g$ gives a constant two-dimensional [density of states](density-of-states.md) $gm/(2\pi\hbar^2)$ above a parabolic [conduction band](conduction-band.md) edge. Integrating the Boltzmann occupation yields the displayed areal [carrier density](carrier-density.md). For one valley and two spin states the coefficient is $mk_BT/(\pi\hbar^2)$. Applying the same count to [holes](electron-hole.md) gives $np=N_cN_v e^{-E_g/(k_BT)}$ and the intrinsic density $n_i=\sqrt{N_cN_v}e^{-E_g/(2k_BT)}\propto T e^{-E_g/(2k_BT)}$, distinct from the three-dimensional $T^{3/2}$ prefactor.
