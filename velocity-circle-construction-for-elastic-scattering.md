# Velocity-circle construction for elastic scattering

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic collision](elastic-collision.md)

In a nonrelativistic [elastic collision](elastic-collision.md) of incident [mass](mass.md) $M$ at [speed](speed.md) $u$ with initially stationary [mass](mass.md) $m$, the [centre-of-momentum frame](center-of-momentum-frame.md) moves at $V=Mu/(M+m)$. The projectile's outgoing [velocity](velocity.md) in that frame has fixed magnitude $w=mu/(M+m)$. Its laboratory velocity tips lie on a [circle](circle.md) of radius $w$ centred at $V\hat{\mathbf x}$. For $M>m$, a tangent from the origin gives the largest laboratory [scattering angle](scattering-angle.md), $\sin\theta_{\max}=w/V=m/M$. This geometrical result follows from [Galilean velocity addition](galilean-velocity-addition.md) and [conservation of energy](conservation-of-energy.md).
