# Lefschetz operator preserves harmonic forms

↑ **Parent:** [Dolbeault Laplacian](dolbeault-laplacian.md)

On a [Kähler manifold](kahler-manifold.md), the [Lefschetz operator of a Kähler manifold](lefschetz-operator-of-a-kahler-manifold.md) $L=\omega\wedge-$ commutes with the [Dolbeault Laplacian](dolbeault-laplacian.md):

$$
[L,\Delta_{\bar\partial}]=0.
$$

Consequently, if $\alpha$ is $\Delta_{\bar\partial}$-harmonic, then every $\alpha\wedge\omega^k=L^k\alpha$ is also harmonic.
