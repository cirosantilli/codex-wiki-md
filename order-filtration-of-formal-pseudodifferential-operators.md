# Order filtration of formal pseudodifferential operators

↑ **Parent:** [Formal pseudodifferential operator](formal-pseudodifferential-operator.md)

For scalar [coefficients](coefficient.md), leading symbols commute. If $P=a\partial^p+\cdots$ and $Q=b\partial^q+\cdots$, their product has leading [coefficient](coefficient.md) $ab$, and their [commutator](commutator.md) has [coefficient](coefficient.md) $pa b'-qb a'$ at order $p+q-1$. Thus product order is at most $p+q$, with equality when $ab$ is not identically zero; [commutator](commutator.md) order is at most $p+q-1$, with equality when its displayed [coefficient](coefficient.md) is not identically zero. Constant-coefficient operators commute. Nonzero [smooth functions](smooth-function.md) with disjoint supports show why product orders need not add over $C^\infty(\mathbb R)$.
