# Surface-electron and bulk-phonon heat-capacity crossover

↑ **Parent:** [Debye heat capacity](debye-heat-capacity.md)

A neutral two-dimensional linear electronic spectrum has [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) proportional to $AT^2$, while three-dimensional acoustic [phonons](phonon.md) have [Debye heat capacity](debye-heat-capacity.md) proportional to $NT^3/\Theta_D^3$. Thus their ratio scales as $Ak_B^2\Theta_D^3/(\hbar^2v^2NT)$. At fixed [crystal](crystal.md) composition and shape, $A/N\propto1/L$ for linear size $L$, favoring the electronic surface contribution in smaller or colder specimens. The continuum laws require thermally populated wavelengths small compared with sample size; their formal low-temperature crossover need not lie inside that regime.
