# Negation as failure

↑ **Parent:** [Logic programming](logic-programming.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Negation_as_failure)

A negative rule premise interpreted through a specified rule semantics rather than as an independently asserted classical negative fact. [Stable model semantics](stable-model-semantics.md) evaluates such premises through a candidate interpretation and its [stable model reduct](stable-model-reduct.md).
