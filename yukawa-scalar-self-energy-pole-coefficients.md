# Yukawa scalar self-energy pole coefficients

↑ **Parent:** [Yukawa interaction](yukawa-interaction.md)

For one four-component [Dirac field](dirac-field.md) with scalar interaction $-y\bar\psi\psi\phi$ and the mostly-plus propagator convention, the [fermion loop](fermion-loop.md) numerator is $4[M^2-k\cdot(k-p)]$. Reduce it to a bubble and tadpole: $-4y^2[(2M^2+p^2/2)J-I]$. [Dimensional regularization](dimensional-regularization.md) gives [pole](pole.md) parts $J=2/(\varepsilon16\pi^2)$ and $I=-2M^2/(\varepsilon16\pi^2)$. The local [pole](pole.md) then requires both [wavefunction renormalization](wave-function-renormalization.md) and a scalar [mass counterterm](mass-counterterm.md).
