# Automorphism of the unit disk

↑ **Parent:** [Unit disc](unit-disc.md)

A [biholomorphism](biholomorphism.md) of the unit disk to itself. Every such map has the form $e^{i\theta}(z-a)/(1-\overline a z)$ with $|a|<1$. These maps normalize an extremal [univalent function](univalent-function.md) in the proof of the [Riemann mapping theorem](riemann-mapping-theorem.md).
