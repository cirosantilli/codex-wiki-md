# Hydrostatic equilibrium along a Killing vector

↑ **Parent:** [Relativistic Euler equation](relativistic-euler-equation.md)

For [metric signature](metric-signature.md) $(+---)$, a stationary [perfect fluid](perfect-fluid.md) follows a timelike [Killing vector field](killing-vector-field.md) $K$ with [four-velocity](four-velocity.md) $U=K/N$. Its [Killing-flow four-acceleration](killing-flow-four-acceleration.md) is $U^b\nabla_bU_a=-\nabla_a\log N$. The [relativistic Euler equation](relativistic-euler-equation.md) and stationarity of the [pressure](pressure.md) give the displayed [hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) equation. This uses stationarity along $K$ and does not require hypersurface orthogonality.
