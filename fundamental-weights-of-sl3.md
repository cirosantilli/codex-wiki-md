# Fundamental weights of sl3

↑ **Parent:** [Fundamental representations of sl3](fundamental-representations-of-sl3.md)

For the [special linear Lie algebra](special-linear-lie-algebra.md) $\mathfrak{sl}_3(\mathbb C)$ with upper triangular [positive roots](positive-root.md), the [simple coroots](simple-coroot.md) are $\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,0)$ and $\operatorname{diag}(0,1,-1)$. Their dual [fundamental weights](fundamental-weight.md) are $\omega_1(\operatorname{diag}(t_1,t_2,t_3))=t_1$ and $\omega_2(\operatorname{diag}(t_1,t_2,t_3))=t_1+t_2=-t_3$. In simple-root coordinates, $\omega_1=(2\alpha_1+\alpha_2)/3$ and $\omega_2=(\alpha_1+2\alpha_2)/3$. The primitive vectors $e_1\in\mathbb C^3$ and $e_1\wedge e_2\in\Lambda^2\mathbb C^3$ realize these [Fundamental representations of sl3](fundamental-representations-of-sl3.md).
