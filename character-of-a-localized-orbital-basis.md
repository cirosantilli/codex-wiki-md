# Character of a localized orbital basis

↑ **Parent:** [Character of a representation](character-of-a-representation.md)

A [symmetry transformation](symmetry-transformation.md) acts on localized [atomic orbitals](atomic-orbital.md) both by moving their centres and by transforming their angular shapes. Only centres left in place can contribute to the [trace](matrix-trace.md) of the corresponding [group representation](group-representation.md). At each fixed centre, sum the diagonal coefficients of the orbital transformation: a reversed [p orbital](p-orbital.md) contributes $-1$, whereas a fixed [s orbital](s-orbital.md) contributes $+1$. For Cartesian displacement vectors at a fixed atom, use the [trace](matrix-trace.md) of the full polar-vector [matrix](matrix.md), not merely the number of fixed components. This rule generates [characters of a representation](character-of-a-representation.md) without writing its entire [matrix](matrix.md).
