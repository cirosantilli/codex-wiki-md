# Cloud ice nucleation versus ice growth

↑ **Parent:** [Atmospheric precipitation](atmospheric-precipitation.md)

Nucleation creates the initial ice embryo, either within liquid or with assistance from a suitable interface. Deposition, [riming](riming.md) and [ice-crystal aggregation](ice-crystal-aggregation.md) enlarge already existing ice. A list of efficient ice-growth processes does not by itself explain how the first crystals formed. Small-particle nucleation probabilities depend on thermodynamic driving, interfaces, volume and exposure time.
