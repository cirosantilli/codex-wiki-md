# Lightning

↑ **Parent:** [Meteorology](meteorology.md)

An atmospheric electrical discharge. Its high-temperature chemistry can produce [nitrogen oxides](nitrogen-oxides.md), influencing [ozone](ozone.md) and [radical](radical-chemistry.md) chemistry away from surface emission sources. The [ozone](ozone.md) response depends on subsequent transport and the local chemical regime.
