# Antenna effective area

↑ **Parent:** [Antenna aperture](antenna-aperture.md)

The [antenna effective area](antenna-effective-area.md) converts incident [energy flux](energy-flux.md) into available received [power](power.md) for a specified direction and matched [polarization of an electromagnetic wave](polarization-of-an-electromagnetic-wave.md). In a lossless free-space link, a transmitter of [antenna gain](antenna-gain.md) $G$ at range $r$ and a receiver of effective area $A_{\rm eff}$ have $P_r/P_t=GA_{\rm eff}/(4\pi r^2)$. Impedance or polarization mismatch reduces the delivered power.
