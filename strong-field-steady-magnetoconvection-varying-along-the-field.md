# Strong-field steady magnetoconvection varying along the field

↑ **Parent:** [Horizontal-field magnetoconvection dispersion relation](horizontal-field-magnetoconvection-dispersion-relation.md)

Restrict the [horizontal-field magnetoconvection dispersion relation](horizontal-field-magnetoconvection-dispersion-relation.md) to $k=0$, $x=m^2>0$. The steady neutral curve becomes $R=\pi^2Q+(Q+3\pi^2)x+x^2+3\pi^4+\pi^6/x$. Its unique minimum satisfies $x^2(Q+3\pi^2+2x)=\pi^6$, since $R''(x)=2+2\pi^6/x^3>0$. Expanding at large [Chandrasekhar number](chandrasekhar-number.md) gives $x\sim\pi^3Q^{-1/2}$ and the stated [asymptotic expansion](asymptotic-expansion.md). Unlike field-aligned rolls, these disturbances bend the field, and their stationary threshold grows with field strength.
