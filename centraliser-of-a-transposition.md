# Centraliser of a transposition

↑ **Parent:** [Centralizer](centralizer.md)

A [permutation](permutation.md) commutes with a transposition exactly when it preserves its two-element support as a set. It may swap those two points and independently permute the remaining points. Thus this [centraliser](centralizer.md) has order $2(n-2)!$.
