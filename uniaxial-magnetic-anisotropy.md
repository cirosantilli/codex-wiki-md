# Uniaxial magnetic anisotropy

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic anisotropy](magnetic-anisotropy.md)

A magnetic energy preference for an axis, with the two opposite directions equivalent in a purely uniaxial model. An easy-axis energy can be written $K\sin^2\theta$ for $K>0$. A unidirectional term distinguishes the two directions and can instead shift a [hysteresis](hysteresis.md).
