# Expected number of observed categories

↑ **Parent:** [Linearity of expectation](linearity-of-expectation.md)

In $n$ independent trials with category probabilities $p_1,\ldots,p_m$, let $K_n$ count the distinct categories observed. Write it as the [sum](sum.md) of [indicator random variables](indicator-random-variable.md) for the categories' appearances. Category $j$ is absent with [probability](probability.md) $(1-p_j)^n$, so [linearity of expectation](linearity-of-expectation.md) gives the displayed formula. The appearance indicators need not be independent. For equally likely categories the expression becomes $m(1-(1-1/m)^n)$; uneven probabilities can substantially delay observation of rare categories.
