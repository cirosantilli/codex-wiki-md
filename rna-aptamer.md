# RNA aptamer

↑ **Parent:** [Ribonucleic acid](ribonucleic-acid.md)

An [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) whose folded structure binds a target with molecular specificity. [Binding affinity](binding-affinity.md) can be expressed by a [dissociation constant](dissociation-constant.md); it does not by itself fix binding and unbinding rates. Recognition can be coupled conceptually to an optical readout without altering the target's genetic material.
