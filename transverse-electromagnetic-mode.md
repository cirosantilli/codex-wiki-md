# Transverse electromagnetic mode

↑ **Parent:** [Electromagnetic wave](electromagnetic-wave-split.md)

A [transverse electromagnetic mode](transverse-electromagnetic-mode.md) has both [electric field](electric-field-split.md) and [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) transverse to its propagation axis. An ideal two-conductor guide can support such a mode without a geometric [waveguide cutoff frequency](waveguide-cutoff-frequency.md); an empty simply connected [perfect conductor](perfect-conductor.md) pipe cannot.
