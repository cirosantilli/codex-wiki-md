# Manifold boundary

↑ **Parent:** [Topological manifold with boundary](topological-manifold-with-boundary.md)

The manifold boundary of a [topological manifold with boundary](topological-manifold-with-boundary.md) consists of the points which, in a half-space [coordinate chart](manifold-chart.md), have last coordinate zero. These are precisely the points without a [neighbourhood](neighbourhood-mathematics.md) [homeomorphic](homeomorphism.md) to an [open set](open-set.md) in the full [Euclidean space](euclidean-norm.md) of the same dimension. It is an intrinsic property, independent of the [coordinate chart](manifold-chart.md). It differs from the [boundary of a set](boundary-of-a-set.md) in an ambient [topological space](topological-space.md): a [circle](circle.md) has empty manifold boundary, but its [boundary of a set](boundary-of-a-set.md) in the [Euclidean plane](euclidean-plane.md) is the entire [circle](circle.md).
