# Bernstein concentration inequality for products of sub-Gaussian variables

↑ **Parent:** [Product of sub-Gaussian random variables is sub-exponential](product-of-sub-gaussian-random-variables-is-sub-exponential.md)

For independent identically distributed pairs $(U_i,V_i)$ whose coordinates are sub-Gaussian with parameter $\sigma/4$, [Bernstein's inequality](bernstein-inequalities-probability-theory.md) applied to the centered products gives

$$
\mathbb P\!\left(
\left|\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n\{U_iV_i-\mathbb E(U_iV_i)\}\right|\geq t
\right)
\leq2\exp\!\left(-\frac{2nt^2}{\sigma^2(\sigma^2+t)}\right).
$$
