# Age-metallicity degeneracy

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar population](stellar-population.md)

Integrated spectra can confuse older [stellar populations](stellar-population.md) with more metal-rich ones because both can redden the light and change [absorption lines](absorption-line.md). Line strengths depend on [stellar metallicity](stellar-metallicity.md), temperatures, ages, and abundance ratios. Weak metal lines in a young population dominated by hot bright [stars](star.md) need not indicate low [stellar metallicity](stellar-metallicity.md).
