# Regular syntactic category

↑ **Parent:** [Regular theory](regular-theory.md)

The regular syntactic category has regular formulas-in-context as objects and provably functional regular relations as arrows. It has finite limits and stable image factorizations, with existential quantification supplying images. The [regular coverage](regular-coverage.md) on this category presents the classifying topos of the theory.
