# Singly rotating six-dimensional Myers-Perry black hole

↑ **Parent:** [Myers-Perry black hole](myers-perry-black-hole.md)

For the singly rotating six-dimensional Myers-Perry solution with $G=1$ and $\mu=r_+(r_+^2+a^2)$,

$$
M=\frac{2\pi}{3}\mu,
\qquad
\Omega_H=\frac{a}{r_+^2+a^2},
\qquad
\kappa=\frac{3r_+^2+a^2}{2r_+(r_+^2+a^2)},
$$

and the horizon area is

$$
A_H=\frac{8\pi^2}{3}r_+^2(r_+^2+a^2).
$$
