# Contraction and admissible fixed spaces

↑ **Parent:** [Admissible representation of a locally profinite group](admissible-representation-of-a-locally-profinite-group.md)

For the upper-triangular subgroup of $\operatorname{GL}_n(F)$, conjugation by $\operatorname{diag}(\varpi^{-(n-1)},\ldots,1)$ expands the upper-unipotent coordinates. For a decomposed [compact-open subgroup](compact-open-subgroup.md) $K=T_0U_0$, its conjugates eventually contain $K$, while their [fixed-vector spaces](fixed-vector-space.md) are isomorphic. Finite dimension therefore forces those spaces to be equal. Inverse conjugation contracts any fixed unipotent element into $U_0$, proving that the unipotent radical acts trivially in every admissible representation of this triangular group.
