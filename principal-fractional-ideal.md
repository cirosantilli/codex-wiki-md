# Principal fractional ideal

↑ **Parent:** [Fractional ideal](fractional-ideal.md)

A principal fractional ideal of an [integral domain](integral-domain.md) $A$ with [fraction field](field-of-fractions.md) $K$ is $aA$ for a nonzero $a\in K$. Its generators differ by a [unit in a ring](unit-in-a-ring.md) in $A$. For a [number field](number-field.md), taking $A=\mathcal O_K$ and quotienting nonzero [fractional ideals](fractional-ideal.md) by [principal fractional ideals](principal-fractional-ideal.md) gives the [ideal class group](ideal-class-group.md); requiring congruences and signs of the generators instead gives a [ray class group](ray-class-group.md).
