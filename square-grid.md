# Square grid

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean geometry](euclidean-geometry-split.md)

Two equally spaced families of perpendicular [lines](straight-line.md) partitioning the [Euclidean plane](euclidean-plane.md) into congruent [squares](square.md). A general [affine map](affine-map.md) can turn this grid into [parallelograms](parallelogram.md), or collapse directions if its [linear part of an affine map](linear-part-of-an-affine-map.md) is not [injective](injective-function.md).
