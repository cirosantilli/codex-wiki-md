# Killed deterministic flow

↑ **Parent:** [Piecewise-deterministic Markov process](piecewise-deterministic-markov-process.md)

The no-jump part of a [piecewise-deterministic Markov process](piecewise-deterministic-markov-process.md). The [chain rule](chain-rule.md) gives $\partial_tP^\alpha_{s,t}f=P^\alpha_{s,t}L_t^\alpha f$, where $L_t^\alpha f=a(t,\cdot)f'-\alpha f$. The time subscript is essential for a time-dependent [vector field](vector-field.md).
