# H1 space on the unit disk

↑ **Parent:** [Hardy space](hardy-space.md)

This [Hardy space](hardy-space.md) consists of [holomorphic functions](holomorphic-function.md) with $\|f\|_1=\sup_{r<1}\int_{\partial\mathbb D}|f(r\xi)|\,dm(\xi)<\infty$, where $m$ is normalized angular measure. The [H1 non-tangential maximal inequality on the disk](h1-non-tangential-maximal-inequality-on-the-disk.md) controls their approach to the boundary, and the [Carleson embedding theorem on the disk](carleson-embedding-theorem-on-the-disk.md) characterizes measures against which all these functions remain integrable.
