# Hoare logic

↑ **Parent:** [Programming language semantics](programming-language-semantics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hoare_logic)

A system of rules for proving [Hoare triples](hoare-triple.md) by following a program's structure. [Variable assignment](variable-assignment.md), sequential composition and loops have corresponding proof obligations.
