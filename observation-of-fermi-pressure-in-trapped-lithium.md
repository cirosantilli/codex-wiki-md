# Observation of Fermi pressure in trapped lithium

↑ **Parent:** [Fermi energy](fermi-energy.md)

In 2001, [Andrew G. Truscott](andrew-g-truscott.md), [Randall G. Hulet](randall-g-hulet.md), and collaborators observed [degeneracy pressure](degeneracy-pressure.md) in trapped [lithium](lithium.md). A [Zeeman slower](zeeman-slower.md) fed a dual [magneto-optical trap](magneto-optical-trap.md); [optical pumping](optical-pumping.md) prepared atoms for a cloverleaf apparatus for [magnetic trapping of neutral atoms](magnetic-trapping-of-neutral-atoms.md). Microwave evaporation removed energetic lithium-7 [bosons](boson.md); elastic [collisions](collision.md) cooled lithium-6 [fermions](fermion.md) by [sympathetic cooling](sympathetic-cooling.md).[Truscott et al., Observation of Fermi Pressure in a Gas of Trapped Atoms (2001), p. 2570, preparation and cooling](observation-of-fermi-pressure-in-a-gas-of-trapped-atoms-by-andrew-g-truscott-and-others.md)

Sequential [absorption imaging](absorption-imaging.md) recorded both trapped clouds on a camera. Fits to axial profiles used the [boson](boson.md) cloud as a [thermometer](thermometer.md). At common [temperature](temperature-split.md), the nearly equal magnetic trapping potentials provided a comparison: the clouds had similar sizes near $T/T_F=1$, but the [fermion](fermion.md) cloud remained broader as cooling reached $T/T_F=0.25$. Figure 3 compared its radius with ideal [Fermi gas](fermi-gas.md) and classical predictions.[pp. 2570–2572, Figs. 1–3, imaging, thermometry, and radius comparison](observation-of-fermi-pressure-in-a-gas-of-trapped-atoms-by-andrew-g-truscott-and-others.md)

Varying the wait before imaging tested [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium-split.md). Finite imaging resolution restricted the analysis to the axial direction; fits neglected interaction-induced density shifts. Cooling eventually suffered from the dwindling [boson](boson.md) [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md). The result supports quantum occupation effects, without directly resolving individual occupied states or reaching zero [temperature](temperature-split.md).[p. 2571, axial resolution and negligible-interaction approximation; p. 2572, equilibration test and cooling limit](observation-of-fermi-pressure-in-a-gas-of-trapped-atoms-by-andrew-g-truscott-and-others.md)

These were neutral alkali atoms in a spatially varying trap. The [Fermi energy of a model electron gas](fermi-energy-of-a-model-electron-gas.md) instead uses uniform [electrons](electron.md), two equally populated [spin](spin.md) states, and the [electron](electron.md) [mass](mass.md). The measured trapped-atom radii are not measurements of that electron model's $p_F$ or $E_F$; comparing their numerical [Fermi energies](fermi-energy.md) requires the appropriate particle [mass](mass.md), internal-state populations, and confining potential.
