# Multiplicity of a filtered module

↑ **Parent:** [Dimension of a filtered module](dimension-of-a-filtered-module.md)

With the fixed algebra-filtration convention in [dimension of a filtered module](dimension-of-a-filtered-module.md), the multiplicity is factorial times the common leading coefficient of the cumulative Hilbert function for $M\ne0$, and is zero for $M=0$. It is a positive integer with a [standard graded algebra](standard-graded-algebra.md) normalization, and can be a positive rational number for a weighted grading. Fixed shifts between [good filtrations of a module](good-filtration-of-a-module.md) preserve its leading Hilbert coefficient. Rescaling the algebra filtration itself can change multiplicity, so its normalization must be specified.
