# Eight-tile Neumann transplantation across orientability

↑ **Parent:** [Transplantation theorem](transplantation-theorem.md)

Take eight congruent hexagonal tiles with three pairwise nonadjacent distinguished faces. On tile indices modulo eight let $\sigma(j)=1-j$, $t(j)=-j$, and use $u_1(j)=3j$ for one assembly and $u_2(j)=3j+4$ for the other. Pair faces by reflection and leave fixed faces, and the three remaining faces, with [Neumann boundary conditions](neumann-boundary-condition.md). If $Re_j=e_{j+1}$, the displayed matrix intertwines all three face actions: its circulant coefficients satisfy $c_r=c_{-r}=c_{3r+4}$. Its [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are $1+(-1)^k+4\cos(\pi k/4)$, which are nonzero for $0\leq k\leq7$. The [transplantation theorem](transplantation-theorem.md) gives equal spectra. The second gluing graph is [bipartite](bipartite-graph.md), with parts $\{0,5,6,7\}$ and $\{1,2,3,4\}$, so it is [orientable](orientable-surface.md). The first has the odd cycle $1,3,6,2,7,1$ and is nonorientable. Both graphs are connected with eight vertices and ten edges, and their bordered surfaces retract up to homotopy to the graphs. Both have [Euler characteristic](euler-characteristic.md) $-2$ and [fundamental groups](fundamental-group.md) free of rank three. Neither is [simply connected](simply-connected-space.md).
