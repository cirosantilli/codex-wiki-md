# Speciation

↑ **Parent:** [Evolution](evolution.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speciation)

[Speciation](speciation.md) is the formation of distinct [species](species.md) through [evolution](evolution.md). Detecting a difference between [biological populations](population-biology.md) is a starting observation; its interpretation depends on the stated [species concept](species-concept.md) and the evidence for lasting separation. [Reproductive isolation](reproductive-isolation.md) is one important route to limiting [gene flow](gene-flow.md) in [sexual reproduction](sexual-reproduction.md).
