# Thomson decoupling rate criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Photon decoupling](photon-decoupling.md)

The physical [Thomson scattering](thomson-scattering.md) rate equals the inverse [photon](photon.md) mean scattering time. Comparing it with the [Hubble parameter](hubble-parameter.md) gives a local approximate decoupling criterion. A more precise last-scattering [temperature](temperature.md) is obtained from optical depth and the visibility maximum; these conventions need not give exactly the same [temperature](temperature.md).
