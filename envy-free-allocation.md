# Envy-free allocation

↑ **Parent:** [Game theory](game-theory-split.md)

An allocation of bundles $B_i$ is envy-free if each agent weakly prefers its own bundle to every other agent's bundle, evaluating both through its own value [function](function-split.md). With an [additive valuation](additive-valuation.md),

$$
v_i(B_i)\ge v_i(B_\ell)
\iff
\sum_{j\in B_i}v_{ij}\ge\sum_{j\in B_\ell}v_{ij}.
$$

The reverse comparison uses $v_\ell$, so both directions are independent requirements. Ties are permitted. Empty bundles and unallocated items are separate modelling choices, not consequences of [envy-freeness](envy-free-allocation.md).
