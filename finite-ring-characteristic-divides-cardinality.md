# Finite ring characteristic divides cardinality

↑ **Parent:** [Characteristic of a ring](characteristic-of-a-ring.md)

In a finite unital [ring](ring.md) $R$, some positive multiple of $1$ is zero, because two of the finitely many successive multiples coincide. If $n=\operatorname{char}R$, the [permutation](permutation.md) $r\mapsto r+1$ has every [cycle of a permutation](cycle-of-a-permutation.md) of length exactly $n$: returning to $r$ after $k$ steps means $k1=0$, whose least positive solution is $n$. These disjoint cycles partition the [finite set](finite-set.md) $R$, so $n$ divides its [cardinality](cardinality.md). The [zero ring](zero-ring.md) has cardinality and characteristic both one.
