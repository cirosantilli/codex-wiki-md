# Trust-region subproblem

↑ **Parent:** [Trust region](trust-region.md)

The [trust-region subproblem](trust-region-subproblem.md) minimizes a [quadratic function](quadratic-function.md) on a closed [Euclidean ball](euclidean-ball.md). Even if $H$ has a negative [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md), the [compactness](compact-space.md) of this ball guarantees a [global minimum](global-minimum.md). An exact answer is often unnecessary: a fixed fraction of the [Cauchy decrease bound](cauchy-decrease-bound.md) supplies a useful inexpensive standard.
