# Scheme of characteristic p

↑ **Parent:** [Scheme](scheme.md)

A [scheme](scheme.md) $X$ has characteristic $p$ when $p\cdot1=0$ in its [structure sheaf of a scheme](structure-sheaf-of-a-scheme.md). Equivalently, its [structure morphism](structure-morphism.md) $X\to\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Z$ factors through $\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb F_p$.
