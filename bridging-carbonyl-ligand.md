# Bridging carbonyl ligand

↑ **Parent:** [Bridging ligand](bridging-ligand.md)

A [bridging carbonyl ligand](bridging-carbonyl-ligand.md) spans more than one metal. A conventional two-metal bridging CO contributes one [electron](electron.md) to each metal in neutral [electron counting](electron-counting.md). Its stretching band often lies below terminal-carbonyl bands because of stronger C–O weakening, but assignment should also use composition and structural evidence.
