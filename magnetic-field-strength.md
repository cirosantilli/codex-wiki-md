# Magnetic field strength

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md)

In macroscopic [electromagnetism](electromagnetism-split.md), [magnetic field strength](magnetic-field-strength.md) is $\mathbf H=\mathbf B/\mu_0-\mathbf M$, where $\mathbf B$ is [magnetic flux density](magnetic-field-split.md) and $\mathbf M$ is [magnetization](magnetization.md). In [SI](international-system-of-units.md) units $\mathbf H$ is measured in $\mathrm{A\,m^{-1}}$, while $\mathbf B$ is measured in [tesla](tesla-unit.md) units. In a linear isotropic medium $\mathbf B=\mu\mathbf H$, where $\mu$ is [magnetic permeability](permeability-electromagnetism.md). The macroscopic [Ampère-Maxwell equation](ampere-s-circuital-law.md) writes its [curl](curl.md) in terms of free current and [displacement current](displacement-current.md). The electromagnetic [Poynting vector](poynting-vector.md) is $\mathbf E\times\mathbf H$ in this convention.
