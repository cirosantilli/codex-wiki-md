# Abrikosov vortex

↑ **Parent:** [Superconducting mixed state](superconducting-mixed-state.md)

An [Abrikosov vortex](abrikosov-vortex.md) has a core where the superconducting [order parameter](order-parameter.md) is suppressed, surrounding circulating [electric currents](electric-current.md), and a winding of its [quantum phase](quantum-phase.md). A singly wound vortex carries one [superconducting flux quantum](superconducting-flux-quantum.md) in the appropriate bulk flux-quantization limit. The exact contour statement is [fluxoid and flux quantization in a superconductor](fluxoid-and-flux-quantization-in-a-superconductor.md); the enclosed [magnetic flux](magnetic-flux.md) alone need not equal a flux quantum on a contour with appreciable [electric current](electric-current.md).
