# Interferogram of an equally spaced spectral multiplet

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier transform spectroscopy](fourier-transform-spectroscopy.md)

For $N$ equally intense spectral lines at $\omega_n=\omega_0+n\Delta\omega$, sum their delayed self-correlations as a [geometric series](geometric-series.md). The result is

$$
g^{(1)}(\tau)=e^{-i[\omega_0+(N-1)\Delta\omega/2]\tau}
\frac{\sin(N\Delta\omega\tau/2)}{N\sin(\Delta\omega\tau/2)}.
$$

The [interferometric visibility](interferometric-visibility.md) revives every $T=2\pi/\Delta\omega$. There are $N-1$ zeros between adjacent revivals, at $\tau=jT/N$ for $j=1,\ldots,N-1$. The removable singularity at a revival has unit modulus. Revival spacing fixes line spacing; the internal zero count fixes $N$ under the equal-intensity model. The interferogram alone does not establish a fixed relative phase between the source lines or prove the emission is a coherent pulse train.
