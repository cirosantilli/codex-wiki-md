# Symmetric decomposition of a stratified mean difference

↑ **Parent:** [Standardization with common weights](standardization-with-common-weights.md)

Suppose group $g$ has stratum weights $w_{gs}$ summing to one and conditional [means](expected-value-split.md) $p_{gs}$. Write $\bar w_s=(w_{1s}+w_{0s})/2$ and $\bar p_s=(p_{1s}+p_{0s})/2$. The crude difference $D=\sum_s w_{1s}p_{1s}-\sum_s w_{0s}p_{0s}$ has the displayed decomposition. Expanding the two products in each stratum cancels the cross terms and leaves $w_{1s}p_{1s}-w_{0s}p_{0s}$, proving the identity. The first term uses common average-group weights; the second records a composition difference. Neither term alone identifies a causal pathway.
