# Strong existence theorem for additive-noise SDEs with bounded measurable drift

↑ **Parent:** [Strong solution of a stochastic differential equation](strong-solution-of-a-stochastic-differential-equation.md)

For bounded Borel $b:\mathbb R^d\to\mathbb R^d$, the [stochastic differential equation](stochastic-differential-equation.md) $dX_t=b(X_t)dt+dB_t$ has [strong existence](strong-existence.md) and [pathwise uniqueness](pathwise-uniqueness.md). The identity diffusion matrix is nondegenerate. A [Girsanov theorem](girsanov-theorem.md) argument alone establishes weak existence and [uniqueness in law](uniqueness-in-law.md); the strong conclusion requires an additional theorem. This statement does not extend without further hypotheses to arbitrary path-dependent drift or arbitrary diffusion matrices. See [the primary bounded-drift strong-solution theorem](https://www.mathnet.ru/php/archive.phtml?jrnid=sm&option_lang=eng&paperid=2601&wshow=paper).
