<h1 id="torsion-free-chern-tangent-connection-characterizes-a-kahler-metric">Torsion-free Chern tangent connection characterizes a Kähler metric</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Chern connection](chern-connection.md)

For a [Hermitian metric](hermitian-metric-on-a-holomorphic-vector-bundle.md) on a [complex manifold](complex-manifold.md), the [Chern connection](chern-connection.md) on its [holomorphic tangent bundle](holomorphic-tangent-bundle.md) corresponds to a real metric-compatible connection preserving the [almost complex structure](almost-complex-manifold.md). Its mixed torsion vanishes, and in holomorphic coordinates its remaining coefficients are $\Gamma^k_{ij}=h^{k\bar l}\partial_i h_{j\bar l}$. Symmetry in $i,j$ is equivalent to $\partial\omega=0$; its conjugate gives $d\omega=0$. Thus zero torsion is exactly the [Kähler metric](kahler-metric.md) condition, and then uniqueness identifies this connection with the [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md).
