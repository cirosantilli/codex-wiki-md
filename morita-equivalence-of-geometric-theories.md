# Morita equivalence of geometric theories

↑ **Parent:** [Classifying topos](classifying-topos.md)

[Geometric theories](geometric-theory.md) are Morita-equivalent when their [classifying toposes](classifying-topos.md) are equivalent. This gives equivalent internal model categories pseudonaturally in every [Grothendieck topos](grothendieck-topos.md). A common [classifying topos](classifying-topos.md) transports intrinsic invariants between different presentations; agreement of set-valued model categories alone is insufficient. The [duality between geometric quotients and subtoposes](duality-between-geometric-quotients-and-subtoposes.md) transfers [geometric theory](geometric-theory.md) extensions along such an equivalence.
