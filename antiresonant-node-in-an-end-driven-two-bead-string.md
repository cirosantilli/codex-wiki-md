# Antiresonant node in an end-driven two-bead string

↑ **Parent:** [End-driven two-bead string](end-driven-two-bead-string.md)

For a cosine drive of amplitude $h_0$, the periodic response of an [end-driven two-bead string](end-driven-two-bead-string.md) has signed amplitudes $A_1=2h_0/[(2-s)(4-s)]$ and $A_2=(3-s)A_1$, where $s=\omega^2/\omega_0^2$. At $s=3$, $A_2=0$ while $A_1=-2h_0$. Equal and opposite transverse forces from the driven end and moving first bead cancel at the second bead. This [antiresonance](antiresonance.md) differs from a natural [resonance](resonance.md): it is a zero of one measured response between the two natural frequencies. Free [normal modes](normal-mode.md) remain unless initial conditions exclude them.
