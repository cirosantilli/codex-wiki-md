# One-nearest-neighbour classifier

↑ **Parent:** [K-nearest neighbors algorithm](k-nearest-neighbors-algorithm.md)

The [classifier](classifier.md) that assigns the query the label of its closest training feature, using [feature-measurable nearest-neighbour tie-breaking](feature-measurable-nearest-neighbour-tie-breaking.md). Its [misclassification risk](misclassification-risk.md) need not converge to [Bayes risk](bayes-risk.md), even with infinitely many [independent and identically distributed random variables](independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md) as [training data](training-data.md).
