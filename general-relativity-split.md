# General relativity

↑ **Parent:** [Branches of physics](branches-of-physics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_relativity)

[General relativity](general-relativity-split.md) describes [gravity](gravity.md) through the geometry of [spacetime](spacetime.md). Its [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md) specifies distances, [proper time](proper-time.md) measured by clocks, and the [causal structure](causal-structure.md) that limits communication. Matter and radiation affect this geometry through the [Einstein field equations](einstein-field-equations-split.md); freely falling [test particles](test-particle.md) follow [geodesics](geodesic.md) of the resulting geometry. A [planet](planet.md)'s orbit and a falling apple therefore belong to the same geometric story. The [Newtonian limit](newtonian-limit.md) recovers familiar [Newtonian gravity](gravitational-acceleration.md) when fields are weak and speeds are small compared with the [speed of light](speed-of-light.md). See [David Tong's introduction to general relativity](https://www.damtp.cam.ac.uk/user/tong/gr/grhtml/S0.html).

The most direct way to understand [spacetime](spacetime.md) [curvature](curvature.md) is to compare neighboring freely falling bodies. Even when each body feels weightless, their separation can change: this is [geodesic deviation](geodesic-deviation.md), observed as a [tidal force](tidal-force.md). [Curvature](curvature.md) concerns these relative measurements, rather than the shape of a drawing in an imagined surrounding space. A second accessible measurement is [gravitational time dilation](gravitational-time-dilation.md): identical clocks maintained at different heights accumulate different [proper times](proper-time.md). Together, tides and clocks turn an abstract geometry into laboratory questions.
