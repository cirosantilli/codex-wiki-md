# Independent head-tail reversal symmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Nematic director](nematic-director.md)

An apolar axis model with pair [energy](energy-split.md) $-(\mathbf s_i\cdot\mathbf s_j)^2$ is invariant when each molecular representative is independently reversed. The [vector](vector.md) average changes under these replacements, but the [nematic order parameter](nematic-order-parameter.md) made from second moments does not. In an unbiased [statistical ensemble](statistical-ensemble.md) the [vector](vector.md) average vanishes; an individual finite representative need not have zero average. For independently chosen signs of a perfectly aligned axis, $\langle|N^{-1}\sum_i\mathbf s_i|^2\rangle=1/N$. This distinguishes exact head-tail invariance of an observable from vanishing only after an average.[§4.3, printed pp.10–11, molecular head-tail equivalence](lectures-on-landau-theory-of-phase-transitions-by-peter-d-olmsted.md)
