# Bragg scattering

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic Bragg scattering condition](elastic-bragg-scattering-condition.md)

Bragg scattering is coherent elastic scattering from a periodic lattice, enhanced when the wavevector transfer is a [reciprocal lattice](reciprocal-lattice.md) vector. Finite crystals have narrow rather than infinitely sharp reciprocal-space peaks.
