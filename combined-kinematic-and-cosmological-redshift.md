# Combined kinematic and cosmological redshift

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmological redshift](cosmological-redshift.md)

Frequency ratios multiply when a photon is transformed first between a source and its local comoving frame, then propagated through an expanding universe. Thus the [kinematic redshift](kinematic-redshift.md) and [cosmological redshift](cosmological-redshift.md) factors multiply. Their redshifts themselves add only to first order when both are small.
