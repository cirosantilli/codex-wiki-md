# Uniform consistency of an empirical moment-generating function

↑ **Parent:** [Moment-generating function](moment-generating-function.md)

If $X\ge0$ and $\mathbb E e^{tX}<\infty$ for some finite $t\ge0$, the estimator $\widehat m_n(s)=n^{-1}\sum_i e^{sX_i}$ satisfies $\sup_{s\in[0,t]}|\widehat m_n(s)-\mathbb E e^{sX}|\to0$ almost surely. The [dominated convergence theorem](dominated-convergence-theorem.md) makes the population [moment-generating function](moment-generating-function.md) uniformly continuous on this interval. Parameter subintervals give [function brackets](bracketing-of-a-function-class.md) whose endpoints are ordered exponentials and whose widths are the corresponding increments of the population mean. Apply the [uniform strong law from finite L1 bracketing](uniform-strong-law-from-finite-l1-bracketing.md). Continuity in the parameter makes the supremum measurable.
