# Tandem solar cell

↑ **Parent:** [Solar cell](solar-cell.md)

A stack of junctions with different [band gaps](band-gap.md) divides the incident spectrum, reducing both below-gap transmission and above-gap [thermalization](thermalization.md) compared with one optimized junction. Current matching and optical and electrical losses remain constraints.
