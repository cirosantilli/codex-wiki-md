# Circular sector

↑ **Parent:** [Circle](circle.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circular_sector)

A [circular sector](circular-sector.md) is the portion of a disc between two radii and the connecting [circle](circle.md) arc. If its [radius](radius.md) is $R$ and its central [angle](angle.md) is $\theta$ [radians](radian.md), its [area](surface-area.md) is

$$
\boxed{A=\frac12\theta R^2.}
$$

Indeed, [polar coordinates](polar-coordinates.md) give $A=\int_0^\theta\int_0^Rr\,dr\,d\phi$. The full disc corresponds to $\theta=2\pi$, a [semicircle](semicircle.md) to $\theta=\pi$, and a quarter-disc to $\theta=\pi/2$. In a solid with horizontal sector cross-sections, [radius](radius.md) $R(z)$ and [angle](angle.md) $\theta(z)$, its [volume](volume.md) is $\tfrac12\int\theta(z)R(z)^2\,dz$. Holding the [angles](angle.md) and heights fixed while scaling every [radius](radius.md) by a factor $s$ scales that [volume](volume.md) by $s^2$.
