# Conduit equation

↑ **Parent:** [Viscous buoyant conduit](viscous-buoyant-conduit.md)

The conduit equation evolves the positive cross-sectional area $A$ of a [viscous buoyant conduit](viscous-buoyant-conduit.md). Its scaling uses axial length $a_0/\sqrt{8\lambda}$ and [velocity](velocity.md) $\Delta\rho ga_0^2/(8\lambda\mu_o)$, where $\lambda=\mu_i/\mu_o$. Linearization at $A=1$ gives $\omega=2k/(1+k^2)$: [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md) is upward but [group velocity](group-velocity.md) changes sign at $|k|=1$. Nonlinear elevation waves obey the [solitary-wave amplitude-speed relation for the conduit equation](solitary-wave-amplitude-speed-relation-for-the-conduit-equation.md).
