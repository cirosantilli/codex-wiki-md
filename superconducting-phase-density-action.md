# Superconducting phase-density action

↑ **Parent:** [Anderson-Higgs mechanism in a superconductor](anderson-higgs-mechanism-in-a-superconductor.md)

A lossless low-energy charged-condensate [action](action.md) has a positive compressibility coefficient $\chi_p$, effective pair density $\rho_0$, mass $m_*$ and signed charge $q$. Coupling its phase to the electromagnetic potentials through the displayed combinations preserves [gauge symmetry](gauge-invariance.md). Adding the Maxwell action and eliminating the longitudinal electric potential gives $\omega^2=q^2\rho_0/(\epsilon_0m_*)+[\rho_0/(m_*\chi_p)]k^2$ in a simple homogeneous bulk model. Removing the electromagnetic coupling leaves neutral sound. Static [Ginzburg-Landau theory of superconductivity](ginzburg-landau-theory-of-superconductivity.md) alone cannot determine these frequencies or their damping.
