# Rubber elasticity

↑ **Parent:** [Elastomer](elastomer.md)

In a flexible crosslinked [polymer](polymer-split.md) network, deformation restricts the configurations of chain segments between [cross-links](cross-link.md). The resulting decrease of [entropy](entropy-split.md) produces a restoring [entropic force](entropic-force.md). For an ideal affine Gaussian network, the [shear modulus](shear-modulus.md) is $G=\nu_s k_BT$, where $\nu_s$ is the [number density](number-density.md) of elastically active strands. A nearly incompressible isotropic rubber has [Young's modulus](young-s-modulus.md) $E\simeq3G$. At fixed network density within the rubbery regime, both increase approximately linearly with absolute [temperature](temperature-split.md). This statement does not describe crossing the glass transition or chemical degradation.
