# Innovation process

↑ **Parent:** [Time series](time-series-split.md)

An innovation measures the part of the current observation not predicted from past observations. The [linear innovation process](linear-innovation-process.md) uses [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md) onto the past linear span. A [conditional expectation](conditional-expectation.md) predictor can use nonlinear information; the two predictors agree for a [Gaussian process](gaussian-process.md).
