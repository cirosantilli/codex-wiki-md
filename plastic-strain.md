# Plastic strain

↑ **Parent:** [Plasticity (physics)](plasticity-physics.md)

In small-[strain](strain.md) elastoplasticity, total [infinitesimal strain tensor](infinitesimal-strain-tensor.md) splits into an elastic part and a generally irreversible plastic part. The elastic [constitutive equation](constitutive-equation.md) determines [stress](stress.md) from $e^{\rm e}$, while a yield condition and a plastic flow law determine the evolution of $e^{\rm p}$. Removing external load need not remove [plastic strain](plastic-strain.md), so using [Hooke's law](hooke-s-law.md) on total [strain](strain.md) after yielding is insufficient.
