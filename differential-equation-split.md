# Differential equation

↑ **Parent:** [Analysis](analysis-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differential_equation)

A [differential equation](differential-equation-split.md) is an equation relating an unknown [function](function-split.md) to its [derivatives](derivative.md). It specifies how change depends on the present state, and sometimes on the independent variables themselves. A [solution of a differential equation](solution-of-a-differential-equation.md) is a [function](function-split.md) satisfying that relation throughout a stated domain; giving a formula without its domain can conceal a point where the equation is undefined.

The central idea is to turn a local law into a whole trajectory. For example, $y'=ry$, with constant $r$, says that the instantaneous [derivative](derivative.md) is proportional to the current value. If $y(0)=100$ and $r=0.2$ per day, the [solution of a differential equation](solution-of-a-differential-equation.md) is $y(t)=100e^{0.2t}$, so $y(5)=100e\approx271.83$. This is an ideal growth model assuming a constant proportional rate; the [exponential function](exponential-function.md) emerges from repeatedly applying the same local rule. Changing the sign of $r$ gives [exponential decay](exponential-decay.md).

An [ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md) uses one independent variable. A [partial differential equation](partial-differential-equation-split.md) can involve several, such as space and time, and uses [partial derivatives](partial-derivative.md). Additional information selects a particular [solution of a differential equation](solution-of-a-differential-equation.md): an [initial condition](initial-condition.md) describes a starting state, whereas a [boundary condition](boundary-condition.md) imposes constraints at the edge of a domain. The useful questions extend beyond finding a formula: does a [solution of a differential equation](solution-of-a-differential-equation.md) exist, is it unique, and what happens to it over time?
