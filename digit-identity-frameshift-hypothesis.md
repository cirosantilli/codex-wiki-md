# Digit identity frameshift hypothesis

↑ **Parent:** [Evolutionary developmental biology](evolutionary-developmental-biology.md)

The classical avian hypothesis that [anatomical digit](anatomical-digit.md) identities I, II and III are expressed at positional condensations II, III and IV. It distinguishes position from developmental identity; evidence for a limited anterior shift does not require invariant identities for every posterior [anatomical digit](anatomical-digit.md).[Original hypothesis](1-2-3-2-3-4-a-solution-to-the-problem-of-the-homology-of-the-digits-in-the-avian-hand-by-gunter-p-wagner-and-jacques-a-gauthier.md)
