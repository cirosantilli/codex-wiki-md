# Resolvent spectral mapping identity

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral mapping theorem](spectral-mapping-theorem.md)

For a closed [linear operator](linear-operator.md) and $z$ in its resolvent, the displayed identity follows by factoring $(A-z)^{-1}-wI$ for $w\ne0$. Its inverse exists exactly when $A-(z+1/w)I$ has a bounded inverse. Products involving $A$ are bounded after composing with a resolvent, since $A(A-\lambda)^{-1}=I+\lambda(A-\lambda)^{-1}$. Zero may be in the bounded resolvent's [spectrum](spectrum-functional-analysis.md) when $A$ is unbounded.
