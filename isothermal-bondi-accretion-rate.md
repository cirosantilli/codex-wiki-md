# Isothermal Bondi accretion rate

↑ **Parent:** [Isothermal Bondi accretion](isothermal-bondi-accretion.md)

The regular spherical isothermal inflow from [mass density](density.md) $\rho_\infty$ at rest at infinity has sonic [mass density](density.md) $e^{3/2}\rho_\infty$. Combining it with the [Bondi sonic point](bondi-sonic-point.md) and [mass conservation](mass-conservation.md) gives $\dot M=\pi e^{3/2}G^2M^2\rho_\infty/c_s^3$.
