# Quasar-cloud redshift separation

↑ **Parent:** [Comoving radial distance](comoving-radial-distance.md)

For nearby redshifts in a homogeneous expanding universe, the [comoving radial distance](comoving-radial-distance.md) increment is $c\,\delta z/H(z)$. Multiplying by the [scale factor](scale-factor-cosmology.md) at the absorber's epoch gives the displayed [proper distance in cosmology](proper-distance-in-cosmology.md). The conversion neglects [peculiar velocities](peculiar-velocity.md); observed redshift differences can also contain such velocities and errors in the [quasar](quasar.md) emission redshift.
