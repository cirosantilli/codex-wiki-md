# Entropy solution

↑ **Parent:** [Inviscid Burgers equation](inviscid-burgers-equation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entropy_solution)

An entropy solution is a [weak solution](weak-solution.md) of a nonlinear [scalar conservation law](scalar-conservation-law.md) that also satisfies entropy inequalities selecting physically admissible shocks. For convex fluxes, this excludes expansion shocks and restores uniqueness for bounded initial data.
