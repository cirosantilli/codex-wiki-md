# Thermopile

↑ **Parent:** [Thermocouple](thermocouple.md)

A [thermopile](thermopile.md) connects multiple [thermocouples](thermocouple.md) in series to turn a [temperature](temperature-split.md) difference into a larger electrical signal. Absorbed [thermal radiation](thermal-radiation.md) can heat its sensing junctions while reference junctions remain cooler; the signal depends on the thermal and optical design.

A [thermopile](thermopile.md) is an array of [thermocouples](thermocouple.md) connected as a [series circuit](series-circuit.md) with their [electromotive forces](electromotive-force.md) adding in the chosen terminal order. It measures a collective [temperature](temperature-split.md) difference through the [Seebeck effect](seebeck-effect.md). An open-circuit [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) requires no externally supplied [electric current](electric-current.md) through the array: a high-input-[electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) [voltmeter](voltmeter.md) reads the [signal](signal.md). The [heat](heat-split.md) that maintains the junction [temperature](temperature-split.md) difference still comes from somewhere, such as absorbed [electromagnetic radiation](electromagnetic-radiation-split.md).

In a membrane detector, an absorbing film heats the measuring junctions while reference junctions lie on the surrounding support. Actual junction [temperatures](temperature-split.md) can differ across the membrane.[p. 2, §2 Structure, Figs. 2-1–2-2: absorbing film, membrane, junction locations and nonuniform temperature](thermopile-detectors-by-hamamatsu-photonics-k-k.md) This converts difficult-to-observe [heat transfer](heat-transfer.md) into an accessible electrical [signal](signal.md); the series connection combines many small contributions without requiring every [thermocouple](thermocouple.md) to have a separate meter.
