# Monadic adjunction

↑ **Parent:** [Eilenberg-Moore category](eilenberg-moore-category.md)

An adjunction is monadic when its comparison functor from the right-hand category to the Eilenberg-Moore category of the induced monad is an equivalence.

The induced [monad](monad.md) determines this comparison; [Beck's monadicity theorem](beck-s-monadicity-theorem.md) gives a criterion for it to be an [equivalence of categories](equivalence-of-categories.md).
