# Barium star

↑ **Parent:** [Wind mass transfer in a binary star](wind-mass-transfer-in-a-binary-star.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barium_star)

A barium star is a main-sequence or giant star enriched in carbon and slow-neutron-capture elements despite being too unevolved to have made them itself. It was polluted by an asymptotic-giant-branch companion that is now a white dwarf.
