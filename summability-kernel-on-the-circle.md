# Summability kernel on the circle

↑ **Parent:** [Approximate identity](approximate-identity.md)

A sequence of continuous periodic functions satisfying the displayed conditions, with normalized [Haar measure](haar-measure.md) $m$ and every $\delta>0$, is a summability kernel. For $f$ in a [homogeneous Banach space on the circle](homogeneous-banach-space-on-the-circle.md), subtract $f\int k_n\,dm$ from its [convolution](convolution.md) with $k_n$. Translation continuity controls the resulting integral near zero, while boundedness by $2\|f\|_B$ and the vanishing tails control its complement. Thus $\|f*k_n-f\|_B\to0$. The nonnegative [Fejér kernel](fejer-kernel.md) is an example with $L^1$ norm one.
