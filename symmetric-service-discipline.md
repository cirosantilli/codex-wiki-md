# Symmetric service discipline

↑ **Parent:** [Service discipline](service-discipline.md)

In an ordered queue, let $\gamma_i(n)$ be the fraction of service capacity assigned to position $i$ when $n$ customers are present. A symmetric discipline inserts an arrival into position $i$ with probability $\gamma_i(n+1)$. The same position rule for service and insertion makes transitions satisfy [detailed balance](detailed-balance.md) for the word weights $\prod_i\alpha_{c_i}/\mu_{c_i}$. It accommodates class-dependent exponential service rates; [processor sharing](processor-sharing.md) is an example.
