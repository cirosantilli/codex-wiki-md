# Vertical dynamical timescale of a disk

↑ **Parent:** [Vertical structure of an astrophysical disk](vertical-structure-of-an-astrophysical-disk.md)

The time for a vertical [pressure](pressure.md) or gravity response. A [sound-crossing time](sound-crossing-time.md) and a small vertical oscillation period have this scale in a [thin disk](thin-disk.md). It need not equal a damping time.
