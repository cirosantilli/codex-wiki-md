# Sonic point of a scalar flux

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar conservation law](scalar-conservation-law.md)

For a differentiable [scalar conservation law](scalar-conservation-law.md) $u_t+f(u)_x=0$, a [sonic point of a scalar flux](sonic-point-of-a-scalar-flux.md) is a state $s$ where its [characteristic speed](characteristic-speed.md) $f'(s)$ vanishes. If $f$ is convex and this zero is unique, $f'<0$ on the left and $f'>0$ on the right, so $s$ is the unique minimum of the flux. The definition refers to a vanishing transport speed in a [scalar conservation law](scalar-conservation-law.md). In compressible [fluid mechanics](fluid-mechanics-split.md), a [sonic point](sonic-point.md) instead refers to flow at the local sound speed.
