# Centered convection-diffusion semidiscretization

↑ **Parent:** [Finite difference method](finite-difference-method.md)

With homogeneous [Dirichlet boundary conditions](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md), centered differences turn one-dimensional diffusion into a symmetric negative-definite matrix and constant advection into a skew-symmetric matrix. Their sum dissipates the discrete [Euclidean norm](euclidean-norm.md), giving mesh-uniform energy stability for every fixed advection coefficient.
