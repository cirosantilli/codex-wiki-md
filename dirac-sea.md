# Dirac sea

↑ **Parent:** [Antiparticle](antiparticle.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dirac_sea)

The historical interpretation fills the negative-energy electron states of the [Dirac equation](dirac-equation.md) and uses the [Pauli exclusion principle](pauli-exclusion-principle.md) to prevent additional electrons from falling into them. Removing an electron of energy $-E$ and charge $-e$ gives a hole of excitation energy $E$ and charge $+e$, interpreted as a positron. A [quantum field theory](quantum-field-theory-split.md) instead treats negative-frequency field coefficients as independent [antiparticle](antiparticle.md) [creation operators](creation-operator.md) in a [Fock space](fock-space.md), with positive excitation energies after [normal ordering](normal-ordering.md). No literal infinite material sea is needed. Bosonic [antiparticles](antiparticle.md) also exist, although a filled-sea exclusion argument cannot stabilize bosonic negative-energy levels.
