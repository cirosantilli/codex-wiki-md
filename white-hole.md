# White hole

↑ **Parent:** [Black hole](black-hole.md)

A white hole is the time reverse of a [black hole](black-hole.md): causal trajectories can emerge from its past singularity, while exterior future-directed trajectories cannot enter through its past horizon. The maximal [Schwarzschild spacetime](schwarzschild-spacetime.md) contains both a future black-hole interior and a past white-hole interior. Both have the same areal-radius range, illustrating why a global time orientation and choice of extension are essential in causal statements.
