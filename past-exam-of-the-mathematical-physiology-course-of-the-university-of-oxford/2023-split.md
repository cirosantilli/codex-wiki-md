# 2023

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the mathematical physiology course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Solutions to all three questions of the [mathematical physiology course of the University of Oxford](../mathematical-physiology-course-of-the-university-of-oxford.md) examination held on 31 May 2023. Original paper: [public examination PDF, inclusive bundle pages 321–324; paper pages 1–4](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/request_for_mathematics_examinat/response/3129786/attach/3/FOI%20202507%20740%20Data.pdf#page=321). All eighteen lettered parts are covered, including the third question even though only the best two answers counted. Question 1(c) contains a printed exponent error; Question 2(f) requires a signed recovery cutoff. Both are explained where they arise.
