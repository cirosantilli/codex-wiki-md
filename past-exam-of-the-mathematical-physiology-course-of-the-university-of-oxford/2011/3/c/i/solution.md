<h1 id="3/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Put $u=1+x$. Then $\dot x=-x(t-\tau)-x(t)x(t-\tau)$, so the [linearization](../../../../../linearization.md) is $\dot x=-x(t-\tau)$. A mode $e^{\lambda t}$ gives the [characteristic equation of a delay differential equation](../../../../../characteristic-equation-of-a-delay-differential-equation.md)

$$
\boxed{\lambda=-e^{-\lambda\tau}.}
$$

For real $\lambda\geq0$, the left side is nonnegative and the right side negative. The two real graphs therefore never meet there, as drawn in the figure. **There is no nonoscillatory exponential instability.** If stability is lost, the unstable [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) must be a nonreal conjugate pair, producing oscillatory growth; absence of a positive real root alone does not imply stability.
