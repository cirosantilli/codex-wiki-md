<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a constant solution $u(t)\equiv U$, its delayed value is also $U$. The equation becomes $0=U(1-U)$, so the only constant equilibria are $\boxed{U=0\text{ and }U=1}$. The [delay differential equation](../../../../delay-differential-equation.md) requires the same constant history to realize each equilibrium.
