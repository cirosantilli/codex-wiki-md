↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

There are four reversible transitions of the [ion channel](../../../../ion-channel.md), each changing exactly one gate:

$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{00}&\mathrel{\mathop{\rightleftharpoons}^{\alpha}_{\beta}}S_{10},&
S_{01}&\mathrel{\mathop{\rightleftharpoons}^{\alpha}_{\beta}}S_{11},\\
S_{00}&\mathrel{\mathop{\rightleftharpoons}^{\gamma}_{\delta}}S_{01},&
S_{10}&\mathrel{\mathop{\rightleftharpoons}^{\gamma}_{\delta}}S_{11}.
\end{aligned}
$$

These are first-order [reaction-rate equations](../../../../rate-equation.md): their [reaction rate constants](../../../../rate-constant.md) are per-gate transition rates. In the [continuous-time Markov chain](../../../../continuous-time-markov-chain-split.md) interpretation, simultaneous changes of both gates have [probability](../../../../probability.md) of order $(dt)^2$, so there is no direct diagonal transition in its [infinitesimal generator matrix](../../../../transition-intensity-matrix.md).
