<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Choose positive reference [concentrations](../../../../concentration.md) $C,C_s$ and a reference [flux](../../../../flux.md) $J_0$, and define

$$
u=\frac cC,\qquad v=\frac{c_s}{C_s},\qquad
\tau=kt,\qquad j_\pm=\frac{J_\pm}{J_0}.
$$

Overdots now mean [derivatives](../../../../derivative.md) with respect to the dimensionless time $\tau$. The store balance becomes

$$
\frac{kC_s}{J_0}\dot v=j_+-j_--\frac{k_sC_s}{J_0}v.
$$

The total balance becomes $\dot u+(C_s/C)\dot v=r/(kC)-u$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\mu=\frac r{kC},\qquad
\gamma=\frac{C_s}{C},\qquad
\varepsilon=\frac{kC_s}{J_0},\qquad
\delta=\frac{k_sC_s}{J_0},}
$$

and

$$
\boxed{\dot u=\mu-u-\gamma\dot v,\qquad
\varepsilon\dot v=j_+-j_--\delta v.}
$$

The reference quantities are scale choices, not additional kinetic variables; typically the response functions supply natural saturation [concentrations](../../../../concentration.md) and a maximal uptake [flux](../../../../flux.md). The identity $\delta/\varepsilon=k_s/k$ checks the [nondimensionalization](../../../../nondimensionalization.md). A small $\varepsilon$ means store exchange is fast compared with external cytosolic removal.
