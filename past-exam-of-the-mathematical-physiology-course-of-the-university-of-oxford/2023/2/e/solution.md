<h1 id="2/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

On an attracting branch of the [critical manifold](../../../../critical-manifold.md), $w=f(v)$ to leading order. The [chain rule](../../../../chain-rule.md) therefore gives

$$
f'(v)\dot v=\dot w=\gamma v-f(v),\qquad
dt=\frac{f'(v)}{\gamma v-f(v)}\,dv.
$$

The initial fast segment ends at $v=1$, and the first slow phase ends at the right [fold point of a critical manifold](../../../../fold-point-of-a-critical-manifold.md), $v_R$. Hence

$$
\boxed{T_1=\int_1^{v_R}g(v)\,dv,\qquad
g(v)=\frac{-3v^2+2(1+a)v-a}{\gamma v-v(a-v)(v-1)},\qquad
v_1=1,\quad v_2=v_R.}
$$

On this interval $f'(v)<0$ and $\gamma v-f(v)>0$. The [integrand](../../../../integrand.md) is negative, but the upper limit is smaller than the lower limit, so $T_1>0$. The numerator tends to zero at the fold while the denominator remains positive; the leading [integral](../../../../integral-split.md) is finite. Fast segments and the [fold delay](../../../../fold-delay.md) give vanishing corrections as $\varepsilon\to0^+$.
