<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In the [Hodgkin-Huxley model](../../../../hodgkin-huxley-model.md), $V$ is the intracellular-minus-extracellular [membrane potential](../../../../membrane-potential-split.md), $C_m$ is the [membrane capacitance](../../../../membrane-capacitance.md) per unit area, and $I_{\mathrm{app}}$ is the applied inward [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md) per unit area. A [space clamp](../../../../space-clamp.md) makes $V$ spatially uniform, leaving temporal [ordinary differential equations](../../../../ordinary-differential-equation.md). With outward ionic [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md) positive, the [capacitance](../../../../capacitance.md) balance is $C_m\dot V=I_{\mathrm{app}}-I_i$.

The variables $m,n,h$ lie between zero and one. They describe [sodium](../../../../sodium.md) activation, [potassium](../../../../potassium.md) activation and availability against [sodium](../../../../sodium.md) inactivation, respectively. Thus larger $h$ means less inactivation. Their voltage-dependent limiting values are $m_\infty,n_\infty,h_\infty$, and $\tau_m,\tau_n,\tau_h$ are their positive relaxation [time constants](../../../../time-constant.md).

Each ionic term has the [Ohm's law](../../../../ohm-s-law.md) form conductance times driving potential. The [sodium](../../../../sodium.md) term has instantaneous [electrical conductance](../../../../electrical-conductance.md) $g_{\mathrm{Na}}m^3h$, the [potassium](../../../../potassium.md) term has instantaneous [electrical conductance](../../../../electrical-conductance.md) $g_{\mathrm K}n^4$, and the leak term has constant [electrical conductance](../../../../electrical-conductance.md) $g_{\mathrm L}$. Here the $g$ constants are maximal [electrical conductances](../../../../electrical-conductance.md) per area, and $V_{\mathrm{Na}},V_{\mathrm K},V_{\mathrm L}$ are the corresponding [reversal potentials](../../../../reversal-potential.md). The factors $m^3h$ and $n^4$ arise from [independent permissive gates in an ion channel](../../../../independent-permissive-gates-in-an-ion-channel.md). A conducting channel carries zero net ionic [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md) at its [reversal potential](../../../../reversal-potential.md).
