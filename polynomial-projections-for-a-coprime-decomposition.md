# Polynomial projections for a coprime decomposition

↑ **Parent:** [Kernel decomposition for coprime polynomials](kernel-decomposition-for-coprime-polynomials.md)

Suppose $p(T)q(T)=0$ for a [linear operator](linear-operator.md) $T$, and [Bézout's identity for polynomials](bezout-s-identity-for-polynomials.md) gives $ap+bq=1$. The [kernel decomposition for coprime polynomials](kernel-decomposition-for-coprime-polynomials.md) is $V=\ker p(T)\oplus\ker q(T)$. The displayed operators are the corresponding [projection operators](projection-linear-algebra.md): $P$ is the identity on $\ker p(T)$ and zero on $\ker q(T)$, while $Q$ has the opposite action. Thus $P+Q=I$, $P^2=P$, $Q^2=Q$ and $PQ=QP=0$. These actions also prove independence from the choice of Bézout coefficients. Every summand is an [invariant subspace](invariant-subspace.md) of $T$, since the projections are [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) in $T$ and commute with it.
