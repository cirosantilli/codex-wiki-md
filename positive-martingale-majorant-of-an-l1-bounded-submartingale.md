# Positive martingale majorant of an L1-bounded submartingale

↑ **Parent:** [Submartingale](submartingale.md)

For a [submartingale](submartingale.md) with $\sup_n\mathbb E|X_n|=C<\infty$, its [positive part](positive-part-of-a-real-valued-function.md) is a [submartingale](submartingale.md) by the [conditional Jensen inequality](conditional-jensen-inequality.md). Thus the displayed [conditional expectations](conditional-expectation.md) increase in $p$. Their limits are finite [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md) and have [expectations](expected-value.md) at most $C$, by the [monotone convergence theorem](monotone-convergence-theorem.md). Conditional monotone convergence and the [tower property of conditional expectation](law-of-total-expectation.md) give $\mathbb E[M_{n+1}\mid\mathcal F_n]=M_n$. Moreover $M_n\geq X_n^+$. Consequently $Y_n=M_n-X_n$ is a nonnegative [supermartingale](supermartingale.md), $\sup_n\mathbb E Y_n\leq2C$, and $X=M-Y$. The resulting [martingale](martingale-split.md) need not have [uniform integrability](uniform-integrability.md).
