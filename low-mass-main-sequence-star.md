# Low-mass main-sequence star

↑ **Parent:** [Star](star.md)

A low-mass star maintained by sustained [hydrogen burning](hydrogen-burning.md), rather than the continuing cooling of a [brown dwarf](brown-dwarf.md). Near the [hydrogen-burning minimum mass](hydrogen-burning-minimum-mass.md) its radius is of order a Jovian radius. Stars below roughly $0.35M_\odot$ are approximately fully convective, with a radiative photosphere; mass, composition and age affect the precise transition.
