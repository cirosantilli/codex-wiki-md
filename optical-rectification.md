# Optical rectification

↑ **Parent:** [Nonlinear optics](nonlinear-optics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optical_rectification)

A static or slowly varying [electric polarization](polarization-density.md) produced by nonlinear response to an oscillating optical [electric field](electric-field-split.md). The constant term in [nonlinear optical susceptibility](nonlinear-optical-susceptibility.md) is its simplest model. An [optical pulse](optical-pulse.md) has a varying envelope, so its cycle-averaged quadratic response can vary during the pulse rather than remaining constant forever. This term alone does not imply a radiated zero-[frequency](frequency.md) wave.
