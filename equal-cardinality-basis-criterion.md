# Equal-cardinality basis criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Basis](basis.md)

Let $V$ have finite [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) $n$ and let $S\subseteq V$ have $n$ [vectors](vector.md). Then spanning, [linear independence](linear-independence.md) and being a [basis](basis.md) are equivalent. From a [spanning set](spanning-set.md), remove redundant [vectors](vector.md) until an independent [spanning set](spanning-set.md) remains. A removal would give a [basis](basis.md) of fewer than $n$ [vectors](vector.md), contradicting [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) invariance. Conversely, if $S$ is independent and a [vector](vector.md) lies outside its [span](linear-span.md), adjoining it gives $n+1$ independent [vectors](vector.md), again impossible. Thus an independent $S$ [spans](linear-span.md).
