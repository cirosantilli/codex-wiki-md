# Amplifier

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical circuit](electrical-circuit-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amplifier)

An [amplifier](amplifier.md) uses supplied [energy](energy-split.md) to increase a [signal](signal.md) amplitude or delivered [power](power.md). An electronic [amplifier](amplifier.md) is an [electrical circuit](electrical-circuit-split.md); real devices add [noise](noise-signal-processing.md) and have a frequency-dependent [frequency response](frequency-response.md).
