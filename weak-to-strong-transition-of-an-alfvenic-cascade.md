<h1 id="weak-to-strong-transition-of-an-alfvenic-cascade">Weak-to-strong transition of an Alfvénic cascade</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Weak Alfvénic cascade](weak-alfvenic-cascade.md)

In a [weak Alfvénic cascade](weak-alfvenic-cascade.md) at fixed $l_\parallel$, $\chi=\tau_A/\tau_{\rm nl}\propto l_\perp^{-1/2}$ grows as the perpendicular scale shrinks. If its outer value is $\chi_0\ll1$ at $L_\perp$, it reaches order one at the displayed scale. Beyond it the weak-interaction ordering fails and a [critical balance](critical-balance.md) model may become appropriate.
