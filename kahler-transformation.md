<h1 id="kahler-transformation">Kähler transformation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Kähler potential](kahler-potential.md)

Adding a [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function.md) $f$ and its conjugate does not change the [Kähler metric](kahler-metric.md). In global [supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md), these terms integrate to zero in the [D-term](d-term.md) action. In Planck-unit [supergravity](supergravity.md), the simultaneous transformation $W\mapsto e^{-f}W$ leaves the [scalar potential](scalar-potential.md) invariant; shifting $K$ while holding $W$ fixed generally does not.
