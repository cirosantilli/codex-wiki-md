# Starting-value obstruction in wedge conjugate maximal inequalities

↑ **Parent:** [Burkholder-Davis-Gundy inequalities](burkholder-davis-gundy-inequalities.md)

Real and imaginary parts of an analytic Brownian image, after subtracting their starting values, have equal [quadratic variation](quadratic-variation.md). The [Burkholder-Davis-Gundy inequalities](burkholder-davis-gundy-inequalities.md) compare their centered maximal moments with constants depending only on $p$. A boundary-point normalization $P(0)=0$ does not remove the starting imaginary value. For $P(z)=iz$ on a wedge of opening $\alpha\pi<\pi/2$, $\sup|\operatorname{Im}z|\leq\tan(\alpha\pi)\sup|\operatorname{Re}z|$. If $\alpha p<1$ both relevant moments are finite, so an uncentered comparison requires a constant at least $\cot^p(\alpha\pi)$; no bound depending only on $p$ can hold over all such wedges. For fixed $\alpha>0$, [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) with finite real-part maximal moment have degree $d<1/(\alpha p)$ by the wedge radial-maximum tail. They form a [finite-dimensional vector space](finite-dimensional-vector-space.md), and [equivalence of norms in finite dimensions](equivalence-of-norms-in-finite-dimensions.md) gives a valid constant depending on $p,\alpha$.
