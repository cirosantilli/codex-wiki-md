# Maximal torsion-free quotient

↑ **Parent:** [Torsion-free module](torsion-free-module.md)

For a module $M$ over an [integral domain](integral-domain.md), the quotient $M/T(M)$ by its [torsion submodule](torsion-submodule.md) is torsion-free. Every homomorphism from $M$ to a torsion-free module factors uniquely through $M/T(M)$ by the [universal property of a quotient module](universal-property-of-a-quotient-module.md).
