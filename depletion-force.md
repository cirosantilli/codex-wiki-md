# Depletion force

↑ **Parent:** [Colloid](colloid.md)

A [depletion force](depletion-force.md) is an effective interaction caused by exclusion of smaller particles or macromolecules from regions around larger objects. Overlap of the excluded regions increases the accessible [volume](volume.md) for the smaller species. At fixed reservoir [osmotic pressure](osmotic-pressure.md) $\Pi_p$, the associated pair [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md) is $-\Pi_p$ times the overlap [volume](volume.md) in the ideal dilute-depletant approximation. It is an entropic attraction for two large hard particles. Surface adsorption can introduce different interactions, so adding an arbitrary polymer does not by itself guarantee depletion attraction.
