↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [entropy chain rule](../../../../../chain-rule-for-information-entropy.md) gives

$$
I(X;Y)=H(X)-H(X\mid Y)=H(Y)-H(Y\mid X).
$$

[Conditional entropy](../../../../../conditional-entropy.md) is nonnegative, so $I(X;Y)\le\min(H(X),H(Y))$. For finite [entropies](../../../../../information-entropy-split.md), if $H(X)\le H(Y)$, equality holds exactly when $H(X\mid Y)=0$, meaning **$X$ is a [function](../../../../../function-split.md) of $Y$ almost surely**. If $H(Y)\le H(X)$, interchange the variables. When their [entropies](../../../../../information-entropy-split.md) are equal, equality requires both functional relations. Zero [conditional entropy](../../../../../conditional-entropy.md) has this characterization because each conditional [probability distribution](../../../../../probability-distribution-split.md) must be a [point mass](../../../../../point-mass.md).
