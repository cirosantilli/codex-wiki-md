<h1 id="4/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A binary [prefix code](../../../../../prefix-code.md) assigns each source symbol a distinct finite binary word, with no assigned word a prefix of another. It is instantaneous because a decoder can identify the symbol as soon as its last digit arrives, without reading digits from the next word. Such a code is optimal when it minimizes

$$
\bar\ell=\sum_xp_x|c(x)|
$$

among all binary prefix codes for that source distribution. Symbols of zero probability may be removed from the optimization.
