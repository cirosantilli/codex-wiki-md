<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

[Gibbs inequality](../../../../gibbs-inequality.md) makes [mutual information](../../../../mutual-information.md) nonnegative, so $C\ge0$. The two conditional-[Shannon entropy](../../../../information-entropy-split.md) expressions give

$$
I(X;Y)=H(X)-H(X\mid Y)=H(Y)-H(Y\mid X)\le\min(H(X),H(Y)).
$$

Each [Shannon entropy](../../../../information-entropy-split.md) is bounded by the base-two logarithm of its alphabet size. Maximizing over the input distribution therefore gives $\boxed{0\le C\le\log_2m}$ for the smaller size $m$.
