[Maximum-likelihood decoding](../../../../../maximum-likelihood-decoding.md) chooses a codeword maximizing $p(y\mid x)$ for the received word $y$. It uses the [likelihood](../../../../../likelihood-function.md) alone, without weighting by the prior probabilities of the codewords.
