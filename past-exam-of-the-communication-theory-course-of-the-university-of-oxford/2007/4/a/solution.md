<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

With equally likely codewords, the prior factor in the ideal-observer criterion is constant, so it agrees with maximum [likelihood](../../../../likelihood-function.md). On the [binary symmetric channel](../../../../binary-symmetric-channel.md),

$$
p(y\mid x)=p^{d_H(x,y)}(1-p)^{n-d_H(x,y)}.
$$

For $0<p<1/2$ this is strictly decreasing in [Hamming distance](../../../../hamming-distance.md), so the [likelihood](../../../../likelihood-function.md) maximizers are precisely the nearest codewords. With the same tie convention all three decoders therefore agree. At $p=0$ every possible received word equals its transmitted codeword, and the rules agree on all positive-probability outputs; outputs having zero probability need no posterior assignment.
