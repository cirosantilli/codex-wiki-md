<h1 id="3/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For finite-valued [random variables](../../../../../random-variable-split.md), their [mutual information](../../../../../mutual-information.md) is

$$
\boxed{I(X;Y)=H(X)+H(Y)-H(X,Y)=H(Y)-H(Y\mid X)=H(X)-H(X\mid Y).}
$$

Equivalently,

$$
I(X;Y)=\sum_{x,y}p(x,y)\log_2\frac{p(x,y)}{p_X(x)p_Y(y)},
$$

with zero joint-probability terms omitted. It is the [relative entropy](../../../../../kullback-leibler-divergence.md) of the joint law from the product of its marginals and is nonnegative, with [equality](../../../../../equality-mathematics.md) exactly when the two variables are [independent](../../../../../independent-random-variables.md).
