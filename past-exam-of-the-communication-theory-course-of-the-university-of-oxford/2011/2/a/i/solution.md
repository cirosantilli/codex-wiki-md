<h1 id="2/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A binary code assigns a finite binary word $c_k$ to each source symbol $x_k$. In the ordinary unframed convention these [codewords](../../../../../codeword.md) are nonempty. It is [uniquely decodable](../../../../../decipherable-code.md) when [concatenation](../../../../../concatenation.md) gives an [injective](../../../../../injective-function.md) map from finite symbol strings to [bit](../../../../../bit.md) strings:

$$
\boxed{c_{i_1}\cdots c_{i_r}=c_{j_1}\cdots c_{j_s}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad r=s\text{ and }i_t=j_t\text{ for all }t.}
$$

The decoder can recover the entire symbol sequence without being told where [codewords](../../../../../codeword.md) end. This is stronger than merely assigning distinct words to individual symbols.
