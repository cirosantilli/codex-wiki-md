↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

An [instantaneous code](../../../../../prefix-code.md) can decode each symbol as soon as its final [bit](../../../../../bit.md) arrives, without waiting for later [bits](../../../../../bit.md). Equivalently it is [prefix-free](../../../../../prefix-code.md): no [codeword](../../../../../codeword.md) is a proper prefix of another, and different symbols receive different [codewords](../../../../../codeword.md).

For nonempty [codewords](../../../../../codeword.md), decoding greedily from the start of the [bit](../../../../../bit.md) string identifies the first [codeword](../../../../../codeword.md) unambiguously; repeating identifies the whole sequence. Thus **an instantaneous code is uniquely decodable** under the ordinary nonempty-word convention.
