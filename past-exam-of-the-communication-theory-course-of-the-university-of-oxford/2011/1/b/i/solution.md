<h1 id="1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For the [uniform distribution](../../../../../continuous-uniform-distribution.md) $u_r=1/n$, direct expansion gives

$$
d(p_X,u)=\sum_rp_X(r)\log_2p_X(r)+\log_2n=\log_2n-H(X).
$$

Part (a) therefore proves

$$
\boxed{H(X)\leq\log_2n,}
$$

with [equality](../../../../../equality-mathematics.md) exactly for $p_X(r)=1/n$ for every $r$. Thus the [uniform distribution](../../../../../continuous-uniform-distribution.md) is the unique [entropy](../../../../../information-entropy-split.md) maximizer.
