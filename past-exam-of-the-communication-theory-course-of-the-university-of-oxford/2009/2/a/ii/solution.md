↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A [prefix code](../../../../../prefix-code.md) has no [codeword](../../../../../codeword.md) that is a proper prefix of another. Its [code tree](../../../../../prefix-tree-of-a-code.md) therefore places source symbols at leaves. It is instantaneous because the decoder knows that a source symbol has ended as soon as its leaf is reached, without needing to inspect later transmitted symbols.
