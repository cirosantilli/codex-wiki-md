<h1 id="2/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

A [compact code](../../../../../optimal-prefix-code.md) or [optimal prefix code](../../../../../optimal-prefix-code.md) minimizes the [expected codeword length](../../../../../expected-codeword-length.md) $\sum_xp_x\ell_x$ for the given source probabilities and coding alphabet. The relevant comparison here is among binary [prefix codes](../../../../../prefix-code.md). The minimum is also the minimum among [uniquely decodable codes](../../../../../decipherable-code.md), since the [Kraft-McMillan inequality](../../../../../kraft-mcmillan-inequality.md) permits every uniquely decodable length list to be realized by a [prefix code](../../../../../prefix-code.md) with the same lengths.
