In the terminology used here, a [block code](../../../../../block-code.md) assigns a distinct word of the same length to every source symbol. For a binary code with $m$ symbols this common length must satisfy $2^\ell\geq m$. The equal-length property is what matters in this question; elsewhere the term can also refer more generally to coding blocks of source symbols.
