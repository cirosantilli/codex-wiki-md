<h1 id="2/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Set $Z=g(X)$. Given $X$, the value of $Z$ is certain, so $H(Z\mid X)=0$. Applying the [entropy](../../../../../information-entropy-split.md) identity in the two orders gives

$$
H(X)=H(X,Z)=H(Z)+H(X\mid Z)\geq H(Z),
$$

because [conditional entropy](../../../../../conditional-entropy.md) is nonnegative. Consequently

$$
\boxed{H(g(X))\leq H(X).}
$$

A deterministic coarsening can merge distinguishable source outcomes, but cannot create additional [Shannon entropy](../../../../../information-entropy-split.md).
