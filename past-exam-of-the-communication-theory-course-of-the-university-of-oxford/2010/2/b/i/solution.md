<h1 id="2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Choose a most probable symbol $x_*$, and let $B=\mathbf1_{\{X=x_*\}}$. Its [binary entropy](../../../../../binary-entropy.md) is

$$
H(B)=h_2(\Pi)=-\Pi\log_2\Pi-(1-\Pi)\log_2(1-\Pi).
$$

Since $B$ is a function of $X$, part (a)(ii) gives $H(X)\geq h_2(\Pi)$. On $[1/2,1]$, the concave function $h_2$ lies above the chord joining $(1/2,1)$ and $(1,0)$. That chord has height $2(1-\Pi)$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{H(X)\geq h_2(\Pi)\geq2(1-\Pi).}
$$

The endpoint $\Pi=1$ is included by [continuity](../../../../../continuous-function.md) and corresponds to a deterministic source.
