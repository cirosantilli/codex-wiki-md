<h1 id="2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Every positive atom has [probability](../../../../../probability.md) at most $\Pi$, so $-\log_2p(x)\geq-\log_2\Pi$. Averaging yields

$$
H(X)=\sum_xp(x)(-\log_2p(x))\geq-\log_2\Pi.
$$

For $0<\Pi\leq1/2$, the supplied inequality $4\Pi\leq4^\Pi$ gives $2+\log_2\Pi\leq2\Pi$. Rearranging proves

$$
\boxed{H(X)\geq-\log_2\Pi\geq2(1-\Pi).}
$$

The two regimes combine into the [entropy lower bound from the largest atom](../../../../../entropy-lower-bound-from-the-largest-atom.md), valid for every finite [probability distribution](../../../../../probability-distribution-split.md).
