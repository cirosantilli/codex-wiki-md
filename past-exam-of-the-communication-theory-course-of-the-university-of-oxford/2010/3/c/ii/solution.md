<h1 id="3/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Every row has [Shannon entropy](../../../../../information-entropy-split.md) $1$, so $H(Y\mid X)=1$ for every input law. Since the output alphabet has three symbols, [Gibbs inequality](../../../../../gibbs-inequality.md) gives $H(Y)\leq\log_23$. The uniform input gives

$$
q=\left(\frac{p_1+p_2}2,\frac{p_1+p_3}2,\frac{p_2+p_3}2\right)=(1/3,1/3,1/3),
$$

so it attains the upper bound. Therefore

$$
\boxed{p_X=(1/3,1/3,1/3),\qquad C=\log_23-1=\log_2(3/2).}
$$

Uniform output is necessary for equality in the [entropy](../../../../../information-entropy-split.md) bound, and the three displayed equations then force the uniform input, so this optimum input law is unique.
