# Block encoding

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum theory](quantum-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Block_encoding)

A block encoding of a matrix $A$ is a [unitary operator](unitary-operator.md) whose designated block equals $A/\alpha$ for a known normalization factor $\alpha$. It gives a quantum circuit coherent access to matrices that need not themselves be unitary.
