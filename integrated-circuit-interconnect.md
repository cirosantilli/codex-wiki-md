# Integrated circuit interconnect

↑ **Parent:** [Integrated circuit](integrated-circuit.md)

An [integrated circuit interconnect](integrated-circuit-interconnect.md) is a conducting path joining circuit regions. In [Robert Noyce](robert-noyce.md)'s patent, deposited metal strips cross retained oxide on [silicon](silicon.md); selected openings permit contact to the [semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md) while oxide prevents unwanted contact across [p-n junctions](p-n-junction.md). Figs. 3–5 combine detection, filtering and [electronic amplifier](electronic-amplifier.md) functions using a [transistor](transistor.md) in one structure. The key is both connectivity and insulation: a metal path must touch its intended terminals while crossing other regions without shorting them.[Description of Figs. 1–5, oxide tongues, metal strips and integrated circuit; claim 1](semiconductor-device-and-lead-structure-by-robert-noyce.md)
