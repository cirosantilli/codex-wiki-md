# Methyl radical

↑ **Parent:** [Radical (chemistry)](radical-chemistry.md)

The [methyl radical](methyl-radical.md) is the neutral [radical](radical-chemistry.md) $\mathrm{CH_3}^{\bullet}$, with an unpaired [Electron](electron.md). It carries the [methane chlorination chain](methane-chlorination-chain.md) between its two propagation steps.
