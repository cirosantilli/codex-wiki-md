# Inverse metric volume variation

↑ **Parent:** [Metric tensor](metric-tensor.md)

For a nondegenerate [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md), differentiating $g_{ac}g^{cb}=\delta_a{}^b$ gives $\delta g_{ab}=-g_{ac}g_{bd}\delta g^{cd}$. Combining this with the [Jacobi determinant derivative formula](jacobi-determinant-derivative-formula.md) proves the displayed volume-density variation. It is the determinant contribution when defining a [stress-energy tensor](stress-energy-tensor.md) by varying the inverse [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md).
