# Multiplicative correction for summable adapted drift

↑ **Parent:** [Almost sure supermartingale convergence theorem](almost-sure-supermartingale-convergence-theorem.md)

Suppose $X_n\geq0$ is an [integrable](integrability.md) [adapted process](adapted-process.md), $Y_n\geq0$ is finite and adapted, and $\mathbb E[X_{n+1}\mid\mathcal F_n]\leq(1+Y_n)X_n$. The displayed correction is a nonnegative [supermartingale](supermartingale.md): its next denominator is [measurable](measurability.md) at time $n$, and its reciprocal is at most one. If $\sum_nY_n<\infty$ [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md), the denominators converge to a finite positive limit since $\log(1+y)\leq y$. The [almost sure supermartingale convergence theorem](almost-sure-supermartingale-convergence-theorem.md) then gives finite [almost sure convergence](almost-sure-convergence.md) of $X_n$. No [integrability](integrability.md) of the infinite product or of $\sum_nY_n$ is needed.
