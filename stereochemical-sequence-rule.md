# Stereochemical sequence rule

↑ **Parent:** [Absolute configuration](absolute-configuration.md)

[Stereochemical sequence rules](stereochemical-sequence-rule.md) rank [substituents](substituent.md) for [R and S configuration](r-and-s-configuration.md). Compare the directly attached [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) by [atomic number](atomic-number.md) first, then resolve ties by comparing successive shells at the first difference. [Isotopes](isotope.md) and multiple [chemical bonds](chemical-bond-split.md) require additional rules. This ranking convention does not involve [optical rotation](optical-rotation.md).[McMurry, Organic Chemistry, §5.5, Rules 1–3](organic-chemistry-by-john-mcmurry/5-5-sequence-rules-for-specifying-configuration.md)
