# Tracial positive functional

↑ **Parent:** [Positive functional on a C-star algebra](positive-functional-on-a-c-star-algebra.md)

A positive [bounded linear functional](continuous-linear-functional.md) is tracial when its value on a product is unchanged by reversing the two factors. When normalized by $\tau(1)=1$, it is a tracial state. A [vector](vector.md) functional $\tau(a)=\langle a\Omega,\Omega\rangle$ is faithful if the [vector](vector.md) is separating, since $\tau(a^*a)=\|a\Omega\|^2$. Its [trace](matrix-trace.md) identity makes the associated [Tomita operator](tomita-operator.md) isometric.
