# Reflexive coequalizer

↑ **Parent:** [Reflexive pair](reflexive-pair.md)

A reflexive coequalizer is a [coequalizer](coequalizer.md) of a [reflexive pair](reflexive-pair.md), meaning a parallel pair $r,s:X\rightrightarrows Y$ with a common section $t:Y\to X$ satisfying $rt=st=1_Y$. A [functor](functor.md) preserves reflexive coequalizers when it takes every such coequalizer to a coequalizer of the image pair.
