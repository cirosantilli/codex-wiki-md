# One-parameter subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Lie group](lie-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One-parameter_subgroup)

A [one-parameter subgroup](one-parameter-subgroup.md) of a [Lie group](lie-group.md) $G$ is a smooth [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md) $\gamma:(\mathbb R,+)\to G$: it satisfies $\gamma(s+t)=\gamma(s)\gamma(t)$, with $\gamma(0)=e$ and $\gamma(-t)=\gamma(t)^{-1}$. Every such map has the form $\gamma(t)=\exp(tX)$ for a unique $X=\gamma'(0)$ in the [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) of $G$. Thus its initial [tangent vector](tangent-vector-split.md) determines the whole motion, for all real times. The map need not be injective; the word subgroup describes its image. See [MIT 18.745, Lecture 7, Proposition 7.1](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-745-lie-groups-and-lie-algebras-i-fall-2020/mit18_745_f20_lec07.pdf).
