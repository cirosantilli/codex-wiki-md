# Sub-Poisson random variable in the right tail

↑ **Parent:** [Concentration inequality](concentration-inequality.md)

A centered [random variable](random-variable-split.md) $X$ is sub-Poisson in the right tail with variance parameter $\sigma^2$ when

$$
\log\mathbb E e^{\lambda X}\leq\sigma^2(e^\lambda-\lambda-1)
$$

for every $\lambda\geq0$.
