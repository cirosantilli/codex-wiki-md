# Shock polar

↑ **Parent:** [Oblique shock](oblique-shock.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shock_polar)

A [shock polar](shock-polar.md) is the locus of possible downstream [velocity](velocity.md) vectors, or an equivalent [pressure](pressure.md)/deflection relation, as the orientation of an [oblique shock](oblique-shock.md) varies for fixed upstream conditions. [Entropy](entropy.md) admissibility selects physical compressive shocks. The strong-shock perfect-gas [velocity](velocity.md) polar is a circle, while finite-Mach polars have a different shape.
