# Quadratic bosonic Hamiltonian

↑ **Parent:** [Bosonic Bogoliubov diagonalization](bosonic-bogoliubov-diagonalization.md)

A quadratic bosonic Hamiltonian contains terms of degree two in [bosonic creation operators](bosonic-creation-operator.md) and [bosonic annihilation operators](bosonic-annihilation-operator.md), with an optional constant. The number-conserving [matrix](matrix.md) $A$ is [Hermitian](hermitian-operator.md) and the pair [matrix](matrix.md) $B$ may be taken [symmetric](symmetric-matrix.md). Positive stable forms can be diagonalized by a canonical [Bogoliubov transformation](bogoliubov-transformation.md); the strict stability condition matters at [zero modes](zero-mode.md).
