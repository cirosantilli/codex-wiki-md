# Zeroth law of black-hole mechanics

↑ **Parent:** [Black-hole thermodynamics](black-hole-thermodynamics.md)

The zeroth law states that the [surface gravity](surface-gravity.md) is constant over a stationary Killing horizon under the standard field-equation and energy assumptions.
