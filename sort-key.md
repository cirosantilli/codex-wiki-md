# Sort key

↑ **Parent:** [Sorting algorithm](sorting-algorithm.md)

A [sort key](sort-key.md) is the value extracted from a complete entry to determine its ordering. Model extraction as a [function](function-split.md) $k$ into a [total order](total-order.md); compare $k(A_i)$ and $k(A_j)$, while moving the complete entries. Ordering [sorting records](sorting-record.md) by an age field does not also compare their names. Repeated [sort keys](sort-key.md) are allowed, so this induces equal-key groups even when the complete [sorting records](sorting-record.md) differ. Keep the [sort key](sort-key.md) [function](function-split.md) and the comparison rule fixed throughout a sort; inconsistent comparisons need not admit any sorted result.
