# Presentation relation sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Relation module](relation-module.md)

If $I_F$ is the augmentation ideal of $\mathbb ZF$, a [free presentation](free-presentation.md) $G\cong F/R$ gives an exact sequence

$$
0\longrightarrow R/[R,R]\longrightarrow\mathbb ZG\otimes_{\mathbb ZF}I_F
\longrightarrow\mathbb ZG\longrightarrow\mathbb Z\longrightarrow0.
$$

The middle module is free over $\mathbb ZG$ on the free generators of $F$.
