# Spectator quark

↑ **Parent:** [Quark](quark.md)

A constituent [quark](quark.md) that does not participate in the elementary weak vertex of a [meson](meson.md) decay. It combines with a produced [quark](quark.md) or [antiquark](antiquark.md) in the final [hadron](hadron.md).
