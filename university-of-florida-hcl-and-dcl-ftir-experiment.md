# University of Florida HCl and DCl FTIR experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular vibration](molecular-vibration.md)

The University of Florida's [Rotation Vibration Spectrum of the HCl Molecule, University of Florida laboratory](rotation-vibration-spectrum-of-the-hcl-molecule-university-of-florida-laboratory.md) protocol, printed pp. 2–3 and 10–11, uses a scanning [Michelson interferometer](michelson-interferometer.md) for [Fourier transform spectroscopy](fourier-transform-spectroscopy.md). An air-filled [silicon](silicon.md)-window cell supplies the background. Supervised preparation adds concentrated [hydrogen chloride](hydrogen-chloride.md) liquid; the cleaned cell subsequently receives DCl liquid. Acquisition settings are identical.

The readout is transmission or [absorbance](absorbance.md) against [spectroscopic wavenumber](spectroscopic-wavenumber.md), resolving [molecular rotation](molecular-rotation.md) alongside stretching. Background addresses instrument and cell contributions; cleaning addresses carryover. Excessive absorption impairs analysis. Assigning [isotopes](isotope.md) and rotational branches precedes fitting band parameters to test [isotope scaling of diatomic vibration](isotope-scaling-of-diatomic-vibration.md). These are prescribed measurements, not numerical observations supplied here.[printed pp. 10–12: Measurements, Preliminary Analysis and Data Analysis, including matched scans, excessive absorption, isotope assignments and fitted band origin](rotation-vibration-spectrum-of-the-hcl-molecule-university-of-florida-laboratory.md)

For a transparent ideal model, add the [linear rigid rotor](linear-rigid-rotor.md) to the [harmonic vibrational energy levels](harmonic-vibrational-energy-levels.md) and assume the same frequency-valued [rotational constant](rotational-constant.md) $B$ in both vibrational states:

$$
E_{v,J}=V_0+h\nu_{\rm harm}(v+1/2)+hB J(J+1).
$$

For a spin-free [diatomic molecule](diatomic-molecule.md) with no electronic [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md), [electric-dipole selection rules](electric-dipole-selection-rule.md) give $\Delta J=\pm1$ for this vibrational absorption. Subtracting initial from final [energy eigenvalues](energy-eigenvalue-split.md) yields

$$
\begin{aligned}
\widetilde\nu_R(J)&=\widetilde\nu_{\rm harm}+2\widetilde B(J+1),&&J=0,1,2,\ldots,\\
\widetilde\nu_P(J)&=\widetilde\nu_{\rm harm}-2\widetilde B J,&&J=1,2,3,\ldots,\qquad \widetilde B=B/c.
\end{aligned}
$$

These are two rotational branches about the vibrational band origin, not a single stretch line. For stipulated dimensionless values $\widetilde\nu_{\rm harm}=100$ and $\widetilde B=1$, the first three R-branch positions are $102,104,106$, and the first three P-branch positions are $98,96,94$. There is no $J=0\to-1$ transition. The numbers illustrate level subtraction; they are not HCl measurements. Real upper and lower [rotational constants](rotational-constant.md) differ, and [centrifugal distortion](centrifugal-distortion.md) shifts lines further.
