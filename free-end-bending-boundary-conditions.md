# Free-end bending boundary conditions

↑ **Parent:** [Self-adjoint endpoint conditions for filament bending](self-adjoint-endpoint-conditions-for-filament-bending.md)

Variation of the small-slope bending energy of an [elastic filament](elastic-filament.md) gives endpoint work $A[h^{(2)}\delta h^{(1)}-h^{(3)}\delta h]$. Arbitrary endpoint displacement and slope variations force the bending moment and transverse force to vanish. These are $h^{(2)}=h^{(3)}=0$. They define a [self-adjoint](self-adjoint-operator.md) nonnegative fourth-order bending operator on a finite interval; its kernel contains rigid translation and tilt.
