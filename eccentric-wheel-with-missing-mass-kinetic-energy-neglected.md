# Eccentric wheel with missing-mass kinetic energy neglected

↑ **Parent:** [Rolling without slipping](rolling-without-slipping.md)

Model an eccentric-hole wheel as a complete uniform [disk](disk-mathematics.md) for [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) only, subtracting the absent disc's [potential energy](potential-energy.md). If $s=R\theta$ and $\theta$ is its hole-centre direction measured clockwise from upward vertical, [rolling without slipping](rolling-without-slipping.md) gives $T\simeq3D\dot s^2/4$. On a slope of [angle](angle.md) $\alpha$, its [potential energy](potential-energy.md) is $U=-(D-h)gs\sin\alpha-hgr_o\cos(s/R)$ up to a constant. Substitution into the [Euler-Lagrange equation](euler-lagrange-equation.md) proves the displayed equation. Treating the hole's [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) exactly introduces angle-dependent inertia, so this simplified equation must retain its explicit modeling assumption.
