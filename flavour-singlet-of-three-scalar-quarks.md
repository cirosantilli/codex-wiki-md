# Flavour singlet of three scalar quarks

↑ **Parent:** [Three-quark colour singlet](three-quark-colour-singlet.md)

If three [quarks](quark.md) are hypothetically spin-zero [bosons](boson.md), their total state is symmetric under particle exchange. In an $L=0$ symmetric spatial state, the unchanged antisymmetric [three-quark colour singlet](three-quark-colour-singlet.md) therefore requires an antisymmetric [quark flavour](quark-flavor.md) state. With three flavours its space is $\bigwedge^3\mathbb C^3$, of dimension one. The sole flavour state contains one quark of each flavour and has total [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) zero.
