# Geometric quantum state synthesis by adjacent rotations

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum control](quantum-control.md)

On a connected transition chain, independently selected resonant pulses realize embedded two-level rotations with tunable angle and phase. Eliminate target amplitudes from one end of the chain using complex [Givens rotations](givens-rotation.md), and invert those rotations to prepare the target from a basis state. The pulse angles come from amplitude ratios and the phases from relative complex arguments. Spectator components are unchanged by each embedded rotation. Work in the [interaction picture](interaction-picture.md) and compensate the known drift phases at the chosen final time when the target is specified in the laboratory frame.
