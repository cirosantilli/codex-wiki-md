# Word metric ball

↑ **Parent:** [Word metric](word-metric.md)

The closed [word metric ball](word-metric-ball.md) of radius $r\geq0$ centred at a [group element](group-element.md) $g$ is

$$
B_S(g,r)=\{h\in G:d_S(g,h)\leq r\}=g\{u\in G:|u|_S\leq r\}.
$$

This is a [closed ball](closed-ball.md) for the [word metric](word-metric.md). A finite [generating set of a group](generating-set-of-a-group.md) gives a finite [word metric ball](word-metric-ball.md), because at most $\sum_{k=0}^{\lfloor r\rfloor}|S|^k$ [words over an alphabet](string.md) can represent its elements. Different [words over an alphabet](string.md) may represent the same element, so this is an upper bound. [Word metric balls in the integer lattice](word-metric-balls-in-the-integer-lattice.md) give exact counts for two choices of moves.
