# Paired Poisson conditional likelihood

↑ **Parent:** [Two-group Poisson ratio conditional likelihood](two-group-poisson-ratio-conditional-likelihood.md)

For conditionally independent paired counts with means $\lambda_i$ and $\lambda_i e^\beta$, condition on every pair total $N_i=Y_{i0}+Y_{i1}$. The joint [conditional likelihood](conditional-likelihood.md) removes all pair-specific rates, leaving the displayed independent [binomial distributions](binomial-distribution.md). A zero-total pair contributes no information. With unrestricted fixed baseline rates, this conditional likelihood has the same parameter-dependent part as the ordinary [profile likelihood](profile-likelihood.md); a specified [Gamma–Poisson hierarchical model](gamma-poisson-hierarchical-model.md) can retain additional information by imposing structure on those rates.
