# Strong law for uniformly L2-bounded uncorrelated random variables

↑ **Parent:** [Strong law of large numbers](strong-law-of-large-numbers.md)

If $\sup_n\mathbb E|X_n|^2<\infty$ and the $X_n$ are pairwise [uncorrelated random variables](uncorrelated-random-variables.md), then $n^{-1}\sum_{k=1}^n(X_k-\mathbb E X_k)\to0$ almost surely. [Independence](independent-random-variables.md) is unnecessary for this version of the [strong law of large numbers](strong-law-of-large-numbers.md). A uniformly bounded [martingale difference sequence](martingale-difference-sequence.md) meets its hypotheses, since the differences have mean zero and are orthogonal in $L^2$.
