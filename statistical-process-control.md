# Statistical process control

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical inference](statistical-inference-split.md)

Statistical process control compares observed process outcomes with a stable reference model and [control limits](control-limits.md) reflecting expected sampling variation. An observation outside those limits is a signal to investigate, not proof of a specific cause. Varying sample volumes require varying limits, and [multiple testing](multiple-hypothesis-testing.md) changes the chance of a chance signal somewhere in the display.
