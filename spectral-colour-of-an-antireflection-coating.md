# Spectral colour of an antireflection coating

↑ **Parent:** [Antireflection coating](antireflection-coating.md)

A thin-film design that suppresses reflected green light more strongly than red and blue can leave a purple residual [reflection](reflection-mathematics.md). A single quarter-wave layer cancels [reflections](reflection-mathematics.md) exactly only at its design [wavelength](wavelength.md) and angle under the ideal index-matching condition. [reflection](reflection-mathematics.md) colour should be distinguished from the much smaller colour change of the transmitted beam; multilayer designs can broaden the low-reflectance band.
