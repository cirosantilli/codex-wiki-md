# Two-pion isospin decomposition

↑ **Parent:** [Isospin](isospin.md)

Two [pions](pion.md), each of [isospin](isospin.md) one, have a nine-dimensional charge-state space. [Clebsch-Gordan coefficients](clebsch-gordan-coefficients.md) decompose it into an isospin-two quintet, an isospin-one triplet and an isospin-zero singlet. Under exchange of the two particle slots, the $I=2,0$ states are symmetric and the $I=1$ states are antisymmetric. Because [pions](pion.md) have zero [spin](spin.md) and obey [bosonic exchange symmetry](bosonic-exchange-symmetry.md), the $I=1$ two-pion channel requires odd relative [orbital angular momentum](orbital-angular-momentum.md), while $I=0,2$ require even orbital angular momentum.
