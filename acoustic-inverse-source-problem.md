# Acoustic inverse source problem

↑ **Parent:** [Acoustics](acoustics.md)

Recover a compactly supported acoustic source $Q$ from measurements of its outgoing field, such as its [far-field pattern](far-field-pattern.md). The [Helmholtz equation](helmholtz-equation.md) forward problem is specified with the [Sommerfeld radiation condition](sommerfeld-radiation-condition.md). At a single frequency, the [source-to-far-field operator at fixed frequency](source-to-far-field-operator-at-fixed-frequency.md) has invisible [fixed-frequency nonradiating sources](fixed-frequency-nonradiating-source.md), and its compactness makes recovery of the visible component unstable.
