# Symmetric part determines a real quadratic functional

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic variational principle for a symmetric positive operator](quadratic-variational-principle-for-a-symmetric-positive-operator.md)

For a bounded operator on a real [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md), $\langle Lv,v\rangle=\langle Sv,v\rangle$ with $S=(L+L^*)/2$. Consequently the [Euler-Lagrange equation](euler-lagrange-equation.md) of $\langle Lv,v\rangle-2\langle f,v\rangle$ is $Sv=f$. It is $Lv=f$ only when $L$ is [self-adjoint](self-adjoint-operator.md) or when the relevant solution also annihilates the skew part. For example $L=\begin{pmatrix}1&-1\\1&1\end{pmatrix}$ has positive quadratic form $\|v\|^2$, but that form contains no information about its skew part.
