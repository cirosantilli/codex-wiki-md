# Schur averaging of rectangular matrices

↑ **Parent:** [Schur orthogonality relations](schur-orthogonality-relations.md)

For chosen inequivalent [unitary irreducible representations](unitary-irreducible-representation.md) $\rho,\sigma$ of a [finite group](finite-group.md) and a $d_\rho\times d_\sigma$ matrix $M$, the average $\mathbb E_x\rho(x)M\sigma(x)^*$ is zero when $\rho\ne\sigma$. For $\rho=\sigma$, it is $(\operatorname{tr}M/d_\rho)I$. This follows from the [Schur lemma](schur-s-lemma.md), since the average intertwines the two [group representations](group-representation.md). Equivalent representations in different bases require the corresponding [intertwiner](intertwiner.md); the identity-matrix formula assumes literally the same representative.
