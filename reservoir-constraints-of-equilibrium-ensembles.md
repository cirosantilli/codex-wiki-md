# Reservoir constraints of equilibrium ensembles

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical ensemble](statistical-ensemble.md)

An isolated [energy](energy-split.md) shell gives the [microcanonical ensemble](microcanonical-ensemble.md); a heat bath fixes $T$ for the [canonical ensemble](canonical-ensemble-split.md); a heat and particle reservoir fixes $T,\mu$ for the [grand canonical ensemble](grand-canonical-ensemble.md). Constraints determine the allowed fluctuations and which [thermodynamic potential](thermodynamic-potential-split.md) is useful.
