↑ **Parent:** [Chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copper(II)_sulfate)

In the [catalysed peroxide comparison](catalysed-peroxide-comparison.md), [copper(II) sulfate](copper-ii-sulfate.md) supplies dissolved [ions](ion.md) before addition of [sodium hydroxide](sodium-hydroxide.md) forms a solid by [precipitation](precipitation-chemistry.md). The comparison tests that solid-forming treatment rather than assuming the dissolved salt is itself an effective [catalyst](catalyst.md).
