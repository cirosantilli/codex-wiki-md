# Clausius statement of the second law

↑ **Parent:** [Second law of thermodynamics](second-law-of-thermodynamics.md)

No cyclic device can have as its sole effect the transfer of [heat](heat.md) from a colder [thermal reservoir](heat-bath.md) to a hotter [thermal reservoir](heat-bath.md). The word “sole” includes the surroundings: the device must return to its starting state, and there must be no net supplied [work](work.md), depletion of a stored resource, or accompanying change elsewhere. The statement excludes a self-running [refrigerator](refrigerator.md), while allowing an ordinary [refrigerator](refrigerator.md) or [heat pump](heat-pump.md) supplied with [work](work.md).[§5.1, statement 2 and Figure 5.2: sole-result restriction](thermodynamics-and-propulsion/5-1-concept-and-statements-of-the-second-law-why-do-we-need-a-second-law.md)

For a proposed transfer $Q>0$ from a fixed cold [thermal reservoir](heat-bath.md) at $T_c$ to a fixed hot [thermal reservoir](heat-bath.md) at $T_h>T_c>0$, with no other effect, the total [entropy](entropy.md) change would be

$$
\Delta S_{\rm tot}=\frac{Q}{T_h}-\frac{Q}{T_c}<0.
$$

The [first law of thermodynamics](first-law-of-thermodynamics.md) permits this [energy](energy.md) balance; the [Second law of thermodynamics](second-law-of-thermodynamics.md) forbids the negative total [entropy](entropy.md) change. For $Q=600\,\mathrm J$, $T_c=300\,\mathrm K$ and $T_h=600\,\mathrm K$, it is $-1\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$. Direct [heat transfer](heat-transfer.md) in the opposite direction has positive total [entropy](entropy.md) change. A powered [heat pump](heat-pump.md) has a different budget: it supplies [work](work.md) and delivers more [heat](heat.md) to the hot side than it extracts from the cold side.

A short equivalence argument links this restriction to the [Kelvin-Planck statement of the second law](kelvin-planck-statement-of-the-second-law.md). Couple a hypothetical device that moves $600\,\mathrm J$ from cold to hot with no other effect to the [heat engine](heat-engine.md) illustrated above, which absorbs $1000\,\mathrm J$, rejects $600\,\mathrm J$, and delivers $400\,\mathrm J$. The cold-side transfers cancel; the combined cyclic device takes $400\,\mathrm J$ from the hot [thermal reservoir](heat-bath.md) and delivers exactly $400\,\mathrm J$ of [work](work.md), violating the [Kelvin-Planck statement of the second law](kelvin-planck-statement-of-the-second-law.md). Matching transfers is an ideal composition argument, not a claim that such a device exists.[§I.D, printed pp. 7–8: equivalence by coupling devices](8-333-statistical-mechanics-i-statistical-mechanics-of-particles/statistical-mechanics-i-lecture-2.md)
