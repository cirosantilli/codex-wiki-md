# Stress-free convection growth-rate polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Rayleigh-Bénard convection](rayleigh-benard-convection.md)

For a fixed-temperature layer with [stress-free boundary conditions](stress-free-boundary-condition.md), a vertical [Fourier sine series](fourier-sine-series.md) has $a^2=\lambda^2+n^2\pi^2$, where $\lambda$ is horizontal [wavenumber](wavenumber.md). The coupled vertical [velocity](velocity.md) and temperature amplitudes satisfy the displayed quadratic for their [growth rate](growth-rate.md) $\sigma$. Its roots are real for positive [Rayleigh number](rayleigh-number.md), while both roots have negative real parts for negative [Rayleigh number](rayleigh-number.md). Setting $\sigma=0$ gives the [free-slip convection neutral curve](free-slip-convection-neutral-curve.md).
