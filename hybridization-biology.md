# Hybridization (biology)

↑ **Parent:** [Hybrid (biology)](hybrid-biology.md)

Crossing between genetically distinct lineages or populations. It can introduce combinations of [alleles](allele.md) into descendants and contribute to [gene flow](gene-flow.md) or [hybrid speciation](hybrid-speciation.md). This process is distinct from [DNA hybridization](dna-hybridization.md), the pairing of complementary nucleic-acid strands.
