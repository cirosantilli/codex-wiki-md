# Cyanide ligand

↑ **Parent:** [Ligand in coordination chemistry](ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md)

A bound $\mathrm{CN^-}$ ligand, usually C-bound in the strongly pi-accepting coordination mode used in elementary spectrochemical comparisons. It is an anionic two-electron donor in ionic [electron counting](electron-counting.md).
