# Cayley transform (complex analysis)

↑ **Parent:** [Möbius transformation](mobius-transformation.md)

This [Möbius transformation](mobius-transformation.md) maps the [upper half-plane](upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md) onto the [unit disk](unit-disk.md). For $\operatorname{Im}z>0$, the identity $1-|C(z)|^2=4\operatorname{Im}z/|z+i|^2$ puts the image inside the [unit disk](unit-disk.md). Its inverse is $z=i(1+w)/(1-w)$, so the map is a [conformal bijection](biholomorphism.md). On the extended real boundary it maps onto the [unit circle](complex-unit-circle.md). It is the scalar complex-analytic version of a Cayley map, rather than the matrix parametrization used in [Cayley transform](cayley-transform-lie-theory.md).
