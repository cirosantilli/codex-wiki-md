# Discretely valued field

↑ **Parent:** [Discrete valuation](discrete-valuation.md)

A discretely valued field is a field equipped with a [discrete valuation](discrete-valuation.md). It is complete when every [Cauchy sequence](cauchy-sequence.md) for the induced metric converges.
