# EIIA glucose

↑ **Parent:** [Phosphotransferase system](phosphotransferase-system.md)

The glucose-specific enzyme-IIA component of the bacterial [phosphotransferase system](phosphotransferase-system.md). Its dephosphorylated form can bind [lactose permease](lactose-permease.md), causing [inducer exclusion](inducer-exclusion.md).
