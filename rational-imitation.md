# Rational imitation

↑ **Parent:** [Imitation](imitation.md)

Selective reproduction of a demonstrated means in relation to contextual constraints and inferred goals. A behavioural difference can also admit attentional or perceptual explanations; selective copying alone is not a unique signature of explicit [belief](belief.md) reasoning.
