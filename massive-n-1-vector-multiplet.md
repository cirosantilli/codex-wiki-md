<h1 id="massive-n-1-vector-multiplet">Massive N=1 vector multiplet</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Supersymmetric vector multiplet](supersymmetric-vector-multiplet.md)

A four-dimensional massive $\mathcal N=1$ [vector multiplet](supersymmetric-vector-multiplet.md) contains one spin-one particle, two spin-one-half particles and one real spin-zero particle. Its on-shell state counts are $3+1=4$ bosonic and $2+2=4$ fermionic. These masses agree in an unbroken [supersymmetric vacuum](supersymmetric-vacuum.md). The [supersymmetric Higgs mechanism](supersymmetric-higgs-mechanism.md) assembles it from a massless [vector multiplet](supersymmetric-vector-multiplet.md) and one [chiral superfield](chiral-superfield.md) per broken gauge generator.
