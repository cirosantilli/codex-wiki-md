# Rank bound for a matrix product

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix rank](matrix-rank.md)

For composable [linear maps](linear-map.md), $\operatorname{im}(AB)=A(\operatorname{im}B)$ lies in $\operatorname{im}A$ and has [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) at most that of $\operatorname{im}B$. This proves the bound on the [rank of a matrix](matrix-rank.md) product. When $A$ is $m\times p$, $B$ is $p\times n$, and $m,n\geq p$, the bound $p$ is attained by $A=\binom{I_p}{0}$ and $B=(I_p\ 0)$.
