# Parity restriction on renormalizable pseudoscalar Yukawa counterterms

↑ **Parent:** [Pseudoscalar Yukawa interaction](pseudoscalar-yukawa-interaction.md)

For an unbroken [parity symmetry in quantum field theory](parity-symmetry-in-quantum-field-theory.md) with $\phi$ a [pseudoscalar](pseudoscalar.md), pure-scalar local [counterterms](counterterm.md) of [mass dimension](mass-dimension.md) at most four are even in $\phi$. A four-vertex [fermion loop](fermion-loop.md) requires a quartic [counterterm](counterterm.md), even when the initial [Lagrangian density](lagrangian-density.md) shows only a [Yukawa interaction](yukawa-interaction.md). The one-point and three-point pure-boson vertices vanish at all orders: their available external [four-momenta](four-momentum.md) cannot form a parity-odd [Lorentz scalar](lorentz-scalar.md).
