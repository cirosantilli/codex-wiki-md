# Mutator allele

↑ **Parent:** [Mutation rate](mutation-rate.md)

An [allele](allele.md) that increases the rate of [mutations](mutation.md). In an asexual lineage it can hitchhike with a beneficial change, despite also increasing the production of harmful changes; [genetic recombination](genetic-recombination.md) can weaken that association.
