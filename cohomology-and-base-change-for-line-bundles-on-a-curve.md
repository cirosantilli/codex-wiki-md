# Cohomology and base change for line bundles on a curve

↑ **Parent:** [Finite complex computing cohomology in a proper flat family](finite-complex-computing-cohomology-in-a-proper-flat-family.md)

In a proper flat family of [algebraic curves](algebraic-curve.md), if the first [sheaf cohomology](sheaf-cohomology.md) of a [line bundle](line-bundle.md) vanishes on every fibre of an open set, its direct image there is a [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) of rank given by the [Riemann-Roch theorem](riemann-roch-theorem.md) and commutes with arbitrary [base change](base-change-of-a-morphism-of-schemes.md). Locally a finite free complex computes both fibre [sheaf cohomology](sheaf-cohomology.md) groups. Vanishing of the last cokernel makes its last map surjective; splitting that map leaves a finite locally free kernel in degree zero. This is the mechanism behind charts of the [Picard scheme of a curve](picard-scheme-of-a-curve.md) and evaluation descriptions of the [theta divisor](theta-divisor.md).
