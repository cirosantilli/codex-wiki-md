# Representativeness heuristic

↑ **Parent:** [Cognitive bias](cognitive-bias.md)

Judging [probability](probability.md) through resemblance to a typical case or generating process. Resemblance need not incorporate prevalence or evidence reliability, so it can yield plausible-seeming but poorly calibrated [probability](probability.md) judgments.
