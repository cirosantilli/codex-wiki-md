A [physicist](physicist-split.md).[Independent biographical identity and professional specialization](https://actorenregister.nationaalarchief.nl/actor-persoon/kamerlingh-onnes-prof-h-te-leiden)

[Helium liquefaction](helium-liquefaction.md) supplied a route to very low [temperatures](temperature-split.md). The [resistance-versus-temperature experiment for superconductivity](resistance-versus-temperature-experiment-for-superconductivity.md) and measurements of [superconducting persistent current](persistent-current-superconductivity.md) show how reaching those conditions opened new questions about electrical behavior.
