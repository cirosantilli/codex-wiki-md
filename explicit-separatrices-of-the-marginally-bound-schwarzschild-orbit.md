# Explicit separatrices of the marginally bound Schwarzschild orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Schwarzschild marginally bound capture](schwarzschild-marginally-bound-capture.md)

A timelike equatorial Schwarzschild [geodesic](geodesic.md) with specific [energy](energy-split.md) $E=1$ and [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) $J=4m$ obeys $(du/d\phi)^2=2u(u-1/4)^2$ for $u=m/r$. Substituting $q=2\sqrt u$ reduces it to $(dq/d\phi)^2=(q^2-1)^2/8$, integrating to the two displayed branches. One lies outside $r=4m$ and the other inside; each approaches the same unstable [circular orbit](circular-orbit.md) exponentially in angle and only after infinite [proper time](proper-time.md). Linearization of $u''=1/16-u+3u^2$ about $u=1/4$ gives $\delta u''=\delta u/2$, confirming instability. These are [separatrix](separatrix.md) approaches or departures, not stable oscillations around the [circular orbit](circular-orbit.md).
