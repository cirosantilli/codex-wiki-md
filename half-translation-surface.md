# Half-translation surface

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic differential](quadratic-differential.md)

A surface with local Euclidean charts whose changes of coordinate are $w\mapsto\pm w+b$, outside finitely many cone points. A nonzero [holomorphic quadratic differential](holomorphic-quadratic-differential.md) defines such a surface; a zero of order $m$ has [cone angle](cone-angle.md) $(m+2)\pi$.
