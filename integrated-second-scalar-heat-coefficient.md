# Integrated second scalar heat coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Heat invariants](heat-invariants.md)

For the positive scalar [Laplace-Beltrami operator](laplace-beltrami-operator.md) on a [closed manifold](closed-manifold.md) with a [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md), the coefficient of $t^2$ in the normalized [heat trace](heat-trace.md) is the displayed integral. The local coefficient additionally has a scalar-curvature divergence term whose integral vanishes. In dimension two, $R=2K$, $|\operatorname{Ric}|^2=2K^2$, $|\operatorname{Rm}|^2=4K^2$, giving $a_2=\frac1{15}\int_MK^2\,dA$. Together with volume and total [Gaussian curvature](gaussian-curvature.md), it determines the variance of curvature and detects constant curvature.
