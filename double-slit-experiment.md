# Double-slit experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Interference (wave propagation)](interference-wave-propagation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Double-slit_experiment)

The [double-slit experiment](double-slit-experiment.md) compares propagation through two openings. Coherent illumination gives [double-slit interference](double-slit-interference.md); independent incoherent source patches contribute [light intensities](light-intensity.md) whose displaced patterns can reduce the observed [interferometric visibility](interferometric-visibility.md).
