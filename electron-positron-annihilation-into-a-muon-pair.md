# Electron-positron annihilation into a muon pair

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum electrodynamics](quantum-electrodynamics.md)

At tree level, the [Feynman diagram](feynman-diagram.md) has an electron-positron current and a muon-antimuon current joined by one virtual [photon](photon.md). In the massless limit, the [spin average](spin-average.md) and final-state [spin sum](spin-sum.md) give $\overline{|\mathcal M|^2}=e^4(1+\cos^2\theta)$, and the integrated [relativistic scattering cross-section](relativistic-scattering-cross-section.md) is $4\pi\alpha^2/(3s)$.
