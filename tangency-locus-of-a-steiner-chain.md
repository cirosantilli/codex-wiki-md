# Tangency locus of a Steiner chain

↑ **Parent:** [Steiner chain](steiner-chain.md)

For a [Steiner chain](steiner-chain.md) between concentric [circles](circle.md), tangencies of neighbouring [circles](circle.md) are midpoints of their centres and lie on the [circle](circle.md) of radius $\sqrt{R\rho}$. Under a [Möbius transformation](mobius-transformation.md), their locus is a [generalized circle](generalized-circle-under-a-mobius-transformation.md), which may be a line. An ordinary-[circle](circle.md) conclusion requires the pole of the inverse transformation to avoid the original locus.
