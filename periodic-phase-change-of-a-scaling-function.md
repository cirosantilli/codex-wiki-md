# Periodic phase change of a scaling function

↑ **Parent:** [Scaling function](scaling-function.md)

If a periodic unimodular [function](function-split.md) $a$ and its inverse have absolutely summable [Fourier coefficients](fourier-coefficient.md), replacing $\widehat\varphi_0$ by $\widehat\varphi=a\widehat\varphi_0$ preserves the [orthonormal](orthonormal-set.md) integer-translate span and integrability of an integrable [scaling function](scaling-function.md). In physical space this is an absolutely summable combination of [integer](integer.md) [function translations](translation-of-a-function.md). The new [MRA low-pass filter](low-pass-filter-of-a-multiresolution-analysis.md) is $m(t)=a(2t)m_0(t)/a(t)$. A rough phase can therefore preserve the [multiresolution analysis](multiresolution-analysis.md) while changing [MRA low-pass filter](low-pass-filter-of-a-multiresolution-analysis.md) regularity.
