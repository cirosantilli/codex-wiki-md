# Crista

↑ **Parent:** [Inner mitochondrial membrane](inner-mitochondrial-membrane.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crista)

A [crista](crista.md) is a fold of the [inner mitochondrial membrane](inner-mitochondrial-membrane.md) into the [mitochondrial matrix](mitochondrial-matrix.md). Its membrane remains continuous with the surrounding [inner mitochondrial membrane](inner-mitochondrial-membrane.md); it is not a separately inserted sac like a [thylakoid](thylakoid.md).[Chapter 12, The Transport of Proteins into Mitochondria and Chloroplasts, Figure 12-22: continuity of cristae and contrast with thylakoids](molecular-biology-of-the-cell-4th-edition-by-bruce-alberts-and-others.md)

The diagram idealizes access through the fold's opening. Connectivity alone does not imply instant mixing or identical [concentrations](concentration.md) throughout the [intermembrane space](intermembrane-space.md). Narrow geometry and molecular interactions can matter to transport. Increasing [surface area](surface-area.md) also does not, by itself, prove a proportional increase in [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) production: the supply of [substrates](substrate-biochemistry.md), available [oxygen](oxygen.md) and the state of the coupling machinery must also support the flux.
