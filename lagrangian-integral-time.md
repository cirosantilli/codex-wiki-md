# Lagrangian integral time

↑ **Parent:** [Taylor turbulent dispersion](taylor-turbulent-dispersion.md)

The Lagrangian integral time is the area under a normalized velocity [autocorrelation](autocorrelation.md) along a [Lagrangian trajectory](lagrangian-trajectory.md). If this correlation is integrable, Taylor turbulent dispersion has asymptotic mean-square displacement $2R_L(0)t_Lt$. The integral time need not equal every short-time decorrelation scale, particularly when the autocorrelation oscillates.
