# Recursive process

↑ **Parent:** [Recursion (computer science)](recursion-computer-science.md)

A computation that retains pending operations while further calls run, then completes those operations as results return. A [recursive procedure](recursive-procedure.md) can instead describe an iterative process: passing an updated accumulator onward may leave no arithmetic pending. Avoiding growing [call stack](call-stack.md) storage in that case depends on the implementation. A fixed number of [variables](variable-mathematics.md) also does not imply fixed storage in [bits](bit.md) when their [integer](integer-split.md) values grow.[Structure and Interpretation of Computer Programs, second edition, §1.2.1: deferred operations, state variables, and implementation dependence of space use; bit-storage qualification added here](structure-and-interpretation-of-computer-programs-by-harold-abelson-and-others.md)
