# RNS spin field

↑ **Parent:** [Ramond sector](ramond-sector.md)

A worldsheet operator that changes fermion boundary conditions to the [Ramond sector](ramond-sector.md) and creates a target spinor state. For $D$ real matter fermions its conformal weight is $D/16$. At $D=10$, combining it with the appropriate superghost factor gives a dimension-one spacetime-supersymmetry current.
