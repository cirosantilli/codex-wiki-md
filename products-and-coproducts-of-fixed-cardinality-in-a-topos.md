# Products and coproducts of fixed cardinality in a topos

↑ **Parent:** [Power-object monadicity](power-object-monadicity.md)

For an [elementary topos](elementary-topos.md), existence of [products in a category](product-category-theory.md) indexed by a fixed set is equivalent to existence of the corresponding [coproducts in a category](coproduct.md). The [power-object monadicity](power-object-monadicity.md) equivalence $\mathcal E^{\mathrm{op}}\simeq\mathcal E^{PP}$ gives the forward direction because [Eilenberg-Moore categories](eilenberg-moore-category.md) create limits. For the converse, the diagonal into the pointwise product category is a [logical functor](logical-functor.md). A coproduct is its [left adjoint](adjoint-functors.md); the [adjoint lifting theorem for monad algebra functors](adjoint-lifting-theorem-for-monad-algebra-functors.md), applied through power-object monadicity, then gives its [right adjoint](adjoint-functors.md), namely the product. The required reflexive coequalizers on the algebra side are opposites of finite equalizers in the topos.
