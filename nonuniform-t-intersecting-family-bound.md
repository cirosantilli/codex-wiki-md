# Nonuniform t-intersecting family bound

↑ **Parent:** [T-intersecting family](t-intersecting-family.md)

If $|A\cap B|\geq t$, then $|A\triangle B|=|A\cup B|-|A\cap B|\leq n-t$. The [Kleitman diametric theorem](kleitman-diametric-theorem.md) therefore gives the sharp nonuniform intersection bound. For $n-t=2q$, all sets of size at least $n-q$ attain it. For $n-t=2q+1$, take all sets of size at least $n-q$, together with all $(n-q-1)$-sets avoiding one fixed point.
