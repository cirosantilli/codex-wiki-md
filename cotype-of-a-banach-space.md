# Cotype of a Banach space

↑ **Parent:** [Banach space](banach-space-split.md)

A [Banach space](banach-space-split.md) $X$ has cotype $q$, $2\le q<\infty$, if a constant $C$ independent of the number of vectors satisfies $(\sum_j\|x_j\|^q)^{1/q}\le C(\mathbb E\|\sum_j\varepsilon_jx_j\|^2)^{1/2}$ for independent symmetric [Rademacher random variables](rademacher-distribution.md). It prevents many large vectors from having a small random sum.
