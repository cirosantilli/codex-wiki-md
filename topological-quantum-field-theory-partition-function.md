# Topological quantum field theory partition function

↑ **Parent:** [Topological quantum field theory](topological-quantum-field-theory.md)

The scalar assigned to a closed spacetime [manifold](topological-manifold-split.md) by a [topological quantum field theory](topological-quantum-field-theory.md). Gluing the two ends of a cylinder over a spatial [manifold](topological-manifold-split.md) $\Sigma$ gives $Z(\Sigma\times S^1)=\operatorname{tr}(1_{V(\Sigma)})=\dim V(\Sigma)$. This is the [ground-state degeneracy](ground-state-degeneracy.md) when the state space describes the topological ground sector of a gapped system.
