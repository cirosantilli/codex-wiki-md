# Black-body radiation

↑ **Parent:** [Planck's law](planck-s-law.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black-body_radiation)

Blackbody radiation is a thermal equilibrium gas of [photons](photon.md). In units with $k_B=1$, its energy density is

$$
u(T)=\frac{\pi^2T^4}{15\hbar^3c^3},
$$

and its isotropic [radiation pressure](radiation-pressure.md) is $p=u/3$.
