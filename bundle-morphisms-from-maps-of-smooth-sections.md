# Bundle morphisms from maps of smooth sections

↑ **Parent:** [Vector bundle morphism](vector-bundle-morphism.md)

Every $C^\infty(M)$-linear map between the [modules of smooth sections](module-of-smooth-sections.md) of finite-rank smooth [vector bundles](vector-bundle.md) is induced by a unique [vector bundle morphism](vector-bundle-morphism.md). A [smooth bump function](smooth-bump-function.md) shows that the map is local. A bumped local frame then proves that a section vanishing at $x$ has image vanishing at $x$. Evaluating the image therefore defines a well-defined fiberwise [linear map](linear-map.md). The images of the bumped frame give smooth matrix columns, proving the resulting morphism is smooth. This elementary argument is also described in [Brian Conrad's bundle notes](https://math.stanford.edu/~conrad/diffgeomPage/handouts/bundle.pdf).
