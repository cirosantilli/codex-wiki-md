# Momentum correction for lateral mass addition

↑ **Parent:** [Shallow water equations](shallow-water-equations.md)

In a sectionally uniform flow, a volume source $Q_x$ per downstream length brings axial velocity $u_a$. [Momentum conservation](momentum-conservation.md) gives the displayed source correction, with $A$ the wetted area. The head derivative is $[h+H+u^2/(2g)]_x=Q_x(u_a-u)/(gA)$. Removing fluid at the local velocity preserves the head in this approximation. Adding fluid at rest does not: its acceleration reduces that head. The volume source alone cannot specify the momentum exchange without a velocity assumption.
