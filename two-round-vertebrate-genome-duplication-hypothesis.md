# Two-round vertebrate genome duplication hypothesis

↑ **Parent:** [Whole-genome duplication](whole-genome-duplication.md)

The hypothesis that two early large-scale duplication rounds contributed to the multiple paralogous genomic regions of jawed [vertebrates](vertebrate.md). [Hox clusters](hox-cluster.md) are one informative set of loci; a single family does not by itself establish [genome](genome.md)-wide duplication.
