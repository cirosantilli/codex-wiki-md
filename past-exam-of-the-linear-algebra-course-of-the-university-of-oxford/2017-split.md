# 2017

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the linear algebra course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Source paper: [earlier Computer Science release](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/ba_computer_science_past_papers/response/2577745/attach/2/202402%20183%20CS%20Papers.zip?cookie_passthrough=1), member `2016/T/a15293w1.pdf`. Its cover dates this examination to 14 June 2017, but its question pages are damaged. The complete three-page counterpart is in [Mathematics Part A release](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/maths_papers_2/response/1850333/attach/3/FOI%2020210708%201%20Data.zip?cookie_passthrough=1), member `FOI-20210708-1 - Data/a15293w1 (2).pdf`.

All seventeen source subparts are answered below. Part 1(b)(iii) needs a [separable polynomial](../separable-polynomial.md) hypothesis over a general [field](../field.md); its intended case, a counterexample to the unrestricted claim, and the corrected general result are distinguished in that solution. This paper uses the [characteristic polynomial](../characteristic-polynomial.md) convention $\chi_T(x)=\det(T-xI)$, which differs by $(-1)^{\dim V}$ from the monic convention.

The department's [solution-release policy](https://www.maths.ox.ac.uk/members/students/undergraduate-courses/examinations-assessments/overview-examinations) covers even examination years and the exceptional release for 2019; it does not advertise 2017 solutions. The exact-paper check found examiner comments, but no advertised worked answers requiring any subpart to be skipped.
