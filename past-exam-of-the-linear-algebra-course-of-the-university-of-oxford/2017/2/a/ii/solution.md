↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [dual map](../../../../../transpose-of-a-linear-map.md) acts by [function composition](../../../../../function-composition.md):

$$
\boxed{T':W'\longrightarrow V',\qquad T'(\phi)=\phi\circ T.}
$$

The composition is a [linear functional](../../../../../linear-functional.md) on $V$, and pointwise operations give $T'(a\phi+b\psi)=aT'\phi+bT'\psi$. Thus this really is a [linear transformation](../../../../../linear-map.md); its direction reverses that of $T$.
