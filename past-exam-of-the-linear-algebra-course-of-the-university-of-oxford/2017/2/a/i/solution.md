The [dual space](../../../../../dual-space-split.md) is

$$
V'=\operatorname{Hom}_F(V,F),
$$

the [vector space](../../../../../vector-space-split.md) of [linear functionals](../../../../../linear-functional.md), with pointwise operations

$$
(\phi+\psi)(v)=\phi(v)+\psi(v),\qquad
(a\phi)(v)=a\phi(v).
$$

Given the [basis](../../../../../basis.md) $e_1,\ldots,e_n$, its [dual basis](../../../../../dual-basis.md) consists of the coordinate [linear functionals](../../../../../linear-functional.md)

$$
\boxed{f_i\!\left(\sum_{j=1}^na_je_j\right)=a_i,
\qquad f_i(e_j)=\delta_{ij}.}
$$

In particular, $\dim V'=\dim V=n$.
