<h1 id="3/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

If $f\in V_n\setminus V_{n-1}$, write $f(x)=a_nx^n+\cdots$ with $a_n\ne0$. In the expression $Tf=f''-2xf'-2f$, the first term has [polynomial degree](../../../../../degree-of-a-polynomial.md) at most $n-2$, while the other two contribute

$$
-2na_nx^n-2a_nx^n=-2(n+1)a_nx^n.
$$

Thus, if $Tf=\lambda f$, comparison of the leading coefficients gives

$$
\boxed{\lambda=-2(n+1).}
$$

For $n=0$ take $V_{-1}=\{0\}$; the same result follows from $T1=-2$. This identifies the [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) conditional on the existence of the [eigenvector](../../../../../eigenvector.md); existence is established next.
