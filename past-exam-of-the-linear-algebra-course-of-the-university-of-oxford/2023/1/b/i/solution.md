The [dual space](../../../../../dual-space-split.md) is the [vector space](../../../../../vector-space-split.md) of all [linear functionals](../../../../../linear-functional.md) on $V$:

$$
\boxed{V'=\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb C}(V,\mathbb C)}.
$$

Addition and [scalar multiplication](../../../../../scalar-multiplication.md) are defined pointwise. The [dual map](../../../../../transpose-of-a-linear-map.md) sends an output measurement back along $T$:

$$
\boxed{T':V'\to V',\qquad T'(\varphi)=\varphi\circ T,\qquad (T'\varphi)(v)=\varphi(Tv)}.
$$

The [function composition](../../../../../function-composition.md) of these [linear maps](../../../../../linear-map.md) is linear, and $T'$ is linear in $\varphi$. This is the algebraic [dual map](../../../../../transpose-of-a-linear-map.md), so its definition involves no [complex conjugation](../../../../../complex-conjugation.md) or [inner product](../../../../../inner-product-split.md).
