<h1 id="1/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [iii](../iii.md)

Suppose $U$ is an [invariant subspace](../../../../../invariant-subspace.md) for $T$. For $\varphi\in U^0$ and $u\in U$, the [dual map](../../../../../transpose-of-a-linear-map.md) satisfies

$$
(T'\varphi)(u)=\varphi(Tu)=0,
$$

because $Tu\in U$ and the [annihilator of a vector subspace](../../../../../annihilator-of-a-vector-subspace.md) consists of the [linear functionals](../../../../../linear-functional.md) vanishing there. Thus

$$
\boxed{T'(U^0)\subseteq U^0}.
$$

This is the correspondence between [invariant subspaces and annihilators](../../../../../invariant-subspaces-and-annihilators.md).
