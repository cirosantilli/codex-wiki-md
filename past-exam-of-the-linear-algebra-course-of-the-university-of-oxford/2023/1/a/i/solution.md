↑ **Parent:** [i](../i.md)

The [primary decomposition theorem for linear maps](../../../../../primary-decomposition-theorem-for-linear-maps.md) starts with a [linear map](../../../../../linear-map.md) $T:V\to V$ on a [finite-dimensional vector space](../../../../../finite-dimensional-vector-space.md) over a [field](../../../../../field.md) $F$. Factor its [minimal polynomial](../../../../../minimal-polynomial.md) as

$$
m_T(x)=\prod_{j=1}^r p_j(x)^{a_j},
$$

where the $p_j$ are distinct [monic polynomials](../../../../../monic-polynomial.md) that are [irreducible polynomials](../../../../../irreducible-polynomial.md) and $a_j\geq1$. Define $V_j=\ker p_j(T)^{a_j}$. Then

$$
\boxed{V=V_1\oplus\cdots\oplus V_r,\qquad T(V_j)\subseteq V_j,\qquad m_{T|_{V_j}}=p_j^{a_j}}.
$$

Thus the [direct sum](../../../../../direct-sum.md) separates the [irreducible polynomial](../../../../../irreducible-polynomial.md) factors into uniquely determined [invariant subspaces](../../../../../invariant-subspace.md). The statement works even when the [minimal polynomial](../../../../../minimal-polynomial.md) does not split into [linear polynomials](../../../../../linear-polynomial.md) over $F$.
