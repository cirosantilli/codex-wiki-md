<h1 id="3/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [ii](../ii.md)

If $R$ and $S$ both satisfy the defining identity of an [adjoint operator](../../../../../adjoint-operator.md), then

$$
\langle u,(R-S)w\rangle=\langle Tu,w\rangle-\langle Tu,w\rangle=0
$$

for every $u,w\in V$. Choosing $u=(R-S)w$ gives $\|(R-S)w\|^2=0$. The [positive definiteness](../../../../../positive-definiteness.md) of the [inner product](../../../../../inner-product-split.md) forces $(R-S)w=0$ for every $w$, proving

$$
\boxed{R=S}.
$$
