↑ **Parent:** [i](../i.md)

An [adjoint operator](../../../../../adjoint-operator.md) of $T:V\to V$ is a [linear map](../../../../../linear-map.md) $T^*:V\to V$ satisfying

$$
\boxed{\langle Tu,w\rangle=\langle u,T^*w\rangle\qquad\text{for all }u,w\in V}.
$$

This identity transfers $T$ from the first input of the [inner product](../../../../../inner-product-split.md) to the second.
