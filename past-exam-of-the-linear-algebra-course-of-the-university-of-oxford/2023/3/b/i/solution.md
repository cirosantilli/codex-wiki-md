<h1 id="3/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [i](../i.md)

For the [normal operator](../../../../../normal-operator.md) $T$, the [adjoint operator](../../../../../adjoint-operator.md) identity and $T^*T=TT^*$ give

$$
\|Tv\|^2=\langle v,T^*Tv\rangle
=\langle v,TT^*v\rangle
=\|T^*v\|^2.
$$

For the last equality, $\langle TT^*v,v\rangle=\langle T^*v,T^*v\rangle$ and [complex conjugation](../../../../../complex-conjugation.md) allow reversal of the two arguments. Taking the nonnegative square roots proves

$$
\boxed{\|Tv\|=\|T^*v\|\quad(v\in V)}.
$$

In particular the two [kernels](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) coincide.
