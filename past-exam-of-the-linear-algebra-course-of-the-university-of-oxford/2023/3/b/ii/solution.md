<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [ii](../ii.md)

Let $w=Tv$. If $T^2v=0$, then $Tw=0$, and part (i) gives $\|T^*w\|=\|Tw\|=0$. Thus $T^*w=0$ and

$$
\|w\|^2=\langle Tv,w\rangle=\langle v,T^*w\rangle=0.
$$

The [positive definiteness](../../../../../positive-definiteness.md) of the [inner product](../../../../../inner-product-split.md) gives $w=0$. Since the reverse [kernel](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) inclusion always holds, we obtain

$$
\boxed{T^2v=0\Longrightarrow Tv=0,\qquad \ker T^2=\ker T}.
$$

This is [kernel stabilization for a normal operator](../../../../../kernel-stabilization-for-a-normal-operator.md).
