<h1 id="3/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [i](../i.md)

Take $N=T-\lambda I$. It is a [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) in the [normal operator](../../../../../normal-operator.md) $T$, so part (b)(iii) makes $N$ a [normal operator](../../../../../normal-operator.md). Also $N^*=T^*-\bar\lambda I$. If $Tv=\lambda v$, part (b)(i), applied to $N$, gives

$$
\|(T^*-\bar\lambda I)v\|=\|N^*v\|=\|Nv\|=0.
$$

The [norm](../../../../../norm.md) vanishes only at the zero [vector](../../../../../vector.md), so

$$
\boxed{T^*v=\bar\lambda v}.
$$

This is the [adjoint eigenvector identity for a normal matrix](../../../../../adjoint-eigenvector-identity-for-a-normal-matrix.md) expressed independently of [coordinates](../../../../../coordinate-linear-algebra.md).
