<h1 id="3/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [ii](../ii.md)

Let $u\in\ker(T-\lambda I)$ and $w\in\ker(T-\mu I)$, with $\lambda\ne\mu$. By part (i) and the [adjoint operator](../../../../../adjoint-operator.md) identity,

$$
\lambda\langle u,w\rangle=\langle Tu,w\rangle
=\langle u,T^*w\rangle
=\langle u,\bar\mu w\rangle
=\mu\langle u,w\rangle.
$$

The last step uses conjugate-linearity in the second argument of the [inner product](../../../../../inner-product-split.md). Therefore $(\lambda-\mu)\langle u,w\rangle=0$, proving the [orthogonality](../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) of the two [eigenspaces](../../../../../eigenspace.md):

$$
\boxed{\ker(T-\lambda I)\perp\ker(T-\mu I)\quad(\lambda\ne\mu)}.
$$
