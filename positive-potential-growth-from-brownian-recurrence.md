# Positive-potential growth from Brownian recurrence

↑ **Parent:** [Feynman-Kac formula with a bounded potential](feynman-kac-formula-with-a-bounded-potential.md)

Suppose bounded $V\geq0$ is bounded below by a positive constant on some nonempty open interval, and $f\geq C>0$. The [Feynman-Kac formula with a bounded potential](feynman-kac-formula-with-a-bounded-potential.md) gives $u(t,x)\geq C\mathbb E_x e^{\int_0^tV(B_s)ds}$. [Infinite occupation time of one-dimensional Brownian motion](infinite-occupation-time-of-one-dimensional-brownian-motion.md) makes that integral tend to infinity [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md). The [monotone convergence theorem](monotone-convergence-theorem.md) then gives $u(t,x)\to\infty$ for every fixed $x$. Thus no globally bounded [classical solution](classical-solution.md) exists under these hypotheses, although boundedness on every finite time slab is compatible with the conclusion.
