# Parallel metrics with a common Levi-Civita connection

↑ **Parent:** [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md)

Two [Riemannian metrics](riemannian-metric.md) have the same [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md) precisely when their relative positive [self-adjoint](self-adjoint-operator.md) [endomorphism](endomorphism.md) $A$ is parallel for that connection. [Parallel transport](parallel-transport.md) preserves both metrics. Consequently agreement at one point implies agreement everywhere on a [connected](connected-space.md) manifold. Without that agreement, constant scalar multiples always give examples; constant nonproportional Euclidean forms show that a common connection does not force scalar proportionality.
