# Michelson stellar interferometer

↑ **Parent:** [Astronomical interferometer](astronomical-interferometer.md)

The [Michelson stellar interferometer](michelson-stellar-interferometer.md), associated with [Albert Abraham Michelson](albert-abraham-michelson.md), uses separated collectors and beam-redirection optics to produce [interference fringes](interference-fringe.md). The collector spacing determines sensitivity to a stellar [angular diameter](angular-diameter.md), independently of the final beam spacing at the combiner. The first null for a uniform disk is $D=1.21967\lambda/\alpha$, where $\alpha$ is the full angular diameter. Different surface-brightness laws, including [limb darkening](limb-darkening.md), change the null.
