# Polynomial equation

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial](polynomial-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polynomial_equation)

A polynomial equation has the form $p(x_1,\ldots,x_n)=0$ for a [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) $p$. Its solutions are the points at which that polynomial vanishes.
