# Principal q-character of the G2 adjoint representation

↑ **Parent:** [Q-character of a highest-weight representation](q-character-of-a-highest-weight-representation.md)

Use $\operatorname{ch}_q V=\operatorname{tr}_V q^{2\rho^\vee}$. The positive [roots of a root system](root-of-a-root-system.md) of the [G2 root system](g2-root-system.md) have simple-root heights $1,1,2,3,4,5$. Each root space has [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) one, and the zero [weight space](weight-space.md) has dimension two. Consequently

$$
\operatorname{ch}_q\mathfrak g=q^{10}+q^8+q^6+q^4+2q^2+2+2q^{-2}+q^{-4}+q^{-6}+q^{-8}+q^{-10}.
$$

By the [classification of finite-dimensional sl2 representations](classification-of-finite-dimensional-sl2-representations.md), restriction to the [Principal sl2 subalgebra](principal-sl2-subalgebra.md) is $L_{10}\oplus L_2$, of dimensions eleven and three. If the grading uses $\rho^\vee$ instead, divide every exponent by two.
