# Sunada local isometry lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Nowhere locally homogeneous metric](nowhere-locally-homogeneous-metric.md)

On a compact smooth manifold without boundary of dimension at least two, a [residual set](residual-set.md) of smooth [Riemannian metrics](riemannian-metric.md) consists of [nowhere locally homogeneous metrics](nowhere-locally-homogeneous-metric.md). Thus such metrics are dense in the smooth topology by the [Baire category theorem](baire-category-theorem.md). In dimension one, arclength coordinates always provide nontrivial [local isometries](local-isometry.md).
