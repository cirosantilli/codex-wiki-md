# Hom-set

↑ **Parent:** [Locally small category](locally-small-category.md)

In a [locally small category](locally-small-category.md), the morphisms from $A$ to $B$ form the [set](set-split.md) $\mathcal C(A,B)$. Postcomposition and precomposition give the corresponding covariant and contravariant [representable functors](representable-functor.md).
