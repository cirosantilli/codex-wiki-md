# Tychonic system

↑ **Parent:** [Geocentric model](geocentric-model.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tychonic_system)

A geoheliocentric arrangement with the [Sun](sun.md) circling a stationary [Earth](earth.md) and other planets circling the [Sun](sun.md). It can reproduce the [phases of Venus](phases-of-venus.md) without an orbiting [Earth](earth.md).
