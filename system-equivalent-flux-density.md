# System-equivalent flux density

↑ **Parent:** [System temperature](system-temperature.md)

The [system-equivalent flux density](system-equivalent-flux-density.md) is the unpolarized [spectral flux density](spectral-flux-density.md) that would produce an [antenna](antenna-radio.md) [temperature](temperature-split.md) equal to $T_{\rm sys}$. With effective collecting area $A_e$, $\mathrm{SEFD}=2k_BT_{\rm sys}/A_e$. The factor two follows from one [polarization of waves](polarization-waves.md) measurement of an unpolarized source. State effective area and [polarization of waves](polarization-waves.md) conventions when comparing sensitivities.[slides 5–7, antenna performance and SEFD](sensitivity-by-joan-wrobel.md)
