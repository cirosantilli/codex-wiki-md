# Diagonal class of complex projective space

↑ **Parent:** [Cohomology ring of complex projective space](cohomology-ring-of-complex-projective-space.md)

Give [Complex projective space](complex-projective-space.md) its complex [orientation](orientation-of-a-simplex.md). In the [homology cross product](homology-cross-product.md) basis of $H_{2n}(\mathbb{CP}^n\times\mathbb{CP}^n;\mathbb Z)$, the [diagonal](diagonal-subset.md) has the displayed class. If $a,b$ are the two normalized degree-two [hyperplane classes](hyperplane-class.md), its [Poincare dual](poincare-dual.md) is $\sum_{j=0}^na^jb^{n-j}$. Indeed every degree-$2n$ monomial $a^rb^{n-r}$ restricts on the [diagonal](diagonal-subset.md) to the top power of the hyperplane class, whose evaluation on the [fundamental class](fundamental-class.md) is one. These evaluations determine all coefficients.
