# Borel construction

↑ **Parent:** [Equivariant cohomology](equivariant-cohomology.md)

For a [group action](group-action.md) on $X$ and a contractible free $G$-space $EG$, the diagonal orbit space $EG\times_GX$ maps to the [classifying space](classifying-space.md) $BG$ with fibre $X$. It defines [equivariant cohomology](equivariant-cohomology.md). When $X\to X/G$ is a principal $G$-bundle, the map $EG\times_GX\to X/G$ has contractible fibre $EG$ and gives a [homotopy equivalence](homotopy-equivalence.md) for spaces of CW type. Freeness without the bundle hypotheses should not be substituted for this assertion for arbitrary topological groups.
