# Nontrivial center of a finite p-group

↑ **Parent:** [Finite p-group](finite-p-group.md)

Every nontrivial [finite p-group](finite-p-group.md) has a nontrivial [center](center-of-a-group.md). The conjugation action partitions the group into conjugacy classes whose noncentral sizes are positive powers of $p$; the class equation then implies $|Z(G)|\equiv|G|\equiv0\pmod p$.
