# Fourier transform has proper dense image from L1 to C0

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md)

The [Riemann-Lebesgue lemma](riemann-lebesgue-lemma.md) and [Fourier inversion](fourier-inversion-theorem.md) give a bounded injective map $L^1(\mathbb R)\to C_0(\mathbb R)$ with dense image. It is not onto: inverse transforms of $\chi_{[-n,n]}*\chi_{[-1,1]}$ have unbounded $L^1$ norms while the targets have [uniform norm](supremum-norm.md) $2$, contradicting the bounded inverse that the [open mapping theorem](open-mapping-theorem-functional-analysis.md) would give.
