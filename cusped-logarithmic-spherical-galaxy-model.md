# Cusped logarithmic spherical galaxy model

↑ **Parent:** [Galaxy dynamics](galaxy-dynamics.md)

This spherical [gravitational potential](newtonian-potential-of-a-point-mass.md), with $a,v_0>0$, has density $\rho=v_0^2(2a+r)/(4\pi Gr(a+r)^2)$ and [circular speed](circular-speed.md) $v_c=v_0\sqrt{r/(a+r)}$. It combines a central $1/r$ density cusp with an outer [flat galaxy rotation curve](flat-galaxy-rotation-curve.md), but its total mass is infinite. It differs from a logarithmic potential with a constant-density core.
