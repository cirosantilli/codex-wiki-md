# Petridis minimal-growth lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Plünnecke-Ruzsa inequality](plunnecke-ruzsa-inequality.md)

If a nonempty finite set $X$ minimizes $|X+B|/|X|$ among the nonempty subsets of $A$, with minimum $K'$, then

$$
|X+B+C|\leq K'|X+C|
$$

for every finite set $C$. Iteration is a short proof of the [Plünnecke-Ruzsa inequality](plunnecke-ruzsa-inequality.md).
