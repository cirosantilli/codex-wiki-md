# Skeletal category

↑ **Parent:** [Category](category-split.md)

A skeletal [category](category-split.md) has no distinct isomorphic objects. A [full and faithful functor](full-and-faithful-functor.md) with [essential surjectivity](essential-surjectivity.md) between two skeletal categories is an [isomorphism of categories](isomorphism-of-categories.md): surjectivity on isomorphism classes becomes surjectivity on objects, and faithfulness and fullness reflect an equality of image objects to an isomorphism, hence an equality, of source objects.
