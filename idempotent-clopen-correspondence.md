<h1 id="idempotent-clopen-correspondence">Idempotent–clopen correspondence</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Structure sheaf of a scheme](structure-sheaf-of-a-scheme.md)

A global [idempotent](idempotent.md) on a [scheme](scheme.md) is zero or one in every [local ring](local-ring.md). Its one-locus is both open and closed. Conversely a clopen subset determines a global [idempotent](idempotent.md) by gluing one on it and zero on its complement. Under this correspondence, decompositions into nonzero [orthogonal idempotents](orthogonal-idempotent.md) correspond to disjoint clopen decompositions. When all [connected components](connected-component.md) are open, the [primitive idempotents](primitive-idempotent.md) correspond exactly to those components. Without orthogonality the assertion fails in characteristic two: $(1,0)=(1,1)+(0,1)$ in $\mathbb F_2\times\mathbb F_2$.
