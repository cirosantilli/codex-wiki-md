# Statistical tangent space

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical tangent set](statistical-tangent-set.md)

The statistical tangent space is the closed linear span in [L2 space](l2-space-is-a-hilbert-space.md) of a [statistical tangent set](statistical-tangent-set.md). Taking this closure makes [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md) available and ensures that a continuous [derivative](derivative.md) specified on attainable [score functions](informant-function.md) extends to the whole space.
