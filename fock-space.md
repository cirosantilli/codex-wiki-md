# Fock space

↑ **Parent:** [Quantized field](quantized-field.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fock_space)

For a one-particle [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) $H$, the bosonic or fermionic [Fock space](fock-space.md) is $\mathcal F_\pm(H)=\bigoplus_{n=0}^{\infty}H^{\otimes_\pm n}$, using symmetric or antisymmetric tensor powers. Its zeroth summand is the one-dimensional vacuum sector. [Creation operators](creation-operator.md) and [annihilation operators](annihilation-operator.md) change particle number. The [bosonic Fock space](bosonic-fock-space.md) and [fermionic Fock space](fermionic-fock-space.md) implement the corresponding exchange statistics.
