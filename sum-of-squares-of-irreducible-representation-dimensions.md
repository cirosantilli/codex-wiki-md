# Sum of squares of irreducible representation dimensions

↑ **Parent:** [Group representation](group-representation.md)

The regular [group representation](group-representation.md) of a finite group over the complex numbers contains each [irreducible representation](irreducible-representation.md) with multiplicity equal to its dimension. Consequently the sum of the squared dimensions is the group order. Once a list of distinct irreducibles exhausts this sum, it is complete.
