# Symplectic capacity

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic geometry](symplectic-geometry-split.md)

A symplectic capacity is monotone under [symplectic embeddings](symplectic-embedding.md), scales by $c(X,a\omega)=a\,c(X,\omega)$ for $a>0$, and has positive unit-ball capacity and finite unit-cylinder capacity. It is invariant under [symplectomorphisms](symplectomorphism.md). A [normalized symplectic capacity](normalized-symplectic-capacity.md) additionally assigns $\pi$ to both unit domains.
