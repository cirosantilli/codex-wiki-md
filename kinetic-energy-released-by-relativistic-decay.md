# Kinetic energy released by relativistic decay

↑ **Parent:** [Relativistic energy](relativistic-energy.md)

For an isolated decay of a parent of [rest mass](invariant-mass.md) $M$ into products of [rest masses](invariant-mass.md) $m_i$, the difference between total final and initial [kinetic energies](kinetic-energy.md) is $(M-\sum_i m_i)c^2$ in every [inertial frame](inertial-frame.md). Conservation of total [energy](energy-split.md) and inclusion of all products are essential.
