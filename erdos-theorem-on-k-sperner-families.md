<h1 id="erdos-theorem-on-k-sperner-families">Erdős theorem on k-Sperner families</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [k-Sperner family](k-sperner-family.md)

Among $k$-Sperner subfamilies of $\mathcal P([n])$, the maximum cardinality is the sum of the $k$ largest [binomial coefficients](binomial-coefficient.md) $\binom nj$. The union of the corresponding levels attains the bound. For the upper bound, the [Lubell mass](lubell-mass.md) is at most $k$, and assigning its available mass to the levels with largest binomial coefficients maximizes cardinality.
