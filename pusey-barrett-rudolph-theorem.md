# Pusey-Barrett-Rudolph theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Ontological model of a quantum system](ontological-model-of-a-quantum-system.md)

Under [preparation independence](preparation-independence.md), an [ontological model of a quantum system](ontological-model-of-a-quantum-system.md) reproducing exact quantum [measurement in quantum mechanics](quantum-measurement-split.md) probabilities cannot assign overlapping preparation measures to distinct [pure states](pure-state.md). An exclusion [measurement in quantum mechanics](quantum-measurement-split.md) on independent copies makes every outcome impossible for one possible preparation; a common ontic region would require every response to vanish there. Thus the conclusion is a [psi-ontic model](psi-ontic-model.md), not a blanket exclusion of hidden variables or all informational interpretations.
