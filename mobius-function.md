<h1 id="mobius-function">Möbius function</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Arithmetic function](arithmetic-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Möbius_function)

The Möbius function is $\mu(1)=1$, is zero on integers divisible by a prime square, and is $(-1)^r$ on products of $r$ distinct primes.
