# Normalized univalent function

↑ **Parent:** [Univalent function](univalent-function.md)

A [normalized univalent function](normalized-univalent-function.md) is a [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function-split.md) on the [unit disc](unit-disc.md) which is [injective](injective-function.md) and has the displayed normalization. Its [Taylor series](taylor-series.md) begins $z+a_2z^2+\cdots$. Normalization makes coefficient bounds and image-size estimates independent of translation and dilation; the [Koebe quarter theorem](koebe-quarter-theorem.md) is the sharp universal image bound.
