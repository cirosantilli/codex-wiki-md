# Grafting-to

↑ **Parent:** [Grafted polymer chain](grafted-polymer-chain.md)

Formation of a tethered [polymer](polymer-split.md) layer by attaching reactive ends of preformed chains to a surface. The chains can be characterized before attachment, but inserting further coils into an existing [polymer brush](polymer-brush.md) costs [conformational entropy](conformational-entropy.md) and produces a coverage-dependent barrier. Reaction chemistry and surface accessibility also limit the final [grafting density](grafting-density.md).
