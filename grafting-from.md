# Grafting-from

↑ **Parent:** [Grafted polymer chain](grafted-polymer-chain.md)

Formation of a tethered [polymer](polymer-split.md) layer by growing chains from reactive sites on a surface. Small [monomers](monomer.md) reach the growth sites without inserting an entire preformed coil, making high [grafting density](grafting-density.md) more accessible than in [grafting-to](grafting-to.md). Initiator availability, termination and control of chain-length distribution remain important.
