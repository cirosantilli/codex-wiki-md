# Polar shear stress in a Newtonian fluid

↑ **Parent:** [Newtonian fluid stress tensor](newtonian-fluid-stress-tensor.md)

For a planar velocity field in polar coordinates,

$$
\sigma_{r\theta}
=\mu\left[
r\frac{\partial}{\partial r}\left(\frac{u_\theta}{r}\right)
+\frac1r\frac{\partial u_r}{\partial\theta}
\right].
$$
