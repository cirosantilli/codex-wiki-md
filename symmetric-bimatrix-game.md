# Symmetric bimatrix game

↑ **Parent:** [Bimatrix game](bimatrix-game.md)

A square [bimatrix game](bimatrix-game.md) is symmetric when exchanging players exchanges their payoffs: its [payoff matrices](payoff-matrix.md) are $P,Q$ with $Q=P^T$. If $(x,y)$ is a [Nash equilibrium](nash-equilibrium.md), then $(y,x)$ is also a [Nash equilibrium](nash-equilibrium.md). A [symmetric bimatrix game](symmetric-bimatrix-game.md) can still have asymmetric equilibria, so the two strategy vectors must be kept separate in the [Lemke-Howson algorithm](lemke-howson-algorithm.md).
