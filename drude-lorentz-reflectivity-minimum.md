# Drude-Lorentz reflectivity minimum

↑ **Parent:** [Drude frequency response](drude-frequency-response.md)

A doped [semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md) at frequencies far below its bound-electron resonances can have

$$
\epsilon_r(\omega)\simeq\epsilon_b-\frac{\omega_{pc}^2}{\omega(\omega+i\gamma_c)},\qquad
\omega_{pc}^2=\frac{n_ce^2}{\epsilon_0m_c^*}.
$$

For a weakly absorbing nonmagnetic half-space adjacent to [vacuum](vacuum.md), the normal-incidence [optical reflectance](reflectance.md) is minimized near refractive-index matching, $\epsilon_r\simeq1$. When $\epsilon_b>1$ and $\gamma_c$ is small this gives $\omega_{\min}\simeq\omega_{pc}/\sqrt{\epsilon_b-1}$. The zero of the [real part](real-part.md) of $\epsilon_r$, near $\omega_{pc}/\sqrt{\epsilon_b}$, is a distinct frequency. Increasing the [carrier density](carrier-density.md) shifts the minimum to shorter [wavelength](wavelength.md) if the carrier [effective mass](band-effective-mass.md) and background response remain fixed. With appreciable loss, the minimum must be found from the full complex [Fresnel equations](fresnel-equations.md).
