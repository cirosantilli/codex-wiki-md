# Null pregeodesic

↑ **Parent:** [Pregeodesic](pregeodesic.md)

A [null pregeodesic](null-pregeodesic.md) has a [null tangent](null-vector.md) and can be parametrized as a [null geodesic](null-geodesic.md). In a two-dimensional [Lorentzian metric](lorentzian-metric.md), the acceleration of a regular [null curve](null-curve.md) is orthogonal to its tangent and therefore proportional to it.
