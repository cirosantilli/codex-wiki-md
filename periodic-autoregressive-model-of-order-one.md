# Periodic autoregressive model of order one

↑ **Parent:** [Autoregressive model](autoregressive-model.md)

A centered model $X_t=\phi_\nu X_{t-1}+\sigma_\nu\varepsilon_t$, with $\nu=t\bmod S$, has season-dependent coefficients and [white noise](white-noise.md) innovations. In the nondegenerate case, the causal stability condition is $|\prod_{\nu=1}^S\phi_\nu|<1$. Individual coefficients can exceed one in modulus while the product remains stable. Its second-order law is a [periodically correlated process](periodically-correlated-process.md).
