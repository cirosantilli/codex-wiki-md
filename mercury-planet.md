# Mercury (planet)

↑ **Parent:** [Planet](planet.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercury_(planet))

The innermost [planet](planet.md) has a large metallic core and a weak but global intrinsic [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md). Its observed field supports an active [planetary dynamo](planetary-dynamo.md) in a fluid conducting part of that core. Slow rotation does not by itself exclude [dynamo action](dynamo-action.md).
