# Plus polarization

↑ **Parent:** [Gravitational wave polarization](gravitational-wave-polarization.md)

A [gravitational wave](gravitational-wave.md) of plus polarization has opposite transverse strain or [geodesic deviation](geodesic-deviation.md) [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) along two fixed perpendicular axes. For a sinusoidal [periodic function](periodic-function.md) profile, the stretching and compressing roles alternate every half-period.
