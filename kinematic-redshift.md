# Kinematic redshift

↑ **Parent:** [Relativistic Doppler effect](relativistic-doppler-effect.md)

A kinematic redshift is the [relativistic Doppler effect](relativistic-doppler-effect.md) from relative motion of emitter and receiver. With positive radial recession speed $v$, its small-velocity limit is $z_{\rm kin}=v/c+O(v^2/c^2)$. A [peculiar velocity](peculiar-velocity.md) gives such a factor in addition to [cosmological redshift](cosmological-redshift.md).
