# Massless longitudinal Pauli-Lubanski eigenvalues

↑ **Parent:** [Pauli-Lubanski pseudovector](pauli-lubanski-pseudovector.md)

With signature $(+---)$ and $\epsilon_{0123}=+1$, a momentum $(k,0,0,k)$ and [helicity](helicity.md) $j_3$ give lower components $W_0=kj_3$ and $W_3=-kj_3$. They obey $W_\mu P^\mu=k(W_0+W_3)=0$. These longitudinal identities require only the momentum and rotation-projection eigenvalues, not a statement about transverse little-group translations.
