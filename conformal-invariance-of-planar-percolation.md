# Conformal invariance of planar percolation

↑ **Parent:** [Percolation theory](percolation-theory.md)

For critical [site percolation](site-percolation-split.md) on the [triangular lattice](triangular-lattice.md), scaling-limit crossing probabilities transform naturally under conformal maps between planar domains. This rigorously established property supports descriptions of interfaces by [SLE](schramm-loewner-evolution.md) and calculations of planar [percolation critical exponents](percolation-critical-exponents.md). Transferring it to a different lattice or to [bond percolation](bond-percolation-split.md) requires a separate universality argument.
