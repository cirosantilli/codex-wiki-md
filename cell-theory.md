# Cell theory

↑ **Parent:** [Biological cell](biological-cell-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_theory)

The account that organisms are organized from [biological cells](biological-cell-split.md) and that cellular continuity is fundamental to their growth and reproduction. Its nineteenth-century formulation developed alongside changing accounts of [cell division](cell-division.md).
