# Distance minimizers on a punctured sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Geodesic](geodesic.md)

On the unit [sphere](sphere.md) with one point deleted, a distinct nonantipodal pair has a length minimizer exactly when its shorter [great circle](great-circle.md) arc avoids the deleted point. Antipodal pairs admit an avoiding semicircle. If the unique shorter arc crosses the puncture, smooth detours approach its length without attaining it.
