# co-RP

↑ **Parent:** [One-sided error](one-sided-error.md)

$\mathbf{co\text{-}RP}$ consists of complements of languages in [RP](rp-complexity.md). Its algorithms always accept positive instances and reject negative instances with probability at least one half.
