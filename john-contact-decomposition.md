# John contact decomposition

↑ **Parent:** [John ellipsoid](john-ellipsoid.md)

For a centrally symmetric convex body in [John position](john-position.md), there are contact points $u_j\in\partial K\cap S^{d-1}$ and positive weights $c_j$ such that $\sum_jc_j u_j\otimes u_j=I$. Taking the [trace](matrix-trace.md) gives $\sum_jc_j=d$. A supporting hyperplane at $u_j$ shows $|\langle u_j,x\rangle|\leq\|x\|_K$.

Here is the maximum-volume argument for the decomposition. If $I$ were outside the [convex cone](convex-cone.md) generated by the contact matrices, a [separating hyperplane](separating-hyperplane.md) would give a [symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md) $A$ with $\operatorname{tr}A>0$ and $\langle Au,u\rangle\leq0$ at every contact. Subtract a small positive multiple of $I$ so these inequalities become strict while the [trace](matrix-trace.md) remains positive. The [support function](support-function.md) of $(I+tA)B_2^d$ is $|(I+tA)v|$. It decreases below $1$ near all contact directions for small positive $t$; away from them, the strict gap $h_K(v)>1$ preserves containment. Its volume increases, since $\det(I+tA)=1+t\operatorname{tr}A+O(t^2)$, a contradiction. The cone is closed because its generators have [trace](matrix-trace.md) $1$ and form a [compact set](compact-space.md); finite-dimensional [convexity](convex-function.md) gives a finite decomposition.
