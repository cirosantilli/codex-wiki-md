# Equidistribution of a quadratic polynomial with irrational leading coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Weyl criterion](weyl-criterion.md)

If $a$ is irrational and $b,c$ are real, the fractional parts of $an^2+bn+c$ form an [equidistributed sequence](equidistributed-sequence.md). In the [Van der Corput lemma](van-der-corput-lemma-hilbert-space-sequences.md), every nonzero-lag correlation of $e^{2\pi im(an^2+bn+c)}$ is a [geometric series](geometric-series.md) with irrational frequency $2mah$ and tends to zero. The [Weyl criterion](weyl-criterion.md) then applies.
