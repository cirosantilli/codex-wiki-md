# Grey body

↑ **Parent:** [Emissivity](emissivity.md)

A [grey body](grey-body.md) has [spectral emissivity](spectral-emissivity.md) independent of [wavelength](wavelength.md) over the range in which the approximation is used. Under [Kirchhoff's law of thermal radiation](kirchhoff-s-law-of-thermal-radiation.md), the corresponding [radiometric absorptance](radiometric-absorptance.md) is also wavelength-independent. This allows the same factor to weight emission at $T$ and black-enclosure irradiation at a different $T_s$ in [net radiative heat exchange](net-radiative-heat-exchange.md). The factor can still depend on [temperature](temperature-split.md); treating it as constant over a cooling curve is an additional assumption. Wavelength independence alone does not imply direction independence.[§19.2, final paragraph: wavelength-independent absorption and emission factors](thermodynamics-and-propulsion.md)
