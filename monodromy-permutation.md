# Monodromy permutation

↑ **Parent:** [Monodromy action of a covering space](monodromy-action-of-a-covering-space.md)

Lifting a based loop in a [covering space](covering-space.md) from every point of its finite fibre gives a permutation of that fibre. Composition of lifted loops yields the [monodromy action of a covering space](monodromy-action-of-a-covering-space.md). If a closed [geodesic](geodesic.md) of length $\ell$ has a cycle of length $m$ in its [monodromy permutation](monodromy-permutation.md), the corresponding lift closes after $m$ traversals and has length $m\ell$. For a primitive base [geodesic](geodesic.md), these cycles describe the primitive lift components.
