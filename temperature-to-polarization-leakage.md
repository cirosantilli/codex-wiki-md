# Temperature-to-polarization leakage

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmic microwave background polarization](cosmic-microwave-background-polarization.md)

Instrumental conversion of a larger temperature-intensity signal into apparent sky polarization, for example through unequal channel gains or mismatched beam responses. Relative [calibration](calibration-differential-geometry.md), beam characterization, scan-angle coverage and map-level null tests are needed to control it. Multifrequency foreground removal alone does not establish that instrumental leakage is negligible.
