# Mean-field electromotive force

↑ **Parent:** [Mean-field dynamo](mean-field-dynamo.md)

The mean-field electromotive force is the vector correlation $\boldsymbol{\mathcal E}=\langle\mathbf u\times\mathbf b\rangle$. Its [curl](curl.md) enters the mean [resistive induction equation](resistive-induction-equation.md). It is a local vector with units of velocity times magnetic field, not the scalar circuit voltage called [electromotive force](electromotive-force.md). A [space-time average of periodic modes](space-time-average-of-periodic-modes.md) is often used to measure its transport coefficients.
