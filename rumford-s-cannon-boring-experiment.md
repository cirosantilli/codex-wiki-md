<h1 id="rumford-s-cannon-boring-experiment">Rumford's cannon-boring experiment</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Caloric theory](caloric-theory.md)

Frictional heating during the boring of cannon was used by [Count Rumford](benjamin-thompson.md) to challenge a simple account of heat as a finite material supply. A quantitative comparison also requires a stated mechanical input and the thermal response of the apparatus.
