# Radiative equilibrium of a single grey cloud layer

↑ **Parent:** [Greenhouse effect](greenhouse-effect.md)

A black surface receiving absorbed stellar [irradiance](irradiance.md) $Q$, beneath a single non-solar-absorbing [cloud](cloud.md) with thermal [emissivity](emissivity.md) $e>0$, satisfies $T_c^4=T_g^4/2$ when both bodies are in pure [radiative equilibrium](radiative-equilibrium.md). The surface balance is $\sigma T_g^4=Q+e\sigma T_c^4$, giving the displayed result. If instead [convection](convection-split.md) pins $T_c=qT_g$, the whole-column balance gives $T_g^4=Q/[\sigma(1-e+eq^4)]$; the nonradiative energy budget cannot then be discarded.
