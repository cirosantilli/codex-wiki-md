# Curvature-induced melting-temperature depression

↑ **Parent:** [Gibbs--Thomson relation](gibbs-thomson-relation.md)

Curvature depresses the equilibrium [melting](melting.md) [temperature](temperature.md) of solid convex into liquid. Here $\mathcal K$ is the sum of [principal curvatures](principal-curvature.md), equal to twice a common convention for [mean curvature](mean-curvature.md), and $T_m$ is absolute [temperature](temperature.md). The coefficient uses [solid-liquid surface energy](solid-liquid-surface-energy.md), [mass density](density.md) and [latent heat](latent-heat.md). This stabilizes fine corrugations of a [solidification](freezing.md) front.
