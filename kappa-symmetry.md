# Kappa symmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Superspace](superspace.md)

A local fermionic gauge symmetry of supersymmetric [brane](brane.md) actions. Since the [kappa symmetry projector](kappa-symmetry-projector.md) has half rank, the symmetry removes half the embedding fermions. The remaining physical fermions pair with the bosonic worldvolume degrees of freedom.
