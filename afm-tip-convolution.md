# AFM tip convolution

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic force microscopy](atomic-force-microscopy.md)

Geometrical broadening of a surface feature by the finite shape of an [AFM probe](afm-probe.md). The measured height envelope is more precisely a morphological dilation than an ordinary linear convolution. Repeated identical particle outlines can be tip images; inaccessible sidewalls prevent a unique inverse reconstruction.
