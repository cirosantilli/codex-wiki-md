# Cellular chain complex of a mapping cone

↑ **Parent:** [Mapping cone (topology)](mapping-cone-topology.md)

For a [cellular map](cellular-map.md) between nonempty [CW complexes](cw-complex.md), the reduced [cellular chain complex](cellular-chain-complex.md) of its [topological mapping cone](mapping-cone-topology.md) has differential

$$
D(y,x)=(d_Yy+f_\#x,-d_Xx).
$$

Here $C_{-1}(X)=0$ and the degree-zero group identifies a vertex $y$ with the reduced chain $y-v$, where $v$ is the cone vertex. Thus this is the [mapping cone](mapping-cone-homological-algebra.md) of $f_\#$, in the displayed ordering of the summands.
