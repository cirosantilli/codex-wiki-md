# Higman group

↑ **Parent:** [Finitely presented group](finitely-presented-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higman_group)

The Higman group has the [finite group presentation](finite-group-presentation.md) $Q=\langle a_1,a_2,a_3,a_4\mid a_i a_{i+1}a_i^{-1}=a_{i+1}^2\ (i\bmod4)\rangle$. Its subgroup $\langle a_1,a_3\rangle$ is a rank-two [free group](free-group.md), and the [normal closure](normal-closure.md) of each $a_i$ is all of $Q$. It has no nontrivial [finite quotients of a group](finite-quotient-of-a-group.md): a least prime divisor among the four generator orders in a finite image contradicts the multiplicative-order constraints imposed by conjugation to squares. These properties give finite-presentation constructions embedding arbitrary finitely presented groups into groups with no nontrivial finite quotients.
