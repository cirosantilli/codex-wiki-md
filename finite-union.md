# Finite union

↑ **Parent:** [Set union](set-union.md)

A [finite union](finite-union.md) is a [union](set-union.md) of finitely many [sets](set-split.md). The indexing [set](set-split.md) is finite; its member [sets](set-split.md) need not be [finite sets](finite-set.md). The [union](set-union.md) of an empty indexed family is the [empty set](empty-set.md).
