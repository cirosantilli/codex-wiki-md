# Social motivation theory of autism

↑ **Parent:** [Autism](autism.md)

A developmental proposal linking differences in social orienting, reward or engagement to later social experience. Reduced observed participation cannot by itself distinguish reduced interest from sensory overload, [anxiety](anxiety.md) or exclusion.
