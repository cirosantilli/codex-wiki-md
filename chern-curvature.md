# Chern curvature

↑ **Parent:** [Chern connection](chern-connection.md)

The [vector-bundle curvature](curvature-form.md) of the unique metric-compatible holomorphic Chern connection is an endomorphism-valued form of type $(1,1)$. It is Dolbeault closed and represents the [curvature Dolbeault class](curvature-dolbeault-class.md). Its trace gives normalized first-Chern forms, while its full [endomorphism](endomorphism.md) action enters the [Lefschetz-Dolbeault curvature commutator](lefschetz-dolbeault-curvature-commutator.md).
