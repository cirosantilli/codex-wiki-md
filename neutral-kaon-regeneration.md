# Neutral kaon regeneration

↑ **Parent:** [CP violation](cp-violation.md)

[Neutral kaon regeneration](neutral-kaon-regeneration.md) is the production of a short-lived component when a neutral [kaon](kaon.md) beam passes through matter. In the phase convention of [kaon CP eigenstates](kaon-cp-eigenstates.md), let the forward [transmission amplitudes](transmission-amplitude.md) of $K^0$ and $\bar K^0$ be $t$ and $\bar t$. Expanding $t|K^0\rangle+\bar t|\bar K^0\rangle$ in the [kaon CP eigenstates](kaon-cp-eigenstates.md) gives the displayed equation. Different forward [scattering amplitudes](scattering-amplitude.md), including absorption, can therefore generate a two-[pion](pion.md) signal even with [CP symmetry](cp-symmetry.md). Ordinary matter is not a [CP](cp-symmetry.md)-symmetric preparation.

Tungsten regeneration calibrated the signal; scanning its position sampled acceptance. Helium regeneration was insufficient, bag walls lay outside acceptance, vertex distributions excluded collimator regeneration, and hydrogen data cross-checked the effect.[p.138, tungsten scan; p.140, helium, walls, vertices and hydrogen controls](evidence-for-the-2-pi-decay-of-the-k%E2%82%82%E2%81%B0-meson-by-james-h-christenson-and-others.md) These controls are central: the question is whether the long-lived component itself reaches two [pions](pion.md), rather than whether material has prepared a fresh short-lived component.
