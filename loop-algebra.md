# Loop algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loop_algebra)

The algebraic loop algebra of a [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) $\mathfrak g$ over a [field](field.md) $k$ consists of finite [Laurent polynomials](laurent-polynomial.md) with coefficients in $\mathfrak g$. Its [Lie bracket](lie-bracket.md) is $[x\otimes t^m,y\otimes t^n]=[x,y]\otimes t^{m+n}$, extended bilinearly. Alternation and the [Jacobi identity](jacobi-identity.md) follow from those of $\mathfrak g$, since the [Laurent polynomial ring](laurent-polynomial-ring.md) is commutative. Appropriate smooth or formal completions give other loop-algebra variants; the [affine current algebra](affine-current-algebra.md) adds a central extension to these Fourier modes. For the affine extension, see [Kac and Wakimoto, Section 0.2](https://math.mit.edu/~kac/not-easily-available/kac-and-wakimoto-1990.pdf).
