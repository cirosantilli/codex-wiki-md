<h1 id="circularly-polarized-nonlinear-alfven-wave">Circularly polarized nonlinear Alfvén wave</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Nonlinear Alfvén wave](nonlinear-alfven-wave.md)

In homogeneous [ideal magnetohydrodynamics](ideal-magnetohydrodynamics.md), a constant longitudinal field $B_0>0$ and transverse field $aB_0(\cos\zeta,\sin\zeta)$, $\zeta=k(z-v_at)$, give an exact traveling wave with constant [mass density](density.md) and [pressure](pressure.md), $u_z=0$, $v_a=B_0/\sqrt{\mu_0\rho_0}$ and the displayed transverse [velocity](velocity.md). Induction and transverse momentum reduce to the same wave relation. Since $|\mathbf B_\perp|^2=a^2B_0^2$ is constant, there is no longitudinal [magnetic pressure](magnetic-pressure.md) [gradient](gradient.md). All nonlinear terms are compatible with the constant-density solution, even in a compressible fluid.
