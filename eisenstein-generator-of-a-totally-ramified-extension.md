# Eisenstein generator of a totally ramified extension

↑ **Parent:** [Totally ramified extension](totally-ramified-extension.md)

A finite extension $L/K$ of complete discretely valued fields is totally ramified exactly when $L=K(\alpha)$ for a root $\alpha$ of an [Eisenstein polynomial](eisenstein-polynomial.md) over $\mathcal O_K$. In the reverse direction one may take $\alpha$ to be any [uniformizer](uniformizer.md) of $L$.
