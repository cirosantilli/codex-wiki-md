# Collinearity for tangent circles between parallel lines

↑ **Parent:** [Tangent circles](tangent-circles.md)

Let two positive-[radius](radius.md) [circles](circle.md) have [external tangency](external-tangency.md) at $Q$ and lie in the closed strip between two [parallel lines](parallel-lines.md), touching opposite boundaries at $P,R$. Then $P,Q,R$ are [collinear](collinearity.md). To prove this, choose coordinates with the boundaries $x=0,d$, put $P=(0,0)$ and $R=(d,h)$, and write the centres as $O_1=(r_1,0)$, $O_2=(d-r_2,h)$. The contact point is

$$
Q=\frac{r_2O_1+r_1O_2}{r_1+r_2}=\frac{r_1}{r_1+r_2}R.
$$

The closed [line segment](line-segment.md) $PR$ is contained in the union of the two closed [disks](disk-mathematics.md): its portions from $P$ to $Q$ and from $Q$ to $R$ are [chords](chord-of-a-circle.md). The strip and [external tangency](external-tangency.md) hypotheses are essential to this particular formulation.
