# Feeling Safe Programme

↑ **Parent:** [Cognitive behavioral therapy for psychosis](cognitive-behavioral-therapy-for-psychosis.md)

A personalised intervention for persistent [persecutory delusions](persecutory-delusion.md), combining modules selected to address relevant maintaining processes. Its [active control](active-control.md) trial compared it with same-therapist befriending alongside usual care. The continuous conviction result and categorical [recovery](clinical-recovery.md) comparison require separate interpretation.[Lancet Psychiatry 8, 696–707; 130 patients, same-therapist befriending, six-month continuous conviction and categorical recovery distinction](comparison-of-a-theoretically-driven-cognitive-therapy-the-feeling-safe-programme-with-befriending-for-the-treatment-of-persistent-persecutory-delusions-a-parallel-single-blind-randomised-controlled-trial-by-daniel-freeman-and-others.md)
