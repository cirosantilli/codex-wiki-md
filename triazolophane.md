# Triazolophane

↑ **Parent:** [Supramolecular chemistry](supramolecular-chemistry.md)

A macrocycle containing triazole and other linking units. Appropriate inward-directed C–H donors can bind anions cooperatively; cavity size and [molecular preorganization](molecular-preorganization.md) can produce strong halide selectivity.
