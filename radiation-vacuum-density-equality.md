# Radiation-vacuum density equality

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md)

The equality of a radiation density proportional to $(1+z)^4$ and a constant vacuum density. It is only a crossing of two components. Matter can dominate both at this epoch, so radiation-vacuum equality need not mark a change in the universe's dominant component.
