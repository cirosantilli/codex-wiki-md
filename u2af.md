# U2AF

↑ **Parent:** [RNA splicing](rna-splicing.md)

An auxiliary splicing factor system involved in recognition of the [polypyrimidine tract](polypyrimidine-tract.md) and 3′ splice-site region. Competition with its recruitment can alter use of alternative acceptors.
