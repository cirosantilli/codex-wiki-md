# Chromosome

↑ **Parent:** [Genetics](genetics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromosome)

A chromosome carries [genes](gene.md) and other genetic material. A [haplotype](haplotype.md) records the [alleles](allele.md) carried on one chromosome at specified [genetic loci](genetic-locus.md).

In a typical [eukaryotic](eukaryote.md) [cell nucleus](cell-nucleus.md), an unreplicated [chromosome](chromosome.md) comprises one long double-stranded [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) [molecule](molecule-split.md) with associated [proteins](protein-split.md); a replicated [chromosome](chromosome.md) comprises two associated [sister chromatids](sister-chromatids.md). It need not be X-shaped: that familiar outline depicts a condensed, replicated state.[Biology 2e by Mary Ann Clark and others; §10.1, Genomic DNA; Eukaryotic Chromosomal Structure and Compaction, especially Figure 10.4 and final paragraph](biology-2e-by-mary-ann-clark-and-others/cell-division.md)

For these ordinary [centromere](centromere.md)-bearing [chromosomes](chromosome.md), count by [centromere](centromere.md) units before separation: the paired sister [centromere](centromere.md) regions count together as one replicated [chromosome](chromosome.md). Once [sister chromatids](sister-chromatids.md) separate in [anaphase](anaphase.md) of [mitosis](mitosis.md) or [meiosis II](meiosis-ii.md), each becomes a daughter [chromosome](chromosome.md). Thus the undivided [anaphase](anaphase.md) [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md) in the $2n=6$ mitotic example transiently contains twelve daughter [chromosomes](chromosome.md), but each pole receives six and each completed daughter [cell nucleus](cell-nucleus.md) remains [diploid](diploid.md). State the counting compartment and stage; summing both poles is not the [ploidy](ploidy.md) of one daughter.
