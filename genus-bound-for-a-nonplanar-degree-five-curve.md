# Genus bound for a nonplanar degree-five curve

↑ **Parent:** [Degree of a projective curve](degree-of-a-projective-curve.md)

The embedding [line bundle](line-bundle.md) has at least four independent [global sections](global-section.md). If it is nonspecial, the [Riemann-Roch theorem](riemann-roch-theorem.md) gives $h^0=6-g$, so $g\leq2$. If it is special, the [Clifford inequality for curves](clifford-inequality-for-curves.md) gives $h^0\leq3$, a contradiction. The [smooth projective curve](smooth-projective-curve.md) need not span its full ambient [projective space](projective-space-split.md).
