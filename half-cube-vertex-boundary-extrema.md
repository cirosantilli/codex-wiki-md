# Half-cube vertex-boundary extrema

↑ **Parent:** [Vertex-isoperimetric inequality in the discrete cube](vertex-isoperimetric-inequality-in-the-discrete-cube.md)

For $n\geq1$, a family of $2^{n-1}$ vertices in the [Boolean hypercube](boolean-hypercube.md) has these minimum and maximum external [vertex boundary](external-vertex-boundary.md) sizes. The [Harper theorem](vertex-isoperimetric-inequality-in-the-discrete-cube.md) gives the minimum: in odd dimension take the lower half of the levels, and in even dimension add the middle-level sets containing coordinate one to all lower levels. The family of even [Hamming weight](hamming-weight.md) attains the maximum, since every remaining vertex has an adjacent even-weight vertex.
