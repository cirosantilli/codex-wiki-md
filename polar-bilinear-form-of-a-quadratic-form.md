# Polar bilinear form of a quadratic form

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic form](quadratic-form.md)

The displayed [bilinear form](bilinear-form.md) is the polar form of a [quadratic form](quadratic-form.md). In odd [characteristic of a field](characteristic-of-a-field.md), $Q(x)=B_Q(x,x)/2$ recovers the [quadratic form](quadratic-form.md); in [characteristic of a field](characteristic-of-a-field.md) two, different forms can have the same [alternating bilinear form](alternating-bilinear-form.md). Over $\mathbb F_2$, two such forms differ by a [linear map](linear-map.md) to the [field](field.md), giving the affine parametrization of [binary quadratic refinements](binary-quadratic-refinement.md).
