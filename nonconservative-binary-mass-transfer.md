# Nonconservative binary mass transfer

↑ **Parent:** [Roche-lobe overflow](roche-lobe-overflow.md)

In nonconservative binary evolution, matter can escape the system and/or external processes change its orbital [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md). Escaping overflow, a [stellar wind](stellar-wind.md) and a [common envelope](common-envelope.md) can invalidate the fixed-total-mass and period-product invariants of [conservative mass transfer](conservative-binary-mass-transfer.md).
