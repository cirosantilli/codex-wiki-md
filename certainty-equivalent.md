# Certainty equivalent

↑ **Parent:** [Expected utility maximization](expected-utility-maximization.md)

A deterministic wealth level $c$ satisfying $U(c)=\mathbb E U(W)$. For normal wealth with [exponential utility](constant-absolute-risk-aversion-utility.md) $U(x)=-e^{-\gamma x}$ it is $c=\mathbb EW-\gamma\operatorname{Var}(W)/2$.
