# Point-count criterion for y squared equals x cubed plus k x

↑ **Parent:** [Elliptic-curve point count over a finite field](elliptic-curve-point-count-over-a-finite-field.md)

For odd $p$ and $k\not\equiv0\pmod p$, take $E:y^2=x^3+kx$. If $p\equiv3\pmod4$, pairing $x$ with $-x$ cancels the [Legendre symbols](legendre-symbol.md). If $p\equiv1\pmod4$, the [coefficient formula for trace of Frobenius modulo p](coefficient-formula-for-trace-of-frobenius-modulo-p.md) gives

$$
a_p\equiv\binom{(p-1)/2}{(p-1)/4}k^{(p-1)/4}\not\equiv0\pmod p.
$$

The [binomial coefficient](binomial-coefficient.md) is nonzero because every factorial involved is shorter than $p$. Thus the [Trace of Frobenius](trace-of-frobenius.md) is not zero in the second case.
