# Delayed iron enrichment

↑ **Parent:** [Alpha element](alpha-element.md)

Massive stars produce prompt enrichment through [core-collapse supernovae](core-collapse-supernova.md), whereas a [Type Ia supernova](type-ia-supernova.md) follows binary stellar evolution and contributes substantial iron after a distribution of delays. A [stellar population](stellar-population.md) formed rapidly before much delayed enrichment can have a larger alpha-element to iron ratio than a [stellar population](stellar-population.md) formed over a prolonged interval. Relative abundance also depends on the [initial mass function](initial-mass-function.md), [stellar yields](stellar-yield.md), and gas exchange; this is a chemical clock with model assumptions.
