# State-price density and local deflator distinction

↑ **Parent:** [State-price density](state-price-density.md)

A [local martingale deflator](local-martingale-deflator.md) only forces deflated asset prices to be local martingales. True expectation pricing requires additional martingale and integrability properties. In a Brownian one-factor market, continuity of the coefficients ensures a pathwise finite market-price-of-risk square integral, but not expectation-one of its [stochastic exponential](doleans-dade-exponential.md). The [reciprocal three-dimensional Bessel strict local martingale](reciprocal-three-dimensional-bessel-strict-local-martingale.md) gives a counterexample to promoting this local conclusion automatically.
