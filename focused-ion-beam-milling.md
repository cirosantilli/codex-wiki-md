# Focused ion beam milling

↑ **Parent:** [Nanotechnology](nanotechnology.md)

Removal of material with a focused ion beam, for example to prepare a thin [transmission electron microscopy](transmission-electron-microscopy.md) specimen. Implantation, amorphization and redeposition can require a low-energy finishing step.
