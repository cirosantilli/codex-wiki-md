# Prime ideal factorization

↑ **Parent:** [Ring of integers](ring-of-integers.md)

Every nonzero ideal of the [ring of integers of a number field](ring-of-integers.md) factors uniquely as a finite product of nonzero [prime ideals](prime-ideal.md). Thus each $x\in K^\times$ has integer valuations $\operatorname{ord}_{\mathfrak p}(x)$, almost all zero, determined by the factorization of its [principal ideal](principal-ideal.md).
