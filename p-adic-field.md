# p-adic field

↑ **Parent:** [Local field](local-field.md)

A p-adic field is a finite extension of the [P-adic number](p-adic-number.md) field $\mathbb Q_p$, equipped with its extended [p-adic absolute value](p-adic-absolute-value.md). Conversely every characteristic-zero, nontrivially valued, non-Archimedean locally compact field is such an extension. A finite quotient $\mathcal O/p\mathcal O$ supplies finitely many generators of $\mathcal O$ over $\mathbb Z_p$ by repeated reduction and completeness.
