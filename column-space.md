# Column space

↑ **Parent:** [Row and column spaces](row-and-column-spaces.md)

The column space of a [matrix](matrix.md) $A$ is the [linear span](linear-span.md) of its columns. It is the [image of a linear map](image-of-a-linear-map.md) represented by $A$.

If an $m\times n$ [matrix](matrix.md) has columns $c_1,\ldots,c_n$, then [matrix multiplication](matrix-multiplication.md) gives $Ax=x_1c_1+\cdots+x_nc_n$. Thus its [column space](column-space.md) consists of exactly the attainable output [vectors](vector.md) in $\mathbb F^m$, and $Ax=b$ has a solution exactly when $b$ belongs to this [column space](column-space.md). Its [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) is the [matrix rank](matrix-rank.md); the [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) instead describes input [vectors](vector.md) in $\mathbb F^n$ whose contributions cancel.[Interactive Linear Algebra, §2.4.3, Solution Sets and Column Spans: attainable outputs and input solution sets occupy different spaces](interactive-linear-algebra-by-dan-margalit-and-others/solution-sets.md)
