# Wnt-beta-catenin asymmetry pathway

↑ **Parent:** [Wnt signalling](wnt-signalling.md)

A signalling system that produces different transcriptional states in sister [cells](biological-cell-split.md). In the [Caenorhabditis elegans](caenorhabditis-elegans.md) EMS division, nuclear [POP-1](pop-1.md) and [SYS-1](sys-1.md) levels establish different coactivator-to-[transcription](transcription-biology.md)-factor ratios.
