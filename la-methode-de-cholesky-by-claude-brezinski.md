<h1 id="la-methode-de-cholesky-by-claude-brezinski">La méthode de Cholesky by Claude Brezinski</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematics bibliography](mathematics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by Claude Brezinski](work-by-claude-brezinski.md)

Historical research article by [Claude Brezinski](claude-brezinski.md), published in Revue d’histoire des mathématiques 11 (2005), pp. 205–238. Numdam hosts the original journal article. The printed received date, 4 November 2004, and revised date, 11 May 2005, are distinct from publication.

Original article: [https://www.numdam.org/item/RHM_2005__11_2_205_0.pdf](https://www.numdam.org/item/RHM_2005__11_2_205_0.pdf)

Section 4 reproduces a primary manuscript authored by [André-Louis Cholesky](andre-louis-cholesky.md) and dated Vincennes, 2 December 1910. Cholesky is the author of that manuscript; Brezinski is the author of the surrounding 2005 article. The manuscript date does not establish an exact invention date or a broad priority claim.

Cited passages: pp. 210–211: constrained geodetic corrections and the matrix $MM^T$; p. 217: manuscript title, 2 December 1910 date and uncertainty about the exact invention date; §4, p. 229: the primary manuscript’s reported 56-equation altitude adjustment for Algerian triangulation and closing date. The primary manuscript transcription was inspected as text; no inspection of handwriting images is claimed.
