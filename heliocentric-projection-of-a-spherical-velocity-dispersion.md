# Heliocentric projection of a spherical velocity dispersion

↑ **Parent:** [Velocity dispersion](velocity-dispersion.md)

For a non-streaming spherical tracer population, the [line of sight](line-of-sight.md) makes angle $\chi$ with the Galactocentric radial direction. The projected [velocity dispersion](velocity-dispersion.md) is

$$
\sigma_{\rm los}^2=\sigma_r^2\cos^2\chi+\sigma_t^2\sin^2\chi
=\sigma_r^2(1-\beta\sin^2\chi),
$$

where $\sigma_t^2$ is one tangential variance and $\beta=1-\sigma_t^2/\sigma_r^2$ is the [velocity-anisotropy parameter](velocity-anisotropy-parameter.md). At a fixed radius, averaging with the observational angular weights gives $\sigma_\odot=\sigma_r\sqrt{1-\beta A}$, where $A=\langle\sin^2\chi\rangle$.
