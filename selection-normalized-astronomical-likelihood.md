# Selection-normalized astronomical likelihood

↑ **Parent:** [Astronomical selection function](astronomical-selection-function.md)

For population parameters $\eta$ and an inclusion region $S$ in measured-data space, a selected datum has [density](density.md) $\mathbf1_S(d)p(d\mid\eta)/P(S\mid\eta)$. The normalization integrates the noisy measurement model and population over the actual selection region. Inference must also specify what is conditioned on: deterministic selection information already contained in the conditioned data must not be counted twice.
