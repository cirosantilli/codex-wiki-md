# Trapezoidal endpoint correction for a right Riemann sum

↑ **Parent:** [Composite trapezoidal rule](composite-trapezoidal-rule.md)

On a uniform partition of $[a,b]$ with width $h$, the right [Riemann sum](riemann-sum.md) and the [composite trapezoidal rule](composite-trapezoidal-rule.md) have equal weights at every interior mesh point. They differ only by adding half a final-endpoint weight and removing half an initial-endpoint weight. This proves the displayed identity. For continuous $f$, both converge to its [definite integral](definite-integral.md); their finite endpoint correction need not be confused with the smaller trapezoidal discretization error for a smooth integrand.
