# Subterminal sheaf

↑ **Parent:** [Subterminal object](subterminal-object.md)

In a sheaf topos on a topological space, [subterminal sheaves](subterminal-sheaf.md) correspond to open sets $U$. Their sections over $V$ are a singleton if $V\subseteq U$ and empty otherwise. An open cover gives a jointly epimorphic family of these [subobjects](subobject.md). The [open cover criterion for a local sheaf topos](open-cover-criterion-for-a-local-sheaf-topos.md) follows by applying a colimit-preserving global-sections [functor](functor.md) to that family.
