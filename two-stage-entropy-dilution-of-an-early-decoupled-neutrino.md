# Two-stage entropy dilution of an early-decoupled neutrino

↑ **Parent:** [Temperature of a decoupled relativistic relic](temperature-of-a-decoupled-relativistic-relic.md)

An extra relativistic [neutrino](neutrino.md) that decouples before ordinary [neutrinos](neutrino.md) misses two stages of plasma heating. Until ordinary decoupling, entropy conservation gives a temperature ratio $(43/(4g_d))^{1/3}$. Electron-positron annihilation then heats [photons](photon.md) but neither [neutrino](neutrino.md) population, multiplying the ratio by $(4/11)^{1/3}$. Here $g_d$ counts the coupled bath at the extra species' decoupling, with no subsequent exotic entropy injection.
