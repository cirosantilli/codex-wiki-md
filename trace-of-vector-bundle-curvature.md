# Trace of vector-bundle curvature

↑ **Parent:** [Curvature form](curvature-form.md)

The [trace](matrix-trace.md) of the [curvature form of a connection](curvature-form.md) is a global [closed differential form](closed-differential-form.md) of degree two. Under a frame change $e'=eg$, the [connection matrix](connection-one-form.md) and curvature change by $A'=g^{-1}Ag+g^{-1}dg$ and $\Theta'=g^{-1}\Theta g$. Thus [trace](matrix-trace.md) is independent of frame. The [Bianchi identity](bianchi-identity.md) gives $d\Theta=\Theta\wedge A-A\wedge\Theta$. Taking the [trace](matrix-trace.md) cancels the two terms: one-forms commute with two-forms in the graded sense, and renaming the [matrix](matrix.md) indices gives equality of their [traces](matrix-trace.md). Locally $\operatorname{tr}\Theta=d\operatorname{tr}A$, since the [trace](matrix-trace.md) of $A\wedge A$ vanishes by pairing its off-diagonal terms. It is the curvature of the [determinant connection](determinant-connection.md).
