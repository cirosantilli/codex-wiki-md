# Tidal bridges and tails

↑ **Parent:** [Galactic tide](galactic-tide.md)

Extended [stellar structures](stellar-structure-split.md) drawn out by time-dependent gravitational interactions between [galaxies](galaxy-split.md). Close, prograde encounters can coherently perturb rotating discs. Restricted particle calculations demonstrate that the structures need not be evidence of direct star-star impacts.[1972 abstract and §II](galactic-bridges-and-tails-by-alar-toomre-and-juri-toomre.md)
