# Complex sine

↑ **Parent:** [Sine](sine.md)

The [sine](sine.md) function extends to an [entire function](entire-function.md) on the [complex plane](complex-plane.md) by the displayed exponential formula. Substituting $z=x+iy$ and grouping the real and imaginary terms gives $\sin(x+iy)=\sin x\cosh y+i\cos x\sinh y$, for real $x,y$. Thus its values grow exponentially in imaginary directions, even though the real sine is bounded.
