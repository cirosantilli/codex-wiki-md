# Gluing three-balls along a boundary disk

↑ **Parent:** [Piecewise linear ball](piecewise-linear-ball.md)

Two [piecewise linear balls](piecewise-linear-ball.md) of dimension three glued along a common [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md) [disk](disk-mathematics.md) form another [piecewise linear ball](piecewise-linear-ball.md). The [Schoenflies theorem](schoenflies-theorem.md) identifies each [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md) pair with a [sphere](sphere.md) and a hemisphere. Coning the resulting [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md) [homeomorphisms](homeomorphism.md) extends them over the balls, which then become the two halves of a standard ball. The same argument shows that removing a ball attached along one [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md) [disk](disk-mathematics.md) leaves a ball.
