# Voltage-gated ion channel

↑ **Parent:** [Membrane potential](membrane-potential.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voltage-gated_ion_channel)

A membrane channel whose opening depends on [membrane potential](membrane-potential.md). Regenerative activation and subsequent inactivation or opposing currents help produce an [action potential](action-potential.md).
