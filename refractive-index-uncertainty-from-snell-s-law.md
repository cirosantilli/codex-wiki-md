<h1 id="refractive-index-uncertainty-from-snell-s-law">Refractive-index uncertainty from Snell's law</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Propagation of uncertainty](propagation-of-uncertainty.md)

For air approximated by index one, $n=\sin i/\sin r$, so $dn/n=\cot i\,di-\cot r\,dr$. Taking the [variance](variance-split.md) gives the displayed first-order error formula, with angles and their errors in radians. Independence removes the [covariance](covariance.md) term; hard error bounds require a different combination rule. Common angular-zero errors can produce [covariance](covariance.md) and should not automatically be added in quadrature.
