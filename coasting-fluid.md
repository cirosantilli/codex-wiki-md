# Coasting fluid

↑ **Parent:** [Barotropic equation of state](barotropic-equation-of-state.md)

A [coasting fluid](coasting-fluid.md) has pressure $p=-\rho/3$. The [cosmological perfect-fluid continuity equation](cosmological-perfect-fluid-continuity-equation.md) makes its density scale as $a^{-2}$. If it alone fills a flat expanding universe, the [scale factor](scale-factor-cosmology.md) grows linearly in physical time. Its background density has the same scale-factor dependence as a spatial-curvature term, although the fluid does not itself create spatial curvature.
