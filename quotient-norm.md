# Quotient norm

↑ **Parent:** [Quotient Banach space](quotient-banach-space.md)

The quotient norm measures the smallest norm among representatives of a coset. Its infimum need not be attained. Nevertheless, for every $\varepsilon>0$, an element $z\in X/Y$ has a representative $v\in X$ with $\|v\|<\|z\|+\varepsilon$. The [quotient map](quotient-map.md) is contractive.
