# Euler class in a generalized cohomology theory

↑ **Parent:** [Euler class of a vector bundle](euler-class-of-a-vector-bundle.md)

For an $h$-oriented real bundle $\xi$ of rank $r$, pull its [Thom class](thom-class.md) back along the [zero section](zero-section-of-a-vector-bundle.md) to obtain its Euler class in $h^r(X)$. It depends on the orientation and the actual bundle, not merely its stable isomorphism class. In [complex cobordism](complex-cobordism.md) the orientation may be supplied by a [stable complex structure on a real vector bundle](stable-complex-structure-on-a-real-vector-bundle.md).
