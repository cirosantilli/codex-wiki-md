# Centered square of a sub-Gaussian random variable

↑ **Parent:** [Subexponential distribution (light-tailed)](subexponential-distribution-light-tailed.md)

If $\mathbb E e^{tX}\leq e^{\sigma^2t^2/2}$, then $X^2-\mathbb E X^2$ is a [sub-exponential random variable](subexponential-distribution-light-tailed.md) with scale at most a universal constant times $\sigma^2$. The [Chernoff bound](chernoff-bound.md) gives $\mathbb P(|X|>x)\leq2e^{-x^2/(2\sigma^2)}$; integrating this tail bounds $\mathbb E|X|^{2q}$ by $2(2\sigma^2)^q q!$. Centering preserves a factorial moment bound, so the [Bernstein bound for independent sub-exponential variables](bernstein-bound-for-independent-sub-exponential-variables.md) apply to independent averages of these squares.
