# Nonisotropic root lemma

↑ **Parent:** [sl2 subalgebra associated with a root](sl2-subalgebra-associated-with-a-root.md)

For a [root of a root system](root-of-a-root-system.md) of a complex [semisimple Lie algebra](semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md), define $t_\alpha$ by $B(t_\alpha,h)=\alpha(h)$ using the [Killing form](killing-form.md) on its [Cartan subalgebra](cartan-subalgebra.md). Then $\alpha(t_\alpha)\ne0$. Otherwise vectors $x\in\mathfrak g_\alpha$, $y\in\mathfrak g_{-\alpha}$ with $B(x,y)=1$ would generate a [solvable Lie algebra](solvable-lie-algebra.md) with $[x,y]=t_\alpha$ central. The [Lie theorem](lie-s-theorem.md) would make $\operatorname{ad}t_\alpha$ nilpotent, while the [root-space decomposition](root-space-decomposition.md) makes it diagonalizable, forcing $t_\alpha$ into the zero [center of a Lie algebra](center-of-a-lie-algebra.md).
