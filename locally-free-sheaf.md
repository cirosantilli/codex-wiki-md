# Locally free sheaf

↑ **Parent:** [Sheaf of modules](sheaf-of-modules.md)

A sheaf of $\mathcal O_X$-modules is locally free of finite rank when every point has an open neighborhood $U$ on which it is isomorphic to $\mathcal O_U^{\oplus r}$ for some finite $r$. If $X$ is connected, the rank is constant.

A sheaf of modules is locally free of rank $r$ if each point has a neighborhood $U$ on which it is isomorphic to $\mathcal O_U^r$. Finite-rank locally free sheaves correspond to [vector bundles](vector-bundle.md) in the standard geometric settings.
