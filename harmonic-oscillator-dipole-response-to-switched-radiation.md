# Harmonic-oscillator dipole response to switched radiation

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamic electric polarizability](dynamic-electric-polarizability.md)

For a localized [quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md) along $z$, let the transverse [magnetic vector potential](magnetic-vector-potential.md) be $A_z(t)=2A_0\cos(\theta-\omega t)$ for $t>0$, with zero potential before the switch. The [Coulomb gauge](coulomb-gauge.md) form of [minimal coupling](minimal-coupling.md) gives $H_1=eA_zp_z/m$. The [creation operator](creation-operator.md) [matrix element](matrix-element.md) $\langle1|p_z|0\rangle=i\sqrt{m\hbar\omega_0/2}$ means that only $|1\rangle$ is reached at first order from the [ground state](ground-state.md). Its [interaction picture](interaction-picture.md) amplitude is

$$
b_1=eA_0\sqrt{\frac{\omega_0}{2m\hbar}}
\left[e^{i\theta}F_{\omega_0-\omega}(t)+e^{-i\theta}F_{\omega_0+\omega}(t)\right],\qquad
F_\nu(t)=\int_0^t e^{i\nu t'}dt'.
$$

In particular $F_0=t$, so resonant finite-time expressions are nonsingular. The induced [electric dipole moment](electric-dipole-moment.md) is $-2e\sqrt{\hbar/(2m\omega_0)}\operatorname{Re}(e^{-i\omega_0t}b_1)$. It contains a driven component with the [dynamic electric polarizability](dynamic-electric-polarizability.md) and free-frequency transients required by the initial conditions. The discontinuity in $A_z$ gives an impulsive [electric field](electric-field-split.md) and an initial mechanical velocity $eA_z(0^+)/m$ even though the initial canonical [momentum](momentum-split.md) has zero mean.
