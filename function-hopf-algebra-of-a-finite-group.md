# Function Hopf algebra of a finite group

↑ **Parent:** [Commutative Hopf algebra](commutative-hopf-algebra.md)

Functions on a finite group form a [Hopf algebra](hopf-algebra.md) under pointwise multiplication, with $(\Delta f)(g,h)=f(gh)$, $\varepsilon(f)=f(1)$ and $(Sf)(g)=f(g^{-1})$. The coproduct is cocommutative exactly when the group is abelian, since delta functions distinguish distinct products $gh,hg$.
