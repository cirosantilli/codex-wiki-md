# Seismic travel-time triplication

↑ **Parent:** [Seismic ray theory](seismic-ray-theory.md)

A ray family can fold in the mapping from ray parameter or turning depth to surface range, so that three rays arrive at the same range with different [travel times](travel-time.md). A sufficiently strong, thin high-positive-gradient zone can produce this effect. The first-arrival curve is the lower envelope of the branches; other branches represent later arrivals. [Gradient](gradient.md) contrast and thickness determine whether folds exist.
