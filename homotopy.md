# Homotopy

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic topology](algebraic-topology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homotopy)

A homotopy between maps $f,g:Y\to X$ is a continuous map $H:Y\times[0,1]\to X$ with $H(y,0)=f(y)$ and $H(y,1)=g(y)$.
