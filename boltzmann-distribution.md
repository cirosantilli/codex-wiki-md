# Boltzmann distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Thermodynamics](thermodynamics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boltzmann_distribution)

The [Boltzmann distribution](boltzmann-distribution.md) assigns [probabilities](probability.md) to individual [microstates](microstate.md) in a [canonical ensemble](canonical-ensemble-split.md), a [statistical ensemble](statistical-ensemble.md) for thermal contact. The system exchanges [energy](energy-split.md) with a [thermal reservoir](heat-bath.md) at fixed positive absolute [temperature](temperature-split.md) $T$, while its particle number $N$ and external parameters, such as [volume](volume.md), stay fixed. The [thermal reservoir](heat-bath.md) is large enough that these exchanges barely change its [temperature](temperature-split.md); the coupling permits [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium-split.md) but contributes negligible interaction [energy](energy-split.md). All the [microstates](microstate.md) considered have the same $N$. No [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) term is needed in their weights; fixed $N$ does not imply that the [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) is zero.

For a finite discrete set of [microstates](microstate.md) with [energies](energy-split.md) $E_i$, write

$$
p_i=\frac{e^{-\beta E_i}}{Z},\qquad
\beta=\frac1{k_BT},\qquad
Z=\sum_i e^{-\beta E_i},\qquad
\sum_i p_i=1.
$$

Here $k_B$ is the [Boltzmann constant](boltzmann-constant.md), $\beta$ is the [inverse temperature](inverse-temperature.md), $e^{-\beta E_i}$ is a [Boltzmann factor](boltzmann-factor.md), and $Z$ is the [partition function](canonical-partition-function.md). The [Boltzmann factor](boltzmann-factor.md) is a weight; dividing by the [partition function](canonical-partition-function.md) makes it a [probability](probability.md). [MIT 5.62 Physical Chemistry II, spring 2008, lecture 2, pages 4–7: fixed N, V, T and canonical probabilities](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/5-62-physical-chemistry-ii-spring-2008/ec4f40d5a0fa41df3692f076e850543d_02_562ln08.pdf)

The useful surprise is that a fixed [temperature](temperature-split.md) still permits [thermal fluctuations](thermal-fluctuation.md) in the system's [energy](energy-split.md). The [Boltzmann distribution](boltzmann-distribution.md) predicts their [probabilities](probability.md) and [expected values](expected-value-split.md) without tracking every microscopic exchange. Its [exponential function](exponential-function.md) also makes rare excited [microstates](microstate.md) sensitive to [temperature](temperature-split.md): an [energy](energy-split.md) gap much larger than $k_BT$ gives strong [Boltzmann suppression](boltzmann-suppression.md), while warming increases their relative populations. A population ratio can therefore reveal a gap or serve as a [thermometer](thermometer.md), provided [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium-split.md) and the relevant [state degeneracies](state-degeneracy.md) are known.

Historically, [Josiah Willard Gibbs](josiah-willard-gibbs.md)'s _Elementary Principles in Statistical Mechanics_ (1902) presents the [canonical ensemble](canonical-ensemble-split.md) in Chapter IV. His equations 90–92 give an [exponential function](exponential-function.md) of [energy](energy-split.md) and its normalization, in classical [phase space](phase-space.md). This classical formulation exhibits the same weighting principle.
