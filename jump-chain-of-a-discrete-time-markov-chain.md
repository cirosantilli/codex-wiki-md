# Jump chain of a discrete-time Markov chain

↑ **Parent:** [Markov chain](markov-chain.md)

For $P_{ii}<1$, observe a discrete-time [Markov chain](markov-chain.md) only when its state changes. The [probability](probability.md) that its next different state is $j$ is $\sum_{r\geq0}P_{ii}^rP_{ij}=P_{ij}/(1-P_{ii})$. Its [holding times](holding-time.md) are finite geometric waiting times. This construction deletes self-transitions and differs from the existing continuous-time jump-chain concept.
