# Holomorphic embedding

↑ **Parent:** [Complex manifold](complex-manifold.md)

A holomorphic embedding is a [holomorphic map](holomorphic-map.md) that is also a [smooth embedding](smooth-embedding.md). Its image is a [complex submanifold](complex-submanifold.md) and the map is a [biholomorphism](biholomorphism.md) onto that image.
