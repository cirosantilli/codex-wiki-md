# FRET proximity readout experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Förster resonance energy transfer](forster-resonance-energy-transfer.md)

In [Receptor sensitivity in bacterial chemotaxis by Victor Sourjik and others](receptor-sensitivity-in-bacterial-chemotaxis-by-victor-sourjik-and-others.md), [Victor Sourjik](victor-sourjik.md) and [Howard C. Berg](howard-c-berg.md) used a cyan fluorescent tag on [CheZ](chez.md) as [FRET donor](fret-donor.md) and a yellow tag on [CheY](chey.md) as [FRET acceptor](fret-acceptor.md). [Escherichia coli](escherichia-coli.md) [biological cells](biological-cell-split.md) adhered to a polylysine-coated coverslip in a continuously perfused chamber. A [fluorescence microscope](fluorescence-microscope.md) excited them with a $442$-nanometre [laser](laser-split.md); split emission bands fed two [photon](photon.md)-counting detectors. The yellow/cyan emission [ratio](ratio.md) fell when attractant was added and rose when it was removed.[Introduction, Materials and Methods: Preparation of Cells and Data Acquisition; Results and Figure 1](receptor-sensitivity-in-bacterial-chemotaxis-by-victor-sourjik-and-others.md)

Saturating attractant increased cyan emission by an amount matching selective bleaching of the yellow tag: removing the [FRET acceptor](fret-acceptor.md) supported relief of [FRET donor](fret-donor.md) transfer. Strains carrying the tagged pair but lacking other [chemotaxis](chemotaxis.md) machinery showed no stimulus-dependent emission [ratio](ratio.md) change. Weak illumination reduced bleaching drift.[Data Analysis and Results: acceptor bleaching comparison, low illumination, and flhC negative control](receptor-sensitivity-in-bacterial-chemotaxis-by-victor-sourjik-and-others.md)

The inference is a change in the population of nearby tagged [CheY](chey.md)/[CheZ](chez.md) pairs associated with [CheY](chey.md) [phosphorylation](phosphorylation.md). It neither measures each pair's separation nor directly observes motor motion. The readout also averages many [biological cells](biological-cell-split.md), and perfusion takes finite time. The pathway interpretation belongs to the [chemotaxis FRET assay](chemotaxis-fret-assay.md); the general measurement lesson is that molecular association can alter [FRET](forster-resonance-energy-transfer.md) while the geometry of an individual associated pair stays the same.
