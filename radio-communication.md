# Radio communication

↑ **Parent:** [Telecommunication](telecommunication.md)

Radio communication conveys information using [radio waves](radio-wave.md) coupled into and out of space by [antennas](antenna-radio.md). [Amplitude modulation](amplitude-modulation.md) and [frequency modulation](frequency-modulation.md) vary a [carrier wave](carrier-wave.md); [wireless telegraphy](wireless-telegraphy.md) can instead encode symbols in transmission intervals. The useful separation is between the rapid electrical [oscillation](oscillation.md) and the slower message timing. A receiver needs both [frequency](frequency.md) selection and a way to turn its selected [signal](signal.md) into a readable output.
