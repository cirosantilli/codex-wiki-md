# Sum of reduced residues

↑ **Parent:** [Euler totient function](euler-totient-function.md)

The map $j\mapsto n-j$ is a [bijection](bijection.md) of the [integers](integer.md) between one and $n-1$ that are [coprime](coprime-integers.md) to $n$. If their sum is $S$, this [bijection](bijection.md) gives $S=n\varphi(n)-S$, so $S=n\varphi(n)/2$. This works even at $n=2$, where the single residue is fixed. The same identity follows from the [weighted inclusion-exclusion principle](weighted-inclusion-exclusion-principle.md) by excluding multiples of the [prime factors](prime-factor.md) of $n$.
