<h1 id="holomorphic-forms-on-a-compact-kahler-manifold-are-closed">Holomorphic forms on a compact Kähler manifold are closed</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Holomorphic differential form](holomorphic-differential-form.md)

A holomorphic $(p,0)$ form is annihilated by $\bar\partial$ and, by bidegree, by $\bar\partial^*$. The [Kähler Laplacian identity](kahler-laplacian-identity.md) makes it harmonic for $d$, hence $d$-closed on a compact [Kähler manifold](kahler-manifold.md).
