# Quantum optics

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum theory](quantum-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_optics)

[Quantum optics](quantum-optics-split.md) studies [light](light.md) using [quantum states](quantum-state.md) of the [electromagnetic field](electromagnetic-field.md) and their interaction with matter. [Photon statistics](photon-statistics.md) asks how detections fluctuate; the [quantum theory of optical coherence](quantum-theory-of-optical-coherence.md) relates those detections to field correlations. A [coherent state](coherent-state.md), [thermal light](thermal-light.md), and a [Fock state](fock-state.md) can have the same mean [photon](photon.md) number yet different pair counts. This distinction makes [photon counting](photon-counting.md) useful for testing sources intended to emit one [photon](photon.md) at a time.
