# Isomorphic grammar

↑ **Parent:** [Equivalent grammar](equivalent-grammar.md)

Two grammars over a common terminal alphabet are isomorphic when a bijection between their nonterminal alphabets maps one start symbol to the other and maps their productions bijectively after being extended to fix every terminal symbol. Isomorphic grammars are [equivalent](equivalent-grammar.md).
