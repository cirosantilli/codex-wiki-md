# Primary momentum constraint of the electromagnetic potential

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical quantization of the electromagnetic field](canonical-quantization-of-the-electromagnetic-field.md)

The Maxwell [Lagrangian density](lagrangian-density.md) contains no time derivative of $A_0$, so its [canonical momentum](canonical-momentum.md) vanishes. The condition is a [first-class constraint](first-class-constraint.md). Its time preservation yields the [Gauss law constraint in gauge theory](gauss-law-constraint-in-gauge-theory.md); $A_0$ acts as the corresponding multiplier in the [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md).
