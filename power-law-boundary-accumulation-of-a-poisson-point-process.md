# Power-law boundary accumulation of a Poisson point process

↑ **Parent:** [Non-homogeneous Poisson point process](non-homogeneous-poisson-point-process.md)

If an [intensity function of a point process](intensity-function-of-a-point-process.md) has $\lambda(x)\sim c(b-x)^{-\alpha}$ at a finite endpoint, with $c>0$ and $\alpha>1$, the cumulative intensity satisfies $F(x)\sim c(b-x)^{1-\alpha}/(\alpha-1)$. The [Poisson mapping theorem](poisson-mapping-theorem.md) turns $F(X_n)$ into unit-rate arrivals. The [strong law for Poisson arrival times](strong-law-for-poisson-arrival-times.md) gives $F(X_n)/n\to1$ [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md), hence the displayed accumulation rate. Reversing the coordinate gives the same argument at a left endpoint. Different singularity exponents produce different powers of the index.
