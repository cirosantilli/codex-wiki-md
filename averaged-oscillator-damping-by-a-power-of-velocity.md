# Averaged oscillator damping by a power of velocity

↑ **Parent:** [Method of multiple scales](method-of-multiple-scales.md)

For $y''+\epsilon(y')^n+y=0$, the [method of multiple scales](method-of-multiple-scales.md) gives no first-order phase drift. If $n$ is odd, the [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) obeys the displayed damping law with $C_n=2^{-(n+1)}\binom{n+1}{(n+1)/2}$. If $n$ is even, the average is zero and the amplitude is unchanged on the first slow time $T=\epsilon t$. The odd case dissipates [mechanical energy](mechanical-energy.md), whereas the even case is reversible and its force is not damping of both velocity signs.
