<h1 id="riemann-s-differential-equation">Riemann's differential equation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Fuchsian differential equation](fuchsian-differential-equation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riemann's_differential_equation)

Riemann's differential equation is a second-order [ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md) with three [regular singular points](regular-singular-point.md), normalized to zero, one and infinity by a [Möbius transformation](mobius-transformation.md). Its local characteristic exponents are encoded by the [Papperitz symbol](papperitz-symbol.md) and constrained by the [Fuchs relation](fuchs-relation.md).
