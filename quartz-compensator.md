# Quartz compensator

↑ **Parent:** [Birefringent optical compensator](birefringent-optical-compensator.md)

A [quartz](quartz.md) retarder used to identify specimen fast and slow directions through changes of [interference colour](interference-colour.md) or compensation. Its own retardation and orientation must be specified before unique final colours can be predicted.
