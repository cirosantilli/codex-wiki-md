# Variational inference

↑ **Parent:** [Bayesian statistics](bayesian-statistics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variational_inference)

[Variational inference](variational-inference.md) approximates a [posterior distribution](bayesian-posterior.md) by minimizing a [Kullback-Leibler divergence](kullback-leibler-divergence.md) over a chosen family of [probability distributions](probability-distribution.md). For an unnormalized [posterior density](posterior-density.md) $h$ and a trial [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) $q$, minimizing $\mathbb E_q[\log q-\log h]$ is equivalent to minimizing $\operatorname{KL}(q\Vert h/\int h)$.
