# Topological embedding

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous map](continuous-map.md)

A [continuous map](continuous-map.md) [injective](injective-function.md) map $f:X\to Y$ that is a [homeomorphism](homeomorphism.md) onto its image with the [subspace topology](subspace-topology.md). A [continuous map](continuous-map.md) [injective](injective-function.md) map from a [compact](compact-space.md) space into a [Hausdorff space](hausdorff-space.md) is a [topological embedding](topological-embedding.md).
