# Soil infiltration

↑ **Parent:** [Porous-media flow](porous-media-flow-split.md)

Entry of surface water into soil pore space. The [infiltration](soil-infiltration.md) rate depends on water availability, soil permeability, capillary suction and the existing moisture profile. In a shallow overland-flow model it is a sink from the surface layer, distinct from [evaporation](evaporation.md) into the atmosphere. An imposed [infiltration](soil-infiltration.md) capacity cannot remove more liquid than is locally available.
