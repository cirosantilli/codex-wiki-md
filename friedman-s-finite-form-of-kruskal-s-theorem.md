<h1 id="friedman-s-finite-form-of-kruskal-s-theorem">Friedman's finite form of Kruskal's theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Kruskal's tree theorem](kruskal-s-tree-theorem.md)

For every natural parameter $k$, there is a finite length $N$ such that every [sequence](sequence.md) of $N$ finite [rooted trees](rooted-tree.md) satisfying $|T_i|\leq k+i$ has an earlier tree homeomorphically embedding into a later tree. It is a true finite-combinatorial principle unprovable in [Peano arithmetic](peano-arithmetic.md) and in [arithmetical transfinite recursion theory](arithmetical-transfinite-recursion-theory.md). Each fixed-parameter instance can still be verified by a sufficiently large finite search.
