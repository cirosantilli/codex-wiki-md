# Directional Hilbert transform

↑ **Parent:** [Hilbert transform](hilbert-transform.md)

For a unit vector $v$, the directional [Hilbert transform](hilbert-transform.md) integrates along lines parallel to $v$. Its [Fourier multiplier](fourier-multiplier.md) is $-i\operatorname{sgn}(\xi\cdot v)$ under the usual transform convention. Applied to a rectangle whose long axis is $v$, it reduces to the one-dimensional logarithmic tail of an interval [indicator function](indicator-function.md), separately on each transverse coordinate.
