# Maximal geodesic

↑ **Parent:** [Complete geodesic](complete-geodesic.md)

A geodesic $\gamma:I\to M$ is maximal when it has no geodesic extension to a strictly larger interval. Every initial position and velocity determine a unique maximal geodesic through the [geodesic equation](geodesic-equation.md).
