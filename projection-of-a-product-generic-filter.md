# Projection of a product-generic filter

↑ **Parent:** [Product forcing](product-forcing.md)

For a [generic filter](generic-filter.md) $H$ in [product forcing](product-forcing.md), let $G_0,G_1$ be its coordinate projections. They are [filters in an ordered set](filter-mathematics.md). Given $p\in G_0$ and $q\in G_1$, choose separate pairs witnessing their membership and then a common strengthening in $H$. Upward closure puts $(p,q)$ in $H$, proving the displayed equality. Every ground-model dense factor [set](set-split.md) lifts to a dense product [set](set-split.md), proving genericity of each projection over the ground model.
