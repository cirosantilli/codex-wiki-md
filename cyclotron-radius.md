# Cyclotron radius

↑ **Parent:** [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md)

The radius of a charged particle orbit perpendicular to a uniform [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md). In a parabolic band $r_c=m^*v_\perp/(|q|B)$, or $r_c=\hbar k_F/(e|B|)$ for a Fermi-level [electron](electron.md). Charge sign sets the curvature direction, while the radius uses magnitudes.
