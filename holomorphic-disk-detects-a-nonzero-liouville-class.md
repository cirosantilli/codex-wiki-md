# Holomorphic disk detects a nonzero Liouville class

↑ **Parent:** [Liouville class of a Lagrangian submanifold](liouville-class-of-a-lagrangian-submanifold.md)

For a smooth nonconstant holomorphic disk $h$ in $\mathbb C^n$ with boundary on a [Lagrangian submanifold](lagrangian-submanifold.md) $L$, its symplectic area is $\int_{D^2}|\partial_sh|^2\,ds\,dt>0$. The [Cauchy-Riemann equations](cauchy-riemann-equations.md) give $\partial_th=J\partial_sh$, so this is the pullback of the standard [symplectic form](symplectic-form.md). The [Stokes theorem](stokes-theorem.md) identifies the area with the period of a primitive along the boundary loop. Thus the [Liouville class of a Lagrangian submanifold](liouville-class-of-a-lagrangian-submanifold.md) evaluates nontrivially on this loop and cannot vanish.
