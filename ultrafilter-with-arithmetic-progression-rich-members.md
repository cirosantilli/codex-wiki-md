# Ultrafilter with arithmetic-progression-rich members

↑ **Parent:** [Ultrafilter](ultrafilter.md)

An [ultrafilter](ultrafilter.md) on the [positive integers](positive-integer.md) with every member containing finite [arithmetic progressions](arithmetic-progression.md) of arbitrarily large length. The [Van der Waerden theorem](van-der-waerden-theorem.md) and [ultrafilter selection from a partition-rich family](ultrafilter-selection-from-a-partition-rich-family.md) give existence. Such an [ultrafilter](ultrafilter.md) must contain the complement of every set with bounded arithmetic-progression length, and it is a [nonprincipal ultrafilter](nonprincipal-ultrafilter.md). No corresponding [ultrafilter](ultrafilter.md) can have an infinite [arithmetic progression](arithmetic-progression.md) in every member, by the [dyadic block obstruction to infinite arithmetic progressions](dyadic-block-obstruction-to-infinite-arithmetic-progressions.md).
