# One-sided representatives of a one-dimensional BV function

↑ **Parent:** [Function of bounded variation on a domain](function-of-bounded-variation-on-a-domain.md)

Both functions represent the same $L^1$ [equivalence class](equivalence-class.md). [Fubini's theorem](fubini-s-theorem.md) shows that the interval-mass function has [derivative](derivative.md) $Du$, so its difference from $u$ is constant. Continuity of a [finite measure](finite-measure.md) gives left continuity for $u_l$ and right continuity for $u_r$. Their opposite one-sided limits differ by the [measure atom](atom-measure-theory.md) $Du(\{t\})$. There are only countably many nonzero [measure atoms](atom-measure-theory.md), since every set of [measure atoms](atom-measure-theory.md) above a fixed magnitude threshold is finite.
