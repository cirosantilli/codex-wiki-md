# Oka-Grauert principle

↑ **Parent:** [Stein manifold](stein-manifold.md)

For a complex Lie group, holomorphic [principal bundles](principal-bundle.md) over a [Stein manifold](stein-manifold.md) are classified up to holomorphic isomorphism by their topological bundle class. In particular, a topologically trivial holomorphic [principal bundle](principal-bundle.md) over a Stein base has a global holomorphic trivialization. This supplies global complex gauges for a flat partial connection on contractible complex affine space; it does not impose prescribed behaviour at infinity.
