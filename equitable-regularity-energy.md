# Equitable regularity energy

↑ **Parent:** [Szemerédi regularity lemma](szemeredi-regularity-lemma.md)

For a [set partition](set-partition.md) $\mathcal P$ of the [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) of a [graph](graph-split.md) of order $n$, define

$$
q(\mathcal P)=\frac1{n^2}\sum_{A,B\in\mathcal P}|A||B|d(A,B)^2,
$$

where the sum is ordered, the adjacency indicator defines the density also on diagonal pairs, and exceptional [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) are treated as singleton parts. Refinement cannot decrease this energy, by the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md). An irregular pair of equal cells of size $m$, with witnesses of relative size at least $\varepsilon$ and density discrepancy greater than $\varepsilon$, increases the energy by more than $\varepsilon^4m^2/n^2$ for each orientation. Splitting refined atoms into equal chunks and leftover singleton parts is a further refinement, so restoring equitability causes no energy loss.
