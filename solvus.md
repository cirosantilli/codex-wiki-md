# Solvus

↑ **Parent:** [Phase diagram](phase-diagram.md)

A boundary giving the equilibrium limit of one [solid solution](solid-solution.md) against coexistence with another solid phase. Moving along a [solvus](solvus.md) during cooling changes equilibrium solid compositions even after all liquid has disappeared.
