# Norm for a finite free ring extension

↑ **Parent:** [Commutative algebra](commutative-algebra-split.md)

If $B$ is a commutative $A$-algebra which is a [finite free module](finite-free-module.md) over $A$, its norm sends $b$ to the [determinant](determinant.md) of multiplication $m_b:B\to B$. The [determinant](determinant.md) is independent of the chosen [basis](basis.md) and is multiplicative because $m_{bc}=m_bm_c$. It commutes with scalar extension. On a separable [field](field.md) extension it becomes the product over embeddings, recovering the [field norm](field-norm.md). An invertible $b$ has invertible norm.
