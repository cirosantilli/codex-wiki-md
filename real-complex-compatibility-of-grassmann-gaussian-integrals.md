# Real-complex compatibility of Grassmann Gaussian integrals

↑ **Parent:** [Real Grassmann Gaussian integral](real-grassmann-gaussian-integral.md)

For an even-dimensional [antisymmetric matrix](skew-symmetric-matrix.md) $M$, put $\theta=(u+iv)/\sqrt2$ and $\bar\theta=(u-iv)/\sqrt2$, with independent [Grassmann variables](grassmann-variable.md) $u,v$. Anticommutation cancels the mixed terms, giving $\bar\theta^TM\theta=(u^TMu+v^TMv)/2$. The constant [Grassmann change-of-variables formula](grassmann-change-of-variables-formula.md) changes only the normalization. The two real [Pfaffians](pfaffian.md) multiply to $\operatorname{Pf}(M)^2=\det M$, agreeing with the complex [Grassmann Gaussian integral](grassmann-gaussian-integral.md). In odd dimension $\det M=0$ and both top-degree integrals vanish.
