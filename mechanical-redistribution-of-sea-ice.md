# Mechanical redistribution of sea ice

↑ **Parent:** [Sea-ice thickness distribution](sea-ice-thickness-distribution.md)

[Mechanical redistribution of sea ice](mechanical-redistribution-of-sea-ice.md) changes thickness-class area through ridging, rafting and lead formation. In the conservative thickness-distribution equation, local volume conservation gives $\int h\psi(h)\,dh=0$; area normalization including open water gives $\int\psi(h)\,dh=\nabla\cdot v$ when thermodynamic boundary fluxes are accounted for consistently.
