# Internet-delivered cognitive behavioral therapy

↑ **Parent:** [Cognitive behavioral therapy](cognitive-behavioral-therapy.md)

Delivery of structured [CBT](cognitive-behavioral-therapy.md) content through an internet platform, potentially with clinician guidance, parent modules and live contact. Efficacy is a property of the specified programme, population and support arrangement, rather than the delivery medium alone.[Lancet Child and Adolescent Health 2, 792–801; 131 children aged 8–12, internet CBT versus child-directed play](efficacy-and-cost-effectiveness-of-therapist-guided-internet-cognitive-behavioural-therapy-for-paediatric-anxiety-disorders-a-single-centre-single-blind-randomised-controlled-trial-by-maral-jolstedt-and-others.md)
