# WKB correction term

↑ **Parent:** [WKB approximation](wkb-approximation.md)

The [Liouville transformation of a second-order equation](liouville-transformation-of-a-second-order-equation.md) with $t=\int^x\sqrt f\,dx$ gives $\ddot U-(1+F)U=0$. Neglecting this correction yields amplitude $f^{-1/4}$ and phase or exponent $\int\sqrt f$. Its smallness measures a local slowly-varying-coefficient approximation, away from zeros of $f$.
