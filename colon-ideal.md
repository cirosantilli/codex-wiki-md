# Colon ideal

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal](ideal.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colon_ideal)

For an ideal $I$ and an element $a$ of a commutative ring,

$$
(I:a)=\{x:xa\in I\}.
$$
