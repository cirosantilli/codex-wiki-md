# Conormal line of a smooth analytic hypersurface

↑ **Parent:** [Holomorphic conormal bundle](holomorphic-conormal-bundle.md)

For reduced local equations $f_j=u_{ji}f_i$ with $u_{ji}$ a holomorphic unit, the [holomorphic line bundle associated to a divisor](holomorphic-line-bundle-associated-to-a-divisor.md) $[-Y]$ has local generators $f_i$ and transition $u_{ji}$. Differentiation along $Y$ gives $df_j|_Y=u_{ji}|_Y\,df_i|_Y$. Sending the restricted local generator to $df_i|_Y$ gives the conormal isomorphism. The term $f_i\,du_{ji}$ disappears only after restricting to $Y$.
