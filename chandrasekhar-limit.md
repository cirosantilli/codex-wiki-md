# Chandrasekhar limit

↑ **Parent:** [White dwarf](white-dwarf.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chandrasekhar_limit)

The Chandrasekhar mass is the limiting [mass](mass.md) of a cold nonrotating [white dwarf](white-dwarf.md) supported by relativistic [electron degeneracy pressure](electron-degeneracy-pressure.md), about $1.4M_\odot$ for ordinary composition. It bounds the retained remnant's [mass](mass.md), rather than the birth [mass](mass.md) of a [star](star.md) that can lose an envelope. The ideal limit assumes fixed [mean molecular weight per electron](mean-molecular-weight-per-electron.md), Newtonian [gravity](gravity.md) and a noninteracting [degenerate electron gas](degenerate-electron-gas.md); [electron capture](electron-capture.md), [general relativity](general-relativity-split.md), [stellar rotation](stellar-rotation.md) and ignition can change the attainable endpoint.

The [Chandrasekhar limit](chandrasekhar-limit.md) belongs to work of the early 1930s. In his 1983 retrospective, [Subrahmanyan Chandrasekhar](subrahmanyan-chandrasekhar.md) reprints the 1935 [mass](mass.md)–[radius](radius.md) sequence and then discusses [stellar evolution](stellar-evolution.md) and relativistic stability.[Section 5, pp. 149–151, Figure 2; sections 7–8, pp. 156–159](on-stars-their-evolution-and-their-stability-by-subrahmanyan-chandrasekhar.md) The 1983 [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics-split.md) recognized his broader theoretical work on [stellar structure](stellar-structure-split.md) and [stellar evolution](stellar-evolution.md); the other half recognized [William Alfred Fowler](william-alfred-fowler.md)'s theoretical and experimental work on [nuclear reactions](nuclear-reaction.md) underlying [chemical element](chemical-element.md) formation.[Award shares and motivations](the-nobel-prize-in-physics-1983-by-nobel-prize-outreach.md)
