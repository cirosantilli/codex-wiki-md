# Shallow finite-square-well energy

↑ **Parent:** [Ground state of a finite square well](ground-state-of-a-finite-square-well.md)

For a well of depth $V_0$ and full width $2a$, measured from zero exterior [potential energy](potential-energy.md), even-state matching gives $q=k\tan ka$. If $a\sqrt{mV_0}/\hbar\ll1$, then $ka\ll1$ and $q\simeq2mV_0a/\hbar^2$. Using $E_0=-\hbar^2q^2/(2m)$ gives the displayed leading binding [energy](energy-split.md). It agrees with the attractive delta-potential result for integrated strength $2aV_0$.
