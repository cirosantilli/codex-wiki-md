# Ideal gas mixture

↑ **Parent:** [Gas mixture](gas-mixture.md)

An idealized [gas mixture](gas-mixture.md) in which component [partial pressures](partial-pressure.md) add and component amounts obey the [ideal gas law](ideal-gas-law.md). At a common [temperature](temperature-split.md) and [pressure](pressure-split.md), component gas volumes before mixing can be used to calculate the mixture's [mole fractions](mole-fraction.md).
