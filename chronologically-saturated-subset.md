# Chronologically saturated subset

↑ **Parent:** [Chronological future](chronological-future.md)

A subset $U$ is chronologically saturated in both directions when $I^+(U)=U=I^-(U)$. If the ambient [spacetime](spacetime.md) is connected and $U$ is nonempty, then $U$ is the whole spacetime.
