# Beta-glucosidase

↑ **Parent:** [Enzyme](enzyme-split.md)

A [beta-glucosidase](beta-glucosidase.md) hydrolyzes beta-glucosidic substrates. Conversion of cellobiose to [glucose](glucose.md) can reduce product accumulation during [cellulose](cellulose.md) saccharification.
