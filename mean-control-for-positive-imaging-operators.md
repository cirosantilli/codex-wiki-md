# Mean control for positive imaging operators

↑ **Parent:** [Positivity-preserving linear operator on L1](positivity-preserving-linear-operator-on-l1.md)

On a bounded connected [Lipschitz domain](lipschitz-domain.md), let $u\ge0$ belong to the [BV space](function-of-bounded-variation-on-a-domain.md), and let $T$ be a [positivity-preserving operator](positivity-preserving-linear-operator-on-l1.md) with $T1\ne0$. Writing $u=c1+(u-c1)$, $c=u_\Omega\ge0$, the [triangle inequality](triangle-inequality.md) and [Poincaré inequality for total variation](poincare-inequality-for-total-variation.md) give $c\|T1\|_1\le\|Tu\|_1+\|T\|\|u-c\|_1\le\|Tu\|_1+C_\Omega\|T\|\operatorname{TV}(u)$. Thus forward-image and variation bounds control the missing constant mode, and hence the full $BV$ [norm](norm.md).
