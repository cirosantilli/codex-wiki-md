# Inverse-variance weight

↑ **Parent:** [Inverse-variance weighted mean](inverse-variance-weighted-mean.md)

A weight $w_i=1/v_i$ gives more weight to a more precise estimate. In [random-effects meta-analysis](random-effects-meta-analysis.md) the [variance](variance-split.md) of the corresponding [marginal distribution](marginal-distribution.md) is $v_i+\tau^2$, giving weight $(v_i+\tau^2)^{-1}$. A percentage weight divides the individual weight by the sum of all weights.
