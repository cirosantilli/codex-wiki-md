# Ising spin echo

↑ **Parent:** [Ising coupling of two qubits](ising-coupling-of-two-qubits.md)

A $\pi$ [rotation about the x-axis](rotation-about-the-x-axis.md) on one qubit conjugates $Z\otimes Z$ to its negative. With ideal instantaneous pulses, two equal free-evolution intervals separated by this pulse and its inverse cancel an [Ising coupling](ising-coupling-of-two-qubits.md) exactly. Finite-duration pulses require accounting for the coupling during the pulses; the error is small only when $|J|$ times the pulse duration is small, or when the control is explicitly designed to compensate it.
