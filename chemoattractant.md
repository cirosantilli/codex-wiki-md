# Chemoattractant

↑ **Parent:** [Chemotaxis](chemotaxis.md)

A [chemoattractant](chemoattractant.md) is a substance whose spatial distribution can bias responsive [biological cells](biological-cell-split.md) to migrate towards higher [concentration](concentration.md). A directional response to its [gradient](gradient.md) is [chemotaxis](chemotaxis.md); a change only in movement speed does not by itself establish a directional response.
