# Logarithmic-derivative matching

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum scattering](quantum-scattering.md)

At an interface where a finite potential changes discontinuously, continuity of a radial wavefunction and its derivative is equivalent to matching $u'/u$ on the two sides. This removes the arbitrary normalization constants and gives an equation for the [scattering phase shift](scattering-phase-shift.md).
