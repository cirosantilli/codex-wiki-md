# Top Ext detects finite projective dimension

↑ **Parent:** [Projective dimension](projective-dimension.md)

Let $M$ be a nonzero [finitely generated module](finitely-generated-module.md) over a commutative [Noetherian ring](noetherian-ring.md). Take a length-$n$ resolution with finite projective terms. If its top [Ext functor](ext-functor.md) with coefficients in $A$ vanished, the dual of its last injection would be surjective. The dual of a finite projective module is projective, so that surjection splits. Finite projective modules are isomorphic to their double duals, so dualizing gives a splitting of the original injection. The resolution then shortens, contradicting minimality of $n$. When $n=0$, a nonzero coordinate of a finite free module containing $M$ as a summand gives a nonzero map $M\to A$.
