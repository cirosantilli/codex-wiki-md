# Odor-taste conditioning

↑ **Parent:** [Learning](learning.md)

A change in an odor's taste-like qualities or associations after pairing it with a taste. Pre/post odor-only judgments can test transfer beyond the original mixture, while controls are needed for expectation and use of response scales. Such transfer supports learned cross-sensory effects but does not by itself prove a distinct developmental [synesthesia](synesthesia.md) mechanism.[Experiment 1, sucrose- and acid-paired odors](the-acquisition-of-taste-properties-by-odors-by-richard-j-stevenson-and-others.md)
