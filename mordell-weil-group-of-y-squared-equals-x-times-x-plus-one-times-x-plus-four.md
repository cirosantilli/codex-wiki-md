# Mordell-Weil group of y squared equals x times x plus one times x plus four

↑ **Parent:** [Elliptic curve](elliptic-curve.md)

For $E:y^2=x(x+1)(x+4)$,

$$
E(\mathbb Q)\cong\mathbb Z/4\mathbb Z\oplus\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z.
$$

The points $(-2,2)$ and $(-1,0)$ generate the two factors, and a [two-descent on an elliptic curve](two-descent-on-an-elliptic-curve.md) proves that the [Mordell-Weil group](mordell-weil-group.md) has rank zero.
