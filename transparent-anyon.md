# Transparent anyon

↑ **Parent:** [Anyon monodromy](anyon-monodromy.md)

An [anyon](anyon.md) whose full [anyon monodromy](anyon-monodromy.md) with every other charge is the identity in every allowed [anyon fusion](anyon-fusion-rule.md) channel. The [vacuum anyon](vacuum-anyon.md) is transparent. Transparent charges and their [anyon fusion](anyon-fusion-rule.md) summands form a symmetric subcategory of the [braided monoidal category](braided-monoidal-category.md). They can have [quantum dimension](quantum-dimension.md) greater than one.
