# Circular orbits in an exponential central potential

↑ **Parent:** [Circular orbit](circular-orbit.md)

For unit mass with specific [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) $l$ in $V(r)=-e^{-r}$, the [effective potential](effective-potential.md) is $-e^{-r}+l^2/(2r^2)$. A positive-radius [circular orbit](circular-orbit.md) satisfies the displayed equation. Since $r^3e^{-r}$ has its unique maximum $27/e^3$ at $r=3$, exactly two radii occur for $0<l^2<27/e^3$. Their radial stability follows from $V_{\rm eff}''=e^{-r}(3/r-1)$: the inner orbit is stable at fixed [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md), the outer unstable. The case $l=0$ has no finite-radius [circular orbit](circular-orbit.md) despite satisfying the upper bound.
