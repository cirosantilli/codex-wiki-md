# Asian option

↑ **Parent:** [European contingent claim](european-contingent-claim.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_option)

An option whose payoff depends on an average of the underlying [stock](stock.md) prices over time. The displayed arithmetic-average call differs from a geometric-average call, which uses the geometric mean. A [convex](convex-function.md) payoff and a nondecreasing positive [numéraire](numeraire.md) give an upper bound by the average costs of same-strike [European call options](european-call-option.md) at the averaging dates.
