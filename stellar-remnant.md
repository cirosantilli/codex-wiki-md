# Stellar remnant

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar evolution](stellar-evolution.md)

A [stellar remnant](stellar-remnant.md) is the object left after a [star](star.md) finishes its principal nuclear-burning evolution, such as a [white dwarf](white-dwarf.md), [neutron star](neutron-star.md) or [black hole](black-hole.md). [Stellar remnants](stellar-remnant.md) retain mass while often contributing little optical light, affecting an old [stellar population](stellar-population.md)'s [mass-to-light ratio](mass-to-light-ratio.md).
