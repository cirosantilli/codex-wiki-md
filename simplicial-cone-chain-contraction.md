# Simplicial cone chain contraction

↑ **Parent:** [Simplicial chain complex](simplicial-chain-complex.md)

In an [augmented chain complex](augmented-chain-complex.md) of a simplex with vertices $v_0,\ldots,v_n$, put $s(1)=[v_0]$ and $s[v_{i_0},\ldots,v_{i_q}]=[v_0,v_{i_0},\ldots,v_{i_q}]$ when $v_0$ is absent, and zero when it is present. Direct expansion of the [boundary operator](boundary-operator.md) gives $ds+sd=1$. This proves that every positive-degree [chain cycle](chain-cycle.md) is a [chain boundary](chain-boundary.md), without invoking [cellular homology](cellular-chain-complex.md).
