# Hydroacylation

↑ **Parent:** [Organic reaction](organic-reaction.md)

Addition of an aldehydic C–H bond across an unsaturated bond, creating a [ketone](ketone.md). Intramolecular [alkene](alkene.md) [hydroacylation](hydroacylation.md) commonly constructs a five-membered ring via an acyl–metal [hydride](hydride.md), [alkene](alkene.md) insertion and [reductive elimination](reductive-elimination.md).
