# Thermostat

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium-split.md)

A [thermostat](thermostat.md) senses [temperature](temperature-split.md) and adjusts a heating or cooling actuator to approach a reference [temperature](temperature-split.md). For precise regulation, sensor placement, thermal delay and feedback stability matter as well as the nominal reference. Thermal shielding can reduce rapid environmental disturbances while feedback corrects slower ones.
