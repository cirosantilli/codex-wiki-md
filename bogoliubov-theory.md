# Bogoliubov theory

↑ **Parent:** [Weakly interacting Bose gas](weakly-interacting-bose-gas.md)

Expand an interacting Bose field about a macroscopically occupied condensate and retain terms quadratic in its fluctuations. A [Bogoliubov transformation](bogoliubov-transformation.md) diagonalizes the stable quadratic Hamiltonian. For contact repulsion this gives the [Bogoliubov quasiparticle dispersion](bogoliubov-quasiparticle-dispersion.md) $E_k=\sqrt{\epsilon_k(\epsilon_k+2g_Bn_0)}$, a linear sound mode at small [momentum](momentum-split.md) and particle-like behavior at large [momentum](momentum-split.md). Mixing creation and [annihilation operators](annihilation-operator.md) also produces [quantum depletion of a Bose condensate](quantum-depletion-of-a-bose-condensate.md). The approximation requires controlled fluctuations; it does not give an exact strongly interacting liquid or the full fluctuation-dominated critical region.
