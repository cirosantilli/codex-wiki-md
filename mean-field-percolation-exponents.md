# Mean-field percolation exponents

↑ **Parent:** [Percolation critical exponents](percolation-critical-exponents.md)

The mean-field predictions model large clusters by [branching processes](branching-process.md). Rigorous results apply in specified high-dimensional or sufficiently spread-out regimes, using tools such as [lace expansion](lace-expansion.md) and the [percolation triangle condition](percolation-triangle-condition.md). The full list involves different observables; the triangle condition alone should not be presented as proving every spatial exponent. With these exponents $2\beta+\gamma=3$, whereas $d\nu=d/2$, explaining the failure of naive [hyperscaling relation](hyperscaling-relation.md) above six.
