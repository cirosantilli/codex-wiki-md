# Cyclic adenosine monophosphate

↑ **Parent:** [Second messenger](second-messenger.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyclic_adenosine_monophosphate)

[Cyclic adenosine monophosphate](cyclic-adenosine-monophosphate.md) is a cyclic [nucleotide](nucleotide.md) made from [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md). It can serve as an intracellular [second messenger](second-messenger.md), changing the activity of target [proteins](protein-split.md) after a signal has been received. Different [biological cells](biological-cell-split.md) can respond differently because they contain different targets. [Clark, Douglas and Choi, Biology 2e, §9.2: cyclic AMP as a second messenger](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/9-2-propagation-of-the-signal)
