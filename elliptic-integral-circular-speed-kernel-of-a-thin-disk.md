# Elliptic-integral circular-speed kernel of a thin disk

↑ **Parent:** [Legendre expansion of thin-disk gravity](legendre-expansion-of-thin-disk-gravity.md)

Integrating a circular ring's [Newtonian gravitational potential](newtonian-gravitational-potential.md) over azimuth gives $d\Phi=-4Gs\Sigma(s)K(k)ds/(r+s)$, with modulus $k=2\sqrt{rs}/(r+s)$. Differentiating using the [complete elliptic integral of the first kind](complete-elliptic-integral-of-the-first-kind.md) derivative gives the displayed [circular speed](circular-speed.md) kernel, where $E$ is the [complete elliptic integral of the second kind](complete-elliptic-integral-of-the-second-kind.md). For an interior point of a smooth disk use a [Cauchy principal value](cauchy-principal-value.md); beyond the outer radius the integral is ordinary. A discontinuous nonzero surface-density edge in an exactly zero-thickness disk has a logarithmically singular in-plane force at that edge.
