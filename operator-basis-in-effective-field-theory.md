# Operator basis in effective field theory

↑ **Parent:** [Effective field theory](effective-field-theory.md)

An [operator basis in effective field theory](operator-basis-in-effective-field-theory.md) is an independent set of allowed [local field operators](local-operator-physics.md) at a chosen order, modulo relevant algebraic identities, [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) and permitted [field redefinitions](field-redefinition.md). [Redundant operators](redundant-operator.md) can be removed without changing on-shell amplitudes, provided their coefficient shifts are accounted for. The basis depends on the light fields and symmetries.
