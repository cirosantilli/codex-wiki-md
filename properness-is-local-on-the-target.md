# Properness is local on the target

↑ **Parent:** [Proper morphism](proper-morphism.md)

If $f:X\to Y$ is a [morphism of schemes](morphism-of-schemes.md) and $(U_i)$ is an open cover of $Y$, then $f$ is [proper](proper-morphism.md) exactly when every restriction $f^{-1}(U_i)\to U_i$ is proper. The three defining properties—finite type, separatedness, and universal closedness—can each be checked on an open cover of the target.
