# Complex differentiability at a point without holomorphy nearby

↑ **Parent:** [Complex differentiability](complex-differentiability.md)

At zero, $[f(h)-f(0)]/h\to1$, so the [complex derivative](complex-derivative.md) exists. At a nonzero point, write $r=|z|$. The [Cauchy-Riemann equations](cauchy-riemann-equations.md) for $x/(1+r)$ and $y/(1+r)$ force $x^2=y^2$ and $xy=0$, impossible when $r>0$. Hence the function is [complex differentiable](complex-differentiability.md) only at zero and is [holomorphic](holomorphic-function-split.md) on no nonempty open subset. A [complex derivative](complex-derivative.md) at one point gives no neighborhood of [complex differentiability](complex-differentiability.md).
