# Free-fall time of a uniform sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Gravitational collapse](gravitational-collapse.md)

A pressureless uniform sphere initially at rest collapses under Newtonian gravity in a time depending only on its initial mass density. Solving the radial shell equation gives $\sqrt{3\pi/(32G\rho)}$. It is commonly used as a dynamical-time benchmark for cooling and [star formation](star-formation.md), with the gravitating density rather than gas density alone when other matter supplies gravity.
