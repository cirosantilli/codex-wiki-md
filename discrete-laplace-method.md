# Discrete Laplace method

↑ **Parent:** [Laplace's method](laplace-s-method.md)

The discrete Laplace method estimates a sum whose positive terms concentrate near the maximum of a smooth exponent. When the Gaussian concentration width contains asymptotically many lattice points, the sum is a Riemann-sum version of [Laplace's method](laplace-s-method.md) with the corresponding lattice-spacing factor.
