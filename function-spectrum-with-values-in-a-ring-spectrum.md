# Function spectrum with values in a ring spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Function spectrum](function-spectrum.md)

If $E$ is a [ring spectrum](ring-spectrum.md), then $F(X_+,E)$ is a [ring spectrum](ring-spectrum.md). Its multiplication is adjoint to evaluating the two functions on the two copies supplied by the diagonal of $X$, then multiplying in $E$. Its unit is the constant unit function. Coassociativity of the diagonal and associativity in $E$ prove the associative identity; the counit and unit identities prove both unit laws.
