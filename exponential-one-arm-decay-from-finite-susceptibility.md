# Exponential one-arm decay from finite susceptibility

↑ **Parent:** [Percolation susceptibility](percolation-susceptibility.md)

The [percolation susceptibility](percolation-susceptibility.md) is $\chi(p)=1+\sum_{m\geq1}\sum_{y\in\partial\Lambda_m}\tau_p(0,y)$. Finiteness gives a boundary sum $a<1$. The [weighted BK boundary-splitting estimate](weighted-bk-boundary-splitting-estimate.md) then gives $g_k\leq a^{\lfloor k/m\rfloor}$. The strict bound $g_1=1-(1-p)^{2d}<1$ absorbs the finitely many smaller radii into a positive exponential rate with unit prefactor. At $p=0$, all positive-radius connection [probabilities](probability.md) are zero.
