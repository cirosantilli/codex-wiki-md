# Canonical form of a second-order partial differential equation

↑ **Parent:** [Linear partial differential equation](linear-partial-differential-equation.md)

For a second-order real [linear partial differential equation](linear-partial-differential-equation.md) in two variables, an invertible local [change of variables](change-of-variables-formula.md) simplifies its principal part. At regular points, the hyperbolic case has mixed-derivative principal part $U_{\xi\eta}$, the elliptic case has principal part $U_{\xi\xi}+U_{\eta\eta}$, and the parabolic case has rank-one principal part $U_{\xi\xi}$, after multiplication by nonzero factors. Lower-order terms and forcing must also be transformed with the [chain rule](chain-rule.md); simplifying only the principal part does not in general give the full canonical equation. In the [hyperbolic partial differential equation](hyperbolic-partial-differential-equation.md) case the new variables can be chosen constant along the two [characteristic](characteristic-of-a-field.md) families.
