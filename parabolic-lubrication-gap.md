# Parabolic lubrication gap

↑ **Parent:** [Lubrication theory](lubrication-theory.md)

Near the closest approach of a circle of radius $a$ to a wall, the gap is $h(x)=h_0+x^2/(2a)+O(x^4/a^3)$ and has streamwise scale $\sqrt{ah_0}$.
