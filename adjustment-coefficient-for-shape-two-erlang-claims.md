# Adjustment coefficient for shape-two Erlang claims

↑ **Parent:** [Adjustment coefficient](adjustment-coefficient.md)

For an [Erlang distribution](erlang-distribution.md) of shape two and rate $2/\beta$, the claim [expected value](expected-value.md) is $\beta$ and its [moment-generating function](moment-generating-function.md) is $(1-\beta r/2)^{-2}$ for $r<2/\beta$. The [adjustment coefficient](adjustment-coefficient.md) equation is $\lambda[(1-\beta R/2)^{-2}-1]=cR$. With $z=\beta R/2$, divide out the zero root to get $k(1-z)^2=2-z$. The smaller quadratic root lies in $(0,1)$ when $c>\lambda\beta$; the larger root lies beyond the finite-transform domain and must be rejected. This proves the displayed coefficient.
