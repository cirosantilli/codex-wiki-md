# Tit for tat

↑ **Parent:** [Reactive strategy (game theory)](reactive-strategy-game-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tit_for_tat)

[Tit for tat](tit-for-tat.md) cooperates initially and then repeats the opponent's previous action. Two such players cooperate forever without mistakes under this initial convention. It retaliates against defection and returns to cooperation after the opponent cooperates.
