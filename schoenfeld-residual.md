# Schoenfeld residual

↑ **Parent:** [Cox proportional-hazards model](cox-proportional-hazards-model.md)

At an untied event time, a Schoenfeld residual is the event subject's covariate vector minus its fitted risk-weighted average over the [risk set](risk-set.md). Under a correctly specified [Cox proportional-hazards model](cox-proportional-hazards-model.md), these residuals have no systematic mean trend with event time. They are defined at event times, unlike [Cox–Snell residuals](cox-snell-residual.md) which can be evaluated at both events and censoring times.
