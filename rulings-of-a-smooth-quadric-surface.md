# Rulings of a smooth quadric surface

↑ **Parent:** [Smooth quadric surface](smooth-quadric-surface.md)

Under $Q\cong\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1$, the fibres of the two [coordinate projections](coordinate-projection.md) are the two rulings of the [smooth quadric surface](smooth-quadric-surface.md). Two distinct fibres in one ruling are disjoint [projective lines](projective-line.md), while one fibre from each ruling meets in one point.
