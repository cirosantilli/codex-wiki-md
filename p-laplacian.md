# p-Laplacian

↑ **Parent:** [Quasilinear partial differential equation](quasilinear-partial-differential-equation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/p-Laplacian)

The p-Laplacian is the nonlinear divergence-form operator

$$
\Delta_pu=\operatorname{div}(|Du|^{p-2}Du).
$$

It is the [Euler-Lagrange operator](euler-lagrange-equation.md) of the [p-energy](p-energy.md). For $p>2$ it is [degenerately elliptic](degenerate-elliptic-operator.md) where $Du=0$.
