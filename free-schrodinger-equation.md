# Free Schrodinger equation

↑ **Parent:** [Schrödinger equation](schrodinger-equation.md)

The free Schrodinger equation describes linear dispersive evolution without a potential term. In this normalization a spectral mode is $e^{ikx-ik^2t}$. On a half-line, boundary data enter through the [global relation for the half-line free Schrodinger equation](global-relation-for-the-half-line-free-schrodinger-equation.md).
