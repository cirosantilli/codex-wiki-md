# Cyclic osmate ester

↑ **Parent:** [Dihydroxylation](dihydroxylation.md)

A [cyclic osmate ester](cyclic-osmate-ester.md) contains the newly formed [carbon](carbon.md)–[oxygen](oxygen.md) bonds of an osmium-mediated [alkene](alkene.md) [dihydroxylation](dihydroxylation.md). Both [oxygen](oxygen.md) atoms attach on the same face; hydrolytic cleavage of metal–[oxygen](oxygen.md) bonds releases the diol.
