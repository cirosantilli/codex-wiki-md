# Minimum-weight edge cover

↑ **Parent:** [Edge cover](edge-cover.md)

An [edge cover](edge-cover.md) minimizing the sum of selected [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) weights. For nonnegative weights and no [isolated vertex](isolated-vertex.md), it is computable in [polynomial time](polynomial-time.md) by the [minimum-weight edge cover by matching savings](minimum-weight-edge-cover-by-matching-savings.md) construction. It need not have minimum [cardinality](cardinality.md).
