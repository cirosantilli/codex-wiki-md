# Entropy power

↑ **Parent:** [Differential entropy](differential-entropy.md)

The [entropy power](entropy-power.md) of an $n$-dimensional [random vector](random-vector.md) with finite [differential entropy](differential-entropy.md) in bits is the [variance](variance-split.md) per coordinate of an isotropic [Gaussian distribution](normal-distribution.md) with the same [differential entropy](differential-entropy.md). The normalization makes $N(X)=\sigma^2$ when $X$ has [covariance matrix](covariance-matrix.md) $\sigma^2I_n$.
