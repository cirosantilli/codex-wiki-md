# Lyot coronagraph

↑ **Parent:** [Coronagraph](coronagraph.md)

A classical [Lyot coronagraph](lyot-coronagraph.md) first forms an image of a bright source on the [optical axis](optical-axis.md). A [coronagraphic focal plane mask](coronagraphic-focal-plane-mask.md) hides its core. Relay optics then form an image of the [entrance pupil](entrance-pupil.md), where a [Lyot stop](lyot-stop.md) blocks the outer rim and, when needed, borders around internal obstructions. Finally an [optical camera](optical-camera.md) forms the science image. The [focal plane](focal-plane.md) sorts incoming directions into image positions; the [optical pupil](optical-pupil.md) sorts contributions across the [aperture](aperture.md). This change of plane makes it possible to reject additional stellar [light](light.md) that survives the first mask. [Olivier Guyon, Coronagraph concepts & systems, 2014, PDF pages 6–8](https://subarutelescope.org/staff/guyon/15teaching.web/03AstrOptics2014.web/wdir.web/AstrOpt2014_20highcontrast.pdf).
