# Instantaneous mixing efficiency

↑ **Parent:** [Stratified mixing energy budget](stratified-mixing-energy-budget.md)

Instantaneous mixing efficiency is the fraction of instantaneous irreversible mechanical-energy loss that raises [background potential energy](background-potential-energy.md), after subtracting internal-to-potential exchange. The ratio $\Gamma=M/\epsilon$ is a different quantity, related by $\eta=\Gamma/(1+\Gamma)$. Replacing $M$ by buoyancy exchange $B$ additionally assumes negligible change of [available potential energy](available-potential-energy.md).
