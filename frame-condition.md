# Frame condition

↑ **Parent:** [Formal specification](formal-specification.md)

A clause stating which parts of a [program state](program-state.md) an operation preserves. A query's returned value alone does not prohibit it from also mutating the receiver; a binary update's result equation alone does not preserve its distinct operand. Explicit [frame conditions](frame-condition.md) rule out these behaviors and make substitution and composition arguments possible.
