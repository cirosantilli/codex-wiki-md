# Smooth nonnegative-curvature replacement of a flat disc is flat

↑ **Parent:** [Gauss-Bonnet theorem](gauss-bonnet-theorem.md)

If a flat surface disc is replaced by a smooth disc agreeing with the original surface outside the same boundary, their boundary [geodesic curvatures](geodesic-curvature.md) agree. [Gauss-Bonnet theorem](gauss-bonnet-theorem.md) therefore forces the replacement’s total [Gaussian curvature](gaussian-curvature.md) to be zero. If its curvature is everywhere nonnegative, [continuity](continuous-function.md) forces it to vanish everywhere. Thus a positive-curvature bulge cannot be glued this way without some negative curvature or a loss of smoothness.
