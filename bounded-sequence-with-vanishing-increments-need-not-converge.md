# Bounded sequence with vanishing increments need not converge

↑ **Parent:** [Convergent sequence](convergent-sequence.md)

The [sequence](sequence.md) is bounded and $|a_{n+1}-a_n|\le\sqrt{n+1}-\sqrt n\to0$ by the [mean value theorem](mean-value-theorem.md). Nevertheless, along the integer parts of $(\pi/2+2\pi j)^2$ its terms tend to one, and along the integer parts of $(3\pi/2+2\pi j)^2$ they tend to minus one. The [square root](square-root.md) rounding error is $O(1/j)$. Thus a [bounded sequence](bounded-sequence.md) with vanishing successive increments need not be a [convergent sequence](convergent-sequence.md).
