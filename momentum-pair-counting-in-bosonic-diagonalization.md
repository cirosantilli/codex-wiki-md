# Momentum-pair counting in bosonic diagonalization

↑ **Parent:** [Bosonic Bogoliubov diagonalization](bosonic-bogoliubov-diagonalization.md)

For a translation-invariant quadratic [bosonic](boson.md) [quantum field](quantum-field.md), anomalous terms couple $\mathbf k$ to $-\mathbf k$. A full-momentum sum uses half the pair-creation coefficient in each term, because both labels describe the same anomalous pair. After a canonical [Bogoliubov transformation](bogoliubov-transformation.md), however, the two members carry distinct quasiparticle occupations; each contributes its own oscillator energy. The vacuum correction is $\frac12\sum_{k\ne0}(E_k-A_k)$ in a full-zone sum. Switching to one representative from each pair requires changing both the occupation sum and constant consistently.
