# Bouguer gravity anomaly

↑ **Parent:** [Gravity anomaly](gravity-anomaly.md)

A [gravity anomaly](gravity-anomaly.md) after removing an approximate topographic [mass](mass.md) contribution, often an infinite slab $2\pi G\rho h$, in addition to free-air reduction. Terrain and spherical-curvature corrections refine the slab model.
