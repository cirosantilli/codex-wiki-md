# Standardization theorem for beta reduction

↑ **Parent:** [Normal-order beta reduction](normal-order-beta-reduction.md)

A finite [beta reduction](beta-reduction.md) can be rearranged into standard order, contracting earlier residual redexes before later ones. In particular, a [lambda term](lambda-term.md) with a [head normal form](head-normal-form.md) reaches one by head reduction, and a term with a [beta-normal form](beta-normal-form.md) reaches it by [normal-order beta reduction](normal-order-beta-reduction.md). Head reduction contracts the outermost redex on the left application spine, including under initial [lambda abstractions](lambda-abstraction.md).
