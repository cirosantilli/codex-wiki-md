# Marshmallow test

↑ **Parent:** [Delay of gratification](delay-of-gratification.md)

A task offering an immediate edible reward or a larger or preferred reward after waiting. Reward visibility, instructions, experimenter [reliability](measurement-reliability.md) and the maximum permitted waiting time affect its interpretation; a duration is not automatically a pure measure of self-control.
