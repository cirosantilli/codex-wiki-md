[https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.66.719](https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.66.719)

Physical Review Letters 66, 719–722. The abstract identifies end-grafted polydimethylsiloxane on porous [silicon dioxide](silicon-dioxide.md) and reports thickness proportional to $M\sigma$ in a [poor solvent](poor-solvent.md) and $M\sigma^{1/3}$ in a [good solvent](good-solvent.md). The chemical system in that abstract differs from the [polystyrene](polystyrene.md) wording of the 2016 exam; the scaling comparison is unaffected.
