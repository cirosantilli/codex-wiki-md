# Morphism of affine varieties

↑ **Parent:** [Affine algebraic set](affine-algebraic-set.md)

A map between [affine varieties](affine-algebraic-set.md) is a morphism when its coordinate functions are restrictions of ambient [polynomials](polynomial-split.md). Pullback identifies these morphisms contravariantly with homomorphisms of their [coordinate rings](coordinate-ring.md).
