# Log-cosh obstacle variational inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Obstacle problem](obstacle-problem.md)

Minimizing the log-cosh energy over [zero-trace](zero-boundary-sobolev-space.md) [functions](function-split.md) above an obstacle yields the displayed inequality for every feasible competitor. [Convexity](convex-function.md) supplies the one-sided admissible segments. On the open noncontact region, compactly supported variations of both signs are feasible because the gap from the obstacle has a positive minimum on their support. Their [first variations](first-variation.md) vanish, giving $-\Delta u+\tanh u=0$ there. This stationarity equation need not hold on the contact [set](set-split.md).
