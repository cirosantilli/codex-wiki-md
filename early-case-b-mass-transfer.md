# Early case B mass transfer

↑ **Parent:** [Case B mass transfer](case-b-mass-transfer.md)

Early case B overflow occurs in an early post-main-sequence donor, commonly crossing the Hertzsprung gap with a largely radiative envelope. A stabilizing mass-loss response can allow transfer at [binary mass ratios](binary-mass-ratio.md) that would destabilize a convective giant. This is an evolutionary-stage classification, distinct from recombination terminology.
