# Diffraction limit of a telescope

↑ **Parent:** [Optical telescope](optical-telescope.md)

The angular [diffraction-limited resolution](diffraction-limited-resolution.md) of a telescope scales as $\lambda/D$, with [wavelength](wavelength.md) $\lambda$ and illuminated diameter $D$. Larger diameter resolves smaller angular structure, while longer wavelength makes atmospheric phase correction easier.
