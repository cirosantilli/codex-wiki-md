# Finite group of prime exponent has prime-power order

↑ **Parent:** [Exponent of a finite group](exponent-of-a-finite-group.md)

If every nonidentity element of a [finite group](finite-group.md) has order equal to a prime $p$, its order is a power of $p$. For any prime divisor $q$ of the [group](group-split.md) order, [Cauchy theorem for groups](cauchy-theorem-for-groups.md) gives an element of order $q$, forcing $q=p$. This statement does not imply that the [group](group-split.md) is abelian; odd-characteristic [upper unitriangular groups](upper-unitriangular-group.md) provide counterexamples.
