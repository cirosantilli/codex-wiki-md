# Open-label placebo

↑ **Parent:** [Placebo effect](placebo-effect.md)

An intervention transparently described as inert rather than concealed as an active medication. Trials require an appropriate comparator and must address unblinded reporting, explanatory rationale and duration; an effect for one symptomatic outcome is not a universal disease-modifying benefit.[PLoS ONE 5, e15591; 80-patient three-week randomized open-label placebo trial and Limitations](placebos-without-deception-a-randomized-controlled-trial-in-irritable-bowel-syndrome-by-ted-j-kaptchuk-and-others.md)
