# 3-adic number

↑ **Parent:** [P-adic number](p-adic-number.md)

A 3-adic number belongs to the completion of $\mathbb Q$ for the [p-adic absolute value](p-adic-absolute-value.md) with prime three. Its nonzero elements have the form $3^k u$, with $k\in\mathbb Z$ and $u\in\mathbb Z_3^\times$. The [cube-class group of the 3-adic numbers](cube-class-group-of-the-3-adic-numbers.md) is generated by the classes of $3$ and $4$.
