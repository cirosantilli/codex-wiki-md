# Identity tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor type](tensor-type.md)

The identity tensor is the canonical type-$(1,1)$ tensor that maps each tangent vector to itself. It pairs a covector $\eta$ and vector $V$ as $\eta(V)$, and its components are the [Kronecker delta](kronecker-delta.md) $\delta^\mu{}_{\nu}$ in every coordinate system.
