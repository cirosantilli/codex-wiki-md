# Nonregular extremal epimorphism in the category of categories

↑ **Parent:** [Extremal epimorphism](extremal-epimorphism.md)

Map two disjoint walking arrows to the two mutually inverse arrows of the walking isomorphism. Their images generate the entire target, so the [functor](functor.md) is an [extremal epimorphism](extremal-epimorphism.md). Its [kernel pair](kernel-pair.md) only identifies the matching endpoint objects, not the inverse-composition relations. Mapping both arrows to a nonidentity idempotent in a one-object [category](category-split.md) equalizes that kernel pair but cannot factor through the target groupoid. Thus the functor is not a [regular epimorphism](regular-epimorphism.md), although the category of small categories has [finite limits](finite-limit.md).
