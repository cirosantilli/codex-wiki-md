# Siegbahn photoelectron spectroscopy experiments

↑ **Parent:** [X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy](x-ray-photoelectron-spectroscopy.md)

[Kai Siegbahn](kai-manne-borje-siegbahn.md)'s early instrument used iron-free magnetic double focusing and a thin-window [Geiger-Müller counter](geiger-counter.md). His later instrument, compatible with ultra-high [vacuum](vacuum.md), used a spherical electrostatic [electron energy analyzer](electron-energy-analyzer.md) and monochromatic [aluminum](aluminium.md) [X-rays](x-ray.md). [Argon](argon.md) mixed with [methane](methane.md) supplied simultaneous $2p$ reference lines.[pp. 65–66, 82–84; Figs. 16–17](electron-spectroscopy-for-atoms-molecules-and-condensed-matter-by-kai-manne-borje-siegbahn.md)

Two [sulfur](sulfur.md) lines in [sodium thiosulfate](sodium-thiosulfate.md) distinguished chemical environments within one compound. A separate [gold](gold.md) comparison showed a fingerprint obscuring substrate lines.[pp. 67–69, Fig. 2; thiosulfate comparison](electron-spectroscopy-for-atoms-molecules-and-condensed-matter-by-kai-manne-borje-siegbahn.md)

For an unknown sample, first document its preparation and transfer, then compare survey and detailed scans under reproducible illumination, contact and acquisition conditions. Repeat a region to test change during exposure. If a cleaning treatment is used, compare before and after rather than assuming that cleaning preserves the original [chemical bonds](chemical-bond-split.md). Report the [photoelectron binding energy reference](photoelectron-binding-energy-reference.md), reference lines, charge-compensation conditions and instrumental settings with the inferred [chemical shift in photoelectron spectroscopy](chemical-shift-in-photoelectron-spectroscopy.md). A chemical interpretation should survive these checks as well as explain the line separation.
