# Rectangle crossing in bond percolation

↑ **Parent:** [Bond percolation](bond-percolation-split.md)

For the induced [square lattice](square-lattice.md) subgraph in $[0,m]\times[0,n]$, $h_p(m,n)$ is the [probability](probability.md) of an open [graph path](path-in-a-graph.md) from its left boundary to its right boundary under independent [bond percolation](bond-percolation-split.md) of density $p$. Width and height count lattice spacings. [Dual bond percolation](dual-bond-percolation.md) exchanges failure with a top-to-bottom dual crossing of dimensions $(m-1)\times(n+1)$, with the usual exterior boundary vertices.
