# Extinction selectivity

↑ **Parent:** [Extinction](extinction.md)

Systematic variation in [extinction](extinction.md) probability with biological or ecological traits. Associations can change between events and taxonomic levels: geographic range, [body size](body-size.md) and [sexual dimorphism](sexual-dimorphism.md) have each been linked to survival differences in particular datasets, but none is a universal predictor across all [mass extinctions](mass-extinction.md).
