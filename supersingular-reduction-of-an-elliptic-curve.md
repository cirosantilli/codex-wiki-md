# Supersingular reduction of an elliptic curve

↑ **Parent:** [Good reduction of an elliptic curve](good-reduction-of-an-elliptic-curve.md)

A good reduction in characteristic $p$ is supersingular when its geometric $p$-torsion is trivial, equivalently its [formal group of an elliptic curve](formal-group-of-an-elliptic-curve.md) has height two. Good inert primes of a [CM elliptic curve](cm-elliptic-curve.md) are supersingular. The ordinary bounded-measure and torsion-Selmer formulation must then be modified, for instance using signed local conditions in the cyclotomic tower.
