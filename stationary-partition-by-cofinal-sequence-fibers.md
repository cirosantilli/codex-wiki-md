# Stationary partition by cofinal-sequence fibers

↑ **Parent:** [Stationary set](stationary-set.md)

For an uncountable [regular cardinal](regular-cardinal.md) $\kappa$, choose cofinal sequences $c_\alpha:\omega\to\alpha$ for the [stationary set](stationary-set.md) of [ordinals](ordinal.md) of [cofinality](cofinality.md) $\omega$. Above any bound, some fixed coordinate exceeds the bound on a stationary [subset](subset.md); [Fodor lemma](fodor-lemma.md) makes that coordinate constant on a stationary [subset](subset.md). Thus the stationary constant fibers, over all coordinates, have unboundedly many values. Regularity makes one coordinate have $\kappa$ such values. Its fibers are disjoint [stationary sets](stationary-set.md); adding all leftover [ordinals](ordinal.md) to one piece partitions $\kappa$.
