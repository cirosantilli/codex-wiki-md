# Unit index of a CM-field

↑ **Parent:** [CM-field](cm-field.md)

For a [CM-field](cm-field.md), put $c$ for conjugation and $E_K=\mathcal O_K^\times$. The map $u\mapsto u/c(u)$ takes values in the cyclic group $\mu_K$ of [roots of unity](root-of-unity.md): all conjugates have absolute value one, and the [Kronecker theorem on algebraic integers in the unit disk](kronecker-theorem-on-algebraic-integers-in-the-unit-disk.md) applies. Its kernel is $E_{K^+}$, while its image on $\mu_K$ is $\mu_K^2$. It therefore embeds $E_K/(E_{K^+}\mu_K)$ into $\mu_K/\mu_K^2$, a group of order two. This proves the index bound without assuming that every unit is a real unit times a root of unity.
