# Elliptic characteristics of a planar transport equation

↑ **Parent:** [Method of characteristics](method-of-characteristics.md)

For the [linear partial differential equation](linear-partial-differential-equation.md) $2y u_x-xu_y=0$, the [method of characteristics](method-of-characteristics.md) gives $\dot x=2y$, $\dot y=-x$, $\dot u=0$. Thus $\phi=x^2+2y^2$ is constant on each [characteristic curve](characteristic-curve.md), whose nonzero level sets are [ellipses](ellipse.md). On a domain containing entire such [ellipses](ellipse.md), the solutions have the form $u=F(\phi)$, subject to the desired regularity at the origin.

For the inhomogeneous equation $2yu_x-xu_y=xy(2y^2-x^2)$, a [particular solution](particular-solution.md) is $u_p=x^2y^2/2$, so the [general solution](general-solution.md) is $u=x^2y^2/2+F(x^2+2y^2)$. Prescribing $u(x,1)=x^2$ fixes $F(s)=(s-2)/2$ only for $s\geq2$. The inner [ellipses](ellipse.md) $s<2$ never intersect that data line, so the boundary values leave their solution values undetermined. Smooth extensions must match the prescribed $F$ at $s=2$; choosing the same linear formula everywhere gives one polynomial extension.
