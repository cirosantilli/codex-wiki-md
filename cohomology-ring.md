# Cohomology ring

↑ **Parent:** [Cup product](cup-product.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cohomology_ring)

The cohomology ring is the graded ring whose multiplication is the [cup product](cup-product.md). It records intersection information that the graded cohomology groups alone do not detect.
