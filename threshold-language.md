# Threshold language

↑ **Parent:** [Recursive language](recursive-language.md)

For a fixed [natural number](natural-number.md) $a$ and a standard encoding of [natural numbers](natural-number.md), validate the [input](input-computer-science.md) and compare its encoded value with $a$. This decides $L_a$. A [well-ordering](well-order.md) argument can prove that an upward-closed [set](set-split.md) of [natural numbers](natural-number.md) has this form without calculating its least element. A decider may contain that fixed least element in its finite description; its existence does not give a uniform method to discover the least element from another program.
