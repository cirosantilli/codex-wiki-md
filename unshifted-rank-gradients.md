# Unshifted rank gradients

↑ **Parent:** [Finitely generated group](finitely-generated-group.md)

These upper and lower quantities use $d(H)$, without subtracting one. For a [free group](free-group.md) $F_n$ with $n\ge1$, the [Nielsen–Schreier formula](nielsen-schreier-formula.md) gives $d(H)/[F_n:H]=n-1+1/[F_n:H]$. The upper value is $n$, attained at the whole group, and the lower value is $n-1$, approached by kernels of maps onto cyclic groups of arbitrarily large finite order. The frequently used shifted rank gradient replaces $d(H)$ by $d(H)-1$ and therefore gives different upper values.
