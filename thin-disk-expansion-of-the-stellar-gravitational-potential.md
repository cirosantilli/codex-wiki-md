# Thin-disk expansion of the stellar gravitational potential

↑ **Parent:** [Thin disk](thin-disk.md)

At fixed [cylindrical radius](cylindrical-radius.md) $R$ with $|z|\ll R$, the central [gravitational potential](newtonian-potential-of-a-point-mass.md) is harmonic in height. The [vertical epicyclic frequency](vertical-epicyclic-frequency.md) equals $\Omega_K$ in this spherical potential.
