# Alternating Lie bracket

↑ **Parent:** [Lie bracket](lie-bracket.md)

A [Lie bracket](lie-bracket.md) is alternating when $[x,x]=0$ for every $x$. This condition is imposed in every [characteristic of a field](characteristic-of-a-field.md). Bilinearity gives $0=[x+y,x+y]=[x,y]+[y,x]$, hence [antisymmetry of a Lie bracket](antisymmetry-of-a-lie-bracket.md). Requiring alternation directly keeps the definition correct in [characteristic two](characteristic-two.md).
