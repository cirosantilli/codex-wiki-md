# Telegraphy

↑ **Parent:** [Telecommunication](telecommunication.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telegraphy)

Telegraphy represents written symbols by agreed sequences of distinguishable [signals](signal.md). In [wireless telegraphy](wireless-telegraphy.md), a key can select short and long transmission intervals; a receiver must recover their timing, rather than reproduce every rapid electrical [oscillation](oscillation.md) as a visible mark.
