# Aerobic respiration

↑ **Parent:** [Cellular respiration](cellular-respiration.md)

[Aerobic respiration](aerobic-respiration.md) uses [molecular oxygen](molecular-oxygen.md) as the final [electron acceptor](electron-acceptor.md). For complete [oxidation](oxidation.md) of [glucose](glucose.md), the net material balance is

$$
\mathrm{C}_6\mathrm{H}_{12}\mathrm{O}_6+6\mathrm{O}_2\longrightarrow6\mathrm{CO}_2+6\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O}.
$$

This records the overall conversion of [glucose](glucose.md), [molecular oxygen](molecular-oxygen.md), [carbon dioxide](carbon-dioxide.md) and [water](water.md); it is not one [enzyme](enzyme-split.md)-catalyzed step, and it omits the separate [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) accounting. For an original [stoichiometry](stoichiometry.md) check, each side contains six [carbon](carbon.md), twelve [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) and eighteen [oxygen](oxygen.md) [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md). If exactly $1\,\mathrm{mmol}$ of [glucose](glucose.md) undergoes this complete conversion, the equation requires $6\,\mathrm{mmol}$ of [molecular oxygen](molecular-oxygen.md) and yields $6\,\mathrm{mmol}$ each of [carbon dioxide](carbon-dioxide.md) and net [water](water.md). This is an equation-based calculation, not an observed seed measurement.

The [molecular oxygen](molecular-oxygen.md) is reduced to [water](water.md), rather than being the source of the [carbon dioxide](carbon-dioxide.md)'s [carbon](carbon.md). A formal [redox electron balance](redox-electron-balance.md), using acidic half-reaction notation, makes the roles explicit:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{C}_6\mathrm{H}_{12}\mathrm{O}_6+6\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O}&\longrightarrow6\mathrm{CO}_2+24\mathrm{H}^{+}+24e^{-},\\
6\mathrm{O}_2+24\mathrm{H}^{+}+24e^{-}&\longrightarrow12\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O}.
\end{aligned}
$$

Adding and cancelling gives the net equation above. Both half-reactions conserve [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) and [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md). The $24$ [electrons](electron.md) balance a net transfer; they are carried through cellular intermediates, rather than released as a pool of free [electrons](electron.md). The final respiratory [reduction](reduction-chemistry.md) can be written $\mathrm{O}_2+4\mathrm{H}^{+}+4e^{-}\longrightarrow2\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O}$. These equations are bookkeeping, not a tracing experiment that assigns every product [oxygen](oxygen.md) [chemical atom](atom-physics.md) to its original [molecule](molecule-split.md).[Biology 2e, §7.4, Electron Transport Chain, Figure 7.12: oxygen reduction to water](biology-2e-by-mary-ann-clark-and-others.md)
