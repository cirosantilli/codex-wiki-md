# Protein aggregation

↑ **Parent:** [Denaturation (biochemistry)](denaturation-biochemistry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protein_aggregation)

[Protein aggregation](protein-aggregation.md) is the association of [proteins](protein.md) into clusters, often involving exposed regions of partly folded or unfolded [polypeptide chains](polypeptide.md). It can prevent recovery of normal function while leaving the [protein primary structures](protein-primary-structure.md) intact. It is distinct from both unfolding one chain and the functional assembly described by [protein quaternary structure](protein-quaternary-structure.md). See [Cooper: partly folded chains, aggregation and chaperone protection](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK9843/).
