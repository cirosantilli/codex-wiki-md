# Unitriangular group

↑ **Parent:** [General linear group](general-linear-group.md)

The [unitriangular group](unitriangular-group.md) $\operatorname{UT}_n(F)$ consists of [upper triangular matrices](upper-triangular-matrix.md) over a [field](field.md) $F$ with every diagonal entry equal to one. Every such [matrix](matrix.md) is $I_n+N$ for a [strictly upper triangular matrix](strictly-upper-triangular-matrix.md) $N$. Products have the same form, and $N^n=0$ gives the inverse

$$
(I_n+N)^{-1}=I_n-N+N^2-\cdots+(-1)^{n-1}N^{n-1}.
$$

Thus these [matrices](matrix.md) form a [subgroup](subgroup.md) of the [general linear group](general-linear-group.md). The case $\operatorname{UT}_3(\mathbb F_3)$ is noncommutative but every element has cube equal to the [identity matrix](identity-matrix.md): $(I_3+N)^3=I_3$ because $N^3=0$ and $3=0$ in the [finite field](finite-field.md) $\mathbb F_3$.
