# Spectral leakage

↑ **Parent:** [Window function](window-function.md)

Multiplying a measured [signal](signal.md) by a finite-duration [window function](window-function.md) convolves its [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md) with the transform of the window. A spectrally sharp component therefore contributes to nearby frequencies rather than remaining a single ideal line. Smooth tapering can reduce distant sidelobes at the cost of widening the main spectral feature. This finite-record effect is distinct from [aliasing](aliasing.md), which superposes copies of the spectrum because of time sampling.
