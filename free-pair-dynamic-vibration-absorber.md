# Free-pair dynamic vibration absorber

↑ **Parent:** [Coupled oscillator](coupled-oscillator.md)

A free mass $M$ driven by $F_0\cos\omega t$ can be coupled by a single [spring](spring.md) of stiffness $k$ to an auxiliary free mass $m$. With no wall attachment or damping, the periodic displacement amplitude of $M$ is $A=F_0(k-m\omega^2)/\{\omega^2[Mm\omega^2-k(M+m)]\}$. Tuning $k=m\omega^2$ gives [antiresonance](antiresonance.md): $A=0$ while the auxiliary mass has signed amplitude $-F_0/k$, supplying an exactly cancelling spring force. A nearby pole occurs at $k=Mm\omega^2/(M+m)$. For relative tuning error $\epsilon=(k-m\omega^2)/(m\omega^2)$ sufficiently small, the primary amplitude relative to its free response grows as $(M/m)|\epsilon|$. A light absorber is therefore narrowband and may require large auxiliary travel. This cancellation dissipates no energy, and undamped free modes persist unless excluded by initial conditions.
