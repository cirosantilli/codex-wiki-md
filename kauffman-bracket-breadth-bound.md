# Kauffman bracket breadth bound

↑ **Parent:** [Kauffman bracket](kauffman-bracket.md)

A state with $k$ B smoothings has at most $s_A+k$ [circles](circle.md), so its largest A exponent is at most $c(D)+2s_A-2$. The dual argument bounds the smallest exponent below by $-c(D)-2s_B+2$. For a connected [link diagram](link-diagram.md), the [Turaev surface](turaev-surface.md) inequality bounds the resulting breadth by $4c(D)$. For a connected [reduced alternating diagram](reduced-alternating-link-diagram.md), adequacy prevents cancellation at both extremes and planar [Euler characteristic](euler-characteristic.md) gives equality. A disconnected projection needs the additional term in the [Jones polynomial breadth bound for a disconnected diagram](jones-polynomial-breadth-bound-for-a-disconnected-diagram.md).
