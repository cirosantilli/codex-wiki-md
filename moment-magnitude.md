# Moment magnitude

↑ **Parent:** [Earthquake](earthquake.md)

A logarithmic measure of [earthquake](earthquake.md) size based on [seismic moment](seismic-moment.md). A commonly used SI relation is $M_w=\frac23[\log_{10}(M_0/(\mathrm{N\,m}))-9.1]$. Because [seismic moment](seismic-moment.md) integrates [fault slip](fault-slip.md) over the [fault plane](fault-plane.md) and includes the [shear modulus](shear-modulus.md), a peak slip value cannot replace the area integral.
