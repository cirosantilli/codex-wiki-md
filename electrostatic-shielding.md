# Electrostatic shielding

↑ **Parent:** [Conductor in electrostatic equilibrium](conductor-in-electrostatic-equilibrium.md)

[Electrostatic shielding](electrostatic-shielding.md) decouples interior and exterior [electric fields](electric-field.md) using a [conductor in electrostatic equilibrium](conductor-in-electrostatic-equilibrium.md). Exterior sources cannot produce an [electric field](electric-field.md) in an empty closed [cavity in a conductor](cavity-in-a-conductor.md), whether grounded or isolated. Outward independence requires grounding: internal sources can affect an isolated enclosure's exterior, as in [Faraday's ice pail experiment](faraday-s-ice-pail-experiment.md). Grounding makes the exterior solution independent of internal sources, while allowing exterior sources' own response. Openings and changing [electromagnetic fields](electromagnetic-field.md) need additional analysis.[II.5, §5–10, concluding paragraphs on inward shielding and closed grounded enclosures](the-feynman-lectures-on-physics-volume-ii-by-richard-feynman-and-others.md)
