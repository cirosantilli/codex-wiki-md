# Holomorphic gauge coupling

↑ **Parent:** [Gauge kinetic function](gauge-kinetic-function.md)

A holomorphic gauge coupling is the coefficient of the gauge kinetic [F-term](f-term.md) in a local [Wilsonian effective action](wilsonian-effective-action.md) written in holomorphic variables. In a common normalization its real part is $1/g_h^2$. The continuous perturbative shift of its topological angle, together with holomorphy, restricts its beta function to one-loop order. [Wave-function renormalization](wave-function-renormalization.md) and anomalous canonical field rescalings distinguish it from the physical gauge coupling.
