# Pure force

↑ **Parent:** [Four-force](four-force.md)

A [four-force](four-force.md) preserving the particle's [rest mass](invariant-mass.md). For a massive particle, $P=mU$ and $U^2=c^2$ in the mostly-minus convention imply $F\cdot U=c^2dm/d\tau$. Thus constant mass is equivalent to orthogonality of the force and [four-velocity](four-velocity.md). [Energy](energy-split.md) transfer in a moving frame is allowed even though [rest mass](invariant-mass.md) does not change.
