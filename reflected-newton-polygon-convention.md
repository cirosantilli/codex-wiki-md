# Reflected Newton polygon convention

↑ **Parent:** [Newton polygon](newton-polygon.md)

For a degree-$d$ [polynomial](polynomial-split.md), reflecting the usual coefficient-index axis replaces $(j,v(a_j))$ by $(d-j,v(a_j))$. The reflected lower polygon has slopes equal to root [valuations](valuation.md), rather than their negatives. Horizontal lengths, and therefore multiplicities, are unchanged. This convention makes positive lower [ramification groups](ramification-group.md) breaks appear as positive slopes of a reduced ramification polygon.
