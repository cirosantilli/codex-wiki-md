# Automorphy character of an orbit Blaschke product

↑ **Parent:** [Blaschke product](blaschke-product.md)

Let a [group](group-split.md) $G$ of [Möbius transformations](mobius-transformation.md) of the [unit disk](unit-disk.md) have an orbit satisfying the [Blaschke condition](blaschke-condition.md), counted once per distinct orbit point. For its [Blaschke product](blaschke-product.md) $B$, invariance of the [pseudohyperbolic distance](pseudohyperbolic-distance.md) and permutation of the orbit give $|B(Tz)|=|B(z)|$. The ratio $B(Tz)/B(z)$ extends across its zeros and has modulus one, so the [open mapping theorem](open-mapping-theorem-complex-analysis.md) makes it a constant $\chi(T)$. Composition gives a [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md) $\chi:G\to\{\lambda:|\lambda|=1\}$. It is essential to compare moduli of the products: two arbitrary bounded [holomorphic functions](holomorphic-function.md) with identical zeros need not have constant quotient.
