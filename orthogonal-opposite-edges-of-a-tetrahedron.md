# Orthogonal opposite edges of a tetrahedron

↑ **Parent:** [Opposite edges of a tetrahedron](opposite-edges-of-a-tetrahedron.md)

Place one vertex of a [tetrahedron](tetrahedron.md) at the origin and denote the other vertex vectors by $a,b,c$. Orthogonality of two pairs of [opposite edges of a tetrahedron](opposite-edges-of-a-tetrahedron.md) forces $a\cdot b=a\cdot c=b\cdot c$. It follows that the third pair is orthogonal too. The three sums of squared lengths of opposite edges all equal $|a|^2+|b|^2+|c|^2-2a\cdot b$. This gives a [dot product](dot-product.md) test for the special geometry without solving for the face planes.
