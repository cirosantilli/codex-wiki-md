# Fundamental theorem of linear programming

↑ **Parent:** [Basic feasible solution](basic-feasible-solution.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fundamental_theorem_of_linear_programming)

If a linear program has an optimal solution, it has an optimal [basic feasible solution](basic-feasible-solution.md). Choose an optimum with minimal positive support. A dependence among its active columns gives a feasible two-sided perturbation; optimality makes its objective slope zero, and moving until one coordinate vanishes contradicts minimality.
