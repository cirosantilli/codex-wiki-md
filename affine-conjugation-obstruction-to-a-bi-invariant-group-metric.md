# Affine conjugation obstruction to a bi-invariant group metric

↑ **Parent:** [Bi-invariant group metric](bi-invariant-group-metric.md)

In the [orientation-preserving affine group of the real line](orientation-preserving-affine-group-of-the-real-line.md), translations $T_{1/n}$ converge to the [identity element](identity-element.md), but [conjugation](conjugation.md) by dilation $D_n$ sends each one to $T_1$. A compatible [bi-invariant group metric](bi-invariant-group-metric.md) would give $d(e,T_{1/n})=d(e,T_1)>0$ for every $n$, contradicting convergence. The group is nonetheless metrizable in its ordinary two real coordinates.
