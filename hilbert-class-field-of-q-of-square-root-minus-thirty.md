# Hilbert class field of Q of square root minus thirty

↑ **Parent:** [Hilbert class field](hilbert-class-field.md)

The [ideal class group](ideal-class-group.md) of $\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-30})$ is $C_2\times C_2$. Its four reduced [binary quadratic forms](binary-quadratic-form.md) have coefficients $(1,0,30)$, $(2,0,15)$, $(3,0,10)$ and $(5,0,6)$. The displayed [multiquadratic field](multiquadratic-field.md) has degree four over the base. The seven quadratic-subfield discriminants are $8,-3,5,-24,40,-15,-120$, whose product in [absolute value](absolute-value.md) is $120^4$. The [conductor-discriminant formula](conductor-discriminant-formula.md) and discriminant tower formula imply that the [relative discriminant](relative-discriminant.md) over $\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-30})$ is the [unit ideal](unit-ideal.md). This unramified abelian degree-four extension is therefore its [Hilbert class field](hilbert-class-field.md).
