# Magnetic anomaly

↑ **Parent:** [Palaeomagnetism](palaeomagnetism.md)

A measured [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) component or magnitude after subtraction of a reference field. Source geometry, [magnetization](magnetization.md) direction and observation height affect its sign and amplitude; an anomaly is not a direct map of rock polarity.
