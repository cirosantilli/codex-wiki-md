# Negative Sobolev regularity of a compactly supported distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Compactly supported distribution](compactly-supported-distribution.md)

A [compactly supported distribution](compactly-supported-distribution.md) of [order of a distribution](order-of-a-distribution.md) at most $M$ has a [smooth function](smooth-function.md) as its [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md), bounded by $C\langle\xi\rangle^M$. It therefore belongs to every [Sobolev space](sobolev-space-split.md) $H^s$ with $s<-M-n/2$.
