# Metacognitive calibration

↑ **Parent:** [Subjective confidence](subjective-confidence.md)

For correctness [indicator function](indicator-function.md) $Y$ and stated probability $C$, ideal calibration means that answers assigned confidence $c$ are correct with conditional probability $c$. Overall overconfidence is $\mathbb E[C-Y]>0$; a zero overall difference does not establish calibration at every confidence level. Estimated calibration depends on sample size and how reports are grouped.
