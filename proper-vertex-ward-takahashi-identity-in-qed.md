# Proper-vertex Ward-Takahashi identity in QED

↑ **Parent:** [Ward identity](ward-identity.md)

Define the proper electromagnetic vertex with the [electric charge](electric-charge.md) removed, and define $S^{-1}$ without the propagator's overall factor $i$. The displayed off-shell identity follows by localizing the vector symmetry in a time-ordered correlation function, including the contact terms at the charged-field insertions, and amputating. Its on-shell form gives the [Ward identity](ward-identity.md) for physical [photon](photon.md) amplitudes. At zero [photon](photon.md) momentum it identifies the vertex with the momentum derivative of the inverse [fermion](fermion.md) propagator.
