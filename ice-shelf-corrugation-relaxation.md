# Ice-shelf corrugation relaxation

↑ **Parent:** [Ice shelf](ice-shelf.md)

Short-wavelength corrugations on the underside of a highly viscous ice shelf relax through local [Stokes flow](stokes-flow-split.md). For a wavelength much smaller than the shelf thickness, the deformation occupies a depth of order the inverse [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) and the ice may be treated as a viscous half-space.
