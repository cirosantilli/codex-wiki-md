# Instantaneous stationary lava-crust thickness

↑ **Parent:** [Radiogenically heated lava-lake crust model](radiogenically-heated-lava-lake-crust-model.md)

In the [radiogenically heated lava-lake crust model](radiogenically-heated-lava-lake-crust-model.md), the thickness [derivative](derivative.md) vanishes at $h_e=1/(B\theta)$ when the liquid superheat $\theta$ is positive. This moving curve balances [thermal conduction](thermal-conduction.md) and convective [heat flux](heat-flux-density.md); it is not itself the evolving crust. Cooling raises the curve and keeps an initially absent crust below it. Warming lowers the curve, allowing [lava-crust overshoot under warming](lava-crust-overshoot-under-warming.md). At zero superheat the stationary thickness is infinite, so its finite formula applies only after positive superheat develops.
