# Quantum pulse area

↑ **Parent:** [Resonant quantum control](resonant-quantum-control.md)

For a fixed dimensionless [Hermitian operator](hermitian-operator.md) $A$ driven by $H(t)=\Omega(t)A$, all instantaneous generators commute. The exact [unitary operator](unitary-operator.md) is $U(T)=\exp(-i\theta A)$, so the area, rather than the detailed envelope, fixes the rotation. For $A^2=I$, it is $\cos\theta\,I-i\sin\theta\,A$. This convention uses the [Quantum Hamiltonian](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) coefficient directly; a convention with $\hbar\Omega_R A/2$ instead has $\theta=\tfrac12\int\Omega_Rdt$.
