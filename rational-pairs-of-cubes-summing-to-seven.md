# Rational pairs of cubes summing to seven

↑ **Parent:** [Diagonal cubic elliptic curve](diagonal-cubic-elliptic-curve.md)

There are infinitely many rational pairs $(u,v)$ with $u^3+v^3=7$. The corresponding [Weierstrass equation of an elliptic curve](weierstrass-equation-of-an-elliptic-curve.md) is $y^2=x^3-21168$, and $(u,v)=(2,-1)$ maps to $P=(84,756)$. The primes $5$ and $11$ are good, with point counts $6$ and $12$ because cubing is bijective in their residue fields. [Reduction of torsion points on an elliptic curve](reduction-of-torsion-points-on-an-elliptic-curve.md) bounds rational torsion by order dividing $6$. The [flexes of a diagonal cubic](flexes-of-a-diagonal-cubic.md) exclude three-torsion for $d=-7$, while $d/2=-7/2$ is not a cube and hence excludes two-torsion. Thus $P$ has infinite order.

All nonzero multiples of $P$ map to distinct rational affine pairs, since the only rational point at infinity of the diagonal cubic is its identity. The inverse map is $u=(y+252)/(6x)$ and $v=(252-y)/(6x)$.
