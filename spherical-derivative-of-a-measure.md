# Spherical derivative of a measure

↑ **Parent:** [Measure density](measure-density.md)

The spherical [derivative](derivative.md) compares the mass of shrinking centered [open balls](open-ball.md) with their [Lebesgue measure](lebesgue-measure.md), when the limit exists. This is a density of one [measure](measure.md) relative to Euclidean volume, rather than the relative density of a set within one fixed [measure](measure.md). A singular finite [Borel measure](borel-measure.md) has spherical [derivative](derivative.md) zero [Lebesgue almost everywhere](almost-everywhere.md).
