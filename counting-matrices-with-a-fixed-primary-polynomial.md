# Counting matrices with a fixed primary polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Primary matrix centralizer formula](primary-matrix-centralizer-formula.md)

Fix an irreducible polynomial f of degree d with nonzero constant term. In $GL_{dm}(q)$, the number of matrices whose minimal polynomial is a power of f is $|GL_{dm}(q)|q^{d(m^2-m)}/|GL_m(q^d)|$. The [primary matrix centralizer formula](primary-matrix-centralizer-formula.md) is identical to the unipotent centralizer formula over $F_{q^d}$, and summing reciprocal centralizer orders reduces to [counting nilpotent matrices by the Fitting decomposition](counting-nilpotent-matrices-by-the-fitting-decomposition.md).
