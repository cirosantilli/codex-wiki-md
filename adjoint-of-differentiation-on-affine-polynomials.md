# Adjoint of differentiation on affine polynomials

↑ **Parent:** [Adjoint operator](adjoint-operator.md)

Give the real [vector space](vector-space-split.md) of degree-at-most-one [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) the [inner product](inner-product-split.md) $\langle f,g\rangle=\int_{-1}^1fg$. In coordinates $f=a+bt$, $g=c+dt$, this is $2ac+2bd/3$. Since $Df=b$, the adjoint identity requires $2bc=\langle a+bt,D^*(c+dt)\rangle$ for every $a,b$. Comparing the two coefficients yields the displayed formula. The [adjoint operator](adjoint-operator.md) depends on both the inner product and the domain; a formal integration-by-parts sign without the boundary term would give the wrong answer.
