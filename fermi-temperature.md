# Fermi temperature

↑ **Parent:** [Fermi energy](fermi-energy.md)

The Fermi temperature expresses a [Fermi energy](fermi-energy.md) as a [temperature](temperature-split.md) scale, $T_F=E_F/k_B$, where $k_B$ is the [Boltzmann constant](boltzmann-constant.md). In the convention $E_F>0$ measured from the relevant band minimum, $T\ll T_F$ is the regime of a strongly degenerate [Fermi gas](fermi-gas.md). For an interacting [Fermi liquid](fermi-liquid-split.md), an effective Fermi scale can be introduced through its quasiparticle dispersion; its convention should be specified.
