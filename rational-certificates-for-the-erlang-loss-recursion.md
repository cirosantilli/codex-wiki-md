# Rational certificates for the Erlang loss recursion

↑ **Parent:** [Erlang loss formula](erlang-loss-formula.md)

For rational [offered load](offered-traffic.md), every step of the stable recursion for the [Erlang loss formula](erlang-loss-formula.md) is rational. Comparing the final numerator and denominator with rational thresholds therefore certifies signs without floating-point uncertainty. Opposite certified signs of a continuous fixed-point residual imply a root by the [intermediate value theorem](intermediate-value-theorem.md); several alternating intervals can prove multiple [Erlang fixed points](erlang-fixed-point-approximation.md) under [alternative routing](alternative-routing.md).
