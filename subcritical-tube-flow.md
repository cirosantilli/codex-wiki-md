# Subcritical tube flow

↑ **Parent:** [Collapsible tube flow](collapsible-tube-flow.md)

A positive [collapsible tube flow](collapsible-tube-flow.md) is subcritical when its [characteristic speeds](characteristic-speed.md) $u+c$ and $u-c$ have opposite signs. A downstream boundary can then send information upstream. For a quadratic pressure-area law and fixed dimensionless flux $q$, this corresponds to $A/A_0>\sqrt q$.
