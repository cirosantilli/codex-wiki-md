# Thermodynamic limit

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical physics](statistical-physics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thermodynamic_limit)

The thermodynamic limit sends particle number and volume to infinity at fixed intensive quantities such as [number density](number-density.md). Distinct symmetry-breaking phases become sharply defined in this limit.
