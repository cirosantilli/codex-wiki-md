<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A useful organizing principle is [Gibbs free energy](../../../../gibbs-free-energy.md): at specified [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) and [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md) a coupled process is favorable when its total $\Delta G$ is negative. For each [chemical species](../../../../chemical-species.md),

$$
\mu_i=\mu_i^\circ+RT\ln a_i,\qquad
\Delta G=\Delta G^\circ+RT\ln Q.
$$

The relative amounts of [chemical substances](../../../../chemical-substance-split.md) determine the available [Gibbs free energy](../../../../gibbs-free-energy.md), beyond counting [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) in individual bonds. Nutrient oxidation and light-driven chemistry can maintain nonequilibrium compositions. [ATP hydrolysis](../../../../atp-hydrolysis.md) can drive biosynthesis, transport and motion of [molecular motors](../../../../molecular-motor.md) when reactions are coupled so that their total free-energy change is favorable. Breaking an [inorganic phosphate](../../../../inorganic-phosphate.md) bond itself costs [energy](../../../../energy-split.md); the favorable net [hydrolysis](../../../../hydrolysis.md) includes formation and solvation of the products and depends on their [thermodynamic activities](../../../../thermodynamic-activity.md).

[Redox](../../../../redox.md) [Gibbs free energy](../../../../gibbs-free-energy.md) comes from transferring electrons between donors and acceptors: $\Delta G=-nF\Delta E$. Electron transfer in respiration or photosynthesis can help establish an [electrochemical gradient](../../../../electrochemical-gradient.md). For a mole of [ions](../../../../ion.md) moving from side 1 to side 2,

$$
\Delta\widetilde\mu=RT\ln(a_2/a_1)+zF(\psi_2-\psi_1).
$$

The [concentration](../../../../concentration.md) and electrical terms together drive transport and can power [ATP synthase](../../../../atp-synthase.md). ATP-driven pumps can conversely build gradients. [Chemiosmosis](../../../../chemiosmosis.md) is the coupling between this gradient and chemical synthesis, not the creation of [Gibbs free energy](../../../../gibbs-free-energy.md) by an [enzyme](../../../../enzyme-split.md).

Mechanical [work](../../../../work.md) and elastic storage are other forms: a motor can do $\int F\,dx$ or $\int\tau\,d\theta$, and an approximately Hookean element stores $kx^2/2$. ATP-driven motors convert [Gibbs free energy](../../../../gibbs-free-energy.md) into motion, while externally forced reverse motion can sometimes drive chemical synthesis. A [photon](../../../../photon.md) brings [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) $h\nu$; light [photon absorption](../../../../photon-absorption.md) initiates chemistry in photosynthesis and light-driven pumps, with some [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) inevitably dissipated. Living cells use these conversions for structure, movement, homeostasis and synthesis, while releasing [heat](../../../../heat-split.md) and exporting [entropy](../../../../entropy-split.md). At a single uniform [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md), dissipated [heat](../../../../heat-split.md) cannot be completely recycled into useful work.
