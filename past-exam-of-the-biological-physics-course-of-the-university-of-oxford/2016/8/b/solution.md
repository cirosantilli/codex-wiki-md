[Ion-channel deactivation](../../../../ion-channel-deactivation.md) and [ion-channel inactivation](../../../../ion-channel-inactivation.md) are different. [Ion-channel deactivation](../../../../ion-channel-deactivation.md) closes an activation gate when the [voltage](../../../../voltage.md) returns toward rest. [Ion-channel inactivation](../../../../ion-channel-inactivation.md) makes a channel unavailable even while depolarization remains favorable for activation. A minimal kinetic picture is a closed state, an open state and an unavailable state, with recovery required before reopening. This helps terminate inward [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md) and produces a [neuronal refractory period](../../../../neuronal-refractory-period.md).

In rapid [voltage-gated potassium channel](../../../../voltage-gated-potassium-channel.md) [N-type inactivation](../../../../ball-and-chain-inactivation.md), a cytoplasmic terminal segment, sometimes supplied by an auxiliary subunit, blocks the open inner pore. The classic [ball-and-chain inactivation](../../../../ball-and-chain-inactivation.md) picture is supported by loss of rapid [ion-channel inactivation](../../../../ion-channel-inactivation.md) after removing the relevant terminal segment and restoration by an appropriate soluble [peptide](../../../../peptide.md). This links a mobile structural element to open-pore blockade; it does not imply that all [potassium](../../../../potassium.md) channels use that mechanism.[1990 primary study, restoration of rapid inactivation](../../../../restoration-of-inactivation-in-mutants-of-shaker-potassium-channels-by-a-peptide-derived-from-shb-by-william-n-zagotta-and-others.md)

Slower [C-type inactivation](../../../../c-type-inactivation.md) involves rearrangement near the [selectivity filter](../../../../selectivity-filter.md) and external pore. Dependence on pore structure, [ion](../../../../ion.md) occupancy and extracellular [ions](../../../../ion.md) distinguishes it from a simple cytoplasmic plugging particle. Different channels can combine several unavailable states, so a single recovery [time constant](../../../../time-constant.md) need not describe every trace.

Fast [ion-channel inactivation](../../../../ion-channel-inactivation.md) in canonical animal voltage-gated [sodium](../../../../sodium.md) channels involves the intracellular linker between domains III and IV, including its hydrophobic IFM motif, and is coupled to domain-IV voltage-sensor activation. The modern structural interpretation is an allosteric latch acting on the gate, rather than a universal literal particle sitting in the [ion](../../../../ion.md) pathway. The older hinged-lid analogy is useful only with that qualification.[2022 primary structural study, discussion distinguishes IFM-mediated allosteric inactivation from an observed alternative N-terminal pore block](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-022-30400-w/)

Functionally, a [voltage clamp](../../../../voltage-clamp.md) trace shows [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md) declining during a maintained step; a second test pulse measures recovery of availability. Holding-potential dependence separates an altered fraction of available channels from a mere change in maximal open [electrical conductance](../../../../electrical-conductance.md). Persistent [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md) after defective [ion-channel inactivation](../../../../ion-channel-inactivation.md), or delayed recovery that reduces subsequent responses, illustrates why both entry into and exit from unavailable states matter for excitability. A decaying macroscopic [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md) alone does not uniquely identify the molecular mechanism.
