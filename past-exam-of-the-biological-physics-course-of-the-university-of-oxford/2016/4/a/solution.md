<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A rising [force](../../../../force-split.md) branch means that an already exposed [polypeptide](../../../../polypeptide.md) segment is being stretched while the remaining structure resists further extraction. At a [force](../../../../force-split.md) peak a structural barrier is crossed; [protein denaturation](../../../../denaturation-biochemistry.md) or release of additional chain increases the available [polymer contour length](../../../../polymer-contour-length.md), so the [force](../../../../force-split.md) falls at almost the same imposed separation. Stretching then loads the next remaining barrier. The sawtooth pattern therefore reports a sequence of mechanically resistant intermediates, not a regular [spring](../../../../spring.md) oscillation. The [cantilever](../../../../cantilever.md) deflection gives [force](../../../../force-split.md), while its own deformation must be removed from the imposed separation to infer molecular extension.

A [membrane protein](../../../../membrane-protein.md) also requires work to disrupt membrane anchoring, helix packing and contacts with [lipids](../../../../lipid.md) or neighboring [molecules](../../../../molecule-split.md). The retinal-containing interior contributes a bulky, specifically packed scaffold; exposing chain must overcome these [steric hindrance](../../../../steric-hindrance.md) as well as [hydrogen bond](../../../../hydrogen-bond.md) and [electrostatic interactions](../../../../electrostatic-interaction.md). A [force](../../../../force-split.md) peak need not correspond one-to-one to one complete helix, and its height depends on loading rate and pathway, not just an equilibrium folding [Gibbs free energy](../../../../gibbs-free-energy.md).

Salt changes [electrostatic screening](../../../../electrostatic-screening.md) and specific [ion](../../../../ion.md) binding. Screening can reduce either repulsion or attraction, so “more salt always weakens the [protein](../../../../protein-split.md)” is false. The primary study underlying these kinds of traces identifies relatively salt-insensitive [steric hindrance](../../../../steric-hindrance.md) around the [retinal](../../../../retinal.md) and additional salt-sensitive barriers. Its interpretation associates some added barriers with screening of destabilizing electrostatic repulsion. The supplied traces likewise show a changed set of intermediate peaks, rather than a uniform rescaling of one curve.[2007 primary study, Results: Unfolding bR from PM cytoplasmic side; Discussion: steric and electrostatic contributions](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1959538/)

Exact assignment of every arrow to a residue requires structural and statistical information beyond one representative trace. No such assignment is inferred here. The printed reference to **[titin](../../../../titin.md)** in the following paragraph is inconsistent with the stated specimen and the membrane-protein traces; the physical interpretation here concerns [bacteriorhodopsin](../../../../bacteriorhodopsin.md), while the worm-like-chain expression is a generic [polymer](../../../../polymer-split.md) approximation.
