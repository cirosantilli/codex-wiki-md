<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Suppose initially $c_1>c_2$. Positive [ions](../../../../ion.md) initially move by [diffusion](../../../../diffusion.md) from side 1 to side 2. Since negative [counterions](../../../../counterion.md) cannot cross, side 1 acquires a negative interfacial charge and side 2 a positive one. The resulting electrical [force](../../../../force-split.md) opposes further positive-ion transfer. The two contributions balance when the [electrochemical potentials](../../../../electrochemical-potential.md) are equal; individual [ions](../../../../ion.md) still cross in both directions, but there is no net [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md) of the permeant [chemical species](../../../../chemical-species.md).

A simple [capacitor](../../../../capacitor.md) model makes the feedback quantitative. If $n$ moles of monovalent positive [ions](../../../../ion.md) move from initially neutral side 1 to side 2, the charge on side 1 is $-Fn$, hence $V=-Fn/C$. The free-energy stationarity condition is

$$
RT\ln\frac{c_1^0-n/\mathcal V}{c_2^0+n/\mathcal V}-\frac{F^2n}{C}=0.
$$

As $n$ increases, both the [concentration](../../../../concentration.md) imbalance and the [voltage](../../../../voltage.md) drive toward reversal diminish the original [diffusion](../../../../diffusion.md) tendency. The equilibrium lies before the bulk [concentrations](../../../../concentration.md) become equal. This demonstrates why a nonzero [Nernst potential](../../../../nernst-equilibrium-potential.md) can coexist with very little change in bulk composition and approximate bulk [electroneutrality](../../../../electroneutrality.md). A [concentration](../../../../concentration.md) difference alone does not maintain an electrical [voltage](../../../../voltage.md) if both [counterions](../../../../counterion.md) can freely follow each other; **[selective permeability](../../../../selective-permeability.md)** is essential to this model.
