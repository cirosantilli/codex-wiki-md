<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use signed [ion](../../../../ion.md) valence $z$ and define $V=\psi_1-\psi_2$. The molar [electrochemical potential](../../../../electrochemical-potential.md) in side $i$ of an ideal dilute solution is

$$
\widetilde\mu_i=\mu^\circ+RT\ln(c_i/c^\circ)+zF\psi_i.
$$

Transfer of an infinitesimal amount $dn$ from side 1 to side 2 changes the total [Gibbs free energy](../../../../gibbs-free-energy.md) by $(\widetilde\mu_2-\widetilde\mu_1)dn$. At equilibrium this derivative is zero, so

$$
RT\ln(c_1/c_2)+zF(\psi_1-\psi_2)=0,
\qquad\boxed{V=\frac{RT}{zF}\ln\frac{c_2}{c_1}.}
$$

For a cell with side 1 inside, this is $E_i=(RT/z_iF)\ln(c_{i,\rm out}/c_{i,\rm in})$. The [concentrations](../../../../concentration.md) are **equilibrium free-ion [concentrations](../../../../concentration.md)**, not automatically their exact initial values. For nonideal solutions the ratio must be a ratio of [thermodynamic activities](../../../../thermodynamic-activity.md). The [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) and solvent must be common to both reservoirs, and an [ion](../../../../ion.md) must actually be able to exchange for this equilibrium condition to be dynamically established.

Equal compartment volumes are unnecessary for deriving the [voltage](../../../../voltage.md) law, but help specify number conservation if [concentration](../../../../concentration.md) changes are appreciable. For equal [volume](../../../../volume.md) $\mathcal V$ and a monovalent cation transfer $n$ from side 1 to 2, $c_1=c_1^0-n/\mathcal V$, $c_2=c_2^0+n/\mathcal V$. Combined with the [membrane capacitance](../../../../membrane-capacitance.md), this gives an implicit finite-reservoir equilibrium rather than treating both [concentrations](../../../../concentration.md) as externally clamped.
