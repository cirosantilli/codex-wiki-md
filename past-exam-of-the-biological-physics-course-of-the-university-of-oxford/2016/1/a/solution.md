<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Treat the membrane as a [capacitor](../../../../capacitor.md), and count the additional positive charge on its inner surface. With radius $r=10^{-6}$ m and length $\ell=10^{-4}$ m, including both end caps gives

$$
A=2\pi r\ell+2\pi r^2=6.3460\times10^{-10}\ \mathrm{m^2},\qquad
\mathcal V=\pi r^2\ell=3.1416\times10^{-16}\ \mathrm{m^3}.
$$

The [membrane capacitance](../../../../membrane-capacitance.md) is $C=C_mA=6.3460$ pF. Thus $\Delta Q=C\Delta V=5.3941\times10^{-13}$ C, corresponding to $\Delta N=\Delta Q/e=3.3667\times10^6$ monovalent [sodium](../../../../sodium.md) [ions](../../../../ion.md). Converting charge to [amount of substance](../../../../amount-of-substance.md) using the [Faraday constant](../../../../faraday-constant.md),

$$
\boxed{\Delta c_{\rm Na}=\frac{C_m A\Delta V}{F\mathcal V}
=0.01780\ \mathrm{mol\,m^{-3}}=0.01780\ \mathrm{mM},\qquad
\frac{\Delta c_{\rm Na}}{15\ \mathrm{mM}}=1.186\times10^{-3}.}
$$

Omitting the ends changes the answer to $0.01762$ mM, a one-percent correction. A large [membrane potential](../../../../membrane-potential-split.md) change therefore requires only about $0.12\%$ of the intracellular [sodium](../../../../sodium.md) population to enter. The bulk liquid remains nearly electrically neutral; excess charge resides chiefly near the [cell membrane](../../../../cell-membrane-split.md). This explains why an [action potential](../../../../action-potential.md) need not exhaust the [concentration](../../../../concentration.md) difference that drives it, and why fixed [reversal potentials](../../../../reversal-potential.md) are a useful approximation during one spike. This [membrane capacitive ion budget](../../../../membrane-capacitive-ion-budget.md) assumes [sodium](../../../../sodium.md) supplies the **minimum net charging requirement**. Simultaneous opposing [electric currents](../../../../electric-current.md) can require more [sodium](../../../../sodium.md) entry; repeated spikes eventually require the [sodium-potassium ATPase](../../../../sodium-potassium-atpase.md) to maintain the gradients.
