A typical [voltage-gated potassium channel](../../../../voltage-gated-potassium-channel.md) has four subunits, each with six membrane-spanning segments. S1–S4 form a [voltage-sensing domain](../../../../voltage-sensing-domain.md); S5–S6 and their intervening loop form the pore and [selectivity filter](../../../../selectivity-filter.md). Conserved positively charged side chains along S4 move through part of the membrane electric field when the [voltage](../../../../voltage.md) changes. Countercharges and hydrated crevices in the surrounding sensor help make this movement possible. Coupling between the sensor and pore, including the S4–S5 region and S6 gate, changes the [probability](../../../../probability.md) of opening. A [voltage-gated sodium channel](../../../../voltage-gated-sodium-channel.md) usually combines four related domains in one large [protein](../../../../protein-split.md), rather than four identical independent [proteins](../../../../protein-split.md).

The evidence separates charge movement, structural arrangement and functional coupling. A transient [ion-channel gating current](../../../../ion-channel-gating-current.md) can persist when permeating ionic [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md) is suppressed: some charged parts of the channel itself move. Comparing integrated [ion-channel gating charge](../../../../ion-channel-gating-charge.md) with channel opening tests their coupling. Charge-neutralization experiments change both total moved charge and [voltage](../../../../voltage.md) dependence, implicating S4 basic residues and countercharges elsewhere; they do not show that S4 alone supplies every charge. [1996 primary abstract: neutralization identifies contributions from S2 and S4](../../../../voltage-sensing-residues-in-the-s2-and-s4-segments-of-the-shaker-k-plus-channel-by-s-a-seoh-and-others.md)

[Crystal structure of a mammalian voltage-dependent Shaker family K+ channel by Stephen B. Long and others](../../../../crystal-structure-of-a-mammalian-voltage-dependent-shaker-family-k-plus-channel-by-stephen-b-long-and-others.md) supplies structural evidence for the arrangement. Voltage-dependent accessibility and optical reports of sensor motion provide complementary functional tests. No one static structure is a movie of gating, and a perturbation that abolishes current can disrupt folding or pore function rather than selectively remove voltage sensing. Agreement between structural, electrical and motion-sensitive measurements supports the mechanism more strongly than any one observation alone.
