↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The average [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md) is the number of channels times their [ion-channel open probability](../../../../ion-channel-open-probability.md) times the [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md) carried by an open channel:

$$
I=N P_O\gamma_K(V_m-V_K),\qquad
\frac{I}{NP_O}=\frac{9\ \mathrm{nA}}{3000\times0.6}=5.0\ \mathrm{pA}.
$$

Consequently the identifiable answer is

$$
\boxed{\gamma_K=\frac{5.0\ \mathrm{pA}}{(-50\ \mathrm{mV})-V_K}.}
$$

Rapid permeation is possible because the hydrated pore and coordinating groups of the [selectivity filter](../../../../selectivity-filter.md) provide a favorable free-energy landscape for repeated [ion](../../../../ion.md) transit. Several [ions](../../../../ion.md) can occupy the filter and their interactions favor onward passage, while the channel remains open for many successive transits. Selectivity need not mean that each transported [ion](../../../../ion.md) remains tightly trapped for a long time. This is passive downhill transport; it does not consume one [ATP](../../../../adenosine-triphosphate.md) [molecule](../../../../molecule-split.md) per [ion](../../../../ion.md) crossing.
