<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

[Voltage](../../../../voltage.md) gating and [ion](../../../../ion.md) selectivity are different structural functions. A typical [voltage-gated potassium channel](../../../../voltage-gated-potassium-channel.md) has four pore-forming subunits; their [voltage-sensing](../../../../voltage-sensing-domain.md) regions move charged residues in the membrane electric field and couple that motion to a pore gate. Carbonyl coordination in the [selectivity filter](../../../../selectivity-filter.md) compensates [potassium](../../../../potassium.md) dehydration in an ion-specific geometry. The smaller [sodium](../../../../sodium.md) [ion](../../../../ion.md) is not automatically passed more readily: its hydration and coordination energetics differ. Structural work on a mammalian Kv channel resolves the sensor/pore architecture and an open activation gate.[Kv1.2 structural study, abstract and open-pore/voltage-sensor architecture](../../../../crystal-structure-of-a-mammalian-voltage-dependent-shaker-family-k-plus-channel-by-stephen-b-long-and-others.md)

A [sodium-channel](../../../../voltage-gated-sodium-channel.md) alpha subunit contains four [homologous](../../../../biological-homology.md) domains assembled into one pore, with separate [voltage sensors](../../../../voltage-sensing-domain.md). Rapid activation and fast [inactivation](../../../../ion-channel-inactivation.md) are distinguishable conformational transitions; an intracellular [inactivation](../../../../ion-channel-inactivation.md) apparatus stabilizes nonconducting states after activation. Repolarization favours recovery of availability. A channel can therefore be closed but activatable, or inactivated and temporarily unavailable, despite zero current in both cases.

Several types of evidence test these ideas. Ionic [reversal potentials](../../../../reversal-potential.md) shift when ionic gradients are changed, establishing selectivity and driving [force](../../../../force-split.md). Single-channel recordings show discrete conducting levels and stochastic dwell times; whole-cell current changes often reflect changed [open probability](../../../../two-state-ion-channel-gating.md) rather than a continuously varying single-channel current. Small [gating-charge](../../../../ion-channel-gating-current.md) transients can remain when ionic permeation is blocked, demonstrating sensor movement rather than [ion](../../../../ion.md) flux. Conditioning and paired-pulse records separate activation, [inactivation](../../../../ion-channel-inactivation.md) and recovery. Structural perturbations of [voltage sensors](../../../../voltage-sensing-domain.md) and of [inactivation](../../../../ion-channel-inactivation.md) machinery alter different kinetic properties, supporting their functional distinction.

These observations constrain models but do not make an individual macroscopic trace a unique structural reconstruction. Different distributions of closed and inactivated states can produce similar averages. In particular, the sustained [potassium](../../../../potassium.md) trace supports little [inactivation](../../../../ion-channel-inactivation.md) on the displayed interval; it does not justify assigning every [potassium channel](../../../../voltage-gated-potassium-channel.md) a universal absence of an [inactivation](../../../../ion-channel-inactivation.md) mechanism.
