<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [sodium](../../../../sodium.md) trace is a rapid inward current after depolarization, followed by loss of current even while [voltage](../../../../voltage.md) remains depolarized. A [voltage-gated sodium channel](../../../../voltage-gated-sodium-channel.md) therefore activates rapidly and then becomes unavailable through [inactivation](../../../../ion-channel-inactivation.md). Under ordinary cardiac ionic gradients, the test potential is below the [sodium](../../../../sodium.md) [reversal potential](../../../../reversal-potential.md), so opening carries inward positive charge. Rapid [sodium](../../../../sodium.md) entry supports the fast action-potential upstroke and propagation in working myocardium; [inactivation](../../../../ion-channel-inactivation.md) and subsequent recovery contribute to [refractoriness](../../../../neuronal-refractory-period.md).

The [potassium](../../../../potassium.md) trace develops more slowly, is outward during the positive step, and remains approximately sustained until repolarization. It is consistent with a delayed outward [potassium](../../../../potassium.md) [conductance](../../../../electrical-conductance.md): activation counteracts depolarization, and deactivation follows the return towards the holding [voltage](../../../../voltage.md). [Potassium](../../../../potassium.md) currents contribute to repolarization and stable diastolic [voltage](../../../../voltage.md). The trace does not identify a unique cardiac [potassium-channel](../../../../voltage-gated-potassium-channel.md) subtype, and not all [potassium](../../../../potassium.md) channels lack [inactivation](../../../../ion-channel-inactivation.md).

Together, fast inward activation initiates excitation and slower outward activation terminates it. Current direction is set by electrochemical driving [force](../../../../force-split.md) as well as gating: $I=N_{\rm ch}g_{\rm single}p_o(V_m-E_{\rm ion})$ in an ohmic approximation. A [voltage clamp](../../../../voltage-clamp.md) fixes $V_m$ and reveals those currents; in a freely firing cell the currents instead jointly determine the [voltage](../../../../voltage.md) through the membrane-capacitance equation. [Calcium](../../../../calcium.md) current is also important for the cardiac plateau, so these two traces alone are not a complete ventricular action-potential model. The sodium-potassium pump maintains long-term gradients but is not the fast gating mechanism seen in these recordings.
