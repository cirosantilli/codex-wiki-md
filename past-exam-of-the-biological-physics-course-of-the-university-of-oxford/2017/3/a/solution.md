<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In [spectrin unfolding by force spectroscopy](../../../../spectrin-unfolding-by-force-spectroscopy.md), the [cantilever](../../../../cantilever.md) measures [tension](../../../../tension.md) through its calibrated deflection, while [displacement](../../../../displacement.md) of the pulling point increases the separation of the [protein](../../../../protein-split.md) attachments. The [protein](../../../../protein-split.md) extension is obtained after allowing for [cantilever](../../../../cantilever.md) compliance and any linker extension; it is not simply the commanded [displacement](../../../../displacement.md).

A folded [spectrin](../../../../spectrin.md) repeat stores much of its backbone in a compact domain. Between [unfolding](../../../../denaturation-biochemistry.md) events, the already released chain is stretched: increasingly straight conformations have fewer accessible configurations, and the resisting [entropic force](../../../../entropic-force.md) rises nonlinearly. A peak is reached when a still-folded domain crosses an [unfolding](../../../../denaturation-biochemistry.md) barrier on the timescale of the imposed loading. Its released backbone increases the available [contour length](../../../../polymer-contour-length.md) abruptly. At nearly the same end-to-end distance the fractional extension $x/L$ is then smaller, so [tension](../../../../tension.md) drops. Pulling resumes on the next, longer-chain elastic branch. Repeated release of domains produces the saw-tooth sequence.

The record is therefore evidence for sequential mechanical [unfolding](../../../../denaturation-biochemistry.md) of modular structure, not repeated stretching of a fixed-length rod. For the nominal eight-domain construct, successive branches correspond to increasing numbers of unfolded repeats. The order and rupture [forces](../../../../force-split.md) can fluctuate because barrier crossing is thermally activated and depends on loading rate, structural heterogeneity and pulling geometry; equivalent repeats need not rupture at identical [force](../../../../force-split.md).

The horizontal separation between worm-like-chain fits at the same [force](../../../../force-split.md) estimates the added [contour length](../../../../polymer-contour-length.md), whereas the vertical [force](../../../../force-split.md) drop at one extension reflects redistribution of [tension](../../../../tension.md). These are distinct measurements. A repeat increment near the specified $31.7\,\mathrm{nm}$ is consistent with repeated similar structural units. The gain is a net release relative to the folded span and attachment geometry; $31.7/106\,\mathrm{nm}$ is not automatically the universal [contour length](../../../../polymer-contour-length.md) of a [peptide](../../../../peptide.md) residue.

During unloading, relaxation and possible refolding can restore compact domains. [Hysteresis](../../../../hysteresis.md) would indicate dissipative or kinetically delayed processes. Neither the peak [force](../../../../force-split.md) alone nor an arbitrary saw-tooth area directly equals an equilibrium [unfolding](../../../../denaturation-biochemistry.md) [free energy](../../../../gibbs-free-energy.md): one must separate chain [elasticity](../../../../elasticity-physics-split.md), [cantilever](../../../../cantilever.md) storage, irreversible loss and pulling-rate effects. The worm-like-chain branches describe released-chain [elasticity](../../../../elasticity-physics-split.md), not the molecular transition state responsible for a rupture peak.
