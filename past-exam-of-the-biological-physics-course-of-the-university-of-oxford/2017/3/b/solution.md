<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Write $u=x/L$ and $A=k_BT L_p$. The [worm-like chain force-extension interpolation](../../../../worm-like-chain-force-extension-interpolation.md) is

$$
f=\frac{k_BT}{L_p}\left[\frac1{4(1-u)^2}-\frac14+u\right].
$$

A fit to an elastic rising branch estimates its [contour length](../../../../polymer-contour-length.md) $L$ and, if not fixed independently, its [persistence length](../../../../persistence-length.md) $L_p$. Comparing successive fitted [contour lengths](../../../../polymer-contour-length.md) gives the length released by a domain and helps distinguish whole-repeat [unfolding](../../../../denaturation-biochemistry.md) from partial events. $L_p$ estimates the flexibility of the unfolded chain and the [bending rigidity](../../../../bending-stiffness.md) $A$; at a stated [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md), $A=k_BT L_p$.

For small extension, expansion gives $f\simeq[3k_BT/(2L_pL)]x$, so the effective entropic [spring constant](../../../../spring-constant.md) is $3k_BT/(2L_pL)$. Close to full extension, $f\sim k_BT/[4L_p(1-x/L)^2]$, giving strong strain [stiffening](../../../../elasticity-physics-split.md). These regimes constrain the fit differently and correlate its parameters if the measured interval is short. The fit does not by itself identify a unique folding barrier or [unfolding](../../../../denaturation-biochemistry.md) kinetic rate.

**Persistence-length convention.** In a three-dimensional isotropic [worm-like chain](../../../../worm-like-chain.md) the standard definition is $\langle\mathbf t(s)\cdot\mathbf t(0)\rangle=e^{-s/L_p}$. Thus at $s=L_p$, $\arccos\langle\cos\theta\rangle=\arccos(e^{-1})\simeq68.4^\circ$. This is not literally the mean angle $\langle\theta\rangle$. For a simple counterexample, equally probable angles $0^\circ$ and $90^\circ$ have mean angle $45^\circ$ but $\arccos\langle\cos\theta\rangle=60^\circ$. The source's angle description is shorthand for the tangent-correlation convention.
