<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A skeletal-muscle fibre contains parallel [myofibrils](../../../../myofibril.md) assembled from repeating [sarcomeres](../../../../sarcomere.md) between Z discs. Thin [actin filaments](../../../../actin-filament.md) extend from the Z discs; bipolar thick [myosin II](../../../../myosin-ii.md) [filaments](../../../../myofilament.md) occupy the central region. Overlap between them allows many [myosin heads](../../../../myosin.md) to interact with [actin](../../../../actin.md) in parallel.

A rise in cytosolic [calcium](../../../../calcium.md) exposes [actin](../../../../actin.md) binding sites through the [troponin](../../../../troponin.md)–[tropomyosin](../../../../tropomyosin.md) regulatory system. [ATP](../../../../adenosine-triphosphate.md) binding releases a strongly attached [myosin](../../../../myosin.md) head; hydrolysis primes its [lever arm](../../../../myosin-lever-arm.md), attachment and product release permit a force-generating stroke, and another [ATP](../../../../adenosine-triphosphate.md) allows detachment. The cycle converts [chemical free energy](../../../../gibbs-free-energy.md) into [mechanical work](../../../../work.md) rather than consuming [actin](../../../../actin.md) or shortening the [filaments](../../../../myofilament.md) themselves.

Relative filament [sliding](../../../../facilitated-target-search.md) draws the Z discs together. The I band and H zone shorten as overlap increases, whereas the thick-filament A-band length is approximately unchanged. [Calcium](../../../../calcium.md) removal restores inhibition and permits relaxation. The many-head ensemble sustains [force](../../../../force-split.md) even though individual cross-bridges repeatedly attach and detach. This sliding-filament description concerns [myosin II](../../../../myosin-ii.md) in [muscle](../../../../muscle.md); the processive two-head walking of [Myosin V](../../../../myosin-v.md) in [a](../../6/a.md) is a related but distinct motor arrangement.
