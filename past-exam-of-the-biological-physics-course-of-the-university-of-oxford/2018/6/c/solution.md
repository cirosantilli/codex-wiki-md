<h1 id="6/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A stable native conformation is favoured when its [Gibbs free energy](../../../../gibbs-free-energy.md), including the [protein](../../../../protein-split.md) and its surrounding [solvent](../../../../solvent.md), is lower than that of competing conformational ensembles under the specified conditions:

$$
\boxed{\Delta G_{\rm fold}=\Delta H_{\rm fold}-T\Delta S_{\rm total}<0.}
$$

Folding greatly reduces the chain's [conformational entropy](../../../../conformational-entropy.md), an unfavourable contribution. It can nevertheless gain favourable hydrogen-bonding and packing interactions, van der Waals contacts, screened [electrostatic interactions](../../../../electrostatic-interaction.md) and [solvent](../../../../solvent.md) [free energy](../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md). In aqueous globular [proteins](../../../../protein-split.md), burying nonpolar surface reduces the cost of exposing it to [water](../../../../water.md); the [hydrophobic effect](../../../../hydrophobic-effect.md) is not an extra covalent attraction between all hydrophobic residues. [Hydrogen bonds](../../../../hydrogen-bond.md) and ionic contacts must be judged relative to the interactions those groups made with [solvent](../../../../solvent.md) before folding, so merely counting bonds in the folded structure does not determine [stability](../../../../stability-of-a-numerical-method.md).

The [protein primary structure](../../../../protein-primary-structure.md) determines which contacts and [protein secondary structures](../../../../protein-secondary-structure.md) can cooperate. A [free-energy landscape](../../../../free-energy-landscape.md) biased towards compatible contacts permits many routes towards the native ensemble, rather than an exhaustive random search through every conformation. [Folding funnels](../../../../folding-funnel.md) describe this bias, while barriers explain metastability and folding rates. Reversible denaturation/refolding experiments show that some [proteins](../../../../protein-split.md) can recover native structure and activity when suitable conditions are restored, supporting a sequence-encoded thermodynamic tendency without asserting that every [protein folds](../../../../protein-fold.md) unaided.

“Stable” means an [equilibrium](../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) preference and a useful lifetime, not one perfectly rigid shape. Native [proteins](../../../../protein-split.md) fluctuate and can have several functional conformations; the unfolded ensemble remains populated with relative weight depending on its full [free energy](../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md). Changes of [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md), [pH](../../../../ph.md), [salt](../../../../salt-chemistry.md), [solvent](../../../../solvent.md), [ligands](../../../../ligand-biochemistry.md) or [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md) alter the balance, and [molecular chaperones](../../../../chaperone-protein.md) can prevent [protein aggregation](../../../../protein-aggregation.md) or assist kinetic access without supplying a different primary sequence. Membrane [proteins](../../../../protein-split.md) require their appropriate environment, and [intrinsically disordered proteins](../../../../intrinsically-disordered-protein.md) need not possess one stable folded state in isolation.

Thus a released stretched chain can gain configurational [entropy](../../../../entropy-split.md) on first recoiling yet later favour a more ordered native structure because the _total_ [free energy](../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) balance, including [solvent](../../../../solvent.md), is different. [Equilibrium](../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) [stability](../../../../stability-of-a-numerical-method.md), barriers to unfolding and resistance to irreversible [protein aggregation](../../../../protein-aggregation.md) are distinct properties. The existence of a native minimum alone does not guarantee rapid, reversible folding under every experimental condition.
