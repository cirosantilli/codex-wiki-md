<h1 id="6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [RNA world hypothesis](../../../../rna-world-hypothesis.md) proposes an early stage in which [RNA](../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md)-like molecules carried heritable sequence information and also performed important catalytic functions, before the present division of labour among [DNA](../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md), [RNA](../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) and [proteins](../../../../protein-split.md). Complementary [base pairing](../../../../base-pairing.md) supplies a route to template-directed information copying, while sequence-dependent [RNA](../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) folding can create catalytic structures. This helps address the apparent circularity of requiring [protein](../../../../protein-split.md) [enzymes](../../../../enzyme-split.md) to copy [genes](../../../../gene.md) whose expression already requires an elaborate [protein](../../../../protein-split.md)-synthesis apparatus.

Modern biology supplies evidence for the necessary capacities. [Ribozymes](../../../../ribozyme.md) catalyse reactions, and [self-splicing introns](../../../../self-splicing-intron.md) demonstrate [RNA](../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md)-dependent processing without a dedicated [protein](../../../../protein-split.md) [enzyme](../../../../enzyme-split.md) for that chemistry. The [ribosome](../../../../ribosome.md)'s peptidyl-transferase centre is built principally from [RNA](../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md), with catalytic geometry located in its [RNA](../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) core; structural work provides direct evidence that this central [protein](../../../../protein-split.md)-synthesis chemistry is [RNA](../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) based.[Structures and substrate-analogue complexes identify an RNA-based peptidyl-transferase active site](../../../../the-structural-basis-of-ribosome-activity-in-peptide-bond-synthesis-by-poul-nissen-and-others.md) [RNA](../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) also remains central as messenger, adaptor and regulatory material. These observations make [RNA](../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) [catalysis](../../../../catalysis.md) and information storage physically plausible rather than assuming that all [catalysts](../../../../catalyst.md) must be [proteins](../../../../protein-split.md).

They do not prove a complete historical sequence. A viable early system still needs plausible [nucleotide](../../../../nucleotide.md) supply and activation, polymerization, template copying with sufficient fidelity, product-strand separation, compatibility of catalytic and copying conditions, an [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) source and some means of maintaining useful combinations. [RNA](../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md)'s chemical lability and the challenge of separating paired products are constraints, not details supplied automatically by [base pairing](../../../../base-pairing.md). The first hereditary chemistry might also have involved related polymers or cooperative [RNA](../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md)–peptide systems rather than an exclusively [RNA](../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) organism.

The later advantages of [DNA](../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) as a relatively stable storage medium and [proteins](../../../../protein-split.md) as [catalysts](../../../../catalyst.md) with chemically varied side chains help explain the present division of labour. The hypothesis is therefore a testable family of evolutionary scenarios supported by molecular capabilities and biological relics, not a direct observation of prebiotic life or proof that an isolated modern [RNA](../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) already self-replicates autonomously.
