↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

**[selective permeability](../../../../selective-permeability.md)** sets the driving-[force](../../../../force-split.md) term. [Potassium](../../../../potassium.md) and [sodium](../../../../sodium.md) channels provide narrow coordinating pathways whose [free energy](../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) balance of hydration and chemical binding favours the appropriate ion. [voltage-gated potassium channel](../../../../voltage-gated-potassium-channel.md) carbonyl coordination differs from the charged filter environment in canonical [sodium](../../../../sodium.md) channels, so selective permeation is not a pore-size ranking. Ion substitution shifts the [reversal potential](../../../../reversal-potential.md), and selective blockers or filter mutations alter the corresponding [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md). An open channel conducts down the [electrochemical gradient](../../../../electrochemical-gradient.md); [ATP](../../../../adenosine-triphosphate.md)-driven pumps maintain the gradients rather than pushing each ion through the pore.

**Voltage-dependent activation** sets channel availability. Charged residues in the S4-containing [voltage-sensing domains](../../../../voltage-sensing-domain.md) move in the membrane [electric field](../../../../electric-field-split.md) and couple to pore opening. A classical [voltage-gated potassium channel](../../../../voltage-gated-potassium-channel.md) is a tetramer; a [voltage-gated sodium channel](../../../../voltage-gated-sodium-channel.md) pore-forming [polypeptide](../../../../polypeptide.md) has four homologous domains. [ion-channel gating current](../../../../ion-channel-gating-current.md) measurements and sensor-residue mutations connect [electric charge](../../../../electric-charge-split.md) motion to opening, while structures separate the sensor from the permeation pathway. The historical $n^4$ and $m^3$ variables describe kinetics phenomenologically and need not map one-to-one onto individual resolved structural movements.[Sensor perturbations and measured gating charge](../../../../voltage-sensing-residues-in-the-s2-and-s4-segments-of-the-shaker-k-plus-channel-by-s-a-seoh-and-others.md)[Voltage-sensor and pore architecture](../../../../crystal-structure-of-a-mammalian-voltage-dependent-shaker-family-k-plus-channel-by-stephen-b-long-and-others.md)

**Time dependence, especially [ion-channel inactivation](../../../../ion-channel-inactivation.md)**, explains the different [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md) waveforms. [Sodium](../../../../sodium.md) activation is rapid, but sustained [depolarization](../../../../depolarization.md) reduces the availability variable $h$, so the [sodium](../../../../sodium.md) [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md) decays despite maintained driving voltage. Fast [sodium](../../../../sodium.md) [ion-channel inactivation](../../../../ion-channel-inactivation.md) involves intracellular machinery including the interdomain III–IV region. [Potassium](../../../../potassium.md) activation is slower, supplying delayed outward [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md); the classical delayed rectifier is relatively sustained on this time scale. Some [voltage-gated potassium channel](../../../../voltage-gated-potassium-channel.md) variants do show rapid N-terminal pore-blocking [ion-channel inactivation](../../../../ion-channel-inactivation.md) or slower filter-associated [ion-channel inactivation](../../../../ion-channel-inactivation.md), which should not be assumed identical to [sodium](../../../../sodium.md)'s mechanism. [voltage clamp](../../../../voltage-clamp.md) steps separate activation from decay; recovery protocols, single-channel recordings and targeted perturbations distinguish gating, [ion-channel inactivation](../../../../ion-channel-inactivation.md) and ion conduction. Removal of the Shaker N-terminal blocker and restoration by a free peptide are particularly direct evidence for one [voltage-gated potassium channel](../../../../voltage-gated-potassium-channel.md) mechanism.[N-terminal perturbations separate rapid inactivation from activation](../../../../biophysical-and-molecular-mechanisms-of-shaker-potassium-channel-inactivation-by-toshinori-hoshi-and-others.md)[Cytoplasmic peptide restores the missing blocking process](../../../../restoration-of-inactivation-in-mutants-of-shaker-potassium-channels-by-a-peptide-derived-from-shb-by-william-n-zagotta-and-others.md)

Together these properties produce $n^4\bar g_K(V-E_K)$ and $m^3h\bar g_{Na}(V-E_{Na})$: selectivity fixes [reversal potential](../../../../reversal-potential.md), maximal [electrical conductance](../../../../electrical-conductance.md) fixes scale, and activation/inactivation variables supply voltage- and history-dependent open [probability](../../../../probability.md).
