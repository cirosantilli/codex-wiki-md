<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [AFM](../../../../atomic-force-microscopy.md) pulls a tethered molecule through its [cantilever](../../../../cantilever.md). Deflection, multiplied by a calibrated [cantilever](../../../../cantilever.md) [spring constant](../../../../spring-constant.md), measures [force](../../../../force-split.md); support motion must be corrected for that deflection and any linker compliance to obtain molecular extension. Initially folded structure can resist extension, then contacts break and more [polypeptide](../../../../polypeptide.md) [polymer contour length](../../../../polymer-contour-length.md) becomes available. The readable trace rises with increasing extension, with fluctuations and a strongly rising large-extension part. It should not be interpreted as a direct image of individual [amino acids](../../../../amino-acid.md) or as establishing a unique microscopic unfolding pathway.

An ideal inextensible [freely jointed chain](../../../../ideal-chain.md) with the stated segment count and length has

$$
\boxed{L_c=Nb=35(0.4\,\mathrm{nm})=14\,\mathrm{nm}.}
$$

Its mean extension is strictly below $14\,\mathrm{nm}$ at every finite [force](../../../../force-split.md). Thus the quoted $15\,\mathrm{nm}$ maximum does not establish ideal [FJC](../../../../ideal-chain.md) behaviour: if both numbers are taken literally, it exceeds the model's [polymer contour length](../../../../polymer-contour-length.md) by about $7\%$. Finite segment stretch, inaccurate effective segment parameters, additional linkers or an extension-zero/calibration error can account for such a difference. If the numerical descriptions are approximate, the discrepancy may be within their uncertainty; even exact agreement with a [polymer contour length](../../../../polymer-contour-length.md) would not prove freely jointed statistics. Low-[force](../../../../force-split.md) fluctuations and the full [force](../../../../force-split.md)–extension relation are separate tests.
