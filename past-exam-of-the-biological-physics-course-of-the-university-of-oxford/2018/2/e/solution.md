<h1 id="2/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

On releasing the tensile load, the extended chain first retracts towards less aligned conformations because they have much greater configurational multiplicity. Viscous [friction](../../../../friction.md) and internal relaxation determine the time course; stored [free energy](../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) is dissipated as [heat](../../../../heat-split.md). An unconstrained [ideal chain](../../../../ideal-chain.md) does not shrink to a point: its [mean vector](../../../../mean-vector.md) is zero but its [root mean square](../../../../root-mean-square.md) end-to-end length is $b\sqrt N\simeq2.37\,\mathrm{nm}$ for the supplied ideal-chain parameters.

A real [polypeptide](../../../../polypeptide.md) also has sequence-dependent interactions. If the original native structure is the stable state in the prevailing [solvent](../../../../solvent.md) and the molecule remains intact and capable of folding, it can subsequently refold, gaining favourable intramolecular and [solvent](../../../../solvent.md) [free energy](../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md). The folded state is not selected by chain [entropy](../../../../entropy-split.md) alone, and formation of native contacts need not be instantaneous. Kinetic traps, [proline](../../../../proline.md) [isomerization](../../../../isomerization.md), [protein aggregation](../../../../protein-aggregation.md), misfolding, surface attachments or chemical damage may prevent complete recovery or produce [hysteresis](../../../../hysteresis.md). Thus reversible refolding is a physically motivated possibility, not guaranteed simply by removal of [force](../../../../force-split.md). This distinction connects the recoil of a stretched polymer to the thermodynamics discussed in Question 6(c).
