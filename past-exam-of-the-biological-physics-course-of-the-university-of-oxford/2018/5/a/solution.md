<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In a bacterial [transcription](../../../../transcription-biology.md) complex, [RNA polymerase](../../../../rna-polymerase.md) binds a [promoter](../../../../promoter-genetics.md) and locally separates the [DNA](../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) strands into a [transcription](../../../../transcription-biology.md) bubble. The template enters the active site in the $3'$ to $5'$ direction, while the complementary [RNA](../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) grows $5'$ to $3'$. The displaced non-template strand passes around the bubble, downstream [DNA](../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) re-forms a duplex, and the nascent [RNA](../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) exits through a separate channel. A short [RNA](../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md)–[DNA](../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) hybrid is maintained in an elongating complex; the first few [nucleotides](../../../../nucleotide.md) of an initiating complex do not yet make a full elongation-length hybrid.

The core [enzyme](../../../../enzyme-split.md) has subunit composition $\alpha_2\beta\beta'\omega$; association with a [sigma factor](../../../../sigma-factor.md) forms a [promoter](../../../../promoter-genetics.md)-selective holoenzyme. Sigma recognizes [promoter](../../../../promoter-genetics.md) features and assists steps leading from a closed bound complex to the open complex. Different [sigma factors](../../../../sigma-factor.md) redirect [transcription](../../../../transcription-biology.md) towards different [promoter](../../../../promoter-genetics.md) classes. The familiar $-10$ and $-35$ elements are examples for a major bacterial sigma family, not a universal sequence rule for all [sigma factors](../../../../sigma-factor.md). [Promoter](../../../../promoter-genetics.md) escape changes these contacts, but complete sigma dissociation at one exact transcript length should not be assumed for every complex.

For the supplied non-template sequence, assuming its first base is the first transcribed position, the complementary template runs $3'$-TCAGTAAGC-$5'$. The [RNA](../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) has the coding-strand order with [uracil](../../../../uracil.md) replacing [thymine](../../../../thymine.md). Therefore the first five [nucleotides](../../../../nucleotide.md) are

$$
\boxed{5'\text{-AGUCA-}3'.}
$$

The top-left panel of the original schematic in part (d) shows the strand polarities and polymerase organisation; it is not a copied experimental illustration.
