<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write the end-to-end [random vector](../../../../random-vector.md) as $\mathbf r=\sum_{j=1}^N b\mathbf u_j$, with independent isotropically distributed unit vectors. At zero applied [force](../../../../force-split.md), $\langle\mathbf u_j\rangle=0$, $\mathbf u_j\cdot\mathbf u_j=1$, and for $i\ne j$ independence gives $\langle\mathbf u_i\cdot\mathbf u_j\rangle=0$. Therefore

$$
\langle\mathbf r\rangle=0,\qquad
\langle r^2\rangle=b^2\sum_{i,j}\langle\mathbf u_i\cdot\mathbf u_j\rangle=Nb^2,
\qquad\boxed{r_{\rm rms}=b\sqrt N}.
$$

Here “size” is the [root mean square](../../../../root-mean-square.md) [end-to-end distance of a polymer chain](../../../../end-to-end-distance-of-a-polymer-chain.md). It is not the magnitude of the mean [random vector](../../../../random-vector.md), which is zero, nor the mean distance $\langle r\rangle$. In the long-chain [Gaussian limit of the end-to-end distribution of a freely jointed chain](../../../../gaussian-limit-of-the-end-to-end-distribution-of-a-freely-jointed-chain.md), $\langle r\rangle\simeq b\sqrt{8N/(3\pi)}$, illustrating why these definitions cannot be interchanged.
