<h1 id="3/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

In a classical [action potential](../../../../action-potential.md), delayed activation of [voltage-gated potassium channels](../../../../voltage-gated-potassium-channel.md) supplies outward [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md) that drives [repolarization](../../../../repolarization.md). In the [Hodgkin-Huxley model](../../../../hodgkin-huxley-model.md), for example, $I_K=\bar g_Kn^4(V-E_K)$ and $\dot n=\alpha_n(V)(1-n)-\beta_n(V)n$. Increasing the opening rate at depolarized [membrane potential](../../../../membrane-potential-split.md), other properties fixed, recruits outward [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md) earlier: the [action potential](../../../../action-potential.md) generally becomes narrower and may have a smaller peak. Slower opening delays [repolarization](../../../../repolarization.md) and broadens the [action potential](../../../../action-potential.md).

Slower closing after [repolarization](../../../../repolarization.md) retains excess [potassium](../../../../potassium.md) [electrical conductance](../../../../electrical-conductance.md), lengthening the after-[hyperpolarization](../../../../hyperpolarization.md) and often the interval of reduced excitability. Faster closing shortens this tail and permits earlier recovery. If activation or the available [electrical conductance](../../../../electrical-conductance.md) is insufficient, normal [repolarization](../../../../repolarization.md) may fail; changing rates can also change the steady open fraction. Duration is therefore determined by the whole balance of inward and outward [electric currents](../../../../electric-current.md), not by a closing rate in isolation.
