<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

An open [ion channel](../../../../ion-channel.md) carries the [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md)

$$
i=g(V-E_K)=(90\,\mathrm{pS})(60\,\mathrm{mV})=5.4\,\mathrm{pA}.
$$

The macroscopic mean is $I=NP_oi$, so $I/i\simeq204$ is the mean number open, not the total number present. Reading $P_o(-20\,\mathrm{mV})\simeq0.40$ from the original graph gives

$$
\boxed{N=\frac{1.1\,\mathrm{nA}}{(0.40)(5.4\,\mathrm{pA})}\simeq5.1\times10^2}.
$$

The graph reading is approximate, so a precision of hundreds is appropriate. Only a few hundred active [ion channels](../../../../ion-channel.md) generate a nanoampere-scale cellular [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md); molecular discreteness and stochastic gating therefore matter. For independent identical [ion channels](../../../../ion-channel.md), the open-count [standard deviation](../../../../standard-deviation.md) is $\sqrt{NP_o(1-P_o)}\simeq11$, corresponding to a [coefficient of variation](../../../../coefficient-of-variation.md) in [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md) of about $5.4\%$. This is an instantaneous ideal independent-gating estimate, before temporal filtering and other noise sources, and requires the [single-channel recording](../../../../single-channel-recording.md) $P_o$ to apply to the cellular recording conditions.
