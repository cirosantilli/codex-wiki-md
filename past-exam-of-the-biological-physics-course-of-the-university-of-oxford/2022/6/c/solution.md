<h1 id="6/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

An [ion channel](../../../../ion-channel.md) can disturb cellular electrical activity by changing unitary [electrical conductance](../../../../electrical-conductance.md), open [probability](../../../../probability.md), gating kinetics or the number delivered to the [cell membrane](../../../../cell-membrane-split.md). A useful organizing relation is

$$
I_j=N_j\gamma_jP_{o,j}(V,t)(V-E_j),\qquad
C_m\dot V=I_{\rm applied}-\sum_jI_j.
$$

Here outward [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md) is positive. Altering one channel's biophysical behavior changes the balance governing [membrane potential](../../../../membrane-potential-split.md) and hence the timing or recurrence of [action potentials](../../../../action-potential.md).

**[Long QT syndrome](../../../../long-qt-syndrome.md), type 3.** Certain disease-associated SCN5A variants impair fast [ion-channel inactivation](../../../../ion-channel-inactivation.md) of the cardiac [voltage-gated sodium channel](../../../../voltage-gated-sodium-channel.md) NaV1.5. Instead of vanishing promptly after the upstroke, a small persistent inward sodium [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md) remains during the plateau and [repolarization](../../../../repolarization.md). Because $V<E_{\rm Na}$ in this phase, the residual [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md) is inward and opposes the outward [electric currents](../../../../electric-current.md) that would terminate the cardiac [action potential](../../../../action-potential.md). The ventricular [action potential](../../../../action-potential.md) lasts longer, extending the QT interval on the [electrocardiogram](../../../../electrocardiogram.md). Excessively prolonged or uneven [repolarization](../../../../repolarization.md) permits early afterdepolarizations and dangerous ventricular rhythm disturbances.

Genetic association identifies the channel, while [voltage clamp](../../../../voltage-clamp.md) distinguishes defective [ion-channel inactivation](../../../../ion-channel-inactivation.md) from a mere increase in channel abundance. Recordings of disease-associated channels show persistent or bursting openings; coupling measured mutant sodium [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md) into an otherwise modeled cardiac membrane through [dynamic clamp](../../../../dynamic-clamp.md) tests whether that kinetic defect prolongs the [action potential](../../../../action-potential.md). The phenotype is variant-specific: not every SCN5A variant increases late [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md) or causes [long QT syndrome](../../../../long-qt-syndrome.md).[Human Molecular Genetics 4, pp. 1603–1607: familial variants affecting the inactivation machinery](../../../../cardiac-sodium-channel-mutations-in-patients-with-long-qt-syndrome-an-inherited-cardiac-arrhythmia-by-qing-wang-and-others.md)[Measured mutant-channel currents coupled to a ventricular-cell model test action-potential prolongation](../../../../long-qt-syndrome-related-sodium-channel-mutations-probed-by-the-dynamic-action-potential-clamp-technique-by-geza-berecki-and-others.md)

**[Myotonia congenita](../../../../myotonia-congenita.md).** CLCN1 encodes the skeletal-muscle ClC-1 [chloride channel](../../../../chloride-channel.md), which normally supplies a large resting stabilizing [electrical conductance](../../../../electrical-conductance.md). Because the chloride [reversal potential](../../../../reversal-potential.md) is near the resting [membrane potential](../../../../membrane-potential-split.md), that pathway resists small depolarizing disturbances. Loss of functional channels, reduced [electrical conductance](../../../../electrical-conductance.md), or a shift in opening toward more positive [membrane potential](../../../../membrane-potential-split.md) reduces the resting electrical shunt. [Potassium](../../../../potassium.md) accumulation in the transverse-tubule space during activity can then produce larger depolarizing afterpotentials and trigger further muscle [action potentials](../../../../action-potential.md) after the intended stimulus has ceased. Continued electrical firing sustains contraction and delays relaxation: this is myotonia, rather than a primary inability of the contractile apparatus to relax.

Patient-family linkage and variant analysis connect the disease to ClC-1. Functional recordings of disease-associated channels demonstrate shifted voltage dependence and reduced availability near resting [membrane potential](../../../../membrane-potential-split.md); single-channel and noise measurements also resolve two conductive protopores and their gating behavior. Different variants can act through different mechanisms, so a gating shift is an example rather than an explanation of every case.[Science 257, pp. 797–800: familial disease linked to the muscle chloride-channel gene](../../../../the-skeletal-muscle-chloride-channel-in-dominant-and-recessive-human-myotonia-by-manuela-c-koch-and-others.md)[Neuron 15, pp. 1455–1463: disease-associated changes in voltage dependence](../../../../mutations-in-dominant-human-myotonia-congenita-drastically-alter-the-voltage-dependence-of-the-clc-1-chloride-channel-by-michael-pusch-and-others.md)[Single-channel and fluctuation analysis of ClC-1 and myotonia-associated gating changes](../../../../the-muscle-chloride-channel-clc-1-has-a-double-barreled-appearance-that-is-differentially-affected-in-dominant-and-recessive-myotonia-by-caterina-saviane-and-others.md)

These examples connect contrasting current defects to their consequences: persistent inward sodium [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md) prolongs cardiac [action potentials](../../../../action-potential.md), whereas loss of stabilizing resting chloride [electrical conductance](../../../../electrical-conductance.md) permits repetitive skeletal-muscle [action potentials](../../../../action-potential.md). Both causal chains require functional channel measurements in addition to a disease-associated sequence change.
