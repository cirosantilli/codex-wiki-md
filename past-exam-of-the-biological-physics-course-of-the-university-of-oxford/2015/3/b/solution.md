<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Full dissociation gives bulk [concentrations](../../../../concentration.md) in $\mathrm{mM}$ of $155$ for [sodium](../../../../sodium.md), $5$ for [potassium](../../../../potassium.md), $2$ for [calcium](../../../../calcium.md), $114$ for chloride, and $25$ for hydrogen phosphate. The positive and negative charge concentrations are both $164\,\mathrm{mM}$, so the bulk is electrically neutral. At $T=298.15\,\mathrm K$,

$$
V_T=\frac{k_BT}{e}=25.6926\,\mathrm{mV},\qquad
u=-\frac{\phi_0}{V_T}=2.84129.
$$

The [Boltzmann distribution](../../../../boltzmann-distribution.md) then gives

$$
\boxed{c_{1+}(0)=160e^\nu=2742.05\,\mathrm{mM},\qquad
c_{2+}(0)=2e^{2\nu}=587.409\,\mathrm{mM}.}
$$

For completeness the coion [concentrations](../../../../concentration.md) are $114e^{-\nu}=6.65196\,\mathrm{mM}$ and $25e^{-2\nu}=0.0851195\,\mathrm{mM}$. They are depleted, not zero. Using $1\,\mathrm{mM}=1\,\mathrm{mol\,m^{-3}}$ and multiplying molar [concentrations](../../../../concentration.md) by the [Avogadro constant](../../../../avogadro-constant.md) in the [Grahame equation](../../../../grahame-equation.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\sigma=-0.103243\,\mathrm{C\,m^{-2}}.}
$$

This calculation retains every species. Since $|\phi_0|/V_T=2.84$, a linear [Debye–Hückel approximation](../../../../debye-huckel-approximation.md) is not justified. The large accumulation, especially of divalent counterions, follows from the valence in the [Boltzmann factor](../../../../boltzmann-factor.md). However, multi-molar local [concentrations](../../../../concentration.md) also warn that point-ion [mean-field theory](../../../../mean-field-theory.md) neglects finite ion size, hydration, specific adsorption, correlations, and changes in local [permittivity](../../../../permittivity-split.md). These are predictions of the stated idealized model, not precise measured interfacial [concentrations](../../../../concentration.md).
