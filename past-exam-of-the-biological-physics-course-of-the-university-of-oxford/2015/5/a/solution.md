<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

At [thermal equilibrium](../../../../thermal-equilibrium-split.md), [detailed balance](../../../../detailed-balance.md) equates the opposing transition fluxes: $k_f p_A^{\rm eq}=k_b p_B^{\rm eq}$. Define the state [free energy](../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) difference explicitly by $\Delta G_{AB}=G_B-G_A$. The equilibrium [Boltzmann distribution](../../../../boltzmann-distribution.md) then gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{k_f}{k_b}=\frac{p_B^{\rm eq}}{p_A^{\rm eq}}=e^{-\Delta G_{AB}/(k_BT)}.}
$$

Any state degeneracy is included in the state [free energies](../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md). Detailed balance is stronger than simply requiring a time-independent total occupancy: it equates each pair of equilibrium transition fluxes.

**Source illustration TODO:** the source's [reaction coordinate](../../../../reaction-coordinate.md) diagram is missing. The definition above fixes the sign independently; no barrier heights or ordering of drawn energy levels are inferred. Below, a large _forward free-energy drop_ means $G_A-G_B\gg k_BT$. If instead $\Delta G_{AB}=G_B-G_A\gg k_BT$, forward transitions are unfavorable: for example $\Delta G_{AB}=10k_BT$ gives $k_f/k_b=e^{-10}$, so neglecting reverse transitions would be wrong.
