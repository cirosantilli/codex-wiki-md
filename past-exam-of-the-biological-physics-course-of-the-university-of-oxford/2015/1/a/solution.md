<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [messenger RNA](../../../../messenger-rna.md) has [steady state](../../../../steady-state.md) $X_{R,*}=\beta_R/\alpha_R$. Since [biological translation](../../../../translation-biology.md) produces [proteins](../../../../protein-split.md) at rate $rX_R$, setting the [protein degradation](../../../../protein-degradation.md) rate equal to this production rate gives

$$
\boxed{X_{P,*}=\frac{r\beta_R}{\alpha_P\alpha_R}=\frac{b\beta_R}{\alpha_P},\qquad b=\frac r{\alpha_R}.}
$$

The [translational burst size](../../../../translational-burst-size.md) is the expected number of [proteins](../../../../protein-split.md) made during one [messenger RNA](../../../../messenger-rna.md) lifetime, whose [expected value](../../../../expected-value-split.md) is $1/\alpha_R$. It is dimensionless, rather than a production rate. With independent [Poisson process](../../../../poisson-process-split.md) translation and an [exponential distribution](../../../../exponential-distribution.md) for the lifetime, the resulting count has a [geometric distribution](../../../../geometric-distribution.md) on nonnegative integers with [expected value](../../../../expected-value-split.md) $b$ and [variance](../../../../variance-split.md) $b(1+b)$.
