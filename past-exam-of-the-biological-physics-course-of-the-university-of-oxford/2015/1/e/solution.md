<h1 id="1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

A [green fluorescent protein](../../../../green-fluorescent-protein.md) readout follows the corresponding periodically regulated expression, so an individual cell has repeated peaks in [fluorescence](../../../../fluorescence-split.md). [Biological translation](../../../../translation-biology.md), fluorophore maturation, and [protein degradation](../../../../protein-degradation.md) introduce a delay and smooth the peaks. For a simple mature [fluorescent reporter](../../../../fluorescent-reporter.md) with periodic production $s(t)$, $\dot G=s(t)-\gamma G$ gives $G(t)=G(0)e^{-\gamma t}+\int_0^t e^{-\gamma(t-u)}s(u)\,du$. A long lifetime therefore makes the modulation weak about an accumulated background. [Intrinsic gene-expression noise](../../../../intrinsic-gene-expression-noise.md) makes successive peak heights and periods irregular; averaging cells with unrelated oscillation phases can hide the single-cell oscillations.
