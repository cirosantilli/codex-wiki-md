Here $V_m$ is the inside-minus-outside [membrane potential](../../../../membrane-potential-split.md), $V_K$ is the [potassium](../../../../potassium.md) [Nernst equilibrium potential](../../../../nernst-equilibrium-potential.md), and $\bar g_K$ is the maximum [potassium](../../../../potassium.md) conductance, or conductance per area if $I_K$ is a [current density](../../../../current-density.md). The factor $n\in[0,1]$ is the permissive-state [probability](../../../../probability.md) of an activation gate. Four [independent permissive gates in an ion channel](../../../../independent-permissive-gates-in-an-ion-channel.md) give the open fraction $n^4$, hence

$$
I_K=n^4\bar g_K(V_m-V_K).
$$

The model assumes identical independent gating units, additive currents from many [ion channels](../../../../ion-channel.md), an approximately ohmic open-pore current, and fixed ionic activities setting $V_K$. Outward positive [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md) is obtained for $V_m>V_K$. The original delayed-rectifier term omits rapid [ion-channel inactivation](../../../../ion-channel-inactivation.md). The fourth power is a successful phenomenological gating description, rather than a deduction of the molecular structure from this equation alone.
