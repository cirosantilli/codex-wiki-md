<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Alan Hodgkin](../../../../alan-hodgkin.md) and [Andrew Huxley](../../../../andrew-huxley.md) recorded the [membrane potential](../../../../membrane-potential-split.md) directly between the inside of a squid axon and the external solution. The crucial result was an [action potential](../../../../action-potential.md) that passed through zero: the interior became positive, rather than merely losing its negative resting [membrane potential](../../../../membrane-potential-split.md). A theory in which excitation simply abolishes membrane selectivity and shorts out the resting [membrane potential](../../../../membrane-potential-split.md) cannot account for that reversal. Their original report establishes the historical interpretation independently of the unavailable examination picture.[Nature 144 (1939), pp. 710–711: intracellular recording and reversal of the potential difference](../../../../action-potentials-recorded-from-inside-a-nerve-fibre-by-alan-hodgkin-and-andrew-huxley.md)

The later [Hodgkin-Huxley model](../../../../hodgkin-huxley-model.md) explains the rising phase through increased [sodium](../../../../sodium.md) permeability and the falling phase through [sodium](../../../../sodium.md) [ion-channel inactivation](../../../../ion-channel-inactivation.md) and increased [potassium](../../../../potassium.md) permeability. Distinct [Nernst equilibrium potentials](../../../../nernst-equilibrium-potential.md) make a selective conductance change capable of reversing the [membrane potential](../../../../membrane-potential-split.md). The recording established the phenomenon; it did not by itself reveal individual [ion channel](../../../../ion-channel.md) structures or all their gating kinetics.
