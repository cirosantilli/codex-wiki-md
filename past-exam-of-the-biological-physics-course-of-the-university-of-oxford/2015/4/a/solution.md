<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In [single-molecule force spectroscopy](../../../../single-molecule-force-spectroscopy.md) of [titin](../../../../titin.md), one end of the [protein](../../../../protein-split.md) is attached to a surface and the other to an [atomic force microscope](../../../../atomic-force-microscope.md) cantilever. Moving the support stretches the molecule; a calibrated cantilever deflection measures the [force](../../../../force-split.md) through $f=k_c\delta$. The molecular extension is the imposed separation minus cantilever deflection and other instrumental offsets, rather than the uncorrected support displacement.

At first, increasing extension removes conformational slack from unstructured segments. Their [conformational entropy](../../../../conformational-entropy.md) decreases and the resisting [force](../../../../force-split.md) rises. A folded [protein domain](../../../../protein-domain.md) then unfolds when a thermally activated transition becomes likely under load. Its residues join the stretched chain, increasing the available [polymer contour length](../../../../polymer-contour-length.md). At almost the same imposed separation, the newly released segment has slack: the [force](../../../../force-split.md) drops. Further pulling straightens this longer chain until another domain unfolds. Repetition produces a sawtooth sequence; each rising branch mainly measures stretching between unfolding events, and a peak marks the end of such a branch.

The peak height measures the resistance to unfolding under the particular loading history, not a direct equilibrium [free energy](../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) or an invariant breaking [force](../../../../force-split.md). Random unfolding times, loading rate, domain identity, attachments, and cantilever compliance affect the record. Releasing tension can allow [protein folding](../../../../protein-folding.md), but refolding has its own kinetics and need not retrace the loading curve. Distinct unfolding events explain how a long [protein](../../../../protein-split.md) can absorb extension in steps rather than failing at a single threshold.
