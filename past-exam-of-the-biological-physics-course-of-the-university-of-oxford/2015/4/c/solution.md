↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

An inextensible [worm-like chain](../../../../worm-like-chain.md) describes [entropic elasticity](../../../../entropic-elasticity.md) and diverges as $x\to L$. Real [protein](../../../../protein-split.md) backbones and folded [protein domains](../../../../protein-domain.md) also deform energetically under large [forces](../../../../force-split.md). For an axial stretch modulus $K_s$, a simple extensible-chain correction is

$$
\frac xL\simeq1-\frac12\sqrt{\frac{k_BT}{fL_p}}+\frac f{K_s}.
$$

The last term supplies extension through bond and structural deformation that the inextensible model excludes. It becomes especially noticeable on the high-force ends of rising branches.[High-force entropic and energetic extension terms](../../../../energetic-extension-of-a-strongly-stretched-worm-like-chain.md) An unfolding transition itself is also outside a single equilibrium fixed-$L$ branch. These mechanisms explain why the source explicitly distinguishes energetic and [entropic elasticity](../../../../entropic-elasticity.md).
