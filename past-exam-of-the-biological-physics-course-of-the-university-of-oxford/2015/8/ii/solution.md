<h1 id="8/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

An [ion channel](../../../../ion-channel.md) is a selective permeation pathway, not simply an aqueous hole of fixed diameter. A hydrated [ion](../../../../ion.md) entering a narrow [selectivity filter](../../../../selectivity-filter.md) must lose or reorganize some hydration water. The energetic cost can be offset by interactions with charged or polar groups in the pore. Different ionic radii, valences, hydration [free energies](../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md), and coordination geometries therefore produce different permeation barriers and binding affinities.

A classical [potassium](../../../../potassium.md) [selectivity filter](../../../../selectivity-filter.md) presents oxygen ligands that stabilize relatively dehydrated [potassium](../../../../potassium.md) in configurations resembling its aqueous coordination. Smaller [sodium](../../../../sodium.md) does not automatically pass more easily: its hydration is stronger and the filter need not provide a compensating configuration at equally low [free energy](../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md). Filter flexibility and multi-ion occupancy matter, so the explanation is an energetic balance, not the assertion that a rigid hole simply blocks every larger or smaller sphere. Ion-ion interactions and coupled displacement can permit rapid conduction while maintaining selectivity.[Science 280 (1998), pp. 69–77: structural basis of potassium coordination and selective permeation](../../../../the-structure-of-the-potassium-channel-molecular-basis-of-k-plus-conduction-and-selectivity-by-declan-a-doyle-and-others.md)

Other [ion channels](../../../../ion-channel.md) use different arrangements. Fixed negative groups can strongly favor divalent [calcium](../../../../calcium.md) over monovalent [ions](../../../../ion.md); occupancy and competition then influence both selectivity and throughput. Selectivity is distinct from activation or [ion-channel inactivation](../../../../ion-channel-inactivation.md), which control access or availability. Selective passive flux follows an [electrochemical potential](../../../../electrochemical-potential.md) gradient; it does not by itself pump [ions](../../../../ion.md) uphill. Selectivity allows cells to establish particular [membrane potentials](../../../../membrane-potential-split.md), generate [action potentials](../../../../action-potential.md), regulate [calcium](../../../../calcium.md) signals, and control solute balance without dissipating every ionic gradient whenever one pathway opens.
