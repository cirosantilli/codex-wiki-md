<h1 id="4/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

For ideal freely mobile [counterions](../../../../counterion.md), their gross [osmotic pressure](../../../../osmotic-pressure.md) is

$$
\boxed{\Pi=\frac{Nk_BT}{V_c}=4.94\times10^6\,\mathrm{Pa}\simeq49\,\mathrm{bar}\quad(T=300\,\mathrm K).}
$$

If the [DNA](../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) volume is excluded from the ion-accessible [volume](../../../../volume.md), use $V_f=V_c-V_D$ instead, giving $\Pi\simeq1.23\times10^7\,\mathrm{Pa}\simeq123\,\mathrm{bar}$. These are ideal-ion estimates; bound [counterions](../../../../counterion.md) contribute less like independent mobile particles, and the net outward [osmotic pressure](../../../../osmotic-pressure.md) also subtracts the external osmotic contribution.

Stored [free energy](../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) associated with this [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md), together with bending and interstrand repulsion, can favor initial genome release when a delivery pathway becomes available. This explains a possible mechanical benefit of dense packaging at a conceptual level. It does not imply that internal [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md) necessarily supplies all the [work](../../../../work.md) of complete genome delivery into a host, whose own environment and interactions also matter.
