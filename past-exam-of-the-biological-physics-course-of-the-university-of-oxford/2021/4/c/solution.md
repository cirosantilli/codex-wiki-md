↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The bare packed-sphere estimate exceeds $2.1\times10^5\,\mathrm{pN\,nm}$ by approximately $659$; subtracting the estimated extended-rod self-energy still leaves a factor of about $629$. The dominant missing effect is [electrostatic screening](../../../../electrostatic-screening.md): mobile positive [counterions](../../../../counterion.md) accompany the negatively charged [DNA](../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md), greatly reducing long-range repulsion. [Counterion condensation](../../../../counterion-condensation.md) can further reduce its effective [electric charge](../../../../electric-charge-split.md). Treating the genome's bare charge as uncompensated throughout and outside a $20\,\mathrm{nm}$ sphere therefore grossly overestimates its long-range [electrostatic energy](../../../../electrostatic-energy.md).

An [optical tweezers](../../../../optical-tweezers.md) measurement integrates an imposed [force](../../../../force-split.md) over a displacement. A reversible extraction or packaging comparison concerns a [free energy](../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) difference relative to the unconfined [DNA](../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) and ionic reservoirs. It can include [entropy](../../../../entropy-split.md), bending and hydration contributions; dissipative pulling can add further [work](../../../../work.md). Extraction can also be assisted by stored internal [free energy](../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md), so the sign of externally supplied [work](../../../../work.md) requires the experimental convention. Here the supplied positive value is compared as an energy magnitude.
