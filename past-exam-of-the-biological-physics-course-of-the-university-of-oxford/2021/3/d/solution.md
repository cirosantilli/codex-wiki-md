<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The small binding [RNA](../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) can serve conceptually as an [RNA aptamer](../../../../rna-aptamer.md) recognition probe. Coupling recognition to an optical label would make a specifically bound [virus](../../../../virus.md) appear as a moving fluorescent spot. [Single-particle tracking](../../../../single-particle-tracking.md) estimates successive spot positions and fits their displacement statistics. Specificity is assessed by comparison with a nonbinding recognition probe or a sample without the target, while the much faster [diffusion](../../../../diffusion.md) of unbound probes distinguishes freely moving label from target-associated signal. Subnanomolar [dissociation constant](../../../../dissociation-constant.md) supports appreciable occupancy at low probe abundance, but affinity alone does not determine the dissociation lifetime because $K_d=k_{\rm off}/k_{\rm on}$. This describes detection principles, not an operational virus preparation or labelling protocol.

For a dilute spherical particle of radius $a=60\,\mathrm{nm}$ in bulk [water](../../../../water.md), the [Stokes–Einstein relation](../../../../stokes-einstein-relation.md) gives, at an explicitly assumed $T=300\,\mathrm K$,

$$
\boxed{D=\frac{k_BT}{6\pi\eta a}=3.66\,\mathrm{\mu m^2\,s^{-1}}.}
$$

A $2\,\mathrm{nm}$ free probe would have $D\simeq110\,\mathrm{\mu m^2\,s^{-1}}$, roughly thirty times larger. The recognition probe changes the target's hydrodynamic size only modestly in this estimate.

For isotropic [Brownian motion](../../../../brownian-motion-split.md), the mean vector displacement is zero. In $t=0.1\,\mathrm s$, distinct measures of how far it moves are

$$
\boxed{\sqrt{\langle|\Delta\mathbf r|^2\rangle}=\sqrt{6Dt}=1.48\,\mathrm{\mu m},\qquad
\langle|\Delta\mathbf r|\rangle=4\sqrt{Dt/\pi}=1.37\,\mathrm{\mu m}.}
$$

A two-dimensional image instead gives projected root-mean-square displacement $\sqrt{4Dt}=1.21\,\mathrm{\mu m}$ and mean projected distance $\sqrt{\pi Dt}=1.07\,\mathrm{\mu m}$. The projected [mean squared displacement](../../../../mean-squared-displacement.md) is $4D\Delta t$; independent localization errors of [variance](../../../../variance-split.md) $\sigma^2$ in each coordinate add $4\sigma^2$. Finite exposure blurs motion, and nearby walls reduce [diffusion](../../../../diffusion.md); those effects must be separated from particle size when interpreting a trajectory.
