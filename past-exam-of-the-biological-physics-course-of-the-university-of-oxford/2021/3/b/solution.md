<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For an inextensible [worm-like chain](../../../../worm-like-chain.md), the [DNA bending energy](../../../../dna-bending-energy.md) is

$$
G_b=\frac{k_BT l_p}{2}\int \kappa(\ell)^2\,d\ell,
$$

where $\kappa$ is curvature and $\ell$ is [polymer contour length](../../../../polymer-contour-length.md). A circle of radius $R_i$ has $\kappa=1/R_i$ and length $2\pi R_i$. Its [elastic energy](../../../../elastic-energy.md) is therefore $(k_BT l_p/2)(2\pi R_i)/R_i^2=\pi l_pk_BT/R_i$. Adding the contributions of $n(R_i)$ rings at each radius gives

$$
\boxed{\Delta G_{\rm elastic}=\pi l_pk_BT\sum_i\frac{n(R_i)}{R_i}.}
$$

This ignores short connecting segments, twist, interactions between neighboring [DNA](../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) segments and end effects. In a cylindrical stack with axial spacing $s$, $n(R_i)\simeq h/s$ away from the ends.
