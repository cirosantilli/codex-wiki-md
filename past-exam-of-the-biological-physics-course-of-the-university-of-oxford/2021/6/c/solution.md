<h1 id="6/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

An [ion channel](../../../../ion-channel.md) supplies a water-accessible pathway through the otherwise strongly insulating, [hydrophobic](../../../../hydrophobicity.md) [lipid bilayer](../../../../lipid-bilayer.md). Moving a hydrated [ion](../../../../ion.md) directly into that low-[permittivity](../../../../permittivity-split.md) region is energetically expensive. The channel's polar pore and coordinating groups reduce the barrier while retaining selectivity. Transport is passive: the [electrochemical potential](../../../../electrochemical-potential.md) difference drives net movement, and the channel does not itself pump an [ion](../../../../ion.md) uphill. For an ideal species of charge number $z$,

$$
\Delta\widetilde\mu_{\rm out\to in}=RT\ln\frac{c_{\rm in}}{c_{\rm out}}+zFV.
$$

The net [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md) of a pathway permeable only to that species reverses at its [Nernst equilibrium potential](../../../../nernst-equilibrium-potential.md). An open channel can carry zero net [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md) at that voltage even while individual [ions](../../../../ion.md) cross both ways.

The [selectivity filter](../../../../selectivity-filter.md) balances loss of the hydration shell against favorable coordination within the pore. In a typical [potassium](../../../../potassium.md) channel, backbone carbonyl oxygen atoms create appropriately arranged binding sites for a substantially dehydrated [potassium](../../../../potassium.md) [ion](../../../../ion.md). The smaller [sodium](../../../../sodium.md) [ion](../../../../ion.md) is not automatically preferred simply because it fits geometrically: its hydration is stronger and the filter does not compensate its dehydration in the same way. Geometry, flexibility, coordination chemistry and ion occupancy all matter. [Sodium](../../../../sodium.md)-selective and [calcium](../../../../calcium.md)-selective channels use different arrangements, often involving charged side chains; a single rigid-sieve explanation is inadequate.

Rapid passage coexists with transient binding because several nearby sites have comparable [free energies](../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) and relatively low barriers between them. Multiple [ions](../../../../ion.md) occupying a pore influence one another through [electrostatic interactions](../../../../electrostatic-interaction.md), enabling correlated exchange and displacement rather than irreversible trapping at one deep site. High throughput therefore requires favorable passage kinetics as well as strong discrimination against unwanted species. A more tightly bound species can even block a pathway instead of being its fastest permeant.

Gating and selectivity are distinct. A [voltage-sensing domain](../../../../voltage-sensing-domain.md) couples [membrane potential](../../../../membrane-potential-split.md) to activation-gate conformation; [ligand-gated ion channels](../../../../ligand-gated-ion-channel.md) couple binding to opening; [mechanosensitive ion channels](../../../../mechanosensitive-ion-channel.md) couple membrane stress to opening. [Ion-channel inactivation](../../../../ion-channel-inactivation.md) introduces additional unavailable states. None of these mechanisms alone identifies which [ion](../../../../ion.md) the open pore prefers.

For $N$ similar independent channels, a local Ohmic approximation with outward-positive [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md) is

$$
\boxed{I=Np_O\gamma(V-E_{\rm rev}).}
$$

Here open [probability](../../../../probability.md) controls availability, single-channel [electrical conductance](../../../../electrical-conductance.md) controls current per open pore, and $V-E_{\rm rev}$ is the electrical driving factor in this approximation. Nonlinear current-voltage relations and mixed-ion permeation may require a fuller model. [Single-channel recording](../../../../single-channel-recording.md) resolves discrete openings; reversal shifts when ionic [concentrations](../../../../concentration.md) change test selectivity; gating [electric currents](../../../../electric-current.md) test sensor motion; structural evidence identifies the pore and its coordinating groups. Together these distinguish rapid selective permeation from the separate process of opening the gate.
