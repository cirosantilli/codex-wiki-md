<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Magnetic tweezers](../../../../magnetic-tweezers.md) apply [force](../../../../force-split.md) and controlled turns to a single torsionally constrained [DNA](../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) tether joining a surface and a magnetic bead. A magnetic-field gradient pulls the bead; rotation of the field rotates the bead and changes imposed turns. Imaging measures bead height, hence [DNA](../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) extension. At sufficient excess twisting and suitable [force](../../../../force-split.md), [DNA supercoiling](../../../../dna-supercoiling.md) creates writhed loops that shorten the tether. The extension-versus-turn curve supplies a calibration relating the removal or addition of supercoiling to vertical motion. Multiple attachments at both ends are needed conceptually to constrain rotation; freely swivelling [DNA](../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) would relax the signal.

During [transcription initiation](../../../../transcription-initiation.md), [RNA polymerase](../../../../rna-polymerase.md) binds a [promoter](../../../../promoter-genetics.md) in a closed complex and then opens a local [DNA](../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) bubble, exposing template for [RNA](../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) synthesis. In bacterial initiation a [sigma factor](../../../../sigma-factor.md) helps recognize the [promoter](../../../../promoter-genetics.md). Opening changes local helical twist. Because the total [linking number](../../../../linking-number.md) is constrained,

$$
Lk=Tw+Wr,\qquad \Delta Tw_{\rm rest}+\Delta Wr=-\Delta Tw_{\rm bubble}.
$$

Unwinding the bubble means $\Delta Tw_{\rm bubble}<0$, so the remainder acquires a positive turn increment. A negatively supercoiled tether therefore loses some negative writhe and becomes longer; a positively supercoiled tether gains positive writhe and becomes shorter. Ordinary [DNA](../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) compaction instead shortens both tethers. Comparing both signs separates local unwinding from compaction rather than assigning every height change to opening.

If $q$ is the number of turns unwound, $s$ the magnitude of the extension change per imposed turn, and $c$ the compaction, the symmetric-slope approximation predicts $\Delta z_-=sq-c$ and $\Delta z_+=-sq-c$. Hence

$$
\boxed{q=\frac{\Delta z_--\Delta z_+}{2s},\qquad
c=-\frac{\Delta z_-+\Delta z_+}{2}.}
$$

Measured unequal slopes can be retained in the two separate calibration equations. Multiplying $q$ by the helical repeat converts turns to an estimated number of unwound [base pairs](../../../../base-pair.md), subject to the mechanical model and the distinction between twist and writhe.

Repeated extension transitions reveal open-complex lifetimes, closed-complex lifetimes and their dependence on mechanical [torque](../../../../torque.md). Initial short [RNA](../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) synthesis can occur while [RNA polymerase](../../../../rna-polymerase.md) remains associated with the [promoter](../../../../promoter-genetics.md), including abortive initiation and [DNA](../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) scrunching. Addition of nucleotides allows progression to [promoter escape](../../../../promoter-escape.md); an unwound bubble can then persist with elongation, so loss of the bubble is not the definition of escape. Successive cumulative unwinding events can identify renewed access to the same [promoter](../../../../promoter-genetics.md) after clearance. These are the measurement principles demonstrated by the paired-sign experiments.[Figures 1–4: extension calibration, opposite-sign opening responses and promoter-clearance readout](../../../../promoter-unwinding-and-promoter-clearance-by-rna-polymerase-detection-by-single-molecule-dna-nanomanipulation-by-andrey-revyakin-and-others.md)

The experiment observes mechanics rather than directly imaging individual opened [base pairs](../../../../base-pair.md). Drift, nonspecific binding, tether integrity, [force](../../../../force-split.md)-dependent buckling and limited time resolution can mimic or obscure steps. A control lacking productive [promoter](../../../../promoter-genetics.md) recognition and agreement of the opposite-sign responses help identify the origin of a signal.
