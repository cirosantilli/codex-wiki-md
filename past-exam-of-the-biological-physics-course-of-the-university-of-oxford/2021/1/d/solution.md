<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The passive upper limiting [membrane potential](../../../../membrane-potential-split.md) for a purely [sodium](../../../../sodium.md)-selective pathway is its [Nernst equilibrium potential](../../../../nernst-equilibrium-potential.md). Equal inside and outside [electrochemical potentials](../../../../electrochemical-potential.md), using ideal [concentrations](../../../../concentration.md), require

$$
RT\ln\frac{c_{\rm in}}{c_{\rm out}}+FV=0,
\qquad
\boxed{E_{\rm Na}=\frac{RT}{F}\ln\frac{145}{15}
=+60.6\,\mathrm{mV}\quad(T=310.15\,\mathrm K).}
$$

This is the maximum absolute inside-minus-outside voltage under the stated passive assumptions. If the reference resting [membrane potential](../../../../membrane-potential-split.md) is $-60\,\mathrm{mV}$, the corresponding increase is about $121\,\mathrm{mV}$.

Actual [action potentials](../../../../action-potential.md) usually peak below $E_{\rm Na}$ because [voltage-gated sodium channels](../../../../voltage-gated-sodium-channel.md) undergo [ion-channel inactivation](../../../../ion-channel-inactivation.md), while outward [potassium](../../../../potassium.md) and leak [electric currents](../../../../electric-current.md) oppose inward [sodium](../../../../sodium.md) [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md). Finite [membrane capacitance](../../../../membrane-capacitance.md) and finite available time also prevent instantaneous equilibration. At $V=E_{\rm Na}$, the [sodium](../../../../sodium.md) driving force itself is zero.
