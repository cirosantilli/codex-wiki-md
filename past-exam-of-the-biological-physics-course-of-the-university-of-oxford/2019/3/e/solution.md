<h1 id="3/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

A fluorescent reporter associated with one phase of the [repressilator](../../../../repressilator.md) should show repeated rises and falls in a tracked cell, with the same underlying oscillatory period but generally delayed and smoothed relative to instantaneous [promoter](../../../../promoter-genetics.md) activity. If mature [GFP](../../../../green-fluorescent-protein.md) is fused to a repressor and is sufficiently fast to mature, [fluorescence](../../../../fluorescence-split.md) approximately tracks that [protein](../../../../protein-split.md)'s abundance. If [GFP](../../../../green-fluorescent-protein.md) is a separately expressed reporter, its own production, maturation, degradation and dilution introduce an additional filter.

For example, $\dot G=\beta_Gg(t)-\delta_GG$ converts a periodic gate $g(t)$ into a periodic, phase-lagged [concentration](../../../../concentration.md). Very stable [GFP](../../../../green-fluorescent-protein.md) can accumulate a large baseline and obscure oscillations rather than reproducing the regulator trace exactly; maturation delay and [bleaching](../../../../photobleaching.md) also matter. Biological noise and cell-to-cell phase differences can make individual cycles irregular and wash out oscillations in a population average. The appropriate sketch is therefore a delayed, rounded oscillatory [fluorescence](../../../../fluorescence-split.md) trace, with amplitude and baseline depending on reporter turnover, rather than an unqualified copy of a Boolean gate.
