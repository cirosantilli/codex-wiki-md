<h1 id="6/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The hydrophobic core of a [lipid bilayer](../../../../lipid-bilayer.md) makes transfer of a hydrated [ion](../../../../ion.md) into bare lipid energetically costly. An [ion channel](../../../../ion-channel.md) supplies a water-accessible route across that barrier. Its narrow [selectivity filter](../../../../selectivity-filter.md) presents coordinating chemical groups that compensate for loss or rearrangement of the [ion](../../../../ion.md)'s [hydration shell](../../../../hydration-shell.md). Selectivity depends on the [free-energy](../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) balance of hydration, coordination, [electrostatics](../../../../electrostatics-split.md) and geometry, rather than a sieve that passes all smaller [ions](../../../../ion.md).

A [potassium](../../../../potassium.md)-channel filter presents backbone carbonyl oxygens in a geometry favourable for [potassium](../../../../potassium.md); smaller [sodium](../../../../sodium.md) does not automatically gain the same energetic compensation. [Sodium](../../../../sodium.md) and [calcium](../../../../calcium.md) channels use different filter architectures: charged side chains and their coordination environments favour particular [ions](../../../../ion.md), with [ion](../../../../ion.md) occupancy and competing species also affecting permeation. In canonical vertebrate [voltage-gated sodium channels](../../../../voltage-gated-sodium-channel.md), the DEKA selectivity ring illustrates chemical discrimination distinct from [potassium](../../../../potassium.md)-channel carbonyl coordination. The precise mechanism therefore depends on the channel family.

Rapid flux requires low _successive_ [free-energy](../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) barriers, not permanent binding of the preferred [ion](../../../../ion.md). Several occupied sites, nearby water and [ion](../../../../ion.md)–[ion](../../../../ion.md) interactions can facilitate passage without requiring the [ion](../../../../ion.md) to cross the lipid. For scale, a $100\,\mathrm{pS}$ open channel driven by $100\,\mathrm{mV}$ carries $10\,\mathrm{pA}$, approximately $6\times10^7$ monovalent [ions](../../../../ion.md) per second. A nanometre-scale route with favourable coordination can support such rapid, thermally driven motion. An [ion channel](../../../../ion-channel.md) is not an [ATP](../../../../adenosine-triphosphate.md)-driven pump: net flux follows the [electrochemical potential](../../../../electrochemical-potential.md) difference,

$$
\boxed{\Delta\mu= k_BT\ln\frac{c_{\rm in}}{c_{\rm out}}+ze(V_{\rm in}-V_{\rm out}),}
$$

which vanishes at the species' [Nernst equilibrium potential](../../../../nernst-equilibrium-potential.md). [ATP](../../../../adenosine-triphosphate.md)-consuming pumps maintain the [gradients](../../../../gradient.md) that make sustained channel currents possible.

Permeation selectivity and opening [probability](../../../../probability.md) are separate properties. Voltage-sensor movement, [ligand](../../../../ligand-biochemistry.md) binding or mechanical tension can regulate access to the pore, while [inactivation](../../../../ion-channel-inactivation.md) can terminate conduction during a maintained stimulus. [Patch-clamp](../../../../patch-clamp.md) unitary currents establish discrete conducting states; reversal potentials under changed ionic compositions quantify selectivity. Structure determination locates the filter and gates, and [mutations](../../../../mutation.md) or specific blockers test their functional roles. Agreement between these measurements is stronger evidence than structure or current shape alone.
