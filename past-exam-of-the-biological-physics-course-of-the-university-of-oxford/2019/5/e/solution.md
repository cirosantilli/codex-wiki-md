<h1 id="5/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

At fixed $N$ and $b$, the [entropic spring](../../../../entropic-spring.md) constant is proportional to absolute [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md), because the [free-energy](../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) penalty for a given loss of [conformational entropy](../../../../conformational-entropy.md) is $-T\Delta S$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{k(10^\circ\mathrm C)=k(25^\circ\mathrm C)\frac{283.15}{298.15}=0.94969\,k(25^\circ\mathrm C).}
$$

For the literal printed chain this is $\boxed{1.287\times10^{-6}\,\mathrm{N\,m^{-1}}}$. The same factor multiplies either alternative contour-consistent [spring constant](../../../../spring-constant.md) from part (d), provided its [Kuhn length](../../../../kuhn-length.md) is held fixed. Real [DNA](../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md)'s [persistence length](../../../../persistence-length.md), [base pairing](../../../../base-pairing.md) and solvent interactions can themselves vary with [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) and salt; then $b(T)$ contributes through $k\propto T/b(T)^2$ at fixed segment count, or $k\propto T/[L_cb(T)]$ if the physical contour is instead held fixed. The simple linear [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) result belongs to the specified ideal-chain model.
