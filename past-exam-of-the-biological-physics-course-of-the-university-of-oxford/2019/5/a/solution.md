<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In a tensile [atomic force microscopy](../../../../atomic-force-microscopy.md) measurement, a [DNA](../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) tether connects the [cantilever](../../../../cantilever.md) tip and a surface. Moving the support stretches the [molecule](../../../../molecule-split.md), and calibrated [cantilever](../../../../cantilever.md) deflection supplies the tensile [force](../../../../force-split.md); extension must be corrected for [cantilever](../../../../cantilever.md) deflection and any linkers. At low [force](../../../../force-split.md), extension mainly straightens an initially fluctuating coil, giving approximately linear [entropic elasticity](../../../../entropic-elasticity.md). At higher [force](../../../../force-split.md) the available conformations become increasingly aligned, so additional extension costs progressively greater [force](../../../../force-split.md). The readable source curve then continues beyond the nominal inextensible-model contour length, which signals a failure of that simple model or of the stated parameter interpretation, rather than negative elasticity.
