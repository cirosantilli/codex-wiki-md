<h1 id="5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

An inextensible [freely jointed chain](../../../../ideal-chain.md) of $N$ links of [Kuhn length](../../../../kuhn-length.md) $b$ has contour length $L_c=Nb$ and cannot be extended beyond it at any finite [force](../../../../force-split.md). There is an explicit numerical contradiction in the printed parameters:

$$
\boxed{186\times7\,\mathrm{nm}=1302\,\mathrm{nm}=1.302\,\mu\mathrm m,\quad\text{not }26\,\mu\mathrm m.}
$$

The discrepancy is present in the original page, not introduced by digitization. To obtain $26\,\mu\mathrm m$ with $186$ links requires $b\simeq140\,\mathrm{nm}$; to obtain it with $b=7\,\mathrm{nm}$ requires $N\simeq3714$. No single inextensible FJC can satisfy all three stated numbers. The graph's much larger extension is an immediate counterexample to treating the literal $N,b$ as an accurate global fit.

Separately, accepting the graph's nominal $26\,\mu\mathrm m$ contour as an effective low-[force](../../../../force-split.md) model, its continuation to $32.7\,\mu\mathrm m$ is about $26\%$ beyond that contour. An inextensible [ideal chain](../../../../ideal-chain.md) contains orientational [entropy](../../../../entropy-split.md) only; it excludes stretching of segments, energetic compliance of bonds, changes in helix structure or [base pairing](../../../../base-pairing.md), and heterogeneous attachments. An [extensible freely jointed chain](../../../../extensible-freely-jointed-chain.md) or a semiflexible-chain model with appropriate energetic terms can describe some departures. Calibration or contour-length assignment can also be wrong. The graph alone does not identify which mechanism contributes, and its displayed moderate [forces](../../../../force-split.md) do not justify assigning a specific high-[force](../../../../force-split.md) [DNA](../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) structural transition. Arithmetic inconsistency in $Nb$ is not explained away by these physical mechanisms.
