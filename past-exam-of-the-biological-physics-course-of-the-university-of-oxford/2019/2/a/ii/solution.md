<h1 id="2/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The intracellular recording by [Alan Hodgkin](../../../../../alan-hodgkin.md) and [Andrew Huxley](../../../../../andrew-huxley.md) shows the membrane voltage rising from a negative resting value to a positive overshoot, with the outside held at zero reference. The [action potential](../../../../../action-potential.md) therefore does more than transiently abolish a resting polarization: it reverses the sign across the membrane. A simple hypothesis that excitation only makes an otherwise [potassium](../../../../../potassium.md)-dominated membrane equally leaky cannot explain that positive overshoot.

The result helped motivate a transient, selective change in membrane permeability, later resolved into rapid inward [sodium](../../../../../sodium.md) current followed by outward [potassium](../../../../../potassium.md) current. The positive [sodium](../../../../../sodium.md) [equilibrium](../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) potential calculated above makes the overshoot physically plausible, while [potassium](../../../../../potassium.md) conductance favours a negative resting voltage and [repolarization](../../../../../repolarization.md). The 1939 voltage trace itself did not identify individual channel [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md) or establish the full later voltage-dependent gating mechanism; those conclusions required ionic substitution and [voltage-clamp](../../../../../voltage-clamp.md) evidence.[1939 intracellular recording and reversal of membrane voltage during excitation](../../../../../action-potentials-recorded-from-inside-a-nerve-fibre-by-alan-hodgkin-and-andrew-huxley.md)
