<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Panel B steps the membrane from approximately $-80$ to $-40\,\mathrm{mV}$ and holds it there. The [sodium](../../../../sodium.md) current becomes inward, peaks rapidly, and decays almost to zero despite maintained [depolarization](../../../../depolarization.md). At $-40\,\mathrm{mV}$ the [sodium](../../../../sodium.md) driving [force](../../../../force-split.md) $V-E_{\rm Na}$ is negative, consistent with the negative current convention. A conductance description is

$$
I_{\rm Na}=N\gamma p_O(V-E_{\rm Na}),
$$

where $N$ is channel number, $\gamma$ single-channel conductance and $p_O$ the open [probability](../../../../probability.md). The trace requires rapid voltage-dependent activation followed by slower [inactivation](../../../../ion-channel-inactivation.md), rather than a passive pore whose only response is an instantaneous constant current. Recovery normally requires [repolarization](../../../../repolarization.md), helping produce a refractory period and directional propagation of excitation.

A typical eukaryotic [voltage-gated sodium channel](../../../../voltage-gated-sodium-channel.md) contains four homologous membrane-spanning domains in one large pore-forming [protein](../../../../protein-split.md). Charged S4 helices sense voltage; pore-associated regions form a selective pathway and activation gate. Fast [inactivation](../../../../ion-channel-inactivation.md) involves intracellular machinery coupled to the pore, classically the interdomain III–IV region, and should not be identified automatically with the tethered [potassium](../../../../potassium.md)-channel blocker. The pore permits rapid downhill electrochemical transport while maintaining selectivity against other [ions](../../../../ion.md).

Evidence separates these properties: clamp-step experiments distinguish voltage dependence and activation from decay at a fixed voltage; changing extracellular [sodium](../../../../sodium.md) shifts reversal potential; selective blockers isolate [sodium](../../../../sodium.md) current; [patch-clamp](../../../../patch-clamp.md) records resolve stochastic single-channel openings; and [mutations](../../../../mutation.md) of sensors, filter residues or the fast-[inactivation](../../../../ion-channel-inactivation.md) machinery alter the corresponding function. A single macroscopic trace alone cannot uniquely determine all microscopic [rate constants](../../../../rate-constant.md).
