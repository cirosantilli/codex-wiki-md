<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The original Panel A is readable: the baseline is roughly $-45\,\mathrm{mV}$ and the peak about $+40\,\mathrm{mV}$, giving a voltage excursion near $85\,\mathrm{mV}$. Using the specific [membrane capacitance](../../../../membrane-capacitance.md) and surface area,

$$
C=0.01\times10^{-9}=10^{-11}\,\mathrm F,\quad
Q=C\Delta V\simeq8.5\times10^{-13}\,\mathrm C,
\quad\boxed{N_{\rm Na,net}=\frac{Q}{e}\simeq5.3\times10^6\text{ ions}.}
$$

A coarser $100\,\mathrm{mV}$ spike estimate gives $6.2\times10^6$ [ions](../../../../ion.md), illustrating the precision appropriate to the graph. These are the net monovalent charges needed to charge the [capacitor](../../../../capacitor.md) if [sodium](../../../../sodium.md) supplies the inward charge; simultaneous opposing currents can make the actual [sodium](../../../../sodium.md) influx larger.

The cell contains $0.015\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}\times3\times10^{-13}\,\mathrm L\times N_A\simeq2.71\times10^9$ intracellular [sodium](../../../../sodium.md) [ions](../../../../ion.md). The capacitive estimate adds only about $0.20\%$, or $0.029\,\mathrm{mM}$. Thus a spike does not equilibrate bulk [concentrations](../../../../concentration.md). The [sodium-potassium ATPase](../../../../sodium-potassium-atpase.md) maintains the [gradients](../../../../gradient.md) over many spikes, exporting three [sodium](../../../../sodium.md) [ions](../../../../ion.md) per two [potassium](../../../../potassium.md) [ions](../../../../ion.md) imported per [ATP](../../../../adenosine-triphosphate.md); the estimated [sodium](../../../../sodium.md) load alone corresponds to at least about $1.8\times10^6$ pump cycles. Rapid [repolarization](../../../../repolarization.md) itself comes mainly from changing channel conductances, while sustained fast firing requires adequate energy supply and [gradient](../../../../gradient.md) restoration. The small stated volume and large area are used as supplied, without assuming a particular cell geometry.
