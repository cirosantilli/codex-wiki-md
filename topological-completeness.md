# Topological completeness

↑ **Parent:** [Complete metric space](complete-metric-space.md)

A metrizable space is topologically complete when some [metric](metric.md) inducing its topology is complete. The given [metric](metric.md) need not be complete: the open interval $(0,1)$ is an example. A [Polish space](polish-space.md) additionally requires separability.
