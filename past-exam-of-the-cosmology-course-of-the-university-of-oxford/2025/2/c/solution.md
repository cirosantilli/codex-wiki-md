<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Define the [momentum density](../../../../momentum-density.md) $q_i=(\bar\rho+\bar P)v_{\perp i}$, lowering its spatial label with $\delta_{ij}$, so the mixed [stress-energy tensor](../../../../stress-energy-tensor-split.md) obeys $T^i{}_0=q_i$ and $T^0{}_i=-q_i$. All products of $J_{ij}$ or its [derivatives](../../../../derivative.md) with $q_i$ are second order and must be dropped in [linear cosmological perturbation theory](../../../../linear-cosmological-perturbation-theory-split.md).

For $\nu=0$, the three terms in [stress-energy conservation](../../../../stress-energy-conservation.md) are

$$
\partial_\mu T^\mu{}_0=\bar\rho'+\partial_iq_i,\qquad
\Gamma^\mu_{\mu\sigma}T^\sigma{}_0=4\mathcal H\bar\rho,\qquad
\Gamma^\sigma_{\mu0}T^\mu{}_\sigma=\mathcal H\bar\rho-3\mathcal H\bar P.
$$

Here the trace $J'_{ii}$ vanishes, $\Gamma^i_{00}=\Gamma^0_{i0}=0$, and $\partial_iq_i=(\bar\rho+\bar P)\partial_iv_\perp^i=0$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\bar\rho'+3\mathcal H(\bar\rho+\bar P)=0.}
$$

For $\nu=i$, spatial [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md) gradients vanish because this is a purely [vector cosmological perturbation](../../../../vector-cosmological-perturbation.md). The first two terms are

$$
\partial_\mu T^\mu{}_i=-q_i',\qquad
\Gamma^\mu_{\mu\sigma}T^\sigma{}_i=-4\mathcal Hq_i.
$$

In the remaining contraction, the two background-connection times [momentum density](../../../../momentum-density.md) terms cancel:

$$
\Gamma^0_{ji}T^j{}_0+\Gamma^j_{0i}T^0{}_j=\mathcal Hq_i-\mathcal Hq_i=0.
$$

The other terms are $\Gamma^0_{0i}\bar\rho=0$ and $-\bar P\Gamma^j_{ji}=0$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{[(\bar\rho+\bar P)v_{\perp i}]'+4\mathcal H(\bar\rho+\bar P)v_{\perp i}=0.}
$$

Expanding this [conservation law](../../../../conservation-law.md) and using the background [cosmological continuity equation](../../../../cosmological-continuity-equation.md) gives the equivalent [peculiar velocity](../../../../peculiar-velocity.md) equation, when $\bar\rho+\bar P\ne0$,

$$
\boxed{v_{\perp i}'+\left(\mathcal H+\frac{\bar P'}{\bar\rho+\bar P}\right)v_{\perp i}=0.}
$$
