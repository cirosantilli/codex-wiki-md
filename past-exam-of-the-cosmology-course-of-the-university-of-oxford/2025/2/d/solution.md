<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The $\nu=0$ result is the background [cosmological perfect-fluid continuity equation](../../../../cosmological-perfect-fluid-continuity-equation.md). For a comoving region of physical [volume](../../../../volume.md) proportional to $a^3$, it is precisely

$$
d(\bar\rho a^3)=-\bar P\,d(a^3),
$$

expressing local [conservation of energy](../../../../conservation-of-energy.md) including the [pressure-volume work](../../../../pressure-volume-work.md) done during [cosmic expansion](../../../../expansion-of-the-universe.md). A [transverse vector field](../../../../transverse-vector-field.md) has no velocity [divergence](../../../../divergence.md), and the vector spatial [metric tensor](../../../../metric-tensor.md) has no trace perturbation, so neither produces a first-order compression or density change.

The spatial result is [vector-fluid momentum dilution](../../../../vector-fluid-momentum-dilution.md) and integrates immediately:

$$
(a^4q_i)'=0,\qquad
\boxed{q_i(\eta)=q_i(\eta_i)\left(\frac{a_i}{a}\right)^4.}
$$

Thus, for any specified background [perfect fluid](../../../../perfect-fluid.md),

$$
\boxed{v_{\perp i}(\eta)=v_{\perp i}(\eta_i)\left(\frac{a_i}{a}\right)^4\frac{\bar\rho_i+\bar P_i}{\bar\rho+\bar P}.}
$$

A statement about $\mathbf q$ alone does not fix the velocity scaling independently of the [equation of state](../../../../equation-of-state.md). For constant [equation-of-state parameter](../../../../equation-of-state-parameter.md) $w\ne-1$, the [cosmological continuity equation](../../../../cosmological-continuity-equation.md) gives $\bar\rho\propto a^{-3(1+w)}$ and hence

$$
\boxed{\mathbf v_\perp\propto a^{3w-1}.}
$$

For [pressureless matter](../../../../pressureless-matter.md), $w=0$ and $\mathbf v_\perp\propto a^{-1}$, exactly as in the Newtonian calculation in part (a). For [radiation in cosmology](../../../../radiation-in-cosmology.md), $w=1/3$ and the vector [peculiar velocity](../../../../peculiar-velocity.md) remains constant, although its [momentum density](../../../../momentum-density.md) still falls as $a^{-4}$. More generally, for a barotropic [perfect fluid](../../../../perfect-fluid.md) with [cosmological adiabatic sound speed](../../../../cosmological-adiabatic-sound-speed.md) $c_a^2=\bar P'/\bar\rho'$, the equivalent equation is $\mathbf v_\perp'+\mathcal H(1-3c_a^2)\mathbf v_\perp=0$. The Newtonian damping law describes nonrelativistic inertia; it need not apply to a relativistic [perfect fluid](../../../../perfect-fluid.md). A pure [cosmological constant](../../../../cosmological-constant.md) has $\bar\rho+\bar P=0$ and supplies no such fluid velocity degree of freedom.
