<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The four contributions have distinct physical origins. The intrinsic [photon temperature perturbation](../../../../photon-temperature-perturbation.md) is $\delta_\gamma/4$: a local [blackbody radiation](../../../../black-body-radiation.md) [energy density](../../../../energy-density.md) satisfies $\rho_\gamma\propto T^4$, so its first-order fractional perturbation is four times the fractional [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) perturbation. The endpoint [cosmological Newtonian potential](../../../../cosmological-newtonian-potential.md) term $\psi_*$ is the [gravitational redshift](../../../../gravitational-redshift.md) between the emitting plasma and the observer; escaping a negative potential well lowers the measured [photon](../../../../photon.md) [energy](../../../../energy-split.md). Together with the intrinsic contribution it produces the ordinary [Sachs-Wolfe effect](../../../../sachs-wolfe-effect.md). The term $-\hat{\mathbf n}\cdot\mathbf v_*$ is the [Doppler CMB anisotropy](../../../../doppler-cmb-anisotropy.md), caused by the plasma's line-of-sight [peculiar velocity](../../../../peculiar-velocity.md) at last scattering, with the source's direction convention.

Finally, $2\int d\chi\,\psi'$ is the [Integrated Sachs-Wolfe effect](../../../../integrated-sachs-wolfe-effect.md). A [photon](../../../../photon.md) passing through a static potential well gains [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) on entry and loses it on exit, with no net shift. When the potential evolves while the [photon](../../../../photon.md) crosses, the two shifts fail to cancel. The factor two is the sum of the temporal and spatial scalar-potential contributions when [anisotropic stress](../../../../anisotropic-stress.md) is negligible, so the two potentials are equal. The prime is a [partial derivative](../../../../partial-derivative.md) at fixed [comoving coordinate](../../../../comoving-coordinate.md); it is not the total [derivative](../../../../derivative.md) along the light ray.

For a low-[cosmological redshift](../../../../cosmological-redshift.md) galaxy sample, the intrinsic, endpoint-potential and last-scattering [Doppler CMB anisotropy](../../../../doppler-cmb-anisotropy.md) terms are localized near $z\simeq1100$, whereas the [galaxy density contrast](../../../../galaxy-density-contrast.md) is measured much nearer us. In the [Limber approximation](../../../../limber-approximation.md) their radial kernels have no overlap, so their leading cross correlations vanish. The late [Integrated Sachs-Wolfe effect](../../../../integrated-sachs-wolfe-effect.md) samples the same nearby evolving potentials as the [galaxies](../../../../galaxy-split.md), giving the desired [late-time CMB-matter cross correlation](../../../../late-time-cmb-matter-cross-correlation.md). The early [Integrated Sachs-Wolfe effect](../../../../integrated-sachs-wolfe-effect.md) near [matter-radiation equality](../../../../matter-radiation-equality.md) is likewise radially separated.

This is the leading linear, separated-kernel description intended here. The primary [Cosmic microwave background anisotropy](../../../../cosmic-microwave-background-anisotropy-split.md) and late [density contrast](../../../../density-contrast.md) share primordial initial conditions, so their exact large-angle correlation need not be identically zero outside the [Limber approximation](../../../../limber-approximation.md). Additional secondary effects, such as the [Sunyaev-Zeldovich effect](../../../../sunyaev-zeldovich-effect.md), can also correlate with [galaxies](../../../../galaxy-split.md) in real maps; they are absent from the stipulated linear expression.
