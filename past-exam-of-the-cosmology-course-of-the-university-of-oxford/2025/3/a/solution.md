<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [cosmic microwave background](../../../../cosmic-microwave-background-split.md) consists of relic [photons](../../../../photon.md) from the early hot, nearly thermal [photon-baryon fluid](../../../../photon-baryon-fluid.md). Before [cosmological recombination](../../../../recombination-cosmology.md), abundant free [electrons](../../../../electron.md) repeatedly scatter [photons](../../../../photon.md) by [Thomson scattering](../../../../thomson-scattering.md). Their [mean free path](../../../../mean-free-path.md) $\lambda_\gamma=(n_e\sigma_T)^{-1}$ is short and their [Thomson scattering](../../../../thomson-scattering.md) rate $n_e\sigma_Tc$ is much greater than the [Hubble parameter](../../../../hubble-parameter.md). An earlier [photon](../../../../photon.md) therefore cannot stream directly to us: subsequent scattering repeatedly changes its direction.

As [cosmic expansion](../../../../expansion-of-the-universe.md) cools the plasma to about $T\simeq3000\,\mathrm K$, [electrons](../../../../electron.md) and [protons](../../../../proton.md) combine into neutral [hydrogen](../../../../hydrogen.md). The free-electron abundance drops rapidly, the scattering [optical depth](../../../../optical-depth.md) to the observer becomes of order one, and [photon decoupling](../../../../photon-decoupling.md) occurs. Since the [cosmic microwave background](../../../../cosmic-microwave-background-split.md) [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) scales as $T=T_0(1+z)$, with $T_0\simeq2.725\,\mathrm K$, this occurs at $z\simeq1100$. The small [baryon-to-photon ratio](../../../../baryon-to-photon-ratio.md) delays neutralization to well below $13.6\,\mathrm{eV}/k_B$: the high-energy tail contains enough [photons](../../../../photon.md) to ionize [hydrogen](../../../../hydrogen.md) until the [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) is much lower than the [Hydrogen binding energy](../../../../hydrogen-binding-energy.md) expressed as a [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md). The observed [cosmic microwave background](../../../../cosmic-microwave-background-split.md) is primarily this pre-existing radiation undergoing its last scattering; its nearly [blackbody spectrum](../../../../blackbody-spectrum.md) is then preserved by [cosmological redshift](../../../../cosmological-redshift.md).

For the requested estimate take a flat [matter-dominated universe](../../../../matter-domination.md), $a(t)=(t/t_0)^{2/3}$. Restore $c$ in distances. The [comoving particle horizon](../../../../comoving-particle-horizon.md) at [cosmological recombination](../../../../recombination-cosmology.md) and the [comoving radial distance](../../../../comoving-radial-distance.md) to the [Cosmic microwave background last-scattering surface](../../../../cosmic-microwave-background-last-scattering-surface.md) are

$$
r_{\mathrm{hor},*}=c\int_0^{t_*}\frac{dt}{a(t)}=3ct_0\sqrt{a_*},\qquad
\chi_*=c\int_{t_*}^{t_0}\frac{dt}{a(t)}=3ct_0(1-\sqrt{a_*}).
$$

The proper [particle horizon](../../../../particle-horizon.md) is $a_*r_{\mathrm{hor},*}=3ct_*$ and the [angular diameter distance](../../../../angular-diameter-distance.md) is $D_A=a_*\chi_*$. Therefore the [angular particle-horizon size in a power-law universe](../../../../angular-particle-horizon-size-in-a-power-law-universe.md), taking one horizon radius as the transverse length, is

$$
\boxed{\theta_{\mathrm{hor}}\simeq\frac{r_{\mathrm{hor},*}}{\chi_*}=\frac1{\sqrt{1+z_*}-1}\simeq0.0311\,\mathrm{rad}\simeq1.78^\circ.}
$$

The angular diameter of a patch of that radius is about $3.56^\circ$. The factor of two is a radius-versus-diameter convention; either gives a horizon scale of only a few degrees. This is a [particle horizon](../../../../particle-horizon.md), rather than the smaller [sound horizon](../../../../sound-horizon.md). Including early [radiation in cosmology](../../../../radiation-in-cosmology.md) changes the numerical estimate but preserves its order of magnitude.

The [horizon problem](../../../../horizon-problem.md) is that the [cosmic microwave background](../../../../cosmic-microwave-background-split.md) has almost the same [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) across the whole sky, including pairs of regions whose past light cones do not overlap in this noninflating model. Local thermal equilibration cannot explain that observation within this history. A sufficiently long period of [cosmic inflation](../../../../cosmic-inflation-split.md) makes the [comoving Hubble radius](../../../../comoving-hubble-radius.md) decrease: a region once small enough to share a causal past can expand to encompass our entire observed [Cosmic microwave background last-scattering surface](../../../../cosmic-microwave-background-last-scattering-surface.md). After [reheating](../../../../reheating.md), it can be homogeneous across many later [particle horizon](../../../../particle-horizon.md) patches. The matter-only estimate integrates from a decelerating [Big Bang](../../../../big-bang.md); extending the earlier history by [cosmic inflation](../../../../cosmic-inflation-split.md) changes that causal conclusion.
