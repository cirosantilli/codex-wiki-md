<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [cosmological principle](../../../../cosmological-principle.md) permits a [Friedmann-Lemaître-Robertson-Walker metric](../../../../friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md). The [Friedmann equation](../../../../friedmann-equations.md), with vanishing [cosmological constant](../../../../cosmological-constant.md), is

$$
H^2=\frac{8\pi G}{3}\bar\rho_m-\frac{k}{a^2}.
$$

For separately conserved [pressureless matter](../../../../pressureless-matter.md), the [cosmological continuity equation](../../../../cosmological-continuity-equation.md) gives $\bar\rho_m=\bar\rho_{m0}a^{-3}$: its rest [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) is conserved while its physical [volume](../../../../volume.md) grows as $a^3$. Define the present [critical density](../../../../critical-density.md) and [matter density parameter](../../../../matter-density-parameter.md) by

$$
\rho_{\mathrm{crit},0}=\frac{3H_0^2}{8\pi G},\qquad \Omega=\frac{\bar\rho_{m0}}{\rho_{\mathrm{crit},0}}.
$$

Evaluating the [Friedmann equation](../../../../friedmann-equations.md) today gives $-k=H_0^2(1-\Omega)>0$. Thus the model has negative [spatial curvature of an FLRW universe](../../../../spatial-curvature-of-an-flrw-universe.md); the second contribution to $H^2$ represents that [spatial curvature of an FLRW universe](../../../../spatial-curvature-of-an-flrw-universe.md), rather than another material fluid. Taking the expanding branch gives

$$
\boxed{H(a)=H_0\sqrt{\Omega a^{-3}+(1-\Omega)a^{-2}}.}
$$

Here $0<\Omega<1$. At early times, $a\ll\Omega/(1-\Omega)$, [matter domination](../../../../matter-domination.md) gives $\dot a\simeq H_0\sqrt\Omega\,a^{-1/2}$, hence, measuring [cosmic time](../../../../cosmic-time.md) from the [Big Bang](../../../../big-bang.md),

$$
\boxed{a(t)\simeq\left(\frac32H_0\sqrt\Omega\,t\right)^{2/3}.}
$$

At late times the $a^{-2}$ term dominates the [Friedmann equation](../../../../friedmann-equations.md). This [curvature-dominated universe](../../../../curvature-dominated-universe.md) has $\dot a\to H_0\sqrt{1-\Omega}$ and therefore

$$
\boxed{a(t)\sim H_0\sqrt{1-\Omega}\,t.}
$$

The late expansion approaches the coasting [Milne model](../../../../milne-model.md). The early limit belongs to this idealized matter-only model; including [radiation in cosmology](../../../../radiation-in-cosmology.md) would alter the sufficiently early evolution.
