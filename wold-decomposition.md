# Wold decomposition

↑ **Parent:** [Time series](time-series-split.md)

A second-order stationary [time series](time-series-split.md) decomposes into a deterministic component and a one-sided square-summable [linear filter of a stationary time series](linear-filter-of-a-stationary-time-series.md) of its mutually orthogonal [linear innovations](linear-innovation-process.md). Normalize $c_0=1$. For a causal invertible [ARMA](autoregressive-moving-average-model.md) process with no deterministic component, the driving [white noise](white-noise.md) is this innovation process: observations and innovations span the same closed past subspace, while the next innovation is orthogonal to it.
