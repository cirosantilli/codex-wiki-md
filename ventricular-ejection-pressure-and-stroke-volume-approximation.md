# Ventricular ejection pressure and stroke-volume approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Ventricular elastance](ventricular-elastance.md)

After a rapid switch to systolic compliance $C_s$, negligible valve resistance equalizes arterial and ventricular pressures. Neglecting runoff during this short stage conserves combined stored volume and gives the displayed [pressure](pressure-split.md). Thereafter, with closed [mitral valve](mitral-valve.md), negligible venous [pressure](pressure-split.md) and constant $C_s$, the [pressure](pressure-split.md) decays as $\widehat p_a e^{-t/[R_c(C_a+C_s)]}$. At firing duration $\Delta t_F$, the [stroke volume](stroke-volume.md) is $V^+-V_0-C_s\widehat p_a e^{-\Delta t_F/[R_c(C_a+C_s)]}$. Each approximation follows from an explicit valve or time-scale separation condition.
