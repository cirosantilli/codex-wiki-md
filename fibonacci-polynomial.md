# Fibonacci polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial](polynomial-split.md)

Fibonacci polynomials are defined by $F_0(x)=0$, $F_1(x)=1$, and $F_{n+1}(x)=xF_n(x)+F_{n-1}(x)$. Setting $x=1$ recovers the [Fibonacci numbers](fibonacci-number.md).
