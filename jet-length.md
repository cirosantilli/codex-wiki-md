# Jet length

↑ **Parent:** [Forced plume](forced-plume.md)

For source kinematic [momentum flux](momentum-flux.md) $m_0$ and [buoyancy flux](buoyancy-flux.md) $f_0>0$, [dimensional analysis](dimensional-analysis.md) gives $L_J=m_0^{3/4}/f_0^{1/2}$. Under constant [Batchelor entrainment](batchelor-entrainment-hypothesis.md), the actual turning length also contains an [entrainment coefficient](entrainment-coefficient.md) factor of order $\alpha^{-1/2}$.
