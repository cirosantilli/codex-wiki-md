# Pants decomposition

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic surface](hyperbolic-surface.md)

A collection of disjoint essential [simple closed curves](simple-closed-curve.md) that cuts a closed [genus](genus-of-a-surface.md)-$g$ surface into $2g-2$ surfaces of type [pair of pants](pair-of-pants-mathematics.md). There are $3g-3$ curves. [Geodesic](geodesic.md) representatives are used for [Fenchel–Nielsen coordinates](fenchel-nielsen-coordinates.md).
