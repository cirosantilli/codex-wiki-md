# Cabibbo angle

↑ **Parent:** [Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa matrix](cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md)

The [Cabibbo angle](cabibbo-angle.md) $\theta_C$ describes the real two-generation [quark mixing](quark-mixing.md) rotation

$$
V=\begin{pmatrix}\cos\theta_C&\sin\theta_C\\-\sin\theta_C&\cos\theta_C\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Thus $V_{ud}=V_{cs}=\cos\theta_C$, $V_{us}=\sin\theta_C$ and $V_{cd}=-\sin\theta_C$. [Nicola Cabibbo](nicola-cabibbo.md) formulated the mixing of [weak charged current](charged-current.md) couplings before the three-generation mechanism of [CP violation](cp-violation.md); this foundational contribution is distinct from the later extension.[description, original weak-current formulation and later three-family extension](nicola-cabibbo-1935-2010-by-cern.md)
