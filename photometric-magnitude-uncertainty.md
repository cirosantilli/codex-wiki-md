# Photometric magnitude uncertainty

↑ **Parent:** [Apparent magnitude](apparent-magnitude.md)

For a fixed [magnitude zero point](magnitude-zero-point.md), the derivative of $m=-2.5\log_{10}(f/f_0)$ gives the small-error [propagation of uncertainty](propagation-of-uncertainty.md) formula $\sigma_m=(2.5/\ln10)\sigma_f/f$. A fractional [observed radiative flux](observed-radiative-flux.md) uncertainty of $0.1$ gives about $0.109$ mag of absolute uncertainty. A fractional magnitude uncertainty additionally needs the value of $m$, is undefined at $m=0$, and depends on the adopted [magnitude zero point](magnitude-zero-point.md).
