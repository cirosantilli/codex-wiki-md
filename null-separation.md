# Null separation

↑ **Parent:** [Minkowski interval](minkowski-interval.md)

Distinct [spacetime events](event-relativity.md) have [null separation](null-separation.md) when their displacement has zero [Minkowski interval](minkowski-interval.md), equivalently $c|\Delta t|=|\Delta\mathbf x|$. They can be joined by a straight vacuum light ray. Their nonzero displacement is a [null vector](null-vector.md), so the zero squared interval does not mean that the [spacetime events](event-relativity.md) coincide. For example, $(t,x)=(3\,\mathrm s,3\,\mathrm{ls})$ is null separated from the origin.
