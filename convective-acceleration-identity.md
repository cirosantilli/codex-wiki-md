# Convective acceleration identity

↑ **Parent:** [Vorticity cross-product identity](vorticity-cross-product-identity.md)

The local vector-calculus identity

$$
(\mathbf u\cdot\nabla)\mathbf u=\nabla(\tfrac12|\mathbf u|^2)-\mathbf u\times(\nabla\times\mathbf u)
$$

separates the convective [acceleration](acceleration.md) into a kinetic-energy gradient and a [vorticity](vorticity.md) cross-product contribution. Substitution into the steady [Euler equations for an inviscid fluid](euler-equations-for-an-inviscid-fluid.md) yields the vorticity form of the [Bernoulli equation](bernoulli-equation.md).
