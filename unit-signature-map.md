# Unit signature map

↑ **Parent:** [Unit group](unit-group.md)

The unit signature map records the signs of a unit under all real [field embeddings](field-embedding.md). Its [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) consists of [totally positive](totally-positive-element-of-a-number-field.md) units. Its image determines the [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) of the homomorphism from the [narrow ideal class group](narrow-ideal-class-group.md) to the ordinary [ideal class group](ideal-class-group.md): this kernel is $\{\pm1\}^{r_1}/\operatorname{sgn}(\mathcal O_K^\times)$.
