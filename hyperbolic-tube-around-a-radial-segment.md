# Hyperbolic tube around a radial segment

↑ **Parent:** [Poincare metric on a Riemann surface](poincare-metric-on-a-riemann-surface.md)

Under the [Cayley transform between the half-plane and disk](cayley-transform-between-the-half-plane-and-disk.md), a radial segment ending at a boundary point becomes a vertical half-ray. If a point is within [hyperbolic distance](hyperbolic-distance.md) $k$ of that half-ray, it lies in a fixed angular sector. Indeed, in the [complex upper half-plane](upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md), $\cosh\rho(w,iy)=(|w|^2+y^2)/(2y\operatorname{Im}w)\geq|w|/\operatorname{Im}w$. This converts bounded hyperbolic approach to a boundary point into nontangential approach, allowing [normal-family proof of angular boundary convergence](normal-family-proof-of-angular-boundary-convergence.md) to transfer radial limits of bounded [holomorphic functions](holomorphic-function.md).
