# Tidal disruption

↑ **Parent:** [Tidal tensor](tidal-tensor.md)

Disruption of a self-gravitating body when differential gravitational [acceleration](acceleration.md) across it exceeds its binding [acceleration](acceleration.md). For a body of radius $R$, [mass](mass.md) $m$ at distance $r$ from a much more massive body $M$, the competing scales are $GMR/r^3$ and $Gm/R^2$; their equality gives $r\sim R(M/m)^{1/3}$, up to geometry and internal-structure factors. The [tidal tensor](tidal-tensor.md) gives the directional differential [acceleration](acceleration.md). A [tidal disruption event](tidal-disruption-event.md) near a black hole is a specific astronomical application.
