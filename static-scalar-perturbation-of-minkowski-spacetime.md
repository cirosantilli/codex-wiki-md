# Static scalar perturbation of Minkowski spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [Linearized gravity](linearized-gravity.md)

For static scalar perturbations with no mixed time-space or spatial shear terms, the displayed [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md) uses signature $(+---)$. With the reversed convention in [curvature-sign convention in Killing derivative identities](curvature-sign-convention-in-killing-derivative-identities.md), its first-order [Einstein tensor](einstein-tensor.md) is $\delta G_{00}=2\Delta C$, $\delta G_{0i}=0$, and $\delta G_{ij}=\partial_i\partial_j(A+C)-\delta_{ij}\Delta(A+C)$. These follow by substituting the metric into the first-order [Christoffel symbols](christoffel-symbol.md) and [Ricci tensor](ricci-tensor.md), discarding products of perturbations. A static source with the [dust stress-energy tensor](dust-stress-energy-tensor.md) obeys $\Delta C=-4\pi G\rho$ and the decaying spatial equation makes $A=-C$. For a point mass the [Newtonian potential of a point mass](newtonian-potential-of-a-point-mass.md) gives $A=-GM/r$, $C=GM/r$. The spatial potential is necessary to determine light propagation; a time potential alone is insufficient.
