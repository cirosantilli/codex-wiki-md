# Polyatomic vibration-rotation interaction

↑ **Parent:** [Vibration-rotation interaction](vibration-rotation-interaction.md)

The first-order dependence of a polyatomic [rotational constant](rotational-constant.md) on vibrational occupations $v_i$, where $d_i$ is the degeneracy of [normal mode](normal-mode.md) $i$. The factor $d_i/2$ accounts for all of that mode's [zero-point energy](zero-point-energy.md). A single band's change in [rotational constant](rotational-constant.md) generally determines one $\alpha_i$, rather than every zero-point correction needed for $B_e$.
