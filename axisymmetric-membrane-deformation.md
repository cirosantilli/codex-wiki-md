# Axisymmetric membrane deformation

↑ **Parent:** [Lipid vesicle](lipid-vesicle.md)

An axisymmetric cylindrical membrane deformation is invariant under rotations about the cylinder axis, so its radius depends only on the axial coordinate. Its [surface area](surface-area.md) is $2\pi\int R\sqrt{1+R_z^2}\,dz$, and its enclosed volume is $\pi\int R^2\,dz$. These scalar shape constraints are the basis of the [fixed-volume capillary spectrum of a cylindrical membrane](fixed-volume-capillary-spectrum-of-a-cylindrical-membrane.md).
