# Weak gravitational field

↑ **Parent:** [General relativity](general-relativity-split.md)

A [weak gravitational field](weak-gravitational-field.md) is a regime in which the [metric](metric.md) differs little from a suitable flat reference [metric](metric.md). In a slow-motion, approximately stationary situation, $g_{tt}\simeq1+2\phi/c^2$ for signature $(+---)$, with Newtonian potential $\phi$. Proper-[clock](clock.md) rates then differ fractionally by $\Delta\phi/c^2$. Small [metric](metric.md) perturbations do not by themselves imply every [velocity](velocity.md) is nonrelativistic; slow motion is a separate approximation when deriving Newtonian dynamics.
