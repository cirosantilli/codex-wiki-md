# Pumped-storage hydroelectricity

↑ **Parent:** [Energy storage](energy-storage.md)

Water is pumped to an upper reservoir and later discharged through a turbine. For approximately constant head, useful discharge [energy](energy-split.md) is $\eta\rho Vgh$ while [power](power.md) is $\eta\rho Qgh$; reservoir [volume](volume.md) and flow rating set different properties.
