# Primitive cohomology

↑ **Parent:** [Hodge decomposition theorem for compact Kähler manifolds](hodge-decomposition-theorem-for-compact-kahler-manifolds.md)

For a compact [Kähler manifold](kahler-manifold.md) of complex dimension $d$ and $k\le d$, this is the cohomology killed by the indicated power of a [Kähler class](kahler-class.md). The Hodge–Riemann bilinear relations polarize its [Hodge structure](hodge-structure.md). On a [K3 surface](k3-surface.md), primitive degree-two cohomology is the orthogonal complement of the chosen positive class.
