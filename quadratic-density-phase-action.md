# Quadratic density-phase action

↑ **Parent:** [Density-phase action of a Bose gas](density-phase-action-of-a-bose-gas.md)

Expand the [density-phase action of a Bose gas](density-phase-action-of-a-bose-gas.md) around $\rho_0=\mu/g>0$ and a constant phase. Density and phase are coupled by $i\delta\rho\partial_\tau\phi$, and retaining density [gradients](gradient.md) gives the full quadratic [Bogoliubov spectrum](bogoliubov-quasiparticle-dispersion.md). Dropping those [gradients](gradient.md) is a long-wavelength approximation. A small-fluctuation Gaussian extension around $\rho_0$ does not exactly remove the original nonnegative-density constraint.
