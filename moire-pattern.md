<h1 id="moire-pattern">Moiré pattern</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Interference (wave propagation)](interference-wave-propagation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moiré_pattern)

A [Moiré pattern](moire-pattern.md) is a large-scale modulation produced by superposing similar periodic structures. Two identical two-dimensional lattices rotated by a small angle $\theta$ have geometric period $L=a/[2\sin(\theta/2)]\simeq a/|\theta|$ in the usual matching geometry. In layered crystals this modulation can act as an electronic [superlattice](superlattice.md); the actual bands additionally depend on interlayer coupling and orbital structure.
