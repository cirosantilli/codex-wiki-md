# Mean curvature of a catenoid

↑ **Parent:** [Mean curvature](mean-curvature.md)

Scale the [catenoid](catenoid.md) by its waist [radius](radius.md) $a>0$ and choose a [unit normal](unit-normal.md) with outward radial component:

$$
X(u,v)=(a\cosh u\cos v,a\cosh u\sin v,au),\qquad N(u,v)=\frac{(\cos v,\sin v,-\sinh u)}{\cosh u}.
$$

The [hyperbolic cosine](hyperbolic-cosine.md) controls the profile $r(z)=a\cosh(z/a)$. Direct [differentiation](differentiation.md) yields the [first fundamental form](first-fundamental-form.md) and [second fundamental form](second-fundamental-form-split.md)

$$
I=a^2\cosh^2u\,(du^2+dv^2),\qquad II=a\,du^2-a\,dv^2.
$$

The coordinate [tangent vectors](tangent-vector.md) are orthogonal, so the meridional and circumferential [principal curvatures](principal-curvature.md) are

$$
k_{\mathrm{mer}}=\frac1{a\cosh^2u},\qquad k_{\mathrm{circ}}=-\frac1{a\cosh^2u},\qquad H=0,\qquad K=-\frac1{a^2\cosh^4u}.
$$

The opposite [principal curvatures](principal-curvature.md) cancel at every point, not just at the waist. This is precisely why the [catenoid is a minimal surface](catenoid-is-a-minimal-surface.md), while its nonzero [second fundamental form](second-fundamental-form-split.md) and negative [Gaussian curvature](gaussian-curvature.md) show that it is not a [plane](plane.md).
