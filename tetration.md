# Tetration

↑ **Parent:** [Exponentiation](exponentiation.md)

Repeated [exponentiation](exponentiation.md) with right association: ${}^{1}a=a$ and ${}^{h+1}a=a^{{}^{h}a}$. For example, ${}^{3}a=a^{a^a}$, rather than $(a^a)^a$. Computing its [remainder](remainder.md) [modulo](modulo-operation.md) an [integer](integer-split.md) often uses a chain of smaller exponent moduli; applying [Euler's theorem](euler-s-theorem.md) requires checking its [coprimality](coprime-integers.md) hypothesis at every level.
