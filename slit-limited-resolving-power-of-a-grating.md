# Slit-limited resolving power of a grating

↑ **Parent:** [Anamorphic magnification of a grating](anamorphic-magnification-of-a-grating.md)

A slit of width $x$ at the focus of a [collimator](collimator.md) subtends $x/f_{\rm coll}$. Its projected width is $x f_{\rm cam}\cos\alpha/(f_{\rm coll}\cos\beta)$. Dividing this width by the [grating dispersion](grating-dispersion.md) gives $\Delta\lambda=xd\cos\alpha/(mf_{\rm coll})$. Thus the [spectral resolving power](spectral-resolving-power.md) is $R=m\lambda f_{\rm coll}/(xd\cos\alpha)$. Omitting the anamorphic factor gives a denominator $\cos\beta$ instead; these agree in the [Littrow configuration](littrow-configuration.md).
