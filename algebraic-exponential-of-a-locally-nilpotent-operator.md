# Algebraic exponential of a locally nilpotent operator

↑ **Parent:** [Locally nilpotent operator](locally-nilpotent-operator.md)

Over a field of characteristic zero, a [locally nilpotent operator](locally-nilpotent-operator.md) has an algebraically defined exponential, because the sum is finite for each vector. Its inverse is $\exp(-T)$. The nilpotence bound may depend on the vector. In an [sl2 Lie algebra](sl2-lie-algebra.md) [Verma module](verma-module.md), the raising operator is locally nilpotent but the lowering operator is not, so the latter's exponential does not define an endomorphism of the ordinary algebraic module.
