# Ramification divisor

↑ **Parent:** [Ramification (mathematics)](ramification-mathematics.md)

For a finite morphism $f:X\to Y$ of smooth curves, the ramification divisor is

$$
R_f=\sum_{P\in X}(e_P-1)P.
$$

The [Riemann-Hurwitz formula](riemann-hurwitz-formula.md) states $2g_X-2=(\deg f)(2g_Y-2)+\deg R_f$.

The coefficients assemble the [ramification indices of a morphism of curves](ramification-index-of-a-morphism-of-curves.md).
