# Reduced cohomology

↑ **Parent:** [Cohomology](cohomology-split.md)

For a based space, reduced cohomology removes the coefficient-ring summand coming from the basepoint. In nonnegative degrees it is the kernel of restriction $H^*(X;R)\to H^*(\{x_0\};R)$. It agrees with ordinary [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) in positive degrees and admits the [suspension isomorphism](suspension-isomorphism.md) and exact sequences for based cofibrations.
