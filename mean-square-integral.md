# Mean-square integral

↑ **Parent:** [Bochner integral](bochner-integral.md)

An ordinary time integral of [random variables](random-variable-split.md) taken in the [L2 space](l2-space-is-a-hilbert-space.md) of the underlying [probability space](probability-space.md). Under strong measurability and $\int_a^b(\mathbb E|X_t|^2)^{1/2}dt<\infty$, it exists as a [Bochner integral](bochner-integral.md); for a mean-square continuous process it is also the limit of [Riemann sums](riemann-sum.md) in that space. If the process is centered and the covariance is integrable, [Fubini's theorem](fubini-s-theorem.md) gives

$$
\operatorname{Var}\left(\int_a^bX_tdt\right)=\int_a^b\int_a^b\operatorname{Cov}(X_u,X_v)\,du\,dv.
$$

For a [Gaussian process](gaussian-process.md), the integral is a [Gaussian random variable](gaussian-random-variable.md), by approximating it in mean square by finite linear combinations of jointly [Gaussian random variables](gaussian-random-variable.md).
