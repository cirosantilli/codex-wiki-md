# Long-wavelength tensor CMB quadrupole and octupole

↑ **Parent:** [Helicity selection for tensor CMB multipoles](helicity-selection-for-tensor-cmb-multipoles.md)

For a matter-era superhorizon tensor with $h=h_{\mathrm{prim}}[1-(k\eta)^2/10+\cdots]$, the [tensor CMB line-of-sight source](tensor-cmb-line-of-sight-source.md) gives $\Theta_{22}\propto h_{\mathrm{prim}}(k\eta_0)^2$ and $\Theta_{32}/\Theta_{22}=-ik\eta_0/(3\sqrt7)$ when $\eta_*\ll\eta_0$. The extra spatial phase makes the octupole one order smaller.
