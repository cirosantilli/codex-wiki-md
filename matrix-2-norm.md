# Matrix 2-norm

↑ **Parent:** [Operator norm](operator-norm.md)

The matrix 2-norm is the [operator norm](operator-norm.md) induced by the [Euclidean norm](euclidean-norm.md):

$$
\|A\|_2=\sup_{x\ne0}\frac{\|Ax\|_2}{\|x\|_2}.
$$

It equals the largest [singular value](singular-value.md) of $A$ and is invariant under multiplication by [orthogonal](orthogonal-matrix.md) or [unitary](unitary-operator.md) matrices.

The [matrix norm](matrix-norm.md) induced by Euclidean vector norms is $\|A\|_2=\sup_{x\ne0}\|Ax\|_2/\|x\|_2$, equal to the largest singular value.
