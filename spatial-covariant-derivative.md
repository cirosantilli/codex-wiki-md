# Spatial covariant derivative

↑ **Parent:** [Spatial projection tensor](spatial-projection-tensor.md)

The spatial covariant derivative projects the spacetime [covariant derivative](covariant-derivative.md) and every free tensor index onto a spatial hypersurface. It is the [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md) of the induced spatial metric.
