# Minimum deviation of a prism

↑ **Parent:** [Prism (geometry)](prism-geometry.md)

For a homogeneous [optical prism](prism-optics.md) in the same medium on both sides, the minimum transmitted-ray deviation occurs for a symmetric path: the two internal refraction angles equal half its apex angle $A$. The expression requires $n\sin(A/2)\leq1$; equality is a grazing limiting path.
