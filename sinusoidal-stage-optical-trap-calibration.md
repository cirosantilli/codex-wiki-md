# Sinusoidal-stage optical trap calibration

↑ **Parent:** [Optical trap Langevin calibration](optical-trap-langevin-calibration.md)

For an overdamped [harmonic optical trap](harmonic-optical-trap.md), impose a known sinusoidal local fluid [velocity](velocity.md) of amplitude $u_0$ and [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) $\Omega$. The coherent bead response has amplitude $A_x=u_0/\sqrt{(k/\zeta)^2+\Omega^2}$. Measuring the thermal corner [frequency](frequency.md) $f_c=k/(2\pi\zeta)$ and detector-voltage amplitude $A_s=A_x/\beta$ therefore determines the position sensitivity in the displayed formula without a drag coefficient as input. Use a thermal record with the drive line removed to obtain stiffness from the [equipartition theorem](equipartition-theorem.md). Fluid-following, detector response and hydrodynamic corrections must remain valid. Combined thermal and stage measurements have experimentally calibrated an optical trap without drag as an input.[2006 preprint abstract: calibration without drag as an input](calibration-of-optical-tweezers-with-positional-detection-in-the-back-focal-plane-by-simon-f-tolic-norrelykke-and-others.md)
