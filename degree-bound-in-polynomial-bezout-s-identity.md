<h1 id="degree-bound-in-polynomial-bezout-s-identity">Degree bound in polynomial Bézout's identity</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Bézout's identity for polynomials](bezout-s-identity-for-polynomials.md)

For two [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) over the [complex numbers](complex-number.md) $p,q$ of equal positive [degree of a polynomial](degree-of-a-polynomial.md) $n$ with no common [root of a polynomial](root-of-a-polynomial.md), there are unique [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) $u,v$ of [degree of a polynomial](degree-of-a-polynomial.md) less than $n$ such that $up+vq=1$. To prove the bound, let $V_m$ be the [vector space](vector-space-split.md) of [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) of [degree of a polynomial](degree-of-a-polynomial.md) at most $m$, including the [zero polynomial](zero-polynomial.md). The [linear map](linear-map.md) $(a,b)\mapsto ap+bq$ from $V_{n-1}\times V_{n-1}$ to $V_{2n-1}$ has zero [kernel of a linear map](kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md): if $ap=-bq$, each [root of a polynomial](root-of-a-polynomial.md) of $p$, counted with its [multiplicity of a root](multiplicity-of-a-root.md), must occur in $b$ because it does not occur in $q$, forcing $b=0$ by the [degree of a polynomial](degree-of-a-polynomial.md) bound, and then $a=0$. Both [vector spaces](vector-space-split.md) have [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) $2n$, so the [rank-nullity theorem](rank-nullity-theorem.md) makes the [linear map](linear-map.md) a [bijection](bijection.md), and $1$ has a unique preimage.
