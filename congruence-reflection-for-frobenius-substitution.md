# Congruence reflection for Frobenius substitution

↑ **Parent:** [Frobenius substitution on cyclotomic power series](frobenius-substitution-on-cyclotomic-power-series.md)

For $R=\mathbb Z_p[[T]]$ and $\phi(f)=f((1+T)^p-1)$, reduction modulo $p$ is the injective substitution $\overline f(T)\mapsto\overline f(T^p)$. Thus $\phi(f)\in pR$ implies $f\in pR$. Dividing by $p$ and repeating proves the displayed equality for every $k\geq1$. In particular, a [ring congruence](congruence-modulo-an-ideal.md) of [Frobenius substitutions on cyclotomic power series](frobenius-substitution-on-cyclotomic-power-series.md) reflects back to the original [formal power series](formal-power-series.md).
