# Double-longitudinal Ward identity for gluon fusion

↑ **Parent:** [Slavnov-Taylor identity](slavnov-taylor-identity.md)

For massless $gg\to q\bar q$, define $i\mathcal M$ by the Feynman-rule product with [quark](quark.md) vertex $igT^a\gamma^\mu$. The ordered [quark](quark.md) exchanges contribute $-g^2\bar u\not k_1[T^a,T^b]v$ after double contraction. Three-gluon exchange contributes $ig^2f^{abc}\bar u\not k_1T^cv$, so the sum vanishes by the [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md). This does not assert off-shell transversality of each open index; the full [Slavnov-Taylor identity](slavnov-taylor-identity.md) includes [Faddeev-Popov ghost](faddeev-popov-ghost.md) terms.
