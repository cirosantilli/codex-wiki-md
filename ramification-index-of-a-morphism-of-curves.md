# Ramification index of a morphism of curves

↑ **Parent:** [Morphism of algebraic varieties](morphism-of-algebraic-varieties.md)

For a nonconstant morphism between nonsingular curves and a local target [uniformizer](uniformizer.md) $t$ at the image of $P$, the index is the [order of vanishing](order-of-vanishing.md) of its pullback. Changing $t$ multiplies it by a local [unit in a ring](unit-in-a-ring.md) and does not change that order. This is a [valuation](valuation.md) definition and remains meaningful for inseparable morphisms.
