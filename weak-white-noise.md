# Weak white noise

↑ **Parent:** [White noise](white-noise.md)

A [white noise](white-noise.md) sequence has zero mean, a common finite variance and zero [autocovariance](autocovariance.md) at every nonzero lag. This requires [uncorrelated random variables](uncorrelated-random-variables.md), rather than [independent random variables](independent-random-variables.md).
