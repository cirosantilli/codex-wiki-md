# Corotational Jeffreys fluid

↑ **Parent:** [Viscoelasticity](viscoelasticity.md)

A [corotational Jeffreys fluid](corotational-jeffreys-fluid.md) obeys $\overset\circ S+S/\tau=2\mu_0\overset\circ D+2\mu_1D/\tau$, with [deviatoric stress](deviatoric-stress.md) $S$, [rate-of-strain tensor](strain-rate-tensor.md) $D$, [Jaumann derivative](jaumann-derivative.md) and relaxation time $\tau>0$. Put $A=S-2\mu_0D$. In the [corotating-frame representation of a Jaumann derivative](corotating-frame-representation-of-a-jaumann-derivative.md), $\widetilde A'+\widetilde A/\tau=2(\mu_1-\mu_0)\widetilde D/\tau$. An [integrating factor](integrating-factor.md) gives an exponentially weighted history transported between the rotating frames. The infinite-past formula selects histories with $e^{t/\tau}\widetilde A(t)\to0$ as $t\to-\infty$; finite initial data also contribute an exponentially decaying homogeneous term.
