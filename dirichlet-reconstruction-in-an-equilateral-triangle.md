# Dirichlet reconstruction in an equilateral triangle

↑ **Parent:** [Polygonal modified Helmholtz global relation](polygonal-modified-helmholtz-global-relation.md)

For equal oriented [Dirichlet boundary data](dirichlet-boundary-data.md) on all three sides and a uniquely solvable elliptic problem, rotation invariance makes their outward [normal derivatives](normal-derivative.md) equal. Pairing the global relation with its [Schwarz conjugation of a spectral function](schwarz-conjugation-of-a-spectral-function.md) at $\ell(k+\lambda/k)=2\pi in$ determines every Fourier coefficient of the common normal trace. The zero mode at $\lambda=0$ follows from zero total flux. At negative parameters, uniqueness and possible removable spectral denominators must be checked rather than assumed.
