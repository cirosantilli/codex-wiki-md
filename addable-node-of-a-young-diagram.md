# Addable node of a Young diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Young diagram](young-diagram.md)

An addable node is a cell outside a [Young diagram](young-diagram.md) whose insertion leaves a Young diagram. Its left and upper predecessors, when present in the positive-coordinate grid, must already belong to the diagram. Different addable nodes have distinct [Young-diagram cell contents](content-of-a-young-diagram-cell.md).
