<h1 id="b/8/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A [minimizing sequence](../../../../../../minimizing-sequence.md) has an energy upper bound $C$. Set $H(t)=\Phi(t)-M\geq0$. Then $\int H(u_j')\leq C-M(b-a)=C_1$. Since $H(t)/|t|\to\infty$, for every $A>0$ there is $R_A$ such that $H(t)\geq A|t|$ for $|t|>R_A$. Consequently, for every measurable $E$,

$$
\int_E|u_j'|\leq R_A|E|+C_1/A.
$$

Taking first $A$ large and then $|E|$ small proves [uniform integrability](../../../../../../uniform-integrability.md); taking $E=(a,b)$ also gives a uniform $L^1$ bound. The [Dunford-Pettis theorem](../../../../../../dunford-pettis-theorem.md) on a finite-measure space identifies bounded [uniformly integrable](../../../../../../uniform-integrability.md) subsets of $L^1$ with relatively weakly compact ones. Thus a subsequence satisfies

$$
\boxed{u_{j_k}'\rightharpoonup v\text{ in }L^1(a,b).}
$$
