<h1 id="b/5/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

In this special case the endpoints are both zero, overriding the previous strict ordering. The affine function is zero and $I(0)=1$. Since $0<f(v)\leq1$, every admissible energy is at most one, proving **zero is a global maximizer**.

For $u_N(x)=N\min\{x,1-x\}$, the endpoint values vanish and its slopes are $\pm N$ [almost everywhere](../../../../../../almost-everywhere.md). Hence

$$
\boxed{I(u_N)=\frac1{1+N^{2008}}\to0,\qquad\inf_{\mathcal A}I=0.}
$$

Every admissible $u$ has essentially bounded derivative, so its integrand is bounded below by the positive constant $1/(1+\|u'\|_\infty^{2008})$. Thus no admissible function has zero energy and **there is no [global minimizer](../../../../../../global-minimizer.md)**.
