A [strong local minimizer of a variational integral](../../../../../../strong-local-minimizer-of-a-variational-integral.md) obeys the same inequality for all admissible $v$ with $\|v-\bar u\|_\infty<\rho$. The derivative of a competitor need not be close in this definition.
