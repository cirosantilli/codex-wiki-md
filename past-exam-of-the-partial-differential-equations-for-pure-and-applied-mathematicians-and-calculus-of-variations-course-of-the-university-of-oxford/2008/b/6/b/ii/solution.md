<h1 id="b/6/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The inequality $\cos t\geq1-t^2/2$ gives

$$
I(u)-I(0)\geq\frac12\int u'^2-\frac12\int u^2.
$$

The elementary endpoint estimate $|u(x)|^2\leq x\int_0^1u'^2$ implies $\int u^2\leq\tfrac12\int u'^2$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{I(u)-1\geq\frac14\int_0^1u'^2>0\quad(u\ne0).}
$$

Thus **zero is the unique [global minimizer](../../../../../../global-minimizer.md)**, and in particular a strict strong and strict weak local minimizer.
