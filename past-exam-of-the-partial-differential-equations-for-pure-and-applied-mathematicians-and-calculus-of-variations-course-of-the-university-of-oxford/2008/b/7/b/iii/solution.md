<h1 id="b/7/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For a fixed nonzero $u_0\in\mathcal A$,

$$
I(\gamma u_0)\leq\gamma^2\int_0^1u_0'^2+|\lambda|-\frac{\gamma^4}{2008}\int_0^1u_0^4\longrightarrow-\infty.
$$

The quartic coefficient is positive. Therefore **the energy is unbounded below and zero is not a [global minimizer](../../../../../../global-minimizer.md)**, for every value of $\lambda$.
