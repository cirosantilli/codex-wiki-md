<h1 id="b/7/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For a fixed nonzero Lipschitz zero-trace $\phi$, positivity for every $\lambda>-\pi^2$ gives $\int\phi'^2+\lambda\int\phi^2>0$. Let $\lambda\downarrow-\pi^2$ to obtain

$$
\boxed{\int_0^1\phi'^2\geq\pi^2\int_0^1\phi^2.}
$$

For general $\phi\in W_0^{1,2}$, approximate by smooth compactly supported functions in the $W^{1,2}$ [norm](../../../../../../norm.md). Both squared norms in the inequality converge, proving it for the full required space. Equality at $\sin(\pi x)$ verifies sharpness.
