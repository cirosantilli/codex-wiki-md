↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Put $w=t\phi$ into the differentiability expansion. The remainder is $o(|t|\|\phi\|)$, so division by $t$ and passage to zero gives **$g'(0)=DI(u)\phi$**. For $\phi=0$ this is immediate as well.
