<h1 id="b/5/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [first variation](../../../../../../first-variation.md) is

$$
DI(u)\phi=\int_0^1x^2(u'\phi+u\phi')dx=\int_0^1x^2(u\phi)'dx=-\int_0^12xu\phi\,dx.
$$

The endpoint terms vanish. The [fundamental lemma of the calculus of variations](../../../../../../fundamental-lemma-of-the-calculus-of-variations.md) gives $xu=0$ almost everywhere, hence $u=0$ on $(0,1)$. A Lipschitz representative is then identically zero and cannot have terminal value one. Therefore **there are no admissible solutions of the stationarity equation**.
