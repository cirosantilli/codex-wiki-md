<h1 id="b/5/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Stationarity says $\int(2u'\phi'+\phi)=0$, or $2u''=1$ in [distributions](../../../../../../distribution-mathematical-analysis.md). Integrating gives $u=x^2/4+Cx+D$. The endpoint values force $D=0$ and $C=3/4$. Conversely this polynomial satisfies the first-variation identity for every zero-trace variation. Thus **the only solution is $u(x)=(x^2+3x)/4$**.
