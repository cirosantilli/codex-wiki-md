<h1 id="b/7/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

If $u$ is a [strong local minimizer of a variational integral](../../../../../../strong-local-minimizer-of-a-variational-integral.md), the Weierstrass condition makes $u'(x)$ a [point of convexity](../../../../../../point-of-convexity.md) of the bounded [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) $g$ for almost every $x$. A global supporting line to a bounded [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) must have zero slope, and its contact value must be a [global minimum](../../../../../../global-minimum.md). Here that value is $-1$. Therefore $g(u')=-1$ almost everywhere and $I(u)=-1$, while every admissible energy is at least $-1$. Hence **every strong local minimizer is global**.
