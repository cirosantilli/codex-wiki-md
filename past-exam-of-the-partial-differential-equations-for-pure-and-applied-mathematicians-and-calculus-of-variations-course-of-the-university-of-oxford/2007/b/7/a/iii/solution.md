<h1 id="b/7/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

A [global minimizer](../../../../../../global-minimizer.md) satisfies $I(\bar u)\leq I(v)$ for every admissible $v$.

A [point of convexity](../../../../../../point-of-convexity.md) $s$ of a differentiable scalar [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) is one where its tangent supports the entire graph: $g(q)\geq g(s)+g'(s)(q-s)$ for every $q\in\mathbb R$. The [Weierstrass necessary condition](../../../../../../weierstrass-necessary-condition.md) for strong minimality, under its differentiability assumptions in the slope variable, is

$$
f(x,\bar u,q)-f(x,\bar u,\bar u')-f_v(x,\bar u,\bar u')(q-\bar u')\geq0\quad(q\in\mathbb R)
$$

for almost every $x$. In other words the reference slope is a [point of convexity](../../../../../../point-of-convexity.md) of the slope integrand. The later example is smooth, so these derivative-based conditions apply there.
