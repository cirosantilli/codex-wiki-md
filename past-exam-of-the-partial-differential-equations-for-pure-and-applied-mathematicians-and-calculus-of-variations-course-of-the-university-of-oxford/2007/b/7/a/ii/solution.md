<h1 id="b/7/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A [strong local minimizer of a variational integral](../../../../../../strong-local-minimizer-of-a-variational-integral.md) has the same property for all admissible $v$ with $\|v-\bar u\|_\infty<\rho$; its derivative need not be close. Since the supremum [norm](../../../../../../norm.md) is bounded by the Sobolev [norm](../../../../../../norm.md), this neighborhood contains a sufficiently small weak neighborhood. Thus **every strong local minimizer is a [weak local minimizer of a variational integral](../../../../../../weak-local-minimizer-of-a-variational-integral.md)**.
