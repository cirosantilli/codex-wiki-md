A [weak local minimizer of a variational integral](../../../../../../weak-local-minimizer-of-a-variational-integral.md) has some $\rho>0$ for which $I(v)\geq I(\bar u)$ whenever $v$ is admissible and $\|v-\bar u\|_{W^{1,\infty}}<\rho$.
