<h1 id="b/8/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Superlinearity and [continuity](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) make $\Phi$ bounded below by a finite constant $M$. Consequently **$I(u)\geq M(b-a)$ for every admissible [function](../../../../../../function-split.md)**. The affine endpoint interpolant has finite energy by [continuity](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) on its compact range, so the infimum is finite. Some other admissible energies can be infinite; this does not affect the bounded-below or minimizing-[sequence](../../../../../../sequence.md) argument.
