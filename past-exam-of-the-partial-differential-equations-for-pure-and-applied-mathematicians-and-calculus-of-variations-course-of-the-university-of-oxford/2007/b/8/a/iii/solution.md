<h1 id="b/8/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Write $l=\inf_{\mathcal A}I$. Since the limit is admissible, the assumed lower-semicontinuity inequality gives $l\leq I(u)\leq\liminf I(u_{j_k})=l$. Thus **$I(u)=l$ and $u$ is a [global minimizer](../../../../../../global-minimizer.md)**.

The extra integrand hypothesis in the [Tonelli existence theorem](../../../../../../tonelli-existence-theorem.md) for one dimension is **[convexity](../../../../../../convex-function.md) in the derivative variable for each fixed $(x,u)$**. Together with the stated [continuity](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) and coercive lower bound it gives the lower-semicontinuity property along the uniform-[function](../../../../../../function-split.md)/weak-derivative convergence just proved.
