# Boyle temperature

↑ **Parent:** [Virial expansion](virial-expansion.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boyle_temperature)

The [Boyle temperature](boyle-temperature.md) is a [temperature](temperature-split.md) at which the [second virial coefficient](second-virial-coefficient.md) vanishes. At that [temperature](temperature-split.md) the [compressibility factor](compressibility-factor.md) has no term linear in [number density](number-density.md), but higher terms in the [virial expansion](virial-expansion.md) need not vanish. For a bounded strictly repulsive finite-range [pair potential](pair-potential.md), $B_2(T)>0$ at every finite positive [temperature](temperature-split.md), with $B_2(T)\to0$ as $T\to\infty$; calling its [Boyle temperature](boyle-temperature.md) infinite describes this limiting cancellation, not a finite root.
