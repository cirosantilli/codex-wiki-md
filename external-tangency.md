# External tangency

↑ **Parent:** [Tangent circles](tangent-circles.md)

Two distinct positive-[radius](radius.md) [circles](circle.md) have external tangency when their closed [disks](disk-mathematics.md) meet at exactly one point. The [Euclidean distance](euclidean-distance.md) between their centres equals the sum of their [radii](radius.md). The contact point divides the segment of centres internally in the ratio $r_1:r_2$.
