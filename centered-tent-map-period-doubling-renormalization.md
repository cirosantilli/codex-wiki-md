# Centered tent-map period-doubling renormalization

↑ **Parent:** [Renormalization of the tent map near its fixed point](renormalization-of-the-tent-map-near-its-fixed-point.md)

For $1<s\leq\sqrt2$, set $h_s(x)=-(s-1)x$ and $J_s=[-(s-1),s-1]$. The [tent map](tent-map.md) sends $J_s$ to $[1+s-s^2,1]$, with disjoint interiors, and $T_s^2(J_s)\subseteq J_s$. Directly,

$$
h_s^{-1}\circ T_s^2\circ h_s=T_{s^2}.
$$

Successive squaring eventually brings every $s>1$ into $(\sqrt2,2]$. A [horseshoe for an interval map](horseshoe-for-an-interval-map.md) of an iterate in the renormalized coordinate lifts to a horseshoe of an iterate of the original map.
