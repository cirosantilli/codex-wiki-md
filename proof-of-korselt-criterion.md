# Proof of Korselt criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Korselt criterion](korselt-criterion.md)

If every integer satisfies $a^n\equiv a\pmod n$, taking $a=p$ shows that $p^2$ cannot divide $n$. Taking a [primitive root](primitive-root-modulo-n.md) modulo each prime divisor $p$ shows $p-1\mid n-1$. Conversely, these divisibilities and squarefreeness let [Fermat's little theorem](fermat-little-theorem.md) prove the congruence modulo each prime divisor, and coprimality of those divisors combines it modulo $n$.
