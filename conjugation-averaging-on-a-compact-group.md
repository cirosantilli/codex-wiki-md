# Conjugation averaging on a compact group

↑ **Parent:** [Class function](class-function.md)

Normalized [Haar measure](haar-measure.md) makes this averaging a norm-one projection onto continuous [class functions](class-function.md). Averaging a [trace](matrix-trace.md) coefficient replaces its coefficient [endomorphism](endomorphism.md) by $\int\theta(h)^{-1}\alpha\theta(h)\,dh$. On an irreducible summand this average is $(\operatorname{tr}\alpha/\dim V)I$ by [Schur lemma](schur-s-lemma.md). It follows that averaging finite-dimensional [matrix coefficients](matrix-coefficient.md) yields finite linear combinations of [irreducible characters](irreducible-character.md). Together with the [Peter-Weyl theorem](peter-weyl-theorem.md), this proves uniform density of those characters among continuous [class functions](class-function.md).
