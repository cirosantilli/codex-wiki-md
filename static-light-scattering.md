# Static light scattering

↑ **Parent:** [Scattering of light](scattering-of-light.md)

Measurement of time-averaged scattering intensity as a function of angle and concentration. For suitable dilute [polymer](polymer-split.md) solutions its small-wave-number behavior gives a [radius of gyration](radius-of-gyration.md); intensity weighting and interparticle correlations require attention.
