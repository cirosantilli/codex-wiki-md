# Paracompact space

↑ **Parent:** [Topological space](topological-space.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paracompact_space)

A topological space is paracompact when every [open cover](open-cover.md) has a locally finite open refinement. Every [metric space](metric-space.md) and every second-countable [topological manifold](topological-manifold.md) is paracompact.
