# Galaxy merger

↑ **Parent:** [Galaxy evolution](galaxy-evolution.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galaxy_merger)

A galaxy merger combines two or more [galaxies](galaxy-split.md) into a remnant. Their orbital [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) and energy are redistributed into internal stellar and halo motions, escaping matter, and, when present, dissipative gas. A bound pair produced by [tidal capture of galaxies](tidal-capture-of-galaxies.md) need not coalesce immediately.
