# Measurable cardinal is one-strong

↑ **Parent:** [Measurable cardinal](measurable-cardinal.md)

The [ultrapower embedding](ultrapower-embedding.md) $j:V_\lambda\to M$ associated with a measure on $\kappa<\lambda$ has critical point $\kappa$. For every $A\in V_{\kappa+1}$,

$$
A=j(A)\cap V_\kappa,
$$

so $V_{\kappa+1}\subseteq M$. Hence every measurable cardinal is [1-strong](one-strong-cardinal.md).
