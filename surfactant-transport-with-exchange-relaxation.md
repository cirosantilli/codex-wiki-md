# Surfactant transport with exchange relaxation

↑ **Parent:** [Surfactant](surfactant.md)

The [material derivative](material-derivative.md) of surface concentration combines tangential and normal area change, [surface diffusion](surface-diffusion.md), and relaxation towards a reservoir concentration $C_0$. The rate $k$ represents exchange; setting $k=0$ recovers transport without exchange. The concentration is measured per interfacial area, so stretching dilutes it.
