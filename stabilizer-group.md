# Stabilizer group

↑ **Parent:** [Pauli group](pauli-group.md)

A stabilizer group is an abelian subgroup of the [Pauli group](pauli-group.md) that does not contain $-I$. Its simultaneous positive eigenspace is its stabilizer subspace.
