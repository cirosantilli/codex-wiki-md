# Zero mode

↑ **Parent:** [Eigenvector](eigenvector.md)

A [zero mode](zero-mode.md) is a nonzero eigenvector of an operator with eigenvalue zero. For a differential fluctuation operator it must also satisfy the specified [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md). A zero mode prevents ordinary inversion of the operator and makes its unmodified [functional determinant](functional-determinant.md) vanish. Gauge directions can produce zero modes of a kinetic operator, while stationary-path focusing can produce them in a semiclassical boundary-value problem. Removing, separately integrating or otherwise treating these directions is necessary before using a Gaussian determinant formula.
