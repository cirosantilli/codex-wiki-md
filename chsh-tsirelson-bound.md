# CHSH Tsirelson bound

↑ **Parent:** [Tsirelson's bound](tsirelson-s-bound.md)

For dichotomic [observables](observable.md) with $A_i^2=B_j^2=I$, let $\mathcal B=A_0\otimes(B_0+B_1)+A_1\otimes(B_0-B_1)$. Expanding gives $\mathcal B^2=4I-[A_0,A_1]\otimes[B_0,B_1]$. Each commutator has [operator norm](operator-norm.md) at most two, so $\|\mathcal B\|^2\le8$ and $|\langle\mathcal B\rangle|\le2\sqrt2$. Appropriate spin measurements of a [Bell state](bell-state-split.md) attain equality. The local hidden-variable bound of the corresponding [CHSH inequality](chsh-inequality.md) is two.
