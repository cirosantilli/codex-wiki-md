# Left singular vector

↑ **Parent:** [Singular value](singular-value.md)

A unit left singular vector of a [matrix](matrix.md) or [compact operator](compact-operator-split.md) $A$ is an [eigenvector](eigenvector.md) of $AA^*$ with [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) $\sigma_j^2$. For $\sigma_j>0$, it pairs with a [right singular vector](right-singular-vector.md) $v_j$ through $Av_j=\sigma_j u_j$ and $A^*u_j=\sigma_j v_j$.
