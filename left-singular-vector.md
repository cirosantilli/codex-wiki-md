# Left singular vector

↑ **Parent:** [Singular value](singular-value.md)

A [left singular vector](left-singular-vector.md) $u_j$ is a [unit vector](unit-vector.md) in the output space satisfying $AA^*u_j=\sigma_j^2u_j$. For positive [singular values](singular-value.md), the pairing also satisfies $A^*u_j=\sigma_jv_j$. Thus $u_1=(1,0)^T$ and $u_2=(0,1)^T$ describe the output axes of the preceding [ellipse](ellipse.md), while the [right singular vectors](right-singular-vector.md) describe its input directions. At zero [singular values](singular-value.md), division by $\sigma_j$ is impossible: complete the [orthonormal bases](orthonormal-basis.md) independently in the [null spaces](kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) of $A$ and $A^*$. Repeated [singular values](singular-value.md) allow different matched [orthonormal bases](orthonormal-basis.md) within the corresponding [eigenspaces](eigenspace.md). The same positive pairing extends to a [compact operator](compact-operator-split.md) through its [singular value system](singular-system-of-a-compact-operator.md).

Even distinct [singular values](singular-value.md) do not fix arrow signs: replacing both $u_j$ and $v_j$ by their negatives preserves $Av_j=\sigma_j u_j$ and the [outer product](outer-product.md) $u_jv_j^*$. Reversing only one arrow would change that term's sign. The paired directions, rather than independently chosen arrow orientations, encode the [matrix](matrix.md).
