# Exterior and symmetric squares of the seven-dimensional G2 representation

↑ **Parent:** [Crystal of the seven-dimensional G2 representation](crystal-of-the-seven-dimensional-g2-representation.md)

For $V=L(\omega_1)$ with the [G2 root system](g2-root-system.md) numbered short-first,

$$
\Lambda^2V=L(\omega_2)\oplus L(\omega_1),\qquad S^2V=L(2\omega_1)\oplus L(0).
$$

In the chain basis of the [crystal of the seven-dimensional G2 representation](crystal-of-the-seven-dimensional-g2-representation.md), normalize $v_1=f_1v_0$, $v_2=f_2v_1$, $v_3=f_1v_2$. Then $v_0\wedge v_1$ and $v_0\wedge v_3-2v_1\wedge v_2$ are nonzero [highest-weight vectors](highest-weight-vector.md) of weights $\omega_2$ and $\omega_1$; the identities $e_1v_1=v_0$, $e_1v_3=2v_2$, $e_2v_2=v_1$ verify both raising conditions. The [Weyl complete reducibility theorem](weyl-complete-reducibility-theorem.md) and [G2 dimension polynomial](g2-dimension-polynomial.md) exhaust the twenty-one dimensions of the [exterior square](exterior-square.md). In the [symmetric square](symmetric-square.md), $v_0^2$ generates the twenty-seven-dimensional summand. Self-duality supplies a [Lie-invariant bilinear form](lie-invariant-bilinear-form.md), which is symmetric because a [nondegenerate](nondegenerate-bilinear-form.md) [alternating bilinear form](alternating-bilinear-form.md) cannot have odd [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md); its inverse gives the remaining invariant line.
