# DDT

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DDT)

An organochlorine insecticide chemically distinct from [DDD](dichlorodiphenyldichloroethane.md). Its use and unintended ecological effects were central to [Silent Spring by Rachel Carson](silent-spring-by-rachel-carson.md).
