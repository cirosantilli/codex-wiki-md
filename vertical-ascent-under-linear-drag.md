# Vertical ascent under linear drag

↑ **Parent:** [Linear drag](linear-drag.md)

For constant downward gravitational [acceleration](acceleration.md) $g$ and [linear drag](linear-drag.md) $-\alpha v$, the upward [velocity](velocity.md) solves $m\dot v=-mg-\alpha v$ with $v(0)=u_0\ge0$. Its first zero occurs at the displayed [time](time-in-physics.md). The dimensionless control variable is $\alpha u_0/(mg)$, and the limit $\alpha\to0$ gives $u_0/g$.
