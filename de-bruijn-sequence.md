# De Bruijn sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Combinatorics](combinatorics-split.md)

A cyclic sequence over an alphabet of size $q$ in which every word of length $n$ appears exactly once as a cyclic window. Its length is $q^n$. The [De Bruijn graph](de-bruijn-graph.md) converts its construction into an [Eulerian cycle](eulerian-cycle.md) problem.
