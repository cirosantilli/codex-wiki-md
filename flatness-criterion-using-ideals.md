# Flatness criterion using ideals

↑ **Parent:** [Flat module](flat-module.md)

The arrow is the canonical multiplication map, not an arbitrary abstract module isomorphism. Its inclusion into $M$ is $\mu_I:I\otimes_AM\to M$. Dualizing identifies $\mu_I^*$ with the restriction $\operatorname{Hom}_A(A,M^*)\to\operatorname{Hom}_A(I,M^*)$. All these maps are surjective precisely when $M^*$ is injective, by the [Baer criterion](baer-criterion.md). The [injective cogenerator of abelian groups](injective-cogenerator-of-abelian-groups.md) detects injectivity of $\mu_I$, and the [Lambek theorem](lambek-theorem.md) gives flatness. No Noetherian or finite-generation hypothesis is needed.
