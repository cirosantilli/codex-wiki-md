# Photoelectric saturation current

↑ **Parent:** [Photoelectric effect](photoelectric-effect.md)

The [photoelectric saturation current](photoelectric-saturation-current.md) is the [photocurrent](photocurrent.md) reached when increasing the collecting [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) no longer increases the fraction of available emitted [electrons](electron.md) collected. It is saturation with collecting voltage at fixed illumination. It does not mean that increasing incident [light intensity](light-intensity.md) cannot increase emission.

If $P$ is incident [radiant flux](radiant-flux.md) on the emitting area and $\eta$ is the external [quantum efficiency](quantum-efficiency.md) for producing escaping [electrons](electron.md), then the incident [photon](photon.md) arrival rate is $P/(h\nu)$ and complete collection gives $I_{\rm sat}=e\eta P/(h\nu)$. For the example's $\nu=8.00\times10^{14}\,\mathrm{Hz}$, choose $\eta=0.10$ and $P=1.00\,\mathrm{\mu W}$. The calculated [photon](photon.md) arrival rate is $1.886488\times10^{12}\,\mathrm{s}^{-1}$ and $I_{\rm sat}=30.224866\,\mathrm{nA}$. Doubling $P$ doubles this current to $60.449731\,\mathrm{nA}$ while leaving $V_s$ fixed. At fixed illuminated area this doubles the [irradiance](irradiance.md). Changing [frequency](frequency.md) at fixed $P$ changes the arrival rate as well as the [photon](photon.md) [energy](energy-split.md); $\eta$ can also change, so there is no general rule that a higher [frequency](frequency.md) gives a larger [photocurrent](photocurrent.md).

A missing plateau can indicate incomplete collection or an [electric field](electric-field-split.md) altered by the emitted [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md). Heating, surface changes and additional emission channels can invalidate a constant $\eta$. Even a small amount of unwanted higher-[frequency](frequency.md) radiation can move an apparent endpoint. A [stopping potential](stopping-potential.md) inferred from zero net current additionally needs background subtraction and a check for emission from the collector itself.
