# Nearest neighbour distance

↑ **Parent:** [Order statistic](order-statistic.md)

For observations $X_1,\ldots,X_n$, the [nearest neighbour distance](nearest-neighbour-distance.md) of order $k$ at $x$ is the $k$th [order statistic](order-statistic.md) of the distances $|X_i-x|$. An atomless distance [distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md) ensures ties occur with probability zero.
