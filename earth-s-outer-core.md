<h1 id="earth-s-outer-core">Earth's outer core</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Earth](earth.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earth's_outer_core)

Earth's outer core is a liquid, electrically conducting region whose buoyant motion and rotation sustain the [geodynamo](geodynamo.md). Its [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) both responds to the flow through induction and modifies that flow through the [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md). A prescribed-velocity kinematic model describes onset rather than the entire nonlinear core dynamics.
