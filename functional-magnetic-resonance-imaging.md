# Functional magnetic resonance imaging

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic resonance imaging](magnetic-resonance-imaging.md)

Imaging task-related or spontaneous changes in magnetic-resonance signals associated with physiology, commonly the [BOLD signal](blood-oxygen-level-dependent-signal.md). Hemodynamic timing and spatial averaging constrain inferences about neural firing and cell-level computations.
