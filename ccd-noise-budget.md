# CCD noise budget

↑ **Parent:** [Charge-coupled device](charge-coupled-device.md)

For source [electrons](electron.md) $S$, sky [electrons](electron.md) $B$, dark-current rate $d$, total exposure $t$, $M$ reads and rms [read noise](read-noise.md) $r$, independent [variances](variance-split.md) add to $S+B+dt+Mr^2$. [calibration](calibration-differential-geometry.md) can remove reproducible means but cannot remove the science exposure's random [photon](photon.md) or dark-current fluctuations. A finite background estimate and imperfect [calibration](calibration-differential-geometry.md) add further uncertainty.
