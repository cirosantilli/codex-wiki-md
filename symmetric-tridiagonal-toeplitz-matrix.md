# Symmetric tridiagonal Toeplitz matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Toeplitz matrix](toeplitz-matrix.md)

An $m\times m$ symmetric tridiagonal Toeplitz matrix has one constant diagonal $\alpha$ and equal constant subdiagonal and superdiagonal $\beta$. Its eigenvectors are the [discrete sine transform](discrete-sine-transform.md) vectors $q^{(k)}_j=\sin(jk\pi/(m+1))$, with eigenvalues $\alpha+2\beta\cos(k\pi/(m+1))$.
