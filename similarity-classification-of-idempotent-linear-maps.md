# Similarity classification of idempotent linear maps

↑ **Parent:** [Projection (linear algebra)](projection-linear-algebra.md)

If $T^2=T$, every [vector](vector.md) splits as $v=(v-Tv)+Tv$ in $\ker T\oplus\operatorname{im}T$. On these summands $T$ is respectively zero and the identity. Its [matrix](matrix.md) in an adapted [basis](basis.md) is therefore $\operatorname{diag}(0,I_r)$, where $r$ is its [rank of a linear map](rank-of-a-linear-map.md). Two [idempotent linear maps](projection-linear-algebra.md) on the same finite-dimensional [vector space](vector-space-split.md) are [similar matrices](matrix-similarity.md) exactly when their ranks agree.
