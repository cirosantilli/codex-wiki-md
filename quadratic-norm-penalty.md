# Quadratic norm penalty

↑ **Parent:** [Tikhonov regularization](tikhonov-regularization.md)

A squared [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) norm penalizes reconstruction size in [Tikhonov regularization](tikhonov-regularization.md). For $\alpha>0$, adding $\alpha\|x\|^2$ to $\|Ax-y\|^2$ produces a [strictly convex](strictly-convex-function.md) [coercive function](coercive-function.md) with unique minimizer. Its first variation contributes $\alpha x$ to the [normal equation for a linear inverse problem](normal-equation-for-a-linear-inverse-problem.md), shifting $A^*A$ to $A^*A+\alpha I$. It selects a minimum-norm representative and controls amplification of noisy small-singular-value components.
