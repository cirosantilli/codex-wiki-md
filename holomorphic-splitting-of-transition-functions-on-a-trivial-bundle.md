# Holomorphic splitting of transition functions on a trivial bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Holomorphic vector bundle](holomorphic-vector-bundle.md)

For a holomorphically [trivial vector bundle](trivial-vector-bundle.md), choose a global [holomorphic](complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md) [bundle frame](frame-of-a-vector-bundle.md). Let the columns of $H_\alpha$ be the coordinates of that frame in the prescribed frame over $U_\alpha$. If fiber coordinates transform as $s_\beta=F_{\alpha\beta}s_\alpha$, then $H_\beta=F_{\alpha\beta}H_\alpha$. Each [matrix](matrix.md) is [holomorphic](complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md) and invertible, giving the displayed splitting. Conversely such [matrices](matrix.md) give agreeing global [holomorphic sections](holomorphic-section.md) forming a frame. Topological triviality alone does not supply this conclusion.
