# Temporal binding window

↑ **Parent:** [Multisensory integration](multisensory-integration.md)

A task-defined range of intersensory delays compatible with grouping or reported simultaneity. Its estimate depends on stimulus content, sensory uncertainty and decision criteria. It is not automatically the duration of one anatomical neural integrator.
