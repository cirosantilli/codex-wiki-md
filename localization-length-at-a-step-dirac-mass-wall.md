# Localization length at a step Dirac mass wall

↑ **Parent:** [Chiral Dirac interface state](chiral-dirac-interface-state.md)

When the mass is constant $V_+>0$ above the wall and $V_-<0$ below it, amplitude decay lengths are $\xi_+=\hbar c/V_+$ and $\xi_-=\hbar c/|V_-|$. A scalar transverse envelope multiplying a unit [spinor](spinor.md) has normalization $\sqrt{2/(\xi_++\xi_-)}$. Density decays twice as fast as amplitude.
