# Angular-diameter ratio under a collinear boost

↑ **Parent:** [Relativistic aberration](relativistic-aberration.md)

For two coincident observers with relative motion along the sightline to a small distant source, its [angular diameter](angular-diameter.md) changes by a [rapidity](rapidity.md) factor. A boost toward the source compresses its incoming angular cone: $\tan(\vartheta'/2)=e^{-\eta}\tan(\vartheta/2)$ for angular radius $\vartheta$. For a small source the larger-to-smaller diameter ratio is $r=e^{|\eta|}$, giving the displayed relative speed. A finite angular size requires the half-angle formula, and arbitrary transverse relative motion cannot be inferred from one diameter ratio alone.
