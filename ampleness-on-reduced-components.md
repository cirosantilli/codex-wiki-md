# Ampleness on reduced components

↑ **Parent:** [Ample line bundle](ample-line-bundle.md)

On a [projective scheme](projective-scheme.md), a [line bundle](line-bundle.md) is [ample](ample-line-bundle.md) exactly when its restriction to every reduced irreducible component is [ample](ample-line-bundle.md). The disjoint union of those components maps finitely and surjectively to the scheme, so finite-surjective descent of the [finite pullback of an ample line bundle](finite-pullback-of-an-ample-line-bundle.md) proves this. The same argument applies to restrictions to any closed subscheme. Thus nilpotent structure does not affect [ampleness](ample-cartier-divisor.md).
