# Polish notation

↑ **Parent:** [Expression notation](expression-notation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_notation)

Place each operator before its operands. With known operator arities and unambiguous tokens, the [abstract syntax tree](abstract-syntax-tree.md) is recovered recursively without precedence rules or parentheses. A binary application is encoded by its operator, the first subtree's encoding and the second subtree's encoding: this is a [preorder traversal](preorder-traversal.md) of the expression tree.
