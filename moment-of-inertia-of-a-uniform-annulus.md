# Moment of inertia of a uniform annulus

↑ **Parent:** [Moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md)

A uniform planar annulus of outer radius $a$, inner radius $b<a$ and mass $M$ has surface density $M/[\pi(a^2-b^2)]$. Integrating $r^2$ over its mass gives the axial [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) $I=M(a^2+b^2)/2$. Subtracting the inner disc from a complete disc gives the same result, provided their surface densities match.
