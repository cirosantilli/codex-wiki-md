# Smooth endoplasmic reticulum

↑ **Parent:** [Endoplasmic reticulum](endoplasmic-reticulum.md)

[Smooth endoplasmic reticulum](smooth-endoplasmic-reticulum.md) lacks attached [ribosomes](ribosome.md). Specialized regions support [lipid](lipid.md) [metabolism](metabolism.md) and processing of foreign compounds; the [endoplasmic reticulum](endoplasmic-reticulum.md) also stores and releases [calcium](calcium.md) [ions](ion.md). Regional specialization does not split the network into two separate [organelles](organelle.md), nor make every smooth region perform every function.[Molecular Biology of the Cell, 4th edition by Bruce Alberts and others; Smooth ER Is Abundant in Some Specialized Cells, Figures 12-38A and 12-38B and calcium-storage paragraphs](molecular-biology-of-the-cell-4th-edition-by-bruce-alberts-and-others/the-endoplasmic-reticulum.md)
