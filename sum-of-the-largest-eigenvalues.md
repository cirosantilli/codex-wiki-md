# Sum of the largest eigenvalues

↑ **Parent:** [Support function](support-function.md)

For a real [symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md) $X$ with [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $\lambda_1\geq\cdots\geq\lambda_n$, the sum of its $k$ largest [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) is the [support function](support-function.md) of the [fantope](fantope.md):

$$
F_k(X)=\max\{\operatorname{tr}(XY):0\preceq Y\preceq I,\ \operatorname{tr}Y=k\}.
$$

Here $\preceq$ is the [Loewner order](loewner-order.md). In an [orthonormal eigenbasis](orthonormal-eigenbasis.md) for $X$, the objective depends only on the diagonal entries of $Y$, which lie in the [capped simplex](capped-simplex.md). Thus the identity reduces to the [sum of the largest components](sum-of-the-largest-components.md) of the [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md).
