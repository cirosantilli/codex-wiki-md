# Collisional ionization equilibrium

↑ **Parent:** [Collisional ionization](collisional-ionization.md)

In [collisional ionization equilibrium](collisional-ionization-equilibrium.md), collision-driven [ionization](ionization.md) and recombination balance for the populated ion stages, determining their relative abundances at a given temperature. The resulting cooling curve includes the loss of line-emitting bound states as temperature increases. Rapidly cooling gas or external photoionization can invalidate this equilibrium assumption.
