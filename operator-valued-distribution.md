# Operator-valued distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Quantized field](quantized-field.md)

An operator-valued distribution assigns a [linear operator](linear-operator.md) on a common dense domain to each [smooth](smooth-function.md) [test function](test-function.md) of [compact support](compact-support.md) $f$, continuously and linearly in the appropriate weak sense. A [quantized field](quantized-field.md) is generally of this type: $\phi(x)$ is formal point notation, while the smeared $\phi(f)$ has operator meaning. Equal-time [canonical commutation relations](canonical-commutation-relation.md) and [momentum](momentum.md) oscillators are interpreted after smearing, which avoids treating a [Dirac delta distribution](dirac-delta-function.md) or a coincident field as a finite ordinary operator. Products at coincident points can need further regularization even when individual smeared fields are defined.
