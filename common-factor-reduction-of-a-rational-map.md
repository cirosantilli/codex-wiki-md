# Common-factor reduction of a rational map

↑ **Parent:** [Rational map (complex analysis)](rational-map-complex-analysis.md)

Cancel the common polynomial factors of $p$ and $q$ before computing the [degree of a rational map of the Riemann sphere](degree-of-a-rational-map-of-the-riemann-sphere.md). Removable common zeros do not contribute sheets. The reduced maximum polynomial degree is the number of inverse images of a generic target value, counted with positive holomorphic local degrees.
