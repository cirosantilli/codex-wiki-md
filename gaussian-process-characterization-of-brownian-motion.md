# Gaussian-process characterization of Brownian motion

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md)

A centered [Gaussian process](gaussian-process.md) with almost surely continuous paths and covariance $\mathbb E[B_sB_t]=\min(s,t)$ is a standard [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md).
