# Stress sensitization

↑ **Parent:** [Diathesis-stress model](diathesis-stress-model.md)

A pattern in which prior [adversity](adversity.md) increases sensitivity to later [psychological stress](psychological-stress.md), such as a lower subsequent [psychological stress](psychological-stress.md) level accompanying [depression](depression.md). Demonstrating the pattern does not by itself identify its mechanism or exclude differences in [exposure](exposure.md) measurement and prior liability.[2000, sensitization hypothesis and prospective test](depression-and-sensitization-to-stressors-among-young-women-as-a-function-of-childhood-adversity-by-constance-hammen-and-others.md)
