<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The two crossings of $j,h$ make their full [anyon monodromy](../../../../anyon-monodromy.md). For a [transparent anyon](../../../../transparent-anyon.md) $h$, this is the identity, so that pair of crossings can be removed together. No single-crossing exchange phase, and no [topological spin](../../../../topological-spin.md) from a twist, is introduced.

After this removal the upper and lower [anyon fusion trees](../../../../anyon-fusion-tree.md) differ only in their intermediate labels. In the [physics normalization of anyon diagrams](../../../../physics-normalization-of-anyon-diagrams.md), successive split-fuse bubbles give the [Kronecker deltas](../../../../kronecker-delta.md) $\delta_{bf}$ and $\delta_{cg}$. The remaining closed normalized state has norm one. Hence, for the admissible labelings of [a](../a.md),

$$
\boxed{\mathcal A_{bc;fg}=\delta_{bf}\delta_{cg}.}
$$

In particular, every admissible diagram satisfying the label matching in [a](../a.md) evaluates to $1$. There is no factor $d_a$ here: the source explicitly permits the [physics normalization of anyon diagrams](../../../../physics-normalization-of-anyon-diagrams.md), in which a closed normalized state has value $1$.
