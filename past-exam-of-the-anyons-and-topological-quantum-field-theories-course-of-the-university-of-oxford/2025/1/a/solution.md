<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Read the arrows upwards through the central part of the diagram. The lower [anyon fusion tree](../../../../anyon-fusion-tree.md) first splits $a$ into $b,c$, then $b$ into $d,j$ and $c$ into $h,e$. The upper [anyon fusion tree](../../../../anyon-fusion-tree.md) combines $d,j$ into $f$, $h,e$ into $g$, and finally $f,g$ into $a$. The two interior crossings are not vertices. Thus admissibility requires the six [anyon fusion multiplicities](../../../../anyon-fusion-multiplicity.md)

$$
N^a_{bc}N^b_{dj}N^c_{he}N^f_{dj}N^g_{he}N^a_{fg}=1.
$$

If any factor vanishes, the diagram has zero value.

Make an [anyon vertex gauge transformation](../../../../anyon-vertex-gauge-transformation.md) by multiplying each splitting vertex $z\to x,y$ by an independent [wave phase](../../../../phase-waves.md) $u^{xy}_z$ and its adjoint fusion vertex by $(u^{xy}_z)^{-1}$. The diagram changes by

$$
G=\frac{u^{bc}_a u^{dj}_b u^{he}_c}{u^{fg}_a u^{dj}_f u^{he}_g}.
$$

Therefore the label-matching condition for cancellation of all [anyon vertex gauge transformation](../../../../anyon-vertex-gauge-transformation.md) factors is

$$
\boxed{b=f,\qquad c=g,}
$$

with $a,d,e,h,j$ otherwise unrestricted apart from the [anyon fusion rules](../../../../anyon-fusion-rule.md). This statement also allows coincident labels: the three ordered vertex triples in the numerator must equal those in the denominator as multisets; matching the triples in any of the six possible orders still forces $b=f,c=g$. Conversely, these equalities visibly give $G=1$.

Closure of the drawing alone does not guarantee invariance under an [anyon vertex gauge transformation](../../../../anyon-vertex-gauge-transformation.md): unmatched fusion and splitting vertices retain their independently chosen basis phases. A zero value is, of course, invariant regardless of phases; the boxed condition describes cancellation of the vertex factors themselves.
