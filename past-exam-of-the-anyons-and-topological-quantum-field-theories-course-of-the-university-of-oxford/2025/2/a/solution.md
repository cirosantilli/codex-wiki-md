<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Cut one nonseparating circle on each handle of the closed [orientable surface](../../../../orientable-surface.md) $\Sigma_g$ of [genus](../../../../genus-of-a-surface.md) $g$. The cut surface is a [sphere](../../../../sphere.md) with $2g$ boundary circles. Gluing a pair back together requires an [anyon](../../../../anyon.md) label $a_i$ on one boundary and the [antianyon](../../../../antianyon.md) label $\bar a_i$ on the oppositely oriented boundary. Sum over these labels independently for the $g$ handles.

For a fixed labeling, the [dimension](../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) of the resulting [anyon fusion space](../../../../anyon-fusion-space.md) on the [sphere](../../../../sphere.md), with total [vacuum anyon](../../../../vacuum-anyon.md) charge, is

$$
\left[N_{a_1}N_{\bar a_1}\cdots N_{a_g}N_{\bar a_g}\right]_{11}.
$$

The gluing rule of the [topological quantum field theory](../../../../topological-quantum-field-theory.md) therefore gives the [handle-cut formula for anyon ground-state degeneracy](../../../../handle-cut-formula-for-anyon-ground-state-degeneracy.md):

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{GSD}(\Sigma_g)=\sum_{a_1,\ldots,a_g}\left[N_{a_1}N_{\bar a_1}\cdots N_{a_g}N_{\bar a_g}\right]_{11}=\left[\left(\sum_aN_aN_{\bar a}\right)^g\right]_{11}.}
$$

Here $1$ denotes the [vacuum anyon](../../../../vacuum-anyon.md), and the [anyon fusion matrices](../../../../anyon-fusion-matrix.md) have entries $(N_b)_{ac}=N^c_{ab}$, as in the source. At $g=0$, the empty product is the identity [matrix](../../../../matrix.md), giving one [ground state](../../../../ground-state.md). At $g=1$, $(N_aN_{\bar a})_{11}=1$ for each simple charge, so the [ground-state degeneracy](../../../../ground-state-degeneracy.md) of the [torus](../../../../torus.md) is the number of [anyon](../../../../anyon.md) types.
