# Divisible module

↑ **Parent:** [Injective module](injective-module.md)

Over an integral domain, a module $M$ is divisible when $rM=M$ for every nonzero $r$. A [divisible group](divisible-group.md) is precisely a divisible $\mathbb Z$-module, and over a principal ideal domain divisibility is equivalent to injectivity.
