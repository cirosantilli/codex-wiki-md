# Bi-invariant Riemannian metric

↑ **Parent:** [Left-invariant metric](left-invariant-metric.md)

A [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md) on a [Lie group](lie-group.md) is bi-invariant if both left and right translations are isometries. Its identity inner product is invariant under the [Adjoint representation of a Lie group](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-group.md); infinitesimally,

$$
\langle[X,Y],Z\rangle+\langle Y,[X,Z]\rangle=0.
$$

This skew-adjointness is the key additional property beyond left invariance.
