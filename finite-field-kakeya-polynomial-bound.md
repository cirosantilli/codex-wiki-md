# Finite-field Kakeya polynomial bound

↑ **Parent:** [Finite-field Kakeya set](finite-field-kakeya-set.md)

For a [finite-field Kakeya set](finite-field-kakeya-set.md) $A\subseteq\mathbb F_q^n$, a smaller [cardinality](cardinality.md) would give a nonzero [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) of [total degree of a polynomial](total-degree-of-a-polynomial.md) at most $q-1$ vanishing on $A$, by the [dimension of a bounded-total-degree polynomial space](dimension-of-a-bounded-total-degree-polynomial-space.md). Its restriction to each complete [affine line in a vector space](affine-line-in-a-vector-space.md) has $q$ [roots of a polynomial](root-of-a-polynomial.md) and degree less than $q$, hence vanishes identically. The top [homogeneous polynomial](homogeneous-polynomial.md) part then vanishes in every direction. A [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) of degree less than $q$ in each variable cannot vanish everywhere on $\mathbb F_q^n$ unless it is zero, giving a contradiction. For the [prime field](prime-field.md), this is the [polynomial nonvanishing below the field size](polynomial-nonvanishing-below-the-field-size.md).
