# Zero surgery on the Hopf link

↑ **Parent:** [Hopf link](hopf-link.md)

The [Hopf link](hopf-link.md) exterior is $T^2\times I$. Each component's [Seifert longitude](seifert-longitude.md) is the other's meridian. Filling the two boundary tori along these longitudes therefore attaches solid tori with meridians intersecting once on a common torus, recovering the standard genus-one splitting of the [three-sphere](three-sphere.md). Merely specifying two linked [unknots](unknot.md) without specifying their link type does not force this answer.
