# Exercise threshold

↑ **Parent:** [American option](american-option.md)

A boundary level of an underlying process at which an exercise policy stops. The displayed [stopping time](stopping-time.md) is an up-crossing policy; put-like claims often use a down-crossing boundary. A specified threshold need not be the optimal one.
