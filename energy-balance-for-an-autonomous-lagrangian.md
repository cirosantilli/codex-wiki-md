# Energy balance for an autonomous Lagrangian

↑ **Parent:** [Hamiltonian conservation law](hamiltonian-conservation-law.md)

For an autonomous [Lagrangian](lagrangian.md) $L(x,\dot x)$, define its [action](action.md) $A=\int L\,dt$ and [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) $H=\dot xL_{\dot x}-L$. The [chain rule](chain-rule.md) gives

$$
\dot H=\dot x\left(\frac d{dt}L_{\dot x}-L_x\right)
=-\dot x\frac{\delta A}{\delta x}.
$$

Thus the identity holds along forced trajectories as well as solutions of the homogeneous [Euler-Lagrange equation](euler-lagrange-equation.md). Explicit time dependence adds $-\partial_tL$; stochastic work requires a consistent regularization or the [Stratonovich chain rule](stratonovich-chain-rule.md).
