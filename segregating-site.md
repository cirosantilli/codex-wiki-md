# Segregating site

↑ **Parent:** [Population genetics](population-genetics.md)

A segregating site is a position at which at least two states occur in the sampled chromosomes. Under an [infinite sites mutation model](infinite-sites-mutation-model.md), mutations below the sample's common ancestor correspond one-to-one to segregating sites. Their number is commonly denoted $S$.
