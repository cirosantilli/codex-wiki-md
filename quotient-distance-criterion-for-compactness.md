# Quotient-distance criterion for compactness

↑ **Parent:** [Compact operator](compact-operator-split.md)

For a [bounded linear operator](continuous-linear-operator.md) into a [Banach space](banach-space-split.md), compactness is equivalent to the displayed condition, where $\pi_Y$ is the [quotient map](quotient-map.md) by a finite-dimensional subspace of the target. A finite net for the image of the unit ball gives such a subspace by taking its span. Conversely, if the image lies within $\varepsilon$ of a finite-dimensional subspace, choose approximants there; they remain bounded because the original image is bounded. A finite net for these approximants gives a slightly larger finite net for the image. This proves [total boundedness](totally-bounded-space.md) and hence compactness. This condition does not require choosing the approximants by a [linear map](linear-map.md), and therefore does not assume an approximation property for the [Banach space](banach-space-split.md).
