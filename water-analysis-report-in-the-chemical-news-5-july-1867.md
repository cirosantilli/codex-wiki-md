# Water analysis report in The Chemical News (5 July 1867)

↑ **Parent:** [Chemistry bibliography](chemistry-bibliography-split.md)

Unsigned report of the Chemical Society meeting of 20 June 1867, published on 5 July. It describes the ammonia procedure and contemporary criticism of the preliminary distillation's behaviour with gelatin and urea. The report is contemporary evidence for a method available before the December examination, not a modern guarantee of water safety.
