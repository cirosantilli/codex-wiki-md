# Equilibrium claim-size distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Classical risk model](classical-risk-model.md)

For a nonnegative claim size $X$ of finite positive mean $\mu$, its equilibrium claim-size distribution has this density and survival function $\overline F_I(x)=\mu^{-1}\int_x^\infty\overline F_X(t)\,dt$. The [tail integral formula for moments](tail-integral-formula-for-moments.md) normalizes the density. Its [moment-generating function](moment-generating-function.md) is $(M_X(r)-1)/(\mu r)$ wherever finite, so at an [adjustment coefficient](adjustment-coefficient.md) it equals one plus the safety loading.
