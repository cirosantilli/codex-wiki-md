# Poisson Stein equation

↑ **Parent:** [Stein-Chen method](stein-chen-method.md)

For indicator test functions $g$, the equation has a solution with $\|\Delta f\|_\infty\le1$. One proof uses an [immigration--death process](immigration-death-process.md) with immigration rate $\mu$ and per-particle death rate one. Its potential $u(k)=-\int_0^\infty(\mathbb Eg(Z_t^k)-\mathbb Eg(Z))\,dt$ satisfies its generator equation, and $f(k)=u(k)-u(k-1)$. Coupling two additional initial particles bounds the second difference of $u$ by $\int_0^\infty2e^{-2t}dt=1$.
