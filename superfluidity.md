# Superfluidity

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical physics](statistical-physics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superfluidity)

[Superfluidity](superfluidity.md) is a quantum-fluid response with [phase stiffness](phase-stiffness.md) and flow without viscous dissipation in an appropriate low-speed regime. A [superfluid](superfluid.md) supports a phase-gradient current; defects and excitations can limit that flow. [Bose-Einstein condensation](bose-einstein-condensation.md) concerns macroscopic one-particle occupation and is a distinct criterion: the Gaussian [phase stiffness](phase-stiffness.md) theory can have divergent absolute-phase fluctuations while retaining a cost for [gradients](gradient.md).
