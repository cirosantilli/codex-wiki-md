# Weighted Poincare inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Poincaré inequality](poincare-inequality.md)

For a [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) $w$ on a domain, a weighted Poincare inequality has the form

$$
\int|h'|^2w\geq\lambda\int|h-\mathbb E_w h|^2w.
$$

The optimal $\lambda$ is the [spectral gap](spectral-gap.md) of the associated weighted diffusion operator.
