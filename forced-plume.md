# Forced plume

↑ **Parent:** [Turbulent plume](turbulent-plume-split.md)

A forced plume has both imposed [momentum flux](momentum-flux.md) and positive [buoyancy flux](buoyancy-flux.md). Its near-source region behaves like a jet, while its far field approaches a [pure plume](pure-plume.md). The crossover is measured by a [jet length](jet-length.md).
