# Aldohexose

↑ **Parent:** [Monosaccharide](monosaccharide.md)

An [aldohexose](aldohexose.md) is a six-[carbon](carbon.md) [aldose](aldose.md): its open-chain reference form has an [aldehyde](aldehyde.md) group at C1. [Glucose](glucose.md) is an example.[McMurry, Organic Chemistry, §25.1: aldose and hexose classification](organic-chemistry-by-john-mcmurry/25-1-classification-of-carbohydrates.md)

The unmodified open-chain skeleton has four [stereocenters](stereocenter.md), C2–C5, giving $2^4=16$ [stereoisomers](stereoisomer.md), or eight [enantiomer](enantiomer.md) pairs. Fixing the C5 [hydroxy group](hydroxy-group.md) on the right leaves $2^3=8$ D forms; D-[glucose](glucose.md)'s right/left/right/right pattern selects one. An [aldohexose](aldohexose.md) label therefore specifies less than a particular [sugar](sugar.md) name, and a [molecular formula](molecular-formula.md) specifies less still. The existing [glucose](glucose.md) article checks its [chemical atom](atom-physics.md) inventory; [monosaccharide ring-chain interconversion](monosaccharide-ring-chain-interconversion.md) follows those same numbered [carbon](carbon.md) positions into a ring.
