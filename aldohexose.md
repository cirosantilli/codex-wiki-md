# Aldohexose

↑ **Parent:** [Monosaccharide](monosaccharide.md)

An [aldohexose](aldohexose.md) is a six-[carbon](carbon.md) [monosaccharide](monosaccharide.md) with an [aldehyde](aldehyde.md) group in its open-chain form. [Glucose](glucose.md) is an example. See [McMurry: carbohydrate classification](https://openstax.org/books/organic-chemistry/pages/25-1-classification-of-carbohydrates).
