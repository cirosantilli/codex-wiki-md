# Plate velocity triangle

↑ **Parent:** [Triple junction](triple-junction.md)

The three plate [velocities](velocity.md) plotted in a common [reference frame](reference-frame.md) form a triangle whose directed edges are the pairwise relative [velocities](velocity.md). Adding the same reference [velocity](velocity.md) translates the triangle without changing its edges.
