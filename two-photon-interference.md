# Two-photon interference

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum optics](quantum-optics-split.md)

Interference between indistinguishable alternatives involving two [photons](photon.md). The [Hong-Ou-Mandel effect](hong-ou-mandel-effect.md) is an example: for identical [photons](photon.md) arriving at opposite inputs of a [balanced beam splitter](balanced-beam-splitter.md), the two alternatives for one [photon](photon.md) in each output cancel. A [Bell state analyzer](bell-state-analyzer.md) exploits the dependence of the output statistics on the symmetry of the joint [photon polarization](photon-polarization.md) state. Matching arrival time, spectrum and spatial mode prevents those alternatives from being distinguished by unobserved degrees of freedom.
