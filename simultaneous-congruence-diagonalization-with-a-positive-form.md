# Simultaneous congruence diagonalization with a positive form

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix congruence](matrix-congruence.md)

If $A$ is real symmetric positive definite and $B$ is real symmetric, let $S=A^{-1/2}$. The matrix $SBS$ is symmetric and has an orthonormal eigenbasis, represented by $Q$. Taking $P=SQ$ gives the two displayed congruences. The diagonal entries are determined by the [generalized eigenvalue problem](generalized-eigenvalue-problem.md) $\det(B-\lambda A)=0$, since $P^T(B-\lambda A)P$ is diagonal with entries $\lambda_j-\lambda$. Positive definiteness of the first form is essential to this construction.
