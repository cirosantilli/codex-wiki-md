# Arcsine distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous probability distribution](continuous-probability-distribution-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arcsine_distribution)

The standard arcsine law has [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) $1/[\pi\sqrt{x(1-x)}]$ on $(0,1)$. Substituting $x=\sin^2v$ in its integral gives [cumulative distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md) $F(x)=2\arcsin\sqrt{x}/\pi$. Hence [inverse transform sampling](inverse-transform-sampling.md) uses $X=\sin^2(\pi U/2)$ for $U$ with a continuous [uniform distribution](continuous-uniform-distribution.md). The law places most of its density near the two endpoints rather than the middle.
