<h1 id="newton-cartan-gravitational-connection">Newton–Cartan gravitational connection</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Newton–Cartan theory](newton-cartan-theory.md)

In Cartesian spatial coordinates and absolute time $t$, the [gravitational potential](newtonian-potential-of-a-point-mass.md) $\Phi(t,x)$ determines an [affine connection](affine-connection.md) by the displayed coefficients, all others zero. The [geodesic equation](geodesic-equation.md) with affine time is $d^2x^i/dt^2=-\partial_i\Phi$. It preserves $dt$ and the spatial inverse metric $h^{ij}=\delta^{ij}$, reproducing freely falling [Newtonian mechanics](newtonian-mechanics.md) in a curved spacetime connection.
