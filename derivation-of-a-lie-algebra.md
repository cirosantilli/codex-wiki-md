# Derivation of a Lie algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md)

A derivation of a [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) is an [algebraic derivation](derivation-differential-algebra.md), namely a [linear map](linear-map.md) $D:L\to L$ satisfying

$$
D[x,y]=[Dx,y]+[x,Dy].
$$

Every adjoint map $\operatorname{ad}z:x\mapsto[z,x]$ is a derivation.
