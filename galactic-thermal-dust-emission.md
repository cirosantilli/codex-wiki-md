# Galactic thermal dust emission

↑ **Parent:** [Galactic multiwavelength astronomy](galactic-multiwavelength-astronomy.md)

Interstellar grains heated by starlight emit a modified [blackbody](blackbody.md) spectrum, with a frequency-dependent emissivity multiplying the [Planck law](planck-s-law.md). This [Galactic thermal dust emission](galactic-thermal-dust-emission.md) rises across much of the microwave/submillimeter band and contaminates high-frequency [cosmic microwave background](cosmic-microwave-background.md) measurements. Its strength and effective emissivity vary across the sky, so the frequency at which it exceeds cosmological anisotropy is not a universal constant.
