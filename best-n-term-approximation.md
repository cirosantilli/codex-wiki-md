# Best N-term approximation

↑ **Parent:** [N-term approximation](n-term-approximation.md)

The best N-term approximation in an [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) retains $N$ coefficients of greatest absolute value. It minimizes the squared [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) error over all index sets of size at most $N$. The selection makes the approximation nonlinear in the data, even though coefficient extraction is linear.
