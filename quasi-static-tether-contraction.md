# Quasi-static tether contraction

↑ **Parent:** [Conservation of angular momentum](conservation-of-angular-momentum.md)

For two equal [point masses](point-mass.md) $m$ joined by a light tether of length $l$, the central [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) is $ml^2/2$. With conserved [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) $L$, the rotational [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) is $L^2/(ml^2)$. Slow, smooth contraction gives $\dot E_{\rm rot}=-T\dot l$ with the displayed leading [tension](tension.md). The exact radial correction is $E_{\rm rad}=m\dot l^2/4$ and $T=2L^2/(ml^3)-m\ddot l/2$, so the total [work-energy theorem](work-energy-theorem.md) remains exact for non-quasi-static schedules too.
