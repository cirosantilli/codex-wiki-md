# Momentum conservation

↑ **Parent:** [Momentum](momentum.md)

Momentum conservation states that the total momentum of an isolated system is constant. In a translation-invariant field theory, integrating over the spacetime zero mode enforces momentum conservation at interactions and scattering amplitudes.
