# Momentum conservation

↑ **Parent:** [Momentum](momentum.md)

[Momentum conservation](momentum-conservation.md) states that total [momentum](momentum.md) stays constant when the net [force](force.md) exerted from outside the chosen collection is zero. Use a closed collection, meaning the same fixed-[mass](mass.md) [point masses](point-mass.md) are followed throughout, and an [inertial frame](inertial-frame.md). Then

$$
\mathbf P=\sum_i\mathbf p_i,\qquad
\frac{d\mathbf P}{dt}=\sum_i\mathbf F_i^{\mathrm{ext}}.
$$

To see why, sum [Newton's second law](newton-s-second-law.md) over the members. If $\mathbf F_{ij}$ is the [force](force.md) on member $i$ from member $j$, [Newton's third law](newton-s-third-law.md) assumes $\mathbf F_{ij}=-\mathbf F_{ji}$. Each pair cancels in the total:

$$
\frac{d\mathbf P}{dt}
=\sum_i\mathbf F_i^{\mathrm{ext}}
+\sum_{i<j}(\mathbf F_{ij}+\mathbf F_{ji})
=\sum_i\mathbf F_i^{\mathrm{ext}}.
$$

The internal [forces](force.md) act on different bodies and can change their individual [momenta](momentum.md); their contributions cancel only in the collection's sum. An [isolated system](isolated-system.md) has no external interaction, but balanced external [forces](force.md) also suffice for [momentum conservation](momentum-conservation.md). A [closed system](closed-system.md) merely excluding matter exchange need not have conserved [momentum](momentum.md). [OpenStax University Physics Volume 1, section 9.3: Conservation of Linear Momentum](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/9-3-conservation-of-linear-momentum)

The usefulness is that the detailed internal [forces](force.md) during a [collision](collision.md) need not be known. Both an [elastic collision](elastic-collision.md) and a [perfectly inelastic collision](perfectly-inelastic-collision.md) obey the same isolated [momentum](momentum.md) balance; [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) conservation is an additional condition for the [elastic collision](elastic-collision.md). If the external [impulse](impulse.md) is small but nonzero, the total [momentum](momentum.md) change is correspondingly small. For a [variable-mass system](variable-mass-system.md) exchanging matter, the entering or leaving [momentum](momentum.md) must also be included.

In a translation-invariant field theory, integrating over the spacetime zero mode enforces momentum conservation at interactions and scattering amplitudes.
