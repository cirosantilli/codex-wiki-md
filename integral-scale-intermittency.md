# Integral-scale intermittency

↑ **Parent:** [Internal intermittency](internal-intermittency.md)

[Integral-scale intermittency](integral-scale-intermittency.md) is modulation of the energy-containing motions and the energy supply on large lengths or long turnover times. Averaging velocity statistics over regions or realizations with differing [viscous dissipation](viscous-dissipation.md) rates can invalidate universal coefficients based only on the global mean [viscous dissipation](viscous-dissipation.md).
