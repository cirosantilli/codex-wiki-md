# Radial unit vector in spherical coordinates

↑ **Parent:** [Spherical coordinate system](spherical-coordinate-system.md)

At fixed angular coordinates, differentiating the [Cartesian coordinates](cartesian-coordinate-system.md) with respect to radial distance gives this [unit vector](unit-vector.md). It is the local direction of increasing radius in the [spherical coordinate system](spherical-coordinate-system.md).
