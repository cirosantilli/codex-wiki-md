# Diffusion arrest of Gaussian packet compression

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian scalar packet in a linear incompressible flow](gaussian-scalar-packet-in-a-linear-incompressible-flow.md)

For a chaotic incompressible planar flow with [Lyapunov exponent](lyapunov-exponent.md) pair $\lambda,-\lambda$, an initially broad [Gaussian scalar packet in a linear incompressible flow](gaussian-scalar-packet-in-a-linear-incompressible-flow.md) stretches and compresses exponentially while diffusion is negligible. Its compressed variance stops decreasing around $\kappa/\lambda$, the [Batchelor scalar microscale](batchelor-scalar-microscale.md) balance, while the larger variance continues to grow. In a constant principal strain, the normalized covariance eigenvalues obey $\dot j_\pm=\pm2\lambda j_\pm+2\kappa$, proving that the compressed one approaches $\kappa/\lambda$. Random finite-correlation strain instead gives a fluctuating compressed width; stationarity and integrability assumptions are required to turn this into exponential statistics of the packet amplitude.
