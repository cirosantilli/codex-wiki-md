# Coefficient ring of complex cobordism

↑ **Parent:** [Complex cobordism](complex-cobordism.md)

The Milnor-Novikov coefficient theorem identifies the coefficient ring of [complex cobordism](complex-cobordism.md) as the displayed graded polynomial ring. In cohomological grading it has no positive-degree or odd-degree coefficients. Homological grading reverses the signs: $MU_*$ has polynomial generators in degrees $2i$.
