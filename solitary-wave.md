# Solitary wave

↑ **Parent:** [Travelling wave](travelling-wave.md)

A solitary wave is a spatially localized [travelling wave](travelling-wave.md) that retains its profile as it propagates. The displayed form uses a zero background; other constant backgrounds are possible. A [soliton](soliton.md) additionally has the characteristic elastic interaction property with other solitons. The existence of a single localized travelling profile does not alone establish that stronger property.
