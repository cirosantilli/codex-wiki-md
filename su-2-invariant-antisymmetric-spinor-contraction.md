<h1 id="su-2-invariant-antisymmetric-spinor-contraction">SU(2)-invariant antisymmetric spinor contraction</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [SU(2) group](su-2-group.md)

For $\varepsilon=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\-1&0\end{pmatrix}$, every two-by-two matrix satisfies $U^T\varepsilon U=(\det U)\varepsilon$. Thus $\phi^T\varepsilon\chi$ is exactly invariant under a common [SU(2) group](su-2-group.md) transformation. This is a bilinear contraction without [complex conjugation](complex-conjugation.md). It vanishes for equal commuting [spinors](spinor.md) and is the alternating tensor defining the [spin singlet state](spin-singlet-state.md). Overall bosonic exchange symmetry must still be enforced if the [spinors](spinor.md) label identical particles.
