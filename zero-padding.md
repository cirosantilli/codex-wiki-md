# Zero padding

↑ **Parent:** [Discrete convolution](discrete-convolution.md)

Zero padding extends a finite [sequence](sequence.md) by zero entries to a specified length. For input lengths $r,s$, their full [discrete convolution](discrete-convolution.md) has length at most $r+s-1$. Padding both inputs to $N\geq r+s-1$ allows [circular convolution](circular-convolution.md) to represent this full result without folded entries.
