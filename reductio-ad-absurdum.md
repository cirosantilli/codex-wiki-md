# Reductio ad absurdum

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical proof](mathematical-proof.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reductio_ad_absurdum)

A reductio rejects an assumption by deriving an absurd or contradictory consequence from it. In a classical [mathematical proof](mathematical-proof.md) of $P$, assume its negation and derive a [contradiction](contradiction.md). Constructively the same argument yields the double negation of $P$; removing it requires an additional logical principle. This differs from proving a negation directly by deriving a contradiction from the positive assumption.
