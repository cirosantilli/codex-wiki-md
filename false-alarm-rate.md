# False alarm rate

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical hypothesis test](statistical-hypothesis-test-split.md)

The expected rate of detections exceeding a stated threshold under a specified background-only model. A time rate and a per-trial [significance level](significance-level.md) require different normalizations.
