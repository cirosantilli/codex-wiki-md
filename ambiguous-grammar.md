# Ambiguous grammar

↑ **Parent:** [Context-free grammar](context-free-grammar.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ambiguous_grammar)

A [context-free grammar](context-free-grammar.md) is ambiguous when some accepted token sequence has two distinct [parse trees](parse-tree.md). Giving a language an unambiguous [context-free grammar](context-free-grammar.md) requires specifying grouping in the productions themselves, rather than relying on an unstated parser conflict rule.
