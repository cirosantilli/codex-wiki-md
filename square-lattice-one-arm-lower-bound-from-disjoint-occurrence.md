# Square-lattice one-arm lower bound from disjoint occurrence

↑ **Parent:** [Van den Berg-Kesten inequality](van-den-berg-kesten-inequality.md)

At density $1/2$, planar duality gives crossing probability $1/2$ for the $2n$-by-$(2n-1)$ lattice rectangle. A self-avoiding left-right crossing passes through one of its $2n$ middle-column vertices. At that vertex its two halves are disjoint edge witnesses for two copies of the radius-$n$ connection event. The [BK inequality](van-den-berg-kesten-inequality.md) and the [union bound](boole-s-inequality.md) give $1/2\leq2n\mathbb P_{1/2}(0\leftrightarrow\partial[-n,n]^2)^2$, proving the estimate.
