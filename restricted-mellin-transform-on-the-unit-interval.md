# Restricted Mellin transform on the unit interval

↑ **Parent:** [Mellin transform](mellin-transform.md)

This is the [Mellin transform](mellin-transform.md) of the function extended by zero beyond the unit interval. The [change of variables](change-of-variables-formula.md) $\lambda=e^{-t}$ identifies it with the unilateral [Laplace transform](laplace-transform.md) of $h(e^{-t})$. The [Bromwich inversion formula](bromwich-inversion-formula.md) therefore gives $h(\lambda)=(2\pi i)^{-1}\int_{\gamma-i\infty}^{\gamma+i\infty}\lambda^{-s}H(s)\,ds$ for $0<\lambda<1$, with $\gamma$ in the convergence half-plane and suitable inversion hypotheses. At a jump, symmetric inversion returns the mean of the two limiting values. In particular, for $0<c<1$, the cutoff $h(\lambda)=\lambda^q\mathbf1_{(0,c)}(\lambda)$ has transform $c^{s+q}/(s+q)$ when $\operatorname{Re}(s+q)>0$. Multiplication by $c^s$ shifts the support threshold in logarithmic coordinates, the [Laplace transform time-shift rule](laplace-transform-time-shift-rule.md) expressed multiplicatively.
