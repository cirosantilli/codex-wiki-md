# Regulator of G protein signalling

↑ **Parent:** [GTPase-activating protein](gtpase-activating-protein.md)

A [protein](protein-split.md) that accelerates [GTP](guanosine-triphosphate.md) [hydrolysis](hydrolysis.md) by an appropriate [G protein alpha subunit](g-protein-alpha-subunit.md), shortening its activated lifetime. Family and tissue specificity matter for therapeutic manipulation.
