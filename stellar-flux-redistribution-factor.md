# Stellar flux redistribution factor

↑ **Parent:** [Day-night heat redistribution](day-night-heat-redistribution.md)

This factor converts substellar incident flux into the representative flux used in an atmospheric model. Uniform global averaging gives $f_r=1/4$; dayside averaging gives $1/2$ if albedo is treated separately. The convention must be stated when defining an [irradiation temperature](irradiation-temperature.md).
