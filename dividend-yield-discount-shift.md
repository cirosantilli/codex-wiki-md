# Dividend-yield discount shift

↑ **Parent:** [Black-Scholes equation with continuous stock dividends](black-scholes-equation-with-continuous-stock-dividends.md)

With constant continuous stock-dividend yield $d$, the [risk-neutral measure](risk-neutral-measure.md) gives stock drift $r-d$ but cash claims are discounted at $r$. The same terminal payoff in a no-dividend [Black-Scholes model](black-scholes-model.md) with interest rate $r-d$ has the identical stock-transition law but a different discount factor. Their prices therefore obey the displayed identity, under the usual payoff [integrability](integrability.md) conditions.
