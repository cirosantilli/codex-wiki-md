# RNA primer

↑ **Parent:** [DNA replication](dna-replication.md)

A short [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) segment providing a paired 3-prime end from which [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) synthesis can be extended. It is subsequently removed or processed during strand maturation.
