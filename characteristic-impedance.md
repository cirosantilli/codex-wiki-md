# Characteristic impedance

↑ **Parent:** [Transmission line](transmission-line.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Characteristic_impedance)

The [characteristic impedance](characteristic-impedance.md) is the voltage-to-current ratio of a single forward travelling wave on a uniform [transmission line](transmission-line.md). For real $Z_0$ and a real load resistance $R_L$, the voltage-wave [reflection coefficient](reflection-coefficient.md) is $(R_L-Z_0)/(R_L+Z_0)$ and the power accepted by the load is the incident power times $4R_LZ_0/(R_L+Z_0)^2$.
