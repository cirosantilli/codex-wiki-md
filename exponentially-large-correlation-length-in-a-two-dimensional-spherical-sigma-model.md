# Exponentially large correlation length in a two-dimensional spherical sigma model

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal eigenvalues of the spherical nonlinear sigma model](thermal-eigenvalues-of-the-spherical-nonlinear-sigma-model.md)

At $D=2$ and $n>2$, the weak-coupling [renormalization-group flow](renormalization-group-flow.md) obeys $dg/d\ell=(n-2)g^2/(2\pi)$. Integrating gives $1/g(\ell)=1/g_0-(n-2)\ell/(2\pi)$. The flow reaches order-one coupling at a length whose logarithm is $2\pi/[(n-2)g_0]+O(1)$ at this order. Restoring the microscopic scale $\Lambda^{-1}$ gives the displayed leading exponential [correlation length](correlation-length.md). Higher loops affect its prefactor, and this conclusion does not apply to the compact $n=2$ model with its [Berezinskii–Kosterlitz–Thouless transition](berezinskii-kosterlitz-thouless-transition.md).
