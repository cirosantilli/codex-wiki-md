# Plasticity-led evolution

↑ **Parent:** [Genetic accommodation](genetic-accommodation.md)

A sequence in which an environmental change induces a [phenotype](phenotype.md) through [phenotypic plasticity](phenotypic-plasticity.md), followed by inherited refinement through [natural selection](natural-selection.md). Evidence requires separating initial induction from subsequent inherited change.
