# Stern-Gerlach experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Spin](spin.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stern–Gerlach_experiment)

A [Stern-Gerlach experiment](stern-gerlach-experiment.md) separates atomic beams according to a component of their [magnetic moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md) in a nonuniform [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md). For an appropriate two-state [spin](spin.md) system, calibrated outputs distinguish the two [spin](spin.md) [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md); [Stern-Gerlach expectation estimation](stern-gerlach-expectation-estimation.md) explains how repeated fresh preparations turn channel counts into an [expectation value](expectation-value.md).

The decisive contrast is between two separated components of an [atomic beam](atomic-beam.md) and the continuous spread predicted for randomly oriented classical [magnetic moments](magnetic-dipole-moment.md). Neither component need be an infinitely narrow spot: [velocity](velocity.md) spread and the resolving power of the [scientific instrument](scientific-instrument.md) affect the spatial image. The apparatus converts an internal [magnetic moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md) into a displacement large enough to inspect, connecting [angular momentum eigenstates](angular-momentum-eigenstate.md) to macroscopic evidence.
