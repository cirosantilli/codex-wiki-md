# Stern-Gerlach experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Spin](spin.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stern–Gerlach_experiment)

A [Stern-Gerlach experiment](stern-gerlach-experiment.md) separates atomic beams according to a component of their [magnetic moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md) in a nonuniform [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md). For an appropriate two-state [spin](spin.md) system, calibrated outputs distinguish the two [spin](spin.md) [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md); [Stern-Gerlach expectation estimation](stern-gerlach-expectation-estimation.md) explains how repeated fresh preparations turn channel counts into an [expectation value](expectation-value.md).
