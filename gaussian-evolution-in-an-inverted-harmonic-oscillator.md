# Gaussian evolution in an inverted harmonic oscillator

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian wave packet](gaussian-wave-packet.md)

For the [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) $H=(p^2-x^2)/2$, a [Gaussian wave packet](gaussian-wave-packet.md) $Ae^{-Bx^2}$ satisfies $\dot A=-i\hbar AB$ and $\dot B=-i/(2\hbar)-2i\hbar B^2$. The displayed solution is normalizable for real $\phi$ exactly when $\sin(2\phi)>0$; its [second moments of a complex Gaussian wave packet](second-moments-of-a-complex-gaussian-wave-packet.md) are

$$
\langle x^2\rangle=\frac{\hbar[\cosh(2t)+\cos(2\phi)]}{2\sin(2\phi)},\qquad
\langle p^2\rangle=\frac{\hbar[\cosh(2t)-\cos(2\phi)]}{2\sin(2\phi)}.
$$

Both grow as $\hbar e^{2t}/[4\sin(2\phi)]$, while their difference and hence the mean [energy](energy.md) remain constant.
