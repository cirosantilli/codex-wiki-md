# Ice-sheet flotation

↑ **Parent:** [Grounding line](grounding-line.md)

At a [grounding line](grounding-line.md), [hydrostatic pressures](hydrostatic-pressure.md) balance when the weight of an [ice](ice.md) column of thickness $H$ and [mass density](density.md) $\rho$ equals the weight of its displaced [water](water.md), of depth $b$ and [mass density](density.md) $\rho_w$. Thus flotation gives $\rho H=\rho_w b$. Its time [derivative](derivative.md) along a moving [grounding line](grounding-line.md) relates local thickness changes to grounding-line speed; it supplies a different boundary condition from the [unbuttressed Newtonian grounding-line stress](unbuttressed-newtonian-grounding-line-stress.md).
