# Magnetic gradient drift

↑ **Parent:** [Guiding-centre drift](guiding-centre-drift.md)

A slowly varying field magnitude exerts the gyroaveraged force $-\mu\nabla B$ on a particle with magnetic moment $\mu=mv_\perp^2/(2B)$. Applying the perpendicular-force [guiding-centre drift](guiding-centre-drift.md) formula gives the displayed velocity. Its direction depends on the sign of the particle charge.
