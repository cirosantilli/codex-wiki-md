# Minimum-variance importance distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Importance sampling](importance-sampling.md)

For $\mu=\int hf$ and $0<A=\int|h|f<\infty$, the proposal $q_*=|h|f/A$ minimizes the [variance](variance-split.md) of the ordinary unbiased [importance sampling](importance-sampling.md) estimator. The [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) gives $\int h^2f^2/q\geq A^2$, so the minimum [variance](variance-split.md) for $n$ independent samples is $(A^2-\mu^2)/n$. The proposal need only cover the nonzero integrand.
