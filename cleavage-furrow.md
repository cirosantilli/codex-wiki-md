# Cleavage furrow

↑ **Parent:** [Cytokinesis](cytokinesis.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cleavage_furrow)

A [cleavage furrow](cleavage-furrow.md) is the inward indentation of the [cell membrane](cell-membrane-split.md) during [cytokinesis](cytokinesis.md). It is the visible deformation; the [actomyosin ring](actomyosin-ring.md) is machinery beneath that boundary.[Biology 2e by Mary Ann Clark and others; §10.2, Cytokinesis, animal-cell paragraph and Figure 10.9](biology-2e-by-mary-ann-clark-and-others/the-cell-cycle.md)
