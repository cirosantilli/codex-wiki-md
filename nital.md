# Nital

↑ **Parent:** [Steel microstructure](steel-microstructure.md)

A nitric-acid/alcohol metallographic etchant. In pearlitic steel it can preferentially remove [ferrite](ferrite.md), leaving [cementite](cementite.md) standing higher. In an [atomic force microscopy](atomic-force-microscopy.md) height image, darker then means lower surface height, not an optical reflectivity or atomic-number contrast.
