# Massless p-form gauge field

↑ **Parent:** [Abelian p-form gauge field](abelian-p-form-gauge-field.md)

A massless Abelian $p$-form in $D$-dimensional flat spacetime transforms on shell as an antisymmetric rank-$p$ tensor of the rotational [little group](little-group.md) $SO(D-2)$. Its physical state count is $\binom{D-2}{p}$. A compatible real self-duality constraint halves this count. A four-dimensional three-form has no local propagating polarization.
