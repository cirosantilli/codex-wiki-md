# Log-sum inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Kullback-Leibler divergence](kullback-leibler-divergence.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Log-sum_inequality)

For nonnegative $a_i,b_i$, with $a=\sum_i a_i$ and $b=\sum_i b_i$, the log-sum inequality is

$$
\sum_i a_i\log\frac{a_i}{b_i}\geq a\log\frac ab.
$$

It proves both [Gibbs inequality](gibbs-inequality.md) and the [data processing inequality for relative entropy](data-processing-inequality-for-relative-entropy.md).
