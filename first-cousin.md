# First cousin

↑ **Parent:** [Pedigree](pedigree.md)

First cousins are children of [full siblings](full-sibling.md). An otherwise outbred first-cousin pair shares two noninbred grandparents and has [kinship coefficient](kinship-coefficient.md) $1/16$.
