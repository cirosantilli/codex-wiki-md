# Force on a resistive coil beside an alternating wire

↑ **Parent:** [Mutual inductance of a straight wire and square coil](mutual-inductance-of-a-straight-wire-and-square-coil.md)

In a fixed square coil beside a long wire, neglect [self-inductance](self-inductance.md) compared with resistance. A changing wire current induces coil current proportional to $dI/dt$. [Forces](force-split.md) on the two perpendicular sides cancel, and the unequal parallel-side fields give magnitude $\mu_0Na|Ii|[1/s-1/(s+a)]/(2\pi)$. When $I=I_0\sin\omega t$, the signed [force](force-split.md) alternates at twice the drive [frequency](frequency.md) and has zero cycle mean, while Joule [dissipation](dissipation.md) is positive with mean $\mathcal M^2\omega^2I_0^2/(2R)$. A fixed coil has no mechanical work despite its time-dependent [force](force-split.md).
