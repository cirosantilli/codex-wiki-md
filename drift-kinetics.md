# Drift kinetics

↑ **Parent:** [Kinetic theory](kinetic-theory-split.md)

The slow dynamics of charged-particle [guiding centers](guiding-center.md) after averaging over rapid [cyclotron motion](gyromotion.md), assuming [gyroradii](gyroradius.md) much smaller than resolved perpendicular length scales. Depending on the ordering, the retained transverse motion may include just the common electric drift or also [magnetic curvature drift](magnetic-curvature-drift.md), [gradient-B drift](gradient-b-drift.md) and [polarization drift](polarization-drift.md). These choices give distinct limits, not interchangeable closures.
