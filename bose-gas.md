# Bose gas

↑ **Parent:** [Bose-Einstein statistics](bose-einstein-statistics.md)

A gas whose indistinguishable constituents are [bosons](boson.md). The ideal model follows [Bose-Einstein statistics](bose-einstein-statistics.md), while interactions modify its excitations and thermodynamics. Macroscopic ground-state occupation can occur under suitable dimension and density conditions, but it does not alone determine a superfluid response.
