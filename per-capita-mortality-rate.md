# Per-capita mortality rate

↑ **Parent:** [Population dynamics](population-dynamics.md)

A [per-capita mortality rate](per-capita-mortality-rate.md) $a$ means that the death [flux](flux.md) from a [biological population](population-biology.md) of size $I$ is $aI$. With constant rate and no other transfers, $\dot I=-aI$ gives [exponential](exponential-function.md) survival $I(t)=I(0)e^{-at}$. In an epidemic model this can specify mortality from the [infective](infectious-disease.md) [compartment](compartmental-models-epidemiology.md) rather than mortality of the entire [population](population-biology.md).
