# Spherical density projection

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar dynamics](stellar-dynamics.md)

A spherical [mass density](density.md) $\rho$ gives projected [surface density](surface-density-of-a-disk.md) $\Sigma(R)=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\rho(\sqrt{R^2+z^2})\,dz$. On the positive half of the [line of sight](line-of-sight.md), substitute $r=\sqrt{R^2+z^2}$ to obtain the displayed [Abel transform](abel-transform.md). Spherical symmetry is essential: a general three-dimensional density cannot be reconstructed uniquely from one projection.
