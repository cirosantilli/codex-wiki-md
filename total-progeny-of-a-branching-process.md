# Total progeny of a branching process

↑ **Parent:** [Galton-Watson process](galton-watson-process-split.md)

The total progeny counts all individuals, including the initial ancestor, in a [Galton-Watson process](galton-watson-process-split.md). It is infinite on survival. If the offspring mean is $m<1$, the [expected generation size in a Galton-Watson process](expected-generation-size-in-a-galton-watson-process.md) and the [monotone convergence theorem](monotone-convergence-theorem.md) give $\mathbb ET=1/(1-m)$.
