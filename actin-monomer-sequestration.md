# Actin monomer sequestration

↑ **Parent:** [Actin monomer](actin-monomer.md)

Binding that reduces the pool of [actin monomers](actin-monomer.md) available for productive [actin filament](actin-filament.md) assembly. Existing [actin filaments](actin-filament.md) can then disappear through ordinary turnover; their loss does not establish direct severing.
