# Pressure projection in a displaced stratified blob

↑ **Parent:** [Internal wave](internal-wave.md)

Incompressibility makes pressure adjust throughout the surrounding fluid. Projecting the linear [Boussinesq equations](boussinesq-equations.md) perpendicular to a Fourier wavevector gives $w_t=[k_h^2/(k_h^2+m^2)]b$ and $b_t=-N^2w$. A localized spherical disturbance contains many wavevector directions and therefore many [internal gravity wave](internal-wave.md) frequencies, rather than one oscillator at $N$. Its shape evolves and energy radiates into the surroundings; a rigid-sphere [added mass](added-mass.md) analogy illustrates extra inertia but does not give an exact coherent frequency for a free material blob.
