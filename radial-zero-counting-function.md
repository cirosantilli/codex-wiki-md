# Radial zero-counting function

↑ **Parent:** [Jensen's formula](jensen-s-formula.md)

For a nonzero [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function-split.md), $n_f(r)$ counts its [zeros of a function](zero-of-a-function.md) in the centred [open disc](open-disc.md) of radius $r$, with [multiplicity of a zero](multiplicity-of-a-zero.md). Thus a double zero counts twice, even though it is a single point. On every smaller closed [disk](disk-mathematics.md) in the domain the count is finite. The strict inequality $|a|<r$ specifies the convention at a zero modulus; elsewhere it agrees with counting in the closed [disk](disk-mathematics.md). In the [polynomial circle log-mean](polynomial-circle-log-mean.md) example the count is zero, one and two on the three displayed intervals.
