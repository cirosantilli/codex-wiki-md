# Rindler wedge

↑ **Parent:** [Minkowski spacetime](minkowski-spacetime.md)

The right [Rindler wedge](rindler-wedge.md) is the open region $X>|T|$ of Minkowski spacetime. Coordinates $T=\rho\sinh\tau$, $X=\rho\cosh\tau$, $\rho>0$, give metric $-\rho^2d\tau^2+d\rho^2+dY^2+dZ^2$. The wedge extends into Minkowski spacetime and is geodesically incomplete, while its constant-$\tau$ slices are Cauchy surfaces for the wedge itself.
