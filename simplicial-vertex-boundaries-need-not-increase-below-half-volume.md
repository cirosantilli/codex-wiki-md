# Simplicial vertex boundaries need not increase below half volume

↑ **Parent:** [Simplicial order on the discrete cube](simplicial-order-on-the-discrete-cube.md)

In dimension three, the first three vertices are $\varnothing,\{1\},\{2\}$. Their closed [vertex neighbourhood](vertex-neighbourhood.md) has seven vertices. Adding $\{3\}$ does not change that [vertex neighbourhood](vertex-neighbourhood.md), so the external [vertex boundary](external-vertex-boundary.md) decreases from four to three even though both family sizes are at most half the cube.
