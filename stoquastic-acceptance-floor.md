# Stoquastic acceptance floor

↑ **Parent:** [Stoquastic circuit](stoquastic-circuit.md)

If the output of a [stoquastic circuit](stoquastic-circuit.md) is $|0\rangle|r_0\rangle+|1\rangle|r_1\rangle$ with nonnegative amplitudes, its plus-outcome probability is $1/2+\operatorname{Re}\langle r_0|r_1\rangle\geq1/2$. Basis [quantum witnesses](quantum-witness.md) with zero and plus [ancilla qubits](ancilla-qubit.md) always have this property. A soundness threshold below one half cannot hold for every such input.
