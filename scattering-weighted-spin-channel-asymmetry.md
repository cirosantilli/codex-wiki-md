# Scattering-weighted spin-channel asymmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Mott two-current model](mott-two-current-model.md)

A channel's [electrical conductivity](electrical-conductivity.md) weights its [density of states](density-of-states.md) by squared velocity and the transport [momentum relaxation time](momentum-relaxation-time.md). In an elementary spin-conserving $s$–$d$ scattering model, $\tau_s^{-1}\propto|V_{sd,s}|^2D_{d,s}(E_F)$, so available heavy final states can reduce the current carried by light states. Band filling, defects and interfaces affect the asymmetry; total spin population alone does not determine its sign. A density-of-states sketch without scattering matrix elements is not a universal transport prediction.
