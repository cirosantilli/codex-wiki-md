# Integrated tail distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Probability distribution](probability-distribution.md)

For a positive random variable $X$ with finite nonzero [expected value](expected-value.md) $\mu$, its integrated tail law has density $f_I(x)=\mathbb P(X>x)/\mu$ on $x\geq0$. The [tail integral formula for moments](tail-integral-formula-for-moments.md) normalizes it. For $r>0$ its exponential transform is $\int e^{rx}f_I(x)\,dx=[M_X(r)-1]/(\mu r)$, with extended-value interpretation when needed.
