# Pivot-driven two-mass spring antiresonance

↑ **Parent:** [Coupled oscillator](coupled-oscillator.md)

Two identical vertical springs connect a driven pivot to two equal masses in sequence. After subtracting the static gravitational equilibrium, the laboratory displacement equation is $m\ddot{\mathbf x}+k\begin{pmatrix}2&-1\\-1&1\end{pmatrix}\mathbf x=k(z,0)^{\mathsf T}$. For $z=z_0\cos\omega_0t$ and $\omega_0^2=k/m$, a particular solution is $\mathbf x=(0,-z_0\cos\omega_0t)^{\mathsf T}$. Thus the upper mass has zero driven response even though this frequency is not an eigenfrequency. The general solution also contains both free [normal modes](normal-mode.md).
