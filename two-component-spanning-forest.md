# Two-component spanning forest

↑ **Parent:** [Forest](forest.md)

A [two-component spanning forest](two-component-spanning-forest.md) of a finite connected [graph](graph-split.md) is an acyclic spanning subgraph with exactly two [connected components of a graph](component-graph-theory.md). It has $|V|-2$ [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md). For distinct terminals $a,z$, a separating two-component forest places them in different components. Deleting an [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) of the $a$-to-$z$ [graph path](path-in-a-graph.md) of a [spanning tree](spanning-tree.md) produces such a forest; adjoining any original [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) across its cut reverses this operation. This is the counting correspondence behind the [mean spanning-tree path current](mean-spanning-tree-path-current.md).
