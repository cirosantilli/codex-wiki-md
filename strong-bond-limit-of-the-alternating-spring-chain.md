# Strong-bond limit of the alternating-spring chain

↑ **Parent:** [Alternating-spring chain dispersion relation](alternating-spring-chain-dispersion-relation.md)

Take equal masses $m$, atomic spacing $a$, and alternating springs $K\gg\kappa>0$. The [primitive unit cell](primitive-unit-cell.md) contains a strong-bonded pair, so its length is $2a$ and the [Brillouin zone](brillouin-zone.md) ends at $\pm\pi/(2a)$. Expanding the exact [alternating-spring chain dispersion relation](alternating-spring-chain-dispersion-relation.md) gives

$$
\omega_-^2(q)\simeq\frac{2\kappa}{m}\sin^2(qa),\qquad
\omega_+^2(q)\simeq\frac{2K}{m}+\frac{2\kappa}{m}\cos^2(qa).
$$

The low-frequency [acoustic phonon branch](acoustic-phonon-branch.md) moves almost rigid dimers of mass $2m$ coupled by weak springs; its [speed of sound](speed-of-sound.md) tends to $a\sqrt{2\kappa/m}$. The high-frequency [optical phonon branch](optical-phonon-branch.md) describes internal dimer stretch. At the zone edge the exact frequencies are $\sqrt{2\kappa/m}$ and $\sqrt{2K/m}$.
