# Communality

↑ **Parent:** [Orthogonal factor model](orthogonal-factor-model.md)

The communality is the variance of the shared-factor contribution to variable $i$. In an [orthogonal factor model](orthogonal-factor-model.md), $\operatorname{Var}(X_i)=h_i^2+\psi_i$, where $\psi_i$ is its specific variance. For standardized variables, $h_i^2\leq1$. It measures variance shared through the modeled factors, not variance in a chosen [principal component](principal-component.md).
