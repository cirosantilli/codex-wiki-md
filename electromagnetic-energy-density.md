# Electromagnetic energy density

↑ **Parent:** [Electromagnetic energy](electromagnetic-energy.md)

In a linear isotropic nondispersive medium with time-independent [permittivity](permittivity-split.md) $\epsilon$ and [magnetic permeability](permeability-electromagnetism.md) $\mu$, the stored [electromagnetic energy](electromagnetic-energy.md) per volume is $u=\epsilon|\mathbf E|^2/2+|\mathbf B|^2/(2\mu)$. For a short derivation, the [Maxwell equations in matter](maxwell-equations-in-matter.md) and the [divergence](divergence.md) identity for a [cross product](cross-product.md) give $\nabla\cdot(\mathbf E\times\mathbf H)=-\mathbf H\cdot\partial_t\mathbf B-\mathbf E\cdot\partial_t\mathbf D-\mathbf J_f\cdot\mathbf E$. The [constitutive relations](constitutive-relations.md) turn the first two terms into $-\partial_tu$, proving the [Poynting theorem](poynting-theorem.md). Choose $u=0$ when the [electric field](electric-field-split.md) and [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) both vanish. Dispersive or time-dependent material response requires a different accounting of stored [energy](energy-split.md).
