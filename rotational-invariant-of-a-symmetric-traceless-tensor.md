# Rotational invariant of a symmetric traceless tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Nematic order parameter](nematic-order-parameter.md)

Under rotation by a [rotation matrix](rotation-matrix.md), a [symmetric second-rank tensor](symmetric-second-rank-tensor.md) transforms by conjugation, so its traces of powers are invariant. For a three-dimensional [traceless second-rank tensor](traceless-second-rank-tensor.md), the [Cayley-Hamilton theorem](cayley-hamilton-theorem.md) implies $\operatorname{Tr}Q^4=(\operatorname{Tr}Q^2)^2/2$. In two dimensions its [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are $s,-s$, so $\operatorname{Tr}Q^3=0$. These identities organize the [Landau-de Gennes free energy](landau-de-gennes-free-energy.md).
