# Transformer model

↑ **Parent:** [Neural network](neural-network.md)

A sequence model built from stacked [transformer blocks](transformer-block.md), using [self-attention](self-attention.md) to mix information between positions and positionwise [feedforward neural networks](feedforward-neural-network.md) to transform their representations. [Positional encoding](positional-encoding.md) supplies sequence-order information. An autoregressive decoder uses [causal self-attention](causal-self-attention.md); generation remains sequential even when training computations over known sequence positions are parallel.
