# Case reproduction number

↑ **Parent:** [Time-varying reproduction number](time-varying-reproduction-number.md)

The [case reproduction number](case-reproduction-number.md) is the expected number of secondary [infections](infection.md) actually generated by a person infected at time $t$, allowing transmission conditions to change during that person's infectious lifetime. It follows an infection cohort through the subsequent conditions it experiences. The [instantaneous reproduction number](instantaneous-reproduction-number.md) instead freezes conditions at the reference time. Cori and colleagues distinguish these two constructions in their framework for [time-varying reproduction numbers](time-varying-reproduction-number.md). [Cori et al., A new framework and software to estimate time-varying reproduction numbers during epidemics, 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24043437/).
