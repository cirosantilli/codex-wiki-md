# Torus knots are prime

↑ **Parent:** [Torus knot](torus-knot.md)

For coprime p,q with absolute values at least two, the [knot group](knot-group.md) is $\langle a,b\mid a^p=b^q\rangle$. Its nontrivial central element $z=a^p=b^q$ maps nontrivially to the abelianization. A meridian $a^ub^v$, where $qu+pv=1$, has infinite order in the quotient $C_{|p|}*C_{|q|}$. A composite [knot](knot.md) [group](group-split.md) would be an [amalgamated free product](amalgamated-free-product.md) of proper factors over its meridian subgroup, whose center lies in that subgroup. Then z would be a meridian power, contradicting the infinite order of the meridian modulo z.
