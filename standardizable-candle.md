# Standardizable candle

↑ **Parent:** [Standard candle](standard-candle.md)

A [standardizable candle](standardizable-candle.md) has a predictable [absolute magnitude](absolute-magnitude.md) after correcting measured source properties, rather than identical intrinsic brightness for every object. [Type Ia supernova cosmology](type-ia-supernova-cosmology.md) uses [light curve](light-curve.md) shape and colour for this purpose.
