# Binary mass ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Binary star](binary-star.md)

A binary [binary mass ratio](binary-mass-ratio.md) is the quotient of its component masses. For mass-transfer stability, specify the donor-to-accretor convention $q=M_d/M_a$ explicitly, since the inverse convention also occurs. Mass-ratio reversal changes which component is more massive.
