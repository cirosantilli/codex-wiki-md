# Claim count

↑ **Parent:** [Aggregate claims model](aggregate-claims-model.md)

The number of insurance claims in a specified exposure period. Its [probability generating function](probability-generating-function.md) combines with the [moment-generating function](moment-generating-function.md) of an independent [claim size](claim-size.md) through the [random-sum transform identity](random-sum-transform-identity.md). A [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md), [geometric distribution](geometric-distribution.md), or [negative binomial distribution](negative-binomial-distribution.md) provides a possible count model, with different dispersion and zero-claim [probabilities](probability.md).
