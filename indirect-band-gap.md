# Indirect band gap

↑ **Parent:** [Band gap](band-gap.md)

An [indirect band gap](indirect-band-gap.md) separates a [valence band](valence-band.md) maximum and a [conduction band](conduction-band.md) minimum at different values of [crystal momentum](crystal-momentum.md). A near-edge radiative transition then usually needs a [phonon](phonon.md) or another momentum-transfer process in addition to the [photon](photon.md), whose momentum is small on the [Brillouin zone](brillouin-zone.md) scale. A [direct band gap](direct-band-gap.md) has the extrema at the same [crystal momentum](crystal-momentum.md) and more readily supports efficient band-edge light emission. A material can have direct transitions at higher energy while its smallest gap is indirect.
