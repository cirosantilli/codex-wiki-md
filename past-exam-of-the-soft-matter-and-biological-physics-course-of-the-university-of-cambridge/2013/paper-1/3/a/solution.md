<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In [gel sieving of synthetic polymers](../../../../../gel-sieving-of-synthetic-polymers.md), [gel electrophoresis](../../../../../gel-electrophoresis.md) combines drift under an [electric field](../../../../../electric-field-split.md) with size- and conformation-dependent obstruction by a porous matrix. In weak-field free solution, [force](../../../../../force-split.md) balance gives $v=\mu E$ and, in a simple effective-charge/effective-friction model, $\mu=q_{\rm eff}/\zeta$. The [mobility](../../../../../order-parameter-mobility.md) is motion relative to the medium, so fluid motion from [electro-osmosis](../../../../../electro-osmosis.md) must be accounted for separately. This is the operational [mobility](../../../../../order-parameter-mobility.md) convention, not a claim that the bare structural charge is always the effective driving charge.[entry E02024, electrophoretic mobility: migration speed divided by electric field strength](../../../../../iupac-compendium-of-chemical-terminology-fifth-edition-gold-book-by-international-union-of-pure-and-applied-chemistry.md)

For a chain with [free-draining polymer friction](../../../../../free-draining-polymer-friction.md) with approximately constant effective [electric charge](../../../../../electric-charge-split.md) per segment, both charge and friction scale with $N$. The free-solution [mobility](../../../../../order-parameter-mobility.md) can therefore become nearly independent of [length](../../../../../length.md), making size separation poor. A gel adds steric constraints. Coils smaller than the mesh can pass relatively easily; larger coils require deformation and pore-to-pore rearrangement. A schematic weak-sieving law is $\mu(c_g)\simeq\mu(0)e^{-K_rc_g}$, where matrix [concentration](../../../../../concentration.md) $c_g$ and retardation coefficient $K_r$ are defined by a calibration; it is not a universal size law.

When a long flexible charged [polymer](../../../../../polymer-split.md) is confined to a tube of available paths, [reptation](../../../../../reptation.md) supplies another regime. An additive-friction model gives contour [diffusion](../../../../../diffusion.md) $D_c\propto N^{-1}$ and renewal time $\tau\sim N^2/D_c\propto N^3$. For ideal large-scale chain statistics, $R^2\propto N$, so centre-of-mass [diffusion](../../../../../diffusion.md) scales as $N^{-2}$. With total effective driving charge proportional to $N$, weak-field [mobility](../../../../../order-parameter-mobility.md) can then scale as $N^{-1}$. This assumes screened/ideal large-scale conformations and [linear response](../../../../../linear-response-theory.md); strong-field alignment, trapping and nonideal matrices can alter the scaling.

Operationally, load narrow zones of independently characterized synthetic standards and sample into a controlled gel, apply a known field and detect migration positions after a measured time. Compare with a free-solution measurement, vary matrix [concentration](../../../../../concentration.md) and field, and control [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), [ionic strength](../../../../../ionic-strength.md), [pH](../../../../../ph.md) and matrix charge. Larger gel pores or lower matrix [concentration](../../../../../concentration.md) usually reduce sieving within a given calibrated material system. Excessive field causes [Joule heating](../../../../../joule-heating.md) and can change conformation and [mobility](../../../../../order-parameter-mobility.md). [adsorption](../../../../../adsorption.md) or electro-osmotic drift can mimic a size effect; standards should match charge chemistry and [topology](../../../../../topology-split.md) as well as nominal mass.

Band centres move as $\mu Et$ while ordinary [diffusion](../../../../../diffusion.md) gives $\sigma_x^2(t)=\sigma_x^2(0)+2Dt$. Resolution depends on separation of centres relative to band widths, not travel distance alone. A neutral tracer helps identify bulk flow, and multiple [concentrations](../../../../../concentration.md) test [adsorption](../../../../../adsorption.md) or overloading. **A [mobility](../../../../../order-parameter-mobility.md) or band position does not uniquely determine size without a regime-specific calibration.** TODO: biomolecular examples, sample preparation and biological applications are user deferred; the account here concerns synthetic charged [polymers](../../../../../polymer-split.md) only.
