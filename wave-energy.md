# Wave energy

↑ **Parent:** [Wave equation](wave-equation-split.md)

For a real scalar [wave equation](wave-equation-split.md), the kinetic and gradient [energy](energy-split.md) is $E_{\mathrm{lin}}(t)=\frac12\int(|u_t|^2+|\nabla u|^2)\,dx$. A potential term is added for a [semilinear wave equation](semilinear-wave-equation.md) $u_{tt}-\Delta u+V^{\prime}(u)=0$, giving the conserved [wave energy](wave-energy.md) $E=E_{\mathrm{lin}}+\int V(u)\,dx$.
