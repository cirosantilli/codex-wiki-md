# Prime signature

↑ **Parent:** [Fundamental theorem of arithmetic](fundamental-theorem-of-arithmetic.md)

The prime signature of $n=\prod_{i=1}^r p_i^{a_i}$ is the [multiset](multiset.md) of positive [prime exponents](p-adic-valuation.md) $a_i$, conventionally sorted. For example, $12=2^2\cdot3$ and $45=3^2\cdot5$ both have signature $(2,1)$. Quantities such as the [divisor function](divisor-function.md) and the number of [ordered factorizations](ordered-factorization.md) depend on this signature rather than the particular [primes](prime-number-split.md).
