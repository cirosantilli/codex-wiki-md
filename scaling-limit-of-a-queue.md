# Scaling limit of a queue

↑ **Parent:** [Queueing theory](queueing-theory-split.md)

An asymptotic description obtained by increasing queue resources or input size, or by approaching critical load while rescaling time and workload. [Large-buffer queue scaling](large-buffer-queue-scaling.md), [many-source queue scaling](many-source-queue-scaling.md), [moderate-deviation queue scaling](moderate-deviation-queue-scaling.md) and the [heavy-traffic limit of a queue](heavy-traffic-limit-of-a-queue.md) preserve different relations between mean load, random fluctuations and storage.
