# Charge-conjugate particle and antiparticle spinors

↑ **Parent:** [Charge conjugation of a Dirac field](charge-conjugation-of-a-dirac-field.md)

Choose the negative-frequency [Dirac spinors](dirac-spinor.md) as the charge conjugates of the positive-frequency spinors. Transposing $\bar u(\not p-m)=0$ and using the [charge-conjugation matrix](charge-conjugation-matrix.md) identity gives $(\not p+m)C\bar u^T=0$, so the displayed choice solves the correct antiparticle equation. In the [mode expansion of a Dirac field](mode-expansion-of-a-dirac-field.md), the unitary charge-conjugation operator then exchanges particle and antiparticle annihilation operators, with conjugate phases on their creation operators. A different spinor basis can insert conventional phases or a spin-index relabeling; the displayed equality specifies a compatible choice.
