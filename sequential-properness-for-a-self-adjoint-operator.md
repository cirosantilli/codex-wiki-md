# Sequential properness for a self-adjoint operator

↑ **Parent:** [Closed-range bound on the kernel complement](closed-range-bound-on-the-kernel-complement.md)

For a bounded [self-adjoint operator](self-adjoint-operator.md) $L$, every bounded sequence whose images converge has a norm-convergent subsequence exactly when its kernel is finite-dimensional and its range is closed, or equivalently $0\notin\Sigma_{\mathrm e}(L)$ for the [essential spectrum of a bounded self-adjoint operator](essential-spectrum-of-a-bounded-self-adjoint-operator.md). Split the sequence into kernel and kernel complement: finite dimensionality gives a subsequence on the first part and the [closed-range bound on the kernel complement](closed-range-bound-on-the-kernel-complement.md) makes the second part a [Cauchy sequence](cauchy-sequence.md). Infinite kernel or approximate null unit vectors in its complement obstruct the property.
