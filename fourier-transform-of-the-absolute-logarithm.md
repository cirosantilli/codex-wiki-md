# Fourier transform of the absolute logarithm

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier transform of a tempered distribution](fourier-transform-of-a-tempered-distribution.md)

The [distributional derivative](distributional-derivative.md) of $\log|x|$ is the [principal-value reciprocal distribution](principal-value-reciprocal-distribution.md). Its transform is $-i\pi\operatorname{sgn}k$. Dividing the derivative-transform relation by $ik$ gives the displayed [finite-part inverse-absolute-value distribution](finite-part-inverse-absolute-value-distribution.md), with an undetermined delta term fixed by the finite-part convention.
