# Mean and variance of a Galton-Watson generation

↑ **Parent:** [Galton-Watson process](galton-watson-process.md)

With one initial ancestor and independent offspring counts of finite mean and [variance](variance-split.md), conditioning on the current generation gives the displayed recurrences. For $\mu\ne1$ the [variance](variance-split.md) is $\sigma^2\mu^{n-1}(\mu^n-1)/(\mu-1)$ for positive generation index. At $\mu=1$ it is $n\sigma^2$. At zero offspring mean the distribution is concentrated at zero and later generations vanish. These formulas are for the [Galton-Watson process](galton-watson-process.md), not arbitrary dependent reproduction.
