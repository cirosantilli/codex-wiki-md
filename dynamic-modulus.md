# Dynamic modulus

↑ **Parent:** [Small-amplitude oscillatory shear](small-amplitude-oscillatory-shear.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamic_modulus)

The [dynamic modulus](dynamic-modulus.md) is the complex [stress](stress.md)-amplitude to [strain](strain.md)-amplitude ratio in steady oscillatory response. With the convention $e^{i\omega t}$, the real part is the [storage modulus](storage-modulus.md) and the imaginary part is the [loss modulus](loss-modulus.md). For $\epsilon=\epsilon_0\cos\omega t$, $\sigma=\epsilon_0(G'\cos\omega t-G''\sin\omega t)$. The average supplied power is $\omega\epsilon_0^2G''/2$, fixing the loss-sign convention. The elastic coefficient is a shear or extension modulus according to the deformation measured; it is unrelated to the [complex modulus](complex-modulus.md) meaning absolute value of a complex number.
