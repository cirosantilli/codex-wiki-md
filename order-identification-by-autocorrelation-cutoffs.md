# Order identification by autocorrelation cutoffs

↑ **Parent:** [Autoregressive moving-average model](autoregressive-moving-average-model.md)

For minimal causal and invertible models, the [partial autocorrelation function](partial-autocorrelation-function.md) cuts off after the order of a pure [autoregressive model](autoregressive-model.md), whereas the [autocorrelation function](autocorrelation.md) cuts off after the order of a pure [moving-average model](moving-average-model.md). Estimated cutoffs suggest candidate orders; finite-sample noise and residual diagnostics still matter.
