# Upper asymptotic density

↑ **Parent:** [Natural density](natural-density.md)

For a [subset](subset.md) $A$ of the [positive integers](positive-integer.md), its upper asymptotic density is

$$
\overline d(A)=\limsup_{N\to\infty}\frac{|A\cap\{1,\ldots,N\}|}{N}.
$$

The [limit superior](limit-superior.md) keeps the largest limiting proportion even when the running proportions oscillate. Because they lie in $[0,1]$, this [limit superior](limit-superior.md) always exists in $[0,1]$. If [natural density](natural-density.md) exists, [upper asymptotic density](upper-asymptotic-density.md) equals it.

The convention here uses $1,\ldots,N$, while [natural density](natural-density.md) above uses $0,\ldots,N-1$. Replacing one interval by the other changes a count by at most one, so the proportions differ by at most $1/N$ and have the same [limit superior](limit-superior.md). For the [set without natural density](set-without-natural-density.md) above, the count in $\{1,\ldots,16\}$ is six rather than five, because $16$ is included and zero is absent. Nevertheless its [upper asymptotic density](upper-asymptotic-density.md) is exactly $2/3$. The even [positive integers](positive-integer.md) have [upper asymptotic density](upper-asymptotic-density.md) $1/2$ and the [prime numbers](prime-number-split.md) have [upper asymptotic density](upper-asymptotic-density.md) zero.

The useful feature is that a large proportion along arbitrarily large initial [integer intervals](integer-interval.md) already supplies structure. The [Szemerédi theorem](szemeredi-s-theorem.md) says that positive [upper asymptotic density](upper-asymptotic-density.md) forces [arithmetic progressions](arithmetic-progression.md) of every finite length, with positive common difference, even without an existing [natural density](natural-density.md). Szemerédi's 1975 paper resolved this general finite-length problem; it makes density a bridge from counting to additive structure. [Endre Szemerédi, On sets of integers containing k elements in arithmetic progression, Acta Arithmetica 27, 199–245, 1975](https://doi.org/10.4064/aa-27-1-199-245)
