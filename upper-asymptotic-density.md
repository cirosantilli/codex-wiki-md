# Upper asymptotic density

↑ **Parent:** [Natural density](natural-density.md)

For a subset $A$ of the positive integers, its upper asymptotic density is $\overline d(A)=\limsup_{N\to\infty}|A\cap\{1,\ldots,N\}|/N$. Unlike [natural density](natural-density.md), it always exists. If [natural density](natural-density.md) exists, the two agree. Positive upper asymptotic density suffices for the [Szemerédi theorem](szemeredi-s-theorem.md).
