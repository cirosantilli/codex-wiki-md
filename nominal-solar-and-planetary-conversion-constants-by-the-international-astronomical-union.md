# Nominal solar and planetary conversion constants by the International Astronomical Union

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)

[2015 ResolutionB3: nominal solar and planetary conversion constants](https://iauarchive.eso.org/static/resolutions/IAU2015_English.pdf#page=7)

The adopted nominal solar [radius](radius.md) is $6.957\times10^8\,\mathrm m$ and nominal solar [mass](mass.md) parameter is $1.3271244\times10^{20}\,\mathrm{m^3\,s^{-2}}$. These are conversion conventions; they do not provide a measured [radius](radius.md) or [mass](mass.md) for another host [star](star.md).
