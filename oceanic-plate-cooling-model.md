# Oceanic plate cooling model

↑ **Parent:** [Lithosphere](lithosphere.md)

An [oceanic plate cooling model](oceanic-plate-cooling-model.md) solves the [heat equation](heat-equation.md) in a layer of fixed thickness $a$, with fixed cold surface and hot basal [temperatures](temperature-split.md). A hot initial interior cools towards a linear steady profile. Unlike a [half-space cooling model](half-space-cooling-model.md), it has a finite limiting thermal thickness and a nonzero old-age surface [heat flux](heat-flux-density.md). Its transient sine coefficients must reproduce the chosen initial profile as well as the boundary values.
