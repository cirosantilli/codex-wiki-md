# Normality (statistics)

↑ **Parent:** [Normal distribution](normal-distribution.md)

A [normality](normality-statistics.md) assumption specifies a [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md) for the relevant observations, errors, or latent effects. These are different assumptions: normal latent [random effects](random-effect.md) do not imply that the observed response is normal. Estimated [regression residuals](regression-residual.md) or fitted [random effects](random-effect.md) are not independent copies of the underlying errors or effects.
