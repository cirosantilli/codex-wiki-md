# Category of coalgebras for a comonad

↑ **Parent:** [Coalgebra for a comonad](coalgebra-for-a-comonad.md)

The category $\mathcal E^G$ consists of [coalgebras for a comonad](coalgebra-for-a-comonad.md) and their structure-preserving morphisms. Its [forgetful functor](forgetful-functor.md) has the [cofree coalgebra](cofree-coalgebra.md) as right adjoint. If $\mathcal E$ is a [topos](elementary-topos.md) and $G$ preserves [finite limits](finite-limit.md), those limits are created by the forgetful functor, while [exponentials in a coalgebra topos](exponentials-in-a-coalgebra-topos.md) and the [subobject classifier of a coalgebra topos](subobject-classifier-of-a-coalgebra-topos.md) can be constructed as equalizers inside cofree objects.
