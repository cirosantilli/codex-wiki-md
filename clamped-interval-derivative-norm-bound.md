# Clamped interval derivative norm bound

↑ **Parent:** [Clamped second-order Sobolev space](clamped-second-order-sobolev-space.md)

For $v\in H_0^2(-1,1)$, integrate its [weak derivative](weak-derivative.md) from the left endpoint, where $v=v'=0$. The [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) gives $\|v'\|_2\leq2\|v''\|_2$ and $\|v\|_2\leq2\|v'\|_2$. Adding the three squared derivative norms gives the displayed bound. The constant is sufficient, rather than asserted sharp. Thus the second-derivative norm is equivalent to the full [Sobolev norm](sobolev-norm.md) on this clamped space.
