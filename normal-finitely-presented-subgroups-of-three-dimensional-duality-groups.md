# Normal finitely presented subgroups of three-dimensional duality groups

↑ **Parent:** [Poincare duality group](poincare-duality-group.md)

Let $N$ be a normal [finitely presented group](finitely-presented-group.md) inside a dimension-three [Poincare duality group](poincare-duality-group.md) $G$, with $G/N$ containing an element of infinite order. Then $N$ is either a [free group](free-group.md) or a dimension-two [Poincare duality group](poincare-duality-group.md). The [Strebel infinite-index subgroup theorem](strebel-infinite-index-subgroup-theorem.md) gives $\operatorname{cd}N\leq2$. If the dimension is two, take the preimage of an infinite cyclic subgroup of the quotient. The [cyclic-extension shift of group-ring cohomology](cyclic-extension-shift-of-group-ring-cohomology.md) gives dimension three, forcing this preimage to have finite index by Strebel. Its duality then gives $H^*(N;\mathbb ZN)$ concentrated in degree two with top group $\mathbb Z$. The quotient is virtually cyclic, and surface automorphism realization gives a finite-index surface mapping-torus group.
