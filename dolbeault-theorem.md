# Dolbeault theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Dolbeault cohomology](dolbeault-cohomology.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dolbeault_theorem)

For the sheaf $\Omega^p$ of holomorphic $p$-forms on a [complex manifold](complex-manifold.md), the [Dolbeault-Poincaré lemma](dolbeault-poincare-lemma.md) makes

$$
0\longrightarrow\Omega^p\longrightarrow\mathcal A^{p,0}\xrightarrow{\bar\partial}\mathcal A^{p,1}\xrightarrow{\bar\partial}\cdots
$$

a resolution by [fine sheaves](fine-sheaf.md). Taking [global sections](global-section.md) therefore gives the natural isomorphism

$$
H^q(X,\Omega^p)\cong H_{\bar\partial}^{p,q}(X).
$$
