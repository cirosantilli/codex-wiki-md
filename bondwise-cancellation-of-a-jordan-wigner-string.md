# Bondwise cancellation of a Jordan-Wigner string

↑ **Parent:** [Jordan–Wigner transformation](jordan-wigner-transformation.md)

For $S_n^+=P_nc_n$ with $P_n=\prod_{m<n}(1-2c_m^\dagger c_m)$, neighbouring [Jordan–Wigner transformation](jordan-wigner-transformation.md) strings differ by only the local parity factor. The [canonical anticommutation relations](canonical-anticommutation-relations.md) give $(c_n+c_n^\dagger)(1-2c_n^\dagger c_n)=c_n^\dagger-c_n$, proving the displayed local [spin](spin.md) identity. The closing bond of a periodic chain instead retains total [fermion parity](fermion-parity.md); cancellation on bulk bonds does not justify discarding that global sector.
