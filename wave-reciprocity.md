# Wave reciprocity

↑ **Parent:** [Wave](wave.md)

In a reciprocal medium, appropriately normalized source and receiver responses can be exchanged: the scalar [Green function](green-s-function.md) satisfies $G(\mathbf r,\mathbf r')=G(\mathbf r',\mathbf r)$. Reciprocity alone does not imply absence of [wave absorption](wave-absorption.md).
