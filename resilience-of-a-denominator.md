# Resilience of a denominator

↑ **Parent:** [Euler totient function](euler-totient-function.md)

For an [integer](integer-split.md) $d>1$, the proportion of proper fractions $1/d,\ldots,(d-1)/d$ already in lowest terms. There are $\varphi(d)$ such fractions, so $R(d)=\varphi(d)/(d-1)$. The [prime factorization](fundamental-theorem-of-arithmetic.md) of $d$ gives $R(d)=\frac d{d-1}\prod_{p\mid d}(1-1/p)$.
