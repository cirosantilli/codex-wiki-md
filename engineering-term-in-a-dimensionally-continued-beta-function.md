# Engineering term in a dimensionally continued beta function

↑ **Parent:** [Beta function (physics)](beta-function-physics.md)

In $d=4-\epsilon$, the quartic scalar coupling has [engineering dimension](engineering-dimension.md) $\epsilon$. Factoring out $\mu^\epsilon$ makes the renormalized [running coupling](running-coupling.md) dimensionless. Differentiating its bare expression at fixed bare parameters generates the linear term $-\epsilon\lambda$, even when the quantum contribution to the [renormalization-group beta function](beta-function-physics.md) begins quadratically. The [simple-pole formulas for minimal-subtraction renormalization group functions](simple-pole-formulas-for-minimal-subtraction-renormalization-group-functions.md) determine the remaining contribution from the simple pole of $F$.
