# Monoclonal antibody

↑ **Parent:** [Antibody](antibody.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monoclonal_antibody)

A [monoclonal antibody](monoclonal-antibody.md) preparation derives from one selected antibody-producing [biological cell](biological-cell.md) and its descendants, or reproduces their specified [antibody](antibody.md) sequences. Monoclonal describes the origin and composition of the preparation; it does not guarantee absence of [cross-reactivity](cross-reactivity.md) or establish [antibody affinity](antibody-affinity.md).
