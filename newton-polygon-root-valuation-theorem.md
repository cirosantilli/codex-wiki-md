# Newton polygon root valuation theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Newton polygon](newton-polygon.md)

For a [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) with nonzero constant coefficient over a complete [discretely valued field](discretely-valued-field.md), plot coefficient exponents against their [valuations](valuation.md). A lower [Newton polygon](newton-polygon.md) segment of slope $s$ and horizontal length $r$ accounts for exactly $r$ roots of [valuation](valuation.md) $-s$, counted with multiplicity in an [algebraic closure](algebraic-closure.md). Zero coefficients are omitted. Reflecting the horizontal axis changes the sign of every slope, so slope conventions must be fixed before reading root [valuations](valuation.md).
