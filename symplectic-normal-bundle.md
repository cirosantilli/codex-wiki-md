# Symplectic normal bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic submanifold](symplectic-submanifold.md)

The symplectic normal bundle of $S$ is the symplectic orthogonal complement $(TS)^\omega\subset TM|_S$. It is a symplectic vector bundle and is naturally isomorphic to the ordinary normal bundle.
