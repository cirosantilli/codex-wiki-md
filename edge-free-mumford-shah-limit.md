<h1 id="edge-free-mumford-shah-limit">Edge-free Mumford–Shah limit</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Mumford–Shah functional](mumford-shah-functional.md)

As the jump-length weight tends to infinity with the [gradient](gradient.md) weight fixed, the limiting problem minimizes $\|u-g\|_2^2+\alpha\|\nabla u\|_2^2$ on $H^1$. It has a unique [minimizer](global-minimizer.md) by the [Lax-Milgram theorem](lax-milgram-theorem.md) and strict convexity. Without prescribed boundary values, its weak equation is $u-\alpha\Delta u=g$ with natural [Neumann boundary conditions](neumann-boundary-condition.md).
