# Mass flow rate

↑ **Parent:** [Volumetric flow rate](volumetric-flow-rate.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mass_flow_rate)

The mass flow rate through an oriented cross-section is the integral of [mass density](density.md) times normal [velocity](velocity.md). For constant density it is density times the [volumetric flow rate](volumetric-flow-rate.md). In a steady pipe without leaks, [conservation of mass](mass-conservation.md) makes this rate independent of cross-section.
