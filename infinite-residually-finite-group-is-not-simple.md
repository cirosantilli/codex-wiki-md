# Infinite residually finite group is not simple

↑ **Parent:** [Residually finite group](residually-finite-group.md)

An infinite residually finite group is not a [simple group](simple-group.md). For a nonidentity element, residual finiteness supplies a map to a finite group in which it survives. Its kernel is proper and cannot be trivial, since an infinite group cannot inject into a finite group.
