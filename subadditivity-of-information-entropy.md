# Subadditivity of information entropy

↑ **Parent:** [Information entropy](information-entropy.md)

The [chain rule for information entropy](chain-rule-for-information-entropy.md) and [conditioning reduces entropy](conditioning-reduces-entropy.md) give

$$
H(X,Y)\leq H(X)+H(Y),
$$

with equality exactly when $X$ and $Y$ are [independent](independent-random-variables.md).
