# Harmonic chain

↑ **Parent:** [Simple harmonic motion](simple-harmonic-motion.md)

A uniform [harmonic chain](harmonic-chain.md) couples neighbouring [displacements](displacement.md) by quadratic [spring](spring.md) energies. With periodic boundaries and spacing $a$, a [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md) gives $\omega_k=2\sqrt{\kappa/m_0}|\sin(ka/2)|$. Quantization makes each nonzero [normal mode](normal-mode.md) a [phonon](phonon.md). The zero-frequency uniform [displacement](displacement.md) is a free centre-of-mass coordinate unless pinned, not an ordinary oscillator.[§§4.1.1 and4.1.4, monatomic chain and quantized vibrations](applications-of-quantum-mechanics-by-david-tong.md)
