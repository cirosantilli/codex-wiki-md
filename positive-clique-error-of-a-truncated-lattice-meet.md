# Positive clique error of a truncated lattice meet

↑ **Parent:** [Finite lattice approximation for monotone clique circuits](finite-lattice-approximation-for-monotone-clique-circuits.md)

Choose a uniform $m$-subset $Z$ and the [graph](graph-split.md) consisting only of its [clique](clique-graph-theory.md). Acceptance by two approximating [set families](set-family.md) but rejection by their intersection requires two minimal witnesses whose union has size greater than $\ell$. At least one has size greater than $\ell/2$. The [sunflower bound for minimal members of a Razborov-closed family](sunflower-bound-for-minimal-members-of-a-razborov-closed-family.md) and $\mathbb P(W\subseteq Z)\leq(m/n)^{|W|}$ give $2\sum_{s>\ell/2}s!(r-1)^s(m/n)^s\leq4q^{\ell/2}$.
