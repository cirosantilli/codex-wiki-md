# Coefficient formula for trace of Frobenius modulo p

↑ **Parent:** [Trace of Frobenius](trace-of-frobenius.md)

For odd $p$, let $y^2=f(x)$ be a nonsingular cubic [Weierstrass equation of an elliptic curve](weierstrass-equation-of-an-elliptic-curve.md) over $\mathbb F_p$. Its [Trace of Frobenius](trace-of-frobenius.md) is $a_p=-\sum_x\chi(f(x))$, where $\chi$ is the [Legendre symbol](legendre-symbol.md) extended by $\chi(0)=0$. By the [Euler criterion](euler-criterion.md), reduce this sum to $f(x)^{(p-1)/2}$. The sum of a positive power $x^j$ over $\mathbb F_p$ is zero unless $p-1$ divides $j$, when it is $-1$. Since the polynomial has degree $3(p-1)/2<2(p-1)$, only its $x^{p-1}$ coefficient contributes.
