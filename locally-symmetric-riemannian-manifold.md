# Locally symmetric Riemannian manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md)

A [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md) is locally symmetric when its [Riemann curvature tensor](riemann-curvature-tensor.md) is parallel for the [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md). Along a [geodesic](geodesic.md), [parallel transport](parallel-transport.md) then conjugates the [Jacobi curvature operator](jacobi-curvature-operator.md) at the initial point to the operator at every later point. Thus its [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) stay constant. The condition is local and does not assert that global point reflections extend to isometries of the whole manifold.
