# Genetic drift

↑ **Parent:** [Population genetics](population-genetics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genetic_drift)

Genetic drift is random variation of [allele](allele.md) frequencies due to finite-population reproduction. In the neutral ancestry description, randomly sampled gene copies merge into common ancestors according to [Kingman's coalescent](kingman-s-coalescent.md).
