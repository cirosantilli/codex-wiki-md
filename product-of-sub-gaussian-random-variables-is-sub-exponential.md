# Product of sub-Gaussian random variables is sub-exponential

↑ **Parent:** [Subexponential distribution (light-tailed)](subexponential-distribution-light-tailed.md)

If $X$ and $Y$ are sub-Gaussian, without requiring independence between them, then $XY$ is [sub-exponential](subexponential-distribution-light-tailed.md). The inequality $2|XY|\leq X^2+Y^2$ converts exponential-square moment bounds for the factors into an exponential moment bound for the product.
