<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

[Coprimality](../../../../coprime-integers.md) gives [polynomials](../../../../polynomial-split.md) $a,b\in\mathbb Q[x]$ with $ag+bh=1$, so the [ideals](../../../../ideal.md) $(g)$ and $(h)$ are comaximal. Also

$$
(g)\cap(h)=(gh).
$$

Indeed, if $u=gr=hs$, multiplying $ag+bh=1$ by $u$ writes $u=aghs+bhgr$, a multiple of $gh$; the reverse inclusion is immediate. Part (b), with $f=gh$, gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbb Q[x]/(f)\cong\mathbb Q[x]/(g)\times\mathbb Q[x]/(h).}
$$

The explicit [isomorphism](../../../../isomorphism.md) takes the class of a [polynomial](../../../../polynomial-split.md) $r$ to its classes modulo $g$ and $h$.
