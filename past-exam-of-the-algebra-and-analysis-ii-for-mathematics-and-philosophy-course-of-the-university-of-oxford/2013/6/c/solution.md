[Liouville's theorem](../../../../liouville-theorem.md) states that every [bounded](../../../../boundedness.md) [entire function](../../../../entire-function.md) is constant:

$$
\boxed{f\text{ holomorphic on }\mathbb C,\quad\sup_{z\in\mathbb C}|f(z)|<\infty\quad\Longrightarrow\quad f\text{ constant}.}
$$

For example, the Cauchy [derivative](../../../../derivative.md) estimate on a [circle](../../../../circle.md) of radius $R$ gives $|f'(z)|\leq M/R$. Letting $R\to\infty$ gives $f'=0$ everywhere, proving the conclusion.
