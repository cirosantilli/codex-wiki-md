<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [conformal map](../../../../conformal-map.md) preserves oriented [angles](../../../../angle.md) between regular smooth curves through each point. For a [holomorphic](../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) map with nonzero [derivative](../../../../derivative.md), the [derivative](../../../../derivative.md) also describes the local scale and [rotation](../../../../rotation-mathematics.md).

Indeed, if two curves $\gamma_1,\gamma_2$ meet at $z$ and have nonzero tangent [vectors](../../../../vector.md) $v_1,v_2$, the real [chain rule](../../../../chain-rule.md) and [complex differentiability](../../../../complex-differentiability.md) give

$$
(f\circ\gamma_j)'=f'(z)v_j.
$$

Because $f'(z)\neq0$, the [image](../../../../image-of-a-function.md) [tangents](../../../../tangent.md) are nonzero and

$$
\boxed{\frac{(f\circ\gamma_2)'}{(f\circ\gamma_1)'}=\frac{v_2}{v_1}.}
$$

Taking arguments proves preservation of oriented [angles](../../../../angle.md). Multiplication by $f'(z)$ rotates every tangent by $\arg f'(z)$ and scales every length by $|f'(z)|$. Thus the hypothesis gives [conformality](../../../../conformality.md) at every point of $U$; global [injectivity](../../../../injective-function.md) is not needed for this local conclusion.
