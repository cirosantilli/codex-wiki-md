<h1 id="6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A [Möbius transformation](../../../../mobius-transformation.md) maps generalized [circles](../../../../circle.md) to generalized [circles](../../../../circle.md) on the [Riemann sphere](../../../../riemann-sphere.md). A [generalized circle](../../../../generalized-circle-under-a-mobius-transformation.md) is an ordinary [circle](../../../../circle.md) or a [straight line](../../../../straight-line.md) together with the point at infinity. Its [image](../../../../image-of-a-function.md) is a [straight line](../../../../straight-line.md) precisely when it contains infinity, equivalently when the [preimage](../../../../preimage.md) of infinity lies on the extended real axis.

If $c=0$, the pole is infinity itself and the transformation is the [affine map](../../../../affine-map.md) $(a/d)z+b/d$, taking the extended real axis to a line. If $c\neq0$, the pole is $-d/c$, which is real exactly when $d/c$ is real. Thus the full condition is

$$
\boxed{c=0\quad\text{or}\quad c\neq0\text{ and }d/c\in\mathbb R.}
$$

Equivalently, it is $\operatorname{Im}(d\bar c)=0$, a condition also valid when $c=0$. If it fails, the [image](../../../../image-of-a-function.md) misses infinity and is therefore **an ordinary [circle](../../../../circle.md)**. The nonzero [determinant](../../../../determinant.md) ensures that none of these generalized [circles](../../../../circle.md) collapses to a point.
