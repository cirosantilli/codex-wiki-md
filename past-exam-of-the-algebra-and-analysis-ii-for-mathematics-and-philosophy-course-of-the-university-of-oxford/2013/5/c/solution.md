<h1 id="5/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A subset $B\subseteq\mathbb C$, with its [subspace topology](../../../../subspace-topology.md), is [compact](../../../../compact-space.md) when every [open cover](../../../../open-cover.md) of $B$ has a [finite subcover](../../../../finite-subcover.md). The [Heine-Borel theorem](../../../../heine-borel-theorem.md) in the [complex plane](../../../../complex-plane.md), identified with $\mathbb R^2$, says

$$
\boxed{B\text{ compact}\iff B\text{ closed and bounded}.}
$$

Closedness is in the ambient [complex plane](../../../../complex-plane.md). For a [continuous](../../../../continuous-function.md) real-valued function on a nonempty [compact](../../../../compact-space.md) set, the [supremum](../../../../supremum.md) and [infimum](../../../../infimum.md) are finite and attained.
