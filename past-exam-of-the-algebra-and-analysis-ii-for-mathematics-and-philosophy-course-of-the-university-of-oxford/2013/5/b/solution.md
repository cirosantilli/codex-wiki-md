↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [residue theorem](../../../../residue-theorem.md) states that if a function is [holomorphic](../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) on and inside a positively oriented simple closed [contour](../../../../complex-integration-contour.md) except at finitely many isolated [singularities](../../../../complex-singularity.md) $a_1,\ldots,a_N$ in its interior, then

$$
\boxed{\int_\gamma f(z)\,dz=2\pi i\sum_{j=1}^N\operatorname{res}(f;a_j).}
$$

No [singularity](../../../../complex-singularity.md) may lie on the [contour](../../../../complex-integration-contour.md). More generally, a closed [contour](../../../../complex-integration-contour.md) has a winding-number-weighted sum of [residues](../../../../residue.md), provided it and its enclosed winding regions lie in the domain on which the isolated [singularities](../../../../complex-singularity.md) are specified.
