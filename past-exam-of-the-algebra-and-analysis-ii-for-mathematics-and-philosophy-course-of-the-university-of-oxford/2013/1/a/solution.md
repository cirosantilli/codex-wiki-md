<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [minimal polynomial](../../../../minimal-polynomial.md) of a [linear transformation](../../../../linear-map.md) $T$ is the unique [monic polynomial](../../../../monic-polynomial.md) of least [degree](../../../../degree-of-a-polynomial.md) among the nonzero [polynomials](../../../../polynomial-split.md) $p\in F[x]$ satisfying $p(T)=0$:

$$
\boxed{m_T\text{ monic},\quad m_T(T)=0,\quad\deg m_T\text{ least possible}.}
$$

Such a [polynomial](../../../../polynomial-split.md) exists because the [endomorphism](../../../../endomorphism.md) space is finite-dimensional, so sufficiently many [powers](../../../../power.md) $I,T,T^2,\ldots$ are [linearly dependent](../../../../linear-dependence.md). Monic normalization is possible over the [field](../../../../field.md) $F$. Two [monic polynomials](../../../../monic-polynomial.md) annihilating $T$ of the same least [degree](../../../../degree-of-a-polynomial.md) have a difference of smaller [degree](../../../../degree-of-a-polynomial.md) annihilating $T$, so their difference is zero. For the zero [vector space](../../../../vector-space-split.md) the convention is $m_T=1$.
