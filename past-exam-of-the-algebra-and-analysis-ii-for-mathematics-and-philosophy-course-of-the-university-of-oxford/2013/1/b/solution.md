<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Apply [polynomial division](../../../../polynomial-division.md) in $F[x]$:

$$
f=q m_T+r,\qquad r=0\text{ or }\deg r<\deg m_T.
$$

Evaluation at $T$ gives $r(T)=f(T)-q(T)m_T(T)=0$. A nonzero remainder could be rescaled to a monic [polynomial](../../../../polynomial-split.md) annihilating $T$ of smaller [degree](../../../../degree-of-a-polynomial.md), contradicting the defining minimality. Hence $r=0$ and

$$
\boxed{m_T\mid f.}
$$

In particular, the annihilating [polynomials](../../../../polynomial-split.md) form the [principal ideal](../../../../principal-ideal.md) $(m_T)$.
