<h1 id="2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

In part (i), set every $b_i=1$. Its squared [norm](../../../../../norm.md) is $n$, giving

$$
\boxed{n\sum_{i=1}^n a_i^2\geq\left(\sum_{i=1}^n a_i\right)^2.}
$$

The equality condition is $a_1=\cdots=a_n$, because $a$ must be a [scalar multiple](../../../../../scalar-multiple.md) of $(1,\ldots,1)$.
