<h1 id="2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use the [Euclidean inner product](../../../../../dot-product.md) on $\mathbb R^n$, with $a=(a_1,\ldots,a_n)$ and $b=(b_1,\ldots,b_n)$. If $b=0$, both sides of the desired inequality are zero. Otherwise take the single [unit vector](../../../../../unit-vector.md) $v=b/\|b\|$ in part (a). Then

$$
\|a\|^2\geq\frac{\langle a,b\rangle^2}{\|b\|^2},
$$

so

$$
\boxed{\left(\sum_{i=1}^n a_i^2\right)\left(\sum_{i=1}^n b_i^2\right)\geq\left(\sum_{i=1}^n a_ib_i\right)^2.}
$$

This is the real [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](../../../../../cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md). Equality holds when the two [vectors](../../../../../vector.md) are [linearly dependent](../../../../../linear-dependence.md).
