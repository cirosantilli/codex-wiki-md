<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $p=\sum_{j=1}^m\langle u,v_j\rangle v_j$. [Orthonormality](../../../../orthonormal-set.md) gives $\langle u-p,v_k\rangle=0$ for every $k$, so $u-p$ is [orthogonal](../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) to $p$. The [Pythagorean theorem for inner product spaces](../../../../pythagorean-theorem-in-an-inner-product-space.md) gives

$$
\|u\|^2=\|u-p\|^2+\|p\|^2
=\|u-p\|^2+\sum_{j=1}^m\langle u,v_j\rangle^2.
$$

Since a squared [norm](../../../../norm.md) is nonnegative,

$$
\boxed{\langle u,u\rangle\geq\sum_{j=1}^m\langle u,v_j\rangle^2.}
$$

This is [Bessel's inequality](../../../../bessel-s-inequality.md), with equality exactly when $u$ belongs to the [span](../../../../linear-span.md) of the [orthonormal set](../../../../orthonormal-set.md).
