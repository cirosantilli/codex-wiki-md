# Generalized Vandermonde matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Vandermonde matrix](vandermonde-matrix.md)

A generalized Vandermonde matrix evaluates a chosen list of [functions](function-split.md) $\phi_0,\ldots,\phi_n$ at [interpolation nodes](interpolation-node.md) $x_0,\ldots,x_n$. For a [basis](basis.md) of [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) of [polynomial degree](degree-of-a-polynomial.md) at most $n$ and distinct nodes, it is an [invertible matrix](invertible-matrix.md) by uniqueness of [polynomial interpolation](polynomial-interpolation.md). Choosing monomials recovers the ordinary [Vandermonde matrix](vandermonde-matrix.md).
