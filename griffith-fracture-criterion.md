# Griffith fracture criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Fracture toughness](fracture-toughness.md)

Crack growth becomes energetically possible when the [energy release rate](energy-release-rate.md) reaches its critical value. For fixed crack geometry this gives a brittle-failure [stress](stress.md) scaling as $\sigma_f\propto\sqrt{EG_c}$.
