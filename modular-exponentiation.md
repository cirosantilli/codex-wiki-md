# Modular exponentiation

↑ **Parent:** [Exponentiation by squaring](exponentiation-by-squaring.md)

Modular exponentiation evaluates a nonnegative integer power modulo $m$. Repeated squaring according to the [binary expansion](binary-expansion.md) of $b$ needs $O(\log(b+1))$ modular multiplications, with intermediate integers reduced modulo $m$. This avoids constructing the potentially enormous integer $a^b$. A [reversible computation](reversible-computation.md) gives the coherent [quantum modular exponentiation](quantum-modular-exponentiation.md) used in [quantum order finding](quantum-order-finding.md) and [discrete-logarithm Fourier sampling](discrete-logarithm-fourier-sampling.md).
