# Substructure of a first-order structure

↑ **Parent:** [First-order structure](first-order-structure.md)

An $L$-[substructure of a first-order structure](substructure-of-a-first-order-structure.md) $A\subseteq B$ has a nonempty domain containing all named constants and closed under the [functions](function-split.md) of $B$, and each relation of $A$ is the restriction of the corresponding relation of $B$. Consequently every [quantifier-free formula](quantifier-free-formula.md) with parameters in $A$ has the same truth value in $A$ and $B$. This is weaker than being an [elementary substructure](elementary-substructure.md), which preserves all [first-order formulas](first-order-formula.md).
