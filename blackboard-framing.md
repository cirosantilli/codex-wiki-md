# Blackboard framing

↑ **Parent:** [Framed link](framed-link.md)

The framing of a [link diagram](link-diagram.md) obtained by pushing a component slightly in the plane of the diagram. Diagram curls change this framing; arbitrary removal of curls changes an [anyon](anyon.md) diagram by its [topological spin](topological-spin.md).
