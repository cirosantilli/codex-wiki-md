# Symmetric cube of a group representation

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor product of group representations](tensor-product-of-group-representations.md)

The symmetric cube of a [group representation](group-representation.md) $V$ is the symmetric part of $V^{\otimes3}$. Over the [complex numbers](complex-number.md), its [character](character-of-a-representation.md) is

$$
\chi_{\operatorname{Sym}^3V}(g)=\frac{\chi_V(g)^3+3\chi_V(g)\chi_V(g^2)+2\chi_V(g^3)}6.
$$

The projection onto symmetric [tensors](tensor.md) averages the six permutations of the three factors. The identity permutation contributes $\chi(g)^3$, the three transpositions each contribute $\chi(g)\chi(g^2)$, and the two three-cycles each contribute $\chi(g^3)$. Cubic [polynomial invariants](polynomial-invariant.md) are fixed vectors in $\operatorname{Sym}^3(V^*)$, whose dimension follows by averaging this [character](character-of-a-representation.md) for a [finite group](finite-group.md).
