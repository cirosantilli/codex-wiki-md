# Modular tensor category

↑ **Parent:** [Braided monoidal category](braided-monoidal-category.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modular_tensor_category)

A finite semisimple ribbon [braided monoidal category](braided-monoidal-category.md) with nondegenerate [modular S-matrix](modular-s-matrix.md). For a unitary [anyon](anyon.md) theory, this means that the only [transparent anyon](transparent-anyon.md) is the [vacuum anyon](vacuum-anyon.md). Such data define a three-dimensional [topological quantum field theory](topological-quantum-field-theory.md).
