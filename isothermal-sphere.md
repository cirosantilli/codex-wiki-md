# Isothermal sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md)

An [isothermal sphere](isothermal-sphere.md) is a spherically symmetric self-gravitating gas equilibrium with [equation of state](equation-of-state.md) $p=K\rho$ and constant $K$. The [hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) equations $p'=-GM\rho/r^2$ and $M'=4\pi r^2\rho$ yield the title equation. Its regular finite-central-density solution is distinct from the [singular isothermal sphere](singular-isothermal-sphere.md). A finite outer radius requires confinement because the regular density does not vanish at finite radius.
