# Large-distance tail of the Hilbert transform

↑ **Parent:** [Hilbert transform](hilbert-transform.md)

For a [Schwartz function](schwartz-function.md) $f$, the [Hilbert transform](hilbert-transform.md) satisfies $\mathcal Hf(x)=(\int f)/(\pi x)+o(|x|^{-1})$ at both infinities. Splitting the inverse [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) at the sign multiplier's jump and integrating each half-line by parts extracts the zero-frequency value $\widehat f(0)$; the remainder vanishes by the [Riemann-Lebesgue lemma](riemann-lebesgue-lemma.md). A nonzero zeroth moment therefore obstructs Schwartz decay.
