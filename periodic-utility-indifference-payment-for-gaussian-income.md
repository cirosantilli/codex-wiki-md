# Periodic utility indifference payment for Gaussian income

↑ **Parent:** [Indifference price](indifference-price.md)

For [Gaussian](normal-distribution.md) income of mean $a$, standard deviation $b$ and tradable [correlation](pearson-correlation-coefficient.md) $\rho$, exponential utility makes the maximum acceptable per-period fee equal to the mean minus the correlated-income hedge cost and the residual [variance](variance-split.md) penalty. Compare optimized utilities with and without the contract; a fee above this level makes the contract strictly inferior. At perfect [correlation](pearson-correlation-coefficient.md) the residual penalty vanishes, leaving only the mean and hedge-cost terms.
