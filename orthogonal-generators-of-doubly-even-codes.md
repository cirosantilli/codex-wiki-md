# Orthogonal generators of doubly even codes

↑ **Parent:** [Doubly even code](doubly-even-code.md)

Suppose the generators of a [binary linear code](binary-linear-code.md) have [Hamming weights](hamming-weight.md) divisible by four and have pairwise zero binary [inner products](inner-product.md). Their [linear span](linear-span.md) is [doubly even](doubly-even-code.md): for any two vectors,

$$
\operatorname{wt}(u+v)=\operatorname{wt}(u)+\operatorname{wt}(v)-2|\operatorname{supp}(u)\cap\operatorname{supp}(v)|.
$$

The binary [inner product](inner-product.md) says the intersection size is even. Induction on the number of generators in a sum proves divisibility by four.
