# Trivial subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Subgroup](subgroup.md)

The trivial subgroup contains only the [identity element](identity-element.md) of a [group](group-split.md). It is a [subgroup](subgroup.md) of every group and is proper whenever the group has more than one element. Thus a group of prime [group order](order-of-a-finite-group.md) has no proper nontrivial subgroups, rather than having no proper subgroups at all.
