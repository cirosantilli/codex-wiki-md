# Cycle cover of a directed graph

↑ **Parent:** [Directed graph](directed-graph.md)

A cycle cover of a finite directed graph is a collection of vertex-disjoint directed cycles containing every vertex exactly once. Cycle covers of the weighted directed graph with adjacency matrix $A$ are the terms in the [permanent of a matrix](permanent-mathematics.md) $\operatorname{perm}A$.

This is a [vertex cycle cover](vertex-cycle-cover.md) with the additional requirements of directed cycles and vertex disjointness.
