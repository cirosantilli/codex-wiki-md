# Transverse modulational instability

↑ **Parent:** [Self-focusing](self-focusing.md)

A broad focusing beam can amplify transverse [intensity](intensity-physics.md) perturbations. Linearizing $iA_z+\nabla_\perp^2A/(2k)+\gamma|A|^2A=0$ about its uniform solution gives the displayed growth rate for transverse wave number $q$. For $\gamma>0$, a finite band grows; noise can develop into filaments. A finite beam and saturating response change the ideal plane-background prediction.
