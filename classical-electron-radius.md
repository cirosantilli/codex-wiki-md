# Classical electron radius

↑ **Parent:** [Electron](electron.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classical_electron_radius)

The [classical electron radius](classical-electron-radius.md) is the length scale $r_e=e^2/(4\pi\epsilon_0m_ec^2)$ formed from the [elementary charge](elementary-charge.md), [vacuum permittivity](vacuum-permittivity.md), [electron mass](electron-mass.md) and [speed of light](speed-of-light.md). It is not a measured material radius of an [electron](electron.md). In the classical low-energy scattering model it sets the [Thomson cross-section](thomson-cross-section.md), $\sigma_T=8\pi r_e^2/3$.
