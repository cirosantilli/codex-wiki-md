# Periodic backward-sawtooth Burgers solution

↑ **Parent:** [Inviscid Burgers equation](inviscid-burgers-equation.md)

For the negative-flux equation $f_Z-ff_\theta=0$, a period-two increasing initial ramp develops rarefaction fans at odd points and steepens at even points. Before $Z=1$, its central ramp is $f=(\theta-2m)/(1-Z)$ on $|\theta-2m|<1-Z$, and its odd-point fans are $f=-(\theta-(2m+1))/Z$. After $Z=1$, stationary compressive shocks occupy the even points and $f=(2m+1-\theta)/Z$ between consecutive shocks. The amplitude decays as $1/Z$. The [Rankine-Hugoniot condition](rankine-hugoniot-conditions.md) and incoming [characteristic speeds](characteristic-speed.md) certify the post-breaking [entropy solution](entropy-solution.md).
