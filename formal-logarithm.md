# Formal logarithm

↑ **Parent:** [Formal group law](formal-group-law.md)

Over a characteristic-zero field, a one-dimensional commutative formal group law has a unique strict isomorphism to the additive formal group, called its formal logarithm. It satisfies $\log_F(F(X,Y))=\log_F(X)+\log_F(Y)$.
