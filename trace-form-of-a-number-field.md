# Trace form of a number field

↑ **Parent:** [Field trace](field-trace.md)

The trace form of a number field $K$ is the symmetric bilinear form

$$
B(x,y)=\operatorname{Tr}_{K/\mathbb Q}(xy).
$$

The pairing uses the [field trace](field-trace.md) of the product, rather than taking a trace of each factor separately.
