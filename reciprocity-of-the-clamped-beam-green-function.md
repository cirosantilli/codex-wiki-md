# Reciprocity of the clamped-beam Green function

↑ **Parent:** [Clamped-beam Green function](clamped-beam-green-function.md)

The [clamped-beam Green function](clamped-beam-green-function.md) is symmetric. Its explicit formula depends only on $x_<=\min(x,\xi)$ and $x_>=\max(x,\xi)$. More generally, two [integrations by parts](integration-by-parts.md) give $\int uv''''dx=\int u''v''dx=\int u''''v\,dx$ when $u=u'=v=v'=0$ at both endpoints. Applying this identity to two point-load responses shows that the displacement at one point caused by a unit force at a second equals the displacement at the second caused by a unit force at the first.
