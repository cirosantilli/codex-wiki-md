# Elastic force dipole

↑ **Parent:** [Body force](body-force.md)

Opposite forces separated along the unit direction $\mathbf e$, with fixed force times separation $D$, have this distributional [body force](body-force.md) limit. Its net force is zero. A symmetric [seismic moment tensor](seismic-moment-tensor.md) is a sum of such dipoles along its principal directions.
