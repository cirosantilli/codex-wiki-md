# VC uniform convergence theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical learning theory](statistical-learning-theory.md)

A finite [VC dimension](vc-dimension.md) gives simultaneous control of [empirical misclassification risk](empirical-misclassification-risk.md) for every member of a [hypothesis class](concept-class.md). Sufficient independent sample size is $A\eta^{-2}(v\log(2/\eta)+\log(2/\delta))$. The displayed event implies an [empirical risk minimization](empirical-risk-minimization.md) rule has [misclassification risk](misclassification-risk.md) at most $\inf_h R(h)+2\eta$, including for unrealizable labels.[Uniform convergence and quantitative fundamental theorem](learnability-and-vc-dimension-in-mit-6-790.md)
