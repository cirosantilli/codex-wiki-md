# SH line-source near-field singularity

↑ **Parent:** [SH-wave](sh-wave.md)

Integrating the [shear-horizontal wave](shear-horizontal-wave.md) equation with force $F(t)\delta^{(2)}(\mathbf x)$ over a shrinking circle gives $2\pi\mu r\partial_ru\to-F(t)$. Thus the local [displacement field](displacement-field-mechanics.md) has the displayed logarithmic singularity. This is a fixed-time near-source limit, distinct from the wavefront limit underlying [SH ray-tube amplitude transport](sh-ray-tube-amplitude-transport.md).
