# Stationary mountain-wave group velocity

↑ **Parent:** [Stationary topographic internal gravity wave](stationary-topographic-internal-gravity-wave.md)

Define intrinsic positive frequency $\omega$ and ground frequency $\hat\omega=Uk+\omega$ for a nonrotating [internal gravity wave](internal-wave.md). Stationarity with $U>0$ selects $k<0$ and $\sqrt{k^2+m^2}=N/U$. Differentiating the full dispersion before imposing that condition gives the displayed ground-frame [group velocity](group-velocity.md). Upward energy propagation requires $m<0$ and $|k|<N/U$. Its ray inclination is $\cos^{-1}(U|k|/N)$. The ground-frame group velocity is opposite the normal intrinsic [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md); the intrinsic group velocity is perpendicular to that phase velocity. A strictly stationary ground-frame phase pattern has zero phase velocity, so mixing frames can produce a false angle claim.
