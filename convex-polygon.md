# Convex polygon

↑ **Parent:** [Convex polytope](convex-polytope.md)

A convex polygon is a two-dimensional [convex polytope](convex-polytope.md), equivalently the [convex hull](convex-hull.md) of a finite planar set with nonempty interior. Its vertices are the extreme points of that hull. Saying that given points form a convex polygon means every specified point is a vertex; points inside the hull or in the interior of a straight boundary segment do not count as vertices.
