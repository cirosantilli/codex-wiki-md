# Triflic anhydride

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md)

[Triflic anhydride](triflic-anhydride.md) is $\mathrm{(CF_3SO_2)_2O}$, an electrophilic sulfonylating reagent. [carbonyl](carbonyl-group.md) [oxygen](oxygen.md) attack can form an O-triflylated intermediate; adjacent [nitrogen](nitrogen.md) donation gives an imidoyl-[triflate](triflate.md) description.
