# LS coupling

↑ **Parent:** [Addition of angular momentum](addition-of-angular-momentum.md)

An atomic [addition of angular momentum](addition-of-angular-momentum.md) scheme in which the individual [orbital angular momentum](orbital-angular-momentum.md) vectors first combine into $\mathbf L$ and the individual [spins](spin.md) into $\mathbf S$, then these combine into total [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) $\mathbf J$. When [spin-orbit coupling](spin-orbit-interaction.md) is weak compared with the interactions between [electrons](electron.md) described by [electrostatics](electrostatics-split.md), $L$ and $S$ remain useful approximate [quantum numbers](quantum-number.md). The [Landé g-factor](lande-g-factor.md) describes the resulting weak-field magnetic splitting.
