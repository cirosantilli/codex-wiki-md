# Exponential martingale of a pure birth process

↑ **Parent:** [Birth process](birth-process.md)

For a [pure birth process](birth-process.md) with fixed initial state and finite statewise rates, the jump-count compensator gives $dM=(e^\theta-1)M_{-}[dN-\lambda(X_{-})dt]$, so $M$ is a [local martingale](local-martingale.md) up to explosion. If $\lambda\le C$, a rate-$C$ Poisson candidate clock with accepted jumps of probability $\lambda(X_{-})/C$ prevents explosion and dominates the jump count. Its finite exponential moments dominate every stopped $M$ on each finite horizon, upgrading the local martingale to a true martingale. A random initial state requires $\mathbb E e^{\theta X_0}<\infty$; uniformly bounded rates do not supply this missing initial integrability.
