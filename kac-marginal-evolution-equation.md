# Kac marginal evolution equation

↑ **Parent:** [Kac master equation](kac-master-equation.md)

For a permutation-symmetric density in the [Kac master equation](kac-master-equation.md), integrate over all but the first velocity. Collisions among integrated coordinates cancel. The $N-1$ pairs containing the first coordinate give equal terms, with coefficient $N(N-1)/(2\pi\binom N2)=1/\pi$. The resulting exact evolution depends on the two-coordinate [marginal distribution](marginal-distribution.md); it is not a closed equation for the first marginal without additional asymptotic independence.
