# Simply connected space

↑ **Parent:** [Fundamental group](fundamental-group.md)

A simply connected space is path connected and has trivial [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md): every closed loop contracts to a point. The [three-sphere](three-sphere.md) is simply connected, whereas [Real projective space](real-projective-space.md) $\mathbb{RP}^3$ has [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md) $\mathbb Z_2$.
