# Theta divisor

↑ **Parent:** [Jacobian variety](jacobian-variety.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theta_divisor)

The [theta divisor](theta-divisor.md) consists of degree-$g-1$ [line bundles](line-bundle.md) with a nonzero [global section](global-section.md). It is the irreducible image of the [symmetric product of a curve](symmetric-product-of-a-curve.md) $C^{(g-1)}$, with its reduced [Cartier divisor](cartier-divisor-split.md) structure. The [determinant description of the theta divisor](determinant-description-of-the-theta-divisor.md) supplies equations, multiplicities and pullbacks in families.
