# Shifted-exponential Poisson count recursion

↑ **Parent:** [Shifted-exponential Poisson mixture](shifted-exponential-poisson-mixture.md)

For the [shifted-exponential Poisson mixture](shifted-exponential-poisson-mixture.md), the displayed relation holds for $n\ge2$, initialized by $p_0=e^{-\alpha}\beta/(\beta+1)$ and $p_1=(\alpha+1/(\beta+1))p_0$. It follows by differentiating the [probability generating function](probability-generating-function.md) and comparing coefficients. Nonnegativity follows from the independent Poisson-geometric [convolution of independent random variables](convolution-of-independent-random-variables.md) representation.
