# Quota share comparison at matched retained variance

↑ **Parent:** [Quota share reinsurance](quota-share-reinsurance.md)

Suppose $S=S_I+S_R$ has finite positive [variance](variance-split.md) $v$, and a matching [quota share reinsurance](quota-share-reinsurance.md) contract exists with fraction $\alpha^*=\sqrt{\operatorname{Var}(S_I)/v}\in[0,1]$. The [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) bounds $\operatorname{Cov}(S,S_I)\leq\alpha^*v$. Expanding $\operatorname{Var}(S-S_I)$ then proves that the matching quota share minimizes the other party's [variance](variance-split.md), and hence the sum of party [variances](variance-split.md). Claimwise retentions $0\leq h(x)\leq x$ in a [compound Poisson distribution](compound-poisson-distribution.md) automatically satisfy the required [variance](variance-split.md) range, because $\operatorname{Var}(S_I)=\lambda\mathbb E[h(X)^2]\leq\lambda\mathbb E[X^2]$.
