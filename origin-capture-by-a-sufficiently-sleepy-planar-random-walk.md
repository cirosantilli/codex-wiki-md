# Origin capture by a sufficiently sleepy planar random walk

↑ **Parent:** [Simple symmetric random walk](simple-symmetric-random-walk.md)

Suppose two independent planar [simple symmetric random walks](simple-symmetric-random-walk.md) are compared while one stays at its $j$th position throughout a deterministic time block. If each block has an unconditional probability bounded below by a positive constant that the other walk visits the origin, simultaneous origin visits occur infinitely often almost surely. [Markov-chain recurrence](recurrent-markov-chain.md) gives infinitely many origin blocks for the sleepy walk. The block bound yields positive probability of infinitely many coincidences; [eventual coupling of planar symmetric random walks](eventual-coupling-of-planar-symmetric-random-walks.md) then makes this future event have probability zero or one. Conditional block bounds or independence of capture attempts are not required.
