# Precision matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Covariance matrix](covariance-matrix.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Precision_matrix)

The precision matrix is the inverse of a nonsingular [covariance matrix](covariance-matrix.md). For a [multivariate normal distribution](multivariate-normal-distribution.md), a zero off-diagonal precision entry encodes conditional independence of the corresponding coordinates given all remaining coordinates.
