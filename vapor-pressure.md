# Vapor pressure

↑ **Parent:** [Phase coexistence curve](phase-coexistence-curve.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vapor_pressure)

The equilibrium [pressure](pressure.md) of a vapor in contact with its [liquid](liquid.md) or [solid](solid.md) at a specified [temperature](temperature.md). For pure [liquid](liquid.md)–[gas](gas.md) [phase coexistence](phase-coexistence.md) with negligible interface effects, it is the [pressure](pressure.md) on their [phase coexistence curve](phase-coexistence-curve.md). In a [gas](gas.md) [mixture](mixture.md), distinguish the component's [partial pressure](partial-pressure.md) from the total [pressure](pressure.md); the pure-substance [Integrated Clausius-Clapeyron relation](integrated-clausius-clapeyron-relation.md) does not describe the total [pressure](pressure.md) of arbitrary humid air.
