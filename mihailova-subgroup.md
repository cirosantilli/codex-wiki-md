# Mihailova subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Fiber product of groups](fiber-product-of-groups.md)

For a [finite group presentation](finite-group-presentation.md) $H=\langle x_1,\ldots,x_m\mid r_1,\ldots,r_n\rangle$, let $F$ be the free group on its generators and $\pi:F\to H$. The corresponding [fiber product of groups](fiber-product-of-groups.md) is generated by $(x_i,x_i)$ and $(1,r_j)$. Conjugation by the diagonal pairs supplies every pair $(1,r)$ with $r$ in the [normal closure](normal-closure.md) of the relators, and $(u,v)=(u,u)(1,u^{-1}v)$ proves the generating claim. Membership of $(1,w)$ is equivalent to $w=1$ in $H$, so this finitely generated subgroup can have undecidable membership even though $F\times F$ has soluble word problem.
