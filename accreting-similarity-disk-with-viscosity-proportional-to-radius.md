# Accreting similarity disk with viscosity proportional to radius

↑ **Parent:** [Heat-equation transform for linear-radius disk viscosity](heat-equation-transform-for-linear-radius-disk-viscosity.md)

For $\bar\nu=Ar$ with $A>0$, use $x=2\sqrt{r/(3A)}$ and $g=Ar^{3/2}\Sigma$. The [Keplerian viscous diffusion equation](keplerian-viscous-diffusion-equation.md) becomes $g_t=g_{xx}$. The [zero-torque inner boundary condition](zero-torque-inner-boundary-condition.md) is $g(0,t)=0$, and the positive solution $g=Cxt^{-3/2}e^{-x^2/(4t)}$ gives $\Sigma\propto r^{-1}t^{-3/2}e^{-r/(3At)}$. Its mass is proportional to $t^{-1/2}$, [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) is constant and inward mass flow at the origin is proportional to $t^{-3/2}$. Its radial [velocity](velocity.md) is $r/t-3A/2$, exhibiting inner accretion and outer spreading.
