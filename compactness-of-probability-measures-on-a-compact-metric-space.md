# Compactness of probability measures on a compact metric space

↑ **Parent:** [Prokhorov's theorem](prokhorov-s-theorem.md)

On a [compact metric space](compact-metric-space.md), every sequence of [Borel probability measures](borel-probability-measure.md) has a subsequence with [weak convergence of probability measures](weak-convergence-of-probability-measures.md) to a [Borel probability measure](borel-probability-measure.md). To see this, choose a countable uniformly dense subset of continuous functions, extract a diagonal subsequence of their bounded integrals, and extend the resulting positive normalized functional to all continuous functions. The [Riesz representation theorem](riesz-representation-theorem.md) supplies its [Borel probability measure](borel-probability-measure.md). This is useful for constructing an [invariant measure](invariant-measure.md) from orbit [empirical measures](empirical-measure.md).
