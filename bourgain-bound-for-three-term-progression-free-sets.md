# Bourgain bound for three-term-progression-free sets

↑ **Parent:** [Density increment](density-increment.md)

If $G$ is a finite [abelian group](abelian-group.md) of odd order $N$ and $A\subseteq G$ contains no nonconstant three-term [arithmetic progression](arithmetic-progression.md), then Bourgain's bound is

$$
|A|\ll N\left(\frac{\log\log N}{\log N}\right)^{1/2}.
$$
