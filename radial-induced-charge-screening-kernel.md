# Radial induced-charge screening kernel

↑ **Parent:** [Dielectric screening](dielectric-screening.md)

For a point source $Ze$ and the [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md) convention $\rho(\mathbf q)=\int e^{-i\mathbf q\cdot\mathbf r}\rho(\mathbf r)d^3r$, self-consistent screening gives $\rho_{\rm ind}(q)=Ze[\epsilon(q)^{-1}-1]$. Angular inversion yields $\rho_{\rm ind}(r)=-(Ze/r)\int_0^\infty g(q)\sin(qr)dq$. The factor $1/(2\pi^2)$ includes the inverse-transform normalization and spherical angular integral. Perfect long-wavelength screening implies total induced charge $-Ze$.
