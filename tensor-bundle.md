# Tensor bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Vector bundle](vector-bundle.md)

For a [smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md) $M$, the tensor bundle $T^r_sM=(TM)^{\otimes r}\otimes(T^*M)^{\otimes s}$ is a [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) whose smooth [sections of a vector bundle](section-of-a-vector-bundle.md) are [tensor fields](tensor-field.md) of type $(r,s)$. Its [transition functions of a vector bundle](transition-function-of-a-vector-bundle.md) are tensor products of the [tangent bundle](tangent-bundle.md) transition matrices and their inverse transposes. [Symmetric tensors](symmetric-tensor.md) and [differential forms](differential-form-split.md) arise from the symmetric and alternating subbundles.
