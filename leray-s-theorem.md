<h1 id="leray-s-theorem">Leray's theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Čech cohomology](cech-cohomology.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leray's_theorem)

If every nonempty finite intersection of members of an open cover has vanishing higher [sheaf cohomology](sheaf-cohomology.md) for $\mathcal F$, then the cover's [Čech cohomology](cech-cohomology.md) computes $H^p(X,\mathcal F)$. An affine open cover of a separated scheme satisfies this condition for a quasi-coherent sheaf because its finite intersections are affine.
