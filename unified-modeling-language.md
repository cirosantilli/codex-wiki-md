# Unified Modeling Language

↑ **Parent:** [Software engineering](software-engineering.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unified_Modeling_Language)

A modeling language for describing software structures and behavior. A [class diagram](class-diagram.md) records types and relationships; a [sequence diagram](sequence-diagram.md) records ordered interactions; a [state diagram](state-diagram.md) records states and event-triggered transitions.
