# Bogomolny bound for the inverse-density Abelian Higgs model

↑ **Parent:** [Inverse-density Abelian Higgs model](inverse-density-abelian-higgs-model.md)

Let $j_i=\operatorname{Im}(\bar\Phi D_i\Phi)$. The identity

$$
|D\Phi|^2=|(D_1+iD_2)\Phi|^2+Bh+\partial_1j_2-\partial_2j_1
$$

and [completing the square](completing-the-square.md) give, when the current boundary integral vanishes,

$$
V=\frac12\int\left[
|(D_1+iD_2)\Phi|^2+
\frac{(B-\tfrac12h(1-h))^2}{h}\right]+\frac12\int B.
$$

For flux $2\pi N>0$, the lower bound is $\pi N$, saturated by the two first-order [Bogomolny equations](bogomolny-equations.md). Division by $h$ is interpreted in the finite-energy sense at its zeros.
