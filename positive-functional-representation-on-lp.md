# Positive functional representation on Lp

↑ **Parent:** [Duality of Lp spaces](duality-of-lp-spaces.md)

On a finite measure space, a positive functional $\Lambda\in(L^p)^*$ defines the finite measure $\nu(E)=\Lambda(\mathbf1_E)$. The [Radon-Nikodym theorem](radon-nikodym-theorem.md) gives $d\nu=\omega\,d\mu$ with $\omega\geq0$. The bound

$$
\int|f|\omega=\Lambda(|f|)
\leq\|\Lambda\|\,\|f\|_p
$$

and $L^p$-$L^q$ norm duality imply $\omega\in L^q$, after which density extends the integral representation to every $f\in L^p$.
