# Galaxy-conditioned neighbour count

↑ **Parent:** [Galaxy two-point correlation function](galaxy-two-point-correlation-function.md)

The mean number of other [galaxies](galaxy-split.md) within distance $R$ of a randomly chosen [galaxy](galaxy-split.md) is the integral of its conditional number density. A random location instead has mean count $4\pi\bar nR^3/3$. For a [power law](power-law.md) $\xi(r)=(r_0/r)^\gamma$, the extra count is $4\pi\bar n r_0^\gamma R^{3-\gamma}/(3-\gamma)$ when $\gamma<3$. The central [galaxy](galaxy-split.md) is excluded; including it adds one. This distinction matters even when the unconditional mean number density is spatially uniform.
