# Finiteness of lines on a smooth surface of degree at least three

↑ **Parent:** [Fano scheme of lines](fano-scheme-of-lines.md)

For a line $L$ on a degree-$d$ smooth surface in $\mathbb P^3$, the [adjunction formula](adjunction-formula.md) gives $L^2=2-d$. If $d\geq3$, all lines are [negative curves](negative-curve-on-a-projective-surface.md), hence there are only countably many by [countability of negative curves](countability-of-negative-curves.md). Their projective [Fano scheme of lines](fano-scheme-of-lines.md) cannot have a positive-dimensional complex component, since such a component has uncountably many points. A zero-dimensional projective scheme of finite type has finitely many points, proving the assertion.
