# Electric dipole transition

↑ **Parent:** [Electric-dipole interaction](electric-dipole-interaction.md)

An [electric dipole transition](electric-dipole-transition.md) is a transition driven, in the long-wavelength approximation, by the [electric-dipole interaction](electric-dipole-interaction.md) $H_{\mathrm{int}}=-\mathbf d\cdot\mathbf E(t)$. Its leading amplitude is proportional to a [matrix element](matrix-element.md) of the [electric dipole moment](electric-dipole-moment.md) between the initial and final states. A vanishing element gives an [electric-dipole selection rule](electric-dipole-selection-rule.md); a nonzero element alone does not ensure a large probability, since the drive frequency, duration and strength also matter. For a charged [quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md) driven along its axis, $x=\sqrt{\hbar/(2m\omega)}(a+a^\dagger)$ gives the single-quantum rule $\Delta n=\pm1$.
