<h1 id="goldberger-wise-stabilization-mechanism">Goldberger–Wise stabilization mechanism</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Radius stabilization](radius-stabilization.md)

The [Goldberger–Wise stabilization mechanism](goldberger-wise-stabilization-mechanism.md) generates a [radion](radion.md) potential in the [Randall–Sundrum model](randall-sundrum-model.md) using a weakly backreacting bulk [scalar field](scalar-field.md). On $0\leq y\leq L$, its static profile obeys $\chi''-4k\chi'-m_\chi^2\chi=0$, hence $\chi=Ae^{(4+\epsilon)ky}+Be^{-\epsilon ky}$, where $\epsilon=\sqrt{4+m_\chi^2/k^2}-2$. Boundary potentials approximately fix $\chi(0)=v_0$, $\chi(L)=v_L$. For $\epsilon\ll1$ and $kL\gg1$, the leading radius-dependent potential is proportional to $e^{-4kL}(v_L-v_0e^{-\epsilon kL})^2$. Its leading minimum satisfies $kL\simeq\epsilon^{-1}\log(v_0/v_L)$. Thus a modest ratio of boundary values can generate a large warp exponent. Corrections shift this minimum; the approximation requires small [scalar field](scalar-field.md) backreaction, and the four-dimensional [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md) still needs adjustment.
