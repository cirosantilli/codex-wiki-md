# Wavelet series

↑ **Parent:** [Wavelet](wavelet.md)

For an [orthonormal wavelet](orthonormal-wavelet.md) and its [scaling function](scaling-function.md), set $\phi_{j,k}(x)=2^{j/2}\phi(2^jx-k)$ and similarly for $\psi$. The coarse scaling functions together with nonnegative-resolution wavelets form an [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) of $L^2(\mathbb R)$. The coefficients are the corresponding [L2 inner products](l2-inner-product.md). The series converges in the [L2 norm](l2-norm.md), and the [Parseval identity for a Hilbertian basis](parseval-identity-for-a-hilbertian-basis.md) equates squared norm with the sum of squared coefficients. Pointwise convergence requires additional hypotheses.
