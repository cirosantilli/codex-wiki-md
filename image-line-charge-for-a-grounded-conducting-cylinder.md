# Image line charge for a grounded conducting cylinder

↑ **Parent:** [Method of images](method-of-images.md)

A real infinite [line charge](line-charge.md) $\lambda$ lies at distance $b>a$ from the axis of a grounded conducting cylinder of radius $a$. An image line $-\lambda$ at radius $a^2/b$ lies inside the excluded conductor. On the circle, real and image distances obey $r_1/r_2=b/a$, so $\phi=(\lambda/(2\pi\epsilon_0))\ln[(b/a)r_2/r_1]$ vanishes there. The image field solves the physical exterior problem with bounded far potential. Its additive constant matters; the interior image field is not a physical field inside the conductor.
