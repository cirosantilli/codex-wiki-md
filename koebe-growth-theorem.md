# Koebe growth theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Koebe distortion theorem](koebe-distortion-theorem.md)

For a [normalized univalent function](normalized-univalent-function.md) $f$ on the [unit disc](unit-disc.md), put $r=|z|<1$. The displayed sharp two-sided bounds hold. Rotations $z/(1-e^{i\theta}z)^2$ of the [Koebe function](koebe-function.md) attain the bounds along suitable radial directions. In particular, bounding the [modulus](modulus.md) controls the radius of a disk containing each smaller image $f(r\mathbb D)$.
