# Serre derivative

↑ **Parent:** [Modular form](modular-form.md)

The Serre derivative sends a level-one weight-$k$ [modular form](modular-form.md) to weight $k+2$. Differentiating $f(-1/z)=z^kf(z)$ produces the extra term $kz^{k+1}f/(2\pi i)$; the anomalous transformation of the [Eisenstein series of weight two](eisenstein-series-of-weight-two.md) cancels it. Translation invariance and the [Fourier expansion of a modular form](fourier-expansion-of-a-modular-form.md) verify the remaining holomorphy conditions. For $k>0$, its constant coefficient is $-ka_0(f)/12$, so it is a [cusp form](cusp-form.md) exactly when $f$ is.
