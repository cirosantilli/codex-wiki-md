# Eddington-Sweet circulation

↑ **Parent:** [Meridional circulation in a star](meridional-circulation-in-a-star.md)

The thermal imbalance of a slowly rotating radiative [star](star.md) can drive [stellar meridional circulation](meridional-circulation-in-a-star.md). Its fractional centrifugal distortion is $\epsilon_\Omega\sim\Omega^2R^3/(GM)$, so balancing an order-$\epsilon_\Omega$ fraction of the [luminosity](luminosity.md) by thermal advection estimates $t_{\rm ES}\sim t_{\rm KH}/\epsilon_\Omega$, where $t_{\rm KH}\sim GM^2/(RL)$. Stable composition gradients can further impede the circulation. This radiative thermal estimate is different from rapid torque-driven circulation in a convective envelope.
