# Riemann-Roch via elementary modifications

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann-Roch theorem](riemann-roch-theorem.md)

For a closed point $p$ on a [smooth projective curve](smooth-projective-curve.md), the quotient in $0\to L(-p)\to L\to L|_p\to0$ has $k$-dimension $[k(p):k]$. Additivity of [Euler characteristic](euler-characteristic.md) therefore changes $\chi(L)$ by that residue-field degree. Every [line bundle](line-bundle.md) is associated with a [divisor](divisor.md), so iteration proves the displayed formula. The [dualizing sheaf on a smooth projective curve](dualizing-sheaf-on-a-smooth-projective-curve.md) identifies $h^1(L)$ with $h^0(\Omega^1_C\otimes L^{-1})$ and converts this Euler-characteristic formula into the usual divisor form of [Riemann-Roch theorem](riemann-roch-theorem.md).
