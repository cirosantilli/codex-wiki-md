# Scalar triangle Feynman integral

↑ **Parent:** [Triangle Feynman diagram](triangle-feynman-diagram.md)

In mostly-minus signature, take $D_j=(\ell+r_j)^2-m^2+i0$. [Feynman parameters](feynman-parameter.md) and a momentum shift give

$$
C=-\frac{i}{16\pi^2}\int_{x_j\ge0}dx_0dx_1dx_2\,\delta(1-\textstyle\sum_jx_j)\left[m^2-\sum_{i<j}x_ix_j(r_i-r_j)^2-i0\right]^{-1}.
$$

For massive internal lines this is ultraviolet finite in four dimensions. Physical external kinematics can still create threshold cuts, encoded by the [Feynman i-epsilon prescription](feynman-i-epsilon-prescription.md).
