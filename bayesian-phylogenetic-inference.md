# Bayesian phylogenetic inference

↑ **Parent:** [Phylogenetics](phylogenetics.md)

Inference of a [posterior distribution](bayesian-posterior.md) over [phylogenetic trees](phylogenetic-tree.md) and model parameters by combining a sequence [likelihood function](likelihood-function.md) with a [prior distribution](prior-probability.md). Integrating nuisance parameters is distinct from maximizing the likelihood.
