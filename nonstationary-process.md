# Nonstationary process

↑ **Parent:** [Stationary process](stationary-process.md)

A process is nonstationary if some finite-dimensional distribution changes under a common time shift. With finite second moments, failure of a constant mean or variance, or failure of lag-only covariance, already rules out a [weakly stationary process](weakly-stationary-process.md). A periodic mean or periodic variance can instead give a [periodically correlated process](periodically-correlated-process.md).
