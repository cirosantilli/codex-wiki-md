<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [covariance of a selected particle momentum sum](../../../../covariance-of-a-selected-particle-momentum-sum.md) follows because, for a fixed set of particle [four-momenta](../../../../four-momentum.md), their sum $P_A^\mu=\sum_{i\in A}p_i^\mu$ is a [four-vector](../../../../four-vector.md) by linearity of the [Lorentz transformation](../../../../lorentz-transformation.md). Therefore

$$
\boxed{\left(\sum_{i\in A}E_i\right)^2-c^2\left|\sum_{i\in A}\mathbf p_i\right|^2
=-c^2P_A^\mu P_{A\mu}}
$$

is invariant. The subset need not comprise the entire plasma, and its particles need not move together. This expression includes cross terms between different particles; it is not the sum of their individual rest-mass squares in [a](../a.md).

The comparison must use the same subset and the transformed [four-momenta](../../../../four-momentum.md) at the same chosen events. Selecting “the lowest-energy half” anew in each frame need not do that. For example, take four [photons](../../../../photon.md) along $\pm x$, with energies $E_0,2E_0$ in each direction. The two lowest in the original frame are an opposite pair and have invariant [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) squared $4E_0^2$. After a boost at $c/2$ along $+x$, both forward [photons](../../../../photon.md) are redshifted enough to become the lowest pair; that new collinear pair has zero [invariant mass](../../../../invariant-mass.md). This does not contradict covariance, because the subset changed.

If energies are resampled on a different [simultaneity](../../../../simultaneity.md) slice in an interacting plasma, they can also refer to different events. A physical conserved subsystem total must then include exchanged field [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) and any boundary flux as appropriate. Such operational conservation requirements are distinct from the exact algebraic covariance of a sum of fixed [four-vectors](../../../../four-vector.md).
