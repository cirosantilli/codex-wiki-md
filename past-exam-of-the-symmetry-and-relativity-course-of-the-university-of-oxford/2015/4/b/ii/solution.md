<h1 id="4/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Use the same event pair already made simultaneous in the rear [instantaneous rest frame](../../../../../instantaneous-rest-frame.md). Its proper spatial separation is the [Minkowski interval](../../../../../minkowski-interval.md) length, so

$$
\boxed{[h(t_B)-f(t_A)]^2-c^2(t_B-t_A)^2=L^2,\qquad
h(t_B)-f(t_A)>0.}
$$

The inequality selects the front rocket rather than the equally long backward extension. Equivalently the Lorentz-transformed spatial difference obeys $\gamma[h(t_B)-f(t_A)-v(t_B-t_A)]=L$. This is not the condition $h(t)-f(t)=L$ at equal laboratory times, which would describe the stretching arrangement in [a](../../a.md).
