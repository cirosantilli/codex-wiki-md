<h1 id="1/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Relativistic [relativistic aberration](../../../../../relativistic-aberration.md) concentrates emission into an angular scale

$$
\boxed{\Delta\theta\sim\gamma^{-1}=1.70\times10^{-4}\ \mathrm{rad}
=0.170\ \mathrm{mrad}.}
$$

This is a half-angle or characteristic divergence scale; a full cone opening is about twice it. The exact half-photon-count cone in [a](../../a.md) applies to isotropic rest-frame emission. A synchrotron emitter has its own rest-frame angular radiation pattern, so that count result should not be treated as an exact median-power formula for the synchrotron beam. It nevertheless gives the correct [relativistic beaming](../../../../../relativistic-beaming.md) scale. Electron-beam emittance, vertical angular spread and the detector's acceptance can broaden the experimentally observed divergence beyond this intrinsic estimate.
