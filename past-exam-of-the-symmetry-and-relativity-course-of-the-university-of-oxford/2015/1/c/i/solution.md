<h1 id="1/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Interpret $3$ GeV as the [electron](../../../../../electron.md)'s total [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md); the alternative kinetic-energy reading changes the estimate only slightly at this scale. The [Lorentz factor](../../../../../lorentz-factor.md) and orbital acceleration are

$$
\gamma=\frac{3000}{0.510999}=5.871\times10^3,\qquad
a=\frac{v^2}{R},\qquad a_0=\gamma^2\frac{v^2}{R}.
$$

Using the supplied [Larmor formula](../../../../../larmor-formula.md), $\mu_0=1/(\epsilon_0c^2)$ and the revolution time $2\pi R/v$,

$$
P=\frac{e^2\gamma^4v^4}{6\pi\epsilon_0c^3R^2},\qquad
\boxed{U_{\rm turn}=\frac{e^2}{3\epsilon_0R}\gamma^4\beta^3.}
$$

For $R=561$ m this gives $P=1.7405\times10^{-10}$ W per [electron](../../../../../electron.md) and $T_{\rm turn}=1.17577\times10^{-5}$ s. Their product is

$$
\boxed{U_{\rm turn}=2.0464\times10^{-15}\ \mathrm J
=1.2773\times10^4\ \mathrm{eV}\simeq12.8\ \mathrm{keV}.}
$$

This is the [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) loss of one [electron](../../../../../electron.md) on a full circular orbit, not its [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) loss per metre or the total loss of the whole bunch. The fractional loss is only about $4.26\times10^{-6}$ of the $3$ GeV [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) each revolution. Maintaining a steady stored [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) requires replacing that [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) through the accelerator's rf system.
