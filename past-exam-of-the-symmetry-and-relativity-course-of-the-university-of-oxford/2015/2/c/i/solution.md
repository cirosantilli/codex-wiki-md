<h1 id="2/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For the source's definition $s^\alpha=\gamma(\mathbf s\cdot\mathbf v/c,\mathbf s)$, direct contraction with $u^\alpha=\gamma(c,\mathbf v)$ gives

$$
s\cdot u=-\gamma^2\mathbf s\cdot\mathbf v+\gamma^2\mathbf s\cdot\mathbf v=0.
$$

It remains zero under the torque-free transport law. Differentiate the contraction, using the product rule and $u^2=-c^2$:

$$
\boxed{\frac d{d\tau}(s\cdot u)
=\frac{s\cdot\dot u}{c^2}u^2+s\cdot\dot u=0.}
$$

Thus an initially spatial [spin](../../../../../spin.md) in the [instantaneous rest frame](../../../../../instantaneous-rest-frame.md) remains orthogonal to the velocity. This is the orthogonal-spin specialization of [Fermi-Walker transport](../../../../../fermi-walker-transport.md). The sign is tied to the negative timelike norm; using the opposite metric sign without changing the transport formula would spoil the cancellation.
