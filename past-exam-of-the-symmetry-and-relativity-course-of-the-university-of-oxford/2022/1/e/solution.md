<h1 id="1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Let $s=k^\mu x_\mu$. The [Lorenz gauge condition](../../../../lorenz-gauge-condition.md) on the [plane electromagnetic wave](../../../../plane-electromagnetic-wave.md) gives

$$
k_\mu\epsilon^\mu=0.
$$

The vacuum [wave equation](../../../../wave-equation-split.md) gives $k_\mu k^\mu=0$ for a nonzero amplitude; equivalently $k^0=\omega/c$ and $|\mathbf k|=\omega/c$. Use the [gauge transformation](../../../../gauge-transformation.md) $A'^\mu=A^\mu+\partial^\mu\chi$ with

$$
\chi=-\lambda\cos s,\qquad \epsilon'^\mu=\epsilon^\mu+\lambda k^\mu.
$$

Because $\Box\chi=0$, this is a [residual Lorenz gauge symmetry](../../../../residual-lorenz-gauge-symmetry.md) and $k_\mu\epsilon'^\mu=0$. For a propagating [plane electromagnetic wave](../../../../plane-electromagnetic-wave.md), $k^0\ne0$, so take $\lambda=-\epsilon^0/k^0$. Then

$$
\boxed{\epsilon'^0=0,\qquad \mathbf k\cdot\boldsymbol\epsilon'=0.}
$$

The second equality follows from $-k^0\epsilon'^0+\mathbf k\cdot\boldsymbol\epsilon'=0$. The [electric field](../../../../electric-field-split.md) and [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) are unchanged by this [gauge transformation](../../../../gauge-transformation.md). Thus [Lorenz gauge](../../../../lorenz-gauge-condition.md) permits a representative in [radiation gauge](../../../../radiation-gauge.md); [Lorenz gauge](../../../../lorenz-gauge-condition.md) alone does not require the spatial amplitude of every representative to be transverse.
