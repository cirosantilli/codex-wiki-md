<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

By [four-momentum conservation](../../../../four-momentum-conservation.md), $p_\pi=p_1+p_2$. With the $(-,+,+,+)$ [Minkowski metric](../../../../minkowski-metric.md), the [neutral pion](../../../../neutral-pion.md) has $p_\pi^2=-M_\pi^2$, while each [photon](../../../../photon.md) has $p_i^2=0$ and $|\mathbf p_i|=E_i$. Taking the [Minkowski norm](../../../../minkowski-norm.md) gives

$$
-M_\pi^2=2p_1\cdot p_2
=2(-E_1E_2+\mathbf p_1\cdot\mathbf p_2)
=-2E_1E_2(1-\cos A).
$$

The half-angle identity therefore yields

$$
\boxed{\sin^2(A/2)=\frac{M_\pi^2}{4E_1E_2}.}
$$

This is a measurement of the [diphoton invariant mass](../../../../diphoton-invariant-mass.md) using two [photon](../../../../photon.md) [energies](../../../../energy-split.md) and their opening angle, independent of the [neutral pion](../../../../neutral-pion.md)'s laboratory [velocity](../../../../velocity.md).
