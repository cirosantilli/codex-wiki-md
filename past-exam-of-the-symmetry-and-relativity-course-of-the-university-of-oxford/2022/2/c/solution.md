<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

In the [center-of-momentum frame](../../../../center-of-momentum-frame.md), [four-momentum conservation](../../../../four-momentum-conservation.md) gives two opposite [photon](../../../../photon.md) momenta, each of [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) $M_\pi/2$. Let $z=\cos\theta_*$, with the axis along the [Lorentz boost](../../../../lorentz-boost.md) from that frame to the laboratory. Reversing the axis specified in the source changes $z$ to $-z$ and has no effect on the following product. The [Lorentz transformation](../../../../lorentz-transformation.md) of [four-momentum](../../../../four-momentum.md) gives

$$
E_1=\frac{\gamma M_\pi}{2}(1+\beta z),\qquad
E_2=\frac{\gamma M_\pi}{2}(1-\beta z).
$$

Writing $s=\sin(A/2)$, [b](../b.md) now implies

$$
s^2=\frac1{\gamma^2(1-\beta^2z^2)},\qquad
|z|=\frac{\sqrt{\gamma^2s^2-1}}{\beta\gamma s}.
$$

The [isotropy](../../../../isotropy.md) assumption is $dN=a\,|dz|$ on $-1\le z\le1$. Each interior opening angle has **two** inverse branches, $z$ and $-z$, both of which must enter the [change of variables for probability densities](../../../../change-of-variables-for-probability-densities.md). The positive branch has [derivative](../../../../derivative.md)

$$
\left|\frac{dz}{dA}\right|
=\frac{\cos(A/2)}{2\beta\gamma\sin^2(A/2)\sqrt{\gamma^2\sin^2(A/2)-1}}.
$$

Consequently the [diphoton opening-angle distribution](../../../../diphoton-opening-angle-distribution.md) is

$$
\boxed{\frac{dN}{dA}=\frac a\gamma\frac{\cos(A/2)}{\beta\sin^2(A/2)\sqrt{\gamma^2\sin^2(A/2)-1}},\qquad f(\gamma)=\gamma^{-1}.}
$$

Its [support](../../../../support.md) is $2\arcsin(1/\gamma)<A<\pi$ for $0<\beta<1$, and its integral is $2a$, the integral of the original angular [probability density](../../../../probability-density.md) before normalization. Thus the normalized [probability density](../../../../probability-density.md) is $(2a)^{-1}dN/dA$. Omitting one inverse branch would incorrectly halve the total count.
