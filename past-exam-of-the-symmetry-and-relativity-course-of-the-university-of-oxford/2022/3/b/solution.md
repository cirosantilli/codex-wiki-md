<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [Lorentz boost](../../../../lorentz-boost.md) from A to B gives

$$
dt'=\gamma_v(dt-v\,dx/c^2),\quad dx'=\gamma_v(dx-v\,dt),\quad dy'=dy,\quad dz'=dz.
$$

Divide each spatial displacement by $dt'=\gamma_vdt(1-vu_x/c^2)$. The [velocity-addition formula](../../../../velocity-addition-formula.md) is

$$
\boxed{w_x=\frac{u_x-v}{1-vu_x/c^2},\qquad
w_y=\frac{u_y}{\gamma_v(1-vu_x/c^2)},\qquad
w_z=\frac{u_z}{\gamma_v(1-vu_x/c^2)}.}
$$

The same result follows by transforming the [four-velocity](../../../../four-velocity.md) and using $w_i=cU'^i/U'^0$. The transverse [velocities](../../../../velocity.md) change because the transformed time increment depends on $dx$, even though the transverse spatial coordinates are unchanged.
