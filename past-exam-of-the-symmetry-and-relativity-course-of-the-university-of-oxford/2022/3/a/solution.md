<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a massive particle, the [proper time](../../../../proper-time.md) is determined by the [Minkowski interval](../../../../minkowski-interval.md) along its [worldline](../../../../world-line.md):

$$
-c^2d\tau^2=-c^2dt^2+d\mathbf x\cdot d\mathbf x
=-c^2dt^2(1-|\mathbf u|^2/c^2).
$$

Choose future-directed time increments. With the [Lorentz factor](../../../../lorentz-factor.md) $\gamma_u=(1-|\mathbf u|^2/c^2)^{-1/2}$,

$$
\boxed{d\tau=dt/\gamma_u,\qquad U^\alpha=\frac{dx^\alpha}{d\tau}=\gamma_u(c,\mathbf u).}
$$

We use $U^\alpha$ for the source's $u^\alpha$ to distinguish [four-velocity](../../../../four-velocity.md) from [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) $\mathbf u$. Since [proper time](../../../../proper-time.md) is invariant and $dx^\alpha$ is a [four-vector](../../../../four-vector.md), $U^\alpha$ is a [four-vector](../../../../four-vector.md), with $U_\alpha U^\alpha=-c^2$. The rest-frame result $U^\alpha=(c,\mathbf0)$ follows immediately.
