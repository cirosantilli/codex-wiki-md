<h1 id="1/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For the standard passive [Lorentz boost](../../../../../lorentz-boost.md), write $\beta=v/c$ and

$$
ct'=\gamma(ct-\beta x),\qquad x'=\gamma(x-\beta ct).
$$

A [proper orthochronous Lorentz transformation](../../../../../proper-orthochronous-lorentz-transformation.md) preserves the sign of the time component of a nonzero future [causal vector](../../../../../causal-vector.md) and preserves its [Minkowski norm](../../../../../minkowski-norm.md). Therefore

$$
\boxed{J^+(0)=\{(t',x'):ct'\ge|x'|\}.}
$$

The upper-right panel of part [c](../../c.md) uses $x'$ horizontally and $ct'$ vertically. Its null rays still have slopes $\pm1$. The grey dashed old axes satisfy $x'=-\beta ct'$ for the old time axis and $ct'=-\beta x'$ for the old spatial axis; changing the coordinate grid has not changed which events are reachable.
