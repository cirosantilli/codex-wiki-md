<h1 id="1/d/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The invariant spacelike condition is $x'^2-c^2t'^2>0$. Hence the primed-coordinate events in neither the [causal future](../../../../../causal-future.md) nor the [causal past](../../../../../causal-past.md) obey

$$
\boxed{|x'|>c|t'|.}
$$

The green regions in part [c](../../c.md) retain their shape in primed coordinates. A particular spacelike event's coordinate [time](../../../../../time-in-physics.md) may change sign, but it never enters either [light cone](../../../../../light-cone.md).
