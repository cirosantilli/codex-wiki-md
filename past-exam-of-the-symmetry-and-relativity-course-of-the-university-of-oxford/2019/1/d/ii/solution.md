↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The same [Lorentz transformation](../../../../../lorentz-transformation.md) maps the past [light cone](../../../../../light-cone.md) to itself. In the primed axes the [causal past](../../../../../causal-past.md) is

$$
\boxed{J^-(0)=\{(t',x'):ct'\le-|x'|\},}
$$

the orange region in the upper-right [spacetime diagram](../../../../../spacetime-diagram.md) of part [c](../../c.md). Future and past are not interchanged by the standard boost.
