<h1 id="1/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A future-directed [causal curve](../../../../../causal-curve.md) from the origin has $|dx/dt|\le c$. Integrating this inequality gives the [causal future](../../../../../causal-future.md)

$$
\boxed{J^+(0)=\{(t,x):ct\ge|x|\}.}
$$

The straight timelike or null segment proves sufficiency. This is the blue region above the two null rays in the upper-left [spacetime diagram](../../../../../spacetime-diagram.md) of part [c](../../c.md).
