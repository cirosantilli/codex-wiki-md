<h1 id="1/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Outside both [light cones](../../../../../light-cone.md), the separation from the origin is spacelike. Thus the events in neither the [causal past](../../../../../causal-past.md) nor the [causal future](../../../../../causal-future.md) are

$$
\boxed{\{(t,x):|x|>c|t|\}.}
$$

No [causal curve](../../../../../causal-curve.md) can connect either endpoint to the other. These are the two green side regions in part [c](../../c.md); the strict inequality excludes the causally reachable null boundaries.
