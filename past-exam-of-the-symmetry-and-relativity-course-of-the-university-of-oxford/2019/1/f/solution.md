For [causal reachability with periodic time](../../../../causal-reachability-with-periodic-time.md), let $P_t=2\pi T$. The lower-right panel of part [c](../c.md) identifies its top and bottom edges. The vertical curve at fixed $x$ is a [closed timelike curve](../../../../closed-timelike-curve.md): after a positive proper duration $P_t$, it returns to the same [spacetime event](../../../../event-relativity.md). The quotient is a mathematically well-defined flat [Lorentzian manifold](../../../../lorentzian-manifold.md), but it violates chronology and admits no usual global causal ordering or ordinary global initial-value evolution.

These are physical causality problems, rather than an algebraic contradiction in the metric. Fields and particle histories must satisfy periodic consistency conditions; an arbitrary initial state followed by irreversible evolution will generally fail to return to itself after a temporal lap. The fact that the [causal past](../../../../causal-past.md) and [causal future](../../../../causal-future.md) overlap everywhere, established below, explains why the ordinary three-way causal partition degenerates.
