<h1 id="1/f/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Choose an integer $n$ sufficiently negative that $c(t+nP_t)<-|x|$. That lift lies in the ordinary [causal past](../../../../../causal-past.md) of the origin, and its projected [causal curve](../../../../../causal-curve.md) reaches the origin. Hence

$$
\boxed{J^-(0)=\text{the entire spacetime}.}
$$

Future-directed curves can connect the origin and an arbitrary event in either order. This property includes the origin's nontrivial [closed timelike curves](../../../../../closed-timelike-curve.md).
