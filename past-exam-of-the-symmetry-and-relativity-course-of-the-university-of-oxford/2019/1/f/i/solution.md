<h1 id="1/f/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For any representative event $(t,x)$, choose an integer $n$ large enough that $c(t+nP_t)>|x|$. A future-directed timelike segment from the origin to that lift projects to a [causal curve](../../../../../causal-curve.md) on the quotient. Therefore

$$
\boxed{J^+(0)=\text{the entire spacetime}.}
$$

Even points with large $|x|$ can be reached by allowing sufficiently many temporal windings. The purple lower-right panel in part [c](../../c.md) denotes simultaneous membership in both the [causal future](../../../../../causal-future.md) and [causal past](../../../../../causal-past.md).
