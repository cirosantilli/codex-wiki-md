<h1 id="1/f/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Every event is in both the [causal future](../../../../../causal-future.md) and [causal past](../../../../../causal-past.md), so

$$
\boxed{\text{events in neither causal set}=\varnothing.}
$$

Drawing just the nearest two null rays on a temporally identified strip would miss winding [causal curves](../../../../../causal-curve.md) and give an incorrect nonempty exterior region.
