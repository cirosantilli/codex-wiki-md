<h1 id="1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

For [causal reachability on a spatial circle](../../../../causal-reachability-on-a-spatial-circle.md), put $P=2\pi R$ and use a representative $\bar x\in[-P/2,P/2)$ of the spatial circle. The lower-left panel of part [c](../c.md) is a fundamental strip with its vertical edges identified. On the covering space the charged [world line](../../../../world-line.md) is still the solution of part [b](../b.md); on the quotient it is

$$
\bar x(t)=x(t)\pmod P.
$$

A jump between opposite strip edges is a coordinate seam, not a discontinuity of the physical [world line](../../../../world-line.md). At its $n$th complete lap, $x=nP$, so

$$
\boxed{\gamma_n=1+\frac{anP}{c^2},\qquad
t_n=\frac ca\sqrt{\left(1+\frac{anP}{c^2}\right)^2-1},\qquad
v_n=c\sqrt{1-\gamma_n^{-2}}.}
$$

For $a=qE_x/m>0$, the [speed](../../../../speed.md) increases on each return while remaining below $c$. Its [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) increases by

$$
\boxed{K_{n+1}-K_n=qE_xP.}
$$

There is no single-valued electrostatic [potential energy](../../../../potential-energy.md) $-qE_x\bar x$ on a circle: the nonzero [electromotive force](../../../../electromotive-force.md) is $\oint\mathbf E\cdot d\mathbf l=E_xP$. A globally defined [magnetic vector potential](../../../../magnetic-vector-potential.md) one-form $A_x=-E_xt$ instead makes the driving explicit. In an embedded circuit, [Faraday's law](../../../../faraday-s-law-of-induction.md) realizes this [electromotive force](../../../../electromotive-force.md) through changing magnetic flux. More generally an external apparatus maintaining the field supplies the increasing particle [energy](../../../../energy-split.md). In an isolated dynamical field-plus-particle system, backreaction would change the field. Thus this is not a cyclic machine extracting work from a conservative potential, and it does not violate [conservation of energy](../../../../conservation-of-energy.md).
