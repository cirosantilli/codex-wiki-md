↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The complement of the [causal future](../../../../../causal-future.md) and [causal past](../../../../../causal-past.md) is

$$
\boxed{c|t|<d(\bar x,0).}
$$

This green region is confined to $|t|<\pi R/c$ and meets the identified strip edges. Spatial periodicity creates alternative signal paths, but time remains noncompact and no [closed timelike curve](../../../../../closed-timelike-curve.md) follows from this spatial identification.
