$$
\boxed{J^-(0)=\{(t,\bar x):-ct\ge d(\bar x,0)\}.}
$$

For $t\le-\pi R/c$, the whole spatial circle can send a causal signal to the origin. The lower-left [spacetime diagram](../../../../../spacetime-diagram.md) in part [c](../../c.md) is therefore completely orange sufficiently far below the origin.
