<h1 id="1/e/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [causal curve](../../../../../causal-curve.md) can wind around the spatial circle. Lift its endpoint to $x+nP$ on the covering space. A lift is reachable from the origin if $ct\ge|x+nP|$ for at least one integer $n$. With shortest circular distance

$$
d(x,0)=\min_{n\in\mathbb Z}|x+nP|\le\frac P2,
$$

the [causal future](../../../../../causal-future.md) is

$$
\boxed{J^+(0)=\{(t,\bar x):ct\ge d(\bar x,0)\}.}
$$

In the centred fundamental strip, $d=|\bar x|$. For $t\ge P/(2c)=\pi R/c$, every spatial point is in the [causal future](../../../../../causal-future.md). This produces the fully blue upper part of the lower-left panel in part [c](../../c.md).
