<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use coordinates $X^\mu=(ct,\mathbf x)$ and the mostly-plus [Minkowski metric](../../../../minkowski-metric.md) $\eta_{\mu\nu}=\operatorname{diag}(-1,1,1,1)$. Along a future-directed timelike [world line](../../../../world-line.md), [proper time](../../../../proper-time.md) satisfies

$$
c^2d\tau^2=-\eta_{\mu\nu}dX^\mu dX^\nu=c^2dt^2-d\mathbf x^2,
\qquad d\tau=dt\sqrt{1-v^2/c^2}.
$$

The [four-velocity](../../../../four-velocity.md) and [four-acceleration](../../../../four-acceleration.md) are

$$
\boxed{U^\mu=\frac{dX^\mu}{d\tau}=\gamma(c,\mathbf v),\qquad
A^\mu=\frac{dU^\mu}{d\tau},\qquad\gamma=(1-v^2/c^2)^{-1/2}.}
$$

The [Minkowski norm](../../../../minkowski-norm.md) is fixed: $U^\mu U_\mu=-c^2$. Differentiating with respect to [proper time](../../../../proper-time.md), using the constant [Minkowski metric](../../../../minkowski-metric.md), gives

$$
0=\frac{d}{d\tau}(U^\mu U_\mu)=2U_\mu A^\mu,
\qquad \boxed{\mathbf U\cdot\mathbf A=0.}
$$

In the instantaneous rest [reference frame](../../../../reference-frame.md), $U^\mu=(c,0,0,0)$, so the orthogonality says $A^0=0$ there. This construction uses a timelike [world line](../../../../world-line.md); a [massless particle](../../../../massless-particle.md) has no rest-frame [proper time](../../../../proper-time.md) parameter.
