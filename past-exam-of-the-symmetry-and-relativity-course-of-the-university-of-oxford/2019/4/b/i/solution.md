<h1 id="4/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [pion](../../../../../pion.md) mass gives a [Yukawa potential](../../../../../yukawa-potential.md) range of order a femtometre, so meson exchange can describe the short range of the residual [nuclear force](../../../../../nuclear-force-split.md). The nuclear suppression relative to a massless potential of the same coupling is only about a factor of two at one femtometre. A Higgs-mass mediator instead has a range around $10^{-18}\,\mathrm m$ and a further factor $e^{-633}$ by nuclear separations; direct Higgs exchange cannot supply an appreciable long-range nuclear binding interaction at those separations. The range comparison does not replace the spin, coupling and many-body physics of actual nuclear forces.
