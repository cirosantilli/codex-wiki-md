<h1 id="4/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

At atomic separations both massive exchange potentials are exponentially negligible. Molecular binding needs an interaction that survives at atomic distances; actual [chemical bonds](../../../../../chemical-bond-split.md) is governed principally by [electromagnetism](../../../../../electromagnetism-split.md) and [quantum mechanics](../../../../../quantum-mechanics-split.md), with a massless [photon](../../../../../photon.md) rather than a scalar mediator. A hypothetical massless scalar would also have an unscreened $1/r$ potential, but its coupling and spin-dependent phenomenology would be different. The [Higgs mechanism](../../../../../higgs-mechanism.md) affects particle [masses](../../../../../mass.md); that does not imply an appreciable direct Higgs-exchange force between atoms separated by $10^{-10}\,\mathrm m$.
