<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use $\hbar=c=1$ and the mostly-plus [Minkowski metric](../../../../minkowski-metric.md). Then the source's [Klein-Gordon equation](../../../../klein-gordon-equation.md) is $(\partial_t^2-\nabla^2+m^2)\phi=0$. For a [plane wave](../../../../plane-wave.md) $\phi=C e^{-i\omega t+i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf x}$, substitution gives

$$
\boxed{\omega^2=|\mathbf k|^2+m^2.}
$$

Thus a real propagating [wave vector](../../../../wavevector.md) requires $|\omega|\ge m$, or $|E|\ge mc^2$ on restoring units. The positive-[energy](../../../../energy-split.md) branch has a rest-energy threshold $mc^2$. At $|\omega|=m$, $\mathbf k=0$; frequencies below the mass threshold give evanescent spatial dependence rather than real-wave-vector propagation. Both signs of $\omega$ occur in the classical equation; a real [scalar field](../../../../scalar-field.md) combines complex-conjugate modes. Its [group velocity](../../../../group-velocity.md) is $\mathbf k/\omega$ and is subluminal, though the positive-branch phase [speed](../../../../speed.md) exceeds one when $m>0$.

For a static spherically symmetric [scalar field](../../../../scalar-field.md), the equation outside the source is

$$
\frac1{r^2}\frac d{dr}\left(r^2\frac{d\phi}{dr}\right)-m^2\phi=0.
$$

Setting $\psi=r\phi$ gives $\psi''-m^2\psi=0$, so

$$
\boxed{\phi(r)=\frac{A e^{-mr}+B e^{mr}}r\quad(r>0).}
$$

Decay at infinity selects $B=0$, the [Yukawa potential](../../../../yukawa-potential.md). It is singular at the origin and describes a point source distributionally; a source-free solution regular at the origin and decaying at infinity must be zero. For $m=0$, $\phi=A/r+B$, and decay sets the constant $B$ to zero.
