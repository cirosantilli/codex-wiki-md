<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The initial [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) magnitude satisfies $p^2/(m^2c^2)=\gamma_i^2-1=288$. Equality of longitudinal and transverse magnitudes therefore gives

$$
p_{z,i}=p_\perp=12mc.
$$

An electric field along $z$ changes $p_z$, while the magnetic [Lorentz force](../../../../lorentz-force.md) only rotates the transverse momentum. Its magnitude $p_\perp$ remains fixed. At the longitudinal turning point $p_z=0$, hence

$$
\boxed{\gamma_f=\sqrt{1+\frac{p_\perp^2}{m^2c^2}}=\sqrt{145}=12.04159.}
$$

For an electron initially moving toward positive $z$, the decelerating field points toward positive $z$ because its charge is $-e$. The electric work over $\Delta z=1\,\mathrm m$ gives

$$
-eE_z\Delta z=(\gamma_f-17)mc^2,
\qquad
\boxed{E_z=2.53375\,\mathrm{MV\,m^{-1}}.}
$$

The radius is determined by transverse momentum rather than total [energy](../../../../energy-split.md):

$$
\boxed{R(z)=\frac{p_\perp}{e|B|}=\frac{12mc}{e|B|},}
$$

so the requested radius plot is horizontal. The cyclotron angular frequency changes as $e|B|/(\gamma m)$ and the helix pitch shrinks to zero. These statements neglect radiation, as stipulated.
