<h1 id="2/c/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Each [photon](../../../../../photon.md) has energy $\varepsilon=\hbar\omega$. Their total [four-momentum](../../../../../four-momentum.md) is

$$
P=\frac\varepsilon c(2,1+\cos\theta,\sin\theta,0).
$$

Its squared invariant is $P^2=2\varepsilon^2(1-\cos\theta)/c^2$. Therefore the system's [rest energy](../../../../../rest-energy.md) is

$$
\boxed{Mc^2=\sqrt{E_{\rm tot}^2-c^2|\mathbf p_{\rm tot}|^2}
=2\hbar\omega\,\left|\sin\frac\theta2\right|.}
$$

Two individually massless [particles](../../../../../particle-physics.md) can thus have positive total [invariant mass](../../../../../invariant-mass.md). Back-to-back photons give rest energy $2\hbar\omega$, whereas exactly parallel photons have zero invariant mass and no massive-system rest frame.
