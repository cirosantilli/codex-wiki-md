<h1 id="4/c/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In a transparent uniform medium, constructive phase interference along the trajectory requires $\omega-\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf v=0$. Since $k=n\omega/c$, the [Cherenkov cone angle](../../../../../cherenkov-cone-angle.md) obeys

$$
\boxed{\cos\theta_C=\frac1{n\beta}.}
$$

Here $n\beta=2\sqrt{1-10^{-4}}>1$, so emission is allowed and

$$
\boxed{\theta_C=\arccos\left[\frac1{2\sqrt{1-10^{-4}}}\right]
=59.99835^\circ.}
$$

The [electron](../../../../../electron.md) is not an isolated vacuum particle: it interacts with the dielectric polarization of the medium. Here the angle describes the [photon](../../../../../photon.md) direction relative to the trajectory; the conical [wavefront](../../../../../wavefront.md) surface has the complementary angle.

This angle is the classical or negligible-recoil result. At finite photon energy, energy-momentum bookkeeping in a nondispersive medium gives a recoil correction proportional to $\hbar\omega/E$; no photon frequency is supplied for such a correction. In a dispersive medium use $n(\omega)$ separately for each transparent frequency band.
