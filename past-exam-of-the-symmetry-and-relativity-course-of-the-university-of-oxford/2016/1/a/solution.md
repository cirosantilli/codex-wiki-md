<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a massive particle on a timelike [world line](../../../../world-line.md), [proper time](../../../../proper-time.md) satisfies $d\tau=dt\sqrt{1-v^2/c^2}=dt/\gamma$. Its [four-velocity](../../../../four-velocity.md) and [four-acceleration](../../../../four-acceleration.md) are $U=dX/d\tau$ and $A=dU/d\tau$. Differentiating $U=\gamma(c,\mathbf v)$ gives

$$
\boxed{A^\mu=\left(\gamma^4\frac{\mathbf v\cdot\mathbf a}{c},\quad
\gamma^2\mathbf a+\gamma^4\frac{\mathbf v\cdot\mathbf a}{c^2}\mathbf v\right).}
$$

The invariant [Minkowski inner products](../../../../minkowski-inner-product.md) are

$$
\boxed{U^2=c^2,\qquad P^2=m^2c^2,\qquad P=mU.}
$$

For the explicitly requested contraction,

$$
U\cdot A=\gamma^5\mathbf v\cdot\mathbf a-\gamma^3\mathbf v\cdot\mathbf a
-\gamma^5\frac{v^2}{c^2}\mathbf v\cdot\mathbf a
=\gamma^3\mathbf v\cdot\mathbf a\,[\gamma^2(1-v^2/c^2)-1]=0.
$$

Thus [four-acceleration](../../../../four-acceleration.md) is orthogonal to [four-velocity](../../../../four-velocity.md). Equivalently this follows by differentiating the constant [Lorentz scalar](../../../../lorentz-scalar.md) $U^2=c^2$ with respect to [proper time](../../../../proper-time.md).
