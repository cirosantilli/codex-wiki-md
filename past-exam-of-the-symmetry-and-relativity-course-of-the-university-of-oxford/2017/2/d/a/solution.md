<h1 id="2/d/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For the [electric-dominated crossed-field orbit](../../../../../electric-dominated-crossed-field-orbit.md), choose a boost $V=c^2B/E$ along $x$. Since $|E|>c|B|$, it is subluminal. The [Lorentz transformation of electromagnetic fields](../../../../../lorentz-transformation-of-electromagnetic-fields.md) gives $B'_z=0$ and $E'_y=E/\Gamma$, where $\Gamma=(1-V^2/c^2)^{-1/2}$. The initially stationary [electron](../../../../../electron.md) has $U'(0)=(\Gamma c,-\Gamma V,0,0)$, not zero [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) in this frame.

Let $q=-e$, $a=qE'_y/m_e$ and $s=a\tau/c$. Neglect radiation reaction, as in the prescribed external-field model. Integrating the [relativistic Lorentz force](../../../../../relativistic-lorentz-force.md) in this purely electric frame gives the exact trajectory

$$
\boxed{t'=\frac{\Gamma c}{a}\sinh s,\qquad
x'=-\Gamma V\tau,\qquad
y'=\frac{\Gamma c^2}{a}(\cosh s-1),\qquad z'=0.}
$$

Indeed $U'^0=\Gamma c\cosh s$, $U'^x=-\Gamma V$, and $U'^y=\Gamma c\sinh s$ satisfy both the force equation and $U'^2=c^2$. The constant transverse [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) must be retained.

The inverse boost gives a useful exact laboratory parametrization as well:

$$
\boxed{t=\Gamma^2\left[\frac ca\sinh s-\frac{V^2}{c^2}\tau\right],\qquad
x=\Gamma^2V\left[\frac ca\sinh s-\tau\right],\qquad y=y'.}
$$

For $E,B>0$, the lab [electron](../../../../../electron.md) starts accelerating toward negative $y$ and bends toward positive $x$, the direction of $\mathbf E\times\mathbf B$. It never acquires a cyclotron orbit in this electric-dominated case. At late times, $v_x\to V$ and $v_y\to\operatorname{sgn}(qE')c/\Gamma$, so its speed approaches $c$ along an oblique asymptote. Reversing field signs reverses the appropriate directions. The zero-$B$ limit is the ordinary electric-field [hyperbolic motion](../../../../../hyperbolic-motion.md).
