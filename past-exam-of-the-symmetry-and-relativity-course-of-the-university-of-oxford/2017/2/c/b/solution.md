↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [centre-of-mass velocity](../../../../../center-of-mass-velocity.md) follows from the total [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) and [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md):

$$
\boxed{\mathbf V_{\mathrm{CM}}=\frac{c^2\mathbf p_{\mathrm{tot}}}{E_{\mathrm{tot}}}
=\left(\frac c2,\frac c2,0\right),\qquad
|\mathbf V_{\mathrm{CM}}|=\frac c{\sqrt2}.}
$$

A boost in this direction with $\Gamma_{\mathrm{CM}}=\sqrt2$ gives zero total spatial [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) and total [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) $\sqrt2\hbar\omega$. The constituent [photons](../../../../../photon.md) remain moving at $c$ in that frame.
