<h1 id="2/c/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Put $\varepsilon=\hbar\omega$. The total [four-momentum](../../../../../four-momentum.md) of the perpendicular [photons](../../../../../photon.md) is

$$
P_{\mathrm{tot}}=\left(\frac{2\varepsilon}{c},\frac\varepsilon c,\frac\varepsilon c,0\right).
$$

Although each constituent is massless, the sum has $P_{\mathrm{tot}}^2=2\varepsilon^2/c^2$. Its [invariant mass](../../../../../invariant-mass.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{M_{\mathrm{sys}}=\frac{\sqrt2\,\hbar\omega}{c^2}.}
$$

The rest [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) is the total [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) in the frame where the two [photon](../../../../../photon.md) [momenta](../../../../../momentum-split.md) cancel, not the sum of individual [photon](../../../../../photon.md) rest [energies](../../../../../energy-split.md).
