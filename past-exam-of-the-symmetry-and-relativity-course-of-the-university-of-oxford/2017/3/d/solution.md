<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Take the stated static [vector potential](../../../../vector-potential.md) with zero [scalar potential](../../../../scalar-potential.md). In the laboratory,

$$
\mathbf E=0,\qquad
\mathbf B=\nabla\times\mathbf A
=\bigl(A_0k_u\sin(k_uz),0,0\bigr).
$$

Let $S'$ move along $z$ at the [electron](../../../../electron.md)'s reference [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) $v$. The inverse transformation is $z=\gamma(z'+vt')$. The [Lorentz transformation of electromagnetic fields](../../../../lorentz-transformation-of-electromagnetic-fields.md) gives

$$
\boxed{B'_x=\gamma A_0k_u\sin[\gamma k_u(z'+vt')],\qquad
E'_y=\gamma vA_0k_u\sin[\gamma k_u(z'+vt')],}
$$

with all other components zero. The sign follows from $\mathbf v\times\mathbf B=vB_x\mathbf e_y$.

Although an observer fixed in $S'$ sees an oscillating field, this is not an exact source-free propagating [electromagnetic wave](../../../../electromagnetic-wave-split.md). The phase covector has $\omega'=-\gamma vk_u$, $k'_z=\gamma k_u$, so its squared norm is

$$
\boxed{\left(\frac{\omega'}c\right)^2-(k'_z)^2=-k_u^2<0.}
$$

It is the boost of a static spatial grating, not a null vacuum wave. Equivalently, $E'/B'=v<c$ and $B'^2-E'^2/c^2=B^2$ is nonzero away from nodes; a vacuum [plane wave](../../../../plane-wave.md) would have vanishing [electromagnetic field invariants](../../../../electromagnetic-field-invariants.md). In the ultrarelativistic limit it can be a useful nearly wave-like approximation along the [electron](../../../../electron.md)'s path, but the exact distinction remains.

A charged [electron](../../../../electron.md) cannot actually maintain the given constant [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) through this nonzero transverse field without an external constraint: the [Lorentz force](../../../../lorentz-force.md) deflects it. The calculation therefore uses the prescribed straight reference trajectory, a weak-deflection approximation, or an instantaneously comoving inertial frame. A globally fixed [electron](../../../../electron.md) rest frame is not available for its exact deflected motion.
