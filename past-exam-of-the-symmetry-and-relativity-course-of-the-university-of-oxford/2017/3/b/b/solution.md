<h1 id="3/b/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For $\mathbf V=(0,v_y,0)$, the laboratory wavevector is perpendicular to the boost. The dot product vanishes, so

$$
\boxed{\omega'=\gamma_y\omega.}
$$

This transverse-observer transformation is a blueshift. It must not be confused with the inverse factor often quoted when transverse refers to a ray perpendicular to the source [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) in the _receiver's_ frame. The angle condition is specified in different frames in those two statements; [relativistic aberration](../../../../../relativistic-aberration.md) relates them.
