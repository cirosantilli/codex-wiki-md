<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A boost into a frame moving with [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) $\mathbf V$ transforms the temporal component of the [four-wavevector](../../../../four-wavevector.md) as $K'^0=\Gamma(K^0-\mathbf V\cdot\mathbf k/c)$. Therefore the general [relativistic Doppler effect](../../../../relativistic-doppler-effect.md) is

$$
\boxed{\omega'=\Gamma(\omega-\mathbf V\cdot\mathbf k)
=\Gamma\omega(1-\beta\cos\vartheta),}
$$

where $\vartheta$ is the laboratory angle between the boost and propagation directions. This is also the frequency measured from the phase rate along the observer's [world line](../../../../world-line.md), $\omega'=K\cdot U_{\mathrm{obs}}$. The frequency is an observer-dependent contraction of the invariant wave phase.
