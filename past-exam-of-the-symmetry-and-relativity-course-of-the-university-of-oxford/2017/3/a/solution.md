<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Three relevant observable effects are the relativistic [Doppler effect](../../../../doppler-effect.md) in spectral-line shifts, including the transverse time-dilation contribution; [relativistic aberration](../../../../relativistic-aberration.md), observable as a change of apparent light direction with observer motion; and [relativistic beaming](../../../../relativistic-beaming.md) of moving emitters, associated with strongly asymmetric jet brightness. [Apparent superluminal motion](../../../../apparent-superluminal-motion.md) supplies a related light-travel-time test: $v_{\mathrm{app}}/c=\beta\sin\vartheta/(1-\beta\cos\vartheta)$ can exceed one without a material speed exceeding $c$. These interpretations require an emission and source-motion model; a cosmological or gravitational redshift by itself is not a direct test of special-relativistic kinematics.

For [Compton scattering](../../../../compton-scattering.md) from an initially stationary [electron](../../../../electron.md), let incoming and outgoing [photon](../../../../photon.md) [energies](../../../../energy-split.md) be $\varepsilon$ and $\varepsilon'$ and their mutual angle be $\psi$. [Four-momentum conservation](../../../../four-momentum-conservation.md) gives $P+Q=P'+Q'$. Squaring $P'=P+Q-Q'$ and using both [electron](../../../../electron.md) mass shells and both [photon](../../../../photon.md) null norms gives

$$
2P\cdot(Q-Q')=2Q\cdot Q',\qquad
m_e(\varepsilon-\varepsilon')=\frac{\varepsilon\varepsilon'}{c^2}(1-\cos\psi).
$$

Consequently the outgoing angular frequency and shift are

$$
\boxed{\omega'=\frac{\omega}{1+\frac{\hbar\omega}{m_ec^2}(1-\cos\psi)},\qquad
\Delta\omega=-\omega\frac{\frac{\hbar\omega}{m_ec^2}(1-\cos\psi)}{1+\frac{\hbar\omega}{m_ec^2}(1-\cos\psi)}.}
$$

Equivalently, the [Compton wavelength shift](../../../../compton-wavelength-shift.md) is $\lambda'-\lambda=h(1-\cos\psi)/(m_ec)$. The [electron](../../../../electron.md) recoil is essential; treating its [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) as unchanged would suppress the effect.
