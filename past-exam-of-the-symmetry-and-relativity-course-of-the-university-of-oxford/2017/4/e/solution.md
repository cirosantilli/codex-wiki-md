<h1 id="4/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Let $\varepsilon=E_{ph}$ and $\gamma=W/(M_0c^2)$. For a head-on [photon](../../../../photon.md) and particle collision, boost to the particle's initial rest frame. The incident [photon](../../../../photon.md) [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) is $\varepsilon_* =\gamma(1+\beta)\varepsilon$. On the inverse-Compton branch $\varepsilon<\gamma\beta M_0c^2$, the maximal outgoing lab [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) corresponds to rest-frame backscattering into the particle's forward direction. The exact [Compton scattering](../../../../compton-scattering.md) recoil formula gives $\varepsilon'_* =\varepsilon_*/[1+2\varepsilon_*/(M_0c^2)]$. Boosting the backscattered [photon](../../../../photon.md) back to the lab gives

$$
\boxed{\varepsilon'_{\max}=\frac{\gamma^2(1+\beta)^2\varepsilon}
{1+2\gamma(1+\beta)\varepsilon/(M_0c^2)}.}
$$

For $\gamma\gg1$ this becomes

$$
\boxed{\varepsilon'_{\max}\simeq\frac{4\gamma^2\varepsilon}
{1+4\gamma\varepsilon/(M_0c^2)}.}
$$

Only in the additional [Thomson scattering](../../../../thomson-scattering.md) limit $4\gamma\varepsilon\ll M_0c^2$ may the denominator be neglected, yielding the printed estimate $4[W/(M_0c^2)]^2E_{ph}$. Ultrarelativistic motion alone does not justify it. For example $\gamma=1000$ and $\varepsilon=M_0c^2$ would give $4\times10^6M_0c^2$ from that estimate, exceeding the available total [energy](../../../../energy-split.md); the recoil-corrected maximum is about $999.75M_0c^2$.

For an arbitrarily hard incident [photon](../../../../photon.md), maximizing over direction needs a further qualification: if $\varepsilon\geq\gamma\beta M_0c^2$, the largest outgoing [photon](../../../../photon.md) [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) is the unchanged $\varepsilon$, rather than this amplified backscattering branch. The exact [photon](../../../../photon.md) [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) gain is $\Delta\varepsilon_{\max}=\varepsilon'_{\max}-\varepsilon$. Calling the outgoing [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) the gain is accurate only when amplification is large. These distinctions prevent the [inverse Compton scattering](../../../../inverse-compton-scattering.md) estimate from violating [conservation of energy](../../../../conservation-of-energy.md) outside its low-rest-frame-photon-energy regime.
