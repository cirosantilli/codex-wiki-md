<h1 id="1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The [electron](../../../../electron.md) moves in the direction for which the [electric field](../../../../electric-field-split.md) does positive work. Its [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) gain is the magnitude $eEL=30\,\mathrm{MeV}$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\gamma=1+\frac{30}{0.511}=59.7084,\qquad
\beta=\sqrt{1-\gamma^{-2}}=0.99985974.}
$$

This uses the [relativistic energy-momentum relation](../../../../energy-momentum-relation.md), rather than the nonrelativistic kinetic-energy formula. [Acceleration](../../../../acceleration.md) is opposite the field direction for a negatively charged [electron](../../../../electron.md), although the [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) gain is positive along its actual displacement.
