<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The equal and opposite incoming [momenta](../../../../momentum-split.md) make the laboratory the [centre-of-mass frame](../../../../center-of-momentum-frame.md). The initial total [four-momentum](../../../../four-momentum.md) is timelike. One outgoing [photon](../../../../photon.md) would have null [four-momentum](../../../../four-momentum.md), so an isolated two-body annihilation cannot produce exactly one [photon](../../../../photon.md). Two [photons](../../../../photon.md) are kinematically possible and must have opposite [momenta](../../../../momentum-split.md) and equal [energies](../../../../energy-split.md):

$$
\boxed{N_{\gamma,\min}=2,\qquad
E_\gamma=\frac{K_{\mathrm{tot}}+2m_ec^2}{2}
=500.511\,\mathrm{MeV}.}
$$

The quoted $0.511\,\mathrm{MeV}$ is the rest [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) $m_ec^2$, not a mass in SI units. The [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) balance includes both rest [energies](../../../../energy-split.md) as well as the $1\,\mathrm{GeV}$ total [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md).

This minimum concerns the complete final state. A detector with incomplete acceptance could register only one of the two [photons](../../../../photon.md), or none; conservation laws do not impose a minimum detected count without an ideal-detection assumption. Additional outgoing [photons](../../../../photon.md) are also possible.
