<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For uniform [circular motion](../../../../circular-motion.md), the transverse [Lorentz force](../../../../lorentz-force.md) supplies the change in direction of [three-momentum](../../../../three-momentum.md):

$$
e uB=\frac{pu}{r},\qquad p=eBr,\qquad
\gamma=\sqrt{1+\left(\frac{eBr}{m_ec}\right)^2}.
$$

The [magnetic rigidity](../../../../magnetic-rigidity.md) relation gives $pc=0.299792458B[\mathrm T]r[\mathrm m]\,\mathrm{GeV}=2.99792458\,\mathrm{GeV}$. Using $m_ec^2=0.511\,\mathrm{MeV}$,

$$
\boxed{\gamma\simeq5.867\times10^3,\qquad
\mathcal E=\gamma m_ec^2\simeq2.998\,\mathrm{GeV}.}
$$

The coordinate [centripetal acceleration](../../../../centripetal-acceleration.md) is $a=u^2/r$. For transverse [acceleration](../../../../acceleration.md), the magnitude of the [four-acceleration](../../../../four-acceleration.md), or [proper acceleration](../../../../proper-acceleration.md), is $a_0=\gamma^2a$. The supplied [Larmor formula](../../../../larmor-formula.md), in its [proper acceleration](../../../../proper-acceleration.md) form, therefore gives

$$
P=\frac{e^2\gamma^4u^4}{6\pi\epsilon_0c^3r^2},\qquad
\mathcal T=\frac{2\pi r}{u},\qquad
\Delta\mathcal E=P\mathcal T
=\frac{e^2}{3\epsilon_0r}\gamma^4\beta^3,
\quad \beta=u/c.
$$

The emitted [power](../../../../power.md) here is also the laboratory total emitted [power](../../../../power.md): in the instantaneous rest [inertial frame](../../../../inertial-frame.md) the dipole [radiation pattern](../../../../radiation-pattern.md) has zero net radiated [three-momentum](../../../../three-momentum.md), so both emitted [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) and elapsed coordinate time gain the same [Lorentz factor](../../../../lorentz-factor.md) on transformation. This does not assert that a directional detector flux is invariant.

Numerically $\mathcal T\simeq1.048\times10^{-5}\,\mathrm s$, $P\simeq2.18\times10^{-10}\,\mathrm W$ per [electron](../../../../electron.md), and

$$
\boxed{\Delta\mathcal E\simeq2.29\times10^{-15}\,\mathrm J
\simeq1.43\times10^4\,\mathrm{eV}=14.3\,\mathrm{keV}\quad\text{per revolution}.}
$$

The [synchrotron](../../../../synchrotron.md)'s radiofrequency accelerating system must replace this lost [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) to keep $r$ and [magnetic rigidity](../../../../magnetic-rigidity.md) fixed over many revolutions. The fraction lost per turn is about $4.77\times10^{-6}$, so a fixed-orbit calculation over one turn is self-consistent.[§12.22, equations (1975)–(1978): transverse radiated power and energy per revolution](../../../../synchrotron-radiation-by-richard-fitzpatrick.md)
