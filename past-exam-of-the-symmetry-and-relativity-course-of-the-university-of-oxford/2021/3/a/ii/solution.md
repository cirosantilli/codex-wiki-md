<h1 id="3/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

In the magnetic-dominated case the suitable [Lorentz boost](../../../../../lorentz-boost.md) instead has

$$
\boxed{\mathbf w=\frac{\mathbf E\times\mathbf B}{B^2}
=\frac EB\hat{\mathbf z},\qquad
\mathbf E'=0,\qquad B'_y=B\sqrt{1-E^2/(c^2B^2)}.}
$$

The hypothesis gives $|w|<c$. The [electromagnetic field invariants](../../../../../electromagnetic-field-invariants.md) now have $E^2-c^2B^2<0$, allowing a purely magnetic [inertial frame](../../../../../inertial-frame.md). The initial [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) is again $-w\hat{\mathbf z}$, perpendicular to $\mathbf B'$. Since a [magnetic field](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) does no [work](../../../../../work.md), the [electron](../../../../../electron.md)'s [relativistic energy](../../../../../relativistic-energy.md) and [speed](../../../../../speed.md) in this [inertial frame](../../../../../inertial-frame.md) remain constant. Its trajectory is a circle in the $x'z'$ plane with [Lorentz factor](../../../../../lorentz-factor.md) $\gamma_w$ and [cyclotron frequency](../../../../../cyclotron-frequency.md) $\Omega=eB'/(\gamma_wm)$.

For $E,B>0$, set $\theta=\Omega t'$ and $\rho=w/\Omega$. A parametrization with the correct initial [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) and [Lorentz force](../../../../../lorentz-force.md) is

$$
x'=\rho(\cos\theta-1),\qquad z'=-\rho\sin\theta,\qquad
u'_x=-w\sin\theta,\quad u'_z=-w\cos\theta.
$$

Transforming the [world line](../../../../../world-line.md) back to the laboratory gives

$$
\boxed{x=\rho(\cos\theta-1),\qquad
z=\gamma_w\rho(\theta-\sin\theta),\qquad
t=\frac{\gamma_w}{\Omega}(\theta-\beta_w^2\sin\theta).}
$$

The laboratory trajectory is a stretched [cycloid](../../../../../cycloid.md), with cusps at $\theta=2\pi n$ where the [electron](../../../../../electron.md) is momentarily at rest. It first accelerates toward $-x$, then curves toward $+z$, returning to its original $x$ coordinate each cycle while advancing in $z$. Its mean drift is $\Delta z/\Delta t=w=E/B$, the [E-cross-B drift](../../../../../e-cross-b-drift.md). Its laboratory [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) oscillates as the [electric field](../../../../../electric-field-split.md) does positive and then negative [work](../../../../../work.md); it does not grow indefinitely. The strict inequalities in the two branches exclude the null case $|E/B|=c$, where neither field can be removed by a finite [Lorentz boost](../../../../../lorentz-boost.md).
