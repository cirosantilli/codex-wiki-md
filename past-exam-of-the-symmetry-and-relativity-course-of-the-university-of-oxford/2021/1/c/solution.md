<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

With the reversed [electric field](../../../../electric-field-split.md), reflection about $x=d/2$ exchanges the [electron](../../../../electron.md) and [Positron](../../../../positron.md). They therefore meet there with equal and opposite [momenta](../../../../momentum-split.md). Each travels $d/2$, and the [work-energy theorem](../../../../work-energy-theorem.md) gives its [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md):

$$
K=eE\frac d2=e(5.0\times10^6\,\mathrm{V\,m^{-1}})(0.100\,\mathrm m),\qquad
\boxed{K_e=K_p=0.500\,\mathrm{MeV}.}
$$

Their combined [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) is $1.000\,\mathrm{MeV}$, while each total [relativistic energy](../../../../relativistic-energy.md) is $\mathcal E=0.511+0.500=1.011\,\mathrm{MeV}$. The laboratory is the [center-of-momentum frame](../../../../center-of-momentum-frame.md) of the colliding pair.

Local [four-momentum conservation](../../../../four-momentum-conservation.md) in the [electron-positron annihilation](../../../../electron-positron-annihilation.md) excludes one real [photon](../../../../photon.md): the incoming total [four-momentum](../../../../four-momentum.md) is timelike, with zero spatial component and positive [energy](../../../../energy-split.md), while a single [photon](../../../../photon.md)'s [four-momentum](../../../../four-momentum.md) is null and has $|\mathbf p|=\mathcal E/c>0$. Two [photons](../../../../photon.md) can balance their [momenta](../../../../momentum-split.md) by travelling in opposite directions, so

$$
\boxed{N_{\min}=2,\qquad \mathcal E_\gamma=1.011\,\mathrm{MeV},\qquad
\lambda=\frac{hc}{\mathcal E_\gamma}\simeq1.226\times10^{-12}\,\mathrm m.}
$$

The equal [photon](../../../../photon.md) [energies](../../../../energy-split.md) follow from opposite equal [momenta](../../../../momentum-split.md), not merely from sharing the incoming [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) arbitrarily. Here $hc\simeq1.23984\,\mathrm{MeV\,pm}$. This is the free local annihilation idealization implicit in the question; significant [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) transfer to the external apparatus would change the kinematic system being considered.
