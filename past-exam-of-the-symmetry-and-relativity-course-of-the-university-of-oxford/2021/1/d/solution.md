<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The laboratory remains the [center-of-momentum frame](../../../../center-of-momentum-frame.md). At the [relativistic collision mass threshold](../../../../relativistic-collision-mass-threshold.md), the two final [W bosons](../../../../w-boson.md) can have arbitrarily small opposite [momenta](../../../../momentum-split.md). The available [relativistic energy](../../../../relativistic-energy.md) is $2m_ec^2+eEd$, so

$$
2m_ec^2+eEd\geq2m_{\mathrm W}c^2,\qquad
\boxed{d_{\min}=\frac{2(m_{\mathrm W}-m_e)c^2}{eE}
=\frac{160.756978\times10^9\,\mathrm{eV}}{5.0\times10^6\,\mathrm{eV\,m^{-1}}}
\simeq3.21514\times10^4\,\mathrm m=32.15\,\mathrm{km}.}
$$

Each incoming particle must acquire about $80.378489\,\mathrm{GeV}$ of [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md), travelling about $16.08\,\mathrm{km}$. Equality is the ideal kinematic threshold; a nonzero production rate needs [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) above it. This estimate neglects acceleration losses and treats the [W bosons](../../../../w-boson.md) with the supplied nominal [rest mass](../../../../invariant-mass.md).

A [linear particle accelerator](../../../../linear-particle-accelerator.md) avoids repeatedly bending high-[energy](../../../../energy-split.md) [electrons](../../../../electron.md) and [Positrons](../../../../positron.md). This greatly reduces the bending [synchrotron radiation](../../../../synchrotron-radiation.md) loss, which at fixed bending radius grows approximately as the fourth power of the [relativistic energy](../../../../relativistic-energy.md) for a given particle species. Its useful collision [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) can therefore be increased without the severe circular [electron](../../../../electron.md) collider radiation penalty. The price is long structures or high accelerating gradients: each bunch normally passes once, requiring substantial beam power, fresh bunches, and very tight focusing for high [luminosity](../../../../luminosity.md). A circular [particle accelerator](../../../../particle-accelerator-split.md) reuses its accelerating structures and circulates bunches through many collisions, but needs a large ring and substantial radiofrequency power to replace [synchrotron radiation](../../../../synchrotron-radiation.md) losses. Heavier particles such as [protons](../../../../proton.md) radiate far less at a given [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) and radius, although a [proton](../../../../proton.md)'s constituent structure makes the hard-collision [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) less sharply specified than in an [electron-positron annihilation](../../../../electron-positron-annihilation.md) experiment.
