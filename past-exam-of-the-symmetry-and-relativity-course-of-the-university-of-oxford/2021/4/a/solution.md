<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [symmetry](../../../../symmetry-physics.md) is a transformation of a physical system that preserves its laws: it maps admissible solutions and consistently transformed data to admissible solutions. The central [symmetry](../../../../symmetry-physics.md) of [special relativity](../../../../special-relativity-split.md) is [Lorentz invariance](../../../../lorentz-invariance.md), expressing equivalence of [inertial frames](../../../../inertial-frame.md) and preservation of the [Minkowski interval](../../../../minkowski-interval.md) and the universal [speed of light](../../../../speed-of-light.md). With spacetime translations included, the spacetime [symmetry group](../../../../symmetry-group.md) is the [Poincaré group](../../../../poincare-group.md).

A practical sufficient check is to express an equation as an equality between [Lorentz tensors](../../../../lorentz-tensor.md) of the same type, with every repeated upper/lower pair correctly contracted and the same free indices on both sides. Every ingredient, including sources and background fields, must have its stated [Lorentz transformation](../../../../lorentz-transformation.md) rule. For example, $p^\mu p_\mu=-m^2c^2$ is a [Lorentz scalar](../../../../lorentz-scalar.md) equality, and $dp^\mu/d\tau=qF^\mu{}_{\nu}U^\nu$ equates [four-vectors](../../../../four-vector.md). Such manifest [Lorentz covariance](../../../../lorentz-covariance.md) makes the form of the equation the same in every [inertial frame](../../../../inertial-frame.md).

This is a sufficient test when the transformation rules are genuine physical ones, not a guarantee from writing index symbols. A fixed preferred [four-vector](../../../../four-vector.md) could make an equation formally covariant if it too were transformed, while its chosen physical background breaks [Lorentz invariance](../../../../lorentz-invariance.md). Conversely, an equation written in three-dimensional components can be fully consistent with [special relativity](../../../../special-relativity-split.md), as the [Lorentz force](../../../../lorentz-force.md) is, even when the covariance is not manifest.
