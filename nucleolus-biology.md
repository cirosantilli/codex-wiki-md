# Nucleolus (biology)

↑ **Parent:** [Cell nucleus](cell-nucleus.md)

The [cell nucleolus](nucleolus-biology.md) is a region inside a [cell nucleus](cell-nucleus.md) without a surrounding membrane, where much [ribosomal RNA](ribosomal-rna.md) is made and processed and [ribosome](ribosome.md) subunits begin to assemble with [proteins](protein.md). The subunits are exported to the [cytoplasm](cytoplasm.md); the [cell nucleolus](nucleolus-biology.md) is not itself a translating [ribosome](ribosome.md). See [Clark, Douglas and Choi, Biology 2e, 4.3: nucleolus and ribosomal subunits](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/4-3-eukaryotic-cells).
