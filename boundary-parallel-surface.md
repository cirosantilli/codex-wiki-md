# Boundary-parallel surface

↑ **Parent:** [Incompressible surface](incompressible-surface.md)

A properly embedded [topological surface](topological-surface.md) is boundary-parallel if it cobounds a product region with a [topological surface](topological-surface.md) in the ambient boundary, with the remaining boundary of that product contained in the ambient boundary. An [annulus](annulus-mathematics.md) parallel into one boundary component has zero net oriented boundary class on that component.
