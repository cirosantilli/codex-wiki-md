# Tetrahedral interstitial site

↑ **Parent:** [Crystal structure](crystal-structure.md)

A position surrounded by four nearest sites at [tetrahedron](tetrahedron.md) vertices. A conventional face-centred cubic cell has eight such sites, at all combinations of quarter and three-quarter fractional coordinates. Whether occupancy constitutes a defect depends on the reference crystal.
