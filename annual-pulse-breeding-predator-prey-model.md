# Annual pulse-breeding predator-prey model

↑ **Parent:** [Lotka-Volterra predator-prey model](lotka-volterra-predator-prey-model.md)

An [annual pulse-breeding predator-prey model](annual-pulse-breeding-predator-prey-model.md) combines continuous [predation](predation.md) and mortality between short reproduction episodes with an annual [difference equation](difference-equation.md). If surviving [predators](predator.md) produce offspring in proportion to [prey](prey.md) surviving the year, retained adults give the displayed map. Here $r$ combines [prey](prey.md) reproduction and natural survival, $s$ is [predator](predator.md) survival, and $\kappa$ integrates [predation](predation.md) over declining [predator](predator.md) abundance. The positive annual [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) is $X_*=(1-s)/(sb)$, $Y_*=\log r/\kappa$ for $r>1$ and $0<s<1$.
