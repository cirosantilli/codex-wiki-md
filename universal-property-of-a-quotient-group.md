# Universal property of a quotient group

↑ **Parent:** [Quotient group](quotient-group.md)

Let $N$ be a [normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md) of $G$, and let $\pi:G\to G/N$ be the [quotient map of groups](quotient-map-of-groups.md). If a [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md) $\varphi:G\to H$ has [group kernel](kernel-of-a-group-homomorphism.md) containing $N$, there is exactly one [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md) $\bar\varphi:G/N\to H$ with $\varphi=\bar\varphi\circ\pi$. Its value is forced: $\bar\varphi(gN)=\varphi(g)$. Every measurement by a [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md) that ignores $N$ can thus be performed on the [quotient group](quotient-group.md) directly. This is the [universal property](universal-property.md) of the construction.
