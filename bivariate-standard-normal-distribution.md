# Bivariate standard normal distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Bivariate normal distribution](bivariate-normal-distribution.md)

A bivariate standard normal distribution is a [bivariate normal distribution](bivariate-normal-distribution.md) whose two marginal means are zero and marginal variances are one; its remaining parameter is their correlation.
