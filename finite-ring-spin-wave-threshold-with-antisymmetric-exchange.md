# Finite-ring spin-wave threshold with antisymmetric exchange

↑ **Parent:** [Linear spin-wave approximation](linear-spin-wave-approximation.md)

For a polarized [spin](spin.md) chain with [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation-split.md) $\epsilon(k)=2S[J(1+u)-J\cos k-D\sin k]$, the first zero mode appears at the maximum over the allowed ring [momenta](momentum-split.md). In the [thermodynamic limit](thermodynamic-limit.md) this threshold is $\sqrt{1+(D/J)^2}-1$, since the maximum of $J\cos k+D\sin k$ is $\sqrt{J^2+D^2}$. At finite size the minimizing [wave vector](wavevector.md) need not be available, so a positive continuum threshold need not give a positive finite-ring threshold. For $L=4$ and $0<D<J$, the latter is zero.
