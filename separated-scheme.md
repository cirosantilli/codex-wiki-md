# Separated scheme

↑ **Parent:** [Separated morphism](separated-morphism.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Separated_scheme)

A scheme over a base $S$ is separated when its structure morphism to $S$ is a [separated morphism](separated-morphism.md). An absolute scheme is usually called separated when it is separated over $\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Z$.
