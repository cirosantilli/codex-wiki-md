# Lung flotation test

↑ **Parent:** [Forensic examination of newborns](forensic-examination-of-newborns.md)

A historical test of gas in the [lungs](lung.md), interpreted alongside decomposition, resuscitation and the whole examination. Flotation is not an infallible demonstration of [live birth](live-birth.md) or drowning.
