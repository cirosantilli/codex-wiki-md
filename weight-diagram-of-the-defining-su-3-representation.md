<h1 id="weight-diagram-of-the-defining-su-3-representation">Weight diagram of the defining SU(3) representation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Weight diagram](weight-diagram.md)

Using $I_3=\operatorname{diag}(1/2,-1/2,0)$ and flavour $Y=\operatorname{diag}(1/3,1/3,-2/3)$, the three coordinate vectors of the [SU(3) group](su-3-group.md) defining [group representation](group-representation.md) have the displayed [weights of a representation](weight-of-a-representation.md). The complex-conjugate [group representation](group-representation.md) has their negatives. These coordinates use [flavor hypercharge](flavor-hypercharge.md); another Cartan normalization rescales the vertical axis.
