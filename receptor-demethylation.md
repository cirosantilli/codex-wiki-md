# Receptor demethylation

↑ **Parent:** [Chemotaxis feedback regulation](chemotaxis-feedback-regulation.md)

Removal of methyl groups from a modified [receptor](receptor-biochemistry.md). In [chemotaxis feedback regulation](chemotaxis-feedback-regulation.md), this reverses the chemical modification introduced by [receptor methylation](receptor-methylation.md); the two processes have different enzyme-dependent rates. Whether the demethylation rate depends on total receptor modification or on active receptors matters for [perfect adaptation](perfect-adaptation.md).
