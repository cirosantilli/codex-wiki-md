# Quasi-experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Causal inference](causal-inference-split.md)

A study design seeking causal information without individual random assignment, using a structured comparison or assignment mechanism. The relevant identifying assumptions should be stated rather than replaced by the label “quasi-experimental.”
