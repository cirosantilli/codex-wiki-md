# Pair-correlation expression for internal energy

↑ **Parent:** [Radial distribution function](radial-distribution-function.md)

For a classical monatomic homogeneous fluid with pairwise central [pair potential](pair-potential.md) $v(r)$, the mean interaction energy is one half the sum over neighbours of a tagged particle. Using the [radial distribution function](radial-distribution-function.md) gives the displayed formula; the factor one half prevents counting each pair twice. The kinetic term follows from the [equipartition theorem](equipartition-theorem.md). Its use requires the relevant integral to exist and a thermodynamic state specified by number density and temperature. Quantum kinetic contributions, many-body forces and internal molecular modes require modifications.
