# Null convergence condition

↑ **Parent:** [Null energy condition](null-energy-condition.md)

The [null convergence condition](null-convergence-condition.md) requires $R_{ab}k^ak^b\geq0$ for every [null vector](null-vector.md). Through the [Einstein field equations](einstein-field-equations.md), it follows from the [null energy condition](null-energy-condition.md), since all terms proportional to the [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md) vanish on a [null vector](null-vector.md).
