# Channel-cavity drainage instability

↑ **Parent:** [Subglacial drainage](subglacial-drainage.md)

At fixed total discharge $Q_R+Q_K$, remove a small amount $\varepsilon$ from a channel and send it through connected cavities. The equilibrium channel effective [pressure](pressure-split.md) drops by $N_R'\varepsilon$, while the cavity [pressure](pressure-split.md) difference drops by $|N_K'|\varepsilon$. Under the displayed inequality the cavity system leaves a common effective [pressure](pressure-split.md) higher than the reduced-flux channel can sustain. Excess creep closure then decreases channel discharge further, amplifying the perturbation.
