↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**The large negative [activation entropy](../../../../../../activation-entropy.md) is consistent with a strongly organized bimolecular [cycloaddition](../../../../../../cycloaddition.md).** Two separately translating and rotating molecules must approach with a restricted orientation to form a compact ring-forming transition state. Losing relative positional and orientational freedom lowers entropy.

Internal conformational restrictions and solvent rearrangement can contribute too. In a nonpolar solvent, a simple association picture is more plausible than assigning the whole effect to strong ionic solvation, but the numerical value is not a direct count of molecular orientations alone.
