<h1 id="a7/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For an elementary bimolecular forward step, $v=k_2[\mathrm R_1][\mathrm R_2]$. A liquid solution is much less compressible than a gas, so applying pressure changes its volume and reactant concentrations only modestly. With composition and temperature otherwise fixed, the observed substantial acceleration is therefore most naturally assigned to a change in the [rate constant](../../../../../../rate-constant.md).

This is an approximation, not exact incompressibility: for precise pressure kinetics, correct the concentrations for compression and distinguish them from pressure-dependent activities.
