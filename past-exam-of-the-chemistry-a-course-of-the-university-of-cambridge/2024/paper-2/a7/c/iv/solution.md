↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

**The negative [activation volume](../../../../../../activation-volume.md) means the transition state occupies less partial molar volume than the two separated reactants.** Pressure therefore favours reaching it and increases the forward rate, consistent with a compact associative [cycloaddition](../../../../../../cycloaddition.md) in which new bonds begin to form.

The magnitude, about $25\ \mathrm{cm^3\,mol^{-1}}$, is molecular in scale. It includes packing and solvent reorganization as well as intrinsic bond-length changes, so it should not be interpreted as a simple geometric volume carved out of two rigid molecular surfaces.
