<h1 id="a6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $\rho_i=N_i/V$ be a molecular [number density](../../../../../number-density.md) and define the volume-independent [molecular partition function density](../../../../../molecular-partition-function-density.md) $f_i=q_i/V$. Then $q_i/N_i=f_i/\rho_i$. Also define the reaction reference-energy difference

$$
\Delta\varepsilon_0=\varepsilon^{0,\mathrm C}-\varepsilon^{0,\mathrm A}-\varepsilon^{0,\mathrm B}.
$$

Substitution into the equilibrium chemical-potential relation gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
0&=-k_{\mathrm B}T\ln\frac{f_{\mathrm C}}{\rho_{\mathrm C}}
+k_{\mathrm B}T\ln\frac{f_{\mathrm A}}{\rho_{\mathrm A}}
+k_{\mathrm B}T\ln\frac{f_{\mathrm B}}{\rho_{\mathrm B}}
+\Delta\varepsilon_0,\\
\frac{\rho_{\mathrm C}}{\rho_{\mathrm A}\rho_{\mathrm B}}
&=\frac{f_{\mathrm C}}{f_{\mathrm A}f_{\mathrm B}}
\exp\left(-\frac{\Delta\varepsilon_0}{k_{\mathrm B}T}\right).
\end{aligned}
$$

An [equilibrium constant](../../../../../equilibrium-constant.md) expressed using concentration activities $a_i=\rho_i/c^\circ$ is dimensionless:

$$
\boxed{K_c=\frac{a_{\mathrm C}}{a_{\mathrm A}a_{\mathrm B}}
=\frac{\rho_{\mathrm C}c^\circ}{\rho_{\mathrm A}\rho_{\mathrm B}}
=\frac{f_{\mathrm C}c^\circ}{f_{\mathrm A}f_{\mathrm B}}e^{-\Delta\varepsilon_0/(k_{\mathrm B}T)}.}
$$

Here every $f_i$ has units $\mathrm{m^{-3}}$, and $c^\circ$ is the chosen [standard concentration](../../../../../standard-concentration.md), initially one molecule per cubic metre. The factor $c^\circ$ is essential: without it the association quotient has units of volume.
