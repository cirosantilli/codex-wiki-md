**The highest expected orbital degeneracy is five.** Ideal [buckminsterfullerene](../../../../../../buckminsterfullerene.md) has [Ih molecular point group](../../../../../../ih-molecular-point-group.md) symmetry. Its irreducible orbital representations include five-dimensional $H_g$ and $H_u$ representations; no single-valued orbital irrep has higher dimension.

This counts distinct spatial [molecular orbitals](../../../../../../molecular-orbital.md). It does not multiply by electron spin, and it excludes accidental coincidences between unrelated irreps. In a real environment, symmetry lowering can split these degeneracies.[Printed page 17, $I_h$ irreducible dimensions](../../../../../../tables-for-group-theory-by-p-w-atkins-and-others.md)
