<h1 id="a2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Differentiate the Gaussian before substituting into the [Hamiltonian](../../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md):

$$
\Psi_0'=-\alpha x\Psi_0,\qquad
\Psi_0''=(\alpha^2x^2-\alpha)\Psi_0.
$$

Thus

$$
\hat H\Psi_0=\left[\frac{\hbar^2\alpha}{2m}
+\left(\frac{m\omega^2}2-\frac{\hbar^2\alpha^2}{2m}\right)x^2\right]\Psi_0.
$$

An [energy eigenvalue](../../../../../energy-eigenvalue-split.md) must be independent of $x$, so the coefficient of $x^2$ vanishes. Normalizability requires $\alpha>0$, giving

$$
\boxed{\alpha=\frac{m\omega}{\hbar},\qquad E_0=\frac12\hbar\omega.}
$$

The constant term is the [quantum harmonic oscillator](../../../../../quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md)'s zero-point energy; a Gaussian width $\alpha^{-1/2}$ balances its kinetic and potential contributions.
