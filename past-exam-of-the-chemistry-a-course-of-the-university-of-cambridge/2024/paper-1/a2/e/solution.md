<h1 id="a2/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Write $\hat N=\hat a^\dagger\hat a$. The [creation and annihilation operators](../../../../../creation-and-annihilation-operators.md) obey

$$
[\hat N,\hat a^\dagger]
=\hat a^\dagger[\hat a,\hat a^\dagger]=\hat a^\dagger,
\qquad
[\hat H,\hat a^\dagger]=\hbar\omega\hat a^\dagger.
$$

Consequently,

$$
\begin{aligned}
\hat H\hat a^\dagger\Psi_0
&=(\hat a^\dagger\hat H+[\hat H,\hat a^\dagger])\Psi_0\\
&=(E_0+\hbar\omega)\hat a^\dagger\Psi_0
=\boxed{\frac32\hbar\omega\,\hat a^\dagger\Psi_0}.
\end{aligned}
$$

The resulting function is not zero: its squared norm is

$$
\|\hat a^\dagger\Psi_0\|^2
=\langle\Psi_0|\hat a\hat a^\dagger|\Psi_0\rangle
=\langle\hat a^\dagger\hat a+1\rangle=1.
$$

Thus **$\hat a^\dagger\Psi_0$ is the normalized first excited state**, up to phase. More generally the [creation operator](../../../../../creation-operator.md) raises the energy by one quantum $\hbar\omega$: $\hat a^\dagger|n\rangle=\sqrt{n+1}\,|n+1\rangle$. The factor is required because applying it to a higher state does not preserve normalization.
