<h1 id="a1/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Within the well, the potential is zero and the [Hamiltonian](../../../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) is purely kinetic:

$$
\boxed{\hat H=-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\frac{\mathrm d^2}{\mathrm dx^2}.}
$$

The infinite walls are encoded in the operator's boundary conditions $\Psi(0)=\Psi(a)=0$, rather than differentiated as part of the interior expression. Since

$$
\Psi_n''(x)=-\left(\frac{n\pi}{a}\right)^2\Psi_n(x),
$$

we obtain

$$
\hat H\Psi_n=\boxed{\frac{n^2\pi^2\hbar^2}{2ma^2}}\Psi_n,
\qquad \boxed{E_n=\frac{n^2h^2}{8ma^2}}.
$$

These positive [energy eigenvalues](../../../../../../energy-eigenvalue-split.md) grow quadratically with the [quantum number](../../../../../../quantum-number.md); the [ground state](../../../../../../ground-state.md) has a nonzero confinement energy.
