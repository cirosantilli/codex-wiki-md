↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Inside the [infinite square well](../../../../../../infinite-square-well.md), the stationary [wavefunctions](../../../../../../wave-function-split.md) can be chosen real:

$$
\boxed{\Psi_n(x)=C_n\sin\frac{n\pi x}{a},\qquad n=1,2,3,\ldots,\quad 0<x<a.}
$$

They vanish outside the well and at its impenetrable endpoints. A sine meets the left boundary condition; the right one requires $\sin(n\pi)=0$. The putative $n=0$ function is identically zero, so it is not a [quantum state](../../../../../../quantum-state.md). The constants $C_n$ are fixed by [wavefunction normalization](../../../../../../wavefunction-normalization.md).
