↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In [atomic units](../../../../../atomic-units.md), $\hbar=m_e=e=4\pi\varepsilon_0=1$, and the [hydrogen atom](../../../../../hydrogen-atom.md) has

$$
\boxed{\hat H\Psi=-\frac{1}{2n^2}\Psi,\qquad
\hat l^2\Psi=l(l+1)\Psi,\qquad \hat l_z\Psi=m\Psi,}
$$



$$
\boxed{\hat s^2\Psi=\frac34\Psi,\qquad \hat s_z\Psi=m_s\Psi.}
$$

The allowed [quantum numbers](../../../../../quantum-number.md) are

$$
n=1,2,\ldots,\qquad l=0,1,\ldots,n-1,\qquad
m=-l,-l+1,\ldots,l,\qquad m_s=\pm\frac12.
$$

Here the fixed [electron spin](../../../../../electron-spin.md) quantum number is $s=1/2$. The [energy eigenvalues](../../../../../energy-eigenvalue-split.md) are in hartrees; restoring units gives $\hbar^2l(l+1)$ and $\hbar m$ for the orbital operators and the corresponding factors for [spin](../../../../../spin.md). The spin-independent hydrogen [Hamiltonian](../../../../../hamiltonian.md) does not split the two [spin](../../../../../spin.md) projections.
