<h1 id="a4/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Each [cyanide](../../../../../../cyanide.md) contributes charge $-1$, so the formal nickel [oxidation state](../../../../../../oxidation-state.md) is $+2$ and the metal has **$d^8$**. The requested diagram retains only the five principally metal-$3d$ [molecular orbitals](../../../../../../molecular-orbital.md); the mainly ligand [bonding molecular orbitals](../../../../../../bonding-orbital.md) lie below them and are omitted.

In the square, $d_{x^2-y^2}$ points directly towards all four ligand donors and gives the highest, strongly [antibonding molecular orbital](../../../../../../antibonding-orbital.md), $B_{1g}$. The $d_{z^2}$ function also has an allowed $A_{1g}$ interaction, but its in-plane overlap is smaller. Including the allowed nickel $4s$ mixing gives a principally $d$ $A_{1g}$ level with relatively weak antibonding character. The $B_{2g}$ and $E_g$ metal functions have no ligand partner in the [sigma bond](../../../../../../sigma-bond.md) donor basis and are nonbonding in this minimal model. The useful qualitative ordering is therefore

$$
\boxed{D_{4h}:\quad (B_{2g},E_g)_{\rm nb}<A_{1g}<B_{1g}.}
$$

The three nonbonding functions are shown together because the sigma-only model does not determine their finer splitting. Additional electrostatic and [pi backbonding](../../../../../../pi-backbonding.md) interactions can change the ordering of the lower $d$ levels; [molecular symmetry](../../../../../../molecular-symmetry.md) alone does not fix these [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) gaps.[2022 lecture notes, square-planar sigma-only molecular-orbital discussion, printed pp. 60–61](../../../../../../bonding-in-molecules-by-john-e-mcgrady.md)

In the tetrahedron, the ligand $T_2$ combinations interact with the metal $T_2$ functions, producing higher $d$-derived [antibonding molecular orbitals](../../../../../../antibonding-orbital.md). The metal $E$ pair has no sigma donor partner and remains nonbonding:

$$
\boxed{T_d:\quad E_{\rm nb}<T_{2,\rm antibonding}.}
$$

The diagram fills both arrangements with eight metal [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md); the square is shown in its fully paired state.
