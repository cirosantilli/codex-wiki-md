<h1 id="a4/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Choose the phase of each inward-pointing ligand [sigma bond](../../../../../../sigma-bond.md) donor lobe consistently. An operation taking one donor to another then acts as a permutation on the four donor functions. For the square, label these functions $L_1,L_2,L_3,L_4$ cyclically along $+x,+y,-x,-y$. Normalized [symmetry-adapted linear combinations](../../../../../../symmetry-adapted-linear-combination.md) are

$$
\begin{aligned}
A_{1g}:&\quad(L_1+L_2+L_3+L_4)/2,\\
B_{1g}:&\quad(L_1-L_2+L_3-L_4)/2,\\
E_u:&\quad(L_1-L_3)/\sqrt2,\quad(L_2-L_4)/\sqrt2.
\end{aligned}
$$

Thus the ligand [group representation](../../../../../../group-representation.md) is **$\Gamma_\sigma=A_{1g}+B_{1g}+E_u$**. Its [irreducible representations](../../../../../../irreducible-representation.md) allow the first two combinations to mix with the matching nickel $d$ [atomic orbitals](../../../../../../atomic-orbital.md); $E_u$ instead matches nickel $p_x,p_y$.

For the tetrahedron in the vertex order specified above, take

$$
\begin{aligned}
A_1:&\quad(L_1+L_2+L_3+L_4)/2,\\
T_2:&\quad(L_1+L_2-L_3-L_4)/2,\\
&\quad(L_1-L_2+L_3-L_4)/2,\\
&\quad(L_1-L_2-L_3+L_4)/2.
\end{aligned}
$$

The three latter functions transform like $x,y,z$. Hence **$\Gamma_\sigma=A_1+T_2$**. These are four ligand [symmetry-adapted linear combinations](../../../../../../symmetry-adapted-linear-combination.md), rather than the five metal $d$ functions of part (ii).
