<h1 id="a4/a/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

For $n$ [unpaired electrons](../../../../../../unpaired-electron.md), the [spin-only magnetic moment](../../../../../../spin-only-magnetic-moment.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mu_{\rm eff}=\sqrt{n(n+2)}\,\mu_{\rm B}
=2\sqrt{S(S+1)}\,\mu_{\rm B},}
$$

where $\mu_{\rm B}$ is the [Bohr magneton](../../../../../../bohr-magneton.md) and the second expression uses $g\simeq2$.

For the square, a large separation of the highest $B_{1g}$ level favours pairing in the lower four [molecular orbitals](../../../../../../molecular-orbital.md): **$S=0$, $n=0$, $\mu_{\rm eff}=0$**. If the cost of occupying $B_{1g}$ were smaller than the relevant [electron pairing energy](../../../../../../electron-pairing-energy.md), a [high-spin complex](../../../../../../high-spin-complex.md) with two [unpaired electrons](../../../../../../unpaired-electron.md) and $S=1$ could instead occur, giving **$\sqrt8\,\mu_{\rm B}\simeq2.83\,\mu_{\rm B}$**.

For ideal tetrahedral $d^8$, the occupation is $e^4t_2^4$. [Hund's rules](../../../../../../hund-s-rules.md) distribute four [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) over the three degenerate $t_2$ [molecular orbitals](../../../../../../molecular-orbital.md) as one pair and two single occupations. Thus **$S=1$ and $\mu_{\rm eff}\simeq2.83\,\mu_{\rm B}$**. Merely increasing the $e$–$t_2$ splitting does not create a conventional paired tetrahedral $d^8$ alternative: four [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) must still occupy $t_2$.

**The absence of [paramagnetism](../../../../../../paramagnetism.md) therefore favours a square-planar, low-spin $d^8$ complex.**
