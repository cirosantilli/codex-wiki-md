<h1 id="a2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

[Wavefunction normalization](../../../../../../wavefunction-normalization.md) requires $\alpha>0$. For the normalized [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) weight proportional to $e^{-\alpha x^2}$, the [Gaussian integral](../../../../../../gaussian-integral.md) and its derivatives give

$$
\langle x^2\rangle=\frac1{2\alpha},\qquad
\langle x^4\rangle=\frac3{4\alpha^2}.
$$

Since $\widetilde\Psi''=(\alpha^2x^2-\alpha)\widetilde\Psi$, the [kinetic energy](../../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) and [potential energy](../../../../../../potential-energy.md) expectations are

$$
\langle T\rangle=\frac\alpha2-\frac{\alpha^2}{2}\langle x^2\rangle=\frac\alpha4,
\qquad
\langle V\rangle=\frac12\langle x^4\rangle=\frac3{8\alpha^2}.
$$

Thus

$$
E(\alpha)=\frac\alpha4+\frac3{8\alpha^2},\qquad
E'(\alpha)=\frac14-\frac3{4\alpha^3}=0
\ \Longrightarrow\ \alpha_*=3^{1/3}.
$$

The second derivative is positive, and the [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) diverges at both ends of $\alpha>0$, so this is the global minimum within the [Gaussian function](../../../../../../gaussian-function.md) family. **The best [Gaussian function](../../../../../../gaussian-function.md) upper bound is**

$$
\boxed{E_0\leq E(\alpha_*)=\frac{3^{4/3}}8\simeq0.54084359.}
$$

This is an upper bound, not the exact quartic ground [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md).
