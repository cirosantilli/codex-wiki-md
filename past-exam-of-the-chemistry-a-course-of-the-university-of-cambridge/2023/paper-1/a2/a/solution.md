<h1 id="a2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $\{\phi_j\}$ be a complete [orthonormal eigenbasis](../../../../../orthonormal-eigenbasis.md) of the [Hamiltonian](../../../../../hamiltonian.md), with $H\phi_j=E_j\phi_j$ and $E_j\geq E_0$. Expand a nonzero admissible [trial wavefunction](../../../../../trial-wavefunction.md) as $\widetilde\Psi=\sum_j c_j\phi_j$. Then

$$
\langle\widetilde\Psi|\widetilde\Psi\rangle=\sum_j|c_j|^2,
\qquad
\langle\widetilde\Psi|H|\widetilde\Psi\rangle=\sum_j|c_j|^2E_j.
$$

Consequently

$$
\widetilde E-E_0
=\frac{\sum_j|c_j|^2(E_j-E_0)}{\sum_j|c_j|^2}\geq0,
\qquad\boxed{\widetilde E\geq E_0.}
$$

Every numerator term is nonnegative and the denominator is positive. Equality requires the trial to lie wholly in the [ground-state subspace](../../../../../ground-state-subspace.md); it need not be an individually chosen eigenvector when the ground [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) is degenerate. This is the [quantum variational principle](../../../../../quantum-variational-principle.md), and [wavefunction normalization](../../../../../wavefunction-normalization.md) before calculation is optional because the quotient removes the overall scale.
