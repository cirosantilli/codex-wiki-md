<h1 id="a3/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The gross requirement is **an anisotropic [polarizability tensor](../../../../../../polarizability-tensor.md)**, so that changing molecular orientation changes the induced response. A permanent [electric dipole moment](../../../../../../electric-dipole-moment.md) is not required; a homonuclear diatomic can therefore have a rotational [Raman scattering](../../../../../../raman-scattering.md) spectrum.

For the inelastic lines of a linear [rigid rotor](../../../../../../rigid-rotor.md), the detailed rule is **$\Delta J=\pm2$**, with $J\geq0$. The $\Delta J=0$ contribution is unshifted [Rayleigh scattering](../../../../../../rayleigh-scattering.md), not a rotational [Stokes Raman scattering](../../../../../../stokes-raman-scattering.md) line. [Stokes Raman scattering](../../../../../../stokes-raman-scattering.md) has $\Delta J=+2$ because the molecule gains rotational [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md).
