<h1 id="a5/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Work with $M=(H-\alpha I)/\beta$. Its nearest-neighbour ring entries are $1$, and its diagonal entries are $1$ on the nitrogen sites and $0$ on the carbon sites. Because $M$ commutes with each [molecular symmetry operation](../../../../../../molecular-symmetry-operation.md), it only mixes [symmetry-adapted linear combinations](../../../../../../symmetry-adapted-linear-combination.md) with the same [irreducible representation](../../../../../../irreducible-representation.md).

In the bases $(N_+,C_0)$ and $(N_-,C_y)$, the two nontrivial [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) blocks are

$$
M_{B_{1u}}=\begin{pmatrix}1&\sqrt2\\\sqrt2&1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
M_{B_{3g}}=\begin{pmatrix}1&\sqrt2\\\sqrt2&-1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Their [characteristic polynomials](../../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) are $(1-\lambda)^2-2$ and $\lambda^2-3$. The remaining single-function blocks are $M_{B_{2g}}=1$ and $M_{A_u}=-1$. Thus, ordered from lowest to highest physical [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) because $\beta<0$, the [molecular orbitals](../../../../../../molecular-orbital.md) are

$$
\boxed{\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\varepsilon&\text{irrep}&\text{electrons}\\\hline
\alpha+(1+\sqrt2)\beta&B_{1u}&2\\
\alpha+\sqrt3\beta&B_{3g}&2\\
\alpha+\beta&B_{2g}&2\\
\alpha+(1-\sqrt2)\beta&B_{1u}&0\\
\alpha-\beta&A_u&0\\
\alpha-\sqrt3\beta&B_{3g}&0
\end{array}}
$$

All six levels are nondegenerate in this parametrization. The right-hand ladder of the diagram in part (ii) shows these levels and occupations. The occupied $B_{2g}$ level is the [highest occupied molecular orbital](../../../../../../highest-occupied-molecular-orbital.md), and the unoccupied $B_{1u}$ level immediately above it is the [lowest unoccupied molecular orbital](../../../../../../lowest-unoccupied-molecular-orbital.md).
