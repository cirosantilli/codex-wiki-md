↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The nitrogen pair and the four-carbon set are separately invariant under the [D2h point group](../../../../../../d2h-point-group.md). Construct [symmetry-adapted linear combinations](../../../../../../symmetry-adapted-linear-combination.md) within these two sets; mixing equivalent representations across the sets is left for the [Hückel method](../../../../../../huckel-method.md) calculation.

For the nitrogen $p_z$ [atomic orbitals](../../../../../../atomic-orbital.md), normalized combinations are

$$
\boxed{N_+=\frac{\chi_1+\chi_4}{\sqrt2}\sim B_{1u},\qquad
N_-=\frac{\chi_1-\chi_4}{\sqrt2}\sim B_{3g}.}
$$

For the carbon $p_z$ [atomic orbitals](../../../../../../atomic-orbital.md), take

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
C_0&=\tfrac12(\chi_2+\chi_3+\chi_5+\chi_6)\sim B_{1u},\\
C_y&=\tfrac12(\chi_2-\chi_3-\chi_5+\chi_6)\sim B_{3g},\\
C_x&=\tfrac12(\chi_2+\chi_3-\chi_5-\chi_6)\sim B_{2g},\\
C_{xy}&=\tfrac12(\chi_2-\chi_3+\chi_5-\chi_6)\sim A_u.
\end{aligned}}
$$

These six functions are [orthonormal](../../../../../../orthonormal-set.md) under the zero-overlap assumption. The suffixes $x,y,xy$ describe coefficient patterns across the carbon sites; the sign acquired by each underlying $p_z$ function must also be included when assigning an [irreducible representation](../../../../../../irreducible-representation.md). In particular, reflection through the molecular plane changes the sign of every function, while inversion sends $\chi_1$ to $-\chi_4$. Ignoring that orbital sign would give incorrect $g/u$ labels.

The complete $\pi$ [group representation](../../../../../../group-representation.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{\Gamma_\pi=2B_{1u}+2B_{3g}+B_{2g}+A_u.}
$$
