<h1 id="a5/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The six $\pi$ [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) give the [Hückel method](../../../../../../huckel-method.md) [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md)

$$
E_\pi=2\{\alpha+(1+\sqrt2)\beta\}
+2(\alpha+\sqrt3\beta)+2(\alpha+\beta)
=6\alpha+(4+2\sqrt2+2\sqrt3)\beta.
$$

The appropriate localized reference contains **two C=N $\pi$ bonds and one C=C $\pi$ bond**. Three C=C bonds would incorrectly replace the nitrogen one-centre parameters. For one isolated C=N pair, the dimensionless [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) is

$$
\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},
$$

whose [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are $(1\pm\sqrt5)/2$. Since $\beta<0$, the occupied [bonding molecular orbital](../../../../../../bonding-orbital.md) has [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) $\alpha+(1+\sqrt5)\beta/2$. Two doubly occupied C=N bonds and one doubly occupied C=C bond consequently give

$$
E_{\rm loc}=2\{2\alpha+(1+\sqrt5)\beta\}+2(\alpha+\beta)
=6\alpha+(4+2\sqrt5)\beta.
$$

Therefore the signed [delocalization energy](../../../../../../delocalization-energy.md) is

$$
\boxed{\Delta E=2(\sqrt2+\sqrt3-\sqrt5)\beta
\simeq1.82039\beta<0.}
$$

The positive stabilization is **$1.82039|\beta|$**, smaller than the [benzene](../../../../../../benzene.md) value $2|\beta|$ in the stated model but still stabilizing.

**[Pyrazine](../../../../../../pyrazine.md) is aromatic:** its planar cyclic [conjugated system](../../../../../../conjugated-system.md) contains six $\pi$ [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) and has a filled set of three bonding $\pi$ [molecular orbitals](../../../../../../molecular-orbital.md). Each ring nitrogen contributes one $p_z$ [electron](../../../../../../electron.md); its in-plane [lone pair](../../../../../../lone-pair.md) is outside this $\pi$ system. The heteroatoms change orbital energies and coefficients without interrupting cyclic conjugation or the $4n+2$ count. [Aromaticity](../../../../../../aromaticity.md) does not require the stabilization to equal [benzene](../../../../../../benzene.md)'s.
