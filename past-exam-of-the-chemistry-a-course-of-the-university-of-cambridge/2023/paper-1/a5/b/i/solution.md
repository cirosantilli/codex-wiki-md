**[Pyrazine](../../../../../../pyrazine.md) belongs to $D_{2h}$**, the [D2h point group](../../../../../../d2h-point-group.md). With the printed axes, its three twofold axes are $x,y,z$; it also has an [inversion centre](../../../../../../inversion-centre.md) and the three coordinate mirror planes. The nitrogen atoms occupy sites 1 and 4 on the $y$ axis. Interchanging carbon and nitrogen is not a [molecular symmetry operation](../../../../../../molecular-symmetry-operation.md), so the sixfold [benzene](../../../../../../benzene.md) symmetry is lost.
