<h1 id="a5/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Filling the three [bonding molecular orbitals](../../../../../../bonding-orbital.md) with two [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) each gives

$$
E_\pi=2(\alpha+2\beta)+4(\alpha+\beta)=6\alpha+8\beta.
$$

Three isolated ethene-like $\pi$ bonds, evaluated with the same [Hückel method](../../../../../../huckel-method.md) parameters, have $E_{\rm loc}=3\{2(\alpha+\beta)\}=6\alpha+6\beta$. Thus the signed [delocalization energy](../../../../../../delocalization-energy.md) is

$$
\boxed{\Delta E=E_\pi-E_{\rm loc}=2\beta<0.}
$$

Equivalently, the positive stabilization is **$-2\beta=2|\beta|$**.

[Benzene](../../../../../../benzene.md) has a planar cyclic [conjugated system](../../../../../../conjugated-system.md) with six $\pi$ [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md), satisfying the $4n+2$ count with $n=1$. All three bonding $\pi$ [molecular orbitals](../../../../../../molecular-orbital.md) are filled and the antibonding levels are empty, giving the closed-shell stabilization associated with [aromaticity](../../../../../../aromaticity.md). The conclusion uses the continuous cyclic overlap as well as the [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) count.
