<h1 id="a10/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Heating promotes electrons across the [band gap](../../../../../../band-gap.md), creating mobile [conduction electrons](../../../../../../conduction-electron.md) and [electron holes](../../../../../../electron-hole.md). Their contributions add:

$$
\boxed{\sigma=e(nu_e+pu_h)},
$$

where $u_e,u_h$ are the [electron mobility](../../../../../../electron-mobility.md) and [hole mobility](../../../../../../hole-mobility.md), and $e$ is the positive elementary-charge magnitude. The increase in [carrier density](../../../../../../carrier-density.md) gives the rising [electrical conductivity](../../../../../../electrical-conductivity.md) in the intrinsic activation regime. Mobility need not itself increase with temperature; the model attributes the dominant increase to carrier generation.
