<h1 id="a9/b/vi/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vi](../vi.md)

There are $L$ primitive cells and therefore $L$ [lattice points](../../../../../../lattice-point.md). The specified reciprocal-space density $LA_c/(2\pi)^2$ counts distinct allowed wavevectors; each supports two electron [spin](../../../../../../spin.md) states. Filling the disk at zero temperature gives

$$
N_e=2\frac{LA_c}{(2\pi)^2}\,\pi k_F^2.
$$

Using $A_c=2a^2$ and $k_F^2=\pi^2/(2a^2)$,

$$
\boxed{\frac{N_e}{L}=\frac{A_c k_F^2}{2\pi}
=\frac{\pi}{2}\simeq1.571\text{ electrons per lattice point}}.
$$

The circle fills a fraction $\pi/4$ of the first-zone area, while one spin-degenerate band has two states per primitive cell; these give the same result. A single-spin count would instead be $\pi/4$, omitting the physical twofold [spin degeneracy](../../../../../../spin-degeneracy.md) of this model. The circle has only just reached the sides, so the zone corners remain unfilled: first contact does not mean filling the entire first band.
