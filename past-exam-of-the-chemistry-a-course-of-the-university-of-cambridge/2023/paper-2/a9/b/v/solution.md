<h1 id="a9/b/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

For an isotropic two-dimensional free-electron model, the occupied states form a disk centered at the reciprocal origin. Its [Fermi circle](../../../../../../fermi-circle.md) first touches a zone side when its radius equals half the distance to a nearest reciprocal point:

$$
\boxed{k_F=\frac{|\mathbf b_1|}{2}
=\frac{\pi}{\sqrt2\,a}}.
$$

The contact points have coordinates $(\pm\pi/(2a),\pm\pi/(2a))$ with all four sign combinations. The distance to a zone vertex is $\pi/a$, which is larger; that distance would describe contact with a corner after the circle had already crossed a side.
