<h1 id="a9/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The stipulated conventional cell is a square of side $2a$. Although introduced as a [centered rectangular lattice](../../../../../../centered-rectangular-lattice.md), this equal-side case is a [square Bravais lattice](../../../../../../square-bravais-lattice.md) viewed with a rotated primitive basis. A corner-to-center separation is

$$
d_{\rm nn}=\sqrt{a^2+a^2}=\sqrt2\,a,
$$

which is shorter than the $2a$ separation between adjacent conventional corners. Consequently the first contact of equal circles occurs at

$$
2r=d_{\rm nn},\qquad r=\frac{a}{\sqrt2}.
$$

The conventional square represents two [lattice points](../../../../../../lattice-point.md): one from its four shared corners and one from its center. Its [packing fraction](../../../../../../packing-fraction.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{\eta=\frac{2\pi r^2}{(2a)^2}=\frac{\pi}{4}\simeq0.7854}.
$$

Equivalently, one circle occupies each primitive area $2a^2$. This is square [circle packing](../../../../../../circle-packing.md); the drawing does not define a triangular close-packed lattice.
