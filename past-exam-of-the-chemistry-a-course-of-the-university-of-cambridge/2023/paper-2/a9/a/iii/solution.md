<h1 id="a9/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Using the printed $x,y$ axes, take a lower vertex of cell B as origin. One allowed pair of [primitive lattice translation vectors](../../../../../../primitive-lattice-translation-vector.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathbf a_1=a(\hat{\mathbf x}+\hat{\mathbf y}),\qquad
\mathbf a_2=a(-\hat{\mathbf x}+\hat{\mathbf y})}.
$$

Both vectors connect nearest [lattice points](../../../../../../lattice-point.md), have length $\sqrt2a$, and are perpendicular. Their determinant has magnitude $2a^2$, consistent with a primitive area. Exchanging them or reversing their directions gives equivalent basis conventions; the corresponding [reciprocal lattice vectors](../../../../../../reciprocal-lattice-vector.md) must then use the same convention.
