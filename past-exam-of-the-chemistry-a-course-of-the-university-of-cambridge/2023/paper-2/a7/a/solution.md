<h1 id="a7/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Choose the ground-state energy as zero and write $x=\varepsilon/(k_BT)$. The [molecular partition function](../../../../../molecular-partition-function.md) must sum individual states, so the triply degenerate excited level contributes three [Boltzmann factors](../../../../../boltzmann-factor.md):

$$
\boxed{q=1+3e^{-x}}.
$$

The probabilities of occupying the two **levels** are

$$
\boxed{P_0=\frac1{1+3e^{-x}}},\qquad
\boxed{P_1=\frac{3e^{-x}}{1+3e^{-x}}}.
$$

Each of the three individual excited states has probability $e^{-x}/q$. At high temperature the four states become equiprobable, so the two levels have probabilities $1/4$ and $3/4$, not $1/2$ each. This is the distinction between a level and its [state degeneracy](../../../../../state-degeneracy.md).
