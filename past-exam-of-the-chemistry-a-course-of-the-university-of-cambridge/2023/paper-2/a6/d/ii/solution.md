<h1 id="a6/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Dividing the total adsorbed population by the number of underlying [adsorption sites](../../../../../../adsorption-site.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\theta_{\rm tot}=\frac{N_{\rm ads}}{M}=\frac{bp}{1-bp}},\qquad
\boxed{b=K_{\rm ads}=\frac{k_a}{k_d}},\qquad 0\leq bp<1.
$$

This multilayer uptake can exceed one because it is a mean column height. The occupied-site fraction remains $1-\theta_0=bp<1$. If each layer has the same [binding energy](../../../../../../binding-energy.md) and microscopic states assumed in the single-layer calculation, the corresponding thermodynamic expression for $b$ is also the one obtained there; the kinetic ratio is the definition supplied by this multilayer model.
