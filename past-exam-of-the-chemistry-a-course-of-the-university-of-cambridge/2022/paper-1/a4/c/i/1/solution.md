<h1 id="a4/c/i/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Fill four spatial [molecular orbitals](../../../../../../../molecular-orbital.md) with the eight $\pi$ [electrons](../../../../../../../electron.md). For negative $\beta_S,\beta_L$ and $|\beta_S|>|\beta_L|$, the occupied [energies](../../../../../../../energy-split.md) are $\alpha+\beta_S+\beta_L$, $\alpha+\beta_S-\beta_L$, and the pair at $\alpha-\sqrt{\beta_S^2+\beta_L^2}$. Therefore

$$
E_\pi=8\alpha+4\beta_S-4\sqrt{\beta_S^2+\beta_L^2}.
$$

For equal integrals, $\beta_S=\beta_L=\beta<0$, this becomes

$$
\boxed{E_\pi^{\rm uniform}=8\alpha+4(1+\sqrt2)\beta.}
$$

At equal bond lengths, the last two [electrons](../../../../../../../electron.md) occupy the frontier degeneracy at $E=\alpha$, so their distribution within that degenerate space does not change the bare [Hückel method](../../../../../../../huckel-method.md) [energy](../../../../../../../energy-split.md).
