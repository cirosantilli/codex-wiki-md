↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

For $\beta_L=0$, four isolated short-bond dimers each contribute $2(\alpha+\beta_S)$:

$$
\boxed{E_\pi^{\rm dimers}=8\alpha+8\beta_S.}
$$

These are different coupling limits. Keeping $\beta_S=\beta$ while simply removing long-bond coupling does not represent a small distortion at fixed mean bond strength.
