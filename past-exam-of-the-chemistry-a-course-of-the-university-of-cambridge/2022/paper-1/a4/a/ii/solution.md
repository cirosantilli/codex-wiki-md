<h1 id="a4/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For the $p_z$ [atomic orbital](../../../../../../atomic-orbital.md) basis, only orbitals whose centres remain fixed contribute to a [character](../../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md). Each such orbital contributes $+1$ if its $z$ direction is preserved and $-1$ if it is reversed. In the standard [D4h molecular point group](../../../../../../d4h-molecular-point-group.md) class order,

$$
\begin{array}{c|rrrrrrrrrr}
&E&2C_4&C_2&2C_2'&2C_2''&i&2S_4&\sigma_h&2\sigma_v&2\sigma_d\\\hline
\chi_{p_z}&8&0&0&0&0&0&0&-8&0&0
\end{array}
$$

No nonidentity operation other than $\sigma_h$ leaves any [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) site fixed. Reflection in the molecular plane fixes all eight sites but reverses every $p_z$ function.

[Molecular character reduction](../../../../../../molecular-character-reduction.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\Gamma_{p_z}=A_{1u}+A_{2u}+B_{1u}+B_{2u}+2E_g.}
$$

The dimensions sum to $1+1+1+1+2\cdot2=8$. The two copies of $E_g$ are separate equivalent [irreducible representations](../../../../../../irreducible-representation.md) which may mix with one another; they do not force all four functions to have a common [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md).
