<h1 id="a1/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Multiply the first-order equation by $\Psi_i^{(0)*}$ and integrate. The first term vanishes because [Hermitian conjugation](../../../../../../hermitian-conjugation.md) moves the unperturbed [Hamiltonian](../../../../../../hamiltonian.md) onto the known bra:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\langle\Psi_i^{(0)}|(\hat H^{(0)}-E_i^{(0)})|\Psi_i^{(1)}\rangle
&=\langle\hat H^{(0)}\Psi_i^{(0)}|\Psi_i^{(1)}\rangle
-E_i^{(0)}\langle\Psi_i^{(0)}|\Psi_i^{(1)}\rangle\\
&=0.
\end{aligned}
$$

Here $E_i^{(0)}$ is real. The remaining equation is

$$
\langle\Psi_i^{(0)}|\hat H^{(1)}|\Psi_i^{(0)}\rangle
-E_i^{(1)}\langle\Psi_i^{(0)}|\Psi_i^{(0)}\rangle=0.
$$

Using [wavefunction normalization](../../../../../../wavefunction-normalization.md) therefore yields

$$
\boxed{E_i^{(1)}=\langle\Psi_i^{(0)}|\hat H^{(1)}|\Psi_i^{(0)}\rangle
=\int\Psi_i^{(0)*}\hat H^{(1)}\Psi_i^{(0)}\,\mathrm d\tau.}
$$

Thus the first-order [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) coefficient is the perturbation's [expectation value](../../../../../../expectation-value.md) in the unperturbed [quantum state](../../../../../../quantum-state.md).
