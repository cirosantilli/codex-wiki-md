↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

For this [quantum harmonic oscillator](../../../../../../quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md), $\hat H^{(1)}=x^4$. The [Gaussian integral](../../../../../../gaussian-integral.md) gives

$$
E_0^{(1)}=\frac1{\sqrt\pi}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}x^4e^{-x^2}\,\mathrm dx
=\frac1{\sqrt\pi}\frac{3\sqrt\pi}{4}
=\boxed{\frac34.}
$$

The distinction between the coefficient and the actual first-order shift matters: **the [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) shift is $3\lambda/4$**, so $E_0=1/2+3\lambda/4+O(\lambda^2)$ in the stated [atomic units](../../../../../../atomic-units.md).
