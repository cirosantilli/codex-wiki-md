<h1 id="a2/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $P_{12}$ exchange the complete labels of the two [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md). The spatial factors obey $P_{12}\Phi_\pm=\pm\Phi_\pm$. The [singlet state](../../../../../../singlet-state.md) [spin](../../../../../../spin.md) factor is antisymmetric, while each [triplet state](../../../../../../spin-one-half-triplet-state.md) [spin](../../../../../../spin.md) factor is symmetric. Thus in all four cases

$$
\boxed{P_{12}\Psi=-\Psi,}
$$

as required by the [Pauli exclusion principle](../../../../../../pauli-exclusion-principle.md). In particular, the $M_S=0$ [triplet state](../../../../../../spin-one-half-triplet-state.md) remains a triplet despite having opposite measured [spin](../../../../../../spin.md) projections in each product term.

For a spin-independent [Hamiltonian](../../../../../../hamiltonian.md) and the same fixed spatial [atomic orbitals](../../../../../../atomic-orbital.md), direct evaluation gives

$$
E_{\rm singlet}=h_a+h_b+J_{ab}+K_{ab},\qquad
E_{\rm triplet}=h_a+h_b+J_{ab}-K_{ab},
$$

where $J_{ab}$ is the direct [Coulomb integral](../../../../../../coulomb-integral.md) and $K_{ab}>0$ is the [exchange integral](../../../../../../exchange-integral.md) for the [helium](../../../../../../helium.md) $1s,2s$ pair. Therefore

$$
\boxed{E_{\rm singlet}-E_{\rm triplet}=2K_{ab}>0.}
$$

The antisymmetric spatial [wavefunction](../../../../../../wave-function-split.md) vanishes when the two positions coincide and reduces their Coulomb repulsion. The lower [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) of the maximum-spin member of this fixed [electron configuration](../../../../../../electron-configuration.md) is the first of [Hund's rules](../../../../../../hund-s-rules.md).
