<h1 id="a2/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [Pauli exclusion principle](../../../../../../pauli-exclusion-principle.md) requires the complete [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) [wavefunction](../../../../../../wave-function-split.md) to change sign on exchange of any two [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md), including both position and [spin](../../../../../../spin.md) labels. Let $a=1s$ and $b=2s$ be normalized, orthogonal spatial [atomic orbitals](../../../../../../atomic-orbital.md), and let $\alpha,\beta$ denote [spin](../../../../../../spin.md) up and down.

The symmetric and antisymmetric spatial functions are

$$
\Phi_\pm=\frac{a(1)b(2)\pm b(1)a(2)}{\sqrt2}.
$$

The [singlet state](../../../../../../singlet-state.md) is

$$
\boxed{\Psi_{S=0}=\Phi_+\,
\frac{\alpha(1)\beta(2)-\beta(1)\alpha(2)}{\sqrt2}.}
$$

The three [triplet states](../../../../../../spin-one-half-triplet-state.md) share the antisymmetric spatial factor:

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
\Psi_{1,+1}&=\Phi_-\alpha(1)\alpha(2),\\
\Psi_{1,0}&=\Phi_-\frac{\alpha(1)\beta(2)+\beta(1)\alpha(2)}{\sqrt2},\\
\Psi_{1,-1}&=\Phi_-\beta(1)\beta(2).
\end{aligned}}
$$

Every factor and complete product is normalized. Here the subscripts are $(S,M_S)$; the spatial [electron configuration](../../../../../../electron-configuration.md) is $1s2s$ in each case.
