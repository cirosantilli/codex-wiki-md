An [electron configuration](../../../../../../electron-configuration.md) specifies the occupied one-electron subshells and their populations, such as $1s^22s^22p^2$, without fixing how all orbital and [spin](../../../../../../spin.md) [angular momenta](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) are coupled.

An [atomic term](../../../../../../atomic-term.md) groups states of a configuration with the same $L$ and $S$. An [atomic fine-structure level](../../../../../../atomic-fine-structure-level.md) additionally specifies $J$; [spin-orbit coupling](../../../../../../spin-orbit-interaction.md) can split one term into several such levels. In zero external field, a level retains $2J+1$ magnetic substates.

The [term symbol](../../../../../../term-symbol.md) is

$$
\boxed{{}^{2S+1}L_J,}
$$

with letters $S,P,D,F,G,\ldots$ denoting $L=0,1,2,3,4,\ldots$. The superscript is the [spin multiplicity](../../../../../../spin-multiplicity.md). Without the $J$ subscript, ${}^{2S+1}L$ identifies an [atomic term](../../../../../../atomic-term.md), rather than an individual fine-structure level.
