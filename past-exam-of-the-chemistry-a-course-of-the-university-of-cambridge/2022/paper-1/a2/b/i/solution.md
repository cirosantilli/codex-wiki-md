<h1 id="a2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

In [LS coupling](../../../../../../ls-coupling.md), add all orbital and [spin](../../../../../../spin.md) [angular momenta](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) separately:

$$
\mathbf L=\sum_a\mathbf l_a,\qquad
\mathbf S=\sum_a\mathbf s_a,\qquad
\mathbf J=\mathbf L+\mathbf S.
$$

The [quantum numbers](../../../../../../quantum-number.md) specify

$$
\begin{aligned}
L^2&\longrightarrow\hbar^2L(L+1),&L_z&\longrightarrow\hbar M_L,\\
S^2&\longrightarrow\hbar^2S(S+1),&S_z&\longrightarrow\hbar M_S,\\
J^2&\longrightarrow\hbar^2J(J+1).
\end{aligned}
$$

Thus $M_L=-L,\ldots,L$ and $M_S=-S,\ldots,S$. The [addition of angular momentum](../../../../../../addition-of-angular-momentum.md) rule gives

$$
\boxed{J=|L-S|,|L-S|+1,\ldots,L+S.}
$$

In a product basis with individual projection labels,

$$
\boxed{M_L=\sum_a m_{l,a},\qquad M_S=\sum_a m_{s,a}.}
$$

The magnitudes $L$ and $S$ are not generally the arithmetic sums of the individual magnitudes; they result from angular-momentum coupling. The sum rules for projections remain valid for every product component of a coupled [angular momentum eigenstate](../../../../../../angular-momentum-eigenstate.md).
