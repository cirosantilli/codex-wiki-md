<h1 id="a2/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Closed subshells have $L=S=0$, so only the open $p$ or $d$ subshell determines each [atomic term](../../../../../../atomic-term.md). Apply [Hund's rules](../../../../../../hund-s-rules.md) in order: maximize $S$, maximize $L$ at that $S$, then choose the lowest $J$ below half filling or the highest $J$ above half filling.

For [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md), the two $2p$ [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) occupy different magnetic [atomic orbitals](../../../../../../atomic-orbital.md) with parallel [spins](../../../../../../spin.md), giving $S=1$. Their largest allowed $M_L$ at that [spin](../../../../../../spin.md) is $1+0=1$, so $L=1$, a $P$ term. The $p^2$ subshell is less than half full, giving $J=|1-1|=0$. **The ground level is ${}^3P_0$.**

For [vanadium](../../../../../../vanadium.md), three parallel-spin $3d$ [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) give $S=3/2$. The largest $M_L$ compatible with the [Pauli exclusion principle](../../../../../../pauli-exclusion-principle.md) is $2+1+0=3$, hence $L=3$, an $F$ term. The $d^3$ subshell is less than half full, so $J=3-3/2=3/2$. **The ground level is ${}^4F_{3/2}$.**

For [cobalt](../../../../../../cobalt.md), the $d^7$ subshell is equivalent to three $d$ holes for the allowed $L,S$ terms, so its maximum-spin, maximum-$L$ term is again ${}^4F$. Explicitly, five spin-up [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) fill all $m_l$ values and two spin-down [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) can occupy $m_l=2,1$, giving $M_S=3/2$ and $M_L=3$. Since $d^7$ is more than half full, $J=3+3/2=9/2$. **The ground level is ${}^4F_{9/2}$.**
