From the complete [normal mode](../../../../../../normal-mode.md) result, the [vibrational infrared activity](../../../../../../vibrational-infrared-activity.md) belongs to

$$
\boxed{\Gamma_{\rm IR}=A_{2u}+3E_{1u}.}
$$

The $A_{2u}$ fundamental couples to the out-of-plane dipole component and each $E_{1u}$ fundamental to the two in-plane components. Counting one frequency for each degenerate pair, the expected number is **$1+3=4$ fundamental bands**, containing seven vibrational coordinates.

This is the symmetry count for the full molecule, including [hydrogen](../../../../../../hydrogen.md) displacements. The experimental compilation likewise lists the out-of-plane $A_{2u}$ fundamental and three $E_{1u}$ fundamentals; anharmonic resonances and weak intensity borrowing can produce additional observed features beyond the ideal fundamental count.[Vibrational energy-level table: A2u row and three E1u fundamentals](../../../../../../benzene-vibrational-data-in-the-nist-chemistry-webbook.md)
