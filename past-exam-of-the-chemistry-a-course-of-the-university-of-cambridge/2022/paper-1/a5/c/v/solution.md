<h1 id="a5/c/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

A coordinate $Q_s$ of an $A_{1g}$ [normal mode](../../../../../../normal-mode.md) is unchanged by every [molecular symmetry operation](../../../../../../molecular-symmetry-operation.md). Every harmonic-oscillator excitation $H_n(Q_s)e^{-Q_s^2/2}$ therefore also has symmetry $A_{1g}$, including odd values of $n$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{B_{2u}\otimes E_{2g}\otimes A_{1g}=E_{1u}}
$$

for any number of quanta in that totally symmetric coordinate. **The same electric-dipole permission survives every $A_{1g}$ excitation.**

This statement concerns symmetry. Actual progression intensities depend on vibrational [inner products](../../../../../../inner-product-split.md), equilibrium displacements and the coordinate dependence of the [electric dipole transition moment](../../../../../../electric-dipole-transition-moment.md); a permitted line need not have measurable intensity for every vibrational quantum number.
