<h1 id="a5/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Each [hydrogen](../../../../../../hydrogen.md) lies on the radial line through its attached [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md). Consequently every [molecular symmetry operation](../../../../../../molecular-symmetry-operation.md) fixes or permutes the [hydrogen](../../../../../../hydrogen.md) sites exactly as it does the [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) sites, and the Cartesian trace is the same. Thus $\Gamma_H=\Gamma_C$ and the complete benzene displacement [group representation](../../../../../../group-representation.md) is **$\Gamma_{3N}=2\Gamma_C$**, of dimension $36$.

Subtract translations $A_{2u}+E_{1u}$ and rotations $A_{2g}+E_{1g}$. The full set of [normal modes](../../../../../../normal-mode.md) is

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
\Gamma_{\rm vib}={}&2A_{1g}+A_{2g}+2B_{2g}+E_{1g}+4E_{2g}\\
&+A_{2u}+2B_{1u}+2B_{2u}+3E_{1u}+2E_{2u}.
\end{aligned}}
$$

The one-dimensional components contribute $2+1+2+1+2+2=10$ coordinates. The two-dimensional components contribute $2(1+4+3+2)=20$, giving **$30=3(12)-6$ vibrational degrees of freedom**. There are twenty distinct symmetry-labelled fundamentals before accidental coincidences, because each doubly degenerate pair has one frequency.

Subtracting translations and rotations directly from the carbon-only result would instead give twelve carbon-skeleton coordinates. That is a six-site mechanical model, not all the [normal modes](../../../../../../normal-mode.md) of $\mathrm{C_6H_6}$. The [hydrogen](../../../../../../hydrogen.md) contribution is required for the actual molecule.
