<h1 id="a8/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

[Chloroform](../../../../../../chloroform.md) has the [C3v molecular point group](../../../../../../c3v-molecular-point-group.md). Its three identical $^{35}\mathrm{Cl}$ atoms are interchanged by three proper rotations, so **$\sigma=3$**. The three mirror operations do not increase the [rotational symmetry number](../../../../../../rotational-symmetry-number.md) to six. Isotopic identity matters for this count.

Using the supplied wavenumber form of $k_{\rm B}$,

$$
\theta_a=\theta_b=\frac{0.110}{0.695}=0.15827\ \mathrm K,\qquad
\theta_c=\frac{0.0593}{0.695}=0.085324\ \mathrm K.
$$

All are far below $298\ \mathrm K$, justifying the high-temperature nonlinear [rigid rotor](../../../../../../rigid-rotor.md) expression. Then

$$
q_{\rm rot}=\frac{\sqrt\pi}{3}
\left[\frac{298^3}{(0.15827)^2(0.085324)}\right]^{1/2}
\simeq6.5741\times10^4,
$$

and

$$
\boxed{S_{\rm rot,m}=R[\ln q_{\rm rot}+3/2]
\simeq104.71\ \mathrm{J\,K^{-1}\,mol^{-1}}.}
$$

The calculation treats the [molecule](../../../../../../molecule-split.md) as rigid and separates electronic, vibrational and nuclear-spin contributions from the requested rotational term.
