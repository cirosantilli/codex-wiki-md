↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The integer molecular mass of the stated isotopologue is

$$
m=(12+1+3\cdot35)u=118u\simeq1.95944\times10^{-25}\ \mathrm{kg}.
$$

Take $p^\circ=1\ \mathrm{bar}=10^5\ \mathrm{Pa}$, the recommended [standard pressure](../../../../../../standard-pressure.md).[Entry S05921, [standard pressure](../../../../../../standard-pressure.md): recommended value 100000 Pa](../../../../../../iupac-compendium-of-chemical-terminology-fifth-edition-gold-book-by-international-union-of-pure-and-applied-chemistry.md)

At $298\ \mathrm K$, the [thermal de Broglie wavelength](../../../../../../thermal-de-broglie-wavelength.md) is $\Lambda\simeq9.3100\times10^{-12}\ \mathrm m$. The result of part (a) gives

$$
S^\circ_{\rm trans,m}
=R\left[\ln\frac{k_{\rm B}(298)}{(10^5)\Lambda^3}+\frac52\right]
\simeq\boxed{168.34\ \mathrm{J\,K^{-1}\,mol^{-1}}.}
$$

If $1\ \mathrm{atm}$ is used instead, the result decreases by $R\ln(1.01325)\simeq0.1094\ \mathrm{J\,K^{-1}\,mol^{-1}}$, giving $168.23$ in the same units. Specifying the [pressure](../../../../../../pressure-split.md) removes that small convention dependence.
