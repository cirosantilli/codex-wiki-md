↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For independent internal rotational states, the rotational factor in the many-molecule [partition function](../../../../../../canonical-partition-function.md) is $q_{\rm rot}^N$. The indistinguishability factor has already been included in the translational part and is not applied a second time to the internal factor. Thus

$$
A_{\rm rot}=-Nk_{\rm B}T\ln q_{\rm rot},\qquad
\frac{S_{\rm rot,m}}R=\ln q_{\rm rot}
+T\left(\frac{\partial\ln q_{\rm rot}}{\partial T}\right).
$$

A nonlinear [rigid rotor](../../../../../../rigid-rotor.md) has three rotational quadratic degrees of freedom; in the high-temperature limit, $q_{\rm rot}\propto T^{3/2}$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{S_{\rm rot,m}=R\left(\ln q_{\rm rot}+\frac32\right).}
$$

This is the [rotational entropy](../../../../../../rotational-entropy.md) contribution, with the appropriate [rotational symmetry number](../../../../../../rotational-symmetry-number.md) already inside $q_{\rm rot}$. It has no ideal-gas [pressure](../../../../../../pressure-split.md) dependence.
