<h1 id="a7/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Chemical equilibrium](../../../../../chemical-equilibrium.md) for $\mathrm M\rightleftharpoons\mathrm{M^+}+\mathrm{e^-}$ requires

$$
\mu_{\rm M}=\mu_{\rm M^+}+\mu_e.
$$

Insert the given [chemical potential](../../../../../chemical-potential-split.md) form and define the positive [ionization energy](../../../../../ionization-energy.md)

$$
E_I=\varepsilon_{\rm M^+}+\varepsilon_e-\varepsilon_{\rm M}.
$$

After collecting logarithms,

$$
k_{\rm B}T\ln\left(\frac{f_{\rm M}c_{\rm M^+}c_e}
{c_{\rm M}f_{\rm M^+}f_e}\right)+E_I=0,
$$

so

$$
\frac{c_{\rm M^+}c_e}{c_{\rm M}}
=\frac{f_{\rm M^+}f_e}{f_{\rm M}}e^{-E_I/k_{\rm B}T}.
$$

Define a dimensionless concentration [equilibrium constant](../../../../../equilibrium-constant.md) using a standard [number density](../../../../../number-density.md) $c^\circ$:

$$
\boxed{K_c=\frac{(c_{\rm M^+}/c^\circ)(c_e/c^\circ)}{c_{\rm M}/c^\circ}
=\frac{f_ef_{\rm M^+}}{f_{\rm M}}\frac1{c^\circ}e^{-E_I/k_{\rm B}T}.}
$$

Each $f_i$ is a [molecular partition function density](../../../../../molecular-partition-function-density.md), with units of inverse volume. Consequently $f_ef_{\rm M^+}/f_{\rm M}$ has density units and division by $c^\circ$ makes $K_c$ dimensionless. All ground-state energies must use the same [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) reference.
