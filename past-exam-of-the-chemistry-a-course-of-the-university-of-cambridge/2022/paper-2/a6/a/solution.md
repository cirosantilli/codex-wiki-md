<h1 id="a6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Take the [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) of an isolated, field-free gas atom as zero, and let $E_b>0$ be the field-independent [binding energy](../../../../../binding-energy.md) at one [adsorption site](../../../../../adsorption-site.md). The two adsorbed [spin](../../../../../spin.md) energies are

$$
\boxed{\varepsilon_\pm=-E_b\pm\mu_{\rm B}B.}
$$

Here $\mu_{\rm B}$ is the [Bohr magneton](../../../../../bohr-magneton.md). The lower state is stabilized by increasing $|B|$; the assignment of the two signs to names for the [spin](../../../../../spin.md) projections is a convention.
