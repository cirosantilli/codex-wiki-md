↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

At [adsorption](../../../../../adsorption.md) equilibrium, $\mu_{\rm ads}=\mu_g$. The expressions from part (d) give

$$
\frac{\theta}{1-\theta}
=z\frac{p\Lambda^3}{2k_{\rm B}T}
=\frac{p\Lambda^3}{k_{\rm B}T}e^{E_b/k_{\rm B}T}\cosh x.
$$

Define

$$
\boxed{b(T,B)=\frac{\Lambda^3}{k_{\rm B}T}
 e^{E_b/k_{\rm B}T}\cosh\!\left(\frac{\mu_{\rm B}B}{k_{\rm B}T}\right).}
$$

Solving the occupation ratio yields

$$
\boxed{\theta=\frac{bp}{1+bp}.}
$$

The coefficient $b$ has units of inverse [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md), so $bp$ is dimensionless. The two [spin](../../../../../spin.md) states contribute a factor two on both sides of the [adsorption](../../../../../adsorption.md) equilibrium and cancel; retaining the surface factor but dropping the bulk degeneracy would incorrectly double $b$.
