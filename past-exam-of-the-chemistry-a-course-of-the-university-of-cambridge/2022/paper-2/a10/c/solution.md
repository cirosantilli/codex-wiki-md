<h1 id="a10/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

[Gallium](../../../../../gallium.md) has three valence [electrons](../../../../../electron.md), one fewer than silicon, and acts as an [acceptor impurity](../../../../../acceptor-impurity.md). A neutral acceptor can be described as a negatively charged centre binding an [electron hole](../../../../../electron-hole.md). Thermal release of these holes gives a [p-type semiconductor](../../../../../p-type-semiconductor.md).

At low [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), the bound hole can absorb far-IR photons in transitions between acceptor levels. The simplest scalar [hydrogenic acceptor state](../../../../../hydrogenic-acceptor-state.md) model predicts

$$
E_n=-\frac{R_A^*}{n^2},\qquad
R_A^*=R_H\frac{m_h^*/m_e}{\epsilon_r^2},
$$

so one expects **a discrete bound-hole absorption series followed by a photoionization continuum**. In that idealization, the first lines would be at $3R_A^*/4$, $8R_A^*/9$ and $15R_A^*/16$, with threshold $R_A^*$.

The hole [effective mass](../../../../../band-effective-mass.md) is not supplied, so the [electron](../../../../../electron.md) mass $0.2m_e$ cannot be reused to give numerical Ga line positions. In a real [valence band](../../../../../valence-band.md), multiple hole bands, anisotropic masses and spin-orbit effects can split and complicate the series; acceptor spectra are generally less accurately described by one scalar hydrogenic ladder.[Section 7.3, printed p. 79: departures from the hydrogenic model for degenerate valence bands and acceptors](../../../../../optical-properties-of-solids-by-mildred-s-dresselhaus.md)
