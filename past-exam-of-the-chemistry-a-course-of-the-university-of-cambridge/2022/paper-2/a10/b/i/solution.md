<h1 id="a10/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

[Arsenic](../../../../../../arsenic.md) has five valence [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md), one more than [silicon](../../../../../../silicon.md). As a substitutional [donor impurity](../../../../../../donor-impurity.md), it supplies a weakly bound extra [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) which is largely thermally released into the [conduction band](../../../../../../conduction-band.md) at room [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md), leaving an ionized positive donor.

Consequently **the mobile [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) density increases far above the intrinsic value**, so the conductivity increases substantially even at ppm dopant levels. The crystal remains macroscopically neutral: fixed donor charge compensates the added mobile charge.
