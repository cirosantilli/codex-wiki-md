<h1 id="a9/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [energy-band widths](../../../../../../energy-band-width.md) are the difference between their extrema:

$$
\boxed{W_s=4t_s=4|\beta_s|,\qquad W_p=4t_p=4|\beta_p|.}
$$

For the separated bands, the top of the $s$ band and bottom of the $p$ band are both at $k=\pi/a$. Hence

$$
\boxed{E_g^{\min}=\alpha_p-\alpha_s-2(t_s+t_p).}
$$

It is a **direct [band gap](../../../../../../band-gap.md) at the zone boundary**, because both limiting states have the same [wavevector](../../../../../../wavevector.md). This result uses the opposite signs of the $s$ and axial-$p$ hoppings.
