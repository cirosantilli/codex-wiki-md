↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

For $0<\gamma<t$, the positive branch ranges from $2\gamma$ to $2t$, and the negative branch from $-2t$ to $-2\gamma$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{W_1=W_2=2(t-\gamma)=2\big(|\beta|-|\beta_{sp}|\big).}
$$

The smallest separation is between the upper-band minimum and lower-band maximum at the same [wavevector](../../../../../../wavevector.md) $k=\pi/(2a)$:

$$
\boxed{E_g^{\min}=4\gamma=4|\beta_{sp}|.}
$$

It is a **direct [band gap](../../../../../../band-gap.md) at the interior [avoided crossing](../../../../../../avoided-crossing.md)**. The zero-mixing limit has zero gap; the width is not $4|\beta|$ because these are the mixed energy-ordered branches, each turning around at the middle of the plotted interval.
