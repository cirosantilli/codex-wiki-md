↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Let the common on-site [energy](../../../../../../../energy-split.md) be zero and the common hopping magnitude be $t=|\beta|$. Consistent with the axial-$p$ convention and the formula in part (iii), the hopping signs are $\beta_s=-t$ and $\beta_p=+t$.

Without mixing,

$$
\boxed{E_s(k)=-2t\cos(ka),\qquad E_p(k)=+2t\cos(ka).}
$$

The curves cross at **$k=\pi/(2a)$, $E=0$**, with no gap. The lower-left panel shows the continuous pure-$s$ and pure-$p$ branches. Their identities remain fixed through the crossing; the energy-ordered lower state changes from $s$ to $p$ character on passing through it.
