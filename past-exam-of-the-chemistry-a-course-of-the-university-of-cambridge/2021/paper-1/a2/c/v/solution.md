↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

Comparing the [variational method](../../../../../../variational-method.md) estimate with the exact [ground-state energy](../../../../../../ground-state-energy.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{\widetilde E}{E_1}=\frac{10}{\pi^2}=1.01321\ldots>1.}
$$

The estimate is about **$1.32\%$ too high**, consistently with the [quantum variational principle](../../../../../../quantum-variational-principle.md). Its closeness does not mean that the trial [wavefunction](../../../../../../wave-function-split.md) is an exact [energy eigenfunction](../../../../../../energy-eigenfunction.md).
