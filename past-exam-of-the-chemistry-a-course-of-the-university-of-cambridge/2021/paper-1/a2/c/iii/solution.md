<h1 id="a2/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Since

$$
\int_0^a x^2(a-x)^2\,dx=\frac{a^5}{30},
$$

an explicitly normalized trial is

$$
\boxed{\widetilde\psi(x)=\sqrt{\frac{30}{a^5}}\,x(a-x),\qquad 0<x<a.}
$$

It is zero outside the box. The original expression $x(x-a)$ differs by a minus sign, which changes only the [global phase](../../../../../../global-phase.md) and leaves [probabilities](../../../../../../probability.md) and [expectation values](../../../../../../expectation-value.md) unchanged.
