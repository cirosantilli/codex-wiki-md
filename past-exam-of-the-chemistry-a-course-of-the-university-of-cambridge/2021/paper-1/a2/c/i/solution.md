↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The trial already vanishes at $x=0$. The second [Dirichlet boundary condition](../../../../../../dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) requires $a(a-c)=0$, hence **$c=a$** for a box of positive length. This makes the trial an admissible [particle in a box](../../../../../../infinite-square-well.md) [wavefunction](../../../../../../wave-function-split.md).
