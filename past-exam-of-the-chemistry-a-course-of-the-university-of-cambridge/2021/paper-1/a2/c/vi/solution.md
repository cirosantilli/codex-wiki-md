<h1 id="a2/c/vi/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vi](../vi.md)

The trial is even under reflection about the midpoint. The normalized box [eigenfunctions](../../../../../../eigenfunction.md) satisfy $\phi_n(a-x)=(-1)^{n+1}\phi_n(x)$, so [orthogonality](../../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) between even and odd functions gives $c_n=0$ for even $n$. If the higher odd contributions $n\geq5$ are neglected, the [weighted mean](../../../../../../weighted-arithmetic-mean.md) from the [quantum variational principle](../../../../../../quantum-variational-principle.md) becomes

$$
\widetilde E\simeq
\frac{|c_1|^2E_1+|c_3|^2E_3}{|c_1|^2+|c_3|^2}.
$$

Rearranging yields the [two-level variational contamination estimate](../../../../../../two-level-variational-contamination-estimate.md)

$$
\boxed{\frac{|c_3|^2}{|c_1|^2}\simeq
\frac{\widetilde E-E_1}{E_3-\widetilde E}.}
$$

The required assumption concerns the neglected contributions to the [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) as well as their small probabilities. Numerically the two-level estimate is $0.0016542$. It is not an exact identity: direct expansion of this parabolic trial gives $c_n\propto n^{-3}$ for odd $n$, hence the exact ratio is $1/729=0.0013717$. The omitted higher odd states account for the difference.
