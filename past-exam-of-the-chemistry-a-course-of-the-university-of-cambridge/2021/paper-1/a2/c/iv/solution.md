<h1 id="a2/c/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

[Integration by parts](../../../../../../integration-by-parts.md) and the zero endpoint values turn the [Rayleigh quotient](../../../../../../rayleigh-quotient.md) into a positive [kinetic energy](../../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) integral:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\widetilde E&=\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\int_0^a|\widetilde\psi'(x)|^2\,dx\\
&=\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\frac{30}{a^5}\int_0^a(a-2x)^2\,dx
=\boxed{\frac{5\hbar^2}{ma^2}}.
\end{aligned}
$$

This is the [parabolic variational trial for a particle in a box](../../../../../../parabolic-variational-trial-for-a-particle-in-a-box.md). The [potential energy](../../../../../../potential-energy.md) vanishes inside the box.
