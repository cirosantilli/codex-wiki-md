<h1 id="a2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Twice [differentiation](../../../../../../differentiation.md) the sine gives

$$
\hat H\sin\frac{n\pi x}{a}
=\frac{n^2\pi^2\hbar^2}{2ma^2}\sin\frac{n\pi x}{a}.
$$

Both [Dirichlet boundary conditions](../../../../../../dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) hold when $n$ is an integer. The zero function at $n=0$ is not an [eigenfunction](../../../../../../eigenfunction.md), and negative $n$ merely changes an overall sign. Therefore

$$
\boxed{n=1,2,\ldots,\qquad E_n=\frac{n^2\pi^2\hbar^2}{2ma^2}.}
$$

The corresponding normalized [eigenfunction](../../../../../../eigenfunction.md) is $\sqrt{2/a}\sin(n\pi x/a)$.
