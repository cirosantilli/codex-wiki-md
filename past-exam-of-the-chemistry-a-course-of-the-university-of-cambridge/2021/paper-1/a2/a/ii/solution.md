<h1 id="a2/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Expand the trial in a complete [orthonormal eigenbasis](../../../../../../orthonormal-eigenbasis.md) of the [Quantum Hamiltonian](../../../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md), $\widetilde\Psi=\sum_nc_n\phi_n$, with $\hat H\phi_n=E_n\phi_n$. [Orthonormality](../../../../../../orthonormal-set.md) gives

$$
\widetilde E=
\frac{\sum_n|c_n|^2E_n}{\sum_n|c_n|^2},\qquad
\widetilde E-E_0=
\frac{\sum_n|c_n|^2(E_n-E_0)}{\sum_n|c_n|^2}\geq0.
$$

Every summand in the numerator is nonnegative because $E_n\geq E_0$. This proves the [quantum variational principle](../../../../../../quantum-variational-principle.md) for the stated discrete expansion; the trial [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) is a [weighted mean](../../../../../../weighted-arithmetic-mean.md) of exact [energy eigenvalues](../../../../../../energy-eigenvalue-split.md).
