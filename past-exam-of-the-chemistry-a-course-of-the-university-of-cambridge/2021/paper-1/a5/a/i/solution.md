A [symmetry orbital](../../../../../../symmetry-adapted-linear-combination.md) is a [linear combination of atomic orbitals](../../../../../../linear-combination-of-atomic-orbitals.md) transforming as a component of an [irreducible representation](../../../../../../irreducible-representation.md) of the molecular [point group](../../../../../../point-group.md). A [molecular orbital](../../../../../../molecular-orbital.md) is a one-electron [wavefunction](../../../../../../wave-function-split.md) for the molecule, obtained as an [eigenfunction](../../../../../../eigenfunction.md) of the chosen effective one-electron [Hamiltonian operator](../../../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md). Symmetry organizes the eigenproblem, but a symmetry label alone does not determine its coefficients.
