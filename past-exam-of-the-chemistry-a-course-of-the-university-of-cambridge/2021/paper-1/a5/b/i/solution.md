<h1 id="a5/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The **$z$ axis is a $C_2$ axis** through the centers of opposite cube faces: a half turn permutes atoms within each species. A **body diagonal through sites 1 and 8 is a $C_3$ axis**: a third turn fixes those two opposite vertices and cycles the other three vertices of each tetrahedral sublattice. These are [symmetry operations](../../../../../../symmetry-operation.md) of the [tetrahedral symmetry](../../../../../../tetrahedral-symmetry.md) group $T_d$; a quarter turn about $z$ would interchange the two chemical species and is not a symmetry of the specified cluster.
