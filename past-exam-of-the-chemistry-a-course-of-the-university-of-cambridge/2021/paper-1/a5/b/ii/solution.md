<h1 id="a5/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For the [tetrahedral symmetry](../../../../../../tetrahedral-symmetry.md) classes $(E,8C_3,3C_2,6S_4,6\sigma_d)$, each set of four $s$ [atomic orbitals](../../../../../../atomic-orbital.md) has

$$
\boxed{\chi_{\rm Li}=\chi_{\rm Na}=(4,1,0,0,2).}
$$

The counts are the fixed sites of each [symmetry operation](../../../../../../symmetry-operation.md): a threefold rotation fixes one vertex of each tetrahedron, a twofold or improper fourfold operation fixes none, and a diagonal mirror fixes two. [Character orthogonality](../../../../../../character-orthogonality.md) reduces each set to

$$
\boxed{\Gamma_{\rm Li}=\Gamma_{\rm Na}=A_1\oplus T_2.}
$$

The dimensions $1+3=4$ provide a check. Thus the complete eight-orbital model contains two copies of each of $A_1$ and $T_2$, which can mix across the two species.
