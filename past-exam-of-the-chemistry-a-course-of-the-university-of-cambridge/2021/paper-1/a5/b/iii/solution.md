<h1 id="a5/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Write $L_i$ for the [lithium](../../../../../../lithium.md) $2s$ orbital at site $i$ and $N_i$ for the [sodium](../../../../../../sodium.md) $3s$ orbital. Reading the printed axes, the lithium coordinates have signs $1=(-,+,+)$, $2=(+,-,+)$, $3=(+,+,-)$ and $4=(-,-,-)$; the sodium sites are $5=(+,+,+)$, $6=(-,-,+)$, $7=(-,+,-)$ and $8=(+,-,-)$. The normalized [tetrahedral s-orbital symmetry combinations](../../../../../../tetrahedral-s-orbital-symmetry-combinations.md) are

$$
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
&\mathrm{Li}&\mathrm{Na}\\ \hline
A_1&(L_1+L_2+L_3+L_4)/2&(N_5+N_6+N_7+N_8)/2\\
T_{2x}&(-L_1+L_2+L_3-L_4)/2&(N_5-N_6-N_7+N_8)/2\\
T_{2y}&(L_1-L_2+L_3-L_4)/2&(N_5-N_6+N_7-N_8)/2\\
T_{2z}&(L_1+L_2-L_3-L_4)/2&(N_5+N_6-N_7-N_8)/2
\end{array}
$$

Each $T_2$ row takes its signs from the corresponding coordinate, so the three functions transform together like $(x,y,z)$. These coefficient vectors are [orthonormal](../../../../../../orthonormal-set.md) when intersite [orbital overlap](../../../../../../orbital-overlap.md) is neglected. A common sign reversal of any row is physically immaterial.
