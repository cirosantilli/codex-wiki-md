↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The calculated [rotational constant](../../../../../../rotational-constant.md) gives $\theta_{\rm rot}=1.18721\ \mathrm K$. At room [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md), taken as $300\ \mathrm K$, the continuous maximum is $J_*=10.7404$; comparing the adjacent integer populations gives **$J_{\max}=11$**. Taking $298.15\ \mathrm K$ instead gives $J_*=10.7057$ and the same integer answer.

At $8.7\ \mathrm K$, $J_*=1.41417$. In particular,

$$
\frac{N_2}{N_1}=\frac53e^{-4\theta_{\rm rot}/T}=0.966\ldots<1,
$$

and $N_1>N_0$, so **$J_{\max}=1$**. The integer comparison avoids treating a noninteger [angular momentum quantum number](../../../../../../angular-momentum-quantum-number.md) as a physical state.
