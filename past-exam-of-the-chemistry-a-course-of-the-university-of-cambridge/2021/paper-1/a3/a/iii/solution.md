<h1 id="a3/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For an axis through the [center of mass](../../../../../../center-of-mass.md) perpendicular to the bond, the point-mass [moment of inertia](../../../../../../moment-of-inertia.md) is

$$
\begin{aligned}
I&=m_{\rm C}r_1^2+m_{\rm S}r_2^2\\
&=\frac{m_{\rm C}m_{\rm S}^2+m_{\rm S}m_{\rm C}^2}
{(m_{\rm C}+m_{\rm S})^2}r^2
=\boxed{\frac{m_{\rm C}m_{\rm S}}{m_{\rm C}+m_{\rm S}}r^2}.
\end{aligned}
$$

The prefactor is the [reduced mass](../../../../../../reduced-mass.md) $\mu$; this is the standard reduction of a two-body [linear rigid rotor](../../../../../../linear-rigid-rotor.md) to $I=\mu r^2$.
