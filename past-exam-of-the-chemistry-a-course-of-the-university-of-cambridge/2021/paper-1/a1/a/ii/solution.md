[Cyclic permutation](../../../../../../cyclic-permutation.md) of the coordinates in the preceding [commutator](../../../../../../commutator.md) calculation gives the remaining [orbital angular momentum commutation relations](../../../../../../orbital-angular-momentum-commutation-relations.md):

$$
\boxed{[\hat j_y,\hat j_z]=i\hbar\hat j_x,\qquad [\hat j_z,\hat j_x]=i\hbar\hat j_y.}
$$

Reversing the order of a [commutator](../../../../../../commutator.md) reverses its sign.
