↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

**The squared [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) and one chosen component can have simultaneously sharp values. All three components cannot have simultaneously sharp values in a nonzero-$j$ state.** The possible measured component values are $m\hbar$, while the squared magnitude is $j(j+1)\hbar^2$. This concerns the [quantum state](../../../../../../quantum-state.md), rather than imperfect experimental resolution. The [Robertson uncertainty relation](../../../../../../robertson-uncertainty-principle.md), for example,

$$
\Delta j_x\,\Delta j_y\geq\frac{\hbar}{2}|\langle\hat j_z\rangle|,
$$

quantifies the restriction; its right-hand side may vanish in some states, without making all three components simultaneously sharp.
