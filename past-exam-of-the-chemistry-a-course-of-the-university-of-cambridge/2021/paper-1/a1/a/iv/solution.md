↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The squared [angular momentum operator](../../../../../../angular-momentum-operator-split.md) and one selected component are [commuting operators](../../../../../../commuting-operators.md), so a complete set of common [eigenfunctions](../../../../../../eigenfunction.md) can be chosen: for the $z$ component these are the usual $|j,m\rangle$ [angular momentum eigenstates](../../../../../../angular-momentum-eigenstate.md). Distinct components do not have a common complete [eigenbasis](../../../../../../eigenbasis.md), because their [commutator](../../../../../../commutator.md) is another nonzero component. A $j=0$ state is an exception in which all components vanish; noncommutation does not forbid every individual common [eigenfunction](../../../../../../eigenfunction.md).
