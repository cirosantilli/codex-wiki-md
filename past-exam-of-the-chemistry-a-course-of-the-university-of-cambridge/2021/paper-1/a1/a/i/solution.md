<h1 id="a1/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Write $D_x=y\partial_z-z\partial_y$, $D_y=z\partial_x-x\partial_z$ and $D_z=x\partial_y-y\partial_x$. The [product rule](../../../../../../product-rule.md) applied to a smooth test [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) $f$ gives

$$
[D_x,D_y]f=y\partial_xf-x\partial_yf=-D_zf.
$$

The mixed second [partial derivatives](../../../../../../partial-derivative.md) cancel because they commute. Since the [orbital angular momentum](../../../../../../orbital-angular-momentum.md) components are $\hat j_i=-i\hbar D_i$, their [commutator](../../../../../../commutator.md) is

$$
\boxed{[\hat j_x,\hat j_y]=(-i\hbar)^2(-D_z)=i\hbar\hat j_z.}
$$

This is one of the [orbital angular momentum commutation relations](../../../../../../orbital-angular-momentum-commutation-relations.md); the [Planck constant](../../../../../../planck-constant.md) enters through the definition of each [linear operator](../../../../../../linear-operator.md).
