<h1 id="a1/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

For a $2p$ [atomic orbital](../../../../../../atomic-orbital.md), $l=1$. The radial factor obeys

$$
R'=Ne^{-r/2}(1-r/2),\qquad R''=Ne^{-r/2}(r/4-1).
$$

Substitution into the [radial Schrödinger equation for a central potential](../../../../../../radial-schrodinger-equation-for-a-central-potential.md) gives

$$
-\frac12R''-\frac{R'}r+\frac{R}{r^2}-\frac Rr=-\frac18R.
$$

Thus the [energy eigenvalue](../../../../../../energy-eigenvalue-split.md) is

$$
\boxed{E=-\frac18\,E_{\rm h}\simeq-3.40\ \mathrm{eV}.}
$$

The [normalization constant](../../../../../../normalizing-constant.md) cancels; $E_{\rm h}$ is the [Hartree energy](../../../../../../hartree-energy.md).
