The [spherical harmonic](../../../../../../spherical-harmonic.md) is also an [eigenfunction](../../../../../../eigenfunction.md) of the $z$ component of [orbital angular momentum](../../../../../../orbital-angular-momentum.md):

$$
\boxed{\hat l_zY_{lm}=mY_{lm},\qquad m=-l,-l+1,\ldots,l.}
$$

These are [atomic units](../../../../../../atomic-units.md); restoring units gives $m\hbar$. The integer $m$ is the [magnetic quantum number](../../../../../../magnetic-quantum-number.md).
