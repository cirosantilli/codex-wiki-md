<h1 id="a1/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

[Separation of variables](../../../../../../separation-of-variables.md) makes the angular factor a [spherical harmonic](../../../../../../spherical-harmonic.md). Applying the [Quantum Hamiltonian](../../../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) gives

$$
\hat H(RY_{lm})=
Y_{lm}\left[-\frac{1}{2r^2}\frac{d}{dr}\left(r^2R'\right)
+\frac{l(l+1)}{2r^2}R-\frac{R}{r}\right].
$$

Consequently $RY_{lm}$ is an [energy eigenfunction](../../../../../../energy-eigenfunction.md) **provided the radial factor satisfies**

$$
\boxed{-\frac{1}{2r^2}\frac{d}{dr}(r^2R')+
\left[\frac{l(l+1)}{2r^2}-\frac1r\right]R=ER.}
$$

This is the [radial Schrödinger equation for a central potential](../../../../../../radial-schrodinger-equation-for-a-central-potential.md), here with the attractive [Coulomb potential](../../../../../../coulomb-potential-energy.md). An arbitrary $R$ does not give an [eigenfunction](../../../../../../eigenfunction.md); regularity, decay and [wavefunction normalization](../../../../../../wavefunction-normalization.md) select admissible bound [quantum states](../../../../../../quantum-state.md).
