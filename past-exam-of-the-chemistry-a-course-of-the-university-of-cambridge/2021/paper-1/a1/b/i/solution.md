↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

It represents the **squared [orbital angular momentum](../../../../../../orbital-angular-momentum.md)**. In [atomic units](../../../../../../atomic-units.md), $\hbar=1$, so its [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are $l(l+1)$. In ordinary units the corresponding observable is $\hbar^2l(l+1)$, with [orbital angular momentum quantum number](../../../../../../orbital-angular-momentum-quantum-number.md) $l=0,1,2,\ldots$.
