↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Use the classes $(E,2C_3,3\sigma_v)$ of the [C3v point group](../../../../../../c3v-molecular-point-group.md). For the three hydrogen $1s$ [atomic orbitals](../../../../../../atomic-orbital.md), the [character of a representation](../../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) counts orbitals fixed by each [symmetry operation](../../../../../../symmetry-operation.md). Identity fixes three, a nontrivial threefold rotation fixes none, and a vertical mirror fixes one. Therefore

$$
\chi_{\rm H}=(3,0,1).
$$

The [character inner product](../../../../../../character-inner-product.md) gives multiplicities

$$
n_{A_1}=\frac{3+3}{6}=1,\quad
n_{A_2}=\frac{3-3}{6}=0,\quad
n_E=\frac{2\times3}{6}=1.
$$

Thus **$\Gamma_{\rm H}=A_1\oplus E$**, with the $E$ contribution accounting for two independent [symmetry orbitals](../../../../../../symmetry-adapted-linear-combination.md).
