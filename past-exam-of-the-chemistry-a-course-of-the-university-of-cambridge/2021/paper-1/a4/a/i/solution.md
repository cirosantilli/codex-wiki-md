The [trigonal pyramidal molecular geometry](../../../../../../trigonal-pyramidal-molecular-geometry.md) has **[C3v point group](../../../../../../c3v-molecular-point-group.md) symmetry**: identity, two rotations about the threefold axis, and three vertical mirror reflections. Choose $z$ along the threefold axis through [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) and the center of the hydrogen triangle.
