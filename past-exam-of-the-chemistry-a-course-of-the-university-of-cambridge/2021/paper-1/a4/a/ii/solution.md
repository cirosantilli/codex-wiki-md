In the [C3v point group](../../../../../../c3v-molecular-point-group.md), the [nitrogen](../../../../../../nitrogen.md) valence [atomic orbitals](../../../../../../atomic-orbital.md) transform as

$$
\boxed{2s:A_1,\qquad 2p_z:A_1,\qquad (2p_x,2p_y):E.}
$$

The $s$ orbital is invariant under every [symmetry operation](../../../../../../symmetry-operation.md); $p_z$ is also invariant because each operation preserves the $z$ coordinate. The other two components mix as a two-dimensional [irreducible representation](../../../../../../irreducible-representation.md).
