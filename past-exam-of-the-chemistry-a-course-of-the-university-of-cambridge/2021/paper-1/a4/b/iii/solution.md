↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The nitrogen-centered [highest occupied molecular orbital](../../../../../../highest-occupied-molecular-orbital.md) of [ammonia](../../../../../../ammonia.md) supplies a [lone pair](../../../../../../lone-pair.md); planar [borane](../../../../../../borane.md) has an empty out-of-plane $p$-derived [lowest unoccupied molecular orbital](../../../../../../lowest-unoccupied-molecular-orbital.md). Approach along this acceptor axis gives constructive [orbital overlap](../../../../../../orbital-overlap.md) and donation of the nitrogen pair into the boron-centered acceptor. The resulting **N-to-B [coordinate covalent bond](../../../../../../coordinate-covalent-bond.md) forms the [ammonia borane](../../../../../../ammonia-borane.md) adduct**.

The occupied [molecular orbital](../../../../../../molecular-orbital.md) formed by this interaction is bonding, while the corresponding antibonding combination remains empty. [Borane](../../../../../../borane.md) pyramidalizes as coordination increases from three to four. [Ammonia](../../../../../../ammonia.md) can use its existing outward-pointing lone pair; its inversion to the planar transition geometry is not a prerequisite. The [Lewis base](../../../../../../lewis-base.md) and [Lewis acid](../../../../../../lewis-acid.md) descriptions summarize the donor and acceptor roles, while the frontier [molecular orbitals](../../../../../../molecular-orbital.md) explain their directional interaction.
