<h1 id="a9/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [reciprocal lattice](../../../../../../reciprocal-lattice.md) is generated by

$$
\boxed{\mathbf b_1=\frac{2\pi}{a}\hat{\mathbf x},\qquad
\mathbf b_2=\frac{2\pi}{a}\hat{\mathbf y},\qquad
\mathbf G_{hk}=h\mathbf b_1+k\mathbf b_2.}
$$

Its points are spaced by $2\pi/a$ along both reciprocal axes. Perpendicular bisectors between the origin and its four nearest reciprocal neighbours enclose the [first Brillouin zone](../../../../../../first-brillouin-zone.md),

$$
\boxed{-\frac\pi a\le k_x\le\frac\pi a,\qquad
-\frac\pi a\le k_y\le\frac\pi a.}
$$

The original drawing labels both the reciprocal-lattice spacing and the nearer zone boundaries. Its shaded disk is the occupied region for one [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) per real-space cell.
