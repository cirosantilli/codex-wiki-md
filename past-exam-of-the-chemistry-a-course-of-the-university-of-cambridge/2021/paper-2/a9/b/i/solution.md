<h1 id="a9/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Using $M_{\rm Au}\simeq196.97\ {\rm g\,mol^{-1}}$ and the [Avogadro constant](../../../../../../avogadro-constant.md), the areal atom count is

$$
n_{\rm Au}=\frac{1.56\times10^{-6}\ {\rm g}}{196.97\ {\rm g\,mol^{-1}}}
\frac{6.02214\times10^{23}\ {\rm mol^{-1}}}{10^{-4}\ {\rm m^2}}
\simeq4.77\times10^{19}\ {\rm m^{-2}}.
$$

One atom occupies each square cell of area $a^2$, so the inferred [square lattice](../../../../../../square-lattice.md) spacing is

$$
\boxed{a=n_{\rm Au}^{-1/2}\simeq1.45\times10^{-10}\ {\rm m}=0.145\ {\rm nm}.}
$$

This uses the given deposited [mass](../../../../../../mass.md) and the assumed single square layer; it does not substitute a bulk [gold](../../../../../../gold.md) lattice parameter for the measured areal count.
