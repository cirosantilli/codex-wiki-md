<h1 id="a9/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

One mobile [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) per [gold](../../../../../../gold.md) atom gives

$$
\boxed{n_e=n_{\rm Au}\simeq4.77\times10^{19}\ {\rm electrons\,m^{-2}}.}
$$

This is an areal [number density](../../../../../../number-density.md); no layer thickness is required and it should not be converted into a volume density for the two-dimensional model.
