<h1 id="a9/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Let $\mathcal A$ be the total two-dimensional area. There are $\mathcal A/(2\pi)^2$ [wavevector](../../../../../../wavevector.md) states per reciprocal-space area per [spin](../../../../../../spin.md). Including [spin degeneracy](../../../../../../spin-degeneracy.md) two and filling a disk gives

$$
N_e=2\frac{\mathcal A}{(2\pi)^2}\pi k_F^2
=\frac{\mathcal A k_F^2}{2\pi}.
$$

For areal [number density](../../../../../../number-density.md) $n_e=N_e/\mathcal A$ this yields

$$
\boxed{k_F=\sqrt{2\pi n_e},\qquad E_F=\frac{\pi\hbar^2 n_e}{m_e}.}
$$

The disk is centred at zero [wavevector](../../../../../../wavevector.md), and its boundary is a [fermi circle](../../../../../../fermi-circle.md). Omitting the two [spin](../../../../../../spin.md) states would incorrectly double the [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) inferred for a given [number density](../../../../../../number-density.md).
