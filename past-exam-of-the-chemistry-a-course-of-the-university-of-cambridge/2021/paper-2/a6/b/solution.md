<h1 id="a6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Write $\beta=(k_{\rm B}T)^{-1}$ and $x=\beta\delta$. The one-site [partition function](../../../../../canonical-partition-function.md) includes both degenerate lower states,

$$
q=e^{\beta\varepsilon}(2+e^{-x}).
$$

The occupied [adsorption sites](../../../../../adsorption-site.md) are distinguishable positions, so the [canonical partition function](../../../../../canonical-partition-function.md) is a product, **without an additional $N!$ divisor**:

$$
\boxed{Z_N=q^N=e^{\beta N\varepsilon}(2+e^{-\beta\delta})^N.}
$$

Differentiating $\ln Z_N$ gives the [internal energy](../../../../../internal-energy-split.md),

$$
\boxed{U=-\left(\frac{\partial\ln Z_N}{\partial\beta}\right)_N
=-N\varepsilon+\frac{N\delta e^{-\beta\delta}}{2+e^{-\beta\delta}}
=-N\varepsilon+\frac{N\delta}{1+2e^{\beta\delta}}.}
$$

The final factor is the population of the perpendicular orientation. The shared binding offset $-\varepsilon$ shifts all [energy levels](../../../../../energy-level.md) equally and does not determine their relative populations.
