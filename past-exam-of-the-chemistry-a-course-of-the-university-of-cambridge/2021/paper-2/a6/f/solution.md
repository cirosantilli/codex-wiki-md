<h1 id="a6/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

Only the two lower orientations remain populated, giving $2^N$ equiprobable [microstates](../../../../../microstate.md). Thus

$$
\boxed{\lim_{T\to0}S=Nk_{\rm B}\ln2.}
$$

This [residual entropy](../../../../../residual-entropy.md) follows from the stipulated independent-site [ground-state degeneracy](../../../../../ground-state-degeneracy.md). The zero-entropy version of the [third law of thermodynamics](../../../../../third-law-of-thermodynamics.md) presupposes a unique ordered [ground state](../../../../../ground-state.md); that assumption does not hold here. Intermolecular interactions or a small splitting of the two in-plane states could remove this degeneracy, but they are excluded from the given model.
