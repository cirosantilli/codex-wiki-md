<h1 id="a6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Both in-plane orientations minimize the one-site [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md). Since every [adsorption site](../../../../../adsorption-site.md) is occupied, the sites label independent choices even though the molecules are chemically identical. The [ground state](../../../../../ground-state.md) of the whole system therefore has

$$
\boxed{U_{\min}=-N\varepsilon,\qquad\Omega_0=2^N.}
$$

The [ground-state degeneracy](../../../../../ground-state-degeneracy.md) is extensive: independently choosing either in-plane orientation at each site costs no [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md). There is no extra vacancy or free-translational contribution in this model.
