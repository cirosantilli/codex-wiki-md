<h1 id="a6/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

At low [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), the upper orientation has negligible [Boltzmann weight](../../../../../boltzmann-factor.md), whereas at high [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) all three orientations become equally probable. Consequently

$$
\boxed{U(0)=-N\varepsilon,\qquad U(\infty)=-N\varepsilon+\frac{N\delta}{3}.}
$$

The high-temperature limit is a finite arithmetic mean of three available one-site [energy levels](../../../../../energy-level.md). It is not an unbounded translational or rotational [equipartition](../../../../../equipartition-theorem.md) contribution: the prescribed site model has only three states per molecule. Their common binding offset can keep this limiting [internal energy](../../../../../internal-energy-split.md) negative without any inconsistency.
