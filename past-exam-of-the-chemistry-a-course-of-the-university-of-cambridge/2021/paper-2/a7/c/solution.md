<h1 id="a7/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A [planar rigid rotor](../../../../../planar-rigid-rotor.md) has angular eigenfunctions $e^{im\phi}$ and single-valuedness imposes $m=0,\pm1,\pm2,\ldots$. Its [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) depends on $m^2$, so if the level is labelled by $J=|m|$, then

$$
\boxed{J=0,1,2,\ldots;\qquad g_0=1,\quad g_J=2\ (J\ge1).}
$$

The two states at $J>0$ represent opposite directions of [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md). If the signed [quantum number](../../../../../quantum-number.md) itself is called $J$, each signed state is counted once instead; one must not both sum over both signs and multiply by two again.

For an unrestricted three-dimensional [linear rigid rotor](../../../../../linear-rigid-rotor.md), $J=0,1,2,\ldots$ has $M=-J,\ldots,J$ and [state degeneracy](../../../../../state-degeneracy.md) $2J+1$, with $E_J=\hbar^2J(J+1)/(2I)$. Planar confinement removes one rotational coordinate and changes both the spectrum and degeneracy. These are the spatial counts before [nuclear spin statistics](../../../../../nuclear-spin-statistics.md). For homonuclear [hydrogen](../../../../../hydrogen.md), the allowed even/odd spatial states must also be paired with the appropriate [parahydrogen](../../../../../parahydrogen.md) or [orthohydrogen](../../../../../orthohydrogen.md) nuclear states, as considered explicitly in A8.
