<h1 id="a8/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Apply part (b) to each supplied rotational sum. Multiplying the odd sum by its constant nuclear-spin factor three does not change $q'/q$ or $q''/q$. At the specified [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) the dimensionless rotational contributions are

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{C_{V,m,p}^{\rm rot}}R
&=\frac{22500(3.58\times10^{-5})}{1.165}
-22500\left(\frac{3.76\times10^{-3}}{1.165}\right)^2
+\frac{300(3.76\times10^{-3})}{1.165}
\simeq1.4253,\\
\frac{C_{V,m,o}^{\rm rot}}R
&=\frac{22500(-3.08\times10^{-5})}{0.970}
-22500\left(\frac{7.64\times10^{-3}}{0.970}\right)^2
+\frac{300(7.64\times10^{-3})}{0.970}
\simeq0.25265.
\end{aligned}
$$

Thus the **rotational [heat capacities](../../../../../../heat-capacity.md) are $1.425R$ for [parahydrogen](../../../../../../parahydrogen.md) and $0.253R$ for [orthohydrogen](../../../../../../orthohydrogen.md)**. For the total gas [heat capacities](../../../../../../heat-capacity.md), including translation and neglecting vibration,

$$
\boxed{C_{V,m,p}\simeq2.925R,\qquad C_{V,m,o}\simeq1.753R.}
$$

The difference is substantial because the permitted rotational ladders differ. The para ladder starts at $J=0$ and next reaches $J=2$, separated by $6k_{\rm B}\theta_{\rm rot}$; its excited population is changing appreciably here. The ortho ladder starts at $J=1$ and next reaches $J=3$, a larger separation of $10k_{\rm B}\theta_{\rm rot}$, so much of the ortho population remains in its lowest rotational manifold. A rotational contribution greater than $R$ is possible in the intermediate quantum regime and is not a violation of high-temperature [equipartition](../../../../../../equipartition-theorem.md).
