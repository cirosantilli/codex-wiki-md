<h1 id="a8/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a fixed number of non-interacting molecules, $Z_N=q^N/N!$ in the dilute [ideal gas](../../../../../../ideal-gas-split.md) limit. The temperature-independent factorial drops out of the [internal energy](../../../../../../internal-energy-split.md) derivative:

$$
U_m=RT^2\left(\frac{\partial\ln q}{\partial T}\right)_V.
$$

Differentiate once more at fixed volume to obtain

$$
\boxed{\frac{C_{V,m}}R=2T\frac{q'}q
+T^2\left[\frac{q''}q-\left(\frac{q'}q\right)^2\right]
=\frac{d}{dT}\left(T^2\frac{d\ln q}{dT}\right).}
$$

Primes denote fixed-volume derivatives. If $q$ factorizes into translational, rotational and other independent contributions, their [heat capacities](../../../../../../heat-capacity.md) add. In particular, the three-dimensional translational factor contributes $3R/2$, while a constant [spin degeneracy](../../../../../../spin-degeneracy.md) factor contributes zero. The formula also follows from the positive [canonical energy variance](../../../../../../canonical-energy-variance.md), $C_V=\operatorname{Var}(E)/(k_{\rm B}T^2)$.
