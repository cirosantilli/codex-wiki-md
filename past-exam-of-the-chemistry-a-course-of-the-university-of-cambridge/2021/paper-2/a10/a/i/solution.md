<h1 id="a10/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Assume dilute nondegenerate [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) and [electron holes](../../../../../../electron-hole.md), parabolic three-dimensional band edges with the stated scalar [band effective masses](../../../../../../band-effective-mass.md), [spin](../../../../../../spin.md) multiplicity two, one effective valley for each edge, and thermal equilibrium. Take the unchanged host [ground state](../../../../../../ground-state.md) to have unit activity and neglect bound excitons. The [effective density of states](../../../../../../effective-density-of-states.md) values are

$$
N_c=2\left(\frac{2\pi m_e^*k_{\rm B}T}{h^2}\right)^{3/2},\qquad
N_v=2\left(\frac{2\pi m_h^*k_{\rm B}T}{h^2}\right)^{3/2}.
$$

For a [Fermi level](../../../../../../fermi-level.md) $\mu$ between the band edges,

$$
n_e=N_c e^{-(E_c-\mu)/(k_{\rm B}T)},\qquad
n_h=N_v e^{-(\mu-E_v)/(k_{\rm B}T)}.
$$

The factors of $\mu$ cancel on multiplication. With $E_g=E_c-E_v$, the [semiconductor mass-action law](../../../../../../semiconductor-mass-action-law.md) is

$$
\boxed{K_n\equiv n_en_h=N_cN_v e^{-E_g/(k_{\rm B}T)}
=4\left(\frac{2\pi k_{\rm B}T}{h^2}\right)^3(m_e^*m_h^*)^{3/2}
e^{-E_g/(k_{\rm B}T)}.}
$$

This density-form equilibrium constant has units $\mathrm{m^{-6}}$. A dimensionless thermodynamic constant uses a specified reference [number density](../../../../../../number-density.md) $n^\circ$, giving $K=K_n/(n^\circ)^2$. The dot representing the host [ground state](../../../../../../ground-state.md) is not a freely translating gas species requiring a further gas partition function. For an [intrinsic semiconductor](../../../../../../intrinsic-semiconductor.md), [charge neutrality](../../../../../../charge-neutrality.md) gives $n_e=n_h=n_i=\sqrt{K_n}$.
