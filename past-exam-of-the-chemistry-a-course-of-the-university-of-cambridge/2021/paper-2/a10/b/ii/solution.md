<h1 id="a10/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Differentiating the [tight binding](../../../../../../tight-binding.md) dispersion produced by the [Hückel method](../../../../../../huckel-method.md) gives

$$
\frac{dE_k}{dk}=-2\beta a\sin(ka),\qquad
\frac{d^2E_k}{dk^2}=-2\beta a^2\cos(ka).
$$

Hence

$$
\boxed{\frac1{m_e^*(k)}=-\frac{2\beta a^2}{\hbar^2}\cos(ka),\qquad
m_e^*(k)=-\frac{\hbar^2}{2\beta a^2\cos(ka)}.}
$$

Unlike a free particle, the [band effective mass](../../../../../../band-effective-mass.md) depends on [wavevector](../../../../../../wavevector.md) and can change sign. At an inflection point $\cos(ka)=0$, the inverse [mass](../../../../../../mass.md) vanishes and the curvature-based [mass](../../../../../../mass.md) diverges; a quadratic approximation then ceases to describe the dispersion locally. For the usual $\beta<0$ convention, the bottom at $k=0$ has positive [mass](../../../../../../mass.md) and the top at $k=\pi/a$ has negative [electron mass](../../../../../../electron-mass.md). The constant $\alpha$ has no effect on curvature.
