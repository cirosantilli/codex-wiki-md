<h1 id="a10/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Two derivatives of the free-electron dispersion give

$$
\frac{d^2E_k}{dk^2}=\frac{\hbar^2}{m_e},\qquad
\boxed{m_e^*=m_e.}
$$

The [band effective mass](../../../../../../band-effective-mass.md) becomes the ordinary [electron mass](../../../../../../electron-mass.md) when no periodic-potential modification of the quadratic [dispersion relation](../../../../../../dispersion-relation-split.md) is present.
