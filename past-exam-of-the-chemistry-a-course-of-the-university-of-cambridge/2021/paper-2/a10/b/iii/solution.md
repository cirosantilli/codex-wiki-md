<h1 id="a10/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

In a weakly doped [n-type semiconductor](../../../../../../n-type-semiconductor.md), the mobile [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) occupy states near the bottom of the [conduction band](../../../../../../conduction-band.md). Their local band curvature is positive, so **their [band effective mass](../../../../../../band-effective-mass.md) is positive and approximately constant sufficiently near that minimum**. For the usual $\beta<0$ convention, expand about $k=0$:

$$
E_k\simeq\alpha+2\beta-\beta a^2k^2,
\qquad
\boxed{m_e^*\simeq\frac{\hbar^2}{2|\beta|a^2}>0.}
$$

Stronger overlap gives larger $|\beta|$, greater curvature and a smaller [mass](../../../../../../mass.md) within this model. Positive $\beta$ simply places the minimum at the zone boundary and again gives a positive edge [mass](../../../../../../mass.md). A negative [electron mass](../../../../../../electron-mass.md) near a band maximum instead motivates the positive-mass [electron hole](../../../../../../electron-hole.md) description; it is not the usual conduction-edge situation for an [n-type semiconductor](../../../../../../n-type-semiconductor.md). No numerical comparison with $m_e$ follows without values of $a$ and $\beta$.
