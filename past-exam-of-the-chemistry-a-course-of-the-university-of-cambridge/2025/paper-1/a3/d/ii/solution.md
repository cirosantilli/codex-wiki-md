<h1 id="a3/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Use the two-body [reduced mass](../../../../../../reduced-mass.md), rather than the chlorine mass or hydrogen mass alone:

$$
\mu=\frac{1.0078\times34.9688}{1.0078+34.9688}m_u
=1.62661\times10^{-27}\ \mathrm{kg}.
$$

The [bond force constant](../../../../../../bond-force-constant.md) gives

$$
\nu_{\rm harm}=\frac1{2\pi}\sqrt{\frac{481}{\mu}}=8.65467\times10^{13}\ \mathrm{s^{-1}},\qquad
\boxed{\widetilde\nu_{\rm harm}=\frac{\nu_{\rm harm}}{2.99792458\times10^{10}\ \mathrm{cm\,s^{-1}}}=2886.89\ \mathrm{cm^{-1}}.}
$$

The requested “frequency in cm−1” is a [spectroscopic wavenumber](../../../../../../spectroscopic-wavenumber.md); the ordinary frequency is the value in s−1 above. This calculation uses the question's force constant in the harmonic model, rather than replacing it with a tabulated spectroscopic constant.
