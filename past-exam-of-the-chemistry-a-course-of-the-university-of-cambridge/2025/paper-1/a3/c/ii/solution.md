A nonlinear triatomic molecule has $3N-6=3$ vibrational [normal modes](../../../../../../normal-mode.md). For water they are the symmetric O–H stretch, the H–O–H bend, and the antisymmetric O–H stretch, shown above. In the symmetric stretch both O–H lengths increase together; in the antisymmetric stretch one increases while the other decreases; the bend predominantly changes the bond angle. All three change a component of the [electric dipole moment](../../../../../../electric-dipole-moment.md) and are infrared-active. Every arrow reverses half a period later; motion of oxygen maintains the molecular [centre of mass](../../../../../../center-of-mass.md).
