The second [vibrational overtone](../../../../../../vibrational-overtone.md) is $v=0\to3$, with $\Delta v=3$, near three times the fundamental wavenumber. It requires higher-order departures from the linear-dipole harmonic model and is ordinarily weaker again. These are qualitative intensity trends; resonances can redistribute intensity between nearby vibrational states.
