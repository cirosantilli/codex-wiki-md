↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The first [vibrational overtone](../../../../../../vibrational-overtone.md) is $v=0\to2$, with $\Delta v=2$. It lies near twice the fundamental wavenumber, generally slightly below it because of [anharmonicity](../../../../../../anharmonicity.md). The linear-dipole harmonic model forbids it; anharmonic wavefunctions or higher terms in the dipole expansion give a weak intensity. Thus it is generally weaker than the corresponding fundamental.
