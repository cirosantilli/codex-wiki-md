↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

A [hot band](../../../../../../hot-band.md) starts from a thermally populated vibrationally excited state, for example $v=1\to2$. It can satisfy $\Delta v=1$ and need not have a small transition moment, but its lower-state population is reduced by the [Boltzmann distribution](../../../../../../boltzmann-distribution.md):

$$
\frac{N_1}{N_0}\simeq\exp[-(E_1-E_0)/(k_BT)].
$$

It is therefore usually weak at low temperature and grows relative to the fundamental on heating. [Anharmonicity](../../../../../../anharmonicity.md) shifts its frequency away from the $0\to1$ transition; in a polyatomic molecule, excitation in another mode can also produce a hot-band sequence.
