↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

In this [C2v point group](../../../../../../c2v-point-group.md) convention, $z\sim A_1$, $x\sim B_1$, and $y\sim B_2$. Therefore all six [normal modes](../../../../../../normal-mode.md) have symmetry-allowed infrared fundamentals: $\boxed{6\text{ IR features}}$, absent accidental coincidences or vanishing intensities.

For [Raman spectroscopy](../../../../../../raman-spectroscopy.md), examine the [polarizability tensor](../../../../../../polarizability-tensor.md): $x^2,y^2,z^2\sim A_1$, $xz\sim B_1$, and $yz\sim B_2$; $xy\sim A_2$ is also Raman-allowed but no $A_2$ vibration occurs here. All six vibrations are therefore Raman-active too, giving $\boxed{6\text{ Raman features and }6\text{ IR/Raman coincidences}}$. The centrosymmetric mutual-exclusion rule does not apply to $C_{2v}$ [formaldehyde](../../../../../../formaldehyde.md).
