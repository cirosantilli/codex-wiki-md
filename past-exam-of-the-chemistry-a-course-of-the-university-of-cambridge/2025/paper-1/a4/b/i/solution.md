With $z$ along C=O and the molecule in the $xz$ plane, [formaldehyde](../../../../../../formaldehyde.md) has $E$, $C_2(z)$, $\sigma_{xz}$ and $\sigma_{yz}$. It belongs to the [C2v point group](../../../../../../c2v-point-group.md), $\boxed{C_{2v}}$. Keeping this axis convention is essential when assigning the labels $B_1$ and $B_2$.
