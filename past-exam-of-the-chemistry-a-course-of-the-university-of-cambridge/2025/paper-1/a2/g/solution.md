<h1 id="a2/g/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [G](../g.md)

The closed argon core contributes $L=S=0$. In the half-filled $3d^5$ shell, [Hund's rules](../../../../../hund-s-rules.md) put one parallel-spin electron in each $m_\ell=-2,-1,0,1,2$ orbital. This gives $S_d=5/2$ and the orbital term $L_d=0$. Adding the parallel $4s$ electron, with $\ell=0$ and $s=1/2$, gives

$$
\boxed{S=3,\qquad L=0,\qquad J=3,\qquad {}^{2S+1}L_J={}^7S_3.}
$$

The [spin multiplicity](../../../../../spin-multiplicity.md) is seven. The magnetic projection labels run $M_S,M_J=-3,-2,\ldots,3$, while $M_L=0$. The [term symbol](../../../../../term-symbol.md) agrees with the observed neutral-[chromium](../../../../../chromium.md) ground level.[Cr I, ground level a7S3](../../../../../energy-levels-of-neutral-chromium-in-the-nist-handbook.md)
