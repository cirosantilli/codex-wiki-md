<h1 id="a1/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [quantum variational principle](../../../../../../quantum-variational-principle.md) states that the energy expectation of an admissible trial [wavefunction](../../../../../../wave-function-split.md) is at least the [ground state](../../../../../../ground-state.md) energy:

$$
\boxed{\frac{\langle f,\widehat Hf\rangle}{\langle f,f\rangle}\ge E_0.}
$$

For the discrete, complete [orthonormal basis](../../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) assumed here, expand in [Hamiltonian operator](../../../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) eigenfunctions, $f=\sum_n c_n\phi_n$, with $\widehat H\phi_n=E_n\phi_n$ and $E_n\ge E_0$. Then

$$
\frac{\langle f,\widehat Hf\rangle}{\langle f,f\rangle}-E_0
=\frac{\sum_n|c_n|^2(E_n-E_0)}{\sum_n|c_n|^2}\ge0.
$$

Every summand is nonnegative. Equality holds precisely when all occupied components lie in the ground eigenspace. Thus minimizing trial energies supplies upper bounds on $E_0$, with exact equality if the trial family contains a [ground state](../../../../../../ground-state.md).
