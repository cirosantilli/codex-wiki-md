<h1 id="a1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Multiply the [eigenfunction expansion](../../../../../../eigenfunction-expansion.md) by $\psi_m^*$ and integrate over the common domain. [Orthogonality](../../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) and [wavefunction normalization](../../../../../../wavefunction-normalization.md) give

$$
\int\psi_m^*(x)f(x)\,dx
=\sum_n c_n\int\psi_m^*(x)\psi_n(x)\,dx
=\sum_n c_n\delta_{mn}=c_m.
$$

Thus $\boxed{c_m=\langle\psi_m,f\rangle}$. For a normalized $f$, the [Born rule](../../../../../../born-rule.md) identifies $|c_m|^2$ as the probability of this basis outcome.
