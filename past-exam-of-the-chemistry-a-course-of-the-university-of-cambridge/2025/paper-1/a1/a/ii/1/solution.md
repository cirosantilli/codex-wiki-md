<h1 id="a1/a/ii/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Let $\widehat Q\psi_n=q_n\psi_n$ with $\psi_n\ne0$. The [Hermitian operator](../../../../../../../hermitian-operator.md) identity and conjugate linearity of the first slot of the [inner product](../../../../../../../inner-product-split.md) imply

$$
q_n\langle\psi_n,\psi_n\rangle
=\langle\psi_n,\widehat Q\psi_n\rangle
=\langle\widehat Q\psi_n,\psi_n\rangle
=q_n^*\langle\psi_n,\psi_n\rangle.
$$

The squared [norm](../../../../../../../norm.md) is positive, so $q_n=q_n^*$: every such [eigenvalue](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) is real.
