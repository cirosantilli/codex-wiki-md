<h1 id="a1/a/ii/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

For two [eigenfunctions](../../../../../../../eigenfunction.md), apply the same [Hermitian operator](../../../../../../../hermitian-operator.md) identity across the two states:

$$
q_n\langle\psi_m,\psi_n\rangle
=\langle\psi_m,\widehat Q\psi_n\rangle
=\langle\widehat Q\psi_m,\psi_n\rangle
=q_m\langle\psi_m,\psi_n\rangle.
$$

Hence $(q_n-q_m)\langle\psi_m,\psi_n\rangle=0$. Distinct [eigenvalues](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) therefore imply [orthogonal](../../../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) [eigenfunctions](../../../../../../../eigenfunction.md). Within a degenerate eigenspace, [orthogonality](../../../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) is a possible basis choice, rather than a consequence for every pair of vectors.
