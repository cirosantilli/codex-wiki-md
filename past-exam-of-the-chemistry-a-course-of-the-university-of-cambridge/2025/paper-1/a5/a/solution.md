↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The planar [para-quinone](../../../../../1-4-benzoquinone.md) framework has three mutually perpendicular twofold axes, inversion through its centre, and the associated mirror planes. With $x$ horizontal, $y$ along the two C=O bonds and $z$ perpendicular to the molecule, it belongs to the [D2h point group](../../../../../d2h-point-group.md), $\boxed{D_{2h}}$.
