↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The primed $f'_{\rm TS}$ is the corresponding [transition state](../../../../../../transition-state.md) [partition function](../../../../../../canonical-partition-function.md) per unit [volume](../../../../../../volume.md) with the unstable reaction-coordinate degree of freedom omitted. Its replacement by the crossing-frequency factor $kT/h$ is already outside the partition-function ratio. For a diatomic atom-pair transition state, the omitted coordinate is the bond stretch, leaving translation and rotation.
