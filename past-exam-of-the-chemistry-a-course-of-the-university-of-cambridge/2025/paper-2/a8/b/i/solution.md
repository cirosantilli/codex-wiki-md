↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The quantities $f_A$ and $f_B$ are molecular [partition functions](../../../../../../canonical-partition-function.md) per unit [volume](../../../../../../volume.md) for the two reactants, with their internal rotational, vibrational and electronic factors included. Thus their translational factors are $(2\pi m_jkT/h^2)^{3/2}$, rather than those factors multiplied by $V$. Their energy zeros must match the separately displayed barrier convention.
