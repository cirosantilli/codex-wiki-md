↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The barrier $\Delta\varepsilon_0^{\ddagger}$ is the energy difference between the transition-state reference level and the separated reactant reference levels. Consistent [zero-point energies](../../../../../../zero-point-energy.md) of stable transverse modes belong in this reference-level difference. It is an energy per reacting pair; a molar activation energy uses $R$ instead of $k$ in the exponential.
