A [transition state](../../../../../../transition-state.md) is the configuration or dividing-surface region associated with passage between reactant and product basins. In the elementary saddle-point picture it is a first-order saddle of the [potential energy surface](../../../../../../potential-energy-surface.md): stable against small displacements in transverse directions but unstable along the reaction direction. [Transition state theory](../../../../../../transition-state-theory.md) counts forward crossings and, in its simplest form, neglects recrossing.
