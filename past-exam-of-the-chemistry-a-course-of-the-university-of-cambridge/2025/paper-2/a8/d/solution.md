<h1 id="a8/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Identify the transition-state separation with contact, $R=r_A+r_B$, take distinct atoms so $\sigma=1$, and let $E_A=N_A\Delta\varepsilon_0^{\ddagger}$. Then $\pi R^2=\rho$ and $\Delta\varepsilon_0^{\ddagger}/kT=E_A/RT$, yielding

$$
\boxed{k_{\rm 2nd}=\rho\sqrt{\frac{8kT}{\pi\mu}}e^{-E_A/RT}}.
$$

The velocity factor is the mean relative speed from the [Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution](../../../../../maxwell-boltzmann-distribution.md). Thus this simple [transition state theory](../../../../../transition-state-theory.md) model reproduces the stated [collision theory](../../../../../collision-theory.md) expression. Molecular orientation constraints, quantum transmission and reaction-surface recrossing can invalidate the equality outside these simplifying assumptions.
