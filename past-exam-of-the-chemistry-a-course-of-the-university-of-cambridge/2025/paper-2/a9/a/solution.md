↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In a [body-centred cubic](../../../../../body-centered-cubic-lattice.md) lattice, touching atoms lie along a body diagonal. Its length is $\sqrt3a$ and spans four radii, so

$$
\boxed{r=\frac{\sqrt3a}{4}=1.37049\ \text{\AA}=137.05\ \mathrm{pm}}.
$$
