<h1 id="a9/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [Fermi sphere](../../../../../fermi-sphere.md) [volume](../../../../../volume.md) in reciprocal space is

$$
\boxed{V_F=\frac{4\pi}3k_F^3=46.9429\ \text{\AA}^{-3}}.
$$

The primitive real-space cell has [volume](../../../../../volume.md) $a^3/2$, so the first [Brillouin zone](../../../../../brillouin-zone.md) has [volume](../../../../../volume.md)

$$
\boxed{V_{\rm BZ}=\frac{(2\pi)^3}{a^3/2}=15.6476\ \text{\AA}^{-3}},\qquad
\boxed{V_F=3V_{\rm BZ}}.
$$

Each band accommodates two [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) per primitive cell when spin is counted. Six [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) therefore require three such zone-volumes of occupied reciprocal-space states in the free-electron counting picture. The extended-zone sphere is not confined to the first zone; folding it produces multiple bands. It does not imply that the actual [tungsten](../../../../../tungsten.md) [Fermi surface](../../../../../fermi-surface.md) is spherical.
