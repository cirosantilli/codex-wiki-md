A standard substitutional acceptor choice is **Mg on a Ga site**: a group-II atom replaces group III, leaving one fewer valence [electron](../../../../../../electron.md). Its size is sufficiently compatible with the cation sublattice to make substitution plausible. This identifies the intended acceptor chemistry; actual [hole](../../../../../../electron-hole.md) density also depends on activation, incomplete ionization and compensation. Size and valence rules alone do not guarantee a shallow, fully ionized acceptor.
