<h1 id="a10/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Suitable substitutional donors include **Si on a Ga site** and **O on an N site**. The former replaces a group-III atom with a group-IV atom; the latter replaces a group-V atom with a group-VI atom. Each provides an extra valence [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) relative to its host site. Si is a plausible substitutional cation-site dopant, and the compact oxygen atom is compatible with an anion site. Site compatibility matters: merely choosing an element with an extra [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) without considering which sublattice it occupies is insufficient.
