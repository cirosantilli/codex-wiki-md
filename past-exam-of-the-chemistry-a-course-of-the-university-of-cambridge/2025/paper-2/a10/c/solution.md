<h1 id="a10/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

At equilibrium, [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) initially diffuse from the n side and [holes](../../../../../electron-hole.md) from the p side. Recombination leaves ionized donors and acceptors in a [depletion region](../../../../../depletion-region.md), producing an internal electric field and [band bending](../../../../../band-bending.md). The [Fermi level](../../../../../fermi-level.md) is constant across the equilibrium [p-n junction](../../../../../p-n-junction.md). With p material on the left, both band edges lie higher on the p side, as in the left panel.

**[Figure 2](#a10/c/image-equilibrium-and-forward-biased-direct-gap-p-n-junction-energy-bands-with-injection-and-photon-emission). Equilibrium and forward-biased direct-gap p-n junction energy bands, with injection and photon emission**.

Forward bias, with the p side positive relative to n, reduces the injection barrier. [Electrons](../../../../../electron.md) and [holes](../../../../../electron-hole.md) enter the junction region and can recombine radiatively, emitting [photons](../../../../../photon.md) with energy close to the [band gap](../../../../../band-gap.md). Under bias, separate [electron](../../../../../electron.md) and [hole](../../../../../electron-hole.md) [quasi-Fermi levels](../../../../../quasi-fermi-level.md) replace a single equilibrium [Fermi level](../../../../../fermi-level.md).

Both [GaN](../../../../../gallium-nitride.md) polymorphs have a [direct band gap](../../../../../direct-band-gap.md), so a conduction-band [electron](../../../../../electron.md) and valence-band [hole](../../../../../electron-hole.md) can recombine without a large crystal-momentum change. [Silicon](../../../../../silicon.md) and [germanium](../../../../../germanium.md) have [indirect band gaps](../../../../../indirect-band-gap.md), requiring [phonon](../../../../../phonon.md) assistance for near-edge radiative recombination, which makes efficient band-edge light emission much harder. A direct gap enables the process; defects, carrier confinement and competing nonradiative channels still determine device efficiency.
