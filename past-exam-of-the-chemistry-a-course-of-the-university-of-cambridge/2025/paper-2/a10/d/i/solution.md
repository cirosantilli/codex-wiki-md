Using $E_\gamma=hc/\lambda$ and an ideal band-edge [photon](../../../../../../photon.md) energy equal to the quoted gap,

$$
\boxed{\lambda_{\alpha}=\frac{1239.84\ \mathrm{eV\,nm}}{3.4\ \mathrm{eV}}
=364.66\ \mathrm{nm}}.
$$

At low [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) an [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) and [hole](../../../../../../electron-hole.md) can form a bound [exciton](../../../../../../exciton.md). Its recombination releases approximately $E_g-E_B$, so its wavelength is longer than $hc/E_g$ evaluated using the gap at that same [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md). [GaN](../../../../../../gallium-nitride.md) excitation and emission measurements resolve such excitonic transitions below the fundamental gap.[Photoluminescence-excitation, reflection and emission comparison; separation of [exciton](../../../../../../exciton.md) transition energy from the fundamental gap](../../../../../../fundamental-energy-gap-of-gan-from-photoluminescence-excitation-spectra-by-bo-monemar.md)
