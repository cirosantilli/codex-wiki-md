↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For the quoted cubic-phase gap,

$$
\boxed{\lambda_{\beta}=\frac{1239.84\ \mathrm{eV\,nm}}{3.2\ \mathrm{eV}}
=387.45\ \mathrm{nm}}.
$$

The same [exciton](../../../../../../exciton.md) binding argument can shift the actual emission to longer wavelength; impurity-bound transitions can also lie below the fundamental gap. The comparison must use $E_g$ at the operating [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md). Ordinary temperature-dependent gap changes can shift the band edge in the opposite direction, so the numerical values based on fixed supplied gaps are not universal lower bounds for every cooled device.
