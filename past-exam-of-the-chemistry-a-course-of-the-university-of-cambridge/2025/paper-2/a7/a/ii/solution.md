<h1 id="a7/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For a linear [rigid rotor](../../../../../../rigid-rotor.md) in the high-temperature regime, $q_{\rm rot}\propto T$. Its molar rotational [entropy](../../../../../../entropy-split.md) is

$$
\boxed{S_{m,\rm rot}=R(\log q_{\rm rot}+1)}.
$$

The [rotational symmetry number](../../../../../../rotational-symmetry-number.md) must already be included in $q_{\rm rot}$.
