<h1 id="a7/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For identical, non-interacting molecules, the translational [canonical partition function](../../../../../../canonical-partition-function.md) is $Q_N=q_{\rm trans}^N/N!$. [Stirling's approximation](../../../../../../stirling-formula.md) gives the [Helmholtz free energy](../../../../../../helmholtz-free-energy.md)

$$
A=-NkT\left[\log(q_{\rm trans}/N)+1\right].
$$

At fixed $V$, $q_{\rm trans}\propto T^{3/2}$, so differentiation gives $S=Nk[\log(q_{\rm trans}/N)+5/2]$. For one mole at the standard-state molar [volume](../../../../../../volume.md),

$$
\boxed{S_{m,\rm trans}^{\circ}=R\left[\log\left(\frac{q_{m,\rm trans}^{\circ}}{N_A}\right)+\frac52\right]}.
$$

Here the quoted molar translational quantity is per mole, and its ratio to $N_A$ is dimensionless. The derivative defining [entropy](../../../../../../entropy-split.md) is taken at fixed [volume](../../../../../../volume.md) before inserting the standard-state [volume](../../../../../../volume.md); differentiating along a constant-pressure path would give the wrong additive constant.
