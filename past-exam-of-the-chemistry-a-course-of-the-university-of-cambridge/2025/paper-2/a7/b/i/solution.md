↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Under the stated vibrational-ground-state approximation and a nondegenerate electronic [ground state](../../../../../../ground-state.md), only translation and rotation contribute. Therefore

$$
S_m^{\circ}=R\left[\log\left(\frac{7.966\times10^{30}}{N_A}\right)+\frac52+log(265.4)+1\right]
=\boxed{211.84\ \mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}\,K^{-1}}}.
$$

A temperature-independent ground-state energy, including [zero-point energy](../../../../../../zero-point-energy.md), contributes no [entropy](../../../../../../entropy-split.md). This estimate deliberately omits thermal vibrational excitation; it need not equal a tabulated value that includes all modes.
