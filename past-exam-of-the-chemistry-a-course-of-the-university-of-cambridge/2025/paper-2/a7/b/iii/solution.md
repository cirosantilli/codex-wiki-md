<h1 id="a7/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The substituted molecule has the larger [entropy](../../../../../../entropy-split.md). Increased [mass](../../../../../../mass.md) and [moment of inertia](../../../../../../moment-of-inertia.md) reduce translational and rotational spacings and make more states thermally accessible. The largest contribution here is the loss of the twofold rotational indistinguishability of the oxygen ends, contributing $R\log2$. It is a [rotational symmetry number](../../../../../../rotational-symmetry-number.md) correction for one isotope-defined species, not an [entropy of mixing](../../../../../../entropy-of-mixing.md) between different molecules.
