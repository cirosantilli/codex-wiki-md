↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Differentiating each [Boltzmann factor](../../../../../../boltzmann-factor.md) yields

$$
\boxed{\frac{\partial q_3}{\partial T}
=\frac{\theta_1e^{-\theta_1/T}+\theta_2e^{-\theta_2/T}}{T^2}}.
$$

The derivative is positive: warming increases occupation of both excited [energy levels](../../../../../../energy-level.md). At $100\ \mathrm K$,

$$
\boxed{\left.\frac{\partial q_3}{\partial T}\right|_{100\,\mathrm K}
=1.56692\times10^{-3}\ \mathrm K^{-1}}.
$$
