<h1 id="a6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [internal energy](../../../../../internal-energy-split.md) is the population-weighted sum of the molecular [energy levels](../../../../../energy-level.md):

$$
U=\sum_i n_i\varepsilon_i=\frac Nq\sum_i\varepsilon_i e^{-\varepsilon_i/kT}.
$$

At fixed [volume](../../../../../volume.md), assume the [energy levels](../../../../../energy-level.md) themselves are temperature-independent. Differentiating the molecular [partition function](../../../../../canonical-partition-function.md) gives

$$
\left(\frac{\partial q}{\partial T}\right)_V
=\frac1{kT^2}\sum_i\varepsilon_i e^{-\varepsilon_i/kT},
\qquad\boxed{U=NkT^2\frac{\partial\log q}{\partial T}}.
$$

Choosing the zero of energy changes $U$ by the corresponding constant per molecule, so the ground-level convention must be consistent throughout.
