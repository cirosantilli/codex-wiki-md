<h1 id="a6/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Relative to the [ground state](../../../../../../ground-state.md), the [quantum harmonic oscillator](../../../../../../quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md) levels are $vhc\widetilde\omega$. Put $\theta_v=hc\widetilde\omega/k$. Summing the [geometric series](../../../../../../geometric-series.md) gives the [vibrational partition function](../../../../../../vibrational-partition-function.md) $q=(1-e^{-\theta_v/T})^{-1}$. Therefore the vibrational [internal energy](../../../../../../internal-energy-split.md) is

$$
\boxed{U=\frac{Nk\theta_v}{e^{\theta_v/T}-1}}
=\frac{Nhc\widetilde\omega}{e^{hc\widetilde\omega/kT}-1}.
$$

The omitted [zero-point energy](../../../../../../zero-point-energy.md) would add $Nhc\widetilde\omega/2$ if absolute oscillator energies were used.
