<h1 id="five-dimensional-schwarzschild-anti-de-sitter-black-hole">Five-dimensional Schwarzschild--anti-de Sitter black hole</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Schwarzschild--anti-de Sitter black hole](schwarzschild-anti-de-sitter-black-hole.md)

The spherical five-dimensional Schwarzschild--anti-de Sitter metric has $f(r)=1+r^2/L^2-\mu/r^2$. Its horizon satisfies $\mu=r_H^2(1+r_H^2/L^2)$ and has area $2\pi^2r_H^3$.
