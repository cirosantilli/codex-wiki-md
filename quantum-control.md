# Quantum control

↑ **Parent:** [Control theory](control-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_control)

[Quantum control](quantum-control.md) chooses externally adjustable interactions to steer a [quantum state](quantum-state.md) or implement a desired [unitary operator](unitary-operator.md). Its achievable transformations depend on the [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) or [Lindblad equation](lindblad-equation.md), the available [control inputs](control-input.md), and physical constraints such as bandwidth and amplitude. [Quantum optimal control](quantum-optimal-control.md) optimizes performance within those constraints.
