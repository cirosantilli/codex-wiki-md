# Reverberation mapping

↑ **Parent:** [Active galactic nucleus](active-galactic-nucleus.md)

Time-dependent inference of an emitting region's response to a variable illuminating continuum. For an active nucleus, a broad-line delay estimates a light-travel scale and a line width estimates a velocity scale. A virial [mass](mass.md) estimate then requires a geometrical and kinematic factor $f_{\rm geom}$; the lag alone neither fixes the geometry nor establishes purely gravitational motion.
