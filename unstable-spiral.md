# Unstable spiral

↑ **Parent:** [Linear stability of a planar equilibrium](linear-stability-of-a-planar-equilibrium.md)

A planar [equilibrium point](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) whose [Jacobian matrix](jacobian-matrix.md) has nonreal conjugate [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) with positive real part. Its nearby linearized trajectories rotate while their amplitude grows exponentially; the smooth nonlinear system has the same local qualitative behavior when the equilibrium is hyperbolic.
