# Born approximation in cosmological lensing

↑ **Parent:** [Gravitational lensing](gravitational-lensing.md)

In a first-order light-deflection calculation, evaluate the [Weyl lensing potential](weyl-lensing-potential.md) and its gradients on the unperturbed [null geodesic](null-geodesic.md). The position correction is first order, so inserting it into an already first-order potential produces a second-order correction. This approximation omits repeated deflection and lens-lens coupling.
