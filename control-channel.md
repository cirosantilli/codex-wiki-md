# Control channel

↑ **Parent:** [Message passing](message-passing.md)

A [message-passing channel](message-passing-channel.md) carrying protocol instructions, such as cancellation or permission to send, rather than ordinary application payloads. A reverse [control channel](control-channel.md) can communicate consumer closure back to a remote producer.
