# Photochemical escape

↑ **Parent:** [Atmospheric escape](atmospheric-escape.md)

Escape following chemical reactions that produce [atoms](atom-physics.md) or molecules with energies above the local gravitational escape threshold. The production altitude, collision environment and reaction [energy](energy-split.md) distribution matter.
