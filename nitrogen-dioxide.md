# Nitrogen dioxide

↑ **Parent:** [Nitrogen oxides](nitrogen-oxides.md)

The atmospheric [nitrogen](nitrogen.md) oxide $\mathrm{NO_2}$. Daylight [photodissociation](photodissociation.md) forms [nitrogen monoxide](nitrogen-monoxide.md) and an [oxygen](oxygen.md) atom; subsequent [third-body recombination](third-body-recombination.md) with [oxygen](oxygen.md) forms [ozone](ozone.md). Whether this makes net [ozone](ozone.md) depends on the pathway that regenerated [nitrogen dioxide](nitrogen-dioxide.md).
