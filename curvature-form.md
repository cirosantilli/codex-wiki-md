# Curvature form

↑ **Parent:** [Fiber bundle](fiber-bundle-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curvature_form)

The [curvature form](curvature-form.md) of a [connection on a vector bundle](connection-vector-bundle.md) is $F=dA+A\wedge A$ in a local frame. A [principal connection](connection-principal-bundle.md) has the corresponding [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md)-valued form $F=dA+\tfrac12[A\wedge A]$. It measures the failure of horizontal directions to be integrable.

The curvature is $F(A)=d_A^2$ and in a local frame satisfies $F(A)=dA+A\wedge A$.
