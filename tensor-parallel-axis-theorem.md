# Tensor parallel-axis theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Inertia tensor](inertia-tensor.md)

If $G$ is the [center of mass](center-of-mass.md) and $X=P-G$, expanding the defining [inertia tensor](inertia-tensor.md) about $P$ gives the displayed formula. The cross terms vanish because $\int r\,dm=0$ in coordinates centred at $G$. Contracting with a unit axis $u$ gives the [parallel axis theorem](parallel-axis-theorem.md), with the squared distance between the parallel axes equal to $|X|^2-(u\cdot X)^2$.
