# Integrated isothermal Bondi flow relation

↑ **Parent:** [Isothermal Bondi equation](isothermal-bondi-equation.md)

With $x=r/r_s$ and $\mathcal M=|u_r|/c_s$, the radial isothermal equation integrates to $\mathcal M^2/2-\ln\mathcal M=2/x+2\ln x+C$. A regular [transonic branch](transonic-branch.md) has $C=-3/2$, and the reservoir-fed accretion branch crosses $(x,\mathcal M)=(1,1)$ with slope $-1$.
