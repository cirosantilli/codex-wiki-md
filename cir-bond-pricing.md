# CIR bond pricing

↑ **Parent:** [Cox–Ingersoll–Ross model](cox-ingersoll-ross-model.md)

For the [CIR model](cox-ingersoll-ross-model.md) under its money-market [risk-neutral measure](risk-neutral-measure.md), the [Feynman-Kac formula](feynman-kac-formula.md) gives $B'=1-bB-\sigma^2B^2/2$ and $A'/A=-aB$, with $B(0)=0$, $A(0)=1$. Solving this [Riccati equation](riccati-equation.md) gives explicit exponential-affine [zero-coupon bond](zero-coupon-bond.md) prices. The positive root parameter is $\sqrt{b^2+2\sigma^2}$; the squared diffusion coefficient, not the volatility itself, enters this expression.
