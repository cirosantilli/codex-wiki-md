# Maurey hierarchy of finite sets

↑ **Parent:** [Rank of a well-founded tree](rank-of-a-well-founded-tree.md)

This hierarchy measures the complexity of successive finite selections. Start with $\mathcal M_0=\{\varnothing\}$. At a successor stage, retain the previous family and allow one smaller integer to be prepended. At a countable limit $\lambda$, choose positive ordinals $\lambda_j\uparrow\lambda$ and admit sets in $\mathcal M_{\lambda_j}$ whose minimum is at least $j$. Each family is hereditary and spreading, has no infinite branch, and its extension-tree rank on every infinite subset is at least $\alpha$. These properties make the hierarchy useful for transfinite constructions of [unconditional basic sequences](unconditional-basic-sequence.md).
