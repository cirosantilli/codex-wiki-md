# Radical of a Lie algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Solvable Lie algebra](solvable-lie-algebra.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radical_of_a_Lie_algebra)

The solvable radical of a finite-dimensional [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) is its largest solvable [ideal of a Lie algebra](ideal-of-a-lie-algebra.md). A Lie algebra is [semisimple](semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md) exactly when its solvable radical is zero.
