# Fermion number

↑ **Parent:** [Dirac field](dirac-field.md)

For a [Dirac field](dirac-field.md) with a [global phase symmetry](global-phase-symmetry.md), [fermion number](fermion-number.md) is the vacuum-subtracted charge of the [Dirac current](dirac-current.md). In a mode expansion it counts particles minus antiparticles, with the overall sign fixed by the chosen generator convention. It is distinct from the total number of fermionic excitations and from [fermion parity](fermion-parity.md), which can survive even when interactions violate the continuous charge.
