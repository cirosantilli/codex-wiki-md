# bc system

↑ **Parent:** [Two-dimensional conformal field theory](two-dimensional-conformal-field-theory.md)

A bc system is the anticommuting counterpart of a [beta-gamma system](beta-gamma-system.md). For weights $h$ and $1-h$, its central charge is $-2(6h^2-6h+1)$.
