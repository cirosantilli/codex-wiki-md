# Local parameter on a smooth algebraic curve

↑ **Parent:** [Local ring of a smooth algebraic curve](local-ring-of-a-smooth-algebraic-curve.md)

At a point of a [smooth algebraic curve](smooth-algebraic-curve.md), its [local ring](local-ring.md) is a [discrete valuation ring](discrete-valuation-ring.md). A local parameter is a generator $t$ of its [maximal ideal](maximal-ideal.md), equivalently an element of [valuation](valuation.md) one. It measures the order of a [zero of a function](zero-of-a-function.md) or [pole](pole.md) by expressing functions as powers of $t$ times units. Over the complex numbers its analytic counterpart is a [local coordinate](local-coordinate.md) vanishing simply at the point. For an [elliptic curve](elliptic-curve.md) in a [Weierstrass equation of an elliptic curve](weierstrass-equation-of-an-elliptic-curve.md), $t=-x/y$ is the standard parameter at the identity.
