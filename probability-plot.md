# Probability plot

↑ **Parent:** [Statistics](statistics.md)

A [probability plot](probability-plot.md) compares [order statistics](order-statistic.md) with plotting positions or model [quantiles](quantile-function.md) to diagnose whether a proposed [probability distribution](probability-distribution-split.md) describes data. If the model can be transformed into a linear identity $T(F(x))=a+bS(x)$, plot $T(i/(n+1))$ against $S(X_{(i)})$. Approximate linearity supports that model's shape; systematic curvature suggests a mismatch.

The plotting position $i/(n+1)$ comes from the exact mean of the $i$th [uniform order statistic](uniform-order-statistic.md), by the [probability integral transform](probability-integral-transform.md). It does not assert equality between the expectation of a nonlinear transformation and that transformation of the expectation. Fitted slope and intercept can estimate model parameters, but the visual plot alone is not a calibrated [hypothesis test](statistical-hypothesis-test-split.md).
