# Permutation cube identity criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Permutation](permutation.md)

For a [bijection](bijection.md) $f$, composing $f^2=f^{-1}$ with $f$ gives $f^3=\operatorname{id}$, and composing the latter with $f^{-1}$ gives the converse. Every orbit therefore has size one or three: the [permutation](permutation.md) consists of fixed points and disjoint three-cycles. This applies to an infinite underlying [set](set-split.md) as well as a finite one, since each individual orbit has at most three elements.
