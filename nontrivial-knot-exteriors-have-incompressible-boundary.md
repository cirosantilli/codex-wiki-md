# Nontrivial knot exteriors have incompressible boundary

↑ **Parent:** [Knot exterior](knot-exterior.md)

A [sphere](sphere.md) in a [knot](knot.md) exterior bounds a ball on the side not containing the [knot](knot.md), so the exterior is irreducible. A compression disk for its boundary [torus](torus.md) would cut the exterior to a manifold with [sphere](sphere.md) boundary; irreducibility makes that manifold a ball, and reversing the compression gives a solid [torus](torus.md) exterior. Its meridian disk has boundary a preferred [knot](knot.md) longitude, so adjoining the boundary annulus gives a spanning disk for the [knot](knot.md), which is then an [unknot](unknot.md). The [Loop theorem](loop-theorem.md) therefore makes the peripheral fundamental [group](group-split.md) injective for every nontrivial [knot](knot.md). In particular its [knot](knot.md) [group](group-split.md) contains an injected copy of the [torus](torus.md) [group](group-split.md) and cannot be cyclic.
