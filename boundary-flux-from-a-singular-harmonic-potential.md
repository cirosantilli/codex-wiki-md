# Boundary flux from a singular harmonic potential

↑ **Parent:** [Gradient pairing with a boundary-constant function and a harmonic function](gradient-pairing-with-a-boundary-constant-function-and-a-harmonic-function.md)

Although $1/r$ is harmonic away from the origin in three dimensions, it is not a regular [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md) on a ball containing the origin. Its [gradient](gradient.md) is $-\widehat r/r^2$, so the displayed improper volume integral follows by canceling the $r^2$ in the spherical volume element. On the annulus $\varepsilon<r<a$, [Green's first identity](green-s-first-identity.md) gives outer boundary contribution $-4\pi$ and inner contribution $4\pi\varepsilon/a$, recovering the same limit. Its global [Laplacian](laplacian.md) contains a [Dirac delta distribution](dirac-delta-function.md), so the regular-harmonic gradient-pairing theorem is inapplicable.
