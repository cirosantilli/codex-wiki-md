# Protocol ossification

↑ **Parent:** [Communication protocol](communication-protocol.md)

[Protocol ossification](protocol-ossification.md) occurs when deployed implementations or intermediaries make established behaviour difficult to change, for example by rejecting an unfamiliar transport format or assuming fields will never evolve. [Endpoint](communication-endpoint.md) support alone may then be insufficient for deployment over real paths.[§§2–5: interface and middlebox constraints on evolution](report-from-the-iab-workshop-on-stack-evolution-in-a-middlebox-internet-rfc-7663.md)
