**The nuclear determination of $|V_{ud}|$**. A $0^+\to0^+$ [beta decay transition](../../../../beta-decay-transition.md) has no nuclear [parity](../../../../parity.md) change and can proceed with zero lepton [orbital angular momentum](../../../../orbital-angular-momentum.md). The allowed scalar operator gives a [Fermi transition](../../../../fermi-transition.md); the rank-one [Gamow-Teller transition](../../../../gamow-teller-transition.md) operator cannot connect total nuclear spins zero to zero. The strong isobaric-analog transitions used for precision measurements are [superallowed beta decays](../../../../superallowed-beta-decay.md). A generic $0^+\to0^+$ assignment alone would not guarantee superallowed strength if the states were nonanalog.

At the [quark](../../../../quark.md) level the [weak charged current](../../../../charged-current.md) converts $d\leftrightarrow u$ and carries $V_{ud}$ in the amplitude. The nuclear Fermi operator is an [isospin](../../../../isospin.md) ladder operator, giving

$$
\mathcal M\propto G_FV_{ud}M_F,\qquad
\boxed{\Gamma\propto G_F^2|V_{ud}|^2|M_F|^2f.}
$$

Here $f$ denotes the known [phase space](../../../../phase-space.md) dependence, with the dimensional factors included in the proportionality constant. In the isospin-symmetric limit, $M_F$ between analog states is fixed by isospin algebra, for example $|M_F|^2=2$ for the usual $T=1$ analog transitions. A measured partial [half-life](../../../../half-life.md) and the computed [phase space](../../../../phase-space.md) factor give the [comparative half-life](../../../../comparative-half-life.md); after [radiative corrections](../../../../radiative-correction.md) and [isospin](../../../../isospin.md) breaking corrections, its inverse determines $|V_{ud}|^2$. The decay rate measures a magnitude squared, not the complex phase of $V_{ud}$.

**Ds decay diagrams and CKM comparisons**. The original PDF has $K^+\bar K^0$, with $\bar K^0=s\bar d$. The local TeX drops that overbar; using $K^0=d\bar s$ instead would give a different flavour channel. Use the PDF's [neutral antikaon](../../../../neutral-antikaon.md) throughout.

In the representative [Ds nonleptonic tree amplitudes](../../../../ds-nonleptonic-tree-amplitudes.md), the [Ds meson](../../../../ds-meson.md) spectator is $\bar s$. The elementary weak transitions are

$$
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\text{Channel}&\text{Quark transition}&\text{CKM magnitude}\\\hline
K^+\pi^0&c\to dW^+,\quad W^+\to u\bar d&|V_{cd}V_{ud}|\\
K^+\bar K^0&c\to sW^+,\quad W^+\to u\bar d&|V_{cs}V_{ud}|\\
\phi\pi^+&c\to sW^+,\quad W^+\to u\bar d&|V_{cs}V_{ud}|
\end{array}
$$

![](../../../../past-exam-of-the-particle-and-nuclear-physics-course-of-the-university-of-cambridge/2018-ds-nonleptonic-diagrams.png)

**[Figure 4](#section-b/b4/image-the-three-ds-spectator-tree-diagrams-using-the-neutral-antikaon-channel-in-the-original-pdf). The three Ds spectator tree diagrams, using the neutral-antikaon channel in the original PDF**.

In the first two channels, the produced $u$ joins the spectator $\bar s$ to form $K^+$; the other quark joins $\bar d$. For the first channel this uses the $-d\bar d/\sqrt2$ component of the [neutral pion](../../../../neutral-pion.md). In the third, $s\bar s$ forms the [phi meson](../../../../phi-meson.md) and $u\bar d$ forms $\pi^+$. The two possible final pairings have different colour contractions and hadronic [matrix elements](../../../../matrix-element.md) even when their weak CKM factors coincide.

The purely CKM rate suppression of $c\to d$ relative to $c\to s$ is

$$
\boxed{\left|\frac{V_{cd}}{V_{cs}}\right|^2
=\left(\frac{0.225}{0.974}\right)^2=0.0533638.}
$$

Thus $K^+\pi^0$ is singly [Cabibbo suppressed](../../../../cabibbo-suppression.md), while $K^+\bar K^0$ and $\phi\pi^+$ are Cabibbo-favored at this level. If reduced hadronic strengths and [phase space](../../../../phase-space.md) effects were equal, including the neutral-pion component would give

$$
\left[\frac{B(K^+\pi^0)}{B(K^+\bar K^0)}\right]_{\rm simple}
=\frac12\left|\frac{V_{cd}}{V_{cs}}\right|^2=0.0266819.
$$

The supplied [branching fractions](../../../../branching-fraction.md) instead give

$$
\frac{B(K^+\pi^0)}{B(K^+\bar K^0)}=\frac{6.3\times10^{-4}}{2.9\times10^{-2}}=0.0217241,
\qquad
\frac{B(K^+\pi^0)}{B(\phi\pi^+)}=0.0140,
$$



$$
\frac{B(K^+\bar K^0)}{B(\phi\pi^+)}=0.64444.
$$

The first observed ratio is $0.814$ times the neutral-pion-corrected simple estimate, requiring only an order-one remaining hadronic and [phase space](../../../../phase-space.md) factor. Relative to $\phi\pi^+$, that remaining factor is $0.525$. The two Cabibbo-favored channels differ by a factor $0.644$ despite equal CKM magnitudes. The CKM hierarchy therefore explains why one channel is much rarer, but it does not calculate the three exclusive [branching fractions](../../../../branching-fraction.md); [spin](../../../../spin.md), [phase space](../../../../phase-space.md), [color charge](../../../../color-charge.md) and [strong interaction](../../../../strong-interaction.md) final-state effects also matter.

**What spin and parity establish**. In either pseudoscalar-pseudoscalar final state, the total intrinsic [spin](../../../../spin.md) is zero. Conservation of [angular momentum](../../../../angular-momentum.md) for a spin-zero parent forces $L=0$, so the final [parity](../../../../parity.md) is

$$
P_f=(-1)(-1)(-1)^0=+1,
$$

opposite to the parent $0^-$ [Ds meson](../../../../ds-meson.md). Consequently

$$
\boxed{D_s^+\to K^+\pi^0,\ K^+\bar K^0\text{ require parity violation and are weak decays}.}
$$

For a [vector meson](../../../../vector-meson.md) plus a [pion](../../../../pion.md), the intrinsic final [spin](../../../../spin.md) is one. Coupling to total $J=0$ requires $L=1$, giving $P_f=(-1)(-1)(-1)^1=-1$, equal to the parent parity. Thus **spin and parity alone permit a parity-conserving $\phi\pi^+$ transition**. That is the limited sense in which it is not necessarily weak from this information alone. In reality it is also weak: the initial charm flavour disappears, which the [strong interaction](../../../../strong-interaction.md) and [electromagnetic interaction](../../../../electromagnetic-interaction.md) conserve.

**W branching fractions**. In the massless-daughter, tree-level estimate, each leptonic channel contributes one common unit of [decay width](../../../../decay-width.md). Each accessible quark pair contributes $3|V_{ij}|^2$ because of the three final colours. The [charged-current W branching-fraction counting](../../../../charged-current-w-branching-fraction-counting.md) denominator is

$$
D=3+3\sum_{i=u,c}\sum_{j=d,s,b}|V_{ij}|^2
=9.000897\simeq9.
$$

Only $u$ and $c$ are accessible up-type quarks; the large $V_{tb}$ is irrelevant because a [W boson](../../../../w-boson.md) cannot decay into the heavier [top quark](../../../../top-quark.md). Hence

$$
\boxed{B(W^-\to e^-\bar\nu_e)=\frac1D\simeq\frac19=11.1\%.}
$$

A final [bottom quark](../../../../bottom-quark.md) arises in $W^-\to b\bar u$ or $b\bar c$, so

$$
\boxed{B(W^-\text{ containing }b)=\frac{3(|V_{ub}|^2+|V_{cb}|^2)}D
=\frac{3(0.004^2+0.041^2)}{9.000897}
=5.66\times10^{-4}\simeq0.057\%.}
$$

This counts the primary decay channels; finite daughter masses and [QCD](../../../../quantum-chromodynamics.md) corrections refine the approximation.

**The requested top-lifetime estimate and its limitation**. Under the instructed equal-coefficient [Sargent's rule](../../../../sargent-s-rule.md) scaling, $\Gamma\propto m^5$ and the [mean lifetime](../../../../mean-lifetime-physics.md) is inversely proportional to that fifth power. Converting both masses to GeV gives

$$
\boxed{\tau_t^{(m^5)}=\tau_\mu\left(\frac{m_\mu}{m_t}\right)^5
=2.2\times10^{-6}\,\mathrm s\left(\frac{0.106}{175}\right)^5
=1.79\times10^{-22}\,\mathrm s.}
$$

This is the requested order-of-magnitude estimate, assuming the same effective coefficient for the two total decay rates. It corresponds to $c\tau\simeq54\,\mathrm{fm}$ and is longer than the [hadronization time](../../../../hadronization-time.md), roughly a few $10^{-24}\,\mathrm s$. Taken literally, it would allow top-flavored hadrons to form; it does not establish the physical statement that the top decays before hadronizing.

The extrapolation fails because [Sargent's rule](../../../../sargent-s-rule.md) uses a low-energy [Fermi interaction](../../../../fermi-interaction.md) approximation, whereas $m_t>m_W+m_b$ permits $t\to bW$ with an on-shell [W boson](../../../../w-boson.md). The physical leading [decay width](../../../../decay-width.md) instead scales as

$$
\Gamma(t\to bW)\simeq\frac{G_Fm_t^3|V_{tb}|^2}{8\pi\sqrt2}
(1-x)^2(1+2x),\qquad x=\frac{m_W^2}{m_t^2}.
$$

Using $m_W\simeq80.4\,\mathrm{GeV}$, the [Fermi constant](../../../../fermi-constant.md) $G_F=1.16638\times10^{-5}\,\mathrm{GeV^{-2}}$ and $|V_{tb}|=0.999$ gives about $1.55\,\mathrm{GeV}$ at the supplied top mass. Then $\tau_t=\hbar/\Gamma_t$, with $\hbar=6.58212\times10^{-25}\,\mathrm{GeV\,s}$, gives $\tau_t\simeq4.2\times10^{-25}\,\mathrm s$. This corrected regime explains [top-quark decay before hadronization](../../../../top-quark-decay-before-hadronization.md).[Top Quark review, section 61.2, equation 61.1 and comparison of lifetime with hadron formation](../../../../review-of-particle-physics-by-particle-data-group-2022.md)
