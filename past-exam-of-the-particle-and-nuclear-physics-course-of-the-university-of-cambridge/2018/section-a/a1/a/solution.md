<h1 id="section-a/a1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Treat the quoted maximum as the resonant [Z boson](../../../../../z-boson.md) contribution described by the [Breit-Wigner resonance cross section](../../../../../breit-wigner-resonance-cross-section.md). The [electron](../../../../../electron.md) and [Positron](../../../../../positron.md) each have [spin](../../../../../spin.md) $1/2$, while the [Z boson](../../../../../z-boson.md) has [spin](../../../../../spin.md) one, so

$$
g=\frac{2(1)+1}{[2(1/2)+1]^2}=\frac34.
$$

Neglect the [electron mass](../../../../../electron-mass.md) at this [centre-of-mass energy](../../../../../center-of-mass-energy.md). Each incident momentum magnitude is then $p_i=E_0/2$ in [natural units](../../../../../natural-units.md). Write $B_e=\Gamma_{ee}/\Gamma$. At the resonance peak the denominator is $\Gamma^2/4$, and the same partial [decay width](../../../../../decay-width.md) appears for production and decay:

$$
\sigma_{\rm peak}=\frac{\pi g}{p_i^2}\frac{\Gamma_{ee}^2}{\Gamma^2/4}
=\frac{12\pi}{E_0^2}B_e^2.
$$

The [peak resonance cross section and branching fractions](../../../../../peak-resonance-cross-section-and-branching-fractions.md) relation contains $B_e^2$, because the electronic channel occurs at both ends of the resonant process.

For an area in ordinary units, restore $(\hbar c)^2$. The original PDF prints $\hbar c=197.5\,\mathrm{MeV}$, which is dimensionally incomplete: the intended standard conversion is $197.5\,\mathrm{MeV\,fm}=0.1975\,\mathrm{GeV\,fm}$. Also,

$$
2.0\,\mathrm{nb}=2.0\times10^{-37}\,\mathrm{m^2}
=2.0\times10^{-7}\,\mathrm{fm^2}.
$$

Consequently

$$
B_e=\sqrt{\frac{\sigma_{\rm peak}E_0^2}{12\pi(\hbar c)^2}}
=\sqrt{\frac{(2.0\times10^{-7})(91.2)^2}{12\pi(0.1975)^2}},
\qquad
\boxed{B(Z^0\to e^+e^-)=0.03363\simeq3.4\%.}
$$

This estimate uses the supplied resonance model. The full physical elastic [electron](../../../../../electron.md)-[Positron](../../../../../positron.md) cross section also contains nonresonant [photon](../../../../../photon.md) exchange and interference; an experimental use of the formula must isolate the resonant contribution.
