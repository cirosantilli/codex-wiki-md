<h1 id="section-a/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

Use the leading lepton-orbital classification of a [beta decay transition](../../../../beta-decay-transition.md). For [lepton](../../../../lepton.md)-pair [orbital angular momentum](../../../../orbital-angular-momentum.md) $l$, [Fermi transitions](../../../../fermi-transition.md) have pair [spin](../../../../spin.md) $S=0$ and total lepton [angular momentum](../../../../angular-momentum.md) $J=l$; [Gamow-Teller transitions](../../../../gamow-teller-transition.md) have $S=1$ and $J=|l-1|,\ldots,l+1$. [Addition of angular momentum](../../../../addition-of-angular-momentum.md) requires

$$
|J_i-J_f|\leq J\leq J_i+J_f,
$$

while the nuclear [parity](../../../../parity.md) change requires $\pi_f/\pi_i=(-1)^l$. The least allowed $l$ gives the order: $l=0$ means an [allowed beta decay](../../../../allowed-beta-decay.md), and $l=n>0$ an $n$th-[forbidden beta decay](../../../../forbidden-beta-decay.md). Fermi-type and Gamow-Teller-type below refer to these spin-independent and spin-dependent contributions, including their higher-orbital generalizations.


- For $^{129}\mathrm I\to{}^{129}\mathrm{Xe}$, the nuclear [spin](../../../../spin.md) changes by $2$ and [parity](../../../../parity.md) is unchanged. An $l=0$ [lepton](../../../../lepton.md) pair cannot carry $J\geq2$; $l=1$ would have the wrong [parity](../../../../parity.md). At $l=2$, [Fermi transitions](../../../../fermi-transition.md) can carry $J=2$, and [Gamow-Teller transitions](../../../../gamow-teller-transition.md) can carry $J=1,2,3$, including the permitted transfers $2$ and $3$. Therefore this is a **second-forbidden, nonunique transition, with both Fermi-type and Gamow-Teller-type contributions permitted**. The quantum numbers do not determine their actual relative matrix elements.
- For $^{73}\mathrm{Kr}\to{}^{73}\mathrm{Br}$, $\Delta J=1$ and [parity](../../../../parity.md) is unchanged. Already $l=0,S=1$ carries $J=1$. The $l=0$ operator for a [Fermi transition](../../../../fermi-transition.md) has rank zero and cannot connect these unequal nuclear [spins](../../../../spin.md). This is an **allowed Gamow-Teller transition**. The decrease of [atomic number](../../../../atomic-number.md) makes the nuclear conversion proton-to-neutron; the [angular momentum](../../../../angular-momentum.md) classification applies to either the energetically available [beta-plus decay](../../../../beta-plus-decay.md) or [electron capture](../../../../electron-capture.md) branch.
- For $^{158}\mathrm{Yb}\to{}^{158}\mathrm{Tm}$, $0^+\to2^-$ requires transfer $J=2$ and a [parity](../../../../parity.md) change. The lowest possible orbital order is $l=1$. The $S=0$ pair for a [Fermi transition](../../../../fermi-transition.md) then has only $J=1$, whereas the $S=1$ pair for a [Gamow-Teller transition](../../../../gamow-teller-transition.md) can attain $J=2$. Thus this is a **first-forbidden unique Gamow-Teller-type transition**. Here unique means the maximal transfer $J=l+1$ at the leading orbital order, as in a [unique forbidden beta decay](../../../../unique-forbidden-beta-decay.md).
- For $^{22}\mathrm{Mg}\to{}^{22}\mathrm{Na}$, $0^+\to0^+$ permits the scalar operator for a [Fermi transition](../../../../fermi-transition.md) with $l=0$. The allowed [Gamow-Teller transition](../../../../gamow-teller-transition.md) operator has rank one, and the triangle rule excludes $0\to0$. Interpreting the printed logarithm in the usual [comparative half-life](../../../../comparative-half-life.md) convention gives


$$
ft=10^{3.48}\,\mathrm s=3.02\times10^3\,\mathrm s.
$$

This small value supports the expected **superallowed Fermi transition** between [isobaric analog states](../../../../isobaric-analog-state.md). The $0^+\to0^+$ quantum numbers alone would establish allowed Fermi character, but would not by themselves establish the large analog-state overlap implied by superallowed strength.

In the order listed, the concise classification is

$$
\boxed{\begin{array}{ll}
1:\ \mathrm{F+GT},\ \text{second forbidden};&2:\ \mathrm{GT},\ \text{allowed};\\
3:\ \mathrm{GT},\ \text{first forbidden unique};&4:\ \mathrm{F},\ \text{superallowed}.
\end{array}}
$$
