<h1 id="4/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The gamma width is $0.10\,\mathrm{eV}=10^{-7}\,\mathrm{MeV}$, so the remaining [proton](../../../../../proton.md) width is $\Gamma_p=0.20-0.15-10^{-7}=0.0499999\,\mathrm{MeV}$. At the peak of the same resonance the total-width denominator and gamma exit width cancel. [alpha particles](../../../../../alpha-particle.md) and [carbon-12](../../../../../carbon-12.md) have [spin](../../../../../spin.md) zero; the [proton](../../../../../proton.md) and the stated nitrogen level each have [spin](../../../../../spin.md) $1/2$. Hence $g_\alpha/g_p=4$, independently of the unspecified resonance [spin](../../../../../spin.md). The [proton](../../../../../proton.md) [reduced mass](../../../../../reduced-mass.md) and [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) are

$$
\mu_pc^2=879.223\,\mathrm{MeV},\qquad p_p^*c=\sqrt{2(879.223)(2.113770)}=60.967\,\mathrm{MeV}.
$$

The [same-resonance entrance-channel cross-section ratio](../../../../../same-resonance-entrance-channel-cross-section-ratio.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{\frac{\sigma_\alpha}{\sigma_p}
=\frac{g_\alpha}{g_p}\frac{p_p^{*2}}{p_\alpha^{*2}}\frac{\Gamma_\alpha}{\Gamma_p}
=4\frac{60.967^2}{205.790^2}\frac{0.15}{0.0499999}\simeq1.053}.
$$

The ratio compares each reaction at its own laboratory resonance [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md). Factors of $\hbar^2$ restoring the [cross section](../../../../../cross-section-physics.md)'s area units cancel. Only the supplied nitrogen [spin](../../../../../spin.md) magnitude is used: its printed positive [parity](../../../../../parity.md) is immaterial to this [spin](../../../../../spin.md) average, so no orbital-parity inference is made from that assignment.
