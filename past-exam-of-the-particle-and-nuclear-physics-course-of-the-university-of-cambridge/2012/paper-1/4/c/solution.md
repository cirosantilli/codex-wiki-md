<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Unpolarized projectile and target [spins](../../../../../spin.md) supply $(2J_x+1)(2J_X+1)$ equally weighted [spin](../../../../../spin.md) orientations. An isolated compound-nucleus level of [spin](../../../../../spin.md) $J$ has $2J+1$ degenerate magnetic substates. Sum over these resonance substates and average over the entrance [spins](../../../../../spin.md) to obtain

$$
\boxed{g=\frac{2J+1}{(2J_x+1)(2J_X+1)}}.
$$

This [resonance spin statistical factor](../../../../../resonance-spin-statistical-factor.md) is a statistical [spin](../../../../../spin.md) weight, not always a probability smaller than one: [orbital angular momentum](../../../../../orbital-angular-momentum.md) also participates in forming the resonance. The partial-wave resonance [cross section](../../../../../cross-section-physics.md) carries the corresponding angular-momentum multiplicity. Polarized beams or unresolved overlapping [spin](../../../../../spin.md) channels require a different average or a sum over channels.
