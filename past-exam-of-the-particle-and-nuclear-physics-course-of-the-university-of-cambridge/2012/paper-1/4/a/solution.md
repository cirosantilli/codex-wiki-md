↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [partial decay width](../../../../../partial-decay-width.md) $\Gamma_x$ is the [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) width associated with decay of the [compound nucleus](../../../../../compound-nucleus.md) back into the entrance channel $x+X$; by [time reversal](../../../../../t-symmetry.md) it also controls formation from that channel. The [partial decay width](../../../../../partial-decay-width.md) $\Gamma_y$ describes the selected exit channel $y+Y$. The total [decay width](../../../../../decay-width.md) sums all energetically accessible channels, including these: $\boxed{\Gamma=\sum_i\Gamma_i=\hbar/\tau}$. The [branching fraction](../../../../../branching-fraction.md) for channel $i$ is $\Gamma_i/\Gamma$. Widths have [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) units, whereas $\Gamma_i/\hbar$ is a decay probability per unit time. The isolated-resonance formula assumes approximately constant widths over the resonance and neglects a nonresonant interfering background.
