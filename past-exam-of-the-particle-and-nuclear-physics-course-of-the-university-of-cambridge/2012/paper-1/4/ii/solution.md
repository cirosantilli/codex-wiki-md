<h1 id="4/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For [resonance channel threshold conversion](../../../../../resonance-channel-threshold-conversion.md), equate the compound-state [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) measured from the two different channel thresholds. The proton-plus-nitrogen threshold exceeds the alpha-plus-carbon threshold by

$$
\Delta=(938.26+13973.30-3728.34-11177.76)\,\mathrm{MeV}=5.46\,\mathrm{MeV}.
$$

Thus $T_p^*=T_\alpha^*-\Delta=2.113770\,\mathrm{MeV}$ and

$$
\boxed{T_{p,\mathrm{lab}}=T_p^*\frac{938.26+13973.30}{13973.30}=2.25570\,\mathrm{MeV}\simeq2.26\,\mathrm{MeV}}.
$$

The positive centre-of-momentum [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) verifies that the [proton](../../../../../proton.md) channel is open. These numbers use the stipulated nonrelativistic kinematics and rounded nuclear masses.
