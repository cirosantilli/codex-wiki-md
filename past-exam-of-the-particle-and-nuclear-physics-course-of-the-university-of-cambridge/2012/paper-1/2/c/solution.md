[nuclear radius scaling](../../../../../nuclear-radius-scaling.md) follows approximately $R=r_0A^{1/3}$ with $r_0\simeq1.2\,\mathrm{fm}$ for a sharp matter-radius convention. Consequently the interior [nucleon](../../../../../nucleon.md) density is nearly independent of [mass number](../../../../../mass-number.md): [nuclear forces](../../../../../nuclear-force-split.md) are short range and exhibit [nuclear matter saturation](../../../../../nuclear-matter-saturation.md). A diffuse [nuclear charge distribution](../../../../../nuclear-charge-distribution.md) has a charge-weighted [root mean square](../../../../../root-mean-square.md) radius, not a hard surface. For a uniform sphere, $r_{\rm ch}=\sqrt{3/5}R$; charge radii and matter radii need not coincide, especially with [neutron](../../../../../neutron-split.md) skins.

Elastic [electron scattering](../../../../../electron-scattering.md) measures the [nuclear charge form factor](../../../../../nuclear-charge-form-factor.md). In a simple one-photon, spherical approximation, with $q$ a wave number,

$$
F(q)=\frac{1}{Ze}\int\rho_{\rm ch}(\mathbf r)e^{i\mathbf q\cdot\mathbf r}\,d^3r,
\qquad F(q)=1-\frac{q^2\langle r^2\rangle}{6}+\cdots.
$$

The scattering [cross section](../../../../../cross-section-physics.md) relative to a point-charge prediction is proportional to $|F|^2$, so its low-$q$ slope constrains the radius and [diffraction](../../../../../diffraction.md) structure constrains the radial distribution. Finite [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) coverage and [Coulomb distortion in electron scattering](../../../../../coulomb-distortion-in-electron-scattering.md) limit reconstruction. In [Robert Hofstadter](../../../../../robert-hofstadter.md)'s experiment a [magnetic spectrometer](../../../../../magnetic-spectrometer.md) separated elastic and inelastic [electron](../../../../../electron.md) peaks; beam-charge normalization and closed-slit background checks prevented raw count structure from being mistaken for a [nuclear charge form factor](../../../../../nuclear-charge-form-factor.md).[§III(b)(1), pp. 222–223; §IV(a), pp. 227–231](../../../../../electron-scattering-and-nuclear-structure-by-robert-hofstadter.md)

A useful intrinsic-shape description is $R(\theta,\phi)=R_0[1+\sum_{\mu=-2}^{2}\alpha_{2\mu}Y_{2\mu}(\theta,\phi)+\cdots]$. An [intrinsic nuclear quadrupole moment](../../../../../intrinsic-nuclear-quadrupole-moment.md) $Q_0=\int\rho_{\rm ch}(3z^2-r^2)\,d^3r$ distinguishes prolate and oblate positive-charge distributions by positive and negative signs. The laboratory [spectroscopic nuclear quadrupole moment](../../../../../spectroscopic-nuclear-quadrupole-moment.md) is state dependent: an $I=0$ state has zero laboratory quadrupole moment even when its intrinsic shape is deformed. [nuclear rotational bands](../../../../../nuclear-rotational-band.md) approximately follow $E_I\propto I(I+1)$; harmonic [nuclear quadrupole vibrations](../../../../../nuclear-quadrupole-vibration.md) instead have equally spaced [phonon](../../../../../phonon.md) energies and multiphonon [spin](../../../../../spin.md) multiplets. Measured [electric quadrupole transitions](../../../../../electric-quadrupole-transition.md) test the collective interpretation in addition to level energies. **Radius probes spatial extent; quadrupole observables and collective spectra probe shape.**
