[beta decay](../../../../../beta-decay.md) is a [weak charged current](../../../../../charged-current.md) process. At [quark](../../../../../quark.md) level $d\to uW^{-*}\to ue^-\bar\nu_e$, with amplitude proportional to $G_FV_{ud}$ at low [momentum transfer](../../../../../momentum-transfer.md). A [neutron](../../../../../neutron-split.md) in a [nucleus](../../../../../atomic-nucleus-split.md) can thereby become a [proton](../../../../../proton.md):

$$
n\to pe^-\bar\nu_e,\qquad p\to ne^+\nu_e,\qquad pe^-\to n\nu_e.
$$

The second reaction denotes an energetically allowed nuclear conversion, not a decay of an isolated free [proton](../../../../../proton.md). In a nuclear beta transition the [mass number](../../../../../mass-number.md) stays fixed and the [atomic number](../../../../../atomic-number.md) changes by one. [baryon number](../../../../../baryon-number.md) and [electric charge](../../../../../electric-charge-split.md) are conserved; the [neutrino](../../../../../neutrino.md) or [antineutrino](../../../../../antineutrino.md) supplies the missing [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md), [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) and [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md). The three-body final state produces a continuous [electron](../../../../../electron.md) spectrum rather than a single [electron](../../../../../electron.md) [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md). Neglecting [neutrino mass](../../../../../neutrino-mass.md) and recoil, an allowed spectrum in [electron](../../../../../electron.md) total [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) is

$$
\frac{d\Gamma}{dE_e}\propto F(Z,E_e)\,p_eE_e(E_0-E_e)^2
\left(g_V^2|M_F|^2+g_A^2|M_{GT}|^2\right).
$$

The [Fermi function](../../../../../fermi-function.md) accounts for the [electron](../../../../../electron.md)'s [Coulomb interaction](../../../../../coulomb-interaction.md) with the daughter; $E_0$ is the [electron](../../../../../electron.md) total-energy endpoint. [forbidden beta decay](../../../../../forbidden-beta-decay.md) adds momentum-dependent nuclear shape factors. The [V-A interaction](../../../../../v-a-interaction.md) violates [parity](../../../../../parity.md): it couples left-chiral [fermion](../../../../../fermion.md) fields, not an equal mixture of left and right currents.

For [allowed beta decay](../../../../../allowed-beta-decay.md) the leading [leptons](../../../../../lepton.md) have zero [orbital angular momentum](../../../../../orbital-angular-momentum.md). [Fermi transitions](../../../../../fermi-transition.md) have $\Delta J=0$; [Gamow-Teller transitions](../../../../../gamow-teller-transition.md) have $\Delta J=0,1$ with $0\to0$ excluded. Both preserve nuclear [parity](../../../../../parity.md). Higher [lepton](../../../../../lepton.md) partial waves permit forbidden transitions with extra suppression. Nuclear matrix elements and [phase space](../../../../../phase-space.md), as well as these [selection rules](../../../../../selection-rule.md), determine lifetimes. Particle-physics examples include free-neutron decay and [pion beta decay](../../../../../pion-beta-decay.md), $\pi^+\to\pi^0e^+\nu_e$. [semileptonic decays](../../../../../semileptonic-decay.md) test [quark](../../../../../quark.md) mixing and the universality of the [weak charged current](../../../../../charged-current.md). **[beta decay](../../../../../beta-decay.md) changes [quark flavour](../../../../../quark-flavor.md) through virtual W exchange; its continuous spectrum reflects an additional [lepton](../../../../../lepton.md) in the final state.**
