<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A bare [gauge boson](../../../../../gauge-boson.md) mass would spoil the electroweak gauge structure. In the [Standard Model](../../../../../standard-model-split.md) a complex [Higgs doublet](../../../../../higgs-field.md) instead has a symmetry-breaking potential. Choose the convention

$$
V=-\mu^2\Phi^\dagger\Phi+\lambda(\Phi^\dagger\Phi)^2,\qquad
\Phi=\frac1{\sqrt2}\binom{0}{v+h},\qquad v^2=\mu^2/\lambda.
$$

The [Higgs mechanism](../../../../../higgs-mechanism.md) rearranges three scalar components into the [longitudinal polarizations](../../../../../longitudinal-polarization.md) of the massive [W bosons](../../../../../w-boson.md) and [Z bosons](../../../../../z-boson.md); the unbroken [electromagnetic gauge symmetry](../../../../../electromagnetic-gauge-symmetry.md) leaves the [photon](../../../../../photon.md) massless. One physical scalar excitation, the [Higgs boson](../../../../../higgs-boson.md) $h$, remains.[pp. 508–509, gauge-field mass generation in a symmetry-breaking scalar model](../../../../../broken-symmetries-and-the-masses-of-gauge-bosons-by-peter-higgs.md) Using [tree-level Feynman diagrams](../../../../../tree-level-feynman-diagram.md),

$$
\boxed{m_W=gv/2,\quad m_Z=\sqrt{g^2+g'^2}\,v/2,\quad m_h^2=2\lambda v^2,\quad m_f=y_fv/\sqrt2}.
$$

These equations use [natural units](../../../../../natural-units.md). [Yukawa interactions](../../../../../yukawa-interaction.md) generate charged-fermion masses and Higgs couplings proportional to those masses; the minimal model does not account for observed [neutrino masses](../../../../../neutrino-mass.md). The gauge kinetic term supplies Higgs couplings to W and Z pairs. The physical Higgs exchange also cancels the otherwise excessive high-energy growth of longitudinal vector-boson scattering. The mechanism does not fix the [Yukawa couplings](../../../../../yukawa-interaction.md) or the Higgs self-coupling, and most [proton](../../../../../proton.md) mass arises from strong-interaction dynamics rather than directly from the light-quark Higgs masses.

The mass-generation mechanism is a statement about the field and its [vacuum expectation value](../../../../../vacuum-expectation-value.md); the observable boson is the excitation about that vacuum.
