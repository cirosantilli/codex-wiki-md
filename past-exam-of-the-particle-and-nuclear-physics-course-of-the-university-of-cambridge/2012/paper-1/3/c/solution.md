<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For the neutral quark-antiquark [mesons](../../../../../meson.md) here, $C=(-1)^{L+S}$. Thus $C(\eta')=C(\pi^0)=+1$, while $C(\rho^0)=C(\gamma)=-1$. The radiative $\rho^0\gamma$ mode conserves [charge conjugation](../../../../../charge-conjugation.md) and admits a quark-line photon-emission diagram. Both components of the supplied $\rho^0$ [wavefunction](../../../../../wave-function-split.md) contribute: the charge-weighted flavour overlap is proportional to $Q_u-Q_d=1$, so it does not cancel. Its [photon](../../../../../photon.md) [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) is $(m_{\eta'}^2-m_\rho^2)/(2m_{\eta'})\simeq165\,\mathrm{MeV}$.

The $\pi^0\gamma$ mode changes [charge conjugation](../../../../../charge-conjugation.md) and, more decisively, a spin-zero particle cannot decay into another spin-zero particle and one real [photon](../../../../../photon.md). In the parent rest frame the [photon](../../../../../photon.md) has [helicity](../../../../../helicity.md) $\pm1$, while the opposite-moving [pion](../../../../../pion.md) has [helicity](../../../../../helicity.md) zero; the [spin-zero two-body helicity selection](../../../../../spin-zero-two-body-helicity-selection.md) rule cannot be satisfied. **Its on-shell two-body amplitude vanishes even if a [weak interaction](../../../../../weak-interaction.md) violates C.** A virtual [photon](../../../../../photon.md) subsequently making another pair is a different final state.

For $\pi^0\rho^0$, angular-momentum conservation requires relative [orbital angular momentum](../../../../../orbital-angular-momentum.md) $L=1$. Its [parity](../../../../../parity.md) is then negative, matching the parent, but its charge-conjugation eigenvalue is $C_\pi C_\rho=-1$: the two distinct self-conjugate particles are not interchanged by C, so there is no additional $(-1)^L$ factor in this C eigenvalue. The decay is forbidden by strong and electromagnetic charge-conjugation conservation despite being above threshold. A weak contribution would require [CP violation](../../../../../cp-violation.md) as well. At the strong/electromagnetic accuracy of the diagram classification,

$$
\boxed{\Gamma(\rho^0\gamma)>\Gamma(\pi^0\rho^0)=\Gamma(\pi^0\gamma)=0}.
$$

If extremely small weak CP-violating amplitudes are retained, $\pi^0\rho^0$ need not be exactly zero; the one-real-photon [spin](../../../../../spin.md) prohibition remains exact for $\pi^0\gamma$.
