<h1 id="3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

A [Feynman diagram](../../../../feynman-diagram.md) specifies a term in the perturbative [scattering amplitude](../../../../scattering-amplitude.md), not a measured microscopic trajectory. Assign external [spinors](../../../../spinor.md) or [polarization vectors](../../../../polarization-vector.md), an interaction factor to each [Feynman vertex](../../../../interaction-vertex.md) and a [propagator](../../../../propagator.md) to each internal line; impose [four-momentum](../../../../four-momentum.md) conservation and integrate over independent [loop momenta](../../../../loop-momentum.md). Sum amplitudes for indistinguishable alternatives, then square, sum final [spins](../../../../spin.md) and average unpolarized initial [spins](../../../../spin.md). A [decay rate](../../../../decay-width.md) uses $d\Gamma=|\mathcal M|^2d\Phi_n/(2M)$ in [natural units](../../../../natural-units.md); a [cross section](../../../../cross-section-physics.md) also divides by incident flux. [interference](../../../../interference-wave-propagation.md) means probabilities of individual diagrams must not simply be added.

The electromagnetic [fermion](../../../../fermion.md) vertex is $f\bar f\gamma$, with coupling proportional to $eQ_f\gamma^\mu$; it preserves [fermion](../../../../fermion.md) flavour. There is no elementary three-photon or four-photon vertex in [quantum electrodynamics](../../../../quantum-electrodynamics.md). The [weak charged current](../../../../charged-current.md) has $W\ell\nu$ and $Wu_id_j$ vertices, with a left-chiral projector and a [Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa matrix](../../../../cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md) element at the [quark](../../../../quark.md) vertex. The [weak neutral current](../../../../neutral-current.md) has $Zf\bar f$ vertices with vector and axial couplings; it includes [neutrinos](../../../../neutrino.md) and is flavour diagonal at tree level. Electroweak gauge interactions also include $WW\gamma$, $WWZ$ and quartic $WWWW$, $WWZZ$, $WWZ\gamma$ and $WW\gamma\gamma$ vertices. Higgs-fermion and Higgs-vector interactions complete the mass-related scalar sector.[§10.1, Eqs. 10.2–10.7 and discussion of bosonic interactions](../../../../electroweak-model-and-constraints-on-new-physics-by-j-erler-and-a-freitas-2024.md)

[Quantum chromodynamics](../../../../quantum-chromodynamics.md) has $q\bar qg$ vertices proportional to $g_s\gamma^\mu T^a$, plus three-gluon and four-gluon [Feynman vertices](../../../../interaction-vertex.md) because [gluons](../../../../gluon.md) carry [color charge](../../../../color-charge.md). A quark-gluon vertex preserves [quark flavour](../../../../quark-flavor.md). Internal particles need not be on their [mass shells](../../../../mass-shell.md), but [electric charge](../../../../electric-charge-split.md) and [four-momentum](../../../../four-momentum.md) are conserved at each vertex.
