<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [nuclear quadrupole vibration](../../../../../nuclear-quadrupole-vibration.md) has five one-phonon magnetic substates, $m=-2,-1,0,1,2$. [Bose-Einstein statistics](../../../../../bose-einstein-statistics.md) restrict three identical [phonons](../../../../../phonon.md) to the [symmetric power](../../../../../symmetric-power.md) $S^3(V_2)$, of dimension $\binom{5+3-1}{3}=35$. Count occupation states $(n_{-2},\ldots,n_2)$ with $\sum n_m=3$ and $\sum mn_m=M$. For nonnegative $M$ the counts are

$$
\begin{array}{c|rrrrrrrr}M&0&1&2&3&4&5&6&7\\\hline d_M&5&4&4&3&2&1&1&0\end{array}.
$$

Each spin-$J$ [irreducible representation](../../../../../irreducible-representation.md) contributes one state at every projection from $-J$ to $J$. [magnetic-substate subtraction](../../../../../magnetic-substate-subtraction.md) therefore gives multiplicity $d_J-d_{J+1}$: one each at $J=0,2,3,4,6$, and none at $1,5$. Thus the [three-quadrupole-phonon multiplet](../../../../../three-quadrupole-phonon-multiplet.md) is

$$
\boxed{S^3(V_2)=V_0\oplus V_2\oplus V_3\oplus V_4\oplus V_6,\qquad J^P=0^+,2^+,3^+,4^+,6^+}.
$$

The dimension check is $1+5+7+9+13=35$. The [parity](../../../../../parity.md) is positive because each constituent [phonon](../../../../../phonon.md) has positive [parity](../../../../../parity.md). Pairwise angular-momentum triangle rules alone would include states incompatible with complete boson symmetrization.
