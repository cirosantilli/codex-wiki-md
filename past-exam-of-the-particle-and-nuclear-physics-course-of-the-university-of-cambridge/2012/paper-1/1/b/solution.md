<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Neglect atomic [binding energy](../../../../../binding-energy.md) and take the target [electron](../../../../../electron.md) at rest. Use [natural units](../../../../../natural-units.md) temporarily. The final state contains three [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) and one [Positron](../../../../../positron.md), so its threshold [invariant mass](../../../../../invariant-mass.md) is $4m_e$. For incident total [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) $E$,

$$
s=(p_1+p_2)^2=2m_e^2+2m_eE=16m_e^2,
\qquad \boxed{E_{\rm th}=7m_ec^2=3.577\,\mathrm{MeV}\simeq3.58\,\mathrm{MeV}}.
$$

The corresponding incident [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) is $6m_ec^2=3.066\,\mathrm{MeV}$. At threshold the products have no relative [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) in the [centre-of-momentum frame](../../../../../center-of-momentum-frame.md); their common laboratory motion requires more [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) than merely the pair's rest [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md).
