<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use the [inverse beta decay threshold](../../../../inverse-beta-decay-threshold.md), with the charged-lepton [mass](../../../../mass.md) generalized to $m_\ell$. For a stationary [proton](../../../../proton.md) and a massless incident [antineutrino](../../../../antineutrino.md), the [Mandelstam invariant](../../../../mandelstam-variables.md) is $s=m_p^2+2m_pE_\nu$. At threshold the final particles have no relative momentum in their [centre-of-momentum frame](../../../../center-of-momentum-frame.md), so $s_{\rm thr}=(m_n+m_\ell)^2$. Therefore

$$
E_{\nu,\rm thr}=\frac{(m_n+m_\ell)^2-m_p^2}{2m_p}=(m_n-m_p+m_\ell)+\frac{(m_n-m_p+m_\ell)^2}{2m_p}.
$$

Using the supplied, rounded [masses](../../../../mass.md) gives

$$
\boxed{E_{\bar\nu_e,\rm thr}=1.813\,\mathrm{MeV},\qquad E_{\bar\nu_\mu,\rm thr}=113.1\,\mathrm{MeV}.}
$$

The second term is the recoil correction; setting the threshold equal just to the final rest-mass excess would miss it.

Reactor [electron antineutrinos](../../../../electron-antineutrino.md) have energies of a few million [electronvolts](../../../../electronvolt.md). They can produce a [Positron](../../../../positron.md) through [inverse beta decay](../../../../inverse-beta-decay.md) above the first threshold, but cannot produce a [muon](../../../../muon.md). Thus reactor [neutrino oscillations](../../../../neutrino-oscillation.md) are measured primarily as **electron-antineutrino disappearance**, through the event rate and energy spectrum relative to the unoscillated prediction. An oscillated [muon antineutrino](../../../../muon-antineutrino.md) cannot be identified by the proposed muon-production channel at reactor energies; disappearance alone does not distinguish all possible final flavours.
