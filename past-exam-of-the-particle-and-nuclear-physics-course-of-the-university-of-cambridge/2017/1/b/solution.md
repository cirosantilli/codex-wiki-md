<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The separate [proton](../../../../proton.md) and [neutron](../../../../neutron-split.md) shell capacities are $2j+1=2,4,2,6,\ldots$. Filled shells and paired particles have zero net [spin](../../../../spin.md) and [magnetic dipole moment](../../../../magnetic-dipole-moment.md). The [single-particle nuclear magnetic moment](../../../../single-particle-nuclear-magnetic-moment.md) is $\mu=g_jj\mu_N$, with orbital factors $g_\ell^p=1$, $g_\ell^n=0$ and $s=1/2$.

For nitrogen, the eight [neutrons](../../../../neutron-split.md) form a closed shell. The seventh [proton](../../../../proton.md) occupies $1p_{1/2}$, so $\ell=1$, $j=1/2$ and the [Landé g-factor](../../../../lande-g-factor.md) gives

$$
g_j=\frac43g_\ell-\frac13g_s=\frac43-\frac{5.586}{3}=-0.52867.
$$

Hence the [nuclear shell model](../../../../nuclear-shell-model.md) predicts

$$
\boxed{\mu({}^{15}\mathrm N)=-0.2643\,\mu_N.}
$$

Its single-particle [spin](../../../../spin.md) and [parity](../../../../parity.md) is $1/2^-$. The negative moment results from the proton spin being predominantly opposed to the total [angular momentum](../../../../angular-momentum.md) in this $j=\ell-1/2$ level.

For oxygen, eight [protons](../../../../proton.md) and the first eight [neutrons](../../../../neutron-split.md) are paired. The ninth [neutron](../../../../neutron-split.md) occupies $1d_{5/2}$, with $\ell=2$, $j=5/2=\ell+1/2$. Since its orbital factor is zero, $g_j=g_n/(2\ell+1)=-3.826/5$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\mu({}^{17}\mathrm O)=-1.913\,\mu_N,\qquad J^P=5/2^+.}
$$

These are ideal single-particle predictions in units of the [nuclear magneton](../../../../nuclear-magneton.md), not fits incorporating nuclear configuration mixing.
