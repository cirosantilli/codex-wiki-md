<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For negligible final [fermion](../../../../fermion.md) masses, the [chiral Z-boson partial width](../../../../chiral-z-boson-partial-width.md) is proportional to $g_L^2+g_R^2$. Both channels have one color state. An [electron](../../../../electron.md) has $Q=-1$, $T_{3L}=-1/2$ and $T_{3R}=0$, giving

$$
g_L^e=-\frac12+0.23=-0.27,\qquad g_R^e=0.23.
$$

For one active [neutrino](../../../../neutrino.md) species, $Q=0$, $T_{3L}=1/2$ and there is no coupled right-handed neutrino field, so $g_L^\nu=1/2$, $g_R^\nu=0$. The common coupling normalization and [phase space](../../../../phase-space.md) cancel in the ratio:

$$
\frac{\Gamma(Z\to\nu_e\bar\nu_e)}{\Gamma(Z\to e^-e^+)}=\frac{(1/2)^2}{(-0.27)^2+(0.23)^2}=1.9873.
$$

The same total [decay width](../../../../decay-width.md) appears in the two [branching fractions](../../../../branching-fraction.md), so

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Br}(Z\to\nu_e\bar\nu_e)=1.9873\times3.36\%=6.68\%.}
$$

There is no factor of three for a single specified flavour. Summing three active [neutrino](../../../../neutrino.md) flavours would give an invisible [branching fraction](../../../../branching-fraction.md) of approximately $20.0\%$ in this approximation.
