<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

**Theory.** [Quantum chromodynamics](../../../../quantum-chromodynamics.md) is a local, non-Abelian [gauge theory](../../../../gauge-theory.md) with gauge group [SU(3)](../../../../su-3-group.md) acting on [color charge](../../../../color-charge.md). Its matter fields are spin-half [quarks](../../../../quark.md) of six flavours, each in three colors; its eight massless spin-one [gluons](../../../../gluon.md) are the gauge fields. Unlike the neutral [photon](../../../../photon.md), [gluons](../../../../gluon.md) themselves carry gauge charge. Consequently there are three-gluon and four-gluon vertices as well as the quark-gluon vertex. A compact [Lagrangian density](../../../../lagrangian-density.md) is

$$
\mathcal L_{\rm QCD}=\sum_f\bar q_f(i\gamma^\mu D_\mu-m_f)q_f-\frac14G_{\mu\nu}^aG^{a\mu\nu},
$$

where $D_\mu$ is the gauge [covariant derivative](../../../../covariant-derivative.md) and the field strength contains the nonlinear term $g_sf^{abc}G_\mu^bG_\nu^c$.

The [running coupling](../../../../running-coupling.md) is weak at high momentum scales. At one loop, for $n_f$ active flavours,

$$
\alpha_s(Q^2)\simeq\frac{4\pi}{(11-2n_f/3)\ln(Q^2/\Lambda_{\rm QCD}^2)}.
$$

The positive coefficient $11-2n_f/3$ produces [asymptotic freedom](../../../../asymptotic-freedom.md): short-distance, large-$Q$ processes admit [perturbation theory](../../../../perturbation-theory.md). The gluon self-interaction is crucial to its sign. At low scales the coupling grows and isolated colored particles are not observed; the physical spectrum consists of color-singlet [hadrons](../../../../hadron.md), the phenomenon of [confinement](../../../../confinement.md). A hard-produced [quark](../../../../quark.md) or [gluon](../../../../gluon.md) radiates and undergoes [hadronization](../../../../hadronization.md), giving a collimated [jet](../../../../jet-particle-physics.md). The one-loop formula is not valid at $Q\sim\Lambda_{\rm QCD}$ and is not, by itself, a mathematical proof of [confinement](../../../../confinement.md).

**Experimental tests.** Different measurements test different ingredients of this account:

- [Deep inelastic scattering](../../../../deep-inelastic-scattering.md) resolves approximately pointlike spin-half constituents. Approximate [Bjorken scaling](../../../../bjorken-scaling.md) supplies the starting parton picture, while the measured logarithmic departures from scaling agree with QCD evolution of [parton distribution functions](../../../../parton-distribution-function.md).
- The [hadronic R ratio](../../../../hadronic-r-ratio.md) in [electron-positron annihilation](../../../../electron-positron-annihilation.md) counts squared quark charges multiplied by three colors, with calculable strong corrections. It tests color multiplicity as well as the quark charge assignments.
- Two-jet events identify a quark-antiquark hard topology. Three-jet events test hard gluon radiation, $e^+e^-\to q\bar qg$; their angular correlations support a spin-one [gluon](../../../../gluon.md). Four-jet correlations and radiation patterns further probe the non-Abelian color factors and gluon self-interactions.
- Determinations of $\alpha_s$ from processes at widely separated scales follow the same [running coupling](../../../../running-coupling.md), rather than independent interaction strengths. Jet rates, event shapes, hadronic weak-boson decays and high-energy hadron collisions provide complementary tests.
- [Lattice QCD](../../../../lattice-qcd.md) calculations reproduce many hadronic masses and other low-energy quantities from the same underlying theory, connecting its strongly coupled regime to the observed spectrum.

The convincing evidence is the quantitative consistency across these probes, with a common color group and coupling evolution. A jet by itself is evidence for a hard parton; its rates and correlations supply the more specific tests of [Quantum chromodynamics](../../../../quantum-chromodynamics.md).[Asymptotic Freedom: From Paradox to Paradigm by Frank Wilczek](../../../../asymptotic-freedom-from-paradox-to-paradigm-by-frank-wilczek.md)
