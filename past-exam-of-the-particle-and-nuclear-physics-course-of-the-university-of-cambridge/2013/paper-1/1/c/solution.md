<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For a pure sample, $N=(1000/239)N_A=2.520\times10^{24}$ [nuclei](../../../../../atomic-nucleus-split.md). [radioactive activity](../../../../../radioactive-activity.md) is $\mathcal A=\lambda N$ with $\lambda=\ln2/t_{1/2}$. Using a year of 365.25 days,

$$
t_{1/2}=7.612\times10^{11}\,\mathrm s,\qquad
\boxed{\mathcal A_0=2.29\times10^{12}\,\mathrm{Bq}\simeq2.3\,\mathrm{TBq}}.
$$

The [mass number](../../../../../mass-number.md) supplies the approximate [molar mass](../../../../../molar-mass.md) in grams per mole. This is initial parent activity; no daughter activity or [isotope](../../../../../isotope.md) mixture has been assumed.
