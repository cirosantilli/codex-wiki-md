<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

[parity](../../../../../parity.md) reverses spatial coordinates and [polar vectors](../../../../../polar-vector.md) such as [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md), while leaving [axial vectors](../../../../../pseudovector.md) such as [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) unchanged. The correlation $\mathbf J\cdot\mathbf p_e$ is therefore [parity](../../../../../parity.md) odd. For polarized beta-emitting [nuclei](../../../../../atomic-nucleus-split.md) one can measure

$$
W(\theta)\propto1+A\mathcal P\frac vc\cos\theta,
$$

where $\theta$ is measured from [nuclear polarization](../../../../../nuclear-polarization.md), $\mathcal P$ its magnitude and $A$ a decay-dependent coefficient. A nonzero [spin](../../../../../spin.md)–[momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) asymmetry is incompatible with [parity](../../../../../parity.md) invariance.

The [cobalt-60](../../../../../cobalt-60.md) experiment polarized [nuclei](../../../../../atomic-nucleus-split.md) at low temperature and compared beta emission along and opposite the polarization axis. Preferential emission on one side established weak-interaction [parity violation](../../../../../parity-violation.md). Reversing polarization tests whether the asymmetry follows nuclear orientation; warming the sample removes polarization, while gamma anisotropy monitors its loss. Detector acceptance, backgrounds and magnetic deflection of charged particles must be separated from a true emission asymmetry.[1957, polarized cobalt-60 experiment and temperature/polarization checks](../../../../../experimental-test-of-parity-conservation-in-beta-decay-by-chien-shiung-wu-and-others.md)

In the modern [weak charged current](../../../../../charged-current.md), the [V-A interaction](../../../../../v-a-interaction.md) couples to left-chiral [fermion](../../../../../fermion.md) fields. In the massless limit this selects left-helicity [neutrinos](../../../../../neutrino.md) and right-helicity [antineutrinos](../../../../../antineutrino.md). Massive charged [leptons](../../../../../lepton.md) need not be pure-helicity states, consistent with the factor $v/c$. Strong and [electromagnetic interactions](../../../../../electromagnetic-interaction.md) conserve [parity](../../../../../parity.md) to the usual experimental approximation; [parity violation](../../../../../parity-violation.md) alone should not be confused with [CP violation](../../../../../cp-violation.md) or a failure of CPT.
