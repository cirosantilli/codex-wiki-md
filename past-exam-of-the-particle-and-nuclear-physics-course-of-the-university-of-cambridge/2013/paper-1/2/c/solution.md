The electroweak [gauge group](../../../../../gauge-group.md) is $SU(2)_L\times U(1)_Y$. Left-handed [fermions](../../../../../fermion.md) form weak-isospin doublets and right-handed charged [fermions](../../../../../fermion.md) are singlets under $SU(2)_L$. Before [symmetry](../../../../../symmetry-physics.md) breaking the [gauge fields](../../../../../gauge-field.md) are $W^1,W^2,W^3$ and $B$, with couplings $g,g'$. A Higgs-doublet [vacuum expectation value](../../../../../vacuum-expectation-value.md) breaks the [symmetry](../../../../../symmetry-physics.md) to electromagnetic $U(1)$.

In units $\hbar=c=1$, the physical combinations and tree-level relations are

$$
W^\pm=\frac{W^1\mp iW^2}{\sqrt2},\quad
A=B\cos\theta_W+W^3\sin\theta_W,\quad
Z=-B\sin\theta_W+W^3\cos\theta_W,
$$



$$
e=g\sin\theta_W=g'\cos\theta_W,\qquad
M_W=gv/2,\qquad M_Z=\frac v2\sqrt{g^2+g'^2},\qquad
\boxed{M_W=M_Z\cos\theta_W}.
$$

The [photon](../../../../../photon.md) remains massless. [charged currents](../../../../../charged-current.md) change members of weak doublets, whereas the $Z$ mediates [weak neutral currents](../../../../../neutral-current.md) with parity-dependent couplings. At low energies $W$ exchange reduces to the Fermi contact interaction, $G_F/\sqrt2=g^2/(8M_W^2)$; the apparent weak suppression reflects the massive mediator. This unifies the gauge description, not the numerical equality of all low-energy couplings.[1967, gauge structure, symmetry breaking and vector-boson mass relations](../../../../../a-model-of-leptons-by-steven-weinberg.md)

Neutral-current [neutrino](../../../../../neutrino.md) interactions and subsequent observation of massive weak vector bosons tested this structure. In a neutral-current [neutrino](../../../../../neutrino.md) experiment, an isolated hadronic interaction without an accompanying charged [lepton](../../../../../lepton.md) is a candidate signal; entering neutral hadrons and missed charged-current [leptons](../../../../../lepton.md) are important backgrounds. Gargamelle's neutral-current evidence supplied a direct experimental check beyond ordinary [beta decay](../../../../../beta-decay.md).[1973, event topology and background analysis](../../../../../observation-of-neutrino-like-interactions-without-muon-or-electron-in-the-gargamelle-neutrino-experiment-by-f-j-hasert.md)
