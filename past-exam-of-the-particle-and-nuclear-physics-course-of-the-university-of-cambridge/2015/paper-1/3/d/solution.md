<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The inequality $m_H<2m_W$ forbids two on-shell W [bosons](../../../../../boson.md) in a two-body final state. It does not forbid the four-fermion states reached through a virtual W propagator. The physical channel is

$$
\boxed{H\longrightarrow WW^*\longrightarrow4\ \text{fermions},}
$$

with at least one W off shell. The virtual particle need not satisfy $p^2=m_W^2$; the final stable particles still obey total four-momentum conservation. The W propagator and reduced [phase space](../../../../../phase-space.md) suppress the rate relative to an above-threshold two-on-shell-W decay, but do not make it zero. The label $H\to WW$ commonly includes this off-shell contribution to [Higgs decay to two W bosons](../../../../../higgs-decay-to-two-w-bosons.md).
