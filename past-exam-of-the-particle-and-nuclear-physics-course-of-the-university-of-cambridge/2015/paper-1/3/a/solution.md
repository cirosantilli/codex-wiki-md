<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

At leading order the [Higgs decay to a fermion pair](../../../../../higgs-decay-to-a-fermion-pair.md) has

$$
\Gamma_f=N_c^f\frac{m_f^2m_H}{8\pi v^2}
\left(1-\frac{4m_f^2}{m_H^2}\right)^{3/2}.
$$

Each [quark](../../../../../quark.md) contributes three distinguishable colour states, whereas a lepton has one. Neglecting the small phase-space factors for these masses, radiative corrections and the scale dependence of [quark](../../../../../quark.md) masses, the requested ratio is

$$
\boxed{\Gamma_\tau:\Gamma_c:\Gamma_b
=m_\tau^2:3m_c^2:3m_b^2
=3.24:6.75:75
=1:2.083:23.15.}
$$

The numerical masses here are those printed with question 3; the tau and [electron](../../../../../electron.md) approximations deliberately differ from those in question 1. Omitting the [quark](../../../../../quark.md) colour multiplicity would give the wrong relative rates.
