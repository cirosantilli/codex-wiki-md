<h1 id="4/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

[Beta decay](../../../../../beta-decay.md) converts a [neutron](../../../../../neutron-split.md) to a [proton](../../../../../proton.md) or vice versa through the [weak interaction](../../../../../weak-interaction.md), leaving the [mass](../../../../../mass.md) number fixed:

$$
n\to p+e^-+\bar\nu_e,\qquad
p\to n+e^++\nu_e.
$$

Inside a nucleus the full initial and final nuclear masses determine whether either process is possible. [Electron capture](../../../../../electron-capture.md), $p+e^-\to n+\nu_e$, is the related bound-electron process. The emitted charged-lepton spectrum is continuous because [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) is shared with the [neutrino](../../../../../neutrino.md) and nuclear recoil.

For [allowed beta decay](../../../../../allowed-beta-decay.md), the lepton [wavefunctions](../../../../../wave-function-split.md)' leading spatial terms are constant over the nucleus. A [Fermi transition](../../../../../fermi-transition.md) has $\Delta J=0$ and no [parity](../../../../../parity.md) change. A [Gamow-Teller transition](../../../../../gamow-teller-transition.md) has $\Delta J=0,1$ and no [parity](../../../../../parity.md) change, excluding $0\to0$. Thus a same-parity transition with nonzero initial and final spins and $\Delta J=0$ can contain both contributions. First-forbidden transitions change [parity](../../../../../parity.md) and permit $|\Delta J|=0,1,2$; the $|\Delta J|=2$ case is [unique forbidden beta decay](../../../../../unique-forbidden-beta-decay.md). Higher forbiddenness requires higher powers of the small lepton [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) times nuclear size. “Forbidden” denotes suppression of the leading allowed operator, not an impossible decay.[Physical PDF page 3, General Policies — Theory, Beta Transitions](../../../../../nuclear-data-sheets-general-policies-by-national-nuclear-data-center.md)

[Fermi's golden rule](../../../../../fermi-s-golden-rule.md) expresses a rate as a squared nuclear [matrix element](../../../../../matrix-element.md) times leptonic [phase space](../../../../../phase-space.md). Neglecting charged-lepton [mass](../../../../../mass.md) and Coulomb distortion, a constant allowed [matrix element](../../../../../matrix-element.md) gives

$$
\lambda\propto |M|^2\int_0^Q E^2(Q-E)^2dE
=\frac{|M|^2Q^5}{30}.
$$

This is the approximate [Sargent rule](../../../../../sargent-rule.md), $\lambda\propto Q^5$ for comparable allowed strengths; it is not an exact law across arbitrary transitions. Finite [electron](../../../../../electron.md) [mass](../../../../../mass.md), the [Fermi function](../../../../../fermi-function.md), forbidden shape factors and nuclear structure change the comparison. A microscopic first-forbidden unique operator introduces additional [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) factors.

Using bare nuclear masses $M_i,M_f$, the kinetic-energy release is

$$
Q_{\beta^-}=(M_i-M_f-m_e)c^2,
\qquad Q_{\beta^+}=(M_i-M_f-m_e)c^2.
$$

The daughter is different in the two cases: its charge is $Z+1$ for beta minus and $Z-1$ for beta plus. The relevant [mass](../../../../../mass.md) difference must exceed one [electron](../../../../../electron.md) [mass](../../../../../mass.md). For neutral atomic masses, to leading order in small electronic binding corrections, the corresponding expressions are $Q_{\beta^-}=(M_i^{\rm at}-M_f^{\rm at})c^2$ and $Q_{\beta^+}=(M_i^{\rm at}-M_f^{\rm at}-2m_e)c^2$. [Electron capture](../../../../../electron-capture.md) is energetically possible approximately when $M_i^{\rm at}>M_f^{\rm at}$, with available shell binding [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) and daughter excitation included. Nuclear masses and atomic masses must not be mixed.
