<h1 id="4/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

In [neutron-proton radiative capture](../../../../../neutron-proton-radiative-capture.md), the initial pair at rest has total [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) $Mc^2$, where $M=m_p+m_n$. The emitted [photon](../../../../../photon.md) carries [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md), requiring equal and opposite [nuclear recoil](../../../../../nuclear-recoil.md). Some [binding energy](../../../../../binding-energy.md) becomes [deuteron](../../../../../deuteron.md) [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md).

Exact two-body kinematics in [natural units](../../../../../natural-units.md) gives

$$
M=E_\gamma+\sqrt{m_d^2+E_\gamma^2},\qquad E_\gamma=\frac{M^2-m_d^2}{2M}.
$$

Since $m_d=M-B$,

$$
E_\gamma=B-\frac{B^2}{2M},\qquad \boxed{B-E_\gamma=\frac{B^2}{2M}.}
$$

With the stated rounded [masses](../../../../../mass.md),

$$
B-E_\gamma=\frac{(2.224)^2}{2(938.272+939.565)}\,\mathrm{MeV}
=0.00131699\,\mathrm{MeV}
=\boxed{1.317\,\mathrm{keV}}.
$$

The [photon](../../../../../photon.md) [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) is $2.222683\,\mathrm{MeV}$. At leading order the difference equals the nonrelativistic recoil estimate $p_\gamma^2/(2m_d)\simeq B^2/(2m_d)$; exact kinematics fixes the small distinction between using $m_d$ and $M$ in that approximation.
