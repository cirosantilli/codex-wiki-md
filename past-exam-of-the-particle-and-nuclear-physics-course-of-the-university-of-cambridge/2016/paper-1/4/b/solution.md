<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [nuclear binding energy](../../../../../nuclear-binding-energy.md) is the separated-nucleon [rest energy](../../../../../rest-energy.md) minus the bound-state [rest energy](../../../../../rest-energy.md). Consistent nuclear [masses](../../../../../mass.md) avoid atomic-electron bookkeeping:

$$
B=(m_p+m_n-m_d)c^2
=(938.272+939.565-1875.613)\,\mathrm{MeV}
=\boxed{2.224\,\mathrm{MeV}.}
$$

It is positive because the bound [deuteron](../../../../../deuteron.md) has lower total [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md).
