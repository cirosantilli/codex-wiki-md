<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [nuclear Fermi gas model](../../../../../nuclear-fermi-gas-model.md) treats [protons](../../../../../proton.md) and [neutrons](../../../../../neutron-split.md) as two species of [spin one-half](../../../../../spin-one-half.md) [fermions](../../../../../fermion.md) moving approximately independently in a confining [nuclear mean field](../../../../../nuclear-mean-field.md). At a [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) small compared with their [Fermi temperature](../../../../../fermi-temperature.md), the [Pauli exclusion principle](../../../../../pauli-exclusion-principle.md) fills the lowest available [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) states. It does not allow all [nucleons](../../../../../nucleon.md) to sit at zero [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md).

For species $i=p,n$, two [spin](../../../../../spin.md) states occupy each momentum-space cell $(2\pi\hbar)^3/V$. Counting a filled sphere gives

$$
N_i=\frac{2V}{(2\pi\hbar)^3}\frac{4\pi p_{F,i}^3}{3},\qquad
p_{F,i}=\hbar(3\pi^2 n_i)^{1/3},\qquad E_{F,i}=\frac{p_{F,i}^2}{2m_N},
$$

where $n_i=N_i/V$. The [Fermi energy](../../../../../fermi-energy.md) is measured from the mean-field well bottom; it need not equal the [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) needed to remove a [nucleon](../../../../../nucleon.md) into vacuum. Occupied-state averaging gives

$$
E_{\mathrm{kin}}=\frac35(NE_{F,n}+ZE_{F,p}),\qquad P_i=\frac25 n_iE_{F,i}.
$$

Even at zero [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) there is [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) and [degeneracy pressure](../../../../../degeneracy-pressure.md). As an explicitly illustrative input, total density $n=0.16\,\mathrm{fm}^{-3}$ and $N=Z$ give $p_Fc\simeq263\,\mathrm{MeV}$ and $E_F\simeq37\,\mathrm{MeV}$. These numbers illustrate the formula; they are not additional data supplied by the paper.

Separate [proton](../../../../../proton.md) and [neutron](../../../../../neutron-split.md) seas explain a kinetic contribution to [nuclear symmetry energy](../../../../../symmetry-energy.md). At fixed $n$, put $\delta=(N-Z)/A$ and define $E_F$ at $n_p=n_n=n/2$. Then

$$
\frac{E_{\mathrm{kin}}}{A}
=\frac35E_F\frac{(1+\delta)^{5/3}+(1-\delta)^{5/3}}2
=\frac35E_F+\frac13E_F\delta^2+O(\delta^4).
$$

Increasing one species' population raises its [Fermi surface](../../../../../fermi-surface.md) and costs [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md), favoring similar populations before electrostatic and interaction terms are included.

[pauli blocking](../../../../../pauli-blocking.md) suppresses collisions deep in a filled sea because scattering needs unoccupied final states. An energetic external probe can knock a [nucleon](../../../../../nucleon.md) into an empty state and reveal its initial [Fermi motion](../../../../../fermi-motion.md), making the model useful for broad inclusive scattering distributions. A finite surface can be approximated using a position-dependent [nuclear mean field](../../../../../nuclear-mean-field.md).

A free gas cannot produce nuclear binding or saturation: those require an attractive interaction and its density dependence. The smooth gas picture also omits discrete [nuclear shells](../../../../../nuclear-shell.md), [nuclear spin-orbit splitting](../../../../../nuclear-spin-orbit-splitting.md), [nuclear pairing](../../../../../nuclear-pairing.md), short-distance correlations and deformation. It cannot assign all ground-state [spins](../../../../../spin.md) from a [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) sphere. **Its useful prediction is the scale and occupancy of [nucleon](../../../../../nucleon.md) motion, with a surrounding mean field supplied separately.**
