The [Standard Model](../../../../../standard-model-split.md) is a [gauge theory](../../../../../gauge-theory.md) with group $SU(3)_c\times SU(2)_L\times U(1)_Y$, matter [quarks](../../../../../quark.md) and [leptons](../../../../../lepton.md), gauge bosons and a [Higgs field](../../../../../higgs-field.md). It combines [Quantum chromodynamics](../../../../../quantum-chromodynamics.md) with the [electroweak interaction](../../../../../electroweak-interaction.md). Its successes do not make its field content a complete account of nature. Established missing phenomena should be distinguished from theoretical questions about its parameters.

[neutrino oscillation](../../../../../neutrino-oscillation.md) demonstrates nonzero mass-squared differences and mixing of flavor states. The minimal renormalizable [Standard Model](../../../../../standard-model-split.md) has no [right-handed neutrino](../../../../../right-handed-neutrino.md) and no [neutrino mass](../../../../../neutrino-mass.md) term. The atmospheric experiment compared zenith-angle distributions of muon-like and electron-like events in a large water [Cherenkov detector](../../../../../cherenkov-detector.md). A direction-dependent muon-neutrino deficit, with different path lengths through Earth and comparison samples, discriminated against a uniform flux-normalization error.[Abstract and zenith-angle distributions, Physical Review Letters 81, pp. 1562–1567 (1998)](../../../../../evidence-for-oscillation-of-atmospheric-neutrinos-by-y-fukuda-and-others.md) One extension adds [right-handed neutrinos](../../../../../right-handed-neutrino.md) and small Dirac Yukawa couplings; another has a [Majorana mass term](../../../../../majorana-mass-term.md) and [seesaw mechanism](../../../../../seesaw-mechanism.md), with light eigenvalue of order $m_D^2/M$. Oscillations alone do not decide whether [neutrinos](../../../../../neutrino.md) are [Majorana neutrinos](../../../../../majorana-neutrino.md).

[dark matter](../../../../../dark-matter.md) is inferred from gravitational observations such as galactic motions and [gravitational lensing](../../../../../gravitational-lensing-split.md); [Standard Model](../../../../../standard-model-split.md) particles do not supply the required dominant cold component. Active [neutrinos](../../../../../neutrino.md) contribute some matter but cannot constitute all the cold [dark matter](../../../../../dark-matter.md). A new stable weakly interacting particle, an [axion](../../../../../axion.md), or other hidden-sector states are possible explanations, with different scattering, production and astrophysical signals. **Dark-matter evidence does not identify a particular new particle.** The institutional account distinguishes gravitational evidence from proposed particle candidates.[Gravitational evidence and candidate-particle discussion](../../../../../dark-matter-by-cern.md)

The matter-antimatter imbalance poses a different problem. Dynamical [baryogenesis](../../../../../baryogenesis.md) needs [Sakharov conditions](../../../../../sakharov-conditions.md), including baryon-number violation, [CP violation](../../../../../cp-violation.md) and departure from [thermal equilibrium](../../../../../thermal-equilibrium-split.md). Although the [Standard Model](../../../../../standard-model-split.md) contains relevant ingredients in principle, they do not generate the observed asymmetry in its conventional thermal history with measured parameters. Additional CP phases, a stronger electroweak transition, or [leptogenesis](../../../../../leptogenesis.md) from heavy [Majorana neutrinos](../../../../../majorana-neutrino.md) are possible extensions. Each needs a quantitative mechanism.

[gravity](../../../../../gravity.md) is absent as a dynamical quantum interaction, motivating [quantum gravity](../../../../../quantum-gravity.md) and unification. The [hierarchy problem](../../../../../hierarchy-problem.md) asks why the Higgs [mass](../../../../../mass.md) scale is small compared with much higher scales when scalar [masses](../../../../../mass.md) receive sensitive quantum corrections. [supersymmetry](../../../../../supersymmetry-split.md) can cancel particular contributions between partner fields if its breaking pattern is suitable; compositeness gives another interpretation of the Higgs sector. Neither motivation proves that the corresponding new particles exist. The [Strong CP problem](../../../../../strong-cp-problem.md) asks why the allowed [QCD](../../../../../quantum-chromodynamics.md) CP-violating parameter is so small; an [axion](../../../../../axion.md) mechanism is one possible answer.

A [grand unified theory](../../../../../grand-unified-theory.md) embeds the separate gauge factors in a larger group and can relate couplings or [fermion](../../../../../fermion.md) representations, sometimes predicting [proton decay](../../../../../proton-decay.md). Its tests differ from dark-matter or neutrino-mass searches. **A useful extension must explain a specified deficiency, preserve successful low-energy predictions and supply distinguishable experimental consequences.** Attractive new symmetries alone are not evidence.
