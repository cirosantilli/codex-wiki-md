↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [muonic atom](../../../../../muonic-atom.md) binds a negative [muon](../../../../../muon.md) in the nuclear [Coulomb potential](../../../../../coulomb-potential-energy.md). The [muon](../../../../../muon.md) is a [spin one-half](../../../../../spin-one-half.md) [lepton](../../../../../lepton.md) with the [electron](../../../../../electron.md)'s charge but about 207 times its [mass](../../../../../mass.md). For a point [nucleus](../../../../../atomic-nucleus-split.md) of [mass](../../../../../mass.md) $M_N$ and charge $Ze$, the [reduced mass](../../../../../reduced-mass.md) and nonrelativistic hydrogenic scales, with [fine-structure constant](../../../../../fine-structure-constant.md) $\alpha$, are

$$
\mu_r=\frac{m_\mu M_N}{m_\mu+M_N},\qquad
a_\mu=\frac{\hbar}{Z\alpha\mu_r c},\qquad
E_n=-\frac{\mu_r c^2(Z\alpha)^2}{2n^2}.
$$

For a heavy [nucleus](../../../../../atomic-nucleus-split.md) $\mu_r\simeq m_\mu$, giving a radius roughly $1/207$ of the corresponding electronic radius and transition [energies](../../../../../energy-split.md) roughly 207 times larger. For hydrogen, [nuclear recoil](../../../../../nuclear-recoil.md) matters: $m_\mu\simeq106\,\mathrm{MeV}/c^2$ gives $\mu_r\simeq95\,\mathrm{MeV}/c^2$ and $a_\mu\simeq284\,\mathrm{fm}$ rather than the infinite-nuclear-mass value about $255\,\mathrm{fm}$.

A slowed negative [muon](../../../../../muon.md) is captured into an excited orbit and undergoes a [radiative cascade](../../../../../radiative-cascade.md), ejecting atomic [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) and emitting muonic X-rays. Inner orbits penetrate the [nuclear charge distribution](../../../../../nuclear-charge-distribution.md), particularly at large $Z$. Replacing a point charge by an extended charge makes the central attraction less singular and shifts states with substantial nuclear overlap. Measured transition [energies](../../../../../energy-split.md) consequently constrain the [nuclear charge radius](../../../../../nuclear-charge-radius.md). Sensitivity is particularly strong for $S$ states, whose contact density scales as $(Z\alpha\mu_r)^3$.

An instructive experiment is [Randolf Pohl](../../../../../randolf-pohl.md)'s team's muonic-hydrogen spectroscopy. A stopped-muon target prepared a metastable $2S$ population; a tunable pulsed laser drove a $2S\to2P$ transition, followed by a detectable $2P\to1S$ X-ray. Scanning laser frequency located a resonance whose interpretation included the [proton](../../../../../proton.md) finite-size contribution to the [Lamb shift](../../../../../lamb-shift.md). Delayed detection selected the long-lived population against prompt cascade backgrounds. Laser-off or detuned measurements provide a background control; [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) and time selection alone would not establish a laser-induced resonance.[Nature 466, pp. 213–216, Fig. 1 experimental principle and Figs. 4–5 time spectra and resonance](../../../../../the-size-of-the-proton-by-randolf-pohl-and-others.md)

Original calculated hydrogenic radial probabilities, plotted against physical radius with probability measured per logarithmic interval. The Bohr radii are stated alongside; these are point-nucleus model curves, not measured nuclear densities.

Extracting a radius requires [quantum electrodynamics](../../../../../quantum-electrodynamics.md), including [vacuum polarization](../../../../../vacuum-polarization.md), recoil and nuclear-structure corrections. A free [muon](../../../../../muon.md) decays on a microsecond timescale; a bound negative [muon](../../../../../muon.md) can instead undergo [muon capture](../../../../../muon-capture.md), for example $\mu^-p\to n\nu_\mu$. These processes limit observation time. In heavy atoms, relativistic motion and nuclear overlap make the simple Bohr formulas only qualitative. **Muonic spectroscopy converts a smaller orbital scale into enhanced sensitivity to nuclear charge structure**, provided atomic and nuclear corrections are controlled.
