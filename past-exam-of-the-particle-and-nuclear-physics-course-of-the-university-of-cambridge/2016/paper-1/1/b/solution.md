<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use [natural units](../../../../../natural-units.md) and write the common light [constituent quark mass](../../../../../constituent-quark-mass.md) as $m$, and the strange one as $M$. For two [spin one-half](../../../../../spin-one-half.md) constituents,

$$
\mathbf S_1\cdot\mathbf S_2=\frac12\left[S(S+1)-\frac32\right]
=\begin{cases}-3/4,&S=0,\\+1/4,&S=1.\end{cases}
$$

The relative hyperfine contributions are $-3$ and $+1$. Absorb their normalization and spatial overlap into pair-dependent parameters $h_{\ell\ell},h_{\ell s},h_{ss}$. The [meson hyperfine mass splitting](../../../../../meson-hyperfine-mass-splitting.md) model gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
m_\pi&=2m-3h_{\ell\ell},&m_\rho&=2m+h_{\ell\ell},\\
m_K&=m+M-3h_{\ell s},&m_{K^*}&=m+M+h_{\ell s},\\
m_\phi&=2M+h_{ss}.
\end{aligned}
$$

A common color-magnetic coefficient gives $h_{ij}\propto1/(m_i m_j)$, but its numerical value is not needed.

In the specified [Eta octet state](../../../../../eta-octet-state.md) used for the [eta meson](../../../../../eta-meson.md) the light-flavor probability is $1/3$ and the strange-flavor probability $2/3$. Assuming a flavor-diagonal [mass](../../../../../mass.md) operator,

$$
m_\eta=\frac13(2m-3h_{\ell\ell})+\frac23(2M-3h_{ss})
=\frac23m+\frac43M-h_{\ell\ell}-2h_{ss}.
$$

Inserting the five [mass](../../../../../mass.md) expressions into the proposed combination makes the coefficient of $h_{\ell s}$ equal to $4-4=0$. Those of $m,M,h_{\ell\ell},h_{ss}$ are $2/3,4/3,-1,-2$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{m_\eta=-\frac13m_{\pi^0}-2m_{\rho^0}+4m_{K^{*0}}+\frac43m_{K^0}-2m_\phi.}
$$

This is an identity within the assumed [mass](../../../../../mass.md) operator and octet flavor assignment. It is not an exact relation among measured [masses](../../../../../mass.md): [eta mixing](../../../../../eta-mixing.md) and additional spin-independent binding effects are outside those assumptions.
