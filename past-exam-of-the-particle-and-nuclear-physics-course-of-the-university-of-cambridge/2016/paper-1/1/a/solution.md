<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In the [nuclear shell model](../../../../../nuclear-shell-model.md), a level with total [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) $j$ has $2j+1$ substates for each [nucleon](../../../../../nucleon.md) species. The first three levels accommodate $2+4+2=8$ [protons](../../../../../proton.md) or eight [neutrons](../../../../../neutron-split.md). [nuclear pairing](../../../../../nuclear-pairing.md) makes a filled shell or a pair of like [nucleons](../../../../../nucleon.md) contribute $J=0$, and an orbital has spatial [parity](../../../../../parity.md) $(-1)^\ell$.

For [oxygen-17](../../../../../oxygen-17.md), eight [protons](../../../../../proton.md) and eight [neutrons](../../../../../neutron-split.md) form a closed core; the remaining [neutron](../../../../../neutron-split.md) occupies $1d_{5/2}$, so **$J^P=5/2^+$**. For [nitrogen-15](../../../../../nitrogen-15.md), eight [neutrons](../../../../../neutron-split.md) close their shell, while the seventh [proton](../../../../../proton.md) occupies $1p_{1/2}$. Equivalently it is a [proton hole](../../../../../proton-hole.md) relative to the closed eight-proton shell, giving **$J^P=1/2^-$**. [beryllium-10](../../../../../beryllium-10.md) is an [even-even nucleus](../../../../../even-even-nucleus.md); pairing its four [protons](../../../../../proton.md) and six [neutrons](../../../../../neutron-split.md) gives **$J^P=0^+$**.

[boron-10](../../../../../boron-10.md) needs an additional qualification. The fifth [proton](../../../../../proton.md) and fifth [neutron](../../../../../neutron-split.md) each occupy $1p_{3/2}$. [addition of angular momentum](../../../../../addition-of-angular-momentum.md) permits

$$
J=0,1,2,3,\qquad P=(-1)^{1+1}=+1.
$$

The experimentally correct ground-state assignment is **$3^+$**, corresponding to aligned unpaired $j=3/2$ contributions in this simple description. However, one-particle level ordering alone does not select it over the other couplings: their splitting requires the [nuclear residual interaction](../../../../../nuclear-residual-interaction.md). Assuming a preference for the aligned proton-neutron coupling gives the intended prediction; without that assumption, the strictly deducible result is the allowed positive-parity multiplet. The evaluated [ground state](../../../../../ground-state.md) is $3^+,T=0$, with a $1^+,T=0$ level above it.[Table 10.18, ground state and first excited state, physical page 1](../../../../../energy-levels-of-boron-10-by-tunl-2004.md)
