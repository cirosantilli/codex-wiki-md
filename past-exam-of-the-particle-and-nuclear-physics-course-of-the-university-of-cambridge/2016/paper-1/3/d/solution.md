<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Use an [inclusive spectator model](../../../../../inclusive-spectator-model.md): one heavy constituent decays through the [Fermi interaction](../../../../../fermi-interaction.md), and sum the open charged-current channels. In [weak-decay channel counting](../../../../../weak-decay-channel-counting.md), each massless final [lepton](../../../../../lepton.md) pair gives basic width

$$
\Gamma_0(m)=\frac{G_F^2m^5}{192\pi^3},\qquad
\tau=\frac{\hbar}{C\Gamma_0(m)}.
$$

A [quark](../../../../../quark.md) pair contributes three colors multiplied by its squared [Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa matrix](../../../../../cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md) element. Do not multiply by three just because the initial [quark](../../../../../quark.md) has three colors: its lifetime is the width of one initial state, averaged over initial color when appropriate. Define

$$
S_u=|V_{ud}|^2+|V_{us}|^2=0.9893,\qquad
S_c=|V_{cd}|^2+|V_{cs}|^2=0.9893.
$$

For the [tau lepton](../../../../../tau-particle.md), two leptonic channels contain an [electron](../../../../../electron.md) or a [muon](../../../../../muon.md); light hadronic channels have $d\bar u$ or $s\bar u$. Therefore

$$
C_\tau=2+3S_u=4.9679,\qquad
\Gamma_\tau=2.0103\times10^{-12}\,\mathrm{GeV},\qquad
\boxed{\tau_\tau\simeq3.27\times10^{-13}\,\mathrm s.}
$$

No charmed [hadron](../../../../../hadron.md) can be produced: the lightest charmed [meson](../../../../../meson.md) exceeds the tau [mass](../../../../../mass.md). [D-zero and D-plus listings, physical page 1 of each, masses 1864.84 and 1869.66 MeV](../../../../../review-of-particle-physics-by-particle-data-group-2022.md) The supplied charm-quark parameter is not a free asymptotic final-particle [mass](../../../../../mass.md). The leading count neglects [electron](../../../../../electron.md) and [muon](../../../../../muon.md) [masses](../../../../../mass.md) and light hadronic threshold effects.

For the [charm quark](../../../../../charm-quark.md), $c\to sW^{+*}$ and $c\to dW^{+*}$ dominate. The emitted current has two light leptonic channels and the same light hadronic channels as above; a tau cannot be produced:

$$
C_c=S_c(2+3S_u)=4.91474347,\qquad
\Gamma_c=8.5232\times10^{-13}\,\mathrm{GeV},\qquad
\boxed{\tau_c^{\mathrm{spectator}}\simeq7.72\times10^{-13}\,\mathrm s.}
$$

For the [bottom quark](../../../../../bottom-quark.md), retain $b\to cW^{-*}$ with the supplied $V_{cb}$ and neglect the much smaller $b\to u$ channel. The emitted current can produce three [lepton](../../../../../lepton.md) flavors and two up-type [quark](../../../../../quark.md) families, with three colors for each family. In particular $b\to c\bar cs$ is open at the given [masses](../../../../../mass.md), although suppressed by [phase space](../../../../../phase-space.md). Counting each open channel as massless gives

$$
C_b=|V_{cb}|^2[3+3S_u+3S_c]=0.01429728,
$$



$$
\Gamma_b=1.0204\times10^{-12}\,\mathrm{GeV},\qquad
\boxed{\tau_b^{\mathrm{spectator}}\simeq6.45\times10^{-13}\,\mathrm s.}
$$

Setting Cabibbo sums to one recovers the familiar counts $5$, $5$ and $9|V_{cb}|^2$. Despite its larger [mass](../../../../../mass.md), the [bottom quark](../../../../../bottom-quark.md) is slowed by its small interfamily mixing factor.

These are leading estimates rather than precise lifetimes of free [quarks](../../../../../quark.md). [confinement](../../../../../confinement.md) makes charm and bottom observable in [hadrons](../../../../../hadron.md), whose lifetimes involve spectator interactions, [Quantum chromodynamics](../../../../../quantum-chromodynamics.md) corrections and bound-state effects. [mass](../../../../../mass.md) corrections are significant for bottom: even the single-charm channel with massless [leptons](../../../../../lepton.md) has the [three-body weak-decay mass correction](../../../../../three-body-weak-decay-mass-correction.md)

$$
f(r)=1-8r+8r^3-r^4-12r^2\ln r,\qquad
r=(m_c/m_b)^2=0.09,\qquad f(r)\simeq0.520.
$$

Tau and double-charm channels have different suppressions, so multiplying the entire width by this one factor is inconsistent. Accurate [hadron](../../../../../hadron.md) lifetimes require those channel-dependent factors and hadronic dynamics; the exercise supports the explicitly stated leading counts.
