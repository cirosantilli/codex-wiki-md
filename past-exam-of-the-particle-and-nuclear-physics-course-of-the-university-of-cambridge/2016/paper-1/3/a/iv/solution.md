**The dominant interaction is weak.** In panel (iv),

$$
\tau^-\longrightarrow\nu_\tau+W^{-*},\qquad
W^{-*}\longrightarrow d\bar u\longrightarrow\pi^-.
$$

The [quark](../../../../../../quark.md) vertex carries the [Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa matrix](../../../../../../cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md) factor $V_{ud}$. The [down quark](../../../../../../down-quark.md) and anti-up [quark](../../../../../../quark.md) form a [color singlet](../../../../../../colour-singlet.md) [pion](../../../../../../pion.md). The [pion](../../../../../../pion.md) bracket represents its bound-state [matrix element](../../../../../../matrix-element.md), conventionally specified by the [pion decay constant](../../../../../../pion-decay-constant.md); it is not an additional weak vertex. The [neutrino](../../../../../../neutrino.md) carries the initial [tau lepton](../../../../../../tau-particle.md) number.
