**This is a weak charged-current decay.** Panel (ii) has one $W^+e^+\nu_e$ vertex. The outgoing [Positron](../../../../../../positron.md) and [electron neutrino](../../../../../../electron-neutrino.md) have total charge $+1$ and cancelling [lepton numbers](../../../../../../lepton-number.md). The [W boson](../../../../../../w-boson.md) is on shell here; a second virtual boson is unnecessary.
