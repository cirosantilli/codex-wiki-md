↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**The dominant interaction is strong.** At [hadron](../../../../../../hadron.md) level, panel (iii) is an [effective hadronic vertex](../../../../../../effective-hadronic-vertex.md) for $\Delta p\pi$. The initial $uuu$ constituents rearrange into $uud$ and $u\bar d$ after creation of a $d\bar d$ pair:

$$
uuu\longrightarrow (uud)+(u\bar d).
$$

One original [up quark](../../../../../../up-quark.md) enters the charged [pion](../../../../../../pion.md); the other two join the new [down quark](../../../../../../down-quark.md) in the [proton](../../../../../../proton.md). Gluon-induced pair creation supplies the [quark-flow diagram](../../../../../../quark-flow-diagram.md) interpretation. This is nonperturbative [hadronization](../../../../../../hadronization.md): the [effective hadronic vertex](../../../../../../effective-hadronic-vertex.md) does not claim that one elementary perturbative [quark](../../../../../../quark.md) graph calculates its width. Charge, [baryon number](../../../../../../baryon-number.md) and net [quark](../../../../../../quark.md) flavor are conserved.
