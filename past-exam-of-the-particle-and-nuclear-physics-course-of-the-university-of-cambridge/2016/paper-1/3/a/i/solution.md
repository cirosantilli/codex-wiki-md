↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**The dominant interaction is electromagnetic.** The [color singlet](../../../../../../colour-singlet.md) charm-anticharm component of the [J/psi meson](../../../../../../j-psi-meson.md) annihilates through a virtual [photon](../../../../../../photon.md), which creates a [muon](../../../../../../muon.md) and an [antimuon](../../../../../../antimuon.md):

$$
c\bar c\longrightarrow\gamma^*\longrightarrow\mu^-\mu^+.
$$

Panel (i) shows the two electromagnetic vertices. Vector quantum numbers allow the one-photon channel. A [Z boson](../../../../../../z-boson.md) contribution is negligible at this [mass](../../../../../../mass.md) scale; a single [gluon](../../../../../../gluon.md) cannot connect a [color singlet](../../../../../../colour-singlet.md) initial state to colorless [leptons](../../../../../../lepton.md). The dominant interaction for this specified final state need not dominate the [meson](../../../../../../meson.md)'s total [decay width](../../../../../../decay-width.md).
