<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The negative [muon](../../../../../muon.md) emits a virtual $W^-$ and becomes a [muon neutrino](../../../../../muon-neutrino.md); the boson creates an [electron](../../../../../electron.md) and an [electron antineutrino](../../../../../electron-antineutrino.md):

$$
\mu^-\to\nu_\mu+W^{-*}\to\nu_\mu+e^-+\bar\nu_e.
$$

Both vertices are [weak charged currents](../../../../../charged-current.md).

<a id="3/c/image-muon-decay-through-a-virtual-charged-weak-boson"></a>
![](../../../../../past-exam-of-the-particle-and-nuclear-physics-course-of-the-university-of-cambridge/2016/paper-1-muon-decay.png)

**[Figure 3](#3/c/image-muon-decay-through-a-virtual-charged-weak-boson). muon decay through a virtual charged weak boson**. Original tree diagram. Arrows indicate fermion-number flow, including the reversed arrow on the outgoing [electron antineutrino](../../../../../electron-antineutrino.md).

Typical transfers satisfy $|q^2|\lesssim m_\mu^2\ll M_W^2$, so the propagator factor $(M_W^2-q^2)^{-1}$ expands as $M_W^{-2}$ plus small corrections. Contracting this short-distance exchange produces the local [Fermi interaction](../../../../../fermi-interaction.md), with strength contained in $G_F$. This effective description does not assume that the real W [mass](../../../../../mass.md) vanishes. The same treatment applies to leptonic tau decays with the relevant [masses](../../../../../mass.md) changed.[Muon Decay Parameters, §57.1 introduction and §57.2](../../../../../review-of-particle-physics-by-particle-data-group-2022.md)

In GeV units, $m_\mu=0.106\,\mathrm{GeV}$ and $\hbar=6.582\times10^{-25}\,\mathrm{GeV\,s}$. Thus

$$
\Gamma_\mu=\frac{(1.166\times10^{-5})^2(0.106)^5}{192\pi^3}\,\mathrm{GeV}
=3.0562\times10^{-19}\,\mathrm{GeV},
$$



$$
\boxed{\tau_\mu=\frac{\hbar}{\Gamma_\mu}=2.154\times10^{-6}\,\mathrm s.}
$$

The modest difference from a precision experimental lifetime is expected from the rounded [mass](../../../../../mass.md) and omitted radiative and final-electron-mass corrections.
