<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

An alpha emitter has an alpha-like cluster coupled to a daughter nucleus. Its positive decay [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) is $Q=[M_P-M_D-M_\alpha]c^2$; atomic [masses](../../../../../mass.md) may be used consistently because the [electron](../../../../../electron.md) counts balance. The daughter and alpha share this [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) according to [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) conservation. The attractive nuclear interior and repulsive exterior [Coulomb potential](../../../../../coulomb-potential-energy.md) produce a quasibound state with a barrier, so positive $Q$ does not imply rapid classical escape.

For daughter charge $Z_de$, the exterior barrier is approximately $C/r$, $C=2Z_de^2/(4\pi\epsilon_0)$, with outer turning radius $b=C/Q$. For zero [orbital angular momentum](../../../../../orbital-angular-momentum.md), the leading WKB [probability](../../../../../probability.md) is

$$
T\sim\exp\left[-\frac2\hbar\int_R^b\sqrt{2\mu(C/r-Q)}\,dr\right],
\qquad\lambda\simeq\nu P_{\rm pre}T,\qquad t_{1/2}=\frac{\ln2}{\lambda}.
$$

Here $R$ is a nuclear matching radius, $\mu$ the [reduced mass](../../../../../reduced-mass.md), $\nu$ an interior assault [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md) and $P_{\rm pre}$ a preformation factor. The barrier factor alone cannot determine the latter two. Nonzero emitted [orbital angular momentum](../../../../../orbital-angular-momentum.md) adds a [centrifugal barrier](../../../../../centrifugal-barrier.md) and can strongly hinder a transition.

The dominant [action](../../../../../action.md) varies roughly as $Z_d/\sqrt Q$, explaining the Geiger-Nuttall relation and the enormous spread of alpha [half-lives](../../../../../half-life.md). [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) and [parity](../../../../../parity.md) constrain the emitted [partial wave](../../../../../partial-wave.md): a spin-zero positive-parity alpha requires $|J_P-J_D|\le L\le J_P+J_D$ and $\pi_P=\pi_D(-1)^L$. Fine-structure alpha groups populate different daughter levels and therefore have different kinetic energies and penetrabilities. Shell structure and alpha clustering also change preformation [probabilities](../../../../../probability.md), so equal $Q$ values do not guarantee equal lifetimes. Escape conserves the total [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) throughout tunnelling; the particle does not borrow a measured [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) from the barrier.
