<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The decay is energetically open, but two spin-zero [pions](../../../../../pion.md) have total [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) equal to their relative [orbital angular momentum](../../../../../orbital-angular-momentum.md) $L$. Conserving the parent [spin](../../../../../spin.md) requires $L=1$. Interchanging two identical [neutral pions](../../../../../neutral-pion.md) multiplies this orbital state by $(-1)^L=-1$, whereas their total state must be symmetric by Bose statistics. There is no different [spin](../../../../../spin.md) or species label to supply another minus sign. Thus **the two-neutral-pion final state is forbidden**. Its [parity](../../../../../parity.md) for $L=1$ would actually be $(-1)^2(-1)^1=-1$, matching the parent; the decisive obstruction is identical-boson [symmetry](../../../../../symmetry-physics.md), not the [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) or the shared quark-wavefunction component.
