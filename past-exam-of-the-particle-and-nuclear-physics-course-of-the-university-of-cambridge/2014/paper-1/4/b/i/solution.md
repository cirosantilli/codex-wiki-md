↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

An electric multipole has radiation [parity](../../../../../../parity.md) $(-1)^L$ and a magnetic multipole [parity](../../../../../../parity.md) $(-1)^{L+1}$. The radiation [quantum numbers](../../../../../../quantum-number.md) are therefore

$$
\boxed{\begin{array}{c|cccccc}
\text{mode}&E1&E2&E3&M1&M2&M3\\\hline
J_\gamma^P&1^-&2^+&3^-&1^+&2^-&3^+
\end{array}.}
$$

Equivalently, an $EL$ transition requires $\pi_f/\pi_i=(-1)^L$, while an $ML$ transition requires $\pi_f/\pi_i=(-1)^{L+1}$.
