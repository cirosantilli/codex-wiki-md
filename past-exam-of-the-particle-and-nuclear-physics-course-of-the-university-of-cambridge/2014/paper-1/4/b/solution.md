The symbols $EL$ and $ML$ [mean](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) electric and magnetic radiation multipoles of order $L$. The orders $L=1,2,3$ are called dipole, quadrupole and octupole. They describe the angular structure of charge/current radiation and the total [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) carried by the radiation; they do not change the [photon](../../../../../photon.md)'s intrinsic [spin](../../../../../spin.md) from one. A real single [photon](../../../../../photon.md) has no monopole radiation mode.
