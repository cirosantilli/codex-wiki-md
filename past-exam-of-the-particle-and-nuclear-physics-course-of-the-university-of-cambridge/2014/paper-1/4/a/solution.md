<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Assume the quoted [intensities](../../../../../intensity-physics.md) represent relative emitted [photon numbers](../../../../../photon-number.md), with efficiency corrections implicit, as required for population-flow bookkeeping. The prominent branch obeys $I_1=I_2+I_4$, and $I_2=I_3$ identifies a serial two-photon branch. The energies obey $E_2+E_3\simeq E_4$ and $E_1+E_4\simeq E_5$ within their uncertainties. The weak fifth line directly spans D to A. Reversing which end contains the line-1 step and interchanging the serial line-2/line-3 order produces the four possibilities.

Take A as zero. A [gamma-ray energy-network fit](../../../../../gamma-ray-energy-network-fit.md) to the five [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) differences gives the following approximate [excitation energies](../../../../../excitation-energy.md) in keV:

$$
\boxed{\begin{array}{c|rrr}
\text{arrangement}&\Delta E_{AB}&\Delta E_{AC}&\Delta E_{AD}\\\hline
\mathrm I&133.02&269.27&615.10\\
\mathrm{II}&133.02&478.85&615.10\\
\mathrm{III}&136.25&482.08&615.10\\
\mathrm{IV}&345.83&482.08&615.10
\end{array}}
$$

The line connections, in the order $1,2,3,4,5$, are respectively: I: $(B\to A,C\to B,D\to C,D\to B,D\to A)$; II: $(B\to A,D\to C,C\to B,D\to B,D\to A)$; III: $(D\to C,B\to A,C\to B,C\to A,D\to A)$; IV: $(D\to C,C\to B,B\to A,C\to A,D\to A)$.

For I and II, the B population entering through lines 2 or 3 and line 4 exits through line 1. For III and IV, line 1 feeds C and its [intensity](../../../../../intensity-physics.md) splits into the direct line 4 and serial lines 2/3. The small direct D-to-A branch bypasses these intermediate balances. The central measured energies do not obey every sum exactly; all four fitted arrangements have $\chi^2\simeq0.81$ for two [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) constraints. Assigning the weakest line's central [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) as an exact [excitation energy](../../../../../excitation-energy.md) would discard the more precise cascade measurements.
