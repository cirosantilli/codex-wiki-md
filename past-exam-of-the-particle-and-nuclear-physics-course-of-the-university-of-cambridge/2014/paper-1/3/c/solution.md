<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Use $K^-=s\bar u$ and $p=uud$. In the first reaction a [proton](../../../../../proton.md) $u$ can join the incoming $\bar u$ to form the $u\bar u$ component of the [neutral pion](../../../../../neutral-pion.md), leaving $uds$. In the second, a $u\bar u$ pair can annihilate into a [gluon](../../../../../gluon.md) which creates $s\bar s$; the remaining $u$ joins $\bar s$ in the outgoing [kaon](../../../../../kaon.md), leaving $dss$. Thus

$$
\boxed{X^0=uds,\qquad Y^-=dss.}
$$

[baryon number](../../../../../baryon-number.md) is one in both cases. Strong [strangeness](../../../../../strangeness.md) conservation provides a separate check: the first [baryon](../../../../../baryon.md) has [strangeness](../../../../../strangeness.md) $-1$; the second has [strangeness](../../../../../strangeness.md) $-2$ because the outgoing $K^+$ carries $+1$. The [Feynman diagrams](../../../../../feynman-diagram.md) show representative [quark-line flavour rearrangement](../../../../../quark-line-flavour-rearrangement.md) and pair-annihilation topologies. Bound [hadrons](../../../../../hadron.md) require nonperturbative binding, so a single drawn [gluon](../../../../../gluon.md) is not an exclusive cross-section calculation.
