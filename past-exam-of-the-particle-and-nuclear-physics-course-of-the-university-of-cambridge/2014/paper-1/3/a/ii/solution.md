<h1 id="3/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The second decay has $\mathbf p_A=-\mathbf p_s$ in the B [rest frame](../../../../../../rest-frame.md). Using $p_A^2=m_A^2$ and $p_s^2=0$ gives

$$
\boxed{|\mathbf p_A|=|\mathbf p_s|=\frac{m_B^2-m_A^2}{2m_B},\qquad
E_A=\frac{m_B^2+m_A^2}{2m_B}.}
$$

This is an ordinary two-body decay in its parent's [rest frame](../../../../../../rest-frame.md).
