<h1 id="3/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Set $c=1$ and use metric $(+---)$. In the [rest frame](../../../../../../rest-frame.md) of B, $p_B=(m_B,\mathbf0)$ and $p_C=p_B+p_t$. Since $p_t^2=0$,

$$
m_C^2=m_B^2+2m_BE_t,
\qquad\boxed{|\mathbf p_C|=|\mathbf p_t|=\frac{m_C^2-m_B^2}{2m_B}.}
$$

The denominator is $m_B$ because this is the B [rest frame](../../../../../../rest-frame.md), not the C [rest frame](../../../../../../rest-frame.md). In this frame C and $t$ have equal, parallel three-momenta.
