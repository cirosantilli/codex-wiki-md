<h1 id="section-a/3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

There are

$$
\boxed{0\text{ fundamental five-gauge-boson vertices}.}
$$

For a [Yang-Mills theory](../../../../../yang-mills-theory.md), schematically $F=\partial A+gAA$. The gauge kinetic [Lagrangian](../../../../../lagrangian.md) is quadratic in $F$, so its terms are at most quartic in the [gauge fields](../../../../../gauge-field.md):

$$
F^2\sim(\partial A)^2+g(\partial A)AA+g^2AAAA.
$$

Thus there is no elementary five-leg pure-gauge [interaction vertex](../../../../../interaction-vertex.md) in the renormalizable [Standard Model](../../../../../standard-model-split.md). A five-boson scattering amplitude can still be assembled from several lower-valence vertices, or generated by [loop diagrams](../../../../../loop-feynman-diagram.md). The number of external legs of a complete [Feynman diagram](../../../../../feynman-diagram.md) is not the valence of a fundamental vertex.
