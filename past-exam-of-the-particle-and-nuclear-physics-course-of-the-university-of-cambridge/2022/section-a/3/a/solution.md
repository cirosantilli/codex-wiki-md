<h1 id="section-a/3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use the usual [Feynman rule](../../../../../feynman-rule.md) convention in which the diagonal quadratic terms of the free [Lagrangian](../../../../../lagrangian.md), including the physical [W boson](../../../../../w-boson.md) and [Z boson](../../../../../z-boson.md) masses, are incorporated into their [propagators](../../../../../propagator.md). Then there are

$$
\boxed{0\text{ fundamental two-gauge-boson interaction vertices}.}
$$

A line joining two ends is a [propagator](../../../../../propagator.md), not an interaction vertex. [Gauge boson self-interactions](../../../../../gauge-boson-self-interaction.md) begin with cubic terms in the [Yang-Mills theory](../../../../../yang-mills-theory.md) [Lagrangian](../../../../../lagrangian.md). The question's $g,W,Z,\gamma$ name four families: they include eight [gluon](../../../../../gluon.md) colour components and the two charged [W bosons](../../../../../w-boson.md) $W^\pm$.

There is a convention to distinguish here: if a calculation expands around massless free fields, quadratic $m_W^2W^+_\mu W^{-\mu}$ and $m_Z^2Z_\mu Z^\mu/2$ terms may be drawn as two-leg mass insertions. Those two insertion types belong to that bookkeeping convention. Similarly, two-point [self-energy](../../../../../self-energy.md) corrections occur in [loop diagrams](../../../../../loop-feynman-diagram.md). Neither is an additional fundamental interaction in the standard physical-mass, tree-level rules used for the count above.
