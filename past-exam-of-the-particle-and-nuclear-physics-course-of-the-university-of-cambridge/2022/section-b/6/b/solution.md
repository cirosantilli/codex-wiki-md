The original table gives the dimensionless [branching fractions](../../../../../branching-fraction.md)

$$
B(\pi^0\to e^+e^+e^-e^-)=(3.34\pm0.16)\times10^{-5},
\qquad
B(\pi^0\to e^+e^-)=(6.46\pm0.33)\times10^{-8}.
$$

Thus the four-lepton channel is about $517$ times as frequent, despite its smaller available [phase space](../../../../../phase-space.md).

The dominant neutral-[pion](../../../../../pion.md) interaction is with two [photons](../../../../../photon.md). In [double Dalitz decay](../../../../../double-dalitz-decay.md), both are virtual and convert to separate [electron](../../../../../electron.md)-[Positron](../../../../../positron.md) pairs:

$$
\pi^0\to\gamma^*\gamma^*\to(e^+e^-)(e^+e^-).
$$

This requires two electromagnetic conversions but does not force the whole final state into a helicity-suppressed two-fermion configuration. The two virtual [photons](../../../../../photon.md) and four final [leptons](../../../../../lepton.md) allow the necessary [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) correlations.

For the two-lepton mode, a single virtual [photon](../../../../../photon.md) cannot mediate a $0^{-+}$ [pion](../../../../../pion.md) into $e^+e^-$: the electromagnetic vector current has the wrong quantum numbers. The leading electromagnetic mechanism involves two virtual [photons](../../../../../photon.md) attached to the same lepton line, with a loop integration. The resulting pseudoscalar two-lepton amplitude vanishes as $m_e\to0$, so its [decay width](../../../../../decay-width.md) has an additional [helicity suppression](../../../../../helicity-suppression.md) factor proportional to $m_e^2/m_\pi^2$.

Both channels involve extra electromagnetic couplings relative to $\pi^0\to\gamma\gamma$. The crucial distinction is the extra leptonic loop and [helicity suppression](../../../../../helicity-suppression.md) of the two-lepton channel; counting final particles or couplings alone misses it. The pion-two-photon interaction itself already contains strong-interaction structure, so “extra loop” here compares the diagrams after representing that common interaction by an effective $\pi^0\gamma\gamma$ vertex.
