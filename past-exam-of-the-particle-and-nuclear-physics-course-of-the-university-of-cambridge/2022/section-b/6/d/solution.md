<h1 id="section-b/6/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For (i), [CP symmetry](../../../../../cp-symmetry.md) relates the negative- and positive-[pion](../../../../../pion.md) radiative channels to excellent accuracy. The original positive-pion table therefore predicts

$$
\boxed{B(\pi^-\to\mu^-\bar\nu_\mu\gamma)
\simeq(2.00\pm0.25)\times10^{-4}.}
$$

The comparison requires the same photon-energy and event-selection definition. The table attaches an unexplained footnote to this radiative channel; it does not specify those cuts in the supplied excerpt. An arbitrarily soft [photon](../../../../../photon.md) is not a separately infrared-finite exclusive observable, so the numerical value must retain the table's definition.

Part (ii) contains an apparent notation error in the original PDF as well as the TeX: it prints $e^-\nu_e e^-e^+$, rather than $e^-\bar\nu_e e^-e^+$. Taken literally, the charged leptons carry net electron-family [lepton number](../../../../../lepton-number.md) $2-1=+1$, and the printed $\nu_e$ adds another $+1$. The initial [pion](../../../../../pion.md) has zero [lepton number](../../../../../lepton-number.md). This is a $\Delta L_e=2$ channel, not the charge conjugate of the listed positive-pion decay. In the ordinary lepton-number-conserving [Standard Model](../../../../../standard-model-split.md) approximation,

$$
\boxed{B(\pi^-\to e^-\nu_e e^-e^+)=0\quad\text{for the channel as printed}.}
$$

Allowing lepton-number-violating [masses](../../../../../mass.md) for [Majorana neutrinos](../../../../../majorana-neutrino.md) would require additional physical assumptions and does not justify assigning the tabulated number to that channel.

For the likely intended charge-conjugate process, the prediction is instead

$$
\boxed{B(\pi^-\to e^-\bar\nu_e e^-e^+)
\simeq(3.2\pm0.5)\times10^{-9}.}
$$

This distinction follows from a [lepton-number test of a decay channel](../../../../../lepton-number-test-of-a-decay-channel.md); replacing the printed [neutrino](../../../../../neutrino.md) by an [antineutrino](../../../../../antineutrino.md) must be stated explicitly.
