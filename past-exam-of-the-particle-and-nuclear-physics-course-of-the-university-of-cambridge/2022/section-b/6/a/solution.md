<h1 id="section-b/6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The charged [pion](../../../../../pion.md) has [spin](../../../../../spin.md) zero. In its [rest frame](../../../../../rest-frame.md), the outgoing [muon](../../../../../muon.md) or [Positron](../../../../../positron.md) and the [neutrino](../../../../../neutrino.md) travel in opposite directions. With a nearly massless left-helicity [neutrino](../../../../../neutrino.md), total [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) zero requires the positive charged [antilepton](../../../../../antilepton.md) to have negative [helicity](../../../../../helicity.md). The [weak charged current](../../../../../charged-current.md) instead creates a massless positive [antilepton](../../../../../antilepton.md) with positive [helicity](../../../../../helicity.md). A nonzero charged-lepton [mass](../../../../../mass.md) supplies the required opposite-helicity component.

Thus the [decay amplitude](../../../../../decay-amplitude.md) is proportional to $m_\ell$: this is [helicity suppression](../../../../../helicity-suppression.md). Explicitly, contracting the pion's axial-current matrix element, proportional to its four-momentum $p_\pi^\mu$, with the leptonic current and applying the [Dirac equation](../../../../../dirac-equation.md) leaves a factor $m_\ell$. The [leptonic pseudoscalar decay width](../../../../../leptonic-pseudoscalar-decay-width.md) is, in the $f_\pi$ convention,

$$
\Gamma(\pi^+\to\ell^+\nu_\ell)=
\frac{G_F^2|V_{ud}|^2f_\pi^2m_\pi m_\ell^2}{8\pi}
\left(1-\frac{m_\ell^2}{m_\pi^2}\right)^2.
$$

The [phase space](../../../../../phase-space.md) advantage of the lighter [Positron](../../../../../positron.md) cannot offset its much stronger [helicity suppression](../../../../../helicity-suppression.md). The common factors cancel in the [pion electron-to-muon decay ratio](../../../../../pion-electron-to-muon-decay-ratio.md):

$$
\boxed{\frac{\Gamma_e}{\Gamma_\mu}
=\frac{m_e^2}{m_\mu^2}
\left[\frac{1-m_e^2/m_\pi^2}{1-m_\mu^2/m_\pi^2}\right]^2
\simeq1.28\times10^{-4}.}
$$

This tree-level estimate explains why the muonic branch dominates by roughly four orders of magnitude. The paper's table gives an electronic [branching fraction](../../../../../branching-fraction.md) $1.230\times10^{-4}$; electromagnetic corrections change the simple ratio by a few percent without changing the mechanism.
