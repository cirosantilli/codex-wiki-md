↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [baryon hyperfine mass formula](../../../../../baryon-hyperfine-mass-formula.md) is a phenomenological [constituent-quark model](../../../../../constituent-quark-model.md). The three [constituent quark masses](../../../../../constituent-quark-mass.md) include effects of strong-interaction dressing and an approximate spin-independent confinement energy; they are not the small running [quark masses](../../../../../quark-mass.md) in a short-distance [Quantum chromodynamics](../../../../../quantum-chromodynamics.md) calculation.

The residual correction has the form of a colour-magnetic [spin-spin interaction](../../../../../spin-spin-interaction.md). A nonrelativistic reduction of one-[gluon](../../../../../gluon.md) exchange produces a contact contribution proportional to

$$
\alpha_s\frac{\mathbf S_i\cdot\mathbf S_j}{m_im_j}\,\delta^3(\mathbf r_i-\mathbf r_j).
$$

After taking its expectation in the common spatial [wavefunction](../../../../../wave-function-split.md) and the [colour singlet](../../../../../colour-singlet.md), the colour factor, coupling, and contact density are absorbed into $A'$. Summing the three quark pairs gives the supplied formula. Positive $A'$ raises aligned-spin configurations relative to lower-spin configurations. In [natural units](../../../../../natural-units.md), the [spin angular momentum](../../../../../spin.md) operators are dimensionless, so $A'$ has units of [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) cubed, as in the given $\mathrm{GeV^3}$.
