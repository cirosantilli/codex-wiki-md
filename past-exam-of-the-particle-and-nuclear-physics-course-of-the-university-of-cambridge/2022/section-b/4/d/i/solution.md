<h1 id="section-b/4/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The four direct [beta decay](../../../../../../beta-decay.md) [branching fractions](../../../../../../branching-fraction.md) exhaust the parent decays in the model, so

$$
\boxed{\Gamma_0+\Gamma_1+\Gamma_2+\Gamma_3=1.}
$$

Here the question's $\Gamma_i$ are dimensionless [branching fractions](../../../../../../branching-fraction.md), despite the conventional use of $\Gamma$ for a dimensionful [decay width](../../../../../../decay-width.md). Its “zeroth excited state” means the daughter [ground state](../../../../../../ground-state.md). Cascades through an excited level do not count as an additional direct beta branch to it.
