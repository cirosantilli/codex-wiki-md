<h1 id="section-b/4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The supplied schematic has four daughter [energy levels](../../../../../energy-level.md), including the [ground state](../../../../../ground-state.md), and all six downward pair transitions. Thus the observed [gamma ray](../../../../../gamma-ray.md) [energies](../../../../../energy-split.md) must be the six positive pairwise differences of

$$
0<E_1<E_2<E_3.
$$

The largest difference fixes $E_3=2.982\,\mathrm{MeV}$. Since $E_1,E_2$ are themselves transitions to the [ground state](../../../../../ground-state.md), each must occur among the measured [energies](../../../../../energy-split.md). Testing those pairs gives exactly **two level schemes**:

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
\mathrm A:\ &(E_1,E_2,E_3)=(0.440,2.076,2.982)\,\mathrm{MeV},\\
\mathrm B:\ &(E_1,E_2,E_3)=(0.906,2.542,2.982)\,\mathrm{MeV}.
\end{aligned}}
$$

They are reflections of one another about half the total span: replacing every level $E$ by $E_3-E$ preserves the set of differences. The assignment of every observed line is

$$
\begin{array}{c|cc}
\text{Transition}&\mathrm A&\mathrm B\\\hline
1\to0&\gamma_1&\gamma_2\\
2\to0&\gamma_4&\gamma_5\\
3\to0&\gamma_6&\gamma_6\\
2\to1&\gamma_3&\gamma_3\\
3\to1&\gamma_5&\gamma_4\\
3\to2&\gamma_2&\gamma_1
\end{array}
$$
