<h1 id="section-b/4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Neon](../../../../../neon.md) has $Z=10$ and [sodium](../../../../../sodium.md) has $Z=11$, while $A=23$ is unchanged. The conversion is therefore [beta-minus decay](../../../../../beta-minus-decay.md),

$$
\boxed{{}^{23}_{10}\mathrm{Ne}\longrightarrow{}^{23}_{11}\mathrm{Na}^{(*)}+e^-+\bar\nu_e.}
$$

A [neutron](../../../../../neutron-split.md) is converted to a [proton](../../../../../proton.md) by the [weak charged current](../../../../../charged-current.md). If an excited daughter is populated, subsequent [gamma decay](../../../../../gamma-decay.md) produces the observed [photons](../../../../../photon.md) without changing either $A$ or $Z$. The initial weak transition and the later electromagnetic [nuclear de-excitation](../../../../../nuclear-de-excitation.md) are distinct stages. A direct transition to the daughter [ground state](../../../../../ground-state.md) produces no nuclear de-excitation [gamma ray](../../../../../gamma-ray.md).
