<h1 id="section-b/5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In the [pion](../../../../../pion.md) [rest frame](../../../../../rest-frame.md), write its [four-momentum](../../../../../four-momentum.md) as $P=(m_\pi,\mathbf0)$ and the charged lepton's as $p_\ell=(E_\ell,\mathbf p)$. By [four-momentum conservation](../../../../../four-momentum-conservation.md), the massless [antineutrino](../../../../../antineutrino.md) has $k=P-p_\ell$, with $k^2=0$. Therefore

$$
0=(P-p_\ell)^2=m_\pi^2+m_\ell^2-2m_\pi E_\ell,
\qquad E_\ell=\frac{m_\pi^2+m_\ell^2}{2m_\pi}.
$$

Its energy is $E_\nu=m_\pi-E_\ell$, and for a massless [antineutrino](../../../../../antineutrino.md), $E_\nu=|\mathbf k|$. The final [momenta](../../../../../momentum-split.md) are equal and opposite, so

$$
\boxed{|\mathbf p_\ell|=|\mathbf k|=E_\nu
=\frac{m_\pi^2-m_\ell^2}{2m_\pi}.}
$$

The expression is positive precisely when the two-body decay is energetically allowed, $m_\pi>m_\ell$, neglecting the [neutrino mass](../../../../../neutrino-mass.md).
