<h1 id="section-b/5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The variable initial [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) $E_0$ replaces $m_\pi$ in the kinematic expression before differentiation. The charged-lepton [mass](../../../../../mass.md) remains fixed:

$$
p(E_0)=\frac{E_0^2-m_\ell^2}{2E_0}
=\frac{E_0}{2}-\frac{m_\ell^2}{2E_0}.
$$

Thus the required [Jacobian determinant](../../../../../jacobian-determinant.md) in this one-dimensional change of variables is

$$
\boxed{\left.\frac{dp}{dE_0}\right|_{E_0=m_\pi}
=\frac12+\frac{m_\ell^2}{2m_\pi^2}
=\frac{m_\pi^2+m_\ell^2}{2m_\pi^2}.}
$$

Equivalently, $E_0=\sqrt{p^2+m_\ell^2}+p$ gives $dE_0/dp=1+p/E_\ell$, so $dp/dE_0=E_\ell/(E_\ell+p)=E_\ell/m_\pi$, the same result. It is dimensionless in [natural units](../../../../../natural-units.md).
