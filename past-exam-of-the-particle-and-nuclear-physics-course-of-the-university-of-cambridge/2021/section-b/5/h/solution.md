<h1 id="section-b/5/h/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [H](../h.md)

Use a high-energy [proton](../../../../../proton.md) beam on a target to produce secondary [pions](../../../../../pion.md), then select and focus the negative ones. A [magnetic horn](../../../../../magnetic-horn.md) and a charge-selecting dipole system remove positive pions, limiting wrong-sign $\nu_\mu$ contamination. Select a narrow range of [magnetic rigidity](../../../../../magnetic-rigidity.md); if additional species rejection is available, velocity or mass discrimination reduces negative [kaon](../../../../../kaon.md) contamination at the same momentum.

<a id="section-b/5/h/image-a-focused-negative-pion-decay-beam-producing-muon-antineutrinos"></a>
![](../../../../../past-exam-of-the-particle-and-nuclear-physics-course-of-the-university-of-cambridge/2021-antineutrino-beam.png)

**[Figure 7](#section-b/5/h/image-a-focused-negative-pion-decay-beam-producing-muon-antineutrinos). A focused negative-pion decay beam producing muon antineutrinos**.

The selected $\pi^-$ travel through an evacuated [decay tunnel](../../../../../decay-tunnel.md) and undergo $\pi^-\to\mu^-\bar\nu_\mu$. Their forward [Lorentz boost](../../../../../lorentz-boost.md) concentrates the [antineutrinos](../../../../../antineutrino.md) within an angle of order $1/\gamma_\pi$. Choose a tunnel long enough for substantial pion decay but short compared with the boosted [muon](../../../../../muon.md) decay length. Since $\tau_\mu\simeq2.2\,\mu\mathrm s$ is much longer than $\tau_\pi\simeq26\,\mathrm{ns}$, this reduces secondary $\mu^-\to e^-\bar\nu_e\nu_\mu$ contamination.

Downstream magnets sweep the remaining charged particles into absorbers, and shielding stops residual hadrons and [muons](../../../../../muon.md). The [antineutrino](../../../../../antineutrino.md) beam continues straight through. Parent-particle collimation, charge selection, and a forward detector improve purity; neutral-kaon decays, remaining wrong-sign parents, and muon decays prevent perfect purity. [Neutrinos](../../../../../neutrino.md) themselves cannot be focused or charge-selected by these magnets. Neutrinos produced after stopped-muon decay or capture are much less forward and can be reduced by directional and energy selection.
