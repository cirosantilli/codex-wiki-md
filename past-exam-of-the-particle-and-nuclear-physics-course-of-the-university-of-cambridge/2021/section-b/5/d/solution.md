<h1 id="section-b/5/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The charged [pion](../../../../../pion.md) has valence content $\pi^-=d\bar u$. Its constituents annihilate into a virtual [W boson](../../../../../w-boson.md), followed by the lepton pair:

$$
d+\bar u\longrightarrow W^{-*}\longrightarrow\ell^-+\bar\nu_\ell,
\qquad \ell=e,\mu.
$$

<a id="section-b/5/d/image-the-leading-quark-level-charged-pion-leptonic-decay-diagram"></a>


![](../../../../../past-exam-of-the-particle-and-nuclear-physics-course-of-the-university-of-cambridge/2021-pion-leptonic-diagram.png)

**[Figure 5](#section-b/5/d/image-the-leading-quark-level-charged-pion-leptonic-decay-diagram). The leading quark-level charged-pion leptonic decay diagram**.

The quark vertex carries the $u$--$d$ [Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa matrix](../../../../../cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md) element, and the leptonic vertex is the [weak charged current](../../../../../charged-current.md). The pion's nonperturbative spatial structure is encoded in the [pion decay constant](../../../../../pion-decay-constant.md) when evaluating the [decay amplitude](../../../../../decay-amplitude.md).
