<h1 id="section-b/5/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

For the charged [lepton](../../../../../lepton.md), $p_\ell=\beta_\ell\gamma_\ell m_\ell$ and $E_\ell=\gamma_\ell m_\ell$. Their ratio is therefore its speed in [natural units](../../../../../natural-units.md). Using part (a),

$$
\boxed{\beta_\ell=\frac{p_\ell}{E_\ell}
=\frac{m_\pi^2-m_\ell^2}{m_\pi^2+m_\ell^2}.}
$$

Numerically,

$$
\boxed{\beta_e=\frac{19600-0.25}{19600+0.25}=0.99997449,\qquad
\beta_\mu=\frac{19600-11236}{19600+11236}=0.271241.}
$$

The [electron](../../../../../electron.md) is almost ultrarelativistic, while the [muon](../../../../../muon.md) is relatively slow. In ordinary units these are approximately $0.999974c$ and $0.271c$.
