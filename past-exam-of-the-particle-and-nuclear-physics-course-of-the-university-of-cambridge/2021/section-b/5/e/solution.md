<h1 id="section-b/5/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

A spin-zero [pion](../../../../../pion.md) has no preferred emission direction. Choose the [antineutrino](../../../../../antineutrino.md) [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) along $+z$. A massless [antineutrino](../../../../../antineutrino.md) has positive [helicity](../../../../../helicity.md), so its [spin](../../../../../spin.md) points along $+z$. The [muon](../../../../../muon.md) moves along $-z$, and total $J_z=0$ requires its [spin](../../../../../spin.md) to point along $-z$:

$$
\begin{array}{c|cc}
&\mu^-&\bar\nu_\mu\\\hline
\text{Momentum}&-p\widehat{\mathbf z}&+p\widehat{\mathbf z}\\
\text{Spin}&-\tfrac12\widehat{\mathbf z}&+\tfrac12\widehat{\mathbf z}\\
\text{Helicity}&+\tfrac12&+\tfrac12
\end{array}
$$

<a id="section-b/5/e/image-allowed-spin-and-momentum-orientations-in-negative-pion-decay"></a>


![](../../../../../past-exam-of-the-particle-and-nuclear-physics-course-of-the-university-of-cambridge/2021-pion-spin-configurations.png)

**[Figure 6](#section-b/5/e/image-allowed-spin-and-momentum-orientations-in-negative-pion-decay). Allowed spin and momentum orientations in negative-pion decay**.

The reversed configuration is obtained by rotating the entire event and is equally allowed. The two spins are opposite, while each is aligned with its own particle's [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md); **both outgoing helicities are positive**. A negative-helicity [muon](../../../../../muon.md) with this fixed antineutrino direction would give a nonzero total spin projection and is forbidden for this two-body decay of a spin-zero parent. The required positive-helicity charged lepton is the suppressed component of the [weak charged current](../../../../../charged-current.md).
