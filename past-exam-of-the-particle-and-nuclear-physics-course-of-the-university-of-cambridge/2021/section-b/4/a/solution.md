↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Parity](../../../../../parity.md) is full [spatial reflection](../../../../../spatial-reflection.md), $\mathbf x\mapsto-\mathbf x$, represented by a [parity operator](../../../../../parity-operator.md) $\mathcal P$. A [polar vector](../../../../../polar-vector.md), such as [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md), reverses, whereas an [axial vector](../../../../../pseudovector.md), such as [spin](../../../../../spin.md), does not. A state of definite [parity](../../../../../parity.md) has $\mathcal P|\psi\rangle=\eta_P|\psi\rangle$, with $\eta_P=\pm1$ in the usual convention. A multiparticle state's [parity](../../../../../parity.md) includes both the product of its [intrinsic parities](../../../../../intrinsic-parity.md) and its orbital spatial factor, for example $(-1)^L$ for relative [orbital angular momentum](../../../../../orbital-angular-momentum.md) $L$.

An interaction conserves [parity](../../../../../parity.md) when its dynamics are invariant under this transformation, equivalently $[H,\mathcal P]=0$ for a time-independent [Hamiltonian](../../../../../hamiltonian.md). [Parity violation](../../../../../parity-violation.md) means that a process and its fully reflected counterpart need not have the same [probability](../../../../../probability.md). It is more than a left-right asymmetry in an unreflected laboratory apparatus.

The electromagnetic and strong sectors of the [Standard Model](../../../../../standard-model-split.md) conserve [parity](../../../../../parity.md) to the accuracy relevant here, taking the strong-sector [Yang-Mills theta term](../../../../../yang-mills-theta-term.md) to vanish. The [weak charged current](../../../../../charged-current.md) selects left-chiral fermions and right-chiral antifermions, as defined by [fermion chirality](../../../../../chirality-physics.md); the reflected right-chiral charged current is not equally present. The neutral weak current also generally has different left- and right-chiral couplings. Consequently the weak sector violates [parity](../../../../../parity.md); this does not mean that every individual weak decay must have opposite initial and final parity. For a massive particle, [helicity](../../../../../helicity.md) and [fermion chirality](../../../../../chirality-physics.md) should be distinguished.
