<h1 id="section-b/4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [antiquark](../../../../../antiquark.md) $\bar d$ in the specified $\overline B_d^0=b\bar d$ is a spectator. For the charmed final state the leading external-[W boson](../../../../../w-boson.md) tree is

$$
b\to c+W^-,\qquad W^-\to d+\bar u,
$$

with $c\bar d=D^+$ and $d\bar u=\pi^-$. For the charmless final state replace the active transition by $b\to u+W^-$, giving $u\bar d=\pi^+$ and the same $d\bar u=\pi^-$. Their weak amplitudes have factors $V_{cb}V_{ud}^*$ and $V_{ub}V_{ud}^*$, respectively, up to an irrelevant overall conjugation convention.

The diagrams determine the leading weak flavour factors. Strong-interaction matrix elements and, particularly for the charmless channel, additional [loop diagrams](../../../../../loop-feynman-diagram.md) can also contribute to physical [branching fractions](../../../../../branching-fraction.md).
