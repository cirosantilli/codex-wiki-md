<h1 id="section-a/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Read the line styles and fermion-number arrows in the original PDF: a wavy line is a [photon](../../../../photon.md), and a coiled line is a [gluon](../../../../gluon.md). The valid fundamental [interaction vertices](../../../../interaction-vertex.md) are

$$
\boxed{\text{QED: (a), (f).}\qquad\text{QCD: (b), (g).}}
$$

Both (a) and (f) have one electron-line arrow entering and one leaving a photon vertex; changing the drawing's orientation does not change the [quantum electrodynamics](../../../../quantum-electrodynamics.md) interaction. The [Quantum chromodynamics](../../../../quantum-chromodynamics.md) vertices (b) and (g) are the cubic and quartic [gluon](../../../../gluon.md) self-interactions.

The remaining cases fail for different reasons. Diagram (c) couples an isolated electron line to gluons and is not an allowed fermion vertex. Diagram (d) changes an [electron](../../../../electron.md) to a [muon](../../../../muon.md) at a photon vertex, violating the flavour-diagonal electromagnetic coupling. Diagram (e) couples a colourless [electron](../../../../electron.md) to a [gluon](../../../../gluon.md). Diagram (h) changes an [up quark](../../../../up-quark.md) into a [down quark](../../../../down-quark.md) at a gluon vertex, whereas [Quantum chromodynamics](../../../../quantum-chromodynamics.md) preserves quark flavour; that assignment also fails [electric charge conservation](../../../../charge-conservation.md).

One measurement of the [strong coupling constant](../../../../strong-coupling-constant.md) uses the fraction of resolved [three-jet events](../../../../three-jet-event.md) in $e^+e^-\to q\bar qg$: with a fixed jet-resolution definition, its leading ratio to two-jet production is proportional to $\alpha_s$, with calculable higher-order corrections.

![](../../../../past-exam-of-the-particle-and-nuclear-physics-course-of-the-university-of-cambridge/2021-three-jet-diagrams.png)

**[Figure 1](#section-a/2/image-the-two-leading-gluon-emission-diagrams-for-a-three-jet-measurement). The two leading gluon-emission diagrams for a three-jet measurement**.

The intermediate current is $\gamma^*$ or $Z^*$, and the extra [gluon](../../../../gluon.md) can be emitted from either the [quark](../../../../quark.md) or the [antiquark](../../../../antiquark.md). Both [Feynman diagrams](../../../../feynman-diagram.md) contribute coherently. The observed jets arise from [hadronization](../../../../hadronization.md); a resolved-jet definition is needed because arbitrarily soft or collinear radiation is not a separate measurable third jet.
