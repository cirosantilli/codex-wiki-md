<h1 id="section-a/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

The original chart has $Z$ vertically and $N$ horizontally. Thus its region (a) is proton-rich relative to the beta-stable band, whereas (b) is neutron-rich.


- In (a), [beta-plus decay](../../../../beta-plus-decay.md) changes a [proton](../../../../proton.md) into a [neutron](../../../../neutron-split.md):


$$
(Z,N)\longrightarrow(Z-1,N+1)+e^++\nu_e.
$$

[Electron capture](../../../../electron-capture.md), $e^-+(Z,N)\to(Z-1,N+1)+\nu_e$, is the other proton-to-neutron weak process; it remains possible for some neutral atoms whose [beta-plus decay](../../../../beta-plus-decay.md) lacks the $2m_e$ atomic-mass threshold.
- In (b), [beta-minus decay](../../../../beta-minus-decay.md) changes a [neutron](../../../../neutron-split.md) into a [proton](../../../../proton.md):


$$
(Z,N)\longrightarrow(Z+1,N-1)+e^-+\bar\nu_e.
$$


- In the heavy region (e), [alpha decay](../../../../alpha-decay.md) moves the parent down and left:


$$
(Z,N)\longrightarrow(Z-2,N-2)+{}^4_2\mathrm{He}.
$$

The emitted [alpha particle](../../../../alpha-particle.md) contains two [protons](../../../../proton.md) and two [neutrons](../../../../neutron-split.md). Very heavy [nuclei](../../../../atomic-nucleus-split.md) can also compete through [nuclear fission](../../../../nuclear-fission.md); the chart alone does not determine each nuclide's branching fractions.

![](../../../../past-exam-of-the-particle-and-nuclear-physics-course-of-the-university-of-cambridge/2021-beta-quark-diagrams.png)

**[Figure 2](#section-a/3/image-quark-level-beta-plus-beta-minus-and-electron-capture-processes). Quark-level beta-plus, beta-minus, and electron-capture processes**.

The quark transitions are $u\to d+W^+$ followed by $W^+\to e^+\nu_e$ in (a), and $d\to u+W^-$ followed by $W^-\to e^-\bar\nu_e$ in (b). The additional panel shows [electron capture](../../../../electron-capture.md) as $u+e^-\to d+\nu_e$ through [W boson](../../../../w-boson.md) exchange. The remaining quarks in each [nucleon](../../../../nucleon.md) are spectators.

Regions (c) and (d) lie beyond the proton and neutron binding limits, respectively. In (c), unbound [protons](../../../../proton.md) can be emitted, $(Z,N)\to(Z-1,N)+p$; in (d), unbound [neutrons](../../../../neutron-split.md) can be emitted, $(Z,N)\to(Z,N-1)+n$. These are the [proton drip line](../../../../proton-drip-line.md) and [neutron drip line](../../../../neutron-drip-line.md) limits, with one- or several-nucleon emission possible according to the [nuclear separation energies](../../../../nuclear-separation-energy.md). They are not merely more distant points on a beta-decay trajectory.
