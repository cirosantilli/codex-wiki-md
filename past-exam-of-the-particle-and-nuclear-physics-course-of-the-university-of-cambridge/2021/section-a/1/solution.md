<h1 id="section-a/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Choose the positive $z$ direction along the incident [electron](../../../../electron.md). With the neglected lepton [masses](../../../../mass.md) and the paper's [natural units](../../../../natural-units.md), the initial [four-momenta](../../../../four-momentum.md) are

$$
p_-=(E_-,0,0,E_-),\qquad p_+=(E_+,0,0,-E_+).
$$

The squared [centre-of-mass energy](../../../../center-of-mass-energy.md) is the [Lorentz invariant](../../../../lorentz-invariance.md)

$$
s=(p_-+p_+)^2=(E_-+E_+)^2-(E_--E_+)^2=4E_-E_+.
$$

Producing the [Upsilon meson](../../../../upsilon-meson.md) as the sole final resonance requires $s=M_\Upsilon^2$. The threshold positron [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{E_+^{\min}=\frac{(10.580\,\mathrm{GeV})^2}{4(9.000\,\mathrm{GeV})}
=3.10934\,\mathrm{GeV}\simeq3.11\,\mathrm{GeV}.}
$$

At that beam setting, [four-momentum conservation](../../../../four-momentum-conservation.md) gives $E_\Upsilon=E_-+E_+=12.10934\,\mathrm{GeV}$ and $p_{\Upsilon,z}=E_--E_+=5.89066\,\mathrm{GeV}$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\beta_\Upsilon=\frac{p_{\Upsilon,z}}{E_\Upsilon}
=\frac{9-3.10934}{9+3.10934}=0.48646.}
$$

The resonance moves in the incident-[electron](../../../../electron.md) direction, with [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) approximately $0.486c$. The laboratory sum of beam [energies](../../../../energy-split.md) exceeds the resonance [rest energy](../../../../rest-energy.md) because the asymmetric beams also give it laboratory [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md).
