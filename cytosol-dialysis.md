# Cytosol dialysis

↑ **Parent:** [Whole-cell recording](whole-cell-recording.md)

[Cytosol dialysis](cytosol-dialysis.md) is exchange between the [cytosol](cytosol.md) and pipette solution through the opening used in conventional [whole-cell recording](whole-cell-recording.md). [Diffusion](diffusion.md) can change [ion](ion.md) [concentrations](concentration.md) and dilute soluble regulators of [ion channels](ion-channel.md). A diminishing response can therefore reflect the recording's alteration of the [biological cell](biological-cell.md), rather than the experimental treatment.

A direct motivating example is the 1988 article [Muscarinic activation of ionic currents measured by a new whole-cell recording method](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2459299/). In rat lacrimal gland [biological cells](biological-cell.md), the [acetylcholine](acetylcholine.md) response in conventional [whole-cell recording](whole-cell-recording.md) lasted about five minutes. With a pore-forming substance in the [patch pipette](patch-pipette.md) instead of open break-in, the response remained undiminished after one hour. The readout was activation of [calcium](calcium.md)-activated [potassium](potassium.md) and $\mathrm{Cl}^-$ [ion](ion.md) [electric currents](electric-current.md); preserving the response supported a role for retained cytoplasmic components. The authors also tested the substance extracellularly and observed reversible increases in [cation](cation.md) [electrical conductance](electrical-conductance.md). This comparison motivates [perforated patch recording](perforated-patch-recording.md), but does not show that every intracellular constituent or every [ion channel](ion-channel.md) response is preserved. [Muscarinic activation of ionic currents measured by a new whole-cell recording method by Richard Horn and others](muscarinic-activation-of-ionic-currents-measured-by-a-new-whole-cell-recording-method-by-richard-horn-and-others.md)

Practical controls are matched pipette and bath compositions, recording elapsed time after break-in, time-matched untreated preparations, and tracking both [patch-clamp series resistance](patch-clamp-series-resistance.md) and baseline leakage. Comparing conventional [whole-cell recording](whole-cell-recording.md) with [perforated patch recording](perforated-patch-recording.md) can separate loss of soluble components from an intended treatment effect, but only if electrical access is characterized in both. These are controls suggested by the mechanisms above, not a new set of measured results.
