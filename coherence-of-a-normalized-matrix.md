# Coherence of a normalized matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Compressed sensing](compressed-sensing.md)

For a [matrix](matrix.md) with at least two columns of unit [Euclidean norm](euclidean-norm.md), its mutual coherence is the largest magnitude of an [inner product](inner-product.md) between distinct columns. It measures the strongest pairwise ambiguity of linear measurements. The [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) gives $0\le\mu\le1$. The order-two [restricted isometry constant](restricted-isometry-constant.md) equals the [mutual coherence](coherence-of-a-normalized-matrix.md), because every two-column [Gram matrix](gram-matrix.md) has eigenvalues $1\pm|\langle a_i,a_j\rangle|$.
