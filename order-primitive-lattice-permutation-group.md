# Order-primitive lattice permutation group

↑ **Parent:** [Lattice-ordered permutation group](lattice-ordered-permutation-group.md)

A transitive [lattice-ordered permutation group](lattice-ordered-permutation-group.md) is order-primitive when its only invariant equivalence relations with convex classes are equality and the universal relation. This concerns convex blocks in a chain, rather than arbitrary blocks of a [primitive group action](primitive-group-action.md).
