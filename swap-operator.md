# Swap operator

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum information theory](quantum-information-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swap_operator)

The swap operator exchanges two tensor factors: $F(|\psi\rangle\otimes|\phi\rangle)=|\phi\rangle\otimes|\psi\rangle$. It has [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) $+1$ on the symmetric subspace and $-1$ on the antisymmetric subspace, and the [partial transpose](partial-transpose.md) of $|\Phi^+\rangle\langle\Phi^+|$ is $F/d$.
