# Myers-Perry black hole

↑ **Parent:** [Black hole](black-hole.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myers-Perry_black_hole)

A Myers-Perry black hole is the higher-dimensional asymptotically flat rotating vacuum generalization of the [Kerr black hole](kerr-black-hole.md). In six dimensions, a solution with one nonzero rotation parameter $a$ and horizon radius $r_+$ has mass parameter $\mu=r_+(r_+^2+a^2)$.
