# Dust trap

↑ **Parent:** [Radial drift of solids in a gas disk](radial-drift-of-solids-in-a-gas-disk.md)

A region that concentrates solids by opposing their loss through radial drift. Near a radial gas-[pressure](pressure-split.md) maximum, the pressure-support drift changes sign and carries particles toward the maximum from both sides. Gas advection and turbulent diffusion can limit retention.
