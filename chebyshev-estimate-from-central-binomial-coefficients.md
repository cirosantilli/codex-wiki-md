# Chebyshev estimate from central binomial coefficients

↑ **Parent:** [Chebyshev estimate](chebyshev-estimate.md)

The central [binomial coefficient](binomial-coefficient.md) satisfies $4^m/(2m+1)\leq\binom{2m}{m}\leq4^m$. Prime factors between $m$ and $2m$ give a dyadic upper bound for the [Chebyshev theta function](chebyshev-theta-function.md). The prime-power exponents in the factorial quotient are sums of zeros and ones, so the central coefficient gives a linear lower bound for the [Second Chebyshev function](second-chebyshev-function.md). Higher prime powers contribute only $O(\sqrt x\log x)$; hence the prime-counting function has upper and lower bounds of order $x/\log x$.
