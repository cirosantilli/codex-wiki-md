# Turbulent kinetic energy

↑ **Parent:** [Turbulence](turbulence-split.md)

Kinetic energy per unit mass in velocity fluctuations. Mean shear, buoyancy and transport supply or redistribute it, while molecular viscosity dissipates it. Its local budget motivates the [local turbulent kinetic energy balance](local-turbulent-kinetic-energy-balance.md) when storage and transport are negligible.
