# Particle isolation

↑ **Parent:** [Particle identification](particle-identification.md)

A collider [isolation](particle-isolation.md) requirement limits additional tracks or deposited [energy](energy-split.md) near a particle candidate. It suppresses candidates embedded in [jets](jet-particle-physics.md) while preserving suitably separated prompt leptons or [photons](photon.md). The cone geometry, [energy](energy-split.md) threshold and collision-pileup correction form part of the observable's experimental definition.
