# Nonstationary oscillatory integral

↑ **Parent:** [Stationary phase method](stationary-phase-method.md)

If the phase derivative has no zero on the support of a smooth amplitude, repeated [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) makes the oscillatory integral decay faster than any prescribed inverse power, subject to corresponding decay and regularity of the amplitude.
