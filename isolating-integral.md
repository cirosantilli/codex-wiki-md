# Isolating integral

↑ **Parent:** [Integral of motion](integral-of-motion.md)

An isolating integral is a regular single-valued [integral of motion](integral-of-motion.md) whose fixed value restricts accessible [phase space](phase-space.md) to a lower-dimensional level set. Functional independence of several such integrals means their differentials are linearly independent on the region considered. A central potential has [specific orbital energy](specific-orbital-energy.md) and the three components of [specific angular momentum](specific-angular-momentum.md) as four generically independent integrals. A [Kepler orbit](kepler-orbit.md) has a fifth independent integral supplied by its fixed apsidal direction, encoded by the [Laplace-Runge-Lenz vector](laplace-runge-lenz-vector.md); its vector components obey two constraints with energy and angular momentum. Circular and radial orbits are degenerate cases of this count.
