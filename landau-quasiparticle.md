# Landau quasiparticle

↑ **Parent:** [Fermi liquid](fermi-liquid-split.md)

A [Landau quasiparticle](landau-quasiparticle.md) is a [fermionic](fermion.md) excitation connected continuously to a [Fermi gas](fermi-gas.md) particle when interactions preserve the phase. Near an isotropic [Fermi surface](fermi-surface.md), its energy relative to the [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) is $\xi_p=\epsilon_p-\mu\simeq v_F(p-p_F)$, with [Fermi momentum](fermi-momentum.md) $p_F$ and [Fermi velocity](fermi-velocity.md) $v_F$.[Section 1, pp.921–922](the-theory-of-a-fermi-liquid-by-lev-davidovich-landau.md)

The [Pauli exclusion principle](pauli-exclusion-principle.md) restricts decay. For a three-dimensional normal [Fermi liquid](fermi-liquid-split.md) with regular short-range interactions, a representative low-energy lifetime model is

$$
\frac{\hbar}{\tau_p}=A\frac{\xi_p^2+(\pi k_BT)^2}{E_*},\qquad A>0,
$$

Here $E_*$ is an [energy](energy-split.md) scale and $A$ is dimensionless; sharpness requires $\hbar/\tau_p\ll|\xi_p|$ at zero [temperature](temperature-split.md).[Section VIII, manuscript p.114, quadratic lifetime corrections](renormalization-group-approach-to-interacting-fermions-by-ramamurti-shankar.md)

For an original example, stipulate $A=1$ and $T=0$. At $|\xi_p|/E_*=0.02$, the decay energy is $0.0004E_*$, just $2\%$ of the excitation [energy](energy-split.md); halving $|\xi_p|$ reduces that ratio to $1\%$. Approaching the [Fermi surface](fermi-surface.md) improves the particle description even though the microscopic interaction has not weakened. This quadratic illustration is three-dimensional; low-dimensional corrections require separate analysis.
