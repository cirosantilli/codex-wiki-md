# Covariance ellipse

↑ **Parent:** [Covariance matrix](covariance-matrix.md)

For a two-dimensional [positive-definite](positive-definite-bilinear-form.md) [covariance matrix](covariance-matrix.md) $\Sigma$, the centered [covariance ellipse](covariance-ellipse.md) is the image of the [unit circle](complex-unit-circle.md) under its positive definite [square root of a matrix](square-root-of-a-matrix.md). Equivalently, it satisfies $x^T\Sigma^{-1}x=1$. Its [semi-major and semi-minor axes](semi-major-and-semi-minor-axes.md) have [lengths](length.md) given by the square roots of the larger and smaller [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md), along the corresponding [eigenvectors](eigenvector.md). The [ellipse](ellipse.md) encodes a second-moment geometry; a probability-coverage interpretation needs additional information about the [probability distribution](probability-distribution.md) and the chosen scale.
