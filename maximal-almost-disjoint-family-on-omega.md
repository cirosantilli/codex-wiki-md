# Maximal almost disjoint family on omega

↑ **Parent:** [Almost disjoint family on omega](almost-disjoint-family-on-omega.md)

An [almost disjoint family on omega](almost-disjoint-family-on-omega.md) that cannot be enlarged by another infinite [subset](subset.md) while retaining almost disjointness. For the [cardinal](cardinal-number.md) invariant $\mathfrak a$, one requires the family itself to be infinite; finite partitions otherwise give trivial maximal families.
