# Magnetization contrast

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic anomaly](magnetic-anomaly.md)

The difference $\Delta\mathbf M=\mathbf M_{\mathrm{target}}-\mathbf M_{\mathrm{reference}}$ between a target region and the reference material it replaces. A weakly magnetic crater infill inside magnetic [basalt](basalt.md) can have a negative induced contrast, even though both materials have positive [magnetic susceptibility](magnetic-susceptibility.md). The sign and direction of this contrast, including [remanent magnetization](remanent-magnetization.md), determine a [total-field magnetic anomaly](total-field-magnetic-anomaly.md) together with geometry and the ambient field. [Geophysical density contrast](geophysical-density-contrast.md) does not determine magnetic contrast.
