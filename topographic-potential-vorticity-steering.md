# Topographic potential-vorticity steering

↑ **Parent:** [Topographic Sverdrup balance](topographic-sverdrup-balance.md)

In a slowly varying inviscid layer, parcels tend to follow contours of $f/H$. If depth increases in the direction of motion, conservation of [potential vorticity](potential-vorticity.md) requires a poleward displacement or a compensating change of relative vorticity. Ekman pumping permits streamlines to cross those contours.
