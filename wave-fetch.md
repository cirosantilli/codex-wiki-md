# Wave fetch

↑ **Parent:** [Surface gravity wave](surface-gravity-wave.md)

Wave fetch is the distance over which wind acts on an uninterrupted water surface to generate waves. Growth also depends on wind speed and duration. A [polynya](polynya.md) can provide a local fetch inside otherwise extensive [sea ice](sea-ice.md).
