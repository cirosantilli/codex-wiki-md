# Homologous polytropes at fixed central temperature

↑ **Parent:** [Polytropic mass-radius relation](polytropic-mass-radius-relation.md)

For finite-radius regular [polytropes](polytrope.md) of one fixed index $n$ and composition, let $\xi_1$ be the first zero of the [Lane-Emden function](lane-emden-function.md) and $Y_n=-\xi_1^2\theta'(\xi_1)$. Hydrostatic scaling gives $GM/R=(n+1)(Y_n/\xi_1)\mathcal RT_c$. Fixed central [temperature](temperature-split.md) therefore implies $M\propto R$. This is not the fixed-entropy-constant sequence; if the index or composition changes, their prefactors cannot be discarded.
