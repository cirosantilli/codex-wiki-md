# Beltrami identity for several dependent variables

↑ **Parent:** [Beltrami identity](beltrami-identity.md)

If the [integrand](integrand.md) $L(q_1,\ldots,q_m,q_1',\ldots,q_m')$ has no explicit dependence on its independent variable $z$, its [Euler-Lagrange equations](euler-lagrange-equation.md) imply the displayed first integral. Indeed, differentiate the left side and cancel the second-derivative terms to obtain $\sum_jq_j'(\partial_{q_j}L-d(\partial_{q_j'}L)/dz)=0$. This is the several-variable form of the [Beltrami identity](beltrami-identity.md). Applied to [Fermat principle](fermat-principle.md), it expresses conservation of the component of the [optical momentum of a ray](optical-momentum-of-a-ray.md) along a translation-invariant direction.
