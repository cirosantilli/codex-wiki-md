# Real quadratic iteration map

↑ **Parent:** [Interval map](interval-map.md)

The family $x_{n+1}=\mu-x_n^2$ has [fixed points](fixed-point.md) $(-1\pm\sqrt{1+4\mu})/2$. Its upper branch attracts linearly for $-1/4<\mu<3/4$ and loses [stability](lyapunov-stability.md) in a [period-doubling bifurcation](period-doubling-bifurcation.md). The resulting [two-cycle](period-two-orbit.md) has points $(1\pm\sqrt{4\mu-3})/2$ and [multiplier](multiplier-of-a-periodic-orbit-of-an-iteration.md) $4(1-\mu)$, [attracting](asymptotic-stability.md) for $3/4<\mu<5/4$. At $\mu=2$, the [map](discrete-dynamical-system-split.md) is [conjugate](topological-conjugacy.md) on $[-2,2]$ to the full [tent map](tent-map.md).
