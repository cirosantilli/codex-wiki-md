# Entropy degrees of freedom

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmological particle freeze-out](cosmological-particle-freeze-out.md)

The effective count appearing in thermal [entropy](entropy-split.md) density $s\propto g_{*s}T^3$. Conservation of comoving [entropy](entropy-split.md) implies $g_{*s}T^3a^3$ is constant, so stable relic dilution includes $g_{*s,0}/g_{*s,f}$ when expressed using [temperatures](temperature-split.md).
