# Magnetic dipole interaction

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic dipole potential energy](magnetic-dipole-potential-energy.md)

The orientation-dependent interaction of two distant localized [magnetic dipole moments](magnetic-dipole-moment.md). Its $r^{-3}$ scale makes close approach important in a concentrated magnetic-particle dispersion. Depending on geometry, it can favor chains, collective alignment or frustrated configurations.
