# Diagonal thermal kernel of a quantum harmonic oscillator

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md)

The diagonal matrix element $K_\beta(x,x)=\langle x|e^{-\beta H}|x\rangle$ combines the [equal-endpoint Euclidean oscillator path](equal-endpoint-euclidean-oscillator-path.md) with its endpoint-independent [Dirichlet oscillator fluctuation determinant](dirichlet-oscillator-fluctuation-determinant.md). It is not itself a normalized [probability density](probability-density.md): its integral is the [canonical partition function](canonical-partition-function.md) $[2\sinh(z/2)]^{-1}$. The normalized position [probability density](probability-density.md) is

$$
p_\beta(x)=\sqrt{\frac{m\omega\tanh(z/2)}{\pi\hbar}}
\exp\!\left[-\frac{m\omega}{\hbar}x^2\tanh(z/2)\right],
\qquad
\langle x^2\rangle=\frac{\hbar}{2m\omega}\coth(z/2).
$$

At high [temperature](temperature-split.md), the [variance](variance-split.md) approaches $1/(\beta m\omega^2)$ as in the classical [canonical ensemble](canonical-ensemble-split.md). At zero [temperature](temperature-split.md), it approaches $\hbar/(2m\omega)$, the [ground state](ground-state.md) [variance](variance-split.md). Dividing $K_\beta(x,x)$ by $K_\beta(0,0)$ cancels its entire fluctuation prefactor.
