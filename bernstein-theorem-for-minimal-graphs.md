# Bernstein theorem for minimal graphs

↑ **Parent:** [Minimal surface equation for a graph](minimal-surface-equation-for-a-graph.md)

Every entire classical minimal graph over $\mathbb R^n$ is affine for $1\leq n\leq7$. The dimension restriction is essential. This theorem applies to the [minimal surface equation for a graph](minimal-surface-equation-for-a-graph.md), without assuming a global bound on its gradient.
