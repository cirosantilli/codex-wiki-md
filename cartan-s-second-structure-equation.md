<h1 id="cartan-s-second-structure-equation">Cartan's second structure equation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Connection 1-form](connection-1-form-split.md)

Cartan's second structure equation defines the curvature forms by

$$
\mathcal R^a{}_b=d\omega^a{}_b+\omega^a{}_c\wedge\omega^c{}_b.
$$
