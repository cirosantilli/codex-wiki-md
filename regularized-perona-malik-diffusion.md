# Regularized Perona-Malik diffusion

↑ **Parent:** [Perona-Malik equation](perona-malik-equation.md)

Computing conductance from a smoothed [gradient](gradient.md) gives $u_t=\operatorname{div}(c(|\nabla(G_\sigma*u)|)\nabla u)$. The [image edge](image-edge.md) detector is nonlocal, while the local highest-order diffusion has positive coefficient when conductance is positive. A smooth kernel, lower conductance bound and appropriate regularity/boundary hypotheses support a well-posed regularized filter.
