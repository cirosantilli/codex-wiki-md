# Different-flavour quark annihilation into a massive pair

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum chromodynamics](quantum-chromodynamics.md)

For distinct incoming and outgoing [quark flavours](quark-flavor.md), tree-level $q\bar q\to Q\bar Q$ has one [gluon](gluon.md) annihilation graph. Averaging the initial [colours](color-charge.md) gives $2/9$, and averaging initial [spins](spin.md) gives $1/4$. The outgoing [fermion spin sum](fermion-spin-sum.md) must use distinct [particle](particle-physics.md) and [antiparticle](antiparticle.md) projectors. For final [mass](mass.md) $M$, $s+t+u=2M^2$ and the displayed result includes all outgoing [colour](color-charge.md) and [spin sums](spin-sum.md). Its [amplitude](scattering-amplitude.md) squared remains finite at [threshold](particle-production-threshold.md) even though the [cross section](cross-section-physics.md) vanishes through the final-state [velocity](velocity.md).
