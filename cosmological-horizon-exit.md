# Cosmological horizon exit

↑ **Parent:** [Bunch-Davies vacuum](bunch-davies-vacuum.md)

A Fourier mode exits the Hubble horizon during inflation when $k=aH$. On superhorizon scales $k\ll aH$, the growing mode of a sufficiently light scalar perturbation approaches a constant.
