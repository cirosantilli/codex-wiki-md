# Block of a finite-dimensional algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Associative algebra](associative-algebra-split.md)

A block of a finite-dimensional [associative algebra](associative-algebra-split.md) $R$ is a two-sided ideal $Re$ associated with a [primitive idempotent](primitive-idempotent.md) $e$ in the [center of an associative algebra](center-of-an-associative-algebra.md). Its identity is $e$, and $R$ is the product of these blocks. An $R$-[module](module-mathematics.md) belongs to this block if $eM=M$. For a [semisimple algebra](semisimple-algebra.md), the [Artin–Wedderburn theorem](artin-wedderburn-theorem.md) says the blocks are its full [matrix algebras](matrix-algebra.md) over division rings. For a [group algebra](group-algebra.md) this specializes to a [block of a group algebra](block-of-a-group-algebra.md).
