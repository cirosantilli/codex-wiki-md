# Logarithm charts for the special unitary group

↑ **Parent:** [Special unitary group](special-unitary-group.md)

Near the identity, the [matrix logarithm](matrix-logarithm.md) of a unitary matrix is skew-Hermitian. Restrict the logarithm's operator norm below $\pi/n$; determinant one then forces its trace to be zero, since $\det U=\exp(\operatorname{tr}\log U)$ and no nonzero multiple of $2\pi i$ fits this bound. The [matrix exponential](matrix-exponential.md) gives the inverse chart on a ball in the displayed real [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md), of dimension $n^2-1$. Left translation supplies compatible charts on the whole [special unitary group](special-unitary-group.md).
