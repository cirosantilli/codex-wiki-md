# Kapitza resistance

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal boundary resistance](thermal-boundary-resistance.md)

[Kapitza resistance](kapitza-resistance.md) is the [thermal boundary resistance](thermal-boundary-resistance.md) encountered between a solid and [helium](helium.md) in [cryogenics](cryogenics.md). A cold [helium](helium.md) [heat bath](heat-bath.md) can coexist with a warmer immersed component under continuous heating. The bath's [temperature](temperature-split.md) alone therefore does not establish the component's [temperature](temperature-split.md). [Kapitza thermal boundary experiment](kapitza-thermal-boundary-experiment.md) supplied an early direct demonstration of the obstacle at the solid–liquid boundary.

A microscopic route to interfacial [heat transfer](heat-transfer.md) is transmission of [phonons](phonon.md). Transmission depends on the two materials and their interface; scattering and the condition of the surface matter. [Swartz and Pohl (1989), abstract: phonon transmission, scattering and surface sensitivity](thermal-boundary-resistance-by-e-t-swartz-and-robert-otto-pohl.md) The [fourth-power thermal boundary model](fourth-power-thermal-boundary-model.md) isolates one useful low-[temperature](temperature-split.md) limiting law without assigning an empirical coefficient to a particular sample.
