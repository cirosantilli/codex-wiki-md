# Emission-pressure degeneracy of a brightness temperature

↑ **Parent:** [Brightness temperature](brightness-temperature.md)

A [brightness temperature](brightness-temperature.md) equates a measured intensity with a [Planck function](planck-function.md); it does not determine its contribution pressure. In [hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md), $\tau_\lambda(P)=\int_0^P\kappa_\lambda\,dP'/g$. Estimating $P\sim g/\kappa_\lambda$ therefore needs gravity and [opacity](opacity.md), as well as a model of the contribution function. Different pressures can produce the same band brightness.
