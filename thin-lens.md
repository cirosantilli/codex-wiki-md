# Thin lens

↑ **Parent:** [Lens (optics)](lens-optics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thin_lens)

A thin lens is modeled with negligible thickness relative to its object and image distances. For real conjugate distances $u,v>0$, it obeys $1/u+1/v=1/f$, with [focal length](focal-length.md) $f$.
