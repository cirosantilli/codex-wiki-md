# Series RLC circuit

↑ **Parent:** [RLC circuit](rlc-circuit.md)

In a series RLC circuit, the same [electric current](electric-current.md) $I=q'$ flows through a resistor, inductor and [capacitor](capacitor.md). Summing their [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) drops gives $Lq''+Rq'+q/C=V$, and differentiating gives

$$
LI''+RI'+I/C=V'.
$$

The homogeneous [damped harmonic oscillator](damped-harmonic-oscillator.md) has angular frequency $\omega_0=1/\sqrt{LC}$ and decay rate $\gamma=R/(2L)$.
