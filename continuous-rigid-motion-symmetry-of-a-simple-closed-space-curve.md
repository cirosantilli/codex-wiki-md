# Continuous rigid-motion symmetry of a simple closed space curve

↑ **Parent:** [Orbit of a one-parameter proper Euclidean motion](orbit-of-a-one-parameter-proper-euclidean-motion.md)

A simple closed curve in $\mathbb R^3$ preserved by a nonconstant smooth family of proper rigid motions is a circle. Differentiating the normalized family at the identity gives a nonzero [infinitesimal proper Euclidean motion](infinitesimal-proper-euclidean-motion.md) tangent to the curve. Compactness rules out its line and nonclosed helical orbits, leaving a circular rotation orbit.
