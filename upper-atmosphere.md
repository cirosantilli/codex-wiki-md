# Upper atmosphere

↑ **Parent:** [Geophysics](geophysics-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Terrestrial atmosphere](terrestrial-atmosphere.md)

The upper part of the [terrestrial atmosphere](terrestrial-atmosphere.md) includes regions where [ionization](ionization.md) makes the response to [electromagnetic waves](electromagnetic-wave-split.md) depend strongly on mobile [electrons](electron.md). The [ionosphere](ionosphere.md) describes that electrical structure; its boundaries need not coincide with the boundaries defined by [temperature](temperature-split.md) in the [mesosphere](mesosphere.md) and [thermosphere](thermosphere.md).
