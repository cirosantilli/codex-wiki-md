# Switch-on drift in a circularly driven parabolic band

↑ **Parent:** [Crystal momentum](crystal-momentum.md)

A charge $-e$ starting at a band minimum when the field $E_0(\sin\omega t,\cos\omega t)$ is applied acquires the displayed [crystal momentum](crystal-momentum.md). Its orbit is a shifted [circle](circle.md). For masses $m_x,m_y$, the real-space [coordinates](coordinate-linear-algebra.md) are proportional to $(\sin\omega t-\omega t)/m_x$ and $(\cos\omega t-1)/m_y$: an elliptic oscillation plus a uniform drift. Omitting the integration constant changes the physical initial condition and reduces the maximum light-axis kinetic energy by a factor of four.
