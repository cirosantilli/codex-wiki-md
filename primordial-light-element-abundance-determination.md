# Primordial light-element abundance determination

↑ **Parent:** [Big Bang nucleosynthesis](big-bang-nucleosynthesis.md)

Primordial abundances require extrapolation to material minimally processed by [stars](star.md). Helium is inferred from hydrogen and helium emission in low-metallicity ionized regions, with ionization and temperature corrections. Deuterium is inferred from isotope-shifted absorption in low-metallicity [quasar](quasar.md) systems. Lithium is measured in old metal-poor stellar atmospheres and requires a correction, or a bound, for stellar depletion. These observables probe different astrophysical environments and systematic errors.
