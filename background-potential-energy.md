# Background potential energy

↑ **Parent:** [Available potential energy](available-potential-energy.md)

The background potential energy is the [gravitational potential energy](gravitational-energy.md) of the statically stable state obtained by adiabatically sorting a fluid's parcels. The difference $A=P-P_b$ is [available potential energy](available-potential-energy.md). Pure rearrangement changes $A$ but not $P_b$; irreversible density mixing can increase $P_b$. Molecular exchange with [internal energy](internal-energy.md) must be separated when defining an irreversible mixing rate.
