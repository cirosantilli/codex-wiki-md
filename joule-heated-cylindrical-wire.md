# Joule-heated cylindrical wire

↑ **Parent:** [Local Joule heating rate](local-joule-heating-rate.md)

A long wire with uniform current [density](density.md) $I/(\pi a^2)$ generates uniform [heat](heat-split.md) per volume $H=I^2/(\pi^2a^4\sigma)$. Radial steady conduction obeys $(rT_r)_r=-Hr/k$. Regularity at the centre removes a logarithmic solution, leaving the displayed parabolic [temperature](temperature-split.md) excess relative to the surface. Conductivity, [electrical conductivity](electrical-conductivity.md) and current [density](density.md) are assumed constant.
