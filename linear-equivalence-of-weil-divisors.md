# Linear equivalence of Weil divisors

↑ **Parent:** [Weil divisor](weil-divisor.md)

Two [Weil divisors](weil-divisor.md) are linearly equivalent when their difference is a [principal Weil divisor](principal-weil-divisor.md). This is the equivalence relation defining the [divisor class group](divisor-class-group.md), and applies to normal varieties of any dimension, not just curves.
