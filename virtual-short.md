# Virtual short

↑ **Parent:** [Operational amplifier](operational-amplifier.md)

Under stable [negative feedback](negative-feedback.md) and within its linear operating range, an ideal voltage-feedback [operational amplifier](operational-amplifier.md) has equal input voltages. This relation is a consequence of large open-loop gain, and does not imply current flow between the inputs.
