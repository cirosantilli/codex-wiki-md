# Infinitesimal neighbourhood of a closed subscheme

↑ **Parent:** [Closed subscheme](closed-subscheme.md)

For a [closed subscheme](closed-subscheme.md) defined by an [ideal sheaf](ideal-sheaf-of-a-closed-subscheme.md) $\mathcal I$, its $n$th [infinitesimal neighbourhood](infinitesimal-neighbourhood-of-a-closed-subscheme.md) has structure sheaf $\mathcal O_X/\mathcal I^{n+1}$. All these neighbourhoods have the same underlying topological space because the radicals of $\mathcal I$ and its positive powers agree. Their [inverse limit](inverse-limit.md) of structure sheaves is the [formal completion of a scheme](formal-completion-of-a-scheme.md).
