# Single-mode optical fiber

↑ **Parent:** [Optical fiber](optical-fiber.md)

A [single-mode optical fiber](single-mode-optical-fiber.md) supports only its fundamental spatial guided [normal mode](normal-mode.md) at the operating [wavelength](wavelength.md), with possible distinct states of [polarization of an electromagnetic wave](polarization-of-an-electromagnetic-wave.md). The designation must include [wavelength](wavelength.md): MIT's fibre laboratory explicitly notes that a fibre sold for single-mode operation can support several [normal modes](normal-mode.md) at its [helium](helium.md)–[neon](neon.md) [laser](laser-split.md) [wavelength](wavelength.md).[Optical Waveguides and Fibers, MIT 6.161 Modern Optics Project Laboratory, Fall 2005, Laboratory Exercise No. 7, p. 8, §7.4(a): operating-wavelength qualification](optical-waveguides-and-fibers-mit-6-161-laboratory-exercise.md)
