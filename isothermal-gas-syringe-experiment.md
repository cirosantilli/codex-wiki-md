# Isothermal gas syringe experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Isothermal compression](isothermal-compression.md)

A [gas syringe](gas-syringe.md) makes the distinction between a constant-[temperature](temperature-split.md) path and equal-[temperature](temperature-split.md) endpoints observable. The [Ideal Gas Law Apparatus (TD-8596A), Instruction Manual 012-08420B by PASCO scientific](ideal-gas-law-apparatus-td-8596a-instruction-manual-012-08420b-by-pasco-scientific.md) specifies a TD-8596A [gas syringe](gas-syringe.md) with a mechanical stop, an internal thermistor, an attached [pressure](pressure-split.md) sensor and data acquisition. Record the stop [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) with the coupling disconnected, then set $40\,\mathrm{mL}$ and seal the coupling. Record [pressure](pressure-split.md) and [temperature](temperature-split.md), compress quickly to the stop near $20\,\mathrm{mL}$, and hold until both readouts settle. The manual gives roughly $30\,\mathrm s$ for settling and about $0.5\,\mathrm s$ for the thermistor response. Releasing the plunger gives a second transient and a final [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) to record.[pp. 2–4: Introduction, Equipment Setup and Experiment 1 procedure](ideal-gas-law-apparatus-td-8596a-instruction-manual-012-08420b-by-pasco-scientific.md)

The manual describes a [pressure](pressure-split.md) overshoot and [temperature](temperature-split.md) rise during rapid compression, followed by cooling at the held [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md); the settled [pressure](pressure-split.md) remains higher than initially. This illustrates approximately [adiabatic compression](adiabatic-compression.md) followed by [heat](heat-split.md) rejection, rather than an [isothermal compression](isothermal-compression.md) throughout. Use the settled initial and compressed readings to test [Boyle's law](boyle-s-law.md), correcting both syringe readings by the unmarked tubing [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) $V_d$:

$$
p_i(V_{\rm read,i}+V_d)=p_f(V_{\rm read,f}+V_d).
$$

Use [absolute pressure](absolute-pressure.md), not [gauge pressure](gauge-pressure.md). A transient comparison of $pV/T$ must pair simultaneous [pressure](pressure-split.md) and [temperature](temperature-split.md) readings: the sensor lag can separate their peaks.[pp. 4–5: Constant Temperature and Varying Temperature analyses; p. 8: answers 1–3](ideal-gas-law-apparatus-td-8596a-instruction-manual-012-08420b-by-pasco-scientific.md)

For an additional test of the path, repeat with small compression steps, wait for stable [temperature](temperature-split.md) and [pressure](pressure-split.md) at every step, and repeat in reverse. Keep the sealed [amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md), connecting tubing and ambient [temperature](temperature-split.md) fixed; prevent hand warming of the barrel and check for leaks by watching for continued [pressure](pressure-split.md) drift at held [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) after [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium-split.md). These are proposed controls, rather than reported measurements. Agreement of equilibrated points with [Boyle's law](boyle-s-law.md) supports the fixed-[temperature](temperature-split.md) [ideal gas law](ideal-gas-law.md) approximation, but does not prove that the rapid segments between them were [isothermal processes](isothermal-process.md). A single local [temperature](temperature-split.md) sensor can miss [temperature gradients](temperature-gradient.md); plunger [friction](friction.md) can produce direction-dependent readings. The [pressure](pressure-split.md) and [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) data estimate reversible [work](work.md) only insofar as the path approaches a [reversible thermodynamic process](reversible-thermodynamic-process.md); they do not directly measure [heat](heat-split.md). After motion stops, cooling at fixed [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) has zero boundary [work](work.md) and $Q=\Delta U<0$, even though the complete equal-endpoint-[temperature](temperature-split.md) [ideal gas](ideal-gas-split.md) run has $\Delta U=0$.
