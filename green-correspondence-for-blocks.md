# Green correspondence for blocks

↑ **Parent:** [Green correspondence](green-correspondence.md)

If $N_G(D)\le H\le G$, [Brauer correspondence](brauer-correspondence.md) pairs the [blocks of a group algebra](block-of-a-group-algebra.md) of $RG$ and $RH$ having [defect group of a block](defect-group-of-a-block.md) $D$. This is [Green correspondence](green-correspondence.md) for their [bimodules](bimodule.md), with [vertex of an indecomposable module](vertex-of-an-indecomposable-module.md) $\Delta D$ in $G\times G$. The normalizer of $\Delta D$ lies in $H\times H$, and the corresponding restriction and induction have unique summands with that vertex.
