# Cube-full number

↑ **Parent:** [Number theory](number-theory-split.md)

A cube-full number is a [positive integer](positive-integer.md) in which every occurring [prime factor](prime-factor.md) has exponent at least three. The number $1$ is included by the empty-product convention.
