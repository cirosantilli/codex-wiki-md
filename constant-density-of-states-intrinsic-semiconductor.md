# Constant-density-of-states intrinsic semiconductor

↑ **Parent:** [Intrinsic semiconductor](intrinsic-semiconductor.md)

In a symmetric model with constant [density of states](density-of-states.md) $D$ per volume and energy in two broad bands, [charge neutrality](charge-neutrality.md) gives $\mu=(E_c+E_v)/2$ and $n=p=Dk_BT\ln[1+e^{-E_g/(2k_BT)}]$. In the nondegenerate limit, $n\simeq Dk_BT e^{-E_g/(2k_BT)}$. Thus the leading exponential activation law can coexist with an algebraic temperature prefactor.
