# Classical mechanics

↑ **Parent:** [Branches of physics](branches-of-physics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classical_mechanics)

[Classical mechanics](classical-mechanics-split.md) describes the motion of bodies by assigning [positions](position.md) and [velocities](velocity.md) and specifying how their interactions change those quantities. A [point mass](point-mass.md) is the simplest model: its size and internal structure are ignored, leaving a [mass](mass.md) and a moving [position](position.md). A [rigid body](rigid-body-dynamics-split.md) retains orientation and [rotational kinetic energy](rotational-kinetic-energy.md). Choosing a model means deciding which features affect the question being asked; a sliding block may be treated as a [point mass](point-mass.md), whereas predicting its [rolling without slipping](rolling-without-slipping.md) requires its [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md).

Three complementary questions organize the subject. [Newton's second law](newton-s-second-law.md) asks how the net [force](force-split.md) determines the instantaneous [acceleration](acceleration.md). The [work-energy theorem](work-energy-theorem.md) asks how that [force](force-split.md), acting through a [displacement](displacement.md), changes [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md). [Conservation of mechanical energy](conservation-of-mechanical-energy.md) asks which combinations of [position](position.md) and [velocity](velocity.md) are accessible when the interactions admit a time-independent [potential energy](potential-energy.md) and no other [force](force-split.md) supplies net [power](power.md). These descriptions are compatible, but answer different questions: an [energy](energy-split.md) calculation may find a maximum [speed](speed.md) without first solving for [position](position.md) at every [time](time-in-physics.md).

The [harmonic oscillator](simple-harmonic-motion.md) below makes this unity visible. A restoring [force](force-split.md) produces a repeating [position](position.md) trace; the corresponding [phase plane](phase-plane.md) has a closed [ellipse](ellipse.md); [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) and [potential energy](potential-energy.md) exchange while their sum stays fixed. [Lagrangian mechanics](lagrangian-mechanics-split.md) and [Hamiltonian mechanics](hamiltonian-mechanics-split.md) provide further ways to organize the same dynamics, especially when coordinates or constraints make individual [forces](force-split.md) awkward to track.

The scope of [classical mechanics](classical-mechanics-split.md) also matters. [Newtonian mechanics](newtonian-mechanics-split.md) approximates motion at [speeds](speed.md) small compared with the [speed of light](speed-of-light.md); [special relativity](special-relativity-split.md) changes that description when this approximation fails. [Quantum mechanics](quantum-mechanics-split.md) is needed for phenomena for which a classical assignment of simultaneous [position](position.md) and [momentum](momentum-split.md) is inadequate. These limitations do not prevent [classical mechanics](classical-mechanics-split.md) from being a useful predictive model within its regime.
