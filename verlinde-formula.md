# Verlinde formula

↑ **Parent:** [Modular S-matrix](modular-s-matrix.md)

The simultaneous diagonalization formula for the [anyon fusion matrices](anyon-fusion-matrix.md) of a [modular tensor category](modular-tensor-category.md). The [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) of $N_b$ in column $x$ of the [modular S-matrix](modular-s-matrix.md) is $S_{bx}/S_{1x}$.
