# One-loop running of the strong coupling

↑ **Parent:** [Running coupling](running-coupling.md)

With $\beta_0>0$, differentiating $\alpha_s=g^2/(4\pi)$ under $\mu\,dg/d\mu=-\beta_0g^3/(16\pi^2)$ gives $d(1/\alpha_s)/d\ln\mu=\beta_0/(2\pi)$. Integration defines the [strong-coupling scale](strong-coupling-scale.md) $\Lambda$ and gives the displayed positive branch for $\mu>\Lambda$. The pole signals breakdown of [perturbative quantum field theory](perturbative-quantum-field-theory-split.md). The equivalence between specifying $\alpha_s$ and specifying $\Lambda$ is [dimensional transmutation](dimensional-transmutation.md).
