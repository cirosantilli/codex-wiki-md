# Principal indecomposable module

↑ **Parent:** [Projective module](projective-module.md)

For a finite-dimensional [algebra](algebra-split.md) $A$, a principal indecomposable module is an indecomposable direct summand of the left regular [module](module-mathematics.md), equivalently $Ae$ for a [primitive idempotent](primitive-idempotent.md) $e$. Its [head of a module](head-of-a-module.md) is simple, and it is the [projective cover](projective-cover.md) of that simple module.
