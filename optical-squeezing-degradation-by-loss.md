# Optical squeezing degradation by loss

↑ **Parent:** [Quadrature squeezing](quadrature-squeezing.md)

Mixing a mode with independent vacuum on a [beam splitter](beam-splitter.md) of [intensity](intensity-physics.md) transmission $\eta$ gives the displayed [optical quadrature](optical-quadrature.md) [variance](variance-split.md) when vacuum [variance](variance-split.md) is $1/4$. It approaches vacuum noise as transmission falls. Phase noise instead mixes squeezed and anti-squeezed axes, and cannot in general be represented by the same efficiency alone.
