<h1 id="rational-quadratic-bezier-curve">Rational quadratic Bézier curve</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Bézier curve](bezier-curve.md)

A weighted quadratic [Bézier curve](bezier-curve.md) with positive middle weight $w$ retains endpoint [interpolation](interpolation-split.md) and endpoint tangent directions while changing the bulge. For a [circular arc](circular-arc.md) of angle $\theta$ less than $180^\circ$, choose endpoints on the [circle](circle.md), their tangent intersection as the middle [control point](control-point.md), and $w=\cos(\theta/2)$. Unlike an ordinary nonconstant polynomial [Bézier curve](bezier-curve.md), this rational representation can describe a [circular arc](circular-arc.md) exactly.
