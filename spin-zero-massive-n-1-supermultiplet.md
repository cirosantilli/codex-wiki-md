<h1 id="spin-zero-massive-n-1-supermultiplet">Spin-zero massive N=1 supermultiplet</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Massive supermultiplet](massive-supermultiplet.md)

At rest the two complex [supercharges](supersymmetry-generator.md) have [anticommutators](anticommutator.md) $2m\delta_{ab}$. A normalized scalar [Clifford vacuum](clifford-vacuum.md) generates four states at occupation levels $0,1,2$: two spin-zero bosonic states and one spin-half pair. Products of three creation charges vanish. The one-creation normalization is $(2m)^{-1/2}$ and the two-creation normalization is $(2m)^{-1}$. The resulting on-shell content is a [complex scalar](complex-scalar-field.md) and a massive [fermion](fermion.md) with equal masses.
