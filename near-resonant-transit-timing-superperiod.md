# Near-resonant transit-timing superperiod

↑ **Parent:** [Transit-timing variation](transit-timing-variation.md)

For two nearly circular [Kepler orbits](kepler-orbit.md) near a first-order $(j+1):j$ period ratio, the slow [resonant argument](resonant-argument.md) circulates on the displayed superperiod when [longitude of periapsis](longitude-of-periapsis.md) precession is negligible. If $P_2/P_1=((j+1)/j)(1+\Delta)$, then $P_t=P_1(1+\Delta)/(j|\Delta|)$. Within [resonant-argument libration](resonant-argument-libration.md), this circulation formula is replaced by an amplitude-dependent libration period.
