# Primordial deuterium as a baryon-density indicator

↑ **Parent:** [Big Bang nucleosynthesis](big-bang-nucleosynthesis.md)

For the standard [Big Bang nucleosynthesis](big-bang-nucleosynthesis.md) parameter branch, a larger [baryon-to-photon ratio](baryon-to-photon-ratio.md) increases the efficiency of nuclear reactions burning [deuterium](deuterium.md) into heavier nuclei, reducing residual primordial D/H. Thus a smaller inferred primordial abundance implies a higher [cosmological baryon density](cosmological-baryon-density.md) when other model parameters are fixed. [Deuterium](deuterium.md) destroyed later by stellar processing must be distinguished from this primordial abundance. Coupling the inference to the [cluster baryon-fraction estimate of matter density](cluster-baryon-fraction-estimate-of-matter-density.md) raises $\Omega_m$ at fixed cluster baryon fraction.
