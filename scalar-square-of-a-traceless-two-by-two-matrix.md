# Scalar square of a traceless two-by-two matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix trace](matrix-trace.md)

A two-by-two [matrix](matrix.md) $M$ with zero [trace](matrix-trace.md) satisfies $M^2=-(\det M)I$ by the [Cayley-Hamilton theorem](cayley-hamilton-theorem.md). Over the [complex numbers](complex-number.md), either $\det M\ne0$ and its two distinct [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) make it diagonalizable, or $M^2=0$. A matrix commutator has zero trace, so this alternative applies to it. Diagonalization over the real numbers need not follow when $-\det M<0$.
