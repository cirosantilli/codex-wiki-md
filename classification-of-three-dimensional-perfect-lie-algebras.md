# Classification of three-dimensional perfect Lie algebras

↑ **Parent:** [Perfect Lie algebra](perfect-lie-algebra.md)

Over an [algebraically closed field](algebraically-closed-field.md) of [characteristic zero](characteristic-zero.md), every three-dimensional [perfect Lie algebra](perfect-lie-algebra.md) is isomorphic to the [sl2 Lie algebra](sl2-lie-algebra.md). Indeed the [trace of a commutator](trace-of-a-commutator.md) gives $\operatorname{tr}(\operatorname{ad}x)=0$ for all $x$, because each element is a sum of [Lie brackets](lie-bracket.md). If every $\operatorname{ad}x$ were a [nilpotent linear map](nilpotent-linear-map.md), the [Engel theorem](engel-s-theorem.md) would make the [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) nilpotent, contradicting its constant nonzero [lower central series of a Lie algebra](lower-central-series-of-a-lie-algebra.md). Some $\operatorname{ad}x$ therefore has a nonzero [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) $\lambda$. Its other [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are $0,-\lambda$, since it kills $x$ and has zero [trace](matrix-trace.md). They are distinct, so its [eigenvectors](eigenvector.md) give a [basis](basis.md) $h,e,f$ after scaling $x$ to $h=2x/\lambda$, with $[h,e]=2e$ and $[h,f]=-2f$. The [Jacobi identity](jacobi-identity.md) puts $[e,f]$ in the zero [eigenspace](eigenspace.md) $kh$. Its coefficient cannot vanish, since otherwise the [derived algebra](derived-algebra.md) would lie in $ke\oplus kf$. Rescaling $f$ gives $[e,f]=h$. Thus this [basis](basis.md) is an [sl2 triple](sl2-triple.md), proving the claimed [Lie algebra isomorphism](lie-algebra-isomorphism.md).
