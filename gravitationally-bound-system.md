# Gravitationally bound system

↑ **Parent:** [Celestial mechanics](celestial-mechanics-split.md)

A system whose mutual [gravity](gravity.md) confines its constituents rather than letting them separate indefinitely. In an isolated [two-body problem](two-body-problem.md), the relative [specific orbital energy](specific-orbital-energy.md) $\varepsilon=v^2/2-G(M_1+M_2)/r$ is negative exactly for bound, noncollisional [Kepler orbits](kepler-orbit.md); $\varepsilon=0$ is the marginal escape case. External forces, collisions and time-dependent potentials require additional qualifications. The separation of a [gravitationally bound system](gravitationally-bound-system.md) is determined by its equations of motion and need not follow the cosmological [Hubble flow](hubble-flow.md).
