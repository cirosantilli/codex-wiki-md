# Potential scale reduction factor

↑ **Parent:** [Markov chain Monte Carlo convergence diagnostics](markov-chain-monte-carlo-convergence-diagnostics.md)

The [potential scale reduction factor](potential-scale-reduction-factor.md) compares variation within simulated chains to a variance estimate also incorporating differences between chains. In its classical form, $\widehat R=\sqrt{\widehat V/W}$, where $W$ is the average within-chain [sample variance](sample-variance.md). Values appreciably above one indicate disagreement; values close to one do not prove convergence. Splitting chains helps expose changes over time, and modern rank-based variants improve robustness.[Posterior Analysis, “Potential scale reduction”, “Split R-hat for detecting non-stationarity”, and “Rank normalization helps when there are heavy tails”](stan-reference-manual-by-stan-development-team.md)
