# Lebesgue theorem on differentiability of monotone functions

↑ **Parent:** [Monotonic function](monotonic-function.md)

Every real-valued [monotone function](monotonic-function.md) on a [real interval](interval-mathematics.md) is [differentiable](differentiable-function.md) with a finite [derivative](derivative.md) at [almost every](almost-everywhere.md) point.

This strengthens the regularity known for a [monotonic function](monotonic-function.md) beyond the restriction on its jump discontinuities.
