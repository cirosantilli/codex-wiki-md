# Wet cupping

↑ **Parent:** [Bloodletting](bloodletting.md)

Use of suction cups on incised [skin](skin.md) to remove [blood](blood.md). [Celsus](aulus-cornelius-celsus.md) describes bronze and horn cups and distinguishes this from [dry cupping](dry-cupping.md). [II.11.1–6](on-medicine-by-aulus-cornelius-celsus.md)
