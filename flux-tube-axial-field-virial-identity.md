# Flux-tube axial field virial identity

↑ **Parent:** [Cylindrical magnetostatic pressure balance](cylindrical-magnetostatic-pressure-balance.md)

For a regular cylindrical [flux tube](flux-tube.md) with $\mathbf B=(0,B_\phi(s),B_z(s))$, vanishing field beyond radius $s_0$, and exterior [pressure](pressure.md) $p_e$, define $\langle h\rangle=2s_0^{-2}\int_0^{s_0}sh(s)\,ds$ and $B_p^2=2\mu_0p_e$. Integrating [cylindrical magnetostatic pressure balance](cylindrical-magnetostatic-pressure-balance.md) against $s^2$ gives the displayed identity. The toroidal [magnetic pressure](magnetic-pressure.md) and hoop [magnetic tension](magnetic-tension.md) cancel in this cross-sectional axial-field average. Nonnegative gas [pressure](pressure.md) gives $\langle B_z^2\rangle\leq B_p^2$, with strict inequality precisely when $\langle p\rangle>0$.
