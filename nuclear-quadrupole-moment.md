# Nuclear quadrupole moment

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear spin](nuclear-spin.md)

The [electric quadrupole moment](electric-quadrupole-moment.md) of an [atomic nucleus](atomic-nucleus-split.md), conventionally specified in its stretched [nuclear spin](nuclear-spin.md) state. It measures a nonspherical nuclear charge distribution. Its interaction with the electronic electric-field gradient contributes to [hyperfine structure](hyperfine-structure.md) and generally changes the magnetic-dipole [Landé interval rule](lande-interval-rule.md). A permanent rank-two moment vanishes for [nuclear spin](nuclear-spin.md) less than one.
