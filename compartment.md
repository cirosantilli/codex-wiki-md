# Compartment

↑ **Parent:** [Compartmental models (epidemiology)](compartmental-models-epidemiology.md)

A [compartment](compartment.md) is one of the state categories in a [compartmental model](compartmental-models-epidemiology.md). Its state variable measures the number or amount in that category, and transfer terms record exchanges between compartments.
