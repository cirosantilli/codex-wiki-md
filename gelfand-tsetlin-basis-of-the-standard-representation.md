<h1 id="gelfand-tsetlin-basis-of-the-standard-representation">Gelfand–Tsetlin basis of the standard representation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Standard representation of the symmetric group](standard-representation-of-the-symmetric-group.md)

For the sum-zero [standard representation of the symmetric group](standard-representation-of-the-symmetric-group.md), the vectors $v_j=e_1+\cdots+e_j-je_{j+1}$ form a [Gelfand–Tsetlin basis](gelfand-tsetlin-basis.md). The [Young–Jucys–Murphy element](jucys-murphy-element.md) $X_i$ has eigenvalue $i-1$ for $i\leq j$, $-1$ for $i=j+1$, and $i-2$ for $i>j+1$. Normalizing by $\sqrt{j(j+1)}$ gives an [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) corresponding to tableaux of shape $(n-1,1)$ whose second-row entry is $j+1$.
