# Mass-action model of micellization

↑ **Parent:** [Critical micelle concentration](critical-micelle-concentration.md)

For equilibrium association of $q$ isolated [amphiphiles](amphiphile.md) into one [micelle](micelle.md), equality of [chemical potentials](chemical-potential-split.md) gives the displayed relation between dimensionless [thermodynamic activities](thermodynamic-activity.md). Large [aggregation number](aggregation-number.md) makes aggregate abundance rise rapidly with monomer activity. A finite-aggregate model gives a concentration crossover, rather than an exactly discontinuous thermodynamic transition.
