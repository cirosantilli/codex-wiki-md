# Voter-model duality

↑ **Parent:** [Voter model](voter-model.md)

Independent [Poisson process](poisson-process.md) copying arrows construct the [voter model](voter-model.md). A backward ancestral line from a site follows a [continuous-time random walk](continuous-time-random-walk.md); lines that meet then coincide. Thus a finite collection of [Ising spins](ising-spin-variable.md) at time $t$ equals initial [Ising spins](ising-spin-variable.md) at the positions of [coalescing random walks](coalescing-random-walks.md). In particular $E_\eta\xi_t(x)=\sum_zp_t(x,z)\eta(z)$, and the [probability](probability.md) of disagreement at two sites is bounded by the [probability](probability.md) their ancestral walks have not yet coalesced.
