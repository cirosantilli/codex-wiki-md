# Gaussian absolute-magnitude Malmquist shift

↑ **Parent:** [Malmquist bias](malmquist-bias.md)

For a uniform Euclidean population, a complete flux-limited sample weights [luminosity](luminosity.md) by accessible volume $L^{3/2}$. Since $L\propto10^{-0.4M}$, an intrinsic [Gaussian](normal-distribution.md) magnitude distribution is multiplied by $e^{-0.6\ln(10)M}$. Completing the square shifts its [mean](expected-value-split.md) by the displayed amount without changing its [variance](variance-split.md). Extra distance bounds, [extinction](extinction.md), evolution and other selection cuts alter this simple result.
