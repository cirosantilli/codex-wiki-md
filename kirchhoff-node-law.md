# Kirchhoff node law

↑ **Parent:** [Current flow](current-flow.md)

The [Kirchhoff node law](kirchhoff-node-law.md) says the sum of outward currents at a [graph vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) is zero unless that [graph vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) is a source or sink. In an [electrical network](electrical-network.md), it is [flow conservation](flow-conservation.md); a [unit flow](unit-flow.md) has divergences $+1$ and $-1$ at its terminals.
