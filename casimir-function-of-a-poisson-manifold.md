# Casimir function of a Poisson manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson manifold](poisson-manifold.md)

A Casimir function $C$ Poisson-commutes with every smooth function, $\{C,f\}=0$. Its regular level sets are unions of [symplectic leaves](symplectic-leaf.md).
