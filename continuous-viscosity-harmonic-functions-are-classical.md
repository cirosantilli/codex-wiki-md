# Continuous viscosity harmonic functions are classical

↑ **Parent:** [Viscosity solution](viscosity-solution.md)

Let a continuous function satisfy both viscosity inequalities for the [Laplace operator](laplace-operator.md). On any relatively compact ball, take its [harmonic replacement](harmonic-replacement.md) $h$ with matching boundary values. If $u-h$ has a positive maximum, the function $h+\varepsilon(R^2-|x-a|^2)$ still lies below $u$ somewhere for sufficiently small positive $\varepsilon$, while matching it on the boundary. At an interior maximum of their difference it is a valid upper test, but its Laplacian is $-2n\varepsilon<0$, a contradiction. The opposite perturbation excludes a negative minimum. Thus $u=h$ throughout the ball and is a smooth [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md). A locally defined test is extended near its touching point using a smooth [cutoff function](cutoff-function.md) when global tests are required.
