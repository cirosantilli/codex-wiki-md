# Subtree

↑ **Parent:** [Binary tree](binary-tree.md)

For a [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) $u$ in a [rooted tree](rooted-tree.md), its [subtree](subtree.md) contains $u$ and every [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) reachable by repeatedly following [children in a rooted tree](child-in-a-rooted-tree.md), together with the connecting [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md); its [root of a tree](root-of-a-tree.md) is $u$. In a [binary tree](binary-tree.md), the left [subtree](subtree.md) is rooted at the left child, and is empty when that child is absent; the right [subtree](subtree.md) is defined similarly. A [binary search tree](binary-search-tree.md) constrains every key in these [subtrees](subtree.md).
