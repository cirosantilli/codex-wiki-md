# Crystallographic restriction theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Point group](point-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crystallographic_restriction_theorem)

A rotation preserving a rank-two [lattice](lattice.md) has order $1,2,3,4$ or $6$. In a lattice basis its matrix is integral, while its trace is $2\cos\theta$. The only integral values in $[-2,2]$ are $-2,-1,0,1,2$, which give precisely those rotation orders. Finiteness of a symmetry group alone would not impose this restriction.
