# Phlogiston theory

↑ **Parent:** [History of chemistry](history-of-chemistry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phlogiston_theory)

A historical account in which burning and related processes involved the release or transfer of a supposed principle, [phlogiston](phlogiston.md). Its interpretation of metallic calces differed from the later account of [oxidation](oxidation.md).
