# Alexander polynomial realization theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Alexander polynomial](alexander-polynomial.md)

Every Laurent polynomial $f(t)\in\mathbb Z[t^{\pm1}]$ satisfying $f(t^{-1})=f(t)$ and $f(1)=1$ is the [Alexander polynomial of a knot](alexander-polynomial.md) up to multiplication by a unit $\pm t^k$.
