# Low-frequency decomposition of the wave propagator

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier transform method for the wave equation](fourier-transform-method-for-the-wave-equation.md)

For zero initial displacement and unit delta initial velocity, the spatial [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) of the [wave equation](wave-equation-split.md) gives the displayed multiplier. A compact [cutoff function](cutoff-function.md) around zero frequency produces an ordinary smooth function after inversion. The remaining sine splits into two [oscillatory integrals](oscillatory-integral.md) with phases $x\cdot\xi\pm ct|\xi|$ and symbols proportional to $(1-\chi)/|\xi|$ of order $-1$. Their stationary directions lie on $|x|=c|t|$, proving a light-cone bound for [singular support](singular-support.md). This does not claim vanishing of smooth tails inside the cone.
