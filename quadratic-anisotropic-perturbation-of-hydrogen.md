# Quadratic anisotropic perturbation of hydrogen

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrogen atom](hydrogen-atom.md)

An axially symmetric quadratic [potential energy](potential-energy.md) perturbation preserves [parity](parity.md) and the $z$ component of [orbital angular momentum](orbital-angular-momentum.md). For $1s,2s,2p_0,2p_{\pm1}$, its first-order [energy](energy-split.md) shifts are respectively $(2\alpha+\beta)a_0^2$, $14(2\alpha+\beta)a_0^2$, $(12\alpha+18\beta)a_0^2$, and $(24\alpha+6\beta)a_0^2$. They follow by multiplying the [hydrogen radial second moment](hydrogen-radial-second-moment.md) by the angular average of $\alpha+(\beta-\alpha)\cos^2\theta$, whose $\cos^2\theta$ averages are $1/3,1/3,3/5,1/5$. Negative $\beta$ makes the globally extended potential unbounded below; the shifts then describe local perturbative [resonance poles](resonance-pole.md) or a potential stabilized at large distance.
