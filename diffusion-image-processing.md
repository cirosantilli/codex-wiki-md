# Diffusion image processing

↑ **Parent:** [Image processing](image-processing.md)

Diffusion filtering evolves an observed [image signal](image-signal.md) using a [diffusion equation](diffusion-equation-split.md). The [heat equation](heat-equation.md) gives Gaussian [image smoothing](image-smoothing.md), while gradient-dependent or tensor-dependent diffusion can reduce transport across [image edges](image-edge.md). A [image smoothing](image-smoothing.md) scale, stopping rule and appropriate boundary conditions are part of the filter.
