# Conic optimization

↑ **Parent:** [Convex optimization](convex-optimization-split.md)

Optimization of a [linear function](linear-function.md) subject to affine constraints and membership in a [closed convex cone](closed-convex-cone.md). Choosing the [positive semidefinite cone](positive-semidefinite-cone.md) gives [semidefinite programming](semidefinite-programming.md); other choices include [copositive optimization](copositive-optimization.md) and [completely positive optimization](completely-positive-optimization.md). [Dual cones](dual-cone.md) produce scalar-product bounds on feasible objectives.
