# Mixed partial derivative

↑ **Parent:** [Partial derivative](partial-derivative.md)

A mixed partial derivative takes [partial derivatives](partial-derivative.md) in different variables, for example $f_{xy}=\partial_y(\partial_x f)$. If both second [partial derivatives](partial-derivative.md) are continuous in a neighbourhood, their order can be interchanged: $f_{xy}=f_{yx}$. The [chain rule](chain-rule.md) transforms mixed partial derivatives under a linear [change of variables](change-of-variables-formula.md) by composing the corresponding first-order differential operators.
