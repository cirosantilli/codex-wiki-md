# Purcell nuclear resonance absorption experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear magnetic resonance](nuclear-magnetic-resonance-split.md)

[Edward Mills Purcell](edward-mills-purcell.md), [Henry Cutler Torrey](henry-cutler-torrey.md), and [Robert Vivian Pound](robert-vivian-pound.md) placed [paraffin wax](paraffin-wax.md) in a capacitively loaded coaxial cavity near 30 MHz between magnet poles. Coupling loops supplied and extracted power; oscillating and steady fields were perpendicular. Output balanced against a generator reference fed amplification, detection and a microammeter. A slow field sweep revealed absorption above fluctuations at room [temperature](temperature-split.md). Weak drive avoided population equalization. Calibration was rough; field inhomogeneity could contribute to linewidth.[pp. 37–38, apparatus and results; weak drive, balanced cavity output, microammeter, field sweep, calibration and linewidth limitations](resonance-absorption-by-nuclear-magnetic-moments-in-a-solid-by-edward-mills-purcell-and-others.md)

These are driven experiments with continuous radiofrequency excitation and changing static field, not demonstrations of modern pulsed [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md) [nuclear magnetic resonance](nuclear-magnetic-resonance-split.md). Bloch's theoretical paper explicitly distinguishes proposed free precession after a pulse from the forced precession actually studied.[pp. 460–461, introduction: proposed pulse/free-precession experiment versus performed forced-precession experiment](nuclear-induction-by-felix-bloch.md)

An experimental failure need not mean absent [nuclear magnetic moments](nuclear-magnetic-moment.md). In the model below, excessive drive reduces the population imbalance through [nuclear magnetic resonance saturation](nuclear-magnetic-resonance-saturation.md); waiting too briefly can leave insufficient polarization. Separately, ordinary electrical losses can cause heating without identifying a nuclear resonance. The absorption experiment measured a balanced electrical [signal](signal.md), rather than using heating as its readout. Useful diagnostic checks include moving off resonance, changing the frequency, checking field uniformity and reducing drive. These are general checks; the historical operations actually documented are specified above.
