# Rotational deflection of a falling body

↑ **Parent:** [Free fall](free-fall.md)

In an Earth-fixed [rotating reference frame](rotating-reference-frame.md), the horizontal displacement of a freely falling body receives contributions from [Coriolis acceleration](coriolis-acceleration.md) and [centrifugal acceleration](centrifugal-acceleration.md). A short-fall approximation substitutes the unperturbed downward [velocity](velocity.md) into the Coriolis term and the Earth's surface [position](position.md) into the centrifugal term. If [gravitational acceleration](gravitational-acceleration.md) excludes centrifugal acceleration, both contributions must be included explicitly.
