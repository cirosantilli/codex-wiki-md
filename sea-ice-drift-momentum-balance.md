# Sea-ice drift momentum balance

↑ **Parent:** [Sea ice](sea-ice.md)

The horizontal [sea-ice drift momentum balance](sea-ice-drift-momentum-balance.md) per unit area includes air and water [drag forces](drag-physics.md), the [Coriolis force](coriolis-force.md), depth-integrated internal ice stress and the sea-surface pressure gradient. Here $m$ is ice-plus-snow mass per area and $\Sigma$ is a depth-integrated [stress tensor](cauchy-stress-tensor.md).
