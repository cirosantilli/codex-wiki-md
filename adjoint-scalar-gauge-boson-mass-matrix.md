# Adjoint scalar gauge-boson mass matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Gauge-boson mass matrix](gauge-boson-mass-matrix.md)

For a Hermitian [adjoint scalar field](adjoint-scalar-field.md) with kinetic term $\operatorname{tr}(D_\mu\Phi(D^\mu\Phi)^\dagger)$, use $D_\mu\Phi=\partial_\mu\Phi+ig[A_\mu,\Phi]$ and generators normalized by $\operatorname{tr}(t^at^b)=\delta^{ab}/2$. Expanding at a constant [vacuum expectation value](vacuum-expectation-value.md) $\Phi_0$ and matching $\mathcal L_{\rm mass}=A_\mu^a(m^2)^{ab}A^{b\mu}/2$ gives the displayed expression. A real combination of generators $X=x_at^a$ has quadratic form $2g^2\operatorname{tr}([X,\Phi_0]^\dagger[X,\Phi_0])\ge0$. Thus the massless directions are exactly the generators commuting with the vacuum.

For $\Phi_0=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_n)$, the two Hermitian generators associated with each [matrix unit](matrix-unit.md) pair $E_{ij},E_{ji}$ have squared [mass](mass.md) $g^2(\lambda_i-\lambda_j)^2$. The surviving group is the [centralizer of a Hermitian matrix in SU(n)](centralizer-of-a-hermitian-matrix-in-su-n.md).
