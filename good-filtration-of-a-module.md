# Good filtration of a module

↑ **Parent:** [Filtration of a module](filtration-of-a-module.md)

For a [filtered algebra](filtered-algebra.md) $A$ with finite-dimensional filtration pieces, a [good filtration of a module](good-filtration-of-a-module.md) on a [finitely generated module](finitely-generated-module.md) is compatible with multiplication and has finitely generated [associated graded module](associated-graded-module.md) over the [associated graded ring](associated-graded-ring.md). Taking bounded-degree translates of a finite set of module generators supplies such a [filtration of a module](filtration-of-a-module.md) when the [associated graded ring](associated-graded-ring.md) is [Noetherian](noetherian-ring.md).
