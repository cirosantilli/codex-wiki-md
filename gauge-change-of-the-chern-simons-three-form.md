# Gauge change of the Chern-Simons three-form

↑ **Parent:** [Chern-Simons 3-form](chern-simons-3-form.md)

For an anti-Hermitian $SU(N)$ [connection one-form](connection-one-form.md), normalize the [Chern-Simons three-form](chern-simons-3-form.md) by $1/(8\pi^2)$ and use $A^g=g^{-1}Ag+g^{-1}dg$. Its change is $-d\operatorname{Tr}(dg\,g^{-1}\wedge A)/(8\pi^2)-\operatorname{Tr}(g^{-1}dg)^3/(24\pi^2)$. On a closed three-manifold the first term integrates to zero, while the latter is an integer with the fundamental [matrix trace](matrix-trace.md). Hence the integral is defined modulo integers; integer-level exponentials are invariant under large [Yang-Mills gauge transformations](yang-mills-gauge-transformation.md).
