# Critical angle for total internal reflection

↑ **Parent:** [Total internal reflection](total-internal-reflection.md)

For [refractive indices](refractive-index.md) $n_->n_+$, the [critical angle for total internal reflection](critical-angle-for-total-internal-reflection.md) satisfies $\sin\theta_c=n_+/n_-$. Above it, the transmitted normal [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) is imaginary and the transmitted field is an [evanescent wave](evanescent-wave.md).

The [critical angle for total internal reflection](critical-angle-for-total-internal-reflection.md) is the boundary between outgoing [refraction](refraction.md) and an [evanescent wave](evanescent-wave.md), rather than an [angle](angle.md) at which the external [electric field](electric-field-split.md) abruptly disappears. Write $n_1=n_-$ and $n_2=n_+$. At the threshold, [Snell law for electromagnetic waves](snell-law-for-electromagnetic-waves.md) has $\theta_t=90^\circ$, hence

$$
\theta_c=\arcsin\!\left(\frac{n_2}{n_1}\right),\qquad 0<n_2<n_1.
$$

Here [inverse sine](inverse-sine.md) selects $0<\theta_c<90^\circ$. For $n_1=1.5$ and $n_2=1$, $\sin\theta_c=2/3$ and $\theta_c\approx41.8103149^\circ$. The threshold is independent of [polarization of an electromagnetic wave](polarization-of-an-electromagnetic-wave.md) under the stated assumptions.

A short proof using [phase matching at a planar wave interface](phase-matching-at-a-planar-wave-interface.md) reveals all three cases at once. Choose a [Cartesian coordinate system](cartesian-coordinate-system.md) with boundary $z=0$, incident medium $z<0$, and transmitted medium $z>0$. With [vacuum](vacuum.md) [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) $k_0=2\pi/\lambda_0$, the shared tangential [wavevector](wavevector.md) component is $q=n_1k_0\sin\theta$. The [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation-split.md) in medium two gives

$$
k_{2z}^{\,2}=n_2^2k_0^2-q^2=k_0^2\left(n_2^2-n_1^2\sin^2\theta\right).
$$

Below $\theta_c$, choose the positive real [square root](square-root.md) for an outgoing [plane electromagnetic wave](plane-electromagnetic-wave.md). At $\theta_c$, $k_{2z}=0$: there is a grazing [wave](wave-split.md), with zero normal average [power](power.md) but no exponential confinement in $z$. Above $\theta_c$, choose $k_{2z}=i\kappa$ with $\kappa>0$, so the [wave amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) decays into the second [half-space](half-space.md).

If $n_1\leq n_2$, no nongrazing [angle of incidence](angle-of-incidence.md) produces this threshold. As $n_2/n_1\to1$ from below, $\theta_c\to90^\circ$; as that ratio tends to zero, $\theta_c\to0$. These [limits](limit-of-a-function.md) explain why a larger [refractive index](refractive-index.md) contrast permits confinement over a wider range of internal [angles](angle.md).
