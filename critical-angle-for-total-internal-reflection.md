# Critical angle for total internal reflection

↑ **Parent:** [Total internal reflection](total-internal-reflection.md)

For refractive indices $n_->n_+$, the critical angle satisfies $\sin\theta_c=n_+/n_-$. Above it, the transmitted normal wavenumber is imaginary and the transmitted field is an [evanescent wave](evanescent-wave.md).
