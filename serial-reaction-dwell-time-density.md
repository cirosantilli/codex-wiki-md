# Serial reaction dwell-time density

↑ **Parent:** [Hypoexponential distribution](hypoexponential-distribution.md)

Two sequential independent memoryless reaction waits have a [hypoexponential distribution](hypoexponential-distribution.md). Convolution gives $p(t)=k_1k_2(e^{-k_1t}-e^{-k_2t})/(k_2-k_1)$. Equal rates give the [Erlang distribution](erlang-distribution.md) $k^2te^{-kt}$. A nonzero mode distinguishes this ideal two-event model from a single exponential wait, but does not chemically identify the events.
