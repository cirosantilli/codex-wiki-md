# Linear displacement equations for a magnetized atmosphere

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetohydrodynamic energy principle](magnetohydrodynamic-energy-principle.md)

For a static equilibrium and a [fluid Lagrangian displacement](lagrangian-displacement-fluid-mechanics.md) $\xi$, [conservation of mass](mass-conservation.md), adiabatic [entropy](entropy.md) and [magnetic flux freezing](magnetic-flux-freezing.md) give $\delta p=-\xi\cdot\nabla p-\gamma p\nabla\cdot\xi$ together with the displayed relations. Linearizing the [pressure](pressure.md)/tension form of the momentum equation yields $\rho\ddot\xi=-\nabla\delta\Pi-g\delta\rho e_z+\mu_0^{-1}(\delta B\cdot\nabla B+B\cdot\nabla\delta B)$, where $\delta\Pi=\delta p+B\cdot\delta B/\mu_0$. Static force balance removes the zeroth-order terms; the [density](density.md) multiplying acceleration is the equilibrium [density](density.md).
