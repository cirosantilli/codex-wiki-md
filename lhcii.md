# LHCII

↑ **Parent:** [Photosystem II](photosystem-ii.md)

[LHCII](lhcii.md) is a major light-harvesting antenna complex in plants. A regulated mobile fraction participates in photosynthetic state transitions; antenna redistribution is distinct from simply dissipating excitation.
