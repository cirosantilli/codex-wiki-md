# Coin-doubling martingale

↑ **Parent:** [Martingale convergence theorem](martingale-convergence-theorem.md)

For independent fair Bernoulli variables, this [nonnegative martingale](nonnegative-martingale.md) starts at one, doubles on every success, and becomes zero permanently on the first failure. Its [expectation](expected-value.md) is always one, but it tends to zero [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md) because the probability of success forever is zero. Thus it satisfies the [martingale convergence theorem](martingale-convergence-theorem.md) while failing [convergence in L1](convergence-in-l1.md). This illustrates how rare large values obstruct [uniform integrability](uniform-integrability.md).
