# Charge-conjugate field

↑ **Parent:** [Charge conjugation](charge-conjugation.md)

The charge-conjugate field of a [fermion](fermion.md) field transforms in the complex-conjugate internal [group representation](group-representation.md). With a compatible [Dirac adjoint](dirac-adjoint.md) and [charge conjugation](charge-conjugation.md) matrix convention it is $\psi^c=C\bar\psi^T$, up to a conventional constant phase. In four spacetime dimensions [charge conjugation](charge-conjugation.md) exchanges the [chirality](chirality-physics.md) of the field; a right-handed quark can therefore be represented by a left-handed conjugate field when listing chiral unified matter. In two-dimensional Minkowski spacetime, [charge conjugation](charge-conjugation.md) can instead preserve [chirality](chirality-physics.md), permitting a [Majorana-Weyl spinor](majorana-weyl-spinor.md).
