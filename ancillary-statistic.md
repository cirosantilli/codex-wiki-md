# Ancillary statistic

↑ **Parent:** [Sufficient statistic](sufficient-statistic.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancillary_statistic)

A [statistic](statistic.md) is ancillary for a [statistical model](statistical-model-split.md) if its [probability distribution](probability-distribution.md) is the same for every parameter value. Its marginal law carries no information about the parameter, but its observed value can identify the precision or configuration of the experiment. [Fisherian conditional inference](fisherian-conditional-inference.md) uses the [conditional distribution](conditional-distribution.md) of an informative [statistic](statistic.md) given the observed ancillary value. Ancillarity does not mean that the ancillary is independent of every informative [statistic](statistic.md).
