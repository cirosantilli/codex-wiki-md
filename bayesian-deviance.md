# Bayesian deviance

↑ **Parent:** [Likelihood function](likelihood-function.md)

A [Bayesian deviance](bayesian-deviance.md) is minus twice the [log-likelihood](log-likelihood.md), with any chosen additive data-only constant held consistent across the models being compared. Its [Bayesian posterior](bayesian-posterior.md) expectation measures average fit. In the [deviance information criterion](deviance-information-criterion.md), evaluating it at a parameter's [posterior mean](posterior-mean.md) also enters the effective complexity penalty. This likelihood-based convention differs by a data-only constant from a saturated-model [exponential-family deviance](exponential-family-deviance.md) when a common saturated model exists.
