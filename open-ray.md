# Open ray

↑ **Parent:** [Ray (geometry)](ray-geometry.md)

A [ray](ray-geometry.md) with its endpoint removed. In the [Argand plane](complex-plane.md), fixing the [argument](argument-complex-analysis.md) of $z-z_0$ specifies an open ray because the [argument](argument-complex-analysis.md) of zero is undefined.
