# Correlation-function susceptibility sum rule

↑ **Parent:** [Connected correlation function](connected-correlation-function.md)

When an energy Hamiltonian contains $-h\sum_n\sigma_n$, differentiating its [partition function](canonical-partition-function.md) gives $\chi=(\beta_{\rm th}/N)\operatorname{Var}(\sum_n\sigma_n)$. Translation invariance converts this to the connected-correlation sum. A dimensionless source $\beta_{\rm th}h$ removes the explicit inverse-temperature factor. In continuum physical coordinates the lattice sum includes the site-density factor $a^{-D}$. A selected [pure thermodynamic phase](pure-thermodynamic-phase.md) is needed below an ordered transition to avoid a macroscopic mixture contribution.
