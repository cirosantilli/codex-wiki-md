<h1 id="even-odd-fast-fourier-transform-recursion">Even--odd fast Fourier transform recursion</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Cooley-Tukey FFT algorithm](cooley-tukey-fft-algorithm.md)

For $\omega_m=e^{-2\pi i/m}$ and length-$m/2$ transforms $E_k,O_k$ of the even and odd samples,

$$
Y_k=E_k+\omega_m^kO_k,
\qquad Y_{k+m/2}=E_k-\omega_m^kO_k.
$$
