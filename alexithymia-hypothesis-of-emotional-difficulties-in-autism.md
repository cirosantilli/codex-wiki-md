# Alexithymia hypothesis of emotional difficulties in autism

↑ **Parent:** [Alexithymia](alexithymia.md)

The proposal that some emotion-processing differences attributed to [autism](autism.md) instead track co-occurring [alexithymia](alexithymia.md). It predicts dimensional relationships across diagnostic groups, not that [alexithymia](alexithymia.md) explains every [autistic](autism.md) experience.
