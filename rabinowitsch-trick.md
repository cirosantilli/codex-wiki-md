# Rabinowitsch trick

↑ **Parent:** [Strong Hilbert Nullstellensatz](strong-hilbert-nullstellensatz.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rabinowitsch_trick)

To prove the [Strong Hilbert Nullstellensatz](strong-hilbert-nullstellensatz.md) from its weak form, adjoin a variable $y$ and the equation $1-yf$. If $f$ vanishes on $V(I)$, the ideal $(I,1-yf)$ has no common zero and is therefore the unit ideal. Substituting $y=f^{-1}$ and clearing denominators yields $f^N\in I$ for some $N$.
