# Kolmogorov-Smirnov confidence band

↑ **Parent:** [Confidence band](confidence-band.md)

For independent observations from a continuous distribution function, the [probability integral transform](probability-integral-transform.md) makes the [Kolmogorov-Smirnov statistic](kolmogorov-smirnov-statistic.md) distribution-free. Choose $c_{n,\alpha}$ from its uniform-sample distribution to have probability $1-\alpha$ of not exceeding that critical value. Clipping the empirical distribution plus/minus this value to the unit interval gives simultaneous coverage at every argument. This is a band for the entire distribution, rather than separate pointwise intervals.
