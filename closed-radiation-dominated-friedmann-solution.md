# Closed radiation-dominated Friedmann solution

↑ **Parent:** [Friedmann equations](friedmann-equations.md)

With positive curvature, zero [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md) and radiation density $\rho_0a^{-4}$ in units $8\pi G=1$, $A=\sqrt{\rho_0/3}$. The [scale factor](scale-factor-cosmology.md) is positive for $0<\tau<\pi$, reaches its maximum at $\pi/2$, and has a [Big Crunch](big-crunch.md) at [conformal time](conformal-time.md) $\pi$ or [proper time](proper-time.md) $2A$. This complements the [closed matter-dominated Friedmann solution](closed-matter-dominated-friedmann-solution.md).
