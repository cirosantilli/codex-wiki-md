# General affine group

↑ **Parent:** [Affine primitive permutation group](affine-primitive-permutation-group.md)

The [group](group-split.md) of maps $x\mapsto Ax+b$ of a finite-dimensional [vector space](vector-space-split.md), with $A$ invertible. Identifying the underlying points with a regular [elementary abelian group](elementary-abelian-group.md) identifies its normalizer in the [symmetric group](symmetric-group.md) with this [semidirect product](semidirect-product.md).
