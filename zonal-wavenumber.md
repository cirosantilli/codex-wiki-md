# Zonal wavenumber

↑ **Parent:** [Wavenumber](wavenumber.md)

On a sphere, the integer $s$ counts complete zonal oscillations around a latitude circle. Its local Cartesian [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) at latitude $\phi$ is $k=s/(a\cos\phi)$ for radius $a$. The integer $s$ and the dimensional local [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) $k$ should not be interchanged in a local [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation-split.md) for [Rossby waves](rossby-wave.md).
