# Most populated rotational level

↑ **Parent:** [Rotational state populations](rotational-state-populations.md)

The [rotational state populations](rotational-state-populations.md) of a spin-free polar [linear rigid rotor](linear-rigid-rotor.md) are proportional to $(2J+1)e^{-\theta_{\rm rot}J(J+1)/T}$. Differentiating their logarithm with respect to continuous $J$ gives the displayed maximum when it is nonnegative. The actual maximum must be selected among nonnegative integers by comparing adjacent populations. This population maximum generally differs from a maximum in measured [spectral line](spectral-line.md) intensity.
