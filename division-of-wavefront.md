# Division of wavefront

↑ **Parent:** [Interferometer](interferometer.md)

In [division of wavefront](division-of-wavefront.md), different spatial portions of an incident [wavefront](wavefront.md) are selected and propagated so that their fields overlap. [Double-slit interference](double-slit-interference.md) is an example. The selected portions need sufficient [spatial optical coherence](spatial-optical-coherence.md) for stable [interference fringes](interference-fringe.md).
