# Limits reflected by full and faithful functors

↑ **Parent:** [Limit-reflecting functor](limit-reflecting-functor.md)

A [full and faithful functor](full-and-faithful-functor.md) reflects every existing shape of [categorical limit](categorical-limit.md). If the image of a cone is limiting, apply its universal property to the image of any competing cone. Fullness lifts the unique mediating morphism; faithfulness proves the cone equations and uniqueness of the lift. No essential-surjectivity hypothesis is needed.
