# Structure sheaf of a scheme

↑ **Parent:** [Scheme](scheme.md)

The structure sheaf $\mathcal O_X$ records the [regular functions](regular-function.md) on every open subset of a [scheme](scheme.md) $X$. Its stalk $\mathcal O_{X,x}$ is the [local ring](local-ring.md) of functions defined near $x$.
