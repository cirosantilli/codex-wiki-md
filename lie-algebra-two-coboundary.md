# Lie algebra two-coboundary

↑ **Parent:** [Lie algebra cohomology](lie-algebra-cohomology.md)

For trivial coefficients in a [field](field.md) $k$, a Lie algebra two-coboundary is a [bilinear map](bilinear-map.md) $\beta(x,y)=t([x,y])$ for a [linear functional](linear-functional.md) $t$ in the [dual space](dual-space-split.md) $\mathfrak g^*$. Some conventions put a minus sign in this formula; the resulting [vector subspace](vector-subspace.md) $B^2(\mathfrak g,k)$ is the same. Each such map is a [Lie algebra two-cocycle](lie-algebra-two-cocycle.md): it is an [alternating bilinear form](alternating-bilinear-form.md), and its cyclic expression is $t([[x,y],z]+[[y,z],x]+[[z,x],y])=0$ by the [Jacobi identity](jacobi-identity.md). Replacing a linear section of a [central extension of a Lie algebra](central-extension-of-a-lie-algebra.md) by $s'(x)=s(x)+t(x)z$ changes its [Lie algebra two-cocycle](lie-algebra-two-cocycle.md) by $-\beta$. Thus $H^2(\mathfrak g,k)=Z^2(\mathfrak g,k)/B^2(\mathfrak g,k)$ in [Lie algebra cohomology](lie-algebra-cohomology.md) records exactly the obstruction to a section that is a [Lie algebra homomorphism](lie-algebra-homomorphism.md).
