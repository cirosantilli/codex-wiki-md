# Divisor restriction exact sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Effective Cartier divisor](effective-cartier-divisor.md)

For an [effective Cartier divisor](effective-cartier-divisor.md) $E$ and any [Cartier divisor](cartier-divisor-split.md) $D$, multiplication by a defining section of $E$ gives the displayed [short exact sequence of sheaves](short-exact-sequence-of-sheaves.md). Its [long exact sequence in sheaf cohomology](long-exact-sequence-in-sheaf-cohomology.md) compares sections and cohomology on $X$ with their restrictions to $E$.
