# Orthogonal structure on a real vector bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Fiber metric](fiber-metric.md)

An orthogonal structure is a reduction of the transition functions of a real rank-$r$ [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) to the [orthogonal group](orthogonal-group.md). Its orthonormal frames define a [fiber metric](fiber-metric.md), independent of the chosen frame because orthogonal transitions preserve the [inner product](inner-product.md). Conversely a [fiber metric](fiber-metric.md) gives smooth orthonormal frames by the [Gram-Schmidt process](gram-schmidt-process.md), with orthogonal transition matrices. This equivalence does not require the [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) to be globally trivial.
