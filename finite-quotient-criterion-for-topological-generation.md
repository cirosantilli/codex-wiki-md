# Finite-quotient criterion for topological generation

↑ **Parent:** [Topological generating set](topological-generating-set.md)

For a [profinite group](profinite-group.md) $G=\varprojlim_jG_j$, a subset $S\subseteq G$ is a [topological generating set](topological-generating-set.md) exactly when $\langle p_j(S)\rangle=p_j(G)$ in every finite quotient. Indeed, a subgroup of a profinite group is dense exactly when its image in every finite continuous quotient is surjective.
