# Retraction

↑ **Parent:** [Topological space](topological-space.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Retraction)

A retraction of a [topological space](topological-space.md) $X$ onto a subspace $A$ is a continuous map $r:X\to A$ whose restriction to $A$ is the identity.
