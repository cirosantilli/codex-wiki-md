# Main-sequence fitting

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmic distance ladder](cosmic-distance-ladder.md)

Compare the extinction-corrected [apparent magnitudes](apparent-magnitude.md) of a cluster's [main sequence](main-sequence.md) with calibrated [absolute magnitudes](absolute-magnitude.md) at matching colour or stellar parameters. The magnitude displacement estimates its [distance modulus](distance-modulus.md). Age, [metallicity](metallicity.md), helium abundance, reddening and unresolved binaries can move or broaden the sequence, so a colour-magnitude displacement is not uniquely a distance change.
