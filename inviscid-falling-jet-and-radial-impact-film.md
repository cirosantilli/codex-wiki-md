# Inviscid falling jet and radial impact film

↑ **Parent:** [Inviscid flow](inviscid-flow.md)

A steady incompressible jet at atmospheric [pressure](pressure.md) conserves [Bernoulli's equation](bernoulli-equation.md) and [volume flux](volumetric-flow-rate.md). Its radius contracts as $R=a[u/u_0]^{-1/2}$. After impact, a thin nearly horizontal axisymmetric film has $2\pi rhv=\pi a^2u_0$. Evaluating [Bernoulli's equation](bernoulli-equation.md) on its [free surface](free-surface.md) gives $v^2=u_0^2+2g(H-h)$, hence $a^4/(4r^2h^2)=1+2g(H-h)/u_0^2$. This inviscid thin-film description applies away from the impact region and does not describe a dissipative hydraulic jump.
