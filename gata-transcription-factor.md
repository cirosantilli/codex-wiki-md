# GATA transcription factor

↑ **Parent:** [Transcription factor](transcription-factor.md)

A [transcription factor](transcription-factor.md) family recognizing GATA-related [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) motifs. Successive GATA factors can implement specification, stabilization and differentiated [gene expression](gene-expression.md) in a developmental [gene regulatory network](gene-regulatory-network.md).
