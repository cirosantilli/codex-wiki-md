# Materials selection chart

↑ **Parent:** [Material selection](material-selection.md)

A plot comparing material property ranges, often on logarithmic axes. Constant [material performance index](material-performance-index.md) values appear as straight guidelines when the index is a product of powers of the plotted properties. A family region is a screening aid, not a certified property value for every grade or loading direction.
