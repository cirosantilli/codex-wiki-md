# Goldstone-boson equivalence theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Higgs mechanism](higgs-mechanism.md)

At tree level and energies much greater than a gauge-vector mass, an amplitude with longitudinal external [gauge bosons](gauge-boson.md) equals the corresponding amplitude with their would-be [Goldstone bosons](goldstone-boson.md), up to conventional external-state phases and relative $O(m_V/E)$ corrections for a nonzero leading amplitude. In a renormalizable gauge, the broken-symmetry identities relate $k_\mu\mathcal M^\mu$ to $m_V\mathcal M_\chi$; inserting $\varepsilon_L^\mu=k^\mu/m_V+O(m_V/E)$ explains the result. Loop-level versions include field-normalization factors. This is different from an [equivalence theorem for field redefinitions](equivalence-theorem-for-field-redefinitions.md).
