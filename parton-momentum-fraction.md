# Parton momentum fraction

↑ **Parent:** [Parton distribution function](parton-distribution-function.md)

In the collinear approximation, a [parton](parton.md) carries a fraction $0<x<1$ of its parent hadron's longitudinal [momentum](momentum-split.md). A [parton distribution function](parton-distribution-function.md) $f_i(x,Q^2)$ gives its distribution at the specified factorization scale. For production of one on-shell particle with no transverse recoil, $x_+x_-s=M^2$ and $x_\pm=(M/\sqrt s)e^{\pm y}$, where $y$ is its [rapidity](rapidity.md).
