# Inertia criterion for an inclined viscous film

↑ **Parent:** [Gravity-driven thin film on an incline](gravity-driven-thin-film-on-an-incline.md)

For a film of thickness $H$, axial length $L\gg H$, and [velocity](velocity.md) $U$, the ratio of convective inertia to transverse viscous stress in [lubrication theory](lubrication-theory.md) is $UH^2/(\nu L)$. On a small incline of slope $\theta$, [lubrication gravity-current flux](lubrication-gravity-current-flux.md) gives $U\sim g\theta H^2/\nu$ when $H\ll\theta L$, and $U\sim gH^3/(\nu L)$ when $H\gg\theta L$. The respective criteria are $g\theta H^4/(\nu^2L)\ll1$ and $gH^5/(\nu^2L^2)\ll1$. Unsteady inertia also requires $H^2/(\nu T)\ll1$ if the evolution timescale $T$ is independent of $L/U$.
