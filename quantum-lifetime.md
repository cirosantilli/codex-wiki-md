# Quantum lifetime

↑ **Parent:** [Electron scattering](electron-scattering.md)

The [quantum lifetime](quantum-lifetime.md) determines the broadening of a single-particle spectral level by scattering. It need not equal the [momentum relaxation time](momentum-relaxation-time.md): small-angle scattering broadens levels while relaxing current weakly. A common Lorentzian broadening convention is a half-width $\Gamma=\hbar/(2\tau_q)$.
