# Cubic cardinal B-spline

↑ **Parent:** [Cardinal B-spline](cardinal-b-spline.md)

This degree-three [Cardinal B-spline](cardinal-b-spline.md) has support $[0,4]$, is twice continuously differentiable, and has values $0,1/6,2/3,1/6,0$ at the consecutive integer knots. Their sum is one. Its [truncated power function](truncated-power-function.md) representation has coefficients whose first four moments vanish, making it zero after its final knot. Four knot intervals are the minimum possible compact support for a nonzero cubic $C^2$ [spline](spline-mathematics.md): with fewer intervals the four moment constraints form a full-column-rank [Vandermonde matrix](vandermonde-matrix.md). Translating and rescaling this function gives a local [basis](basis.md) for cubic [spline interpolation](spline-interpolation.md) on a uniform mesh.
