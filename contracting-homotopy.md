# Contracting homotopy

↑ **Parent:** [Chain homotopy](chain-homotopy.md)

A contracting homotopy on a [chain complex](chain-complex.md) is a degree-one map $h$ whose displayed identity makes the identity chain-homotopic to zero. It implies that all homology vanishes. The converse is not valid for arbitrary complexes of modules.
