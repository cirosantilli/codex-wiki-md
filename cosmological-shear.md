# Cosmological shear

↑ **Parent:** [Spatial homogeneity](spatial-homogeneity.md)

The traceless symmetric part of the galaxy velocity-gradient [matrix](matrix.md) changes shape without isotropic expansion. An antisymmetric part instead represents rigid rotation. A [matrix](matrix.md) proportional to the identity plus an antisymmetric [matrix](matrix.md) has no shear.
