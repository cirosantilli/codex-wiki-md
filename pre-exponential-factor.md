# Pre-exponential factor

↑ **Parent:** [Arrhenius equation](arrhenius-equation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pre-exponential_factor)

The [pre-exponential factor](pre-exponential-factor.md) multiplies the [exponential function](exponential-function.md) in the [Arrhenius equation](arrhenius-equation.md). It has the same units as the [rate constant](rate-constant.md), so for a [first-order reaction](first-order-reaction.md) both have units $\mathrm{s^{-1}}$. Its physical interpretation depends on the [reaction mechanism](reaction-mechanism.md); an assumed constant $A$ should not be identified with a universal collision frequency. Comparing [activation energies](activation-energy.md) alone does not rank absolute [rate constants](rate-constant.md) when the [pre-exponential factors](pre-exponential-factor.md) differ. See the [pre-exponential factor](iupac-compendium-of-chemical-terminology-gold-book-by-international-union-of-pure-and-applied-chemistry/pre-exponential-factor.md).
