# Even-function spectral gap on a hypercube

↑ **Parent:** [Coordinate-flip generator on a hypercube](coordinate-flip-generator-on-a-hypercube.md)

Global sign-even functions have zero coefficients on odd-size [Walsh characters](walsh-character.md). Every nonconstant remaining character has generator [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) at most $-2$, yielding the displayed improvement from gap one to gap two. Combining this with the convexity of a [norm](norm.md) proves the [sharp Rademacher second-moment inequality](sharp-rademacher-second-moment-inequality.md).
