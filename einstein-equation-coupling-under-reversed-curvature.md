# Einstein-equation coupling under reversed curvature

↑ **Parent:** [Einstein field equations](einstein-field-equations.md)

With the metric and [stress-energy tensor](stress-energy-tensor.md) fixed and zero [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md), changing the sign convention for the [Riemann curvature tensor](riemann-curvature-tensor.md) changes the signs of the [Ricci tensor](ricci-tensor.md), [scalar curvature](scalar-curvature.md) and [Einstein tensor](einstein-tensor.md). The same physical field equation is therefore written with the opposite coupling sign. For signature $(+---)$, the convention $R^a{}_{bcd}=\partial_d\Gamma^a_{bc}-\partial_c\Gamma^a_{bd}-\Gamma^a_{ec}\Gamma^e_{bd}+\Gamma^a_{ed}\Gamma^e_{bc}$ and $R_{ab}=R^c{}_{acb}$ gives $R_{00}\simeq-\Delta\Phi$ in a static [Newtonian limit](newtonian-limit.md). The trace-reversed equation and $\Delta\Phi=4\pi G\rho$ then require $G_{ab}=-8\pi GT_{ab}$.
