# Electron fugacity in the Saha equation

↑ **Parent:** [Saha ionization equation](saha-ionization-equation.md)

For classical nondegenerate [Electrons](electron.md), the free-particle Boltzmann occupancy has the form $z_e\exp[-p^2/(2mkT)]$, with chemical potential measured relative to its rest energy. Counting the two spin states in phase space gives $n_e=2z_e(2\pi mkT/h^2)^{3/2}$. A bound level of energy $-I_n$ has relative occupancy $(g_n/g_+)z_e e^{I_n/kT}$ against its parent ion. Eliminating $z_e$ gives the level-specific [Saha ionization equation](saha-ionization-equation.md).
