# Qubit polarization

↑ **Parent:** [Bloch vector](bloch-vector.md)

The ensemble [Bloch vector](bloch-vector.md) of a two-level [quantum state](quantum-state.md). With $\rho=(I+\mathbf P\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma)/2$, full polarization has $|\mathbf P|=1$ and is pure, while zero polarization has $\rho=I/2$ and is maximally mixed. This internal-state ensemble polarization differs from the [polarization](polarization-waves.md) of a light wave used to drive it.
