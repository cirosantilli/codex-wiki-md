# Affine dependence

↑ **Parent:** [Affine independence](affine-independence.md)

An [affine dependence](affine-dependence.md) of points is a nonzero coefficient family satisfying the displayed identities. Equivalently, the augmented [vectors](vector.md) $(x_i,1)$ are [linearly dependent](linear-dependence.md). Both identities are independent of the chosen origin. The absence of an [affine dependence](affine-dependence.md) is [affine independence](affine-independence.md).
