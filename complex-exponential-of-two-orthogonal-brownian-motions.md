# Complex exponential of two orthogonal Brownian motions

↑ **Parent:** [Orthogonal continuous local martingales](orthogonal-continuous-local-martingales.md)

For [orthogonal continuous local martingales](orthogonal-continuous-local-martingales.md) $B,\vartheta$ that are standard [Brownian motions](brownian-motion-split.md), the [real part](real-part.md) $X=e^B\cos\vartheta$ and [imaginary part](imaginary-part.md) $Y=e^B\sin\vartheta$ are [continuous local martingales](continuous-local-martingale.md). The [Itô formula](ito-s-lemma.md) gives $dX=X\,dB-Y\,d\vartheta$ and $dY=Y\,dB+X\,d\vartheta$, since the two diagonal second-order terms cancel. Their [quadratic variations](quadratic-variation.md) equal $\int_0^te^{2B_s}ds$ and their [quadratic covariation](quadratic-covariation.md) is zero. If $C=[B,\vartheta]$ is nonzero, the omitted drift terms are respectively $-Y\,dC$ and $X\,dC$, so the orthogonality hypothesis is essential.
