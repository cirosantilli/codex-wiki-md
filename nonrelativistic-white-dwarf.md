# Nonrelativistic white dwarf

↑ **Parent:** [White dwarf](white-dwarf.md)

A cold nonrelativistic white dwarf supported by [electron degeneracy pressure](electron-degeneracy-pressure.md) has a [polytropic equation of state](polytropic-equation-of-state.md) $P=K_{\rm NR}\rho^{5/3}$, so its [polytropic index](polytropic-index.md) is $3/2$. The [Lane-Emden mass formula](lane-emden-mass-formula.md) and [polytropic mass-radius relation](polytropic-mass-radius-relation.md) give $R\propto K_{\rm NR}M^{-1/3}$ at fixed composition. As density rises, relativistic corrections soften the exponent toward $4/3$ and lead toward the [Chandrasekhar mass](chandrasekhar-limit.md) regime.
