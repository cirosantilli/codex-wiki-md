# First-order rough-surface scattered field

↑ **Parent:** [Small-height Dirichlet scattering](small-height-dirichlet-scattering.md)

Taylor expansion of the [Dirichlet boundary condition](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) gives the first-order boundary trace $-h\partial_z\psi^{[0]}$ at the mean plane. The [outgoing angular spectrum](outgoing-angular-spectrum.md) then propagates it. A zero-mean surface gives zero mean for this linear rough correction, while the complete [scattered field](scattered-wave.md) still contains its nonzero flat reflection.
