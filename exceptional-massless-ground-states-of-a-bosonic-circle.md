# Exceptional massless ground states of a bosonic circle

↑ **Parent:** [Momentum and winding modes](momentum-and-winding-modes.md)

In the critical [closed bosonic string](closed-string.md), compactification of one coordinate gives $M^2=n^2/R^2+w^2R^2/\alpha'^2+2(N+\widetilde N-2)/\alpha'$ and $\widetilde N-N+nw=0$. At oscillator level zero, level matching forces $nw=0$. Thus a pure [momentum](momentum.md) [ground state](ground-state.md) becomes a massless [scalar field](scalar-field.md) at $R=|n|\sqrt{\alpha'}/2$, while a pure winding [ground state](ground-state.md) does so at $R=2\sqrt{\alpha'}/|w|$. These are threshold states descended from the bosonic [tachyon](tachyon.md); they are additional to the neutral [graviton](graviton.md), two-form, vectors, [dilaton](dilaton.md) and radius modulus. At the [self-dual circle](self-dual-circle-compactification.md), the four charges $(\pm2,0),(0,\pm2)$ contribute to the enhanced massless [scalar field](scalar-field.md) spectrum.
