# Weierstrass function

↑ **Parent:** [Positive lacunary trigonometric series](positive-lacunary-trigonometric-series.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weierstrass_function)

A standard [Weierstrass function](weierstrass-function.md) is a uniformly convergent lacunary cosine series, here parameterized by $a>1$ and an integer frequency multiplier $b>1$. Its continuity follows from the [Weierstrass M-test](weierstrass-m-test.md). In the range $1<a<b$, its [modulus of continuity](modulus-of-continuity.md) has the fractional-power scale $\delta^{\log a/\log b}$ for the positive odd-frequency model; the critical case $a=b$ can have the larger scale $\delta\log(1/\delta)$. These examples distinguish small approximation error from ordinary [Lipschitz continuity](lipschitz-continuity.md).
