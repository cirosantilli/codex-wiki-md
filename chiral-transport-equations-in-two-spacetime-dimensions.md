# Chiral transport equations in two spacetime dimensions

↑ **Parent:** [Dirac field in two spacetime dimensions](dirac-field-in-two-spacetime-dimensions.md)

The [massless Dirac equation](massless-dirac-equation.md) in one spatial dimension separates into opposite [chirality](chirality-physics.md) sectors. Its initial profiles evolve as $\psi_R(t,x)=f(x-t)$ and $\psi_L(t,x)=h(x+t)$, by the [chain rule](chain-rule.md) along the straight characteristics. Thus [wave packets](wave-packet.md) translate without distortion at the [speed of light](speed-of-light.md). A nonzero [Dirac mass term](dirac-mass-term.md) couples the two sectors, so these separate rigid-translation formulas do not apply to a massive field.
