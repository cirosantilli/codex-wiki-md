# Independence of eigenvectors for distinct eigenvalues

↑ **Parent:** [Eigenvector](eigenvector.md)

Applying the product of the other eigenvalue factors to a linear relation isolates each coefficient. Distinct [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) therefore give [linearly independent](linear-independence.md) [eigenvectors](eigenvector.md). A complete eigenvector [basis](basis.md) diagonalizes the associated [linear system of ordinary differential equations](linear-system-of-differential-equations.md).
