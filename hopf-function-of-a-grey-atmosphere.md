# Hopf function of a grey atmosphere

↑ **Parent:** [Grey atmosphere](grey-atmosphere.md)

The Hopf function encodes the departure of a plane-parallel [grey atmosphere](grey-atmosphere.md) from a purely linear source profile. With physical bolometric [radiative flux](radiative-flux.md) $F$ and standard [mean intensity](mean-intensity.md) $J$, it is defined by $J=3F[\tau+q(\tau)]/(4\pi)$. In thermal [radiative equilibrium](radiative-equilibrium.md), $T^4=3T_{\rm eff}^4[\tau+q(\tau)]/4$. The [Eddington closure approximation](eddington-closure-approximation.md) with its approximate surface condition gives constant $q=2/3$; exact angular transfer generally gives a depth-dependent function.
