# Finite-source filling-box front with a side opening

↑ **Parent:** [Filling box model](filling-box-model.md)

For a source in [pure plume balance](pure-plume-balance.md), $Q(z)=C F_s^{1/3}(z+z_v)^{5/3}$ and $Q_s=Q(0)$. A single ideal exhaust passes the source volume flux. Above the exhaust height, it removes unprocessed lower fluid and the [filling-box first front](filling-box-first-front.md) descends at rate $-\pi Q/A_c$. Below it, processed fluid leaves and only entrained volume grows the processed region. The floor is reached in finite time if the opening is at the floor; otherwise the final approach is exponential. A finite-height aperture supporting bidirectional exchange requires a different model.
