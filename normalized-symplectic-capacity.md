# Normalized symplectic capacity

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic capacity](symplectic-capacity.md)

A normalized [symplectic capacity](symplectic-capacity.md) has the displayed unit-ball and unit-cylinder values. Dilation gives $\pi R^2$ for both radius-$R$ domains. Monotonicity then implies the sharp [Gromov non-squeezing theorem](non-squeezing-theorem.md).
