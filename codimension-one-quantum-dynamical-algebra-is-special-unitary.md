# Codimension-one quantum dynamical algebra is special unitary

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamical Lie algebra](dynamical-lie-algebra.md)

For $N\ge2$, let $\mathfrak g$ be a [Lie subalgebra](lie-subalgebra.md) of $\mathfrak u(N)$ with codimension one. With inner product $-\operatorname{Tr}(XY)$ on skew-Hermitian matrices, its orthogonal complement is a line $\mathbb R Z$. Invariance of the inner product shows that $[X,Z]$ lies in that line for every $X\in\mathfrak g$; skew-adjointness of the adjoint action makes $[X,Z]$ orthogonal to $Z$, hence zero. A nonscalar skew-Hermitian $Z$ has a centralizer of dimension at most $(N-1)^2+1<N^2-1$, by its orthogonal eigenspace decomposition, and cannot centralize all of $\mathfrak g$. Thus $Z$ is scalar and $\mathfrak g=\mathfrak{su}(N)$. This gives [density operator controllability](density-operator-controllability.md) and all determinant-one gates, but not the central phase direction needed for [unitary operator controllability](unitary-operator-controllability.md).
