# Slater determinant

↑ **Parent:** [Two-particle antisymmetrization](two-particle-antisymmetrization.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slater_determinant)

A [Slater determinant](slater-determinant.md) extends [two-particle antisymmetrization](two-particle-antisymmetrization.md) to $N$ [fermions](fermion.md). Here $x_i$ contains position and [spin](spin.md) or other internal variables. Distinct [orthonormal](orthonormal-set.md) [one-particle states](one-particle-state.md) give the normalized [wavefunction](wave-function.md) displayed in the title. Repeating a [one-particle state](one-particle-state.md) repeats a column of the [determinant](determinant.md), so the [wavefunction](wave-function.md) vanishes. Exchanging particle slots exchanges rows and reverses its sign. A general [fermionic](fermion.md) [quantum state](quantum-state.md) can be a [quantum superposition](quantum-superposition.md) of [Slater determinants](slater-determinant.md); it need not be one [Slater determinant](slater-determinant.md).
