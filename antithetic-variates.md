# Antithetic variates

↑ **Parent:** [Monte Carlo integration](monte-carlo-integration.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antithetic_variates)

[Antithetic variates](antithetic-variates.md) deliberately couple [unbiased estimators](unbiased-estimator.md) of the same [expectation](expected-value.md) with negative [covariance](covariance.md). Their average can have smaller [variance](variance-split.md) than an average using independent draws. For example, evaluating a monotone integrand at $U$ and $1-U$, where $U$ has a [uniform distribution](continuous-uniform-distribution.md), gives opposite fluctuations while preserving both marginal distributions.
