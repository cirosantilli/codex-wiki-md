# Momentum representation in quantum mechanics

↑ **Parent:** [Wave function](wave-function-split.md)

The [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md) in the title represents a one-dimensional state in the [wavevector](wavevector.md) basis. Its [probability density](probability-density.md) is $|\phi(k)|^2$, normalized by $\int|\phi(k)|^2dk=1$ when $\int|\psi(x)|^2dx=1$. For physical [momentum](momentum-split.md) $p=\hbar k$, the density per unit momentum is $|\phi(p/\hbar)|^2/\hbar$. A confined stationary [energy eigenstate](energy-eigenstate.md) need not be a [momentum eigenstate](momentum-eigenstate.md).
