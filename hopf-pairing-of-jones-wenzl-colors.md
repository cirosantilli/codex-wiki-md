# Hopf pairing of Jones-Wenzl colors

↑ **Parent:** [Fusion of Jones-Wenzl colors](fusion-of-jones-wenzl-colors.md)

The full bracket of a zero-framed [Hopf link](hopf-link.md) colored i and j is the displayed pairing. Its normalized [matrix](matrix.md) is the finite sine transform with parity signs. Sine orthogonality gives $H^2=\mathcal D^2I$, where $\mathcal D^2=\sum_jd_j^2=r/(2\sin^2(\pi/r))$. Thus the pairing is nondegenerate in the reduced theory.
