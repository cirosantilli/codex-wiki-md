# Logarithmic loss

↑ **Parent:** [Loss function](loss-function.md)

The [loss function](loss-function.md) assigning $-\log q(y)$ to a predicted [probability distribution](probability-distribution-split.md) $q$ and observed outcome $y$. It is the negative [logarithmic scoring rule](logarithmic-scoring-rule.md); its [expected value](expected-value-split.md) under the true [probability distribution](probability-distribution-split.md) is [cross-entropy](cross-entropy.md). Predicting zero [probability](probability.md) for an observed outcome gives infinite loss.
