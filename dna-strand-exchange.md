# DNA strand exchange

↑ **Parent:** [DNA repair](dna-repair.md)

[DNA strand exchange](dna-strand-exchange.md) replaces a strand's original pairing partner with another complementary strand. [RecA](reca.md) and [RAD51](rad51.md) organize the substrates and promote local donor opening; new pairing produces [heteroduplex DNA](heteroduplex-dna.md).
