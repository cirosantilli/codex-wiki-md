# Principal stress formula for incompressible hyperelasticity

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperelastic material](hyperelastic-material.md)

For an [isotropic](isotropy.md) [hyperelastic material](hyperelastic-material.md) with [incompressibility](incompressible-flow.md), the [principal stresses](principal-stress.md) are $t_i=\lambda_i\partial W/\partial\lambda_i-p$. This follows by evaluating the [Cauchy stress tensor](cauchy-stress-tensor.md) formula $T=F(\partial_FW)^T-pI$ in principal directions. For the reduced [strain energy density](strain-energy-density.md) $h(\lambda)=W(\lambda^{-2},\lambda,\lambda)$, the [chain rule](chain-rule.md) and equality of the tangential derivatives give $t_\theta-t_r=\lambda h'(\lambda)/2$.
