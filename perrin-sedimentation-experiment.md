# Perrin sedimentation experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md)

A [colloidal](colloid.md) grain in a [liquid](liquid.md) is large enough to see with a [microscope](microscope.md), yet small enough for [thermal motion](thermal-motion.md) to compete with its tendency to settle. In the [Perrin sedimentation experiment](perrin-sedimentation-experiment.md), the [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) [number density](number-density.md) falls with height: a visible miniature atmosphere. Counting grains at different heights measures an energy scale; tracking their [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) measures a transport rate. Connecting both to the same [Avogadro constant](avogadro-constant.md) makes the experiment a quantitative test of [kinetic theory](kinetic-theory-split.md). The [Wiener measure](wiener-measure.md) describes an idealized path law; these measurements instead test a physical model and its material parameters.

[Jean Baptiste Perrin](jean-baptiste-perrin.md)'s 1909 memoir traces his [sedimentation](sedimentation.md) experiments to May 1908. It reports relative counts $100$, $47$, $22.6$, $12$ at heights $5$, $35$, $65$, $95\,\mathrm{\mu m}$, and compares radius estimates from [Stokes settling velocity](stokes-settling-velocity.md), counted grain [mass](mass.md), and rows of touching grains.[1909 memoir, §§15, 20–22; scan pp. 28, 41–49](mouvement-brownien-et-realite-moleculaire-by-jean-baptiste-perrin.md)

The 1926 lecture describes [resin](resin.md) precipitated from alcohol into water, washed and fractionated by [centrifugation](centrifugation.md) to obtain nearly [monodisperse](monodisperse.md) [gamboge](gamboge.md) or mastic grains. Diameter and [density](density.md) were measured separately. A sealed slide-and-cover-glass chamber prevented evaporation; a vertical [microscope](microscope.md) resolved roughly $2\,\mathrm{\mu m}$ layers through a roughly $0.1\,\mathrm{mm}$ depth. Refocusing and photographing compared layer populations after [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md); changing [temperature](temperature-split.md) reversibly changed the distribution.[Nobel Lectures, Physics 1922–1941; Discontinuous structure of matter, 11 December 1926, pp. 148–150](nobel-lectures-physics-1922-1941/discontinuous-structure-of-matter-by-jean-baptiste-perrin.md)

In _Les Atomes_ (1913), [Jean Baptiste Perrin](jean-baptiste-perrin.md) restricts the eyepiece view to fewer than five or six grains and alternates observations between heights. Focus displacement requires a [refractive index](refractive-index.md) correction to recover actual layer separation. Horizontal positions were marked with a [camera lucida](camera-lucida.md), usually every $30\,\mathrm s$, and distances calibrated with a micrometer; the polygon joins sampled positions rather than recording the intervening trajectory.[Les Atomes, chapter III, §§61–64](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Les_Atomes/III)[Les Atomes, chapter IV, §§70–75](https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Les_Atomes/IV)
