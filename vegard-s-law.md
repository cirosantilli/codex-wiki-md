<h1 id="vegard-s-law">Vegard's law</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Solid solution](solid-solution.md)

An approximate linear interpolation of a substitutional [solid solution](solid-solution.md)'s [lattice parameter](lattice-constant.md) with atomic composition. Converting the inferred [atomic fraction](atomic-fraction.md) to [mass fraction](mass-fraction.md) requires molar masses. Ordering, nonideal mixing, [strain](strain.md) and multiple phases can invalidate a simple two-endpoint interpolation.
