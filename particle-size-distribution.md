# Particle size distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Colloid](colloid.md)

The distribution of particle dimensions in an ensemble, with number-, volume- or intensity-weighting specified. Broad size distributions can broaden a [melting](melting.md) transition, frustrate colloidal crystallization and bias scattering-based size estimates. A mean size alone is then insufficient.
