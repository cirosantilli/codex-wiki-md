<h1 id="exact-buhlmann-credibility-for-gamma-claims">Exact Bühlmann credibility for gamma claims</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Gamma scale inverse-gamma conjugacy](gamma-scale-inverse-gamma-conjugacy.md)

With the [gamma scale inverse-gamma conjugacy](gamma-scale-inverse-gamma-conjugacy.md) model and prior shape $k>2$, the [Bühlmann model](buhlmann-model.md) has $v/a=(k-1)/\alpha$. Its [Bühlmann credibility premium](buhlmann-credibility-premium.md) equals $\alpha(\lambda+\sum_jX_j)/(k+n\alpha-1)$, exactly the [Bayes estimator under squared error loss](bayes-estimator-under-squared-error-loss.md) of $\alpha\Theta$. Equality holds because the [posterior mean](posterior-mean.md) is already affine in the observed [sample mean](sample-mean.md).
