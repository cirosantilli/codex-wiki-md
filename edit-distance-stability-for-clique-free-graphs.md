# Edit-distance stability for clique-free graphs

↑ **Parent:** [Turán's theorem](turan-s-theorem.md)

A clique-free [graph](graph-split.md) near the [Turan theorem](turan-s-theorem.md) [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) bound is close to a complete multipartite [graph](graph-split.md). Choose a maximum-degree [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md), split its neighbourhood from the remaining [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md), and apply induction inside the neighbourhood. The degree bound implies that missing cross [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) are at least twice the number of [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) inside the remaining class. This inequality controls all three types of edits and gives the constant three.
