# Lefschetz duality

↑ **Parent:** [Poincare duality](poincare-duality.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lefschetz_duality)

For a compact oriented $n$-manifold $W$ with boundary, cap product with its relative [fundamental class](fundamental-class.md) gives

$$
H^q(W;R)\cong H_{n-q}(W,\partial W;R),\qquad H^q(W,\partial W;R)\cong H_{n-q}(W;R).
$$
