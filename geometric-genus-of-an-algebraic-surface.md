# Geometric genus of an algebraic surface

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic surface](algebraic-surface.md)

The geometric genus of a smooth projective surface is

$$
p_g(X)=h^0(X,K_X)=h^2(X,\mathcal O_X).
$$
