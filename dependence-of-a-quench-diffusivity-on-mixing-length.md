# Dependence of a quench diffusivity on mixing length

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical quench level](chemical-quench-level.md)

For a vertical [eddy diffusivity](eddy-diffusivity.md) $K_{zz}$, mixing over a length $\ell$ takes $\tau_{\rm mix}\sim\ell^2/K_{zz}$. The condition that transport outrun a [chemical relaxation time](chemical-relaxation-time.md) therefore gives $K_{zz}\gtrsim\ell^2/\tau_{\rm chem}$. Taking $\ell=0.1H$ instead of one [atmospheric scale height](atmospheric-scale-height.md) $H$ changes this estimate by a factor of one hundred. The [atmospheric scale height](atmospheric-scale-height.md) itself depends on both temperature and gravity.
