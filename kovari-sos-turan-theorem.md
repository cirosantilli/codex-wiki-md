<h1 id="kovari-sos-turan-theorem">Kővári–Sós–Turán theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Extremal graph theory](extremal-graph-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kővári–Sós–Turán_theorem)

For each fixed positive integer $t$,

$$
\operatorname{ex}(n,K_{t,t})=O\left(n^{2-1/t}\right).
$$

The standard proof counts $t$-element subsets of [graph neighbourhoods](graph-neighbourhood.md) in a bipartite subgraph. If one side has degrees $d_y$, the absence of $K_{t,t}$ gives

$$
\sum_y\binom{d_y}{t}\leq(t-1)\binom nt.
$$

[convexity](convex-function.md) then bounds the number of edges.
