# Pressure compatibility at a no-slip wall

↑ **Parent:** [No-slip boundary condition](no-slip-boundary-condition.md)

For a smooth incompressible [Navier-Stokes equation](navier-stokes-equation.md) trajectory with zero [velocity](velocity.md) at a fixed flat wall, the normal momentum equation determines the normal [pressure gradient](pressure-gradient.md). In a perturbation model with buoyancy term $\mathrm{Ri}_B\theta\hat{\mathbf y}$, it gives $\partial_np=\nu\mathbf n\cdot\Delta\mathbf u-\mathrm{Ri}_B\theta\,\mathbf n\cdot\hat{\mathbf y}$. An extra homogeneous pressure [Neumann boundary condition](neumann-boundary-condition.md) is valid only when the right side vanishes; it does not follow from the [no-slip boundary condition](no-slip-boundary-condition.md).
