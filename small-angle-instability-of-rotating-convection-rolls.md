# Small-angle instability of rotating convection rolls

↑ **Parent:** [Weakly nonlinear rotating-convection roll stability](weakly-nonlinear-rotating-convection-roll-stability.md)

For finite [Prandtl number](prandtl-number.md) and stress-free boundaries, nearly parallel [convection rolls](convection-roll.md) can interact with a weakly damped large-scale [Eulerian mean flow](eulerian-mean-flow.md). The ordinary regular two-roll amplitude expansion becomes nonuniform as the angle tends to zero, so the mean-flow mode must be retained. This mechanism is not automatically the finite-angle [Küppers–Lortz instability](kuppers-lortz-instability.md), and thresholds depend on the stated boundary and parameter regime.
