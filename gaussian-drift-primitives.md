# Gaussian drift primitives

↑ **Parent:** [Dawson function](dawson-function.md)

Define $E_0(z)=\int_0^z e^{-t^2}\int_0^t e^{u^2}\,du\,dt$ and $E_1(z)=\int_0^z e^{-t^2}\int_0^t e^{u^2}\operatorname{erf}u\,du\,dt$. They satisfy $(D^2+2zD)E_0=1$ and $(D^2+2zD)E_1=\operatorname{erf}z$. The first is an [even function](even-function.md), the second an [odd function](odd-function.md), and their real tails are respectively $\frac12\log|z|+C_1+o(1)$ and $\operatorname{sgn}z[\frac12\log|z|+C_2+o(1)]$. Their derivatives have inverse-linear tails, explaining the logarithmic matching constants in a [singular perturbation](singular-perturbation.md).
