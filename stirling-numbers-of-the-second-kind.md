# Stirling numbers of the second kind

↑ **Parent:** [Set partition](set-partition.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stirling_numbers_of_the_second_kind)

The Stirling number of the second kind $\left\{\begin{smallmatrix}n\\k\end{smallmatrix}\right\}$ counts the [set partitions](set-partition.md) of an $n$-element set into $k$ blocks. It satisfies

$$
\left\{\begin{matrix}n\\k\end{matrix}\right\}
=k\left\{\begin{matrix}n-1\\k\end{matrix}\right\}
+\left\{\begin{matrix}n-1\\k-1\end{matrix}\right\}.
$$
