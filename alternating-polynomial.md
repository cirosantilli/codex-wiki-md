# Alternating polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Multivariate polynomial](multivariate-polynomial.md)

Over a field of [characteristic zero](characteristic-zero.md), an alternating [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) changes sign when two variables are interchanged. It vanishes when two coordinates agree, so each factor $x_i-x_j$ divides it. These distinct linear factors are relatively prime in the [polynomial ring](polynomial-ring.md), hence their product, the [Vandermonde determinant](vandermonde-determinant.md), divides it. The quotient is a [symmetric polynomial](symmetric-polynomial.md).
