# Unitary connection

↑ **Parent:** [Metric connection](metric-connection.md)

A connection on a complex vector bundle with [Hermitian metric](hermitian-metric-on-a-holomorphic-vector-bundle.md) $h$ is unitary when

$$
d\,h(s,t)=h(\nabla s,t)+h(s,\nabla t).
$$

In a unitary local frame its connection matrix is skew-Hermitian, and so is its curvature matrix.
