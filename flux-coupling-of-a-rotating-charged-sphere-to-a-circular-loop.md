# Flux coupling of a rotating charged sphere to a circular loop

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic field of a rotating charged spherical body](magnetic-field-of-a-rotating-charged-spherical-body.md)

A uniformly charged rotating sphere has an exactly dipolar exterior [magnetic vector potential](magnetic-vector-potential.md). For a concentric equatorial loop of radius $b$ outside the sphere, [Stokes theorem](stokes-theorem.md) gives $\Phi_b=2\pi b A_\varphi(b,\pi/2)=\mu_0m/(2b)$. If its effective source [electric current](electric-current.md) is defined by $m=\pi a^2I_{\rm eff}$, its [mutual inductance](mutual-inductance.md) is $\mu_0\pi a^2/(2b)$. The finite-source [vector potential](vector-potential.md) is regular through the spanning disk; an exterior point-dipole expression should not be integrated through its singular origin.
