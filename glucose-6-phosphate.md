# Glucose-6-phosphate

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md)

[Glucose-6-phosphate](glucose-6-phosphate.md) is glucose phosphorylated at carbon 6. It links glucose trapping to pathways including glycolysis and glycogen synthesis; accumulation of this product and the incoming phosphorylation flux are different quantities.
