# Polar form of a complex number

↑ **Parent:** [Complex number](complex-number.md)

A nonzero [complex number](complex-number.md) has the form $z=re^{i\theta}$ with $r=|z|>0$ and an argument determined modulo $2\pi$. Multiplication multiplies moduli and adds arguments. This makes powers and [roots of a complex number](root-of-a-complex-number.md) particularly transparent.
