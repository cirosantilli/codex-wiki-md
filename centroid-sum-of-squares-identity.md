# Centroid sum-of-squares identity

↑ **Parent:** [K-means clustering](k-means-clustering.md)

For a nonempty finite collection of [Euclidean vectors](euclidean-vector.md), its [sample mean](sample-mean.md) $\bar x=n^{-1}\sum_i x_i$ uniquely minimizes the sum of squared [Euclidean distances](euclidean-distance.md) to a proposed [centroid](centroid.md) $z$. Expand each squared [Euclidean norm](euclidean-norm.md) around $\bar x$ and use $\sum_i(x_i-\bar x)=0$ to cancel the cross term. The displayed identity leaves a nonnegative term $n\|z-\bar x\|^2$, equal to zero exactly at $z=\bar x$.
