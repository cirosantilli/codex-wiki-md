# Single-electron transfer

↑ **Parent:** [Redox](redox.md)

Transfer of one [electron](electron.md) between species, often converting an [enol](enol.md) or [enolate](enolate.md) into a [radical](radical-chemistry.md). It must be distinguished from transfer of a two-electron [hydride](hydride.md).
