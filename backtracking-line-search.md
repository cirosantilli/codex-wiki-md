# Backtracking line search

↑ **Parent:** [Line search](line-search.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Backtracking_line_search)

Start with a trial [step size](step-size.md) $\alpha_0>0$ and repeatedly replace $\alpha$ by $\rho\alpha$, with $0<\rho<1$, until the [Armijo condition](armijo-condition.md) holds. For a [differentiable function](differentiable-function.md) and a [descent direction](descent-direction.md), the short-step argument in the [Armijo condition](armijo-condition.md) proves finite termination. This alone does not enforce the curvature part of the [Wolfe conditions](wolfe-conditions.md).
