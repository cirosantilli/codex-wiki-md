# Modular operator

↑ **Parent:** [Tomita operator](tomita-operator.md)

The [positive operator](positive-operator.md) in the polar decomposition $S=J\Delta^{1/2}$ of the [Tomita operator](tomita-operator.md). In a matrix-algebra standard representation with cyclic [vector](vector.md) $D>0$, it is $\Delta(X)=D^2XD^{-2}$. For a tracial [vector](vector.md) it is the identity.
