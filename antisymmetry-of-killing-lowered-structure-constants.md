# Antisymmetry of Killing-lowered structure constants

↑ **Parent:** [Structure constant of a Lie algebra](structure-constant-of-a-lie-algebra.md)

Lower the output index of the [structure constants of a Lie algebra](structure-constant-of-a-lie-algebra.md) with its [Killing form](killing-form.md). The [Lie bracket](lie-bracket.md) makes the result antisymmetric in the first two indices, while invariance gives $\kappa([t_a,t_b],t_c)=\kappa(t_a,[t_b,t_c])$, making it antisymmetric in the last two indices. These two adjacent transpositions imply total antisymmetry. Nondegeneracy of the [Killing form](killing-form.md) is unnecessary.
