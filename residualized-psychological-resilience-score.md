# Residualized psychological resilience score

↑ **Parent:** [Measurement of psychological resilience](measurement-of-psychological-resilience.md)

An [exposure](exposure.md)-conditioned adaptation measure. With distress $D_i$, [exposure](exposure.md) $S_i$ and predicted distress $\widehat m(S_i)$, the displayed convention makes higher scores mean less distress than predicted. The result depends on [exposure](exposure.md) measurement, reference population and model. A [regression residual](regression-residual.md) can include [measurement error](measurement-error.md) and omitted causes rather than a distinct protective capacity.

For [ordinary least squares](ordinary-least-squares.md) $D=X\widehat\beta+e$, the [normal equation](normal-equation.md) gives $X^Te=0$. Thus $R=-e$ is also orthogonal to the fitted predictors; with an intercept its sample mean is zero. A zero in-sample linear association between [exposure](exposure.md) and this score can therefore be an algebraic consequence of its construction, not evidence that [exposure](exposure.md) has no effect. Independent validation is needed before assigning substantive meaning to the [regression residual](regression-residual.md).
