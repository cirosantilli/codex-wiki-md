# Weinstein neighborhood theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrangian submanifold](lagrangian-submanifold.md)

A neighborhood of a compact [Lagrangian submanifold](lagrangian-submanifold.md) $L\subset(M,\omega)$ is symplectomorphic, by a map restricting to the identity on $L$, to a neighborhood of the zero section in $(T^*L,d\lambda)$, with the sign chosen to match the convention for the canonical form.
