# Uniform recurrence

↑ **Parent:** [Symbolic dynamics](symbolic-dynamics.md)

A two-sided [sequence](sequence.md) over a finite [alphabet](alphabet.md) is [uniformly recurrent](uniform-recurrence.md) if every finite [word over an alphabet](string.md) occurring in it occurs with bounded gaps. More precisely, for each such word some $M$ ensures that every length-$M$ interval contains a complete occurrence. This is equivalent to being a [minimal point](minimal-point.md) of the [full shift](full-shift.md): a finite cover of the [orbit closure](orbit-closure.md) by preimages of a word's [cylinder set](cylinder-set.md) bounds its return gaps; conversely, bounded gaps pass to all points of the [orbit closure](orbit-closure.md) and make every forward [orbit](orbit-dynamical-system.md) dense there. The property concerns finite words, not infinite [integer intervals](integer-interval.md).
