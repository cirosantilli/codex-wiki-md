# Characteristic of an integral domain

↑ **Parent:** [Characteristic of a ring](characteristic-of-a-ring.md)

The positive [characteristic of a ring](characteristic-of-a-ring.md) which is an [integral domain](integral-domain.md) is a [prime number](prime-number-split.md). The characteristic cannot be one because $1\ne0$. If its least positive value were $n=ab$ with $1<a,b<n$, minimality would make $a1$ and $b1$ nonzero, while $(a1)(b1)=n1=0$ would give [zero divisors](zero-divisor.md).

The analogous result holds for a nonzero commutative [nonunital ring](rng-algebra.md) without [zero divisors](zero-divisor.md), using the [characteristic of a rng](characteristic-of-a-rng.md). If $n=ab$ were composite and minimal, choose $r$ with $ar\ne0$. For every $s$, $(ar)(bs)=n(rs)=0$, so cancellation forces $bs=0$ for every $s$, contradicting minimality of $n$.
