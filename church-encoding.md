# Church encoding

↑ **Parent:** [Lambda calculus](lambda-calculus.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Church_encoding)

[Church encoding](church-encoding.md) represents data and operations as terms of the [lambda calculus](lambda-calculus.md). A [Church numeral](church-numeral.md) represents a natural number by repeated function application; a [Church Boolean](church-boolean.md) selects one of two arguments.
