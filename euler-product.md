# Euler product

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet series](dirichlet-series.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euler_product)

An Euler product factors a [Dirichlet series](dirichlet-series.md) into local factors indexed by [prime numbers](prime-number.md). For a [multiplicative arithmetic function](multiplicative-function.md) $f$ and in a half-plane of absolute convergence,

$$
\sum_{n\geq1}\frac{f(n)}{n^s}
=\prod_p\sum_{j\geq0}\frac{f(p^j)}{p^{js}}.
$$
