# Canonical bundle formula for a point blowup

↑ **Parent:** [Blowup of a complex manifold at a point](blowup-of-a-complex-manifold-at-a-point.md)

For the [blowup of a complex manifold at a point](blowup-of-a-complex-manifold-at-a-point.md) in complex dimension $n$, the differential gives a section of $K_{\widetilde M}\otimes(\phi^*K_M)^{-1}$ whose [divisor on a complex manifold](divisor-on-a-complex-manifold.md) is $(n-1)E$. Indeed, a local chart has $x_i=u$ and $x_j=ut_j$ for $j\ne i$, so its [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md) is $\pm u^{n-1}$. The [holomorphic line bundle associated to a divisor](holomorphic-line-bundle-associated-to-a-divisor.md) then gives the formula for the [canonical bundle](canonical-bundle.md).
