# Translational repression

↑ **Parent:** [Translation (biology)](translation-biology.md)

Reduction of [protein](protein-split.md) production from an [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) without requiring an initial decrease in its abundance. [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) stability and translation measurements are needed to distinguish coupled effects.
