# Modified Bessel differential equation

↑ **Parent:** [Modified Bessel function](modified-bessel-function.md)

The [differential equation](differential-equation-split.md) defining [modified Bessel functions](modified-bessel-function.md) of order $\nu$. For real fixed $\nu$ and positive $z$, its independent standard solutions are $I_\nu(z)$ and $K_\nu(z)$.
