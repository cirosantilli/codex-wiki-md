# Schwarzschild conformal compactification

↑ **Parent:** [Penrose diagram](penrose-diagram.md)

For the [Kruskal–Szekeres coordinates](kruskal-szekeres-coordinates.md) of a positive-mass [Schwarzschild spacetime](schwarzschild-spacetime.md), $UV=(1-r/(2M))e^{r/(2M)}$. The displayed [conformal compactification](conformal-compactification.md) makes radial [null geodesics](null-geodesic.md) lines of slope $\pm1$. The [Killing horizons](killing-horizon.md) are $T=\pm X$, and the spacelike [curvature singularities](curvature-singularity.md) $r=0$ lie at $T=\pm\pi/4$, with $|X|<\pi/4$. Two asymptotically flat exterior regions each have [past null infinity](past-null-infinity.md), [null infinity](null-infinity.md) to the future, [timelike infinity](timelike-infinity.md) and [spacelike infinity](spacelike-infinity.md). The two exterior regions cannot communicate by any [causal curve](causal-curve.md); future-directed curves that cross a future horizon instead end at the black-hole singularity.
