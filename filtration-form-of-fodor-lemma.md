# Filtration form of Fodor lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Fodor lemma](fodor-lemma.md)

If $S\subseteq\kappa$ is stationary and $f(\alpha)\in A_\alpha$ for a [kappa-filtration](kappa-filtration.md), then $f$ is constant on a stationary [subset](subset.md). Restrict to limit indices and use continuity to find a smaller stage containing each value. [Fodor lemma](fodor-lemma.md) fixes that stage on a stationary [subset](subset.md). Its size is less than $\kappa$, so [club filter completeness](club-filter-completeness.md) makes one value fiber stationary.
