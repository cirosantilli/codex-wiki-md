# Ample curve on an abelian surface generates the surface

↑ **Parent:** [Abelian surface](abelian-surface.md)

Let $C$ be a smooth connected [ample](ample-line-bundle.md) curve in an [abelian surface](abelian-surface.md) $A$. Translate its chosen basepoint to zero and apply the [universal property of the Jacobian variety](universal-property-of-the-jacobian-variety.md). The image of $J(C)\to A$ is a closed connected algebraic subgroup containing the translated curve. If it had dimension one, that curve would be the subgroup itself; a distinct coset is disjoint and numerically equivalent, forcing $C^2=0$. This contradicts ampleness, so the homomorphism is surjective.
