# Dispersion with a nonlinear velocity map

↑ **Parent:** [Transport equation](transport-equation.md)

For $f(t,x,v)=f_0(x-ta(v),v)$, apply the [area formula](area-formula-geometric-measure-theory.md) to the velocity map and bound the initial data by their velocity [essential supremum](essential-supremum.md). This gives the displayed [mixed Lebesgue norm](mixed-lebesgue-norm.md) estimate whenever the [weighted inverse multiplicity](weighted-inverse-multiplicity.md) is essentially bounded. If $a$ is injective and $|\det Da|\geq\alpha>0$, the constant is at most $1/\alpha$. In dimension one a derivative bounded away from zero gives a global [diffeomorphism](diffeomorphism.md); in higher dimensions determinant bounds alone do not.
