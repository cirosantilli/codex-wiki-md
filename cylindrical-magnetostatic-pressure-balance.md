# Cylindrical magnetostatic pressure balance

↑ **Parent:** [Cylindrical magnetostatic equilibrium](cylindrical-magnetostatic-equilibrium.md)

For an [axisymmetric vector field](axisymmetric-vector-field.md) independent of the axial coordinate and regular on the axis, [Gauss's law for magnetism](gauss-s-law-for-magnetism.md) gives $B_R=0$. The magnetostatic [Ampère-Maxwell equation](ampere-s-circuital-law.md) then gives $j_R=0$, $j_\phi=-cB_z'/(4\pi)$, and $j_z=c(RB_\phi)'/(4\pi R)$ in Gaussian units. Radial [magnetostatic equilibrium](magnetostatic-equilibrium.md) is

$$
\frac d{dR}\left(p+\frac{B_z^2+B_\phi^2}{8\pi}\right)+\frac{B_\phi^2}{4\pi R}=0.
$$

For enclosed axial [electric current](electric-current.md) $I(R)$, the magnetostatic [Ampère-Maxwell equation](ampere-s-circuital-law.md) gives $B_\phi=2I/(cR)$, hence

$$
\frac d{dR}\left(p+\frac{B_z^2}{8\pi}\right)
+\frac1{2\pi c^2R^2}\frac{dI^2}{dR}=0.
$$

The first term contains [pressure](pressure.md) and axial [magnetic pressure](magnetic-pressure.md); the second combines toroidal [magnetic pressure](magnetic-pressure.md) with the hoop force from [magnetic tension](magnetic-tension.md).
