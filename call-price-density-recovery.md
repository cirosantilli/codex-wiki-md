# Call-price density recovery

↑ **Parent:** [European call option](european-call-option.md)

In a zero-interest one-period market with a continuous terminal [stock](stock.md) law under a pricing measure, $C(K)=\mathbb E_Q(S-K)^+$ implies $-C'(K)=Q(S>K)$ and $C''(K)=f_Q(K)$. Thus a full differentiable call curve determines its pricing density. A finite collection of strikes generally does not. With a deterministic nonunit [discount factor](discount-factor.md), divide the call curve by that factor before recovering the probability density.
