# Samarium hexaboride

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samarium_hexaboride)

[Samarium hexaboride](samarium-hexaboride.md) is a [Kondo insulator](kondo-insulator.md) whose narrow samarium $4f$-derived states mix with more dispersive conduction states. It motivates effective models of [band hybridization](band-hybridization.md) between a nearly [flat band](flat-band.md) and a light dispersive band.
