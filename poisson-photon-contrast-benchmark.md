# Poisson photon contrast benchmark

↑ **Parent:** [Photon shot noise](photon-shot-noise.md)

For a known-background Poisson measurement with mean $N$, the [standard deviation](standard-deviation.md) is $\sqrt N$. A small added mean $\Delta N$ has approximate [signal-to-noise ratio](signal-to-noise-ratio.md) $\Delta N/\sqrt N$ when background counting noise dominates. A fixed criterion $z$ therefore gives $\Delta N\approx z\sqrt N$ and fractional contrast $z/\sqrt N$. This is a stated observation model, not a universal human threshold or a two-alternative comparison formula.
