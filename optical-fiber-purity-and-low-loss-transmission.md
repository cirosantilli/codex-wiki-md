# Optical fiber purity and low-loss transmission

↑ **Parent:** [Optical fiber](optical-fiber.md)

Guiding [light](light.md) and retaining useful [optical power](optical-power.md) are different achievements. [Charles Kuen Kao](charles-kuen-kao.md) and [George Alfred Hockham](george-alfred-hockham.md) argued that impurities need not impose the measured losses of existing [glasses](glass.md) on every future [optical fiber](optical-fiber.md). Their 1966 paper treats [wave absorption](wave-absorption.md), [scattering of light](scattering-of-light.md) and guidance separately; reducing impurity absorption leaves intrinsic material losses and geometric losses to address. This was a route toward practical communication, building on existing [optical fibers](optical-fiber.md), rather than the invention of every use of guided [light](light.md).[pp. 1152–1154, §§3.1–4.2; p. 1158, §7](dielectric-fibre-surface-waveguides-for-optical-frequencies-by-charles-kuen-kao-and-george-alfred-hockham.md)

The launch experiment used clad [glass](glass.md) [optical fibers](optical-fiber.md) fixed in capillary tubes with epoxy for end polishing. [Laser](laser-split.md) illumination gave transverse [normal mode](normal-mode.md) patterns; input-spot displacement and [polarization of an electromagnetic wave](polarization-of-an-electromagnetic-wave.md) controlled excitation. These observations tested guidance, not kilometre-scale [optical fiber attenuation](optical-fiber-attenuation.md).[pp. 1157–1158, §6.2, Fig. 15, Table 2](dielectric-fibre-surface-waveguides-for-optical-frequencies-by-charles-kuen-kao-and-george-alfred-hockham.md)

[Charles Kuen Kao](charles-kuen-kao.md)'s lecture describes the complementary material experiment: short, polished [glass](glass.md) samples, including plasma-deposited fused [silicon dioxide](silicon-dioxide.md), were examined with purpose-built single-beam and later double-beam [spectrophotometers](spectrophotometer.md). A separate [ellipsometry](ellipsometry.md) measurement assessed end-surface [specular reflection](specular-reflection.md), a check against confusing interface loss with bulk [wave absorption](wave-absorption.md). The readout was loss versus [wavelength](wavelength.md). Comparing [glass](glass.md) samples supported impurity reduction as a route to lower loss. Surface contamination and the tiny bulk loss of short samples limited inference; the result did not itself demonstrate a long drawn [optical fiber](optical-fiber.md). The lecture also credits subsequent manufacturing work at Corning.[Published 2010 version of the 2009 lecture, p. 2302, §V, short-sample measurements, ellipsometer and later fiber manufacture](nobel-lecture-sand-from-centuries-past-send-future-voices-fast-by-charles-kuen-kao.md)

An original scale comparison makes the material problem tangible. In the uniform [optical fiber attenuation](optical-fiber-attenuation.md) model, a stipulated $20\,\mathrm{dB/km}$ leaves $10^{-2}=0.01$ of launched [optical power](optical-power.md) after $1\,\mathrm{km}$; a stipulated $0.2\,\mathrm{dB/km}$ leaves $10^{-0.02}\simeq0.95499$. Both guides could satisfy the same [total internal reflection](total-internal-reflection.md) condition. A successful spot pattern at the output therefore cannot by itself establish a useful communication distance.
