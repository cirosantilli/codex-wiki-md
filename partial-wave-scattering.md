# Partial-wave scattering

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum scattering](quantum-scattering.md)

[Partial-wave scattering](partial-wave-scattering.md) decomposes a [scattering state](scattering-state.md) into [partial waves](partial-wave.md) with definite [orbital angular momentum](orbital-angular-momentum.md). For a [central potential](central-potential.md), each channel has its own [scattering phase shift](scattering-phase-shift.md) and [Partial-wave S-matrix](partial-wave-s-matrix.md).
