# Tau hadronic polarimetry

↑ **Parent:** [Tau (particle)](tau-particle.md)

The visible [angular distribution](angular-distribution.md) and laboratory [energy](energy-split.md) in $\tau^-\to h^-\nu_\tau$ carry information about the parent's [spin](spin.md). For fixed hadronic [invariant mass](invariant-mass.md), $E_h=\gamma(E_h^*+\beta p_h^*\cos\vartheta)$. A spin-zero recoil has analyzing coefficient $\alpha_h=1$. For a spin-one recoil coupled through the [V-A interaction](v-a-interaction.md), longitudinal and transverse partial rates have weights $m_\tau^2:2m_h^2$ and opposite slopes, giving $\alpha_h=(m_\tau^2-2m_h^2)/(m_\tau^2+2m_h^2)$. Thus the harder-helicity ordering can reverse when the recoil [mass](mass.md) exceeds $m_\tau/\sqrt2$. A mean [invariant mass](invariant-mass.md) without spin-dependent spectral weights cannot fix the sign for a mixed sample.
