# Logit

↑ **Parent:** [Link function](link-function.md)

The logit link maps a mean probability to its [log odds](log-odds.md): $g(p)=\log[p/(1-p)]$, $0<p<1$. Its inverse is $p=(1+e^{-\eta})^{-1}$, used in [logistic regression](logistic-regression.md).
