# Centre of gravity

↑ **Parent:** [Center of mass](center-of-mass.md)

The point through which the resultant [gravitational force](gravitational-force.md) acts when that force has a single point of application. In a uniform [gravitational field](gravitational-field.md), it coincides with the [center of mass](center-of-mass.md); a nonuniform field need not give the same point.
