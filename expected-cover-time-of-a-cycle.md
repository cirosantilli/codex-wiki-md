# Expected cover time of a cycle

↑ **Parent:** [Cover time](cover-time.md)

A [simple random walk](simple-random-walk.md) on a [cycle graph](cycle-graph.md) of $n\geq3$ vertices has [expected value](expected-value.md) of its [cover time](cover-time.md) equal to $n(n-1)/2$, from any starting vertex. Lift the walk to the integers using the same $\pm1$ increments. Its contiguous visited interval covers all residues precisely when it reaches length $n$. Sum the [expected time to expand a random-walk range](expected-time-to-expand-a-random-walk-range.md), $1+2+\cdots+(n-1)$, using [linearity of expectation](linearity-of-expectation.md); no independence of expansion times is needed.
