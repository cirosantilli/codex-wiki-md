<h1 id="poincare-rank">Poincaré rank</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Irregular singular point](irregular-singular-point.md)

For a first-order meromorphic [linear ordinary differential equation](linear-ordinary-differential-equation.md) $Y_t=A(t)Y$, with a coefficient [pole](pole.md) of order $p>1$ in a fixed local coordinate and gauge, its Poincaré rank is $p-1$. At infinity one must transform the differential system, not just the [matrix](matrix.md)-valued function: $Y_\lambda=A_0\lambda^mY+\cdots$ becomes $Y_t=-A_0t^{-m-2}Y+\cdots$ under $t=1/\lambda$, giving rank $m+1$. A singular gauge can lower an apparent rank; distinct leading [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) prevent the elementary degeneracy in the unramified setting.
