# Normal magnetic coercivity

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic coercivity](magnetic-coercivity.md)

The magnitude of reverse [magnetic field strength](magnetic-field-strength.md) at which a previously magnetized specimen's [magnetic flux density](magnetic-field-split.md) $B$ vanishes on a specified [magnetic hysteresis](magnetic-hysteresis.md) branch. Intrinsic [magnetic coercivity](magnetic-coercivity.md) instead refers to zero [magnetization](magnetization.md). Because $B=\mu_0(H+M)$, their zero conditions generally occur at different applied fields. The preparation, branch and specimen geometry must be specified when comparing measurements.
