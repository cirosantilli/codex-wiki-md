# Nonreduced reducible fibre between integral schemes

↑ **Parent:** [Scheme-theoretic fibre](scheme-theoretic-fibre.md)

A [morphism of schemes](morphism-of-schemes.md) between [integral schemes](integral-scheme.md) can have a [scheme-theoretic fibre](scheme-theoretic-fibre.md) that is neither reduced nor irreducible. For the map of [affine lines](affine-line.md) given by $t=x^2(x-1)^2$, the fibre at $t=0$ has ring $k[x]/(x^2(x-1)^2)$. The [Chinese remainder theorem](chinese-remainder-theorem.md) identifies it with the product of two [dual number](dual-number.md) rings, so it consists of two [nonreduced double points](nonreduced-double-point.md). Its nilpotent structure is invisible to the set-theoretic fibre.
