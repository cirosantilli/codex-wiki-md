# Survival and susceptibility thresholds for directed percolation

↑ **Parent:** [Directed percolation](directed-percolation.md)

For the out-cluster $C_x$ in [directed percolation](directed-percolation.md), define $p_H=\inf\{p:\mathbb P_p(|C_x|=\infty)>0\}$ and $p_T=\inf\{p:\mathbb E_p|C_x|=\infty\}$, with value $1$ when the relevant set is empty below $1$. The first is a survival threshold; the second is a [percolation susceptibility](percolation-susceptibility.md) threshold. Both depend on the root in a general [directed graph](directed-graph.md). Exploring a [site percolation](site-percolation-split.md) cluster and using each fresh vertex coin for its first tested incoming bond gives a [coupling of probability distributions](coupling.md) with [bond percolation](bond-percolation-split.md) such that $\theta_s(p)\le p\theta_b(p)$ and $\chi_s(p)\le p\chi_b(p)$. Consequently $p_H^b\le p_H^s$ and $p_T^b\le p_T^s$.
