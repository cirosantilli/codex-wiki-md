# Splice junction

↑ **Parent:** [RNA splicing](rna-splicing.md)

The junction between segments joined during [RNA splicing](rna-splicing.md). A read crossing an [exon](exon.md)–[exon](exon.md) junction can distinguish a processed transcript from the corresponding unspliced precursor.
