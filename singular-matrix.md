# Singular matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Invertible matrix](invertible-matrix.md)

A square matrix is singular when it is not [invertible](invertible-matrix.md), equivalently when its [determinant](determinant.md) is zero or its [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) is nontrivial.
