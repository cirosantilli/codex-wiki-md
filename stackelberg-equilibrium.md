# Stackelberg equilibrium

↑ **Parent:** [Stackelberg competition](stackelberg-competition.md)

A solution of [Stackelberg competition](stackelberg-competition.md) in which the follower chooses a [best response](best-response.md) after each observed leader action and the leader optimizes given those responses. Indifference may require a specified response or leader-selection convention; it cannot be silently treated as a strict inequality. In a [sequential private-value all-pay contest](sequential-private-value-all-pay-contest.md), the leader's objective is $vF(b)-b$.
