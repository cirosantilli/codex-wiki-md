# Killing-form Lagrangian for an adjoint scalar

↑ **Parent:** [Yang-Mills theory](yang-mills-theory.md)

An adjoint scalar has [adjoint covariant derivative](adjoint-covariant-derivative.md) $D_\mu\phi=\partial_\mu\phi+[A_\mu,\phi]$. A symmetric [invariant bilinear form on a Lie algebra](invariant-bilinear-form-on-a-lie-algebra.md) contracts the [gauge field strength](gauge-field-strength.md) and scalar kinetic terms to give [gauge invariance](gauge-invariance.md). On a [compact real form](compact-real-form-of-a-complex-semisimple-lie-algebra.md) the positive internal metric is $-\kappa$, because the [Killing form](killing-form.md) itself is negative definite; complex scalar components require the associated Hermitian pairing. The Killing form is degenerate on Abelian factors, where another invariant metric is needed for a nondegenerate kinetic term.
