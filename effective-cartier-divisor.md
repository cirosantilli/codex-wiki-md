# Effective Cartier divisor

↑ **Parent:** [Cartier divisor](cartier-divisor-split.md)

An effective Cartier divisor is cut out locally by one [non-zero-divisor](non-zero-divisor.md); it is a codimension-one closed subscheme. On an integral scheme a nonzero [global section](global-section.md) of a [line bundle](line-bundle.md) defines such a divisor, possibly empty. On a nonreduced scheme a nonzero section need not be regular, so its zero locus need not be a Cartier divisor.
