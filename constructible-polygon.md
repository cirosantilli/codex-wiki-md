# Constructible polygon

↑ **Parent:** [Straightedge and compass construction](straightedge-and-compass-construction-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constructible_polygon)

A [constructible polygon](constructible-polygon.md) is a [polygon](polygon.md) whose vertices can be obtained by a [compass-and-straightedge construction](straightedge-and-compass-construction-split.md) from the specified initial data. The classical question for a [regular polygon](regular-polygon.md) asks which numbers of sides permit an exact construction from a given side or circumscribed [circle](circle.md).
