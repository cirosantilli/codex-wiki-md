# History of Catalan enumeration

↑ **Parent:** [Catalan number](catalan-number.md)

[Leonhard Euler](leonhard-euler.md)'s 1751 correspondence already studied [triangulations of a convex polygon](triangulation-of-a-convex-polygon.md) and gave a closed formula for their counts. [Johann Andreas von Segner](johann-andreas-von-segner.md) supplied a combinatorial [recurrence relation](recurrence-relation.md) in a paper written in 1758. [Eugène Charles Catalan](eugene-charles-catalan.md)'s nineteenth-century work connected the formula to bracketed products. This history, documented in [Igor Pak](igor-pak.md)'s account, shows how the same counting structure reappeared through different objects; the modern name alone does not establish discovery priority.[§§2–3 and §5, printed pp. 2–4: Euler correspondence, Segner recurrence and Catalan's bracketed products](history-of-catalan-numbers-by-igor-pak.md)
