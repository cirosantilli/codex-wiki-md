# Mohr-Coulomb failure criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Failure envelope](failure-envelope.md)

A frictional shear-strength approximation $|\tau|=c+\sigma_n'\tan\varphi$, with [cohesion](cohesion.md) $c$ and [internal friction angle](internal-friction-angle.md) $\varphi$. Effective compression increases shear resistance. A separate tensile cutoff is generally needed at low confinement. The names refer to [Christian Otto Mohr](christian-otto-mohr.md) and [Charles-Augustin de Coulomb](charles-augustin-de-coulomb.md).
