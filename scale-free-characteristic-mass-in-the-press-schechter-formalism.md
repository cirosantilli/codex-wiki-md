# Scale-free characteristic mass in the Press-Schechter formalism

↑ **Parent:** [Press-Schechter halo mass function](press-schechter-halo-mass-function.md)

If the linearly extrapolated rms amplitude scales as $\sigma(M)\propto M^{-s}$, a fixed peak-height cutoff in the [Press-Schechter halo mass function](press-schechter-halo-mass-function.md) has $M_*\propto D^{1/s}$, where $D$ is the [linear growth factor](linear-growth-factor.md). The differential prefactor scales as $M^{s-2}$ and the exponential argument as $M^{2s}$. The cutoff convention must be stated when fixing its absolute normalization: $\nu=1$ and $\nu^2/2=1$ select different masses, although their redshift scaling is the same.
