# Restriction and norm compatibility of the Artin map

↑ **Parent:** [Artin reciprocity law](artin-reciprocity-law.md)

For an [abelian extension](abelian-extension.md) $L/K$ of [number fields](number-field.md), restriction to an intermediate [field](field.md) takes each [Artin symbol](artin-symbol.md) to that of the smaller extension. For a finite base extension $E/K$, restriction identifies $\operatorname{Gal}(LE/E)$ with $\operatorname{Gal}(L/(L\cap E))$. At an unramified prime $\mathfrak q$ of $E$ above $\mathfrak p$, its residue-field Frobenius restricts to the $f(\mathfrak q/\mathfrak p)$th power of the [Frobenius automorphism](frobenius-automorphism.md) at $\mathfrak p$. Since $N_{E/K}\mathfrak q=\mathfrak p^{f(\mathfrak q/\mathfrak p)}$, the [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md) property proves the displayed identity for [fractional ideals](fractional-ideal.md) supported over unramified primes.
