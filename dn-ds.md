<h1 id="dn-ds">dN/dS</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular genetics](molecular-genetics-split.md)

The ratio of nonsynonymous to synonymous substitution rates after accounting for relevant [mutation](mutation.md) opportunities. Somatic-cancer analyses need background models appropriate to somatic [mutation](mutation.md) processes rather than raw event ratios.
