# Eliashberg theory

↑ **Parent:** [BCS theory](bcs-theory.md)

Eliashberg theory retains the frequency dependence of the electron–phonon interaction and the normal and anomalous electronic self-energies in a strong-coupling treatment of conventional [superconductivity](superconductivity-split.md). The phonon spectral function $\alpha^2F(\omega)$ and a screened Coulomb pseudopotential enter the pairing equations. High phonon frequencies alone do not specify a transition temperature without the coupling strength and competing repulsion.
