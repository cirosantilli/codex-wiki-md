# Photoevaporation

↑ **Parent:** [Astrophysics](astrophysics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photoevaporation)

Radiation heats gas sufficiently to drive an escaping thermal outflow. It can remove material from [protoplanetary disks](protoplanetary-disk.md), planetary atmospheres and irradiated clouds. In a heated [Keplerian disk](keplerian-disk.md), the [photoevaporative gravitational radius](photoevaporative-gravitational-radius.md) compares the [sound speed](speed-of-sound.md) with the orbital speed. The mass-loss rate depends on the heating, density and wind geometry rather than radiation pressure alone.
