# Cognitive neurology

↑ **Parent:** [Neurology](neurology-split.md)

The study of how neurological disease and injury affect cognition, using clinical patterns and experiments to constrain explanations of normal cognitive function. A [brain lesion](brain-lesion.md) can disrupt connected regions and several computations, so a syndrome is evidence to explain rather than a complete processing model.
