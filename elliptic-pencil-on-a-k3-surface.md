# Elliptic pencil on a K3 surface

↑ **Parent:** [K3 surface](k3-surface.md)

A primitive effective nef class of square zero on a complex [K3 surface](k3-surface.md) defines a basepoint-free pencil with connected genus-one fibres. Its general fibre is a smooth [elliptic curve](elliptic-curve.md). A section is a [smooth rational curve](smooth-rational-curve.md) $\Gamma$ with $E\cdot\Gamma=1$ and $\Gamma^2=-2$.
