# Magma intrusion

↑ **Parent:** [Magma](magma.md)

A magma intrusion is the emplacement of [magma](magma.md) into surrounding rock. A shallow, approximately axisymmetric intrusion can lift an [elastic plate](elastic-plate.md) roof, with spreading determined by [viscous peeling of an elastic plate](viscous-peeling-of-an-elastic-plate.md) or [fracture toughness](fracture-toughness.md).
