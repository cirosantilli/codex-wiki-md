# Uniform random permutation

↑ **Parent:** [Permutation](permutation.md)

A uniform random permutation of $n$ distinct objects gives each of the $n!$ [permutations](permutation.md) the same [probability](probability.md). Restricting the order to any fixed subset gives a uniform ordering on that subset. Relative ranks encode such a permutation bijectively by independent choices of an insertion position.
