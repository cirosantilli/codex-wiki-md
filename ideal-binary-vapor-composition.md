# Ideal binary vapor composition

↑ **Parent:** [Raoult's law](raoult-s-law.md)

For the stipulated A/B [ideal solution](ideal-solution.md), put $x=x_A$. At the fixed [temperature](temperature-split.md) $300\,\mathrm K$, [Raoult's law](raoult-s-law.md) reduces to

$$
P=(20+60x)\,\mathrm{kPa},\qquad
y_A=\frac{80x}{20+60x}=\frac{4x}{1+3x}.
$$

The [relative volatility](relative-volatility.md) is $\alpha=p_A^*/p_B^*=4$. For $0<x<1$,

$$
y_A-x=\frac{3x(1-x)}{1+3x}>0.
$$

Hence every interior [vapor-liquid equilibrium](vapor-liquid-equilibrium.md) pair in this chosen model has A-enriched [vapor](vapor.md); neither pure endpoint is enriched. Conversely,

$$
x_A=\frac{y_A}{4-3y_A}.
$$

For example, $y_A=1/2$ corresponds to $x_A=1/5$, $p_A=p_B=16\,\mathrm{kPa}$ and $P=32\,\mathrm{kPa}$. Equal [vapor](vapor.md) [mole fractions](mole-fraction.md) therefore need not mean equal [liquid](liquid.md) [mole fractions](mole-fraction.md).

These are fixed-[temperature](temperature-split.md) predictions. During [boiling](boiling.md) at a specified external [pressure](pressure-split.md), the [temperature](temperature-split.md) instead adjusts until $\sum_i x_i p_i^*(T)=P_{\rm external}$. The two stipulated [vapor pressures](vapor-pressure.md) specify only one [temperature](temperature-split.md); they provide no [boiling](boiling.md)-[temperature](temperature-split.md) curve.
