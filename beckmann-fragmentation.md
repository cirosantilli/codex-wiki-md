# Beckmann fragmentation

↑ **Parent:** [Beckmann rearrangement](beckmann-rearrangement.md)

Fragmentation of an activated [oxime](oxime.md) by cleavage of a carbon–carbon bond anti to its N–O leaving group, yielding a nitrile and a carbon electrophile or a product derived from it. Stabilization of a developing carbocation can favour fragmentation over migration. It differs from the usual [Beckmann rearrangement](beckmann-rearrangement.md), which inserts nitrogen into the migrating carbon–carbon bond and yields an amide after hydrolysis.
