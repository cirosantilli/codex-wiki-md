# Robin spectral determinant for defocusing NLS

↑ **Parent:** [Linearizable Robin scattering for defocusing NLS](linearizable-robin-scattering-for-defocusing-nls.md)

The effective boundary coefficient is $\widetilde\Gamma=-(2k-ic)b^\sharp(-k)/(a\mathcal D)$. All its ingredients come from initial scattering and the [Robin boundary condition](robin-boundary-condition.md). Genuine zeros produce pole data; common zeros in numerator and denominator can instead be removable. It is incorrect to assert absence of boundary poles for every real Robin parameter.
