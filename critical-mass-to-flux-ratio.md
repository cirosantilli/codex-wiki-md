# Critical mass-to-flux ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Mass-to-flux ratio](mass-to-flux-ratio.md)

For fixed cloud shape, gravitational energy scales as $-C_gGM^2/L$ and [magnetic energy](magnetic-energy.md) as $C_B\Phi^2/(\mu_0L)$. The magnetic-criticality threshold for negligible [pressure](pressure.md) is $M/\Phi>\sqrt{C_B/C_g}/\sqrt{\mu_0G}$. Ideal flux freezing keeps this ratio fixed during homologous collapse; geometry determines the numerical coefficient.
