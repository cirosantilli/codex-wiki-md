# Inverse hyperbolic functions

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic function](hyperbolic-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inverse_hyperbolic_functions)

The inverse hyperbolic functions invert the [hyperbolic functions](hyperbolic-function.md), with real branches on their standard ranges and complex branches obtained through [analytic continuation](analytic-continuation.md). For example, $\operatorname{arsinh}z=\log(z+\sqrt{z^2+1})$ on a chosen branch; the [multivalued inverse hyperbolic sine](multivalued-inverse-hyperbolic-sine.md) records all inverse values.
