# Leptonic tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Deep inelastic scattering](deep-inelastic-scattering.md)

The product of lepton-current [matrix elements](matrix-element.md) with a specified [spin sum](spin-sum.md) or [spin average](spin-average.md). Its normalization must be stated when quoting a [differential scattering cross-section](differential-scattering-cross-section.md).
