# Nonemptiness theorem for inverse limits of finite sets

↑ **Parent:** [Inverse limit](inverse-limit.md)

An inverse system of nonempty finite sets over a directed index set has nonempty inverse limit. Give each set the discrete topology: the compatibility conditions are closed subsets of the compact product, and directedness gives them the [finite intersection property](finite-intersection-property.md).
