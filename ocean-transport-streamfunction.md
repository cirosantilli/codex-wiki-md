# Ocean transport streamfunction

↑ **Parent:** [Sverdrup balance](sverdrup-balance.md)

An ocean transport streamfunction defines depth-integrated horizontal transport. For constant depth $H$, it is $H$ times the velocity [streamfunction](stream-function.md). Its difference between two boundaries gives signed transport across a connecting line.
