# Edge boundary of a down-set in a cube

↑ **Parent:** [Down-set](down-set.md)

For a [down-set](down-set.md) $D$ in the [Boolean lattice](boolean-lattice.md), every member $A$ has all its $|A|$ immediate lower neighbors in $D$. Counting an internal edge at its upper endpoint gives $e(D)=\sum_{A\in D}|A|$. The degree identity for the [edge boundary](edge-boundary-in-a-graph.md) of the [hypercube graph](hypercube-graph.md) then gives the displayed formula.
