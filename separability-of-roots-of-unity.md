# Separability of roots of unity

↑ **Parent:** [Separable polynomial](separable-polynomial.md)

If the [characteristic](characteristic-of-a-field.md) of a field does not divide the [positive integer](positive-integer.md) $m$, then $X^m-1$ is [separable](separable-polynomial.md). Its [formal derivative](formal-derivative.md) is $mX^{m-1}$, and a common root would have to be both nonzero and zero.
