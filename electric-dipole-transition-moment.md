# Electric dipole transition moment

↑ **Parent:** [Electric dipole transition](electric-dipole-transition.md)

The [matrix element](matrix-element.md) of the [electric dipole moment](electric-dipole-moment.md) operator between two [quantum states](quantum-state.md). Electric-dipole transition strength is proportional to its squared magnitude; [molecular symmetry](molecular-symmetry.md) can force every Cartesian component to vanish.
