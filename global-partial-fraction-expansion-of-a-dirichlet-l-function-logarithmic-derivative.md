# Global partial-fraction expansion of a Dirichlet L-function logarithmic derivative

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet L-function](dirichlet-l-function.md)

For a primitive nonprincipal [Dirichlet character](dirichlet-character.md) of conductor $q$ and parity $a$, the [completed Dirichlet L-function](completed-dirichlet-l-function.md) is entire of order one. Its genus-one [Hadamard factorization](hadamard-factorization-theorem.md) gives the displayed [logarithmic derivative](logarithmic-derivative.md), with $B_\chi$ the logarithmic derivative of the completion at zero and zeros counted with multiplicity. Combined summands converge locally normally away from zeros. For an imprimitive character induced by $\chi^*$, add $\sum_{p\mid Q,\ p\nmid q}\chi^*(p)\log p/(p^s-\chi^*(p))$ for the removed Euler factors. The primitive principal case is the [global partial-fraction expansion of the zeta logarithmic derivative](global-partial-fraction-expansion-of-the-zeta-logarithmic-derivative.md).
