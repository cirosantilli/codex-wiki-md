# Autozygosity

↑ **Parent:** [Identity by descent](identity-by-descent.md)

[Autozygosity](autozygosity.md) is [homozygosity](homozygosity.md) by descent: the maternal and paternal copies in one individual derive from the same ancestral copy. Unlike ordinary [homozygosity](homozygosity.md), it concerns the ancestry of chromosome copies rather than only their observed states. In a [pedigree](pedigree.md) with one ancestral disease-allele copy and fully penetrant [recessive inheritance](recessive-inheritance.md), every affected individual is [autozygous](autozygosity.md) for that copy.
