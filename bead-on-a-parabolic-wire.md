# Bead on a parabolic wire

↑ **Parent:** [Constraint (mechanics)](constraint-mechanics.md)

For a smooth stationary wire $z=x^2/(4a)$ and release from rest at height $h$, the [normal reaction](normal-force.md) does no work. The energy equation is $[1+x^2/(4a^2)]\dot x^2/2+gx^2/(4a)=gh$. Integrating the travel time between turning points gives the displayed period. Its small-amplitude expansion is $T=2\pi\sqrt{2a/g}[1+h/(4a)+O((h/a)^2)]$. The mass cancels because both gravitational and inertial forces are proportional to it.
