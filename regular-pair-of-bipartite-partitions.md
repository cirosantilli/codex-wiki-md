# Regular pair of bipartite partitions

↑ **Parent:** [Regular pair of vertex sets](regular-pair-of-vertex-sets.md)

Let $X=X_1\sqcup\cdots\sqcup X_r$ and $Y=Y_1\sqcup\cdots\sqcup Y_s$ be [set partitions](set-partition.md) of the two parts of a [bipartite graph](bipartite-graph.md). The pair of partitions is $\varepsilon$-regular when

$$
\sum_{(i,j):\,(X_i,Y_j)\text{ is not }\varepsilon\text{-regular}}|X_i||Y_j|
\leq\varepsilon|X||Y|.
$$

Thus a uniformly random pair in $X\times Y$ lies in an irregular cell pair with [probability](probability.md) at most $\varepsilon$.
