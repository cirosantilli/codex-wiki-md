# Rigid dense subset of the real line

↑ **Parent:** [Order automorphism](order-automorphism.md)

An [order-dense subset](order-dense-subset.md) with no nonidentity [order automorphism](order-automorphism.md). A transfinite diagonal construction marks an included point and an excluded image for every nonidentity real-line [order automorphism](order-automorphism.md), yielding such a subset of size $2^{\aleph_0}$.
