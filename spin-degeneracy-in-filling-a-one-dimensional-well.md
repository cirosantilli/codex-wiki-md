# Spin degeneracy in filling a one-dimensional well

↑ **Parent:** [Pauli exclusion principle](pauli-exclusion-principle.md)

When [spin](spin.md) does not split the orbital energies, a fermionic orbital can contain $2s+1$ particles with distinct [spin](spin.md) projections. Fill these complete states in order of increasing [energy](energy-split.md) to find a noninteracting many-particle [ground state](ground-state.md). Pauli exclusion does not require different spatial orbitals for particles with different available [spin](spin.md) states. Imposed [spin](spin.md) polarization or interactions can change the filling.
