<h1 id="su-3-gauge-theory">SU(3) gauge theory</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Yang-Mills theory](yang-mills-theory.md)

An SU(3) gauge theory has eight gauge fields, one for each generator of $SU(3)$. A vacuum expectation value of one scalar in the [fundamental representation](fundamental-representation.md) generically leaves an $SU(2)$ subgroup unbroken.
