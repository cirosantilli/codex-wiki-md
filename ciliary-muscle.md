# Ciliary muscle

↑ **Parent:** [Eye](eye.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ciliary_muscle)

A ring of [smooth muscle](smooth-muscle.md) in the [ciliary body](ciliary-body.md). Its contraction alters the geometry of the [ciliary zonule](ciliary-zonule.md) and permits the [crystalline lens](crystalline-lens.md) to become more curved for near [ocular accommodation](accommodation-vertebrate-eye.md).
