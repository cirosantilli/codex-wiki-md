# Buoyancy polarization of a plane internal gravity wave

↑ **Parent:** [Internal-wave polarization](internal-wave-polarization.md)

For a nonrotating [plane internal gravity wave](plane-internal-gravity-wave.md) with phase $kx+mz-\omega t$, let $K^2=k^2+m^2$, $k\ne0$, and use [kinematic pressure](kinematic-pressure.md). The [Linearized Boussinesq equations](linearized-boussinesq-equations.md) and $\mathbf u=\partial_t\boldsymbol\xi$ give $\widehat u=-im\omega\widehat\sigma/(kN^2)$, $\widehat\xi=m\widehat\sigma/(kN^2)$ and $\widehat p=-im\widehat\sigma/K^2$, together with the displayed vertical relations. [Buoyancy](buoyancy.md) and [displacement field](displacement-field-mechanics.md) are in phase or opposite phase; [velocity](velocity.md) and [pressure](pressure.md) are in [quadrature](phase-quadrature.md) with them. [Incompressibility](incompressible-flow.md) makes both the oscillating [velocity](velocity.md) and [displacement field](displacement-field-mechanics.md) perpendicular to the [wavevector](wavevector.md).
