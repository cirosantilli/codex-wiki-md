# Mean particle mass

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal gas](ideal-gas-split.md)

The [mean particle mass](mean-particle-mass.md) is the [mass density](density.md) divided by the total [number density](number-density.md) of freely moving particles. It enters $P=\rho k_BT/\bar m$. [ionization](ionization.md) changes the particle count without significantly changing the [mass](mass.md), so it changes $\bar m$. The [dimensionless](dimensionless-quantity.md) [mean molecular weight](mean-molecular-weight.md) is $\bar m$ divided by a stated reference [mass](mass.md), commonly the atomic [mass](mass.md) unit; it must not be substituted as a [dimensionless](dimensionless-quantity.md) number into a formula requiring a [mass](mass.md).
