# Acceleration potential of a homogeneous motion

↑ **Parent:** [Acceleration potential](acceleration-potential.md)

A [homogeneous motion](homogeneous-elastic-deformation.md) has an [acceleration potential](acceleration-potential.md) exactly when $A=\ddot F F^{-1}$ is a [symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md). Necessity follows because $A$ is the spatial [gradient](gradient.md) of the [material acceleration](material-acceleration.md), which must be a negative [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md). For sufficiency, put $\mathbf y=\mathbf x-\mathbf c(t)$ and take $\xi=-\mathbf y^TA\mathbf y/2-\ddot{\mathbf c}\cdot\mathbf y+g(t)$. Direct spatial differentiation gives $-\nabla\xi=A\mathbf y+\ddot{\mathbf c}$. Equivalently $F^T\dot F-\dot F^TF$ is constant: its time derivative is $F^T\ddot F-\ddot F^TF$.
