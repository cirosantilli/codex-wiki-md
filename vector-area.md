# Vector area

↑ **Parent:** [Vector area element](vector-area-element.md)

The vector area of an oriented surface is the integral of its unit [normal vector](normal-vector.md) times its area element. If its oriented boundary is a closed curve $C$, [Stokes theorem](stokes-theorem.md) gives

$$
\mathbf S=\frac12\oint_C\mathbf r\times d\mathbf r.
$$

Consequently it depends only on the oriented boundary, not on the spanning surface. For a planar surface it is signed area times a unit [normal vector](normal-vector.md). A current loop has [magnetic dipole moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md) $\mathbf m=I\mathbf S$; the scalar area of a nonplanar spanning surface is not interchangeable with this vector.
