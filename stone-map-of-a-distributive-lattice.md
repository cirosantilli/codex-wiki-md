# Stone map of a distributive lattice

↑ **Parent:** [Priestley dual space](priestley-dual-space.md)

The Stone map sends an element $a$ of a [distributive lattice](distributive-lattice.md) to the [clopen up-set](clopen-up-set.md) $a^*=\{P:a\in P\}$ of its [Priestley dual space](priestley-dual-space.md). The [Stone prime filter theorem](stone-prime-filter-theorem.md) makes this lattice homomorphism injective.
