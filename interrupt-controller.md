# Interrupt controller

↑ **Parent:** [Interrupt](interrupt.md)

Hardware managing and routing [interrupt](interrupt.md) requests to a [CPU](central-processing-unit.md), with architecture-dependent masking and priority rules. The [Linux kernel](linux-kernel.md) encapsulates its operations separately from generic flow handlers. [Linux kernel documentation, generic IRQ handling: chip-level hardware encapsulation](https://docs.kernel.org/core-api/genericirq.html)
