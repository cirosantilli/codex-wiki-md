# Waveguide

↑ **Parent:** [Wave](wave-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waveguide)

A [waveguide](waveguide.md) confines a wave transversely while allowing propagation along a longitudinal direction. Material properties and [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md) select transverse [normal modes](normal-mode.md), whose longitudinal [dispersion relations](dispersion-relation-split.md) may have cutoffs even when the bulk material is nondispersive. Examples include an [optical fiber](optical-fiber.md), a hollow [electromagnetic waveguide](electromagnetic-waveguide.md), and a [membrane waveguide](membrane-waveguide.md). Guiding reduces geometric spreading along the chosen route but does not eliminate absorption, scattering or leakage.
