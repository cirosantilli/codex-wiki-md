# Automorphism invariance of parameter-free definability

↑ **Parent:** [Parameter-free definability](parameter-free-definability.md)

Every parameter-free [definable set](definable-set.md) is preserved setwise by every [automorphism of a first-order structure](automorphism-of-a-first-order-structure.md). For [atomic formulas](atomic-formula.md), preservation follows from the definition of a structure automorphism. [Logical connectives](logical-connective.md) preserve that equivalence, and a bijection transports existential and universal witnesses in both directions. Induction on [first-order formulas](first-order-formula.md) therefore gives $M\models\varphi(\mathbf a)$ exactly when $M\models\varphi(\sigma\mathbf a)$. Applied to a defining formula, this proves the displayed equality. With parameters from $B$, restrict to automorphisms fixing $B$ pointwise. An automorphism moving a proposed singleton or taking a proposed function-graph point outside the graph is consequently a direct obstruction to definability.
