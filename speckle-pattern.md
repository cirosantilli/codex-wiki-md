# Speckle pattern

↑ **Parent:** [Physical optics](physical-optics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speckle_pattern)

A speckle pattern is a granular intensity pattern from coherent [wave interference](interference-wave-propagation.md). In stellar imaging, residual [wavefront errors](wavefront-error.md) produce speckles around the central [point spread function](point-spread-function.md).
