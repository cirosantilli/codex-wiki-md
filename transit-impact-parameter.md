# Transit impact parameter

↑ **Parent:** [Exoplanet transit](exoplanet-transit.md)

The minimum projected planet-star separation during an [exoplanet transit](exoplanet-transit.md), divided by the stellar radius. It determines the stellar chord length and hence the [transit duration of a small planet](transit-duration-of-a-small-planet.md).
