# Zero-free region of the Riemann zeta function

↑ **Parent:** [Nontrivial zero of the Riemann zeta function](nontrivial-zero-of-the-riemann-zeta-function.md)

The classical zero-free region asserts that for some $c>0$,

$$
\zeta(\sigma+it)\ne0
\quad\text{if}\quad
\sigma\geq1-\frac c{\log(|t|+3)}.
$$

Together with bounds for the [logarithmic derivative](logarithmic-derivative.md), it permits contour arguments with exponentially small errors in $\sqrt{\log x}$.
