# Idele

↑ **Parent:** [Idele group](idele-group.md)

An idele is an element of the [idele group](idele-group.md): a tuple of nonzero elements of the [completion of a valued field](completion-of-a-valued-field.md) at all places, with finite-place components in the local unit groups except at finitely many places.
