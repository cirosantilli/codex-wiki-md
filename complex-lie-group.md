# Complex Lie group

↑ **Parent:** [Lie group](lie-group.md)

A complex [Lie group](lie-group.md) is a [complex manifold](complex-manifold.md) whose multiplication and inversion are [holomorphic maps between complex manifolds](holomorphic-map-between-complex-manifolds.md). Its [tangent space](tangent-space.md) at the identity is a complex [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md); the [Adjoint representation of a Lie group](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-group.md) and the [Lie exponential map](exponential-map-of-a-lie-group.md) are holomorphic. Compatibility with a [complex manifold](complex-manifold.md) structure is essential: an arbitrary even-dimensional real [Lie group](lie-group.md) need not have holomorphic multiplication.
