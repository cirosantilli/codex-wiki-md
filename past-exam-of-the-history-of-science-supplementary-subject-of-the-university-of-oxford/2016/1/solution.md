**The relevant letter is dated 12 April 1615; the paper's 1616 date conflates it with the subsequent restriction of Copernican teaching.** [Robert Bellarmine](../../../robert-bellarmine.md) addressed [Paolo Antonio Foscarini](../../../paolo-antonio-foscarini.md)'s defence of [heliocentrism](../../../heliocentric-model.md) by distinguishing a useful mathematical supposition from an established account of the world's physical arrangement. That distinction determines the conditions of his limited permission. It is not a general licence to teach the Earth's [motion](../../../motion.md) as an already demonstrated fact.[12 April 1615, first and third numbered arguments; the reproduced document gives its date explicitly](../../../letter-to-foscarini-by-robert-bellarmine.md)

First, a [mathematician](../../../mathematician.md) could work conditionally: assume that the [Earth](../../../earth.md) moves and the [Sun](../../../sun.md) is stationary, and investigate whether the resulting [astronomical model](../../../astronomical-model.md) accounts for the observed appearances. Successful calculation would establish what follows from those assumptions. It would not, merely by succeeding, demonstrate that the assumptions describe the true causes and arrangement of the heavens. This was a recognizable distinction between mathematical treatment and [natural philosophy](../../../natural-philosophy.md), although it should not be made into a complete description of what all early modern [mathematicians](../../../mathematician.md) believed. A person might be licensed to use a computational scheme without being authorized to assert its physical truth.

Second, [Robert Bellarmine](../../../robert-bellarmine.md) treated interpretation of Scripture as an institutional constraint. His appeal to the Council of Trent and the claimed agreement of earlier commentators meant that an individual investigator could not simply replace the received reading on the strength of an attractive [astronomical model](../../../astronomical-model.md). He also rejected the answer that a claim about the [Earth](../../../earth.md) was irrelevant to faith just because its subject was not a central Christian doctrine: the authority of the scriptural speaker mattered to him. These are his grounds for caution, not neutral rules of [astronomy](../../../astronomy-split.md) which every participant was obliged to accept.

Third, he conceded an important conditional. If a genuine [demonstration](../../../demonstration-in-early-modern-natural-philosophy.md) of the Earth's [motion](../../../motion.md) were available, apparently contrary scriptural passages would require careful reinterpretation; it would be better to admit that their meaning had not been understood than to deny a demonstrated conclusion. Nevertheless, he denied that such a [demonstration](../../../demonstration-in-early-modern-natural-philosophy.md) had been supplied. Thus the concession does not amount to contemporary acceptance of [heliocentrism](../../../heliocentric-model.md), and the acceptable hypothetical discourse must be distinguished from this more demanding route to physical affirmation.[Second argument on interpretive authority; third argument distinguishing an actual demonstration from saving appearances](../../../letter-to-foscarini-by-robert-bellarmine.md)

The distinction had a genuine evidential setting. [Galileo Galilei](../../../galileo-galilei.md)'s [observations](../../../observation.md) challenged parts of the inherited celestial picture. Bodies accompanying [Jupiter](../../../jupiter.md) showed that not every celestial circuit had the [Earth](../../../earth.md) as its immediate centre. The full sequence of [phases of Venus](../../../phases-of-venus.md) was incompatible with the traditional arrangement that always kept [Venus](../../../venus.md) on the near side of the [Sun](../../../sun.md). Yet neither [observation](../../../observation.md) alone selected a moving [Earth](../../../earth.md): a [Tychonic system](../../../tychonic-system.md) could have the planets circle the [Sun](../../../sun.md) while the [Sun](../../../sun.md) circled a stationary [Earth](../../../earth.md). The relevant distinction is between refuting one [geocentric model](../../../geocentric-model.md) and proving every premise of a particular [heliocentric model](../../../heliocentric-model.md).[1610, nightly positions of the bodies accompanying Jupiter](../../../sidereus-nuncius-by-galileo-galilei.md)[Competing Ptolemaic, Copernican and Tychonic geometries; the Venus observations followed the publication of Sidereus Nuncius](../../../phases-of-venus-by-museo-galileo.md)

The defensible reading is therefore a conditional permission accompanied by a restrictive burden of [proof](../../../mathematical-proof.md). [Robert Bellarmine](../../../robert-bellarmine.md) allowed mathematical use without an absolute assertion, and envisaged reconsidering interpretation if physical [demonstration](../../../demonstration-in-early-modern-natural-philosophy.md) compelled it. He neither declared all mathematical accounts fictional nor recognized an unrestricted freedom to announce new physical truths regardless of theological jurisdiction. The 1615 letter and the later proceedings belong to a developing controversy, rather than one timeless pronouncement about the relation of [science](../../../science.md) and [theology](../../../theology.md).
