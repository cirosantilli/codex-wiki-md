A [mathematician](../../../mathematician.md) in [Galileo Galilei](../../../galileo-galilei.md)'s world was a practitioner situated within a particular division of knowledge and work. The word could embrace demonstrative [geometry](../../../geometry.md) and [arithmetic](../../../arithmetic-split.md), but also [astronomy](../../../astronomy-split.md), [optics](../../../optics-split.md), [mechanics](../../../mechanics.md), surveying, calendar calculation and the design or use of [scientific instruments](../../../scientific-instrument.md). Such [mixed mathematics](../../../mixed-mathematics.md) treated features of natural or practical objects through mathematical relations. It was neither identical to a present-day university department nor confined to calculation without claims about nature.

The distinction from a [natural philosopher](../../../natural-philosopher.md) was important. Within a traditional university hierarchy, [natural philosophy](../../../natural-philosophy.md) investigated the causes and principles of physical change. [Mathematics](../../../mathematics-split.md) could establish a relation between abstract quantities with great certainty, while opponents doubted whether it captured the constitution of a real body. Showing what follows for an ideal [inclined plane](../../../inclined-plane.md), for example, leaves a further question about whether a particular apparatus realizes the assumptions closely enough. That question is not an objection which can be answered merely by producing a longer geometrical [proof](../../../mathematical-proof.md). The relation of abstraction, [measurement](../../../measurement.md) and physical explanation was itself contested.[Book II, chapter 2: mathematical abstraction and the study of natural bodies](../../../physics-by-aristotle.md)

There were also several routes to employment. A university teacher, an [scientific instrument](../../../scientific-instrument.md) maker and someone serving a court or military patron might all perform mathematical work while possessing different income, status and audiences. Practical expertise in [fortification](../../../fortification.md), surveying or [optics](../../../optics-split.md) could bring rewards without establishing that its holder had authority over every question of [natural philosophy](../../../natural-philosophy.md). Conversely, a learned author could rely on the skills of people whose work was not credited equally in print. The important historical questions are what tasks were recognized, who paid for them, and whose testimony carried weight. A modern boundary between theoretical research and technical service would obscure these connections.

[Galileo Galilei](../../../galileo-galilei.md)'s career makes the contested boundary visible. The [Sidereus Nuncius by Galileo Galilei](../../../sidereus-nuncius-by-galileo-galilei.md) communicated new [observations](../../../observation.md) through a manufactured [telescope](../../../telescope.md), successive drawings and an account intended to make unfamiliar objects credible. The work also addressed a courtly audience: naming the bodies accompanying [Jupiter](../../../jupiter.md) was part of presenting an achievement to a patron, not an incidental ornament added after a purely private discovery. The title and dedication situate mathematical work within relationships of employment and reputation.[Title, dedication and sequence of observations: the telescope, lunar surface and Medicean bodies](../../../sidereus-nuncius-by-galileo-galilei.md)

His mathematical treatment of [motion](../../../motion.md) likewise pressed beyond the role of calculating celestial appearances. In [Two New Sciences by Galileo Galilei](../../../two-new-sciences-by-galileo-galilei.md), a definition of uniform [acceleration](../../../acceleration.md) leads to a geometrical result in which travelled [distance](../../../distance.md) varies as the square of elapsed [time](../../../time-in-physics.md). An [inclined plane](../../../inclined-plane.md) stretches the [time](../../../time-in-physics.md) of descent, making a comparison more manageable. In modern notation the relationship is $s=at^2/2$ for release from rest. The notation is ours; the historically significant combination is an idealized [proof](../../../mathematical-proof.md), a proposed physical regularity and a reported experimental test.[Third Day: definition of uniformly accelerated motion, Theorem II and the inclined-plane trials](../../../two-new-sciences-by-galileo-galilei.md)

The label [mathematician](../../../mathematician.md) therefore cannot be read as a declaration that [Galileo Galilei](../../../galileo-galilei.md) made no physical claims. Nor does his insistence on mathematical explanation prove that other occupations or disciplinary standards were irrelevant. His 1610 transition into Tuscan service, with the combined style of [mathematician](../../../mathematician.md) and philosopher, is evidence of the status being sought, rather than [proof](../../../mathematical-proof.md) that the existing hierarchy had already disappeared. The case shows an investigator using [scientific instruments](../../../scientific-instrument.md), patronage and mathematical [demonstration](../../../demonstration-in-early-modern-natural-philosophy.md) to enlarge the jurisdiction of his expertise. It also explains why a seemingly terminological distinction could become consequential in disputes over [heliocentrism](../../../heliocentric-model.md).[Chief Mathematician and Philosopher to the Grand Duke of Tuscany, 1610–1611](../../../galileo-s-tuscan-appointment-by-museo-galileo.md)
