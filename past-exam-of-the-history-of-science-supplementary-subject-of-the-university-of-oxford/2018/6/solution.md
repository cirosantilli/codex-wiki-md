**Originality lay chiefly in an explanatory mechanism and a sustained evidential synthesis, rather than in the first suggestion that living forms might change.** [Charles Darwin](../../../charles-darwin.md) combined existing problems and resources in a new way, while [Alfred Russel Wallace](../../../alfred-russel-wallace.md)'s independent work prevents the mechanism of [natural selection](../../../natural-selection.md) from being assigned to a wholly solitary origin.

[Jean-Baptiste Lamarck](../../../jean-baptiste-lamarck.md) had already offered a connected account of the transformation of living forms. His explanation gave substantial roles to changed circumstances, altered habits, use and disuse, and the transmission of acquired modifications. This was a serious attempt to explain organic change, not simply the absurd proposition that an animal obtains anything it wishes for. Nevertheless, a process that transforms individuals through altered activity differs from differential persistence and [reproduction](../../../reproduction-split.md) among variants. Recognizing that difference allows comparison without pretending that every earlier idea of [evolution](../../../evolution-split.md) was already [natural selection](../../../natural-selection.md).[part I, chapter VII: circumstances, needs, habits, use and disuse, and the preservation of acquired changes](../../../zoological-philosophy-by-jean-baptiste-lamarck.md)

[Charles Darwin](../../../charles-darwin.md) also drew on practices that were not inventions of an isolated theorist. Breeders' [artificial selection](../../../artificial-selection.md) demonstrated that cumulative choice among inherited differences could alter domestic forms. [Thomas Robert Malthus](../../../thomas-robert-malthus.md)'s population argument made unrestricted multiplication and limited subsistence a problem requiring explanation. [Geology](../../../geology.md), the [fossil record](../../../fossil-record.md) and the geographical distribution of organisms supplied historical and spatial patterns that a theory of species had to address. None of these ingredients alone entailed [Charles Darwin](../../../charles-darwin.md)'s conclusion. A population check might eliminate individuals without producing cumulative adaptive change; breeding involved a human chooser whose intentions could not simply be projected into nature.[first edition, chapters I and III: domestic selection, multiplication and the extended meaning of struggle](../../../on-the-origin-of-species-by-charles-darwin.md)

The distinctive inference joined inherited differences to unequal success in leaving descendants. If some variants leave more surviving offspring under particular conditions, and some relevant differences recur in their descendants, the composition of a population can change over successive generations. [Natural selection](../../../natural-selection.md) consequently requires neither an organism's foresight nor a breeder intentionally specifying its destination. Competition also has a wider meaning than physical combat: it includes dependence on other organisms and on conditions necessary for [reproduction](../../../reproduction-split.md). In this way an apparently purposive fit between organism and environment could be given a historical explanation.[first edition, chapters III–IV: struggle used metaphorically and the preservation of favourable differences](../../../on-the-origin-of-species-by-charles-darwin.md)

[Charles Darwin](../../../charles-darwin.md)'s larger contribution was to make that process part of a theory of branching [common descent](../../../common-descent.md). Divergence, [extinction](../../../extinction.md), the affinities used in classification, the distribution of organisms on islands and continents, and similarities of structure could be treated as connected consequences of a history of descent with modification. The argument accumulated partially independent kinds of evidence. It did not rest on displaying one adaptation and declaring that the rest of organic history must follow. It also openly discussed difficulties, including complex organs, hybridism and imperfections in the [fossil record](../../../fossil-record.md).[first edition, chapters IV, VI, VIII–XIII and the concluding recapitulation](../../../on-the-origin-of-species-by-charles-darwin.md)

The case of [Alfred Russel Wallace](../../../alfred-russel-wallace.md) is decisive for judging intellectual priority. In the Introduction to the 1859 [On the Origin of Species by Charles Darwin](../../../on-the-origin-of-species-by-charles-darwin.md), [Charles Darwin](../../../charles-darwin.md) explicitly acknowledged the arrival of [Alfred Russel Wallace](../../../alfred-russel-wallace.md)'s memoir and the publication of their materials together. [Alfred Russel Wallace](../../../alfred-russel-wallace.md)'s essay on varieties departing from the original type argued that struggle could favour particular variants and permit continuing divergence. Its later reprint identifies the 1858 publication and distinguishes that essay from subsequent additions to the collected volume.[first-edition Introduction: Wallace's memoir and the extracts from Darwin's manuscripts](../../../on-the-origin-of-species-by-charles-darwin.md)[1871 second-edition preface and essay II, originally published in 1858](../../../contributions-to-the-theory-of-natural-selection-by-alfred-russel-wallace.md) Independent convergence shows both that the mechanism was a real intellectual achievement and that the surrounding problems and observations were not private possessions.

Finally, the historical theory must be distinguished from later [genetics](../../../genetics-split.md). [Charles Darwin](../../../charles-darwin.md) did not possess a Mendelian explanation of [heredity](../../../heredity.md), and the 1859 book allowed roles for use and disuse and other causes of variation. It is therefore inaccurate to identify its entire content with a modern opposition between strictly genetic [natural selection](../../../natural-selection.md) and a wholly separate Lamarckian alternative.[first edition, chapter V, Effects of Use and Disuse](../../../on-the-origin-of-species-by-charles-darwin.md) The qualified judgment is that [Charles Darwin](../../../charles-darwin.md) produced an unusually powerful synthesis and a major new causal argument, independently paralleled by [Alfred Russel Wallace](../../../alfred-russel-wallace.md). Dependence on predecessors limits a claim of creation from nothing; it does not reduce the theory to the repetition of those predecessors.
