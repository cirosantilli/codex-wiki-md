↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

The case for a [Chemical Revolution](../../../chemical-revolution.md) is strongest if it concerns a reorganization of chemical explanation, measurement and classification. It is much weaker if it means that one isolated observation immediately overthrew a unified older theory. The production of the gas later called [oxygen](../../../oxygen.md) and the acceptance of an [oxygen](../../../oxygen.md)-based chemistry were related but distinct developments.

[Carl Wilhelm Scheele](../../../carl-wilhelm-scheele.md) and [Joseph Priestley](../../../joseph-priestley.md) investigated an unusually [combustion](../../../combustion.md)-supporting gas, yet interpreted their results within the resources of [phlogiston theory](../../../phlogiston-theory.md). Priestley's term [dephlogisticated air](../../../dephlogisticated-air.md) was not simply an incorrect label on an otherwise complete modern explanation: it expressed a different account of what [combustion](../../../combustion.md) did. Scheele's preparations and Priestley's work also make a single-person priority story inadequate. Experimental preparation, publication, recognition of a material's properties and a new interpretation need not occur together.[experiments on fire air and its relation to ordinary air](../../../chemical-treatise-on-air-and-fire-by-carl-wilhelm-scheele.md)[account of dephlogisticated air](../../../experiments-and-observations-on-different-kinds-of-air-by-joseph-priestley-1775.md)

[Antoine Lavoisier](../../../antoine-lavoisier.md)'s programme connected the gas with the quantitative study of [combustion](../../../combustion.md) and metallic calces. On the [oxygen](../../../oxygen.md) account, a metal's conversion into an oxide involves incorporation of material from the surrounding gas. The increase in the solid's [mass](../../../mass.md) is therefore compatible with [conservation of mass](../../../mass-conservation.md) for the whole vessel and its contents. Simply weighing only the metal is not the same experiment as accounting for the gaseous reactant as well. This change in what must be measured helped make the explanatory alternatives testable.

The shift also concerned chemical language. A nomenclature organized around composition made new relations among substances expressible and teachable. In [Elements of Chemistry by Antoine Lavoisier](../../../elements-of-chemistry-by-antoine-lavoisier.md), the treatment of simple substances and compounds is part of a systematic programme rather than an appendix to one gas experiment. Methods, instruments, names and explanations reinforced one another.[preliminary discourse and Part I: combustion, simple substances and the organization of chemical explanation](../../../elements-of-chemistry-by-antoine-lavoisier.md)

There are important qualifications. Older investigators were not incapable experimenters, and phlogiston accounts could be adjusted in response to new results. The newer chemistry also contained commitments subsequently rejected: [caloric theory](../../../caloric-theory.md) and the claim that [oxygen](../../../oxygen.md) was universally essential to acidity were not permanent truths. [Hydrogen chloride](../../../hydrogen-chloride.md) provides a later counterexample to a universal [oxygen](../../../oxygen.md)-based account of acids. A theory can be a major advance without every element of its programme surviving.

Accordingly, calling the episode revolutionary need not deny continuity in apparatus, experiments or personnel. It identifies a substantial change in how chemical processes were described and coordinated. Conversely, [oxygen](../../../oxygen.md) preparation by itself does not establish a sharp boundary between rational and irrational chemistry. The explanatory achievement depended on interpreting material exchanges, connecting them to measurements and building a coherent working system. The discovery of the gas contributed to that transformation, but the historical revolution cannot be reduced to its first preparation.
