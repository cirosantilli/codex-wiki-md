<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

[Isaac Newton](../../../isaac-newton.md)'s achievement was not to introduce mathematics into a previously nonmathematical study of nature. [Astronomy](../../../astronomy-split.md), [optics](../../../optics-split.md) and [mechanics](../../../mechanics.md) already had substantial mathematical traditions; [Two New Sciences by Galileo Galilei](../../../two-new-sciences-by-galileo-galilei.md), for example, connects geometrical reasoning with accelerated [motion](../../../motion.md). The distinctive achievement of the [Philosophiae Naturalis Principia Mathematica by Isaac Newton](../../../philosophiae-naturalis-principia-mathematica-by-isaac-newton.md) was to connect mathematical results about [motion](../../../motion.md) and [forces](../../../force-split.md) with an ambitious, quantitatively constrained account of terrestrial and celestial phenomena.

The method separates a conditional mathematical proposition from its application. Given a specified [force law](../../../force-law.md) and [initial conditions](../../../initial-condition.md), determine a [trajectory](../../../trajectory.md). Conversely, given a suitably idealized [trajectory](../../../trajectory.md) and time law, infer what [force](../../../force-split.md) would produce it. This makes the explanation more demanding than fitting a geometrical curve to positions: the timing and dynamical assumptions matter as well.

A modern calculation illustrates the connection. For a planar [orbit](../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md) under a central [force](../../../force-split.md), let $h=r^2\dot\theta$ be the conserved specific [angular momentum](../../../angular-momentum.md) and put $u=1/r$. An [ellipse](../../../ellipse.md) with the attracting point at a focus has $u=(1+e\cos\theta)/p$. Thus $u''+u=1/p$, and [Binet's equation](../../../binet-equation.md) gives the radial [acceleration](../../../acceleration.md)

$$
a_r=-h^2u^2(u''+u)=-\frac{h^2}{p r^2}.
$$

The [orbit](../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md) and equal-area timing therefore imply an inverse-square [acceleration](../../../acceleration.md) under the stated assumptions. The modern notation is explanatory; it is not an assertion that Newton wrote this particular differential-equation derivation. The geometrical treatment of central [forces](../../../force-split.md) in Book I and its astronomical application in Book III supply the historical connection.[Book I, central-force propositions, and Book III, application to planetary and satellite motions](../../../the-mathematical-principles-of-natural-philosophy-by-isaac-newton-1846-edition.md)

The programme also joined apparently different domains. [Newton's law of universal gravitation](../../../newton-s-law-of-universal-gravitation-split.md) connects orbital [motion](../../../motion.md) with terrestrial falling [motion](../../../motion.md) through the same inverse-square dependence, with [distance](../../../distance.md) and [time](../../../time-in-physics.md) scales accounted for. That unification produced further tests rather than merely attaching one name to two phenomena. Departures from a perfect two-body [ellipse](../../../ellipse.md), including disturbances by other gravitating bodies, became quantitative problems for the theory instead of automatic refutations of it.

Measurement did not disappear into deduction. Astronomical observations provide imperfectly known inputs; assumptions about [mass](../../../mass.md) distribution and neglected interactions restrict an ideal model. A discrepancy can expose a mistaken approximation, an observational error or a deficiency in the dynamical account. The mathematical proof alone does not decide which. The laws and initial data therefore create a disciplined relationship between measurement and explanation, not a proof of all nature from definitions.

Newton also did not claim that successfully describing a [force](../../../force-split.md) mathematically supplied a complete mechanical account of its ultimate cause. Distinguishing a quantitative law from a speculative mechanism allowed a powerful programme of prediction while leaving explanatory questions open. The achievement was a new integration of mathematical demonstration, empirical inference and common dynamical principles. Its strength lies in the constraints this integration places on physical explanations, rather than in a simple opposition between mathematics and experiment.
