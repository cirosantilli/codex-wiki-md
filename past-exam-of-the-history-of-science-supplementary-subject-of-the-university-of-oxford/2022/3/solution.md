**[Isaac Newton](../../../isaac-newton.md) made the greater contribution if the criterion is the scope and explanatory integration of mathematical [natural philosophy](../../../natural-philosophy.md); [Galileo Galilei](../../../galileo-galilei.md) has the stronger claim if the criterion is the formative transformation of how terrestrial [motion](../../../motion.md) could be investigated.** The first criterion gives a defensible overall verdict for [Isaac Newton](../../../isaac-newton.md), but a serious comparison must show what each changed rather than award credit according to who worked later.

[Galileo Galilei](../../../galileo-galilei.md)'s contribution was not simply to attach numbers to familiar events. In the Third Day of [Two New Sciences by Galileo Galilei](../../../two-new-sciences-by-galileo-galilei.md), he defines uniformly accelerated [motion](../../../motion.md) by equal additions of [speed](../../../speed.md) in equal intervals of [time](../../../time-in-physics.md), then derives consequences. Starting from rest, the modern notation is $v=at$; the mean [speed](../../../speed.md) over the interval is $at/2$, so

$$
s=\frac12at^2.
$$

Hence [distances](../../../distance.md) in successive equal intervals have the ratios $1:3:5:7:\ldots$. The novelty lies in making a precisely specified kind of [motion](../../../motion.md) the subject of mathematical demonstration, with observed falls assessed against its consequences. This is not the same as deriving the underlying physical [cause](../../../cause.md) of [gravity](../../../gravity.md).[Third Day: definition of naturally accelerated motion, Theorem I on mean speed and Theorem II with its corollaries](../../../two-new-sciences-by-galileo-galilei.md)

The reported apparatus shows how such an idealization could be investigated. A smooth bronze ball descended in a straight grooved wooden channel, raised at one end. Water discharged through a small opening into a vessel during the descent; its collected [mass](../../../mass.md) served as a measure of elapsed [time](../../../time-in-physics.md). Repeating runs over different portions of the channel compared the predicted squared dependence of [time](../../../time-in-physics.md) on [distance](../../../distance.md). The [inclined plane](../../../inclined-plane.md) slowed the event, while repetition and weighing made a temporal comparison possible without a modern stopwatch. The published report is evidence of [Galileo Galilei](../../../galileo-galilei.md)'s experimental presentation, not a surviving raw-data archive from which every reported precision can now be reconstructed.[Third Day, discussion following Theorem II: grooved channel, bronze ball, water timing and repeated trials](../../../two-new-sciences-by-galileo-galilei.md)

A necessary physical qualification is often missed. A ball in [rolling without slipping](../../../rolling-without-slipping.md) is not a point particle sliding without [friction](../../../friction.md). For a solid [sphere](../../../sphere.md) on an ideal [inclined plane](../../../inclined-plane.md), modern [rigid body dynamics](../../../rigid-body-dynamics-split.md) gives $a=5g\sin\alpha/7$, rather than $g\sin\alpha$. Both are constant [accelerations](../../../acceleration.md), so both give $s\propto t^2$. The trial can therefore test the temporal form without directly establishing the free-fall coefficient. This distinction makes the experiment more intelligible: the evidence supports a specified relation under approximations, rather than every detail of a modern idealization.

The Fourth Day extends this approach to [projectile motion](../../../projectile-motion.md). Combine uniform horizontal [velocity](../../../velocity.md) $u$ with constant downward [acceleration](../../../acceleration.md) $g$, taking downward displacement positive. Then $x=ut$ and $y=gt^2/2$. Eliminating [time](../../../time-in-physics.md) gives

$$
\boxed{y=\frac{g}{2u^2}x^2,}
$$

a [parabola](../../../parabola.md). Actual projectiles experience [air resistance](../../../air-resistance.md), but the ideal curve organizes the effects that need separate treatment. [Galileo Galilei](../../../galileo-galilei.md) thus gave a powerful example of how mathematical abstraction and physical observation could cooperate.[Fourth Day, Theorem I: the parabolic trajectory formed by uniform horizontal and naturally accelerated vertical motion](../../../two-new-sciences-by-galileo-galilei.md)

[Isaac Newton](../../../isaac-newton.md) went further by connecting kinds of [motion](../../../motion.md) through a general account of [force](../../../force-split.md). [Newton's second law](../../../newton-s-second-law.md) links the change of [momentum](../../../momentum-split.md) to the impressed [force](../../../force-split.md); for constant [mass](../../../mass.md), its modern differential expression is $\mathbf F=m\mathbf a$. [Newton's third law](../../../newton-s-third-law.md) makes interaction reciprocal, enabling a consistent analysis of interacting bodies. The [Philosophiae Naturalis Principia Mathematica by Isaac Newton](../../../philosophiae-naturalis-principia-mathematica-by-isaac-newton.md) develops consequences of [central forces](../../../central-force.md) and then asks which laws fit astronomical phenomena. Mathematical deduction now connects measurable [orbits](../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md) to an underlying [force law](../../../force-law.md), rather than just describing each trajectory separately.[Definitions, Laws II–III; Book I, section II, propositions I–III; section III, propositions XI–XIII](../../../the-mathematical-principles-of-natural-philosophy-by-isaac-newton-1846-edition.md)

A simple modern reconstruction shows the increase in explanatory reach. For a circular [orbit](../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md) of [radius](../../../radius.md) $r$ and [orbital period](../../../orbital-period.md) $T$ about a much more massive central body, [centripetal acceleration](../../../centripetal-acceleration.md) is $4\pi^2r/T^2$. [Kepler's third law](../../../kepler-s-third-law.md), $T^2\propto r^3$, therefore implies an [acceleration](../../../acceleration.md) proportional to $r^{-2}$. This circular argument is illustrative; [Isaac Newton](../../../isaac-newton.md)'s treatment of elliptical [orbits](../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md) is more general. With [Newton's law of universal gravitation](../../../newton-s-law-of-universal-gravitation-split.md),

$$
\boxed{F=\frac{GMm}{r^2},\qquad T^2=\frac{4\pi^2a^3}{G(M+m)},}
$$

the two-body [orbital period](../../../orbital-period.md) is tied to the [semimajor axis](../../../semi-major-axis.md) $a$ and both [masses](../../../mass.md). The same theory connects the Moon's orbital [acceleration](../../../acceleration.md) with terrestrial [gravity](../../../gravity.md), and supports further investigations of [tides](../../../tide.md), [comets](../../../comet.md) and departures from a simple two-body [orbit](../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md).[Book I, proposition XV and section XI; Book III, proposition IV and the subsequent treatment of universal gravitation](../../../the-mathematical-principles-of-natural-philosophy-by-isaac-newton-1846-edition.md)

This achievement depended on more than two individuals. [Johannes Kepler](../../../johannes-kepler.md)'s relations supplied crucial astronomical constraints, and both authors inherited [geometry](../../../geometry.md), observational records and technical practices. Nor should the [Philosophiae Naturalis Principia Mathematica by Isaac Newton](../../../philosophiae-naturalis-principia-mathematica-by-isaac-newton.md) be described simply as the first application of symbolic [calculus](../../../calculus-split.md): its published arguments are largely geometrical. What distinguishes [Isaac Newton](../../../isaac-newton.md) is the demonstrative network uniting terrestrial and celestial problems, together with explicit idealizations whose failures generated further calculable questions.

**On breadth, quantitative unification and the inference from [motion](../../../motion.md) to [force](../../../force-split.md), [Isaac Newton](../../../isaac-newton.md)'s contribution was greater. [Galileo Galilei](../../../galileo-galilei.md)'s work supplied an exemplary way to mathematize terrestrial [motion](../../../motion.md) and test its idealized relations.** Giving the former the overall verdict should therefore acknowledge the latter's distinct foundational achievement, not portray the [Philosophiae Naturalis Principia Mathematica by Isaac Newton](../../../philosophiae-naturalis-principia-mathematica-by-isaac-newton.md) as a creation without predecessors.
