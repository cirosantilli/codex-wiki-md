# Supergiant

↑ **Parent:** [Star](star.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supergiant)

A highly luminous evolved [star](star.md), associated with luminosity class I. Its location in a [Hertzsprung-Russell diagram](hertzsprung-russell-diagram.md) is distinct from the low-[luminosity](luminosity.md), compact [white dwarf](white-dwarf.md) region.
