# Recurrence relation

↑ **Parent:** [Sequence](sequence.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recurrence_relation)

A recurrence relation specifies members of a [sequence](sequence.md) in terms of previous members, together with enough initial values to determine the sequence. A three-term recurrence for [orthogonal polynomials](orthogonal-polynomial-split.md) relates consecutive orders. For example, the physicists' [Hermite polynomials](hermite-polynomial.md) satisfy $H_{n+1}(x)=2xH_n(x)-2nH_{n-1}(x)$, starting from $H_0=1$ and $H_1=2x$.
