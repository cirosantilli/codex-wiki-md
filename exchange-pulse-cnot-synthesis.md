# Exchange-pulse CNOT synthesis

↑ **Parent:** [Square root of the SWAP gate](square-root-of-the-swap-gate.md)

Two [square roots of the SWAP gate](square-root-of-the-swap-gate.md), separated and followed by local $z$ rotations, implement a [controlled phase gate](controlled-phase-gate.md). Local $y$ rotations of its target convert it into a [CNOT gate](controlled-not-gate.md). The construction assumes isotropic exchange and independent one-qubit control; pulse errors and extra [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) terms change the implemented operation.
