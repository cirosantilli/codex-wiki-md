# Particle stresslet tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Stokes flow](stokes-flow-split.md)

The symmetric first moment of the [traction](traction.md) exerted by a rigid inclusion, with the surface normal pointing into the inclusion. Its contraction with the background [rate-of-strain tensor](strain-rate-tensor.md) is the strain-related contribution to [extra dissipation due to a rigid inclusion](extra-dissipation-due-to-a-rigid-inclusion.md). A trace-free version is equivalent for an [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md). This tensor is distinct from the axisymmetric [force-dipole flow](force-dipole-flow.md) used as a far-field singularity.
