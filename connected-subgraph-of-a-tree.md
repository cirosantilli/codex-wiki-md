# Connected subgraph of a tree

↑ **Parent:** [Tree (graph theory)](tree-graph-theory-split.md)

A nonempty connected [subgraph](subgraph.md) of a [tree](tree-graph-theory-split.md). Between any two included vertices, it contains the unique tree path: its connectedness supplies a path, and the ambient tree has only one. Such subgraphs satisfy the [Helly property](helly-property.md). They need not contain every descendant of an included vertex in any chosen rooting, unlike the descendant-closed data-structure [subtree](subtree.md) convention.
