# Janson lower-tail bound by independent thinning

↑ **Parent:** [Janson inequality](janson-inequality.md)

For increasing containment indicators, attach independent Bernoulli selectors of parameter $t$. [Janson inequality](janson-inequality.md) applied to the selected events gives $\mathbb E(1-t)^X\le\exp(-t\mu+t^2\Delta/2)$. Set $t=1-e^{-s}$ and apply the [exponential Markov bound](exponential-markov-bound.md). The estimates $s-s^2/2\le1-e^{-s}\le s$ give exponent at most $-sa+s^2(\mu+\Delta)/2$. Optimizing at $s=a/(\mu+\Delta)$ proves the displayed lower-tail bound, with the dependency sum ordered and excluding the diagonal.
