# Surface area element

↑ **Parent:** [Surface integral](surface-integral.md)

For a regular [parametrization](parametric-equation.md) $\mathbf X(u,v)$ of a surface in three-dimensional [Euclidean space](euclidean-norm.md), the scalar surface area element is the area of the infinitesimal parameter parallelogram after mapping. Its value is the displayed [cross product](cross-product.md) magnitude. The [vector surface area element](vector-surface-area-element.md) additionally records the chosen orientation.
