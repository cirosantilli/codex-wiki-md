# Helium-3 freeze-out estimate with Gamow factors

↑ **Parent:** [Temperature for helium-3 freeze-out](temperature-for-helium-3-freeze-out.md)

In the pp-I limit, [helium-3 relaxation time](helium-3-relaxation-time.md) is $\tau^{-1}=n_1\sqrt{2\lambda_{11}\lambda_{33}}$. At fixed proton [number density](number-density.md), each [thermonuclear reaction rate](thermonuclear-reaction-rate.md) coefficient obeys $\lambda(T)/\lambda(T_0)=(T/T_0)^{-2/3}\exp[-\eta(T_0)((T_0/T)^{1/3}-1)]$. Taking their geometric mean gives the displayed relaxation ratio with $\bar\eta=[\eta_{11}(T_0)+\eta_{33}(T_0)]/2$. A local power-law approximation instead gives $\tau\propto T^{-(\alpha+\beta)/2}$. These are estimates rather than a complete radial model: the proton [number density](number-density.md), composition and pp-II branch change along a real [stellar composition profile](stellar-composition-profile.md).
