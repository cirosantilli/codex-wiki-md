# Finite-index characteristic soluble subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Virtually solvable group](virtually-solvable-group.md)

Every [virtually soluble group](virtually-solvable-group.md) $K$ has a soluble [characteristic subgroup](characteristic-subgroup.md) of finite index. First take a soluble [normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md) $K_0$ of finite index by the [subgroup core](core-group-theory.md) construction. Choose a soluble normal subgroup $R\supseteq K_0$ maximizing its image size in the finite quotient $K/K_0$. For every soluble normal subgroup $S$, the product $RS$ is soluble, because its quotient by $R$ is a quotient of $S$. Maximality forces $S\leq R$. Hence $R$ is the unique largest soluble normal subgroup and is invariant under all [automorphisms](automorphism.md). This avoids incorrectly intersecting infinitely many conjugates when proving closure under [group extensions](group-extension.md).
