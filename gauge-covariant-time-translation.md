# Gauge-covariant time translation

↑ **Parent:** [Gauge transformation](gauge-transformation.md)

For the [Maxwell field](electromagnetic-field.md), the variation $\delta A_\nu=\alpha F_{0\nu}=\alpha(\partial_0A_\nu-\partial_\nu A_0)$ is time translation combined with a [gauge transformation](gauge-transformation.md). With constant $\alpha$, $\delta F_{\mu\nu}=\alpha\partial_0F_{\mu\nu}$, and the Lagrangian changes by $\alpha\partial_0\mathcal L$. Its [Noether current](noether-current.md) is directly the time component of the [gauge-invariant Maxwell stress-energy tensor](gauge-invariant-maxwell-stress-energy-tensor.md), providing the physical energy density without a potential-dependent improvement step.
