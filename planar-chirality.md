# Planar chirality

↑ **Parent:** [Stereochemistry](stereochemistry-split.md)

Chirality associated with the arrangement of [substituents](substituent.md) relative to a plane, as in an unsymmetrically substituted coordinated cyclopentadienyl ring. Two planar-chiral [coordination ligands](ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md) can give a meso arrangement and a pair of enantiomeric arrangements.
