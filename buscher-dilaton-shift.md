# Buscher dilaton shift

↑ **Parent:** [Buscher procedure](buscher-procedure.md)

The quantum measure in abelian [T-duality](t-duality.md) changes the conventional [dilaton](dilaton.md) by $\varphi'=\varphi-\frac12\log g_{yy}$ in alpha-prime-one units for a spacelike isometry. The regulated Gaussian integration over the auxiliary gauge field produces this curvature coupling. It is consistent with invariance of the string-frame density $e^{-2\varphi}\sqrt{\det g}$ under the local Buscher rules.
