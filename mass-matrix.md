# Mass matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Finite element method](finite-element-method.md)

The mass matrix is the [Gram matrix](gram-matrix.md) of a [finite element](finite-element.md) basis in the [L2 inner product](l2-inner-product.md). Its coefficient [quadratic form](quadratic-form.md) is the squared [L2 norm](l2-norm.md) of the represented trial function, so it is a [positive-definite matrix](positive-definite-matrix.md) for a linearly independent basis.
