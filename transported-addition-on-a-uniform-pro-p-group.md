# Transported addition on a uniform pro-p group

↑ **Parent:** [Uniform pro-p group](uniform-pro-p-group.md)

For a [uniform pro-p group](uniform-pro-p-group.md), the power map $x\mapsto x^{p^n}$ is a [homeomorphism](homeomorphism.md) onto $G^{p^n}$. Transport multiplication back to $G$ by

$$
x+_ny=(x^{p^n}y^{p^n})^{p^{-n}}.
$$

Unique roots make this an associative [group](group-split.md) operation. For odd $p$, [group commutator](group-commutator.md) collection gives $x+_ny\equiv y+_nx\pmod{G^{p^{n+1}}}$ and $x+_ny\equiv x+_{n-1}y\pmod{G^{p^n}}$. The operations converge to addition in the [Lie algebra of a uniform pro-p group](lie-algebra-of-a-uniform-pro-p-group.md).
