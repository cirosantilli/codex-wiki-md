# Principal part of a Laurent series

↑ **Parent:** [Laurent series](laurent-series.md)

The principal part consists of all negative-power terms of a [Laurent series](laurent-series.md) about its center. On a punctured neighborhood of an isolated singularity it is zero for a [removable singularity](removable-singularity.md), a nonzero finite sum for a [pole](pole.md), and an infinite series for an [essential singularity](essential-singularity.md). A pole's order is the largest $N$ with $a_{-N}\ne0$. At infinity use the local variable $w=1/z$: negative powers of $w$ correspond to positive powers of $z$. A Laurent series on an annulus with strictly positive inner radius may have a principal part without the function being defined close enough to the center to classify an isolated singularity there.
