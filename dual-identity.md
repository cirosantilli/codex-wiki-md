# Dual identity

↑ **Parent:** [Social identity](social-identity.md)

Concurrent representation of a shared superordinate identity and distinct subgroup identities. For example, different departments can remain recognizable within one university. [Dual identity](dual-identity.md) can combine inclusion with distinctiveness, but its effects depend on whether both memberships are credible and valued.
