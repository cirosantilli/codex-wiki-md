# Flavor-changing neutral current

↑ **Parent:** [Neutral current](neutral-current.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flavor-changing_neutral_current)

A flavor-changing neutral current changes fermion flavor without changing electric charge. Such currents are absent at tree level in the Standard Model and are strongly suppressed at loop level by the [Glashow-Iliopoulos-Maiani mechanism](glashow-iliopoulos-maiani-mechanism.md).
