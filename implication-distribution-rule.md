# Implication distribution rule

↑ **Parent:** [Natural deduction](natural-deduction.md)

From $\phi\to(\psi\to\rho)$ infer $(\phi\to\psi)\to(\phi\to\rho)$. Assume $\phi\to\psi$, then assume $\phi$. [Implication elimination rule](implication-elimination-rule.md) gives $\psi$ and $\psi\to\rho$, hence $\rho$. Discharge $\phi$ and then $\phi\to\psi$ by the [implication introduction rule](implication-introduction-rule.md). The original premise and any surrounding assumptions remain available.
