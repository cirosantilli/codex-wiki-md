# Pure thermodynamic phase

↑ **Parent:** [Phase coexistence](phase-coexistence.md)

A [pure thermodynamic phase](pure-thermodynamic-phase.md) is an extremal equilibrium Gibbs state rather than a probabilistic mixture of distinct macroscopic phases. On an ordered Ising branch, taking the [thermodynamic limit](thermodynamic-limit.md) before removing a selecting [conjugate field](field-conjugate-to-an-order-parameter.md) gives a nonzero [magnetization](magnetization.md). A symmetric mixture can instead have zero mean but a nondecaying two-point contribution. The [connected correlation function](connected-correlation-function.md) and [correlation length](correlation-length.md) describing ordinary critical fluctuations should be evaluated in a chosen pure phase when this distinction matters.
