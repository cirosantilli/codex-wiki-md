# Weierstrass addition formula

↑ **Parent:** [Weierstrass elliptic function](weierstrass-elliptic-function.md)

For generic fixed $w$, regard the right side as an [elliptic function](elliptic-function.md) of $z$. The apparent [pole](pole.md) at zero cancels, giving limiting value $\wp(w)$. At $z=w$ the slope ratio is finite. At $z=-w$, its squared slope has the same double principal part as $\wp(z+w)$ and no [simple pole](simple-pole.md) term. These exhaust the possible [poles](pole.md) because the [Weierstrass elliptic function](weierstrass-elliptic-function.md) has degree two. The difference is therefore an entire [elliptic function](elliptic-function.md), hence constant by [Liouville theorem](liouville-theorem.md), and its value at zero is zero. [Meromorphic](meromorphic-function.md) continuation extends the identity to the exceptional parameters.
