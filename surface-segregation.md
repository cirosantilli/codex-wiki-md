# Surface segregation

↑ **Parent:** [Alloy](alloy.md)

Enrichment of one component at a material surface relative to its bulk composition. Surface free energies, adsorbates, elastic [strain](strain.md) and the energetic cost of changing bulk bonding jointly determine equilibrium enrichment.
