# Magic state

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum state](quantum-state.md)

In the qubit stabilizer resource theory, a state outside the [convex hull](convex-hull.md) of [stabilizer states](stabilizer-state.md). For one qubit this means outside the [stabilizer octahedron](stabilizer-octahedron.md). Such a state can supply a non-Clifford resource in a circuit otherwise restricted to Clifford operations and standard measurements.
