# Passive disk

↑ **Parent:** [Thin disk](thin-disk.md)

A passive [astrophysical disk](astrophysical-disk.md) receives most of its heat from external irradiation rather than internal [viscous dissipation](viscous-dissipation.md). For a stellar disk, a rough criterion is $GM\dot M/R_*\ll L_*$. The actual local requirement compares absorbed stellar flux with viscous flux; incidence angle, shadowing and absorption can invalidate a luminosity comparison alone.
