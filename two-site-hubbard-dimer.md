# Two-site Hubbard dimer

↑ **Parent:** [Hubbard model](hubbard-model.md)

Two localized orbitals coupled by hopping, with an on-site penalty for [double occupancy](double-occupancy.md). At two-electron filling the competition between hopping and local repulsion can be solved exactly within the finite basis. It gives a compact example of a molecular bonding crossover and [electron correlation](electron-correlation.md); it is not by itself a complete real-molecule binding curve.
