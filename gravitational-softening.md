# Gravitational softening

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar dynamics](stellar-dynamics.md)

[Gravitational softening](gravitational-softening.md) regularizes a simulation particle's short-distance [force](force.md), for example by replacing $-Gm/r$ by $-Gm/\sqrt{r^2+\epsilon^2}$. It suppresses artificial close encounters and effectively raises the diffusion cutoff to order $\max(\epsilon,Gm/u^2)$, but alters the resolved [force](force.md) below $\epsilon$. Particle number, softening and duration must be chosen together; a point-particle relaxation estimate alone does not specify a simulation's accuracy.
