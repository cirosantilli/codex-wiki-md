# Exponential of an idempotent linear map

↑ **Parent:** [Projection (linear algebra)](projection-linear-algebra.md)

For an [idempotent linear map](projection-linear-algebra.md) $P$, induction gives $P^j=P$ for all integers $j\geq1$. Summing the [matrix exponential](matrix-exponential.md) series therefore proves the displayed identity. The [direct sum](direct-sum.md) of its [kernel of a linear map](kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) and [image of a linear map](image-of-a-linear-map.md) is fixed and scaled by $e^\alpha$, respectively. The result requires no [Hermitian matrix](hermitian-operator.md) hypothesis and is valid for real or complex $\alpha$.
