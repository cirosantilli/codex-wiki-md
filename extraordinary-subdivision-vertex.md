# Extraordinary subdivision vertex

↑ **Parent:** [Subdivision surface](subdivision-surface.md)

An [extraordinary subdivision vertex](extraordinary-subdivision-vertex.md) has a valence different from that of the regular refinement lattice. Regular [box spline](box-spline.md) calculations do not alone establish smoothness at this vertex. The finite local [subdivision matrix](subdivision-matrix.md), its [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md), and the associated [characteristic map of a subdivision surface](characteristic-map-of-a-subdivision-surface.md) determine convergence and tangent behavior.
