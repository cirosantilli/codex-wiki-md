# Eigenpair

↑ **Parent:** [Eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md)

An eigenpair consists of an [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) and a nonzero corresponding [eigenvector](eigenvector.md) of a specified [linear operator](linear-operator.md). Scaling the [eigenvector](eigenvector.md) by a nonzero scalar gives another representation of the same eigendirection. For an approximate pair $(\widehat\lambda,\widehat v)$, its [eigenpair residual](eigenpair-residual.md) is $A\widehat v-\widehat\lambda\widehat v$; a small residual alone need not give a small [eigenvector](eigenvector.md) error without spectral separation and conditioning information.
