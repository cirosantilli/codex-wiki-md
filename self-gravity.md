# Self-gravity

↑ **Parent:** [Newtonian gravitational field](newtonian-gravitational-field.md)

Self-gravity is the [gravitational acceleration](gravitational-acceleration.md) produced by a system's own [mass density](density.md). It is obtained by solving the [Poisson equation for Newtonian gravity](poisson-equation-for-newtonian-gravity.md) for that density, rather than treating the [Newtonian gravitational potential](newtonian-gravitational-potential.md) as an externally fixed field. It competes with [pressure](pressure.md) and rotational support in [gravitational instability of an astrophysical disk](gravitational-instability-of-an-astrophysical-disk-split.md).
