# Optimal cylinder for a reciprocal-square constraint

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrange multiplier](lagrange-multiplier.md)

For $q,C>0$, minimize a cylinder's [volume](volume.md) $V=\pi R^2H$ over $R,H>0$ subject to $q^2/R^2+\pi^2/H^2=C$. Set $u=q^2/R^2$ and $v=\pi^2/H^2$. Then $u+v=C$ and $V=\pi^2q^2/(u\sqrt v)$. Minimizing [volume](volume.md) is equivalent to maximizing $u^2(C-u)$ on $0<u<C$. Its unique interior maximum is at $u=2C/3$, $v=C/3$, with divergence of [volume](volume.md) at both endpoints. Thus the unique global optimum has

$$
\frac HR=\frac{\sqrt2\pi}{q},\qquad
V_{\min}=\frac{3\sqrt3\pi^2q^2}{2C^{3/2}}.
$$
