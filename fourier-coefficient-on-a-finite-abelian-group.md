# Fourier coefficient on a finite abelian group

↑ **Parent:** [Normalized Fourier analysis on a finite abelian group](normalized-fourier-analysis-on-a-finite-abelian-group.md)

For a scalar function on a [finite abelian group](finite-abelian-group.md), its coefficient at an [additive character](additive-character.md) $\chi$ is the displayed uniform [expectation](expected-value.md). On $\mathbb F_3^d$, write $\chi_\xi(x)=e^{2\pi i\xi\cdot x/3}$. The coefficient at the constant character is the mean of $f$, and the [Parseval identity on a finite group](parseval-identity-on-a-finite-group.md) becomes $\sum_\chi|\widehat f(\chi)|^2=\mathbb E|f|^2$ after relabelling characters to match the transform convention.
