# Coefficient-dominated entrywise positivity

↑ **Parent:** [Schur product theorem](schur-product-theorem.md)

Let real [power series](power-series.md) $f(t)=\sum_{k\geq0}f_kt^k$ and $g(t)=\sum_{k\geq0}g_kt^k$ converge on $[-1,1]$, with $f_k\geq|g_k|$ for every index including zero. If $X\succeq0$ has entries there, applying $f$ within two diagonal blocks and $g$ across them preserves [positive semidefiniteness](positive-semidefinite-matrix.md). Indeed the [coefficient](coefficient.md) [matrix](matrix.md) $H_k$ is a [bipartite block-constant positive semidefinite matrix](bipartite-block-constant-positive-semidefinite-matrix.md). Each $H_k\circ X^{\circ k}$ is [positive semidefinite](positive-semidefinite-matrix.md) by the [Schur product theorem](schur-product-theorem.md), and their sum converges to the transformed [matrix](matrix.md). Closedness of the [positive semidefinite cone](positive-semidefinite-cone.md) completes the proof. Convergence at one gives $\sum f_k<\infty$, so [coefficient](coefficient.md) domination ensures [absolute convergence](absolute-convergence.md) of both series throughout the interval. Without the zero-index condition, $f=-1$, $g=0$ and $X=0$ give a counterexample.
