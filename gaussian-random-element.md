# Gaussian random element

↑ **Parent:** [Hilbert-space-valued random variable](hilbert-space-valued-random-variable.md)

A random element $G$ of a [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) is Gaussian when every continuous linear functional of $G$ has a [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md). Its mean and [covariance operator](covariance-operator.md) determine its distribution.
