# Modified Arrhenius equation

↑ **Parent:** [Arrhenius equation](arrhenius-equation.md)

The [Modified Arrhenius equation](modified-arrhenius-equation.md) allows a power of [thermodynamic temperature](thermodynamic-temperature.md) in the [pre-exponential factor](pre-exponential-factor.md):

$$
k(T)=A_*\left(\frac{T}{T_*}\right)^n\exp\left(-\frac{E_0}{RT}\right),
\qquad T_*>0.
$$

Here $A_*$, $E_0$, $n$, and $T_*$ are constant model parameters. Using the [dimensionless quantity](dimensionless-quantity.md) $T/T_*$ leaves $A_*$ with the same units as $k$; in the equivalent convention $BT^n\exp[-E_0/(RT)]$, $B$ instead has units of $k$ multiplied by $\mathrm K^{-n}$. The power exponent $n$ here is not an [order of reaction](order-of-reaction.md). The IUPAC definition uses that equivalent $BT^n$ convention.[modified Arrhenius equation](iupac-compendium-of-chemical-terminology-gold-book-by-international-union-of-pure-and-applied-chemistry/modified-arrhenius-equation.md)

Applying the [apparent activation energy](apparent-activation-energy.md) definition yields the original short calculation

$$
\frac{d\ln(k/k_{\mathrm{ref}})}{dT}=\frac nT+\frac{E_0}{RT^2},
\qquad E_{a,\mathrm{app}}(T)=E_0+nRT.
$$

For the orange synthetic [Arrhenius plot](arrhenius-plot.md), $n=2$, $T_*=300\,\mathrm K$, and $E_0=60\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$. Its [pre-exponential factor](pre-exponential-factor.md) changes by $(310/300)^2\approx1.06778$ from $300$ to $310\,\mathrm K$; combined with the [exponential function](exponential-function.md), $k(310\,\mathrm K)\approx0.231991\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$, compared with $0.217266\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$ for the constant-[pre-exponential factor](pre-exponential-factor.md) model. The diagram uses those models only to reveal the difference between an exponent parameter and a slope-derived [activation energy](activation-energy.md).
