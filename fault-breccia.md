# Fault breccia

↑ **Parent:** [Fault rock](fault-rock.md)

Coarse angular rock fragments produced by crushing during [fault slip](fault-slip.md), commonly in a finer matrix. Fine-grained, poorly cohesive comminuted material is [fault gouge](fault-gouge.md); cohesive material formed by similar grain-size reduction can be called [cataclasite](cataclasite.md).
