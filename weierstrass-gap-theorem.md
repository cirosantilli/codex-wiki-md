# Weierstrass gap theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Weierstrass gap](weierstrass-gap.md)

At every point of a smooth projective curve of genus $g$, exactly $g$ positive integers are [Weierstrass gap](weierstrass-gap.md). Indeed, each quotient $L(np)/L((n-1)p)$ has dimension zero or one, while [Riemann--Roch](riemann-roch-theorem.md) gives $\ell(np)=n+1-g$ for all $n>2g-2$.
