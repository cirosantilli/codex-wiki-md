# Hyperbolic sine modulus identity

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic sine](hyperbolic-sine.md)

For real $x,y$, the [exponential function](exponential-function.md) definitions give $\sinh(x+iy)=\sinh x\cos y+i\cosh x\sin y$. Taking the squared [complex modulus](complex-modulus.md) and using $\cosh^2x-\sinh^2x=1$ gives the identity. In particular $|\sinh(x+iy)|\geq|\sinh x|$, uniformly in $y$. This controls the vertical sides of rectangular [contours](complex-integration-contour.md) in [residue theorem](residue-theorem.md) arguments with [hyperbolic functions](hyperbolic-function.md).
