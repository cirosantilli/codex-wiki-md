# Black-hole mass measurement

↑ **Parent:** [Astrophysical black hole](astrophysical-black-hole-split.md)

An inference of a compact object's gravitating [mass](mass.md) from specified tracers and a dynamical or [radiation](radiation.md) model. Resolved stellar or gas orbits, [spectroscopic binary](spectroscopic-binary.md) dynamics, and [reverberation mapping](reverberation-mapping.md) supply complementary approaches with different distance, geometry and equilibrium assumptions. A short variability timescale constrains size but is not a unique [mass](mass.md) measurement.
