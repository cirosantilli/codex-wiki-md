# Potential-vorticity conservation

↑ **Parent:** [Potential-vorticity evolution equation](potential-vorticity-evolution-equation.md)

In unforced adiabatic inviscid motion, [potential vorticity](potential-vorticity.md) is conserved following a parcel:

$$
Dq/Dt=0.
$$

The linearization about a basic state is $(\partial_t+\bar{\mathbf u}\cdot\nabla)q'+\mathbf u'\cdot\nabla\bar q=0$. This separates advection of a disturbance from its generation by displacement across a basic [potential-vorticity gradient](potential-vorticity-gradient.md).
