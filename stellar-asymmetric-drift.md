# Stellar asymmetric drift

↑ **Parent:** [Galaxy dynamics](galaxy-dynamics.md)

[Stellar asymmetric drift](stellar-asymmetric-drift.md) is the lag of a pressure-supported [stellar population](stellar-population.md)'s mean rotation behind the local [circular speed](circular-speed.md). Neglecting the tilted [velocity ellipsoid](velocity-ellipsoid.md), radial [Jeans equation](jeans-equation.md) balance gives

$$
v_c^2-\overline v_\phi^2=\sigma_R^2\left[-\frac{d\log(n\sigma_R^2)}{d\log R}-1+\frac{\sigma_\phi^2}{\sigma_R^2}\right].
$$

A declining density and larger random velocities generally increase the lag; gravitational balance is supplied partly by random-motion stresses.
