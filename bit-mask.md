# Bit mask

↑ **Parent:** [Programming language](programming-language-split.md)

A word selecting specified binary positions under a bitwise operation. To extract a stored bit at position $b$, logically shift by $b$ and combine with the mask `1`. Grouping thirty-two cells into one [Java](java-programming-language.md) integer permits a compact representation of a [Conway Game of Life](conway-game-of-life.md) board.
