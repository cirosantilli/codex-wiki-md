# Ice-floe oil painting fraction

↑ **Parent:** [Oil spill under sea ice](oil-spill-under-sea-ice.md)

If a [buoyant plume](buoyant-plume.md) of diameter $d$ supplies oil at volume rate $Q$ to an [ice floe](ice-floe.md) moving at speed $v$, the plume sweeps area $dv$ per unit time. Retaining a layer of depth $h$ over that area would require volume rate $dvh$, so [conservation of mass](mass-conservation.md) gives the painted fraction $\min\{1,Q/(dvh)\}$. Discontinuous painting starts when $v>Q/(dh)$. This estimate assumes all arriving oil is retained and a constant track width equal to the plume diameter; the plume's circular cross-sectional area does not enter the swept-area rate.
