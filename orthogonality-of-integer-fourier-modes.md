# Orthogonality of integer Fourier modes

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md)

For an integer $m$, direct integration gives zero unless $m=0$, in which case it gives one. Products of this identity on the unit cube turn [integrals](integral.md) of finite [exponential sums](exponential-sum.md) into counts of integer solutions. This is the elementary orthogonality used in the [Vinogradov mean value](vinogradov-mean-value.md).
