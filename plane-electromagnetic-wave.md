# Plane electromagnetic wave

↑ **Parent:** [Electromagnetic wave](electromagnetic-wave-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plane_electromagnetic_wave)

A monochromatic vacuum [plane electromagnetic wave](plane-electromagnetic-wave.md) has phase $\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf r-\omega t$, where $\mathbf k$ is the [wavevector](wavevector.md), $\omega>0$ the [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md), and $\omega=c|\mathbf k|$. Its field amplitudes obey $\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf E_0=\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf B_0=0$ and $\mathbf B_0=\mathbf k\times\mathbf E_0/\omega$.

For the [linear polarization](linear-polarization.md) and orientation in the visualization, choose a right-handed [Cartesian coordinate system](cartesian-coordinate-system.md) and put

$$
\mathbf E(z,t)=E_0\cos(kz-\omega t)\widehat{\mathbf x},\qquad
\mathbf B(z,t)=\frac{E_0}{c}\cos(kz-\omega t)\widehat{\mathbf y},\qquad
k>0,\quad\omega=ck.
$$

Constant phase means $z=ct+\text{constant}$, so propagation is along $+z$. The [cross product](cross-product.md) $\widehat{\mathbf x}\times\widehat{\mathbf y}=\widehat{\mathbf z}$ fixes the positive [Poynting vector](poynting-vector.md). Both fields reverse together every half-period and are in phase. Reversing the propagation direction with the same electric orientation reverses the magnetic orientation.

These fields are spatially uniform across every transverse plane. An infinite [plane electromagnetic wave](plane-electromagnetic-wave.md) is an idealization with infinite total [electromagnetic energy](electromagnetic-energy.md); it is useful as a local approximation and as a building block for [superposition](superposition-principle.md). For [frequency](frequency.md) $f=100\,\mathrm{MHz}$, $\omega=2\pi f$ and [wavelength](wavelength.md) $\lambda=c/f\simeq3.00\,\mathrm m$, independently of $E_0$.
