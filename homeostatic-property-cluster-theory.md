# Homeostatic property cluster theory

↑ **Parent:** [Natural kind](natural-kind.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homeostatic_property_cluster_theory)

An account of some [natural kinds](natural-kind.md) in terms of traits whose association is sustained by causal processes, without requiring every member to possess every trait. For [species](species.md), shared descent, [gene flow](gene-flow.md) and developmental processes can sustain relevant similarities. Whether a cluster supports reliable inference is an empirical issue.[sections 0.0–0.1](homeostasis-species-and-higher-taxa-by-richard-boyd.md)
