# Graphene zone-boundary band separation

↑ **Parent:** [Graphene](graphene.md)

The local [energy](energy-split.md) difference between the conduction and [valence bands](valence-band.md) along the boundary of the first [Brillouin zone](brillouin-zone.md). In a nearest-neighbour model it vanishes at the corner valleys and reaches $2t$ at edge midpoints; this angular variation is compatible with a globally gapless material.
