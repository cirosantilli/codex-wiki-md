# Prime Gamma 1 cusp counts and widths

↑ **Parent:** [Number of cusps of Gamma 1 of prime level](number-of-cusps-of-gamma-1-of-prime-level.md)

For $p\ge5$, the [Gamma 1 congruence subgroup](gamma-1-congruence-subgroup.md) has $(p-1)/2$ [modular cusps](cusp-of-a-modular-group.md) of [cusp width](width-of-a-cusp.md) one and $(p-1)/2$ of [cusp width](width-of-a-cusp.md) $p$. Primitive columns modulo $p$, taken up to sign, are acted on by $(a,c)\mapsto(a+bc,c)$. The classes with $c=0$ have [cusp width](width-of-a-cusp.md) one, the others [cusp width](width-of-a-cusp.md) $p$. There are no effective elliptic stabilizers. The [genus formula for a modular curve](genus-formula-for-a-modular-curve.md) gives genus $(p-5)(p-7)/24$.
