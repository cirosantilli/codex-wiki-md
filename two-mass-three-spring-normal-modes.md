# Two-mass three-spring normal modes

↑ **Parent:** [Normal mode](normal-mode.md)

Two positive masses linked in series between fixed walls by three identical [springs](spring.md) of [spring constant](spring-constant.md) $k$ have [equations of motion](equation-of-motion.md) $m_1\ddot x_1=-2kx_1+kx_2$ and $m_2\ddot x_2=kx_1-2kx_2$. A [normal mode](normal-mode.md) $x_j=A_j\cos\omega t$ exists when $(2k-m_1\omega^2)(2k-m_2\omega^2)-k^2=0$, giving the displayed [angular frequencies](angular-frequency.md). Its amplitude ratio is $A_2/A_1=(2k-m_1\omega^2)/k$: positive for the lower [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md), negative for the higher one. Equal masses give amplitude ratios $+1,-1$ and squared [angular frequencies](angular-frequency.md) $k/m,3k/m$.
