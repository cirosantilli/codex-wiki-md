# Neap tide

↑ **Parent:** [Tide](tide.md)

A [neap tide](neap-tide.md) has a reduced high-to-low tidal range when the directions to the [Moon](moon.md) and [Sun](sun.md) make approximately a right [angle](angle.md) at the [Earth](earth.md). Their principal tidal contributions then partly oppose one another. The solar and lunar contributions are not equal, so this does not mean that tides disappear.
