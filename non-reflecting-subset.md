# Non-reflecting subset

↑ **Parent:** [Stationary set](stationary-set.md)

A [subset](subset.md) $X\subseteq\lambda$ is non-reflecting in the indicated sense if it is not stationary below any $\delta<\lambda$ of uncountable [cofinality](cofinality.md). The [subset](subset.md) itself need not be stationary. Nonreflection is inherited by [subsets](subset.md). With this definition every [subset](subset.md) of $\omega_1$ is non-reflecting, since all smaller [ordinals](ordinal.md) have countable [cofinality](cofinality.md).
