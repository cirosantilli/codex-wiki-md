# Phase-space identification of the Polyakov metric

↑ **Parent:** [Polyakov action](polyakov-action.md)

For positive lapse, eliminating momentum gives $(\dot X-uX')^2/(2e)-eT^2X'^2/2$. This is the [Polyakov action](polyakov-action.md) for $\gamma_{ab}=\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix}u^2-e^2T^2&u\\u&1\end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$, up to a [Weyl transformation](weyl-transformation.md). Thus $e=1/T,u=0$ is [conformal gauge](conformal-gauge.md).
