# Ocean heat content

↑ **Parent:** [Planetary surface energy balance](planetary-surface-energy-balance.md)

Ocean heat content relative to a reference [temperature](temperature.md) $T_f$ is an integral through the water column. For a uniform column of depth $H$, $Q=\rho_wc_wH(T-T_f)$ per horizontal area. Melting ice already at its fusion temperature requires its [latent heat](latent-heat.md); warming cold ice first requires additional sensible [heat](heat.md). A surface temperature alone does not determine the column integral.
