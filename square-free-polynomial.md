# Square-free polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Factorization of polynomials](factorization-of-polynomials.md)

A nonzero [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) over a [field](field.md) is square-free if no square of a nonconstant [irreducible polynomial](irreducible-polynomial.md) divides it. Equivalently, in its [factorization of polynomials](factorization-of-polynomials.md), every [irreducible polynomial](irreducible-polynomial.md) factor occurs with exponent one. For example, $x(x^2+1)$ is square-free over the [real numbers](real-number.md), whereas $(x-1)^2$ is not. Square-freeness over a given [field](field.md) concerns factors in that [field](field.md); over the [real numbers](real-number.md) or [complex numbers](complex-number.md), it is equivalent to having no repeated [polynomial roots](root-of-a-polynomial.md) after splitting over the [complex numbers](complex-number.md).
