# Ramsey ensemble coherence

↑ **Parent:** [Ramsey interferometry](ramsey-interferometry.md)

For an ensemble with static detunings $\delta+\eta$, ideal same-phase quarter-turn pulses give $\overline p_2(T)=[1+\operatorname{Re}(e^{i\delta T}\chi(T))]/2$. Here $\chi$ is the [characteristic function](characteristic-function-split.md) of the offset distribution. A mean-zero Gaussian of RMS width $\sigma$ gives $\chi=e^{-\sigma^2T^2/2}$; a symmetric uniform interval gives a [sinc function](sinc-function.md). A width alone does not uniquely specify the envelope.
