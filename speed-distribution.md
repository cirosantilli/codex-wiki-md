# Speed distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Kinetic theory](kinetic-theory-split.md)

A [speed distribution](speed-distribution.md) is the [probability distribution](probability-distribution-split.md) of the magnitude of [velocity](velocity.md). When it has a [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) $f$, the fraction of particles with [speeds](speed.md) between $v$ and $v+dv$ is $f(v)\,dv$, and $\int_0^\infty f(v)\,dv=1$. The [Maxwell speed distribution](maxwell-speed-distribution.md) describes an equilibrium [ideal gas](ideal-gas-split.md); the [effusive beam speed distribution](effusive-beam-speed-distribution.md) weights that population by its rate of arrival.
