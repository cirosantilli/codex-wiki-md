# Penalty method

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical optimization](mathematical-optimization-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penalty_method)

A [penalty method](penalty-method.md) incorporates constraint violation into the objective with a parameter controlling its cost. For an equality constraint $c(x)=0$, the [quadratic penalty method](quadratic-penalty-method.md) adds a multiple of $\|c(x)\|_2^2$; a [power penalty](power-penalty.md) uses higher even powers. The penalty can allow infeasible iterates while encouraging feasibility as the cost grows. This differs from a [logarithmic barrier](logarithmic-barrier.md), whose domain itself excludes the constraint boundary and exterior. Existence of a penalized stationary point, convergence of its constraint violation and convergence of the primal points are separate questions.
