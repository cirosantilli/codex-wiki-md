# Electron-hole recombination

↑ **Parent:** [Electron hole](electron-hole.md)

An [Electron](electron.md) filling an [electron hole](electron-hole.md) removes both excitations relative to the filled-band background. Their [electric charges](electric-charge.md) cancel, consistent with [charge conservation](charge-conservation.md); the remaining [Electrons](electron.md) are still present. The released [energy](energy.md) can leave through [photon emission](photon-emission.md) or other channels, including [phonons](phonon.md). Formation of an [exciton](exciton.md) instead retains the excited [Electron](electron.md) and [electron hole](electron-hole.md) as a bound pair; recombination removes the pair. A [valence band](valence-band.md) [Electron](electron.md) filling a nearby vacancy merely moves the [electron hole](electron-hole.md) and does not remove the total vacancy count.
