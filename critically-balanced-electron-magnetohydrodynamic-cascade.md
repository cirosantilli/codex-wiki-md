# Critically balanced electron-magnetohydrodynamic cascade

↑ **Parent:** [Electron-magnetohydrodynamic cascade](electron-magnetohydrodynamic-cascade.md)

[Critical balance](critical-balance.md) sets $\tau_{\mathrm{nl}}\sim\tau_w$, hence $l_\parallel\sim l_\perp/b_l$. A local strong cascade with constant [energy flux](energy-flux.md) has $\epsilon\sim v_A^3d_i b_l^3/l_\perp^2$, giving the displayed exponents and perpendicular [magnetic energy spectrum](magnetic-energy-spectrum.md) $E_B(k_\perp)\propto k_\perp^{-7/3}$. The anisotropy becomes stronger towards small perpendicular scales.
