# Center of the mapping class group of the torus

↑ **Parent:** [Alexander method](alexander-method.md)

The center of $\operatorname{Mod}(T^2)$ has order two. A central class commutes with twists about two curves intersecting once, hence preserves both curve classes. The [Alexander method](alexander-method.md) reduces it to the identity or the elliptic involution $\iota:x\mapsto-x$, and both are central.
