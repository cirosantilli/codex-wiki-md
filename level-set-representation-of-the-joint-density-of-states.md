# Level-set representation of the joint density of states

↑ **Parent:** [Joint density of states](joint-density-of-states.md)

For a smooth two-dimensional transition [energy](energy-split.md) $D(\mathbf k)$ and a regular level $D=E$, the coarea identity gives $\int d^2k\,\delta(D(\mathbf k)-E)=\int_{D=E}dl/|\nabla D|$. Critical points require a limiting or distributional treatment; the apparent small contour at a parabolic edge is compensated by its small [energy](energy-split.md) gradient.
