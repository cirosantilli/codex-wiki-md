# Frequency ratio of corotating clocks

↑ **Parent:** [Relativistic Doppler effect](relativistic-doppler-effect.md)

For a freely propagating [photon](photon.md) between emitters and receivers rotating with the same [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md) in flat [spacetime](spacetime.md), $\nu_r/\nu_e=\gamma_r/\gamma_e$, provided the [photon](photon.md) path is unobstructed. At equal radii the ratio is one. Positions and [velocities](velocity.md) at the actual emission and reception events must be used.
