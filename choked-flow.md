# Choked flow

↑ **Parent:** [Compressible flow](compressible-flow-split.md)

Flow whose [mass](mass.md) flux is limited by a sonic condition in a controlling section, for fixed upstream thermodynamic conditions. In a smooth isentropic converging-diverging [nozzle](nozzle.md), the throat reaches [Mach number](mach-number.md) one. Lowering downstream [pressure](pressure-split.md) further cannot indefinitely increase that fixed-upstream throat [mass](mass.md) flux; shocks and downstream structure can still change.
