# Titre

↑ **Parent:** [Titrant standardization](titrant-standardization.md)

Here a [titre](titre.md) means the delivered [titrant](titrant.md) [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) needed to reach the specified [titration endpoint](titration-endpoint.md), obtained from final minus initial [burette](burette.md) readings. Report explicitly whether a quoted [titre](titre.md) includes or excludes a [titration blank](titration-blank.md). This volume convention is distinct from uses of titre to express a solution's reacting strength.
