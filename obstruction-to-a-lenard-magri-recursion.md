# Obstruction to a Lenard-Magri recursion

↑ **Parent:** [Lenard-Magri recursion](lenard-magri-recursion.md)

The next step of a [Lenard-Magri recursion](lenard-magri-recursion.md) requires $-\Pi_2dH_{n-1}$ to be a [Hamiltonian vector field](hamiltonian-vector-field.md) for $\Pi_1$. It must be tangent to the [symplectic leaves](symplectic-leaf.md), and its contraction with the leaf [symplectic form](symplectic-form.md) must have a global potential, with a compatible smooth extension between leaves. Even the first step may fail: on $\mathbb R^2$, take $\Pi_1=0$, $\Pi_2=\partial_x\wedge\partial_y$ and $H_0=x$. Both [Poisson structures](poisson-structure.md) are compatible, but $\Pi_1dH_1=-\Pi_2dx$ cannot hold, because its left side is zero and its right side is nonzero.
