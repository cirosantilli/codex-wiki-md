# Membrane curvature

↑ **Parent:** [Cell membrane](cell-membrane-split.md)

Membrane shape affects the energetic cost and geometry of a bud, neck or protrusion. Proteins, lipid composition and mechanical forces can change curvature; curvature alone does not perform the topological reconnection required for [membrane fusion](membrane-fusion.md) or [membrane fission](membrane-fission.md).
