# Weak geometric p-rough path

↑ **Parent:** [Rough path](rough-path.md)

A weak geometric $p$-rough path is a continuous path in the [free step-N nilpotent Lie group](free-step-n-nilpotent-lie-group.md), with $N=\lfloor p\rfloor$, whose increments have finite $p$-variation for the [Carnot-Carathéodory distance](carnot-caratheodory-distance.md). Its multiplicative increments are $\mathbf X_{s,t}=\mathbf X_s^{-1}\otimes\mathbf X_t$. They obey the [Chen identity](chen-identity.md) and the algebraic symmetries of [path signatures](signature-of-a-bounded-variation-path.md).
