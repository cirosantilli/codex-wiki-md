# Osculating circle

↑ **Parent:** [Curvature of a plane curve](curvature-of-a-plane-curve.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osculating_circle)

At a point of a twice continuously differentiable [regular curve](regular-curve.md) with positive [curvature](curvature.md) $\kappa$, its osculating circle has [radius](radius.md) $1/\kappa$ and center $c=r+N_p/\kappa$, where $N_p$ is the [principal normal vector](principal-normal-vector.md). It matches the point, [tangent line](tangent-line.md) and [curvature](curvature.md). For a [space curve](space-curve.md), it lies in the plane spanned by the [unit tangent vector](unit-tangent-vector.md) and [principal normal vector](principal-normal-vector.md). The [parabola](parabola.md) example under [curvature of a plane curve](curvature-of-a-plane-curve.md) gives center $(0,1/2)$ and [radius](radius.md) $1/2$ at the origin.
