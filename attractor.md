# Attractor

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamical systems](dynamical-systems-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attractor)

An invariant set approached by trajectories from a neighborhood in the [state space](state-space.md) of a [dynamical system](dynamical-system.md). An attracting [equilibrium point](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) and an attracting [limit cycle](limit-cycle.md) are elementary examples. In more precise definitions, compactness and an appropriate stability or trapping condition are often also required.
