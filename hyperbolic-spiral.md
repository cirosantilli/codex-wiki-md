# Hyperbolic spiral

↑ **Parent:** [Plane curve](plane-curve.md)

A hyperbolic spiral is a [plane curve](plane-curve.md) whose radial [distance](distance.md) in [polar coordinates](polar-coordinates.md) is inversely proportional to the angular displacement from a fixed angle: $r=a/(\theta-\theta_0)$, on a branch where $r>0$. For $a>0$ and $\theta>\theta_0$, it winds infinitely often towards the origin as $\theta\to\infty$. Its geometric winding alone does not determine whether a moving [particle](particle-physics.md) traverses it in finite time: that also requires a law for the angular [velocity](velocity.md).
