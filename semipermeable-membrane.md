# Semipermeable membrane

↑ **Parent:** [Selective permeability](selective-permeability.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semipermeable_membrane)

A [semipermeable membrane](semipermeable-membrane.md) ideally passes the [solvent](solvent.md) while excluding the specified [solutes](solute.md). In the aqueous [osmosis](osmosis.md) model below, [water](water.md) can cross but the counted dissolved particles cannot. Real [selective permeability](selective-permeability.md) is often a matter of different rates, so this idealization needs checking over the measurement interval. Neither an impermeable sheet nor a freely mixing opening realizes this model.[Gold Book, osmotic pressure: definition of the solvent-only membrane](iupac-compendium-of-chemical-terminology-fifth-edition-gold-book-by-international-union-of-pure-and-applied-chemistry/osmotic-pressure.md)
