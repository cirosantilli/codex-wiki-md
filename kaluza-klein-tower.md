# Kaluza-Klein tower

↑ **Parent:** [Kaluza-Klein mode](kaluza-klein-mode.md)

For a field periodic on a circle of radius $R$, a [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md) gives modes with quantized internal momentum $n/R$. Each mode is a lower-dimensional field with the displayed squared mass. The entire sequence is the tower; retaining only modes below a chosen energy gives an [effective field theory](effective-field-theory.md). The tower is distinct from any individual [Kaluza-Klein mode](kaluza-klein-mode.md).
