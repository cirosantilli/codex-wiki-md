# Octahedral graph

↑ **Parent:** [Complete multipartite graph](complete-multipartite-graph.md)

The octahedral graph is the [graph](graph-split.md) obtained from $K_6$ by removing a [perfect matching](perfect-matching.md); equivalently it is $K_{2,2,2}$. Its [chromatic number](chromatic-number.md) is three, and it contains two vertex-disjoint [triangles in a graph](triangle-in-a-graph.md). A [complete bipartite graph](complete-bipartite-graph.md) with a single additional internal edge cannot contain it: every triangle in that host uses the additional edge.
