# Matrix norm induced by an energy norm

↑ **Parent:** [Induced matrix norm](induced-matrix-norm.md)

For a real [symmetric positive-definite matrix](symmetric-positive-definite-matrix.md) $A$, the [energy norm](energy-norm.md) is $\|x\|_A=(x^TAx)^{1/2}=\|A^{1/2}x\|_2$. In the defining [supremum](supremum.md) of the [induced matrix norm](induced-matrix-norm.md), substitute $y=A^{1/2}x$. The [matrix square root](square-root-of-a-matrix.md) is an [invertible matrix](invertible-matrix.md), so this ranges over every nonzero [vector](vector.md) and proves the displayed identity. This [matrix similarity](matrix-similarity.md) is useful because contraction in the [energy norm](energy-norm.md) becomes an ordinary Euclidean [operator norm](operator-norm.md) estimate after a change of coordinates.
