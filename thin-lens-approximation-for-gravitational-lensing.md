# Thin-lens approximation for gravitational lensing

↑ **Parent:** [Gravitational lensing](gravitational-lensing-split.md)

The [gravitational lensing](gravitational-lensing-split.md) approximation in which deflection is concentrated in a plane whose thickness is small relative to the relevant observer-lens-source distances. The reduced angular deflection includes the distance ratio. A projected [surface mass density](projected-surface-mass-density.md) then determines the lensing potential and the image-to-source mapping. This is distinct from a refracting thin optical lens.
