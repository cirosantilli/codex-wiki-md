# Antiferromagnetic Ising ring

↑ **Parent:** [Antiferromagnetic Ising model](antiferromagnetic-ising-model.md)

An [antiferromagnetic Ising model](antiferromagnetic-ising-model.md) on a closed one-dimensional ring, with [Ising spins](ising-spin-variable.md) $S_j=\pm1$ and the displayed [periodic boundary conditions](periodic-boundary-conditions.md). Every opposite neighboring pair minimizes its bond [energy](energy-split.md). An even ring admits two completely alternating [ground states](ground-state.md); an odd ring must contain at least one parallel pair, an example of [geometric frustration](geometrical-frustration.md).
