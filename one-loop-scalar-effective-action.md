# One-loop scalar effective action

↑ **Parent:** [Effective action](effective-action.md)

In Euclidean signature, for a canonically normalized [real scalar field](real-scalar-field.md) with potential $V$, the one-loop contribution to the [quantum effective action](effective-action.md) is $\Gamma_1[\phi]=\tfrac12\operatorname{Tr}\log[-\partial^2+V''(\phi)]$, with a suitable [regularization in quantum field theory](regularization-in-quantum-field-theory.md). Expanding this [functional determinant](functional-determinant.md) generates [one-particle-irreducible Feynman diagrams](one-particle-irreducible-feynman-diagram.md) with arbitrarily many external fields.
