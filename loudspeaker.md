# Loudspeaker

↑ **Parent:** [Acoustic transducer](acoustic-transducer.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loudspeaker)

A [loudspeaker](loudspeaker.md) converts an electrical [signal](signal.md) into [sound](soundness.md). It can drive an [acoustic wave](acoustic-wave.md) whose [frequency](frequency.md) is controlled independently of the spatial measurement.
