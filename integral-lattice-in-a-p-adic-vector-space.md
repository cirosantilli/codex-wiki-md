# Integral lattice in a p-adic vector space

↑ **Parent:** [P-adic field](p-adic-field.md)

An integral lattice in a finite-dimensional $\mathbb Q_p$-vector space is a free $\mathbb Z_p$-submodule of full rank. Any two such lattices are commensurable: their intersection has finite index in each, because a sufficiently large power of $p$ multiplies either into the other. A finite group acting on the space admits invariant lattices by taking a sum of translates. This allows [Herbrand quotient](herbrand-quotient.md) comparisons via finite quotient modules.
