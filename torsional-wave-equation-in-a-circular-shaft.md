# Torsional wave equation in a circular shaft

↑ **Parent:** [Torsional waves in a solid circular rod](torsional-waves-in-a-solid-circular-rod.md)

For a slender homogeneous circular shaft in small-amplitude [linear elasticity](linear-elasticity.md), torque is $GJ_p\theta_x$ and axial angular inertia per length is $\rho J_p$. Their common geometric factor cancels in the angular equation of motion, leaving the displayed [wave equation](wave-equation-split.md). Here $J_p=\int r^2dA$ is the polar second moment of area, rather than the mass [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) itself.
