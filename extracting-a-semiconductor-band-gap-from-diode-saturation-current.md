# Extracting a semiconductor band gap from diode saturation current

↑ **Parent:** [Diode saturation current](diode-saturation-current.md)

In a diffusion-dominated [p-n junction](p-n-junction.md), $I_s=C(T)n_i^2$ with a transport prefactor determined by minority-carrier diffusion and dopant densities. Combining this with the [intrinsic carrier concentration](intrinsic-carrier-concentration.md) gives, for fixed gap and effective masses,

$$
\log\frac{I_s}{C(T)T^3}=\text{constant}-\frac{E_g}{k_BT}.
$$

The slope against $1/T$ is $-E_g/k_B$. A measured [diode ideality factor](diode-ideality-factor.md) helps identify the transport regime: generation or recombination current can have a different dependence on $n_i$, so its activation slope cannot automatically be interpreted with the diffusion formula.
