# Waveplate retardance detuning

↑ **Parent:** [Phase retardation](phase-retardation.md)

A zero-order [quarter-wave plate](quarter-wave-plate.md) satisfies $\omega_0d\Delta n(\omega_0)/c=\pi/2$. Its [retardance](retardance.md) error at nearby [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) is $\epsilon=\omega d\Delta n(\omega)/c-\pi/2$. Taylor expansion gives the title expression, including material dispersion. Neglecting that dispersion yields $\epsilon=(\pi/2)\Delta\omega/\omega_0$. A [half-wave plate](half-wave-plate.md) made from twice the same material thickness has exactly twice this [phase](phase-waves.md) error. Common propagation [phase](phase-waves.md) can be removed from its [Jones matrix](jones-matrix.md) without changing [polarization](polarization-waves.md).
