# One-shot classical-quantum coding converse

↑ **Parent:** [Holevo's theorem](holevo-s-theorem.md)

For $k$ equiprobable quantum code states with average decoding error $\epsilon<1$, [data-processing inequality for quantum relative entropy](data-processing-inequality-for-quantum-relative-entropy.md) applied to the [binary test for quantum decoding success](binary-test-for-quantum-decoding-success.md) gives

$$
I(X:Q)\geq(1-\epsilon)\log_2k-h_2(\epsilon)-\epsilon\log_2(1-1/k)\geq(1-\epsilon)\log_2k-1
$$

for $k\geq2$. Here $h_2$ is [binary entropy](binary-entropy.md). The case $k=1$ is trivial. Taking the supremum over allowed input distributions yields a code-size bound in terms of the largest [Holevo quantity](holevo-quantity.md); at $\epsilon=1$ the undivided inequality is vacuous.
