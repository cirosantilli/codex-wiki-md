# Titrant

↑ **Parent:** [Acid-base titration](acid-base-titration.md)

A [titrant](titrant.md) is the reagent added in measured quantities during a titration; its known [concentration](concentration.md) and delivered [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) determine the added [amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md).
