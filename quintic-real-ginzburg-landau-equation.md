# Quintic real Ginzburg-Landau equation

↑ **Parent:** [Real Ginzburg–Landau equation](real-ginzburg-landau-equation.md)

A cubic destabilizing term and quintic saturation describe a subcritical stationary pattern bifurcation. A plane wave $A=Re^{iQX}$ has squared amplitude $x=R^2$ satisfying $\mu-Q^2+\alpha x-x^2=0$. The nonzero branches meet at $x=\alpha/2$, $\mu=Q^2-\alpha^2/4$. The lower branch is unstable to amplitude perturbations; the upper branch can also undergo an [Eckhaus instability](eckhaus-instability.md) of its phase.
