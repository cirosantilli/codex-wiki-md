# Character orthogonality for compact groups

↑ **Parent:** [Schur orthogonality relations](schur-orthogonality-relations.md)

For finite-dimensional [unitary representations](unitary-representation.md) of a [compact group](compact-group.md), average the action on $\operatorname{Hom}(W,V)$ against normalized [Haar measure](haar-measure.md). This is the [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md) onto the [intertwining operators](intertwining-operator.md), and its [trace](matrix-trace.md) is the displayed integral. The [Schur lemma](schur-s-lemma.md) therefore makes distinct [irreducible](irreducible-representation.md) [characters](character-of-a-representation.md) [orthogonal](orthogonal-vectors.md) and each [irreducible](irreducible-representation.md) [character](character-of-a-representation.md) of norm one.
