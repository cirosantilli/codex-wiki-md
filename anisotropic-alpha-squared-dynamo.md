# Anisotropic alpha-squared dynamo

↑ **Parent:** [Alpha-squared dynamo](alpha-squared-dynamo.md)

An [alpha-squared dynamo](alpha-squared-dynamo.md) may use a tensorial [alpha effect](alpha-effect.md) rather than an isotropic coefficient. For constant [alpha tensor](alpha-tensor.md) $\boldsymbol\alpha$ and [magnetic diffusivity](magnetic-diffusivity.md) $\eta>0$, a steady nonzero [Fourier mode](fourier-mode.md) with wavevector $\mathbf K$ satisfies $\eta K^2\mathbf B=i\mathbf K\times(\boldsymbol\alpha\mathbf B)$ and $\mathbf K\cdot\mathbf B=0$. Anisotropy can change both the critical alpha magnitude and the preferred wavevector direction.
