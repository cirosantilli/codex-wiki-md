# Central-force radial turning point

↑ **Parent:** [Conservation of angular momentum](conservation-of-angular-momentum.md)

For unit mass in a [central force](central-force.md) with potential $\Phi(r)$ and conserved angular momentum $L$, a radial turning point $r_*$ has zero radial speed and therefore satisfies

$$
E=\frac{L^2}{2r_*^2}+\Phi(r_*).
$$
