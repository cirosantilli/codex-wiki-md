# Jackknife variance estimator

↑ **Parent:** [Jackknife resampling](jackknife-resampling.md)

For leave-one-out estimates $\widehat\theta_{(-i)}$ and their average $\overline\theta_J$, the [Jackknife variance estimator](jackknife-variance-estimator.md) is $\widehat V_J=(n-1)n^{-1}\sum_i(\widehat\theta_{(-i)}-\overline\theta_J)^2$. For a [sample mean](sample-mean.md) it equals the [sample variance](sample-variance.md) divided by $n$. Its general interpretation as an estimated [sampling variance](variance-of-an-estimator.md) requires appropriate smoothness of the [statistic](statistic.md).
