# Frozen-generator product approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Evolution family](evolution-family.md)

For a partition $s=t_0<\cdots<t_N=t$, the frozen-generator approximation is

$$
U_N(t,s)=e^{(t_N-t_{N-1})A(t_{N-1})}\cdots e^{(t_1-t_0)A(t_0)}.
$$

Under the hypotheses of the nonautonomous generation theorem, these products converge strongly and uniformly on compact time triangles to the [evolution family](evolution-family.md).
