# Stellar convective instability

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar convective stability](stellar-convective-stability.md)

Stellar convective instability occurs when an [adiabatic process](adiabatic-process.md) displaces a fluid element and [buoyancy](buoyancy.md) amplifies the displacement. For uniform composition the [Schwarzschild criterion](schwarzschild-criterion.md) gives instability when the actual [temperature gradient](temperature-gradient.md) exceeds the [adiabatic temperature gradient](adiabatic-temperature-gradient.md); composition gradients instead require the [Ledoux criterion](ledoux-criterion.md).
