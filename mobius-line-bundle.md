<h1 id="mobius-line-bundle">Möbius line bundle</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Real line bundle](real-line-bundle.md)

The Möbius line bundle over the circle is

$$
L=(\mathbb R\times\mathbb R)/((t+2\pi,a)\sim(t,-a)),
$$

with projection to $t$ modulo $2\pi$. It is a smooth rank-one [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md). A section is represented by a function $s$ satisfying $s(t+2\pi)=-s(t)$, so the [intermediate value theorem](intermediate-value-theorem.md) forces every [continuous](continuous-function.md) section to vanish somewhere. Thus it has no [nowhere-zero section](nowhere-zero-section.md) and is not isomorphic to the [trivial vector bundle](trivial-vector-bundle.md) of rank one.
