# Viscous peeling of an elastic plate

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic plate](elastic-plate.md)

A fluid advancing under an [elastic plate](elastic-plate.md) can be resisted mainly by [lubrication theory](lubrication-theory.md) flow near its front. For front gap $h_f$ and peeling length $l_p$, the elastic pressure gradient is of order $Bh_f/l_p^5$, while the gap [volume flux](volumetric-flow-rate.md) is of order $Bh_f^4/(\mu l_p^5)$. Mass balance therefore gives peeling speed $U_p\sim Bh_f^3/(\mu l_p^5)$.
