# Composition of quantum channels

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum channel](quantum-channel.md)

The [function composition](function-composition.md) of two [quantum channels](quantum-channel.md) is another [quantum channel](quantum-channel.md). If their [Kraus representations](kraus-representation.md) use $A_k$ and $B_j$, the composed map uses $B_jA_k$. Its completeness relation follows from $\sum_{j,k}A_k^\dagger B_j^\dagger B_jA_k=I$. Tensoring either map with the identity on an arbitrary [ancilla](quantum-ancilla.md) also shows directly that [complete positivity](completely-positive-map.md) is preserved by [function composition](function-composition.md).
