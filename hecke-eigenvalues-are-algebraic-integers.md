# Hecke eigenvalues are algebraic integers

↑ **Parent:** [Hecke eigenform](hecke-eigenform.md)

The rational space of level-one [cusp forms](cusp-form.md) has a full lattice of forms with integral [Fourier coefficients](fourier-coefficient.md). It has finite rank by the [valence formula for the modular group](valence-formula-for-the-modular-group.md), and is preserved by the [Fourier coefficients of a composite-index Hecke operator](fourier-coefficients-of-a-composite-index-hecke-operator.md) formula. Thus every [Hecke operator](hecke-operator.md) has a monic integral characteristic polynomial on this lattice, and its [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are [algebraic integers](algebraic-integer.md). The common eigenvalue field is totally real by the [Petersson inner product](petersson-inner-product.md) self-adjointness.
