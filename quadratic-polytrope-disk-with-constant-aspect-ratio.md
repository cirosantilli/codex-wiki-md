# Quadratic-polytrope disk with constant aspect ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Accretion disk](accretion-disk.md)

For $p=K\rho^2$, a point-mass [gravitational potential](newtonian-potential-of-a-point-mass.md) and a zero-density free surface at $z=\pm\epsilon r$, the [barotropic cylindrical rotation theorem](barotropic-cylindrical-rotation-theorem.md) gives $\Omega^2=GM/(r^3\sqrt{1+\epsilon^2})$. Vertical balance gives $\rho=GM[1/\sqrt{r^2+z^2}-1/(r\sqrt{1+\epsilon^2})]/(2K)$. Integrating over the finite height produces the displayed constant [surface density](surface-density-of-a-disk.md). This is a local radial equilibrium family; infinite radial extent would have infinite mass.
