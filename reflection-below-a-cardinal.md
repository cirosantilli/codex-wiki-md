# Reflection below a cardinal

↑ **Parent:** [Reflection by a beta-strong embedding](reflection-by-a-beta-strong-embedding.md)

A property $P$ reflects below a cardinal $\kappa$ when $\{\mu<\kappa:P(\mu)\}$ is unbounded in $\kappa$. For an [elementary embedding](elementary-embedding.md) with [critical point](critical-point-of-an-elementary-embedding.md) $\kappa$, one often proves this by using $\kappa$ as a witness below $j(\kappa)$ in the target and then invoking elementarity.
