# Alluvial fan

↑ **Parent:** [Geology](geology.md)

A fan-shaped deposit formed where a sediment-carrying stream spreads and loses transport capacity. Offset or warped fan surfaces can preserve evidence of [fault slip](fault-slip.md) or [folding](folding.md).
