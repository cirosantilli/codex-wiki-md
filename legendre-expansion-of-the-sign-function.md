# Legendre expansion of the sign function

↑ **Parent:** [Legendre polynomial](legendre-polynomial.md)

For normalized [Legendre polynomials](legendre-polynomial.md) $p_n=\sqrt{(2n+1)/2}P_n$, parity makes the sign function orthogonal to every even index. Integrating the [Legendre differential equation](legendre-differential-equation.md) gives $\int_0^1P_n=P_n'(0)/(n(n+1))$. The differentiated [generating function](generating-function.md) $z(1+z^2)^{-3/2}$ gives the derivatives at zero and hence the displayed odd coefficients. The [Parseval identity](parseval-identity.md) and squared [norm](norm.md) two then evaluate $\sum_{m\geq0}((2m)!)^2(4m+3)/[2^{4m}(m!(m+1)!)^2]=4$.
