# Index of a central simple algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Brauer group](brauer-group.md)

If $A\cong M_r(D)$ with $D$ a [central division algebra](central-division-algebra.md), its index is $\deg D$. It depends only on the [Brauer group](brauer-group.md) class. The index divides every finite splitting-field degree and equals the degree of a maximal subfield of the division representative.
