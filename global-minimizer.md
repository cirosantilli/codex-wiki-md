# Global minimizer

↑ **Parent:** [Global minimum](global-minimum.md)

A [global minimizer](global-minimizer.md) is a point attaining the [infimum](infimum.md) of an objective. For an extended-real [functional](functional.md), it is usual to require a finite objective value; this excludes points outside its [effective domain](effective-domain.md). A convention allowing $+\infty$ as the attained value gives every point as a formal minimizer of the identically infinite function.
