# Parity-check dependencies determine minimum distance

↑ **Parent:** [Parity-check matrix](parity-check-matrix.md)

In the usual column convention, a [codeword](codeword.md) $c$ of a [linear code](linear-code.md) obeys $Hc^T=0$, so its nonzero coordinates give a [linear dependence](linear-dependence.md) among columns of its [parity-check matrix](parity-check-matrix.md). Conversely a dependence gives a nonzero codeword supported on those coordinates. Minimizing the support gives the [minimum Hamming distance of a linear code](minimum-hamming-distance-of-a-linear-code.md). In the transposed convention $cP=0$, the corresponding objects are the rows of $P$. The size of a dependent set means the number of its coordinate positions, even when some check vectors coincide.
