# Postulates of quantum mechanics

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum mechanics](quantum-mechanics-split.md)

For an isolated quantum system, pure states are normalized rays in a complex [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md), observables are [self-adjoint operators](self-adjoint-operator.md), and their probabilities obey the [Born rule](born-rule.md). A spectral projector $P_a$ has outcome probability $\langle\psi|P_a|\psi\rangle$; an ideal repeatable [projective measurement](projective-measurement.md) has conditional state $P_a\psi/\sqrt{\langle\psi|P_a|\psi\rangle}$. The [Schrödinger equation](schrodinger-equation.md) specifies continuous unitary evolution between such ideal measurements, given a [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md). General measurements need not have the projective state-update rule.

Composite systems use the [tensor product](tensor-product.md) of their state spaces. Identical particles additionally obey the bosonic or fermionic exchange symmetry appropriate to their species. The [photoelectric effect](photoelectric-effect.md), [Davisson–Germer experiment](davisson-germer-experiment.md) and [double-slit experiment](double-slit-experiment.md) motivate energy quanta, matter-wave phases and probability interference, respectively; these experiments constrain the framework but do not separately prove every abstract postulate or specify every system's Hamiltonian.
