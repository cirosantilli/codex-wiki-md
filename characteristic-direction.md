# Characteristic direction

↑ **Parent:** [Characteristic hypersurface](characteristic-hypersurface.md)

For a scalar second-order [partial differential equation](partial-differential-equation-split.md) in two variables, a tangent direction along which [Cauchy data](cauchy-data.md) fail to determine the highest transverse derivative through the equation. For principal part $Au_{xx}+2Bu_{xy}+Cu_{yy}$, a tangent $(dx,dy)$ is characteristic exactly when $A(dy)^2-2B\,dx\,dy+C(dx)^2=0$. Equivalently its conormal annihilates the [principal symbol](principal-symbol-of-a-partial-differential-equation.md). A [hyperbolic partial differential equation](hyperbolic-partial-differential-equation.md) has two distinct real characteristic directions at each point.
