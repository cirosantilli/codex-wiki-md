# Wess-Zumino brane coupling

↑ **Parent:** [Brane](brane.md)

A charged [brane](brane.md) couples to an appropriate differential-form [gauge potential](gauge-field.md) through its [pullback](pullback-category-theory.md). Under $A\mapsto A+d\Lambda$, a closed worldvolume changes the action by a boundary term, by [Stokes theorem](stokes-theorem.md). Reversing the brane charge reverses this coupling, which matters in probe-force and supersymmetry calculations.
