# One-dimensional Sobolev representative

↑ **Parent:** [First-order Sobolev space](first-order-sobolev-space.md)

Every element of $W^{1,p}(a,b)$ for $1\leq p<\infty$ has an [absolutely continuous](absolutely-continuous-function.md) representative. Its ordinary derivative exists [almost everywhere](almost-everywhere.md), equals its [weak derivative](weak-derivative.md), and belongs to $L^p(a,b)$.
