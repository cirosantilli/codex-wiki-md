# Extremal Reissner-Nordstrom causal crossing

↑ **Parent:** [Reissner-Nordstrom spacetime](reissner-nordstrom-spacetime.md)

A neutral radial timelike geodesic of an [extremal black hole](extremal-black-hole.md) has conserved specific energy $E=(1-M/r)^2\dot t$, giving the displayed radial equation. An infalling geodesic reaches $r=M$ with finite nonzero $\dot r=-E$ and finite proper time. In ingoing coordinates, outgoing radial null curves have $dr/dv=(1-M/r)^2/2$; those starting inside approach $r=M$ only at infinite $v$ and cannot return to the same exterior future null infinity. This distinguishes causal access from length on a static slice, and supplies an operational [event horizon](event-horizon.md) test even without strictly trapped round spheres.
