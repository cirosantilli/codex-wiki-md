# Zero morphism

↑ **Parent:** [Zero object](zero-object.md)

In a [pointed category](pointed-category.md), the zero morphism $0_{A,B}:A\to B$ is the composite through its [zero object](zero-object.md). Composition on either side by any [morphism](morphism.md) remains zero. In a [commutative-monoid enrichment](commutative-monoid-enrichment.md) on a pointed category, the additive zero must coincide with this zero morphism: morphisms to and from the zero object belong to singleton hom-sets.
