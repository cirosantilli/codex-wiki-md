# Standard candle

↑ **Parent:** [Absolute magnitude](absolute-magnitude.md)

A standard candle is an astronomical source whose calibrated [absolute magnitude](absolute-magnitude.md) or [luminosity](luminosity.md) allows its [apparent magnitude](apparent-magnitude.md) or flux to determine a distance. A population with unknown common absolute magnitude only determines relative distances without an external luminosity calibration. [Type Ia supernova cosmology](type-ia-supernova-cosmology.md) models this distinction explicitly.
