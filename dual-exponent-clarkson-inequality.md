# Dual-exponent Clarkson inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Clarkson's inequalities](clarkson-s-inequalities.md)

For $p\geq2$ and $p'=p/(p-1)$, interpolate the two-coordinate map $(x,y)\mapsto(x+y,x-y)$ between $\ell^1\to\ell^\infty$ [norm](norm.md) one and $\ell^2\to\ell^2$ [norm](norm.md) $\sqrt2$. This proves the scalar inequality with factor $2^{1/p}$. Integrating that estimate and applying [Minkowski inequality](minkowski-inequality.md) to $|f|^{p'}+|g|^{p'}$ in $L^{p/p'}$ gives the displayed function inequality.
