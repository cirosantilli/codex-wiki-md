# Superior temporal sulcus

↑ **Parent:** [Temporal lobe](temporal-lobe.md)

A sulcus bordering temporal cortical regions that contribute to auditory, visual and language-related processing. Responses to sentence structure and modality depend on the particular subdivision and comparison.
