<h1 id="alfven-velocity">Alfvén velocity</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Alfvén speed](alfven-speed.md)

The [Alfvén velocity](alfven-velocity.md) is the signed vector $\mathbf v_a=\mathbf B/\sqrt{\mu_0\rho}$, whose magnitude is the [Alfvén speed](alfven-speed.md). It follows the orientation of the [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md), unlike the nonnegative scalar [speed](speed.md).
