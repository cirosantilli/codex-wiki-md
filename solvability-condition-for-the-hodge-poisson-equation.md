# Solvability condition for the Hodge Poisson equation

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson equation for differential forms](poisson-equation-for-differential-forms.md)

The [Poisson equation for differential forms](poisson-equation-for-differential-forms.md) on a closed oriented [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md) is solvable exactly when its source is $L^2$-orthogonal to every [harmonic differential form](harmonic-differential-form.md) of the same degree. Necessity follows from the [formal adjoint](formal-adjoint.md) identity $\langle\Delta\alpha,h\rangle=\langle\alpha,\Delta h\rangle=0$. For sufficiency, the [Green operator of the Hodge Laplacian](green-operator-of-the-hodge-laplacian.md) gives $\alpha=G\beta$, since $\Delta G=I-P_{\mathcal H}$.
