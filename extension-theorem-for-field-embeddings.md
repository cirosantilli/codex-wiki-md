# Extension theorem for field embeddings

↑ **Parent:** [Field embedding](field-embedding.md)

An embedding of a subfield into an algebraically closed field extends over an algebraic extension: for a simple extension, map its generator to a root of the transported [minimal polynomial](minimal-polynomial.md), then iterate or use [Zorn lemma](zorn-s-lemma.md). If the larger extension is normal over the fixed base, its image under the extended embedding is itself.
