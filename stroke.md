# Stroke

↑ **Parent:** [Neurology](neurology-split.md)

A sudden neurological injury caused by disruption of cerebral blood supply or bleeding. Vascular anatomy can make damage to neighboring tissue and pathways covary, limiting attempts to infer one-to-one mappings from [brain lesions](brain-lesion.md) to computations.
