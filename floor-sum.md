# Floor sum

↑ **Parent:** [Floor function](floor-function.md)

A floor sum adds values of a [floor function](floor-function.md), often in the form $F(n,a,b,m)=\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}\lfloor(ai+b)/m\rfloor$ for [integers](integer-split.md) $n\ge0$, $m>0$, $a$, and $b$. If $a,b\ge0$, it counts [lattice points](lattice-point.md) $(i,j)$ with $0\le i<n$ and $1\le j\le(ai+b)/m$. Splitting $a$ and $b$ into quotients and remainders isolates an [arithmetic progression](arithmetic-progression.md) and a constant contribution; swapping the remaining counting axes leads to an [Euclidean algorithm](euclidean-algorithm.md) reduction.
