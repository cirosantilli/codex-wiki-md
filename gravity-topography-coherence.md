# Gravity-topography coherence

↑ **Parent:** [Gravity-topography admittance](gravity-topography-admittance.md)

For cross-spectrum $S_{gh}$ and autospectra $S_{gg},S_{hh}$, squared coherence is $|S_{gh}|^2/(S_{gg}S_{hh})$. It measures the fraction of spectral association between [gravity](gravity.md) and topography. Low coherence can signal uncorrelated internal loading, noise or shortcomings of a simple surface-load model; interpreting it as [elastic thickness](effective-elastic-thickness.md) requires a loading model.
