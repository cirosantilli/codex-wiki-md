# Laue X-ray diffraction experiment of 1912

↑ **Parent:** [X-ray diffraction](x-ray-diffraction.md)

[Max von Laue](max-von-laue.md) proposed crystal [X-ray diffraction](x-ray-diffraction.md) and wrote the theoretical section; [Walther Friedrich](walther-friedrich.md) and [Paul Knipping](paul-knipping.md) performed the experiments.[pp. 303–304, 311: section authorship](interferenz-erscheinungen-bei-rontgenstrahlen-by-walther-friedrich-paul-knipping-and-max-von-laue.md)

An [X-ray tube](x-ray-tube.md), lead apertures and shielding supplied a narrow beam through a crystal on a [goniometer](goniometer.md). [Photographic plates](photographic-plate.md) recorded the direct beam and separate spots. [Copper(II) sulfate](copper-ii-sulfate.md) trials preceded oriented [zinc blende](sphalerite.md). Optical alignment was checked with [X-rays](x-ray.md). Removing the crystal removed the extra spots; grinding destroyed their ordered arrangement; translation preserved it, while tilt changed it. Rotation about the beam rotated the pattern.[pp. 311–318: apparatus and controls](interferenz-erscheinungen-bei-rontgenstrahlen-by-walther-friedrich-paul-knipping-and-max-von-laue.md)

Spots and orientation dependence supported [wave interference](interference-wave-propagation.md) and three-dimensional crystal periodicity. They determined neither all atomic positions nor an absolute [wavelength](wavelength.md) scale. The authors left open whether the selected radiation originated as crystal [fluorescence](fluorescence-split.md) or was already in the incident beam. This differs from the modern [Laue method](laue-method.md).[pp. 310–311, 320–322: inference and spectral uncertainty](interferenz-erscheinungen-bei-rontgenstrahlen-by-walther-friedrich-paul-knipping-and-max-von-laue.md)

The 1914 [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics-split.md) went to [Max von Laue](max-von-laue.md); the discovery's experimental account remains jointly credited to [Walther Friedrich](walther-friedrich.md) and [Paul Knipping](paul-knipping.md).[1914 award and laureate](the-nobel-prize-in-physics-1914-by-nobel-prize-outreach.md) The subsequent inference of particular [crystal structures](crystal-structure.md) is developed under [X-ray crystallography](x-ray-crystallography.md).
