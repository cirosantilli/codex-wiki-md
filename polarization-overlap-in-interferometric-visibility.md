# Polarization overlap in interferometric visibility

↑ **Parent:** [First-order optical coherence](first-order-optical-coherence.md)

For perfectly temporally coherent beams with unit [Jones vectors](jones-vector.md) $\mathbf e_1,\mathbf e_2$, their interference term is proportional to the complex inner product $\mathbf e_1^\dagger\mathbf e_2$. Their [interferometric visibility](interferometric-visibility.md) is the displayed expression. Equal beam [light intensities](light-intensity.md) reduce it to the magnitude of the polarization overlap. Two [linear polarizations](linear-polarization.md) at relative angle $\theta$ give $|\cos\theta|$; a linear and a [circular polarization](circular-polarization.md) give $1/\sqrt2$. Perfect coherence does not remove loss of contrast from unequal intensities or orthogonal polarizations.
