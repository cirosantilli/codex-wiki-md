# Odd filling obstructs a spin-degenerate band insulator

↑ **Parent:** [Band insulator](band-insulator.md)

In an independent-electron crystal with two spin states and no spin splitting, each completely filled [energy band](energy-band.md) contains two [electrons](electron.md) per primitive cell. An odd [electron](electron.md) count per primitive cell therefore cannot fill an integer number of such bands. Enlarging the actual primitive cell, breaking the relevant degeneracy or including correlated-electron physics changes the premises. Even filling is necessary in this model but not sufficient: bands can overlap.
