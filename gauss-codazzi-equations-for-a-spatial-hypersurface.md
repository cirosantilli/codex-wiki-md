<h1 id="gauss-codazzi-equations-for-a-spatial-hypersurface">Gauss–Codazzi equations for a spatial hypersurface</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Extrinsic curvature of a spatial hypersurface](extrinsic-curvature-of-a-spatial-hypersurface.md)

With signature $(-,+,+,+)$, future [unit normal](unit-normal.md) $n$ and $K_{ab}=-P^c{}_aP^d{}_b\nabla_cn_d$, the [Gauss–Codazzi equations](gauss-codazzi-equations.md) become $(\perp R)_{abcd}=\mathcal R_{abcd}+K_{ac}K_{db}-K_{ad}K_{cb}$ and $(\perp R)_{abcd}n^d=-D_aK_{bc}+D_bK_{ac}$, where in the second expression only the first three indices are projected. The intrinsic curvature and [spatial covariant derivative](spatial-covariant-derivative.md) use the spatial [induced metric](induced-metric.md). The normal is timelike, so the normal-sign convention differs from a spacelike normal in a positive-definite ambient metric.
