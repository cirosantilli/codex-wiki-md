# Incenter

↑ **Parent:** [Triangle](triangle.md)

The intersection of a [triangle](triangle.md)'s internal [angle bisectors](angle-bisector.md). It is the center of the circle tangent to all three sides, and its distance to any side is the [inradius](inradius.md).
