# Affine autoregressive process

↑ **Parent:** [Affine process](affine-process.md)

An [autoregressive process of order one](autoregressive-process-of-order-one.md) with [independent and identically distributed random variables](independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md) as innovations is an [affine process](affine-process.md) when the innovation [exponential moments](exponential-moment.md) are finite. Its one-step coefficients are $A(\theta)=a\theta$ and $B(\theta)=b\theta+\psi(\theta)$, where $\psi$ is the innovation [cumulant-generating function](cumulant-generating-function.md).
