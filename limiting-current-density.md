# Limiting current density

↑ **Parent:** [Electrochemical polarization](electrochemical-polarization.md)

A limiting rate of charge transfer per area imposed by reactant transport or another bottleneck. In a simple [diffusion](diffusion.md) film, $j_L=nFDc_b/\delta$ and a consuming [electrode](electrode.md) has $c_s/c_b=1-j/j_L$. Increasing internal roughness does not necessarily increase external mass-transfer flux proportionately.
