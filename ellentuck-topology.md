# Ellentuck topology

↑ **Parent:** [Space of infinite subsets of the natural numbers](space-of-infinite-subsets-of-the-natural-numbers.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ellentuck_topology)

For a finite set $s$ and an infinite set $A$ lying above $\max s$, put

$$
[s,A]=\{s\cup B:B\in[A]^\omega\}.
$$

These sets form the basic neighborhoods of the Ellentuck topology, also called the star topology. It is finer than the [ordinary topology on infinite subsets](ordinary-topology-on-infinite-subsets.md). Every basic neighborhood is a [clopen set](clopen-set.md) in the Ellentuck topology, and a [closed set](closed-set.md) in the ordinary topology.
