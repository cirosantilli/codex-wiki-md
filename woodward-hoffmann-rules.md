# Woodward-Hoffmann rules

↑ **Parent:** [Pericyclic reaction](pericyclic-reaction.md)

Orbital-symmetry rules associated with [Robert Burns Woodward](robert-burns-woodward.md) and [Roald Hoffmann](roald-hoffmann.md) for concerted reactions. Ground-state and photochemically excited occupations can have different allowed stereochemical modes; an allowed mode does not determine every kinetic detail or guarantee a large reaction rate.
