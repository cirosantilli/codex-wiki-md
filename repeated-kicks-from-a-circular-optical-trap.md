# Repeated kicks from a circular optical trap

↑ **Parent:** [Passage through a translating optical trap](passage-through-a-translating-optical-trap.md)

For a passing [optical trap](optical-tweezers.md) on a circle of circumference $L$ with nonoverlapping support, a particle that is stationary between encounters receives displacement $\Delta x$ in each encounter. The trap revolution [frequency](frequency.md) $f_T$ and particle revolution [frequency](frequency.md) obey $Lf_p=\Delta x(f_T-f_p)$. This corrects the naive independent-kick estimate when the particle motion changes the encounter rate.
