# Brannerite

↑ **Parent:** [Mineral](mineral.md)

A [uranium](uranium.md) titanate with ideal composition $\mathrm{UTi_2O_6}$. It occurs in natural actinide-bearing minerals and can appear in titanate [ceramic](ceramic.md) waste forms.
