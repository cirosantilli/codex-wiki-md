# Kolmogorov 1941 theory

↑ **Parent:** [Energy cascade](energy-cascade.md)

[Kolmogorov 1941 theory](kolmogorov-1941-theory.md) assumes locally universal small-scale velocity statistics set by mean [viscous dissipation](viscous-dissipation.md) and [kinematic viscosity](kinematic-viscosity.md) at large [Reynolds number](reynolds-number.md). In its [inertial range](inertial-range.md) dimensional form, $S_p(r)\sim\epsilon^{p/3}r^{p/3}$. Large-scale modulation and [internal intermittency](internal-intermittency.md) require qualifying this unconditional scaling.
