# Complex balance

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical reaction network](chemical-reaction-network.md)

At a positive concentration vector $c$, the total incoming deterministic reaction flux equals the total outgoing flux at every [reaction complex](reaction-complex.md). Here $c^y=\prod_i c_i^{y_i}$ is the [law of mass action](law-of-mass-action.md) monomial. This is stronger than balancing net production of each [chemical species](chemical-species.md), but does not require pairwise [detailed balance](detailed-balance.md) between reverse [chemical reactions](chemical-reaction-split.md). A directed cycle can have nonzero circulation while satisfying complex balance.
