# Standard solution

↑ **Parent:** [Solution (chemistry)](solution-chemistry.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Titration](titration.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standard_solution)

A [solution](solution-chemistry.md) whose [concentration](concentration.md) has been established for use as a measurement reference, for example in [titration](titration.md). It can be prepared from a [primary standard](primary-standard.md) or established by [titrant standardization](titrant-standardization.md). The word “standard” does not imply one universal concentration.
