# Ideal norm

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic number theory](algebraic-number-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ideal_norm)

For a nonzero integral ideal $I\subseteq\mathcal O_K$, its norm is the finite index $N(I)=|\mathcal O_K/I|$. Norms multiply under ideal multiplication.
