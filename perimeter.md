# Perimeter

↑ **Parent:** [Set of finite perimeter](set-of-finite-perimeter.md)

The [perimeter](perimeter.md) of a measurable set relative to an open domain is the [total variation seminorm](total-variation-seminorm-on-a-domain.md) of its [indicator function](indicator-function.md) there. For a smooth region it equals the [Hausdorff measure](hausdorff-measure.md) of the boundary lying inside the domain. Global [perimeter](perimeter.md) uses the entire space as the domain; an internal interface shared by two regions appears in both relative perimeters and must be counted only once in a segmentation energy.
