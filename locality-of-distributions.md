# Locality of distributions

↑ **Parent:** [Distribution (mathematical analysis)](distribution-mathematical-analysis.md)

A [distribution](distribution-mathematical-analysis.md) which vanishes on a neighborhood of each point vanishes globally. To prove this, choose a smooth [partition of unity](partition-of-unity.md) subordinate to the neighborhoods. Every [test function](test-function.md) is a finite sum of its products with the partition members, since its [support](support.md) is compact. The [distribution](distribution-mathematical-analysis.md) vanishes on each summand. Applying the result to a difference proves that two [distributions](distribution-mathematical-analysis.md) agreeing locally agree globally. This justifies defining the [support of a distribution](support-of-a-distribution.md) by removing the union of all open regions on which it is zero.
