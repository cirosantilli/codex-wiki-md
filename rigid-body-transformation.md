# Rigid-body transformation

↑ **Parent:** [Rigid transformation](rigid-transformation.md)

An orientation-preserving [rigid transformation](rigid-transformation.md), composed of a [proper rotation](proper-rotation.md) and a [translation](translation-geometry.md). This solid-modelling convention distinguishes continuous rigid motion from orientation-reversing distance-preserving maps such as a [reflection](reflection-mathematics.md).
