# Fourier-Walsh transform

↑ **Parent:** [Boolean function](boolean-function.md)

For $f:\{-1,1\}^n\to\mathbb R$, the characters $\chi_S(x)=\prod_{i\in S}x_i$ form an [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md), and the Fourier-Walsh expansion is

$$
f=\sum_{S\subseteq[n]}\widehat f(S)\chi_S,
\qquad
\widehat f(S)=\mathbb E[f\chi_S].
$$

On the finite Boolean hypercube, these coefficients are the [Hadamard transform](hadamard-transform.md) of the vector of function values, with expectation normalization.
