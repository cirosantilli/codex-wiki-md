# Shearing-coordinate magnetic flux equation

↑ **Parent:** [Resistive induction equation](resistive-induction-equation.md)

For a planar [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) written as $\mathbf B=\nabla\times(A\widehat{\mathbf z})$ and a circular [velocity](velocity.md) $\mathbf u=s\Omega(s)\widehat{\boldsymbol\phi}$, the [resistive induction equation](resistive-induction-equation.md) reduces, after choosing the additive [gauge transformation](gauge-transformation.md), to $\partial_tA+\Omega\partial_\phi A=\eta\nabla^2A$. A [Fourier mode](fourier-mode.md) $A=a(s,t)e^{im[\phi-\Omega(s)t]}$ therefore obeys the equation in the title. Indeed, each radial [derivative](derivative.md) acting on the exponential replaces $\partial_s$ by $\partial_s-imt\Omega'$, while the azimuthal [Laplacian](laplacian.md) contributes $-m^2/s^2$. At zero [magnetic diffusivity](magnetic-diffusivity.md), $a$ is constant in time, but the physical [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) can still be amplified algebraically by [differential rotation](differential-rotation.md).
