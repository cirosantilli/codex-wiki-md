# Bessel inverse-power expansion at infinity

↑ **Parent:** [Bessel function](bessel-function.md)

For $w''+[1-A/z^2]w=0$, with $A=n^2-1/4$, substitution gives $s=\pm i$, exponent zero and $c_{k+1}=[k(k+1)-A]c_k/[2s(k+1)]$. Thus the first correction is $c_1=sAc_0/2$. For integer $n$ no coefficient truncation occurs and coefficient ratios grow as $k/(2s)$; this is an [asymptotic expansion](asymptotic-expansion.md), not a convergent series at a finite $z$. The leading [Liouville–Green approximation](wkb-approximation.md) $q^{-1/4}e^{\pm i\int\sqrt q}$ has the same $1/z$ correction.
