# Ferroelectric poling

↑ **Parent:** [Ferroelectricity](ferroelectricity.md)

Application of an [electric field](electric-field-split.md) to favour [ferroelectric domains](ferroelectric-domain.md) whose polarization aligns with the field, leaving a net remanent polarization. In a ceramic the [crystal grains](grain-in-a-polycrystal.md) remain differently oriented; domain switching does not align their crystallographic axes.
