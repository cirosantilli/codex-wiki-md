# Weyl criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Equidistributed sequence](equidistributed-sequence.md)

A sequence $(x_n)$ in $\mathbb R/\mathbb Z$ is an [equidistributed sequence](equidistributed-sequence.md) if and only if $N^{-1}\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}e^{2\pi i m x_n}\to0$ for every nonzero integer $m$. Approximation of continuous functions by [trigonometric polynomials](trigonometric-polynomial.md) proves the criterion.
