# Lefschetz fibration

↑ **Parent:** [Lefschetz pencil](lefschetz-pencil.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lefschetz_fibration)

A Lefschetz fibration is a map to a surface whose critical points have the complex Morse local model $z_1^2+\cdots+z_n^2$. Away from the critical values it is a smooth [fiber bundle](fiber-bundle-split.md).
