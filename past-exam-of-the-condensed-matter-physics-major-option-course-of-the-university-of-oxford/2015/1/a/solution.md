A [screw axis](../../../../screw-axis.md) combines rotation about a line with translation parallel to that line. An $n_p$ screw rotates through $2\pi/n$ and advances by $p/n$ of the lattice repeat along the axis; a $2_1$ screw advances by half a repeat after a half-turn. The combined operation, rather than either component separately, must map every atomic species onto equivalent sites.

A [glide plane](../../../../glide-plane.md) combines reflection in a plane with a fractional translation parallel to it. Reflecting alone need not preserve the crystal. A [crystallographic point symmetry](../../../../crystallographic-point-symmetry.md) is an isometry with a fixed point, such as a rotation, reflection, inversion, or [rotoinversion](../../../../rotoinversion.md). Point operations have no irreducible translational advance: a screw or a glide with nonzero fractional advance cannot be described merely as a rotation or mirror at a different origin. A crystal's [point group](../../../../point-group.md) records the rotational parts of its space-group operations, whereas the full [space group](../../../../space-group.md) retains their translations.
