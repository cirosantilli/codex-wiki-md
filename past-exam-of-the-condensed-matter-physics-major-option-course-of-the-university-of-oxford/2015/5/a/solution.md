<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A parabolic isotropic band has [group velocity](../../../../group-velocity.md) $\mathbf v=\hbar\mathbf k/m^*$ and semiclassical force $\hbar\dot{\mathbf k}=-e(\mathbf E+\mathbf v\times\mathbf B)$ for electrons with charge $-e$, where $e>0$. In the [relaxation-time approximation](../../../../relaxation-time-approximation.md) the nonequilibrium drift velocity loses momentum at rate $1/\tau$. Combining these gives

$$
\boxed{m^*(\dot{\mathbf v}+\mathbf v/\tau)=-e\mathbf E-e\mathbf v\times\mathbf B.}
$$

The relaxation term represents the average effect of scattering rather than a force on every carrier between collisions. Linear response, an approximately energy-independent $m^*$ and $\tau$ near the [Fermi surface](../../../../fermi-surface.md), and wavelengths long enough for a local bulk response are implicit. The equilibrium random velocities average to zero and do not themselves contribute a drift current.
