<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Subtract the [reciprocal lattice vector](../../../../reciprocal-lattice-vector.md) $(0,0,8)$, leaving a reduced [wave vector](../../../../wavevector.md) $(0.2,0,0)$ along $a$. A linear-acoustic estimate gives

$$
q=0.2\frac{2\pi}{0.353\ \mathrm{nm}}=3.560\times10^9\ \mathrm{m^{-1}},\qquad
v_g\simeq\frac{E}{\hbar q}=2.99\times10^3\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}.
$$

Interpret the quoted intrinsic width as the full width at half maximum in energy, $\Gamma=0.1$ meV. The corresponding energy-relaxation [mean lifetime](../../../../mean-lifetime-physics.md) is $\tau=\hbar/\Gamma=6.58\times10^{-12}$ s, giving [mean free path](../../../../mean-free-path.md) $v_g\tau\simeq19.7$ nm. If the given width instead denotes a half width, the inferred [mean lifetime](../../../../mean-lifetime-physics.md) differs by a factor of two; the convention must be specified.

Approximating the thermal average by this velocity and [mean lifetime](../../../../mean-lifetime-physics.md), and using the isotropic angular factor $\langle v_a^2\rangle=v_g^2/3$, kinetic theory gives

$$
\boxed{\kappa_{{\rm ph},a}\simeq\tfrac13 C_Vv_g^2\tau
=0.72\ \mathrm{W\,m^{-1}\,K^{-1}}.}
$$

Using a single purely $a$-directed effective channel without angular averaging gives $2.17$ W m$^{-1}$ K$^{-1}$, still far below the observed total $39$ W m$^{-1}$ K$^{-1}$. The isotropic estimate is roughly $54$ times smaller than the measured total.

The calculation is not a complete thermal-transport average. Electrons in this metallic compound can carry substantial heat, the supplied total [heat capacity](../../../../heat-capacity.md) need not be entirely phononic, and one $7$ meV [phonon](../../../../phonon.md) need not represent all thermally active branches and [mean lifetimes](../../../../mean-lifetime-physics.md). At $20$ K, $k_BT\simeq1.72$ meV, so that measured mode is relatively high in the thermal distribution; lower-energy modes can have much longer [mean lifetimes](../../../../mean-lifetime-physics.md). A measured [phase velocity](../../../../phase-velocity.md) is also only an estimate of the [group velocity](../../../../group-velocity.md) if the branch is nonlinear.

[electron-phonon interaction](../../../../electron-phonon-interaction.md) is a plausible damping mechanism in a phonon-mediated [superconductor](../../../../superconductor.md), but the measurement is **above** its $15$ K transition and probes the normal state. It cannot be attributed directly to decay into an already established superconducting condensate. Below the transition an energy gap can modify electron-hole damping. The weak-coupling scale $2\Delta\simeq3.52k_BT_c\simeq4.55$ meV is below the $7$ meV [phonon](../../../../phonon.md) energy, so even superconductivity need not prohibit all pair-breaking damping of this mode. Temperature-dependent spectra would distinguish that mechanism from defects or anharmonic [phonon](../../../../phonon.md) decay.
