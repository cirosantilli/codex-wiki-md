<h1 id="2/b/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The difference of the two wave-vector labels is $(1,0,-1)$, an allowed [reciprocal lattice vector](../../../../../reciprocal-lattice-vector.md) because $1+0-1$ is even. [Bloch's theorem](../../../../../bloch-s-theorem-split.md) of the lattice dynamical matrix therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\omega_\nu(1,0,0)=\omega_\nu(0,0,1)}
$$

for corresponding [phonon](../../../../../phonon.md) branches. Neither individual odd-sum point needs to be a [reciprocal lattice](../../../../../reciprocal-lattice.md) point. It is their **difference** that establishes equivalence. Structure factors and neutron [polarization](../../../../../polarization-waves.md) factors can still change the observed intensities at those two scattering vectors, so equal frequencies do not require identical measured spectra peak by peak in intensity.
