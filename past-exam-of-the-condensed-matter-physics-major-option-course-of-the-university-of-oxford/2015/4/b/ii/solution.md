<h1 id="4/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For collinear antiferromagnetic order in the $xy$ plane, take $\mathbf S_n=(-1)^nS\hat{\mathbf x}$. Nearest neighbors are antiparallel, giving $-S^2$, whereas second neighbors lie on the same sublattice and remain parallel, giving $+S^2$. Thus

$$
\boxed{E_{\rm AF}=J_1S^2-J_2S^2=S^2(J_1-J_2).}
$$

The result follows from substituting into the specified XY [Hamiltonian operator](../../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md), regardless of whether that trial state is the global minimum for the chosen exchanges. Comparing trial-state energies does not eliminate the possibility of a lower helical state.
