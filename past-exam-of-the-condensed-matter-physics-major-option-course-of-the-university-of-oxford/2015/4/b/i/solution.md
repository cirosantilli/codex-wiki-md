<h1 id="4/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For the classical XY ferromagnet choose every [spin](../../../../../spin.md) parallel within the $xy$ plane. Each nearest-neighbor and next-nearest-neighbor in-plane dot product is $S^2$. The printed sum counts one bond of each range per [spin](../../../../../spin.md), so

$$
\boxed{E_{\rm F}=-S^2(J_1+J_2).}
$$

There is no additional factor of two from counting both directions: the [Hamiltonian operator](../../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) already lists only $n\to n+1$ and $n\to n+2$. Alignment along $z$ would give zero XY exchange energy and is not the in-plane ordered state used for these expressions.
