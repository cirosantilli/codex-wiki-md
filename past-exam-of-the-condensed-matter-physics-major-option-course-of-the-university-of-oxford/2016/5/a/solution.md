↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [nearly-free electron model](../../../../nearly-free-electron-model.md) expands the [Bloch state](../../../../bloch-state.md) in [plane waves](../../../../plane-wave.md),

$$
\phi_{\mathbf k+\mathbf G}(\mathbf r)=V^{-1/2}e^{i(\mathbf k+\mathbf G)\cdot\mathbf r},\qquad \psi_{b\mathbf k}=\sum_{\mathbf G}c_{b\mathbf k}(\mathbf G)\phi_{\mathbf k+\mathbf G}.
$$

All basis functions have the same translation [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md) because $e^{i\mathbf G\cdot\mathbf R}=1$. A weak periodic potential mixes them most strongly near Bragg degeneracies and opens [band gaps](../../../../band-gap.md). Delocalized $s,p$ conduction states of simple metals, such as sodium and aluminium, are typical applications; the approximation need not describe tightly bound core states.
