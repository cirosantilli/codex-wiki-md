<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The two nonzero stationary points merge when their discriminant vanishes, giving the upper [spinodal](../../../../spinodal.md)

$$
\boxed{T_1=T_c+\frac{b^2}{4ac},\qquad \kappa_1=\frac14}.
$$

At a nonzero stationary point, $a(T-T_c)=bP-cP^2$, so its [free energy](../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) reduces to $F=P^3(b/6-cP/4)$. Equality with $F(0)=0$ therefore requires $P=2b/(3c)$. Substitution gives

$$
\boxed{T_2=T_c+\frac{2b^2}{9ac},\quad \kappa_2=\frac29,\quad P=0\ \text{or}\ \frac{2b}{3c}}.
$$

Both have positive curvature $2b^2/(9c)$ at coexistence. The intervening maximum is at $P=b/(3c)$. For $T_2<T<T_1$ the positive-polarization minimum is [metastable](../../../../metastability.md); for $T_c<T<T_2$ the zero-polarization minimum is [metastable](../../../../metastability.md).
