<h1 id="2/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

In equilibrium without [hysteresis](../../../../hysteresis.md), the system selects the lower free-energy minimum. At $T_2$ the [order parameter](../../../../order-parameter.md) jumps between $0$ and $2b/(3c)$, which identifies a [first-order phase transition](../../../../first-order-phase-transition.md). Differentiating the minimized [free energy](../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) does not add a $dP/dT$ contribution because $F'=0$. Thus the polarization-dependent [entropy](../../../../entropy-split.md) is $S=-aP^2/2$, giving

$$
S_{\rm ordered}-S_{\rm disordered}=-\frac{2ab^2}{9c^2}.
$$

The heat absorbed on heating from the ordered to the disordered phase is

$$
\boxed{Q_l=\frac{2ab^2T_2}{9c^2}}.
$$

The same amount is released on cooling. If $\Delta S$ is defined in the opposite direction, the signed transition heat is correspondingly negative.
