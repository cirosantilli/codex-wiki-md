<h1 id="3/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For a partially filled $4f$ shell, the outer $5s$ and $5p$ shells shield the localized orbitals from the ligands. [Coulomb interactions](../../../../../coulomb-interaction.md) establish $L,S$ through [Hund's rules](../../../../../hund-s-rules.md), and [spin-orbit coupling](../../../../../spin-orbit-interaction.md) usually exceeds the much smaller [crystal field splitting](../../../../../crystal-field-splitting.md). First form a ground $J$ multiplet using $\mathbf J=\mathbf L+\mathbf S$, then split that multiplet by the crystal environment. The orbital contribution is therefore generally retained, and the [g-factor](../../../../../g-factor-physics.md) is a multiplet Landé factor rather than an almost spin-only value. Weak external Zeeman and hyperfine interactions can subsequently resolve the remaining states. Exceptions with $J=0$ or unusual valence require separate treatment.
