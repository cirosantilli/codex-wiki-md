<h1 id="3/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a partially filled $3d$ shell, [electron](../../../../../electron.md)–[electron](../../../../../electron.md) Coulomb repulsion and [Hund's rules](../../../../../hund-s-rules.md) first favour particular total [spin](../../../../../spin.md) and orbital terms. The [crystal field splitting](../../../../../crystal-field-splitting.md) is substantial because these orbitals overlap the ligand environment; it normally exceeds the [spin-orbit coupling](../../../../../spin-orbit-interaction.md) and often quenches the orbital moment. Competition between the splitting and the [electron pairing energy](../../../../../electron-pairing-energy.md) determines high-spin versus low-spin filling. A common hierarchy is Coulomb term energies, then crystal-field and Hund-exchange scales, then [spin](../../../../../spin.md)–orbit and weak-field Zeeman energies; the relative sizes of crystal-field splitting and pairing are material-dependent. Hyperfine coupling, if the nucleus carries [spin](../../../../../spin.md), adds a smaller splitting. An isolated ion has no inter-ion exchange ordering field.
