<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The broad feature has the scale expected for a carrier [plasmon](../../../../plasmon.md), a longitudinal collective oscillation of the mobile electron density. Using the parameters just estimated and $N=10^{24}\,\mathrm m^{-3}$ gives

$$
\widetilde\nu_p=\frac{\omega_p}{2\pi c}=\frac1{2\pi c}\sqrt{\frac{Ne^2}{\epsilon_0\epsilon_\infty m_e^*}}\simeq\boxed{316\,\mathrm{cm}^{-1}}.
$$

Conversely a bare screened [plasma oscillation](../../../../plasma-oscillation.md) at $340\,\mathrm{cm}^{-1}$ would require $N\simeq1.16\times10^{18}\,\mathrm{cm}^{-3}$ for the same parameters, consistent with the source's approximate density. Its broadness is also consistent with carrier damping, unlike the much sharper lattice line near $270\,\mathrm{cm}^{-1}$.

This identifies a **plasmon-related** feature, not a uniquely determined undamped pure electronic mode. [Gallium arsenide](../../../../gallium-arsenide.md) is polar, and its longitudinal [optical phonon](../../../../optical-phonon.md) couples to the carrier [plasmon](../../../../plasmon.md). A more complete dielectric model has longitudinal eigenfrequencies

$$
\omega_\pm^2=\frac12\left[\omega_{\mathrm{LO}}^2+\omega_p^2\pm\sqrt{(\omega_{\mathrm{LO}}^2+\omega_p^2)^2-4\omega_p^2\omega_{\mathrm{TO}}^2}\right].
$$

The measured [Raman scattering](../../../../raman-scattering.md) maximum can also shift with damping and the scattering matrix elements. Experimental [Raman scattering](../../../../raman-scattering.md) from these coupled modes and carrier-collision broadening is established independently.[Abstract: differently doped samples, coupled modes, and linewidths dominated by electron collisions](../../raman-scattering-by-coupled-optical-phonon-plasmon-modes-in-gaas-1968.md) The supplied approximate density and one broad spectrum do not determine a unique coupled-mode fit. Treating $340\,\mathrm{cm}^{-1}$ as an exact pure-[plasmon](../../../../plasmon.md) eigenfrequency would overstate the available information.
