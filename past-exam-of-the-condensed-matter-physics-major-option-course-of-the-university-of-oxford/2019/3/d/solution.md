<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

A possible assignment is a zone-centre transverse [optical phonon](../../../../optical-phonon.md) of [gallium arsenide](../../../../gallium-arsenide.md): the two atomic sublattices oscillate oppositely, producing a narrow lattice [Raman scattering](../../../../raman-scattering.md) line near $270\,\mathrm{cm}^{-1}$. Its energy is $hc\widetilde\nu\simeq33.5\,\mathrm{meV}$, a reasonable optical-phonon scale. Unlike the broad carrier feature, its frequency is set mainly by interatomic force constants and ionic masses.

Whether this transverse [optical phonon](../../../../optical-phonon.md) is allowed in an ideal first-order [Raman scattering](../../../../raman-scattering.md) geometry depends on the surface orientation and incident and scattered [polarizations](../../../../polarization-waves.md); disorder or imperfect geometry can relax a forbidden selection rule. Those experimental details are unspecified, so this is a possible explanation rather than an exclusive assignment.
