<h1 id="1/d/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

$P_1$ has primitive coordinates $(1/2,1/2)$ and therefore fails the first-order optical momentum condition; its local $V_1$ pattern also has zero dipole derivative. $P_2$ is a zone-centre mode with the dipole-active $V_2$ pattern, so its molecular dipole changes add coherently.

$$
\boxed{P_1\text{ is IR inactive};\qquad P_2\text{ is IR active}.}
$$

The allowed absorption for $P_2$ uses an [polarization of an electromagnetic wave](../../../../../polarization-of-an-electromagnetic-wave.md) with an out-of-plane component. At normal incidence on an ideal free-standing layer the electric field is in the plane, so that specific measurement geometry would suppress it despite the mode's intrinsic [vibrational infrared activity](../../../../../vibrational-infrared-activity.md).
