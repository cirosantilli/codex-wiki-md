<h1 id="1/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Apply the same change of [reciprocal lattice](../../../../../reciprocal-lattice.md) coordinates to the [phonon](../../../../../phonon.md) [wave vectors](../../../../../wavevector.md):

$$
\boxed{\mathbf q_{P_1}:\ (1,0)_r\mapsto(\tfrac12,\tfrac12)_p,\qquad\mathbf q_{P_2}:\ (0,0)_r\mapsto(0,0)_p.}
$$

Although $(1,0)_r$ is an integer coordinate of the rectangular reciprocal grid, it is not a [reciprocal lattice vector](../../../../../reciprocal-lattice-vector.md) of the actual primitive crystal. Thus $P_1$ is not equivalent to the [Brillouin zone](../../../../../brillouin-zone.md) centre.
