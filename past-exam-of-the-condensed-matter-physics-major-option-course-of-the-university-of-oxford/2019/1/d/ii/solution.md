<h1 id="1/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For ordinary one-phonon [infrared](../../../../../infrared.md) [optical absorption](../../../../../optical-absorption.md), both an energy and a momentum selection rule must hold. The optical [photon](../../../../../photon.md) has negligible [crystal momentum](../../../../../crystal-momentum.md) on the scale of the [Brillouin zone](../../../../../brillouin-zone.md), so [crystal momentum conservation](../../../../../crystal-momentum-conservation.md) selects $\mathbf q\simeq0$ modulo a genuine [reciprocal lattice vector](../../../../../reciprocal-lattice-vector.md). In addition, the mode must change the macroscopic [electric polarization](../../../../../polarization-density.md), with a component along the incident [polarization of an electromagnetic wave](../../../../../polarization-of-an-electromagnetic-wave.md).

In terms of atomic displacements $\mathbf u_j$ and [Born effective charge](../../../../../born-effective-charge.md) tensors $Z_j^*$, the relevant derivative is proportional to $\sum_jZ_j^*\mathbf u_j$ per [primitive unit cell](../../../../../primitive-unit-cell.md). Thus an [optical phonon](../../../../../optical-phonon.md) at $\mathbf q=0$ is not automatically infrared active: its dipole contributions may cancel. Disorder-assisted and multiphonon processes relax these simple first-order selection rules.
