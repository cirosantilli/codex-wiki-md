<h1 id="1/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

There are six atoms in the nonlinear [oxalate](../../../../../oxalate.md) ion, hence $3\cdot6=18$ positional degrees of freedom. Subtract three rigid translations and three rigid rotations to obtain $3N-6=12$ vibrational [normal modes](../../../../../normal-mode.md). Planarity does not remove out-of-plane motions. Since three modes have been displayed,

$$
\boxed{12-3=9\text{ additional vibrational normal modes}.}
$$
