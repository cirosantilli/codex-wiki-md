<h1 id="1/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

[Infrared spectroscopy](../../../../../infrared-spectroscopy.md) measures absorption as the incident [photon](../../../../../photon.md) frequency is swept through molecular [normal mode](../../../../../normal-mode.md) frequencies. In the electric-dipole approximation the interaction is $-\mathbf p\cdot\mathbf E$. Expanding the molecular [electric dipole moment](../../../../../electric-dipole-moment.md) in a [normal coordinate](../../../../../normal-coordinate.md) $Q$ gives

$$
\mathbf p(Q)=\mathbf p(0)+\left(\frac{\partial\mathbf p}{\partial Q}\right)_0Q+\cdots.
$$

The fundamental one-quantum vibrational [transition dipole moment](../../../../../electric-dipole-transition-moment.md) is proportional to the derivative multiplying $Q$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\left(\partial\mathbf p/\partial Q\right)_0\ne0}
$$

is the criterion for [vibrational infrared activity](../../../../../vibrational-infrared-activity.md); the derivative must also have a component along the incident [polarization of an electromagnetic wave](../../../../../polarization-of-an-electromagnetic-wave.md). A permanent molecular [electric dipole moment](../../../../../electric-dipole-moment.md) is not necessary. Absorption occurs near $\hbar\omega=E_{v+1}-E_v$, with broadened lines reflecting finite lifetimes and the molecular environment.
