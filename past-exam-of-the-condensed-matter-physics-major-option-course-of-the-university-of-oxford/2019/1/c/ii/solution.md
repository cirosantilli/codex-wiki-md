<h1 id="1/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let the [reciprocal lattice vector](../../../../../reciprocal-lattice-vector.md) be $\mathbf G=h_r\mathbf a_r^*+k_r\mathbf b_r^*=h_p\mathbf a_p^*+k_p\mathbf b_p^*$, with each dual pair obeying $\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf a^*=\mathbf b\cdot\mathbf b^*=2\pi$ and zero cross products. Its coefficients are obtained by dotting with the corresponding direct-space [primitive lattice translation vectors](../../../../../primitive-lattice-translation-vector.md):

$$
\begin{aligned}
h_p&=\frac{\mathbf G\cdot\mathbf a_p}{2\pi}=\frac{h_r-k_r}{2},\\
k_p&=\frac{\mathbf G\cdot\mathbf b_p}{2\pi}=\frac{h_r+k_r}{2}.
\end{aligned}
$$

Hence

$$
\boxed{h_p=\tfrac12h_r-\tfrac12k_r,\qquad k_p=\tfrac12h_r+\tfrac12k_r.}
$$

For actual integer-indexed [reciprocal lattice vectors](../../../../../reciprocal-lattice-vector.md), this also explains the centred-rectangular condition that $h_r$ and $k_r$ have the same parity. General [phonon](../../../../../phonon.md) [wave vectors](../../../../../wavevector.md) need not have integer coefficients.
