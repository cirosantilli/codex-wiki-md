<h1 id="2/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

For the electron-doped sample, sketch six closed ellipsoids centred on the $\Gamma$--X axes, just inside the six square faces of the face-centred-cubic [first Brillouin zone](../../../../first-brillouin-zone.md), with long axes pointing radially toward X. In the parabolic approximation,

$$
\frac{\delta k_\perp^2}{2m_1(\mu-E_c)/\hbar^2}+\frac{\delta k_\parallel^2}{2m_2(\mu-E_c)/\hbar^2}=1.
$$

The radial-to-transverse semiaxis ratio is $\sqrt{m_2/m_1}\simeq2.2$. The readable conduction minimum near X and its crossing of $\mu$ support these electron [Fermi surface](../../../../fermi-surface.md) pockets. The two $[001]$ [cyclotron resonance](../../../../cyclotron-resonance.md) fields arise from the two distinct orientations of the six ellipsoids, rather than two unrelated carrier species. The left panel of the original sketch includes axes, $\Gamma$, and the three positive-axis X points; the negative-axis X points are symmetry-equivalent.

The surviving valence maximum lies roughly $1.2\,\mathrm{eV}$ below the original $\mu$. Lowering $\mu$ by $1.5\,\mathrm{eV}$ therefore places it in the upper [valence band](../../../../valence-band.md) in the conventional [silicon](../../../../silicon.md) band interpretation. The electron pockets disappear and one sketches a [hole pocket](../../../../hole-pocket.md) centred at $\Gamma$, as in the middle panel. Detailed valence-band warping and the number of spin-orbit-resolved sheets cannot be extracted from the damaged figure, and the single drawn surface is schematic rather than a claim that all such sheets coincide.

A discriminating physical property is the [Hall coefficient](../../../../hall-coefficient.md): electron-dominated transport gives a negative value, whereas hole-dominated transport gives a positive value in the ordinary single-dominant-carrier regime. One should not infer a numerical resistivity change without the carrier densities and mobilities.
