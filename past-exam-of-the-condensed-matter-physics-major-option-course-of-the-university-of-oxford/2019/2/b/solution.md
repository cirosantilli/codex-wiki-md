<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a nondegenerate band $E(\mathbf k)$, define the [reciprocal effective mass tensor](../../../../reciprocal-effective-mass-tensor.md) by

$$
\boxed{(M^{-1})_{ij}=\frac1{\hbar^2}\frac{\partial^2E}{\partial k_i\partial k_j}.}
$$

It is the curvature tensor of the [energy band](../../../../energy-band.md). Near an extremum, $E\simeq E_0+\hbar^2\delta\mathbf k^TM^{-1}\delta\mathbf k/2$, so positive principal curvatures give positive electron [effective masses](../../../../band-effective-mass.md). Together with the [group velocity](../../../../group-velocity.md) $\mathbf v=\hbar^{-1}\nabla_{\mathbf k}E$ and $\hbar\dot{\mathbf k}=\mathbf F$, it yields $\dot v_i=(M^{-1})_{ij}F_j$. Negative electron curvature at a [valence band](../../../../valence-band.md) maximum is conventionally recast as a positive [band effective mass](../../../../band-effective-mass.md) for missing electrons.
