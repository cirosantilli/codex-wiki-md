The lower curve is a [valence band](../../../../valence-band.md) and the upper curve is a [conduction band](../../../../conduction-band.md), separated by an indirect [band gap](../../../../band-gap.md). The readable upper curve has a minimum just inside the X boundary and slightly below the plotted [chemical potential](../../../../chemical-potential-split.md). It is therefore partially occupied, while the visible valence states lie below the [chemical potential](../../../../chemical-potential-split.md) and are filled at low [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md). The sample is electron-doped, with [electron pockets](../../../../electron-pocket.md) around the six symmetry-related conduction minima of [silicon](../../../../silicon.md).

The full high-symmetry path and part of the lower-left dispersion are absent from the held image. **Source gap:** those missing path labels and curve segments cannot be read or reconstructed from this copy. The identification of the conventional six silicon valleys and the valence maximum at $\Gamma$ uses the usual [silicon](../../../../silicon.md) band model, rather than purported readings of missing pixels.

Two methods for calculating such an [electronic band structure](../../../../electronic-band-structure.md) are [density functional theory](../../../../density-functional-theory.md), solving a self-consistent periodic electronic problem, and a [tight-binding model](../../../../tight-binding.md), diagonalizing a hopping Hamiltonian built from localized [atomic orbitals](../../../../atomic-orbital.md). Neither is a measurement of the plotted [dispersion relation](../../../../dispersion-relation-split.md).
