<h1 id="5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Measure several allowed magnetic reflections from an oriented single crystal by [magnetic neutron diffraction](../../../../magnetic-neutron-diffraction.md), preferably with polarization analysis. A [neutron](../../../../neutron-split.md) couples to the component of the ordered [magnetic moment](../../../../magnetic-dipole-moment.md) perpendicular to the scattering vector $\mathbf Q$. For a collinear arrangement, the geometric intensity factor is

$$
1-(\widehat{\mathbf Q}\cdot\widehat{\mathbf m})^2.
$$

Compare reflections whose $\mathbf Q$ point in different crystal directions, correcting for the [magnetic form factor](../../../../magnetic-form-factor.md) and known experimental geometry. Their relative intensities distinguish candidate spin directions; a moment parallel to $\mathbf Q$ gives no magnetic signal from that component. Polarized [neutrons](../../../../neutron-split.md) help separate nuclear and magnetic contributions. Observing only peak positions establishes periodicity, not the spin direction.
