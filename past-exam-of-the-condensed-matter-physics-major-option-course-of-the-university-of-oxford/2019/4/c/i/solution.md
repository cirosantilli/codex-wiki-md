<h1 id="4/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The term $-\alpha\epsilon$ is the leading electronic energy gain from splitting the initially degenerate orbitals and occupying the lower levels. Its negative sign expresses the lowering of the occupied-electron energy for the chosen elongation branch of the [Jahn-Teller effect](../../../../../jahn-teller-effect.md). The positive coefficient $\alpha$ is a linear electron-lattice coupling in this reduced model.

The term $\beta\epsilon^2/2$ is the harmonic elastic cost of moving the ligands away from the undistorted reference structure. Mechanical stability requires $\beta>0$. The competition between linear gain and quadratic cost produces a finite equilibrium distortion rather than indefinite elongation.
