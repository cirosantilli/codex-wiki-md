<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In weak-coupling [BCS theory](../../../../bcs-theory.md), a useful scale is

$$
\boxed{k_BT_c\simeq1.14\hbar\omega_D\exp\left[-\frac1{N(0)V_{\rm eff}}\right].}
$$

Here the [phonon](../../../../phonon.md) spectrum sets the attractive-interaction cutoff, $N(0)$ is the consistently defined Fermi-level [density of states](../../../../density-of-states.md) and $V_{\rm eff}$ the effective pairing attraction after repulsion is accounted for. Light hydrogen supports high vibrational frequencies; strong [electron](../../../../electron.md)–[phonon](../../../../phonon.md) coupling and favourable metallic [density of states](../../../../density-of-states.md) can raise $T_c$. Pressure changes bonding, phase stability, electronic structure and [phonon](../../../../phonon.md) frequencies. It is not simply an independent multiplier in this expression. In a real strongly coupled hydride, [Eliashberg theory](../../../../eliashberg-theory.md) uses the full [spectral function](../../../../spectral-function.md) and a screened Coulomb pseudopotential; the elementary exponential is a mechanism guide rather than a controlled quantitative fit to $203\,\mathrm K$.

Measure a four-terminal resistance that falls below the instrumental bound, and test that a [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) suppresses the transition. Independently detect diamagnetic screening and, where feasible, field-cooled flux expulsion or an AC susceptibility signal with controlled cell background. A resistance drop alone can reflect contact changes or a filamentary path; a consistent magnetic response is important evidence for [superconductivity](../../../../superconductivity-split.md). A reproducible isotope shift upon replacing hydrogen by deuterium tests the involvement of [lattice motion](../../../../phonon.md), although it is not by itself proof of [superconductivity](../../../../superconductivity-split.md).

Extreme-pressure samples are microscopic and enclosed in a diamond-anvil cell. Reliable separate electrical contacts, homogeneous pressure, pressure [calibration](../../../../calibration-differential-geometry.md) during cooling, chemical stability and the tiny magnetic signal relative to the anvils and gasket are practical difficulties. Structural identification matters because a sulfur-rich hydrogen compound formed under pressure need not retain the starting stoichiometry.

**Historical correction:** the original primary report associates the $203\,\mathrm K$ transition with pressures near $155\,\mathrm{GPa}$, while roughly $90\,\mathrm{GPa}$ is the metallization scale, not that same transition point. The printed pairing of $203\,\mathrm K$ with $90\,\mathrm{GPa}$ should not be treated as an established measured pair. The statement about the highest conventional transition [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) is also a historical statement in this 2018 examination.[Primary 2015 report: metallization near 90 GPa, high-temperature transition near 155 GPa, transport, magnetic and isotope evidence](../../../../conventional-superconductivity-at-203-kelvin-at-high-pressures-in-the-sulfur-hydride-system-by-a-p-drozdov-and-others.md)
