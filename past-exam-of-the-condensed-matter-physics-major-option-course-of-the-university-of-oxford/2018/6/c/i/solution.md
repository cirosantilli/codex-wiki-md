At $B_z=10\,\mathrm T$, the nominal filling is

$$
\boxed{\nu_{10}=\frac{N_Sh}{10e},\qquad
E_{n,\sigma}=\hbar\frac{eB_z}{m^*}(n+\tfrac12)+\sigma\frac{g^*\mu_BB_z}{2}.}
$$

A labelled density-of-states picture has disorder-broadened peaks at these orbital and [spin](../../../../../spin.md) energies, localized tails and extended states near their centres. A [chemical potential](../../../../../chemical-potential-split.md) in the localized region corresponds to a Hall plateau and small [longitudinal resistivity](../../../../../longitudinal-sheet-resistivity.md); passing through an extended-state centre corresponds to a transition.
