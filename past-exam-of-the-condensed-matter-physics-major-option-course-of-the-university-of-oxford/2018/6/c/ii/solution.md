↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

At $B_z=8\,\mathrm T$,

$$
\boxed{\nu_8=\frac{N_Sh}{8e}=\frac54\nu_{10}.}
$$

The cyclotron spacing is $\hbar e(8\,\mathrm T)/m^*$, smaller than at 10 T, and each resolved level's degeneracy per area is also reduced by $4/5$. Thus the same carrier density occupies more levels. This ratio is determined without the missing graph, but it does not specify whether the [chemical potential](../../../../../chemical-potential-split.md) lies in a localized tail or an extended-state centre.
