<h1 id="1/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

At the zone centre, the [dynamical matrix](../../../../../dynamical-matrix.md) commutes with the point-group representation on displacements. An [irreducible representation](../../../../../irreducible-representation.md) of [dimension](../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) two enforces a doublet; distinct one-dimensional representations need not have equal frequencies. The tetragonal [point group](../../../../../point-group.md) $D_{4h}$ has two-dimensional $E_g$ and $E_u$ representations, so [optical modes](../../../../../optical-phonon.md) transforming that way are doubly degenerate at the centre. Atomic positions and the displacement representation would be needed to assign every [optical branch](../../../../../optical-phonon-branch.md) to a particular representation; the point-group label alone is not a complete mode decomposition.

For propagation along the tetragonal fourfold axis, the two transverse acoustic polarizations transform together and have equal [sound speeds](../../../../../speed-of-sound.md) and frequencies. For example $\omega_{{\rm TA},x}=\omega_{{\rm TA},y}\simeq v_T|q_z|$, whereas the longitudinal speed is independent. An optical doublet may split away from the centre along a direction whose [little group](../../../../../little-group.md) has only one-dimensional representations. It need not remain degenerate at every nearby [wavevector](../../../../../wavevector.md).

All [irreducible representations](../../../../../irreducible-representation.md) of $D_{2h}$ are one-dimensional, so the orthorhombic [point group](../../../../../point-group.md) enforces no analogous optical doublet or equality of transverse [sound speeds](../../../../../speed-of-sound.md). The three acoustic frequencies are nevertheless all zero at the exact centre in both [crystals](../../../../../crystal.md), because of translations; that common zero is not evidence that their slopes are equal. Accidental equalities remain possible. For a polar [crystal](../../../../../crystal.md), a direction-dependent macroscopic [electric field](../../../../../electric-field-split.md) also distinguishes longitudinal from transverse infrared-active limits; an analytic zone-centre [symmetry](../../../../../symmetry-physics.md) classification alone does not remove LO–TO splitting.
