<h1 id="1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Each atom supplies three displacement coordinates. Translational invariance gives three [acoustic branches](../../../../../acoustic-phonon-branch.md) for either structure, leaving

$$
\boxed{\begin{array}{c|cc}
 &\text{acoustic}&\text{optical}\\
\text{tetragonal, }r=4&3&9\\
\text{orthorhombic, }r=8&3&21
\end{array}}
$$

These count branches at a general [wavevector](../../../../../wavevector.md); a doubly degenerate pair still counts as two modes. The stated atom counts refer to primitive cells, so no additional centring factor is needed.
