<h1 id="1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

To obtain a [crystal](../../../../../crystal.md)'s [point group](../../../../../point-group.md), discard the translation parts of its space-group operations. A screw becomes a [rotation](../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) and a glide becomes a mirror. Thus the tetragonal group's $4/n$, $2_1/m$ and $2/m$ operations give

$$
\boxed{4/mmm\equiv D_{4h}.}
$$

For the orthorhombic structure the three perpendicular twofold/glide or screw/mirror directions give

$$
\boxed{mmm\equiv D_{2h}.}
$$

The primitive [Bravais lattice](../../../../../bravais-lattice.md) symbol does not itself add a point operation; the fractional translations matter for the [space group](../../../../../space-group.md) but disappear from this point-group reduction.
