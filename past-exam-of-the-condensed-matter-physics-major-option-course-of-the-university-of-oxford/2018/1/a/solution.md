<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [phonon](../../../../phonon.md) is one quantum of a [normal vibrational mode](../../../../normal-mode.md) of a [crystal](../../../../crystal.md). In the [harmonic approximation](../../../../harmonic-approximation.md), each mode with [wavevector](../../../../wavevector.md) $\mathbf q$, branch index $s$ and [angular frequency](../../../../angular-frequency.md) $\omega_s(\mathbf q)$ is an [oscillator](../../../../oscillator.md) with energies $\hbar\omega_s(n+1/2)$. A [phonon](../../../../phonon.md) changes the occupation $n$ by one; it is not a particular atom moving through the solid.

For a three-dimensional [primitive cell](../../../../primitive-unit-cell.md) containing $r$ atoms there are $3r$ branches. Three [acoustic branches](../../../../acoustic-phonon-branch.md) approach zero frequency as $\mathbf q\to0$: at zero [wavevector](../../../../wavevector.md) they represent rigid translations, with all [basis](../../../../crystal-motif.md) atoms displaced together. Their small-[wavevector](../../../../wavevector.md) frequencies usually grow linearly with [wavevector](../../../../wavevector.md). The remaining $3r-3$ [optical branches](../../../../optical-phonon-branch.md) have internal relative motion of [basis](../../../../crystal-motif.md) atoms and generally nonzero zone-centre frequencies. The names reflect their coupling to radiation in suitable polar [crystals](../../../../crystal.md), not a rule that every optical mode must be infrared active. Acoustic and [optical branches](../../../../optical-phonon-branch.md) can each have longitudinal or transverse polarizations.
