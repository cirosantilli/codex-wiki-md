<h1 id="1/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For [neutron](../../../../../neutron-split.md) [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) loss, $E_f=30-20=10\,\mathrm{meV}$. Write $\mathbf Q=\mathbf k_i-\mathbf k_f$. The [neutron](../../../../../neutron-split.md) dispersion $E=\hbar^2k^2/(2m_n)$, or $E[\mathrm{meV}]=2.0721\,k[\mathrm{\mathring A^{-1}}]^2$, gives

$$
k_i=3.805\,\mathrm{\mathring A^{-1}},\qquad k_f=2.197\,\mathrm{\mathring A^{-1}}.
$$

A triangle of these [wavevectors](../../../../../wavevector.md) exists if

$$
|k_i-k_f|\le Q\le k_i+k_f,
\qquad1.608\le Q\le6.002\,\mathrm{\mathring A^{-1}}.
$$

With $c=4.50\,\mathrm{\mathring A}$, the requested [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) transfers have $Q=(2\pi/c)|l|=1.3963|l|\,\mathrm{\mathring A^{-1}}$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{l=\pm2,\ \pm3,\ \pm4\quad\text{are kinematically accessible}.}
$$

For example $|l|=1$ is too small even for forward scattering at these unequal [neutron](../../../../../neutron-split.md) energies, and $|l|=5$ exceeds the backscattering maximum. This identifies accessible [reciprocal lattice](../../../../../reciprocal-lattice.md) points, not guaranteed nonzero intensity: a one-[phonon](../../../../../phonon.md) [structure factor](../../../../../structure-factor.md) contains the atomic scattering lengths, [eigenvectors](../../../../../eigenvector.md) and factors $\mathbf Q\cdot\mathbf e_j$. A transverse [eigenvector](../../../../../eigenvector.md) perpendicular to $\mathbf Q$ may be invisible, and [basis](../../../../../crystal-motif.md) interference can cause extinctions. The supplied geometry and cell constants do not specify that full intensity selection rule.
