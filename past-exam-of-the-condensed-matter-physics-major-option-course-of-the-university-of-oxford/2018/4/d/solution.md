<h1 id="4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

In [Raman scattering](../../../../raman-scattering.md), the [photon](../../../../photon.md) transfers $\hbar\omega=\hbar(\omega_i-\omega_s)$ and $\mathbf q=\mathbf k_i-\mathbf k_s$ to a collective excitation. Changing scattering geometry selects different $q$ and probes its dispersion.

The open circles rise and then flatten near $45\,\mathrm{meV}$: they trace the lower transverse [phonon polariton](../../../../phonon-polariton.md) branch. It is strongly dispersive on its [photon](../../../../photon.md)-like side, then becomes predominantly TO-[phonon](../../../../phonon.md)-like and approaches the TO [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) at larger [wavevector](../../../../wavevector.md). The open triangles stay near $50\,\mathrm{meV}$ and are the nearly nondispersive longitudinal [optical phonon](../../../../optical-phonon.md). Longitudinal motion creates its own polarization field and is shifted above the transverse resonance. It does not hybridize with a propagating transverse [photon](../../../../photon.md) in the same way, and it should not be misidentified as the upper transverse [polariton](../../../../polariton.md) branch, which rises with [wavevector](../../../../wavevector.md). Raman tensor and polarization selection rules determine which excitation is visible.

**Scale qualification:** the source horizontal label appears to print $10^{-4}\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$. Taken literally, one plotted unit would have vacuum [photon](../../../../photon.md) [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) only $\hbar c\times0.01\,\mathrm{m^{-1}}\simeq2\times10^{-6}\,\mathrm{meV}$, inconsistent with the observed tens of meV and the dispersive [polariton](../../../../polariton.md). The physically compatible scale is of order $10^4\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$; the qualitative branch identification does not require silently treating the printed negative exponent as correct. The original model figure in [solution](../b/solution.md) explicitly labels its calculated [wavevector](../../../../wavevector.md) scale.
