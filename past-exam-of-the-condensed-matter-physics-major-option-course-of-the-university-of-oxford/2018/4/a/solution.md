<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Seek the steady forced displacement as $x=x_0e^{-i\omega t}$. The [oscillator](../../../../oscillator.md) equation gives

$$
x_0=\frac{qE_0/\mu}{\omega_{\rm TO}^2-\omega^2-i\gamma\omega}.
$$

For $n$ ion pairs per volume, the ionic polarization is $P_{\rm ion}=nqx$. Add the electronic polarization $\epsilon_0(\epsilon_\infty-1)E$, assumed frequency-independent over this infrared range. The complex [relative permittivity](../../../../relative-permittivity.md) is therefore

$$
\epsilon_r(\omega)=\epsilon_\infty+
\frac{nq^2/(\epsilon_0\mu)}{\omega_{\rm TO}^2-\omega^2-i\gamma\omega}.
$$

For a longitudinal free oscillation without external free charge, $\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf D=0$ and the longitudinal [electric field](../../../../electric-field-split.md) requires $\epsilon_r(\omega_{\rm LO})=0$ in the lossless limit. Hence

$$
\omega_{\rm LO}^2-\omega_{\rm TO}^2=\frac{nq^2}{\epsilon_0\mu\epsilon_\infty}.
$$

Eliminating the microscopic [oscillator](../../../../oscillator.md) strength gives

$$
\boxed{\epsilon_r(\omega)=\epsilon_\infty
\frac{\omega_{\rm LO}^2-\omega^2-i\gamma\omega}
{\omega_{\rm TO}^2-\omega^2-i\gamma\omega}.}
$$

Here $\omega_{\rm LO}$ denotes the undamped longitudinal frequency; with finite [damping](../../../../damping.md) the zero is at a complex frequency. The sign of the imaginary term follows the stated $e^{-i\omega t}$ convention and gives positive absorptive $\operatorname{Im}\epsilon_r$ for $\omega>0$, $\gamma>0$. At zero frequency the same model yields the [Lyddane-Sachs-Teller relation](../../../../lyddane-sachs-teller-relation.md) $\epsilon_r(0)/\epsilon_\infty=\omega_{\rm LO}^2/\omega_{\rm TO}^2$. The high-frequency limit here is above the [phonon](../../../../phonon.md) resonance but below electronic resonances, not arbitrarily high [photon](../../../../photon.md) [energy](../../../../energy-split.md).
