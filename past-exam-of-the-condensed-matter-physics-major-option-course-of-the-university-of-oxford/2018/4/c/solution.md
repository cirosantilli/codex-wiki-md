<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For a transverse electromagnetic [plane wave](../../../../plane-wave.md) in a nonmagnetic medium, [Maxwell's equations](../../../../maxwell-equations-split.md) give $c^2k^2=\omega^2\epsilon_r(\omega)$. Set [damping](../../../../damping.md) to zero and define $u=c^2k^2/\epsilon_\infty$. Substitution yields

$$
u(\omega_{\rm TO}^2-\omega^2)=\omega^2(\omega_{\rm LO}^2-\omega^2),
$$

so $\omega^4-(\omega_{\rm LO}^2+u)\omega^2+u\omega_{\rm TO}^2=0$. Therefore the two [phonon polariton](../../../../phonon-polariton.md) branches are

$$
\boxed{\omega_\pm^2(k)=\frac12\left[\omega_{\rm LO}^2+u
\pm\sqrt{(\omega_{\rm LO}^2+u)^2-4u\omega_{\rm TO}^2}\right].}
$$

Take the nonnegative square root for the positive physical frequencies. At $k=0$, $\omega_-=0$ and $\omega_+=\omega_{\rm LO}$. At small [wavevector](../../../../wavevector.md) the lower branch is [photon](../../../../photon.md)-like with velocity $c/\sqrt{\epsilon_r(0)}$; at large [wavevector](../../../../wavevector.md) it approaches $\omega_{\rm TO}$ from below. The upper branch ultimately becomes [photon](../../../../photon.md)-like with velocity $c/\sqrt{\epsilon_\infty}$. There is no real propagating transverse branch with frequency strictly between $\omega_{\rm TO}$ and $\omega_{\rm LO}$ in this lossless bulk model. Coupling changes the eigenmodes from a bare [photon](../../../../photon.md) crossing a flat [oscillator](../../../../oscillator.md) into mixed [normal modes](../../../../normal-mode.md).
