<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

[Angle-resolved photoemission spectroscopy](../../../../angle-resolved-photoemission-spectroscopy.md) illuminates a surface with [photons](../../../../photon.md) of known [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) and measures the [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) and emission direction of the escaping [electrons](../../../../electron.md). Referencing the initial binding [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) to the [Fermi level](../../../../fermi-level.md) gives

$$
\boxed{E_B=h\nu_{\rm light}-\Phi-E_{\rm kin},\qquad
k_\parallel=\frac{\sqrt{2m_eE_{\rm kin}}}{\hbar}\sin\theta.}
$$

Here $\Phi$ is the sample/analyser work-function reference appropriate to the calibrated [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) scale, and $\theta$ is the emission angle from the surface normal. Parallel [crystal momentum](../../../../crystal-momentum.md) is conserved modulo a surface [reciprocal lattice](../../../../reciprocal-lattice.md) vector; measuring two angular coordinates maps the two in-plane [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) components.

Sweeping angle and [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) traces the occupied spectral weight and its dispersion. In a quasi-two-dimensional sheet, no detailed free-[electron](../../../../electron.md) model of the perpendicular final-state [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) is required to map the in-plane bands. Peaks reflect the occupied single-particle [spectral function](../../../../spectral-function.md), multiplied by Fermi occupation and photoemission [matrix](../../../../matrix.md) elements and broadened by finite lifetimes and resolution. Ordinary photoemission does not directly measure empty conduction bands; a gap may need complementary information. A clean surface, vacuum and charge control are especially important for an insulating specimen.
