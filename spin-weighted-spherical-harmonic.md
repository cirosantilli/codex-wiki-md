# Spin-weighted spherical harmonic

↑ **Parent:** [Spin weight](spin-weight.md)

The angular basis ${}_sY_{\ell m}$ for a spin-weight-$s$ field generalizes ordinary [spherical harmonics](spherical-harmonic.md). It has $\ell\ge|s|$. For scalar-source [CMB polarization](cosmic-microwave-background-polarization.md) about a wavevector, only $m=0$ survives and ${}_{\pm2}Y_{20}\propto\sin^2\theta$.
