# Recurrent graph

↑ **Parent:** [Random walk on a graph](random-walk-on-a-graph.md)

A connected [locally finite graph](locally-finite-graph.md) is recurrent if its [simple random walk](simple-random-walk.md) returns to its starting vertex [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md). In that case the walk hits every fixed vertex [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md) from every starting vertex.
