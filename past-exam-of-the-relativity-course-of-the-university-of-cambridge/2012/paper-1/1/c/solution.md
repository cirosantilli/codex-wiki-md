<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [geodesics of the product-weighted diagonal plane metric](../../../../../geodesics-of-the-product-weighted-diagonal-plane-metric.md) follow from the [geodesic Lagrangian](../../../../../geodesic-lagrangian.md) $L=(y^2\dot x^2+x^2\dot y^2)/2$. With dots denoting an [affine parameter](../../../../../affine-parameter.md) [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md), its [Euler-Lagrange equations](../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) give

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
y^2\ddot x+2y\dot y\dot x-x\dot y^2&=0,\\
x^2\ddot y+2x\dot x\dot y-y\dot x^2&=0.
\end{aligned}}
$$

Substituting $y=mx$ with constant nonzero $m$ reduces both equations to $x\ddot x+\dot x^2=0$. Thus $x\dot x$ is constant, and a suitable affine parametrization exists; explicitly, $x^2=C_1\lambda+C_2$ on a segment of fixed sign.

**These straight rays are [geodesics](../../../../../geodesic.md) only in the regular domain $xy\ne0$.** The [metric tensor](../../../../../metric-tensor.md) [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) is $x^2y^2$. On the axes, including the origin, this is a [degenerate metric tensor](../../../../../degenerate-metric-tensor.md): the calculation does not establish a regular [geodesic](../../../../../geodesic.md) continuing through the origin. The slope-zero ray is outside the regular domain. Geometrically, on each quadrant the change $\rho=|xy|/\sqrt2$, $\vartheta=\log|y/x|$ gives $ds^2=d\rho^2+\rho^2d\vartheta^2$. A fixed slope has constant $\vartheta$ and is therefore locally a radial [geodesic](../../../../../geodesic.md) of a flat [metric tensor](../../../../../metric-tensor.md).
