<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In [general relativity](../../../../../general-relativity-split.md), [light](../../../../../light.md) follows [null geodesics](../../../../../null-geodesic.md). A weak [gravitational field](../../../../../gravitational-field.md) outside an isolated spherical [mass](../../../../../mass.md) gives the [Schwarzschild light deflection](../../../../../schwarzschild-light-deflection.md)

$$
\boxed{\alpha=\frac{4GM}{bc^2}}
$$

for [impact parameter](../../../../../impact-parameter.md) $b\gg GM/c^2$. At the solar limb this is approximately $1.75$ arcseconds. A Newtonian corpuscular calculation gives half this value; the full result depends on spatial [curvature](../../../../../curvature.md) as well as the [gravitational time dilation](../../../../../gravitational-time-dilation.md). The [parametrized post-Newtonian formalism](../../../../../parametrized-post-newtonian-formalism.md) predicts $\alpha=2(1+\gamma_{\rm PPN})GM/(bc^2)$, with $\gamma_{\rm PPN}=1$ in [general relativity](../../../../../general-relativity-split.md).

The [Eddington experiment](../../../../../eddington-experiment.md) compared photographic star positions near the eclipsed [Sun](../../../../../sun.md) with reference exposures taken without the [Sun](../../../../../sun.md) in that field. Rays bend toward the [Sun](../../../../../sun.md), but their backward extrapolations place the apparent stellar images farther from it. Plate scale, focus changes, [atmospheric refraction](../../../../../atmospheric-refraction.md) and comparison-field alignment must be separated from a radial displacement proportional to $1/b$. The original report discusses the instruments, plate reductions and competing predicted deflections; the test is a quantitative comparison with uncertainty, not a unique proof of a gravitational theory.[1920, pp. 291–333: eclipse observations, instruments and plate reductions](../../../../../a-determination-of-the-deflection-of-light-by-the-sun-s-gravitational-field-by-frank-watson-dyson-and-others.md)

[radio interferometry](../../../../../radio-interferometry.md) permits an independent test without an eclipse. A 2005 [Very Long Baseline Array](../../../../../very-long-baseline-array.md) experiment observed four compact sources at 43, 23 and 15 GHz. Differential [astrometry](../../../../../astrometry.md) suppressed common antenna errors, while a fit $\mathbf p(\nu)=\mathbf p_0+\mathbf I\nu^{-2}$ separated [refraction](../../../../../refraction.md) in the [solar corona](../../../../../solar-corona.md) from achromatic gravitational deflection. Tropospheric errors and radio-source structure also required calibration. The reported $\gamma_{\rm PPN}=0.9998\pm0.0003$ was consistent with [general relativity](../../../../../general-relativity-split.md).[2009, §§II–III on observations, calibration and coronal subtraction; §V on the reported result](../../../../../progress-in-measurements-of-the-gravitational-bending-of-radio-waves-using-the-vlba-by-edward-fomalont-and-others.md)
