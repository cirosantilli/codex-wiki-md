<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

[tidal forces](../../../../../tidal-force.md) measure differences in [gravitational acceleration](../../../../../gravitational-acceleration.md) across an extended region. Neighboring freely falling particles each have zero [four-acceleration](../../../../../four-acceleration.md), but their separation need not accelerate uniformly. Let $u^\mu$ be the tangent to a reference [timelike geodesic](../../../../../timelike-geodesic.md) and $\xi^\mu$ its infinitesimal separation from a neighboring one, chosen from an affinely parametrized [geodesic congruence](../../../../../geodesic-congruence-split.md). Using the [Riemann curvature tensor](../../../../../riemann-curvature-tensor-split.md) convention specified in Solution 3,

$$
\frac{D^2\xi^\alpha}{D\tau^2}
=-R^\alpha{}_{\beta\gamma\delta}
u^\beta u^\gamma\xi^\delta .
$$

Changing the overall [curvature](../../../../../curvature.md) convention changes this displayed sign. The [covariant derivatives](../../../../../covariant-derivative.md) compare [vectors](../../../../../vector.md) by [parallel transport](../../../../../parallel-transport.md), avoiding a coordinate-dependent subtraction of [accelerations](../../../../../acceleration.md).

In the [Newtonian limit](../../../../../newtonian-limit.md), expand the force field at two nearby points:

$$
\ddot\xi_i=-\partial_i\partial_j\Phi\,\xi_j.
$$

For $\Phi=-GM/r$, a local [orthonormal frame](../../../../../orthonormal-frame-in-spacetime.md) aligned with the radius gives

$$
\boxed{\ddot{\boldsymbol\xi}
=\frac{GM}{r^3}\operatorname{diag}(2,-1,-1)\boldsymbol\xi.}
$$

There is radial stretching and transverse compression; the trace vanishes outside the [mass](../../../../../mass.md). Two freely falling test [masses](../../../../../mass.md) and a measurement of their changing separation implement [gravity gradiometry](../../../../../gravity-gradiometry.md). Their common [acceleration](../../../../../acceleration.md) drops out, while a relative [acceleration](../../../../../acceleration.md) proportional to separation remains. A [local inertial frame](../../../../../local-inertial-frame.md) removes [Christoffel symbols](../../../../../christoffel-symbol.md) at a point, but cannot remove nonzero [curvature](../../../../../curvature.md) over a finite region.
