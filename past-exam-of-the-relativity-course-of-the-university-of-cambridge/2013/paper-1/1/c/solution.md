<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [Christoffel symbols of a spatially flat FLRW metric](../../../../../christoffel-symbols-of-a-spatially-flat-flrw-metric.md) depend on the time-coordinate convention. Here $x^0$ is $t$, not $ct$. For an [affine parameter](../../../../../affine-parameter.md) $\lambda$, take $\mathcal L=\tfrac12[c^2\dot t^2-a^2(t)\delta_{ij}\dot x^i\dot x^j]$. Its [Euler-Lagrange equations](../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) are

$$
\ddot t+\frac{aa'}{c^2}\delta_{ij}\dot x^i\dot x^j=0,\qquad
\ddot x^i+2\frac{a'}a\dot t\dot x^i=0.
$$

Comparison with the [geodesic equation](../../../../../geodesic-equation.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\Gamma^0{}_{ij}=\frac{aa'}{c^2}\delta_{ij},\qquad
\Gamma^i{}_{0j}=\Gamma^i{}_{j0}=\frac{a'}a\delta^i{}_j}.
$$

All others vanish because $g_{00}$ is constant and there are no spatial [metric tensor](../../../../../metric-tensor.md) [derivatives](../../../../../derivative.md). The lower-index interchange is essential: the printed list specifies one member of each symmetric mixed pair. The factor of two in the spatial [geodesic equation](../../../../../geodesic-equation.md) comes from those two members.
