<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For [geodesic motion of uncharged dust](../../../../../geodesic-motion-of-uncharged-dust.md), use the pressureless stress-energy form, $T^{\mu\nu}=\rho u^\mu u^\nu$ and $u_\nu u^\nu=c^2$. [stress-energy conservation](../../../../../stress-energy-conservation.md) expands to

$$
0=\nabla_\mu T^{\mu\nu}
=u^\nu\nabla_\mu(\rho u^\mu)+\rho u^\mu\nabla_\mu u^\nu.
$$

Contracting with $u_\nu$ kills the second term, since $u_\nu\nabla_\mu u^\nu=\tfrac12\nabla_\mu(c^2)=0$. Hence $\nabla_\mu(\rho u^\mu)=0$, and wherever $\rho>0$,

$$
\boxed{u^\mu\nabla_\mu u^\nu=0}.
$$

On a flow line $u^\mu=dx^\mu/d\tau$, so this becomes $d^2x^\nu/d\tau^2+\Gamma^\nu{}_{\alpha\beta}(dx^\alpha/d\tau)(dx^\beta/d\tau)=0$. Each [relativistic dust](../../../../../relativistic-dust.md) element therefore follows a [timelike geodesic](../../../../../timelike-geodesic.md) with [proper time](../../../../../proper-time.md) as an [affine parameter](../../../../../affine-parameter.md). [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) gradients or external nongravitational [forces](../../../../../force-split.md) would add an [acceleration](../../../../../acceleration.md) term.
