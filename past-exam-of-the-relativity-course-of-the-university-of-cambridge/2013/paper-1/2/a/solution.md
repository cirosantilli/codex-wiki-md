<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Universality of [free fall](../../../../../free-fall.md) equates the passive gravitational response to [inertial mass](../../../../../inertial-mass.md) up to a universal choice of units. The [Einstein equivalence principle](../../../../../einstein-equivalence-principle.md) further states that local nongravitational experiments in a freely falling laboratory obey [special relativity](../../../../../special-relativity-split.md) independently of the laboratory's location and [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md). A supported laboratory instead has [proper acceleration](../../../../../proper-acceleration.md), just as an accelerating laboratory in flat spacetime does.

Around an event, freely falling coordinates can be chosen so that $g_{\mu\nu}=\eta_{\mu\nu}$ and $\Gamma^\alpha{}_{\mu\nu}=0$ there. The first [metric tensor](../../../../../metric-tensor.md) [derivatives](../../../../../derivative.md) vanish at that event, and nearby corrections begin at order curvature times squared separation. The [metric tensor](../../../../../metric-tensor.md)'s second [derivatives](../../../../../derivative.md) retain the [Riemann tensor](../../../../../riemann-curvature-tensor-split.md). Thus **a [local inertial frame](../../../../../local-inertial-frame.md) removes [Christoffel symbols](../../../../../christoffel-symbol.md) at an event, while tidal gravity remains measurable over a finite region**. Relative motion of separated freely falling test particles detects this curvature through [geodesic deviation](../../../../../geodesic-deviation.md). Freely falling axes carried along an observer's [world line](../../../../../world-line.md) extend the local construction, but do not make an extended curved region globally Minkowskian.
