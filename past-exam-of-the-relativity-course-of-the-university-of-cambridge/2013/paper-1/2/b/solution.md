[accretion-disk tests of strong-field gravity](../../../../../accretion-disk-tests-of-strong-field-gravity.md) put radiating matter in a strong gravitational field at relativistic orbital speeds. [general relativity](../../../../../general-relativity-split.md) predicts an [innermost stable circular orbit](../../../../../innermost-stable-circular-orbit.md), $r_{\rm ISCO}=6GM/c^2$ for a nonrotating [black hole](../../../../../black-hole-split.md), with a spin-dependent radius in the [Kerr metric](../../../../../kerr-metric.md). If the radiating inner edge tracks that orbit, its fitted radius and binding-energy release probe the geometry. For a thin disk with negligible inner-edge torque, $L\simeq\eta\dot Mc^2$; the Schwarzschild efficiency is $\eta=1-\sqrt{8/9}\simeq0.057$.

In [relativistic iron-line spectroscopy](../../../../../relativistic-iron-line-spectroscopy.md), a fluorescent iron line supplies a rest-energy reference. The [relativistic Doppler effect](../../../../../relativistic-doppler-effect.md) from orbital motion, [relativistic beaming](../../../../../relativistic-beaming.md), [gravitational redshift](../../../../../gravitational-redshift.md) and ray bending produce an asymmetric broadened profile, including a red wing from the deepest emitting radii. The ASCA observation of MCG-6-30-15 showed a broad asymmetric iron feature relative to the approximately $6.4\ \mathrm{keV}$ neutral-iron [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md), interpreted through relativistic disk-line models.[Mission result description: broad iron line, low-energy asymmetry and relativistic interpretation](../../../../../asca-mcg-6-30-15-by-nasa-heasarc.md) The observed line must be separated from the illuminating continuum, [absorption](../../../../../absorption-electromagnetic-radiation.md) and other [reflection](../../../../../reflection-mathematics.md) features; inclination, ionization and the radial illumination law affect the inferred radius.

[thermal-continuum fitting of an accretion disk](../../../../../thermal-continuum-fitting-of-an-accretion-disk.md) provides an independent method for a geometrically thin, optically thick disk. Its spectrum and normalization constrain a characteristic inner area, provided [mass](../../../../../mass.md), distance, inclination and atmospheric spectral hardening are controlled. A pre-2013 application selected thermal-state observations of GRS 1915+105 at relatively low disk [luminosity](../../../../../luminosity.md) to make the thin-disk approximation appropriate.[Abstract: relativistic continuum fit, thermal-state selection, luminosity cut and inner-edge assumptions](../../../../../the-spin-of-the-near-extreme-kerr-black-hole-grs-1915-plus-105-by-jeffrey-e-mcclintock-and-others.md)

**Consistency among inner radius, line transfer and thermal emission tests the strong-field disk picture.** A [black-hole spin](../../../../../black-hole-spin.md) estimate obtained by assuming a [Kerr metric](../../../../../kerr-metric.md) is not by itself a model-independent verification of [general relativity](../../../../../general-relativity-split.md). Stress across the nominal inner edge, finite disk thickness, coronal geometry, calibration and competing [absorption](../../../../../absorption-electromagnetic-radiation.md) models must be constrained; a gravity test compares alternative geometries while allowing for those astrophysical uncertainties. An emitting material surface or boundary layer can further complicate disks around [neutron stars](../../../../../neutron-star.md).
