<h1 id="section-a/1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use the $+---$ [metric signature](../../../../../metric-signature.md), with the time component of [four-momentum](../../../../../four-momentum.md) equal to $E/c$. The incident particle has [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) $\gamma mc^2$ and [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) of magnitude $mc\sqrt{\gamma^2-1}$. Adding the stationary target's [four-momentum](../../../../../four-momentum.md) gives total laboratory quantities

$$
E_{\rm tot}=(\gamma+2)mc^2,\qquad |\mathbf p_{\rm tot}|=mc\sqrt{\gamma^2-1}.
$$

The [centre-of-momentum energy](../../../../../centre-of-momentum-energy.md) $W$ follows from the invariant [Minkowski norm](../../../../../minkowski-norm.md) of the total [four-momentum](../../../../../four-momentum.md):

$$
W^2=E_{\rm tot}^2-c^2|\mathbf p_{\rm tot}|^2
=m^2c^4\big[(\gamma+2)^2-(\gamma^2-1)\big]
=(4\gamma+5)m^2c^4.
$$

At the [relativistic collision mass threshold](../../../../../relativistic-collision-mass-threshold.md), the two final particles have zero relative [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) in their [centre-of-momentum frame](../../../../../center-of-momentum-frame.md). Their total [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) there is therefore $2\alpha mc^2$. [Four-momentum conservation](../../../../../four-momentum-conservation.md) requires $W\geq2\alpha mc^2$, so

$$
\boxed{\gamma_{\min}=\alpha^2-\frac54.}
$$

The condition $\alpha>3/2$ ensures $\gamma_{\min}>1$. This is the **minimum required** [Lorentz factor](../../../../../lorentz-factor.md); larger values allow final [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) in the [centre-of-momentum frame](../../../../../center-of-momentum-frame.md). Equivalently, the incident laboratory [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) at threshold is $(\alpha^2-9/4)mc^2$.
