<h1 id="section-b/b2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B2](../b2.md)

The two [postulates of special relativity](../../../../postulates-of-special-relativity.md) are: **all nongravitational laws have the same form in every [inertial frame](../../../../inertial-frame.md)**, and **light in vacuum has the same finite [speed of light](../../../../speed-of-light.md) $c$ in every [inertial frame](../../../../inertial-frame.md), independently of the motion of its source**. The first is [Einstein's principle of relativity](../../../../einstein-s-principle-of-relativity.md); the second supplies an experimentally identified invariant speed.

For the stated standard configuration, the [Lorentz transformation](../../../../lorentz-transformation.md) is

$$
x'=\gamma_v(x-vt),\qquad t'=\gamma_v\left(t-\frac{vx}{c^2}\right),\qquad y'=y,\qquad z'=z,
\qquad \gamma_v=\frac1{\sqrt{1-v^2/c^2}}.
$$

Take [differentials](../../../../differential-of-a-smooth-map.md) along the particle's [worldline](../../../../world-line.md), rather than comparing coordinate displacements at equal times in the two frames. Writing $u_i=dx^i/dt$, one obtains

$$
dt'=\gamma_v\left(1-\frac{vu_x}{c^2}\right)dt,\qquad
dx'=\gamma_v(u_x-v)dt,\qquad dy'=u_y\,dt,\qquad dz'=u_z\,dt.
$$

Dividing by the transformed time interval proves the [velocity-addition formula](../../../../velocity-addition-formula.md):

$$
\boxed{u'_x=\frac{u_x-v}{1-vu_x/c^2},\quad
u'_y=\frac{u_y}{\gamma_v(1-vu_x/c^2)},\quad
u'_z=\frac{u_z}{\gamma_v(1-vu_x/c^2)}.}
$$

For a massive particle, $|\mathbf u|<c$ and $|v|<c$, so the denominator $1-vu_x/c^2$ is positive.
