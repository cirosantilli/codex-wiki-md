<h1 id="section-b/b3/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The relativistic orbit-shape equation can be derived without dividing by $\dot r$ at a turning point. Substitute the norm constraint into the radial [geodesic equation](../../../../../geodesic-equation.md) to eliminate $\dot t$ and $\dot r^2$. Using $f'=2\mu/r^2$ gives

$$
\ddot r=-\frac{\mu\kappa}{r^2}+\frac{h^2}{r^3}-\frac{3\mu h^2}{r^4}.
$$

For a massive particle with [proper time](../../../../../proper-time.md) as parameter, $\kappa=c^2$. Put $u=1/r$, and use primes in this paragraph for [derivatives](../../../../../derivative.md) with respect to $\phi$. The constant [specific angular momentum](../../../../../specific-angular-momentum.md) gives $\dot\phi=hu^2$, so the [chain rule](../../../../../chain-rule.md) yields

$$
\dot r=\frac{dr}{d\phi}\dot\phi=-hu',\qquad
\ddot r=-h^2u^2u''.
$$

Substituting these expressions into the radial equation and dividing by $h^2u^2$ for a nonradial orbit gives

$$
\boxed{u''+u-3\mu u^2=\frac{\mu c^2}{h^2}=\frac{GM}{h^2}.}
$$

The extra $3\mu u^2$ term is responsible for [Schwarzschild perihelion precession](../../../../../schwarzschild-perihelion-precession.md). It vanishes in the Newtonian weak-field limit $\mu/r\to0$. The derivation uses a massive particle; for a [null geodesic](../../../../../null-geodesic.md), $\kappa=0$ and the constant $GM/h^2$ on the right is absent.
