<h1 id="section-b/6/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For coordinates $(t,x)$ the inverse metric is $g^{tt}=1/(\alpha^2x^2)$, $g^{xx}=-1$. Only $\partial_xg_{tt}=2\alpha^2x$ is nonzero, giving the [Christoffel symbols](../../../../../christoffel-symbol.md)

$$
\boxed{\Gamma^t{}_{tx}=\Gamma^t{}_{xt}=\frac1x,\qquad
\Gamma^x{}_{tt}=\alpha^2x,}
$$

with all remaining components zero.

On the specified constant-$x_0$ [worldline](../../../../../world-line.md), assume $x_0>0$. The [four-velocity](../../../../../four-velocity.md) is $u^t=c/(\alpha x_0)$, $u^x=0$, and its norm is $c^2$, so $\tau$ is indeed [proper time](../../../../../proper-time.md). The coordinate components are constant, but the covariant acceleration is nonzero:

$$
\boxed{a^t=0,\qquad a^x=\Gamma^x{}_{tt}(u^t)^2=\frac{c^2}{x_0}.}
$$

The [proper acceleration of a static Rindler observer](../../../../../proper-acceleration-of-a-static-rindler-observer.md) is

$$
\boxed{\sqrt{-g_{\mu\nu}a^\mu a^\nu}=\frac{c^2}{x_0}=\text{constant}.}
$$

The acceleration has constant components in this accelerated chart and constant invariant magnitude. It is not a covariantly constant vector: its inertial-frame components change along the hyperbolic trajectory. For $x_0<0$, the magnitude is $c^2/|x_0|$, with the appropriate choice of future time orientation.
