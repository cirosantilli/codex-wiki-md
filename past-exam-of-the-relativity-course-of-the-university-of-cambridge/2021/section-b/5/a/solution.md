<h1 id="section-b/5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use the [metric signature](../../../../../metric-signature.md) $\eta=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,-1,-1)$ and $c=1$ throughout this question. The [trace-reversed metric perturbation](../../../../../trace-reversed-metric-perturbation.md) satisfies $\phi=\eta_{\mu\nu}\phi^{\mu\nu}=-h$, so its inverse relation is

$$
h_{\mu\nu}=\phi_{\mu\nu}-\frac12\eta_{\mu\nu}\phi.
$$

Set $C_\nu=\partial^\sigma\phi_{\nu\sigma}$. Substituting this inverse relation into the linearized field operator cancels the second derivatives of the [trace](../../../../../matrix-trace.md), leaving

$$
\mathcal E_{\mu\nu}=\partial_\mu C_\nu+\partial_\nu C_\mu
-\Box\phi_{\mu\nu}-\eta_{\mu\nu}\partial^\rho C_\rho.
$$

The imposed [Lorenz gauge in linearized gravity](../../../../../lorenz-gauge-in-linearized-gravity.md) is $C_\nu=0$. Therefore the [Linearized Einstein equations](../../../../../linearized-einstein-equations.md) in vacuum reduce to

$$
\boxed{\Box\phi^{\mu\nu}=\partial_\sigma\partial^\sigma\phi^{\mu\nu}=0,\qquad
\Box=\partial_t^2-\nabla^2.}
$$

The overall sign of the field operator is immaterial for this homogeneous equation.
