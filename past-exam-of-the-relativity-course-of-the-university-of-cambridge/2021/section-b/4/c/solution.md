<h1 id="section-b/4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

An affine [geodesic](../../../../../geodesic.md) has constant squared tangent norm, but that constant depends on the scale of its parameter. Write

$$
\mathcal N=g_{AB}\dot x^A\dot x^B=\text{constant},\qquad
 du^2=\eta_{ab}dx^a dx^b.
$$

Using $W=\xi$ in the line element gives

$$
\left(\frac{du}{d\lambda}\right)^2=\mathcal N-\xi^2,
\qquad
\boxed{du^2=(\mathcal N-\xi^2)d\lambda^2.}
$$

The [affine normalization in a Kaluza-Klein geodesic reduction](../../../../../affine-normalization-in-a-kaluza-klein-geodesic-reduction.md) matters: the specific formula requested in the paper uses $\mathcal N=1$, namely $\lambda$ equal to the positive five-dimensional arclength. With that normalization,

$$
\boxed{d\tilde u^2=\frac{du^2}{1-\xi^2}.}
$$

A timelike four-dimensional projection has $du^2=c^2d\tau^2>0$, requiring $|\xi|<1$ in this normalization. At $|\xi|=1$ the projection is null and has no proper-time [four-velocity](../../../../../four-velocity.md). For an arbitrary [affine parameter](../../../../../affine-parameter.md) the first boxed relation, with $\mathcal N$ retained, is the general result.
