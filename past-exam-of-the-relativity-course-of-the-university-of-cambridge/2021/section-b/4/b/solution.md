<h1 id="section-b/4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let a dot denote differentiation with respect to the five-dimensional [affine parameter](../../../../../affine-parameter.md) $\lambda=\tilde u$, and define $W=\dot x^4+A_a\dot x^a$. Use the quadratic [geodesic Lagrangian](../../../../../geodesic-lagrangian.md)

$$
L=\frac12\left[\eta_{ab}\dot x^a\dot x^b+W^2\right].
$$

The assumed independence of $x^4$ makes it a [cyclic coordinate](../../../../../cyclic-coordinate.md), so the corresponding [Euler-Lagrange equation](../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) is

$$
\frac{d}{d\lambda}\frac{\partial L}{\partial\dot x^4}=0.
$$

Since $\partial L/\partial\dot x^4=W$, this gives

$$
\boxed{\dot x^4+A_a\dot x^a=\xi,\qquad \dot\xi=0.}
$$

Geometrically, this is the conserved momentum associated with the fifth-direction [Killing vector field](../../../../../killing-vector-field-split.md) $\partial_4$. It is the conserved fibre momentum underlying the [Kaluza-Klein geodesic Lorentz force](../../../../../kaluza-klein-geodesic-lorentz-force.md).
