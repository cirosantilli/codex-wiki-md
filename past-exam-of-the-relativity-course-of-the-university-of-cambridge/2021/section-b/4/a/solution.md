<h1 id="section-b/4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For [rest mass](../../../../../invariant-mass.md) $m$ and [electric charge](../../../../../electric-charge-split.md) $q$, the generally covariant [Lorentz force law](../../../../../lorentz-force.md) is

$$
\boxed{m\frac{Du^a}{d\tau}
=m\left(\frac{du^a}{d\tau}+\Gamma^a{}_{bc}u^bu^c\right)
=qF^a{}_b u^b,\qquad u^a=\frac{dx^a}{d\tau}.}
$$

Here $F_{ab}=\partial_aA_b-\partial_bA_a$ is the [electromagnetic field tensor](../../../../../electromagnetic-field-tensor.md) for the chosen [electromagnetic four-potential](../../../../../electromagnetic-four-potential.md) normalization, and the [covariant derivative](../../../../../covariant-derivative.md) includes the spacetime [Levi-Civita connection](../../../../../levi-civita-connection.md). Antisymmetry of $F_{ab}$ ensures $u_aDu^a/d\tau=0$, preserving the [four-velocity](../../../../../four-velocity.md) norm and the particle's [rest mass](../../../../../invariant-mass.md). An uncharged particle follows a [geodesic](../../../../../geodesic.md).
