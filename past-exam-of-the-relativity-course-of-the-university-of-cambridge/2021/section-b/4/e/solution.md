<h1 id="section-b/4/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Normalize the five-dimensional tangent as in part (c), and let $s=\sqrt{1-\xi^2}>0$. Since $u=c\tau$, one has $d\tau/d\lambda=s/c$, so

$$
\dot x^a=\frac{s}{c}u^a,\qquad
\ddot x^a=\frac{s^2}{c^2}\frac{du^a}{d\tau}.
$$

Substitution into the corrected equation in part (d) gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{du^a}{d\tau}=\frac{c\xi}{\sqrt{1-\xi^2}}F^a{}_b u^b.}
$$

This is the [Lorentz force](../../../../../lorentz-force.md) form in four-dimensional [Minkowski spacetime](../../../../../minkowski-spacetime.md). The effective charge-to-mass coefficient is proportional to the conserved fifth-direction momentum: in $c=1$ units and a potential normalization identifying $A$ with the [electromagnetic four-potential](../../../../../electromagnetic-four-potential.md), $q/m=\xi/\sqrt{1-\xi^2}$. The factor converting the geometrical $A$ to an SI potential fixes the physical charge units. Opposite signs of $\xi$ give opposite effective charges, and $\xi=0$ gives a neutral inertial projection. Antisymmetry gives $d(\eta_{ab}u^au^b)/d\tau=0$.

This is a [Kaluza-Klein theory](../../../../../kaluza-klein-theory.md) mechanism: a higher-dimensional [geodesic](../../../../../geodesic.md) appears as forced motion after projection. With the explicitly supplied $+---$ base signature, the positive fibre term gives a second positive direction in the five-dimensional metric. The derivation is a valid geometrical reduction of that metric; a conventional single-time spacetime would require a spacelike extra direction and corresponding sign changes. If one instead retains the sign printed in part (d), the inferred effective charge reverses, but that version is not the [geodesic equation](../../../../../geodesic-equation.md) of the metric supplied here.
