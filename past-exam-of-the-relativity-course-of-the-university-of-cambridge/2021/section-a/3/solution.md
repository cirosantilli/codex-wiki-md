<h1 id="section-a/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

Interpret the orbit in the [Schwarzschild metric](../../../../schwarzschild-spacetime-split.md) as an unforced circular [timelike geodesic](../../../../timelike-geodesic.md) and choose its plane as $\theta=\pi/2$. Put $f=1-2\mu/r$. The radial [geodesic equation](../../../../geodesic-equation.md) at constant radius uses the [Christoffel symbols](../../../../christoffel-symbol.md)

$$
\Gamma^r{}_{tt}=\frac{c^2\mu f}{r^2},\qquad
\Gamma^r{}_{\phi\phi}=-fr,
$$

and gives the [Angular velocity of a circular Schwarzschild geodesic](../../../../angular-velocity-of-a-circular-schwarzschild-geodesic.md)

$$
\Omega^2=\left(\frac{d\phi}{dt}\right)^2=\frac{c^2\mu}{r^3}=\frac{GM}{r^3}.
$$

The [proper-time normalization](../../../../proper-time-normalization.md) then yields

$$
\left(\frac{d\tau}{dt}\right)^2=f-\frac{r^2\Omega^2}{c^2}=1-\frac{3\mu}{r}.
$$

Over one revolution, the [Proper period of a circular Schwarzschild geodesic](../../../../proper-period-of-a-circular-schwarzschild-geodesic.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{\Delta\tau=2\pi\sqrt{\frac{r^3}{GM}}\sqrt{1-\frac{3GM}{c^2r}},\qquad r>3GM/c^2.}
$$

Both the gravitational and orbital kinematic contributions to [time dilation](../../../../time-dilation.md) are included. Circular timelike geodesics exist for $r>3\mu$; they are stable for $r>6\mu$ and unstable for $3\mu<r<6\mu$, with marginal stability at $6\mu$.

If “circular orbit” instead means a forced trajectory with an externally chosen [angular velocity](../../../../angular-velocity.md), the radius alone does not fix the answer. Its elapsed time is $\Delta\tau=\Delta t\sqrt{1-2\mu/r-r^2\Omega^2/c^2}$, or $2\pi\sqrt{1-2\mu/r-r^2\Omega^2/c^2}/|\Omega|$ per revolution. The [geodesic](../../../../geodesic.md) interpretation supplies the missing [angular velocity](../../../../angular-velocity.md).
