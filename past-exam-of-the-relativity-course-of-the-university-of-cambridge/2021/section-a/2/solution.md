<h1 id="section-a/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Put $\kappa=8\pi G/c^4$ and $T=T_\nu{}^\nu$. The [contracted Bianchi identity](../../../../contracted-bianchi-identity.md) and [metric compatibility](../../../../metric-compatibility.md) give

$$
\left(\frac12-\alpha\right)\partial_\mu R
=-\kappa\nabla_\nu T_\mu{}^\nu.
$$

Taking the [trace](../../../../matrix-trace.md) of the field equation in four dimensions gives $(1-4\alpha)R=-\kappa T$. For $\alpha\ne1/4$, eliminate $R$:

$$
\boxed{\nabla_\nu T_\mu{}^\nu
=\frac{\tfrac12-\alpha}{1-4\alpha}\,\partial_\mu T.}
$$

Ordinary covariantly coupled matter obeys [stress-energy conservation](../../../../stress-energy-conservation.md), $\nabla_\nu T_\mu{}^\nu=0$. Its [trace](../../../../matrix-trace.md) need not be constant, so the [trace coefficient fixed by stress-energy conservation](../../../../trace-coefficient-fixed-by-stress-energy-conservation.md) is

$$
\boxed{\alpha=\frac12.}
$$

This restores the [Einstein tensor](../../../../einstein-tensor.md) and is the only coefficient compatible with arbitrary conventional matter without adding a new constraint or changing the matter conservation law. Other coefficients can work for specially restricted sources with constant [trace](../../../../matrix-trace.md), but do not give the same universal matter coupling.

The excluded case $\alpha=1/4$ must be treated before dividing: its [trace](../../../../matrix-trace.md) equation requires $T=0$. If stress-energy is also conserved, its divergence equation requires $\partial_\mu R=0$. It can describe a restricted traceless-source sector with constant scalar curvature, not general matter such as ordinary dust. In particular it is not the general [trace](../../../../matrix-trace.md)-free Einstein equation with source $T_{\mu\nu}-g_{\mu\nu}T/4$, which is a different equation.
