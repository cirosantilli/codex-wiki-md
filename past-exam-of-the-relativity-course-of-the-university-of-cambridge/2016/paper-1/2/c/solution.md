<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A [light](../../../../../light.md) ray follows a [null geodesic](../../../../../null-geodesic.md). For a weak, approximately spherical field and [impact parameter](../../../../../impact-parameter.md) $b\gg GM/c^2$, the asymptotic [Schwarzschild light deflection](../../../../../schwarzschild-light-deflection.md) is

$$
\boxed{\Delta\theta=\frac{4GM}{bc^2}.}
$$

At the solar limb this is about $1.75$ arcseconds, whereas a simple Newtonian fast-particle calculation gives half that value. The difference tests relativistic [spacetime](../../../../../spacetime.md) geometry beyond the universality of slow-body free fall. The quoted [angle](../../../../../angle.md) compares incoming and outgoing directions far from the deflector, not a locally measured turn at a sharp vertex.

For an eclipse measurement, photograph background stars near the obscured [Sun](../../../../../sun.md) and compare their apparent positions with reference plates taken when that field is away from the [Sun](../../../../../sun.md). Back-projecting the deflected incoming [light](../../../../../light.md) makes a star appear displaced outward from the [Sun](../../../../../sun.md)'s apparent position. Fit the radial displacement against inverse angular separation from the [Sun](../../../../../sun.md), while allowing plate translation, rotation and scale. The expected pattern must be separated from [atmospheric refraction](../../../../../atmospheric-refraction.md), [optical distortion](../../../../../distortion-optics.md) and changes of focus or scale, rather than inferred from one displaced star.

The 1919 expeditions obtained observations at Sobral and Príncipe, published by [Frank Watson Dyson](../../../../../frank-watson-dyson.md), [Arthur Eddington](../../../../../arthur-eddington.md) and [Charles Davidson](../../../../../charles-rundle-davidson.md) in 1920. The original report compares eclipse and reference plates and discusses instrument scale and image-quality limitations; it should not be treated as an exact modern precision measurement.[pp. 291–333, observing arrangements, reference photographs, plate reductions and final deflection comparisons](../../../../../a-determination-of-the-deflection-of-light-by-the-sun-s-gravitational-field-by-frank-watson-dyson-and-others.md) Repeated observations with independent instruments and calibrated reference fields test whether the displacement follows the predicted geometry. [vacuum](../../../../../vacuum.md) gravitational bending is achromatic to this order, a useful distinction from dispersive propagation effects.
