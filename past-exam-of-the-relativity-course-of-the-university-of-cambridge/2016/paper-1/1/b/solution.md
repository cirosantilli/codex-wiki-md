<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Denote the parent [rest mass](../../../../../invariant-mass.md) by $M$, the daughter [rest masses](../../../../../invariant-mass.md) by $m_i$, and their total energies in any [inertial frame](../../../../../inertial-frame.md) by $E_{\rm before}$ and $E_{\rm after}$. For an isolated disintegration with all products included, [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) conservation gives $E_{\rm after}=E_{\rm before}$. The corresponding [kinetic energies](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) are $K_{\rm before}=E_{\rm before}-Mc^2$ and $K_{\rm after}=E_{\rm after}-\sum_i m_ic^2$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\Delta K=K_{\rm after}-K_{\rm before}=\left(M-\sum_i m_i\right)c^2.}
$$

Every [mass](../../../../../mass.md) on the right is a [rest mass](../../../../../invariant-mass.md), hence invariant under a [Lorentz transformation](../../../../../lorentz-transformation.md). This proves the [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) released by relativistic decay is frame independent even though either total [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) separately is not. In the parent [rest frame](../../../../../rest-frame.md) the initial [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) vanishes and the final [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) equals the [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) associated with this [mass defect](../../../../../mass-defect.md). External [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) input or omission of radiation would invalidate the energy-cancellation step; if [photons](../../../../../photon.md) are products, include their energies with zero rest-mass subtraction.
