<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use an [affine parameter](../../../../../affine-parameter.md) $\lambda$, with dots denoting its derivatives, and take $\alpha\ne0$ so the metric is nondegenerate for $r>0$. The [geodesic Lagrangian](../../../../../geodesic-lagrangian.md) is $\mathcal L=(\dot r^2+\alpha^2r^4\dot\phi^2)/2$. The [Euler-Lagrange equations](../../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) give

$$
\boxed{\ddot r-2\alpha^2r^3\dot\phi^2=0,\qquad \frac{d}{d\lambda}(\alpha^2r^4\dot\phi)=0,}
$$

or $\ddot\phi+4\dot r\dot\phi/r=0$. The conserved cyclic [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) is

$$
\boxed{J=\frac{\partial\mathcal L}{\partial\dot\phi}=\alpha^2r^4\dot\phi.}
$$

This is the [cyclic angular momentum in a rotational metric](../../../../../cyclic-angular-momentum-in-a-rotational-metric.md), associated with the rotational [Killing vector](../../../../../killing-vector-field-split.md) $\partial_\phi$. Explicitly, $\dot J=\alpha^2(4r^3\dot r\dot\phi+r^4\ddot\phi)=0$ by the angular equation.

Differentiating the [Lagrangian](../../../../../lagrangian.md) and substituting both equations gives

$$
\frac{d\mathcal L}{d\lambda}=\dot r\ddot r+2\alpha^2r^3\dot r\dot\phi^2+\alpha^2r^4\dot\phi\ddot\phi=(2+2-4)\alpha^2r^3\dot r\dot\phi^2=0.
$$

Thus **the squared tangent norm $2\mathcal L$ is constant**. If $\lambda$ is [arc length](../../../../../arc-length.md), it equals one; its numerical value otherwise depends on affine normalization. An arbitrary nonaffine parametrization would not preserve this constant-speed statement.
