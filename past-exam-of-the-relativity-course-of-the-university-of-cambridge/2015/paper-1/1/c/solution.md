<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Use [metric signature](../../../../../metric-signature.md) $(+---)$ and [four-velocities](../../../../../four-velocity.md) $U_i^a=\gamma_i c(1,\boldsymbol\beta_i)$, with $\gamma_i=(1-\beta_i^2)^{-1/2}$. Their [Lorentz invariant](../../../../../lorentz-invariance.md) [inner product](../../../../../inner-product-split.md) is

$$
\frac{U_1\cdot U_2}{c^2}
=\gamma_1\gamma_2(1-\boldsymbol\beta_1\cdot\boldsymbol\beta_2).
$$

In the [rest frame](../../../../../rest-frame.md) of either particle this same [scalar](../../../../../scalar.md) is the relative [Lorentz factor](../../../../../lorentz-factor.md) $\gamma_{\rm rel}$. Hence

$$
\beta_{\rm rel}^2
=1-\frac{(1-\beta_1^2)(1-\beta_2^2)}
{(1-\boldsymbol\beta_1\cdot\boldsymbol\beta_2)^2}.
$$

Let $d=\boldsymbol\beta_1\cdot\boldsymbol\beta_2$. The numerator after combining terms is

$$
(1-d)^2-(1-\beta_1^2)(1-\beta_2^2)
=\beta_1^2+\beta_2^2-2d-\beta_1^2\beta_2^2+d^2.
$$

Use $|\boldsymbol\beta_1\times\boldsymbol\beta_2|^2=\beta_1^2\beta_2^2-d^2$ to obtain

$$
\boxed{\beta_{\rm rel}^2=
\frac{|\boldsymbol\beta_1-\boldsymbol\beta_2|^2-
|\boldsymbol\beta_1\times\boldsymbol\beta_2|^2}
{(1-\boldsymbol\beta_1\cdot\boldsymbol\beta_2)^2}.}
$$

The two [rest frames](../../../../../rest-frame.md) agree on the relative speed, although their [velocity vectors](../../../../../velocity-vector.md) are expressed in different spatial frames. Checks include equal velocities giving zero, collinear velocities giving $|\beta_1-\beta_2|/|1-\beta_1\beta_2|$, and low speeds reducing to the Galilean relative speed. For timelike particles the [four-velocity](../../../../../four-velocity.md) [inner product](../../../../../inner-product-split.md) also ensures $0\le\beta_{\rm rel}<1$.
