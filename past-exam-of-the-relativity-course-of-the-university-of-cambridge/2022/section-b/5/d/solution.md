<h1 id="section-b/5/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

A photon arriving from infinity crosses the barrier precisely when $c^2k^2/2$ exceeds the maximum in part (c):

$$
\boxed{k^2>\frac{h^2}{27\mu^2c^2}.}
$$

At infinity its spatial tangent has speed in affine-parameter units $ck$, while $h$ is its angular momentum per unit affine normalization. A straight asymptotic ray with [impact parameter](../../../../../impact-parameter.md) $b$ therefore has $|h|=bck$. Thus

$$
\boxed{b=\frac{|h|}{ck},\qquad b<3\sqrt3\mu.}
$$

At equality, the incoming ray approaches the unstable [photon sphere](../../../../../photon-sphere.md) asymptotically and is not captured. A larger impact parameter gives an outer turning point and scattering back to infinity.
