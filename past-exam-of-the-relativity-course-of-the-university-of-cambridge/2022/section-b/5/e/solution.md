<h1 id="section-b/5/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Measure $\alpha$ in the [orthonormal tetrad](../../../../../orthonormal-frame-in-spacetime.md) of a static observer at $r>2\mu$. This specifies the local frame needed to define an angle. If $E_\infty$ is the conserved photon energy, [gravitational redshift](../../../../../gravitational-redshift.md) gives $E_{\rm loc}=E_\infty/\sqrt{1-2\mu/r}$. The tangential local [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) is $E_{\rm loc}\sin\alpha/c$, so the [impact parameter](../../../../../impact-parameter.md) is

$$
b=\frac{r\sin\alpha}{\sqrt{1-2\mu/r}}.
$$

Inside the [photon sphere](../../../../../photon-sphere.md), an outward ray must have $b<3\sqrt3\mu$ to cross the potential barrier. The [Schwarzschild photon escape cone](../../../../../schwarzschild-photon-escape-cone.md) therefore has critical half-angle

$$
\boxed{\alpha_{\rm crit}(r)=\arcsin\left[\frac{3\sqrt3\mu}{r}\sqrt{1-\frac{2\mu}{r}}\right],\qquad 0\le\alpha<\alpha_{\rm crit}.}
$$

The upper bound is strict: a critical ray only approaches the circular orbit. At $r=3\mu$, the critical half-angle is $\pi/2$, and exactly tangential light is circular. As $r\downarrow2\mu$, the cone shrinks to zero opening. No static observer exists exactly on the [Schwarzschild event horizon](../../../../../schwarzschild-event-horizon.md), and no future-directed photon emitted there escapes to exterior infinity; the zero-angle result is an exterior limit.
