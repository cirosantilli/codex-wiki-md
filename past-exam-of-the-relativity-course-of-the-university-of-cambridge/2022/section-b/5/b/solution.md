<h1 id="section-b/5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Set $E=0$ in part (a), substitute the two conserved quantities, and multiply the normalization by $f$:

$$
c^2k^2-\dot r^2-f\frac{h^2}{r^2}=0.
$$

Consequently the [null effective potential in Schwarzschild spacetime](../../../../../null-effective-potential-in-schwarzschild-spacetime.md) is

$$
\boxed{\frac12\dot r^2+V_{\rm eff}(r)=\frac12c^2k^2,\qquad
V_{\rm eff}(r)=\frac{h^2}{2r^2}\left(1-\frac{2\mu}{r}\right).}
$$

Only radii where the total radial energy is at least this [effective potential](../../../../../effective-potential.md) are accessible.
