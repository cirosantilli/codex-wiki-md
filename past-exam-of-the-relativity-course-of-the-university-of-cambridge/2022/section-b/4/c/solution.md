<h1 id="section-b/4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Choose the null [affine parameter](../../../../../affine-parameter.md) so that the [photon](../../../../../photon.md) tangent is its [four-momentum](../../../../../four-momentum.md) $P^\mu$. A [fundamental observer](../../../../../comoving-observer.md) has $U^\mu=(1,0,0,0)$ in cosmic-time coordinates, so the measured energy is $E=P_\mu U^\mu=c^2P^t$. The null normalization and conserved radial quantity in part (b) give

$$
P^t=\frac{|C|}{ac},\qquad E=\frac{c|C|}{a}.
$$

Thus [redshifting of peculiar momentum](../../../../../redshifting-of-peculiar-momentum.md) gives $E\propto a^{-1}$. Since $E=h\nu$, with $h$ the [Planck constant](../../../../../planck-constant.md), the received-to-emitted [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md) ratio is $\nu_R/\nu_E=a(t_E)/a(t_R)$. By the definition of [cosmological redshift](../../../../../cosmological-redshift.md),

$$
\boxed{1+z=\frac{\nu_E}{\nu_R}=\frac{a(t_R)}{a(t_E)}.}
$$
