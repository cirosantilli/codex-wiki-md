<h1 id="section-b/4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

A nearby [fundamental observer](../../../../../comoving-observer.md) measures radial proper distance $d\ell=a\,d\chi$ and proper time $dt$. Thus the signed radial [peculiar velocity](../../../../../peculiar-velocity.md) is

$$
\boxed{v=a\frac{d\chi}{dt}=\frac{a\dot\chi}{\dot t}.}
$$

Substitute $a^2\dot\chi=C$ and the massive normalization from part (b):

$$
\boxed{v(a)=\frac{C/a}{\sqrt{1+C^2/(a^2c^2)}},\qquad a\gamma v=C.}
$$

Equivalently, for velocity $v_0$ at scale factor $a_0$,

$$
v(a)=\frac{v_0a_0}{\sqrt{a^2(1-v_0^2/c^2)+a_0^2v_0^2/c^2}}.
$$

This is the exact law of [redshifting of peculiar momentum](../../../../../redshifting-of-peculiar-momentum.md). In the nonrelativistic limit, $v\propto a^{-1}$; an initially relativistic speed stays below $c$ and decreases as the universe expands.
