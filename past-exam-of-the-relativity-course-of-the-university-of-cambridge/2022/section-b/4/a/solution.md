<h1 id="section-b/4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [fundamental observer](../../../../../comoving-observer.md) follows the coarse-grained cosmological matter, with fixed [comoving coordinates](../../../../../comoving-coordinate.md). The [cosmological principle](../../../../../cosmological-principle.md) asserts [spatial homogeneity](../../../../../spatial-homogeneity.md) and [cosmological isotropy](../../../../../cosmological-isotropy.md) on large scales for this family of observers. A moving observer generally sees a preferred direction, for example a Doppler dipole in the [cosmic microwave background](../../../../../cosmic-microwave-background-split.md), even when the underlying cosmology is isotropic.

In an [FLRW metric](../../../../../friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md), these worldlines are orthogonal to homogeneous spatial slices. Synchronize their clocks to define [cosmic time](../../../../../cosmic-time.md) $t$, which is their [proper time](../../../../../proper-time.md). Along a fixed spatial label, $ds^2=c^2dt^2$, giving $g_{tt}=c^2$. Orthogonality of the spatial coordinate directions to the observers' [four-velocity](../../../../../four-velocity.md) gives $g_{ti}=0$. Thus, in the $+---$ [metric signature](../../../../../metric-signature.md),

$$
\boxed{ds^2=c^2dt^2+g_{ij}(t,\mathbf x)\,dx^idx^j,}
$$

where the spatial matrix $g_{ij}$ is negative definite. [Spatial homogeneity](../../../../../spatial-homogeneity.md) and [cosmological isotropy](../../../../../cosmological-isotropy.md) further restrict it to $-a(t)^2\gamma_{ij}(\mathbf x)$, with $\gamma$ a constant-curvature spatial [metric](../../../../../metric.md).
