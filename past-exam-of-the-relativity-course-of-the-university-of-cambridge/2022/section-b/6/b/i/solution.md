<h1 id="section-b/6/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The inverse transformation is $t=(p+q)/2$, $x=(q-p)/2$. Thus

$$
ds^2=\frac14\big[(dp+dq)^2-(dq-dp)^2\big]=dp\,dq.
$$

The [metric tensor](../../../../../../metric-tensor.md) in these [light-cone coordinates](../../../../../../light-cone-coordinates.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{(g_{ab})_{(p,q)}=\begin{pmatrix}0&1/2\\1/2&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
(g^{ab})=\begin{pmatrix}0&2\\2&0\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

Both off-diagonal entries are needed, since the quadratic line element sums over both index orders.
