<h1 id="section-b/6/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [Lorentz transformation](../../../../../../lorentz-transformation.md) is $t'=\gamma(t-vx)$, $x'=\gamma(x-vt)$ in units $c=1$. Adding and subtracting gives

$$
p'=\gamma(1+v)(t-x),\qquad q'=\gamma(1-v)(t+x).
$$

Since $\gamma(1+v)=\sqrt{(1+v)/(1-v)}$ and the other factor is its reciprocal,

$$
\boxed{p'=\alpha p,\qquad q'=q/\alpha,\qquad\alpha=\sqrt{\frac{1+v}{1-v}}=e^\eta.}
$$

Here $\eta$ is the boost [rapidity](../../../../../../rapidity.md). Their product is unchanged, as required by invariance of the [Minkowski interval](../../../../../../minkowski-interval.md).
