<h1 id="section-b/6/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [proper time](../../../../../../proper-time.md) normalization of the astronaut's [four-velocity](../../../../../../four-velocity.md) gives $\dot t^2-\dot x^2=1$. In the [light-cone coordinates](../../../../../../light-cone-coordinates.md) this is

$$
\boxed{\dot p\dot q=1.}
$$

The [four-acceleration](../../../../../../four-acceleration.md) has squared norm $\ddot t^2-\ddot x^2=-a^2$, because $a$ is the constant [proper acceleration](../../../../../../proper-acceleration.md). The same coordinate change therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\ddot p\ddot q=-a^2.}
$$

Equivalently write $\dot q=e^\eta$, $\dot p=e^{-\eta}$. Their second derivatives give $\ddot p\ddot q=-\dot\eta^2$, so the [rapidity form of one-dimensional proper acceleration](../../../../../../rapidity-form-of-one-dimensional-proper-acceleration.md) makes $\dot\eta=\pm a$, with sign fixing the acceleration direction.
