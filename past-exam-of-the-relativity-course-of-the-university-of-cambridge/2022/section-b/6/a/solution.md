<h1 id="section-b/6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

At any event on a massive observer's [worldline](../../../../../world-line.md), choose an [orthonormal tetrad](../../../../../orthonormal-frame-in-spacetime.md) with timelike basis vector $e_{(0)}=U/c$. Its components obey $g(e_{(a)},e_{(b)})=\eta_{ab}=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,-1,-1)$, so locally $ds^2=\eta_{ab}\,dX^{(a)}dX^{(b)}$: the [Minkowski metric](../../../../../minkowski-metric.md). A [local inertial frame](../../../../../local-inertial-frame.md) can also remove the [Christoffel symbols](../../../../../christoffel-symbol.md) at that event. Curvature cannot be removed on a finite region, and a frame following an accelerated observer need not remain inertial in its neighbourhood.

In general coordinates, $X^\mu(\tau)$ labels the trajectory, while the [four-velocity](../../../../../four-velocity.md) and [four-acceleration](../../../../../four-acceleration.md) are

$$
U^\mu=\frac{dX^\mu}{d\tau},\qquad
A^\mu=\frac{DU^\mu}{d\tau}=\frac{dU^\mu}{d\tau}+\Gamma^\mu_{\nu\rho}U^\nu U^\rho.
$$

Coordinate values $X^\mu$ are not themselves a vector under arbitrary nonlinear coordinate changes; their tangent $U$ is. For fixed rest mass $m$, the [four-momentum](../../../../../four-momentum.md) is $P^\mu=mU^\mu$ and the [four-force](../../../../../four-force.md) is $f^\mu=DP^\mu/d\tau=mA^\mu$. With the $+---$ [metric signature](../../../../../metric-signature.md),

$$
\boxed{U^\mu U_\mu=c^2,\qquad U^\mu A_\mu=0,\qquad A^\mu A_\mu=-a^2\le0,}
$$

where $a$ is the [proper acceleration](../../../../../proper-acceleration.md). The middle identity follows by differentiating the constant velocity norm; in the instantaneous [rest frame](../../../../../rest-frame.md), $U=(c,\mathbf0)$ and $A=(0,\mathbf a)$, proving the last identity.
