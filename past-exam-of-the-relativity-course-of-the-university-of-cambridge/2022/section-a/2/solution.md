<h1 id="section-a/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

The [metric tensor](../../../../metric-tensor.md) and its inverse are diagonal: $g_{xx}=1$, $g_{yy}=f^2$, $g^{xx}=1$, $g^{yy}=f^{-2}$ on a patch where $f\ne0$. Only $\partial_xg_{yy}=2ff'$ is nonzero. The [Christoffel symbol](../../../../christoffel-symbol.md) formula therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\Gamma^x_{yy}=-ff',\qquad\Gamma^y_{xy}=\Gamma^y_{yx}=\frac{f'}f,}
$$

with all other components zero. Using the stated [Riemann curvature tensor](../../../../riemann-curvature-tensor-split.md) convention,

$$
R^x{}_{yxy}=\partial_x\Gamma^x_{yy}-\Gamma^y_{yx}\Gamma^x_{yy}
=-(f'^2+ff'')+f'^2,
$$

so $\boxed{R^x{}_{yxy}=-ff''}$. This is the [curvature of a two-dimensional warped metric](../../../../curvature-of-a-two-dimensional-warped-metric.md); its [Gaussian curvature](../../../../gaussian-curvature.md) is $-f''/f$.
