<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

With $\gamma=(1-v^2/c^2)^{-1/2}$, [four-momentum conservation](../../../../../four-momentum-conservation.md) gives total [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) $E=(\gamma m_1+m_2)c^2$ and [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) $\mathbf p=\gamma m_1\mathbf v$. The compound system therefore has

$$
\boxed{M=\sqrt{m_1^2+m_2^2+2\gamma m_1m_2},\qquad
\mathbf V=\frac{\gamma m_1}{\gamma m_1+m_2}\mathbf v}.
$$

Here $M^2c^4=E^2-c^2p^2$. The excess over $m_1+m_2$ is retained [internal energy](../../../../../internal-energy-split.md), assuming [absorption](../../../../../absorption-electromagnetic-radiation.md) without escaping [radiation](../../../../../radiation.md).
