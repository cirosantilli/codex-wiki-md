<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use coordinates $x^\mu=(ct,\mathbf x)$, signature $(+---)$, and the convention $F^{0i}=-E_i/c$, $F^{ij}=-\epsilon_{ijk}B_k$. The antisymmetric [electromagnetic field tensor](../../../../../electromagnetic-field-tensor.md) combines six field components:

$$
F^{\mu\nu}=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&-E_x/c&-E_y/c&-E_z/c\\
E_x/c&0&-B_z&B_y\\
E_y/c&B_z&0&-B_x\\
E_z/c&-B_y&B_x&0
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

It transforms as $F'^{\mu\nu}=\Lambda^\mu{}_\alpha\Lambda^\nu{}_\beta F^{\alpha\beta}$. Lowering indices changes the time–space signs: $F_{0i}=E_i/c$, while $F_{ij}=F^{ij}$. The [covariant tensor](../../../../../covariant-tensor.md) instead uses the inverse coordinate Jacobian on each lower index.

For a frame moving with [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) $\mathbf v$ relative to the original one,

$$
\boxed{\mathbf E'_\parallel=\mathbf E_\parallel,\quad
\mathbf E'_\perp=\gamma(\mathbf E+\mathbf v\times\mathbf B)_\perp},
$$



$$
\boxed{\mathbf B'_\parallel=\mathbf B_\parallel,\quad
\mathbf B'_\perp=\gamma\left(\mathbf B-\frac{\mathbf v\times\mathbf E}{c^2}\right)_\perp}.
$$

Electric and [magnetic fields](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) therefore mix under a boost; they are frame-dependent parts of one tensor. For example, a purely transverse [electric field](../../../../../electric-field-split.md) acquires a [magnetic field](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) in a boosted frame. The [electromagnetic field invariants](../../../../../electromagnetic-field-invariants.md) $B^2-E^2/c^2$ and $\mathbf E\cdot\mathbf B$ restrict which fields can be eliminated by a frame change. The covariant [force](../../../../../force-split.md) law $dp^\mu/d\tau=qF^\mu{}_\nu U^\nu$ guarantees consistent particle dynamics in all [inertial frames](../../../../../inertial-frame.md).
