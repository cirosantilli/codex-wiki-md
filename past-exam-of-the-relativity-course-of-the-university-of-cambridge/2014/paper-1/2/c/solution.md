<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The strongest pre-2014 evidence combines large gravitating mass with extreme compactness. In an [X-ray binary](../../../../../x-ray-binary.md), companion-star radial velocities determine a [binary mass function](../../../../../binary-mass-function.md); [orbital inclination](../../../../../orbital-inclination.md), donor properties and distance complete the mass inference. An unseen component above conventional neutron-star [masses](../../../../../mass.md) is a black-hole candidate. For example, a 2011 Cygnus X-1 analysis combined optical photometry, spectroscopy and distance information to infer a compact-object mass about $14.8M_\odot$, with model and observational uncertainties.[Abstract and dynamical-model analysis](../../../../../the-mass-of-the-black-hole-in-cygnus-x-1-by-jerome-a-orosz-and-others.md)

Near [Sagittarius A\*](../../../../../sagittarius-a.md), infrared stellar [astrometry](../../../../../astrometry.md) and radial velocities trace nearly [Keplerian orbits](../../../../../kepler-orbit.md) around a dark compact mass. The concentration of several million solar [masses](../../../../../mass.md) in a small volume strongly challenges an extended cluster of ordinary remnants; source confusion, reference-frame errors, distance and possible extended mass must be fitted.[Abstract and orbital-fit/systematic-error discussion](../../../../../measuring-distance-and-properties-of-the-milky-way-s-central-supermassive-black-hole-with-stellar-orbits-by-andrea-m-ghez-and-others.md) Gas and [maser](../../../../../maser-split.md) dynamics provide related compact-mass tests in other nuclei.

[accretion](../../../../../accretion.md) explains luminous X-rays, rapid variability and broad relativistic disk features. These constrain size and the strong-field environment, but emission alone is not unique to an [event horizon](../../../../../event-horizon.md). Lack of surface-like signatures is supporting, model-dependent evidence. **Mass, compactness and consistent [accretion](../../../../../accretion.md) phenomenology together support the black-hole interpretation**; large-radius mass measurements alone do not directly demonstrate a horizon.
