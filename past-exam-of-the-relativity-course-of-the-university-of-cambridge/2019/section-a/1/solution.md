<h1 id="section-a/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

The conserved initial [four-momentum](../../../../four-momentum.md) of a particle with positive [rest mass](../../../../invariant-mass.md) satisfies $P_\mu P^\mu=m^2c^2>0$. A single [photon](../../../../photon.md) instead has null [four-momentum](../../../../four-momentum.md), $K_\mu K^\mu=0$. [Four-momentum conservation](../../../../four-momentum-conservation.md) for an isolated one-body decay would require $P^\mu=K^\mu$, contradicting these [Lorentz invariants](../../../../lorentz-invariance.md). Thus **decay into a single photon is impossible in every inertial frame**.

Equivalently, the incoming [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) and [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) obey $E^2-c^2|\mathbf p|^2=m^2c^4$, whereas a single outgoing [photon](../../../../photon.md) obeys $E=c|\mathbf p|$. Changing the laboratory [speed](../../../../speed.md) cannot change this mismatch of the invariant [mass shell](../../../../mass-shell.md).
