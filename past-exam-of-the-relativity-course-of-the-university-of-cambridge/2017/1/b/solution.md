<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a [scalar field](../../../../scalar-field.md), $\nabla_\mu\phi=\partial_\mu\phi$. Apply the [product rule](../../../../product-rule.md) to the [stress-energy tensor](../../../../stress-energy-tensor-split.md), using [metric compatibility](../../../../metric-compatibility.md) to pass the [covariant derivative](../../../../covariant-derivative.md) through $g^{\mu\nu}$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\nabla_\mu T^{\mu\nu}
={}&(\Box\phi)\nabla^\nu\phi
+\nabla^\mu\phi\,\nabla_\mu\nabla^\nu\phi\\
&-\nabla_\sigma\phi\,\nabla^\nu\nabla^\sigma\phi
+m^2\phi\nabla^\nu\phi.
\end{aligned}
$$

The middle terms cancel because the [Levi-Civita connection](../../../../levi-civita-connection.md) gives a [symmetric Hessian of a scalar field](../../../../symmetric-hessian-of-a-scalar-field.md). Thus, with the sign conventions of this question, the [scalar stress-energy divergence identity](../../../../scalar-stress-energy-divergence-identity.md) becomes

$$
\boxed{\nabla_\mu T^{\mu\nu}=(\Box\phi+m^2\phi)\nabla^\nu\phi=0.}
$$

The last equality is the [Klein-Gordon equation](../../../../klein-gordon-equation.md). This [stress-energy conservation](../../../../stress-energy-conservation.md) follows from the field equation and [metric compatibility](../../../../metric-compatibility.md); it does not require imposing the [Einstein field equations](../../../../einstein-field-equations-split.md).
