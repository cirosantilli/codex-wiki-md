# Laurent operator

↑ **Parent:** [von Neumann stability analysis](von-neumann-stability-analysis.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurent_operator)

A Laurent operator on the whole integer lattice is a translation-invariant bi-infinite matrix. Under the [discrete Fourier transform](discrete-fourier-transform.md) it becomes multiplication by its symbol, so its $2$-operator norm is the essential supremum of the symbol's modulus.
