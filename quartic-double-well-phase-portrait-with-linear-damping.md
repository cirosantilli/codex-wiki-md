# Quartic double-well phase portrait with linear damping

↑ **Parent:** [Phase portrait](phase-portrait.md)

For $\dot x=\alpha x-y+y^3$, $\dot y=-x$, the [energy](energy.md) $H=x^2-y^2+y^4/2$ satisfies $\dot H=2\alpha x^2$. At $\alpha=0$, its compact [level sets](level-set.md) give two families of closed [periodic orbits](periodic-orbit.md) around $(0,\pm1)$ and an outer family around both; $H=0$ is a figure-eight [homoclinic orbit](homoclinic-orbit.md) pair through the [saddle point](saddle-point.md) at the origin. The two minima of $H$ are [center equilibria](center-equilibrium.md) for zero damping, [stable foci](stable-spiral.md) for small negative $\alpha$, and unstable [foci](focus-dynamical-systems.md) for small positive $\alpha$. For $\alpha\ne0$, strict monotonicity along nonconstant solutions excludes [periodic orbits](periodic-orbit.md) and [homoclinic orbits](homoclinic-orbit.md).
