# Frame dragging inside a slowly rotating spherical shell

↑ **Parent:** [Lense-Thirring precession](lense-thirring-precession.md)

For a weakly gravitating thin spherical shell of mass $M$, radius $R$ and uniform angular velocity $\Omega$, its interior [linearized gravity](linearized-gravity.md) metric has constant scalar potentials $A=-GM/R$, $C=GM/R$ and $g_{0i}=\omega(\widehat{\mathbf z}\times\mathbf x)_i$. The scalar mass shell is a monopole. Its mass current is a dipole: regular interior and decaying exterior radial solutions are proportional to $r$ and $r^{-2}$, and their derivative jump fixes the factor $4/3$. Local inertial axes co-rotate with the shell at $\omega$ relative to asymptotic nonrotating axes. This is the leading interior [Lense-Thirring precession](lense-thirring-precession.md), neglecting quadratic rotation and higher gravitational orders.
