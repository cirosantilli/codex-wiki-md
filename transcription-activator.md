# Transcription activator

↑ **Parent:** [Transcription factor](transcription-factor.md)

A [transcription factor](transcription-factor.md) that increases [transcription](transcription-biology.md) of a target [gene](gene.md). It can act by recruiting or modulating the [RNA polymerase](rna-polymerase.md) machinery and its associated factors.
