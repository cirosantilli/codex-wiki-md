# Angular momentum raising operator

↑ **Parent:** [Angular momentum ladder operator](angular-momentum-ladder-operator.md)

The [angular momentum raising operator](angular-momentum-raising-operator.md) $L_+=L_1+iL_2$ satisfies $[L_3,L_+]=\hbar L_+$ and $[L^2,L_+]=0$. It preserves the total [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) while increasing the third-component [magnetic quantum number](magnetic-quantum-number.md) by one, whenever its result is nonzero. Its [adjoint operator](adjoint-operator.md) is the [angular momentum lowering operator](angular-momentum-lowering-operator.md) $L_-$. Expanding the product using the [angular momentum commutation relations](angular-momentum-commutation-relations.md) gives $L_-L_+=L^2-L_3^2-\hbar L_3$, hence

$$
\|L_+\psi\|^2=\hbar^2[\lambda-m(m+1)]\|\psi\|^2
$$

for simultaneous [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $\lambda\hbar^2,m\hbar$. For normalized $|l,m\rangle$ this yields $L_+|l,m\rangle=\hbar\sqrt{(l-m)(l+m+1)}|l,m+1\rangle$ after fixing consistent phases. At $m=l$ the result is zero, the upper [angular momentum ladder endpoint](angular-momentum-ladder-endpoint.md). For a different [quantization axis](quantization-axis.md), cyclically permute the components; for example $L_2+iL_3$ raises the $L_1$ projection.
