# Uranium-238

↑ **Parent:** [Uranium](uranium.md)

A [fertile material](fertile-material.md) whose [neutron capture](neutron-capture.md) produces [uranium-239](uranium-239.md). It can undergo neutron-induced fission at sufficiently high incident neutron energies, while slow-neutron capture can instead initiate breeding of [plutonium-239](plutonium-239.md).
