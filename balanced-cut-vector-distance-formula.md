# Balanced cut-vector distance formula

↑ **Parent:** [Kahn-Kalai counterexample to the Borsuk conjecture](kahn-kalai-counterexample-to-the-borsuk-conjecture.md)

Represent a balanced partition of $[4p]$ by the zero-one vector recording which unordered pairs lie across its cut. Complementary subsets represent the same cut; choose the representative containing coordinate one. Two such cuts differ on the pairs crossing the [symmetric difference](symmetric-difference.md) of their sides, giving the displayed distance. Its maximum is $4p^2$, attained exactly when $t=p$. Every cut has $4p^2$ occupied coordinates, so the vectors lie in an [affine hyperplane](affine-hyperplane.md) of dimension at most $\binom{4p}{2}-1$.
