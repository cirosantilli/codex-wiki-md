# de Broglie phase harmony

↑ **Parent:** [Matter wave](matter-wave.md)

In the 1923 note _Waves and Quanta_, [Louis de Broglie](louis-de-broglie.md) proposed a periodic process associated with [rest energy](rest-energy.md), remaining in phase with a wave along the moving particle's path. He distinguished the internal [frequency](frequency.md) slowed by [time dilation](time-dilation.md) from the wave [frequency](frequency.md) based on total [energy](energy-split.md).[Nature 112, p. 540, 13 October 1923, opening paragraph](waves-and-quanta-by-louis-de-broglie.md) The proposal developed into his 1924 thesis.[Nobel Lectures, Physics 1922–1941; Louis de Broglie, Biographical, printed p. 257: 1924 thesis and doctoral degree](nobel-lectures-physics-1922-1941/louis-de-broglie-biographical.md) In the 1929 lecture _The wave nature of the electron_, he reconstructed the argument using a [Lorentz transformation](lorentz-transformation.md).[Nobel Lectures, Physics 1922–1941; 12 December 1929, printed pp. 247–249: rest-frame periodicity, Lorentz transformation and momentum](nobel-lectures-physics-1922-1941/the-wave-nature-of-the-electron-by-louis-de-broglie.md)

Here is that [quantum phase](quantum-phase.md) argument in modern notation. Set $\omega_0=mc^2/\hbar$ in the [rest frame](rest-frame.md). A [Lorentz transformation](lorentz-transformation.md) to a frame where the particle has [velocity](velocity.md) $v$ gives

$$
t'=\gamma\left(t-\frac{vx}{c^2}\right),\qquad
\gamma=\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-v^2/c^2}},\qquad
\phi=\omega_0t'=\frac{Et-px}{\hbar},\qquad
E=\gamma mc^2,\quad p=\gamma mv.
$$

Along $x=vt$, [proper time](proper-time.md) is $\tau=t/\gamma$, so

$$
\phi(vt,t)=\frac{(E-pv)t}{\hbar}
=\frac{mc^2t}{\gamma\hbar}=\omega_0\tau.
$$

This is the harmony: evaluating the spatial wave at the moving particle gives the slower clock phase. At fixed $x$, its [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) is instead $E/\hbar=\gamma\omega_0$. Since $k=p/\hbar$, the [de Broglie wavelength](de-broglie-wavelength.md) follows as $\lambda=2\pi/k=h/p$. The proposed clock was a theoretical construction, not an observed mechanical vibration.
