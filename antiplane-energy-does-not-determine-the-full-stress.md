# Antiplane energy does not determine the full stress

↑ **Parent:** [Antiplane shear](antiplane-shear.md)

On an [antiplane shear](antiplane-shear.md) $F=I+e_3\otimes(p,q,0)$, the [first strain invariant](first-strain-invariant.md) satisfy $I_1=I_2=3+p^2+q^2$, $I_3=1$. Restricting an isotropic objective [strain energy density](strain-energy-density.md) to this family determines only derivatives tangent to the family. It fixes $S_{31}=2w'(p^2+q^2)p$ and $S_{32}=2w'(p^2+q^2)q$, but does not specify the whole [Cauchy stress tensor](cauchy-stress-tensor.md). For example the [neo-Hookean solid](neo-hookean-solid.md) energy $W=\mu(|F|^2-3)/2-\mu\log\det F$ gives $S=\mu(B-I)$ on this family. Even though the reference state is stress free, this is not generally a scalar multiple of $B$.
