# Steady-state creep rate

↑ **Parent:** [Creep (deformation)](creep-deformation.md)

The approximately constant [strain](strain.md) rate in secondary [material creep](creep-deformation.md) at specified [stress](stress.md), [temperature](temperature-split.md) and material state. A lifetime estimate $t\simeq\varepsilon_f/\dot\varepsilon$ assumes that rate remains representative; evolving area, damage and tertiary [material creep](creep-deformation.md) can shorten it.
