# A Hermitian nilpotent BRST charge requires an indefinite auxiliary space

↑ **Parent:** [BRST charge](brst-charge.md)

On a positive-definite Hilbert space, these two identities imply $\|Q\psi\|^2=\langle\psi,Q^2\psi\rangle=0$, hence $Q=0$ on its invariant domain. A nontrivial covariant [BRST charge](brst-charge.md) therefore acts before reduction on an indefinite-inner-product gauge/ghost space. Exact vectors are null and orthogonal to closed vectors, and [BRST cohomology](brst-cohomology.md) quotients them out. Positivity of the reduced physical space requires the additional gauge-theory structure; it does not follow from abstract nilpotence alone.
