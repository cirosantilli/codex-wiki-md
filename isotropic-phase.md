# Isotropic phase

↑ **Parent:** [Liquid crystal](liquid-crystal.md)

An isotropic phase has no preferred spatial direction. In a [liquid crystal](liquid-crystal.md), its orientational [nematic order parameter](nematic-order-parameter.md) vanishes, although the molecules themselves may be anisotropic.
