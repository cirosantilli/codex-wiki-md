# Exponential dispersion family of order one

↑ **Parent:** [Exponential dispersion model](exponential-dispersion-model.md)

An order-one [exponential dispersion family](exponential-dispersion-model.md) has a scalar [natural parameter](natural-parameter-of-an-exponential-family.md) and canonical sufficient statistic $y$; its density or mass function has the displayed form on parameter-independent support. Differentiating its normalization gives $\mathbb E Y=b'(\theta)$ and $\operatorname{Var}(Y)=\phi b''(\theta)$. Known observation weights $w$ replace $\phi$ by $\phi/w$. Its [variance function](variance-function.md) is $V(\mu)=b''((b')^{-1}(\mu))$, and its [canonical link function](canonical-link-function.md) is $(b')^{-1}$.
