# Affine momentum normalization of a geodesic

↑ **Parent:** [Affine parameter](affine-parameter.md)

For physical [four-momentum](four-momentum.md) $P^\mu=m\,dx^\mu/d\tau$, the covariant-component equation is $dP_\mu/d\tau=(\partial_\mu g_{\nu\sigma})P^\nu P^\sigma/(2m)$. The equation without $1/m$ instead uses a tangent parametrization, or the [affine parameter](affine-parameter.md) $\lambda=\tau/m$ when the tangent is the physical momentum.
