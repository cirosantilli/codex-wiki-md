# Gaussian approximation to a nonresonant thermonuclear rate

↑ **Parent:** [Gamow peak](gamow-peak.md)

For a slowly varying nuclear factor and penetration factor $\exp[-2\sqrt{E_B/E}]$, the thermal integral is $\int_0^\infty e^{-\Phi(E)}dE$, where $\Phi(E)=E/(k_BT)+2\sqrt{E_B/E}$. The [Gamow peak](gamow-peak.md) occurs at $E_0=[E_B(k_BT)^2]^{1/3}$, with $\Phi(E_0)=3E_0/(k_BT)$ and $\Phi''(E_0)=3/(2k_BTE_0)$. The [Gaussian integral](gaussian-integral.md) gives $\sqrt{4\pi k_BTE_0/3}e^{-3E_0/(k_BT)}$. Combined with the Maxwellian prefactor $(k_BT)^{-3/2}$ this produces the displayed [thermonuclear reaction rate](thermonuclear-reaction-rate.md) at fixed reactant density. The approximation assumes $E_B\gg k_BT$ and excludes strong resonances or rapidly varying nuclear factors.
