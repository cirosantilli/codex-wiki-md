# Temporal sensing in bacterial chemotaxis

↑ **Parent:** [Chemotaxis](chemotaxis.md)

[Temporal sensing in bacterial chemotaxis](temporal-sensing-in-bacterial-chemotaxis.md) compares changing stimulation during movement with a recent adapted baseline. Attractant increases can lengthen runs and decreases can increase tumbling, producing biased movement up a chemical [gradient](gradient.md). Rapid spatially uniform concentration steps distinguish a temporal response from a requirement for front-to-back spatial comparison.[pp. 2509–2512, temporal gradient apparatus and motility responses](the-gradient-sensing-mechanism-in-bacterial-chemotaxis-by-robert-m-macnab-and-daniel-e-koshland-jr.md)
