# Sum of future-pointing null vectors

↑ **Parent:** [Null vector](null-vector.md)

In [Minkowski spacetime](minkowski-spacetime.md) with [metric signature](metric-signature.md) $(-,+,+,+)$, write two nonzero future-pointing [null vectors](null-vector.md) as $A=a(1,\mathbf n)$ and $B=b(1,\mathbf m)$, where $a,b>0$ and $\mathbf n,\mathbf m$ are [unit vectors](unit-vector.md). Their [Lorentzian inner product](lorentzian-inner-product.md) gives

$$
(A+B)\cdot(A+B)=-2ab(1-\mathbf n\cdot\mathbf m)\leq0.
$$

Thus their sum is a future-pointing [causal vector](causal-vector.md). It is a [null vector](null-vector.md) exactly when the spatial directions coincide, and a [timelike vector](timelike-vector.md) otherwise. The positivity of the time components is essential: adding past- and future-pointing [null vectors](null-vector.md) can instead produce a [spacelike vector](spacelike-vector.md).
