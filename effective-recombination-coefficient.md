# Effective recombination coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Radiative recombination](radiative-recombination.md)

The effective recombination coefficient for a [spectral line](spectral-line.md) counts recombinations and subsequent [radiative cascades](radiative-cascade.md) producing that line. At sufficiently low density, its photon production rate per volume is $n_en_{\rm parent}\alpha_\ell^{\rm eff}$. It depends on gas temperature, optical-depth assumptions such as [Case B recombination](case-b-recombination.md), and sometimes density. It differs from the total rate coefficient removing the parent ion.
