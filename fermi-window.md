# Fermi window

↑ **Parent:** [Fermi-Dirac distribution](fermi-dirac-distribution.md)

The [Fermi window](fermi-window.md) measures sensitivity of occupation to [energy](energy-split.md) near the [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md). It is positive, even about $\mu$, has width of order $k_BT$, and integrates to one over the full [energy](energy-split.md) line. Its centred [variance](variance-split.md) is $\pi^2(k_BT)^2/3$. A band restricted to nonnegative [energy](energy-split.md) contains weight $n_F(0,\mu)$ instead of exactly one; extending to the full line is exponentially accurate when $\mu\gg k_BT$. At zero [temperature](temperature-split.md) the window tends to a [Dirac delta function](dirac-delta-function.md) at the [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md).
