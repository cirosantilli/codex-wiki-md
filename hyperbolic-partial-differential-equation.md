# Hyperbolic partial differential equation

↑ **Parent:** [Principal symbol of a partial differential equation](principal-symbol-of-a-partial-differential-equation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperbolic_partial_differential_equation)

A scalar second-order equation

$$
Au_{tt}+2Bu_{tx}+Cu_{xx}+\text{lower-order terms}=0
$$

is hyperbolic where $B^2-AC>0$. Equivalently, its [principal symbol](principal-symbol-of-a-partial-differential-equation.md) has two distinct real characteristic directions.
