# Chebotarev density theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet density](dirichlet-density.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chebotarev_density_theorem)

For a finite [Galois extension](finite-galois-extension.md) $L/K$ and a [conjugacy class](conjugacy-class.md) $C$ in $G=\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$, the [unramified](unramified-extension.md) primes with [Frobenius conjugacy class](frobenius-conjugacy-class.md) $C$ have [Dirichlet density](dirichlet-density.md) $|C|/|G|$. In particular,

$$
\sum_{\mathfrak p:\,\operatorname{Frob}_{\mathfrak p}\in C}(N\mathfrak p)^{-s}
=\frac{|C|}{|G|}\log\frac1{s-1}+O(1)\quad(s\downarrow1).
$$

One proof uses [Brauer induction](brauer-induction.md), the [Artin L-function order at one](artin-l-function-order-at-one.md), and [character orthogonality](character-orthogonality.md). The trivial [Artin L-function](artin-l-function.md) is the [Dedekind zeta function](dedekind-zeta-function.md) of $K$. The full theorem also gives the natural density, but this logarithmic estimate alone establishes the [Dirichlet density](dirichlet-density.md) assertion.
