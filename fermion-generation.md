# Fermion generation

↑ **Parent:** [Standard Model fermion](standard-model-fermion.md)

A generation groups corresponding [quarks](quark.md) and [leptons](lepton.md) with the same gauge quantum numbers as their counterparts in other generations. The [Standard Model](standard-model-split.md) has three such families. [Yukawa matrices](yukawa-matrix.md) assign their different masses, and the mismatch of up-type and down-type mass bases produces the [CKM matrix](cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md).
