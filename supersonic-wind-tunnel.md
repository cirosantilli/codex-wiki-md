# Supersonic wind tunnel

↑ **Parent:** [Wind tunnel](wind-tunnel.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supersonic_wind_tunnel)

A [supersonic wind tunnel](supersonic-wind-tunnel.md) produces controlled [supersonic flow](supersonic-flow.md) in its test section. Its [nozzle](nozzle.md) geometry and imposed [pressure](pressure-split.md) conditions set the test [Mach number](mach-number.md).
