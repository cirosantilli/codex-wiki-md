# Quasistatic spherical Stefan problem with capillarity

↑ **Parent:** [Stefan problem](stefan-problem.md)

For a one-phase spherical [Stefan problem](stefan-problem.md) with melting-temperature boundary value $T_s-\Gamma/a$ and supercooling $\Delta T=T_s-T_\infty>0$, use the latent-to-sensible heat ratio $S=\mathcal L/(c_p\Delta T)$. Large $S$ separates heat diffusion from slow interface motion. The exterior harmonic temperature is $T=T_\infty+(\Delta T-\Gamma/a)a/r$. The [Stefan condition](stefan-condition.md) gives the displayed radius equation with $a_c=\Gamma/\Delta T$. This is the large inverse-[Stefan number](stefan-number.md) convention; the common sensible-to-latent Stefan number is $1/S$.
