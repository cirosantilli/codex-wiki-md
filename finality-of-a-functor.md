# Finality of a functor

↑ **Parent:** [Final functor](final-functor.md)

The finality property of a [functor](functor.md) means every required [comma category](comma-category.md) is nonempty and connected. It is the condition defining a [final functor](final-functor.md); the displayed criterion uses [categorical connected components](connected-component-of-a-category.md).
