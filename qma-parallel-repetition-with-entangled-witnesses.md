# QMA parallel repetition with entangled witnesses

↑ **Parent:** [QMA](qma.md)

Independent verifier circuits on $r$ witness registers induce a threshold acceptance effect built from tensor products of $M$ and $I-M$, where $M$ is the [quantum verifier acceptance operator](quantum-verifier-acceptance-operator.md). In a product eigenbasis of $M$, the effect's [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are threshold probabilities for independent [Bernoulli random variables](bernoulli-distribution.md) with parameters given by the selected eigenvalues of $M$. Bounding every parameter on a NO instance bounds the effect's [operator norm](operator-norm.md), even if the actual submitted witness is entangled. Independence is used only for the spectral calculation, not assumed for the witness.
