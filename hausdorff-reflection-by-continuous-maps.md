# Hausdorff reflection by continuous maps

↑ **Parent:** [Quotient topology](quotient-topology.md)

For a [topological space](topological-space.md) $X$, the displayed relation is an [equivalence relation](equivalence-relation.md). Give its quotient the [quotient topology](quotient-topology.md). Every inverse image under a continuous map from $X$ to a [Hausdorff space](hausdorff-space.md) is a [saturation of a subset](saturation-of-a-subset.md). Distinct quotient classes can be distinguished by some such map; disjoint open neighborhoods of their images pull back to disjoint saturated open sets, whose quotient images are open. Thus the quotient is Hausdorff. Each continuous map from $X$ to a Hausdorff space factors uniquely through this quotient, and the factor is continuous by the quotient topology. This is its universal reflection property.
