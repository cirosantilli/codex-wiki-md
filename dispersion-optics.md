# Dispersion (optics)

↑ **Parent:** [Geometrical optics](geometrical-optics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dispersion_(optics))

Optical dispersion is the dependence of [refractive index](refractive-index.md) or [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md) on [frequency](frequency.md). It gives an [optical prism](prism-optics.md) wavelength-dependent deviation and causes [chromatic aberration](chromatic-aberration.md).
