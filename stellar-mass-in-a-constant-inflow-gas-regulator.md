# Stellar mass in a constant-inflow gas regulator

↑ **Parent:** [Constant-inflow gas regulator solution](constant-inflow-gas-regulator-solution.md)

For initially no stars, $M_\star=\Phi t+(M_0-\Phi/\epsilon)(1-e^{-\epsilon t})$. Adding the [gas](gas.md) solution verifies $M_g+M_\star=M_0+\Phi t$. Thus equilibrium of the [gas](gas.md) reservoir coexists with continuing linear growth of stellar [mass](mass.md).
