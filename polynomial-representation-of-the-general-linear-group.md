# Polynomial representation of the general linear group

↑ **Parent:** [Rational representation of the general linear group](rational-representation-of-the-general-linear-group.md)

In a [polynomial representation](polynomial-representation-of-the-general-linear-group.md), every matrix coefficient is a [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) in the matrix entries. The action extends to the monoid of all matrices. The scalar action splits it into homogeneous [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) degrees; each homogeneous degree-$n$ category is the [module](module-mathematics.md) category of the [Schur algebra](schur-algebra.md) $S(m,n)$. Its irreducibles are the [Schur modules](schur-module.md) $D_\lambda(\mathbb C^m)$ with $\lambda\vdash n$ and at most $m$ rows.
