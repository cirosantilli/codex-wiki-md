# Bartlett-corrected likelihood-ratio statistic

↑ **Parent:** [Bartlett correction](bartlett-correction.md)

If $\mathbb E_0W=d(1+b/n+O(n^{-2}))$, the Bartlett-corrected statistic has mean $d+O(n^{-2})$. It is commonly calibrated against a [chi-squared distribution](chi-squared-distribution.md) with $d$ degrees of freedom. Equivalently, to the displayed order, one may multiply $W$ by $1-b/n$.
