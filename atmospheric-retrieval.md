# Atmospheric retrieval

↑ **Parent:** [Exoplanet atmospheric observatory](exoplanet-atmospheric-observatory.md)

An atmospheric retrieval fits a parametrized [radiative transfer](radiative-transfer.md) model to an [exoplanet transmission spectrum](exoplanet-transmission-spectrum.md) or [exoplanet emission spectrum](exoplanet-emission-spectrum.md). Temperature, abundances, clouds and reference radius/pressure can trade off against one another. Posterior precision within one model is not proof that its structural assumptions are unique.
