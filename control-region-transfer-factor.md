# Control-region transfer factor

↑ **Parent:** [Particle-physics control region](particle-physics-control-region.md)

A ratio translating a background yield in a [particle-physics control region](particle-physics-control-region.md) into its yield in a [signal region](signal-region.md). It may be estimated with simulation and [calibration](calibration-differential-geometry.md) measurements. Its uncertainty must include acceptance, response and physics differences between the regions; contaminating backgrounds are subtracted or fitted jointly. A [profile likelihood](profile-likelihood.md) can constrain the control and signal counts together rather than treating the transferred estimate as exact.
