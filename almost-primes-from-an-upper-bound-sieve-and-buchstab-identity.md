# Almost-primes from an upper-bound sieve and Buchstab identity

↑ **Parent:** [Selberg upper-bound sieve](selberg-upper-bound-sieve.md)

For a product of finitely many admissible linear forms, first omit a fixed finite set of locally obstructing primes. Apply the [Buchstab identity](buchstab-identity.md) at a large fixed $w$, and bound each removed term by the [Selberg upper-bound sieve](selberg-upper-bound-sieve.md). The convergent tail $\sum_{p\geq w}1/(p(\log p)^k)$ leaves a positive proportion with no prime divisor in $[w,X^\delta)$. Their distinct prime divisors below $w$ are finite in number, while those above $X^\delta$ number at most $O(1/\delta)$, producing infinitely many almost-prime values.
