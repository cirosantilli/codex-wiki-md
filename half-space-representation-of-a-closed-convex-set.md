# Half-space representation of a closed convex set

↑ **Parent:** [Convex set](convex-set.md)

A closed [convex set](convex-set.md) in a finite-dimensional Euclidean space is the intersection of all containing [closed half-spaces](closed-half-space.md). For a point outside a nonempty set, its [Euclidean projection onto a convex set](euclidean-projection-onto-a-convex-set.md) gives a separating normal. Applied to an [epigraph](epigraph.md), this turns geometric separation into affine lower bounds and is a key step in the [Fenchel-Moreau theorem](fenchel-moreau-theorem.md).
