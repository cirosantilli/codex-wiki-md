# Coherent sheaf

↑ **Parent:** [Quasi-coherent sheaf](quasi-coherent-sheaf.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coherent_sheaf)

On a locally Noetherian scheme, a coherent sheaf is a [quasi-coherent sheaf](quasi-coherent-sheaf.md) that is locally associated with a finitely generated module. Kernels, cokernels, and finite direct sums of coherent sheaves remain coherent.
