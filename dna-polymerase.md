# DNA polymerase

↑ **Parent:** [DNA replication](dna-replication.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DNA_polymerase)

A replicating [DNA polymerase](dna-polymerase.md) is an [enzyme](enzyme-split.md) that extends a paired strand end with [nucleotides](nucleotide.md) selected against a [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) template. New [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) grows $5'\to3'$ while the template is read $3'\to5'$. [Base pairing](base-pairing.md) specifies the partner; the [DNA polymerase](dna-polymerase.md) catalyzes its incorporation. See [Alberts et al.: DNA polymerase](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK26850/).
