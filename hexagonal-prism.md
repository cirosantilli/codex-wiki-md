# Hexagonal prism

↑ **Parent:** [Prism (geometry)](prism-geometry.md)

A prism with two hexagonal faces and six quadrilateral side faces. Its twelve vertices and eighteen edges provide an alternating [aluminium](aluminium.md)–[oxygen](oxygen.md) cage model when the graph is coloured bipartitely.
