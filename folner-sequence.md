<h1 id="folner-sequence">Følner sequence</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Følner condition](folner-condition.md)

A Følner sequence is a sequence of nonempty finite subsets $F_n\subseteq G$ such that $|gF_n\mathbin\triangle F_n|/|F_n|\to0$ for every $g\in G$.
