# Plane through a point and a line given by a cross product

↑ **Parent:** [Plane](plane.md)

Suppose $\mathbf a\ne\mathbf0$ and $\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b=0$. The [line](straight-line.md) $\mathbf r\times\mathbf a=\mathbf b$ has [parametrization](parametric-equation.md) $\mathbf r=\mathbf r_0+t\mathbf a$, where $\mathbf r_0=(\mathbf a\times\mathbf b)/|\mathbf a|^2$. For a [point](point-geometry.md) $\mathbf y$ outside the [line](straight-line.md), a [normal vector](normal-vector.md) of the unique [plane](plane.md) containing both is $\mathbf N=\mathbf a\times(\mathbf y-\mathbf r_0)=\mathbf b+\mathbf a\times\mathbf y$. Thus its equation is the one in the title. The [vector triple product](vector-triple-product.md) verifies $\mathbf r_0\times\mathbf a=\mathbf b$, and cyclic invariance of the [scalar triple product](scalar-triple-product.md) verifies that every [point](point-geometry.md) of the [line](straight-line.md) satisfies the equation. The excluded case $\mathbf N=\mathbf0$ is exactly $\mathbf y$ lying on the [line](straight-line.md), when the [plane](plane.md) is not unique.
