# Extension cocycle

↑ **Parent:** [Second group cohomology classifies group extensions](second-group-cohomology-classifies-group-extensions.md)

An extension cocycle is the [two-cocycle](two-cocycle.md) obtained from a section of a [group extension](group-extension.md). It measures the failure of the section to be a [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md).
