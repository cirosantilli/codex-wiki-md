# Characteristic ideal

↑ **Parent:** [Iwasawa module structure theorem](iwasawa-module-structure-theorem.md)

For a finitely generated torsion [Iwasawa module](iwasawa-module.md), its characteristic ideal is the product of the height-one prime ideals raised to the lengths of the corresponding localizations. In the elementary-divisor description it is generated by $p^{\sum a_i}\prod f_j^{b_j}$. It is unchanged by finite modules and multiplicative in [short exact sequences](short-exact-sequence.md) of torsion modules.
