# Parallel D-brane no-force identity

↑ **Parent:** [D-brane](d-brane.md)

Identical, parallel, equally oriented type-II [D-branes](d-brane.md) preserve the same sixteen spacetime [supercharges](supersymmetry-generator.md). Their annulus oscillator sum has the displayed numerator, which vanishes by the [Jacobi abstruse identity](jacobi-abstruse-identity.md). In the closed-string channel this is cancellation of attractive NS-NS exchange and repulsive RR exchange. The force vanishes in the flat supersymmetric configuration; anti-oriented branes, relative angles or supersymmetry-breaking backgrounds need not have this cancellation.
