# Unilateral beam contact

↑ **Parent:** [Contact mechanics](contact-mechanics.md)

An [elastic beam](elastic-beam.md) above a smooth obstacle cannot penetrate it, and a nonadhesive obstacle can only push. The displayed constraints express this when $R$ is an upward distributed [normal contact force](normal-contact-force.md); concentrated [point forces](point-force.md) must also be nonnegative. In the small-[slope](slope.md) [Euler-Bernoulli beam equation](euler-bernoulli-beam-equation.md),

$$
Bw''''-Tw''+p=R.
$$

Integrating across a contact point with no applied couple gives [jump conditions](jump-condition.md) $[w]=[w']=[Bw'']=0$ and $[Bw''']=R_i$, provided the [tension](tension.md) is continuous. For a flat obstacle, no distributed load and zero [tension](tension.md), an open contact interval has $R=0$; its entire [normal contact force](normal-contact-force.md) can therefore be concentrated at its endpoints.
