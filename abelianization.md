# Abelianization

↑ **Parent:** [Commutator subgroup](commutator-subgroup.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abelianization)

The abelianization of a group is the quotient $G^{\mathrm{ab}}=G/G'$ by its [commutator subgroup](commutator-subgroup.md). It is the largest abelian quotient of $G$.
