<h1 id="mantel-haenszel-pooled-odds-ratio">Mantel–Haenszel pooled odds ratio</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Fixed-effect meta-analysis](fixed-effect-meta-analysis.md)

For independent two-by-two tables with counts $a_i,b_i,c_i,d_i$ and total $n_i$, the Mantel–Haenszel pooled [odds ratio](odds-ratio.md) is the displayed ratio of sums. When all $b_ic_i>0$, it is also the weighted arithmetic mean of study odds ratios with weights $b_ic_i/n_i$. These differ from inverse estimated [variance](variance-split.md) weights for [log odds ratios](log-odds-ratio.md). Both constructions can represent a [fixed-effect meta-analysis](fixed-effect-meta-analysis.md), but they should not be silently interchanged when interpreting a supplied plot.
