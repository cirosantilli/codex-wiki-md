# Resistive evolution of the magnetic second-moment tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic second-moment tensor](magnetic-second-moment-tensor.md)

For constant [magnetic diffusivity](magnetic-diffusivity.md), apply the product rule to $M_{ij}=B_iB_j$ and the incompressible [resistive induction equation](resistive-induction-equation.md). The identity $\nabla^2(B_iB_j)=B_i\nabla^2B_j+B_j\nabla^2B_i+2\partial_nB_i\partial_nB_j$ proves the displayed formula. Omitting the gradient-product term gives the wrong tensor evolution. Its average generally requires information beyond the averaged [magnetic second-moment tensor](magnetic-second-moment-tensor.md).
