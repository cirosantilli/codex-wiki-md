# Surface acoustic impedance

↑ **Parent:** [Normal acoustic impedance](normal-acoustic-impedance.md)

At an acoustic boundary, the [surface acoustic impedance](surface-acoustic-impedance.md) is the [pressure](pressure.md) amplitude divided by the [velocity](velocity.md) amplitude directed into that boundary. With upward surface [velocity](velocity.md) $-V$ and harmonic convention $e^{i\omega t}$, it is $Z=P/V$. Reflection compares this impedance with the incident fluid's [normal acoustic impedance](normal-acoustic-impedance.md). The [velocity](velocity.md) orientation and harmonic convention must be fixed before interpreting signs of inertial, elastic, and radiation terms.
