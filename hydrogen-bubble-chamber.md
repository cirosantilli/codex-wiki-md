# Hydrogen bubble chamber

↑ **Parent:** [Bubble chamber](bubble-chamber.md)

A [bubble chamber](bubble-chamber.md) using [liquid](liquid.md) [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) as both target and recording medium. For a beam interaction on a [proton](proton.md), the target's initial [momentum](momentum-split.md) can usually be approximated as zero on the scale of the beam [momentum](momentum-split.md). This makes [four-momentum conservation](four-momentum-conservation.md) especially useful: the initial state does not include the unknown motion of a [proton](proton.md) bound inside a heavier [atomic nucleus](atomic-nucleus-split.md). The approximation still neglects molecular binding and thermal motion.
