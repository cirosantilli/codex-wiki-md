# Quadratic Zeeman shift of a clock transition

↑ **Parent:** [Clock transition](clock-transition.md)

For the two $m=0$ branches of the [Breit-Rabi formula](breit-rabi-formula.md), their splitting is $\sqrt{\Delta_{\rm hf}^2+[(g_J-g_I)\mu_BB]^2}$. Expanding at small field gives the quadratic [frequency](frequency.md) shift displayed above. Vanishing linear [frequency](frequency.md) sensitivity at zero field therefore does not eliminate field-gradient [dephasing](dephasing-channel.md) at a finite bias field.
