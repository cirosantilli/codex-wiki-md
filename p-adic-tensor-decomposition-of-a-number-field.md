# p-adic tensor decomposition of a number field

↑ **Parent:** [Completion of a number field at a prime ideal](completion-of-a-number-field-at-a-prime-ideal.md)

For a [number field](number-field.md) $K$ and a rational prime $p$, the natural diagonal map gives an isomorphism of $\mathbb Q_p$-algebras

$$
K\otimes_{\mathbb Q}\mathbb Q_p\cong\prod_{\mathfrak p\mid p}K_{\mathfrak p}.
$$
