# Constant mass flux in a collisional cascade

↑ **Parent:** [Collisional cascade](collisional-cascade.md)

In a steady [collisional cascade](collisional-cascade.md) supplied at large sizes and depleted only at small sizes, [mass conservation](mass-conservation.md) makes the downward mass flux across every intermediate size threshold equal. With self-similar fragmentation and comparable transfer between logarithmic bins, the mass removed per bin per unit time is consequently independent of size. Nonlocal or size-dependent fragmentation, additional sources, and proximity to the cutoffs can invalidate the simplified bin-by-bin relation.
