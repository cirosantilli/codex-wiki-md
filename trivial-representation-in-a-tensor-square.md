# Trivial representation in a tensor square

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor product of group representations](tensor-product-of-group-representations.md)

For a finite-dimensional irreducible complex representation $V$, invariant tensors in $V\otimes V$ correspond to equivariant maps $V^*\to V$. [Schur's lemma](schur-s-lemma.md) makes their dimension one if $V$ is self-dual and zero otherwise. In particular a real-character irreducible representation has precisely one trivial copy in its tensor square. For arbitrary complex irreducibles, the unconditional statement is that $V\otimes V^*$ contains the trivial representation once.
