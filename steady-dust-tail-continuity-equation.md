# Steady dust-tail continuity equation

↑ **Parent:** [Dust tail](dust-tail.md)

For a single-stream [dust tail](dust-tail.md) with injection rate $\dot N$, monotone angular distance $s(t)$, and survival probability $P(t)$, the number per unit angle is $n(s)=\dot N P(t(s))/\dot s$. A vanishing angular drift creates an idealized density enhancement; velocity dispersion rounds it.
