# Normalized velocity-reset projection

↑ **Parent:** [Normalized velocity-reset collision operator](normalized-velocity-reset-collision-operator.md)

For a nonnegative normalized velocity density $M$, $Pg(x,v)=M(v)\int g(x,w)dw$ is a positive [norm](norm.md)-one projection on phase-space $L^1$. Normalization gives $P^2=P$. Its range consists of fields $a(x)M(v)$. This Banach-space projection need not be an [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md) in unweighted $L^2$; no such assertion is needed for the $L^1$ collision estimates.
