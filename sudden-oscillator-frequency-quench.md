# Sudden oscillator frequency quench

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md)

A jump from [positive frequency](positive-frequency-solution.md) $\omega$ to $\Omega$ keeps an oscillator mode and its first derivative continuous. A normalized positive-frequency incident solution has outgoing coefficients $\alpha=(\Omega+\omega)/(2\sqrt{\Omega\omega})$ and $\beta=(\Omega-\omega)/(2\sqrt{\Omega\omega})$, satisfying $\alpha^2-\beta^2=1$. This temporal matching is not a sum-one spatial-scattering [probability](probability.md) rule.
