# Stopping-time shift of a stochastic integral

↑ **Parent:** [Stochastic integral](stochastic-integral.md)

For a finite [stopping time](stopping-time.md) $T$, use the shifted filtration $\mathcal G_s=\mathcal F_{T+s}$. An [martingale](martingale-split.md) with a bounded $L^2$ norm shifts to the [martingale](martingale-split.md) $M_{T+s}-M_T$; a continuous bounded adapted integrand shifts to a predictable integrand. Their [quadratic variation](quadratic-variation.md) and integration intervals shift by subtracting the value at $T$. Both integrals have identical left-endpoint sums, and the [Itô isometry](ito-isometry.md) passes the equality to the limit. The starting jump at $T$ is excluded.
