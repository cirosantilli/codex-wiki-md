# Boundary obstruction for commuting volume-preserving vector fields

↑ **Parent:** [Volume-preserving vector field](volume-preserving-vector-field.md)

Suppose a nonempty compact $(n+1)$-manifold with a [volume form](volume-form.md) has $n$ pointwise independent [volume-preserving vector fields](volume-preserving-vector-field.md) that commute and are tangent to its boundary. If restriction $H^1_{\rm dR}(M)\to H^1_{\rm dR}(\partial M)$ is injective, the boundary must have at least two connected components.

Indeed $\eta=\iota_{X_1}\cdots\iota_{X_n}\omega$ is nowhere zero. [Cartan's magic formula](cartan-s-magic-formula.md) and commutation show $d\eta=0$, while boundary tangency gives $j^*\eta=0$. Injectivity in [de Rham cohomology](de-rham-cohomology.md) makes $\eta=df$. On a connected boundary $f$ is constant; a maximum or minimum differing from that boundary value would be an interior [critical point](critical-point.md), contradicting $df\ne0$. Empty boundary is also impossible.
