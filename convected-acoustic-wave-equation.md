# Convected acoustic wave equation

↑ **Parent:** [Linear acoustics](linear-acoustics-split.md)

For a uniform background [velocity](velocity.md) $U_0$ and [speed of sound](speed-of-sound.md) $c_0$, linearized [conservation of mass](mass-conservation.md), the [Euler equation](euler-equations-for-an-inviscid-fluid-split.md) and the local [equation of state](equation-of-state.md) $p_1=c_0^2\rho_1$ give this [wave equation](wave-equation-split.md). In the coordinate $x-U_0t$ it is the ordinary [one-dimensional wave equation](one-dimensional-wave-equation.md). Its two [characteristic curves](characteristic-curve.md) travel at laboratory [velocities](velocity.md) $U_0+c_0$ and $U_0-c_0$; both point downstream in [supersonic flow](supersonic-flow.md).
