<h1 id="isothermal-transonic-accretion-in-the-paczynski-wiita-potential">Isothermal transonic accretion in the Paczyński-Wiita potential</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Paczyński-Wiita potential](paczynski-wiita-potential.md)

For $\beta=16c_s^2/c^2$, the exterior [sonic point](sonic-point.md) of the [barotropic spherical accretion equation](barotropic-spherical-accretion-equation.md) lies outside $r_G=2GM/c^2$. Its [mass density](density.md) is $\rho_c=\rho_\infty\exp[4/(1+\sqrt{1+\beta})-1/2]$. The selected smooth transonic accretion rate is $4\pi r_c^2\rho_cc_s$. Subsonic formal solutions are not assigned this rate without transonic selection.
