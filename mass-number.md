# Mass number

↑ **Parent:** [Isotope](isotope.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mass_number)

The [mass number](mass-number.md) $A$ is the total number of [nucleons](nucleon.md), namely [protons](proton.md) plus [neutrons](neutron.md), in an [atomic nucleus](atomic-nucleus.md):

$$
A=Z+N,\qquad N=A-Z.
$$

It is an exact positive [integer](integer.md) with no units, rather than a measured [mass](mass.md). [Carbon-12](carbon-12.md) has $A=12$ because its [atomic nucleus](atomic-nucleus.md) contains six [protons](proton.md) and six [neutrons](neutron.md). No improvement in a [mass](mass.md) measurement can turn that count into $11.999$ or $12.001$.[mass number](iupac-compendium-of-chemical-terminology-gold-book-by-international-union-of-pure-and-applied-chemistry/mass-number.md)

The left superscript in ${}^{22}_{10}\mathrm{Ne}$ denotes [mass number](mass-number.md) $22$, not an [atomic mass](atomic-mass.md) of exactly $22\,\mathrm u$. For [neon-22](neon-22.md), the displayed reference [atomic mass](atomic-mass.md) is about $21.991385114\,\mathrm u$. Even a [weighted mean](weighted-arithmetic-mean.md) of [mass numbers](mass-number.md), such as $20.1877$ for the illustrative [neon](neon.md) population below, is a population average, not the [mass number](mass-number.md) of an individual [atomic nucleus](atomic-nucleus.md).[Atomic Weights and Isotopic Compositions for Neon](atomic-weights-and-isotopic-compositions-for-neon.md)
