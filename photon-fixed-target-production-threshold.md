# Photon fixed-target production threshold

↑ **Parent:** [Fixed-target production threshold](fixed-target-production-threshold.md)

For a [photon](photon.md) of energy $h\nu$ hitting a stationary target of mass $m_2$, the incoming invariant energy satisfies $W^2=m_2^2c^4+2m_2c^2h\nu$. The massive multi-product threshold is the displayed value when $\sum_i\mu_i>m_2$. If the algebraic bound is nonpositive, the lower limit of allowed positive frequencies can be zero. A single final product instead fixes the frequency by invariant-mass equality, and a final massless particle can make a nominal threshold an infimum rather than an attained value.
