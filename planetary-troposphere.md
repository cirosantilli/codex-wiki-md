# Planetary troposphere

↑ **Parent:** [Atmospheric pressure-temperature profile](atmospheric-pressure-temperature-profile.md)

The lower part of a planetary atmosphere where temperature generally decreases outward and [convection](convection.md) can carry intrinsic or surface heat. Its upper boundary is a [tropopause](tropopause.md). Stellar irradiation, composition gradients and clouds affect the extent of this region; pressure alone does not universally identify it.
