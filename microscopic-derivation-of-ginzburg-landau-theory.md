# Microscopic derivation of Ginzburg-Landau theory

↑ **Parent:** [Ginzburg-Landau theory of superconductivity](ginzburg-landau-theory-of-superconductivity.md)

Expanding the BCS theory near the transition in small pair amplitude and slow spatial gradients produces a local complex order-parameter free energy. The microscopic theory fixes its normalization and coefficients, including the pair's twice-electron charge. This establishes a controlled near-transition relation rather than an exact all-temperature replacement for BCS theory.[1959 abstract and near-transition reduction](microscopic-derivation-of-the-ginzburg-landau-equations-in-the-theory-of-superconductivity-by-lev-gor-kov.md)
