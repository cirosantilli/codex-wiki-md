# Bohr-set almost periodicity of a convolution

↑ **Parent:** [Almost period of a function](almost-period-of-a-function.md)

Let $A\subseteq\mathbb Z_n$ have [density of a finite subset](density-of-a-finite-subset.md) $\alpha$ and let $f=\mathbf1_A*\mathbf1_A$ use [normalized convolution on a finite group](normalized-convolution-on-a-finite-group.md). If $K=\{r:|\widehat{\mathbf1_A}(r)|\geq\theta\}$ and $u\in B(K;\varepsilon)$, then the displayed estimate holds for normalized physical-space [L2 norm](l2-norm.md) and $T_uf(x)=f(x-u)$. The [Bohr set](bohr-set.md) controls the translation factors on the [large spectrum](large-spectrum.md); the [fourth Fourier moment bound for an indicator function](fourth-fourier-moment-bound-for-an-indicator-function.md) controls their total weight. Outside $K$, use $|1-e^{2\pi iru/n}|\leq2$ and [Parseval identity on a finite group](parseval-identity-on-a-finite-group.md).
