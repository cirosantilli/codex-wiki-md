# Muon storage-ring time-dilation experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Time dilation](time-dilation.md)

Accelerator-produced [muons](muon.md) circulating in a magnetic storage ring allow a direct comparison between their measured laboratory [mean lifetime](mean-lifetime-physics.md) and $\gamma$ times the rest lifetime. The 1977 experiment at $\gamma=29.33$ measured positive- and negative-muon lifetimes near $64.4\,\mathrm{\mu s}$, consistent with [time dilation](time-dilation.md). This is a controlled complement to atmospheric survival measurements, with different systematic effects.[1977 abstract, Lorentz factor, lifetimes and comparison with the lifetime at rest](measurements-of-relativistic-time-dilatation-for-positive-and-negative-muons-in-a-circular-orbit-by-j-bailey-and-others.md)
