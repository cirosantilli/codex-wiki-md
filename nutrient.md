# Nutrient

↑ **Parent:** [Biology](biology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nutrient)

A [nutrient](nutrient.md) supplies material or energy used by an organism. In [mathematical biology](mathematical-biology-split.md), a [nutrient](nutrient.md) [concentration](concentration.md) can be transported, produced and consumed, and can influence [population dynamics](population-dynamics.md) or act as a [chemoattractant](chemoattractant.md).
