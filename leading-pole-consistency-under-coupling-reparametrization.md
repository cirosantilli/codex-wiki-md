# Leading-pole consistency under coupling reparametrization

↑ **Parent:** [Simple-pole formulas for minimal-subtraction renormalization group functions](simple-pole-formulas-for-minimal-subtraction-renormalization-group-functions.md)

With $\beta_0=-\epsilon g_0$, the [chain rule](chain-rule.md) gives $\beta(g)=\beta_0\,dg/dg_0$. Inverting the displayed [coupling](coupling.md) expansion yields $g_0=g-a_1g^2/\epsilon+(2a_1^2-b_1)g^3/\epsilon^2$. Substitution gives $\beta=-\epsilon g-a_1g^2+2(a_1^2-b_1)g^3/\epsilon+O(g^4)$, so finiteness requires the displayed double-pole condition. An omitted simple-pole term $c_1g_0^3/\epsilon$ supplies a finite $-2c_1g^3$ term; higher-pole consistency does not by itself determine the finite two-loop [beta function](beta-function.md).
