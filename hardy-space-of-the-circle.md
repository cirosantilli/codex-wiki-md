# Hardy space of the circle

↑ **Parent:** [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md)

The Hardy space of the circle is the closed subspace of [L2 space](l2-space-is-a-hilbert-space.md) whose negative [Fourier coefficients](fourier-coefficient.md) vanish. The [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md) onto it is denoted $P_+$. A continuous symbol $f$ defines a [Toeplitz operator](toeplitz-operator.md) $T_f=P_+M_f|_{H^2}$. For continuous $f,g$, $T_fT_g-T_{fg}$ is compact: it is finite rank for Laurent polynomials, and uniform approximation extends the result.
