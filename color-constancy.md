# Color constancy

↑ **Parent:** [Color vision](color-vision.md)

Relative stability of perceived surface [color](color.md) across changes in illumination or viewing conditions. The measured degree depends on task, adaptation and available cues. Perfect physical recovery is impossible in unrestricted scenes because illumination and [reflectance](reflectance.md) can be nonunique.
