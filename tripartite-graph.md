# Tripartite graph

↑ **Parent:** [Graph](graph-split.md)

A tripartite [graph](graph-split.md) has a [set partition](set-partition.md) of its [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) into three classes, with every [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) joining different classes. Each pair of classes induces a [bipartite graph](bipartite-graph.md), and every [triangle in a graph](triangle-in-a-graph.md) uses one [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) from each class.
