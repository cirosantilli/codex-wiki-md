# Conditional selection bias after futility continuation

↑ **Parent:** [Futility boundary](futility-boundary.md)

In a two-stage normal trial, conditioning on a first-stage [sample mean](sample-mean.md) exceeding $c$ raises that mean. With stage standard error $s$, mean parameter $\delta$, and combined mean $S$, the [estimator bias](bias-of-an-estimator.md) after continuation is $E(S\mid X\ge c)-\delta=(s/2)\lambda((c-\delta)/s)$, where $\lambda$ is the upper-tail [Inverse Mills ratio](inverse-mills-ratio.md). It is positive and tends to zero as continuation becomes virtually certain.
