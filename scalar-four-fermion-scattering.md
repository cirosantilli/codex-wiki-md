# Scalar four-fermion scattering

↑ **Parent:** [Four-fermion interaction](four-fermion-interaction.md)

For $\mathcal L_{\rm int}=g^2(\bar\psi\psi)^2/2$ with one species of [Dirac field](dirac-field.md), the [tree-level Feynman diagrams](tree-level-feynman-diagram.md) for $f(p_1)\bar f(p_2)\to f(p_3)\bar f(p_4)$ give

$$
\mathcal M=g^2\big[(\bar u_3u_1)(\bar v_2v_4)-(\bar v_2u_1)(\bar u_3v_4)\big].
$$

The [fermionic sign](fermionic-sign.md) is the antisymmetry of the two external contractions. The initial [spin average](spin-average.md) is one quarter of the [spin sum](spin-sum.md). The two diagonal contributions are $g^4(t-4m^2)^2$ and $g^4(s-4m^2)^2$; the interference [Dirac trace](gamma-matrix-trace.md) is $R=(s-4m^2)^2+(t-4m^2)^2-(u-4m^2)^2$, giving the displayed result after subtracting $g^4R/2$. Here the [Mandelstam variables](mandelstam-variables.md) satisfy $s+t+u=4m^2$. At threshold this gives $16g^4m^4$; for zero [mass](mass.md) it gives $g^4(s^2+t^2+u^2)/2$. The formula describes the leading [scattering amplitude](scattering-amplitude.md) of an effective interaction and is not a statement of perturbative [renormalizability](renormalizable-quantum-field-theory.md).
