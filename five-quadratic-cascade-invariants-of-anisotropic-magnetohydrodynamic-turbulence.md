# Five quadratic cascade invariants of anisotropic magnetohydrodynamic turbulence

↑ **Parent:** [Reduced magnetohydrodynamics](reduced-magnetohydrodynamics.md)

For ideal [reduced magnetohydrodynamics](reduced-magnetohydrodynamics.md) with periodic or vanishing-flux boundaries, the five quadratic cascade channels are $I_A^\pm=\int|\mathbf z_\perp^\pm|^2dV$, $I_S^\pm=\int(f^\pm)^2dV$ and $I_e=\int s^2dV$. The perpendicular [Elsässer variables](elsasser-variable.md), slow-wave amplitudes $f^\pm$, and [entropy](entropy.md) fluctuation $s$ each obey an equation with [divergence-free](solenoidal-vector-field.md) advection. Multiplying each equation by its own field and integrating makes both advection and propagation terms boundary fluxes, while the perpendicular [pressure](pressure.md) term vanishes by solenoidality. Each integral is therefore separately conserved. The two Alfvén cascades distort one another without exchanging their integrated invariants; the other three are passively cascaded by the Alfvénic motion without feedback at this order. This count concerns quadratic fluctuation-energy channels: an advected scalar also conserves arbitrary integrals $\int F(s)dV$, which are not extra independent quadratic modal channels.
