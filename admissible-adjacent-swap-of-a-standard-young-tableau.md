# Admissible adjacent swap of a standard Young tableau

↑ **Parent:** [Standard Young tableau](standard-young-tableau.md)

Swapping consecutive labels $i,i+1$ preserves a [standard Young tableau](standard-young-tableau.md) exactly when their cells are incomparable in the row-and-column order. Their contents then differ by more than one in absolute value. These admissible swaps connect all standard tableaux of any fixed shape, as adjacent swaps connect [linear extensions of a partially ordered set](linear-extension.md).
