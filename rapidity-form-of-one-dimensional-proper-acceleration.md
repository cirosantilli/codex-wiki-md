# Rapidity form of one-dimensional proper acceleration

↑ **Parent:** [Proper acceleration for one-dimensional motion](proper-acceleration-for-one-dimensional-motion.md)

For rectilinear motion write the [four-velocity](four-velocity.md) in an [inertial frame](inertial-frame.md) as $U=(c\cosh\eta,c\sinh\eta)$, where $\eta$ is the [rapidity](rapidity.md) and $\tau$ is [proper time](proper-time.md). [Differentiation](differentiation.md) gives $A=c\dot\eta(\sinh\eta,\cosh\eta)$, with invariant $A\cdot A=-c^2\dot\eta^2$ in the $+-$ [metric signature](metric-signature.md). The signed [proper acceleration](proper-acceleration.md) is therefore $a=c\dot\eta$. Constant signed acceleration gives $\eta=\eta_0+a\tau/c$; specifying initial position alone leaves the initial rapidity $\eta_0$ undetermined.
