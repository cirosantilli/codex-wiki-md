# Stochastic gradient second-moment identity

↑ **Parent:** [Unbiased stochastic gradient](unbiased-stochastic-gradient.md)

For an [unbiased stochastic gradient](unbiased-stochastic-gradient.md) $H$ at $X$ with finite conditional second moment, expand $H=\nabla f(X)+(H-\nabla f(X))$ in the squared [Euclidean norm](euclidean-norm.md). The cross term has zero [conditional expectation](conditional-expectation.md), which proves the displayed identity. It separates the squared true [gradient](gradient.md) from the total conditional [variance](variance-split.md) of its random estimate.
