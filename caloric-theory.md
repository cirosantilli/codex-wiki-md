# Caloric theory

↑ **Parent:** [Thermodynamics](thermodynamics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caloric_theory)

The historical caloric theory treated [heat](heat-split.md) as a conserved substance. The modern [first law of thermodynamics](first-law-of-thermodynamics-split.md) conserves [energy](energy-split.md), allowing net [heat](heat-split.md) input to become [work](work.md) in a [heat engine](heat-engine.md).
