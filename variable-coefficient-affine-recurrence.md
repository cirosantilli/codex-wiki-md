# Variable-coefficient affine recurrence

↑ **Parent:** [Linear recurrence relation](linear-recurrence-relation.md)

A variable-coefficient affine recurrence is a [linear recurrence relation](linear-recurrence-relation.md) with coefficients allowed to depend on the index. If every $a_n$ is nonzero, a [discrete integrating factor](discrete-integrating-factor.md) reduces it to a [telescoping series](telescoping-series.md).
