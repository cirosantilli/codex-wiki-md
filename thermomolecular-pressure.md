# Thermomolecular pressure

↑ **Parent:** [Premelting](premelting.md)

Thermomolecular pressure is the [temperature](temperature.md)-dependent force per area transmitted through a thin liquid film between a solid and a substrate. For a repulsive ice-film interaction $p_T=A/(6\pi a^3)$, it can drive water through a porous material and support a load. Its thermodynamic relation to undercooling is the [Clapeyron pressure relation for equal-density phases](clapeyron-pressure-relation-for-equal-density-phases.md).
