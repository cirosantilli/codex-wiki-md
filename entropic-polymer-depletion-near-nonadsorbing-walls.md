# Entropic polymer depletion near nonadsorbing walls

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer](polymer-split.md)

A [polymer](polymer-split.md) near an impenetrable nonadsorbing wall has fewer allowed configurations than a coil in the bulk. This entropic loss suppresses its equilibrium [density](density.md) over a scale comparable with its [radius of gyration](radius-of-gyration.md). Strong confinement, adsorption and strong flow can change the profile; electrical neutrality does not remove configurational [entropy](entropy-split.md).
