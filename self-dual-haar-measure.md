# Self-dual Haar measure

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier transform over a local field](fourier-transform-over-a-local-field.md)

Relative to a nontrivial [additive character](additive-character.md), the self-dual additive [Haar measure](haar-measure.md) makes the twice-applied [Fourier transform over a local field](fourier-transform-over-a-local-field.md) equal to reflection. If the [annihilator of the valuation ring](annihilator-of-the-valuation-ring.md) is $\pi^c\mathcal O_F$ and its [residue field](residue-field.md) has $q$ elements, the normalization is $\operatorname{vol}(\mathcal O_F)=q^{c/2}$. Volumes of a compact open subgroup and its [character annihilator](character-annihilator.md) then multiply to one.
