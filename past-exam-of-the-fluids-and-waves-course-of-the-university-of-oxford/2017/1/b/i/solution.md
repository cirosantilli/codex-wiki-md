<h1 id="1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Work away from the [line source](../../../../../line-source.md), so $r>0$. In [polar coordinates](../../../../../polar-coordinates.md), $u_r=Q/(2\pi r)$ and $u_\theta=0$. The supplied [divergence](../../../../../divergence.md) and [curl](../../../../../curl.md) formulas give

$$
\nabla\cdot\mathbf u=\frac1r\partial_r\left(\frac Q{2\pi}\right)=0,
\qquad
(\nabla\times\mathbf u)_z=\frac1r\left[\partial_r(ru_\theta)-\partial_\theta u_r\right]=0.
$$

Hence **the field defines an [incompressible flow](../../../../../incompressible-flow.md) and an [irrotational flow](../../../../../irrotational-flow.md) for $r>0$**. The restriction matters: the outward [volume flux](../../../../../volumetric-flow-rate.md) through any enclosing [circle](../../../../../circle.md) is

$$
\int_0^{2\pi}\frac{Q}{2\pi r}r\,d\theta=Q.
$$

The origin is the excluded injection point. Applying the [divergence theorem](../../../../../divergence-theorem.md) to a disk through that [singularity](../../../../../complex-singularity.md) would violate its smoothness hypothesis.
