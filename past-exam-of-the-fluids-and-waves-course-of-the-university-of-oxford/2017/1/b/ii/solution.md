<h1 id="1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A [velocity potential](../../../../../velocity-potential.md) satisfies $\mathbf u=\nabla\phi$. Integration of $\phi_r=Q/(2\pi r)$ and $r^{-1}\phi_\theta=0$ gives

$$
\boxed{\phi=\frac Q{2\pi}\log r.}
$$

Choose the [streamfunction](../../../../../stream-function.md) convention $u=\psi_y$, $v=-\psi_x$. Its [streamfunction in polar coordinates](../../../../../streamfunction-in-polar-coordinates.md) satisfies $u_r=r^{-1}\psi_\theta$ and $u_\theta=-\psi_r$, so

$$
\boxed{\psi=\frac Q{2\pi}\theta,\qquad w(z)=\phi+i\psi=\frac Q{2\pi}\log z.}
$$

Additive constants are immaterial. This [complex potential of a two-dimensional potential flow](../../../../../complex-potential-of-a-two-dimensional-potential-flow.md) is defined on a chosen [logarithm branch](../../../../../branch-of-the-complex-logarithm.md). Its [complex velocity](../../../../../complex-velocity.md) $w'=Q/(2\pi z)=u-iv$ is single-valued away from the origin. Continuing once around the [line source](../../../../../line-source.md) increases $\psi$ by $Q$, consistently with its nonzero [volume flux](../../../../../volumetric-flow-rate.md).
