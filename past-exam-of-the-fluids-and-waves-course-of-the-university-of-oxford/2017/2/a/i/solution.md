<h1 id="2/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Orient the planar [material curve](../../../../../material-curve.md) $C(t)$ counterclockwise, and let $\mathbf t$ and $\mathbf n$ be its unit tangent and outward [unit normal vector](../../../../../unit-normal.md). The [circulation](../../../../../circulation-physics.md) and outward [volume flux](../../../../../volumetric-flow-rate.md) are

$$
\boxed{\Gamma(t)=\oint_{C(t)}\mathbf u\cdot\mathbf t\,ds,\qquad M(t)=\oint_{C(t)}\mathbf u\cdot\mathbf n\,ds.}
$$

For $\mathbf u=(u,v)$ and this orientation, $\mathbf t\,ds=(dx,dy)$ and $\mathbf n\,ds=(dy,-dx)$, giving

$$
\Gamma=\oint_C(u\,dx+v\,dy),\qquad M=\oint_C(u\,dy-v\,dx).
$$

Here $M$ is the instantaneous [flux](../../../../../flux.md) of the [velocity field](../../../../../velocity-field.md), rather than transport relative to the moving [material curve](../../../../../material-curve.md); that relative transport is zero. If the enclosed region contains no source and the [velocity field](../../../../../velocity-field.md) is smooth there, [incompressibility](../../../../../incompressible-flow.md) and the planar [divergence theorem](../../../../../divergence-theorem.md) imply $M=0$.
