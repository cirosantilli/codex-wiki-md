<h1 id="2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Multiply the [complex velocity](../../../../../complex-velocity.md) by the complex displacement:

$$
\frac{dw}{dz}\,dz=(u-iv)(dx+i\,dy)=(u\,dx+v\,dy)+i(u\,dy-v\,dx).
$$

The real term is the [circulation](../../../../../circulation-physics.md) integrand. With a counterclockwise curve, $\mathbf n\,ds=(dy,-dx)$ is outward, so the imaginary term is the outward [volume flux](../../../../../volumetric-flow-rate.md) integrand. Integrating gives

$$
\boxed{\oint_{C(t)}\frac{dw}{dz}\,dz=\Gamma(t)+iM(t).}
$$

This also fixes the sign of $M$: a positive [line source](../../../../../line-source.md) has $w'=Q/(2\pi z)$ and the integral is $iQ$, so its outward [volume flux](../../../../../volumetric-flow-rate.md) is $Q$.
