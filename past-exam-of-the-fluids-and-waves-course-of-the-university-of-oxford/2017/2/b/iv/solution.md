<h1 id="2/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

For the same counterclockwise [line vortex](../../../../../line-vortex.md) strength $\kappa$, its local [velocity potential](../../../../../velocity-potential.md) and [streamfunction](../../../../../stream-function.md) are $\phi=\kappa\theta/(2\pi)$ and $\psi=-\kappa\log r/(2\pi)$. Consequently

$$
w(z)=-\frac{i\kappa}{2\pi}\log z,\qquad w'(z)=-\frac{i\kappa}{2\pi z},\qquad b_1=-\frac{i\kappa}{2\pi}.
$$

Substitution gives

$$
\boxed{2\pi i b_1=\kappa=\Gamma+iM,\qquad M=0,}
$$

in agreement with the direct [circulation](../../../../../circulation-physics.md) and [volume flux](../../../../../volumetric-flow-rate.md) calculation. The sign of the imaginary coefficient is essential for a positive counterclockwise [circulation](../../../../../circulation-physics.md).
