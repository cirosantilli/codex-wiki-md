<h1 id="2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For planar [incompressible flow](../../../../../incompressible-flow.md) and [irrotational flow](../../../../../irrotational-flow.md), combine a [velocity potential](../../../../../velocity-potential.md) and a [streamfunction](../../../../../stream-function.md) into the [complex potential of a two-dimensional potential flow](../../../../../complex-potential-of-a-two-dimensional-potential-flow.md):

$$
\boxed{w(z)=\phi(x,y)+i\psi(x,y),\qquad z=x+iy.}
$$

The chosen conventions give

$$
u=\phi_x=\psi_y,\qquad v=\phi_y=-\psi_x.
$$

These are the [Cauchy-Riemann equations](../../../../../cauchy-riemann-equations.md), so $w$ is a [holomorphic function](../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) wherever the flow is regular and a consistent local potential is chosen. Its [complex derivative](../../../../../complex-derivative.md) is the [complex velocity](../../../../../complex-velocity.md)

$$
\boxed{\frac{dw}{dz}=\phi_x+i\psi_x=u-iv.}
$$

A spatially [uniform flow](../../../../../uniform-flow.md) has $w=(U-iV)z+$ a constant. The following [contour integral](../../../../../contour-integral.md) identities also apply to nonuniform [potential flow](../../../../../potential-flow.md), including the specified [line vortex](../../../../../line-vortex.md). A [complex potential of a two-dimensional potential flow](../../../../../complex-potential-of-a-two-dimensional-potential-flow.md) may be multivalued around a source or vortex even though its derivative is single-valued.
