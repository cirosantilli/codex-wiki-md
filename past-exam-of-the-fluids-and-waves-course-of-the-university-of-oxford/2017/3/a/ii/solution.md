<h1 id="3/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A fixed [wave phase](../../../../../phase-waves.md) satisfies $kx-\omega t=$ a constant. Differentiating gives the [phase velocity](../../../../../phase-velocity.md), called the [wave speed](../../../../../wave-speed.md) here:

$$
\boxed{c=\frac\omega k.}
$$

It is the speed of an individual crest or other fixed-phase point. The [group velocity](../../../../../group-velocity.md) is

$$
\boxed{c_g=\frac{d\omega}{dk},}
$$

evaluated on the relevant [dispersion relation](../../../../../dispersion-relation-split.md). To see its meaning, superpose two equal-[amplitude](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) [waves](../../../../../wave-split.md) with nearby $(k,\omega)$:

$$
\cos(k_1x-\omega_1t)+\cos(k_2x-\omega_2t)
=2\cos\frac{\Delta k\,x-\Delta\omega\,t}{2}\cos(\bar kx-\bar\omega t).
$$

The slowly varying [wave envelope](../../../../../envelope-waves.md) moves with speed $\Delta\omega/\Delta k$, tending to $d\omega/dk$ as the two components approach one another. Thus [group velocity](../../../../../group-velocity.md) describes a narrow [wave packet](../../../../../wave-packet.md) and, for conservative linear [surface gravity waves](../../../../../surface-gravity-wave.md) in still water, their [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) transport. A single [time-harmonic wave](../../../../../time-harmonic-wave.md) has no distinguished packet envelope. When $c=c(k)$, the equivalent relation is $c_g=c+k\,dc/dk$.
