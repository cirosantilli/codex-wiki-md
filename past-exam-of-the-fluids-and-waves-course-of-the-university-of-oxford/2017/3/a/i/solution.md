<h1 id="3/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Represent a [time-harmonic wave](../../../../../time-harmonic-wave.md) by $A\cos(kx-\omega t+\delta)$, with $k,\omega>0$. The [angular frequency](../../../../../angular-frequency.md) $\omega$ is the rate at which the [wave phase](../../../../../phase-waves.md) changes at a fixed position, measured in radians per unit time. The [wavenumber](../../../../../wavenumber.md) $k$ is its spatial rate of change, measured in radians per unit length. One full spatial cycle defines the [wavelength](../../../../../wavelength.md) $\lambda$, and one temporal cycle defines the [wave period](../../../../../wave-period.md) $T$, so

$$
\boxed{k=\frac{2\pi}\lambda,\qquad\omega=\frac{2\pi}T.}
$$

The ordinary [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md) is $f=1/T=\omega/(2\pi)$; the paper's symbol $\omega$ denotes [angular frequency](../../../../../angular-frequency.md).
