↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [Joukowski map](../../../../../joukowski-map.md) is a [holomorphic function](../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) for $z\ne0$, with

$$
g'(z)=1-\frac1{z^2}.
$$

A [holomorphic function](../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) is a local [conformal map](../../../../../conformal-map.md) wherever its [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) is nonzero. Hence the two finite nonzero critical points are

$$
\boxed{z=1,\qquad z=-1}.
$$

They lie on the boundary of the fluid region. At $z=0$ the displayed formula has a [pole](../../../../../pole.md) and is undefined as a map to the finite [complex plane](../../../../../complex-plane.md); zero is outside the fluid region. Thus $g$ is locally [conformal](../../../../../conformal-map.md) everywhere in the fluid.
