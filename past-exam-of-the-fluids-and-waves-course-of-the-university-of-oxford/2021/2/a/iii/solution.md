<h1 id="2/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [quadratic formula](../../../../../quadratic-formula.md) gives two algebraic possibilities. To select one consistent [holomorphic square root](../../../../../holomorphic-square-root.md), set

$$
S(\zeta)=\sqrt{\zeta-2}\,\sqrt{\zeta+2},\qquad \operatorname{Im}\zeta>0,
$$

where each factor is the [principal square root](../../../../../principal-square-root-of-a-complex-number.md). Both arguments lie in the [upper half-plane](../../../../../upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md), so neither crosses its [branch cut](../../../../../branch-cut.md). This choice satisfies $S(\zeta)^2=\zeta^2-4$ and $S(\zeta)\sim\zeta$ at large $\zeta$ in the [upper half-plane](../../../../../upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md). The required [inverse function](../../../../../inverse-function.md) is

$$
\boxed{z=g^{-1}(\zeta)=\frac{\zeta+S(\zeta)}2
=\frac{\zeta+\sqrt{\zeta-2}\sqrt{\zeta+2}}2}.
$$

For $\zeta=iR$, $R>0$, this gives $z=i(R+\sqrt{R^2+4})/2$, whose modulus exceeds one. As $\zeta$ varies continuously in the [upper half-plane](../../../../../upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md), this root cannot cross $|z|=1$, since that would make $\zeta$ real. Hence the selected root always lies in the fluid region. The other root is its reciprocal and lies inside the unit [disk](../../../../../disk-mathematics.md). Simply writing the [principal square root](../../../../../principal-square-root-of-a-complex-number.md) of $\zeta^2-4$ would not specify this branch correctly throughout the [upper half-plane](../../../../../upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md).
