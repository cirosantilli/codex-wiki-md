<h1 id="2/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The physical [complex potential of a two-dimensional potential flow](../../../../../complex-potential-of-a-two-dimensional-potential-flow.md) is $w(z)=W(g(z))$. Applying the [chain rule](../../../../../chain-rule.md) gives the [complex velocity](../../../../../complex-velocity.md)

$$
\boxed{u-iv=w'(z)=\frac\Gamma{2\pi i}
\left(1-\frac1{z^2}\right)
\left[\frac1{z+z^{-1}-c-c^{-1}}
-\frac1{z+z^{-1}-\bar c-\bar c^{-1}}\right]}.
$$

The physical [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) is $(\operatorname{Re}w',-\operatorname{Im}w')$. A useful equivalent expression follows from

$$
g(z)-g(c)=\frac{(z-c)(z-c^{-1})}{z}.
$$

Factoring the image expression in the same way makes the $-1/z$ terms cancel, leaving

$$
\boxed{u-iv=\frac\Gamma{2\pi i}
\left[\frac1{z-c}+\frac1{z-c^{-1}}
-\frac1{z-\bar c}-\frac1{z-\bar c^{-1}}\right]}.
$$

This represents the physical [line vortex](../../../../../line-vortex.md) at $c$, an image [line vortex](../../../../../line-vortex.md) of the same strength at $c^{-1}$, and image [point vortices](../../../../../line-vortex.md) of opposite strength at $\bar c$ and $\bar c^{-1}$. All three image locations are outside the fluid region. Both boundary pieces inherit the constant [streamfunction](../../../../../stream-function.md) of the real axis in the mapped [complex plane](../../../../../complex-plane.md).
