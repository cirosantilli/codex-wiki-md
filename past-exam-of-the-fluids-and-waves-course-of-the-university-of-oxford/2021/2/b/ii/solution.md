<h1 id="2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Take positive $\Gamma$ to mean counterclockwise [circulation](../../../../../circulation-physics.md). A [line vortex](../../../../../line-vortex.md) at $\zeta_0$ has [complex potential of a two-dimensional potential flow](../../../../../complex-potential-of-a-two-dimensional-potential-flow.md) $\Gamma\log(\zeta-\zeta_0)/(2\pi i)$. The [method of images](../../../../../method-of-images.md) imposes the [no-penetration boundary condition](../../../../../no-penetration-boundary-condition.md) on the real axis by placing an image [line vortex](../../../../../line-vortex.md) of strength $-\Gamma$ at $\bar\zeta_0$. Thus

$$
\boxed{W(\zeta)=\frac{\Gamma}{2\pi i}
\log\!\left(\frac{\zeta-\zeta_0}{\zeta-\bar\zeta_0}\right)+C}.
$$

For real $\zeta$, the numerator and denominator have equal modulus, so the [streamfunction](../../../../../stream-function.md) is

$$
\operatorname{Im}W=-\frac\Gamma{2\pi}
\log\left|\frac{\zeta-\zeta_0}{\zeta-\bar\zeta_0}\right|
=\text{constant}.
$$

The real axis is therefore a [streamline](../../../../../streamline.md). The image [line vortex](../../../../../line-vortex.md) lies outside the mapped fluid region, while the [complex velocity](../../../../../complex-velocity.md)

$$
W'(\zeta)=\frac\Gamma{2\pi i}
\left(\frac1{\zeta-\zeta_0}-\frac1{\zeta-\bar\zeta_0}\right)
$$

decays as $|\zeta|\to\infty$. This selects the flow due to the [line vortex](../../../../../line-vortex.md) and the boundary, without superposing an imposed background [velocity field](../../../../../velocity-field.md).
