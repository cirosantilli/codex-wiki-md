↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [line vortex](../../../../../line-vortex.md) is mapped to

$$
\boxed{\zeta_0=g(c)=c+\frac1c}.
$$

Its [imaginary part](../../../../../imaginary-part.md) is positive because $c$ lies in the fluid region. A [conformal map](../../../../../conformal-map.md) preserves the [circulation](../../../../../circulation-physics.md) of the local [line vortex](../../../../../line-vortex.md), so its strength in the mapped [complex plane](../../../../../complex-plane.md) is still $\Gamma$.
