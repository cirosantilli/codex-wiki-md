<h1 id="1/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Since $b>1$, the denominator in

$$
F_x=\frac{2\pi\rho}{b^2(b^2-1)}\,[b-U(b^2-1)]
$$

is positive. The positive root of $Ub^2-b-U=0$ is

$$
\boxed{b_* =\frac{1+\sqrt{1+4U^2}}{2U}>1}.
$$

The other root is negative, so the horizontal [force](../../../../../force-split.md) points in the positive $x$ direction for $1<b<b_*$, vanishes at $b=b_*$, and points in the negative $x$ direction for $b>b_*$. In particular, the nearby [line source](../../../../../line-source.md) pulls the [cylinder](../../../../../cylinder-geometry.md) towards itself as $b\downarrow1$, while at large $b$ the horizontal [force](../../../../../force-split.md) is $F_x\sim-2\pi\rho U/b^2$.
