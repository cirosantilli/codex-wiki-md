<h1 id="1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use $w=\phi+i\psi$ for the [complex potential of a two-dimensional potential flow](../../../../../complex-potential-of-a-two-dimensional-potential-flow.md), so its [complex velocity](../../../../../complex-velocity.md) is $q=w'=u-iv$. The incident [uniform flow](../../../../../uniform-flow.md) and [line source](../../../../../line-source.md) have

$$
f(z)=Uz+\log(z-b).
$$

The coefficient of the [complex logarithm](../../../../../complex-logarithm.md) is one because a [line source](../../../../../line-source.md) of strength $Q$ has [complex potential of a two-dimensional potential flow](../../../../../complex-potential-of-a-two-dimensional-potential-flow.md) $(Q/(2\pi))\log(z-b)$, and here $Q=2\pi$. Applying the [Milne-Thomson circle theorem](../../../../../milne-thomson-circle-theorem.md) to the unit [circle](../../../../../circle.md) gives

$$
w(z)=f(z)+\overline{f(1/\bar z)}
=Uz+\log(z-b)+\frac Uz+\log(1/z-b).
$$

Since $1/z-b=-b(z-1/b)/z$, locally consistent branches of the [complex logarithm](../../../../../complex-logarithm.md) give

$$
\boxed{w(z)=Uz+\frac Uz+\log(z-b)
+\log(z-1/b)-\log z+C}.
$$

Changes of branch only alter the constant in a local expression; $w'$ is single valued away from its singularities. The added terms represent a [two-dimensional potential doublet](../../../../../two-dimensional-potential-doublet.md) at the origin, an image [line source](../../../../../line-source.md) at $1/b$, and a compensating [line sink](../../../../../line-sink.md) at the origin. The image [line source](../../../../../line-source.md) and [line sink](../../../../../line-sink.md) have equal and opposite strengths, so no net [volume flux](../../../../../volumetric-flow-rate.md) enters the [cylinder](../../../../../cylinder-geometry.md).

On $|z|=1$, the [Milne-Thomson circle theorem](../../../../../milne-thomson-circle-theorem.md) makes $w=f(z)+\overline{f(z)}$ up to a constant. Consequently the [streamfunction](../../../../../stream-function.md) is constant there, establishing the [no-penetration boundary condition](../../../../../no-penetration-boundary-condition.md). All [line source](../../../../../line-source.md) strengths are real, and the [two-dimensional potential doublet](../../../../../two-dimensional-potential-doublet.md) has zero [circulation](../../../../../circulation-physics.md), so the [circulation](../../../../../circulation-physics.md) around the [cylinder](../../../../../cylinder-geometry.md) is zero. There is no additional [line vortex](../../../../../line-vortex.md) term.
