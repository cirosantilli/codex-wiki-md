# 2023

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the fluids and waves course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Solutions to all three questions of the [University of Oxford](../university-of-oxford-split.md) Part A fluids and waves paper, sat on 16 June 2023. Source: [original released PDF, bundle pages 242–245; paper pages 1–4](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/bsc_mathematics_part_a_examinati/response/2820674/attach/3/FOI%20202410%20944%20Data.pdf#page=242). Question statements are omitted. Throughout, the liquid has constant positive [mass density](../density.md) $\rho$; a nonzero doublet and positive [bending stiffness](../bending-stiffness.md) are understood in the corresponding questions.
