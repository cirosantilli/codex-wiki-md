<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use $W=\phi+i\psi$ for the [complex potential of a two-dimensional potential flow](../../../../complex-potential-of-a-two-dimensional-potential-flow.md), so $W'(z)=u-iv$. The [Milne-Thomson circle theorem](../../../../milne-thomson-circle-theorem.md) says that if $f(z)$ is an incident [complex potential of a two-dimensional potential flow](../../../../complex-potential-of-a-two-dimensional-potential-flow.md) whose singularities lie outside $|z|=a$, then

$$
W(z)=f(z)+\overline{f\!\left(\frac{a^2}{\bar z}\right)}
$$

makes $|z|=a$ a [streamline](../../../../streamline.md). Indeed, on that circle $a^2/\bar z=z$, so $W=f+\bar f$ is real and the [stream function](../../../../stream-function.md) is constant. This imposes the [no-penetration boundary condition](../../../../no-penetration-boundary-condition.md).

For the [two-dimensional potential doublet](../../../../two-dimensional-potential-doublet.md), put $M=me^{i\alpha}$ and $f=M/(z-b)$, where $b>a$ is real. The theorem gives

$$
\boxed{W(z)=\frac{me^{i\alpha}}{z-b}
+\frac{me^{-i\alpha}z}{a^2-bz}}.
$$

Up to an additive constant, this is equivalently

$$
W(z)=\frac{me^{i\alpha}}{z-b}
-\frac{ma^2e^{-i\alpha}}{b^2(z-a^2/b)}.
$$

The [method of images](../../../../method-of-images.md) has placed the image [two-dimensional potential doublet](../../../../two-dimensional-potential-doublet.md) at $a^2/b$, inside the obstacle. We use the solution with zero added [circulation](../../../../circulation-physics.md) and no imposed far-field flow; the original doublet is the only singularity in the liquid.
