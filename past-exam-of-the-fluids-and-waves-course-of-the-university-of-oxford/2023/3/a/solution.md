<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a [plane wave](../../../../plane-wave.md) with [amplitude](../../../../wave-amplitude.md) $\alpha$, [wavenumber](../../../../wavenumber.md) $k>0$ and [angular frequency](../../../../angular-frequency.md) $\omega>0$, the [dispersion relation](../../../../dispersion-relation-split.md) $\omega=\omega(k)$ specifies which frequencies the governing equations permit. A point of fixed [wave phase](../../../../phase-waves.md) obeys $kx-\omega t=\text{constant}$, so the [phase speed](../../../../phase-speed.md) is

$$
\boxed{c_p=\frac\omega k}.
$$

It is the speed of individual crests. For a narrow [wave packet](../../../../wave-packet.md), two nearby [plane waves](../../../../plane-wave.md) with differences $\Delta k$ and $\Delta\omega$ have a slowly varying [wave envelope](../../../../envelope-waves.md) depending on $\Delta k\,x-\Delta\omega\,t$. Taking the limit gives the [group velocity](../../../../group-velocity.md)

$$
\boxed{c_g=\frac{d\omega}{dk}}.
$$

This is the envelope's propagation speed and, in the present linear lossless wave model, the [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) transport speed for a narrow packet.

A [dispersive wave](../../../../dispersive-wave.md) has [phase speed](../../../../phase-speed.md) dependent on [wavenumber](../../../../wavenumber.md), so different components can change their relative phases and alter the packet's shape. A [nondispersive wave](../../../../nondispersive-wave.md) on the chosen propagation branch has constant [phase speed](../../../../phase-speed.md) over the relevant band:

$$
\boxed{\omega(k)=ck,\qquad c_p=c_g=c}.
$$

Indeed $dc_p/dk=(c_g-c_p)/k$, so equality throughout a band is equivalent to constant [phase speed](../../../../phase-speed.md) there. Equality at an isolated [wavenumber](../../../../wavenumber.md) does not make the entire wave family nondispersive.
