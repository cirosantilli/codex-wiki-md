<h1 id="3/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Define the dimensionless parameter

$$
\delta=\left(\frac{\rho gh^4}{B}\right)^{1/4}.
$$

The stipulated hierarchy $kh\ll\delta\ll1$ implies both $kh\ll1$ and

$$
\frac{Bk^4}{\rho g}=\left(\frac{kh}{\delta}\right)^4\ll1.
$$

Thus the finite-depth correction uses $\tanh(kh)\sim kh$, while the restoring effect of [bending stiffness](../../../../../bending-stiffness.md) is small compared with [gravity](../../../../../gravity.md). The [dispersion relation](../../../../../dispersion-relation-split.md) becomes

$$
\omega^2
=ghk^2\left[1+\left(\frac{kh}{\delta}\right)^4\right]
\frac{\tanh(kh)}{kh}
\sim ghk^2.
$$

Hence, to leading order, these [surface gravity waves](../../../../../surface-gravity-wave.md) are [nondispersive waves](../../../../../nondispersive-wave.md) with

$$
\boxed{\omega\sim k\sqrt{gh},\qquad c_p\sim c_g\sim c=\sqrt{gh}}.
$$

For clarity, the first corrections to the [phase speed](../../../../../phase-speed.md) are

$$
\frac{c_p}{\sqrt{gh}}
=1+\frac12\left(\frac{kh}{\delta}\right)^4
-\frac16(kh)^2
+O\!\left((kh/\delta)^8+(kh)^4+(kh/\delta)^4(kh)^2\right).
$$

They exhibit the weak residual [wave dispersion](../../../../../wave-dispersion.md) at finite [wavenumber](../../../../../wavenumber.md). The claimed nondispersive behavior is the asymptotic leading-order result, not exact equality for the complete relation.
