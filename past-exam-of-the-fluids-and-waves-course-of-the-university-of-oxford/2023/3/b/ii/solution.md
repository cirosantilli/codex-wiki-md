<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

At the rigid bed, the [no-penetration boundary condition](../../../../../no-penetration-boundary-condition.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\phi_z=0\quad\text{at }z=-h}.
$$

The displaced surface is material, so taking the [material derivative](../../../../../material-derivative.md) of $z-\eta(x,t)$ gives the exact [kinematic boundary condition for a free-surface graph](../../../../../kinematic-boundary-condition-for-a-free-surface-graph.md):

$$
\boxed{\eta_t+\phi_x\eta_x=\phi_z\quad\text{at }z=\eta(x,t)}.
$$

With upward vertical coordinate $z$, the gravitational potential per unit [mass](../../../../../mass.md) is $gz$. The [Unsteady Bernoulli equation](../../../../../unsteady-bernoulli-equation.md), evaluated on the surface with the prescribed [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) $p=B\eta_{xxxx}$, gives

$$
\boxed{\phi_t+\frac12(\phi_x^2+\phi_z^2)
+g\eta+\frac B\rho\eta_{xxxx}=0
\quad\text{at }z=\eta(x,t)}.
$$

Here the spatially uniform time-dependent Bernoulli term has been absorbed into the [velocity potential](../../../../../velocity-potential.md). The [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) is measured relative to the constant exterior pressure. This dynamic boundary condition uses the specified massless bending-cover model, with no ice inertia or tension.
