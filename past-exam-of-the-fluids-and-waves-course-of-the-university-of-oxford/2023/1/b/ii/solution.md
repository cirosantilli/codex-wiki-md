<h1 id="1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Put $A=R^2\dot R$. The [partial derivative](../../../../../partial-derivative.md) in the [Unsteady Bernoulli equation](../../../../../unsteady-bernoulli-equation.md) is taken at fixed spatial position, so

$$
\phi_t=\dot G-\frac{\dot A}{r},\qquad
|\nabla\phi|^2=\frac{A^2}{r^4}.
$$

At infinity the [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) vanishes and $p\to p_\infty$, giving $F(t)=\dot G+p_\infty/\rho$. Substitution cancels the arbitrary $G$ and yields the [pressure field of a pulsating sphere](../../../../../pressure-field-of-a-pulsating-sphere.md):

$$
\boxed{\frac{p(r,t)}\rho
=\frac1r\frac d{dt}(R^2\dot R)
-\frac{R^4\dot R^2}{2r^4}+\frac{p_\infty}\rho}.
$$

The distinction between $\partial_t\phi$ at fixed $r$ and the [material derivative](../../../../../material-derivative.md) along the moving boundary is essential here.
