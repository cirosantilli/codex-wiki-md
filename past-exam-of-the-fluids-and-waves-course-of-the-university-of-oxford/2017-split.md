# 2017

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the fluids and waves course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Solutions to all three questions of the [University of Oxford](../university-of-oxford-split.md) Part A fluids and waves examination, sat on 17 June 2017. Source: [released examination archive](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/maths_papers_2/response/1850333/attach/3/FOI%2020210708%201%20Data.zip?cookie_passthrough=1), member `FOI-20210708-1 - Data/a15292w1 (2).pdf`, printed pages 1–4. Question statements are omitted. Oxford's [published solutions policy](https://www.maths.ox.ac.uk/members/students/undergraduate-courses/examinations-assessments/overview-examinations) excludes 2017; a bounded search found no advertised full solutions for this exact paper.
