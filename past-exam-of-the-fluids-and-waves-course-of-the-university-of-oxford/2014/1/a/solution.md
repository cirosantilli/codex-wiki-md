<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Choose the planar [streamfunction](../../../../stream-function.md) convention $u=\psi_y$, $v=-\psi_x$. For an [irrotational flow](../../../../irrotational-flow.md), a [velocity potential](../../../../velocity-potential.md) gives $\mathbf u=\nabla\phi$. Thus

$$
\boxed{u=\phi_x=\psi_y,\qquad v=\phi_y=-\psi_x.}
$$

The [streamfunction](../../../../stream-function.md) enforces [incompressibility](../../../../incompressible-flow.md), since $u_x+v_y=\psi_{yx}-\psi_{xy}=0$. Conversely, [incompressibility](../../../../incompressible-flow.md) permits a local [streamfunction](../../../../stream-function.md), while zero [curl](../../../../curl.md) permits a local [velocity potential](../../../../velocity-potential.md). These are the [Cauchy-Riemann equations](../../../../cauchy-riemann-equations.md) for the [complex potential of a two-dimensional potential flow](../../../../complex-potential-of-a-two-dimensional-potential-flow.md) $w=\phi+i\psi$.

The [gradients](../../../../gradient.md) of the two functions satisfy

$$
\nabla\phi\cdot\nabla\psi=(u,v)\cdot(-v,u)=0.
$$

Each [gradient](../../../../gradient.md) is normal to its regular level curves. Therefore **regular curves of constant [velocity potential](../../../../velocity-potential.md) and constant [streamfunction](../../../../stream-function.md) meet orthogonally**. At a [stagnation point](../../../../stagnation-point.md), both gradients vanish and this tangent-direction argument needs a limiting interpretation. The conclusion concerns regular portions of the two families of curves.
