<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Parameterize the [unit circle](../../../../complex-unit-circle.md) by $\zeta=e^{i\theta}$. Under the [Joukowski map](../../../../joukowski-map.md),

$$
z=e^{i\theta}+a^2e^{-i\theta}
=(1+a^2)\cos\theta+i(1-a^2)\sin\theta.
$$

Consequently its image is the [ellipse](../../../../ellipse.md)

$$
\boxed{\frac{x^2}{(1+a^2)^2}+\frac{y^2}{(1-a^2)^2}=1,}
$$

with horizontal and vertical [semi-axes](../../../../semi-axis.md)

$$
\boxed{A=1+a^2,\qquad B=1-a^2.}
$$

Both are positive because $0<a<1$. The horizontal axis is the major axis. The derivative $dz/d\zeta=1-a^2/\zeta^2$ is nonzero for $|\zeta|\geq1$. Also, equality of two images implies $(\zeta_1-\zeta_2)(1-a^2/(\zeta_1\zeta_2))=0$; two distinct exterior points cannot satisfy the second factor since $|\zeta_1\zeta_2|>1>a^2$. Thus the exterior map is one-to-one, mapping the exterior of the [unit circle](../../../../complex-unit-circle.md) conformally onto the exterior of the [ellipse](../../../../ellipse.md).
