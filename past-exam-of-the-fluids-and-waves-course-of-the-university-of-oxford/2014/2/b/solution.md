<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A [uniform flow](../../../../uniform-flow.md) with physical [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) $(U\cos\alpha,U\sin\alpha)$ has [complex velocity](../../../../complex-velocity.md) $Ue^{-i\alpha}$ and incident potential $f(\zeta)=Ue^{-i\alpha}\zeta$. Apply the [Milne-Thomson circle theorem](../../../../milne-thomson-circle-theorem.md) with $R=1$:

$$
\boxed{W(\zeta)=U\left(e^{-i\alpha}\zeta+\frac{e^{i\alpha}}\zeta\right).}
$$

Indeed, on $\zeta=e^{i\theta}$ it is $W=2U\cos(\theta-\alpha)$, which is real, so the boundary is a [streamline](../../../../streamline.md). At infinity, $W'(\zeta)\to Ue^{-i\alpha}$, verifying the specified incoming [uniform flow](../../../../uniform-flow.md).
