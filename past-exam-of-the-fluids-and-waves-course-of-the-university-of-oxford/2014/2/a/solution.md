<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Milne-Thomson circle theorem](../../../../milne-thomson-circle-theorem.md) constructs a circular impermeable boundary in planar [potential flow](../../../../potential-flow.md). Suppose $f(\zeta)$ is an incident [complex potential of a two-dimensional potential flow](../../../../complex-potential-of-a-two-dimensional-potential-flow.md) with no singularities on or inside $|\zeta|=R$. Then

$$
\boxed{W(\zeta)=f(\zeta)+\overline{f\!\left(\frac{R^2}{\bar\zeta}\right)}}
$$

is a [complex potential of a two-dimensional potential flow](../../../../complex-potential-of-a-two-dimensional-potential-flow.md) outside the [circle](../../../../circle.md), with the same exterior singularities and their strengths as the incident flow; the added image singularities lie inside the excluded disk. On $|\zeta|=R$, $R^2/\bar\zeta=\zeta$, so $W=f+\bar f$ is real. Its imaginary part, the [streamfunction](../../../../stream-function.md), is constant on the [circle](../../../../circle.md), proving the [no-penetration boundary condition](../../../../no-penetration-boundary-condition.md). The theorem gives the usual zero-[circulation](../../../../circulation-physics.md) construction; a separately prescribed [circulation](../../../../circulation-physics.md) can be added by its logarithmic vortex potential.
