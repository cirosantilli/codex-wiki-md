<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The total [velocity fields](../../../../velocity-field.md) on the two sides are

$$
\mathbf u_+=(U,0)+\nabla\phi_+,\qquad \mathbf u_-=\nabla\phi_-.
$$

Each base flow is divergence-free. [Incompressibility](../../../../incompressible-flow.md) therefore makes both disturbance [velocity potentials](../../../../velocity-potential.md) satisfy the [Laplace equation](../../../../laplace-equation.md):

$$
\boxed{\phi_{+,xx}+\phi_{+,yy}=0,\qquad
\phi_{-,xx}+\phi_{-,yy}=0.}
$$

At the fixed horizontal walls, impermeability requires zero vertical [velocity](../../../../velocity.md):

$$
\boxed{\phi_{+,y}(x,h,t)=0,\qquad\phi_{-,y}(x,-h,t)=0.}
$$

The discontinuity in tangential base [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) at the interface is a [vortex sheet](../../../../vortex-sheet.md). The assumption of [irrotational flow](../../../../irrotational-flow.md) is understood separately in each open fluid region; it cannot hold distributionally across that sheet when $U\ne0$.
