# 2014

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the fluids and waves course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Solutions to all three questions of the [University of Oxford](../university-of-oxford-split.md) Part A examination of 17 June 2014, printed as “Waves and Fluids.” Source: [released examination archive](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/maths_papers_2/response/1850333/attach/3/FOI%2020210708%201%20Data.zip?cookie_passthrough=1), member `FOI-20210708-1 - Data/4329.pdf`, printed pages 1–3. Question statements are omitted. A bounded exact-paper check found no advertised full or partial solutions. The department's [published policy](https://www.maths.ox.ac.uk/members/students/undergraduate-courses/examinations-assessments/overview-examinations) explicitly describes even-year coverage back to at least 2016, which does not by itself establish availability for this 2014 paper. Energies and effective masses below are per unit length along the cylinder axis.
