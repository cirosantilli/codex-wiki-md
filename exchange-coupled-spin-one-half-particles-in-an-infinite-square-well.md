# Exchange-coupled spin-one-half particles in an infinite square well

↑ **Parent:** [Exchange symmetry of two identical spin-one-half fermions](exchange-symmetry-of-two-identical-spin-one-half-fermions.md)

For well width $a$, put $\varepsilon=\pi^2\hbar^2/(2ma^2)$ and $J=\lambda\hbar^2$ when the interaction is $-\lambda\mathbf S_1\cdot\mathbf S_2$. The [spin-dot-product eigenvalue](spin-dot-product-eigenvalue.md) gives exchange energies $3J/4$ for the [singlet state](singlet-state.md) and $-J/4$ for the [triplet states](spin-one-half-triplet-state.md). Identical [fermions](fermion.md) require symmetric spatial orbitals with the singlet and antisymmetric orbitals with the triplet. Two particles in the same orbital can therefore only be singlets.

For $0<J\ll\varepsilon$, the three lowest distinct [energy levels](energy-level.md) are the orbital-$11$ singlet at $2\varepsilon+3J/4$, orbital-$12$ triplet at $5\varepsilon-J/4$, and orbital-$12$ singlet at $5\varepsilon+3J/4$, with multiplicities $1,3,1$. Distinguishable particles instead allow an orbital-$11$ triplet, making the lowest three levels $2\varepsilon-J/4$, $2\varepsilon+3J/4$, and $5\varepsilon-J/4$, with multiplicities $3,1,6$. The sixfold level includes both orbital orderings. For negative $J$, the spin-sector orderings reverse within each fixed orbital sum.
