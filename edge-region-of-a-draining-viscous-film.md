# Edge region of a draining viscous film

↑ **Parent:** [Draining viscous film on a finite horizontal plate](draining-viscous-film-on-a-finite-horizontal-plate.md)

The outer [similarity solution](similarity-solution.md) approaches a plate edge as $h/h_0\sim[(4C/5)(1-x/L)]^{1/4}$, where $C=B(4/5,1/2)$. Its slope becomes order one at

$$
\ell\sim L(h_0/L)^{4/3}.
$$

This region lies outside [lubrication theory](lubrication-theory.md) because its vertical and horizontal scales are comparable. The outer zero-thickness boundary condition is consistent at leading order since the matching height is $O(\ell)\ll h_0$, while the actual edge flow requires an inner solution. A finite outward flux survives even though the outer thickness vanishes, because the slope diverges.
