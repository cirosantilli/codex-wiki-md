# Chaotic stellar orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar dynamics](stellar-dynamics.md)

A stellar orbit with sensitive dependence on its initial phase-space point, often diagnosed by a positive [Lyapunov exponent](lyapunov-exponent.md). It need not explore the whole energy hypersurface: regular islands and disconnected chaotic regions can coexist. If an orbit is dense in a connected energy-shell region, every continuous [integral of motion](integral-of-motion.md) is constant on its closure; that stronger condition excludes further independent continuous constraints within that region.
