# Robinson arithmetic

↑ **Parent:** [First-order theory](first-order-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robinson_arithmetic)

[Robinson arithmetic](robinson-arithmetic.md) is a weak finitely axiomatized first-order theory sufficient for numeralwise representation of computable finite calculations and the [diagonal lemma](diagonal-lemma.md). Its extensions are sufficiently strong for the soundness-based incompleteness argument.
