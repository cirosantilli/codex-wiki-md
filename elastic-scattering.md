# Elastic scattering

↑ **Parent:** [Scattering amplitude](scattering-amplitude.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elastic_scattering)

Elastic scattering preserves the total kinetic energy. For one particle scattered by a fixed target, the incoming and outgoing [wavevectors](wavevector.md) therefore have equal magnitudes.
