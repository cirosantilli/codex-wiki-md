# Bolometer

↑ **Parent:** [Photodetector](photodetector.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bolometer)

A [bolometer](bolometer.md) detects absorbed [electromagnetic radiation](electromagnetic-radiation-split.md) through the resulting change in [temperature](temperature-split.md), often read as a change in [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md). Its response depends on absorbed [power](power.md), so interpreting an [optical spectrum](optical-spectrum.md) requires accounting for the incident [wavelength](wavelength.md) band and detector calibration.
