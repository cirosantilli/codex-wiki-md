# Coquasitriangular structure

↑ **Parent:** [Bimonoid](bimonoid.md)

In a [symmetric monoidal category](symmetric-monoidal-category.md), a convolution-invertible scalar pairing $\gamma:H\otimes H\to I$ on a [bimonoid](bimonoid.md) inducing the [comodule](comodule.md) braiding $u\otimes v\mapsto\sum v_{(0)}\otimes u_{(0)}\gamma(u_{(1)},v_{(1)})$. Its multiplicativity, normalization and commutation axioms express the hexagon, unit and colinearity conditions. The notation abbreviates morphisms and ambient symmetries, not a requirement for elements.
