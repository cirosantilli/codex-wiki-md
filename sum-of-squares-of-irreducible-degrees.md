# Sum of squares of irreducible degrees

↑ **Parent:** [Regular representation](regular-representation.md)

If $\rho_1,\ldots,\rho_k$ are the complex irreducible representations of a finite group $G$ and $n_j=\dim\rho_j$, decomposition of the [regular representation](regular-representation.md) gives

$$
\sum_{j=1}^k n_j^2=|G|.
$$
