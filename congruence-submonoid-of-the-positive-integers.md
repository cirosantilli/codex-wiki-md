# Congruence submonoid of the positive integers

↑ **Parent:** [Cancellative commutative monoid](cancellative-commutative-monoid.md)

For an integer $m\geq2$, the positive integers congruent to one modulo $m$ form a [cancellative commutative monoid](cancellative-commutative-monoid.md) under multiplication. The identity is one, and it is the only unit. Proper factors outside this congruence class do not witness reducibility inside the monoid. Hence [irreducible elements of a commutative monoid](irreducible-element-of-a-commutative-monoid.md) need not coincide with ordinary [prime numbers](prime-number.md).
