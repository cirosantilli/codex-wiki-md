# Recombination cooling of ionized hydrogen

↑ **Parent:** [Radiative recombination](radiative-recombination.md)

[Radiative recombination](radiative-recombination.md) removes the [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) of captured [electrons](electron.md) from a plasma. The thermal cooling per event is not the entire emitted [photon](photon.md) energy: [binding energy](binding-energy.md) had been stored as [ionization energy](ionization-energy.md). If recombination is the only cooling and [ionization](ionization.md) is balanced, the mean removed [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) equals the mean [photoelectron](photoelectron.md) heating energy.
