# Mean-square completion of trigonometric polynomials

↑ **Parent:** [Hilbert space completion](hilbert-space-completion.md)

Start with finite real linear combinations of $1$, $\cos(\lambda x)$ and $\sin(\lambda x)$ with arbitrary positive real frequencies, and use the [inner product](inner-product.md) $\langle f,g\rangle_M=\lim_{R\to\infty}R^{-1}\int_{-R}^Rfg$. Product-to-sum identities give existence of these averages and mutual [orthogonality](orthogonal-vectors.md) of distinct frequencies. The squared [norm](norm.md) is $2a_0^2+\sum_\lambda(a_\lambda^2+b_\lambda^2)$. Its [Hilbert space completion](hilbert-space-completion.md) contains an uncountable [orthonormal set](orthonormal-set.md), so is not a [separable Hilbert space](separable-hilbert-space.md). This construction does not equip all [locally square-integrable functions](locally-square-integrable-function.md) with an [inner product](inner-product.md): compactly supported nonzero functions have zero mean square, and other functions have divergent or nonexistent averages.
