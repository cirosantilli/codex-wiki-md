# Polar coordinates for the electroweak Higgs doublet

↑ **Parent:** [Higgs field](higgs-field.md)

A nonzero [Higgs doublet](higgs-field.md) has a real radial magnitude and three real angular coordinates. For a normalized vector $z=(z_1,z_2)^T$, the [SU(2) matrix](su-2-matrix.md) $U=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}z_2^*&z_1\\-z_1^*&z_2\end{smallmatrix}\right)$ satisfies $U(0,1)^T=z$. Near the vacuum, the three broken generators can equivalently be parameterized by $e^{-i\boldsymbol n\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma+in_3}$. A local [gauge transformation](gauge-transformation.md) removes these angular fields in [unitary gauge](unitary-gauge.md). This coordinate choice is local about a nonzero vacuum and need not extend through zeros or topological defects.
