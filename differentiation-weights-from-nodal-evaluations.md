# Differentiation weights from nodal evaluations

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrange polynomial](lagrange-polynomial.md)

At distinct nodes $a_0,\ldots,a_n$, the [Lagrange interpolation polynomial](lagrange-polynomial.md) has basis $\ell_j(x)=\prod_{i\ne j}(x-a_i)/(a_j-a_i)$. Differentiating its exact expression for a polynomial of degree at most $n$ gives the displayed weights. Their uniqueness follows because the nodal evaluations form a basis of the [dual space](dual-space.md) of that polynomial space.
