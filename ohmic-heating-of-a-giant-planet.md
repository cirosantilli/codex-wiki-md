# Ohmic heating of a giant planet

↑ **Parent:** [Heating of an inflated giant planet](heating-of-an-inflated-giant-planet.md)

Atmospheric winds across a magnetic field can induce electric currents. Their [Ohmic heating](joule-heating.md) converts mechanical energy into heat, at local rate $q=\mathbf J\cdot\mathbf E=J^2/\sigma$ in the simplest resistive description. To maintain [hot-Jupiter radius inflation](hot-jupiter-radius-inflation.md), current paths and deposited heat must influence the deep interior; dissipation restricted to optically thin upper layers need not maintain the planet's entropy.
