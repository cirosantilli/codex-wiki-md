# Worldline gauge-orbit determinant

↑ **Parent:** [Proper-time modulus](proper-time-modulus.md)

On a unit interval with gauge parameters vanishing at its ends, $e=s+\dot\epsilon$ separates the [worldline einbein](worldline-einbein.md) into its invariant average $s$ and a [canonical gauge transformation](canonical-gauge-transformation.md). The Jacobian for nonconstant modes is $\det\partial_t$ between the appropriate boundary-condition spaces. On a circle its prime excludes the constant gauge-parameter zero mode. This [Faddeev-Popov determinant](faddeev-popov-determinant.md) can be represented by anticommuting $b,c$ fields with an action proportional to $\int b\dot c\,dt$.
