# Leading-residue obstruction to partition regularity

↑ **Parent:** [Rado theorem for one equation](rado-theorem-for-one-equation.md)

For nonzero [integer](integer.md) coefficients $a_i$, choose a [prime number](prime-number.md) $p>\sum_i|a_i|$ and colour each positive integer by its nonzero leading residue after removing its power of $p$. In a [monochromatic](monochromatic-set.md) zero-sum solution, divide by the least occurring power of $p$. Reduction modulo $p$ then makes the sum of the coefficients attached to the least-valuation variables vanish modulo $p$. Its absolute value is less than $p$, so that sum vanishes as an integer. This gives the necessity direction of the one-equation [partition regularity](partition-regular-matrix.md) criterion without using the general [Rado theorem](rado-s-theorem.md).
