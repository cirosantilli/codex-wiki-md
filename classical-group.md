# Classical group

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix group](matrix-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classical_group)

A [classical group](classical-group.md) is a standard family of linear groups, principally the general/special linear groups and groups preserving nondegenerate alternating, symmetric, Hermitian or quadratic forms. Finite-field versions yield many [finite simple groups](finite-simple-group.md) after imposing the appropriate determinant or derived-subgroup condition and quotienting the scalar [center of a group](center-of-a-group.md). Small dimensions and small fields have exceptional isomorphisms and nonsimple cases.
