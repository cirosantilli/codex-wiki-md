<h1 id="continuous-levy-process">Continuous Lévy process</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Lévy process](levy-process.md)

A real [Lévy process](levy-process.md) with continuous paths is [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) with deterministic linear drift: $X_t=bt+\sigma W_t$. Continuity forces the jump measure in the [Lévy–Khintchine formula](levy-khintchine-formula.md) to vanish, leaving characteristic function $\exp(t(ibu-\sigma^2u^2/2))$. If its law is also invariant under $X_t\mapsto\lambda^{-1}X_{\lambda^2t}$ for every $\lambda>0$, then $b=0$. The zero-variance case is included.
