# Faddeev-Popov gauge-orbit identity

↑ **Parent:** [Faddeev-Popov determinant](faddeev-popov-determinant.md)

Suppose a [gauge fixing](gauge-fixing.md) condition intersects each [gauge orbit](gauge-orbit.md) once in the local region under consideration, and its linearized [Faddeev-Popov operator](faddeev-popov-operator.md) has no residual zero modes after boundary conditions are imposed. The [functional Jacobian](functional-jacobian.md) for changing from the [gauge transformation](gauge-transformation.md) parameter to the gauge condition gives the displayed identity. Inserting it in a [gauge-invariant](gauge-invariance.md) [functional integral](functional-measure.md) allows the integration along the [gauge orbit](gauge-orbit.md) to factor out and cancel the formal gauge-group volume. In a perturbative neighborhood the Jacobian's sign or phase is fixed; a field-independent determinant factor is absorbed into normalization. The [Gribov ambiguity](gribov-ambiguity.md) prevents treating this local identity as a globally unique gauge choice without further qualifications.
