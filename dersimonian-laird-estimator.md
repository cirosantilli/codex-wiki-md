<h1 id="dersimonian-laird-estimator">DerSimonian–Laird estimator</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Between-study heterogeneity](between-study-heterogeneity.md)

For $m$ studies let $w_i=v_i^{-1}$, $W=\sum_iw_i$, $C=W-\sum_iw_i^2/W$, and let $Q$ be [Cochran's Q statistic](cochran-s-q-statistic.md). The moment estimate is $\widehat\tau^2=\max\{0,[Q-(m-1)]/C\}$ when $C>0$. It estimates the [variance](variance-split.md) of underlying study effects, not their sampling variance.
