# Hall angle

↑ **Parent:** [Hall effect](hall-effect.md)

In an isotropic single-carrier [Drude model](drude-model.md), let the [electric current density](current-density.md) point along $x$, with zero transverse current and [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) along $z$. The [Hall coefficient](hall-coefficient.md) is $R_H=1/(nq)$ and the longitudinal [electrical conductivity](electrical-conductivity.md) is $\sigma=n|q|\mu$, where the [charge carrier mobility](charge-carrier-mobility.md) $\mu$ is positive. Consequently $E_y/E_x=R_H\sigma B=\operatorname{sgn}(q)\mu B$. This signed Hall angle convention describes the angle of the electric field relative to the current; a convention measuring current relative to field has the opposite sign.
