# Root-space reducedness lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Root-space decomposition](root-space-decomposition.md)

For a complex [semisimple Lie algebra](semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md), restrict the module $\mathfrak h\oplus\bigoplus_{j\ne0}\mathfrak g_{j\alpha}$ to the [sl2 subalgebra associated with a root](sl2-subalgebra-associated-with-a-root.md). All its weights are even. Its raising operator maps the weight-zero space $\mathfrak h$ onto the one-dimensional line of the chosen [root vector](root-vector.md), so there is exactly one nontrivial irreducible summand: the adjoint module of highest weight $2$. Thus the root spaces at $\pm\alpha$ are one-dimensional and $2\alpha$ is not a root. For any parallel root $c\alpha$, the integers $2c$ and $2/c$ have product $4$; excluding double and half roots leaves only $c=\pm1$.
