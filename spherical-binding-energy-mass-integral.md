# Spherical binding-energy mass integral

↑ **Parent:** [Newtonian gravitational potential energy](newtonian-gravitational-potential-energy.md)

Assembling a spherical mass distribution in shells gives [gravitational potential energy](gravitational-energy.md) $W=-G\int M(r)\,dM(r)/r$. Integration by parts gives the displayed identity when $M(r)^2/r$ vanishes at both endpoints. It includes each interacting pair once; no extra factor of one half belongs in the original shell-assembly integral.
