# Unitary equivalence

↑ **Parent:** [Unitary operator](unitary-operator.md)

Two operators on [Hilbert spaces](hilbert-space-split.md) are unitarily equivalent if a [unitary operator](unitary-operator.md) maps their operator domains onto one another and intertwines their actions. Their [spectra](spectrum-functional-analysis.md) and [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) multiplicities then agree. For nonnegative [self-adjoint operators](self-adjoint-operator.md) it suffices that the unitary maps their closed [quadratic form](quadratic-form.md) domains onto one another and preserves the forms, since the associated operator is determined by the form.
