# Degree of a power map of the Riemann sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Degree of a holomorphic map](degree-of-a-holomorphic-map.md)

For $k\ge1$, the power map has $k$ distinct preimages of every nonzero finite [regular value](regular-value.md), all with positive local [degree of a map between oriented manifolds](degree-of-a-map-between-oriented-manifolds.md). Integrating the [pullback of a differential form](pullback-of-a-differential-form.md) of $\Omega=i\,d\zeta\wedge d\bar\zeta/(1+|\zeta|^2)^2$ gives the same answer, since $\int\Omega=2\pi$ and $\int f^*\Omega=2\pi k$. For $k=0$ use the constant extension $1$ at infinity; its [degree of a map between oriented manifolds](degree-of-a-map-between-oriented-manifolds.md) is zero.
