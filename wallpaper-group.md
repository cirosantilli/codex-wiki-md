# Wallpaper group

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean geometry](euclidean-geometry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wallpaper_group)

A [plane crystallographic group](wallpaper-group.md) is a discrete group of Euclidean plane isometries with compact quotient. Its translation subgroup is a rank-two [lattice](lattice.md), and the quotient by translations is its finite [point group](point-group.md). This separates the periodic translational geometry from the finite rotational and reflection symmetries.
