# Priestley dual space

↑ **Parent:** [Priestley duality](priestley-duality.md)

The Priestley dual space $\widehat L$ of a bounded [distributive lattice](distributive-lattice.md) $L$ consists of its [prime filters](prime-filter-of-a-distributive-lattice.md), ordered by inclusion and topologized by the sets $a^*=\{P:a\in P\}$ and their complements.

This construction yields a [Priestley space](priestley-space.md): a compact ordered topological space in which suitable clopen upward-closed sets separate distinct comparable-order failures.
