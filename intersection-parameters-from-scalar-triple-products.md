# Intersection parameters from scalar triple products

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar triple product](scalar-triple-product.md)

Consider [lines](straight-line.md) $r=a+sm$ and $r=b+tn$ with nonzero direction [vectors](vector.md). If they intersect, then $a-b=tn-sm$, so the necessary coplanarity condition is $[a-b,m,n]=0$. For nonparallel directions it is also sufficient, since the [orthogonal complement](orthogonal-complement.md) of $m\times n$ is the [linear span](linear-span.md) of $m,n$. For parallel directions the condition alone is insufficient: distinct parallel [lines](straight-line.md) also satisfy it.

Assume intersection and $D=[a,m,n]\ne0$. Take the [scalar triple products](scalar-triple-product.md) $[a,b,n]$ and $[a,b,m]$ of $b=a+sm-tn$. The [scalar triple product](scalar-triple-product.md) is an [alternating multilinear map](alternating-multilinear-map.md), giving respectively $sD$ and $tD$, proving the title formulas. The nonzero denominator fixes a convenient determinant chart; intersections with $D=0$ can still exist and require a different auxiliary [vector](vector.md) or another method.
