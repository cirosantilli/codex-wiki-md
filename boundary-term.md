# Boundary term

↑ **Parent:** [Integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md)

A boundary term is an endpoint or boundary integral left when derivatives are transferred by [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md). It vanishes under some boundary conditions and otherwise supplies natural boundary conditions, conserved charges, or physical surface forces.
