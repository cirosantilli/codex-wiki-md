# Floor-impulse dependence of a rolling-cylinder collision

↑ **Parent:** [Rolling without slipping](rolling-without-slipping.md)

A smooth central [collision](collision.md) leaves a cylinder's [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md) unchanged if the floor supplies negligible tangential [impulse](impulse.md). Imposing [rolling without slipping](rolling-without-slipping.md) during the impact instead requires a floor [impulse](impulse.md) and changes the cylinder's effective translational [mass](mass.md) to $m+I/r^2$. Consequently a pre-impact rolling condition alone does not determine the outgoing [velocities](velocity.md): the impulsive contact law must also be specified. For a uniform solid cylinder striking a stationary [point mass](point-mass.md) $2m$ in an [elastic collision](elastic-collision.md), the target acquires $2u/3$ with negligible floor [impulse](impulse.md), and $6u/7$ with an enforced rolling constraint, where $u$ is the incoming centre [speed](speed.md).
