<h1 id="thevenin-equivalent">Thévenin equivalent</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Thévenin's theorem](thevenin-s-theorem.md)

A [Thévenin equivalent](thevenin-equivalent.md) represents a network at two terminals by a [voltage source](voltage-source.md) in series with its [Thévenin resistance](thevenin-resistance.md). It reproduces the terminal relation under the assumptions of [Thévenin's theorem](thevenin-s-theorem.md).
