# Parity-check matrix of a bar product

↑ **Parent:** [Bar product of binary linear codes](bar-product-of-binary-linear-codes.md)

If $H_i$ is a [parity-check matrix](parity-check-matrix.md) of $C_i$, then a parity-check matrix of $C_1|C_2$ is

$$
\begin{pmatrix}H_1&0\\H_2&H_2\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Its equations say that the first half $u$ lies in $C_1$ and that the sum of the two halves lies in $C_2$.
