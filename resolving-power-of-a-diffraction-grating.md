# Resolving power of a diffraction grating

↑ **Parent:** [Diffraction grating](diffraction-grating.md)

For $N$ coherently illuminated grating lines used in [diffraction order](diffraction-order.md) $m$, the [Rayleigh criterion](rayleigh-criterion.md) yields the displayed ideal [resolving power of a diffraction grating](resolving-power-of-a-diffraction-grating.md). The angular maximum-to-first-zero separation is $\lambda/(ND\cos\theta_m)$, while nearby wavelengths are separated by $|m|\delta\lambda/(D\cos\theta_m)$. The order must exist, carry appreciable [light intensity](light-intensity.md) and remain free from dominant instrumental broadening. Line count alone therefore does not determine whether a particular instrument resolves a specified doublet.
