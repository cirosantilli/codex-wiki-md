# Homopolar disc dynamo

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamo action](dynamo-action.md)

Connect a [homopolar generator](homopolar-generator.md) to a [solenoid](solenoid.md) producing its own axial field. Reinforcing winding and rotation give $C=\mu_0Na^2/(2l)$ and $L\dot I=(C\omega-R)I$. A seed field grows for $\omega>R/C$, is marginal at equality, and decays below threshold. An identically zero initial field remains zero in this homogeneous ideal model; startup requires a seed or perturbation.
