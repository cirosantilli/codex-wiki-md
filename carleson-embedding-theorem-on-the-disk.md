# Carleson embedding theorem on the disk

↑ **Parent:** [Carleson measure](carleson-measure.md)

A positive [Borel measure](borel-measure.md) on the [unit disc](unit-disc.md) satisfies $\int|f|\,d\mu\le N\|f\|_1$ for every [H1 space on the unit disk](h1-space-on-the-unit-disk.md) function exactly when it is a [Carleson measure](carleson-measure.md), with comparable optimal constants. The [normalized squared Cauchy kernel](normalized-squared-cauchy-kernel.md) tests necessity. For sufficiency, the boundary shadow of every point with $|f(z)|>t$ lies inside a component arc of $\{Nf>t\}$. The [geodesic-cap and Carleson-box comparison](geodesic-cap-and-carleson-box-comparison.md) bounds the measure of each shadow region by its component length, giving $\mu\{|f|>t\}\le C\|\mu\|_{\rm Carl}m\{Nf>t\}$. The [layer cake representation](layer-cake-representation.md) and [H1 non-tangential maximal inequality on the disk](h1-non-tangential-maximal-inequality-on-the-disk.md) give the embedding. The broader kernel and covering mechanism is discussed in [Terence Tao's harmonic-analysis notes, Exercise 2 after Lemma 4.1](https://www.math.ucla.edu/~tao/247b.1.07w/notes6.pdf); the analytic endpoint here uses the fractional-power argument just given.
