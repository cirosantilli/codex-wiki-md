# Zero-flux thermocapillary film profiles

↑ **Parent:** [Thermocapillary thin-film equation](thermocapillary-thin-film-equation.md)

Positive steady solutions of the balanced [thermocapillary thin-film equation](thermocapillary-thin-film-equation.md) with zero [volume flux](volumetric-flow-rate.md) satisfy $H_{XX}+\ln H=0$ after choosing the thickness scale. Their first integral has potential $V(H)=H(\ln H-1)$, with minimum $V(1)=-1$ and limiting value $V(0^+)=0$. At $E=-1$ the film is uniform; for $-1<E<0$ it is positive and periodic. At $E=0$ a limiting drop has peak $e$, half-width $\sqrt{\pi e}$, zero limiting [contact angle](contact-angle.md) and divergent curvature at zero thickness. Small periodic profiles have [period](period-of-a-function.md) $2\pi$ and approach the neutral, rather than fastest-growing, [normal mode](normal-mode.md) of the [thermocapillary film instability](thermocapillary-thin-film-equation.md).
