# Elliptic and cusp ramification for principal level

↑ **Parent:** [Principal congruence modular curve genus](principal-congruence-modular-curve-genus.md)

For $N\geq2$, the effective [principal congruence subgroup](principal-congruence-subgroup.md) is torsion-free. Its [modular curve](modular-curve.md) cover of $X(1)$ has [analytic ramification indices](ramification-index-of-a-holomorphic-map.md) $2,3,N$ above the two elliptic orbits and the [modular cusp](cusp-of-a-modular-group.md). If $\mu$ is its projective index, the numbers of points above these three values are $\mu/2,\mu/3,\mu/N$. Applying the [Riemann-Hurwitz formula](riemann-hurwitz-formula.md) gives $2g-2=\mu(1/6-1/N)$. Here $\mu=6$ for $N=2$ and $\mu=N^3\prod_{p\mid N}(1-p^{-2})/2$ for $N\geq3$; level one is the identity cover.
