# Eisenstein-integer norm

↑ **Parent:** [Eisenstein integer](eisenstein-integer.md)

The Eisenstein-integer norm is $N(z)=z\overline z$. It is multiplicative and takes $a+b\omega$ to $a^2-ab+b^2$.
