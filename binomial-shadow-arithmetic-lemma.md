# Binomial-shadow arithmetic lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Kruskal-Katona theorem](kruskal-katona-theorem.md)

Let $f_r(m)$ be the lower-shadow size prescribed by the binomial representation in the [Kruskal-Katona theorem](kruskal-katona-theorem.md). Then

$$
f_r(a+b)\leq\max\{f_r(a),b\}+f_{r-1}(b).
$$

Induction using [Pascal's identity](pascal-s-rule.md) proves the inequality and drives the standard ground-set induction for the theorem.
