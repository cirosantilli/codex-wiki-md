# Weak isospin

↑ **Parent:** [Isospin](isospin.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weak_isospin)

[Weak isospin](weak-isospin.md) labels representations of the [SU(2)](su-2-group.md) factor in the electroweak gauge group. A left-handed [lepton](lepton.md) doublet has $T=1/2$ with third-component [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $+1/2,-1/2$; a right-handed charged-[lepton](lepton.md) singlet has $T=0$. Along with [electric charge](electric-charge.md), the third component determines the [weak neutral current](neutral-current.md) through $T^3-Q\sin^2\theta_W$.
