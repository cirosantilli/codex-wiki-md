# Exterior square of a direct sum

↑ **Parent:** [Exterior square](exterior-square.md)

The [exterior square](exterior-square.md) of a [direct sum](direct-sum.md) splits into wedges of two vectors from $V$, two from $W$, or one from each. The mixed map sends $v\otimes w$ to $(v,0)\wedge(0,w)$. A combined [basis](basis.md) proves it is an isomorphism, and the [exterior-power Lie algebra representation](exterior-power-lie-algebra-representation.md) proves equivariance for modules. No division by $2$ is used.
