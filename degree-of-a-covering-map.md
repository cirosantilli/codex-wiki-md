# Degree of a covering map

↑ **Parent:** [Covering space](covering-space.md)

For a [covering map](covering-space.md) with nonempty [connected](connected-space.md) base, the [degree of a covering map](degree-of-a-covering-map.md) is the common [cardinality](cardinality.md) of its [fibres](fiber-of-a-function.md). Each [evenly covered neighbourhood](evenly-covered-neighbourhood.md) makes this number [locally constant](locally-constant-function.md), so [connectedness](connected-space.md) makes it constant. The total [covering space](covering-space.md) need not be connected. On a [path-connected](path-connected-space.md) base the [path lifting theorem](path-lifting-theorem.md) gives a [fibre bijection by path lifting](fibre-bijection-by-path-lifting.md). A disconnected base may have different degrees on different [connected components](connected-component.md).
