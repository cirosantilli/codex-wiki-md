# Line subbundle

↑ **Parent:** [Vector bundle](vector-bundle.md)

A line subbundle is an injective map from a [line bundle](line-bundle.md) into a [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) whose quotient is [locally free](locally-free-sheaf.md). Equivalently the injection splits locally. On a [smooth algebraic curve](smooth-algebraic-curve.md), saturation of a rank-one coherent subsheaf produces a [saturated line subbundle on a smooth curve](saturated-line-subbundle-on-a-smooth-curve.md).
