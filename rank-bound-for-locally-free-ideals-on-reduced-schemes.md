# Rank bound for locally free ideals on reduced schemes

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal sheaf of a closed subscheme](ideal-sheaf-of-a-closed-subscheme.md)

A finite [locally free sheaf](locally-free-sheaf.md) of ideals on a [reduced scheme](reduced-scheme.md) has rank at most one at every point. On a nonempty [affine open subscheme](affine-open-subscheme.md) where its rank is $r$, localize its inclusion into the structure sheaf at a [minimal prime ideal](minimal-prime-ideal.md). The resulting [local ring](local-ring.md) is a field $K$, giving an injection $K^r\hookrightarrow K$, hence $r\le1$. This works without a Noetherian assumption. The empty scheme is a vacuous exception to claims phrased as nonexistence of a sheaf of a prescribed rank.
