# Discounted bond price martingale

↑ **Parent:** [Heath-Jarrow-Morton model](heath-jarrow-morton-model.md)

In the one-factor [Heath-Jarrow-Morton model](heath-jarrow-morton-model.md), put $b_t^T=\int_t^T\sigma(t,u)du$. The [Itô formula](ito-s-lemma.md) gives $dP/P=r_tdt-b_t^TdW_t$. Thus multiplying by the [discount factor](discount-factor.md) yields the displayed [stochastic exponential](doleans-dade-exponential.md). Bounded forward volatility on a finite maturity horizon implies the [Novikov condition](novikov-s-condition.md), so the discounted bond is a true [martingale](martingale-split.md).
