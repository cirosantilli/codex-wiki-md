# k-connected graph

↑ **Parent:** [Vertex connectivity](vertex-connectivity.md)

A graph with more than $k$ vertices is $k$-connected when deleting fewer than $k$ vertices always leaves it connected, equivalently when its [vertex connectivity](vertex-connectivity.md) is at least $k$.
