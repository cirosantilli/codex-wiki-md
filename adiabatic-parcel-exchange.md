# Adiabatic parcel exchange

↑ **Parent:** [Baroclinic instability](baroclinic-instability.md)

Exchange two equal [fluid elements](fluid-element.md) of volume $V$ without thermal diffusion in a [Boussinesq approximation](boussinesq-approximation.md). Each retains its [buoyancy](buoyancy.md). If the separation is $(\delta y,\delta z)$ and the background [buoyancy](buoyancy.md) difference is $\Delta b=B\delta y+N^2\delta z$, the pair's [gravitational potential energy](gravitational-energy.md) changes by $\rho_0V\delta z\Delta b$. For $B<0$, $N^2>0$ and poleward-rising slope $s=\delta z/\delta y$, this is negative exactly when $0<s<-B/N^2$. The minimum at fixed $\delta y$ is attained at half the [isopycnal](isopycnal.md) slope. This energetic test alone is not sufficient for dynamical [baroclinic instability](baroclinic-instability.md): rotation and constraints on parcel motion must also be considered.
