# Ultraviolet sensitivity of the one-loop matter power spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Effective field theory of large-scale structure](effective-field-theory-of-large-scale-structure.md)

For internal momentum $q\gg k$, the [P22 contribution to the one-loop matter power spectrum](p22-contribution-to-the-one-loop-matter-power-spectrum.md) starts at $k^4$, while twice the [P13 contribution to the one-loop matter power spectrum](p13-contribution-to-the-one-loop-matter-power-spectrum.md) starts at $k^2P(k)$. The latter has the same momentum dependence as the [effective sound-speed counterterm in large-scale structure](effective-sound-speed-counterterm-in-large-scale-structure.md). Short-scale stochastic contributions start at $k^4$ by mass and [momentum conservation](momentum-conservation.md).
