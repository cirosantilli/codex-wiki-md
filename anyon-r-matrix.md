# Anyon R-matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Anyon fusion space](anyon-fusion-space.md)

The [unitary operator](unitary-operator.md) implementing a chosen positive exchange of adjacent [anyons](anyon.md) $a,b$ in total charge $c$. In a multiplicity-free theory it is a scalar. An inverse crossing uses its inverse. Two positive exchanges give the [anyon monodromy](anyon-monodromy.md) $R^{ab}_cR^{ba}_c$.
