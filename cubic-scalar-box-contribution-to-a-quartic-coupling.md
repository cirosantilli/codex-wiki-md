# Cubic scalar box contribution to a quartic coupling

↑ **Parent:** [Six-dimensional cubic scalar field theory](six-dimensional-cubic-scalar-field-theory.md)

In a [six-dimensional cubic scalar field theory](six-dimensional-cubic-scalar-field-theory.md), three labelled [box Feynman diagrams](box-feynman-diagram.md) give the one-loop local quartic vertex at zero external momentum. With an effective-action term $g_4\phi^4/4!$, their contribution is $\delta g_4=-3g^4I_4$. To check its sign and multiplicity, expand the [one-loop scalar effective action](one-loop-scalar-effective-action.md) as $\tfrac12\operatorname{Tr}\log(D+g\phi)$, where $D=-\partial^2+m^2$. Its fourth-order term is $-g^4\operatorname{Tr}(D^{-1}\phi)^4/8$, giving the stated coefficient. The associated amputated connected diagram insertion has the opposite sign.
