# Complexification fibres of a real scheme

↑ **Parent:** [Base change of a morphism of schemes](base-change-of-a-morphism-of-schemes.md)

For a [scheme](scheme.md) over $\mathbb R$, its [base change of a morphism of schemes](base-change-of-a-morphism-of-schemes.md) to $\mathbb C$ has [scheme-theoretic fibre](scheme-theoretic-fibre.md) over a point $p$ equal to $\operatorname{Spec}(\kappa(p)\otimes_{\mathbb R}\mathbb C)$. This is the spectrum of $\kappa(p)[s]/(s^2+1)$. It has two points if $-1$ is a square in the [residue field](residue-field.md), and otherwise one point with a quadratic [field extension](field-extension.md). In both cases it is reduced, because the quadratic polynomial has no repeated root in [characteristic zero](characteristic-zero.md). The distinction applies to [generic points](generic-point.md) as well as [closed points](closed-point.md).
