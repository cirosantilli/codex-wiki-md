# Two-level observable transition probability

↑ **Parent:** [Born rule](born-rule.md)

For two orthonormal [energy eigenstates](energy-eigenstate.md) $\psi_1,\psi_2$, let an [observable](observable.md) have distinct nondegenerate [eigenstates](eigenstate.md) $\phi_\pm=(\psi_1\pm\psi_2)/\sqrt2$. Starting in $\phi_-$, [unitary time evolution](unitary-time-evolution.md) gives $(e^{-iE_1t/\hbar}\psi_1-e^{-iE_2t/\hbar}\psi_2)/\sqrt2$. Its [probability amplitude](probability-amplitude.md) along $\phi_+$ is $(e^{-iE_1t/\hbar}-e^{-iE_2t/\hbar})/2$. The [Born rule](born-rule.md) therefore gives the stated transition probability. The energy difference determines the oscillation frequency, while a common energy shift has no effect.
