# CDMS experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Dark-matter direct detection](dark-matter-direct-detection.md)

Cryogenic [germanium](germanium.md) or [silicon](silicon.md) detectors combining [phonon](phonon.md) and [ionization](ionization.md) signals. Relative charge yield discriminates [electron](electron.md) and [nuclear recoils](nuclear-recoil.md), while [phonon](phonon.md) timing helps identify surface backgrounds. Lower thresholds extend light-WIMP sensitivity but require renewed efficiency, energy-scale and leakage studies.[detector discussion and lowered-threshold selection](results-from-a-low-energy-analysis-of-the-cdms-ii-germanium-data-by-cdms-collaboration.md)
