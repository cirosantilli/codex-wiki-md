# Orbit dimension formula

↑ **Parent:** [Orbit-stabilizer theorem](orbit-stabilizer-theorem.md)

For an [algebraic group action](algebraic-group-action.md), the orbit dimension is the dimension of the group minus that of its stabilizer. For a finite-dimensional representation, the stabilizer is an automorphism group, open in its [endomorphism ring](endomorphism-ring.md), so their dimensions agree. In a [quiver representation space](quiver-representation-space.md), the [Ringel form](ringel-form.md) turns the orbit codimension into the dimension of the self-extension group.
