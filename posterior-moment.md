# Posterior moment

↑ **Parent:** [Bayesian posterior](bayesian-posterior.md)

A [posterior moment](posterior-moment.md) is a [moment](moment.md) computed under a [posterior distribution](bayesian-posterior.md). Its first raw [moment](moment.md) is the [posterior mean](posterior-mean.md); its second central [moment](moment.md) is the [posterior variance](posterior-variance.md).
