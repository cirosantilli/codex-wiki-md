# Reading frame

↑ **Parent:** [Genetic code](genetic-code.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reading_frame)

A [reading frame](reading-frame.md) specifies how consecutive [nucleotides](nucleotide.md) are grouped into [codons](codon.md). Starting at position 1 of AUGGCUUAA gives AUG / GCU / UAA; starting at position 2 gives UGG / CUU with AA left over. The letters are unchanged but the groups differ. A possible grouping alone does not establish an actual [biological translation](translation-biology.md) initiation site.

For the twelve-[nucleotide](nucleotide.md) example, number positions from the $5'$ end. The three possible forward [reading frames](reading-frame.md) start at positions 1, 2 and 3:

$$
\begin{aligned}
1:&\quad\mathrm{AUG\;GCU\;UUC\;UGA},\\
2:&\quad\mathrm{UGG\;CUU\;UCU}\quad(\mathrm{GA}),\\
3:&\quad\mathrm{GGC\;UUU\;CUG}\quad(\mathrm{A}).
\end{aligned}
$$

Parentheses enclose incomplete trailing groups. The second and third rows do not assert initiation of [biological translation](translation-biology.md) in a [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md). A chosen start fixes which positions belong together; the [ribosome](ribosome.md) then advances through consecutive [codons](codon.md) rather than searching each three-letter window independently.
