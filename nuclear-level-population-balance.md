# Nuclear level population balance

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear level scheme](nuclear-level-scheme.md)

For a complete ensemble of downward [nuclear de-excitation](nuclear-de-excitation.md) cascades, let $F_i$ be the direct population of excited level $i$ and $C_{i\to j}$ the total number of transitions from $i$ to $j$. Conservation of population gives the displayed outward-minus-inward formula. A negative $F_i$ rules out a proposed scheme under the assumed complete transition inventory. [Gamma ray](gamma-ray.md) counts equal transition counts only when unreported [internal conversion](internal-conversion.md) and other competing paths can be neglected or corrected. Summing over all excited levels cancels internal transitions and leaves the transitions into the [ground state](ground-state.md).
