<h1 id="orthonormal-coframe-in-painleve-gullstrand-coordinates">Orthonormal coframe in Painlevé–Gullstrand coordinates</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Painlevé–Gullstrand coordinates](painleve-gullstrand-coordinates.md)

An orthonormal coframe is

$$
e^0=dt,
\qquad e^1=dr+\sqrt{\frac{2M}{r}}\,dt,
\qquad e^2=r\,d\theta,
\qquad e^3=r\sin\theta\,d\phi.
$$
