# Prime-support equivalence relation

↑ **Parent:** [Equivalence relation induced by a function](equivalence-relation-induced-by-a-function.md)

Positive [integers](integer.md) are equivalent when they have the same finite set of [prime factors](prime-factor.md). Equivalently, each divides a positive power of the other. This [equivalence relation](equivalence-relation.md) is induced by the [radical of an integer](radical-of-an-integer.md); its [equivalence classes](equivalence-class.md) correspond to finite prime supports. The empty support gives the singleton $\{1\}$, and every nonempty support gives an infinite class by varying exponents.
