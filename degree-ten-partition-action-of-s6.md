# Degree-ten partition action of S6

↑ **Parent:** [Two-transitive group action](two-transitive-group-action.md)

There are $\binom63/2=10$ partitions of six letters into two unordered triples. A stabilizer is $S_3\wr S_2$, also the [normalizer](normalizer.md) of the [Sylow subgroup](sylow-subgroup.md) generated by one 3-cycle in each triple. Relative to one partition, all other partitions have intersection pattern $1+2$ up to interchange, giving one orbit of the stabilizer and hence a [two-transitive](two-transitive-group-action.md) action. A natural $S_5$ fixing one letter is transitive on these partitions, giving $S_6=S_5(S_3\wr S_2)$.
