# Harmonic functions of Brownian motion

↑ **Parent:** [Harmonic function](harmonic-function.md)

For a $C^2$ [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md) $u$ and [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) stopped before leaving its domain, [Itô formula](ito-s-lemma.md) gives $du(B_t)=\nabla u(B_t)\cdot dB_t+\frac12\Delta u(B_t)dt$. The drift vanishes. On a compact subdomain, the stopped process is a bounded [martingale](martingale-split.md). [Optional stopping theorem](optional-sampling-theorem-for-a-supermartingale.md) at an almost-surely finite exit time therefore gives the boundary mean formula. At the centre of a disk, [rotational invariance of planar Brownian motion](rotational-invariance-of-planar-brownian-motion.md) makes the exit law uniform on the boundary circle. This proves the [mean value property for harmonic functions](mean-value-property-for-harmonic-functions.md) probabilistically.
