<h1 id="balog-szemeredi-gowers-theorem">Balog-Szemerédi-Gowers theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Additive energy](additive-energy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balog-Szemerédi-Gowers_theorem)

If a finite set $A$ has at least $\eta|A|^3$ [additive quadruples](additive-quadruple.md), then it contains $A'$ with $|A'|\geq\eta^C|A|$ and $|A'+A'|\leq\eta^{-C}|A'|$ for an absolute constant $C$.
