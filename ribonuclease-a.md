# Ribonuclease A

↑ **Parent:** [Protein](protein.md)

[Ribonuclease A](ribonuclease-a.md) is an [enzyme](enzyme.md) that catalyzes cleavage of [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md). The bovine pancreatic [ribonuclease A](ribonuclease-a.md) studied by Anfinsen and colleagues has 124 [amino acid residues](amino-acid-residue.md) and four internal [protein disulfide bonds](protein-disulfide-bond.md). It supplied a particularly informative test of the relationship between [protein primary structure](protein-primary-structure.md), [protein folding](protein-folding.md) and biological function: recovery could be measured through restored [enzyme](enzyme.md) activity. See the primary sources under [ribonuclease A refolding experiment](ribonuclease-a-refolding-experiment.md).
