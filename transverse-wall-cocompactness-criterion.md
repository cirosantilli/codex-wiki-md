# Transverse-wall cocompactness criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Cubulation of a group](cubulation-of-a-group.md)

The action of $G$ on the dual cube complex is cocompact exactly when there are finitely many $G$-orbits of finite transverse wall collections. Equivalently, there is a uniform bound on their sizes and, for every size, only finitely many orbits.
