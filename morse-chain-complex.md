# Morse chain complex

↑ **Parent:** [Morse theory](morse-theory.md)

The free chain group in degree $i$ has one oriented generator for each index-$i$ [critical point](critical-point.md). A [cellular chain complex](cellular-chain-complex.md) obtained from the [Morse handle-attachment theorem](morse-handle-attachment-theorem.md) computes the integral [homology](homology-split.md). Under the [Morse-Smale gradient flow](morse-smale-gradient-flow.md) condition its differential can instead be obtained by signed counts of connecting trajectories. Over a field, if $r_i$ is the rank of $\partial_i$, then $c_i=b_i+r_i+r_{i+1}$. This identity implies both weak and strong [Morse inequalities](morse-inequalities.md).
