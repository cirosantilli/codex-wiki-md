# Convex regular spherical polygon

↑ **Parent:** [Spherical geometry](spherical-geometry.md)

A nondegenerate convex regular spherical $n$-gon contained in an [open hemisphere](open-hemisphere.md) has equal interior angles $\alpha$ in the displayed interval. The [spherical excess formula](spherical-excess-formula.md) gives area $n\alpha-(n-2)\pi>0$. Conversely, put equally spaced vertices at colatitude $t\in(0,\pi/2)$ around a pole. Dividing each central triangle in half gives $\cot(\alpha/2)=\cos t\tan(\pi/n)$ by the [spherical law of cosines](spherical-law-of-cosines.md). As $t$ increases, the angle ranges continuously from the Euclidean limiting angle to $\pi$.
