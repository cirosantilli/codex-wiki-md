# Principal normal vector

↑ **Parent:** [Frenet frame](frenet-frame.md)

For a unit-speed [curve](curve.md) with positive [curvature of a space curve](curvature-of-a-space-curve.md) $\kappa$, the principal normal is $N=T^{\prime}/\kappa$, with [unit tangent vector](unit-tangent-vector.md) $T$. It is perpendicular to $T$ and points in the direction in which the tangent is turning. At zero curvature this formula does not define $N$.
