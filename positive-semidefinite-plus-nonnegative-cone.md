# Positive-semidefinite-plus-nonnegative cone

↑ **Parent:** [Copositive cone](copositive-cone.md)

The sums $P+N$ of a real [positive semidefinite matrix](positive-semidefinite-matrix.md) $P$ and a symmetric [nonnegative matrix](nonnegative-matrix.md) $N$ form a [convex cone](convex-cone.md) inside the [copositive cone](copositive-cone.md). Both terms have nonnegative [quadratic forms](quadratic-form.md) on the [nonnegative orthant](nonnegative-orthant.md). The [Horn copositive matrix](horn-copositive-matrix.md) shows that the inclusion is strict in dimension five; the [sum of squares criterion for a biquadratic form](sum-of-squares-criterion-for-a-biquadratic-form.md) explains this cone's relation to [semidefinite programming](semidefinite-programming.md).
