# Centrifuge rotor

↑ **Parent:** [Centrifugation](centrifugation.md)

A [centrifuge rotor](centrifuge-rotor.md) holds sample vessels around the rotation axis. A fixed-angle [centrifuge rotor](centrifuge-rotor.md) holds tubes at a fixed inclination; a swinging-bucket [centrifuge rotor](centrifuge-rotor.md) lets them swing outward during rotation. Material moves away from the axis, so a [centrifugation pellet](centrifugation-pellet.md) can first collect against an outer tube wall rather than travel along the entire tube axis. Geometry sets both the [rotor radius](rotor-radius.md) and the travel distance.[Basics: Centrifugal Force & Centrifuge Type, Fixed-angle or swing-bucket rotors? and Fixed-angle rotor: tube orientation and pellet position](basics-centrifugal-force-and-centrifuge-type-by-eppendorf.md)
