# Egyptian fraction

↑ **Parent:** [Unit fraction](unit-fraction.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egyptian_fraction)

An [Egyptian fraction](egyptian-fraction.md) expresses a nonnegative [rational number](rational-number.md) as a finite [sum](sum.md) of positive [unit fractions](unit-fraction.md). A computational representation may permit repeated denominators, while the classical distinct-denominator convention adds a further requirement. The empty sum represents zero. For example, $3/7=1/3+1/11+1/231$; each denominator is a positive [integer](integer-split.md). A representation that permits repetition also has the simple expansion $n/d=\sum_{j=1}^{n}1/d$ for nonnegative [integers](integer-split.md) $n$ and positive $d$.
