# Three-term calorimeter resolution

↑ **Parent:** [Electromagnetic calorimeter](electromagnetic-calorimeter.md)

Independent [stochastic](statistical-fluctuation.md), approximately fixed-noise and fractional-response contributions add in [variance](variance-split.md). The relative resolution is $A/\sqrt E\oplus B/E\oplus C$. Signal-counting and sampling fluctuations enter $A$; fixed equivalent [energy](energy-split.md) [noise](noise-signal-processing.md) enters $B$; residual response nonuniformity enters $C$. The powers approximate a fitted [energy](energy-split.md) range rather than describing every leakage or [Pile-up](collision-pileup.md) effect exactly.
