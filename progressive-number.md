# Progressive number

↑ **Parent:** [Positive integer](positive-integer.md)

A [positive integer](positive-integer.md) $n$ for which some division $n=dq+r$ with [integers](integer-split.md) $d,q,r>0$ and $r<d$ has $d,q,r$ as consecutive terms of a [geometric progression](geometric-progression.md) in some order. Writing a reduced [common ratio](common-ratio.md) as $a/b$ with $a>b>0$ and $\gcd(a,b)=1$, the three terms have the form $tb^2,tab,ta^2$ for a [positive integer](positive-integer.md) $t$.
