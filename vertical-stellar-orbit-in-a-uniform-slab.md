# Vertical stellar orbit in a uniform slab

↑ **Parent:** [Self-gravitating incompressible slab](self-gravitating-incompressible-slab.md)

A test [star](star.md) inside an infinite planar slab of uniform [mass density](density.md) $\rho$ experiences acceleration $\ddot z=-4\pi G\rho z$. Reflection symmetry gives zero vertical [gravitational field](gravitational-field.md) at the mid-plane, and integrating the gravitational [Poisson equation](poisson-s-equation.md) gives the field linear in height. The interior orbit is therefore [simple harmonic motion](simple-harmonic-motion.md); release from rest at any height within the slab gives its first mid-plane crossing after $\pi/(2\sqrt{4\pi G\rho})$. Finite radial extent, a varying vertical [mass density](density.md) or leaving the slab changes this result.
