# Blocking pair in stable matching

↑ **Parent:** [Stable matching in a bipartite graph](stable-matching-in-a-bipartite-graph.md)

An acceptable unused [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) whose endpoints both prefer each other to their partners in a [matching in a graph](matching-graph-theory-split.md), treating every acceptable partner as preferable to being unmatched. A [stable matching](stable-matching-in-a-bipartite-graph.md) has no such [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md). In the worked example, $(B,Y)$ blocks $N$ even though $A$ already has its first choice.
