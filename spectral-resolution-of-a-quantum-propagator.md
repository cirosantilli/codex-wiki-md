# Spectral resolution of a quantum propagator

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum propagator](quantum-propagator.md)

Insert a complete resolution of identity in [energy eigenstates](energy-eigenstate.md) on either side of the [time-evolution operator](time-evolution-operator.md). Each state evolves by its scalar [energy](energy-split.md) phase, yielding the displayed [quantum propagator](quantum-propagator.md). Its spectral sum includes an integral over any [continuous spectrum](continuous-spectrum.md), with the measure fixed by the continuum normalization. Omitting the [scattering states](scattering-state.md) when [bound states](bound-state.md) are also present destroys completeness and the delta-function short-time limit.
