# Aquifer drainage hydrograph

↑ **Parent:** [Aquifer](aquifer.md)

An [aquifer drainage hydrograph](aquifer-drainage-hydrograph.md) plots the time-dependent discharge from an [aquifer](aquifer.md). In the shallow constant-recharge model, early discharge grows linearly according to the [rain-fed aquifer similarity solution](rain-fed-aquifer-similarity-solution.md). Sustained recharge $R$ on a finite length $L$ gives eventual steady discharge $RL$ when the other end is impermeable. After recharge stops, a [separable aquifer drawdown](separable-aquifer-drawdown.md) has height proportional to $1/(t+t_0)$ and discharge proportional to $1/(t+t_0)^2$; this is generally a late-time description rather than an exact solution for arbitrary inherited height profiles.
