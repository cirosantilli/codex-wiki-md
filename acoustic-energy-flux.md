# Acoustic energy flux

↑ **Parent:** [Continuum mechanics](continuum-mechanics-split.md)

For harmonic acoustic amplitudes, $\langle\mathbf I\rangle=\frac12\operatorname{Re}(p\mathbf u^*)$.

The instantaneous acoustic energy flux is $p\mathbf u$ for linear acoustics. Its time average is the [sound intensity](sound-intensity.md).
