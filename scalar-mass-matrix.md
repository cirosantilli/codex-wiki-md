# Scalar mass matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Spontaneous symmetry breaking](spontaneous-symmetry-breaking.md)

The scalar mass matrix is the [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) of the [scalar potential](scalar-potential.md) at a vacuum. Its eigenvalues are the tree-level squared masses; broken symmetry directions are zero eigenvectors before a [Higgs mechanism](higgs-mechanism.md) or explicit symmetry breaking removes the corresponding massless modes.
