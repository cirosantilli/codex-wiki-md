# Plumb-line deflection from cylindrical gravity

↑ **Parent:** [Gravitational field of an infinite uniform cylinder](gravitational-field-of-an-infinite-uniform-cylinder.md)

A pendulum of length $L$ in vertical [gravity](gravity.md) $g$ is weakly attracted horizontally by an added long circular cylinder of line [density](density.md) $\lambda$. Outside it, $g_h=2G\lambda/r$ at axis distance $r$. Small-angle equilibrium gives horizontal deflection $d\simeq Lg_h/g=2GL\lambda/(rg)$. Removing the cylinder's [mass](mass.md) reverses the change. It is a before/after differential estimate; finite length, a nonhorizontal radial direction and mechanical motion require corrections.
