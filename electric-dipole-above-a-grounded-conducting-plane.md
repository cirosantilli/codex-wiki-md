# Electric dipole above a grounded conducting plane

↑ **Parent:** [Method of images](method-of-images.md)

An ideal [electric dipole](electric-dipole.md) at height $a$ above a grounded plane has an image [electric dipole moment](electric-dipole-moment.md) with reversed tangential component and unchanged normal component. If $\theta$ is its angle to the normal, its conductor-response [potential energy](potential-energy.md) is the displayed expression. The factor one half in $U=-\mathbf p\cdot\mathbf E_{\rm im}/2$ accounts for induced [surface charge](surface-charge.md). Therefore its downward [force](force-split.md) magnitude is $3p^2(1+\cos^2\theta)/(64\pi\epsilon_0a^4)$, and its orienting [torque](torque.md) follows from $-\partial_\theta U$. The two normal orientations are stable against tilting; the parallel orientation is unstable. The net induced plane [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) is zero for a neutral source, although its local [surface charge density](surface-charge-density.md) is nonzero.
