# Pull-off force

↑ **Parent:** [Contact mechanics](contact-mechanics.md)

The tensile [force](force-split.md) magnitude needed to detach contacting surfaces during retraction. A force-probe measurement reads it from the [force](force-split.md) minimum relative to the noncontact baseline; roughness, capillary [forces](force-split.md) and contact geometry affect its interpretation.
