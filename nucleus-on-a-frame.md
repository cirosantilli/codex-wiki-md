# Nucleus on a frame

↑ **Parent:** [Complete Heyting algebra](complete-heyting-algebra.md)

A nucleus is an inflationary, idempotent, finite-meet-preserving map on a [frame](complete-heyting-algebra.md). Its fixed elements form a [frame](complete-heyting-algebra.md), whose joins are obtained by applying $j$ to ambient joins. The induced [locale](locale.md) is a [sublocale](sublocale.md). The [nuclei on a frame](nucleus-on-a-frame.md) themselves form a [frame](complete-heyting-algebra.md) in pointwise order; their meets are pointwise, but their joins usually are not.
