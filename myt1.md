# Myt1

↑ **Parent:** [CDK1 phosphorylation state](cdk1-phosphorylation-state.md)

[Myt1](myt1.md) is a [protein kinase](protein-kinase.md) distinct from [Wee1](wee1.md). The cloned [Xenopus laevis](xenopus-laevis.md) [Myt1](myt1.md) phosphorylated [Cdc2](cyclin-dependent-kinase-1.md) at both inhibitory sites Thr14 and Tyr15; depletion experiments implicated it as the predominant Thr14-directed activity in [Xenopus laevis egg extracts](xenopus-laevis-egg-extract.md).[Mueller, Coleman, Kumagai and Dunphy (1995), abstract: Xenopus clone, both inhibitory sites and immunodepletion result](myt1-a-membrane-associated-inhibitory-kinase-that-phosphorylates-cdc2-on-both-threonine-14-and-tyrosine-15-by-p-r-mueller-and-others.md)
