# Finite order

↑ **Parent:** [Order of a group element](order-of-a-group-element.md)

A [group](group-split.md) element has [finite order](finite-order.md) if some positive integer power equals the [identity element](identity-element.md). Its [order of a group element](order-of-a-group-element.md) is the least such positive integer.
