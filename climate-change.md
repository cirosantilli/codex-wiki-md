# Climate change

↑ **Parent:** [Geophysics](geophysics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climate_change)

[Climate change](climate-change.md) is a persistent alteration of the [statistics](statistics.md) of a [planet](planet.md)'s climate. External [radiative forcing](radiative-forcing.md), internal variability and [climate feedbacks](climate-feedback.md) all matter, and the timescale must be specified. A [climate model](climate-model.md) connects mechanisms to observable changes; the [radiative-convective climate column](radiative-convective-climate-column.md) separates vertical [heat transfer](heat-transfer.md) from whole-planet [conservation of energy](conservation-of-energy.md), while the [Hasselmann stochastic climate model](hasselmann-stochastic-climate-model.md) explains how fast weather can drive slow thermal variability.
