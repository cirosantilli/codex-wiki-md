# Climate change

↑ **Parent:** [Geophysics](geophysics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climate_change)

Climate change is a persistent alteration of the statistical behaviour of a planet's climate. External forcing and [climate feedbacks](climate-feedback.md) both matter, and the timescale must be specified.
