# Partial derivatives and superfield covariance

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar superfield transformation](scalar-superfield-transformation.md)

A [supersymmetry transformation](supersymmetry-transformation.md) mixes the bosonic and odd coordinates of [superspace](superspace.md). A bare derivative with respect to an odd coordinate therefore acquires a spacetime-derivative term under the coordinate Jacobian and is not a covariant spinor [superfield](superfield.md). [Supersymmetric covariant derivatives](supersymmetric-covariant-derivative.md) add terms linear in the other odd coordinate; their graded anticommutation with the [supercharges](supersymmetry-generator.md) makes their application to a scalar [superfield](superfield.md) transform covariantly.
