# Turbulent dissipation anomaly

↑ **Parent:** [Zeroth law of turbulence](zeroth-law-of-turbulence.md)

Although the local [viscous dissipation](viscous-dissipation.md) is $2\nu S_{ij}S_{ij}$, increasingly intense [velocity gradients](velocity-gradient.md) can compensate for vanishing [kinematic viscosity](kinematic-viscosity.md). A [Burgers vortex](burgers-vortex.md) illustrates this mechanism: its radius shrinks while its excess integrated [viscous dissipation](viscous-dissipation.md) remains finite. Such a local model does not by itself prove the [zeroth law of turbulence](zeroth-law-of-turbulence.md) for a whole flow.
