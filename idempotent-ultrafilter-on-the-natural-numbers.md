# Idempotent ultrafilter on the natural numbers

↑ **Parent:** [Addition on the Stone-Čech compactification of the natural numbers](addition-on-the-stone-cech-compactification-of-the-natural-numbers.md)

An ultrafilter $\mathcal U\in\beta\mathbb N$ is idempotent when $\mathcal U+\mathcal U=\mathcal U$. The [Ellis–Numakura lemma](ellis-numakura-lemma.md) guarantees such an ultrafilter and supplies the main algebraic input to the [Idempotent-ultrafilter proof of Hindman's theorem](idempotent-ultrafilter-proof-of-hindman-s-theorem.md).
