# Ward identity contact terms

↑ **Parent:** [Ward identity](ward-identity.md)

Localizing an infinitesimal symmetry inside a normalized [path integral](path-integral.md) turns current conservation into a distributional identity. The variation of each inserted field contributes a delta function at that insertion. The displayed sign uses $\delta S=-\int(\partial_\mu\epsilon^a)j_a^\mu$; the opposite current convention reverses the left side. Measure anomalies or noninvariant boundary states can add terms.
