# Canonical completion of independent commuting functions

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical transformation](canonical-transformation.md)

Independent functions $Q_1,\ldots,Q_n$ on a $2n$-dimensional [symplectic manifold](symplectic-manifold.md) can locally form the position half of canonical coordinates exactly when $\{Q_i,Q_j\}=0$ throughout a neighbourhood. Their commuting [Hamiltonian vector fields](hamiltonian-vector-field.md) span the fibres of $Q$. Flow from a transverse section gives coordinates $(Q,t)$ with $\omega=\sum dt_i\wedge dQ_i+\beta(Q)$. Write $\beta=d\alpha$ locally and take $P_i=-t_i-\alpha_i(Q)$. Then $\omega=\sum dQ_i\wedge dP_i$.
