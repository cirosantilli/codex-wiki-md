# Homogeneous Sobolev space

↑ **Parent:** [Sobolev space](sobolev-space-split.md)

A homogeneous Sobolev space controls derivatives without an independent low-frequency [L2 norm](l2-norm.md): formally $\|f\|_{\dot H^s}^2=\int |\xi|^{2s}|\widehat f(\xi)|^2\,d\xi$, with the normalization chosen according to the [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md). Completions or quotient conventions must be specified when polynomial ambiguities occur. In three dimensions $\dot H^1$ is represented by $L^6$ functions with [gradient](gradient.md) in $L^2$, and $\|f\|_6\leq C\|\nabla f\|_2$.
