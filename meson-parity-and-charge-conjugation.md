# Meson parity and charge conjugation

↑ **Parent:** [Meson](meson.md)

For an ordinary [quark](quark.md)-[antiquark](antiquark.md) [meson](meson.md), opposite [intrinsic parities](intrinsic-parity.md) and [orbital parity](orbital-parity.md) give $P=(-1)^{L+1}$. For a self-conjugate flavor state, [charge conjugation](charge-conjugation.md) exchanges the two constituents. Reversal of their relative momentum gives $(-1)^L$, spin exchange gives $(-1)^{S+1}$, and reordering the two fermionic creation operators gives one extra minus sign. Their product is $C=(-1)^{L+S}$. Electrical neutrality alone does not imply that a state is an eigenstate of charge conjugation; charged mesons have no individual C eigenvalue.
