# Conjugate-linear Hodge star

↑ **Parent:** [Hodge star operator](hodge-star-operator.md)

For a [Hermitian manifold](hermitian-manifold.md) with the pointwise [Hermitian inner product](hermitian-form.md) linear in the first variable, define the conjugate-linear star by $\alpha\wedge *\beta=\langle\alpha,\beta\rangle\,\omega^n/n!$. It is the usual real [Hodge star operator](hodge-star-operator.md) extended complex-linearly and then composed with complex conjugation. It has the displayed bidegree and satisfies $*^2=(-1)^{p+q}$ on $(p,q)$-forms.
