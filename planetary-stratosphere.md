# Planetary stratosphere

↑ **Parent:** [Inversion (meteorology)](inversion-meteorology.md)

An upper atmospheric region above the [tropopause](tropopause.md) in which shortwave heating can produce an [atmospheric thermal inversion](inversion-meteorology.md). [Ozone](ozone.md) on [Earth](earth.md), and [methane](methane.md) and aerosols on giant planets, are examples of absorbers that help heat it. Its positive altitude [temperature gradient](temperature-gradient.md) makes it stable against homogeneous-gas thermal [convection](convection.md).
