# Western blot

↑ **Parent:** [Protein](protein-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_blot)

A [Western blot](western-blot.md) detects selected [proteins](protein-split.md) after their separation and transfer to a membrane, using [antibodies](antibody.md). In the [yeast cross-species complementation experiment](yeast-cross-species-complementation-experiment.md), detection helps distinguish failed [protein](protein-split.md) production from failure of an expressed [protein](protein-split.md) to restore growth.

The useful idea is to preserve where [proteins](protein-split.md) migrated in [gel electrophoresis](gel-electrophoresis.md), then ask which positions retain an [antibody](antibody.md). [Western blot separation](western-blot-separation.md), [Western blot transfer](western-blot-transfer.md) and [Western blot antibody detection](western-blot-antibody-detection.md) answer different questions. A band is evidence of retained label at a position; [Western blot band interpretation](western-blot-band-interpretation.md) requires [Western blot controls](western-blot-controls.md) before assigning identity or comparing abundance.
