# Deflated wealth equation with consumption

↑ **Parent:** [State-price density](state-price-density.md)

A [self-financing](self-financing-portfolio.md) investor with [consumption](consumption.md) satisfies $X=H\cdot P$ and $dX=H\cdot dP-cdt$. The [Itô product rule](ito-product-rule.md) and $d[Y,X]=H\cdot d[Y,P]$ give the displayed equation. For a positive [local martingale deflator](local-martingale-deflator.md) $Y$, the first term is a [stochastic integral](stochastic-integral.md) against the vector of deflated asset-price [local martingales](local-martingale.md), not their current levels.
