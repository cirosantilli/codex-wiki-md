# Weibull accelerated-life and proportional-hazards families

↑ **Parent:** [Accelerated life family](accelerated-life-family.md)

[Weibull distributions](weibull-distribution.md) with a common shape $k>0$ form both an [accelerated life family](accelerated-life-family.md) and a [proportional hazards family](proportional-hazards-family.md). Their [hazard functions](hazard-function.md) are $h_z(t)=k\lambda_z t^{k-1}$, whose ratio is $\lambda_z/\lambda_0$. Their time multiplier is $(\lambda_0/\lambda_z)^{1/k}$. In the representation $\log T_z=a+bz+cX$ with $c>0$ and density $e^{x-e^x}$ for $X$, the parameters are $k=1/c$ and $\lambda_z=e^{-(a+bz)/c}$, giving time multiplier $e^{bz}$ and hazard multiplier $e^{-bz/c}$.
