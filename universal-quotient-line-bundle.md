# Universal quotient line bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Projectivization by quotients](projectivization-by-quotients.md)

On the [projectivization by quotients](projectivization-by-quotients.md), the fibre over a one-dimensional quotient of $E_x$ is that quotient line itself. These lines glue to the universal quotient. Its restriction to a projective fibre has degree one. It is the dual of the relative tautological line after identifying quotient projectivization of $E$ with line projectivization of $E^*$.
