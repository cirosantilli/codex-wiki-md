# Second-order arithmetic

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical logic](mathematical-logic-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second-order_arithmetic)

[Second-order arithmetic](second-order-arithmetic.md) has variables for [natural numbers](natural-number.md) and for sets of [natural numbers](natural-number.md). Subsystems restrict comprehension and induction; [arithmetical transfinite recursion theory](arithmetical-transfinite-recursion-theory.md) is one important subsystem stronger than the first-order system [Peano arithmetic](peano-arithmetic.md).
