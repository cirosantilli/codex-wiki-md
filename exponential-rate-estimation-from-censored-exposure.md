# Exponential-rate estimation from censored exposure

↑ **Parent:** [Survival likelihood](survival-likelihood.md)

Under [independent censoring](independent-censoring.md) and a constant event rate, $d$ observed events over total observed [person-time](person-time.md) $X$ give likelihood proportional to $\lambda^d e^{-\lambda X}$ and estimate $d/X$ for $d,X>0$. Censored observations belong in $X$. With no events the positive-rate likelihood has a boundary supremum.
