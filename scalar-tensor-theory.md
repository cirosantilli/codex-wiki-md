# Scalar-tensor theory

↑ **Parent:** [Modified gravity](modified-gravity.md)

A [scalar-tensor theory](scalar-tensor-theory.md) uses a [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md) and an additional [scalar field](scalar-field.md) to mediate gravity. A [scalar](scalar.md) multiplier of the [Ricci scalar](ricci-scalar.md) makes the [curvature](curvature.md) coupling depend on that field. The [Brans-Dicke theory](brans-dicke-theory.md) is a standard example; the choice of [scalar](scalar.md) potential and kinetic coupling distinguishes different members of the class.
