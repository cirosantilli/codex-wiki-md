<h1 id="o-n-invariant-s-matrix">O(N)-invariant S-matrix</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Factorized scattering](factorized-scattering.md)

For particles in the [vector representation](vector-representation.md) of the [orthogonal group](orthogonal-group.md), the two-body [S-matrix](s-matrix.md) is a linear combination of the identity $I$, permutation $P$ and trace contraction $K$: $S=S_2I+S_3P+S_1K$. Here $K(e_i\otimes e_j)=\delta_{ij}\sum_a e_a\otimes e_a$, $P^2=I$, $PK=KP=K$ and $K^2=NK$. The [orthogonal-invariant scattering channels](orthogonal-invariant-scattering-channels.md) diagonalize these three operators simultaneously.
