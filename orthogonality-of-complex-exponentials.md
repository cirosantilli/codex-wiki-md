# Orthogonality of complex exponentials

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier analysis](fourier-analysis-split.md)

For [integers](integer.md) $m$ and $n$,

$$
\int_0^1 e^{2\pi imx}\overline{e^{2\pi inx}}\,dx=
\begin{cases}1,&m=n,\\0,&m\ne n.\end{cases}
$$

This orthogonality extracts matching coefficients when two [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md) are integrated over one period.
