# Supermultiplet

↑ **Parent:** [Four-dimensional N=1 supersymmetry](four-dimensional-n-1-supersymmetry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supermultiplet)

A supermultiplet is an irreducible representation of the [Super-Poincaré algebra](super-poincare-algebra.md). Conserved supercharges pair its bosonic and fermionic states at equal four-momentum.
