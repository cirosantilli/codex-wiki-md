# QED one-loop running-mass to pole-mass conversion

↑ **Parent:** [Fermion self-energy](fermion-self-energy.md)

In [quantum electrodynamics](quantum-electrodynamics.md), the [modified minimal subtraction scheme](modified-minimal-subtraction-scheme.md) running mass differs from the perturbative [pole mass](pole-mass.md) by a finite correction. The displayed relation follows by evaluating the renormalized [fermion self-energy](fermion-self-energy.md) at the mass shell and imposing a zero of the inverse [Dirac propagator](dirac-propagator.md). The sign assigned to the self-energy insertion must be matched to its denominator convention; changing that naming convention does not change the conversion. A wave-function coefficient counterterm and multiplicative mass renormalization are also distinct: the coefficient of the mass term includes both at first order.
