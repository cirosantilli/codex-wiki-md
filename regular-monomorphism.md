# Regular monomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Strong monomorphism](strong-monomorphism.md)

A regular monomorphism is a morphism that occurs as the [equalizer](equaliser.md) of some parallel pair. Every regular monomorphism is a [strong monomorphism](strong-monomorphism.md).

This is the equalizer-defined subclass of [monomorphisms](monomorphism.md).
