# Elliptope

↑ **Parent:** [Positive semidefinite cone](positive-semidefinite-cone.md)

The set of real [positive semidefinite matrices](positive-semidefinite-matrix.md) with unit diagonal. Equivalently, its members are [Gram matrices](gram-matrix.md) of unit [vectors](vector.md), by the [real spectral theorem](real-spectral-theorem.md). Every two-by-two [principal minor](principal-minor.md) gives $|X_{ij}|\leq1$, so the set is bounded; it is also closed, hence [compact](compact-space.md) in finite [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md). This gives attainment of linear objectives used in [semidefinite programming](semidefinite-programming.md).
