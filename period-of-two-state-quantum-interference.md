# Period of two-state quantum interference

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum interference](quantum-interference.md)

A superposition of two distinct [energy eigenstates](energy-eigenstate.md) gives the [expectation value](expectation-value.md) of a time-independent [observable](observable.md) an interference term proportional to $e^{-i(E_2-E_1)t/\hbar}$. The displayed beat period applies when that term has nonzero amplitude. A vanishing off-diagonal [matrix element](matrix-element.md), zero state coefficient or equal [energies](energy-split.md) instead gives a constant [expectation value](expectation-value.md).
