# Long-wave convection equation with broken Boussinesq symmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Convection](convection.md)

A reduced long-wave [convection](convection.md) model can combine linear damping, destabilizing second derivatives, stabilizing fourth derivatives and gradient nonlinearities. In the displayed normalization the quadratic coefficient $s$ breaks [Boussinesq symmetry](boussinesq-up-down-symmetry.md), while the cubic gradient term dissipates the mean-square temperature. The linear [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation.md) is $\lambda(k)=-1+\mu k^2-k^4$, with critical [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) $k=1$ at $\mu=2$. Smoothness and spatial averaging hypotheses must be specified before applying the [energy method](energy-method.md) on an infinite interval.
