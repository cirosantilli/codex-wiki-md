# State degeneracy

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical physics](statistical-physics-split.md)

This counts [microstates](microstate.md) sharing a [macrostate](macrostate.md); when the shared quantity is energy, it gives the multiplicity of [degenerate energy levels](degenerate-energy-levels.md). The degeneracy $g(l)$ of a [macrostate](macrostate.md) labelled by $l$ is the number of distinct [microstates](microstate.md) having that value of $l$.
