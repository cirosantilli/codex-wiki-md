# Finite Hilbert transform

↑ **Parent:** [Hilbert transform](hilbert-transform.md)

The finite Hilbert transform is a [singular integral operator](singular-integral-operator.md) whose integration is restricted to a finite interval. The displayed convention uses the denominator $t-x$; reversing that denominator reverses the sign. The [Cauchy principal value](cauchy-principal-value.md) deletes equal intervals on the two sides of the interior point $x$. Endpoint behavior matters: the integrable [function](function-split.md) $1/\sqrt{x(1-x)}$ belongs to the null space for this interval. This nontrivial null space is one reason that an inversion formula needs a normalization or a stated endpoint class.
