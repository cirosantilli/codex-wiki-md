# Cataclysmic-variable period minimum

↑ **Parent:** [Cataclysmic-variable orbital-period distribution](cataclysmic-variable-orbital-period-distribution.md)

For a [Roche lobe](roche-lobe.md)-filling donor with local radius response $R_2\propto M_2^\zeta$, the [Roche-lobe-filling period-density relation](roche-lobe-filling-period-density-relation.md) gives $P\propto M_2^{(3\zeta-1)/2}$. As mass is lost, the period falls for $\zeta>1/3$ and rises for $\zeta<1/3$, with a minimum at the transition. The reversal is called a period bounce. Slow evolution near the turning point produces an accumulation in the period distribution: for a steady flow of systems along one evolutionary branch, $N(P)\propto1/|\dot P|$.
