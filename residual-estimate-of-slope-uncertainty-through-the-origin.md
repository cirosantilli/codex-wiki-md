# Residual estimate of slope uncertainty through the origin

↑ **Parent:** [Linear regression through the origin](linear-regression-through-the-origin.md)

For independent errors of common unknown [variance](variance-split.md) in $Y_i=\beta x_i+\varepsilon_i$, the [ordinary least squares](ordinary-least-squares.md) estimate is $\widehat\beta=\sum_i x_iY_i/\sum_i x_i^2$. Fitting one parameter leaves $n-1$ residual [degrees of freedom](degree-of-freedom.md). Therefore $s^2=\sum_i(Y_i-\widehat\beta x_i)^2/(n-1)$ estimates the common error [variance](variance-split.md), and $s^2/\sum_i x_i^2$ estimates the slope [variance](variance-split.md). For errors with a [Gaussian distribution](normal-distribution.md), the slope's standardized deviation follows a [Student's t-distribution](student-s-t-distribution.md) with $n-1$ [degrees of freedom](degree-of-freedom.md). A common [calibration](calibration-differential-geometry.md) bias does not appear as independent residual scatter.
