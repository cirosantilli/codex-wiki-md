# Hydrogenic acceptor state

↑ **Parent:** [Acceptor impurity](acceptor-impurity.md)

A shallow acceptor can bind an [electron hole](electron-hole.md) through the screened [Coulomb potential](coulomb-potential-energy.md) of a negatively charged impurity. A scalar [band effective mass](band-effective-mass.md) approximation gives a hydrogen-like bound-state ladder. Actual valence-band degeneracy and anisotropy can split levels and limit that approximation.
