# Snell law for electromagnetic waves

↑ **Parent:** [Phase matching at a planar wave interface](phase-matching-at-a-planar-wave-interface.md)

For refractive indices $n_-$ and $n_+$,

$$
\theta_R=\theta_I,
\qquad
n_-\sin\theta_I=n_+\sin\theta_T.
$$
