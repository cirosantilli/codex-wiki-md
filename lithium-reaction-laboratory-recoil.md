# Lithium reaction laboratory recoil

↑ **Parent:** [Lithium proton reaction energy](lithium-proton-reaction-energy.md)

Equal product [masses](mass.md) imply equal [kinetic energies](kinetic-energy.md) and opposite [momenta](momentum-split.md) in the [center of mass](center-of-mass.md) frame. The laboratory frame has nonzero initial [momentum](momentum-split.md) because the [proton](proton.md) is moving. [Conservation of momentum](momentum-conservation.md) therefore forbids the two laboratory [momenta](momentum-split.md) from always being equal and opposite. The products are equal in laboratory [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) only for particular emission directions, not for every event.

An exact two-body calculation makes this distinction quantitative. Write $a=m_pc^2$, $b=m_{\mathrm{Li}}c^2$, $h=m_\alpha c^2$, and $T$ for incident [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md). Express every quantity below in the same [energy](energy-split.md) units; $\mathcal P=pc$ is [momentum](momentum-split.md) times the [speed of light](speed-of-light.md). For a stationary target,

$$
\mathcal P=\sqrt{T(T+2a)},\quad
W=\sqrt{(a+b)^2+2bT},\quad
\beta=\frac{\mathcal P}{a+b+T},\quad
\gamma=\frac{a+b+T}{W}.
$$

Here $W$ is the [center-of-mass energy](center-of-mass-energy.md), and $\beta$ is the center-of-mass [speed](speed.md) divided by the [speed of light](speed-of-light.md). Each outgoing [alpha particle](alpha-particle.md) has center-of-mass total [energy](energy-split.md) $E_*=W/2$ and momentum-times-$c$ magnitude $\mathcal P_*=\sqrt{E_*^2-h^2}$. A [Lorentz transformation](lorentz-transformation.md) gives the paired laboratory [kinetic energies](kinetic-energy.md), with $\theta_*$ measured from the incident beam in the [center of mass](center-of-mass.md) frame:

$$
K_1=\gamma(E_*+\beta\mathcal P_*\cos\theta_*)-h,\qquad
K_2=\gamma(E_*-\beta\mathcal P_*\cos\theta_*)-h.
$$

Their sum is independent of $\theta_*$ and equals $Q+T$. Their difference is $2\gamma\beta\mathcal P_*\cos\theta_*$; the symmetry belongs to the product interchange and the [center of mass](center-of-mass.md) frame.

For the chosen $T=0.500\,\mathrm{MeV}$ model, subtract $Z$ electron rest [masses](mass.md) from each neutral-atom mass, neglect electronic [binding energies](binding-energy.md), and use $m_ec^2=0.51099895069\,\mathrm{MeV}$.[CODATA 2022 wall chart, electron mass, energy equivalent in MeV](codata-recommended-values-of-the-fundamental-physical-constants-2022-by-national-institute-of-standards-and-technology.md) At $\theta_*=0$, the paired laboratory [kinetic energies](kinetic-energy.md) are approximately $9.979$ and $7.867\,\mathrm{MeV}$; at $\theta_*=90^\circ$ both are approximately $8.923\,\mathrm{MeV}$. In the latter case both products have a forward laboratory [momentum](momentum-split.md) component, so even equal laboratory [kinetic energies](kinetic-energy.md) do not imply back-to-back laboratory trajectories. A real thick target and finite detector acceptance would broaden or select these ideal kinematic possibilities.
