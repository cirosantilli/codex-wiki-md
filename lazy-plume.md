# Lazy plume

↑ **Parent:** [Turbulent plume](turbulent-plume-split.md)

A buoyant plume with insufficient momentum for the corresponding [pure plume balance](pure-plume-balance.md). In the constant-entrainment top-hat model it has [plume balance parameter](plume-balance-parameter.md) $\Gamma>1$, unlike the momentum-excess [forced plume](forced-plume.md). The equations accelerate the plume and drive the balance parameter toward one downstream. The definition depends on the chosen flux and [entrainment](fluid-entrainment.md) convention; it is not merely a synonym for slow vertical speed.
