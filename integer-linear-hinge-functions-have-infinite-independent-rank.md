# Integer-linear hinge functions have infinite independent rank

↑ **Parent:** [Piecewise integer-linear representation of a free Abelian lattice-ordered group](piecewise-integer-linear-representation-of-a-free-abelian-lattice-ordered-group.md)

The [piecewise linear functions](piecewise-linear-function.md) $h_n$, for distinct integer indices, are linearly independent over the integers. Restrict a finite integer relation to $y=1$. At $x=n$, only $h_n$ has a jump of one in its slope; hence the coefficient of that function must be zero. This proves that the underlying group of the two-generated [free Abelian lattice-ordered group](free-abelian-lattice-ordered-group.md) has infinite [rank of an abelian group](rank-of-an-abelian-group.md).
