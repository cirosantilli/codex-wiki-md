# Prime chain

↑ **Parent:** [Krull dimension](krull-dimension.md)

A strictly increasing finite sequence of [prime ideals](prime-ideal.md). Its length is the number of strict inclusions, so the displayed chain has length $s$. The [Krull dimension](krull-dimension.md) of a ring is the supremum of such lengths; the [height of a prime ideal](height-of-a-prime-ideal.md) restricts to chains ending at that prime.
