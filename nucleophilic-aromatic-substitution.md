# Nucleophilic aromatic substitution

↑ **Parent:** [Nucleophilic substitution](nucleophilic-substitution.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nucleophilic_aromatic_substitution)

In an addition–elimination pathway, a [nucleophile](nucleophile.md) attacks an aromatic carbon and forms a [Meisenheimer complex](meisenheimer-complex.md); leaving-group loss restores [aromaticity](aromaticity.md). Electron-withdrawing groups ortho or para to the attacked site can stabilize the addition intermediate.
