# Doppler effect

↑ **Parent:** [Wave](wave.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doppler_effect)

The Doppler effect changes the observed [frequency](frequency.md) of a [wave](wave.md) when source and observer move relative to one another. For light and a small radial recession speed $v_r\ll c$, $\Delta\lambda/\lambda\simeq v_r/c$, where $\lambda$ is [wavelength](wavelength.md). This is the basis of [Doppler spectroscopy](doppler-spectroscopy.md).
