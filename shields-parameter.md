# Shields parameter

↑ **Parent:** [Sediment transport](sediment-transport.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shields_parameter)

The [Shields parameter](shields-parameter.md) is $\Theta=\tau/[(\rho_p-\rho_f)gd]$, comparing bed [shear stress](shear-stress.md) with a grain-scale [submerged weight](submerged-weight.md) per area. Its critical value depends on the assumed [drag coefficient](drag-coefficient.md), [static friction](static-friction.md), lift, and contact geometry.
