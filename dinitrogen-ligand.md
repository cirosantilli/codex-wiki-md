# Dinitrogen ligand

↑ **Parent:** [Ligand in coordination chemistry](ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md)

An intact $\mathrm N_2$ group coordinated to a [metal](metal.md). End-on neutral dinitrogen normally counts as a two-electron L-type donor; metal-to-ligand [pi backbonding](pi-backbonding.md) can weaken its N–N bond without making it a fully cleaved nitride.
