# Solar eclipse

↑ **Parent:** [Eclipse](eclipse.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_eclipse)

A solar eclipse occurs when the [Moon](moon.md) obscures part or all of the [Sun](sun.md) as seen from a specified location. Totality allows observations of nearby background [stars](star.md) that ordinary daylight hides.
