# Ultrafilter construction of a Banach limit

↑ **Parent:** [Banach limit](banach-limit.md)

A [nonprincipal ultrafilter](nonprincipal-ultrafilter.md) on the [natural numbers](natural-number.md) gives a [Banach limit](banach-limit.md) by taking the [ultralimit](ultralimit.md) of the [Cesaro means](cesaro-mean.md) of each bounded sequence. Compactness of a closed bounded interval, or disk for complex sequences, supplies the limit. Finite intersections in the filter give linearity; nonnegative averages give positivity; constants give norm one. The [Cesaro theorem for convergent sequences](cesaro-theorem-for-convergent-sequences.md) gives agreement with ordinary limits because the ultrafilter contains every cofinite set. For the [left shift on bounded sequences](left-shift-on-bounded-sequences.md), the two means differ by $(x_{n+1}-x_1)/n$, which tends to zero, proving shift invariance.
