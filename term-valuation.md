# Term valuation

↑ **Parent:** [First-order term](first-order-term.md)

A [first-order structure](first-order-structure.md) and an [assignment](assignment-in-a-first-order-structure.md) determine the value of every [term](first-order-term.md) recursively: a [variable](logical-variable.md) has its assigned value, a [constant](constant-symbol.md) has its interpreted value, and a compound [function](function-split.md) [term](first-order-term.md) is evaluated by applying the interpreted [function](function-split.md) to the recursively evaluated arguments. This operation supplies the input values for the [satisfaction](satisfaction-for-a-set-structure.md) clauses of [atomic formulas](atomic-formula.md).
