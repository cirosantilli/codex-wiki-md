# TLS termination

↑ **Parent:** [Transport Layer Security](transport-layer-security.md)

[TLS termination](tls-termination.md) occurs at a [TLS](transport-layer-security.md) peer that decrypts incoming records and participates in the channel authentication and key agreement. An intermediary terminating one [TLS](transport-layer-security.md) channel and initiating another is an [endpoint](communication-endpoint.md) of each channel, not an opaque forwarder.
