# Molar volume

↑ **Parent:** [Volume (thermodynamics)](volume-thermodynamics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Molar_volume)

The [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) per [amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md), with $n$ measured in [moles](mole-unit.md). For a uniform pure substance, $v_m=M/\rho$, where $M$ is its [molar mass](molar-mass.md) and $\rho$ its [mass density](density.md). The [Clapeyron equation](clausius-clapeyron-relation.md) pairs a difference of [molar volumes](molar-volume.md) with [latent heat](latent-heat.md) per [mole](mole-unit.md).
