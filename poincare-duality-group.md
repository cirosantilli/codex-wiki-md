# Poincare duality group

↑ **Parent:** [Group cohomology](group-cohomology.md)

A Poincare duality group of dimension $n$ has a finite-length resolution of the trivial [group ring](group-ring.md) module $\mathbb Z$ by [finitely generated projective modules](finite-projective-module.md), and $H^k(G,\mathbb ZG)$ vanishes except in degree $n$, where it is infinite cyclic as an abelian group. The top module $D$ can have an orientation-sign action. Reversing the dual projective resolution gives $H^k(G,V)\cong\operatorname{Tor}^{\mathbb ZG}_{n-k}(D,V)$, equivalently $H_{n-k}(G,D\otimes V)$ with the appropriate diagonal action. Untwisted duality requires trivial orientation action. In positive dimension $H^0(G,\mathbb ZG)=0$, since a finitely supported invariant element cannot exist for an infinite group.
