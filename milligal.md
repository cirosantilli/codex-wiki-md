# Milligal

↑ **Parent:** [Gravitational acceleration](gravitational-acceleration.md)

A geophysical [gravitational acceleration](gravitational-acceleration.md) unit: $1\ \mathrm{mGal}=10^{-5}\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$. It is used for small [gravity anomalies](gravity-anomaly.md) and should be converted before substitution into SI forms of the [Bouguer slab](bouguer-slab.md) formula.
