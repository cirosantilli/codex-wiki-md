# Spinor azimuth convention

↑ **Parent:** [Future-null spinor factorization](future-null-spinor-factorization.md)

With $X_{12}=x+iy$, a [spinor](spinor.md) whose components have phases $e^{-i\phi/2}$ and $e^{i\phi/2}$ has $x+iy\propto e^{-i\phi}$, so its physical [azimuth](azimuth.md) is $-\phi$. The usual positive [azimuth](azimuth.md) for those phases uses $X_{12}=x-iy$. Both conventions are valid if used consistently.
