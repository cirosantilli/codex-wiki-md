# Elastomer

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer](polymer-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elastomer)

An elastomer is a polymeric material capable of large substantially recoverable deformation. Flexible chain segments supply configurational [entropy](entropy-split.md), while a network of permanent or sufficiently long-lived [cross-links](cross-link.md) prevents unrestricted macroscopic flow on the observation time scale. Its [dynamic modulus](dynamic-modulus.md) depends on whether segmental relaxation can follow the imposed deformation. The [rubbery plateau](rubbery-plateau.md) and high-frequency glassy response refer to different accessible relaxation regimes.
