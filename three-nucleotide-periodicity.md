# Three-nucleotide periodicity

↑ **Parent:** [Ribosome profiling](ribosome-profiling.md)

Frame-related enrichment at a three-base interval in correctly positioned translating-ribosome reads. It supports [biological translation](translation-biology.md) but is difficult to establish for very short or low-count [reading frames](reading-frame.md).
