# Center and radius of a sphere-plane intersection

↑ **Parent:** [Sphere-plane intersection](sphere-plane-intersection.md)

For a [sphere](sphere.md) with centre $c$ and radius $a$, and a [plane](plane.md) $n\cdot r=p$ with unit normal $n$, the orthogonal projection of $c$ onto the [plane](plane.md) is $b=c+(p-n\cdot c)n$. The orthogonal decomposition $r-c=(r-b)+(b-c)$ gives the intersection [circle](circle.md) radius by the [Pythagorean theorem](pythagorean-theorem.md). A genuine [circle](circle.md) requires $|p-n\cdot c|<a$; equality gives tangency and larger distance gives no intersection. The signed displacement is not determined by the two radii alone: it is $\pm\sqrt{a^2-\rho^2}$ with sign fixed by the oriented [plane](plane.md).
