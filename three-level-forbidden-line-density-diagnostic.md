# Three-level forbidden-line density diagnostic

↑ **Parent:** [Forbidden emission line](forbidden-emission-line.md)

In a three-level ion, [statistical equilibrium](statistical-equilibrium-of-atomic-level-populations.md) must include collisional coupling between both upper levels as well as excitations from the ground. The upper populations solve a two-by-two linear system when expressed relative to the ground population. Their line ratio is a rational function of [electron density](electron-density.md), interpolating between a collisionally excited low-density limit and a collisional Boltzmann limit. Closely spaced upper energies reduce [temperature](temperature-split.md) sensitivity, but neglecting upper-level collisional mixing can bias the inferred [density](density.md).
