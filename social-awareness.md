# Social awareness

↑ **Parent:** [Social intelligence](social-intelligence.md)

Sensitivity to relevant social cues and consequences. Its operational meaning depends on the instrument; a factor with this label in a [self-report](self-report.md) scale is not automatically identical to a performance ability.
