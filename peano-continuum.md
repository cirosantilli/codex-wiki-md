# Peano continuum

↑ **Parent:** [Hahn-Mazurkiewicz theorem](hahn-mazurkiewicz-theorem.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peano_continuum)

A Peano continuum is a compact, connected, locally connected metric space. By the [Hahn-Mazurkiewicz theorem](hahn-mazurkiewicz-theorem.md), every Peano continuum is the image of a continuous surjection from $[0,1]$.
