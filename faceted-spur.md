# Faceted spur

↑ **Parent:** [Geomorphology](geomorphology.md)

A ridge spur ending in a relatively planar face along a mountain front. Repeated [normal faulting](normal-faulting.md) and incision of adjacent drainages can create triangular facets, but their preservation and geometry also depend on [erosion](erosion.md) and [rock](rock-geology.md) strength.
