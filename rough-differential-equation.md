# Rough differential equation

↑ **Parent:** [Rough path](rough-path.md)

A rough differential equation interprets a differential equation using the higher-order data of its driving [rough path](rough-path.md). For $2<p<3$, its local expansion includes $V_i(Y_s)X^i_{s,t}+DV_j(Y_s)V_i(Y_s)\mathbb X^{ij}_{s,t}$, with a remainder controlled at order greater than one. For a smooth driver it is the usual controlled [ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md); for [enhanced Brownian motion](enhanced-brownian-motion.md) it is the corresponding Stratonovich equation.
