# Linear homentropic acoustic equations

↑ **Parent:** [Linear acoustics](linear-acoustics-split.md)

For perturbations $(\rho',p',\mathbf u)$ about a uniform fluid of density $\rho_0$, the linearized [continuity equation](continuity-equation.md), [momentum equation](euler-equations-for-an-inviscid-fluid.md), and [homentropic pressure perturbation](homentropic-pressure-perturbation.md) relation are

$$
\rho'_t+\rho_0\nabla\mathbin\cdot\mathbf u=0,
\qquad
\rho_0\mathbf u_t=-\nabla p',
\qquad
p'=c_0^2\rho'.
$$

For [irrotational flow](irrotational-flow.md) $\mathbf u=\nabla\phi$, they imply $p'=-\rho_0\phi_t$ and $\phi_{tt}=c_0^2\nabla^2\phi$.
