# Drift-diffusion decay time

↑ **Parent:** [Advection-diffusion equation](advection-diffusion-equation.md)

For constant [diffusion coefficient](diffusion-coefficient.md) $D$ and [drift velocity](drift-velocity.md) $v\ne0$, the balance $|v|t\sim\sqrt{Dt}$ gives the characteristic [length](length.md) $D/|v|$ and [time](time-in-physics.md) $D/v^2$. The fundamental [Gaussian distribution](normal-distribution.md) of the [advection-diffusion equation](advection-diffusion-equation.md) has a fixed-position exponential factor $\exp[-v^2t/(4D)]$. Total [probability](probability.md) is still conserved: this describes [probability](probability.md) moving away from a fixed observation region, rather than disappearance of the [particle](particle-physics.md). At zero drift, this finite exponential [time](time-in-physics.md) scale is absent.
