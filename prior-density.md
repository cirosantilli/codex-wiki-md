# Prior density

↑ **Parent:** [Prior probability](prior-probability.md)

A [prior density](prior-density.md) is the [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) of a continuous [prior distribution](prior-probability.md) with respect to a specified reference measure. A proper prior density is nonnegative and integrates to one. Prior-density factors enter [Bayesian posterior](bayesian-posterior.md) and [RJ-MCMC](reversible-jump-markov-chain-monte-carlo.md) acceptance formulas along with the likelihood and model prior probabilities.
