# Dense approximation under a weak-type maximal bound

↑ **Parent:** [Weak type with a normed domain](weak-type-with-a-normed-domain.md)

Suppose [linear operators](linear-operator.md) $A_t$ and $A_0$ on a [normed space](normed-vector-space.md) satisfy $|A_tf|,|A_0f|\leq Sf$, where $S$ has [weak type with a normed domain](weak-type-with-a-normed-domain.md), and all relevant suprema are measurable. If $A_tg\to A_0g$ [almost everywhere](almost-everywhere.md) on a dense subspace, the same holds for every input. For a fixed approximation $g$, the limiting error is at most $2S(f-g)$. The measure where it exceeds $a$ is at most $(2C\|f-g\|/a)^q$, which tends to zero as the approximation improves. This is a convergence-to-a-specified-limit version of [almost-everywhere convergence from weak-type domination](almost-everywhere-convergence-from-weak-type-domination.md).
