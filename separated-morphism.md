# Separated morphism

↑ **Parent:** [Morphism of schemes](morphism-of-schemes.md)

A morphism $f:X\to Y$ is separated when its diagonal $X\to X\times_YX$ is a [closed immersion](closed-immersion.md). This is the scheme-theoretic analogue of the Hausdorff property.

A morphism of [schemes](scheme.md) is separated when its diagonal is a [closed immersion](closed-immersion.md). This is a property of the morphism, distinct from the broader morphism or diagonal constructions.
