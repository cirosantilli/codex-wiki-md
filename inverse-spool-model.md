# Inverse spool model

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer physics](polymer-physics.md)

An idealized arrangement of confined [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) as approximately concentric loops around a central hole, with neighboring strands locally close-packed. It separates curvature-dependent [DNA bending energy](dna-bending-energy.md) from interstrand interactions. It is a geometrical model, not a universal experimentally established arrangement in every [capsid](viral-capsid.md).
