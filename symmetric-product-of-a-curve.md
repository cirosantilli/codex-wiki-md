# Symmetric product of a curve

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic curve](algebraic-curve.md)

The [scheme](scheme.md) $C^{(d)}$ parametrizes effective degree-$d$ [Cartier divisors](cartier-divisor-split.md) on a smooth [algebraic curve](algebraic-curve.md). Near a [Cartier divisor](cartier-divisor-split.md) $\sum m_iP_i$, its completed local ring is a power-series ring in the elementary symmetric coordinates of $m_i$ local parameters at each $P_i$. It is therefore smooth of dimension $d$, even when the [characteristic of a field](characteristic-of-a-field.md) divides $d!$. For projective $C$ it is projective.
