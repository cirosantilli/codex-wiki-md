# Zero-noise limit with a bounded potential

↑ **Parent:** [Feynman-Kac formula with a bounded potential](feynman-kac-formula-with-a-bounded-potential.md)

For globally Lipschitz drift $b$, couple $dX^\varepsilon=b(X^\varepsilon)dt+\varepsilon dW$ and $\dot x=b(x)$ from the same point. The [Gronwall inequality](gronwall-inequality.md) gives $\sup_{s\le t}|X_s^\varepsilon-x_s|\le\varepsilon e^{Lt}\sup_{s\le t}|W_s|$. Thus convergence is uniform on each finite horizon. The [Feynman-Kac formula](feynman-kac-formula.md) for generator $\varepsilon^2\Delta/2+b\cdot\nabla+c$ has weight $\exp\int c$, with a positive sign. Bounded continuous $c$ is uniformly continuous on a compact neighbourhood of the deterministic trajectory, and bounded continuous $f$ supplies a deterministic integrable bound. The [dominated convergence theorem](dominated-convergence-theorem.md) proves the displayed limit without requiring global uniform continuity of $c$.
