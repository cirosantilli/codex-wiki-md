# MSSM tree-level sfermion mass constraint

↑ **Parent:** [Minimal supersymmetric Standard Model](minimal-supersymmetric-standard-model.md)

With canonical tree-level global [supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md), neutral [F-term](f-term.md) breaking and no [D-term](d-term.md) mass shifts, the [tree-level supertrace mass sum rule](tree-level-supertrace-mass-sum-rule.md) applies in conserved electric and color charge blocks. The corresponding scalar squared-mass average equals the fermion squared-mass average, so not every scalar partner can lie above that average. This obstructs making every [squark](squark.md) heavy through direct canonical visible-sector breaking while retaining light quarks. Effective [soft supersymmetry breaking](soft-supersymmetry-breaking.md), noncanonical interactions, radiative effects or [supergravity](supergravity.md) invalidate the assumptions of that argument.
