# Veronese embedding

↑ **Parent:** [Veronese subring](veronese-subring.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Veronese_embedding)

The degree-$d$ Veronese embedding sends a [projective point](projective-point.md) to all monomials of degree $d$ in its homogeneous coordinates. For example,

$$
\nu_2:\mathbb P^1\longrightarrow\mathbb P^2,
\qquad [x:y]\longmapsto[x^2:xy:y^2],
$$

is a closed immersion with image the conic $uw-v^2=0$.
