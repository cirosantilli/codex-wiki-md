# Radiogenic heating

↑ **Parent:** [Radioactive decay](radioactive-decay.md)

Radiogenic heating is thermal energy deposited by [radioactive decay](radioactive-decay.md). If its specific heating rate is $Q$ in watts per kilogram, the volumetric rate is $\rho Q$ and the heat input per horizontal area to a column of depth $H$ is $\rho QH$. The distinction between specific and volumetric rates matters in [dimensional analysis](dimensional-analysis.md) of a convective [heat flux](heat-flux-density.md) law.
