# Character table from a cyclic quotient and two elementary-abelian orbits

↑ **Parent:** [Character table](character-table.md)

A group of order thirty-six with a normal $C_3^2$ and a cyclic quotient of order four acting freely on its eight nonzero elements has six [conjugacy classes](conjugacy-class.md) of sizes $1,4,4,9,9,9$. Its four [linear characters](linear-character.md) are lifted from the cyclic quotient. The remaining irreducible degrees are four and four, by the sum of squared degrees. [Column orthogonality](column-orthogonality.md) forces the last three values of both degree-four rows to vanish. The square of the order-four action is inversion on $C_3^2$, making the first two nontrivial class values real. Orthogonality then gives sum minus one and product minus two, so the rows are $(4,1,-2,0,0,0)$ and $(4,-2,1,0,0,0)$. This illustrates why reality must be justified when reconstructing a [character table](character-table.md) from orthogonality.
