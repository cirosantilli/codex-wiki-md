# Additive twist of a cusp-form L-function

↑ **Parent:** [L-function of a cusp form](l-function-of-a-cusp-form.md)

For a [cusp form](cusp-form.md) $f=\sum c_nq^n$ and coprime integers $a,N$, the additive twist inserts the additive character $e^{2\pi ian/N}$. It is the [Dirichlet series](dirichlet-series.md) associated with the rational translate $f(a/N+\tau)$. Its completed [Mellin transform](mellin-transform.md) uses the scale $N^s$ and the [Gamma function](gamma-function.md).
