# Pauli Y gate

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum theory](quantum-theory-split.md)

The Pauli $Y$ gate is $Y=iXZ$ and maps $|0\rangle$ to $i|1\rangle$ and $|1\rangle$ to $-i|0\rangle$.

This gate acts by the corresponding [Pauli matrix](pauli-matrices.md), viewed as a unitary operator on one qubit.
