# Inductance correction to a rotating-loop radiation estimate

↑ **Parent:** [Frequency doubling of an induction-driven rotating loop](frequency-doubling-of-an-induction-driven-rotating-loop.md)

For constant [self-inductance](self-inductance.md) L, the steady induced current satisfies $L\dot I+RI=BA\omega\sin\omega t$, giving [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) $I_0=BA\omega/\sqrt{R^2+\omega^2L^2}$ and [phase](phase-waves.md) lag. Multiplication by the rotating loop normal again gives a static moment plus a twice-frequency rotating moment of magnitude $AI_0/2$, independently of lag. Therefore replace the resistance-only denominator $R^2$ in the radiation [power](power.md) by $R^2+\omega^2L^2$. A wire resistance alone is not sufficient for an exact prediction if [inductance](inductance.md) is appreciable.
