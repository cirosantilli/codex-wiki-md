# Ordered factorization

↑ **Parent:** [Integer factorization](integer-factorization.md)

An ordered factorization of a [positive integer](positive-integer.md) $n>1$ is an ordered list of factors greater than one whose product is $n$. For example, $6$, $2\cdot3$, and $3\cdot2$ are the three ordered factorizations of $6$. Taking successive partial products gives a [bijection](bijection.md) with strictly increasing [divisor](divisor.md) chains from $1$ to $n$.
