<h1 id="symmetric-cubic-representation-of-su-3">Symmetric cubic representation of SU(3)</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetric traceless SU(3) tensor representation](symmetric-traceless-su-3-tensor-representation.md)

The degree-three monomials $z_1^az_2^bz_3^c$, with $a+b+c=3$, form a basis of this ten-dimensional [highest-weight representation](highest-weight-representation.md). Its highest monomial is $z_1^3$, and $E_{21}=z_2\partial_{z_1}$ and $E_{32}=z_3\partial_{z_2}$ generate every basis monomial. Its [Dynkin labels](dynkin-label.md) are $(3,0)$, and the weight of a monomial is $(a-b,b-c)$. In coordinates $(h_1,(h_1+2h_2)/\sqrt3)$, the ten weights form four horizontal rows of lengths $4,3,2,1$, all with [weight multiplicity](weight-multiplicity.md) one. The [baryon decuplet](baryon-decuplet.md) realizes this representation of approximate [flavor symmetry](flavor-symmetry.md).
