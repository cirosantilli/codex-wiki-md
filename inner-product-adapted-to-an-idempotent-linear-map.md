# Inner product adapted to an idempotent linear map

↑ **Parent:** [Projection (linear algebra)](projection-linear-algebra.md)

For an [idempotent linear map](projection-linear-algebra.md) $P$ on a finite-dimensional real [vector space](vector-space-split.md), take any positive definite [inner product](inner-product.md) on that space. The displayed new [inner product](inner-product.md) is positive definite since its squared norm vanishes only if both $Pu$ and $(I-P)u$ vanish. It makes the [kernel of a linear map](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) and [image of a linear map](image-of-a-linear-map.md) orthogonal. Since $P^2=P$, one has $\langle Pu,v\rangle_P=\langle u,Pv\rangle_P$; thus $P$ becomes an [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md).
