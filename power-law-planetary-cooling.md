# Power-law planetary cooling

↑ **Parent:** [Intrinsic planetary luminosity](intrinsic-planetary-luminosity.md)

If otherwise comparable isolated [planets](planet.md) of one [mass](mass.md) follow $L_{\rm int}=Ct^{-\alpha}$, their intrinsic [luminosity](luminosity.md) ratio is $L_{\rm int}(t_1)/L_{\rm int}(t_2)=(t_2/t_1)^\alpha$. The normalization depends on composition and initial [entropy](entropy.md). Strong stellar irradiation, deep heating, or accretion can invalidate extrapolating the same cooling law.
