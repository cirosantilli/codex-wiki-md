# Two-parameter Fermi nuclear charge distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear charge distribution](nuclear-charge-distribution.md)

A common spherical model of a heavy [nuclear charge distribution](nuclear-charge-distribution.md) uses a half-density radius $c$ and surface diffuseness $a>0$. The normalization $\rho_0$ is fixed by $4\pi\int_0^\infty r^2\rho(r)\,dr=Ze$. The 90%-to-10% surface thickness is $t=4a\ln3$: solve $\rho/\rho_0=0.9$ and $0.1$ and subtract the two radii. Typical fits have an approximately constant surface thickness and a radius scaling roughly as the cube root of [mass number](mass-number.md). This is a spatial density model, not an assertion of a thermal [Fermi-Dirac distribution](fermi-dirac-distribution.md) for nuclear positions.
