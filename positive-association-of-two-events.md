# Positive association of two events

↑ **Parent:** [Conditional probability](conditional-probability.md)

For nontrivial [events](event.md), strict attraction under [conditional probability](conditional-probability.md) is equivalent to a positive covariance of their indicator variables. This condition is symmetric in the two [events](event.md). Complementing exactly one of the [events](event.md) reverses the strict inequality. This two-event condition should be distinguished from association of an entire family of random variables.
