# Weakly coordinating anion

↑ **Parent:** [Counterion](counterion.md)

A [weakly coordinating anion](weakly-coordinating-anion.md) balances charge while binding only weakly to the cationic reaction center. Delocalized charge, low basicity and steric shielding can leave a [metal](metal.md) coordination site accessible. Weak coordination is relative, not an assertion that ion pairing is absent.
