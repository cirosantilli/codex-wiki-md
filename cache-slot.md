# Cache slot

↑ **Parent:** [Cache line](cache-line.md)

A resident storage entry that can hold one [cache block](cache-line.md) and metadata identifying its contents. [Cache line](cache-line.md) identity belongs to backing [computer memory](computer-memory.md); [cache slot](cache-slot.md) identity belongs to the consulted [memory cache](cache-computing.md).
