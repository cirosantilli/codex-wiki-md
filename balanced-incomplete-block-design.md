# Balanced incomplete block design

↑ **Parent:** [Block design](block-design.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balanced_incomplete_block_design)

A binary [block design](block-design.md) has $t$ [treatments](treatment.md), $b$ blocks of size $k<t$, every treatment in $r$ blocks, and every distinct pair in $\lambda$ blocks. Counting occurrences and pairs gives $tr=bk$ and $r(k-1)=\lambda(t-1)$. Its [incidence matrix of a set system](incidence-matrix-of-a-set-system.md) $N$ satisfies $NN^T=(r-\lambda)I+\lambda\mathbf1\mathbf1^T$. For statistical comparison of all treatment effects one also needs connectedness; $\lambda>0$ guarantees it, whereas singleton blocks with $\lambda=0$ do not.
