# Panic disorder

↑ **Parent:** [Anxiety disorder](anxiety-disorder.md)

A disorder involving recurrent unexpected panic attacks and persistent concern or behavioral change related to them. Cognitive accounts examine how interpretations of bodily sensations contribute to recurrent fear; the diagnosis does not establish that every bodily symptom is psychologically caused.
