# Synchronous coupling

↑ **Parent:** [Coupling](coupling.md)

A synchronous coupling drives two processes with the same fresh random inputs. For example, recursions $X_{n+1}=F(X_n,\xi_{n+1})$ and $Y_{n+1}=F(Y_n,\xi_{n+1})$ use the same input $\xi_{n+1}$ at each step. This can reveal [synchronous contraction of a random affine recursion](synchronous-contraction-of-a-random-affine-recursion.md) even when independently simulated trajectories would conceal the relationship.

A synchronous coupling drives two solutions of a stochastic differential equation by the same Brownian motion. Their difference has noise coefficient $\sigma(X)-\sigma(Y)$. A dissipativity inequality for the drift and this coefficient gives the [mean-square contraction of synchronously coupled diffusions](mean-square-contraction-of-synchronously-coupled-diffusions.md).
