# Forced eccentricity circle

↑ **Parent:** [Complex eccentricity](complex-eccentricity.md)

With a fixed planetary [pericentre](periapsis.md), the linear secular equation $\dot z=iA(z-Z_f)$ has solution $z=Z_f+(z(0)-Z_f)e^{iAt}$. An initially circular orbit follows $z=Z_f(1-e^{iAt})$, a counterclockwise circle in the complex plane centered at the [forced eccentricity](forced-eccentricity.md) $Z_f$, with [free eccentricity](proper-eccentricity.md) amplitude $Z_f$. Its maximum [orbital eccentricity](orbital-eccentricity.md) is $2Z_f$ and its phase-averaged mean [orbital eccentricity](orbital-eccentricity.md) is $4Z_f/\pi$.
