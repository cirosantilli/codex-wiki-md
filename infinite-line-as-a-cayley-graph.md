# Infinite line as a Cayley graph

↑ **Parent:** [Cayley graph](cayley-graph.md)

The undirected infinite line is both the [Cayley graph](cayley-graph.md) of the [infinite cyclic group](infinite-cyclic-group.md) $\mathbb Z$ with generating set $\{1\}$ and the Cayley graph of the [infinite dihedral group](infinite-dihedral-group.md) $C_2*C_2$ with its two involutory generators. Thus an unlabelled Cayley graph need not determine its group.
