# Vanishing for divisor twists on a complex torus

↑ **Parent:** [Kodaira vanishing theorem](kodaira-vanishing-theorem.md)

Let $L$ be positive and $V$ a closed smooth hypersurface in a [complex torus](complex-torus.md). The [semipositive canonical bundle of a submanifold of a complex torus](semipositive-canonical-bundle-of-a-submanifold-of-a-complex-torus.md) makes $P_a=(L\otimes\mathcal O(aV))|_V$ positive. Since $K_V=\mathcal O(V)|_V$, the restriction of the $a$-th ambient twist is $K_V\otimes P_{a-1}$ when $a\geq1$. Apply the [Kodaira vanishing theorem](kodaira-vanishing-theorem.md) on $V$, and use $0\to L((a-1)V)\to L(aV)\to L(aV)|_V\to0$ and its long exact sequence to induct from the positive bundle $L$ on the torus. No unproved ambient positivity of $\mathcal O(V)$ is needed.
