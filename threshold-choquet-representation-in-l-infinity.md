# Threshold Choquet representation in L-infinity

↑ **Parent:** [Choquet theorem](choquet-theorem.md)

For a real [function](function-split.md) in the $L^\infty$ unit ball, push uniform [measure](measure.md) on $t\in[0,1]$ forward by the displayed sign-valued [functions](function-split.md). In the [weak-star topology](weak-star-topology.md) this is a Borel probability concentrated on the [extreme points](extreme-point.md), and [Fubini's theorem](fubini-s-theorem.md) shows that its [barycenter](barycenter.md) is $f$. Weak-star compactness and separability of the predual $L^1$ place the example within [Choquet theorem](choquet-theorem.md).
