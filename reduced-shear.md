# Reduced shear

↑ **Parent:** [Lensing shear](lensing-shear.md)

The combination of [lensing shear](lensing-shear.md) and [lensing convergence](lensing-convergence.md) governing the local shape distortion of background sources. Galaxy ellipticities estimate it statistically under a specified ellipticity convention and intrinsic-orientation model. It is invariant under the [mass-sheet degeneracy](mass-sheet-degeneracy.md), so shape information alone does not generally fix absolute projected [mass](mass.md) normalization.
