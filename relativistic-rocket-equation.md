# Relativistic rocket equation

↑ **Parent:** [Rocket equation](rocket-equation.md)

For collinear motion without external forces, a rocket ejecting material backwards at constant speed $u$ in its instantaneous rest frame obeys $m\gamma^2\,dv+u\,dm=0$. Here $m$ is the rocket's [rest mass](invariant-mass.md), including stored [internal energy](internal-energy.md), and $\gamma$ is its [Lorentz factor](lorentz-factor.md) in the chosen [inertial frame](inertial-frame.md). The equation follows from [momentum conservation](momentum-conservation.md), [conservation of relativistic energy](conservation-of-relativistic-energy.md), and the [relativistic velocity-addition formula](velocity-addition-formula.md); expelled rest mass is not simply the decrease in rocket rest mass.
