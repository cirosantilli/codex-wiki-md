# Metallicity distribution in the half-rate infall model

↑ **Parent:** [Proportional-infall model of galactic chemical evolution](proportional-infall-model-of-galactic-chemical-evolution.md)

For $\nu=1/2$ and initially pristine gas, $M=M_0(1-Z/(2y))$ and the formed stellar mass is $S=2(M_0-M)=M_0Z/y$. If a fixed [initial mass function](initial-mass-function.md) supplies $\kappa$ surviving tracers per unit formed mass, then $N(<Z)=\kappa M_0Z/y$ and $dN/dZ=\kappa M_0/y$ until the current gas abundance. Complete exhaustion gives a uniform normalized [metallicity distribution function](metallicity-distribution-function.md) on $0<Z<2y$. This is uniform in linear metallicity, not in its logarithm.
