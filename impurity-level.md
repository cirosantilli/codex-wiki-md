# Impurity level

↑ **Parent:** [Semiconductor doping](semiconductor-doping.md)

An [impurity level](impurity-level.md) is an electronic [energy level](energy-level.md) associated with a dopant or defect in a [semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md). A shallow [donor impurity](donor-impurity.md) introduces a level just below the [conduction band](conduction-band.md), and a shallow [acceptor impurity](acceptor-impurity.md) introduces one just above the [valence band](valence-band.md). Under electrostatic [band bending](band-bending.md), these levels shift with the local band edges in the same material; their occupation is determined relative to the equilibrium [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md).
