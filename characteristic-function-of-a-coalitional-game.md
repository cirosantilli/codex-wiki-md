# Characteristic function of a coalitional game

↑ **Parent:** [Transferable utility game](transferable-utility-game.md)

The characteristic function assigns every [coalition](coalition-game-theory.md) its total attainable transferable payoff, with $v(\varnothing)=0$. It determines the efficiency condition of an [imputation](imputation-in-a-coalitional-game.md) and all constraints of the [core of a cooperative game](core-game-theory.md). For the [glove game](glove-game.md), $v(S)=\omega\min(|S\cap L|,|S\cap R|)$; for miners requiring $r$ people to carry one lump, $v(S)=\lfloor|S|/r\rfloor$. This game-theoretic function is distinct from the [characteristic function](characteristic-function.md) of a probability distribution.
