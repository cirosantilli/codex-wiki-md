# Single-domain ferromagnetic particle

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic domain](magnetic-domain.md)

A ferromagnetic particle with no internal domain wall. [Magnetization](magnetization.md) reversal can proceed by rotation or nonuniform modes; [thermal activation](thermal-activation.md) and [anisotropy](anisotropy.md) determine blocking and [coercivity](coercive-function.md). Small size alone does not guarantee a permanent hard magnet.
