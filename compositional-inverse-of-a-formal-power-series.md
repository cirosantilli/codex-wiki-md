# Compositional inverse of a formal power series

↑ **Parent:** [Formal power series](formal-power-series.md)

A [formal power series](formal-power-series.md) with zero constant coefficient has a compositional inverse over $R$ exactly when its linear coefficient is a unit. Starting with $b_1=a_1^{-1}$, the degree-$k$ coefficient of $f(b_1T+\cdots+b_kT^k)$ is $a_1b_k$ plus an expression already determined by lower coefficients, so recursion gives a unique inverse. For a homomorphism of [formal group laws](formal-group-law.md), cancellation in the homomorphism identity proves that this inverse is also a formal-group homomorphism.
