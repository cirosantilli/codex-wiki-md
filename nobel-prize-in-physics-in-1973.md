# Nobel Prize in Physics in 1973

↑ **Parent:** [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics-split.md)

One half was shared by [Leo Esaki](leo-esaki.md) and [Ivar Giaever](ivar-giaever.md) for experimental discoveries of [quantum tunnelling](quantum-tunnelling.md) in [semiconductors](semiconductor-split.md) and [superconductivity](superconductivity-split.md), respectively. The other half went to [Brian D. Josephson](brian-d-josephson.md) for predicting supercurrents across a tunnel barrier and the [Josephson effect](josephson-effect.md).[Individual official summary: Esaki and Giaever one-quarter each, Josephson one-half; experimental and theoretical motivations separately.](the-nobel-prize-in-physics-1973-by-nobel-prize-outreach.md)
