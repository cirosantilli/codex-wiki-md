# Half-line delta-well amplitude enhancement

↑ **Parent:** [Resonance pole](resonance-pole.md)

A hard wall at the origin and an attractive [delta potential](delta-potential.md) at distance $a$ form a leaky quantum cavity. For unit incident amplitude from outside, the interior [standing wave](standing-wave.md) has coefficient $A=-i\lambda x/[1-e^{2ix}+i\lambda x]$, where $x=ka$ and $\lambda=\hbar^2/(mqa)>0$. Its [modulus](modulus.md) squared is the title expression. A [resonance pole](resonance-pole.md) $p_n=x_n-i\gamma_n$ produces an enhancement with a locally [Lorentzian line shape](lorentzian-line-shape.md), proportional to $[(x-x_n)^2+\gamma_n^2]^{-1}$. At fixed low $n$ and small $\lambda$, $x_n\simeq n\pi(1+\lambda/2+\lambda^2/4)$ and $\gamma_n\simeq(\lambda n\pi/2)^2$.

The coefficient can exceed one because it measures a standing-wave amplitude, while the net interior [probability current](probability-current.md) is zero. Conservation of [probability current](probability-current.md) gives unit reflected flux: no current passes through the hard wall. Near zero, $|A|^2\to\lambda^2/(2-\lambda)^2$ when $\lambda\ne2$; the threshold case $\lambda=2$ requires a separate limit.
