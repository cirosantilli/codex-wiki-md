# Fermion bilinear

↑ **Parent:** [Dirac field](dirac-field.md)

A local expression containing two [fermion](fermion.md) fields and a matrix acting on [Dirac spinor](dirac-spinor.md) indices. The choices $\Gamma=\gamma^\mu$ and $\Gamma=\gamma^\mu\gamma^5$ give the [Dirac current](dirac-current.md) and [axial current](axial-current.md).
