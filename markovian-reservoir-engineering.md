# Markovian reservoir engineering

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum control](quantum-control.md)

Design [Lindblad operators](lindblad-operator.md) and a [Quantum Hamiltonian](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) so that desired [density operators](density-matrix.md) are stationary and unwanted perturbations decay. A dissipative interaction can change the [spectrum](spectrum-functional-analysis.md) and [purity of a density operator](purity-of-a-density-operator.md), reaching targets unavailable to purely coherent [Hamiltonian engineering](hamiltonian-engineering.md). The engineered stationary state must be physical, and uniqueness alone must be distinguished from an explicit proof of asymptotic convergence.
