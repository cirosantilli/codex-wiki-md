# Hirsch length

↑ **Parent:** [Polycyclic group](polycyclic-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hirsch_length)

The Hirsch length of a [polycyclic group](polycyclic-group.md) counts infinite cyclic factors in a polycyclic series. Refinement shows it is independent of the series, additive in extensions, and unchanged in finite-index subgroups. A finite-index [poly-infinite cyclic group](poly-infinite-cyclic-group.md) has one infinite cyclic factor for each unit of Hirsch length.
