# Pulsar death line

↑ **Parent:** [Pulsar magnetosphere](pulsar-magnetosphere.md)

A model-dependent boundary in spin-period and spin-down parameter space beyond which particle acceleration and [pair production](pair-production.md) cannot maintain a specified radio-emission mechanism. Crossing it means loss of an emission state, not destruction of the [neutron star](neutron-star.md). Selection and beaming obscure an exact empirical universal boundary.
