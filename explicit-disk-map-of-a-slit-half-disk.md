# Explicit disk map of a slit half disk

↑ **Parent:** [Conformal map](conformal-map.md)

For the upper half disk $|z|<2$ slit along $z=iy$, $0<y\le1$, squaring maps onto $|w|<4$ minus $[-1,4)$. After scaling the radius-four disc, the [disk automorphism](automorphism-of-the-unit-disk.md) $v=4(w+1)/(w+16)$ sends this slit to $[0,1)$. Its [square root](square-root.md) with argument in $(0,\pi)$ maps onto the upper half [unit disc](unit-disc.md). There $u+u^{-1}$ maps bijectively onto the [lower half-plane](lower-half-plane.md): its [imaginary part](imaginary-part.md) is $\operatorname{Im}u(1-|u|^{-2})<0$, and the two roots of its inverse equation are reciprocal, with exactly one inside the unit [circle](circle.md). The final [Möbius map](mobius-transformation.md) gives the displayed conformal disk [bijection](bijection.md).
