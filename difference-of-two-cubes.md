# Difference of two cubes

↑ **Parent:** [Factorization of polynomials](factorization-of-polynomials.md)

The [polynomial identity](polynomial-identity.md)

$$
\boxed{a^3-b^3=(a-b)(a^2+ab+b^2)}
$$

follows by multiplying out the right side. For real $a,b$, the quadratic factor is $a^2+ab+b^2=(a+b/2)^2+3b^2/4$, so it is nonnegative and vanishes only when $a=b=0$. The identity is a special case of the [finite geometric series](finite-geometric-series.md) factorisation $a^n-b^n=(a-b)\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}a^{n-1-j}b^j$.
