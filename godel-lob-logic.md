<h1 id="godel-lob-logic">Gödel-Löb logic</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Provability logic](provability-logic.md)

The [Gödel-Löb logic](godel-lob-logic.md) has propositional tautologies, $\Box(A\to B)\to(\Box A\to\Box B)$ and $\Box(\Box A\to A)\to\Box A$ as axiom schemes. Its rules are [modus ponens rule](implication-elimination-rule.md) and the [necessitation rule](necessitation-rule.md); uniform [propositional substitution](propositional-substitution.md) is built into the schemes. Under an arithmetic realization, each axiom and each rule preserves provability in a theory with the [Hilbert-Bernays-Löb derivability conditions](hilbert-bernays-lob-derivability-conditions.md) and diagonalization. This proves arithmetic soundness by induction on derivations; it is distinct from claiming that every true arithmetic sentence is provable.
