# Category of bounded metric spaces and non-expansive maps

↑ **Parent:** [Category of metric spaces and non-expansive maps](category-of-metric-spaces-and-non-expansive-maps.md)

The category $\mathbf{Met}_b$ is the full subcategory on bounded [metric spaces](metric-space.md). It has finite products: the terminal space is a singleton and

$$
d_{X\times Y}((x,y),(x',y'))=\max\{d_X(x,x'),d_Y(y,y')\}.
$$
