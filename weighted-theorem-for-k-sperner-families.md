# Weighted theorem for k-Sperner families

↑ **Parent:** [k-Sperner family](k-sperner-family.md)

For positive weights $w(i)$ depending only on rank, put $u_i=\binom ni w(i)$. The largest weight of a [k-Sperner family](k-sperner-family.md) in $\mathcal P([n])$ is the sum of the $k$ largest $u_i$. Every maximizing family is a union of $k$ full rank levels. To see the equality assertion, its [Lubell mass](lubell-mass.md) must be $k$; decomposing it into $k$ [antichains](antichain.md) by chain height and using [equality in the LYM inequality](equality-in-the-lym-inequality.md) forces each part to be a full level.
