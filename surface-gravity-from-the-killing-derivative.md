# Surface gravity from the Killing derivative

↑ **Parent:** [Surface gravity](surface-gravity.md)

At a regular [Killing horizon](killing-horizon.md), the generator is hypersurface orthogonal and satisfies $\nabla_KK=\kappa K$. The [Killing derivative contraction identity](killing-derivative-contraction-identity.md) gives the displayed squared [surface gravity](surface-gravity.md), including through a [bifurcation surface](bifurcation-surface.md) by continuity. The generator's normalization is part of this formula.
