# Descent from rest under quadratic drag

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic drag](quadratic-drag.md)

For positive downward speed, gravity and [quadratic drag](quadratic-drag.md) give $m\dot v=mg-\alpha v^2$, $v(0)=0$. Separation and the integral of $(1-s^2)^{-1}$ give the displayed approach to [terminal velocity](terminal-velocity.md). The fallen distance is $(m/\alpha)\log\cosh(\sqrt{\alpha g/m}\,t)$. The limit as $\alpha\to0$ recovers $v=gt$ and distance $gt^2/2$.
