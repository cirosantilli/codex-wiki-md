# Equatorial plane

↑ **Parent:** [Sphere](sphere.md)

Given a polar axis, the [equatorial plane](equatorial-plane.md) passes through the [sphere](sphere.md)'s centre and is perpendicular to that axis. In [spherical coordinates](spherical-coordinate-system.md) with [polar angle](polar-angle.md) $\theta$, it is the plane $\theta=\pi/2$. For motion in a spherically symmetric gravitational field, conservation of [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) permits rotation of axes so that a nonradial orbit lies in such a plane. This is a choice of [orientation](orientation-of-a-simplex.md), rather than an additional physical restriction on a spherically symmetric problem.
