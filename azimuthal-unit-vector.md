# Azimuthal unit vector

↑ **Parent:** [Cylindrical basis](cylindrical-basis.md)

The azimuthal [unit vector](unit-vector.md) is tangent to increasing [azimuthal angle](azimuthal-angle-in-cylindrical-coordinates.md) at fixed cylindrical radius and height. For $r>0$ it equals $(-y/r,x/r,0)$. A counterclockwise circle has tangent displacement $d\boldsymbol r=r\hat\phi\,d\phi$. Unlike a fixed Cartesian [basis vector](basis-vector.md), its direction depends on position.
