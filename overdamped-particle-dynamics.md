# Overdamped particle dynamics

↑ **Parent:** [Linear drag](linear-drag.md)

When momentum relaxes much faster than the imposed motion, [force](force.md) balance replaces the inertial equation: $\zeta\dot x=F(x,t)$ in a deterministic one-dimensional model. The [linear friction coefficient](linear-friction-coefficient.md) sets the time scale. Thermal forcing, if included, gives [overdamped Langevin dynamics](overdamped-langevin-dynamics.md).
