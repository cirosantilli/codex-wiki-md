# Identity augmentation in quantum controllability

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamical Lie algebra](dynamical-lie-algebra.md)

For $N\ge2$, suppose a skew-Hermitian [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) becomes all of $\mathfrak u(N)$ after adding the scalar identity direction. Scalar matrices commute, so $[\mathfrak g,\mathfrak g]=[\mathfrak u(N),\mathfrak u(N)]=\mathfrak{su}(N)$ is contained in $\mathfrak g$. If $\mathfrak g$ contains any nonzero-trace generator, subtracting its traceless part supplies $iI$, and $\mathfrak g$ was already all of $\mathfrak u(N)$. This permits using identity-augmented commutator calculations without assuming an additional physical identity control.
