# Binomial random hypergraph

↑ **Parent:** [Uniform hypergraph](uniform-hypergraph.md)

A binomial random $r$-[uniform hypergraph](uniform-hypergraph.md) on $n$ labelled [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) includes each of the $\binom nr$ possible [hyperedges](hyperedge.md) independently with [probability](probability.md) $p$. Thus indicators of different possible [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) are [independent](independent-random-variables.md) [Bernoulli random variables](bernoulli-distribution.md). A fixed [set](set-split.md) of $k$ [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) spans a complete $r$-uniform [hypergraph](hypergraph-split.md) with [probability](probability.md) $p^{\binom kr}$, and the expected number of such complete [sets](set-split.md) is $\binom nkp^{\binom kr}$. Counts for two [sets](set-split.md) depend only on shared possible [hyperedges](hyperedge.md), which appear exactly when their intersection has at least $r$ [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md). For $r=3,p=1/2$, balancing the [logarithm](logarithm.md) of this [expectation](expected-value-split.md) gives the largest-clique scale $k$ of order $\sqrt{\log n}$; [expectation](expected-value-split.md) alone proves the upper obstruction, not the corresponding existence assertion.
