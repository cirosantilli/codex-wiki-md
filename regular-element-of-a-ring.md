# Regular element of a ring

↑ **Parent:** [Noncommutative algebra](noncommutative-algebra-split.md)

A regular element of a [ring](ring.md) is a two-sided [non-zero-divisor](non-zero-divisor.md). The two cancellation conditions must both hold in a [noncommutative ring](noncommutative-ring.md). Products of regular elements remain regular.
