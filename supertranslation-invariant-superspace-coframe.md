# Supertranslation-invariant superspace coframe

↑ **Parent:** [Superspace](superspace.md)

The displayed one-forms give an invertible triangular change from the coordinate coframe on flat Majorana [superspace](superspace.md). A rigid [supertranslation](supertranslation.md) has $d\epsilon=0$, and the variation of $dX^m$ cancels that of $i\bar\theta\gamma^m d\theta$. Their [Maurer-Cartan equation](maurer-cartan-equation.md) is $d\Pi^m=i\,d\bar\theta\gamma^m d\theta$. The bosonic frame transforms as a Lorentz vector and the odd frame as a [Majorana spinor](majorana-spinor.md).
