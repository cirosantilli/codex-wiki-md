# High harmonic generation

↑ **Parent:** [Attosecond physics](attosecond-physics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_harmonic_generation)

[High harmonic generation](high-harmonic-generation.md) converts an intense driving [electric field](electric-field-split.md) into radiation at high integer multiples of its [frequency](frequency.md). [High harmonic generation](high-harmonic-generation.md) in a [gas](gas.md) is a process in [nonlinear optics](nonlinear-optics.md): an [electron](electron.md) can leave a neutral target by [tunnel ionization](tunnel-ionization.md), gain [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) in the driving [electric field](electric-field-split.md), and return to radiate upon recombination. This three-step picture is an approximate account of a [probability amplitude](probability-amplitude.md) for emission in [quantum mechanics](quantum-mechanics-split.md), not a measured path of one [electron](electron.md).[Generation and Measurement of Isolated Attosecond Light Pulses, slide 5: tunnelling, acceleration and return](generation-and-measurement-of-isolated-attosecond-light-pulses-by-michael-hentschel.md)

Why odd multiples? In an ideal centrosymmetric [gas](gas.md) driven by a single [frequency](frequency.md), assume the radiating [electric dipole moment](electric-dipole-moment.md) satisfies $d(t+T/2)=-d(t)$, where $T$ is the driving period. Its [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md) coefficient obeys $d_q=-(-1)^q d_q$, so every even coefficient vanishes. A finite envelope, an oriented target or an additional driving [frequency](frequency.md) can invalidate that symmetry argument. Producing many odd components also does not prove that their [spectral phases](spectral-phase.md) form a short [optical pulse](optical-pulse.md).

The 1988 report involving [Anne L'Huillier](anne-l%E2%80%99huillier.md) observed odd multiples of a $1064\,\mathrm{nm}$ driving [wavelength](wavelength.md) in rare gases, reaching order 33 in [argon](argon.md). The unexpectedly slow fall of yield beyond the fifth order supplied a broad spectral plateau. A spectral observation alone did not establish the relative [spectral phases](spectral-phase.md) needed for an [attosecond pulse train](attosecond-pulse-train.md).[Multiple-harmonic conversion of 1064 nm radiation in rare gases, abstract](multiple-harmonic-conversion-of-1064-nm-radiation-in-rare-gases-by-m-ferray-and-others.md)
