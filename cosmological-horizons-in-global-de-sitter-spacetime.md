# Cosmological horizons in global de Sitter spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [Closed slicing of de Sitter spacetime](closed-slicing-of-de-sitter-spacetime.md)

For a complete comoving observer at the north pole of the [closed slicing of de Sitter spacetime](closed-slicing-of-de-sitter-spacetime.md), radial null rays have $d\chi=\pm dT$. The finite past conformal interval gives the displayed [particle horizon](particle-horizon.md); the finite remaining future interval gives the displayed [observer event horizon](observer-event-horizon.md). The angular distances range strictly between zero and $\pi$ at every finite proper time. Complete constant-$T$ three-spheres are [Cauchy hypersurfaces](cauchy-surface.md), so their domains of dependence cover the global spacetime and they have no [Cauchy horizons](cauchy-horizon.md). Cosmological horizons are observer-dependent and are not failures of global hyperbolicity.
