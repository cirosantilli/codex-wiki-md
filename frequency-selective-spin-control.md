# Frequency-selective spin control

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum control](quantum-control.md)

Individual [quantum control](quantum-control.md) of [spins](spin.md) by exploiting different [resonance](resonance.md) frequencies. A sufficiently narrow-band [radio frequency](radio-frequency.md) pulse can predominantly affect one resonance when the separation is large compared with the excitation bandwidth and coupling effects are accounted for. Equal resonance frequencies remove this frequency-based distinction. Whether individual gates remain possible then depends on other available interactions, spatial resolution, or differences in control coupling; frequency equality alone is not a universal impossibility theorem.
