# Polytropic enthalpy

↑ **Parent:** [Specific enthalpy](specific-enthalpy.md)

For $p=K\rho^{1+1/n}$ with $K,n>0$, the specific [enthalpy](enthalpy-split.md) relative to vacuum is $Q=(n+1)K\rho^{1/n}=(n+1)p/\rho$. It obeys $\nabla Q=\nabla p/\rho$ and $nD_tQ=-Q\nabla\cdot\mathbf u$. The vacuum reference is fixed when these equations are combined with the [polytropic equation of state](polytropic-equation-of-state.md).
