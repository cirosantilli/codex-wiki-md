# Cofiber sequence of spectra

↑ **Parent:** [Cofiber of spectra](cofiber-of-spectra.md)

A [cofiber sequence of spectra](cofiber-sequence-of-spectra.md) $A\to B\to C\to\Sigma A$ induces exact sequences after either mapping into or out of a fixed [topological spectrum](spectrum-topology.md), and on [stable homotopy groups](stable-homotopy-group.md). In particular the second map is zero precisely when the first map admits a right inverse in the [stable homotopy category](stable-homotopy-category.md).
