# Geostatistics

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical modelling](statistical-modelling-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geostatistics)

[Geostatistics](geostatistics.md) models spatial dependence for estimation and prediction. A mean or drift describes systematic variation, while a [covariogram](covariogram.md) or [semivariogram](semivariogram.md) describes residual dependence. [Kriging](kriging.md) combines the two for spatial prediction.
