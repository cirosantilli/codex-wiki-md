# Salt budget

↑ **Parent:** [Salinity](salinity.md)

The [conservation law](conservation-law.md) for salt in a reservoir: the rate of change of its salt content equals inflowing minus outflowing salt transport and any explicit salt sources. Freshwater addition changes [salinity](salinity.md) without directly adding salt. For a well-mixed fixed-volume reservoir, salt content is proportional to its volume times [salinity](salinity.md).
