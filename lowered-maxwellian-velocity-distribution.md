# Lowered Maxwellian velocity distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Galactic distribution function](galactic-distribution-function.md)

A [lowered Maxwellian velocity distribution](lowered-maxwellian-velocity-distribution.md) subtracts the value of a [Maxwell-Boltzmann velocity distribution](maxwell-boltzmann-velocity-distribution.md) at the [escape velocity](escape-velocity.md) and sets the result to zero beyond escape. This gives a nonnegative [phase-space distribution function](phase-space-distribution-function.md) vanishing continuously at the boundary. If $A=n_0/(2\pi\sigma^2)^{3/2}$ and $x_e=v_e/(\sqrt2\sigma)$, its actual [number density](number-density.md) is $n=n_0[\operatorname{erf}x_e-2x_ee^{-x_e^2}/\sqrt\pi-4x_e^3e^{-x_e^2}/(3\sqrt\pi)]$. Thus $n_0$ is a normalization parameter, not generally the integrated density. Integrating only speeds below $V<v_e$ gives the same bracket with the first two terms at $X=V/(\sqrt2\sigma)$ and the last term $-4X^3e^{-x_e^2}/(3\sqrt\pi)$.
