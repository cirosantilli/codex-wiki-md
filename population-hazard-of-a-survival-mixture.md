# Population hazard of a survival mixture

↑ **Parent:** [Hazard function](hazard-function.md)

For an equally weighted mixture of individual [survivor functions](survival-function.md) $F_i$, the population [hazard function](hazard-function.md) is $\overline h(t)=\sum_i F_i(t)h_i(t)/\sum_i F_i(t)$. Thus the weights are the surviving proportions, and the [cumulative hazard function](cumulative-hazard-function.md) is $-\log(n^{-1}\sum_i e^{-H_i(t)})$, not generally the mean individual integrated hazard.
