# Max-stable distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Extreme value theory](extreme-value-theory.md)

A non-degenerate [distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md) is max-stable when every [sample maximum](sample-maximum.md) of $n$ [independent and identically distributed random variables](independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md) can be positively scaled and centered to have the original law. Thus for every integer $n\geq1$ there are $a_n>0,b_n\in\mathbb R$ with $F(a_nx+b_n)^n=F(x)$. The [extremal types theorem](extremal-types-theorem.md) classifies these laws, up to affine changes, as the [Gumbel distribution](gumbel-distribution.md), [Fréchet distribution](frechet-distribution.md), and [negative Weibull distribution](negative-weibull-distribution.md).
