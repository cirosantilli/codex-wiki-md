# Empirical likelihood with mixed censoring

↑ **Parent:** [Empirical likelihood](empirical-likelihood.md)

For independent event times with [survivor function](survival-function.md) $S(t)=\mathbb P(T>t)$, an exact event at $t$ contributes mass $S(t-)-S(t)$, a right-censored observation at $c$ contributes $S(c)$, and an interval-censored observation $l<T\leq u$ contributes $S(l)-S(u)$. Multiplying these factors gives the event-time likelihood when the observation mechanism is noninformative. Maximizing over nonincreasing right-continuous survivor functions gives a [nonparametric maximum-likelihood estimator](nonparametric-maximum-likelihood-estimator.md) rather than requiring a parametric density.
