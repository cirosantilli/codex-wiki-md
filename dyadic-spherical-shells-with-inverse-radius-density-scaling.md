# Dyadic spherical shells with inverse-radius density scaling

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson equation for Newtonian gravity](poisson-equation-for-newtonian-gravity.md)

Suppose the shell $2^{-n}<r<2^{1-n}$ has [mass density](density.md) $2^{n-1}\rho_0$. Its mass is $(14\pi\rho_0/3)4^{-n}$, by integrating $4\pi r^2\rho\,dr$. Summing the [geometric series](geometric-series.md) gives total mass $14\pi\rho_0/9$. At $r=2^{-N}$ the enclosed shells have indices $n\geq N+1$, so the enclosed mass is the total mass times $r^2$. The magnitude of the [Newtonian gravitational field](newtonian-gravitational-field.md) at every such boundary is therefore the same, $14\pi G\rho_0/9$. This does not assert that the field is constant throughout each shell.
