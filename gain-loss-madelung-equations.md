# Gain-loss Madelung equations

↑ **Parent:** [Condensate number current](condensate-number-current.md)

For the pumped [Gross–Pitaevskii equation](gross-pitaevskii-equation.md) with gain term $i(\gamma-\Gamma n)\psi$, put $\psi=\sqrt n\exp(im\phi/\hbar)$. The physical [superfluid velocity](superfluid-velocity.md) is $\nabla\phi$. The imaginary part gives the displayed number balance, while the real part gives $m\phi_t+m|\nabla\phi|^2/2+g n-\hbar^2\nabla^2\sqrt n/(2m\sqrt n)=0$. These [Madelung equations](madelung-equations.md) hold away from density zeros. The source term permits stationary density with nonzero current divergence; local gain need not balance local loss pointwise.
