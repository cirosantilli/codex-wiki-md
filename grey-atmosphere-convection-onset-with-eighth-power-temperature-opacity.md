# Grey-atmosphere convection onset with eighth-power temperature opacity

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrostatic equilibrium in optical depth](hydrostatic-equilibrium-in-optical-depth.md)

Take an [ideal gas](ideal-gas.md) [grey atmosphere](grey-atmosphere.md) with $T^4=T_e^4(3\tau+2)/4$, constant [surface gravity of a star](surface-gravity-of-a-star.md) $g$, vanishing external pressure and $\kappa=\kappa_0P^{1/2}T^8$. Integrating [hydrostatic equilibrium in optical depth](hydrostatic-equilibrium-in-optical-depth.md) gives $P^{3/2}=8g[1/2-(3\tau+2)^{-1}]/(\kappa_0T_e^8)$. Its logarithmic temperature-pressure gradient is $9\tau/16$, so the [Schwarzschild criterion](schwarzschild-criterion.md) for a monatomic gas is crossed at $\tau_c=32/45$. The radiative continuation beyond that depth is unstable and must be replaced by convective transport.
