# Shuffle identity for iterated integrals

↑ **Parent:** [Iterated integral of a path](iterated-integral-of-a-path.md)

The product of two coordinate [iterated integrals of a path](iterated-integral-of-a-path.md) is the sum over order-preserving interleavings of their integration variables. Split the product of the two ordered simplices according to the joint order of all variables. This identity explains the algebraic constraints on a geometric [rough path](rough-path.md) and the cancellation of low-degree terms in the composition of its local Euler maps.
