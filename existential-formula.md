# Existential formula

↑ **Parent:** [Existential quantification](existential-quantification.md)

An existential formula is an existential block followed by a [quantifier-free formula](quantifier-free-formula.md), with remaining free variables serving as parameters. Truth is preserved by [structure embeddings](structure-embedding.md). Reflection of truth for every such formula defines an [existential embedding](existential-embedding.md).
