# Absorptivity

↑ **Parent:** [Blackbody](blackbody.md)

The fraction of incident radiant [power](power.md) absorbed under stated spectral and angular conditions. At thermal [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md), [Kirchhoff's law of thermal radiation](kirchhoff-s-law-of-thermal-radiation.md) equates matching directional spectral absorptivity and [emissivity](emissivity.md). A [blackbody](blackbody.md) has both equal to one.
