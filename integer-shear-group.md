# Integer shear group

↑ **Parent:** [Infinite cyclic group](infinite-cyclic-group.md)

These [invertible matrices](invertible-matrix.md), for $n\in\mathbb Z$, form an [infinite cyclic group](infinite-cyclic-group.md). The displayed multiplication rule, $B_0=I$, and $B_n^{-1}=B_{-n}$ give a [group isomorphism](group-isomorphism.md) from the additive [group of integers](additive-group-of-integers.md) via $n\mapsto B_n$. Its generator is $B_1$. The [subgroups of an infinite cyclic group](subgroup-of-an-infinite-cyclic-group.md) become $\{B_{mk}:k\in\mathbb Z\}$ for integers $m\geq0$, and their [subgroup indices](index-of-a-subgroup.md) are $m$ when $m>0$. The case $m=0$ is the trivial subgroup and has infinite index. As transformations, these matrices send $(x,y)$ to $(x+ny,y)$.
