# Holomorphic conormal sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Holomorphic normal bundle](holomorphic-normal-bundle.md)

$$
0\longrightarrow N_{Y/X}^*\longrightarrow\Omega_X^1|_Y\longrightarrow\Omega_Y^1\longrightarrow0.
$$
