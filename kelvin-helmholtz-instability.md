# Kelvin-Helmholtz instability

↑ **Parent:** [Vortex sheet](vortex-sheet.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelvin–Helmholtz_instability)

For a planar vortex sheet separating equal-density streams of velocities $U_1$ and $U_2$, a mode of nonzero [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) $k$ has complex growth exponent

$$
\sigma=-ik\frac{U_1+U_2}{2}
\mathbin{\pm}\frac{|k|\,|U_1-U_2|}{2}.
$$

One sign has positive [growth rate](growth-rate.md), so every nonzero wavenumber is unstable in the inviscid zero-thickness model.
