# Maxwell fluid dynamic modulus

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamic modulus](dynamic-modulus.md)

A series [spring](spring.md) of modulus $G$ and [dashpot](dashpot.md) of [viscosity](dynamic-viscosity-split.md) $\eta$ have $\tau=\eta/G$ and $\dot\gamma=\dot\sigma/G+\sigma/\eta$. With $e^{i\omega t}$ amplitudes, $G'=G(\omega\tau)^2/[1+(\omega\tau)^2]$ and $G''=G\omega\tau/[1+(\omega\tau)^2]$. Low [frequencies](frequency.md) are fluid-like; high [frequencies](frequency.md) retain an elastic plateau. The loss peaks at $\omega\tau=1$.
