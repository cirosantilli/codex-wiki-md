# Permittivity tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Permittivity](permittivity-split.md)

The [permittivity tensor](permittivity-tensor.md) is the linear material-response matrix relating [electric displacement field](electric-displacement-field.md) to [electric field](electric-field-split.md) at a specified [frequency](frequency.md). An isotropic local medium has $\epsilon_{ij}=\epsilon\delta_{ij}$. A magnetically biased transparent medium can have an imaginary antisymmetric transverse contribution; its eigenmodes can have opposite [circular polarizations](circular-polarization.md) and different [refractive indices](refractive-index.md).
