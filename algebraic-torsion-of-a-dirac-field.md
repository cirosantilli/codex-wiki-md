# Algebraic torsion of a Dirac field

↑ **Parent:** [Einstein-Cartan theory](einstein-cartan-theory.md)

Use $K_{abc}=e_a{}^\mu(\omega-\omega(e))_{\mu bc}$ and $T_{abc}=K_{bac}-K_{cab}$. For the symmetrized Dirac [action](action.md) $-e\bar\chi\gamma^\mu\overleftrightarrow\nabla_\mu\chi/2-em\bar\chi\chi$, varying the [spin connection](spin-connection.md) gives $-e\bar\chi\gamma^{\mu ab}\chi\,\delta\omega_{\mu ab}/4$. Only totally antisymmetric contorsion is sourced. Its [action](action.md) is $-eK^2/(2\kappa^2)-eK_{abc}\bar\chi\gamma^{abc}\chi/4$, hence the stated solution. Substitution generates $e\kappa^2(\bar\chi\gamma^{abc}\chi)(\bar\chi\gamma_{abc}\chi)/32$. The torsion is an [auxiliary field](auxiliary-field.md), not an independent propagating degree of freedom.
