# Control disturbance

↑ **Parent:** [Plant (control theory)](plant-control-theory.md)

A [control disturbance](control-disturbance.md) is an influence on the [controlled plant](plant-control-theory.md) that is not the chosen [control input](control-input.md). In $\dot x=u+w$, a constant $w=0.2$ increases $x$ by $0.2$ per unit $t$ whenever $u=0$.
