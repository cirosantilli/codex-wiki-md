# Mahler coefficient exponential generating function

↑ **Parent:** [Mahler coefficient](mahler-coefficient.md)

For the [Mahler coefficients](mahler-coefficient.md) of a [continuous function](continuous-function.md) on the [p-adic integers](p-adic-integer.md), finite [binomial inversion](binomial-inversion.md) gives $c_n=\sum_{j=0}^n(-1)^{n-j}\binom njf(j)$. Equivalently their exponential generating function is the displayed identity in the formal power-series ring $\mathbb Q_p[[T]]$. Each coefficient involves only finitely many terms; no claim of convergence for every $p$-adic value of $T$ is needed.
