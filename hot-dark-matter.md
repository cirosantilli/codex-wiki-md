# Hot dark matter

↑ **Parent:** [Dark matter](dark-matter.md)

A matter component with relativistic motion during an important part of structure formation. Its [free streaming](free-streaming.md) suppresses small-scale perturbations. Making a light thermal [neutrino](neutrino.md) Majorana rather than Dirac does not by itself turn this component into [cold dark matter](cold-dark-matter.md).
