<h1 id="3/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Solving $u=F(x)$ gives $1-(1-p)e^{-\beta x}=p^{1-u}$. Hence the [quantile function](../../../../../quantile-function.md) is

$$
\boxed{F^{-1}(u)=-\frac1\beta\log\!\left(\frac{1-p^{1-u}}{1-p}\right),\qquad0<u<1.}
$$

For such $u$, $p<p^{1-u}<1$, making the [logarithm](../../../../../logarithm.md)'s argument lie in $(0,1)$ and the output positive. Its endpoint limits are $F^{-1}(0)=0$ and $F^{-1}(1)=+\infty$. Substitution into (i)'s [cumulative distribution function](../../../../../cumulative-distribution-function.md) recovers $u$.
