↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Integrating the [joint probability density](../../../../../joint-probability-density.md) over the auxiliary coordinate, as in (i), gives the [marginal distribution](../../../../../marginal-distribution.md)

$$
\boxed{\pi_X(x)=\int_0^1\pi(x,u)\,du=p(x).}
$$

In particular, augmenting with $U$ preserves the target [probability distribution](../../../../../probability-distribution-split.md) of $X$. The other [marginal distribution](../../../../../marginal-distribution.md), though not needed to identify $X$, is $\pi_U(u)=M\int_\Omega q(x)\mathbf1_{\{w(x)\geq Mu\}}\,dx$ for $0<u<1$. It need not be a [uniform distribution](../../../../../continuous-uniform-distribution.md) on $(0,1)$.
