# Shifted Chebyshev equation and polynomial termination

↑ **Parent:** [Chebyshev polynomial](chebyshev-polynomial.md)

At zero the [indicial equation](indicial-equation.md) is $\sigma(2\sigma-1)=0$. The two [Frobenius series](frobenius-solution.md) have exponents zero and one half, with recurrence

$$
a_{n+1}=\frac{\alpha^2-(n+\sigma)^2}{(n+\sigma+1)(2n+2\sigma+1)}a_n.
$$

The denominators never vanish for these exponents. Unless a series terminates its coefficient ratio tends to $-1/2$, giving convergence within distance two of zero. If $\alpha=m$ is a [positive integer](positive-integer.md), the exponent-zero series terminates at degree $m$. The substitution $t=z+1$ gives the standard [Chebyshev differential equation](chebyshev-differential-equation.md), and this solution is a multiple of $T_m(z+1)$. For half-integer $\alpha$, termination can instead occur in the power-series factor of the exponent-one-half solution.
