# Protein tyrosine phosphatase

↑ **Parent:** [Protein phosphatase](protein-phosphatase.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protein_tyrosine_phosphatase)

A [protein tyrosine phosphatase](protein-tyrosine-phosphatase.md) acts on [phosphotyrosine residues](phosphotyrosine-residue.md). Removal can undo a particular receptor modification; its consequences for the whole [signal transduction](signal-transduction-split.md) pathway depend on the target.
