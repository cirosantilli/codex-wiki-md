# Irreducible mass

↑ **Parent:** [Kerr black hole](kerr-black-hole.md)

The [irreducible mass](irreducible-mass.md) of a four-dimensional uncharged [Kerr black hole](kerr-black-hole.md) is $M_{\mathrm{irr}}=\sqrt{A/(16\pi)}$. Its [mass](mass.md) satisfies $M^2=M_{\mathrm{irr}}^2+J^2/(4M_{\mathrm{irr}}^2)$. [Hawking's area theorem](hawking-s-area-theorem.md) prevents a classical process from decreasing $M_{\mathrm{irr}}$ under the theorem's energy and global hypotheses.
