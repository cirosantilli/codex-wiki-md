# Constant polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial](polynomial-split.md)

A [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) consisting only of a [constant term](constant-term.md). Over an [integral domain](integral-domain.md), a nonzero [constant polynomial](constant-polynomial.md) has [polynomial degree](degree-of-a-polynomial.md) zero; it is a [unit](unit-in-a-ring.md) of the [polynomial ring](polynomial-ring.md) exactly when its value is a [unit](unit-in-a-ring.md) of the coefficient [ring](ring.md).
