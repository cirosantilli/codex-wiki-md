# Brownian exit-time ball averaging identity

↑ **Parent:** [Uniform ball sampling by Brownian stopping](uniform-ball-sampling-by-brownian-stopping.md)

Let $g_D(x)=\mathbb E_xT_D$, possibly infinite, and suppose the closed ball $\overline{B(x,r)}$ lies in the open set $D$. A [Strong Markov property](strong-markov-property.md) decomposition at an [independent](independent-random-variables.md) random-radius exit time, using [uniform ball sampling by Brownian stopping](uniform-ball-sampling-by-brownian-stopping.md), gives the displayed identity in the nonnegative extended reals. Indeed the expected exit time from a radius-$s$ ball started at its centre is $s^2/n$, and averaging this against $ns^{n-1}/r^n$ gives $r^2/(n+2)$. The identity gives local integrability whenever $g_D(x)$ is finite.
