# Minimal exponential family

↑ **Parent:** [Exponential family](exponential-family-split.md)

An order-$p$ [exponential family](exponential-family-split.md) is minimal if no nonzero linear combination of its $p$ canonical [sufficient statistics](sufficient-statistic.md) is almost surely constant under the base measure. Equivalently, its sufficient-statistic support lies in no proper affine hyperplane. Its [cumulant function](cumulant-function-of-an-exponential-family.md) then has positive-definite [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) on the interior natural domain, because that Hessian is a [covariance matrix](covariance-matrix.md).
