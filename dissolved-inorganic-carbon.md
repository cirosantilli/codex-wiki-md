# Dissolved inorganic carbon

↑ **Parent:** [Carbon cycle](carbon-cycle.md)

Dissolved inorganic carbon is the total dissolved carbon in inorganic species, principally aqueous [carbon dioxide](carbon-dioxide.md), bicarbonate and carbonate. A reservoir model may express its [concentration](concentration.md) in moles of carbon per kilogram of [water](water.md). Air–sea exchange, river inputs, carbonate chemistry and burial affect its inventory; a single linear [concentration](concentration.md)-[pressure](pressure-split.md) relation is a model simplification.
