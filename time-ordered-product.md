# Time-ordered product

↑ **Parent:** [Time ordering](time-ordering.md)

A [time-ordered product](time-ordered-product.md) sorts operator insertions from latest time on the left to earliest time on the right. Reordering odd [fermionic operators](fermionic-operator.md) contributes the sign of their permutation. For bosonic $A,B$, $T[A(t)B(t')]=\theta(t-t')A(t)B(t')+\theta(t'-t)B(t')A(t)$. Vacuum expectations of free [time-ordered products](time-ordered-product.md) are evaluated by the [Wick theorem](wick-s-theorem.md).
