# Order characteristic variety of a Weyl algebra module

↑ **Parent:** [Dimension of a Weyl algebra module for the order filtration](dimension-of-a-weyl-algebra-module-for-the-order-filtration.md)

The [algebraic set](algebraic-set.md) defined by the [annihilator of a module](annihilator-of-a-module.md) of the [associated graded module](associated-graded-module.md) for a [good filtration for the order filtration](good-filtration-for-the-order-filtration.md) is the order characteristic variety. It lies in [affine space](affine-space.md) with coordinates $x_1,\ldots,x_n,\xi_1,\ldots,\xi_n$ and is invariant under scaling the $\xi_i$. Its [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) is the [dimension of a Weyl algebra module for the order filtration](dimension-of-a-weyl-algebra-module-for-the-order-filtration.md). The [order filtration of a Weyl algebra](order-filtration-of-a-weyl-algebra.md) assigns degrees zero to the $x_i$ and one to the $\xi_i$; a characteristic variety formed with the [Bernstein filtration](bernstein-filtration.md) need not be the same [algebraic set](algebraic-set.md).
