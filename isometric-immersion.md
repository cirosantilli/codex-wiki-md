# Isometric immersion

↑ **Parent:** [Immersion](immersion.md)

An isometric immersion is an [immersion](immersion.md) $f:M\to N$ between [Riemannian manifolds](riemannian-manifold.md) whose derivative preserves the [inner products](inner-product.md) of [tangent vectors](tangent-vector.md); equivalently, $f^*g_N=g_M$. Its derivative is injective and the target may have larger dimension. A [local isometry](local-isometry.md) additionally requires a [local diffeomorphism](local-diffeomorphism.md), so its tangent maps are [linear isomorphisms](linear-isomorphism.md). For example, a [sphere](sphere.md) with its induced [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md) is isometrically immersed in [Euclidean space](euclidean-norm.md), although its positive intrinsic [sectional curvature](sectional-curvature.md) differs from that of the ambient space.
