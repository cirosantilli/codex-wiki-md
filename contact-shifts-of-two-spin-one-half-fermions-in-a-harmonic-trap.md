# Contact shifts of two spin-one-half fermions in a harmonic trap

↑ **Parent:** [Exchange symmetry of two identical spin-one-half fermions](exchange-symmetry-of-two-identical-spin-one-half-fermions.md)

For two identical [fermions](fermion.md) of mass $m$ in the same one-dimensional [quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md), use [center of mass](center-of-mass.md) $X=(x_1+x_2)/2$ and separation $r=x_1-x_2$. The corresponding oscillator masses are $2m$ and [reduced mass](reduced-mass.md) $m/2$, with the same frequency $\omega$. If $N,n$ are their occupation numbers, the unperturbed energy is $\hbar\omega(N+n+1)$. The [fermionic exchange symmetry](fermi-dirac-statistics.md) requires even $n$ with a [spin-one-half singlet state](spin-one-half-singlet-state.md) and odd $n$ with a [spin-one-half triplet state](spin-one-half-triplet-state.md).

For a [delta potential](delta-potential.md) $\lambda\delta(r)$ the first-order shift is $\lambda|\varphi_n^{\mathrm{rel}}(0)|^2$. Every odd relative [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) vanishes at contact, so these states remain exact unaffected eigenstates of the spatial problem. For the even relative ground state, the [oscillator length](oscillator-length.md) is $\sqrt2\ell$, where $\ell=\sqrt{\hbar/(m\omega)}$, and the shift is $\lambda/(\sqrt{2\pi}\ell)$, independent of $N$. This separates interaction-sensitive singlets from contact-insensitive triplets.
