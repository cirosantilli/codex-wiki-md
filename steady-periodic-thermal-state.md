# Steady periodic thermal state

↑ **Parent:** [Ångström periodic heating method](angstrom-periodic-heating-method.md)

A [steady periodic thermal state](steady-periodic-thermal-state.md) repeats each drive period $P$ after the startup transient has decayed. It is time dependent, unlike a time-independent [steady state](steady-state.md). For [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) $\omega=2\pi/P$, its single-frequency part has the [phasor](phasor.md) representation $\theta(x,t)=\operatorname{Re}\{\widehat\theta(x)e^{i\omega t}\}$. Here $\theta$ is the oscillating [temperature](temperature-split.md) about the mean profile. Repeating the fitted [amplitudes](wave-amplitude.md) and [wave phases](phase-waves.md) over successive cycles checks that startup drift is small; merely completing one drive cycle is insufficient.
