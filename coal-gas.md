# Coal gas

↑ **Parent:** [Mixture](mixture-split.md)

A historical [fuel](fuel.md) gas mixture made by destructive distillation of [coal](coal.md). Its composition depends on the feed and retort conditions; it is not synonymous with natural gas or pure [methane](methane.md).
