# Renormalization-group relevance

↑ **Parent:** [Renormalization group](renormalization-group.md)

The relevance of a perturbation is determined by the eigenvalue of its dimensionless coupling under linearized [renormalization-group flow](renormalization-group-flow.md). Positive, negative and zero eigenvalues define relevant, irrelevant and marginal perturbations, respectively.
