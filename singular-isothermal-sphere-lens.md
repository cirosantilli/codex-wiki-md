# Singular isothermal sphere lens

↑ **Parent:** [Singular isothermal sphere](singular-isothermal-sphere.md)

The projected density $\Sigma(R)=\sigma^2/(2GR)$ gives cylindrical mass $\pi\sigma^2R/G$, so the reduced deflection magnitude is the constant displayed angular [Einstein radius](einstein-radius.md). The signed [thin gravitational lens equation](thin-gravitational-lens-equation.md) is $\beta=\theta-\theta_E\operatorname{sgn}\theta$. A source with $0<\beta<\theta_E$ has two images; one with $\beta>\theta_E$ has one. The [lensing convergence](lensing-convergence.md) and [lensing shear](lensing-shear.md) magnitude are both $\theta_E/(2|\theta|)$.
