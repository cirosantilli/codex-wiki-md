# Wilsonian effective action

↑ **Parent:** [Effective action](effective-action.md)

A Wilsonian effective action is obtained by integrating out field modes above a chosen momentum scale while retaining lower-momentum modes as backgrounds. Its local couplings change with the scale according to a [renormalization-group flow](renormalization-group-flow.md).
