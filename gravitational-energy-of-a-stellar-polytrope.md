# Gravitational energy of a stellar polytrope

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar polytrope](stellar-polytrope.md)

For a spherical [stellar polytrope](stellar-polytrope.md) of finite radius and zero surface pressure, the [specific enthalpy](specific-enthalpy.md) is $(n+1)P/\rho$. [Hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) gives $h+\Phi=-GM/R$, hence $(n+1)\int P\,dV=-GM^2/R-2\Omega$. Eliminating the pressure integral with the [stellar virial theorem](stellar-virial-theorem.md) proves the displayed formula. For a gas [adiabatic stellar polytrope](adiabatic-stellar-polytrope.md), $n=1/(\gamma-1)$ and $U=GM^2/[(5\gamma-6)R]$; a general [polytropic index](polytropic-index.md) need not have this relation to the [specific-heat ratio](heat-capacity-ratio.md).
