# Microscope eyepiece

↑ **Parent:** [Optical microscope](optical-microscope.md)

The viewing [optical lens](lens-optics.md) assembly that acts as a magnifier of the [intermediate microscope image](intermediate-microscope-image.md). With that image at its front [focal plane](focal-plane.md), the outgoing [light rays](light-ray.md) from each point are parallel. In the [small-angle approximation](small-angle-approximation.md), the viewed [angle](angle.md) is $h_i/f_e$, versus $h_i/D$ for the same image-sized object at reference distance $D$, giving $M_e=D/f_e$.[University Physics Volume 3, §2.8, relaxed viewing and Eq. 2.37](university-physics-volume-3-by-samuel-j-ling-and-others/2-8-microscopes-and-telescopes.md)

A finite-distance [virtual image](virtual-image.md) requires a different eyepiece conjugate. In the ideal [thin lens](thin-lens.md) model, with the eye close to the eyepiece and the [virtual image](virtual-image.md) at distance $D$, the [angular magnification](angular-magnification.md) becomes $1+D/f_e$. State the viewing condition before multiplying by the objective's [transverse magnification](transverse-magnification.md); the relaxed-eye example above uses $D/f_e$.
