# Rotlet interaction of two spheres

↑ **Parent:** [Method of reflections for Stokes flow](method-of-reflections-for-stokes-flow.md)

A torque-driven sphere advects and rotates a distant force-free sphere through its [rotlet](rotlet.md). The incident rate of strain makes the second sphere emit a [stresslet](force-dipole-flow.md); the vorticity of that reflected field supplies the first correction to the original sphere's angular velocity.
