# Synchrotron cooling

↑ **Parent:** [Synchrotron radiation](synchrotron-radiation.md)

At fixed [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md), ultrarelativistic [electrons](electron.md) lose [energy](energy-split.md) through [synchrotron radiation](synchrotron-radiation.md) at a rate proportional to $E^2$. For steady injection $Q(E)\propto E^{-s}$, negligible escape and $s>1$, integrating the energy-space continuity equation gives $N(E)=|\dot E|^{-1}\int_E^\infty Q(E')\,dE'\propto E^{-(s+1)}$. Above the cooling break, the optically thin [radiation spectral index](radiation-spectral-index.md) therefore steepens from $(s-1)/2$ to $s/2$.
