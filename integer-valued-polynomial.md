# Integer-valued polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial ring](polynomial-ring.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Integer-valued_polynomial)

For an [integral domain](integral-domain.md) $R$, an integer-valued [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) is a [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) over its [fraction field](field-of-fractions.md) that maps $R$ into itself. Its coefficients need not lie in $R$: $X(X-1)/2$ maps $\mathbb Z$ into $\mathbb Z$. These [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) form a [ring](ring.md) containing $R[X]$.
