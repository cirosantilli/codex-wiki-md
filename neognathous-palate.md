# Neognathous palate

↑ **Parent:** [Palate](palate.md)

An avian palatal arrangement with more mobile pterygoid–palatine articulation and reduced bracing compared with the [palaeognathous palate](palaeognathous-palate.md). Mobility varies among species and cannot be read from the group name alone.
