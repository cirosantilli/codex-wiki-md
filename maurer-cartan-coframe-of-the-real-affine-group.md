# Maurer-Cartan coframe of the real affine group

↑ **Parent:** [Orientation-preserving affine group of the real line](orientation-preserving-affine-group-of-the-real-line.md)

In the faithful matrices $g=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$ with $a>0$, the [Maurer-Cartan form](maurer-cartan-form.md) has entries $da/a$ and $db/a$. Thus the [left-invariant differential forms](left-invariant-differential-form.md) $\sigma^D=da/a$, $\sigma^T=db/a$ obey $d\sigma^D=0$ and $d\sigma^T=-\sigma^D\wedge\sigma^T$. The [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) generators satisfy $[D,T]=T$.
