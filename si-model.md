# SI model

↑ **Parent:** [Compartmental models (epidemiology)](compartmental-models-epidemiology.md)

The SI model divides a closed population into susceptible and permanently infectious compartments. Under frequency-dependent homogeneous mixing,

$$
\dot S=-\beta SI/N,
\qquad
\dot I=\beta SI/N,
$$

and [conservation of population](conservation-of-population.md) gives $S+I=N$.
