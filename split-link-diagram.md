# Split link diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Split link](split-link.md)

A split diagram has two nonempty parts separated by a simple closed curve disjoint from the diagram. For a two-component [link](link.md), this is equivalent to disconnectedness of its projection graph. An [alternating link diagram](alternating-link-diagram.md) representing a split [link](link.md) cannot have a connected projection graph, as its Jones value at minus one detects a nonzero spanning-tree count.
