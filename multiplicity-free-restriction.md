# Multiplicity-free restriction

↑ **Parent:** [Group representation](group-representation.md)

Restriction of an [irreducible representation](irreducible-representation.md) to a [subgroup](subgroup.md) is multiplicity-free when its [semisimple representation](semisimple-representation.md) decomposition has no repeated irreducible summands. Over the [complex numbers](complex-number.md), for [finite groups](finite-group.md), this is equivalent to the restricted module's endomorphism algebra being commutative. This follows by writing that algebra as a product of [matrix algebras](matrix-algebra.md) of sizes equal to the multiplicities.
