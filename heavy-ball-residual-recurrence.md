# Heavy-ball residual recurrence

↑ **Parent:** [Heavy-ball method](heavy-ball-method.md)

For $F(x)=x^TAx/2-b^Tx$ and $r_k=b-Ax_k$,

$$
r_{k+1}=((1+\beta)I-\alpha A)r_k-\beta r_{k-1}.
$$

Thus $r_k$ is a polynomial in $A$ applied to the initial residual and lies in the corresponding [Krylov subspace](krylov-subspace.md).
