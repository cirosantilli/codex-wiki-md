# Diamond operator

↑ **Parent:** [Gamma 1 level structure on a complex lattice](gamma-1-level-structure-on-a-complex-lattice.md)

Multiplication of a marked point by a unit modulo $N$ gives the diamond action. In modular coordinates this is the slash action of a lift in $\Gamma_0(N)$ with lower-right entry $d$. Its [Dirichlet character](dirichlet-character.md) eigenspaces give nebentypus [modular forms](modular-form.md). For a nonzero form, the action of $-1$ imposes $\chi(-1)=(-1)^k$.
