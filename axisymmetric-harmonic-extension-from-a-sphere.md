# Axisymmetric harmonic extension from a sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Axisymmetric harmonic function](axisymmetric-harmonic-function.md)

For boundary data $f(u)$ on the sphere $r=a$, with $u=\cos\theta$, define its [Legendre polynomial](legendre-polynomial.md) coefficients by $f_\ell=(2\ell+1)\int_{-1}^1f(u)P_\ell(u)\,du/2$. [Separation of variables](separation-of-variables.md) in the [Laplace equation](laplace-equation.md) gives radial factors $r^\ell$ and $r^{-\ell-1}$. Regularity at the origin selects the first for the interior [Dirichlet problem](dirichlet-problem.md); decay at infinity selects the second for the exterior problem. Matching each boundary coefficient gives the displayed [series](series-mathematics.md). For continuous boundary data the resulting [harmonic functions](harmonic-function.md) attain those data, with convergence interpreted through their harmonic extension rather than requiring uniform convergence of the unweighted boundary series. The [maximum principle for harmonic functions](maximum-principle-for-harmonic-functions.md) proves uniqueness in the bounded interior and decaying exterior classes.
