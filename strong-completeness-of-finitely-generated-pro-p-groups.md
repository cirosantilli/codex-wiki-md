# Strong completeness of finitely generated pro-p groups

↑ **Parent:** [Pro-p group](pro-p-group.md)

Every abstract [subgroup](subgroup.md) of finite index in a finitely generated [pro-p group](pro-p-group.md) is open. [Fixed-generator commutator collection in nilpotent groups](fixed-generator-commutator-collection-in-nilpotent-groups.md) makes the abstract [commutator subgroup](commutator-subgroup.md) closed. The resulting abelian quotient is a quotient of a finite power of the [p-adic integers](p-adic-integer.md), so its subgroup of $p$th powers is closed and of finite index. Thus the algebraic $G^p[G,G]$ is open and every abstract index-$p$ [subgroup](subgroup.md) is open. An arbitrary finite quotient is a [finite p-group](finite-p-group.md); induction on its order, using a normal subgroup of index $p$ and the [Schreier lemma](reidemeister-schreier-theorem.md), gives the assertion. The [normal core](core-group-theory.md) reduces nonnormal finite-index [subgroups](subgroup.md) to this case.
