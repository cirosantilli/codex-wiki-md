# Slow neutron

↑ **Parent:** [Neutron](neutron-split.md)

A slow [neutron](neutron-split.md) has low [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) relative to the fast [neutrons](neutron-split.md) commonly emitted in [nuclear reactions](nuclear-reaction.md). The term covers an [energy](energy-split.md) range, rather than a single [speed](speed.md). A [neutron moderator](neutron-moderator.md) transfers [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) to surrounding matter through collisions. Slowing a [neutron](neutron-split.md) can increase its chance of causing particular [neutron capture](neutron-capture.md) reactions, even though each collision has reduced its [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md). The [Fermi slow-neutron activation experiment](fermi-slow-neutron-activation-experiment.md) made this surprising distinction experimentally visible.
