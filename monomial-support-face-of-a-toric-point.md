# Monomial support face of a toric point

↑ **Parent:** [Orbit-cone correspondence](orbit-cone-correspondence.md)

At a point $p$ of $\operatorname{Spec}k[C\cap M]$, the exponents $m$ with $\chi^m(p)\ne0$ form a [semigroup face](face-of-an-additive-monoid.md). They are the lattice points of one face of $C$. Points with that same support face form a single algebraic torus orbit: their nonzero monomial evaluations extend to homomorphisms from the lattice generated by the face into $k^*$, and restriction from the full torus is surjective.
