# Precursor film

↑ **Parent:** [Thin-film equation](thin-film-equation.md)

A thin liquid layer extending ahead of a thicker drop can replace a sharp zero-height [contact line](contact-line.md) in a continuum film model. Keeping the height positive avoids the zero-mobility singularity of a purely no-slip $h^3$ [lubrication law](lubrication-theory.md) at the apparent contact edge. Additional microscopic surface interactions usually determine its thickness and matching to the macroscopic apparent [contact angle](contact-angle.md). It is one possible contact-line regularization, rather than a universal assumption for every substrate.
