# Radial spring oscillator on a rotating guide

↑ **Parent:** [Simple harmonic motion](simple-harmonic-motion.md)

A [point mass](point-mass.md) constrained to a straight guide through the axis of a uniformly [rotating reference frame](rotating-reference-frame.md), with signed central displacement $x$ and [Hooke's law](hooke-s-law.md) [force](force-split.md) $-kx$, satisfies $m\ddot x=-kx+m\Omega^2x$. The transverse [Coriolis force](coriolis-force.md) is balanced by the guide. The central [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) is stable for $\Omega^2<k/m$, neutral at equality and unstable above it. The [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) is the positive square root in the title.
