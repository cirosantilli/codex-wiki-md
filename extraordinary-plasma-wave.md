# Extraordinary plasma wave

↑ **Parent:** [Cold magnetized plasma](cold-magnetized-plasma.md)

For propagation perpendicular to the equilibrium [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md), this [plasma](plasma-physics.md) [wave](wave-split.md) has its [electric field](electric-field-split.md) perpendicular to that [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) and generally both parallel and perpendicular to its [wavevector](wavevector.md). Its [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation-split.md) is $n^2=RL/S$, using the [magnetized cold-plasma dielectric tensor](magnetized-cold-plasma-dielectric-tensor.md) conventions $R=S-g$, $L=S+g$.
