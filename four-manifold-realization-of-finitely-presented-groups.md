# Four-manifold realization of finitely presented groups

↑ **Parent:** [Smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md)

Every finitely presented [group](group-split.md) is the [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md) of a closed connected smooth oriented four-manifold. Build a compact four-dimensional [handle decomposition](handle-decomposition.md) with one zero-handle, a one-handle for each [generator of a group](generator-of-a-group.md) and a two-handle for each [relator](relator.md). Turning the [handle decomposition](handle-decomposition.md) upside down shows that its boundary surjects on the [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md). Its [double of a manifold](double-of-a-manifold.md) has the same [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md) by the [Seifert-van Kampen theorem](seifert-van-kampen-theorem.md).
