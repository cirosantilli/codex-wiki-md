# Terminal-age main sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Main sequence](main-sequence.md)

The terminal-age main sequence denotes the end of central [hydrogen burning](hydrogen-burning.md). It does not mark complete exhaustion of hydrogen throughout the star; a [hydrogen-burning shell](hydrogen-burning-shell.md) can subsequently supply its luminosity.
