# Adaptive substitution

↑ **Parent:** [Population genetics](population-genetics-split.md)

An inherited sequence change that spreads to [allele fixation](allele-fixation.md) because of a favorable effect on [genotype fitness](genotype-fitness.md) in the relevant context. The distribution of effects among substitutions differs from that among newly arising [mutations](mutation.md).
