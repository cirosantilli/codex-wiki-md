# Nonnegativity of the Hodge Laplacian

↑ **Parent:** [Hodge Laplacian](hodge-laplacian.md)

On a compact oriented [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md) without boundary, the [codifferential](codifferential.md) is the $L^2$ [adjoint operator](adjoint-operator.md) of the [exterior derivative](exterior-derivative.md). Consequently

$$
\langle\Delta\alpha,\alpha\rangle_{L^2}
=\lVert d\alpha\rVert_{L^2}^2+\lVert\delta\alpha\rVert_{L^2}^2\geq0.
$$

Every [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) of the Hodge Laplacian is therefore nonnegative.
