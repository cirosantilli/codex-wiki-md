# Free product

↑ **Parent:** [Amalgamated free product](amalgamated-free-product.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_product)

The free product $A*B$ is the [amalgamated free product](amalgamated-free-product.md) over the trivial group. Its nonidentity elements have unique reduced alternating normal forms.
