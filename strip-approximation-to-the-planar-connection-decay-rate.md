# Strip approximation to the planar connection decay rate

↑ **Parent:** [Connection decay rate in a percolation strip](connection-decay-rate-in-a-percolation-strip.md)

Let $q(n)$ be the unrestricted planar two-point [percolation two-point connection probability](percolation-two-point-connection-probability.md). Every finite connecting [graph path](path-in-a-graph.md) is contained in some strip, so $q_k(n)\uparrow q(n)$ for each fixed $n$. Therefore

$$
\lim_{k\to\infty}f_k(p)=\inf_{k\geq1}\inf_{n\geq1}\frac{-\log q_k(n)}n=\inf_{n\geq1}\frac{-\log q(n)}n.
$$

The equality uses commuting infima and monotonicity, not an unjustified interchange of two general limits. The right-hand side is the whole-plane connection decay rate by the [Fekete lemma](fekete-s-lemma.md).
