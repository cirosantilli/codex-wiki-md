# Local alpha-Omega dynamo wave dispersion

↑ **Parent:** [Parker dynamo wave](parker-dynamo-wave.md)

Take a right-handed local Cartesian frame with $x$ towards the equator, $y$ azimuthal and $z$ radially outwards in the northern hemisphere. A local radial shear $U_y=Sz$ and a [poloidal magnetic field](poloidal-magnetic-field.md) represented by $A\widehat{\mathbf y}$ give the [alpha-Omega dynamo](alpha-omega-dynamo.md) equations $A_t=\alpha B+\eta_T A_{xx}$ and $B_t=S A_x+\eta_T B_{xx}$. Substitution of a [Fourier mode](fourier-mode.md) proportional to $e^{pt+ikx}$ gives the displayed [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation.md). The growing branch has $\operatorname{Re}p=-\eta_Tk^2+\sqrt{|\alpha Sk|/2}$ and $\operatorname{Im}p=\operatorname{sgn}(\alpha Sk)\sqrt{|\alpha Sk|/2}$. Its phase velocity is $-\operatorname{Im}p/k$: it propagates equatorwards when $\alpha S<0$, polewards when $\alpha S>0$. The result assumes local constant coefficients and no [meridional circulation in a star](meridional-circulation-in-a-star.md); advection and spatially separated field regeneration can alter migration.
