# Supersymmetric domain wall

↑ **Parent:** [Wess–Zumino model](wess-zumino-model.md)

In a canonical [Wess–Zumino model](wess-zumino-model.md), complete the static transverse energy into $|\phi'-e^{i\alpha}\overline{W'}|^2+2\operatorname{Re}(e^{-i\alpha}dW/dz)$. Choosing the phase of $\Delta W$ gives the tension bound. A solution of the displayed first-order equation attains it and preserves two of four real [supercharges](supersymmetry-generator.md). For $W=\mu^2\Phi/g-g\Phi^3/3$, $\phi(z)=(\mu/g)\tanh(\mu z)$ interpolates between the two vacua and has tension $8\mu^3/(3g^2)$.
