# Bertini smoothness theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Linear system of divisors](linear-system-of-divisors.md)

On a smooth variety over a field of characteristic zero, a general member of a basepoint-free [linear system of divisors](linear-system-of-divisors.md) is smooth. More generally it is smooth outside the base locus. Irreducibility is a separate assertion and requires suitable hypotheses on the associated morphism; a pencil composed with a map to a curve can have disconnected general divisors.
