# Right-angle Compton scattering in the centre-of-momentum frame

↑ **Parent:** [Four-momentum conservation](four-momentum-conservation.md)

For a [photon](photon.md) and a massive particle with zero total [momentum](momentum-split.md), their momenta are opposite and equal. Conservation of total [energy](energy-split.md) fixes the same [momentum](momentum-split.md) magnitude before and after elastic scattering, independently of direction. A right-angle [photon](photon.md) turn therefore makes the recoiling particle move perpendicular to the original [photon](photon.md) direction in that frame. Transform both [velocity](velocity.md) components, or the complete [four-momentum](four-momentum.md), to obtain the laboratory recoil; a transverse component is reduced by the boost [Lorentz factor](lorentz-factor.md) when the particle has zero longitudinal [velocity](velocity.md) in the zero-momentum frame.
