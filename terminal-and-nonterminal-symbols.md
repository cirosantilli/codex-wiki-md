# Terminal and nonterminal symbols

↑ **Parent:** [Formal language theory](formal-language-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terminal_and_nonterminal_symbols)

In a [formal grammar](formal-grammar.md), [terminal symbols](terminal-symbol.md) are symbols of completed words; a [nonterminal symbol](nonterminal-symbol.md) is an auxiliary symbol that may be replaced by a production. The two alphabets are disjoint.
