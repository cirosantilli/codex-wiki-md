# Acyclic intersection cover homology bound

↑ **Parent:** [Mayer–Vietoris sequence](mayer-vietoris-sequence.md)

If a space is a union of $n\geq2$ subcomplexes and every nonempty intersection is acyclic, its homology vanishes in degrees at least $n-1$. Induction with the [Mayer–Vietoris sequence](mayer-vietoris-sequence.md) proves the bound; the boundary of an $(n-1)$-simplex covered by its facets shows sharpness.
