# Feigenbaum attractor

↑ **Parent:** [Feigenbaum period-doubling map](feigenbaum-period-doubling-map.md)

The Feigenbaum attractor is the compact Cantor set obtained from the nested restrictive-interval partitions of the [Feigenbaum map](feigenbaum-period-doubling-map.md). Every level interval has two descendant intervals. Their lengths vary with symbolic address, so a geometric [topological pressure](topological-pressure.md) is useful for estimating its [Hausdorff dimension](hausdorff-dimension.md).
