# Family resemblance

↑ **Parent:** [Prototype theory](prototype-theory.md)

A pattern of overlapping attributes among members without requiring one attribute set shared identically by every member. The distribution of attributes in competing categories matters as well as their occurrence within one category. [Family resemblance](family-resemblance.md) can describe a category structure without specifying how it is learned or stored.
