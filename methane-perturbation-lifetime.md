# Methane perturbation lifetime

↑ **Parent:** [Atmospheric lifetime](atmospheric-lifetime.md)

The e-folding relaxation time of an added [methane](methane.md) burden after accounting for chemical feedbacks. It need not equal burden divided by the contemporaneous [methane](methane.md) loss rate: extra [methane](methane.md) can consume [hydroxyl radical](hydroxyl-radical.md) and reduce its own removal. A fixed-OH exponential estimate omits that coupled adjustment.
