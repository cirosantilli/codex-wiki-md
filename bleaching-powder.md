# Bleaching powder

↑ **Parent:** [Inorganic chemistry](inorganic-chemistry.md)

The historical commercial material made by exposing [calcium hydroxide](calcium-hydroxide.md) to [chlorine](chlorine.md). Its useful oxidizing content is described by [available chlorine](available-chlorine.md); it should not be identified unconditionally with pure [calcium hypochlorite](calcium-hypochlorite.md).
