# One-loop inverse gauge coupling evolution

↑ **Parent:** [Running coupling](running-coupling.md)

From $\mu\,dg_i/d\mu=b_i g_i^3/(16\pi^2)$ and $\alpha_i=g_i^2/(4\pi)$, the [chain rule](chain-rule.md) gives $d\alpha_i/d\log\mu=b_i\alpha_i^2/(2\pi)$. Taking its reciprocal makes the differential equation linear, proving the displayed relation. It assumes a fixed set of active fields and constant one-loop coefficients between the two scales; mass thresholds require piecewise matching. A negative coefficient gives [asymptotic freedom](asymptotic-freedom.md).
