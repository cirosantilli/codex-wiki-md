# Nonuniqueness of norm-preserving point evaluation extensions

↑ **Parent:** [Point evaluation functional](point-evaluation-functional.md)

View $C([0,1])$ inside the complex [Lp space](lp-space.md) $L^\infty((0,1))$. Fix $x_0\in[0,1]$. If a unit [function](function-split.md) $\psi$ satisfies $\|g+\psi\|_\infty\geq|g(x_0)|+1$ for every [continuous function](continuous-function.md) $g$, then the possible values at $\psi$ of norm-one extensions of [point evaluation](point-evaluation-functional.md) at $x_0$ form exactly the closed complex unit disc.

The estimate first excludes $\psi\in C([0,1])$. On the [direct sum](direct-sum.md) $C([0,1])\oplus\langle\psi\rangle$, prescribe $L_a(g+c\psi)=g(x_0)+ca$ for $|a|\leq1$. Scaling the estimate gives $\|g+c\psi\|_\infty\geq|g(x_0)|+|c|\geq|L_a(g+c\psi)|$, and $L_a(1)=1$ proves [operator norm](operator-norm.md) one. The [complex Hahn-Banach theorem](complex-hahn-banach-theorem.md) extends $L_a$ to the whole [Lp space](lp-space.md). No value outside the disc is possible because $\|\psi\|_\infty=1$.
