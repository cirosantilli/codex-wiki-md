# Principal divisor on a toric variety

↑ **Parent:** [Toric divisor](toric-divisor.md)

For a character $\chi^m$ of the dense torus,

$$
\operatorname{div}(\chi^m)=\sum_\rho\langle m,v_\rho\rangle D_\rho.
$$
