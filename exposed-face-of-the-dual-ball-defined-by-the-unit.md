# Exposed face of the dual ball defined by the unit

↑ **Parent:** [State on a C-star algebra](state-on-a-c-star-algebra.md)

For a nonzero unital [C-star algebra](c-star-algebra.md), a [positive functional on a C-star algebra](positive-functional-on-a-c-star-algebra.md) obeys $\|x\|=x(1_A)$, and conversely this equality for a bounded functional implies positivity. Therefore its [state on a C-star algebra](state-on-a-c-star-algebra.md) functionals are exactly the maximizers of the real linear evaluation $x\mapsto\operatorname{Re}x(1_A)$ on the closed dual unit ball. If a [convex combination](convex-combination.md) reaches that maximum, each constituent must reach it: each real part is at most one, and their weighted mean is one. The modulus bound then forces evaluation to equal one, so each constituent is again a state. Hence the state space is an [exposed face](exposed-face.md) and a [face of a convex set](face-of-a-convex-set.md).
