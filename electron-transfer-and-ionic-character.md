# Electron transfer and ionic character

↑ **Parent:** [Ionic bonding](ionic-bonding.md)

The bookkeeping steps $\mathrm{Na}\to\mathrm{Na}^++e^-$ and $\mathrm{Cl}+e^-\to\mathrm{Cl}^-$ conserve [electric charge](electric-charge.md) and describe the [Electron](electron.md) inventories of separated species. They do not establish a chronological mechanism in which a labeled [Electron](electron.md) jumps between two pre-existing [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) and becomes a classical object owned exclusively by one of them. In a bound material, [quantum mechanics](quantum-mechanics-split.md) determines the [electron density](electron-density.md) and the total [energy](energy.md).

[Ionic bonding](ionic-bonding.md) and a [covalent bond](covalent-bond.md) are useful limiting descriptions with a continuum between them. [Electronegativity](electronegativity.md) helps anticipate unequal sharing of [electron density](electron-density.md), but a numerical cutoff does not measure a universal fraction of [ionic bonding](ionic-bonding.md). A [partial atomic charge](partial-atomic-charge.md) depends on how the [electron density](electron-density.md) is partitioned; integer [formal charges](formal-charge.md) are a bookkeeping convention. Using $\pm e$ in a point-charge model therefore does not claim that every partition of the actual [electron density](electron-density.md) assigns exactly those values.[§30–2, “Chemical bonds in crystals”: gradations between covalent and ionic bonding](the-feynman-lectures-on-physics-volume-ii-by-richard-feynman-and-others.md)[§7.2, “Electronegativity and Bond Type”: general guide and exceptions](chemistry-2e-by-paul-flowers-and-others.md)
