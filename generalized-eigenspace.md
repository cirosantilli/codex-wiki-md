# Generalized eigenspace

↑ **Parent:** [Generalized eigenvector](generalized-eigenvector.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generalized_eigenspace)

The generalized eigenspace of a [linear operator](linear-operator.md) $A$ for an [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) $\lambda$ is the union of the kernels $\ker(A-\lambda I)^N$. In finite dimension this union stabilizes for sufficiently large $N$.
