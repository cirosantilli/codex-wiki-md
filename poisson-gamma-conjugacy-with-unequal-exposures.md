# Poisson-gamma conjugacy with unequal exposures

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson-gamma conjugacy](poisson-gamma-conjugacy.md)

Given $\Theta$, independent observations $Y_j$ have [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md) with means $m_j\Theta$ for known exposures. A [gamma distribution](gamma-distribution.md) prior of shape $\alpha$ and rate $\beta$ yields a gamma posterior with shape $\alpha+\sum_jY_j$ and rate $\beta+\sum_jm_j$. The likelihood kernel is $\theta^{\sum_jY_j}e^{-\theta\sum_jm_j}$. Thus the [posterior mean](posterior-mean.md) depends on total claims and total exposure, not the number of periods alone.
