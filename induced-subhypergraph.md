# Induced subhypergraph

↑ **Parent:** [Subhypergraph](subhypergraph.md)

For a subset $W$ of the [vertex set](vertex-set.md) of a [hypergraph](hypergraph-split.md) $H$, the induced subhypergraph retains exactly the edges wholly contained in $W$. This is the restriction used by a [hereditary hypergraph property](hereditary-hypergraph-property.md); it differs from intersecting every edge with $W$, which can change uniformity.
