# Radiation pressure on circumplanetary dust

↑ **Parent:** [Circumplanetary orbit](circumplanetary-orbit.md)

The leading uniform stellar [radiation pressure](radiation-pressure.md) is a differential [acceleration](acceleration.md) relative to a [planet](planet.md), of scale $\beta GM_\star/a_p^2$. Relative to planetocentric gravity at dust radius $a$, its magnitude is

$$
\eta_{\rm rad}=\beta\frac{M_\star}{M_p}\left(\frac a{a_p}\right)^2.
$$

Thus lying inside a [Hill sphere](hill-sphere.md) alone does not guarantee small radiative perturbations. Approximately circular tightly bound dust requires this ratio sufficiently small, as well as weak tides; the uniform radiative [force](force.md) can otherwise excite [orbital eccentricity](orbital-eccentricity.md) or unbind grains.
