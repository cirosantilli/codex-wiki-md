# Projectile motion

↑ **Parent:** [Classical mechanics](classical-mechanics-split.md)

Motion after launch under a specified [gravitational field](gravitational-field.md), with negligible [drag force](drag-physics.md) and no thrust. In a constant downward [gravitational acceleration](gravitational-acceleration.md) $g$, horizontal [velocity](velocity.md) is constant and vertical [position](position.md) obeys $y(t)=y_0+v_{y0}t-gt^2/2$. Eliminating time gives a [parabola](parabola.md) when horizontal velocity is nonzero. Over larger distances the changing direction and magnitude of gravity require an orbital model instead.
