# Testing a differential thermal actuator

↑ **Parent:** [Sun valve](sun-valve.md)

A proposed bench test can isolate the mechanism without a gas burner. Illuminate a blackened and a polished sample of matched material and geometry, record both [temperatures](temperature-split.md) and their relative displacement, then block the beam. Measure incident [irradiance](irradiance.md) separately. Swap the surface treatments, hold the ambient [temperature](temperature-split.md) fixed for the illumination test, and change it with the beam blocked for a common-heating control. This is a model-based experiment proposal, not a report of measurements.

A larger response of the dark sample would support a difference in absorbed [power](power.md); the displacement and cooling curves test the assumed [linear thermal expansion coefficient](linear-thermal-expansion-coefficient.md) and [time constant](time-constant.md). A stable illumination step that never reaches the switching gap challenges the chosen parameter model rather than the existence of [thermal expansion](thermal-expansion.md). Nonuniform sample [temperature](temperature-split.md), unequal losses and mechanical friction can separate the physical device from the ideal equations.
