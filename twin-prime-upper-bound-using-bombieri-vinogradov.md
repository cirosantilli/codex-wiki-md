<h1 id="twin-prime-upper-bound-using-bombieri-vinogradov">Twin-prime upper bound using Bombieri–Vinogradov</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Twin prime](twin-prime.md)

Apply the [Selberg upper-bound sieve](selberg-upper-bound-sieve.md) to the numbers $p+2$ with $p\leq x$ [prime](prime-number.md). For [odd](odd-function.md) [squarefree integers](squarefree-integer.md) $d$, the local count is $\pi(x;d,-2)$ and the density is $1/\phi(d)$. With sieve level $D=x^{1/3}$, the normalizing sum is $\gg\log x$ by the [truncated Euler-product lower bound](truncated-euler-product-lower-bound.md). The [Selberg least-common-multiple weights](selberg-least-common-multiple-weights.md) have [absolute values](absolute-value.md) at most $3^{\omega(d)}$. The [weighted arithmetic-progression error bound](weighted-arithmetic-progression-error-bound.md) and [Bombieri–Vinogradov theorem](bombieri-vinogradov-theorem.md) make their total error $O(x/\log^3x)$. Consequently the number of [twin prime](twin-prime.md) pairs with smaller member at most $x$ is $O(x/\log^2x)$.
