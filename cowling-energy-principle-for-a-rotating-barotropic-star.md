# Cowling energy principle for a rotating barotropic star

↑ **Parent:** [Axisymmetric adiabatic displacement operator](axisymmetric-adiabatic-displacement-operator.md)

With vanishing surface [pressure](pressure.md)/[mass density](density.md) and regular admissible displacements, [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) makes the [axisymmetric adiabatic displacement operator](axisymmetric-adiabatic-displacement-operator.md) symmetric in the [mass density](density.md)-weighted [inner product](inner-product.md). Completing the [pressure](pressure.md) square gives the displayed energy form. Nonnegative form for every admissible displacement excludes exponentially growing axisymmetric adiabatic modes in the [Cowling approximation](cowling-approximation.md). A negative trial [Rayleigh quotient](rayleigh-quotient.md) proves negative spectrum by the [Rayleigh-Ritz variational principle](rayleigh-ritz-variational-principle.md). Nonnegative stratification and epicyclic coefficients give a simple sufficient stability condition.
