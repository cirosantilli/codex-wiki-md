# Product of two compact spaces

↑ **Parent:** [Tychonoff's theorem](tychonoff-s-theorem.md)

The [product topology](product-topology.md) of two [compact spaces](compact-space.md) is [compact](compact-space.md), without a [Hausdorff condition](hausdorff-space.md). Refine an [open cover](open-cover.md) locally by basis rectangles. For fixed $a\in A$, [compactness](compact-space.md) of $B$ selects finitely many rectangles whose second factors cover $B$. Their first factors have an [open](open-set.md) finite [intersection](set-intersection.md) $U_a$ containing $a$, and the finitely many original covering members cover $U_a\times B$. Use [compactness](compact-space.md) of $A$ to choose finitely many $U_a$ covering $A$. The associated finitely many original members form a [finite subcover](finite-subcover.md) of $A\times B$. Repeating the argument proves the finite-factor case of [Tychonoff's theorem](tychonoff-s-theorem.md).
