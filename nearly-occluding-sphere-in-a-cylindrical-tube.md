# Nearly occluding sphere in a cylindrical tube

↑ **Parent:** [Lubrication theory](lubrication-theory.md)

For a sphere of radius $(1-\epsilon)a$ displaced by $\epsilon\lambda a$ from the axis of a tube of radius $a$, the narrow gap is

$$
h(\theta,x)=\epsilon a(1+\lambda\cos\theta)+\frac{x^2}{2a}.
$$

Its thickness is $O(\epsilon a)$ and its axial length is $O(a\sqrt\epsilon)$, so [lubrication pressure dominates shear stress](lubrication-pressure-dominates-shear-stress.md) by $O(\epsilon^{-1/2})$.
