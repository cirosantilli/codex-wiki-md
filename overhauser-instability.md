# Overhauser instability

↑ **Parent:** [Hartree-Fock electron gas](hartree-fock-electron-gas.md)

The uniform paramagnetic three-dimensional electron-gas determinant is unstable within Hartree-Fock theory to a spin-density-wave deformation, even at high density. Comparing only spatially uniform polarized and unpolarized determinants therefore does not find the unrestricted Hartree-Fock [ground state](ground-state.md).[abstract and p. 1437](spin-density-waves-in-an-electron-gas-by-a-w-overhauser.md)
