# Spin-independent axis-angle composition

↑ **Parent:** [Angular momentum operator](angular-momentum-operator-split.md)

The axis and angle of a product of [rotations](rotation-mathematics.md) are properties of the group elements, independent of which [spin](spin.md) [representation](group-representation.md) acts on a state. Higher-spin generators do not obey the two-term Pauli exponential, but [representations](group-representation.md) preserve the resulting [rotation](rotation-mathematics.md) product. The central $2\pi$ [rotation](rotation-mathematics.md) acts as $(-1)^{2S}I$.
