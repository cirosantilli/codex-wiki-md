# Cetrimonium bromide

↑ **Parent:** [Surfactant](surfactant.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cetrimonium_bromide)

A cationic [surfactant](surfactant.md) with a sixteen-carbon [hydrocarbon](hydrocarbon.md) tail and a positively charged trimethylammonium head group, accompanied by a $\mathrm{Br}^-$ [counterion](counterion.md). It is an [amphiphile](amphiphile.md) that can form aqueous [micelles](micelle.md). Its head-group charge has the opposite sign to that of [sodium dodecyl sulfate](sodium-dodecyl-sulfate.md).
