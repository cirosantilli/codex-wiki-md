# Coulomb diamond capacitance extraction

↑ **Parent:** [Coulomb diamond](coulomb-diamond.md)

With source biased and drain grounded, a constant-interaction island has diamond edge slopes $s_-=-C_g/C_s$ and $s_+=C_g/(C_\Sigma-C_s)$. The latter includes the gate [capacitance](capacitance.md). A gate period for charging without an orbital gap is $\Delta V_g=e/C_g$. Combining widths and slopes determines [capacitances](capacitance.md) only under a stated bias and single-island model; shell gaps must not be mistaken for a universal gate period.
