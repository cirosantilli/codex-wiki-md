# Power penalty multiplier limit

↑ **Parent:** [Power penalty](power-penalty.md)

At a stationary point of the [power penalty](power-penalty.md), define the displayed multiplier estimates. Its stationarity equation becomes $\nabla f-Dc^Ty=0$. If $\mu\downarrow0$ and these multipliers are bounded, then each $c_i\to0$. Along any convergent primal subsequence, a further multiplier subsequence converges and yields equality-constrained first-order [KKT conditions](karush-kuhn-tucker-conditions.md) at the limit. This is a statement about finite cluster points: bounded multipliers alone do not prove convergence or boundedness of the primal iterates.
