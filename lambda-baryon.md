# Lambda baryon

↑ **Parent:** [Baryon](baryon.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lambda_baryon)

The $\Lambda^0$ in the [baryon octet](baryon-octet.md) is an [isospin](isospin.md) singlet with [strangeness](strangeness.md) $-1$ and zero [electric charge](electric-charge.md). Its ordinary valence [quark](quark.md) content is $uds$.
