# Linear-drag boundary-current consistency

↑ **Parent:** [Stommel boundary layer](stommel-boundary-layer.md)

If the dimensional [linear drag](linear-drag.md) acceleration is $-r\mathbf u$, then $r$ has units of inverse time and [Stommel boundary layer](stommel-boundary-layer.md) balance $\beta v=-rv_x$ gives $\delta_S=r/\beta$. For returned [volume flux](volumetric-flow-rate.md) $Q$ in depth $H$, the boundary speed is $Q/(H\delta_S)$ and [relative vorticity](relative-vorticity.md) is of order $Q/(H\delta_S^2)$. A gyre return varying along a distance $B$ has a generic nonlinear-to-planetary [vorticity](vorticity-split.md) advection ratio $V_b/(\beta B\delta_S)$. Large relative vorticity alone does not force nonlinear advection: a perfectly parallel $v=v(x)$, $u=0$ has zero material relative-vorticity advection.
