# Weak-ball metrizability requires a norm-separable dual

↑ **Parent:** [Metrization of the weak topology on a bounded set](metrization-of-the-weak-topology-on-a-bounded-set.md)

If the relative [weak topology](weak-topology-split.md) on the unit ball of a [normed vector space](normed-vector-space.md) is first countable at zero, choose a countable collection of finite families of [continuous linear functionals](continuous-linear-functional.md) defining a local base. For any $f\in E'$ and $\epsilon>0$, one family $F$ has common kernel $M$ on which $\|f|_M\|\leq\epsilon$. Extend that restriction to $h\in E'$ with $\|h\|\leq\epsilon$ using the [Hahn-Banach theorem](hahn-banach-theorem.md). Then $f-h$ vanishes on $M$, so it belongs to $\operatorname{span}F$ by finite-dimensional factorization. Consequently the countable union of these families has norm-dense linear span in $E'$. The converse is the [metrization of the weak topology on a bounded set](metrization-of-the-weak-topology-on-a-bounded-set.md) construction from a norm-dense sequence in $E'$. In particular, separability of $E$ alone does not suffice: $\ell^1$ has nonseparable dual $\ell^\infty$.
