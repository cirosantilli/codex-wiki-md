# Classical first-order logic

↑ **Parent:** [First-order logic](first-order-logic.md)

[Classical first-order logic](classical-first-order-logic.md) has the quantifier and [logical equality](logical-equality.md) rules of [natural deduction](natural-deduction.md) together with unrestricted [double-negation elimination](double-negation-elimination.md), or equivalently the [law of excluded middle](law-of-excluded-middle.md). It is interpreted in ordinary two-valued [first-order structures](first-order-structure.md).
