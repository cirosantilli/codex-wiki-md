# Opium

↑ **Parent:** [Pharmacology](pharmacology-split.md)

The dried latex of the opium poppy, containing several [alkaloids](alkaloid.md). Crude preparations must not be treated as interchangeable with a standardized [opioid](opioid.md) medicine.
