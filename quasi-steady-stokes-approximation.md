# Quasi-steady Stokes approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Stokes flow](stokes-flow-split.md)

Neglecting both [convective acceleration](convective-acceleration.md) and local [acceleration](acceleration.md) in the [Navier-Stokes equation](navier-stokes-equation.md) requires a small [Reynolds number](reynolds-number.md) and an imposed evolution time $T$ long compared with the [viscous diffusion time](viscous-diffusion-time.md) $L^2/\nu$. A slowly changing configuration may then be modeled by a sequence of steady [Stokes flows](stokes-flow-split.md). Rapid small-amplitude forcing can have small [Reynolds number](reynolds-number.md) while requiring the [Unsteady Stokes equation](unsteady-stokes-equation.md) instead.
