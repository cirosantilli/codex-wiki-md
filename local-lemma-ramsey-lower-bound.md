# Local lemma Ramsey lower bound

↑ **Parent:** [Lovász local lemma](lovasz-local-lemma.md)

In an independent random two-colouring of $K_N$, a given $k$-vertex [set](set-split.md) is monochromatic with [probability](probability.md) $2^{1-\binom k2}$. [Events](event.md) on [sets](set-split.md) intersecting in at most one vertex use disjoint edges. Thus dependency degree is at most $\binom k2\binom{N-2}{k-2}$. The [Lovász local lemma](lovasz-local-lemma.md) gives a colouring avoiding all monochromatic $K_k$ when $e\,2^{1-\binom k2}(1+\binom k2\binom{N-2}{k-2})\leq1$. For $N=ck2^{k/2}$ the expression is $O(k^{3/2}(ec/\sqrt2)^k)$, by [Stirling's approximation](stirling-formula.md). Taking $c$ tending sufficiently slowly to $\sqrt2/e$ proves $R(k)\geq(\sqrt2/e+o(1))k2^{k/2}$.
