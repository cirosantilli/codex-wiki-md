# Teleportation with an ambiguous Bell-state phase label

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum teleportation](quantum-teleportation.md)

Suppose a perfect $|\phi^+\rangle$ [Bell pair](bell-pair.md) is used for [quantum teleportation](quantum-teleportation.md), but the sender cannot distinguish the $\phi^+$ and $\phi^-$ [Bell-basis measurement](bell-basis-measurement.md) outcomes and guesses their labels uniformly. Each of the four actual outcomes has [probability](probability.md) $1/4$. Either ambiguous outcome gives an incorrect [Pauli Z gate](pauli-z-gate.md) correction with conditional [probability](probability.md) $1/2$, so the total phase-flip [probability](probability.md) is $1/4$. The unconditional output is therefore the displayed [phase-flip channel](phase-flip-channel.md). For $|\psi\rangle=\alpha|0\rangle+\beta|1\rangle$, its [teleportation fidelity](teleportation-fidelity.md) is $\langle\psi|\mathcal E(|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|)|\psi\rangle=1-|\alpha|^2|\beta|^2$. It ranges from $3/4$ on the [Bloch sphere](bloch-sphere.md) equator to $1$ at its poles. This averages the output [density operators](density-matrix-split.md), including the ambiguous rounds.
