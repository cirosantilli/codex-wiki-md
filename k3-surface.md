# K3 surface

↑ **Parent:** [Complex surface](complex-surface.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K3_surface)

A complex [K3 surface](k3-surface.md) is a compact, simply connected [complex surface](complex-surface.md) with trivial [canonical bundle](canonical-bundle.md). For projective [algebraic surfaces](algebraic-surface.md), one can equivalently require a trivial [canonical bundle](canonical-bundle.md) and $H^1(X,\mathcal O_X)=0$. Nonprojective complex [K3 surfaces](k3-surface.md) are also included, which is essential for the full [moduli space of Ricci-flat K3 metrics](moduli-space-of-ricci-flat-k3-metrics.md).
