# Phenetic species concept

↑ **Parent:** [Species concept](species-concept.md)

A [species concept](species-concept.md) based on overall measured similarity among organisms, with explicit or implicit choices of characters, weights and grouping thresholds. A [morphological species concept](morphological-species-concept.md) emphasizes anatomical similarity, while phenetic comparisons can use a wider range of [phenotypic traits](phenotypic-trait.md). [Cryptic species](cryptic-species.md), [phenotypic plasticity](phenotypic-plasticity.md), [sexual dimorphism](sexual-dimorphism.md) and [convergent evolution](convergent-evolution.md) explain why similarity need not coincide with [reproductive isolation](reproductive-isolation.md) or a unique evolutionary lineage.
