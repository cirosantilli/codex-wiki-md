# Subtour elimination constraints

↑ **Parent:** [Travelling salesman problem](travelling-salesman-problem.md)

Binary arc variables with one outgoing and one incoming arc at each vertex describe a [cycle cover of a directed graph](cycle-cover-of-a-directed-graph.md). For every nonempty proper vertex subset $S$, the displayed inequality forbids a directed cycle confined to $S$. Together these inequalities make the cycle cover one [Hamiltonian cycle](hamilton-cycle.md). They can be generated as violated cuts or used in [branch and bound](branch-and-bound.md), rather than all being stored initially.
