# 22-degree halo

↑ **Parent:** [Optical halo](optical-halo.md)

The common [optical halo](optical-halo.md) has a radius of approximately $22^\circ$ around the Sun or Moon. Refraction through a $60^\circ$ ice-crystal [prism](prism-geometry.md) supplies a minimum-deviation edge. Nearby incident directions are concentrated near that edge, forming a [caustic](wave-caustic.md).[Section 3.2.3.1.1, 22° halo](international-cloud-atlas-by-world-meteorological-organization.md)
