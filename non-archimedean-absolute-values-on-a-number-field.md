# Non-Archimedean absolute values on a number field

↑ **Parent:** [Ostrowski's theorem](ostrowski-s-theorem.md)

The nontrivial non-Archimedean absolute values on a [number field](number-field.md) $K$, up to equivalence, are indexed by the nonzero [prime ideals](prime-ideal.md) $\mathfrak p$ of its ring of integers. The value associated with $\mathfrak p$ is a fixed exponential of the discrete valuation $v_{\mathfrak p}$.
