# Spherical-particle wrapping by a fluid membrane

↑ **Parent:** [Helfrich energy](helfrich-energy.md)

In a bound-cap model, an adhered sphere of [radius](radius.md) $r$ covers [area](surface-area.md) $A=2\pi r^2\xi$ with $\xi=1-\cos\alpha\in[0,2]$. Its excess [area](surface-area.md) over its circular footprint is $\pi r^2\xi^2$. The [Helfrich energy](helfrich-energy.md) of that spherical cap is $4\pi\kappa\xi$, adhesion gives $-wA$, and [membrane tension](membrane-tension.md) gives $\sigma\pi r^2\xi^2$. Setting $\bar w=wr^2/\kappa$ and $\bar\sigma=\sigma r^2/\kappa$ yields the displayed expression. Free-surface deformation, neck structure, [Gaussian curvature](gaussian-curvature.md) and [topology](topology-split.md) changes are omitted. Full wrapping alone does not prove scission.
