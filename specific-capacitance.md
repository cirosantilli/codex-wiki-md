# Specific capacitance

↑ **Parent:** [Capacitance](capacitance.md)

[Capacitance](capacitance.md) normalized by a stated mass or volume. Mass-specific differential capacitance is $C_{\rm sp}=m^{-1}dQ/dV$; electrode and full-device mass conventions must be distinguished.
