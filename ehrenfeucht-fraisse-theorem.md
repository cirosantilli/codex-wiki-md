<h1 id="ehrenfeucht-fraisse-theorem">Ehrenfeucht-Fraïssé theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ehrenfeucht-Fraïssé game](ehrenfeucht-fraisse-game.md)

For a finite relational [first-order language](first-order-language.md), with finitely many constants allowed, the Duplicator wins the $n$-round game exactly when the structures are [rank-n equivalent](rank-n-equivalence-of-structures.md). Game-winning implies rank equivalence in arbitrary languages; the converse needs a finiteness condition and can fail even for an infinite unary vocabulary. Over a finite relational vocabulary, winning every announced finite horizon is equivalent to [elementary equivalence](elementary-equivalence.md).
