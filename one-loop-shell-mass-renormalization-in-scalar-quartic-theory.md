# One-loop shell mass renormalization in scalar quartic theory

↑ **Parent:** [Momentum-shell renormalization group](momentum-shell-renormalization-group.md)

For interaction $V=u\int\phi^4/4!$, split $\phi=\varphi+\eta$ into slow and fast [Fourier modes](fourier-mode.md), with a centered fast [Gaussian measure](gaussian-measure.md). The first [cumulant expansion](cumulant-expansion.md) term is $\langle V\rangle=(u/24)\int[\varphi^4+6G_>(0)\varphi^2+3G_>(0)^2]$ by [Wick theorem](wick-s-theorem.md). The constant shifts the [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md), the quadratic term gives $\delta r=uG_>(0)/2$, and there is no [gradient](gradient.md) correction at this order. Rescaling gives $r'=b^2[r+uG_>(0)/2]+O(u^2)$ and $u'=b^{4-D}u+O(u^2)$. The mass shift mixes thermal and quartic perturbations; the interacting critical tuning need not have zero bare mass.
