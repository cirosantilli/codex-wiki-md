# Degree-ordered independent-set entropy bound

↑ **Parent:** [Independent set (graph theory)](independent-set-graph-theory.md)

Order vertices by nonincreasing positive degrees $d_i$, and let $b_i$ count preceding neighbors. The [Madiman-Tetali inequality](madiman-tetali-entropy-inequality.md) applied to preceding-neighbor sets and $b_i$ copies of singleton $i$ bounds the entropy of a uniformly chosen [independent set](independent-set-graph-theory.md). In each local term, all-zero neighbor assignment permits two choices at $i$, while every other assignment permits at most one. The maximum-entropy bound gives $\log_2(2^{b_i+1}-1)$ for that term. Complete bipartite graphs $K_{d,d}$ attain equality when one part is ordered before the other.
