# Left ideal

↑ **Parent:** [Associative algebra](associative-algebra-split.md)

A left ideal $L$ of an [associative algebra](associative-algebra-split.md) $A$ is a [vector subspace](vector-subspace.md) satisfying $AL\subseteq L$. Equivalently, it is a submodule of the left regular $A$-module.
