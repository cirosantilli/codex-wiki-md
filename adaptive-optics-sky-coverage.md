# Adaptive-optics sky coverage

↑ **Parent:** [Adaptive optics](adaptive-optics.md)

Adaptive-optics sky coverage is the fraction of targets for which a sufficiently bright nearby [guide star](guide-star-adaptive-optics.md) allows useful correction. [Anisoplanatism](anisoplanatism.md) limits the offset, and photon statistics limit the sensing speed. A [laser guide star](laser-guide-star.md) improves coverage but does not remove all low-order and finite-distance limitations.
