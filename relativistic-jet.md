# Relativistic jet

↑ **Parent:** [Astrophysical jet](astrophysical-jet.md)

A [jet](jet-particle-physics.md) with bulk velocity sufficiently close to the speed of light that relativistic aberration, frequency shifts and arrival-time effects strongly affect its appearance. The moving emitting pattern need not track the speed of every [plasma](plasma-physics.md) component.
