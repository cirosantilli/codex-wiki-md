# Beta equivalence

↑ **Parent:** [Beta reduction](beta-reduction.md)

Beta equivalence is the smallest equivalence relation containing [beta reduction](beta-reduction.md); equivalently, $M\equiv_\beta N$ when a finite sequence of beta reductions and reversed beta reductions connects $M$ and $N$.
