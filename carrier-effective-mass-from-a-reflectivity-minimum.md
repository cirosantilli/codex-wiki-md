# Carrier effective mass from a reflectivity minimum

↑ **Parent:** [Drude-Lorentz reflectivity minimum](drude-lorentz-reflectivity-minimum.md)

For a weakly absorbing doped semiconductor with one dominant carrier species and approximately constant background dielectric function $\epsilon_b>1$, the [Drude model](drude-model.md) gives a normal-incidence reflectivity minimum near $\epsilon_r=1$. Thus $\omega_{pc}^2=(\epsilon_b-1)\omega_{\min}^2$, with $\omega_{pc}^2=ne^2/(\epsilon_0m^*)$ and $\omega_{\min}=2\pi c/\lambda_{\min}$. Eliminating the plasma frequency proves the displayed mass estimate. The real-dielectric zero instead has $\omega_0\simeq\omega_{pc}/\sqrt{\epsilon_b}$ and is the nearby screened plasma edge, so its wavelength requires a different denominator. With appreciable loss, fit the full complex [Fresnel equations](fresnel-equations.md) rather than identifying the minimum with an exactly real dielectric value.
