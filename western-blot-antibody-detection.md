# Western blot antibody detection

↑ **Parent:** [Western blot](western-blot.md)

Blocking occupies unused binding sites on the [Western blot membrane](western-blot-membrane.md). The [primary antibody](primary-antibody.md) is applied, unbound material is washed away, and a labelled [secondary antibody](secondary-antibody.md) detects retained [primary antibody](primary-antibody.md). A [fluorescence](fluorescence-split.md) label emits detectable [light](light.md) after excitation; an [enzyme](enzyme-split.md)-linked label can instead generate a colored product or [light](light.md). Washing reduces background but does not prove that remaining binding is selective.

In the original experiment, blocking used excess [protein](protein-split.md). An anti-[ribosome](ribosome.md)-[protein](protein-split.md) serum followed by labelled [secondary antibody](secondary-antibody.md) produced colored or [fluorescence](fluorescence-split.md) bands; preimmune serum supplied comparison strips. The color readout was an [enzyme](enzyme-split.md) reaction product, rather than a measurement of target [enzyme](enzyme-split.md) activity. [Towbin, Staehelin and Gordon, pp. 4351–4353: Immunological Detection of Proteins on Nitrocellulose and Figure 4, including preimmune-serum controls](electrophoretic-transfer-of-proteins-from-polyacrylamide-gels-to-nitrocellulose-sheets-procedure-and-some-applications-by-harry-towbin-and-others.md)
