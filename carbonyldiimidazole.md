# Carbonyldiimidazole

↑ **Parent:** [Organic chemistry](organic-chemistry.md)

A [carbonyl](carbonyl-group.md) compound with two imidazolyl leaving groups. It activates carboxylic acids as acyl imidazoles, releasing carbon dioxide and imidazole.
