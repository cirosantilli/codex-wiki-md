# Mutual inclination of nearly edge-on orbits

↑ **Parent:** [Mutual inclination](mutual-inclination.md)

For two nearly [edge-on orbits](edge-on-orbit.md) with small [orbital inclination](orbital-inclination.md) difference $\Delta I$ and small [longitude of ascending node](longitude-of-ascending-node.md) difference $\Delta\Omega$, expanding the [dot product](dot-product.md) of their normals gives $I_m^2=(\Delta I)^2+(\Delta\Omega)^2+O(\delta^4)$, with angles in radians and all offsets from edge-on of order $\delta$. Thus equality of observed sky-plane inclinations alone does not imply aligned [orbital planes](orbital-plane.md).
