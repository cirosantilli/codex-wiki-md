# Bond-dissociation enthalpy

↑ **Parent:** [Bond-dissociation energy](bond-dissociation-energy.md)

A [bond-dissociation enthalpy](bond-dissociation-enthalpy.md) is the molar [enthalpy](enthalpy-split.md) change for the specified [homolysis](homolysis.md) at [temperature](temperature-split.md) $T$, with reactant and products in specified [standard states](standard-state.md). Its usual units are $\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$. The superscript $\circ$ specifies [standard states](standard-state.md); it does not fix $T$ to room [temperature](temperature-split.md).[definition and symbols: standard molar enthalpy for homolysis, Δdiss H°](iupac-compendium-of-chemical-terminology-fifth-edition-gold-book-by-international-union-of-pure-and-applied-chemistry/bond-dissociation-enthalpy.md)

Write $\delta h_i(T)=H_{m,i}^{\circ}(T)-H_{m,i}^{\circ}(0)$ for each species' molar thermal [enthalpy](enthalpy-split.md) increment. With $D_0$ an [energy](energy-split.md) per parent [molecule](molecule-split.md),

$$
\Delta_{\rm diss}H^{\circ}(T)=N_{\rm A}D_0+\delta h_A(T)+\delta h_B(T)-\delta h_{AB}(T).
$$

Here $N_{\rm A}$ is the [Avogadro constant](avogadro-constant.md). The thermal increments include translational [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md), [molecular rotation](molecular-rotation.md), thermally excited [molecular vibration](molecular-vibration.md) and electronic states when populated, and the $pV$ contribution to [enthalpy](enthalpy-split.md). They are additional to the [zero-point energies](zero-point-energy.md) already included in $D_0$. If values of [standard enthalpy of formation](standard-enthalpy-of-formation.md) at the same $T$ are available, [Hess's law](hess-s-law.md) gives the same quantity as $\Delta_fH^{\circ}(A)+\Delta_fH^{\circ}(B)-\Delta_fH^{\circ}(AB)$.

Units alone do not identify the convention. Multiplying either $D_e$ or $D_0$ by the [Avogadro constant](avogadro-constant.md) gives molar [energy](energy-split.md), but does not add thermal [enthalpy](enthalpy-split.md). One [electronvolt](electronvolt.md) per dissociation corresponds to $96.485332\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$. A [spectroscopic wavenumber](spectroscopic-wavenumber.md) $\widetilde\nu$ represents [energy](energy-split.md) $hc\widetilde\nu$, using the [Planck constant](planck-constant.md) $h$ and [speed of light](speed-of-light.md) $c$; $1\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$ corresponds to $0.011962657\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$.[SI defining constants h, c, e and Avogadro constant; conversions calculated from them](fundamental-physical-constants-complete-listing-by-national-institute-of-standards-and-technology-and-others.md)
