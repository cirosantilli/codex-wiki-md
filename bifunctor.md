# Bifunctor

↑ **Parent:** [Functor](functor.md)

A [bifunctor](bifunctor.md) is a [functor](functor.md) on the product of two [categories](category-split.md). It assigns $F(c,d)$ to each pair of objects and $F(u,v)$ to a pair of [morphisms](morphism.md), preserving [identity morphisms](identity-morphism.md) and [composition in a category](composition-in-a-category.md). Equivalently, it is functorial separately in each argument, with the two actions commuting: $F(1,v)F(u,1)=F(u,1)F(1,v)$ whenever the expressions have the corresponding sources and targets. The product [category](category-split.md) has pairs as objects, pairs of [morphisms](morphism.md) as arrows, and componentwise [composition in a category](composition-in-a-category.md). A [monoidal tensor product](monoidal-tensor-product.md) is a [bifunctor](bifunctor.md) from a [category](category-split.md) times itself.
