# Power-set failure in hereditarily small sets

↑ **Parent:** [Hereditarily small set](hereditarily-small-set.md)

The [hereditarily small set](hereditarily-small-set.md) structure $H_{\omega_2}$ fails the [Axiom of power set](axiom-of-power-set.md) at $\omega_1$. Every subset of $\omega_1$ belongs to $H_{\omega_2}$, but their full [power set](power-set.md) has [cardinality](cardinality.md) at least $\aleph_2$ by the [Cantor theorem](cantor-s-theorem.md), so cannot itself be hereditarily smaller than $\aleph_2$.
