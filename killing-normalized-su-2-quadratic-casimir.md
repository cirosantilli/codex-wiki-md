<h1 id="killing-normalized-su-2-quadratic-casimir">Killing-normalized SU(2) quadratic Casimir</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Killing form of the SU(2) Lie algebra](killing-form-of-the-su-2-lie-algebra.md)

With Hermitian angular-momentum generators $[J_a,J_b]=i\epsilon_{abc}J_c$, the [Killing form](killing-form.md) is $2\delta_{ab}$ and its inverse contraction is $C_\kappa=\tfrac12\sum_aJ_a^2$. Equivalently the anti-Hermitian basis $T_a=-iJ_a$ has [Killing form](killing-form.md) $-2\delta_{ab}$ and gives the same operator. In the complex highest-weight basis, $C_\kappa=H^2/8+(E^+E^-+E^-E^+)/4$. It acts on spin $j$ by $j(j+1)/2$, so it differs in normalization from the usual angular-momentum [quadratic Casimir operator](quadratic-casimir-operator.md).
