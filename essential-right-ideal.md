# Essential right ideal

↑ **Parent:** [Essential submodule](essential-submodule.md)

An essential right ideal is an [essential submodule](essential-submodule.md) of the right regular [module](module-mathematics.md) of a [ring](ring.md). Every [right ideal](right-ideal.md) containing an essential right ideal is essential.
