# Product-form stationary distribution of an open migration process

↑ **Parent:** [Open migration process](open-migration-process.md)

For an [open migration process](open-migration-process.md), colony $j$ with population $k$ has total departure rate $\phi_j(k)$. Its stationary weight is $b_j(k)=\alpha_j^k/\prod_{h=1}^k\phi_j(h)$. The colony weights factor independently and normalize when $\sum_{k\geq0}b_j(k)<\infty$ for every colony. [Global balance for a continuous-time Markov chain](global-balance-for-a-continuous-time-markov-chain.md) proves the formula even when routing is not reversible.
