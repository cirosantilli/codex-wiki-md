# Dry adiabatic lapse rate

↑ **Parent:** [Adiabatic temperature gradient](adiabatic-temperature-gradient.md)

For a homogeneous dry [ideal gas](ideal-gas-split.md) in a hydrostatic adiabat, $dT/dz=-g/C_p$, where $z$ increases upwards and $C_p$ is the [specific heat capacity at constant pressure](specific-heat-capacity-at-constant-pressure.md) per unit mass. It follows from $dT/dP=1/(\rho C_p)$ and [hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md). The altitude version of the [Schwarzschild criterion](schwarzschild-criterion.md) is $dT/dz<-g/C_p$ for instability; equality is marginal. Moisture, composition gradients and nonideal equations of state modify the criterion.
