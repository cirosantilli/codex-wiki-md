# Markov decision process

↑ **Parent:** [Controlled Markov process](controlled-markov-process.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Markov_decision_process)

A Markov decision process specifies states, admissible actions, action-dependent [transition probabilities](transition-probability.md) and rewards. A policy chooses actions from observed information; a time-homogeneous problem uses transition and reward rules independent of the date. A [Bellman equation](bellman-equation.md) optimizes current reward plus the conditional value of subsequent decisions. For nonnegative rewards, finite-horizon values increase as the horizon grows, while identification of their limit with the infinite-horizon value requires an approximation argument for admissible policies.
