# Diffusion limit of the damped heat-transmission equation

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier-mode Green function of the telegraph equation](fourier-mode-green-function-of-the-telegraph-equation.md)

At small wavenumber compared with $\alpha c$, the slow decay rate of the damped [wave](wave-split.md) operator is $k^2/(2\alpha)$ and the other root decays on time $1/(2\alpha c^2)$. Long-time, long-wavelength motion therefore approaches [heat](heat-split.md) diffusion with the displayed [diffusivity](diffusion-coefficient.md). The original hyperbolic equation nevertheless has finite signal speed $c$, unlike the parabolic diffusion approximation.
