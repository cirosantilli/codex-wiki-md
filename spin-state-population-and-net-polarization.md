# Spin-state population and net polarization

↑ **Parent:** [Spin-one-half paramagnetic magnetization](spin-one-half-paramagnetic-magnetization.md)

For moment projections $\pm\mu$, $p_+=(1+\tanh x)/2$ but $M/M_s=\tanh x$, with $x=\mu B/(k_BT)$. At zero field half the moments occupy each projection while the net [magnetization](magnetization.md) is zero. An effective Curie moment is also distinct from the saturated projection used in this two-state model.
