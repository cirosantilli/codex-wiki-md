# Free homotopy

↑ **Parent:** [Homotopy](homotopy.md)

A [free homotopy](free-homotopy.md) between maps is an ordinary [homotopy](homotopy.md) with no specified basepoint held fixed. Two loops at the same basepoint are freely homotopic precisely when their elements of the [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md) are conjugate: a [homotopy](homotopy.md) traces a connecting basepoint path, and the boundary of its parameter square gives conjugation. Conversely that connecting path can be slid around the loop to realize the conjugation.
