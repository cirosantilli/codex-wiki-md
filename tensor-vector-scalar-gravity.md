# Tensor-vector-scalar gravity

↑ **Parent:** [Modified gravity](modified-gravity.md)

A relativistic gravitational theory constructed by [Jacob Bekenstein](jacob-bekenstein.md) using tensor, vector and [scalar fields](scalar-field.md), together with a specified physical [metric](metric.md) for matter. It illustrates how the low-acceleration phenomenology of [MOND](modified-newtonian-dynamics.md) can be embedded in a theory with calculable light deflection. Matching rotation curves is not sufficient to establish its lensing or cosmological predictions.[Relativistic action and physical metric](relativistic-gravitation-theory-for-the-mond-paradigm-by-jacob-bekenstein.md)
