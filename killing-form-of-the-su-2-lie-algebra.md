<h1 id="killing-form-of-the-su-2-lie-algebra">Killing form of the SU(2) Lie algebra</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [SU(2) Lie algebra](su-2-lie-algebra.md)

In the anti-Hermitian basis $T^a=-i\sigma^a/2$, the [SU(2) Lie algebra](su-2-lie-algebra.md) has $[T^a,T^b]=\varepsilon^{abc}T^c$ and [Killing form](killing-form.md) $\kappa(T^a,T^b)=-2\delta^{ab}$. This is negative definite; the positive internal form for compact [Yang-Mills theory](yang-mills-theory.md) is its negative.
