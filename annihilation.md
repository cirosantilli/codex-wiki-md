# Annihilation

↑ **Parent:** [Antiparticle](antiparticle.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annihilation)

[Particle-antiparticle annihilation](annihilation.md) converts a particle and its [antiparticle](antiparticle.md) into other particles, conserving [energy](energy-split.md) and applicable quantum numbers. Baryon-antibaryon annihilation commonly produces mesons whose subsequent decays can generate radiation.
