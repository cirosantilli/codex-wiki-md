# Number of electrons in a Debye sphere from a plasmon energy ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Debye sphere](debye-sphere.md)

For a three-dimensional ideal degenerate electron gas, let $r=\hbar\omega_{\rm pe}/E_F$. If one heuristically defines a [Debye length](debye-length.md) by replacing $k_{\rm B}T$ with the [Fermi energy](fermi-energy.md), then $k_F\lambda_D=\sqrt2/r$ and the associated electron count is the displayed expression. A count below one signals failure of the classical many-particle screening interpretation. It does not prohibit plasmons. The zero-temperature compressibility instead gives the [Thomas-Fermi screening](thomas-fermi-screening.md) length squared $2\epsilon_0E_F/(3n_ee^2)$.
