# Symmetric simple exclusion process

↑ **Parent:** [Exclusion process](exclusion-process.md)

On $\mathbb Z$, put independent rate-$c$ [Poisson processes](poisson-process.md) on unoriented nearest-neighbour [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md), and exchange their endpoint occupations at each mark. Its generator on cylinder functions is $Lf(\eta)=c\sum_x[f(\eta^{x,x+1})-f(\eta)]$. Equal occupations do not change the state, and a particle next to a vacancy jumps across at rate $c$. The [stirring representation of symmetric exclusion](stirring-representation-of-symmetric-exclusion.md) constructs it for arbitrary initial configurations. The simple occupation-product [product self-duality of symmetric exclusion](product-self-duality-of-symmetric-exclusion.md) requires symmetry and is not the ordinary duality of an asymmetric process.
