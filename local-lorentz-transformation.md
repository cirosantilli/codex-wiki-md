# Local Lorentz transformation

↑ **Parent:** [Lorentz transformation](lorentz-transformation.md)

An [orthonormal coframe in spacetime](orthonormal-coframe-in-spacetime.md) can be rotated by a position-dependent Lorentz matrix without changing the spacetime [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md). Its [spin connection](spin-connection.md) transforms as $\omega'=\Lambda\omega\Lambda^{-1}-d\Lambda\,\Lambda^{-1}$. A [spinor field](spinor-field.md) transforms through a chosen spin lift $\psi'=S(\Lambda)\psi$, not through the vector matrix $\Lambda$. With $S^{-1}\gamma^A S=\Lambda^A{}_B\gamma^B$, an infinitesimal parameter gives $S=1+\tfrac14\alpha_{AB}\gamma^{AB}+O(\alpha^2)$, where $\gamma^{AB}=\tfrac12[\gamma^A,\gamma^B]$. Consistent global transition lifts require a [spin structure](spin-structure.md); a $2\pi$ spatial rotation lifts to minus the identity on spinors.
