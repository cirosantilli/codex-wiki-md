# Fourier-mode Green function of the telegraph equation

↑ **Parent:** [Damped wave equation](damped-wave-equation.md)

For operator $c^{-2}\partial_t^2+2\alpha\partial_t-\partial_x^2$, define $\gamma=\alpha c^2$ and $d_k^2=\gamma^2-c^2k^2$. Retarded support, continuity at zero time and the [derivative](derivative.md) jump $G_k'(0^+)=c^2$ fix the displayed normalization. Imaginary $d_k$ produces a damped sine; zero $d_k$ gives $c^2\tau e^{-\gamma\tau}$. The spatially uniform mode tends to $1/(2\alpha)$ rather than decaying to zero.
