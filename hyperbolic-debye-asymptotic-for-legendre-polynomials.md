# Hyperbolic Debye asymptotic for Legendre polynomials

↑ **Parent:** [Debye asymptotic for Legendre polynomials](debye-asymptotic-for-legendre-polynomials.md)

For fixed $\xi>0$,

$$
P_n(\cosh\xi)
\sim
\frac{e^{(n+1/2)\xi}}{\sqrt{2\pi n\sinh\xi}}.
$$
