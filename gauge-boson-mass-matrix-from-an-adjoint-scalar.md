# Gauge-boson mass matrix from an adjoint scalar

↑ **Parent:** [Higgs mechanism](higgs-mechanism.md)

For generators normalized by $\operatorname{Tr}(t^at^b)=\delta^{ab}/2$, a constant adjoint [vacuum expectation value](vacuum-expectation-value.md) $\Phi_0$ gives this [matrix](matrix.md). A generator commuting with $\Phi_0$ corresponds to a massless [gauge boson](gauge-boson.md).
