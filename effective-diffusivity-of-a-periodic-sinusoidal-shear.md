# Effective diffusivity of a periodic sinusoidal shear

↑ **Parent:** [Taylor dispersion](taylor-dispersion.md)

For a [passive scalar](passive-scalar.md) transported by $U(t)\sin(my)$ with transverse period $2\pi/m$, positive [diffusivity](diffusion-coefficient.md) $\kappa$ and periodic $U$, let $g$ be the unique periodic solution of $g'+\kappa m^2g=U$. A [multiple-scale expansion](method-of-multiple-scales.md) has first corrector $-g(t)\sin(my)\partial_X\overline\chi$. Averaging the next-order equation over the space-time cell gives the displayed longitudinal [diffusion coefficient](diffusion-coefficient.md). Averaging $gg'+\kappa m^2g^2=Ug$ proves the second form. This is a long-time, long-wavelength effective equation, after transverse and periodic transients have decayed, rather than an exact finite-time closure for arbitrary concentrations.
