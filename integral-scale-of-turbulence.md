# Integral scale of turbulence

↑ **Parent:** [Homogeneous turbulence](homogeneous-turbulence.md)

The [integral scale of turbulence](integral-scale-of-turbulence.md) characterizes energy-containing velocity correlations; in [isotropic turbulence](isotropic-turbulence.md) one definition is $\ell=\int_0^\infty f(r)\,dr$. Its turnover time is of order $\ell/u$. Large-scale intensity fluctuations can change the energy supplied to the [equilibrium range](equilibrium-range.md).
