<h1 id="b/7/vi/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vi](../vi.md)

Use the uniform lower step bound from part (iv):

$$
0\leq\|g(x_k)\|_2^2\leq\frac{\alpha_k\|g(x_k)\|_2^2}{\alpha_{\min}}\longrightarrow0.
$$

Thus $\boxed{g(x_k)\to0}$. This is convergence to stationarity in the usual global convergence theorem for the [steepest descent method](../../../../../gradient-descent-split.md). It does not itself prove that the iterates converge, or that any stationary limit is a global minimizer of a nonconvex objective.
