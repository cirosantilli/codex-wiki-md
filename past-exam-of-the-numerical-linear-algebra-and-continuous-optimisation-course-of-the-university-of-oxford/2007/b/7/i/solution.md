<h1 id="b/7/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $g=\nabla f(x)\ne0$, the unit [steepest descent direction](../../../../../steepest-descent-direction.md) is $-g/\|g\|_2$ by the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](../../../../../cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md). The usual unnormalized direction is $\boxed{p=-g}$; choosing its scale separately as a line-search step gives the same ray. It satisfies $\boxed{g^Tp=-\|g\|_2^2<0}$, so it is a [descent direction](../../../../../descent-direction.md). The first-order [Taylor expansion](../../../../../taylor-expansion.md) then guarantees decrease for sufficiently small positive steps. The subsequent bounds use this unnormalized negative-gradient convention.
