<h1 id="b/8/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

With $L(x,y)=f(x)-y^Tc(x)$, the [Karush-Kuhn-Tucker conditions](../../../../../karush-kuhn-tucker-conditions.md) are

$$
\boxed{\nabla f(x_*)-Dc(x_*)^Ty_*=0,\quad c(x_*)\geq0,\quad y_*\geq0,\quad y_{*,i}c_i(x_*)=0\quad\text{for all }i.}
$$

Under a suitable [constraint qualification](../../../../../constraint-qualification.md), such as the [linear independence constraint qualification](../../../../../linear-independence-constraint-qualification.md) on active constraint gradients, they are necessary for a local minimum. Smoothness alone is insufficient. The sign convention here corresponds to constraints written as $c_i\geq0$ and a minus sign in the [optimization Lagrangian](../../../../../optimization-lagrangian.md).
