A full real [singular value decomposition](../../../../../singular-value-decomposition.md) is

$$
\boxed{A=U\Sigma V^T,\quad U\in\mathbb R^{m\times m},\quad V\in\mathbb R^{n\times n},\quad U^TU=I_m,\quad V^TV=I_n,}
$$

where $\Sigma\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$ has only diagonal entries, with $\sigma_1\geq\cdots\geq\sigma_{\min(m,n)}\geq0$. These are the [singular values](../../../../../singular-value.md). The number of positive [singular values](../../../../../singular-value.md) is the [matrix rank](../../../../../matrix-rank.md). The columns of $V$ are right singular vectors and those of $U$ are left singular vectors, giving orthonormal input and output coordinate systems.
