<h1 id="b/6/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The equation $F_\mu(x,y)=0$, together with $c(x)>0$ and $y>0$, is the perturbed [Karush-Kuhn-Tucker conditions](../../../../../karush-kuhn-tucker-conditions.md): stationarity is unchanged, while exact [complementary slackness](../../../../../complementary-slackness.md) becomes $c_i(x)y_i=\mu$ for each $i$. A finite limit of such points as $\mu\downarrow0$ satisfies the original conditions by continuity.

For a strictly feasible $x$, the second block uniquely specifies $y_i=\mu/c_i(x)$. Substitution into the first block gives precisely $\nabla B_\mu(x)=0$. Thus

$$
\boxed{F_\mu(x,y)=0\iff x\text{ is a critical point of }B_\mu\text{ and }y_i=\mu/c_i(x),}
$$

within the stated strictly positive domain. These are stationary barrier points; they need not all be local minimizers in a nonconvex problem.
