<h1 id="b/6/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

On the strictly feasible region $c_i(x)>0$, the [logarithmic barrier](../../../../../logarithmic-barrier.md) objective is

$$
\boxed{B_\mu(x)=f(x)-\mu\sum_{i=1}^m\log c_i(x).}
$$

With $y_i(x)=\mu/c_i(x)$, its [gradient](../../../../../gradient.md) is

$$
\boxed{\nabla B_\mu(x)=g(x)-A(x)^Ty(x).}
$$

The positive coefficients $y_i$ are the barrier's multiplier estimates.
