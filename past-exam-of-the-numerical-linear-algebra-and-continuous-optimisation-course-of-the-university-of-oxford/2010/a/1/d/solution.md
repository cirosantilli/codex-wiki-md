↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [nullspace](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) is the vector subspace $\boxed{\ker A=\{x\in\mathbb R^n:Ax=0\}}$. A full real [singular value decomposition](../../../../../singular-value-decomposition.md) is

$$
\boxed{A=U\Sigma V^T,\quad U\in\mathbb R^{m\times m},\quad \Sigma\in\mathbb R^{m\times n},\quad V\in\mathbb R^{n\times n},}
$$

where $U,V$ are [orthogonal matrices](../../../../../orthogonal-matrix.md) and $\Sigma_{ij}=0$ for $i\ne j$, with $\Sigma_{jj}=\sigma_j\geq0$ for $1\leq j\leq n$. We may order $\sigma_1\geq\cdots\geq\sigma_n\geq0$. The columns of $V$ form an [orthonormal basis](../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) of the input space $\mathbb R^n$, and the columns of $U$ form one of the output space $\mathbb R^m$. The positive [singular values](../../../../../singular-value.md) number exactly the [matrix rank](../../../../../matrix-rank.md).
