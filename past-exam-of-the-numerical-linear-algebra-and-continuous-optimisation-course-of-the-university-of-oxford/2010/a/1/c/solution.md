<h1 id="a/1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The perpendicular decomposition in part (a) gives, by the [Pythagorean theorem](../../../../../pythagorean-theorem.md),

$$
\|x\|_2^2=\|Px\|_2^2+\|(I-P)x\|_2^2\geq\|Px\|_2^2.
$$

Thus an [orthogonal projection](../../../../../orthogonal-projection.md) cannot lengthen a vector and $\boxed{\|P\|_2\leq1}$. Algebraically the cross term vanishes because $P^T(I-P)=P-P^2=0$, giving the same identity directly from the hypotheses. If $P\ne0$, take a nonzero $x$ in its range; then $Px=x$ and $\|P\|_2=1$. For $P=0$ the norm is zero.
