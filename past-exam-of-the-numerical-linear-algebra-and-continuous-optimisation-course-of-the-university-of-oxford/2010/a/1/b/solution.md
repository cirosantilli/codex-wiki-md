The vector [Euclidean norm](../../../../../euclidean-norm.md) is $\boxed{\|x\|_2=(x^Tx)^{1/2}=(\sum_i x_i^2)^{1/2}}$, the ordinary length from the origin. The [induced matrix norm](../../../../../induced-matrix-norm.md) is

$$
\boxed{\|A\|_2=\sup_{x\ne0}\frac{\|Ax\|_2}{\|x\|_2}=\max_{\|x\|_2=1}\|Ax\|_2.}
$$

Geometrically this is the largest stretch of a unit vector. A linear map sends the unit sphere to an ellipsoid, possibly degenerate; its largest semiaxis has length $\|A\|_2$. Equivalently the [matrix 2-norm](../../../../../matrix-2-norm.md) is the largest [singular value](../../../../../singular-value.md).
