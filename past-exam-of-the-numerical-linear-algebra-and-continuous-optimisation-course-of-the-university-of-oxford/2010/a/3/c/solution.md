<h1 id="a/3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

An [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) of $A$ is a scalar $\lambda$ for which $Az=\lambda z$ has a nonzero [eigenvector](../../../../../eigenvector.md). Allowing initially $z\in\mathbb C^m$, symmetry of the real matrix gives $A^*=A$, so $z^*Az$ is real. Since $z^*z>0$,

$$
\boxed{\lambda=\frac{z^*Az}{z^*z}\in\mathbb R.}
$$

This proves reality without presuming it when choosing the [eigenvector](../../../../../eigenvector.md). Once $\lambda$ is real, the real or imaginary part of $z$ supplies a nonzero real [eigenvector](../../../../../eigenvector.md).
