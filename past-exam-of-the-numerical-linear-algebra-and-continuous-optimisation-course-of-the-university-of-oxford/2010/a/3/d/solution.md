<h1 id="a/3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For a unit [eigenvector](../../../../../eigenvector.md) of $A$, $(A-I)x=(\lambda-1)x$. The [matrix 2-norm](../../../../../matrix-2-norm.md) bound from part (a) gives $|\lambda-1|\leq2/3$. Hence every [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) lies in

$$
\boxed{[\alpha,\beta]=[1/3,5/3].}
$$

In particular the [condition number](../../../../../condition-number.md) in the Euclidean [operator norm](../../../../../operator-norm.md) satisfies $\kappa_2(A)=\lambda_{\max}/\lambda_{\min}\leq5$.
