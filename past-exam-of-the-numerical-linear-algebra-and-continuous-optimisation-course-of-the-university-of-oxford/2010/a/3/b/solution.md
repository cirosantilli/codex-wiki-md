<h1 id="a/3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A real symmetric matrix is [positive-definite](../../../../../positive-definite-bilinear-form.md) when $x^TAx>0$ for every nonzero real vector $x$. The preceding [matrix 2-norm](../../../../../matrix-2-norm.md) bound gives

$$
x^TAx=\|x\|_2^2+x^TTx\geq\|x\|_2^2-\|x\|_2\|Tx\|_2
\geq\boxed{\frac13\|x\|_2^2>0}.
$$

Thus $A$ is a [symmetric positive-definite matrix](../../../../../symmetric-positive-definite-matrix.md), independently of the detailed positions of the nonzero entries of $t$.
