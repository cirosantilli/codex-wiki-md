The [energy norm](../../../../../energy-norm.md) associated with this [symmetric positive-definite matrix](../../../../../symmetric-positive-definite-matrix.md) is

$$
\boxed{\|e\|_A=(e^TAe)^{1/2}.}
$$

It is the norm induced by the inner product $\langle u,v\rangle_A=u^TAv$. The [conjugate gradient method](../../../../../conjugate-gradient-method.md) minimizes this error norm over its successive affine [Krylov subspaces](../../../../../krylov-subspace.md).
