# Ideal vector-meson mixing

↑ **Parent:** [Vector meson](vector-meson.md)

Mixing of the [flavour](quark-flavor.md) [singlet](singlet-representation.md) and isoscalar [octet](octet-representation.md) vector states so that the [mass eigenstates](mass-eigenstate.md) separate into nonstrange and strange [flavour](quark-flavor.md). In the basis $|1\rangle=(u\bar u+d\bar d+s\bar s)/\sqrt3$, $|8_0\rangle=(2s\bar s-u\bar u-d\bar d)/\sqrt6$, the determinant-one rotation to $(\omega,\phi)$ has angle $-\arctan(1/\sqrt2)$. Basis phases change the quoted angle but not the [flavour](quark-flavor.md) content.
