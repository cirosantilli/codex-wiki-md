# Hurwitz proof of the planar isoperimetric inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Planar isoperimetric inequality](planar-isoperimetric-inequality.md)

Parametrize a positively oriented closed [plane curve](plane-curve.md) at constant [arc length](arc-length.md) speed on $[0,2\pi]$, and let $c_n$ be its complex-position [Fourier coefficients](fourier-coefficient.md). [Green's theorem](green-theorem.md) and [Parseval's identity](parseval-identity.md) give $A=\pi\sum_n n|c_n|^2$ and $L^2/(4\pi^2)=\sum_n n^2|c_n|^2$. Since $n\leq n^2$ for integers, the [planar isoperimetric inequality](planar-isoperimetric-inequality.md) follows. Equality leaves only the constant and positive first harmonic, a translated circle.
