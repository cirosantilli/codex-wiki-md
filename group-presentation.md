# Group presentation

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric group theory](geometric-group-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_presentation)

A group presentation $\langle S\mid R\rangle$ describes the quotient of the [free group](free-group.md) $F(S)$ by the normal closure of the relators $R$. It is finite when both $S$ and $R$ are finite.
