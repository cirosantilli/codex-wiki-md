# Scalar mass

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar field](scalar-field.md)

The mass of a canonically normalized [scalar field](scalar-field.md) is determined by its quadratic fluctuation potential at a stationary vacuum. With several real fields, physical squared masses are the [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) of the [scalar mass matrix](scalar-mass-matrix.md). A negative squared mass signals an unstable direction rather than a stable massive particle.
