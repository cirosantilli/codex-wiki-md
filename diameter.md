# Diameter

↑ **Parent:** [Metric space](metric-space.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diameter)

The diameter of a subset $S$ of a metric space is

$$
\operatorname{diam}(S)=\sup\{d(x,y):x,y\in S\}.
$$

The diameter of a nonempty subset $S$ of a [metric space](metric-space.md) is $\operatorname{diam}(S)=\sup\{d(x,y):x,y\in S\}$. For a Euclidean circle it is the longest chord length; the same definition applies to arbitrary metric subsets.
