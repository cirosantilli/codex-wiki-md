# First integral of the Chebyshev function

↑ **Parent:** [Second Chebyshev function](second-chebyshev-function.md)

For the [Second Chebyshev function](second-chebyshev-function.md), finite summation gives $\Psi_1(x)=\sum_{n\le x}(x-n)\Lambda(n)$. For $c>1$, Mellin inversion of the kernel $1/(s(s+1))$ gives

$$
\Psi_1(x)=\frac1{2\pi i}\int_{c-i\infty}^{c+i\infty}-\frac{\zeta'(s)}{\zeta(s)}\frac{x^{s+1}}{s(s+1)}\,ds.
$$

The extra denominator makes this smoothing useful: the resulting sum over [Nontrivial zeros of the Riemann zeta function](nontrivial-zero-of-the-riemann-zeta-function.md) is absolutely convergent. This integral differs from the [integrated Chebyshev sum](integrated-chebyshev-sum.md), which denotes a sum of the values $\psi(x/n)$.
