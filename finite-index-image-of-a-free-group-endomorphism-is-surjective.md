# Finite-index image of a free-group endomorphism is surjective

↑ **Parent:** [Free group](free-group.md)

An [endomorphism](endomorphism.md) of $F_n$ has image generated by at most $n$ elements. If this image has finite index $d$, the [Nielsen–Schreier formula](nielsen-schreier-formula.md) gives rank $1+d(n-1)$. Its [abelianization](abelianization.md) requires at least that many generators. For $n\geq2$, comparison with $n$ forces $d=1$.
