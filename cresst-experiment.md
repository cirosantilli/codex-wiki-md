# CRESST experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Dark-matter direct detection](dark-matter-direct-detection.md)

A cryogenic direct-detection search using scintillating crystal calorimeters. Calcium-tungstate modules measure deposited [energy](energy-split.md) through [phonons](phonon.md) and compare a separate light signal to discriminate recoil populations. Oxygen, calcium and tungsten recoil differently for a given [WIMP](weakly-interacting-massive-particle.md) [mass](mass.md); a model fit must include their responses and backgrounds.[calcium-tungstate detector and signal-region analysis](results-from-730-kg-days-of-the-cresst-ii-dark-matter-search-by-g-angloher-and-others.md)
