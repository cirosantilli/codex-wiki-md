# Hartree-Fock electron gas

↑ **Parent:** [Hartree-Fock method](hartree-fock-method.md)

A variational single-Slater-determinant treatment of interacting [electrons](electron.md) and a neutralizing positive background. For uniform plane-wave occupations the direct background and [electron](electron.md) terms cancel, leaving kinetic and same-spin exchange [energy](energy-split.md). Allowing [spin](spin.md) [polarization](polarization-waves.md) or spatially modulated orbitals enlarges the variational class.
