# Natural density

↑ **Parent:** [Number theory](number-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_density)

For $S\subseteq\mathbb Z_{\geq0}$, its natural density, when it exists, is $d(S)=\lim_{N\to\infty}|S\cap\{0,\ldots,N-1\}|/N$.
