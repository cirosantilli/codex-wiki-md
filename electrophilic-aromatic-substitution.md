# Electrophilic aromatic substitution

↑ **Parent:** [Organic reaction](organic-reaction.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrophilic_aromatic_substitution)

An [electrophile](electrophile.md) replaces a substituent, commonly hydrogen, on an aromatic system. Initial bond formation can make an [arenium ion](arenium-ion.md); loss of a proton then restores [aromaticity](aromaticity.md).
