# Weight-four Eisenstein basis at level two

↑ **Parent:** [Eisenstein series](eisenstein-series.md)

There are no weight-four [cusp forms](cusp-form.md) at level two. A nonzero form would have a weight-twelve [coset norm of a modular form](coset-norm-of-a-modular-form.md) with [modular cusp](cusp-of-a-modular-group.md) order at least two, contradicting the simple [modular cusp](cusp-of-a-modular-group.md) order and interior nonvanishing of the [modular discriminant](modular-discriminant.md). The two independent displayed [Eisenstein series](eisenstein-series.md) span the weight-four space, since its cusp-constant map embeds it in a two-dimensional space.
