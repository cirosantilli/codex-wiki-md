# Subclass

↑ **Parent:** [Class inheritance](class-inheritance.md)

A [software class](class-programming.md) declared to inherit from another [software class](class-programming.md). In nominal [object-oriented programming](object-oriented-programming.md), subclass relationships commonly induce [subtype](subtyping.md) relationships. Valid behavioral substitution also requires satisfying the parent's observable contract, beyond merely declaring the inheritance.
