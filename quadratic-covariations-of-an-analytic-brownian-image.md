# Quadratic covariations of an analytic Brownian image

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic covariation](quadratic-covariation.md)

For an [analytic function](space-of-holomorphic-functions.md) $f=u+iv$ and planar [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) $Z=X+iY$ with [independent](independent-random-variables.md) standard coordinates, the [Cauchy-Riemann equations](cauchy-riemann-equations.md) make $u,v$ harmonic. The [Itô formula](ito-s-lemma.md) gives $dU=u_xdX+u_ydY$ and $dV=-u_ydX+u_xdY$. Localizing the derivatives proves both coordinates are [local martingales](local-martingale.md). Squaring their diffusion coefficients and multiplying the two rows gives the displayed [quadratic variations](quadratic-variation.md) and zero [quadratic covariation](quadratic-covariation.md).
