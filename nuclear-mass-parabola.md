# Nuclear mass parabola

↑ **Parent:** [Semi-empirical mass formula](semi-empirical-mass-formula.md)

At fixed [mass number](mass-number.md) $A$, the smooth [semi-empirical mass formula](semi-empirical-mass-formula.md) makes the atomic [mass](mass.md) approximately quadratic and convex in [atomic number](atomic-number.md) $Z$. Neighboring [nuclear isobars](isobar-nuclide.md) undergo energetically allowed [beta decay](beta-decay.md) toward a discrete local minimum, subject to the electron-mass threshold for [beta-plus decay](beta-plus-decay.md). For odd $A$ there is one smooth branch; for even $A$, [nuclear pairing](nuclear-pairing.md) splits the even-even and odd-odd branches. A local minimum against a single change of $Z$ need not be stable against [double beta decay](double-beta-decay.md).
