# Magnetic-dominated crossed-field orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Constant-field relativistic Lorentz-force solution](constant-field-relativistic-lorentz-force-solution.md)

Uniform perpendicular fields with $E^2<c^2B^2$ admit a subluminal [Lorentz boost](lorentz-boost.md) eliminating the [electric field](electric-field-split.md). A laboratory particle initially at rest follows a circle in the adapted frame and a stretched cycloid in the laboratory. The mean drift [velocity](velocity.md) is $\mathbf E\times\mathbf B/B^2$, while its laboratory kinetic [energy](energy-split.md) is periodic rather than unbounded.
