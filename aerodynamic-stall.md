# Aerodynamic stall

↑ **Parent:** [Airfoil](airfoil.md)

A loss of the approximately attached-flow relation between [angle of attack](angle-of-attack.md) and [lift coefficient](lift-coefficient.md), associated with substantial [flow separation](flow-separation.md). A stalled [airfoil](airfoil.md) can still produce [lift](lift-force.md); its behavior must be measured or modeled beyond [thin airfoil theory](thin-airfoil-theory.md).
