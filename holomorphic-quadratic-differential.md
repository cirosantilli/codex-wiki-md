# Holomorphic quadratic differential

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic differential](quadratic-differential.md)

A [quadratic differential](quadratic-differential.md) with [holomorphic](complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md) local coefficients. If it is nonzero, its zeros have total order $4g-4$ on a [compact](compact-space.md) [genus](genus-of-a-surface.md)-$g$ [Riemann surface](riemann-surfaces.md). Away from its zeros, a nonzero differential has [flat coordinates](natural-coordinate-of-a-holomorphic-differential.md) whose changes of coordinate have the form $w\mapsto\pm w+c$.
