# Centromere

↑ **Parent:** [Chromosome](chromosome.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centromere)

A [centromere](centromere.md) is the [chromosome](chromosome.md) region where a [kinetochore](kinetochore.md) assembles.[Biology 2e by Mary Ann Clark and others; §11.1, Prometaphase I, kinetochore proteins at centromeres](biology-2e-by-mary-ann-clark-and-others/the-process-of-meiosis.md) Following [DNA replication](dna-replication.md), both [sister chromatids](sister-chromatids.md) have their own copied [centromere](centromere.md) regions; the counting convention groups the attached sisters as one replicated [chromosome](chromosome.md). [Cohesin](cohesin.md) maintains sister association: a [centromere](centromere.md) is not an extra [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) bridge joining two separate [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) [molecules](molecule-split.md).[Biology 2e by Mary Ann Clark and others; §10.1, Eukaryotic Chromosomal Structure and Compaction, final paragraph, centromeric region and cohesin](biology-2e-by-mary-ann-clark-and-others/cell-division.md)
