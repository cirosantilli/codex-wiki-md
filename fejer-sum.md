<h1 id="fejer-sum">Fejér sum</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Fejér kernel](fejer-kernel.md)

The $n$th Fejér sum is the arithmetic mean of the first $n$ [Fourier partial sums](fourier-partial-sum.md). Equivalently, it is the convolution of the function with the [Fejér kernel](fejer-kernel.md).
