# Empirical occupation proportions of a finite Markov chain

↑ **Parent:** [Markov chain](markov-chain-split.md)

The empirical occupation proportion is the fraction of the observed [Markov chain](markov-chain-split.md) states equal to $j$. These nonnegative fractions sum to one. To simulate them from a [transition matrix](stochastic-matrix.md), sample an initial state and then draw each successive state from the current state's row, accumulating visit counts. They are empirical frequencies for a finite path, whether or not the chain is already stationary. Long-run convergence to a stationary distribution requires the corresponding recurrence and irreducibility hypotheses.
