# Two-dimensional quartic scalar sunset mass shift

↑ **Parent:** [Sunset diagram](sunset-diagram.md)

In a massive two-dimensional [quartic scalar field theory](quartic-interaction.md), the two-loop [sunset diagram](sunset-diagram.md) is [ultraviolet finite](ultraviolet-finiteness.md) but changes the [pole mass](pole-mass.md). [Feynman parameters](feynman-parameter.md) give, below the three-particle threshold,

$$
|\Sigma_{\rm sun}(s)|=\frac{g_4^2}{6(4\pi)^2}\int_{x,y,z\geq0}dx\,dy\,dz\,
\frac{\delta(1-x-y-z)}{m^2(xy+xz+yz)-sxyz}.
$$

At $s=m^2$ the denominator is positive in the interior of the simplex, and its corners are integrable. Thus the result is finite and nonzero for $g_4\ne0$. Absence of new divergent [counterterms](counterterm.md) does not imply absence of finite [mass counterterms](mass-counterterm.md) in an [on-shell renormalization scheme](on-shell-renormalization-scheme.md).
