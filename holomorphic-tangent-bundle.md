# Holomorphic tangent bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Complex manifold](complex-manifold.md)

The holomorphic tangent bundle of a [complex manifold](complex-manifold.md) $X$ is locally spanned in [holomorphic coordinates](holomorphic-coordinate.md) by $\partial/\partial z^1,\ldots,\partial/\partial z^n$. Under a holomorphic coordinate change $w=w(z)$, the [chain rule](chain-rule.md) gives

$$
\frac{\partial}{\partial z^j}
=\sum_k\frac{\partial w^k}{\partial z^j}\frac{\partial}{\partial w^k},
$$

whose coefficient matrix is holomorphic; these frames therefore define a [holomorphic vector bundle](holomorphic-vector-bundle.md).
