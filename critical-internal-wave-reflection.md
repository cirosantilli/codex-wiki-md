# Critical internal-wave reflection

↑ **Parent:** [Internal-wave slope criticality](internal-wave-slope-criticality.md)

At critical internal-wave reflection, the reflected group velocity is tangent to the boundary and the inviscid reflected wavenumber diverges. [Kinematic viscosity](kinematic-viscosity.md), [mass diffusivity](mass-diffusivity.md), nonlinear steepening, and wave breaking regularize the ideal singularity.
