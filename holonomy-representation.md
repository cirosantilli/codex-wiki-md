# Holonomy representation

↑ **Parent:** [Riemannian holonomy group](riemannian-holonomy-group.md)

The holonomy representation is the natural action of the [Riemannian holonomy group](riemannian-holonomy-group.md) on $T_pM$. It induces representations on cotangent spaces and tensor powers. Irreducibility means that no nonzero proper real linear subspace of $T_pM$ is invariant.
