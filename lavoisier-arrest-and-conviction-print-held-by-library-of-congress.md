# Lavoisier arrest and conviction print held by Library of Congress

↑ **Parent:** [History of science bibliography](history-of-science-bibliography.md)

[https://www.loc.gov/item/2002735664/](https://www.loc.gov/item/2002735664/)

Primary contemporary or near-contemporary representation, Tissandier collection, LOT 13400 no. 60. The catalogue identifies the vignette with arrest and conviction as a tax farmer. Its engraved caption has a faulty trial-date label; use it for contemporary representation, not to override the 8 May 1794 execution chronology.
