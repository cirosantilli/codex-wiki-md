# Nitro ligand

↑ **Parent:** [Ligand in coordination chemistry](ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md)

A [nitro ligand](nitro-ligand.md) is N-bound [nitrite](nitrite.md), $\mathrm{M{-}N(O)_2}$, normally treated as a [sigma-donor ligand](sigma-donor-ligand.md) and [pi-acceptor ligand](pi-acceptor-ligand.md). The word here concerns coordinated [nitrite](nitrite.md), not an independently introduced organic nitro substituent.
