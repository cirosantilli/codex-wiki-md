# Landau critical velocity

↑ **Parent:** [Landau criterion](landau-criterion.md)

The excitation-based kinematic velocity bound in the [Landau criterion](landau-criterion.md). For a heavy weak obstacle and an isotropic excitation spectrum, emitting momentum parallel to the obstacle's motion costs $E(p)-vp$; requiring this to remain positive for every excitation gives the displayed infimum. Vortex nucleation or other dynamical processes can make a measured critical velocity lower than this bound.
