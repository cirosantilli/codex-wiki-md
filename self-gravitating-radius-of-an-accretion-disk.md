# Self-gravitating radius of an accretion disk

↑ **Parent:** [Toomre's stability criterion](toomre-s-stability-criterion.md)

The self-gravitating radius of an accretion disk is the radius at which its [Toomre stability criterion](toomre-s-stability-criterion.md) reaches $Q=1$. Beyond it, a smooth thin disk is vulnerable to spiral structure, gravitational transport, or fragmentation.
