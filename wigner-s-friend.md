<h1 id="wigner-s-friend">Wigner's friend</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Measurement problem](measurement-problem.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wigner's_friend)

A thought experiment placing a friend's physical measurement record inside a larger isolated quantum laboratory. Conditioned on a record the friend has a definite outcome, while an external observer using universal unitary evolution can assign an entangled laboratory state. Its contrast with a collapsed mixture is an instance of the [quantum measurement problem](measurement-problem.md); it does not require giving consciousness a special dynamical law. [Eugene Wigner](eugene-paul-wigner.md) used the observer-within-the-system construction to examine such tensions.
