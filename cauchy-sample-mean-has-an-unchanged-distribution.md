# Cauchy sample mean has an unchanged distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Cauchy distribution](cauchy-distribution.md)

Independent standard [Cauchy distribution](cauchy-distribution.md) variables sum to a Cauchy law of scale $n$, so their sample mean has its original distribution for every sample size. This follows by induction using [convolution of probability densities](convolution-of-independent-random-variables.md). It does not contradict the usual [weak law of large numbers](weak-law-of-large-numbers.md), since the Cauchy law has no finite first absolute moment and its expectation is undefined. Symmetry does not turn a divergent expectation into zero.
