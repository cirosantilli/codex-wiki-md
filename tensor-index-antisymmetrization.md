# Tensor index antisymmetrization

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor](tensor.md)

Antisymmetrization over selected [tensor](tensor.md) indices averages their permutations with the [sign of a permutation](sign-of-a-permutation.md). Square brackets denote it, for example $T_{[ab]}=(T_{ab}-T_{ba})/2$. It projects onto the alternating part. Antisymmetrized second derivatives of a scalar vanish for a [torsion-free connection](torsion-free-connection.md), but antisymmetrized later derivatives act on a tensor and can produce [curvature](curvature.md).
