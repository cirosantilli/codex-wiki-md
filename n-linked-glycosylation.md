# N-linked glycosylation

↑ **Parent:** [Protein glycosylation](protein-glycosylation.md)

[N-linked glycosylation](n-linked-glycosylation.md) attaches a glycan through a nitrogen atom, commonly the side-chain amide nitrogen of asparagine. In the ER pathway, an assembled lipid-linked oligosaccharide is transferred to an appropriate [protein](protein-split.md) acceptor.
