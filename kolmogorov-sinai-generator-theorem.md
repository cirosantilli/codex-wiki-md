# Kolmogorov-Sinai generator theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Generating measurable partition](generating-measurable-partition.md)

For an invertible probability [measure-preserving system](measure-preserving-system.md) with a finite or countable finite-entropy [generating measurable partition](generating-measurable-partition.md) $\xi$, $h_\mu(T)=h_\mu(T,\xi)$. For a noninvertible system the same conclusion holds for a finite-entropy [one-sided generator](one-sided-generator.md). The finite-entropy hypothesis is part of this form of the theorem.
