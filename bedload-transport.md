# Bedload transport

↑ **Parent:** [Sediment transport](sediment-transport.md)

[Bedload transport](bedload-transport.md) is motion of sediment grains along or close to the bed, represented in a depth-averaged model by a solid-volume [flux](flux.md) per unit width. The [Exner equation](exner-equation.md) balances this [flux](flux.md) divergence and changes in porous-bed solid storage against exchange with [suspended sediment](suspended-sediment.md). Consistent [mass](mass.md) units are necessary when adding this [equation](equation.md) to a suspended mass-concentration balance.
