# Reluctance of a conical magnetic pole

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic reluctance](magnetic-reluctance.md)

For a linear-radius taper from $R$ to $r$ over [length](length.md) $s$, constant absolute permeability $\mu$, and a common approximately axial flux, integrate $dx/[\mu\pi\{R-(R-r)x/s\}^2]$ to obtain the displayed reluctance. Leakage, fringing, nonuniform transverse fields and saturation limit this one-dimensional magnetic-circuit approximation.
