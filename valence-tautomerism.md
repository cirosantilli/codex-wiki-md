# Valence tautomerism

↑ **Parent:** [Coordination chemistry](coordination-chemistry.md)

Interconversion between electronic forms that differ in the formal distribution of oxidation among [metal](metal.md) and ligands, without changing overall composition. A [cobalt](cobalt.md) dioxolene example exchanges low-spin Co(III)–catecholate–semiquinonate and high-spin Co(II)–bis(semiquinonate) forms. This differs from changing only the [spin](spin.md) state at fixed [metal](metal.md) and ligand charges.
