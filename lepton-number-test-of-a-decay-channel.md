# Lepton-number test of a decay channel

↑ **Parent:** [Lepton number](lepton-number.md)

Sum the [lepton numbers](lepton-number.md) of every final particle and compare with the initial sum. For example, $e^-\nu_e e^-e^+$ has $L_e=2$, whereas $e^-\bar\nu_e e^-e^+$ has $L_e=0$. The two differ physically; changing a neutrino bar is not a harmless typographic simplification. Overall [lepton number](lepton-number.md) is conserved by the usual charged-current [Standard Model](standard-model-split.md) amplitudes with Dirac or massless [neutrinos](neutrino.md).
