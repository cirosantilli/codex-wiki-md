# Unimodular basis test for an integer direct summand

↑ **Parent:** [Direct summand](direct-summand.md)

Integer row vectors forming a square [unimodular matrix](unimodular-matrix.md) are a basis of the corresponding [free abelian group](free-abelian-group.md): the adjugate formula makes the inverse integral. The subgroup spanned by any subset of that basis is therefore a [direct summand](direct-summand.md), complemented by the remaining basis vectors. In contrast, a proper finite-index subgroup of a free abelian group cannot be a direct summand: its nonzero finite quotient would have to embed as a subgroup of a torsion-free group.
