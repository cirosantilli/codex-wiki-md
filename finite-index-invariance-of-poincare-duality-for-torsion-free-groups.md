# Finite-index invariance of Poincare duality for torsion-free groups

↑ **Parent:** [Poincare duality group](poincare-duality-group.md)

For a [torsion-free group](torsion-free-group.md) $G$ and [finite-index subgroup](finite-index-subgroup.md) $H$, $G$ is a [Poincare duality group](poincare-duality-group.md) of dimension $n$ if and only if $H$ is. Finite-index induction and coinduction of $\mathbb ZH$ coincide and give $\mathbb ZG$, so [Shapiro's lemma](shapiro-s-lemma.md) identifies their group-ring cohomology as abelian groups. The finite-index finiteness and cohomological-dimension lemmas supply finite projective resolutions. Without torsion-freeness of $G$ the converse fails: $\mathbb Z\times C_2$ contains the index-two $PD^1$ subgroup $\mathbb Z$ but has infinite integral cohomological dimension.
