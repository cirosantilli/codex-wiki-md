# Transverse energy instability of nonconstant temperature rolls

↑ **Parent:** [Increasing-gradient functional for poorly conducting convection](increasing-gradient-functional-for-poorly-conducting-convection.md)

A nonconstant smooth periodic roll obeys $\mu\langle T_0'^2\rangle-\langle T_0''^2\rangle-\langle T_0'^4\rangle=0$. The orthogonal-roll perturbation raises the [increasing-gradient functional for poorly conducting convection](increasing-gradient-functional-for-poorly-conducting-convection.md) by the displayed amount. The [variance](variance-split.md) is strictly positive: a nonconstant periodic function has a derivative that vanishes somewhere and is nonzero somewhere. The [Hessian](hessian-matrix.md) of $V$ therefore has a positive direction. Its self-adjoint linearized evolution operator has a positive [Rayleigh quotient](rayleigh-quotient.md) and hence a positive [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md), proving transverse [linear instability](linear-instability.md) wherever a nonconstant roll exists.
