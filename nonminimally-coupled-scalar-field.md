# Nonminimally coupled scalar field

↑ **Parent:** [Real scalar field](real-scalar-field.md)

A nonminimal scalar coupling includes an interaction between the field and spacetime curvature, such as $-\xi R\Phi^2$ in the [Lagrangian density](lagrangian-density.md). The [Euler-Lagrange field equation](euler-lagrange-field-equation.md) for $\mathcal L=-\frac12(\nabla\Phi)^2-\xi R\Phi^2$ is $\Box\Phi-2\xi R\Phi=0$. The coefficient is convention dependent: an action written with $-\frac12\xi R\Phi^2$ has $\xi$ instead of $2\xi$.
