# Skyrmion

↑ **Parent:** [Skyrme model](skyrme-model.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skyrmion)

A Skyrmion is a finite-energy topological soliton of the [Skyrme model](skyrme-model.md). Its baryon number is the degree of the compactified spatial map $S^3\to SU(2)\simeq S^3$.
