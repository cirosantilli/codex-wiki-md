# Affine plane

↑ **Parent:** [Affine scheme](affine-scheme.md)

The affine plane over a field $k$ is the [affine scheme](affine-scheme.md) $\operatorname{Spec}k[x,y]$.

The two-dimensional scheme has an [affine space](affine-space.md) of rational points over its base field; its scheme structure also records non-rational points and local rings.
