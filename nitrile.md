# Nitrile

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical compound](chemical-compound.md)

A compound containing a carbon-attached $\mathrm{-C\equiv N}$ group. Organomagnesium addition can generate an anionic [nitrogen](nitrogen.md) intermediate whose hydrolysis yields a [ketone](ketone.md).
