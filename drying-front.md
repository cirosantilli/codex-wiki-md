# Drying front

↑ **Parent:** [Free boundary problem](free-boundary-problem.md)

A [drying front](drying-front.md) separates a positive liquid layer from a region where its thickness is zero. Its position is an unknown boundary determined with the wet-region solution. At a continuous zero-height front, a jump balance alone need not fix its speed; the vanishing-depth profile and removal law can supply that information. Source terms representing [evaporation](evaporation.md) or [infiltration](soil-infiltration.md) must respect nonnegative water availability in the dry region.
