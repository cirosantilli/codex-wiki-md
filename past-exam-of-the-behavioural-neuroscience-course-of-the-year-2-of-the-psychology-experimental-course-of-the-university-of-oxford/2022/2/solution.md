**Monoamine signalling contributes to mood regulation and to the actions of several antidepressants, but a universal “low transmitter causes depression” account is not established.** Three propositions must be separated: a drug changes a molecular target; that change helps produce improvement; and the untreated disorder was caused by an opposite abnormality. Evidence for one proposition does not logically establish the next.

The [monoamine hypothesis of depression](../../../monoamine-hypothesis-of-depression.md) developed from pharmacology. [Monoamine oxidase](../../../monoamine-oxidase.md) inhibition reduces transmitter metabolism, while some tricyclic antidepressants inhibit transmitter reuptake; [SSRI](../../../selective-serotonin-reuptake-inhibitor.md) principally inhibit [serotonin](../../../serotonin.md) reuptake. [Norepinephrine](../../../norepinephrine.md), [serotonin](../../../serotonin.md) and [dopamine](../../../dopamine.md) are distinct [monoamines](../../../monoamine.md), acting through different receptors and pathways. The historical catecholamine proposal emphasized [norepinephrine](../../../norepinephrine.md) and already acknowledged the limitations of extrapolating from drug effects to disease mechanisms. Treating every low affective state as depletion of a single interchangeable brain substance is a substantially stronger claim.[1965 abstract and pp. 509–522: proposed catecholamine account and explicit limits on pharmacological extrapolation](../../../the-catecholamine-hypothesis-of-affective-disorders-a-review-of-supporting-evidence-by-joseph-j-schildkraut.md)

Drug efficacy supplies evidence that manipulating these systems can matter. A 2018 [network meta-analysis](../../../network-meta-analysis.md) synthesized 522 double-blind trials in adults with [major depressive disorder](../../../major-depressive-disorder.md) and found each included antidepressant more efficacious than placebo on its response outcome. This is evidence synthesis, not a direct assay of patients' transmitter levels. Average effects, trial selection, [publication bias](../../../publication-bias.md), blinding and symptom-scale outcomes limit interpretation, and not every patient benefits. Even a perfectly demonstrated effect would show that an intervention can change a pathological state, not that it reverses its original cause. A downstream compensatory mechanism can be therapeutically useful without being the initiating lesion.[Methods, Findings and Discussion: acute adult trials, response outcome and limitations](../../../comparative-efficacy-and-acceptability-of-21-antidepressant-drugs-for-the-acute-treatment-of-adults-with-major-depressive-disorder-by-andrea-cipriani-and-others.md)

Measuring the proposed deficiency is itself difficult. Peripheral [serotonin](../../../serotonin.md) is not a direct measurement of release at a particular central [synapse](../../../synapse.md). Metabolites combine synthesis, release, reuptake and degradation; receptor or transporter imaging is not simply a concentration gauge. A difference observed after illness onset might be causal, compensatory, treatment-related or associated with [physiological stress](../../../physiological-stress.md). Establishing a general deficiency would require convergent measures in appropriate untreated populations, temporal precedence and a relationship to well-defined symptoms, not merely one group-average biomarker.

Precursor depletion supplies a more informative intervention. In a double-blind crossover study, patients in antidepressant-induced remission underwent acute [tryptophan](../../../tryptophan.md) depletion and a control condition. Fourteen of 21 experienced a transient depressive relapse after depletion, whereas control testing did not produce significant behavioural effects. This supports dependence of some maintained remissions on [serotonin](../../../serotonin.md) precursor availability. It does not show that all untreated depressed patients began with low [serotonin](../../../serotonin.md), that depletion is sufficient to generate the full syndrome in everyone, or that [norepinephrine](../../../norepinephrine.md) and [dopamine](../../../dopamine.md) are irrelevant. The selected, previously treated sample is central to the causal question actually tested.[Abstract and pp. 411–418: remitted sample, crossover control and 14/21 transient relapses](../../../serotonin-function-and-the-mechanism-of-antidepressant-action-by-pedro-l-delgado-and-others.md)

Time course also challenges a simple replenishment story. Initial transporter inhibition can occur before substantial clinical improvement. That discrepancy motivates slower receptor, circuit, learning and plasticity changes; it does not imply that early drug effects are absent. In a randomized placebo-controlled experiment, an acute dose of a norepinephrine reuptake inhibitor changed positive emotional processing in depressed participants before subjective mood or anxiety changed. Facial-expression judgments and emotionally valenced categorization and recall supplied behavioural readouts. These observations support an [affective processing bias](../../../affective-processing-bias.md) account: altered experience processing could accumulate into mood change. They do not establish that the measured early shift mediates every later clinical response.[Abstract; Methods and Results, pp. 1178–1184: randomized emotional-processing battery before mood change](../../../effect-of-acute-antidepressant-administration-on-negative-affective-bias-in-depressed-patients-by-catherine-j-harmer-and-others.md)

Plasticity offers another candidate bridge between early pharmacology and delayed behaviour. In mice, preventing antidepressant-associated [adult hippocampal neurogenesis](../../../adult-hippocampal-neurogenesis.md) blocked behavioural effects in particular experimental assays. The evidence included genetic and localized irradiation approaches. It supplies a mechanistic dependency within that model, rather than merely a [correlation](../../../pearson-correlation-coefficient.md) between a drug and cell proliferation. However, the assays are not human [major depressive disorder](../../../major-depressive-disorder.md), the interventions can have additional effects, and necessity for one behavioural endpoint does not establish either sufficiency or a universal human mechanism.[Abstract and pp. 805–809: interference with neurogenesis and model-specific behavioural effects](../../../requirement-of-hippocampal-neurogenesis-for-the-behavioral-effects-of-antidepressants-by-luca-santarelli-and-others.md)

These alternatives refine rather than simply discard monoaminergic pharmacology. [Serotonin](../../../serotonin.md) or [norepinephrine](../../../norepinephrine.md) could regulate plasticity and learning within distributed [neural circuits](../../../neural-circuit.md); baseline concentrations need not be uniformly low. Receptor sensitivity, pathway-specific activity and environmental experience could determine the effect of an intervention. Conversely, observing altered [dopamine](../../../dopamine.md)-related motivation would not prove that all [anhedonia](../../../anhedonia.md), cognitive slowing and sadness have one cause. [Major depressive disorder](../../../major-depressive-disorder.md) groups multiple symptom patterns, so a mechanism associated with one dimension can be diluted or misleading when treated as a universal disease explanation.

The strongest conclusion available at the examination date is therefore conditional. Particular [monoamine](../../../monoamine.md) manipulations can alter affective processing and symptoms, and some treatment responses depend on monoaminergic function. The evidence does not identify a single brain-wide deficit that is necessary and sufficient for human depression. A useful successor account specifies transmitter, receptor, circuit, time scale and symptom, and tests how biological changes interact with learning and environment. This evaluation concerns explanatory evidence, not a recommendation to start, stop or choose a medication.
