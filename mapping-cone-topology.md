# Mapping cone (topology)

↑ **Parent:** [Mapping cylinder](mapping-cylinder.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mapping_cone_(topology))

The topological mapping cone of $f:X\to Y$ is $Y\cup_f CX$, where $CX$ is the cone on $X$ and its base is identified with $Y$ through $f$. For nonempty $X$, the cone vertex supplies a distinguished basepoint. This construction is related to, but distinct from, the [mapping cone](mapping-cone-homological-algebra.md) of a [chain map](chain-map.md).
