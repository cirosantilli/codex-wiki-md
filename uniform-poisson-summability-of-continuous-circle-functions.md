# Uniform Poisson summability of continuous circle functions

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson kernel on the circle](poisson-kernel-on-the-circle.md)

The [Poisson kernel on the circle](poisson-kernel-on-the-circle.md) is nonnegative, has normalized integral one, and its mass outside any fixed arc about zero tends to zero as $r\uparrow1$. Split the convolution error into a short arc, controlled by the [modulus of continuity](modulus-of-continuity.md), and its complement, controlled by the vanishing kernel mass. This proves [uniform convergence](uniform-convergence.md) for every continuous periodic function. Its Fourier multiplier is $r^{|n|}$, so the convolution is the Abel sum of the [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md).
