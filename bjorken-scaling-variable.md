# Bjorken scaling variable

↑ **Parent:** [Deep inelastic scattering](deep-inelastic-scattering.md)

In [deep inelastic scattering](deep-inelastic-scattering.md), the scaling variable is $x=Q^2/(2P\cdot q)$ with $Q^2=-q^2$. In the massless collinear [parton model](parton-model.md), the outgoing on-shell condition $(xP+q)^2=0$ identifies $x$ with the struck parton's incoming longitudinal momentum fraction.
