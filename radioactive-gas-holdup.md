# Radioactive gas holdup

↑ **Parent:** [Radioactive waste management](radioactive-waste-management.md)

Temporary retention of radioactive gas so that nuclear [radioactive decay](radioactive-decay.md) lowers the [radioactive activity](radioactive-activity.md) released. A guaranteed minimum [residence time](residence-time.md), an average [residence time](residence-time.md) and a tank filling time are different design quantities.
