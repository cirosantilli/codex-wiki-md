# Radial integration over a ball

↑ **Parent:** [Volume of an n-ball](volume-of-an-n-ball.md)

Let $V_d$ be the [volume of an n-ball](volume-of-an-n-ball.md) of unit radius in $\mathbb R^d$. A radial shell has volume $dV_dr^{d-1}dr$, by differentiating $V_dr^d$. Integrating shells, or using [hyperspherical coordinates](hyperspherical-coordinates.md), therefore reduces a radial integrand over a [Euclidean ball](euclidean-ball.md) to the displayed one-dimensional integral. The constant $dV_d$ is the [surface area of an n-sphere](surface-area-of-an-n-sphere.md) of unit radius, with sphere dimension $d-1$.
