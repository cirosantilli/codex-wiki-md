# Functional group interaction profile

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrogen bond](hydrogen-bond.md)

A [functional group interaction profile](functional-group-interaction-profile.md) maps the free-energy contribution of a solute donor–acceptor contact after accounting for competing solvent contacts. Solvent polarity and preferential solvation determine favourable and unfavourable regions.
