# Sign of an abacus hook-removal sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Murnaghan–Nakayama rule](murnaghan-nakayama-rule.md)

The parity of the sum of the leg lengths in any sequence that removes all $e$-hooks from $\lambda$ is independent of the sequence. Its sign $\varepsilon_e(\lambda)$ is therefore well defined and supplies the common sign in repeated applications of the [Murnaghan–Nakayama rule](murnaghan-nakayama-rule.md).
