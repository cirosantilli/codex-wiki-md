# Global sections functor

↑ **Parent:** [Elementary topos](elementary-topos.md)

Global sections are the maps from the terminal object: $\Gamma(X)=\operatorname{Hom}(1,X)$. For a [Grothendieck topos](grothendieck-topos.md), this is the direct image of its geometric morphism to sets. In a [presheaf category](presheaf-category.md), it is right adjoint to the constant-presheaf functor.
