# Site (category theory)

↑ **Parent:** [Grothendieck topology](grothendieck-topology.md)

A [site](site-category-theory.md) is a category equipped with a [Grothendieck topology](grothendieck-topology.md), specifying which sieves are covering. The [sheaves on a site](sheaf-on-a-site.md) satisfy unique amalgamation of matching families on those sieves. A small [site](site-category-theory.md) presents a [Grothendieck topos](grothendieck-topos.md).
