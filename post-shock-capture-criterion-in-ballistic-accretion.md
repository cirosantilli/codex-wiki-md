# Post-shock capture criterion in ballistic accretion

↑ **Parent:** [Ballistic streamline focusing by a moving star](ballistic-streamline-focusing-by-a-moving-star.md)

If transverse [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) is dissipated at the downstream axis, the remaining [specific orbital energy](specific-orbital-energy.md) is $v^2/2-GM/b$. Binding requires $b<2GM/v^2$, corresponding to incident impact parameter $d<2GM/v^2$. Integrating the captured [mass flux](mass-flux.md) gives the [Bondi--Hoyle--Lyttleton accretion rate](bondi-hoyle-lyttleton-accretion-rate.md).
