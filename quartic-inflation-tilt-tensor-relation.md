# Quartic-inflation tilt-tensor relation

↑ **Parent:** [Monomial inflation potential](monomial-inflation-potential.md)

For canonical slow-roll inflation with $V(\phi)=\lambda\phi^4$, the leading [scalar spectral index](scalar-spectral-index.md) and [tensor-to-scalar ratio](tensor-to-scalar-ratio.md) obey

$$
n_s-1=-\frac3{16}r.
$$

It is a parameter-free consistency check of the quartic potential at leading slow-roll order.
