# Uniform power bound from separated amplification roots

↑ **Parent:** [von Neumann stability analysis](von-neumann-stability-analysis.md)

For a uniformly bounded two-level Fourier companion [matrix](matrix.md) $C(\theta)$ with distinct roots $g_1,g_2$ of modulus at most one, its powers satisfy

$$
C^n=\frac{g_1^n(C-g_2I)-g_2^n(C-g_1I)}{g_1-g_2}.
$$

A positive frequency-uniform gap therefore gives a uniform bound on every power. The [Parseval identity](parseval-identity.md) then supplies spatial [L2 norm](l2-norm.md) stability. When the gap closes at a repeated unit root, a [Jordan block](jordan-block.md) can produce growth proportional to the number of steps despite both roots having modulus one.
