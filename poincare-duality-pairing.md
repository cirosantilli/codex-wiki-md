# Poincare duality pairing

↑ **Parent:** [Poincare duality](poincare-duality.md)

For a closed oriented $d$-dimensional [manifold](topological-manifold.md) over a [field](field.md) $F$, the [cup product](cup-product.md) pairing

$$
H^q(M;F)\times H^{d-q}(M;F)\to F,\qquad (a,b)\mapsto\langle a\smile b,[M]_F\rangle
$$

is a [perfect pairing](perfect-pairing.md) between complementary degrees. The [cap product](cap-product.md) with the [fundamental class](fundamental-class.md) is an [isomorphism](isomorphism.md) by [Poincare duality](poincare-duality.md), and the [universal coefficient theorem for cohomology](universal-coefficient-theorem-for-cohomology.md) over $F$ identifies the complementary [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) with the full [dual space](dual-space.md) of the resulting [homology](homology-split.md). Evaluation is therefore a perfect pairing. Taking the top-degree part of the same formula defines a nondegenerate [bilinear form](bilinear-form.md) on the full graded [cohomology](cohomology-split.md). A boundary requires relative groups and [Poincare-Lefschetz duality](lefschetz-duality.md) instead.
