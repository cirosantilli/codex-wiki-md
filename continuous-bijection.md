# Continuous bijection

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous map](continuous-map.md)

A continuous bijection is a [continuous map](continuous-map.md) that is a [bijection](bijection.md). It need not be a [homeomorphism](homeomorphism.md), since its [inverse function](inverse-function.md) need not be [continuous](continuous-function.md). The [compact-to-Hausdorff continuous bijection theorem](compact-to-hausdorff-continuous-bijection-theorem.md) supplies a useful sufficient condition for the stronger conclusion.
