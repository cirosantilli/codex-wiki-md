# Shrinking cone energy argument

↑ **Parent:** [Finite propagation speed](finite-propagation-speed.md)

For the homogeneous speed-one [wave equation](wave-equation-split.md), integrate its [wave energy](wave-energy.md) density over $B(x_*,T-t)$. The moving boundary contributes $-e$, while the usual flux contributes $u_t\partial_nu$. Their sum is $-\frac12(u_t-\partial_nu)^2-\frac12|\nabla_{\mathrm{tan}}u|^2\leq0$. Zero [Cauchy data](cauchy-data.md) in the initial ball therefore force zero solution in its backward [light cone](light-cone.md).
