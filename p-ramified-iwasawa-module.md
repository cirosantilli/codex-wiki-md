# p-ramified Iwasawa module

↑ **Parent:** [Iwasawa module](iwasawa-module.md)

Let $M_\infty$ be the maximal abelian pro-$p$ extension of $F_\infty$ unramified away from primes over $p$. Its [Galois group](galois-group.md) is the p-ramified Iwasawa module. In the cyclotomic tower of $\mathbb Q(\mu_p)$ for odd $p$, its [Iwasawa-module rank](iwasawa-module-rank.md) is $(p-1)/2$. It is therefore quite different from the torsion [unramified Iwasawa module](unramified-iwasawa-module.md).
