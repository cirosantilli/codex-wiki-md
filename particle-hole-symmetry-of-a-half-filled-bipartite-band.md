# Particle-hole symmetry of a half-filled bipartite band

↑ **Parent:** [Tight binding](tight-binding.md)

A nearest-neighbour [tight-binding model](tight-binding.md) on a bipartite lattice, with identical on-site [energy](energy-split.md) $\epsilon_0$, has paired single-particle [energies](energy-split.md) $\epsilon_0\pm x$. At half filling, $f(\epsilon_0+x)+f(\epsilon_0-x)=1$ for the [Fermi-Dirac distribution](fermi-dirac-distribution.md) with $\mu=\epsilon_0$. Thus its fixed-density [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) equals $\epsilon_0$ at all positive [temperatures](temperature-split.md) within the isolated-band model. An anisotropic rectangular nearest-neighbour band retains this property; additional same-sublattice hopping or unequal on-site [energies](energy-split.md) can remove it.
