# Correlation time

↑ **Parent:** [Autocorrelation](autocorrelation.md)

A correlation time measures how rapidly a stationary [stochastic process](stochastic-process-split.md) loses temporal dependence. Specify whether it means an exponential decay time, an integral of the normalized [autocorrelation](autocorrelation.md), or another width. For $R(t)=R(0)e^{-|t|/\tau}$, the exponential time and the one-sided integral $\int_0^\infty R(t)dt/R(0)$ both equal $\tau$.
