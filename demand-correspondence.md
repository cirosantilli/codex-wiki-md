# Demand correspondence

↑ **Parent:** [Exchange economy](exchange-economy.md)

A [demand correspondence](demand-correspondence.md) assigns a nonempty set of chosen consumption bundles to each price and wealth pair. Every chosen bundle satisfies the [budget constraint](budget-constraint.md). A [utility function](utility-function-split.md) rationalizes the [demand correspondence](demand-correspondence.md) when the chosen bundles are exactly its maximizers over the budget set.
