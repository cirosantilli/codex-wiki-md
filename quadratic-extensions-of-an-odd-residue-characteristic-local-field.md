# Quadratic extensions of an odd-residue-characteristic local field

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic extensions from square classes](quadratic-extensions-from-square-classes.md)

For a [p-adic field](p-adic-field.md) with odd residue characteristic, the square map is an isomorphism on $1+\mathfrak m_K$ by [Hensel lemma](hensel-s-lemma.md). The parity of the valuation and the square class of the residue unit therefore determine the [square-class group of a field](square-class-group-of-a-field.md). Its three nontrivial classes give the three [quadratic extensions](quadratic-extension.md): one unramified extension and two totally ramified extensions.
