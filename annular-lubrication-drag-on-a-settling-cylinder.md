# Annular lubrication drag on a settling cylinder

↑ **Parent:** [Moving-boundary lubrication flux](moving-boundary-lubrication-flux.md)

For a cylinder of radius $a$ in a coaxial container with gap $h\ll a$, global displacement forces reverse flow through the gap and creates

$$
\Delta p\simeq\frac{6\mu aLU}{h^3}.
$$

The pressure drag exceeds side shear by order $a/h$. Balancing it with excess weight gives $U\simeq\Delta\rho,gh^3/(6\mu a)$.
