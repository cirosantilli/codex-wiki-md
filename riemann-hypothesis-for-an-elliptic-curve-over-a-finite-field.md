# Riemann hypothesis for an elliptic curve over a finite field

↑ **Parent:** [Zeta function of an elliptic curve over a finite field](zeta-function-of-an-elliptic-curve-over-a-finite-field.md)

If $1-aT+qT^2=(1-\alpha T)(1-\beta T)$ is the numerator of the zeta function, the Riemann hypothesis in this case is $|\alpha|=|\beta|=\sqrt q$. It is equivalent to the [Hasse theorem for elliptic curves](hasse-s-theorem-on-elliptic-curves.md).
