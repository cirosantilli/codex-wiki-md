# Isospin breaking

↑ **Parent:** [Isospin](isospin.md)

Departures from exact [isospin](isospin.md) arise principally from unequal up- and down-quark [masses](mass.md) and the [electromagnetic interaction](electromagnetic-interaction.md). Equal-rate isospin relations are leading approximations, especially when different charged and neutral thresholds affect the available [phase space](phase-space.md).
