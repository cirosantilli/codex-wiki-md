# Geodesic trace formula for the Laplace-Beltrami operator

↑ **Parent:** [Laplace-Beltrami operator](laplace-beltrami-operator.md)

Choose an [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) $e_i$ of the [tangent space](tangent-space.md) at $p$ and the unit-speed [geodesics](geodesic.md) with initial velocities $e_i$. Their covariant accelerations vanish, so $(f\circ\gamma_i)''(0)=\operatorname{Hess}_g f(e_i,e_i)$. Taking the [metric trace](metric-trace.md) of the [Riemannian Hessian](riemannian-hessian.md) proves the formula for $\Delta=\operatorname{div}\nabla$. The [positive Laplace-Beltrami operator](positive-laplace-beltrami-operator.md) has the opposite sign.
