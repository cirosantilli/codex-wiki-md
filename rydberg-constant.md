# Rydberg constant

↑ **Parent:** [Rydberg formula](rydberg-formula.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rydberg_constant)

The inverse-length constant in the infinite-nuclear-mass hydrogenic spectrum, $R_\infty=m_ee^4/[8\epsilon_0^2h^3c]$. A nucleus of finite mass replaces it by $R_M=R_\infty\mu/m_e$, where $\mu$ is the electron-nucleus [reduced mass](reduced-mass.md). Some spectroscopic fits call $Z^2R_M$ an effective Rydberg constant; the charge-inclusive and reduced-mass-only conventions must be distinguished.
