# Semantic consequence under an assignment

↑ **Parent:** [First-order logic](first-order-logic.md)

In a fixed [first-order structure](first-order-structure.md), the satisfaction implication at a [first-order variable assignment](first-order-variable-assignment.md) $v$ says that if all formulas of $\Gamma$ hold at $v$, then $\varphi$ holds at $v$. Semantic consequence requires that implication in every relevant structure and assignment. The assignment's codomain belongs to its structure; different domains are not silently assigned one common map. When all formulas are [first-order sentences](first-order-sentence.md), assignments do not affect any of their truth values.
