<h1 id="gelfand-kirillov-dimension-of-a-module">Gelfand–Kirillov dimension of a module</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Gelfand–Kirillov dimension](gelfand-kirillov-dimension.md)

For a nonzero right [module](module-mathematics.md) $M$ that is a [finitely generated module](finitely-generated-module.md), choose a finite-dimensional generating [vector subspace](vector-subspace.md) $W$ and define $\operatorname{GKdim}_AM=\limsup_{n\to\infty}\log(\dim_kWV^n)/\log n$, where $V$ generates the [algebra](algebra-split.md) and contains $1$. For a left [module](module-mathematics.md) use $V^nW$. It is independent of these choices and measures polynomial-order growth of a generating [filtration of a module](filtration-of-a-module.md).
