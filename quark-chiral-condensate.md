# Quark chiral condensate

↑ **Parent:** [Chiral symmetry](chiral-symmetry.md)

A nonzero vacuum value of the [scalar](scalar.md) [quark](quark.md) density breaks the massless nonsinglet [chiral symmetry](chiral-symmetry.md) to its diagonal vector subgroup. Indeed, the [nonsinglet axial charge algebra of quark densities](nonsinglet-axial-charge-algebra-of-quark-densities.md) gives $\langle[Q_{5,a},P_b]\rangle=-i\delta_{ab}\langle\bar q q\rangle$, whereas invariance of the vacuum under every conserved [axial charge](axial-charge.md) would make these expectations vanish. The three broken generators produce three massless [Goldstone bosons](goldstone-boson.md) in the exact chiral limit. Small equal [quark masses](quark-mass.md) explicitly break that [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md) and turn the [pions](pion.md) into [pseudo-Goldstone bosons](pseudo-goldstone-boson.md), with squared masses linear in the [quark](quark.md) [mass](mass.md) at leading order.
