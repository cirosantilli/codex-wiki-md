# Integrable power-law density at an axis or center

↑ **Parent:** [Density](density.md)

A nonnegative [mass density](density.md) proportional to $r^p$ has finite local mass near a straight axis if $\int_0^\varepsilon r^{p+1}\,dr$ converges, namely $p>-2$. Near a three-dimensional center the corresponding condition is convergence of $\int_0^\varepsilon r^{p+2}\,dr$, namely $p>-3$. These follow from the [cylindrical volume element](cylindrical-volume-element.md) and [spherical volume element](spherical-volume-element.md). At equality the divergence is logarithmic. An unbounded density does not by itself imply infinite [mass](mass.md).
