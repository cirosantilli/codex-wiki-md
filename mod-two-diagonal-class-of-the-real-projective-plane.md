# Mod-two diagonal class of the real projective plane

↑ **Parent:** [Cohomology class of the diagonal](cohomology-class-of-the-diagonal.md)

The [cohomology ring](cohomology-ring.md) of the product of two [real projective planes](real-projective-plane.md) is $\mathbb F_2[x,y]/(x^3,y^3)$, with $|x|=|y|=1$. Pairing against $x^2$, $xy$ and $y^2$ identifies the [Poincare dual](poincare-dual.md) of the diagonal as $x^2+xy+y^2$. For a self-map $f$ with $f^*x=\varepsilon x$, the graph pullback evaluates on the mod-two [fundamental class](fundamental-class.md) as $1+\varepsilon+\varepsilon^2=1$. Thus no graph of a map on the whole projective plane can avoid the diagonal. The argument uses a relative [Thom class](thom-class.md) and does not assume transverse intersections.
