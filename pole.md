# Pole

↑ **Parent:** [Classification of isolated singularities](classification-of-isolated-singularities.md)

A function has a pole of order $k$ at $a$ when $(z-a)^kf(z)$ extends holomorphically and is nonzero at $a$.

A [pole](pole.md) is the negative-order case in the [zeros and poles](zeros-and-poles.md) classification of a meromorphic function.
