# Completely noisy binary symmetric channel

↑ **Parent:** [Binary symmetric channel](binary-symmetric-channel.md)

At crossover probability $p=1/2$, the two rows of the [transition matrix](stochastic-matrix.md) of a [binary symmetric channel](binary-symmetric-channel.md) coincide. Its output is then [independent](independent-random-variables.md) of its input and its [capacity](binary-symmetric-channel-capacity.md) is zero.
