# Nobel Prize in Physics in 1978

↑ **Parent:** [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics-split.md)

One half went to [Pyotr Leonidovich Kapitza](pyotr-leonidovich-kapitza.md) for inventions and discoveries in low-temperature [physics](physics-split.md). [Arno Allan Penzias](arno-allan-penzias.md) and [Robert Woodrow Wilson](robert-woodrow-wilson.md) received one quarter each for discovering the [cosmic microwave background](cosmic-microwave-background-split.md).[Individual official summary: Kapitza one-half for low-temperature work; Penzias and Wilson one-quarter each for cosmic microwave background discovery.](the-nobel-prize-in-physics-1978-by-nobel-prize-outreach.md)
