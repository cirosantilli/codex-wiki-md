# Peres product condition

↑ **Parent:** [Ratio definition of cutoff](ratio-definition-of-cutoff.md)

For finite [irreducible Markov chains](irreducible-markov-chain.md) that are [aperiodic Markov chains](aperiodic-markov-chain.md) and [reversible Markov chains](reversible-markov-chain.md), the [ratio definition of cutoff](ratio-definition-of-cutoff.md) and $T_n=t_{\mathrm{mix}}^{(n)}(1/4)\to\infty$ imply the Peres product condition, with $\gamma_n=1-\lambda_{2,n}$. If $\gamma_nT_n\leq B$ on a subsequence, then $\gamma_{*,n}\leq\gamma_n$ and the [eigenvalue lower bound for total variation mixing](eigenvalue-lower-bound-for-total-variation-mixing.md) give

$$
\frac{t_{\mathrm{mix}}^{(n)}(\varepsilon)}{T_n}\geq\left(\frac1B-\frac1{T_n}\right)\log\frac1{2\varepsilon}.
$$

For sufficiently small fixed $\varepsilon$, this contradicts the limit one forced by [cutoff for Markov chains](cutoff-for-markov-chains.md). The same argument proves the stronger conclusion $\gamma_{*,n}T_n\to\infty$.
