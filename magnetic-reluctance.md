# Magnetic reluctance

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic circuit](magnetic-circuit.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnetic_reluctance)

The [magnetic reluctance](magnetic-reluctance.md) of a linear section of [magnetic circuit](magnetic-circuit.md) is the ratio of the winding's integrated [magnetic field intensity](magnetic-field-intensity.md) to the [magnetic flux](magnetic-flux.md), with units $\mathrm{A\,Wb^{-1}}$. For uniform length $\ell$, cross-sectional [area](surface-area.md) $A$, and relative [magnetic permeability](permeability-electromagnetism.md) $\mu_r$, $\mathcal R=\ell/(\mu_0\mu_rA)$. Series sections carrying the same [magnetic flux](magnetic-flux.md) have additive [magnetic reluctances](magnetic-reluctance.md), and $NI=\Phi\sum_i\mathcal R_i$.
