# Club filter completeness

↑ **Parent:** [Club filter](club-filter.md)

The [club filter](club-filter.md) on a regular uncountable [cardinal number](cardinal-number.md) $\kappa$ is $\kappa$-complete. Intersections of fewer than $\kappa$ [club sets](club-set.md) are closed. For unboundedness, repeatedly step past a point of each club and take a countable supremum; regularity keeps all suprema below $\kappa$, and closure puts the final supremum in every club.
