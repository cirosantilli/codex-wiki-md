# Empty product

↑ **Parent:** [Multiplication](multiplication.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Empty_product)

A product over the empty [set](set-split.md) has value one, the identity for [multiplication](multiplication.md). This makes splitting a product into successive pieces valid even when one piece contains no factors. In particular, the [factorial](factorial.md) convention is $0!=1$.
