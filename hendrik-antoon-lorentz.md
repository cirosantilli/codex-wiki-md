# Hendrik Antoon Lorentz

↑ **Parent:** [List of physicists](list-of-physicists.md)

A [physicist](physicist-split.md).[Biographical identity and professional specialization](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/physics/1902/lorentz/biographical/)

The [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md) connects charged-particle motion to the [electric field](electric-field-split.md) and [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md). His theoretical account of the [Zeeman effect](zeeman-splitting.md) connects that motion to changes in emitted [light](light.md).
