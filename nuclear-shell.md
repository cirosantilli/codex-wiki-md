# Nuclear shell

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear shell model](nuclear-shell-model.md)

A nuclear shell groups single-particle [energy levels](energy-level.md) for [protons](proton.md) or [neutrons](neutron-split.md) in a [nuclear mean field](nuclear-mean-field.md). Filling all states below a sufficiently large gap gives a [nuclear shell closure](nuclear-shell-closure.md). These shells describe quantum-state occupation rather than thin spatial layers. Their discrete filling effects explain departures from the smooth [semi-empirical mass formula](semi-empirical-mass-formula.md).
