# Jones polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Kauffman bracket](kauffman-bracket.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jones_polynomial)

The Jones polynomial is obtained from the [Kauffman bracket](kauffman-bracket.md) of an oriented diagram by the writhe normalization

$$
V_L(t)=(-A^3)^{-w(D)}\langle D\rangle\big|_{t=A^{-4}}.
$$
