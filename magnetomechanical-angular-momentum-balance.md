# Magnetomechanical angular momentum balance

↑ **Parent:** [Einstein-de Haas effect](einstein-de-haas-effect.md)

Let $m_z=\int_V M_z\,dV$ be the axial [magnetic dipole moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md), where $M_z$ is the axial [magnetization](magnetization.md). Write $J_z$ for the internal [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) associated with that [magnetic dipole moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md), and $I\dot\theta$ for the body's rotational [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md), with [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) $I$. In a regime admitting a constant signed [gyromagnetic ratio](gyromagnetic-ratio.md) $m_z=\gamma J_z$, axial [conservation of angular momentum](conservation-of-angular-momentum.md) gives

$$
\frac{d}{dt}(I\dot\theta+J_z)=\tau_{\rm ext},
\qquad I\ddot\theta=\tau_{\rm ext}-\frac{\dot m_z}{\gamma},
\qquad \tau_{\rm EdH}=-\frac{\dot m_z}{\gamma}.
$$

Here $\tau_{\rm EdH}$ names the internal reaction [torque](torque.md) on the body, not an additional external [torque](torque.md). The external term includes the suspension. The model assumes that the changing internal [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) is transferred to the measured mechanical motion, with other storage or escape channels negligible on the observation timescale. A changing or non-collinear mixture of [orbital angular momentum](orbital-angular-momentum.md) and [spin angular momentum](spin.md) need not admit a single constant $\gamma$.

During a reversal fast compared with the mechanical response, with negligible external angular [impulse](impulse.md), integrating the balance gives

$$
\Delta L_{\rm body}=-\Delta J_z=-\frac{\Delta m_z}{\gamma}.
$$

For $m_z:+m_s\to-m_s$ and an electronic $\gamma<0$, the body acquires $\Delta L_{\rm body}=2m_s/\gamma<0$: its initial rotation is toward $-z$ by the [right-hand rule](right-hand-rule.md). Reversing the reversal reverses the impulse. Over a complete periodic cycle, $\Delta m_z=0$, so the net reaction angular [impulse](impulse.md) vanishes; a resonant alternating response does not imply steadily accumulating [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md).

The [orbital angular momentum](orbital-angular-momentum.md) prediction can be derived without a material model. A charge $q$ traveling around a circle of [radius](radius.md) $r$ at [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) $\omega$ has loop [electric current](electric-current.md) $q\omega/(2\pi)$ and [magnetic dipole moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md) $m=q\omega r^2/2$. Its [orbital angular momentum](orbital-angular-momentum.md) is $L=m_e\omega r^2$, hence, for an [electron](electron.md) with [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) $-e$,

$$
\mathbf m_L=-\frac{e}{2m_e}\mathbf L,
\qquad \gamma_L=-\frac{e}{2m_e}.
$$

The [electron magnetic moment](electron-magnetic-moment.md) associated with intrinsic [spin](spin.md) instead obeys

$$
\mathbf m_S=-g_s\frac{e}{2m_e}\mathbf S,
\qquad \gamma_S=-g_s\frac{e}{2m_e},\qquad g_s\simeq2.
$$

Here $e>0$ is the [elementary charge](elementary-charge.md), $m_e$ is the [electron mass](electron-mass.md), and $g_s$ is the [electron spin g-factor](electron-spin-g-factor.md).[pp. 299–300: orbital and intrinsic moments and the Dirac value](the-magnetic-moment-of-the-electron-by-polykarp-kusch.md) Thus the same change of [magnetic dipole moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md) corresponds to about half as much transferred [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) in the pure-[spin](spin.md) model as in the [orbital angular momentum](orbital-angular-momentum.md) model. This factor of two is a relation between [magnetic dipole moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md) and [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md), not evidence that an [electron](electron.md) is literally a rotating charged sphere. In a material with both contributions, the inferred coefficient is a material-dependent magnetomechanical [gyromagnetic ratio](gyromagnetic-ratio.md).
