# Frozen ortho-para mixture

↑ **Parent:** [Spin isomers of hydrogen](spin-isomers-of-hydrogen.md)

A mixture whose [orthohydrogen](orthohydrogen.md) and [parahydrogen](parahydrogen.md) mole fractions are fixed on the observation timescale, although each species can equilibrate over its own rotational levels. Its molar [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) is $x_oC_o+x_pC_p$. [normal hydrogen](normal-hydrogen.md) retained during cooling has $x_o=3/4$ and $x_p=1/4$. A mixture initially prepared at some other equilibrium composition can also be frozen; its subsequent heat response does not include a changing-composition term.
