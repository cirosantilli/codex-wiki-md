# Charged-particle radial equation in Reissner-Nordstrom spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [Reissner-Nordstrom spacetime](reissner-nordstrom-spacetime.md)

For a massive [test particle](test-particle.md) with [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) $e$, choose the [electromagnetic four-potential](electromagnetic-four-potential.md) $A_t=-Q/r$ and [proper time](proper-time.md) $\tau$. The [cyclic coordinates](cyclic-coordinate.md) $t,\phi$ in the equatorial [Lagrangian](lagrangian.md) give the [conserved quantities](conserved-quantity.md) $E=mF\dot t+eQ/r$ and $l=r^2\dot\phi$. Here $l$ is [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) per unit [mass](mass.md). Substituting these quantities into the [four-velocity](four-velocity.md) normalization $g(U,U)=-1$ gives

$$
\dot r^2=\frac{(E-eQ/r)^2}{m^2}-F\left(1+\frac{l^2}{r^2}\right).
$$

The [energy](energy-split.md) includes the gauge-dependent electrostatic term; the displayed convention fixes that gauge by taking the potential to vanish at infinity.
