# Accretion column

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetically channelled accretion](magnetically-channelled-accretion.md)

An [accretion column](accretion-column.md) carries an inward [accretion flow](accretion-flow.md) onto a localized region of a star. In the strong-field slender approximation, the [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) controls the [flux-tube area](flux-tube-area.md) while longitudinal pressure and gravity determine acceleration. The signed outward-coordinate velocity is negative; the inward speed and [mass accretion rate](mass-accretion-rate.md) are usually defined positive.
