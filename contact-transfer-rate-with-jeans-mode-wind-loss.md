# Contact transfer rate with Jeans-mode wind loss

↑ **Parent:** [Wind-driven Roche-lobe overflow](wind-driven-roche-lobe-overflow.md)

Let [Roche-lobe overflow](roche-lobe-overflow.md) transfer mass conservatively within a circular binary while a separate fast wind escapes with the donor's specific orbital [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md). Then $\dot M_d=\dot M-\dot M_a$ and $\dot a/a=-\dot M/M+2\dot M_a(1/M_d-1/M_a)$. Differentiating the donor and [Roche lobe](roche-lobe.md) radii and enforcing contact gives the displayed rate. It is positive and stabilizing when the wind drives overfill and $6q<5-3n$. When the latter inequality is reversed, transfer increases the overfill, producing runaway rather than a steady negative-transfer solution.
