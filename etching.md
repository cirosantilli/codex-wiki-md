# Etching

↑ **Parent:** [Photolithography](photolithography.md)

Controlled removal of a material by a chemical, plasma or physical process. [etch selectivity](etch-selectivity.md) compares removal rates for different materials; directionality compares removal rates along different geometric directions. High selectivity does not imply a vertical profile, and high directionality does not guarantee an undamaged surface.
