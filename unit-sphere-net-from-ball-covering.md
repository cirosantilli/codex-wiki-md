# Unit sphere net from ball covering

↑ **Parent:** [Metric net](metric-net.md)

A cover of the [unit ball](unit-ball.md) by balls of radius $\delta/2$ gives a radius-$\delta$ [metric net](metric-net.md) on the [unit sphere](unit-sphere.md): discard balls missing the sphere and choose a sphere point in each remaining ball. Every sphere point lies within $\delta$ of the selected point in its ball. The argument keeps the number of balls and ensures that the selected points have unit length, regardless of the initial covering centers.
