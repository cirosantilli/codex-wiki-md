# Identity by state

↑ **Parent:** [Identity by descent](identity-by-descent.md)

Two allele copies are identical by state when their observed states agree, regardless of whether they derive from one recent ancestral copy. Equal marker [haplotypes](haplotype.md) can therefore reflect either [identity by descent](identity-by-descent.md) or matching founder haplotypes.
