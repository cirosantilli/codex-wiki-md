# Closed-form expression

↑ **Parent:** [Expression (mathematics)](expression-mathematics.md)

A [mathematical expression](expression-mathematics.md) given by a finite combination of specified operations and standard [functions](function-split.md), rather than an unevaluated [recurrence relation](recurrence-relation.md) or an unbounded procedure. Which [functions](function-split.md) are admitted is part of the convention. For example, the [sum](sum.md) of the first $n$ [positive integers](positive-integer.md) has the [closed-form expression](closed-form-expression.md) $n(n+1)/2$.
