# Central element of a group

↑ **Parent:** [Center of a group](center-of-a-group.md)

An element of a [group](group-split.md) is central when it commutes with every element of that [group](group-split.md). The set of all such elements is the [center of a group](center-of-a-group.md). Equivalently, its [conjugacy class](conjugacy-class.md) is a singleton: $xgx^{-1}=g$ for every $x$ is exactly the commutation condition. This lets centrality identify one-element [conjugacy classes](conjugacy-class.md) without enumerating every [conjugation](conjugation.md).
