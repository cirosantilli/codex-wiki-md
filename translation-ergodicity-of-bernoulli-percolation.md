# Translation ergodicity of Bernoulli percolation

↑ **Parent:** [Bond percolation](bond-percolation-split.md)

The independent edge law of [bond percolation](bond-percolation-split.md) on $\mathbb Z^d$ is ergodic under lattice translations: every translation-invariant event has probability zero or one.
