# Generalized Bloch representation

↑ **Parent:** [Bloch vector](bloch-vector.md)

Choose a [Hilbert-Schmidt inner product](hilbert-schmidt-inner-product.md) orthonormal Hermitian operator basis with final element $I/\sqrt N$. A [density operator](density-matrix.md) has real coefficients $r_k=\operatorname{Tr}(\sigma_k\rho)$ and fixed final coefficient $1/\sqrt N$. The remaining coefficients form its generalized [Bloch vector](bloch-vector.md). Positivity restricts these real vectors to a compact convex body; for $N>2$ this body is not the entire Euclidean ball allowed by a purity bound.
