# Kinematic magnetic dynamo

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetohydrodynamics](magnetohydrodynamics.md)

A [kinematic magnetic dynamo](kinematic-magnetic-dynamo.md) studies [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) amplification by a prescribed [fluid flow](fluid-flow.md), neglecting the field's feedback on the velocity through the [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md). With incompressibility and zero [magnetic diffusivity](magnetic-diffusivity.md), a Lagrangian field vector evolves by its local velocity-gradient matrix. A growth law derived in this approximation cannot hold indefinitely once magnetic stresses significantly change the flow.
