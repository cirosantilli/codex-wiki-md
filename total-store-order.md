# Total store order

↑ **Parent:** [Memory model](memory-model.md)

An architectural [memory model](memory-model.md) preserving store-to-store and load-to-load ordering while allowing a later load to a different address to bypass a locally buffered store. This permits the two-zero store-buffering outcome while requiring no extra hardware fence for simple immutable pointer publication.
