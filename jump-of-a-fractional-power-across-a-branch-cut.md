# Jump of a fractional power across a branch cut

↑ **Parent:** [Branch cut](branch-cut.md)

For an entire function $f$, let $F(z)=f(z)z^{-\alpha}$ with noninteger real $\alpha$, and choose the [branch of the complex logarithm](branch-of-the-complex-logarithm.md) with $\phi<\arg z<\phi+2\pi$. On the cut ray $z=re^{i\phi}$, the bank whose argument approaches $\phi$ has value $F_+=f(re^{i\phi})r^{-\alpha}e^{-i\alpha\phi}$, while the other bank has argument approaching $\phi+2\pi$ and value $F_-=e^{-2\pi i\alpha}F_+$. The signed jump is therefore $(1-e^{-2\pi i\alpha})F_+$ when defined as $F_+-F_-$. Stating the branch and order of subtraction removes the sign ambiguity. A [keyhole contour](keyhole-contour.md) uses this phase relation to convert its two bank integrals into a constant multiple of one real or radial integral.
