# Mitsunobu reaction

↑ **Parent:** [Organic reaction](organic-reaction.md)

Substitution of an [alcohol](alcohol.md) using a phosphine and an azodicarboxylate reagent with a suitable acidic [nucleophile](nucleophile.md). Carboxylate displacement normally inverts a stereogenic [alcohol](alcohol.md) [carbon](carbon.md); subsequent [ester](ester.md) [hydrolysis](hydrolysis.md) can unveil the inverted [alcohol](alcohol.md).
