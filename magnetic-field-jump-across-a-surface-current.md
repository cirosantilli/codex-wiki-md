# Magnetic-field jump across a surface current

↑ **Parent:** [Current sheet](current-sheet.md)

For a surface current density $\mathbf K$ on an interface with unit normal $\mathbf n$, [Ampère's law](ampere-s-circuital-law.md) gives

$$
\mathbf n\times(\mathbf B_+-\mathbf B_-)=\frac{4\pi}{c}\mathbf K
$$

in Gaussian units, or $\mu_0\mathbf K$ in SI units.
