# Inclined porous gravity current

↑ **Parent:** [Porous gravity current](porous-gravity-current.md)

For a thin buoyant layer beneath an inclined impermeable roof, the [hydrostatic approximation](hydrostatic-approximation.md) and [Darcy's law](darcy-law.md) give $u=k g\Delta\rho\sin\theta/\mu$ and the [volume flux per unit width](volume-flux-per-unit-width.md) $F=uh-u\cot\theta\,hh_x$. The first term transports fluid upslope; the second spreads the layer through its hydrostatic pressure gradient. The [porosity](porosity.md) multiplies the stored fluid volume, so the advection speed is $u/\phi$, not the [Darcy velocity](darcy-velocity.md) $u$.
