# Burgers Riemann problem with negative flux

↑ **Parent:** [Inviscid Burgers equation](inviscid-burgers-equation.md)

For left state zero and right state $U$, negative-flux [Inviscid Burgers equation](inviscid-burgers-equation.md) gives a shock at $\theta=-UZ/2$ when $U>0$, and a [rarefaction wave](rarefaction-wave.md) $f=-\theta/Z$ on $0<\theta<-UZ$ when $U<0$. This is the standard positive-flux equation after setting $u=-f$; [entropy solution](entropy-solution.md) selection reverses which step direction is compressive.
