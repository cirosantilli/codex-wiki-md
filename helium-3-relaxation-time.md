# Helium-3 relaxation time

↑ **Parent:** [Helium-3 equilibrium abundance](helium-3-equilibrium-abundance.md)

A small abundance displacement from the [helium-3 equilibrium abundance](helium-3-equilibrium-abundance.md) obeys $\dot x=-x/\tau$ to linear order. Rapid relaxation relative to background evolution allows the abundance to track its equilibrium. If the equilibrium drifts, the displacement equation also contains $-\dot n_{3e}$. In the pp-I limit the rate is $n_1\sqrt{2\lambda_{11}\lambda_{33}}$, so both production and destruction [temperature](temperature.md) dependences matter.
