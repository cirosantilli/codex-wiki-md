# Sheafification by locally representable germs

↑ **Parent:** [Sheafification](sheafification.md)

For a [presheaf](presheaf-of-sets-on-a-topological-space.md), take families of elements of its [stalks](stalk-of-a-sheaf.md) which near each point are the [germs](germ-of-a-sheaf-section.md) of one [presheaf](presheaf-of-sets-on-a-topological-space.md) section. Pointwise operations and restriction make these families into a [sheaf](sheaf-mathematics.md). The map sending a section to all its [germs](germ-of-a-sheaf-section.md) is its [sheafification](sheafification.md) map.
