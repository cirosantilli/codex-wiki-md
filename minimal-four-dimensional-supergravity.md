# Minimal four-dimensional supergravity

↑ **Parent:** [Supergravity](supergravity.md)

Minimal four-dimensional [supergravity](supergravity.md) couples a graviton to one Majorana [gravitino](gravitino.md). In the normalization $\delta e_\mu{}^a=2\kappa\bar\epsilon\gamma^a\psi_\mu$, $\delta\psi_\mu=\nabla_\mu\epsilon/\kappa$, its [action](action.md) through quadratic [fermion](fermion.md) order is $\int e[R/(2\kappa^2)-2\bar\psi_\mu\gamma^{\mu\nu\rho}\nabla_\nu\psi_\rho]$. Rescaling the [gravitino](gravitino.md) and parameter by two gives the usual canonical kinetic coefficient $-1/2$. The [curvature](curvature.md) identity $\gamma^{\mu\nu\rho}\nabla_\nu\nabla_\rho\epsilon=G^{\mu\nu}\gamma_\nu\epsilon/2$ cancels the Einstein-Hilbert variation against the Rarita-Schwinger variation. The omitted cubic-fermion variations require the quartic-fermion completion.
