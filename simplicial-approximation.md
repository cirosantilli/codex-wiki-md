# Simplicial approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Simplicial map](simplicial-map.md)

A map satisfying this condition is the object whose existence after subdivision is guaranteed by the [Simplicial approximation theorem](simplicial-approximation-theorem.md).

A simplicial map $g:K\to L$ is a simplicial approximation to a continuous map $f:|K|\to|L|$ when

$$
f(\operatorname{st}_K(v))
\subseteq\operatorname{st}_L(g(v))
$$

for every vertex $v$ of $K$.
