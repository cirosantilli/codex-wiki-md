# Finitely generated subgroup of the rational additive group

↑ **Parent:** [Additive group](additive-group.md)

A nonzero finitely generated [subgroup](subgroup.md) of the additive [rational numbers](rational-number.md) is an [infinite cyclic group](infinite-cyclic-group.md). Put the generators over a common positive denominator. Their integer numerators generate the ideal given by their [greatest common divisor](greatest-common-divisor.md), and [Bezout identity](bezout-identity.md) expresses that divisor as an integer combination of them. Dividing back by the denominator gives a single generator.
