# Non-squeezing theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic manifold](symplectic-manifold.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-squeezing_theorem)

If there is a symplectic embedding

$$
B^{2n}(R)\hookrightarrow B^2(r)\times\mathbb R^{2n-2},
$$

then $R\leq r$. Symplectic volume alone does not imply this radius obstruction.
