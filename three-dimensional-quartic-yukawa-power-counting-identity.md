# Three-dimensional quartic Yukawa power-counting identity

↑ **Parent:** [Superficial degree of divergence](superficial-degree-of-divergence.md)

For a connected amputated [Feynman diagram](feynman-diagram.md) with the [quartic Yukawa interaction in three spacetime dimensions](quartic-yukawa-interaction-in-three-spacetime-dimensions.md), scalar quartic and scalar sextic vertices, $b$ external bosonic legs, $f$ external fermionic legs and $n_4$ scalar quartic vertices, the [superficial degree of divergence](superficial-degree-of-divergence.md) is the displayed expression. Indeed $\omega=3L-2I_b-I_f$, $2I_b+b=2n_Y+4n_4+6n_6$, $2I_f+f=2n_Y$ and $L=I_b+I_f-n_Y-n_4-n_6+1$. Substitution eliminates the counts of the two marginal vertices. The bound concerns overall [ultraviolet divergence](ultraviolet-divergence.md), before numerator cancellations and subtraction of [ultraviolet subdivergences](ultraviolet-subdivergence.md).
