# Burger number

↑ **Parent:** [Geophysical fluid dynamics](geophysical-fluid-dynamics-split.md)

The [Burger number](burger-number.md) compares the square of a relevant [deformation radius](deformation-radius.md) with the square of the horizontal flow scale: $\mathrm{Bu}=R_d^2/l^2$. In a two-layer geostrophic model $R_d^2=g\prime H_1H_2/[f_0^2(H_1+H_2)]$. Its inverse measures the scale ratio of baroclinic [available potential energy](available-potential-energy.md) to [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md). Keeping it of order unity retains both interface and relative-vorticity contributions in QG scaling.
