# Permutation order and sign do not determine conjugacy

↑ **Parent:** [Cycle type](cycle-type.md)

[Conjugation](conjugation.md) preserves [order of a group element](order-of-a-group-element.md), because $(gxg^{-1})^k=gx^kg^{-1}$, and preserves the [sign of a permutation](sign-of-a-permutation.md), because sign is a [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md) into $\{1,-1\}$. These invariants do not determine [cycle type](cycle-type.md). In $S_6$, $(123)$ and $(123)(456)$ both have order three and positive sign, but have three and zero fixed points respectively. Conjugation relabels cycles, so these [permutations](permutation.md) are not conjugate.
