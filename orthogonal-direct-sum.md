# Orthogonal direct sum

↑ **Parent:** [Direct sum](direct-sum.md)

An orthogonal direct sum is a [direct sum](direct-sum.md) whose distinct summands are mutually [orthogonal](orthogonal-vectors.md). For $v\in V$ and $w\in W$, the [Pythagorean identity](pythagorean-theorem-in-an-inner-product-space.md) gives $\|v+w\|^2=\|v\|^2+\|w\|^2$. In a [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md), a [closed subspace of a Hilbert space](closed-subspace-of-a-hilbert-space.md) and its [orthogonal complement](orthogonal-complement.md) give an orthogonal direct sum of the entire space. The component maps are [orthogonal projections](orthogonal-projection.md) and have [operator norm](operator-norm.md) one when their ranges are nonzero.
