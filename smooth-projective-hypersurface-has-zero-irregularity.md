# Smooth projective hypersurface has zero irregularity

↑ **Parent:** [Irregularity of an algebraic surface](irregularity-of-an-algebraic-surface.md)

A smooth surface hypersurface $X_d\subseteq\mathbb P^3$ has $q(X_d)=0$. This follows from the [structure-sheaf sequence of a hypersurface](structure-sheaf-sequence-of-a-hypersurface.md) and the vanishing of the intermediate cohomology of line bundles on $\mathbb P^3$.
