# Contact-point velocity

↑ **Parent:** [Rigid body dynamics](rigid-body-dynamics-split.md)

The instantaneous [velocity](velocity.md) of the material point of a [rigid body](rigid-body-dynamics-split.md) touching another body. In the displayed formula, $\mathbf r_P$ is its position relative to the [centre of mass](center-of-mass.md), and $\boldsymbol\Omega$ is the body's [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md). The rotational term is a [cross product](cross-product.md). This material-point [velocity](velocity.md) differs from the motion of the geometrical contact location: in [rolling without slipping](rolling-without-slipping.md) on a stationary plane it vanishes even while the contact location advances. For a sphere touching a horizontal plane, with rightward centre [velocity](velocity.md) $v$ and anticlockwise spin $\Omega$, its tangential component is $v+a\Omega$.
