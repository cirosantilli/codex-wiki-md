# Synchronous photon brightness equation

↑ **Parent:** [Photon brightness perturbation](photon-brightness-perturbation.md)

For collisionless photons in [synchronous gauge](synchronous-gauge-in-cosmology.md), their perturbed momentum obeys $q'=-qh'_{ij}n^in^j/2$. Terms in $q'\partial_qf_1$ and direction deflection times angular derivatives of $f_1$ are second order. Integrating the remaining linear [Collisionless Boltzmann equation](collisionless-boltzmann-equation.md) over $q^3dq$ gives the displayed equation, because $\int q^4f_0' dq=-4\int q^3f_0dq$.
