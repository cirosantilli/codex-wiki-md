# Cold neutral medium

↑ **Parent:** [Multiphase interstellar medium](multiphase-interstellar-medium.md)

The cold predominantly atomic neutral phase of interstellar gas, with characteristic [temperatures](temperature-split.md) of order $10^2\ \mathrm K$. It is denser than the warm neutral phase at comparable thermal [pressure](pressure-split.md). Its existence depends on radiation, grain heating and atomic-line cooling; one universal [density](density.md) or [temperature](temperature-split.md) does not define every environment.
