<h1 id="mobius-group">Möbius group</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Projective linear group](projective-linear-group.md)

The Möbius group is the [group](group-split.md) of all [Möbius transformations](mobius-transformation.md) of the [Riemann sphere](riemann-sphere.md), under [function composition](function-composition.md). An invertible $2\times2$ complex matrix represents $z\mapsto(az+b)/(cz+d)$, and nonzero scalar multiples represent the same transformation. Rescaling a representative to determinant one gives a surjective [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md) $SL_2(\mathbb C)\to\mathcal M$ with [kernel of a group homomorphism](kernel-of-a-group-homomorphism.md) $\{I,-I\}$. Thus it is also the [projective linear group](projective-linear-group.md) $PGL_2(\mathbb C)$ and the quotient $PSL_2(\mathbb C)=SL_2(\mathbb C)/\{\pm I\}$.
