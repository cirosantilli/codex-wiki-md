# Homotopy pushout

↑ **Parent:** [Homotopy category](homotopy-category.md)

For maps $A\to X$ and $A\to Y$, the double [mapping cylinder](mapping-cylinder.md) $X\sqcup(A\times I)\sqcup Y$, with each endpoint attached by its given map, models the homotopy pushout. A map out of this model is a pair of maps on $X$ and $Y$ together with a [homotopy](homotopy.md) between their restrictions to $A$. This is why compatible [homotopy classes](homotopy-class.md) extend over a homotopy pushout. For a [CW pair](cw-pair.md), the inclusion is a [cofibration](cofibration.md) and the ordinary pushout gives the same homotopy type.
