# Dislocation creep

↑ **Parent:** [Creep (deformation)](creep-deformation.md)

[Dislocation creep](dislocation-creep.md) is time-dependent crystal deformation through motion and interactions of dislocations. A common constitutive model is $\dot\epsilon=A\sigma^n\exp[-(E^*+PV^*)/(RT)]$ with $n>1$, giving stress-dependent effective [viscosity](dynamic-viscosity-split.md). Unlike [diffusion creep](diffusion-creep.md), it often has weak grain-size dependence over the regime being modelled.
