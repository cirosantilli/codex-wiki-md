# London equations

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic field](magnetic-field.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_equations)

The London equations relate the superconducting [electric current](electric-current.md) to the [electromagnetic field](electromagnetic-field.md): $\partial_t\mathbf J=\mathbf E/(\mu_0\lambda_L^2)$ and $\nabla\times\mathbf J=-\mathbf B/(\mu_0\lambda_L^2)$. In a gauge with constant condensate phase the second follows from $\mathbf J=-\mathbf A/(\mu_0\lambda_L^2)$; it implies exponential [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) screening.
