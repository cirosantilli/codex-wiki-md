# Hamiltonian flow preserves the symplectic form

↑ **Parent:** [Hamiltonian vector field](hamiltonian-vector-field.md)

If $\iota_{X_{H_t}}\omega=-dH_t$ and $\phi_t$ is its flow, then [Cartan's magic formula](cartan-s-magic-formula.md) gives

$$
\frac d{dt}\phi_t^*\omega
=\phi_t^*\mathcal L_{X_{H_t}}\omega
=\phi_t^*d\iota_{X_{H_t}}\omega=0.
$$

Thus every defined time slice of a [Hamiltonian isotopy](hamiltonian-isotopy.md) is a [symplectomorphism](symplectomorphism.md).
