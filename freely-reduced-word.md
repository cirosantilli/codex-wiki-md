# Freely reduced word

↑ **Parent:** [Free group](free-group.md)

A word in generators and their formal inverses is freely reduced if no consecutive letters are mutual inverses. Deleting such inverse pairs gives the unique reduced representative in a [free group](free-group.md). In a general [group presentation](group-presentation.md), [free reduction](free-reduction.md) preserves the represented group element but may not produce a shortest representative.
