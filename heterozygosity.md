# Heterozygosity

↑ **Parent:** [Genotype](genotype.md)

[Heterozygosity](heterozygosity.md) means that the two [allele](allele.md) states at a diploid [genetic locus](genetic-locus.md) differ; an individual with that property is a [heterozygote](heterozygote.md). [Heterozygous](heterozygosity.md) parental [genotypes](genotype.md) provide alternative [alleles](allele.md) for a [transmission disequilibrium test](transmission-disequilibrium-test.md).
