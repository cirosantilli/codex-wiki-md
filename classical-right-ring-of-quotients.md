# Classical right ring of quotients

↑ **Parent:** [Right Ore condition](right-ore-condition.md)

The classical right ring of quotients inverts all [regular elements of a ring](regular-element-of-a-ring.md). Existence requires the [right Ore condition](right-ore-condition.md) for these denominators. The original [ring](ring.md) embeds, every regular element becomes a [unit](unit-in-a-ring.md), and every fraction has a right denominator. The [right Ore condition](right-ore-condition.md) also permits finitely many fractions to be placed over a common right denominator.
