# Komar current from trace-reversed stress-energy

↑ **Parent:** [Komar charge](komar-charge.md)

A [Killing vector field](killing-vector-field.md) gives an antisymmetric tensor $\nabla^\mu\xi^\nu$ whose divergence is this current, up to overall surface-orientation convention. The four-dimensional [Einstein field equations](einstein-field-equations.md) express it as $8\pi G(T^\mu{}_\nu-\tfrac12T\delta^\mu{}_\nu)\xi^\nu$ at zero cosmological constant. The [contracted Bianchi identity](contracted-bianchi-identity.md) and Killing invariance of [scalar curvature](scalar-curvature.md) prove its divergence is zero. Stress conservation alone does not cancel the trace derivative without Killing invariance of the trace.
