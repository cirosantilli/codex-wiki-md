# Edge-hinged cube with a central spherical cavity

↑ **Parent:** [Physical pendulum](physical-pendulum.md)

For a uniform cube of [mass](mass.md) $M$ and side $a$ hinged along an edge, the centre is $a/\sqrt2$ from the hinge and $I_h=2Ma^2/3$. Removing a concentric [sphere](sphere.md) of [radius](radius.md) $a/2$ removes [mass](mass.md) $\pi M/6$ and hinge inertia $\pi Ma^2/10$, without shifting the centre. Combining the reduced weight and reduced inertia in $\omega^2=Mgd/I_h$ proves the displayed [frequency](frequency.md) ratio. Updating only the inertia would miss the change of gravitational restoring [torque](torque.md).
