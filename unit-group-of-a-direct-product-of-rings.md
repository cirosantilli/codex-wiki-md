# Unit group of a direct product of rings

↑ **Parent:** [Direct product of rings](direct-product-of-rings.md)

A tuple in a [direct product of rings](direct-product-of-rings.md) is a [unit](unit-in-a-ring.md) exactly when each coordinate is a [unit](unit-in-a-ring.md). Indeed, multiplying by an inverse tuple gives an inverse in each coordinate; conversely, the coordinate inverses form an inverse tuple. This identifies the [unit group](unit-group.md) with the [direct product of groups](direct-product-of-groups.md) of the individual [unit groups](unit-group.md), for finite or infinite families of unital [rings](ring.md).
