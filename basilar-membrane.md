# Basilar membrane

↑ **Parent:** [Cochlea](cochlea.md)

A mechanically graded structure supporting frequency-dependent traveling-wave responses in the [cochlea](cochlea.md). High-frequency responses peak nearer the base and low-frequency responses nearer the apex. Its living response is modified by the [cochlear amplifier](cochlear-amplifier.md), so passive mechanical measurements do not capture the entire operating system.
