# Measurable partition

↑ **Parent:** [Measure theory](measure-theory-split.md)

A countable measurable partition is a countable family of pairwise disjoint measurable sets whose union has full measure. Its members are its atoms; null atoms can be discarded in statements made [almost everywhere](almost-everywhere.md).
