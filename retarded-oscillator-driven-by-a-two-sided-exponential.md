# Retarded oscillator driven by a two-sided exponential

↑ **Parent:** [Retarded unit-frequency oscillator response](retarded-unit-frequency-oscillator-response.md)

For $g(t)=\alpha e^{-\alpha|t|}/2$ with $\alpha>0$, convolution gives

$$
Q_R(t)=\frac{\alpha}{2(1+\alpha^2)}
\begin{cases}e^{\alpha t},&t<0,\\e^{-\alpha t}+2\alpha\sin t,&t\ge0.\end{cases}
$$

The coordinate and velocity are continuous at zero. At late times the undamped oscillation has amplitude $\alpha^2/(1+\alpha^2)$; [causality](causality.md) fixes the [homogeneous solution](homogeneous-solution.md) only after the no-incoming-oscillation condition is specified.
