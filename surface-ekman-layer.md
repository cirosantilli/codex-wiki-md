# Surface Ekman layer

↑ **Parent:** [Ekman layer](ekman-layer.md)

A surface [Ekman layer](ekman-layer.md) is the stress-driven boundary correction below a rotating fluid’s upper boundary. For upward $z\le0$, $f>0$, constant [kinematic viscosity](kinematic-viscosity.md) and imposed complex [wind stress](wind-stress.md) $T$, its steady velocity is $W_E=T e^{\lambda z}/(\rho\nu\lambda)$, $\lambda=(1+i)/\delta_E$. The underlying [geostrophic flow](geostrophic-flow.md) is independent of $T$ unless an additional boundary velocity condition couples them.
