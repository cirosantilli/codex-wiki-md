# Flux through a spherical quadrant by planar caps

↑ **Parent:** [Divergence theorem](divergence-theorem.md)

For the outward spherical patch $r=R$, $y\leq0$, $z\leq0$, and the polynomial [vector field](vector-field.md) $F=(yx^2,xy^2-3z^4,x^3+y^2)$, close the quarter-ball with its two planar half-discs. The [divergence](divergence.md) is $4xy$, whose [volume integral](volume-integral.md) vanishes by symmetry in $x$. The caps have [fluxes](flux.md) $-3\pi R^6/16$ and $\pi R^4/8$, obtained from half-disc fourth and second moments. The [divergence theorem](divergence-theorem.md) gives the displayed spherical [flux](flux.md). All pieces use normals pointing out of the same closed volume; the caps are auxiliary and must be subtracted.
