<h1 id="auslander-reiten-quiver">Auslander–Reiten quiver</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Module theory](module-theory-split.md)

For a finite-dimensional [algebra](algebra-split.md), the Auslander–Reiten quiver has one vertex for each isomorphism class of finite-dimensional [indecomposable modules](indecomposable-module.md), and its arrows record irreducible module homomorphisms. A homomorphism is irreducible when it is neither a split injection nor a split surjection and every factorization forces the first map to split injectively or the second to split surjectively.
