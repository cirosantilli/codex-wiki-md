# Radio astronomy

↑ **Parent:** [Observational astronomy](observational-astronomy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radio_astronomy)

Radio astronomy studies celestial sources through their [radio waves](radio-wave.md). The long [wavelengths](wavelength.md) make [angular resolution](angular-resolution.md) expensive: a filled telescope of diameter $D$ has a characteristic [diffraction](diffraction.md) scale of order $\lambda/D$. [Aperture synthesis](aperture-synthesis.md) instead combines measurements from separated [antennas](antenna-radio.md), using their separations to measure fine structure while their individual collecting areas determine how much radiation they receive. A kilometre-wide arrangement of small [antennas](antenna-radio.md) does not collect the power intercepted by a filled kilometre-wide dish.
