# Poissonization

↑ **Parent:** [Probability theory](probability-theory-split.md)

Poissonization replaces a fixed number of independent trials by a [Poisson process](poisson-process-split.md) clock. Independent exponential arrival clocks can then simplify occupancy and collection problems, after which the original counting quantity is recovered from the clock rate.
