# Particle ellipses for rotating shallow-water waves

↑ **Parent:** [Inertia-gravity wave](inertia-gravity-wave.md)

For a plane [inertia-gravity wave](inertia-gravity-wave.md) on a resting layer, $\eta=\eta_0\cos(kx-\omega t)$, the horizontal velocities are $u=U\cos\theta$ and $v=V\sin\theta$, where $U=\omega\eta_0/(kh_0)$ and $V=f\eta_0/(kh_0)$. First-order integration at a fixed equilibrium coordinate gives $\xi=-U\sin\theta/\omega$, $\upsilon=V\cos\theta/\omega$. The particle trajectory is an [ellipse](ellipse.md) with axis ratio $|f|/\omega$. For positive $f$, the orbit is clockwise in the $(x,y)$ plane.
