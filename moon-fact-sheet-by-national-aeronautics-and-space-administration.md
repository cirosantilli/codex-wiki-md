# Moon Fact Sheet by National Aeronautics and Space Administration

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by National Aeronautics and Space Administration](work-by-national-aeronautics-and-space-administration.md)

[https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/moonfact.html](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/moonfact.html)

The bulk-parameter table gives the Moon's volumetric mean [radius](radius.md) as 1737.4 km and solar [irradiance](irradiance.md) as $1361\,\mathrm{W\,m^{-2}}$. It separately lists [Bond albedo](bond-albedo.md) 0.11 and [geometric albedo](geometric-albedo.md) 0.12; these are different measures of reflection.
