# Pressure wave propagation

↑ **Parent:** [Wave propagation](wave-propagation.md)

A changing [pressure](pressure-split.md) disturbance travels through a material with finite speed. This transient response is distinct from the eventual equilibrium balance of [hydrostatic pressure](hydrostatic-pressure.md). In an elastic liquid-filled tube the wall and liquid jointly determine the propagation speed.
