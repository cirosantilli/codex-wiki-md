# Digital optical module

↑ **Parent:** [IceCube Neutrino Observatory](icecube-neutrino-observatory.md)

A glass-enclosed [photomultiplier](photomultiplier.md) with local digitization: pulse times and charges enter event reconstruction. Reciprocal electrical pulses synchronize module clocks to the surface time base.[Sections 2.1, 3.3: module and reciprocal timing](the-icecube-neutrino-observatory-instrumentation-and-online-systems-by-icecube-collaboration.md)

[Weak charged current](charged-current.md) interactions of [muon neutrinos](muon-neutrino.md) usually give extended [muon](muon.md) tracks. Compact [particle showers](particle-shower.md) occur in other channels, including [weak neutral current](neutral-current.md) interactions of every [neutrino flavor](neutrino-flavor.md). Tracks generally constrain direction better; escaping [particles](particle-physics.md) complicate [energy](energy-split.md) inference.[Section 1.1.1: tracks and showers](the-icecube-neutrino-observatory-instrumentation-and-online-systems-by-icecube-collaboration.md)
