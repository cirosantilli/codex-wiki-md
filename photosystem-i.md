# Photosystem I

↑ **Parent:** [Photosystem](photosystem.md)

[Photosystem I](photosystem-i.md) is a light-driven reaction-centre complex in oxygenic photosynthesis. Excitation supports electron transfer to acceptors including ferredoxin; the balance between linear and cyclic routes affects reductant and ATP supply.
