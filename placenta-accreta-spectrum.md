# Placenta accreta spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Obstetrics](obstetrics.md)

Abnormal adherence of the [placenta](placenta.md) to, or invasion into, the [myometrium](myometrium.md), associated with deficiency of the normal intervening [decidua](decidua.md). Attempted forced separation may cause severe [haemorrhage](haemorrhage.md).
