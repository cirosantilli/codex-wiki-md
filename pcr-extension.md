# PCR extension

↑ **Parent:** [PCR thermal cycle](pcr-thermal-cycle.md)

[PCR extension](pcr-extension.md) is template-directed construction of [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) from an annealed [PCR primer](pcr-primer.md) by a [DNA polymerase](dna-polymerase.md). Incoming [deoxynucleoside triphosphates](deoxynucleoside-triphosphate.md) supply the new [nucleotides](nucleotide.md). Each addition lengthens the growing strand at its $3'$ end, so the product grows $5'\to3'$ while the template is read $3'\to5'$. The direction refers to the [nucleic acid](nucleic-acid.md) backbone, not to left or right on a page.[Description, final paragraph: extension of annealed primers by dNTP incorporation](principles-of-pcr-by-thermo-fisher-scientific.md)

**[Figure 9](#image-pcr-extension-follows-the-paired-3-end-on-either-template-orientation). PCR extension follows the paired $3'$ end on either template orientation**. Original eight-position [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) example with separated complementary templates. Dark rails are existing templates; coloured rails are new strands. The blue [PCR primer](pcr-primer.md) grows rightward and the green [PCR primer](pcr-primer.md) grows leftward. Orange marks the material added by [DNA polymerase](dna-polymerase.md); both arrows point from the original paired $3'$ end toward the new $3'$ end. Labels at each end show that both products grow $5'\to3'$. The templates stop at the displayed boundaries for this teaching example; real starting templates can extend farther. Four-[nucleotide](nucleotide.md) [PCR primers](pcr-primer.md) are deliberately too short to constitute an experimental design.

For a complete worked copying step, start with template $3'\text{-}\mathrm{TCAGGGAC}\text{-}5'$ and paired [PCR primer](pcr-primer.md) $5'\text{-}\mathrm{AGTC}\text{-}3'$. The next four template letters are GGAC, encountered in the template's $3'$-to-$5'$ direction. The [base pairing](base-pairing.md) rule therefore selects CCTG in order, giving

$$
5'\text{-}\underbrace{\mathrm{AGTC}}_{\text{primer}}\underbrace{\mathrm{CCTG}}_{\text{extension}}\text{-}3'.
$$

The [PCR primer](pcr-primer.md) supplies four [nucleotides](nucleotide.md), and [PCR extension](pcr-extension.md) adds four more; this copying event consumes four [deoxynucleoside triphosphates](deoxynucleoside-triphosphate.md). On the oppositely oriented template $5'\text{-}\mathrm{AGTCCCTG}\text{-}3'$, the other [PCR primer](pcr-primer.md) is $5'\text{-}\mathrm{CAGG}\text{-}3'$. It pairs at the right and extends leftward. Reading that template from right to left encounters CTGA after the paired region, so addition of GACT gives $5'\text{-}\mathrm{CAGGGACT}\text{-}3'$. Both finished products are the [reverse complements](reverse-complement.md) of their respective templates.

The direction argument is short: the [PCR primer](pcr-primer.md) and its template are [antiparallel nucleic acid strands](antiparallel-nucleic-acid-strands.md); extension takes place at the [PCR primer](pcr-primer.md)'s $3'$ end; hence forward progress along the new strand is backward progress along the template. Reflecting a drawing reverses its arrows on the page without changing either chemical direction. This is why a leftward arrow can represent $5'\to3'$ synthesis.

The time needed depends on how much [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) remains to be copied and the chosen [DNA polymerase](dna-polymerase.md), while [temperature](temperature-split.md) must support that [enzyme](enzyme-split.md)'s activity.[Primer extension considerations, paragraphs 1 and 3: enzyme activity, synthesis rate and target length](pcr-cycling-parameters-six-key-considerations-for-success-by-thermo-fisher-scientific.md) Separating [PCR annealing](pcr-annealing.md) and [PCR extension](pcr-extension.md) into different holds is convenient, but some compatible conditions allow a combined hold. The three chemical jobs therefore need not always require three distinct [temperatures](temperature-split.md).[Description, paragraphs 2–3: enzyme-dependent extension temperature and two-step cycling](principles-of-pcr-by-thermo-fisher-scientific.md)
