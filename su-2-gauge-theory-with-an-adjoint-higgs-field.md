<h1 id="su-2-gauge-theory-with-an-adjoint-higgs-field">SU(2) gauge theory with an adjoint Higgs field</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [SU(2) gauge theory](su-2-gauge-theory.md)

A real [scalar field](scalar-field.md) in the [Adjoint representation](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md) can have a nonzero [vacuum expectation value](vacuum-expectation-value.md), leaving one massless and two massive [gauge bosons](gauge-boson.md). This breaking differs from [electroweak symmetry breaking](electroweak-symmetry-breaking.md) by a complex [Higgs doublet](higgs-field.md).
