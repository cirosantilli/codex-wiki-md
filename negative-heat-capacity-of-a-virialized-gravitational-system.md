# Negative heat capacity of a virialized gravitational system

↑ **Parent:** [Virial theorem](virial-theorem.md)

An isolated Newtonian system in [virial equilibrium](virial-equilibrium.md) has total [energy](energy-split.md) $E=K+W=-K$. Along an equilibrium sequence at fixed particle number, define the kinetic [temperature](temperature-split.md) by $K=3Nk_B\Theta/2$. Its [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) is therefore negative, as displayed: losing total energy increases the kinetic temperature. A [collisionless stellar system](collisionless-stellar-system.md) need not have a thermodynamic temperature; this is a kinetic-temperature statement unless thermal equilibrium is additionally specified. Boundary pressure and external confinement can change the energy relation.
