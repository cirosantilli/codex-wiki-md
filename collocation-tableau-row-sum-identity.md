# Collocation tableau row-sum identity

↑ **Parent:** [Collocation Runge-Kutta method](collocation-runge-kutta-method.md)

For a [collocation Runge-Kutta method](collocation-runge-kutta-method.md), $a_{ij}=\int_0^{c_i}\ell_j(t)dt$, where the [Lagrange interpolation](lagrange-polynomial.md) basis obeys $\sum_j\ell_j=1$. Hence each row sum is its collocation node. Failure of this identity disproves a claimed collocation tableau with those nodes. The identity alone is necessary rather than sufficient: the individual integrated basis coefficients must also agree.
