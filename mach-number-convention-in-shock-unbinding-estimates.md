# Mach-number convention in shock unbinding estimates

↑ **Parent:** [Planar shock compression of a finite slab](planar-shock-compression-of-a-finite-slab.md)

For a strong monatomic shock, the [planar shock compression of a finite slab](planar-shock-compression-of-a-finite-slab.md) gives bulk speed $3V_s/4$ and kinetic energy $E_{\rm bulk}=9M_cV_s^2/32$. The initial [internal energy](internal-energy-split.md) is $E_{\rm th}=3M_cp/(2\rho)$. A bulk-ballistic escape estimate $E_{\rm bulk}\gtrsim W$ uses positive binding-energy magnitude $W$, not negative gravitational potential energy. With [adiabatic sound speed](adiabatic-sound-speed.md) $c_{\rm ad}^2=5p/(3\rho)$ it yields the first displayed coefficient. Using the [isothermal sound speed](isothermal-sound-speed.md) $c_T^2=p/\rho$ instead yields $16/3\simeq5.3$. The definitions obey $\mathcal M_T^2=(5/3)\mathcal M_{\rm ad}^2$. Heating, subsequent expansion and energy loss can alter the physical escape threshold, so the coefficient is not independent of the energy budget and Mach convention.
