# Spectral reconstruction of an attenuated Radon transform

↑ **Parent:** [Attenuated Radon transform](attenuated-radon-transform.md)

Complexifying the directional [transport equation](transport-equation.md) yields spectral solutions analytic inside and outside the unit circle. An [integrating factor](integrating-factor.md) built from known attenuation removes its multiplication term. The limiting Green kernels encode weighted line measurements and transverse [Hilbert transforms](hilbert-transform.md). Their jump gives a [Riemann-Hilbert problem](riemann-hilbert-problem.md); its solution recovers the source from an asymptotic coefficient. A general primary treatment of this spectral construction is [https://www.stat.uchicago.edu/~guillaumebal/PAPERS/AttRadTh.pdf](https://www.stat.uchicago.edu/~guillaumebal/PAPERS/AttRadTh.pdf) .
