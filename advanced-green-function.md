# Advanced Green function

↑ **Parent:** [Green's function](green-s-function-split.md)

An [advanced Green function](advanced-green-function.md) has support only before its source time. It is selected by a future [boundary condition](boundary-condition.md), rather than an initial causal response. For $L=\partial_t^2+2\lambda\partial_t+\omega_0^2$ with $\lambda<0$ and $\Omega^2=\omega_0^2-\lambda^2>0$, the real-axis inverse [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md) of $(-\omega^2-2i\lambda\omega+\omega_0^2)^{-1}$ is $-H(-t)e^{-\lambda t}\sin(\Omega t)/\Omega$. Its first derivative jumps by one at zero. A growing retarded solution exists with a different inverse [contour](complex-integration-contour.md); this illustrates [causality versus stability of a Green function](causality-versus-stability-of-a-green-function.md).
