# Vaporization

↑ **Parent:** [Phase transition](phase-transition.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vaporization)

[Vaporization](vaporization.md) changes a [liquid](liquid.md) into a [gas](gas.md). It includes [evaporation](evaporation.md) at a surface and [boiling](boiling.md) within the liquid. Below the [liquid-gas critical point](liquid-gas-critical-point.md), equilibrium conversion along the liquid-vapour [phase coexistence curve](phase-coexistence-curve.md) involves [latent heat](latent-heat.md); the liquid and vapour have equal [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) there.
