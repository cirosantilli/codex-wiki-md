# BCR-ABL

↑ **Parent:** [Protein kinase](protein-kinase.md)

An oncogenic fusion protein with deregulated tyrosine-kinase activity, classically associated with chronic myeloid leukaemia. Its signalling can be inhibited by drugs including [imatinib](imatinib.md).
