# Logarithmic-regime giant component

↑ **Parent:** [Giant component](giant-component.md)

Fix $k\geq1$, $\gamma_0>1/(k+1)$, and $\gamma_0\leq\gamma\leq1-\omega(n)/\log n$ with $\omega(n)\to\infty$. A [binomial random graph](binomial-random-graph.md) has one [giant component](giant-component.md) of the displayed order and all other [graph components](component-graph-theory.md) are [tree components](tree-component.md) of orders at most $k$, [with high probability](with-high-probability.md). The [expected value](expected-value.md) and [variance](variance-split.md) of the [isolated vertex](isolated-vertex.md) count concentrate it around $n^{1-\gamma}\to\infty$. A [Cayley formula](cayley-s-formula.md) upper bound for connected sets excludes component sizes $k+1$ through a small fixed fraction of $n$; the empty-[graph cut](graph-cut.md) bound excludes the remaining sizes up to $n/2$. An extra-[edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) count excludes cyclic small [graph components](component-graph-theory.md). The [expected value](expected-value.md) of the number of [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) in small nonisolated [tree components](tree-component.md) is $O(n d e^{-2d})=o(n e^{-d})$, where $d=np$. The [Markov inequality](markov-inequality.md) completes the count of [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) outside the unique large [graph component](component-graph-theory.md).
