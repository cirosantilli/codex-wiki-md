<h1 id="expected-subgraph-count-in-the-erdos-renyi-model">Expected subgraph count in the Erdős-Rényi model</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Erdős-Rényi model](erdos-renyi-model.md)

If a fixed graph $H$ has $v$ vertices, $e$ edges, and $N_H(n)$ unlabelled copies in $K_n$, then the number $X_H$ of its copies in $G(n,p)$ satisfies

$$
\mathbb E X_H=N_H(n)p^e.
$$

This follows by writing $X_H$ as a sum of [indicator random variables](indicator-random-variable.md).
