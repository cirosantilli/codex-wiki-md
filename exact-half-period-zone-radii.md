# Exact half-period zone radii

↑ **Parent:** [Zone plate](zone-plate.md)

For a [plane wave](plane-wave.md) incident on a flat [zone plate](zone-plate.md), the path difference from [radius](radius.md) $r_n$ to axial focal distance $f$ is $\sqrt{f^2+r_n^2}-f$. Setting it equal to $n\lambda/2$ gives the title relation. The paraxial limit is $r_n\simeq\sqrt{nf\lambda}$, and the fine outer-zone width is approximately $f\lambda/(2r_n)$. Alternately removing half-period annuli makes the remaining contributions reinforce at the principal focus. Choosing the number of zones sets the aperture and fabrication scale, not the principal focal distance independently of these [radii](radius.md).
