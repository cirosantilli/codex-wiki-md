# Inversion centre

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetry (physics)](symmetry-physics.md)

A point such that replacing every position $\mathbf r$ relative to it by $-\mathbf r$ leaves the structure unchanged. Molecular orbitals can then be classified as $g$ or $u$ according to their [parity](parity.md).
