# Known-rate Laplace location confidence interval

↑ **Parent:** [Laplace distribution](laplace-distribution.md)

For an [independent and identically distributed](independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md) [Laplace distribution](laplace-distribution.md) sample with density $\lambda e^{-\lambda|x-\mu|}/2$ and known rate $\lambda>0$, the [sample mean](sample-mean.md) has [mean](expected-value-split.md) $\mu$ and [variance](variance-split.md) $2/(n\lambda^2)$. The [central limit theorem](central-limit-theorem.md) gives the displayed asymptotic [confidence interval](confidence-interval.md) for $\mu$ at confidence level $1-\alpha$. The [sample mean](sample-mean.md) need not be a [maximum-likelihood estimator](maximum-likelihood-estimator.md) for this construction to be valid. The [Laplace location and scale maximum likelihood](laplace-location-and-scale-maximum-likelihood.md) location is a [sample median](sample-median.md); that is a different choice of statistic.
