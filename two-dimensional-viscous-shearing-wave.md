# Two-dimensional viscous shearing wave

↑ **Parent:** [Exact incompressible shearing wave](exact-incompressible-shearing-wave.md)

For $k_z=v_z=0$ and $k_y\ne0$, define $Z=i(k_xv_y-k_yv_x)$. Uniform rotation does not enter its evolution: $\dot Z=-\nu k^2Z$, $v_x=ik_yZ/k^2$ and $v_y=-ik_xZ/k^2$. With $T=k_x/k_y$ and $\mathrm{Re}=A/(\nu k_y^2)$, the perturbation [energy](energy.md) ratio is $E(T)/E(T_0)=[(1+T_0^2)/(1+T^2)]\exp[-(T+T^3/3-T_0-T_0^3/3)/\mathrm{Re}]$. Leading waves can gain [energy](energy.md) as shear reduces their [wavevector](wavevector.md) magnitude, before [viscosity](dynamic-viscosity.md) and subsequent winding damp them.
