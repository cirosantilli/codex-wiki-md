# Critical Casimir force

↑ **Parent:** [Fluctuation-induced force](fluctuation-induced-force.md)

Confining a near-critical fluctuating order parameter changes its [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md). Its range is governed by the growing [correlation length](correlation-length.md), and its sign depends on the boundary preferences. This is a thermal collective effect, distinct from the zero-[temperature](temperature-split.md) ideal-conductor electromagnetic formula.
