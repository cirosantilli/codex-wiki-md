# Small-angle approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Trigonometric function](trigonometric-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Small-angle_approximation)

As $x\to0$, the [limit](limit-of-a-function.md) $\sin x/x\to1$ gives $\sin x\sim x$, while the [Taylor series](taylor-series.md) gives $\arcsin x\sim x$ and $\cos x\sim1-x^2/2$.
