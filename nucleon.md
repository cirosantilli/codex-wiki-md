# Nucleon

↑ **Parent:** [Baryon](baryon.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nucleon)

The proton and neutron form the nucleon [isospin](isospin.md) doublet, with [spin angular momentum](spin.md) $1/2$ and [baryon number](baryon-number.md) one. A unit [Skyrmion](skyrmion.md) with the fermionic [Finkelstein-Rubinstein constraints](finkelstein-rubinstein-constraints.md) gives these quantum numbers in its lowest rotational multiplet.
