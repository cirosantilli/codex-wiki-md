# Fermi level

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum ideal-gas statistics](quantum-ideal-gas-statistics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fermi_level)

The Fermi level is the [chemical potential](chemical-potential.md) controlling electron occupations. For an ideal [Fermi gas](fermi-gas.md) at zero temperature it equals the [Fermi energy](fermi-energy.md); at nonzero temperature the chemical potential need not equal that zero-temperature energy.
