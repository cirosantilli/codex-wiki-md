# First supplementary law for quadratic reciprocity

↑ **Parent:** [Euler criterion](euler-criterion.md)

Euler's criterion at $a=-1$ gives

$$
\left(\frac{-1}{p}\right)=(-1)^{(p-1)/2}.
$$
