# Isoelectronic species

↑ **Parent:** [Electron configuration](electron-configuration.md)

Atoms, molecules or ions with the same [electron](electron.md) count, often compared because their orbital occupations or bonding patterns are related. Equal [electron](electron.md) counts do not guarantee identical energies or geometry.
