# Brownian displacement conventions

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md)

For ordinary long-time [diffusion](diffusion.md), the signed mean displacement vanishes while one-coordinate mean-square displacement is $2Dt$ and the three-dimensional mean-square radial displacement is $6Dt$. An rms distance is neither mean signed displacement nor cumulative path length.
