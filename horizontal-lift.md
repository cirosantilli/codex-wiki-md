# Horizontal lift

↑ **Parent:** [Connection (vector bundle)](connection-vector-bundle.md)

A [horizontal lift](horizontal-lift.md) is determined by the horizontal distribution of a [principal connection](connection-principal-bundle.md) or the corresponding [connection on a vector bundle](connection-vector-bundle.md). A horizontal lift of a base curve is a curve in the bundle projecting to it and tangent to the horizontal distribution. In a local frame its fiber coordinate solves a linear ordinary differential equation.
