# Sunlight coherence comparison

↑ **Parent:** [Optical coherence](optical-coherence.md)

Broadband sunlight has a short longitudinal [coherence length](optical-coherence-length.md), controlled by spectral [bandwidth](bandwidth-signal-processing.md), but a wider transverse coherence region because the solar disk has a small angular size. The scales are $\ell_c\sim c/\Delta\nu$ and $w_c\sim\lambda/\delta$ for angular diameter $\delta$. Filtering changes the comparison.
