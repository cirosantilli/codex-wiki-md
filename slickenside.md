# Slickenside

↑ **Parent:** [Geological fault](geological-fault.md)

A polished or striated surface on a [fault](geological-fault.md), produced or modified by sliding. Striation direction constrains the orientation of [fault slip](fault-slip.md); determining the sense of motion can require additional asymmetric indicators.
