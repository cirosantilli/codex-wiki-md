# Tadpole integral

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean massive loop integral](euclidean-massive-loop-integral.md)

The one-propagator Euclidean [loop momentum](loop-momentum.md) integral associated with a [tadpole diagram](tadpole-diagram.md). [Dimensional regularization](dimensional-regularization.md) gives $T_d(m)=\Gamma(1-d/2)(m^2)^{d/2-1}/(4\pi)^{d/2}$. Near $d=3$ this has no pole and $T_3(m)=-m/(4\pi)$ for $m>0$. A momentum-cutoff definition instead contains a linear [ultraviolet divergence](ultraviolet-divergence.md); the absence of a dimensional-regularization pole here concerns logarithms, not the positivity or convergence of the unregularized integral.
