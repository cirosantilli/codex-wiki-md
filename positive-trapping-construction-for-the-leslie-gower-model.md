# Positive trapping construction for the Leslie-Gower model

↑ **Parent:** [Leslie-Gower predator-prey model](leslie-gower-predator-prey-model.md)

A rectangular upper bound $u,v\leq M$, $M>1$, is inward pointing for the [Leslie-Gower predator-prey model](leslie-gower-predator-prey-model.md). To avoid the singular edge $u=0$, use $P=ue^{-v}$ and the inequality $\dot P/P\geq1-u[1+(a/d+b)^2/(4b)]$, obtained by completing a square. Choose $\varepsilon e^M$ sufficiently small and $0<\eta<\varepsilon$. Then $u\leq M$, $\eta\leq v\leq M$, $P\geq\varepsilon$ is a compact forward-invariant set in the positive quadrant. Removing an outward-flow neighborhood of a repelling equilibrium leaves a trapping region without equilibria, so the [Poincaré-Bendixson theorem](poincare-bendixson-theorem.md) yields a periodic omega-limit orbit.
