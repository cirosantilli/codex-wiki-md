# Secant (trigonometry)

↑ **Parent:** [Trigonometric function](trigonometric-function.md)

The secant function is the reciprocal of the [cosine](cosine.md), $\sec x=1/\cos x$, where the denominator is nonzero. Its [derivative](derivative.md) is $\sec x\tan x$, and $\sec^2x=1+\tan^2x$ follows from the [Pythagorean trigonometric identity](pythagorean-trigonometric-identity.md).
