# Throughput region of an interference graph

↑ **Parent:** [Interference graph](interference-graph.md)

For feasible [independent set](independent-set-graph-theory.md) activation vectors $S$, the throughput region is their [convex hull](convex-hull.md). Its downward closure is the same set because deleting active vertices preserves feasibility. Ideal [carrier-sense multiple access](carrier-sense-multiple-access.md) can supply strict margins above every vector in its interior by adjusting attempt rates.
