# Bitonic sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Sequence](sequence.md)

A [sequence](sequence.md) formed by an ascending part followed by a descending part, or the reverse arrangement, with either part possibly empty. In [sorting algorithms](sorting-algorithm.md), the monotonicity convention normally allows equal keys. Joining one ascending [sequence](sequence.md) to the reversal of another ascending [sequence](sequence.md) produces a [bitonic sequence](bitonic-sequence.md).
