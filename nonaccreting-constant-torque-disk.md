# Nonaccreting constant-torque disk

↑ **Parent:** [Viscous torque in an accretion disk](viscous-torque-in-an-accretion-disk.md)

A steady [Keplerian disk](keplerian-disk.md) can transport [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) by viscous stress with zero mass flux. The radial drift vanishes, but the [torque](torque.md) is constant and nonzero. An inner source and outer sink must supply its boundary angular-momentum exchange. This is distinct from a decretion flow, which transports mass outward.
