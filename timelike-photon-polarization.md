# Timelike photon polarization

↑ **Parent:** [Polarization vector](polarization-vector.md)

A timelike basis polarization in covariant photon quantization has positive [Minkowski metric](minkowski-metric.md) squared length, conventionally $\epsilon^{0\mu}=(1,\mathbf0)$ with signature $(+,-,-,-)$. It is distinct from the spatial [longitudinal polarization](longitudinal-polarization.md) $\epsilon^{3\mu}=(0,\widehat{\mathbf p})$. The extra minus sign in the covariant oscillator [commutator](commutator.md) makes its one-photon state a [negative-norm photon state](negative-norm-photon-state.md). Neither this temporal polarization nor an isolated longitudinal photon survives the [Gupta-Bleuler null-state quotient](gupta-bleuler-null-state-quotient.md).
