# Boundary variation of the Yang-Mills theta term

↑ **Parent:** [Yang-Mills theta term](yang-mills-theta-term.md)

For $F=dA+A\wedge A$, the [gauge-theory Bianchi identity](gauge-theory-bianchi-identity.md) is $DF=0$ and the variation is $\delta F=D\delta A$. The graded product rule for the [covariant exterior derivative](exterior-covariant-derivative.md) gives the displayed boundary integral. Thus a constant-coefficient [Yang-Mills theta term](yang-mills-theta-term.md) leaves the bulk [Yang-Mills equations](yang-mills-equations.md) unchanged, but can change the boundary variational condition. Its four-form density is exactly [gauge-invariant](gauge-invariance.md) by conjugation of $F$ and cyclicity of the [matrix trace](matrix-trace.md), even if the gauge parameter is nonzero on the boundary. Gauge changes of a local [Chern-Simons 3-form](chern-simons-3-form.md) must not be confused with gauge changes of this globally defined curvature density.
