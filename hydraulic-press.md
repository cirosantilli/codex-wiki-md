# Hydraulic press

↑ **Parent:** [Pascal's law](pascal-s-law.md)

Two connected [pistons](piston.md) apply [Pascal's law](pascal-s-law.md) to convert [force](force-split.md) and [displacement](displacement.md). For an ideal incompressible [fluid](fluid.md), $A_1s_1=A_2s_2$ and $F_1/A_1=F_2/A_2$, hence $F_1s_1=F_2s_2$: output [force](force-split.md) gain is not an [energy](energy-split.md) gain.
