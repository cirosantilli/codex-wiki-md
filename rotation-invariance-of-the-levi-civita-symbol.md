# Rotation invariance of the Levi-Civita symbol

↑ **Parent:** [Levi-Civita symbol](levi-civita-symbol.md)

The [determinant](determinant.md) transformation formula for the [Levi-Civita symbol](levi-civita-symbol.md) shows that it is invariant under every proper [rotation matrix](rotation-matrix.md), for which $\det R=1$. Under an orthogonal reflection its sign reverses. This distinguishes proper-rotation isotropy from the [pseudotensor](pseudotensor.md) transformation convention when both orientations are included.
