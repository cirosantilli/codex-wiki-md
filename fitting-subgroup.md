# Fitting subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Finite group theory](finite-group-theory-split.md)

The largest [nilpotent normal subgroup](nilpotent-normal-subgroup.md) of a [finite group](finite-group.md) $G$. If $O_p(G)$ denotes its [p-core](p-core.md), distinct [p-cores](p-core.md) commute, and $F(G)=\prod_pO_p(G)$. Every normal [nilpotent group](nilpotent-group.md) has [Sylow subgroups](sylow-subgroup.md) which are [characteristic subgroups](characteristic-subgroup.md), so it is contained in this product.
