# Stark effect of the hydrogen ground state

↑ **Parent:** [Stark effect](stark-effect.md)

The even, spherically symmetric [hydrogen atom](hydrogen-atom.md) ground state has no permanent [electric dipole](electric-dipole.md), so a uniform [electric field](electric-field.md) gives no first-order energy shift. The [electric-dipole selection rules for hydrogen](electric-dipole-selection-rules-for-hydrogen.md) allow odd-parity excited states to mix in at second order, yielding a negative shift quadratic in the field.
