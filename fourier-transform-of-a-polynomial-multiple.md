# Fourier transform of a polynomial multiple

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md)

With [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md) convention $\widehat f(k)=\int f(x)e^{-ikx}dx$, differentiation under the [integral](integral-split.md) gives $\partial_k^n\widehat f=\int(-ix)^nf(x)e^{-ikx}dx$, proving the displayed identity. For example it applies when all $x^jf(x)$ for $0\leq j\leq n$ are absolutely integrable, or in the [tempered distribution](tempered-distribution.md) sense. It is the dual counterpart of the [Fourier transform of a derivative](fourier-transform-of-a-derivative.md).
