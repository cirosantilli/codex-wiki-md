# Ricker population map two-cycle

↑ **Parent:** [Ricker population map](ricker-population-map.md)

For $H(u)=rue^{-u}$ and $L=\log r$, a nonzero second-iterate [fixed point](fixed-point.md) satisfies $u+H(u)=2L$. A genuine pair has the displayed form, and its [periodic-orbit multiplier](multiplier-of-a-periodic-orbit-of-an-iteration.md) is $(1-L)^2-d^2$. At $r=9$, the pair is exactly $\log3,3\log3$, with [periodic-orbit multiplier](multiplier-of-a-periodic-orbit-of-an-iteration.md) $(1-\log3)(1-3\log3)\simeq0.2264$. For large $r$ its upper point is asymptotic to $2\log r$ and the pair [periodic-orbit multiplier](multiplier-of-a-periodic-orbit-of-an-iteration.md) to $1-2\log r$, so eventual instability follows despite the small [derivative](derivative.md) at the upper point.
