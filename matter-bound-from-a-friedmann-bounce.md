# Matter bound from a Friedmann bounce

↑ **Parent:** [Friedmann bounce for pressureless matter and positive cosmological constant](friedmann-bounce-for-pressureless-matter-and-positive-cosmological-constant.md)

In a dust-plus-cosmological-constant universe with an [FRW metric](friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md) with $a_0=1$, a nondegenerate minimum at $a_*<1$ has $H(a_*)=0$ and $\ddot a(a_*)>0$. Combining these conditions with $\Omega_m+\Omega_\Lambda+\Omega_k=1$ gives the strict version of the displayed bound; equality is the limiting stationary case. A source at [cosmological redshift](cosmological-redshift.md) $z$ requires $z_*=a_*^{-1}-1\geq z$, so a high-redshift source bounds the allowed present [matter density parameter](matter-density-parameter.md) even without directly observing the bounce.
