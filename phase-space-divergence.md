# Phase-space divergence

↑ **Parent:** [Dissipative Liouville equation](dissipative-liouville-equation.md)

The [divergence](divergence.md) of the vector field generating a flow on [phase space](phase-space.md). Its time integral determines the [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md) of the [characteristic flow map](characteristic-flow-map.md): $d\log|\det D\Phi_t|/dt=(\nabla_z\cdot b)(\Phi_t,t)$. A [Hamiltonian vector field](hamiltonian-vector-field.md) has zero phase-space divergence in [canonical coordinates](canonical-variables.md).
