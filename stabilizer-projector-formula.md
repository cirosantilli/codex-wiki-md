# Stabilizer-projector formula

↑ **Parent:** [Stabilizer subspace](stabilizer-subspace.md)

For a [stabilizer group](stabilizer-group.md) $G=\langle g_1,\ldots,g_l\rangle$, the [orthogonal projector](orthogonal-projection.md) onto its [stabilizer subspace](stabilizer-subspace.md) is

$$
\Pi_G=\frac1{|G|}\sum_{g\in G}g
=\prod_{i=1}^l\frac{I+g_i}{2}.
$$
