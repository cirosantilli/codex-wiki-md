# Monomial alternant

↑ **Parent:** [Alternating polynomial](alternating-polynomial.md)

A monomial alternant is the [determinant](determinant.md) formed from powers with an integer exponent tuple $\ell$. It is a [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) when the exponents are nonnegative, and otherwise a [Laurent polynomial](laurent-polynomial.md). Equal exponents give equal columns and zero [determinant](determinant.md); interchanging exponents changes its sign. Ordered strictly decreasing nonnegative exponents give a [basis](basis.md) of alternating [polynomials](polynomial-split.md), by grouping monomials into permutation orbits.
