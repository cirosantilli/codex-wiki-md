# Connective spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Spectrum (topology)](spectrum-topology.md)

A [connective spectrum](connective-spectrum.md) has $\pi_qE=0$ for $q<0$. This makes the homological [Atiyah-Hirzebruch spectral sequence](atiyah-hirzebruch-spectral-sequence.md) first quadrant for a [based space](based-space.md), and prevents negative coefficient degrees from affecting its first nonzero [represented homology theory](represented-homology-theory.md).
