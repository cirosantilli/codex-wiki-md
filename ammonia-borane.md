# Ammonia borane

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical compound](chemical-compound.md)

A [Lewis acid-base adduct](lewis-acid-base-adduct.md) in which the [ammonia](ammonia.md) nitrogen [lone pair](lone-pair.md) donates into the empty boron-centered acceptor orbital of [borane](borane.md). A [coordinate covalent bond](coordinate-covalent-bond.md) links nitrogen and boron.
