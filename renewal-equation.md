# Renewal equation

↑ **Parent:** [Renewal process](renewal-process.md)

A renewal equation decomposes a process at its first renewal. For returns of a [Markov chain](markov-chain.md) to a fixed state, $f_k$ is the first-return probability and $u_n$ the probability of being at that state at time $n$. Their [probability generating functions](probability-generating-function.md) satisfy $U=1+FU$.
