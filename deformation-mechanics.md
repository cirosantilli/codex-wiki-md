# Deformation (mechanics)

↑ **Parent:** [Continuum mechanics](continuum-mechanics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deformation_(mechanics))

Deformation changes a body's shape or size. [Strain](strain.md) quantifies relative changes in lengths and angles, whereas a rigid translation or rotation alone produces no [strain](strain.md). The [continuum approximation](continuum-approximation.md) describes these changes using [displacement fields](displacement-field-mechanics.md) and their spatial variation.
