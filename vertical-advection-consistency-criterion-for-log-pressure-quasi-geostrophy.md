# Vertical-advection consistency criterion for log-pressure quasi-geostrophy

↑ **Parent:** [Log-pressure quasi-geostrophic potential vorticity](log-pressure-quasi-geostrophic-potential-vorticity.md)

For scales $U,L,D$, thermal balance estimates $w\sim f_0U^2/(N^2D)$. Density-weighted continuity gives horizontal [ageostrophic flow](ageostrophic-flow.md) of order $Lw/\min(D,H)$. It is small relative to $U$ only if

$$
\epsilon_a=\frac{f_0^2L^2}{N^2D\min(D,H)}\frac{U}{|f_0|L}\ll1.
$$

This condition supplements small [Rossby number](rossby-number.md) and small relative variation $\beta L/|f_0|$ of the [Coriolis parameter](coriolis-parameter.md).
