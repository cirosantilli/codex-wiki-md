# Unbounded directional risk of fixed ridge shrinkage

↑ **Parent:** [Ridge regression](ridge-regression.md)

For fixed positive [ridge regression](ridge-regression.md) penalty, choose a unit [eigenvector](eigenvector.md) $v$ of $X^{\mathsf T}X$ with eigenvalue $d>0$, and signal $\beta^0=Bv$. The directional ridge risk is $(\lambda^2B^2+\sigma^2d)/(d+\lambda)^2$, whereas [ordinary least squares](ordinary-least-squares.md) has risk $\sigma^2/d$. Letting $|B|$ grow makes the ridge squared bias arbitrarily large.
