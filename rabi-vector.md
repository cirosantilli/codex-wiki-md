# Rabi vector

↑ **Parent:** [Driven two-level Rabi model](driven-two-level-rabi-model.md)

For a rotating-frame two-state [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) $H=\hbar\mathbf W\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma/2$, the [Bloch vector](bloch-vector.md) obeys $\dot{\mathbf R}=\mathbf W\times\mathbf R$. Thus $\mathbf W$ specifies a rotation axis and angular speed. With detuning defined as drive [frequency](frequency.md) minus transition [frequency](frequency.md) and the lower state at positive $R_z$, a phase-zero transverse drive can give $\mathbf W=(\Omega,0,\delta)$. Changing basis or detuning convention requires changing the corresponding signs consistently.
