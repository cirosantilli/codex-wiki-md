# Ordinary topology on infinite subsets

↑ **Parent:** [Space of infinite subsets of the natural numbers](space-of-infinite-subsets-of-the-natural-numbers.md)

The ordinary topology on $[\mathbb N]^\omega$ has basic sets $[s]=\{X:s\sqsubset X\}$ for finite $s$. It is the [product topology](product-topology.md) on increasing enumerations, and also the subspace topology on infinite-subset [indicator functions](indicator-function.md) in $\{0,1\}^{\mathbb N}$.
