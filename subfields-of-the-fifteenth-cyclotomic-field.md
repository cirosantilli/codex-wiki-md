# Subfields of the fifteenth cyclotomic field

↑ **Parent:** [Cyclotomic field](cyclotomic-field.md)

The [Cyclotomic character of a finite cyclotomic extension](cyclotomic-character-of-a-finite-cyclotomic-extension.md) and the [Chinese remainder theorem](chinese-remainder-theorem.md) give the displayed [Galois group](galois-group.md). It has three order-two [subgroups](subgroup.md) and three order-four [subgroups](subgroup.md); the latter are kernels of its three nonzero [group homomorphisms](group-homomorphism.md) to $C_2$. By the [Fundamental theorem of Galois theory](fundamental-theorem-of-galois-theory.md), its proper intermediate [fields](field.md) strictly above $\mathbb Q$ are the three quadratic [fields](field.md)

$$
\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3}),\quad\mathbb Q(\sqrt5),\quad\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-15})
$$

and the three quartic [fields](field.md)

$$
\mathbb Q(\zeta_{15}^3),\quad\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3},\sqrt5),\quad\mathbb Q(\zeta_{15}+\zeta_{15}^{-1}).
$$

The first quartic has cyclic [Galois group](galois-group.md) of order four and unique quadratic [subfield](subfield.md) $\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)$; the second is a [biquadratic extension](biquadratic-extension.md), so they differ. The last is real whereas the others are not, by the [maximal real subfield of a cyclotomic field](maximal-real-subfield-of-a-cyclotomic-field.md) formula. Also $\sqrt{-3}+\sqrt5$ generates the second quartic: if $s=\sqrt{-3}+\sqrt5$, then $\sqrt{-3}=(s-8/s)/2$ and $\sqrt5=(s+8/s)/2$.
