# Fixed-energy initial-condition optimality

↑ **Parent:** [Direct-adjoint looping](direct-adjoint-looping.md)

For a differentiable objective on an admissible real [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) $H$, with $\|\mathbf u_0\|^2/2=E_0>0$, stationarity on the [sphere in a normed vector space](sphere-in-a-normed-vector-space.md) of fixed [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) means that the projected [gradient](gradient.md) is normal to the [sphere in a normed vector space](sphere-in-a-normed-vector-space.md). Thus $P_H\nabla J=\lambda\mathbf u_0$ for a real [Lagrange multiplier](lagrange-multiplier.md). It follows by testing all tangent variations [orthogonal](orthogonal-vectors.md) to $\mathbf u_0$. The multiplier need not be positive; second-order conditions are still needed for a local minimum.
