# Compact injective immersion is an embedding

↑ **Parent:** [Immersed submanifold](immersed-submanifold.md)

An injective [immersion](immersion.md) from a [compact](compact-space.md) smooth manifold into a [Hausdorff](hausdorff-space.md) smooth manifold is a [smooth embedding](smooth-embedding.md). Indeed, it is a continuous bijection onto its image, and compactness makes its inverse continuous.
