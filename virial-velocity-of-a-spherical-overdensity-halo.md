# Virial velocity of a spherical-overdensity halo

↑ **Parent:** [Virial radius of a dark-matter halo](virial-radius-of-a-dark-matter-halo.md)

For halo mean density $\Delta_v$ times the [critical density](critical-density.md) at its epoch, the circular virial velocity obeys $v_{\rm vir}^2=GM_h/R_h=(\Delta_v/2)H^2R_h^2$. Thus $v_{\rm vir}=HR_h\sqrt{\Delta_v/2}$. Specifying whether overdensity is relative to mean matter or [critical density](critical-density.md) is necessary for numerical inference.
