# Source-to-far-field operator at fixed frequency

↑ **Parent:** [Acoustic inverse source problem](acoustic-inverse-source-problem.md)

For $(\Delta+k^2)\psi=Q$, the [outgoing Green function for the three-dimensional Helmholtz equation](outgoing-green-function-for-the-three-dimensional-helmholtz-equation.md) yields the [far-field pattern](far-field-pattern.md)

$$
(T_kQ)(\boldsymbol\theta)=-\frac1{4\pi}\int_A e^{-ik\boldsymbol\theta\cdot\mathbf r}Q(\mathbf r)\,d^3\mathbf r,\qquad |\boldsymbol\theta|=1.
$$

The sign corresponds to the Green function satisfying $(\Delta+k^2)G=-\delta$. This [Hilbert-Schmidt operator](hilbert-schmidt-operator.md) samples the source [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) on the sphere $|\boldsymbol\xi|=k$; for a ball of radius $r_0$, its squared Hilbert-Schmidt norm is $r_0^3/3$.
