# Linear dilaton counterexample to zero integration constant

↑ **Parent:** [Dilaton equation from contracted Bianchi identity](dilaton-equation-from-contracted-bianchi-identity.md)

In flat target space, $\varphi=q_aX^a$ has zero Hessian and therefore solves the [leading metric-dilaton Weyl condition](leading-metric-dilaton-weyl-condition.md) for every constant covector $q$. But $\Box\varphi-2|\nabla\varphi|^2=-2q^2$, which need not vanish. This demonstrates the integration constant left by the [contracted Bianchi identity](contracted-bianchi-identity.md). Full quantum Weyl invariance fixes the allowed $q^2$ through the [central charge](central-charge.md), as in a [linear dilaton conformal field theory](linear-dilaton-conformal-field-theory.md); the metric equation alone does not.
