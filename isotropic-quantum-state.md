# Isotropic quantum state

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum information theory](quantum-information-theory-split.md)

For two $d$-dimensional systems, an isotropic quantum state has the form

$$
\rho_p=p|\Phi^+\rangle\langle\Phi^+|+(1-p)\frac{I}{d^2},
$$

where $|\Phi^+\rangle=d^{-1/2}\sum_j|jj\rangle$ is a [maximally entangled state](maximally-entangled-state.md). This parametrization is separable exactly when $p\leq1/(d+1)$.

The defining symmetry is $U\otimes\overline U$ invariance. This differs from the $U\otimes U$ invariance of a [Werner state](werner-state.md); members above the displayed threshold are entangled.
