# Turing threshold of the quadratic activator-inhibitor model

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic activator-inhibitor model](quadratic-activator-inhibitor-model.md)

A mode with squared [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) $q$ in the [quadratic activator-inhibitor model](quadratic-activator-inhibitor-model.md) has negative trace $b-1-(1+d)q$ when $0<b<1$. Its determinant is $D(q)=dq^2+(1-db)q+b$. A positive minimum location and a negative minimum require $db>1$ and $(db-1)^2>4db$, equivalent to $db>3+2\sqrt2$. At onset, the double determinant root is $q_c=(1+\sqrt2)/d$. In a bounded domain an admissible nonzero spatial mode must additionally lie in the open negative-determinant band.
