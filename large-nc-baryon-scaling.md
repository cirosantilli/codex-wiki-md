# Large-Nc baryon scaling

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum chromodynamics](quantum-chromodynamics.md)

In the large-color limit of [QCD](quantum-chromodynamics.md) with $g_s^2N_c$ fixed, baryon masses scale as $N_c$ while meson masses remain of order one. A [Skyrme model](skyrme-model.md) action with coefficients of order $N_c$ has soliton mass and rotational moment of inertia of order $N_c$, and rotor splittings of order $1/N_c$. This motivates a semiclassical mesonic description of [baryons](baryon.md); it does not uniquely determine every term in a finite-color effective theory.
