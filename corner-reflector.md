# Corner reflector

↑ **Parent:** [Retroreflector](retroreflector.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corner_reflector)

Three mutually perpendicular mirrors reverse a ray's direction when it encounters all three. With [orthonormal](orthonormal-set.md) normals $a,b,c$, write the reflections as $I-2P_a$, $I-2P_b$, $I-2P_c$. Their [orthogonal projection matrices](orthogonal-projection-matrix.md) have pairwise products zero and sum to $I$ by the [resolution of the identity in an orthonormal basis](resolution-of-the-identity-in-an-orthonormal-basis.md). The product of the three reflections is therefore $-I$. It reverses every incident direction independently of the order in which the mirrors are met. Real apertures restrict which incoming rays actually reach all three reflecting faces. Such [retroreflectors](retroreflector.md) provide the returning-light geometry in [lunar laser ranging](lunar-laser-ranging.md).
