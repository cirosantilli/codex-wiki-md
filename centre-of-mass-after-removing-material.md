# Centre of mass after removing material

↑ **Parent:** [Center of mass](center-of-mass.md)

Suppose a body of [mass](mass.md) $M_0$ has [centre of mass](center-of-mass.md) $\mathbf R_0$, and material of [mass](mass.md) $M_c<M_0$ with [centre of mass](center-of-mass.md) $\mathbf R_c$ is removed. Additivity of the first [mass](mass.md) moment gives the remaining [centre of mass](center-of-mass.md)

$$
\mathbf R=\frac{M_0\mathbf R_0-M_c\mathbf R_c}{M_0-M_c}.
$$

When the original [centre of mass](center-of-mass.md) is at the origin, the remaining [centre of mass](center-of-mass.md) points opposite the removed material's first [mass](mass.md) moment. For a constrained [rigid body](rigid-body-dynamics.md) rotating about a fixed axis, this displaced [centre of mass](center-of-mass.md) undergoes [centripetal acceleration](centripetal-acceleration.md) even if the applied axial [torque](torque.md) is zero.
