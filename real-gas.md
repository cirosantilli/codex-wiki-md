# Real gas

↑ **Parent:** [Thermodynamics](thermodynamics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_gas)

A [real gas](real-gas.md) departs from the [ideal gas](ideal-gas.md) law because interactions and molecular volume matter. Nonideal [equations of state](equation-of-state.md), including the [Dieterici equation](dieterici-equation.md), model such departures.
