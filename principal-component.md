# Principal component

↑ **Parent:** [Principal component analysis](principal-component-analysis.md)

A principal component is the projection of centered data onto a unit [eigenvector](eigenvector.md) of its [covariance matrix](covariance-matrix.md). Ordering the components by their [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) orders their variances. Repeated [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) allow any [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) of the corresponding [eigenspace](eigenspace.md).
