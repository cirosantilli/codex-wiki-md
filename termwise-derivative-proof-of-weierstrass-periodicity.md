# Termwise derivative proof of Weierstrass periodicity

↑ **Parent:** [Weierstrass elliptic function](weierstrass-elliptic-function.md)

The [Normal convergence of the Weierstrass elliptic-function series](normal-convergence-of-the-weierstrass-elliptic-function-series.md) allows its [derivative](derivative.md) to be written as an absolutely locally uniformly convergent sum of inverse cubes. For a [period lattice](period-lattice.md) $\Lambda$, this derivative sum is invariant under translation by any lattice vector, by reindexing. Consequently $\wp(z+\omega)-\wp(z)$ is constant; its apparent [poles](pole.md) cancel. The [Weierstrass elliptic function](weierstrass-elliptic-function.md) is even, so for a primitive lattice generator $\omega$, evaluation at $z=-\omega/2$ makes that constant zero. Applying this to two generators proves that this is a [periodic function](periodic-function.md), namely $\wp$. This argument avoids separating the original convergent series into two divergent inverse-square sums.
