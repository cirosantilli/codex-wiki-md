# Diameter-rod oscillation in cylindrical gravity

↑ **Parent:** [Gravitational field of an infinite uniform cylinder](gravitational-field-of-an-infinite-uniform-cylinder.md)

A thin rod of length $2R$ and [linear mass density](linear-mass-density.md) $\lambda$ slides along a diameter of an infinite uniform source cylinder of radius $R$. Neglect the narrow hole's perturbation of the source field. If the rod centre is displaced by $x$, $F(x)=\lambda\int_{x-R}^{x+R}g_y(y)\,dy$. The endpoint derivative gives $F'(0)=\lambda[g_y(R)-g_y(-R)]=-4\pi G\rho\lambda R$. Dividing the restoring coefficient by rod [mass](mass.md) $2R\lambda$ gives the displayed small-oscillation [frequency](frequency.md).
