# Palaeognathae

↑ **Parent:** [Aves](aves.md)

The crown-bird lineage containing [ostriches](ostrich.md), emus, kiwis, [tinamous](tinamou.md) and relatives. Its [palaeognathous palate](palaeognathous-palate.md) is an anatomical specialization, not evidence that its members are ancestors of [neognaths](neognathae.md).
