# Skolem normal form

↑ **Parent:** [Skolemization](skolemization.md)

A [first-order sentence](first-order-sentence.md) consisting of a block of [universal quantifiers](universal-quantification.md) followed by a [quantifier-free formula](quantifier-free-formula.md), obtained by [Skolemization](skolemization.md) in an expanded [first-order signature](first-order-signature.md). Its matrix can additionally be put into [conjunctive normal form](conjunctive-normal-form.md) to obtain [clausal form](clausal-form.md).
