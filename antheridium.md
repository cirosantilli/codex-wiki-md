# Antheridium

↑ **Parent:** [Gametophyte](gametophyte.md)

A male gamete-producing structure in relevant plant lineages. In [bryophytes](bryophyte.md) and many [ferns](fern.md), it produces motile [sperm](sperm.md) whose movement to an egg requires a suitable water connection.
