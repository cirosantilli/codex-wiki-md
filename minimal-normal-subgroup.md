# Minimal normal subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md)

A minimal normal subgroup is a nontrivial [normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md) containing no smaller nontrivial normal subgroup of the ambient group. It need not be a minimal nontrivial subgroup. In a finite [soluble group](solvable-group.md), it is elementary abelian: its derived subgroup must be trivial, a characteristic Sylow subgroup selects one prime, and the characteristic subgroup of elements killed by that prime gives exponent $p$.
