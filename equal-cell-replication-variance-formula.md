# Equal-cell replication variance formula

↑ **Parent:** [Cell-means parametrization](cell-means-parametrization.md)

Suppose a [normal linear model](normal-linear-model.md) gives an unrestricted mean to each cell and has $m$ [independent](independent-random-variables.md) observations per cell, with common error [variance](variance-split.md) $\sigma^2$. Each fitted cell mean is its [sample mean](sample-mean.md), with [variance](variance-split.md) $\sigma^2/m$; different fitted cell means are [independent](independent-random-variables.md). Estimate $\sigma^2$ by the pooled within-cell [residual sum of squares](residual-sum-of-squares.md) divided by $n-q$, where $q$ is the number of cells. Thus a cell mean has [standard error](standard-error.md) $s/\sqrt m$, a difference of two cell means has [standard error](standard-error.md) $s\sqrt{2/m}$, and a two-by-two difference-of-differences has [standard error](standard-error.md) $2s/\sqrt m$.
