# Infrared limitations of one-loop QCD running

↑ **Parent:** [One-loop running of the strong coupling](one-loop-running-of-the-strong-coupling.md)

For positive $\beta_0$, the one-loop [running coupling](running-coupling.md) tends to zero at high energy and grows as energy decreases. Near the [strong-coupling scale](strong-coupling-scale.md), omitted perturbative terms cease to be small; the apparent infrared pole is not a controlled prediction of a physical infinity. Continuing the algebraic formula below $\Lambda$ gives a negative coupling and is not a valid physical continuation. [Confinement](confinement.md) and hadron formation are nonperturbative phenomena, not a theorem proved by this one-loop pole. The integration constant obeys $\Lambda=\mu_0\exp[-1/(2\beta_0\alpha_0)]$, so an order-one coefficient alone cannot determine an absolute mass scale; a reference coupling or empirical input is also necessary.
