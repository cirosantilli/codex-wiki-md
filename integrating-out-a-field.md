# Integrating out a field

↑ **Parent:** [Effective field theory](effective-field-theory.md)

Integrating out a field means performing its path integral while retaining the other fields as backgrounds. The result is generally a nonlocal [quantum effective action](effective-action.md); for a field much heavier than all external momenta, a derivative expansion turns it into local effective interactions.
