# Lower-level depopulation in a four-level laser

↑ **Parent:** [Population inversion](population-inversion.md)

A pump feeds an auxiliary high level, rapid relaxation populates the upper [laser](laser-split.md) level, and rapid decay empties the lower [laser](laser-split.md) level. This separates pumping from the [laser](laser-split.md) transition and helps sustain net stimulated gain. Gain saturation and cavity loss still determine the oscillating steady state.
