# Order-parameter phase

↑ **Parent:** [Order parameter](order-parameter.md)

For a two-component [order parameter](order-parameter.md) of nonzero magnitude, write $m_x+im_y=\bar m e^{i\theta}$. Its phase is the orientation angle modulo $2\pi$, not a [thermodynamic phase](thermodynamic-phase.md) such as an ordered or disordered state. A global continuous [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md) rotates this angle uniformly; [spin waves](spin-wave.md) are smooth spatial changes, while a [phase vortex](phase-vortex.md) has nonzero winding around an excluded core. At a zero of the magnitude the angle is undefined, allowing the topology of an orientation field to change.
