# Regularity of finite Borel measures on Euclidean space

↑ **Parent:** [Radon measure](radon-measure.md)

Every finite positive [Borel measure](borel-measure.md) on Euclidean space is inner regular by [compact sets](compact-space.md) and outer regular by open sets. For an [open set](open-set.md) $U$, the [compact sets](compact-space.md) $\{x:|x|\le n,\ \operatorname{dist}(x,U^c)\ge1/n\}$ increase to $U$, so [continuity from below of a measure](continuity-from-below-of-a-measure.md) gives inner approximation. The empty complement is handled by compact balls. The class of Borel sets admitting both approximations is closed under complements: interchange inner and outer approximations, then truncate the inner closed set by a large compact ball, using finiteness of the [measure](measure.md). For a countable union, approximate its components from outside with summable errors, and approximate finitely many components from inside after making the remaining union's [measure](measure.md) small. Thus this class is a [sigma-algebra](sigma-algebra.md) containing the open sets. In particular, a finite [measure](measure.md) carried by a Borel [null set](null-set.md) has [compact subsets](compact-space.md) of that carrier capturing arbitrarily nearly all its mass.
