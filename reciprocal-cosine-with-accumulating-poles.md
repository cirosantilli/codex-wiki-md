# Reciprocal cosine with accumulating poles

↑ **Parent:** [Non-isolated singularity](non-isolated-singularity.md)

The function $1/\cos(1/z)$ has simple [poles](pole.md) at $z=1/[\pi(n+1/2)]$, accumulating at zero. Its origin is therefore a [non-isolated singularity](non-isolated-singularity.md), despite the underlying cosine of $1/z$ having an isolated essential singularity.
