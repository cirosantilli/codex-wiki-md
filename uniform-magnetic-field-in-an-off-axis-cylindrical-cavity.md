# Uniform magnetic field in an off-axis cylindrical cavity

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetostatics](magnetostatics.md)

Subtract a cylinder of [current density](current-density.md) $\mathbf J$ occupying the cavity from a complete current-carrying cylinder. Inside each cylinder, $\mathbf B=\mu_0\mathbf J\times\mathbf r/2$. The difference is independent of observation point and equals $\mu_0\mathbf J\times\mathbf b/2$, where $\mathbf b$ is the offset of the cavity axis. This assumes parallel axes, uniform [current density](current-density.md) in the remaining metal, and negligible end effects.
