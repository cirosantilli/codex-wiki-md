# Dual Thurston polytope

↑ **Parent:** [Thurston norm](thurston-norm.md)

The dual polytope consists of the real linear functionals $\alpha$ on $H_2(Y,\partial Y;\mathbb R)$ such that $|\alpha(u)|\leq\|u\|_T$ for every $u$. Thus it is the polar of the unit ball of the [Thurston norm](thurston-norm.md) and annihilates that [seminorm](seminorm.md)'s kernel.
