# Micelle

↑ **Parent:** [Amphiphile](amphiphile.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Micelle)

A micelle is a finite aggregate of [amphiphiles](amphiphile.md) in a [solvent](solvent.md). In an ordinary aqueous micelle, [hydrophobic](hydrophobicity.md) tails occupy the interior and [hydrophilic](hydrophilicity.md) heads contact the surrounding [water](water.md). Association can lower the [Gibbs free energy](gibbs-free-energy.md) by reducing unfavorable tail exposure, despite loss of molecular translational [entropy](entropy-split.md). Head-group interactions and chain packing help select the aggregate size and shape. A micelle has a filled tail-rich core, unlike a [lipid vesicle](lipid-vesicle.md) with a solvent-filled interior bounded by a [lipid bilayer](lipid-bilayer.md).
