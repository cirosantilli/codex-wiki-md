# Myofilament

↑ **Parent:** [Sarcomere](sarcomere.md)

A contractile filament in striated muscle. Thin actin-containing and thick myosin-containing filaments slide relative to one another during sarcomere shortening; their individual lengths are approximately conserved over an ordinary contraction.
