# Dense above a forcing condition

↑ **Parent:** [Dense subset of a forcing order](dense-subset-of-a-forcing-order.md)

For the convention that larger conditions are stronger, density above $p$ means every strengthening of $p$ has a further strengthening in $D$. It is the extension-cone density usually called [dense below a forcing condition](dense-below-a-forcing-condition.md) when smaller conditions are stronger. The sign must be translated with the order convention, rather than copied unchanged.
