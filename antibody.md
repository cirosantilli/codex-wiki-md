# Antibody

↑ **Parent:** [Protein](protein.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antibody)

An [antibody](antibody.md) is a [protein](protein.md) that recognizes particular molecular targets through binding. [Antibodies](antibody.md) participate in [immunity](immunity-medical.md); laboratory assays such as [Western blots](western-blot.md) exploit their selective binding to detect chosen [proteins](protein.md).

An [IgG](immunoglobulin-g.md) gives a concrete architecture: two [immunoglobulin heavy chains](immunoglobulin-heavy-chain.md) and two [immunoglobulin light chains](immunoglobulin-light-chain.md), joined by [disulfide bonds](disulfide-bond.md). Each arm ends in a [paratope](paratope.md) assembled from an [antibody variable region](antibody-variable-region.md) of each kind of chain. The two [paratopes](paratope.md) recognize the same [epitope](epitope.md) in this conventional [IgG](immunoglobulin-g.md); the flexible hinge allows different arm separations. The [antibody constant regions](antibody-constant-region.md) form the remaining portions. This is an illustrative [IgG](immunoglobulin-g.md), rather than the architecture of every [antibody](antibody.md) format. [Immunobiology, 5th edition, §§3-1, 3-2 and 3-4, Figures 3.2 and 3.4: four chains, paired sites and hinge flexibility](immunobiology-the-immune-system-in-health-and-disease-5th-edition-by-charles-a-janeway-jr-and-others/the-structure-of-a-typical-antibody-molecule.md)

**[Figure 28](#image-igg-chains-paratopes-and-two-arm-binding). IgG chains, paratopes and two-arm binding**. Original schematic of a conventional [IgG](immunoglobulin-g.md). Color distinguishes [immunoglobulin heavy chains](immunoglobulin-heavy-chain.md) from [immunoglobulin light chains](immunoglobulin-light-chain.md); darker tips mark [antibody variable regions](antibody-variable-region.md). Each outlined [paratope](paratope.md) includes both chain tips. The second panel contrasts one contact with two contacts to appropriately spaced [epitopes](epitope.md) on an imagined surface. Shapes, spacing and chain paths are illustrative, with no structural coordinates or physical scale.

Three questions must be kept separate. [Antibody specificity](antibody-specificity.md) asks which [antigens](antigen.md) are recognized. [Antibody affinity](antibody-affinity.md) describes one [paratope](paratope.md)–[epitope](epitope.md) interaction. [Avidity](avidity.md) describes the combined attachment when several contacts can cooperate. A selective [antibody](antibody.md) can have modest [antibody affinity](antibody-affinity.md), and a [monoclonal antibody](monoclonal-antibody.md) can still show [cross-reactivity](cross-reactivity.md). For an assay, the practical question is whether the observed readout identifies the intended [antigen](antigen.md) under the tested conditions; [Western blot antibody specificity controls](western-blot-antibody-specificity-control.md) make that question concrete.
