# Lambda point

↑ **Parent:** [Superfluidity](superfluidity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lambda_point)

The lambda point is the [temperature](temperature-split.md) of the transition between ordinary [liquid](liquid.md) [helium-4](helium-4.md) and its [superfluid](superfluid.md) phase, named after the sharp lambda-shaped [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) feature. At saturated [vapor pressure](vapor-pressure.md) it lies near $2.17\,\mathrm K$. Its value varies with [pressure](pressure-split.md).
