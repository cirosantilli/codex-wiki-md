# Functional equation of an additive cusp-form twist

↑ **Parent:** [Additive twist of a cusp-form L-function](additive-twist-of-a-cusp-form-l-function.md)

For even weight $k$, complete the [additive twist of a cusp-form L-function](additive-twist-of-a-cusp-form-l-function.md) as $M(f,a/N,s)=(N/(2\pi))^s\Gamma(s)L(f,a/N,s)$. Split its [Mellin transform](mellin-transform.md) at $y=1/N$ and use [rational-cusp inversion of a level-one cusp form](rational-cusp-inversion-of-a-level-one-cusp-form.md) in the lower integral. The resulting two integrals over $[1/N,\infty)$ converge locally uniformly for every complex $s$, giving an entire continuation and the displayed [functional equation](functional-equation.md). The relation $ad\equiv1\pmod N$ identifies the opposite cusp.
