# Lithium iron disulfide battery

↑ **Parent:** [Primary battery](primary-battery.md)

A [primary battery](primary-battery.md) using metallic [lithium](lithium.md) and [iron disulfide](iron-disulfide.md). The overall discharge conversion consumes four [lithium](lithium.md) atoms and releases four [electrons](electron.md) per $\mathrm{FeS_2}$ formula unit. This is distinct from the rechargeable host-to-host transport in a conventional [lithium-ion battery](lithium-ion-battery.md).
