# Effective porosity

↑ **Parent:** [Porosity](porosity.md)

[Effective porosity](effective-porosity.md) is the pore-volume fraction accessible to connected transport under the specified measurement or model. It excludes isolated pore volume and may also exclude poorly exchanging regions on the transport timescale, so $0\leq\phi_e\leq\phi$ for total [porosity](porosity.md) $\phi$. In a simple mobile-volume model the mean [pore velocity](pore-velocity.md) is $q/\phi_e$; the assumptions behind that model matter when interpreting tracer arrival times. A sample with total [porosity](porosity.md) $0.25$ but mobile pore fraction $0.20$ and [Darcy flux](darcy-velocity.md) $2.0\times10^{-5}\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ has mobile mean [pore velocity](pore-velocity.md) $1.0\times10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$. Woessner and Poeter discuss the role of connectivity and slowly exchanging pores.[https://books.gw-project.org/hydrogeologic-properties-of-earth-materials-and-principles-of-groundwater-flow/chapter/effective-porosity/](https://books.gw-project.org/hydrogeologic-properties-of-earth-materials-and-principles-of-groundwater-flow/chapter/effective-porosity/)
