# Antagonistic pleiotropy

↑ **Parent:** [Quantitative genetics](quantitative-genetics.md)

[pleiotropy](pleiotropy.md) in which a change has benefits and costs in different traits, environments, sexes or life stages. Whether an [allele](allele.md) spreads depends on its net contribution to [genotype fitness](genotype-fitness.md), rather than the sign of any one effect.
