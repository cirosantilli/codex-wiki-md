# Fluorescence recovery after photobleaching

↑ **Parent:** [Fluorescence](fluorescence-split.md)

[fluorescence recovery after photobleaching](fluorescence-recovery-after-photobleaching.md) follows fluorescence return to a bleached region as unbleached labeled molecules redistribute. Recovery depends on geometry, transport, binding and immobile fractions; it is not a direct measurement of every aspect of membrane fluidity.
