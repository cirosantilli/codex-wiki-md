# Isotropic stellar pressure deprojection

↑ **Parent:** [Spherical Abel deprojection](spherical-abel-deprojection.md)

For a spherical, nonstreaming [stellar population](stellar-population.md) with isotropic [velocity dispersion](velocity-dispersion.md), each component of the random [pressure tensor](pressure-tensor.md) equals $p=n\langle v_r^2\rangle$. The observed second [stellar velocity moment](stellar-velocity-moment.md) satisfies $N(R)\sigma_{\rm los}^2(R)=2\int_R^\infty p(r)r/\sqrt{r^2-R^2}\,dr$. Composing this [Abel transform](abel-transform.md) with itself gives the displayed inverse under sufficient decay and regularity. The [Spherical Jeans equation](spherical-jeans-equation.md) then gives $\psi'=p'/n$, with [gravitational acceleration](gravitational-acceleration.md) $\nabla\psi$. The [Poisson equation for Newtonian gravity](poisson-equation-for-newtonian-gravity.md) recovers total gravitating [mass density](density.md), not just the tracer density. Without velocity isotropy the same projected moment also contains anisotropic stresses.
