# Forbidden emission line

↑ **Parent:** [Emission line](emission-line.md)

A [forbidden emission line](forbidden-emission-line.md) is suppressed by the electric-dipole [selection rules](selection-rule.md) but can occur through weaker radiative channels such as magnetic-dipole or electric-quadrupole emission. Its relatively long-lived upper atomic level is vulnerable to collisional de-excitation in dense gas. Low-density astrophysical plasmas can therefore display strong forbidden-line emission even when laboratory dense sources do not.
