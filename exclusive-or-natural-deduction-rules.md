# Exclusive-or natural deduction rules

↑ **Parent:** [Natural deduction](natural-deduction.md)

Introduce [exclusive or](exclusive-or.md) from either $P,\neg Q$ or $\neg P,Q$. To eliminate $P\oplus Q$, derive the same conclusion $K$ in two subproofs, one under $P,\neg Q$ and the other under $\neg P,Q$, then discharge both pairs of case assumptions. These rules express exactly the two disjuncts of the displayed [disjunctive normal form](disjunctive-normal-form.md). [Conjunction introduction](conjunction-introduction-rule.md) and [disjunction introduction](disjunction-introduction-rule.md) derive that formula in each elimination branch; conversely [disjunction elimination](disjunction-elimination-rule.md) and [conjunction elimination](conjunction-elimination-rule.md) supply the two introduction cases. This proves equivalence without adding an unproved truth-table inference to a formal derivation.
