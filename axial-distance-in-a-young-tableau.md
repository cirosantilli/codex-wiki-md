# Axial distance in a Young tableau

↑ **Parent:** [Content vector of a standard Young tableau](content-vector-of-a-standard-young-tableau.md)

The axial distance from label $j$ to label $i$ in a [standard Young tableau](standard-young-tableau.md) is the difference of their [Young-diagram cell contents](content-of-a-young-diagram-cell.md). This sign convention makes $c_T(r+1)-c_T(r)$ equal to $+1$ for consecutive entries in a row and $-1$ in a column. The reciprocal distance supplies the diagonal coefficient of [Young orthogonal form](young-orthogonal-form.md).
