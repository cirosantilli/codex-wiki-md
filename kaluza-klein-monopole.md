# Kaluza-Klein monopole

↑ **Parent:** [Kaluza-Klein theory](kaluza-klein-theory.md)

A gravitational magnetic monopole for a compact circle, whose fibre is nontrivially twisted over a surrounding two-sphere. With flat spectator directions its nonsingular local geometry is a [Taub–NUT space](taub-nut-space.md). Reducing along the M-circle yields the [D6-brane](d6-brane.md). The magnetic dual of the IIB circle vector is instead a five-brane in nine noncompact dimensions.
