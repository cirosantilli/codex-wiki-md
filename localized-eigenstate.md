# Localized eigenstate

↑ **Parent:** [Anderson localization](anderson-localization.md)

A [localized eigenstate](localized-eigenstate.md) is an [energy eigenstate](energy-eigenstate.md) whose spatial weight remains concentrated near some position as the system grows. An exponentially [localized eigenstate](localized-eigenstate.md) has a decaying envelope characterized by a [localization length](localization-length.md). A small [participation ratio](participation-ratio.md) measures concentration in a chosen site basis; it does not by itself establish an exponential tail or a transport law.

An [energy eigenstate](energy-eigenstate.md) changes only by an overall [wave phase](phase-waves.md) under time evolution, whether localized or extended. Its stationary probability distribution alone therefore does not distinguish transport mechanisms. The important distinction concerns spatial extent and how superpositions of [energy eigenstates](energy-eigenstate.md) carry or retain a disturbance. A wave packet assembled from [localized eigenstates](localized-eigenstate.md) with nearby centres can remain confined, whereas extended modes can support propagation.
