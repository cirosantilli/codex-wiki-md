# Perfect integral Hecke pairing

↑ **Parent:** [Integral Hecke algebra of level-one cusp forms](integral-hecke-algebra-of-level-one-cusp-forms.md)

The pairing $(T,f)\mapsto a_1(Tf)$ identifies the [integral Hecke algebra of level-one cusp forms](integral-hecke-algebra-of-level-one-cusp-forms.md) with the integral dual of the cusp-form lattice. An integral basis beginning $q,q^2,\ldots,q^m$ gives a unitriangular first-$m$ coefficient matrix, so $a_1,\ldots,a_m$ form a dual basis. Commutativity of the [Hecke operators](hecke-operator.md) proves nondegeneracy on the algebra, and $T_1,\ldots,T_m$ then form its integral basis.
