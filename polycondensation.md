# Polycondensation

↑ **Parent:** [Step-growth polymerization](step-growth-polymerization.md)

[Polycondensation](polycondensation.md) is [step-growth polymerization](step-growth-polymerization.md) in which joining reactions eliminate small [molecules](molecule.md). Its chain-bearing [molecule](molecule.md) count excludes those byproducts; otherwise two chains joining while releasing one small product would misleadingly appear to conserve the counted population.[polycondensation, P04722, definition and growth-step equation](iupac-compendium-of-chemical-terminology-fifth-edition-gold-book-by-international-union-of-pure-and-applied-chemistry.md)
