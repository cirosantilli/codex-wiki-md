# Newtonian gravitational stress tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Newtonian gravitational field](newtonian-gravitational-field.md)

For a [Newtonian gravitational potential](newtonian-gravitational-potential.md) obeying the [Poisson equation for Newtonian gravity](poisson-equation-for-newtonian-gravity.md), define $T_{ij}=[\partial_i\Phi\partial_j\Phi-\delta_{ij}|\nabla\Phi|^2/2]/(4\pi G)$. Commutation of mixed [partial derivatives](partial-derivative.md) gives $\partial_jT_{ij}=\rho\partial_i\Phi$. Thus the gravitational force density can be put in conservative form as $-\partial_jT_{ij}$. The sign is chosen so that $T_{ij}$ is added to the momentum flux; reversing the stress convention reverses the divergence sign.
