# Local isometry between the helicoid and catenoid

↑ **Parent:** [Local isometry](local-isometry.md)

The [helicoid](helicoid.md) $H(s,v)=(\sinh s\cos v,\sinh s\sin v,v)$ and [catenoid](catenoid.md) $C(s,v)=(\cosh s\cos v,\cosh s\sin v,s)$ have the same [first fundamental form](first-fundamental-form.md) $\cosh^2s\,(ds^2+dv^2)$. Therefore matching equal parameter pairs gives a [local isometry](local-isometry.md). The result is local: a full turn changes the height on the [helicoid](helicoid.md) while the angular coordinate on the [catenoid](catenoid.md) repeats. Equal intrinsic lengths do not require the same embedding into Euclidean space.
