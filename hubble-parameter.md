# Hubble parameter

↑ **Parent:** [Expansion of the universe](expansion-of-the-universe.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hubble_parameter)

The [Hubble parameter](hubble-parameter.md) is the instantaneous fractional rate of [cosmic expansion](expansion-of-the-universe.md):

$$
H=\frac{\dot a}{a},
$$

where the dot is a [derivative](derivative.md) with respect to [cosmic time](cosmic-time.md). Thus a short interval $\Delta t$ changes any fixed-comoving [proper distance in cosmology](proper-distance-in-cosmology.md) by the fraction $\Delta d/d\simeq H\Delta t$. Its dimensions are inverse time; $H>0$ describes expansion and $H<0$ contraction. The [Hubble parameter identity](hubble-parameter-identity.md) gives $\dot H+H^2=\ddot a/a$.
