# Quicksort

↑ **Parent:** [Sorting algorithm](sorting-algorithm.md)

A [divide-and-conquer algorithm](divide-and-conquer-algorithm.md) that partitions entries relative to a pivot, recursively sorts the smaller and larger partitions, and combines their results. A three-way partition keeps all equal keys in a central bucket. Correctness follows from the partition order and recursive correctness; excluding the pivot from child inputs provides decreasing finite lengths. The choice of pivot controls balance and therefore running time.
