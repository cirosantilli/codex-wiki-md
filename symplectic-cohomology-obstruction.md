# Symplectic cohomology obstruction

↑ **Parent:** [Exact symplectic manifold](exact-symplectic-manifold.md)

On a nonempty compact $2n$-dimensional [symplectic manifold](symplectic-manifold.md) without boundary, with $n\geq1$, the class $[\omega]^n$ in top-degree [de Rham cohomology](de-rham-cohomology.md) is nonzero, since its integral in the [symplectic orientation](symplectic-orientation.md) is positive. In particular $\omega$ cannot be an [exact differential form](exact-differential-form.md): if $\omega=d\alpha$, then $\omega^n=d(\alpha\wedge\omega^{n-1})$ and the [Generalized Stokes theorem](generalized-stokes-theorem.md) would make that integral zero. Thus vanishing second [de Rham cohomology](de-rham-cohomology.md) obstructs a closed positive-dimensional [symplectic manifold](symplectic-manifold.md).
