# Bromodeoxyuridine

↑ **Parent:** [Nucleoside](nucleoside.md)

A brominated [thymidine](thymidine.md) analogue incorporated during [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) synthesis. Substitution changes [buoyant density](buoyant-density.md), allowing copied strands to be distinguished from unsubstituted strands.
