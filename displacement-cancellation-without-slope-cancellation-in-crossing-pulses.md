# Displacement cancellation without slope cancellation in crossing pulses

↑ **Parent:** [Two-sided string data with zero slope](two-sided-string-data-with-zero-slope.md)

For [two-sided string data with zero slope](two-sided-string-data-with-zero-slope.md), take $f(t)=\sin(\pi t/a)$ on $[-a,a]$ and zero outside. At $x=ca/2$, the [displacement](displacement.md) vanishes throughout $|t|<a/2$, whereas the spatial [derivative](derivative.md) is $y_x=-\pi\sin(\pi t/a)/(ca)$. Cancellation along a line in spacetime need not cancel the [derivative](derivative.md) transverse to that line. At $x=ca,t=0$, however, oddness gives $y(x,0)=0$ for every $x$, so the [spatial derivative](spatial-derivative.md) is zero even though its one-sided temporal limits there differ.
