<h1 id="attractive-branch-of-the-kepler-isochrone-transformation">Attractive branch of the Kepler–isochrone transformation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Kepler–isochrone radial transformation](kepler-isochrone-radial-transformation.md)

For bound old [Kepler orbits](kepler-orbit.md), $E<0$, $\lambda<0$ and $\bar E>0$ give a positive radius, positive isochrone scale $b$, and positive transformed [mass](mass.md). The shifted [energy](energy.md) is $\bar E-k=\bar E/\lambda<0$. For unbound old orbits with $E>0$, $1/2<\lambda\leq1$ gives an attractive unbound isochrone branch. The case $\lambda=0$ is handled by the separate [Kepler–harmonic radial duality](kepler-harmonic-radial-duality.md).
