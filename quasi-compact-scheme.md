# Quasi-compact scheme

↑ **Parent:** [Scheme](scheme.md)

A scheme is quasi-compact when its underlying [topological space](topological-space.md) is [quasi-compact](compact-space.md): every [open cover](open-cover.md) has a finite subcover. Since [affine open subsets](affine-open-subscheme.md) form a base, this is equivalent to possessing a finite [affine open cover](affine-open-cover.md). Every [affine scheme](affine-scheme.md) is quasi-compact.
