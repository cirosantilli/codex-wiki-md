# First uncountable ordinal is regular

↑ **Parent:** [First uncountable ordinal](first-uncountable-ordinal.md)

The [first uncountable ordinal](first-uncountable-ordinal.md) has [cofinality](cofinality.md) $\omega_1$. Indeed, the supremum of any [countable set](countable-set.md) of countable ordinals is still a [countable ordinal](countable-ordinal.md), so no countable sequence is cofinal in $\omega_1$.
