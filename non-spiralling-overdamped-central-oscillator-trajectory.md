# Non-spiralling overdamped central oscillator trajectory

↑ **Parent:** [Initially circular damped planar oscillator](initially-circular-damped-planar-oscillator.md)

For the [initially circular damped planar oscillator](initially-circular-damped-planar-oscillator.md) with [overdamping](overdamping.md) $\gamma>\omega$, put $\alpha=\sqrt{\gamma^2-\omega^2}$. The exact solution replaces $\cos\nu t,\sin\nu t/\nu$ by $\cosh\alpha t,\sinh\alpha t/\alpha$. Resolving along the starting radius and its anticlockwise tangent yields

$$
\tan\Delta\phi(t)=\frac{\omega\sinh\alpha t}{\alpha\cosh\alpha t+\gamma\sinh\alpha t}.
$$

Its limiting angle is the title value. Both coordinates remain positive when $c>0$, so the trajectory returns to the origin without repeated revolutions. It initially moves tangentially and bends toward its slow decaying [eigenmode](normal-mode.md), rather than following a radial straight line from the start.
