<h1 id="asymptotic-riemann-roch">Asymptotic Riemann–Roch</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Euler characteristic of a coherent sheaf](euler-characteristic-of-a-coherent-sheaf.md)

For a [Cartier divisor](cartier-divisor-split.md) $D$ on an $n$-dimensional [projective scheme](projective-scheme.md), $\chi(X,\mathcal O_X(mD))$ is a polynomial in $m$ whose leading term is $D^nm^n/n!$. The [intersection product](intersection-product-of-cartier-divisors.md) uses the fundamental cycle, with the generic multiplicity of each component. Thus the statement also applies to nonreduced schemes.
