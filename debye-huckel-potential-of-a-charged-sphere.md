<h1 id="debye-huckel-potential-of-a-charged-sphere">Debye-Hückel potential of a charged sphere</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Debye–Hückel approximation](debye-huckel-approximation.md)

For a uniformly charged sphere of radius $a$, total [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) $Q$, and an exterior dilute electrolyte of [permittivity](permittivity-split.md) $\varepsilon$, the [Debye–Hückel approximation](debye-huckel-approximation.md) gives $(r^2\phi')'=\kappa^2r^2\phi$. Decay at infinity and [Gauss's law](gauss-s-law.md), $-\varepsilon\phi'(a)=Q/(4\pi a^2)$, select the displayed [electric potential](electric-potential.md). Substituting $u=r\phi$ reduces the proof to $u''=\kappa^2u$. The screening parameter is $\kappa^2=\sum_i z_i^2e^2n_{i,\infty}/(\varepsilon k_BT)$, and the [ion](ion.md) profiles are $n_i\simeq n_{i,\infty}(1-z_ie\phi/(k_BT))$. The validity condition is $|z_i e\phi(a)|\ll k_BT$ for every relevant species. Integrating the diffuse [charge density](charge-density.md) using the radial equation gives total screening charge $-Q$.
