# Characteristic line field

↑ **Parent:** [Coisotropic submanifold](coisotropic-submanifold.md)

For a hypersurface $N$ of a [symplectic manifold](symplectic-manifold.md), the restriction of the [symplectic form](symplectic-form.md) has a one-dimensional kernel, its characteristic line field. On a [regular value](regular-value.md) level $N=H^{-1}(c)$, this line is generated by the [Hamiltonian vector field](hamiltonian-vector-field.md) of $H$.
