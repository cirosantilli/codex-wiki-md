# Horizontal forced-plume trajectory

↑ **Parent:** [Inclined forced plume](inclined-forced-plume.md)

For zero source [volume flux](volumetric-flow-rate.md), constant [Batchelor entrainment](batchelor-entrainment-hypothesis.md), and a horizontal source, the near-source trajectory is cubic: $z\sim\alpha\sqrt{\pi}f_0x^3/(3m_0^{3/2})$. Far from the source the centreline approaches a vertical asymptote, with its horizontal distance from that asymptote proportional to $z^{-1/3}$.
