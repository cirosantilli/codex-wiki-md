# Theory of successor

↑ **Parent:** [Complete theory](complete-theory.md)

The [complete theory](complete-theory.md) of the standard [successor structure](successor-structure.md) is axiomatized by [injectivity](injective-function.md) of $s$, $0$ outside its range, a predecessor for every nonzero element, and the sentences $s^n(x)\ne x$ for each positive integer $n$. Its models have a single distinguished one-sided chain generated by zero and any collection of two-sided integer chains. The [classification of models of the successor theory](classification-of-models-of-the-successor-theory.md) proves completeness and explains why the theory is categorical at every [uncountable](uncountable-set.md) [cardinal](cardinal-number-split.md) but not at the [countable](countable-set.md) [cardinal](cardinal-number-split.md).
