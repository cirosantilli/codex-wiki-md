# Pointwise minimum

↑ **Parent:** [Function](function-split.md)

The [function](function-split.md) taking the lesser value at every common-domain point. It forms the lower envelope of two graphs. For finitely many [affine functions](affine-function.md) it is a [concave function](concave-function.md): each affine branch is at least the corresponding [convex combination](convex-combination.md) of the two endpoint minimum values, so taking the minimum preserves that lower bound. A maximizing [mixed strategy](mixed-strategy.md) in a two-player [zero-sum game](zero-sum-game.md) maximizes such a lower envelope of opposing pure-choice [expected payoffs](expected-payoff.md).
