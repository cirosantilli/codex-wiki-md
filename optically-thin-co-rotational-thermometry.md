# Optically thin CO rotational thermometry

↑ **Parent:** [Carbon-monoxide tracer of molecular hydrogen](carbon-monoxide-tracer-of-molecular-hydrogen.md)

For one optically thin LTE carbon-monoxide component, $L_{ul}=N_uA_{ul}h\nu_{ul}$ and $N_J\propto(2J+1)e^{-E_J/(k_BT)}$. Thus $C=7A_{32}\nu_{32}/(3A_{10}\nu_{10})$ for the 3–2/1–0 luminosity ratio. The temperature then determines the $J=1$ population fraction and hence the CO mass. Multiple excitation components, optical depth or subthermal excitation invalidate a single-temperature inference.
