# Burgers' equation

↑ **Parent:** [Partial differential equation](partial-differential-equation-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burgers'_equation)

The one-dimensional equation $u_t+uu_x=\nu u_{xx}$ combines nonlinear transport with diffusion. Positive $\nu$ gives the [viscous Burgers equation](viscous-burgers-equation.md); setting $\nu=0$ gives the [Inviscid Burgers equation](inviscid-burgers-equation.md).
