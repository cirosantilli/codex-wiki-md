<h1 id="erdos-rado-theorem-for-pairs">Erdős-Rado theorem for pairs</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Partition relation](partition-relation.md)

For every infinite [cardinal](cardinal-number.md) $\lambda$, every coloring of pairs from $(2^\lambda)^+$ by $\lambda$ colors has a [monochromatic](monochromatic-set.md) subset of size $\lambda^+$. Construct an end-homogeneous tree by routing a new vertex through the least preceding vertices that match its colors to all previously chosen ancestors. An ancestor's color to every later vertex on a branch is constant. A node at height $\alpha$ is determined by its color sequence of length $\alpha$, so levels below $\lambda^+$ have size at most $\lambda^{|\alpha|}\leq2^\lambda$. The whole tree has more than $2^\lambda$ vertices, forcing a branch of length $\lambda^+$. One of the $\lambda$ ancestor-colors occurs $\lambda^+$ times, giving the required set.
