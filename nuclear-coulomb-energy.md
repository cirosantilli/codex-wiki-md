# Nuclear Coulomb energy

↑ **Parent:** [Semi-empirical mass formula](semi-empirical-mass-formula.md)

[Nuclear Coulomb energy](nuclear-coulomb-energy.md) is the positive electrostatic mass-energy contribution from proton repulsion in a [nucleus](atomic-nucleus-split.md). A uniform-charge model gives scaling $Z^2/R$, and approximately constant nuclear density gives $R\propto A^{1/3}$. It reduces conventional positive [nuclear binding energy](nuclear-binding-energy.md).
