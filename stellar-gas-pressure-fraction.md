# Stellar gas-pressure fraction

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar structure](stellar-structure-split.md)

The gas-pressure fraction describes the relative support by an [ideal gas](ideal-gas.md) and [radiation pressure](radiation-pressure.md). For monatomic gas plus equilibrium radiation, it determines the [adiabatic exponents of a monatomic gas-radiation mixture](adiabatic-exponents-of-a-monatomic-gas-radiation-mixture.md). Its value at the unperturbed state may be specified, but it generally changes during an isentropic parcel displacement.
