# Ghost decoupling in strict axial gauge

↑ **Parent:** [Axial gauge](axial-gauge.md)

The [Faddeev-Popov operator](faddeev-popov-operator.md) for $n\cdot A=0$ is $n\cdot D$. Its interaction contains $\bar c(n\cdot A)c$ and vanishes on the strict gauge slice. Equivalently, its ghost-gluon vertex is proportional to $n_\mu$ and the [axial-gauge propagator](axial-gauge-propagator.md) obeys $n_\mu D^{\mu\nu}=0$. The residual determinant is field independent; its zero modes and $n\cdot p$ singularities still require a compatible [axial-gauge pole prescription](axial-gauge-pole-prescription.md). [Faddeev-Popov ghost](faddeev-popov-ghost.md) decoupling does not mean that the free [Faddeev-Popov ghost](faddeev-popov-ghost.md) two-point function is zero.
