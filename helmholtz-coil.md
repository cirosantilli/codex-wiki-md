# Helmholtz coil

↑ **Parent:** [Current loop](current-loop.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helmholtz_coil)

A [Helmholtz coil](helmholtz-coil.md) arrangement uses two identical coaxial circular windings separated by their [radius](radius.md), carrying equal [electric currents](electric-current.md) in the same circulation sense. Their combined [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) is especially uniform near the midpoint, making it useful for a [current-loop torque experiment](current-loop-torque-experiment.md). The cancellation of the axial second [derivative](derivative.md) there follows from the [on-axis magnetic field of a circular current loop](on-axis-magnetic-field-of-a-circular-current-loop.md).
