# Psychopathy

↑ **Parent:** [Psychopathology](psychopathology.md)

A construct involving characteristic affective, interpersonal and behavioural features. Findings in a selected offender sample should not be generalized to everyone with antisocial behaviour or used to infer an individual’s moral character from a brain signal.
