# Resolved shear stress

↑ **Parent:** [Slip system](slip-system.md)

For uniaxial [stress](stress.md) $\sigma$, projection onto a slip-plane normal and a slip direction gives the displayed shear stress. The angles $\phi$ and $\lambda$ are measured from the tensile axis to that normal and direction respectively.
