# Gauge-fixing fermion

↑ **Parent:** [BRST symmetry](brst-symmetry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gauge-fixing_fermion)

A gauge-fixing fermion is a Grassmann-odd functional whose [BRST transformation](brst-symmetry.md) $s\Psi$ supplies the gauge-fixing and ghost terms. Nilpotence gives $s(s\Psi)=0$ immediately.
