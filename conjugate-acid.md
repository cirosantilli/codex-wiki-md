# Conjugate acid

↑ **Parent:** [Conjugate base](conjugate-base.md)

The [conjugate acid](conjugate-acid.md) of a [chemical base](base-chemistry.md) is the [chemical species](chemical-species.md) formed when it accepts one [proton](proton.md). This is a relationship between partners, rather than a claim that the partner is a [strong acid](strong-acid.md).
