# Tectonic shortening

↑ **Parent:** [Structural geology](structural-geology.md)

Reduction of distance between material markers during tectonic deformation. [Folding](folding.md), [reverse faulting](reverse-faulting.md) and distributed [strain](strain.md) can accommodate it; the shortening direction alone does not determine all three [principal stresses](principal-stress.md).
