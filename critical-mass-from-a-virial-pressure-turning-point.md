# Critical mass from a virial pressure turning point

↑ **Parent:** [Virial pressure-radius curve with uniform-sphere binding energy](virial-pressure-radius-curve-with-uniform-sphere-binding-energy.md)

Eliminate cloud radius using its [mean density](mean-density.md) and the critical radius $r_c=4GM/(15c_s^2)$. The displayed threshold scales as $T^{3/2}\bar\rho^{-1/2}$ at fixed mean particle [mass](mass.md). Equivalently, the maximum confining [pressure](pressure-split.md) is $10125c_s^8/(1024\pi G^3M^2)$. The numerical coefficient depends on the assumed [density](density.md) profile and on whether the [density](density.md) refers to the mean or the boundary.
