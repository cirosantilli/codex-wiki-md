# Moderate deviation principle

↑ **Parent:** [Large deviation principle](large-deviation-principle.md)

A [large deviation principle](large-deviation-principle.md) for fluctuations larger than the usual [central limit theorem](central-limit-theorem.md) scale but smaller than a fixed displacement of the empirical mean. For the displayed sum scaling, the natural [large-deviation speed](large-deviation-speed.md) is $b_n^2$. Under suitable exponential-moment hypotheses and positive [variance](variance-split.md) $\sigma^2$, its [rate function](rate-function.md) is the quadratic $x^2/(2\sigma^2)$.
