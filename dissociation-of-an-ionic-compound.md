# Dissociation of an ionic compound

↑ **Parent:** [Dissolution](dissolution.md)

[Dissociation of an ionic compound](dissociation-of-an-ionic-compound.md) separates its constituent [ions](ion.md). It differs from creating charged species by [ionization](ionization.md) of neutral [molecules](molecule.md). Dissolved [ions](ion.md) can also associate into [ion pairs](ion-pair.md), so a dissociated description does not exclude all subsequent association.
