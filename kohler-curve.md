<h1 id="kohler-curve">Köhler curve</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Köhler theory](kohler-theory.md)

The equilibrium [saturation ratio](saturation-ratio.md) over a [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md) droplet as a function of wet radius for fixed solute content. In the dilute radius approximation $S_{\rm eq}\simeq1+A/r-B/r^3$, its maximum occurs at $r_c=\sqrt{3B/A}$. The corresponding [critical supersaturation](critical-supersaturation.md) is $2A/(3r_c)$. Diameter and radius coefficients differ and must not be mixed.
