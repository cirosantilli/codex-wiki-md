# Coset action

↑ **Parent:** [Group action](group-action.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coset_action)

A group acts on the left cosets of a subgroup by left multiplication; its kernel is the core of that subgroup.
