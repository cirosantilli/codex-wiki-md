# Tensoriality

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor field](tensor-field.md)

A [multilinear](multilinear-map.md) operation on [vector fields](vector-field.md) is [tensorial](tensoriality.md) when it is [linear](linearity.md) over [smooth functions](smooth-function.md) in every argument. Its value at a point then depends only on the argument [vectors](vector.md) at that point. To see this, use a [smooth cutoff function](smooth-cutoff-function.md) to reduce to local fields, expand them in a [local frame](frame-of-a-vector-bundle.md), and apply [linearity](linearity.md) over [smooth functions](smooth-function.md) to their coefficients. A smoothly valued [tensorial](tensoriality.md) operation therefore defines a [tensor field](tensor-field.md). The [curvature](curvature.md) of an [affine connection](affine-connection.md) is [tensorial](tensoriality.md), whereas a [covariant derivative](covariant-derivative.md) differentiates a [scalar](scalar.md) coefficient in its second argument and is not [tensorial](tensoriality.md) there.
