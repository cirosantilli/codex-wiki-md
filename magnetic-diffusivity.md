# Magnetic diffusivity

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic diffusion](magnetic-diffusion.md)

Magnetic diffusivity is the coefficient of [magnetic diffusion](magnetic-diffusion.md). In a scalar-conductivity fluid with vacuum permeability and negligible [displacement current](displacement-current.md), $\eta_m=1/(\mu_0\sigma)$. Its dimensions are length squared per time, and a diffusion time on scale $L$ is $L^2/\eta_m$.
