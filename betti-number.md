# Betti number

↑ **Parent:** [Singular homology](singular-homology.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Betti_number)

A Betti number is the dimension of a real [singular homology](singular-homology.md) group; on a smooth manifold it is also the dimension of the corresponding [de Rham cohomology](de-rham-cohomology.md) group. For a finite-dimensional cohomology group on a closed Riemannian manifold, it equals the dimension of its space of harmonic representatives. Betti numbers can increase under a finite cover, so equality is a separate hypothesis, not a general covering-space theorem.
