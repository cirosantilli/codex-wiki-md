# Adaptive walk

↑ **Parent:** [Population genetics](population-genetics-split.md)

A sequence of changes between [genotypes](genotype.md) or [phenotypes](phenotype.md) under [natural selection](natural-selection.md). A model with one substitution at a time is an approximation that can fail when many beneficial lineages compete.
