# Stable cohomology operation

↑ **Parent:** [Cohomology operation](cohomology-operation.md)

A cohomology operation is stable if it commutes with the [suspension isomorphism](suspension-isomorphism.md) on [reduced cohomology](reduced-cohomology.md). Ordinary [cup products](cup-product.md) become zero on a suspension, but stable operations can still connect its nonzero classes. The [Steenrod squares](steenrod-square.md) and [Steenrod reduced powers](steenrod-reduced-power.md) are examples.
