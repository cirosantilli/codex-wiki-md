# Additivity of a cooperative-game value

↑ **Parent:** [Transferable utility game](transferable-utility-game.md)

A value has [cooperative-game additivity](additivity-of-a-cooperative-game-value.md) when adding two characteristic [functions](function-split.md) on the same player [set](set-split.md) adds their allocation [vectors](vector.md). Here $(v+w)(S)=v(S)+w(S)$ for every [coalition](coalition-game-theory.md). The sum of two [simple cooperative games](simple-cooperative-game.md) need not be simple; the axiom is naturally stated on [transferable utility games](transferable-utility-game.md).
