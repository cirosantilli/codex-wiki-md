# Riesz potential

↑ **Parent:** [Distribution theory](distribution-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riesz_potential)

For $0<\alpha<n$, the Riesz potential is convolution with a constant multiple of $|x|^{\alpha-n}$. Its [Fourier multiplier](fourier-multiplier-operator.md) is a constant multiple of $|\xi|^{-\alpha}$.
