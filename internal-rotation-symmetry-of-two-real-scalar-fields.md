# Internal rotation symmetry of two real scalar fields

↑ **Parent:** [Real scalar field](real-scalar-field.md)

For two free [real scalar fields](real-scalar-field.md), the internal rotation $\delta\phi_1=\theta\phi_2$, $\delta\phi_2=-\theta\phi_1$ is a symmetry precisely when their squared masses agree. Its [Noether current](noether-current.md) is $j^\mu=\phi_2\partial^\mu\phi_1-\phi_1\partial^\mu\phi_2$. Without mass degeneracy, $\partial_\mu j^\mu=(m_2^2-m_1^2)\phi_1\phi_2$.
