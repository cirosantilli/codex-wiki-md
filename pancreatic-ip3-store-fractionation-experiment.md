# Pancreatic IP3 store fractionation experiment

↑ **Parent:** [IP3-sensitive calcium store](ip3-sensitive-calcium-store.md)

A 1984 primary experiment localized [IP3](inositol-trisphosphate.md)-evoked release using [pancreatic acinar cells](pancreatic-acinar-cell.md) isolated from rat [pancreas](pancreas.md) with [collagenase](collagenase.md). A homogenized preparation was separated by [differential centrifugation](differential-centrifugation.md), then by a [sucrose](sucrose.md) step [density gradient](density-gradient.md) or precipitation with [magnesium](magnesium.md) chloride. The readout was [IP3](inositol-trisphosphate.md)-induced [calcium](calcium.md) release per mass of [protein](protein-split.md) across fractions. Release tracked two [endoplasmic reticulum](endoplasmic-reticulum.md) markers, [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) content and an [enzyme](enzyme-split.md) activity, across several separation methods. Marker activities for [mitochondria](mitochondria.md) and the [cell membrane](cell-membrane-split.md) supplied contrasting comparisons: release correlated negatively with them.[Streb, Bayerdörffer, Haase, Irvine and Schulz, Effect of inositol-1,4,5-trisphosphate on isolated subcellular fractions of rat pancreas (1984), abstract: preparation, separation methods, protein-normalized release and marker correlations](effect-of-inositol-1-4-5-trisphosphate-on-isolated-subcellular-fractions-of-rat-pancreas-by-h-streb-and-others.md)

Agreement across distinct fractionations supports an [endoplasmic reticulum](endoplasmic-reticulum.md)-associated source in this preparation. “Nonmitochondrial” alone only excludes a candidate; positive compartment markers add localization evidence. Marker comparisons control the localization inference, rather than replacing a no-[IP3](inositol-trisphosphate.md) release comparison. Fractionation may mix or damage compartments, and correlation does not itself identify the [IP3 receptor](inositol-trisphosphate-receptor.md) [protein](protein-split.md). This [calcium](calcium.md) release readout also does not directly measure secretion or establish identical responses in every [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md).
