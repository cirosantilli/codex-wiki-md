# Uniform-Pareto conjugacy

↑ **Parent:** [Conjugate prior](conjugate-prior.md)

A [Pareto distribution](pareto-distribution.md) prior for the upper limit of independent [uniform distributions](continuous-uniform-distribution.md) remains Pareto after observing data. The likelihood adds the sample size to the shape and replaces the lower bound by the larger of the prior bound and sample maximum.
