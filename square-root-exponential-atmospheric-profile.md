# Square-root exponential atmospheric profile

↑ **Parent:** [Atmospheric pressure-temperature profile](atmospheric-pressure-temperature-profile.md)

For $P_0>0$, $\alpha\ne0$, $T\ge T_0$ and physical $T>0$, the [atmospheric pressure-temperature profile](atmospheric-pressure-temperature-profile.md) $P=P_0\exp(\alpha\sqrt{T-T_0})$ is equivalent to

$$
T=T_0+\frac{\log^2(P/P_0)}{\alpha^2},\qquad \frac{\log(P/P_0)}{\alpha}\ge0.
$$

The branch restriction is essential: squaring does not create a second physical branch. Its [temperature gradient](temperature-gradient.md) is $dT/d\log P=2\sqrt{T-T_0}/\alpha$. Thus, in [hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md), $\alpha<0$ gives an [atmospheric thermal inversion](inversion-meteorology.md), while $\alpha>0$ gives outward cooling. At $T=T_0$ the inverse branch has zero [derivative](derivative.md); $\alpha=0$ gives constant [pressure](pressure.md) and cannot describe a nontrivial hydrostatic vertical interval.
