# Brauer p-divisibility in characteristic p

↑ **Parent:** [Brauer group](brauer-group.md)

In characteristic $p$, the exact sequence $1\to K_s^{\times}\xrightarrow{p}K_s^{\times}\to K_s^{\times}/(K_s^{\times})^p\to1$ has a quotient killed by $p$. The [characteristic p Galois dimension bound](characteristic-p-galois-dimension-bound.md) makes its second cohomology zero. Hence multiplication by $p$ on $\operatorname{Br}(K)$ is surjective. The root map is injective, and its failure of surjectivity on a separable closure is encoded by the quotient.
