# Linkage isomerization

↑ **Parent:** [Linkage isomerism](linkage-isomerism.md)

[Linkage isomerization](linkage-isomerization.md) changes which atom of an [ambidentate ligand](ambidentate-ligand.md) is bound to a central atom. Its rate depends on a reaction barrier, whereas the final equilibrium depends on relative free energies.
