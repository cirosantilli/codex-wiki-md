# Absence of exponential instability in the semi-infinite Eady model

↑ **Parent:** [Semi-infinite Eady model](semi-infinite-eady-model.md)

A growing complex phase speed cannot equal the real basic velocity. The linear interior [potential-vorticity equation](potential-vorticity-evolution-equation.md) then forces zero anomaly everywhere, decay fixes one exponential, and the wall fixes the real speed $c=\Lambda/m$. Thus there is no growing smooth decaying edge-wave eigenmode. This excludes exponential normal-mode instability without claiming that every initial disturbance lacks transient amplification or a singular neutral component.
