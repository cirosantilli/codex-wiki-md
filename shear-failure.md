# Shear failure

↑ **Parent:** [Brittle fracture](brittle-fracture.md)

Loss of load-bearing strength accompanied by relative displacement tangential to a surface, rather than primarily opening normal to it. The [Mohr-Coulomb failure criterion](mohr-coulomb-failure-criterion.md) models frictional [shear failure](shear-failure.md) by $|\tau|=c+\mu\sigma'_n$, using [cohesion](cohesion.md), a friction coefficient and compressive [effective stress](effective-stress.md) normal to the potential sliding plane.
