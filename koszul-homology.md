# Koszul homology

↑ **Parent:** [Koszul complex](koszul-complex.md)

Koszul homology is the homology of the [Koszul complex with module coefficients](koszul-complex-with-module-coefficients.md). Its degree-zero term is $M/(f)M$, and its positive terms record failures of regularity. Each generator acts trivially on homology by the [Koszul homotopy for multiplication by a generator](koszul-homotopy-for-multiplication-by-a-generator.md).
