# Propagator

↑ **Parent:** [Quantized field](quantized-field.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Propagator)

A propagator is a two-point kernel describing propagation and, in a free theory, a [Green function](green-s-function.md) for the quadratic kinetic operator. Its boundary prescription determines its type: the time-ordered [Feynman propagator](feynman-propagator.md) uses an $i\epsilon$ prescription, while retarded and advanced propagators impose causal boundary conditions.
