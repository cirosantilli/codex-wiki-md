# Spin-averaged electromagnetic parton tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Hadronic tensor](hadronic-tensor.md)

For an on-shell massless [quark](quark.md) with incoming momentum $k$ and outgoing momentum $k+q$, average the initial spin and sum the final spin. The [gamma matrix trace identities](gamma-matrix-trace-identities.md) give $S^{\mu\nu}=\tfrac12\operatorname{Tr}[\not k\gamma^\mu(\not k+\not q)\gamma^\nu]=2[k^\mu(k+q)^\nu+k^\nu(k+q)^\mu-g^{\mu\nu}k\cdot q]$. On shell $2k\cdot q=Q^2=-q^2$, and defining $T^{\mu\nu}=-g^{\mu\nu}+q^\mu q^\nu/q^2$ and $\widetilde k=k-(k\cdot q/q^2)q$ yields the displayed transverse decomposition. With the hadronic-tensor prefactor $1/(4\pi)$, one-particle [Lorentz-invariant phase space](lorentz-invariant-phase-space.md) gives $w_f^{\mu\nu}=(Q_f^2/2)\delta_+((k+q)^2)S^{\mu\nu}$. The antiquark has the same unpolarized tensor and squared electric charge.
