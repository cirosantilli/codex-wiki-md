# Ising-chain field derivative and transverse polarization

↑ **Parent:** [Ising-chain ground-state energy](ising-chain-ground-state-energy.md)

For a [Transverse-field Ising model](transverse-field-ising-model.md) whose field term is $-2Jg\sum_nS_n^z$, the [Hellmann–Feynman theorem](hellmann-feynman-theorem.md) gives the displayed relation. Differentiating $E_0=-J\sum_k\sqrt{1+g^2-2g\cos k}$ gives $\sum_n\langle S_n^z\rangle=\sum_k(g-\cos k)/(2\sqrt{1+g^2-2g\cos k})$. This is polarization along the imposed field, not spontaneous order along the exchange axis. In the thermodynamic limit it is zero per site at g=0, tends to1/2 at large positive g and equals $1/\pi$ at g=1.
