# Ultrafilter characterization of compactness

↑ **Parent:** [Compact space](compact-space.md)

A [topological space](topological-space.md) is [compact](compact-space.md) if and only if every [ultrafilter](ultrafilter.md) on it converges to at least one point, assuming the [ultrafilter lemma](ultrafilter-lemma.md). No [Hausdorff](hausdorff-space.md) assumption is needed. In a compact space, the closures of the filter members have the [finite intersection property](finite-intersection-property.md), so choose a point in their intersection. Every open neighbourhood of that point belongs to the ultrafilter, since otherwise its closed complement would be a filter member. Conversely, extend the complements of a cover with no finite subcover to an ultrafilter; a limit point lies in a covering open set, contradicting the simultaneous membership of that set and its complement.
