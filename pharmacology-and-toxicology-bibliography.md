## Acute Renal Failure Following Oxalic Acid Poisoning: A Case Report

↑ **Parent:** [Pharmacology and toxicology bibliography](pharmacology-and-toxicology-bibliography.md)

[https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3527234/](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3527234/)

Original case report; renal biopsy showed tubular injury with refractile [calcium oxalate](biology.md#calcium-oxalate) crystals. The case does not establish that every exposure produces [hypocalcaemia](biology.md#hypocalcaemia). Indexed full text was available; direct retrieval intermittently displayed a browser-verification page.
