# Kinetic theory of gases

↑ **Parent:** [Kinetic theory](kinetic-theory-split.md)

A microscopic account of gas properties using the motion and collisions of its constituents. For a dilute monatomic ideal gas, translational kinetic [energy](energy-split.md) gives $p=Nm\langle v^2\rangle/(3V)$ and $U=3Nk_{\rm B}T/2$. Interactions and additional molecular modes require extensions.
