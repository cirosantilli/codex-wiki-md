# Classical risk model

↑ **Parent:** [Actuarial statistics](actuarial-statistics-split.md)

The classical risk model has surplus $U_t=u+ct-\sum_{j=1}^{N_t}X_j$, where $N_t$ is a [Poisson process](poisson-process.md) of rate $\lambda$, independent claim sizes are positive, and premiums flow in at constant rate $c$. The [relative safety loading](relative-safety-loading.md) is $\rho=c/(\lambda\mathbb EX_1)-1$. Ruin is the first time surplus becomes negative.
