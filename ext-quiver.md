# Ext quiver

↑ **Parent:** [Basic algebra](basic-algebra.md)

For a finite-dimensional [associative algebra](associative-algebra-split.md) over an [algebraically closed field](algebraically-closed-field.md), the [Ext quiver](ext-quiver.md) has a vertex for each [simple module](irreducible-module.md) isomorphism class and $a_{ij}$ arrows $i\to j$. For a [basic algebra](basic-algebra.md) with $P_i=Ae_i$, applying $\operatorname{Hom}_A(-,S_j)$ to the projective-cover sequence gives $a_{ij}=\dim e_j(J/J^2)e_i$. Our [path algebra](path-algebra.md) convention multiplies consecutive arrows $\alpha:i\to j$, $\beta:j\to l$ as $\beta\alpha$, making left modules have arrow maps $V_i\to V_j$. The orientation convention must be stated when comparing other definitions.
