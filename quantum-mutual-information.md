# Quantum mutual information

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum relative entropy](quantum-relative-entropy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_mutual_information)

The quantum mutual information of a bipartite state is

$$
I(A:B)_\rho
=D(\rho_{AB}\|\rho_A\otimes\rho_B)
=S(\rho_A)+S(\rho_B)-S(\rho_{AB}).
$$

Its nonnegativity is equivalent to [Subadditivity of Von Neumann entropy](subadditivity-of-von-neumann-entropy.md).
