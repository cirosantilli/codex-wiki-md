# Small forcing preservation of finite-subset partition properties

↑ **Parent:** [Finite-subset partition property](finite-subset-partition-property.md)

If inaccessible $\lambda$ has the finite-subset partition property for every color number below $\lambda$, forcing of size below $\lambda$ preserves its two-color version. Color ground-model finite sets by their full [forcing decision patterns](forcing-decision-pattern.md); strong-limit cardinal arithmetic bounds the number of patterns.
