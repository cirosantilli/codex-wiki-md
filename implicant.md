# Implicant

↑ **Parent:** [Boolean function](boolean-function.md)

A [logical conjunction](logical-conjunction.md) $T$ of [literals](boolean-literal.md) is an implicant of a [Boolean function](boolean-function.md) $f$ if every [Boolean valuation](boolean-valuation.md) making $T$ true also makes $f$ true. Every term in a [disjunctive normal form](disjunctive-normal-form.md) is an [implicant](implicant.md) of the represented [Boolean function](boolean-function.md). To rule out a one-[literal](boolean-literal.md) term, it suffices to find a false [Boolean valuation](boolean-valuation.md) of $f$ where that [literal](boolean-literal.md) is true.
