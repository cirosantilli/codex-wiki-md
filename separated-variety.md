# Separated variety

↑ **Parent:** [Separated scheme](separated-scheme.md)

An [algebraic variety](algebraic-variety.md) that is separated over its ground field. Two affine open charts have an affine intersection: their intersection is the inverse image of the closed diagonal inside the product of the two [affine charts](affine-chart-of-a-variety.md). This ensures a finite affine cover is acyclic for [quasi-coherent sheaves](quasi-coherent-sheaf.md).
