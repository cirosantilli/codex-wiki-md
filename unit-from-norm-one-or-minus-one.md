# Unit from norm one or minus one

↑ **Parent:** [Field norm](field-norm.md)

For a [number field](number-field.md) $K$ and an [algebraic integer](algebraic-integer.md) $\alpha\in\mathcal O_K$, the criterion is

$$
\alpha\in\mathcal O_K^\times\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad N_{K/\mathbb Q}(\alpha)=\pm1.
$$

Here the absolute field norm means the [field norm](field-norm.md) down to $\mathbb Q$; its [absolute value](absolute-value.md) is one. Necessity follows from $N(\alpha)N(\alpha^{-1})=1$ with both factors [integers](integer-split.md). Conversely, multiplication by $\alpha$ on an [integral basis](integral-basis.md) is a [matrix](matrix.md) with [integer](integer-split.md) entries and [determinant](determinant.md) $\pm1$. Its inverse also has [integer](integer-split.md) entries, and applying that inverse to $1$ gives $\alpha^{-1}\in\mathcal O_K$. The integrality hypothesis matters: $\alpha=(3+\sqrt2)/(3-\sqrt2)=(11+6\sqrt2)/7$ has field norm one, but is not in $\mathbb Z[\sqrt2]$.

In $\mathbb Z[\sqrt2]$, the criterion becomes $a^2-2b^2=\pm1$. Thus $(1+\sqrt2)^{-1}=\sqrt2-1$ and $(3+2\sqrt2)^{-1}=3-2\sqrt2$ are again [algebraic integers](algebraic-integer.md). These [units](unit-in-a-ring.md) produce infinite families of solutions to the [Pell equation](pell-equation.md).
