# Lenard-Magri recursion

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson pencil](poisson-pencil.md)

Start from a [Casimir function](casimir-function-of-a-poisson-manifold.md) $H_0$ of the first [Poisson structure](poisson-structure.md). Whenever possible, choose $H_n$ whose first [Hamiltonian vector field](hamiltonian-vector-field.md) is minus the second [Hamiltonian vector field](hamiltonian-vector-field.md) of $H_{n-1}$. A sequence extending indefinitely is the [coefficient](coefficient.md) sequence of a formal [Casimir function](casimir-function-of-a-poisson-manifold.md) for the [Poisson pencil](poisson-pencil.md). Its [functions](function-split.md) pairwise commute for both [Poisson brackets](poisson-bracket.md). Existence at each stage is an additional solvability requirement; compatibility alone does not guarantee it.
