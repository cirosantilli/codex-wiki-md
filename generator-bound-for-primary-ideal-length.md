# Generator bound for primary-ideal length

↑ **Parent:** [Krull height theorem](krull-height-theorem.md)

If $I$ is an $\mathfrak m$-primary [ideal](ideal.md) generated by $n>0$ elements in a [Noetherian local ring](noetherian-local-ring.md) and $L=\operatorname{length}(R/I)$, then $I^j/I^{j+1}$ is a quotient of $\binom{j+n-1}{n-1}$ copies of $R/I$. Summing their lengths gives the displayed bound. Since $I\subseteq\mathfrak m$, it also bounds $\operatorname{length}(R/\mathfrak m^t)$. Together with the [prime-chain lower bound for local length](prime-chain-lower-bound-for-local-length.md), localization at a minimal prime proves the [Krull height theorem](krull-height-theorem.md).
