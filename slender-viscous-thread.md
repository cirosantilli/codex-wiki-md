# Slender viscous thread

↑ **Parent:** [Lubrication theory](lubrication-theory.md)

A slender viscous thread has a cross-sectional scale much smaller than its axial variation scale. For an axisymmetric Newtonian thread of area $A$ and nearly uniform axial velocity $w$, [mass conservation](mass-conservation.md) and the [Trouton ratio](trouton-ratio.md) give

$$
A_t+(Aw)_z=0,
\qquad
\rho gA+\partial_z(3\mu A w_z)=0
$$

when inertia and [surface tension](surface-tension.md) are negligible and $z$ points downward.
