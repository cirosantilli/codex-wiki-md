# Wild inertia group

↑ **Parent:** [Inertia group](inertia-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wild_inertia_group)

The wild inertia group is the first [ramification group](ramification-group.md) $G_1$. It is the unique Sylow subgroup of the [inertia group](inertia-group.md) for the residue characteristic.
