# Exponential cardinality supermartingale for the contact process on a tree

↑ **Parent:** [Contact process](contact-process.md)

For the [contact process](contact-process.md) with per-[edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) infection rate $\lambda$ and recovery rate one on a degree-$(d+1)$ [regular tree](regular-tree.md), the [infinitesimal generator](infinitesimal-generator-stochastic-processes.md) gives $L\nu_\rho(A)=(1-\rho)\nu_\rho(A)(|A|/\rho-\lambda|\partial_E A|)$. The [edge boundary of a finite forest in a regular tree](edge-boundary-of-a-finite-forest-in-a-regular-tree.md) implies $L\nu_\rho\le0$ whenever $\rho\lambda(d-1)\ge1$, including the absorbing empty state where the generator is zero. Thus the displayed functional is a bounded [supermartingale](supermartingale.md), and the extinction [probability](probability.md) from $A$ is at most $\rho^{|A|}$.
