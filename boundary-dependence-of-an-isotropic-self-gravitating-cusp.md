# Boundary dependence of an isotropic self-gravitating cusp

↑ **Parent:** [Spherical Jeans equation](spherical-jeans-equation.md)

For an isotropic self-gravitating [collisionless stellar system](collisionless-stellar-system.md) with $k<3$ and no central point [mass](mass.md), $M(r)=4\pi Ar^{3-k}/(3-k)$. Integrating the [Spherical Jeans equation](spherical-jeans-equation.md) to an outer radius $b$ gives the displayed pressure, with $P_b=\rho(b)\sigma_r^2(b)$. For $k<1$ the inner pressure is set by outer radii and $\sigma_r^2\sim Cr^k$, with $C$ depending on the boundary. An unbounded pure power law has divergent pressure in this case. For $1<k<3$, inner radii dominate and $\sigma_r^2\sim2\pi GA r^{2-k}/[(3-k)(k-1)]$. At $k=1$ a logarithm appears. For $k\geq3$ the enclosed central [mass](mass.md) diverges unless the density law is modified at small radii.
