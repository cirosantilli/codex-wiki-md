# Screened sinusoidal surface-charge mode

↑ **Parent:** [Debye–Hückel approximation](debye-huckel-approximation.md)

A lateral sinusoidal [surface charge density](surface-charge-density.md) with wave number $k$ in the [Debye–Hückel approximation](debye-huckel-approximation.md) generates vertical exponential or hyperbolic functions with inverse length $p=\sqrt{k^2+\kappa_D^2}$. In a homogeneous electrolyte filling all space, an isolated sheet at $z_s$ produces $\phi=\sigma_k(x)e^{-p|z-z_s|}/(2\varepsilon p)$. Boundary conditions determine the normalization when the electrolyte occupies only a gap.
