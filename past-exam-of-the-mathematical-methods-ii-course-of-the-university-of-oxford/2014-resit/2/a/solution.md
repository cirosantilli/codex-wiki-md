<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Multiply the convergent [Taylor series](../../../../taylor-series.md) $\cos x=1-x^2/2+x^4/24+\cdots$ and $\sinh y=y+y^3/6+y^5/120+\cdots$. Keeping all terms through total degree five gives

$$
\boxed{\cos x\sinh y=y+\frac{y^3}{6}-\frac{x^2y}{2}+\frac{y^5}{120}-\frac{x^2y^3}{12}+\frac{x^4y}{24}+O((|x|+|y|)^7).}
$$

There are six nonzero [monomials](../../../../monomial.md) displayed, so the answer does not select only part of the tied total-degree-five terms when asking for at least four terms. Both original series converge for every finite real $x,y$.
