<h1 id="1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

For a continuously differentiable [vector field](../../../vector-field.md) on a bounded region with suitably piecewise smooth boundary, the [divergence theorem](../../../divergence-theorem.md) gives

$$
\oint_S\mathbf F\cdot\hat{\mathbf n}\,dS=\int_V\nabla\cdot\mathbf F\,dV,
$$

where the [normal vector](../../../normal-vector.md) points outward. In three dimensions, the [position vector](../../../position-vector.md) is $\mathbf r=(x,y,z)$ and $\nabla\cdot\mathbf r=1+1+1=3$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\oint_S\mathbf r\cdot d\mathbf S=3V.}
$$

The result depends only on the enclosed [volume](../../../volume.md), not on the shape or its location relative to the origin. Reversing the surface orientation reverses the sign.
