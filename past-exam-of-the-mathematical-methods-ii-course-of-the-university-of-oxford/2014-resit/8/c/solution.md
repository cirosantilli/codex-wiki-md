<h1 id="8/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A symmetric quadratic form has twice its off-diagonal entry as the coefficient of the mixed product. Comparing with the preceding [potential energy](../../../../potential-energy.md) and [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) expressions gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbf V=mgl\begin{pmatrix}4&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad \mathbf T=ml^2\begin{pmatrix}8&2\\2&1\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

Here $\mathbf V$ is the stiffness matrix and $\mathbf T$ the mass matrix in angular coordinates; their units differ accordingly. The dimensionless mass-matrix determinant is $8-4=4>0$ and its first diagonal entry is positive, so the [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) is positive for every nonzero angular velocity.
