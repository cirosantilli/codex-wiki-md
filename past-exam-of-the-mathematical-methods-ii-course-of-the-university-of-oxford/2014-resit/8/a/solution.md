<h1 id="8/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Measure each rod angle from the downward vertical, as in the held diagram. The upper mass rises by $2l(1-\cos\theta_1)$; the lower mass rises by $2l(1-\cos\theta_1)+l(1-\cos\theta_2)$. With the hanging equilibrium as the zero of [gravitational potential energy](../../../../gravitational-energy.md),

$$
U=mgl[4(1-\cos\theta_1)+(1-\cos\theta_2)]\simeq\boxed{\frac{mgl}{2}(4\theta_1^2+\theta_2^2).}
$$

The retained expression is quadratic in the small displacements. Both masses contribute to the upper rod's gravitational term.
