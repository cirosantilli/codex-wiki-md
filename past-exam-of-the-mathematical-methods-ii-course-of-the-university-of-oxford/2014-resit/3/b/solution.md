<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Orient the rectangle with [normal vector](../../../../normal-vector.md) $+\hat{\mathbf z}$. The [surface integral](../../../../surface-integral.md) of the [curl](../../../../curl.md) is an ordinary planar integral:

$$
I=\int_0^a\int_0^b4y\,dy\,dx=\boxed{2ab^2.}
$$

If the opposite orientation is chosen, both this flux and the corresponding boundary [line integral](../../../../line-integral.md) change sign.
