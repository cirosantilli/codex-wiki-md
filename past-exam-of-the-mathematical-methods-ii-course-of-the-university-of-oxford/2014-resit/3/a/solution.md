<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Stokes theorem](../../../../stokes-theorem.md) equates the circulation of a continuously differentiable [vector field](../../../../vector-field.md) around an oriented boundary to the flux of its [curl](../../../../curl.md) across a spanning oriented [surface](../../../../topological-surface-split.md):

$$
\oint_{\partial S}\mathbf A\cdot d\mathbf r=\int_S(\nabla\times\mathbf A)\cdot\hat{\mathbf n}\,dS.
$$

The boundary direction follows the [right-hand rule](../../../../right-hand-rule.md) relative to the [normal vector](../../../../normal-vector.md). For the polynomial [vector field](../../../../vector-field.md), only the $z$ component of the [curl](../../../../curl.md) can be nonzero, and

$$
\boxed{\nabla\times\mathbf A=\left(\frac{\partial(2xy)}{\partial x}-\frac{\partial(x^2-y^2)}{\partial y}\right)\hat{\mathbf z}=4y\hat{\mathbf z}.}
$$
