<h1 id="11/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write $\boldsymbol\omega=(\omega_x,\omega_y,\omega_z)$. The [cross product](../../../../cross-product.md) gives $\mathbf v=(\omega_yz-\omega_zy,\omega_zx-\omega_xz,\omega_xy-\omega_yx)$. Since the components of $\boldsymbol\omega$ are constant, direct [partial differentiation](../../../../partial-derivative.md) gives

$$
\nabla\times\mathbf v=(\omega_x-(-\omega_x),\omega_y-(-\omega_y),\omega_z-(-\omega_z))=2\boldsymbol\omega.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{\boldsymbol\omega=\tfrac12\nabla\times\mathbf v.}
$$

The [vorticity](../../../../vorticity-split.md) is twice the local angular velocity of this rigid-body rotation.
