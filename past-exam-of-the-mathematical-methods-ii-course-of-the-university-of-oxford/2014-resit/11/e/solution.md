<h1 id="11/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The only contributions to the [Laplacian](../../../../laplacian.md) come from the pure quadratic terms. Hence

$$
\boxed{\nabla^2\phi=1+1-2=0.}
$$

The scalar potential is therefore a [harmonic function](../../../../harmonic-function.md). Since $\mathbf F=\nabla\phi$,

$$
\boxed{\nabla\cdot\mathbf F=\nabla\cdot\nabla\phi=0.}
$$

Thus this field is both [irrotational](../../../../irrotational-vector-field.md) and divergence-free. By the [divergence theorem](../../../../divergence-theorem.md), its net flux through every sufficiently regular closed surface is zero.
