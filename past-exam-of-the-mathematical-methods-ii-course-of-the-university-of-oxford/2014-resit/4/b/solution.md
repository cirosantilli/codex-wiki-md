<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

On bounded intervals the [exponential series](../../../../exponential-series.md) converges uniformly, allowing termwise [integration](../../../../integral-split.md). Since $e^{-t^2}=1-t^2+t^4/2+O(t^6)$,

$$
I(x)=\frac2{\sqrt\pi}\left(x-\frac{x^3}{3}+\frac{x^5}{10}+O(x^7)\right).
$$

The requested first two nonzero terms are therefore

$$
\boxed{I(x)=\frac{2x}{\sqrt\pi}-\frac{2x^3}{3\sqrt\pi}+O(x^5).}
$$

The function is the [error function](../../../../error-function.md), with $I(0)=0$ and derivative $2e^{-x^2}/\sqrt\pi$.
