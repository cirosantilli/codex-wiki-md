<h1 id="10/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a short string element $dx$, small slope makes its tension magnitude approximately constant and its vertical component approximately $Ty_x$. The net vertical force is $T[y_x(x+dx,t)-y_x(x,t)]\simeq Ty_{xx}dx$. Its [mass](../../../../mass.md) is $\rho\,dx$. [Newton's second law](../../../../newton-s-second-law.md) gives

$$
\rho\,dx\,y_{tt}=Ty_{xx}\,dx,\qquad \boxed{y_{tt}-c^2y_{xx}=0,\quad c=\sqrt{T/\rho}.}
$$

This assumes uniform equilibrium tension, small slopes, negligible bending stiffness and dissipation. The symbol $\rho$ is linear mass density here, not volume density.
