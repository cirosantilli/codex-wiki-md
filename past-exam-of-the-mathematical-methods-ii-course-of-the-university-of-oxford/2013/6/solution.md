<h1 id="6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

The [change of variables](../../../change-of-variables-formula.md) has inverse $x=(u+3v)/7$, $y=(v-2u)/7$, with absolute inverse [Jacobian determinant](../../../jacobian-determinant.md) $1/7$. The two lines $y=-2x+1, -2x+4$ become $v=1,4$, and $y=x/3,(x-7)/3$ become $u=0,7$. Hence the parallelogram maps to a rectangle and

$$
\boxed{\iint_D\frac{x-3y}{2x+y}\,dx\,dy=\frac17\int_0^7\int_1^4\frac uv\,dv\,du=\frac72\ln4=7\ln2.}
$$

The denominator $v$ is bounded away from zero throughout the rectangle, so this transformation introduces no improper-integral issue.
