<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [gradient](../../../../gradient.md) is $\nabla T=2(x,y)/(x^2+y^2)$ and at $(1,2)$ it is $(2/5,4/5)$. The [directional derivative](../../../../directional-derivative.md) along a unit vector is maximal in the gradient direction. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\hat{\mathbf n}_{\rm increase}=\frac{(1,2)}{\sqrt5},\qquad |\nabla T|=\frac2{\sqrt5}.}
$$

The direction is radially outward from the origin, perpendicular to the circular [level curve](../../../../level-curve.md) through the point.
