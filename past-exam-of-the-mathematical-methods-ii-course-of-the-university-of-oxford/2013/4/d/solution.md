<h1 id="4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

A stationary direction at the point has zero [directional derivative](../../../../directional-derivative.md), hence is perpendicular to $(1,2)$. The two opposite unit directions are

$$
\boxed{\hat{\mathbf t}=\pm\frac{(2,-1)}{\sqrt5}.}
$$

They are tangents to the circular [level curve](../../../../level-curve.md). “Stationary” here means zero first-order change. A finite straight move in a tangent direction gives squared radius $5+s^2$ and therefore a positive second-order change $\ln(1+s^2/5)$; following the curved level set instead keeps $T$ exactly constant.
