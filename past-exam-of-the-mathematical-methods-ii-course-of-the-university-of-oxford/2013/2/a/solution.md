<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Put $h=x-\pi/2$. Periodicity gives $\tan(2x)=\tan(\pi+2h)=\tan(2h)$. The local [Taylor expansion](../../../../taylor-expansion.md) $\tan(2h)=2h+O(h^3)$ therefore yields

$$
\boxed{\lim_{x\to\pi/2}\frac{\tan(2x)}{x-\pi/2}=2.}
$$

Although $\tan x$ is singular at $\pi/2$, the numerator here is $\tan(2x)$, which is smooth and zero there.
