<h1 id="10/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a nonzero plane-wave amplitude, substituting $u=Ae^{i(kx-\omega t+\theta_0)}$ gives $u_{tt}=-\omega^2u$ and $u_{xx}=-k^2u$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\omega^2=c^2k^2+a,\qquad\omega(k)=\sqrt{c^2k^2+a}\text{ on the positive-frequency branch}.}
$$

This [dispersion relation](../../../../dispersion-relation-split.md) is not proportionality between frequency and wavenumber: for $a>0$, the phase speed depends on $k$. Thus a superposition generally experiences [dispersion](../../../../wave-dispersion.md). The negative-frequency branch is its opposite; $A=0$ is the trivial solution and imposes no constraint on the unused phase parameters.
