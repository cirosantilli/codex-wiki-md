<h1 id="10/g/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [G](../g.md)

For a medium with constant coefficients, $\bar\omega=\omega(\bar k)$ on the selected branch. The spatial [chain rule](../../../../chain-rule.md) gives $\bar\omega_x=(d\omega/dk)_{\bar k}\bar k_x=c_g(\bar k)\bar k_x$. Thus wave conservation becomes

$$
\boxed{\bar k_t+c_g(\bar k)\bar k_x=0.}
$$

Along a [characteristic curve](../../../../characteristic-curve.md) with $dx/dt=c_g(\bar k)$, the total derivative $d\bar k/dt$ vanishes. A slowly varying wavenumber feature, and hence the corresponding modulation of a narrow-band packet, travels with the group velocity rather than the carrier's phase velocity. This interpretation assumes the modulation remains smooth on the scale required in part (e).
