<h1 id="10/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Differentiate the positive-frequency [dispersion relation](../../../../dispersion-relation-split.md) with respect to $k$:

$$
\boxed{c_g=\frac{d\omega}{dk}=\frac{c^2k}{\sqrt{c^2k^2+a}}=\frac{c^2k}{\omega}.}
$$

Where $k\ne0$, $c_pc_g=c^2$. For $a>0$ and $c\ne0$, the magnitude of the [group velocity](../../../../group-velocity.md) is below $|c|$ even though the positive-$k$ [phase velocity](../../../../phase-velocity.md) exceeds $|c|$. At $k=0$, the group velocity is zero. The two velocities describe envelope and phase transport, respectively.
