<h1 id="9/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

There is no contradiction. The domain on which the field is smooth is the [punctured plane](../../../../punctured-complex-plane.md), which is not simply connected. A loop enclosing the origin cannot be spanned by a planar region entirely inside that domain, so the smoothness hypothesis needed to apply [Green's theorem](../../../../green-theorem.md) to its full interior fails. A loop that bounds a region avoiding the origin does have zero circulation.

Locally the field is the gradient of the [polar angle](../../../../polar-angle.md), but that angle cannot be made a single-valued continuous potential on the whole [punctured plane](../../../../punctured-complex-plane.md). The nonzero period $2\pi$ around the hole measures this global obstruction, despite the field having zero local curl.
