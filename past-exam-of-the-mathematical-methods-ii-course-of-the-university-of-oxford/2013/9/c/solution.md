<h1 id="9/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Along a path avoiding the origin, polar coordinates give

$$
\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r=\frac{-y\,dx+x\,dy}{x^2+y^2}=d\theta,
$$

where the angle is followed continuously along the path. For a closed path its total change is an integer number of turns, so

$$
\boxed{\oint_C\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r=2\pi\operatorname{wind}(C,0).}
$$

For a simple counterclockwise loop, the value is $2\pi$ if the origin lies inside and zero if it lies outside. Clockwise orientation reverses the nonzero sign. The [winding number](../../../../winding-number.md) formula also handles repeated or self-intersecting loops. A curve passing through the origin is outside the ordinary line integral's domain.
