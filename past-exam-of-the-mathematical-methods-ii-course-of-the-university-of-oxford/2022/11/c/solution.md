<h1 id="11/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For the supplied system, the small-$\alpha$ [Taylor expansion](../../../../taylor-expansion.md) gives $\sqrt{1+2\alpha+5\alpha^2}=1+\alpha+O(\alpha^2)$, hence $\omega_-^2=\alpha g/l+O(\alpha^2g/l)$. Setting $\omega_-=(1/4)\sqrt{g/l}$ at this order yields

$$
\boxed{\alpha\simeq\frac1{16}.}
$$

This is an approximation in the squared [frequency](../../../../frequency.md). The exact positive solution of $\lambda_-=1/16$ is $\alpha=(\sqrt{229}-13)/32\simeq0.06665$, showing the size of the neglected correction. The physical unequal-mass [double pendulum](../../../../double-pendulum.md) has a different small-mass limit, so the source's requested tuning does not follow for that model.
