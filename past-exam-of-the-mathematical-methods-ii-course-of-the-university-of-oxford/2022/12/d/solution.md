<h1 id="12/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Substitute the three nonzero initial-[velocity](../../../../velocity.md) coefficients into the general [Fourier sine series](../../../../fourier-sine-series.md), with $\omega_1=\pi c/L$:

$$
\boxed{y(x,t)=\frac{V_0}{\omega_1}\left[
\sin\frac{\pi x}{L}\sin\omega_1t+
\frac14\sin\frac{2\pi x}{L}\sin2\omega_1t+
\frac29\sin\frac{3\pi x}{L}\sin3\omega_1t\right].}
$$

Every term satisfies the [wave equation](../../../../wave-equation-split.md) and the endpoint conditions. Differentiating in time at zero recovers the three coefficients $V_0,V_0/2,2V_0/3$, providing a direct initial-data check.
