<h1 id="12/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Assume $V_0\ne0$. [Linear independence](../../../../linear-independence.md) of the [Fourier sine basis](../../../../fourier-sine-basis.md) makes a flat string equivalent to vanishing of all three temporal coefficients. The fundamental requires $\sin(\omega_1t)=0$, which automatically zeros the other two. Thus the earliest positive return is

$$
\boxed{t_{\rm flat}=\frac{\pi}{\omega_1}=\frac Lc.}
$$

The [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) need not have returned to its initial value at that time; full recurrence of the displacement-and-velocity data occurs at $2L/c$.

A nonzero pure fundamental profile would require $\sin2\omega_1t=\sin3\omega_1t=0$. The common zeros have $\omega_1t$ an integer multiple of $\pi$, so the fundamental vanishes too. A pure second or third harmonic would already require the fundamental to vanish, again making the whole string flat. **The string never has a nonzero pure sine profile.** If $V_0=0$, it remains identically zero and there is no earliest positive isolated return.
