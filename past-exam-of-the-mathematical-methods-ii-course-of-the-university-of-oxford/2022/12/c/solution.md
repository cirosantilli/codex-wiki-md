<h1 id="12/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The product-to-sum identity gives $V_1=V_0$, $V_2=V_0/2$ and $V_3=2V_0/3$, with all other modal coefficients zero. If $\mu$ is the string's [linear mass density](../../../../linear-mass-density.md), the initial [potential energy](../../../../potential-energy.md) vanishes and [orthogonality](../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) gives the [modal energy of a string](../../../../modal-energy-of-a-string.md)

$$
E_n=\frac\mu2\int_0^LV_n^2\sin^2\frac{n\pi x}{L}\,dx=\frac{\mu L}{4}V_n^2.
$$

Consequently $\boxed{E_1:E_2:E_3=36:9:16}$ for $V_0\ne0$. **The fundamental mode carries the most energy**, with fraction $36/61$. Comparing displacement coefficients without their [frequency](../../../../frequency.md) factors would give the wrong energy test.
