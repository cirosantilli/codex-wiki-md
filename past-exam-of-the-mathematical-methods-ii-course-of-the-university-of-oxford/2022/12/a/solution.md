<h1 id="12/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use [separation of variables](../../../../separation-of-variables.md), $y=X(x)T(t)$. A nontrivial separated solution satisfies $X''/X=T''/(c^2T)=-k^2$. Fixed endpoints force $X(0)=X(L)=0$, so $X=\sin(n\pi x/L)$, $n=1,2,\ldots$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\omega_n=\frac{n\pi c}{L},\qquad f_n=\frac{nc}{2L}.}
$$

Here $c>0$ is the wave [speed](../../../../speed.md). Zero or negative $k^2$ gives no nonzero fixed-end spatial solution. Each time coefficient is a [simple harmonic oscillator](../../../../simple-harmonic-motion.md).
