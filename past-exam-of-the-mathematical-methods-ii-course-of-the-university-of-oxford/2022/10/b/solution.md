<h1 id="10/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [divergence](../../../../divergence.md) of $\mathbf F=\nabla z$ is the [Laplacian](../../../../laplacian.md)

$$
\nabla\cdot\mathbf F=6xy(x^2+y^2-8).
$$

Its area [integral](../../../../integral-split.md) over the square is

$$
\int_0^2\int_0^2 6xy(x^2+y^2-8)\,dx\,dy
=6(8+8-32)=\boxed{-96}.
$$

For the boundary [flux](../../../../flux.md), $\int_0^2A(s)\,ds=-4$. On $x=0$, $\mathbf F\cdot\mathbf n=4A(y)$, giving $-16$; on $x=2$ it is $8A(y)$, giving $-32$. The corresponding horizontal sides give $-16$ and $-32$. The total is again $-96$, verifying the [divergence theorem](../../../../divergence-theorem.md) with outward normals. With a counterclockwise boundary, this is the same orientation as $M\,dy-N\,dx$.
