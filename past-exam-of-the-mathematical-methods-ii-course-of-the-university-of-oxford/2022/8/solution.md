<h1 id="8/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [8](../8.md)

For positive [wavenumber](../../../wavenumber.md), the condition gives $\omega=Ck^{-n/2}$ on the positive-[frequency](../../../frequency.md) branch. The [group velocity](../../../group-velocity.md) and [phase velocity](../../../phase-velocity.md) are consequently related by

$$
v_g=\frac{d\omega}{dk}=-\frac n2\frac\omega k=-\frac n2v_p.
$$

The required positive ratio $v_g/v_p=2$ gives $\boxed{n=-4}$. Isotropy makes these [velocities](../../../velocity.md) parallel to the wave vector when this coefficient is positive. Identifying [group velocity](../../../group-velocity.md) with [energy](../../../energy-split.md) transport assumes the usual lossless narrowband [wave packet](../../../wave-packet.md) regime.
