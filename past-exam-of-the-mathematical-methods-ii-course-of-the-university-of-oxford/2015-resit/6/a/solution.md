↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [divergence theorem](../../../../divergence-theorem.md) states that, for a sufficiently smooth [vector field](../../../../vector-field.md) $\mathbf F$ on a volume $\Omega$ with piecewise smooth closed boundary $\partial\Omega$,

$$
\boxed{\oiint_{\partial\Omega}\mathbf F\cdot\hat{\mathbf n}\,dS=\iiint_\Omega\nabla\cdot\mathbf F\,dV}.
$$

Here $\hat{\mathbf n}$ is the outward unit normal. The left-hand side is the total outward [flux](../../../../flux.md); the right-hand side adds the local [divergence](../../../../divergence.md) throughout the volume. The orientation and the requirement to include the complete closed boundary are essential.
