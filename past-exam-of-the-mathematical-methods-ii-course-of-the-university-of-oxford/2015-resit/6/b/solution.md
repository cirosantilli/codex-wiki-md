<h1 id="6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Read the given [vector field](../../../../vector-field.md) as the three-dimensional field $\mathbf F=(x,y,0)$. Its [divergence](../../../../divergence.md) is the sum of the three matching-coordinate [partial derivatives](../../../../partial-derivative.md):

$$
\boxed{\nabla\cdot\mathbf F=\frac{\partial x}{\partial x}+\frac{\partial y}{\partial y}+\frac{\partial0}{\partial z}=2}.
$$

The absence of a $z$ component does not make the three-dimensional [flux](../../../../flux.md) vanish: the field still flows outward through surface normals with horizontal components.
