<h1 id="11/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Seek a separated [normal mode](../../../../normal-mode.md) $y(x,t)=X(x)Q(t)$. Substitution in the [wave equation](../../../../wave-equation-split.md) gives $XQ''=c^2X''Q$. For an oscillatory mode choose the separation constant $-\omega^2$:

$$
Q''+\omega^2Q=0,\qquad\boxed{X''+k^2X=0,\qquad k=\omega/c}.
$$

The fixed endpoints impose $X(0)=X(l)=0$. In $X=A\sin kx+B\cos kx$, the first condition gives $B=0$, and the second requires $\sin kl=0$. Nonzero displacement therefore requires

$$
\boxed{k_n=\frac{n\pi}{l},\quad\omega_n=\frac{n\pi c}{l},\quad f_n=\frac{nc}{2l},\qquad n=1,2,3,\ldots}.
$$

Each spatial shape $\sin(n\pi x/l)$ has fixed nodes and an oscillating overall amplitude: a [standing wave](../../../../standing-wave.md). A general lossless motion is a superposition of these standing [normal modes](../../../../normal-mode.md), not necessarily one single-frequency standing wave. The $n=0$ solution is trivial under both fixed-end conditions and does not supply an additional vibrational mode.
