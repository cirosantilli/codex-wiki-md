<h1 id="8/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Embed the planar [vector field](../../../../vector-field.md) as $\mathbf V=(A(x,y),B(x,y),0)$, independent of $z$. Applying the Cartesian [curl](../../../../curl.md) formula gives

$$
\nabla\times\mathbf V=\left(0,0,\frac{\partial B}{\partial x}-\frac{\partial A}{\partial y}\right).
$$

Consequently, for continuously differentiable components,

$$
\boxed{\nabla\times\mathbf V=0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad B_x=A_y}.
$$

This is the local closedness condition for the one-form $A\,dx+B\,dy$. To infer global [path independence](../../../../path-independence.md) from this condition alone, the domain also matters: it suffices that the planar domain is a [simply connected space](../../../../simply-connected-space.md). The polynomial field in the next calculation is defined on the whole plane, so there is no missing-hole obstruction.
