<h1 id="10/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The plane cuts the positive coordinate axes at $(3,0,0)$, $(0,3,0)$ and $(0,0,3/2)$. Choose its [unit normal vector](../../../../../unit-normal.md) $(1,1,2)/\sqrt6$, pointing away from the origin. In the parametrization $(x,3-x-2z,z)$, the [vector surface element](../../../../../vector-area-element.md) for this orientation is $(1,1,2)\,dx\,dz$. Thus

$$
\mathbf A\cdot d\mathbf S=(x,-x,z)\cdot(1,1,2)\,dx\,dz=2z\,dx\,dz,
$$

and the triangular projection gives

$$
\boxed{\iint_S\mathbf A\cdot d\mathbf S=\int_0^3\int_0^{(3-x)/2}2z\,dz\,dx=\frac94.}
$$

For the opposite orientation the answer is $-9/4$. The normal direction must be stated because this open surface has no prescribed orientation in the question.
