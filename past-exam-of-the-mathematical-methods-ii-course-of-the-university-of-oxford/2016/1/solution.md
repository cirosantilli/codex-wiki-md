<h1 id="1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

The [arc length](../../../arc-length.md) of this [parametric curve](../../../parametric-curve.md) is the integral of its speed. [Differentiation](../../../differentiation.md) gives $x'(t)=at\cos t$ and $y'(t)=at\sin t$, so

$$
\sqrt{x'(t)^2+y'(t)^2}=|a|t\qquad(0\leq t\leq1),\qquad \boxed{\ell=\int_0^1|a|t\,dt=\frac{|a|}{2}.}
$$

For a positive scale parameter this is $a/2$. The absolute value is necessary when $a$ is allowed to be any real constant; $a=0$ gives a degenerate curve of zero length. The nondegenerate curve is an [involute](../../../involute.md) of a circle.
