<h1 id="8/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a continuously differentiable [vector field](../../../../vector-field.md) $\mathbf F$ near a piecewise smooth [oriented surface](../../../../oriented-surface.md) $S$, [Stokes theorem](../../../../stokes-theorem.md) states

$$
\boxed{\oint_{\partial S}\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r=\iint_S(\nabla\times\mathbf F)\cdot\hat{\mathbf n}\,dS.}
$$

The [circulation](../../../../circulation-physics.md) around the whole boundary equals the [flux](../../../../flux.md) of the [curl](../../../../curl.md) through the surface. The [unit normal vector](../../../../unit-normal.md) and boundary direction must be compatible with the [right-hand rule](../../../../right-hand-rule.md): curling the right-hand fingers in the boundary direction points the thumb along the normal. Reversing both orientations reverses both integrals.
