<h1 id="11/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The zero initial [displacement](../../../../displacement.md) selects sine time factors. Put $\omega_1=\pi c/L$. The [Fourier sine series](../../../../fourier-sine-series.md) of the initial [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) has coefficients $V_1=V_0/2$, $V_2=-3V_0/4$, with all other coefficients zero. Since differentiation in time multiplies a sine by its [angular frequency](../../../../angular-frequency.md),

$$
\boxed{y(x,t)=\frac{V_0}{2\omega_1}\sin\left(\frac{\pi x}{L}\right)\sin(\omega_1t)-\frac{3V_0}{8\omega_1}\sin\left(\frac{2\pi x}{L}\right)\sin(2\omega_1t).}
$$

The [normal mode coordinates](../../../../normal-coordinate.md) are $q_1(t)=V_0\sin(\omega_1t)/(2\omega_1)$ and $q_2(t)=-3V_0\sin(2\omega_1t)/(8\omega_1)$. Their peak displacement [amplitudes](../../../../wave-amplitude.md) are $|V_0|/(2\omega_1)$ and $3|V_0|/(8\omega_1)$. Both the initial [displacement](../../../../displacement.md) and the initial [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) can be checked directly from this expression.
