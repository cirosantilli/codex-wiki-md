<h1 id="11/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For either [normal mode](../../../../normal-mode.md), let $k_n=n\pi/L$ and write its contribution as $B_n\sin(k_nx)\sin(\omega_nt)$. The product-to-sum identity gives

$$
\boxed{B_n\sin(k_nx)\sin(\omega_nt)=\frac{B_n}{2}\left[\cos(k_nx-\omega_nt)-\cos(k_nx+\omega_nt)\right].}
$$

This [standing wave](../../../../standing-wave.md) is a [superposition](../../../../superposition-principle.md) of two [travelling waves](../../../../travelling-wave.md) of equal [amplitude](../../../../wave-amplitude.md) $|B_n|/2$, moving oppositely with [phase velocity](../../../../phase-velocity.md) $\omega_n/k_n=c$. Their cancellation at $x=0,L$ enforces the fixed-end [boundary conditions](../../../../boundary-condition.md) at every time.
