<h1 id="11/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The fixed-end [boundary conditions](../../../../boundary-condition.md) require $f(0)=f(L)=0$. For $\alpha=0$, the spatial solution is linear and both endpoints force it to vanish; for $\alpha>0$, the exponential solutions likewise cannot satisfy both conditions nontrivially. Write $\alpha=-k^2$ with $k>0$. Then $f=A\sin(kx)$ and $\sin(kL)=0$, giving

$$
\boxed{\alpha_n=-\left(\frac{n\pi}{L}\right)^2,\qquad f_n(x)=A\sin\left(\frac{n\pi x}{L}\right),\qquad n=1,2,\ldots.}
$$

Their [angular frequencies](../../../../angular-frequency.md) are $\omega_n=cn\pi/L$. Unlike the freely translating chain, this fixed string has no translational zero [normal mode](../../../../normal-mode.md).
