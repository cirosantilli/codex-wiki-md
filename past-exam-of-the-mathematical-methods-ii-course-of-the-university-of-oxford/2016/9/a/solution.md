<h1 id="9/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Each spring [force](../../../../force-split.md) is its extension multiplied by the [spring constant](../../../../spring-constant.md), acting to restore the [equilibrium](../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md). The [equations of motion](../../../../equation-of-motion.md) are

$$
\boxed{m\ddot x_1=k(x_2-x_1),\qquad M\ddot x_2=k(x_1-2x_2+x_3),\qquad m\ddot x_3=k(x_2-x_3).}
$$

No end is attached to a wall. The spring extensions are $x_2-x_1$ and $x_3-x_2$, giving [potential energy](../../../../potential-energy.md) $k[(x_2-x_1)^2+(x_3-x_2)^2]/2$.
