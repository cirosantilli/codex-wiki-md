<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Maclaurin series](../../../../maclaurin-series.md) $\sin x=x-x^3/6+O(x^5)$ cancels the numerator's linear term, leaving

$$
\frac{\sin x-x}{x^3}=-\frac16+O(x^2).
$$

Thus $\boxed{\lim_{x\to0}(\sin x-x)/x^3=-1/6}$. The same expansion applies for positive and negative $x$, so this is a two-sided [limit](../../../../limit-of-a-function.md).
