<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Combine the terms before taking their [limit](../../../../limit-of-a-function.md):

$$
\frac1x-\frac1{e^x-1}=\frac{e^x-1-x}{x(e^x-1)}.
$$

Using the [exponential function](../../../../exponential-function.md) [Maclaurin series](../../../../maclaurin-series.md), the numerator is $x^2/2+O(x^3)$ and the denominator is $x^2+O(x^3)$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\lim_{x\to0}\left(\frac1x-\frac1{e^x-1}\right)=\frac12}.
$$

The cancellation removes the two individual singularities. Subtracting their separate divergent limits would not determine the finite answer.
