<h1 id="12/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

On the specified parametrized [curve](../../../../../curve.md), $\mathbf r(t)=(t^2,2t,t^3)$ and $\mathbf r'(t)=(2t,2,3t^2)$. Substitution into the field gives $\mathbf F(\mathbf r(t))=(4t^7,2t^8,8t^6)$. Hence

$$
\mathbf F\cdot\mathbf r'=8t^8+4t^8+24t^8=36t^8.
$$

The [line integral](../../../../../line-integral.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{\int_C\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r=\int_0^1 36t^8\,dt=4}.
$$

Both the field and the tangent vector must be evaluated along the [curve](../../../../../curve.md); integrating one field component alone would miss two contributions.
