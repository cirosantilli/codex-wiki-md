<h1 id="12/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Interpret the axis-directed route as successive coordinate-axis-parallel segments

$$
(0,0,0)\longrightarrow(1,0,0)\longrightarrow(1,2,0)\longrightarrow(1,2,1).
$$

On the first segment, $y=z=0$, so the field vanishes. On the second, $z=0$, so it again vanishes. On the last, $x=1$, $y=2$ and only $dz$ is nonzero, giving $\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r=8z\,dz$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\int_C\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r=0+0+\int_0^1 8z\,dz=4}.
$$

The second and third segments are parallel to the corresponding global axes; they do not return to the global $y$- or $z$-axis before reaching the endpoint.
