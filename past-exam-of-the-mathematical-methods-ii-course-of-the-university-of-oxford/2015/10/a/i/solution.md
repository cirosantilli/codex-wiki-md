<h1 id="10/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $x_1,x_2$ be [displacements](../../../../../displacement.md) from equilibrium, with positive directions chosen alike. [Hooke's law](../../../../../hooke-s-law.md) and [Newton's second law](../../../../../newton-s-second-law.md) give

$$
m_1\ddot x_1=-(k_1+k_2)x_1+k_2x_2,\qquad m_2\ddot x_2=k_2x_1-(k_2+k_3)x_2.
$$

For a [normal mode](../../../../../normal-mode.md) $x_j=a_j\cos\Omega t$, the [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) condition is

$$
\bigl(k_1+k_2-m_1\Omega^2\bigr)\bigl(k_2+k_3-m_2\Omega^2\bigr)-k_2^2=0.
$$

Solving the quadratic for $\Omega^2$ gives

$$
\boxed{\Omega_\pm^2=\frac12\left[\frac{k_1+k_2}{m_1}+\frac{k_2+k_3}{m_2}\pm\sqrt{\left(\frac{k_1+k_2}{m_1}-\frac{k_2+k_3}{m_2}\right)^2+\frac{4k_2^2}{m_1m_2}}\right]}.
$$

The [angular frequencies](../../../../../angular-frequency.md) are the positive square roots. Positive masses and [spring constants](../../../../../spring-constant.md) make the stiffness [quadratic form](../../../../../quadratic-form-split.md) $k_1x_1^2+k_2(x_2-x_1)^2+k_3x_2^2$ positive definite, ensuring both squared [frequencies](../../../../../frequency.md) are positive. Distinct [spring constants](../../../../../spring-constant.md) do not require equal masses or any symmetry approximation.
