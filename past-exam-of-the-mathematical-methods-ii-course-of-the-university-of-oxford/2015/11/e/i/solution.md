<h1 id="11/e/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For equal [linear mass densities](../../../../../linear-mass-density.md) and common [tension](../../../../../tension.md), $k_1=k_2$. The junction relations then give

$$
\boxed{r=0,\qquad t=1,\qquad\mathcal R=0,\qquad\mathcal T=1}.
$$

There is no mechanical-impedance discontinuity, so no reflected wave is needed to satisfy [displacement](../../../../../displacement.md) and [force](../../../../../force-split.md) continuity. The transmitted [displacement](../../../../../displacement.md) is in [phase](../../../../../phase-waves.md) with the incident wave and has the same [amplitude](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md); all incident [power](../../../../../power.md) crosses the junction.
