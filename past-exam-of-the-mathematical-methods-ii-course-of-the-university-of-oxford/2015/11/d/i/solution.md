<h1 id="11/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The transmitted and incident waves have the same [angular frequency](../../../../../angular-frequency.md) and [tension](../../../../../tension.md), but the transmitted [wavenumber](../../../../../wavenumber.md) is twice as large. The time-average string-power formula therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{P_t}{P_i}=\frac{k_2|A_t|^2}{k_1|A_i|^2}=2\left(\frac23\right)^2=\frac89}.
$$

The extra factor $k_2/k_1$ is essential. Squaring the [displacement](../../../../../displacement.md) transmission coefficient alone would give $4/9$, which is not the transmitted [power](../../../../../power.md) fraction in strings with different [linear mass densities](../../../../../linear-mass-density.md).
