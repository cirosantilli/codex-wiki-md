<h1 id="11/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Place the massless junction at $x=0$, with common [tension](../../../../tension.md) $T$ on both sides. Using a common time factor $e^{-i\omega t}$, write the left-hand wave as $A_i e^{ik_1x}+A_r e^{-ik_1x}$ and the right-hand wave as $A_t e^{ik_2x}$. A stationary junction does not change the [frequency](../../../../frequency.md), while $k_j=\omega\sqrt{\rho_j/T}$.

[displacement](../../../../displacement.md) continuity requires $A_i+A_r=A_t$. Transverse [force](../../../../force-split.md) balance on a massless junction requires equal tension-weighted slopes, giving $k_1(A_i-A_r)=k_2A_t$. Hence the signed [displacement](../../../../displacement.md) coefficients are

$$
\boxed{r=\frac{A_r}{A_i}=\frac{k_1-k_2}{k_1+k_2},\qquad t=\frac{A_t}{A_i}=\frac{2k_1}{k_1+k_2}}.
$$

Here $\rho_2=4\rho_1$, so $k_2=2k_1$ and $\boxed{A_r=-A_i/3,\quad A_t=2A_i/3}$. The negative reflected coefficient signifies inversion. If “reflection and transmission coefficients” instead denotes [power](../../../../power.md) fractions, their values are $\mathcal R=1/9$ and $\mathcal T=8/9$, as derived separately below; [power](../../../../power.md) and [displacement](../../../../displacement.md) coefficients must not be conflated.
