<h1 id="9/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For every [stationary point](../../../../stationary-point.md) found above, $\cos x_s\ne0$ and hence $z_s\ne0$. The [Hessian matrix](../../../../hessian-matrix.md) is diagonal, with entries $-(4x_s^2+3)z_s$ and $-2z_s$, so

$$
D=2(4x_s^2+3)z_s^2>0.
$$

The entries always have the same sign. Therefore

$$
\boxed{z_s>0:\ \text{strict local maximum};\qquad z_s<0:\ \text{strict local minimum}}.
$$

The root on tangent branch $(n\pi-\pi/2,n\pi+\pi/2)$ has cosine sign $(-1)^n$. Thus even-numbered branches supply maxima and odd-numbered branches minima, symmetrically paired about the origin. There are no saddle points among these nondegenerate [stationary points](../../../../stationary-point.md). The origin is a maximum with $z_s=1$, and is in fact the global maximum since $\cos x\le1$ and $e^{-x^2-y^2}\le1$.
