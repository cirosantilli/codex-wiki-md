<h1 id="9/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The first [partial derivatives](../../../../partial-derivative.md) are

$$
z_x=e^{-x^2-y^2}(-\sin x-2x\cos x),\qquad z_y=-2y\cos x\,e^{-x^2-y^2}.
$$

If $\cos x=0$, then $z_x=-\sin x\,e^{-x^2-y^2}\ne0$, so no [stationary point](../../../../stationary-point.md) is lost by excluding those values. The remaining condition $z_y=0$ forces $y=0$, and $z_x=0$ then gives

$$
\boxed{\tan x_s=-2x_s,\qquad k(x)=-2x}.
$$

There are infinitely many such points. On every tangent branch $(n\pi-\pi/2,n\pi+\pi/2)$, the [function](../../../../function-split.md) $\tan x+2x$ has [derivative](../../../../derivative.md) $\sec^2x+2>0$ and goes from $-\infty$ to $+\infty$. The [intermediate value theorem](../../../../intermediate-value-theorem.md) and strict monotonicity therefore give exactly one root on every branch. The central root is $x_s=0$, and oddness pairs the other roots at opposite signs.
