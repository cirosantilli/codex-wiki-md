<h1 id="6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

The integration region is the triangle $0\leq y\leq x\leq\pi$. Reversing its order gives

$$
I=\int_0^\pi\int_0^x\frac{\sin x}{x}\,dy\,dx=\int_0^\pi\sin x\,dx=\boxed{2.}
$$

The factor of $x$ from the inner interval cancels the denominator. At $x=0$, $\sin x/x$ has a removable singularity, so no divergent integral is being hidden by this change of order. [Fubini's theorem](../../../fubini-s-theorem.md) applies to its continuous extension on the bounded triangle.
