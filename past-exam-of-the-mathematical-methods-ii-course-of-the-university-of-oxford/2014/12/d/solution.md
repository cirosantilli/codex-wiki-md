<h1 id="12/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Parametrize any actual particle trajectory by time, with $\mathbf v=\dot{\mathbf r}$ and acceleration $\mathbf F=\dot{\mathbf v}$. The [chain rule](../../../../chain-rule.md) gives

$$
\frac{d}{dt}\left(\frac12|\mathbf v|^2\right)=\mathbf v\cdot\dot{\mathbf v}=\mathbf F\cdot\dot{\mathbf r}.
$$

Integrating from the origin to the endpoint gives

$$
I=\int\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r=K_A-K_O,\qquad\boxed{\Delta K=1.}
$$

Here $K$ is [specific kinetic energy](../../../../specific-kinetic-energy.md), because the source defines it without a mass factor. Equivalently, $|\mathbf v_A|^2-|\mathbf v_O|^2=2$ in the chosen units. The individual initial or final speeds are not fixed by this result. For a particle of mass $m$, the change of ordinary [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) would be $mI$.
