<h1 id="12/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For the given polynomial [vector field](../../../../vector-field.md), direct differentiation gives

$$
(\nabla\times\mathbf G)_x=x^2-x^2=0,\quad (\nabla\times\mathbf G)_y=(2xy-1)-(2xy-1)=0,\quad (\nabla\times\mathbf G)_z=(3+2xz)-(3+2xz)=0.
$$

An [exact differential](../../../../exact-differential.md) is a differential one-form equal to $d\phi=\phi_xdx+\phi_ydy+\phi_zdz$ for a single-valued scalar function. This field is smooth on the simply connected domain $\mathbb R^3$, so its vanishing [curl](../../../../curl.md) is sufficient for exactness. More concretely,

$$
\boxed{\mathbf G\cdot d\mathbf r=d(3xy+x^2yz-xz).}
$$

The explicit potential proves exactness globally, rather than relying on curl-free behavior without checking the domain.
