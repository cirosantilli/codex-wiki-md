<h1 id="5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

A perpendicular [geometric cross-section](../../../geometric-collision-cross-section.md) at position $x$ is a disk of radius $r=x^2$, so its [area](../../../surface-area.md) is $\pi x^4$. Slicing along the axis gives

$$
\boxed{V=\int_0^1\pi x^4dx=\frac\pi5.}
$$

The result uses the coordinate units implicit in the source's relation between radius and axial coordinate.
