<h1 id="11/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For an element $dx$, the vertical components of the approximately constant [tension](../../../../tension.md) are $Ty_x$ at its two ends. Their difference is $Ty_{xx}dx$ to first order in the small slope. Its inertial response is $\rho\,dx\,y_{tt}$. Thus [Newton's second law](../../../../newton-s-second-law.md) gives

$$
\boxed{y_{tt}-c^2y_{xx}=0,\qquad c=\sqrt{T/\rho}.}
$$

Here $\rho$ is mass per unit length. Small slopes, negligible gravity, uniform tension and negligible bending stiffness or dissipation are the assumptions in this [wave equation on a string](../../../../wave-equation-on-a-string.md).
