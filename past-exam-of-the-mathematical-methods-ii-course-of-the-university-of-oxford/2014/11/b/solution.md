<h1 id="11/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use [separation of variables](../../../../separation-of-variables.md), $y(x,t)=X(x)Q(t)$. The [wave equation](../../../../wave-equation-split.md) implies $X''/X=\ddot Q/(c^2Q)=-k^2$, giving $X''+k^2X=0$ and $\ddot Q+c^2k^2Q=0$. The fixed-end [boundary conditions](../../../../boundary-condition.md) $X(0)=X(l)=0$ select

$$
X_n(x)=\sin\frac{n\pi x}l,\qquad k_n=\frac{n\pi}l,\qquad \boxed{\omega_n=\frac{n\pi c}l,\quad f_n=\frac{nc}{2l},\quad n=1,2,\ldots.}
$$

Each such product is a [standing wave](../../../../standing-wave.md). A general released string is a superposition of these [normal modes](../../../../normal-mode.md), not necessarily a single separated standing wave. Absence of damping keeps their amplitudes constant.
