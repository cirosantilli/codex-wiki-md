<h1 id="9/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Set $\mathbf x=\mathbf a\cos(\omega t+\varphi)$. The two [normal modes](../../../../normal-mode.md) satisfy

$$
\left[\begin{pmatrix}2&-1\\-1&1\end{pmatrix}-s\mathbf I\right]\mathbf a=0,\qquad s=\frac{m\omega^2}{k}.
$$

The [characteristic equation](../../../../characteristic-equation-of-a-constant-coefficient-differential-equation.md) is $(2-s)(1-s)-1=s^2-3s+1=0$. Hence

$$
\boxed{a_\pm=\frac{3\pm\sqrt5}{2},\qquad\omega_\pm^2=\frac km\frac{3\pm\sqrt5}{2}.}
$$

Here $a_\pm$ denotes the scalar coefficient requested in the source, whereas bold $\mathbf a$ denotes a mode-amplitude vector. Both roots are positive.
