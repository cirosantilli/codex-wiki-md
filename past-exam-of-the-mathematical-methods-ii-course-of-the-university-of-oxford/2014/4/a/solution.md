<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a branch of the [dispersion relation](../../../../dispersion-relation-split.md) $\omega=\omega(k)$ with nonzero $k$, the carrier [phase velocity](../../../../phase-velocity.md) and narrow-band [group velocity](../../../../group-velocity.md) are

$$
\boxed{v_p=\frac\omega k,\qquad v_g=\frac{d\omega}{dk}.}
$$

The first follows the constant-phase points of a monochromatic wave; the second follows a slowly varying envelope under the usual weak-distortion wavepacket assumptions.
