<h1 id="1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

By the [product rule](../../../../product-rule.md) and [chain rule](../../../../chain-rule.md),

$$
f'(x)=e^{x^2}(1+2x^2)>0\qquad(x\in\mathbb R).
$$

Thus there are $\boxed{0}$ [stationary points](../../../../stationary-point.md); the function is strictly increasing everywhere.
