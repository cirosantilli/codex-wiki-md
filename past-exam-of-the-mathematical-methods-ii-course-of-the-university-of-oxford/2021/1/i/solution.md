↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [stationary point](../../../../stationary-point.md) occurs where the [derivative](../../../../derivative.md) vanishes. Here

$$
f'(x)=6+12\sin(3x)=0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\sin(3x)=-\frac12.
$$

The two families are

$$
\boxed{x=\frac{7\pi}{18}+\frac{2n\pi}{3}\quad\text{or}\quad x=\frac{11\pi}{18}+\frac{2n\pi}{3},\qquad n\in\mathbb Z.}
$$

Consequently there are **infinitely many** [stationary points](../../../../stationary-point.md) on the real line. The second [derivative](../../../../derivative.md) is $36\cos(3x)$: the first family consists of strict [local minima](../../../../local-minimum.md) and the second of strict [local maxima](../../../../local-maximum.md).
