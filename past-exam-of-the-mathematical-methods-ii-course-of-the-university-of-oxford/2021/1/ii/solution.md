<h1 id="1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [product rule](../../../../product-rule.md) and [chain rule](../../../../chain-rule.md) give

$$
f'(x)=10e^{3-x^2}(1-2x^2).
$$

The [exponential function](../../../../exponential-function.md) is strictly positive, so there are exactly **two** [stationary points](../../../../stationary-point.md), at $\boxed{x=\pm1/\sqrt2}$. The [derivative](../../../../derivative.md) is negative outside these two values and positive between them. Thus the negative value is a strict [local minimum](../../../../local-minimum.md) and the positive value a strict [local maximum](../../../../local-maximum.md).
