<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [probability density function](../../../../probability-density-function.md) is normalized because $\int_1^\infty3x^{-4}\,dx=1$. Its [expected value](../../../../expected-value-split.md) and second moment are

$$
\mathbb E[X]=3\int_1^\infty x^{-3}\,dx=\frac32,\qquad
\mathbb E[X^2]=3\int_1^\infty x^{-2}\,dx=3.
$$

Both [improper integrals](../../../../improper-integral.md) converge, so the [variance](../../../../variance-split.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[X]=\frac32,\qquad \operatorname{Var}(X)=3-\left(\frac32\right)^2=\frac34.}
$$
