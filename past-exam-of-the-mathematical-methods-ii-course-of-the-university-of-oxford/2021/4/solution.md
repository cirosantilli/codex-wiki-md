<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

At their intersection, $x^2+y^2=1$ and $z^2=1$. The [gradients](../../../gradient.md) of the defining functions give [normal vectors](../../../normal-vector.md) to the [sphere](../../../sphere.md) and [cylinder](../../../cylinder-geometry.md):

$$
\mathbf n_s=(2x,2y,2z),\qquad \mathbf n_c=(2x,2y,0).
$$

The acute angle between the [tangent planes](../../../tangent-plane.md) is the acute angle between these [normal vectors](../../../normal-vector.md). Their [dot product](../../../dot-product.md) gives

$$
\cos\alpha=\frac{|\mathbf n_s\cdot\mathbf n_c|}{|\mathbf n_s|\,|\mathbf n_c|}
=\frac4{(2\sqrt2)(2)}=\frac1{\sqrt2},\qquad
\boxed{\alpha=\frac\pi4=45^\circ.}
$$

It is the same at every intersection point; the supplementary oriented angle is $135^\circ$.
