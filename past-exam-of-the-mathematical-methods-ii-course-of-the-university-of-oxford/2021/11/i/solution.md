<h1 id="11/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

[Wave dispersion](../../../../wave-dispersion.md) means that different [frequencies](../../../../frequency.md) propagate with different [phase velocities](../../../../phase-velocity.md), so a [wave packet](../../../../wave-packet.md) generally changes shape. Equivalently the [dispersion relation](../../../../dispersion-relation-split.md) is not proportional to $k$ over the relevant band. Three natural examples are the colour separation in a [rainbow](../../../../rainbow.md), where the refractive index of water depends on [wavelength](../../../../wavelength.md); deep-water [surface gravity waves](../../../../surface-gravity-wave.md), for which $\omega^2=gk$ and long waves have larger [phase velocity](../../../../phase-velocity.md); and [capillary waves](../../../../capillary-wave.md), whose short-wave limit is $\omega^2=(\gamma/\rho)k^3$ and whose shorter waves have larger [phase velocity](../../../../phase-velocity.md). Here $g$ is [gravitational acceleration](../../../../gravitational-acceleration.md), $\gamma$ is [surface tension](../../../../surface-tension-split.md), and $\rho$ is fluid [mass density](../../../../density.md), with positive $k$ and negligible viscosity.

A distinct application is an [optical prism](../../../../prism-optics.md) [spectrometer](../../../../spectrometer.md): the wavelength-dependent [refractive index](../../../../refractive-index.md) produces different [refraction](../../../../refraction.md) angles, separating spectral components for measurement. This exploits [wave dispersion](../../../../wave-dispersion.md) deliberately.
