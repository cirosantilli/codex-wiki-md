<h1 id="7/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For $\Delta t=\Delta x/c$,

$$
y(x+\Delta x,t+\Delta t)=f\bigl(ct+c\Delta t-x-\Delta x\bigr)=f(ct-x)=y(x,t).
$$

Equivalently $\boxed{y(x,t+\Delta t)=y(x-c\Delta t,t)}$. Thus every value of the [displacement](../../../../displacement.md) is translated by the same distance $c\Delta t$: the entire [travelling wave](../../../../travelling-wave.md) profile keeps its shape. The [wave speed](../../../../wave-speed.md) is independent of the profile, as expected for this nondispersive [wave equation](../../../../wave-equation-split.md).
