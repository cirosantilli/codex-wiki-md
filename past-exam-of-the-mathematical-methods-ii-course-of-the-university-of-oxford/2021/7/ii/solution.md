<h1 id="7/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Put $u=ct-x$ and assume $f$ has continuous second [derivatives](../../../../derivative.md). The [chain rule](../../../../chain-rule.md) yields

$$
y_x=-f'(u),\quad y_{xx}=f''(u),\quad
y_t=cf'(u),\quad y_{tt}=c^2f''(u).
$$

Thus the [wave equation](../../../../wave-equation-split.md) is satisfied. A point of fixed [wave phase](../../../../phase-waves.md) $u=u_0$ has $x=ct-u_0$, so for $c>0$ this [travelling wave](../../../../travelling-wave.md) moves in the $\boxed{\text{positive }x\text{ direction at speed }c}$.
