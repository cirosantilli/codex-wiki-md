<h1 id="7/iv/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

At $\tau=1/2$, the two [triangular pulses](../../../../../triangular-function.md) coincide with opposite signs:

$$
\boxed{y(x,L/(2c))=0\quad\text{for every }x\le0.}
$$

The instantaneous sketch is the straight equilibrium line. This is destructive [interference](../../../../../interference-wave-propagation.md), not the disappearance of the [wave energy](../../../../../wave-energy.md). For example, on $-1/2<u<0$ the transverse [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) is $y_t=-4Hc/L$, so the [mechanical string](../../../../../mechanical-string.md) is passing through equilibrium with nonzero [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md).
