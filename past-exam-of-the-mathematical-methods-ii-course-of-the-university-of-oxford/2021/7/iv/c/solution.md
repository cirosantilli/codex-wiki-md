<h1 id="7/iv/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

At $\tau=3/4$, the reflected [pulse](../../../../../pulse.md) dominates the overlap. The total [displacement](../../../../../displacement.md) is zero outside $[-3/4,0]$ and consists of straight segments through

$$
\boxed{(u,y/H)=(-3/4,0),\ (-1/4,-1),\ (0,0).}
$$

This is the inverted version of the quarter-reflected profile. The entire negative [pulse](../../../../../pulse.md) has not yet separated from the incident [pulse](../../../../../pulse.md).
