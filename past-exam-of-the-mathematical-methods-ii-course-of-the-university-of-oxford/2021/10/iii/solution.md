<h1 id="10/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For this [vector field](../../../../vector-field.md),

$$
\nabla\cdot\mathbf G=3x^2+3y^2+3z^2=3r^2.
$$

The [divergence theorem](../../../../divergence-theorem.md) and [spherical coordinates](../../../../spherical-coordinate-system.md) therefore give the outward [flux](../../../../flux.md)

$$
\boxed{\oiint_S\mathbf G\cdot\mathbf n\,dS
=\iiint_V3r^2\,dV
=12\pi\int_0^a r^4\,dr=\frac{12\pi a^5}{5}.}
$$

The $a^5$ dependence also follows by combining the cubic field magnitude with the quadratic scaling of [surface area](../../../../surface-area.md).
