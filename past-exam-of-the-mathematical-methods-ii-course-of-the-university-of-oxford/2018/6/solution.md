<h1 id="6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

Multiply the [secant function](../../../secant-trigonometry.md) integrand by $(\sec\theta+\tan\theta)/(\sec\theta+\tan\theta)$:

$$
\int\sec\theta\,d\theta
=\int\frac{\sec^2\theta+\sec\theta\tan\theta}{\sec\theta+\tan\theta}\,d\theta
=\boxed{\log|\sec\theta+\tan\theta|+C.}
$$

The numerator is the [derivative](../../../derivative.md) of the denominator. The formula holds on any real interval avoiding zeros of $\cos\theta$; on such an interval $\sec\theta+\tan\theta$ cannot vanish because $(\sec\theta+\tan\theta)(\sec\theta-\tan\theta)=1$.
