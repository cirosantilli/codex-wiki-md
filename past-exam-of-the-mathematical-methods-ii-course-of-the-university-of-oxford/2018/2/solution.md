<h1 id="2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

The [velocity vector](../../../velocity-vector.md) of the [helix](../../../helix.md) is $r'(t)=(-\sin t,\cos t,1)$, whose [Euclidean norm](../../../euclidean-norm.md) is $\sqrt2$. Thus its [arc length](../../../arc-length.md) over the stated parameter interval is

$$
\boxed{L=\int_0^{2\pi}\|r'(t)\|\,dt=2\pi\sqrt2.}
$$

Omitting the two endpoints does not change this finite [arc length](../../../arc-length.md).
