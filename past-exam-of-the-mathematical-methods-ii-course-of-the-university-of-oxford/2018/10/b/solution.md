<h1 id="10/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Put $\kappa=k/m$, $q=g/l$. The positive stiffness-per-mass [matrix](../../../../matrix.md) is

$$
K=\begin{pmatrix}\kappa+q&-q\\-q&q\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Substitution of a [normal mode](../../../../normal-mode.md) $x=v\cos\omega t$ requires $Kv=\omega^2v$. Its [characteristic equation](../../../../characteristic-equation-of-a-constant-coefficient-differential-equation.md) is $\lambda^2-(\kappa+2q)\lambda+\kappa q=0$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\omega_\pm^2=\frac\kappa2+q\pm\sqrt{\frac{\kappa^2}{4}+q^2}.}
$$

For $k,m,g,l>0$, their sum and product are positive, so both squared [angular frequencies](../../../../angular-frequency.md) are positive and $\omega_+>\omega_->0$.
