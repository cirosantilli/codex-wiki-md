<h1 id="10/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $\delta=x_2-x_1$. The inextensible-string constraint gives the upward displacement $y_2=l-\sqrt{l^2-\delta^2}=\delta^2/(2l)+O(\delta^4/l^3)$. Thus the quadratic [potential energy](../../../../potential-energy.md) is $U=kx_1^2/2+mg(x_2-x_1)^2/(2l)$. The vertical [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) of the second [mass](../../../../mass.md) is of second order in the small motion, so the quadratic [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) is $m(\dot x_1^2+\dot x_2^2)/2$.

The [Euler-Lagrange equations](../../../../euler-lagrange-equation.md) for this quadratic [Lagrangian](../../../../lagrangian.md) give

$$
\boxed{\ddot x_1=-\left(\frac km+\frac gl\right)x_1+\frac glx_2,\qquad
\ddot x_2=\frac glx_1-\frac glx_2.}
$$

Small [displacements](../../../../displacement.md) are used both in the quadratic approximation to the [gravitational potential energy](../../../../gravitational-energy.md) and in neglecting the higher-order vertical [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md). These are the [spring-supported pendulum normal modes](../../../../spring-supported-pendulum-normal-modes.md) at linear order. The corresponding force picture sets the string [tension](../../../../tension.md) to $mg$ at leading order and its horizontal angle to $\delta/l$.
