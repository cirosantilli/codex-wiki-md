<h1 id="10/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Define $D=\sqrt{\kappa^2/4+q^2}$. Normalizing the first [displacement](../../../../displacement.md) [amplitude](../../../../wave-amplitude.md) to one, either row of the [eigenvector](../../../../eigenvector.md) equation gives

$$
\boxed{v_\pm=\begin{pmatrix}1\\r_\pm\end{pmatrix},\qquad r_\pm=\frac{\kappa/2\mp D}{q}.}
$$

The two [normal modes](../../../../normal-mode.md) are $x(t)=A_\pm v_\pm\cos(\omega_\pm t+\phi_\pm)$, and arbitrary small motion is their [superposition](../../../../superposition-principle.md). The lower-frequency mode has $r_->0$, so the [masses](../../../../mass.md) move in phase; the upper-frequency mode has $r_+<0$, so they move in opposite phase. The identity $r_+r_-=-1$ verifies [orthogonality](../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) of these [eigenvectors](../../../../eigenvector.md) for equal [masses](../../../../mass.md).
