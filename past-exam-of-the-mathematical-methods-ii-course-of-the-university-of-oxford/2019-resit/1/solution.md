<h1 id="1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Convert the fixed position to $x=0.20\ \mathrm m$. The element's [displacement](../../../displacement.md) is then $y(t)=0.27\sin(2.4-500t)\ \mathrm{mm}$. Taking time [derivatives](../../../derivative.md) at fixed position gives its transverse [velocity](../../../velocity.md) and [acceleration](../../../acceleration.md):

$$
\boxed{v_y(t)=-135\cos(2.4-500t)\ \mathrm{mm\,s^{-1}},\qquad
a_y(t)=-67500\sin(2.4-500t)\ \mathrm{mm\,s^{-2}}.}
$$

Equivalently the prefactors in SI units are $-0.135\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ and $-67.5\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$. The [phase](../../../phase-waves.md) is measured in radians. At $t=4\ \mathrm s$,

$$
\boxed{y(4)=0.27\sin(-1997.6)\ \mathrm{mm}
\simeq0.118152\ \mathrm{mm}=1.18152\times10^{-4}\ \mathrm m.}
$$

These are the transverse motions of the material element, not the propagation speed of the [wave](../../../wave-split.md) along the string.
