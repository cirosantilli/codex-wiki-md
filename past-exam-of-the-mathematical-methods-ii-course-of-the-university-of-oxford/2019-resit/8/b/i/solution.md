↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Away from the origin, the [electric field](../../../../../electric-field-split.md) is $E=A r/r^3$, with $r$ here denoting the position vector in the numerator and its [Euclidean norm](../../../../../euclidean-norm.md) in the denominator. The spherical-coordinate formula for [divergence](../../../../../divergence.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\nabla\cdot E=\frac1{r^2}\frac{d}{dr}\left(r^2\frac A{r^2}\right)=0,\qquad r>0.}
$$

At the origin the [electric field](../../../../../electric-field-split.md) is singular, so the ordinary [divergence](../../../../../divergence.md) is undefined there. The equation above must not be extended to the whole space as an ordinary smooth-field identity. Its correct [distributional divergence](../../../../../distributional-divergence.md), derived in [solution](../ii/solution.md), includes the point source.
