<h1 id="8/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

In [cylindrical coordinates](../../../../../cylindrical-coordinate-system.md), $F=r^{1-2n}\hat\theta$. The tangential coefficient is positive for every real $n$ and $r>0$, so the large-scale circulation is always counterclockwise when viewed from positive $z$.

By [solution](../i/solution.md), the infinitesimal local [angular velocity](../../../../../angular-velocity.md) is $(1-n)r^{-2n}\hat z$. Its sign gives

$$
\boxed{\begin{array}{c|c}
n<1&\text{local spin in the same sense as circulation}\\
n=1&\text{zero local spin}\\
n>1&\text{local spin opposite to circulation}
\end{array}}
$$

“Zero local spin” does not mean zero [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md): at $n=1$ a marked small element is still carried around the axis while its infinitesimal rotational component is zero. Orbital motion around the axis and local [vorticity](../../../../../vorticity-split.md) are different measurements.
