<h1 id="8/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Write $r^2=x^2+y^2$ and work away from the $z$ axis, where $r>0$. The [vector field](../../../../../vector-field.md) is independent of $z$ and has zero vertical component, so only the vertical [curl](../../../../../curl.md) can be nonzero:

$$
\begin{aligned}
(\nabla\times F)_z
&=\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\big[x(x^2+y^2)^{-n}\big]
-\frac{\partial}{\partial y}\big[-y(x^2+y^2)^{-n}\big]\\
&=2r^{-2n}-2n(x^2+y^2)r^{-2n-2}.
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{\nabla\times F=2(1-n)r^{-2n}\,\hat z,\qquad r>0.}
$$

This [local spin in an azimuthal power-law flow](../../../../../local-spin-in-an-azimuthal-power-law-flow.md) depends on the radial variation of the tangential speed, not merely on the sign of that speed.
