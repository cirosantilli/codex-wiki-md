A [normal mode](../../../normal-mode.md) is a motion of a linear oscillatory system in which every coordinate has the same [angular frequency](../../../angular-frequency.md) and a fixed relative amplitude and phase. For a conservative small-displacement system it has the form

$$
\boldsymbol x(t)=q\cos(\omega t+\phi),\qquad Kq=\omega^2Mq,
$$

where $q$ is the [mode shape](../../../mode-shape.md), $K$ the [stiffness matrix](../../../stiffness-matrix.md), and $M$ the [mass matrix](../../../mass-matrix.md). Superposing [normal modes](../../../normal-mode.md) gives the general small-oscillation motion when their [eigenvectors](../../../eigenvector.md) form a complete [basis](../../../basis.md).

Two common examples are standing transverse oscillations of a string fixed at both ends, and oscillations of coupled masses and springs. The string's mode shapes are sinusoidal along its length; the coupled system's mode shapes are fixed patterns of particle displacements.
