<h1 id="2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Use the outward [normal vector](../../../normal-vector.md). The [vector field](../../../vector-field.md) is $\mathbf F=(xz,2y,-z^2)$, with [divergence](../../../divergence.md) $\nabla\cdot\mathbf F=z+2-2z=2-z$. By the [divergence theorem](../../../divergence-theorem.md), its outward flux equals the integral of this divergence over the solid cylinder. Each horizontal slice has area $8\pi$, so

$$
\boxed{\oint_{\partial V}\mathbf F\cdot\hat{\mathbf n}\,d\sigma=8\pi\int_0^3(2-z)\,dz=12\pi.}
$$

For an independent boundary check, the cylindrical side contributes $84\pi$, the top contributes $(-3^2)(8\pi)=-72\pi$, and the bottom contributes zero. Their sum is again $12\pi$. Reversing all normals changes its sign.
