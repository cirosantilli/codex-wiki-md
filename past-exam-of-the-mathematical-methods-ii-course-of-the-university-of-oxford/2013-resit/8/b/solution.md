<h1 id="8/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a twice continuously differentiable scalar function $\psi$, equality of mixed [partial derivatives](../../../../partial-derivative.md) gives $\nabla\times\nabla\psi=0$. Substitute $\mathbf F=\nabla\psi$ into the preceding product rule:

$$
\nabla\times(\phi\nabla\psi)=\nabla\phi\times\nabla\psi.
$$

[Stokes theorem](../../../../stokes-theorem.md) on a spanning surface with consistent boundary orientation then gives

$$
\boxed{\oint_{\partial S}\phi\nabla\psi\cdot d\mathbf l=\iint_S(\nabla\phi\times\nabla\psi)\cdot d\mathbf S.}
$$

This is a [weighted gradient circulation identity](../../../../weighted-gradient-circulation-identity.md). All fields must be defined and sufficiently smooth on a neighborhood of the surface; a scalar prefactor can create nonzero circulation even when the unweighted field is a gradient.
