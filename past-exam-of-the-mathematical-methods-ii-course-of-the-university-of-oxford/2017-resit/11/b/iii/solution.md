<h1 id="11/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Free ends give $Y'(0)=Y'(L)=0$, so the oscillatory [normal modes](../../../../../normal-mode.md) are

$$
\boxed{k_n=\frac{n\pi}L,\qquad
y_n=A_n\cos\frac{n\pi x}L\cos(ck_nt+\phi_n),\qquad n=1,2,\ldots.}
$$

There is also the [zero mode](../../../../../zero-mode.md) $k_0=0$, with uniform displacement $y_0(x,t)=a+bt$: a free string can translate without a restoring force. It is not a positive-frequency oscillation. The first three oscillatory shapes are $\cos(\pi x/L)$, $\cos(2\pi x/L)$ and $\cos(3\pi x/L)$, with an [antinode](../../../../../antinode.md) at each end. The figure includes the uniform mode too, so that the zero-frequency limit is explicit.
