<h1 id="11/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A free end has zero transverse force, hence $TY'(L)=0$, rather than zero displacement. With $Y(0)=0$, write $Y=A\sin kx$. The remaining condition is $\cos kL=0$, giving

$$
\boxed{k_n=\frac{(n+1/2)\pi}L,\qquad
y_n=A_n\sin(k_nx)\cos(ck_nt+\phi_n),\qquad n=0,1,2,\ldots.}
$$

The first three shapes have one, three and five quarter-waves. Each has a [node of a standing wave](../../../../../node-physics.md) at the fixed end and an [antinode](../../../../../antinode.md) at the free end; the mode frequencies are in the ratio $1:3:5$.
