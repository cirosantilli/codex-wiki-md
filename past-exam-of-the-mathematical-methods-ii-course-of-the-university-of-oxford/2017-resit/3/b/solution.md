<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Using $r=(x,y,z)$ and constant $a$, the [divergence](../../../../divergence.md) is

$$
\partial_i(\epsilon_{ijk}r_ja_k)=\epsilon_{ijk}\delta_{ij}a_k=0.
$$

For the [curl](../../../../curl.md), the [curl of a cross product](../../../../curl-of-a-cross-product.md) identity gives

$$
\nabla\times(r\times a)=r(\nabla\cdot a)-a(\nabla\cdot r)+(a\cdot\nabla)r-(r\cdot\nabla)a
=0-3a+a-0.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{\nabla\cdot(r\times a)=0,\qquad\nabla\times(r\times a)=-2a.}
$$

For a quick sign check, taking $a=e_z$ gives $r\times a=(y,-x,0)$, whose [curl](../../../../curl.md) is $(0,0,-2)$.
