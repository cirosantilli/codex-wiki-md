<h1 id="7/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [7](../7.md)

The field is the [gradient](../../../gradient.md) of $\Phi=x^2-3y^2+4z^2$ on all of $\mathbb R^3$. It is therefore a [conservative vector field](../../../conservative-vector-field.md), and the [fundamental theorem for line integrals](../../../fundamental-theorem-for-line-integrals.md) gives $\int_C F\cdot dr=\Phi(r_{\rm end})-\Phi(r_{\rm start})$. The endpoints are $(3,0,0)$ and $(0,2,-1)$, so

$$
\boxed{\int_C F\cdot dr=(-12+4)-9=-17.}
$$

The complicated intermediate path does not affect the work. Directly, $\nabla\times F=0$, consistent with the globally defined [potential function](../../../potential-function.md).
