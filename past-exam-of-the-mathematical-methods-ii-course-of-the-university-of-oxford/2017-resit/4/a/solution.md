<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Take any differentiable curve $r(s)$ on the [level surface](../../../../level-surface.md). The [chain rule](../../../../chain-rule.md) gives

$$
0=\frac{d}{ds}f(r(s))=\nabla f\cdot r'(s).
$$

Its tangent vector $r'(s)$ can be any tangent direction at a regular point. Thus a nonzero [gradient](../../../../gradient.md) is orthogonal to the entire tangent plane, giving the [unit normal](../../../../unit-normal.md) $n=\pm\nabla f/|\nabla f|$. Nonvanishing is essential: at a [critical point](../../../../critical-point.md), the zero gradient alone does not determine a normal or guarantee a smooth [level surface](../../../../level-surface.md).
