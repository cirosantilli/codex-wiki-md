<h1 id="8/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For differentiable [vector fields](../../../../vector-field.md), expand the [divergence](../../../../divergence.md) using the [Levi-Civita symbol](../../../../levi-civita-symbol.md) and the product rule:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\nabla\cdot(F\times G)
&=\partial_i(\epsilon_{ijk}F_jG_k)\\
&=\epsilon_{ijk}(\partial_iF_j)G_k+\epsilon_{ijk}F_j\partial_iG_k\\
&=G\cdot(\nabla\times F)-F\cdot(\nabla\times G).
\end{aligned}
$$

The first contraction uses the cyclic identity $\epsilon_{ijk}=\epsilon_{kij}$; the second uses $\epsilon_{ijk}=-\epsilon_{jik}$. This fixes the relative minus sign without guessing the orientation of the [cross products](../../../../cross-product.md).
