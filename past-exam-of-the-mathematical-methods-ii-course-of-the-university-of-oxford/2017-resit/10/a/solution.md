<h1 id="10/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

With $x=e^t$, the [chain rule](../../../../chain-rule.md) gives $xy'=\dot y$ and $x^2y''=\ddot y-\dot y$. The [Euler differential equation](../../../../cauchy-euler-equation.md) becomes

$$
\boxed{\ddot y+2\dot y-7y=0.}
$$

Its [characteristic polynomial](../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) has roots $-1\pm2\sqrt2$, so, in addition to the requested transformation, its general solution is $y(x)=C_1x^{-1+2\sqrt2}+C_2x^{-1-2\sqrt2}$ on $x>0$.
