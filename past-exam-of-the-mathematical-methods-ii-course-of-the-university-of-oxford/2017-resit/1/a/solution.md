<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The successive [derivatives](../../../../derivative.md) of cosine cycle through $\cos a,-\sin a,-\cos a,\sin a$. Its [Taylor series](../../../../taylor-series.md) about $a$ therefore separates into even and odd powers:

$$
\cos(a+x)=\cos a\left(1-\frac{x^2}{2!}+\frac{x^4}{4!}-\cdots\right)
-\sin a\left(x-\frac{x^3}{3!}+\frac{x^5}{5!}-\cdots\right).
$$

The two brackets are the [Maclaurin series](../../../../maclaurin-series.md) of cosine and sine, giving

$$
\boxed{\cos(a+x)=\cos a\cos x-\sin a\sin x.}
$$

Both series converge absolutely for every real $x$, so regrouping the even and odd terms is valid everywhere on the real axis.
