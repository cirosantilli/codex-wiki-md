<h1 id="3/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [vector calculus identity](../../../../vector-calculus-identity.md) $\nabla\cdot(\mathbf A\times\mathbf B)=\mathbf B\cdot(\nabla\times\mathbf A)-\mathbf A\cdot(\nabla\times\mathbf B)$ gives zero because a constant [vector field](../../../../vector-field.md) has zero [curl](../../../../curl.md) and $\nabla\times\mathbf r=0$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\nabla\cdot(\mathbf a\times\mathbf r)=0.}
$$

Equivalently, the Cartesian components are $(a_yz-a_zy,a_zx-a_xz,a_xy-a_yx)$, and each component is independent of the coordinate used in its contribution to the [divergence](../../../../divergence.md).
