<h1 id="3/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Away from the origin, the [product rule](../../../../product-rule.md) and $\nabla r=\mathbf r/r$ give

$$
\nabla\cdot(r^{-3}\mathbf r)
=r^{-3}(3)+\mathbf r\cdot(-3r^{-5}\mathbf r)
=\boxed{0\quad(r\ne0).}
$$

The field is undefined at $r=0$. It has outward [flux](../../../../flux.md) $4\pi$ through any sphere centred there, so its global [distributional divergence](../../../../distributional-divergence.md) is $4\pi\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf r)$, not the zero distribution. The pointwise result must not be extended through the singularity when applying the [divergence theorem](../../../../divergence-theorem.md).
