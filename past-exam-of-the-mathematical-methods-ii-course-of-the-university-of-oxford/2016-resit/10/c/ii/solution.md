<h1 id="10/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

At the same [tension](../../../../../tension.md), $k\propto\sqrt\rho$, so $k_2/k_1=3$, $t=1/2$ and $r=-1/2$. The [transmitted power](../../../../../transmitted-power.md) fraction is not $t^2$, because the two strings have different [wave impedances](../../../../../wave-impedance.md). Using the [energy flux of a transverse string wave](../../../../../energy-flux-of-a-transverse-string-wave.md),

$$
\boxed{\mathcal T=\frac{\rho_2c_2}{\rho_1c_1}|t|^2
=\sqrt{\frac{\rho_2}{\rho_1}}\,\frac14=\frac34.}
$$

The reflected fraction is $\mathcal R=|r|^2=1/4$, so $\mathcal R+\mathcal T=1$. The transmitted displacement amplitude is $2.5\,\mathrm{mm}$ and its [wavelength](../../../../../wavelength.md) is $0.5/3\,\mathrm m$. The negative $r$ indicates a reflected phase reversal; it does not mean negative reflected energy.
