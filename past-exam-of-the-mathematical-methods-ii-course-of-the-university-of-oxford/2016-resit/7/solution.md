<h1 id="7/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [7](../7.md)

Subtract the [Maclaurin series](../../../maclaurin-series.md) of $\log(1-x)$ from that of $\log(1+x)$. Their even powers cancel, giving

$$
\boxed{\log\frac{1+x}{1-x}=2x+\frac23x^3+\frac25x^5+O(x^7).}
$$

The full [power series](../../../power-series.md) is $2\sum_{n\ge0}x^{2n+1}/(2n+1)$, with [radius of convergence](../../../radius-of-convergence.md) one. For the real logarithm, the stated series applies on $|x|<1$.
