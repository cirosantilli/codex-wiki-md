<h1 id="11/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Release from rest gives $B=D=0$. At $t=0$, $A+2C=d$ and $A-C=0$, so $A=C=d/3$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{y_1(t)=\frac d3\bigl(\cos\omega_-t+2\cos2\omega_-t\bigr),\qquad
y_2(t)=\frac d3\bigl(\cos\omega_-t-\cos2\omega_-t\bigr).}
$$

The [frequency ratio](../../../../frequency-ratio.md) is exactly two, so both motions repeat after $2\pi/\omega_-$. These are commensurate [normal modes](../../../../normal-mode.md), rather than a pair of nearly equal frequencies producing slow [beats](../../../../beat.md).
