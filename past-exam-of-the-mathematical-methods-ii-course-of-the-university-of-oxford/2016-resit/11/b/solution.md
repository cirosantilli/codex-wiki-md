<h1 id="11/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $\kappa=T/(2ma)$ and seek a [normal mode](../../../../normal-mode.md) $\mathbf y=\mathbf a e^{i\omega t}$. Its [eigenvalue equation](../../../../eigenvalue-equation.md) is

$$
\det\begin{pmatrix}3-\omega^2/\kappa&-2\\-1&2-\omega^2/\kappa\end{pmatrix}=0,
$$

or $\lambda^2-5\lambda+4=(\lambda-1)(\lambda-4)=0$, where $\lambda=\omega^2/\kappa$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\omega_-=\sqrt{\frac{T}{2ma}},\qquad
\omega_+=2\sqrt{\frac{T}{2ma}}.}
$$

These are [angular frequencies](../../../../angular-frequency.md); the corresponding ordinary [frequencies](../../../../frequency.md) are $f_\pm=\omega_\pm/(2\pi)$.
