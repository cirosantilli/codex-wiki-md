<h1 id="11/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The three nearly horizontal segments have lengths $2a,a,a$. To first order in small transverse slopes, the upward [force](../../../../force-split.md) on the first [point mass](../../../../point-mass.md) is $T[(y_2-y_1)/a-y_1/(2a)]$. The force on the second is $T[(y_1-y_2)/a-y_2/a]$. Applying [Newton's second law](../../../../newton-s-second-law.md) to masses $m,2m$ therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\ddot y_1+\frac{T}{2ma}(3y_1-2y_2)=0,\qquad
\ddot y_2+\frac{T}{2ma}(2y_2-y_1)=0.}
$$

The approximation treats the string as massless, retains the imposed equilibrium [tension](../../../../tension.md) and neglects gravity, as stipulated.
