<h1 id="6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

The field is the [gradient](../../../gradient.md) of a globally defined polynomial:

$$
\phi(x,y,z)=2xy+y^2z,\qquad
\nabla\phi=(2y,2x+2yz,y^2)=\mathbf v.
$$

It is therefore a [conservative vector field](../../../conservative-vector-field.md) on all of $\mathbb R^3$. The [fundamental theorem for line integrals](../../../fundamental-theorem-for-line-integrals.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\oint_C\mathbf v\cdot d\mathbf l=\phi(C_{\rm end})-\phi(C_{\rm start})=0}
$$

for every closed piecewise smooth loop. Constructing the global [vector-field potential](../../../potential-of-a-conservative-vector-field.md) avoids relying on a zero-[curl](../../../curl.md) test without checking the topology of the domain.
