<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $-1\le y\le0$, a horizontal slice runs from $x=-1$ to the line $x=y$. For $0\le y\le1$, it runs from $x=-1$ to the negative branch $x=-\sqrt y$ of the [parabola](../../../../parabola.md). Thus the reversed [iterated integral](../../../../iterated-integral.md) is

$$
\boxed{\int_{-1}^{0}\int_{-1}^{y}y\,dx\,dy
+\int_0^1\int_{-1}^{-\sqrt y}y\,dx\,dy.}
$$

The positive branch $x=\sqrt y$ is outside the original [integration region](../../../../domain-of-integration.md).
