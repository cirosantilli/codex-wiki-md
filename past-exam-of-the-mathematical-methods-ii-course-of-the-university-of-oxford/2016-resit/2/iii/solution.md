<h1 id="2/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

In the original [order of integration](../../../../order-of-integration.md),

$$
I=\frac12\int_{-1}^0(x^4-x^2)\,dx
=\frac12\left(\frac15-\frac13\right)=\boxed{-\frac1{15}.}
$$

In the reversed order, the below-axis contribution is $\int_{-1}^0y(1+y)\,dy=-1/6$, and the above-axis contribution is $\int_0^1y(1-\sqrt y)\,dy=1/10$. Their sum is again $-1/15$. A negative answer is possible because the [integrand](../../../../integrand.md) $y$ changes sign; the integral is not the [area](../../../../surface-area.md) of the region.
