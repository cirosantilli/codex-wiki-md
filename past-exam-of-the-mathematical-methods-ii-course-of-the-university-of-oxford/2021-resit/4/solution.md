<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

Differentiate the [parametric curve](../../../parametric-curve.md):

$$
\dot x=at\cos t,\qquad \dot y=at\sin t.
$$

The [arc length](../../../arc-length.md) element is $ds=\sqrt{\dot x^2+\dot y^2}\,dt=|a|t\,dt$ on $0\le t\le2$. Thus

$$
\boxed{L=\int_0^2|a|t\,dt=2|a|.}
$$

For a positive geometric scale $a$, this is $2a$. The absolute value is necessary when the unspecified real constant $a$ is negative; $a=0$ gives zero [arc length](../../../arc-length.md).
