<h1 id="3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

Apply the [product-to-sum formula](../../../product-to-sum-formula.md) $2\cos u\cos v=\cos(u+v)+\cos(u-v)$ to the [amplitude modulation](../../../amplitude-modulation.md):

$$
\boxed{A=a\cos(\omega t-kx)
+\frac b2\cos\bigl((\omega+\omega_m)t-kx\bigr)
+\frac b2\cos\bigl((\omega-\omega_m)t-kx\bigr).}
$$

The [amplitude modulation sidebands](../../../amplitude-modulation-sidebands.md) have [angular frequencies](../../../angular-frequency.md) $\omega+\omega_m$ and $\omega-\omega_m$, each with [amplitude](../../../wave-amplitude.md) $b/2$ in this real [cosine](../../../cosine.md) convention. The [carrier wave](../../../carrier-wave.md) has [angular frequency](../../../angular-frequency.md) $\omega$ and [amplitude](../../../wave-amplitude.md) $a$.

This identity is exact for the supplied space-time expression. A physical qualification is needed if every term is meant to be a freely propagating [electromagnetic wave](../../../electromagnetic-wave-split.md) with [speed of light](../../../speed-of-light.md) $c$: the [dispersion relation](../../../dispersion-relation-split.md) requires $k_\pm=(\omega\pm\omega_m)/c$, whereas the prescribed modulation at every position leaves all three [wavenumbers](../../../wavenumber.md) equal to $k=\omega/c$. A freely propagating modulated [wave](../../../wave-split.md) instead has envelope $a+b\cos(\omega_m(t-x/c))$; its analogous expansion has $k_\pm$. For example, at $x=t=0$ the prescribed expression has $A_{tt}-c^2A_{xx}=-b\omega_m^2$, which is nonzero for nontrivial modulation. The given formula can describe modulation imposed throughout space or the temporal signal at one position.
