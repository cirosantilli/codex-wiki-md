<h1 id="9/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Regard the planar [vector field](../../../../vector-field.md) as $(A(x,y),B(x,y),0)$, with no dependence on $z$. Its [curl](../../../../curl.md) is

$$
\nabla\times\mathbf V=
\begin{vmatrix}
\mathbf i&\mathbf j&\mathbf k\\
\partial_x&\partial_y&\partial_z\\
A&B&0
\end{vmatrix}
=\left(\frac{\partial B}{\partial x}-\frac{\partial A}{\partial y}\right)\mathbf k.
$$

Thus, for continuously differentiable $A$ and $B$, the necessary and sufficient pointwise condition is

$$
\boxed{\frac{\partial B}{\partial x}=\frac{\partial A}{\partial y}.}
$$

To deduce global [path independence](../../../../path-independence.md) from this local condition also requires a suitable domain, for example a [simply connected domain](../../../../simply-connected-domain.md).
