↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Integrating the [probability density function](../../../../../probability-density-function.md) over the stated interval gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(T<8)=\int_0^8\frac1{25}e^{-t/25}\,dt
=1-e^{-8/25}=0.274\quad\text{(three significant figures)}.}
$$

The strict endpoint does not affect the [probability](../../../../../probability.md) for this [continuous probability distribution](../../../../../continuous-probability-distribution-split.md).
