<h1 id="7/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Subtract the two values of the [survival function](../../../../../survival-function.md), or integrate the [probability density function](../../../../../probability-density-function.md) directly:

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(14<T<30)
=\int_{14}^{30}\frac1{25}e^{-t/25}\,dt
=e^{-14/25}-e^{-30/25}
=0.270\quad\text{(three significant figures)}.}
$$

The final zero records the requested third significant figure.
