<h1 id="7/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [survival function](../../../../../survival-function.md) of this [exponential distribution](../../../../../exponential-distribution.md) is $\mathbb P(T>t)=e^{-t/25}$ for $t\ge0$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(T>20)=\int_{20}^\infty\frac1{25}e^{-t/25}\,dt
=e^{-20/25}=0.449\quad\text{(three significant figures)}.}
$$
