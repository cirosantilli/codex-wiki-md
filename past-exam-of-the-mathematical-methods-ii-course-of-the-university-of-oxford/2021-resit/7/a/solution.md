<h1 id="7/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [probability density function](../../../../probability-density-function.md) is nonnegative and vanishes on negative lifetimes. Since $0.04=1/25$, its [normalization of a probability distribution](../../../../normalization-of-a-probability-distribution.md) is

$$
\boxed{\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f_T(t)\,dt
=\int_0^\infty\frac1{25}e^{-t/25}\,dt
=\left[-e^{-t/25}\right]_0^\infty=1.}
$$

It is an [exponential distribution](../../../../exponential-distribution.md) with rate $1/25$ per month.
