<h1 id="11/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

To find the [normal modes of two arc-spring-coupled beads on a hoop](../../../../normal-modes-of-two-arc-spring-coupled-beads-on-a-hoop.md), take $x_i=a_i e^{i\Omega t}$ for a [normal mode](../../../../normal-mode.md). The resulting [generalized eigenvalue problem](../../../../generalized-eigenvalue-problem.md) is

$$
\begin{pmatrix}
2k-m_1\Omega^2&-2k\\
-2k&2k-qm_1\Omega^2
\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}a_1\\a_2\end{pmatrix}=0.
$$

Its [determinant](../../../../determinant.md) is

$$
(2k-m_1\Omega^2)(2k-qm_1\Omega^2)-4k^2
=m_1\Omega^2\left(qm_1\Omega^2-2k(1+q)\right).
$$

Thus the [angular frequencies](../../../../angular-frequency.md) are

$$
\boxed{\Omega_0=0,\qquad
\omega=\sqrt{\frac{2k(1+q)}{qm_1}}.}
$$

The ordinary nonzero [frequency](../../../../frequency.md) is $\omega/(2\pi)$. The [zero mode](../../../../zero-mode.md) has [mode shape](../../../../mode-shape.md) $(1,1)$ and represents a common [rotation](../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) with no change in [spring](../../../../spring.md) length. The oscillatory [mode shape](../../../../mode-shape.md) is $(1,-1/q)$.
