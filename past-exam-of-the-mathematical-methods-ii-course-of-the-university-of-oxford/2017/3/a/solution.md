<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Expand $f(s)=\sqrt s$ about $s=25$, with increment one. Since $f'(25)=1/10$ and $f''(25)=-1/500$, the second-order [Taylor series](../../../../taylor-series.md) approximation is

$$
\boxed{\sqrt{26}\simeq5+\frac1{10}-\frac1{1000}=5.099.}
$$

The third derivative is $3/(8s^{5/2})>0$, decreasing on $[25,26]$. The [Taylor remainder](../../../../taylor-remainder.md) is therefore positive and less than $f'''(25)/6=1/50000$, so $0<\sqrt{26}-5.099<0.00002$.
