<h1 id="1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

For an [exact differential](../../../exact-differential.md) $Mdx+Ndy$, equality of the [mixed partial derivatives](../../../mixed-partial-derivative.md) requires $M_y=N_x$. Here these are $\sin x+b\cos y$ and $\sin x+3\cos y$, so $\boxed{b=3}$. Integrating $M$ with respect to $x$ gives $f=-y\cos x+3x\sin y+g(y)$. Comparison with $N$ gives $g'=y^2$, and the specified value fixes the integration constant. Therefore

$$
\boxed{f(x,y)=-y\cos x+3x\sin y+\frac{y^3}{3}-\frac23.}
$$

Differentiating recovers both coefficients, and $f(0,2)=-2+8/3-2/3=0$. The explicit [potential function](../../../potential-function.md) also proves exactness globally, without relying only on a local compatibility test.
