<h1 id="8/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [divergence theorem](../../../../divergence-theorem.md) states $\iint_SF\cdot n\,dS=\iiint_V\nabla\cdot F\,dV$ for a sufficiently smooth field and a piecewise smooth closed boundary with outward [unit normal](../../../../unit-normal.md). Choose $F=r/3$. Since $\nabla\cdot r=3$,

$$
\boxed{V=\frac13\iint_Sn\cdot r\,dS.}
$$

The factor $1/3$ comes from the three spatial dimensions; the outward orientation is especially important on the inner wall of the drilled solid.
