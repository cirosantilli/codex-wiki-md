<h1 id="8/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $a=h/2$ and place the hole axis along $z$. At the rim, $r_0^2=R^2-a^2$. A horizontal slice at $|z|\le a$ is an annulus of area $\pi[(R^2-z^2)-r_0^2]=\pi(a^2-z^2)$. Thus the [napkin ring problem](../../../../napkin-ring-problem.md) gives

$$
\boxed{V=\pi\int_{-a}^{a}(a^2-z^2)\,dz
=\frac{4\pi a^3}{3}=\frac{\pi h^3}{6}.}
$$

Each slice depends only on $a$ and $z$, explaining the independence from the original sphere radius, rather than obtaining it merely by cancellation after integration.
