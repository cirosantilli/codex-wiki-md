<h1 id="9/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In one [normal mode](../../../../normal-mode.md), all moving bobs share the same frequency and fixed amplitude ratios. A real mode vector allows their phases to be chosen equal or differing by $\pi$, according to the signs of its components. A component equal to zero means that bob remains at rest in that mode and has no oscillation phase to assign.
