<h1 id="9/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For small horizontal displacement $x_i\simeq l\theta_i$, the gravitational restoring force is $-mg\theta_i\simeq-(mg/l)x_i$. A spring between adjacent bobs has extension equal to their displacement difference; [Hooke's law](../../../../hooke-s-law.md) pulls each bob towards its neighbour's displaced position. Thus comparison with the stated equations gives

$$
\boxed{a=\frac gl,\qquad b=k.}
$$

The small-angle approximation makes the bob motion horizontal to first order; its vertical shift changes spring geometry only at higher order. Multiplying all force equations by minus one puts the positive restoring coefficients on the displayed right-hand side.
