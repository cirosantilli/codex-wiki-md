<h1 id="6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

A [standing wave](../../../standing-wave.md) has a fixed spatial amplitude profile and oscillates in time, with stationary [nodes of a standing wave](../../../node-physics.md). It arises from equal-amplitude waves of the same frequency travelling in opposite directions. For a uniform string with [linear mass density](../../../linear-mass-density.md) $\mu$, the [wave speed](../../../wave-speed.md) is $c=\sqrt{T/\mu}$ and a separated mode has $Y''+k^2Y=0$.

The fixed boundary requires $Y(0)=0$, while the free boundary requires $TY'(L)=0$. Thus $Y=A\sin kx$ and $\cos kL=0$, giving

$$
\boxed{k_n=\frac{(n+1/2)\pi}{L},\qquad
y_n(x,t)=A_n\sin(k_nx)\cos(ck_nt+\phi_n),\quad n=0,1,2,\ldots.}
$$

The fixed end is a [node of a standing wave](../../../node-physics.md) and the free end an [antinode](../../../antinode.md). Arbitrary small motions are superpositions of these [normal modes](../../../normal-mode.md); the [tension](../../../tension.md) alone determines their frequencies only when $\mu$ is also specified.
