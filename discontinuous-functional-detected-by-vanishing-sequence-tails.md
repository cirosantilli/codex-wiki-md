# Discontinuous functional detected by vanishing sequence tails

↑ **Parent:** [Linear functional](linear-functional.md)

If a [linear functional](linear-functional.md) on a [normed vector space](normed-vector-space.md) has $f(v_N)=1$ for a sequence tending to zero in norm, it is discontinuous and unbounded. An explicit example is the algebraic span in $\ell^2$ of the coordinate vectors and $w=(2^{-n})_{n\geq1}$. Define $f(w)=1$ and $f(e_n)=0$. The tails $v_N=w-\sum_{n=1}^N2^{-n}e_n$ have norm $2^{-N}/\sqrt3$ but image one. Thus the functional vanishes on the dense finite-support subspace while being nonzero elsewhere.
