# Angular momentum ladder operator

↑ **Parent:** [Orbital angular momentum](orbital-angular-momentum.md)

The operators $L_\pm=L_x\pm iL_y$ satisfy

$$
[L_z,L_\pm]=\pm\hbar L_\pm,
\qquad
[L^2,L_\pm]=0.
$$

They therefore change the [magnetic quantum number](magnetic-quantum-number.md) $m$ by $\pm1$ while preserving the total angular-momentum quantum number $\ell$.
