<h1 id="rossler-system">Rössler system</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamical systems](dynamical-systems-split.md)

An [autonomous differential equation](autonomous-system-mathematics.md) in three variables. Its linear planar rotation couples to a nonlinear vertical variable through $xz$. Depending on parameters, its solutions can approach an [equilibrium point](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md), a [periodic orbit](periodic-orbit.md), or a more complicated [attractor](attractor.md). The specialization $b=a$ is used in the 2011 Oxford combined paper.
