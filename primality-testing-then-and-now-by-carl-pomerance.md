# Primality testing: then and now by Carl Pomerance

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematics bibliography](mathematics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by Carl Pomerance](work-by-carl-pomerance.md)

By [Carl Pomerance](carl-pomerance.md). Slides for the ICERM meeting "Seventy-five years of Mathematics of Computation", 1–3 November 2018.

Online text: [https://math.dartmouth.edu/~carlp/mathcomptalk2.pdf](https://math.dartmouth.edu/~carlp/mathcomptalk2.pdf)

Cited passages: slide 14 states [Pépin's test](pepin-s-test.md) and outlines its proof; slides 15–16 describe [Fermat numbers](fermat-number.md) and their role in [integer factorization](integer-factorization.md); slide 18 gives the [Gauss-Wantzel theorem](gauss-wantzel-theorem.md) and distinguishes Gauss's construction result from Wantzel's necessity proof. Numerical records elsewhere in these 2018 slides are historical records, not claims about current records.
