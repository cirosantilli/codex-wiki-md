# Piwi-interacting RNA

↑ **Parent:** [Small regulatory RNA](small-regulatory-rna.md)

A [guide RNA](guide-rna.md) associated with Piwi-family [proteins](protein-split.md). Most characterized [animal](animal.md) [piRNA](piwi-interacting-rna.md) pathways use [Dicer](dicer.md)-independent processing of single-stranded precursors; the approximately 21-nucleotide C. elegans 21U [RNAs](ribonucleic-acid.md) are distinct from the longer typical fly and mammalian piRNAs.[2009, pp. 522–535, somatic and germline pathway-mutant comparisons](specialized-pirna-pathways-act-in-germline-and-somatic-tissues-of-the-drosophila-ovary-by-colin-d-malone-and-others.md)
