# Polarized fermionic Fock space

↑ **Parent:** [Fermionic Fock space](fermionic-fock-space.md)

An orthogonal projection $P$ splits a one-particle [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) into empty and filled modes. Take completed exterior Fock spaces of empty particles and of holes in the filled modes, with the graded tensor product enforcing the [canonical anticommutation relations](canonical-anticommutation-relations.md) between both factors. The vacuum is killed by particle annihilation in $PH$ and by particle creation in $(I-P)H$. Only finite particle-hole excitations are needed for a dense basis. Normal ordering subtracts the filled-vacuum expectation from bilinear observables.
