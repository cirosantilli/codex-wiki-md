# Harmonic extension in a circular sector

↑ **Parent:** [Laplace equation in polar coordinates](laplace-equation-in-polar-coordinates.md)

For $0<\theta<\gamma$ and $0<r<R$, a bounded [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md) that vanishes on the straight edges and has outer boundary data $\varphi$ has coefficients

$$
b_n=\frac2\gamma\int_0^\gamma\varphi(\theta)\sin\frac{n\pi\theta}\gamma\,d\theta.
$$

This follows by [separation of variables](separation-of-variables.md) and [Fourier orthogonality](fourier-orthogonality.md). Negative radial powers are excluded by boundedness at the vertex. Reflection-symmetric data $\varphi(\theta)=\varphi(\gamma-\theta)$ have only odd [Fourier modes](fourier-mode.md). Knowing the resulting solution on the bisector determines all these odd coefficients by its radial [power series](power-series.md). Without reflection symmetry, the even angular modes vanish on the bisector and cannot be recovered from that restriction alone.
