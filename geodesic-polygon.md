# Geodesic polygon

↑ **Parent:** [Riemannian geometry](riemannian-geometry-split.md)

A geodesic polygon is a closed polygonal curve whose sides are [geodesic segments](geodesic-segment.md). A [geodesic triangle](geodesic-triangle.md) has three sides. Surface angle excess is governed by the [Gauss-Bonnet theorem](gauss-bonnet-theorem.md).
