# Dihydrotestosterone

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical compound](chemical-compound.md)

[Dihydrotestosterone](dihydrotestosterone.md) is the 5α-reduced steroid formed from [testosterone](testosterone.md). Reduction saturates the C4=C5 bond while retaining the C3 ketone and the other steroid functional groups.
