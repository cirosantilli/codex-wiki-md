# Dislocation junction

↑ **Parent:** [Dislocation](dislocation.md)

A line segment formed by interaction of dislocations from different slip planes. A junction can impede further [dislocation glide](dislocation-glide.md). Energetic favourability alone does not establish whether the resulting segment is mobile on a particular plane.
