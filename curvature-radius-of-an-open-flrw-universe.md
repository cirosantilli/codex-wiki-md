# Curvature radius of an open FLRW universe

↑ **Parent:** [Friedmann-Lemaître-Robertson-Walker metric](friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md)

Normalize the present [scale factor](scale-factor-cosmology.md) to one. In a dust-only open cosmology with no [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md), the [Friedmann equation](friedmann-equations.md) gives $-kc^2=H_0^2(1-\Omega_m)$. The present comoving and physical curvature radii are therefore $r_c=c/[H_0\sqrt{1-\Omega_m}]$. At another epoch the physical curvature radius is $a r_c$.
