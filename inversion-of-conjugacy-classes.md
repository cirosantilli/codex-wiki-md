# Inversion of conjugacy classes

↑ **Parent:** [Conjugacy class](conjugacy-class.md)

For a [group](group-split.md) $G$, inversion sends the [conjugacy class](conjugacy-class.md) of $g$ to that of $g^{-1}$, because $(xgx^{-1})^{-1}=xg^{-1}x^{-1}$. It therefore defines an [involution](involution.md) on the collection of [conjugacy classes](conjugacy-class.md), even in a noncommutative [group](group-split.md) where inversion is not a [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md).
