# Schwarzschild circular-ring energy shift

↑ **Parent:** [Comparison of circular-orbit and static Schwarzschild clocks](comparison-of-circular-orbit-and-static-schwarzschild-clocks.md)

For the receding edge of a circular emitter ring, the direct-projection model gives $1+z=(1-3r_g/r)^{-1/2}[1+\sqrt{r_g/(r-2r_g)}\sin i]$, with $r_g=GM/c^2$. Gravitational and transverse shifts combine with the line-of-sight [Doppler factor](doppler-factor.md). Mapping the distant inclination directly to a local [photon](photon.md) direction neglects gravitational ray bending.
