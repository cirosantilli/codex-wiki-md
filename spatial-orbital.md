# Spatial orbital

↑ **Parent:** [Pauli exclusion principle](pauli-exclusion-principle.md)

A [spatial orbital](spatial-orbital.md) is a normalized spatial [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) used in a one-particle description. An [atomic orbital](atomic-orbital.md) is the corresponding notion for a [chemical atom](atom-physics.md); a [molecular orbital](molecular-orbital.md) belongs to a [molecule](molecule-split.md). A [spatial orbital](spatial-orbital.md) is not a trajectory and does not by itself specify [spin](spin.md).
