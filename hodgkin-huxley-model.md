# Hodgkin-Huxley model

↑ **Parent:** [Computational neuroscience](computational-neuroscience.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hodgkin-Huxley_model)

A conductance-based model couples [membrane potential](membrane-potential.md) to voltage-dependent channel-gating variables. It explains generation of [action potentials](action-potential.md) through regenerative and opposing ionic currents, while its parameters and channel assumptions determine its biological domain of validity.
