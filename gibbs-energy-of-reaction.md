# Gibbs energy of reaction

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical equilibrium](chemical-equilibrium.md)

The derivative of [Gibbs energy](gibbs-free-energy.md) with respect to reaction extent at fixed [temperature](temperature-split.md) and [pressure](pressure-split.md): $\Delta_rG=\sum_i\nu_i\mu_i$. For a reversible electrochemical cell reaction, $\Delta_rG=-nFE$.
