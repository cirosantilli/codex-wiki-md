# Three-dimensional parabolic interband joint density of states

↑ **Parent:** [Joint density of states](joint-density-of-states.md)

For isotropic direct-gap parabolic bands, $m_r^{-1}=m_e^{*-1}+m_h^{*-1}$ and transition [energy](energy-split.md) is $E_g+\hbar^2k^2/(2m_r)$. Differentiating the two-spin state count $k^3/(3\pi^2)$ per volume gives the displayed density for $E>E_g$, and zero below. Density per [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) is $\hbar g_j(\hbar\omega)$, not the same dimensioned quantity.
