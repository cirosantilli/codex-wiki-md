# Target-change locality of SLE6

↑ **Parent:** [Locality property of SLE](locality-property-of-sle.md)

Changing the target boundary point by a [conformal automorphism of the upper half-plane](conformal-automorphism-of-the-upper-half-plane.md) preserves the local chordal [SLE](schramm-loewner-evolution.md) law at parameter six, up to a capacity time change and until the relevant boundary point is swallowed. The [transformed SLE driving function](transformed-sle-driving-function.md) has zero drift at six; the [Dambis-Dubins-Schwarz theorem](dambis-dubins-schwarz-theorem.md) makes it Brownian in image capacity time.
