# Mean transverse force from a rocking rod

↑ **Parent:** [Oscillating rigid rod in resistive-force theory](oscillating-rigid-rod-in-resistive-force-theory.md)

For $x=\epsilon a\cos\omega t$ and a tangent $(\sin\theta,\cos\theta)$ with $\theta=\epsilon\cos(\omega t+\phi)$, the leading [force](force.md) on the fluid is $\langle F_y\rangle=-(c_\perp-c_\parallel)L\epsilon^2a\omega\sin\phi/2$. The sign changes under reversal of the tilt convention. Quadrature strokes maximize its magnitude; reciprocal strokes give zero, consistent with [kinematic reversibility of Stokes flow](kinematic-reversibility-of-stokes-flow.md).
