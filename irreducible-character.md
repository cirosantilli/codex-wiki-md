# Irreducible character

↑ **Parent:** [Character of a representation](character-of-a-representation.md)

An irreducible character is the character of an [irreducible representation](irreducible-representation.md). Over the complex numbers, every finite-group character decomposes uniquely as a nonnegative integer combination of irreducible characters.
