# Tits form of a quiver

↑ **Parent:** [Ringel form](ringel-form.md)

The quadratic form is $q_Q(\mathbf n)=\langle\mathbf n,\mathbf n\rangle_Q=\sum_i n_i^2-\sum_{\rho:i\to j}n_in_j$. It depends on the underlying graph rather than its orientation. For a representation it equals $\dim\operatorname{End}_Q(X)-\dim\operatorname{Ext}^1_Q(X,X)$. Its positive definiteness forces indecomposables to be rigid [brick modules](brick-module.md), using the [Ringel lemma on bricks](ringel-lemma-on-bricks.md).
