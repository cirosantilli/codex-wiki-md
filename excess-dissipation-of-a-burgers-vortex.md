# Excess dissipation of a Burgers vortex

↑ **Parent:** [Burgers vortex](burgers-vortex.md)

The swirl [viscous dissipation](viscous-dissipation.md) of a steady [Burgers vortex](burgers-vortex.md), integrated per unit length and per unit density, is $\nu\int\omega_z^2\,dA=\alpha\Gamma^2/(8\pi)$. It remains finite as [kinematic viscosity](kinematic-viscosity.md) tends to zero at fixed strain and [circulation](circulation-physics.md). This is excess over the background strain: the uniform strain itself has infinite total [viscous dissipation](viscous-dissipation.md) over an infinite cross-section.
