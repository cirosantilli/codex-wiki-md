# Repulsive spherical barrier scattering length

↑ **Parent:** [Scattering length from a partial-wave S-matrix](scattering-length-from-a-partial-wave-s-matrix.md)

For a barrier of radius $a$ and potential $\hbar^2\gamma^2/(2m)$, matching the regular inner hyperbolic-sine wave to the outer sine wave gives $a_s=a-\tanh(\gamma a)/\gamma$. The low-energy $s$-wave [scattering amplitude](scattering-amplitude.md) is $-a_s$.
