# Isotopologue

↑ **Parent:** [Isotope](isotope.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isotopologue)

A molecular species differing from another only in its isotopic composition. Different [reduced masses](reduced-mass.md) alter vibrational and rotational spacings even when the underlying electronic potential is nearly the same.
