# Auxiliary-field transfer operator for an Ising chain

↑ **Parent:** [Classical Ising chain as a quantum particle](classical-ising-chain-as-a-quantum-particle.md)

For a nearest-neighbor auxiliary-field representation of an [Ising model](ising-model.md), sharing each local potential between adjacent bonds gives the displayed symmetric [integral operator](integral-operator.md). Its periodic [partition function](canonical-partition-function.md) is the trace of its $N$th power, up to Gaussian normalization constants. In the slowly varying continuum limit, a slice of length $\epsilon$ has kernel $[M/(2\pi\epsilon)]^{1/2}\exp[-M(m'-m)^2/(2\epsilon)-\epsilon\{U(m')+U(m)\}/2]$. Expanding its action on a smooth function gives $1-\epsilon\widehat H+O(\epsilon^2)$, with $\widehat H=-(2M)^{-1}\partial_m^2+U$. Thus fixed endpoints give an imaginary-time transition kernel and periodic endpoints give a [Euclidean path integral](euclidean-path-integral.md) trace. The finite-spacing integral operator and the continuum differential operator need not have identical high-energy spectra.
