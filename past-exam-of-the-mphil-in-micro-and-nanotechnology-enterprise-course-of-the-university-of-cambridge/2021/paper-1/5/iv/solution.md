<h1 id="5/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

One design is an **[anthracene](../../../../../anthracene.md)–methylene–[aza-15-crown-5](../../../../../aza-15-crown-5.md) sensor**, with the [tertiary amine](../../../../../tertiary-amine.md) of the aza-crown linked through $\mathrm{CH_2}$ to [anthracene](../../../../../anthracene.md). The crown supplies the sodium-binding [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md)/nitrogen cavity, the [amine](../../../../../amine.md) supplies an [electron donor](../../../../../electron-donor.md) and [anthracene](../../../../../anthracene.md) supplies the [fluorophore](../../../../../fluorophore.md). A sufficiently rigid sodium-sized [cryptand](../../../../../cryptand.md) could improve discrimination further.

Without a bound [cation](../../../../../cation.md), [photoinduced electron transfer](../../../../../photoinduced-electron-transfer.md) from the [amine](../../../../../amine.md) can quench excited [anthracene](../../../../../anthracene.md). [sodium](../../../../../sodium.md) coordination lowers the electron-donating ability of that lone pair, suppressing this quenching route and increasing [fluorescence](../../../../../fluorescence-split.md). The methylene spacer allows electronic communication by [electron](../../../../../electron.md) transfer while limiting direct conjugation between the binding site and [fluorophore](../../../../../fluorophore.md).

A cavity appropriate to [sodium](../../../../../sodium.md) is preferable to an 18-crown-6-like potassium-sized cavity. **Selectivity must be tested, not inferred perfectly from cavity size.** Potassium binding, including possible sandwich complexes of flexible crowns, can still compete. At the intended [concentrations](../../../../../concentration.md) the design should satisfy $K_{\mathrm{Na}}[\mathrm{Na^+}]\gg K_{\mathrm K}[\mathrm{K^+}]$ and should give a [fluorescence](../../../../../fluorescence-split.md) change on [sodium](../../../../../sodium.md) binding. Rigidity, cavity size and suppression of competing stoichiometries are useful design controls. [fluorescence](../../../../../fluorescence-split.md) calibration in the actual potassium-containing [solvent](../../../../../solvent.md) establishes the usable detection range.
