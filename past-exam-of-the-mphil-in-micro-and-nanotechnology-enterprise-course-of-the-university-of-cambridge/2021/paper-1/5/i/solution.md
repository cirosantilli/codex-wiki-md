<h1 id="5/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The important change is **preorganization of the chloride-binding [amide](../../../../../amide.md) groups**. In the hydroxylated host, intramolecular [hydrogen bonds](../../../../../hydrogen-bond.md) from the phenolic OH groups to the [amide](../../../../../amide.md) [carbonyl](../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) [oxygens](../../../../../oxygen.md) restrict rotation and favor a geometry in which the [amide](../../../../../amide.md) NH donors converge on the anion-binding cavity. [chloride](../../../../../chloride.md) can therefore accept two favorable [hydrogen bonds](../../../../../hydrogen-bond.md) with a smaller conformational penalty than in the unsubstituted host. The hydroxyl groups need not be counted as two additional freely available [chloride](../../../../../chloride.md) donors: their role includes organizing the existing donors.

The [association constant](../../../../../association-constant.md) increases by $5230/195=26.8$. At $298\,\mathrm K$, the corresponding difference in standard [Gibbs free energy](../../../../../gibbs-free-energy.md) is

$$
\Delta\Delta G^\circ=-RT\ln(5230/195)\simeq\boxed{-8.15\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}}.
$$

This is a modest molecular free-energy advantage amplified into a substantial change in the [equilibrium constant](../../../../../equilibrium-constant.md). [solvent](../../../../../solvent.md) competition and changes in [solvation](../../../../../solvation.md) also contribute to measured binding; the structures chiefly suggest the preorganization explanation.
