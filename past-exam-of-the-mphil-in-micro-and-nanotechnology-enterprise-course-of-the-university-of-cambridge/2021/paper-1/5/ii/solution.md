The [molecule](../../../../../molecule-split.md) combines three chemically different binding regions.

- **[sodium](../../../../../sodium.md): the oxygen-rich [calixarene](../../../../../calixarene.md)/[polyether](../../../../../polyether.md) region**, including the [ether](../../../../../ether.md) [oxygens](../../../../../oxygen.md) and suitably oriented [ester](../../../../../ester.md) [carbonyl](../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) [oxygens](../../../../../oxygen.md). [sodium](../../../../../sodium.md) is a hard [Lewis acid](../../../../../lewis-acid.md) and favors multidentate coordination by hard [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) donors. The scaffold holds several donors near one another and can reduce the conformational cost of binding.
- **[nitrate](../../../../../nitrate.md): the two [urea](../../../../../urea.md) groups.** Their NH groups donate [hydrogen bonds](../../../../../hydrogen-bond.md) to [nitrate](../../../../../nitrate.md) [oxygens](../../../../../oxygen.md). Several NH donors can cooperate around an [anion](../../../../../anion.md); the [carbonyl](../../../../../carbonyl-group.md) [oxygen](../../../../../oxygen.md) of a [urea](../../../../../urea.md) is an acceptor and is not the proposed nitrate-binding donor.
- **[silver](../../../../../silver.md): the terminal bipyridyl nitrogen donors.** The [pyridine](../../../../../pyridine.md) lone pairs coordinate the softer [silver](../../../../../silver.md) [ion](../../../../../ion.md). The actual coordination may involve donor sites on more than one arm or more than one receptor, depending on geometry and [concentration](../../../../../concentration.md).

These assignments identify the preferred local sites, rather than guaranteeing three independent binding constants: occupancy can alter [conformation](../../../../../conformation.md), electrostatics and the affinity of neighboring sites.
