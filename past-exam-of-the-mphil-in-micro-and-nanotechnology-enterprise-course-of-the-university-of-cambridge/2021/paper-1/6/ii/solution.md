<h1 id="6/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Clean and degrease the [nickel](../../../../../nickel.md) surface, remove its inhibiting [oxide](../../../../../oxide.md) by an appropriate brief surface treatment, rinse, and immerse it in an aqueous bath containing reducible [gold](../../../../../gold.md)(III) species. Control [concentration](../../../../../concentration.md), [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) and immersion time, then rinse after the desired deposition. A solution of a [gold](../../../../../gold.md) complex is practical, but its actual [reduction](../../../../../reduction-chemistry.md) potential must be evaluated for that complex and bath: it need not equal the supplied free-ion standard potential.

At anodic sites [nickel](../../../../../nickel.md) dissolves as $\mathrm{Ni^{2+}}$, releasing [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) into the conducting substrate. Those [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) reduce [gold](../../../../../gold.md) [ions](../../../../../ion.md) at cathodic sites, nucleating and growing metallic [gold](../../../../../gold.md). The [nickel](../../../../../nickel.md) itself is the reducing agent, so **no external electrical supply or separate reducing reagent is required**. The coupled [electron](../../../../../electron.md) balance is three [nickel](../../../../../nickel.md) atoms dissolved for two [gold](../../../../../gold.md) atoms deposited for the stated [oxidation states](../../../../../oxidation-state.md).

The process can slow or stop when a sufficiently continuous [gold](../../../../../gold.md) coating prevents the [electrolyte](../../../../../electrolyte.md) from reaching [nickel](../../../../../nickel.md). This is not an absolute thickness rule: pores, cracks or exposed edges preserve [nickel](../../../../../nickel.md) dissolution and allow continued deposition, sometimes with undercutting. [nickel](../../../../../nickel.md) [passivation](../../../../../passivation.md), depletion of [gold](../../../../../gold.md) [ions](../../../../../ion.md) near the surface, [diffusion](../../../../../diffusion.md) through a porous layer, bath speciation and competing reactions also affect the rate. Dense [gold](../../../../../gold.md) coverage and porous [gold](../../../../../gold.md) coverage therefore have very different limiting behavior.
