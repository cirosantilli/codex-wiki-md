<h1 id="6/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Use a shaped [nickel](../../../../../nickel.md) particle or [nanowire](../../../../../nanowire.md) as both **sacrificial template and reducing agent**. [gold](../../../../../gold.md) grows where [nickel](../../../../../nickel.md) is consumed, allowing gold-coated particles, porous shells and, with suitable removal of the remaining [nickel](../../../../../nickel.md), hollow [gold](../../../../../gold.md) [nanostructures](../../../../../nanostructure.md). The original template controls the overall shape; the balance of dissolution, deposition and [diffusion](../../../../../diffusion.md) controls wall thickness and [porosity](../../../../../porosity.md).

A hollow product is not guaranteed simply by writing the reaction: deposition may seal a solid [nickel](../../../../../nickel.md) core, whereas defects can maintain access to the interior and permit further replacement. Controlled composition and transport can give a progression from core–shell to porous or hollow structures. This is useful for high-surface-area [electrodes](../../../../../electrode.md), catalytic surfaces and plasmonic structures, while avoiding an electrical lead to every individual [nanoparticle](../../../../../nanoparticle.md).
