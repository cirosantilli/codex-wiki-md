<h1 id="6/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The species that oxidizes [nickel](../../../../../nickel.md) is **[gold](../../../../../gold.md)(III) [ion](../../../../../ion.md)**, rather than metallic [gold](../../../../../gold.md). The [half-reactions](../../../../../half-reaction.md) are

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Ni}&\longrightarrow\mathrm{Ni^{2+}}+2e^-,\\
\mathrm{Au^{3+}}+3e^-&\longrightarrow\mathrm{Au}.
\end{aligned}
$$

Multiplying the first by three and the second by two gives

$$
\boxed{\mathrm{3Ni+2Au^{3+}\longrightarrow3Ni^{2+}+2Au}}.
$$

Standard [electrode potentials](../../../../../electrode-potential.md) are not multiplied by the balancing factors. Thus $E^\circ_{\rm cell}=1.50-(-0.25)=1.75\,\mathrm V$ and six [moles](../../../../../mole-unit.md) of [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) are transferred per [mole](../../../../../mole-unit.md) of this balanced reaction:

$$
\boxed{\Delta G^\circ=-6FE^\circ_{\rm cell}\simeq-1.01\times10^6\,\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}}}.
$$

The negative sign predicts a thermodynamically favorable [galvanic replacement](../../../../../galvanic-replacement.md) under standard conditions.
