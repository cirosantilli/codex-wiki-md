<h1 id="9/ii/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

[forward bias](../../../../../../forward-bias.md) opposes the built-in [electric field](../../../../../../electric-field-split.md) and lowers the junction barrier from approximately $V_{\rm bi}$ to $V_{\rm bi}-V$. In the abrupt-junction [depletion approximation](../../../../../../depletion-approximation.md),

$$
W=\sqrt{\frac{2\epsilon_s}{q}\left(\frac1{N_A}+\frac1{N_D}\right)(V_{\rm bi}-V)}.
$$

Thus **the [depletion region](../../../../../../depletion-region.md) narrows** and carriers cross the reduced barrier more readily. [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) injection into the p side and hole injection into the n side increase strongly, allowing [radiative recombination](../../../../../../radiative-recombination.md) in an [LED](../../../../../../light-emitting-diode.md).

The injected minority-carrier [concentrations](../../../../../../concentration.md), and therefore the ideal [diffusion](../../../../../../diffusion.md) [electric current](../../../../../../electric-current.md), vary approximately as $\exp(qV/k_BT)$. This produces the steep forward branch of the [electric current](../../../../../../electric-current.md)–[voltage](../../../../../../voltage.md) curve. One should not extrapolate the depletion-width formula to claim that the junction must lose its entire [depletion region](../../../../../../depletion-region.md) before conducting; appreciable [electric current](../../../../../../electric-current.md) flows while a barrier remains, and high injection and series resistance eventually invalidate the simple formulas.
