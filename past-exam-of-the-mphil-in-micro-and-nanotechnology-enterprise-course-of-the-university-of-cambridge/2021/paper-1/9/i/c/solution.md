<h1 id="9/i/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Use a patterned resist or hard mask and **anisotropic chlorine-based [reactive-ion etching](../../../../../../reactive-ion-etching.md)**, for example an appropriate $\mathrm{Cl_2/BCl_3}$ [inductively coupled plasma](../../../../../../inductively-coupled-plasma.md) process for GaAs/AlGaAs. Ion-assisted removal gives a well-defined mesa with less lateral undercut than an isotropic wet etch. Control etch time or endpoint to obtain the specified approximately $1.2\,\mu\mathrm m$ depth.

The mask must survive the etch and protect the active aperture. Control [ion](../../../../../../ion.md) damage and sidewall roughness, remove residues and passivate the sidewall where required. Compatibility with the metal stack determines whether the mesa is etched before or after front-contact deposition.
