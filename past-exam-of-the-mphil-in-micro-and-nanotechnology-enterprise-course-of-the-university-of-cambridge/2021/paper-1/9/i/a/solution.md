<h1 id="9/i/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Grow the [semiconductor](../../../../../../semiconductor-split.md) stack epitaxially by **[molecular beam epitaxy](../../../../../../molecular-beam-epitaxy.md) or metalorganic vapor-phase [epitaxy](../../../../../../epitaxy.md)** on a suitable crystalline GaAs substrate. Sequential changes of source flux or precursor supply set the GaAs/AlGaAs compositions, while dopant supply sets the n-type and p-type regions. Growth time and calibrated rate control the thicknesses of the InGaAs [quantum well](../../../../../../quantum-well.md) and the alternating reflector layers.

[epitaxy](../../../../../../epitaxy.md) preserves a common crystal orientation and clean, abrupt [semiconductor](../../../../../../semiconductor-split.md) interfaces. These are important both for carrier confinement in the [quantum well](../../../../../../quantum-well.md) and for low-loss optical reflection. For a [DBR](../../../../../../distributed-bragg-reflector.md) centered at free-space [wavelength](../../../../../../wavelength.md) $\lambda_0$, the approximate quarter-wave condition is $n_jd_j=\lambda_0/4$ in each layer. Ordinary deposition of a polycrystalline coating would not provide the required active [semiconductor heterostructure](../../../../../../semiconductor-heterostructure.md).
