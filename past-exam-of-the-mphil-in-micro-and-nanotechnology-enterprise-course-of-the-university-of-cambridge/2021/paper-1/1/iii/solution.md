<h1 id="1/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

[friction force microscopy](../../../../../friction-force-microscopy.md) records [cantilever](../../../../../cantilever.md) torsion caused by lateral tip–sample force. Choose the detector sign so that [friction](../../../../../friction.md) on a flat region contributes positively during a left-to-right trace. On a common laboratory-position axis, write the two signals schematically as

$$
S_+(x)=B+G(x)+F_f(x),\qquad
S_-(x)=B+G(x)-F_f(x).
$$

Here $B$ is an offset and $G$ represents a slope-related lateral-force or detector cross-talk contribution. A reversed electronic convention reverses all plotted signs without changing the physical conclusions.

At the same controlled [normal contact force](../../../../../normal-contact-force.md) $N$, the flat-region contribution is approximately $F_f=\mu N$. **The lower [perovskite structure](../../../../../perovskite-structure.md) plateaux give a larger trace–retrace separation than the upper inorganic surface**, because their [coefficient of kinetic friction](../../../../../coefficient-of-kinetic-friction.md) is larger. Genuine [friction](../../../../../friction.md) reverses sign with scan direction, so a larger [friction](../../../../../friction.md) magnitude means a higher positive trace and a more negative retrace in this convention.

Sloped edges produce additional peaks or shoulders. The normal reaction to a slope has a lateral component, and the finite tip flank can contact the rim, so edge contrast is not a direct map of the local [friction](../../../../../friction.md) coefficient. With the two scans registered and load control maintained,

$$
\frac{S_+-S_-}{2}\simeq F_f,\qquad
\frac{S_++S_-}{2}\simeq B+G.
$$

The half-difference therefore emphasizes [friction](../../../../../friction.md) while the half-sum diagnoses common geometrical contributions. Real [contact mechanics](../../../../../contact-mechanics.md) and imperfect registration can prevent exact cancellation. The bottom panel of the figure shows these two kinds of contrast separately.
