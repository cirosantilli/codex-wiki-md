↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The reaction consumes one [mole](../../../../../../mole-unit.md) of CF for each [mole](../../../../../../mole-unit.md) of [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md). Using $M_{\rm CF}=12.01+19.00=31.01\,\mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$,

$$
\boxed{m_{\rm CF}=\frac{630720}{96500}(31.01)=202.7\,\mathrm g}.
$$

This is active CF alone for the same single-cell-equivalent assumption, not the full positive-electrode or pack mass. Conductive additive, binder, [electrolyte](../../../../../../electrolyte.md), [battery separator](../../../../../../battery-separator.md), collectors and casing add mass, and incomplete utilization increases the required CF.
