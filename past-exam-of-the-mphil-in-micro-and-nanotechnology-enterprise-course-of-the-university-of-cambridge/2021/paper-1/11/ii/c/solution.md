<h1 id="11/ii/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For the $x=1$ reaction there is one [mole](../../../../../../mole-unit.md) of transferred [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) per [mole](../../../../../../mole-unit.md) of reaction. **Treating the supplied fresh-cell [open-circuit voltage](../../../../../../open-circuit-voltage.md) as the standard equilibrium cell potential** gives

$$
\boxed{\Delta G^\circ=-FE^\circ=-(96500)(4.57)=-441005\,\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}}
\simeq-441\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}}.
$$

A fresh-cell [voltage](../../../../../../voltage.md) alone would not normally establish standard conditions or equilibrium. The result above is the formal standard-free-energy estimate under the interpretation required to use the given datum; a metastable [open-circuit voltage](../../../../../../open-circuit-voltage.md) should not be mistaken for an independently measured standard thermodynamic potential.
