<h1 id="11/ii/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Using $365$ days per year, the required time is $2\times365\times24=17520\,\mathrm h$. Hence

$$
\boxed{Q=(0.010)(17520)=175.2\,\mathrm{Ah}=630720\,\mathrm C}.
$$

[lithium](../../../../../../lithium.md) transfers one [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) per atom, so

$$
n_{\rm Li}=\frac{Q}{F}=\frac{630720}{96500}=6.53596\,\mathrm{mol},
\qquad\boxed{m_{\rm Li}=6.53596(6.94)=45.4\,\mathrm g}.
$$

This is the stoichiometric minimum for one cell, or one cell-equivalent parallel assembly, with complete utilization and no self-discharge. Practical loading must be larger. If a pack uses several cells in series, each series cell must carry the full delivered charge and the total [lithium](../../../../../../lithium.md) mass increases accordingly; no required pack [voltage](../../../../../../voltage.md) or series count is supplied.
