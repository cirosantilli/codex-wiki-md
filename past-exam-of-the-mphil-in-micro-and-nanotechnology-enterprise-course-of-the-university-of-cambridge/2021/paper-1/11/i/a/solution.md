<h1 id="11/i/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use a **lithium-metal negative [electrode](../../../../../../electrode.md) and a carbon-monofluoride composite positive [electrode](../../../../../../electrode.md)**, separated by a porous, electrically insulating [battery separator](../../../../../../battery-separator.md) wetted by a lithium-salt [electrolyte](../../../../../../electrolyte.md) in a suitable nonaqueous [solvent](../../../../../../solvent.md). Conducting [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) and a binder provide electronic contact through the fluorinated-carbon [electrode](../../../../../../electrode.md) to its [electric current](../../../../../../electric-current.md) collector; the [lithium](../../../../../../lithium.md) [electrode](../../../../../../electrode.md) has its own [electric current](../../../../../../electric-current.md) collector. A sealed stacked or wound assembly supplies useful area and contains the [electrolyte](../../../../../../electrolyte.md).

During discharge [lithium](../../../../../../lithium.md) is the [anode](../../../../../../anode.md) and the carbon-monofluoride composite is the [cathode](../../../../../../cathode.md). [lithium](../../../../../../lithium.md) [ions](../../../../../../ion.md) move through the [electrolyte](../../../../../../electrolyte.md) while [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) flow through the external load. The [battery separator](../../../../../../battery-separator.md) prevents electronic shorting while permitting ionic transport. An aqueous [electrolyte](../../../../../../electrolyte.md) is unsuitable because metallic [lithium](../../../../../../lithium.md) reacts with water. The requested very long operating time requires enough active material and low self-discharge, not merely a high nominal [voltage](../../../../../../voltage.md).
