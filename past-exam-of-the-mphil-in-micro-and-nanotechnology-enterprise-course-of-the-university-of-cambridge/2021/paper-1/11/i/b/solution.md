↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For the specified $x=1$, the discharge [half-reactions](../../../../../../half-reaction.md) and their sum are

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Anode:}\quad&\mathrm{Li\longrightarrow Li^++e^-},\\
\text{Cathode:}\quad&\mathrm{CF+Li^++e^-\longrightarrow C+LiF},\\
\text{Overall:}\quad&\boxed{\mathrm{Li+CF\longrightarrow C+LiF}}.
\end{aligned}
$$

Each [mole](../../../../../../mole-unit.md) of CF formula units consumes one [mole](../../../../../../mole-unit.md) of [lithium](../../../../../../lithium.md) and transfers one [mole](../../../../../../mole-unit.md) of [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md). For general $x$, the corresponding formula-unit reaction would be $\mathrm{CF}_x+x\mathrm{Li}\longrightarrow\mathrm C+x\mathrm{LiF}$.
