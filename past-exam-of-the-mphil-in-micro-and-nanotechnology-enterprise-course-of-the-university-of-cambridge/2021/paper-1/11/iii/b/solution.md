<h1 id="11/iii/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Increase the **electrochemically accessible area and reduce transport resistance** rather than merely forcing more [electric current](../../../../../../electric-current.md) through the same thick [electrode](../../../../../../electrode.md). A thinner, porous composite distributed over a larger area, a conductive-carbon network, good [electrolyte](../../../../../../electrolyte.md) wetting and short ionic paths reduce local [current density](../../../../../../current-density.md) and [electric polarization](../../../../../../polarization-density.md). Adequate [battery separator](../../../../../../battery-separator.md) conductivity and low-resistance collectors also help. Parallel cells can distribute total [electric current](../../../../../../electric-current.md) while retaining the total active-material inventory.

The objective is to preserve usable capacity at a higher rate by avoiding premature [voltage](../../../../../../voltage.md) cutoff and inaccessible active material. It does not create more charge: a fixed inventory with $175.2\,\mathrm{Ah}$ delivers a greater [electric current](../../../../../../electric-current.md) for a proportionally shorter time. Maintaining the original two-year duration at greater [electric current](../../../../../../electric-current.md) requires proportionally more capacity.
