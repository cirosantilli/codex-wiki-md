<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**Use low-voltage, preferably aberration-corrected [HRTEM](../../../../../high-resolution-transmission-electron-microscopy.md), with about 60–80 kV as a defensible operating range.** An approximately 80 kV starting point balances useful lattice resolution against [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) displacement damage; use the lower end if the instrument still resolves the lattice and defect stability is the priority.

A higher accelerating [voltage](../../../../../voltage.md) reduces the [electron](../../../../../electron.md) [wavelength](../../../../../wavelength.md), but can remove [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) atoms by [knock-on displacement](../../../../../knock-on-displacement.md). Measurements on pristine single-layer [graphene](../../../../../graphene.md) show a strong increase in displacement probability above the vicinity of 80 kV, so simply selecting a conventional 200–300 kV setting is a poor way to preserve the original lattice.[Beam-energy dependence of displacement cross sections, Physical Review Letters 108, 196102](../../../../../accurate-measurement-of-electron-beam-induced-displacement-cross-sections-for-single-layer-graphene-by-jannik-c-meyer-and-others.md)

[reduced graphene oxide](../../../../../reduced-graphene-oxide.md) is already defective and chemically modified. Its vulnerable sites need not share the pristine-graphene threshold, and [ionization](../../../../../ionization.md), contamination and chemical etching remain possible at low [voltage](../../../../../voltage.md). Minimize dose, spread the beam while aligning and compare successive short exposures. **The [voltage](../../../../../voltage.md) choice suppresses one damage mechanism; it does not guarantee damage-free imaging.**
