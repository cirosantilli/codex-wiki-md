<h1 id="2/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**Atomic-resolution contrast is primarily [electron phase contrast](../../../../../electron-phase-contrast.md).** [elastic scattering](../../../../../elastic-scattering.md) by the specimen potential changes the phase of the transmitted [electron](../../../../../electron.md) wave. [interference](../../../../../interference-wave-propagation.md) between transmitted and diffracted components, modified by the objective lens, converts that phase change into intensity variations. Appropriate [defocus](../../../../../defocus-aberration.md), sufficiently small [optical aberrations](../../../../../optical-aberration.md), good [spatial optical coherence](../../../../../spatial-optical-coherence.md) and transfer of the [graphene](../../../../../graphene.md) [spatial frequencies](../../../../../spatial-frequency.md) are needed. At exact focus an ideal weak phase object need not give a strong visible lattice.

Mass–thickness or amplitude contrast can also distinguish thicker overlap regions, folds, contamination and support film, especially when scattered [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) are excluded by an aperture. It is comparatively weak for a clean monolayer of low-$Z$ [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md). [diffraction contrast](../../../../../diffraction-contrast.md) becomes important in crystalline, multilayer or differently oriented regions when Bragg-scattered beams are selected or excluded. Relative rotation of layers can additionally produce a [moiré pattern](../../../../../moire-pattern.md).

A dark region alone is not a reliable layer count: [electron phase contrast](../../../../../electron-phase-contrast.md) can change sign with [defocus](../../../../../defocus-aberration.md), and contamination also changes intensity. Use fold-edge fringes, [diffraction](../../../../../diffraction.md) information and calibrated image simulations or thickness-sensitive measurements together. Defect identification should likewise compare a structural model with the actual imaging conditions, rather than reading every dark lattice site as a missing atom.
