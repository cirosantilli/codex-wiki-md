↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

**Aberration-corrected [scanning transmission electron microscopy](../../../../../scanning-transmission-electron-microscopy.md) with annular dark-field imaging and spatially resolved [EELS](../../../../../electron-energy-loss-spectroscopy.md)** can locate the Si atoms and test their chemical environment. [silicon](../../../../../silicon.md) scatters much more strongly to high angles than [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md), so an isolated Si impurity can stand out in a thin [graphene](../../../../../graphene.md) lattice. Neighboring [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) positions reveal whether a site is, for example, three- or four-coordinated in projection.

Acquire a spectrum image at the same time. A [silicon](../../../../../silicon.md) core-loss edge identifies the element, while its [energy-loss near-edge structure](../../../../../energy-loss-near-edge-structure.md) contains information about the unoccupied states and bonding. This combination has experimentally distinguished individual Si coordination environments in [graphene](../../../../../graphene.md).[Figures 1–2, atomic-resolution imaging and Si-edge spectra](../../../../../direct-determination-of-the-chemical-bonding-of-individual-impurities-in-graphene-by-wu-zhou-and-others.md)

Advantages are atom-scale spatial localization, simultaneous structural and chemical evidence, and stronger chemical identification than brightness alone. Limitations include low counts at one atom, background subtraction, finite [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) resolution, drift and the high dose needed for a useful spectrum. The beam can move or damage an impurity, and a two-dimensional projection does not by itself determine its out-of-plane geometry. Compare spectra and images with plausible structural calculations rather than assigning coordination solely from one bright pixel.
