<h1 id="8/ii/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In [high-angle annular dark-field imaging](../../../../../../high-angle-annular-dark-field-imaging.md) [scanning transmission electron microscopy](../../../../../../scanning-transmission-electron-microscopy.md), [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) scattered to large angles give predominantly incoherent mass–thickness and atomic-number contrast. For comparable thickness, **[gold](../../../../../../gold.md) is much brighter than [nickel](../../../../../../nickel.md)–[phosphorus](../../../../../../phosphorus.md)** because its [atomic number](../../../../../../atomic-number.md) is much larger. [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) in the protective cap scatters weakly, whereas its [tungsten](../../../../../../tungsten.md) component scatters strongly. The cap is not a homogeneous low-atomic-number reference.

The [gold](../../../../../../gold.md) layer follows a curved, locally distorted interface. The dark central opening in the overview is consistent with a crack, void or locally missing/thinned foil: little material along the beam path gives little high-angle scattering. The granular and columnar variations beneath the [gold](../../../../../../gold.md) indicate nanoscale heterogeneity in the affected NiP$_y$ region. Differences in thickness, density, [porosity](../../../../../../porosity.md) and composition can all contribute; [diffraction](../../../../../../diffraction.md)/channeling effects are reduced in [high-angle annular dark-field imaging](../../../../../../high-angle-annular-dark-field-imaging.md) but not eliminated in every geometry.

A black region is therefore evidence of low projected scattering mass, not by itself the identity of a chemical phase. In particular, a single [high-angle annular dark-field imaging](../../../../../../high-angle-annular-dark-field-imaging.md) image does not distinguish a pre-existing void from material lost during specimen preparation.
