<h1 id="12/i/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

At fixed spectrum and [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md), photocurrent is approximately proportional to [irradiance](../../../../../../irradiance.md), so the magnitude of [short-circuit current](../../../../../../short-circuit-current.md) increases nearly linearly. In the ideal model,

$$
V_{\rm oc}=n\frac{k_BT}{q}\ln\!\left(1+\frac{I_{\rm ph}}{I_0}\right),
$$

so [open-circuit voltage](../../../../../../open-circuit-voltage.md) rises only logarithmically with [irradiance](../../../../../../irradiance.md). More incident optical [power](../../../../../../power.md) generally raises the maximum electrical [power](../../../../../../power.md), within the device's thermal and resistive limits.

Increasing [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) strongly increases the dark saturation [electric current](../../../../../../electric-current.md). In [silicon](../../../../../../silicon.md) this normally reduces [open-circuit voltage](../../../../../../open-circuit-voltage.md) and maximum [power](../../../../../../power.md), despite a small increase in [short-circuit current](../../../../../../short-circuit-current.md) associated with the [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) dependence of absorption and [band gap](../../../../../../band-gap.md). The thermal-voltage factor alone is insufficient to predict the sign; the [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) dependence of $I_0$ matters.

**[maximum power point tracking](../../../../../../maximum-power-point-tracking.md) adjusts the load seen by the cell** as [irradiance](../../../../../../irradiance.md) and [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) change. A controlled DC–DC converter can transform the external load so that operation stays near the changing maximum of $-VI$, instead of at the generally suboptimal intersection with a fixed load. It matches operating conditions and cannot generate additional [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) beyond the photovoltaic output.
