<h1 id="3/iii/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

**Use [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) lenses and a digitally scanned, blanked [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) beam; no physical mask is required for direct writing.** Electromagnetic lenses focus the charged-particle beam, deflectors place it, and the pattern generator controls position and dose. Electrostatic elements can also form part of [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) optics, but these are not refractive glass lenses.

The [extreme ultraviolet](../../../../../../extreme-ultraviolet.md) mask mentioned in the problem is the object being manufactured by this maskless write process, rather than a mask placed in the [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) path.
