<h1 id="3/iii/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

**A transmissive mask is placed in contact with the resist, or a small distance above it for proximity printing.** Chromium-patterned glass or quartz is a typical mask. Illumination is normally collimated [ultraviolet](../../../../../../ultraviolet.md) light, with refractive illumination optics used to prepare the beam.

There is no projection objective imaging a reduced mask pattern onto the resist: the mask shadows the resist approximately one-to-one. The finite gap in proximity printing introduces [diffraction](../../../../../../diffraction.md) and limits edge sharpness; contact printing reduces that gap at the cost of mask wear and contamination risk.
