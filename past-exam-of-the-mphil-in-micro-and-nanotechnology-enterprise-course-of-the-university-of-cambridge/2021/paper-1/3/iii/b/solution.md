<h1 id="3/iii/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

**Use multilayer reflective optics and a reflective patterned mask.** Near the usual [extreme ultraviolet](../../../../../../extreme-ultraviolet.md) [wavelength](../../../../../../wavelength.md) of 13.5 nm, ordinary lenses and thick transmissive masks absorb too strongly. Multilayer mirrors focus and project the image, while an absorber pattern on a multilayer-coated mask defines the exposed regions.

The optical path operates in [vacuum](../../../../../../vacuum.md) to avoid absorption by air. The key distinction is reflective mask projection, rather than a transparent chromium-on-glass mask or a scanned [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) probe.
