↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**The resist is positive tone**, because the written regions are the regions removed during development. A suitable example is [poly(methyl methacrylate)](../../../../../poly-methyl-methacrylate.md), conventionally [PMMA](../../../../../poly-methyl-methacrylate.md), used in its ordinary positive-tone electron-beam exposure regime. Electron-induced [chain scission](../../../../../chain-scission.md) reduces its [molar mass](../../../../../molar-mass.md) and increases its solubility in the developer.

The fact that this writing step fabricates an [extreme ultraviolet](../../../../../extreme-ultraviolet.md) mask does not make the resist an EUV-exposed resist: the exposure process being classified here is the electron-beam mask-writing step.
