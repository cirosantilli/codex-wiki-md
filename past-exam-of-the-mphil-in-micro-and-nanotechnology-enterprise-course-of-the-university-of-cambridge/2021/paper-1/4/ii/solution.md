<h1 id="4/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The printed aerial image has approximately $I_{\max}=2.0$ and $I_{\min}=0.5\ \mathrm{mW\,cm^{-2}}$. Thus its modulation, and its modulation transfer for a fully modulated object, is

$$
\boxed{M=\frac{I_{\max}-I_{\min}}{I_{\max}+I_{\min}}
=\frac{2.0-0.5}{2.0+0.5}=0.60.}
$$

**The image does not meet the full-thickness printing criterion**, because $0.60<0.667$. Equivalently, clearing the bright regions requires $t\geq100/2=50$ s, while preserving the full thickness in the dark regions requires $t\leq20/0.5=40$ s. There is no common exposure time.

Partial thickness modulation may remain visible, but that is different from a clean grating with fully cleared spaces and retained full-thickness lines. Small ripple peaks in the source drawing do not change this conclusion.
