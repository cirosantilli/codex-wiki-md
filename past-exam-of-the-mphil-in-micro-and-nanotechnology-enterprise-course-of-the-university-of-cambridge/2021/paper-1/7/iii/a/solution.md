<h1 id="7/iii/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A pyroelectric material has a spontaneous [electric polarization](../../../../../../polarization-density.md) that changes with [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md). [electrodes](../../../../../../electrode.md) collect the resulting change in compensating charge. For a uniform element,

$$
\Delta Q=A p\Delta T,\qquad i=A p\frac{dT}{dt}.
$$

Consequently **[temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) variation in time**, rather than a steady [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) difference alone, drives a [pyroelectric generator](../../../../../../pyroelectric-generator.md). A cyclic thermal input and a suitable electrical circuit are needed for repeated [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) extraction.

The [primary pyroelectric effect](../../../../../../primary-pyroelectric-effect.md) is the change of [electric polarization](../../../../../../polarization-density.md) at fixed [strain](../../../../../../strain.md). The [secondary pyroelectric effect](../../../../../../secondary-pyroelectric-effect.md) arises because [thermal expansion](../../../../../../thermal-expansion.md) [strains](../../../../../../strain.md) a piezoelectric crystal and thereby changes its [electric polarization](../../../../../../polarization-density.md). In component notation its contribution is

$$
p_i^{\rm secondary}=\sum_j e_{ij}\alpha_j,\qquad
p_i^{\rm free}=p_i^{\rm clamped}+p_i^{\rm secondary},
$$

with piezoelectric coefficients $e_{ij}$ and thermal-expansion coefficients $\alpha_j$. Choosing orientation, mechanical constraint and substrate expansion so that the secondary contribution reinforces the primary one can increase the response; the signs can instead oppose one another.

An array of slender [nanowires](../../../../../../nanowire.md) can expand more freely than a film clamped to a substrate and can exchange heat rapidly because of its high [surface-to-volume ratio](../../../../../../surface-to-volume-ratio.md). Reduced clamping can recover a secondary response suppressed in the film. Faster thermal cycling can also increase [electric current](../../../../../../electric-current.md) through $dT/dt$. This is a geometrical opportunity rather than a universal increase of the intrinsic coefficient: screening, leakage, orientation and the actual thermal excursion must be compared. ZnO [nanowire](../../../../../../nanowire.md) generators provide an experimental realization of pyroelectric [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) conversion.[Abstract: vertically aligned ZnO nanowire arrays used for pyroelectric generation](../../../../../../pyroelectric-nanogenerators-for-harvesting-thermoelectric-energy-by-ya-yang-and-others.md)
