<h1 id="7/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Three standard approaches are **piezoelectric, electromagnetic and [electrostatic energy harvesting](../../../../../electrostatic-energy-harvesting.md)**.

- A piezoelectric microcantilever or [nanowire](../../../../../nanowire.md) develops [electric polarization](../../../../../polarization-density.md) when strained. An inertial mass, bending substrate or vibration bends the element; [electrodes](../../../../../electrode.md) and a rectifier collect charge. [PZT](../../../../../lead-zirconate-titanate.md), ZnO and [PVDF](../../../../../polyvinylidene-fluoride.md) illustrate different material choices. This approach needs no external bias for transduction and can give appreciable [voltage](../../../../../voltage.md) in a compact element. Its [capacitance](../../../../../capacitance.md) gives high source impedance and relatively small [electric current](../../../../../electric-current.md); brittle materials, fatigue, depolarization and a narrow mechanical [resonance](../../../../../resonance.md) can limit performance.
- Electromagnetic harvesting uses relative motion of a magnet and coil to change [magnetic flux](../../../../../magnetic-flux.md), producing $\mathcal E=-N\,d\Phi/dt$. A micromachined coil and movable magnet can implement it at the microscale. It can offer low source impedance and useful [electric current](../../../../../electric-current.md), but the small flux and coil area make [voltage](../../../../../voltage.md) and [power](../../../../../power.md) increasingly difficult to maintain on miniaturization; coil resistance and fabrication of magnets also matter. A practical microscale device does not imply efficient scaling all the way to individual [nanowires](../../../../../nanowire.md).
- Electrostatic harvesting uses a variable [capacitor](../../../../../capacitor.md), for example [MEMS](../../../../../microelectromechanical-system.md) comb fingers or moving plates. Mechanical motion changes [capacitance](../../../../../capacitance.md); an initial charge, electrical bias or [electret](../../../../../electret.md) allows this change to do electrical work. Small gaps favor strong coupling and compatibility with microfabrication. Limitations include the need for charge or bias, parasitic [capacitance](../../../../../capacitance.md), leakage, pull-in, stiction and [dielectric breakdown](../../../../../dielectric-breakdown.md).

In all three cases the output is constrained by the available mechanical input and damping. Nanoscale dimensions alone do not supply an [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) advantage.
