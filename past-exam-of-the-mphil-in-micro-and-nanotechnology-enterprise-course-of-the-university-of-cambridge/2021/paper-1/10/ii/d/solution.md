<h1 id="10/ii/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Use **two-dimensional [NOESY](../../../../../../noesy.md)**, or [ROESY](../../../../../../roesy.md) where molecular tumbling makes [NOESY](../../../../../../noesy.md) less favorable, to look for through-space correlations between proton sites on the two ends that are brought together in the closed [conformation](../../../../../../conformation.md). Assign [resonances](../../../../../../resonance.md) first and compare predicted ring contacts with an open reference or model.

The [nuclear Overhauser effect](../../../../../../nuclear-overhauser-effect.md) involves [NMR cross-relaxation](../../../../../../nmr-cross-relaxation.md) through magnetic dipole–dipole coupling. Its strong approximate $r^{-6}$ dependence makes it sensitive to short interproton distances. A [NOESY](../../../../../../noesy.md) cross-peak therefore reports spatial proximity, including between sites far apart along the covalent skeleton; it is not a [NMR scalar coupling](../../../../../../nmr-scalar-coupling.md) transmitted along that skeleton.

Run a dilution series: end-to-end contacts that persist at [concentrations](../../../../../../concentration.md) where intermolecular association is negligible support intramolecular ring closure. Hydrogen-bonded NH [chemical shifts](../../../../../../chemical-shift.md) and [diffusion](../../../../../../diffusion.md) measurements can support the assignment, but neither alone proves ring topology. A flexible open chain can transiently bring ends together, and intermolecular contacts can also give NOEs. Several geometry-consistent cross-peaks, [concentration](../../../../../../concentration.md) behavior and controlled mixing times make the case much stronger than one peak.
