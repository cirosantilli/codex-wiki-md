<h1 id="10/ii/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

[DMSO](../../../../../../dimethyl-sulfoxide.md) is a strong hydrogen-bond acceptor. It competes for the NH donors of the [UPy units](../../../../../../ureidopyrimidinone.md) and solvates the association sites, weakening their mutual hydrogen bonding.

For A, **the intermolecular chains should shorten or dissociate**, reducing the association-driven [viscosity](../../../../../../dynamic-viscosity-split.md). For B, **the intramolecular ring should become less populated**, increasing the fraction of open [conformations](../../../../../../conformation.md). Sufficient [DMSO](../../../../../../dimethyl-sulfoxide.md) also weakens rings of A and any intermolecular associations of B. The covalent molecular skeletons remain intact.

The extent depends on [DMSO](../../../../../../dimethyl-sulfoxide.md) fraction and the association [free energies](../../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) in the mixed [solvent](../../../../../../solvent.md). [solvent](../../../../../../solvent.md) [viscosity](../../../../../../dynamic-viscosity-split.md) itself also changes, so the prediction concerns the supramolecular contribution rather than an unconditional numerical value for total solution [viscosity](../../../../../../dynamic-viscosity-split.md).
