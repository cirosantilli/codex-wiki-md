<h1 id="10/ii/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The terminal **[ureidopyrimidinone](../../../../../../ureidopyrimidinone.md) groups** supply the association sites. Their [urea](../../../../../../urea.md)/pyrimidinone NH donors and [carbonyl](../../../../../../carbonyl-group.md)/ring-nitrogen acceptors can form a self-complementary array of four [hydrogen bonds](../../../../../../hydrogen-bond.md) between two [UPy units](../../../../../../ureidopyrimidinone.md). Joining the two terminal units of one [molecule](../../../../../../molecule-split.md) by this noncovalent association closes the ring.

The ether-containing arms provide flexibility and spacing; they are not covalently joined together during ring formation. The ring is therefore a supramolecular [conformation](../../../../../../conformation.md) of the existing [molecule](../../../../../../molecule-split.md), rather than a new covalent [ether](../../../../../../ether.md) [macrocycle](../../../../../../macrocycle.md).
