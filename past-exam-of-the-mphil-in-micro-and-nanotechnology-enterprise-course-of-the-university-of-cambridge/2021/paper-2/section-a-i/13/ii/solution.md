<h1 id="section-a-i/13/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

With the [graphene](../../../../../../graphene.md) lattice-vector length $a=0.246\ \mathrm{nm}$, the [carbon nanotube chiral vector](../../../../../../carbon-nanotube-chiral-vector.md) is $\mathbf C_h=n\mathbf a_1+m\mathbf a_2$. The vectors meet at $60^\circ$, giving

$$
C=a\sqrt{n^2+nm+m^2},\qquad d=\frac C\pi,\qquad
\theta=\tan^{-1}\!\left(\frac{\sqrt3\,m}{2n+m}\right).
$$

Here $C$ is circumference, $d$ diameter, and the [carbon nanotube chiral angle](../../../../../../carbon-nanotube-chiral-angle.md) $\theta$ is measured from the zigzag direction. [Nanotube zone folding](../../../../../../nanotube-zone-folding.md) gives a nominal metallic tube when $n-m\equiv0\pmod3$ and a semiconducting tube otherwise. Evaluating all the supplied indices gives the following derived results:

$$
\begin{array}{c|rrr|c}
(n,m)&d\ (\mathrm{nm})&C\ (\mathrm{nm})&\theta\ ({}^\circ)&\text{type}\\\hline
(5,4)&0.6116&1.9213&26.33&\mathrm S\\
(6,4)&0.6826&2.1446&23.41&\mathrm S\\
(7,3)&0.6960&2.1865&17.00&\mathrm S\\
(6,5)&0.7470&2.3467&27.00&\mathrm S\\
(8,3)&0.7712&2.4228&15.30&\mathrm S\\
(10,0)&0.7830&2.4600&0.00&\mathrm S\\
(9,2)&0.7947&2.4966&9.83&\mathrm S\\
(7,5)&0.8175&2.5683&24.50&\mathrm S\\
(8,4)&0.8287&2.6034&19.11&\mathrm S\\
(7,6)&0.8824&2.7723&27.46&\mathrm S\\
(11,1)&0.9030&2.8370&4.31&\mathrm S
\end{array}
$$

**All eleven listed tubes are semiconducting in the zone-folding classification.** Curvature can open small gaps in some nominally metallic non-armchair tubes, but none of these indices falls in that nominal metallic class.

Diameter gives a useful inverse-size trend in transition energies, and the full chiral indices constrain the [electronic band structure](../../../../../../electronic-band-structure.md) much more strongly. They do not by themselves give the perceived solution colour through a simple geometrical rule: one needs the visible [absorption spectrum](../../../../../../absorption-spectrum.md), transition strengths and widths, [exciton](../../../../../../exciton.md)/environment shifts, [concentration](../../../../../../concentration.md), optical path and illumination. The observed transmitted colour is complementary to the absorbed spectrum, not a direct label for the [band gap](../../../../../../band-gap.md). Structure-assigned excitation–emission measurements provide an experimental connection between chiral structure and optical transitions.[Science 298 (2002), pp. 2361–2366, structure assignment of semiconducting-tube optical transitions](../../../../../../structure-assigned-optical-spectra-of-single-walled-carbon-nanotubes-by-sergei-m-bachilo-and-others.md)
