↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Rolling [graphene](../../../../../../graphene.md) into a [single-walled carbon nanotube](../../../../../../single-walled-carbon-nanotube.md) quantizes the circumferential [wave vector](../../../../../../wavevector.md), leaving a set of one-dimensional longitudinal subbands. At each subband edge the [group velocity](../../../../../../group-velocity.md) tends to zero, producing a [Van Hove singularity](../../../../../../van-hove-singularity.md) in the [density of states](../../../../../../density-of-states.md). For a simple gapped Dirac subband, the contribution scales as $|E|/\sqrt{E^2-\Delta_j^2}$ outside its edge. At low [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) the valence states below the [Fermi level](../../../../../../fermi-level.md) are occupied and the conduction states above it are empty. A semiconducting tube has a gap; an ideal metallic tube has gapless channels as well as higher-subband singularities.

For light polarized along the tube, the strongest allowed interband transitions connect corresponding subbands while approximately conserving axial [wave vector](../../../../../../wavevector.md) and circumferential [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md). The large [joint density of states](../../../../../../joint-density-of-states.md) makes distinct absorption resonances, conventionally labelled $E_{11},E_{22},\ldots$. Strong [electron](../../../../../../electron.md)–hole interactions produce bound [excitons](../../../../../../exciton.md), so the observed optical resonances differ from the noninteracting band separations. Defects, doping, bundling and [dielectric screening](../../../../../../dielectric-screening.md) change peak energies and [oscillator strengths](../../../../../../oscillator-strength.md).
