<h1 id="section-b/19/iii/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For the [silver nanowire](../../../../../../../silver-nanowire.md) network, synthesize or source long, narrow wires, remove excess [ligand](../../../../../../../ligand-biochemistry.md) without causing aggregation, formulate a stable coating ink and coat the flexible web by a scalable method such as [slot-die coating](../../../../../../../slot-die-coating.md) or [gravure printing](../../../../../../../gravure-printing.md). Dry below the substrate's damage [temperature](../../../../../../../temperature-split.md), then press or locally weld junctions and embed or cap the network if required. Define 100 µm stripes by masked coating, a printing-compatible patterned [wetting](../../../../../../../wetting.md) layer, or post-coating [laser ablation](../../../../../../../laser-ablation.md); provide robust end contacts and remove stray bridges between adjacent stripes. Roll processing offers throughput, but [coffee-ring effect](../../../../../../../coffee-ring-effect.md), wire alignment, random weak links and ablation damage require inline optical/electrical inspection. Verify that the local junction treatment improves resistance without simply darkening the whole film.

For the [silver](../../../../../../../silver.md) nanoparticle-derived grid, use a repeatable [nanoimprint lithography](../../../../../../../nanoimprint-lithography.md) to form a fine mesh inside the outer 25 mm by 100 µm stripe pattern. Fill the channels with a [silver](../../../../../../../silver.md) [nanoparticle ink](../../../../../../../nanoparticle-ink.md), remove excess from the transparent openings, and consolidate the metal by substrate-compatible chemical, photonic or low-temperature thermal [sintering](../../../../../../../sintering.md). Transfer or embed the grid into the [polymer](../../../../../../../polymer-split.md) surface and add electrical pads. The quantitative design in part (b) sets target pitch, line width and thickness; the large-scale process must maintain those dimensions and a continuous metal cross section. A master enables repeated replication and deterministic open area, but tooling cost, incomplete filling, residual metal in windows, shrinkage cracks, imperfect [sintering](../../../../../../../sintering.md) and adhesion are important limitations. Test the finished patterned stripes, rather than relying on a large unpatterned test coupon alone.
