<h1 id="section-b/19/iii/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Three credible architectures are **a welded [silver nanowire](../../../../../../../silver-nanowire.md) network; a thin [silver nanowire](../../../../../../../silver-nanowire.md) network with a [conducting polymer](../../../../../../../conducting-polymer.md) overcoat; and a nanocrystalline [silver](../../../../../../../silver.md) grid formed from [nanoparticle ink](../../../../../../../nanoparticle-ink.md)**. [Silver](../../../../../../../silver.md) provides low intrinsic resistivity. A sparse long-wire network can be both flexible and highly transmitting if its junctions are well connected. A thin, optically weak [conducting polymer](../../../../../../../conducting-polymer.md) overcoat can bridge junctions and smooth the surface, provided its added absorption and moisture sensitivity are controlled. A fine grid offers a more directly designed optical/electrical trade-off and does not rely on operating near random [percolation](../../../../../../../percolation-theory-split.md).

For a quantitative grid feasibility screen, take pitch $p=5\ \mathrm{\mu m}$, line width $w=100\ \mathrm{nm}$ and metal thickness $t=200\ \mathrm{nm}$. An opaque-line square-grid estimate gives $T_{\rm grid}\simeq(1-w/p)^2=0.9604$. With a transmitting [polymer](../../../../../../../polymer-split.md) substrate of 0.95, the combined estimate is 0.912. The [sheet resistance](../../../../../../../sheet-resistance.md) is approximately $R_\square=\rho_{\rm eff}p/(wt)$. Even an effective [silver](../../../../../../../silver.md) resistivity four times $1.6\times10^{-8}\ \Omega\,\mathrm m$ gives **$R_\square\simeq16\ \Omega$**, with a coating thinner than 400 nm. There are about 20 mesh periods across the stripe, making the finite-width design more credible than a pitch comparable with its whole width.

These are feasible material/process choices, not guaranteed specifications for every film carrying those names. For the two [nanowire](../../../../../../../nanowire.md) architectures, choose wire dimensions, loading and junction treatment to pass the same finished-stripe measurements; the hybrid's optical budget must include its overcoat. Measure transmission at 550 nm with its reference stated, [sheet resistance](../../../../../../../sheet-resistance.md), profile thickness, bend-cycle stability and pattern continuity. Bare nanotube or [graphene](../../../../../../../graphene.md) networks should not be assumed to meet this demanding resistance–transmission combination without additional metallic conduction or other demonstrated improvement.
