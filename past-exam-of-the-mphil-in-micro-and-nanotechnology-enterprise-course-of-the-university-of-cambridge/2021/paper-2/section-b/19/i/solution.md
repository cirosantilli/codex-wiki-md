↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Three suitable examples are **[cubes](../../../../../../cube.md) bounded mainly by $\{100\}$ [facets](../../../../../../facet.md), [octahedra](../../../../../../octahedron.md) bounded by $\{111\}$ [facets](../../../../../../facet.md), and [truncated octahedra](../../../../../../truncated-octahedron.md) bounded by both $\{111\}$ and $\{100\}$ [facets](../../../../../../facet.md)**. These are crystallographically distinct shapes rather than three arbitrary rounded particle outlines.

At fixed volume the [thermodynamic equilibrium](../../../../../../thermodynamic-equilibrium.md) shape minimizes $G_{\rm surf}=\sum_i\gamma_iA_i$, with an edge/corner correction when particles are very small. In the [Wulff construction](../../../../../../wulff-construction.md), [facet](../../../../../../facet.md) support-plane distances scale as their interfacial free energies. [Silver](../../../../../../silver.md)'s clean low-index energies, selective [adsorption](../../../../../../adsorption.md) of [ligands](../../../../../../ligand-biochemistry.md) or ions, [solvent](../../../../../../solvent.md) [chemical potential](../../../../../../chemical-potential-split.md) and possible support contact alter those energies. [Crystal twinning](../../../../../../crystal-twinning.md) and elastic [strain](../../../../../../strain.md) can add further costs or stabilize multiply twinned forms. A face with slow attachment kinetics can also survive during growth even when that morphology is not the [thermodynamic equilibrium](../../../../../../thermodynamic-equilibrium.md) Wulff shape. [Homogeneous nucleation](../../../../../../homogeneous-nucleation.md) describes where nuclei form; it does not guarantee an [thermodynamic equilibrium](../../../../../../thermodynamic-equilibrium.md) final shape.
