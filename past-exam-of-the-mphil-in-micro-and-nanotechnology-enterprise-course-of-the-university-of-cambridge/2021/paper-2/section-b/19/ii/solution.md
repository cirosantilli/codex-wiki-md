<h1 id="section-b/19/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

In a [polyol process](../../../../../../polyol-process.md), a hot polyol such as [ethylene glycol](../../../../../../ethylene-glycol.md) acts as [solvent](../../../../../../solvent.md) and reductant for a [silver](../../../../../../silver.md) precursor. [Nucleation](../../../../../../nucleation.md) creates seeds; controlled [reduction](../../../../../../reduction-chemistry.md) supplies atoms for their subsequent growth, while [polyvinylpyrrolidone](../../../../../../polyvinylpyrrolidone.md) [adsorption](../../../../../../adsorption.md) and seed [crystal twinning](../../../../../../crystal-twinning.md) favour anisotropic growth. For the common [silver nanowire](../../../../../../silver-nanowire.md) route, a fivefold-twinned seed can develop into a pentagonal wire with predominantly $\{100\}$ side faces and growth at less-passivated ends. Cross-sectional microscopy and [diffraction](../../../../../../diffraction.md) of microtomed wires provide evidence for that twinned structure rather than an inference from a long outline alone.[Nano Letters 3 (2003), pp. 955–960, pentagonal cross sections and fivefold twinning](../../../../../../polyol-synthesis-of-uniform-silver-nanowires-a-plausible-growth-mechanism-and-the-supporting-evidence-by-yugang-sun-and-others.md)

The [aspect ratio](../../../../../../aspect-ratio.md) depends on seed population and twin survival, relative end/side growth rates, precursor feed and [reduction](../../../../../../reduction-chemistry.md) rate, [PVP](../../../../../../polyvinylpyrrolidone.md) coverage, [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md), trace halides, dissolved oxygen and growth time. High [aspect ratio](../../../../../../aspect-ratio.md) requires a suitable population of twinned seeds, suppression of competing particle [nucleation](../../../../../../nucleation.md) and continued atom supply while sidewall thickening is restrained. These variables must be controlled together; adding more capping [polymer](../../../../../../polymer-split.md) alone is not a universal recipe. Advantages are solution processing, substantial yield and tunable dimensions without an etched hard template. Disadvantages include sensitive batch reproducibility, particle by-products, residual insulating [ligand](../../../../../../ligand-biochemistry.md), purification, oxidation/tarnish and [nanowire junction resistance](../../../../../../nanowire-junction-resistance.md) in later films. Compare seed/capping/halide controls using microscopy and size distributions, not only solution colour.
