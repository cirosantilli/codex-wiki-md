<h1 id="section-b/22/vi/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vi](../vi.md)

The [Flory interaction parameter](../../../../../../flory-interaction-parameter.md) $\chi_{AB}$ measures the effective [free energy](../../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) penalty for unlike A–B contacts relative to like contacts, and $N$ is the total number of statistical segments per chain. Their product is the segregation strength: contact-energy preference competes with the [conformational entropy](../../../../../../conformational-entropy.md) cost of separating covalently joined blocks. $N$ does not grow during [solvent](../../../../../../solvent.md) evaporation. For an approximately nonselective [solvent](../../../../../../solvent.md), increasing [polymer](../../../../../../polymer-split.md) [concentration](../../../../../../concentration.md) instead increases the effective importance of A–B contacts, so a roughly fixed-composition path moves from weak toward stronger effective segregation.

The diagram's labels are **DIS: disordered; S: [block-copolymer sphere phase](../../../../../../block-copolymer-sphere-phase.md); H: [hexagonal cylinder block-copolymer phase](../../../../../../hexagonal-cylinder-block-copolymer-phase.md); G: [double gyroid](../../../../../../double-gyroid.md); L: [lamellar microphase](../../../../../../lamellar-microphase.md)**. At $f_A=0.15$, A is the minority component and B forms the continuous matrix. Reading the supplied diagram vertically at this composition gives disorder initially, a sphere region when segregation first becomes sufficient, and a cylinder region if sufficiently strong segregation is reached within the displayed range. The thin gyroid and central lamellar regions are not the [thermodynamic equilibrium](../../../../../../thermodynamic-equilibrium.md) path at this composition. The dry endpoint cannot be fixed numerically without the final $\chi_{AB}N$ value. The cited theoretical calculation determines [thermodynamic equilibrium](../../../../../../thermodynamic-equilibrium.md) phase stability, not a universal evaporation trajectory.[Macromolecules 39 (2006), pp. 2449–2451, equilibrium diblock phase stability](../../../../../../stability-of-the-gyroid-phase-in-diblock-copolymers-at-strong-segregation-by-eric-w-cochran-and-others.md)

Crucially, at least one block is glassy at room [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md). [Solvent](../../../../../../solvent.md) can initially plasticize it, but [solvent](../../../../../../solvent.md) loss can raise its effective glass-transition [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) above the processing [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md). Chain relaxation then becomes too slow to equilibrate. Even slow evaporation can therefore freeze disorder, spheres, cylinders or another transient morphology before the [thermodynamic equilibrium](../../../../../../thermodynamic-equilibrium.md) dry state is reached. [Solvent](../../../../../../solvent.md) [concentration](../../../../../../concentration.md), [annealing](../../../../../../annealing-materials-science.md) time and segmental mobility must be followed alongside the [phase diagram](../../../../../../phase-diagram.md).
