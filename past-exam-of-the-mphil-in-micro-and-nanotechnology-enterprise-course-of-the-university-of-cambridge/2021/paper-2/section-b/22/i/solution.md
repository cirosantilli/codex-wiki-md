[Brownian motion](../../../../../../brownian-motion-split.md) is the irregular thermal displacement produced by fluctuating molecular forces from the surrounding liquid. In the overdamped regime its long-time displacement is diffusive, with [mean squared displacement](../../../../../../mean-squared-displacement.md) proportional to time. It brings nanoscale building blocks into contact and lets them sample orientations and escape weak incorrect contacts. Interparticle forces bias this exploration toward lower-free-energy structures; [thermal motion](../../../../../../thermal-motion.md) alone does not select a particular [crystal](../../../../../../crystal.md). Strong irreversible attraction, high [viscosity](../../../../../../dynamic-viscosity-split.md) or glassy arrest can stop the defect correction needed for successful [self-assembly](../../../../../../self-assembly.md).
