<h1 id="section-b/22/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

At the theta condition, effective [excluded volume](../../../../../../excluded-volume.md) approximately cancel and a freely jointed [ideal chain](../../../../../../ideal-chain.md) has $\langle R^2\rangle=Nb^2$. With the stipulated segment length $b=2a$,

$$
\boxed{R_{\rm rms,\theta}=2a\sqrt N.}
$$

In a [good solvent](../../../../../../good-solvent.md) repulsive [excluded volume](../../../../../../excluded-volume.md) swells the coil, giving $R\sim bN^\nu$ with $\nu\simeq0.588$ in three dimensions, or the [Flory approximation](../../../../../../flory-approximation.md) $\nu=3/5$. In a [poor solvent](../../../../../../poor-solvent.md) effective segment attraction favours a compact globule, whose linear size scales as $bN^{1/3}$ at roughly fixed internal density. The latter are scaling laws with model-dependent prefactors. A real chemical [monomer](../../../../../../monomer.md) need not be a statistical [Kuhn length](../../../../../../kuhn-length.md); using $2a$ as the independent segment length is part of this question's idealized model.
