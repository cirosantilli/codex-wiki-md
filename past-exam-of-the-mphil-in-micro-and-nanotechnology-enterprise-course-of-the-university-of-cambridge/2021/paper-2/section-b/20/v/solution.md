<h1 id="section-b/20/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

Treat the top gate as a [parallel-plate capacitor](../../../../../../parallel-plate-capacitor.md), neglecting fringing and assuming its voltage change couples to the 2DEG without a dominant trap or series-capacitance correction. Its [capacitance](../../../../../../capacitance.md) per area and induced [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) density are

$$
\frac CA=\frac{\epsilon_0\epsilon_r}{d}=1.15102\times10^{-3}\ \mathrm{F\,m^{-2}},\qquad
\Delta n_s=\frac{\epsilon_0\epsilon_r\Delta V}{ed}
=1.43698\times10^{15}\ \mathrm{m^{-2}}.
$$

In this [top-gate density tuning](../../../../../../top-gate-density-tuning.md), a positive gate change increases the [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) density in this model, giving $n_s'=4.43371\times10^{15}\ \mathrm{m^{-2}}$. Since $B_j\propto k_F\propto\sqrt{n_s}$,

$$
\boxed{|B'_1|=0.2197\ \mathrm T,\qquad |B'_2|=0.4395\ \mathrm T,\qquad |B'_3|=0.6592\ \mathrm T.}
$$

The fields rise by a factor $\sqrt{n_s'/n_s}=1.2163$, not by the density ratio itself. Finite [quantum capacitance](../../../../../../quantum-capacitance.md) is a small correction here: the one-subband value $e^2m^*/(\pi\hbar^2)$ is much larger than the stated geometric [capacitance](../../../../../../capacitance.md) per area.
