↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Substituting $r_c=\hbar k_F/(e|B|)$ into $L=2jr_c$ gives

$$
B_j=\frac{2j\hbar k_F}{eL},\qquad L=10^{-6}\ \mathrm m.
$$

Using the density-derived Fermi [wave vector](../../../../../../../wavevector.md) gives the first three peak magnitudes

$$
\boxed{|B_1|=0.1807\ \mathrm T,\qquad |B_2|=0.3613\ \mathrm T,\qquad |B_3|=0.5420\ \mathrm T.}
$$

The equally spaced sequence follows from the number of reflected half-orbits, not from successive quantum Hall fillings. The field polarity selecting a given injector–collector orientation follows from the [Lorentz force](../../../../../../../lorentz-force.md) direction.
