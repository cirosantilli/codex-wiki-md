↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [two-dimensional electron gas](../../../../../../two-dimensional-electron-gas.md) is confined into discrete subbands perpendicular to an interface while retaining nearly free motion in its plane. It is not a literal zero-thickness [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) sheet. In a [GaAs](../../../../../../gallium-arsenide.md)/[AlGaAs](../../../../../../aluminium-gallium-arsenide.md) [semiconductor heterostructure](../../../../../../semiconductor-heterostructure.md), the [band offset](../../../../../../band-offset.md) and self-consistent [band bending](../../../../../../band-bending.md) confine [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) on the [GaAs](../../../../../../gallium-arsenide.md) side. Donors in [AlGaAs](../../../../../../aluminium-gallium-arsenide.md) can supply [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) while an undoped spacer separates them from the ionized dopants, reducing impurity scattering. This modulation-doping arrangement yields a high-mobility channel, with the lowest confinement subband occupied when the confinement spacing exceeds the Fermi and thermal scales. Gates tune the density or locally deplete the channel into constrictions. Low-temperature Hall and longitudinal measurements test the resulting sheet density and transport quality.
