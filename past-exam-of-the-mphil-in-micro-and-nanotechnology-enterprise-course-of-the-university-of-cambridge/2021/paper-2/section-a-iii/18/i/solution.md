<h1 id="section-a-iii/18/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For this [self-assembly](../../../../../../self-assembly.md) into a [colloidal crystal](../../../../../../colloidal-crystal.md), four useful elements are **building blocks, interactions, environment and reversible dynamics**. The building blocks here are nearly monodisperse [polymer](../../../../../../polymer-split.md) spheres; their shape and size distribution constrain the accessible packing. Their interactions include [excluded volume](../../../../../../excluded-volume.md), [Van der Waals forces](../../../../../../van-der-waals-force.md) and electrostatic or [steric stabilization](../../../../../../steric-stabilization.md), which set the [free energy](../../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) landscape. The aqueous environment supplies [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md), [viscosity](../../../../../../dynamic-viscosity-split.md), [ionic strength](../../../../../../ionic-strength.md), confinement and [concentration](../../../../../../concentration.md) controls. Finally, [Brownian motion](../../../../../../brownian-motion-split.md) and rotation must let particles encounter one another and rearrange or detach from incorrect contacts. Attractions that bind every collision irreversibly favour an amorphous aggregate rather than an annealed [colloidal crystal](../../../../../../colloidal-crystal.md). These four categories include both thermodynamic selection and the transport/kinetic access needed to realize it.
