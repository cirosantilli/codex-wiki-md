**The floating-catalyst direct-spinning route commonly requires a small [sulfur](../../../../../../sulfur.md) promoter addition.** [sulfur](../../../../../../sulfur.md) changes catalyst-particle nucleation and surface chemistry, influencing the active Fe-particle population, carbon incorporation and whether sufficiently long, numerous tubes form a drawable aerogel.

A sulfur-rich catalyst surface or Fe–S-containing state can alter [surface energy](../../../../../../surface-energy.md), wetting and particle coarsening; a low-melting interfacial state can also affect catalyst activity. These are process-dependent mechanisms, not a statement that [sulfur](../../../../../../sulfur.md) is a structural constituent required in every [carbon nanotube](../../../../../../carbon-nanotube.md). Excess [sulfur](../../../../../../sulfur.md) can suppress useful growth or worsen impurities. The useful dose therefore has a window and depends on catalyst and feed chemistry.

A controlled comparison changes [sulfur](../../../../../../sulfur.md) supply while holding the carbon/catalyst feed and collection conditions fixed, then measures tube population, catalyst residue and spinnability rather than judging only the presence of soot. The primary direct-spinning study specifically treats sulfur-dependent processing and purity.[2014, abstract and sulfur-promoter model](../../../../../../spinning-of-carbon-nanotube-fibres-using-the-floating-catalyst-high-temperature-route-purity-issues-and-the-critical-role-of-sulphur-by-t-s-gspann-and-others.md)
