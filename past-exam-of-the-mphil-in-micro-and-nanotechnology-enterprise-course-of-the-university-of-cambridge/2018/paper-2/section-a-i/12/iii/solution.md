<h1 id="section-a-i/12/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Since [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) and [angular frequency](../../../../../../angular-frequency.md) are related by $E=\hbar\omega$, the given [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) relation yields

$$
E_i=E_P\sqrt{\frac{1+\lambda_i}{2}}.
$$

With $E_P=4.3\ {\rm eV}$,

$$
\boxed{
E_1=4.3\sqrt{0.285}\simeq2.296\ {\rm eV},\quad
E_2=4.3\sqrt{0.325}\simeq2.451\ {\rm eV},\quad
E_3=4.3\sqrt{0.400}\simeq2.720\ {\rm eV}.}
$$

The $\lambda_i$ are dimensionless modal [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md), not optical wavelengths despite the symbol. The same $\hbar$ multiplies both mode and bulk angular frequencies and cancels in the [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) ratio. These are bare-core predictions of the stipulated lossless [Drude model](../../../../../../drude-model.md); adding a dielectric/metallic shell, interband response or damping changes the actual resonances.
