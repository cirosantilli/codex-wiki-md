<h1 id="section-a-i/12/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [Wulff construction](../../../../../../wulff-construction.md) minimizes total orientation-dependent surface [free energy](../../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) at fixed crystal [volume](../../../../../../volume.md):

$$
G_{\rm surf}=\sum_i\gamma_iA_i,\qquad h_i=\Lambda\gamma_i.
$$

Planes at distances proportional to their [surface energies](../../../../../../surface-energy.md) bound the equilibrium shape. The construction is thermodynamic; observed growth rates need not select the same shape.

For a mixed-metal particle, minimize a more complete [free energy](../../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) including bulk mixing/ordering, Au–Ag interface [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md), exposed-surface composition, adsorbates and elastic [strain](../../../../../../strain.md), while conserving the amount of each metal. Important parameters include orientation-dependent [surface energies](../../../../../../surface-energy.md), metal chemical potentials, interface coherency, [lattice mismatch](../../../../../../lattice-mismatch.md), [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) and ligand coverage. [surface segregation](../../../../../../surface-segregation.md) can change the exposed composition and hence the relevant $\gamma_i$.

**A generalized free-energy minimization can address a multicomponent particle; the elementary [Wulff construction](../../../../../../wulff-construction.md) alone does not predict its core-shell composition.** Sequential Au-core formation and Ag deposition can preserve a kinetically trapped interface when diffusion is too slow to reach equilibrium. If shape relaxation is fast but interdiffusion slow, an external shape may approach a constrained minimum even though the complete particle is not at global equilibrium. Growth kinetics, ligand stabilization and finite-size effects limit a bare-surface equilibrium model. A faceted image is therefore insufficient proof that either shape or composition is an equilibrium state.
