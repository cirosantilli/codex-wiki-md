<h1 id="section-b/19/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A common implementation uses a [gallium arsenide](../../../../../../gallium-arsenide.md)/[aluminium gallium arsenide](../../../../../../aluminium-gallium-arsenide.md) heterostructure with a [two-dimensional electron gas](../../../../../../two-dimensional-electron-gas.md) near the interface. [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) supplied by remote donors occupy quantized normal-direction states, while an undoped spacer reduces ionized-impurity scattering. Surface metallic gates, insulated from the conducting layer, deplete selected regions when negatively biased.

Leave a small conducting island bounded by depleted regions and connect it weakly to source and drain reservoirs through two tunable constrictions. A plunger gate controls the island potential/population; barrier gates control tunnel coupling. Ohmic contacts connect the reservoirs to the measurement circuit. **Vertical heterostructure confinement plus lateral [depletion force](../../../../../../depletion-force.md) defines the dot.** A 2DEG without lateral gates would remain extended in its plane.

The original device diagram labels the gates, island, tunnel barriers and reservoirs. Charge stability and spectroscopy versus gate/bias should verify that an isolated weakly coupled island has actually formed; a lithographic outline alone is insufficient.
