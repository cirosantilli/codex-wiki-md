↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

An initially localized escaping [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) occupies a superposition of lead [momentum](../../../../../../momentum-split.md) states. In a uniform free one-dimensional lead,

$$
\psi(x,t)=\int a(k)e^{ikx-i\hbar k^2t/(2m^*)}\,dk.
$$

A packet centered at $k_0$ moves with [group velocity](../../../../../../group-velocity.md) $\hbar k_0/m^*$ and generally spreads because its components have different velocities. For an initially unchirped minimum-uncertainty [Gaussian wave packet](../../../../../../gaussian-wave-packet.md),

$$
\sigma_x(t)=\sigma_x(0)
\sqrt{1+\left[\frac{\hbar t}{2m^*\sigma_x^2(0)}\right]^2}.
$$

This illustrates dispersive evolution; the actual emission contact determines $a(k)$ and whether only outward-propagating components are appreciable. It does not instantaneously become a single lead [plane wave](../../../../../../plane-wave.md).

A **[single-electron transistor](../../../../../../single-electron-transistor.md) used as a sensitive electrometer** exploits [Coulomb blockade](../../../../../../coulomb-blockade.md): a nearby charge shifts the offset $Q_{\rm ext}$ and therefore the conductance peaks. This requires stable calibrated [capacitances](../../../../../../capacitance.md) and low charge noise, rather than merely a small conductor.
