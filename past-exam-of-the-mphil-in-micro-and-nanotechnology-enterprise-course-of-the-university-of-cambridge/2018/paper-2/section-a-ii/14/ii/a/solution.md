<h1 id="section-a-ii/14/ii/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Only $n=1,2$ have energies below $6\epsilon$. Squaring the corresponding expansion coefficients gives $0.2^2+0.3^2=0.13$.

The printed rounded coefficients have norm

$$
S=0.2^2+0.3^2+0.4^2+0.843^2=1.000649.
$$

Thus the expected rounded answer is **$P(E<6\epsilon)\simeq0.13$**. If the decimal coefficients are treated as literal exact inputs and the state normalized, the exact [probability](../../../../../../../probability.md) for that input is **$0.13/S\simeq0.129916$**. This distinction avoids treating rounding error as a physical departure from [wavefunction normalization](../../../../../../../wavefunction-normalization.md).
