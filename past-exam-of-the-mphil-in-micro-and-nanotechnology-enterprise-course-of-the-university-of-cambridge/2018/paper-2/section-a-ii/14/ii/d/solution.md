<h1 id="section-a-ii/14/ii/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The result $16\epsilon$ projects the state onto the corresponding nondegenerate [energy eigenstate](../../../../../../../energy-eigenstate.md). Immediately after an ideal measurement,

$$
\boxed{\psi_{\rm after}(x)=e^{i\chi}\psi_4(x)}
$$

for an unobservable overall phase $\chi$. Subsequent isolated evolution adds $e^{-16i\epsilon t/\hbar}$ but does not mix in other [energy eigenstates](../../../../../../../energy-eigenstate.md). **A repeat ideal [energy](../../../../../../../energy-split.md) measurement gives $16\epsilon$ with [probability](../../../../../../../probability.md) one**, provided the [hamiltonian](../../../../../../../hamiltonian.md) is unchanged and no intervening disturbance occurs.
