<h1 id="section-a-ii/14/ii/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Orthonormal [energy eigenstates](../../../../../../../energy-eigenstate.md) contribute incoherently to the expectation of [energy](../../../../../../../energy-split.md):

$$
\langle E\rangle=
\frac{\epsilon}{S}\left[1(0.2)^2+4(0.3)^2+9(0.4)^2+16(0.843)^2\right]
=\frac{13.210384}{1.000649}\epsilon.
$$

Therefore **$\langle E\rangle\simeq13.2018\epsilon$** for the normalized literal decimals. Ignoring the small rounding-induced norm error gives $13.2104\epsilon$, conventionally reported at this precision as about **$13.2\epsilon$**. The mean need not equal an individual [energy](../../../../../../../energy-split.md) [eigenvalue](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md).
