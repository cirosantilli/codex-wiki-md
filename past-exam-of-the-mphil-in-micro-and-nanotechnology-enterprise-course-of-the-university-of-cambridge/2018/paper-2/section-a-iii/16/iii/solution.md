<h1 id="section-a-iii/16/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Take a positive surface potential $\Phi_0>0$, so positive ions are [co-ions](../../../../../../co-ion.md) and negative ions [counterions](../../../../../../counterion.md). With $u_0=e\Phi_0/(k_BT)\ll1$,

$$
\boxed{n_{\rm co}(x)\simeq n_0[1-u_0e^{-x/\lambda_D}],\qquad
n_{\rm counter}(x)\simeq n_0[1+u_0e^{-x/\lambda_D}].}
$$

The [counterion](../../../../../../counterion.md) density is slightly enhanced at the surface, the [co-ion](../../../../../../co-ion.md) density slightly depleted, and both approach $n_0$ over a [Debye length](../../../../../../debye-length.md). To first order their sum is $2n_0$, while their difference supplies the screening charge. Reverse the surface sign and the ionic roles interchange. These are number-density profiles, not trajectories of individual ions.
