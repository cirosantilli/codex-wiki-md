<h1 id="section-a-i/1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [scanning transmission electron microscope](../../../../../../scanning-transmission-electron-microscope.md) makes an image by assigning a transmitted-electron signal to each position of a scanned probe. **Its essential chain is [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) source, probe-forming optics, scan system, thin specimen, transmitted-electron detectors and synchronized acquisition.**

An [electron gun](../../../../../../electron-gun.md) and accelerating supply provide a stable beam of the chosen [kinetic energy](../../../../../../kinetic-energy.md). [Condenser lens](../../../../../../condenser-lens.md) optics and an aperture control probe current and convergence; the probe-forming [objective lens](../../../../../../electron-objective-lens.md) focuses a small spot on the specimen. Alignment, focusing and [astigmatism](../../../../../../astigmatism-optical-systems.md) correction keep the probe usable across the field.

Scan coils deflect the probe in a raster. A stable specimen holder positions an electron-transparent region, and a high-vacuum column limits gas scattering and contamination. A [bright-field transmission electron microscopy](../../../../../../bright-field-transmission-electron-microscopy.md) detector collects the central transmitted beam; an annular detector can select [annular dark-field electron imaging](../../../../../../annular-dark-field-electron-imaging.md), including [high-angle annular dark-field imaging](../../../../../../high-angle-annular-dark-field-imaging.md). Different angular selections give different contrasts, rather than a universal map of height.

Detector electronics, scan timing and a computer assign the collected signal to the corresponding image pixel. Spectrometers are useful additions but not essential to basic image formation. The post-specimen optics can adjust detector collection geometry; they do not set scan-image [magnification](../../../../../../magnification.md) in the same way as the projection lenses of conventional transmission imaging.
