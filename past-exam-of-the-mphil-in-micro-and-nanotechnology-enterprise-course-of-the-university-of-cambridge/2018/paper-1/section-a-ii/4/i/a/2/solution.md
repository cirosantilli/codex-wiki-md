↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

**Normal [evaporative deposition](../../../../../../../../evaporative-deposition.md) gives approximately horizontal films on the resist tops and exposed substrate, with little coating of vertical walls.** A small distant electron-beam-heated source in high [vacuum](../../../../../../../../vacuum.md) gives nearly line-of-sight arrival. Near-normal rays reach the opening floor, while vertical walls present almost no projected area.

The floor film and top film can remain disconnected, which helps a later [lift-off process](../../../../../../../../lift-off-process.md). A finite source size, tilted incidence, scattering or sample rotation changes that idealization and may coat one wall or shadow part of the floor. The stated $200\,\mathrm{nm}$ thickness is well below the $1\,\mathrm{\mu m}$ resist height, but this alone does not guarantee a clean break at every real edge.
