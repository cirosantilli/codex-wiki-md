↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

**An approximately isotropic wet etch produces lateral undercut as well as downward removal.** In the [hydrofluoric acid](../../../../../../../../hydrofluoric-acid.md) and [nitric acid](../../../../../../../../nitric-acid.md) mixture, oxidation of [silicon](../../../../../../../../silicon.md) and dissolution of the oxidized material cooperate. This is not the strongly crystal-anisotropic KOH-type picture.

If the exposed horizontal surface recedes about $200\,\mathrm{nm}$ and the local rate is isotropic, the etch front advances a comparable distance beneath each resist edge. A section through the front has rounded corners, and the [silicon](../../../../../../../../silicon.md) support under the mask narrows. The mouth can consequently widen laterally by roughly $400\,\mathrm{nm}$ in total at the original surface level. The precise contour depends on transport, chemistry and mask integrity; the diagram represents the isotropic geometrical limit.
