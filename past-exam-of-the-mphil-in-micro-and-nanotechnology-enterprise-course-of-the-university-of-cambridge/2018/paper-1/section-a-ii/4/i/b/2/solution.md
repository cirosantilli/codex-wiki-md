<h1 id="section-a-ii/4/i/b/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

**Near-normal [ion milling](../../../../../../../../ion-milling.md) preferentially removes exposed horizontal [silicon](../../../../../../../../silicon.md) and gives a more directional trench with much less lateral undercut.** Energetic argon ions eject surface atoms by momentum transfer. The resist blocks direct trajectories to the protected regions.

After about $200\,\mathrm{nm}$ removal of exposed [silicon](../../../../../../../../silicon.md), expect an approximately flat recessed floor and steep transitions near the mask edges. The mask is also sputtered: finite [etch selectivity](../../../../../../../../etch-selectivity.md), angular yield variations, mask-edge faceting and redeposition can produce sloped walls, fences or residue. No resist erosion depth can be deduced without its sputter rate. Thus a perfectly unchanged rectangular mask is only the ideal sketch.
