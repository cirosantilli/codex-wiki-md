↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [thermoelectric generator](../../../../../../thermoelectric-generator.md) uses the [Seebeck effect](../../../../../../seebeck-effect.md): a maintained [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) difference across suitable conductors produces an open-circuit electromotive [force](../../../../../../force-split.md). For a pair of legs,

$$
V_{\rm oc}=\int_{T_c}^{T_h}(S_p-S_n)\,dT
\simeq S_{\rm eff}(T_h-T_c)
$$

when the effective [Seebeck coefficient](../../../../../../seebeck-coefficient.md) is approximately constant. Repeated p- and n-type legs are usually connected electrically in series and thermally in parallel.

**Connecting a load lets part of the heat flowing from the hot side to the cold side become electrical [power](../../../../../../power.md).** Both a heat source and a cold-side heat-removal path are necessary: a uniformly warm device with no maintained [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) difference supplies no sustained output. Internal electrical resistance, heat leakage, contact losses and the finite [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) difference reduce the harvested [power](../../../../../../power.md); the converter is a heat engine rather than a source of energy from [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) alone.
