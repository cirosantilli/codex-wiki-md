<h1 id="section-a-iii/6/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**Use a [Thévenin equivalent](../../../../../../thevenin-equivalent.md): an ideal [voltage](../../../../../../voltage.md) source $V_{\rm oc}=S_{\rm eff}\Delta T$ in series with the internal resistance $R$, driving the load $R_L$.**

<a id="section-a-iii/6/ii/image-original-fixed-temperature-difference-equivalent-circuit-of-a-thermoelectric-generator-with-internal-and-load-resistances-distinguished"></a>
![](../../../../../../past-exam-of-the-mphil-in-micro-and-nanotechnology-enterprise-course-of-the-university-of-cambridge/2018/paper-1-teg-circuit.png)

**[Figure 5](#section-a-iii/6/ii/image-original-fixed-temperature-difference-equivalent-circuit-of-a-thermoelectric-generator-with-internal-and-load-resistances-distinguished). Original fixed-temperature-difference equivalent circuit of a thermoelectric generator, with internal and load resistances distinguished**.

The same current flows through both resistances, $I=V_{\rm oc}/(R+R_L)$. The load terminal [voltage](../../../../../../voltage.md) is $IR_L$, smaller than the open-circuit [voltage](../../../../../../voltage.md). The electrical battery symbol represents the maintained thermoelectric source under this model; heat input is still required.
