↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

**Conducting organic materials can be attractive for flexible, lightweight and near-ambient heat harvesters; inorganic materials offer a wider established range of high-performance and high-temperature options.** The appropriate comparison includes device geometry and durability as well as peak $ZT$.

[Conducting polymers](../../../../../../../conducting-polymer.md) can have low lattice [thermal conductivity](../../../../../../../thermal-conductivity.md), be solution processed and accommodate mechanically compliant large-area devices. Their carrier concentration and ordering can be modified through doping and processing. Contact resistance, environmental stability, reproducibility and obtaining stable complementary p- and n-type materials can limit practical modules. Their organic character does not imply that they remain stable at high [temperature](../../../../../../../temperature-split.md).

Inorganic thermoelectrics can provide high [power](../../../../../../../power.md) factors, [temperature](../../../../../../../temperature-split.md) stability and mature transport data over application-specific ranges. Some compositions are brittle, dense, expensive or contain elements requiring careful environmental management; they are not all interchangeable. Interfaces, thermal cycling and interconnects can dominate device lifetime.

An experimental example of organic optimization controlled the oxidation level of a [conducting polymer](../../../../../../../conducting-polymer.md) to balance transport and its low [thermal conductivity](../../../../../../../thermal-conductivity.md), showing that organics are not categorically excluded from thermoelectric performance improvements.[Nature Materials10, pp.429–433, abstract: oxidation-level control and low thermal conductivity](../../../../../../../optimization-of-the-thermoelectric-figure-of-merit-in-pedot-by-olga-bubnova-and-others.md) Material selection should use measured properties across the actual [temperature](../../../../../../../temperature-split.md) interval and the achievable thermal gradient, not a blanket organic-versus-inorganic ranking.
