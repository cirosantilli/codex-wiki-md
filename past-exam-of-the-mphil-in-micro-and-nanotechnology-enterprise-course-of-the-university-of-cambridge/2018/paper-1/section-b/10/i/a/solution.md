<h1 id="section-b/10/i/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Above-gap [photon absorption](../../../../../../../photon-absorption.md) generates [electrons](../../../../../../../electron.md) and [electron holes](../../../../../../../electron-hole.md). A [p-n junction](../../../../../../../p-n-junction.md)'s built-in field and carrier diffusion direct them toward selective contacts; their nonequilibrium populations can maintain separated quasi-Fermi levels and deliver electrical [power](../../../../../../../power.md). The equilibrium built-in [voltage](../../../../../../../voltage.md) alone is not a battery supplying continuous [power](../../../../../../../power.md).

Choose diode current positive into the cell. An idealized characteristic is

$$
I_{\rm dark}=I_0(e^{eV/(nk_BT)}-1),\qquad
I_{\rm light}=I_{\rm dark}-I_{\rm ph}.
$$

Illumination shifts the curve downward. At short circuit $I(0)=-I_{\rm sc}$; at open circuit $I(V_{\rm oc})=0$. Between those points, $V>0$ and $I<0$, so the delivered [power](../../../../../../../power.md) $P=-VI$ is positive.

**The [solar-cell fill factor](../../../../../../../solar-cell-fill-factor.md) compares maximum delivered [power](../../../../../../../power.md) with the open/short-circuit rectangle**:

$$
\boxed{\mathrm{FF}=\frac{V_{\rm mp}|I_{\rm mp}|}{V_{\rm oc}I_{\rm sc}}.}
$$

Series resistance, leakage, [recombination](../../../../../../../recombination-cosmology.md) and [temperature](../../../../../../../temperature-split.md) change the shape and hence the fill factor. Neither the short-circuit nor the open-circuit point itself delivers [power](../../../../../../../power.md).
