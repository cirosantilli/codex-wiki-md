<h1 id="section-b/9/i/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A useful net bookkeeping reaction for [thiol](../../../../../../../thiol.md) [adsorption](../../../../../../../adsorption.md) is

$$
\mathrm{Au_{surface}+HS(CH_2)_nOH\longrightarrow
Au_{surface}{-}S(CH_2)_nOH+H_{ads}},
$$

with removal or reaction of the [hydrogen](../../../../../../../hydrogen.md) depending on environment. Equivalently, a simplified overall balance can combine two [hydrogen](../../../../../../../hydrogen.md) atoms into $\mathrm{H_2}$. **These balances identify sulfur anchoring; they are not a claim that one elementary [adsorption](../../../../../../../adsorption.md) step universally releases [hydrogen](../../../../../../../hydrogen.md) gas.**

Molecules arrive at the interface, adsorb through sulfur, and reorganize laterally as coverage grows. Interactions between the chains favor packing and order, with the hydroxyl end groups facing outward. [Gold](../../../../../../../gold.md) surface structure, defects and [adsorption](../../../../../../../adsorption.md) conditions affect the final film.
