<h1 id="section-b/9/vi-first-occurrence/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vi (first occurrence)](../vi-first-occurrence.md)

This is the first printed “(vi)”, concerning adhesion. The reversible work per unit area to separate the solid–liquid interface into solid–vapor and liquid–vapor interfaces is

$$
W=\gamma_{SV}+\gamma_{LV}-\gamma_{SL}.
$$

Substitute $\gamma_{SV}-\gamma_{SL}=\gamma_{LV}\cos\theta_C$ from [Young's equation](../../../../../../young-s-equation.md):

$$
\boxed{W=\gamma_{LV}(1+\cos\theta_C).}
$$

This is the [Young-Dupré equation](../../../../../../young-dupre-equation.md). It has units $\mathrm{J\,m^{-2}}$, equivalent to $\mathrm{N\,m^{-1}}$. The liquid–vapor tension is the multiplier; the abbreviated printed subscript does not denote a new interface. Within this equilibrium model, smaller [water](../../../../../../water.md) [contact angle](../../../../../../contact-angle.md) means larger reversible work of adhesion for fixed liquid–vapor tension.
