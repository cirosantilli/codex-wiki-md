<h1 id="section-b/9/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

At the [theta temperature](../../../../../../theta-temperature.md), use the long-chain ideal-coil relations

$$
\langle R^2\rangle\propto M,\qquad R_g^2=\frac{\langle R^2\rangle}{6}.
$$

The two stated molar masses have ratio $M_2/M_1=5$. Therefore

$$
R_{{\rm rms},2}=11.2\sqrt5\,\mathrm{nm},\qquad
\boxed{R_{g,2}=11.2\sqrt{\frac56}\,\mathrm{nm}\simeq10.2\,\mathrm{nm}.}
$$

The input $11.2\,\mathrm{nm}$ is an end-to-end root-mean-square distance, not a [radius of gyration](../../../../../../radius-of-gyration.md): omitting $\sqrt6$ would give the wrong result. The apparent missing superscript in the printed masses does not affect the factor-five ratio used here. The estimate assumes the same chain architecture and unperturbed segment statistics, and uses the long-chain rather than finite-link correction.
