<h1 id="section-b/7/vi/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

**Use [Kelvin probe force microscopy](../../../../../../../kelvin-probe-force-microscopy.md) to quantify a local contact-potential difference.** A conducting tip and sample form a position-dependent capacitor. Apply a [voltage](../../../../../../../voltage.md) with adjustable direct and small alternating components,

$$
V(t)=V_{\rm dc}-V_{\rm CPD}+V_{\rm ac}\cos\omega t.
$$

The electrostatic [force](../../../../../../../force-split.md) is proportional to $C'(z)V(t)^2/2$, so its component at $\omega$ is proportional to

$$
F_\omega=C'(z)(V_{\rm dc}-V_{\rm CPD})V_{\rm ac}\cos\omega t
$$

up to the chosen force-direction convention. A [lock-in amplifier](../../../../../../../lock-in-amplifier.md) detects this component and a second controller adjusts $V_{\rm dc}$ to null it. **At the null, $V_{\rm dc}=V_{\rm CPD}$.** The compensation [voltage](../../../../../../../voltage.md) becomes the potential image.[Surface Potential Imaging: conducting probe and electrostatic null measurement](../../../../../../../scanning-probe-microscopy-modes-by-bruker.md)

A lift pass can reduce direct topographic interactions. Calibration on a reference with a known [work function](../../../../../../../work-function.md) is required for an absolute work-function interpretation, and the sign convention must state which electrode is biased. On a [semiconductor](../../../../../../../semiconductor-split.md) the result can include surface band bending, trapped charge and surface chemistry, so it need not equal a simple map of bulk electrochemical potential.
