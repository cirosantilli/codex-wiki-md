<h1 id="section-a-i/1/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

A coarse fringe in a [high-resolution transmission electron microscopy](../../../../../../high-resolution-transmission-electron-microscopy.md) image need not directly identify the lattice spacing of a new [phase](../../../../../../phase-waves.md). Check whether overlapping [crystals](../../../../../../crystal.md), a particle and its substrate, or differently oriented domains can produce the corresponding [moiré fringe](../../../../../../moire-fringe.md). Compare the underlying reciprocal-space peaks and their difference, use the particle boundary to constrain how many periods are actually sampled, and examine neighbouring or differently oriented regions. For the closest pair here, fitting a periodicity from less than one complete period is particularly unreliable. Distinguishing lattice contrast from a beat envelope prevents misidentifying a [phase](../../../../../../phase-waves.md) or over-interpreting local strain.
