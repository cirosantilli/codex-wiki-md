<h1 id="section-a-i/1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let the relevant projected [reciprocal lattice vectors](../../../../../../reciprocal-lattice-vector.md) have magnitudes $g_1=1/d_1$ and $g_2=1/d_2$, with angle $\theta$ between them. Subtracting their phases gives the slow spatial modulation $2\pi(\mathbf g_1-\mathbf g_2)\cdot\mathbf r$. The [moiré fringe spacing formula](../../../../../../moire-fringe-spacing-formula.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{D=\frac1{|\mathbf g_1-\mathbf g_2|}=\frac{d_1d_2}{\sqrt{d_1^2+d_2^2-2d_1d_2\cos\theta}}.}
$$

For parallel planes, $\theta=0$ and $D=d_1d_2/|d_1-d_2|$. For equal spacings, $D=d/[2|\sin(\theta/2)|]\simeq d/|\theta|$ at a small angle measured in [radians](../../../../../../radian.md). The apparent divergence as both mismatch and [rotation](../../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) vanish means that there is no finite beat period; it does not predict an infinite number of visible fringes.
