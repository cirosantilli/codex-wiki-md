↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

**Lateral [oscillation](../../../../../../../oscillation.md) or dithering** is added to the vertical [oscillation](../../../../../../../oscillation.md). It provides a tip-sidewall interaction signal that can drive lateral feedback while the vertical channel maintains height control. This is how a suitable flared tip can track steep or reentrant walls. Treating the additional signal as merely a larger vertical tapping [amplitude](../../../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) would not supply the required sidewall sensing.[Lateral-dither contribution to the effective tip envelope](../../../../../../../effects-of-lateral-tip-control-in-cd-afm-width-metrology-by-ronald-g-dixson-and-others.md)
