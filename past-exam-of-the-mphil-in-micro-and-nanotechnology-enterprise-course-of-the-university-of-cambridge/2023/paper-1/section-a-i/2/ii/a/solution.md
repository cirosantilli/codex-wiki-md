↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In [tapping-mode atomic force microscopy](../../../../../../../tapping-mode-atomic-force-microscopy.md), the cantilever is driven near a [resonance](../../../../../../../resonance.md) and its [oscillation](../../../../../../../oscillation.md) [amplitude](../../../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) is monitored. Tip-surface interaction changes that [amplitude](../../../../../../../wave-amplitude.md). A controller compares it with a chosen setpoint and moves the vertical piezoelectric actuator to reduce the error. The recorded actuator [displacement](../../../../../../../displacement.md) supplies the topography channel. A smaller [amplitude](../../../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) during approach commonly triggers withdrawal and an increased [amplitude](../../../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) during separation triggers approach, with the actual feedback sign set by the instrument's operating convention. Correct tracking requires enough response [bandwidth](../../../../../../../bandwidth-signal-processing.md) without destabilizing the loop.
