A conventional tapping tip tapers toward its apex, so its shank contacts an overhanging feature before its apex can probe underneath it. [Critical-dimension atomic force microscopy](../../../../../../../critical-dimension-atomic-force-microscopy.md) instead uses a calibrated flared or laterally extended tip, with a relatively narrow neck and side-sensitive control. The lateral extension can contact a reentrant sidewall while the neck clears the upper edge. The measured profile still includes the tip's finite shape and must be interpreted with a tip calibration; changing the tip does not automatically eliminate all [convolution](../../../../../../../convolution.md) errors.[Flared tips and bidirectional sidewall sensing](../../../../../../../effects-of-lateral-tip-control-in-cd-afm-width-metrology-by-ronald-g-dixson-and-others.md)
