<h1 id="section-a-i/2/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

An attached particle changes the effective tip shape, potentially creating a second contact apex. The image can then show duplicated or broadened copies of surface features, shifted edge positions and altered apparent roughness: a [double-tip artefact](../../../../../../double-tip-artefact.md) or more general [AFM tip-convolution artefact](../../../../../../afm-tip-convolution-artefact.md). Detachment can produce a sudden line-to-line change in that image kernel, so a region may abruptly recover a sharper profile. A change that follows the probe state or scan history should not be interpreted as a real reconstruction of the semiconductor surface.
