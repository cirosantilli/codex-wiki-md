<h1 id="section-a-i/2/i/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Excessive feedback gain can make the closed loop underdamped or unstable. Edge transitions then produce overshoot, ringing or oscillatory streaks in the recorded height. The apparent structure is a controller response rather than a real series of surface ridges. The exact [amplitude](../../../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) and frequency depend on the actuator, cantilever, loop delay and controller settings; without those data one can sketch the qualitative ringing but cannot predict a unique quantitative trace.
