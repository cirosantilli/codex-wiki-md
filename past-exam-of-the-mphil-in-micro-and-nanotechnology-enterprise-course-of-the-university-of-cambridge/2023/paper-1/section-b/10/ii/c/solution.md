<h1 id="section-b/10/ii/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

**The drawn aryl dichloride is expected to undergo no analogous substitution under ordinary ethoxide conditions.** Its chlorines are attached directly to sp2 aromatic carbons, rather than to the benzylic sp3 carbon of the previous substrate.

A normal [SN2 reaction](../../../../../../../sn2-reaction.md) cannot follow the accessible backside geometry available at a primary benzylic carbon. A simple [SN1 reaction](../../../../../../../sn1-reaction.md) would require a highly unfavorable aryl cation. Addition–elimination aromatic substitution would need suitable ring activation, such as a strong electron-withdrawing group in an appropriate position, which the drawing lacks. Forcing aryne chemistry cannot be inferred from ethoxide alone.

The conclusion addresses the actual E-plus-ethoxide scheme shown. The accompanying reference to replacing product B is inconsistent with the preceding substrate labels; interpreting it literally would not define the intended substitution comparison.
