**CNS-DDA contains an [amide](../../../../../../../amide.md) linkage**, $\mathrm{surface{-}C(O){-}NH{-}C_{12}H_{25}}$. The amine nitrogen becomes bonded to the surface carbonyl carbon.

**CNS-EG contains an [ester](../../../../../../../ester.md) linkage**, $\mathrm{surface{-}C(O){-}O{-}CH_2CH_2OH}$. One alcohol group forms the ester while the other remains an outer hydroxyl group. The latter is not an ether linkage at the surface carbonyl.
