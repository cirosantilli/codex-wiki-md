<h1 id="section-a-i/14/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

An [atomic layer deposition](../../../../../../atomic-layer-deposition.md) recipe depends on the surface chemistry during the first cycles. A silicon wafer normally carries native oxide with reactive hydroxyl groups. A pristine [graphene](../../../../../../graphene.md) basal plane has no comparable dense population of dangling bonds or hydroxyl groups, so precursor adsorption and initial nucleation are much less uniform.

Consequently the nominal number of cycles calibrated to give $2\,\mathrm{nm}$ on silicon can instead give isolated alumina islands, preferential decoration of edges, grain boundaries and defects, and uncovered graphene between islands. Adsorbed transfer residues may provide additional, irregular nucleation sites. Once alumina has nucleated, later cycles grow on the alumina normally; the problem is delayed, spatially uneven nucleation. The resulting local island height can exceed the film's area-averaged equivalent thickness.[Pristine graphene nucleation at edges and defects, and uniform growth after noncovalent functionalization](../../../../../../atomic-layer-deposition-of-metal-oxides-on-pristine-and-functionalized-graphene-by-xinran-wang-and-others.md)

Two suitable improvements are:

- Introduce a dense, controlled nucleation layer, for example a noncovalently adsorbed functional layer carrying reactive groups or a very thin oxidized metal seed. This supplies precursor-binding sites while limiting damage to the conducting carbon network.
- Optimize initial exposure rather than reuse the silicon recipe unchanged: use longer precursor residence/soak times and suitably reduced initial temperature to increase adsorption and nucleation, then continue normal cycles after closure. Direct sub-$2\,\mathrm{nm}$ continuous growth with extended residence times has been demonstrated.[Pulse/residence-time control of initial nucleation and continuous ultrathin films](../../../../../../parameter-space-of-atomic-layer-deposition-of-ultrathin-oxides-on-graphene-by-adrianus-i-aria-and-others.md)

Ozone or a mild plasma is another possible activation route, but it must be controlled because creating covalent defects can degrade the electrical properties that motivate using [graphene](../../../../../../graphene.md).
