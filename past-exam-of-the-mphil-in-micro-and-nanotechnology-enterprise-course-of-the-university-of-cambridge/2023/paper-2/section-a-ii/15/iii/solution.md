<h1 id="section-a-ii/15/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [integer quantum Hall effect](../../../../../../integer-quantum-hall-effect.md) requires a conducting [two-dimensional electron gas](../../../../../../two-dimensional-electron-gas.md) with well-resolved [Landau levels](../../../../../../landau-level.md). In the simple parabolic-band model their orbital spacing is

$$
\hbar\omega_c=\frac{\hbar eB}{m^*}.
$$

Low [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) and sufficiently low disorder broadening are needed so that $k_BT$ and the relevant level width $\Gamma$ are small compared with this spacing. A high-mobility sample with $\omega_c\tau\gg1$ is therefore useful.

Some disorder has a constructive role: localized states between extended Landau-level states allow the [Fermi energy](../../../../../../fermi-energy.md) to move through a range of carrier populations while the transport response stays on a plateau. The longitudinal response is then very small and the transverse response is quantized. Whether odd or only even integer plateaux are resolved also depends on whether spin splitting is resolved.
