<h1 id="section-a-iii/18/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Reading the lowest point of the order-disorder boundary in the supplied experimental diagram gives approximately

$$
(\chi N)_{\min}\simeq18,\qquad f_{PI}\simeq0.45
$$

(about $0.45$–$0.47$, allowing for the graphical resolution). The ordered region just above that minimum is lamellar.

This is a reading of the supplied [polyisoprene](../../../../../../polyisoprene.md)-[polystyrene](../../../../../../polystyrene.md) phase diagram. It should not be replaced by the often quoted symmetric-chain [mean-field theory](../../../../../../mean-field-theory.md) value $10.5$: that refers to a different idealization and is not the minimum drawn here.
