An [exciton](../../../../../../exciton.md) is a correlated electron–hole pair with a full [wavefunction](../../../../../../wave-function-split.md) $\Psi(\mathbf r_e,\mathbf r_h)$. In strong confinement, a useful leading approximation is a product of lowest electron and hole envelope functions, followed by a Coulomb correction. A spherical hard-wall ground envelope is nodeless, proportional to $\sin(\pi r/R)/r$, and finite at the origin. Finite barriers produce exterior tails.

For a [core-shell semiconductor nanocrystal](../../../../../../core-shell-semiconductor-nanocrystal.md) with **[type-I band alignment](../../../../../../type-i-band-alignment.md)**, both conduction- and valence-band offsets confine the carriers mainly to the core. Their envelopes penetrate the shell but overlap less with external traps. Chemical [surface passivation](../../../../../../surface-passivation.md) and spatial separation from defects can suppress nonradiative losses and increase [photoluminescence](../../../../../../photoluminescence.md) [quantum yield](../../../../../../quantum-yield.md). Shell thickness, interfacial quality and strain must be controlled; a defective shell can introduce losses.

**A larger shell [band gap](../../../../../../band-gap.md) alone does not guarantee type-I alignment.** A staggered [type-II band alignment](../../../../../../type-ii-band-alignment.md) can move one carrier toward the shell, reduce electron–hole overlap and alter lifetime and emission energy. The sketch illustrates the conditional type-I case. In CdSe/ZnS experiments, optical spectra were combined with structural and compositional measurements to test shell-growth interpretations.[1997, abstract: spectroscopy, structural/compositional characterization and locally epitaxial shell growth](../../../../../../cdse-zns-core-shell-quantum-dots-synthesis-and-characterization-of-a-size-series-of-highly-luminescent-nanocrystallites-by-b-o-dabbousi-and-others.md)
