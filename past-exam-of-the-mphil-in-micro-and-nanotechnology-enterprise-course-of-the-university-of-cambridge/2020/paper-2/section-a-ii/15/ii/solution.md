<h1 id="section-a-ii/15/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Measure nonlocal alignment-dependent resistance in otherwise comparable devices with several injector–detector spacings $L$. Steady [spin accumulation](../../../../../../spin-accumulation.md) obeys a diffusion–relaxation equation,

$$
D\frac{d^2\mu_s}{dx^2}-\frac{\mu_s}{\tau_s}=0,\qquad
\mu_s(L)\propto e^{-L/\lambda_s},\qquad
\boxed{\lambda_s=\sqrt{D\tau_s}}.
$$

In a weakly invasive detector limit, $\Delta R_{\rm NL}=(V_P-V_{AP})/I\propto e^{-L/\lambda_s}$; a semilog plot gives slope $-1/\lambda_s$. With transparent Py/Cu interfaces, fit a spin-resistance model including contact absorption and wire cross-section rather than assuming that every amplitude reduction is a bulk Cu loss.

Keep Cu processing, cross-section and [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) fixed, characterize [contact resistances](../../../../../../contact-resistance.md), reverse injector current and subtract alignment-independent offsets. Repeat at different [temperatures](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) to assess interface and bulk contributions. A [Hanle spin-precession measurement](../../../../../../hanle-spin-precession-measurement.md) provides an independent check of spin relaxation, with diffusion times and electrode rotation included in its model.
