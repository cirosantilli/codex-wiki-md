<h1 id="section-b/22/iv/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

With [planar anchoring](../../../../../../../planar-anchoring.md) on the cylindrical side wall, **uniform axial alignment $\mathbf n=\hat{\mathbf z}$ is a simple defect-free profile**. The axial direction is tangent at every wall point, so it satisfies the lateral boundary everywhere without elastic distortion.

This assumes degenerate [planar anchoring](../../../../../../../planar-anchoring.md), or an azimuthal preference compatible with axial alignment. End constraints, selected circumferential anchoring or imposed twist can favor another state. [planar anchoring](../../../../../../../planar-anchoring.md) alone does not require a circumferential vortex. The original axial-section sketch shows the uniform axial option.
