<h1 id="section-b/22/iii/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

[planar anchoring](../../../../../../../planar-anchoring.md) favors the [nematic director](../../../../../../../nematic-director.md) tangent to a surface. A flat wall at $z=0$ permits uniform $\mathbf n=\hat{\mathbf x}$, as in the sketch. A rubbed surface can select one in-plane axis; degenerate [planar anchoring](../../../../../../../planar-anchoring.md) selects only the tangent plane.

For the degenerate case, $f_s=(W/2)(\mathbf n\cdot\boldsymbol\nu)^2$ has minima at $\mathbf n\cdot\boldsymbol\nu=0$. A remote incompatible boundary produces elastic distortion. **Tangency alone does not specify an azimuthal direction within the surface.**
