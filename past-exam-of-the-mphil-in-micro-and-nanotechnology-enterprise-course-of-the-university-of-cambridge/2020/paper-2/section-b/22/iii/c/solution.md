<h1 id="section-b/22/iii/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Strong [homeotropic anchoring](../../../../../../../homeotropic-anchoring.md) requires radial orientation just outside a sphere, while the remote director is uniform. A plausible **dipolar texture contains a compensating [hyperbolic hedgehog defect](../../../../../../../hyperbolic-hedgehog-defect.md) on one side**. The particle's radial boundary has charge $+1$ under a consistent convention, balanced by an exterior point defect of charge $-1$ so the far field is uniform. The original cross-section depicts this configuration.

An alternative has an equatorial [Saturn-ring defect](../../../../../../../saturn-ring-defect.md). Its local disclination winding is half-integer; that local winding is not itself the net three-dimensional compensating charge. Particle size, elastic constants, confinement and anchoring strength select between configurations. The elastic theory explicitly compares dipolar, Saturn-ring and surface-ring states.[1999, configuration comparison and dependence on particle/surface parameters](../../../../../../../director-field-configurations-around-a-spherical-particle-in-a-nematic-liquid-crystal-by-holger-stark.md)
