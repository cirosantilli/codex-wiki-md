<h1 id="section-a-i/13/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Four advantages are **strong electrostatic gate control, rapid charge transport, high current-carrying capability and mechanical flexibility**.

A semiconducting [single-walled carbon nanotube](../../../../../../single-walled-carbon-nanotube.md) has a nanometre-scale channel cross-section, allowing efficient gate control in a [field-effect transistor](../../../../../../field-effect-transistor.md). A clean short tube can have a long [mean free path](../../../../../../mean-free-path.md) and correspondingly little channel scattering. Strong covalent bonding supports large [current densities](../../../../../../current-density.md) for interconnect applications, while high axial [thermal conductivity](../../../../../../thermal-conductivity.md) can help distribute heat. Small mass, large [aspect ratio](../../../../../../aspect-ratio.md) and mechanical compliance also permit conducting or semiconducting networks on flexible substrates. Metallic interconnects and low-leakage transistors require different electronic tube populations.

Manufacturing must control diameter, [carbon nanotube chiral vector](../../../../../../carbon-nanotube-chiral-vector.md), purity, alignment and placement. Metallic contaminants short-circuit transistor networks; low network density reduces current, whereas excessive density makes isolation difficult. Tube–metal [contact resistance](../../../../../../contact-resistance.md), [schottky barriers](../../../../../../schottky-barrier.md), tube–tube junctions, catalyst residues and substrate charge traps can dominate the intrinsic channel response. Thermal boundary resistance can limit cooling despite high axial [thermal conductivity](../../../../../../thermal-conductivity.md). Compare sorted and unsorted networks at matched density and contact geometry, measure contact and channel contributions separately, and assess uniformity across many devices. High intrinsic mobility alone is not evidence of a reproducible integrated circuit.
