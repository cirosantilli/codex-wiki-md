↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The arrangement is **diode sputter deposition**: the Au target, **a**, is attached to the upper cathode assembly **c**; positive Ar [ions](../../../../../../ion.md) bombard it and sputter Au onto the wafer. The lower wafer-holder [electrode](../../../../../../electrode.md) **b is the anode** in the simple two-electrode arrangement shown.

Unlike evaporation, [sputtering](../../../../../../sputtering.md) needs enough inert [gas](../../../../../../gas.md) to sustain a discharge. Over a low-pressure operating interval, raising Ar pressure can increase [ionization](../../../../../../ionization.md) and [ion](../../../../../../ion.md) current and hence the [sputtering](../../../../../../sputtering.md) rate at comparable applied [voltage](../../../../../../voltage.md). This is not a universal monotonic law: at higher pressure, scattering and thermalization of sputtered Au reduce the fraction reaching the wafer; at fixed regulated power, target [voltage](../../../../../../voltage.md) and current also adjust. **A pressure increase can initially increase the rate, but excessive pressure lowers transport efficiency.**
