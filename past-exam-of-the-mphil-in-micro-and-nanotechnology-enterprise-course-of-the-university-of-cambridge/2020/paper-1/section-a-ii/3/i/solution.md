↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A **high-pressure [Mercury](../../../../../../mercury-planet.md) arc lamp** is a conventional source for [proximity photolithography](../../../../../../proximity-photolithography.md). An electric discharge excites [Mercury](../../../../../../mercury-planet.md) vapour; radiative transitions produce strong spectral lines, selected by filters and delivered through collimating optics. Common lines are **$365\,\mathrm{nm}$ (i-line)** and **$436\,\mathrm{nm}$ (g-line)**. The finite mask-wafer gap gives near-field [Fresnel diffraction](../../../../../../fresnel-diffraction.md), rather than the far-field approximation. Diffracted light spreads into nominally dark regions, limiting the minimum printable feature.
