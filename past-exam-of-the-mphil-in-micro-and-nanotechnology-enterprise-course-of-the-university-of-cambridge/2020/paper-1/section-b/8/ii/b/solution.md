<h1 id="section-b/8/ii/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Common processing fits and subtracts a low-order polynomial background, often a quadratic surface or a polynomial for each scan line. This [AFM image flattening](../../../../../../../afm-image-flattening.md) removes scanner curvature on an assumed flat sample. Here the desired specimen signal is itself roughly quadratic, so such subtraction can remove the actual $200\,\mathrm{nm}$ bulge along with the artefact. **The two contributions cannot be separated by polynomial fitting to this image alone.** A plane subtraction removes tilt but does not independently calibrate curvature.
