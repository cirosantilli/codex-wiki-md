<h1 id="section-b/9/iii/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Start with a lightly boron-doped p-type Si wafer. Define an implantation opening for the pMOS region using an oxide/nitride implantation mask, then implant donor [ions](../../../../../../../ion.md), commonly [phosphorus](../../../../../../../phosphorus.md), at energies and doses giving the required well depth and concentration. An anneal activates donors and repairs damage; thermal drive-in can deepen and smooth the well profile. The donor concentration must exceed the original acceptor concentration in the well. Retain p-type material outside it for the nMOS device. Form a later n+ well contact to tie the n-well to the positive supply; the p+ substrate contact ties the substrate to ground. [Diffusion](../../../../../../../diffusion.md) from a donor source is an alternative to [ion implantation](../../../../../../../ion-implantation.md) and drive-in.
