<h1 id="section-b/11/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

An alkaline **[zinc-air battery](../../../../../../zinc-air-battery.md)** combines a porous Zn negative [electrode](../../../../../../electrode.md), aqueous KOH, an ion-permeable separator and a gas-diffusion positive [electrode](../../../../../../electrode.md). At discharge, oxygen is reduced at the air [electrode](../../../../../../electrode.md):

$$
\mathrm O_2+2\mathrm H_2\mathrm O+4e^-\to4\mathrm{OH}^-.
$$

Zinc [oxidation](../../../../../../oxidation.md) can be written $\mathrm{Zn}+4\mathrm{OH}^-\to\mathrm{Zn(OH)}_4^{2-}+2e^-$, followed by ZnO precipitation and hydroxide regeneration. The net reaction is $2\mathrm{Zn}+\mathrm O_2\to2\mathrm{ZnO}$.

High energy requires high accessible Zn loading and low inactive mass; high power requires short [ion](../../../../../../ion.md) paths, good electronic conduction and a large catalytically active [gas](../../../../../../gas.md)/[electrolyte](../../../../../../electrolyte.md)/[electron](../../../../../../electron.md) interface. A porous hydrophobic gas-diffusion layer admits oxygen while controlling [electrolyte](../../../../../../electrolyte.md) flooding and drying. Zn morphology and [electrolyte](../../../../../../electrolyte.md) composition must avoid premature passivation, excessive corrosion and hydrogen evolution; atmospheric CO2 can carbonate the alkaline [electrolyte](../../../../../../electrolyte.md). A very high loading can impair transport, so energy and power must be optimized together.

**Electrical recharge is possible, but is not guaranteed by a primary Zn-air design.** It requires reversible Zn deposition with controlled morphology and an oxygen [electrode](../../../../../../electrode.md) that can evolve oxygen efficiently without rapid catalyst or support corrosion. Dendrites, shape change, ZnO redistribution, [gas](../../../../../../gas.md) evolution and large oxygen-reaction overpotentials limit cycling. Bifunctional oxygen catalysts, or separate oxygen-reduction and oxygen-evolution [electrodes](../../../../../../electrode.md), help. Mechanical replacement of spent Zn is a different form of refuelling.[Demonstration of rechargeable alkaline Zn-air [electrodes](../../../../../../electrode.md) and transport/passivation limitations](../../../../../../phase-transition-tailored-nanoporous-zinc-metal-electrodes-by-liangyu-li-and-others.md)
