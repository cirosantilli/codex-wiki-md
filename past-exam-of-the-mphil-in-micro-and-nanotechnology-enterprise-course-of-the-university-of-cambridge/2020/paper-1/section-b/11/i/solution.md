<h1 id="section-b/11/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A **[lead-acid battery](../../../../../../lead-acid-battery.md)** is suitable. Its positive [electrode](../../../../../../electrode.md) reduces PbO2 to PbSO4 and its negative [electrode](../../../../../../electrode.md) oxidizes Pb to PbSO4. The net discharge reaction is

$$
\mathrm{Pb}+\mathrm{PbO}_2+4\mathrm H^++2\mathrm{SO}_4^{2-}\to2\mathrm{PbSO}_4+2\mathrm H_2\mathrm O.
$$

Using the supplied rounded potentials gives $E^\circ=1.70-(-0.35)=2.05\,\mathrm V$. The [Nernst equation](../../../../../../nernst-equation.md), taking solid and water activities as unity, gives

$$
E=2.05+\frac{RT}{2F}\ln\left(a_{\mathrm H^+}^4a_{\mathrm{SO}_4^{2-}}^2\right).
$$

At $\mathrm{pH}=1$ and $298\,\mathrm K$,

$$
\boxed{E\simeq1.932+0.0591\log_{10}a_{\mathrm{SO}_4^{2-}}\;\mathrm V.}
$$

Thus **$1.93\,\mathrm V$ if [sulfate](../../../../../../sulfate.md) activity is assumed unity**. The pH alone does not specify [sulfate](../../../../../../sulfate.md) activity, particularly where bisulfate formation and nonideal concentrated acid matter; without that assumption the numerical [voltage](../../../../../../voltage.md) is underdetermined.

For recharge, reverse both [electrode](../../../../../../electrode.md) reactions without irreversible loss of active material. Use porous, electronically connected Pb and PbO2 on acid-compatible conducting grids, with enough pore volume for PbSO4 formation and [electrolyte](../../../../../../electrolyte.md) transport. Fine, reversible [sulfate](../../../../../../sulfate.md) deposits are desirable; coarse persistent crystals cause [sulfation](../../../../../../sulfation.md). The separator must remain acid-resistant, electronically insulating and permeable to [electrolyte](../../../../../../electrolyte.md) [ions](../../../../../../ion.md) while preventing shorts; selective [ion](../../../../../../ion.md) exchange is not intrinsically required. A compatible lead-alloy grid is preferable to assuming any metallic substrate survives the positive [electrode](../../../../../../electrode.md)'s oxidizing acidic environment. Control current and charging [voltage](../../../../../../voltage.md) to limit hydrogen evolution, oxygen evolution, water loss, corrosion, heating and active-material shedding. Nickel hydroxide-based alkaline [electrodes](../../../../../../electrode.md) are poor choices in this acidic [electrolyte](../../../../../../electrolyte.md) because their phase stability and reaction conditions differ from those of their tabulated alkaline half-reactions.
