<h1 id="section-b/12/i/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A **[primary battery](../../../../../../../primary-battery.md)** is intended for a single discharge; a **[secondary battery](../../../../../../../rechargeable-battery.md)** is designed for repeated electrical recharge through sufficiently reversible reactions. A **[microbattery](../../../../../../../microbattery.md)** is defined by its small dimensions or microfabricated construction, not by rechargeability. Therefore microbatteries can belong to either class. A thin-film Li-ion [microbattery](../../../../../../../microbattery.md) with reversible insertion [electrodes](../../../../../../../electrode.md) is secondary because lithium can shuttle back and forth during discharge and charging; a miniature cell using irreversible primary chemistry remains primary. **Small size alone does not justify assigning every [microbattery](../../../../../../../microbattery.md) to the secondary class.**
