<h1 id="section-b/12/i/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Two [electrons](../../../../../../../electron.md) are transferred per formula unit of Li2O2. Its theoretical [specific capacity](../../../../../../../specific-capacity.md) is

$$
\boxed{q=\frac{2F}{3600M}=\frac{2(96500)}{3600(46)}=1.165\,\mathrm{Ah\,g^{-1}}.}
$$

At $3\,\mathrm V$, the corresponding theoretical [specific energy](../../../../../../../specific-energy.md) is **$3q=3.50\,\mathrm{Wh\,g^{-1}}$**, or $3500\,\mathrm{Wh\,kg^{-1}}$. The mass basis is Li2O2, equivalently the combined stoichiometric masses of consumed Li and O2; a value normalized to Li metal alone would be different.

A real [lithium-air battery](../../../../../../../lithium-air-battery.md) also includes [electrolyte](../../../../../../../electrolyte.md), porous positive-electrode support, separator, current collectors, excess reactants, packaging and possibly oxygen treatment. Insulating Li2O2 can block pores and [electron](../../../../../../../electron.md) transport, limiting utilization; polarization, [electrolyte](../../../../../../../electrolyte.md) decomposition, moisture/CO2 contamination and parasitic reactions lower useful [voltage](../../../../../../../voltage.md) and reversible capacity. A [lithium-ion battery](../../../../../../../lithium-ion-battery.md) instead shuttles Li [ions](../../../../../../../ion.md) between pre-existing host [electrodes](../../../../../../../electrode.md), generally in a closed cell without consuming atmospheric oxygen; the oxygen/peroxide reaction and its mass/transport requirements make the Li-air mechanism distinct.
