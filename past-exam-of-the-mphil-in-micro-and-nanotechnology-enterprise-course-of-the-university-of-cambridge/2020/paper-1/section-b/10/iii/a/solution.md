<h1 id="section-b/10/iii/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Under uniform geometric scaling, write $\mathbf r'_i=\lambda\mathbf r_i$. Each volume element contributes a factor $\lambda^3$, while the inverse-sixth-power separation contributes $\lambda^{-6}$. The double integral therefore scales as $\lambda^3\lambda^3\lambda^{-6}=1$, and

$$
\boxed{V'_{\rm vdW}=V_{\rm vdW}.}
$$

This assumes unchanged material [Hamaker constant](../../../../../../../hamaker-constant.md), pairwise-additive nonretarded interactions and the same intervening medium. It is a simultaneous scaling of both body sizes and their separation, not merely moving fixed bodies apart.
