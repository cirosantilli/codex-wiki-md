<h1 id="section-b/10/iii/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For nonretarded attraction between planar half-spaces, the interaction [free energy](../../../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) per area is $W(h)=-A_H/(12\pi h^2)$. The [Derjaguin approximation](../../../../../../../derjaguin-approximation.md) gives $F=2\pi R_{\rm eff}W(h)$, with $R_{\rm eff}=R_1R_2/(R_1+R_2)=a/2$ for equal spheres. Thus

$$
\boxed{F=-\frac{A_Ha}{12h^2}=-\frac{(15.6\times10^{-20})(10^{-3})}{12(10^{-9})^2}=-1.30\times10^{-5}\,\mathrm N.}
$$

The [force](../../../../../../../force-split.md) is **$13.0\,\mu\mathrm N$, attractive**. Scaling both radii and the gap by two gives $F'=F/2$, or **$6.50\,\mu\mathrm N$ attractive**. This agrees with the invariant energy $V\simeq-A_Ha/(12h)$: differentiating with respect to a gap that doubles makes the [force](../../../../../../../force-split.md) halve. The approximation requires $h\ll a$ and neglects roughness and retardation.
