<h1 id="section-a-i/2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Treat the magnetic [electron lens](../../../../../../electron-lens.md) as a paraxial [thin lens](../../../../../../thin-lens.md). A point in the specimen sends rays through different lens positions; the lens brings these to a conjugate point in the image plane, with $1/u+1/v=1/f$ and transverse [magnification](../../../../../../magnification.md) $-v/u$. Conversely, waves leaving different specimen points at the same angle are focused to the same point in the [back focal plane](../../../../../../back-focal-plane.md). This plane therefore contains the [electron diffraction](../../../../../../electron-diffraction.md) pattern, with radius $r\simeq f\theta$ for small scattering angle $\theta$. The specimen is just beyond the front focal plane, giving a magnified real image farther downstream.
