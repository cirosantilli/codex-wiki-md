↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Two useful properties are **[adhesion](../../../../../../../adhesion.md)** and **contact [elastic modulus](../../../../../../../elastic-modulus.md)**. The [pull-off force](../../../../../../../pull-off-force.md), the most negative [force](../../../../../../../force-split.md) on retraction relative to the noncontact baseline, maps [adhesion](../../../../../../../adhesion.md). Fit the contact part of the force-indentation relation using a suitable [contact mechanics](../../../../../../../contact-mechanics.md) model to obtain a reduced [elastic modulus](../../../../../../../elastic-modulus.md); for example, the [DMT contact model](../../../../../../../dmt-contact-model.md) uses

$$
F-F_{\rm adh}=\frac43E^*\sqrt R\,\delta^{3/2},
$$

where $F_{\rm adh}<0$, $R$ is the calibrated tip radius and $\delta$ is indentation. Converting $E^*$ to a sample [Young's modulus](../../../../../../../young-s-modulus.md) also requires the tip modulus and the two [Poisson's ratios](../../../../../../../poisson-s-ratio.md). The approach curve alone does not supply a retraction pull-off measurement. The loop area can additionally map dissipated energy; it is not automatically a purely viscous loss.[Sections 2.5.1, 2.5.3 and 2.5.5: modulus, [adhesion](../../../../../../../adhesion.md) and dissipation](../../../../../../../peakforce-qnm-user-guide-by-bruker.md)
