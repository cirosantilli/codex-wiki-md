<h1 id="section-a-iii/5/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Use **[steric stabilization](../../../../../../steric-stabilization.md)**: attach or strongly adsorb a dense layer of [polymer](../../../../../../polymer-split.md) chains or compatible ligands, with tails well solvated by the nonpolar liquid. Overlap of these layers costs osmotic and conformational [free energy](../../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) and prevents particle cores reaching the strongly attractive contact region. The anchoring groups must bind the particle while the exposed chains prefer the solvent; sufficient coverage and a good solvent are essential. Nonpolar media do not make charging impossible, but electrostatic stabilization there requires an appropriate ion-generation and charge-stabilization mechanism, rather than assuming aqueous dissociation behaviour.
