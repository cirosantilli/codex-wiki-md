<h1 id="section-a-iii/5/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

Adsorbed or grafted [polymers](../../../../../../polymer-split.md) **stabilize** a [colloid](../../../../../../colloid.md) through [steric stabilization](../../../../../../steric-stabilization.md) when coverage is high and the solvent keeps the chains extended. Charged chains can add electrostatic repulsion, giving [electrosteric stabilization](../../../../../../electrosteric-stabilization.md).

They can **destabilize** it through [polymer bridging flocculation](../../../../../../polymer-bridging-flocculation.md) when a chain adsorbs onto two particles, especially at incomplete coverage; through [depletion force](../../../../../../depletion-force.md) when nonadsorbing [polymer](../../../../../../polymer-split.md) is excluded from the gap and the surrounding solution's [osmotic pressure](../../../../../../osmotic-pressure.md) pushes particles together; or through collapse of an adsorbed layer in a poor solvent. [Polymer](../../../../../../polymer-split.md) identity, molecular size, solvent quality, adsorption and concentration determine which mechanism dominates.
