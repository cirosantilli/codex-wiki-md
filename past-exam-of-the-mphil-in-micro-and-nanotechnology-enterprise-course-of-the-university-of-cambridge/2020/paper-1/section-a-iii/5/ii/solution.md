↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The **[critical coagulation concentration](../../../../../../critical-coagulation-concentration.md)** is the [electrolyte](../../../../../../electrolyte.md) concentration at which rapid, approximately diffusion-limited [colloidal coagulation](../../../../../../colloidal-coagulation.md) begins: the electrostatic barrier has become ineffective and nearly every sufficiently close encounter sticks. It is an operational threshold for the specified [colloid](../../../../../../colloid.md) and [electrolyte](../../../../../../electrolyte.md), not a universal concentration or a guarantee of a mathematically exact zero barrier in every system.
