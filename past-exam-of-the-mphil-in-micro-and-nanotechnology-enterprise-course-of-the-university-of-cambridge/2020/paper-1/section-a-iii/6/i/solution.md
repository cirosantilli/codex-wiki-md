<h1 id="section-a-iii/6/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The discharge reaction is $2\mathrm{Li}+\mathrm I_2\to2\mathrm{LiI}$. It transfers two [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) and has $\Delta G^\circ=-539.4\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$ per reaction as written. The reversible [open-circuit voltage](../../../../../../open-circuit-voltage.md) is

$$
\boxed{E^\circ=-\frac{\Delta G^\circ}{2F}=\frac{539400}{2(96500)}=2.795\,\mathrm V.}
$$

The supplied [free energy](../../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) is already at approximately the requested temperature, so an additional temperature factor is unnecessary. Actual activities or discharge polarization could alter the terminal [voltage](../../../../../../voltage.md).
