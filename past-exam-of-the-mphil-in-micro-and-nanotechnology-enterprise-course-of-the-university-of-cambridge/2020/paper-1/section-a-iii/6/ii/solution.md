<h1 id="section-a-iii/6/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The stored energy is $0.75(7)=5.25\,\mathrm{Wh}$, and the load draws $P=IV=(20\times10^{-6})(2.5)=50\,\mu\mathrm W$. Hence

$$
\boxed{t=\frac{5.25}{50\times10^{-6}}=105000\,\mathrm h\simeq12.0\text{ years}.}
$$

This is an ideal energy-budget estimate using 365.25 days per year. It assumes the stated energy is usable at this load, constant current and [voltage](../../../../../../voltage.md), and negligible self-discharge, ageing and reserve capacity. No biological operating model is required.
