<h1 id="section-a-iii/7/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Tune doping to the optimum carrier concentration; a rise in conductivity is useful only if it outweighs the accompanying loss of thermopower and extra electronic heat conduction. Band convergence, favourable [density of states](../../../../../../density-of-states.md) and energy-dependent transport can improve the power factor $S^2\sigma$, provided [mobility](../../../../../../order-parameter-mobility.md) is retained.

Reduce lattice [thermal conductivity](../../../../../../thermal-conductivity.md) through alloy disorder, heavy or complex unit cells, grain boundaries and nanoscale inclusions that scatter [phonons](../../../../../../phonon.md). Aim to scatter heat-carrying [phonons](../../../../../../phonon.md) much more strongly than charge carriers; indiscriminate disorder can reduce both thermal and [electrical conductivity](../../../../../../electrical-conductivity.md). Nanostructuring and interfaces can therefore help, but an improvement must be demonstrated in **total $ZT$ at the operating temperature**, not inferred from lower $\kappa$ alone.[Strategies for improving electronic transport and reducing lattice heat conduction](../../../../../../complex-thermoelectric-materials-by-g-jeffrey-snyder-and-eric-s-toberer.md)
