↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

In [surface-enhanced Raman spectroscopy](../../../../../../surface-enhanced-raman-spectroscopy.md), **[electromagnetic SERS enhancement](../../../../../../electromagnetic-sers-enhancement.md)** comes from the strong local optical field near a plasmonic metal nanostructure. A [localized surface plasmon resonance](../../../../../../localized-surface-plasmon-resonance.md) in [gold](../../../../../../gold.md) or [silver](../../../../../../silver.md) can increase both the field driving a molecular [Raman transition](../../../../../../raman-transition.md) and the coupling of its emitted light to the far field. A common estimate is

$$
G_{\rm EM}\sim\left|\frac{E_{\rm loc}(\omega_L)}{E_0}\right|^2
\left|\frac{E_{\rm loc}(\omega_S)}{E_0}\right|^2,
$$

often summarized as an $|E|^4$ enhancement when pump and Stokes frequencies see similar responses. Sharp tips and narrow interparticle gaps can produce intense hot spots.

**[Chemical SERS enhancement](../../../../../../chemical-sers-enhancement.md)** changes the molecule's Raman polarizability through adsorption, electronic hybridization or a metal–molecule charge-transfer resonance. It is more sensitive to molecular identity and binding geometry than a purely electromagnetic field enhancement, and generally requires close contact.

Tune particle size, shape and gap geometry so that a plasmon resonance overlaps the excitation and Raman frequencies, and bring the target into the enhanced-field region. Reproducible adsorption sites, low fluorescence background and clean reference spectra are as important as a large local enhancement. A hot spot does not guarantee that a dilute sample occupies it; a detection limit cannot be computed from concentration alone. **Au or Ag improve the signal per suitably located molecule, while sampling and background determine the practical limit.**
