<h1 id="section-a-iii/19/ii/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Under the same positive, real-permittivity assumptions,

$$
\boxed{\text{negative dielectrophoresis: }\epsilon_p<\epsilon_m.}
$$

The particle is less polarizable than the surrounding medium and moves towards weaker-field regions. The sign describes the **polarizability contrast**, not a negative net electric charge. Conducting or lossy media require the real part of the full complex [Clausius-Mossotti factor](../../../../../../../clausius-mossotti-factor.md); a comparison of only the real dielectric constants is then insufficient.
