<h1 id="section-a-iii/19/ii/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For ordinary lossless dielectrics with positive real permittivities,

$$
K=\frac{\epsilon_p-\epsilon_m}{\epsilon_p+2\epsilon_m}.
$$

The denominator is positive, so

$$
\boxed{\text{positive dielectrophoresis: }\epsilon_p>\epsilon_m.}
$$

The particle is more polarizable than the displaced medium and is driven towards the region where its polarization lowers the field-plus-source energy most effectively. If the two permittivities match, this ideal dipole contrast vanishes.
