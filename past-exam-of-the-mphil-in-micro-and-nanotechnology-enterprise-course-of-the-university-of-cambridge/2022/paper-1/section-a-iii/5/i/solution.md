<h1 id="section-a-iii/5/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use consistent units for the [Gibbs free energy](../../../../../../gibbs-free-energy.md):

$$
\Delta G^\circ=\Delta H^\circ-T\Delta S^\circ
=-15.0-300(0.020)=-21.0\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}.
$$

Then $\Delta G^\circ=-RT\ln K$ gives

$$
\boxed{K=\exp\!\left[\frac{21000}{8.314(300)}\right]=4.53\times10^3.}
$$

This is a dimensionless thermodynamic [equilibrium constant](../../../../../../equilibrium-constant.md) defined through activities. For an ideal dilute $1:1$ association, the numerical concentration association constant is about $4.53\times10^3\,\mathrm{M^{-1}}$ when concentrations use the $1\,\mathrm M$ standard state. Both favourable enthalpy and positive entropy favour association here; association need not always have a negative entropy change because solvent release can outweigh the loss of translational freedom.
