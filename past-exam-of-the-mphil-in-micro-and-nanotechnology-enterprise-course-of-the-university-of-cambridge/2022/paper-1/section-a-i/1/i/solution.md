[Diffraction contrast](../../../../../../diffraction-contrast.md) arises because [elastic scattering](../../../../../../elastic-scattering.md) transfers transmitted [electron](../../../../../../electron.md) intensity between the direct beam and [Bragg diffraction](../../../../../../bragg-diffraction.md) beams. A local change in [crystal orientation](../../../../../../crystal-orientation.md), [strain](../../../../../../strain.md) or thickness changes that transfer. An [objective aperture](../../../../../../objective-aperture.md) selecting the direct beam makes strongly diffracting regions relatively dark in [bright-field transmission electron microscopy](../../../../../../bright-field-transmission-electron-microscopy.md); selecting a diffracted beam reverses the selection in [dark-field transmission electron microscopy](../../../../../../dark-field-transmission-electron-microscopy.md). This is a change in scattering conditions, not necessarily a change in chemical composition.

Three characteristic effects are **bend contours, thickness fringes and dislocation contrast**. [Bend contours](../../../../../../bend-contour.md) follow positions where a gently curved crystal meets a particular [Bragg diffraction](../../../../../../bragg-diffraction.md) condition. [Thickness fringes](../../../../../../thickness-fringe.md) arise from thickness-dependent exchange of intensity between coupled transmitted and diffracted waves. A [dislocation](../../../../../../dislocation.md) distorts nearby planes, changing their diffraction condition and producing localized contrast; changing the diffraction vector can suppress particular dislocations. [Moiré fringes](../../../../../../moire-fringe.md) from overlapping lattices provide another acceptable example, but their coarse spacing is a beat spacing rather than an atomic spacing.
