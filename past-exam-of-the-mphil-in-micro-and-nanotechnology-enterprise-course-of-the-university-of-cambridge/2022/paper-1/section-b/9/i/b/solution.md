<h1 id="section-b/9/i/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

**Diffuse a donor such as [phosphorus](../../../../../../../phosphorus.md) into the textured p-type surface, then control the thermal drive-in to form the shallow n-type emitter.** A phosphorus-containing vapour source can supply the donor over the exposed area. Diffusion and activation create an n-on-p [p-n junction](../../../../../../../p-n-junction.md); remove the phosphorus-rich surface glass and isolate the rear and wafer edges as required to avoid shunts.

An alternative is [ion implantation](../../../../../../../ion-implantation.md) followed by an activation and damage-repair anneal. The diffusion route is particularly convenient for large-area coverage of a textured wafer. The indicated roughly $1\,\mu\mathrm m$ layer is a depth target for process control, not an instruction to grow an undoped film and assume that it becomes n-type.
