<h1 id="section-b/9/i/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

**Deposit hydrogenated [silicon nitride](../../../../../../../silicon-nitride.md) by [plasma-enhanced chemical vapor deposition](../../../../../../../plasma-enhanced-chemical-vapor-deposition.md).** A plasma activates a silicon-containing precursor and a nitrogen source, enabling a conformal thin film at a lower substrate temperature than a purely thermal process. Tune gas composition, power and time to control thickness, refractive index, stress and hydrogen content.

The film serves as an [antireflection coating](../../../../../../../antireflection-coating.md) and can also provide chemical and field-effect [surface passivation](../../../../../../../surface-passivation.md). As a simple optical check, a quarter-wave thickness is $d\simeq\lambda_{\rm design}/(4n)$; $d\simeq75\,\mathrm{nm}$ and $n\simeq2$ correspond to a design wavelength near $600\,\mathrm{nm}$. The textured surface and broadband illumination require a fuller optical optimization, so this check is an order-of-magnitude explanation rather than an exact reflectance calculation.
