<h1 id="section-a-iii/19/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

A suitable donor-acceptor heterojunction provides an energetically favourable route from a bound excitation to an interfacial charge-transfer state and then separated carriers. The electron is preferentially transported in the acceptor and the hole in the donor. The interface facilitates dissociation that is inefficient in a single strongly excitonic material; it must also avoid excessive [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) loss and rapid interfacial [recombination](../../../../../../recombination-cosmology.md).

An idealized [bulk heterojunction](../../../../../../bulk-heterojunction.md) places most generated [excitons](../../../../../../exciton.md) within roughly a [diffusion](../../../../../../diffusion.md) length of an interface while maintaining continuous donor paths to the hole contact and acceptor paths to the electron contact. Domain dimensions should therefore be comparable to the material's [exciton](../../../../../../exciton.md) transport scale, not arbitrarily large. The total absorbing thickness should capture substantial light yet remain within the charge-collection capability set by mobility, field and [recombination](../../../../../../recombination-cosmology.md). Excessive mixing can impair transport or increase [recombination](../../../../../../recombination-cosmology.md); very coarse domains waste [excitons](../../../../../../exciton.md) before they reach an interface.[[Exciton](../../../../../../exciton.md) [diffusion](../../../../../../diffusion.md) as a constraint on planar and [bulk heterojunction](../../../../../../bulk-heterojunction.md) dimensions](../../../../../../tailored-exciton-diffusion-in-organic-photovoltaic-cells-by-s-matthew-menke-and-others.md)
