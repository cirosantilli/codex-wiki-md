Two examples are **[poly(3-hexylthiophene)](../../../../../../poly-3-hexylthiophene.md), [P3HT](../../../../../../poly-3-hexylthiophene.md)**, a conjugated polymer commonly used as an electron donor, and **[PCBM](../../../../../../pcbm.md)**, a soluble fullerene derivative commonly used as an electron acceptor. Both are [organic semiconductors](../../../../../../organic-semiconductor.md); “organic” does not imply that their transport properties are identical. Their blend is a familiar donor-acceptor photovoltaic model system.
