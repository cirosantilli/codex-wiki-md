↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The **[Winterbottom construction](../../../../../../winterbottom-construction.md)** extends the [Wulff construction](../../../../../../wulff-construction.md) to a crystal supported on a substrate. It includes the particle-substrate interfacial [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) and the substrate [surface energy](../../../../../../surface-energy.md) displaced by contact, so the equilibrium Wulff body is truncated by the support plane. The relevant contact contribution is $(\gamma_{ps}-\gamma_s)A_{\rm contact}$; it determines wetting and contact geometry. The basic unsupported construction accounts only for orientation-dependent free surface energies.[Supported-particle equilibrium construction](../../../../../../equilibrium-shape-of-a-small-particle-in-contact-with-a-foreign-substrate-by-w-l-winterbottom.md)
