<h1 id="section-a-i/14/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Use complementary composition, spatial and population measurements:

- **Insufficient Pt:** digest representative aliquots and measure calibrated ICP-OES or ICP-MS to obtain bulk Pt/Pd ratio and trace Fe. Blanks, recovery checks and standards control contamination and matrix effects. This is sensitive and quantitative but destructive and supplies no particle-by-particle spatial information.
- **Poor Pt-Pd mixing:** use STEM-EDS maps or line profiles of many individual particles. Characteristic X-ray energies identify the elements; background subtraction and calibrated sensitivity factors permit local composition estimates. HAADF-STEM supplies complementary atomic-number-sensitive contrast, with Pt generally brighter than Pd for comparable thickness. Core-shell segregation or distinct domains can then be tested. Projected overlap can imitate mixing, and low counts, thickness and beam damage limit nanoscale quantification; tomography or multiple views can resolve important ambiguities.
- **Fe impurities:** [EDS](../../../../../../energy-dispersive-x-ray-spectroscopy.md)/[EELS](../../../../../../electron-energy-loss-spectroscopy.md) can locate Fe-rich inclusions or surface-associated material, while the bulk digest establishes whether a trace signal is representative. Failure to detect Fe in a few [TEM](../../../../../../transmission-electron-microscopy.md) fields does not rule out dilute contamination below local detection limits. [XPS](../../../../../../x-ray-photoelectron-spectroscopy.md) can additionally assess near-surface composition and oxidation state, but is not a bulk assay.
- **Size uniformity:** obtain calibrated [TEM](../../../../../../transmission-electron-microscopy.md) images from randomly chosen independent fields, segment individual particles and report a number-weighted size histogram with sample count and shape descriptors. Avoid overlap and selection of only well-dispersed or attractive particles. A projected diameter is not a complete three-dimensional volume; scattering methods can corroborate ensemble size but weight particles differently.

The strength of [TEM](../../../../../../transmission-electron-microscopy.md) is correlating structure, size and local chemistry; its limited sampling is why a representative bulk chemical assay remains valuable.
