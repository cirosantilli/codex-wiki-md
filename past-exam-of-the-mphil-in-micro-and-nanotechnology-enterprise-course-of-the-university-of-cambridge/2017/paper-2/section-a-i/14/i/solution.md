<h1 id="section-a-i/14/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A bulk fcc atom has twelve nearest neighbours, whereas surface atoms are undercoordinated: ideal (111) terrace atoms have nine and (100) terrace atoms eight. Steps, kinks, edges and corners can have still lower coordination and different bond-angle environments. These sites change electronic structure, [adsorption](../../../../../../adsorption.md) energies and activation barriers, making geometrically different sites catalytically inequivalent.

In a Pt-Pd particle, a site's neighbours may be Pt, Pd or a mixture; [surface segregation](../../../../../../surface-segregation.md) means surface composition need not equal overall composition. Alloying also changes strain and electronic interactions. Surface relaxation, reconstruction, adsorbed [ligands](../../../../../../ligand-biochemistry.md), oxide or contamination can further alter accessibility and chemical state. A high nominal Pt fraction therefore does not establish either accessible Pt sites or a particular catalytic activity.
