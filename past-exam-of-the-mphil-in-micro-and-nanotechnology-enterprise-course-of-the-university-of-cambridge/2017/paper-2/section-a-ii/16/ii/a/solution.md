<h1 id="section-a-ii/16/ii/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Along the **easy axis**, expect an approximately rectangular loop: after saturation the remanent [magnetization](../../../../../../../magnetization.md) is close to $\pm M_s$, and reversal occurs near $\pm H_c$ through [nucleation](../../../../../../../nucleation.md) and domain-wall motion. Permalloy is magnetically soft, so $H_c$ is ordinarily small, although defects and growth conditions set its actual value.

The axes are $M$ in $\mathrm{A\,m^{-1}}$ and applied $\mu_0H$ in $\mathrm{mT}$. $M_s,H_c,H_k$ are symbolic because their measured values are not supplied. The $50\,\mathrm{mT}$ deposition field establishes an axis; it is not the measured coercive or anisotropy field.
