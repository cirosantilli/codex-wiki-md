<h1 id="section-b/21/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For a finite potential step and constant [mass](../../../../../../mass.md), $\psi$ and $\psi'$ are continuous. A finite jump of $\psi'$ would require a delta-function contribution in the integrated equation, while a jump of $\psi$ would be still more singular. Matching the preceding amplitudes gives

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
A+B&=C+D,\\
ik(A-B)&=\kappa(C-D),\\
Ce^{\kappa a}+De^{-\kappa a}&=Fe^{ika},\\
\kappa(Ce^{\kappa a}-De^{-\kappa a})&=ikFe^{ika}.
\end{aligned}}
$$

The [transmission probability](../../../../../../transmission-probability.md) is $|F/A|^2$ because the incident and transmitted regions have equal [wave vector](../../../../../../wavevector.md) and [mass](../../../../../../mass.md); in unequal leads a velocity ratio is also required. These matching conditions do not apply unmodified to infinite walls, delta barriers or [mass](../../../../../../mass.md) discontinuities.
