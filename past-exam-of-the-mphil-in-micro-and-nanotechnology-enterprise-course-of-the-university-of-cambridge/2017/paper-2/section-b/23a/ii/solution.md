↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Relevant interactions include **van der Waals attraction**, **electrostatic attraction or screened repulsion**, **[hydrogen bonding](../../../../../../hydrogen-bonding.md)**, and **hydrophobic/solvophobic association**. Coordination bonding, magnetic dipole interactions and capillary [forces](../../../../../../force-split.md) can also organize suitable building blocks. Entropic effects, including depletion attraction and packing [entropy](../../../../../../entropy-split.md), can drive ordering even without a new chemical bond. Which contribution favours assembly depends on solvent, [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md), concentration and surface chemistry; the complete balance is a free-energy change, not a requirement that every interaction be attractive.
