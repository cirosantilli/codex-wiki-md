<h1 id="section-a-iii/6/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Approximate the reaction [enthalpy](../../../../../../enthalpy-split.md) and [entropy](../../../../../../entropy-split.md) as constant over the measured interval, so $\Delta_rG^\circ=\Delta_rH^\circ-T\Delta_rS^\circ$. A [linear least squares](../../../../../../linear-least-squares.md) fit of all three [free energies](../../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) against [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) gives

$$
\Delta_rG^\circ\simeq-478580.96+183.9664T\quad\mathrm{J\,mol_{O_2}^{-1}},
$$

and hence

$$
\boxed{\Delta_rH^\circ\simeq-478.6\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol_{O_2}^{-1}},\qquad\Delta_rS^\circ\simeq-184.0\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}\,mol_{O_2}^{-1}}}.
$$

The negative [entropy](../../../../../../entropy-split.md) change is reasonable because [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) gas is incorporated into solids. The fit residuals are $+0.129,-0.257,+0.129\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol_{O_2}^{-1}}$, so the data support this approximate straight-line description. Extrapolating its intercept to zero [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) is a way of estimating a constant local [enthalpy](../../../../../../enthalpy-split.md), not evidence that the [heat capacities](../../../../../../heat-capacity.md) vanish or that the same phases persist to zero [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md). More [temperatures](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) and repeated heating/cooling measurements would test curvature and equilibration.
