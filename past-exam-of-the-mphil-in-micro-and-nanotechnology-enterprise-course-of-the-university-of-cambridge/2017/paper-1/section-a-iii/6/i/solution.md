<h1 id="section-a-iii/6/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The solid [electrolyte](../../../../../../electrolyte.md) conducts oxide [ions](../../../../../../ion.md). Equilibrating [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) at the two interfaces couples one $\mathrm{O_2}$ molecule to four [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md). Taking the positive measured [electromotive force](../../../../../../electromotive-force.md) as the air-reference potential relative to the Ni/NiO side, the [Nernst equation](../../../../../../nernst-equation.md) is

$$
E=\frac{RT}{4F}\ln\frac{p_{\rm air}}{p_{\rm eq}},\qquad \frac{p_{\rm eq}}{p^\circ}=\frac{p_{\rm air}}{p^\circ}\exp\left(-\frac{4FE}{RT}\right).
$$

Assume air at one bar with $p_{\rm air}/p^\circ=0.21$, pure solid phases of unit [thermodynamic activity](../../../../../../thermodynamic-activity.md), and $p^\circ=1\,\mathrm{bar}$. For the reaction normalized to one mole of [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md),

$$
2\mathrm{Ni}(s)+\mathrm{O_2}(g)\longrightarrow2\mathrm{NiO}(s),\qquad \Delta_rG^\circ=RT\ln\frac{p_{\rm eq}}{p^\circ}=RT\ln0.21-4FE.
$$

The negative sign is physically consistent with a strongly reducing Ni/NiO equilibrium relative to air. Using the supplied $R$ and $F$ gives

$$
\begin{array}{c|c|r}
T\ (\mathrm K)&p_{\rm eq}\ (\mathrm{bar})&\Delta_rG^\circ\ (\mathrm{kJ\,mol_{O_2}^{-1}})\\\hline
900&6.92\times10^{-19}&-312.88\\
1000&3.95\times10^{-16}&-294.87\\
1100&7.75\times10^{-14}&-276.09
\end{array}
$$

**The tabulated [free energies](../../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) are per mole of $\mathrm{O_2}$, not per mole of NiO.** Values normalized to one mole of NiO would be half as large. A reliable [oxygen concentration cell](../../../../../../oxygen-concentration-cell.md) measurement requires reversible interfaces, calibrated uniform [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md), a known reference [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) [pressure](../../../../../../pressure-split.md) and predominantly ionic rather than electronic conduction through [yttria-stabilized zirconia](../../../../../../yttria-stabilized-zirconia.md). Air is not pure [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md): dropping the $RT\ln0.21$ term changes the inferred [entropy](../../../../../../entropy-split.md).
