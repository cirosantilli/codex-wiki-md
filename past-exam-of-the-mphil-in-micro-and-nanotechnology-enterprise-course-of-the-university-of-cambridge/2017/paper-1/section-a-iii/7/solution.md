A [thermoelectric generator](../../../../../thermoelectric-generator.md) maintains hot and cold junctions of two materials with different [Seebeck coefficients](../../../../../seebeck-coefficient.md). Mobile charge carriers redistribute in the [temperature gradient](../../../../../temperature-gradient.md), producing an open-circuit [voltage](../../../../../voltage.md) of magnitude $|V|=|S_p-S_n|\Delta T$ for constant coefficients. A load permits [electric current](../../../../../electric-current.md); pairs of p-type and n-type legs are commonly connected electrically in series and thermally in parallel. Maintaining the output requires continuous [heat transfer](../../../../../heat-transfer.md) from the hot reservoir and rejection of [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) to the cold reservoir. The [thermoelectric figure of merit](../../../../../thermoelectric-figure-of-merit.md) $ZT=S^2\sigma T/\kappa$ expresses the favorable combination of large [Seebeck coefficient](../../../../../seebeck-coefficient.md), large [electrical conductivity](../../../../../electrical-conductivity.md) and small [thermal conductivity](../../../../../thermal-conductivity.md). Contact losses, Joule heating and parasitic [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) leaks reduce practical efficiency.

A [pyroelectric generator](../../../../../pyroelectric-generator.md) instead exploits a change of spontaneous [electric polarization](../../../../../polarization-density.md) with [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md). If the relevant component of the [pyroelectric coefficient](../../../../../pyroelectric-coefficient.md) is $p=dP_s/dT$, an [electrode](../../../../../electrode.md) of area $A$ receives a short-circuit [electric current](../../../../../electric-current.md) approximately $I=pA\,dT/dt$. A heating stroke and a cooling stroke produce opposite charge changes. Rectification or a synchronized electrical cycle can transfer [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) to storage. **A spatial [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) difference drives the thermoelectric device; a temporal [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) change drives the pyroelectric device.** Uniform, constant [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) gives no continuing current from [pyroelectricity](../../../../../pyroelectricity.md) even if the material is hot. Reported nanowire generators deliberately use changing [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), rather than infer continuous generation from a static hot specimen.[Nano Letters 12 (2012), pp.2833–2838, transient-temperature operating principle](../../../../../pyroelectric-nanogenerators-for-harvesting-thermoelectric-energy-by-ya-yang-and-others.md)

Both approaches can be compact, silent and free of macroscopic moving parts. The [thermoelectric generator](../../../../../thermoelectric-generator.md) gives continuous output under a sustained gradient, but needs two effective thermal contacts and sufficient [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) flow; very small $\Delta T$ gives a small [voltage](../../../../../voltage.md) and thermal contacts can consume much of the available gradient. Thin films exhibiting [pyroelectricity](../../../../../pyroelectricity.md) can respond to intermittent heating without a permanently maintained hot/cold separation across their thickness, but require repeated thermal cycling, low electrical leakage and suitable charge extraction. Their thermal [time constant](../../../../../time-constant.md) limits useful cycling [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md), and sensible heating/cooling can dominate the [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) budget. A steady hot pipe with an available cold sink favors a [thermoelectric generator](../../../../../thermoelectric-generator.md); periodic radiative heating can favor a [pyroelectric generator](../../../../../pyroelectric-generator.md). Neither converts equilibrium thermal motion at a single fixed [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) into sustained useful [work](../../../../../work.md).
