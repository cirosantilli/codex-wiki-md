↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Indexed [electron diffraction](../../../../../../electron-diffraction.md) ring positions from a polycrystalline deposit identify candidate phases and give [lattice parameters](../../../../../../lattice-constant.md) after camera-length calibration. Spot patterns from a small selected area establish [zone axis](../../../../../../zone-axis.md) directions, epitaxial orientation relationships or [crystal twinning](../../../../../../crystal-twinning.md). Nanobeam mapping can reveal grain orientation changes, while systematic spot shifts can measure relative [strain](../../../../../../strain.md) if lens distortions and scan drift are controlled. Spot or ring broadening can reflect finite coherent size, but also [strain](../../../../../../strain.md) and instrumental broadening, so it is not an unqualified particle-diameter measurement. Use a calibration standard and an empty support region, vary dose to check [electron beam damage](../../../../../../electron-beam-damage.md), and combine spacing information with elemental analysis when chemically distinct phases have similar [lattice parameters](../../../../../../lattice-constant.md).
