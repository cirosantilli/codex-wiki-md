<h1 id="section-b/12/iii/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

This [lithium iron disulfide battery](../../../../../../../lithium-iron-disulfide-battery.md) consumes a metallic [lithium](../../../../../../../lithium.md) [anode](../../../../../../../anode.md) and converts [iron disulfide](../../../../../../../iron-disulfide.md) into [iron](../../../../../../../iron.md) and [lithium sulfide](../../../../../../../lithium-sulfide.md). Commercial cells of this chemistry are [primary batteries](../../../../../../../primary-battery.md). A conventional [lithium-ion battery](../../../../../../../lithium-ion-battery.md) is designed for reversible cycling: [lithium](../../../../../../../lithium.md) [ions](../../../../../../../ion.md) transfer between two host [electrodes](../../../../../../../electrode.md), commonly a carbon insertion [anode](../../../../../../../anode.md) and a transition-metal compound [cathode](../../../../../../../cathode.md), while [electrons](../../../../../../../electron.md) travel in the external circuit. Metallic [lithium](../../../../../../../lithium.md) is not the intended normal [anode](../../../../../../../anode.md) reactant in that design. **Primary Li/FeS₂ conversion is the key distinction from the rechargeable lithium-ion host-electrode system.** The fact that both transport [lithium](../../../../../../../lithium.md) [ions](../../../../../../../ion.md) does not make them the same [battery](../../../../../../../electric-battery.md) chemistry, and a nominal 1.5 V is not a definition of either class.[title, printed pp.3–4, metallic-lithium construction and conversion discharge; p.16, nonrechargeable product instruction](../../../../../../../cylindrical-primary-lithium-handbook-and-application-manual-lithium-iron-disulfide-by-energizer.md)
