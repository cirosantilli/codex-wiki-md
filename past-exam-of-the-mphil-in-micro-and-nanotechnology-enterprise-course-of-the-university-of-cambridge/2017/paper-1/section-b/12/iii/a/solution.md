<h1 id="section-b/12/iii/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Four moles of [lithium](../../../../../../../lithium.md) react per mole of [iron disulfide](../../../../../../../iron-disulfide.md). Using molar [masses](../../../../../../../mass.md) $M_{\rm Li}=6.94\,\mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$, $M_{\rm Fe}=55.845\,\mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$ and $M_{\rm S}=32.06\,\mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$ gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{m_{\rm Li}}{m_{\rm FeS_2}}=\frac{4(6.94)}{55.845+2(32.06)}=0.2314}.
$$

Thus the stoichiometric [mass](../../../../../../../mass.md) ratio is **Li:FeS₂ = 1:4.322**, or approximately 18.79 wt% [lithium](../../../../../../../lithium.md) and 81.21 wt% [iron disulfide](../../../../../../../iron-disulfide.md) within the combined active reactants. This excludes [electrolyte](../../../../../../../electrolyte.md), current collectors, casing and any deliberate excess of one reactant. The underlying conversion reaction, rather than the nominal cell [voltage](../../../../../../../voltage.md), sets that ratio.[printed p.4, the two-step discharge reaction sums to four Li per FeS₂](../../../../../../../cylindrical-primary-lithium-handbook-and-application-manual-lithium-iron-disulfide-by-energizer.md)
