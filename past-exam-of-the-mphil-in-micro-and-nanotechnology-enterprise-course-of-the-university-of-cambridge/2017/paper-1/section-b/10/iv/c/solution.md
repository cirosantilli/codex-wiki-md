<h1 id="section-b/10/iv/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let $P_{\rm sat}$ denote the flat-interface [saturation pressure](../../../../../../../vapor-pressure.md) at the same [temperature](../../../../../../../temperature-split.md), $P_V$ the surrounding equilibrium [vapor pressure](../../../../../../../vapor-pressure.md), and $\overline V_l$ the incompressible [liquid](../../../../../../../liquid.md) [molar volume](../../../../../../../molar-volume.md). Equating [liquid](../../../../../../../liquid.md) and ideal-vapor [chemical potentials](../../../../../../../chemical-potential-split.md) to their common reference at $P_{\rm sat}$ gives

$$
\overline V_l(p_l-P_{\rm sat})=RT\ln\frac{P_V}{P_{\rm sat}}.
$$

Thus the [pressure](../../../../../../../pressure-split.md) contribution to the attractive normal [force](../../../../../../../force-split.md) on a wetted circular area is

$$
F_p=\pi a^2(P_V-p_l)=\pi a^2\left[\frac{RT}{\overline V_l}\ln\frac{P_{\rm sat}}{P_V}+P_V-P_{\rm sat}\right].
$$

For [capillary pressures](../../../../../../../capillary-pressure.md) large compared with the vapor-pressure difference, and a bridge [wetting](../../../../../../../wetting.md) essentially the plate area,

$$
\boxed{F\simeq\frac{\pi a^2RT}{\overline V_l}\ln\frac{P_{\rm sat}}{P_V}}.
$$

Here $F$ is the magnitude of the external separating [force](../../../../../../../force-split.md) balancing attraction, and $P_V<P_{\rm sat}$. A finite [contact angle](../../../../../../../contact-angle.md) adds a contact-line [force](../../../../../../../force-split.md) $2\pi a\gamma\sin\theta$ at each plate's rim, counted once in the [force](../../../../../../../force-split.md) on that plate, while the exact [pressure](../../../../../../../pressure-split.md) also retains both curvatures. The rim term is normally smaller than the narrow-gap [pressure](../../../../../../../pressure-split.md) term by order $d/a$ for appreciable [wetting](../../../../../../../wetting.md); it cannot be neglected uniformly near $\theta=90^\circ$. For complete [wetting](../../../../../../../wetting.md) the leading equivalent expression is $F\simeq2\pi a^2\gamma/d$. The corresponding equilibrium humidity is $P_V/P_{\rm sat}\simeq\exp[-2\gamma\overline V_l/(RTd)]$.

These are quasistatic, equilibrium expressions for an existing bridge, neglecting viscous suction, contact-angle hysteresis, disjoining [forces](../../../../../../../force-split.md) and deformation of the plates. The wetted radius must replace $a$ if the [liquid](../../../../../../../liquid.md) does not reach the plate edge. During actual separation a bridge can shrink, depin or break, so this local [force](../../../../../../../force-split.md) balance is not a promise of a constant [force](../../../../../../../force-split.md) for arbitrary travel.
