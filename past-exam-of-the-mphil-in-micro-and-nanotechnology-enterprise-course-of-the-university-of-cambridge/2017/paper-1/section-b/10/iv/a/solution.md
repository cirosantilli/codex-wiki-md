<h1 id="section-b/10/iv/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use the normal boiling point as the reference, $T_B=373.15\,\mathrm K$ and $P_B=101325\,\mathrm{Pa}$. At $T=313.15\,\mathrm K$,

$$
P_{\rm sat}\simeq101325\exp\left[\frac{41000}{8.314}\left(\frac1{373.15}-\frac1{313.15}\right)\right]=\boxed{8.05\times10^3\,\mathrm{Pa}}.
$$

This is approximately 0.0795 atmosphere. It uses the supplied constant [enthalpy of vaporization](../../../../../../../enthalpy-of-vaporization.md) approximation across a 60 K interval; it should not be presented as an exact tabulated [vapor pressure](../../../../../../../vapor-pressure.md). Absolute [temperature](../../../../../../../temperature-split.md), rather than Celsius values, belongs in the exponential.
