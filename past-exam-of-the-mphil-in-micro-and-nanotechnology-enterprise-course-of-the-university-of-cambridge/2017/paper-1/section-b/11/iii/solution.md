<h1 id="section-b/11/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

[nickel](../../../../../../nickel.md) is less noble than [copper](../../../../../../copper.md) and can dissolve anodically, but its [reduction](../../../../../../reduction-chemistry.md) requires a much more negative cathodic [electrode potential](../../../../../../electrode-potential.md). It therefore normally accumulates in solution rather than codepositing with [copper](../../../../../../copper.md). [lead](../../../../../../lead.md) can also oxidize, but sulfate can precipitate the resulting [lead](../../../../../../lead.md) [ions](../../../../../../ion.md) as poorly soluble [lead sulfate](../../../../../../lead-sulfate.md), contributing to [anode slime](../../../../../../anode-slime.md) or [passivation](../../../../../../passivation.md). [gold](../../../../../../gold.md) normally remains in the solid residue. [silver](../../../../../../silver.md) is normally recovered predominantly in [anode slime](../../../../../../anode-slime.md), but its path can include dissolution and reprecipitation or cementation: a permanent refusal of every [silver](../../../../../../silver.md) atom to dissolve is not the mechanism. Experiments with controlled Cu–Ag–Pb [anodes](../../../../../../anode.md), a fixed sulfate [electrolyte](../../../../../../electrolyte.md) and SEM/EDS plus [diffraction](../../../../../../diffraction.md) analysis explicitly find metallic [silver](../../../../../../silver.md) and sulfate-containing slime phases.[printed pp.137–138 and 141, experimental design and identification of silver/lead-containing slime](../../../../../../the-role-of-lead-in-suppressing-passivation-of-high-silver-containing-copper-anodes-during-electrorefining-by-labone-l-godirilwe-and-others.md)

Selective [electrode potential](../../../../../../electrode-potential.md), solution management and physical exclusion of slime permit purer [copper](../../../../../../copper.md) deposition. However, **the unusually large printed anodic slope prevents an unconditional silver-retention claim here**. The preceding calculation places the [anode](../../../../../../anode.md) near $0.34+0.6788=1.0188$ V versus the [standard hydrogen electrode](../../../../../../standard-hydrogen-electrode.md), above the tabulated $\mathrm{Ag}^+$/Ag value. [silver](../../../../../../silver.md) [oxidation](../../../../../../oxidation.md) is then thermodynamically possible; any [silver](../../../../../../silver.md) [ions](../../../../../../ion.md) reaching the [cathode](../../../../../../cathode.md) can be reduced preferentially to [copper](../../../../../../copper.md). Actual activities, precipitation, cementation and transport determine contamination. The requested 99.999 wt% purity cannot be established from the listed standard potentials alone. Monitor [anode](../../../../../../anode.md) potential against a reference, assay the [electrolyte](../../../../../../electrolyte.md) and deposit, and prevent slime entrainment; do not silently replace the printed 0.295 V/decade with a smaller number to remove this issue.
