<h1 id="section-b/11/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Gas formation is decided by each interfacial [electrode potential](../../../../../../electrode-potential.md) and reaction kinetics, rather than by comparing the terminal cell [voltage](../../../../../../voltage.md) directly with 1.23 V. On the conditional model above, the [cathode](../../../../../../cathode.md) potential is $E_c\simeq0.34-0.12854=+0.21146$ V versus the [standard hydrogen electrode](../../../../../../standard-hydrogen-electrode.md). At unit proton activity and unit hydrogen [pressure](../../../../../../pressure-split.md) this is above the hydrogen equilibrium potential, so hydrogen evolution is not favored there. The [anode](../../../../../../anode.md) is at $E_a\simeq1.0188$ V, below the unit-activity [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) equilibrium potential of 1.23 V, so [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) evolution is likewise not favored at the intended point. **Neither gas is expected in that model, despite a terminal [voltage](../../../../../../voltage.md) near 2 V; 1.2 V is lost in the [electrolyte](../../../../../../electrolyte.md).**

Without the missing cathodic kinetic information, the exact [cathode](../../../../../../cathode.md) potential is not fixed, so the hydrogen conclusion is conditional. Locally depleted [copper](../../../../../../copper.md) [ions](../../../../../../ion.md), increased current, [electrode](../../../../../../electrode.md) blocking or strong [passivation](../../../../../../passivation.md) can move an interface into gas evolution. Actual proton/[oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md)/hydrogen activities shift equilibrium through the [Nernst equation](../../../../../../nernst-equation.md), and gas [nucleation](../../../../../../nucleation.md) and activation barriers further affect observable bubbles. A reference [electrode](../../../../../../electrode.md) at each interface and direct gas observation distinguish these effects from an increased solution-resistance drop.
