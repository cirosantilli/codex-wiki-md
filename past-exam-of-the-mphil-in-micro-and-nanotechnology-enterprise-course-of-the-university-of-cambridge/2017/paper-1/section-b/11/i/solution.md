<h1 id="section-b/11/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The desired net process transfers [copper](../../../../../../copper.md) from an impure [anode](../../../../../../anode.md) to a purer [cathode](../../../../../../cathode.md). In the idealized common-electrolyte approximation, both interfaces use the same $\mathrm{Cu}^{2+}$/Cu equilibrium potential. Setting solid [copper](../../../../../../copper.md) activities to one and interfacial [ion](../../../../../../ion.md) activities equal gives **$E_{\rm rev}=0$ V**: subtracting two equal 0.34 V [reduction](../../../../../../reduction-chemistry.md) potentials yields zero, rather than 0.34 V.

A strictly thermodynamic refining [voltage](../../../../../../voltage.md) need not be exactly zero. At equal dissolved-ion activity, the required reversible applied [voltage](../../../../../../voltage.md) is

$$
V_{\rm rev}=\frac{RT}{2F}\ln\frac{a_{\rm Cu,cath}}{a_{\rm Cu,an}}.
$$

Dilution of [copper](../../../../../../copper.md) in the impure phase can therefore require positive reversible [work](../../../../../../work.md). Weight percentage alone does not specify that phase's [thermodynamic activity](../../../../../../thermodynamic-activity.md) or impurity molar composition. The zero result is the intended pure-solid approximation, not a precise chemical-potential measurement from the stated weight purities.
