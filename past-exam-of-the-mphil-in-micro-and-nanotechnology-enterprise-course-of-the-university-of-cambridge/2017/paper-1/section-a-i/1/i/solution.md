↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The electron-optical column contains an [electron gun](../../../../../../electron-gun.md), an accelerating supply, a [condenser lens](../../../../../../condenser-lens.md) system, an objective aperture, an [electron objective lens](../../../../../../electron-objective-lens.md), scan coils and alignment/stigmation controls. The [electron gun](../../../../../../electron-gun.md) may use [thermionic emission](../../../../../../thermionic-emission-split.md) or [field emission](../../../../../../field-electron-emission.md). The [condenser lens](../../../../../../condenser-lens.md) system sets the incident probe current and source demagnification; the [electron objective lens](../../../../../../electron-objective-lens.md) focuses the probe on the specimen. A movable specimen stage controls position, tilt and working distance. The column and specimen chamber need a [vacuum](../../../../../../vacuum.md) system, appropriate pumps and specimen-transfer hardware. Detectors collect [secondary electrons](../../../../../../secondary-electron.md), [backscattered electrons](../../../../../../backscattered-electron.md) or characteristic [X-rays](../../../../../../x-ray.md); amplification and synchronized raster acquisition assign a measured signal to each image pixel. The first two signals provide different imaging information, while characteristic [X-rays](../../../../../../x-ray.md) allow elemental analysis. **A source, probe-forming optics, raster scan, specimen stage, detectors, acquisition electronics and [vacuum](../../../../../../vacuum.md) system form the essential chain.**[printed pp.3–6, construction of the column, detectors and vacuum system](../../../../../../scanning-electron-microscope-a-to-z-by-jeol.md)
