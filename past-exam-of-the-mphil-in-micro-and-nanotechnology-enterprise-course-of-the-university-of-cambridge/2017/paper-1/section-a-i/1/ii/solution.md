<h1 id="section-a-i/1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The image annotation gives probe [electric current](../../../../../../electric-current.md) $I=43\,\mathrm{pA}=4.3\times10^{-11}\,\mathrm A$ and working distance $L=2.4\,\mathrm{mm}$. Interpret $d_o$ as the probe diameter and $\alpha$ as its convergence semiangle, in radians. To estimate that angle from the available dimensions, approximate the effective aperture plane as a distance $L$ from the specimen. With aperture radius $r_a=25\,\mathrm{\mu m}$,

$$
\alpha\simeq\frac{r_a}{L}=1.0417\times10^{-2},\qquad d_o=\sqrt{\frac{4I}{\pi^2\alpha^2\beta}}=\boxed{4.01\,\mathrm{nm}}.
$$

The [electron beam brightness](../../../../../../electron-beam-brightness.md) $\beta$ measures [electric current](../../../../../../electric-current.md) per probe area per [solid angle](../../../../../../solid-angle.md); the supplied convention corresponds to a circular probe and a paraxial cone of [solid angle](../../../../../../solid-angle.md) approximately $\pi\alpha^2$. The aperture diameter must be halved, and the annotated current is in picoamperes. Treating the full aperture angle as $\alpha$ would introduce a factor-of-two error in $d_o$.

This is a conditional optical estimate: the working distance is not necessarily the actual electron-optical distance from the effective aperture to the focused probe. The answer assumes that approximation, the quoted [electron beam brightness](../../../../../../electron-beam-brightness.md) at the operating [voltage](../../../../../../voltage.md), and negligible [optical aberrations](../../../../../../optical-aberration.md). The 30 kV annotation gives a much shorter [de Broglie wavelength](../../../../../../de-broglie-wavelength.md) than 4 nm, so a nanometre-scale probe is plausible. Actual [electron microscope resolution](../../../../../../electron-microscope-resolution.md) also depends on [diffraction](../../../../../../diffraction.md), scattering in the specimen, signal generation and collection, [signal-to-noise ratio](../../../../../../signal-to-noise-ratio.md), drift and specimen charging. A 4 nm probe does not establish that every measured edge width is 4 nm.
