<h1 id="section-a-ii/3/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**Oxygen-plasma ashing**, preferably a remote or downstream arrangement when low damage matters, removes organic [photoresist](../../../../../../photoresist.md) chemically. Feed [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) through a plasma source energized by radiofrequency or microwave [power](../../../../../../power.md). Electron-impact dissociation generates reactive [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) species; neutral radicals reach the wafer in a separate downstream region. Keep the wafer off a strongly biased driven [electrode](../../../../../../electrode.md) so energetic [ion](../../../../../../ion.md) bombardment is much smaller than in directional [reactive-ion etching](../../../../../../reactive-ion-etching.md). A barrel asher with shielded wafers is another chemical-stripping implementation.

$$
\mathrm{O_2}+e^-\longrightarrow2\mathrm O+e^-,\qquad\mathrm C_x\mathrm H_y+(2x+y/2)\mathrm O\longrightarrow x\mathrm{CO_2}+(y/2)\mathrm{H_2O}.
$$

These equations represent radical production and an ideal complete-oxidation balance; actual exhaust can also contain CO and fragments. Pumping removes volatile products. Directional shape fidelity is unnecessary for blanket stripping, while access around surface relief and high chemical removal selectivity are useful. Monitor endpoint and wafer [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md), and verify removal with a witness surface. [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) can oxidize exposed materials, charging can still damage sensitive structures, and inorganic or metal-containing residues may require a compatible separate clean. **Ashing removes organic contamination; it is not a universal residue-removal step.**[lecture transcript, discussion of barrel and parallel-plate plasma ashing, and comparison with RIE](../../../../../../physics-of-microfabrication-front-end-processing-lecture-20-by-massachusetts-institute-of-technology.md)
