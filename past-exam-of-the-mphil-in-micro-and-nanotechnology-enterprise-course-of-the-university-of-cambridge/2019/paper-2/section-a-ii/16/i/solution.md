↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [spin valve](../../../../../../spin-valve.md) contains a magnetically soft free [ferromagnet](../../../../../../ferromagnet.md), a thin nonmagnetic metallic spacer and a reference [ferromagnet](../../../../../../ferromagnet.md) pinned by [exchange bias](../../../../../../exchange-bias.md) from an [antiferromagnet](../../../../../../antiferromagnetism.md). The recording track's stray field rotates or reverses the free-layer moment while the reference remains approximately fixed. Spin-dependent scattering makes the resistance lower for parallel moments and higher for antiparallel moments. A sense current converts this into a [voltage](../../../../../../voltage.md) signal, or a fixed [voltage](../../../../../../voltage.md) into a current signal.

A useful angular approximation is $R(\theta)=R_P+(R_{AP}-R_P)(1-\cos\theta)/2$. Biasing the equilibrium moments near $90^\circ$ gives an approximately [linear response](../../../../../../linear-response-theory.md) to small track fields. Pinning stability, [magnetic shielding](../../../../../../magnetic-shielding.md) and the sensor's spatial extent matter for reading adjacent narrow bits.[Spin-dependent transport and spin-valve read-head principle](../../../../../../scientific-background-on-giant-magnetoresistance-by-the-royal-swedish-academy-of-sciences.md)
