<h1 id="section-a-ii/15/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [wave function](../../../../../../wave-function-split.md) vanishes in regions I and III. In region II it is sinusoidal and satisfies $\psi(0)=\psi(L)=0$. The time-independent equation there is

$$
\boxed{-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\frac{d^2\psi}{dx^2}=E\psi,\qquad0<x<L.}
$$

A nonzero [stationary state](../../../../../../stationary-state.md) therefore has standing-wave nodes at the two walls; it cannot have a nonzero constant ground-state [wave function](../../../../../../wave-function-split.md).
