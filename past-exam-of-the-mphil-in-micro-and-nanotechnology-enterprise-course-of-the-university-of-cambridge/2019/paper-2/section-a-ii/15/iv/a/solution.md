<h1 id="section-a-ii/15/iv/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Only $n=1,2,3$ have $E_n<10c$. The printed squared coefficients sum to $S=0.1^2+0.4^2+0.3^2+0.86^2=0.9996$, rather than exactly one. Normalizing those numbers gives

$$
\boxed{P(E<10c)=\frac{0.01+0.16+0.09}{0.9996}=0.260104\ldots\simeq26.0\%.}
$$

Treating the coefficients as rounded values of an already normalized state gives the intended approximately $0.26$ answer. Probabilities are sums of squared amplitudes because the [energy eigenstates](../../../../../../../energy-eigenstate.md) are [orthonormal](../../../../../../../orthonormal-set.md).
