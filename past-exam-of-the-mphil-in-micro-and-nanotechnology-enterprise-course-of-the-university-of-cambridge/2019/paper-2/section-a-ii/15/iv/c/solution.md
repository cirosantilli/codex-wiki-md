<h1 id="section-a-ii/15/iv/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The measured [energy](../../../../../../../energy-split.md) selects $n=3$. Immediately after an ideal [projective measurement](../../../../../../../projective-measurement.md), the normalized state is **$\psi_3(x)$ up to an overall phase**, with no surviving $n=1,2,4$ components. At later times before any further disturbance it is $e^{-i9c(t-t_0)/\hbar}\psi_3(x)$, where $t_0$ is the measurement time. A repeated [energy measurement](../../../../../../../energy-measurement.md) returns **$9c$ with [probability](../../../../../../../probability.md) one**.
