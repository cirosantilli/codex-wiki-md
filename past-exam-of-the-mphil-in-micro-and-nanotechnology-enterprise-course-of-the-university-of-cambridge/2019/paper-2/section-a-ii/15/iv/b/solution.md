<h1 id="section-a-ii/15/iv/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $(a_1,a_2,a_3,a_4)=(0.1,0.4,0.3,0.86)$ and $S=0.9996$. For the time-independent well,

$$
\boxed{\psi(x,t)=\frac1{\sqrt S}\sum_{n=1}^4a_n\sqrt{\frac2L}\sin\!\left(\frac{n\pi x}{L}\right)e^{-icn^2t/\hbar},\quad0<x<L.}
$$

It remains zero outside the well. Each expansion coefficient acquires a phase of unit magnitude, so every [energy](../../../../../../../energy-split.md) [probability](../../../../../../../probability.md), including the preceding $26.0\%$, is time-independent. The position [probability density](../../../../../../../probability-density.md) generally changes because cross terms between different energies oscillate.
