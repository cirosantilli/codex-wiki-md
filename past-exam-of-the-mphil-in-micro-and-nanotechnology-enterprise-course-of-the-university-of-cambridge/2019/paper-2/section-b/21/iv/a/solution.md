<h1 id="section-b/21/iv/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For longitudinal motion in transverse subband $i$,

$$
\boxed{\epsilon_i(k)=E_i+\frac{\hbar^2k^2}{2m},\qquad v(k)=\frac1\hbar\frac{d\epsilon_i}{dk}=\frac{\hbar k}{m}.}
$$

For a completely [free particle](../../../../../../../free-particle.md) take $E_i=0$ and use the full [wave vector](../../../../../../../wavevector.md). In a [semiconductor](../../../../../../../semiconductor-split.md) the parabolic-band [mass](../../../../../../../mass.md) is the appropriate [effective mass](../../../../../../../band-effective-mass.md) rather than necessarily the bare [electron](../../../../../../../electron.md) [mass](../../../../../../../mass.md).
