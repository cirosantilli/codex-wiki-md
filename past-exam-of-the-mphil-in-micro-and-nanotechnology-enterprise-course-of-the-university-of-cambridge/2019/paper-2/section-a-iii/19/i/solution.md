<h1 id="section-a-iii/19/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Negligible rail resistance makes all left-hand groove ends equipotential and all right-hand ends equipotential. The $N$ equal [hydraulic resistances](../../../../../../hydraulic-resistance.md) therefore connect in parallel:

$$
\boxed{R_{\rm chip}=\frac{R_g}{N}=\frac{R_g}{50}.}
$$

Here $\Delta p=R_{\rm chip}Q$ uses the total inlet flow. The two blind rail ends carry no flow beyond their final grooves.
