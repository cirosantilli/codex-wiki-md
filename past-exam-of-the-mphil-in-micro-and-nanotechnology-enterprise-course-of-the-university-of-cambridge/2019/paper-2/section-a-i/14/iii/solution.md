<h1 id="section-a-i/14/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Place the Ag particles **near the emitting shell**, along the outer sidewalls or accessible shell at the base. Its $690\,\mathrm{nm}$ emission is close to the $680\,\mathrm{nm}$ [localized surface plasmon resonance](../../../../../../localized-surface-plasmon-resonance.md); the $615\,\mathrm{nm}$ core emission is more detuned and farther from an externally placed particle. The plasmon near field decays with distance, so the local emitter-field overlap matters as well as spectral overlap. A thin spacer can reduce direct-contact nonradiative quenching.

These facts identify a favourable placement for enhanced interaction; they do not by themselves prove the spectroscopic **strong-coupling regime**, which additionally requires coherent coupling to exceed the relevant losses and normally produces resolvable mode splitting.
