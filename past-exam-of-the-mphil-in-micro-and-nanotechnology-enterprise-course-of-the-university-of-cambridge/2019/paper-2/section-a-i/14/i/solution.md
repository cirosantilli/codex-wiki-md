In **[selective-area epitaxy](../../../../../../selective-area-epitaxy.md)**, precursors supplied by [metal-organic vapour-phase epitaxy](../../../../../../metalorganic-vapour-phase-epitaxy.md) incorporate on exposed crystalline InP through patterned openings in a silicon-nitride mask. Epitaxial facet-dependent incorporation and surface [diffusion](../../../../../../diffusion.md) select axial and radial [growth rates](../../../../../../growth-rate.md). Advantages include lithographic position and pitch control, an epitaxial orientation relationship and the absence of a foreign metal [catalyst](../../../../../../catalyst.md). Limitations include mask patterning, selective [nucleation](../../../../../../nucleation.md) requirements, facet-dependent composition, loading and pitch effects, and unwanted radial growth.

In **[vapour-liquid-solid growth](../../../../../../vapor-liquid-solid-growth.md)**, precursors decompose and supply material to a liquid [catalyst](../../../../../../catalyst.md) droplet; [supersaturation](../../../../../../supersaturation.md) drives precipitation at the liquid-solid interface. Droplet size can control wire diameter and enable high [aspect ratios](../../../../../../aspect-ratio.md). Limitations include possible [catalyst](../../../../../../catalyst.md) contamination, droplet stability and coarsening, composition memory at heterointerfaces, and restrictions from the [catalyst](../../../../../../catalyst.md) [phase diagram](../../../../../../phase-diagram.md). Patterned [catalyst](../../../../../../catalyst.md) placement can also control position, so positional ordering is not exclusive to selective-area growth. Conversely, selective-area wires are not automatically free of taper or compositional interfaces.[Selective-area growth and composition-dependent [nanowire](../../../../../../nanowire.md) structure](../../../../../../efficient-green-emission-from-wurtzite-alinp-nanowires-by-l-gagliano-and-others.md)
