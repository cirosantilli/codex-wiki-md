<h1 id="section-a-i/13/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For the upper **core-shell precursor**, first reduce a precursor of metal A to make seeds; then introduce and reduce a precursor of B under conditions favouring heterogeneous deposition on those seeds rather than independent [nucleation](../../../../../../nucleation.md). Control added quantity, reduction rate and surface [ligands](../../../../../../ligand-biochemistry.md) to build a shell.

For the lower **disordered-alloy precursor**, reduce both metal precursors together so both metals enter the growing particles. Match their effective reduction rates by precursor chemistry, [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md), complexation or controlled feeding; otherwise sequential reduction can produce segregation instead of co-incorporation. Quenching or sufficiently mild growth can retain a disordered [alloy](../../../../../../alloy.md) before the later anneal. These routes need not initially produce equilibrium atomic arrangements.[Liquid-phase synthesis of core-shell and [alloy](../../../../../../alloy.md) architectures](../../../../../../bimetallic-nanocrystals-by-kyle-d-gilroy-and-others.md)
