<h1 id="section-a-i/13/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A **faceted, approximately equiaxed crystal** is indicated. A [truncated octahedron](../../../../../../truncated-octahedron.md) bounded by low-energy $\{111\}$ and $\{100\}$ facets is a plausible three-dimensional shape for an fcc metal particle; the hexagonal section alone does not uniquely distinguish it from other faceted polyhedra.

The [Wulff construction](../../../../../../wulff-construction.md) minimizes $\sum_i\gamma_iA_i$ at fixed particle volume. Place a plane with normal $\hat{\mathbf n}_i$ a distance $h_i=C\gamma_i$ from a common centre and take the inner envelope. Low-surface-energy orientations survive as large facets. Annealing permits [diffusion](../../../../../../diffusion.md) toward this equilibrium morphology, provided the time is sufficient. For a supported or strained [alloy](../../../../../../alloy.md), substrate adhesion and [elastic energy](../../../../../../elastic-energy.md) modify this simple free-particle construction.
