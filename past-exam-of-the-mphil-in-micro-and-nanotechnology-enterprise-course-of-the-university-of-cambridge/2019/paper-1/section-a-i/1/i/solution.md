<h1 id="section-a-i/1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The repeated, similarly oriented rounded triangular outlines in panel (a) are characteristic of [AFM tip convolution](../../../../../../afm-tip-convolution.md): a narrow raised object can image the shape of a blunt or contaminated [AFM probe](../../../../../../afm-probe.md), rather than reveal its own lateral shape. A finite tip radius broadens lateral dimensions. Replace or clean the [AFM probe](../../../../../../afm-probe.md), check it on an independently characterized sharp-feature standard, and repeat at low load; agreement after a tip change is stronger evidence than a visually pleasing image. Image processing cannot uniquely recover unmeasured sidewalls or prove that every particle with a similar outline is an artefact.

Panel (b) contains horizontal streaks associated with steep features. The natural instrumental diagnosis is [AFM feedback artefact](../../../../../../afm-feedback-artefact.md): the height controller does not track a rapid change faithfully, or rings after it. Reduce the scan speed and adjust proportional/integral gains below the oscillation threshold. Compare trace and retrace and rotate the fast-scan direction: a streak following the scan rather than the specimen is instrumental. Loose contamination can also produce scan-line streaks, so clean or change the [AFM probe](../../../../../../afm-probe.md) if tuning does not remove them. **Tip-shape broadening in (a), and scan-direction tracking/streaking in (b), are the principal diagnoses; the images alone do not uniquely establish every underlying cause.**
