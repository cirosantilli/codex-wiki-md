↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

**A steady-current image is not supported by the electrical connection drawn.** [tunnelling atomic force microscopy](../../../../../../../tunnelling-atomic-force-microscopy.md) requires a conducting [AFM probe](../../../../../../../afm-probe.md), an applied [bias voltage](../../../../../../../bias-voltage.md) and a continuous return path from the contacted region to the current amplifier. [graphene](../../../../../../../graphene.md) is conducting, but a flake on insulating [glass](../../../../../../../glass.md) is not electrically grounded merely because the other side or edge of the [glass](../../../../../../../glass.md) is earthed. Charging transients or displacement current are not its steady conductivity. Add a metal contact actually touching the [graphene](../../../../../../../graphene.md) before using its current contrast; ordinary force-based [surface topography](../../../../../../../surface-topography.md) can still be recorded without that contact. The manufacturer's nanostructure measurements explicitly distinguish objects connected to conducting pads from unconnected particles on the same insulating substrate.[application note 132, Figure 11 and accompanying discussion of electrical connection](../../../../../../../simultaneous-electrical-and-mechanical-property-mapping-at-the-nanoscale-with-peakforce-tuna-by-bruker.md)
