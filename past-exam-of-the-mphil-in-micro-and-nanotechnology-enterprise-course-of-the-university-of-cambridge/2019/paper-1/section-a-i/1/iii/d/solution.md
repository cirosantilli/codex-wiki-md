↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

**Conventional [semiconductor](../../../../../../../semiconductor-split.md) [scanning capacitance microscopy](../../../../../../../scanning-capacitance-microscopy.md) is not a reliable dopant-contrast image of this specimen as drawn.** Its usual $dC/dV$ signal relies on a tip–insulator–[semiconductor](../../../../../../../semiconductor-split.md) [capacitance](../../../../../../../capacitance.md) whose depletion region changes under AC bias. An isolated conducting [graphene](../../../../../../../graphene.md) sheet on thick insulating [glass](../../../../../../../glass.md) provides neither that conventional depletion geometry nor a controlled charge reservoir; simply earthing [glass](../../../../../../../glass.md) does not supply one. Capacitive or electrostatic contrast may be detectable with a suitably designed back [electrode](../../../../../../../electrode.md), a contacted flake and an appropriate capacitance-sensitive instrument, but that is an additional electrical device specification. Force-based [surface topography](../../../../../../../surface-topography.md) remains possible independently of the specialized [capacitance](../../../../../../../capacitance.md) channel.
