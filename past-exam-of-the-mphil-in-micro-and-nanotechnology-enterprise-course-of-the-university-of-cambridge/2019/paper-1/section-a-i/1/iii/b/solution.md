**[quantitative nanomechanical mapping](../../../../../../../quantitative-nanomechanical-mapping.md) can image the flake without an electrical return path.** Repeated [AFM force curves](../../../../../../../afm-force-curve.md) provide [surface topography](../../../../../../../surface-topography.md), [adhesion](../../../../../../../adhesion.md) and deformation or effective [elastic modulus](../../../../../../../elastic-modulus.md) channels. The step and differences in tip–surface interaction can distinguish [graphene](../../../../../../../graphene.md) from [glass](../../../../../../../glass.md). For an atomically thin supported sheet the contact response is strongly substrate dependent, so fitting a three-dimensional half-space contact model does not directly measure the intrinsic in-plane [Young's modulus](../../../../../../../young-s-modulus.md) of free [graphene](../../../../../../../graphene.md). Calibrate the [cantilever spring constant](../../../../../../../cantilever-spring-constant.md), deflection sensitivity and tip radius, use small loads, and compare bare substrate. The manufacturer's description specifies the force-curve origin of the mechanical channels.[application note 132, Quantitative Mechanical Property Mapping](../../../../../../../simultaneous-electrical-and-mechanical-property-mapping-at-the-nanoscale-with-peakforce-tuna-by-bruker.md)
