<h1 id="section-a-i/1/iii/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

**[friction force microscopy](../../../../../../../friction-force-microscopy.md) is suitable.** Contact scanning twists the [AFM cantilever](../../../../../../../afm-cantilever.md); the lateral photodetector difference measures [friction](../../../../../../../friction.md) and can distinguish the supported [graphene](../../../../../../../graphene.md) and [glass](../../../../../../../glass.md) even if their heights are close. Acquire both trace and retrace and use half their lateral-signal difference to suppress slope-induced cross-talk. Keep the normal load low enough to avoid dragging or damaging the flake, control humidity and contamination, and calibrate torsional sensitivity if a quantitative [coefficient of friction](../../../../../../../coefficient-of-friction.md) is required. An electrical connection is unnecessary.
