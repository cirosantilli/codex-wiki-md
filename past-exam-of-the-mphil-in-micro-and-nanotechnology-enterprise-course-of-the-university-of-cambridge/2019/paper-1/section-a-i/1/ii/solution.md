[piezoelectric scanner creep](../../../../../../piezoelectric-scanner-creep.md) is the continued slow displacement after a step in scanner drive, even when the commanded voltage has stopped changing. The scanner's [strain](../../../../../../strain.md) continues to relax, and the motion can resemble a slowly decaying, approximately logarithmic drift. After changing scan size, position or offset, early lines can therefore have stretched or compressed coordinates. Allow settling, avoid large abrupt moves, use a position-sensor [closed-loop control](../../../../../../closed-loop-control.md) system, and compare repeated images. This time-dependent response differs from the direction-dependent [piezoelectric hysteresis](../../../../../../piezoelectric-hysteresis.md) and from ordinary thermal drift.
