<h1 id="section-a-i/2/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Three suitable approaches give complementary contrast. [tapping-mode atomic force microscopy](../../../../../../tapping-mode-atomic-force-microscopy.md) supplies [surface topography](../../../../../../surface-topography.md) and an oscillation-phase image. It reduces lateral drag and is often effective for soft [block copolymer](../../../../../../block-copolymer.md) domains. Its phase contrast depends on elastic response, [adhesion](../../../../../../adhesion.md), dissipation and operating setpoint together: a bright phase domain is not automatically the stiffer polymer. Check contrast as amplitude and setpoint change and use a known reference.

[quantitative nanomechanical mapping](../../../../../../quantitative-nanomechanical-mapping.md) fits the repulsive portions of many [AFM force curves](../../../../../../afm-force-curve.md) and separately maps effective [elastic modulus](../../../../../../elastic-modulus.md), [adhesion](../../../../../../adhesion.md) and deformation. It directly addresses the stiffness difference and limits maximum [force](../../../../../../force-split.md). Its quantitative result depends on tip radius, [force](../../../../../../force-split.md) calibration, contact model, loading rate and film thickness; domains narrower than the contact footprint are mixed and the substrate can dominate a thin film. Use small indentation, substrate controls and a suitable viscoelastic interpretation.

[friction force microscopy](../../../../../../friction-force-microscopy.md) can reveal domains with different frictional and adhesive response, using torsional deflection during contact scans. It is simple and sensitive to surface chemistry, but continuous shear may deform or displace soft domains, and slope/topography can contaminate the lateral signal. Acquire trace–retrace pairs at several low normal loads and check reproducibility. **Use a mechanical-modulus map to test the stiffness assignment; phase or [friction](../../../../../../friction.md) contrast alone does not uniquely identify stiffness or chemical composition.**
