<h1 id="section-a-i/2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

At position (1), the [AFM probe](../../../../../../afm-probe.md) is far from the surface, with approximately zero [force](../../../../../../force-split.md). At (2), attractive interactions bend the [AFM cantilever](../../../../../../afm-cantilever.md) toward the surface; when the attractive [force](../../../../../../force-split.md) gradient exceeds the [cantilever spring constant](../../../../../../cantilever-spring-constant.md), the equilibrium becomes unstable and [jump-to-contact](../../../../../../jump-to-contact.md) occurs. Position (3) is on the mechanical-contact branch: changes in imposed height largely bend the [AFM cantilever](../../../../../../afm-cantilever.md) and, for a compliant specimen, indent the sample. This branch can include compressive contact and adhesive contact under tension; contact does not itself imply a positive [force](../../../../../../force-split.md) at every point. At (4), during withdrawal the tip remains attached and pulls the [AFM cantilever](../../../../../../afm-cantilever.md) downward until the [pull-off force](../../../../../../pull-off-force.md) is reached and it detaches. **Approach and retraction follow different paths because [adhesion](../../../../../../adhesion.md) creates a hysteretic contact.** The numbered points identify stages on the source curve, not four extra question parts.
