<h1 id="section-a-iii/6/i/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The more negative standard [reduction](../../../../../../../reduction-chemistry.md) potential identifies iron as the species more readily oxidized. Combining $\mathrm{Fe\to Fe^{2+}+2e^-}$ with $\mathrm{Sn^{2+}+2e^-\to Sn}$ gives the spontaneous standard [galvanic cell](../../../../../../../galvanic-cell.md) reaction

$$
\boxed{\mathrm{Fe(s)+Sn^{2+}(aq)\longrightarrow Fe^{2+}(aq)+Sn(s)}}.
$$

The [electron](../../../../../../../electron.md) balance is two per reaction, and the activities of the pure metals are unity.
