<h1 id="section-a-iii/6/i/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a [galvanic cell](../../../../../../../galvanic-cell.md), subtract the standard [anode](../../../../../../../anode.md) [reduction](../../../../../../../reduction-chemistry.md) potential from the standard [cathode](../../../../../../../cathode.md) [reduction](../../../../../../../reduction-chemistry.md) potential:

$$
E^\circ_{\rm cell}=E^\circ_{\rm Sn^{2+}/Sn}-E^\circ_{\rm Fe^{2+}/Fe}
=-0.136-(-0.440)=\boxed{+0.304\ \mathrm V}.
$$

A [reduction](../../../../../../../reduction-chemistry.md) potential is not multiplied by the number of transferred [electrons](../../../../../../../electron.md).
