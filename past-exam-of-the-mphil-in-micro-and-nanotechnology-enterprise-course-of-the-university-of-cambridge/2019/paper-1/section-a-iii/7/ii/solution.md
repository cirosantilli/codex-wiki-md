<h1 id="section-a-iii/7/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

An ordinary [capacitor](../../../../../../capacitor.md) stores [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) by separating charge across a [dielectric](../../../../../../dielectric.md): $E=CV^2/2$ and $Q=CV$. Electronic charge redistribution is rapid, giving high [specific power](../../../../../../specific-power.md), but the [dielectric](../../../../../../dielectric.md) breakdown field and modest [capacitance](../../../../../../capacitance.md) limit [specific energy](../../../../../../specific-energy.md). Series resistance limits pulse power and produces heating; a large nominal [capacitance](../../../../../../capacitance.md) does not remove those losses.

A [supercapacitor](../../../../../../supercapacitor.md) has a much larger interfacial area and nanometre-scale charge separation. An [electric double-layer capacitor](../../../../../../electric-double-layer-capacitor.md) stores charge without a bulk [redox](../../../../../../redox.md) transformation; [pseudocapacitance](../../../../../../pseudocapacitance.md) adds sufficiently fast surface or near-surface [redox](../../../../../../redox.md) storage. [ion](../../../../../../ion.md) motion in pores, [electrolyte](../../../../../../electrolyte.md) stability and series resistance constrain its response. Its [specific energy](../../../../../../specific-energy.md) usually exceeds an ordinary [capacitor](../../../../../../capacitor.md)'s while high [specific power](../../../../../../specific-power.md) and cycling durability remain possible.

An [electric battery](../../../../../../electric-battery.md) stores chemical [free energy](../../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) through coupled [electrode](../../../../../../electrode.md) [oxidation-reduction reactions](../../../../../../redox.md). It can store substantial [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) per [mass](../../../../../../mass.md), but charge transfer, ionic transport, [diffusion](../../../../../../diffusion.md) and heat removal constrain rapid discharge. The terminal voltage and accessible capacity depend on discharge rate and state of charge. Accordingly the plotted order follows the storage and transport mechanisms; it is not a claim that every [battery](../../../../../../electric-battery.md) has less power than every [supercapacitor](../../../../../../supercapacitor.md).
