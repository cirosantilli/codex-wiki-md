<h1 id="section-a-iii/7/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [Ragone plot](../../../../../../ragone-plot.md) has [specific energy](../../../../../../specific-energy.md) $e_s=E/m$ on its horizontal logarithmic axis and [specific power](../../../../../../specific-power.md) $p_s=P/m$ on its vertical logarithmic axis, normally in $\mathrm{Wh\,kg^{-1}}$ and $\mathrm{W\,kg^{-1}}$. The comparison must use the same [mass](../../../../../../mass.md) convention: complete devices cannot fairly be compared with electrode-active-material values. At approximately constant delivered power, the discharge time is

$$
t_{\rm h}=\frac{e_s}{p_s},\qquad t_{\rm s}=3600\frac{e_s}{p_s}.
$$

Thus constant-time lines slope upward on the log–log plot. **Ordinary [capacitors](../../../../../../capacitor.md) lie toward high power and low [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md); [batteries](../../../../../../electric-battery.md) toward higher [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) and lower power; [supercapacitors](../../../../../../supercapacitor.md) bridge the two.** Technology and operating limits produce overlapping regions, not universal sharp boxes.
