In a [galvanic cell](../../../../../../galvanic-cell.md), a spontaneous [oxidation-reduction reaction](../../../../../../redox.md) supplies electrical work: $\Delta_rG<0$ for discharge, the [anode](../../../../../../anode.md) is negative, and the [cathode](../../../../../../cathode.md) positive. A discharging [electric battery](../../../../../../electric-battery.md) and a hydrogen–oxygen [fuel cell](../../../../../../fuel-cell.md) are two practical examples. The first stores its reactants internally; the second is supplied continuously with fuel and oxidant.

An [electrolytic cell](../../../../../../electrolytic-cell.md) consumes externally supplied electrical [work](../../../../../../work.md) to drive electrode reactions or material transfer under an applied potential. Its [anode](../../../../../../anode.md) is positive and [cathode](../../../../../../cathode.md) negative. [water electrolysis](../../../../../../water-electrolysis.md) for hydrogen production and metal [electroplating](../../../../../../electroplating.md) are two practical examples. **[oxidation](../../../../../../oxidation.md) always occurs at the [anode](../../../../../../anode.md) and [reduction](../../../../../../reduction-chemistry.md) at the [cathode](../../../../../../cathode.md) in both cell types; the [electrode](../../../../../../electrode.md) signs change.** The [electrolyte](../../../../../../electrolyte.md) conducts [ions](../../../../../../ion.md), while [electrons](../../../../../../electron.md) flow through the external circuit. A rechargeable [battery](../../../../../../electric-battery.md) can operate in either regime depending on whether it is discharging or being charged.
