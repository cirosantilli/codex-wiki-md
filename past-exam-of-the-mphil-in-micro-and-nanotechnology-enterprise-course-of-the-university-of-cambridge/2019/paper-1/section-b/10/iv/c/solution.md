<h1 id="section-b/10/iv/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A $0.10\ \mathrm M$ formula-unit [concentration](../../../../../../../concentration.md) of fully dissociated $\mathrm{AlCl_3}$ gives $0.10\ \mathrm M$ trivalent cations and $0.30\ \mathrm M$ chloride:

$$
I=\frac12[0.10(3)^2+0.30(1)^2]=0.60\ \mathrm M.
$$

At unchanged [temperature](../../../../../../../temperature-split.md) and [permittivity](../../../../../../../permittivity-split.md), the [Debye–Hückel screening length](../../../../../../../debye-huckel-screening-length.md) scales as $I^{-1/2}$, giving **$\kappa^{-1}=0.96/\sqrt6\simeq0.39\ \mathrm{nm}$**. It falls by a factor $\sqrt6$, not by three. This is a formal screening estimate: at this [ionic strength](../../../../../../../ionic-strength.md), trivalent-ion activity, finite [ion](../../../../../../../ion.md) size, hydrolysis and specific [adsorption](../../../../../../../adsorption.md) make the ideal dilute model quantitatively limited.
