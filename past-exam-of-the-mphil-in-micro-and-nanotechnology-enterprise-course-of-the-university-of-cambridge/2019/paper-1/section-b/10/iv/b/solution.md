<h1 id="section-b/10/iv/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For an ideal dilute [electrolyte](../../../../../../../electrolyte.md) with molar [ionic strength](../../../../../../../ionic-strength.md) $I=\tfrac12\sum_i c_i z_i^2$, measured in $\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}$, the [Debye–Hückel screening length](../../../../../../../debye-huckel-screening-length.md) is

$$
\kappa^{-1}=\left(\frac{\varepsilon_r\varepsilon_0RT}{2F^2(1000)I}\right)^{1/2}.
$$

Taking water's relative [permittivity](../../../../../../../permittivity-split.md) as approximately $78.5$ at $25^\circ\mathrm C$, $I=0.10\ \mathrm M$ for NaCl. Therefore **$\kappa^{-1}\simeq0.96\ \mathrm{nm}$**. This is an exponential screening [length](../../../../../../../length.md), not a hard outer edge of the diffuse layer. The factor of $1000$ converts molarity into $\mathrm{mol\,m^{-3}}$.
