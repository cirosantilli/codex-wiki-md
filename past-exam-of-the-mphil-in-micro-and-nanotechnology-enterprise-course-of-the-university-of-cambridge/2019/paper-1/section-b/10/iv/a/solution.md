<h1 id="section-b/10/iv/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The change from a positive surface potential to a negative potential outside the compact layer is consistent with specific phosphate [adsorption](../../../../../../../adsorption.md) that overcompensates the positive oxide charge. The negative compact-layer charge then has a diffuse excess of positive [counterions](../../../../../../../counterion.md). This [charge reversal](../../../../../../../charge-reversal.md) is an interfacial-chemistry interpretation, not something a single monotone double-layer profile with an unchanged positive boundary charge could generate by screening alone. Surface potential and [zeta potential](../../../../../../../zeta-potential.md) at a slip plane are distinct observables.

The [Stern model](../../../../../../../stern-model.md) separates the specifically adsorbed compact layer from a diffuse layer. In a spherical diffuse layer, a [Poisson-Boltzmann equation](../../../../../../../poisson-boltzmann-equation.md) description is

$$
\frac1{r^2}\frac{d}{dr}\!\left(r^2\frac{d\psi}{dr}\right)
=-\frac1\varepsilon\sum_i z_i e n_{i,\infty}
\exp\!\left(-\frac{z_i e\psi}{k_BT}\right).
$$

At sufficiently small $|z_ie\psi|/k_BT$, the [Debye–Hückel approximation](../../../../../../../debye-huckel-approximation.md) gives $\nabla^2\psi=\kappa^2\psi$. If $a_s$ is the outer compact-layer radius, its decaying solution is

$$
\psi(r)=\psi(a_s)\frac{a_s}{r}\exp[-\kappa(r-a_s)].
$$

For a locally flat surface, the geometric factor tends to unity. Thus the far-field negative potential approaches zero from below over the [Debye–Hückel screening length](../../../../../../../debye-huckel-screening-length.md). Neither $+120$ nor $-70\ \mathrm{mV}$ is safely in the low-potential linear regime at room [temperature](../../../../../../../temperature-split.md), so the simple exponential is a far-field approximation, not a globally valid fit. The phosphate layer requires [adsorption](../../../../../../../adsorption.md) chemistry or an equivalent specified boundary model.
