<h1 id="section-b/10/i/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

An [amphiphile](../../../../../../../amphiphile.md) adsorbs at the oil–water interface, reducing the [interfacial tension](../../../../../../../interfacial-tension.md). At fixed drop radius and oil volume, $E_s(c)=A\gamma_{ow}(c)$ therefore falls with increasing [concentration](../../../../../../../concentration.md) and tends toward a plateau once the interface is nearly saturated and further [amphiphile](../../../../../../../amphiphile.md) mostly enters [micelles](../../../../../../../micelle.md). For an approximately ideal dilute nonionic [surfactant](../../../../../../../surfactant.md), the [Gibbs adsorption equation](../../../../../../../gibbs-adsorption-equation.md) $d\gamma=-RT\Gamma\,d\ln a$ connects the fall in [interfacial tension](../../../../../../../interfacial-tension.md) to positive surface excess $\Gamma$. Ionic systems require the appropriate [counterion](../../../../../../../counterion.md) activities.

The [critical micelle concentration](../../../../../../../critical-micelle-concentration.md) gives the familiar break in a tension-versus-log-concentration curve, rather than a fall to zero interfacial [energy](../../../../../../../energy-split.md). If mixing creates a different droplet size distribution as [amphiphile](../../../../../../../amphiphile.md) is added, area also changes; a tension [reduction](../../../../../../../reduction-chemistry.md) alone does not determine the total interfacial [energy](../../../../../../../energy-split.md). The plotted prediction holds area fixed.
