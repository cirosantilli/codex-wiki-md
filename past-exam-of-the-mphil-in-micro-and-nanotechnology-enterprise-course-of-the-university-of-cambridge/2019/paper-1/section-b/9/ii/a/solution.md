<h1 id="section-b/9/ii/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

One workable route uses two resist coatings and a metal-defined optical aperture. The following are eleven fabrication operations; exposure/development is grouped as one lithographic operation, and rear metallization includes its contact activation if required. Detailed cleaning, rinsing and inspection occur between compatible operations.

1. Spin-coat a first [photoresist](../../../../../../../photoresist.md) on the front surface to define a mesa mask.  
2. Expose and develop that mask, retaining resist over the complete active-cell mesa, including its future front contacts.  
3. Etch the exposed [semiconductor](../../../../../../../semiconductor-split.md) through the p-type layers and slightly into the n-type base. This removes the unwanted junction around the cell and produces the lateral mesa step.  
4. Strip the first [photoresist](../../../../../../../photoresist.md), leaving a clean mesa with its cap intact.  
5. Spin-coat a second [photoresist](../../../../../../../photoresist.md) suitable for the [lift-off process](../../../../../../../lift-off-process.md).  
6. Expose and develop openings at the intended front grid or contact pads, positioned safely inside the mesa edge.  
7. Evaporate a p-contact metal stack onto the exposed [gallium arsenide](../../../../../../../gallium-arsenide.md) and resist. Au/Zn-based contact metallization is a conventional candidate, subject to doping and contact-resistance qualification.  
8. Dissolve the second [photoresist](../../../../../../../photoresist.md) to lift off unwanted metal, leaving the front contacts on the cap.  
9. Selectively remove the cap from the light-receiving aperture, using the front metal as a hard mask and the [aluminium gallium arsenide](../../../../../../../aluminium-gallium-arsenide.md) window as the etch stop.  
10. Deposit the rear n-contact metallization, for example a qualified AuGe/Ni/Au system, with an appropriate contact-activation anneal if required and compatible with the front contact.  
11. Deposit an antireflection [dielectric](../../../../../../../dielectric.md) over the optical aperture through a mechanical shadow mask that keeps the contact pads accessible. Its [refractive index](../../../../../../../refractive-index.md) and thickness are chosen for the target optical band.

**The essential material changes are junction isolation at the mesa edge, metal retained on the front cap, cap removed only from the optical aperture, and a conducting rear contact.** The metal systems and anneal are a process design, not compositions specified by the drawing. A workable design must also qualify the passivation of etched sidewalls and the front-contact surface preparation.
