<h1 id="section-b/12/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

A [wireless sensor node](../../../../../../wireless-sensor-node.md) combines a sensor, signal conditioning and conversion, a processor, a radio and a power subsystem. It can use a [battery](../../../../../../electric-battery.md), a wired supply where practical, or local harvesting such as light, vibration or a thermal gradient, usually with a storage [capacitor](../../../../../../capacitor.md), [supercapacitor](../../../../../../supercapacitor.md) or rechargeable [battery](../../../../../../electric-battery.md) to bridge interruptions. Harvested power must meet the time-averaged demand and storage must supply peak radio current.

Reduce power by [duty cycling](../../../../../../duty-cycling.md) the sensor, processor and radio; use a low-leakage sleep mode and switch off unused peripherals. Buffer and batch data, transmit fewer unnecessary bits, use event-triggered acquisition when it meets the application, and reduce retries with a reliable link and an appropriate transmit-power level. Include wake-up, sensing, processing and communication [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) in the budget: reducing payload alone may not reduce a radio dominated by startup overhead.
