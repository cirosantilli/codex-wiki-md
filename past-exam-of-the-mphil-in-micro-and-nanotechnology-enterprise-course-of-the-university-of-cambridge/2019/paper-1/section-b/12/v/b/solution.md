<h1 id="section-b/12/v/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For $D=0.001$, the same [duty cycle](../../../../../../../duty-cycle.md) model gives $\bar I=25\ \mathrm{\mu A}+0.999(6.1\ \mathrm{\mu A})=31.0939\ \mathrm{\mu A}$. Thus **$\bar P=62.1878\ \mathrm{\mu W}$**. The tenfold [reduction](../../../../../../../reduction-chemistry.md) in active fraction reduces average power by only about $8.23$ because the sleep current remains. The ideal limit as $D\to0$ is **$VI_s=12.2\ \mathrm{\mu W}$**, before leakage elsewhere or wake-up [energy](../../../../../../../energy-split.md). Active and sleep contributions become comparable at a [duty cycle](../../../../../../../duty-cycle.md) of roughly $I_s/(I_a+I_s)=0.0244\%$; below that scale lowering sleep current is particularly valuable.
