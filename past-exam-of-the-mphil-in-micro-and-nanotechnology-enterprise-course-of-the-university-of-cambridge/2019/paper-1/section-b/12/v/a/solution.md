<h1 id="section-b/12/v/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [duty cycle](../../../../../../../duty-cycle.md) $D=t_{\rm active}/(t_{\rm active}+t_{\rm sleep})$ is the fraction of time spent active. At a constant supply voltage and with switching [energy](../../../../../../../energy-split.md) neglected,

$$
\bar P=V\,[DI_a+(1-D)I_s].
$$

For $D=0.01$, the average current is $0.01(25\ \mathrm{mA})+0.99(6.1\ \mathrm{\mu A})=256.039\ \mathrm{\mu A}$. Thus **$\bar P=512.078\ \mathrm{\mu W}\simeq0.512\ \mathrm{mW}$**. Multiplying only the active power by the [duty cycle](../../../../../../../duty-cycle.md) would omit the sleep contribution.
