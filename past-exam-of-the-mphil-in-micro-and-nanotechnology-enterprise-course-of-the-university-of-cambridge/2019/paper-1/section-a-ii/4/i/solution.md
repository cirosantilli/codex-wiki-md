<h1 id="section-a-ii/4/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Solve the stated [depth of focus](../../../../../../depth-of-focus.md) relation before substituting into the resolution expression:

$$
NA=\sqrt{\frac{k_2\lambda}{DOF}}
=\sqrt{\frac{0.5(248)}{300}}=0.6429,
\qquad
R=k_1\sqrt{\frac{\lambda\,DOF}{k_2}}
=\boxed{289.3\ \mathrm{nm}}.
$$

This uses the supplied empirical $k_1$ and $k_2$ unchanged and interprets $R$ as the minimum line or space width of an equal-line/space square grating. Under that convention its pitch is **$2R=578.6\ \mathrm{nm}$**. Other pattern shapes or a different definition of the quoted [depth of focus](../../../../../../depth-of-focus.md) would require different process factors.
