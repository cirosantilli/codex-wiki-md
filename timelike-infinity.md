# Timelike infinity

↑ **Parent:** [Penrose diagram](penrose-diagram.md)

Future and past [timelike infinity](timelike-infinity.md) are ideal endpoints of asymptotically escaping timelike trajectories in a [conformal completion](conformal-completion.md). For the [Minkowski conformal compactification](minkowski-conformal-compactification.md), they are the vertices $(T,R)=(\pi,0)$ and $(-\pi,0)$; these vertices are not physical spacetime events.
