# Circular bending energy of an elastic filament

↑ **Parent:** [Worm-like chain](worm-like-chain.md)

A circular loop of [radius](radius.md) $R$ has constant curvature $1/R$ and contour [length](length.md) $2\pi R$. Integrating the quadratic bending [energy](energy-split.md) [density](density.md) $K_b/(2R^2)$ gives the displayed [energy](energy-split.md). With $K_b=k_BTA$ this is $\pi k_BTA/R$. Applied-force work, axial [strain](strain.md), twist and contact energies are separate terms; the loop formula alone does not determine an actual loaded loop's [radius](radius.md).
