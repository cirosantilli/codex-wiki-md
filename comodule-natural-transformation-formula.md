# Comodule natural transformation formula

↑ **Parent:** [Corestriction functor for comodules](corestriction-functor-for-comodules.md)

A [natural transformation](natural-transformation.md) between corestriction functors over a [field](field.md) has components $\beta_M(u)=\sum u_{(0)}\alpha(u_{(1)})$, recovered by $\alpha=\varepsilon\beta_H$. Monoidality forces $\alpha$ to be a unital [algebra homomorphism over a field](algebra-homomorphism-over-a-field.md); for a [Hopf algebra](hopf-algebra.md), $\alpha S$ supplies the inverse.
