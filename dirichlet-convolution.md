# Dirichlet convolution

↑ **Parent:** [Arithmetic function](arithmetic-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dirichlet_convolution)

The Dirichlet convolution of arithmetic functions $f$ and $g$ is

$$
(f*g)(n)=\sum_{d\mid n}f(d)g(n/d).
$$

It is associative and commutative, its identity is the function supported at $1$, and the [Möbius function](mobius-function.md) is the convolution inverse of the constant-one function.
