# Radial density inversion of the Earth

↑ **Parent:** [Geophysics](geophysics-split.md)

Infer a spherically averaged [density](density.md) profile using seismic travel times, normal-mode frequencies, [mass](mass.md), [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md), hydrostatic structure and material constraints. Seismic [speeds](speed.md) alone measure elastic-modulus-to-density ratios, so they do not uniquely supply [density](density.md).
