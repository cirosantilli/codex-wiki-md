# Charge conjugation of fermion bilinears

↑ **Parent:** [Charge conjugation](charge-conjugation.md)

For [normal ordering](normal-ordering.md) of a [fermion bilinear](fermion-bilinear.md), [charge conjugation](charge-conjugation.md) sends $\bar\psi\Gamma\psi$ to $\bar\psi(C^{-1}\Gamma C)^T\psi$. In particular the [Dirac current](dirac-current.md) is odd and the [axial current](axial-current.md) is even under [charge conjugation](charge-conjugation.md).
