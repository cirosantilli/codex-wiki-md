# Upstream open reading frame

↑ **Parent:** [Translation (biology)](translation-biology.md)

A translated reading region upstream of a main coding region in a [messenger RNA](messenger-rna.md). Its effects depend on initiation, termination, scanning and reinitiation, rather than its mere presence.
