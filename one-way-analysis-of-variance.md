# One-way analysis of variance

↑ **Parent:** [Analysis of variance](analysis-of-variance.md)

An [analysis of variance](analysis-of-variance.md) comparing a quantitative [response variable](response-variable.md) across levels of one factor. The ordinary independent-groups [F-test](f-test.md) evaluates equality of group [population means](population-mean.md) under the [one-way normal linear model](one-way-normal-linear-model.md). Rejection says that not all means are equal, rather than identifying every differing pair.
