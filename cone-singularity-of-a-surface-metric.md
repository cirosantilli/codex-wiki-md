# Cone singularity of a surface metric

↑ **Parent:** [Riemannian surface](riemannian-surface.md)

A [surface](topological-surface-split.md) metric has a cone singularity of total [angle](angle.md) $\Theta>0$ if a punctured [neighbourhood](neighbourhood-mathematics.md) has the metric obtained by joining the radial boundaries of a sector of [angle](angle.md) $\Theta$. For constant [Gaussian curvature](gaussian-curvature.md) $-1$ away from the vertex, its polar expression is $dr^2+(\Theta/2\pi)^2\sinh^2r\,d\theta^2$, where $\theta$ has period $2\pi$. A circle of small [radius](radius.md) $r$ has [length](length.md) asymptotic to $\Theta r$. A smooth [Riemannian surface](riemannian-surface.md) instead has circumference asymptotic to $2\pi r$, so the metric extends smoothly at the vertex exactly when $\Theta=2\pi$.
