# Invariant bilinear form as an intertwiner

↑ **Parent:** [Dual representation](dual-representation.md)

A bilinear form $B$ on $V$ is invariant exactly when $v\mapsto B(v,-)$ is a $G$-homomorphism $V\to V^*$. On an irreducible representation, Schur's lemma makes a nonzero such form [nondegenerate](nondegenerate-bilinear-form.md) and unique up to scale.
