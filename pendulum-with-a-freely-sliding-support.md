# Pendulum with a freely sliding support

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrangian mechanics](lagrangian-mechanics-split.md)

For a pendulum whose support slides without horizontal [force](force-split.md), the support coordinate is cyclic and total horizontal [momentum](momentum-split.md) is conserved. At zero horizontal [momentum](momentum-split.md), eliminating support [velocity](velocity.md) leaves the displayed angle-dependent effective inertia. The [energy](energy-split.md) is $M_{\rm eff}\dot\phi^2/2-m_2gl\cos\phi$. Fixing the [momentum](momentum-split.md) before evaluating motion is distinct from fixing the support position.
