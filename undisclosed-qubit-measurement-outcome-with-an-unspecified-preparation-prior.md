# Undisclosed qubit measurement outcome with an unspecified preparation prior

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum measurement](quantum-measurement-split.md)

After a [computational-basis](computational-basis.md) [projective measurement](projective-measurement.md), an observer who lacks the result assigns a diagonal [density matrix](density-matrix-split.md). If the observer's preparation prior has mean $q=\mathbb E|\alpha|^2$, the assignment is the displayed [matrix](matrix.md). No outcome record and no stated prior do not logically force $q=1/2$; that value follows from a symmetric or maximum-entropy ignorance convention. An observer who knows the preparation instead uses $q=|\alpha|^2$.
