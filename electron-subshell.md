# Electron subshell

↑ **Parent:** [Electron shell](electron-shell.md)

An [electron subshell](electron-subshell.md) groups the allowed electronic [quantum states](quantum-state.md) within an [electron shell](electron-shell.md) by their [orbital angular momentum](orbital-angular-momentum.md) quantum number $\ell$. Labels $s$ and $p$ mean $\ell=0$ and $\ell=1$, respectively; their capacities are two and six [Electrons](electron.md). The leading number in $2p$ identifies the [electron shell](electron-shell.md), while $p$ identifies the [electron subshell](electron-subshell.md).
