# Constant-reservoir diffusion-controlled particle growth

↑ **Parent:** [Diffusion-controlled droplet growth](diffusion-controlled-droplet-growth.md)

For an isolated growing [sphere](sphere.md), a quasi-steady exterior [diffusion equation](diffusion-equation-split.md), constant surface [concentration](concentration.md) $c_s$, and a maintained far-field [concentration](concentration.md) $c_\infty$ give $c(r)=c_\infty-(c_\infty-c_s)a/r$. A sharp moving boundary with interior [concentration](concentration.md) $c_p$ then obeys $(c_p-c_s)\dot a=D(c_\infty-c_s)/a$. The displayed integrated law assumes all concentrations and the [diffusion coefficient](diffusion-coefficient.md) are constant. It becomes $a\propto\sqrt t$ when the seed radius is negligible. This reservoir-fed law differs from curvature-controlled [Ostwald ripening](ostwald-ripening.md) in a depleting many-particle system.
