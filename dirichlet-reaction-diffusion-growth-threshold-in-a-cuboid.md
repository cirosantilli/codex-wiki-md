# Dirichlet reaction-diffusion growth threshold in a cuboid

↑ **Parent:** [Reaction–diffusion system](reaction-diffusion-system.md)

For $n_t=\mu\Delta n+\gamma n$ in a rectangular box with zero [Dirichlet boundary conditions](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md), [separation of variables](separation-of-variables.md) gives spatial [eigenfunctions](eigenfunction.md) $\sin(p\pi x/L_x)\sin(q\pi y/L_y)\sin(r\pi z/L_z)$ and temporal rates $\gamma-\mu\pi^2(p^2/L_x^2+q^2/L_y^2+r^2/L_z^2)$. The largest rate is the displayed one. Every nonzero nonnegative initial density has positive projection onto this first [eigenfunction](eigenfunction.md), so its sign determines eventual exponential growth or decay; equality leaves a stationary first mode. At fixed volume, write $a_j=L_j^{-2}$: their product is fixed, and the [arithmetic-geometric mean inequality](arithmetic-geometric-mean-inequality.md) minimizes their sum when all three are equal. Thus the cube maximizes the growth rate among rectangular boxes of fixed volume; making a box sufficiently thin suppresses growth.
