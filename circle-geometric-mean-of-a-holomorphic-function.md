# Circle geometric mean of a holomorphic function

↑ **Parent:** [Circle log-mean of a holomorphic function](circle-log-mean-of-a-holomorphic-function.md)

The continuous angular [geometric mean](geometric-mean.md) of the positive boundary [complex modulus](complex-modulus.md) is

$$
G_f(R)=\exp\left(\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}\log|f(Re^{i\theta})|\,d\theta\right)
=|f(0)|\prod_{j=1}^{N}\frac{R}{|a_j|}.
$$

Here the empty product is one. This differs from the angular [arithmetic mean](arithmetic-mean.md) $A_f(R)=(2\pi)^{-1}\int_0^{2\pi}|f(Re^{i\theta})|\,d\theta$. In general $G_f(R)\le A_f(R)$ by the [concavity](concave-function.md) of the [logarithm](logarithm.md); equality requires constant boundary [complex modulus](complex-modulus.md). For a [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function-split.md) with no [zeros of a function](zero-of-a-function.md) in the closed [disk](disk-mathematics.md), [Jensen's formula](jensen-s-formula.md) gives $G_f(R)=|f(0)|$, even when the boundary [complex modulus](complex-modulus.md) varies with angle. Thus replacing the mean of a [logarithm](logarithm.md) by the [logarithm](logarithm.md) of an [arithmetic mean](arithmetic-mean.md) loses the exact zero identity.
