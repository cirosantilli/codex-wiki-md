# Faddeev-Popov determinant

↑ **Parent:** [Gauge fixing](gauge-fixing.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faddeev-Popov_determinant)

The Faddeev-Popov determinant is the functional Jacobian

$$
\Delta_{\mathrm{FP}}[A]=\det\left(\frac{\delta G(A^\alpha)}{\delta\alpha}\right)_{G=0}
$$

that compensates for the change from integration along a gauge orbit to a gauge-fixing condition $G(A)=0$. It can be represented by a path integral over a [Faddeev-Popov ghost field](faddeev-popov-ghost.md) pair.
