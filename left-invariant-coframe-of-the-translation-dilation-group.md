# Left-invariant coframe of the translation-dilation group

↑ **Parent:** [Translation-dilation group of the plane](translation-dilation-group-of-the-plane.md)

The [Maurer-Cartan form](maurer-cartan-form.md) of the [translation-dilation group of the plane](translation-dilation-group-of-the-plane.md) has these coefficients in its dilation–translation basis, with $a=1,2$. Left multiplication rescales each coordinate differential and $\rho$ equally, proving they are [left-invariant differential forms](left-invariant-differential-form.md). Their dual [left-invariant vector fields](left-invariant-vector-field.md) are $\rho\partial_\rho$ and $\rho\partial_{x^a}$. The [Maurer-Cartan equation](maurer-cartan-equation.md) is $d\lambda^0=0$, $d\lambda^a=-\lambda^0\wedge\lambda^a$. The tensor $\sum_j\lambda^j\otimes\lambda^j$ is a positive definite [left-invariant metric](left-invariant-metric.md).
