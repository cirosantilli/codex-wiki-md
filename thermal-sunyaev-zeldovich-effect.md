# Thermal Sunyaev-Zeldovich effect

↑ **Parent:** [Sunyaev-Zeldovich effect](sunyaev-zeldovich-effect.md)

For a [Planck photon distribution](planck-photon-distribution.md), the hot-electron [Kompaneets equation](kompaneets-equation.md) gives $\Delta n=yx e^x(e^x-1)^{-2}[x\coth(x/2)-4]$, where $x=h\nu/(k_BT_\gamma)$. The equivalent thermodynamic [temperature](temperature.md) perturbation has the displayed form, while the [Rayleigh-Jeans law](rayleigh-jeans-law.md) gives $\Delta T/T=-2y$. The distortion changes sign at $x\simeq3.830$. This first-order expression assumes a small [Compton y parameter](compton-y-parameter.md) and nonrelativistic thermal [Electrons](electron.md).
