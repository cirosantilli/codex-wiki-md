# Von Mangoldt divisor identity

↑ **Parent:** [Von Mangoldt function](von-mangoldt-function.md)

For every [positive integer](positive-integer.md) $n$,

$$
\sum_{d\mid n}\Lambda(d)=\log n.
$$

Equivalently, the constant-one function convolved with the [Von Mangoldt function](von-mangoldt-function.md) is the natural logarithm.
