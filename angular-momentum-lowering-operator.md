# Angular momentum lowering operator

↑ **Parent:** [Angular momentum operator](angular-momentum-operator-split.md)

The operator $J_-=J_x-iJ_y$ acts by $J_-|j,m\rangle=\hbar\sqrt{(j+m)(j-m+1)}|j,m-1\rangle$. It generates lower magnetic states and determines [Clebsch-Gordan coefficients](clebsch-gordan-coefficients.md).
