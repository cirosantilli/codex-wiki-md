<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

[Magnetic flux freezing](../../../../magnetic-flux-freezing.md) ties a transverse fluid displacement to a bend in the [magnetic field lines](../../../../magnetic-field-line.md). For the uniform background, the transverse displacement equation is

$$
\rho_0\partial_t^2\boldsymbol\xi_\perp=\mathcal T_0\partial_z^2\boldsymbol\xi_\perp.
$$

For a sinusoidal bend, $\partial_z^2\boldsymbol\xi_\perp=-k_\parallel^2\boldsymbol\xi_\perp$. Positive [pressure-anisotropy-modified magnetic tension](../../../../pressure-anisotropy-modified-magnetic-tension.md) therefore gives a restoring [force](../../../../force-split.md), and [inertia](../../../../inertia.md) produces oscillation. In an isotropic [plasma](../../../../plasma-physics.md), this is precisely the positive [magnetic tension](../../../../magnetic-tension.md) $B_0^2/(4\pi)$.

Particles moving predominantly along bent [magnetic field lines](../../../../magnetic-field-line.md) contribute a transverse [momentum flux](../../../../momentum-flux.md). Their larger parallel [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md) opposes the restoring curvature [force](../../../../force-split.md) supplied by the [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) and perpendicular [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md). Once $p_{\parallel0}-p_{\perp0}>B_0^2/(4\pi)$, the net [force](../../../../force-split.md) points along the original displacement: a larger bend produces a larger outward [force](../../../../force-split.md), giving positive feedback. Equivalently, the bending contribution to the quadratic [potential energy](../../../../potential-energy.md) is $\frac12\mathcal T_0\lvert\partial_z\boldsymbol\xi_\perp\rvert^2$ and has become negative.

In this static, homogeneous fluid model the unstable roots are

$$
\boxed{\omega=\pm i\gamma,\qquad \gamma=|k_\parallel|\sqrt{\frac{p_{\parallel0}-p_{\perp0}-B_0^2/(4\pi)}{\rho_0}}.}
$$

With $e^{-i\omega t}$ the root $+i\gamma$ grows. Its real [angular frequency](../../../../angular-frequency.md) is zero, so the unstable pattern grows without propagation in the equilibrium rest frame; its partner decays. Advection by a background flow would change that frame-dependent statement. The growth increasing without bound as $|k_\parallel|$ increases is a limitation of this long-[wavelength](../../../../wavelength.md) model: finite [gyroradius](../../../../gyroradius.md) and other kinetic corrections cannot be neglected at sufficiently short scales.
