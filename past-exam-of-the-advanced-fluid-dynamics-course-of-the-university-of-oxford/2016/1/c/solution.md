<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For $k_\parallel\ne0$ the [Alfvén wave](../../../../alfven-wave.md) becomes a [firehose instability](../../../../firehose-instability.md) when its squared [angular frequency](../../../../angular-frequency.md) is negative:

$$
\boxed{p_{\parallel0}-p_{\perp0}>\frac{B_0^2}{4\pi},\qquad \frac{p_{\perp0}-p_{\parallel0}}{p_{\parallel0}}<-\frac{2}{\beta},\qquad \beta=\frac{8\pi p_{\parallel0}}{B_0^2}.}
$$

Equality is marginal, rather than exponential growth. The [pressure anisotropy](../../../../pressure-anisotropy.md) must have negative sign; its absolute magnitude alone is insufficient.

For the intergalactic [plasma](../../../../plasma-physics.md), a negative relative [pressure anisotropy](../../../../pressure-anisotropy.md) of $-10^{-2}$ reaches this fluid threshold only for $\beta>200$. In particular, the literal pair $\beta=100$ and relative [pressure anisotropy](../../../../pressure-anisotropy.md) $-0.01$ is stable because the threshold is $-0.02$. Since the quoted numbers are approximate and allow larger [plasma beta](../../../../plasma-beta.md), this weakly magnetized [plasma](../../../../plasma-physics.md) is susceptible, rather than necessarily unstable at every quoted value. For example, $\beta=300$ and relative [pressure anisotropy](../../../../pressure-anisotropy.md) $-0.01$ are unstable. Positive [pressure anisotropy](../../../../pressure-anisotropy.md) does not excite this branch.

For the fusion-device [plasma](../../../../plasma-physics.md), $\beta\sim10^{-2}$ requires a negative relative [pressure anisotropy](../../../../pressure-anisotropy.md) below roughly $-200$, whereas its magnitude is only about $0.1$. The stabilizing [magnetic tension](../../../../magnetic-tension.md) dominates, so it is not susceptible to this fluid [firehose instability](../../../../firehose-instability.md) under the stated conditions. Indeed, nonnegative perpendicular [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md) requires the relative [pressure anisotropy](../../../../pressure-anisotropy.md) to be no smaller than $-1$; at such a small [plasma beta](../../../../plasma-beta.md) even this extreme cannot satisfy the threshold.
