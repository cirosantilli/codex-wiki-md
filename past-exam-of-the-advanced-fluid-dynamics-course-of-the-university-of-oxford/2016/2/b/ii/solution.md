<h1 id="2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Linearize about a relaxed state $\mathsf T=0$ with constant [mass density](../../../../../density.md) $\rho_0$. In the unforced [momentum conservation](../../../../../momentum-conservation.md) equation $\rho_0D_t\mathbf u=-\nabla p+\nabla\cdot\mathsf T$, the $x$ component of the [pressure gradient](../../../../../pressure-gradient.md) is zero for a freely evolving [unidirectional flow](../../../../../unidirectional-flow.md) independent of $x$. A prescribed external [pressure gradient](../../../../../pressure-gradient.md) would add a forcing term.

For $\mathbf u=u(y,t)\widehat{\mathbf x}$, [incompressibility](../../../../../incompressible-flow.md) is automatic and the only nonzero entries of the [rate-of-strain tensor](../../../../../strain-rate-tensor.md) are $E_{xy}=E_{yx}=\frac12\partial_yu$. Products of [stress](../../../../../stress.md) and [velocity gradient](../../../../../velocity-gradient.md) are quadratic in the small amplitude. The [constitutive equation](../../../../../constitutive-equation.md) consequently becomes $\mathsf T+\tau\partial_t\mathsf T=2\mu\mathsf E$ to first order. With $T=T_{xy}=T_{yx}$, its shear component and the $x$ component of [momentum conservation](../../../../../momentum-conservation.md) are

$$
\boxed{\rho_0\partial_tu=\partial_yT,\qquad T+\tau\partial_tT=\mu\partial_yu.}
$$

The diagonal [stress](../../../../../stress.md) components have only homogeneous relaxation at this order and vanish if initially relaxed. In particular, $\alpha$ does not enter this linear response.
