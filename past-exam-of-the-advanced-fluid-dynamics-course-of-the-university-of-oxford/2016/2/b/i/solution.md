<h1 id="2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

When deformation is stopped, the [rate-of-strain tensor](../../../../../strain-rate-tensor.md) and [velocity gradient](../../../../../velocity-gradient.md) vanish, leaving $\mathsf T+\tau\partial_t\mathsf T=0$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\mathsf T(t)=\mathsf T(0)e^{-t/\tau}.}
$$

The positive constant $\tau$ is the [viscoelastic relaxation time](../../../../../viscoelastic-relaxation-time.md). For slow weak deformation, all the transport and quadratic terms are smaller than $\mathsf T$, so $\mathsf T\simeq2\mu\mathsf E$. Thus $\mu>0$ is the zero-rate [dynamic viscosity](../../../../../dynamic-viscosity-split.md), measured in [stress](../../../../../stress.md) times time. The corresponding short-time [shear modulus](../../../../../shear-modulus.md) of the [Maxwell fluid](../../../../../linear-maxwell-fluid.md) response is $G_{\rm el}=\mu/\tau$. The source's $\alpha$ has units of time, since $\alpha\mathsf T\mathsf E$ must have units of [stress](../../../../../stress.md).
