<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Linearized [magnetic flux freezing](../../../../magnetic-flux-freezing.md) gives the Eulerian [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) perturbation

$$
\delta\mathbf B=\nabla\times(\boldsymbol\xi\times\mathbf B_0)=(\mathbf B_0\cdot\nabla)\boldsymbol\xi-\mathbf B_0\nabla\cdot\boldsymbol\xi.
$$

The background is uniform, and [incompressibility](../../../../incompressible-flow.md) gives $\nabla\cdot\boldsymbol\xi=0$. For a horizontal displacement proportional to $e^{ikz}$, $\delta\mathbf B=ikB_0\boldsymbol\xi$ and $\delta B_z=0$. Thus the first-order [magnetic pressure](../../../../magnetic-pressure.md) perturbation vanishes, and the [magnetic-tension response to an incompressible displacement](../../../../magnetic-tension-response-to-an-incompressible-displacement.md) is

$$
\boxed{\delta\mathbf f=\frac{B_0}{\mu_0}\partial_z\delta\mathbf B=-\frac{k^2B_0^2}{\mu_0}\boldsymbol\xi.}
$$

Bending a field line produces a restoring [force](../../../../force-split.md) proportional to displacement, like a spring obeying [Hooke's law](../../../../hooke-s-law.md). Per unit [mass](../../../../mass.md), its squared spring [frequency](../../../../frequency.md) is $k^2v_A^2$, where $v_A=B_0/\sqrt{\mu_0\rho}$ is the [Alfvén speed](../../../../alfven-speed.md). The equilibrium can be independent of $z$ while the perturbation varies with $z$; neglect of background vertical structure does not set this perturbation's [wavenumber](../../../../wavenumber.md) to zero.
