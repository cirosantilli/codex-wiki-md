<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In an inertial frame, a [circular orbit](../../../../circular-orbit.md) at radius $R$ has radial [acceleration](../../../../acceleration.md) $-R\Omega(R)^2\mathbf e_R$. Neglecting fluid and magnetic forces gives

$$
-\rho R\Omega(R)^2=-\rho\frac{d\Phi}{dR},\qquad\boxed{\frac{d\Phi}{dR}=R\Omega(R)^2.}
$$

In a local rotating frame with $\Omega_0=\Omega(R_0)$, the fluid is instantaneously at rest at the reference radius $R_0$. The radial [centrifugal force](../../../../centrifugal-force.md) $+\rho R_0\Omega_0^2$ then balances the inward [gravitational force](../../../../gravitational-force.md). Away from $R_0$, the fluid's nonzero rotating-frame [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) must also be retained; the entire differentially rotating disk is not at rest in that one frame.
