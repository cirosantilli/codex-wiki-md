<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

In [ideal magnetohydrodynamics](../../../../ideal-magnetohydrodynamics-split.md), the first-order [electric field](../../../../electric-field-split.md) is $\mathbf E_1=-\mathbf v\times B_0\mathbf e_z$. Retaining its product with both the background and first-order [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) gives the [Poynting vector](../../../../poynting-vector.md)

$$
\mathbf S=\frac1{\mu_0}\mathbf E_1\times(B_0\mathbf e_z+\mathbf b)
=\frac{B_0^2}{\mu_0}\mathbf v-\frac{B_0}{\mu_0}(\mathbf v\cdot\mathbf b)\mathbf e_z.
$$

The first term is transverse and oscillatory, with zero period average. The second is quadratic and transports wave [energy](../../../../energy-split.md). A potential quadratic ideal electric-field term $-\mathbf v\times\mathbf b$ vanishes here because the real traveling-wave fields are parallel or antiparallel.

For the $+z$ branch, $\mathbf b=-\sqrt{\mu_0\rho_0}\mathbf v$, so

$$
\boxed{\langle\mathbf S\rangle=\rho_0v_A\langle|\mathbf v|^2\rangle\mathbf e_z=v_A\langle\mathcal E\rangle\mathbf e_z.}
$$

For the opposite branch, its sign reverses. The averaged [Poynting flux](../../../../poynting-flux.md) is the total wave [energy density](../../../../energy-density.md), including equal kinetic and magnetic shares, multiplied by the [group velocity](../../../../group-velocity.md). This agrees with [Alfvén wave energy transport](../../../../alfven-wave-energy-transport.md). Although the flux in this expression is electromagnetic, it supplies the transport of both components of the coupled fluid-field wave.
