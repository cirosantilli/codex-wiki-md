<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Subtract the constant equilibrium [magnetic energy](../../../../magnetic-energy.md) density. Since $\mathbf B_0\cdot\mathbf b=0$, the perturbation densities are

$$
\mathcal E_K=\frac12\rho_0|\mathbf v|^2,\qquad
\mathcal E_B=\frac{|\mathbf b|^2}{2\mu_0}.
$$

The traveling-wave [amplitude](../../../../wave-amplitude.md) relation from part [a](../a.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\mathcal E_B=\mathcal E_K,\qquad\mathcal E=\mathcal E_K+\mathcal E_B=\rho_0|\mathbf v|^2=\frac{|\mathbf b|^2}{\mu_0}.}
$$

This is pointwise [Alfvén wave energy transport](../../../../alfven-wave-energy-transport.md) for one traveling wave, not a claim that every superposition or [standing wave](../../../../standing-wave.md) has pointwise equipartition.

For the real fields written as $\mathbf v=\operatorname{Re}(\widehat{\mathbf v}e^{i(kz-\omega t)})$ and similarly for $\mathbf b$, the period averages are

$$
\langle\mathcal E_K\rangle=\frac{\rho_0}{4}|\widehat{\mathbf v}|^2,\qquad
\langle\mathcal E_B\rangle=\frac1{4\mu_0}|\widehat{\mathbf b}|^2,\qquad
\boxed{\langle\mathcal E\rangle=\frac{\rho_0}{2}|\widehat{\mathbf v}|^2.}
$$

The factors one half distinguish a sinusoid's peak [amplitude](../../../../wave-amplitude.md) from its mean squared value; the same complex-vector convention also covers circular [polarization of waves](../../../../polarization-waves.md).
