<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

At the [terminal velocity](../../../../terminal-velocity.md), buoyancy-corrected weight balances the [Stokes drag law](../../../../stokes-s-law.md):

$$
\frac{4\pi a^3}{3}(\widetilde\rho-\rho)\mathbf g-6\pi\mu a\mathbf U=0.
$$

Hence

$$
\boxed{\mathbf U=\frac{2a^2(\widetilde\rho-\rho)}{9\mu}\mathbf g,\qquad
|\mathbf U|=\frac{2a^2|\widetilde\rho-\rho|}{9\mu}|\mathbf g|.}
$$

A denser sphere falls in the direction of $\mathbf g$; a lighter one rises. Equal densities give no sedimentation. The calculation assumes negligible fluid [Reynolds number](../../../../reynolds-number.md), an unbounded fluid, and negligible transient body [acceleration](../../../../acceleration.md) after terminal settling. The fluid [mass density](../../../../density.md) enters through [buoyancy](../../../../buoyancy.md) and through the small-[Reynolds number](../../../../reynolds-number.md) condition.
