<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Take the [trace](../../../../matrix-trace.md) of the statistically isotropic [magnetic second-moment tensor](../../../../magnetic-second-moment-tensor.md). There are three spatial directions, so

$$
\langle B^2\rangle
=\langle B_iB_i\rangle
=v_A^2\delta_{ii}=3v_A^2.
$$

Choosing the nonnegative [speed](../../../../speed.md),

$$
\boxed{v_A=\sqrt{\frac{\langle B^2\rangle}{3}}
=\sqrt{\frac{\langle B_{\rm phys}^2\rangle}{3\mu_0\rho_0}}.}
$$

Thus $v_A$ is the root-mean-square value of any one Cartesian [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) component in [Alfvén velocity](../../../../alfven-velocity.md) units. It is not the magnitude of the mean [Alfvén velocity](../../../../alfven-velocity.md), which is zero here. [Statistical isotropy](../../../../statistical-isotropy.md) fixes a second moment even when the first moment vanishes.
