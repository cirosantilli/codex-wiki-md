<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

With zero [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) and no time dependence, the [magnetic second-moment tensor](../../../../magnetic-second-moment-tensor.md) equation holds automatically, but the [magnetohydrodynamic momentum equation](../../../../magnetohydrodynamic-momentum-equation.md) still requires

$$
\boxed{\partial_jM_{ij}^{(0)}=\partial_i p_0.}
$$

The [magnetic tension](../../../../magnetic-tension.md) must therefore be a [pressure gradient](../../../../pressure-gradient.md). Locally, its necessary integrability condition is

$$
\nabla\times\bigl(\nabla\cdot M^{(0)}\bigr)=0;
$$

on a simply connected region this is also sufficient for a scalar $p_0$, subject to the global [boundary conditions](../../../../boundary-condition.md). In physical units the same balance is $\nabla P_0=\mathbf J_0\times\mathbf B_{{\rm phys},0}$: the [Lorentz force density](../../../../lorentz-force-density.md) is balanced by mechanical [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md), rather than necessarily vanishing. A zero mean [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) does not make the local [magnetic tension](../../../../magnetic-tension.md) zero, so an arbitrary tangle is not automatically a static [equilibrium](../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md).
