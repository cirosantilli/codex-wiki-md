# Diffusion limit

↑ **Parent:** [Markov diffusion](markov-diffusion.md)

A diffusion limit rescales a sequence of stochastic processes so that its limit is a [diffusion process](markov-diffusion.md). For suitably rescaled independent small jumps, the first two jump moments determine the limiting drift and diffusion coefficient. [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) arises when the limiting drift is zero and the variance grows linearly in time.
