# Nuclear fuel

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear materials](nuclear-materials.md)

A material that supplies energy through a [nuclear reaction](nuclear-reaction.md), usually [nuclear fission](nuclear-fission.md) in a power [nuclear reactor](nuclear-reactor.md). Its usable performance depends on its thermal and mechanical response as well as its nuclear composition.
