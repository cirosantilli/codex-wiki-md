# Linear growth bound for integrable observables

↑ **Parent:** [Birkhoff ergodic theorem](birkhoff-ergodic-theorem.md)

In a probability [measure-preserving system](measure-preserving-system.md), $f\in L^1$ implies $f(T^nx)/n\to0$ [almost everywhere](almost-everywhere.md). For every $\varepsilon>0$, the [tail integral formula for moments](tail-integral-formula-for-moments.md) gives $\sum_{n\geq1}\mu(|f|>\varepsilon n)\leq\|f\|_1/\varepsilon$. Invariance and the [Borel-Cantelli lemma](borel-cantelli-lemmas.md) finish the proof. Consequently division by $n^a$ also gives zero for every $a\geq1$.
