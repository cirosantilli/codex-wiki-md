# Associativity of ordinal addition

↑ **Parent:** [Ordinal addition](ordinal-addition.md)

For all ordinals $\alpha,\beta,\gamma$,

$$
(\alpha+\beta)+\gamma=\alpha+(\beta+\gamma).
$$

This follows by [transfinite induction](transfinite-induction.md) on $\gamma$ from the recursive definition of [ordinal addition](ordinal-addition.md).
