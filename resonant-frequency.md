# Resonant frequency

↑ **Parent:** [Resonance](resonance.md)

A [frequency](frequency.md) at which a specified driven response is resonant. The value can depend on the observable and damping: the [electric current](electric-current.md) amplitude of a driven [series RLC circuit](series-rlc-circuit.md) peaks at $f_0=1/(2\pi\sqrt{LC})$, while a different circuit readout need not peak at the same [frequency](frequency.md).
