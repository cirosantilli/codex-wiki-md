# Covalent catalysis

↑ **Parent:** [Catalysis](catalysis.md)

[Covalent catalysis](covalent-catalysis.md) creates a temporary covalent bond between catalyst and [substrate](substrate-biochemistry.md), then breaks that bond while regenerating the catalyst. Merely forming a [substrate](substrate-biochemistry.md)–[water](water.md) bond does not constitute [covalent catalysis](covalent-catalysis.md) by an [enzyme](enzyme-split.md) residue.
