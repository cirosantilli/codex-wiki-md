# Daubechies wavelet

↑ **Parent:** [Orthonormal wavelet](orthonormal-wavelet.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daubechies_wavelet)

A Daubechies wavelet of order $q$ is a [compact support](compact-support.md) [orthonormal wavelet](orthonormal-wavelet.md) with $q$ [vanishing moments](vanishing-moment.md) and a minimal-length finite refinement filter. The minimal filter has $2q$ taps, giving support length $2q-1$ in the standard dyadic normalization. Its low-pass symbol has a zero of order $q$ at $\pi$. Increasing order improves polynomial cancellation and, for sufficiently large order, regularity, at the price of a wider support.
