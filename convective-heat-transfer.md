# Convective heat transfer

↑ **Parent:** [Heat transfer](heat-transfer.md)

Energy transport by bulk fluid motion together with local [thermal conduction](thermal-conduction.md). A prescribed [adiabatic process](adiabatic-process.md) profile constrains [temperature](temperature-split.md) but does not require zero convergence of this energy transport. A [cloud](cloud.md) held at a specified [temperature](temperature-split.md) can therefore have nonzero net radiative heating balanced by [convection](convection-split.md).
