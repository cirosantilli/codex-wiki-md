# Focusing nonlinear Schrodinger modulation dispersion

↑ **Parent:** [Modulational instability](modulational-instability.md)

For $-iA_t+\chi A_{xx}-\mu A+\delta A|A|^2=0$ with positive coefficients, linearize around $A=\sqrt{\mu/\delta}$ using real amplitude and phase perturbations. Their two-by-two [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation.md) gives the displayed equation. The [modulational instability](modulational-instability.md) band is $0<K^2<2\mu/\chi$, with maximum [growth rate](growth-rate.md) $\mu$ at $K^2=\mu/\chi$.
