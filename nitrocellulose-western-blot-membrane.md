# Nitrocellulose Western blot membrane

↑ **Parent:** [Western blot membrane](western-blot-membrane.md)

A [nitrocellulose Western blot membrane](nitrocellulose-western-blot-membrane.md) is a [Western blot membrane](western-blot-membrane.md) made from nitrated [cellulose](cellulose.md). Its retention capacity is finite; the original [Western blot transfer](western-blot-transfer.md) experiment tested breakthrough with a second receiving sheet.
