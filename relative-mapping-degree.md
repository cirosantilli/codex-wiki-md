# Relative mapping degree

↑ **Parent:** [Degree of a continuous mapping](degree-of-a-continuous-mapping.md)

For a map of oriented disk pairs, the multiplier on top [relative homology](relative-homology.md) is its relative [mapping degree](degree-of-a-continuous-mapping.md). Naturality of the [connecting homomorphism](connecting-homomorphism.md) identifies it with the [mapping degree](degree-of-a-continuous-mapping.md) of the boundary map.
