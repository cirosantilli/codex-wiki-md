# Null current of a commuting Majorana spinor

↑ **Parent:** [Causal Dirac spinor current](causal-dirac-spinor-current.md)

In four dimensions a single commuting [Majorana spinor](majorana-spinor.md) is specified by a two-component complex spinor $\xi$. Its future [Dirac current](dirac-current.md) is proportional to $(\xi^\dagger\xi,\xi^\dagger\boldsymbol\sigma\xi)$, and the [Pauli matrix multiplication law](pauli-matrix-multiplication-law.md) implies $|\xi^\dagger\boldsymbol\sigma\xi|^2=(\xi^\dagger\xi)^2$. Thus it is null. A Dirac spinor or a sum of independent Majorana currents can be timelike. This distinction matters when applying a timelike-current classification to minimal real supersymmetry.
