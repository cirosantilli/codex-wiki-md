# Teacher expectation

↑ **Parent:** [Educational psychology](educational-psychology.md)

A teacher's prediction or assumption about a learner's performance or potential. To establish a self-fulfilling effect, research must show how expectations change treatment or opportunity and subsequent [learning](learning.md), rather than infer causation from a correlation.
