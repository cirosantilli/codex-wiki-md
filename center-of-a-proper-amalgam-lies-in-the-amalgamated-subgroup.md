# Center of a proper amalgam lies in the amalgamated subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Normal form theorem for an amalgamated free product](normal-form-theorem-for-an-amalgamated-free-product.md)

If C is proper in both factors of $G=G_1*_CG_2$, every central element lies in C. In the [Bass-Serre tree](bass-serre-tree.md), a central element fixing a vertex fixes its entire orbit and then the whole tree, hence an edge. A central element acting as a translation would have an invariant axis; minimality forces the tree to be that axis, but a vertex stabilizer then contains a reflection, contradicting centrality of the translation. Thus the center fixes an edge and is contained in its stabilizer C.
