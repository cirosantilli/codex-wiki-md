# Chloroplast

↑ **Parent:** [Biological cell](biological-cell-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chloroplast)

A [chloroplast](chloroplast.md) is a photosynthetic [plastid](plastid.md) in [plant](plant.md) and [algae](algae.md) [biological cells](biological-cell-split.md). The green-plant [chloroplast](chloroplast.md) modeled here has a [chloroplast envelope](chloroplast-envelope.md) surrounding [chloroplast stroma](chloroplast-stroma.md) and internal [thylakoids](thylakoid.md). [Light-dependent reactions](light-dependent-reactions.md) occur at the [thylakoid membrane](thylakoid-membrane.md); the [Calvin cycle](calvin-cycle.md) operates in the [chloroplast stroma](chloroplast-stroma.md).[Biology 2e, 8.1, Basic Photosynthetic Structures and The Two Parts of Photosynthesis](biology-2e-by-mary-ann-clark-and-others/8-1-overview-of-photosynthesis.md)

[Cyanobacteria](cyanobacteria.md) perform [oxygenic photosynthesis](oxygenic-photosynthesis.md) without [chloroplasts](chloroplast.md): the [prokaryotic](prokaryote.md) [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md) itself contains the photosynthetic machinery. Thus [chloroplast](chloroplast.md) identifies an [organelle](organelle.md), while [oxygenic photosynthesis](oxygenic-photosynthesis.md) identifies a process. [Endosymbiotic theory](endosymbiotic-theory.md) explains their historical connection; [organelle protein import](organelle-protein-import.md) explains how many nuclear-encoded [proteins](protein-split.md) reach the modern [organelle](organelle.md).

**[Figure 15](#image-chloroplast-compartments-and-boundary-counting). Chloroplast compartments and boundary counting**. Original simplified section and compartment-access model, not a micrograph or scale drawing. The enlargement separates [thylakoid lumen](thylakoid-lumen.md) from the [chloroplast stroma](chloroplast-stroma.md) between stacked sacs. The route counts crossings of membrane barriers, not molecular leaflets; actual permeability and transport mechanisms are omitted.

The useful feature is selective separation: [enzymes](enzyme-split.md) and [substrates](substrate-biochemistry.md) can share an [organelle](organelle.md) without sharing a compartment. For a constructed accessibility example, put a soluble [enzyme](enzyme-split.md) in [chloroplast stroma](chloroplast-stroma.md) and its [substrate](substrate-biochemistry.md) in [thylakoid lumen](thylakoid-lumen.md). Sharing the same [chloroplast](chloroplast.md) does not permit direct contact; contact requires a transport or boundary-disruption step. Placing the [substrate](substrate-biochemistry.md) between adjacent [thylakoids](thylakoid.md) instead puts it on the stromal side. The visual stack alone cannot establish chemical accessibility.

For the simplest boundary-counting model, represent surrounding [cytosol](cytosol.md), [chloroplast intermembrane space](chloroplast-intermembrane-space.md), [chloroplast stroma](chloroplast-stroma.md) and [thylakoid lumen](thylakoid-lumen.md) by four [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) in a chain. Its three [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) represent the outer envelope, inner envelope and [thylakoid membrane](thylakoid-membrane.md), respectively. A route from [cytosol](cytosol.md) to [chloroplast stroma](chloroplast-stroma.md) crosses two barriers; extending it into [thylakoid lumen](thylakoid-lumen.md) adds a third. This follows by listing the [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) along the chain. Counting the two leaflets of each [lipid bilayer](lipid-bilayer.md) separately would answer a different question. These counts describe this idealized geometry, not the number of [protein targeting](protein-targeting.md) signals or the transport itinerary of every [protein](protein-split.md).

The [light-dependent reactions](light-dependent-reactions.md) article develops [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md), [NADPH](nadph.md) and [electrochemical gradient](electrochemical-gradient.md) coupling, and the [Calvin cycle](calvin-cycle.md) article develops [carbon fixation](carbon-fixation.md) bookkeeping. Their different locations make the [chloroplast](chloroplast.md) interesting as an organized chemical workspace. Light-independent [chemical reactions](chemical-reaction-split.md) still depend on supplied carriers and regulation; the name does not prescribe nighttime operation.
