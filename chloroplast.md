# Chloroplast

↑ **Parent:** [Biological cell](biological-cell.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chloroplast)

A [chloroplast](chloroplast.md) is a compartment in photosynthetic [plant](plant.md) and [algae](algae.md) [biological cells](biological-cell.md). Its internal [thylakoids](thylakoid.md) carry out [light-dependent reactions](light-dependent-reactions.md); its surrounding [chloroplast stroma](chloroplast-stroma.md) contains the [enzymes](enzyme.md) of the [Calvin cycle](calvin-cycle.md). [Cyanobacteria](cyanobacteria.md) perform [oxygenic photosynthesis](oxygenic-photosynthesis.md) without [chloroplasts](chloroplast.md). See [Alberts et al., Molecular Biology of the Cell: chloroplasts and photosynthesis](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK26819/).
