# Common power residues from coprime exponent congruences

↑ **Parent:** [Chinese remainder theorem](chinese-remainder-theorem.md)

Choose a [primitive root](primitive-root-modulo-n.md) $g$ modulo $p$ and write the nonzero coefficients $3,5,7$ as $g^a,g^b,g^c$. The [Chinese remainder theorem](chinese-remainder-theorem.md) gives an exponent $r$ congruent to $a,b,c$ modulo $3,5,7$, respectively. Then $n=g^r$ and the three roots $g^{(r-a)/3}$, $g^{(r-b)/5}$, $g^{(r-c)/7}$ give the displayed common nonzero residue. This technique converts multiplicative power constraints into simultaneous additive [modular congruences](modular-congruence.md) for exponents.
