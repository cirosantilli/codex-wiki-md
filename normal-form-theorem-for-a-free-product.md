# Normal form theorem for a free product

↑ **Parent:** [Normal form for free groups and free product of groups](normal-form-for-free-groups-and-free-product-of-groups.md)

Every element of a [free product](free-product.md) $G*H$ has a unique expression $u_1\cdots u_n$ in which each $u_i$ is a nonidentity element of one factor and adjacent syllables belong to different factors. The identity corresponds to the empty word.
