# Fundamental forms of an elliptic ring torus

↑ **Parent:** [Second fundamental form](second-fundamental-form-split.md)

Rotate an ellipse with positive semiaxes $a,b$ about an axis a distance $r>a$ from its centre. For the [parametrized surface](parametrized-surface.md) $\sigma(u,v)=((r+a\sin u)\cos v,(r+a\sin u)\sin v,b\cos u)$, put $R=r+a\sin u$ and $D^2=a^2\cos^2u+b^2\sin^2u$. An outward [unit normal](unit-normal.md) is $N=(b\sin u\cos v,b\sin u\sin v,a\cos u)/D$. Direct [differentiation](differentiation.md) gives the [first fundamental form](first-fundamental-form.md) and [second fundamental form](second-fundamental-form-split.md), with the convention $II_{ij}=\langle\sigma_{ij},N\rangle$:

$$
I=D^2du^2+R^2dv^2,\qquad II=-\frac{ab}{D}du^2-\frac{bR\sin u}{D}dv^2.
$$

Reversing the [unit normal](unit-normal.md) reverses $II$, but the [Gaussian curvature](gaussian-curvature.md) $K=ab^2\sin u/(RD^4)$ is unchanged.
