# Boolean lattice

↑ **Parent:** [Set family](set-family.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boolean_lattice)

The Boolean lattice is the [power set](power-set.md) of a finite set ordered by inclusion. Its rank-$h$ level consists of all $h$-element subsets.
