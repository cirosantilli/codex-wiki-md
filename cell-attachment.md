# Cell attachment

↑ **Parent:** [CW complex](cw-complex.md)

Attaching a $q$-cell along $\phi:S^{q-1}\to X$ forms the quotient of $X\sqcup D^q$ identifying each boundary point with its image under $\phi$. A constant attaching map gives $X\vee S^q$. An attaching map can affect the [cohomology ring](cohomology-ring.md) even when it does not change the additive cohomology groups.
