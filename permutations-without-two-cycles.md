# Permutations without two-cycles

↑ **Parent:** [Cycle decomposition of a permutation](cycle-decomposition-of-a-permutation.md)

These [permutations](permutation.md) have no cycle of length two, while fixed points and longer cycles are allowed. [Inclusion-exclusion](inclusion-exclusion-principle.md) over specified two-cycles gives the count shown: only disjoint specified pairs can coexist. Their fraction among all [permutations](permutation.md) tends to $e^{-1/2}$. This condition differs from being a derangement and from refusing to express a permutation as a product of [transpositions](transposition-permutation.md).
