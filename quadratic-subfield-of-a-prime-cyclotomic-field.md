# Quadratic subfield of a prime cyclotomic field

↑ **Parent:** [Cyclotomic field](cyclotomic-field.md)

For an odd prime $\ell$, the unique quadratic subfield of $\mathbb Q(\zeta_\ell)$ is the displayed field. A quadratic Gauss sum squares to $(-1)^{(\ell-1)/2}\ell$, and the cyclic [Galois group](galois-group.md) has a unique subgroup of index two.
