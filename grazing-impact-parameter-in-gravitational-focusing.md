# Grazing impact parameter in gravitational focusing

↑ **Parent:** [Gravitational focusing](gravitational-focusing.md)

For a point projectile approaching an absorbing spherical body of radius $R$ in a prescribed exterior [inverse-square force](inverse-square-force.md), [conservation of energy](conservation-of-energy.md) and [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) give $b^2=r_p^2+2GMr_p/v_\infty^2$. This is increasing in the [periapsis](periapsis.md) radius $r_p$, so $r_p=R$ gives the collision threshold displayed above. The [gravitational scattering angle](gravitational-scattering-angle.md) obeys $\tan(\chi/2)=GM/(bv_\infty^2)$. On the grazing family its deflection approaches zero at high asymptotic speed and $\pi$ as $v_\infty\to0^+$. Strict noncollision requires $b>b_{\rm crit}$; the equality is the tangency limit.
