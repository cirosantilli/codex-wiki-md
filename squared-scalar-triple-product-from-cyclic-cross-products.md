# Squared scalar triple product from cyclic cross products

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar triple product](scalar-triple-product.md)

Apply the [vector triple product identity](vector-triple-product.md) with first vector $b\times c$. Its contraction with $c$ vanishes, so $(b\times c)\times(c\times a)=[a\cdot(b\times c)]c$. Dotting with $a\times b$ gives the square of the [scalar triple product](scalar-triple-product.md). This is also the [Gram determinant](gram-determinant.md) of $a,b,c$, hence is nonnegative even when the vectors are linearly dependent.
