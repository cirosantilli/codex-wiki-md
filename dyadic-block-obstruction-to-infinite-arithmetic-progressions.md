# Dyadic block obstruction to infinite arithmetic progressions

↑ **Parent:** [Arithmetic progression](arithmetic-progression.md)

Colour each interval $[2^j,2^{j+1})\cap\mathbb N$ by the parity of $j$. Every infinite [arithmetic progression](arithmetic-progression.md) of positive [common difference](common-difference.md) d meets every sufficiently large such interval, since the interval has length greater than d. Both colours occur infinitely often. Thus neither colour class contains an infinite [arithmetic progression](arithmetic-progression.md), despite the finite-length conclusion of the [Van der Waerden theorem](van-der-waerden-theorem.md).
