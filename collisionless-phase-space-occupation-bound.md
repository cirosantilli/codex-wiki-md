# Collisionless phase-space occupation bound

↑ **Parent:** [Tremaine-Gunn bound](tremaine-gunn-bound.md)

Fine-grained occupation is conserved along trajectories of a collisionless distribution. Coarse graining cannot increase its maximum. For [fermions](fermion.md) the primordial occupation is bounded, so a measured halo density and velocity scale restrict the particle [mass](mass.md) required to produce that halo.
