# Stratonovich integral

↑ **Parent:** [Stochastic integral](stochastic-integral.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stratonovich_integral)

For continuous [semimartingales](semimartingale.md), the Stratonovich integral is defined by $\int_0^tY_s\circ dX_s=\int_0^tY_s\,dX_s+\frac12[Y,X]_t$, where the first term is an [Itô integral](ito-integral.md) and the correction is [quadratic covariation](quadratic-covariation.md). It obeys the ordinary chain rule and is the limit in [probability](probability.md) of symmetric endpoint sums.
