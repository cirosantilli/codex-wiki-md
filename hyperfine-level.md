# Hyperfine level

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperfine structure](hyperfine-structure.md)

An [energy level](energy-level.md) resolved by coupling the [nuclear spin](nuclear-spin.md) $I$ to electronic total [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) $J$. For a rotationally invariant [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md), a fixed $F$ has $2F+1$ degenerate [magnetic sublevels](magnetic-sublevel.md). Spectral component counts identify the number of upper [hyperfine levels](hyperfine-level.md) only when lower [hyperfine structure](hyperfine-structure.md), [selection rules](selection-rule.md), unresolved overlaps and missing lines have been accounted for.
