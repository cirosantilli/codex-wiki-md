# Ozone isopleth diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Photochemical ozone production](photochemical-ozone-production.md)

A contour plot of a specified [ozone](ozone.md) outcome against the loadings of [nitrogen oxides](nitrogen-oxides.md) and [volatile organic compounds](volatile-organic-compound.md). The ridge separating opposite NOx sensitivities distinguishes an [NOx-limited ozone-production regime](nox-limited-ozone-production-regime.md) from a [VOC-limited ozone-production regime](voc-limited-ozone-production-regime.md). The outcome may be peak [concentration](concentration.md), a time-integrated measure or a model production rate; axes and averaging conditions must be stated. A schematic diagram is not a calibrated emissions-response prediction.
