# Dirac field in two spacetime dimensions

↑ **Parent:** [Dirac field](dirac-field.md)

For [Minkowski metric](minkowski-metric.md) $\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)$, the displayed [gamma matrices](gamma-matrices.md) satisfy the [Clifford algebra](clifford-algebra.md) and $\gamma^5=\gamma^0\gamma^1$ has [chirality](chirality-physics.md) [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $+1,-1$. Ordering the [spinor](spinor.md) as $(\psi_R,\psi_L)^{\mathsf T}$ gives $i(\partial_t+\partial_x)\psi_R=m\psi_L$ and $i(\partial_t-\partial_x)\psi_L=m\psi_R$. A [plane wave](plane-wave.md) has the [Dirac Hamiltonian](dirac-hamiltonian.md) $p\sigma_3+m\sigma_1$, whose square is $(p^2+m^2)1$, proving $E^2=p^2+m^2$.
