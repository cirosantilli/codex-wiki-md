The [weak topology](../../../../weak-topology-split.md) $\sigma(X,X^*)$ is the coarsest topology on $X$ making every [continuous linear functional](../../../../continuous-linear-functional.md) $f\in X^*$ continuous. A basic neighborhood of $x_0$ is specified by finitely many inequalities $|f_j(x-x_0)|<\epsilon$.

The [weak-star topology](../../../../weak-star-topology.md) $\sigma(X^*,X)$ is the coarsest topology on $X^*$ making every evaluation $f\mapsto f(x)$, for $x\in X$, continuous. Its basic neighborhoods use finitely many such evaluations. Nets converge in these topologies exactly when all the corresponding [scalar](../../../../scalar.md) evaluations converge.

Let $J_X:X\to X^{**}$ be the canonical isometric embedding, $J_Xx(f)=f(x)$. **[Goldstine theorem](../../../../goldstine-theorem.md)** states

$$
\boxed{\overline{J_X(B_X)}^{\sigma(X^{**},X^*)}=B_{X^{**}}.}
$$

Here the balls are closed [unit balls](../../../../unit-ball.md). Thus any bidual unit [vector](../../../../vector.md) can be approximated in finitely many dual evaluations by unit [vectors](../../../../vector.md) from $X$; this does not claim [norm](../../../../norm.md) density or sequential density in [complete](../../../../completeness.md) generality.
