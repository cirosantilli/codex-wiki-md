<h1 id="2/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The domain carries $\sigma(Z^{**},Z^*)$, and the target carries $\sigma(X^*,X)$. For each $x\in X\subseteq Z^*$,

$$
(R\theta)(x)=\theta(x),
$$

which is one of the defining weak-star continuous evaluations on $Z^{**}$. Thus **$R$ is weak-star continuous**.

Restriction cannot increase a functional's [norm](../../../../norm.md), so $R(B_{Z^{**}})\subseteq B_{X^*}$. Conversely, any $f\in B_{X^*}$ is a bounded [linear functional](../../../../linear-functional.md) on the [subspace](../../../../vector-subspace.md) $X$ of $Z^*$. The [Hahn-Banach theorem](../../../../hahn-banach-theorem.md) extends it to a functional $\theta\in(Z^*)^*=Z^{**}$ with the same [norm](../../../../norm.md). Then $R\theta=f$ and $\|\theta\|\leq1$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{R(B_{Z^{**}})=B_{X^*}.}
$$

This proves exact surjectivity on closed balls, not just density of the image.
