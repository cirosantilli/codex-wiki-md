↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A **[biorthogonal system](../../../../biorthogonal-system.md)** is a family $(x_i,f_i)_{i\in I}$ with $x_i\in X$, $f_i\in X^*$, satisfying

$$
\boxed{f_i(x_j)=\delta_{ij}\quad(i,j\in I).}
$$

Thus each continuous coordinate functional takes value one on its own [vector](../../../../vector.md) and zero on all others. The [vectors](../../../../vector.md) need not be orthogonal in any inner product, and totality of their [linear span](../../../../linear-span.md) is an additional condition.
