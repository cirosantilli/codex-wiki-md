<h1 id="1/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $A_n$ be finite-rank bounded operators with $\|T-A_n\|\to0$ in [operator norm](../../../../../operator-norm.md). Fix $\epsilon>0$ and choose $n$ with $\|T-A_n\|<\epsilon/3$. The set $A_n(B_X)$ is bounded in a finite-dimensional [subspace](../../../../../vector-subspace.md), hence has a finite $\epsilon/3$-net $y_1,\ldots,y_m$.

For each $x\in B_X$, some $j$ satisfies

$$
\|Tx-y_j\|\leq\|(T-A_n)x\|+\|A_nx-y_j\|<2\epsilon/3.
$$

Thus $T(B_X)$ is [totally bounded](../../../../../totally-bounded-space.md). Its [closure](../../../../../closure-topology.md) is [totally bounded](../../../../../totally-bounded-space.md) and, as a closed subset of the [Banach space](../../../../../banach-space-split.md) $Y$, [complete](../../../../../completeness.md). A [complete](../../../../../completeness.md) [totally bounded](../../../../../totally-bounded-space.md) metric space is [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md). Consequently **$T$ is [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md)**.

The operator-norm limit is bounded, since $\|T\|\leq\|A_n\|+\|T-A_n\|$ for any sufficiently good approximation. This proves directly that an [approximable operator](../../../../../approximable-operator.md) into a [Banach space](../../../../../banach-space-split.md) is [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md); no assertion that every [compact operator](../../../../../compact-operator-split.md) on an arbitrary [Banach space](../../../../../banach-space-split.md) is approximable is needed.
