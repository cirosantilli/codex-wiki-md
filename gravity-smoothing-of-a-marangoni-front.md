# Gravity smoothing of a Marangoni front

↑ **Parent:** [Finite-mass Marangoni spreading on a liquid film](finite-mass-marangoni-spreading-on-a-liquid-film.md)

When the smoothing width exceeds the [capillary length](capillary-length.md), gravity dominates capillarity. Balancing hydrostatic flux $\rho gh_0^4/(\mu\Delta)$ against the [Marangoni stress](marangoni-effect.md) flux gives $\Delta\sim\rho gh_0^2x_N^2/(AM)\propto t^{2/3}$. The condition is $\Delta^2\gg\gamma_0/(\rho g)$ in physical units. The smoothing region catches up with the spreading pool at $x_N\sim AM/(\rho gh_0^2)$ and $t\sim\mu(AM)^2/[(\rho g)^3h_0^7]$, after which the sharp-front outer [similarity solution](similarity-solution.md) ceases to apply.
