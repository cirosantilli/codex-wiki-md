# Squarefree reduction excludes a prime from the power-basis index

↑ **Parent:** [Kummer-Dedekind theorem](kummer-dedekind-theorem.md)

For an [algebraic integer](algebraic-integer.md) $\alpha$ generating a [number field](number-field.md), the [polynomial discriminant](polynomial-discriminant.md) of its [minimal polynomial](minimal-polynomial.md) $f$ equals the [discriminant of elements of a number field](discriminant-of-elements-of-a-number-field.md) for its power basis. A squarefree reduction modulo $p$ has nonzero [resultant](resultant.md) with its derivative, so $p\nmid\operatorname{disc}(f)$. The [discriminant-index formula for an integral lattice](discriminant-index-formula-for-an-integral-lattice.md), $\operatorname{disc}(f)=[\mathcal O_K:\mathbb Z[\alpha]]^2d_K$, proves the assertion.
