# Sequentially closed set

↑ **Parent:** [Closed set](closed-set.md)

A [sequentially closed set](sequentially-closed-set.md) is one that contains every limit of its convergent sequences. This is weaker than being a [closed set](closed-set.md) in a general [topological space](topological-space.md), but the two conditions agree in [metric spaces](metric-space.md).
