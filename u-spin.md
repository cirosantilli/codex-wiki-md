# U-spin

↑ **Parent:** [Flavor symmetry](flavor-symmetry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U-spin)

The $SU(2)$ [flavor symmetry](flavor-symmetry.md) rotating down and strange [quarks](quark.md), with the up [quark](quark.md) a singlet. Since down and strange [quarks](quark.md) have equal [electric charge](electric-charge.md), the charge operator commutes with all U-spin generators, and every irreducible U-spin multiplet has a common charge. Unequal down- and strange-quark masses break this symmetry.
