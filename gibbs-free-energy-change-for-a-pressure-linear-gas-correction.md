# Gibbs free-energy change for a pressure-linear gas correction

↑ **Parent:** [Gibbs free energy](gibbs-free-energy.md)

For the [equation of state](equation-of-state.md) $V=Nk_BT/p-NA(T)$, integrate $(\partial G/\partial p)_{T,N}=V$ along an isotherm. This gives the displayed [Gibbs free energy](gibbs-free-energy.md) difference. Its negative [temperature](temperature-split.md) [derivative](derivative.md) at fixed endpoint pressures gives $\Delta S=-Nk_B\ln(p_1/p_0)+NA'(T)(p_1-p_0)$. The physically admissible branch must retain positive [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md).
