# Elementary abelian regular kernels in doubly transitive groups

↑ **Parent:** [Two-transitive group action](two-transitive-group-action.md)

A [point stabilizer](stabilizer-subgroup.md) in a doubly transitive [group](group-split.md) acts transitively by conjugation on the nonidentity elements of a regular [normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md) $N$. All those elements have the same order; Cauchy's theorem makes this a prime p and excludes other primes from $|N|$. A finite p-group has a nontrivial characteristic center. Transitivity then forces all of $N$ into its center, so $N$ is elementary abelian. Choosing a vector-space [basis](basis.md) identifies the original [permutation](permutation.md) [group](group-split.md) with a [subgroup](subgroup.md) of the corresponding general affine [group](group-split.md).
