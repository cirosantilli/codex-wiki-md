# Three-particle bound state from equal-mass fusion

↑ **Parent:** [Bound-state fusion of factorized S-matrices](bound-state-fusion-of-factorized-s-matrices.md)

For the [unit-modulus hyperbolic scattering block](unit-modulus-hyperbolic-scattering-block.md) with elementary [mass](mass.md) $m$, a pair bound at [rapidity](rapidity.md) difference $iu$ has [mass](mass.md) $m_2=2m\cos(u/2)$. [Bootstrap fusion](bound-state-fusion-of-factorized-s-matrices.md) with another elementary particle gives a further direct [pole](pole.md) at $3iu/2$. It is inside the [physical rapidity strip](physical-rapidity-strip.md) with positive imaginary [residue](residue.md) only for $0<u<2\pi/3$. In that range the new [bound state](bound-state.md) has rest-frame constituent [rapidities](rapidity.md) $iu,0,-iu$, so its energy is $m(1+2\cos u)=m\sin(3u/2)/\sin(u/2)$. Charges add at the bound-state vertex. At $u=2\pi/3$ the apparent pole cancels; beyond that point it lies outside the physical strip. The formula is conditional on this particular scattering block and its direct-pole interpretation, not a universal three-body binding law.
