# Thermal stability of externally heated blackbody dust

↑ **Parent:** [Blackbody dust sublimation radius](blackbody-dust-sublimation-radius.md)

At fixed irradiation, heating per grain is independent of [temperature](temperature-split.md) while cooling is proportional to $T^4$. With positive heat capacity and intact grains, a positive [temperature](temperature-split.md) perturbation increases net cooling, and a negative one increases net heating. Fixed dust properties and sufficient dust-[gas](gas.md) thermal coupling are necessary assumptions.
