# Jaynes-Cummings collapse and revival

↑ **Parent:** [Jaynes-Cummings model](jaynes-cummings-model.md)

An initially excited atom interacting resonantly with a [coherent state](coherent-state.md) has inversion $\sum_n P_n\cos(2g\sqrt{n+1}\,t)$, where $P_n$ is its [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md) of [photon number](photon-number.md). Different [Rabi frequencies](rabi-frequency.md) dephase on a time scale $1/g$ and approximately rephase near $2\pi\sqrt{\bar n}/g$. The revival estimate follows from the adjacent-component frequency difference $g/\sqrt{\bar n}$. Large mean [photon number](photon-number.md) makes the early oscillations nearly classical without removing the discrete spectrum responsible for revivals.
