# Bremsstrahlung

↑ **Parent:** [Electromagnetic radiation](electromagnetic-radiation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bremsstrahlung)

[Bremsstrahlung](bremsstrahlung.md) is [electromagnetic radiation](electromagnetic-radiation.md) emitted when a charged particle accelerates during interaction with another charged particle or field. The microscopic process $e^-+Z\to e^-+Z+\gamma$ creates a [photon](photon.md), while its inverse absorbs one. Thus it contributes to a photon-number collision source in the [photon number current](photon-number-current.md), unlike ordinary [Compton scattering](compton-scattering.md). [Thermal bremsstrahlung](thermal-bremsstrahlung.md) averages this emission over a thermal distribution of charged particles.
