# Covering sheet

↑ **Parent:** [Evenly covered neighbourhood](evenly-covered-neighbourhood.md)

A [covering sheet](covering-sheet.md) over an [evenly covered neighbourhood](evenly-covered-neighbourhood.md) $U$ is one of the disjoint [open subsets](open-set.md) of $p^{-1}(U)$ mapped onto $U$ by a [homeomorphism](homeomorphism.md) obtained by restricting the [covering map](covering-space.md).
