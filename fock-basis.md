# Fock basis

↑ **Parent:** [Fock space](fock-space.md)

An orthonormal basis labelled by the occupations of specified single-particle modes. For one bosonic mode, $|n\rangle=(a^\dagger)^n|0\rangle/\sqrt{n!}$ and the [number operator](number-operator.md) has eigenvalue $n$. A [coherent state](coherent-state.md)'s expansion in this basis gives its overlap and photon-number statistics.
