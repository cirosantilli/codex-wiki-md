# Lecture Notes for Solid State Physics by Steven H. Simon

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by Steven H. Simon](work-by-steven-h-simon.md)

Oxford undergraduate lecture notes. The source is distinct from the later quantum-matter notes by the same author. Section 16.2, printed pp. 178–179, describes semiconductor electron and hole transport. The displayed mobility ratio in this draft has a reversed speed-to-field typo; use the stated Drude equations and $\mu=|v_d|/|E|=|e\tau/m^*|$.
