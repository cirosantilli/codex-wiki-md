# Isotope shift

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic spectroscopy](atomic-spectroscopy.md)

An [isotope shift](isotope-shift.md) is a difference between corresponding atomic transition [frequencies](frequency.md) in different [isotopes](isotope.md). One contribution comes from nuclear [mass](mass.md), through recoil and electron correlations; another comes from the changing [nuclear charge distribution](nuclear-charge-distribution.md). For a specified transition, the leading finite-size contribution is often written $\delta\nu_{\rm field}=F\,\delta\langle r^2\rangle$, with an electronic factor $F$. Extracting changes of [nuclear charge radius](nuclear-charge-radius.md) therefore requires separating mass and finite-size contributions rather than identifying the entire shift with radius.
