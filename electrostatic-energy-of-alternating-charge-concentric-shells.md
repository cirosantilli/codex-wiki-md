# Electrostatic energy of alternating-charge concentric shells

↑ **Parent:** [Electrostatic energy](electrostatic-energy.md)

A first spherical shell with charge $q$ followed by shells of charges $2(-1)^{n+1}q$ leaves enclosed charge $(-1)^{n+1}q$ after each shell. [Gauss's law](gauss-s-law.md) therefore gives the same squared [electric field](electric-field.md) $q^2/(16\pi^2\epsilon_0^2r^4)$ in every gap outside the first radius. Integrating the [electrostatic energy](electrostatic-energy.md) density over all space gives $W=q^2/(8\pi\epsilon_0r_1)$, independent of the other shell radii.
