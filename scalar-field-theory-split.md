# Scalar field theory

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum field theory](quantum-field-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scalar_field_theory)

A scalar field theory describes one or more [Lorentz scalar](lorentz-scalar.md) fields through an [action](action.md). The displayed [Lagrangian density](lagrangian-density.md) is a common classical normalization for a [real scalar field](real-scalar-field.md); a [complex scalar field](complex-scalar-field.md) has two real components. [Canonical quantization](canonical-quantization.md) turns these classical degrees of freedom into operators. The free [real scalar field](real-scalar-field.md) gives spin-zero particles, while interactions arise from the potential $V$.
