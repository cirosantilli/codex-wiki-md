# Henry reaction

↑ **Parent:** [Organic reaction](organic-reaction.md)

Addition of a nitroalkane-derived [nitronate](nitronate.md) to an [aldehyde](aldehyde.md) or [ketone](ketone.md) gives a beta-nitro [alcohol](alcohol.md) after [protonation](protonation.md). Subsequent [dehydration](dehydration.md) is possible under some conditions but is a separate step.
