# Multistability

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamical system](dynamical-system.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multistability)

Coexistence of multiple stable long-time states at the same parameter values. [Bistability](bistability.md) is the case of two states; distinct [basins of attraction](basin-of-attraction.md) allow a system to remember its initial state or a sufficiently large perturbation.
