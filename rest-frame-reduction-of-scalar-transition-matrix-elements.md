# Rest-frame reduction of scalar transition matrix elements

↑ **Parent:** [Translation covariance of a quantum field](translation-covariance-of-a-quantum-field.md)

For $p^2=m^2>0$, let a [unitary operator](unitary-operator.md) $U$ implement a [Lorentz boost](lorentz-boost.md) taking $p$ to $(m,\mathbf0)$. For a [Lorentz scalar](lorentz-scalar.md) field, $\langle q|\phi(x)|p\rangle=\langle\Psi|\phi(0)|m,\mathbf0\rangle$ with $|\Psi\rangle=e^{-i(q-p)\cdot x}U|q\rangle$. The phase follows from [translation covariance of a quantum field](translation-covariance-of-a-quantum-field.md); its complex conjugate enters the bra. The transformed [energy eigenvalue](energy-eigenvalue-split.md) is $(\Lambda q)^0=p\cdot q/m$ by invariance of the [Minkowski inner product](minkowski-inner-product.md). The reduction requires a massive initial state.
