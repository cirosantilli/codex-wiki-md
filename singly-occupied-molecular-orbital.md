# Singly occupied molecular orbital

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular orbital](molecular-orbital.md)

A spatial [molecular orbital](molecular-orbital.md) occupied by one [electron](electron.md). In an open-shell molecule, its empty opposite-spin slot can accept another [electron](electron.md) even though the spatial orbital is occupied.
