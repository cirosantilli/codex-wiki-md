# Subsemigroup

↑ **Parent:** [Semigroup](semigroup.md)

A subsemigroup is a nonempty subset of a [semigroup](semigroup.md) closed under its [associative operation](associative-operation.md). In a [compact Hausdorff space](compact-hausdorff-space.md) carrying a [left-topological semigroup](left-topological-semigroup.md), descending chains of nonempty compact subsemigroups have a nonempty compact subsemigroup as their intersection. This supplies the minimal object used in the [Ellis–Numakura lemma](ellis-numakura-lemma.md).
