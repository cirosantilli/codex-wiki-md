# Substrate dichotomy

↑ **Parent:** [Lipid](lipid.md)

[substrate dichotomy](substrate-dichotomy.md) in fatty-acid engineering describes incompatible carrier preferences of sequential reactions, especially lipid-linked desaturation followed by acyl-CoA-dependent elongation. Limited transfer between pools can block flux even when both catalysts are active.
