# Homology cross product

↑ **Parent:** [Künneth theorem](kunneth-theorem.md)

The singular-chain product induces this natural pairing on [homology](homology-split.md). Composing it with multiplication of a loop space gives the [Pontryagin product on loop-space homology](pontryagin-product-on-loop-space-homology.md). This is different from the vector [cross product](cross-product.md).
