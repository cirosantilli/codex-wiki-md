# Baryon acoustic oscillation

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmological standard ruler](cosmological-standard-ruler.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baryon_acoustic_oscillation)

Baryon acoustic oscillations are the relic spatial scale of sound waves in the pre-recombination [photon-baryon fluid](photon-baryon-fluid.md). The scale appears both as a preferred separation in galaxy clustering and as oscillatory structure in the [cosmological density power spectrum](matter-power-spectrum.md).
