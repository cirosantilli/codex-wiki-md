# Exact line search

↑ **Parent:** [Line search](line-search.md)

A [line search](line-search.md) that finds a [global minimizer](global-minimizer.md) along the chosen ray, provided one exists. For a negative [gradient](gradient.md) direction, its decrease in the [objective function](objective-function.md) is at least that of any admissible fixed [step size](step-size.md). The cost of solving this one-dimensional [minimization problem](minimization-problem.md) must be included when comparing computation time.
