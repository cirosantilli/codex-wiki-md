<h1 id="orsted-s-experiment">Ørsted's experiment</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Electromagnetism](electromagnetism-split.md)

A nearby compass needle changes direction when a wire carries an [electric current](electric-current.md). Reversing the current or exchanging above/below positions reverses the magnetic action, connecting [electric currents](electric-current.md) with [magnetic fields](magnetic-field-split.md). The long-wire idealization has $B=\mu_0 I/(2\pi r)$ and circular orientation given by the [right-hand rule](right-hand-rule.md).
