# Exciton binding energy

↑ **Parent:** [Exciton](exciton.md)

The [exciton binding energy](exciton-binding-energy.md) is the positive [energy](energy-split.md) needed to separate a bound [electron](electron.md) and [electron hole](electron-hole.md) into the relevant unbound [scattering states](scattering-state.md). Compare the [exciton](exciton.md) [energy](energy-split.md) $E_X$ with the continuum threshold $E_{\rm cont}$ at the same total [crystal momentum](crystal-momentum.md) and for the same [energy bands](energy-band.md). The negative internal [energy eigenvalue](energy-eigenvalue-split.md) $-E_B$ and the positive separation cost $E_B$ are different sign conventions for related quantities. An [exciton](exciton.md) may move freely through a perfect crystal while remaining internally bound: “free exciton” does not mean an unbound pair.
