# Scaling refinement equation

↑ **Parent:** [Scaling function](scaling-function.md)

Nesting in an orthonormal [multiresolution analysis](multiresolution-analysis.md) expresses a [scaling function](scaling-function.md) in the finer-scale [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md). The associated low-pass symbol is $m(\xi)=2^{-1/2}\sum_kh_ke^{-ik\xi}$, and $\widehat\varphi(2\xi)=m(\xi)\widehat\varphi(\xi)$. If $\varphi$ has [compact support](compact-support.md), only finitely many coefficients $h_k$ are nonzero. With $\widehat\varphi(0)=1$, iteration gives $\widehat\varphi(\xi)=\prod_{j\ge1}m(\xi/2^j)$ when the limit exists.
