# Horizon redshift of a Schwarzschild infaller

↑ **Parent:** [Schwarzschild spacetime](schwarzschild-spacetime.md)

For smooth radial infall through a future [Schwarzschild event horizon](schwarzschild-event-horizon.md), outgoing retarded time obeys $u\sim-4M\log(\tau_H-\tau)$. A distant observer sees the [redshift](redshift.md) grow as $e^{t_{\rm rec}/(4M)}$. Its exponent in emission [Schwarzschild time](schwarzschild-time.md) is instead $1/(2M)$, because $t_{\rm rec}\sim2t_{\rm em}$. At finite fixed observer radius the wavelength ratio includes $\sqrt{1-2M/r_B}$.
