# Subgroups of C4 times C2

↑ **Parent:** [Direct product of cyclic groups of orders four and two](direct-product-of-cyclic-groups-of-orders-four-and-two.md)

With the generators $c,a$ of the [direct product of cyclic groups of orders four and two](direct-product-of-cyclic-groups-of-orders-four-and-two.md), the [subgroups](subgroup.md) are the trivial group, the whole group, the three order-two groups $\langle c^2\rangle$, $\langle a\rangle$, $\langle c^2a\rangle$, and the three order-four groups $\langle c\rangle$, $\langle ca\rangle$, $\langle c^2,a\rangle$. These are exhaustive by [Lagrange's theorem](lagrange-s-theorem.md): a proper nontrivial [subgroup](subgroup.md) has order two or four; an order-four [subgroup](subgroup.md) either contains an element of order four and is one of the two cyclic examples, or is precisely the four-element set killed by squaring. The latter is a [Klein four-group](klein-four-group.md).
