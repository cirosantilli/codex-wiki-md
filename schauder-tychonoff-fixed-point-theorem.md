# Schauder-Tychonoff fixed-point theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Fixed-point theorem](fixed-point-theorem.md)

Every continuous self-map of a nonempty compact convex subset of a [locally convex space](locally-convex-space.md) has a [fixed point](fixed-point.md). Compactness is in the locally convex topology, which may be weaker than a norm topology. This version permits fixed-point arguments on a [weak-star topology](weak-star-topology.md) compact set of [linear functionals](linear-functional.md). See [Hartman's fixed-point chapter, Theorem 0.2](https://www.math.purdue.edu/~yipn/543/hartmanODE12-ImpFctFixedPts.pdf).
