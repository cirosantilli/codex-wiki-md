# Circumference

↑ **Parent:** [Circle](circle.md)

The circumference of a [circle](circle.md) is its boundary [arc length](arc-length.md). For [radius](radius.md) $r$, it equals $2\pi r$. This growing boundary length accounts for the radial factor in the [Rayleigh distribution](rayleigh-distribution.md).
