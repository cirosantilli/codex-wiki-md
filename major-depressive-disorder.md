# Major depressive disorder

↑ **Parent:** [Psychiatry](psychiatry-split.md)

A clinically assessed syndrome involving persistent depressed mood and/or [anhedonia](anhedonia.md), with associated cognitive, motivational, sleep and bodily symptoms and functional impairment. Similar symptom profiles need not have one biological cause.
