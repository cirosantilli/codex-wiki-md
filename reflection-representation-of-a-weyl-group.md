# Reflection representation of a Weyl group

↑ **Parent:** [Weyl group](weyl-group.md)

The reflection representation is the action of the [Weyl group](weyl-group.md) on the real span of its [root system](root-system.md), generated by its [Weyl reflections](weyl-reflection.md). For an [irreducible root system](irreducible-root-system.md), it is an [irreducible representation](irreducible-representation.md): if $U$ is an [invariant subspace](invariant-subspace.md), each [root](root-of-a-root-system.md) either lies in $U^\perp$ or, by subtracting a reflected vector of $U$, lies in $U$. These alternatives partition the roots into [orthogonal](orthogonal-vectors.md) sets. Irreducibility and spanning force $U=0$ or the whole space.
