<h1 id="jordan-holder-factor">Jordan–Hölder factor</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Composition series](composition-series.md)

A Jordan–Hölder factor is a simple quotient in a [composition series](composition-series.md). The [Jordan–Hölder theorem](jordan-holder-theorem.md) says that the multiset of such factors is independent of the chosen composition series.
