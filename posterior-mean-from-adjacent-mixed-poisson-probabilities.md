# Posterior mean from adjacent mixed Poisson probabilities

↑ **Parent:** [Mixed Poisson distribution](mixed-poisson-distribution.md)

For a [mixed Poisson distribution](mixed-poisson-distribution.md) with known exposure $n>0$, multiply the unnormalized [posterior density](posterior-density.md) $\theta^s e^{-n\theta}\pi(\theta)$ by $\theta$ and compare its integral with the next [probability](probability.md) mass. The factors $n^s/s!$ give the displayed identity whenever $p_n(s)>0$. Both mass [probabilities](probability.md) have the same exposure $n$. For a future [conditionally independent](conditional-independence.md) unit-exposure [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md) count, this is also its [posterior predictive](posterior-predictive-distribution.md) [expected value](expected-value.md). It holds for arbitrary proper mixing distributions, with integrals taken against the [prior](prior-probability.md) measure when there is no density.
