# Regular tree

↑ **Parent:** [Tree (graph theory)](tree-graph-theory-split.md)

An infinite connected [tree](tree-graph-theory-split.md) is regular of degree $k$ if every [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) has exactly $k$ neighbours. Necessarily $k\ge2$. For $k=2$ it is a two-sided infinite path. For $k\ge3$, rooting it gives $k$ children at the root and $k-1$ children at each other [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md); this constructs the unique such tree up to [graph isomorphism](graph-isomorphism.md).
