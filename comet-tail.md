# Comet tail

↑ **Parent:** [Comet](comet.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comet_tail)

A comet tail is an extended structure formed from material released by a comet. Its dust tail is shaped largely by [radiation pressure](radiation-pressure.md), while an ion tail is shaped by the solar wind and magnetic field. The [cometary dust tail](cometary-dust-tail.md) is the solid-grain component.
