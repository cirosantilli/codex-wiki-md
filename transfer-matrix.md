# Transfer matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Combinatorics](combinatorics-split.md)

A transfer matrix stores the weights of allowed transitions between finitely many boundary states. Its powers sum weights of compatible sequences of transitions. For a cyclic boundary condition, the [matrix trace](matrix-trace.md) $\operatorname{tr}(T^n)$ counts weighted closed sequences of length $n$.
