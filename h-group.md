# H-group

↑ **Parent:** [H-space](h-space.md)

An [H-space](h-space.md) whose multiplication is homotopy associative and admits a homotopy inverse. These operations give $[X,Y]_*$ a [group](group-split.md) structure for every based [CW complex](cw-complex.md) $X$. A [loop space](loop-space.md) is the basic example: concatenate paths, use reversal for inversion, and use reparametrization for homotopy associativity. A bare [H-space](h-space.md) should not automatically be treated as a group object.
