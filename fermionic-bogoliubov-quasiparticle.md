# Fermionic Bogoliubov quasiparticle

↑ **Parent:** [BCS theory](bcs-theory.md)

A fermionic excitation obtained by a canonical particle–hole [Bogoliubov transformation](bogoliubov-transformation.md) of a paired mean-field Hamiltonian. The uniform spin-singlet problem has the displayed positive energy and weights $|u|^2=(1+\xi/E)/2$, $|v|^2=(1-\xi/E)/2$. It interpolates between particle-like and hole-like excitations across the Fermi surface. These single-particle excitations differ from collective phase and amplitude modes of the [order parameter](order-parameter.md).
