# Metabolic Seifert form

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic concordance of knots](algebraic-concordance-of-knots.md)

A nonsingular Seifert form on a $2g$-dimensional space is metabolic when it vanishes on a $g$-dimensional subspace called a metabolizer.

The metabolizer is a half-dimensional subspace on which the [Seifert form](seifert-form.md) restricts to zero.
