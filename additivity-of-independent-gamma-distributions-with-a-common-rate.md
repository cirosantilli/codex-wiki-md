# Additivity of independent gamma distributions with a common rate

↑ **Parent:** [Gamma distribution](gamma-distribution.md)

If independent random variables $X_i$ have [gamma distributions](gamma-distribution.md) with shapes $a_i$ and one common rate $r$, then

$$
\sum_{i=1}^nX_i\sim\operatorname{Gamma}\left(\sum_{i=1}^na_i,r\right).
$$

Their [Laplace transforms of nonnegative random variables](laplace-transform-of-a-nonnegative-random-variable.md) multiply to $(r/(r+s))^{\sum_i a_i}$, which is the transform of the displayed gamma distribution; uniqueness of the Laplace transform proves the claim.
