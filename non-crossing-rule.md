# Non-crossing rule

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum mechanics](quantum-mechanics-split.md)

For a generic one-parameter Hermitian [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md), two coupled states of the same conserved [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md) avoid an exact crossing. Opposite [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md) or a vanishing coupling can permit crossings. The rule does not supply numerical energies or prove that every possible reaction path shares the stipulated [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md).
