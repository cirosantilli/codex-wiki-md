# Mere-exposure effect

↑ **Parent:** [Learning](learning.md)

An increase in preference associated with prior exposure. Preference and explicit [recognition memory](recognition-memory.md) can dissociate. This does not by itself determine whether evaluation of the stimulus's significance occurred.
