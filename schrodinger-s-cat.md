<h1 id="schrodinger-s-cat">Schrödinger's cat</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Measurement problem](measurement-problem.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schrödinger's_cat)

A thought experiment correlating a microscopic quantum alternative with macroscopically distinct records, introduced by [Erwin Schrödinger](erwin-schrodinger.md) to sharpen the problem of applying unitary evolution to a measuring apparatus. The resulting entangled superposition and the mixture produced by selecting an outcome are different joint [density operators](density-matrix-split.md). [Quantum decoherence](quantum-decoherence.md) can make the records' reduced density operator approximately diagonal without selecting a single global component.
