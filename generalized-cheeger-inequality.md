# Generalized Cheeger inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral profile](spectral-profile.md)

For the isoperimetric profile $\Phi_*(r)$ of a reversible chain and $r\leq1/2$,

$$
\frac{\Phi_*(r)^2}{2}\leq\Lambda(r)\leq\frac{\Phi_*(r)}{1-r}.
$$
