# Outer hair cell

↑ **Parent:** [Hair cell](hair-cell.md)

A cochlear hair-cell class contributing mechanical feedback that increases weak-sound sensitivity and sharpens frequency selectivity. Its feedback-dependent effects are part of the [cochlear amplifier](cochlear-amplifier.md).
