# Variance-minimizing exponential retention

↑ **Parent:** [Total variance stationary condition for excess of loss](total-variance-stationary-condition-for-excess-of-loss.md)

For [exponential distribution](exponential-distribution.md) claims of mean $\mu$ and [compound Poisson distribution](compound-poisson-distribution.md) count parameter $\lambda$, the total party [variance](variance-split.md) under [excess of loss reinsurance](excess-of-loss-reinsurance.md) is $g(M)=2\lambda\mu^2(1-(M/\mu)e^{-M/\mu})$. Its derivative is $2\lambda e^{-M/\mu}(M-\mu)$, so $M=\mu$ is the unique global minimum, with value $2\lambda\mu^2(1-e^{-1})$.
