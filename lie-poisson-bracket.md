# Lie-Poisson bracket

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson manifold](poisson-manifold.md)

The [dual space](dual-space.md) $\mathfrak g^*$ of a finite-dimensional [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) has this canonical [Poisson bracket](poisson-bracket.md). For a basis $v^i$, linear coordinates $x^i(\ell)=\ell(v^i)$ give $\{x^i,x^j\}=c^{ij}_kx^k$. The Lie algebra's [Jacobi identity](jacobi-identity.md) is precisely the [coordinate Jacobi condition for a Poisson bivector](coordinate-jacobi-condition-for-a-poisson-bivector.md). This construction is intrinsic on the dual space; writing the same formula in an arbitrary manifold chart does not by itself provide a compatible global atlas.
