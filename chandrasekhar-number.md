# Chandrasekhar number

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetoconvection](magnetoconvection.md)

For an imposed field $B_0$ in a layer of depth $d$, the [Chandrasekhar number](chandrasekhar-number.md) compares magnetic tension with viscous and magnetic-diffusive effects. Here $\eta$ is [magnetic diffusivity](magnetic-diffusivity.md) and $\nu$ is [kinematic viscosity](kinematic-viscosity.md). In thermal-diffusion units the linear magnetic-force coefficient is $Q\zeta$, where $\zeta=\eta/\kappa$ and $\kappa$ is [thermal diffusivity](thermal-diffusivity.md).
