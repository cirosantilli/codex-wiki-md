# Reduced fraction

↑ **Parent:** [Rational number](rational-number.md)

A reduced fraction $a/b$ has [coprime integers](coprime-integers.md) $a,b$ and positive denominator $b$. Distinct reduced fractions with denominators at most $Q$ differ by at least $Q^{-2}$, since their difference has a nonzero [integer](integer-split.md) numerator over a denominator at most $Q^2$.
