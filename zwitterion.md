# Zwitterion

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical substance](chemical-substance.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zwitterion)

A [zwitterion](zwitterion.md) has opposite formal [electric charges](electric-charge.md) in different regions of a [molecule](molecule.md), with zero net [electric charge](electric-charge.md). [Phosphatidylcholine](phosphatidylcholine.md) provides a membrane-related example. This is stronger than merely having [chemical polarity](chemical-polarity.md) through fractional [partial atomic charges](partial-atomic-charge.md). See [IUPAC Gold Book: zwitterions](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/Z06752).
