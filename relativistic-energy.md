# Relativistic energy

↑ **Parent:** [Energy–momentum relation](energy-momentum-relation.md)

For a massive free particle with invariant [rest mass](invariant-mass.md) $m$, [speed](speed.md) $v<c$, and [Lorentz factor](lorentz-factor.md) $\gamma=(1-v^2/c^2)^{-1/2}$, its total [energy](energy-split.md) is $E=\gamma mc^2$. It includes the rest [energy](energy-split.md) $mc^2$ and the [relativistic kinetic energy](relativistic-kinetic-energy.md) $(\gamma-1)mc^2$. Here $c$ is the [speed of light](speed-of-light.md). At fixed total [energy](energy-split.md), a larger [speed](speed.md) requires a smaller [rest mass](invariant-mass.md).
