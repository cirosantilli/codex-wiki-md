# Multimode optical fiber

↑ **Parent:** [Optical fiber](optical-fiber.md)

A [multimode optical fiber](multimode-optical-fiber.md) supports several guided [normal modes](normal-mode.md) at the operating [wavelength](wavelength.md). Different mode populations can change its output cone, so launch conditions matter when measuring [numerical aperture](numerical-aperture.md) or interpreting [focal-ratio degradation](focal-ratio-degradation.md).
