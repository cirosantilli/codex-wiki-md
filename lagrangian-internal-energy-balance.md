# Lagrangian internal energy balance

↑ **Parent:** [Continuum thermodynamics](continuum-thermodynamics.md)

For the material-first [nominal stress tensor](nominal-stress-tensor.md), the displayed local [conservation of energy](conservation-of-energy.md) law balances mechanical work, heat supply and outgoing [heat flux](heat-flux-density.md). Integrating over a fixed reference material volume and using the [divergence theorem](divergence-theorem.md) gives its integral form. The full total-energy balance includes [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) and external mechanical work; [momentum conservation](momentum-conservation.md) reduces it to this internal-energy balance.
