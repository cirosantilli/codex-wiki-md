# Power objects in a slice category

↑ **Parent:** [Power object](power-object.md)

If a [Cartesian category](cartesian-category.md) has [power objects](power-object.md), its [slice categories](slice-category.md) do too. In $\mathcal E/A$, the constant family $A\times X\to A$ has power object $A\times PX\to A$, since its product with a family $Y\to A$ is $X\times Y$. An arbitrary family $p:X\to A$ is a subobject of the constant family through its graph $(p,1_X)$, so the [power object of a subobject](power-object-of-a-subobject.md) construction applies in the slice.
