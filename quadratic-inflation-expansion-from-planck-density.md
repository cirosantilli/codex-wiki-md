# Quadratic-inflation expansion from Planck density

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic-potential slow-roll solution](quadratic-potential-slow-roll-solution.md)

For $V=m^2\phi^2/2$ and unreduced [Planck mass](planck-mass.md), the [slow-roll e-fold count](slow-roll-e-fold-count.md) is $N=2\pi(\phi_i^2-\phi_f^2)/m_P^2$. A formal initial potential $V_i\sim m_P^4$ gives $\phi_i^2\sim2m_P^4/m^2$, hence the displayed large-field estimate and expansion factor $e^N$. The final-field contribution is order one in $N$. Without specifying $m/m_P$ there is no unique numerical expansion factor. The extrapolation to Planck density is a classical estimate, rather than a controlled treatment of quantum-gravity corrections.
