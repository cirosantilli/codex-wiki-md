# 2019

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the digital social research: statistics core course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Solutions to all six questions of the 12 December 2019 Statistics Core examination. [Oxford examination release ZIP](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/admission_statistics_and_past_pa/response/2722250/attach/2/Exam%20papers.zip?cookie_passthrough=1), member `Digital Social Research Statistics Core/2019 Michaelmas a14564w1.pdf`. All seven numbered figures and the unnumbered output for Question 1 are available. The printed group means and overall mean in Question 1 cannot describe one complete sample; that inconsistency is kept explicit. Calculations below use the supplied summaries and counts, without inventing individual records.
