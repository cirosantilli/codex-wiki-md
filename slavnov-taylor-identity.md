# Slavnov-Taylor identity

↑ **Parent:** [BRST Ward identity](brst-ward-identity.md)

A [Slavnov-Taylor identity](slavnov-taylor-identity.md) is a [BRST symmetry](brst-symmetry.md) constraint relating [correlation functions](correlation-function.md) or [interaction vertices](interaction-vertex.md) in a gauge-fixed [Non-Abelian gauge theory](yang-mills-theory.md). Unlike a simple statement that every open gauge index is transverse, the general identities include [Faddeev-Popov ghost fields](faddeev-popov-ghost.md). After putting external legs [on shell](on-shell.md) and making all the other external states physical, they imply decoupling when one massless vector [polarization vector](polarization-vector.md) is replaced by its [four-momentum](four-momentum.md). They also control cancellations of auxiliary covariant components needed for physical [unitarity](unitary-operator.md).
