# Deep reactive-ion etching

↑ **Parent:** [Reactive-ion etching](reactive-ion-etching.md)

Directional plasma [etching](etching.md) used for deep, high-aspect-ratio features. Alternating fluorine-based [silicon](silicon.md) [etching](etching.md) and sidewall [passivation](passivation.md) is one implementation; transport, mask erosion, charging, scallops and aspect-ratio-dependent etch rate constrain the achievable structure.
