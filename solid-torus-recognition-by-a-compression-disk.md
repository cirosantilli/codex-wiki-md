# Solid-torus recognition by a compression disk

↑ **Parent:** [Solid torus](solid-torus.md)

A compact orientable [irreducible three-manifold](irreducible-three-manifold.md) with connected torus boundary which is compressible is a [solid torus](solid-torus.md). Compressing along a [compression disk](compression-disk.md) gives an embedded sphere. The compressed component has one sphere boundary and irreducibility makes it a ball; undoing compression attaches one one-handle, giving $D^2\times S^1$.
