# Mean-square criterion for convergence in density

↑ **Parent:** [Convergence in density of a sequence](convergence-in-density-of-a-sequence.md)

The displayed condition implies [convergence in density of a sequence](convergence-in-density-of-a-sequence.md) to $\ell$, since the fraction of indices with $|a_n-\ell|\geq\varepsilon$ is at most $\varepsilon^{-2}$ times the averaged squared error. A signed [Cesaro limit](cesaro-convergence-of-a-sequence.md) alone is insufficient: $a_n=(-1)^n$ has zero signed average but no density limit.
