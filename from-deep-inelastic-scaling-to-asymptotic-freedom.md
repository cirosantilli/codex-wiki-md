# From deep inelastic scaling to asymptotic freedom

↑ **Parent:** [Asymptotic freedom](asymptotic-freedom.md)

The 1969 [SLAC-MIT deep inelastic electron scattering experiment](slac-mit-deep-inelastic-electron-scattering-experiment.md) found approximate [Bjorken scaling](bjorken-scaling.md): an estimated [deep-inelastic structure function](deep-inelastic-structure-function.md) depended mainly on a ratio of kinematic variables. Its restricted angular coverage did not separately determine both [deep-inelastic structure functions](deep-inelastic-structure-function.md), and the extraction assumed transverse [virtual photons](virtual-photon.md). This was motivation for a microscopic theory, not already a measurement of the [QCD coupling](strong-coupling-constant.md).[Printed p. 935, abstract; the stated transverse-photon assumption and limited separation of W1 and W2](observed-behavior-of-highly-inelastic-electron-proton-scattering-by-m-breidenbach-and-others.md)

The 1973 [David Gross](david-j-gross.md)–[Frank Wilczek](frank-wilczek.md) calculation supplied a [quantum field theory](quantum-field-theory-split.md) explanation of approximately free [partons](parton.md) with calculable logarithmic corrections. [Asymptotic freedom](asymptotic-freedom.md) therefore permits approximate [Bjorken scaling](bjorken-scaling.md) without requiring exact scale independence. The [QCD coupling](strong-coupling-constant.md) governs the corrections, while [parton distribution functions](parton-distribution-function.md) and their evolution connect the short-distance calculation to a [proton](proton.md).[Section 2, pp. 5–6: finite-energy radiation and calculable corrections](asymptotic-freedom-from-paradox-to-paradigm-by-frank-wilczek.md)

In a later direct test, H1 collided [Positrons](positron.md) and [protons](proton.md) at HERA and selected [deep inelastic scattering](deep-inelastic-scattering.md) events. A liquid-argon [particle calorimeter](calorimeter-particle-physics.md) identified the outgoing [Positron](positron.md) with an associated track; calorimeter deposits and charged tracks reconstructed [particle jets](jet-particle-physics.md) in the [Breit frame](breit-frame.md). Vertex, timing and longitudinal [energy](energy-split.md)-balance cuts suppressed backgrounds; two simulations checked detector corrections. Fits of [particle jet](jet-particle-physics.md) [cross sections](cross-section-physics.md) to next-to-leading-order [QCD](quantum-chromodynamics.md) extracted $\alpha_s(E_T)$ in transverse-[energy](energy-split.md) bins, consistent with decreasing [QCD coupling](strong-coupling-constant.md). [Hadronization](hadronization.md), [parton distribution functions](parton-distribution-function.md) and [renormalization scale](renormalization-scale.md) choices limited the inference. This measures rates and fits a coupling, rather than directly reading a force meter.[2007, sections 2.1–2.4 and 5; Fig. 5](measurement-of-inclusive-jet-production-in-deep-inelastic-scattering-at-high-q2-and-determination-of-the-strong-coupling-by-h1-collaboration.md)
