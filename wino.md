# Wino

↑ **Parent:** [Gaugino](gaugino.md)

A [wino](wino.md) is a spin-one-half [gaugino](gaugino.md) of the weak $SU(2)_L$ [gauge group](gauge-group.md). Before [electroweak symmetry breaking](electroweak-symmetry-breaking.md), its three adjoint components transform as $(\mathbf1,\mathbf3,0)$ and can be described by a triplet of [Majorana spinors](majorana-spinor.md). After symmetry breaking, the neutral component can mix with the [bino](bino.md) and neutral [higgsinos](higgsino.md) into [neutralinos](neutralino.md).
