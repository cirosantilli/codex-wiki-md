# Organolithium aggregation

↑ **Parent:** [Organolithium reagent](organolithium-reagent.md)

[Organolithium aggregation](organolithium-aggregation.md) associates multiple organolithium or related [lithium](lithium.md)-anion units, often through bridging donor atoms. Strong donor solvents can change aggregate structure without necessarily making only monomers.
