↑ **Parent:** [Stereochemistry](stereochemistry-split.md)

The [Cahn-Ingold-Prelog priority rules](cahn-ingold-prelog-priority-rules.md) rank substituent paths to assign stereochemical descriptors. Compare the next shell only after a tie; multiple bonds and aromatic paths require the prescribed duplicate-atom treatment. Spatial orientation and priority ranking are separate steps.
