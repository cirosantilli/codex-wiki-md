# Square planar molecular geometry

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular geometry](molecular-geometry.md)

In [square planar molecular geometry](square-planar-molecular-geometry.md), four donor atoms surround a center in a plane. Two matching ligands can be cis or trans. Low-spin $d^8$ complexes of many late transition metals commonly have this geometry.
