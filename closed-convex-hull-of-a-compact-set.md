# Closed convex hull of a compact set

↑ **Parent:** [Convex hull](convex-hull.md)

In a [Banach space](banach-space-split.md), the [closed convex hull of a compact set](closed-convex-hull-of-a-compact-set.md) is compact in the [norm topology](norm-topology.md). Given $\varepsilon>0$, choose a finite $\varepsilon$-net $\{y_i\}$ for $K$. Every [convex combination](convex-combination.md) of points of $K$ is within $\varepsilon$ in [norm](norm.md) of $\operatorname{conv}\{y_i\}$, by replacing its terms by their approximating $y_i$. This finite-dimensional compact [convex set](convex-set-split.md) has a finite $\varepsilon$-net, giving a finite $2\varepsilon$-net for $\operatorname{conv}K$. Its closure is therefore [totally bounded](totally-bounded-space.md) and [complete](completeness.md), hence compact. [Completeness](completeness.md) of the ambient space is used at this last step.
