<h1 id="biology-2e-by-mary-ann-clark-and-others/binary-fission">Binary fission</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Cell Division](cell-division.md)

Division of one cell into two. Ciliates can increase cell number through this vegetative process independently of a conjugation event.
