# Electrical admittance

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical impedance](electrical-impedance.md)

The reciprocal of [electrical impedance](electrical-impedance.md), relating [electric current](electric-current.md) and [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) [phasors](phasor.md) by $\widetilde I=Y\widetilde V$. Its unit is the siemens, $\mathrm S=\Omega^{-1}$. [Electrical admittances](electrical-admittance.md) add for branches in a [parallel circuit](parallel-circuit.md).
