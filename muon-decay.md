# Muon decay

↑ **Parent:** [Muon](muon.md)

A [weak charged current](charged-current.md) decay described at energies much below the [W boson](w-boson.md) mass by a [four-fermion interaction](four-fermion-interaction.md) with coefficient given by the [Fermi constant](fermi-constant.md). At leading order with negligible final-state masses, its width is $G_F^2m_\mu^5/(192\pi^3)$.
