# Monochromator

↑ **Parent:** [Optical instrument](optical-instrument.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monochromator)

A [monochromator](monochromator.md) selects a narrow, adjustable [wavelength](wavelength.md) interval from an [optical spectrum](optical-spectrum.md). Its finite spectral bandwidth matters when calibrating a [photodetector](photodetector.md)'s [spectral responsivity](spectral-responsivity.md) in [calibrated radiant-flux measurement](calibrated-radiant-flux-measurement.md).
