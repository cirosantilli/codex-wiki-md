# Fluorite structure

↑ **Parent:** [Crystal structure](crystal-structure.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fluorite_structure)

An $AB_2$ [crystal structure](crystal-structure.md) with A on a [face-centered cubic lattice](face-centered-cubic-lattice.md) and B occupying all eight tetrahedral sites of its conventional cubic [unit cell](unit-cell.md). The [coordination numbers](coordination-number.md) are eight for A and four for B. The cubic cell contains four $AB_2$ [formula units](formula-unit.md).
