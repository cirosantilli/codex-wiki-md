# Cubic crystal system

↑ **Parent:** [Bravais lattice](bravais-lattice.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cubic_crystal_system)

The [cubic crystal system](cubic-crystal-system.md) has cubic point symmetry. Its three cubic [Bravais lattices](bravais-lattice.md) are primitive, body-centered, and face-centered. The [body-centered cubic lattice](body-centered-cubic-lattice.md) is one member of that classification.
