# Helicity suppression

↑ **Parent:** [Charged current](charged-current.md)

A spin-zero parent decaying through a chiral [weak charged current](charged-current.md) to a charged lepton and a massless [neutrino](neutrino.md) cannot supply the helicities selected by a massless interaction. A charged-lepton mass insertion makes the amplitude proportional to $m_\ell$ and the [decay width](decay-width.md) proportional to $m_\ell^2$. A spin-one [W boson](w-boson.md) does not suffer this suppression.
