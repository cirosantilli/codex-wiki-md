# Weak Condorcet winner

↑ **Parent:** [Condorcet winner](condorcet-winner.md)

A [weak Condorcet winner](weak-condorcet-winner.md) is never defeated by a strict pairwise majority, allowing ties. For even electorates with [single-peaked preferences](single-peaked-preferences.md), all alternatives between the two middle peaks have this property. A single-valued selection requires a tie convention; arbitrary tie selection is not automatically [strategyproof](strategyproofness.md).
