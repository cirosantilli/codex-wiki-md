# Radiation budget

↑ **Parent:** [Geophysics](geophysics-split.md)

The [energy](energy-split.md) entering and leaving a system through [electromagnetic radiation](electromagnetic-radiation-split.md). A top-of-atmosphere [radiation budget](radiation-budget.md) compares absorbed sunlight with outgoing [thermal radiation](thermal-radiation.md). A latitude-dependent imbalance can be balanced by atmospheric and [ocean heat fluxes](ocean-heat-flux.md) even when the global mean is zero.
