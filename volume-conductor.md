# Volume conductor

↑ **Parent:** [Bioelectromagnetism](bioelectromagnetism.md)

A [volume conductor](volume-conductor.md) is a conducting three-dimensional medium through which [electric currents](electric-current.md) flow. In the quasistatic bioelectric model, the total [current density](current-density.md) is $\mathbf J=\mathbf J^p-\sigma\nabla u$, comprising an impressed source current and its Ohmic return current. [Electric charge conservation](charge-conservation.md) gives $\nabla\cdot(\sigma\nabla u)=\nabla\cdot\mathbf J^p$. An insulating boundary has zero normal total current.
