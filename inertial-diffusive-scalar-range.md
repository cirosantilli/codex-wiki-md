# Inertial-diffusive scalar range

↑ **Parent:** [Obukhov-Corrsin theory](obukhov-corrsin-theory.md)

For scalar diffusivity much larger than [kinematic viscosity](kinematic-viscosity.md), scalar diffusion dominates at lengths between the Kolmogorov and scalar cutoff scales. The velocity may still have inertial-range variations, but the scalar is smooth to leading order. The leading isotropic [scalar structure function](scalar-structure-function.md) is quadratic, with coefficient fixed by the mean-square gradient and the [scalar dissipation rate](scalar-dissipation-rate.md), rather than by a continuing inertial-convective two-thirds law.
