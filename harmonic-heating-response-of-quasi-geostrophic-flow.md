# Harmonic heating response of quasi-geostrophic flow

↑ **Parent:** [Diabatically forced quasi-geostrophic potential vorticity](diabatically-forced-quasi-geostrophic-potential-vorticity.md)

For constant [buoyancy frequency](buoyancy-frequency.md), [density](density.md) forcing $r_0e^{-\mu z}\cos kx\cos\omega_0t$ gives two frequencies $\omega=\pm\omega_0$. The [quasi-geostrophic streamfunction](quasi-geostrophic-streamfunction.md) satisfies $\lambda_\omega^2=(N^2/f_0^2)(k^2+\beta k/\omega)$ and $C_\omega=ig\mu r_0/(2\rho_0f_0\omega)$. With zero [streamfunction](stream-function.md) at the bottom, a positive $\lambda_\omega^2$ selects the decaying solution $C_\omega(e^{-\mu z}-e^{-\lambda_\omega z})/(\mu^2-\lambda_\omega^2)$. For $\beta,k>0$, the westward branch propagates vertically when $\omega_0<\beta/k$; a [radiation condition](radiation-condition.md) selects positive vertical [group velocity](group-velocity.md), not merely bounded amplitude. Coincident exponential exponents are handled by the finite limit $-C_\omega z e^{-\mu z}/(2\mu)$.
