# Piecewise-constant covariate approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous-time multi-state model](continuous-time-multi-state-model.md)

Approximate changing [covariates](covariate.md) by constant values on observation intervals. The [generator matrix](generator-matrix.md) is then constant on each interval, with [transition matrix](stochastic-matrix.md) $\exp(Q_j\Delta t_j)$. Multiplying the relevant entries gives a conditional [likelihood function](likelihood-function.md) for discretely observed states.
