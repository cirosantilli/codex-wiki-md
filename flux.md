# Flux

↑ **Parent:** [Conservation law](conservation-law.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flux)

A flux records transport of a quantity through a surface per unit time. A flux density $\mathbf j$ gives total transport rate $\int_S\mathbf j\cdot\mathbf n\,dS$, where $\mathbf n$ is the unit [normal vector](normal-vector.md). A [conservation law](conservation-law.md) relates its [divergence](divergence.md) to the local change in the quantity's density.
