# Generalized Langevin equation

↑ **Parent:** [Langevin dynamics](langevin-dynamics.md)

A [Langevin equation](langevin-dynamics.md) with a friction [memory kernel](memory-kernel.md), rather than only instantaneous friction. The equation couples the [velocity](velocity.md) to its earlier history through a causal [convolution](convolution.md). Equilibrium noise and friction are related by a [generalized fluctuation-dissipation relation](generalized-fluctuation-dissipation-relation.md). The name extends the [Langevin equation](langevin-dynamics.md) associated with [Paul Langevin](paul-langevin.md).
