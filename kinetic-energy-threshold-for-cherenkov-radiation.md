# Kinetic-energy threshold for Cherenkov radiation

↑ **Parent:** [Cherenkov radiation](cherenkov-radiation.md)

At a specified [frequency](frequency.md) with transparent-medium [phase](phase-waves.md) index $n>1$, [Cherenkov radiation](cherenkov-radiation.md) requires $\beta>1/n$. Inserting the threshold [speed](speed.md) into [relativistic kinetic energy](relativistic-kinetic-energy.md) gives $K_{\rm th}=mc^2[(1-n^{-2})^{-1/2}-1]$. Equality is the kinematic limit; the cone angle and emission vanish there. A dispersive material has different thresholds in different spectral intervals. The result uses [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md), not [group velocity](group-velocity.md).
