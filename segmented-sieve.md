# Segmented sieve

↑ **Parent:** [Number-theoretic sieve](number-theoretic-sieve.md)

A [number-theoretic sieve](number-theoretic-sieve.md) that processes a large search range in smaller [intervals](interval-mathematics.md). To find [primes](prime-number-split.md) in $[L,H]$, first generate the [primes](prime-number-split.md) at most $\sqrt H$, then mark their multiples in the current segment. Memory for the segment depends on its width rather than on $H$.
