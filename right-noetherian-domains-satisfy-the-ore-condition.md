# Right Noetherian domains satisfy the Ore condition

↑ **Parent:** [Ore theorem](ore-theorem.md)

If two nonzero principal [right ideals](right-ideal.md) in a [right Noetherian domain](right-noetherian-domain.md) had zero intersection, the sums $bR\oplus abR\oplus\cdots\oplus a^mbR$ would be strictly increasing. Directness follows by splitting the lowest term into $bR$ and the other terms into $aR$, then cancelling $a$ and repeating. This contradicts the [ascending chain condition](ascending-chain-condition.md). A nonzero common element $au=bv$ gives the right [Ore condition](ore-condition.md). The left version follows from the [opposite ring](opposite-ring.md). Consequently a [noncommutative domain](noncommutative-domain.md) [Noetherian](noetherian-ring.md) on both sides has a single [division ring](division-ring.md) of fractions with both left and right fraction presentations.
