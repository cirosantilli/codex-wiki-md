# Strong existence

↑ **Parent:** [Strong solution of a stochastic differential equation](strong-solution-of-a-stochastic-differential-equation.md)

A [stochastic differential equation](stochastic-differential-equation.md) has strong existence for specified coefficients and initial data if a [strong stochastic solution](strong-solution-of-a-stochastic-differential-equation.md) can be constructed on every prescribed stochastic basis carrying the required initial variable and driving [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md). This existence assertion is distinct from [pathwise uniqueness](pathwise-uniqueness.md) and from a definition of the solution itself.
