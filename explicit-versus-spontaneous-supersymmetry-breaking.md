# Explicit versus spontaneous supersymmetry breaking

↑ **Parent:** [Supersymmetry breaking](supersymmetry-breaking.md)

Explicit breaking changes the action so that the former [supercharges](supersymmetry-generator.md) are not conserved symmetries of the full Hamiltonian. Spontaneous breaking preserves the action and algebra but has a vacuum that some [supercharge](supersymmetry-generator.md) does not annihilate. [Energy positivity in global supersymmetry](energy-positivity-in-global-supersymmetry.md) then implies positive vacuum energy density, and a [Goldstino](goldstino.md) accompanies the broken symmetry. That exact-algebra energy argument does not apply to an arbitrarily explicitly broken Hamiltonian. The physical particle spectrum in a broken vacuum need not assemble into the finite equal-mass [supermultiplets](supermultiplet.md) of an invariant vacuum.
