# Sarvas formula

↑ **Parent:** [Spherical volume conductor model](spherical-volume-conductor-model.md)

For the exterior of a [spherical volume conductor model](spherical-volume-conductor-model.md), put $P=|\mathbf r-\mathbf r_0|$, $r=|\mathbf r|$ and $F=rP^2+P\mathbf r\cdot(\mathbf r-\mathbf r_0)$. The displayed [Sarvas formula](sarvas-formula.md) follows by taking the radial component of the [Geselowitz formula](geselowitz-formula.md) and integrating the [magnetic scalar potential](magnetic-scalar-potential.md) from infinity. It is independent of a spherically symmetric conductivity profile. The sign shown uses $\mathbf B=+\mu_0\nabla U/(4\pi)$; conventions defining a magnetic potential with a minus sign reverse $U$. [Sarvas's original paper](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3823129/) derives the spherical-conductor field and its consequences for the inverse problem.
