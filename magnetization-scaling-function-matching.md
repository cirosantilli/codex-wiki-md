# Magnetization scaling-function matching

↑ **Parent:** [Scaling hypothesis for critical phenomena](scaling-hypothesis-for-critical-phenomena.md)

Differentiate $f_s=|t|^{2-\alpha}g_{f,\pm}(h/|t|^\Delta)$ with respect to its [conjugate field](field-conjugate-to-an-order-parameter.md). A finite nonzero one-sided ordered-branch [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) gives $\beta=2-\alpha-\Delta$. To approach a finite critical isotherm as $t\to0$ at fixed small $h$, the large-argument function must behave as $g_m(x)\sim C\operatorname{sgn}(x)|x|^{1/\delta}$. Cancelling the remaining thermal power yields $\beta=\Delta/\delta$. An analytic background, branch selection and [thermodynamic limit](thermodynamic-limit.md) must be distinguished from the singular [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md) being differentiated.
