# Helly family

↑ **Parent:** [Set family](set-family.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helly_family)

A [Helly family](helly-family.md) of order $k$ is a [set family](set-family.md) for which every finite subfamily has nonempty total intersection whenever each of its subfamilies with at most $k$ members has nonempty intersection. Convex subtrees of a tree have Helly order two.
