# Falling cylinder near a wall

↑ **Parent:** [Torque-free cylinder in a lubrication gap](torque-free-cylinder-in-a-lubrication-gap.md)

For a cylinder falling parallel to a wall through a minimum gap $\epsilon a/2$, the [parabolic lubrication gap](parabolic-lubrication-gap.md) has length scale $a\sqrt\epsilon$. The leading tangential drag scales as $\mu U/\sqrt\epsilon$, while the leading rotational resistance scales as $\mu\Omega a/\sqrt\epsilon$.
