# Fixed point of boxed negated provability

↑ **Parent:** [Gödel-Löb logic](godel-lob-logic.md)

A formula provably equivalent to a boxed formula satisfies $F\to\Box F$ in [GL](godel-lob-logic.md). The displayed [fixed point](logical-fixed-point.md) then gives both $\Box\Box F$ and $\Box\neg\Box F$ under $F$, hence $\Box\bot$. Boxed explosion proves the reverse implication. Under arithmetic realization this is a formal inconsistency statement; [Sigma-1 soundness](sigma-1-soundness.md) excludes its provability, while second incompleteness excludes provability of its [negation](negation.md).
