# Beta-carbon elimination

↑ **Parent:** [Organometallic chemistry](organometallic-chemistry.md)

Cleavage of a [carbon](carbon.md)–[carbon](carbon.md) bond beta to a metal, often from a metal [alkoxide](alkoxide.md), coupled to [carbonyl](carbonyl-group.md) formation and transfer of a [carbon](carbon.md) fragment to the metal. For a tertiary benzylic [alkoxide](alkoxide.md) this can generate a [ketone](ketone.md) and a metal–aryl bond.
