# Integral field basis by denominator clearing

↑ **Parent:** [Integral closure](integral-closure.md)

If $A$ is an [integral domain](integral-domain.md) with [fraction field](field-of-fractions.md) $K$ and $L/K$ is finite, a $K$-basis of $L$ can be rescaled to consist of [integral elements](integral-element.md) over $A$. For a monic algebraic equation, choose $a\in A$ clearing the coefficient denominators; replacing $u$ by $au$ multiplies the coefficient of degree $r-j$ by $a^j$, putting it in $A$.
