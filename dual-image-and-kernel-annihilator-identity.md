# Dual image and kernel annihilator identity

↑ **Parent:** [Transpose of a linear map](transpose-of-a-linear-map.md)

For a [linear map](linear-map.md) between finite-dimensional [vector spaces](vector-space-split.md), the image of its [dual map](transpose-of-a-linear-map.md) is the [annihilator of a vector subspace](annihilator-of-a-vector-subspace.md) given by its kernel. Inclusion follows by composition. Conversely a [linear functional](linear-functional.md) vanishing on the kernel descends to the image and extends to the target by [basis extension](basis-extension.md). The identity and the [rank-nullity theorem](rank-nullity-theorem.md) show that a linear map and its dual have equal rank.
