# Separable-profile closure for an age-structured population

↑ **Parent:** [Age-structured population equation](age-structured-population-equation.md)

For $n_i(a,t)=N_i(t)f_i(a)$ with $\int f_i=1$, integrating the [age-structured population equation](age-structured-population-equation.md) replaces its exit integral by $\Lambda_iN_i$. For one-to-one transition from stage one to stage two and two-to-one reproduction from stage two to stage one, the population [matrix](matrix.md) is $\left(\begin{smallmatrix}-\Lambda_1&2\Lambda_2\\\Lambda_1&-\Lambda_2\end{smallmatrix}\right)$. This follows by integrating the age [derivative](derivative.md) and substituting the incoming [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md). Exact time-independent positive profiles also force a constant stage ratio and common exponential growth through those [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md). Thus the constant-coefficient closure is not an exact transient description for arbitrary age-dependent [hazard functions](hazard-function.md) and arbitrary [initial conditions](initial-condition.md); constant [hazard functions](hazard-function.md) are an important exception for the integrated populations.
