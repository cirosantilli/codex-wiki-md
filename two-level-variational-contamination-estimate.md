# Two-level variational contamination estimate

↑ **Parent:** [Variational method](variational-method.md)

If a trial [quantum state](quantum-state.md) is dominated by two [energy eigenstates](energy-eigenstate.md) of distinct [energies](energy-split.md) $E_a<E_b$, then its [Rayleigh quotient](rayleigh-quotient.md) is approximately their probability-weighted mean. Rearranging that mean gives the displayed relative admixture. Neglected states must make small contributions to the energy numerator as well as to [wavefunction normalization](wavefunction-normalization.md); small probabilities alone need not suffice if their energies are large.
