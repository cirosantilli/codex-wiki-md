# Bradley-Terry likelihood Hessian

↑ **Parent:** [Bradley-Terry score equation](bradley-terry-score-equation.md)

In log abilities, the negative [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) is a weighted [Graph Laplacian](laplacian-matrix.md) of the comparison graph. If that comparison graph is a [connected graph](connected-graph.md) and the abilities are finite, the quadratic form vanishes only for constant vectors. Thus the [log-likelihood](log-likelihood.md) is strictly concave after fixing the common additive constant. If the directed graph of observed wins is a [strongly connected directed graph](strong-connectivity.md), letting contrasts diverge forces at least one observed-win probability to zero, so the log-likelihood tends to negative infinity. A finite maximizer exists and is unique up to common scaling of abilities.
