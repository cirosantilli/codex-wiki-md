# Proper vertex coloring

↑ **Parent:** [Graph coloring](graph-coloring-split.md)

A proper vertex coloring assigns labels to the [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) of a [graph](graph-split.md) so that [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) joined by an [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) have different labels. Each [colour class](colour-class.md) is an [independent set](independent-set-graph-theory.md). The [chromatic number](chromatic-number.md) is the least number of labels needed for this form of [graph coloring](graph-coloring-split.md).
