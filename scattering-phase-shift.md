# Scattering phase shift

↑ **Parent:** [Partial-wave S-matrix](partial-wave-s-matrix.md)

For elastic scattering by a real central potential, the phase shift $\delta_l(k)$ measures the phase difference between the asymptotic radial wave in angular-momentum channel $l$ and the corresponding free wave. Its channel [Partial-wave S-matrix](partial-wave-s-matrix.md) is $S_l=e^{2i\delta_l}$.

The phase shift parametrizes elastic [partial-wave scattering](partial-wave-scattering.md), rather than general scattering phenomena.
