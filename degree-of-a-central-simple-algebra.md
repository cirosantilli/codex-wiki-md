# Degree of a central simple algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Central simple algebra](central-simple-algebra.md)

A [central simple algebra](central-simple-algebra.md) $A$ has dimension $d^2$ over its center. Its degree is this integer $d$. Over a splitting field it becomes $M_d$, so degree records the matrix size independently of the chosen splitting.
