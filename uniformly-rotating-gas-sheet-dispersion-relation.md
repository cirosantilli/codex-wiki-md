# Uniformly rotating gas-sheet dispersion relation

↑ **Parent:** [Gravitational instability of an astrophysical disk](gravitational-instability-of-an-astrophysical-disk-split.md)

For a homogeneous inviscid [razor-thin disk approximation](razor-thin-disk-approximation.md) with [solid-body rotation](solid-body-rotation.md) and barotropic [sound speed](speed-of-sound.md) $c$, the compressive wave branch obeys $\omega^2=c^2k^2-2\pi G\Sigma_0|k|+4\Omega^2$. The [razor-thin disk Poisson kernel](razor-thin-disk-poisson-kernel.md) supplies the [self-gravity](self-gravity.md) term, and the [radial epicyclic frequency](radial-epicyclic-frequency.md) is $2|\Omega|$. Growing modes have $\omega^2<0$; a separate zero-frequency balanced mode can also exist.
