# Diffusive broadening of electrophoretic bands

↑ **Parent:** [Electrophoresis](electrophoresis.md)

A narrow population band undergoing uniform [electrophoresis](electrophoresis.md) and ordinary [diffusion](diffusion.md) has mean position $x_0+\mu Et$ and [variance](variance-split.md) $\sigma_0^2+2Dt$. This follows from the [advection-diffusion equation](advection-diffusion-equation.md) by changing to the coordinate $x-\mu Et$. For two species with mobility difference $\Delta\mu$, their centre separation is $|\Delta\mu|Et$, whereas diffusion widths grow only as $\sqrt t$. Starting from point-like bands with common [diffusion coefficient](diffusion-coefficient.md) $D$, a required separation of $q$ standard deviations needs

$$
t\geq\frac{2q^2D}{(\Delta\mu E)^2}.
$$

A prescribed distance between centres alone is a resolution convention; finite loading widths and other dispersion mechanisms must be included when interpreting actual [electrophoresis](electrophoresis.md) data.
