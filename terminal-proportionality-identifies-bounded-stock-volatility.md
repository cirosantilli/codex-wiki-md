# Terminal proportionality identifies bounded stock volatility

↑ **Parent:** [Perfect positive correlation](perfect-positive-correlation.md)

For two strictly positive zero-rate stock [martingales](martingale-split.md) with bounded volatilities and the same initial value, proportional terminal prices must be equal because their [expectations](expected-value.md) coincide. [Conditional expectation](conditional-expectation.md) then makes the whole paths equal. Comparing their stochastic integrals by the [Itô isometry](ito-isometry.md) gives equality of the volatility coefficients almost everywhere in time and probability. [Perfect positive correlation](perfect-positive-correlation.md) alone does not provide the required proportionality.
