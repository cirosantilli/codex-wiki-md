# Rotating-disc mixed-layer depth law

↑ **Parent:** [Fixed-energy turbulent mixed layer](fixed-energy-turbulent-mixed-layer.md)

With constant interface thickness and the local [entrainment](fluid-entrainment.md) exponent $3/2$, the [fixed-energy turbulent mixed layer](fixed-energy-turbulent-mixed-layer.md) satisfies $\dot h=C\Omega^4R^6/(N^3\delta^{3/2}h^{7/2})$. Integration gives a linear growth law for $h^{9/2}$. Once an initial-depth offset is negligible, $h/R$ scales as $[\Omega^2R/(N^2\delta)]^{1/3}(\Omega t)^{2/9}$. The law applies before the mixed layer reaches the tank bottom and after the initial forcing transient.
