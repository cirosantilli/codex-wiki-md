# Clifford algebra bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Clifford algebra](clifford-algebra.md)

A [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) $E$ with a fibrewise [quadratic form](quadratic-form.md) $Q$ defines a bundle whose fibre over $x$ is the [Clifford algebra](clifford-algebra.md) $\operatorname{Cl}(E_x,Q_x)$. When the polar form is a [nondegenerate bilinear form](nondegenerate-bilinear-form.md), local orthonormal frames identify its fibres with a fixed [Clifford algebra](clifford-algebra.md), and orthogonal transition maps induce the algebra transition maps. A [Clifford module bundle](clifford-module-bundle.md) is a bundle of modules over these algebras, rather than the algebra bundle itself. For a [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md), use its [cotangent bundle](cotangent-bundle.md) and the metric [quadratic form](quadratic-form.md), with the sign convention specified.
