# Hardy-Littlewood approximation to the Riemann zeta function

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann zeta function](riemann-zeta-function.md)

For $s=\sigma+it$, $\sigma>0$, and $x\ge|t|/\pi$, the displayed approximation holds away from the [pole](pole.md). Apply the [Van der Corput sum-integral lemma](van-der-corput-sum-integral-lemma.md) to $-t\log w/(2\pi)$ on $w\ge x$, where its derivative has modulus at most one half. [Abel summation](abel-s-summation-formula.md) with $w^{-\sigma}$ converts the bounded unweighted discrepancy to $O(x^{-\sigma})$. [Locally uniform convergence](locally-uniform-convergence.md) of this weighted discrepancy extends the identity from $\sigma>1$ to $\sigma>0$. It turns estimates for finite [exponential sums](exponential-sum.md) into estimates for the [Riemann zeta function](riemann-zeta-function.md).
