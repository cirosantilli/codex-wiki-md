# Vaughan identity proof

↑ **Parent:** [Vaughan's identity](vaughan-s-identity.md)

For [Dirichlet convolution](dirichlet-convolution.md), let $\mathbf1(n)=1$ and $\epsilon(n)=1_{n=1}$. [Prime factorization](fundamental-theorem-of-arithmetic.md) gives $\mu*\mathbf1=\epsilon$ and $\Lambda*\mathbf1=\log$. With subscripts denoting truncation at $U,V$,

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mu*\log&=\Lambda,\\
\mu_{>U}*\mathbf1*\Lambda_{>V}
&=(\epsilon-\mu_{\leq U}*\mathbf1)*\Lambda_{>V}\\
&=\Lambda_{>V}-\mu_{\leq U}*\log+\mu_{\leq U}*\mathbf1*\Lambda_{\leq V}.
\end{aligned}
$$

Rearranging proves the [Vaughan identity](vaughan-s-identity.md).
