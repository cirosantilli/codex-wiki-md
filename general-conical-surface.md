# General conical surface

↑ **Parent:** [Parametric surface](parametric-surface.md)

A general conical surface is swept out by straight generators through one apex $A$. An invertible [affine map](affine-map.md) sends it to another such surface, since it sends each generator to a line through the transformed apex. A [projective transformation](projective-linear-transformation.md) preserves the projective concurrence of generators, but can send the apex to infinity, producing parallel generators in the affine chart. Therefore finite-apex cones and cylinders are not separately projectively invariant Euclidean classes.
