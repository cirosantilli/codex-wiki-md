# Completely positive cone

↑ **Parent:** [Convex cone](convex-cone.md)

The [convex cone](convex-cone.md) of [completely positive matrices](completely-positive-matrix.md):

$$
\operatorname{CP}_n=\operatorname{cone}\{xx^T:x\in\mathbb R_{\geq0}^n\}.
$$

It is a [closed convex cone](closed-convex-cone.md) and the [dual cone](dual-cone.md) of the [copositive cone](copositive-cone.md) under the [Frobenius inner product](frobenius-inner-product.md). The finite-sum definition imposes no closure by fiat; [closedness of the completely positive cone](closedness-of-the-completely-positive-cone.md) supplies that fact.
