# Affine map

↑ **Parent:** [Affine space](affine-space.md)

A map between [affine spaces](affine-space.md) whose difference map is linear. After choosing origins it is $x\mapsto Ax+b$, where $A$ is its [linear part of an affine map](linear-part-of-an-affine-map.md) and $b$ its translation. This definition allows noninvertible maps; invertibility requires $A$ invertible.
