# Cosmological isotropy

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmological principle](cosmological-principle.md)

[Cosmological isotropy](cosmological-isotropy.md) means that coarse-grained spatial observations or their statistics are unchanged by rotations about an observer. In an [FRW metric](friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md) it holds about every comoving location. [cosmological isotropy](cosmological-isotropy.md) about a single location is weaker than [spatial homogeneity](spatial-homogeneity.md): a radially inhomogeneous model centred on that location can also have an isotropic sky.
