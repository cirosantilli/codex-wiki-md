<h1 id="1-3-dipolar-cycloaddition">1,3-dipolar cycloaddition</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Organic reaction](organic-reaction.md)

A [cycloaddition](cycloaddition.md) joining a three-atom 1,3-dipole to a two-atom unsaturated unit and forming a five-membered ring. The atom order of the dipole fixes which heteroatoms become adjacent.
