# Two-prime formal-kernel test for nontorsion

↑ **Parent:** [Formal kernel of a minimal Weierstrass equation](formal-kernel-of-a-minimal-weierstrass-equation.md)

A nonidentity rational point in the [formal kernel of a minimal Weierstrass equation](formal-kernel-of-a-minimal-weierstrass-equation.md) at two distinct [primes](prime-number.md) has infinite [order of a group element](order-of-a-group-element.md). Indeed, the [prime-to-residue-characteristic multiplication on a formal group](prime-to-residue-characteristic-multiplication-on-a-formal-group.md) implies that a finite order in the kernel at $p$ must be a power of $p$. Membership in the kernel at a different prime $q$ also forces a power of $q$. The only common possibility is order one, contradicting nonidentity. For an integral model, negative [valuations](valuation.md) of the affine coordinates provide a convenient kernel-membership test.
