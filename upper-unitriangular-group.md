# Upper unitriangular group

↑ **Parent:** [General linear group over a finite field](general-linear-group-over-a-finite-field.md)

The upper unitriangular group consists of upper triangular matrices with every diagonal entry equal to one. Over $\mathbb F_p$ it has order $p^{n(n-1)/2}$ and is a [Sylow subgroup](sylow-subgroup.md) of $GL_n(\mathbb F_p)$.

Its elements form the upper triangular, diagonal-one subset of [triangular matrices](triangular-matrix.md).
