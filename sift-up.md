# Sift up

↑ **Parent:** [Binary heap](binary-heap.md)

After inserting or lowering a key in a minimum-oriented [binary heap](binary-heap.md), compare it with its [parent in a rooted tree](parent-in-a-rooted-tree.md). Exchange them while the moving key is smaller, then continue at its new index. Stop at the [root of a tree](root-of-a-tree.md) or when the [heap order](heap-order.md) is satisfied. This repair assumes the rest of the [binary heap](binary-heap.md) was valid before the insertion or key decrease.
