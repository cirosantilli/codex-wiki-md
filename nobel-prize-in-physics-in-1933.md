# Nobel Prize in Physics in 1933

↑ **Parent:** [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics-split.md)

Awarded jointly to [Erwin Schrödinger](erwin-schrodinger.md) and [Paul Dirac](paul-dirac.md) for developments in atomic theory.[Joint award: Erwin Schrödinger and Paul Adrien Maurice Dirac, each receiving one half](the-nobel-prize-in-physics-1933-by-nobel-prize-outreach.md)

The [Schrödinger equation](schrodinger-equation.md) and relativistic theory of the [electron](electron.md) are two routes through that work. The [Davisson–Germer experiment](davisson-germer-experiment.md) supplies related experimental evidence for [matter waves](matter-wave.md).
