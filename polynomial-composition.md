# Polynomial composition

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial](polynomial-split.md)

For polynomials $p$ and $q$ over a common [commutative ring](commutative-ring.md), their composition is $(p\circ q)(x)=p(q(x))$. It substitutes the whole polynomial $q$ for each occurrence of the indeterminate in $p$, rather than multiplying their coefficient arrays.
