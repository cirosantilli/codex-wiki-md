# Seifert fiber

↑ **Parent:** [Seifert fibered space](seifert-fibered-space.md)

A Seifert fiber is one of the circles of a [Seifert fibered space](seifert-fibered-space.md). A regular fiber has a product neighborhood; an exceptional fiber has a fibered [solid torus](solid-torus.md) neighborhood with multiplicity greater than one.
