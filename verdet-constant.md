# Verdet constant

↑ **Parent:** [Faraday effect](faraday-effect.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verdet_constant)

The [Verdet constant](verdet-constant.md) is the coefficient relating [Faraday rotation](faraday-effect.md) to the [magnetic flux density](magnetic-field-split.md) path integral. Its [SI](international-system-of-units.md) units are $\mathrm{rad\,T^{-1}\,m^{-1}}$. It depends on material, [wavelength](wavelength.md) and [temperature](temperature-split.md); a sign requires the stated viewing convention.[RP Photonics Encyclopedia, Faraday Effect, Rüdiger Paschotta: What is the Faraday Effect? and Verdet Constants of Materials](rp-photonics-encyclopedia-by-rudiger-paschotta/faraday-effect.md)

The name commemorates [Émile Verdet](emile-verdet.md). For nonuniform material or [temperature](temperature-split.md), use $\theta=\int V(\ell)B_\parallel(\ell)\,d\ell$ when the local scalar description remains valid. A single central [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) reading cannot generally replace this weighted [integral](integral-split.md). Strong additional linear [birefringence](birefringence.md) or appreciable [circular dichroism](circular-dichroism.md) requires following the full [polarization of an electromagnetic wave](polarization-of-an-electromagnetic-wave.md), rather than treating the output as merely a rotated line.
