# Kaon CP eigenstates

↑ **Parent:** [Neutral kaon mixing](neutral-kaon-mixing.md)

The [CP eigenstates](cp-eigenstate.md) of neutral [kaons](kaon.md) depend on a flavor-state phase convention. Using $CP|K^0\rangle=-|\bar K^0\rangle$, set $|K_1\rangle=(|K^0\rangle-|\bar K^0\rangle)/\sqrt2$ and $|K_2\rangle=(|K^0\rangle+|\bar K^0\rangle)/\sqrt2$. They are respectively [CP](cp-symmetry.md) even and odd. Reversing the flavor-exchange sign reverses which written sum is even; it does not change the [CP](cp-symmetry.md) eigenvalues. With [CP violation](cp-violation.md), the physical short- and long-lived states need not equal these [CP eigenstates](cp-eigenstate.md); [kaon CP mixing parameter](kaon-cp-mixing-parameter.md) and [neutral-kaon CP impurity and two-pion decay](neutral-kaon-cp-impurity-and-two-pion-decay.md) describe the mixtures.
