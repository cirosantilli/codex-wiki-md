# Normalised elemental release

↑ **Parent:** [Waste-glass corrosion](waste-glass-corrosion.md)

The solution-released mass of an element divided by its initial [glass](glass.md) [mass fraction](mass-fraction.md) and the exposed [glass](glass.md) area: $NL_e=m_e/(f_eS)$. It has units of glass-equivalent mass per area; it does not alone establish congruent dissolution.
