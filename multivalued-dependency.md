# Multivalued dependency

↑ **Parent:** [Relational schema](relational-schema.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multivalued_dependency)

For [database tuples](database-tuple.md) agreeing on $X$, requires the combinations obtained by swapping their values on $Y\setminus X$ and on the remaining [database attributes](database-attribute.md) to occur too. A [functional dependency](functional-dependency.md) implies the corresponding [multivalued dependency](multivalued-dependency.md); complementation exchanges $Y$ with the remaining [database attributes](database-attribute.md). A [multivalued dependency](multivalued-dependency.md) is trivial if $Y\subseteq X$ or $X\cup Y$ is the full [relational schema](relational-schema.md).
