# Additive polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial](polynomial-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Additive_polynomial)

Over a [field](field.md) of characteristic $p$, additive [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) are sums of $p$-power monomials, $L(X)=\sum_i a_iX^{p^i}$. They act linearly over the prime [field](field.md). The identity follows from the [Frobenius endomorphism](frobenius-endomorphism.md); the converse follows by comparing mixed binomial coefficients. Their zero sets are additive groups, and a nonzero linear coefficient makes all roots simple.
