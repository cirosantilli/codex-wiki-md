# Integer-valued hypothesis in the tail-sum formula

↑ **Parent:** [Tail-sum formula](tail-sum-formula.md)

For a nonnegative integer-valued variable, $X=\sum_{m\ge0}\mathbf1_{\{X>m\}}$ and the [tail-sum formula](tail-sum-formula.md) gives $\mathbb E X=\sum_{m\ge0}\mathbb P(X>m)$. For arbitrary positive real values, that integer-indexed sum equals $\mathbb E\lceil X\rceil$ instead. Two independent constants equal to $3/2$ demonstrate that the product of such tail sums need not equal the tail sum of their product: the two sides are four and three.
