# Closed linear span

↑ **Parent:** [Linear span](linear-span.md)

In a [normed vector space](normed-vector-space-split.md), the closed linear span of $S$ is the [closure](closure-topology.md) of its [linear span](linear-span.md) in the [norm topology](norm-topology.md). It includes [limits](limit-of-a-function.md) of finite [linear combinations](linear-combination.md). In a [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md), an infinite [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) spans in this closed sense, allowing norm-convergent series in addition to finite sums.
