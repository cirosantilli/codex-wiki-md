# Linked list node

↑ **Parent:** [Linked list](linked-list.md)

One record holding an item and the references that connect it into a [linked list](linked-list.md). In the singly linked examples, its `next` identifies the successor or is `null`. A [linked list node](linked-list-node.md)'s position in the [sequence](sequence.md) can change when links change, without moving the [linked list node](linked-list-node.md) in [computer memory](computer-memory-split.md).
