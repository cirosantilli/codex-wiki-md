# Line graph of a regular graph is Eulerian

↑ **Parent:** [Line graph](line-graph.md)

If $G$ is connected and $r$-regular, then $L(G)$ is connected and every one of its vertices has degree $2r-2$. The [Euler circuit criterion](euler-circuit-criterion.md) therefore applies.
