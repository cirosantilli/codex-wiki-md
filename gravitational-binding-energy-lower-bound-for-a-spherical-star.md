# Gravitational binding-energy lower bound for a spherical star

↑ **Parent:** [Gravitational binding energy](gravitational-binding-energy.md)

If all mass of a spherical body lies within radius $R$, then $r(m)\le R$ in the shell-assembly [integral](integral-split.md) for its [gravitational binding energy](gravitational-binding-energy.md). Therefore $|\Omega|=\int_0^M Gm/r(m)dm\ge(G/R)\int_0^M m\,dm=GM^2/(2R)$. The inequality is strict for an extended interior mass distribution; a thin shell gives equality. Dividing by a stipulated [luminosity](luminosity.md) gives a lower bound on that reservoir-to-luminosity ratio. A [Kelvin-Helmholtz cooling time](kelvin-helmholtz-cooling-time.md) also needs a structure model and the accessible fraction of gravitational release.
