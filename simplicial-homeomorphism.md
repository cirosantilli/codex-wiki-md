# Simplicial homeomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Simplicial map](simplicial-map.md)

A simplicial homeomorphism is the [homeomorphism](homeomorphism.md) of [geometric realizations](geometric-realization-of-a-simplicial-complex.md) induced by an [isomorphism](isomorphism.md) of [simplicial complexes](simplicial-complex.md): a [bijection](bijection.md) of their vertex sets such that a finite vertex set is a [simplex](simplex.md) exactly when its image is a [simplex](simplex.md). The map acts linearly in each [barycentric coordinate system](barycentric-coordinate-system.md). It and its inverse are [continuous](continuous-function.md) by the realization [topology](topology-split.md). Between the boundaries of two triangles, any [bijection](bijection.md) of the three vertices extends linearly on edges to a simplicial homeomorphism. This ensures that the edge identifications agree at common endpoints.
