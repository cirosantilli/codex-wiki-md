# Backward-sign Airy half-line global relation

↑ **Parent:** [Airy equation](airy-equation.md)

For the [Airy equation](airy-equation.md) with opposite dispersive sign, $q_t-q_{xxx}=0$ on $x>0$, let $Q$ be the initial [Half-range Fourier transform](half-range-fourier-transform.md) and $F_j(k,t)=\int_0^t e^{ik^3s}\partial_x^jq(0,s)\,ds$. Three [integrations by parts](integration-by-parts.md) give the displayed [global relation](global-relation-for-a-linear-boundary-value-problem.md). The spatial transform is [holomorphic](complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md) for $\operatorname{Im}k<0$, whereas the [finite-time spectral boundary transforms](finite-time-spectral-boundary-transform.md) are [entire functions](entire-function.md). The symmetry $k\mapsto\alpha k$, $\alpha^3=1$, preserves their time exponent.
