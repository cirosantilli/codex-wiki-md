# Harmonic balance

↑ **Parent:** [Method of multiple scales](method-of-multiple-scales.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harmonic_balance)

Harmonic balance substitutes a finite [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md) into a differential equation and projects the residual onto the retained harmonics. It determines their amplitudes and phases. A nonlinear equation generally generates additional harmonics, so vanishing of the projected residual alone does not make the finite series an exact solution. In a [weakly nonlinear expansion](weakly-nonlinear-expansion.md), nonresonant generated harmonics can be found at the next order by solving their linear equations.
