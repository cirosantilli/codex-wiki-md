# Molecular-weight scaling of an ideal-chain gyration radius

↑ **Parent:** [Radius of gyration](radius-of-gyration.md)

For a long [ideal chain](ideal-chain.md) of $N$ independent statistical steps of [length](length.md) $b$, $R_g^2=Nb^2/6$. With fixed chain chemistry and $b$, the [molar mass](molar-mass.md) $M$ is proportional to $N$. Thus multiplying [molar mass](molar-mass.md) by $s$ multiplies the [radius of gyration](radius-of-gyration.md) by $\sqrt{s}$; [solvent quality](solvent-quality.md) can change both the statistical segment [length](length.md) and the size exponent.
