# Genotype

↑ **Parent:** [Genetics](genetics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genotype)

A [genotype](genotype.md) records the inherited [alleles](allele.md) carried by an individual at one or more [genetic loci](genetic-locus.md). At a two-copy [diploid](diploid.md) [genetic locus](genetic-locus.md) with [alleles](allele.md) $A,a$, the possible unordered [genotypes](genotype.md) are $AA,Aa,aa$. The first and last are [homozygotes](homozygote.md); the middle is a [heterozygote](heterozygote.md). The notation records inherited states, without yet asserting their [phenotypes](phenotype.md). See [NHGRI: genotype](https://www.genome.gov/genetics-glossary/genotype).

An unordered $Aa$ combines the two parental orders $Aa$ and $aA$. This small bookkeeping point explains the factor two in [Hardy-Weinberg proportions](hardy-weinberg-principle.md) and in an $Aa\times Aa$ cross. If parental origin or arrangement across several [genetic loci](genetic-locus.md) matters, a [phase-known genotype](phase-known-genotype.md) retains extra information about the two [haplotypes](haplotype.md).
