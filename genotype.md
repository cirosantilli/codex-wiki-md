# Genotype

↑ **Parent:** [Genetics](genetics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genotype)

A genotype records the inherited [alleles](allele.md) carried by an individual at one or more [genetic loci](genetic-locus.md). For a diploid autosomal locus it is an unordered allele pair unless parental origin or chromosome phase is specified.
