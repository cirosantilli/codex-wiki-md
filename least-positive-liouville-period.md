# Least positive Liouville period

↑ **Parent:** [Rational Lagrangian submanifold](rational-lagrangian-submanifold.md)

For a nonzero discrete Liouville period group, this is its positive generator. In Euclidean symplectic space, the same group is obtained from signed symplectic areas of disks with boundary on $L$, by the [Stokes theorem](stokes-theorem.md) and contractibility of the ambient space. A product of circles of common radius $r$ is a [rational Lagrangian submanifold](rational-lagrangian-submanifold.md) with $\gamma(L)=\pi r^2$. Translation changes a primitive only by an exact form and hence leaves the period group unchanged.
