# Bilinear cancellation for badly approximable phases

↑ **Parent:** [Bilinear sum](bilinear-sum.md)

Let $\alpha$ be a [badly approximable number](badly-approximable-number.md), and sum $B=\sum_{D<d\leq2D}\sum_{W<w\leq2W,dw\leq X}a_db_we(\alpha dw)$ with $|a_d|\leq\tau(d)$ and $|b_w|\leq1$. The [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md), [divisor-square summatory bound](divisor-square-summatory-bound.md) and geometric sums give $|B|^2\ll D\log^3X(DW+W\sum_{h\leq W}\min(D,\|h\alpha\|^{-1}))$. Separated rotations bound the last sum by $O_\alpha(W\log(2W))$, giving the displayed estimate. A hyperbolic cutoff remains valid because the allowed first-variable values after expansion form an interval.
