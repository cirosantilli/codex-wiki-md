# Filtered cochain complex

↑ **Parent:** [Cochain complex](cochain-complex.md)

A [cochain complex](cochain-complex.md) with a decreasing filtration by [cochain subcomplexes](cochain-subcomplex.md). A filtration finite in each degree is bounded in the degreewise sense; uniform bounds $F^aC=C$ and $F^bC=0$ are sufficient. The associated [spectral sequence](spectral-sequence.md) starts with the graded quotient complexes.
