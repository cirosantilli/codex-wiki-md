# Complex K-theory of complex projective space

↑ **Parent:** [Topological K-theory](topological-k-theory.md)

If $t=1-[\overline\gamma]$ for the tautological complex line bundle, then

$$
K^0(\mathbb{CP}^n)\cong\mathbb Z[t]/(t^{n+1}),
\qquad
K^{-1}(\mathbb{CP}^n)=0.
$$
