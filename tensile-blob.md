# Tensile blob

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer physics](polymer-physics.md)

A [tensile blob](tensile-blob.md) is the scale below which thermal conformations of a stretched [polymer](polymer-split.md) are weakly disturbed by tension $f$. Balancing stretching [work](work.md) $f\xi_t$ with $k_BT$ gives $\xi_t\sim k_BT/f$. For free-chain size law $R\sim bg^\nu$, each blob contains $g_t\sim(\xi_t/b)^{1/\nu}$ [monomers](monomer.md). A stretched string of blobs has extension $z\sim(n/g_t)\xi_t$ and stretching [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md) of order $k_BTn/g_t$. This description requires blobs larger than individual statistical segments and smaller than the unperturbed coil.
