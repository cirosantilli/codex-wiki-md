# Finite-dimensional real division-algebra classification

↑ **Parent:** [Division algebra](division-algebra.md)

A finite-dimensional associative [division algebra](division-algebra.md) over $\mathbb R$ is one of the three displayed algebras. A nonreal element has an irreducible quadratic [minimal polynomial](minimal-polynomial.md) and yields $i^2=-1$. Its centralizer is a [division algebra](division-algebra.md) over central $\mathbb C$, hence is $\mathbb C$ by splitting [minimal polynomials](minimal-polynomial.md). Decompose the whole algebra into the plus and minus eigenspaces of conjugation by $i$. If the minus space is zero, the algebra is $\mathbb C$; otherwise it is $\mathbb Cj$, since multiplying by $j^{-1}$ takes that space into the centralizer. One has $jz=\overline z j$ and $j^2\in\mathbb R$. Division forces $j^2<0$, which can be rescaled to $-1$, yielding $\mathbb H$. Of these, only $\mathbb R,\mathbb H$ have center $\mathbb R$.
