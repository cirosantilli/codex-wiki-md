# Best uniform approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Uniform approximation](uniform-approximation-split.md)

Given a family $A$ of approximating [functions](function-split.md), a best uniform approximation to $f$ is an element $p\in A$ satisfying

$$
\lVert f-p\rVert_\infty=\inf_{q\in A}\lVert f-q\rVert_\infty.
$$
