# Club sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Club set](club-set.md)

A sequence $\langle C_\delta\rangle$ in which $C_\delta$ is closed and unbounded in $\delta$. At a successor ordinal its predecessor is a club singleton. Such sequences define [minimal walks along a club sequence](minimal-walk-along-a-club-sequence.md).
