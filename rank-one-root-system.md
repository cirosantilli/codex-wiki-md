# Rank-one root system

↑ **Parent:** [Root system](root-system.md)

A reduced [root system](root-system.md) of rank one is $Φ=\{\alpha,-\alpha\}$. Its [fundamental weight](fundamental-weight.md) is $\alpha/2$, and its [Weyl group](weyl-group.md) has two elements. Its ordinary [Dynkin diagram](dynkin-diagram.md) has one node; adjoining $\alpha_0=-\alpha$ gives affine [Cartan matrix](cartan-matrix.md) $\begin{pmatrix}2&-2\\-2&2\end{pmatrix}$, drawn as a double bond between equal-length roots.
