# Mixture identifiability

↑ **Parent:** [Finite mixture model](finite-mixture-model.md)

Simultaneously applying a [permutation](permutation.md) to the [mixture weights](mixture-weight.md) and [mixture components](mixture-component.md) leaves $f$ unchanged. Thus [identifiability](identifiability.md) of named components requires a convention, such as ordering distinct component [means](expected-value-split.md); [label switching](label-switching.md) describes the associated inferential issue.[§21.1.5, printed pp. 444-445: observational equivalence and label degeneracy](advanced-data-analysis-from-an-elementary-point-of-view-2015-draft-by-cosma-rohilla-shalizi/mixture-models.md)

Even after choosing label names, identical [mixture components](mixture-component.md) reveal only their combined [mixture weight](mixture-weight.md), and an inactive [mixture component](mixture-component.md) has undetermined [statistical parameters](statistical-parameter.md). Adding [mixture components](mixture-component.md) increases flexibility but can cause [overfitting](overfitting.md); judge predictions using [cross-validation](cross-validation.md), rather than interpreting every fitted component as a discovered population.[§21.1.6, printed pp. 445-446: probabilistic clustering and evaluation on future data](advanced-data-analysis-from-an-elementary-point-of-view-2015-draft-by-cosma-rohilla-shalizi/mixture-models.md)
