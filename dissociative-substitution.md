# Dissociative substitution

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical reaction](chemical-reaction-split.md)

A [dissociative substitution](dissociative-substitution.md) loses a ligand before binding the incoming replacement. A saturated 18-electron carbonyl can first form a 16-electron intermediate; the dissociation barrier and intermediate rearrangement influence rates and product geometry.
