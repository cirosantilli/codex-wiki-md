# Three-to-one mass double-pendulum modes

↑ **Parent:** [Normal modes of a double pendulum](normal-modes-of-a-double-pendulum.md)

An upper [mass](mass.md) $3m$ and lower [mass](mass.md) $m$ joined by equal [light](light.md) strings of length $\ell$ have small-angle [mass](mass.md) and [stiffness matrices](stiffness-matrix.md) $m\ell^2\left(\begin{smallmatrix}4&1\\1&1\end{smallmatrix}\right)$ and $mg\ell\left(\begin{smallmatrix}4&0\\0&1\end{smallmatrix}\right)$. The squared [frequencies](frequency.md) are $2g/(3\ell)$ and $2g/\ell$, with angular eigenvectors $(1,2)$ and $(1,-2)$ respectively. Equal-angle release decomposes with coefficients $3\alpha/4$ and $\alpha/4$. The angles are measured from vertical, not between strings.
