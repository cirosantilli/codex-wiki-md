# Triangular-profile wall line plume

↑ **Parent:** [Wall line plume](wall-line-plume.md)

In a triangular-profile wall line plume, axial velocity and [reduced gravity](reduced-gravity-split.md) decrease linearly from their wall values to zero across the plume width $b$. If the wall speed is $W$, its volume and kinematic momentum fluxes per unit span are $V=bW/2$ and $J=bW^2/3$.
