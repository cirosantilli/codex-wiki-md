# Electric dipole operator

↑ **Parent:** [Electric-dipole interaction](electric-dipole-interaction.md)

The [electric dipole operator](electric-dipole-operator.md) is the charge-weighted position operator of a system. It is odd under [parity](parity.md) and transforms as a rank-one [spherical tensor operator](spherical-tensor-operator.md). In the nonrelativistic electric-dipole approximation it acts on spatial coordinates without directly changing spin, explaining the parity, angular-momentum and approximate spin selection rules.
