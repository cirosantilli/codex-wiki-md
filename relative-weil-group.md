# Relative Weil group

↑ **Parent:** [Weil group](weil-group.md)

For a Galois extension $L/K$ of non-Archimedean local fields, the relative Weil group consists of the automorphisms whose residue action is an integral power of [Frobenius](frobenius-automorphism.md). It fits into

$$
1\longrightarrow I(L/K)\longrightarrow W(L/K)\longrightarrow\mathbb Z\longrightarrow1
$$

when the residue extension is the full algebraic extension.
