# Deoxyribonucleic acid

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer](polymer.md)

A [deoxyribonucleic acid](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) molecule is a nucleotide [polymer](polymer.md) that stores genetic information. Its double-stranded form is often modeled mechanically as a [worm-like chain](worm-like-chain.md), with elasticity and effective excluded volume depending on solvent conditions.
