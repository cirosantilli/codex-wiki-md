# Twistor line

↑ **Parent:** [Twistor correspondence](twistor-correspondence.md)

The projective line associated to a two-plane $W_x$ representing a point of [complexified compactified Minkowski space](complexified-compactified-minkowski-space.md). Two distinct [twistor lines](twistor-line.md) intersect precisely when their spacetime points are null separated. On the Lorentzian real slice, the line lies entirely in [projective null twistor space](projective-null-twistor-space.md) and parametrizes the null geodesics through the point.
