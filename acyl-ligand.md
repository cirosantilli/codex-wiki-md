# Acyl ligand

↑ **Parent:** [Ligand in coordination chemistry](ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md)

A carbon-bound group $\mathrm{-C(O)R}$, formally anionic and normally a two-electron donor in ionic [electron counting](electron-counting.md). It differs from a neutral [carbonyl ligand](carbonyl-ligand.md) $\mathrm{CO}$.
