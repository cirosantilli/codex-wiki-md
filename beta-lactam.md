# Beta-lactam

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md)

A four-membered cyclic [amide](amide.md). Hydrolytic opening of this ring is a major mechanism by which [beta-lactamases](beta-lactamase.md) inactivate susceptible antibiotics.
