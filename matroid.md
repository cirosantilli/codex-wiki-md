# Matroid

↑ **Parent:** [Combinatorics](combinatorics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matroid)

A [matroid](matroid.md) on a finite [set](set-split.md) $E$ consists of a nonempty family of independent [subsets](subset.md) closed under taking [subsets](subset.md) and satisfying exchange: if independent [sets](set-split.md) $I,J$ obey $|I|<|J|$, some $j\in J\setminus I$ has $I\cup\{j\}$ independent. [Linear independence](linear-independence.md) of [vectors](vector.md) is the motivating example. A [pregeometry](pregeometry.md) is a finitary closure-theoretic extension to possibly infinite [sets](set-split.md).
