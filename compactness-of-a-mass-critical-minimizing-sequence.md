# Compactness of a mass-critical minimizing sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Sharp Gagliardo-Nirenberg inequality](sharp-gagliardo-nirenberg-inequality.md)

Suppose $\|u_n\|_2=\|Q\|_2$, $\|\nabla u_n\|_2=\|\nabla Q\|_2$, and $E(u_n)\to0$. The [profile decomposition modulo translations](profile-decomposition-modulo-translations.md) and the sharp inequality force exactly one nonzero profile: splitting the mass would make the sharp inequality strict. Hence some translations $u_n(\cdot+x_n)$ converge strongly in $H^1$, and therefore in $L^{2+4/d}$, to an optimizer.
