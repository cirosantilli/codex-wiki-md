# Outgoing Green function for the three-dimensional Helmholtz equation

↑ **Parent:** [Helmholtz equation](helmholtz-equation.md)

The outgoing [Green function](green-s-function.md) satisfies $(\Delta+k^2)G_k=-\delta_{\mathbf r'}$ and the [Sommerfeld radiation condition](sommerfeld-radiation-condition.md). For time dependence $e^{-i\omega t}$, its radial phase travels outward. Away from its source it solves the homogeneous [Helmholtz equation](helmholtz-equation.md). At large $r$, $G_k=e^{ikr}e^{-ik\widehat{\mathbf r}\cdot\mathbf r'}/(4\pi r)+O(r^{-2})$.
