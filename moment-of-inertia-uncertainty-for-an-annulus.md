# Moment-of-inertia uncertainty for an annulus

↑ **Parent:** [Propagation of uncertainty](propagation-of-uncertainty.md)

For axial [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) $I=m(a^2+b^2)/2$, linear sensitivity to [mass](mass.md) and outer/inner radius is $dI=(a^2+b^2)dm/2+ma\,da+mb\,db$. [Independent](independent-random-variables.md) [standard uncertainties](standard-uncertainty.md) give $\sigma_I^2=[(a^2+b^2)\sigma_m/2]^2+(ma\sigma_a)^2+(mb\sigma_b)^2$. Correlated measurements instead require the full [covariance](covariance.md) quadratic form. For deterministic bounds, add the absolute linear contributions rather than their squares.
