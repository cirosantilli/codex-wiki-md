<h1 id="1/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A real square [orthogonal matrix](../../../../../orthogonal-matrix.md) $Q$ satisfies $\boxed{Q^TQ=QQ^T=I}$. Equivalently its columns form an [orthonormal basis](../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md). In particular it preserves the [Euclidean norm](../../../../../euclidean-norm.md), since $\|Qx\|_2^2=x^TQ^TQx=\|x\|_2^2$.
