# Gaussian orthogonal ensemble

↑ **Parent:** [Random matrix](random-matrix.md)

In the unscaled variance-one convention, a Gaussian orthogonal ensemble [matrix](matrix.md) $M$ is a real [symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md) whose upper-triangular entries are [independent](independent-random-variables.md), with $M_{ij}\sim N(0,1)$ for $i<j$ and $M_{ii}\sim N(0,2)$. Its [joint probability density function](joint-probability-density.md) relative to the upper-triangular coordinates is proportional to $\exp[-\operatorname{tr}(M^2)/4]$. The scaled convention uses $M/\sqrt n$; the scaling must be stated. If $X$ has independent standard [normal](normal-distribution.md) entries, $(X+X^T)/\sqrt2$ realizes the unscaled convention. Expanding in the entries of $X$ gives the [covariance](covariance.md) identity $\mathbb E[M_{ij}M_{kl}]=\delta_{ik}\delta_{jl}+\delta_{il}\delta_{jk}$, with [Kronecker deltas](kronecker-delta.md).
