# Guanosine diphosphate

↑ **Parent:** [Nucleotide](nucleotide.md)

[guanosine diphosphate](guanosine-diphosphate.md), abbreviated [GDP](guanosine-diphosphate.md), is the diphosphate form of a guanine nucleotide. Exchange between [GDP](guanosine-diphosphate.md) and [GTP](guanosine-triphosphate.md), rather than receptor-catalyzed phosphorylation of GDP, controls the usual [G protein](g-protein.md) switch.
