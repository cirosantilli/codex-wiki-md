# Light curve

↑ **Parent:** [Astrophysics](astrophysics-split.md)

A light curve is the brightness of an astronomical source as a function of time, expressed as flux or [apparent magnitude](apparent-magnitude.md). A periodic light curve repeats after a characteristic time; [Gaussian process](gaussian-process.md) models can reconstruct irregularly sampled light curves and propagate observational uncertainty.
