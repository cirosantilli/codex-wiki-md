# Letter to Richard Bentley of 25 February 1693 by Isaac Newton

Primary manuscript transcription, Trinity College Library 189.R.4.47, folios 7–8. Folios 7r–7v reject innate, unmediated gravity and leave the agent's material or immaterial nature open. The dual-year date accommodates the historical English calendar convention.
