# Direct current

↑ **Parent:** [Electric current](electric-current.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Direct_current)

A [direct current](direct-current.md) is an [electric current](electric-current.md) that flows in one direction; its magnitude may vary with [time](time-in-physics.md). A constant or steady [electric current](electric-current.md) additionally has a value independent of [time](time-in-physics.md). Thus a [direct current](direct-current.md) need not be constant. The steady [direct current](direct-current.md) model of a [voltage divider](voltage-divider.md) assumes a constant input [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) and fixed [electrical resistances](electrical-resistance.md).
