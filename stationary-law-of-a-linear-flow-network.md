# Stationary law of a linear flow network

↑ **Parent:** [Linear flow network](linear-flow-network.md)

For the [proportionally fair allocation on a linear flow network](proportionally-fair-allocation-on-a-linear-flow-network.md), independent Poisson flow arrivals at rates $\nu_r$ and exponential document sizes of rates $\mu_r$ give a [continuous-time Markov chain](continuous-time-markov-chain.md) with departures $\mu_rn_rx_r(n)$. Put $\rho_r=\nu_r/\mu_r$. Under $\rho_0+\rho_i<1$ for each local route, the normalizing constant is $Z=(1-\rho_0)^{I-1}/\prod_i(1-\rho_0-\rho_i)$. The [binomial coefficient](binomial-coefficient.md) weight has adjacent-state ratios equal to the aggregate service rates, proving [detailed balance](detailed-balance.md). For more general network topologies, [proportional fairness](proportional-fairness.md) need not give this reversible product form.
