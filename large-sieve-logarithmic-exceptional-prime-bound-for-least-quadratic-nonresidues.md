# Large-sieve logarithmic exceptional-prime bound for least quadratic nonresidues

↑ **Parent:** [Least quadratic nonresidue](least-quadratic-nonresidue.md)

On a dyadic interval $Q<p\leq2Q$, every such exceptional prime makes all $Q^{0.01}$-smooth integers quadratic residues. By [positive density of power-smooth integers](positive-density-of-power-smooth-integers.md), there are $\gg Q^2$ of these integers up to $Q^2$. Apply the [variance form of the large sieve](variance-form-of-the-large-sieve.md) or its forbidden-residue consequence with interval length $Q^2$ and prime cutoff $2Q$. Each exceptional prime excludes $(p-1)/2$ residue classes and contributes at least a fixed positive amount to the sieve denominator. Hence each dyadic interval contains $O(1)$ exceptional primes. Summing the intervals gives the logarithmic bound.
