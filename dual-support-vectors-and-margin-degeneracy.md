# Dual support vectors and margin degeneracy

↑ **Parent:** [Soft-margin support vector machine](soft-margin-support-vector-machine.md)

A dual [support vector](support-vector.md) has $\alpha_i>0$. Every such point has signed margin at most one, but a point exactly on the margin can have $\alpha_i=0$ in a degenerate solution. If $\alpha_i=0$, complementary slackness forces $\xi_i=0$, so the point is correctly classified with margin at least one.
