# Radiative Sigma-to-Lambda transition

↑ **Parent:** [Sigma baryon](sigma-baryon.md)

The neutral [Sigma baryon](sigma-baryon.md) and [Lambda baryon](lambda-baryon.md) both have valence content $uds$, but different spin-isospin couplings. An electromagnetic transition changes those internal couplings without changing any quark flavour. In the simple ground-state quark model, the nonzero magnetic transition is associated with the up- and down-quark electromagnetic currents; the strange-quark spin operator alone cannot change the $ud$ pair from spin one to spin zero. [Four-momentum conservation](four-momentum-conservation.md) gives photon energy $E_\gamma=(M_\Sigma^2-M_\Lambda^2)/(2M_\Sigma)$ in [natural units](natural-units.md).
