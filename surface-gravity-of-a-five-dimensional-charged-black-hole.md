# Surface gravity of a five-dimensional charged black hole

↑ **Parent:** [Surface gravity of a static spherical horizon](surface-gravity-of-a-static-spherical-horizon.md)

The time translation is normalized at infinity. For the roots $r_\pm^2=M\pm\sqrt{M^2-Q^2}$, evaluate $V'(r)/2$ to get the signed horizon values. The outer [Hawking temperature](hawking-temperature.md) in natural units is $\sqrt{M^2-Q^2}/(\pi r_+^3)$. The extremal limit has zero surface gravity; the neutral case has only the outer positive-radius horizon and temperature $1/(2\pi\sqrt{2M})$.
