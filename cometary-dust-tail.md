# Cometary dust tail

↑ **Parent:** [Comet tail](comet-tail.md)

A cometary dust tail is the extended distribution of solid grains released by a comet. [Radiation pressure](radiation-pressure.md) sorts its grains by size: high-$\beta$ grains are displaced rapidly in the anti-stellar direction, while larger low-$\beta$ grains remain closer to the projected orbit and form a more curved trail.
