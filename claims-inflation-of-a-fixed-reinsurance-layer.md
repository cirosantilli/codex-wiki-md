# Claims inflation of a fixed reinsurance layer

↑ **Parent:** [Conditional lognormal layer recovery](conditional-lognormal-layer-recovery.md)

For positive inflation factor $h$, $L_{M,A}(hX)=hL_{M/h,A/h}(X)$. The conditioning event also changes to $X>M/h$. If claims have a [lognormal distribution](log-normal-distribution.md), the same result follows by replacing their log-mean $\mu$ by $\mu+\log h$ and keeping the contractual attachment and limit fixed. Merely multiplying the original conditional mean by $h$ would hold those effective thresholds incorrectly fixed.
