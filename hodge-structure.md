# Hodge structure

↑ **Parent:** [Hodge theory](hodge-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hodge_structure)

A pure integral Hodge structure is a finite-rank free abelian group with a complex decomposition satisfying $\overline{H^{p,q}}=H^{q,p}$ and $p+q=w$. The integer $w$ is its weight. Its equivalent [Hodge filtration](hodge-filtration.md) is $F^p=\bigoplus_{r\ge p}H^{r,w-r}$.
