# Two generators for elliptic curves over finite fields

↑ **Parent:** [Elliptic-curve point count over a finite field](elliptic-curve-point-count-over-a-finite-field.md)

For an [elliptic curve](elliptic-curve.md) over a finite field of characteristic $p$, its rational-point group is finite and abelian. [Prime-to-characteristic geometric torsion](prime-to-characteristic-geometric-torsion.md) bounds every other prime-torsion dimension by two. Multiplication by $p$ has degree $p^2$ and inseparable degree at least $p$, so the $p$-torsion dimension is at most one. The structure theorem for [finite abelian groups](finite-abelian-group.md) therefore gives two cyclic factors with $m\mid n$ and $p\nmid m$.
