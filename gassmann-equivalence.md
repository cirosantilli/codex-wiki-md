# Gassmann equivalence

↑ **Parent:** [Permutation representation](permutation-representation.md)

Subgroups $H_1,H_2$ of a finite [group](group-split.md) $G$ are Gassmann equivalent if $|H_1\cap C|=|H_2\cap C|$ for every [conjugacy class](conjugacy-class.md) $C$. Equivalently their coset [permutation representations](permutation-representation.md) have the same [character of a representation](character-of-a-representation.md). They need not be conjugate subgroups. The [Sunada theorem](sunada-theorem.md) turns this equality into equality of [Laplacian eigenfunction](laplacian-eigenfunction.md) multiplicities.
