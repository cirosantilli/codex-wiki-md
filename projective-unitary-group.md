# Projective unitary group

↑ **Parent:** [Unitary group](unitary-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Projective_unitary_group)

The quotient identifies unitary operators differing by a scalar phase. A continuous [projective unitary representation](projective-unitary-representation.md) takes values in this quotient, with the quotient of the strong operator topology. A local continuous section fixes the phase by requiring a chosen nonzero matrix coefficient to be positive real.
