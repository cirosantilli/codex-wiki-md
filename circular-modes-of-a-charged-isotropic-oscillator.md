# Circular modes of a charged isotropic oscillator

↑ **Parent:** [Normal mode](normal-mode.md)

An isotropic planar [harmonic oscillator](simple-harmonic-motion.md) of positive charge in a perpendicular [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) has two circular [normal modes](normal-mode.md) of opposite handedness. With $\omega_c=qB/m>0$ and $\omega_0^2=k/m$, the signed angular frequencies have the displayed form for trajectories $x+iy\propto e^{i\omega t}$. A static [electric field](electric-field-split.md) shifts the equilibrium without changing these frequencies. The clockwise mode has the larger [frequency](frequency.md) magnitude.
