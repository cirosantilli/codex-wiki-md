# Multiplicativity of the nonarchimedean polynomial norm

↑ **Parent:** [Gauss lemma for polynomials](gauss-lemma-for-polynomials.md)

For a [field](field.md) with a [Non-Archimedean absolute value](non-archimedean-absolute-value.md), define the coefficient norm $\|f\|_G=\max_i|a_i|$ for $f(T)=\sum_i a_iT^i$. Then $\|fg\|_G=\|f\|_G\|g\|_G$. For nonzero [polynomials](polynomial-split.md), scale each by one of its coefficients of maximum absolute value; the resulting [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) have integral coefficients and at least one [unit](unit-in-a-ring.md) coefficient. Their reductions in the [residue field](residue-field.md) are nonzero, so their product is nonzero. The product therefore also has coefficient norm $1$, proving the formula after reversing the scaling. This compares binary-polynomial coefficient heights with the heights of their roots.
