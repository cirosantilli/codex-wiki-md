# Constant phase-group velocity product for cutoff dispersion

↑ **Parent:** [Dispersion relation](dispersion-relation-split.md)

On a real positive-frequency branch of $\omega^2=a^2+c^2k^2$, [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md) is $v_P=\omega/k$ and [group velocity](group-velocity.md) is $v_G=d\omega/dk=c^2k/\omega$. Their product is $c^2$. Real propagation requires $\omega^2>a^2$ when solving for a nonzero real $k$; the inequality $\omega>a$ alone assumes $a$ is nonnegative. The phase-group product does not, without further assumptions on the medium, prove a general statement about signal-front propagation.
