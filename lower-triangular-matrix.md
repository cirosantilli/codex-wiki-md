# Lower triangular matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Triangular matrix](triangular-matrix.md)

A lower triangular matrix has zero entries above its main diagonal. Its [determinant](determinant.md) is the product of its diagonal entries, and an invertible lower triangular linear system is solved by forward substitution.
