# Gluon self-energy

↑ **Parent:** [Self-energy](self-energy.md)

The proper two-point correction to the [gluon propagator](gluon-propagator.md) in [QCD](quantum-chromodynamics.md). At one loop it receives [quark](quark.md), [gluon](gluon.md), [Faddeev-Popov ghost field](faddeev-popov-ghost.md) and four-gluon [tadpole diagram](tadpole-diagram.md) contributions. The [Slavnov-Taylor identity](slavnov-taylor-identity.md) makes the total tensor transverse, $k_\mu\Pi^{\mu\nu,ab}=0$, although the individual [gluon](gluon.md) and [ghost loop](ghost-loop.md) contributions need not be transverse. The sign of the symbol must be tied to whether the amputated insertion is defined as $i\Pi$ or $-i\Pi$.
