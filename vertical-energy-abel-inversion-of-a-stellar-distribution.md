# Vertical-energy Abel inversion of a stellar distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Jeans theorem](jeans-theorem.md)

For a phase-mixed one-dimensional [collisionless stellar system](collisionless-stellar-system.md), $E=v_z^2/2+\psi(z)$ is an [integral of motion](integral-of-motion.md) and $f_z=F(E)$. Integrating both signs of velocity gives $\nu(\psi)=\sqrt2\int_\psi^\infty F(E)(E-\psi)^{-1/2}\,dE$. Applying the [Abel transform](abel-transform.md) a second time gives $\int_E^\infty\nu(\psi)(\psi-E)^{-1/2}\,d\psi=\pi\sqrt2\int_E^\infty F(t)\,dt$; differentiating proves the inverse. Sufficient convergence, regularity, and correct escape-energy boundary conditions are essential. An arbitrary tracer-density/potential pair is physical only if the reconstructed [phase-space distribution function](phase-space-distribution-function.md) is nonnegative. Applying this inversion to a [galactic disk](galactic-disk.md) requires a locally separable vertical dynamics or a justified approximation to it, and control of radial mixing and the tilted [velocity ellipsoid](velocity-ellipsoid.md).
