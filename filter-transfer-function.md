# Filter transfer function

↑ **Parent:** [Filter generating function](filter-generating-function.md)

The transfer function is the [filter generating function](filter-generating-function.md) evaluated on the [unit circle](complex-unit-circle.md), with a sign convention matching the Fourier representation. Filtering a sinusoid of angular frequency $\lambda$ multiplies its complex amplitude by $H_A(\lambda)$. Its modulus is the [filter gain](filter-gain.md); filtering a [weakly stationary process](weakly-stationary-process.md) multiplies its [time-series spectral density](spectral-density-of-a-stationary-process.md) by $|H_A(\lambda)|^2$. Using the opposite Fourier sign conjugates the response for real coefficients and leaves that squared modulus unchanged.
