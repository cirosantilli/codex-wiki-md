# Particle on a fixed plane curve

↑ **Parent:** [Smooth mechanical constraint](smooth-mechanical-constraint.md)

A [point mass](point-mass.md) constrained to the fixed graph $y=y(x)$ has [velocity](velocity.md) $(\dot x,y'\dot x)$. Under uniform downward [gravity](gravity.md), its [Lagrangian](lagrangian.md) is $L=m(1+y'^2)\dot x^2/2-mgy$, so its [canonical momentum](canonical-momentum.md) is $p_x=m(1+y'^2)\dot x$, not the horizontal mechanical [momentum](momentum-split.md). The [Euler-Lagrange equation](euler-lagrange-equation.md) gives the equation in the title, and the conserved [energy](energy-split.md) is $m(1+y'^2)\dot x^2/2+mgy$. A straight segment with constant slope $s$ has constant horizontal [acceleration](acceleration.md) $-gs/(1+s^2)$; zero curvature does not imply zero [acceleration](acceleration.md). A cart held to a track satisfies this [mechanical constraint](constraint-mechanics.md) only while the assumed contact or retaining mechanism remains effective.
