<h1 id="lubin-tate-series">Lubin–Tate series</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Lubin–Tate formal group](lubin-tate-formal-group.md)

For a [uniformizer](uniformizer.md) $\pi$ of a non-Archimedean [local field](local-field.md) $K$ with [residue field](residue-field.md) of cardinality $q$, a [Lubin–Tate series](lubin-tate-series.md) is a series $f(T)\in\mathcal O_K[[T]]$ satisfying

$$
f(T)\equiv\pi T\pmod{T^2},\qquad f(T)\equiv T^q\pmod\pi.
$$

It determines the associated two-variable [Lubin–Tate formal group](lubin-tate-formal-group.md). A useful example is $f(T)=\pi T+T^q$. For $K=\mathbb Q_p$ and $\pi=p$, the choice $(1+T)^p-1$ gives the [formal multiplicative group](formal-multiplicative-group.md) and the [Lubin–Tate cyclotomic example](lubin-tate-cyclotomic-example.md).
