# Vertical oscillations of a floating cylinder

↑ **Parent:** [Floating equilibrium](floating-equilibrium.md)

A vertical cylinder of constant cross-sectional [area](surface-area.md) $A$, partly immersed to depth $h_0$ in a fluid of [mass density](density.md) $\rho_f$, satisfies $mg=\rho_f gAh_0$ by [Archimedes' principle](archimedes-principle.md). A small extra downward displacement $z$ increases the [buoyant force](buoyancy.md) by $\rho_f gAz$, so $m\ddot z=-\rho_f gAz$. This is [simple harmonic motion](simple-harmonic-motion.md) with the displayed [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) and [oscillation period](period-of-an-oscillation.md) $2\pi\sqrt{h_0/g}$. The ideal model neglects fluid added inertia, [drag](drag-physics.md) and surface effects; it requires partial immersion throughout the motion.
