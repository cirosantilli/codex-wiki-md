<h1 id="poincare-horizon">Poincaré horizon</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Poincare coordinates on anti-de Sitter spacetime](poincare-coordinates-on-anti-de-sitter-spacetime.md)

The boundary of a chosen [Poincare coordinates on anti-de Sitter spacetime](poincare-coordinates-on-anti-de-sitter-spacetime.md) patch inside the full spacetime is a null coordinate horizon. It is not the timelike [conformal boundary](conformal-boundary.md), and not a curvature singularity. Timelike [geodesics](geodesic.md) can cross it in finite proper time while the patch time diverges. In a signed-diameter conformal strip, the patch containing $T=\rho=0$ satisfies $|T|<\pi/2+\rho$.
