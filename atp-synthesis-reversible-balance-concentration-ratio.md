# ATP synthesis reversible-balance concentration ratio

↑ **Parent:** [ATP synthase energy and proton budget](atp-synthase-energy-and-proton-budget.md)

For tight coupling of n downhill [protons](proton.md) to m synthesized [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) per cycle, reversible balance gives $\Delta G_{\rm hyd}=(n/m)F\Delta p$ with a negative signed driving [proton-motive force](proton-motive-force.md). Inserting $\Delta G_{\rm hyd}=\Delta G_{\rm hyd}^\circ+RT\ln(a_{\rm ADP}a_P/a_{\rm ATP})$ determines a concentration-ratio threshold. Finite net synthesis requires a driving margin, so balance alone does not specify the ratio in a working cell.
