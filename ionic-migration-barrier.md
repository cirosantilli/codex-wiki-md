# Ionic migration barrier

↑ **Parent:** [Ionic conductivity](ionic-conductivity.md)

The activation free-energy barrier for an [ion](ion.md) to hop between sites. [ionic conductivity](ionic-conductivity.md) also depends on the concentration of mobile defects and correlations between successive hops.
