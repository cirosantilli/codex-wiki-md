# Square Bravais lattice

↑ **Parent:** [Two-dimensional Bravais lattice](two-dimensional-bravais-lattice.md)

A [two-dimensional Bravais lattice](two-dimensional-bravais-lattice.md) with perpendicular primitive vectors of the same length $\ell$. An orthogonal matrix $R$ gives its orientation, and its primitive area is $\ell^2$. Its nearest-neighbor graph is a [square lattice](square-lattice.md); the set of crystal translations and the graph with chosen adjacency are related but distinct objects.
