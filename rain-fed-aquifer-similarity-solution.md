# Rain-fed aquifer similarity solution

↑ **Parent:** [Boussinesq equation for an unconfined aquifer](boussinesq-equation-for-an-unconfined-aquifer.md)

For the dimensionless rainfall-driven [Boussinesq equation for an unconfined aquifer](boussinesq-equation-for-an-unconfined-aquifer.md), $h_t=(hh_x)_x+1$, balancing every time power in $h=t^af(x/t^b)$ gives $a=b=1$. The [similarity profile](similarity-profile.md) obeys $(ff')'+\eta f'-f+1=0$. A river drained to base level gives $f(0)=0$, while undrained storage far away gives $f(\infty)=1$. The solution approximates a finite [aquifer](aquifer.md) before its drainage-affected length reaches the far boundary. If $J=\lim_{\eta\downarrow0}ff'>0$, the dimensional drainage flux is $\sqrt K R^{3/2}Jt/\phi$.
