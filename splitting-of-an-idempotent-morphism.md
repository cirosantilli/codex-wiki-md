# Splitting of an idempotent morphism

↑ **Parent:** [Idempotent morphism](idempotent-morphism.md)

An [idempotent morphism](idempotent-morphism.md) $e:E\to E$ splits if there are $r:E\to H$ and $s:H\to E$ with $sr=e$ and $rs=1_H$. The object $H$ is a retract of $E$. In a [functor category](functor-category.md), the splitting maps must be [natural transformations](natural-transformation.md); objectwise splittings without compatible functorial choices are not enough.
