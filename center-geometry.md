# Center (geometry)

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean geometry](euclidean-geometry-split.md)

The center of a [circle](circle.md) or [sphere](sphere.md) is the unique point $\mathbf c$ at the fixed positive [radius](radius.md) $R$ from every point of that [circle](circle.md) or [sphere](sphere.md). In [Cartesian coordinates](cartesian-coordinate-system.md), its equation is $|\mathbf x-\mathbf c|^2=R^2$. In the [complex plane](complex-plane.md), a [circle](circle.md) has equation $|z-c|^2=R^2$; [completing the square](completing-the-square.md) identifies $c$ as its center. For example, $|z+b|^2=R^2$ has center $-b$.
