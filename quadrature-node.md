# Quadrature node

↑ **Parent:** [Quadrature rule](quadrature-rule.md)

A [quadrature node](quadrature-node.md) is a point at which the [integrand](integrand.md) is sampled by a [quadrature rule](quadrature-rule.md). Under the [affine map](affine-map.md) $x=(a+b)/2+(b-a)t/2$ from $[-1,1]$ to $[a,b]$, a reference [quadrature node](quadrature-node.md) $t_i$ becomes $x_i=(a+b)/2+(b-a)t_i/2$; for ordinary [numerical integration](numerical-integration-split.md), the corresponding [quadrature weight](quadrature-weight.md) is multiplied by $(b-a)/2$.
