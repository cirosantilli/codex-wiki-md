# Subadditivity of harmonic hull capacity

↑ **Parent:** [Harmonic capacity from infinity in the upper half-plane](harmonic-capacity-from-infinity-in-the-upper-half-plane.md)

When a finite union is a [compact H-hull](compact-h-hull.md), hitting that union before the real axis implies hitting at least one member before the real axis. The [union bound](boole-s-inequality.md) followed by the defining limit proves subadditivity of [harmonic capacity from infinity in the upper half-plane](harmonic-capacity-from-infinity-in-the-upper-half-plane.md).
