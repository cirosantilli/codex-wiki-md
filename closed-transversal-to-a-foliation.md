# Closed transversal to a foliation

↑ **Parent:** [Taut foliation](taut-foliation.md)

A closed transversal is a closed curve everywhere transverse to the leaves of a [foliation](foliation.md). A single such curve meeting every leaf proves that the [foliation](foliation.md) is a [taut foliation](taut-foliation.md).
