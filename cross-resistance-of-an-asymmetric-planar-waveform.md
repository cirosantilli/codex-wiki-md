# Cross-resistance of an asymmetric planar waveform

↑ **Parent:** [Finite-amplitude propulsion of an inextensible periodic filament](finite-amplitude-propulsion-of-an-inextensible-periodic-filament.md)

An asymmetric periodic planar filament can have nonzero cross-tangent average $\delta$ despite periodic vertical position. Its translational resistance per arclength period is $c_\perp\Lambda[I+(\rho-1)M]$, with $M_{xx}=\beta$, $M_{xy}=\delta$ and $M_{yy}=1-\beta$. Full [force](force.md) balance can therefore yield transverse translation, so an axial-only propulsion formula needs a symmetry or constraint assumption.
