<h1 id="mobius-pointwise-stabilizer-of-two-points">Möbius pointwise stabilizer of two points</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Möbius group](mobius-group.md)

For two distinct points of the [Riemann sphere](riemann-sphere.md), conjugate by a [Möbius transformation](mobius-transformation.md) sending them to zero and infinity. A transformation fixing those two points has the form $z\mapsto\lambda z$, $\lambda\ne0$. Composition multiplies the parameter, so the [pointwise stabilizer](pointwise-stabilizer.md) is isomorphic to the [complex multiplicative group](complex-multiplicative-group.md). For zero and one, conjugation by $z/(1-z)$ gives $z\mapsto\lambda z/[1+(\lambda-1)z]$.
