# Adjacency-matrix quadratic relation for a complete graph

↑ **Parent:** [Complete graph](complete-graph.md)

For the adjacency matrix $A$ of $K_n$,

$$
A=J-I,
\qquad
A^2=(n-2)A+(n-1)I.
$$

Hence $I,A,A^2$ are linearly dependent for every $n\geq2$.
