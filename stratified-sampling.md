# Stratified sampling

↑ **Parent:** [Probability sampling](probability-sampling.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stratified_sampling)

Stratified sampling partitions a population into strata and samples separately within each. It guarantees specified representation and can improve precision when the strata describe population variation. Equal sampling fractions give equal unit inclusion [probabilities](probability.md); unequal fractions require appropriate weighting for population summaries.
