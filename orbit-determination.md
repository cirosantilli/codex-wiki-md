# Orbit determination

↑ **Parent:** [Spacecraft trajectory](spacecraft-trajectory.md)

[Orbit determination](orbit-determination.md) infers a body's [position](position.md) and [velocity](velocity.md) from observations and an [equation of motion](equation-of-motion.md). It is an [inverse problem](inverse-problem-split.md); a [residual of an inverse problem](residual-of-an-inverse-problem.md) compares an observed readout with its modeled prediction.
