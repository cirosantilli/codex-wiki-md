# Wall-impact speed distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution](maxwell-boltzmann-distribution.md)

The [speed distribution](speed-distribution.md) of atoms striking a wall is weighted by the normal component of [velocity](velocity.md), relative to the distribution of atoms present in the bulk. For an isotropic classical monatomic gas, the impact speed density is proportional to $v^3e^{-mv^2/(2k_BT)}$, and the mean impact [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) is $2k_BT$, rather than $3k_BT/2$.
