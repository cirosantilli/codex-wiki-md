# Rocket equation

↑ **Parent:** [Momentum](momentum.md)

For a body of varying mass $M$ ejecting material at fixed relative velocity, conservation of [momentum](momentum.md) gives a differential relation between $dM$ and the body's change $dv$. Integrating it produces a logarithm of the mass ratio.
