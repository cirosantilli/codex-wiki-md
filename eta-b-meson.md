# Eta b meson

↑ **Parent:** [Quarkonium](quarkonium.md)

A hidden-bottom [pseudoscalar meson](pseudoscalar-meson.md), with leading [quark model](quark-model.md) content $b\bar b$. Its lowest configuration has $L=S=J=0$ and $J^{PC}=0^{-+}$. Its net bottom flavor is zero, so annihilation into [gluons](gluon.md) is possible.
