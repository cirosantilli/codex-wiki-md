# Analyses of tubercular material by Robert Mortimer Glover

↑ **Parent:** [Microbiology and infectious disease bibliography](microbiology-and-infectious-disease-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by Robert Mortimer Glover](work-by-robert-mortimer-glover.md)

Original analyses in [Robert Mortimer Glover](robert-mortimer-glover.md)'s On the Pathology and Treatment of Scrofula, reproduced as quoted primary passages in the contemporary article Dr. Glover on Scrofula. Inspected reproduction: printed pp. 516–517, explicitly locating the first quoted original analysis at book p. 73. It supports solvent fractionation, protein-rich residue, mineral salts and variation between lesions. The entire original book was not inspected; therapeutic and etiological beliefs in the surrounding review are not endorsed.
