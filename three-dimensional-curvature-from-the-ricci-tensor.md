# Three-dimensional curvature from the Ricci tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann curvature tensor](riemann-curvature-tensor.md)

In dimension three the [Riemann curvature tensor](riemann-curvature-tensor.md) is $R_{ijpq}=g_{ip}S_{jq}-g_{iq}S_{jp}-g_{jp}S_{iq}+g_{jq}S_{ip}$, where $S$ is the [Schouten tensor](schouten-tensor.md). Consequently every three-dimensional [Ricci-flat Riemannian manifold](ricci-flat-riemannian-manifold.md) is a [flat Riemannian manifold](flat-manifold.md). The implication fails in higher dimensions.
