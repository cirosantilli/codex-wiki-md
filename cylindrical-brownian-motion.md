# Cylindrical Brownian motion

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md)

On a real [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) $H$, this centered jointly [Gaussian process](gaussian-process.md) is linear in its test vector $h$ and has the displayed covariance, with independent increments in time. Equivalently choose an orthonormal basis and independent real [Brownian motions](brownian-motion-split.md) $W^j$, and define stochastic integration by $\int\langle v_s,dW_s\rangle=\sum_j\int\langle v_s,e_j\rangle dW_s^j$ whenever $\int\|v_s\|^2ds<\infty$. In infinite dimension it need not be an $H$-valued [random variable](random-variable-split.md); the integral is the meaningful object.
