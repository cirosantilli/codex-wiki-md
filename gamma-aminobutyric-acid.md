# Gamma-aminobutyric acid

↑ **Parent:** [Neurotransmitter](neurotransmitter.md)

A major transmitter commonly associated with inhibitory signalling through appropriate [receptors](receptor-biochemistry.md). Its cellular effect depends on [receptor](receptor-biochemistry.md) and ionic conditions; suppressing an inhibitory [neuron](neuron-split.md) can disinhibit its targets.
