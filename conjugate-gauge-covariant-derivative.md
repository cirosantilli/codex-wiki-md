# Conjugate gauge covariant derivative

↑ **Parent:** [Gauge covariant derivative](gauge-covariant-derivative.md)

If a [complex scalar field](complex-scalar-field.md) uses $D_\mu\phi=(\partial_\mu+ieA_\mu)\phi$, its conjugate uses $(D_\mu\phi)^*=(\partial_\mu-ieA_\mu)\phi^*$. Under $\phi\mapsto e^{-ie\alpha}\phi$ and $A\mapsto A+d\alpha$, the two derivatives transform with opposite phases. Their product is gauge invariant. Applying the same plus-charge operator to both factors leaves an unwanted term proportional to $\partial_\mu\alpha$ and fails [gauge invariance](gauge-invariance.md).
