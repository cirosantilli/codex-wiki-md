# Eady-wave phase tilt and heat flux

↑ **Parent:** [Eady model](eady-model.md)

For $\psi'=A[\cosh(Kz)\cos(kx)-\alpha\sinh(Kz)\sin(kx)]$ with $k,K,\alpha>0$, its [phase](phase-waves.md) increases with height and its crests tilt westward. The derivative $b'=f_0\psi'_z$ has decreasing continuous [phase](phase-waves.md) and eastward-tilting crests. Multiplying $v'=\psi'_x$ by $b'$ and averaging over a zonal [wavelength](wavelength.md) gives the displayed [eddy buoyancy flux](eddy-buoyancy-flux.md), using $\cosh^2(Kz)-\sinh^2(Kz)=1$. Its sign is poleward for the associated eastward-increasing [thermal wind](thermal-wind.md) in either hemisphere. An untilted separable perturbation has zero zonally averaged flux under periodic endpoint conditions.
