<h1 id="ertel-s-theorem">Ertel's theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ertel potential vorticity](ertel-potential-vorticity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ertel's_theorem)

An inviscid fluid with conservative body force, a materially conserved stratifying scalar $\alpha$, and an [equation of state](equation-of-state.md) making $\nabla\rho,\nabla p,\nabla\alpha$ coplanar conserves its [Ertel potential vorticity](ertel-potential-vorticity.md). The stretching of [absolute vorticity](absolute-vorticity.md) per unit [density](density.md) cancels the deformation of the scalar [gradient](gradient.md); the remaining baroclinic triple product vanishes. In an incompressible density-stratified ideal fluid, choosing the advected [density](density.md) as $\alpha$ gives that cancellation directly.
