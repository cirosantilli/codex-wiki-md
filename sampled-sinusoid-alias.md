# Sampled sinusoid alias

↑ **Parent:** [Aliasing of discrete Fourier modes](aliasing-of-discrete-fourier-modes.md)

At uniform sampling times $t_n=n/f_s$, adding an integer multiple of the [sampling rate](sampling-rate.md) to a sinusoid's [frequency](frequency.md) changes its complex phase by an integer multiple of $2\pi n$. The samples therefore agree. Real [cosines](cosine.md) also identify the two signs of frequency, giving the displayed representative below the [Nyquist frequency](nyquist-frequency.md). A chosen nonzero sampling-time origin changes the equivalent phase, rather than the alias frequency.
