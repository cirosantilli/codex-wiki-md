# Multitrait-multimethod design

↑ **Parent:** [Psychometrics](psychometrics.md)

A measurement design applying multiple methods to multiple proposed traits. Cross-method agreement can support [convergent validity](convergent-validity.md), while comparison among traits supports [discriminant validity](discriminant-validity.md). Trait and method must not be completely confounded in the design.
