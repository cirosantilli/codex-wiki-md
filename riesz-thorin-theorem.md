# Riesz-Thorin theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Operator norm](operator-norm.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riesz–Thorin_theorem)

For a [linear operator](linear-operator.md) bounded from $L^{p_i}$ to $L^{q_i}$ with [norms](norm.md) $M_i$, $i=0,1$, interpolate reciprocals by $1/p=(1-\theta)/p_0+\theta/p_1$ and $1/q=(1-\theta)/q_0+\theta/q_1$. The intermediate [operator norm](operator-norm.md) is at most $M_0^{1-\theta}M_1^\theta$. The complex interpolation proof first treats [simple functions](simple-function.md); completeness and [continuity](continuous-function.md) on the endpoint sum spaces identify the extension with the original operator. On finite counting spaces it also gives [norm](norm.md) bounds between sequence spaces.
