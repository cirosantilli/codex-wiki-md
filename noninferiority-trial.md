# Noninferiority trial

↑ **Parent:** [Clinical trial](clinical-trial.md)

A trial testing whether an intervention is no worse than a comparator by more than a prespecified clinically justified margin. For a higher-is-better outcome and difference $d$, a lower confidence bound above $-\Delta$ supports the stated margin. Failure of a superiority test is not sufficient; [fidelity](treatment-fidelity.md), [attrition](study-attrition.md) and intention-to-treat and sensitivity analyses affect interpretation.
