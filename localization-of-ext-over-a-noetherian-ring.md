# Localization of Ext over a Noetherian ring

↑ **Parent:** [Ext functor](ext-functor.md)

Over a [Noetherian ring](noetherian-ring.md), a [finitely generated module](finitely-generated-module.md) $N$ has a free resolution with every term finitely generated. Hom from a finite [free module](free-module.md) commutes with localization, and [exactness of localization](exactness-of-localization.md) commutes with cohomology. Thus the displayed isomorphism holds for every $i\geq0$ and arbitrary $M$. The finiteness condition is on the first argument; the second need not be finitely generated.
