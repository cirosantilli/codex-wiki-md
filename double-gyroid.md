# Double gyroid

↑ **Parent:** [Microphase separation](microphase-separation.md)

A bicontinuous domain morphology containing two interpenetrating, nonintersecting networks of one component within the other. In [diblock copolymers](diblock-copolymer.md) it can appear between lamellar and cylindrical morphologies in part of the phase diagram.
