# Phytoplankton biomass

↑ **Parent:** [Phytoplankton](phytoplankton.md)

The standing stock of living [phytoplankton](phytoplankton.md), often expressed as carbon mass per water volume. [Chlorophyll a](chlorophyll-a.md) is an accessible proxy, but its ratio to carbon is variable.
