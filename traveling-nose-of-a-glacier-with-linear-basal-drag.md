# Traveling nose of a glacier with linear basal drag

↑ **Parent:** [Shallow-ice flux with linear basal drag](shallow-ice-flux-with-linear-basal-drag.md)

A locally steady [glacier](glacier.md) nose moving at speed $v>0$ has $q=vh$ by [mass conservation](mass-conservation.md). At distance $s$ behind its edge, the [linear basal drag law](linear-basal-drag-law.md) then gives $s=(\rho g/\mu v)(h^3/9+h^2/(2\beta))$. The thin nose has $h\propto s^{1/2}$ from [basal sliding](basal-sliding.md); a thicker outer nose has $h\propto s^{1/3}$ from internal deformation. A thick bulk therefore still has a sliding-controlled inner tip. The divergent idealized edge slope limits the immediate validity of the [shallow-ice approximation](shallow-ice-approximation.md).
