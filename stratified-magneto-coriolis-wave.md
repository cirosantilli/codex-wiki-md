# Stratified magneto-Coriolis wave

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetohydrodynamics](magnetohydrodynamics.md)

An incompressible [Boussinesq](boussinesq-approximation.md) fluid with uniform [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md), uniform vertical [rotation](rotation-mathematics.md) and uniform temperature gradient supports waves combining [magnetic tension](magnetic-tension.md), [Coriolis force](coriolis-force.md) and [buoyancy](buoyancy.md). Put $K=|\mathbf k|$, $h^2=K^2-k_z^2$, $a^2=(\mathbf B\cdot\mathbf k)^2/(\mu_0\rho)$ and $H=g\alpha\beta$, with $\partial_zT_0=-\beta$. Eliminating [pressure](pressure.md) and the temperature and magnetic perturbations gives the displayed [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation.md) for nonzero frequencies. Stable thermal stratification has $H<0$. Clearing frequency denominators requires checking any resulting zero roots in the original equations; a stationary magnetostatic-buoyancy balance can exist independently of the propagating branches.
