# Cerebral autoregulation

↑ **Parent:** [Cerebral circulation](cerebral-circulation.md)

Adjustment of cerebral [vascular resistance](vascular-resistance.md) that moderates changes in [blood flow](blood-flow.md) as perfusion pressure varies. It has finite physiological limits and is not a guarantee of constant flow.
