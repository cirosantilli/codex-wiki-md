# Radioisotope thermoelectric generator

↑ **Parent:** [Thermoelectric generator](thermoelectric-generator.md)

A [thermoelectric generator](thermoelectric-generator.md) whose heat supply comes from [radioactive decay](radioactive-decay.md). The [Seebeck effect](seebeck-effect.md) converts part of a temperature-driven heat flow into electrical output. The source's thermal [power](power.md) and the electrical [power](power.md) delivered by the converter are distinct quantities.
