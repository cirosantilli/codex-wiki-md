# Chiral scalar-doublet Yukawa model

↑ **Parent:** [Yukawa interaction](yukawa-interaction.md)

The combined transformation $\psi\mapsto e^{-i\alpha\gamma_5/2}\psi$ and $(\phi_1+i\phi_2)\mapsto e^{i\alpha}(\phi_1+i\phi_2)$ preserves this interaction. The [Dirac adjoint](dirac-adjoint.md) transforms with the same axial exponential as $\psi$. For potential $-\mu^2r^2/2+\lambda r^4/4$, $v=\mu/\sqrt\lambda$ gives a radial scalar of squared [mass](mass.md) $2\lambda v^2$, a massless [Goldstone boson](goldstone-boson.md) and [fermion](fermion.md) physical [mass](mass.md) $|g|v$. Cubic scalar vertices appear after the shift: $hhh$ has factor $-6i\lambda v$ and $h\pi\pi$ has factor $-2i\lambda v$.
