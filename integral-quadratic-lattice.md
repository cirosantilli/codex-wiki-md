# Integral quadratic lattice

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic form](quadratic-form.md)

An integral quadratic lattice is a finite-rank [free abelian group](free-abelian-group.md) with a nondegenerate integral symmetric [bilinear form](bilinear-form.md). Its norm convention here is $q(x)=B(x,x)$. A basis gives an integral [Gram matrix](gram-matrix.md); basis changes preserve its determinant up to multiplication by the square of a unit.
