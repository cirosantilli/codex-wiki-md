# Right-adjoint criterion using comma-category colimits

↑ **Parent:** [Adjoint functors](adjoint-functors.md)

A [functor](functor.md) $F:\mathcal X\to\mathcal A$ preserving the possibly large colimits of the projections $U_A:(F\downarrow A)\to\mathcal X$ has a [right adjoint](adjoint-functors.md) exactly when all those [colimits](colimit.md) exist. The universal cocone makes its vertex a [terminal object](terminal-object.md) of $(F\downarrow A)$, hence a representation of $\mathcal A(F-,A)$.
