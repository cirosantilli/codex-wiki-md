<h1 id="brauer-s-permutation-lemma">Brauer's permutation lemma</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Character theory](character-theory.md)

A group of [automorphisms](automorphism.md) of a [finite group](finite-group.md) permutes both its [conjugacy classes](conjugacy-class.md) and its [irreducible characters](irreducible-character.md). The two [permutation representations](permutation-representation.md) have the same [character](character-of-a-representation.md): each automorphism fixes equally many conjugacy classes and irreducible characters. The invertible [character table](character-table.md) intertwines the two permutation matrices, which have equal traces. The claim concerns fixed classes, not merely fixed elements.
