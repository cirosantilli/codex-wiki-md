# Formal monodromy

↑ **Parent:** [Monodromy](monodromy.md)

An unramified formal [fundamental matrix](fundamental-matrix-of-a-linear-differential-equation.md) $\widehat Y=\widehat G(\lambda)\lambda^\Theta e^{Q(\lambda)}$ acquires the right factor $e^{2\pi i\Theta}$ on a positive turn of the [logarithm](logarithm.md) branch. This is [formal monodromy](formal-monodromy.md). It can be nontrivial even when the true equation has an entire [fundamental matrix](fundamental-matrix-of-a-linear-differential-equation.md) and trivial actual [monodromy](monodromy.md), because [Stokes matrices](stokes-matrix.md) compensate for it.
