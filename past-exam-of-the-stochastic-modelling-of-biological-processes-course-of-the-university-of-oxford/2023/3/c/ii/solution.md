<h1 id="3/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The integrated friction is exactly

$$
\widetilde\kappa_c(0)=\int_0^\infty ce^{-ct}\,dt=1.
$$

For every finite $c>0$, the roots of $s^2+cs+c$ have negative real parts. The [Green-Kubo formula for diffusion](../../../../../green-kubo-formula-for-diffusion.md) therefore gives the [diffusion coefficient](../../../../../diffusion-coefficient.md) directly:

$$
D_c=\widetilde\chi_c(0)=\frac5{\widetilde\kappa_c(0)}=5.
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{\lim_{c_1\to\infty}D=5.}
$$

Integrating the limiting [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) [autocorrelation function](../../../../../autocorrelation.md) gives the same value, $\int_0^\infty5e^{-\tau}\,d\tau=5$. Computing $D_c$ before taking the limit also avoids having to justify an interchange of the infinite-time [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md) and the parameter limit.
