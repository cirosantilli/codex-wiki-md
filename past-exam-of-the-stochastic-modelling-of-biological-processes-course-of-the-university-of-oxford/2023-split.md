# 2023

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the stochastic modelling of biological processes course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Solutions to all three questions of the [stochastic modelling of biological processes course of the University of Oxford](../stochastic-modelling-of-biological-processes-course-of-the-university-of-oxford.md) Part B examination, sat on 8 June 2023. Source: [original released PDF, bundle pages 272–275; paper pages 1–4](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/request_for_mathematics_examinat/response/3129786/attach/3/FOI%20202507%20740%20Data.pdf#page=272). The solutions discuss the two readings of Question 1(c), correct the repeated noise-coordinate label in Question 2, and state the boundary qualification needed in Question 2(a).
