# Existential common-substructure test for quantifier elimination

↑ **Parent:** [Common-substructure test for quantifier elimination](common-substructure-test-for-quantifier-elimination.md)

For a [first-order theory](first-order-theory.md) $T$, suppose every existential [quantifier-free formula](quantifier-free-formula.md) over a common [substructure](substructure-of-a-first-order-structure.md) has a witness in either of two $T$-models exactly when it has one in the other. Then $T$ has [quantifier elimination](quantifier-elimination.md). One may test a single quantified variable, since elimination of these formulas inductively eliminates all quantifiers. A language with a constant avoids empty-generated-substructure and quantifier-free-sentence conventions.
