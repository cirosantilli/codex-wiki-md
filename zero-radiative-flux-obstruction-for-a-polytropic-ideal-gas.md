# Zero-radiative-flux obstruction for a polytropic ideal gas

↑ **Parent:** [Polytropic vertical structure in stellar gravity](polytropic-vertical-structure-in-stellar-gravity.md)

For positive finite [opacity](opacity.md) and nonvacuum [ideal gas](ideal-gas.md) satisfying [radiative diffusion](radiative-diffusion.md), zero flux implies $dT/dz=0$. Write $P=\mathcal R\rho T$, where $\mathcal R=k_B/(\mu_m m_p)$ for constant [mean molecular weight](mean-molecular-weight.md) $\mu_m$ in proton-mass units. If a [polytropic equation of state](polytropic-equation-of-state.md) $P=K\rho^\gamma$ with constant $K>0$ is also imposed, then $T=(K/\mathcal R)\rho^{\gamma-1}$. For $\gamma\ne1$, constant [temperature](temperature.md) would force constant density, contradicting vertical [hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) $P'=-\rho\Omega^2z$ on an open interval away from the midplane. The familiar finite polytropic slab for $\gamma>1$ is therefore a solution of the hydrostatic/adiabatic closure, not of this finite-opacity zero-radiative-flux system. For $\gamma=1$, the consistent isothermal profile is $\rho=\rho_m\exp[-\Omega^2z^2/(2\mathcal R T_m)]$, with no finite surface.
