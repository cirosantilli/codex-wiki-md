# Derivative of the matrix exponential

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix exponential](matrix-exponential.md)

This formula allows a [matrix](matrix.md) and its derivative to fail to commute. Differentiate the absolutely convergent [power series](power-series.md) of the [matrix exponential](matrix-exponential.md) to obtain $\sum_{k\geq1}k!^{-1}\sum_{j=0}^{k-1}f^jf'f^{k-1-j}$. Expanding the integral gives the same coefficients, since $\int_0^1(1-u)^au^b\,du=a!b!/(a+b+1)!$. The formula reduces to $e^ff'$ when $[f,f']=0$.
