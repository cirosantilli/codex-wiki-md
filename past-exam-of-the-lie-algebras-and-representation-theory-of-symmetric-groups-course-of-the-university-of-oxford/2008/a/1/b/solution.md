↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Start with $I_0=I$ and set $I_{k+1}=[L,I_k]$. Every term lies in $I$, and $I_k\subseteq L_{k+1}$, so the sequence eventually vanishes. Choose its last nonzero term $I_j$. Then $[L,I_j]=0$, hence $0\ne I_j\subseteq I\cap Z(L)$. Therefore $\boxed{I\cap Z(L)\ne\{0\}}$.
