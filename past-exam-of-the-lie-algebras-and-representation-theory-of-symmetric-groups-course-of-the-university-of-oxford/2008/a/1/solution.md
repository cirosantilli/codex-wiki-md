<h1 id="a/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Define $L_1=L$ and $L_{n+1}=[L,L_n]$, where brackets of subspaces mean their linear span. Each term is an [ideal](../../../../ideal-of-a-lie-algebra.md). Inductively, if $L_n$ is an [ideal](../../../../ideal-of-a-lie-algebra.md), the [Jacobi identity](../../../../jacobi-identity.md) gives

$$
[z,[x,y]]=[[z,x],y]+[x,[z,y]]\in[L,L_n]\quad(z,x\in L,\ y\in L_n),
$$

so $L_{n+1}$ is an [ideal](../../../../ideal-of-a-lie-algebra.md) as well. Also $L_{n+1}\subseteq L_n$. Nilpotence means that this descending series eventually reaches zero; class $c$ means $L_{c+1}=0$ with $L_c\ne0$ for nonzero $L$.
