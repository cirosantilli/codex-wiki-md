↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The idealizer here is the normalizer $\operatorname{Id}_L(A)=\{x\in L:[x,A]\subseteq A\}$. Since $A$ is a subalgebra it contains its own brackets, so $A\subseteq\operatorname{Id}_L(A)$.

Because $L_1\not\subseteq A$ and the lower central series terminates, choose $j$ with $L_j\not\subseteq A$ but $L_{j+1}\subseteq A$. Take $x\in L_j\setminus A$. Then $[x,A]\subseteq[L_j,L]=L_{j+1}\subseteq A$. Thus $x$ belongs to the idealizer and not to $A$, proving $\boxed{A<\operatorname{Id}_L(A)}$.
