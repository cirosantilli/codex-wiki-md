<h1 id="b/8/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For the [characteristic-three Specht module for the two-by-two partition](../../../../../characteristic-three-specht-module-for-the-two-by-two-partition.md), reduce the [matrices](../../../../../matrix.md) modulo three. The vector $e_1+e_2$ is fixed by every displayed [transposition](../../../../../transposition-permutation.md): $(12)$ and $(34)$ send it to $e_1-2e_2=e_1+e_2$, and $(23)$ interchanges its two terms. Thus its span is a proper nonzero **trivial [submodule](../../../../../submodule.md)**.

On the quotient impose $e_2=-e_1$. Each generating [transposition](../../../../../transposition-permutation.md) then acts as minus one, so the quotient is the **[sign representation](../../../../../sign-representation.md)**. Consequently

$$
\boxed{0\longrightarrow\mathbf1\longrightarrow S^{(2,2)}\longrightarrow\operatorname{sgn}\longrightarrow0,}
$$

and the two [composition factors](../../../../../composition-factor.md) are trivial and sign, each once. In the $D$ notation they are $D^{(4)}$ and $D^{(2,2)}$ respectively. Indeed the restricted [tabloid](../../../../../tabloid.md) form has [Gram matrix](../../../../../gram-matrix.md) $\begin{pmatrix}4&2\\2&4\end{pmatrix}$, whose characteristic-three radical is precisely the trivial line, so the simple quotient is $D^{(2,2)}$.

The extension does not split: the minus-one [eigenspace](../../../../../eigenspace.md) of $\rho(12)$ is the line through $e_2$, but $\rho(23)$ sends that line to the line through $e_1$. Thus there is no sign [submodule](../../../../../submodule.md) complementing the trivial line, consistent with the failure of semisimplicity.
