<h1 id="b/5/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The equality $\sum_\lambda(\dim S^\lambda)^2=n!$ exhausts the [dimension](../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) of the [regular representation](../../../../../regular-representation.md). There is no room for another irreducible constituent, since it would contribute its positive squared [dimension](../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md). Thus

$$
\boxed{\{S^\lambda:\lambda\vdash n\}\text{ is a complete irredundant family of irreducible representations in characteristic zero}.}
$$

Each has the standard-[polytabloid](../../../../../polytabloid.md) [basis](../../../../../basis.md) just proved. The usual split characteristic-zero statement follows over any characteristic-zero [field](../../../../../field.md) because these integral Specht models remain irreducible after extension to an algebraic closure and already exhaust all dimensions there. In the literal wording of the source, [polytabloids](../../../../../polytabloid.md) themselves are vectors; it is the [Specht modules](../../../../../specht-module.md) with those bases that form the family of [representations](../../../../../group-representation.md).
