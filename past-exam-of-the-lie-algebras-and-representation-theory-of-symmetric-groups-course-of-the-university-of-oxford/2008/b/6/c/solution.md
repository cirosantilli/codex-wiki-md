<h1 id="b/6/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The two [partition](../../../../../integer-partition.md) diagrams are transposes, so their characteristic-zero [Specht modules](../../../../../specht-module.md) differ by tensoring with the [sign representation](../../../../../sign-representation.md). A [transposition](../../../../../transposition-permutation.md) has sign minus one. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\chi^{(2,1^{n-2})}((i,j))=-\chi^{(n-1,1)}((i,j))=3-n.}
$$

The middle [character](../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) is $n-3$, since the standard [representation](../../../../../group-representation.md) is the point-permutation [representation](../../../../../group-representation.md) minus its trivial line, and a [transposition](../../../../../transposition-permutation.md) fixes $n-2$ points.
