<h1 id="b/6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

If the cycle [partition](../../../../../integer-partition.md) contains a part $r$, the [Murnaghan–Nakayama rule](../../../../../murnaghan-nakayama-rule.md) is

$$
\boxed{\chi^\lambda((r,\nu))=\sum_{\substack{\mu\subseteq\lambda\\\lambda/\mu\text{ a border strip of size }r}}(-1)^{\operatorname{ht}(\lambda/\mu)}\chi^\mu(\nu).}
$$

A [border strip](../../../../../rim-hook.md) is a connected skew [Young diagram](../../../../../young-diagram.md) with no $2\times2$ block; its height is its number of occupied rows minus one. The remaining boxes must be a [partition](../../../../../integer-partition.md) diagram. The recursion starts with [character](../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) one for the empty diagram at the empty [cycle type](../../../../../cycle-type.md), and an empty sum is zero. Repeated removal for all cycle lengths computes the [character](../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) value.
