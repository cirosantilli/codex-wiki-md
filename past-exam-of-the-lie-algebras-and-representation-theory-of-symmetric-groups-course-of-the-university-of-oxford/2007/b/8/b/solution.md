<h1 id="b/8/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Realize $S^{(n-1,1)}$ as the augmentation [submodule](../../../../../submodule.md)

$$
W=\{(x_1,\ldots,x_n)\in F^n:\sum_i x_i=0\}
$$

of the natural [permutation module](../../../../../permutation-module.md): its [polytabloids](../../../../../polytabloid.md) are the differences $e_i-e_j$, which span exactly $W$. Let $U$ be a [submodule](../../../../../submodule.md). If it contains a [vector](../../../../../vector.md) $v$ with two unequal coordinates $v_i,v_j$, then

$$
v-(i\ j)v=(v_i-v_j)(e_i-e_j)\in U.
$$

The coefficient is nonzero, so $e_i-e_j\in U$; its symmetric-group orbit spans $W$, forcing $U=W$. Therefore every proper nonzero [submodule](../../../../../submodule.md) consists only of constant-coordinate [vectors](../../../../../vector.md). Those form at most a one-dimensional trivial [submodule](../../../../../submodule.md). If such a trivial [submodule](../../../../../submodule.md) is absent, no proper nonzero [submodule](../../../../../submodule.md) exists, so **the [Specht module](../../../../../specht-module.md) is simple**.
