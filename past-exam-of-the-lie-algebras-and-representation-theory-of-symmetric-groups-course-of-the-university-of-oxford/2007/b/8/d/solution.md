↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

In characteristic three with $n=5$, a constant [vector](../../../../../vector.md) $(c,c,c,c,c)$ belongs to the augmentation [submodule](../../../../../submodule.md) only if $5c=0$, which forces $c=0$. Thus $S^{(4,1)}$ has no trivial [submodule](../../../../../submodule.md). Part (b) proves that it is **simple**, of dimension four, and therefore equals $D^{(4,1)}$.
