<h1 id="b/8/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The [standard polytabloid](../../../../../standard-polytabloid.md) [basis](../../../../../basis.md) is defined over the [integers](../../../../../integer-split.md) and remains a [basis](../../../../../basis.md) after reduction modulo three. Thus $\dim S^{(3,2)}=f^{(3,2)}=\binom52-\binom51=5$. The given [submodule](../../../../../submodule.md) has dimension four by part (d), so the quotient is one-dimensional and part (c) leaves only the trivial or sign action.

A transposition has ordinary trace one on $S^{(3,2)}$: the [Murnaghan–Nakayama rule](../../../../../murnaghan-nakayama-rule.md) has only the horizontal bottom-row two-strip, leaving shape $(3)$ with dimension one. Reduction of its integral action [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) preserves this trace in $F$. Its trace on $D^{(4,1)}=S^{(4,1)}$ is $3-1=2$, since the natural five-point [permutation](../../../../../permutation.md) [representation](../../../../../group-representation.md) has three fixed coordinates and contains the trivial line. Therefore its trace on the one-dimensional quotient is $1-2=-1$, distinct from one in characteristic three. This gives the [characteristic-three sign quotient of the Specht module of shape three two](../../../../../characteristic-three-sign-quotient-of-the-specht-module-of-shape-three-two.md). The quotient is the [sign representation](../../../../../sign-representation.md):

$$
\boxed{\sigma\cdot\overline v=\operatorname{sgn}(\sigma)\,\overline v
\quad\text{on }S^{(3,2)}/D^{(4,1)}.}
$$
