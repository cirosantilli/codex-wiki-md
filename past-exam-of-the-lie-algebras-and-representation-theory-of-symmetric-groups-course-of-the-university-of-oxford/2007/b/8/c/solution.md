<h1 id="b/8/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

On a one-dimensional [representation](../../../../../group-representation.md), $s_i$ acts by a nonzero scalar $\varepsilon_i$. Its square is one. The braid relation becomes $\varepsilon_i^2\varepsilon_{i+1}=\varepsilon_{i+1}^2\varepsilon_i$, and hence $\varepsilon_{i+1}=\varepsilon_i$. Thus all adjacent transpositions act by one common scalar $\varepsilon$ with $\varepsilon^2=1$.

In characteristic different from two, $\varepsilon=1$ or $-1$. Since adjacent transpositions generate the group, these choices give exactly the [trivial representation](../../../../../trivial-representation.md) and [sign representation](../../../../../sign-representation.md). For $n\le1$ they coincide, leaving only the [trivial representation](../../../../../trivial-representation.md).
