<h1 id="b/6/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [Robinson-Schensted-Knuth correspondence](../../../../../robinson-schensted-knuth-correspondence.md) is a bijection between [permutations](../../../../../permutation.md) and ordered pairs of [standard Young tableaux](../../../../../standard-young-tableau.md) of the same shape. Hence the class with shape $\lambda$ has size $(f^\lambda)^2$. For $\lambda=(4,2,1,1)$, the [hook lengths](../../../../../hook-length.md) are

$$
\begin{array}{rrrr}7&4&2&1\\4&1\\2\\1\end{array},
$$

with product $448$. The [hook-length formula](../../../../../hook-length-formula.md) gives $f^\lambda=8!/448=90$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\text{class size}=90^2=8100.}
$$
