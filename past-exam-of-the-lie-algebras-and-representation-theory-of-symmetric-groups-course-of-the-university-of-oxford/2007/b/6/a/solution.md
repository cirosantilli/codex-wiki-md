For an [integer partition](../../../../../integer-partition.md) $\lambda=(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_r)$, its [Young diagram](../../../../../young-diagram.md) has $\lambda_i$ left-justified cells in row $i$. A [Young tableau](../../../../../young-tableau.md) of shape $\lambda$ is a filling of its cells. Under the usual standard numbering convention, a [standard Young tableau](../../../../../standard-young-tableau.md) uses each [integer](../../../../../integer-split.md) $1,\ldots,n$ once and increases strictly along every row and down every column. For a different fixed ordered set of distinct labels, the same inequalities define the corresponding ordered-label version, which becomes standard after replacing its labels by their ranks.
