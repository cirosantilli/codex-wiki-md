<h1 id="b/7/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [symmetric group](../../../../../symmetric-group.md) acts on [tabloids](../../../../../tabloid.md) by permuting their entries; extending linearly gives the [permutation module](../../../../../permutation-module.md) $M^\mu$. Its dimension is

$$
\boxed{\dim M^\mu=\frac{n!}{\prod_i\mu_i!}.}
$$

A tableau of type $\mu$ contains exactly $\mu_i$ copies of the label $i$. Fix a numbering of the $\lambda$-diagram's cells by $1,\ldots,n$. For a filling $T$, let the $i$th row set of an associated $\mu$-tabloid be the set of cell numbers whose labels in $T$ equal $i$. This is a bijection from type-$\mu$ fillings to $\mu$-tabloids.

Define $\sigma T$ by moving labels along the numbered positions: its label at cell $j$ is the old label at cell $\sigma^{-1}j$. The associated row set is then the image under $\sigma$ of the old row set. Thus the bijection is equivariant, and its linear extension makes $F[\mathcal T_{\lambda,\mu}]$ and $M^\mu$ [isomorphic representations](../../../../../isomorphic-representations.md). Extend from group elements to the [group algebra](../../../../../group-algebra.md) by linearity.
