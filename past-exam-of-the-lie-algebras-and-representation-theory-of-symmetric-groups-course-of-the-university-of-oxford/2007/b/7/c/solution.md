<h1 id="b/7/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For type $(1^n)$, all labels are distinct, so a semistandard tableau is precisely a [standard Young tableau](../../../../../standard-young-tableau.md). Thus $K_{\lambda,(1^n)}=f^\lambda$, and [Young's rule](../../../../../young-s-rule.md) gives

$$
\boxed{M^{(1^n)}\cong\bigoplus_{\lambda\vdash n}f^\lambda S^\lambda,\qquad
m_\lambda=f^\lambda=\frac{n!}{\prod_{c\in\lambda}h(c)}.}
$$

This is also the decomposition of the [regular representation](../../../../../regular-representation.md), since [tabloids](../../../../../tabloid.md) with one entry per row are naturally indexed by all [permutations](../../../../../permutation.md).
