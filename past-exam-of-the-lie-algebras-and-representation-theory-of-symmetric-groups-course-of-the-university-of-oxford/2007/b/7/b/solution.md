[Young's rule](../../../../../young-s-rule.md) states that in [characteristic zero](../../../../../characteristic-zero.md),

$$
\boxed{M^\mu\cong\bigoplus_{\lambda\vdash n}K_{\lambda\mu}S^\lambda,}
$$

where the [Kostka number](../../../../../kostka-number.md) $K_{\lambda\mu}$ counts [Semistandard Young tableaux](../../../../../semistandard-young-tableau.md) of shape $\lambda$ and type $\mu$: rows are weakly increasing, columns strictly increasing, and label $i$ occurs $\mu_i$ times. The summands are irreducible [Specht modules](../../../../../specht-module.md), and the coefficients are nonnegative [integer](../../../../../integer-split.md) multiplicities.
