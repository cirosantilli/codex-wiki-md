<h1 id="b/5/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For a [cycle type](../../../../../cycle-type.md) $\mu=(m,\nu)$, the [Murnaghan–Nakayama rule](../../../../../murnaghan-nakayama-rule.md) is

$$
\chi^\lambda(m,\nu)=\sum_{\lambda/\rho\text{ a rim hook of size }m}
(-1)^{\operatorname{ht}(\lambda/\rho)}\chi^\rho(\nu).
$$

The sum is over partitions $\rho$ whose diagrams remain after removing the specified border strip; $\nu$ is the remaining [cycle type](../../../../../cycle-type.md), and the recursion ends with the empty [character](../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) equal to one. At the identity, $\chi^\rho(1^{|\rho|})=f^\rho$. Incompatible total sizes contribute zero.

For shape $(2,2)$ and a transposition, a horizontal strip in the bottom row leaves $(2)$ with positive sign; a vertical strip in the right column leaves $(1,1)$ with negative sign. Both remaining dimensions are one. Thus

$$
\boxed{\chi^{(2,2)}(2,1,1)=1-1=0.}
$$
