<h1 id="b/5/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For shape $(n-2,2)$ with $n\ge6$, there are exactly two removable [rim hooks](../../../../../rim-hook.md) of size two: the bottom row, leaving $(n-2)$, and the final two cells of the first row, leaving $(n-4,2)$. Both have height zero. A vertical pair cannot be removed while leaving a [Young diagram](../../../../../young-diagram.md) because the first row is longer than the second by at least two.

By the [Murnaghan–Nakayama rule](../../../../../murnaghan-nakayama-rule.md) and part (b),

$$
\begin{aligned}
\chi^{(n-2,2)}(2,1^{n-2})
&=1+f^{(n-4,2)}\\
&=1+\binom{n-2}{2}-\binom{n-2}{1}
=\boxed{\frac{(n-4)(n-3)}2}.
\end{aligned}
$$
