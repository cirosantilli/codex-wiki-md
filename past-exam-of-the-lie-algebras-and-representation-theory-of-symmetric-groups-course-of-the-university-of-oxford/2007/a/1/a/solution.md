<h1 id="a/1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

On the space of [endomorphisms](../../../../../endomorphism.md) of $V$, left and right multiplication by $x$ commute, and $\operatorname{ad}x$ is their difference. The [binomial theorem](../../../../../binomial-theorem.md) therefore gives

$$
(\operatorname{ad}x)^m(y)=\sum_{j=0}^m(-1)^j\binom mj x^{m-j}yx^j.
$$

For $m=2n-1$, every term has either $j\ge n$ or $m-j\ge n$, so every term vanishes when $x^n=0$. Restriction to the invariant [Lie subalgebra](../../../../../lie-subalgebra.md) $L$ proves

$$
\boxed{(\operatorname{ad}_Lx)^{2n-1}=0.}
$$

The argument works over every [field](../../../../../field.md); no division by a binomial coefficient is used.
