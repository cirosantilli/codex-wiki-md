<h1 id="a/1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Start with the given [basis](../../../../../basis.md) of $U$, and follow it by arbitrary lifts of the given [basis](../../../../../basis.md) of $V/U$. Since $U$ is an invariant [linear subspace](../../../../../vector-subspace.md), every acting [endomorphism](../../../../../endomorphism.md) has block form

$$
\begin{pmatrix}A&*\\0&B\end{pmatrix},
$$

where $A$ and $B$ are its [matrices](../../../../../matrix.md) on $U$ and $V/U$. Both are strictly upper triangular in the chosen [bases](../../../../../basis.md). Therefore the whole [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) has zero entries on and below its diagonal. The same lifted [basis](../../../../../basis.md) works simultaneously for all elements of $L$.
