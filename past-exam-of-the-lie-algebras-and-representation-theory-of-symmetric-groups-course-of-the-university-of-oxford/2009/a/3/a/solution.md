<h1 id="a/3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [base of a root system](../../../../../fundamental-system-of-a-root-system.md) is a vector-space [basis](../../../../../basis.md) $\Delta$ of the ambient Euclidean space such that every root is an integer combination of its elements with all coefficients nonnegative or all nonpositive. The corresponding nonzero nonnegative combinations are the [positive roots](../../../../../positive-root.md).

If distinct $\alpha,\beta\in\Delta$ had positive [inner product](../../../../../inner-product-split.md), the allowed root-difference fact would make $\alpha-\beta$ a root. Its simple-root coefficients have opposite signs, contradicting the definition. Hence **distinct [simple roots](../../../../../simple-root.md) have nonpositive [inner product](../../../../../inner-product-split.md)**.

For a [positive root](../../../../../positive-root.md) $\gamma=\sum n_\alpha\alpha$ which is not simple, the identity $(\gamma,\gamma)=\sum n_\alpha(\gamma,\alpha)>0$ gives a [simple root](../../../../../simple-root.md) $\alpha$ with $n_\alpha>0$ and $(\gamma,\alpha)>0$. The allowed fact makes $\gamma-\alpha$ a root. Its coefficients remain nonnegative, and it is nonzero because $\gamma$ is not simple. It has smaller height $\sum n_\alpha$. Induction expresses it as a sum of [simple roots](../../../../../simple-root.md) with each partial sum a root; appending $\alpha$ gives the required expression for $\gamma$.

Finally, reflection in a [simple root](../../../../../simple-root.md) changes only that root's coefficient:

$$
\sigma_\alpha(\gamma)=\gamma-\frac{2(\gamma,\alpha)}{(\alpha,\alpha)}\alpha.
$$

For positive $\gamma\ne\alpha$, at least one other simple coefficient is positive: in a reduced [root system](../../../../../root-system.md) the only [positive root](../../../../../positive-root.md) on the line of $\alpha$ is $\alpha$ itself. That other coefficient is unchanged. The reflected vector is a root, so its coefficients cannot all be nonpositive and must all be nonnegative. Thus **$\sigma_\alpha$ preserves all [positive roots](../../../../../positive-root.md) except $\alpha$**, which it sends to $-\alpha$.
