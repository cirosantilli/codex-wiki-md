<h1 id="a/1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Polarizing $[x,[x,z]]=0$ gives

$$
[x,[y,z]]+[y,[x,z]]=0.
$$

Let $T=[x,[y,z]]$. Antisymmetry and this identity give $[y,[z,x]]=T$ and $[z,[x,y]]=T$. The [Jacobi identity](../../../../../jacobi-identity.md) therefore gives $3T=0$. Since the characteristic is not three, $T=0$ for every triple. Consequently

$$
\boxed{[L,[L,L]]=0,\qquad L\text{ has nilpotency class at most two}.}
$$

No division by two was used, so the proof also applies in characteristic two with the usual alternating definition of a [Lie bracket](../../../../../lie-bracket.md).
