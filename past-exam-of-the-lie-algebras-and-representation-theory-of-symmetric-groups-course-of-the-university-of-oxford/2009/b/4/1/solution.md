↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

The [Young diagram](../../../../../young-diagram.md) is $[\lambda]=\{(i,j):1\leq i\leq k,\ 1\leq j\leq\lambda_i\}$. The [hook length](../../../../../hook-length.md) of a box is

$$
h(i,j)=\lambda_i-j+\lambda'_j-i+1,
$$

counting the box itself, the boxes to its right in the same row, and the boxes below it in the same column. The [hook-length formula](../../../../../hook-length-formula.md) is

$$
\boxed{f^\lambda=\frac{n!}{\prod_{(i,j)\in[\lambda]}h(i,j)}.}
$$

The first implication in the equivalence is immediate: **if every [hook length](../../../../../hook-length.md) is odd, no hook can have length two**.
