<h1 id="b/4/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Assume no hook has length two. The last row has length one, since otherwise its penultimate box has [hook length](../../../../../hook-length.md) two. If two adjacent row lengths are equal, take the last two rows in the maximal block of that length; the last box in the upper of those two rows has [hook length](../../../../../hook-length.md) two. Thus all row lengths are distinct. If a gap $\lambda_i-\lambda_{i+1}$ were at least two, the penultimate box in row $i$ would again have [hook length](../../../../../hook-length.md) two, since there would be no boxes below it. Therefore **every successive gap equals one and the final part equals one**, proving (2) implies (3).
