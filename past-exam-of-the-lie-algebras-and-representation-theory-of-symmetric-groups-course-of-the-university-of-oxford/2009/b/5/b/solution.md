↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A permutation sends $v_a$ to $v_{ga}$, $w_{ab}$ to $w_{ga,gb}$ and $x_{abc}$ to $x_{ga,gb,gc}$. Relabeling the summation indices gives

$$
g\phi_1(v_a)=\sum_{b\ne a}w_{ga,gb}=\phi_1(v_{ga}),\qquad g\phi_2(w_{ab})=\sum_{c\notin\{a,b\}}x_{ga,gb,gc}=\phi_2(w_{ga,gb}).
$$

Linearity therefore proves **both maps are [module homomorphisms](../../../../../module-homomorphism.md)**. The map denoted $\phi$ in the second displayed source formula is $\phi_2$.
