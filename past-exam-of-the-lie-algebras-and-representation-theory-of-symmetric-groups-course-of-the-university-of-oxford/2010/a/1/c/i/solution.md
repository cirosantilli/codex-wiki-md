<h1 id="a/1/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $A$ be the two-dimensional affine [Lie algebra](../../../../../../lie-algebra-split.md) with [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) $u,v$ and bracket $[u,v]=v$, and put

$$
\boxed{L=\mathfrak{sl}_2(\mathbb C)\oplus A.}
$$

Both factors have zero centre, so $Z(L)=0$. The line $\mathbb Cv$ in the second factor is an ideal, making $L$ [PSS](../../../../../../pre-supersoluble-lie-algebra.md). But its quotient onto the nonabelian simple algebra $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ is not soluble. A quotient of a soluble algebra would be soluble, so $L$ is not soluble.
