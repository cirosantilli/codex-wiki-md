<h1 id="a/2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Choose a [basis](../../../../../basis.md) $(u,v)$ with $U=Fu$. The [representation](../../../../../group-representation.md) matrices are

$$
\rho(h)=\begin{pmatrix}\lambda(h)&a(h)\\0&\mu(h)\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The one-dimensional modules $U,V/U$ have characters $\lambda,\mu$. If they coincide, these modules are isomorphic and the first alternative holds.

Otherwise choose $h$ with $\delta(h)\ne0$, where $\delta=\lambda-\mu$. Replacing $v$ by $v-a(h)u/\delta(h)$ makes the upper-right entry of $\rho(h)$ zero. The [representation](../../../../../group-representation.md) identity on commutators now gives, for every $x\in H$,

$$
a([h,x])=\delta(h)a(x).
$$

Iteration yields $a((\operatorname{ad}h)^r x)=\delta(h)^r a(x)$. Nilpotence of $H$ implies $(\operatorname{ad}h)^r x=0$ for sufficiently large $r$, by its lower central series. Since $\delta(h)\ne0$, it follows that $a(x)=0$ for all $x$. The new line $W=Fv$ is therefore a [submodule](../../../../../submodule.md), and

$$
\boxed{V=U\oplus W.}
$$

The argument works over the stated arbitrary [field](../../../../../field.md), including positive characteristic.
