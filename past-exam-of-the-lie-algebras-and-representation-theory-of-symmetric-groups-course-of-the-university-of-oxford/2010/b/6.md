↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $\pi^\lambda$ be the [permutation character](../../../permutation-character.md) of $M^\lambda$. [Young's rule](../../../young-s-rule.md) gives $\pi^\lambda=\chi^\lambda+\sum_{\mu>\lambda}K_{\mu\lambda}\chi^\mu$, with integer Kostka multiplicities. This dominance-triangular system has diagonal entries one, so recursive subtraction expresses every [irreducible character](../../../irreducible-character.md) as an integer linear combination of permutation characters. The latter count fixed tabloids, proving that all $\chi^\lambda(g)$ are integers.

For a $k$-cycle, the [Murnaghan–Nakayama rule](../../../murnaghan-nakayama-rule.md) reduces to

$$
\chi^\lambda(g)=\sum_{\substack{\nu\subseteq\lambda,\ |\nu|=n-k\\\lambda/\nu\text{ a rim hook of length }k}}
(-1)^{\operatorname{ht}(\lambda/\nu)}f^\nu,
$$

where height is the number of occupied rows minus one, and $f^\nu$ is the degree of the remaining Specht [character](../../../character-of-a-representation.md) at the identity. In particular a full $n$-cycle has [character](../../../character-of-a-representation.md) zero unless $\lambda=(n-r,1^r)$ is a hook, in which case the value is $(-1)^r$.
