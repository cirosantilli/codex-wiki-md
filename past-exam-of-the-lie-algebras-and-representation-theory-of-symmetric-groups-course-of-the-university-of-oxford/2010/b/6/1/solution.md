<h1 id="b/6/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

There is more than one solution: the [trivial representation](../../../../../trivial-representation.md) $\lambda=(6)$ has [character](../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) $1$ everywhere and the [sign representation](../../../../../sign-representation.md) $\lambda=(1^6)$ has [character](../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) $\pm1$ everywhere. Thus

$$
\boxed{\lambda=(6)\text{ and }(1^6)\text{ both work}.}
$$

In fact they are the only ones. The full-cycle formula in the preceding discussion forces any candidate to be a hook $(6-r,1^r)$. For $r=1$, the standard [character](../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) is the number of fixed points minus one, so it vanishes on cycle type $(5,1)$; its sign twist $r=4$ does too. For $r=2$, the hook [representation](../../../../../group-representation.md) is the [exterior square](../../../../../exterior-square.md) of the standard [representation](../../../../../group-representation.md) and has [character](../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) $\tfrac12(\chi(g)^2-\chi(g^2))$. At cycle type $(4,1,1)$ both standard-[character](../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) values are one, so this [character](../../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) is zero. Its sign twist $r=3$ also vanishes there. Only $r=0,5$ remain.
