<h1 id="b/6/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

[Irreducible character](../../../../../irreducible-character.md) orthogonality gives $\langle\chi,\chi\rangle=1$. Under the proposed vanishing condition, only the identity contributes, so

$$
1=\frac{\chi(1)^2}{|S_6|}=\frac{\chi(1)^2}{720}.
$$

But $\chi(1)$ is an integer [dimension](../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) and $720$ is not a square. Thus **no such partition exists**.
