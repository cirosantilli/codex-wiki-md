<h1 id="b-plus-tree">B+ tree</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Data structure](data-structure-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B+_tree)

A balanced search [tree](tree-graph-theory-split.md) used for database indexes. Records occur at the leaves; internal nodes direct searches using separator keys. A [B+ tree](b-plus-tree.md) with maximum fan-out $f$ has at most $f$ children per internal node.
