# American option

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical finance](mathematical-finance-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_option)

An American option may be exercised at any time up to maturity. Its value is the [Snell envelope](snell-envelope.md) of its discounted exercise payoff.
