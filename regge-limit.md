# Regge limit

↑ **Parent:** [Regge trajectory](regge-trajectory.md)

The Regge limit of a scattering amplitude takes the center-of-mass energy invariant $s$ large at fixed momentum-transfer invariant $t$. A leading trajectory produces a power $s^{\alpha(t)}$, with signature and residue factors. For the bosonic [Virasoro–Shapiro amplitude](virasoro-shapiro-amplitude.md) in dimensionless string variables, $\alpha(t)=2+2t$. This differs from fixed-angle high-energy scattering.
