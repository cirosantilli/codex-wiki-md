# Dynamic meniscus

↑ **Parent:** [Surface tension](surface-tension.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamic_meniscus)

A dynamic meniscus is a curved transition region joining a moving thin film to a bulk fluid reservoir.
