# Between-person slope quantile

↑ **Parent:** [Random slope](random-slope.md)

When a person's latent slope has [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md) $N(\beta_1,\tau_1^2)$, its $u$th [quantile](quantile-function.md) is $\beta_1+\tau_1\Phi^{-1}(u)$. Multiply by an elapsed time to obtain the corresponding latent change [quantile](quantile-function.md). This describes population variation between people; it is distinct from a [confidence interval](confidence-interval.md) for the mean slope and from a [prediction interval](prediction-interval.md) that includes measurement error.
