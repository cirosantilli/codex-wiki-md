# Absoluteness of cardinalhood in limit ranks

↑ **Parent:** [Set-theoretic absoluteness](set-theoretic-absoluteness.md)

For [limit ordinals](limit-ordinal.md) $\alpha\leq\beta$, the structures $V_\alpha$ and $V_\beta$ agree on whether any shared [set](set-split.md) is a [cardinal number](cardinal-number.md). They agree on being an [ordinal](ordinal.md). If $\delta<\alpha$ is not a [cardinal number](cardinal-number.md), a [bijection](bijection.md) from some $\gamma<\delta$ onto $\delta$ has [rank of a set](rank-of-a-set.md) at most $\delta+3$, hence belongs to $V_\alpha$. Its defining properties are [bounded formulas in set theory](bounded-formula-in-set-theory.md). Both structures therefore see the same failure witness. This full agreement is stronger than [downward absoluteness of cardinalhood](downward-absoluteness-of-cardinalhood.md) between arbitrary [transitive models](transitive-model.md).
