<h1 id="manganese-iii-mediated-radical-cyclization">Manganese(III)-mediated radical cyclization</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Organic reaction](organic-reaction.md)

[oxidation](oxidation.md) of an [enol](enol.md) or [enolate](enolate.md) can generate a carbon-centred [radical](radical-chemistry.md) that adds intramolecularly to an [alkene](alkene.md). Further [oxidation](oxidation.md) or [oxygen](oxygen.md) capture determines the final functional group.
