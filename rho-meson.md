# Rho meson

↑ **Parent:** [Meson](meson.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rho_meson)

The [rho meson](rho-meson.md) is an isospin-one vector [meson](meson.md). Its neutral member has [charge conjugation](charge-conjugation.md) value minus one, so all three charge states have [G parity](g-parity.md) plus one. The two-pion decay is allowed by G parity, whereas a purely strong isospin-conserving three-pion decay is excluded.
