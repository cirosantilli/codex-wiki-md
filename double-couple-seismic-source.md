# Double-couple seismic source

↑ **Parent:** [Seismic moment tensor](seismic-moment-tensor.md)

A [double-couple seismic source](double-couple-seismic-source.md) has a moment [tensor](tensor.md) proportional to $\mathbf b\otimes\mathbf n+\mathbf n\otimes\mathbf b$, with the slip direction $\mathbf b$ perpendicular to the [fault plane](fault-plane.md)'s normal $\mathbf n$. Its [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are proportional to $(1,0,-1)$. A general symmetric [seismic moment tensor](seismic-moment-tensor.md) need not have this restricted form.
