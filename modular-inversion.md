# Modular inversion

↑ **Parent:** [Modular multiplicative inverse](modular-multiplicative-inverse.md)

The computation of an [integer](integer-split.md) $x$ satisfying $ax\equiv1\pmod m$. An inverse exists exactly when $\gcd(a,m)=1$. The [extended Euclidean algorithm](extended-euclidean-algorithm.md) produces such an $x$ from a [Bezout identity](bezout-s-identity.md) $ax+my=1$.
