# Modular intersection polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Frankl-Wilson theorem](frankl-wilson-theorem.md)

If member sizes modulo a [prime number](prime-number.md) $p$ avoid $L$ while distinct intersections lie in $L$, evaluation at [characteristic vectors of sets](characteristic-vector-of-a-set.md) gives a diagonal matrix with nonzero diagonal. After [multilinear reduction on the Boolean cube](multilinear-reduction-on-the-boolean-cube.md), these [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) remain [linearly independent](linear-independence.md) and have degree at most $|L|$. Counting square-free [monomials](monomial.md) proves $|\mathcal F|\leq\sum_{j=0}^{|L|}\binom nj$. On a fixed uniform layer, the [low-degree evaluation rank on a uniform layer](low-degree-evaluation-rank-on-a-uniform-layer.md) sharpens this to $\binom n{|L|}$ when $|L|$ does not exceed the layer rank.
