# Open local operator

↑ **Parent:** [Lawvere-Tierney topology](lawvere-tierney-topology.md)

For a [subterminal object](subterminal-object.md) classified by $u$, the open local operator is $p\mapsto(u\Rightarrow p)$. Its dense monos are exactly those whose image contains the pullback of that subterminal. It is complementary to the corresponding [closed local operator](closed-local-operator.md).
