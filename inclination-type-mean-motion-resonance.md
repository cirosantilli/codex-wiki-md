# Inclination-type mean-motion resonance

↑ **Parent:** [Mean-motion resonance](mean-motion-resonance.md)

An inclination-type mean-motion resonance has a leading [resonant argument](resonant-argument.md) containing a [longitude of ascending node](longitude-of-ascending-node.md). Reflection symmetry forces the total nodal coefficient in each disturbing-function harmonic to be even, so the leading resonant strength is an even power of $\sin(I/2)$.
