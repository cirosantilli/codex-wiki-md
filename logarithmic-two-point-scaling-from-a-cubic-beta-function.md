# Logarithmic two-point scaling from a cubic beta function

↑ **Parent:** [Asymptotic freedom](asymptotic-freedom.md)

For $\beta=-bg^3$ and $\gamma=cg^2$, integrate the [running coupling](running-coupling.md) equation and then the anomalous-dimension multiplier. The correlator correction grows as a power $c/b$ of the logarithm of energy, rather than as a fixed power of energy. Higher perturbative orders change subleading terms while retaining this leading logarithmic exponent.
