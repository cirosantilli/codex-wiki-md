# Identical-by-descent segment

↑ **Parent:** [Identity by descent](identity-by-descent.md)

An identical-by-descent segment is a chromosomal tract inherited from the same ancestral chromosome without an intervening breakpoint in the tracked ancestry. With a specified total number $m$ of independent [meioses](meiosis.md) and a uniform recombination map, the probability that a fixed tract of genetic length $d$ survives without [genetic recombination](genetic-recombination.md) is approximately $e^{-md}$. Recent common ancestry therefore tends to give longer shared tracts. Lengths of tracts selected by covering a random point are length-biased relative to tracts sampled by their boundaries.
