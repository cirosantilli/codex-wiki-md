# Light clock

↑ **Parent:** [Time dilation](time-dilation.md)

A light clock measures time by counting round trips of a [light](light.md) pulse between mirrors. In its [rest frame](rest-frame.md), mirrors separated by $h$ give a tick duration $2h/c$, where $c$ is the [speed of light](speed-of-light.md). Comparing this round trip in another [inertial frame](inertial-frame.md) provides the geometric [time dilation](time-dilation.md) argument above.
