# Quantum-dot charge sensing

↑ **Parent:** [Electrostatically defined quantum dot](electrostatically-defined-quantum-dot.md)

Detection of a dot's charge through its capacitive influence on a nearby [quantum point contact](quantum-point-contact.md) or [single-electron transistor](single-electron-transistor.md). Discrete charge changes shift the sensor response, allowing individual tunnel events to be resolved even when direct current through the dot is too small to measure.
