# Kahane-Khintchine inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Khintchine inequality](khintchine-inequality.md)

For a [Rademacher sum](rademacher-sum.md) in a [normed vector space](normed-vector-space.md), any two fixed positive moments of its [norm](norm.md) are comparable with constants depending only on those moments. The [sharp Rademacher second-moment inequality](sharp-rademacher-second-moment-inequality.md) gives the sharp first-to-second moment comparison $\|S\|_{L^2}\leq\sqrt2\|S\|_{L^1}$ for arbitrary coefficients.
