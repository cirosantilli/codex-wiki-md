# Odd-only sieve

↑ **Parent:** [Number-theoretic sieve](number-theoretic-sieve.md)

An odd-only sieve stores only odd candidates, handling the [prime](prime-number-split.md) two separately. This reduces the candidate storage and avoids marking even composites repeatedly.
