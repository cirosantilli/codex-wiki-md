# Bohr-Sommerfeld quantization

↑ **Parent:** [Semiclassical quantization](semiclassical-quantization.md)

Single-valued semiclassical phase on a closed classical orbit quantizes its action, with [Maslov index](maslov-index.md) $\mu$ accounting for turning-point phase corrections. A cyclic angle has no oscillator turning points: its wavefunctions are $e^{i\ell q}$, with signed $\ell\in\mathbb Z$ for a $2\pi$ period and [canonical momentum](canonical-momentum.md) $\hbar\ell$. There is no half-integer shift for that free angular coordinate.
