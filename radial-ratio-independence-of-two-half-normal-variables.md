# Radial-ratio independence of two half-normal variables

↑ **Parent:** [Half-normal distribution](half-normal-distribution.md)

If $X,Y$ are independent with density $2e^{-x^2}/\sqrt\pi$ on the positive axis, the [Jacobian matrix](jacobian-matrix.md) of the inverse transformation has determinant $1/[2(1+v^2)]$. The joint density is $2e^{-u}/[\pi(1+v^2)]$ for $u,v>0$, factoring into an [exponential distribution](exponential-distribution.md) for $U$ and a unit-scale [half-Cauchy distribution](half-cauchy-distribution.md) for $V$. The underlying radial symmetry makes radius and angle independent.
