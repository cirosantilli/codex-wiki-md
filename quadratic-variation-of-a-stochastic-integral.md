# Quadratic variation of a stochastic integral

↑ **Parent:** [Stochastic integral](stochastic-integral.md)

For a continuous local martingale $M$,

$$
\left[\int H\,dM\right]_t=\int_0^tH_s^2\,d[M]_s.
$$
