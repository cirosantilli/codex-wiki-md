# Hard magnetic material

↑ **Parent:** [Ferromagnetism](ferromagnetism.md)

A [hard magnetic material](hard-magnetic-material.md) strongly resists reversal of its [magnetization](magnetization.md), giving high coercivity and useful remanence for permanent magnets. The relevant hardness is magnetic, not a mechanical strength rating.
