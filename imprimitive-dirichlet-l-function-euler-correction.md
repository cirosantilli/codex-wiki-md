# Imprimitive Dirichlet L-function Euler correction

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet L-function](dirichlet-l-function.md)

The inducing [primitive Dirichlet character](primitive-dirichlet-character.md) has [conductor of a Dirichlet character](conductor-of-a-dirichlet-character.md) $q_0$. Increasing the modulus removes Euler factors only at new [prime](prime-number.md) divisors. The correction creates zeros on the imaginary axis; raising the exponents of [primes](prime-number.md) already dividing the [conductor of a Dirichlet character](conductor-of-a-dirichlet-character.md) changes no Euler factor. Primitive gamma factors continue to use $q_0$. For real-even characters this includes principal primitive [conductor of a Dirichlet character](conductor-of-a-dirichlet-character.md) one, with its [Riemann zeta function](riemann-zeta-function.md) [pole](pole.md) treated separately.
