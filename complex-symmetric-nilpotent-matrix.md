# Complex symmetric nilpotent matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md)

The displayed nonzero [matrix](matrix.md) is symmetric under transpose but not [Hermitian](hermitian-operator.md). It is [nilpotent](nilpotent.md), so all its [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are zero and it cannot be [diagonalizable](diagonalizable-matrix.md). Thus real-symmetric spectral conclusions require reality or a Hermitian hypothesis; complex transpose symmetry alone is insufficient.
