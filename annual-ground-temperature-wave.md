# Annual ground-temperature wave

↑ **Parent:** [Heat equation](heat-equation.md)

A sinusoidal surface [temperature](temperature-split.md) applied to a homogeneous semi-infinite conductor produces a periodic depth profile with penetration length $\delta=\sqrt{2\kappa/(\omega C)}$. It follows by substituting a complex exponential into $C\partial_tT=\kappa\partial_z^2T$ and taking the root that decays at depth. A depth $z$ attenuates the amplitude by $e^{-z/\delta}$ and delays the phase by $z/\delta$. This is [diffusion](diffusion.md) with phase delay, rather than a nondispersive travelling wave.
