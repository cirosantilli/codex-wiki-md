# Static electron correlation

↑ **Parent:** [Electron correlation](electron-correlation.md)

[Electron correlation](electron-correlation.md) caused by the need for multiple important configurations, often when several [molecular orbitals](molecular-orbital.md) are nearly degenerate. Stretching a homonuclear two-electron bond makes a single doubly occupied orbital retain unphysical ionic contributions; a suitable combination of configurations cancels them.
