# Conservative restriction of a vector field to a plane

↑ **Parent:** [Conservative vector field](conservative-vector-field.md)

A [vector field](vector-field.md) can have nonzero ambient [curl](curl.md) while its tangential restriction to a [plane](plane.md) has an exact [differential one-form](one-form.md). On a simply connected [plane](plane.md), the [Stokes theorem](stokes-theorem.md) gives the relevant criterion: the component of the [curl](curl.md) normal to the [plane](plane.md) vanishes. All closed planar [line integrals](line-integral.md) then vanish, and open ones depend only on endpoints. For example, $(y-z,z-x,x-y)$ restricts on $x-z=1$ to $F\cdot dr=d(x-y)$, despite ambient [curl](curl.md) $-2(1,1,1)$.
