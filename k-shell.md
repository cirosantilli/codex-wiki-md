# K shell

↑ **Parent:** [Electron shell](electron-shell.md)

The innermost atomic [electron shell](electron-shell.md), with principal quantum number $n=1$. Its $s$ orbital has nonzero nuclear overlap, making its electrons important in [electron capture](electron-capture.md).
