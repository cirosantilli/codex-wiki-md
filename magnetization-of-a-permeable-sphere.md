# Magnetization of a permeable sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Demagnetizing field](demagnetizing-field.md)

For a homogeneous linear isotropic [sphere](sphere.md) in a uniform applied field, the internal field is $\mathbf H_i=\mathbf H_0-\mathbf M/3$. Combining this geometric [demagnetizing field](demagnetizing-field.md) with $\mathbf M=\chi\mathbf H_i$ gives the title expression. Matching the scalar magnetic potentials $-H_ir\cos\theta$ inside and $-H_0r\cos\theta+C\cos\theta/r^2$ outside provides an independent boundary-value derivation. Tangential $H$ is continuous without free surface current and normal $B$ is continuous. A negative susceptibility makes [magnetization](magnetization.md) antiparallel, not parallel in sense, to the applied field.
