# Stokes-Einstein experimental consistency test

↑ **Parent:** [Stokes–Einstein relation](stokes-einstein-relation.md)

For dilute isolated [spheres](sphere.md) in a Newtonian [solvent](solvent.md), $D\eta a/T=k_B/(6\pi)$. An experimental check independently measures [temperature](temperature-split.md), [viscosity](dynamic-viscosity-split.md) and particle size, then obtains [diffusion](diffusion.md) from a displacement variance or a scattering decay. Drift, walls, [concentration](concentration.md) effects, aggregation and a [hydrodynamic radius](hydrodynamic-radius.md) different from a structural radius can defeat an unqualified comparison.
