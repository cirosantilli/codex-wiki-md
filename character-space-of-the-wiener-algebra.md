# Character space of the Wiener algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Wiener algebra](wiener-algebra.md)

Every [algebra character](character-of-an-algebra.md) of the [Wiener algebra](wiener-algebra.md) is evaluation at a unique point of the [unit circle](complex-unit-circle.md). Writing $u(w)=w$, both $u$ and $u^{-1}$ have [norm](norm.md) one. Thus $z=\phi(u)$ satisfies $|z|\leq1$ and $|z^{-1}|\leq1$, hence $|z|=1$. Continuity then gives $\phi(\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}a_nu^n)=\sum a_nz^n$. Conversely, evaluation defines a continuous [algebra character](character-of-an-algebra.md). The resulting bijection is a [homeomorphism](homeomorphism.md) for the [Gelfand topology](gelfand-topology.md).
