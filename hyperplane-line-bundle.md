# Hyperplane line bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Complex tautological line bundle](complex-tautological-line-bundle.md)

The dual of the [complex tautological line bundle](complex-tautological-line-bundle.md). On the [complex projective line](complex-projective-line.md) the induced frames satisfy $e_1=w e_0$ on the overlap of its standard affine charts.
