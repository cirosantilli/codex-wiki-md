# Partition of a sample space

↑ **Parent:** [Sample space](sample-space.md)

A family of disjoint [events](event.md) whose union is the [sample space](sample-space.md). For the countable [law of total probability](law-of-total-probability.md) and [law of total expectation](law-of-total-expectation.md), use a finite or countably infinite family. Conditioning formulas sum only positive-probability parts, or interpret zero-probability contributions as zero without assigning a conditional expectation there.
