<h1 id="15/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [characteristic equation](../../../../../../characteristic-equation-of-a-constant-coefficient-differential-equation.md) has roots $-2,-6$, and a trial multiple of $e^{-4t}$ gives the [particular solution](../../../../../../particular-solution.md) $-3e^{-4t}$. Thus

$$
f(t)=Ae^{-2t}+Be^{-6t}-3e^{-4t}.
$$

The first two conditions give $A+B=3$ and $-2A-6B+12=0$, hence $A=B=3/2$. At $t_*=\log\sqrt2$, the exponentials $e^{-2t_*},e^{-4t_*},e^{-6t_*}$ equal $1/2,1/4,1/8$. Therefore

$$
f(t_*)=\frac34+\frac3{16}-\frac34=\frac3{16}\ne0.
$$

**No solution satisfies all three conditions.** The first two initial data already determine a unique solution of the [second-order linear differential equation](../../../../../../second-order-linear-differential-equation.md); the extra condition is inconsistent with it.
