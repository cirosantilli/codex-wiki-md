<h1 id="15/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The repeated factor in the [differential operator](../../../../../differential-operator.md) suggests $y=e^{-x}u(x)$. Direct differentiation gives $y''+2y'+y=e^{-x}u''$, so $u''=4$ and

$$
u=2x^2+Bx+C.
$$

The first value gives $C=1$. The second gives $u(1)=ey(1)=1$, hence $2+B+1=1$ and $B=-2$. Therefore

$$
y(x)=e^{-x}(2x^2-2x+1),\qquad \boxed{y(2)=5e^{-2}}.
$$
