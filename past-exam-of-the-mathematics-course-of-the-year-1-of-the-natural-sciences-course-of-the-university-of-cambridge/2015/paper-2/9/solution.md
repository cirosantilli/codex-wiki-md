<h1 id="9/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [9](../9.md)

The original PDF contains the product $\sin x(1+4\cos x)$; the transcription drops the $x$ after the first [sine](../../../../sine.md). Using $2\sin x\cos x=\sin2x$ gives the finite [Fourier sine series](../../../../fourier-sine-series.md)

$$
\boxed{f(x)=\sin x+2\sin2x}.
$$

Thus $b_1=1$, $b_2=2$, and all other [sine](../../../../sine.md) coefficients vanish. No [cosine](../../../../cosine.md) or constant terms are present.
