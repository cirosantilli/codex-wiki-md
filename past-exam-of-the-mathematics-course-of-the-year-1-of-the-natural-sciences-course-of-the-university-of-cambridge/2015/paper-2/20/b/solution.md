<h1 id="20/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The steady profile has no time [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md). Substituting $u=\widetilde u+u_s$ and using its steady equation cancels the forcing, giving

$$
\boxed{\widetilde u_t=\nu\widetilde u_{xx},\qquad
\widetilde u(0,t)=\widetilde u(1,t)=0}.
$$

The initial condition is

$$
\boxed{\widetilde u(x,0)=-u_s(x)=-\frac{G}{\nu n^2\pi^2}\sin(n\pi x)}.
$$

The resulting [heat equation](../../../../../heat-equation.md) is independent of $G$, as are its [boundary conditions](../../../../../boundary-condition.md). Its initial amplitude still depends on $G$; removing a source from the evolution equation does not remove it from the whole problem.
