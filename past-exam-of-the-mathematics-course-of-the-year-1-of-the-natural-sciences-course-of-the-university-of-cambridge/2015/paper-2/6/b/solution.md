<h1 id="6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Each Cartesian component of $a\mathbf r$ contributes $a$ to the [divergence](../../../../../divergence.md), while the constant vector contributes zero. Hence

$$
\boxed{\nabla\cdot(a\mathbf r-\mathbf b)=3a}.
$$
