<h1 id="1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

The [cross product](../../../../cross-product.md) of the two in-plane directions is $\mathbf u\times\mathbf v=(-5,1,2)$, with [Euclidean norm](../../../../euclidean-norm.md) $\sqrt{30}$. Either sign gives a [unit normal vector](../../../../unit-normal.md):

$$
\boxed{\hat{\mathbf n}=\pm\frac{(-5,1,2)}{\sqrt{30}}}.
$$

The [plane](../../../../plane.md) equation is $\hat{\mathbf n}\cdot(\mathbf r-\mathbf A)=0$, so the [distance from a point to a plane](../../../../distance-from-a-point-to-a-plane.md) gives

$$
\boxed{p=|\hat{\mathbf n}\cdot\mathbf A|=\frac2{\sqrt{30}}}.
$$
