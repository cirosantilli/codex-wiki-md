<h1 id="17/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The pairwise [dot products](../../../../../dot-product.md) of the normalized vectors are

$$
\frac{1-1}{2\sqrt2}=0,\qquad
\frac{1-2+1}{4}=0,\qquad
\frac{1-1}{2\sqrt2}=0.
$$

Their squared norms are respectively $(1+2+1)/4$, $(1+1)/2$, and $(1+2+1)/4$, all one. Hence they form an **[orthonormal basis](../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md)**. This also follows from the [spectral theorem for real symmetric matrices](../../../../../spectral-theorem-for-real-symmetric-matrices.md), because $D$ is symmetric and its three [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are distinct.
