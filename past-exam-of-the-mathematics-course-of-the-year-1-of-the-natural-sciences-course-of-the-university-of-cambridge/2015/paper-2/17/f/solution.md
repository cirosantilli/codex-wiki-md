<h1 id="17/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

The largest angular-frequency magnitude is $\omega_3=\sqrt{(2+\sqrt2)k/m}$. With overall amplitude $A$, the corresponding displacements are

$$
\boxed{x_1=x_3=\frac A2\cos(\omega_3t),\qquad
x_2=-\frac A{\sqrt2}\cos(\omega_3t)}.
$$

Thus the two outer masses move together, while the middle [mass](../../../../../mass.md) moves oppositely with $\sqrt2$ times their amplitude. All three have the same period $2\pi/\omega_3$.
