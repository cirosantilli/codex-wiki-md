<h1 id="17/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Each proposed displacement has second [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) $\ddot x_i=-\omega^2a_i\cos\omega t$. Matching the coefficients of the common time factor in the three equations gives

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
m\omega^2a_1&=k(2a_1-a_2),\\
m\omega^2a_2&=k(-a_1+2a_2-a_3),\\
m\omega^2a_3&=k(-a_2+2a_3).
\end{aligned}}
$$

These algebraic relations hold for the entire oscillation, including the isolated times when the common [cosine](../../../../../cosine.md) vanishes.
