<h1 id="17/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

An [eigenvalue equation](../../../../../eigenvalue-equation.md) has the form $A\mathbf a=\lambda\mathbf a$, where $\mathbf a\ne0$ is an [eigenvector](../../../../../eigenvector.md) and $\lambda$ its [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md). It means that the [linear map](../../../../../linear-map.md) preserves the vector's line and acts there by multiplication by $\lambda$. Nonzero solutions occur precisely when $\det(A-\lambda I)=0$; the zero vector is excluded because it would satisfy the equation for every $\lambda$.
