<h1 id="17/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Expanding the [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) along the first row gives

$$
\det(D-\lambda I)=(2-\lambda)\bigl[(2-\lambda)^2-2\bigr].
$$

The [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) and normalized spatial [eigenvectors](../../../../../eigenvector.md), in increasing frequency order, are

$$
\boxed{\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\lambda_j&\mathbf a^{(j)}&\omega_j^2\\\hline
2-\sqrt2&\tfrac12(1,\sqrt2,1)&(k/m)(2-\sqrt2)\\
2&\tfrac1{\sqrt2}(1,0,-1)&2k/m\\
2+\sqrt2&\tfrac12(1,-\sqrt2,1)&(k/m)(2+\sqrt2)
\end{array}}.
$$

For each mode both signs of $\omega_j$ give the same [cosine](../../../../../cosine.md) displacement. An arbitrary overall mode amplitude remains free. These vectors are the $N=3$ instances of the [sine eigenvectors of the fixed-end spring chain](../../../../../sine-eigenvectors-of-the-fixed-end-spring-chain.md).
