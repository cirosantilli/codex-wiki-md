<h1 id="17/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

With $\mathbf a=(a_1,a_2,a_3)^T$, the [eigenvalue equation](../../../../../eigenvalue-equation.md) is

$$
\boxed{D\mathbf a=\lambda\mathbf a,\qquad
D=\begin{pmatrix}2&-1&0\\-1&2&-1\\0&-1&2\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\lambda=\frac{m\omega^2}{k}}.
$$

The dimensionless matrix $D$ is the [stiffness matrix](../../../../../stiffness-matrix.md) divided by $k$; its [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) determine squared angular frequencies.
