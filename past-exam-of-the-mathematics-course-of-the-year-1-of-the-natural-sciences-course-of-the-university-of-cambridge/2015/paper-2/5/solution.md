<h1 id="5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

Taking each [partial derivative](../../../../partial-derivative.md) of the [scalar potential](../../../../scalar-potential.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbf F=\left(\cos(y^5)\sinh z,
-5xy^4\sin(y^5)\sinh z,
x\cos(y^5)\cosh z\right)}.
$$

The factor $5y^4$ in the middle component comes from the [chain rule](../../../../chain-rule.md). The potential is smooth, so the [curl of a gradient](../../../../curl-of-a-gradient.md) identity gives $\boxed{\nabla\times\mathbf F=\mathbf0}$.
