<h1 id="11/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Set $s=\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf r$. Taking the [dot product](../../../../../../dot-product.md) of the vector equation with $\mathbf a$ gives

$$
(1+\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b)s=\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf c.
$$

Here $\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b=5$ and $\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf c=6$, so $s=1$. Substitution then gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbf r=\mathbf c-\mathbf b=(1,0,0)}.
$$

Geometrically the possible vector must lie on the line $\mathbf c-s\mathbf b$, and the scalar [constraint](../../../../../../constraint-mechanics.md) picks one point. Equivalently the [rank-one matrix update](../../../../../../rank-one-matrix-update.md) $I+\mathbf b\mathbf a^T$ is invertible because $1+\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b=6\ne0$. This is the nonsingular case of [solvability of an affine rank-one vector equation](../../../../../../solvability-of-an-affine-rank-one-vector-equation.md).
