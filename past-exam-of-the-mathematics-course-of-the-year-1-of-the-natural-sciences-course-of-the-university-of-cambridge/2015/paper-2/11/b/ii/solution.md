<h1 id="11/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The original PDF has $\mathbf c=(-2,1,1)$ here; the transcription loses its minus sign. Now $\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b=-1$ and $\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf c=0$, so the scalar condition becomes $0=0$ and leaves $s$ arbitrary. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\mathbf r=\mathbf c-s\mathbf b=(-2-s,1+s,1+s),\qquad s\in\mathbb R}.
$$

The [solution set](../../../../../../solution-set.md) is a straight line parallel to $\mathbf b$, equivalently the intersection of $y=z$ and $x+y=-1$.

For the geometric mechanism, let $L=I+\mathbf b\mathbf a^T$. Since $\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b=-1$, $L^2=L$, $L\mathbf b=0$, and $\mathbf a\cdot L\mathbf r=0$. Thus $L$ is an [oblique projection](../../../../../../oblique-projection.md) onto the [plane](../../../../../../plane.md) $\mathbf a^\perp$ along $\mathbf b$. The specified $\mathbf c$ lies in this image [plane](../../../../../../plane.md), and adding any multiple of the [kernel](../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) direction $\mathbf b$ leaves its image unchanged.
