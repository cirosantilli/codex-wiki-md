<h1 id="13/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [curl](../../../../../curl.md) of the first [vector field](../../../../../vector-field.md) is

$$
\nabla\times\mathbf F=(0,-xy\sinh z,x\cosh z),
$$

which is not identically zero. Thus **$\mathbf F$ is not conservative** on $\mathbb R^3$ and cannot equal a negative [gradient](../../../../../gradient.md) there.

For the second field, direct differentiation shows

$$
\mathbf G=\nabla(x^2y\cosh z).
$$

Therefore **$\mathbf G$ is conservative**, and the requested negative-gradient potential is

$$
\boxed{\Phi=-x^2y\cosh z+C},\qquad\mathbf G=-\nabla\Phi.
$$

Its [curl](../../../../../curl.md) vanishes, consistently with the [curl of a gradient](../../../../../curl-of-a-gradient.md) identity.
