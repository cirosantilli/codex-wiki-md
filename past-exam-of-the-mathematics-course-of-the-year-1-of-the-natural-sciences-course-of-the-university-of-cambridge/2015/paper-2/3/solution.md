<h1 id="3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

The [Taylor series](../../../../taylor-series.md) of the logarithm gives

$$
\log(1+x^2)=x^2-\frac{x^4}{2}+O(x^6).
$$

The first nonzero term is therefore $\boxed{x^2}$. It is positive on either side of the origin, so the [stationary point](../../../../stationary-point.md) is a **[strict local minimum](../../../../strict-local-minimum.md)**. In fact $\log(1+x^2)\geq0$, with equality only at zero, making it the [global minimum](../../../../global-minimum.md) as well.
