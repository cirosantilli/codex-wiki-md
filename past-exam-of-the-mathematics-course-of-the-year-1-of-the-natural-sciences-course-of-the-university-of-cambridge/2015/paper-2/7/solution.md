<h1 id="7/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [7](../7.md)

On $x\geq1$, an [integrating factor](../../../../integrating-factor.md) is $1/x$. Multiplication gives $(y/x)'=1$, so the [general solution](../../../../general-solution.md) is $y=x^2+Cx$. The initial value $y(1)=1$ forces $C=0$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{y=x^2,\qquad x\geq1}.
$$
