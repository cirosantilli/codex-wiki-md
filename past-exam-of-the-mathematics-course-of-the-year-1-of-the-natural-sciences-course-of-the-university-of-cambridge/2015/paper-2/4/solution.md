<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

The [characteristic polynomial](../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) is $(1-\lambda)^2-4=(\lambda-3)(\lambda+1)$. For $\lambda=3$ the components are equal; for $\lambda=-1$ they are opposite. After normalization, the [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md) and associated [eigenvectors](../../../../eigenvector.md) are

$$
\boxed{\lambda_1=3,\quad\mathbf v_1=\frac{(1,1)}{\sqrt2};\qquad
\lambda_2=-1,\quad\mathbf v_2=\frac{(1,-1)}{\sqrt2}}.
$$

An overall sign change of either normalized [eigenvector](../../../../eigenvector.md) is equivalent.
