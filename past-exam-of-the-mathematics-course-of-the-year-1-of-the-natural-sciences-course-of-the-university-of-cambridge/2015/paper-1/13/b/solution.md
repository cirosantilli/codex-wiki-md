<h1 id="13/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a [Gaussian function](../../../../../gaussian-function.md), the required [derivatives](../../../../../derivative.md) are

$$
v'=-2cyv,\qquad v''=(-2c+4c^2y^2)v.
$$

Substitute these into the reduced [ordinary differential equation](../../../../../ordinary-differential-equation.md) and compare the constant and quadratic coefficients:

$$
-\frac12+cy^2=\lambda(-2c+4c^2y^2).
$$

The constant terms give $c=1/(4\lambda)$, and this value also satisfies the quadratic terms. Hence

$$
\boxed{c=\frac1{4\lambda},\qquad
u(x,t)=\frac1{\sqrt{t+a}}
\exp\left[-\frac{(x+b)^2}{4\lambda(t+a)}\right],\qquad t>-a.}
$$

A constant multiple is also a solution by [linearity](../../../../../linearity.md).
