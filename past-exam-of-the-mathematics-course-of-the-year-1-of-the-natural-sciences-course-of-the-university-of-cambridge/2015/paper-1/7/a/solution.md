<h1 id="7/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use the [binomial series](../../../../../binomial-series.md) with exponent $1/2$ and argument $-3x$:

$$
(1-3x)^{1/2}=1+\frac12(-3x)
+\frac{(1/2)(-1/2)}{2!}(-3x)^2+O(x^3).
$$

Thus the first three nonzero terms are

$$
\boxed{1-\frac32x-\frac98x^2+O(x^3).}
$$
