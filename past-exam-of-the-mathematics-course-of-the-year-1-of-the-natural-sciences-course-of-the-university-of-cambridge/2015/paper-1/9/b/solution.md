<h1 id="9/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [normal line to a plane curve](../../../../../normal-line-to-a-plane-curve.md) is perpendicular to the [tangent line](../../../../../tangent-line.md), so its slope is the negative reciprocal $1/\sqrt6$. The required equation is

$$
\boxed{y-1=\frac1{\sqrt6}\left(x-\frac{\sqrt6}{2}\right),
\qquad\text{equivalently}\quad y=\frac{x}{\sqrt6}+\frac12.}
$$

The normal direction also agrees with the [gradient](../../../../../gradient.md) $(4x,2y)$ of the defining level [function](../../../../../function-split.md).
