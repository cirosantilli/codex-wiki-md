<h1 id="9/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The positive horizontal coordinate is $x=\sqrt{3/2}=\sqrt6/2$. By [implicit differentiation](../../../../../implicit-differentiation.md),

$$
2y\frac{dy}{dx}+4x=0,
\qquad \frac{dy}{dx}=-\frac{2x}{y}.
$$

At the selected point this gives **$dy/dx=-\sqrt6$**.
