<h1 id="18/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $\mathbf e_1,\mathbf e_2,\mathbf e_3$ be the Cartesian [basis vectors](../../../../../basis-vector.md), with the given [vector](../../../../../vector.md) $\mathbf x=\mathbf e_1$. The [permutation matrix](../../../../../permutation-matrix.md) cycles them forward:

$$
\boxed{A\mathbf x=\begin{pmatrix}0\\1\\0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
A^2\mathbf x=\begin{pmatrix}0\\0\\1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
A^3\mathbf x=\begin{pmatrix}1\\0\\0\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

Thus three applications return the original [vector](../../../../../vector.md).
