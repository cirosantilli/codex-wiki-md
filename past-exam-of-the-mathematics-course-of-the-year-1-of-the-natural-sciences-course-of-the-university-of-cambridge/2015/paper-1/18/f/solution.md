↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

The [symmetric part of a matrix](../../../../../symmetric-part-of-a-matrix.md) is

$$
\boxed{S=\frac12(A+A^{\mathsf T}).}
$$

It satisfies $S^{\mathsf T}=S$. The complementary term $(A-A^{\mathsf T})/2$ is a [skew-symmetric matrix](../../../../../skew-symmetric-matrix.md).
