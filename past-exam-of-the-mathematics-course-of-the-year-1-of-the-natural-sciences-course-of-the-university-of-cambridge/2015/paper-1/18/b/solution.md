↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Interchanging the rows and columns gives the [matrix transpose](../../../../../transpose.md)

$$
\boxed{A^{\mathsf T}=\begin{pmatrix}0&1&0\\0&0&1\\1&0&0\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

It cycles the three [basis vectors](../../../../../basis-vector.md) in the opposite direction. Hence

$$
\boxed{A^{\mathsf T}\mathbf x=\begin{pmatrix}0\\0\\1\end{pmatrix},\quad
(A^{\mathsf T})^2\mathbf x=\begin{pmatrix}0\\1\\0\end{pmatrix},\quad
(A^{\mathsf T})^3\mathbf x=\begin{pmatrix}1\\0\\0\end{pmatrix}.}
$$
