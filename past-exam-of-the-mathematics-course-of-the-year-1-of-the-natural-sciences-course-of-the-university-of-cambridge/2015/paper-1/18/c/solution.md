<h1 id="18/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The preceding cycles show the action on a full [basis](../../../../../basis.md), not only on one direction: $A$ sends $\mathbf e_1\to\mathbf e_2\to\mathbf e_3\to\mathbf e_1$, and $A^{\mathsf T}$ reverses each arrow. Therefore either composition fixes every [basis vector](../../../../../basis-vector.md), and by [linearity](../../../../../linearity.md) fixes every [vector](../../../../../vector.md). Thus

$$
\boxed{A^{\mathsf T}A=AA^{\mathsf T}=I.}
$$

In particular $A$ is an [orthogonal matrix](../../../../../orthogonal-matrix.md), $A^{\mathsf T}=A^{-1}=A^2$, and $A^3=I$.
