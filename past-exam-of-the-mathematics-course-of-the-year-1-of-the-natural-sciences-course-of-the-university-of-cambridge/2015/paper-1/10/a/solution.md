<h1 id="10/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Pair the first and last terms of the [arithmetic series](../../../../../arithmetic-series.md). There are $500$ pairs, each summing to $1001$, so

$$
\boxed{N_1=\frac{1000\cdot1001}{2}=500\,500.}
$$
