↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [finite geometric series](../../../../../finite-geometric-series.md) has eleven terms, from $2^1$ through $2^{11}$. With common ratio $2$,

$$
\boxed{N_2=2\frac{2^{11}-1}{2-1}=4094.}
$$

Counting the powers explicitly avoids omitting the final $2048$ term.
