<h1 id="16/a/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

Let $I_j$ be the [indicator random variable](../../../../../../indicator-random-variable.md) of a black ball at draw $j$. The black count after three draws is $N(B_3)=I_1+I_2+I_3$. By [linearity of expectation](../../../../../../linearity-of-expectation.md),

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[N(B_3)]=\sum_{j=1}^3\mathbb E[I_j]
=3\frac47=\frac{12}{7}.}
$$

[Independence](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) is not required for this step. This is also the [mean](../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) of the corresponding [hypergeometric distribution](../../../../../../hypergeometric-distribution.md).
