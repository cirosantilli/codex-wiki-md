<h1 id="16/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Uniform [sampling without replacement](../../../../../../sampling-without-replacement.md) is equivalent to revealing a uniformly random [permutation](../../../../../../permutation.md) of seven labelled balls. Each draw [position](../../../../../../position.md) is equally likely to contain any label, so its colour has the original proportions. Thus

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(W_1)=\frac37,\qquad
\mathbb P(B_2)=\frac47,\qquad
\mathbb P(W_3)=\frac37.}
$$

For example the [law of total probability](../../../../../../law-of-total-probability.md) gives $\mathbb P(B_2)=(4/7)(3/6)+(3/7)(4/6)=4/7$. Equal marginal [probabilities](../../../../../../probability.md) express [exchangeability](../../../../../../exchangeable-random-variables.md), rather than [independence](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) between successive draws.
