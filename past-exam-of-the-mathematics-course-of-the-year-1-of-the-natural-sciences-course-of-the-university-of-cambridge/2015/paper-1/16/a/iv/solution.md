<h1 id="16/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Use the [inclusion-exclusion principle](../../../../../../inclusion-exclusion-principle.md) for two events:

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(B_1\cup B_2)
=\frac47+\frac47-\frac27=\frac67.}
$$

As a check, its complement is two white draws, with [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) $(3/7)(2/6)=1/7$.
