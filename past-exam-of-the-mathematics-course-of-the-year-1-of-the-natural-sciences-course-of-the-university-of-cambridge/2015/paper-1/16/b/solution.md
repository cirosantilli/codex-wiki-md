<h1 id="16/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Distinguish the $N$ balls by labels. For a fixed [position](../../../../../position.md) $n$, each label appears there in exactly $(N-1)!$ of the $N!$ equally likely [permutations](../../../../../permutation.md). Since $N_B$ labels are black,

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(B_n)=\frac{N_B(N-1)!}{N!}=\frac{N_B}{N},
\qquad 1\leq n\leq N.}
$$

This is the same marginal law at every [position](../../../../../position.md), even the last one. The argument averages over earlier draw outcomes; it does not assert that a [conditional probability](../../../../../conditional-probability.md) after a specified history remains $N_B/N$. The original PDF uses $B_n$, correcting the transcription's uppercase subscript $B_N$.
