<h1 id="12/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For a smooth [exact differential form](../../../../../../exact-differential-form.md) $Mdx+Ndy$, its coefficients must satisfy $M_y=N_x$ by [Clairaut's theorem](../../../../../../symmetry-of-second-derivatives.md). Here

$$
M_y=\sin x\cos y,\qquad N_x=-\sin x\cos y.
$$

These are not equal as [functions](../../../../../../function-split.md) on the plane, so **the form is not exact**. Equality at isolated points or along some lines is insufficient: an exactness test must hold throughout an open domain.
