<h1 id="12/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The two coefficients are the corresponding [partial derivatives](../../../../../../partial-derivative.md) of $e^{x+y}$. Therefore the [differential form](../../../../../../differential-form-split.md) is exact, with

$$
\boxed{f(x,y)=e^{x+y}+C,\qquad df=e^{x+y}dx+e^{x+y}dy.}
$$

The arbitrary constant does not change the [total differential](../../../../../../total-differential.md).
