<h1 id="12/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The coefficients follow by differentiating the same monomial:

$$
d(x^2y^3z^4)=2xy^3z^4dx+3x^2y^2z^4dy+4x^2y^3z^3dz.
$$

Thus this [exact differential form](../../../../../../exact-differential-form.md) has

$$
\boxed{f(x,y,z)=x^2y^3z^4+C.}
$$

Exhibiting a global [scalar potential](../../../../../../scalar-potential.md) proves exactness directly, without needing an additional domain argument.
