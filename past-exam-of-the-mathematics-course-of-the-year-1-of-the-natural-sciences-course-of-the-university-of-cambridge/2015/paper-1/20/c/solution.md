↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [curl](../../../../../curl.md) gives the [vorticity](../../../../../vorticity-split.md):

$$
\boldsymbol\nabla\times\mathbf v
=\bigl(-\partial_z(\alpha xz),\ \partial_z(-\alpha yz),\
\partial_x(\alpha xz)-\partial_y(-\alpha yz)\bigr)
=(-\alpha x,-\alpha y,2\alpha z).
$$

Since $x\mathbf i+y\mathbf j=\rho\widehat{\boldsymbol\rho}$, the requested form is

$$
\boxed{\boldsymbol\nabla\times\mathbf v
=-\alpha\rho\widehat{\boldsymbol\rho}+2\alpha z\mathbf k.}
$$

At the axis the radial [unit vector](../../../../../unit-vector.md) is undefined, but its coefficient vanishes and the Cartesian expression remains smooth. The radial [vorticity](../../../../../vorticity-split.md) reflects the change in rotation rate with height.
