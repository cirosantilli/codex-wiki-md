<h1 id="14/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Because $dx/dt>0$ along the arch, its area above the baseline is the [definite integral](../../../../../definite-integral.md) $\int y\,dx$. Changing to the parameter gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal A
&=a^2\int_0^{2\pi}(1-\cos t)^2dt\\
&=a^2\left[\int_0^{2\pi}1\,dt-2\int_0^{2\pi}\cos t\,dt
+\int_0^{2\pi}\cos^2t\,dt\right]\\
&=a^2(2\pi+\pi)=\boxed{3\pi a^2}.
\end{aligned}
$$

Adding or removing the two endpoint points does not change the area. This is the area formula in [area and length of a cycloid arch](../../../../../area-and-length-of-a-cycloid-arch.md).
