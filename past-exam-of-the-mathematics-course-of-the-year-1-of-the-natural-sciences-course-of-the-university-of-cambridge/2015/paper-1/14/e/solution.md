<h1 id="14/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The [arc length](../../../../../arc-length.md) follows from the [speed](../../../../../speed.md) of the parametrization:

$$
\sqrt{\left(\frac{dx}{dt}\right)^2+\left(\frac{dy}{dt}\right)^2}
=a\sqrt{2-2\cos t}=2a|\sin(t/2)|.
$$

Here $\sin(t/2)>0$ inside the given interval. Therefore

$$
\boxed{L=2a\int_0^{2\pi}\sin(t/2)dt=8a.}
$$

The result scales as a length, while the preceding area scales as $a^2$, as required by [dimensional analysis](../../../../../dimensional-analysis.md).
