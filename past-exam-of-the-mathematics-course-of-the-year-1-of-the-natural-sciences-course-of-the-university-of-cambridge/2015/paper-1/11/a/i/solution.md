<h1 id="11/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Direct substitution in the [Möbius transformation](../../../../../../mobius-transformation.md) gives

$$
w(0)=\frac{-i}{i}=\boxed{-1},\qquad
w(1)=\frac{1-i}{1+i}=\frac{(1-i)^2}{2}=\boxed{-i}.
$$

Neither substitution meets the excluded denominator point $z=-i$.
