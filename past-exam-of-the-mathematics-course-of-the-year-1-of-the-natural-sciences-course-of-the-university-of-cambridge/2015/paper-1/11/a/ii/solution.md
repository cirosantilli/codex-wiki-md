<h1 id="11/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For real $\lambda$, the numerator and denominator have equal [complex modulus](../../../../../../complex-modulus.md):

$$
|\lambda-i|=\sqrt{\lambda^2+1}=|\lambda+i|.
$$

The denominator is always nonzero. Hence

$$
\boxed{|w|=\frac{|\lambda-i|}{|\lambda+i|}=1.}
$$

Thus the real axis maps into the unit [circle](../../../../../../circle.md) in the [complex plane](../../../../../../complex-plane.md).
