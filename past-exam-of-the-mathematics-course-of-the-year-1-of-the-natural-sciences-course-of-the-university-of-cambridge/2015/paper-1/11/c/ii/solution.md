<h1 id="11/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

On this [circle](../../../../../../circle.md) the radial coordinate is constant, $r=2$, so **$\dot r=\ddot r=0$**. The [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) is a [tangent vector](../../../../../../tangent-vector-split.md) to the [circle](../../../../../../circle.md) in the anticlockwise direction, represented by $ie^{i\theta}$ when $\dot\theta>0$. Substituting into part (b),

$$
\boxed{\dot z=2i\dot\theta e^{i\theta},\qquad
\ddot z=(-2\dot\theta^2+2i\ddot\theta)e^{i\theta}.}
$$

The inward component is the [centripetal acceleration](../../../../../../centripetal-acceleration.md); the transverse component allows the [speed](../../../../../../speed.md) to change. Anticlockwise motion does **not** imply uniform [speed](../../../../../../speed.md) or $\ddot\theta=0$. The preceding figure includes the required tangent arrow.
