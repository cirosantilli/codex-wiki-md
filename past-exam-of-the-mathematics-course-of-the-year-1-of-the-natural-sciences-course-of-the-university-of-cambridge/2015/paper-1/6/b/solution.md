<h1 id="6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For this [parametric curve](../../../../../parametric-curve.md), the [chain rule](../../../../../chain-rule.md) gives

$$
\frac{dx}{dt}=-\sin t,\qquad \frac{dy}{dt}=3\cos(3t),\qquad
\boxed{\frac{dy}{dx}=-\frac{3\cos(3t)}{\sin t}.}
$$

The quotient is a finite slope when $\sin t\ne0$. At $t=0$ and $t=\pi$, the horizontal component of the tangent vanishes while the vertical component does not; these have vertical [tangent lines](../../../../../tangent-line.md), rather than missing finite values of the displayed quotient.
