<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

On any interval where $\sin x>0$, the [chain rule](../../../../../chain-rule.md) gives $y'=\cos x/\sin x=\cot x$. Differentiating again,

$$
\boxed{y''=-\csc^2x=-\frac1{\sin^2x}.}
$$

The domain condition is necessary because the real [natural logarithm](../../../../../natural-logarithm.md) requires a positive argument.
