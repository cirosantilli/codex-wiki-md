<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

At a single point of contact the line is a [tangent line](../../../../../tangent-line.md), so its slope equals the [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) of the [exponential function](../../../../../exponential-function.md). At the contact coordinate $x_0$,

$$
m=e^{x_0},\qquad mx_0=e^{x_0}.
$$

Since $m>0$, dividing gives $x_0=1$, hence **$m=e$**. To verify uniqueness, the difference $e^x-ex$ has positive second [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) everywhere and its unique minimum is zero at $x=1$. Thus this line touches the curve once.
