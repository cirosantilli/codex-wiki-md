<h1 id="15/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use the [Maclaurin series](../../../../../../maclaurin-series.md) $\sin x=x-x^3/6+O(x^5)$ and $(1+x)^{-2}=1-2x+3x^2+O(x^3)$. Multiplying through the cubic order gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{\sin x}{(1+x)^2}
=x-2x^2+\frac{17}{6}x^3+O(x^4).}
$$

These are the first three nonzero terms; the constant term vanishes.
