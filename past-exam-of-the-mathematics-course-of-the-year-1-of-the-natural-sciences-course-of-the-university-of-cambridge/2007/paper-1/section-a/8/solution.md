<h1 id="section-a/8/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [8](../8.md)

Call the centre of the [circle](../../../../../circle.md) $O$, the left endpoint on the diameter $A$, the point of tangency $T$, and the external intersection $E$. Since $OA=OT$ are [radii](../../../../../radius.md), the [isosceles triangle](../../../../../isosceles-triangle.md) $AOT$ has equal base [angles](../../../../../angle.md): $\angle OAT=\angle ATO=30^\circ$. Therefore $\angle AOT=120^\circ$, and, because $OA$ and $OE$ point in opposite directions, $\angle TOE=60^\circ$. The [tangent to a circle](../../../../../tangent-to-a-circle.md) $TE$ is perpendicular to the [radius](../../../../../radius.md) $OT$, so in the right [triangle](../../../../../triangle.md) $OTE$,

$$
\boxed{\theta=180^\circ-90^\circ-60^\circ=30^\circ.}
$$
