<h1 id="section-a/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

The [factor theorem](../../../../../factor-theorem.md) gives a factor $x-1$. [Polynomial division](../../../../../polynomial-division.md) and then factorization give

$$
2x^3+x^2-5x+2=(x-1)(2x^2+3x-2)=(x-1)(2x-1)(x+2).
$$

Consequently all three [roots of a polynomial](../../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md) are

$$
\boxed{x=1,\quad\frac12,\quad-2.}
$$
