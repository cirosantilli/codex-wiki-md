<h1 id="section-a/7/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [7](../7.md)

The double-angle identity for the [sine](../../../../../sine.md) factors the equation as $2\cos\theta(\sin\theta-\cos\theta)=0$. The first factor gives $\theta=\pi/2$ in the specified interval; the second gives $\tan\theta=1$ and hence $\theta=\pi/4$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\theta=\frac\pi4,\quad\frac\pi2.}
$$

Keeping the first factor avoids losing the solution where division by $\cos\theta$ would be invalid.
