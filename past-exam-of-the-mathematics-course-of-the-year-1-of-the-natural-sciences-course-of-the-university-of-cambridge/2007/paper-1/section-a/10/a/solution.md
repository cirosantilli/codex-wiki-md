<h1 id="section-a/10/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) is nonnegative and satisfies $\cos^2(\pi-x)=\cos^2x$. Its [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) is $-\sin(2x)$, so it decreases from $(0,1)$ to its [minimum](../../../../../../minimum-of-a-subset-of-a-total-order.md) $(\pi/2,0)$ and then increases to $(\pi,1)$. These three points all have horizontal [tangent lines](../../../../../../tangent-line.md).
