<h1 id="section-a/10/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The denominator vanishes only at $x=0$, giving a vertical [asymptote](../../../../../../asymptote.md). Since $1-e^x=-x+O(x^2)$, the [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) tends to $+\infty$ from the left and $-\infty$ from the right. Its [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) is

$$
\frac{d}{dx}\frac1{1-e^x}=\frac{e^x}{(1-e^x)^2}>0,
$$

so each branch is increasing. The horizontal [asymptotes](../../../../../../asymptote.md) are $y=1$ as $x\to-\infty$ and $y=0$ as $x\to+\infty$. The left branch lies above $1$; the right branch lies below $0$, as in the preceding figure.
