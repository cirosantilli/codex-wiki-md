<h1 id="section-a/6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

On this [parametric curve](../../../../../parametric-curve.md), $x=1$ permits $t=0$ or $t=-2$, but only $t=0$ also gives $y=0$. The [chain rule](../../../../../chain-rule.md) gives the [slope](../../../../../slope.md)

$$
\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{dy/dt}{dx/dt}=\frac{12-3t^2}{2(1+t)}.
$$

At the required point, $t=0$, so

$$
\boxed{\frac{dy}{dx}=6.}
$$
