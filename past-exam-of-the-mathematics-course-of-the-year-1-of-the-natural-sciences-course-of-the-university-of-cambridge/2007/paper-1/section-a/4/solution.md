[Completing the square](../../../../../completing-the-square.md) puts the equation of the [circle](../../../../../circle.md) in the form

$$
x^2+(y+3)^2=4.
$$

Hence its **centre is $(0,-3)$** and its [radius](../../../../../radius.md) is **$2$**.
