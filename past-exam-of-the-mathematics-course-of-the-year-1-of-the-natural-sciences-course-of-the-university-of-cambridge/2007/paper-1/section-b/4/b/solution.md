↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $D$, $C$, and $V_0$ denote the three disjoint categories, and $+$ or $-$ the test result. The [law of total probability](../../../../../../law-of-total-probability.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(+)=0.1(0.95)+0.2(0.9)+0.7(0.05)=0.31.}
$$

To calculate the requested [conditional probability](../../../../../../conditional-probability.md), first combine the two categories with the virus. Their joint negative-test [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) is $0.1(0.05)+0.2(0.1)=0.025$, whereas $\mathbb P(-)=1-0.31=0.69$. [Bayes' theorem](../../../../../../bayes-theorem.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(D\cup C\mid-)=\frac{0.025}{0.69}=\frac5{138}\approx0.03623.}
$$

These numbers use the specified population proportions and test [probabilities](../../../../../../probability.md); the negative result reduces the initial combined proportion $0.3$ to about $3.62\%$ within this model.
