<h1 id="section-b/5/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Writing the [differential one-form](../../../../../../../one-form.md) as $M\,dx+N\,dy$, its coefficients satisfy $M_y=e^y\cos x=N_x$. It is an [exact differential form](../../../../../../../exact-differential-form.md), with potential $F=(1+e^y)\sin x$, because

$$
dF=(1+e^y)\cos x\,dx+e^y\sin x\,dy.
$$

The implicit solution is therefore

$$
\boxed{(1+e^y)\sin x=C.}
$$
