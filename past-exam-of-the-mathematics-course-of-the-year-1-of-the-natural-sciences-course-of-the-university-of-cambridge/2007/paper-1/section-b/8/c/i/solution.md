<h1 id="section-b/8/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Computing the [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) with the convention $\det(\lambda I-H)$ gives

$$
\det(\lambda I-H)=\lambda(\lambda+1)(\lambda-3).
$$

Thus the three [eigenvalues](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are

$$
\boxed{-1,\quad0,\quad3.}
$$

They can also be checked directly on the respective [eigenvectors](../../../../../../../eigenvector.md) $(1,0,-1)^T$, $(1,-1,1)^T$, and $(1,2,1)^T$.
