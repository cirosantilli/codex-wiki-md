<h1 id="section-b/8/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

On an [eigenvector](../../../../../../../eigenvector.md) of $A$ with [eigenvalue](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $\lambda$, the [linear operator](../../../../../../../linear-operator.md) $B=I-A$ has [eigenvalue](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $1-\lambda$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{spec}(B)\subseteq\{0,1\}.}
$$

The multiplicities are exchanged: if the [matrix rank](../../../../../../../matrix-rank.md) of $A$ is $r$, then $B$ has $n-r$ copies of $1$ and $r$ copies of $0$. Again, a value with zero multiplicity is absent.
