<h1 id="section-b/8/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

If $A\mathbf v=\lambda\mathbf v$ for a nonzero [eigenvector](../../../../../../../eigenvector.md), then $A^2\mathbf v=\lambda^2\mathbf v=A\mathbf v=\lambda\mathbf v$. Thus $\lambda(\lambda-1)=0$, so

$$
\boxed{\text{every eigenvalue is }0\text{ or }1.}
$$

Both values need not occur: the zero [matrix](../../../../../../../matrix.md) has only $0$, and the [identity matrix](../../../../../../../identity-matrix.md) only $1$. More precisely, an [idempotent linear map](../../../../../../../projection-linear-algebra.md) whose [rank of a linear map](../../../../../../../rank-of-a-linear-map.md) is $r$ has $r$ copies of $1$ and $n-r$ copies of $0$. Indeed, its [image of a linear map](../../../../../../../image-of-a-linear-map.md) and [kernel of a linear map](../../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) form a [direct sum](../../../../../../../direct-sum.md), giving an [eigenbasis](../../../../../../../eigenbasis.md) without assuming an [orthogonal projection](../../../../../../../orthogonal-projection.md).
