<h1 id="section-b/8/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**True.** Applying the [matrices](../../../../../../../matrix.md) in order gives $AB\mathbf x=A(b\mathbf x)=ab\mathbf x$. The [eigenvalue](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) is

$$
\boxed{ab.}
$$

This conclusion only uses the shared [eigenvector](../../../../../../../eigenvector.md); it does not require $A$ and $B$ to commute on other [vectors](../../../../../../../vector.md).
