↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**False.** Take the [diagonal matrix](../../../../../../../diagonal-matrix.md) $A=\operatorname{diag}(1,2)$ and its [eigenvectors](../../../../../../../eigenvector.md) $\mathbf x=(1,0)^T$, $\mathbf y=(0,1)^T$. Their sum $(1,1)^T$ is mapped to $(1,2)^T$, which is not a [scalar multiple](../../../../../../../scalar-multiple.md) of that sum. More generally, $A(\mathbf x+\mathbf y)=a\mathbf x+c\mathbf y$ need not have the form $\lambda(\mathbf x+\mathbf y)$ when $a\ne c$.
