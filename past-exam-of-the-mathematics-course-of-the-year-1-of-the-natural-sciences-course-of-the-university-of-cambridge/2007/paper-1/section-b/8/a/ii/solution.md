↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**True.** By [linearity](../../../../../../../linearity.md), $(A+B)\mathbf x=A\mathbf x+B\mathbf x=(a+b)\mathbf x$. Since $\mathbf x\ne0$, it is an [eigenvector](../../../../../../../eigenvector.md) of $A+B$ with [eigenvalue](../../../../../../../eigenvalue.md)

$$
\boxed{a+b.}
$$
