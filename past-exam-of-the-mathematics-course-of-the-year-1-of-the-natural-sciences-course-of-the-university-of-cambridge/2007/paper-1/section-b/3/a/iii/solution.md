<h1 id="section-b/3/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The square is on the [cosine](../../../../../../../cosine.md): near zero, $\ln(\cos^2x)=2\ln(\cos x)$. Substituting $\cos x=1-x^2/2+x^4/24+O(x^6)$ into the [logarithmic series](../../../../../../../mercator-series.md) gives

$$
\ln(\cos x)=-\frac{x^2}{2}+\frac{x^4}{24}-\frac12\frac{x^4}{4}+O(x^6)=-\frac{x^2}{2}-\frac{x^4}{12}+O(x^6).
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{\ln(\cos^2x)=-x^2-\frac{x^4}{6}+O(x^6).}
$$
