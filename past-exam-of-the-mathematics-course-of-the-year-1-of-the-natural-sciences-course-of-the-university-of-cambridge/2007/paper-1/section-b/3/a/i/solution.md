<h1 id="section-b/3/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Since the [derivative](../../../../../../../derivative.md) of the [arctangent](../../../../../../../inverse-tangent.md) is $1/(1+x^2)=1-x^2+O(x^4)$ near zero, termwise [integration](../../../../../../../integral-split.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\arctan x=x-\frac{x^3}{3}+O(x^5).}
$$

The integration constant is zero because $\arctan0=0$.
