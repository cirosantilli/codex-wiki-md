<h1 id="section-b/3/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The expression in the PDF is $1/a^x-1$, with the subtraction outside the fraction. Using the [exponential series](../../../../../../../exponential-series.md) with $a^{-x}=e^{-x\ln a}$ gives

$$
\boxed{a^{-x}-1=-(\ln a)x+\frac{(\ln a)^2}{2}x^2+O(x^3).}
$$

For $a>0$, $a\ne1$, these are the first two nonzero terms. If $a=1$, the [function](../../../../../../../function-split.md) is identically zero, so there are no nonvanishing terms.
