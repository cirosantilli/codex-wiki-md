<h1 id="section-b/3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Since $g(0)=0$, only the first three positive powers of $g$ contribute through degree three. The [composition coefficients of power series](../../../../../../composition-coefficients-of-power-series.md) follow by using $g^2=g_1^2x^2+2g_1g_2x^3+O(x^4)$ and $g^3=g_1^3x^3+O(x^4)$ in $f_0+f_1g+f_2g^2+f_3g^3+\cdots$. Hence

$$
\boxed{f(g(x))=f_0+f_1g_1x+(f_1g_2+f_2g_1^2)x^2+(f_1g_3+2f_2g_1g_2+f_3g_1^3)x^3+O(x^4).}
$$

Here $f_m$ and $g_m$ are coefficients of the [power series](../../../../../../power-series.md), rather than raw [derivatives](../../../../../../derivative.md); their definitions already contain any factorial factors.
