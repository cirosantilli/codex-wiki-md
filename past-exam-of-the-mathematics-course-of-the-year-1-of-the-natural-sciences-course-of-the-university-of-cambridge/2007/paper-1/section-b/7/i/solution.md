<h1 id="section-b/7/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The region is $0\leq y\leq1$, $2y\leq x\leq2$. Reversing the order describes the same triangle as $0\leq x\leq2$, $0\leq y\leq x/2$. Since the [integrand](../../../../../../integrand.md) is continuous on this compact region, [Fubini's theorem](../../../../../../fubini-s-theorem.md) permits

$$
\int_0^1\int_{2y}^2 e^{x^2}\,dx\,dy=\int_0^2\int_0^{x/2}e^{x^2}\,dy\,dx=\frac12\int_0^2xe^{x^2}\,dx=\boxed{\frac{e^4-1}{4}}.
$$

The new inner [integral](../../../../../../integral-split.md) introduces the factor $x$ needed for the [change of variables](../../../../../../change-of-variables-formula.md) $u=x^2$.
