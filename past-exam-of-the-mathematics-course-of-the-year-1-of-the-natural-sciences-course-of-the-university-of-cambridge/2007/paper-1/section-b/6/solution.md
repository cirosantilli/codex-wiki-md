<h1 id="section-b/6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

Put $S=\theta_1+\theta_2$ and $D=\theta_1-\theta_2$. The [gradient](../../../../../gradient.md) has components $3\sin S+\sin D$ and $3\sin S-\sin D$, so a [stationary point](../../../../../stationary-point.md) requires $\sin S=\sin D=0$. In the closed square, $S\in[0,2\pi]$ and $D\in[-\pi,\pi]$, and the compatible possibilities are precisely the four corners and $(\pi/2,\pi/2)$.

The [Hessian matrix](../../../../../hessian-matrix.md) is

$$
\begin{pmatrix}3\cos S+\cos D&3\cos S-\cos D\\3\cos S-\cos D&3\cos S+\cos D\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Its [eigenvectors](../../../../../eigenvector.md) $(1,1)$ and $(1,-1)$ have [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $6\cos S$ and $2\cos D$. The [second-derivative test](../../../../../second-derivative-test.md) gives the following classification:

| Point || $U$ || Hessian eigenvalues || Classification | $(0,0),(\pi,\pi)$ | $-4$ | $6,2$ | [Minima](../../../../../minimum-of-a-subset-of-a-total-order.md) | $(\pi,0),(0,\pi)$ | $4$ | $-6,-2$ | [Maxima](../../../../../maximum-of-a-subset-of-a-total-order.md) | $(\pi/2,\pi/2)$ | $2$ | $-6,2$ | [Saddle point of a scalar function](../../../../../saddle-point-of-a-scalar-function.md) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

The corner classifications also hold for the smooth extension beyond the square, and hence for the restricted region. The global bounds $-4\leq U\leq4$ show that the stated [minima](../../../../../minimum-of-a-subset-of-a-total-order.md) and [maxima](../../../../../maximum-of-a-subset-of-a-total-order.md) are global within it. The central point has opposite curvature along the two diagonal directions.
