<h1 id="section-b/1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The displayed [power series](../../../../../../power-series.md) is the [matrix exponential](../../../../../../matrix-exponential.md) $G=e^H$. From $H^3=-\alpha^2H$, its odd powers and nonconstant even powers sum separately:

$$
G=I+\left(1-\frac{\alpha^2}{3!}+\frac{\alpha^4}{5!}-\cdots\right)H+\left(\frac1{2!}-\frac{\alpha^2}{4!}+\frac{\alpha^4}{6!}-\cdots\right)H^2.
$$

The scalar [power series](../../../../../../power-series.md) are $\sin\alpha/\alpha$ and $(1-\cos\alpha)/\alpha^2$, with continuous values $1$ and $1/2$ at zero. Substituting $H\mathbf x=\alpha\mathbf n\times\mathbf x$ and the preceding formula for $H^2\mathbf x$ yields

$$
G\mathbf x=\mathbf x+\sin\alpha\,\mathbf n\times\mathbf x+(1-\cos\alpha)\mathbf n\times(\mathbf n\times\mathbf x).
$$

This is the [Rodrigues rotation formula](../../../../../../rodrigues-rotation-formula.md): the component parallel to $\mathbf n$ is fixed, while the perpendicular component undergoes a [rotation](../../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) through $\alpha$. For $\alpha=0$ the [linear operator](../../../../../../linear-operator.md) is the [identity operator](../../../../../../identity-operator.md).
