<h1 id="section-b/1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Because $\mathbf b\cdot\mathbf x$ is a [scalar](../../../../../../scalar.md), applying the [linear operator](../../../../../../linear-operator.md) again gives

$$
K^2\mathbf x=K\bigl[\mathbf a(\mathbf b\cdot\mathbf x)\bigr]=\mathbf a\bigl[\mathbf b\cdot\mathbf a(\mathbf b\cdot\mathbf x)\bigr]=(\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b)K\mathbf x.
$$

This is the first nontrivial case of the [powers of a rank-one operator](../../../../../../powers-of-a-rank-one-operator.md); its [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) is the [outer product](../../../../../../outer-product.md) $\mathbf a\mathbf b^T$.
