<h1 id="section-b/10/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Assume $h$ is continuously [differentiable](../../../../../../../differentiable-function.md) on the interval concerned. For fixed $x>0$, take $u=h(t)$ and $dv=(x-t)^{-1/2}dt$, so $v=-2\sqrt{x-t}$. [Integration by parts](../../../../../../../integration-by-parts.md), interpreted first with an endpoint cutoff and then by a [limit](../../../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md), gives

$$
g(x)=\left[-2h(t)\sqrt{x-t}\right]_{t=0}^{t=x}+2\int_0^xh'(t)\sqrt{x-t}\,dt=2h(0)\sqrt x+2\int_0^xh'(t)\sqrt{x-t}\,dt.
$$

The upper endpoint term vanishes, and the lower endpoint supplies the explicit square-root term.
