<h1 id="section-b/10/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The kernel has an [integrable singularity](../../../../../../../integrable-singularity.md) at the moving endpoint $t=x$. Direct substitution into the ordinary [Leibniz integral rule](../../../../../../../leibniz-integral-rule.md) would produce the undefined boundary value $h(x)/\sqrt{x-x}$ and the nonintegrable differentiated kernel $-h(t)/[2(x-t)^{3/2}]$. Those separately divergent expressions cannot be treated as ordinary finite terms. The [derivative of an Abel integral](../../../../../../../derivative-of-an-abel-integral.md) is obtained by rewriting the [integral](../../../../../../../integral-split.md) before differentiating.
