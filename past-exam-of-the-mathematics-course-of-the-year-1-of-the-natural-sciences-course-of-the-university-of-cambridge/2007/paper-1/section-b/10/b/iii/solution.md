<h1 id="section-b/10/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Differentiate the rewritten expression. Its new kernel vanishes at the moving upper endpoint, and its [partial derivative](../../../../../../../partial-derivative.md) is an [integrable](../../../../../../../integrability.md) inverse square root. The [derivative of an Abel integral](../../../../../../../derivative-of-an-abel-integral.md) is

$$
\boxed{g'(x)=\frac{h(0)}{\sqrt x}+\int_0^x\frac{h'(t)}{\sqrt{x-t}}\,dt,\qquad x>0.}
$$

For a rigorous route, rewrite $2\int_0^xh'(t)\sqrt{x-t}\,dt$ as $\int_0^x\int_0^s h'(t)/\sqrt{s-t}\,dt\,ds$ using [Fubini's theorem](../../../../../../../fubini-s-theorem.md), and apply the [fundamental theorem of calculus](../../../../../../../fundamental-theorem-of-calculus.md) to the continuous inner [integral](../../../../../../../integral-split.md). The assumption $h(0)\ne0$ implies $g(x)=2h(0)\sqrt x+O(x^{3/2})$, so the extension $g(0)=0$ has no finite right [derivative](../../../../../../../derivative.md) at zero.
