<h1 id="section-a/4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Each component depends only on its own coordinate, so all cross [partial derivatives](../../../../../../partial-derivative.md) vanish. Therefore the [curl](../../../../../../curl.md) is

$$
\boxed{\nabla\times\mathbf f=\mathbf0.}
$$

This also follows from the identity that the [curl of a gradient](../../../../../../curl-of-a-gradient.md) vanishes for a twice continuously [differentiable](../../../../../../differentiable-function.md) [potential energy](../../../../../../potential-energy.md).
