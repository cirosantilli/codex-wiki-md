<h1 id="section-a/6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

The two factors have [moduli](../../../../../modulus.md) $\sqrt2$ and $2$, and [complex arguments](../../../../../argument-complex-analysis.md) $\pi/4$ and $\pi/6$. Multiplication multiplies the [moduli](../../../../../modulus.md) and adds the [complex arguments](../../../../../argument-complex-analysis.md), giving

$$
\boxed{|z|=2\sqrt2,\qquad\arg z=\frac{5\pi}{12}\pmod{2\pi}.}
$$

The displayed angle is the [principal argument](../../../../../principal-argument.md) in radians; equivalently the argument is $75^\circ$ modulo $360^\circ$.
