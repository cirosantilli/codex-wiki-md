<h1 id="section-a/9/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

There are $\binom72=21$ equally likely unordered pairs, and $3\cdot4=12$ contain one ball of each colour. Thus the [hypergeometric distribution](../../../../../../hypergeometric-distribution.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(\text{different colours})=\frac{12}{21}=\frac47.}
$$
