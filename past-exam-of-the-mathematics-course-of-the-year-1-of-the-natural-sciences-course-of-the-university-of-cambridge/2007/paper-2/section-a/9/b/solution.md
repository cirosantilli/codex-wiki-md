<h1 id="section-a/9/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $I_1,I_2$ be [indicator random variables](../../../../../../indicator-random-variable.md) for the two draws being red. Each draw has red [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) $3/7$, even though the draws are dependent. By [linearity of expectation](../../../../../../linearity-of-expectation.md), the number $R=I_1+I_2$ satisfies

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[R]=\frac37+\frac37=\frac67.}
$$
