<h1 id="section-b/7/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [permutation matrix](../../../../../../permutation-matrix.md) cycles the three coordinate [vectors](../../../../../../vector.md), so three applications give $C^3=I$. Consequently $C^2C=CC^2=I$, proving $C^{-1}=C^2$. Since $16=5\cdot3+1$,

$$
\boxed{C^{16}=C=\begin{pmatrix}0&0&1\\1&0&0\\0&1&0\end{pmatrix}.}
$$
