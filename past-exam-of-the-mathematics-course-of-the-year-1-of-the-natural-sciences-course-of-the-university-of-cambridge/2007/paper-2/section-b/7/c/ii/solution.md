<h1 id="section-b/7/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $\mu\ne0$, the [adjugate matrix](../../../../../../../adjugate-matrix.md) formula, or elimination on the three unit right-hand sides, gives

$$
\boxed{M^{-1}=\begin{pmatrix}\frac1{2\mu}&\frac12&-\frac1{2\mu}\\\frac12&-\frac\mu2&\frac12\\-\frac1{2\mu}&\frac12&\frac1{2\mu}\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

Multiplication by $M$ gives the [identity matrix](../../../../../../../identity-matrix.md), checking all signs and coefficients.
