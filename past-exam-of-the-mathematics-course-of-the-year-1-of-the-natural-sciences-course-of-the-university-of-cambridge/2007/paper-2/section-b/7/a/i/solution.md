↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**True.** Multiplicativity of the [determinant](../../../../../../../determinant.md) and the identity $\det(B^{-1})=1/\det B$ give $\det(B^{-1}AB)=\det(B^{-1})\det A\det B=\det A$. This is invariance under [matrix similarity](../../../../../../../matrix-similarity.md).
