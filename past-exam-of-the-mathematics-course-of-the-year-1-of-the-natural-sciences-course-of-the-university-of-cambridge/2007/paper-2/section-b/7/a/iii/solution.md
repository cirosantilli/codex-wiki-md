<h1 id="section-b/7/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**True.** Distribute the [matrix product](../../../../../../../matrix-product.md) while retaining its order:

$$
A^{-1}(A+B)B^{-1}=A^{-1}AB^{-1}+A^{-1}BB^{-1}=B^{-1}+A^{-1}=A^{-1}+B^{-1}.
$$

Only addition is commuted; no commutation of $A$ and $B$ is assumed.
