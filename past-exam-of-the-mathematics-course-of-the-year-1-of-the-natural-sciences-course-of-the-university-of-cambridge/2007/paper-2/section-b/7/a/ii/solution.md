↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**False.** The [cyclic property of the trace](../../../../../../../cyclic-property-of-the-trace.md) permits $ABC\mapsto BCA$, but not an arbitrary interchange. Even with $A$ and $B$ both [invertible matrices](../../../../../../../invertible-matrix.md), take

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&2\end{pmatrix},\quad B=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\quad C=\begin{pmatrix}0&0\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Then $\operatorname{Tr}(ABC)=1$ and $\operatorname{Tr}(BAC)=2$, providing a counterexample.
