<h1 id="section-b/6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Integrating $\psi_x=-(2xy+z)$ with respect to $x$ gives $\psi=-x^2y-xz+h(y,z)$. Matching $\psi_y=-(x^2+2y)$ requires $h=-y^2+j(z)$, and matching $\psi_z=-x$ requires $j$ constant. A [scalar potential](../../../../../../scalar-potential.md) with the requested sign is

$$
\boxed{\psi=-x^2y-xz-y^2+C.}
$$

Thus the [force field](../../../../../../force-field-physics.md) is a [conservative vector field](../../../../../../conservative-vector-field.md) on all of $\mathbb R^3$.
