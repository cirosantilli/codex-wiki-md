<h1 id="section-b/6/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Parametrize the [straight line](../../../../../../straight-line.md) by $\mathbf x(t)=(t,t,t)$, $0\leq t\leq1$. The [line integral](../../../../../../line-integral.md) is

$$
\int\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf x=\int_0^1\bigl[(2t^2+t)+(t^2+2t)+t\bigr]dt=\int_0^1(3t^2+4t)dt=\boxed{3}.
$$

The change in [scalar potential](../../../../../../scalar-potential.md) is $\psi(1,1,1)-\psi(0,0,0)=-3$, so the answer is $-\Delta\psi$, as required by the [fundamental theorem for line integrals](../../../../../../fundamental-theorem-for-line-integrals.md) for $\mathbf F=-\nabla\psi$.
