<h1 id="section-b/5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

If $u\,dx+v\,dy=dF$ with continuous second [partial derivatives](../../../../../../partial-derivative.md) of $F$, then $u=F_x$ and $v=F_y$. The [symmetry of second derivatives](../../../../../../symmetry-of-second-derivatives.md) gives the necessary condition for an [exact differential form](../../../../../../exact-differential-form.md):

$$
\boxed{\left(\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}\right)_x=\left(\frac{\partial v}{\partial x}\right)_y.}
$$

This condition is also locally sufficient on a small rectangle for continuously [differentiable](../../../../../../differentiable-function.md) coefficients; global sufficiency additionally needs suitable topology of the domain.
