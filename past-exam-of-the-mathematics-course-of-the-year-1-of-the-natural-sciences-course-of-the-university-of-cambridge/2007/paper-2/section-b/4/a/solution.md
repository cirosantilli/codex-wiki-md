<h1 id="section-b/4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [characteristic equation](../../../../../../characteristic-equation-of-a-constant-coefficient-differential-equation.md) for the [homogeneous solution](../../../../../../homogeneous-solution.md) is $(r+2)^2=0$, giving $(C_1+C_2x)e^{-2x}$. For a [particular solution](../../../../../../particular-solution.md), substitute $Ae^{2x}$: the left side becomes $16Ae^{2x}$, so $A=1/16$. The [general solution](../../../../../../general-solution.md) is

$$
\boxed{y=(C_1+C_2x)e^{-2x}+\frac1{16}e^{2x}.}
$$
