<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The function $x^2\sin x$ is an [odd function](../../../../../odd-function.md), since $x^2$ is even and the [sine](../../../../../sine.md) is odd. Its [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md) on the symmetric interval therefore cancels:

$$
\boxed{\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}x^2\sin x\,dx=0.}
$$
