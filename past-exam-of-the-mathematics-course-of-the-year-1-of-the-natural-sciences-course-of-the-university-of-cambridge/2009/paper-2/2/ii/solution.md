<h1 id="2/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The double-angle identity gives $\cos^2x-\sin^2x=\cos(2x)$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\int_0^\pi(\cos^2x-\sin^2x)dx=\left[\tfrac12\sin(2x)\right]_0^\pi=0.}
$$
