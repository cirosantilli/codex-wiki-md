<h1 id="20/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Write $v=(x+1)^l(x-1)^l$. In the [Leibniz rule](../../../../../leibniz-rule.md) for its $l$th derivative at one, any term with fewer than $l$ derivatives on $(x-1)^l$ vanishes. The sole surviving term leaves $(x+1)^l$ undifferentiated and differentiates the second factor $l$ times, giving

$$
v^{(l)}(1)=2^l l!.
$$

The normalization in [Rodrigues' formula](../../../../../rodrigues-formula.md) consequently gives

$$
\boxed{P_l(1)=\frac{2^ll!}{2^ll!}=1.}
$$

The same argument covers $l=0$, with both zeroth powers equal to one.
