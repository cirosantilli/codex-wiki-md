<h1 id="20/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The polynomial $v$ has leading term $x^{2l}$. Its $l$th derivative has leading term $(2l)!x^l/l!$, and all other terms have lower degree. Thus the [Rodrigues formula for Legendre polynomials](../../../../../rodrigues-formula-for-legendre-polynomials.md) defines a polynomial with nonzero leading coefficient

$$
\boxed{P_l(x)=\frac{(2l)!}{2^l(l!)^2}x^l+\text{lower-degree terms}.}
$$

It therefore has degree exactly $l$, including the constant case $P_0=1$.
