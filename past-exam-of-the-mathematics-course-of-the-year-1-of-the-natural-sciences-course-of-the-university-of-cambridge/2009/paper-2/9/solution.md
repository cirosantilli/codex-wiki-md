<h1 id="9/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [9](../9.md)

The inequality is equivalent to $|\cos\theta|>1/\sqrt2$. The [cosine](../../../../cosine.md) exceeds $1/\sqrt2$ close to zero and $2\pi$, and is less than $-1/\sqrt2$ close to $\pi$. Within the specified interval this gives

$$
\boxed{\theta\in[0,\pi/4)\ \cup\ (3\pi/4,5\pi/4)\ \cup\ (7\pi/4,2\pi].}
$$

The four equality angles are excluded because the inequality is strict; zero and $2\pi$ satisfy it and are included.
