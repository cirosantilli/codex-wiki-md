<h1 id="16/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For constant $m$, the [curl of a cross product](../../../../../curl-of-a-cross-product.md) identity reduces to

$$
\nabla\times(m\times\nabla\phi)=m\Delta\phi-(m\cdot\nabla)\nabla\phi.
$$

Commuting mixed second [partial derivatives](../../../../../partial-derivative.md) gives $(m\cdot\nabla)\nabla\phi=\nabla(m\cdot\nabla\phi)$. Hence the requested sum equals $m\Delta\phi$, which is zero because $\phi$ is [harmonic](../../../../../harmonic-function.md). The argument assumes the indicated second derivatives are continuous on the domain.
