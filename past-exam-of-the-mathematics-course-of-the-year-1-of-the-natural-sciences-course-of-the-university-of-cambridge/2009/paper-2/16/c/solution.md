<h1 id="16/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

From the preceding gradient, $\partial_x^2(r^{-1})=-r^{-3}+3x^2r^{-5}$. Summing the three Cartesian second [partial derivatives](../../../../../partial-derivative.md) gives

$$
\Delta(1/r)=-3r^{-3}+3(x^2+y^2+z^2)r^{-5}=0,\qquad r>0.
$$

Thus the reciprocal radius is [harmonic](../../../../../harmonic-function.md) away from the origin. Its negative gradient is

$$
\boxed{E=\frac{r}{r^3},\qquad\nabla\times E=0\quad(r>0).}
$$

The [curl](../../../../../curl.md) vanishes because $E$ is a gradient field and mixed second derivatives commute. The exclusion of the origin is essential: the potential and field are singular there.
