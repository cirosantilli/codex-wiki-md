<h1 id="16/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The vector $m$ is constant, so $\nabla(m\cdot r)=m$. Also $\nabla(r^{-3})=-3r/r^5$, by the same [chain rule](../../../../../../chain-rule.md). The [product rule](../../../../../../product-rule.md) therefore yields

$$
\boxed{\nabla\left(\frac{m\cdot r}{r^3}\right)=\frac m{r^3}-\frac{3(m\cdot r)r}{r^5},\qquad r>0.}
$$

The three components follow by differentiating $(\alpha x+\beta y+\gamma z)(x^2+y^2+z^2)^{-3/2}$.
