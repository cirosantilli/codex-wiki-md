<h1 id="13/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For the graph surface $Z=z(x,y)$, an upward normal is $(-z_x,-z_y,1)$. At $x=y=1$, let $q=e^{-1/2}$; then $z_x=q$ and $z_y=0$. Normalizing gives the [unit normal vector](../../../../../../unit-normal.md)

$$
\boxed{n=\frac{(-q,0,1)}{\sqrt{1+q^2}}=\frac{(-e^{-1/2},0,1)}{\sqrt{1+e^{-1}}}.}
$$

Its negative is the equally valid downward normal.
