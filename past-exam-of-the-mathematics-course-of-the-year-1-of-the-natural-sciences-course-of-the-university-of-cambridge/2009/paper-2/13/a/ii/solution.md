<h1 id="13/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

At $(X,y)=(0,0)$ the [Hessian matrix](../../../../../../hessian-matrix.md) is $\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$, with [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $1$ and $-1$, so $(x,y)=(1,0)$ is a [saddle point](../../../../../../saddle-point.md). At the other four points the mixed second [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) vanishes and the two diagonal entries both equal $-2Xy/e$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\begin{array}{c|c|c}
(x,y)&\text{type}&z\\\hline
(2,1),(0,-1)&\text{maxima}&e^{-1}\\
(0,1),(2,-1)&\text{minima}&-e^{-1}\\
(1,0)&\text{saddle}&0
\end{array}.}
$$

The four extrema are global: $|z|=(|X|e^{-X^2/2})(|y|e^{-y^2/2})\leq e^{-1}$ because each factor has maximum $e^{-1/2}$ at absolute argument one.
