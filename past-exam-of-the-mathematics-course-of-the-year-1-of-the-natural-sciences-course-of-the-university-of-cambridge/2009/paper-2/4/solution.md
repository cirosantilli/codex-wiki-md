<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

Zero is an [eigenvalue](../../../../eigenvalue.md) exactly when the [determinant](../../../../determinant.md) vanishes. Here $\det A=1-\alpha^2$, so

$$
\boxed{\alpha=1\quad\text{or}\quad\alpha=-1.}
$$

For either value the other [eigenvalue](../../../../eigenvalue.md) is two, as follows from the [trace](../../../../matrix-trace.md).
