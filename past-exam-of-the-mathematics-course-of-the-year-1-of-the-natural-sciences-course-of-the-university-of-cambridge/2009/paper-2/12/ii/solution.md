<h1 id="12/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The damped [angular frequency](../../../../../angular-frequency.md) is $\Omega=\sqrt{k-\lambda^2/4}$. The oscillation envelope is proportional to $e^{-\lambda t/2}$. A decrease by a quarter leaves three quarters of the original amplitude, so $e^{-\lambda t/2}=3/4$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\Omega=\sqrt{k-\lambda^2/4},\qquad t=\frac2\lambda\log\frac43.}
$$

This concerns the envelope; it does not ask when the instantaneous displacement first equals three quarters of a chosen value.
