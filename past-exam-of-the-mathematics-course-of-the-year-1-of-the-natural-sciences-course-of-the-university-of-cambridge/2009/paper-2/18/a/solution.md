<h1 id="18/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The integrand is continuous on the compact rectangle, so [Fubini's theorem](../../../../../fubini-s-theorem.md) allows integrating in $y$ first. For $x\ne0$, $\int_0^1xe^{xy}dy=e^x-1$; the same formula holds by continuity at $x=0$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\int_0^1\int_0^2xe^{xy}dx\,dy=\int_0^2(e^x-1)dx=e^2-3.}
$$
