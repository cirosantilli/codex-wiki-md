<h1 id="10/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [10](../10.md)

For $\lambda\geq0$, the exponential [power series](../../../../power-series.md) gives the normalization of the [Poisson distribution](../../../../poisson-distribution.md):

$$
\sum_{k=0}^\infty\frac{\lambda^ke^{-\lambda}}{k!}=e^{-\lambda}e^\lambda=1.
$$

For $\lambda=0$, the limiting law assigns probability one to $k=0$ and zero to every positive [integer](../../../../integer-split.md) $k$. Negative $\lambda$ would not define the stated [probability distribution](../../../../probability-distribution-split.md).
