<h1 id="14/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [cofactor matrix](../../../../../../cofactor-matrix.md) is $\begin{pmatrix}-3&-1&-1\\5&-1&3\\-1&1&1\end{pmatrix}$. Transpose it to form the [adjugate matrix](../../../../../../adjugate-matrix.md), and divide by the determinant $-4$:

$$
\boxed{A^{-1}=\frac14\begin{pmatrix}3&-5&1\\1&1&-1\\1&-3&-1\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

Direct matrix multiplication gives $AA^{-1}=I$, checking all entries.
