<h1 id="6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

Expanding gives $u=2x^2+2c^2t^2$, so $u_{tt}=4c^2$ and $u_{xx}=4$. Hence $u_{tt}=c^2u_{xx}$, proving that it solves the [one-dimensional wave equation](../../../../one-dimensional-wave-equation.md). Equivalently its two summands are oppositely travelling profiles, as in [D'Alembert's formula](../../../../d-alembert-s-formula.md).
