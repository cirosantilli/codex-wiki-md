↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Write $y=\tfrac12\sin(2x)$. It is an [odd function](../../../../../odd-function.md) with amplitude $1/2$ and [period](../../../../../period-of-a-function.md) $\pi$. Its zeros on the specified interval are $-\pi,-\pi/2,0,\pi/2,\pi$. The maxima are at $x=-3\pi/4,\pi/4$ and the minima at $x=-\pi/4,3\pi/4$, with values $1/2$ and $-1/2$. The middle panel of the preceding figure shows the two full oscillations.
