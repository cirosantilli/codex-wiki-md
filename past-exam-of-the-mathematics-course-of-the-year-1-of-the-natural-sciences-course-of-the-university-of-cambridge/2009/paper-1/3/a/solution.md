↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For the real [natural logarithm](../../../../../natural-logarithm.md) the domain is $x>0$. The [chain rule](../../../../../chain-rule.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{d}{dx}\ln(x^3)=\frac{3x^2}{x^3}=\frac3x.}
$$
