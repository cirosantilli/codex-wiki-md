<h1 id="20/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [Maclaurin series](../../../../../../maclaurin-series.md) give $\sin(3x)=3x+O(x^3)$ and $\sinh x=x+O(x^3)$. Dividing by $x$ in a punctured neighborhood of zero gives

$$
\boxed{\lim_{x\to0}\frac{\sin(3x)}{\sinh x}=3.}
$$
