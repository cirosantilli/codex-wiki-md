<h1 id="20/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For $a_n=n^n/n!$, successive terms satisfy

$$
\frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n}=\left(1+\frac1n\right)^n\longrightarrow e>1.
$$

They eventually grow by a factor strictly greater than one and cannot tend to zero. Hence the series **diverges to $+\infty$**, by the [ratio test](../../../../../../ratio-test.md) or the [term test for divergence](../../../../../../term-test-for-divergence.md).
