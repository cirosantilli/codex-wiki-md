<h1 id="16/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Set $t=\log(1/x)$, so $x=e^{-t}$ and $dx=-e^{-t}dt$. The [improper integral](../../../../../improper-integral.md) becomes

$$
\int_0^1[\log(1/x)]^4dx=\int_0^\infty t^4e^{-t}dt=\Gamma(5).
$$

The [Gamma function recurrence](../../../../../gamma-function-recurrence.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\int_0^1[\log(1/x)]^4dx=24.}
$$

The exponential-transformed integrand also proves convergence at the logarithmically singular endpoint.
