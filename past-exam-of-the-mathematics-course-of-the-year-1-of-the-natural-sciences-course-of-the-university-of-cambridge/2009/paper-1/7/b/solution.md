<h1 id="7/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

This [geometric series](../../../../../geometric-series.md) has first term one and ratio $-1/4$, whose [absolute value](../../../../../absolute-value.md) is less than one. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\left(-\frac14\right)^n=\frac1{1+1/4}=\frac45.}
$$
