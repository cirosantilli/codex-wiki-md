<h1 id="11/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

An axial section gives similar right triangles. At height $x$ above the base, the cone's remaining height is $h-x$, so the cylinder's radius is $r=(h-x)\tan\alpha$. Therefore its [volume](../../../../../../volume.md) is

$$
\boxed{V(x)=\pi x(h-x)^2\tan^2\alpha,\qquad0\leq x\leq h.}
$$

Assume $h>0$ and $0<\alpha<\pi/2$ for a nondegenerate cone. The geometry and the dimensionless optimization curves are illustrated below.
