<h1 id="11/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

At fixed $h,\alpha$, the [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) is $V'=\pi\tan^2\alpha\,(h-x)(h-3x)$. It is positive for $0<x<h/3$ and negative for $h/3<x<h$. The endpoint volumes are zero. Thus the unique positive-volume global [maximum](../../../../../../maximum-of-a-subset-of-a-total-order.md) is at

$$
\boxed{x=\frac h3.}
$$

Its value is $4\pi h^3\tan^2\alpha/27$, whereas the cone volume is $\pi h^3\tan^2\alpha/3$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\frac{V_{\rm cylinder}}{V_{\rm cone}}=\frac49.}
$$

This is the volume case of the [optimal cylinder inscribed in a cone](../../../../../../optimal-cylinder-inscribed-in-a-cone.md).
