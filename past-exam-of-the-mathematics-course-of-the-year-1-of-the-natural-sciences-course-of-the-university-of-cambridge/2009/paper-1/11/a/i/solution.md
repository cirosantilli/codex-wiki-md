<h1 id="11/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Differentiate the numerator and denominator using the [product rule](../../../../../../product-rule.md) and [quotient rule](../../../../../../quotient-rule.md):

$$
y'=\frac{(4x^3+3x^2)(3x-2)-12x^3(x+1)}{(3x-2)^5}.
$$

The numerator simplifies to $-11x^3-6x^2$, giving

$$
\boxed{y'=-\frac{x^2(11x+6)}{(3x-2)^5},\qquad x\ne\frac23.}
$$
