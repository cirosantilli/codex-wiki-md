<h1 id="2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Isolate the [square root](../../../../square-root.md): $\sqrt{x+16}=x-4$. A real solution must have $x\geq4$, because a real principal [square root](../../../../square-root.md) is nonnegative. Squaring gives $x+16=(x-4)^2$, hence $x(x-9)=0$. The candidate zero violates $x\geq4$; substitution verifies the other candidate. Thus

$$
\boxed{x=9.}
$$

The sign restriction prevents the extraneous solution introduced by squaring.
