<h1 id="13/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Expanding the [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) gives $\det(\lambda I-M)=(\lambda-1)[(\lambda-1)^2-25]$. Solving each [eigenvalue equation](../../../../../eigenvalue-equation.md) yields

$$
\boxed{\begin{array}{c|c}
\lambda&\text{one eigenvector}\\\hline
6&(4,5,3)^T\\
-4&(4,-5,3)^T\\
1&(3,0,-4)^T
\end{array}.}
$$

Their pairwise [inner products](../../../../../inner-product-split.md) are $16-25+9=0$, $12-12=0$ and $12-12=0$. Their squared [Euclidean norms](../../../../../euclidean-norm.md) are $50,50,25$, respectively, so division by $\sqrt{50},\sqrt{50},5$ gives an [orthonormal basis](../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md). This also follows from the [spectral theorem for real symmetric matrices](../../../../../spectral-theorem-for-real-symmetric-matrices.md): symmetry makes [eigenvectors](../../../../../eigenvector.md) for distinct [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) orthogonal.
