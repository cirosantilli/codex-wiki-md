<h1 id="10/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Eliminating the parameter gives $x^2+y^2/4=1$, an [ellipse](../../../../../ellipse.md) centered at the origin with horizontal semiaxis one and vertical semiaxis two. At $t=0$ the particle is at $(1,0)$ with [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) $(0,2)$, so it travels **counterclockwise**, reaching $(0,2),(-1,0),(0,-2)$ successively and returning after $2\pi$. The right panel of the graph figure shows this direction.
