<h1 id="10/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a [parametric curve](../../../../../parametric-curve.md) with $dx/dt\ne0$, the [chain rule](../../../../../chain-rule.md) gives

$$
\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{2\cos t}{-\sin t}.
$$

The specified point has $\cos t=\sin t=1/\sqrt2$, hence $t=\pi/4$ in the given traversal. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\frac{dy}{dx}=-2.}
$$
