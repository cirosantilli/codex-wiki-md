<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use [partial fraction decomposition](../../../../../partial-fraction-decomposition.md):

$$
\frac1{x(x-4)}=-\frac1{4x}+\frac1{4(x-4)}.
$$

Integrating the two logarithmic [derivatives](../../../../../derivative.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\int\frac{dx}{x^2-4x}=\frac14\log\left|\frac{x-4}{x}\right|+C.}
$$

This holds on each interval avoiding the two [poles](../../../../../pole.md) $0$ and $4$, with an independent integration constant on each interval.
