<h1 id="14/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The values of the [complex logarithm](../../../../../complex-logarithm.md) at $-1$ are $i(2m+1)\pi$, $m\in\mathbb Z$. For any such value and any [integer](../../../../../integer-split.md) $n$,

$$
-i(2n+1)\log(-1)=(2n+1)(2m+1)\pi.
$$

This is an integer multiple of $\pi$, so its [sine](../../../../../sine.md) is zero. In particular the result holds with the principal value $\log(-1)=i\pi$. The argument does not require treating the multivalued logarithm as a single globally defined function.
