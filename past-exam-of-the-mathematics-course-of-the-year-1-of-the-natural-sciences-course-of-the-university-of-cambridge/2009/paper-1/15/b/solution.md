<h1 id="15/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

By the [vector triple product identity](../../../../../vector-triple-product.md),

$$
(a\times b)\times(a\times c)=a[(a\times b)\cdot c]-c[(a\times b)\cdot a]=[a,b,c]a.
$$

The last inner product vanishes by perpendicularity. Taking the [inner product](../../../../../inner-product-split.md) with $d$ gives

$$
[a\times b,a\times c,d]=d\cdot[(a\times b)\times(a\times c)]=(a\cdot d)[a,b,c].
$$

This is the identity for [cross products with a common vector](../../../../../cross-products-with-a-common-vector.md), and holds without nondegeneracy assumptions.
