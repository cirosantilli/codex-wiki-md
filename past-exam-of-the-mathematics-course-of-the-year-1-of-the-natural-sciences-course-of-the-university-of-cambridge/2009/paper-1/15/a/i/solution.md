↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [scalar triple product](../../../../../../scalar-triple-product.md) is the [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) of the matrix with columns $a,b,c$. A cyclic permutation of three columns is a product of two swaps, so its determinant sign is positive. Therefore $[a,b,c]=[b,c,a]$. This uses the alternating multilinearity of the [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md), and remains true for linearly dependent vectors.
