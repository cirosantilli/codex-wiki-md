<h1 id="19/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A constant [sequence](../../../../../../sequence.md) must satisfy the [fixed point](../../../../../../fixed-point.md) equation $y_*=\sqrt{2+y_*}$. The [square root](../../../../../../square-root.md) forces $y_*\geq0$. Squaring gives $(y_*-2)(y_*+1)=0$, so the negative algebraic root is inadmissible. Hence

$$
\boxed{y_*=2.}
$$

Starting at $y_0=2$ produces the constant [sequence](../../../../../../sequence.md) $2,2,\ldots$.
