<h1 id="20/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [curve](../../../../../../curve.md) has $x\ne0$ and can be written $y=1/x-x$. It is a [hyperbola](../../../../../../hyperbola.md) with vertical [asymptote](../../../../../../asymptote.md) $x=0$ and oblique [asymptote](../../../../../../asymptote.md) $y=-x$, crossing the horizontal axis at $(\pm1,0)$. It is symmetric under $(x,y)\mapsto(-x,-y)$.

On each branch $dy/dx=-1/x^2-1<0$. For $x>0$, the branch descends from $+\infty$ as $x\downarrow0$, passes through $(1,0)$, and approaches $y=-x$ from above as $x\to+\infty$. The negative branch follows by the central symmetry.
