↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [binomial theorem](../../../../../binomial-theorem.md) assigns [binomial coefficient](../../../../../binomial-coefficient-split.md) $\binom93$ to $x^3$. Since $\binom93=9\cdot8\cdot7/(3\cdot2\cdot1)=84$, the requested term is **$84x^3$**.
