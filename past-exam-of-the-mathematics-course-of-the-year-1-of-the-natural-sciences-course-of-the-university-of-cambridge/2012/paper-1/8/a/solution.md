<h1 id="8/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

This [geometric series](../../../../../geometric-series.md) has first term $2^{-4}=1/16$ and common ratio $1/2$, whose [absolute value](../../../../../absolute-value.md) is less than one. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\sum_{n=4}^\infty2^{-n}=\frac{1/16}{1-1/2}=\frac18.}
$$
