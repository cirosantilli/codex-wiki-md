<h1 id="12/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Write a [square root](../../../../../square-root.md) as $u+iv$, with $u,v$ real. Equating the [real part](../../../../../real-part.md) and [imaginary part](../../../../../imaginary-part.md) of its square gives $u^2-v^2=-1$ and $2uv=1$. Taking [complex modulus](../../../../../complex-modulus.md) also gives $u^2+v^2=\sqrt2$. Therefore

$$
u^2=\frac{\sqrt2-1}{2},\qquad v^2=\frac{\sqrt2+1}{2}.
$$

Since $uv>0$, the signs agree. The two roots are

$$
\boxed{\pm\left(\sqrt{\frac{\sqrt2-1}{2}}+i\sqrt{\frac{\sqrt2+1}{2}}\right).}
$$

The $\pm$ changes both signs together, rather than independently.
