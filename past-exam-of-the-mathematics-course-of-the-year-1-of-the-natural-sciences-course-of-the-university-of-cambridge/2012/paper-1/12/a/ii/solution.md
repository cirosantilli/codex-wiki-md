<h1 id="12/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

In [polar form of a complex number](../../../../../../polar-form-of-a-complex-number.md), $1+i=\sqrt2\,e^{i\pi/4}$. By [De Moivre's theorem](../../../../../../de-moivre-s-theorem.md),

$$
(1+i)^{10}=2^5e^{5\pi i/2}=32i,
\qquad\boxed{\Re z=0,\qquad\Im z=32.}
$$
