<h1 id="12/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [complex sine function](../../../../../../complex-sine-function.md) identity $\sin(u+iv)=\sin u\cosh v+i\cos u\sinh v$ follows from the exponential definition of [sine](../../../../../../sine.md). With $u=\pi/2$, its imaginary term vanishes, while

$$
\cosh(\ln2)=\frac{e^{\ln2}+e^{-\ln2}}2=\frac{2+1/2}{2}=\frac54.
$$

Consequently **the [real part](../../../../../../real-part.md) is $5/4$ and the [imaginary part](../../../../../../imaginary-part.md) is $0$**.
