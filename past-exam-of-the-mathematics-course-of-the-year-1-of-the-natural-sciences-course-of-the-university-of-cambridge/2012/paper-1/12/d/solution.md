<h1 id="12/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Using the exponential definition of [hyperbolic cosine](../../../../../hyperbolic-cosine.md), multiply $e^z+e^{-z}=-2$ by the nonzero quantity $e^z$. This yields $(e^z+1)^2=0$, hence $e^z=-1$.

If $z=x+iy$, its [complex modulus](../../../../../complex-modulus.md) requires $e^x=1$, so $x=0$; its phase then requires $y=(2k+1)\pi$. Thus all complex solutions are

$$
\boxed{z=(2k+1)\pi i,\qquad k\in\mathbb Z.}
$$

There are no real solutions, since real $\cosh x\geq1$.
