<h1 id="10/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [10](../10.md)

A cross-section perpendicular to the [rotation](../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) axis is a [disk](../../../../disk-mathematics.md) of [radius](../../../../radius.md) $\sin x$. [Disc integration](../../../../disc-integration.md) therefore gives

$$
V=\pi\int_0^\pi\sin^2x\,dx
=\frac\pi2\int_0^\pi(1-\cos2x)\,dx
=\boxed{\frac{\pi^2}{2}}.
$$

The result is in cubic coordinate units; the full [rotation](../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) is already accounted for by the disk [area](../../../../surface-area.md).
