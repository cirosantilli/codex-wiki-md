<h1 id="5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

Use [integration by parts](../../../../integration-by-parts.md) twice. First,

$$
I=[-x^2\cos x]_0^\pi+2\int_0^\pi x\cos x\,dx
=\pi^2+2\big([x\sin x]_0^\pi-\int_0^\pi\sin x\,dx\big).
$$

The bracket containing $x\sin x$ vanishes, while the last [definite integral](../../../../definite-integral.md) is $2$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{I=\pi^2-4.}
$$
