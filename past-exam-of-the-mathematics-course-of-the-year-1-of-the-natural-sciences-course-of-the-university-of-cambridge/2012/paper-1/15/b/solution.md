<h1 id="15/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Each radial segment from $0$ to $1+\cos\theta$ lies inside the [cardioid](../../../../../cardioid.md). Integrating the [polar area element](../../../../../polar-area-element.md) gives

$$
\mathcal A=\int_0^{2\pi}\int_0^{1+\cos\theta}r\,dr\,d\theta
=\frac12\int_0^{2\pi}(1+2\cos\theta+\cos^2\theta)\,d\theta.
$$

The three integrals are respectively $2\pi$, $0$ and $\pi$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\mathcal A=\frac{3\pi}{2}}
$$

in square coordinate units, agreeing with the scale-one case of the [area and perimeter of a cardioid](../../../../../area-and-perimeter-of-a-cardioid.md).
