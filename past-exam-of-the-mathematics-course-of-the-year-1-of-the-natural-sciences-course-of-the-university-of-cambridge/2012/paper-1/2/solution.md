<h1 id="2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

The [derivative](../../../../derivative.md) of the [parabola](../../../../parabola.md) is $dy/dx=2+2x$, giving [tangent line](../../../../tangent-line.md) slope $4$ at $(1,3)$. A [normal line to a plane curve](../../../../normal-line-to-a-plane-curve.md) has the negative reciprocal slope, since its direction is [orthogonal](../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) to a [tangent vector](../../../../tangent-vector-split.md). Hence

$$
\boxed{y-3=-\frac14(x-1),\qquad x+4y=13.}
$$

The point $(1,3)$ satisfies the equation, and the product of the two slopes is $-1$.
