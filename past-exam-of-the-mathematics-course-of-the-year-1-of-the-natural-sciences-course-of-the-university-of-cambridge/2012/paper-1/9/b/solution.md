<h1 id="9/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [absolute value](../../../../../absolute-value.md) in the original PDF reflects every negative [sine](../../../../../sine.md) arch upward. The graph is nonnegative, with zeros at $x=k\pi$ for $k=0,1,2,3,4$, and it lies below the decaying envelope $e^{-x}$. On the $k$th arch, [differentiation](../../../../../differentiation.md) gives a factor proportional to $\cos x-\sin x$. Thus its unique [local maximum](../../../../../local-maximum.md) is

$$
x_k=\frac\pi4+k\pi,\qquad
f(x_k)=\frac{e^{-(\pi/4+k\pi)}}{\sqrt2},\qquad k=0,1,2,3.
$$

Successive maximum heights have ratio $e^{-\pi}$. At each interior zero the one-sided slopes have opposite signs, producing a corner. The smaller panels below magnify the later arches so that all four remain visible.
