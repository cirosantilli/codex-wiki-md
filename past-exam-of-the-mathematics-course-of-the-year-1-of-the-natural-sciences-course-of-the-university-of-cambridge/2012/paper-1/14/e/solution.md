<h1 id="14/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

With $b\cdot c=0$, substitute $a=\alpha b+\beta c$ into the [plane](../../../../../plane.md) constraints. They become $\alpha|b|^2=\lambda$ and $\beta|c|^2=\mu$, giving

$$
\boxed{\alpha=\frac{\lambda}{|b|^2},\qquad\beta=\frac{\mu}{|c|^2}.}
$$

The [Pythagorean theorem](../../../../../pythagorean-theorem.md) for the two [orthogonal](../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) terms in $a$ then gives the closest distance

$$
\boxed{d_{\min}=|a|=\sqrt{\frac{\lambda^2}{|b|^2}+\frac{\mu^2}{|c|^2}}.}
$$

Zero values of either [scalar](../../../../../scalar.md) are allowed, including zero distance if both planes contain the origin.
