<h1 id="14/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [vector triple product identity](../../../../../vector-triple-product.md) is

$$
\boxed{a\times(b\times c)=b(a\cdot c)-c(a\cdot b).}
$$

Thus the expression lies in the [linear span](../../../../../linear-span.md) of $b$ and $c$, with respective [scalar](../../../../../scalar.md) coefficients $a\cdot c$ and $-a\cdot b$. The order and the minus sign matter because the [cross product](../../../../../cross-product.md) is not an [associative operation](../../../../../associative-property.md).
