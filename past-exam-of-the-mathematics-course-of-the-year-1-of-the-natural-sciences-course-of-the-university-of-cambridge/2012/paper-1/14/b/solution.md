<h1 id="14/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The nonzero [vectors](../../../../../vector.md) $b,c$ are [normal vectors](../../../../../normal-vector.md) to the two [planes](../../../../../plane.md). If $b\times c=0$, then $c=qb$ for some nonzero real $q$. Their equations are therefore $r\cdot b=\lambda$ and $q(r\cdot b)=\mu$.

When $\mu=q\lambda$, the [planes](../../../../../plane.md) coincide and their intersection is an entire [plane](../../../../../plane.md). Otherwise they are distinct parallel planes and have no intersection. Neither case has exactly a [line](../../../../../straight-line.md) as its intersection. Hence **$b\times c\ne0$ is necessary** for intersection in a [line](../../../../../straight-line.md). It means that the two [normal vectors](../../../../../normal-vector.md) are not parallel; in that case it is also sufficient.
