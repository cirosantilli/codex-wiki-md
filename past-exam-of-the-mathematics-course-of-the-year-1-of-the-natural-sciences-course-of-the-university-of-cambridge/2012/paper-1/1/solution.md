<h1 id="1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Use [completing the square](../../../../completing-the-square.md) after taking out the leading [coefficient](../../../../coefficient.md):

$$
3(x^2-4x)+11=3[(x-2)^2-4]+11
=\boxed{3(x-2)^2-1.}
$$

Thus $a=3$, $b=-2$ and $c=-1$. Since a real square is nonnegative, the [global minimum](../../../../global-minimum.md) is **$-1$, attained at $x=2$**.
