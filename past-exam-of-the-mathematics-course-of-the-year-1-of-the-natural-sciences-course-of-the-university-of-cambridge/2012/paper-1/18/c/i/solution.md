<h1 id="18/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Normalize the [probability density function](../../../../../../probability-density-function.md) on its nonnegative support:

$$
1=A\int_0^\infty xe^{-\lambda x}\,dx=\frac A{\lambda^2}.
$$

The integral follows from one [integration by parts](../../../../../../integration-by-parts.md), with the boundary term zero since $\lambda>0$. Hence **$A=\lambda^2$**. This is a [gamma distribution](../../../../../../gamma-distribution.md) of shape $2$ and rate $\lambda$.
