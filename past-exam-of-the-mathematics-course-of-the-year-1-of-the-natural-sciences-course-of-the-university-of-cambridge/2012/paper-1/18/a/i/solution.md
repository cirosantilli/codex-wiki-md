<h1 id="18/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Under independent daily tosses, attending every lecture is the intersection of $24$ [independent events](../../../../../../independent-events.md) of [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) $3/4$. Hence

$$
\boxed{P(N=24)=\left(\frac34\right)^{24}\approx0.00100339.}
$$
