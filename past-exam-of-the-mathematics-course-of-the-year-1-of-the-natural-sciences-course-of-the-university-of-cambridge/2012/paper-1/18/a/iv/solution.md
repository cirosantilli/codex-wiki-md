<h1 id="18/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

[Independence](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) makes the [variances](../../../../../../variance-split.md) of the attendance indicators additive. The [binomial distribution](../../../../../../binomial-distribution.md) therefore gives $\operatorname{Var}(N)=np(1-p)=24(3/4)(1/4)=9/2$. Its positive [square root](../../../../../../square-root.md) is the [standard deviation](../../../../../../standard-deviation.md):

$$
\boxed{\sigma_N=\frac3{\sqrt2}\approx2.12132\ \text{lectures}.}
$$
