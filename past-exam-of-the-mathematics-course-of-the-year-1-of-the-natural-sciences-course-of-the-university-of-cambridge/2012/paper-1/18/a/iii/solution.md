<h1 id="18/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Write the count as a sum of $24$ [Bernoulli random variables](../../../../../../bernoulli-distribution.md), one for each attendance indicator. Each [indicator random variable](../../../../../../indicator-random-variable.md) has [expected value](../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) $3/4$, so [linearity of expectation](../../../../../../linearity-of-expectation.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[N]=24\cdot\frac34=18\ \text{lectures}.}
$$

Equivalently, $N$ has a [binomial distribution](../../../../../../binomial-distribution.md) with parameters $24$ and $3/4$.
