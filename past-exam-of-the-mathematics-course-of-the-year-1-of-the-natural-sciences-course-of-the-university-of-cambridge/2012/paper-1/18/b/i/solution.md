<h1 id="18/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Interpreting $f$ as the [probability density function](../../../../../../probability-density-function.md) of the [random variable](../../../../../../random-variable-split.md), for $\alpha\leq\beta$ one has

$$
\boxed{P(\alpha\leq X\leq\beta)=\int_\alpha^\beta f(x)\,dx.}
$$

A single point has zero [probability](../../../../../../probability.md), so including or excluding either endpoint does not change this result.
