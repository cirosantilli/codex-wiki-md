<h1 id="16/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

At the origin the [Hessian matrix](../../../../../hessian-matrix.md) is

$$
H_g(0,0)=\begin{pmatrix}2&0\\0&-2\end{pmatrix},\qquad\det H_g(0,0)=-4<0.
$$

Thus the [second-derivative test](../../../../../second-derivative-test.md) gives a **[saddle point](../../../../../saddle-point.md)**. Its opposite signs can also be checked without a test formula: $g(x,0)=x^2e^{-x^2/2}>0$ for $x\ne0$, whereas $g(0,y)=-y^2e^{-y^2/2}<0$ for $y\ne0$. Both types of values occur arbitrarily close to the zero value at the origin, excluding either a [local maximum](../../../../../local-maximum.md) or a [local minimum](../../../../../local-minimum.md).
