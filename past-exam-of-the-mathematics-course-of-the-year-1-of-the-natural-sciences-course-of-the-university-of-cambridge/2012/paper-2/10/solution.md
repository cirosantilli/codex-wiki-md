<h1 id="10/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [10](../10.md)

The [derivative](../../../../derivative.md) is $f'(x)=2x\cos(x^2-2)$. Thus a [stationary point](../../../../stationary-point.md) occurs either at $x=0$ or where $x^2-2=\pi/2+k\pi$. Since $-2\leq x^2-2<2$ on the specified open interval, the only allowed cosine zeros are $-\pi/2$ and $\pi/2$. The five stationary abscissae are

$$
\boxed{x=0,\qquad x=\pm\sqrt{2-\frac\pi2},\qquad
x=\pm\sqrt{2+\frac\pi2}}.
$$

Their ordinates are $-\sin2$, $-1$, and $1$, respectively. For completeness, $f''=2\cos(x^2-2)-4x^2\sin(x^2-2)$ shows that the inner pair are [local minima](../../../../local-minimum.md) and the outer pair are [local maxima](../../../../local-maximum.md); $x=0$ is also a [local maximum](../../../../local-maximum.md) because $2\cos2<0$.
