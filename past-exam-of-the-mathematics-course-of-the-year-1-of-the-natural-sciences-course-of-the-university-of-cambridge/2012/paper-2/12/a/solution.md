<h1 id="12/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Apply the [fundamental theorem of calculus](../../../../../fundamental-theorem-of-calculus.md) separately to the upper and lower limits. No evaluation of the [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md) is needed:

$$
\boxed{\frac{\partial I_{n,k}}{\partial x}=\cos^kx(1-\sin^3x)^n,\qquad
\frac{\partial I_{n,k}}{\partial y}=-\cos^ky(1-\sin^3y)^n}.
$$

The first result has no $y$ dependence and the second no $x$ dependence, so the mixed [partial derivative](../../../../../partial-derivative.md) is

$$
\boxed{\frac{\partial^2I_{n,k}}{\partial x\partial y}=0}.
$$
