<h1 id="12/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Apply the [power-complement integral recurrence](../../../../../power-complement-integral-recurrence.md) three times, starting from $I_{0,1}(\pi/2,0)=1$:

$$
\boxed{I_{3,1}(\pi/2,0)=\frac9{10}\frac67\frac34=\frac{81}{140}}.
$$

Each factor corresponds to reducing $n$ by one; the final unreduced [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md) is just $\int_0^{\pi/2}\cos u\,du=1$.
