<h1 id="3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

The [determinant](../../../../determinant.md) is $2\cdot4-3\cdot1=5\ne0$, so the [matrix inverse](../../../../matrix-inverse-split.md) exists. Using the two-by-two [adjugate matrix](../../../../adjugate-matrix.md) formula gives

$$
\boxed{A^{-1}=\frac15\begin{pmatrix}4&-1\\-3&2\end{pmatrix}}.
$$

Multiplication on either side gives the [identity matrix](../../../../identity-matrix.md), which also checks the signs of the off-diagonal entries.
