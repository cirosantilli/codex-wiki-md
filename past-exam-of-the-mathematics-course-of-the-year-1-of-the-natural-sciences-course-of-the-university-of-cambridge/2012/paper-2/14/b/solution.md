↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [volume](../../../../../volume.md) of a [parallelepiped](../../../../../parallelepiped.md) is the absolute [scalar triple product](../../../../../scalar-triple-product.md) of its three edge [vectors](../../../../../vector.md). Since $\overrightarrow{OP}\times\overrightarrow{OQ}=(0,0,3)$,

$$
\boxed{\mathcal V=\left|\bigl(\overrightarrow{OP}\times\overrightarrow{OQ}\bigr)
\cdot\overrightarrow{OR}\right|=3}.
$$

The factor $1/6$ appropriate to a [tetrahedron](../../../../../tetrahedron.md) must not be applied to this parallelepiped.
