<h1 id="14/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use outward [normal vectors](../../../../../../normal-vector.md) to the two faces. The [cross products](../../../../../../cross-product.md)

$$
\mathbf n_1=\overrightarrow{OP}\times\overrightarrow{OR}=(1,-2,2),\qquad
\mathbf n_2=\overrightarrow{OR}\times\overrightarrow{OQ}=(-2,1,-1)
$$

point away from the opposite vertices, since $\mathbf n_1\cdot\overrightarrow{OQ}=-3$ and $\mathbf n_2\cdot\overrightarrow{OP}=-3$. Their [Euclidean norms](../../../../../../euclidean-norm.md) are $3$ and $\sqrt6$, and their [dot product](../../../../../../dot-product.md) is $-6$. The [interior dihedral angle of a convex polyhedron](../../../../../../interior-dihedral-angle-of-a-convex-polyhedron.md) is supplementary to the outward-normal angle, so

$$
\boxed{\cos\theta=-\frac{\mathbf n_1\cdot\mathbf n_2}{|\mathbf n_1||\mathbf n_2|}
=\frac2{\sqrt6}=\sqrt{\frac23}}.
$$

This also equals the cosine of the acute angle between the unoriented face planes. The outward-normal angle itself has cosine $-\sqrt{2/3}$; keeping that distinction avoids a sign ambiguity.
