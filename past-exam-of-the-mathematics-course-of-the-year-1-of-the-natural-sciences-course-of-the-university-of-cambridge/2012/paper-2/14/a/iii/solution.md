<h1 id="14/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The normal from (ii) gives the [plane](../../../../../../plane.md) equation $x+y+2z=3$. The [distance from a point to a plane](../../../../../../distance-from-a-point-to-a-plane.md) is the absolute value of the normalized plane equation at that point. At the origin this gives

$$
\boxed{d=\frac3{\sqrt{1^2+1^2+2^2}}=\frac3{\sqrt6}=\frac{\sqrt6}{2}}.
$$

For a geometric check, the perpendicular foot is $H=(1/2,1/2,1)$, parallel to the normal and satisfying the plane equation. Its [Euclidean norm](../../../../../../euclidean-norm.md) is the same distance.
