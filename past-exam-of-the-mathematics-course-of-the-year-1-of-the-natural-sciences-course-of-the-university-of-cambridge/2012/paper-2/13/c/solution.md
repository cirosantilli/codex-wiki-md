<h1 id="13/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [characteristic equation](../../../../../characteristic-equation-of-a-constant-coefficient-differential-equation.md) factors as $(r+1)(r+2)=0$, giving $y_h=C_1e^{-x}+C_2e^{-2x}$. The [superposition principle](../../../../../superposition-principle.md) lets us find [particular solutions](../../../../../particular-solution.md) for the two forcing terms separately. A trial $Ax+B$ gives $2A=1$ and $3A+2B=0$, so it is $x/2-3/4$. A trial $Ce^{3x}$ gives $(9+9+2)C=1$, so $C=1/20$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{y(x)=C_1e^{-x}+C_2e^{-2x}+\frac x2-\frac34+\frac1{20}e^{3x}}.
$$
