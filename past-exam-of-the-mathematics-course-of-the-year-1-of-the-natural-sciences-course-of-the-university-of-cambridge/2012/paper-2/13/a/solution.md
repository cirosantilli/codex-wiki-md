<h1 id="13/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [characteristic equation](../../../../../characteristic-equation-of-a-constant-coefficient-differential-equation.md) is $r^2+r-2=(r-1)(r+2)=0$, giving $y=Ae^x+Be^{-2x}$. The [initial conditions](../../../../../initial-condition.md) give $A+B=1$ and $A-2B=-1$. Solving yields $A=1/3$, $B=2/3$, hence

$$
\boxed{y(x)=\frac13e^x+\frac23e^{-2x}}.
$$

The two distinct characteristic roots supply all solutions of this second-order [linear ordinary differential equation](../../../../../linear-ordinary-differential-equation-split.md).
