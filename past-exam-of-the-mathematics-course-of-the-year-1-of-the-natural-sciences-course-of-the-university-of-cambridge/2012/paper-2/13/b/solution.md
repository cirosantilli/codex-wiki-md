<h1 id="13/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [characteristic equation](../../../../../characteristic-equation-of-a-constant-coefficient-differential-equation.md) has the repeated root $r=-3$. Its [homogeneous solution](../../../../../homogeneous-solution.md) is therefore $(C_1+C_2x)e^{-3x}$. For a [particular solution](../../../../../particular-solution.md), try $y_p=Ax+B$. Substitution gives $9Ax+6A+9B=3x+1$, so $A=1/3$ and $B=-1/9$. Thus the [general solution](../../../../../general-solution.md) is

$$
\boxed{y(x)=(C_1+C_2x)e^{-3x}+\frac x3-\frac19}.
$$

The extra factor $x$ in the second homogeneous term is required by the repeated root.
