↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [trace](../../../../../matrix-trace.md) is the sum of the [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md), counting multiplicity, while the [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) is their product:

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{tr}A=1-2+1=0=4-2-2,\qquad
\det A=16=4(-2)(-2)}.
$$

A direct expansion along the middle row checks the latter: $(-2)(1-9)=16$.
