↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Using the ordered [orthonormal basis](../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) chosen in (c), the column [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) is

$$
\boxed{B=\begin{pmatrix}
1/\sqrt2&1/\sqrt2&0\\
0&0&1\\
1/\sqrt2&-1/\sqrt2&0
\end{pmatrix}}.
$$

Other signs or another [orthonormal](../../../../../orthonormal-set.md) choice within the repeated [eigenspace](../../../../../eigenspace.md) are equally valid, provided the same choice is retained in the remaining parts.
