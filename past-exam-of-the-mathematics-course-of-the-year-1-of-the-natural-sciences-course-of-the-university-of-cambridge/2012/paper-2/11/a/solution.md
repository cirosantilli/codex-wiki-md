<h1 id="11/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Solving $(A-4I)\mathbf v=0$ gives $v_2=0$ and $v_1=v_3$. A convenient normalized [eigenvector](../../../../../eigenvector.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathbf e_1=\frac1{\sqrt2}(1,0,1),\qquad A\mathbf e_1=4\mathbf e_1}.
$$

The displayed nonzero [vector](../../../../../vector.md) proves that four is an [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md). Every nonzero multiple represents the same eigendirection.
