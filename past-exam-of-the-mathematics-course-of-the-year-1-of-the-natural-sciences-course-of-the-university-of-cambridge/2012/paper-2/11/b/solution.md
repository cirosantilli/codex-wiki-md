<h1 id="11/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A [vector](../../../../../vector.md) perpendicular to $\mathbf e_1$ has the form $\mathbf v=(s,t,-s)$. Direct [matrix multiplication](../../../../../matrix-multiplication.md) gives

$$
A\mathbf v=(-2s,-2t,2s)=-2\mathbf v.
$$

Thus every **nonzero** such [vector](../../../../../vector.md) is an [eigenvector](../../../../../eigenvector.md) with $\boxed{\lambda=-2}$. The zero [vector](../../../../../vector.md) must be excluded from the literal claim about “any” perpendicular [vector](../../../../../vector.md), since an [eigenvector](../../../../../eigenvector.md) is nonzero. The [eigenspace](../../../../../eigenspace.md) is two-dimensional. Equivalently, $A=-2I+6\mathbf e_1\mathbf e_1^{\mathsf T}$ exhibits the [spectrum of a scalar matrix plus a rank-one projector](../../../../../spectrum-of-a-scalar-matrix-plus-a-rank-one-projector.md).
