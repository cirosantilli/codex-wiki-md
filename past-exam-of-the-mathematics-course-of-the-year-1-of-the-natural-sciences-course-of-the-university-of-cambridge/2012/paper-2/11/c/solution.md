<h1 id="11/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Choose two perpendicular unit directions in the $-2$ [eigenspace](../../../../../eigenspace.md):

$$
\boxed{\mathbf e_1=\frac{(1,0,1)}{\sqrt2},\qquad
\mathbf e_2=\frac{(1,0,-1)}{\sqrt2},\qquad
\mathbf e_3=(0,1,0)}.
$$

Their pairwise [dot products](../../../../../dot-product.md) vanish and their [Euclidean norms](../../../../../euclidean-norm.md) are one. They form an [orthonormal basis](../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) of [eigenvectors](../../../../../eigenvector.md), with [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $4,-2,-2$, as guaranteed for a real [symmetric matrix](../../../../../symmetric-matrix.md) by the [spectral theorem for real symmetric matrices](../../../../../spectral-theorem-for-real-symmetric-matrices.md).
