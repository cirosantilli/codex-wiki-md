<h1 id="18/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

There are eighteen equally likely first choices. Counting the relevant subsets gives

$$
\boxed{P(A)=\frac{13}{18},\qquad P(B)=\frac{10}{18}=\frac59,\qquad
P(A\cap B)=\frac8{18}=\frac49}.
$$

For [conditional probabilities](../../../../../conditional-probability.md), restrict the denominator to the conditioning subset:

$$
\boxed{P(A\mid B)=\frac8{10}=\frac45,\qquad P(B\mid A)=\frac8{13}}.
$$

The five counts come from the same finite [sample space](../../../../../sample-space.md), so no independence assumption is involved.
