<h1 id="18/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Insert the values from (a) into [Bayes' theorem](../../../../../bayes-theorem.md):

$$
\frac{P(A\mid B)P(B)}{P(A)}
=\frac{(4/5)(5/9)}{13/18}=\frac8{13}=P(B\mid A).
$$

Thus the identity is **satisfied**. Equivalently, both $P(A\mid B)P(B)$ and $P(B\mid A)P(A)$ equal the joint [probability](../../../../../probability.md) $4/9$.
