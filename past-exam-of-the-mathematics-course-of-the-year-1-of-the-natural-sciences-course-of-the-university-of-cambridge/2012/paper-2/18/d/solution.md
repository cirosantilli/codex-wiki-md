<h1 id="18/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Let $C$ denote a green pear on the first draw and $D$ a red apple on the second. The two categories are disjoint, so after $C$ there are still three red apples among seventeen remaining fruits. Thus $P(C\cap D)=(8/18)(3/17)$. Using $P(D)=1/6$ from (c), [conditional probability](../../../../../conditional-probability.md) gives

$$
\boxed{P(C\mid D)=\frac{(8/18)(3/17)}{1/6}=\frac8{17}}.
$$

Equivalently, conditioning on the second fruit being one particular category removes a red apple and leaves the other seventeen fruits symmetrically eligible for the first position.
