↑ **Parent:** [8](../8.md)

In [Cartesian coordinates](../../../../cartesian-coordinate-system.md), calculate the [divergence](../../../../divergence.md) by differentiating each component with respect to its matching coordinate:

$$
\boxed{\nabla\cdot\mathbf F=2x+x+6z=3x+6z}.
$$

For the [curl](../../../../curl.md), take the alternating cross-derivatives:

$$
\boxed{\nabla\times\mathbf F
=(\partial_yF_z-\partial_zF_y,\partial_zF_x-\partial_xF_z,
\partial_xF_y-\partial_yF_x)=(0,2x,3y)}.
$$
