<h1 id="9/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [9](../9.md)

Parametrize the directed segment by $\mathbf r(t)=(t,t,0)$, $0\leq t\leq1$. Its tangent is $(1,1,0)$, while the restricted [vector field](../../../../vector-field.md) is $(t^3,t^3,0)$. The [line integral](../../../../line-integral.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{\int_0^1\mathbf G(\mathbf r(t))\cdot\mathbf r'(t)\,dt
=\int_0^12t^3\,dt=\frac12}.
$$

Alternatively, $\mathbf G=\nabla(x^2y^2/2)$, so the [fundamental theorem for line integrals](../../../../fundamental-theorem-for-line-integrals.md) gives the same endpoint difference.
