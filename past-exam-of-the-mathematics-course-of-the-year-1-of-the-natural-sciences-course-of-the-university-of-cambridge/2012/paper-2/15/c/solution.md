<h1 id="15/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The condition at infinity gives $C_{\rm out}=0$. Matching the [electric potential](../../../../../electric-potential.md) at $r=a$ gives

$$
\frac{k}{2a}+C_{\rm in}=-\frac ka,
\qquad C_{\rm in}=-\frac{3k}{2a}.
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{V(r)=\begin{cases}
\dfrac{k}{2a^3}(r^2-3a^2),&0\leq r<a,\\
-\dfrac kr,&r\geq a.
\end{cases}}
$$

This [function](../../../../../function-split.md) is finite and differentiable at the origin. At the joining radius, both $V$ and $V'$ match, giving $V(a)=-k/a$ and $V'(a)=k/a^2$. Thus the [electric field](../../../../../electric-field-split.md) is continuous there as well as being conservative on each side. This is the [potential of a uniform spherical source](../../../../../potential-of-a-uniform-spherical-source.md), with source coefficient $3k/a^3$.
