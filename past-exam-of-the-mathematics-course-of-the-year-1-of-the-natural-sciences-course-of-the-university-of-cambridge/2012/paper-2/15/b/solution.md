<h1 id="15/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a radial [electric potential](../../../../../electric-potential.md), $\mathbf E=-V'(r)\mathbf r/r$. Comparing radial coefficients gives

$$
V'_{\rm in}(r)=\frac{kr}{a^3},\qquad V'_{\rm out}(r)=\frac{k}{r^2}.
$$

Integrating these one-variable [derivatives](../../../../../derivative.md) gives the general local expressions

$$
\boxed{V_{\rm in}(r)=\frac{kr^2}{2a^3}+C_{\rm in},\qquad
V_{\rm out}(r)=-\frac kr+C_{\rm out}}.
$$

The two constants are initially independent because these integrations were performed in separate radial regions.
