<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

Multiply by the [complex conjugate](../../../../complex-conjugate.md) of the denominator:

$$
\frac{2+3i}{4-5i}=\frac{(2+3i)(4+5i)}{4^2+5^2}
=\frac{-7+22i}{41}.
$$

Thus the [real part](../../../../real-part.md) and [imaginary part](../../../../imaginary-part.md) are respectively

$$
\boxed{-\frac7{41},\qquad\frac{22}{41}}.
$$
