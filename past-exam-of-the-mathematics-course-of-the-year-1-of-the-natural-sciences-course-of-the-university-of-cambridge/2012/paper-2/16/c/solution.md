<h1 id="16/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

With $a=2$, integrate the $dx$ coefficient with respect to $x$:

$$
\Phi(x,y)=\frac{x^3}{3}+\frac{x^2y}{2}-xy^2+g(y).
$$

Its other [partial derivative](../../../../../partial-derivative.md) is $\Phi_y=x^2/2-2xy+g'(y)$. Matching the $dy$ coefficient gives $g'=0$. Therefore the [differential equation](../../../../../differential-equation-split.md) is $d\Phi=0$, and its implicit [general solution](../../../../../general-solution.md) is

$$
\boxed{\frac{x^3}{3}+\frac{x^2y}{2}-xy^2=C}.
$$

These are [level curves](../../../../../level-curve.md) of the [potential of a conservative vector field](../../../../../potential-of-a-conservative-vector-field.md). Where $N\ne0$, they give the usual graph solution $dy/dx=-M/N$. At $N=0$, the implicit description retains possible vertical tangents instead of dividing by zero.
