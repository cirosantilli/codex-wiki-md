<h1 id="16/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Write the [differential one-form](../../../../../one-form.md) as $M\,dx+N\,dy$. An [exact differential form](../../../../../exact-differential-form.md) must satisfy equality of mixed [partial derivatives](../../../../../partial-derivative.md), $M_y=N_x$. Here

$$
M_y=x-2y,\qquad N_x=x-ay.
$$

Equality at every $(x,y)$ holds precisely when $\boxed{a=2}$. Since these [polynomials](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) are smooth on the simply connected [plane](../../../../../plane.md), the closed-form condition is also sufficient for exactness; the explicit [potential of a conservative vector field](../../../../../potential-of-a-conservative-vector-field.md) constructed in (c) verifies that sufficiency directly.
