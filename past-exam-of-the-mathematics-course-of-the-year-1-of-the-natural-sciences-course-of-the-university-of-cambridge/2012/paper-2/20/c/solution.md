<h1 id="20/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Insert the linear initial profile into the transformed [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md) from (b):

$$
u(x,t)=\frac a{\sqrt\pi}\int_{\mathbb R}
(x+2\sqrt t\,\eta)e^{-\eta^2}\,d\eta.
$$

The constant part integrates to $ax$. The other part is an absolutely integrable [odd function](../../../../../odd-function.md), so its [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md) vanishes. Hence

$$
\boxed{u(x,t)=ax\qquad(t\geq0)}.
$$

As a direct check, $u_t=u_{xx}=0$ and $u(x,0)=ax$. Thus a linear [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) profile is stationary on the whole line. More generally, every [affine function](../../../../../affine-function.md) is preserved by this [heat-kernel solution](../../../../../heat-kernel-solution.md).
