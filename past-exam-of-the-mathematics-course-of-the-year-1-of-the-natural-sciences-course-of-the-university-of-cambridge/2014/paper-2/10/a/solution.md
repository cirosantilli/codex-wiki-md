<h1 id="10/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The support is $x\geq1$, and its normalized [Pareto distribution](../../../../../pareto-distribution.md) density integrates to

$$
\boxed{P(X\geq1)=\int_1^\infty\alpha x^{-\alpha-1}\,dx
=\left[-x^{-\alpha}\right]_1^\infty=1}.
$$
