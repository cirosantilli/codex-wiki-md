<h1 id="10/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Integrate the [probability density function](../../../../../probability-density-function.md) over the specified interval:

$$
\boxed{P(2\leq X\leq3)=\int_2^3\alpha x^{-\alpha-1}\,dx
=2^{-\alpha}-3^{-\alpha}}.
$$

The endpoint inclusions make no difference for this continuous distribution.
