<h1 id="12/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

At $a=1$, the reduced system gives $z=1$ and $x+y=2$. Thus all solutions are

$$
\boxed{(x,y,z)=(2-s,s,1),\qquad s\in\mathbb R}.
$$

Geometrically the [solution set](../../../../../../solution-set.md) is an affine straight line, and substitution verifies all three original equations.
