<h1 id="12/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A real square [orthogonal matrix](../../../../../../orthogonal-matrix.md) $Q$ satisfies

$$
\boxed{Q^TQ=I},
$$

equivalently $Q^{-1}=Q^T$. Its columns, and also its rows, form an [orthonormal basis](../../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md). Consequently it preserves [dot products](../../../../../../dot-product.md) and [Euclidean norms](../../../../../../euclidean-norm.md).
