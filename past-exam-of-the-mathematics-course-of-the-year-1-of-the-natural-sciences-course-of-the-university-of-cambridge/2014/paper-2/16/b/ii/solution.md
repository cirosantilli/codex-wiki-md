<h1 id="16/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $\mathbf r(t)=(t,t,2t^3)$, the conservative field is $(6t^7,4t^7,t^5)$ and the tangent is $(1,1,6t^2)$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\int_A^B\mathbf F_1\cdot d\mathbf r=\int_0^1 16t^7\,dt=2}.
$$

The two different paths give the same value because their [line integrals](../../../../../../line-integral.md) depend only on the potential difference $\phi_1(B)-\phi_1(A)=2$. There is no [scalar potential](../../../../../../scalar-potential.md) for $\mathbf F_2$ to which the conditional request could be applied.
