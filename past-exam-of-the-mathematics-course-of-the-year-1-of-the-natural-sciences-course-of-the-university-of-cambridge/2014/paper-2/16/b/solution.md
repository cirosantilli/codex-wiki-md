<h1 id="16/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The first field has vanishing [curl](../../../../../curl.md) and is the [gradient](../../../../../gradient.md) of a [monomial](../../../../../monomial.md):

$$
\boxed{\nabla\times\mathbf F_1=\mathbf0,\qquad
\phi_1=x^3y^2z+C,\qquad\mathbf F_1=\nabla\phi_1}.
$$

For the second field, direct component [differentiation](../../../../../differentiation.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\nabla\times\mathbf F_2=
(-2x^3y,\,3x^2z(2y-z),\,3x^2z(2y-z))}.
$$

It is not identically zero. Thus **$\mathbf F_1$ is conservative and $\mathbf F_2$ is not** on $\mathbb R^3$. The conditional path-integral requests therefore apply to $\mathbf F_1$.
