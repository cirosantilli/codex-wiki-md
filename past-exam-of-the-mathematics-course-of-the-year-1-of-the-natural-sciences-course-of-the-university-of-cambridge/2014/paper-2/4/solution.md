<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

The [characteristic equation](../../../../characteristic-equation-of-a-constant-coefficient-differential-equation.md) of the homogeneous equation is $(r-1)(r-3)=0$. Thus the [complementary function](../../../../homogeneous-solution.md) is

$$
\boxed{y_c=Ae^x+Be^{3x}}.
$$

A linear trial $y_p=ax+b$ gives $3ax+(3b-4a)=3x$, so $a=1$, $b=4/3$. One [particular integral](../../../../particular-solution.md) is therefore $\boxed{y_p=x+4/3}$.
