<h1 id="1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

The $z$ component of the [cross product](../../../../cross-product.md) is $2a-2$. For the result to lie in the $x$-$y$ [plane](../../../../plane.md), it must vanish, giving $a=1$. The [dot product](../../../../dot-product.md) condition is then $2a+2-3b=0$, so

$$
\boxed{a=1,\qquad b=\frac43}.
$$
