<h1 id="8/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [8](../8.md)

Stationarity requires both first [partial derivatives](../../../../partial-derivative.md) to vanish:

$$
z_x=-\sin x\sin y=0,\qquad z_y=\cos x\cos y=0.
$$

For example, choosing $x=\pi$ and $\cos y=0$ gives two points strictly inside the prescribed rectangle:

$$
\boxed{(x,y)=(\pi,\pi/2),\qquad(\pi,3\pi/2)}.
$$

Both satisfy the two [derivative](../../../../derivative.md) equations directly.
