<h1 id="18/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For the composite [square root](../../../../../square-root.md), $y>1$ throughout the restricted $x$ interval. Differentiate with respect to $y$:

$$
\boxed{\frac{dz}{dy}=\frac1{2y\sqrt{\log y}}},\qquad
\boxed{\frac{d^2z}{dy^2}=-\frac{2\log y+1}{4y^2(\log y)^{3/2}}}.
$$

The second expression comes from differentiating both $y^{-1}$ and $(\log y)^{-1/2}$. The endpoints where $y=1$ are excluded here.
