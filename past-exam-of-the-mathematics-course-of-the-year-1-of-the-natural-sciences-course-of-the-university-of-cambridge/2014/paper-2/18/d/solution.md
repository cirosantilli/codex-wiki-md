<h1 id="18/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

On $0<x<\pi$, $\sqrt{\log y}=\sqrt{\sin^2x}=\sin x>0$. The [chain rule](../../../../../chain-rule.md) gives

$$
\frac{dz}{dx}=\frac{dz}{dy}\frac{dy}{dx}
=\frac{1}{2y\sin x}\,y\sin2x.
$$

Using $\sin2x=2\sin x\cos x$ yields

$$
\boxed{\frac{dz}{dx}=\cos x}.
$$
