<h1 id="18/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The [second derivative chain rule](../../../../../second-derivative-chain-rule.md) is

$$
\frac{d^2z}{dx^2}=\frac{d^2z}{dy^2}(y')^2+\frac{dz}{dy}y''.
$$

Put $s=\sin x>0$ and $c_0=\cos x$. Substituting the preceding results and $y'=2ysc_0$ gives

$$
\frac{d^2z}{dx^2}
=-\frac{(2s^2+1)c_0^2}{s}
+2sc_0^2+\frac{c_0^2-s^2}{s}=-s.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{\frac{d^2z}{dx^2}=-\sin x}.
$$
