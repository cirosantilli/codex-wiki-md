<h1 id="18/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

Substitution first makes the square-root branch transparent:

$$
z=\sqrt{\log(e^{\sin^2x})}=\sqrt{\sin^2x}=|\sin x|.
$$

On the given interval this is exactly $\sin x$, so direct [differentiation](../../../../../differentiation.md) gives

$$
\boxed{z''=-\sin x},
$$

in agreement with the chain-rule calculation. Outside this interval one must retain the absolute value; there are cusps at integer multiples of $\pi$.
