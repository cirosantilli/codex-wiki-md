<h1 id="11/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $C$ [mean](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) control-group membership and $E$ the adverse event. Equal allocation gives $P(C)=P(C^c)=1/2$, so the [law of total probability](../../../../../law-of-total-probability.md) gives $P(E)=(p_0+p_1)/2$. [Bayes' theorem](../../../../../bayes-theorem.md) then gives

$$
\boxed{P(C\mid E)=\frac{p_0P(C)}{p_0P(C)+p_1P(C^c)}
=\frac{p_0}{p_0+p_1}}.
$$

This is defined when $p_0+p_1>0$. If both are zero, the conditioning event has zero [probability](../../../../../probability.md) and the stated [conditional probability](../../../../../conditional-probability.md) is undefined.
