<h1 id="5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

Take the Cartesian [partial derivatives](../../../../partial-derivative.md) component by component. The [divergence](../../../../divergence.md) is

$$
\boxed{\nabla\cdot\mathbf F=-e^{-x}\cos z-e^{-y}\sin z}.
$$

The constant third component has zero [derivatives](../../../../derivative.md). The [curl](../../../../curl.md) consequently has only two nonzero components:

$$
\boxed{\nabla\times\mathbf F=(-e^{-y}\cos z,-e^{-x}\sin z,0)}.
$$
