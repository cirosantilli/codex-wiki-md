<h1 id="17/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For a unit direction $\mathbf v$, the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](../../../../../../cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) gives $\nabla f\cdot\mathbf v\leq|\nabla f|$, with equality in the [gradient](../../../../../../gradient.md) direction. Its norm here is $\sqrt{4+9+1}=\sqrt{14}$, so the direction of fastest increase is

$$
\boxed{\mathbf v_{\max}=\frac{(2,-3,1)}{\sqrt{14}}}.
$$

The maximal [directional derivative](../../../../../../directional-derivative.md) itself is $\sqrt{14}$.
