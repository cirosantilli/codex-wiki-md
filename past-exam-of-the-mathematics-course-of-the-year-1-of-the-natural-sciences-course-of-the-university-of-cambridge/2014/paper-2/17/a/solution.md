<h1 id="17/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Treat $R$ and $a$ as constants and use the [total differential](../../../../../total-differential.md) in the independent variables $n,V,T$:

$$
\boxed{dp=\left(\frac{RT}{V}-\frac{2na}{V^2}\right)dn
+\left(-\frac{nRT}{V^2}+\frac{2n^2a}{V^3}\right)dV
+\frac{nR}{V}\,dT}.
$$

Each coefficient differentiates with respect to its displayed variable while holding the other two fixed; the formula applies where $V\ne0$.
