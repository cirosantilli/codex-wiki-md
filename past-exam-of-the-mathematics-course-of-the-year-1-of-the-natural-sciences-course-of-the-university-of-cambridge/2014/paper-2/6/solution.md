<h1 id="6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

Set $\xi=x-ct$. The [chain rule](../../../../chain-rule.md) gives $u_t=-cf'(\xi)$ and $u_x=f'(\xi)$, so $u_t+cu_x=0$ for any differentiable $f$. The initial profile fixes $f(\xi)=\cos\xi$, giving

$$
\boxed{u(x,t)=\cos(x-ct)}.
$$

This is the translated initial profile, as described by the [transport equation](../../../../transport-equation-split.md).
