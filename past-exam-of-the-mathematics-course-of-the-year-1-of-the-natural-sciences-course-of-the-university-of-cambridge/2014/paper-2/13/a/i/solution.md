<h1 id="13/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The hyperbolic identity $\cosh^2x-\sinh^2x=1$ and $\cosh x+\sinh x=e^x$ reduce the integrand to $1+e^x$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\int(\cosh^2x+\cosh x-\sinh^2x+\sinh x)\,dx=x+e^x+C}.
$$
