<h1 id="13/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The argument $3x^3+2x$ is odd, so its squared [sine](../../../../../sine.md) is even. Replacing $x$ by $-x$ interchanges the numerator and denominator inside the [logarithm](../../../../../logarithm.md), changing that factor's sign. Thus the full integrand is an [odd function](../../../../../odd-function.md). It is continuous on the symmetric interval because $|x|\leq1/\pi<1$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\int_{-1/\pi}^{1/\pi}\sin^2(3x^3+2x)
\log\frac{1-x^5}{1+x^5}\,dx=0}.
$$
