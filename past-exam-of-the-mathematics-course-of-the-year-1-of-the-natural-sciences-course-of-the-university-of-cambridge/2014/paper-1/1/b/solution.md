<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The outer [exponential function](../../../../../exponential-function.md) differentiates to itself, and the inner [sine](../../../../../sine.md) has [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) [cosine](../../../../../cosine.md). The [chain rule](../../../../../chain-rule.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\frac d{dx}e^{\sin x}=e^{\sin x}\cos x.}
$$
