<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [sphere](../../../../../sphere.md) has surface area $4\pi R^2=400\pi\,\mathrm{m^2}$. Dividing the given surface-patch area by that total gives the dimensionless fraction

$$
\boxed{\frac{10}{400\pi}=\frac1{40\pi}\approx0.00796,}
$$

or about **$0.796\%$** of the surface. The patch area is already measured on the surface, so no planar projection or spherical-cap conversion is needed.
