<h1 id="6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Interpret the stated side lengths as all eight edges of the [square pyramid](../../../../../square-pyramid.md). The equal lateral edges put its apex above the base [centre](../../../../../center-geometry.md). The base-centre-to-vertex distance is half the diagonal, $1/\sqrt2\,\mathrm m$. The [Pythagorean theorem](../../../../../pythagorean-theorem.md) therefore gives

$$
h=\sqrt{1^2-(1/\sqrt2)^2}=\frac1{\sqrt2}\,\mathrm m.
$$

Using the [pyramid](../../../../../pyramid.md) volume formula with a $1\,\mathrm{m^2}$ base,

$$
\boxed{V=\frac13(1)\frac1{\sqrt2}
=\frac1{3\sqrt2}\,\mathrm{m^3}\approx0.2357\,\mathrm{m^3}.}
$$
