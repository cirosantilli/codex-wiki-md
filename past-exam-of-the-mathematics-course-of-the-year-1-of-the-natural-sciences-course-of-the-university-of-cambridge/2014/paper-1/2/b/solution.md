<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Set $u=\ln x$, so $du=dx/x$. The real domain is $x>1$, ensuring $u>0$. The [integration by substitution](../../../../../integration-by-substitution.md) followed by [integration by parts](../../../../../integration-by-parts.md) gives

$$
\int\frac{\ln(\ln x)}x\,dx=\int\ln u\,du=u\ln u-u+C.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{\ln x\,\ln(\ln x)-\ln x+C,\qquad x>1.}
$$
