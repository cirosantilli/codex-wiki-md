<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For positive $a$, write $a^{-x}=\exp(-x\ln a)$. Differentiating by the [chain rule](../../../../../chain-rule.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\frac d{dx}a^{-x}=-(\ln a)a^{-x}.}
$$

The [natural logarithm](../../../../../natural-logarithm.md) of the constant base supplies the factor; it must not be omitted when $a\ne e$.
