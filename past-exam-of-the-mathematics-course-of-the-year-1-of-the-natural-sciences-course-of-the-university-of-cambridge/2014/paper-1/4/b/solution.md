<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [general solution](../../../../../general-solution.md) of this first-order [linear ordinary differential equation](../../../../../linear-ordinary-differential-equation-split.md) is $y=Ce^{3x}$. Its [initial condition](../../../../../initial-condition.md) gives $C=3$, so

$$
\boxed{y(x)=3e^{3x}.}
$$

[Differentiation](../../../../../differentiation.md) verifies $y'=3y$ and substitution at zero gives the required value.
