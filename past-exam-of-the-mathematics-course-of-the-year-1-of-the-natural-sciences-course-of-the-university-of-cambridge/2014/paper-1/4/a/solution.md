<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The specified range ensures $\cos y\ne0$, so the [separable differential equation](../../../../../separable-differential-equation.md) can be written $\sec^2y\,dy=\sin x\,dx$. Integrating gives $\tan y=C-\cos x$. The range selects the principal [inverse tangent](../../../../../inverse-tangent.md), hence

$$
\boxed{y(x)=\arctan(C-\cos x),\qquad C\in\mathbb R.}
$$

Every finite $C$ produces a solution within the required range. Constant solutions at the excluded values $y=\pm\pi/2$ are not part of this family or of the question's domain.
