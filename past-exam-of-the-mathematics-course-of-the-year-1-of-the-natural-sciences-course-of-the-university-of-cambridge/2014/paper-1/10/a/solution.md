<h1 id="10/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The original line meets the horizontal axis at $(-3/2,0)$. A perpendicular line has slope $-1/2$, the negative reciprocal of $2$, so

$$
\boxed{y=-\frac12\left(x+\frac32\right)
=-\frac x2-\frac34.}
$$

Substituting $x=-3/2$ verifies the required common point.
