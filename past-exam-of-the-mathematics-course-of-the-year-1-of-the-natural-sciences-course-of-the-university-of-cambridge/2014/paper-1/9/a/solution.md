<h1 id="9/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

On $0\leq x\leq1/2$, the [quadratic polynomial](../../../../../quadratic-polynomial.md) is positive. The required area is therefore its [definite integral](../../../../../definite-integral.md) above the horizontal axis:

$$
\mathcal A=\int_0^{1/2}(x^2-3x+2)dx
=\left[\frac{x^3}{3}-\frac{3x^2}{2}+2x\right]_0^{1/2}
=\frac1{24}-\frac38+1=\boxed{\frac23}.
$$

The units are square coordinate units.
