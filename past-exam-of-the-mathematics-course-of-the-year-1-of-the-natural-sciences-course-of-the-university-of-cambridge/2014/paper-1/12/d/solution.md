<h1 id="12/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

By [De Moivre's theorem](../../../../../de-moivre-s-theorem.md), $(\cos\theta+i\sin\theta)^4=\cos4\theta+i\sin4\theta$. Take real parts of the binomial expansion:

$$
\cos4\theta=\cos^4\theta-6\cos^2\theta\sin^2\theta+\sin^4\theta.
$$

Eliminate $\sin^2\theta$ using $1-\cos^2\theta$ and collect powers:

$$
\boxed{\cos4\theta=8\cos^4\theta-8\cos^2\theta+1.}
$$
