<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use the real [antiderivative](../../../../../antiderivative.md) $\ln|x|$, valid on either half-line away from zero:

$$
\int_{-2}^{-1}\frac{dx}{x}
=\big[\ln|x|\big]_{-2}^{-1}=\ln1-\ln2=\boxed{-\ln2}.
$$

The negative answer is consistent with a negative integrand and increasing [integration](../../../../../integral-split.md) limits.
