<h1 id="19/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Multiply the [Maclaurin series](../../../../../maclaurin-series.md) $e^{-x}=1-x+x^2/2+O(x^3)$ by the [binomial series](../../../../../binomial-series.md) $(1+x)^{-1/2}=1-x/2+3x^2/8+O(x^3)$. The constant, linear and quadratic coefficients are therefore

$$
\boxed{e^{-x}(1+x)^{-1/2}=1-\frac32x+\frac{11}{8}x^2+O(x^3).}
$$
