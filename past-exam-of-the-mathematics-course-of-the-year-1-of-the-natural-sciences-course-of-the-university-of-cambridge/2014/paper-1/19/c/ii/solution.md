<h1 id="19/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Use the bound $|\sin n|\leq1$:

$$
\left|\frac{\sin n}{n^2}\right|\leq\frac1{n^2}.
$$

The comparison [p-series](../../../../../../p-series.md) has exponent $2>1$ and converges. Hence, by the [comparison test for series](../../../../../../comparison-test-for-series.md), the original series is **absolutely convergent**. No alternating-sign or monotonicity assumption about $\sin n$ is needed.
