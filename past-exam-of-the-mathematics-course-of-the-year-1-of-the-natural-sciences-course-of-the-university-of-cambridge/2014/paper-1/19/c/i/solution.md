<h1 id="19/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

There is an indexing defect in the original PDF: the term at $n=1$ is undefined because $\ln1=0$. Thus the series as literally indexed is not defined. The natural finite-index repair is to start at $n=2$, and that repaired series still **diverges**.

Indeed, for $n\geq2$ the terms are positive and

$$
\frac{u_{n+1}}{u_n}
=2\frac{n\ln n}{(n+1)\ln(n+1)}\longrightarrow2>1.
$$

They eventually increase at a geometric rate and do not tend to zero. Since the [terms of a convergent series tend to zero](../../../../../../terms-of-a-convergent-series-tend-to-zero.md), no choice of a finite replacement for the missing first term could make the tail converge.
