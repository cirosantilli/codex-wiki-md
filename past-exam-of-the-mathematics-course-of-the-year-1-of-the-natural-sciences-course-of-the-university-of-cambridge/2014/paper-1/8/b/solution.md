<h1 id="8/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The second [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) is $y''=6x$, so $y''(1)=6>0$. By the [second-derivative test](../../../../../second-derivative-test.md), the [stationary point](../../../../../stationary-point.md) is a **strict [local minimum](../../../../../local-minimum.md)**, at $(1,5)$. It is not a global minimum, since this cubic is unbounded below toward negative infinity.
