<h1 id="18/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The three preceding [probabilities](../../../../../probability.md) satisfy

$$
\boxed{\frac{28}{45}+\frac{16}{45}+\frac1{45}=1.}
$$

Their [expected value](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) and second [moment](../../../../../moment.md) are

$$
\mu=\frac{16+2}{45}=\frac25,\qquad
\mathbb E[X^2]=\frac{16+4}{45}=\frac49.
$$

Subtracting the squared [mean](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\mu=\frac25,\qquad\sigma^2=\frac49-\frac4{25}=\frac{64}{225}.}
$$

The value $\mu=2(2/10)$ also follows from [linearity of expectation](../../../../../linearity-of-expectation.md). The [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) includes the finite-population reduction caused by [sampling without replacement](../../../../../sampling-without-replacement.md).
