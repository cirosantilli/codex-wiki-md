<h1 id="13/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) of the homogeneous equation is $r^2+7r+12=(r+3)(r+4)$. Thus the [complementary function](../../../../../homogeneous-solution.md) is $C_1e^{-3x}+C_2e^{-4x}$. The forcing resonates with the first term, so a [resonant exponential particular solution](../../../../../resonant-exponential-particular-solution.md) is $y_p=Kxe^{-3x}$. Substitution gives $y_p''+7y_p'+12y_p=Ke^{-3x}$, hence $K=2$.

The full solution is $y=(C_1+2x)e^{-3x}+C_2e^{-4x}$. At zero, the two [initial conditions](../../../../../initial-condition.md) give $C_1+C_2=1$ and $2-3C_1-4C_2=0$. Solving yields $C_1=2$, $C_2=-1$, so

$$
\boxed{y(x)=(2+2x)e^{-3x}-e^{-4x}.}
$$
