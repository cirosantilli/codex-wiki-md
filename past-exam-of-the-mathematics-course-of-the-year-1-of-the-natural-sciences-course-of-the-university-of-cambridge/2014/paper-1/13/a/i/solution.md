<h1 id="13/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

An [integrating factor](../../../../../../integrating-factor.md) is $e^{3x}$. Multiplication gives $(e^{3x}y)'=8e^{3x}$, so $y=8/3+Ce^{-3x}$. The [initial condition](../../../../../../initial-condition.md) gives $C=4/3$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{y(x)=\frac83+\frac43e^{-3x}.}
$$
