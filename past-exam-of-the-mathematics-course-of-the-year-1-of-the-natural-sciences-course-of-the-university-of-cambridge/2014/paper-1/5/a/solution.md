<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Substituting the line into the [hyperbola](../../../../../hyperbola.md) gives

$$
7x^2-(x+1)^2=7
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
3x^2-x-4=(3x-4)(x+1)=0.
$$

The two [polynomial roots](../../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md) give all intersections:

$$
\boxed{(x,y)=(-1,0),\qquad (4/3,7/3).}
$$

Both satisfy the original line as well as the curve.
