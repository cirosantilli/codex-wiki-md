<h1 id="15/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [polar area element](../../../../../../polar-area-element.md) is $r\,dr\,d\phi$. Integrating first in [radius](../../../../../../radius.md) and using symmetry gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal A
&=\frac12\int_{-\pi/3}^{\pi/3}\left[(1+\cos\phi)^2-\frac94\right]d\phi\\
&=\int_0^{\pi/3}\left[-\frac34+2\cos\phi+\frac12\cos2\phi\right]d\phi\\
&=\left[-\frac{3\phi}{4}+2\sin\phi+\frac14\sin2\phi\right]_0^{\pi/3}
=\boxed{\frac{9\sqrt3}{8}-\frac\pi4.}
\end{aligned}
$$

This is the specialization $a=1$, $c=3/2$ of the [polar cap between a cardioid and a circle](../../../../../../polar-cap-between-a-cardioid-and-a-circle.md) area formula.
