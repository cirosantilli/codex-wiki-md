<h1 id="11/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Expand an arbitrary [vector](../../../../../vector.md) in the [orthonormal eigenbasis](../../../../../orthonormal-eigenbasis.md):

$$
\mathbf r=r_-\mathbf e_-+r_0\mathbf e_0+r_+\mathbf e_+,
\qquad A\mathbf r=-6r_-\mathbf e_-+12r_+\mathbf e_+.
$$

Its image has no component along $\mathbf e_0$, so

$$
\boxed{(A\mathbf r)\cdot\mathbf e_0=0\quad\text{for every }\mathbf r,
\qquad\mathbf e_0=\frac1{\sqrt6}(1,2,1)^{\mathsf T}\ne0.}
$$

Alternatively, symmetry gives $(A\mathbf r)\cdot\mathbf e_0=\mathbf r\cdot A\mathbf e_0=0$. This is [image-kernel orthogonality for an adjoint](../../../../../image-kernel-orthogonality-for-an-adjoint.md), with $A^{\mathsf T}=A$.
