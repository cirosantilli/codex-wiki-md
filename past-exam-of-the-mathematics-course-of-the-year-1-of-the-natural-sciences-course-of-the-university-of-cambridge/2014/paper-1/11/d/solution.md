<h1 id="11/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Resolve the input along the three perpendicular eigendirections. The [linear map](../../../../../linear-map.md) **removes the component along $(1,2,1)$, stretches the component along $(1,-1,1)$ by $12$, and reverses and stretches the component along $(1,0,-1)$ by $6$**. Every output lies in the [plane](../../../../../plane.md) perpendicular to $(1,2,1)$. A nonzero input in the [kernel of a linear map](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) $A$ is sent to zero; other nonzero inputs remain in that output [plane](../../../../../plane.md). Equivalently, take the [orthogonal projection](../../../../../orthogonal-projection.md) onto this [plane](../../../../../plane.md), then scale by the two signed [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) along its perpendicular eigendirections.
