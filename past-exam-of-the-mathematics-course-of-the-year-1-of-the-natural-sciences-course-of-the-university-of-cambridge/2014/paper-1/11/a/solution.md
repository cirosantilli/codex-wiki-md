<h1 id="11/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Expanding the [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) along the first row gives

$$
\det A=1(4-16)+4(-4+28)+7(16-28)
=-12+96-84=\boxed{0}.
$$

The [matrix trace](../../../../../matrix-trace.md) is the sum of the diagonal entries, **$\operatorname{tr}A=1+4+1=6$**. Since the [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) is the product of the [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md), at least one [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) is zero. Thus $A$ is singular; the [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) alone does not specify the two other [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md).
