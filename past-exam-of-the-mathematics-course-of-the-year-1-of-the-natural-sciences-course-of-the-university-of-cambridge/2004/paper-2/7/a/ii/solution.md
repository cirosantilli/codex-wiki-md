<h1 id="7/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Orthogonal projection of a small surface patch onto the $xy$ plane multiplies its scalar area by $\cos\alpha=\mathbf n\cdot\mathbf k>0$. Hence $dx\,dy=\cos\alpha\,dS$ and $d\mathbf S=\mathbf n\,dx\,dy/\cos\alpha$. Since $\mathbf n=\mathbf u/|\mathbf u|$ and $\mathbf u\cdot\mathbf k=1$, one has $\cos\alpha=1/|\mathbf u|$. The [vector area element of an implicit surface](../../../../../../vector-area-element-of-an-implicit-surface.md) is thus

$$
\boxed{d\mathbf S=\mathbf u\,dx\,dy=\left(\frac{3x^2+y}{y},\frac{x+z}{y},1\right)dx\,dy.}
$$

The positive final component implements the specified upward orientation.
