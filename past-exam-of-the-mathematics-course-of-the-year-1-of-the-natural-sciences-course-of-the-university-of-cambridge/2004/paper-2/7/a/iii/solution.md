<h1 id="7/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Take the [dot product](../../../../../../dot-product.md) of the given field with the upward [vector area element](../../../../../../vector-area-element.md). The surface relation simplifies its integrand:

$$
\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf S=[y(x+z)+x]dx\,dy=(1-x^3+x)dx\,dy.
$$

The projected domain is exactly the rectangle $1/2\leq x,y\leq1$, since $y>0$ determines a unique $z=(1-x^3)/y-x$. Therefore the [surface integral](../../../../../../surface-integral.md) is

$$
\boxed{\int_{S_1}\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf S=\frac12\int_{1/2}^1(1-x^3+x)dx=\frac12\left[x-\frac{x^4}{4}+\frac{x^2}{2}\right]_{1/2}^1=\frac{41}{128}.}
$$
