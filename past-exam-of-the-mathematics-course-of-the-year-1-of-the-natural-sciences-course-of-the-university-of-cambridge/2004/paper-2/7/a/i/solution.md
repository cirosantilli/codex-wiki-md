<h1 id="7/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Set $H(x,y,z)=x^3+xy+yz-1$. The [gradient](../../../../../../gradient.md) $\nabla H=(3x^2+y,x+z,y)$ is a [normal vector](../../../../../../normal-vector.md) to its [level set](../../../../../../level-set.md). Since $y\geq1/2$ on the given patch, division by its last component is valid and gives the unique normalized field

$$
\boxed{\mathbf u=\frac{\nabla H}{y}=\left(\frac{3x^2+y}{y},\frac{x+z}{y},1\right).}
$$

Its dot product with $\mathbf k$ is one, as required; it is not generally a unit vector.
