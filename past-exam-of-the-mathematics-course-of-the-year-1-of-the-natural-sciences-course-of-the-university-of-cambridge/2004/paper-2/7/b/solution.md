<h1 id="7/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The defining function $x^3+xy-1$ is independent of $z$, so a [normal vector](../../../../../normal-vector.md) is $(3x^2+y,x,0)$. It never vanishes here because $x>0$. Every normal is horizontal, whereas $\mathbf G=(0,0,x)$ is vertical and tangent to the surface. Their [dot product](../../../../../dot-product.md) is zero at every point, giving

$$
\boxed{\int_{S_2}\mathbf G\cdot d\mathbf S=0.}
$$

This is independent of orientation. Although the surface extends without a bound on $x$, the integrand vanishes identically, so no convergence problem arises for this flux.
