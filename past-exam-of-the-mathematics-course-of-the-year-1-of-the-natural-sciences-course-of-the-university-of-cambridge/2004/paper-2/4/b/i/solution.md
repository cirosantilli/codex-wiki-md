<h1 id="4/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $0<\theta<\pi$, the [chain rule](../../../../../../chain-rule.md) for a [parametric curve](../../../../../../parametric-curve.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{-4\sin4\theta}{-\sin\theta}=16\cos\theta\cos2\theta.}
$$

At $\theta=0$ the quotient of parameter derivatives is formally $0/0$, but the simplified expression has limit $16$. Thus **the limiting tangent at $(x,y)=(1,1)$ has gradient 16**. This is an endpoint limit of the given open parameter interval, or the ordinary tangent of the smooth polynomial extension $y=8x^4-8x^2+1$.
