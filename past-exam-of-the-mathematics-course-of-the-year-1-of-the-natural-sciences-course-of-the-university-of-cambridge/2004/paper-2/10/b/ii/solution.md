<h1 id="10/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Subtract the first four terms from the given full [p-series](../../../../../../p-series.md) sums. Write

$$
S_2=\frac{\pi^2}{6}-\left(1+\frac14+\frac19+\frac1{16}\right)\approx0.2213229557,
$$

and

$$
S_4=\frac{\pi^4}{90}-\left(1+\frac1{16}+\frac1{81}+\frac1{256}\right)\approx0.0035713047.
$$

Solving $I_p(k_p)=S_p$ yields $k_p=5-[(p-1)S_p]^{-1/(p-1)}$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{k_2=5-S_2^{-1}\approx0.482,\qquad k_4=5-(3S_4)^{-1/3}\approx0.464.}
$$

The unrounded shifts are approximately $0.4817158633$ and $0.4638861447$.
