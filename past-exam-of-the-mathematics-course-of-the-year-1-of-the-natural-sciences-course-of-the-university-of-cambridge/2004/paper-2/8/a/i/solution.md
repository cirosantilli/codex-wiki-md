<h1 id="8/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Multiply the [Taylor expansions](../../../../../../taylor-expansion.md) $\ln(1+x)=x-x^2/2+x^3/3+O(x^4)$ and $(1-x)^{-1}=1+x+x^2+O(x^3)$. Collecting the first three nonzero coefficients gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{\ln(1+x)}{1-x}=x+\frac{x^2}{2}+\frac{5x^3}{6}+O(x^4).}
$$

The cubic coefficient is $1-1/2+1/3=5/6$.
