<h1 id="8/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Multiply the two [power series](../../../../../../power-series.md) and collect equal powers of $x$. The [Cauchy product](../../../../../../cauchy-product.md) gives

$$
\boxed{c_0=a_0b_0,\qquad c_1=a_0b_1+a_1b_0,\qquad c_2=a_0b_2+a_1b_1+a_2b_0.}
$$

Only terms whose two exponents add to the target exponent contribute.
