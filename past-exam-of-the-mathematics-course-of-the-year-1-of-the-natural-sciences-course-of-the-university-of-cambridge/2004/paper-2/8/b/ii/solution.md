<h1 id="8/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

In the [Cauchy product](../../../../../../cauchy-product.md), $a_jx^j$ combines with $b_{i-j}x^{i-j}$ to give a term of degree $i$. Hence

$$
\boxed{c_i=\sum_{j=0}^i a_jb_{i-j}.}
$$

This coefficient identity holds for [formal power series](../../../../../../formal-power-series.md). For convergent [Taylor series](../../../../../../taylor-series.md) it also gives the function product throughout the common open interval of convergence, where absolute convergence justifies regrouping.
