<h1 id="9/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Set $h=k/100$ and $j=m/100$. The exact ratio is $(1+h)^p(1+j)^q$. Multiplying the two [binomial series](../../../../../../binomial-series.md) and retaining total degree at most two gives the [second-order relative change of a monomial](../../../../../../second-order-relative-change-of-a-monomial.md):

$$
\frac{\Delta f}{f}=ph+qj+\frac{p(p-1)}2h^2+pqhj+\frac{q(q-1)}2j^2+O((|h|+|j|)^3).
$$

Multiply by one hundred to express the change as a percentage:

$$
\boxed{pk+qm+\frac{p(p-1)k^2+2pqkm+q(q-1)m^2}{200}.}
$$

The cross term accounts for the simultaneous changes in both inputs and cannot be recovered by simply adding their separate second-order percentage changes.
