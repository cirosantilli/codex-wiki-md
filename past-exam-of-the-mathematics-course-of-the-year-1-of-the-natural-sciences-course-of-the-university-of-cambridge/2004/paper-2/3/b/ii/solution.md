<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Use $JE_1=-E_2$, $E_1J=E_2$ and $JE_1J=E_1$ to expand the [matrix product](../../../../../../matrix-product.md):

$$
AE_1A^T=c^2E_1+cs(JE_1-E_1J)-s^2JE_1J=(c^2-s^2)E_1-2csE_2.
$$

The double-angle identities therefore give

$$
\boxed{AE_1A^T=\cos\theta\,E_1-\sin\theta\,E_2.}
$$

Similarly $JE_2=E_1$, $E_2J=-E_1$ and $JE_2J=E_2$, so

$$
\boxed{AE_2A^T=\sin\theta\,E_1+\cos\theta\,E_2.}
$$

This [conjugation](../../../../../../conjugation.md) rotates the two-dimensional span of the two matrices through the double angle described by [orthogonal rotations generated by anticommuting involutions](../../../../../../orthogonal-rotations-generated-by-anticommuting-involutions.md).
