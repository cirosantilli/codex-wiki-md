<h1 id="3/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The given [symmetric matrices](../../../../../../symmetric-matrix.md) satisfy $E_j^TE_j=E_j^2=I$, so both are [orthogonal matrices](../../../../../../orthogonal-matrix.md). Put $J=E_1E_2$, $c=\cos(\theta/2)$ and $s=\sin(\theta/2)$. Anticommutation gives

$$
J^T=E_2E_1=-J,\qquad J^2=E_1E_2E_1E_2=-I.
$$

Thus $A=cI+sJ$ and $A^T=cI-sJ$, giving $AA^T=(c^2+s^2)I=I$. These are [orthogonal rotations generated by anticommuting involutions](../../../../../../orthogonal-rotations-generated-by-anticommuting-involutions.md). Also $A-A^T=2sJ$. Since $J^2=-I$ makes $J$ invertible, **$A$ is symmetric exactly when $\sin(\theta/2)=0$**.
