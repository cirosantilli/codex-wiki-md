<h1 id="3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

For [orthogonal matrices](../../../../orthogonal-matrix.md) $Q_1,\ldots,Q_m$ of the same size, transpose reverses the order of multiplication. Therefore

$$
(Q_1\cdots Q_m)^T(Q_1\cdots Q_m)=Q_m^T\cdots Q_1^TQ_1\cdots Q_m=I,
$$

by canceling the adjacent pairs $Q_j^TQ_j=I$ successively. Hence every finite product is an [orthogonal matrix](../../../../orthogonal-matrix.md).
