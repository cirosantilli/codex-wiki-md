<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $\Omega$ be the interior of the [tetrahedron](../../../../../tetrahedron.md), and take any constant [vector field](../../../../../vector-field.md) $\mathbf q$. Its [divergence](../../../../../divergence.md) is zero, so the [divergence theorem](../../../../../divergence-theorem.md) gives

$$
\mathbf q\cdot\sum_{i=1}^4\mathbf V_i=\sum_{i=1}^4\int_{f_i}\mathbf q\cdot\mathbf n_i\,dS=\int_\Omega\nabla\cdot\mathbf q\,dV=0.
$$

Choosing $\mathbf q$ to be each Cartesian basis vector proves $\sum_i\mathbf V_i=\mathbf0$. This is the general fact that [vector areas of a closed polyhedron sum to zero](../../../../../vector-areas-of-a-closed-polyhedron-sum-to-zero.md), specialized to four faces. The outward orientations are essential: the scalar face areas are all positive and do not sum to zero.
