↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

The [comparison test for series](../../../../comparison-test-for-series.md) says that $0\leq v_n\leq Cw_n$ eventually and convergence of $\sum w_n$ imply convergence of $\sum v_n$. Conversely, an eventual positive lower comparison with a divergent positive series proves divergence. The [ratio test](../../../../ratio-test.md) uses $L=\lim_{n\to\infty}v_{n+1}/v_n$: $L<1$ proves convergence, $L>1$ proves divergence, and $L=1$ is inconclusive. Removing finitely many initial zero terms does not affect these conclusions.
