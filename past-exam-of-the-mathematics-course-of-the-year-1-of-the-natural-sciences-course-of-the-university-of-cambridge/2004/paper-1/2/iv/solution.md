<h1 id="2/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

For $n\geq1$, the ratio of positive terms is

$$
\frac{(n+1)^4e^{-(n+1)^2}}{n^4e^{-n^2}}=\left(1+\frac1n\right)^4e^{-2n-1}\longrightarrow0.
$$

The [ratio test](../../../../../ratio-test.md) proves **the series converges**. Its zero term at $n=0$ has no effect.
