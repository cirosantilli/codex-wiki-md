<h1 id="10/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The modulus equation says that $z$ has fixed distance eight from $-1-i$. Thus its locus is **a circle of radius eight centered at $(-1,-1)$**, with Cartesian equation $(x+1)^2+(y+1)^2=64$.
