<h1 id="10/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Writing $u=X+iY$ gives $X=2x$, $Y=y$. Substitute $x=X/2$, $y=Y$ into the original circle equation. This [affine image of a circle](../../../../../../affine-image-of-a-circle.md) is the [ellipse](../../../../../../ellipse.md)

$$
\boxed{\frac{(X+2)^2}{16^2}+\frac{(Y+1)^2}{8^2}=1,}
$$

centered at $(-2,-1)$ with horizontal semiaxis sixteen and vertical semiaxis eight. The map covers the whole ellipse as $z$ covers the circle.
