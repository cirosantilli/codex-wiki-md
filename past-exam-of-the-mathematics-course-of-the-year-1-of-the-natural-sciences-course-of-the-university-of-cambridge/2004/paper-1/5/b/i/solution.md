<h1 id="5/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The continuous nonnegative part has total mass $\lim_{y\to\infty}(1-e^{-y})/2=1/2$. The remaining mass is at $-1$, giving $\boxed{m=1/2}$. This is a [mixed probability distribution](../../../../../../mixed-probability-distribution.md), specifically a [mixture of a point mass and an exponential distribution](../../../../../../mixture-of-a-point-mass-and-an-exponential-distribution.md); its atom must be included in normalization.
