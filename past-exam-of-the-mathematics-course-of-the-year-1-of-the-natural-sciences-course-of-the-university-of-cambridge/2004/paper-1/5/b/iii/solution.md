↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The second moment of the [mixed probability distribution](../../../../../../mixed-probability-distribution.md) is $\mathbb E[Y^2]=\tfrac12(-1)^2+\tfrac12\int_0^\infty y^2e^{-y}dy=1/2+1=3/2$. Since the mean is zero,

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Var}(Y)=\frac32.}
$$
