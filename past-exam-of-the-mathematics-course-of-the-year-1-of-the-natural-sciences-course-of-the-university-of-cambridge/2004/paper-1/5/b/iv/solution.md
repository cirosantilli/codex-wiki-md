<h1 id="5/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The conditioning event selects the exponential component: $P(Y\geq0)=1/2$ and $P(Y\geq1)=e^{-1}/2$. Thus [conditional probability](../../../../../../conditional-probability.md) gives

$$
\boxed{P(Y\geq1\mid Y\geq0)=\frac{e^{-1}/2}{1/2}=e^{-1}.}
$$

The atom at $-1$ is excluded by the conditioning event.
