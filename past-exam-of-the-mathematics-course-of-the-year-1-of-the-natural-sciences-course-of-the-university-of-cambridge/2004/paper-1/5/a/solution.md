<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [cumulative distribution function](../../../../../cumulative-distribution-function.md) must tend to one at infinity, so $\boxed{k=1}$. Differentiation gives the [probability density function](../../../../../probability-density-function.md) $f_X(x)=e^{-x}$ for $x>0$ and zero for $x<0$; there is no atom at zero. This is an [exponential distribution](../../../../../exponential-distribution.md) of rate one. Its first two moments are $\int_0^\infty xe^{-x}dx=1$ and $\int_0^\infty x^2e^{-x}dx=2$, so

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[X]=1,\qquad\operatorname{Var}(X)=2-1^2=1.}
$$
