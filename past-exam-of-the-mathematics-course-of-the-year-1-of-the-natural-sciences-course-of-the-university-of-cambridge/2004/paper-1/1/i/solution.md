<h1 id="1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Take $P=(2,-1,2)$, $Q=(1,2,3)$ and $R=(4,1,0)$. The two plane directions are $Q-P=(-1,3,1)$ and $R-P=(2,2,-2)$. Their [cross product](../../../../../cross-product.md) is $(-8,0,-8)$, so a [normal vector](../../../../../normal-vector.md) is $(1,0,1)$. The [Cartesian equation of a plane](../../../../../cartesian-equation-of-a-plane.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{x+z=4.}
$$

Each given point satisfies it, and the nonzero cross product confirms that the points determine a unique plane.
