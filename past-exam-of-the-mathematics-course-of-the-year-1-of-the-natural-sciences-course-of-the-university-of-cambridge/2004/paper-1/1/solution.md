↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

In components, the [scalar triple product](../../../../scalar-triple-product.md) is

$$
\mathbf a\cdot(\mathbf b\times\mathbf c)=a_1(b_2c_3-b_3c_2)+a_2(b_3c_1-b_1c_3)+a_3(b_1c_2-b_2c_1).
$$

It is the [determinant](../../../../determinant.md) with rows $\mathbf a,\mathbf b,\mathbf c$. A cyclic permutation of the rows has positive sign, so $\mathbf a\cdot(\mathbf b\times\mathbf c)=\mathbf c\cdot(\mathbf a\times\mathbf b)=(\mathbf a\times\mathbf b)\cdot\mathbf c$. Equivalently the [Levi-Civita symbol](../../../../levi-civita-symbol.md) expression $\epsilon_{ijk}a_ib_jc_k$ is unchanged by that cyclic relabeling.
