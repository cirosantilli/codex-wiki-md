↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [volume](../../../../../volume.md) of a [parallelepiped](../../../../../parallelepiped.md) is the absolute [scalar triple product](../../../../../scalar-triple-product.md) of its edge vectors. Here

$$
\boxed{V=\left|\det\begin{pmatrix}1&3&0\\-1&3&-2\\0&1&1\end{pmatrix}\right|=|8|=8.}
$$

The absolute value removes the orientation of the chosen edge ordering.
