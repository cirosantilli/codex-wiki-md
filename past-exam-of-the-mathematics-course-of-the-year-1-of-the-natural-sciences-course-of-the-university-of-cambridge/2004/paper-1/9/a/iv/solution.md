<h1 id="9/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

For the positive real base ten, use its real [natural logarithm](../../../../../../natural-logarithm.md). Then [Euler's formula](../../../../../../euler-s-formula.md) gives

$$
\boxed{10^i=e^{i\ln10}=\cos(\ln10)+i\sin(\ln10).}
$$
