<h1 id="9/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The modulus of $i$ is one and its arguments are $\pi/2+2k\pi$. Thus $\boxed{\log i=i(\pi/2+2k\pi)}$, with principal value $\boxed{i\pi/2}$.
