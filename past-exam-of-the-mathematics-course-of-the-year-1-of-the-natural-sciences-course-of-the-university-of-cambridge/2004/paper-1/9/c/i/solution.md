<h1 id="9/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The fifth power of the [odd](../../../../../../odd-function.md) function $\tanh x$ is odd and continuous. Its integral over the symmetric interval is therefore $\boxed{\int_{-1000}^{1000}\tanh^5x\,dx=0}$.
