<h1 id="6/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

At $k=6$, the second row is twice the first. The independent equations are $x+3y=0$ and $4x+z=0$. Therefore the [null space](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) is

$$
\boxed{(x,y,z)=t(-3,1,12),\qquad t\in\mathbb R.}
$$

It has dimension one, consistent with matrix rank two.
