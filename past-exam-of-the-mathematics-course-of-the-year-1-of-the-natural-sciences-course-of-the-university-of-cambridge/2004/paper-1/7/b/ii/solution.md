<h1 id="7/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The antisymmetric contribution is the negative of twice the [cross-product matrix](../../../../../../cross-product-matrix.md) for $\mathbf a$:

$$
M=\begin{pmatrix}1&2a_3&-2a_2\\-2a_3&1&2a_1\\2a_2&-2a_1&1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The [cross-product matrix spectrum](../../../../../../cross-product-matrix-spectrum.md) is $0,\pm i|\mathbf a|$, so $M$ has eigenvalues $1,1\pm2i|\mathbf a|$. Multiplication gives

$$
\boxed{\det M=1+4|\mathbf a|^2.}
$$

Direct expansion of the displayed matrix yields the same real positive determinant.
