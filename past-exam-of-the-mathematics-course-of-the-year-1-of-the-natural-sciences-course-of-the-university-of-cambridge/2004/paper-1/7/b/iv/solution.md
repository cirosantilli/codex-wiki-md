<h1 id="7/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Taking [determinants](../../../../../../determinant.md) in $MN=N$ gives $(\det M-1)\det N=0$. Here $\det M-1=4|\mathbf a|^2>0$ because $\mathbf a\ne0$, so $\boxed{\det N=0}$. This also agrees with $N=\mathbf a\mathbf b^T$ being an [outer product](../../../../../../outer-product.md) of rank one.
