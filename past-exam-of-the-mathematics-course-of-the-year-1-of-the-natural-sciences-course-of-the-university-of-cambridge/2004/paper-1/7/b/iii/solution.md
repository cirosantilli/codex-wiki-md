<h1 id="7/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Use index contraction:

$$
(MN)_{ij}=(\delta_{ik}+2\epsilon_{ik\ell}a_\ell)a_kb_j=a_ib_j+2\epsilon_{ik\ell}a_ka_\ell b_j=N_{ij}.
$$

The last term vanishes because $a_ka_\ell$ is symmetric in the contracted indices while the [Levi-Civita symbol](../../../../../../levi-civita-symbol.md) is antisymmetric. Hence $\boxed{MN=N}$.
