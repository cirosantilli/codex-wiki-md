<h1 id="7/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [trace](../../../../../matrix-trace.md) is unchanged by transposition and is cyclic under a product. With $S^T=S$ and $A^T=-A$,

$$
\operatorname{tr}(AS)=\operatorname{tr}[(AS)^T]=\operatorname{tr}(-SA)=-\operatorname{tr}(AS).
$$

Thus $\boxed{\operatorname{tr}(AS)=0}$. In components this is the vanishing contraction of a [symmetric tensor](../../../../../symmetric-tensor.md) with an [antisymmetric tensor](../../../../../antisymmetric-tensor.md).
