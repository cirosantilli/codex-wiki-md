↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The function is a [probability density function](../../../../../../probability-density-function.md), rather than a cumulative distribution: its integral over both intervals must be one. Thus $2\alpha+2\alpha=1$ and

$$
\boxed{\alpha=\frac14.}
$$
