↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $p=3/4$, the five [probabilities](../../../../../../probability.md) in increasing white-egg order are $(1,12,54,108,81)/256$. The largest is $108/256=27/64$, so the **most common nest has three white eggs and one brown egg**, $W_3B_1$.
