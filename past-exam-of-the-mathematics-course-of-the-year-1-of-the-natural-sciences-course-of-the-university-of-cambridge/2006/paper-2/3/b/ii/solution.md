<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

On the first segment only $z$ varies and $F_z=x^2y=0$. On the second only $y$ varies and $F_y=x^2z=0$. On the last segment $y=z=\pi$, so the [line integral](../../../../../../line-integral.md) is

$$
\boxed{0+0+\int_0^\pi(2\pi^2x+\sin x)dx=\pi^4+2.}
$$

The agreement is the expected [path independence](../../../../../../path-independence.md) of a [conservative vector field](../../../../../../conservative-vector-field.md).
