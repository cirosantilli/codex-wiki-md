<h1 id="3/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Integrating the first component and checking the remaining [partial derivatives](../../../../../../partial-derivative.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\phi_3=x^2yz-\cos x+C,\qquad\mathbf F_3=\nabla\phi_3.}
$$

This explicit [scalar potential](../../../../../../scalar-potential.md) proves that the field is [conservative](../../../../../../conservative-vector-field.md).
