<h1 id="11/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Differentiate the first-order [chain rule](../../../../../chain-rule.md) again, including derivatives of the coordinate coefficients. The [second derivatives under a two-variable coordinate change](../../../../../second-derivatives-under-a-two-variable-coordinate-change.md) are

$$
\boxed{f_{xx}=f_{uu}u_x^2+2f_{uv}u_xv_x+f_{vv}v_x^2+f_u\,u_{xx}+f_v\,v_{xx},}
$$

and

$$
\boxed{f_{yy}=f_{uu}u_y^2+2f_{uv}u_yv_y+f_{vv}v_y^2+f_u\,u_{yy}+f_v\,v_{yy}.}
$$

The last two terms account for derivatives of the coordinate functions; they vanish for an [affine map](../../../../../affine-map.md). Smoothness justifies equality of the two mixed partial derivatives.
