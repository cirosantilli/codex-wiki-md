<h1 id="9/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Differentiate $z'=yz$ without dividing by $z$:

$$
z''=(y'+y^2)z=[(a+b)y-ab]z=(a+b)z'-abz.
$$

Thus the [linearization of a Riccati equation](../../../../../linearization-of-a-riccati-equation.md) gives

$$
\boxed{z''-(a+b)z'+abz=0.}
$$

Its [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) is $(\lambda-a)(\lambda-b)$, explaining both the distinct-root exponentials and the repeated-root factor $A+Bx$. Nonzero solutions reconstruct $y=z'/z$ between their zeros; the identically zero $z$ also satisfies the linear equation.
