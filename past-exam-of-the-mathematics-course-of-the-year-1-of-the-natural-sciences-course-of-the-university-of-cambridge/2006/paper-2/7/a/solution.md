<h1 id="7/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

With $M=2xy^2+4$ and $N=2x^2y-6$, the cross derivatives agree: $M_y=N_x=4xy$. This is an [exact first-order ordinary differential equation](../../../../../exact-first-order-ordinary-differential-equation.md). Integrating $M$ with respect to $x$ gives a potential $x^2y^2+4x+g(y)$; matching $N$ gives $g'=-6$. The implicit solution is

$$
\boxed{x^2y^2+4x-6y=C.}
$$
