<h1 id="10/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Put $p=k-1>0$. The preceding inequality bounds $n^{-k}<[ (n-1)^{-p}-n^{-p}]/p$ for $n\geq2$. For $N\geq2$, summing the [telescoping series](../../../../../telescoping-series.md) gives

$$
\sum_{n=1}^Nn^{-k}<1+\frac{1-N^{-(k-1)}}{k-1}<1+\frac1{k-1}.
$$

These nonnegative partial sums increase and are bounded, so the [monotone convergence theorem for sequences](../../../../../bounded-monotone-sequence-theorem.md) proves **convergence for every $k>1$**. This is the [telescoping bound for a p-series](../../../../../telescoping-bound-for-a-p-series.md), an alternative to the [integral test](../../../../../integral-test-for-convergence.md).
