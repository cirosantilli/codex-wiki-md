<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

With the [change of variables](../../../../../change-of-variables-formula.md) $u=\tan x$, one has $dx=du/(1+u^2)$, $\sin x=u/\sqrt{1+u^2}$ and $\cos x=1/\sqrt{1+u^2}$. The transformed [improper integral](../../../../../improper-integral.md) is integrable at its zero endpoint and becomes

$$
\int_0^{1/\sqrt3}(u^{-1/2}+u^{3/2})du=\left[2u^{1/2}+\frac25u^{5/2}\right]_0^{1/\sqrt3}.
$$

Hence

$$
\boxed{I=\frac{32}{15\sqrt[4]3}.}
$$
