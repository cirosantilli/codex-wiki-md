<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For real $x$, the angle $\arctan x$ lies in $(-\pi/2,\pi/2)$ and has sine $x/\sqrt{1+x^2}$. Thus the principal [arcsine](../../../../../inverse-sine.md) in the expression equals the [arctangent](../../../../../inverse-tangent.md) of $x$. Its [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) is

$$
\boxed{\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac1{1+x^2},\qquad x\in\mathbb R.}
$$

The [chain rule](../../../../../chain-rule.md) gives the same result directly, using $1-x^2/(1+x^2)=1/(1+x^2)$ with a positive square root.
