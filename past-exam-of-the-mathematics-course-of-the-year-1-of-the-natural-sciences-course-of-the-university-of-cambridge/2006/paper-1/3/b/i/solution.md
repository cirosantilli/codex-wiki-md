<h1 id="3/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Write $-8i=8e^{-i\pi/2}$. The three distinct [complex roots](../../../../../../complex-root.md) are $z=2e^{i(-\pi/6+2k\pi/3)}$, $k=0,1,2$. In Cartesian form,

$$
\boxed{z=\sqrt3-i,\quad 2i,\quad-\sqrt3-i.}
$$
