<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Use the [complex exponential](../../../../../complex-exponential-function.md) forms of the [sine](../../../../../sine.md) and [cosine](../../../../../cosine.md), and put $w=e^{2iz}$. Multiplying the equation by $2e^{iz}$ gives $(2-i)(w-1)/i+(w+1)=0$, which simplifies to $w=1-i$. Taking all branches of the [complex logarithm](../../../../../complex-logarithm.md),

$$
2iz=\frac12\ln2+i\left(-\frac\pi4+2\pi n\right).
$$

Thus every solution is

$$
\boxed{z=n\pi-\frac\pi8-\frac{i}{4}\ln2,\qquad n\in\mathbb Z.}
$$

The exponential factor used to multiply the equation is nonzero, so no solutions were introduced or lost.
