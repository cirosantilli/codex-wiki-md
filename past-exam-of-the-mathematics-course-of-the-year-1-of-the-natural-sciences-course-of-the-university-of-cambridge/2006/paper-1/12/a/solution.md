↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For [Laplace's equation](../../../../../laplace-equation.md), seek a nonzero separated product $f=X(x)Y(y)$. Dividing by $XY$ gives $X''/X=-Y''/Y$, so both sides are a [separation constant](../../../../../separation-constant.md). Choosing it as $-k^2$ gives $X''+k^2X=0$, $Y''-k^2Y=0$, with trigonometric and [hyperbolic functions](../../../../../hyperbolic-function.md), respectively. A zero constant gives linear factors; a positive constant interchanges the two types. [Boundary conditions](../../../../../boundary-condition.md) determine which modes are allowed, and [linearity](../../../../../linearity.md) permits their superposition to match the remaining boundary data.
