<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let $S_m=I+D+\cdots+D^m$. The finite [matrix geometric series](../../../../../matrix-geometric-series.md) telescopes to $(I-D)S_m=I-D^{m+1}$. By the assumed convergence, its terms $D^{m+1}$ tend to zero, so the [limit](../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md) $S$ satisfies $(I-D)S=I$. Consequently $S=(I-D)^{-1}$. This is the finite-dimensional [Neumann series](../../../../../neumann-series.md) argument; invertibility by itself would not imply convergence.

For the stated [nilpotent matrix](../../../../../nilpotent-matrix.md), $D^3=0$ and only the first three terms remain:

$$
\boxed{(I-D)^{-1}=I+D+D^2=\begin{pmatrix}1&1&1\\0&1&1\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix}.}
$$
