<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a [matrix proportional to its transpose](../../../../../matrix-proportional-to-its-transpose.md), applying the [transpose](../../../../../transpose.md) twice gives $A^T=5A$ and therefore $A=25A$. Over the [real numbers](../../../../../real-number.md) or [complex numbers](../../../../../complex-number.md), $24A=0$ implies

$$
\boxed{A=0.}
$$
