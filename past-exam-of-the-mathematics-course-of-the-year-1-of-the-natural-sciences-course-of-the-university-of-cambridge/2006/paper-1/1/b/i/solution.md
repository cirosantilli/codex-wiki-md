↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [symmetric matrix](../../../../../../symmetric-matrix.md) that is also an [orthogonal matrix](../../../../../../orthogonal-matrix.md) satisfies $C=C^{-1}$. Multiplying by the commuting invertible factors gives $(I-B)^2=(I+B)^2$, hence $4B=0$. Thus **there is no nonzero choice of $B$**. The only symmetric case is $B=0$, giving $C=I$.
