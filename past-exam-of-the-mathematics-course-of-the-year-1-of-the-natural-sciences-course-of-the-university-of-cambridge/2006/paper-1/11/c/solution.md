<h1 id="11/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

At $x=0$, the [Fourier series](../../../../../fourier-series-split.md) equals $f(0)=0$. Isolating the first term gives

$$
0=\frac13+\sum_{m=1}^\infty\frac1{4-(2m+1)^2}.
$$

Changing the sign of the remaining denominators proves

$$
\boxed{\sum_{m=1}^\infty\frac1{(2m+1)^2-4}=\frac13.}
$$

The [absolute convergence](../../../../../absolute-convergence.md) justifies the evaluation and rearrangement.
