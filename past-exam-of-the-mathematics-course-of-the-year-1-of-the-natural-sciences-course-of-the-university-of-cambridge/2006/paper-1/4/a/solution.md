<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a sufficiently smooth [vector field](../../../../../vector-field.md) on an oriented piecewise-smooth [surface](../../../../../topological-surface-split.md) $S$ with boundary $\partial S$, [Stokes theorem](../../../../../stokes-theorem.md) states

$$
\oint_{\partial S}\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r=\iint_S(\nabla\times\mathbf F)\cdot\mathbf n\,dS.
$$

Here $\mathbf n$ is the chosen [unit normal](../../../../../unit-normal.md), $dS$ is the scalar surface-area element, and $d\mathbf r$ follows the positively oriented boundary. The [boundary orientation](../../../../../boundary-orientation.md) is fixed by the right-hand rule: the fingers curl along $\partial S$ when the thumb points along $\mathbf n$.
