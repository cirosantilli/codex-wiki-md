<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

**No scalar potential exists on the full three-dimensional domain.** A sufficiently smooth [gradient field](../../../../../gradient-field.md) has zero [curl](../../../../../curl.md), since mixed [partial derivatives](../../../../../partial-derivative.md) commute. The calculated [curl](../../../../../curl.md) is not identically zero. The fact that it vanishes at some individual points does not make the whole field a [gradient field](../../../../../gradient-field.md).
