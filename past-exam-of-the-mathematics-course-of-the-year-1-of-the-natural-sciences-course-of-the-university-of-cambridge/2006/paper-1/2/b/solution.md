<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The upper-left block is a planar [rotation matrix](../../../../../rotation-matrix.md). Its real [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) is $(\lambda-\cos\theta)^2+\sin^2\theta$, which is strictly positive for real $\lambda$ because the angle excludes integer multiples of $\pi$. Hence any real [eigenvector](../../../../../eigenvector.md) has $x=y=0$. The remaining equation is $5z=\lambda z$, giving

$$
\boxed{\lambda=5,\qquad (x,y,z)=(0,0,z),\quad z\in\mathbb R\setminus\{0\}.}
$$

There are infinitely many nonzero solutions, spanning **one independent eigenvector direction**.
