<h1 id="2/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) is $\det(\lambda I-M)=\lambda(\lambda^2-2)$. Solving the three [eigenvector](../../../../../../eigenvector.md) equations gives

$$
\boxed{\lambda=\sqrt2,\ 0,\ -\sqrt2;\qquad v_+=(1,\sqrt2,1)^T,\quad v_0=(1,0,-1)^T,\quad v_-=(1,-\sqrt2,1)^T.}
$$

Each [eigenspace](../../../../../../eigenspace.md) consists of the [scalar multiples](../../../../../../scalar-multiple.md) of its displayed nonzero [vector](../../../../../../vector.md). Their pairwise [dot products](../../../../../../dot-product.md) vanish: $v_+\cdot v_-=1-2+1=0$ and $v_\pm\cdot v_0=1-1=0$. This also agrees with the [orthogonal diagonalization of a real symmetric matrix](../../../../../../orthogonal-diagonalization-of-a-real-symmetric-matrix.md).
