↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [Euclidean norms](../../../../../../euclidean-norm.md) are $\|v_\pm\|=2$ and $\|v_0\|=\sqrt2$. With normalized [eigenvectors](../../../../../../eigenvector.md) as rows,

$$
\boxed{R=\begin{pmatrix}1/2&1/\sqrt2&1/2\\1/\sqrt2&0&-1/\sqrt2\\1/2&-1/\sqrt2&1/2\end{pmatrix},\qquad RMR^T=\operatorname{diag}(\sqrt2,0,-\sqrt2).}
$$

The rows form an [orthonormal basis](../../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md), so $RR^T=I$ and $R$ is an [orthogonal matrix](../../../../../../orthogonal-matrix.md). The zero [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) means $Mv_0=0$ for a nonzero [vector](../../../../../../vector.md); thus **$M$ is singular** and has no [matrix inverse](../../../../../../matrix-inverse-split.md).
