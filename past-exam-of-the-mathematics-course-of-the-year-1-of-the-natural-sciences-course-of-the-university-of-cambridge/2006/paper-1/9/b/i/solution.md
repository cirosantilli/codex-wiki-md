<h1 id="9/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Put $h=x-1$. The [Taylor series](../../../../../../taylor-series.md) of the [natural logarithm](../../../../../../natural-logarithm.md) gives the first four nonzero terms:

$$
\boxed{\ln x=h-\frac{h^2}{2}+\frac{h^3}{3}-\frac{h^4}{4}+O(h^5).}
$$

The corresponding infinite series converges for $|h|<1$.
