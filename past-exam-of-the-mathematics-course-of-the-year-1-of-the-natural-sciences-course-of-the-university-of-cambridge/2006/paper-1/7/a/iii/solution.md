<h1 id="7/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The negative-test [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) is $P(-)=1-0.05094=0.94906$. Applying [Bayes' theorem](../../../../../../bayes-theorem.md) again,

$$
\boxed{P(G\mid-)=\frac{P(-\mid G)P(G)}{P(-)}=\frac{0.01(0.001)}{0.94906}=1.05\times10^{-5}\quad\text{(3 s.f.)}.}
$$
