<h1 id="7/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

This is a [two-turn renewal game](../../../../../../two-turn-renewal-game.md). A full pair of throws has probability $(1/2)^2=1/4$ of passing twice and returning to the original state. The probability that $A$ wins before that restart is $5/12$. Thus [first-step analysis](../../../../../../first-step-analysis.md) gives $p=5/12+p/4$, or

$$
\boxed{P(A\text{ wins})=\frac59.}
$$

Equivalently sum the [geometric series](../../../../../../geometric-series.md) $(5/12)\sum_{j\geq0}(1/4)^j$. The probability of surviving infinitely many pairs is zero, so the eventual winner is well defined with probability one.
