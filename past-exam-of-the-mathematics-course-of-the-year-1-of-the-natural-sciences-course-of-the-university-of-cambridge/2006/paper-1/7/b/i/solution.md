<h1 id="7/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $B$ to make a first throw, $A$ must first pass, with [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) $1/2$. For $B$ then to win on that throw, $B$ must roll one of two winning faces, with probability $1/3$. The throws are [independent](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md), giving

$$
\boxed{P=\frac12\frac13=\frac16.}
$$

A first throw of $3$ by $A$ would make $B$ win without throwing, and is excluded by the requested event.
