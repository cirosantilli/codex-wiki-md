<h1 id="5/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Put $S=a^{-2}+b^{-2}+c^{-2}$. The [orthogonal projection onto an intercept plane](../../../../../orthogonal-projection-onto-an-intercept-plane.md) is $\mathbf d=\mathbf n/S$. Comparing its coordinates with $(\lambda a,\mu b,\nu c)$ gives

$$
\boxed{\lambda=\frac{a^{-2}}S,\qquad\mu=\frac{b^{-2}}S,\qquad\nu=\frac{c^{-2}}S.}
$$

Their sum is one, as the [plane equation](../../../../../cartesian-equation-of-a-plane.md) requires.
