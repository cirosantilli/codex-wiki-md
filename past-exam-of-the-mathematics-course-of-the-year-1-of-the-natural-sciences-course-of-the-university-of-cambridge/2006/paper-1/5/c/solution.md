↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [distance from a point to a plane](../../../../../distance-from-a-point-to-a-plane.md), applied at the origin, is the plane constant divided by the [Euclidean norm](../../../../../euclidean-norm.md) of its [normal vector](../../../../../normal-vector.md). Therefore

$$
\boxed{|OD|=\frac1{\sqrt{a^{-2}+b^{-2}+c^{-2}}}.}
$$

The [closest point on a plane](../../../../../closest-point-on-a-plane.md) lies on the normal through the origin; this is an [orthogonal projection onto an intercept plane](../../../../../orthogonal-projection-onto-an-intercept-plane.md).
