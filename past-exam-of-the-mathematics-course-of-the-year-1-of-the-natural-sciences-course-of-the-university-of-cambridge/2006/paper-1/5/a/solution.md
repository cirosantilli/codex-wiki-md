↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The coordinate intercepts suggest the [normal vector](../../../../../normal-vector.md)

$$
\boxed{\mathbf n=(1/a,1/b,1/c).}
$$

Indeed $\mathbf n\cdot\mathbf a=\mathbf n\cdot\mathbf b=\mathbf n\cdot\mathbf c=1$, so the two independent edge [vectors](../../../../../vector.md) in the plane are both [orthogonal](../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) to $\mathbf n$.
