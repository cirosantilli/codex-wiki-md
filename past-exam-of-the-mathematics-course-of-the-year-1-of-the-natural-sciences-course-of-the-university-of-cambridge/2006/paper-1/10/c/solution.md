<h1 id="10/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The differential operator is $(D-1)^2$, where $D=d/dx$. Set $y=e^xv$; the [product rule](../../../../../product-rule.md) reduces the equation to $v''=1+x$. Twice integrating gives $v=x^2/2+x^3/6+Ax+B$. The initial conditions yield $B=A=0$, so

$$
\boxed{y(x)=e^x\left(\frac{x^2}{2}+\frac{x^3}{6}\right).}
$$

This substitution removes the repeated characteristic root before finding a [particular solution](../../../../../particular-solution.md).
