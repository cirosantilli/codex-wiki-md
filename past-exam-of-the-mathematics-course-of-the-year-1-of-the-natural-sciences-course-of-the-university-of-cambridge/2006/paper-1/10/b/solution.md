<h1 id="10/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use $\cos^2x=(1+\cos2x)/2$. The constant forcing has [particular solution](../../../../../particular-solution.md) $1/8$, while the $\cos2x$ term satisfies the [resonance condition](../../../../../resonance-condition.md) and needs the trial $Cx\sin2x$. Since $(d^2/dx^2+4)(x\sin2x)=4\cos2x$, the forced term is $x\sin2x/8$. Adding the [homogeneous solution](../../../../../homogeneous-solution.md) and imposing the initial conditions gives

$$
\boxed{y(x)=\frac{1-\cos2x+x\sin2x}{8}.}
$$
