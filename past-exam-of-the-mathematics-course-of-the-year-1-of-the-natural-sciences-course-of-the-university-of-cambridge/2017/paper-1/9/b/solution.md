<h1 id="9/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [initial condition](../../../../../initial-condition.md) gives $1=1/(C-1)$, hence $C=2$. Thus

$$
\boxed{y(x)=\frac1{2-x},\qquad x<2.}
$$

The displayed interval is the maximal connected interval containing the initial point $x=1$; the [solution of a differential equation](../../../../../solution-of-a-differential-equation.md) diverges at $x=2$.
