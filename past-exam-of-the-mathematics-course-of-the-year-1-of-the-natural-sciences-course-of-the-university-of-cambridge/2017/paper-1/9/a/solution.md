<h1 id="9/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For $y\ne0$, the [separable differential equation](../../../../../separable-differential-equation.md) gives $dy/y^2=dx$. Integrating gives $-1/y=x+C_0$, or

$$
\boxed{y(x)=\frac1{C-x},\qquad x\ne C.}
$$

Each solution is considered on an interval not containing its pole. The additional [stationary solution](../../../../../stationary-solution.md) $y=0$ is excluded by the stated condition.
