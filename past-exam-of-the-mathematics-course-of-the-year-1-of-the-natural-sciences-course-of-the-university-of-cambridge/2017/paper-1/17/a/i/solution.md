<h1 id="17/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use [integration by substitution](../../../../../../integration-by-substitution.md) with $u=\ln x$, so $du=dx/x$. For $x>1$, also $u>0$, and

$$
\int\frac{dx}{x\ln x}=\int\frac{du}{u}=\boxed{\ln(\ln x)+C}.
$$

The domain condition explains why no absolute value is needed around the inner logarithm.
