↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The function $g(x)=1/(x\ln^2x)$ is likewise positive and decreasing on $[2,\infty)$, with $g'(x)=-(\ln x+2)/(x^2\ln^3x)<0$. Now

$$
\int_2^\infty\frac{dx}{x\ln^2x}
=\left[-\frac1{\ln x}\right]_2^\infty=\frac1{\ln2}<\infty.
$$

The [integral test for convergence](../../../../../../integral-test-for-convergence.md) shows that **$\displaystyle\sum_{n=2}^{\infty}1/(n\ln^2n)$ converges**. This comparison [integral](../../../../../../integral-split.md) determines convergence, not the numerical value of the series itself.
