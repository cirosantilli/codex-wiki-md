<h1 id="7/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Put $\theta=\pi/12$. The double-angle [trigonometric identities](../../../../../trigonometric-identity.md) imply $\cos^2(2\theta)=1-\sin^2(2\theta)$. Since $2\theta=\pi/6$ is in the first quadrant, the positive [square root](../../../../../square-root.md) is required. The [half-angle identities](../../../../../half-angle-identities.md) then give the requested expression in terms of $\sin(\pi/6)$:

$$
\boxed{\sin^2\frac\pi{12}=\frac{1-\sqrt{1-\sin^2(\pi/6)}}2.}
$$
