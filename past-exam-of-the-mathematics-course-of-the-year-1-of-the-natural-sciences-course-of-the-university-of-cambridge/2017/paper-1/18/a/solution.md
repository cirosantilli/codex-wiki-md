<h1 id="18/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A row's squared [Euclidean norm](../../../../../euclidean-norm.md) is $[(1+\sqrt3)^2+1+(1-\sqrt3)^2]/6=3/2$, and the [dot product](../../../../../dot-product.md) of the two rows is $[2(1-3)+1]/6=-1/2$. Thus the row [Gram matrix](../../../../../gram-matrix.md) is

$$
\boxed{AA^{\mathsf T}=\begin{pmatrix}3/2&-1/2\\-1/2&3/2\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

For the column [Gram matrix](../../../../../gram-matrix.md), the first and third squared column norms are $4/3$, the middle one is $1/3$, and the respective cross-products are $1/3,-2/3,1/3$. Hence

$$
\boxed{A^{\mathsf T}A=\frac13\begin{pmatrix}4&1&-2\\1&1&1\\-2&1&4\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

The two products are square [matrices](../../../../../matrix.md) of different sizes; their unequal dimensions are consistent with $A$ being rectangular.
