<h1 id="18/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) with equal diagonal entries, the symmetric and antisymmetric directions $(1,1)$ and $(1,-1)$ are [eigenvectors](../../../../../eigenvector.md). Direct multiplication of $AA^{\mathsf T}$ gives [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $3/2-1/2=1$ and $3/2+1/2=2$. Dividing each direction by its [Euclidean norm](../../../../../euclidean-norm.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\lambda_1=1,\quad \mathbf u_1=\frac1{\sqrt2}\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\end{pmatrix};\qquad
\lambda_2=2,\quad \mathbf u_2=\frac1{\sqrt2}\begin{pmatrix}1\\-1\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

The two normalized [eigenvectors](../../../../../eigenvector.md) are [orthogonal](../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md); either may independently be replaced by its negative.
