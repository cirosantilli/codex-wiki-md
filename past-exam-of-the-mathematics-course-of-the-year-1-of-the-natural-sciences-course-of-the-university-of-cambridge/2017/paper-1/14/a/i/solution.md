<h1 id="14/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

An interior [stationary point](../../../../../../stationary-point.md) has vanishing [gradient](../../../../../../gradient.md):

$$
\boxed{\nabla f(x_0,y_0)=\mathbf0,\qquad f_x(x_0,y_0)=f_y(x_0,y_0)=0.}
$$

For a differentiable [function](../../../../../../function-split.md), this is the necessary first-order condition for an interior [local minimum](../../../../../../local-minimum.md) or [local maximum](../../../../../../local-maximum.md).
