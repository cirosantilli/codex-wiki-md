<h1 id="14/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For $a=-1$, the [local minimum](../../../../../../local-minimum.md) at the origin has $f=1$. Its full [level set](../../../../../../level-set.md) is

$$
\boxed{y^2=\frac{x^4}{2}-x^2.}
$$

The connected component containing the minimum is just the isolated origin. The same level also has the outer branches $y=\pm\sqrt{x^4/2-x^2}$ for $|x|\geq\sqrt2$; these are different connected components, not curves approaching the origin.

Both [saddle points](../../../../../../saddle-point.md) $(\pm1,0)$ have $f=e^{1/4}$. Their common [level set](../../../../../../level-set.md) factors as

$$
y^2=\frac{x^4}{2}-x^2+\frac12=\frac{(x^2-1)^2}{2}.
$$

Its explicit equations are therefore

$$
\boxed{y=\frac{x^2-1}{\sqrt2}\quad\text{or}\quad y=-\frac{x^2-1}{\sqrt2}.}
$$

The two [parabolas](../../../../../../parabola.md) cross at each saddle, explaining the change of contour connectivity there. Each displayed equation can be restricted to the square of part (ii) if only that sketch window is wanted.
