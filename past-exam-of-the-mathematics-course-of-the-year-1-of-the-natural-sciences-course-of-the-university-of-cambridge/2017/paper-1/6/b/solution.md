<h1 id="6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [function graph](../../../../../graph-of-a-function.md) is a horizontally shifted and scaled fourth power. It is nonnegative and touches the horizontal axis at its fourfold [polynomial root](../../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md). The axis intersections are

$$
\boxed{(-1/2,0)\quad\text{and}\quad(0,1).}
$$

The [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) is $8(1+2x)^3$, so the graph decreases to its flat [local minimum](../../../../../local-minimum.md) at $x=-1/2$ and then increases. Its endpoint values on the requested interval are $f(-1)=1$ and $f(1)=81$. The original sketch includes a closer view of the minimum so that the small values are visible beside the much larger right endpoint.
