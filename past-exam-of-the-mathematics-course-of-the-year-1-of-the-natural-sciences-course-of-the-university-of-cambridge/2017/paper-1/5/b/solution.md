<h1 id="5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

By the [factor theorem](../../../../../factor-theorem.md), $x-2$ divides the [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md). Division gives $2x^2+7x+3=(2x+1)(x+3)$, so

$$
\boxed{2x^3+3x^2-11x-6=(x-2)(2x+1)(x+3).}
$$

Its three [polynomial roots](../../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md) are $2,-1/2,-3$.
