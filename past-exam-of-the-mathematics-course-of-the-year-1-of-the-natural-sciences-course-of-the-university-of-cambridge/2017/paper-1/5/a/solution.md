<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Writing the [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) as $p(x)$, direct evaluation gives $p(0)=-6$ and $p(3)=42$. A [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) is continuous, so the [intermediate value theorem](../../../../../intermediate-value-theorem.md) guarantees a [polynomial root](../../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md) between them. Trying the integer $2$ gives $16+12-22-6=0$, hence **$x=2$ is a root**.
