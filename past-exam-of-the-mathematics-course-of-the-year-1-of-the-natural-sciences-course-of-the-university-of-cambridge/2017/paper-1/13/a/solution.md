<h1 id="13/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The homogeneous [characteristic equation](../../../../../characteristic-equation-of-a-constant-coefficient-differential-equation.md) is $r^2+2r=r(r+2)=0$, giving the [complementary solution](../../../../../homogeneous-solution.md) $C_1+C_2e^{-2x}$. For a [particular solution](../../../../../particular-solution.md), try $Ax^2+Bx$. Substitution gives $2A+4Ax+2B=4x$, so $A=1$ and $B=-1$. Therefore the [general solution](../../../../../general-solution.md) is

$$
\boxed{y=x^2-x+C_1+C_2e^{-2x}.}
$$

As a direct check, the [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) part contributes $2+2(2x-1)=4x$, while both complementary terms contribute zero.
