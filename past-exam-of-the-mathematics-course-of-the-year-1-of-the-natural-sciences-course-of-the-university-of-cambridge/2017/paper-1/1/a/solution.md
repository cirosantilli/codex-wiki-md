<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

On the negative half-line, an [antiderivative](../../../../../antiderivative.md) of $1/x$ is $\ln|x|$. The [fundamental theorem of calculus](../../../../../fundamental-theorem-of-calculus.md) therefore gives

$$
\int_{-2}^{-1}\frac{dx}{x}=\big[\ln|x|\big]_{-2}^{-1}=\ln1-\ln2=\boxed{-\ln2}.
$$

The negative sign agrees with the negative integrand throughout the interval.
