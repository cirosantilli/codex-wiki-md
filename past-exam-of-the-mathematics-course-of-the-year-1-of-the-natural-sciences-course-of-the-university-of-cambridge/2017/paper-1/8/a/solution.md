<h1 id="8/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The double-angle [trigonometric identity](../../../../../trigonometric-identity.md) gives $\cos x=2\sin x\cos x$, or $\cos x(1-2\sin x)=0$. Preserve both factors: $\cos x=0$ contributes the endpoint, while $\sin x=1/2$ contributes the interior solution. In the specified interval,

$$
\boxed{x=\frac\pi6,\quad\frac\pi2.}
$$
