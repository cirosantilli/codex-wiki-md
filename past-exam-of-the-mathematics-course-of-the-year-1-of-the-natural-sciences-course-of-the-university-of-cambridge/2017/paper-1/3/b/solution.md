<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Differentiate each coordinate with respect to the parameter $t$ and use the [chain rule](../../../../../chain-rule.md):

$$
\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{dy/dt}{dx/dt}
=\frac{\sec^2t}{-\csc^2t}=\boxed{-\tan^2t}.
$$

Both coordinates are defined where $\sin t\ne0$ and $\cos t\ne0$. As a check, $xy=1$ there, so the same [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) is $-1/x^2$.
