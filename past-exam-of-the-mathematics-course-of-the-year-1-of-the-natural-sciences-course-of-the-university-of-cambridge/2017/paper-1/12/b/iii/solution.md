<h1 id="12/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The nonzero factor $ab/D$ can be cancelled in the local [differential equation](../../../../../../differential-equation-split.md), leaving $x\,dy-y\,dx=0$. On an interval with $x\ne0$,

$$
\frac{d}{dx}\left(\frac yx\right)=\frac{xy'-y}{x^2}=0.
$$

Hence the explicit [general solution](../../../../../../general-solution.md) is

$$
\boxed{y(x)=Cx,\qquad C\in\mathbb R,}
$$

on any interval that avoids the excluded origin. It also follows from fixing the local angle potential, with slope $C=(a/b)\tan(f-C_0)$. As parameterized curves there are also vertical rays $x=0$, which cannot be represented as single-valued functions $y(x)$. Geometrically the connected [integral curves](../../../../../../integral-curve-of-a-vector-field.md) are rays, not lines continued through the undefined origin.
