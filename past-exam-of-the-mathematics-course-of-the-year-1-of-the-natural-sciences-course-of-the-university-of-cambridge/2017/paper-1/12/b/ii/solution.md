<h1 id="12/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

On a chart with $x\ne0$, set

$$
\boxed{f(x,y)=\arctan\!\left(\frac{by}{ax}\right)+C.}
$$

The [chain rule](../../../../../../chain-rule.md) verifies both components:

$$
f_x=\frac1{1+(by/(ax))^2}\left(-\frac{by}{ax^2}\right)
=-\frac{aby}{D}=P,\qquad
f_y=\frac1{1+(by/(ax))^2}\frac{b}{ax}
=\frac{abx}{D}=Q.
$$

A coordinate-independent description of the local potential is a continuous branch of $\arg(ax+iby)$, the [complex argument](../../../../../../argument-complex-analysis.md) of the scaled coordinates. Such branches can also cover points where $x=0$, using a different angular chart. Continuing once around the missing origin changes that angle by a nonzero multiple of $2\pi$, so **there is no global single-valued $f$ on the full stated domain**.
