<h1 id="11/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Set $w=e^z\ne0$. The [hyperbolic cosine](../../../../../hyperbolic-cosine.md) equation becomes $w+w^{-1}=-4$, hence $w^2+4w+1=0$. Its two [polynomial roots](../../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md) are $w=-2\pm\sqrt3$, both negative. Taking every value of the [complex logarithm](../../../../../complex-logarithm.md) yields

$$
z=\ln|w|+i(2k+1)\pi,\qquad k\in\mathbb Z.
$$

Since $(2-\sqrt3)(2+\sqrt3)=1$, their real logarithms have opposite signs. All solutions are therefore

$$
\boxed{z=\pm\ln(2+\sqrt3)+i(2k+1)\pi,\qquad k\in\mathbb Z.}
$$

The positive real constant is also $\operatorname{arcosh}2$, the [inverse hyperbolic cosine](../../../../../inverse-hyperbolic-cosine.md) of $2$. Substitution gives $\cosh z=-\cosh(\ln(2+\sqrt3))=-2$ for both signs and every integer $k$.
