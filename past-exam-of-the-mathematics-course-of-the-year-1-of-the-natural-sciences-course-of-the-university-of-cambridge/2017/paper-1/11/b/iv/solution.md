<h1 id="11/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Expand the defining [complex exponential functions](../../../../../../complex-exponential-function.md):

$$
\cosh(x+iy)=\frac{e^{x+iy}+e^{-x-iy}}2
=\cosh x\cos y+i\sinh x\sin y.
$$

Thus the [real part](../../../../../../real-part.md) and [imaginary part](../../../../../../imaginary-part.md) of the [hyperbolic cosine](../../../../../../hyperbolic-cosine.md) are

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Re}\cosh z=\cosh x\cos y,\qquad
\operatorname{Im}\cosh z=\sinh x\sin y.}
$$
