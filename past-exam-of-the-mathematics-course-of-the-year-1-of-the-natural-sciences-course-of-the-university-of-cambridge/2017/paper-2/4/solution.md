<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

Use the [Taylor series](../../../../taylor-series.md) $\cos x=1-x^2/2+O(x^4)$. Squaring gives $\cos^2x=1-x^2+O(x^4)$, and hence

$$
\boxed{x^3\cos^2x=x^3-x^5+O(x^7)}.
$$

The first two nonzero terms are therefore $x^3$ and $-x^5$.
