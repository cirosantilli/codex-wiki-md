<h1 id="7/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a [particular integral](../../../../../particular-solution.md), try $y_p=A\cos4x$. Substitution gives $(-16+9)A\cos4x=-7\cos4x$, so $A=1$. Hence

$$
\boxed{y_p=\cos4x}.
$$

The forcing frequency differs from the homogeneous frequency, so no resonant factor of $x$ is needed.
