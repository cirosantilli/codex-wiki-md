↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The homogeneous [characteristic equation](../../../../../characteristic-equation-of-a-constant-coefficient-differential-equation.md) is $r^2+9=0$, with roots $r=\pm3i$. Its real [complementary function](../../../../../homogeneous-solution.md) is

$$
\boxed{y_c=C_1\cos3x+C_2\sin3x}.
$$
