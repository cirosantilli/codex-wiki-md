<h1 id="14/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The zero-goal term contributes nothing to the [expected value](../../../../../../expected-value-split.md). Shift the summation index in the remaining terms:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mu&=e^{-\lambda}\sum_{k=1}^\infty k\frac{\lambda^k}{k!}
=\lambda e^{-\lambda}\sum_{k=1}^\infty\frac{\lambda^{k-1}}{(k-1)!}\\
&=\lambda e^{-\lambda}e^\lambda=\boxed{\lambda}.
\end{aligned}
$$

The exponential series justifies the convergent reindexing. Thus the parameter is the [mean](../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) goals per game.
