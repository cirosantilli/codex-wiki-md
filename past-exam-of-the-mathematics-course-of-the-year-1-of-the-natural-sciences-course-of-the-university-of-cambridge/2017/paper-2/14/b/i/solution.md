<h1 id="14/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Normalization of the [probability mass function](../../../../../../probability-mass-function.md) requires

$$
1=A\sum_{k=0}^\infty\frac{\lambda^k}{k!}=Ae^\lambda.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{A=e^{-\lambda}}.
$$

The resulting law is the [Poisson distribution](../../../../../../poisson-distribution.md) with parameter $\lambda$.
