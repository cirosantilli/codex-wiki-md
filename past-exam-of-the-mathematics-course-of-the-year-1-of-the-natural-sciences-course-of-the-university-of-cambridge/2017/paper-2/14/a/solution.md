<h1 id="14/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write $P_k=P(X=k)$ for $k=0,1,2,\ldots$, with $\sum_kP_k=1$. Provided the relevant moments exist, the [expected value](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) and [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) are

$$
\boxed{\mu=\sum_{k=0}^\infty kP_k,\qquad
\sigma^2=\sum_{k=0}^\infty(k-\mu)^2P_k
=\sum_{k=0}^\infty k^2P_k-\mu^2}.
$$

The displayed support includes zero, so these are nonnegative integer values.
