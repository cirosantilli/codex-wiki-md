↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The intended season calculation needs the additional assumption that scores in different games are [independent random variables](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md). The printed statement gives their common one-game distribution but does not explicitly state [independence](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md); identical marginals alone do not determine season probabilities.

Under [independence](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md), each game has [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) $1-P(X=0)=1-e^{-\lambda}$ of at least one goal, so

$$
\boxed{P(\text{at least one goal in every game})=(1-e^{-\lambda})^{10}}.
$$

Without [independence](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) this product is not determined by the supplied marginal distribution.
