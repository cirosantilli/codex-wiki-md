<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [complex exponential](../../../../../complex-exponential-function.md) satisfies $e^{iz}=e^{-y}(\cos x+i\sin x)$. Multiplying by $x+iy$ gives

$$
f(z)=e^{-y}\big[(x\cos x-y\sin x)+i(x\sin x+y\cos x)\big].
$$

Thus its [real part](../../../../../real-part.md) is

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Re}f=e^{-y}(x\cos x-y\sin x)}.
$$
