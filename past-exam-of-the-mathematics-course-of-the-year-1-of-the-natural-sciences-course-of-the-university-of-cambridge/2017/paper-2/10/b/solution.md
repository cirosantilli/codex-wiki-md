<h1 id="10/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

By [Bayes' theorem](../../../../../bayes-theorem.md), provided $p+q>0$,

$$
\boxed{P(M\mid S)=\frac{P(S\mid M)P(M)}{P(S)}=\frac{p}{p+q}}.
$$

If $p=q=0$, singing has [probability](../../../../../probability.md) zero and the stated observation cannot occur in this model; that [conditional probability](../../../../../conditional-probability.md) is undefined.
