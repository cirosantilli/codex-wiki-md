<h1 id="13/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Differentiate the components directly:

$$
\begin{aligned}
(\nabla\times\mathbf F)_x&=\partial_y(-3xz^2-1)-\partial_z(x^2-2y)=0,\\
(\nabla\times\mathbf F)_y&=\partial_z(2xy-z^3)-\partial_x(-3xz^2-1)=-3z^2+3z^2=0,\\
(\nabla\times\mathbf F)_z&=\partial_x(x^2-2y)-\partial_y(2xy-z^3)=2x-2x=0.
\end{aligned}
$$

Thus $\boxed{\nabla\times\mathbf F=0}$. The field is smooth on all of $\mathbb R^3$, a [simply connected domain](../../../../../simply-connected-domain.md), so the zero-curl condition is sufficient for a [conservative vector field](../../../../../conservative-vector-field.md). The domain condition matters: zero [curl](../../../../../curl.md) alone need not imply a global potential on a region with holes.
