<h1 id="13/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

For $\mathbf r(t)=(t,t,t^2)$, the tangent [vector](../../../../../vector.md) is $\mathbf r'(t)=(1,1,2t)$. Substitute into the field:

$$
\mathbf F(\mathbf r(t))=(2t^2-t^6,\ t^2-2t,\ -3t^5-1).
$$

Its [scalar product](../../../../../dot-product.md) with the tangent is $3t^2-4t-7t^6$. Hence the explicitly parametrized [line integral](../../../../../line-integral.md) is

$$
\boxed{\int_C\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r
=\int_1^2(3t^2-4t-7t^6)\,dt
=[t^3-2t^2-t^7]_1^2=-126}.
$$

As a check, the potential from part (d) has values $-128+C$ at $(2,2,4)$ and $-2+C$ at $(1,1,1)$, giving the same difference.
