<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For $\lambda=a$, the components of an [eigenvector](../../../../../eigenvector.md) are equal; for $\lambda=-a$, they are opposite. Dividing each [vector](../../../../../vector.md) by its [Euclidean norm](../../../../../euclidean-norm.md) gives

$$
\boxed{u_+=\frac1{\sqrt2}\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\end{pmatrix}\quad(\lambda=a),\qquad
u_-=\frac1{\sqrt2}\begin{pmatrix}1\\-1\end{pmatrix}\quad(\lambda=-a)}.
$$

They are mutually [orthogonal](../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) and have unit norm. The labels specify their [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) even when $a<0$.
