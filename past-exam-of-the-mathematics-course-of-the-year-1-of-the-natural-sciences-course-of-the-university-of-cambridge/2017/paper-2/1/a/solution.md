<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The direction [vectors](../../../../../vector.md) are $v=(-1,-1,1)$ and $w=(1,1,2)$. Their [scalar product](../../../../../dot-product.md) is $v\cdot w=-1-1+2=0$. Both are nonzero, so the lines are perpendicular:

$$
\boxed{\theta=\frac\pi2=90^\circ}.
$$
