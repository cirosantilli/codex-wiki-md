<h1 id="8/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The specified unit [normal vector](../../../../../normal-vector.md) is $\mathbf e_z$, while $F_z=0$ everywhere. Thus the normal component of the field vanishes pointwise, giving [flux](../../../../../flux.md)

$$
\boxed{\int_S\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf S=0}.
$$
