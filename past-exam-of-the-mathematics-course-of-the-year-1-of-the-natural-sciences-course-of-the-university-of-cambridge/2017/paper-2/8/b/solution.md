Here $y=0$, the unit [normal vector](../../../../../normal-vector.md) is $\mathbf e_y$, and $\mathbf F\cdot\mathbf e_y=x^2$. The square has $-1/2\leq x,z\leq1/2$, so its outward [flux](../../../../../flux.md) is

$$
\boxed{\int_{-1/2}^{1/2}\int_{-1/2}^{1/2}x^2\,dx\,dz=\frac1{12}}.
$$
