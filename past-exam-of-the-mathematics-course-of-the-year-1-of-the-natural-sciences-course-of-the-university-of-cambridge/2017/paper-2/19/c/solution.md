<h1 id="19/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The ratio of the two maximal volumes is

$$
\boxed{\frac{V_{c,\max}}{V_{p,\max}}=\frac\pi2>1}.
$$

Thus **the [cylinder](../../../../../cylinder-geometry.md) has the larger [maximum](../../../../../maximum-of-a-subset-of-a-total-order.md) [volume](../../../../../volume.md)**, by a factor $\pi/2$. Both optimal shapes have height $H/2$, while their horizontal cross-sections have areas $\pi R^2/2$ and $R^2$, respectively.
