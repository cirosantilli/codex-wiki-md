<h1 id="19/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use the diagram's labels: $a,b$ are horizontal side lengths and $c$ is the height of the [rectangular parallelepiped](../../../../../rectangular-parallelepiped.md). Its four top vertices are at horizontal distance $\sqrt{a^2+b^2}/2$ from the axis. Thus the contact [constraint](../../../../../constraint-mechanics.md) and [volume](../../../../../volume.md) are

$$
c+\frac{H(a^2+b^2)}{4R^2}=H,\qquad V_p=abc.
$$

Apply a [Lagrange multiplier](../../../../../lagrange-multiplier.md):

$$
\mathcal L=abc-\lambda\left(c+\frac{H(a^2+b^2)}{4R^2}-H\right).
$$

For positive dimensions, the [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) equations are

$$
bc=\lambda\frac{Ha}{2R^2},\qquad
ac=\lambda\frac{Hb}{2R^2},\qquad ab=\lambda.
$$

Substituting the last equation into the first two gives

$$
c=\frac{Ha^2}{2R^2}=\frac{Hb^2}{2R^2}.
$$

Hence $a=b$, and the [constraint](../../../../../constraint-mechanics.md) becomes $2c=H$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{a=b=R,\qquad c=\frac H2,\qquad V_{p,\max}=\frac{R^2H}{2}}.
$$

These are the [maximal cylinder and box in an elliptic paraboloid](../../../../../maximal-cylinder-and-box-in-an-elliptic-paraboloid.md) dimensions for the rectangular case. A horizontal rotation of the square footprint gives the same [volume](../../../../../volume.md) by rotational symmetry.
