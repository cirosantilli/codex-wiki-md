<h1 id="9/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For real coordinates, work on $y>0$. The [chain rule](../../../../../chain-rule.md) gives $\xi_y=y^{-1/2}$ and $\xi_{yy}=-\tfrac12y^{-3/2}$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial y^2}
=u''(\xi)(\xi_y)^2+u'(\xi)\xi_{yy}
=\frac{u''(\xi)}y-\frac{u'(\xi)}{2y^{3/2}}}.
$$

The [derivatives](../../../../../derivative.md) on the right are with respect to $\xi$. Behaviour at $y=0$ requires separate regularity of the composed [function](../../../../../function-split.md).
