<h1 id="18/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For this odd [function](../../../../../function-split.md), the [Parseval identity](../../../../../parseval-identity.md) from part (a) gives

$$
\frac1\pi\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}(x^3-\pi^2x)^2\,dx
=144\sum_{n=1}^\infty\frac1{n^6}.
$$

The integrand is even. Expanding and integrating it gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac2\pi\int_0^\pi(x^6-2\pi^2x^4+\pi^4x^2)\,dx
&=2\pi^6\left(\frac17-\frac25+\frac13\right)\\
&=\frac{16\pi^6}{105}.
\end{aligned}
$$

Dividing by $144$ therefore proves

$$
\boxed{\sum_{n=1}^\infty\frac1{n^6}=\frac{\pi^6}{945}}.
$$

This evaluates the [Riemann zeta function](../../../../../riemann-zeta-function.md) at six by using the norm of a simple [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md).
