<h1 id="15/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Write the differential form as $M\,dx+N\,dy$, with the coefficients as printed. A [monomial integrating factor](../../../../../../monomial-integrating-factor.md) can be found from $\mu=x^\alpha y^\beta$. The exactness condition $\partial_y(\mu M)=\partial_x(\mu N)$ gives

$$
2(\beta+2)=-(\alpha+2),\qquad -(\beta+1)=2(\alpha+1).
$$

Solving yields $\alpha=0,\beta=-3$. Thus, on $y\ne0$,

$$
\boxed{\mu(y)=y^{-3}}.
$$

After multiplication, the differential form is

$$
\left(\frac{2x}{y}-\frac1{y^2}\right)dx+
\left(\frac{2x}{y^3}-\frac{x^2}{y^2}\right)dy
=d\left(\frac{x^2}{y}-\frac{x}{y^2}\right).
$$

Hence the implicit solutions are

$$
\boxed{\frac{x^2}{y}-\frac{x}{y^2}=C\quad(y\ne0)}.
$$

The branch $\boxed{y=0}$ was excluded by the [integrating factor](../../../../../../integrating-factor.md) but satisfies the original equation and must be restored. On any graph branch with $y\ne0$, differentiation of the boxed [first integral](../../../../../../first-integral.md) recovers the original [differential equation](../../../../../../differential-equation-split.md).
