<h1 id="17/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

At a [steady state](../../../../../steady-state.md), $K\mathbf y_0=\mathbf f_0$. All [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) of $K$ are nonzero, so it is invertible and

$$
\boxed{\mathbf y_0=K^{-1}\mathbf f_0,\qquad
K^{-1}=\frac14\begin{pmatrix}3&1&0\\1&3&0\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix}}.
$$

For the specified constant source this gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbf y_0=(1,1,1/4)^T}.
$$

Indeed the first two rows of $K\mathbf y_0$ each give one, and the third gives $4(1/4)=1$.
