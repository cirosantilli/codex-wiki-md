<h1 id="17/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Take the switch-off time to be $t=0$. The state remains [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md) through the source step, so $\mathbf y(0)=(1,1,1/4)^T$. Its eigendirection decomposition is

$$
\mathbf y(0)=(1,1,0)^T+\frac14(0,0,1)^T.
$$

The antisymmetric mode has zero amplitude because the first two initial components are equal. Applying the decay factors from part (b),

$$
\boxed{\mathbf y(t)=(e^{-t},\ e^{-t},\ \tfrac14e^{-4t})^T\qquad(t\geq0)}.
$$

The first two responses decay at rate one and the third at rate four, approaching the new zero-source [steady state](../../../../../steady-state.md).
