<h1 id="17/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

First divide each of the first two equations by two. Then

$$
\boxed{K=\begin{pmatrix}3/2&-1/2&0\\-1/2&3/2&0\\0&0&4\end{pmatrix}}.
$$

The equal-component and opposite-component directions diagonalize the upper block, while the third coordinate is independent. Direct multiplication gives the [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) and unit [eigenvectors](../../../../../eigenvector.md)

$$
\boxed{\begin{array}{c|c}
\gamma&\text{normalized eigenvector}\\\hline
1&\frac1{\sqrt2}(1,1,0)^T\\
2&\frac1{\sqrt2}(1,-1,0)^T\\
4&(0,0,1)^T
\end{array}}.
$$

They form an [orthonormal eigenbasis](../../../../../orthonormal-eigenbasis.md) because $K$ is a real [symmetric matrix](../../../../../symmetric-matrix.md).
