<h1 id="16/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The two independently computed answers agree:

$$
\boxed{\oint_S\mathbf F\cdot\mathbf n\,dS
=\int_V\nabla\cdot\mathbf F\,dV=-\frac{44}{3}}.
$$

This verifies the [divergence theorem](../../../../../../divergence-theorem.md). Its hypotheses hold because the field is smooth and the prism has a closed piecewise smooth boundary with consistently outward normals.
