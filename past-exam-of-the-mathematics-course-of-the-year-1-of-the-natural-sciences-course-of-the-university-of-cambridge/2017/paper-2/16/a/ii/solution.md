↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The Cartesian [curl](../../../../../../curl.md) is

$$
\boxed{\nabla\times\mathbf F=
\left(\partial_yF_z-\partial_zF_y,\ \partial_zF_x-\partial_xF_z,\ \partial_xF_y-\partial_yF_x\right)}.
$$

Each component measures circulation in the [plane](../../../../../../plane.md) perpendicular to its corresponding coordinate axis.
