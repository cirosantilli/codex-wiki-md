<h1 id="6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

From the factorisation in part (a), $x=0$ or $x=1$. Substitution into the [parabola](../../../../../parabola.md) equation gives respectively $y=1$ or $y=0$. Thus the two intersections of the [parabola](../../../../../parabola.md) and the [unit circle](../../../../../complex-unit-circle.md) are

$$
\boxed{(x,y)=(0,1)\text{ and }(1,0).}
$$
