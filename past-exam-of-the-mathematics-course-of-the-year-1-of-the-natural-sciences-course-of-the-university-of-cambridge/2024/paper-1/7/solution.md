<h1 id="7/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [7](../7.md)

Since $\cos x>0$ throughout the given [interval](../../../../interval-mathematics.md), multiplying by $\cos x$ is reversible. The equation simplifies to

$$
2-\sin x=\sqrt3\cos x,\qquad \sin x+\sqrt3\cos x=2\sin(x+\pi/3)=2.
$$

The [sine](../../../../sine.md) equals $1$ only at $\pi/2+2j\pi$. Here $x+\pi/3\in[\pi/3,5\pi/6)$, so only $x+\pi/3=\pi/2$ is allowed. Hence

$$
\boxed{x=\pi/6.}
$$
