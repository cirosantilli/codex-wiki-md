↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Leibniz integral rule](../../../../../leibniz-integral-rule.md) combines contributions from both moving endpoints with [differentiation under the integral sign](../../../../../differentiation-under-the-integral-sign.md):

$$
\boxed{\frac d{dx}\int_{a(x)}^{b(x)}f(x,t)\,dt=f(x,b(x))b'(x)-f(x,a(x))a'(x)+\int_{a(x)}^{b(x)}\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}(x,t)\,dt.}
$$

The [partial derivative](../../../../../partial-derivative.md) in the last [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md) holds $t$ fixed. Suitable regularity and convergence are assumed as stipulated.
