<h1 id="20/c/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

For $x>-1$, interpret the endpoint $-1$ by a one-sided limit. Part (iii) shows that $g$ extends continuously there with value zero, so both nested [integrals](../../../../../../integral-split.md) converge. Let $F(x)$ denote the nested expression inside the [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md). The [fundamental theorem of calculus](../../../../../../fundamental-theorem-of-calculus.md), applied twice with moving lower endpoints, gives

$$
F'(x)=-\int_x^{-1}g(t)\,dt,\qquad F''(x)=g(x),\qquad F^{(3)}(x)=g'(x).
$$

By part (iii), $g'(x)=1$, hence

$$
\boxed{F^{(3)}(x)=1\qquad(x>-1).}
$$

A direct check is $\int_s^{-1}(t+1)\,dt=-(s+1)^2/2$, so $F(x)=(x+1)^3/6$, with the same third [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md).
