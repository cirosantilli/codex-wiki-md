↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Holding $x$ fixed when taking the [antiderivative](../../../../../../antiderivative.md) with respect to $t$ gives

$$
h(x)=[x^2t^2-x\cos t]_x^{\pi x^2}=\pi^2x^6-x^4-x\cos(\pi x^2)+x\cos x.
$$

Use the [product rule](../../../../../../product-rule.md) and the [chain rule](../../../../../../chain-rule.md) to differentiate this explicit expression:

$$
\boxed{h'(x)=6\pi^2x^5-4x^3-\cos(\pi x^2)+2\pi x^2\sin(\pi x^2)+\cos x-x\sin x.}
$$

This agrees term by term with part (i).
