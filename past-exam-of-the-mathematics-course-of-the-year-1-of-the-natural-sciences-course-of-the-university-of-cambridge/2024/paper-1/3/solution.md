<h1 id="3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

The [chain rule](../../../../chain-rule.md) gives

$$
\boxed{f'(x)=-2x\exp(2-x^2).}
$$

Since $x^2\geq0$, the exponent is at most $2$, with equality precisely at $x=0$. The [exponential function](../../../../exponential-function.md) is increasing, so

$$
\boxed{\max_{x\in\mathbb R}f(x)=e^2,\qquad\text{attained at }x=0.}
$$

The sign of the [derivative](../../../../derivative.md), positive for $x<0$ and negative for $x>0$, gives the same conclusion.
