<h1 id="19/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For the positive terms $a_n=n^4/3^n$, the consecutive ratio is

$$
\frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n}=\frac13\left(1+\frac1n\right)^4\longrightarrow\frac13<1.
$$

The [ratio test](../../../../../../ratio-test.md) therefore proves that **the [series](../../../../../../series-mathematics.md) converges absolutely**. More explicitly, for all sufficiently large $n$ the ratio is at most a fixed $q<1$, so the tail is bounded by a convergent [geometric series](../../../../../../geometric-series.md).
