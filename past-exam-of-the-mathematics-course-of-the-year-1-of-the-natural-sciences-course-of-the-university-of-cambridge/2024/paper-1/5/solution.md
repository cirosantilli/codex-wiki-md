<h1 id="5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

Rearrangement gives the [quadratic inequality](../../../../quadratic-inequality.md) $x^2-3x+1\leq0$. The associated [quadratic equation](../../../../quadratic-equation.md) has roots $(3\pm\sqrt5)/2$. Since the coefficient of $x^2$ is positive, the [quadratic inequality](../../../../quadratic-inequality.md) holds between these roots, including the endpoints:

$$
\boxed{\frac{3-\sqrt5}{2}\leq x\leq\frac{3+\sqrt5}{2}.}
$$
