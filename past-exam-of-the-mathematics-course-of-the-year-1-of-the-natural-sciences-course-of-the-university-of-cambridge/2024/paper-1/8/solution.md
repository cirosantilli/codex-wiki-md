<h1 id="8/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [8](../8.md)

Divide by four and use [completing the square](../../../../completing-the-square.md):

$$
x^2-2x+y^2+y=\frac{11}{4},\qquad (x-1)^2+\left(y+\frac12\right)^2=4.
$$

This is the standard equation of a [circle](../../../../circle.md), so

$$
\boxed{\text{centre}=(1,-1/2),\qquad\text{radius}=2.}
$$
