<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [dot product](../../../../../dot-product.md) is $1\cdot0+2(-1)+(-1)\cdot1=-3$, while the [Euclidean norms](../../../../../euclidean-norm.md) are $\sqrt6$ and $\sqrt2$. Therefore the [angle](../../../../../angle.md) $\theta\in[0,\pi]$ satisfies

$$
\cos\theta=\frac{-3}{\sqrt6\sqrt2}=-\frac{\sqrt3}{2},\qquad \boxed{\theta=\frac{5\pi}{6}=150^\circ.}
$$
