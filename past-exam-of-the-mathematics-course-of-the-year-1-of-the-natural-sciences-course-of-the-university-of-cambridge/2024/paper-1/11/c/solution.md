<h1 id="11/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A counterclockwise [rotation](../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) through $\alpha$ about the origin in the [complex plane](../../../../../complex-plane.md) sends $z_j$ to $e^{i\alpha}z_j$. By part (a), and because the [complex conjugate](../../../../../complex-conjugate.md) of $e^{i\alpha}$ is $e^{-i\alpha}$,

$$
(e^{i\alpha}z_1)(e^{i\alpha}z_2)^*=e^{i\alpha}z_1e^{-i\alpha}z_2^*=\boxed{z_1z_2^*.}
$$

Thus $Q$ is invariant under the common [rotation](../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md).
