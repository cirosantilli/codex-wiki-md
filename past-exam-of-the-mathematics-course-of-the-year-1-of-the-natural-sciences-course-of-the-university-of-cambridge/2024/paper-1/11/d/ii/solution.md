↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

From the same multiplication, the [imaginary part](../../../../../../imaginary-part.md) is

$$
\boxed{\Im Q=y_1x_2-x_1y_2=-(\mathbf v_1\times\mathbf v_2)_z.}
$$

Here the planar [vectors](../../../../../../vector.md) are embedded in three dimensions with zero third coordinate. Its magnitude is the [area](../../../../../../surface-area.md) of their [parallelogram](../../../../../../parallelogram.md); its sign is positive when the oriented [angle](../../../../../../angle.md) from $\mathbf v_2$ to $\mathbf v_1$ has positive [sine](../../../../../../sine.md). The reversed order matters: $\Im(z_1z_2^*)$ is the negative of the usual signed [area](../../../../../../surface-area.md) for the order $(\mathbf v_1,\mathbf v_2)$.
