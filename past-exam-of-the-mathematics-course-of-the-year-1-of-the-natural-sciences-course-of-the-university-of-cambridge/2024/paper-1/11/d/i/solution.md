<h1 id="11/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Write $z_j=x_j+iy_j$. Multiplication gives

$$
Q=x_1x_2+y_1y_2+i(y_1x_2-x_1y_2).
$$

Therefore the [real part](../../../../../../real-part.md) is the [dot product](../../../../../../dot-product.md) of the corresponding [vectors](../../../../../../vector.md):

$$
\boxed{\Re Q=x_1x_2+y_1y_2=\mathbf v_1\cdot\mathbf v_2.}
$$

For nonzero [vectors](../../../../../../vector.md), this is $|z_1||z_2|\cos(\arg z_1-\arg z_2)$, measuring their alignment.
