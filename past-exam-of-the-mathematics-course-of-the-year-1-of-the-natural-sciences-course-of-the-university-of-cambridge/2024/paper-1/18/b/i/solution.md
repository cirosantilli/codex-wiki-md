<h1 id="18/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The three coordinate [basis vectors](../../../../../../basis-vector.md) are [eigenvectors](../../../../../../eigenvector.md), so $M$ is diagonal in those coordinates. Exactly one coordinate is multiplied by $-1$, while the other two are fixed. The three possibilities are

$$
\boxed{M=\operatorname{diag}(-1,1,1),\quad\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,1),\quad\operatorname{diag}(1,1,-1).}
$$

They are respectively reflections in the coordinate [planes](../../../../../../plane.md) $x=0$, $y=0$ and $z=0$. Each is a [Householder reflection](../../../../../../householder-transformation.md) with [unit normal](../../../../../../unit-normal.md) equal to the coordinate [eigenvector](../../../../../../eigenvector.md) carrying the [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $-1$.
