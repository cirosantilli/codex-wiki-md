<h1 id="13/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Using the general solution in part (a), the [initial condition](../../../../../../initial-condition.md) gives

$$
1=4+\frac13+C,\qquad C=-\frac{10}{3}.
$$

Therefore the solution of this [initial value problem](../../../../../../initial-value-problem.md) is

$$
\boxed{y(x)=\frac{3x}{x^4-10x+12}.}
$$

The formula is used on the [interval](../../../../../../interval-mathematics.md) containing $x=1$ where the original [ordinary differential equation](../../../../../../ordinary-differential-equation.md) and the displayed expression are defined.
