<h1 id="13/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

On an [interval](../../../../../interval-mathematics.md) with $x\ne0$, the equation is a [separable differential equation](../../../../../separable-differential-equation.md). For $y\ne0$,

$$
\frac{dy}{y^2}=\left(\frac4{x^2}-x^2\right)dx.
$$

Taking [antiderivatives](../../../../../antiderivative.md) gives $-1/y=-4/x-x^3/3+C$. Renaming the constant, the explicit general nonzero solution is

$$
\boxed{y(x)=\frac1{4/x+x^3/3+C}.}
$$

It is defined on any [interval](../../../../../interval-mathematics.md) avoiding $x=0$ and zeros of its denominator. The separation divided by $y^2$, so the additional solution $\boxed{y(x)\equiv0}$ must be included.
