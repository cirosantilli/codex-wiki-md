<h1 id="17/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The original PDF places the $-1$ outside the square root. Use the [substitution rule](../../../../../integration-by-substitution.md) with $t=\sqrt x$, so $dx=2t\,dt$ and the endpoints become $2$ and $3$. Polynomial division gives

$$
\int_2^3\frac{2t}{t-1}\,dt=2\int_2^3\left(1+\frac1{t-1}\right)dt=2[t+\ln(t-1)]_2^3.
$$

Hence the [definite integral](../../../../../definite-integral.md) is

$$
\boxed{2+2\ln2.}
$$
