<h1 id="17/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use the [substitution rule](../../../../../integration-by-substitution.md) $t=\tan x$, with $dt=(1+\tan^2x)\,dx$. The endpoints become $1$ and $\sqrt3$, so

$$
\int_1^{\sqrt3}\frac{dt}{(1+t)^2}=\left[-\frac1{1+t}\right]_1^{\sqrt3}=\frac12-\frac1{1+\sqrt3}=\boxed{1-\frac{\sqrt3}{2}}.
$$
