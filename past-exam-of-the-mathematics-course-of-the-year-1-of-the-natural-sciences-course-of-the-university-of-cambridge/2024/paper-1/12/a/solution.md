<h1 id="12/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Take the dumbbell axis to be the $z$ axis, with its midpoint at $z=0$. The right-hand [sphere](../../../../../sphere.md) has centre $z=\alpha a$ and [radius](../../../../../radius.md) $R=\beta a$, while its junction with the [cylinder](../../../../../cylinder-geometry.md) is at $z=\gamma a$. Put $d=(\alpha-\gamma)a$, so $0<d<R$. The [Pythagorean theorem](../../../../../pythagorean-theorem.md) in a meridional cross-section gives the handle [radius](../../../../../radius.md) $H$ from $H^2+d^2=R^2$. Thus

$$
\boxed{H=a\sqrt{\beta^2-(\alpha-\gamma)^2}.}
$$
