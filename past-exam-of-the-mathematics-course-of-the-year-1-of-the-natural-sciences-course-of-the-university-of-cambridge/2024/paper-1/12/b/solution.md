<h1 id="12/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use [cylindrical coordinates](../../../../../cylindrical-coordinate-system.md) $(r,\phi,z)$ about the dumbbell axis. Their [Jacobian determinant](../../../../../jacobian-determinant.md) contributes the volume factor $r$, so the handle's [mass](../../../../../mass.md) is the [volume integral](../../../../../volume-integral.md)

$$
M_h=\rho\int_{-\gamma a}^{\gamma a}\int_0^{2\pi}\int_0^H r\,dr\,d\phi\,dz=\rho(2\gamma a)(2\pi)\frac{H^2}{2}.
$$

Inserting the [radius](../../../../../radius.md) from part (a) yields

$$
\boxed{M_h=2\pi\rho\gamma a^3\left[\beta^2-(\alpha-\gamma)^2\right].}
$$
