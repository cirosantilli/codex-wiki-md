<h1 id="14/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Pythagorean theorem](../../../../../pythagorean-theorem.md) gives $c^2=a^2+b^2$. Taking a [total differential](../../../../../total-differential.md) gives $2c\,dc=2a\,da+2b\,db$, so

$$
\boxed{\frac{dc}{c}=\frac{a\,da+b\,db}{a^2+b^2}=\frac{a^2}{a^2+b^2}\frac{da}{a}+\frac{b^2}{a^2+b^2}\frac{db}{b}.}
$$

The [area](../../../../../surface-area.md) of the [right triangle](../../../../../right-triangle.md) is $A=ab/2$, so the [product rule](../../../../../product-rule.md) gives $dA=(b\,da+a\,db)/2$ and

$$
\boxed{\frac{dA}{A}=\frac{da}{a}+\frac{db}{b}.}
$$

The prescribed small changes therefore give $dA/A\approx0.01-0.02=-0.01$: **the area decreases by approximately $1\%$**. For comparison, retaining the product of the two finite percentage changes gives $(1.01)(0.98)-1=-0.0102$, an exact decrease of $1.02\%$; the [total differential](../../../../../total-differential.md) retains only the first-order terms.
