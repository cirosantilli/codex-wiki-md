<h1 id="16/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) of two black balls is the complement of the other two possibilities:

$$
P(BB)=1-p_w-p_d=1-2x+p_w.
$$

The [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) of equal colours is $P(WW)+P(BB)=1-2x+2p_w$. Thus the required [conditional probability](../../../../../../conditional-probability.md) is

$$
\boxed{P(BB\mid\text{same colour})=\frac{1-2x+p_w}{1-2x+2p_w}.}
$$

This is defined when the conditioning event has positive [probability](../../../../../../probability.md). If $N=2$ with one ball of each colour, that event is impossible and the [conditional probability](../../../../../../conditional-probability.md) is undefined.
