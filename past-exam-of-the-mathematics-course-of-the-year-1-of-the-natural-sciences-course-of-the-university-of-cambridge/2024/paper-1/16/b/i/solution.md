<h1 id="16/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) that the first ball is white is $x$. Partitioning that event according to the second colour shows that $P(WB)=x-p_w$. Uniform [sampling without replacement](../../../../../../sampling-without-replacement.md) makes the two draw positions exchangeable, so $P(BW)=P(WB)$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{p_d=2(x-p_w).}
$$

For a direct count, the same value is $2(xN)((1-x)N)/[N(N-1)]$.
