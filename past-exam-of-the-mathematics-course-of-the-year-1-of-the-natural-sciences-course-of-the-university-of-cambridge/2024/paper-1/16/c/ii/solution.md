<h1 id="16/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

There are ten balls of each colour. Under uniform [sampling without replacement](../../../../../../sampling-without-replacement.md), all $\binom{20}{12}$ subsets of twelve labelled balls are equally likely. A favourable subset chooses four of the ten white balls and eight of the ten black balls, giving $\binom{10}{4}\binom{10}{8}$ choices. The [hypergeometric distribution](../../../../../../hypergeometric-distribution.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{P(\text{four white})=\frac{\binom{10}{4}\binom{10}{8}}{\binom{20}{12}}.}
$$

Thus the requested integers are $\boxed{N_1=N_2=10,\ m_1=4,\ m_2=8}$, with $N=20$ and $k=12$.
