<h1 id="16/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

With equally many balls of each colour, uniform [sampling without replacement](../../../../../../sampling-without-replacement.md) gives

$$
p_w=\frac{N/2}{N}\frac{N/2-1}{N-1}\frac{N/2-2}{N-2}=\frac{N-4}{8(N-1)}.
$$

Here an admissible population producing positive [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) for three white balls has even $N\geq6$, so the cancellation is legitimate. Equating this to $2/19$ gives $19(N-4)=16(N-1)$, hence

$$
\boxed{N=20.}
$$
