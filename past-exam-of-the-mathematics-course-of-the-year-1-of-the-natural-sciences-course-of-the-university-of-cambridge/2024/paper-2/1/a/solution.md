The [volume](../../../../../volume.md) of the [parallelepiped](../../../../../parallelepiped.md) is the [absolute value](../../../../../absolute-value.md) of the [scalar triple product](../../../../../scalar-triple-product.md):

$$
V=|\mathbf a\cdot(\mathbf b\times\mathbf c)|
=|(1,0,0)\cdot(1,0,0)|=\boxed{1}.
$$
