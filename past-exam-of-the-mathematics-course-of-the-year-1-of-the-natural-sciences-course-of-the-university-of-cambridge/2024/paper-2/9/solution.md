<h1 id="9/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [9](../9.md)

Expanding the [quadratic form](../../../../quadratic-form-split.md) gives

$$
(x\ y)\begin{pmatrix}1&4\\a_{21}&a_{22}\end{pmatrix}\binom xy
=x^2+(4+a_{21})xy+a_{22}y^2.
$$

Matching [coefficients](../../../../coefficient.md) of $xy$ and $y^2$ yields

$$
\boxed{a_{21}=4,\qquad a_{22}=4}.
$$
