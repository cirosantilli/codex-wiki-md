<h1 id="18/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The square is even, and direct [integration](../../../../../../integral-split.md) gives

$$
\int_{-1}^1f(x)^2\,dx
=2\int_0^1x^2(1-x)^2\,dx
=2\left(\frac13-\frac12+\frac15\right)=\frac1{15}.
$$

Using the nonzero [Fourier coefficients](../../../../../../fourier-coefficient.md) in [Parseval's identity](../../../../../../parseval-identity.md),

$$
\frac1{30}=\frac12\sum_{m=0}^\infty\frac{64}{\pi^6(2m+1)^6}.
$$

Hence

$$
\boxed{\sum_{m=0}^\infty\frac1{(2m+1)^6}=\frac{\pi^6}{960},\qquad A=\frac1{960}}.
$$
