<h1 id="18/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use the real [inner product](../../../../../inner-product-split.md)

$$
\langle f,g\rangle=\int_{-L}^{L}f(x)g(x)\,dx.
$$

[Orthogonality](../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) means this [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md) is zero for any two distinct listed [functions](../../../../../function-split.md). The individual squared [norms](../../../../../norm.md) are positive: the constant function has squared [norm](../../../../../norm.md) $2L$, while every listed [sine](../../../../../sine.md) or [cosine](../../../../../cosine.md) has squared [norm](../../../../../norm.md) $L$. Thus they are orthogonal but not normalized to unit length in this [inner product](../../../../../inner-product-split.md).
