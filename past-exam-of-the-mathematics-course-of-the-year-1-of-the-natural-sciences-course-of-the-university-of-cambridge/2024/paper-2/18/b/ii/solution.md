↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The analogous [orthogonal projection](../../../../../../orthogonal-projection.md) onto the cosine terms gives

$$
\boxed{a_n=\frac1L\int_{-L}^{L}f(x)\cos(n\pi x/L)\,dx,\qquad n\geq0}.
$$

For $n=0$, this means $a_0=L^{-1}\int_{-L}^{L}f(x)\,dx$; the actual constant in the [Fourier series](../../../../../../fourier-series-split.md) is $a_0/2$ because the constant function has squared [norm](../../../../../../norm.md) $2L$.
