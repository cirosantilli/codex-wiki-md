<h1 id="16/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Put $q=x^2yz$. The [chain rule](../../../../../chain-rule.md) and [product rule](../../../../../product-rule.md) give

$$
\phi_{xx}=(2yz+4x^2y^2z^2)e^q,\qquad
\phi_{yy}=x^4z^2e^q,\qquad
\phi_{zz}=x^4y^2e^q.
$$

Summing these [partial derivatives](../../../../../partial-derivative.md) gives the [Laplacian](../../../../../laplacian.md)

$$
\boxed{\nabla^2\phi=e^{x^2yz}\left[2yz+4x^2y^2z^2+x^4(y^2+z^2)\right]}.
$$
