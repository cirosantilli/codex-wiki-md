<h1 id="16/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Add the four outward [fluxes](../../../../../../flux.md):

$$
\boxed{\Phi_{\mathrm{closed}}=-\frac16-\frac14-\frac13+1=\frac14}.
$$

For a check, $\nabla\cdot\mathbf F=2(x+y+z)$. The [divergence theorem](../../../../../../divergence-theorem.md) gives the same result from

$$
\iiint_{x,y,z\geq0,\ x+y+z\leq1}2(x+y+z)\,dx\,dy\,dz=\frac14.
$$
