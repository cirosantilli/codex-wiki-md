<h1 id="16/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

On the face $OBA$, $z=0$ and the outward [unit normal](../../../../../../unit-normal.md) is $-\mathbf e_z$. The [flux integral](../../../../../../flux-integral.md) is therefore

$$
\Phi_{OBA}=-\int_0^1\int_0^{1-x}(1-x-y)\,dy\,dx
=-\frac12\int_0^1(1-x)^2\,dx
=\boxed{-\frac16}.
$$

The negative sign comes from the outward [orientation](../../../../../../orientation-of-a-simplex.md), which points below the tetrahedron on this face.
