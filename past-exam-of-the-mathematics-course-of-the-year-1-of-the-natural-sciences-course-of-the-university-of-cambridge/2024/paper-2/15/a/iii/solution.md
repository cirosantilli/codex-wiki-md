↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [characteristic roots](../../../../../../characteristic-root-of-a-constant-coefficient-differential-equation.md) are

$$
m=\frac{-3\pm\sqrt{9-16}}2=-\frac32\pm i\frac{\sqrt7}{2}.
$$

Taking real [linear combinations](../../../../../../linear-combination.md) of the two [complex exponential](../../../../../../complex-exponential-function.md) solutions yields

$$
\boxed{y(x)=e^{-3x/2}\left[C_1\cos\left(\frac{\sqrt7}{2}x\right)
+C_2\sin\left(\frac{\sqrt7}{2}x\right)\right]}.
$$

The coefficient $\beta$ is three here, as confirmed in the PDF; the OCR replaces it by an undefined symbol.
