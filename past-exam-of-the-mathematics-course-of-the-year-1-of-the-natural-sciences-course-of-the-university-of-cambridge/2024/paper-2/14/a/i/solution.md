↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A displayed minute contains one minute of possible start times, so the specified [uniform distribution](../../../../../../continuous-uniform-distribution.md) has [probability density function](../../../../../../probability-density-function.md)

$$
\boxed{p(s)=\begin{cases}1,&0\leq s<1,\\0,&\text{otherwise}.\end{cases}}
$$

Endpoint choices for a [probability density function](../../../../../../probability-density-function.md) do not affect any [probabilities](../../../../../../probability.md).
