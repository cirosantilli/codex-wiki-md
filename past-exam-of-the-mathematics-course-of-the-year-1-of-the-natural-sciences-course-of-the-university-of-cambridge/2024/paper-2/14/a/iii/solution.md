↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For the [uniform distribution](../../../../../../continuous-uniform-distribution.md), the [expected value](../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) and second [moment](../../../../../../moment.md) are

$$
\mathbb E[S]=\int_0^1s\,ds=\frac12,\qquad
\mathbb E[S^2]=\int_0^1s^2\,ds=\frac13.
$$

Hence the [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[S]=\frac12\text{ minute},\qquad
\operatorname{Var}(S)=\frac13-\frac14=\frac1{12}\text{ minute}^2}.
$$
