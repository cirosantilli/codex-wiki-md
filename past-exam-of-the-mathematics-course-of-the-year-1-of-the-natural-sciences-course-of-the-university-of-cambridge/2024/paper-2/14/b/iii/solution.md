<h1 id="14/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Since $q$ is an [even function](../../../../../../even-function.md), $tq(t)$ is an [odd function](../../../../../../odd-function.md) and its [integral](../../../../../../integral-split.md) over the symmetric interval vanishes. Thus $\mathbb E[T]=0$. Its [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md) is

$$
\mathbb E[T^2]=2\int_0^1t^2(1-t)\,dt
=2\left(\frac13-\frac14\right)=\frac16.
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[T]=0,\qquad\operatorname{SD}(T)=\frac1{\sqrt6}\text{ minute}}.
$$

The [standard deviation](../../../../../../standard-deviation.md) is approximately $0.40825$ minute, or $24.49$ seconds.
