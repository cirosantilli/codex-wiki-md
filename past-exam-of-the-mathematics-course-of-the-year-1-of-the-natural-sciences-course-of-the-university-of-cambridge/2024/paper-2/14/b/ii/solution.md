↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [probability density function](../../../../../../probability-density-function.md) graph joins $(-1,0)$ to $(0,1)$ to $(1,0)$ and is zero elsewhere, as shown in the right panel of the timing figure. Its [area](../../../../../../surface-area.md) is

$$
\int_{-\infty}^\infty q(t)\,dt
=2\int_0^1(1-t)\,dt=\boxed{1}.
$$

Thus the [probability distribution](../../../../../../probability-distribution-split.md) is normalized.
