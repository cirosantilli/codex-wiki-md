<h1 id="14/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

An excess of thirty seconds is $T>1/2$ in minute units. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(T>1/2)=\int_{1/2}^1(1-t)\,dt=\frac18}.
$$

This is the shaded triangular [area](../../../../../../surface-area.md) under the [probability density function](../../../../../../probability-density-function.md).
