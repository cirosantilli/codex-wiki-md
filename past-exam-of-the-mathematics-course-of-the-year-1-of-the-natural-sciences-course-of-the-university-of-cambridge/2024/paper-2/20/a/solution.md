<h1 id="20/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [wave equation](../../../../../wave-equation-split.md) is a homogeneous [linear partial differential equation](../../../../../linear-partial-differential-equation.md). Consequently, if $\psi_1$ and $\psi_2$ solve it, every constant [linear combination](../../../../../linear-combination.md) $A\psi_1+B\psi_2$ also solves it. This is the [superposition principle](../../../../../superposition-principle.md), because [partial differentiation](../../../../../partial-derivative.md) distributes over the [linear combination](../../../../../linear-combination.md).

The same statement holds for the homogeneous [Neumann boundary conditions](../../../../../neumann-boundary-condition.md). [Initial conditions](../../../../../initial-condition.md) combine in the same way; an arbitrary combination need not retain a fixed nonzero initial displacement.
