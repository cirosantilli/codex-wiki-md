<h1 id="19/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A central cross-section of the [sphere](../../../../../sphere.md) gives the constraint $r^2+h^2/4=R^2$, and the cylinder's [volume](../../../../../volume.md) is $V=\pi r^2h$. At an interior optimum with $r,h>0$, the [Lagrange multiplier](../../../../../lagrange-multiplier.md) equations for this [constrained optimization](../../../../../constrained-optimization.md) problem are

$$
2\pi rh=2\lambda r,\qquad
\pi r^2=\lambda h/2,\qquad
r^2+h^2/4=R^2.
$$

The first gives $\lambda=\pi h$; substitution into the second gives $r^2=h^2/2$. The constraint then gives

$$
\boxed{h=\frac{2R}{\sqrt3},\qquad r=R\sqrt{\frac23}}.
$$

The feasible nonnegative $(r,h)$ form a compact arc, whose endpoints have zero [volume](../../../../../volume.md). Since this is the only positive stationary candidate, it is the [global maximum](../../../../../global-maximum.md). Its [volume](../../../../../volume.md) and fraction of the sphere's [volume](../../../../../volume.md) are

$$
\boxed{V_{\max}=\frac{4\pi R^3}{3\sqrt3},\qquad
\frac{V_{\max}}{V_{\mathrm{sphere}}}=\frac1{\sqrt3}}.
$$
