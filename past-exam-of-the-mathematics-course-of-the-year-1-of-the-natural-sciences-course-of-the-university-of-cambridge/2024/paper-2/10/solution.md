<h1 id="10/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [10](../10.md)

The [trigonometric identity](../../../../trigonometric-identity.md) gives $\sin x\cos x=\tfrac12\sin2x$, and the complementary-angle [trigonometric identity](../../../../trigonometric-identity.md) gives $\cos(\pi/2-x)=\sin x$. Thus the [Fourier series](../../../../fourier-series-split.md) is already the finite expression

$$
\boxed{\sin x+\frac12\sin2x}.
$$

The only nonzero [Fourier coefficients](../../../../fourier-coefficient.md) are $b_1=1$ and $b_2=1/2$.
