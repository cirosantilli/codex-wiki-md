<h1 id="11/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The scalar $\mathbf r\cdot\hat{\mathbf u}$ is the signed [scalar projection](../../../../../scalar-projection.md) along the [unit vector](../../../../../unit-vector.md) $\hat{\mathbf u}$. Multiplying by that [unit vector](../../../../../unit-vector.md) makes $P_{\hat{\mathbf u}}(\mathbf r)$ the [orthogonal projection](../../../../../orthogonal-projection.md) of $\mathbf r$ onto its span.

For the other expression, the [vector triple product identity](../../../../../vector-triple-product.md) gives

$$
\boxed{R_{\hat{\mathbf u}}(\mathbf r)
=\mathbf r(\hat{\mathbf u}\cdot\hat{\mathbf u})
-\hat{\mathbf u}(\hat{\mathbf u}\cdot\mathbf r)
=\mathbf r-\hat{\mathbf u}(\mathbf r\cdot\hat{\mathbf u})}.
$$

This is the [orthogonal projection onto a plane](../../../../../closest-point-on-a-plane.md) perpendicular to $\hat{\mathbf u}$ through the origin. Its [dot product](../../../../../dot-product.md) with $\hat{\mathbf u}$ is zero, while the difference from $\mathbf r$ is parallel to $\hat{\mathbf u}$.
