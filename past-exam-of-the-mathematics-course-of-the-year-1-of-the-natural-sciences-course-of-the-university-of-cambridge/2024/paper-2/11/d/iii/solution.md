<h1 id="11/d/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Decompose $\mathbf p$ into its [orthogonal projections](../../../../../../orthogonal-projection.md) parallel and perpendicular to $\hat{\mathbf a}$. Since $\mathbf p\times\hat{\mathbf a}$ removes the parallel component,

$$
|\mathbf c|=|\mathbf p\times\hat{\mathbf a}|
=|\mathbf p-(\mathbf p\cdot\hat{\mathbf a})\hat{\mathbf a}|.
$$

The last [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) is the displacement to the point of the [straight line](../../../../../../straight-line.md) nearest the origin. Hence

$$
\boxed{c\text{ is the perpendicular distance from the origin to the line}}.
$$

The original PDF has $c=|\mathbf c|$, not the squared magnitude inserted by the OCR.
