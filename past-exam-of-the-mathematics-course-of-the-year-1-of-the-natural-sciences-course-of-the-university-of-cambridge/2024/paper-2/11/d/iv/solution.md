<h1 id="11/d/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) $\mathbf c=\mathbf p\times\hat{\mathbf a}$ is a [normal vector](../../../../../../normal-vector.md) to the span of $\mathbf p$ and $\hat{\mathbf a}$. Since $\mathbf c\ne0$, these [vectors](../../../../../../vector.md) determine a [plane](../../../../../../plane.md). Thus the missing words are

**the line and the origin**.

Indeed this [plane](../../../../../../plane.md) consists of all [linear combinations](../../../../../../linear-combination.md) of $\mathbf p$ and the line's direction [vector](../../../../../../vector.md).
