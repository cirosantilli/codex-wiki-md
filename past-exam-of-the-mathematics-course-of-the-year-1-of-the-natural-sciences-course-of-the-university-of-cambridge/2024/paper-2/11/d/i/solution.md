<h1 id="11/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Take the [cross product](../../../../../../cross-product.md) with $\hat{\mathbf q}$:

$$
\mathbf r\times\hat{\mathbf q}
=\mathbf p\times\hat{\mathbf q}
+t(\hat{\mathbf q}\times\hat{\mathbf q})
=\mathbf p\times\hat{\mathbf q}.
$$

Thus one choice is

$$
\boxed{\hat{\mathbf a}=\hat{\mathbf q},\qquad\mathbf c=\mathbf p\times\hat{\mathbf q}}.
$$

Conversely, this [cross product](../../../../../../cross-product.md) equation implies $(\mathbf r-\mathbf p)\times\hat{\mathbf q}=0$, so $\mathbf r-\mathbf p$ is parallel to $\hat{\mathbf q}$. It therefore describes precisely the original [straight line](../../../../../../straight-line.md).
