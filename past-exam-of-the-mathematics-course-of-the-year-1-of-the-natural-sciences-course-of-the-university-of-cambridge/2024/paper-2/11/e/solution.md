<h1 id="11/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

A necessary compatibility condition is $\mathbf f\cdot\hat{\mathbf g}=0$, because a [cross product](../../../../../cross-product.md) with $\hat{\mathbf g}$ is perpendicular to it. If this condition fails, there is no solution.

If it holds, take $\hat{\mathbf g}\times$ the equation and use the [vector triple product identity](../../../../../vector-triple-product.md):

$$
\hat{\mathbf g}\times\mathbf f
=\hat{\mathbf g}\times(\mathbf r\times\hat{\mathbf g})
=\mathbf r-(\mathbf r\cdot\hat{\mathbf g})\hat{\mathbf g}.
$$

Thus the perpendicular [orthogonal projection](../../../../../orthogonal-projection.md) is fixed and the parallel component is free:

$$
\boxed{\mathbf r=\hat{\mathbf g}\times\mathbf f+s\hat{\mathbf g},\quad s\in\mathbb R,
\qquad\text{provided }\mathbf f\cdot\hat{\mathbf g}=0}.
$$

Conversely, $(\hat{\mathbf g}\times\mathbf f)\times\hat{\mathbf g}=\mathbf f$ under the compatibility condition, so every displayed [vector](../../../../../vector.md) solves the equation. The solution set is a [straight line](../../../../../straight-line.md), including the line through the origin when $\mathbf f=0$.
