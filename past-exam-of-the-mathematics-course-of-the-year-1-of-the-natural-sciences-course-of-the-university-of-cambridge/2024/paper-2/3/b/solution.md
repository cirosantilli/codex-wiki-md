<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The chord through the endpoints is the [straight line](../../../../../straight-line.md) $y=(x-1)\log2$. At $\xi=1/\log2$, the [tangent line](../../../../../tangent-line.md) is

$$
y=\log\xi+\frac{x-\xi}{\xi}
=\log\xi+(x-\xi)\log2.
$$

These two [straight lines](../../../../../straight-line.md) have the same [slope](../../../../../slope.md), as required by the [mean value theorem](../../../../../mean-value-theorem.md). Since $f''(x)=-1/x^2<0$, the [natural logarithm](../../../../../natural-logarithm.md) is a [concave function](../../../../../concave-function.md): its graph lies above the chord and below this [tangent line](../../../../../tangent-line.md).
