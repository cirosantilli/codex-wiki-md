↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For the [natural logarithm](../../../../../natural-logarithm.md), $f'(x)=1/x$. The chord [slope](../../../../../slope.md) is $(\log2-\log1)/(2-1)=\log2$. Hence the [mean value theorem](../../../../../mean-value-theorem.md) point satisfies $1/\xi=\log2$, giving

$$
\boxed{\xi=\frac1{\log2}}.
$$

It lies strictly between one and two. For example, $\log2=\int_1^2du/u$ lies strictly between $1/2$ and one.
