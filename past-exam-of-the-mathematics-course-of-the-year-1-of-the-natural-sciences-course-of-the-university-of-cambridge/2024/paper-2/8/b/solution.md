<h1 id="8/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [divergence of a curl is zero](../../../../../divergence-of-a-curl-is-zero.md), so its squared [absolute value](../../../../../absolute-value.md) is

$$
\boxed{|\nabla\cdot(\nabla\times\mathbf F)|^2=0}.
$$

Both identities follow by cancellation of equal [mixed partial derivatives](../../../../../mixed-partial-derivative.md) under the usual smoothness assumptions of [vector calculus](../../../../../vector-calculus.md).
