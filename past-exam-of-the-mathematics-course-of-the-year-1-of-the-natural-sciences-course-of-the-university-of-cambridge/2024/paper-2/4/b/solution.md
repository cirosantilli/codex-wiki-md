<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use the [product rule](../../../../../product-rule.md) and the [fundamental theorem of calculus](../../../../../fundamental-theorem-of-calculus.md):

$$
\frac d{dx}\left[\sin x\int_0^x\sin y\,dy\right]
=\cos x\int_0^x\sin y\,dy+\sin^2x.
$$

Since the [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md) is $1-\cos x$, this simplifies to

$$
\boxed{\cos x-\cos(2x)}.
$$
