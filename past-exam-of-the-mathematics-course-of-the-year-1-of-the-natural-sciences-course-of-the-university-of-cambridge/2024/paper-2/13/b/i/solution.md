<h1 id="13/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Parameterize the [straight line](../../../../../../straight-line.md) by $\mathbf r(t)=(t,t,t)^T$, $0\leq t\leq1$. Then $\mathbf r'=(1,1,1)^T$ and the [line integral](../../../../../../line-integral.md) becomes

$$
\int_0^1\left[2t^2+(\mu+1)t^2+(\lambda+1)t^2\right]dt
=\boxed{\frac{\lambda+\mu+4}{3}}.
$$
