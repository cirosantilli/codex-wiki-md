<h1 id="17/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Apply the determinant-preserving [row operation](../../../../../elementary-row-operation.md) $R_3\leftarrow R_3+3R_2$. The new third row is $(0,0,9-3\alpha)$, so expansion of the [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) along it gives

$$
\det A=(9-3\alpha)\det\begin{pmatrix}1&2\alpha\\1&-1\end{pmatrix}
=\boxed{3(\alpha-3)(2\alpha+1)}.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{\det A=0\iff\alpha=3\text{ or }\alpha=-\frac12,\qquad\beta\text{ arbitrary}}.
$$

The parameter $\beta$ does not affect the [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md).
