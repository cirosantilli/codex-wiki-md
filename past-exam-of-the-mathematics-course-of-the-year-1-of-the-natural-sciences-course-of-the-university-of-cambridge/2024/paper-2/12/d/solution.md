<h1 id="12/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [Hessian matrix](../../../../../hessian-matrix.md) is

$$
H=\begin{pmatrix}30x^4-48y^2&-96xy\\-96xy&30y^4-48x^2\end{pmatrix}.
$$

At each nonzero [stationary point](../../../../../stationary-point.md), its diagonal entries are $1536$ and the off-diagonal entries are $\pm768$. Its [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are $768$ and $2304$, both positive. Thus the [Hessian matrix](../../../../../hessian-matrix.md) is [positive definite](../../../../../positive-definite-matrix.md), and the [second-derivative test](../../../../../second-derivative-test.md) proves that

$$
\boxed{\text{all four nonzero stationary points are strict local minima}}.
$$

Their common value is $h=-512$. In fact they are [global minima](../../../../../global-minimum.md): with $u=|xy|$, the [arithmetic-geometric mean inequality](../../../../../arithmetic-geometric-mean-inequality.md) gives $x^6+y^6\geq2u^3$, so

$$
h\geq2u^3-24u^2\geq-512.
$$

The last one-variable [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) attains its minimum at $u=8$, and equality in the first bound requires $|x|=|y|$, precisely the four points above.
