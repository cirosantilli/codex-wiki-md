<h1 id="12/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A [stationary point](../../../../../stationary-point.md) must satisfy both factored equations from part (a). If either coordinate is zero, the other equation forces the other coordinate to vanish. This gives $(0,0)$.

Otherwise $x^4=8y^2$ and $y^4=8x^2$. With $u=x^2>0$ and $v=y^2>0$, these become $u^2=8v$ and $v^2=8u$. Dividing gives $(u/v)^3=1$, so $u=v$. Then $u^2=8u$ forces $u=v=8$. Hence all five [stationary points](../../../../../stationary-point.md) are

$$
\boxed{(0,0),\qquad(2\sqrt2\,\varepsilon,\ 2\sqrt2\,\delta),\quad \varepsilon,\delta\in\{-1,1\}}.
$$
