<h1 id="7/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [7](../7.md)

The [chain rule](../../../../chain-rule.md) gives $d(e^{xy})=e^{xy}(y\,dx+x\,dy)$, so the given [total differential](../../../../total-differential.md) is exact. Thus $f=e^{xy}+C$; two such [functions](../../../../function-split.md) differ by a constant because their [gradient](../../../../gradient.md) difference vanishes on the [connected](../../../../connected-space.md) [plane](../../../../plane.md). The assigned value at the origin forces $C=-1$, giving

$$
\boxed{f(x,y)=e^{xy}-1}.
$$
