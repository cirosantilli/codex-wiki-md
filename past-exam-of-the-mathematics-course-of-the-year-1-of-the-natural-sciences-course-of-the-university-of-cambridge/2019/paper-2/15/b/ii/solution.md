<h1 id="15/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [characteristic equation](../../../../../../characteristic-equation-of-a-constant-coefficient-differential-equation.md) is $m^2+2m+2=0$, with roots $-1\pm i$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{u(t)=e^{-t}(A\cos t+B\sin t)}.
$$

If the second component is also wanted, the relation $v=u'+3u$ gives

$$
v(t)=e^{-t}\bigl[(2A+B)\cos t+(2B-A)\sin t\bigr].
$$

These are damped oscillations associated with the complex conjugate [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) of the coefficient matrix.
