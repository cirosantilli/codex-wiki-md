<h1 id="15/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The first [initial condition](../../../../../../initial-condition.md) gives $A=1$. The original first equation gives $u'(0)=-3u(0)+v(0)=-2$, whereas the [general solution](../../../../../../general-solution.md) gives $u'(0)=B-A$. Thus $B=-1$ and

$$
\boxed{u(t)=e^{-t}(\cos t-\sin t)}.
$$

The corresponding $v=e^{-t}(\cos t-3\sin t)$ has $v(0)=1$, checking the other [initial condition](../../../../../../initial-condition.md).
