<h1 id="15/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [characteristic equation](../../../../../../characteristic-equation-of-a-constant-coefficient-differential-equation.md) of the [homogeneous linear differential equation](../../../../../../homogeneous-linear-differential-equation.md) is

$$
m^2-4m+13=0,\qquad m=2\pm3i.
$$

The real [general solution](../../../../../../general-solution.md) is $y=e^{2x}(A\cos3x+B\sin3x)$. The first boundary value gives $A=\pi$. At $x=-\pi/2$, $\cos(-3\pi/2)=0$ and $\sin(-3\pi/2)=1$, so $1=e^{-\pi}B$. Hence

$$
\boxed{y(x)=e^{2x}\bigl(\pi\cos3x+e^\pi\sin3x\bigr)}.
$$
