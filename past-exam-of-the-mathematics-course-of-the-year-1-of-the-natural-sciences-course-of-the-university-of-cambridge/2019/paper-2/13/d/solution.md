<h1 id="13/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

With $\beta=0$, integrating $\Phi_x=2\alpha x+\alpha^2y+\alpha z$ gives

$$
\Phi=\alpha x^2+\alpha^2xy+\alpha xz+K(y,z).
$$

The other two [partial derivatives](../../../../../partial-derivative.md) require $K_y=K_z=0$. The origin condition fixes the remaining constant to zero. Hence the [scalar potential](../../../../../scalar-potential.md) is

$$
\boxed{\Phi(x,y,z)=\alpha x^2+\alpha^2xy+\alpha xz}.
$$

Its value at $(1,1,1)$ gives $\alpha^2+2\alpha=1$, so

$$
\boxed{\alpha=-1\pm\sqrt2}.
$$
