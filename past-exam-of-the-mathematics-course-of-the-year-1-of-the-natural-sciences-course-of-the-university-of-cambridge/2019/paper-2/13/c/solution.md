<h1 id="13/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A continuously differentiable [conservative vector field](../../../../../conservative-vector-field.md) has zero [curl](../../../../../curl.md). Here

$$
\nabla\times\mathbf F=
\left(\frac{\partial F_z}{\partial y}-\frac{\partial F_y}{\partial z},\frac{\partial F_x}{\partial z}-\frac{\partial F_z}{\partial x},\frac{\partial F_y}{\partial x}-\frac{\partial F_x}{\partial y}\right)
=\bigl(\beta(2y-1),0,0\bigr).
$$

This vanishes everywhere if and only if $\boxed{\beta=0}$. Since the field is smooth on the [simply connected](../../../../../simply-connected-space.md) domain $\mathbb R^3$, zero [curl](../../../../../curl.md) is also sufficient; the explicit [scalar potential](../../../../../scalar-potential.md) in the next part confirms it.
