<h1 id="17/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Multiplying an $n\times n$ matrix by $\alpha$ scales every row, giving a factor $\alpha^n$ in its [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md). Also $|A^{-1}|=1/|A|$, since $AA^{-1}=I$. Hence

$$
\boxed{|\alpha A^{-1}|=\frac{\alpha^n}{|A|}}.
$$

Only $A$ needs to be invertible; the result remains valid when $\alpha=0$.
