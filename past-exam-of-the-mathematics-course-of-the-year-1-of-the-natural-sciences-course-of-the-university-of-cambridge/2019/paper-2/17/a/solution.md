<h1 id="17/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [left eigenvector](../../../../../left-eigenvector.md) satisfies $(M-\lambda I)^T\mathbf x=0$. Here $M$ is a [symmetric matrix](../../../../../symmetric-matrix.md), so its left and right [eigenspaces](../../../../../eigenspace.md) have the same components. The [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) factors as

$$
\det(M-\lambda I)=(1-\lambda)\bigl[(1-\lambda)^2-4\bigr].
$$

The [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) and all corresponding nonzero row [eigenvectors](../../../../../eigenvector.md) are

$$
\boxed{\begin{array}{c|c}
\lambda&\mathbf x^T\\\hline
3&c(1,1,0)\\
-1&c(1,-1,0)\\
1&c(0,0,1)
\end{array}\qquad c\ne0}.
$$

For example, in the $xy$ block, equal components give the eigenvalue $3$ and opposite components give $-1$, while the $z$ coordinate is unaffected.
