<h1 id="6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Write $U=A\cup B\cup C$. Since $D\subseteq U$, their [intersection](../../../../../set-intersection.md) is $U\cap D=D$. Therefore the definition of [conditional probability](../../../../../conditional-probability.md) gives

$$
\boxed{P(U\mid D)=\frac{P(U\cap D)}{P(D)}=1},\qquad P(D)>0.
$$

The positive-probability condition is necessary: if $P(D)=0$, this elementary definition of [conditional probability](../../../../../conditional-probability.md) does not assign a value.
