Here $I_0=x$ up to a constant. Substitution of $n=0$ in the [integration by parts](../../../../../integration-by-parts.md) recurrence gives

$$
\boxed{I_1=x\ln x-x+C},\qquad x>0.
$$

Indeed, its [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) is $\ln x+1-1=\ln x$.
