<h1 id="7/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [7](../7.md)

This is a [generalized eigenvalue problem](../../../../generalized-eigenvalue-problem.md). A nonzero [vector](../../../../vector.md) lies in the [kernel](../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) of $A-\lambda B$ precisely when its [determinant](../../../../determinant.md) vanishes:

$$
0=\det\begin{pmatrix}1&2-2\lambda\\2-2\lambda&1\end{pmatrix}
=1-4(1-\lambda)^2.
$$

Hence

$$
\boxed{\lambda=\frac12\quad\text{or}\quad\lambda=\frac32}.
$$

The corresponding [eigenvectors](../../../../eigenvector.md) can be taken as $(1,-1)^T$ and $(1,1)^T$, respectively, which also checks that both values give nontrivial solutions.
