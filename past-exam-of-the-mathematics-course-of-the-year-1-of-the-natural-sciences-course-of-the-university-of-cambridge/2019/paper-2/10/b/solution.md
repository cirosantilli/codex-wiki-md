<h1 id="10/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The direction must first be represented by a [unit vector](../../../../../unit-vector.md): $\hat{\mathbf q}=(1,1,1)/\sqrt3$. The [scalar projection](../../../../../scalar-projection.md) of the [vector area](../../../../../vector-area.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{\mathbf S\cdot\hat{\mathbf q}=\frac{\pi a^2}{2\sqrt3}}.
$$

If a [vector projection](../../../../../vector-projection.md) is intended, multiply this scalar by $\hat{\mathbf q}$:

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{proj}_{\mathbf q}\mathbf S=\frac{\pi a^2}{6}(1,1,1)}.
$$
