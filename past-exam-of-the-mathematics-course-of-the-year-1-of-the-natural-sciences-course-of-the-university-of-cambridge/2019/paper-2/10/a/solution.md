<h1 id="10/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For the outward orientation of a [spherical cap](../../../../../spherical-cap.md), its [vector area element](../../../../../vector-area-element.md) is

$$
d\mathbf S=\hat{\mathbf r}\,a^2\sin\theta\,d\theta\,d\phi,\qquad
\hat{\mathbf r}=(\sin\theta\cos\phi,\sin\theta\sin\phi,\cos\theta).
$$

In the [surface integral](../../../../../surface-integral.md) defining the [vector area](../../../../../vector-area.md), the $x$ and $y$ components vanish by integration over a complete azimuth. The remaining component is

$$
S_z=2\pi a^2\int_0^{\pi/4}\cos\theta\sin\theta\,d\theta
=\frac{\pi a^2}{2}.
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{\mathbf S=\left(0,0,\frac{\pi a^2}{2}\right)}.
$$

This also equals the area of the cap's projection onto the horizontal plane, directed upwards.
