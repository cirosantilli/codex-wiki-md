<h1 id="8/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [8](../8.md)

Use the [product-to-sum formula](../../../../product-to-sum-formula.md) and the double-angle [trigonometric identities](../../../../trigonometric-identity.md):

$$
\cos^3x\sin x=\frac14(1+\cos2x)\sin2x
=\frac14\sin2x+\frac18\sin4x.
$$

By [Fourier orthogonality](../../../../fourier-orthogonality.md), these are already its only nonzero [Fourier series](../../../../fourier-series-split.md) coefficients:

$$
\boxed{a_n=0\ (n\geq0),\qquad b_2=\frac14,\quad b_4=\frac18,\quad b_n=0\ (n\ne2,4)}.
$$
