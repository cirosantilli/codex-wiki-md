↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [position vectors](../../../../../../position-vector.md) end at the midpoints of $BC$, $CA$ and $AB$, respectively. The drawing places $E$, $F$ and $G$ on those edges and shows the arrows from the origin. The volume of their [parallelepiped](../../../../../../parallelepiped.md) is the absolute [scalar triple product](../../../../../../scalar-triple-product.md):

$$
\mathbf e\times\mathbf f=\left(\frac14,\frac14,-\frac14\right),\qquad
V=|(\mathbf e\times\mathbf f)\cdot\mathbf g|=\boxed{\frac14}.
$$

Equivalently, this is the absolute [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) of the matrix whose columns are the three vectors. Since it is nonzero, the vectors are [linearly independent](../../../../../../linear-independence.md). Three [linearly independent](../../../../../../linear-independence.md) vectors in the three-dimensional [vector space](../../../../../../vector-space-split.md) $\mathbb R^3$ form a [basis](../../../../../../basis.md).
