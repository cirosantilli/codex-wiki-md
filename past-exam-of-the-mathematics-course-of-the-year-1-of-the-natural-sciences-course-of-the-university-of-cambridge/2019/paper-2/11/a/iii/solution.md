<h1 id="11/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Writing $\overrightarrow{OA}=a\mathbf e+b\mathbf f+c\mathbf g$ and comparing [Cartesian coordinates](../../../../../../cartesian-coordinate-system.md) gives

$$
\frac{b+c}{2}=1,\qquad \frac{a+c}{2}=0,\qquad \frac{a+b}{2}=0.
$$

The unique solution, guaranteed by the [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) property, is $(a,b,c)=(-1,1,1)$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\overrightarrow{OA}=-\mathbf e+\mathbf f+\mathbf g}.
$$
