<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [stationary point](../../../../../stationary-point.md) has zero [gradient](../../../../../gradient.md). The two [partial derivatives](../../../../../partial-derivative.md) give

$$
f_x=2x-1+y=0,\qquad f_y=x-3-2y=0.
$$

Solving this [system of linear equations](../../../../../system-of-linear-equations.md) gives

$$
\boxed{(x,y)=(1,-1)},\qquad f(1,-1)=0.
$$
