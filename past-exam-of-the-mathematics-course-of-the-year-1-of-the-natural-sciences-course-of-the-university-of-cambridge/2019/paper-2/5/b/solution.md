<h1 id="5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [Hessian matrix](../../../../../hessian-matrix.md) is

$$
H=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\1&-2\end{pmatrix},\qquad \det H=-5<0.
$$

Its [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) have opposite signs, so the [stationary point](../../../../../stationary-point.md) is a **[saddle point](../../../../../saddle-point.md)**. More directly, putting $x=1+u$ and $y=-1+v$ gives $f=u^2+uv-v^2$: it increases along $v=0$ and decreases along $u=0$.
