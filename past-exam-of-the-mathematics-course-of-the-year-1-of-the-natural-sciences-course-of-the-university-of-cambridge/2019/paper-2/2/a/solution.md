<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Substitute $x^3$ into the [Taylor series](../../../../../taylor-series.md) for the [exponential function](../../../../../exponential-function.md):

$$
\boxed{e^{x^3}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{x^{3n}}{n!}}.
$$

The general nonzero term is $x^{3n}/n!$; the [power series](../../../../../power-series.md) coefficients of powers not divisible by three are zero. The [radius of convergence](../../../../../radius-of-convergence.md) is infinite.
