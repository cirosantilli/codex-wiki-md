<h1 id="1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Over the [complex numbers](../../../../complex-number.md), the [polynomial factorization](../../../../factorization-of-polynomials.md) $z^2+1=(z-i)(z+i)$ gives the [partial fraction decomposition](../../../../partial-fraction-decomposition.md)

$$
\frac{z+1}{z^2+1}=\frac{A}{z-i}+\frac{B}{z+i}.
$$

Multiplication by $(z-i)(z+i)$ and comparison of [polynomial](../../../../polynomial-split.md) coefficients give $A+B=1$ and $i(A-B)=1$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\frac{z+1}{z^2+1}=\frac{1-i}{2(z-i)}+\frac{1+i}{2(z+i)}},\qquad z\ne\pm i.
$$
