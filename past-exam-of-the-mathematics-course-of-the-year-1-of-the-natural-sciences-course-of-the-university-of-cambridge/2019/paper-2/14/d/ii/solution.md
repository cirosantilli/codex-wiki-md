<h1 id="14/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Insert the two densities into the [convolution of probability densities](../../../../../../convolution-of-independent-random-variables.md):

$$
h(z)=\frac1\pi\int_{-1/2}^{1/2}\frac{dy}{1+(z-y)^2}.
$$

Using the [antiderivative](../../../../../../antiderivative.md) $-\arctan(z-y)$ with respect to $y$ gives

$$
\boxed{h(z)=\frac{\arctan(z+1/2)-\arctan(z-1/2)}{\pi}},\qquad z\in\mathbb R.
$$

This is the [convolution of a Cauchy distribution and a uniform distribution](../../../../../../convolution-of-a-cauchy-distribution-and-a-uniform-distribution.md) with half-width $1/2$.
