<h1 id="14/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [uniform distribution](../../../../../../continuous-uniform-distribution.md) has constant [probability density](../../../../../../probability-density.md) on its interval, and the integral of that density must be one. The interval has length one, so

$$
\boxed{g(y)=\begin{cases}1,&-1/2\leq y\leq1/2,\\0,&\text{otherwise}.\end{cases}}
$$

Changing either endpoint value has no effect on the [probability distribution](../../../../../../probability-distribution-split.md).
