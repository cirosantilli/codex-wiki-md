<h1 id="14/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [independence of random variables](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) means that fixing $Y=y$ leaves the [conditional probability density](../../../../../../conditional-density.md) of $X$ equal to $f$. Given that value of $Y$, $Z$ is the translate $X+y$. Hence

$$
\boxed{P(z\leq Z\leq z+dz\mid Y=y)=\int_{z-y}^{z+dz-y}f(x)\,dx}.
$$

At a continuity point of $f$, the expression is $f(z-y)\,dz+o(dz)$. Since $P(Y=y)=0$, this conditioning uses a [conditional probability density](../../../../../../conditional-density.md), rather than division by the probability of the single event $Y=y$.
