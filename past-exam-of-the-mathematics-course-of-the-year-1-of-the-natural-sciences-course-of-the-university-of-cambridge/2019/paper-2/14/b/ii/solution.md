<h1 id="14/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Apply the preceding interval formula on a short interval and use the [change of variables](../../../../../../change-of-variables-formula.md) $v=x+c$:

$$
P(u\leq U\leq u+du)=\int_{u-c}^{u+du-c}f(x)\,dx
=\int_u^{u+du}f(v-c)\,dv.
$$

Comparison with the defining integral for a [probability density](../../../../../../probability-density.md) gives

$$
\boxed{t(u)=f(u-c)}.
$$

At continuity points, this can also be read as $P(u\leq U\leq u+du)=f(u-c)\,du+o(du)$.
