<h1 id="12/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [gradient](../../../../../gradient.md) vanishes when

$$
f_x=x(1+2y)=0,\qquad f_y=y+x^2-y^2=0.
$$

If $x=0$, the second equation gives $y=0$ or $y=1$. If $y=-1/2$, it gives $x^2=3/4$. These exhaust the possibilities. The [stationary points](../../../../../stationary-point.md) and their values are

$$
\boxed{\begin{array}{c|c}
(x,y)&f(x,y)\\\hline
(0,0)&0\\
(0,1)&1/6\\
(\sqrt3/2,-1/2)&1/6\\
(-\sqrt3/2,-1/2)&1/6
\end{array}}.
$$
