<h1 id="12/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

At each regular point, the [gradient](../../../../../gradient.md)

$$
\boxed{\nabla f=\bigl(x(1+2y),\ y+x^2-y^2\bigr)}
$$

is normal to the [level set](../../../../../level-set.md) and points towards increasing $f$. The arrows in the [contour plot](../../../../../contour-plot.md) above show this direction; their lengths have been normalized to keep the outer arrows legible. Near the origin they point outwards, as expected for a [local minimum](../../../../../local-minimum.md). Near $(0,1)$, write $\eta=y-1$; the linear approximation is $\nabla f\simeq(3x,-\eta)$. Thus the arrows point away along the horizontal direction and towards the point along the vertical direction. The other two [saddle points](../../../../../saddle-point.md) have the same behaviour after rotations through $2\pi/3$. All four [stationary points](../../../../../stationary-point.md) have zero [gradient](../../../../../gradient.md), so no direction arrow is assigned at the points themselves.
