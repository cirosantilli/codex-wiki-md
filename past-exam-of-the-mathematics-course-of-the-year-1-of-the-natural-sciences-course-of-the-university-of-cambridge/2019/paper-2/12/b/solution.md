<h1 id="12/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [Hessian matrix](../../../../../hessian-matrix.md) is

$$
H(x,y)=\begin{pmatrix}1+2y&2x\\2x&1-2y\end{pmatrix}.
$$

At the origin it is the identity, so the origin is a strict **[local minimum](../../../../../local-minimum.md)**. At $(0,1)$ it is $\operatorname{diag}(3,-1)$, so this point is a **[saddle point](../../../../../saddle-point.md)**. At either remaining [stationary point](../../../../../stationary-point.md),

$$
H=\begin{pmatrix}0&\pm\sqrt3\\\pm\sqrt3&2\end{pmatrix},\qquad \det H=-3<0,
$$

which again gives a **[saddle point](../../../../../saddle-point.md)**. All three [saddle points](../../../../../saddle-point.md) have [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $3$ and $-1$; there are no [local maxima](../../../../../local-maximum.md).
