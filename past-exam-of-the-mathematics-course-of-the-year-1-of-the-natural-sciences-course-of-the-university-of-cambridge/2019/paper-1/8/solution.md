<h1 id="8/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [8](../8.md)

Multiply the [partial fraction decomposition](../../../../partial-fraction-decomposition.md) by $(x+1)(x+2)^2$ to obtain a [polynomial identity](../../../../polynomial-identity.md):

$$
x^2+1=a(x+2)^2+b(x+1)(x+2)-5(x+1).
$$

At $x=-1$, this gives $a=2$. Comparing the coefficients of $x^2$ then gives $a+b=1$, so

$$
\boxed{a=2,\qquad b=-1.}
$$

The remaining coefficients agree: $4a+3b-5=0$ and $4a+2b-5=1$. The original rational identity is understood away from its [poles](../../../../pole.md) $-1$ and $-2$.
