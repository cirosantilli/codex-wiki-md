<h1 id="17/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For [integration by parts](../../../../../../integration-by-parts.md), take $u=\arctan x$ and $dv=x^{-2}\,dx$. Then $du=dx/(1+x^2)$ and $v=-1/x$, so

$$
\int\frac{\arctan x}{x^2}\,dx=-\frac{\arctan x}{x}+\int\frac{dx}{x(1+x^2)}.
$$

Use [partial fraction decomposition](../../../../../../partial-fraction-decomposition.md) $1/[x(1+x^2)]=1/x-x/(1+x^2)$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\int\frac{\arctan x}{x^2}\,dx=-\frac{\arctan x}{x}+\ln|x|-\frac12\ln(1+x^2)+C,\qquad x\ne0.}
$$

The [constant of integration](../../../../../../constant-of-integration.md) may be chosen independently on the positive and negative intervals.
