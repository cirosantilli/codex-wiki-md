<h1 id="17/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use [integration by substitution](../../../../../integration-by-substitution.md) $u=\ln x$, $du=dx/x$. The bounds become $3$ and $4$, and the remaining denominator factorizes as $(u-1)(u-2)$. The [partial fraction decomposition](../../../../../partial-fraction-decomposition.md) is

$$
\frac{3u-4}{(u-1)(u-2)}=\frac1{u-1}+\frac2{u-2}.
$$

Consequently the [definite integral](../../../../../definite-integral.md) is

$$
\boxed{\left[\ln(u-1)+2\ln(u-2)\right]_3^4
=\ln\frac32+2\ln2=\ln6.}
$$

All logarithm arguments here are positive on the integration interval.
