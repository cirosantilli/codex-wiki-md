↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Condition on whether replacement occurs. Both alternatives have blue second-draw [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) $2/5$, so the [law of total probability](../../../../../../law-of-total-probability.md) gives

$$
\boxed{P(B_2)=r\frac25+(1-r)\frac25=\frac25,\qquad0\le r\le1.}
$$
