For [sampling without replacement](../../../../../../sampling-without-replacement.md), sum the two outcomes with a blue second draw:

$$
\boxed{P(B_2)=\frac1{10}+\frac3{10}=\frac25\qquad(r=0).}
$$

The marginal blue [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) equals the initial blue fraction even though the two draws are dependent.
