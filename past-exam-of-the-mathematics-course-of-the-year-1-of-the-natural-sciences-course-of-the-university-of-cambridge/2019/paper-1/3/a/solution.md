<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

At a point of tangency, the [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) of the curve equals the [slope](../../../../../slope.md) one of the straight line. The [chain rule](../../../../../chain-rule.md) gives $2\cos2x=1$. Since $0\le2x\le\pi$, the unique solution is $x=\pi/6$. The corresponding height is $\sin(\pi/3)=\sqrt3/2$. Substitution in the [tangent line](../../../../../tangent-line.md) gives

$$
\boxed{c=\frac{\sqrt3}{2}-\frac\pi6.}
$$
