<h1 id="11/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [complex conjugate](../../../../../complex-conjugate.md) is $z^*=x-iy$. From the complex [sine](../../../../../sine.md) and [cosine](../../../../../cosine.md) identities,

$$
\sin(x-iy)=\sin x\cosh y-i\cos x\sinh y,\qquad
\cos(x-iy)=\cos x\cosh y+i\sin x\sinh y.
$$

Divide these expressions and multiply by the [complex conjugate](../../../../../complex-conjugate.md) of the denominator. Its squared [modulus](../../../../../modulus.md) is $\cos^2x\cosh^2y+\sin^2x\sinh^2y=(\cos2x+\cosh2y)/2$. The numerator simplifies using $\cosh^2y-\sinh^2y=1$ and the [double-angle formulas](../../../../../double-angle-formula.md). Thus

$$
\tan(z^*)=\frac{\sin2x-i\sinh2y}{\cos2x+\cosh2y},
\qquad\boxed{\Re\tan(z^*)=\frac{\sin2x}{\cos2x+\cosh2y},\quad\Im\tan(z^*)=-\frac{\sinh2y}{\cos2x+\cosh2y}.}
$$

These expressions apply except at the [poles](../../../../../pole.md) $y=0$, $x=\pi/2+k\pi$ with integer $k$, where the original [tangent function](../../../../../tangent-function.md) is undefined.
