<h1 id="15/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Set $h=x+1$, the displacement from the expansion point. The numerator has [Taylor series](../../../../../../taylor-series.md) $\sinh h=h+h^3/6+O(h^5)$, while the reciprocal denominator has [geometric series](../../../../../../geometric-series.md) $(1+h)^{-1}=1-h+h^2-h^3+h^4+O(h^5)$. Multiplication gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{\sinh(x+1)}{x+2}=(x+1)-(x+1)^2+\frac76(x+1)^3-\frac76(x+1)^4+O((x+1)^5).}
$$

The local [power series](../../../../../../power-series.md) converges for $|x+1|<1$, the distance to the denominator's zero at $x=-2$.
