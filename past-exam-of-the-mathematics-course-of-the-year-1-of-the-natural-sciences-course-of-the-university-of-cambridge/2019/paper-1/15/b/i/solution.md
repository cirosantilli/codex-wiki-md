<h1 id="15/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Write the [sine](../../../../../../sine.md) argument as $\pi/2+\delta$, with $\delta=\tfrac\pi2(e^x-1)=\tfrac\pi2x+\tfrac\pi4x^2+O(x^3)$. The [sine addition formula](../../../../../../sine-addition-formula.md) gives $\sin(\pi/2+\delta)=\cos\delta$. Its [Maclaurin series](../../../../../../maclaurin-series.md) is $1-\delta^2/2+O(\delta^4)$, so

$$
\boxed{\sin\left(\frac{\pi e^x}{2}\right)=1-\frac{\pi^2}{8}x^2+O(x^3).}
$$

There is no linear term because the outer [sine](../../../../../../sine.md) has zero [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) at $\pi/2$.
