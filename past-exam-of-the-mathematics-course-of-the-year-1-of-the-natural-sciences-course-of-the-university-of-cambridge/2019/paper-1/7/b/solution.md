↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

This is a [finite geometric series](../../../../../finite-geometric-series.md) with first term and common ratio both equal to four. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\sum_{n=1}^{5}4^n=\frac{4(4^5-1)}{4-1}=1364.}
$$
