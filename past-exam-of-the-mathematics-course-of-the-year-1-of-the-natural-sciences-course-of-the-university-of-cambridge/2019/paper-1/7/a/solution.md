<h1 id="7/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

There are $100$ terms in this [arithmetic series](../../../../../arithmetic-series.md), starting at $4$ and ending at $202$. Pairing the first and last terms, the second and penultimate terms, and so on, gives

$$
\boxed{\sum_{n=2}^{101}2n=\frac{100}{2}(4+202)=10300.}
$$

The summand in the PDF is $2n$, not $2^n$.
