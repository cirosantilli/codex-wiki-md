<h1 id="14/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Take the [total differential](../../../../../../total-differential.md) of the [logarithm](../../../../../../logarithm.md) of the [relativistic energy](../../../../../../relativistic-energy.md), with the [speed of light](../../../../../../speed-of-light.md) fixed:

$$
\ln E=\ln m+2\ln c-\frac12\ln\left(1-\frac{v^2}{c^2}\right).
$$

Using the [chain rule](../../../../../../chain-rule.md),

$$
\frac{dE}{E}=\frac{dm}{m}+\frac{v\,dv}{c^2-v^2}
=\boxed{\frac{dm}{m}+\frac{v^2}{c^2-v^2}\frac{dv}{v}.}
$$

Every term is a dimensionless fractional change. The differential relation is exact; applying it to finite small changes is a first-order [Taylor expansion](../../../../../../taylor-expansion.md).
