<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The triangle $ADB$ is right-angled at $A$, with $AB=\sqrt2$ and $AD=1/\sqrt2$. Another application of the [Pythagorean theorem](../../../../../pythagorean-theorem.md) gives $DB=\sqrt{2+1/2}=\sqrt{5/2}$. At the [angle](../../../../../angle.md) with vertex $D$, the adjacent side is $AD$ and the hypotenuse is $DB$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\cos\angle ADB=\frac{AD}{DB}=\frac1{\sqrt5}.}
$$
