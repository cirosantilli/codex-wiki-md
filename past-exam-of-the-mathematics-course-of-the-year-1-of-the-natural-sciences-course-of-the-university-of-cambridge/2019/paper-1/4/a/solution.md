<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The two equal legs meet at the [right angle](../../../../../right-angle.md), so the [Pythagorean theorem](../../../../../pythagorean-theorem.md) gives $AB^2+AC^2=BC^2=4$, with $AB=AC$. Thus $AC=\sqrt2$. The midpoint condition then yields

$$
\boxed{AD=\frac{AC}{2}=\frac1{\sqrt2}\text{ units}.}
$$
