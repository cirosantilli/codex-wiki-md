<h1 id="9/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

By [Thales's theorem](../../../../../thales-s-theorem.md), the two sides meeting on the circular arc are [orthogonal](../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md), because the opposite side is a diameter. Both [vectors](../../../../../vector.md) point towards that meeting point; reversing both does not change their [dot product](../../../../../dot-product.md). Consequently

$$
\boxed{\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b=0.}
$$
