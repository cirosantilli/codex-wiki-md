<h1 id="9/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let the semicircle have radius $R$, and write $A=|\mathbf a|$, $B=|\mathbf b|$. The [right triangle](../../../../../right-triangle.md) has hypotenuse $2R$, so the [Pythagorean theorem](../../../../../pythagorean-theorem.md) gives $A^2+B^2=4R^2$. Its [area](../../../../../surface-area.md) is $AB/2$, whereas half the [area](../../../../../surface-area.md) of the semicircle is $\pi R^2/4$. Equating these and eliminating $R$ gives

$$
\boxed{AB=\frac\pi8(A^2+B^2).}
$$

Equivalently, the relation between the two [norms](../../../../../norm.md) can be written

$$
\boxed{\frac AB=\frac{4\pm\sqrt{16-\pi^2}}{\pi}.}
$$

The two possible ratios are reciprocals, corresponding to interchanging the two sides.
