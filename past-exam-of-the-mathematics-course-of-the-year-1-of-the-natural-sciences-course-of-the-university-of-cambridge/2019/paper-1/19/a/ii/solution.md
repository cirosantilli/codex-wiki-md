<h1 id="19/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For each fixed $n$, the inner [geometric series](../../../../../../geometric-series.md) sums to $2^{-n}/(1-1/2)=2^{1-n}$. Summing this second [geometric series](../../../../../../geometric-series.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\sum_{n=0}^\infty\sum_{m=n}^\infty2^{-m}=\sum_{n=0}^\infty2^{1-n}=4.}
$$

All terms are nonnegative, so there is no conditional-rearrangement issue; the iterated sum is finite and the [double series](../../../../../../double-series.md) converges. Equivalently, collecting equal values of $m$ gives $\sum_{m=0}^\infty(m+1)2^{-m}=4$.
