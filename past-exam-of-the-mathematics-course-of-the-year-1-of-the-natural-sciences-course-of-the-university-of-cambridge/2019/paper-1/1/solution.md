<h1 id="1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Bring all terms to the left and factor the [quadratic polynomial](../../../../quadratic-polynomial.md):

$$
2x^2+x-6=(2x-3)(x+2).
$$

Its leading coefficient is positive, so it is nonpositive between its two [polynomial roots](../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md), including the endpoints. Therefore

$$
\boxed{-2\le x\le\frac32.}
$$
