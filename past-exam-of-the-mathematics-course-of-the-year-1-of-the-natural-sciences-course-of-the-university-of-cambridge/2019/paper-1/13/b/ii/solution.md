<h1 id="13/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The real equation has domain $x>0$. Its [integrating factor](../../../../../../integrating-factor.md) is

$$
\mu(x)=\exp\left(\int(1+\ln x)\,dx\right)=e^{x\ln x}=x^x.
$$

Thus the [linear ordinary differential equation](../../../../../../linear-ordinary-differential-equation-split.md) becomes $(x^xy)'=x^xx^{-x}=1$. After integration, $x^xy=x+C$. The [initial condition](../../../../../../initial-condition.md) gives $2=1+C$, hence

$$
\boxed{y(x)=(x+1)x^{-x},\qquad x>0.}
$$
