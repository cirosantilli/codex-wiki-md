<h1 id="18/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Each prediction is a linear combination of the constant parameter and the coefficient of $x_i$. Thus the [design matrix](../../../../../../design-matrix.md) is

$$
\boxed{M=\begin{pmatrix}1&x_1\\1&x_2\\\vdots&\vdots\\1&x_n\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

Its $i$th row multiplied by the parameter [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) $(\alpha,\beta)^{\mathsf T}$ gives the required prediction $\alpha+\beta x_i$.
