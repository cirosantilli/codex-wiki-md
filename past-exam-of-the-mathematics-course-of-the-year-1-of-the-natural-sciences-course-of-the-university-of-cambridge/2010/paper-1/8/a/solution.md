<h1 id="8/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [domain of a function](../../../../../domain-of-a-function.md) excludes $x=3$. Both branches are positive, with a vertical [asymptote](../../../../../asymptote.md) at $x=3$ approached from both sides with value $+\infty$, and horizontal [asymptote](../../../../../asymptote.md) $y=0$ at either infinity. The graph is symmetric about $x=3$ because it depends only on $|x-3|$.

For $x<3$, the formula is $2/(3-x)$ and the [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) is positive; for $x>3$, it is $2/(x-3)$ and the [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) is negative. It passes through $(0,2/3)$ and has no zeros.
