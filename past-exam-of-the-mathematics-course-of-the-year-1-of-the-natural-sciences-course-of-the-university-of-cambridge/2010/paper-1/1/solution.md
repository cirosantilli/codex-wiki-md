<h1 id="1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

The known root gives a factor $x-1$ by the [factor theorem](../../../../factor-theorem.md). [Polynomial division](../../../../polynomial-division.md) leaves $2x^2-7x+6=(2x-3)(x-2)$, so

$$
2x^3-9x^2+13x-6=(x-1)(2x-3)(x-2).
$$

Consequently all the roots are

$$
\boxed{x=1,\quad x=\frac32,\quad x=2.}
$$

The three linear factors account for the full degree of the [polynomial](../../../../polynomial-split.md), so there are no further complex roots.
