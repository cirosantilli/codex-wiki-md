<h1 id="20/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The cosine-difference identity gives $\cos x-\cos3x=2\sin2x\sin x$. Hence

$$
\frac{\cos x-\cos3x}{x^2}
=4\frac{\sin2x}{2x}\frac{\sin x}{x}.
$$

Each [sine](../../../../../../sine.md) ratio tends to one, so

$$
\boxed{\lim_{x\to0}\frac{\cos x-\cos3x}{x^2}=4.}
$$

Equivalently, the quadratic terms of the two [cosine](../../../../../../cosine.md) expansions differ by $4x^2$.
