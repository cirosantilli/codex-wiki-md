<h1 id="20/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $a_n=2^n/n!$. The [ratio test](../../../../../../ratio-test.md) gives $a_{n+1}/a_n=2/(n+1)\to0<1$, so **the series converges absolutely**. In fact the [exponential series](../../../../../../exponential-series.md) identifies its value as

$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{2^n}{n!}=e^2-1,
$$

with the $n=0$ term subtracted.
