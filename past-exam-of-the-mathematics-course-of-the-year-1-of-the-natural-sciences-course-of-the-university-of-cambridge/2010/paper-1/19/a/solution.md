<h1 id="19/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

If $P\,dx+Q\,dy=dF$ with continuous second [partial derivatives](../../../../../partial-derivative.md) of $F$, then $P=F_x$, $Q=F_y$. The [symmetry of second derivatives](../../../../../symmetry-of-second-derivatives.md) requires

$$
\boxed{P_y=Q_x.}
$$

This is a necessary condition for an [exact differential](../../../../../exact-differential.md). With continuously [differentiable](../../../../../differentiable-function.md) [coefficients](../../../../../coefficient.md) it is also locally sufficient, and is globally sufficient on a simply connected open domain; a domain with holes can require additional global information.
