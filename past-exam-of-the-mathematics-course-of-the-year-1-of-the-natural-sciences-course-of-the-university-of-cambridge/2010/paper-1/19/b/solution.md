<h1 id="19/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [partial derivatives](../../../../../partial-derivative.md) are $f_x=e^{xy}(1+xy)$ and $f_y=x^2e^{xy}$. At $(1,1)$ they have values $2e$ and $e$. The first-order [Multivariate Taylor polynomial](../../../../../multivariate-taylor-polynomial.md) therefore gives the change

$$
\boxed{\delta f=2e\,\delta x+e\,\delta y+O(\delta x^2+\delta y^2).}
$$

Thus the requested linear approximation is $e(2\delta x+\delta y)$; it is the change from $f(1,1)=e$, not the new [function](../../../../../function-split.md) value itself.
