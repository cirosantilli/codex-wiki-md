<h1 id="11/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [11](../11.md)

The first four terms form the cubic [Taylor polynomial](../../../../taylor-polynomial.md) about $x_0$:

$$
\boxed{f(x_0)+f'(x_0)(x-x_0)+\frac{f''(x_0)}{2!}(x-x_0)^2
+\frac{f^{(3)}(x_0)}{3!}(x-x_0)^3.}
$$

With a continuous fourth [derivative](../../../../derivative.md) nearby, [Taylor's theorem](../../../../taylor-s-theorem.md) gives a remainder $O((x-x_0)^4)$. Continuous [derivatives](../../../../derivative.md) through third order suffice for the weaker remainder $o((x-x_0)^3)$. These are local approximations; equality with an infinite [Taylor series](../../../../taylor-series.md) requires additional convergence information.
