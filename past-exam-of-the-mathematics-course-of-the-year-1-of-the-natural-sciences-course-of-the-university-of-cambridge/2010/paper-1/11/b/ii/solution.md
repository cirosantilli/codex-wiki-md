<h1 id="11/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Put $v=x+x^2$ in the [logarithmic series](../../../../../../mercator-series.md) $\ln(1+v)=v-v^2/2+v^3/3+O(v^4)$. Since $v^2=x^2+2x^3+O(x^4)$ and $v^3=x^3+O(x^4)$,

$$
\ln(1+x+x^2)=x+x^2-\frac12(x^2+2x^3)+\frac13x^3+O(x^4).
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{\ln(1+x+x^2)=x+\frac{x^2}{2}-\frac{2x^3}{3}+O(x^4).}
$$
