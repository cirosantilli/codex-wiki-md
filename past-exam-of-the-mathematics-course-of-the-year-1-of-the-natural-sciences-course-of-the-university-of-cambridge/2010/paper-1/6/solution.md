<h1 id="6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

Let the contact point have positive horizontal coordinate $x_0$. Equality of slopes between the [tangent line](../../../../tangent-line.md) and the logarithmic [curve](../../../../curve.md) requires $k=1/x_0$. The point must also satisfy both equations, giving $\ln x_0=kx_0+1=2$. Hence

$$
\boxed{x_0=e^2,\qquad k=e^{-2}.}
$$

The contact point is $(e^2,2)$, and the resulting [line](../../../../straight-line.md) $y=e^{-2}x+1$ has exactly the required [derivative](../../../../derivative.md) there.
