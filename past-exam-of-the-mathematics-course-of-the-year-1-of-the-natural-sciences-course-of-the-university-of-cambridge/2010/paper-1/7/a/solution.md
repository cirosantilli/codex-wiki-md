<h1 id="7/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

This [geometric series](../../../../../geometric-series.md) has first term $1$ and ratio $0.999$, which lies strictly inside the unit [interval](../../../../../interval-mathematics.md). Therefore

$$
\boxed{\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(0.999)^n=\frac1{1-0.999}=1000.}
$$

The decimal is treated as the exact value $999/1000$.
