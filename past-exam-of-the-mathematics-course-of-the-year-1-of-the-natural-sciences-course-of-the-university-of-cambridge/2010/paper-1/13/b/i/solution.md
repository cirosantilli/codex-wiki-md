<h1 id="13/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Write the [linear system](../../../../../../system-of-linear-equations.md) as $Ar=d$ with

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}1&2&3\\3&4&5\\1&3&4\end{pmatrix},\qquad
r=\begin{pmatrix}x\\y\\z\end{pmatrix},\qquad
d=\begin{pmatrix}1\\2\\3\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Expansion along the first row gives $\det A=(16-15)-2(12-5)+3(9-4)=2$. Since **$\det A=2\ne0$**, $A$ is an [invertible matrix](../../../../../../invertible-matrix.md), and $r=A^{-1}d$ is the unique solution for this right side.
