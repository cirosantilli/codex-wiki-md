<h1 id="13/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For either sign, the [dot product](../../../../../../dot-product.md) with $v_1$ is

$$
v_1\cdot v_\pm=(-5\pm\sqrt{15})+(2\mp\sqrt{15})+3=0.
$$

The remaining pair also satisfies

$$
v_+\cdot v_-=(25-15)+(4-15)+1=\boxed{0}.
$$

Hence the three [eigenvectors](../../../../../../eigenvector.md) are mutually [orthogonal](../../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md). This also follows from the [spectral theorem for real symmetric matrices](../../../../../../spectral-theorem-for-real-symmetric-matrices.md), since $M=M^T$ and its [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are distinct, but the displayed products verify the actual chosen [vectors](../../../../../../vector.md) directly.
