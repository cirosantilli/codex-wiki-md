<h1 id="14/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The logarithm's argument is $\sqrt3/2+i/2=e^{i\pi/6}$, with [complex modulus](../../../../../../complex-modulus.md) one. The principal [complex logarithm](../../../../../../complex-logarithm.md) is therefore $\ln1+i\pi/6=i\pi/6$, giving

$$
\boxed{\Re\operatorname{Log}z=0,\qquad\Im\operatorname{Log}z=\frac\pi6.}
$$

If $\ln z$ denotes all logarithm values rather than the principal branch, they are $i(\pi/6+2\pi k)$ for $k\in\mathbb Z$: the [real part](../../../../../../real-part.md) remains zero and the [imaginary part](../../../../../../imaginary-part.md) changes by integer multiples of $2\pi$. The principal value is the conventional single-valued answer.
