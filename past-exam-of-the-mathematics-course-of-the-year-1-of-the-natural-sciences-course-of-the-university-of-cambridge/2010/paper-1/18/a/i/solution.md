<h1 id="18/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Put $s=b^at^ax$ and write $y'$ for [differentiation](../../../../../../differentiation.md) with respect to $s$. Work on a real branch where the powers are defined and [differentiable](../../../../../../differentiable-function.md); $b>0$, $t>0$ suffices for arbitrary real $a$, and the parameter choice below has $b>0$. At fixed $x$, $\partial s/\partial t=as/t$. The [product rule](../../../../../../product-rule.md) and [chain rule](../../../../../../chain-rule.md) therefore give

$$
\boxed{\frac{\partial u}{\partial t}=at^{a-1}[y(s)+s y'(s)].}
$$
