<h1 id="18/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

At fixed $t$, the argument is linear in $x$, with $\partial s/\partial x=b^at^a$. Each [differentiation](../../../../../../differentiation.md) therefore contributes this same constant factor, while the outside factor $t^a$ remains unchanged. Thus

$$
\boxed{\frac{\partial^3u}{\partial x^3}=t^a(b^at^a)^3y^{(3)}(s)=b^{3a}t^{4a}y^{(3)}(s).}
$$

Here $y^{(3)}$ denotes the third [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) of the single-variable profile, not a partial [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) of $u$.
