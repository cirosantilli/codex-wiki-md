<h1 id="16/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The increasing substitution $x=\sin^2\theta$ maps $0\leq\theta\leq\pi/2$ onto $0\leq x\leq1$, with $dx=2\sin\theta\cos\theta\,d\theta$. Since [sine](../../../../../sine.md) and [cosine](../../../../../cosine.md) are nonnegative there, powers combine without a sign ambiguity:

$$
\boxed{B(p,q)=2\int_0^{\pi/2}\sin^{2p-1}\theta\cos^{2q-1}\theta\,d\theta.}
$$

This is the [trigonometric form of the beta function](../../../../../trigonometric-form-of-the-beta-function.md). The powers at the endpoints are integrable because $p,q>0$.
