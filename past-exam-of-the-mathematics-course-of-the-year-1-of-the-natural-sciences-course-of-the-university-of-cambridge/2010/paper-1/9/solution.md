<h1 id="9/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [9](../9.md)

Isolate the [square root](../../../../square-root.md): $\sqrt{2x^2+17}=3-x$. Its right side must be nonnegative, so retain the condition $x\leq3$ before squaring. Squaring then gives

$$
2x^2+17=(3-x)^2,\qquad x^2+6x+8=(x+2)(x+4)=0.
$$

Both candidates pass the original equation: $\sqrt{25}-2=3$ and $\sqrt{49}-4=3$. Thus

$$
\boxed{x=-2,\quad x=-4.}
$$

The substitution check excludes the possibility of extraneous roots introduced by squaring.
