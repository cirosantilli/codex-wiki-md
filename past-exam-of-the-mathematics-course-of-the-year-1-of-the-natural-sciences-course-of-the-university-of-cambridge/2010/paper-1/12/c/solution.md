<h1 id="12/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The meeting [position](../../../../../position.md) $N$ is the sum of $F$ independent [Bernoulli random variables](../../../../../bernoulli-distribution.md), each of [mean](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) $p$ and [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) $pq$. [Linearity of expectation](../../../../../linearity-of-expectation.md) and [variance additivity for independent random variables](../../../../../variance-additivity-for-independent-random-variables.md) give

$$
\boxed{\mu=Fp,\qquad\sigma^2=Fpq.}
$$

These are the [mean](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) and [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) of the unwrapped integer label specified in the question, rather than circular angular statistics.
