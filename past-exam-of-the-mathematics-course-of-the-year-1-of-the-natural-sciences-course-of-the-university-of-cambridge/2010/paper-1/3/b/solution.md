<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [Gaussian function](../../../../../gaussian-function.md) $e^{-x^2}$ is even and $\sin x$ is odd, so their product is an [odd function](../../../../../odd-function.md). Moreover $|e^{-x^2}\sin x|\leq e^{-x^2}$, whose integral on the real [line](../../../../../straight-line.md) is finite. Thus the [improper integral](../../../../../improper-integral.md) is absolutely convergent, not merely a symmetric principal value, and the contributions of its two half-lines cancel:

$$
\boxed{\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}e^{-x^2}\sin x\,dx=0.}
$$
