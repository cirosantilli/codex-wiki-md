<h1 id="18/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Split the integrand into its three coordinate contributions. Each integrates independently over the rectangular box:

$$
\begin{aligned}
I&=\left(\frac23\right)(4)(6)+(2)\left(\frac{16}{3}\right)(6)
+(2)(4)(18)\\
&=16+64+144=\boxed{224}.
\end{aligned}
$$

For example, the last factor is $\int_{-3}^3z^2dz=18$. No spherical or cylindrical Jacobian is needed for this Cartesian [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md).
