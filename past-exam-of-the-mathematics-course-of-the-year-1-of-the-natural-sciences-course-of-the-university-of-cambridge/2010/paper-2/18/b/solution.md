<h1 id="18/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The region is the [triangle](../../../../../triangle.md) $0\leq y\leq x\leq1$. Reversing the integration order makes the inner [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md) elementary:

$$
\int_0^1\int_y^1e^{x^2}\,dx\,dy
=\int_0^1\int_0^xe^{x^2}\,dy\,dx
=\int_0^1xe^{x^2}\,dx
=\boxed{\frac{e-1}{2}}.
$$

The integrand is continuous on this compact [triangle](../../../../../triangle.md), so [Fubini theorem](../../../../../fubini-s-theorem.md) justifies exchanging the order.
