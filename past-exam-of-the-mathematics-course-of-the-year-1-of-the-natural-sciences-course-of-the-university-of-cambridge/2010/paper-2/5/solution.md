<h1 id="5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

Multiply numerator and denominator by the [complex conjugate](../../../../complex-conjugate.md) $5+4i$:

$$
\frac{3+5i}{5-4i}=\frac{(3+5i)(5+4i)}{25+16}
=\frac{-5+37i}{41}.
$$

The [real part](../../../../real-part.md) and [imaginary part](../../../../imaginary-part.md) are therefore

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Re}=-\frac5{41},\qquad\operatorname{Im}=\frac{37}{41}}.
$$
