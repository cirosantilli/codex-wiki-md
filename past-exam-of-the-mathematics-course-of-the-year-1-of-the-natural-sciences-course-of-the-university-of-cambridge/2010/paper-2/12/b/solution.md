<h1 id="12/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Set $\omega=\sqrt{k/m}$. Each coordinate satisfies the [harmonic oscillator equation](../../../../../simple-harmonic-motion.md), so the [general solution](../../../../../general-solution.md) is

$$
x=A\cos\omega t+B\sin\omega t,\qquad
y=C\cos\omega t+D\sin\omega t.
$$

The four initial data give $A=X_0$, $B=C=0$, and $D=V_0/\omega$. Thus

$$
\boxed{x(t)=X_0\cos\omega t,\qquad y(t)=\frac{V_0}{\omega}\sin\omega t}.
$$

This is the position component of the [isotropic harmonic oscillator flow](../../../../../isotropic-harmonic-oscillator-flow.md). Substitution also gives the constant [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) $E=(kX_0^2+mV_0^2)/2$.
