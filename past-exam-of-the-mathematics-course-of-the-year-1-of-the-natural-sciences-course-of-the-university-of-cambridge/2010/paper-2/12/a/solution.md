<h1 id="12/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Differentiate the sum of [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) and [potential energy](../../../../../potential-energy.md) using the [dot product](../../../../../dot-product.md) product rule:

$$
\frac{dE}{dt}=m\dot{\mathbf r}\cdot\ddot{\mathbf r}
+k\mathbf r\cdot\dot{\mathbf r}
=\dot{\mathbf r}\cdot(m\ddot{\mathbf r}+k\mathbf r)=0.
$$

The equations of motion make the bracket zero. Hence the [mechanical energy](../../../../../mechanical-energy.md) is **constant in [time](../../../../../time-in-physics.md)**. The central [restoring force](../../../../../restoring-force.md) can exchange kinetic and [potential energy](../../../../../potential-energy.md) but supplies no change in their sum.
