<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

The [characteristic polynomial](../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) is

$$
\det(\lambda I-A)=(\lambda-1/3)(\lambda+1/3)-3
=\lambda^2-\frac{28}{9}.
$$

Its roots give the two [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md)

$$
\boxed{\lambda=\pm\frac{2\sqrt7}{3}}.
$$

Their sum is zero, agreeing with the [trace](../../../../matrix-trace.md) of the [matrix](../../../../matrix.md).
