<h1 id="14/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The three columns of $N=\sqrt8\,A$ are $(\sqrt3,1,2)$, $(-\sqrt2,\sqrt6,0)$, and $(-\sqrt3,-1,2)$. Each has squared [Euclidean norm](../../../../../../euclidean-norm.md) eight. Their pairwise [dot products](../../../../../../dot-product.md) are

$$
-\sqrt6+\sqrt6=0,\qquad -3-1+4=0,\qquad \sqrt6-\sqrt6=0.
$$

Hence $N^{\mathsf T}N=8I$, so $\boxed{A^{\mathsf T}A=I}$. Thus $A$ is an [orthogonal matrix](../../../../../../orthogonal-matrix.md), with $A^{-1}=A^{\mathsf T}$. The [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) from (i) places it in the determinant-one component.
