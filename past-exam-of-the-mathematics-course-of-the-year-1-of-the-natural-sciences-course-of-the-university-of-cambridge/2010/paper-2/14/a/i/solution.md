<h1 id="14/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $N=\sqrt8\,A$ be the unscaled [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md). Expansion along its third row gives

$$
\det N=2(\sqrt2+\sqrt{18})+2(\sqrt{18}+\sqrt2)=16\sqrt2.
$$

Scaling a three-by-three [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) by $1/\sqrt8$ scales its [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) by $(\sqrt8)^{-3}$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\det A=\frac{16\sqrt2}{(\sqrt8)^3}=1}.
$$
