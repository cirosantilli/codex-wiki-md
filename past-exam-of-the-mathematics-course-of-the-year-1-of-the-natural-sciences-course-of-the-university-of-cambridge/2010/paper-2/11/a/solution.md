<h1 id="11/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In [plane polar coordinates](../../../../../plane-polar-coordinates.md), $r$ is distance from the origin and $\theta$ is the angle from positive $x$ toward positive $y$. The conversion to [Cartesian coordinates](../../../../../cartesian-coordinate-system.md) is

$$
\boxed{x=r\cos\theta,\qquad y=r\sin\theta}.
$$

Conversely $r=\sqrt{x^2+y^2}$, with the angular coordinate undefined at the origin.
