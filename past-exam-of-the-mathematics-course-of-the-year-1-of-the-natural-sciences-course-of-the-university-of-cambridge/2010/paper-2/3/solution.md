<h1 id="3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

The first [partial derivatives](../../../../partial-derivative.md) factor as

$$
f_x=4x(x^2+2y^2-1),\qquad f_y=4y(2x^2+1).
$$

Both vanish at the origin, verifying that it is a [stationary point](../../../../stationary-point.md). Since $2x^2+1>0$, every [stationary point](../../../../stationary-point.md) has $y=0$; the first equation then gives $x=0,\pm1$. Thus one requested additional point is $\boxed{(1,0)}$; the other is $\boxed{(-1,0)}$.
