↑ **Parent:** [16](../16.md)

The first field is $\mathbf F_1=(z-y,x,-x)$, so direct [curl](../../../../curl.md) calculation gives

$$
\boxed{\nabla\times\mathbf F_1=(0,2,2)=2\mathbf a}.
$$

It is **not conservative** because its [curl](../../../../curl.md) is nonzero. The second is a radial [gradient](../../../../gradient.md) field:

$$
\mathbf F_2=\nabla\left[-\frac1{\sqrt{1+r^2}}\right],
\qquad\boxed{\nabla\times\mathbf F_2=0}.
$$

The [scalar](../../../../scalar.md) expression is a [smooth function](../../../../smooth-function.md) and single-valued on all of space, so $\mathbf F_2$ is a [conservative vector field](../../../../conservative-vector-field.md). Its [potential of a conservative vector field](../../../../potential-of-a-conservative-vector-field.md) is $\Phi=-(1+r^2)^{-1/2}$. Parts (a) and (b) check path dependence directly.
