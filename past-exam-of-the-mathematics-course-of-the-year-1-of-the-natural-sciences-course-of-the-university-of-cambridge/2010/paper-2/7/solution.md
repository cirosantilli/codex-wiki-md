<h1 id="7/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [7](../7.md)

For this [separable differential equation](../../../../separable-differential-equation.md), away from $x=1$, separation gives $\ln|y+1|=\ln|x-1|+C$, or $y+1=K(x-1)$ on a connected interval. The [initial condition](../../../../initial-condition.md) gives $2=-K$, hence

$$
\boxed{y=1-2x}.
$$

The maximal interval containing $x=0$ on which this solves the printed [differential equation](../../../../differential-equation-split.md) is $\boxed{-\infty<x<1}$. Although the linear formula extends through one, the right-hand side there is $0/0$ and the original equation is undefined. The [equilibrium](../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) solution $y=-1$, lost if one divides by $y+1$, does not satisfy the [initial condition](../../../../initial-condition.md).
