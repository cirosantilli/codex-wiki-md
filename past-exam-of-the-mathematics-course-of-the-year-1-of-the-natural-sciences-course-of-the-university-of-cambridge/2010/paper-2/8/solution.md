<h1 id="8/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [8](../8.md)

For independent fair dice, the [probability](../../../../probability.md) of avoiding one on each die is $5/6$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{P(\text{neither is one})=\left(\frac56\right)^2=\frac{25}{36}}.
$$

The [complement of an event](../../../../complement-of-an-event.md) gives

$$
\boxed{P(\text{at least one is one})=1-\frac{25}{36}=\frac{11}{36}}.
$$
