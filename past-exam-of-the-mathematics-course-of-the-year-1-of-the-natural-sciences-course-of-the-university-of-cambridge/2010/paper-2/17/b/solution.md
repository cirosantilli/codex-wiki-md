<h1 id="17/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Every seven-element subset of the hundred individually identifiable tiles is equally likely. Choose three of the thirty-four vowels and four of the sixty-four consonants; the favourable draw has no blank tiles. Thus

$$
\boxed{P=\frac{\binom{34}{3}\binom{64}{4}}{\binom{100}{7}}}.
$$

This is a [multivariate hypergeometric distribution](../../../../../multivariate-hypergeometric-distribution.md) count, including the implicit factor $\binom20=1$ for blanks. Dividing by $\binom{98}{7}$ instead would incorrectly condition on having drawn no blanks.
