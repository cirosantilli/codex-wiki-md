↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [law of total probability](../../../../../../law-of-total-probability.md) weights each jar's red fraction by its selection [probability](../../../../../../probability.md):

$$
\boxed{P(R)=\frac12\frac{20}{50}+\frac12\frac{180}{350}
=\frac15+\frac9{35}=\frac{16}{35}}.
$$

The large difference in jar sizes does not change their equal selection [probabilities](../../../../../../probability.md); pooling their contents would describe a different experiment.
