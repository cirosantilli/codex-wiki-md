By [Bayes' theorem](../../../../../../bayes-theorem.md),

$$
\boxed{P(X\mid R)=\frac{P(R\mid X)P(X)}{P(R)}
=\frac{(20/50)(1/2)}{16/35}=\frac7{16}}.
$$

The denominator sums red outcomes from both possible jar choices, as calculated in (i).
