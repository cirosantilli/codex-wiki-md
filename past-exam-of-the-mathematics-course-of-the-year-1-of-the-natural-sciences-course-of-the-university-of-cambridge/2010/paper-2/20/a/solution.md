In the physicists' [Hermite polynomial](../../../../../hermite-polynomial.md) convention, begin by differentiating the [Gaussian function](../../../../../gaussian-function.md):

$$
\frac d{dx}e^{-x^2}=-2xe^{-x^2},\qquad
\frac{d^2}{dx^2}e^{-x^2}=(4x^2-2)e^{-x^2}.
$$

The defining prefactor then gives

$$
\boxed{H_0=1,\qquad H_1=2x,\qquad H_2=4x^2-2}.
$$

The derivative-based definition uses nonnegative integers; negative orders are not part of this [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) definition.
