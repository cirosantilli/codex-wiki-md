<h1 id="19/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Divide the objective [area](../../../../../surface-area.md) by $2\pi$, which does not change its maximizer, and use $F=a^2+a\ell$ with constraint $G=a^2+\ell^2/4=1$. The [Lagrange multiplier](../../../../../lagrange-multiplier.md) equations are

$$
2a+\ell=2\lambda a,\qquad a=\frac{\lambda\ell}{2}.
$$

At a nondegenerate [cylinder](../../../../../cylinder-geometry.md) $a,\ell>0$, the second gives $\lambda=2a/\ell$. Eliminating it gives $\ell^2+2a\ell=4a^2$. Hence $q=\ell/a$ satisfies $q^2+2q-4=0$. Select its positive root:

$$
\boxed{\frac\ell a=\sqrt5-1,\qquad D=-1,\qquad E=5}.
$$

This is the sphere-inscribed case of [optimal aspect ratios of closed cylinders](../../../../../optimal-aspect-ratios-of-closed-cylinders.md). One may check maximality from $A(q)=2\pi(1+q)/(1+q^2/4)$: its [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) has the sign of $4-2q-q^2$, so it changes from positive to negative at the selected root.
