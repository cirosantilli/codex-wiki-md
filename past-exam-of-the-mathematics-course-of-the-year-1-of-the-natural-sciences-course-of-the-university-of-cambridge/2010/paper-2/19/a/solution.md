<h1 id="19/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Fix the total [area](../../../../../surface-area.md) at $A_0>0$. Apply [Lagrange multipliers](../../../../../lagrange-multiplier.md) to $V=\pi a^2\ell$ with constraint $G=2\pi a(a+\ell)=A_0$. The two [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) equations are

$$
2\pi a\ell=\lambda(4\pi a+2\pi\ell),\qquad
\pi a^2=\lambda\,2\pi a.
$$

Since $a>0$, the second gives $\lambda=a/2$. Substitution in the first gives $\ell=2a$. The constraint then yields $a^2=A_0/(6\pi)$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{V_{\max}=2\pi\left(\frac{A_0}{6\pi}\right)^{3/2}
=\frac13\sqrt{\frac{A_0^3}{6\pi}},\qquad C=6}.
$$

For completeness, eliminating $\ell$ makes $V=(A_0/2)a-\pi a^3$ on $0<a<\sqrt{A_0/(2\pi)}$. Its [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) changes from positive to negative at the calculated radius and its [volume](../../../../../volume.md) tends to zero at both domain endpoints. Thus this stationary [cylinder](../../../../../cylinder-geometry.md) is indeed the maximum. It is the fixed-area case of [optimal aspect ratios of closed cylinders](../../../../../optimal-aspect-ratios-of-closed-cylinders.md).
