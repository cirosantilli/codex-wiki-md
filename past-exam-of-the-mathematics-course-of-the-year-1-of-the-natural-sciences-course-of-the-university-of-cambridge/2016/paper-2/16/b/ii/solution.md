<h1 id="16/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The sole green ball comes from the first bag exactly on the event $(X,Y)=(1,0)$. Divide this joint [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) by the conditioning event's [probability](../../../../../../probability.md):

$$
\boxed{P(X=1,Y=0\mid X+Y=1)
=\frac{(3/5)(2/3)}{13/30}=\frac{12}{13}}.
$$

This is the [conditional probability](../../../../../../conditional-probability.md) form of [Bayes' theorem](../../../../../../bayes-theorem.md).
