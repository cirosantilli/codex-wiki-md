<h1 id="16/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $X$ count green balls. Sampling without replacement gives a [hypergeometric distribution](../../../../../../hypergeometric-distribution.md). The possible values are $0,1,2$, and counting equally likely three-element samples gives

$$
P(X=k)=\frac{\binom2k\binom3{3-k}}{\binom53}.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{P(X=0)=\frac1{10},\qquad P(X=1)=\frac35,\qquad P(X=2)=\frac3{10}}.
$$

Their sum is $(1+6+3)/10=1$, as required for a [probability distribution](../../../../../../probability-distribution-split.md).
