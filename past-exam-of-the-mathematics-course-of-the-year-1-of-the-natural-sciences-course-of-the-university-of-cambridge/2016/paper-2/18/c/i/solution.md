<h1 id="18/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Apart from its endpoint convention, the ramp is odd, so its constant and [cosine](../../../../../../cosine.md) [Fourier coefficients](../../../../../../fourier-coefficient.md) vanish. For the [sine](../../../../../../sine.md) coefficient, evenness of the integrand and [integration by parts](../../../../../../integration-by-parts.md) give

$$
b_n=\frac{4K}{L^2}\int_0^{L/2}x\sin\frac{2\pi nx}{L}\,dx
=\frac{4K}{L^2}\left[-\frac{xL}{2\pi n}\cos\frac{2\pi nx}{L}
+\frac{L^2}{4\pi^2n^2}\sin\frac{2\pi nx}{L}\right]_0^{L/2}.
$$

Hence

$$
\boxed{a_0=a_n=0,\qquad b_n=\frac{K(-1)^{n+1}}{\pi n}}.
$$

This is the [Fourier series of a periodic sawtooth](../../../../../../fourier-series-of-a-periodic-sawtooth.md). At the periodic jump, the series sums to zero rather than the assigned endpoint value $K/2$; changing a value at one endpoint does not change the coefficients.
