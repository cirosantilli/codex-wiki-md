<h1 id="18/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Write the translated [Fourier series](../../../../../../fourier-series-split.md) in the convention $a_0^{(g)}/2+\sum_{n\geq1}(a_n^{(g)}\cos k_nx+b_n^{(g)}\sin k_nx)$. Comparing with (i) gives

$$
\boxed{a_0^{(g)}=0,\qquad a_n^{(g)}=-b_n\sin\frac{2\pi n\ell}{L},\qquad
b_n^{(g)}=b_n\cos\frac{2\pi n\ell}{L}}.
$$

The minus sign in the [cosine](../../../../../../cosine.md) coefficient records translation in the positive $x$ direction.
