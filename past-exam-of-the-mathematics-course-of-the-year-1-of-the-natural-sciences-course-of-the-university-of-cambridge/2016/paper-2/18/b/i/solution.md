<h1 id="18/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use the periodic extension of $f$ when the translated argument falls outside the original interval. With $k_n=2\pi n/L$, the [sine](../../../../../../sine.md) subtraction identity gives

$$
\boxed{g(x)=\sum_{n=1}^\infty b_n
\left[\sin(k_nx)\cos(k_n\ell)-\cos(k_nx)\sin(k_n\ell)\right]}.
$$

This [translation of a Fourier series](../../../../../../translation-of-a-fourier-series.md) generally introduces [cosine](../../../../../../cosine.md) terms: a translated [odd function](../../../../../../odd-function.md) need not be odd about the original origin. Equality is understood wherever the original [Fourier series](../../../../../../fourier-series-split.md) represents its periodic extension, with midpoint values at jumps.
