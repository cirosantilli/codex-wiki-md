<h1 id="18/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The required property is **odd symmetry**, $\boxed{f(-x)=-f(x)}$, up to irrelevant changes on a set of measure zero when discussing [Fourier coefficients](../../../../../fourier-coefficient.md). An [odd function](../../../../../odd-function.md) has zero mean, and its products with [cosine](../../../../../cosine.md) are odd, so every [cosine](../../../../../cosine.md) coefficient vanishes. Its [sine](../../../../../sine.md) coefficients may remain nonzero.

For pointwise representation, the [Fourier series](../../../../../fourier-series-split.md) takes the average of the one-sided limits at a jump. In particular a pure [Fourier sine series](../../../../../fourier-sine-series.md) has value zero at the origin and at the endpoints; arbitrary assigned values there need not equal the series sum.
