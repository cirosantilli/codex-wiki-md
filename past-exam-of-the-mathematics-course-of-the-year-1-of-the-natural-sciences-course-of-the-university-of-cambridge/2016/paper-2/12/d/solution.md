↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For $a/|b|\ll1$, apply the [binomial series](../../../../../binomial-series.md) to the result in (c):

$$
\left(1+\frac{4a^2}{b^2}\right)^{3/2}
=1+\frac{6a^2}{b^2}+\frac{6a^4}{b^4}+O(a^6/b^6).
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{A=\frac{\alpha b^2}{2}+\frac{\alpha a^2}{2}+O(a^4/b^2)
\sim\frac{\alpha b^2}{2}}.
$$

For $\alpha=2\pi$, the leading term is $\pi b^2$, the [area](../../../../../surface-area.md) of the base disk. A very shallow cap is nearly planar; its first [area](../../../../../surface-area.md) excess is $\pi a^2$.
