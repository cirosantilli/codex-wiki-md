<h1 id="12/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Put $R=|b|$ so that the radial range respects the stated allowance $b\ne0$. With the azimuth measured from the starting meridian, the [cylindrical coordinates](../../../../../cylindrical-coordinate-system.md) of the curved [surface](../../../../../topological-surface-split.md) satisfy

$$
\boxed{0\leq r\leq R,\qquad0\leq\theta\leq\alpha,\qquad
z=a\left(1-\frac{r^2}{b^2}\right)}.
$$

At $r=0$ all azimuths describe the same apex. The [surface](../../../../../topological-surface-split.md) is a sector of an [elliptic paraboloid](../../../../../elliptic-paraboloid.md).
