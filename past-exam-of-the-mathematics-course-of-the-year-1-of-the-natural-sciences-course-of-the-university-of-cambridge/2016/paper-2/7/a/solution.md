<h1 id="7/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use the double-angle identity $2\sin x\cos x=\sin2x$. The [Fourier series](../../../../../fourier-series-split.md) is already a finite trigonometric sum:

$$
\boxed{f(x)=\sin x+\sin2x+\sin3x}.
$$

In the usual [Fourier sine series](../../../../../fourier-sine-series.md) convention, $b_1=b_2=b_3=1$, all other $b_n$ vanish, and all [cosine](../../../../../cosine.md) coefficients and the constant coefficient vanish.
