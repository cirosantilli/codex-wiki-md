↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

**Yes.** The matrix is a real [symmetric matrix](../../../../../symmetric-matrix.md), so the [spectral theorem for real symmetric matrices](../../../../../spectral-theorem-for-real-symmetric-matrices.md) provides an [orthonormal basis](../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) of real [eigenvectors](../../../../../eigenvector.md) and hence a real diagonal form. Explicitly its [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) is $\lambda^2-7\lambda-4$, with distinct real [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md)

$$
\boxed{\lambda_\pm=\frac{7\pm\sqrt{65}}2}.
$$
