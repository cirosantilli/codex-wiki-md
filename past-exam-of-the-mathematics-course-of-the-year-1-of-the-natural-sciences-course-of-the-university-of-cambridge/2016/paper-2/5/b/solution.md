<h1 id="5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) is $\lambda^2-2\lambda\cos\theta+1$, with [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $\cos\theta\pm i\sin\theta$. A real diagonal form can have only real [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md), so it requires $\sin\theta=0$. Conversely, when $\theta=k\pi$, the [rotation matrix](../../../../../rotation-matrix.md) is already $(-1)^kI$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\text{real diagonalization is possible exactly when }\theta\in\pi\mathbb Z}.
$$
