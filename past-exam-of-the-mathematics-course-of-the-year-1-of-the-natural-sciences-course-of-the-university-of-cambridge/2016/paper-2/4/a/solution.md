<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A double six has [probability](../../../../../probability.md) $p=1/36$ on each independent throw. For Alice to win on her second turn, her first throw and Bob's first throw must both fail, then her second throw must succeed. Therefore

$$
\boxed{P=\left(\frac{35}{36}\right)^2\frac1{36}=\frac{1225}{46656}}.
$$
