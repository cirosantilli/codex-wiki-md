<h1 id="17/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The homogeneous equation is the [dot product](../../../../../../dot-product.md) condition $(2y,-x)\cdot\nabla u=0$. A suitable [vector field](../../../../../../vector-field.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{\mathbf v(x,y)=(2y,-x)}.
$$

Where nonzero, it is tangent to the level curves of $u$ and perpendicular to its [gradient](../../../../../../gradient.md). At the origin this vector vanishes, so no direction is defined there.
