<h1 id="17/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Write $u(x,y)=F(\phi(x,y))$ for any differentiable $F$. The [multivariable chain rule](../../../../../../multivariable-chain-rule.md) gives $u_x=2xF'(\phi)$ and $u_y=4yF'(\phi)$. Consequently

$$
2yu_x-xu_y=4xyF'(\phi)-4xyF'(\phi)=0.
$$

Thus $\boxed{u=F(x^2+2y^2)}$ solves the homogeneous equation. The [method of characteristics](../../../../../../method-of-characteristics.md) also explains completeness on the full [plane](../../../../../../plane.md): the [characteristic curves](../../../../../../characteristic-curve.md) are the connected [ellipses](../../../../../../ellipse.md) $x^2+2y^2=\text{constant}$, and $u$ is constant on each one. These are the [elliptic characteristics of a planar transport equation](../../../../../../elliptic-characteristics-of-a-planar-transport-equation.md).
