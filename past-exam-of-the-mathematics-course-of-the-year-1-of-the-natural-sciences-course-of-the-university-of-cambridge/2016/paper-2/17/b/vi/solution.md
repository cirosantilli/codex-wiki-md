<h1 id="17/b/vi/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vi](../vi.md)

Substitution of $y=1$ into the [general solution](../../../../../../general-solution.md) gives

$$
\frac{x^2}{2}+F(x^2+2)=x^2.
$$

Thus $F(s)=(s-2)/2$ for $s\geq2$. Choosing this same formula everywhere gives the [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) solution

$$
\boxed{u(x,y)=\frac12x^2y^2+\frac12x^2+y^2-1}.
$$

It directly satisfies the differential equation and $u(x,1)=x^2$.

**The [boundary condition](../../../../../../boundary-condition.md) does not determine a unique solution on the entire [plane](../../../../../../plane.md).** Every characteristic [ellipse](../../../../../../ellipse.md) $x^2+2y^2<2$ misses the data line $y=1$. More generally, $F$ is arbitrary for $0\leq s<2$, subject to matching the chosen regularity at $s=2$ and at the origin. The displayed [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) is one global extension; it is forced on $x^2+2y^2\geq2$. A smooth [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) supported strictly inside $0<s<2$ may be added to $F$ without changing the boundary data.
