<h1 id="17/b/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

For a [monomial](../../../../../../monomial.md) trial $u_p=Ax^my^n$, the operator gives

$$
\mathcal Lu_p=2Amx^{m-1}y^{n+1}-Anx^{m+1}y^{n-1}.
$$

Choosing $m=n=2$ matches the two powers in the forcing, and $A=1/2$ matches their coefficients. A [particular solution](../../../../../../particular-solution.md) is therefore $u_p=x^2y^2/2$. Adding the homogeneous solutions gives

$$
\boxed{u(x,y)=\frac12x^2y^2+F(x^2+2y^2)}.
$$

The arbitrary [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) labels the values on the [characteristic curves](../../../../../../characteristic-curve.md); it must have sufficient regularity for the desired classical solution.
