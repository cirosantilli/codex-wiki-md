<h1 id="17/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Taking the second [partial derivatives](../../../../../partial-derivative.md) of the [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) gives

$$
w_{xx}=\frac{180x^2y^2-30x^4+30y^4}{360},\qquad
w_{yy}=\frac{180x^2y^2+30x^4-30y^4}{360}.
$$

Adding cancels the pure fourth powers and leaves

$$
\boxed{w_{xx}+w_{yy}=x^2y^2}.
$$

Thus direct substitution verifies the stated [Poisson equation](../../../../../poisson-s-equation.md).
