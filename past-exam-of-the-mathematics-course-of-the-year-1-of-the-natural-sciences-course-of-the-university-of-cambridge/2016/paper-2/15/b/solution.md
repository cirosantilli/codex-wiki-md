<h1 id="15/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Subtracting the third row from the first gives $z=-1$, and the third row gives $y=2-x$. The second row then reduces to

$$
(1-t)x=s-t.
$$

For $t\ne1$, the unique solution is

$$
\boxed{x=\frac{s-t}{1-t},\qquad y=\frac{2-t-s}{1-t},\qquad z=-1}.
$$

Consequently $s=1$ gives $\boxed{(x,y,z)=(1,1,-1)}$ for every $t\ne1$, whereas $s=6$ gives

$$
\boxed{(x,y,z)=\left(\frac{6-t}{1-t},\frac{-4-t}{1-t},-1\right),\qquad t\ne1}.
$$

When $t=1$, the first and second left-hand sides coincide. If $s=1$, they are the same [plane](../../../../../plane.md), and the remaining independent [plane](../../../../../plane.md) cuts it in the line

$$
\boxed{(x,y,z)=(\lambda,2-\lambda,-1),\qquad\lambda\in\mathbb R}.
$$

If $s=6$, the two [planes](../../../../../plane.md) are distinct and parallel, so **there is no solution**. Geometrically, for $t\ne1$ the three [planes](../../../../../plane.md) intersect at a single point; at $t=1$ the coincident-plane case leaves an entire line, while the parallel-plane case has empty intersection.
