<h1 id="15/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The homogeneous [linear system](../../../../../system-of-linear-equations.md) has [solution set](../../../../../solution-set.md) $\ker A$, a [vector subspace](../../../../../vector-subspace.md). If it contains only the zero vector, no two different solutions of the full system can exist: their difference would be a nonzero homogeneous solution.

A [particular solution](../../../../../particular-solution.md) $\mathbf v_p$ to the full system exists exactly when $\mathbf w$ belongs to the [image of a linear map](../../../../../image-of-a-linear-map.md) $\operatorname{im}A$. If it exists, every solution is

$$
\boxed{\mathbf v=\mathbf v_p+\mathbf v_h,\qquad\mathbf v_h\in\ker A}.
$$

Indeed, subtracting $A\mathbf v_p=\mathbf w$ from $A\mathbf v=\mathbf w$ gives $A(\mathbf v-\mathbf v_p)=0$, and conversely adding a [kernel](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) vector preserves the right-hand side. Thus there are **no solutions**, **one solution** if the [kernel](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) is trivial, or **infinitely many solutions** if it is nontrivial. The last [solution set](../../../../../solution-set.md) is an [affine subspace](../../../../../affine-subspace.md) of [dimension](../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) $\dim\ker A$, determined by the [rank-nullity theorem](../../../../../rank-nullity-theorem.md). For a square nonsingular matrix there is one solution for every right-hand side.
