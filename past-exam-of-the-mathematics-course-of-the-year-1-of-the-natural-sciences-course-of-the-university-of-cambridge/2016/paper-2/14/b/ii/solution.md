↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**Yes: $y=2-x$.** Its [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) is $-1$, while substitution into the right-hand side gives $-2/(6-4)=-1$. This is the equilibrium $v=2$ of the transformed [autonomous differential equation](../../../../../../autonomous-system-mathematics.md); it must be restored separately after dividing by $v-2$ in the separated solution.

It is the only straight-line solution. Substituting $y=mx+d$ into the original equation shows that $m\ne-1$ would require $m=-1/3$ from the $x$ coefficients, but the constant coefficients would then give $4/3=0$. For $m=-1$, the constant equation gives $d=2$.
