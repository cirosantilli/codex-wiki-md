<h1 id="10/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [gradient](../../../../../gradient.md) vanishes at the origin. Its [Hessian matrix](../../../../../hessian-matrix.md) there is

$$
H=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},
$$

with [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $1$ and $-1$. The quadratic term is therefore indefinite, so the origin is a **[saddle point of a scalar function](../../../../../saddle-point-of-a-scalar-function.md)**. Equivalently, the leading term $xy$ is positive along $y=x$ and negative along $y=-x$.
