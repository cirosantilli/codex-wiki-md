↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In a multivariable [Taylor series](../../../../../taylor-series.md), order means total degree. The only nonzero [monomial](../../../../../monomial.md) of total degree at most three is $xy$; there are no constant, linear, or cubic terms. Thus

$$
\boxed{\phi(x,y)=xy+O\left((x^2+y^2)^2\right)}
$$

near the origin.
