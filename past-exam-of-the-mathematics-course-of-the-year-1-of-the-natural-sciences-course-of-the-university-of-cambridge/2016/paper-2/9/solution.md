<h1 id="9/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [9](../9.md)

For a spherically symmetric [mass density](../../../../density.md), the angular integral in [spherical coordinates](../../../../spherical-coordinate-system.md) is $4\pi$. The two disjoint shell contributions therefore give the [mass](../../../../mass.md)

$$
M=4\pi\int_a^b r^2\,dr+4\pi\int_c^d r^{-2}\,dr,
$$

so

$$
\boxed{M=4\pi\left[\frac{b^3-a^3}{3}+\frac1c-\frac1d\right]}.
$$

Both contributions are positive because $a<b$ and $c<d$.
