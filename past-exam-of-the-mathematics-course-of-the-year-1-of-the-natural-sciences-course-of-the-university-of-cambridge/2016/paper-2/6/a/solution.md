<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The potential is smooth everywhere, so its mixed [partial derivatives](../../../../../partial-derivative.md) commute. The [curl of a gradient](../../../../../curl-of-a-gradient.md) therefore vanishes:

$$
\boxed{\nabla\times\mathbf F=\nabla\times\nabla\phi=\mathbf0}.
$$
