<h1 id="6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [fundamental theorem for line integrals](../../../../../fundamental-theorem-for-line-integrals.md) evaluates this [conservative vector field](../../../../../conservative-vector-field.md) integral by the change in potential. At the initial point $\phi=0$. At the final point $z=0$, so the exponential factor is one, and

$$
\boxed{\int_C\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r
=\phi(C_{\rm end})-\phi(C_{\rm start})
=\sin\frac\pi8\cos\frac\pi4
=\frac{\sqrt2}{2}\sin\frac\pi8}.
$$

The detailed shape of the path does not affect this [line integral](../../../../../line-integral.md).
