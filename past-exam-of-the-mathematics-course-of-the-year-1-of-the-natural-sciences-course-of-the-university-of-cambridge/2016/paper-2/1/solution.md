<h1 id="1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

The [complex modulus](../../../../complex-modulus.md) is a nonnegative [real number](../../../../real-number.md). Equality therefore requires $z-1$ itself to be real and nonnegative. Its [imaginary part](../../../../imaginary-part.md) gives $y=0$, and its [real part](../../../../real-part.md) then gives $x-1\geq0$. Conversely every such point satisfies the equality. Hence the complete [solution set](../../../../solution-set.md) is

$$
\boxed{y=0,\qquad x\geq1}.
$$

Geometrically this is the closed ray on the [real axis](../../../../real-axis.md) starting at $z=1$.
