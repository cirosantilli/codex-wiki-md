<h1 id="2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

The common ratio in the two [plane](../../../../plane.md) equations can be denoted by $u$. Their intersection then has $x=1+4u$, $y=2+3u$, and $z=-1+2u$. The direction vector $(4,3,2)$ has [Euclidean norm](../../../../euclidean-norm.md) $\sqrt{29}$, so the requested [unit vector](../../../../unit-vector.md) form is

$$
\boxed{\mathbf r=(1,2,-1)+\lambda\frac{(4,3,2)}{\sqrt{29}},\qquad\lambda\in\mathbb R}.
$$

Substituting this expression into both equations gives the same ratio $\lambda/\sqrt{29}$, verifying the entire line.
