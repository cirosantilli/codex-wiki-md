<h1 id="20/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The initial displacement is already the first spatial [normal mode](../../../../../normal-mode.md), so [sine](../../../../../sine.md) [orthogonality](../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) gives $A_1=1$ and every other $A_n=0$. Zero initial velocity gives every $B_n=0$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{f(x,t)=\sin\frac{\pi x}{L}\cos\frac{\pi ct}{L}}.
$$

Both fixed-end values vanish for all time, and differentiation shows that the initial velocity is zero. This is the fundamental [standing wave](../../../../../standing-wave.md), with [angular frequency](../../../../../angular-frequency.md) $\pi c/L$.
