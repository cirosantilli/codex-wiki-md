<h1 id="11/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Take two edge vectors in the face, $\mathbf b-\mathbf a$ and $\mathbf c-\mathbf a$. Their [cross product](../../../../../cross-product.md) is the face [normal vector](../../../../../normal-vector.md)

$$
\mathbf n=(\mathbf b-\mathbf a)\times(\mathbf c-\mathbf a)
=\mathbf a\times\mathbf b+\mathbf b\times\mathbf c+\mathbf c\times\mathbf a.
$$

The face [plane](../../../../../plane.md) is $(\mathbf r-\mathbf a)\cdot\mathbf n=0$. Every term in $\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf n$ except $\mathbf a\cdot(\mathbf b\times\mathbf c)$ vanishes, giving

$$
\boxed{\mathbf r\cdot(\mathbf a\times\mathbf b+\mathbf b\times\mathbf c+\mathbf c\times\mathbf a)
=\mathbf a\cdot(\mathbf b\times\mathbf c)}.
$$

The [scalar triple product](../../../../../scalar-triple-product.md) is nonzero, so this normal cannot vanish.
