<h1 id="11/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The opposite face lies in the [plane](../../../../../plane.md) spanned by $\mathbf a,\mathbf b$ through the origin. Directly,

$$
\mathbf a\times\mathbf b=(-2,2,4),\qquad
\mathbf c\cdot(\mathbf a\times\mathbf b)=12,\qquad
|\mathbf a\times\mathbf b|=2\sqrt6.
$$

The [distance from a point to a plane](../../../../../distance-from-a-point-to-a-plane.md) is the absolute normal component, so

$$
\boxed{d=\frac{12}{2\sqrt6}=\sqrt6}.
$$

Here $\mathbf c=(\mathbf a\times\mathbf b)/2$, so the perpendicular foot is the origin, a vertex of the opposite triangular face. Thus the distance to the actual face equals the distance to its containing [plane](../../../../../plane.md).
