<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For $u>0$, the principal [inverse tangent](../../../../../inverse-tangent.md) satisfies $\arctan u+\arctan(1/u)=\pi/2$: the two positive acute angles are complementary. Taking $u=\pi/4$ therefore makes the argument of the [natural logarithm](../../../../../natural-logarithm.md) equal to $(2/\pi)(\pi/2)=1$. The requested value is **$\ln1=0$**.
