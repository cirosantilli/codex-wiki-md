<h1 id="11/d/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**No finite choice of $b$ and $c$ gives a line.** The coefficient of $zz^*=x^2+y^2$ is fixed at one, so completing the square always gives a [circle](../../../../../../circle.md), a point, or the empty set. A general equation $Azz^*+bz^*+b^*z+c=0$ could describe a line when $A=0$ and $b\ne0$, but changing the fixed quadratic coefficient is not permitted here. A limiting sequence of increasingly large [circles](../../../../../../circle.md) does not make any one of these finite equations a line.
