<h1 id="11/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The same completed square gives $|z+b|^2=|b|^2-c$. A nondegenerate [circle](../../../../../../circle.md) therefore occurs exactly when

$$
\boxed{c<|b|^2,\qquad\text{centre }-b,\quad\text{radius }\sqrt{|b|^2-c}.}
$$

For completeness, $c>|b|^2$ gives an empty [locus](../../../../../../locus-mathematics.md) because a squared [complex modulus](../../../../../../complex-modulus.md) cannot be negative.
