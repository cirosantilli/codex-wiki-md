<h1 id="20/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Take the [curl](../../../../../../curl.md) component by component:

$$
\mathbf F=\left(\partial_y\frac{y^3}{3}-\partial_z\frac{x^3}{3},\partial_z\frac{z^3}{3}-\partial_x\frac{y^3}{3},\partial_x\frac{x^3}{3}-\partial_y\frac{z^3}{3}\right)
=\boxed{(y^2,z^2,x^2).}
$$

Its [divergence](../../../../../../divergence.md) is zero, as expected because the [divergence of a curl is zero](../../../../../../divergence-of-a-curl-is-zero.md).
