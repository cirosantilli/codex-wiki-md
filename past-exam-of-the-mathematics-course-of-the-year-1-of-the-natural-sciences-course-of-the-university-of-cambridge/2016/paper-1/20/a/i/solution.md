<h1 id="20/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

On a connected domain, a [vector field](../../../../../../vector-field.md) is a [conservative vector field](../../../../../../conservative-vector-field.md) if and only if it is the [gradient](../../../../../../gradient.md) of a single-valued [scalar potential](../../../../../../scalar-potential.md), $\boxed{\mathbf H=\boldsymbol\nabla\phi}$. Equivalently, its [line integral](../../../../../../line-integral.md) is path independent, or its integral around every closed curve is zero. For a continuously differentiable field on a [simply connected](../../../../../../simply-connected-space.md) domain, this is equivalent to $\boldsymbol\nabla\times\mathbf H=\mathbf0$. Without the domain hypothesis, vanishing [curl](../../../../../../curl.md) alone is only a local test and is not the necessary-and-sufficient global statement.
