<h1 id="20/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a smooth oriented surface $S$ with piecewise smooth boundary $C=\partial S$ and a continuously differentiable [vector field](../../../../../../vector-field.md) $\mathbf G$, the [Stokes theorem](../../../../../../stokes-theorem.md) states

$$
\boxed{\int_S(\boldsymbol\nabla\times\mathbf G)\cdot\mathbf n\,dS
=\oint_{\partial S}\mathbf G\cdot d\mathbf r.}
$$

The [boundary orientation](../../../../../../boundary-orientation.md) is fixed by the [right-hand rule](../../../../../../right-hand-rule.md): curling the fingers along the positive boundary direction makes the thumb point along the surface normal. For an outward upper cap, its boundary [ellipse](../../../../../../ellipse.md) is traversed counterclockwise when viewed from the positive $z$ direction. The figure shows two possible caps with the same oriented boundary, anticipating the last part.
