<h1 id="17/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Impose the five [initial conditions](../../../../../../initial-condition.md) at zero on the preceding [general solution](../../../../../../general-solution.md). They give

$$
\begin{aligned}
C_0+C_1+C_2+C_3&=1,\\
C_1-C_2+C_4&=0,\\
C_1+C_2-C_3-1&=0,\\
C_1-C_2-C_4&=0,\\
C_1+C_2+C_3&=0.
\end{aligned}
$$

The second and fourth equations give $C_1=C_2$ and $C_4=0$. The third and fifth then yield $C_1=C_2=1/4$ and $C_3=-1/2$, while the first gives $C_0=1$. Thus

$$
\boxed{y(x)=1+\frac14(e^x+e^{-x})-\frac12\cos x-\frac{x^2}{2}
=1+\frac12(\cosh x-\cos x)-\frac{x^2}{2}.}
$$

For a direct check, the difference of its fifth and first [derivatives](../../../../../../derivative.md) is $x$. At zero its value is one; its first, second, third and fourth [derivatives](../../../../../../derivative.md) vanish. In particular the quadratic term cancels the otherwise nonzero second [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) of the hyperbolic-cosine and [cosine](../../../../../../cosine.md) combination.
