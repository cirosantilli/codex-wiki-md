<h1 id="17/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

In the homogeneous equation, substitute the [exponential function](../../../../../../exponential-function.md) $e^{kx}$. The [characteristic equation](../../../../../../characteristic-equation-of-a-constant-coefficient-differential-equation.md) is

$$
k^5-k=k(k^4-1)=k(k-1)(k+1)(k^2+1)=0.
$$

Its distinct roots are $0,1,-1,i,-i$. Combining the complex-conjugate pair into real [sine](../../../../../../sine.md) and [cosine](../../../../../../cosine.md) gives the [complementary function](../../../../../../homogeneous-solution.md)

$$
\boxed{y_c=C_0+C_1e^x+C_2e^{-x}+C_3\cos x+C_4\sin x.}
$$

Five linearly independent terms supply the required five homogeneous degrees of freedom.
