<h1 id="17/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Integrating $y''=0$ once gives a constant first [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md), and integrating again gives

$$
\boxed{y(x)=C_0+C_1x.}
$$

There are two independent constants, as expected for a second-order [ordinary differential equation](../../../../../../ordinary-differential-equation.md).
