<h1 id="16/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The remaining arrivals occur in the next stop phase: $R\leq t+\tau_0\leq R+t$, or $R-t\leq\tau_0\leq R$. B waits until $R+r$, while A departed at $r$, giving $\tau_1=R\geq\tau_0$. This interval has length $t$, hence

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(\tau_1\geq\tau_0\mid t)=\frac tR.}
$$

Endpoint assignments do not affect [probabilities](../../../../../../probability.md). The three outcomes are exhaustive and satisfy

$$
\boxed{\frac{r-t}{R}+\frac{R-r}{R}+\frac tR=1.}
$$

These are [conditional probabilities](../../../../../../conditional-probability.md) for the stated fixed $t$; there is no additional averaging over A's arrival phase in the question.
