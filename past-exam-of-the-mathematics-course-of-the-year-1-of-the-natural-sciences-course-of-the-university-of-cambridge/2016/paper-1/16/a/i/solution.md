<h1 id="16/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The arrival phase has a [uniform distribution](../../../../../../continuous-uniform-distribution.md) over a cycle of length $R$. The no-delay phases occupy length $R-r$, so

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(S=0)=\frac{R-r}{R}=1-\frac rR.}
$$

Individual phase endpoints have [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) zero. The model neglects clearance time and releases waiting vehicles together when the stop interval ends; the degenerate case $r=R$ is interpreted as release at successive cycle boundaries.
