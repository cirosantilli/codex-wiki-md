<h1 id="16/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) of a positive delay is the integral of its density:

$$
\mathbb P(S>0)=\int_0^r\frac{ds}{R}=\frac rR.
$$

Adding the separate no-delay event gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(S=0)+\mathbb P(S>0)=\left(1-\frac rR\right)+\frac rR=1.}
$$

This is the [normalization of a probability distribution](../../../../../../normalization-of-a-probability-distribution.md) with both discrete and continuous parts.
