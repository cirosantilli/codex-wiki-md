<h1 id="16/a/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

The atom at zero contributes zero to the [expected value](../../../../../../expected-value-split.md), while all positive delays must retain their unconditional density. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\mu=\mathbb E[S]=0\left(1-\frac rR\right)+\int_0^r\frac{s}{R}\,ds=\frac{r^2}{2R}.}
$$

This [mean](../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) is $r/R$ times the [mean](../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) $r/2$ conditional on encountering a stop; using $r/2$ alone would omit the vehicles passing without delay.
