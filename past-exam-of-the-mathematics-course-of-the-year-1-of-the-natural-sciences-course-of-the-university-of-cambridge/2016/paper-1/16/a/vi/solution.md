<h1 id="16/a/vi/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vi](../vi.md)

The second [moment](../../../../../../moment.md) is

$$
\mathbb E[S^2]=\int_0^r\frac{s^2}{R}\,ds=\frac{r^3}{3R}.
$$

Subtracting the squared [expected value](../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) gives the [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md) and [standard deviation](../../../../../../standard-deviation.md):

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Var}(S)=\frac{r^3}{3R}-\frac{r^4}{4R^2}
=\frac{r^3(4R-3r)}{12R^2},\qquad
\sigma=\sqrt{\frac{r^3(4R-3r)}{12R^2}}.}
$$

The [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md) is nonnegative because $0<r\leq R$. Both $\mu$ and $\sigma$ have units of time. At $r=R$ the result reduces to $R/\sqrt{12}$, the [standard deviation](../../../../../../standard-deviation.md) of a [uniform distribution](../../../../../../continuous-uniform-distribution.md) over one full cycle.
