<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Since $x^2-1=(x-1)(x+1)$, write the [partial fraction decomposition](../../../../../partial-fraction-decomposition.md) as $A/(x-1)+B/(x+1)$. Matching the numerator gives $A+B=1$ and $A-B=2$, hence

$$
\boxed{\frac{x+2}{x^2-1}=\frac{3}{2(x-1)}-\frac{1}{2(x+1)},\qquad x\ne\pm1.}
$$
