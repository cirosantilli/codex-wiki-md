<h1 id="10/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [10](../10.md)

The first curve is a [circle](../../../../circle.md) of [radius](../../../../radius.md) $1$ centred at $(2,1)$. A line through the origin with equation $kx-y=0$ intersects it precisely when the perpendicular distance from that [centre](../../../../center-geometry.md) is at most the [radius](../../../../radius.md). The [distance from a point to a line](../../../../distance-from-a-point-to-a-line.md) gives

$$
\frac{|2k-1|}{\sqrt{k^2+1}}\leq1
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
(2k-1)^2\leq k^2+1
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad k(3k-4)\leq0.
$$

Hence the full parameter range is

$$
\boxed{0\leq k\leq\frac43.}
$$

The two endpoint lines are tangent, at $(2,0)$ and $(6/5,8/5)$ respectively; interior values give two real intersections. Both endpoints are therefore included.
