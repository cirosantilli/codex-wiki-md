<h1 id="15/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) of the [inverse tangent](../../../../../../inverse-tangent.md) is $(1+x^2)^{-1}$. Expand it as a [geometric series](../../../../../../geometric-series.md) and integrate term by term near zero, using $\arctan0=0$:

$$
\arctan x=\int_0^x(1-t^2+t^4+O(t^6))dt
=\boxed{x-\frac{x^3}{3}+\frac{x^5}{5}+O(x^7).}
$$
