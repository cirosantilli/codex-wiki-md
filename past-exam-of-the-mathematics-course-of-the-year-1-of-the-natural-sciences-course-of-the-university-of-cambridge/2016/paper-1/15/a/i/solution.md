<h1 id="15/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Expand the [natural logarithm](../../../../../../natural-logarithm.md) and reciprocal separately:

$$
\ln(2+x)=\ln2+\frac x2-\frac{x^2}{8}+O(x^3),\qquad
\frac1{2-x}=\frac12\left(1+\frac x2+\frac{x^2}{4}+O(x^3)\right).
$$

Multiplication gives the first three nonzero terms of the [Maclaurin series](../../../../../../maclaurin-series.md):

$$
\boxed{\frac{\ln(2+x)}{2-x}=\frac{\ln2}{2}
+\frac{1+\ln2}{4}x+\frac{1+2\ln2}{16}x^2+O(x^3).}
$$

The local expansions converge for $|x|<2$.
