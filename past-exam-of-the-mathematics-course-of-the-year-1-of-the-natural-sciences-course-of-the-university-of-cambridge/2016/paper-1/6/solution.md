<h1 id="6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

Separate variables near the nonzero [initial condition](../../../../initial-condition.md): $y^{-2}dy=dx$. [Integration](../../../../integral-split.md) gives $-1/y=x+C$, and $y(1)=1$ fixes $C=-2$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{y(x)=\frac1{2-x},\qquad -\infty<x<2.}
$$

This is the maximal connected interval containing the initial point. Direct [differentiation](../../../../differentiation.md) gives $y'=1/(2-x)^2=y^2$; the pole at $x=2$ prevents continuation as a finite classical [solution of a differential equation](../../../../solution-of-a-differential-equation.md) through that point.
