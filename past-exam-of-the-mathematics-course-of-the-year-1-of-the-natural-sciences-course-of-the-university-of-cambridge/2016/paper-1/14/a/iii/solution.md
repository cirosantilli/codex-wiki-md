<h1 id="14/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

If every [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) is nonzero, define $B=\sum_n\lambda_n^{-1}P_n$, where $P_n=\mathbf e_n\mathbf e_n^{\mathsf T}$. Using the mutually [orthogonal projection](../../../../../../orthogonal-projection.md) identity $P_nP_m=\delta_{nm}P_n$,

$$
AB=\sum_{n,m}\frac{\lambda_n}{\lambda_m}P_nP_m=\sum_nP_n=I.
$$

The same calculation gives $BA=I$. Thus the [matrix inverse](../../../../../../matrix-inverse-split.md) is

$$
\boxed{A^{-1}=\sum_{n=1}^N\frac1{\lambda_n}\mathbf e_n\mathbf e_n^{\mathsf T}.}
$$
