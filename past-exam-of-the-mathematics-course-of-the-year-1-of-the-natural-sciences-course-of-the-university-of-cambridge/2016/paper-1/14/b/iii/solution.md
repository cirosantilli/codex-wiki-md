<h1 id="14/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Summing the diagonal entries gives

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{tr}(A^{-1})=\frac38+\frac38+1=\frac74.}
$$

The [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) of the inverse are the reciprocals $1/2,1/4,1$ of those of $A$, whose sum is also $7/4$. This is precisely what the [trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md) identity predicts.
