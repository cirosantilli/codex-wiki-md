<h1 id="13/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write $\mathbf a=(a_x,a_y,a_z)$. The [cross product](../../../../../cross-product.md) gives $\mathbf v_1=(a_yz-a_zy,a_zx-a_xz,a_xy-a_yx)$, whose three diagonal [partial derivatives](../../../../../partial-derivative.md) vanish. For the second [vector field](../../../../../vector-field.md), direct [differentiation](../../../../../differentiation.md) gives $\partial_x(2xy)=2y$, $\partial_y(x^2+3y^2+z^2)=6y$, and $\partial_z(2yz)=2y$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\boldsymbol\nabla\cdot\mathbf v_1=0,\qquad
\boldsymbol\nabla\cdot\mathbf v_2=10y.}
$$
