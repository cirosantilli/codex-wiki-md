<h1 id="7/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) of $\cot u$ is $-\csc^2u$. The [chain rule](../../../../../chain-rule.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\int\frac{dx}{\sin^2(3x+1)}=-\frac13\cot(3x+1)+C.}
$$

The [antiderivative](../../../../../antiderivative.md) applies on any interval containing no zero of $\sin(3x+1)$.
