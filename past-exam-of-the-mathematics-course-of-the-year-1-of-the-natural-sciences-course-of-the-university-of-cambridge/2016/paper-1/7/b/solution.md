<h1 id="7/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Additivity of the [definite integral](../../../../../definite-integral.md) combines the adjacent intervals into $[0,n]$. For an integer $n\geq1$, the [power rule](../../../../../power-rule.md) gives

$$
\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}\int_j^{j+1}(x-2)^n\,dx
=\int_0^n(x-2)^n\,dx
=\boxed{\frac{(n-2)^{n+1}-(-2)^{n+1}}{n+1}}.
$$

For $n=0$, interpret the indexed sum as an [empty sum](../../../../../empty-sum.md), equal to zero; the displayed expression also gives zero. This convention resolves the otherwise degenerate endpoint of the stated nonnegative-integer range.
