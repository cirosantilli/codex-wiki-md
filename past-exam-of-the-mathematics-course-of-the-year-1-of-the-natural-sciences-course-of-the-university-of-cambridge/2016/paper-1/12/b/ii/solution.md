↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Within the requested first-quadrant square, the [stationary points](../../../../../../stationary-point.md) visible in the [contour plot](../../../../../../contour-plot.md) are the corner minimum $(0,0)$ and the saddle $(1,1)$. Near the origin, $g\simeq4(x^2+y^2)$, so the low positive contours look like quarter-circles. Along either coordinate axis, $g=4t^2+t^4$ rises monotonically. Along the diagonal, $g(t,t)=8t^2-4t^4$ rises to $4$ at $t=1$, returns to zero at $t=\sqrt2$, then becomes negative.

The saddle contour $g=4$ crosses at $(1,1)$ and separates the central positive contours from the outer diagonal region. Negative contours occur toward $(2,2)$, where $g=-32$, while the axis corners $(2,0)$ and $(0,2)$ have $g=32$. These checks determine both the contour ordering and the change of connectivity at the saddle; the figure shows these level sets.
