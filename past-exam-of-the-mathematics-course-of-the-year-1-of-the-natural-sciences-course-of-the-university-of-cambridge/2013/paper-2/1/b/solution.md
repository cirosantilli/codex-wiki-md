<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [cross product](../../../../../cross-product.md) is $\mathbf a\times\mathbf b=(1,-7,-3)$. Taking its [dot product](../../../../../dot-product.md) with $\mathbf c$ gives the [scalar triple product](../../../../../scalar-triple-product.md)

$$
\boxed{(\mathbf a\times\mathbf b)\cdot\mathbf c=-7+3=-4}.
$$
