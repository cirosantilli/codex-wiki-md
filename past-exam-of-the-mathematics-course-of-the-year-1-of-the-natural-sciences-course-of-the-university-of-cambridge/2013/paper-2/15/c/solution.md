<h1 id="15/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Solving for $y$ gives $y=\pm(b/a)\sqrt{x^2-a^2}$ with $x\geq a$. For large $x$, the [square root](../../../../../square-root.md) is $x-a^2/(2x)+O(x^{-3})$, so the two [asymptotes](../../../../../asymptote.md) are

$$
\boxed{y=\frac ba x,\qquad y=-\frac ba x}.
$$

The curve lies between these lines on the right side and has its nearest point, with vertical tangent, at $(a,0)$. As $t$ increases it travels from the lower unbounded end through that vertex to the upper unbounded end. The left [hyperbola](../../../../../hyperbola.md) branch is not part of the given curve.
