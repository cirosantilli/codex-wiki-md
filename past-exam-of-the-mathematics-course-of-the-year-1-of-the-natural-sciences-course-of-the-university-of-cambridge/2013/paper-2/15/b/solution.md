<h1 id="15/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [hyperbolic functions](../../../../../hyperbolic-function.md) identity eliminates $t$ from the [parametric curve](../../../../../parametric-curve.md):

$$
\boxed{\frac{x^2}{a^2}-\frac{y^2}{b^2}=1,\qquad x\geq a}.
$$

The restriction is essential: $\cosh t\geq1$, so the parametrization traces only the right branch of the full [hyperbola](../../../../../hyperbola.md). Conversely, every point on that right branch is reached by taking $t=\operatorname{arsinh}(y/b)$.
