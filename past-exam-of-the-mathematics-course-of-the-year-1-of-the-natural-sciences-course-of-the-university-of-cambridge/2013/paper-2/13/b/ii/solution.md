<h1 id="13/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The integrand for $I_1$ is an [odd function](../../../../../../odd-function.md) with [absolute convergence](../../../../../../absolute-convergence.md), so $I_1=0$. For $I_2$, the recurrence gives $I_2=I_0/2$, where the supplied [Gaussian integral](../../../../../../gaussian-integral.md) is $I_0=\sqrt\pi$. Hence

$$
\boxed{I_1=0,\qquad I_2=\frac{\sqrt\pi}{2}}.
$$

The zero first moment is specific to the symmetric full-line domain.
