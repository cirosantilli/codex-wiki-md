<h1 id="13/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [integral](../../../../../../integral-split.md) converges absolutely: near zero it behaves like $\log x$, and at infinity like $(\log x)/x^2$. Let its value be $J$. The [integration by substitution](../../../../../../integration-by-substitution.md) $x=1/t$ gives

$$
J=\int_0^\infty\frac{-\log t}{1+t^2}\,dt=-J.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{J=0}.
$$

[Absolute convergence](../../../../../../absolute-convergence.md) makes this cancellation between the two ends legitimate.
