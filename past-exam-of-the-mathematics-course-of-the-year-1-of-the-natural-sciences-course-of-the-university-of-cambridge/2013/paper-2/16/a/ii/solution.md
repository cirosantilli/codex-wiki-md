<h1 id="16/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [cylinder](../../../../../../cylinder-geometry.md) is a [level set](../../../../../../level-set.md) of $h=x^2+y^2-8$, so its outward [normal vector](../../../../../../normal-vector.md) is parallel to $\nabla h=(2x,2y,0)$. At the point this is $(4,4,0)$, with norm $4\sqrt2$. Choosing the outward [orientation](../../../../../../orientation-of-a-simplex.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\hat{\mathbf n}=\frac{(1,1,0)}{\sqrt2}}.
$$

Its negative is the equally valid inward [unit normal vector](../../../../../../unit-normal.md); the next [directional derivative](../../../../../../directional-derivative.md) then changes sign.
