<h1 id="16/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Assume the components of $\mathbf F$ have continuous second [partial derivatives](../../../../../partial-derivative.md). Expanding the [divergence](../../../../../divergence.md) of its [curl](../../../../../curl.md) gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
\nabla\cdot(\nabla\times\mathbf F)
={}&\partial_x\partial_yF_z-\partial_x\partial_zF_y
+\partial_y\partial_zF_x-\partial_y\partial_xF_z\\
&+\partial_z\partial_xF_y-\partial_z\partial_yF_x.
\end{aligned}
$$

Each positive term cancels a negative term because the corresponding mixed [derivatives](../../../../../derivative.md) commute. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\nabla\cdot\mathbf G=\nabla\cdot(\nabla\times\mathbf F)=0}.
$$
