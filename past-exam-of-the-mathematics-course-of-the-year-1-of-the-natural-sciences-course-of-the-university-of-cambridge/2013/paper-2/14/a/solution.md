<h1 id="14/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [characteristic equation](../../../../../characteristic-equation-of-a-constant-coefficient-differential-equation.md) has roots zero and three, giving $y_h=C_1+C_2e^{3x}$. Since the forcing is quadratic and the [homogeneous solution](../../../../../homogeneous-solution.md) contains a constant, use the [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) trial $y_p=Ax^3+Bx^2+Cx$. Substitution gives

$$
y_p''-3y_p'=-9Ax^2+(6A-6B)x+2B-3C.
$$

Matching coefficients gives $A=-1/3$, $B=-1/3$, and $C=-8/9$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{y=C_1+C_2e^{3x}-\frac{x^3}{3}-\frac{x^2}{3}-\frac{8x}{9}}.
$$
