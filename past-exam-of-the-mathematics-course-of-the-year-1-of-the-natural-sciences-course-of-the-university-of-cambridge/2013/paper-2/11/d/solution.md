<h1 id="11/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Integrating the [probability density function](../../../../../probability-density-function.md) from its lower endpoint to $x$ gives the [cumulative distribution function](../../../../../cumulative-distribution-function.md)

$$
\boxed{F(x)=\begin{cases}0,&x\leq1,\\1-x^{-\alpha},&x>1.\end{cases}}
$$

It is continuous and strictly increasing on the support. Hence the unique [median](../../../../../median.md) $m$ satisfies $F(m)=1/2$, or $m^{-\alpha}=1/2$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{m=2^{1/\alpha}}.
$$
