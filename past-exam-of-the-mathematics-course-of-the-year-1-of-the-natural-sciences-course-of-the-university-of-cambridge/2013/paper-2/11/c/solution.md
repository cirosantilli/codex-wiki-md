<h1 id="11/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The second [moment](../../../../../moment.md) is $E[X^2]=\alpha\int_1^\infty x^{1-\alpha}\,dx$, finite exactly for $\alpha>2$, where it equals $\alpha/(\alpha-2)$. Subtracting the square of the [mean](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Var}(X)=\frac{\alpha}{\alpha-2}
-\frac{\alpha^2}{(\alpha-1)^2}
=\frac{\alpha}{(\alpha-1)^2(\alpha-2)},\qquad\alpha>2}.
$$

For $1<\alpha\leq2$, the [mean](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) exists but the [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) is infinite. For $0<\alpha\leq1$, the ordinary centered [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) is not defined because the [mean](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) is not finite. Thus there is no finite [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) for any $\alpha\leq2$.
