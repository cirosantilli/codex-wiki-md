<h1 id="11/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [probability density function](../../../../../probability-density-function.md) is nonnegative for $\alpha>0$. Its [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md) over the support is

$$
\int_1^\infty\alpha x^{-\alpha-1}\,dx
=\left[-x^{-\alpha}\right]_1^\infty=1,
$$

so it is **normalized for every $\alpha>0$**. This is the unit-threshold [Pareto distribution](../../../../../pareto-distribution.md).
