<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a [real number](../../../../../real-number.md) $x$, the [hyperbolic functions](../../../../../hyperbolic-function.md) satisfy $\cosh x>0$ and $1-\tanh^2x=\operatorname{sech}^2x$. Hence $\cosh x=1/\sqrt{1-u^2}$, and multiplying it by $\tanh x=u$ gives

$$
\boxed{\sinh x=\frac{u}{\sqrt{1-u^2}},\qquad-1<u<1}.
$$

The sign of the numerator preserves the sign of $\sinh x$.
