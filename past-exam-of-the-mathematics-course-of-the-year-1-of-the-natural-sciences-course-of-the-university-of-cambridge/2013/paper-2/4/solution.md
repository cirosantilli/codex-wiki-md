<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

The [gradient](../../../../gradient.md) is $\nabla\phi=(2xy+z,x^2,x)$, with value $(3,1,1)$ at the point. Normalize the direction to a [unit vector](../../../../unit-vector.md): $\hat{\mathbf s}=(2,0,1)/\sqrt5$. The [directional derivative](../../../../directional-derivative.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{D_{\hat{\mathbf s}}\phi=\nabla\phi\cdot\hat{\mathbf s}
=\frac{6+1}{\sqrt5}=\frac7{\sqrt5}}.
$$
