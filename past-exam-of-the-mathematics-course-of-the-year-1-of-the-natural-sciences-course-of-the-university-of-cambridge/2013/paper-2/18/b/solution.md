<h1 id="18/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For the specified [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md),

$$
F_y=2(y-x^2),\qquad F_x=-4x(y-x^2)-5x^4.
$$

The first equation $F_y=0$ forces $y=x^2$; substitution into $F_x=0$ then gives $x=0$, and hence $y=0$. It also satisfies the curve equation. Therefore the unique [singular point](../../../../../singular-point-of-an-algebraic-variety.md) is

$$
\boxed{(x,y)=(0,0)}.
$$
