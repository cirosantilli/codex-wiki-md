<h1 id="18/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For $x>0$, differentiate the two explicit branches:

$$
y_\pm'=x\left(2\pm\frac52\sqrt x\right),\qquad
 y_\pm''=2\pm\frac{15}{4}\sqrt x.
$$

The plus branch is strictly increasing and strictly [convex](../../../../../convex-function.md) for $x>0$. Its only zero is $x=0$, also its one-sided [global minimum](../../../../../global-minimum.md); it has no interior extremum or [inflection point](../../../../../inflection-point.md).

The minus branch rises initially, then has $y_-'=0$ at $\sqrt x=4/5$, changing from positive to negative. Its second [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) changes sign at $\sqrt x=8/15$. Its zeros follow from $y_-=x^2(1-\sqrt x)$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\begin{array}{c|c|c}
&y_+&y_-\\\hline
\text{zeros}&0&0,\ 1\\
\text{interior maximum}&\text{none}&16/25\\
\text{interior minimum}&\text{none}&\text{none}\\
\text{inflection}&\text{none}&64/225
\end{array}}
$$

The origin is a one-sided [local minimum](../../../../../local-minimum.md) of both graph branches, because their domain ends there; it is not an interior [critical point](../../../../../critical-point.md). The minus branch has no [global minimum](../../../../../global-minimum.md) since it tends to $-\infty$ as $x\to\infty$. Its maximum and inflection heights, useful for the sketch, are $256/3125$ and $28672/759375$, respectively.
