<h1 id="18/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Along a differentiable graph satisfying the implicit equation, the [multivariable chain rule](../../../../../multivariable-chain-rule.md) gives

$$
\frac{d}{dx}F(x,y(x))=F_x+F_y\frac{dy}{dx}=0.
$$

Thus, wherever $F_y\ne0$,

$$
\boxed{\frac{dy}{dx}=-\frac{F_x}{F_y}}.
$$

The [implicit function theorem](../../../../../implicit-function-theorem.md) supplies such a local graph under that nonvanishing condition. At a [singular point](../../../../../singular-point-of-an-algebraic-variety.md) both numerator and denominator vanish, so this division cannot determine its tangent.
