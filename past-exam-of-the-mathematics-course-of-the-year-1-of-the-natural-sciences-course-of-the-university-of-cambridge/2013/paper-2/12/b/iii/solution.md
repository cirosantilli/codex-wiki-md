<h1 id="12/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Direct multiplication gives $A^2=-I/4$. Therefore $A(-4A)=I=(-4A)A$, yielding

$$
\boxed{A^2=\begin{pmatrix}-1/4&0\\0&-1/4\end{pmatrix},\qquad
A^{-1}=-4A=\begin{pmatrix}0&-2\\2&0\end{pmatrix}}.
$$

The nonzero [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) ensures that this two-sided inverse is unique as a [matrix inverse](../../../../../../matrix-inverse-split.md).
