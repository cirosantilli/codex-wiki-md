<h1 id="12/b/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

Repeatedly using the quadratic identity gives

$$
\boxed{A^{2n}=\left(-\frac14\right)^nI,\qquad
A^{2n+1}=\left(-\frac14\right)^nA}.
$$

In entries these are $(-1)^n4^{-n}\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$ and $(-1)^n2^{-(2n+1)}\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\-1&0\end{pmatrix}$, respectively. This is the basic reduction for [functions of a two-dimensional antisymmetric matrix](../../../../../../functions-of-a-two-dimensional-antisymmetric-matrix.md).
