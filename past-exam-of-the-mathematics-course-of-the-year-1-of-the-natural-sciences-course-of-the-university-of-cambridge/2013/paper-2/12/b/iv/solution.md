<h1 id="12/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Multiply $A^2=-I/4$ by $A$ once more:

$$
\boxed{A^3=-\frac14A=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1/8\\1/8&0\end{pmatrix}}.
$$

No additional [diagonalization](../../../../../../diagonalization-of-a-matrix.md) is needed.
