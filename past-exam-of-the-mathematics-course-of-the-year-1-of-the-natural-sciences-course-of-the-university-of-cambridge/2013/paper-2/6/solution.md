<h1 id="6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

Put $h=x-\pi/4$. The [trigonometric identities](../../../../trigonometric-identity.md) give $\cos x\sin x=\tfrac12\sin2x=\tfrac12\cos2h$. Expanding [cosine](../../../../cosine.md) at zero gives

$$
\boxed{f(x)=\frac12-\left(x-\frac\pi4\right)^2
+O\left((x-\pi/4)^4\right)}.
$$

The linear and cubic terms vanish, so these are the first two nonzero [Taylor series](../../../../taylor-series.md) terms.
