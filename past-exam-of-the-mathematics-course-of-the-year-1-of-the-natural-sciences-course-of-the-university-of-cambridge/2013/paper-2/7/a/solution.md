<h1 id="7/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use the convention that $\theta$ is the [polar angle](../../../../../polar-angle.md) from positive $z$, and $\phi$ the [azimuth](../../../../../azimuth.md) from positive $x$. The radius is

$$
r=\sqrt{\frac3{16}+\frac9{16}+\frac94}=\sqrt3.
$$

Then $\cos\theta=z/r=\sqrt3/2$, while $\tan\phi=y/x=\sqrt3$ and both planar coordinates are positive. Thus the [spherical coordinates](../../../../../spherical-coordinate-system.md) are

$$
\boxed{(r,\theta,\phi)=\left(\sqrt3,\frac\pi6,\frac\pi3\right)}.
$$
