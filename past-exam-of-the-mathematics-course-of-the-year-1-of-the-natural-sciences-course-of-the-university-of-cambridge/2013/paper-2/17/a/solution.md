<h1 id="17/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For fixed amount of substance, take the [internal energy](../../../../../internal-energy-split.md) $U=U(S,V)$. The [first law of thermodynamics](../../../../../first-law-of-thermodynamics-split.md) identifies $T=U_S|_V$ and $-P=U_V|_S$. Equality of mixed second [derivatives](../../../../../derivative.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\left(\frac{\partial T}{\partial V}\right)_S
=-\left(\frac{\partial P}{\partial S}\right)_V}.
$$

For the [Helmholtz free energy](../../../../../helmholtz-free-energy.md), subtract the product differential $d(TS)$ from the [first law of thermodynamics](../../../../../first-law-of-thermodynamics-split.md) to obtain $dF=-S\,dT-P\,dV$. Its natural variables are $(T,V)$, so $F_T=-S$ and $F_V=-P$. Exactness therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\left(\frac{\partial S}{\partial V}\right)_T
=\left(\frac{\partial P}{\partial T}\right)_V}.
$$

For the [Gibbs free energy](../../../../../gibbs-free-energy.md), the [product rule](../../../../../product-rule.md) gives $dG=-S\,dT+V\,dP$. Thus $G_T=-S$, $G_P=V$, and

$$
\boxed{\left(\frac{\partial S}{\partial P}\right)_T
=-\left(\frac{\partial V}{\partial T}\right)_P}.
$$

For the [enthalpy](../../../../../enthalpy-split.md), $dH=T\,dS+V\,dP$, giving $H_S=T$, $H_P=V$ and

$$
\boxed{\left(\frac{\partial T}{\partial P}\right)_S
=\left(\frac{\partial V}{\partial S}\right)_P}.
$$

These are the four [Maxwell relations](../../../../../maxwell-relations.md), each following from exactness in the appropriate pair of natural variables.
