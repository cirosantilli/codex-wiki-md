<h1 id="7/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write the [partial fraction decomposition](../../../../../partial-fraction-decomposition.md) as $A/(x-3)+B/(x+7)$. After multiplying through, $7x+29=A(x+7)+B(x-3)$. Setting $x=3$ gives $A=5$, and setting $x=-7$ gives $B=2$, so

$$
\boxed{\frac{7x+29}{(x-3)(x+7)}=\frac5{x-3}+\frac2{x+7},\qquad x\ne3,-7.}
$$
