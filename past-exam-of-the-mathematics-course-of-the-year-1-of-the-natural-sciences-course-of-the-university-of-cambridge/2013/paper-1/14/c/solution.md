<h1 id="14/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Use $(0,1,1)$ as an anchor. Two directions in the required [plane](../../../../../plane.md) are

$$
\mathbf u=(2,0,2),\qquad \mathbf v=(4,1,0).
$$

Their [cross product](../../../../../cross-product.md) is $(-2,8,2)$, so a [normal vector](../../../../../normal-vector.md) is $\mathbf n=(-1,4,1)$. The [Cartesian equation of a plane](../../../../../cartesian-equation-of-a-plane.md) is

$$
\mathbf n\cdot[(x,y,z)-(0,1,1)]=0,
\qquad \boxed{-x+4y+z=5.}
$$

Direct substitution verifies all three given points. The independent direction [vectors](../../../../../vector.md) ensure the [plane](../../../../../plane.md) is uniquely determined.
