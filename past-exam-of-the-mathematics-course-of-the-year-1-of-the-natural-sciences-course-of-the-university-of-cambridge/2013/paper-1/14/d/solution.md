↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The axis intercepts of the [plane](../../../../../plane.md) are

$$
P=(-5,0,0),\qquad Q=(0,5/4,0),\qquad R=(0,0,5).
$$

Use two edge [vectors](../../../../../vector.md) from $P$:

$$
(Q-P)\times(R-P)
=(5,5/4,0)\times(5,0,5)=(25/4,-25,-25/4).
$$

The [triangle](../../../../../triangle.md) area is half this [cross product](../../../../../cross-product.md) magnitude:

$$
\boxed{\mathcal A=\frac12\frac{25}{4}\sqrt{1+16+1}
=\frac{75\sqrt2}{8}.}
$$
