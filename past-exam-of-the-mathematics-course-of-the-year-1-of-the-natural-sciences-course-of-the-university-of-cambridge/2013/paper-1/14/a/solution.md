<h1 id="14/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

If either [vector](../../../../../vector.md) is zero, both sides vanish. Otherwise let $\theta$ be the angle between them. The magnitudes of the [cross product](../../../../../cross-product.md) and [dot product](../../../../../dot-product.md) are described by

$$
|\mathbf a\times\mathbf b|=|\mathbf a|\,|\mathbf b|\sin\theta,
\qquad \mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b=|\mathbf a|\,|\mathbf b|\cos\theta.
$$

Squaring and using $\sin^2\theta=1-\cos^2\theta$ proves [Lagrange's identity](../../../../../lagrange-identity-for-the-cross-product.md):

$$
\boxed{|\mathbf a\times\mathbf b|^2
=|\mathbf a|^2|\mathbf b|^2-(\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b)^2.}
$$
