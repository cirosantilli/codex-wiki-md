<h1 id="13/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For the first-order [linear ordinary differential equation](../../../../../linear-ordinary-differential-equation-split.md), an [integrating factor](../../../../../integrating-factor.md) is

$$
I(x)=\exp\int\frac{4x}{1+x^2}dx=(1+x^2)^2.
$$

Multiplication converts the left side to a [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md):

$$
\frac d{dx}\big[(1+x^2)^2y\big]=\frac1{1+x^2}.
$$

Integrating gives $(1+x^2)^2y=\arctan x+C$. The initial value fixes $C=1$, so

$$
\boxed{y(x)=\frac{1+\arctan x}{(1+x^2)^2},\qquad x\in\mathbb R.}
$$
