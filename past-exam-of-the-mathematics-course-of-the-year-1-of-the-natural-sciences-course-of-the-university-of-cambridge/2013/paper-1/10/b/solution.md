<h1 id="10/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Subtract the lower curve from the upper curve on the intersection interval. The [definite integral](../../../../../definite-integral.md) is

$$
\mathcal A=\int_{-1}^1[(1-x^4)-(x^2-1)]dx
=2\int_0^1(2-x^2-x^4)dx
=\boxed{\frac{44}{15}}.
$$

The result is in square coordinate units.
