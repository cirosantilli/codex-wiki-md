<h1 id="5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Substitution into the [circle](../../../../../circle.md) gives $x^2\sec^2x=25$, or $x=5|\cos x|$ since $x\geq0$. Necessarily $x\leq5$, and each admissible $x$ fixes a unique $y=x\tan x$.

On $[0,\pi/2)$, $x-5\cos x$ is strictly increasing from a negative value to a positive value, giving one root. On $(\pi/2,3\pi/2)$, the equation is $x+5\cos x=0$. This [function](../../../../../function-split.md) is strictly convex, positive at the two interval ends and negative at $\pi$, giving exactly two roots.

There are none on the remaining possible interval $3\pi/2<x\leq5$. Put $\delta=x-3\pi/2>0$; then $\cos x=\sin\delta\leq\delta$, so

$$
x-5\cos x\geq\frac{3\pi}{2}-4\delta
\geq\frac{15\pi}{2}-20>0.
$$

Hence the number of simultaneous real solutions is **$3$**, as marked in the preceding graph.
