<h1 id="15/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For positive constant [mass density](../../../../../../density.md) $\rho_0$, reflection symmetry makes the horizontal coordinates of the [centre of mass](../../../../../../center-of-mass.md) zero. The total mass and the vertical first moment are

$$
M=\rho_0\frac{\pi ah^2}{2},\qquad
\int z\rho_0\,dV=\rho_0\int_0^h z(\pi az)dz
=\frac{\rho_0\pi ah^3}{3}.
$$

Their ratio gives

$$
\boxed{(\bar x,\bar y,\bar z)=(0,0,2h/3).}
$$

This is the [centre](../../../../../../center-geometry.md) of the liquid [volume](../../../../../../volume.md), rather than of the curved wall. The density cancels because it is uniform; the larger disks near the top place the [centre](../../../../../../center-geometry.md) above half-height.
