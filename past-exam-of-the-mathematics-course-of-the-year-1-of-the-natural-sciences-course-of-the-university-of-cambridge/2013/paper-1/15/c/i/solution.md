<h1 id="15/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The liquid occupies $0\leq z\leq h$ and $0\leq r\leq\sqrt{az}$. A horizontal slice is a disk of area $\pi az$. Integrating these areas gives

$$
\boxed{V=\int_0^h\pi az\,dz=\frac{\pi ah^2}{2}.}
$$

Equivalently, the [triple integral](../../../../../../triple-integral.md) is $\int_0^{2\pi}\int_0^h\int_0^{\sqrt{az}}r\,dr\,dz\,d\theta$, including the cylindrical [Jacobian determinant](../../../../../../jacobian-determinant.md) factor $r$.
