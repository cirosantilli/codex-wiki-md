<h1 id="15/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In [cylindrical coordinates](../../../../../cylindrical-coordinate-system.md), $x=r\cos\theta$ and $y=r\sin\theta$, so $x^2+y^2=r^2$. Thus the circular [elliptic paraboloid](../../../../../elliptic-paraboloid.md) has equation

$$
\boxed{az=r^2,\quad\text{or}\quad z=r^2/a.}
$$

At height $h\geq0$, the rim [radius](../../../../../radius.md) is $\sqrt{ah}$.
