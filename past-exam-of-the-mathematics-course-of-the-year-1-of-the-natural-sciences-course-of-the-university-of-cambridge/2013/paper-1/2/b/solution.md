<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The two [derivatives](../../../../../derivative.md) are $2x$ and $2(x-1)$. At the intersection their slopes are $1$ and $-1$, so their direction [vectors](../../../../../vector.md) $(1,1)$ and $(1,-1)$ have zero [dot product](../../../../../dot-product.md). Thus the [tangent lines](../../../../../tangent-line.md) are perpendicular and their angle is

$$
\boxed{\frac\pi2\ \text{radians}=90^\circ.}
$$
