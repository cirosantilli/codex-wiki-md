↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [law of total probability](../../../../../../law-of-total-probability.md) gives

$$
\mathbb P(D)=\frac15\alpha+\frac25\frac\alpha2+\frac25\frac\alpha4=\frac\alpha2.
$$

For $\alpha>0$, [Bayes' theorem](../../../../../../bayes-theorem.md) therefore yields

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(A\mid D)=\frac{\alpha/5}{\alpha/2}=\frac25.}
$$

At the allowed endpoint $\alpha=0$, no bulb is defective: $\mathbb P(D)=0$. This [conditional probability](../../../../../../conditional-probability.md) is then **undefined**, rather than $2/5$. The value $2/5$ is its limit as $\alpha\to0^+$, but does not define conditioning on the impossible event within the stated discrete model.
