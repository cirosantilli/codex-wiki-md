↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $A,B,C$ denote the mutually exclusive supplier events and $D$ the defective-bulb event. Their [probabilities](../../../../../probability.md) and conditional defect rates are

$$
\begin{array}{c|ccc}
 &A&B&C\\\hline
\mathbb P(\text{supplier})&1/5&2/5&2/5\\
\mathbb P(D\mid\text{supplier})&\alpha&\alpha/2&\alpha/4.
\end{array}
$$

These supplier events partition the possible bulbs. The following calculations use the [law of total probability](../../../../../law-of-total-probability.md) and [Bayes' theorem](../../../../../bayes-theorem.md) with that partition.
