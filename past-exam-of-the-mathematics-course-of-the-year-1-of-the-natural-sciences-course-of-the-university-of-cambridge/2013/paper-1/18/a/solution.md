<h1 id="18/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

All size-six subsets are equally likely under uniform [sampling without replacement](../../../../../sampling-without-replacement.md). There are $\binom{12}{6}$ subsets in total, and $\binom63\binom63$ contain the required three members from each group. Thus the [hypergeometric distribution](../../../../../hypergeometric-distribution.md) gives

$$
\boxed{P=\frac{\binom63^2}{\binom{12}{6}}
=\frac{400}{924}=\frac{100}{231}\approx0.4329.}
$$
