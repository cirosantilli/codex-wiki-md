<h1 id="12/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The quadratic [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) factors as

$$
z^2-(3+i)z+(2+i)=(z-1)(z-2-i),
$$

because the two roots sum to $3+i$ and multiply to $2+i$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{z=1,\qquad z=2+i.}
$$
