<h1 id="12/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Real coefficients imply that the conjugate $-i$ is also a [polynomial root](../../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md). Write the remaining root as $r$. Then

$$
p(z)=2(z-2)(z-r)(z^2+1).
$$

The coefficient of $z$ in this product is $-2(2+r)$. That coefficient is zero in the specified [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md), so $r=-2$. Hence the two other roots are

$$
\boxed{-i,\qquad -2.}
$$

As a check, the full [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) is $2(z^2-4)(z^2+1)=2z^4-6z^2-8$, corresponding to $a=0$, $b=-6$ and $c=-8$.
