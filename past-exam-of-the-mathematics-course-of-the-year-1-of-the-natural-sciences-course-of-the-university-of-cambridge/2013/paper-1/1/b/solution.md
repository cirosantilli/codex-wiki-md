↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The defining equation gives $y^2=2-(2/3)x^2\leq2$. Equality is attainable at $x=0$, with the positive branch giving

$$
\boxed{y_{\max}=\sqrt2\quad\text{at }(0,\sqrt2).}
$$
