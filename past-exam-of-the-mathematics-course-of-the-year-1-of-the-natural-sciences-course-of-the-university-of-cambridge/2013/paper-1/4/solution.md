<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

At $x=3$, the point on the [parabola](../../../../parabola.md) is $(3,8)$ and the [tangent line](../../../../tangent-line.md) has slope $f'(3)=6$. The [normal line to a plane curve](../../../../normal-line-to-a-plane-curve.md) therefore has slope $-1/6$. Its equation is

$$
y-8=-\frac16(x-3),\qquad y=-\frac x6+\frac{17}{2}.
$$

Thus the requested results are

$$
\boxed{\text{gradient }-\frac16,\qquad\text{vertical-axis intercept }(0,17/2).}
$$
