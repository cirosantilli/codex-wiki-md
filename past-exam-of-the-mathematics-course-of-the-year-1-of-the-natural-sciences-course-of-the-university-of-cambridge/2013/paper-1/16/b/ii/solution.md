<h1 id="16/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Choose $F(s)=G(s)=\tfrac12\sin s$. The two travelling profiles then add by the [sine](../../../../../../sine.md) addition identity:

$$
\boxed{y(x,t)=\frac12\sin(2x+5t)+\frac12\sin(2x-5t)
=\sin2x\cos5t.}
$$

This is a [standing wave](../../../../../../standing-wave.md). At $x=0$ and $x=\pi$ it vanishes for all $t$. At $t=0$ its displacement is $\sin2x$, and its time [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) $-5\sin2x\sin5t$ is zero. Also $y_{tt}=-25y$ and $y_{xx}=-4y$, directly checking the original equation.
