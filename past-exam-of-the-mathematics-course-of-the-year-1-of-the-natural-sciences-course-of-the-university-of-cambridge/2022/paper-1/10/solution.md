↑ **Parent:** [10](../10.md)

The [dot product](../../../../dot-product.md) is

$$
\boxed{(a,a+1)\cdot(a+1,a)=2a(a+1)=2a^2+2a.}
$$

Both [vectors](../../../../vector.md) have squared [Euclidean norm](../../../../euclidean-norm.md) $a^2+(a+1)^2=2a^2+2a+1$. Under the given condition $a^2+a=1/2$, their [dot product](../../../../dot-product.md) is $1$ and each squared [Euclidean norm](../../../../euclidean-norm.md) is $2$. Hence

$$
\cos\theta=\frac1{\sqrt2\sqrt2}=\frac12,\qquad\boxed{\theta=\pi/3=60^\circ.}
$$
