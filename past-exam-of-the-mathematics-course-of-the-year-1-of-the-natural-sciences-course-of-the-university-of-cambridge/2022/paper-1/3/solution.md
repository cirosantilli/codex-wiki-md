<h1 id="3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

The domain excludes $x=0$. Rearranging the [inequality](../../../../inequality-mathematics.md) gives

$$
\frac{x+1}{x}\geq0.
$$

A sign check on the [intervals](../../../../interval-mathematics.md) separated by $-1$ and $0$ shows that the ratio is nonnegative when $x\leq-1$ or $x>0$. The point $-1$ is allowed because it makes the numerator zero. Therefore

$$
\boxed{x\in(-\infty,-1]\cup(0,\infty).}
$$
