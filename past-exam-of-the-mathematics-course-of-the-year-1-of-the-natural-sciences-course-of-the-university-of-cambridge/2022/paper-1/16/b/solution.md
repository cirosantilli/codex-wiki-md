<h1 id="16/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Put $N=6-2e^{-2}$. The [function graph](../../../../../graph-of-a-function.md) is symmetric about the vertical axis, zero outside $[-2,2]$, and has its unique maximum at $x=0$ with height $2/N$. On $0<x<2$, it decreases as $(1+e^{-x})/N$; the negative side is its reflection. The one-sided interior endpoint values are $(1+e^{-2})/N$, so the [probability density function](../../../../../probability-density-function.md) jumps to zero at both ends of its support. It also has a corner at its central maximum because of the [absolute value](../../../../../absolute-value.md) in the exponent.
