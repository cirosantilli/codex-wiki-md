<h1 id="16/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Let $F$ be the [cumulative distribution function](../../../../../cumulative-distribution-function.md) of $X$. Normalisation of the proposed [probability density function](../../../../../probability-density-function.md) requires $M=\int_{-2}^2F(y)\,dy$. The [integrated cumulative distribution function on a bounded interval](../../../../../integrated-cumulative-distribution-function-on-a-bounded-interval.md) identity gives

$$
M=2-\mathbb E[X]=\boxed{2}.
$$

For a direct symmetry proof, the continuous symmetric [probability distribution](../../../../../probability-distribution-split.md) satisfies $F(-y)=1-F(y)$. Hence

$$
\int_{-2}^2F(y)\,dy=\int_0^2[F(y)+F(-y)]\,dy=\int_0^2 1\,dy=2.
$$

In particular $g(y)=F(y)/2$ is nonnegative and has total [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md) one over its stated support.
