<h1 id="6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

The [product rule](../../../../product-rule.md) gives

$$
y'=e^{-x}(3x^2-x^3)=x^2e^{-x}(3-x).
$$

For $x>0$, the [derivative](../../../../derivative.md) is positive before $3$ and negative after $3$. Also $y(0)=0$ and $x^3e^{-x}\to0$ as $x\to\infty$. Thus the global maximum on the requested range is

$$
\boxed{x=3,\qquad y_{\max}=27e^{-3}.}
$$
