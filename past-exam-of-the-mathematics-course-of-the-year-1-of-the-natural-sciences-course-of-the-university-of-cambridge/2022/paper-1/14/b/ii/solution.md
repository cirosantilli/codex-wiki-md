<h1 id="14/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Apply the [product rule](../../../../../../product-rule.md) and the [chain rule](../../../../../../chain-rule.md) once more to each expression in part (i). The [second derivative chain rule](../../../../../../second-derivative-chain-rule.md) gives

$$
\boxed{f_{xx}=2f_u+2y^2f_v+4x^2f_{uu}+8x^2y^2f_{uv}+4x^2y^4f_{vv},}
$$



$$
\boxed{f_{yy}=2f_u+2x^2f_v+4y^2f_{uu}+8x^2y^2f_{uv}+4x^4y^2f_{vv},}
$$

and

$$
\boxed{f_{xy}=4xyf_{uu}+4xy(x^2+y^2)f_{uv}+4x^3y^3f_{vv}+4xyf_v.}
$$

For example, the coefficient of $f_{uv}$ in the mixed [partial derivative](../../../../../../partial-derivative.md) is $u_xv_y+v_xu_y=4x^3y+4xy^3$, while the final term is $v_{xy}f_v=4xyf_v$. The symmetry $f_{uv}=f_{vu}$ is used in combining the mixed terms under the usual twice-continuously-differentiable interpretation.
