<h1 id="17/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Start with the supplied [Gaussian integral](../../../../../../gaussian-integral.md) $I(0)=\sqrt\pi/2$ and part (i)'s $I(1)=1/2$. Repeated use of the [Gaussian moment recurrence](../../../../../../gaussian-moment-recurrence.md) gives

$$
\boxed{I(2)=\frac{\sqrt\pi}{4},\quad I(3)=\frac12,\quad I(4)=\frac{3\sqrt\pi}{8},\quad I(5)=1,\quad I(6)=\frac{15\sqrt\pi}{16}.}
$$

The even indices inherit the $\sqrt\pi$ factor from $I(0)$, while the odd indices inherit rational values from $I(1)$.
