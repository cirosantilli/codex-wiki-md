<h1 id="8/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [8](../8.md)

Use the [substitution rule](../../../../integration-by-substitution.md) with $u=\sqrt x$, for which $du=dx/(2\sqrt x)$ on $x>0$. The [integral](../../../../integral-split.md) becomes $\int e^u\,du$, giving the [antiderivative](../../../../antiderivative.md)

$$
\boxed{e^{\sqrt x}+C.}
$$
