<h1 id="18/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [vector](../../../../../vector.md) $(1,-1,0)^T$ is an [eigenvector](../../../../../eigenvector.md) with [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $4$. The complementary subspace of [vectors](../../../../../vector.md) with first two components equal is preserved by $A$: a vector $(s,s,t)^T$ maps to $(4s+2t,4s+2t,4s+2t)^T$. There, the [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) equations reduce to the $2\times2$ [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) $\begin{pmatrix}4&2\\4&2\end{pmatrix}$, whose [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) is $\lambda(\lambda-6)$. For $\lambda=6$, $t=s$; for $\lambda=0$, $t=-2s$.

Normalising by the [Euclidean norm](../../../../../euclidean-norm.md), a complete [orthonormal basis](../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) of [eigenvectors](../../../../../eigenvector.md) is

$$
\boxed{\begin{array}{c|c}
\lambda&\text{unit eigenvector}\\\hline
0&\mathbf e_0=(1,1,-2)^T/\sqrt6\\
4&\mathbf e_4=(1,-1,0)^T/\sqrt2\\
6&\mathbf e_6=(1,1,1)^T/\sqrt3
\end{array}}
$$

Sign changes of any of these [eigenvectors](../../../../../eigenvector.md) are equally valid. The [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) also check part (a): their sum is $10$ and the sum of their squares is $52$.
