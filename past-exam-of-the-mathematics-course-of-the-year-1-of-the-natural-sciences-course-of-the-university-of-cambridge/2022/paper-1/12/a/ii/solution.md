<h1 id="12/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The inequalities describing the region are $0\leq y\leq1$ and $2y\leq x\leq2$. It is the closed [triangle](../../../../../../triangle.md) bounded by $y=0$, $x=2$ and $x=2y$, with vertices

$$
\boxed{(0,0),\qquad(2,0),\qquad(2,1).}
$$

A horizontal slice runs from the sloping edge $x=2y$ to the vertical edge $x=2$.
