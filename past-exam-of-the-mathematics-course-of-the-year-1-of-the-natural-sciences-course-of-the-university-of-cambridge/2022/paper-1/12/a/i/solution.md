<h1 id="12/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The stated $x$ bounds depend on $y$, so $y$ must be held fixed while the [integral](../../../../../../integral-split.md) over $x$ is performed. The intended [iterated integral](../../../../../../iterated-integral.md) is

$$
\boxed{\int_0^1\left(\int_{2y}^2x^2y^2\,dx\right)dy.}
$$

The original PDF places the two sets of bounds beside the opposite integral signs relative to the printed $dx\,dy$. That placement cannot be read literally as a conventional nested [integral](../../../../../../integral-split.md); the dependence of the lower $x$ bound on $y$, and the explicit instruction to integrate over $x$ first, specify the intended region unambiguously.
