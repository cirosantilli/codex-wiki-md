<h1 id="12/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

For a vertical slice through the [triangle](../../../../../../triangle.md), $0\leq x\leq2$ and $0\leq y\leq x/2$. Reversing the order by [Fubini's theorem](../../../../../../fubini-s-theorem.md) gives

$$
\int_0^2\left(\int_0^{x/2}x^2y^2\,dy\right)dx=\int_0^2\frac{x^5}{24}\,dx=\boxed{\frac49}.
$$

The agreement checks that both orders describe the same region.
