<h1 id="12/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

With $y$ held fixed, the inner [antiderivative](../../../../../../antiderivative.md) gives

$$
\int_{2y}^2x^2y^2\,dx=\frac{y^2}{3}[x^3]_{2y}^2=\frac83(y^2-y^5).
$$

The remaining [integral](../../../../../../integral-split.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{\frac83\int_0^1(y^2-y^5)\,dy=\frac83\left(\frac13-\frac16\right)=\frac49.}
$$
