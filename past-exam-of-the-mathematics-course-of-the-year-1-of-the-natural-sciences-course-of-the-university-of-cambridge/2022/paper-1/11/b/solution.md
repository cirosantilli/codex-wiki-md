<h1 id="11/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [complex modulus](../../../../../complex-modulus.md) $|z-(-1-i)|$ is the [Euclidean distance](../../../../../euclidean-distance.md) from $z$ to $-1-i$. Thus the locus is

$$
\boxed{(x+1)^2+(y+1)^2=64,}
$$

a [circle](../../../../../circle.md) with centre $(-1,-1)$ and [radius](../../../../../radius.md) $8$. Its extreme horizontal coordinates are $-9$ and $7$, and its extreme vertical coordinates are also $-9$ and $7$.
