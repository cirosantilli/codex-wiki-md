<h1 id="11/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Write $z=x+iy$ and use the [complex conjugate](../../../../../../complex-conjugate.md) $z^*=x-iy$. Then

$$
u=\frac32(x+iy)+\frac12(x-iy)=2x+iy.
$$

Thus the [real part](../../../../../../real-part.md) is stretched by a factor of two while the [imaginary part](../../../../../../imaginary-part.md) is unchanged. If $u=X+iY$, substitution into the [circle](../../../../../../circle.md) equation gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{(X+2)^2}{16^2}+\frac{(Y+1)^2}{8^2}=1.}
$$

This is an [ellipse](../../../../../../ellipse.md) with centre $(-2,-1)$, horizontal semiaxis $16$ and vertical semiaxis $8$. It is the second panel in the figure in part (b).
