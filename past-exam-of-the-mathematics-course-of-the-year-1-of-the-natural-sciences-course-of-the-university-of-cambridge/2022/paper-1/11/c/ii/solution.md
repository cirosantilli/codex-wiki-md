↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Adding $4+3i$ is a [translation](../../../../../../translation-geometry.md) by $(4,3)$ in the [complex plane](../../../../../../complex-plane.md). The [ellipse](../../../../../../ellipse.md) of part (i) therefore keeps its semiaxes and axis directions, with new centre $(2,2)$. Writing $v=X+iY$, its equation is

$$
\boxed{\frac{(X-2)^2}{16^2}+\frac{(Y-2)^2}{8^2}=1.}
$$

The third panel in the figure in part (b) shows the translated [ellipse](../../../../../../ellipse.md).
