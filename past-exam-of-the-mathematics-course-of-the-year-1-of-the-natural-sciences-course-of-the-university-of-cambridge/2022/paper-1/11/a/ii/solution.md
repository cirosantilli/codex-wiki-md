<h1 id="11/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The inner power takes the values $r_k=e^{-(\pi/2+2\pi k)}$. For each such real exponent, all values of the outer [complex exponentiation](../../../../../../complex-exponentiation.md) are obtained from all [complex logarithm](../../../../../../complex-logarithm.md) values of its base $i$:

$$
i^{(i^i)}=\exp\left[i\left(\frac\pi2+2\pi m\right)e^{-(\pi/2+2\pi k)}\right],\qquad k,m\in\mathbb Z.
$$

Writing $\Theta_{k,m}=(\pi/2+2\pi m)e^{-(\pi/2+2\pi k)}$, the answer is

$$
\boxed{\Re(i^{(i^i)})=\cos\Theta_{k,m},\qquad\Im(i^{(i^i)})=\sin\Theta_{k,m},\qquad k,m\in\mathbb Z.}
$$

All values lie on the [unit circle](../../../../../../complex-unit-circle.md). The principal choice is $k=m=0$, giving $\cos[(\pi/2)e^{-\pi/2}]+i\sin[(\pi/2)e^{-\pi/2}]$. In particular the two nestings in parts (i) and (ii) are different: [complex exponentiation is not associative](../../../../../../complex-exponentiation-is-not-associative.md).
