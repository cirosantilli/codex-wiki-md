<h1 id="11/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use the full set of [complex logarithm](../../../../../../complex-logarithm.md) values of $i$:

$$
\log i=i\left(\frac\pi2+2\pi k\right),\qquad k\in\mathbb Z.
$$

By the definition of [complex exponentiation](../../../../../../complex-exponentiation.md), the inner power can therefore take any value $i^i=\exp[-(\pi/2+2\pi k)]$, a positive real number. Its [complex logarithm](../../../../../../complex-logarithm.md) values are $-(\pi/2+2\pi k)+2\pi im$, where $m\in\mathbb Z$. Thus

$$
(i^i)^i=\exp\left[-i\left(\frac\pi2+2\pi k\right)-2\pi m\right]=-ie^{-2\pi m}.
$$

All possible [real parts](../../../../../../real-part.md) and [imaginary parts](../../../../../../imaginary-part.md) are consequently

$$
\boxed{\Re((i^i)^i)=0,\qquad\Im((i^i)^i)=-e^{-2\pi m},\quad m\in\mathbb Z.}
$$

Choosing the principal [complex logarithm](../../../../../../complex-logarithm.md) at both stages gives the particular value $-i$. The independent logarithm choices are needed because the question asks for all possible values.
