<h1 id="14/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $N_L$ count successful lemon seeds. By [independence](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) it has a [binomial distribution](../../../../../../binomial-distribution.md) with five trials and success [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) $y$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(N_L=2)=\binom52y^2(1-y)^3=10y^2(1-y)^3}.
$$
