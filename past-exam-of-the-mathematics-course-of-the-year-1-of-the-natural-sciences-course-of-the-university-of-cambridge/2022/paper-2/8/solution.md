<h1 id="8/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [8](../8.md)

Use the [Maclaurin series](../../../../maclaurin-series.md) $\sin3x=3x-\tfrac92x^3+O(x^5)$ and $\cos x=1-\tfrac12x^2+O(x^4)$. Multiplication gives

$$
\boxed{f(x)=3x-6x^3+O(x^5)}.
$$

The same terms follow from the two sine harmonics in the [Fourier series](../../../../fourier-series-split.md) found above.
