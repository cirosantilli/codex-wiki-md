The [product-to-sum formula](../../../../product-to-sum-formula.md) gives the finite [Fourier series](../../../../fourier-series-split.md)

$$
\boxed{f(x)=\frac12\sin4x+\frac12\sin2x}.
$$

Thus the only nonzero [Fourier coefficients](../../../../fourier-coefficient.md) are $b_2=b_4=1/2$.
