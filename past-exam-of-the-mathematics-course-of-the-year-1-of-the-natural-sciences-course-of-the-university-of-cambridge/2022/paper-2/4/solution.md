<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

The coefficient [matrix](../../../../matrix.md) has [determinant](../../../../determinant.md) $-1$, so it is an [invertible matrix](../../../../invertible-matrix.md). Its [homogeneous linear system](../../../../homogeneous-linear-system.md) therefore has only the zero solution:

$$
\boxed{x=y=z=0}.
$$

One can also subtract the first equation from the second to get $z=0$, then subtract the first from the third to get $y+2z=0$, giving $y=0$ and finally $x=0$. Thus **there are no nonzero solutions**.
