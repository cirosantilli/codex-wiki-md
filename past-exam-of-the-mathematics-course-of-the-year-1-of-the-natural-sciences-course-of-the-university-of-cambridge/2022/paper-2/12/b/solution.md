↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

At $A=(1/2,1/4)$, the [gradient](../../../../../gradient.md) is $(-3/64,-9/32)^T$. The position [vector](../../../../../vector.md) has length $\sqrt5/4$, so its normalized direction is $(2,1)^T/\sqrt5$. Hence the [directional derivative](../../../../../directional-derivative.md) is

$$
\boxed{D_{\hat{\mathbf a}}f(A)
=\frac{(2,1)}{\sqrt5}\cdot\left(-\frac3{64},-\frac9{32}\right)
=-\frac{3\sqrt5}{40}}.
$$
