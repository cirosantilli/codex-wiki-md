<h1 id="12/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For a [stationary point](../../../../../stationary-point.md), the two factors from part (a) must vanish. If $y=0$, the second equation gives $x(x^2-1)=0$; if $x=0$, the first gives $y(y^2-1)=0$. Thus the axis points are $(0,0),(\pm1,0),(0,\pm1)$.

If both coordinates are nonzero, solve $3x^2+y^2=1$ and $x^2+3y^2=1$, giving $x^2=y^2=1/4$. These four points complete the list of nine [stationary points](../../../../../stationary-point.md).

For classification, the [Hessian matrix](../../../../../hessian-matrix.md) is

$$
H=\begin{pmatrix}6xy&3x^2+3y^2-1\\3x^2+3y^2-1&6xy\end{pmatrix}.
$$

At $(0,0)$ its [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are $1,-1$, and at the four unit-axis points they are $2,-2$. These five points are [saddle points of a scalar function](../../../../../saddle-point-of-a-scalar-function.md), all with value zero.

At $(1/2,1/2)$ and $(-1/2,-1/2)$ the [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are one and two, so both are strict [local minima](../../../../../local-minimum.md) of value $-1/8$. At $(1/2,-1/2)$ and $(-1/2,1/2)$ they are minus one and minus two, so both are strict [local maxima](../../../../../local-maximum.md) of value $1/8$. This follows from the [second-derivative test](../../../../../second-derivative-test.md). These extrema are local: the [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) is unbounded above and below on the full [plane](../../../../../plane.md).
