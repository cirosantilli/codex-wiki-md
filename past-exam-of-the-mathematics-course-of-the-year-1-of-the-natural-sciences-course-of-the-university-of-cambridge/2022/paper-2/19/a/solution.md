<h1 id="19/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

At a regular constrained extremum, every [tangent vector](../../../../../tangent-vector-split.md) to the [level set](../../../../../level-set.md) $g=A$ gives zero [directional derivative](../../../../../directional-derivative.md) of $f$. Thus $\nabla f$ is perpendicular to the tangent [plane](../../../../../plane.md), and must be a scalar multiple of its nonzero [normal vector](../../../../../normal-vector.md) $\nabla g$. The [Lagrange multiplier](../../../../../lagrange-multiplier.md) equations are

$$
\boxed{\nabla f=\lambda\nabla g,\qquad g=A}.
$$

One solves these four equations for $x,y,z,\lambda$, evaluates $f$ at the candidates, and classifies or compares the values as required. Points where $\nabla g=0$, and any boundary or limiting cases of the feasible domain, require separate checks because the regularity argument does not cover them.
