<h1 id="16/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Both patches project onto the unit [disk](../../../../../../disk-mathematics.md) $D$ in the $xy$-[plane](../../../../../../plane.md). Their outward [oriented surface elements](../../../../../../oriented-surface-element.md) are

$$
d\mathbf S_+=\left(\frac{x}{\rho},\frac{y}{\rho},1\right)^Tdx\,dy,
\qquad d\mathbf S_-=(2x,2y,-1)^Tdx\,dy.
$$

The cone's apex has zero [area](../../../../../../surface-area.md) and does not affect the [surface integral](../../../../../../surface-integral.md). For $k=0$, the [vector field](../../../../../../vector-field.md) is $(y,x,1)^T$, so

$$
\Phi_+=\iint_D\left(\frac{2xy}{\rho}+1\right)dx\,dy=\pi,
\qquad
\Phi_-=\iint_D(4xy-1)dx\,dy=-\pi.
$$

The terms involving $xy$ vanish by reflection symmetry of the [disk](../../../../../../disk-mathematics.md). Thus the total outward [flux](../../../../../../flux.md) is

$$
\boxed{\Phi=0}.
$$

This calculation evaluates the two [surface integrals](../../../../../../surface-integral.md) directly.
