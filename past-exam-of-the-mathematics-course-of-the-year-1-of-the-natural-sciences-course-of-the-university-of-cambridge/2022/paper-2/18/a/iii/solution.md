The standard [Fourier series](../../../../../../fourier-series-split.md) is

$$
f(x)\sim\frac{a_0}{2}+\sum_{n=1}^\infty(a_n\cos nx+b_n\sin nx),
$$

with [Fourier coefficients](../../../../../../fourier-coefficient.md)

$$
\boxed{a_n=\frac1\pi\int_{-\pi}^\pi f(x)\cos nx\,dx\quad(n\geq0),\qquad
b_n=\frac1\pi\int_{-\pi}^\pi f(x)\sin nx\,dx\quad(n\geq1)}.
$$

These follow by taking [inner products](../../../../../../inner-product-split.md) with the individual [basis vectors](../../../../../../basis-vector.md) and using their [orthogonality](../../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) and squared [norms](../../../../../../norm.md).
