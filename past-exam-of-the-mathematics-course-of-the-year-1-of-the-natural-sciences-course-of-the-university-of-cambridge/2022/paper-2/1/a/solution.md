<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Expanding the [cross product](../../../../../cross-product.md) componentwise gives

$$
(1,2,3)^T\times(3,2,1)^T
=(2-6,\ 9-1,\ 2-6)^T
=\boxed{(-4,8,-4)^T}.
$$

Its [dot product](../../../../../dot-product.md) with each original [vector](../../../../../vector.md) is zero, as required.
