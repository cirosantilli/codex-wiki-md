↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

First $(1,-1,0)^T\times(0,0,1)^T=(-1,-1,0)^T$. Taking the remaining [cross product](../../../../../cross-product.md) gives

$$
(1,2,0)^T\times(-1,-1,0)^T=\boxed{(0,0,1)^T}.
$$

The order of the two [cross products](../../../../../cross-product.md) matters because the operation is not associative.
