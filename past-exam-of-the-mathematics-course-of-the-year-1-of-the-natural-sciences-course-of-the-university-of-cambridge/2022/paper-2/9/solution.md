<h1 id="9/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [9](../9.md)

Apply [integration by parts](../../../../integration-by-parts.md) with $u=\log x$ and $dv=x\,dx$. Since $du=dx/x$ and $v=x^2/2$,

$$
\int x\log x\,dx=\frac{x^2}{2}\log x-\frac12\int x\,dx
=\boxed{\frac{x^2}{2}\log x-\frac{x^2}{4}+C},\qquad x>0.
$$

The original PDF specifies this [integral](../../../../integral-split.md); the local OCR replaces the entire question by unrelated text.
