<h1 id="11/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Take the [cross product](../../../../../../cross-product.md) with $\mathbf c$. Since $\mathbf c\times\mathbf c=0$, the equation implies

$$
\mathbf x\times\mathbf c=\mathbf a\times\mathbf c,
\qquad(\mathbf x-\mathbf a)\times\mathbf c=0.
$$

As $\mathbf c\ne0$, the displacement is parallel to $\mathbf c$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\mathbf x=\mathbf a+\lambda\mathbf c,\qquad\lambda\in\mathbb R}.
$$

This is a necessary condition; substitution must still determine which points on this [straight line](../../../../../../straight-line.md) satisfy the full equation.
