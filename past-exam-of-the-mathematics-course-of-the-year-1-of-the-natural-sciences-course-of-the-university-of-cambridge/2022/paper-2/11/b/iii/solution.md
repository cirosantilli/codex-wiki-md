<h1 id="11/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

In the nonsingular case the unique solution is

$$
\boxed{\mathbf x=\mathbf a+\frac{\mathbf b\cdot\mathbf a}{1-\mathbf b\cdot\mathbf c}\,\mathbf c,
\qquad \mathbf b\cdot\mathbf c\ne1}.
$$

Geometrically this is a single selected point of the [straight line](../../../../../../straight-line.md) through $\mathbf a$ parallel to $\mathbf c$.

When $\mathbf b\cdot\mathbf c=1$ and $\mathbf b\cdot\mathbf a=0$, the solution set is that entire [straight line](../../../../../../straight-line.md), $\mathbf a+\lambda\mathbf c$. The remaining case has empty solution set. In [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) terms the equation is $(I-\mathbf c\mathbf b^T)\mathbf x=\mathbf a$, a [rank-one matrix update](../../../../../../rank-one-matrix-update.md) of the [identity matrix](../../../../../../identity-matrix.md).
