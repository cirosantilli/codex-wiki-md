<h1 id="15/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [characteristic equation](../../../../../characteristic-equation-of-a-constant-coefficient-differential-equation.md) of the associated [homogeneous linear differential equation](../../../../../homogeneous-linear-differential-equation.md) is $m^2+7m+12=(m+3)(m+4)=0$. A constant [particular integral](../../../../../particular-solution.md) is $1/2$, so

$$
y=\frac12+Ae^{-3x}+Be^{-4x}.
$$

The first [boundary condition](../../../../../boundary-condition.md) gives $A+B=-1/2$. The second gives $Ae^{-1}+Be^{-4/3}=-e^{-1}/2$. Eliminating $A$ yields $B(e^{-4/3}-e^{-1})=0$, hence $B=0$ and $A=-1/2$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{y(x)=\frac{1-e^{-3x}}2,\qquad y(1)=\frac{1-e^{-3}}2}.
$$
