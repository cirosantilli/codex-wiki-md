↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Here $C=0$ as well, but the branch through $5\pi/6$ is the other inverse-sine branch:

$$
\boxed{y(x)=\pi-\arcsin x,\qquad -1<x<1}.
$$

Its [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) is $-1/\sqrt{1-x^2}$, and both the sign of $\tan y$ and the original equation agree with this choice.
