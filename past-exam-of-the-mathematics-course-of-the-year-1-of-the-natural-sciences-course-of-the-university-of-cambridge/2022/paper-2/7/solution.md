<h1 id="7/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [7](../7.md)

Let $V$ be the enclosed region with piecewise smooth closed boundary $S$, oriented by its outward [unit normal](../../../../unit-normal.md) $\mathbf n$. For a continuously [differentiable](../../../../differentiable-function.md) [vector field](../../../../vector-field.md) on a neighbourhood of $V$, the [divergence theorem](../../../../divergence-theorem.md) states

$$
\boxed{\oiint_S\mathbf F\cdot\mathbf n\,dS
=\iiint_V\nabla\cdot\mathbf F\,dV}.
$$

The outward [flux integral](../../../../flux-integral.md) equals the [volume integral](../../../../volume-integral.md) of the [divergence](../../../../divergence.md).
