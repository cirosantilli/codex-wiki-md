<h1 id="13/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $\mathbf r(t)=(t^2,t)^T$, the [tangent vector](../../../../../../tangent-vector-split.md) is $(2t,1)^T$ and $\mathbf F(\mathbf r(t))=(t^3,t^4+t)^T$. Thus

$$
\int_0^1(2t^4+t^4+t)\,dt=\frac35+\frac12=\boxed{\frac{11}{10}}.
$$

The unequal values for the same endpoints already rule out a [conservative vector field](../../../../../../conservative-vector-field.md).
