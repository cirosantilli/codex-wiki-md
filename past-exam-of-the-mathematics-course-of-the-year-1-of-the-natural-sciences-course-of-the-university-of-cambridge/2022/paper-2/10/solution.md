<h1 id="10/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [10](../10.md)

First $f_x=2xy^3$ and $f_y=3x^2y^2$. Differentiating again gives the four second-order [partial derivatives](../../../../partial-derivative.md):

$$
\boxed{f_{xx}=2y^3,\qquad f_{xy}=6xy^2,\qquad f_{yx}=6xy^2,\qquad f_{yy}=6x^2y}.
$$

The two [mixed partial derivatives](../../../../mixed-partial-derivative.md) agree, as expected for this smooth [polynomial](../../../../polynomial-split.md).
