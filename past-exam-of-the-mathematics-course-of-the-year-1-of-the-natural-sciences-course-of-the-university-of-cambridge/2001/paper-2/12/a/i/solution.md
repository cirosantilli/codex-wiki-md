<h1 id="12/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Using the standard [sine](../../../../../../sine.md) [limit](../../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md) $\sin t/t\to1$, with angles in radians,

$$
\boxed{\lim_{x\to0}\frac{\sin2x}{x}=2\lim_{x\to0}\frac{\sin2x}{2x}=2.}
$$

Thus the two-sided [limit](../../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md) exists and is finite.
