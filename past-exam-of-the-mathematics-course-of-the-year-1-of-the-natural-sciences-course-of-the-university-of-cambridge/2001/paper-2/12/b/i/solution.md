↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [series](../../../../../../series-mathematics.md) $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}u_n$ is a [convergent series](../../../../../../convergent-series.md) if its [partial sums](../../../../../../partial-sum.md) $S_N=\sum_{n=1}^Nu_n$ have a finite [limit](../../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md) $S$ as $N\to\infty$. The number $S$ is the sum of the [series](../../../../../../series-mathematics.md). Explicitly, for every $\varepsilon>0$ there is an integer $N_0$ such that $|S_N-S|<\varepsilon$ whenever $N\geq N_0$. The condition $u_n\to0$ alone is insufficient: the [harmonic series](../../../../../../harmonic-series.md) has terms tending to zero but diverges.
