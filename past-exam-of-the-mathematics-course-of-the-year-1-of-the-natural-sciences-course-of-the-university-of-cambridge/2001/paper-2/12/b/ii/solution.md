↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [series](../../../../../../series-mathematics.md) $\sum u_n$ has [absolute convergence](../../../../../../absolute-convergence.md) if $\sum|u_n|$ is a [convergent series](../../../../../../convergent-series.md), meaning its nonnegative [partial sums](../../../../../../partial-sum.md) have a finite [limit](../../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md). [Absolute convergence](../../../../../../absolute-convergence.md) implies convergence of the original real or complex [series](../../../../../../series-mathematics.md). The converse fails: the [alternating harmonic series](../../../../../../alternating-harmonic-series.md) converges, while its absolute-value [series](../../../../../../series-mathematics.md) is the divergent [harmonic series](../../../../../../harmonic-series.md).
