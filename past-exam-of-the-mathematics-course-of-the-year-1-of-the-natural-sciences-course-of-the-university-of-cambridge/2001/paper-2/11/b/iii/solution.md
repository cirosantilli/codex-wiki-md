<h1 id="11/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

After a red first draw, one red remains among six counters. The multiplication rule for [conditional probability](../../../../../../conditional-probability.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(E_1\cap E_2)=\mathbb P(E_1)\mathbb P(E_2\mid E_1)=\frac27\frac16=\frac1{21}.}
$$
