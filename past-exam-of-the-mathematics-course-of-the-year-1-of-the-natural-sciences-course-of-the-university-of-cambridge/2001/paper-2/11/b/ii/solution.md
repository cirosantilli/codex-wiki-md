<h1 id="11/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Use the [law of total probability](../../../../../../law-of-total-probability.md) according to the first draw. Without replacement, the second-draw red [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) is $1/6$ after a red and $2/6$ after a green. Thus

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(E_2)=\frac27\frac16+\frac57\frac26=\frac27.}
$$

Equivalently, each counter has the same [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) of occupying the second position as the first. Equal marginal [probabilities](../../../../../../probability.md) do not make the two draw events [independent](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md).
