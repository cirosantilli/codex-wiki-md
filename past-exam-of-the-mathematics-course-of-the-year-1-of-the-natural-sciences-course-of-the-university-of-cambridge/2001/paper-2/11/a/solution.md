<h1 id="11/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

With replacement, successive draws are [independent](../../../../../independent-random-variables.md), with red [probability](../../../../../probability.md) $2/7$ and green [probability](../../../../../probability.md) $5/7$. The first red on draw $n$ requires $n-1$ greens followed by one red. Hence

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(N=n)=\left(\frac57\right)^{n-1}\frac27,\qquad n=1,2,\ldots.}
$$

This is a [geometric distribution](../../../../../geometric-distribution.md). Its total [probability](../../../../../probability.md) is one because the [geometric series](../../../../../geometric-series.md) sums to $(2/7)/(1-5/7)=1$.
