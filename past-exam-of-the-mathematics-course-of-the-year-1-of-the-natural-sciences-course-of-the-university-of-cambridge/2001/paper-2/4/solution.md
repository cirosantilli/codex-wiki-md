<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

At an ordinary point, an order-$n$ [linear ordinary differential equation](../../../../linear-ordinary-differential-equation-split.md) has $n$ arbitrary constants in its general solution. Determining them uniquely requires $n$ independent, consistent scalar conditions. Prescribing $y,y',\ldots,y^{(n-1)}$ at one ordinary point gives uniqueness under the standard continuous-coefficient assumptions. Conditions at different endpoints need an additional nonsingularity check: for example, $y''+y=0$, $y(0)=y(\pi)=0$ leaves every multiple of $\sin x$ possible. Thus counting [boundary conditions](../../../../boundary-condition.md) alone does not guarantee uniqueness for a [boundary value problem](../../../../boundary-value-problem-split.md).
