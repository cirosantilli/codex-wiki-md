<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Now the [characteristic equation](../../../../../characteristic-equation-of-a-constant-coefficient-differential-equation.md) is $(m+1)^2=0$, with a [repeated root](../../../../../multiple-root.md), so the [complementary function](../../../../../homogeneous-solution.md) is $(C_1+C_2x)e^{-x}$. For the polynomial forcing, substitute $ax^2+bx+c$ to obtain

$$
ax^2+(4a+b)x+(2a+2b+c)=x^2.
$$

Thus $a=1$, $b=-4$, $c=6$. For the forcing $2e^{-x}$, both $e^{-x}$ and $xe^{-x}$ already occur in the [complementary function](../../../../../homogeneous-solution.md); the [resonant exponential particular solution](../../../../../resonant-exponential-particular-solution.md) therefore starts with $Kx^2e^{-x}$. The identity

$$
\left(\frac{d^2}{dx^2}+2\frac d{dx}+1\right)(e^{-x}v)=e^{-x}v''
$$

gives $K=1$. The resulting general solution is

$$
\boxed{y=(C_1+C_2x+x^2)e^{-x}+x^2-4x+6.}
$$
