<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Recognize the left-hand side as a [product rule](../../../../../product-rule.md) [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md), and use $\tfrac12\sin2x=\sin x\cos x$:

$$
\frac{d}{dx}(y\sin x)=\sin x\cos x,\qquad y\sin x=\frac12\sin^2x+C.
$$

At $x=\pi/6$, both $y\sin x$ and $\tfrac12\sin^2x$ equal $1/8$, so $C=0$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{y(x)=\frac12\sin x.}
$$

Dividing by $\sin x$ first obtains this solution on $(0,\pi)$, the interval containing the [initial condition](../../../../../initial-condition.md). The explicit expression is smooth on the entire real line and satisfies the original, undivided [differential equation](../../../../../differential-equation-split.md) even where $\sin x=0$.
