Here the vertical bars denote [determinants](../../../../../determinant.md). Since $B=B^{-1}$, taking [determinants](../../../../../determinant.md) gives $(\det B)^2=1$. The condition $\det B>0$ selects $\det B=1$. By the multiplicative property of the [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md),

$$
\boxed{\det A=\frac{\det(AB)}{\det B}=\frac{-2}{1}=-2.}
$$

The hypotheses determine this [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md), while allowing many possible [matrices](../../../../../matrix.md) $A$ and $B$.
