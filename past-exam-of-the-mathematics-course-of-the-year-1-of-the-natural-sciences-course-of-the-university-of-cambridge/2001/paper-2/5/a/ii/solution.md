<h1 id="5/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For the curved path $r(t)=(t,t^2,t^3)$, $r'(t)=(1,2t,3t^2)$. The [vector field](../../../../../../vector-field.md) along this [parametric curve](../../../../../../parametric-curve.md) is

$$
v(r(t))=(t^2e^t,\ e^t+2t^{11},\ 3t^{10}).
$$

Its [dot product](../../../../../../dot-product.md) with the tangent is $(t^2+2t)e^t+13t^{12}$. Therefore the direct [line integral](../../../../../../line-integral.md) is

$$
\boxed{\int_Cv\cdot dr=\int_0^1\bigl[(t^2+2t)e^t+13t^{12}\bigr]\,dt=\left[t^2e^t+t^{13}\right]_0^1=e+1.}
$$
