<h1 id="5/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Parametrize the [straight line](../../../../../../straight-line.md) by $r(t)=(t,t,t)$, $0\leq t\leq1$, with $r'(t)=(1,1,1)$. Substituting into the [vector field](../../../../../../vector-field.md) and taking the [dot product](../../../../../../dot-product.md) gives

$$
v(r(t))\cdot r'(t)=(t+1)e^t+5t^4.
$$

Hence the direct [line integral](../../../../../../line-integral.md) is

$$
\boxed{\int_Cv\cdot dr=\int_0^1\bigl[(t+1)e^t+5t^4\bigr]\,dt=\left[te^t+t^5\right]_0^1=e+1.}
$$
