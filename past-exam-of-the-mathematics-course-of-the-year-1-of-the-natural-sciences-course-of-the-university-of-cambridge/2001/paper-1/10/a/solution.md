<h1 id="10/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use the [Einstein summation convention](../../../../../einstein-notation.md), with repeated component indices summed. The following three expressions are listed separately below. For cyclic invariance of the [trace](../../../../../matrix-trace.md),

$$
\operatorname{tr}(ABC)=a_{ij}b_{jk}c_{ki}=c_{ki}a_{ij}b_{jk}=\operatorname{tr}(CAB),
$$

where the middle equality commutes scalar entries, not the [matrices](../../../../../matrix.md) themselves. If $A$ is invertible, this gives

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{tr}(ABA^{-1})=\operatorname{tr}(BA^{-1}A)=\operatorname{tr}B.}
$$

This is [similarity invariance of trace](../../../../../similarity-invariance-of-trace.md).
