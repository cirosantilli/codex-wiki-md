<h1 id="12/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The triangular [probability density function](../../../../../probability-density-function.md) is normalized because $2\int_0^1(1-x)dx=1$. It is [even](../../../../../even-function.md), so the mean integrand is [odd](../../../../../odd-function.md) and $\mu=0$. Its [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) is consequently its second [moment](../../../../../moment.md):

$$
\boxed{\mu=0,\qquad\sigma^2=2\int_0^1x^2(1-x)dx=2\left(\frac13-\frac14\right)=\frac16.}
$$
