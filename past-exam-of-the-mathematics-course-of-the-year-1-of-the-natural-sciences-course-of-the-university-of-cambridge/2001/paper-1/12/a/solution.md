<h1 id="12/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a [probability density function](../../../../../probability-density-function.md) $P$, the [mean](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) and [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) are

$$
\boxed{\mu=\int_{-\infty}^\infty xP(x)dx,\qquad \sigma^2=\int_{-\infty}^\infty(x-\mu)^2P(x)dx=\mathbb E[X^2]-\mu^2.}
$$

These formulas assume the required [moments](../../../../../moment.md) are finite; finiteness of the second moment ensures a finite mean as well.
