<h1 id="12/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The standard [normal density](../../../../../../normal-density.md) is [even](../../../../../../even-function.md), so $x^3P(x)$ is [odd](../../../../../../odd-function.md). Since [Gaussian decay dominates every power](../../../../../../gaussian-decay-dominates-every-power.md), the integrand is absolutely integrable. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[X^3]=\int_{-\infty}^\infty x^3P(x)dx=0.}
$$
