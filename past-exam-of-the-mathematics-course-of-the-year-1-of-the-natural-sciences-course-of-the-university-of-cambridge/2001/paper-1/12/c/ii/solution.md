<h1 id="12/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For the standard [normal density](../../../../../../normal-density.md), $P'(x)=-xP(x)$. Using [integration by parts](../../../../../../integration-by-parts.md),

$$
\mathbb E[X^4]=-\int_{-\infty}^\infty x^3P'(x)dx=-[x^3P(x)]_{-\infty}^\infty+3\int_{-\infty}^\infty x^2P(x)dx.
$$

The boundary term vanishes because [Gaussian decay dominates every power](../../../../../../gaussian-decay-dominates-every-power.md). Since the given [mean](../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) is zero and [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md) is one, $\mathbb E[X^2]=1$, yielding the [Gaussian fourth moment](../../../../../../gaussian-fourth-moment.md)

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[X^4]=3.}
$$
