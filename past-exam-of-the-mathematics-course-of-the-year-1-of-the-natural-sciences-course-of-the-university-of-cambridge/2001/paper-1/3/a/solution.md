<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Put $c=\cos\theta$, $s=\sin\theta$. The [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) is $(c-\lambda)^2-s^2$, giving

$$
\boxed{\lambda_+=c+s,\qquad\lambda_-=c-s.}
$$

When $s=0$, these [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) coincide and $A=cI=\pm I$.
