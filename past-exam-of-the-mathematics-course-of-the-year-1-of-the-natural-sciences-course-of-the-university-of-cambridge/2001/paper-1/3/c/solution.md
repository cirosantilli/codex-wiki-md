<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

If $Av=\lambda v$, then $A^2v=\lambda^2v$. Alternatively, direct [matrix multiplication](../../../../../matrix-multiplication.md) gives

$$
B=\begin{pmatrix}1&\sin2\theta\\\sin2\theta&1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Thus the normalized [eigenvectors](../../../../../eigenvector.md) $v_+,v_-$ from part (b) have respective [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md)

$$
\boxed{(c+s)^2=1+\sin2\theta,\qquad(c-s)^2=1-\sin2\theta.}
$$

If $\sin2\theta=0$, then $B=I$, and the whole two-dimensional space is its [eigenspace](../../../../../eigenspace.md). This degeneracy includes cases where the two [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) of $A$ were distinct but opposite.
