<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Subtract the two position [vectors](../../../../../vector.md) to obtain the direction $\mathbf b-\mathbf a=(-3,-1,3)$. The [affine line in a vector space](../../../../../affine-line-in-a-vector-space.md) through the points is therefore

$$
\boxed{\mathbf r=(2,-1,-2)+t(-3,-1,3),\qquad t\in\mathbb R.}
$$

The values $t=0,1$ recover the two specified points.
