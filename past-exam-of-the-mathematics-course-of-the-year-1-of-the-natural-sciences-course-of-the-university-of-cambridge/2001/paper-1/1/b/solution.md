↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A [normal vector](../../../../../normal-vector.md) to the [plane](../../../../../plane.md) spanned by $\mathbf a,\mathbf b$ is their [cross product](../../../../../cross-product.md):

$$
\mathbf a\times\mathbf b=(-5,0,-5),\qquad |\mathbf a\times\mathbf b|=5\sqrt2.
$$

Dividing by this [Euclidean norm](../../../../../euclidean-norm.md) gives the two possible choices of [unit normal](../../../../../unit-normal.md),

$$
\boxed{\mathbf n=\pm\frac{(1,0,1)}{\sqrt2}.}
$$
