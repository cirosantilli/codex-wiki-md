<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Use the [normal vector](../../../../../normal-vector.md) $\mathbf N=(-8,-3,-9)$ found from the edge [vectors](../../../../../vector.md), rather than the [normal vector](../../../../../normal-vector.md) to the origin-based [linear span](../../../../../linear-span.md) in part (b). The required [plane](../../../../../plane.md) satisfies $\mathbf N\cdot(\mathbf r-\mathbf a)=0$. Since $\mathbf N\cdot\mathbf a=5$,

$$
\boxed{-8x-3y-9z=5.}
$$

After dividing by $|\mathbf N|=\sqrt{154}$, its equation has a [unit normal](../../../../../unit-normal.md). Its perpendicular distance from the origin is thus $\boxed{5/\sqrt{154}}$.
