↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The two edge [vectors](../../../../../vector.md) based at $A$ are $\mathbf b-\mathbf a=(-3,-1,3)$ and $\mathbf c-\mathbf a=(-3,2,2)$. Their [cross product](../../../../../cross-product.md) is $(-8,-3,-9)$, with squared [Euclidean norm](../../../../../euclidean-norm.md) $64+9+81=154$. The [triangle](../../../../../triangle.md) occupies half the corresponding [parallelogram](../../../../../parallelogram.md), so

$$
\boxed{\text{area}=\frac12| (\mathbf b-\mathbf a)\times(\mathbf c-\mathbf a)|=\frac{\sqrt{154}}2.}
$$
