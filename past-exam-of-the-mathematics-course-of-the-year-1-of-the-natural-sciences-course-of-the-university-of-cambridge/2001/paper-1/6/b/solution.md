<h1 id="6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Differentiate the ellipsoidal parametrization. Its outward [vector surface element](../../../../../vector-area-element.md) is

$$
\mathbf r_\theta\times\mathbf r_\phi=(bc\sin^2\theta\cos\phi,\ ac\sin^2\theta\sin\phi,\ ab\sin\theta\cos\theta).
$$

For $a,b,c>0$, its [dot product](../../../../../dot-product.md) with $\mathbf r$ simplifies to $abc\sin\theta$. Thus the requested direct [surface integral](../../../../../surface-integral.md) is

$$
\boxed{\int_S\mathbf r\cdot d\mathbf S=abc\int_0^{2\pi}\int_0^\pi\sin\theta\,d\theta\,d\phi=4\pi abc.}
$$

As a check, the [divergence](../../../../../divergence.md) of $\mathbf r$ is three, and the [ellipsoid](../../../../../ellipsoid.md) has [volume](../../../../../volume.md) $4\pi abc/3$, so the [divergence theorem](../../../../../divergence-theorem.md) gives the same value.
