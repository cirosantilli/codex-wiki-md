<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Take the outward [orientation of a surface](../../../../../orientation-of-a-surface.md). The [divergence](../../../../../divergence.md) is $\nabla\cdot\mathbf F=y^2+z^2+x^2$, so the [divergence theorem](../../../../../divergence-theorem.md) converts the closed [surface flux](../../../../../flux-integral.md) into an integral over the box:

$$
\int_S\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf S=\int_{-a}^a\int_{-b}^b\int_{-c}^c(x^2+y^2+z^2)\,dz\,dy\,dx.
$$

Each term separates; for example the $x^2$ term is $(2a^3/3)(2b)(2c)$. Summing gives

$$
\boxed{\int_S\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf S=\frac{8abc}3(a^2+b^2+c^2).}
$$

Here $a,b,c>0$ are the box half-lengths. Reversing [orientation of a surface](../../../../../orientation-of-a-surface.md) would negate the result.
