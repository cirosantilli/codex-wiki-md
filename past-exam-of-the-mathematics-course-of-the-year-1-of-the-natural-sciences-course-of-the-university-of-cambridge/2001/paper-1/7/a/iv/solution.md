↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The first [matrix product](../../../../../../matrix-product.md) gives the column $(37,37,37)^T$. Its product with the final row is an [outer product](../../../../../../outer-product.md), not a scalar:

$$
\boxed{\begin{pmatrix}37\\37\\37\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}-2&0&2\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}-74&0&74\\-74&0&74\\-74&0&74\end{pmatrix}.}
$$
