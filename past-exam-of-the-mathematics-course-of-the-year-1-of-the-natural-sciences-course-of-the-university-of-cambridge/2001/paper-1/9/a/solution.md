<h1 id="9/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Integrate the [geometric series](../../../../../geometric-series.md) for $1/(1+x)$ from zero to $x$. The first three nonzero terms are

$$
\boxed{\ln(1+x)=x-\frac{x^2}2+\frac{x^3}3+O(x^4).}
$$

The resulting [Taylor series](../../../../../taylor-series.md) converges for $|x|<1$.
