<h1 id="9/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For fixed real $a\ne0$, write the square root as $|a|\sqrt{1+x^2/a^2}$ and use the [binomial series](../../../../../binomial-series.md) with exponent $-1/2$:

$$
\boxed{\frac{x}{\sqrt{x^2+a^2}}=\frac x{|a|}-\frac{x^3}{2|a|^3}+\frac{3x^5}{8|a|^5}+O(x^7).}
$$

The [absolute values](../../../../../absolute-value.md) are necessary for negative $a$. This [Taylor series](../../../../../taylor-series.md) is valid for $|x|<|a|$. If $a=0$, the quotient is $x/|x|$ for $x\ne0$; it has no continuous extension at zero and therefore **has no Taylor expansion about zero**.
