<h1 id="11/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Multiply the numerator and denominator by the [complex conjugate](../../../../../../complex-conjugate.md) $1+i$: $(1+i)/(1-i)=(1+i)^2/2=i$, whose square is $-1$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Re}=-1,\qquad\operatorname{Im}=0.}
$$
