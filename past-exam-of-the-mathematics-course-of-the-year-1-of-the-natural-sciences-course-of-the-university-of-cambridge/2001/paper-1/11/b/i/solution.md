↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

In [polar form](../../../../../../polar-form-of-a-complex-number.md), a cube root of one has [modulus](../../../../../../modulus.md) one and [argument](../../../../../../argument-complex-analysis.md) $2\pi k/3$. The three [roots of unity](../../../../../../root-of-unity.md) are

$$
\boxed{1,\qquad\frac{-1+i\sqrt3}2,\qquad\frac{-1-i\sqrt3}2.}
$$

They are equally spaced on the [unit circle](../../../../../../complex-unit-circle.md).
