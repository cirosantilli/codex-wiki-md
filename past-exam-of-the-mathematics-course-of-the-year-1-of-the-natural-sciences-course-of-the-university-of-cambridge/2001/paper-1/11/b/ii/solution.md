<h1 id="11/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Write $-8=8e^{i(\pi+2\pi k)}$. Its cube roots have [modulus](../../../../../../modulus.md) two and [arguments](../../../../../../argument-complex-analysis.md) $(\pi+2\pi k)/3$, for $k=0,1,2$. Thus

$$
\boxed{1+i\sqrt3,\qquad -2,\qquad 1-i\sqrt3.}
$$

Each cube equals $-8$, and a cubic [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) has no further distinct roots.
