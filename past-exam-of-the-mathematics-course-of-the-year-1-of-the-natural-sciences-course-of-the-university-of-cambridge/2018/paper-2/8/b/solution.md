<h1 id="8/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use the outward [unit normal](../../../../../unit-normal.md) on the unit [sphere](../../../../../sphere.md) and the convention that $\theta$ is measured from the positive vertical axis. Then $\mathbf F\cdot\mathbf n=\sin\theta$, while the [spherical coordinates](../../../../../spherical-coordinate-system.md) [area element](../../../../../area-element-of-a-surface.md) is $dS=\sin\theta\,d\theta\,d\phi$. The [flux](../../../../../flux.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{\int_S\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf S=\int_0^{2\pi}\int_0^\pi\sin^2\theta\,d\theta\,d\phi=\pi^2.}
$$
