<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For $r>0$, the [partial derivatives](../../../../../partial-derivative.md) of radius are $r_x=x/r$, $r_y=y/r$ and $r_z=z/r$. Applying the [chain rule](../../../../../chain-rule.md) to the outer [sine](../../../../../sine.md) and then the radial profile gives the [Cartesian coordinates](../../../../../cartesian-coordinate-system.md) of the [gradient](../../../../../gradient.md):

$$
\boxed{\nabla\sin[f(r)]=\frac{f'(r)\cos[f(r)]}{r}(x,y,z).}
$$

At the origin these coordinate formulas require a differentiable radial extension; differentiability of $f$ as a one-variable profile alone does not guarantee it.
