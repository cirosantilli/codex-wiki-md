<h1 id="20/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The first [boundary condition](../../../../../../boundary-condition.md) requires $u(x,0)=ax^2+d=0$ for all $x$, so $a=d=0$. Since $u_y=bx+2cy$, the second [boundary condition](../../../../../../boundary-condition.md) gives $b=0$. The [partial differential equation](../../../../../../partial-differential-equation-split.md) then reads $2a-2c=1$, fixing $c=-1/2$. Thus

$$
\boxed{a=0,\quad b=0,\quad c=-\frac12,\quad d=0,\qquad u(x,y)=-\frac{y^2}{2}.}
$$
