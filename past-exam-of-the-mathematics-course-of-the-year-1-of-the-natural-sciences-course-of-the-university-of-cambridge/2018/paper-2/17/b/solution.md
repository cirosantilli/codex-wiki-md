<h1 id="17/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Multiplication of a [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) by a column [vector](../../../../../vector.md) forms the linear combination of its columns with coefficients from that [vector](../../../../../vector.md). Here

$$
\boxed{\mathbf c_1=(1,1,2)^{\mathsf T},\quad\mathbf c_2=(\mu,-1,-2)^{\mathsf T},\quad\mathbf c_3=(0,3,\mu)^{\mathsf T},}
$$

so $A\mathbf r=x\mathbf c_1+y\mathbf c_2+z\mathbf c_3$. Equating this expression with $\mathbf b$ reproduces the original three [linear equations](../../../../../linear-equation.md).
