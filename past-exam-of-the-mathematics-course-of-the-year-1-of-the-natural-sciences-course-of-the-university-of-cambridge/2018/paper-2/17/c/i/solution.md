<h1 id="17/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $\mathbf b=0$, a nonzero coefficient [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) $(x,y,z)$ with $x\mathbf c_1+y\mathbf c_2+z\mathbf c_3=0$ is precisely a linear dependence of the three columns. Three [vectors](../../../../../../vector.md) in three dimensions are linearly dependent exactly when the [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) of their column [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md), or their [scalar triple product](../../../../../../scalar-triple-product.md), is zero. Thus

$$
\boxed{\text{a nontrivial solution requires }[\mathbf c_1,\mathbf c_2,\mathbf c_3]=0.}
$$

For this square homogeneous [linear system](../../../../../../system-of-linear-equations.md), the condition is also sufficient.
