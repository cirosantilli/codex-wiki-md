<h1 id="14/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Divide the relevant joint [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) by the marginal stopping [probability](../../../../../../probability.md). By [Bayes' theorem](../../../../../../bayes-theorem.md),

$$
\boxed{P(D\mid x_1)=\frac{(1-p)d_1}{pg_1+(1-p)d_1}.}
$$

The denominator is positive under the stated assumptions.
