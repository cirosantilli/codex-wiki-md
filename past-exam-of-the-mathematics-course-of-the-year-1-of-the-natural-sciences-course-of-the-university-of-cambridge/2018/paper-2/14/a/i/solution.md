↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Distinguish the production type as well as the stopping position. The [sample space](../../../../../../sample-space.md) has the six outcomes

$$
\boxed{\Omega=\{G\cap x_0,G\cap x_1,G\cap x_2,D\cap x_0,D\cap x_1,D\cap x_2\}.}
$$

Here $x_k$ in an event means $X=x_k$. Their joint [probabilities](../../../../../../probability.md) are $pg_k$ and $(1-p)d_k$, respectively. The conditional stopping laws obey $\sum_{k=0}^2g_k=\sum_{k=0}^2d_k=1$; with the stated strict positivity all six outcomes are possible.
