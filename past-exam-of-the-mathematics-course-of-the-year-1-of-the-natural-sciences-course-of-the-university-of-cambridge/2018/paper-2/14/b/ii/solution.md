<h1 id="14/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The event includes stopping positions zero through $k$. Applying [Bayes' theorem](../../../../../../bayes-theorem.md) to their joint and marginal [probabilities](../../../../../../probability.md) gives

$$
\boxed{P(G\mid X\le x_k)=\frac{p\sum_{j=0}^kg_j}{p\sum_{j=0}^kg_j+(1-p)\sum_{j=0}^kd_j}.}
$$

This [conditional probability](../../../../../../conditional-probability.md) is defined whenever its conditioning event has positive [probability](../../../../../../probability.md). At $k=n$, it reduces to $p$.
