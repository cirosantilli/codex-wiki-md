<h1 id="15/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Under $y=xu$, the [product rule](../../../../../product-rule.md) gives $y'=u+xu'$ and $y''=2u'+xu''$. Substitute these into the original equation:

$$
x(2u'+xu'')-(u+xu')+u=x^2u''+xu'=x(xu''+u')=0.
$$

Since $x>0$, this is precisely $xu''+u'=0$, or $(xu')'=0$. Integrating twice gives $xu'=C_1$ and $u=C_1\ln x+C_2$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{y(x)=x(C_1\ln x+C_2),\qquad x>0.}
$$

The two independent [solutions of a differential equation](../../../../../solution-of-a-differential-equation.md) supply the [general solution](../../../../../general-solution.md) of this second-order [linear ordinary differential equation](../../../../../linear-ordinary-differential-equation-split.md).
