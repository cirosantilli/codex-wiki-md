<h1 id="15/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For the [homogeneous linear differential equation](../../../../../../homogeneous-linear-differential-equation.md), the [characteristic equation](../../../../../../characteristic-equation-of-a-constant-coefficient-differential-equation.md) is $r^2-2r+2=0$, with [polynomial roots](../../../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md) $1\pm i$. Its [homogeneous solution](../../../../../../homogeneous-solution.md) is therefore $e^x(A\cos x+B\sin x)$.

Use the [method of undetermined coefficients](../../../../../../method-of-undetermined-coefficients.md) for a [particular solution](../../../../../../particular-solution.md) $y_p=ax^2+bx+c$. Substitution gives

$$
2ax^2+(2b-4a)x+(2a-2b+2c)=2x^2.
$$

Equating coefficients yields $a=1$, $b=2$ and $c=1$. Hence the [general solution](../../../../../../general-solution.md) is

$$
\boxed{y=e^x(A\cos x+B\sin x)+x^2+2x+1.}
$$
