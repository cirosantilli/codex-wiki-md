<h1 id="15/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) factors as $(r+1)(r+2)$, so the [homogeneous solution](../../../../../../homogeneous-solution.md) is $Ae^{-x}+Be^{-2x}$. The forcing $e^{-x}$ already occurs in that solution, so a constant multiple of $e^{-x}$ cannot be a [particular solution](../../../../../../particular-solution.md). Use $y_p=Cxe^{-x}$ instead. Its [derivatives](../../../../../../derivative.md) are $C(1-x)e^{-x}$ and $C(x-2)e^{-x}$, so substitution gives $Ce^{-x}$ and fixes $C=1$. Thus

$$
\boxed{y=Ae^{-x}+Be^{-2x}+xe^{-x}.}
$$

The extra factor of $x$ accounts for the repeated forcing exponent in the [method of undetermined coefficients](../../../../../../method-of-undetermined-coefficients.md).
