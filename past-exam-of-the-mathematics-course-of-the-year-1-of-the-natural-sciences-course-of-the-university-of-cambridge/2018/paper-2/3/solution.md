<h1 id="3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

The [vectors](../../../../vector.md) $(1,1)^{\mathsf T}$ and $(1,-1)^{\mathsf T}$ are [eigenvectors](../../../../eigenvector.md): multiplication adds or subtracts the two entries in a row. Their [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md) are therefore

$$
\boxed{\lambda_+=e^\phi+e^{-\phi}=2\cosh\phi,\qquad\lambda_-=e^\phi-e^{-\phi}=2\sinh\phi.}
$$

The second [eigenvalue](../../../../eigenvalue.md) may be positive, zero or negative as the real parameter changes.
