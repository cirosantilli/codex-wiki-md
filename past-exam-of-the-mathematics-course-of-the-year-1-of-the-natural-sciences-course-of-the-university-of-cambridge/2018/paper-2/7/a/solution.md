↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [integrating factor](../../../../../integrating-factor.md) for this [linear ordinary differential equation](../../../../../linear-ordinary-differential-equation-split.md) is $e^x$. Multiplication gives $(e^xy)'=1$, so $e^xy=x+C$. The [initial condition](../../../../../initial-condition.md) sets $C=0$, giving

$$
\boxed{y(x)=xe^{-x}.}
$$
