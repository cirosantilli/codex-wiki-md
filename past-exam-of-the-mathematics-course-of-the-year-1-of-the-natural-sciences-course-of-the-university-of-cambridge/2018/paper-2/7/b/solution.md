<h1 id="7/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Near the [initial condition](../../../../../initial-condition.md) $y=1$, divide by $y^2$ and separate variables: $y^{-2}\,dy=-x^2\,dx$. Integration gives $-1/y=-x^3/3+C$; the initial value fixes $C=-1$. Thus the solution of the [separable differential equation](../../../../../separable-differential-equation.md) is

$$
\boxed{y(x)=\frac1{1+x^3/3}=\frac3{x^3+3},\qquad x>-\sqrt[3]3.}
$$

The stated interval is the maximal connected interval containing zero: the solution diverges at its left endpoint. The constant solution $y=0$ does not satisfy the [initial condition](../../../../../initial-condition.md).
