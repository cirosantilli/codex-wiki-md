↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [expected value](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) follows by [integration by parts](../../../../../integration-by-parts.md):

$$
E[X]=\frac12\int_0^\pi x\sin x\,dx
=\frac12[-x\cos x+\sin x]_0^\pi=\boxed{\frac\pi2.}
$$

The same result follows from symmetry of the [probability density function](../../../../../probability-density-function.md) about $\pi/2$.
