<h1 id="9/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [9](../9.md)

The [gradient](../../../../gradient.md) components are

$$
u_x=x^2(3-2x^2)e^{-x^2-y^2},\qquad u_y=-2yx^3e^{-x^2-y^2}.
$$

Away from the vertical axis, $x\ne0$, so $u_y=0$ forces $y=0$. The other [stationary point](../../../../stationary-point.md) equation then gives $x^2=3/2$. Therefore the two requested points are

$$
\boxed{\left(\sqrt{\frac32},0\right),\qquad\left(-\sqrt{\frac32},0\right).}
$$

Every point with $x=0$ also has zero [gradient](../../../../gradient.md), but those points are explicitly excluded.
