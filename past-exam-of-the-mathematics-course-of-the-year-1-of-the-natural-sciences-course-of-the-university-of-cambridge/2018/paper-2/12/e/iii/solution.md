<h1 id="12/e/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

At $(1,2)$, the [gradient](../../../../../../gradient.md) is $(4,2)$. The requested direction must be normalized to the [unit vector](../../../../../../unit-vector.md) $(1,1)/\sqrt2$. The [directional derivative](../../../../../../directional-derivative.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{D_{(1,1)/\sqrt2}f(1,2)=\nabla f(1,2)\cdot\frac{(1,1)}{\sqrt2}=3\sqrt2.}
$$

Using $(1,1)$ without normalization would instead measure change per unit of a differently scaled path parameter.
