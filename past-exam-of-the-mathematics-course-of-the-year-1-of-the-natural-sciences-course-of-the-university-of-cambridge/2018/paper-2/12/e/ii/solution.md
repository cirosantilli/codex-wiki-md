<h1 id="12/e/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

In the positive quadrant, both components of the [gradient](../../../../../../gradient.md) are positive. Along $y=x$ the arrows point along $(1,1)$; near the horizontal axis they point mainly upwards, and near the vertical axis mainly rightwards. The [level sets](../../../../../../level-set.md) are the [hyperbolas](../../../../../../hyperbola.md) $xy=f/2$, including the coordinate axes for level zero. The [gradient](../../../../../../gradient.md) is [orthogonal](../../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) to each regular [level set](../../../../../../level-set.md) and points towards increasing values of $f$.
