<h1 id="11/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Write $s=\widehat{\mathbf n}\cdot\mathbf x$. Taking the [dot product](../../../../../dot-product.md) of the equation with the [unit vector](../../../../../unit-vector.md) $\widehat{\mathbf n}$ gives $2s+s^2=-1$, so $(s+1)^2=0$ and $s=-1$.

Now take the [cross product](../../../../../cross-product.md) with $\widehat{\mathbf n}$. The [vector triple product](../../../../../vector-triple-product.md) gives $\widehat{\mathbf n}\times(\widehat{\mathbf n}\times\mathbf x)=s\widehat{\mathbf n}-\mathbf x=-\widehat{\mathbf n}-\mathbf x$, so

$$
2\widehat{\mathbf n}\times\mathbf x-\widehat{\mathbf n}-\mathbf x=\widehat{\mathbf n}\times\mathbf b.
$$

The original equation, now with $s^2=1$, also gives $\widehat{\mathbf n}\times\mathbf x=\mathbf b-\widehat{\mathbf n}-2\mathbf x$. Substitution eliminates the remaining [cross product](../../../../../cross-product.md) of the unknown and yields

$$
\boxed{\mathbf x=\frac{2\mathbf b-3\widehat{\mathbf n}-\widehat{\mathbf n}\times\mathbf b}{5}.}
$$

Its [dot product](../../../../../dot-product.md) with $\widehat{\mathbf n}$ is $-1$, and direct substitution using the [vector triple product](../../../../../vector-triple-product.md) verifies the original equation. The elimination gives a unique solution without choosing components. It is also an application of the [inverse of a scalar plus a cross-product operator](../../../../../inverse-of-a-scalar-plus-a-cross-product-operator.md) after the scalar component has been fixed.
