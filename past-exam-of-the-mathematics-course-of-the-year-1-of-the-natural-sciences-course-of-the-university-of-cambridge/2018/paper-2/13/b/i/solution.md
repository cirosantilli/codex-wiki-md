<h1 id="13/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Substituting the endpoint parameter values gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbf r(0)=(1,0,0),\qquad\mathbf r(\pi/12)=(0,1,2\pi/3).}
$$

The curve lies on the unit circular [cylinder](../../../../../../cylinder-geometry.md) $x^2+y^2=1$. Its azimuth increases from zero to $\pi/2$ while its height increases linearly, so it is one quarter-turn of a rising [helix](../../../../../../helix.md). The arrow in the sketch indicates increasing parameter.
