<h1 id="13/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [arc length](../../../../../../arc-length.md) factor is the [norm](../../../../../../norm.md) of the tangent:

$$
\left|\frac{d\mathbf r}{dt}\right|=\sqrt{36\sin^26t+36\cos^26t+64}=10.
$$

Hence $ds=10\,dt$ and $\Phi=xy=\cos6t\sin6t=\tfrac12\sin12t$. The scalar [line integral](../../../../../../line-integral.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{\int_\Gamma\Phi\,ds=5\int_0^{\pi/12}\sin12t\,dt=\frac56.}
$$

This uses scalar [arc length](../../../../../../arc-length.md), rather than the oriented displacement used in the vector [line integrals](../../../../../../line-integral.md) of part (a).
