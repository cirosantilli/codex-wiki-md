<h1 id="13/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The field $\mathbf F$ is not a [conservative vector field](../../../../../conservative-vector-field.md): either nonzero closed [line integral](../../../../../line-integral.md) computed below rules out a [scalar potential](../../../../../scalar-potential.md). Equivalently, its planar [curl](../../../../../curl.md) is $\partial_xF_y-\partial_yF_x=1-(-1)=2$.

For $\mathbf G$, integrating $\Phi_x=2xy^2$ gives $\Phi=x^2y^2+h(y)$. Matching $\Phi_y=2yx^2$ then gives $h'=0$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\mathbf F\text{ is not conservative};\qquad\mathbf G=\nabla\Phi,\quad\Phi=x^2y^2+C.}
$$

This also explains why both closed [line integrals](../../../../../line-integral.md) of $\mathbf G$ vanish.
