<h1 id="13/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Starting at $(1,0)$, traverse the right, top, left and bottom sides in that order. For $\mathbf F$, the differential expression is $-y\,dx+x\,dy$. The four [line integral](../../../../../../line-integral.md) contributions are respectively $1,1,0,0$, so the total is two.

For $\mathbf G$, the expression is $2xy^2\,dx+2yx^2\,dy$. The right side contributes $\int_0^12y\,dy=1$, and the top side contributes $\int_1^02x\,dx=-1$; the other sides contribute zero. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\oint_{\rm square}\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r=2,\qquad
\oint_{\rm square}\mathbf G\cdot d\mathbf r=0.}
$$
