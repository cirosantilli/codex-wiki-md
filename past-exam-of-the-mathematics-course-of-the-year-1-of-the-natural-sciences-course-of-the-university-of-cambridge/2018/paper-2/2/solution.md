<h1 id="2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

The exponential definition of [hyperbolic sine](../../../../hyperbolic-sine.md) gives $e^z-e^{-z}=0$, or $e^{2z}=1$. Taking every branch of the [complex logarithm](../../../../complex-logarithm.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\sinh z=0\iff z=i\pi k,\qquad k\in\mathbb Z.}
$$

For [hyperbolic cosine](../../../../hyperbolic-cosine.md), multiply $\cosh z+1=0$ by $2e^z$ to obtain $(e^z+1)^2=0$. Thus $e^z=-1$, giving

$$
\boxed{\cosh z+1=0\iff z=i(2k+1)\pi,\qquad k\in\mathbb Z.}
$$
