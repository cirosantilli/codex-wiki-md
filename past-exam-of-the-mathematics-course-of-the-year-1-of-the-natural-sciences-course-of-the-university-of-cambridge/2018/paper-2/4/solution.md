<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

Multiply the [Maclaurin series](../../../../maclaurin-series.md) $\ln(1+x^2)=x^2-x^4/2+O(x^6)$ and the [geometric series](../../../../geometric-series.md) $(1+x^2)^{-1}=1-x^2+O(x^4)$. The first two nonzero terms are

$$
\boxed{\frac{\ln(1+x^2)}{1+x^2}=x^2-\frac32x^4+O(x^6).}
$$
