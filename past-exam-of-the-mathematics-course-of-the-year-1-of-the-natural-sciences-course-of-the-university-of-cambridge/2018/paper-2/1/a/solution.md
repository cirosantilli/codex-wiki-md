↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The direction [vectors](../../../../../vector.md) are $\mathbf u=(1,-2,1)$ and $\mathbf v=(0,-2,1)$. Their [cross product](../../../../../cross-product.md) is $(0,-1,-2)$, with [norm](../../../../../norm.md) $\sqrt5$. A [unit normal](../../../../../unit-normal.md) to the plane is therefore

$$
\boxed{\mathbf n=\frac{(0,1,2)}{\sqrt5},\quad\text{or its negative}.}
$$

The two intersecting directions span the plane, so a [vector](../../../../../vector.md) [orthogonal](../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) to both is a [normal vector](../../../../../normal-vector.md) to that plane.
