<h1 id="16/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use the orientation induced by the boundary order $O\to A\to B\to O$. The triangle's [vector area element](../../../../../../vector-area-element.md) integrates to $\tfrac12\mathbf a\times\mathbf b$. Every point $\mathbf r$ in its plane is a linear combination of $\mathbf a,\mathbf b$, so $\mathbf r\cdot(\mathbf a\times\mathbf b)=0$. The radial term of the field therefore contributes no [flux](../../../../../../flux.md). The constant term gives

$$
\boxed{\int_{OAB}\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf S
=(\mathbf a\times\mathbf b)\cdot\frac{\mathbf a\times\mathbf b}{2}
=\frac12|\mathbf a\times\mathbf b|^2.}
$$

Reversing the orientation reverses this sign. If $\mathbf a$ and $\mathbf b$ are collinear, the triangle has zero [area](../../../../../../surface-area.md) and the displayed value is zero by the degenerate-area convention.
