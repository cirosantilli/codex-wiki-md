<h1 id="16/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The fixed [vectors](../../../../../../vector.md) have constant components, so $\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b$ is constant, $\nabla\cdot\mathbf a=0$ and $\nabla\cdot\mathbf r=3$. Hence the [divergence](../../../../../../divergence.md) is

$$
\boxed{\nabla\cdot[2(\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b)\mathbf r+\mathbf a]=6\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b.}
$$
