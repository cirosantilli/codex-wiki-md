<h1 id="18/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

With the real [Fourier series](../../../../../fourier-series-split.md) convention,

$$
\boxed{f(\theta)\sim\frac{a_0}{2}+\sum_{n=1}^\infty[a_n\cos(n\theta)+b_n\sin(n\theta)],}
$$

where the [Fourier coefficients](../../../../../fourier-coefficient.md) are

$$
\boxed{a_n=\frac1\pi\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}f(t)\cos(nt)\,dt\quad(n\ge0),\qquad
b_n=\frac1\pi\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}f(t)\sin(nt)\,dt\quad(n\ge1).}
$$

For a square-integrable periodic [function](../../../../../function-split.md), [Parseval's identity](../../../../../parseval-identity.md) is

$$
\boxed{\frac1\pi\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}|f(t)|^2\,dt=\frac{|a_0|^2}{2}+\sum_{n=1}^\infty(|a_n|^2+|b_n|^2).}
$$

The [Fourier coefficients](../../../../../fourier-coefficient.md) require integrability, and the stated [Parseval identity](../../../../../parseval-identity.md) requires square integrability. The expansion agrees with $f$ in the [L2 space](../../../../../l2-space-is-a-hilbert-space.md) sense; for piecewise smooth data it converges pointwise to the average of the two one-sided limits. Without such qualifications an arbitrary periodic [function](../../../../../function-split.md) need not have a pointwise-convergent [Fourier series](../../../../../fourier-series-split.md).
