<h1 id="19/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Eliminate $z$ using the constraint: $z=x-x^2-y^2$. The restricted objective is the [quadratic polynomial](../../../../../../quadratic-polynomial.md) $F(x,y)=axy+x-x^2-y^2$, with constant [Hessian matrix](../../../../../../hessian-matrix.md)

$$
H=\begin{pmatrix}-2&a\\a&-2\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Its [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are $-2+a$ and $-2-a$. Hence

$$
\boxed{|a|<2:\text{ strict constrained maximum};\qquad |a|>2:\text{ constrained saddle}.}
$$

For $|a|<2$, both [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are negative and the [quadratic polynomial](../../../../../../quadratic-polynomial.md) is strictly concave, so the maximum is global on the entire constraint surface. For $|a|>2$, the opposite signs give increasing and decreasing directions. At $a=\pm2$ there is no [stationary point](../../../../../../stationary-point.md), as already found, rather than a degenerate extremum to classify. No parameter value produces a constrained [local minimum](../../../../../../local-minimum.md).
