<h1 id="16/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Differentiate the [cumulative distribution function](../../../../../cumulative-distribution-function.md) using the [chain rule](../../../../../chain-rule.md):

$$
\boxed{f(y)=F'(y)=\frac1{\pi x_0}\frac1{1+(y/x_0)^2}=\frac{x_0}{\pi(x_0^2+y^2)}.}
$$

This is a [Cauchy distribution](../../../../../cauchy-distribution.md) with location zero and scale $x_0$. The [probability density function](../../../../../probability-density-function.md) is nonnegative and integrates to $F(\infty)-F(-\infty)=1$.
