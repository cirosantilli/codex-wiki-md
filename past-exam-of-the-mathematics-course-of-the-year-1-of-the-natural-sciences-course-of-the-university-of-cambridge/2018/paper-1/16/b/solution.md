<h1 id="16/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The angular interval has width $\pi$, so its [continuous uniform distribution](../../../../../continuous-uniform-distribution.md) has [probability density function](../../../../../probability-density-function.md)

$$
\boxed{g(\theta)=\begin{cases}1/\pi,&-\pi/2<\theta<\pi/2,\\0,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}}
$$

Integrating this [probability density function](../../../../../probability-density-function.md) gives the [cumulative distribution function](../../../../../cumulative-distribution-function.md)

$$
\boxed{G(\theta)=\begin{cases}0,&\theta\le-\pi/2,\\\frac12+\theta/\pi,&-\pi/2<\theta<\pi/2,\\1,&\theta\ge\pi/2.\end{cases}}
$$

Values of the [probability density function](../../../../../probability-density-function.md) at the two individual endpoints may be assigned arbitrarily without changing any [probability](../../../../../probability.md).
