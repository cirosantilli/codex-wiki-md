<h1 id="16/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [tangent function](../../../../../tangent-function.md) is strictly increasing on the angular interval. Thus $Y\le y$ is equivalent, up to the zero-probability endpoints, to $\Theta\le\arctan(y/x_0)$. Substituting into the angular [cumulative distribution function](../../../../../cumulative-distribution-function.md) gives

$$
\boxed{F(y)=\frac12+\frac1\pi\arctan\frac y{x_0},\qquad-\infty<y<\infty.}
$$

Its [limits](../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md) are zero and one at the two infinite endpoints. This is the construction in which the [tangent of a uniform angle has a Cauchy distribution](../../../../../tangent-of-a-uniform-angle-has-a-cauchy-distribution.md).
