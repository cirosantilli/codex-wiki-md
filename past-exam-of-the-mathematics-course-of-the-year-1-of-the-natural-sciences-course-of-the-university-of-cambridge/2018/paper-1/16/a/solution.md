<h1 id="16/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [slope](../../../../../slope.md) of the particle's straight trajectory is $\tan\theta_i=y_i/x_0$, so

$$
\boxed{y_i=x_0\tan\theta_i.}
$$

The endpoint directions $\pm\pi/2$ are parallel to the screen and do not give finite collisions, but each has zero [probability](../../../../../probability.md) in the continuous angular model. The relation therefore holds [almost surely](../../../../../almost-sure-convergence.md).
