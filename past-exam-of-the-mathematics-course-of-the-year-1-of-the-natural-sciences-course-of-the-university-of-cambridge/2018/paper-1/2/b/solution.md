<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [quadratic formula](../../../../../quadratic-formula.md) gives the two [polynomial roots](../../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md) $(-3\pm\sqrt5)/2$. Its leading coefficient is positive, so the [quadratic polynomial](../../../../../quadratic-polynomial.md) is strictly negative between them. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\frac{-3-\sqrt5}{2}<x<\frac{-3+\sqrt5}{2}.}
$$
