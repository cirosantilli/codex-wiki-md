<h1 id="8/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [8](../8.md)

The [product rule](../../../../product-rule.md) gives $y'=e^x(\cos x-\sin x)$. The [exponential function](../../../../exponential-function.md) never vanishes, so a [stationary point](../../../../stationary-point.md) requires $\tan x=1$. In the given interval this means $x=\pi/4$. Substitution gives

$$
\boxed{(x,y)=\left(\frac\pi4,\frac{e^{\pi/4}}{\sqrt2}\right).}
$$

The [derivative](../../../../derivative.md) changes from positive to negative there, making it a strict [local maximum](../../../../local-maximum.md).
