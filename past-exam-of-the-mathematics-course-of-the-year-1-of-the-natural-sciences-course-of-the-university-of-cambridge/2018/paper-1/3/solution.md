<h1 id="3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

Matching the two [parametric equations](../../../../parametric-equation.md) at an intersection gives $\cos\phi=-t$ and $\sin\phi=1+t$. The [Pythagorean trigonometric identity](../../../../pythagorean-trigonometric-identity.md) therefore requires

$$
t^2+(1+t)^2=1,\qquad2t(t+1)=0.
$$

For $t=0$, the [sine](../../../../sine.md) is one and the [cosine](../../../../cosine.md) is zero; for $t=-1$, the [cosine](../../../../cosine.md) is one and the [sine](../../../../sine.md) is zero. Keeping the stated half-open angular interval gives

$$
\boxed{(t,\phi)=(0,\pi/2)\quad\text{or}\quad(-1,0).}
$$

The corresponding points are $(0,1)$ and $(2,0)$; both satisfy the original [parametric equations](../../../../parametric-equation.md).
