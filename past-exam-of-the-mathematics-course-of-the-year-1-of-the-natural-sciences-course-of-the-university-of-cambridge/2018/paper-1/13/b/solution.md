<h1 id="13/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Rewrite the [linear ordinary differential equation](../../../../../linear-ordinary-differential-equation-split.md) as $y'+y=e^{3x}$. Multiplication by its [integrating factor](../../../../../integrating-factor.md) $e^x$ gives $(e^xy)'=e^{4x}$. Integrating yields

$$
\boxed{y(x)=\frac14e^{3x}+Ce^{-x}.}
$$

The [general solution](../../../../../general-solution.md) consists of this [particular solution](../../../../../particular-solution.md) and the [homogeneous solution](../../../../../homogeneous-solution.md) $Ce^{-x}$.
