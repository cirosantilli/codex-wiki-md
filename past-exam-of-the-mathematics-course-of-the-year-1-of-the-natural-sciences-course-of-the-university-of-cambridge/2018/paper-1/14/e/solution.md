<h1 id="14/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The completed [contour plot](../../../../../contour-plot.md) is shown in the right-hand panel. Its geometry follows from the symmetries $f(-x,-y)=f(x,y)$ and $f(y,x)=f(x,y)$, together with $f\to0$ as $x^2+y^2\to\infty$. For orientation, the two [local minima](../../../../../local-minimum.md) have value $-2e^{-3/2}$, the two [local maxima](../../../../../local-maximum.md) have value $2e^{-1/2}$, and the [saddle point](../../../../../saddle-point.md) has value one.

Negative [level sets](../../../../../level-set.md) between $-2e^{-3/2}$ and zero form separate closed curves around the two [local minima](../../../../../local-minimum.md) in the first and third quadrants. Positive [level sets](../../../../../level-set.md) below one form a single closed curve enclosing the origin and both [local maxima](../../../../../local-maximum.md). At level one, a critical [level set](../../../../../level-set.md) crosses itself at the origin and surrounds the two [local maxima](../../../../../local-maximum.md) in two lobes. Between one and $2e^{-1/2}$, the [level sets](../../../../../level-set.md) split into separate closed curves around those [local maxima](../../../../../local-maximum.md). The zero [level set](../../../../../level-set.md) remains the two unbounded branches $xy=1/4$.

Near the origin, the [Taylor expansion](../../../../../taylor-expansion.md) is $f=1-x^2-4xy-y^2+O((x^2+y^2)^2)$. Consequently the crossing directions at the saddle height satisfy $y=(-2\pm\sqrt3)x$, giving the local shape of the critical [level set](../../../../../level-set.md).
