<h1 id="14/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The sign of $f$ is the sign of $1-4xy$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{f>0\text{ when }xy<\frac14,\qquad f<0\text{ when }xy>\frac14.}
$$

The positive region includes both coordinate axes, the second and fourth quadrants, and the portions of the first and third quadrants on the origin side of the [hyperbola](../../../../../hyperbola.md). The two negative regions lie beyond its branches. The left-hand panel shows these regions; its markers locate the [stationary points](../../../../../stationary-point.md) calculated next.
