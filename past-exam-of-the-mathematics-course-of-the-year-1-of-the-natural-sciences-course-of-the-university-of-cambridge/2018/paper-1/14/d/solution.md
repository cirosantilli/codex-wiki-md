<h1 id="14/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Evaluate the [Hessian matrix](../../../../../hessian-matrix.md) at the five [stationary points](../../../../../stationary-point.md). At the origin it is

$$
H(0,0)=\begin{pmatrix}-2&-4\\-4&-2\end{pmatrix},
$$

whose [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are $-6$ and $2$, so the origin is a [saddle point](../../../../../saddle-point.md). At the two points with $x=y=\pm\sqrt3/2$,

$$
H=e^{-3/2}\begin{pmatrix}10&2\\2&10\end{pmatrix},
$$

whose [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are $12e^{-3/2}$ and $8e^{-3/2}$. Both are positive, so these are strict [local minima](../../../../../local-minimum.md). At the two points with $x=-y=\pm1/2$,

$$
H=e^{-1/2}\begin{pmatrix}-6&-2\\-2&-6\end{pmatrix},
$$

whose [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are $-8e^{-1/2}$ and $-4e^{-1/2}$. Both are negative, so these are strict [local maxima](../../../../../local-maximum.md). In summary,

$$
\boxed{(0,0):\text{ saddle};\quad x=y=\pm\sqrt3/2:\text{ minima};\quad x=-y=\pm1/2:\text{ maxima}.}
$$

The quadratic changes along the [orthogonal](../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) directions $(1,1)$ and $(1,-1)$ also provide a direct justification of these classifications.
