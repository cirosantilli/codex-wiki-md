↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Completing the squares gives the standard equation of a [circle](../../../../../circle.md):

$$
\left(x+\frac12\right)^2+(y+1)^2=\frac94.
$$

Its centre is therefore

$$
\boxed{\left(-\frac12,-1\right).}
$$
