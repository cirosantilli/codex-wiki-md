<h1 id="11/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Using the exponential definition of [hyperbolic tangent](../../../../../hyperbolic-tangent.md), set $q=e^{2z}$:

$$
\frac{q-1}{q+1}=-i,\qquad(1+i)q=1-i,\qquad q=-i.
$$

Here $q+1=1-i\ne0$, so the resulting points are not [poles](../../../../../pole.md). Taking all values of the [complex logarithm](../../../../../complex-logarithm.md) gives $2z=i(-\pi/2+2\pi k)$. Thus

$$
\boxed{z=-\frac{i\pi}{4}+i\pi k,\qquad k\in\mathbb Z.}
$$

Equivalently $\tanh(iu)=i\tan u$, so these are exactly the imaginary-axis solutions of $\tan u=-1$.
