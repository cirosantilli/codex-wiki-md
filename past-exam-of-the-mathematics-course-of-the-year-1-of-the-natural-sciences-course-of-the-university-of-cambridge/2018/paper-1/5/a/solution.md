<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The two labelled regions in the original diagram are complementary [circular sectors](../../../../../circular-sector.md). Their [areas](../../../../../surface-area.md) add to $\pi r^2$ and satisfy $A=2B$, so $B=\pi r^2/3$. The sector with central [angle](../../../../../angle.md) $\theta$ has [area](../../../../../surface-area.md) $r^2\theta/2$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\theta=\frac{2\pi}{3}=120^\circ.}
$$
