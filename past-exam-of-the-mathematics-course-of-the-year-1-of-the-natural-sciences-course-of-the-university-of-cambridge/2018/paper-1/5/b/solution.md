<h1 id="5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The two radii enclose the [angle](../../../../../angle.md) $2\pi/3$, so the triangle has [area](../../../../../surface-area.md) $T=\tfrac12r^2\sin(2\pi/3)=\sqrt3r^2/4$. From the sector calculation, $r^2=3B/\pi$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{T=\frac{3\sqrt3}{4\pi}\,B.}
$$
