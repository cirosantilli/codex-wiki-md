<h1 id="19/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For the positive terms $a_n=n!/2^n$, the ratio is $a_{n+1}/a_n=(n+1)/2\to\infty$. The [ratio test](../../../../../../ratio-test.md) therefore gives divergence. More directly, the terms increase by a factor at least two for all $n\ge3$, so they fail the necessary [term test for divergence](../../../../../../term-test-for-divergence.md) condition $a_n\to0$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\sum_{n=1}^\infty\frac{n!}{2^n}\text{ diverges to }+\infty.}
$$
