<h1 id="18/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The middle row is $-1/2$ times the sum of the first and third rows, so the rows are linearly dependent and the [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) is zero. The [matrix trace](../../../../../matrix-trace.md) is the sum of diagonal entries, $1+4+1=6$. A [transpose](../../../../../transpose.md) preserves the [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md), while the [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) of a product is the product of its [determinants](../../../../../determinant.md). Consequently

$$
\boxed{\det A=0,\quad\operatorname{Tr}A=6,\quad\det(A^{\mathsf T})=0,\quad\det(A^2)=0.}
$$
