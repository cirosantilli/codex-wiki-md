↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Equating the two [polynomials](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) gives $x^3-3x^2+3x-1=(x-1)^3=0$. Thus the only intersection has $x=1$ and $y=4$:

$$
\boxed{(1,4).}
$$
