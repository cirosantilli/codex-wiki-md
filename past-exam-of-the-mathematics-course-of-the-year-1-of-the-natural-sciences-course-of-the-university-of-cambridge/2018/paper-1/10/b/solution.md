<h1 id="10/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The two [derivatives](../../../../../derivative.md) are $3x^2+3$ and $6x$, both equal to six at $x=1$. Their common [tangent line](../../../../../tangent-line.md) through $(1,4)$ is $y-4=6(x-1)$, or $y=6x-2$. Setting $x=0$ gives its intersection with the vertical axis:

$$
\boxed{(0,-2).}
$$
