<h1 id="12/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Scale the [ellipsoid](../../../../../../ellipsoid.md) to a unit [ball](../../../../../../ball-mathematics.md) using

$$
x=ar\sin\theta\cos\phi,\qquad y=br\sin\theta\sin\phi,\qquad z=cr\cos\theta,
$$

with $0\le r\le1$, $0\le\theta\le\pi$ and $0\le\phi<2\pi$. This is [spherical coordinates](../../../../../../spherical-coordinate-system.md) followed by a diagonal [linear transformation](../../../../../../linear-map.md), so the [Jacobian determinant](../../../../../../jacobian-determinant.md) is $abc\,r^2\sin\theta$. The required [volume integral](../../../../../../volume-integral.md) is

$$
\boxed{V=abc\int_0^{2\pi}\int_0^\pi\int_0^1r^2\sin\theta\,dr\,d\theta\,d\phi
=\frac{4\pi abc}{3}.}
$$
