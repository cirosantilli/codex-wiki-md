<h1 id="12/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [ellipsoid](../../../../../../ellipsoid.md) and the exponential factor are invariant under separate reflections of any coordinate. Split the integrand into its two polynomial terms. The term $x^4y^2z$ times that exponential is odd in $z$, so its [volume integral](../../../../../../volume-integral.md) vanishes by reflecting $z\mapsto-z$. The other term $xy^2z^4$ times the exponential is odd in $x$, so its [volume integral](../../../../../../volume-integral.md) vanishes by reflecting $x\mapsto-x$. Both integrands are continuous on a bounded region. Therefore [odd-integrand cancellation by reflection](../../../../../../odd-integrand-cancellation-by-reflection.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\iiint_V(x^4y^2z+xy^2z^4)e^{-(x/a)^2-(y/b)^2-(z/c)^2}\,dx\,dy\,dz=0.}
$$
