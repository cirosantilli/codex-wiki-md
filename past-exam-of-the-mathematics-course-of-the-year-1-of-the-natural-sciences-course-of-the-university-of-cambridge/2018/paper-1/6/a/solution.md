<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A space diagonal of the unit [cube](../../../../../cube.md) has displacement [vector](../../../../../vector.md) $(1,1,1)$. Its Euclidean [norm](../../../../../norm.md), or two successive applications of the [Pythagorean theorem](../../../../../pythagorean-theorem.md), gives

$$
\boxed{d=\sqrt{1^2+1^2+1^2}=\sqrt3\text{ units}.}
$$

The requested diagonal joins opposite vertices; $\sqrt2$ would be the diagonal of a face.
