<h1 id="6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Choose two distinct space-diagonal directions $\mathbf u=(1,1,1)$ and $\mathbf v=(-1,1,1)$. Their [dot product](../../../../../dot-product.md) is one and their [norms](../../../../../norm.md) are both $\sqrt3$. Thus the acute [angle](../../../../../angle.md) between their unoriented lines is

$$
\boxed{\theta=\arccos\frac13\simeq70.53^\circ.}
$$

Any two distinct space diagonals have an absolute [dot product](../../../../../dot-product.md) of one when represented with components $\pm1$, so they give the same acute [angle](../../../../../angle.md).
