<h1 id="17/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Temporarily label the individual pieces. A random placement is a uniform [injection](../../../../../injective-function.md) of thirty-two distinct pieces into sixty-four squares, giving ${}_{64}P_{32}$ equally likely outcomes. For a colour-matching placement, the black pieces have ${}_{32}P_{16}$ available [injections](../../../../../injective-function.md), and independently the white pieces have the same number within their disjoint set of squares. Therefore

$$
\boxed{P=\frac{({}_{32}P_{16})^2}{{}_{64}P_{32}}
=\frac{(32!)^3}{(16!)^2\,64!}}.
$$

Here ${}_nP_r=n!/(n-r)!$. Treating same-colour pieces as indistinguishable gives the same [probability](../../../../../probability.md), because the common within-colour labeling factors cancel. The two colour choices in the favourable count occupy disjoint square sets; no independence assumption about unrestricted placements is required.
