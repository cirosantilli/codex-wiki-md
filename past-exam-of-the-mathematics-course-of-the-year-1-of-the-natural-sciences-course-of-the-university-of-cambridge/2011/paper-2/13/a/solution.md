<h1 id="13/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For $x>0$, the exponential definitions of the [hyperbolic functions](../../../../../hyperbolic-function.md) give $\cosh x-\sinh x=e^{-x}>0$ and $\sinh x>0$. Taking reciprocals of these positive quantities proves $1/\cosh x<1/\sinh x$. Also $\cosh x>1$ for $x>0$, so division by the positive $\sinh x$ gives $1/\sinh x<\cosh x/\sinh x$. Combining,

$$
\boxed{\frac1{\cosh x}<\frac1{\sinh x}<\coth x\qquad(x>0)}.
$$
