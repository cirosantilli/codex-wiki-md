<h1 id="13/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The specified [unit vector](../../../../../unit-vector.md) is $\mathbf u=(\cos(\pi/3),\sin(\pi/3))=(1/2,\sqrt3/2)$; the positive $y$ component selects that direction. The [gradient](../../../../../gradient.md) is $\nabla f=e^{xy}(y,x)$, giving $(0,-2)$ at the point. The [directional derivative](../../../../../directional-derivative.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{D_{\mathbf u}f=\nabla f\cdot\mathbf u=-\sqrt3}.
$$
