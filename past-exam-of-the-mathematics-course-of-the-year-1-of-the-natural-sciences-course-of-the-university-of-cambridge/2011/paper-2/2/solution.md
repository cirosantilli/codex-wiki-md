<h1 id="2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

The numerator is the [derivative](../../../../derivative.md) of the denominator. Using the [chain rule](../../../../chain-rule.md) in reverse gives the [antiderivative](../../../../antiderivative.md)

$$
\boxed{\ln|x^2+3x-2|+C}.
$$

The [absolute value](../../../../absolute-value.md) makes this real on every interval where the denominator is nonzero. The integration constant may be chosen independently on the intervals separated by $x=(-3\pm\sqrt{17})/2$.
