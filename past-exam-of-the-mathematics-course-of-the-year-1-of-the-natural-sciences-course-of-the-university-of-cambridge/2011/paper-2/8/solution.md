<h1 id="8/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [8](../8.md)

Write $z=x+iy$. The [complex modulus](../../../../complex-modulus.md) condition is equivalent to $(x-1)^2+y^2=4$. Thus the locus is a **[circle](../../../../circle.md) of radius two centred at $(1,0)$** in the [complex plane](../../../../complex-plane.md). Its extreme points are $(-1,0)$, $(3,0)$, and $(1,\pm2)$.
