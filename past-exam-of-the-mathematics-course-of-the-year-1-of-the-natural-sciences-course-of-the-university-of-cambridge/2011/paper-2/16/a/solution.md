<h1 id="16/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use the [integration by substitution](../../../../../integration-by-substitution.md) $x=\sin\theta$, with $-\pi/6\leq\theta\leq\pi/6$. On this interval $\cos\theta>0$, so it cancels the positive denominator [square root](../../../../../square-root.md). The transformed [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md) is

$$
\frac12\int_{-\pi/6}^{\pi/6}\sqrt{1-\frac49\sin^2\theta}\,d\theta.
$$

Its integrand is even. Therefore the answer in the PDF's modulus-amplitude convention for the [elliptic integral of the second kind](../../../../../elliptic-integral-of-the-second-kind.md) is

$$
\boxed{E\left(\frac23,\frac\pi6\right)}.
$$

The modulus is $2/3$, not the squared parameter $4/9$.
