<h1 id="16/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Differentiating the parametrization gives $dx=a\cos\theta\,d\theta$ and $dy=-b\sin\theta\,d\theta$. Thus the [arc length](../../../../../arc-length.md) element is

$$
ds=\sqrt{a^2\cos^2\theta+b^2\sin^2\theta}\,d\theta
=a\sqrt{1-e^2\sin^2\theta}\,d\theta.
$$

All four quadrants give the same contribution. Consequently the [ellipse](../../../../../ellipse.md) perimeter is

$$
\boxed{L=a\int_0^{2\pi}\sqrt{1-e^2\sin^2\theta}\,d\theta
=4aE(e,\pi/2)}.
$$

The final factor is the [complete elliptic integral of the second kind](../../../../../complete-elliptic-integral-of-the-second-kind.md), evaluated at the [eccentricity of a conic](../../../../../eccentricity-of-a-conic.md) $e$.
