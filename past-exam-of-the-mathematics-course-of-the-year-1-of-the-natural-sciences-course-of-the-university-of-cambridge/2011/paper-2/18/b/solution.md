<h1 id="18/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The intersections satisfy $6x-x^2=2x+3$, or $(x-1)(x-3)=0$, so the bounded region has $1\leq x\leq3$. Over that interval the [parabola](../../../../../parabola.md) is above the line, and their height difference is $(x-1)(3-x)$. Thus its [area](../../../../../surface-area.md) is

$$
\boxed{A=\int_1^3(4x-x^2-3)\,dx
=\left[2x^2-\frac{x^3}{3}-3x\right]_1^3=\frac43}.
$$

Using upper minus lower ensures a positive geometric [area](../../../../../surface-area.md).
