<h1 id="18/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The projection onto the $x$-$y$ [plane](../../../../../plane.md) is the [disk](../../../../../disk-mathematics.md) $x^2+y^2\leq4$. In [polar coordinates](../../../../../polar-coordinates.md), the height is $4-r^2$ and the [polar area element](../../../../../polar-area-element.md) is $r\,dr\,d\theta$. The [volume](../../../../../volume.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{\mathcal V=\int_0^{2\pi}\int_0^2(4-r^2)r\,dr\,d\theta
=2\pi\left[2r^2-\frac{r^4}{4}\right]_0^2=8\pi}.
$$

The radial factor here is required because this [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md) is being constructed from the geometric [volume](../../../../../volume.md), unlike the already specified [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md) in (a).
