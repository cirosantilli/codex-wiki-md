<h1 id="12/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A [plane](../../../../../plane.md) through the origin has equation $lx+my+nz=0$ for some nonzero normal $(l,m,n)$. Dividing that normal by its [Euclidean norm](../../../../../euclidean-norm.md) makes $l^2+m^2+n^2=1$ without changing the [plane](../../../../../plane.md). Substitute the [spherical coordinates](../../../../../spherical-coordinate-system.md) from (a) and cancel the nonzero radius:

$$
\boxed{l\sin\theta\cos\phi+m\sin\theta\sin\phi+n\cos\theta=0,\qquad
l^2+m^2+n^2=1}.
$$

Its intersection with the [sphere](../../../../../sphere.md) is the desired [great circle](../../../../../great-circle.md); changing the sign of all three coefficients describes the same [circle](../../../../../circle.md).
