↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Using the physics convention for [spherical coordinates](../../../../../spherical-coordinate-system.md), the [polar angle](../../../../../polar-angle.md) $\theta$ is measured from positive $z$ and the [azimuth](../../../../../azimuth.md) $\phi$ from positive $x$ toward positive $y$. The [Cartesian coordinates](../../../../../cartesian-coordinate-system.md) are

$$
\boxed{x=r\sin\theta\cos\phi,\qquad y=r\sin\theta\sin\phi,\qquad z=r\cos\theta}.
$$
