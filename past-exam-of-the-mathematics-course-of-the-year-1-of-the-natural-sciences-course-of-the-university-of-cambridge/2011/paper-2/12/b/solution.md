<h1 id="12/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Hold $r$ fixed and differentiate the position [vector](../../../../../vector.md) $\mathbf X(\theta,\phi)$. Its coordinate tangent [vectors](../../../../../vector.md) are

$$
\mathbf X_\theta=r(\cos\theta\cos\phi,\cos\theta\sin\phi,-\sin\theta),\qquad
\mathbf X_\phi=r(-\sin\theta\sin\phi,\sin\theta\cos\phi,0).
$$

Their [dot product](../../../../../dot-product.md) is zero, and their squared [Euclidean norms](../../../../../euclidean-norm.md) are $r^2$ and $r^2\sin^2\theta$. To first order, the displacement is $\delta\mathbf X=\mathbf X_\theta\delta\theta+\mathbf X_\phi\delta\phi+O((|\delta\theta|+|\delta\phi|)^2)$. Squaring gives

$$
\boxed{\delta s^2\simeq r^2(\delta\theta^2+\sin^2\theta\,\delta\phi^2)}.
$$

This is the [line element on a sphere](../../../../../line-element-on-a-sphere.md). The chord distance and the short surface [arc length](../../../../../arc-length.md) agree to the order retained here.
