<h1 id="5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

Rationalize with the [complex conjugate](../../../../complex-conjugate.md) $4-3i$:

$$
\frac{5-2i}{4+3i}=\frac{(5-2i)(4-3i)}{4^2+3^2}
=\frac{14-23i}{25}.
$$

Thus the [real part](../../../../real-part.md) and [imaginary part](../../../../imaginary-part.md) are

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Re}=\frac{14}{25},\qquad\operatorname{Im}=-\frac{23}{25}}.
$$
