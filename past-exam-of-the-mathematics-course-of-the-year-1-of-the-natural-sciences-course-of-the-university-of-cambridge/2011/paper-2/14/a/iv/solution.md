<h1 id="14/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

For the particular [eigenvectors](../../../../../../eigenvector.md) chosen in (iii), the [dot products](../../../../../../dot-product.md) are

$$
\mathbf e_1\cdot\mathbf e_2=0,\qquad
\mathbf e_1\cdot\mathbf e_3=2,\qquad
\mathbf e_2\cdot\mathbf e_3=-6.
$$

Thus **only $\mathbf e_1$ and $\mathbf e_2$ are [orthogonal](../../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md)** for this choice. The [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) is not symmetric, so distinct [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) alone do not imply [orthogonality](../../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md). Another basis within the repeated [eigenspace](../../../../../../eigenspace.md) may change which chosen [vectors](../../../../../../vector.md) are perpendicular; the dot products above specify the convention completely.
