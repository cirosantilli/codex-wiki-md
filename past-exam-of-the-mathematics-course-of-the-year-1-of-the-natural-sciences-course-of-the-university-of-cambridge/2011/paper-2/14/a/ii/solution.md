↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md), using the convention $\det(\lambda I-A)$, is

$$
p(\lambda)=\lambda^3+\lambda^2-21\lambda-45
=(\lambda-5)(\lambda+3)^2.
$$

Its zeros give the [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md), including [algebraic multiplicity](../../../../../../algebraic-multiplicity.md):

$$
\boxed{5,\qquad-3,\qquad-3}.
$$

Their product is $45$ and their sum is $-1$, agreeing with the [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) and [trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md) of the [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md).
