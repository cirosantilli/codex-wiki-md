↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Expand the [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) along the first row:

$$
\det A=(-2)(-12)-2(-6)+(-3)(-3)=24+12+9.
$$

Thus $\boxed{\det A=45}$. The signs in the middle cofactor account for its positive contribution.
