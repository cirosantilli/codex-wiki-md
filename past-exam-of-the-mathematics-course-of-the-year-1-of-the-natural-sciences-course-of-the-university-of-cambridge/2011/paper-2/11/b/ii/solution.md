<h1 id="11/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [scalar](../../../../../../scalar.md) integrand still equals $10e$ throughout the unit [sphere](../../../../../../sphere.md), and the upper [hemisphere](../../../../../../hemisphere.md) has the same [surface area](../../../../../../surface-area.md) $2\pi$. Excluding its equator changes no [area](../../../../../../surface-area.md). Therefore

$$
\boxed{\int_{S'}(\nabla\cdot\mathbf v)\,dS=20\pi e}.
$$

There is no sign reversal between hemispheres because this [scalar](../../../../../../scalar.md) [surface integral](../../../../../../surface-integral.md) has no choice of normal orientation.
