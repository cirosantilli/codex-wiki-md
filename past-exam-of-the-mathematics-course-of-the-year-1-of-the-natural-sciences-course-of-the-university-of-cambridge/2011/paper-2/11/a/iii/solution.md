<h1 id="11/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Where $\mathbf A=|\mathbf v|^2\mathbf w$ is twice continuously differentiable, the identity [divergence of a curl is zero](../../../../../../divergence-of-a-curl-is-zero.md) gives $\nabla\cdot(\nabla\times\mathbf A)=0$. All regular portions of the parameter interval lie in this region, hence

$$
\boxed{\int_{\Gamma_2}\nabla\cdot[\nabla\times(|\mathbf v|^2\mathbf w)]\,ds=0}
$$

as an [improper line integral](../../../../../../improper-line-integral.md). Besides the $\operatorname{arctanh}(x^2)$ endpoint problem from (i), the [curve](../../../../../../curve.md) touches $y=1$ at $\theta=\pi/2$, where the printed real $\arcsin y$ expression has no smooth open neighbourhood. The identity applies on the intervening open segments, and its zero value has a unique continuous extension along the [curve](../../../../../../curve.md). A literal classical integrand at every parameter is not supplied by the printed field.
