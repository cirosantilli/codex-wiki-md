<h1 id="19/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Simplify the real component to $u=(1-x^2-y^2)/D$. Thus $u=0$ is the unit [circle](../../../../../circle.md) with $(1,0)$ excluded from the mapping's domain. Its upper semicircle has $v>0$ and its lower semicircle has $v<0$; the point $(-1,0)$ maps to $u=v=0$. Inside the [disk](../../../../../disk-mathematics.md), $u>0$, so the principal arctangent is unambiguous. Setting $\alpha=V$ gives

$$
\boxed{\Psi(x,y)=\frac{2V}{\pi}\arctan\frac{2y}{1-x^2-y^2},\qquad x^2+y^2<1}.
$$

The [Laplace equation](../../../../../laplace-equation.md) follows from (b). Approaching an open upper [boundary](../../../../../boundary-of-a-set.md) arc makes the ratio tend to $+\infty$, giving $+V$; the lower arc gives $-V$. This solution is $V(2F-1)$ in terms of the [harmonic measure of a semicircle in a disk](../../../../../harmonic-measure-of-a-semicircle-in-a-disk.md) $F$.

The [boundary](../../../../../boundary-of-a-set.md) data jump at $(\pm1,0)$, so no path-independent continuous [boundary](../../../../../boundary-of-a-set.md) value can be imposed at those two junctions. The displayed bounded [harmonic function](../../../../../harmonic-function.md) is the solution with the specified limits on the open arcs; those endpoint discontinuities are part of the idealized [capacitor](../../../../../capacitor.md) geometry.
