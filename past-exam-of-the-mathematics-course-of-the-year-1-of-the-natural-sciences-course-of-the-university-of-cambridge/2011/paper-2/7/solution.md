<h1 id="7/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [7](../7.md)

This is a [separable differential equation](../../../../separable-differential-equation.md). Multiply by $e^{-y}$ and integrate:

$$
-e^{-y}=\frac12\ln(1+x^2)+C.
$$

The [initial condition](../../../../initial-condition.md) gives $C=-1$. Hence

$$
\boxed{y(x)=-\ln\left(1-\frac12\ln(1+x^2)\right)}.
$$

For a real solution the outer [logarithm](../../../../logarithm.md) requires a positive argument. The maximal interval containing the initial point is therefore $\boxed{|x|<\sqrt{e^2-1}}$. At either endpoint $y$ diverges, so the real solution cannot continue through it with a finite value.
