<h1 id="15/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

First solve for the [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) components. The [integrating factor](../../../../../integrating-factor.md) $e^{\gamma t}$ gives

$$
\dot x=V_{0x}e^{-\gamma t},\qquad
\dot z=\left(V_{0z}+\frac g\gamma\right)e^{-\gamma t}-\frac g\gamma.
$$

Integrating once more and imposing the initial position gives

$$
\boxed{x(t)=\frac{V_{0x}}\gamma(1-e^{-\gamma t}),\qquad
z(t)=\frac{V_{0z}+g/\gamma}\gamma(1-e^{-\gamma t})-\frac g\gamma t}.
$$

These formulas describe [projectile motion with linear drag](../../../../../projectile-motion-with-linear-drag.md). Differentiation checks both second-order [differential equations](../../../../../differential-equation-split.md) and both initial velocities. At large positive [time](../../../../../time-in-physics.md), the horizontal [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) tends to zero and the vertical [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) tends to the terminal value $-g/\gamma$.
