<h1 id="3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

The [quotient rule](../../../../quotient-rule.md) simplifies the [derivative](../../../../derivative.md) to

$$
y'=\frac{7(x+1)(x+5)}{(x-1)^2(x+2)^2}.
$$

Its zeros are $-5$ and $-1$, both in the domain. Substitution gives the two [stationary points](../../../../stationary-point.md)

$$
\boxed{(-5,25/9),\qquad(-1,9)}.
$$

The first is a [local maximum](../../../../local-maximum.md) and the second a [local minimum](../../../../local-minimum.md), since the numerator of $y'$ changes sign from positive to negative at $-5$ and from negative to positive at $-1$. The denominator of the original [rational function](../../../../rational-function.md) vanishes at

$$
\boxed{x=-2,\qquad x=1}.
$$

The numerator is nonzero at both values, so these discontinuities are poles, not removable holes.
