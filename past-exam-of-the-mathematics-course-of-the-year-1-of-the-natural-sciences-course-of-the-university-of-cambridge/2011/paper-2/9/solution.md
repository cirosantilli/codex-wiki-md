<h1 id="9/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [9](../9.md)

The [derivative](../../../../derivative.md) is $dy/dx=3x^2$, giving slope twelve at $x=2$. The point on the [curve](../../../../curve.md) is $(2,9)$. The point-slope equation of the [tangent line](../../../../tangent-line.md) is consequently

$$
\boxed{y-9=12(x-2),\qquad y=12x-15}.
$$
