<h1 id="19/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Take the [sphere](../../../../../sphere.md)'s outward normal. Since $F=k(x,y,z)$ with constant $k$, its [divergence](../../../../../divergence.md) is $3k$. The [divergence theorem](../../../../../divergence-theorem.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\int_SF\cdot dS=3k\,\frac{4\pi r^3}{3}=4\pi kr^3.}
$$

The translated [centre](../../../../../center-geometry.md) does not enter the answer, because the [divergence](../../../../../divergence.md) is constant. Equivalently the constant field produced by translating coordinates has zero net [flux](../../../../../flux.md) through a closed [sphere](../../../../../sphere.md). The inward orientation would negate the result.
