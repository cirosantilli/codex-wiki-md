<h1 id="19/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [divergence](../../../../../divergence.md) is

$$
\nabla\cdot F=\partial_x(y^2)+\partial_y(e^{xz})+\partial_z(e^{xy})=0.
$$

For any closed piecewise [smooth](../../../../../smooth-function.md) surface bounding a [volume](../../../../../volume.md), use the [divergence theorem](../../../../../divergence-theorem.md) with the outward orientation:

$$
\boxed{\int_SF\cdot dS=\int_V\nabla\cdot F\,dV=0.}
$$

Reversing the orientation still gives zero. The field is [smooth](../../../../../smooth-function.md) everywhere, so there is no hidden singular source inside the surface.
