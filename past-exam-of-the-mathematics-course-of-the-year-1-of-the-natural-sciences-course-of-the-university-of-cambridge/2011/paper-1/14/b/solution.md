<h1 id="14/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Differentiate the [Fourier series](../../../../../fourier-series-split.md) term by term and collect [cosine](../../../../../cosine.md) and [sine](../../../../../sine.md) terms. Substitution into the equation gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{a_0}{2}+\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}
[(a_n+\sqrt3\,n b_n)\cos(nx)+(b_n-\sqrt3\,n a_n)\sin(nx)]
=4\sin x.}
$$

For a [smooth](../../../../../smooth-function.md) periodic solution, [differentiation](../../../../../differentiation.md) of the series is justified. Alternatively, multiply the [differential equation](../../../../../differential-equation-split.md) by each trigonometric mode and integrate by parts; periodicity cancels the boundary terms and gives the same [coefficient](../../../../../coefficient.md) constraints without first differentiating an infinite sum.
