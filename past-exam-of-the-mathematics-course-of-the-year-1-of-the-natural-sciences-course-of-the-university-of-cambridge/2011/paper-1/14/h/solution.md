<h1 id="14/h/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [H](../h.md)

Square the two-term form and integrate directly:

$$
\int_0^{2\pi}(\sin x-\sqrt3\cos x)^2dx
=\int_0^{2\pi}\sin^2x\,dx+3\int_0^{2\pi}\cos^2x\,dx
-2\sqrt3\int_0^{2\pi}\sin x\cos x\,dx.
$$

The first two integrals are each $\pi$ before their prefactors, while the cross term is zero because its integrand is $\tfrac12\sin2x$. Thus the result is again **$4\pi$**, agreeing with [Parseval's identity](../../../../../parseval-identity.md).
