<h1 id="14/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Using the [sine addition formula](../../../../../sine-addition-formula.md), combine the surviving modes:

$$
\boxed{y(x)=\sin x-\sqrt3\cos x=2\sin(x-\pi/3).}
$$

It has [amplitude](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) $2$ and a [phase](../../../../../phase-waves.md) delay $\pi/3$. The full [general solution](../../../../../general-solution.md) would add $Ce^{-x/\sqrt3}$, but this exponential is periodic only for $C=0$, so the displayed periodic solution is unique.
