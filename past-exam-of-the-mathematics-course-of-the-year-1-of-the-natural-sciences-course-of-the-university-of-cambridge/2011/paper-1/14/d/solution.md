<h1 id="14/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The constant [coefficient](../../../../../coefficient.md) is $a_0=0$. For every $n\geq1$ the two mode equations are

$$
a_n+\sqrt3\,n b_n=0,\qquad b_n-\sqrt3\,n a_n=4\delta_{n1},
$$

where $\delta_{n1}$ is the [Kronecker delta](../../../../../kronecker-delta.md). The first equation gives $a_n=-\sqrt3\,n b_n$, so the second becomes $(1+3n^2)b_n=4\delta_{n1}$. Hence

$$
\boxed{a_0=0,\qquad a_1=-\sqrt3,\quad b_1=1,\qquad a_n=b_n=0\ (n\geq2).}
$$

No other modes survive. This is the single-frequency case of the [Fourier solution of a first-order equation with periodic forcing](../../../../../fourier-solution-of-a-first-order-equation-with-periodic-forcing.md).
