<h1 id="14/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

The [curve](../../../../../curve.md) starts at $y(0)=-\sqrt3$ and initially rises. It crosses zero at $x=\pi/3$ and $4\pi/3$, attains its maximum at **$(5\pi/6,2)$** and minimum at **$(11\pi/6,-2)$**, then returns to $y(2\pi)=-\sqrt3$. These follow either from the shifted [sine](../../../../../sine.md) form or from $y'=2\cos(x-\pi/3)$.
