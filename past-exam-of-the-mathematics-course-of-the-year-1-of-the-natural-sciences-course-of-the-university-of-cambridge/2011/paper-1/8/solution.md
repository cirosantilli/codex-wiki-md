<h1 id="8/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [8](../8.md)

The integrand is a [continuous function](../../../../continuous-function.md), so the [fundamental theorem of calculus](../../../../fundamental-theorem-of-calculus.md) differentiates the upper limit directly:

$$
\boxed{\frac d{dx}\int_0^x e^{-y^2}\sin y\,dy=e^{-x^2}\sin x.}
$$

No explicit [antiderivative](../../../../antiderivative.md) is needed.
