↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The point has positive [real part](../../../../../../real-part.md) and negative [imaginary part](../../../../../../imaginary-part.md). Its [complex modulus](../../../../../../complex-modulus.md) is $\sqrt{1+3}=2$, and its principal [complex argument](../../../../../../argument-complex-analysis.md) is $-\pi/3$. Hence

$$
\boxed{|z|=2,\qquad\arg z=-\frac\pi3+2\pi k,\quad k\in\mathbb Z.}
$$
