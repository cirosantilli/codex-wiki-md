<h1 id="13/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The chosen [eigenvectors](../../../../../../eigenvector.md) have [dot product](../../../../../../dot-product.md)

$$
\boxed{x_1\cdot x_2=\frac{1-1}{2}=0.}
$$

They are therefore [orthogonal](../../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md), and their unit lengths make them an [orthonormal eigenbasis](../../../../../../orthonormal-eigenbasis.md). This agrees with the [spectral theorem for real symmetric matrices](../../../../../../spectral-theorem-for-real-symmetric-matrices.md), since $A$ is real symmetric and its two [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are distinct.
