<h1 id="13/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Interchange the diagonal entries, negate the off-diagonal entries, and divide by the nonzero [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md). The [matrix inverse](../../../../../../matrix-inverse-split.md) is

$$
\boxed{A^{-1}=\frac1{16}\begin{pmatrix}5&3\\3&5\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

Multiplication by $A$ gives the [identity matrix](../../../../../../identity-matrix.md), since each diagonal product sum is $25-9=16$ and each off-diagonal product sum is zero.
