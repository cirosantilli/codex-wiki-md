↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Multiply the [quadratic form](../../../../../../quadratic-form-split.md) out directly:

$$
(x\ y)A\begin{pmatrix}x\\y\end{pmatrix}
=x(5x-3y)+y(-3x+5y)=5x^2-6xy+5y^2.
$$

Thus **$b=5$, $c=-6$ and $d=5$**. Both [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are positive, so this is a [positive-definite quadratic form](../../../../../../positive-definite-quadratic-form.md); its positive unit [level set](../../../../../../level-set.md) is an [ellipse](../../../../../../ellipse.md). The next part exhibits its principal axes explicitly.
