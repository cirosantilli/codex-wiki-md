<h1 id="12/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Take $s$ on the horizontal axis and $t$ on the vertical axis, both nonnegative. There is no interruption when the noise occurs after the scheduled ending, namely **$t>s$**. This is the part of the first quadrant above the diagonal $t=s$. Equality has [probability](../../../../../probability.md) zero, so assigning the boundary either way does not affect the calculation.

The same original diagram below also marks the region used in part (e); its lower boundary offset is an illustrative positive choice of $r_0$.
