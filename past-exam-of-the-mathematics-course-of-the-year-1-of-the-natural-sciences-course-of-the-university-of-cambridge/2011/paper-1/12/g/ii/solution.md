<h1 id="12/g/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

When $\bar s=\bar t$, the prefactor is $1/2$. Therefore **$P(r>r_0)\to1/2$ as $r_0\downarrow0$**. The clocks then have identical independent continuous laws, so either is equally likely to finish first. A tie has [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) zero. The limit is the interruption [probability](../../../../../../probability.md), rather than one, because non-interrupted outcomes retain the negative sentinel value.
