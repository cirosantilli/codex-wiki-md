<h1 id="12/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Integrate over the region $0\leq s<t$:

$$
P(T>S)=\int_0^\infty\frac{e^{-s/\bar s}}{\bar s}
\left[\int_s^\infty\frac{e^{-t/\bar t}}{\bar t}dt\right]ds
=\frac1{\bar s}\int_0^\infty e^{-s(1/\bar s+1/\bar t)}ds.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{P(\text{no interruption})=\frac{\bar t}{\bar s+\bar t}.}
$$

This is also the two-clock case of [competing exponential clocks](../../../../../competing-exponential-clocks.md). A larger [mean](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) time to noise increases this [probability](../../../../../probability.md), while a larger [mean](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) service [length](../../../../../length.md) decreases it.
