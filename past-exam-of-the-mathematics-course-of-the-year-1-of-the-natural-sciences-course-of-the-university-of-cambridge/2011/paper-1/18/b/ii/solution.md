<h1 id="18/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $w=x+iy$. Then $y-ix=-iw$, and the complex trigonometric identity $\sin(-iw)=-i\sinh w$ gives

$$
\sinh(x+iy)dx-\sin(y-ix)dy
=\sinh w(dx+i\,dy)=d[\cosh(x+iy)].
$$

Thus **the form is exact**, with complex-valued [scalar potential](../../../../../../scalar-potential.md) $F(x,y)=\cosh(x+iy)$. Differentiating that potential with respect to the two real variables checks both [coefficients](../../../../../../coefficient.md) individually.
