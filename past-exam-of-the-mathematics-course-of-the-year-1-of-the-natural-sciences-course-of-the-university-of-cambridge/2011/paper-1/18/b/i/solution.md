<h1 id="18/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For the printed [coefficients](../../../../../../coefficient.md), $p=2\sin x\cosh y$ and $q=\sin^2x\sinh y$. Their cross [derivatives](../../../../../../derivative.md) are

$$
p_y=2\sin x\sinh y,\qquad q_x=2\sin x\cos x\sinh y.
$$

They are not equal as [functions](../../../../../../function-split.md); their difference is $2\sin x(1-\cos x)\sinh y$. Hence **the form is not exact**. Agreement on certain [lines](../../../../../../straight-line.md) or isolated points is insufficient, because exactness requires the [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) identity throughout an open domain.
