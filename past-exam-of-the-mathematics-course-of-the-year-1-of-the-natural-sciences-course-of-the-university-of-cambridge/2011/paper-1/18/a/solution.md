<h1 id="18/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

If the form is $dF$ for a [scalar](../../../../../scalar.md) [function](../../../../../function-split.md) with continuous second [partial derivatives](../../../../../partial-derivative.md), then $p=F_x$ and $q=F_y$. Equality of the mixed [partial derivatives](../../../../../partial-derivative.md) therefore requires

$$
\boxed{\frac{\partial p}{\partial y}=\frac{\partial q}{\partial x}.}
$$

This is necessary for an [exact differential](../../../../../exact-differential.md). For continuously [differentiable](../../../../../differentiable-function.md) [coefficients](../../../../../coefficient.md) on a simply connected open domain it is also sufficient; on a domain with holes, the equality alone need not give a single-valued global potential.
