<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Differentiating the [hyperbolic cosine](../../../../../hyperbolic-cosine.md) with the [chain rule](../../../../../chain-rule.md) gives $g'(x)=3\cosh^2x\sinh x$. For real $x$, $\cosh x>0$, so the only zero of the [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) is $\sinh x=0$, namely $x=0$. Thus

$$
\boxed{x_1=0,\qquad g(x_1)=1.}
$$

Since $\cosh x\geq1$, this [stationary point](../../../../../stationary-point.md) is the [global minimum](../../../../../global-minimum.md).
