<h1 id="15/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

At an intersection, the two [position](../../../../../position.md) [vectors](../../../../../vector.md) agree, so $a-b=tn-sm$. The [scalar triple product](../../../../../scalar-triple-product.md) with $m,n$ is therefore

$$
[a-b,m,n]=t[n,m,n]-s[m,m,n]=\boxed{0},
$$

since a [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) with repeated columns vanishes. Geometrically the displacement of the two anchor points must lie in the same [plane](../../../../../plane.md) as the two [line](../../../../../straight-line.md) directions.

This is a necessary condition. If $m,n$ are not parallel, it is also sufficient, because their span is exactly the [plane](../../../../../plane.md) perpendicular to $m\times n$. For parallel directions the triple product vanishes automatically, even for distinct [lines](../../../../../straight-line.md), so the condition alone cannot guarantee intersection.
