↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [vector triple product identity](../../../../../vector-triple-product.md) gives

$$
\boxed{a\times(b\times c)=(a\cdot c)b-(a\cdot b)c.}
$$

It is the requested [linear combination](../../../../../linear-combination.md) of $b$ and $c$. The notation $\wedge$ in this question denotes the three-dimensional [cross product](../../../../../cross-product.md).
