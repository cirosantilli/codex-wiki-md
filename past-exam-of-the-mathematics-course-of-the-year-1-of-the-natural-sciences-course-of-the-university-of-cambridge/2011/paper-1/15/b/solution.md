<h1 id="15/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Expand the same double [cross product](../../../../../cross-product.md) in two ways. First expanding with $c\times d$ as the inner [cross product](../../../../../cross-product.md) gives

$$
(a\times b)\times(c\times d)
=[(a\times b)\cdot d]c-[(a\times b)\cdot c]d
=[a,b,d]c-[a,b,c]d.
$$

Alternatively, use $(a\times b)\times w=(a\cdot w)b-(b\cdot w)a$ with $w=c\times d$ to get

$$
(a\times b)\times(c\times d)=[a,c,d]b-[b,c,d]a.
$$

Equating these expressions and moving every term to one side proves

$$
[b,c,d]a-[a,c,d]b+[a,b,d]c-[a,b,c]d=0.
$$

This [four-vector scalar triple product identity](../../../../../four-vector-scalar-triple-product-identity.md) is valid without assuming that any three of the four [vectors](../../../../../vector.md) are independent.
