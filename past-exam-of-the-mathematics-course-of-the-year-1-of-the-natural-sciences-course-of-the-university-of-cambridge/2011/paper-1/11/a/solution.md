<h1 id="11/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The cubic [Taylor polynomial](../../../../../taylor-polynomial.md) about $a$ is

$$
\boxed{f(a)+f'(a)(x-a)+\frac{f''(a)}{2!}(x-a)^2+\frac{f^{(3)}(a)}{3!}(x-a)^3.}
$$

If $f$ has a continuous fourth [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) near $a$, [Taylor's theorem](../../../../../taylor-s-theorem.md) gives $f(x)$ equal to this [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) plus $O((x-a)^4)$. With continuous [derivatives](../../../../../derivative.md) only through third order, the corresponding remainder is $o((x-a)^3)$. An equality with an infinite [Taylor series](../../../../../taylor-series.md) would require additional convergence information, rather than just the existence of these four terms.
