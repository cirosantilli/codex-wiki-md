<h1 id="11/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Near zero the logarithm's argument is positive, so $\ln[(2+x)^3]=3\ln(2+x)=3\ln2+3\ln(1+x/2)$. Use the [Maclaurin series](../../../../../../maclaurin-series.md) of the [natural logarithm](../../../../../../natural-logarithm.md):

$$
\boxed{\ln[(2+x)^3]=3\ln2+\frac32x-\frac38x^2+\frac18x^3+O(x^4).}
$$

The local calculation uses $x>-2$ for the real logarithm; the [power series](../../../../../../power-series.md) converges for $|x|<2$.
