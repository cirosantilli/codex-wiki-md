<h1 id="11/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Use $\sin x=x-x^3/6+O(x^5)$ inside the [exponential series](../../../../../../exponential-series.md):

$$
e^{\sin x}=1+\left(x-\frac{x^3}{6}\right)+\frac{x^2}{2}
+\frac{x^3}{6}+O(x^4).
$$

The two cubic contributions cancel, leaving

$$
\boxed{e^{\sin x}=1+x+\frac{x^2}{2}+O(x^4).}
$$

Thus the [coefficient](../../../../../../coefficient.md) of $x^3$ is zero, even though the [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) itself is not an [even function](../../../../../../even-function.md).
