<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

At the stated point, the radial distance is $7\pi/2$, so $4\pi-t=7\pi/2$ and $t=\pi/2$. Differentiate the [parametric curve](../../../../../parametric-curve.md):

$$
\dot x=-\sin t+(4\pi-t)\cos t,\qquad
\dot y=-\cos t-(4\pi-t)\sin t.
$$

At $t=\pi/2$, these are $\dot x=-1$ and $\dot y=-7\pi/2$. Since $\dot x\ne0$, the [tangent line](../../../../../tangent-line.md) has finite [slope](../../../../../slope.md)

$$
\boxed{\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{\dot y}{\dot x}=\frac{7\pi}{2}.}
$$
