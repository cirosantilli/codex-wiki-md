<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The distance from the origin is $r=4\pi-t$. The direction angle, measured counterclockwise from the positive horizontal axis, is $\theta=\pi/2-t$ modulo $2\pi$. Thus the path is an [Archimedean spiral](../../../../../archimedean-spiral.md), turning clockwise through two complete revolutions as its [radius](../../../../../radius.md) decreases linearly from $4\pi$ to zero.

It starts at $(0,4\pi)$, reaches $(7\pi/2,0)$ at $t=\pi/2$, crosses $(0,-3\pi)$ at $t=\pi$, returns to the positive vertical axis at $(0,2\pi)$ when $t=2\pi$, and ends at the origin when $t=4\pi$. These crossings fix both the scale and direction of the sketch.
