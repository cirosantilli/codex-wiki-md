<h1 id="16/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [complementary function](../../../../../homogeneous-solution.md) is $C\cos2x+D\sin2x$. A [particular solution](../../../../../particular-solution.md) is $(\sin x+\cos x)/3$, since applying $d^2/dx^2+4$ to either [sine](../../../../../sine.md) or [cosine](../../../../../cosine.md) multiplies it by $3$. Thus

$$
y=C\cos2x+D\sin2x+\frac13(\sin x+\cos x).
$$

At zero, $C+1/3=3$, giving $C=8/3$. At $x=\pi/4$, the [cosine](../../../../../cosine.md) term vanishes and the particular term is $\sqrt2/3$, so $D=-\sqrt2/3$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{y(x)=\frac{8\cos2x-\sqrt2\sin2x+\sin x+\cos x}{3}.}
$$

The two boundary conditions determine the two constants uniquely because their evaluation matrix on $\cos2x,\sin2x$ is the identity.
