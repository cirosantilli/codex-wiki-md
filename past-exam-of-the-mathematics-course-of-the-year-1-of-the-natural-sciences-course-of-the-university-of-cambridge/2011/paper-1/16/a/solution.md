<h1 id="16/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Set $v=y'$ to reduce the [second-order linear differential equation](../../../../../second-order-linear-differential-equation.md) to $xv'+v=0$. Equivalently, $(xv)'=0$, so $v=C_1/x$ on any [interval](../../../../../interval-mathematics.md) excluding zero. The [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) condition at $x=1$ gives $C_1=2$.

Integrating on the positive [interval](../../../../../interval-mathematics.md) containing the initial point yields $y=2\ln x+C_2$. The value $y(1)=-1$ then fixes $C_2=-1$, giving

$$
\boxed{y(x)=2\ln x-1,\qquad x>0.}
$$

Its [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) is $2/x$, and direct substitution verifies the equation. The selected solution diverges as $x\downarrow0$ and cannot extend through zero as a finite-valued [function](../../../../../function-split.md).
