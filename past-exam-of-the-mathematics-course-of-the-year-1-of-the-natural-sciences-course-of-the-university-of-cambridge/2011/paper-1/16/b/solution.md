<h1 id="16/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Construct the [scalar potential](../../../../../scalar-potential.md) $F(x,y)=ye^{x^2}-x^2$. Its differential is

$$
dF=2x(ye^{x^2}-1)dx+e^{x^2}dy,
$$

which proves exactness directly. Multiplying the [differential equation](../../../../../differential-equation-split.md) by $e^{x^2}$ shows that a solution satisfies $dF/dx=0$, so $ye^{x^2}-x^2=C$. The initial value at zero gives $C=-1$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{y(x)=(x^2-1)e^{-x^2},\qquad x\in\mathbb R.}
$$

The factor $e^{x^2}$ is also an [integrating factor](../../../../../integrating-factor.md) for the associated first-order linear equation.
