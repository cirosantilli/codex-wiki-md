<h1 id="18/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For a real $2L$-periodic square-integrable function with the convention $f\sim a_0/2+\sum_{n\geq1}(a_n\cos(n\pi x/L)+b_n\sin(n\pi x/L))$, [Parseval's identity](../../../../../parseval-identity.md) is

$$
\boxed{\frac1L\int_{-L}^L|f(x)|^2dx
=\frac{a_0^2}{2}+\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}(a_n^2+b_n^2).}
$$

It states that the squared [L2 norm](../../../../../l2-norm.md) equals the sum of the squared components in the orthogonal [Fourier basis](../../../../../fourier-basis.md), with the normalization factors shown. In the present even-function case every $b_n$ vanishes.
