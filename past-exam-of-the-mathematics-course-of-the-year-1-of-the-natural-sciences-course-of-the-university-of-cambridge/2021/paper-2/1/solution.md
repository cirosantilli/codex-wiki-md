↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

The two orthogonal directions $(1,1)^T$ and $(1,-1)^T$ are preserved by this [symmetric matrix](../../../../symmetric-matrix.md). Direct multiplication, using the [hyperbolic function](../../../../hyperbolic-function.md) identities $\cosh u\pm\sinh u=e^{\pm u}$, gives the [eigenvalue](../../../../eigenvalue.md)–[eigenvector](../../../../eigenvector.md) pairs

$$
\boxed{\lambda_+=e^u,\quad \boldsymbol v_+=\binom11;
\qquad \lambda_-=e^{-u},\quad \boldsymbol v_-=\binom1{-1}.}
$$

Each [eigenvector](../../../../eigenvector.md) can be multiplied by any nonzero scalar. For $u\ne0$ these give the two one-dimensional [eigenspaces](../../../../eigenspace.md). When $u=0$, the [matrix](../../../../matrix.md) is the identity: the only [eigenvalue](../../../../eigenvalue.md) is $1$, with multiplicity two, and every nonzero [vector](../../../../vector.md) is an [eigenvector](../../../../eigenvector.md).
