<h1 id="14/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [probability density](../../../../../../probability-density.md) is symmetric about $300\,\mathrm{mL}$, vanishes at $270$ and $330\,\mathrm{mL}$, and has its single maximum $\pi/(120\,\mathrm{mL})$ at $300\,\mathrm{mL}$. It is zero outside that interval. Its area is one: with the dimensionless [change of variables](../../../../../../change-of-variables-formula.md)

$$
X=\frac{\pi(v-300\,\mathrm{mL})}{60\,\mathrm{mL}},
\qquad p_X(x)=\frac12\cos x,\quad -\frac\pi2\leq x\leq\frac\pi2,
$$

normalization is $\tfrac12\int_{-\pi/2}^{\pi/2}\cos x\,dx=1$.
