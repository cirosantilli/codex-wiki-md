↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The second [partial derivatives](../../../../../../partial-derivative.md) are $f_{xx}=4$, $f_{xy}=f_{yx}=3+2y$ and $f_{yy}=2+2x+30y$. Evaluating at the origin gives the [Hessian matrix](../../../../../../hessian-matrix.md)

$$
\boxed{H_f(0,0)=\begin{pmatrix}4&3\\3&2\end{pmatrix}.}
$$
