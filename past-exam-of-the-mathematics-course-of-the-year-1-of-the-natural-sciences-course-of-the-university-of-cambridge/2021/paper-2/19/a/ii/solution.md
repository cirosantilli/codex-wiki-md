<h1 id="19/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) of the [Hessian matrix](../../../../../../hessian-matrix.md) is $4\cdot2-3^2=-1<0$. Hence it has one positive and one negative [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) (explicitly $3\pm\sqrt{10}$), so its quadratic form is indefinite. The cubic terms are of higher order and cannot remove these opposite local directions of change. Therefore

$$
\boxed{(0,0)\text{ is a saddle point}.}
$$

For example the quadratic term is positive along $y=0$ but negative along $y=-3x/2$, confirming the [saddle point of a scalar function](../../../../../../saddle-point-of-a-scalar-function.md) classification.
