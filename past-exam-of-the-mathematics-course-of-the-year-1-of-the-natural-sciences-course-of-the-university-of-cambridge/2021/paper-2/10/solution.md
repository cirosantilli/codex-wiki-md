<h1 id="10/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [10](../10.md)

On the disc $z=0$, the upward normal selects only the third component, which equals $e^0/(1+0)=1$. Hence the [surface integral](../../../../surface-integral.md) is the area of the [unit disk](../../../../unit-disk.md):

$$
\boxed{\int_D\boldsymbol F\cdot\boldsymbol n\,dS
=\int_D1\,dS=\pi.}
$$
