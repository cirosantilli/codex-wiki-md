<h1 id="16/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The logarithm is defined away from the $z$ axis, where $x^2+y^2>0$. For the [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) potential $\psi\widehat{\boldsymbol e}_z=(0,0,\psi)$, its [curl](../../../../../../curl.md) is $(\psi_y,-\psi_x,0)$. Since

$$
\psi_x=\frac{ax}{x^2+y^2},\qquad \psi_y=\frac{ay}{x^2+y^2},
$$

we obtain

$$
\boxed{\boldsymbol u=\left(\frac{ay}{x^2+y^2},-\frac{ax}{x^2+y^2},0\right),\qquad x^2+y^2>0.}
$$

For $a>0$ this is clockwise circulation about the axis, the velocity structure of a [point vortex](../../../../../../line-vortex.md) in each transverse plane.
