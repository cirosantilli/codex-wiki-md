<h1 id="16/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

On the same domain, differentiate the Cartesian components:

$$
\nabla\cdot\boldsymbol u
=-\frac{2axy}{(x^2+y^2)^2}
+\frac{2axy}{(x^2+y^2)^2}+0.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{\nabla\cdot\boldsymbol u=0\qquad(x^2+y^2>0).}
$$

This is also an instance of [divergence of a curl is zero](../../../../../../divergence-of-a-curl-is-zero.md), since the potential is smooth off the axis. The classical field is undefined on the axis, so that identity must not be applied there without specifying an extension.
