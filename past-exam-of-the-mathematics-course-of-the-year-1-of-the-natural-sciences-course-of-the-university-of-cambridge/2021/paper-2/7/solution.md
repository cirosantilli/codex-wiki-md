<h1 id="7/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [7](../7.md)

Normalization of the [probability mass function](../../../../probability-mass-function.md) and the [geometric series](../../../../geometric-series.md) give

$$
1=c\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}e^{-n}=\frac c{1-e^{-1}},
\qquad \boxed{c=1-e^{-1}.}
$$

The tail is another [geometric series](../../../../geometric-series.md):

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(X\geq N)
=c\sum_{n=N}^{\infty}e^{-n}
=\frac{ce^{-N}}{1-e^{-1}}=e^{-N}.}
$$
