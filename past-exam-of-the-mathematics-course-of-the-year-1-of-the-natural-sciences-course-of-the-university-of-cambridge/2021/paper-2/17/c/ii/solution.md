↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [matrix trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md) and [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) of the [matrices](../../../../../../matrix.md) in part (i) are

$$
\operatorname{Tr}Y=2\alpha,\qquad \det Y=\alpha^2+2\beta^2.
$$

Since $\operatorname{Tr}X=0$ and $\det X=2$, the extra conditions force $\alpha=0$ and $\beta^2=1$. Hence

$$
\boxed{Y=X\quad\text{or}\quad Y=-X.}
$$

Both choices are real, commute with $X$, and satisfy the required trace and [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) conditions.
