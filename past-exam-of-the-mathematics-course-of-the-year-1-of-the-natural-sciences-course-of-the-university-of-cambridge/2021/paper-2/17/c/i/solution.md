<h1 id="17/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $Y=\begin{pmatrix}p&q\\r&s\end{pmatrix}$. The [commutativity](../../../../../../commutativity.md) condition gives

$$
XY=\begin{pmatrix}2r&2s\\-p&-q\end{pmatrix},\qquad
YX=\begin{pmatrix}-q&2p\\-s&2r\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Equality is equivalent to $s=p$ and $q=-2r$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{Y=\begin{pmatrix}\alpha&2\beta\\-\beta&\alpha\end{pmatrix}
=\alpha I+\beta X,\qquad \alpha,\beta\in\mathbb R.}
$$

Each displayed [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) commutes with $X$, since it is a polynomial in $X$. The [centralizer of a nonscalar two by two matrix](../../../../../../centralizer-of-a-nonscalar-two-by-two-matrix.md) explains why this two-parameter form is typical.
