<h1 id="17/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Use [Einstein notation](../../../../../../einstein-notation.md), with repeated suffixes summed. The [matrix multiplication](../../../../../../matrix-multiplication.md) and transpose give

$$
C_{ij}=A_{ik}(AB)_{jk}=A_{ik}A_{j\ell}B_{\ell k}.
$$

Interchange the free indices, then relabel the dummy indices $k,\ell$:

$$
C_{ji}=A_{jk}A_{i\ell}B_{\ell k}
=A_{j\ell}A_{ik}B_{k\ell}
=-A_{ik}A_{j\ell}B_{\ell k}=-C_{ij}.
$$

The negative sign uses $B_{k\ell}=-B_{\ell k}$. Therefore **$C$ is skew-symmetric**. No orthogonality of $A$ is needed for this part: [matrix congruence](../../../../../../matrix-congruence.md) preserves skew-symmetry.
