<h1 id="2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

The boundary order fixes the orientation. Two edges issuing from $A$ are $\overrightarrow{AB}=(0,0,1)$ and $\overrightarrow{AD}=(1,1,0)$. Their [cross product](../../../../cross-product.md) gives the oriented [vector area](../../../../vector-area.md):

$$
\boxed{\boldsymbol A=\overrightarrow{AB}\times\overrightarrow{AD}
=\begin{pmatrix}-1\\1\\0\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

Its [norm](../../../../norm.md) is $\sqrt2$, the ordinary rectangle area. The sign agrees with traversing $A\to B\to C\to D\to A$ by the right-hand orientation rule.
