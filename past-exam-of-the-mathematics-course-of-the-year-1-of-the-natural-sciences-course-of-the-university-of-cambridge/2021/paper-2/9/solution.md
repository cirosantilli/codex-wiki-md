<h1 id="9/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [9](../9.md)

The [chain rule](../../../../chain-rule.md) gives

$$
u_t=a f'(x+at),\qquad u_x=f'(x+at),\qquad
u_t+u_x=(a+1)f'(x+at).
$$

For this to vanish for every differentiable $f$, its coefficient must be zero:

$$
\boxed{a=-1.}
$$

The resulting $f(x-t)$ is a profile transported to the right at unit speed by the [transport equation](../../../../transport-equation-split.md).
