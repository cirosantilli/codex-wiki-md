<h1 id="13/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Split the [vector field](../../../../../vector-field.md) into $a(-y,x,0)$ and $e^{-r^2}(x,y,z)$. The second part is the [gradient](../../../../../gradient.md) of $-\tfrac12e^{-r^2}$, so its [curl](../../../../../curl.md) vanishes. The first part has only a vertical [curl](../../../../../curl.md) component, $\partial_x(ax)-\partial_y(-ay)=2a$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\nabla\times\boldsymbol F=(0,0,2a).}
$$
