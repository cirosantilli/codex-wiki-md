<h1 id="13/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The upward unit [vector](../../../../../vector.md) extracts the vertical [curl](../../../../../curl.md) component:

$$
(\nabla\times\boldsymbol F)\cdot\boldsymbol k=2a.
$$

It is constant over the unit disc, so

$$
\boxed{I=\int_D2a\,dS=2\pi a.}
$$

This agrees with part (d) by [Stokes theorem](../../../../../stokes-theorem.md): the boundary circle has counterclockwise orientation when viewed from the upward normal, including for the parametrizations $2\pi t^n$.
