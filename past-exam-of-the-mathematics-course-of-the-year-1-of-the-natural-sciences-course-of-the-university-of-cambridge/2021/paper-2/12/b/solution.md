<h1 id="12/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Set $D=1+x^2+y^2>0$. The [quotient rule](../../../../../quotient-rule.md) gives the [partial derivatives](../../../../../partial-derivative.md)

$$
f_x=-\frac{4x(1+y+y^2)}{D^2},\qquad
f_y=\frac{2(1+x^2-y^2+2x^2y)}{D^2}.
$$

Since $1+y+y^2=(y+1/2)^2+3/4>0$, the first equation $f_x=0$ forces $x=0$. The second then reduces to $1-y^2=0$. Thus the only [stationary points](../../../../../stationary-point.md) and their field values are

$$
\boxed{(x,y)=(0,1),\ f=1;\qquad (x,y)=(0,-1),\ f=-1.}
$$

No division by a potentially vanishing factor of $y$ was used, so no stationary points have been discarded.
