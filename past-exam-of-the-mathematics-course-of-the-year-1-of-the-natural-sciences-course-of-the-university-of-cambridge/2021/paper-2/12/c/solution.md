<h1 id="12/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [level set](../../../../../level-set.md) $f=c$ can be rearranged, without changing its domain, as

$$
(c+2)x^2+cy^2-2y+c=0.
$$

The requested special contours are therefore

$$
\boxed{f=0:\ y=x^2;\qquad
f=1:\ (x,y)=(0,1);\qquad
f=-1:\ y+1=\pm x.}
$$

The first is a [parabola](../../../../../parabola.md), the second a single point, and the last two intersecting straight lines. Moreover

$$
1-f=\frac{3x^2+(y-1)^2}{D}\geq0,
$$

with equality only at $(0,1)$. Hence $(0,1)$ is a strict global maximum. At $(0,-1)$ the crossing critical contour separates sectors with values above and below $-1$, so it is a [saddle point of a scalar function](../../../../../saddle-point-of-a-scalar-function.md).

As a [Hessian matrix](../../../../../hessian-matrix.md) check, $H_f(0,1)=\operatorname{diag}(-3,-1)$ is negative definite, while $H_f(0,-1)=\operatorname{diag}(-1,1)$ is indefinite. There is no stationary minimum.
