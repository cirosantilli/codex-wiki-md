<h1 id="12/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For $c\ne0$, [completing the square](../../../../../completing-the-square.md) gives the [conic section](../../../../../conic-section.md)

$$
(c+2)x^2+c\left(y-\frac1c\right)^2=\frac{1-c^2}{c}.
$$

The possible levels follow from

$$
f+2=\frac{2(y^2+y+1)}{D}>0,\qquad f\leq1:
\qquad \boxed{-2<f\leq1.}
$$

For $0<c<1$, the contours are [ellipses](../../../../../ellipse.md) centred at $(0,1/c)$, shrinking to the maximum as $c\uparrow1$. The zero contour is the [parabola](../../../../../parabola.md) from part (c). For $-1<c<0$, the contours are vertical-opening [hyperbolas](../../../../../hyperbola.md). At $c=-1$ they degenerate into the intersecting straight lines, and for $-2<c<-1$ they are horizontal-opening [hyperbolas](../../../../../hyperbola.md). These descriptions specify the remaining contour families and their symmetry about the $y$ axis. The lower bound $-2$ is approached as $|x|\to\infty$ at fixed $y$ but is never attained.
