<h1 id="11/d/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The square $d(t)^2=(t+1)^2+1/2$ is minimized when its first term vanishes. Therefore

$$
\boxed{t_{\min}=-1,\qquad d_{\min}=\frac1{\sqrt2}=s.}
$$

This agrees with the [distance between skew lines](../../../../../../distance-between-skew-lines.md) found in part (b). The corresponding parameter on the first line is its [orthogonal projection](../../../../../../orthogonal-projection.md) value

$$
\lambda_{\min}=\frac{(\boldsymbol r_2(-1)-\boldsymbol a_1)\cdot\boldsymbol n_1}{\|\boldsymbol n_1\|^2}=-\frac32.
$$

The closest pair is $(-1/2,-3/2,0)$ and $(-1,-1,0)$; their connecting [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) $(-1/2,1/2,0)$ is perpendicular to both direction [vectors](../../../../../../vector.md).
