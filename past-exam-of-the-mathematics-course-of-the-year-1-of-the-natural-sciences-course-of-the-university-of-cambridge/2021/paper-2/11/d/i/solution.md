<h1 id="11/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a point $\boldsymbol p=\boldsymbol r_2(t)$, the magnitude of $(\boldsymbol p-\boldsymbol a_1)\times\boldsymbol n_1$ is the area of a parallelogram. Dividing by its base length $\|\boldsymbol n_1\|$ gives the perpendicular [Euclidean distance](../../../../../../euclidean-distance.md) to the first line. Explicitly,

$$
\boldsymbol p-\boldsymbol a_1=(t-1,t,t+1),\qquad
(\boldsymbol p-\boldsymbol a_1)\times\boldsymbol n_1=(-(t+1),t+1,-1).
$$

Hence

$$
\boxed{d(t)=\frac{\sqrt{2(t+1)^2+1}}{\sqrt2}
=\sqrt{(t+1)^2+\frac12}.}
$$

This use of the [cross product](../../../../../../cross-product.md) removes any component parallel to the first line automatically.
