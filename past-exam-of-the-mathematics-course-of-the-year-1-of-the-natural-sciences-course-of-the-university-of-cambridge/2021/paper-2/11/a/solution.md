<h1 id="11/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The evaluated position [vectors](../../../../../vector.md) are $\boldsymbol r_1(1)=(2,1,0)$ and $\boldsymbol r_2(-1)=(-1,-1,0)$. Their difference is $(3,2,0)$, so the [Euclidean distance](../../../../../euclidean-distance.md) is

$$
\boxed{\|\boldsymbol r_1(1)-\boldsymbol r_2(-1)\|=\sqrt{3^2+2^2}=\sqrt{13}.}
$$
