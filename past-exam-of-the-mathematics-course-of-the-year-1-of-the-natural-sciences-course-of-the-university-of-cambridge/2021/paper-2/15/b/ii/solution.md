<h1 id="15/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [characteristic equation](../../../../../../characteristic-equation-of-a-constant-coefficient-differential-equation.md) of the homogeneous equation is $\lambda^2-4=0$, giving exponential solutions $e^{2t}$ and $e^{-2t}$. A constant particular solution is $v_p=-1/2$. Thus the [general solution](../../../../../../general-solution.md) is

$$
\boxed{v(t)=Ae^{2t}+Be^{-2t}-\frac12,\qquad A,B\in\mathbb R.}
$$
