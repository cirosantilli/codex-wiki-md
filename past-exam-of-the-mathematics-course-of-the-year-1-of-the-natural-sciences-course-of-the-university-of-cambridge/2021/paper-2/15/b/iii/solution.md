<h1 id="15/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The equation $u=v'$ fixes $u$ from the same two constants:

$$
\boxed{u(t)=2Ae^{2t}-2Be^{-2t}.}
$$

Indeed $u'=4Ae^{2t}+4Be^{-2t}=4v+2$, so both original equations hold. No further independent integration constant is available in this two-dimensional [linear system of ordinary differential equations](../../../../../../linear-system-of-differential-equations.md).
