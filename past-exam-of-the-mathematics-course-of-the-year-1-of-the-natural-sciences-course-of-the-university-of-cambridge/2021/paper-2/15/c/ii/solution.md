<h1 id="15/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For the [general solution](../../../../../../general-solution.md) in part (i),

$$
y(0)=A+B=1,\qquad y'(0)=-\frac34-2B=0.
$$

Thus $B=-3/8$ and $A=11/8$, giving the solution of the [initial value problem](../../../../../../initial-value-problem.md)

$$
\boxed{y(x)=\frac34x^2-\frac34x+\frac{11}{8}-\frac38e^{-2x}.}
$$

Direct substitution checks both the differential equation and the two initial conditions.
