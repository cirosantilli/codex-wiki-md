<h1 id="15/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The dependent variable itself does not occur, so reduce order by putting $p=y'$. Then $p'+2p=3x$, a [first-order linear differential equation](../../../../../../first-order-linear-differential-equation.md). Its [integrating factor](../../../../../../integrating-factor.md) is $e^{2x}$:

$$
(e^{2x}p)'=3xe^{2x},\qquad
p=\frac32x-\frac34+Ce^{-2x}.
$$

Integrating once more and renaming the constants gives

$$
\boxed{y(x)=\frac34x^2-\frac34x+A+Be^{-2x}.}
$$

The two homogeneous terms are the constant solution and the exponential associated with the roots $0,-2$ of the [characteristic equation](../../../../../../characteristic-equation-of-a-constant-coefficient-differential-equation.md).
