<h1 id="5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

Complete the square in the velocity variable:

$$
H=V(x)+pv-\frac12mv^2
=V(x)+\frac{p^2}{2m}-\frac m2\left(v-\frac pm\right)^2.
$$

Since $m>0$, the final term is nonpositive and vanishes at exactly one velocity. Therefore

$$
\boxed{v_*=\frac pm,\qquad H_{\max}(x,p)=\frac{p^2}{2m}+V(x).}
$$

This is the [Legendre transform in mechanics](../../../../legendre-transform-in-mechanics.md) that constructs the [Hamiltonian mechanics](../../../../hamiltonian-mechanics-split.md) energy from the kinetic-minus-potential Lagrangian.
