<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

For $n=0$, the [change of variables](../../../../change-of-variables-formula.md) $s=\sqrt a\,x$ reduces the [integral](../../../../integral-split.md) to the given [Gaussian integral](../../../../gaussian-integral.md):

$$
\boxed{\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}e^{-ax^2}dx
=\frac1{\sqrt a}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}e^{-s^2}ds=\sqrt{\frac\pi a}.}
$$

For $n=1$, the integrand is an integrable [odd function](../../../../odd-function.md), so its contributions from the two half-lines cancel:

$$
\boxed{\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}x e^{-ax^2}dx=0.}
$$

The condition $a>0$ ensures convergence in both cases.
