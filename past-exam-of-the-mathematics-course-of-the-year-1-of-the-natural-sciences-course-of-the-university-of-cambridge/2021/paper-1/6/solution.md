<h1 id="6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

Both segments start at $(1,1,1)$, so their direction [vectors](../../../../vector.md) are $\mathbf a=(-1,-1,-1)$ and $\mathbf b=(1,1,0)$. Their [dot product](../../../../dot-product.md) is $-2$, and their [norms](../../../../norm.md) are $\sqrt3$ and $\sqrt2$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\cos\theta=\frac{\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b}{|\mathbf a||\mathbf b|}=-\frac2{\sqrt6}=-\sqrt{\frac23}.}
$$

The angle between the rays lies in $[0,\pi]$, so its [sine](../../../../sine.md) is nonnegative:

$$
\boxed{\sin\theta=\sqrt{1-\cos^2\theta}=\frac1{\sqrt3}.}
$$

The negative [cosine](../../../../cosine.md) correctly identifies an obtuse angle. One also obtains the [sine](../../../../sine.md) from $|\mathbf a\times\mathbf b|/(|\mathbf a||\mathbf b|)=\sqrt2/\sqrt6$, using the [cross product](../../../../cross-product.md).
