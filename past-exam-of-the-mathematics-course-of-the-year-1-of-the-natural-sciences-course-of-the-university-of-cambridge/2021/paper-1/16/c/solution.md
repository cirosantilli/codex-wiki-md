<h1 id="16/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Of the ten outcomes in $F$, all except $(1,1,1)$ have exactly one repeated pair. Hence $P(T\mid F)=9/10$. Applying [Bayes' theorem](../../../../../bayes-theorem.md) in the requested direction,

$$
\boxed{P(F\mid T)=\frac{P(T\mid F)P(F)}{P(T)}=\frac{(9/10)(10/216)}{90/216}=\frac1{10}.}
$$

Equivalently there are nine favorable outcomes among the ninety outcomes in $T$. This direct count confirms the [conditional probability](../../../../../conditional-probability.md) calculation.
