<h1 id="16/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Choose the first face in six ways, the second in five ways avoiding the first, and the third in four ways avoiding both. Thus

$$
\boxed{P(D)=\frac{6\cdot5\cdot4}{216}=\frac59.}
$$

Each ordered outcome is equally likely in this [sample space](../../../../../../sample-space.md), so dividing the count by $216$ gives the [probability](../../../../../../probability.md).
