<h1 id="16/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The [sample space](../../../../../../sample-space.md) outcomes with qualifying sums have totals three, four and five. Their ordered patterns are $(1,1,1)$, the three permutations of $(1,1,2)$, and the three permutations each of $(1,1,3)$ and $(1,2,2)$. Thus there are $1+3+3+3=10$ qualifying outcomes:

$$
\boxed{P(F)=\frac{10}{216}=\frac5{108}.}
$$
