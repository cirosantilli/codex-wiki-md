<h1 id="16/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Assume the three standard dice are fair and [independent](../../../../../independent-random-variables.md). For one die $X$, a [discrete uniform distribution](../../../../../discrete-uniform-distribution.md) on $1,\ldots,6$ gives

$$
\mathbb EX=\frac{1+2+3+4+5+6}{6}=\frac72,\qquad\mathbb EX^2=\frac{1+4+9+16+25+36}{6}=\frac{91}6.
$$

The [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) is $\operatorname{Var}X=\mathbb EX^2-(\mathbb EX)^2=35/12$. For the sum $Q=X_1+X_2+X_3$, [linearity of expectation](../../../../../linearity-of-expectation.md) and addition of [variances](../../../../../variance-split.md) for [independent random variables](../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) give

$$
\boxed{\mathbb EQ=\frac{21}2,\qquad\operatorname{Var}Q=3\frac{35}{12}=\frac{35}4.}
$$
