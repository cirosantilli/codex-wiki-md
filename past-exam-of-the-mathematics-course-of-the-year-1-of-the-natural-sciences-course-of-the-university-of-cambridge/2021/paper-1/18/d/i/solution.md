<h1 id="18/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

An [orthogonal matrix](../../../../../../orthogonal-matrix.md) has orthonormal columns. The first column of $L$ has squared [norm](../../../../../../norm.md) $1+c^2$, so $c=0$. Orthogonality of the two columns then gives $-b+cd=-b=0$, hence $b=0$. The second column must have squared [norm](../../../../../../norm.md) $d^2=1$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{b=c=0,\qquad d=\pm1.}
$$

Both resulting matrices satisfy $L^TL=I$ directly.
