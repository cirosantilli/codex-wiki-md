<h1 id="18/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The expression assumes $Y$ is invertible. By the [cyclic property of the trace](../../../../../cyclic-property-of-the-trace.md),

$$
\operatorname{Tr}(YXY^{-1})=\operatorname{Tr}(XY^{-1}Y)=\operatorname{Tr}X.
$$

For a real or complex [antisymmetric matrix](../../../../../skew-symmetric-matrix.md), $X^T=-X$ forces every diagonal entry to satisfy $x_{ii}=-x_{ii}$, hence $x_{ii}=0$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Tr}Z=\operatorname{Tr}X=0.}
$$

This also demonstrates invariance of the [matrix trace](../../../../../matrix-trace.md) under [matrix similarity](../../../../../matrix-similarity.md). Antisymmetry of $Z$ itself is not needed.
