<h1 id="18/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Expanding the [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) along the first row gives

$$
\det M=-a^2-b^2+3b+3.
$$

Under the [matrix trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md) condition $a^2=3$, this simplifies to $\det M=b(3-b)$. A square [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) is invertible exactly when its [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) is nonzero. Thus

$$
\boxed{a=\pm\sqrt3,\qquad b\in\mathbb R\setminus\{0,3\}.}
$$
