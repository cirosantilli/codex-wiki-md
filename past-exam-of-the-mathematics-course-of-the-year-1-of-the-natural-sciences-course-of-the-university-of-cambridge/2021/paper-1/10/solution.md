<h1 id="10/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [10](../10.md)

By [implicit differentiation](../../../../implicit-differentiation.md) of the [ellipse](../../../../ellipse.md) equation,

$$
18x+32y\frac{dy}{dx}=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\left.\frac{dy}{dx}\right|_{(1,1)}=-\frac9{16}.
$$

The [tangent line](../../../../tangent-line.md) through $(1,1)$ is therefore

$$
\boxed{y-1=-\frac9{16}(x-1),\qquad\text{equivalently }9x+16y=25.}
$$

Its [normal vector](../../../../normal-vector.md) is $(9,16)$, parallel to the [gradient](../../../../gradient.md) $(18,32)$ of $9x^2+16y^2$ at that point.
