<h1 id="9/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [9](../9.md)

The [tangent half-angle substitution](../../../../tangent-half-angle-substitution.md) gives $\sin\theta=2t/(1+t^2)$ and $\cos\theta=(1-t^2)/(1+t^2)$. Dividing and then taking the reciprocal square of the [cosine](../../../../cosine.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\tan\theta=\frac{2t}{1-t^2},\qquad\sec^2\theta=\frac{(1+t^2)^2}{(1-t^2)^2}.}
$$

These formulas apply where $t$ is defined and $t\ne\pm1$; at $t=\pm1$ the original [tangent](../../../../tangent.md) and [secant function](../../../../secant-trigonometry.md) are undefined because $\cos\theta=0$.
