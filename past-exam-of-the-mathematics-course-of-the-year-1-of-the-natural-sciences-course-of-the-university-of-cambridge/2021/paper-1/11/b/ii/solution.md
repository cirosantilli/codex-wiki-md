<h1 id="11/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Squaring gives

$$
u=z^2=t^2-t^{-2}+2i.
$$

Thus $\operatorname{Im}u=2$ is constant, while $d(\operatorname{Re}u)/dt=2t+2t^{-3}>0$. The trajectory is the horizontal line segment

$$
\boxed{u:\ (-15/4,2)\longrightarrow(15/4,2),\qquad\operatorname{Im}u=2.}
$$

It crosses the [imaginary axis](../../../../../../imaginary-axis.md) at $\boxed{(0,2)}$, when $t=1$, and never crosses the [real axis](../../../../../../real-axis.md). The middle panel shows the rightward direction.
