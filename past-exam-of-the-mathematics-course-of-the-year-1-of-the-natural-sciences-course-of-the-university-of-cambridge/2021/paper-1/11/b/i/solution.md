<h1 id="11/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Write $z=X+iY$. Its coordinates are $X=t$, $Y=1/t$, so $XY=1$. For the given positive range of $t$, this traces a segment of the first-quadrant [hyperbola](../../../../../../hyperbola.md), moving rightward and downward:

$$
\boxed{z:\ (1/2,2)\longrightarrow(2,1/2).}
$$

The [complex conjugate](../../../../../../complex-conjugate.md) is $z^*=t-i/t$. It is the reflection in the [real axis](../../../../../../real-axis.md), tracing $XY=-1$ in the fourth quadrant, rightward and upward:

$$
\boxed{z^*:\ (1/2,-2)\longrightarrow(2,-1/2).}
$$

Neither trajectory crosses either axis, because $t$ and $1/t$ remain nonzero. The left panel of the figure marks the endpoints and directions.
