<h1 id="11/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Put $s=t^2-t^{-2}$. Part (b)(ii) gives $u=s+2i$, so

$$
v=u^2=(s^2-4)+4si.
$$

The parameter $s$ increases strictly from $-15/4$ to $15/4$. Therefore the [imaginary part](../../../../../../imaginary-part.md) $Y=4s$ increases from $-15$ to $15$, while the [real part](../../../../../../real-part.md) is $X=s^2-4$. The endpoints are

$$
\boxed{v:\ (161/16,-15)\longrightarrow(161/16,15).}
$$

The [real axis](../../../../../../real-axis.md) crossing is at $s=0$, giving $\boxed{(-4,0)}$ at $t=1$. The two [imaginary axis](../../../../../../imaginary-axis.md) crossings satisfy $s^2=4$, giving

$$
\boxed{(0,-8)\text{ at }t=\sqrt{\sqrt2-1},\qquad(0,8)\text{ at }t=\sqrt{\sqrt2+1}.}
$$

In order, the trajectory passes from its lower endpoint through $(0,-8)$, then the leftmost point $(-4,0)$, then $(0,8)$, to its upper endpoint. It is a right-opening [parabola](../../../../../../parabola.md) segment, traversed continuously upward as shown in the right panel.
