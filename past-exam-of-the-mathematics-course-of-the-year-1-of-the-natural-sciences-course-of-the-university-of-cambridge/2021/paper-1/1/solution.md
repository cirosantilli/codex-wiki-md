<h1 id="1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Apply the [chain rule](../../../../chain-rule.md) twice: once to the square and once to the argument $x^3$ of the [sine](../../../../sine.md). The inner [derivative](../../../../derivative.md) is $y'=3x^2\cos(x^3)-2\sin(2x)$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\frac{d(y^2)}{dx}=2\bigl[\sin(x^3)+\cos(2x)\bigr]\bigl[3x^2\cos(x^3)-2\sin(2x)\bigr].}
$$

The original PDF has $\sin(x^3)$, rather than the cube of $\sin x$.
