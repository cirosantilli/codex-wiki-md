<h1 id="14/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Put $D=\sigma^2>0$ and $\xi=x-x_0$. Logarithmic [differentiation](../../../../../differentiation.md) of the positive [Gaussian function](../../../../../gaussian-function.md) gives

$$
\phi_t=\left(-\frac1{2t}+\frac{\xi^2}{4Dt^2}\right)\phi,\qquad\phi_x=-\frac{\xi}{2Dt}\phi.
$$

Differentiating once more in space gives

$$
\phi_{xx}=\left(-\frac1{2Dt}+\frac{\xi^2}{4D^2t^2}\right)\phi.
$$

Multiplying by $D$ proves

$$
\boxed{\phi_t=D\phi_{xx}=\sigma^2\phi_{xx}\qquad(t>0).}
$$

The assumption $\sigma\ne0$ ensures both the normalization and exponential denominator are defined. This is the [heat kernel](../../../../../heat-kernel.md), with [diffusion coefficient](../../../../../diffusion-coefficient.md) $D$ and centre $x_0$.
