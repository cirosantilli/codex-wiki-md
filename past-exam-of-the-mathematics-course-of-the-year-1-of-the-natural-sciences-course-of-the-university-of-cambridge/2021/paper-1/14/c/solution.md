<h1 id="14/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The polar-coordinate [Jacobian matrix](../../../../../jacobian-matrix.md) and its [Jacobian determinant](../../../../../jacobian-determinant.md) are

$$
\frac{\partial(x,y)}{\partial(r,\theta)}=\begin{pmatrix}\cos\theta&-r\sin\theta\\\sin\theta&r\cos\theta\end{pmatrix},\qquad\boxed{J=r\cos^2\theta+r\sin^2\theta=r.}
$$

For $r>0$, the corresponding [area element](../../../../../area-element-of-a-surface.md) is $dx\,dy=r\,dr\,d\theta$. The determinant vanishes at $r=0$, where the polar angle does not give a unique coordinate representation.
