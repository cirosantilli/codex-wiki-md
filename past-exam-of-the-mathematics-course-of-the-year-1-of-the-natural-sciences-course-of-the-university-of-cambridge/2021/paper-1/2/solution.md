<h1 id="2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Find two numbers with sum $-1$ and product $-2$: they are $-2$ and $1$. Thus the [quadratic polynomial](../../../../quadratic-polynomial.md) factorizes as

$$
\boxed{x^2-x-2=(x-2)(x+1).}
$$

The product is negative precisely when its two factors have opposite signs. This occurs between its two [polynomial roots](../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md), so

$$
\boxed{-1<x<2.}
$$

The endpoints give zero and are excluded by the strict inequality.
