<h1 id="17/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A suitable real [antiderivative](../../../../../../antiderivative.md) on the disconnected domain is

$$
\boxed{G(x)=\ln|x|,\qquad x\ne0.}
$$

For $x>0$, $G'=1/x$ directly; for $x<0$, $G=\ln(-x)$ and the [chain rule](../../../../../../chain-rule.md) again gives $G'=1/x$. The graph is even, crosses zero at $x=\pm1$, tends to $-\infty$ on either side of zero, and tends to $+\infty$ as $|x|\to\infty$. Independent additive constants may be chosen on the two disconnected half-lines; both are set to zero here.
