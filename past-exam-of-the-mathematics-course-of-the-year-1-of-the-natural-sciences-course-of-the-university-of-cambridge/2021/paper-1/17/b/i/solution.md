<h1 id="17/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

On the negative half-line, [differentiation](../../../../../../differentiation.md) gives $d\ln(-u)/du=1/u$. The integration path from $-1$ to $x<0$ stays away from the singularity at zero. Therefore

$$
\boxed{F(x)=\int_{-1}^{x}\frac{du}{u}=\ln(-x)-\ln1=\ln(-x),\qquad x<0.}
$$

This choice satisfies $F(-1)=0$.
