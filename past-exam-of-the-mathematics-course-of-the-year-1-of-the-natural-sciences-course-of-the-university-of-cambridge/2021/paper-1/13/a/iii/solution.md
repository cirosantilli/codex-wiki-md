<h1 id="13/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Combine the [complementary function](../../../../../../homogeneous-solution.md) and [particular solution](../../../../../../particular-solution.md):

$$
y=C_1\cos x+C_2\sin x+\frac12+\frac16\cos2x.
$$

The [boundary condition](../../../../../../boundary-condition.md) at zero gives $C_1+2/3=0$, while that at $\pi/2$ gives $C_2+1/3=0$. Hence

$$
\boxed{y(x)=-\frac23\cos x-\frac13\sin x+\frac12+\frac16\cos2x.}
$$

The two [boundary conditions](../../../../../../boundary-condition.md) determine both constants uniquely; substitution at each endpoint gives zero.
