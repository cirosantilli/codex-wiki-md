↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [homogeneous linear differential equation](../../../../../../homogeneous-linear-differential-equation.md) is $y''+y=0$. Its [characteristic equation](../../../../../../characteristic-equation-of-a-constant-coefficient-differential-equation.md) is $\lambda^2+1=0$, with roots $\pm i$. The general real [complementary function](../../../../../../homogeneous-solution.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{y_h(x)=C_1\cos x+C_2\sin x.}
$$

The two trigonometric solutions are linearly independent, supplying the two arbitrary constants required for this second-order [linear ordinary differential equation](../../../../../../linear-ordinary-differential-equation-split.md).
