<h1 id="13/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Use the [method of undetermined coefficients](../../../../../../method-of-undetermined-coefficients.md), choosing a [particular solution](../../../../../../particular-solution.md) $y_p=A+B\cos2x$. Substitution gives

$$
y_p''+y_p=A-3B\cos2x.
$$

Matching the constant and [cosine](../../../../../../cosine.md) coefficients to $\tfrac12-\tfrac12\cos2x$ gives $A=1/2$, $B=1/6$. Thus

$$
\boxed{y_p(x)=\frac12+\frac16\cos2x.}
$$

Neither forcing term is resonant with the homogeneous [angular frequency](../../../../../../angular-frequency.md) one.
