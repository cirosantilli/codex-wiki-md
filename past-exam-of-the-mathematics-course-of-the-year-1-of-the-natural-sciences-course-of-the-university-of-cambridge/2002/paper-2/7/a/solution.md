<h1 id="7/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [characteristic equation](../../../../../characteristic-equation-of-a-constant-coefficient-differential-equation.md) of the associated homogeneous [linear ordinary differential equation](../../../../../linear-ordinary-differential-equation-split.md) is $m^2+5m+6=(m+2)(m+3)=0$, so the [complementary function](../../../../../homogeneous-solution.md) is $C_1e^{-2x}+C_2e^{-3x}$. For a [particular solution](../../../../../particular-solution.md), try $y_p=ax+b$. Substitution gives $6ax+5a+6b=6x$, so $a=1$, $b=-5/6$. The unrestricted solution is therefore

$$
y=C_1e^{-2x}+C_2e^{-3x}+x-\frac56.
$$

Its [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) at zero is $-2C_1-3C_2+1$. The single prescribed condition imposes $2C_1+3C_2=1$ and leaves one arbitrary constant. Thus the most general permitted solution is

$$
\boxed{y=Ce^{-2x}+\frac{1-2C}{3}e^{-3x}+x-\frac56,\qquad C\in\mathbb R.}
$$

There is no condition fixing $y(0)$, so the remaining arbitrary constant is essential.
