↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The last two terms in the preceding result equal $v_s$. Therefore

$$
v_{tt}=y^2u_{xx}-2xyu_{xy}+x^2u_{yy}+v_s.
$$

The left-hand side of the original [partial differential equation](../../../../../partial-differential-equation-split.md) is consequently $v_{tt}-v_s$, giving

$$
\boxed{v_{tt}-v_s=0,\qquad\text{equivalently }v_s=v_{tt}.}
$$

In these variables it has the form of a [heat equation](../../../../../heat-equation.md), with logarithmic radial coordinate $s$ playing the evolution variable and angular coordinate $t$ playing the spatial variable. If $u$ is single-valued around the whole punctured [plane](../../../../../plane.md), then $v(s,t+2\pi)=v(s,t)$ as well; that periodicity is a global coordinate condition, not an extra local transformation term.
