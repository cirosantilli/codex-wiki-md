<h1 id="11/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The coordinate [partial derivatives](../../../../../partial-derivative.md) are $x_s=-x$, $y_s=-y$, $x_t=y$, and $y_t=-x$. Apply the [multivariable chain rule](../../../../../multivariable-chain-rule.md) to $v(s,t)=u(x(s,t),y(s,t))$:

$$
\boxed{v_s=-xu_x-yu_y,\qquad v_t=yu_x-xu_y.}
$$

All [partial derivatives](../../../../../partial-derivative.md) of $u$ are evaluated at $(e^{-s}\sin t,e^{-s}\cos t)$. This is a [logarithmic polar coordinate change](../../../../../logarithmic-polar-coordinate-change.md); its [Jacobian determinant](../../../../../jacobian-determinant.md) is $x^2+y^2=e^{-2s}>0$, so it is locally invertible away from the origin. A full angular turn repeats the same point, as usual for [polar coordinates](../../../../../polar-coordinates.md).
