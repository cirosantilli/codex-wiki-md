<h1 id="9/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $g(x)=\tan(x+\pi/4)$. The [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) identity for [tangent](../../../../../tangent.md) gives $g'=1+g^2$, so $g(0)=1$, $g'(0)=2$, and $g''(0)=2g(0)g'(0)=4$. Its first three nonzero [Taylor expansion](../../../../../taylor-expansion.md) terms are therefore

$$
\boxed{\tan(x+\pi/4)=1+2x+2x^2+O(x^3).}
$$

The factor $1/2!$ on the second [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) is what turns $g''(0)=4$ into the coefficient $2$.
