↑ **Parent:** [9](../9.md)

The [Taylor expansion](../../../../taylor-expansion.md) about $a$ has coefficients given by the successive [derivatives](../../../../derivative.md) at $a$:

$$
f(x)=\sum_{k=0}^N\frac{f^{(k)}(a)}{k!}(x-a)^k+R_N(x).
$$

When $f$ is $N+1$ times continuously differentiable on the interval between $a$ and $x$, [Taylor's theorem](../../../../taylor-s-theorem.md) gives $R_N(x)=f^{(N+1)}(\xi)(x-a)^{N+1}/(N+1)!$ for an intermediate point $\xi$. If the remainders tend to zero, this yields the infinite [Taylor series](../../../../taylor-series.md) $f(x)=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}f^{(k)}(a)(x-a)^k/k!$. The three [functions](../../../../function-split.md) below are [real analytic](../../../../real-analytic-function.md) near zero after taking the continuous extension in part (i).
