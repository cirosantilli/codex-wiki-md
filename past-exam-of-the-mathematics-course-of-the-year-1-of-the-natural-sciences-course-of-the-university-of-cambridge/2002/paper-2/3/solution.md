<h1 id="3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

Use [spherical polar coordinates](../../../../spherical-coordinate-system.md) with $\theta$ measured from the positive $z$ axis and $\phi$ around that axis:

$$
\boxed{x=r\sin\theta\cos\phi,\quad y=r\sin\theta\sin\phi,\quad z=r\cos\theta,\qquad dV=r^2\sin\theta\,dr\,d\theta\,d\phi.}
$$

The standard full-space ranges are $r\geq0$, $0\leq\theta\leq\pi$, $0\leq\phi<2\pi$. The factor $r^2\sin\theta$ is the absolute [Jacobian determinant](../../../../jacobian-determinant.md) and gives the [volume element](../../../../volume-element.md).
