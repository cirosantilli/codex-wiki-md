↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In the first octant, [spherical polar coordinates](../../../../../spherical-coordinate-system.md) have $0\leq\theta\leq\pi/2$ and $0\leq\phi\leq\pi/2$. The [charge density](../../../../../charge-density.md) is $\lambda xy=\lambda r^2\sin^2\theta\cos\phi\sin\phi$. Hence the total [electric charge](../../../../../electric-charge-split.md) is

$$
Q=\lambda\int_0^a r^4\,dr\int_0^{\pi/2}\sin^3\theta\,d\theta\int_0^{\pi/2}\cos\phi\sin\phi\,d\phi.
$$

The three factors are $a^5/5$, $2/3$, and $1/2$, respectively. For the middle [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md), set $u=\cos\theta$ to obtain $\int_0^1(1-u^2)\,du=2/3$. Thus

$$
\boxed{Q=\frac{\lambda a^5}{15}.}
$$
