<h1 id="10/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Rationalize the difference to reveal its small size instead of comparing its two large summands separately:

$$
0\leq\sqrt{n^4+\alpha^4}-n^2=\frac{\alpha^4}{\sqrt{n^4+\alpha^4}+n^2}\leq\frac{\alpha^4}{2n^2}.
$$

For any fixed real $\alpha$, the right-hand [series](../../../../../series-mathematics.md) is a constant multiple of a convergent [P-series](../../../../../p-series.md). The [comparison test for series](../../../../../comparison-test-for-series.md) proves

$$
\boxed{\text{convergence for every }\alpha\in\mathbb R.}
$$

The case $\alpha=0$ is the identically zero [series](../../../../../series-mathematics.md). For nonzero $\alpha$, its summand is asymptotic to $\alpha^4/(2n^2)$, explaining the same convergence behavior as the inverse-square [series](../../../../../series-mathematics.md).
