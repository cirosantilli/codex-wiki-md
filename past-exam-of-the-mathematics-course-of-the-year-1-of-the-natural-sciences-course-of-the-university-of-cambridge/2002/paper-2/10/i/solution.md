<h1 id="10/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For every real $\alpha$, $|\cos(\alpha n)/n^2|\leq1/n^2$. The bounding [P-series](../../../../../p-series.md) with exponent two converges. The [comparison test for series](../../../../../comparison-test-for-series.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\text{absolute convergence for every }\alpha\in\mathbb R.}
$$

Oscillations of [cosine](../../../../../cosine.md) are unnecessary for convergence here.
