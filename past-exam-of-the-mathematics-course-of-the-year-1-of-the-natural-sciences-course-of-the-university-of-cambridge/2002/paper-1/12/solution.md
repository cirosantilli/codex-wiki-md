<h1 id="12/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [12](../12.md)

For $k>0$, the two ordinary differential equations have solutions

$$
\boxed{h(x)=A\cosh kx+B\sinh kx,\qquad g(x)=C\cos kx+D\sin kx.}
$$

Their products $h(x)g(y)$ and $g(x)h(y)$ solve [Laplace's equation](../../../../laplace-equation.md), since the two second derivatives contribute opposite $k^2$ terms. For $k=0$, both factors are linear functions. This is [separation of variables](../../../../separation-of-variables.md) with positive, negative and zero separation constants.
