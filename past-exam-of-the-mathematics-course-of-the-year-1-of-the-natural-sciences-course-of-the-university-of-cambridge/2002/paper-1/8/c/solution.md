<h1 id="8/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For the specified coordinate vectors,

$$
L=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad M=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\-1&1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Choose the [orthogonal matrix](../../../../../orthogonal-matrix.md) representing a quarter-turn,

$$
\boxed{S=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

Then $S^TS=I$ and direct [matrix multiplication](../../../../../matrix-multiplication.md) gives $S^TLS=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\-1&1\end{pmatrix}=M$. Thus these two [shear mappings](../../../../../shear-mapping.md) are orthogonally similar.
