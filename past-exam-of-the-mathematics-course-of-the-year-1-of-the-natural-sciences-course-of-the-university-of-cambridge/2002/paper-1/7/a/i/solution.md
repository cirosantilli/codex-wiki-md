<h1 id="7/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

On an interval avoiding $x=\pm1$, [separation of variables](../../../../../../separation-of-variables.md) gives, for $y\ne-1$,

$$
\ln|y+1|=\int\frac{dx}{x^2-1}=\frac12\ln\left|\frac{x-1}{x+1}\right|+C_0.
$$

Absorb the sign into the integration constant and include the [equilibrium solution](../../../../../../equilibrium-solution-of-an-ordinary-differential-equation.md) $y=-1$ by allowing $C=0$. The complete real family is

$$
\boxed{y=-1+C\sqrt{\left|\frac{x-1}{x+1}\right|},\qquad C\in\mathbb R.}
$$

Each solution is considered on a connected interval in $\mathbb R\setminus\{-1,1\}$, where the original equation is defined.
