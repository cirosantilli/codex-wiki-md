<h1 id="7/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [Bernoulli differential equation](../../../../../bernoulli-differential-equation.md) becomes linear after $z=1/y$: multiplying by $-y^{-2}$ gives $z'-2z=-x^2$. A quadratic [particular solution](../../../../../particular-solution.md) is $z_p=x^2/2+x/2+1/4$, while the [homogeneous solution](../../../../../homogeneous-solution.md) is $Ce^{2x}$. The [initial condition](../../../../../initial-condition.md) gives $z(0)=1$ and $C=3/4$. Hence

$$
\boxed{y(x)=\frac4{3e^{2x}+2x^2+2x+1}.}
$$

The denominator is positive for all real $x$, since $2x^2+2x+1=2(x+1/2)^2+1/2$. Thus this [initial value problem](../../../../../initial-value-problem.md) has the displayed solution on the entire real line. The separate solution $y=0$ does not satisfy its [initial condition](../../../../../initial-condition.md).
