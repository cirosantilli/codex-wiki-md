<h1 id="3/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use [implicit differentiation](../../../../../../implicit-differentiation.md) on $F(x,y)=xy^3-yx^3-6=0$. Along the curve,

$$
y'=-\frac{F_x}{F_y}=-\frac{y^3-3yx^2}{3xy^2-x^3}.
$$

At $(1,2)$, $F_x=2$ and $F_y=11\ne0$, so the slope is $-2/11$. The [tangent line](../../../../../../tangent-line.md) is

$$
\boxed{y-2=-\frac2{11}(x-1),\quad\text{equivalently }2x+11y=24.}
$$
