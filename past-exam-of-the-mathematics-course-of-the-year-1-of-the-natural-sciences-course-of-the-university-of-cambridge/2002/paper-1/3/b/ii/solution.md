<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $F(x,y)=e^{xy}+y\ln x-x^2$, the real domain has $x>0$. Its [partial derivatives](../../../../../../partial-derivative.md) are $F_x=ye^{xy}+y/x-2x$ and $F_y=xe^{xy}+\ln x$. At $(1,0)$ they equal $-2$ and $1$, respectively. Thus [implicit differentiation](../../../../../../implicit-differentiation.md) gives $y'=2$, and the [tangent line](../../../../../../tangent-line.md) is

$$
\boxed{y=2(x-1).}
$$
