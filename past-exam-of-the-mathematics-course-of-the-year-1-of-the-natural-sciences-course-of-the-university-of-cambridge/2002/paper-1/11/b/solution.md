<h1 id="11/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

[Euler's formula](../../../../../euler-s-formula.md) and its version with $-\theta$ give

$$
\cos\theta=\frac{e^{i\theta}+e^{-i\theta}}2,\qquad \sin\theta=\frac{e^{i\theta}-e^{-i\theta}}{2i}.
$$

Squaring $e^{i\theta}=\cos\theta+i\sin\theta$ and comparing real and imaginary parts with $e^{2i\theta}$ yields the [double-angle formulas](../../../../../double-angle-formula.md)

$$
\boxed{\cos2\theta=\cos^2\theta-\sin^2\theta=2\cos^2\theta-1=1-2\sin^2\theta,\qquad \sin2\theta=2\sin\theta\cos\theta.}
$$
