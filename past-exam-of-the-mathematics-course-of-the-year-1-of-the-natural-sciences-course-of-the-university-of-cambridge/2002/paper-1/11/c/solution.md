<h1 id="11/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Factor $e^{i\phi/2}$ from the numerator and denominator:

$$
w=\frac{e^{i\phi}-1}{e^{i\phi}+1}=\frac{2ie^{i\phi/2}\sin(\phi/2)}{2e^{i\phi/2}\cos(\phi/2)}=i\tan\frac\phi2.
$$

For $0<\phi<\pi$, the tangent ranges over all positive real values. Thus $\boxed{\operatorname{Re}w=0,\quad\operatorname{Im}w>0}$. Using the principal [complex logarithm](../../../../../complex-logarithm.md),

$$
\boxed{\log w=\ln\tan(\phi/2)+\frac{i\pi}2.}
$$

The real part ranges over the entire real line, while the imaginary part is fixed: the locus is the horizontal line $\operatorname{Im}\log w=\pi/2$. Any other fixed [logarithm branch](../../../../../branch-of-the-complex-logarithm.md) gives the parallel line $\operatorname{Im}\log w=\pi/2+2\pi k$ for one integer $k$; allowing every branch gives the union of those lines.
