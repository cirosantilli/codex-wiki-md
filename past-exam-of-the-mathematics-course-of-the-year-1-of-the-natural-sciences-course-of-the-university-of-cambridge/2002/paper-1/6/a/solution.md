↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Direct [matrix multiplication](../../../../../matrix-multiplication.md) gives

$$
A^2=\begin{pmatrix}13&12\\12&13\end{pmatrix},\qquad \operatorname{tr}A=6,\qquad\det A=5.
$$

Consequently $\boxed{A^2-6A+5I=0}$, the [Cayley-Hamilton theorem](../../../../../cayley-hamilton-theorem-split.md) for its [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) $t^2-6t+5$. Rearranging gives $A(6I-A)=5I$, so

$$
\boxed{A^{-1}=\frac{6I-A}5=\frac15\begin{pmatrix}3&-2\\-2&3\end{pmatrix}.}
$$
