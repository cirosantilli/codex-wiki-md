<h1 id="1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [normal vector](../../../../../../normal-vector.md) must be parallel to $\mathbf b$, and the plane must contain $\mathbf a$. Therefore one choice is

$$
\boxed{\mathbf n=\frac{\mathbf b}{|\mathbf b|},\qquad \mu=\frac{\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b}{|\mathbf b|}.}
$$

The nonzero [scalar triple product](../../../../../../scalar-triple-product.md) ensures $\mathbf b\ne0$. Replacing both $\mathbf n$ and $\mu$ by their negatives describes the same [affine hyperplane](../../../../../../affine-hyperplane.md).
