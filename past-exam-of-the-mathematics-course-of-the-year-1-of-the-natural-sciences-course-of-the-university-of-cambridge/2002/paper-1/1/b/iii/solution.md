<h1 id="1/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

This plane contains the direction $\mathbf b-\mathbf a$ and is parallel to $\mathbf c$. Put $\mathbf N=(\mathbf b-\mathbf a)\times\mathbf c$. Its [scalar triple product](../../../../../../scalar-triple-product.md) with $\mathbf a$ is again $D\ne0$, proving $\mathbf N\ne0$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\mathbf n=\frac{(\mathbf b-\mathbf a)\times\mathbf c}{|(\mathbf b-\mathbf a)\times\mathbf c|},\qquad \mu=\frac D{|(\mathbf b-\mathbf a)\times\mathbf c|}.}
$$

The [dot product](../../../../../../dot-product.md) with $\mathbf c$ vanishes, while the [dot products](../../../../../../dot-product.md) with $\mathbf a$ and $\mathbf b$ equal $\mu$ after normalization. These verify both incidence and parallelism.
