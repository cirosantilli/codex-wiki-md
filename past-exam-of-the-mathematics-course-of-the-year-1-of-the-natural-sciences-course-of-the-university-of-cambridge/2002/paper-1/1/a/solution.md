<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write $\mathbf r=\mu\mathbf n+\mathbf v$. Since $\mathbf n$ is a [unit vector](../../../../../unit-vector.md), the equation becomes $\mathbf v\cdot\mathbf n=0$. Thus $\mathbf v$ ranges over the two-dimensional [orthogonal complement](../../../../../orthogonal-complement.md) of $\mathbf n$: the locus is an [affine hyperplane](../../../../../affine-hyperplane.md) through $\mu\mathbf n$, with [unit normal](../../../../../unit-normal.md) $\mathbf n$. Conversely, every such vector satisfies the equation. Its perpendicular distance from the origin is $\boxed{|\mu|}$; $\mu$ is the signed distance in the chosen normal direction.
