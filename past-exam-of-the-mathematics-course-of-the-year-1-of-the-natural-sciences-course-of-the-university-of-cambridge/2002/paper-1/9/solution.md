<h1 id="9/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [9](../9.md)

In Cartesian components, $\mathbf F=(z-y,x,-x)$, so

$$
\boxed{\nabla\times\mathbf F=(0,2,2)\ne0.}
$$

It cannot be a [conservative vector field](../../../../conservative-vector-field.md). For the second field, put $\Phi(\mathbf r)=-(1+|\mathbf r|^2)^{-1/2}$. Its [gradient](../../../../gradient.md) is $\nabla\Phi=\mathbf r/(1+|\mathbf r|^2)^{3/2}=\mathbf G$. This smooth global scalar function proves $\mathbf G$ is [conservative](../../../../conservative-vector-field.md), with $\boxed{\nabla\times\mathbf G=0}$. The following path integrals exhibit the different behavior directly.
