↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [triangle inequality](../../../../../../triangle-inequality.md) bounds the distance sum below by the separation of the two foci, which is two. Equality holds exactly for points on the closed segment joining them. Hence the [locus](../../../../../../locus-mathematics.md) is

$$
\boxed{x=0,\qquad0\le y\le2,\quad\text{equivalently }z=iy\ (0\le y\le2).}
$$

Both endpoints are included. It is the segment on the positive [imaginary axis](../../../../../../imaginary-axis.md) shown in the right-hand panel above.
