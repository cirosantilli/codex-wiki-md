<h1 id="11/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Multiply numerator and denominator by the [complex conjugate](../../../../../../complex-conjugate.md) $2+i$:

$$
\frac1{2-i}=\frac{2+i}{(2-i)(2+i)}=\frac{2+i}5.
$$

Thus the [real part](../../../../../../real-part.md) and [imaginary part](../../../../../../imaginary-part.md) are

$$
\boxed{\Re z=\frac25,\qquad\Im z=\frac15.}
$$
