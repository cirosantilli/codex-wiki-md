<h1 id="11/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Powers of the [imaginary unit](../../../../../../imaginary-unit.md) repeat in blocks $i,-1,-i,1$, each with sum zero. The first $44$ terms comprise eleven complete blocks; the last is $i^{45}=i$. Applying [Euler's formula](../../../../../../euler-s-formula.md) to the resulting [complex exponential](../../../../../../complex-exponential-function.md) gives $e^i=\cos1+i\sin1$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\Re z=\cos1,\qquad\Im z=\sin1.}
$$

The [angles](../../../../../../angle.md) here are in [radians](../../../../../../radian.md).
