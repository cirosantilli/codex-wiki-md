<h1 id="11/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The exponential definitions of [hyperbolic sine](../../../../../../hyperbolic-sine.md) and [hyperbolic cosine](../../../../../../hyperbolic-cosine.md), together with [Euler's formula](../../../../../../euler-s-formula.md), give $\sinh(iu)=i\sin u$ and $\cosh(iu)=\cos u$. The argument of the [complex logarithm](../../../../../../complex-logarithm.md) therefore reduces to $i\sin(\pi/2)+\cos(9\pi/2)=i$.

Using the [principal logarithm](../../../../../../principal-complex-logarithm.md), $\log i=i\pi/2$, so

$$
\boxed{\Re z=0,\qquad\Im z=\frac\pi2\quad\text{(principal value)}.}
$$

If no branch of the [complex logarithm](../../../../../../complex-logarithm.md) is selected, its complete set of values is $i(\pi/2+2\pi k)$, $k\in\mathbb Z$. The [real part](../../../../../../real-part.md) remains zero, while the [imaginary part](../../../../../../imaginary-part.md) is $\pi/2+2\pi k$. The original paper does not explicitly state a branch convention.
