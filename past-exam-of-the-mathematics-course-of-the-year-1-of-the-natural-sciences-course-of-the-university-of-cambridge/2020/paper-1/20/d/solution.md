<h1 id="20/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For the second [vector field](../../../../../vector-field.md), the [curl](../../../../../curl.md) is

$$
\boxed{\nabla\times\mathbf G=\bigl(2y-2z,\ 2z-2x,\ 2x-2y\bigr).}
$$

It is nonzero, for example $(0,-2,2)$ at $(1,0,0)$. A differentiable [scalar potential](../../../../../scalar-potential.md) would have zero [curl](../../../../../curl.md) of its [gradient](../../../../../gradient.md), so **$\mathbf G$ is not conservative**. The fact that its [curl](../../../../../curl.md) vanishes on the line $x=y=z$ does not make it vanish throughout the domain.
