<h1 id="20/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Parametrize the segment in its stated direction by $\mathbf x(t)=(1-t,t,t)$, $0\le t\le1$. Then $d\mathbf x=(-1,1,1)\,dt$, and substitution into the two [vector fields](../../../../../vector-field.md) gives

$$
\mathbf F(\mathbf x(t))=(2t,1,1),\qquad
\mathbf G(\mathbf x(t))=(2t^2,1-2t+2t^2,1-2t+2t^2).
$$

Taking each [dot product](../../../../../dot-product.md) with $d\mathbf x$ evaluates the [line integrals](../../../../../line-integral.md):

$$
\boxed{\int\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf x=\int_0^1(2-2t)\,dt=1,\qquad
\int\mathbf G\cdot d\mathbf x=\int_0^1(2-4t+2t^2)\,dt=\frac23.}
$$
