<h1 id="1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Write the remaining [quadratic polynomial](../../../../quadratic-polynomial.md) as $x^2+bx+c$. Comparing coefficients in $(x-2)(x^2+bx+c)$ with the given [cubic polynomial](../../../../cubic-polynomial.md) gives $b-2=-1$ and $-2c=-2$, hence $b=c=1$. The coefficient of $x$ is then $c-2b=-1$, as required. Thus

$$
\boxed{x^3-x^2-x-2=(x-2)(x^2+x+1),\qquad\text{other factor }x^2+x+1.}
$$
