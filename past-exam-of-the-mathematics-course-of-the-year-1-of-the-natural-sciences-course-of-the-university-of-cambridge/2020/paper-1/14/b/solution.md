<h1 id="14/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [Hessian matrix](../../../../../hessian-matrix.md) has entries

$$
f_{xx}=4(s-1)+8x^2,\qquad f_{xy}=8xy,\qquad f_{yy}=4(s-1)+8y^2+\frac{7(3-y^2)}{(3+y^2)^2}.
$$

At each [stationary point](../../../../../stationary-point.md) the mixed entry vanishes, so the signs of the diagonal entries directly classify the local quadratic change:

$$
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\text{point}&H&\text{nature}\\\hline
(\pm1,0)&\operatorname{diag}(8,7/3)&\text{strict local minima}\\
(0,0)&\operatorname{diag}(-4,-5/3)&\text{strict local maximum}\\
(0,\pm1/\sqrt2)&\operatorname{diag}(-2,24/7)&\text{saddle points}
\end{array}
$$

In particular the two [local minima](../../../../../local-minimum.md) are also [global minima](../../../../../global-minimum.md): the squared term is nonnegative and $\ln(3+y^2)\ge\ln3$, with equality in both bounds exactly at $(\pm1,0)$. The [local maximum](../../../../../local-maximum.md) at the origin is not a [global maximum](../../../../../global-maximum.md), since $f$ grows without bound as $x^2+y^2\to\infty$. The opposite diagonal signs at the other two points establish their [saddle point](../../../../../saddle-point.md) character.
