<h1 id="14/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [gradient](../../../../../gradient.md) is [orthogonal](../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) to each regular [level set](../../../../../level-set.md) and points towards increasing $f$. Near either [minimum](../../../../../minimum-of-a-subset-of-a-total-order.md), the linear approximation is $(8\delta x,\tfrac73\delta y)$, so arrows point away from the point. Near the [maximum](../../../../../maximum-of-a-subset-of-a-total-order.md) at the origin it is $(-4\delta x,-\tfrac53\delta y)$, so arrows point inwards. Near either [saddle point](../../../../../saddle-point.md) it is $(-2\delta x,\tfrac{24}{7}\delta y)$: arrows point towards the vertical axis horizontally and away from the saddle vertically.

On the horizontal axis the [gradient](../../../../../gradient.md) is $(4x(x^2-1),0)$. Its arrows point left on $x<-1$, right on $-1<x<0$, left on $0<x<1$, and right on $x>1$. On the vertical axis it is

$$
\nabla f(0,y)=\left(0,\frac{y(2y^2-1)(2y^2+5)}{3+y^2}\right).
$$

Consequently its arrows point down on $y<-1/\sqrt2$, up on $-1/\sqrt2<y<0$, down on $0<y<1/\sqrt2$, and up on $y>1/\sqrt2$. These directions, and representative arrows near each [stationary point](../../../../../stationary-point.md), appear in the right-hand panel. The [gradient](../../../../../gradient.md) itself is zero at all five [stationary points](../../../../../stationary-point.md).
