<h1 id="18/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A square homogeneous [linear system](../../../../../system-of-linear-equations.md) has a nonzero solution exactly when its [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) vanishes. Expanding along the second row gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
\det A&=3\left[(18-\lambda)+2\lambda(1-\lambda)\right]
-3\left[(9-\lambda)+\lambda(2-\lambda)\right]\\
&=3(9-\lambda^2).
\end{aligned}
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{\lambda=3\quad\text{or}\quad\lambda=-3.}
$$

For an explicit check, the [null space](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) contains $(3,-1,1)^{\mathsf T}$ at $\lambda=3$ and $(3,1,-1)^{\mathsf T}$ at $\lambda=-3$.
