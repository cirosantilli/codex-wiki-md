↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For the $C$ obtained above, the [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) is

$$
\det(C-\mu I)=(-4-\mu)(4-\mu)-9=\mu^2-25.
$$

Thus the [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are $5$ and $-5$. For $\mu=5$, $(C-5I)\mathbf v=0$ gives $v_2=-3v_1$; for $\mu=-5$, it gives $v_1=3v_2$. Normalizing the [eigenvectors](../../../../../../eigenvector.md) yields

$$
\boxed{\mu=5:\ \mathbf v_+=\frac1{\sqrt{10}}\begin{pmatrix}1\\-3\end{pmatrix};\qquad
\mu=-5:\ \mathbf v_-=\frac1{\sqrt{10}}\begin{pmatrix}3\\1\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

Both are [unit vectors](../../../../../../unit-vector.md), their [dot product](../../../../../../dot-product.md) is zero, and either overall sign is allowed for each [eigenvector](../../../../../../eigenvector.md).
