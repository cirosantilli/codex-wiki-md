<h1 id="7/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [7](../7.md)

The two directed segments have [vectors](../../../../vector.md) $\mathbf u=(1,0,-1)$ and $\mathbf v=(0,1,1)$. Their [dot product](../../../../dot-product.md) is $-1$ and both [norms](../../../../norm.md) equal $\sqrt2$. Hence

$$
\cos\theta=\frac{\mathbf u\cdot\mathbf v}{|\mathbf u||\mathbf v|}=-\frac12,
\qquad\boxed{\theta=\frac{2\pi}3=120^\circ.}
$$

The common starting point fixes the obtuse [angle](../../../../angle.md) between these segments.
