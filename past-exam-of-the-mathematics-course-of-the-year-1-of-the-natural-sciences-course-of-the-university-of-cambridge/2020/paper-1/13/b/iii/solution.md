<h1 id="13/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

A linear [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) trial $y=ux+v$ has $y'=u$ and $y''=0$. Substituting gives $3ux+(\sqrt3u+3v)=x$, so $u=1/3$ and $v=-\sqrt3/9=-1/(3\sqrt3)$. Thus a [particular solution](../../../../../../particular-solution.md) is

$$
\boxed{y_{p,2}(x)=\frac x3-\frac1{3\sqrt3}.}
$$
