<h1 id="13/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Insert the [exponential function](../../../../../../exponential-function.md) trial $y=e^{rx}$ into the [homogeneous linear differential equation](../../../../../../homogeneous-linear-differential-equation.md). Its [characteristic equation](../../../../../../characteristic-equation-of-a-constant-coefficient-differential-equation.md) is $r^2+\sqrt3r+3=0$, with [polynomial roots](../../../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md)

$$
r=-\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\pm\frac32i.
$$

Taking real combinations of the two complex exponential solutions produces the [general solution](../../../../../../general-solution.md)

$$
\boxed{y_c(x)=e^{-\sqrt3x/2}\left(A\cos\frac{3x}{2}+B\sin\frac{3x}{2}\right).}
$$
