↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a first-order [linear ordinary differential equation](../../../../../../linear-ordinary-differential-equation-split.md) $y'+p(x)y=q(x)$, an [integrating factor](../../../../../../integrating-factor.md) is $\exp(\int p(x)\,dx)$. Here $p(x)=\cot x$, so

$$
\mu(x)=\exp(\ln\sin x)=\boxed{\sin x.}
$$

The [sine](../../../../../../sine.md) is positive on the specified interval, so this [integrating factor](../../../../../../integrating-factor.md) never vanishes there. Any nonzero constant multiple is equally suitable.
