<h1 id="13/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Multiplication by the [integrating factor](../../../../../../integrating-factor.md) and the [product rule](../../../../../../product-rule.md) turn the left side into a single [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md):

$$
(y\sin x)'=\sin x\cos^2x.
$$

Using [integration by substitution](../../../../../../integration-by-substitution.md) $u=\cos x$ gives $y\sin x=-\cos^3x/3+C$. Therefore the [general solution](../../../../../../general-solution.md) is

$$
\boxed{y(x)=\frac{C-\frac13\cos^3x}{\sin x},\qquad0<x<\frac\pi2.}
$$

There is one arbitrary [constant of integration](../../../../../../constant-of-integration.md), as expected for a first-order [ordinary differential equation](../../../../../../ordinary-differential-equation.md).
