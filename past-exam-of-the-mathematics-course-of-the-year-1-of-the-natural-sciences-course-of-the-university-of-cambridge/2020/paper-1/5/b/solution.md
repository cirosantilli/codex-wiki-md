<h1 id="5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [quadratic formula](../../../../../quadratic-formula.md) gives $t=-1\pm\sqrt2$. The [tangent function](../../../../../tangent-function.md) is positive in the first quadrant, so the positive [polynomial root](../../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md) is selected:

$$
\boxed{\tan\frac\pi8=\sqrt2-1.}
$$
