<h1 id="17/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For [integration by parts](../../../../../../integration-by-parts.md), choose $u=\ln x$ and $dv=x^{-4}\,dx$. Then $du=dx/x$ and $v=-1/(3x^3)$, giving

$$
\int\frac{\ln x}{x^4}\,dx=-\frac{\ln x}{3x^3}+\frac13\int x^{-4}\,dx
=\boxed{-\frac{3\ln x+1}{9x^3}+C,\qquad x>0.}
$$

Here $C$ is an arbitrary [constant of integration](../../../../../../constant-of-integration.md).
