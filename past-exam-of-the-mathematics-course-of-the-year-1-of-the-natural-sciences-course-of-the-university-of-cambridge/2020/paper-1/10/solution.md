<h1 id="10/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [10](../10.md)

For this [polynomial](../../../../polynomial-split.md), $f'(x)=2x$, so the specified [Newton root-finding iteration](../../../../newton-root-finding-iteration.md) simplifies to

$$
x_{n+1}=\frac12\left(x_n+\frac2{x_n}\right).
$$

Starting at one gives $x_1=(1+2)/2=3/2$, followed by $x_2=(3/2+4/3)/2=17/12$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{x_1=1.5,\qquad x_2\approx1.42\text{ to two decimal places}.}
$$
