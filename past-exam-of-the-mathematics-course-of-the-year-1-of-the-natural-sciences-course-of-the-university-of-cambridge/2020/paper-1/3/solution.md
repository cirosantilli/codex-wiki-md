<h1 id="3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

Apply the [product rule](../../../../product-rule.md) to $x\sin(1/x)$ and the [chain rule](../../../../chain-rule.md) to the [sine](../../../../sine.md). Since $d(1/x)/dx=-1/x^2$, the [derivative](../../../../derivative.md) is

$$
\boxed{\frac{dy}{dx}=\sin\frac1x-\frac1x\cos\frac1x,\qquad x\ne0.}
$$
