<h1 id="19/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The new [cone](../../../../../cone-geometry.md) can be parametrized by

$$
\mathbf R'(r,\theta)=(r\cos\theta,b(1-r/a),r\sin\theta).
$$

The outward [vector area element](../../../../../vector-area-element.md) is $r(\tfrac ba\cos\theta,1,\tfrac ba\sin\theta)\,dr\,d\theta$. This is $\mathbf R'_\theta\times\mathbf R'_r\,dr\,d\theta$; reversing the order is needed to retain the outward orientation after the exchange of coordinates. Its [dot product](../../../../../dot-product.md) with $\mathbf F(\mathbf R')$ is again $br\,dr\,d\theta$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\int_{C'}\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf S=\pi a^2b.}
$$

This equality also follows because a rigid [rotation](../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) takes the first [cone](../../../../../cone-geometry.md) into the second and leaves the radial [vector field](../../../../../vector-field.md) $\mathbf F(\mathbf x)=\mathbf x$ invariant.
