↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In the [Cartesian coordinate system](../../../../../cartesian-coordinate-system.md), the [Laplacian](../../../../../laplacian.md) of a [scalar field](../../../../../scalar-field.md) is the sum of its three unmixed second [partial derivatives](../../../../../partial-derivative.md). Each squared coordinate here contributes one, so

$$
\boxed{\nabla^2\psi=\psi_{xx}+\psi_{yy}+\psi_{zz}=1+1+1=3.}
$$

Equivalently $\nabla\psi=(x,y,z)$ and its [divergence](../../../../../divergence.md) is three.
