<h1 id="19/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The sloping height relation on the [cone](../../../../../cone-geometry.md) is $z=b(1-r/a)$. A [parametric equation](../../../../../parametric-equation.md) of its curved surface is therefore

$$
\mathbf R(r,\theta)=(r\cos\theta,r\sin\theta,b(1-r/a)),\qquad0\le r\le a,\quad0\le\theta<2\pi.
$$

Its two [tangent vectors](../../../../../tangent-vector-split.md) are $\mathbf R_r=(\cos\theta,\sin\theta,-b/a)$ and $\mathbf R_\theta=r(-\sin\theta,\cos\theta,0)$. Multiplication by $dr$ and $d\theta$ produces the two infinitesimal sides requested in the paper. Their [cross product](../../../../../cross-product.md) is the [vector area element](../../../../../vector-area-element.md):

$$
\boxed{d\mathbf S=(\mathbf R_r\times\mathbf R_\theta)\,dr\,d\theta
=r\left(\frac ba\cos\theta,\frac ba\sin\theta,1\right)\,dr\,d\theta.}
$$

Its radial component is positive, so this [normal vector](../../../../../normal-vector.md) points outwards from the solid [cone](../../../../../cone-geometry.md). Taking its [norm](../../../../../norm.md) gives the scalar [area element](../../../../../area-element-of-a-surface.md)

$$
\boxed{dS=r\sqrt{1+\frac{b^2}{a^2}}\,dr\,d\theta.}
$$

The [parametric equation](../../../../../parametric-equation.md) degenerates at the apex, but that single point contributes no [area](../../../../../surface-area.md).
