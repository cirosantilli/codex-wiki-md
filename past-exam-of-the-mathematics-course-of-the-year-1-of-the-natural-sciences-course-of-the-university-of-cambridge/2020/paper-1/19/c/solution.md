<h1 id="19/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Compute the [flux](../../../../../flux.md) directly using the outward [vector area element](../../../../../vector-area-element.md), as required. Restrict the [vector field](../../../../../vector-field.md) to the surface [parametric equation](../../../../../parametric-equation.md) and take the [dot product](../../../../../dot-product.md):

$$
\mathbf F(\mathbf R)\cdot d\mathbf S
=\left[\frac ba r^2(\cos^2\theta+\sin^2\theta)+br\left(1-\frac ra\right)\right]\,dr\,d\theta
=br\,dr\,d\theta.
$$

The terms quadratic in $r$ cancel. Therefore the [surface integral](../../../../../surface-integral.md) is

$$
\boxed{\int_C\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf S=\int_0^{2\pi}\int_0^a br\,dr\,d\theta=\pi a^2b.}
$$

This is a direct evaluation of the [flux of the position vector through a cone](../../../../../flux-of-the-position-vector-through-a-cone.md).
