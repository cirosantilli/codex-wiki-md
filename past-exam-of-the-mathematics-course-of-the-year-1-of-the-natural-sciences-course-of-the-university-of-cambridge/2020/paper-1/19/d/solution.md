<h1 id="19/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

On the [sphere](../../../../../sphere.md) of radius $a$, the outward [unit normal](../../../../../unit-normal.md) is $\mathbf n=(x,y,z)/a$. Thus $\mathbf F\cdot\mathbf n=a$ everywhere. Multiplying by the [surface area](../../../../../surface-area.md) $4\pi a^2$ gives

$$
\boxed{\Phi=\int\mathbf F\cdot\mathbf n\,dS=4\pi a^3.}
$$

The same [surface integral](../../../../../surface-integral.md) follows in [spherical coordinates](../../../../../spherical-coordinate-system.md) from $dS=a^2\sin\vartheta\,d\vartheta\,d\varphi$.
