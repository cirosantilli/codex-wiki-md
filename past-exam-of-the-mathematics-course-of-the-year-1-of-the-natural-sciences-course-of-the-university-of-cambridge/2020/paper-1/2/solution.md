<h1 id="2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

The [Pythagorean trigonometric identity](../../../../pythagorean-trigonometric-identity.md) makes the second factor $1+\cos^2\theta$, which is strictly positive. It is therefore enough to solve $\sin\theta-\cos\theta\le0$. The [sine addition formula](../../../../sine-addition-formula.md) gives

$$
\sin\theta-\cos\theta=\sqrt2\sin(\theta-\pi/4).
$$

The shifted [angle](../../../../angle.md) lies between $\pi/4$ and $5\pi/4$. On this interval the [sine](../../../../sine.md) is nonpositive precisely from $\pi$ onwards. Restoring the original [angle](../../../../angle.md) and retaining its strict upper bound gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{5\pi}{4}\le\theta<\frac{3\pi}{2}.}
$$
