<h1 id="15/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For $f(x)=x^{1/5}$, the first two [derivatives](../../../../../derivative.md) are $f'(x)=x^{-4/5}/5$ and $f''(x)=-4x^{-9/5}/25$. Since $32=2^5$,

$$
f(32)=2,\qquad f'(32)=\frac1{80},\qquad f''(32)=-\frac1{3200}.
$$

The first three terms of the [Taylor expansion](../../../../../taylor-expansion.md), with $x-32=1$, therefore give

$$
\boxed{33^{1/5}\approx2+\frac1{80}-\frac1{6400}=\frac{12879}{6400}=2.01234375.}
$$

For a check on accuracy, $f^{(3)}(x)=36x^{-14/5}/125$ is positive and decreasing on $[32,33]$. The [Taylor remainder](../../../../../taylor-remainder.md) is consequently positive and less than $f^{(3)}(32)/6=3/1024000<3\times10^{-6}$. The approximation is a slight underestimate.
