<h1 id="12/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [cardioid](../../../../../../cardioid.md) has a nonnegative radial boundary, so each ray from the origin contributes $0\le r\le a(1+\cos\theta)$. Integrating the [polar coordinates](../../../../../../polar-coordinates.md) [area element](../../../../../../area-element-of-a-surface.md) yields

$$
A=\int_0^{2\pi}\int_0^{a(1+\cos\theta)}r\,dr\,d\theta
=\frac{a^2}2\int_0^{2\pi}(1+2\cos\theta+\cos^2\theta)\,d\theta.
$$

The three angular integrals are $2\pi$, zero and $\pi$, respectively, using the [double-angle formula](../../../../../../double-angle-formula.md) for the last. Thus

$$
\boxed{A=\frac{3\pi a^2}{2}.}
$$
