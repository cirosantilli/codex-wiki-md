<h1 id="12/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For [polar coordinates](../../../../../polar-coordinates.md), $x=r\cos\theta$ and $y=r\sin\theta$. The [Jacobian determinant](../../../../../jacobian-determinant.md) is $r$, so the [area element](../../../../../area-element-of-a-surface.md) is

$$
\boxed{dS=r\,dr\,d\theta\qquad(r\ge0).}
$$

This accounts for the increasing length of an angular strip as its distance from the origin increases.
