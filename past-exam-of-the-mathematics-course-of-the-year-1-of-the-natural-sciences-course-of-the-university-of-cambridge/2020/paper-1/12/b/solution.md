↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [volume element](../../../../../volume-element.md) is the absolute [Jacobian determinant](../../../../../jacobian-determinant.md) times the coordinate differentials. In the [Cartesian coordinate system](../../../../../cartesian-coordinate-system.md), [cylindrical coordinates](../../../../../cylindrical-coordinate-system.md) $(r,\theta,z)$ and [spherical coordinates](../../../../../spherical-coordinate-system.md) $(\rho,\vartheta,\varphi)$, respectively,

$$
\boxed{dV=dx\,dy\,dz,\qquad dV=r\,dr\,d\theta\,dz,\qquad dV=\rho^2\sin\vartheta\,d\rho\,d\vartheta\,d\varphi.}
$$

Here $\rho$ is distance from the origin, $\vartheta$ is the polar [angle](../../../../../angle.md) from the positive $z$ axis, and $\varphi$ is the azimuthal [angle](../../../../../angle.md). The [spherical coordinates](../../../../../spherical-coordinate-system.md) convention fixes which [angle](../../../../../angle.md) enters the [sine](../../../../../sine.md) factor.
