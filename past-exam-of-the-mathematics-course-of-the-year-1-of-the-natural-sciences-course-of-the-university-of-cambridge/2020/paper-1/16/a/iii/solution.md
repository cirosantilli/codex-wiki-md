<h1 id="16/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Each position has apple [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) $1/2$. Applying [Bayes' theorem](../../../../../../bayes-theorem.md) to the preceding [conditional probability](../../../../../../conditional-probability.md) gives

$$
\boxed{P(A_2\mid A_3)=\frac{P(A_3\mid A_2)P(A_2)}{P(A_3)}=\frac{(4/9)(1/2)}{1/2}=\frac49.}
$$

This also agrees with [exchangeability](../../../../../../exchangeable-random-variables.md) of the second and third positions.
