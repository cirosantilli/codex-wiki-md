<h1 id="16/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

By the [inclusion-exclusion principle for probabilities](../../../../../../inclusion-exclusion-principle-for-probabilities.md),

$$
P(B_1\cup A_2)=P(B_1)+P(A_2)-P(B_1\cap A_2).
$$

After an initial banana, five of the nine remaining pieces are apples, so the intersection [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) is $(1/2)(5/9)=5/18$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{P(B_1\cup A_2)=\frac12+\frac12-\frac5{18}=\frac{13}{18}.}
$$

As a check, the complementary event is an apple followed by a banana, which also has [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) $5/18$.
