<h1 id="16/a/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

Let $I_j$ be the [indicator random variable](../../../../../../indicator-random-variable.md) of an apple in lunch $j$. The number remaining is $R=5-I_1-I_2-I_3$. Each [expected value](../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) $E[I_j]=P(A_j)=1/2$. The [linearity of expectation](../../../../../../linearity-of-expectation.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{E[R]=5-3\left(\frac12\right)=\frac72\text{ apples}.}
$$

Although the [indicator random variables](../../../../../../indicator-random-variable.md) are dependent under [sampling without replacement](../../../../../../sampling-without-replacement.md), the [linearity of expectation](../../../../../../linearity-of-expectation.md) needs no [independence](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) assumption.
