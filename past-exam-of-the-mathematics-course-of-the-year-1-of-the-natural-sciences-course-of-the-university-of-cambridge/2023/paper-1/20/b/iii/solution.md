<h1 id="20/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The acute [angles](../../../../../../angle.md) follow from part (ii) and the [Snell's law](../../../../../../snell-s-law.md) relation:

$$
\boxed{\theta=\pi/6=30^\circ,\qquad\phi=\pi/4=45^\circ.}
$$

The path [lengths](../../../../../../length.md) below and above the interface are respectively

$$
\ell_1=\frac{\sqrt3}{\cos(\pi/6)}=2,\qquad\ell_2=\frac1{\cos(\pi/4)}=\sqrt2.
$$

Hence the total distance and minimum travel [time](../../../../../../time-in-physics.md) are

$$
\boxed{\ell=2+\sqrt2,\qquad T_{\min}=\frac2{c/2}+\frac{\sqrt2}{c/\sqrt2}=\frac6c.}
$$

For a sketch to scale, choose the interface as $y=0$, with $A=(0,-\sqrt3)$ and $B=(2,1)$. The crossing is $C=(1,0)$ because $d_1\tan\theta=d_2\tan\phi=1$. The figure uses equal axis scales and shows the two normal-relative [angles](../../../../../../angle.md).
