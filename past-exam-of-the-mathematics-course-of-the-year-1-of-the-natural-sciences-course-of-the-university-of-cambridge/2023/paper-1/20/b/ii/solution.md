<h1 id="20/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Put $s=\sin\theta$. By part (i), $\sin\phi=\sqrt2s$. The acute-angle geometry requires $0<s<1/\sqrt2$. Expressing the [tangents](../../../../../../tangent.md) in terms of their [sines](../../../../../../sine.md) in the geometric constraint gives the required [implicit equation](../../../../../../implicit-equation.md)

$$
\boxed{\frac{\sqrt3s}{\sqrt{1-s^2}}+\frac{\sqrt2s}{\sqrt{1-2s^2}}=2.}
$$

The hint suggests $s=1/m$ for an integer $m\geq2$. Testing $m=2$ gives

$$
\frac{\sqrt3/2}{\sqrt3/2}+\frac{1/\sqrt2}{1/\sqrt2}=1+1=2.
$$

Thus $\boxed{\sin\theta=1/2}$. Each term on the left is strictly increasing in $s$ over the allowed [interval](../../../../../../interval-mathematics.md), so this solution is unique.
