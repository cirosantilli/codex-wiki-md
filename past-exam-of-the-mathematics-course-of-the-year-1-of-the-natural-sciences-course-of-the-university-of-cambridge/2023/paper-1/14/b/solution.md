<h1 id="14/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The stated [total differential](../../../../../total-differential.md) is that of $\pi r^2h$, so $V=\pi r^2h+C$. The zero-volume condition gives $C=0$. For positive $r,h$, divide the [total differential](../../../../../total-differential.md) by this [volume](../../../../../volume.md) to obtain

$$
\boxed{\frac{dV}{V}=2\frac{dr}{r}+\frac{dh}{h}.}
$$

For the specified small changes, $dV/V\approx2(0.01)+0.02=0.04$, so **the volume increases by approximately $4\%$**. The fractional expression requires $V>0$; at a degenerate zero-volume state it is undefined. For finite changes, the exact multiplier is $(1.01)^2(1.02)$, whose difference from $1$ is $4.0502\%$; the [total differential](../../../../../total-differential.md) gives its first-order approximation.
