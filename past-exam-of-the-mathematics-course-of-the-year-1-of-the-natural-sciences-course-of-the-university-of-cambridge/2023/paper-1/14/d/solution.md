<h1 id="14/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [chain rule](../../../../../chain-rule.md) expresses the coordinate [differential operators](../../../../../differential-operator.md) as

$$
\partial_x=a\partial_\xi+b\partial_\eta,\qquad \partial_y=-b\partial_\xi+a\partial_\eta.
$$

The coefficients are constant, so a second application gives

$$
u_{xx}=a^2u_{\xi\xi}+ab(u_{\xi\eta}+u_{\eta\xi})+b^2u_{\eta\eta},
$$

and

$$
u_{yy}=b^2u_{\xi\xi}-ab(u_{\xi\eta}+u_{\eta\xi})+a^2u_{\eta\eta}.
$$

Adding cancels the mixed [partial derivatives](../../../../../partial-derivative.md), and $a^2+b^2=1$ gives

$$
\boxed{u_{xx}+u_{yy}=u_{\xi\xi}+u_{\eta\eta}.}
$$

The transformation has [rotation matrix](../../../../../rotation-matrix.md) $\begin{pmatrix}a&-b\\b&a\end{pmatrix}$, so this identity expresses the [rotational invariance of the Laplacian](../../../../../rotational-invariance-of-the-laplacian.md).
