<h1 id="14/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let $t=y/x$. The [product rule](../../../../../product-rule.md) and the [chain rule](../../../../../chain-rule.md) give the [partial derivatives](../../../../../partial-derivative.md)

$$
u_x=\phi(t)+x\phi'(t)\left(-\frac y{x^2}\right)=\phi(t)-t\phi'(t),\qquad u_y=x\phi'(t)\frac1x=\phi'(t).
$$

Hence the terms containing $\phi'$ cancel:

$$
xu_x+yu_y=x\phi(t)-xt\phi'(t)+y\phi'(t)=x\phi(t)=\boxed{u}.
$$

This also follows from the [Euler theorem for homogeneous functions](../../../../../euler-theorem-for-homogeneous-functions.md), because $u(\lambda x,\lambda y)=\lambda u(x,y)$ for $\lambda>0$.
