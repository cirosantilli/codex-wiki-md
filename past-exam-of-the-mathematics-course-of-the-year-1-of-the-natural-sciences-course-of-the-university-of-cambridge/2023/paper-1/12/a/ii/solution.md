<h1 id="12/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

At each height, the removed [circular sector](../../../../../../circular-sector.md) has the same [angle](../../../../../../angle.md) and $4/5$ of the original [radius](../../../../../../radius.md). Its [area](../../../../../../surface-area.md) and hence its [volume](../../../../../../volume.md) are therefore multiplied by $(4/5)^2=16/25$. Equating the removed [volume](../../../../../../volume.md) to that of the reformed solid [sphere](../../../../../../sphere.md) gives

$$
\frac43\pi a^3=\frac{16}{25}V=\frac{2\pi R^2H}{25}(3-e^{-2}).
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{a=\left[\frac{3R^2H}{50}(3-e^{-2})\right]^{1/3}.}
$$
