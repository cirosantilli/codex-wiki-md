<h1 id="12/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

At height $z$, the horizontal [triangle](../../../../../../triangle.md) has half-width $L\sqrt{1-z/(2H)}$ along $x$ and depth $D(1-z/H)$ along $y$. These shrink with $z$, so its [orthogonal projection](../../../../../../orthogonal-projection.md) onto the $(x,y)$ [plane](../../../../../../plane.md) is the base [triangle](../../../../../../triangle.md) with vertices $(-L,0)$, $(L,0)$ and $(0,D)$:

$$
0\leq y\leq D,\qquad |x|\leq L(1-y/D).
$$

The [orthogonal projection](../../../../../../orthogonal-projection.md) onto the $(y,z)$ [plane](../../../../../../plane.md) is the [triangle](../../../../../../triangle.md) with vertices $(0,0)$, $(D,0)$ and $(0,H)$:

$$
0\leq z\leq H,\qquad0\leq y\leq D(1-z/H).
$$

The [orthogonal projection](../../../../../../orthogonal-projection.md) onto the $(x,z)$ [plane](../../../../../../plane.md) is bounded by $z=0$, $z=H$ and the two curves

$$
\boxed{x=\pm L\sqrt{1-z/(2H)},\qquad0\leq z\leq H.}
$$

Equivalently, these curves lie on the [parabola](../../../../../../parabola.md) $z=2H(1-x^2/L^2)$, truncated at height $H$. In particular the top width is $\sqrt2L$, so this projection has a horizontal upper edge from $-L/\sqrt2$ to $L/\sqrt2$; it is not triangular. The top horizontal cross-section has zero depth, consistent with this edge.
