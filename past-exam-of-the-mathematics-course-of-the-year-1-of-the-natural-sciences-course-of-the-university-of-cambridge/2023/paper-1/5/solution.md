<h1 id="5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

The addition formula for the [sine](../../../../sine.md) gives

$$
A\sin(t+\phi)=A\cos\phi\sin t+A\sin\phi\cos t.
$$

Equating the [sine](../../../../sine.md) and [cosine](../../../../cosine.md) coefficients gives $A\cos\phi=\sqrt3$ and $A\sin\phi=1$. Squaring and adding yields $A^2=4$, so $A=2$ because $A>0$. Both coefficients are positive, so $\phi$ is in the first quadrant. Consequently

$$
\boxed{A=2,\qquad\phi=\pi/6.}
$$
