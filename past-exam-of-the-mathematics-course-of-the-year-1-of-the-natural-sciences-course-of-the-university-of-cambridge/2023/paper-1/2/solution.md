<h1 id="2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

The given [polynomial root](../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md) supplies the factor $2x-3$. [Polynomial division](../../../../polynomial-division.md) gives

$$
2x^3-5x^2+x+3=(2x-3)(x^2-x-1).
$$

Solving the remaining [quadratic equation](../../../../quadratic-equation.md), the other two [polynomial roots](../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md) are

$$
\boxed{x=\frac{1+\sqrt5}{2}\quad\text{and}\quad x=\frac{1-\sqrt5}{2}.}
$$
