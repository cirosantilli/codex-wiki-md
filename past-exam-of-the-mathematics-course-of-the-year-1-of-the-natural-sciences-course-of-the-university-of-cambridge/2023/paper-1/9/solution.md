<h1 id="9/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [9](../9.md)

The [partial fraction decomposition](../../../../partial-fraction-decomposition.md) is

$$
\frac{2x+1}{x(x+1)}=\frac1x+\frac1{x+1}.
$$

Taking [antiderivatives](../../../../antiderivative.md) and using the positive endpoints gives

$$
\int_2^3\frac{2x+1}{x(x+1)}\,dx=[\ln x+\ln(x+1)]_2^3=\ln\frac{3\cdot4}{2\cdot3}=\ln2.
$$

Hence $\boxed{n=2}$.
