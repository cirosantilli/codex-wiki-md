<h1 id="17/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

With the standard unweighted [inner product](../../../../../inner-product-split.md) on real [square-integrable functions](../../../../../square-integrable-function.md), orthogonality on $[-L,L]$ means

$$
\boxed{\langle f,g\rangle=\int_{-L}^Lf(x)g(x)\,dx=0.}
$$

Multiplying this [inner product](../../../../../inner-product-split.md) by a common nonzero normalising constant does not change [orthogonality](../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md).
