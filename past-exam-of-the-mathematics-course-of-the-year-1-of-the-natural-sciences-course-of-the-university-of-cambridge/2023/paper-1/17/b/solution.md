<h1 id="17/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [Fourier coefficients](../../../../../fourier-coefficient.md) follow by multiplying the expansion by $1$, by the appropriate [cosine](../../../../../cosine.md), or by the appropriate [sine](../../../../../sine.md), then using [orthogonality](../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md). In the convention with constant term $a_0/2$,

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
a_0&=\frac1L\int_{-L}^Lh(x)\,dx,\\
a_n&=\frac1L\int_{-L}^Lh(x)\cos\frac{n\pi x}{L}\,dx,\qquad n\geq1,\\
b_n&=\frac1L\int_{-L}^Lh(x)\sin\frac{n\pi x}{L}\,dx,\qquad n\geq1.
\end{aligned}}
$$

The factor $1/L$ comes from the squared [norms](../../../../../norm.md) of the nonconstant [sine](../../../../../sine.md) and [cosine](../../../../../cosine.md) functions: the [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md) of each square over $[-L,L]$ is $L$. The constant [function](../../../../../function-split.md) has squared [norm](../../../../../norm.md) $2L$.
