<h1 id="17/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

[Termwise differentiation of a Fourier series](../../../../../termwise-differentiation-of-a-fourier-series.md), applied to the continuous periodic extension in part (c), gives for $-1<x<1$

$$
2x=-\frac4\pi\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^n}{n}\sin(n\pi x).
$$

Hence the required [Fourier sine series](../../../../../fourier-sine-series.md) is

$$
\boxed{x=\frac2\pi\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^{n+1}}n\sin(n\pi x),\qquad-1<x<1.}
$$

The endpoint deserves care: at $x=1$ every term is zero, so the [Fourier series](../../../../../fourier-series-split.md) sums to $0$, the average of the periodic one-sided limits $1$ and $-1$. It therefore does not reproduce the specified endpoint value $g(1)=1$. Changing a value at one [point](../../../../../point-geometry.md) does not change any [Fourier coefficient](../../../../../fourier-coefficient.md), so this is still the [Fourier series](../../../../../fourier-series-split.md) of the stated [function](../../../../../function-split.md) on $(-1,1]$.
