<h1 id="11/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Set $w=e^z\ne0$. The [hyperbolic cosine](../../../../../hyperbolic-cosine.md) identity gives $w+w^{-1}=2i$, hence the [quadratic equation](../../../../../quadratic-equation.md)

$$
w^2-2iw+1=0,\qquad w=i(1\pm\sqrt2).
$$

Let $L=\ln(1+\sqrt2)>0$. The first root has modulus $1+\sqrt2$ and [complex argument](../../../../../argument-complex-analysis.md) $\pi/2$; the second has modulus $\sqrt2-1=e^{-L}$ and [complex argument](../../../../../argument-complex-analysis.md) $-\pi/2$. Taking all [complex logarithm](../../../../../complex-logarithm.md) values gives

$$
\boxed{z=L+i\left(\frac\pi2+2k\pi\right)\quad\text{or}\quad z=-L+i\left(-\frac\pi2+2k\pi\right),\qquad k\in\mathbb Z.}
$$

The two families are distinct because their [real parts](../../../../../real-part.md) are respectively $L$ and $-L$. This exhausts the solutions because both roots for $w$ and all possible [complex arguments](../../../../../argument-complex-analysis.md) have been included.
