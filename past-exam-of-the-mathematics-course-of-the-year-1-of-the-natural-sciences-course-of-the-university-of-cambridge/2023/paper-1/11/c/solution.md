<h1 id="11/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For real $x$, $\cosh x>0$, so multiplication by the [hyperbolic cosine](../../../../../hyperbolic-cosine.md) is reversible. The identities $\cosh x-\sinh x=e^{-x}$ and $\operatorname{sech}x=1/\cosh x$ give

$$
e^{-x}\cosh x=2,\qquad \frac{1+e^{-2x}}2=2.
$$

Thus $e^{-2x}=3$. The [exponential function](../../../../../exponential-function.md) is injective, so the unique real solution is

$$
\boxed{x=-\frac12\ln3.}
$$
