<h1 id="11/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Multiply numerator and denominator by the [complex conjugate](../../../../../../complex-conjugate.md) $4-3i$:

$$
\frac{3-2i}{4+3i}=\frac{(3-2i)(4-3i)}{4^2+3^2}=\frac{6-17i}{25}.
$$

Thus the [real part](../../../../../../real-part.md) is $\boxed{6/25}$ and the [imaginary part](../../../../../../imaginary-part.md) is $\boxed{-17/25}$.
