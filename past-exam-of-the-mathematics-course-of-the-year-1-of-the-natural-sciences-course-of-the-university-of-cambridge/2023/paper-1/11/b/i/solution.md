<h1 id="11/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Since $z^2=(x^2-y^2)+2ixy$, equating its [real part](../../../../../../real-part.md) and [imaginary part](../../../../../../imaginary-part.md) gives $x^2-y^2=2xy$, or

$$
y^2+2xy-x^2=0.
$$

The [quadratic equation](../../../../../../quadratic-equation.md) in $y$ factors as $[y-(-1+\sqrt2)x][y-(-1-\sqrt2)x]=0$. Thus the set is the union of two [straight lines](../../../../../../straight-line.md) through the origin:

$$
\boxed{y=(-1+\sqrt2)x\quad\text{or}\quad y=(-1-\sqrt2)x.}
$$

Their slopes multiply to $-1$, so they are perpendicular. Their [angles](../../../../../../angle.md) to the real axis are $\pi/8$ and $-3\pi/8$, modulo $\pi$. The origin is included.
