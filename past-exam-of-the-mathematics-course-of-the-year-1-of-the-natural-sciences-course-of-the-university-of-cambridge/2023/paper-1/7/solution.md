<h1 id="7/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [7](../7.md)

The [chain rule](../../../../chain-rule.md) and the [quotient rule](../../../../quotient-rule.md) give

$$
f'(x)=\exp\left(\frac{x}{1+x^2}\right)\frac{1-x^2}{(1+x^2)^2}.
$$

The [exponential function](../../../../exponential-function.md) and denominator are positive, so the [stationary points](../../../../stationary-point.md) occur exactly at $x=\pm1$. Their values are

$$
\boxed{f(-1)=e^{-1/2},\qquad f(1)=e^{1/2}.}
$$

The [derivative](../../../../derivative.md) is negative for $|x|>1$ and positive for $|x|<1$, so $x=-1$ is a [local minimum](../../../../local-minimum.md) and $x=1$ a [local maximum](../../../../local-maximum.md). They are also global extrema, since $-1/2\leq x/(1+x^2)\leq1/2$.
