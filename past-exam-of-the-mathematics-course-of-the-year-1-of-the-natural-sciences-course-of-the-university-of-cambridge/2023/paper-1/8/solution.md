<h1 id="8/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [8](../8.md)

Apply [implicit differentiation](../../../../implicit-differentiation.md), remembering that $y$ depends on $x$:

$$
1+y'+e^x+e^y y'=0.
$$

Since $1+e^y>0$, division is always legitimate. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\frac{dy}{dx}=-\frac{1+e^x}{1+e^y}.}
$$
