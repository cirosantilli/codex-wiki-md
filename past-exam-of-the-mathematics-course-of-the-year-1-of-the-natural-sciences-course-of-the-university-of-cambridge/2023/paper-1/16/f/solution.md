<h1 id="16/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

At the [mode](../../../../../mode-statistics.md), the [cumulative distribution function](../../../../../cumulative-distribution-function.md) has value

$$
F(2/3)=4\left(\frac23\right)^3-3\left(\frac23\right)^4=\frac{16}{27}>\frac12.
$$

Since $F$ is strictly increasing on $(0,1)$, its unique crossing of $1/2$ occurs earlier. Therefore **the [median](../../../../../median.md) is less than the [mode](../../../../../mode-statistics.md)**.
