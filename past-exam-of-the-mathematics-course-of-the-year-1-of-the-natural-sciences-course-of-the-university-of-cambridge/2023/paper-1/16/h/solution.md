↑ **Parent:** [H](../h.md)

The requested bounds are $\mathbb E[X]-\sigma=2/5$ and $\mathbb E[X]+\sigma=4/5$, both within the support. Since the [probability distribution](../../../../../probability-distribution-split.md) is continuous, endpoints have zero [probability](../../../../../probability.md), and subtraction of the [cumulative distribution function](../../../../../cumulative-distribution-function.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P\left(\mathbb E[X]-\sigma\leq X\leq\mathbb E[X]+\sigma\right)=4\left[\left(\frac45\right)^3-\left(\frac25\right)^3\right]-3\left[\left(\frac45\right)^4-\left(\frac25\right)^4\right].}
$$

This leaves the answer in rational fractions as requested.
