<h1 id="16/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The full [cumulative distribution function](../../../../../cumulative-distribution-function.md) is

$$
\boxed{F(x)=\begin{cases}0,&x<0,\\4x^3-3x^4,&0\leq x\leq1,\\1,&x>1.\end{cases}}
$$

Thus the [function graph](../../../../../graph-of-a-function.md) is horizontal at zero for $-1\leq x\leq0$, rises continuously from $(0,0)$ to $(1,1)$, and is horizontal at one for $1\leq x\leq2$. Its [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) $12x^2(1-x)$ is positive on $(0,1)$ and zero at both endpoints. The second [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) $12x(2-3x)$ changes sign at $x=2/3$, giving an inflection [point](../../../../../point-geometry.md) there.
