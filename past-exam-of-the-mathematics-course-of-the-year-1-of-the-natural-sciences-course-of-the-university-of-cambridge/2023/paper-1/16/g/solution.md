↑ **Parent:** [G](../g.md)

The [expected value](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) is

$$
\mathbb E[X]=\int_0^1x\,12x^2(1-x)\,dx=12\left(\frac14-\frac15\right)=\frac35.
$$

The second moment is $\mathbb E[X^2]=12(1/5-1/6)=2/5$. Hence the [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) and [standard deviation](../../../../../standard-deviation.md) are

$$
\operatorname{Var}(X)=\frac25-\left(\frac35\right)^2=\frac1{25},\qquad\boxed{\mathbb E[X]=\frac35,\quad\sigma=\frac15.}
$$
