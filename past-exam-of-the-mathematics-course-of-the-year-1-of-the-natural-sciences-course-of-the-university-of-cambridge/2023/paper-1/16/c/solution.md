↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [probability density function](../../../../../probability-density-function.md) is the [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) of the [cumulative distribution function](../../../../../cumulative-distribution-function.md):

$$
\boxed{f(x)=\begin{cases}12x^2(1-x),&0\leq x\leq1,\\0,&x<0\text{ or }x>1.\end{cases}}
$$

It is nonnegative and has [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md) $F(1)-F(0)=1$. This is a [beta distribution](../../../../../beta-distribution.md) with parameters $3$ and $2$.
