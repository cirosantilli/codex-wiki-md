<h1 id="16/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The interior [stationary point](../../../../../stationary-point.md) of the [probability density function](../../../../../probability-density-function.md) solves $12x(2-3x)=0$ with $0<x<1$, giving $x=2/3$. The sign change of $f'$ established in part (d), together with zero endpoint values, makes this the global maximum. Thus

$$
\boxed{\text{mode}=\frac23,\qquad f(2/3)=\frac{16}{9}.}
$$
