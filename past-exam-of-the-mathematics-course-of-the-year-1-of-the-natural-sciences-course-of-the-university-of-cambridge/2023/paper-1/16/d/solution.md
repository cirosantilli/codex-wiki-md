<h1 id="16/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [probability density function](../../../../../probability-density-function.md) is zero outside $[0,1]$. Inside that [interval](../../../../../interval-mathematics.md), it rises from $(0,0)$, reaches its only interior maximum at $x=2/3$, and falls to $(1,0)$. The [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) is

$$
f'(x)=12x(2-3x),
$$

positive for $0<x<2/3$ and negative for $2/3<x<1$. Its maximum height is $f(2/3)=16/9$. The second panel of the figure in part (b) shows the [function graph](../../../../../graph-of-a-function.md) on the requested full range, with this [mode](../../../../../mode-statistics.md) marked.
