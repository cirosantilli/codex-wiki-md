↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

This is a [finite geometric series](../../../../finite-geometric-series.md) with common ratio $r=1/\sqrt2$. Since $r^{10}=1/32$,

$$
\sum_{n=1}^{10}2^{-n/2}=\frac{r(1-r^{10})}{1-r}=\frac{31}{32}\frac1{\sqrt2-1}=\frac{31+31\sqrt2}{32}.
$$

Thus $\boxed{p=31,\ q=31}$.
