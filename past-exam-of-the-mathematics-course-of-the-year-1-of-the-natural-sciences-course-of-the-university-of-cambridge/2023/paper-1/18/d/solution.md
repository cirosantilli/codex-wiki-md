<h1 id="18/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

[Completing the square](../../../../../completing-the-square.md) in the [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) gives

$$
\det A_\alpha=10\left(\alpha-\frac12\right)^2+\frac{11}{2}>0
$$

for every real $\alpha$. A [square matrix](../../../../../square-matrix.md) is invertible exactly when its [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) is nonzero, so **$A_\alpha$ is nonsingular for every real $\alpha$**.
