<h1 id="18/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Let $p(t)=\det(tI-A_\alpha)$ be the real [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md). It is a monic cubic, so $p(t)\to+\infty$ as $t\to+\infty$. At zero,

$$
p(0)=\det(-A_\alpha)=-\det A_\alpha<0.
$$

The [intermediate value theorem](../../../../../intermediate-value-theorem.md) therefore gives some $t_0>0$ with $p(t_0)=0$. A root of the [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) is an [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md), hence **$A_\alpha$ has a positive real [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) for every real $\alpha$**. This uses the general fact that an [odd-dimensional real matrix with positive determinant has a positive eigenvalue](../../../../../odd-dimensional-real-matrix-with-positive-determinant-has-a-positive-eigenvalue.md).
