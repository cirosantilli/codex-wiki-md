<h1 id="1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

The [binomial theorem](../../../../binomial-theorem.md) gives $(1+x)^8=\sum_{k=0}^8\binom8k x^k$. The required [binomial coefficients](../../../../binomial-coefficient-split.md) are therefore

$$
\boxed{[x^4](1+x)^8=\binom84=70,\qquad [x^5](1+x)^8=\binom85=56.}
$$

Here $[x^k]$ denotes the coefficient of $x^k$ in the [polynomial](../../../../polynomial-split.md).
