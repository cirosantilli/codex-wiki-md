<h1 id="19/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The term at $k=0$ is finite. For every $k\geq1$,

$$
0<\frac1{1+k^2}\leq\frac1{k^2}.
$$

The comparison [P-series](../../../../../../p-series.md) $\sum k^{-2}$ converges, so the [comparison test for series](../../../../../../comparison-test-for-series.md) proves that **the given [series](../../../../../../series-mathematics.md) converges absolutely**.
