<h1 id="10/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [10](../10.md)

The [function graphs](../../../../graph-of-a-function.md) intersect when $2x=4xe^{-x}$, so $x=0$ or $x=\ln2$. For $0<x<\ln2$, the [exponential function](../../../../exponential-function.md) satisfies $e^{-x}>1/2$, making $4xe^{-x}$ the upper graph. The enclosed [area](../../../../surface-area.md) is therefore

$$
\begin{aligned}
A&=\int_0^{\ln2}(4xe^{-x}-2x)\,dx\\
&=[-4(x+1)e^{-x}-x^2]_0^{\ln2}\\
&=\boxed{2-2\ln2-(\ln2)^2.}
\end{aligned}
$$

The [antiderivative](../../../../antiderivative.md) of $xe^{-x}$ follows by [integration by parts](../../../../integration-by-parts.md).
