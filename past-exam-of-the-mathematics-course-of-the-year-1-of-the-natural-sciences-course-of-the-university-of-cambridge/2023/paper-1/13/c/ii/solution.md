<h1 id="13/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [initial condition](../../../../../../initial-condition.md) gives $C=\sin(\pi/3)=\sqrt3/2$. Since $\pi/3$ belongs to the principal [inverse sine](../../../../../../inverse-sine.md) range, choose $k=0$ in part (i). The particular solution is

$$
\boxed{y(x)=x\arcsin\left(\frac{\sqrt3x}{2}\right),\qquad0<x<\frac2{\sqrt3}.}
$$

The upper endpoint is excluded because $y/x\to\pi/2$ there and the original [tangent](../../../../../../tangent.md) ceases to be defined.
