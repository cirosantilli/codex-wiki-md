<h1 id="13/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Again $C=\sin(2\pi/3)=\sqrt3/2$, but the principal [inverse sine](../../../../../../inverse-sine.md) would give $\pi/3$ at $x=1$. The required branch is $v=\pi-\arcsin(Cx)$, corresponding to $k=1$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{y(x)=x\left[\pi-\arcsin\left(\frac{\sqrt3x}{2}\right)\right],\qquad0<x<\frac2{\sqrt3}.}
$$

At $x=1$ the bracket is $2\pi/3$, as required. Both the [initial condition](../../../../../../initial-condition.md) and the original [differential equation](../../../../../../differential-equation-split.md) are thus satisfied on the stated branch.
