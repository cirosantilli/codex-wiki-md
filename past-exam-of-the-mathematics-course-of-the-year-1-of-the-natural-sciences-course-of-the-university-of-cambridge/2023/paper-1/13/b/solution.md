<h1 id="13/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For this [first-order linear differential equation](../../../../../first-order-linear-differential-equation.md), an [integrating factor](../../../../../integrating-factor.md) on an [interval](../../../../../interval-mathematics.md) avoiding zeros of $\cos x$ is $\cos x$. The [product rule](../../../../../product-rule.md) then gives

$$
(y\cos x)'=\cos x,\qquad y\cos x=\sin x+C.
$$

The [initial condition](../../../../../initial-condition.md) gives $3/\sqrt2=1/\sqrt2+C$, hence $C=\sqrt2$. Thus

$$
\boxed{y(x)=\frac{\sin x+\sqrt2}{\cos x},\qquad-\frac\pi2<x<\frac\pi2.}
$$

The displayed [interval](../../../../../interval-mathematics.md) is the maximal one containing the specified initial [point](../../../../../point-geometry.md), since the coefficient $\tan x$ is undefined at its endpoints.
