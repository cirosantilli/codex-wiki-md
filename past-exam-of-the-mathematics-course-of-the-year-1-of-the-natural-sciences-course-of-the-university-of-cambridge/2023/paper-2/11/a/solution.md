<h1 id="11/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For the specified [standard basis](../../../../../standard-basis.md) vectors, $\mathbf a\times\mathbf b=\hat{\mathbf k}$, so

$$
(\mathbf a\times\mathbf b)\times\mathbf c=\hat{\mathbf k}\times\hat{\mathbf k}=0.
$$

On the other hand, $\mathbf b\times\mathbf c=\hat{\mathbf i}-\hat{\mathbf j}$ and hence

$$
\mathbf a\times(\mathbf b\times\mathbf c)
=\hat{\mathbf i}\times(\hat{\mathbf i}-\hat{\mathbf j})=-\hat{\mathbf k}.
$$

These results are unequal, proving that the [cross product](../../../../../cross-product.md) does not satisfy the [associative property](../../../../../associative-property.md).
