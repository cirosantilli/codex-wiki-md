<h1 id="17/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Suppose $B\mathbf x=0$. The given [quadratic form](../../../../../quadratic-form-split.md) bound then gives

$$
0=\mathbf x^TB\mathbf x\geq|\mathbf x|^2,
$$

so $\mathbf x=0$. Thus the [kernel](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) of $B$ is trivial. A square [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) with trivial [kernel](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) is an [invertible matrix](../../../../../invertible-matrix.md), proving the assertion. No symmetry assumption on $B$ is needed.
