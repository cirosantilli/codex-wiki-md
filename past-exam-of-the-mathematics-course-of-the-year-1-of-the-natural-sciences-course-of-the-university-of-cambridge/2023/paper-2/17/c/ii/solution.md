↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Direct [matrix multiplication](../../../../../../matrix-multiplication.md) gives

$$
AP=\begin{pmatrix}2&4\\-1&4\end{pmatrix},\qquad
P^{-1}AP=\frac13\begin{pmatrix}1&-1\\1&2\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}2&4\\-1&4\end{pmatrix}
=\boxed{\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&4\end{pmatrix}}.
$$

Thus $D$ is diagonal. The columns of $P$ are [eigenvectors](../../../../../../eigenvector.md) of $A$ with [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) one and four.
