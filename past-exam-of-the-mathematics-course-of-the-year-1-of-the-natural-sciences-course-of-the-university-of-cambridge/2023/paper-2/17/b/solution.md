<h1 id="17/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The two prescribed directions have [dot product](../../../../../dot-product.md) zero. Normalize them to

$$
\mathbf q_1=\frac{(1,1,1)^T}{\sqrt3},\qquad
\mathbf q_2=\frac{(0,-1,1)^T}{\sqrt2}.
$$

Their [cross product](../../../../../cross-product.md) is the perpendicular [unit vector](../../../../../unit-vector.md) $\mathbf q_3=(2,-1,-1)^T/\sqrt6$. Using these as columns gives

$$
\boxed{Q=\begin{pmatrix}
1/\sqrt3&0&2/\sqrt6\\
1/\sqrt3&-1/\sqrt2&-1/\sqrt6\\
1/\sqrt3&1/\sqrt2&-1/\sqrt6
\end{pmatrix}}.
$$

The columns form an [orthonormal basis](../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md), so $Q^TQ=I$ and $Q$ is an [orthogonal matrix](../../../../../orthogonal-matrix.md).
