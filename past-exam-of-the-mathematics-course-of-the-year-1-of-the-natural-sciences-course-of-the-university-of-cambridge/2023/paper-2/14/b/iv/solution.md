<h1 id="14/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The product is

$$
\boxed{48\cdot15=720=6!}.
$$

Every [permutation](../../../../../../permutation.md) determines exactly one bin partition, and each such partition has exactly forty-eight internal orderings. Thus the fifteen classes partition the original [sample space](../../../../../../sample-space.md) of $720$ arrangements, without overlap or omission.
