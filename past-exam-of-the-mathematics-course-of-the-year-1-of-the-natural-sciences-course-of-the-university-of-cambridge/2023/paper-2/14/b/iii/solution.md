<h1 id="14/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Choose the unordered pair of letters in the second bin; the first bin is then determined. The [binomial coefficient](../../../../../../binomial-coefficient-split.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\binom62=\frac{6!}{4!\,2!}=15}.
$$

The first and second bins are distinguished, so their roles are not interchangeable.
