<h1 id="14/a/vi/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vi](../vi.md)

Requiring exactly one condition means taking the [symmetric difference](../../../../../../symmetric-difference.md) of the two [sets](../../../../../../set-split.md). Subtract their shared cases twice from the two individual counts:

$$
\boxed{|E_5\mathbin{\triangle}F_6|=5!+5!-2\cdot4!=192}.
$$

Equivalently, remove the twenty-four arrangements satisfying both conditions from the inclusive count $216$.
