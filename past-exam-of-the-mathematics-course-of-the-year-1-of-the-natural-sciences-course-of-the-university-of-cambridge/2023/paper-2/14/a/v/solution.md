<h1 id="14/a/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

Let $E_5$ denote the [set](../../../../../../set-split.md) of arrangements with $E$ fifth, and $F_6$ those with $F$ sixth. Each contains $5!$ arrangements, while their [intersection](../../../../../../set-intersection.md) contains $4!$. The [inclusion-exclusion principle](../../../../../../inclusion-exclusion-principle.md) gives

$$
\boxed{|E_5\cup F_6|=5!+5!-4!=216}.
$$
