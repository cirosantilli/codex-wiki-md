<h1 id="10/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

All three [linear equations](../../../../../linear-equation.md) are multiples of the first, leaving the single constraint $x+y+z=0$. Choose two [real numbers](../../../../../real-number.md) $s,t$ freely:

$$
\boxed{(x,y,z)=(s,t,-s-t),\qquad s,t\in\mathbb R}.
$$

A [basis](../../../../../basis.md) for this two-dimensional [linear subspace](../../../../../vector-subspace.md) is $(1,0,-1)^T,(0,1,-1)^T$.
