↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The original PDF places [complement](../../../../../complement-of-a-set.md) bars on the second $A$ in each parenthesis and on the final $A$. Thus the first parenthesis is the [empty set](../../../../../empty-set.md), whereas the second is the whole [sample space](../../../../../sample-space.md). Their [union](../../../../../set-union.md) with any further [subset](../../../../../subset.md) is still the whole space:

$$
\boxed{Y=\Omega}.
$$

The bars are missing from the local OCR, so using its unbarred expression would give the wrong result.
