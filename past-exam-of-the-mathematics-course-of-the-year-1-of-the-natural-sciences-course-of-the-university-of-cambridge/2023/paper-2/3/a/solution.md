↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [vector triple product identity](../../../../../vector-triple-product.md) is

$$
\mathbf a\times(\mathbf b\times\mathbf c)
=\mathbf b(\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf c)-\mathbf c(\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b).
$$

Consequently a choice of the requested [coefficients](../../../../../coefficient.md) is

$$
\boxed{\alpha=0,\qquad\beta=\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf c,\qquad\gamma=-\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b}.
$$

These are the standard identity's [coefficients](../../../../../coefficient.md), even if the particular three [vectors](../../../../../vector.md) are [linearly dependent](../../../../../linear-dependence.md) and a representation need not be unique.
