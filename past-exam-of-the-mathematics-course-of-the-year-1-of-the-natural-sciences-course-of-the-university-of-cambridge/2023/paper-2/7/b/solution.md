<h1 id="7/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Normalize the direction to the [unit vector](../../../../../unit-vector.md) $\hat{\mathbf u}=(1,1,0)^T/\sqrt2$. The [directional derivative](../../../../../directional-derivative.md) is the [dot product](../../../../../dot-product.md) of this [unit vector](../../../../../unit-vector.md) with the [gradient](../../../../../gradient.md):

$$
\boxed{D_{\hat{\mathbf u}}\Phi(0)=(-1,-1,-1)\cdot\frac{(1,1,0)}{\sqrt2}=-\sqrt2}.
$$
