<h1 id="12/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Here the [stationary point](../../../../../../stationary-point.md) is the origin, of value two. The [Taylor expansion](../../../../../../taylor-expansion.md) is

$$
h=2+\frac{x^2+y^2}{2}+\frac{x^3+y^3}{6}+O((|x|+|y|)^4).
$$

Thus nearby [level curves](../../../../../../level-curve.md) are approximately concentric circles. Globally the [level curves](../../../../../../level-curve.md) above two are closed around the unique [minimum](../../../../../../minimum-of-a-subset-of-a-total-order.md), symmetric under interchanging $x$ and $y$, but not under changing either coordinate's sign. The faster exponential growth for positive coordinates compresses the curves on their positive sides.
