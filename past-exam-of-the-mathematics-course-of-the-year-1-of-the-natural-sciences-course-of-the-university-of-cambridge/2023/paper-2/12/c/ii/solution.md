↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Now $h=e^x-e^y-x+y$ and the origin has stationary value zero. Near it,

$$
h=\frac{x^2-y^2}{2}+\frac{x^3-y^3}{6}+O((|x|+|y|)^4).
$$

The [level curves](../../../../../../level-curve.md) therefore resemble hyperbolas near the [saddle point of a scalar function](../../../../../../saddle-point-of-a-scalar-function.md). Along the $x$-axis $h=e^x-x-1>0$ for $x\ne0$, while along the $y$-axis $h=1-e^y+y<0$ for $y\ne0$. The zero [level set](../../../../../../level-set.md) includes the exact diagonal $x=y$ and another branch crossing it at the origin; this second branch is tangent to $y=-x$ but is curved away from the origin. The right panel of the contour figure displays these features.
