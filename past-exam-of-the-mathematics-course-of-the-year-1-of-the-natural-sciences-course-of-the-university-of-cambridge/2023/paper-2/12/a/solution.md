<h1 id="12/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [stationary point](../../../../../stationary-point.md) equations are $ae^x=c$ and $be^y=d$. The sign assumptions make both ratios positive, so each equation has exactly one real solution. Therefore

$$
\boxed{(x_*,y_*)=\left(\log\frac ca,\ \log\frac db\right)}.
$$

Substitution gives the stationary value

$$
\boxed{h_*=c+d-c\log\frac ca-d\log\frac db}.
$$
