<h1 id="15/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Differentiate the first [ordinary differential equation](../../../../../../ordinary-differential-equation.md) and substitute the second:

$$
u''=au'+bv'=au'+bcu+bdv.
$$

The original first equation also gives $bv=u'-au$, so

$$
u''=(a+d)u'+(bc-ad)u.
$$

Therefore the requested [coefficients](../../../../../../coefficient.md) are

$$
\boxed{\alpha=a+d,\qquad\beta=ad-bc}.
$$

They are the [trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md) and [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) of the coefficient [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md). This derivation never divides by $b$ and is valid even if $b=0$. In that degenerate case the scalar second-order equation is a necessary consequence, while the original constraint $u'=au$ must also be retained when reconstructing the coupled system.
