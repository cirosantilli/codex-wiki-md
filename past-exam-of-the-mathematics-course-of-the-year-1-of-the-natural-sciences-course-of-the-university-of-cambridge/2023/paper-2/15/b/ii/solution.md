Here $\alpha=4$ and $\beta=4$, so the reduced [ordinary differential equation](../../../../../../ordinary-differential-equation.md) is $(D-2)^2u=0$. Its repeated [characteristic root](../../../../../../characteristic-root-of-a-constant-coefficient-differential-equation.md) gives $u=(A+Bt)e^{2t}$. The first [initial condition](../../../../../../initial-condition.md) sets $A=0$. The original equation $u'=3u-v$ gives $u'(0)=-1$, hence $B=-1$.

Recover $v=3u-u'$ to obtain

$$
\boxed{u(t)=-te^{2t},\qquad v(t)=(1-t)e^{2t}}.
$$

Direct differentiation verifies $v'=u+v$ and both [initial conditions](../../../../../../initial-condition.md).
