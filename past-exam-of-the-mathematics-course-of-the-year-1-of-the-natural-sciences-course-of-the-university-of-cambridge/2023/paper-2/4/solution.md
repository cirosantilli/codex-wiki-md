↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

The traversal fixes the [orientation](../../../../orientation-of-a-simplex.md) of the [vector area](../../../../vector-area.md) as half the [cross product](../../../../cross-product.md) of the edges $AB$ and $AC$:

$$
\mathbf S_{ABCA}=\frac12(1,1,0)^T\times(1,1,1)^T
=\boxed{\frac12(1,-1,0)^T}.
$$

Its [Euclidean norm](../../../../euclidean-norm.md) $1/\sqrt2$ is the [area](../../../../surface-area.md) of the [triangle](../../../../triangle.md), and its direction follows the [right-hand rule](../../../../right-hand-rule.md).
