↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [volume form](../../../../../../volume-form.md) in [cylindrical coordinates](../../../../../../cylindrical-coordinate-system.md) is $dV=r\,dr\,d\phi\,dz$. Integrating around the axis and over the height gives cylindrical-shell [volume](../../../../../../volume.md) $2\pi hr\,dr$. Hence the [volume integral](../../../../../../volume-integral.md) is

$$
\boxed{K=\pi h\int_0^a r\,v(r)^2\,dr
=\pi h\int_0^a r^3\omega(r)^2\,dr}.
$$

For unit [density](../../../../../../density.md), this is the [kinetic energy](../../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) of the rotational motion.
