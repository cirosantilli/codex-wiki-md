<h1 id="19/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Equality in the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](../../../../../../cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) requires $\sqrt r\,v(r)=\Omega r^{3/2}$ for one constant $\Omega$. Thus $v(r)=\Omega r$ and

$$
\boxed{\omega(r)=\Omega\text{ independent of }r}.
$$

This is [solid-body rotation](../../../../../../solid-body-rotation.md). At fixed $L$, the constant is $\Omega=2L/(Va^2)$. The value at the single axis point $r=0$ does not affect the [integrals](../../../../../../integral-split.md); if $\omega$ is continuous, it has the same constant value there as well.
