<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In the [cylindrical coordinate system](../../../../../cylindrical-coordinate-system.md), $r=\sqrt{x^2+y^2}=5$ and $z=-1$. The point is in the third quadrant, so a representative azimuth in $[0,2\pi)$ is $\pi+\arctan(4/3)$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{(r,\theta,z)=\left(5,\ \pi+\arctan\frac43,\ -1\right)}.
$$

The angle is defined modulo $2\pi$.
