<h1 id="9/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Contracting the [Kronecker delta](../../../../../kronecker-delta.md) identifies $i=k$. The remaining two independent sums are [dot products](../../../../../dot-product.md), so the [Einstein summation convention](../../../../../einstein-notation.md) gives

$$
a_i\delta_{ik}b_ma_mc_k
=\left(\sum_k a_kc_k\right)\left(\sum_m b_ma_m\right)
=\boxed{(\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf c)(\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b)}.
$$

The index expression here is verified against the PDF because the OCR corrupts it completely.
