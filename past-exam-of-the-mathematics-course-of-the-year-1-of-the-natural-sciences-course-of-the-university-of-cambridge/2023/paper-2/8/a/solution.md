<h1 id="8/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Reflection in the $y$-axis preserves the [disk](../../../../../disk-mathematics.md) but changes the sign of the [integrand](../../../../../integrand.md) $xy$. The [double integral](../../../../../double-integral.md) is therefore its own negative and must be

$$
\boxed{0}.
$$

Equivalently, for each fixed $y$ the [integrand](../../../../../integrand.md) is an [odd function](../../../../../odd-function.md) of $x$ on a symmetric interval.
