<h1 id="13/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For $\mathbf F$, all three [curl](../../../../../curl.md) components vanish for every $a$. More constructively,

$$
\mathbf F=\nabla\phi_F,\qquad
\phi_F=2x^2+y^2+z^2-2xy-2xz+ayz.
$$

Hence **$\mathbf F$ is conservative for every real $a$**. Its endpoint [vector-field potential](../../../../../potential-of-a-conservative-vector-field.md) difference is $a$, checking both [line integrals](../../../../../line-integral.md).

For the other [vector field](../../../../../vector-field.md), direct [partial differentiation](../../../../../partial-derivative.md) gives

$$
\nabla\times\mathbf G=2b(1,1,1)^T.
$$

It therefore has a [vector-field potential](../../../../../potential-of-a-conservative-vector-field.md) exactly when $b=0$, with no restriction on $c$:

$$
\boxed{b=0,\quad c\text{ arbitrary},\qquad
\phi_G=\sin(xy)+cxz+C}.
$$

The value of $a$ is irrelevant to $\mathbf G$. Its rotational part for general $b$ is $b(1,1,1)^T\times\mathbf r$. Both selected paths lie in $x=y$, and this rotational part has zero [dot product](../../../../../dot-product.md) with every [tangent vector](../../../../../tangent-vector-split.md) to that [plane](../../../../../plane.md). That explains why these paths cannot detect its nonzero [curl](../../../../../curl.md) when $b\ne0$.
