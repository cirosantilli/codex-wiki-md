<h1 id="13/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

On the first segment, parameterize $\mathbf r_1(t)=(t,t,0)^T$, $0\leq t\leq1$. The [dot products](../../../../../../dot-product.md) with $\mathbf r_1'=(1,1,0)^T$ are

$$
\mathbf F(\mathbf r_1)\cdot\mathbf r_1'=2t,\qquad
\mathbf G(\mathbf r_1)\cdot\mathbf r_1'=2t\cos(t^2).
$$

Their [line integrals](../../../../../../line-integral.md) are one and $\sin1$, respectively.

On the second segment take $\mathbf r_2(t)=(1,1,t)^T$, with [tangent vector](../../../../../../tangent-vector-split.md) $(0,0,1)^T$. The corresponding [integrands](../../../../../../integrand.md) are $a-2+2t$ and $c$, whose [integrals](../../../../../../integral-split.md) are $a-1$ and $c$. Adding the segments yields

$$
\boxed{\int_{C_1}\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r=a,\qquad
\int_{C_1}\mathbf G\cdot d\mathbf r=\sin1+c}.
$$
