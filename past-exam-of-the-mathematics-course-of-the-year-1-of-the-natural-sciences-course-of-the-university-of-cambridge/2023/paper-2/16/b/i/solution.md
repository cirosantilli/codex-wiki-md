<h1 id="16/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Parameterize the surface by $\mathbf r(x,z)=(x,1+z,z)^T$. Its two [tangent vectors](../../../../../../tangent-vector-split.md) are $(1,0,0)^T$ and $(0,1,1)^T$, whose [cross product](../../../../../../cross-product.md) is $(0,-1,1)^T$ of [Euclidean norm](../../../../../../euclidean-norm.md) $\sqrt2$. Thus the scalar [area element of a surface](../../../../../../area-element-of-a-surface.md) is

$$
\boxed{dS=\sqrt2\,dx\,dz}.
$$

Equivalently, projection onto the $xz$-[plane](../../../../../../plane.md) reduces the surface [area](../../../../../../surface-area.md) by $|\mathbf n\cdot\mathbf e_y|=1/\sqrt2$.
