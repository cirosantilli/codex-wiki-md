<h1 id="16/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

On the surface $y=1+z$, the [integrand](../../../../../../integrand.md) is $x+z+z^2$. Using the [area element of a surface](../../../../../../area-element-of-a-surface.md) from part (i),

$$
\begin{aligned}
\iint_S(x+yz)\,dS
&=\sqrt2\int_0^{2\pi}\int_0^1(x+z+z^2)\,dz\,dx\\
&=\sqrt2\int_0^{2\pi}\left(x+\frac56\right)dx\\
&=\boxed{\sqrt2\left(2\pi^2+\frac{5\pi}{3}\right)}.
\end{aligned}
$$
