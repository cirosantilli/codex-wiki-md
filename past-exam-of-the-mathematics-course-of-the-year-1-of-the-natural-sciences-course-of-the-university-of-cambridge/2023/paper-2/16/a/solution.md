<h1 id="16/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The cube is $1\leq x\leq2$, $0\leq y,z\leq1$, so $|x|=x$ throughout it. Evaluate the six outward [surface integrals](../../../../../surface-integral.md) directly.

On $x=2$ the normal component is $2$, giving [flux](../../../../../flux.md) $2$; on $x=1$ it is $-1$, giving [flux](../../../../../flux.md) $-1$. On $y=1$ the normal component is $2x$, giving $\int_1^2\int_0^12x\,dz\,dx=3$; on $y=0$ it is zero. On $z=1$ the normal component is $e^{-1}$ and on $z=0$ it is $-1$, so those unit-area faces give $e^{-1}$ and $-1$.

Adding all six contributions yields

$$
\boxed{\Phi=2-1+3+0+e^{-1}-1=3+e^{-1}}.
$$

Only direct [surface integral](../../../../../surface-integral.md) was used, as requested.
