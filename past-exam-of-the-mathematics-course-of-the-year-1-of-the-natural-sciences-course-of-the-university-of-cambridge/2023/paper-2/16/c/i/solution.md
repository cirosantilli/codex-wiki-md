<h1 id="16/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

On the unit [sphere](../../../../../../sphere.md), the outward [unit normal](../../../../../../unit-normal.md) is $\mathbf n=(x,y,z)^T$, so the normal component of the radial [vector field](../../../../../../vector-field.md) is $\mathbf F\cdot\mathbf n=x^2+y^2+z^2=1$. The direct [surface integral](../../../../../../surface-integral.md) is

$$
\Phi=\int_0^{2\pi}\int_0^\pi1\cdot\sin\theta\,d\theta\,d\phi
=\boxed{4\pi}.
$$

Here $dS=\sin\theta\,d\theta\,d\phi$ is the unit-sphere [area element of a surface](../../../../../../area-element-of-a-surface.md) in [spherical coordinates](../../../../../../spherical-coordinate-system.md).
