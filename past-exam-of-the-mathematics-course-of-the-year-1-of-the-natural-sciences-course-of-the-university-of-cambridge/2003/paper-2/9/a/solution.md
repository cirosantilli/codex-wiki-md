<h1 id="9/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

At fixed particle number, the differential of the [internal energy](../../../../../internal-energy-split.md) identifies $T=(\partial U/\partial S)_V$ and $p=-(\partial U/\partial V)_S$. Equality of continuous [mixed partial derivatives](../../../../../mixed-partial-derivative.md) gives the first [Maxwell relation](../../../../../maxwell-relations.md):

$$
\boxed{\left(\frac{\partial T}{\partial V}\right)_S=\frac{\partial^2U}{\partial V\partial S}=-\left(\frac{\partial p}{\partial S}\right)_V.}
$$

For the [enthalpy](../../../../../enthalpy-split.md), $dH=dU+p\,dV+V\,dp=T\,dS+V\,dp$. Thus $T=(\partial H/\partial S)_p$, $V=(\partial H/\partial p)_S$, and the same argument gives the [enthalpy Maxwell relation](../../../../../enthalpy-maxwell-relation.md)

$$
\boxed{\left(\frac{\partial T}{\partial p}\right)_S=\frac{\partial^2H}{\partial p\partial S}=\left(\frac{\partial V}{\partial S}\right)_p.}
$$

The smoothness and independent thermodynamic coordinates are understood within a single phase.
