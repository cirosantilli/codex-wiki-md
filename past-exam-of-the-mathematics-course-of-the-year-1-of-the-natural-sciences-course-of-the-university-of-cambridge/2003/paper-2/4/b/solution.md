<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [divergence](../../../../../divergence.md) is $\nabla\cdot F=2xyz+2xyz+2xyz=6xyz$. With outward orientation, the [divergence theorem](../../../../../divergence-theorem.md) converts the [surface flux](../../../../../flux-integral.md) into a [volume integral](../../../../../volume-integral.md):

$$
\boxed{\int_SF\cdot dS=6\int_0^1\int_0^1\int_0^1xyz\,dx\,dy\,dz=6\left(\frac12\right)^3=\frac34.}
$$

Directly, the three faces lying in coordinate [planes](../../../../../plane.md) contribute zero, and each opposite face contributes $1/4$, confirming the sign and normalization.
