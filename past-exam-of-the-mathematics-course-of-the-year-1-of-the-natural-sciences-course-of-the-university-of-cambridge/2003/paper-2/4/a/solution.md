<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a bounded [volume](../../../../../volume.md) $V$ with piecewise smooth closed boundary $\partial V$, oriented by the outward [unit normal vector](../../../../../unit-normal.md), and a continuously differentiable [vector field](../../../../../vector-field.md) on a neighborhood of $\overline V$, the [divergence theorem](../../../../../divergence-theorem.md) states

$$
\int_V\nabla\cdot F\,dV=\int_{\partial V}F\cdot n\,dS.
$$

For an oriented piecewise smooth [surface](../../../../../topological-surface-split.md) $S$ with piecewise smooth boundary $C=\partial S$, and a continuously differentiable [vector field](../../../../../vector-field.md) on a neighborhood of $S$, [Stokes theorem](../../../../../stokes-theorem.md) states

$$
\int_S(\nabla\times F)\cdot n\,dS=\oint_CF\cdot dr.
$$

The boundary orientation follows the right-hand rule relative to the chosen [normal vector](../../../../../normal-vector.md). Reversing that orientation reverses both sides of the latter identity.
