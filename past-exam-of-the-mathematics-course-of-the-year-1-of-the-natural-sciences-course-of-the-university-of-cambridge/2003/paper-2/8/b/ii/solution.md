<h1 id="8/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Apply [trace orthogonality of symmetric and antisymmetric matrices](../../../../../../trace-orthogonality-of-symmetric-and-antisymmetric-matrices.md). In detail, invariance of the [trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md) under [transpose](../../../../../../transpose.md) and its cyclic property give

$$
\operatorname{tr}(SA)=\operatorname{tr}((SA)^T)=\operatorname{tr}(A^TS^T)=-\operatorname{tr}(AS)=-\operatorname{tr}(SA).
$$

Therefore $\boxed{\operatorname{tr}(SA)=0}$. The negative sign comes from $A^T=-A$, while $S^T=S$.
