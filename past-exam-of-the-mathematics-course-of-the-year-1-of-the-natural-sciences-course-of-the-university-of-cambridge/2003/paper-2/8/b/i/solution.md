↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [transpose](../../../../../../transpose.md) reverses a [matrix product](../../../../../../matrix-product.md). Since $S^T=S$, we have $(S^2)^T=S^TS^T=S^2$. Therefore **$S^2$ is a [symmetric matrix](../../../../../../symmetric-matrix.md)**.
