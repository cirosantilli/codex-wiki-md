↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md) sums the diagonal entries, so $\operatorname{tr}P=\sum_i1-\sum_i a_i^2-\sum_i b_i^2$. The two [unit vectors](../../../../../../unit-vector.md) have squared [Euclidean norms](../../../../../../euclidean-norm.md) one, giving $\boxed{\operatorname{tr}P=n-2}$.
