<h1 id="8/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [matrix product](../../../../../../matrix-product.md) is $Pa=a-a(a^Ta)-b(b^Ta)=a-a=0$. Thus $\boxed{Pa=0}$, and the nonzero [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) $a$ is in its [kernel](../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md). Hence $P$ is singular and $\boxed{\det P=0}$. This includes $n=2$, when $a,b$ span the whole space and $P=0$.
