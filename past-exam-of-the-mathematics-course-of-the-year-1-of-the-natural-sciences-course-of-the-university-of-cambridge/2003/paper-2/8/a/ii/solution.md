<h1 id="8/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Write $P=I-Q$ with $Q=aa^T+bb^T$. The [orthonormality](../../../../../../orthonormal-set.md) conditions $a^Ta=b^Tb=1$ and $a^Tb=b^Ta=0$ give

$$
Q^2=a(a^Ta)a^T+a(a^Tb)b^T+b(b^Ta)a^T+b(b^Tb)b^T=aa^T+bb^T=Q.
$$

Consequently $P^2=I-2Q+Q^2=I-Q=P$. It is the [orthogonal projection matrix](../../../../../../orthogonal-projection-matrix.md) onto the [orthogonal complement](../../../../../../orthogonal-complement.md) of the [linear span](../../../../../../linear-span.md) of $a,b$.
