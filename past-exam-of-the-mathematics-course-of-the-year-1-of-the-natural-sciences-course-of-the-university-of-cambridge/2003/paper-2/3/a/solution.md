↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The unique [symmetric and antisymmetric parts of a matrix](../../../../../symmetric-and-antisymmetric-parts-of-a-matrix.md) are $S=(M+M^T)/2$ and $A=(M-M^T)/2$, giving

$$
\boxed{S=\frac12\begin{pmatrix}0&a+b\\a+b&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad A=\frac12\begin{pmatrix}0&b-a\\a-b&0\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

Their [matrix products](../../../../../matrix-product.md) with their [transposes](../../../../../transpose.md) are $S^TS=(a+b)^2I/4$ and $A^TA=(a-b)^2I/4$. Each is an [orthogonal matrix](../../../../../orthogonal-matrix.md) precisely when the corresponding factor is one. Thus $a+b=\pm2$ and $a-b=\pm2$, with independent sign choices, and the simultaneous possibilities are

$$
\boxed{(a,b)=(2,0),\ (-2,0),\ (0,2),\ (0,-2).}
$$
