↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [survival function](../../../../../../survival-function.md) of this [exponential distribution](../../../../../../exponential-distribution.md) is $\mathbb P(X\geq t)=e^{-kt}$ for $t\geq0$. Since $\{X\geq3\}\subseteq\{X\geq1\}$, the [conditional probability](../../../../../../conditional-probability.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(X\geq3\mid X\geq1)=\frac{e^{-3k}}{e^{-k}}=e^{-2k}.}
$$

This is the [memoryless property](../../../../../../memorylessness-of-the-exponential-distribution.md): after surviving the first unit, surviving two additional units has the same [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) as it did initially.
