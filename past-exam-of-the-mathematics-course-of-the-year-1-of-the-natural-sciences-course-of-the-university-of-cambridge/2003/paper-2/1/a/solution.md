<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

With [normal vector](../../../../../normal-vector.md) $n=(-1,-2,2)$, a [plane](../../../../../plane.md) through $A=(2,-1,1)$ has equation $n\cdot(r-A)=0$. As $n\cdot A=2$, its [plane equation](../../../../../cartesian-equation-of-a-plane.md) is

$$
\boxed{-x-2y+2z=2.}
$$

The [distance from a point to a plane](../../../../../distance-from-a-point-to-a-plane.md) is the absolute signed offset divided by the [Euclidean norm](../../../../../euclidean-norm.md) of its [normal vector](../../../../../normal-vector.md). Hence the [Euclidean distance](../../../../../euclidean-distance.md) from the origin is $\boxed{2/3}$, since $|n|=3$. The closest point is $2n/9=(-2,-4,4)/9$, whose [Euclidean distance](../../../../../euclidean-distance.md) from the origin is $2/3$ and which satisfies the [plane equation](../../../../../cartesian-equation-of-a-plane.md).
