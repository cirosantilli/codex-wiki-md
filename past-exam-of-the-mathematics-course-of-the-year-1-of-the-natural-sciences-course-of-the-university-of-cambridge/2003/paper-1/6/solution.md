<h1 id="6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

For a nonzero separated solution $U=X(x)T(t)$, [separation of variables](../../../../separation-of-variables.md) gives $X''/X=T'/T=-\lambda$. The zero [Dirichlet boundary conditions](../../../../dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) [force](../../../../force-split.md) $X=\sin nx$, $\lambda=n^2$, with [integers](../../../../integer-split.md) $n\geq1$; the time factors are $e^{-n^2t}$. Expanding the initial profile in this [Fourier sine series](../../../../fourier-sine-series.md) requires only the two already displayed spatial modes. Thus

$$
\boxed{U(x,t)=3e^{-t}\sin x-e^{-9t}\sin3x.}
$$

Both terms individually solve the [heat equation](../../../../heat-equation.md), vanish at the endpoints and give the required initial profile at $t=0$. The initial function is also $4\sin^3x\geq0$ on the bar; the later solution stays nonnegative and tends to zero as [heat](../../../../heat-split.md) leaves through the fixed-temperature ends.
