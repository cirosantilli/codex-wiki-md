<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The Gaussian decay makes the [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md) [absolutely convergent](../../../../../absolute-convergence.md), so [odd](../../../../../odd-function.md) symmetry gives $\boxed{\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f(x)dx=0}$. On the positive half-line substitute $u=3x^2/2$, so $x^3dx=2u\,du/9$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\int_0^\infty f(x)dx=\frac2{27}\int_0^\infty ue^{-u}du=\frac2{27}.}
$$

The last [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md) is $\Gamma(2)=1$, an instance of the [odd Gaussian moments on a half-line](../../../../../odd-gaussian-moments-on-a-half-line.md).
