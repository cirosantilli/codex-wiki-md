<h1 id="2/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Write $R(x)=(x^2+3)(2x^2-1)/(3x^4-x^3-1)$. This [rational function](../../../../../../rational-function.md) is [continuous](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) at both finite points because its denominator is nonzero there. Substitution gives the first two [limits](../../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md); at infinity divide numerator and denominator by $x^4$ and use their leading coefficients. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\lim_{x\to0}R(x)=3,\qquad\lim_{x\to1}R(x)=4,\qquad\lim_{x\to\infty}R(x)=\frac23.}
$$
