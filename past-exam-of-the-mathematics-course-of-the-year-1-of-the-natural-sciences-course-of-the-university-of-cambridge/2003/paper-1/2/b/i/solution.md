<h1 id="2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The numerator and denominator both vanish at zero. The [fundamental theorem of calculus](../../../../../../fundamental-theorem-of-calculus.md) differentiates the numerator, while the denominator [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) is nonzero near zero. [L'Hôpital's rule](../../../../../../l-hopital-s-rule.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\lim_{x\to0}\frac{\int_0^xe^{-y^2}dy}{3x-x^3}=\lim_{x\to0}\frac{e^{-x^2}}{3-3x^2}=\frac13.}
$$
