<h1 id="8/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For $F(x)=e^{e^x}$, the [chain rule](../../../../../../chain-rule.md) gives $F'=e^xe^{e^x}$ and $F''=(e^x+e^{2x})e^{e^x}$. Thus $F(0)=e$, $F'(0)=e$ and $F''(0)=2e$, yielding

$$
\boxed{e^{e^x}=e+ex+ex^2+O(x^3).}
$$
