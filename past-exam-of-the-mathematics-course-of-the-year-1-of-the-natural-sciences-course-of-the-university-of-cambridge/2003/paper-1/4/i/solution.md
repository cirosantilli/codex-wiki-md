<h1 id="4/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Put $u=\ln x$, so $du=dx/x$. The [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md) becomes $\int du/u$, giving

$$
\boxed{\int\frac{dx}{x\ln x}=\ln|\ln x|+C.}
$$

For the real [logarithm](../../../../../logarithm.md), choose an interval in $0<x<1$ or $x>1$; the original [integrand](../../../../../integrand.md) is singular at one and undefined for nonpositive $x$.
