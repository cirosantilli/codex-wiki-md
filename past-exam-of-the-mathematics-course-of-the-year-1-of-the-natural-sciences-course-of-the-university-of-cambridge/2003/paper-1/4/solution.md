<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

Use [integration by parts](../../../../integration-by-parts.md) with $u=\ln(1+x^2)$ and $dv=(1+3x^2)dx$, giving $v=x+x^3=x(1+x^2)$. The remaining [integrand](../../../../integrand.md) simplifies because $du=2x\,dx/(1+x^2)$:

$$
\boxed{\int(1+3x^2)\ln(1+x^2)dx=x(1+x^2)\ln(1+x^2)-\int2x^2dx=x(1+x^2)\ln(1+x^2)-\frac23x^3+C.}
$$

The real formula is valid for every [real number](../../../../real-number.md) $x$.
