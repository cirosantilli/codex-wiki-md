<h1 id="7/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Parametrize the line by $\mathbf r(t)=(t,t,t)$, $0\leq t\leq1$. Its [tangent vector](../../../../../tangent-vector-split.md) is $(1,1,1)$. On this line the three [force](../../../../../force-split.md) components sum to $\mu t+(1-\lambda)t+(\lambda-1-\mu)t=0$. Therefore the [line integral](../../../../../line-integral.md) is $\boxed{\int\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r=0}$ for every parameter choice.
