<h1 id="7/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The field is smooth on the [simply connected](../../../../../simply-connected-space.md) domain $\mathbb R^3$. Its [curl](../../../../../curl.md) is $\nabla\times\mathbf F=(2\lambda-2,2\mu,0)$, so a necessary and sufficient condition for a [conservative vector field](../../../../../conservative-vector-field.md) is

$$
\boxed{\lambda=1,\qquad\mu=0.}
$$

At those values it is explicitly the [gradient](../../../../../gradient.md) of $\Phi=xy-x^2/2-yz+z^2/2$. Equality of the two previous path [integrals](../../../../../integral-split.md) would impose only $\lambda-\mu=1$, which is weaker: [coinciding path integrals need not imply a conservative field](../../../../../coinciding-path-integrals-need-not-imply-a-conservative-field.md). For example $\lambda=2,\mu=1$ makes those two [integrals](../../../../../integral-split.md) equal but leaves nonzero curl.
