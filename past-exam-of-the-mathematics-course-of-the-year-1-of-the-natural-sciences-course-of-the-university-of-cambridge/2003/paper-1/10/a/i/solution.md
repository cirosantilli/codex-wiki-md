<h1 id="10/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $n\geq6$ and $n-1<x<n$, monotonicity gives $(x+1)^{-2}<n^{-2}<x^{-2}$. Integrating over each unit interval and summing proves the [integral test](../../../../../../integral-test-for-convergence.md) tail bounds

$$
\boxed{\int_5^\infty\frac{dx}{(x+1)^2}=\frac16<\sum_{n=6}^\infty\frac1{n^2}<\int_5^\infty\frac{dx}{x^2}=\frac15.}
$$

The inequalities remain strict because the very first rectangle already has a strictly positive difference from either bounding [integral](../../../../../../integral-split.md).
