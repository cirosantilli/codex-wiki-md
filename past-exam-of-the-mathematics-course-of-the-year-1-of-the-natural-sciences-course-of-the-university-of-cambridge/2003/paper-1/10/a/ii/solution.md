<h1 id="10/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Add the supplied five-term [partial sum](../../../../../../partial-sum.md) to each side of the tail inequality. Since $1/6=600/3600$ and $1/5=720/3600$,

$$
\boxed{\frac{5869}{3600}<\sum_{n=1}^\infty\frac1{n^2}<\frac{5989}{3600}.}
$$

Numerically this is approximately $1.63028<\sum n^{-2}<1.66361$.
