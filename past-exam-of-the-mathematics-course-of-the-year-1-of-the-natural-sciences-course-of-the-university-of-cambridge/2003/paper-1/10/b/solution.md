<h1 id="10/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Apply the [integral bounds for a finite harmonic sum](../../../../../integral-bounds-for-a-finite-harmonic-sum.md) to the omitted terms from thirteen through one hundred:

$$
\ln\frac{101}{13}<\sum_{n=13}^{100}\frac1n<\ln\frac{100}{12}.
$$

Adding the given $H_{12}=86021/27720$ gives $5.15338\ldots<H_{100}<5.22348\ldots$. Both bounds lie between $5.15$ and $5.25$, so every value they permit rounds to the same two [significant figures](../../../../../significant-figures.md):

$$
\boxed{H_{100}=\sum_{n=1}^{100}\frac1n\simeq5.2\quad\text{to two significant figures}.}
$$

The rounding follows from the bounds, without computing all the intervening terms.
