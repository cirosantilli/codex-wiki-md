<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Suppose $r\mathbf A+s\mathbf B+t\mathbf C=0$. Take its [dot product](../../../../../dot-product.md) successively with $\mathbf a,\mathbf b,\mathbf c$. The reciprocal pairings give $r=s=t=0$. Thus $\mathbf A,\mathbf B,\mathbf C$ are [linearly independent](../../../../../linear-independence.md) and therefore noncoplanar in three dimensions.
