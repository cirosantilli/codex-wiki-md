↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

For the numerical [vectors](../../../../vector.md), $\mathbf b\times\mathbf c=(-2,1,-2)$ and $D=(-1,1,0)\cdot(-2,1,-2)=3\ne0$, proving noncoplanarity. The [reciprocal basis](../../../../reciprocal-basis.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathbf A=\frac{(-2,1,-2)}3,\qquad\mathbf B=\frac{(1,1,1)}3,\qquad\mathbf C=\frac{(1,1,-2)}3.}
$$

Reciprocal [dot products](../../../../dot-product.md) extract the coefficients of any [vector](../../../../vector.md) in the original [basis](../../../../basis.md). Here $\mathbf A\cdot\mathbf d=-1/3$, $\mathbf B\cdot\mathbf d=2/3$, and $\mathbf C\cdot\mathbf d=5/3$, so

$$
\boxed{\mathbf d=-\frac13\mathbf a+\frac23\mathbf b+\frac53\mathbf c.}
$$

Substitution in Cartesian components recovers $(2,1,-1)$.
