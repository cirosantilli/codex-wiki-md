<h1 id="11/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Use [integrating factor](../../../../../../integrating-factor.md) $e^{-x^3/3}$ for this [first-order linear differential equation](../../../../../../first-order-linear-differential-equation.md). Then $(ye^{-x^3/3})'=x^2e^{-x^3/3}=-(e^{-x^3/3})'$. Integrating gives

$$
\boxed{y(x)=Ce^{x^3/3}-1.}
$$

This includes the constant particular solution $y=-1$ when $C=0$.
