<h1 id="11/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a nonzero solution, [separation of variables](../../../../../../separation-of-variables.md) gives $dy/y=k\,dx/(1+x^2)$, so $\ln|y|=k\arctan x+C$. Restoring the zero solution by allowing an arbitrary real multiplicative constant gives

$$
\boxed{y(x)=Ce^{k\arctan x},\qquad C\in\mathbb R.}
$$

It is valid for all [real numbers](../../../../../../real-number.md) $x$, including the constant solutions when $k=0$.
