↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The shapes in $MM^T$ are $(3\times n)(n\times3)$, whose inner dimensions always match. Hence **every [positive integer](../../../../../../positive-integer.md) $n$ is allowed**, and the resulting [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) is $3\times3$.
