↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

A two-sided [matrix inverse](../../../../../../matrix-inverse-split.md) requires a [square matrix](../../../../../../square-matrix.md), so $\boxed{n=3}$ is necessary. It exists only if that [square matrix](../../../../../../square-matrix.md) is also an [invertible matrix](../../../../../../invertible-matrix.md), equivalently $\det M\ne0$; choosing the dimension alone does not ensure invertibility.
