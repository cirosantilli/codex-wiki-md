<h1 id="5/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Use $A=M$ and $B=N$ from part (a). The proposed unreversed product is

$$
A^{-1}B^{-1}=\begin{pmatrix}-19&15\\14&-11\end{pmatrix},
$$

which differs from the actual inverse $\begin{pmatrix}-25&18\\7&-5\end{pmatrix}$. Both original [matrices](../../../../../../matrix.md) are [invertible matrices](../../../../../../invertible-matrix.md), so this is a valid counterexample. The universally valid order is $\boxed{(AB)^{-1}=B^{-1}A^{-1}}$.
