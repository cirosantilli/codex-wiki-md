<h1 id="5/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Take $A=B=I_2$, which are [invertible matrices](../../../../../../invertible-matrix.md). Then $\det(A+B)=\det(2I_2)=4$, whereas $\det A+\det B=2$. This proves that the [determinant is not additive](../../../../../../determinant-is-not-additive.md). Being a [multilinear map](../../../../../../multilinear-map.md) of separate columns does not imply additivity in the entire [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md).
