↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Again take $A=B=I_2$. Their sum is also invertible, but $(A+B)^{-1}=I_2/2$, while $A^{-1}+B^{-1}=2I_2$. Thus [matrix inversion is not additive](../../../../../../matrix-inversion-is-not-additive.md), [even](../../../../../../even-function.md) when every inverse in the proposed identity exists.
