<h1 id="9/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Two applications of [integration by parts](../../../../../integration-by-parts.md) give $I=\cosh x\sin x-\int\sinh x\sin x\,dx$ and $\int\sinh x\sin x\,dx=-\sinh x\cos x+I$. Thus an [antiderivative](../../../../../antiderivative.md) is $[\cosh x\sin x+\sinh x\cos x]/2$. Evaluating its endpoints gives

$$
\boxed{\int_0^{\pi/2}\cosh x\cos x\,dx=\frac12\cosh\frac\pi2.}
$$
