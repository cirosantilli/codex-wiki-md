<h1 id="9/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Adding and subtracting the [exponential function](../../../../../exponential-function.md) [power series](../../../../../power-series.md) cancels the appropriate [odd](../../../../../odd-function.md) or [even](../../../../../even-function.md) terms:

$$
\boxed{\sinh x=\sum_{n=0}^\infty\frac{x^{2n+1}}{(2n+1)!}=x+\frac{x^3}{3!}+\cdots,\qquad\cosh x=\sum_{n=0}^\infty\frac{x^{2n}}{(2n)!}=1+\frac{x^2}{2!}+\cdots.}
$$

Both series converge absolutely for every [complex number](../../../../../complex-number.md) $x$, as follows from the [ratio test](../../../../../ratio-test.md).
