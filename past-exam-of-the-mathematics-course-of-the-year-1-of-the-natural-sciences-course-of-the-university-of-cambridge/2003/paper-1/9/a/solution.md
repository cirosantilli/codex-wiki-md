<h1 id="9/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [exponential function](../../../../../exponential-function.md) definitions of the [hyperbolic functions](../../../../../hyperbolic-function.md) and [Euler's formula](../../../../../euler-s-formula.md) give

$$
\sinh(ix)=\frac{e^{ix}-e^{-ix}}2=i\sin x,\qquad\cosh(ix)=\frac{e^{ix}+e^{-ix}}2=\cos x.
$$

Dividing these identities wherever $\cos x\ne0$ gives $\boxed{\tanh(ix)=i\tan x}$. The two undivided identities hold for every [complex number](../../../../../complex-number.md) $x$; the quotient identity excludes its poles.
