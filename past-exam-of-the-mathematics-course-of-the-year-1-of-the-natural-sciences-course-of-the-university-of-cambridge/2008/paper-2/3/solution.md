<h1 id="3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

The denominator $D=x^2+xy+y^2=(x+y/2)^2+3y^2/4$ is positive away from the origin. The [partial derivatives](../../../../partial-derivative.md) are

$$
f_x=\frac{y(y^2-x^2)}{D^2},\qquad f_y=\frac{x(x^2-y^2)}{D^2}.
$$

They both vanish exactly on

$$
\boxed{y=x\quad\text{and}\quad y=-x,\qquad(x,y)\ne(0,0).}
$$

These are lines of [stationary points](../../../../stationary-point.md), with values $1/3$ and $-1$, respectively.
