<h1 id="8/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Within this polynomial family, $u(0,0)=0$ already holds and $u(1,0)=1+\beta=0$ gives $\beta=-1$. Thus one solution is

$$
\boxed{u(x,t)=x^2-x+t.}
$$

Two specified point values alone do not give uniqueness among all solutions of the [heat equation](../../../../../heat-equation.md).
