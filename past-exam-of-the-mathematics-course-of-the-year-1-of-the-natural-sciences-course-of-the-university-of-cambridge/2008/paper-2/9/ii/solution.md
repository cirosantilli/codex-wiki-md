<h1 id="9/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Conditioned on being inside the [circle](../../../../../circle.md), the point is a [uniform random point in a disk](../../../../../uniform-random-point-in-a-disk.md) of radius $1/2$. The annulus from $r$ to $r+dr$ has area $2\pi r\,dr+O(dr^2)$. Dividing by the disk's area gives

$$
\boxed{P(r<R<r+dr\mid R<1/2)=8r\,dr+O(dr^2),\qquad0\leq r<1/2.}
$$

The radial [probability density function](../../../../../probability-density-function.md) is $8r$ on $[0,1/2]$ and integrates to one. Without conditioning, the corresponding annulus probability for a point in the original square is $2\pi r\,dr+O(dr^2)$.
