<h1 id="9/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a [uniform distribution](../../../../../continuous-uniform-distribution.md) on the unit square, probability is the ratio of [areas](../../../../../surface-area.md). The [incircle](../../../../../incircle-of-a-triangle.md) has radius $1/2$, so

$$
\boxed{P(\text{inside circle})=\frac{\pi(1/2)^2}{1}=\frac\pi4.}
$$
