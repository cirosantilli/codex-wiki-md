<h1 id="15/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Integrating the first component in $x$ gives $\phi=x^3y+h(y,z)$. Matching the second requires $h_y=-2yz^2$, so $h=-y^2z^2+k(z)$. Matching the third gives $k'=0$. Thus the field is [conservative](../../../../../../conservative-vector-field.md), with [scalar potential](../../../../../../scalar-potential.md)

$$
\boxed{\phi=x^3y-y^2z^2+C.}
$$

Direct differentiation verifies $\boldsymbol F_1=\nabla\phi$ on all of $\mathbb R^3$.
