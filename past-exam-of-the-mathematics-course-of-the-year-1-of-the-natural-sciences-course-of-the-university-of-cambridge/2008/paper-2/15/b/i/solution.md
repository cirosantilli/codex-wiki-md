↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

On the first segment, $x=z=0$, so both [line integrals](../../../../../../line-integral.md) vanish. On the second, $y=1,z=0$, giving contributions $\int_0^23x^2dx=8$ and $\int_0^22x\,dx=4$. On the last, $x=2,y=1$, the contributions are $\int_0^3-2z\,dz=-9$ and $\int_0^3-6z^2dz=-54$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\int\boldsymbol F_1\cdot d\boldsymbol r=-1,\qquad\int\boldsymbol F_2\cdot d\boldsymbol r=-50.}
$$

The first also equals $\phi(B)-\phi(A)=8-9=-1$, as the [fundamental theorem for line integrals](../../../../../../fundamental-theorem-for-line-integrals.md) predicts.
