<h1 id="15/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [parametric curve](../../../../../../parametric-curve.md) runs from $t=0$ to $t=1$, with $\boldsymbol r'=(2,3t^2,6t)$. Substitution into the two [line integrals](../../../../../../line-integral.md) gives

$$
\boldsymbol F_1(\boldsymbol r(t))\cdot\boldsymbol r'=48t^5-90t^9,\qquad
\boldsymbol F_2(\boldsymbol r(t))\cdot\boldsymbol r'=20t^4-351t^6.
$$

Hence

$$
\boxed{\int\boldsymbol F_1\cdot d\boldsymbol r=-1,\qquad\int\boldsymbol F_2\cdot d\boldsymbol r=-\frac{323}7.}
$$

The first is unchanged because the field is [conservative](../../../../../../conservative-vector-field.md). The two different answers for the second exhibit its path dependence, consistent with its nonzero [curl](../../../../../../curl.md).
