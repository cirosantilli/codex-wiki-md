<h1 id="17/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Every harmonic in this [Fourier series](../../../../../fourier-series-split.md) has period $2L$. Since the first-harmonic coefficient is nonzero, the fundamental [period](../../../../../period-of-a-function.md) cannot be smaller: translating by a period $T$ must multiply that nonzero complex coefficient by $e^{i\pi T/L}=1$. Thus, taking $L>0$,

$$
\boxed{T=2L.}
$$
