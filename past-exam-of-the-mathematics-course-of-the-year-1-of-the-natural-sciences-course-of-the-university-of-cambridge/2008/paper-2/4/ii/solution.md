<h1 id="4/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [product rule for divergence](../../../../../product-rule-for-divergence.md) gives $\nabla\cdot(r^2\boldsymbol r)=\nabla(r^2)\cdot\boldsymbol r+r^2\nabla\cdot\boldsymbol r$. Since $\nabla\cdot\boldsymbol r=3$, the result is

$$
\boxed{\nabla\cdot(r^2\boldsymbol r)=5r^2.}
$$
