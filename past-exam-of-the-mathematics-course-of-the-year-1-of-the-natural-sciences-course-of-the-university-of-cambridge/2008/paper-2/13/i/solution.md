<h1 id="13/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [characteristic equation](../../../../../characteristic-equation-of-a-constant-coefficient-differential-equation.md) $(r-3)(r-4)=0$ gives complementary terms $Ce^{3x}+De^{4x}$. A linear [particular solution](../../../../../particular-solution.md) is $12x+7$, since its substitution gives $144x$. The initial conditions give $C+D=-7$ and $3C+4D=-12$, hence

$$
\boxed{y=12x+7-16e^{3x}+9e^{4x}.}
$$
