<h1 id="13/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The complementary [characteristic roots](../../../../../characteristic-root-of-a-constant-coefficient-differential-equation.md) are $-1,-2$. Substituting $A\sin x+B\cos x$ into the differential operator gives $(A-3B)\sin x+(3A+B)\cos x$, so a [particular solution](../../../../../particular-solution.md) is $\sin x-3\cos x$. The initial conditions then require $C+D=3$ and $C+2D=1$, giving

$$
\boxed{y=\sin x-3\cos x+5e^{-x}-2e^{-2x}.}
$$
