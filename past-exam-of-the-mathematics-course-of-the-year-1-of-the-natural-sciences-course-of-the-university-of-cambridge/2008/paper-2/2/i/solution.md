<h1 id="2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) of $\tan x$ is $1/\cos^2x$. The [fundamental theorem of calculus](../../../../../fundamental-theorem-of-calculus.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\int_0^{\pi/4}\frac{dx}{\cos^2x}=\tan(\pi/4)-\tan0=1.}
$$
