<h1 id="16/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [permutation matrix](../../../../../../permutation-matrix.md) acts by $A(x,y,z)^T=(y,z,x)^T$. Applying this cyclic permutation three times returns every [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) to itself, proving $A^3=I$. Explicitly $A^2=\begin{pmatrix}0&0&1\\1&0&0\\0&1&0\end{pmatrix}$ and multiplication once more gives the [identity matrix](../../../../../../identity-matrix.md).
