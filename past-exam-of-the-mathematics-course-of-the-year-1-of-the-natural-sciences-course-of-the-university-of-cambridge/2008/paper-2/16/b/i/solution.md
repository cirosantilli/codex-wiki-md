<h1 id="16/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

With $\nu=0$, the [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) factors into $(1-\lambda)[\lambda^2-\lambda-(\mu^2+4)]$, up to the overall sign convention. The [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are $1$ and $(1\pm\sqrt{17+4\mu^2})/2$. Integer values require an odd integer $m$ satisfying $(m-2\mu)(m+2\mu)=17$. The positive integer factorization $17=1\cdot17$ forces $m=9$ and

$$
\boxed{\mu=4.}
$$

The resulting [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) and corresponding nonzero [eigenvectors](../../../../../../eigenvector.md) are

$$
\boxed{\begin{array}{c|c}1&(2,0,-1)^T\\5&(1,2,2)^T\\-4&(2,-5,4)^T\end{array}.}
$$

Any nonzero scalar multiple is equally valid. The factorization also shows that no other positive integer $\mu$ works.
