<h1 id="16/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $\nu=5$, $B$ is real symmetric. [Sylvester's criterion](../../../../../../sylvester-s-criterion.md) says that all [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are positive exactly when its leading principal [matrix minors](../../../../../../minor-linear-algebra.md) are positive. These are $1$, $5-4=1$, and $\det B=1-\mu^2$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{-1<\mu<1.}
$$

At either endpoint one [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) is zero, so the strict positivity requirement excludes it.
