<h1 id="11/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Every set of $S$ students is equally likely to be the specified college. There are $\binom NS$ choices in total and $\binom LS$ choices consisting entirely of the specified group. Therefore the [hypergeometric distribution](../../../../../../hypergeometric-distribution.md) gives

$$
\boxed{P(\text{all lazy})=\frac{\binom LS}{\binom NS}.}
$$
