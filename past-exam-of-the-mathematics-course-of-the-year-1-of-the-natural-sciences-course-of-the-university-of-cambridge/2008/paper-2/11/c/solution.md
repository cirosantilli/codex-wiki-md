<h1 id="11/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The lecture attendances are independent [Bernoulli trials](../../../../../bernoulli-trial.md) with success probability $f$. Their count has the [binomial distribution](../../../../../binomial-distribution.md):

$$
\boxed{P(k\mid K,f)=\binom Kk f^k(1-f)^{K-k},\qquad0\leq k\leq K.}
$$
