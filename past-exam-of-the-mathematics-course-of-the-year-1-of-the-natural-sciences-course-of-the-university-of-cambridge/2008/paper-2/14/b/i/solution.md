<h1 id="14/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Differentiate the equation for $f$ with respect to $x$, commuting mixed [partial derivatives](../../../../../../partial-derivative.md) for a sufficiently smooth solution. With $y=f_x$ this gives

$$
f_{xt}+f_xf_{xx}=kf_{xxx},\qquad\boxed{y_t+yy_x=ky_{xx}.}
$$

This is the [viscous Burgers equation](../../../../../../viscous-burgers-equation.md) when $k>0$.
