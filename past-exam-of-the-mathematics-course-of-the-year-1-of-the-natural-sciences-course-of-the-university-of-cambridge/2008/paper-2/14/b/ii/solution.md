<h1 id="14/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $k\ne0$, the [chain rule](../../../../../../chain-rule.md) gives $g_t=-f_tg/(2k)$ and $g_{xx}=(-f_{xx}/(2k)+f_x^2/(4k^2))g$. Consequently

$$
g_t-kg_{xx}=-\frac g{2k}\left(f_t+\frac12f_x^2-kf_{xx}\right)=0.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{g_t=kg_{xx}.}
$$

This is the potential form of the [Cole-Hopf transformation](../../../../../../cole-hopf-transformation.md), with $y=-2k\,g_x/g$. For $k>0$ it is a forward [diffusion equation](../../../../../../diffusion-equation-split.md); at $k=0$ the exponential transformation is undefined, while negative $k$ gives a backward heat equation.
