<h1 id="14/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [domain of integration](../../../../../domain-of-integration.md) is $0\leq x\leq2$, $0\leq y\leq x^3$. Reversing the order in the [double integral](../../../../../double-integral.md) and then putting $u=x^4+1$ gives

$$
\int_0^2\int_0^{x^3}\sqrt{x^4+1}\,dy\,dx
=\int_0^2x^3\sqrt{x^4+1}\,dx
=\left[\frac{(x^4+1)^{3/2}}6\right]_0^2.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{I=\frac{17\sqrt{17}-1}{6}.}
$$
