<h1 id="6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

The point on the [parabola](../../../../parabola.md) is $(1,2)$. [Implicit differentiation](../../../../implicit-differentiation.md) gives $2y\,dy/dx=4$, so the [tangent line](../../../../tangent-line.md) has slope one there. Its perpendicular [normal line to a plane curve](../../../../normal-line-to-a-plane-curve.md) therefore has slope $-1$. The required line is

$$
\boxed{y-2=-(x-1),\qquad\text{or }y=3-x.}
$$
