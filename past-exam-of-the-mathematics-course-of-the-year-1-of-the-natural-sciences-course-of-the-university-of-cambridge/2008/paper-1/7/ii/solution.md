<h1 id="7/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The domain is $x>0$, and $x\log x\to0$ from below as $x\downarrow0$. Its [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) is $1+\log x$, so it decreases up to $x=e^{-1}$ and then increases, with its unique global [minimum](../../../../../minimum-of-a-subset-of-a-total-order.md) at $(e^{-1},-e^{-1})$. It crosses the horizontal axis at $(1,0)$ and tends to $+\infty$ as $x\to\infty$. Its [second derivative](../../../../../second-derivative.md) is $1/x>0$, so it is strictly [convex](../../../../../convex-function.md). The right panel of the preceding figure shows these features; zero is an excluded endpoint with a finite limiting value, rather than a vertical [asymptote](../../../../../asymptote.md).
