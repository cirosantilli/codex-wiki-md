<h1 id="7/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Write $x/(x-1)=1+1/(x-1)$. The graph is a translated [hyperbola](../../../../../hyperbola.md), with vertical [asymptote](../../../../../asymptote.md) $x=1$ and horizontal [asymptote](../../../../../asymptote.md) $y=1$. It passes through $(0,0)$, lies below $y=1$ for $x<1$ and above it for $x>1$, and has [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) $-1/(x-1)^2<0$ on each component of its domain. The left panel below shows both branches.
