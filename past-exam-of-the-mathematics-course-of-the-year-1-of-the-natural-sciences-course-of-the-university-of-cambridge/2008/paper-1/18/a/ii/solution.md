↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The matrix of [cofactors](../../../../../../cofactor.md) is $\begin{pmatrix}-1&2&-3\\2&0&-2\\-3&-2&3\end{pmatrix}$. Transposing and dividing by the nonzero [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) gives the [inverse matrix](../../../../../../matrix-inverse-split.md)

$$
\boxed{S^{-1}=\begin{pmatrix}1/4&-1/2&3/4\\-1/2&0&1/2\\3/4&1/2&-3/4\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

Multiplication by $S$ verifies the [identity matrix](../../../../../../identity-matrix.md).
