<h1 id="18/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The coefficient [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) has [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md)

$$
\det\begin{pmatrix}2&1&0\\1&3&\mu\\0&1&2\end{pmatrix}=2(6-\mu)-2=10-2\mu.
$$

A homogeneous [linear system](../../../../../system-of-linear-equations.md) has a nonzero solution exactly when this is a [singular matrix](../../../../../singular-matrix.md). Therefore

$$
\boxed{\mu=5.}
$$

At this value all solutions are $(x,y,z)=t(1,-2,1)$, with $t\ne0$ for a nonzero solution.
