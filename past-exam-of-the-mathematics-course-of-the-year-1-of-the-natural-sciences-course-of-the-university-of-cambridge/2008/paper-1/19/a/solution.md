<h1 id="19/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Continuity](../../../../../continuous-function.md) at $x_0$ requires that $f(x_0)$ be defined and that $\lim_{x\to x_0}f(x)=f(x_0)$. In the epsilon-delta definition, for each $\varepsilon>0$ there must be a $\delta>0$ such that $|f(x)-f(x_0)|<\varepsilon$ whenever $|x-x_0|<\delta$ within the domain. [Differentiability](../../../../../differentiability.md) requires the finite two-sided limit

$$
f'(x_0)=\lim_{h\to0}\frac{f(x_0+h)-f(x_0)}h.
$$

In particular, merely having a punctured [limit of a function](../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md) does not imply [continuity](../../../../../continuous-function.md) unless the assigned value agrees with that limit.
