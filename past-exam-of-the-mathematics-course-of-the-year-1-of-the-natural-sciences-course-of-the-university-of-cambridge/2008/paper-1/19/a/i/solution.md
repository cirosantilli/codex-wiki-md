<h1 id="19/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For every $n\geq1$, $|x^n\sin(1/x)|\leq|x|^n\to0$, so the [squeeze theorem](../../../../../../squeeze-theorem.md) gives limit zero. For $n=0$, sequences $x_k=(\pi/2+2k\pi)^{-1}$ and $\widetilde x_k=(3\pi/2+2k\pi)^{-1}$ give function values $1$ and $-1$, so no limit exists. Hence

$$
\boxed{n=1,2,3,4.}
$$

The value assigned at zero when $n=4$ does not affect this punctured [limit](../../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md).
