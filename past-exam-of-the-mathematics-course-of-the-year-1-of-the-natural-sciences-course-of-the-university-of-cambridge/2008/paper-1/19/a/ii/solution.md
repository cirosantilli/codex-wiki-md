<h1 id="19/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $n=1,2,3$ the limit zero equals the assigned value at zero. For $n=4$ the limit is zero but the assigned value is one, giving a [removable discontinuity](../../../../../../removable-discontinuity.md). For $n=0$ there is no limit. Thus [continuity](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) at zero holds precisely for

$$
\boxed{n=1,2,3.}
$$
