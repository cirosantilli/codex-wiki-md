The [gradient](../../../../../../gradient.md) is $\nabla f=(y(1-2x+y),x(1-x+2y))$. At the three specified points it is respectively

$$
\boxed{(0,1/4),\qquad(1/4,-1/4),\qquad(-1/4,0).}
$$

Each [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) is perpendicular to its straight boundary edge and points out of the [triangle](../../../../../../triangle.md), where $f$ increases from its negative interior values. The arrows in the preceding figure mark these directions.
