<h1 id="17/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Inside the [triangle](../../../../../../triangle.md), both $x$ and $y$ are nonzero. Thus $\nabla f=0$ reduces to $1-2x+y=0$ and $1-x+2y=0$, whose unique solution is $(1/3,-1/3)$. At that point

$$
H=\begin{pmatrix}2/3&-1/3\\-1/3&2/3\end{pmatrix},\qquad f=-\frac1{27}.
$$

The [Hessian matrix](../../../../../../hessian-matrix.md) has positive [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $1$ and $1/3$, so this is a strict local [minimum](../../../../../../minimum-of-a-subset-of-a-total-order.md). It is also the minimum on the closed [triangle](../../../../../../triangle.md): $f=0$ on the boundary, $f<0$ inside, and there is no other interior [stationary point](../../../../../../stationary-point.md). Therefore

$$
\boxed{(x,y)=(1/3,-1/3),\qquad f_{\min}=-1/27.}
$$
