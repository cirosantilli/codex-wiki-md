<h1 id="17/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The interior values satisfy $-1/27<f<0$ away from the [minimum](../../../../../../minimum-of-a-subset-of-a-total-order.md). The [contours](../../../../../../complex-integration-contour.md) are closed curves surrounding $(1/3,-1/3)$, approximately [ellipses](../../../../../../ellipse.md) near that point, and approach the three edges as the level tends to zero. To see their shape globally, put $u=x$, $v=-y$, $w=1-x+y$. These are positive inside and satisfy $u+v+w=1$, while $f=-uvw$. Since $\log(uvw)=\log u+\log v+\log w$ is strictly [concave](../../../../../../concave-function.md), its nonempty superlevel sets are convex and surround the unique maximum of $uvw$. Thus the negative contours nest without additional components, as in the figure.
