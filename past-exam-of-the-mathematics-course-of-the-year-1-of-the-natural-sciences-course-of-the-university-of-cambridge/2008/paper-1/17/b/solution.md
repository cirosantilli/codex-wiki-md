<h1 id="17/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [gradient](../../../../../gradient.md) is $(-y^2-2x-3x^2,2y(1-x))$. Its second component vanishes only if $y=0$ or $x=1$. The latter gives the impossible first-component equation $-y^2-5=0$. With $y=0$, the first component gives $x=0$ or $x=-2/3$. The [Hessian matrix](../../../../../hessian-matrix.md) is

$$
H_g=\begin{pmatrix}-2-6x&-2y\\-2y&2(1-x)\end{pmatrix}.
$$

At $(0,0)$ it has [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $-2,2$, giving a **saddle point**. At $(-2/3,0)$ its [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are $2,10/3$, giving a **strict local minimum**, of value $-4/27$. These are all real [stationary points](../../../../../stationary-point.md). The minimum is local: for example $g(x,0)\to-\infty$ as $x\to+\infty$.
