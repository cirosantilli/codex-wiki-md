<h1 id="9/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [9](../9.md)

Let $A$ be the top vertex, $O$ the center, and $B,C$ the two other points on the [circle](../../../../circle.md). The diameter bisects the angle at $A$, so the two right [triangles](../../../../triangle.md) obtained by drawing the diameter to the opposite point show that $B,C$ have coordinates $(\mp\sin\theta,-\cos\theta)$ when $O=(0,0)$ and $A=(0,1)$. The chord $BC$ cuts off a lower [circular segment](../../../../circular-segment.md) of central angle $2\theta$. The [triangle](../../../../triangle.md) $ABC$ has area $\sin\theta(1+\cos\theta)$, while the segment has area $\theta-\sin\theta\cos\theta$, by subtracting the central triangle from its [circular sector](../../../../circular-sector.md). Adding gives

$$
\boxed{\text{shaded area}=\theta+\sin\theta,}
$$

with $\theta$ measured in [radians](../../../../radian.md). This is the unit-radius case of the [area enclosed by two chords and a circular arc](../../../../area-enclosed-by-two-chords-and-a-circular-arc.md).
