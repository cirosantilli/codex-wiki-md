<h1 id="15/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The density is the symmetric sum

$$
g(z)=\frac3{\pi^2\sqrt{2\pi}\sigma}\sum_{n\ne0}\frac{e^{-(z-n)^2/(2\sigma^2)}}{n^2}.
$$

For small $\sigma$, the [normal distributions](../../../../../../normal-distribution.md) are narrow and well separated, giving peaks near each nonzero integer. The largest two peaks lie near $-1$ and $1$; those near $\pm2$ have approximately one quarter of their height, and the envelope falls as $1/n^2$. There is no component centered at zero, and the density there is exponentially small. The left panel shows the resulting smooth comb.
