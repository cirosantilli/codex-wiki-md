↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The relation rearranges to $(z_1+z_3)/2=(z_2+z_4)/2$, so the diagonals $AC$ and $BD$ have the same [midpoint](../../../../../midpoint.md). Equivalently $z_2-z_1=z_3-z_4$ and $z_4-z_1=z_3-z_2$: each pair of opposite sides has the same displacement [vector](../../../../../vector.md). Thus the given quadrilateral is a [parallelogram](../../../../../parallelogram.md). The identities also describe degenerate configurations if coincident or collinear vertices are allowed.
