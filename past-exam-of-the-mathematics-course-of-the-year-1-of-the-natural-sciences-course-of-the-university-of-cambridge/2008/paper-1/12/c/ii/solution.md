<h1 id="12/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Writing $z=x+iy$ and comparing [real parts](../../../../../../real-part.md) and [imaginary parts](../../../../../../imaginary-part.md) gives $\sin x\cosh y=\cosh2$ and $\cos x\sinh y=0$. A real solution $y=0$ is impossible since $\cosh2>1$. Thus $\cos x=0$, and positivity forces $\sin x=1$. Consequently $x=\pi/2+2k\pi$ and $\cosh y=\cosh2$, giving

$$
\boxed{z=\frac\pi2+2k\pi\pm2i,\qquad k\in\mathbb Z.}
$$

The right [Argand diagram](../../../../../../complex-plane.md) in the preceding figure shows representative points on the lines $\operatorname{Im}z=\pm2$; each row continues with spacing $2\pi$.
