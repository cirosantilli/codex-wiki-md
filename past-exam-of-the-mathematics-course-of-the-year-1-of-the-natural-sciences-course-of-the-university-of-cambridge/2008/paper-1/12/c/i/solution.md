<h1 id="12/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Multiplying by $z^2-1$ gives $z^6-1=0$. The sixth [roots of unity](../../../../../../root-of-unity.md) $1,-1$ do not solve the original equation, since substituting either gives three. The other four do, because the extra factor is nonzero. Hence

$$
\boxed{z=e^{ik\pi/3},\qquad k=1,2,4,5.}
$$

Their [real parts](../../../../../../real-part.md) are $\pm1/2$ and their [imaginary parts](../../../../../../imaginary-part.md) are $\pm\sqrt3/2$, giving the four corners in the left [Argand diagram](../../../../../../complex-plane.md) below.
