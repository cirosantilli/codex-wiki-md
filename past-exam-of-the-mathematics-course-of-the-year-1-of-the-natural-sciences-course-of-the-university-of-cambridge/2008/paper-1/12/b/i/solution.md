<h1 id="12/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Multiply numerator and denominator by the [complex conjugate](../../../../../../complex-conjugate.md) of $1+z$. For $z\ne-1$ this gives

$$
\frac{1-z}{1+z}=\frac{1-x^2-y^2-2iy}{(1+x)^2+y^2}.
$$

Thus its [real part](../../../../../../real-part.md) and [imaginary part](../../../../../../imaginary-part.md) are respectively

$$
\boxed{\frac{1-x^2-y^2}{(1+x)^2+y^2},\qquad\frac{-2y}{(1+x)^2+y^2}.}
$$
