<h1 id="10/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The favourable outcomes are $(j,j+1)$ and $(j+1,j)$ for $j=1,\ldots,5$. These are ten distinct ordered outcomes among the $36$ equally likely elements of the [sample space](../../../../../sample-space.md), so the [probability](../../../../../probability.md) is

$$
\boxed{\frac{10}{36}=\frac5{18}}.
$$
