<h1 id="13/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Apply [integration by parts](../../../../../integration-by-parts.md) twice. Taking an [antiderivative](../../../../../antiderivative.md) of $e^{x/2}$ to be $2e^{x/2}$ gives

$$
\int x^2e^{x/2}\,dx=2x^2e^{x/2}-4\int xe^{x/2}\,dx,
$$

and the second application gives $\int xe^{x/2}\,dx=2xe^{x/2}-4e^{x/2}+C$. Therefore the required [definite integral](../../../../../definite-integral.md) is

$$
\left[2e^{x/2}(x^2-4x+8)\right]_0^1=\boxed{10\sqrt e-16}.
$$
