<h1 id="13/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The factor $x^2$ is an [even function](../../../../../even-function.md) and $\sin x$ is an [odd function](../../../../../odd-function.md), so their product is an [odd function](../../../../../odd-function.md). Its [definite integral](../../../../../definite-integral.md) over the symmetric [interval](../../../../../interval-mathematics.md) vanishes:

$$
\boxed{0}.
$$

Indeed the [substitution rule](../../../../../integration-by-substitution.md) $x=-t$ changes the integral over $[-\pi/2,0]$ into the negative of the integral over $[0,\pi/2]$.
