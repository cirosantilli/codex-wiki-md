<h1 id="13/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Split the [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md) into

$$
\int\frac{dx}{\sqrt{1+x^2}}+\int\frac{x\,dx}{\sqrt{1+x^2}}.
$$

The first is the standard [antiderivative](../../../../../antiderivative.md) $\operatorname{arsinh}x=\ln(x+\sqrt{1+x^2})$ from the [inverse hyperbolic functions](../../../../../inverse-hyperbolic-functions.md). For the second, the [substitution rule](../../../../../integration-by-substitution.md) $t=1+x^2$ gives $\tfrac12\int t^{-1/2}\,dt=\sqrt{1+x^2}$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\ln(x+\sqrt{1+x^2})+\sqrt{1+x^2}+C.}
$$

The [logarithm](../../../../../logarithm.md)'s argument is positive for every real $x$.
