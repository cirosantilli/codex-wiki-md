<h1 id="7/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Writing $v=y-1$ turns the [first-order linear differential equation](../../../../../first-order-linear-differential-equation.md) into $v'=v$. Its general solution is $v=Ce^x$, so $y=1+Ce^x$. The [initial condition](../../../../../initial-condition.md) gives $C=-1$, hence

$$
y(x)=1-e^x,\qquad \boxed{y(1)=1-e}.
$$
