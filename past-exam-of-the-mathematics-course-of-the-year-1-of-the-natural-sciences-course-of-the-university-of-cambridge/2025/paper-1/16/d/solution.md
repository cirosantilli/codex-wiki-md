<h1 id="16/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [gradient](../../../../../gradient.md) is

$$
\boxed{\nabla f=E\bigl(v(1-u^2),u(1-v^2)\bigr)^T.}
$$

It is perpendicular to each nonzero [level set](../../../../../level-set.md) and points towards increasing $f$. Near the two positive [local maxima](../../../../../local-maximum.md), the [Hessian matrix](../../../../../hessian-matrix.md) is $-2e^{-1}I$, so the [gradient](../../../../../gradient.md) arrows point inward. Near the two negative [local minima](../../../../../local-minimum.md), the [Hessian matrix](../../../../../hessian-matrix.md) is $2e^{-1}I$, so they point outward. These arrows are shown on the figure in part (c).

At the [saddle point](../../../../../saddle-point.md), $\nabla f\approx(v,u)^T$. Along $v=u$, the arrows point away from the centre on both sides; along $v=-u$, they point towards the centre on both sides. On the positive $u$ axis they point up, on the negative $u$ axis down, on the positive $v$ axis right, and on the negative $v$ axis left. This specifies the four intervening sectors of the [gradient](../../../../../gradient.md) pattern.
