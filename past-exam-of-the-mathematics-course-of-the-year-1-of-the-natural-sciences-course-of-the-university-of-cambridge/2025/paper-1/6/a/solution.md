<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Group consecutive terms in pairs. The $j$th pair, for $j=1,\ldots,25$, is

$$
-\frac{4j-3}{2}+\frac{4j-1}{2}=1.
$$

There are $25$ such pairs in this finite [sum](../../../../../sum.md), so the answer is $\boxed{25}$.
