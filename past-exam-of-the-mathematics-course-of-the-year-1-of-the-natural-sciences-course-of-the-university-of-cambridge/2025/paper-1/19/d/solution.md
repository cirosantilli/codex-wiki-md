<h1 id="19/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

A [rational lower bound for the natural logarithm](../../../../../rational-lower-bound-for-the-natural-logarithm.md) proves positivity for every $n$, including $n=1$. Define

$$
h(t)=\ln(1+t)-\frac{2t}{2+t},\qquad t\geq0.
$$

Then $h(0)=0$, while direct [differentiation](../../../../../differentiation.md) gives

$$
h'(t)=\frac1{1+t}-\frac4{(2+t)^2}=\frac{t^2}{(1+t)(2+t)^2}>0\quad(t>0).
$$

Thus $h(t)>0$ for $t>0$. Setting $t=1/n$ in the expression from part (b) gives

$$
u_n=\frac{2+t}{2t}\left[\ln(1+t)-\frac{2t}{2+t}\right]=\frac{2+t}{2t}h(t)>0.
$$

Therefore $\boxed{u_n>0\text{ for every }n\geq1}$, and $(a_n)$ is a strictly increasing [sequence](../../../../../sequence.md).
