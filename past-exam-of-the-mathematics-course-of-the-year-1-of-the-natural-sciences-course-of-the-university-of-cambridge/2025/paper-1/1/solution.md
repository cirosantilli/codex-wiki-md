<h1 id="1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Set $t=2^x>0$. The [exponential function](../../../../exponential-function.md) identity $4^x=(2^x)^2$ reduces the problem to the [quadratic equation](../../../../quadratic-equation.md)

$$
t^2-t-2=(t-2)(t+1)=0.
$$

Positivity excludes $t=-1$, so $2^x=2$. Since the [exponential function](../../../../exponential-function.md) $2^x$ is strictly increasing, the unique answer is $\boxed{x=1}$.
