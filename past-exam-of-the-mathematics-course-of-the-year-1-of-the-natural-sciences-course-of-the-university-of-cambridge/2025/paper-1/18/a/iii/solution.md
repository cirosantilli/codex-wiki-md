<h1 id="18/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Put $D_n=(-1)^ne^\pi-1$. The two [linear equations](../../../../../../linear-equation.md) from parts (i) and (ii) give $I_n=D_n+nJ_n$ and $J_n=-nI_n$, so $(1+n^2)I_n=D_n$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{I_n=\frac{(-1)^ne^\pi-1}{1+n^2},\qquad J_n=\frac{n\left[1-(-1)^ne^\pi\right]}{1+n^2}.}
$$

These formulas include $n=0$, when $I_0=e^\pi-1$ and $J_0=0$.
