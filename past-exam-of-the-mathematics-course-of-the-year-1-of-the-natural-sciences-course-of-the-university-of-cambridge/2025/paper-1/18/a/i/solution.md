↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $I_n$ denote the [integral](../../../../../../integral-split.md) with the [cosine](../../../../../../cosine.md) factor and $J_n$ the one with the [sine](../../../../../../sine.md) factor. In [integration by parts](../../../../../../integration-by-parts.md), take $u=\cos(nx)$ and $dv=e^x\,dx$. Then

$$
I_n=[e^x\cos(nx)]_0^\pi+nJ_n=\boxed{(-1)^ne^\pi-1+nJ_n}.
$$

The integer restriction gives $\cos(n\pi)=(-1)^n$.
