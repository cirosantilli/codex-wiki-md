<h1 id="18/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

At $x=\pi/2$, all odd-indexed [cosine](../../../../../cosine.md) terms vanish, while $\cos(2k\pi/2)=(-1)^k$. Thus the [Fourier series](../../../../../fourier-series-split.md) from part (c) gives

$$
e^{\pi/2}=\frac{e^\pi-1}{\pi}+\frac{2(e^\pi-1)}\pi\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^k}{4k^2+1}.
$$

Divide by $2(e^\pi-1)/\pi$ and rearrange. The [hyperbolic sine](../../../../../hyperbolic-sine.md) identity $e^\pi-1=2e^{\pi/2}\sinh(\pi/2)$ yields

$$
\boxed{\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^k}{4k^2+1}=\frac{\pi}{4\sinh(\pi/2)}-\frac12,\qquad m=4.}
$$

The evaluation is legitimate by the convergence established in part (c).
