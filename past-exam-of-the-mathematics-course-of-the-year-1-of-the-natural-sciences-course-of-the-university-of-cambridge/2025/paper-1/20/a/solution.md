<h1 id="20/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $G=x^2/a^2+y^2/a^2+z^2/b^2-1$. Its [gradient](../../../../../gradient.md) cannot vanish on the [ellipsoid](../../../../../ellipsoid.md), since the origin is not on the surface. Thus the [Lagrange multiplier](../../../../../lagrange-multiplier.md) equations capture every constrained [stationary point](../../../../../stationary-point.md):

$$
\boxed{4x^3=\frac{2\lambda x}{a^2},\qquad 2yz^2=\frac{2\lambda y}{a^2},\qquad 2y^2z=\frac{2\lambda z}{b^2},\qquad \frac{x^2+y^2}{a^2}+\frac{z^2}{b^2}=1.}
$$

The [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) is a [sum](../../../../../sum.md) of squares, so $T\geq0$. Equality requires $x=0$ and $yz=0$. The surface constraint then gives the four coldest [points](../../../../../point-geometry.md):

$$
\boxed{T_{\min}=0,\qquad (x,y,z)=(0,\pm a,0)\text{ or }(0,0,\pm b).}
$$

These are all such [points](../../../../../point-geometry.md), because a [sum](../../../../../sum.md) of nonnegative terms can vanish only when each term vanishes.
