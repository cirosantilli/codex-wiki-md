<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Since $\mathbf v=-\mathbf u$ and $\mathbf u\ne\mathbf0$, the [vectors](../../../../../vector.md) point in opposite directions. More explicitly, their [dot product](../../../../../dot-product.md) gives

$$
\cos\theta=\frac{\mathbf u\cdot\mathbf v}{\|\mathbf u\|\|\mathbf v\|}=-1.
$$

Hence their [angle](../../../../../angle.md) is $\boxed{\theta=\pi\text{ radians}=180^\circ}$.
