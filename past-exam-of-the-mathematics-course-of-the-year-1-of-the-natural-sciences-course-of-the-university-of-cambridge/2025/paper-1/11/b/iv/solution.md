<h1 id="11/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Use the [distance from a point to a plane](../../../../../../distance-from-a-point-to-a-plane.md) formula with the [unit normal](../../../../../../unit-normal.md) $\hat{\mathbf n}$. Since the [plane](../../../../../../plane.md) passes through the origin, the distance is

$$
\boxed{d=|\mathbf p\cdot\hat{\mathbf n}|=\frac{|2+0|}{\sqrt2}=\sqrt2}.
$$

For a direct check, the [closest point on a plane](../../../../../../closest-point-on-a-plane.md) is $\mathbf p-(\mathbf p\cdot\hat{\mathbf n})\hat{\mathbf n}=(1,\sqrt2,-1)^T$, whose displacement from $\mathbf p$ has [Euclidean norm](../../../../../../euclidean-norm.md) $\sqrt2$.
