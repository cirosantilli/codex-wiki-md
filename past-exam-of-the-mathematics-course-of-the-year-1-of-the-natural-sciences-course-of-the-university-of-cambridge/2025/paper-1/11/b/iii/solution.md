<h1 id="11/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [plane equation](../../../../../../cartesian-equation-of-a-plane.md) $x+z=0$ has [normal vector](../../../../../../normal-vector.md) $(1,0,1)^T=\sqrt2\hat{\mathbf n}$. Thus its [normal vector](../../../../../../normal-vector.md) is parallel to the [cylinder](../../../../../../cylinder-geometry.md)'s axis and the [plane](../../../../../../plane.md) is perpendicular to that axis. It passes through the origin, so the intersection is **a [circle](../../../../../../circle.md) of radius $2$ centred at the origin in that [plane](../../../../../../plane.md)**.

Indeed, with the [orthonormal basis](../../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) $\mathbf e=(1,0,-1)^T/\sqrt2$ and $\mathbf f=(0,1,0)^T$ of the [plane](../../../../../../plane.md), write $\mathbf r=s\mathbf e+t\mathbf f$. The [cylinder](../../../../../../cylinder-geometry.md) equation becomes $s^2+t^2=4$. Equivalently, the intersection has the [parametric equation](../../../../../../parametric-equation.md)

$$
\boxed{\mathbf r(\theta)=2\cos\theta\,\mathbf e+2\sin\theta\,\mathbf f,\quad0\leq\theta<2\pi.}
$$
