<h1 id="11/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [dot product](../../../../../../dot-product.md) with the given [unit vector](../../../../../../unit-vector.md) is $\mathbf r\cdot\hat{\mathbf n}=(x+z)/\sqrt2$. Subtracting the corresponding [orthogonal projection](../../../../../../orthogonal-projection.md) gives

$$
\mathbf r-\hat{\mathbf n}(\mathbf r\cdot\hat{\mathbf n})=\left(\frac{x-z}{2},y,\frac{z-x}{2}\right)^T.
$$

Taking its squared [Euclidean norm](../../../../../../euclidean-norm.md) yields the equivalent [Cartesian](../../../../../../cartesian-coordinate-system.md) equation

$$
\boxed{\frac{(x-z)^2}{2}+y^2=4}.
$$
