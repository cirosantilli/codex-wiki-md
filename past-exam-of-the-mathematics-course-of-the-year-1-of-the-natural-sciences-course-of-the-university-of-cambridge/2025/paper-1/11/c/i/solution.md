<h1 id="11/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $\mathbf d=\mathbf a_2-\mathbf a_1$ and $\mathbf w=\mathbf v_1\times\mathbf v_2$. The assumed [linear independence](../../../../../../linear-independence.md) gives $\mathbf w\ne\mathbf0$. Every displacement joining one [line](../../../../../../straight-line.md) to the other is

$$
\mathbf s=\mathbf d+\lambda_2\mathbf v_2-\lambda_1\mathbf v_1.
$$

Its [dot product](../../../../../../dot-product.md) with $\mathbf w$ is always $\mathbf d\cdot\mathbf w$. The component in that direction is therefore fixed, so [orthogonal decomposition](../../../../../../orthogonal-decomposition-by-a-closed-subspace.md) gives $\|\mathbf s\|\geq|\mathbf d\cdot\mathbf w|/\|\mathbf w\|$.

The two [linearly independent vectors](../../../../../../linear-independence.md) $\mathbf v_1,\mathbf v_2$ span the [plane](../../../../../../plane.md) perpendicular to $\mathbf w$. Suitable $\lambda_1,\lambda_2$ cancel the component of $\mathbf d$ in that [plane](../../../../../../plane.md), attaining the lower bound. Thus the [distance between skew lines](../../../../../../distance-between-skew-lines.md) formula, also valid when the [lines](../../../../../../straight-line.md) intersect, gives

$$
\boxed{d(L_1,L_2)=\frac{|(\mathbf a_2-\mathbf a_1)\cdot(\mathbf v_1\times\mathbf v_2)|}{\|\mathbf v_1\times\mathbf v_2\|}.}
$$
