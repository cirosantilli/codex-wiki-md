<h1 id="11/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The nonzero direction [vectors](../../../../../../vector.md) now have [linear dependence](../../../../../../linear-dependence.md), so the [lines](../../../../../../straight-line.md) are parallel or coincident. Subtract from $\mathbf d=\mathbf a_2-\mathbf a_1$ its [orthogonal projection](../../../../../../orthogonal-projection.md) along $\mathbf v_1$; the remaining component is the shortest joining displacement. The [distance between parallel lines](../../../../../../distance-between-parallel-lines.md) is

$$
\boxed{d(L_1,L_2)=\left\|\mathbf d-\frac{\mathbf d\cdot\mathbf v_1}{\mathbf v_1\cdot\mathbf v_1}\mathbf v_1\right\|=\frac{\|\mathbf d\times\mathbf v_1\|}{\|\mathbf v_1\|}.}
$$

The second expression follows from the relation between the [cross product](../../../../../../cross-product.md) and the perpendicular component. It includes distance zero for coincident [lines](../../../../../../straight-line.md).
