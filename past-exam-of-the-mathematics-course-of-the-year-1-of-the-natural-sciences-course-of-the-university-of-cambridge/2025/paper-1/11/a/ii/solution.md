For example, choose

$$
\boxed{\mathbf u=(-2,1,0)^T,\qquad \mathbf v=(3,0,1)^T.}
$$

Both [vectors](../../../../../../vector.md) satisfy the [plane equation](../../../../../../cartesian-equation-of-a-plane.md). They are [linearly independent vectors](../../../../../../linear-independence.md): in $\alpha\mathbf u+\beta\mathbf v=\mathbf0$, the second coordinate gives $\alpha=0$ and the third gives $\beta=0$.
