**No.** Every [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) in the [plane](../../../../../../plane.md) can be written as

$$
(x,y,z)^T=(-2y+3z,y,z)^T=y(-2,1,0)^T+z(3,0,1)^T.
$$

The two [linearly independent vectors](../../../../../../linear-independence.md) in part (ii) therefore form a [vector space basis](../../../../../../basis.md) for this [vector subspace](../../../../../../vector-subspace.md). Its [dimension](../../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) is $2$, so any three [vectors](../../../../../../vector.md) in it have [linear dependence](../../../../../../linear-dependence.md).
