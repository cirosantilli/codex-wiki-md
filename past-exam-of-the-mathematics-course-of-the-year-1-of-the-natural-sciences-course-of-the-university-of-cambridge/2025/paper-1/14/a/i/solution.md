<h1 id="14/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [probability density function](../../../../../../probability-density-function.md) must have total [integral](../../../../../../integral-split.md) one. Put $h=\pi/6$. The term $ax$ is an [odd function](../../../../../../odd-function.md), so

$$
1=\int_{-h}^{h}(ax+b\cos x)\,dx=2b\sin h=b.
$$

Thus $\boxed{b=1}$.
