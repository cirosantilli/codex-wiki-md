<h1 id="14/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

With $b=1$, nonnegativity of the [probability density function](../../../../../../probability-density-function.md) at both $t$ and $-t$ requires

$$
\cos t\geq |a|t,\qquad 0<t\leq h=\pi/6.
$$

This is equivalent to $|a|\leq\inf_{0<t\leq h}\cos t/t$. The [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md)

$$
\frac d{dt}\left(\frac{\cos t}{t}\right)=-\frac{t\sin t+\cos t}{t^2}<0
$$

shows that this infimum occurs at $t=h$. Therefore the necessary and sufficient restriction is

$$
\boxed{-\frac{3\sqrt3}{\pi}\leq a\leq\frac{3\sqrt3}{\pi}.}
$$

Sufficiency follows from the same decreasing-ratio argument at every interior [point](../../../../../../point-geometry.md). The endpoint values of $a$ are allowed: the [probability density function](../../../../../../probability-density-function.md) merely vanishes at one endpoint.
