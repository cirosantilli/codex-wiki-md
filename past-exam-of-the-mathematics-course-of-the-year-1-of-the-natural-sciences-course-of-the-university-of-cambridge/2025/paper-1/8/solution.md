<h1 id="8/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [8](../8.md)

For the [partial fraction decomposition](../../../../partial-fraction-decomposition.md), multiply by $(x-1)(x-2)$ to obtain the [polynomial identity](../../../../polynomial-identity.md)

$$
x^2+1=a(x-1)(x-2)+b(x-2)+c(x-1).
$$

Comparing the $x^2$ coefficients gives $a=1$. Substitution of $x=1$ gives $2=-b$, while $x=2$ gives $5=c$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{a=1,\qquad b=-2,\qquad c=5.}
$$

The original identity holds for $x\ne1,2$; evaluating the multiplied [polynomial identity](../../../../polynomial-identity.md) at those values is valid.
