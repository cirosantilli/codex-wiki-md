<h1 id="12/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use the [exponential series](../../../../../../exponential-series.md) to obtain the [Maclaurin series](../../../../../../maclaurin-series.md) of the [hyperbolic sine](../../../../../../hyperbolic-sine.md) and [hyperbolic cosine](../../../../../../hyperbolic-cosine.md):

$$
\sinh x=x+\frac{x^3}{6}+O(x^5),\qquad \cosh x=1+\frac{x^2}{2}+O(x^4).
$$

Dividing these [power series](../../../../../../power-series.md) gives the [hyperbolic tangent](../../../../../../hyperbolic-tangent.md) expansion

$$
\tanh x=\left(x+\frac{x^3}{6}+O(x^5)\right)\left(1-\frac{x^2}{2}+O(x^4)\right)=\boxed{x-\frac{x^3}{3}+O(x^5)}.
$$

The displayed remainder uses [Big O notation](../../../../../../big-o-notation.md) as $x\to0$.
