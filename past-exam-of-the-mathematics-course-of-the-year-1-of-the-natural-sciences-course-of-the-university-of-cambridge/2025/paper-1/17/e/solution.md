<h1 id="17/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) is

$$
P=I-\mathbf p\mathbf p^T=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1&0\\-1&0&0\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix},
$$

so

$$
\boxed{P\mathbf x=(-x_2,-x_1,x_3)^T.}
$$

Here $\|\mathbf p\|^2=2$, so $P=I-2\hat{\mathbf p}\hat{\mathbf p}^T$ with $\hat{\mathbf p}=\mathbf p/\sqrt2$. This is a [Householder transformation](../../../../../householder-transformation.md): **reflection in the [plane](../../../../../plane.md) $x_1+x_2=0$**. The component parallel to the [normal vector](../../../../../normal-vector.md) $\mathbf p$ changes sign, while the perpendicular component is fixed.
