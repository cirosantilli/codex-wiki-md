<h1 id="17/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

The second [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) acts by

$$
Q=\begin{pmatrix}0&1&0\\1&0&0\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad Q\mathbf x=(x_2,x_1,x_3)^T.
$$

Applying $P$ from part (e) then gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbf y=PQ\mathbf x=(-x_1,-x_2,x_3)^T,\qquad PQ=\operatorname{diag}(-1,-1,1).}
$$

The [Householder transformation](../../../../../householder-transformation.md) $Q$ reflects in the [plane](../../../../../plane.md) $x_1-x_2=0$. Its [normal vector](../../../../../normal-vector.md) $\mathbf q$ is perpendicular to $\mathbf p$, so the two reflecting [planes](../../../../../plane.md) meet at a right angle along the $x_3$ axis. Their composition is **a rotation through $\pi$ about the $x_3$ axis**, as is also immediate from the displayed [rotation matrix](../../../../../rotation-matrix.md).
