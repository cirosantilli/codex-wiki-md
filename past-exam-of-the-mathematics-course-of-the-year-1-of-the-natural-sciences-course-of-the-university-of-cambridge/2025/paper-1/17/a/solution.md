<h1 id="17/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

By the definition of [matrix multiplication](../../../../../matrix-multiplication.md) and [trace](../../../../../matrix-trace.md), with all indices summed from $1$ to $D$,

$$
\operatorname{tr}(PQR)=\sum_{i,j,k}P_{ij}Q_{jk}R_{ki}.
$$

The entries are scalars, so their multiplication commutes. Reordering them and renaming the summed indices gives

$$
\sum_{i,j,k}Q_{jk}R_{ki}P_{ij}=\sum_{j,k,i}Q_{jk}R_{ki}P_{ij}=\operatorname{tr}(QRP).
$$

Equivalently, in [Einstein notation](../../../../../einstein-notation.md), $P_{ij}Q_{jk}R_{ki}=Q_{jk}R_{ki}P_{ij}$. This proves the [cyclic property of the trace](../../../../../cyclic-property-of-the-trace.md).
