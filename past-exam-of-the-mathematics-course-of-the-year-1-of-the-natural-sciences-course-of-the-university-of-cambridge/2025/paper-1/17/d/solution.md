<h1 id="17/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Using [Einstein notation](../../../../../einstein-notation.md) and the [Kronecker delta](../../../../../kronecker-delta.md),

$$
\operatorname{tr}M=M_{ii}=\delta_{ii}-\hat n_i\hat n_i=3-\|\hat{\mathbf n}\|^2=\boxed{2}.
$$

The [unit vector](../../../../../unit-vector.md) condition is essential in the last step.
