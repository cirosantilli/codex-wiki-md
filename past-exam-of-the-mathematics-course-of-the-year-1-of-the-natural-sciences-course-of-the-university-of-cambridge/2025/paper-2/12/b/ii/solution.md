<h1 id="12/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Success after two draws occurs on the two [mutually exclusive events](../../../../../../disjoint-events.md) $GR$ and $RG$. With $N=g+r$, [sampling without replacement](../../../../../../sampling-without-replacement.md) gives

$$
\mathbb P(GR)+\mathbb P(RG)
=\frac gN\frac r{N-1}+\frac rN\frac g{N-1}
=\boxed{\frac{2gr}{N(N-1)}}.
$$
