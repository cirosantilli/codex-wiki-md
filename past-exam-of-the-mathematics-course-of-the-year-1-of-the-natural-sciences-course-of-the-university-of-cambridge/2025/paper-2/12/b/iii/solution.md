<h1 id="12/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The equality must be a [first return time](../../../../../../first-return-time.md): an order starting $GR$ or $RG$ would already have stopped at draw two. The only successful four-draw orders are therefore $GGRR$ and $RRGG$. Their [probabilities](../../../../../../probability.md) under [sampling without replacement](../../../../../../sampling-without-replacement.md) add to

$$
\boxed{\frac gN\frac{g-1}{N-1}\frac r{N-2}\frac{r-1}{N-3}
+\frac rN\frac{r-1}{N-1}\frac g{N-2}\frac{g-1}{N-3}}
=\boxed{\frac{2g(g-1)r(r-1)}{N(N-1)(N-2)(N-3)}}.
$$

Here $N=g+r$. Counting all balanced four-draw orders would include games that have already stopped.
