<h1 id="12/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write $N=g+r$. Successive [conditional probabilities](../../../../../conditional-probability.md) account for the changing bag under [sampling without replacement](../../../../../sampling-without-replacement.md):

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(GGG)=\frac gN\frac{g-1}{N-1}\frac{g-2}{N-2}}.
$$

On this event no equality of the two colour counts has occurred, so the game's stopping rule does not interrupt these three draws.
