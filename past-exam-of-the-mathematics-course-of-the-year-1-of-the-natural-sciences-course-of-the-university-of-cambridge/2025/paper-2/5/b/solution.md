↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

By the definitions of [matrix multiplication](../../../../../matrix-multiplication.md), [transpose](../../../../../transpose.md) and [trace](../../../../../matrix-trace.md),

$$
\operatorname{tr}(C^TD)
=\sum_i\sum_j(C^T)_{ij}D_{ji}
=\sum_i\sum_jC_{ji}D_{ji}.
$$

Relabelling the summed indices gives

$$
\boxed{C_{ij}D_{ij}=\operatorname{tr}(C^TD)=\operatorname{tr}(CD^T)},
$$

the [Frobenius inner product](../../../../../frobenius-inner-product.md) of these real [matrices](../../../../../matrix.md).
