<h1 id="18/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Choose the unnormalized [eigenvectors](../../../../../../eigenvector.md)

$$
\mathbf u_+=(1,1)^T,\qquad S\mathbf u_+=3\mathbf u_+,
\qquad
\mathbf u_-=(1,-1)^T,\qquad S\mathbf u_-=-\mathbf u_-.
$$

Their [linear combination](../../../../../../linear-combination.md) giving the specified [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) is

$$
\mathbf x=\frac12\mathbf u_++\frac12\mathbf u_-.
$$

Applying the reciprocal [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) of the [inverse matrix](../../../../../../matrix-inverse-split.md) therefore yields

$$
\boxed{\mathbf y=\frac16\mathbf u_+-\frac12\mathbf u_-}.
$$

In [Cartesian coordinates](../../../../../../cartesian-coordinate-system.md), this is $(-1/3,2/3)^T$, and direct [matrix multiplication](../../../../../../matrix-multiplication.md) gives $S\mathbf y=(1,0)^T$.
