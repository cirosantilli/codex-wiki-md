<h1 id="18/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Since every [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) is nonzero, $S$ is an [invertible matrix](../../../../../../invertible-matrix.md). Multiplying $S\mathbf u_i=\lambda_i\mathbf u_i$ by the [inverse matrix](../../../../../../matrix-inverse-split.md) gives

$$
\boxed{S^{-1}\mathbf u_i=\lambda_i^{-1}\mathbf u_i}.
$$

Hence the [eigenvectors](../../../../../../eigenvector.md) are unchanged and the corresponding [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are $1/\lambda_i$.
