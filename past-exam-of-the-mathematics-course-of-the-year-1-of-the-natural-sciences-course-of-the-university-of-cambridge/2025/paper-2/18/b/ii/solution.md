<h1 id="18/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Induction on $p$ gives $S^p\mathbf u_i=\lambda_i^p\mathbf u_i$, including $p=0$. The absolutely convergent [matrix exponential](../../../../../../matrix-exponential.md) series may therefore be applied term by term to each [eigenvector](../../../../../../eigenvector.md):

$$
C\mathbf u_i=\sum_{p=0}^\infty\frac{S^p\mathbf u_i}{p!}
=\left(\sum_{p=0}^\infty\frac{\lambda_i^p}{p!}\right)\mathbf u_i
=e^{\lambda_i}\mathbf u_i.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{\text{eigenvectors }\mathbf u_i,\qquad \text{eigenvalues }e^{\lambda_i}}.
$$

For a repeated [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md), every nonzero [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) in its [eigenspace](../../../../../../eigenspace.md) remains an [eigenvector](../../../../../../eigenvector.md). Because the real [exponential function](../../../../../../exponential-function.md) is [injective](../../../../../../injective-function.md), distinct real [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) of $S$ do not merge under this transformation.
