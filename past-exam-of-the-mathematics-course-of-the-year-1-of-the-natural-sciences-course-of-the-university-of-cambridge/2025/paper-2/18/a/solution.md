<h1 id="18/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [symmetric and antisymmetric parts of a matrix](../../../../../symmetric-and-antisymmetric-parts-of-a-matrix.md) are

$$
\boxed{S=\frac12(M+M^T),\qquad A=\frac12(M-M^T)}.
$$

Indeed $S+A=M$, while the [transpose](../../../../../transpose.md) identities $(M^T)^T=M$ give $S^T=S$ and $A^T=-A$. Thus $S$ is a [symmetric matrix](../../../../../symmetric-matrix.md) and $A$ is an [antisymmetric matrix](../../../../../skew-symmetric-matrix.md). The decomposition is also unique: a [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) that is both symmetric and antisymmetric must equal its own negative and hence vanish.
