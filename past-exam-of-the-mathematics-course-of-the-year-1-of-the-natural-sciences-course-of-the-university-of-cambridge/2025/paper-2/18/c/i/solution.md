<h1 id="18/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The defining condition for an [antisymmetric matrix](../../../../../../skew-symmetric-matrix.md) forces

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}0&a\\-a&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
A(x,y)^T=(ay,-ax)^T.
$$

Its [dot product](../../../../../../dot-product.md) with $(x,y)^T$ is zero, and its [Euclidean norm](../../../../../../euclidean-norm.md) is $|a|\sqrt{x^2+y^2}$. Thus it combines a right-angle [rotation](../../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) with a uniform [dilation](../../../../../../uniform-dilation.md) by $|a|$. More precisely, the [rotation](../../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) is clockwise for $a>0$ and anticlockwise for $a<0$. A direction-independent description is a clockwise right-angle [rotation](../../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) followed by multiplication by the signed scalar $a$.

A second application gives

$$
\boxed{A^2=-a^2I,\qquad A^2\mathbf v=-a^2\mathbf v}.
$$

Geometrically this is a half-turn combined with a [dilation](../../../../../../uniform-dilation.md) by $a^2$. Because $A$ is nonzero, $a\ne0$.
