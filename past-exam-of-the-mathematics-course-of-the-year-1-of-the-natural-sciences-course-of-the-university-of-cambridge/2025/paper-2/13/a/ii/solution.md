<h1 id="13/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [characteristic equation](../../../../../../characteristic-equation-of-a-constant-coefficient-differential-equation.md) of the reduced [homogeneous linear differential equation](../../../../../../homogeneous-linear-differential-equation.md) is $m^2-\beta=0$. Its [polynomial roots](../../../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md) change type with the sign of $\beta$, giving

$$
\boxed{y(t)=
\begin{cases}
C_1e^{\sqrt\beta\,t}+C_2e^{-\sqrt\beta\,t},&\beta>0,\\[3pt]
C_1+C_2t,&\beta=0,\\[3pt]
C_1\cos(\sqrt{-\beta}\,t)+C_2\sin(\sqrt{-\beta}\,t),&\beta<0.
\end{cases}}
$$

For $\beta=0$, the zero [polynomial root](../../../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md) is repeated, so the second independent solution is $t$. For $\beta<0$, the [polynomial roots](../../../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md) are the [complex conjugate](../../../../../../complex-conjugate.md) pair $\pm i\sqrt{-\beta}$, and the displayed [sine](../../../../../../sine.md) and [cosine](../../../../../../cosine.md) give a real [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) of solutions. The constants are arbitrary real numbers. In every case $x(t)=y'(t)-\cos t$ reconstructs the corresponding [general solution](../../../../../../general-solution.md) of the coupled system.
