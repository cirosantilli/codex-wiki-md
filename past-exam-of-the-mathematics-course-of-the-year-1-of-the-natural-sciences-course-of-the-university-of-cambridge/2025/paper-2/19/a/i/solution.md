↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) $f$ is [continuous](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) at $x_0$ if

$$
\boxed{\forall\varepsilon>0\ \exists\delta>0\ \forall x\in\mathbb R:
\quad |x-x_0|<\delta\ \Longrightarrow\ |f(x)-f(x_0)|<\varepsilon}.
$$

The choice of $\delta$ may depend on $\varepsilon$ and $x_0$, but must work for every $x$ in that neighbourhood. This is the [epsilon-delta definition of continuity](../../../../../../epsilon-delta-definition-of-continuity.md).
