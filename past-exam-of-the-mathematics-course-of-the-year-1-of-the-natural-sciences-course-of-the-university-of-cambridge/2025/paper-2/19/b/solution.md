<h1 id="19/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Along the first [sequence](../../../../../sequence.md), $\sin(1/x_n)=\sin(2n\pi+\pi/6)=1/2$, whereas along the second [sequence](../../../../../sequence.md), $\sin(1/x'_n)=\sin(2n\pi)=0$. The outer [sine](../../../../../sine.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{f_0(x_n)=\sin(\pi/2)=1,\qquad f_0(x'_n)=0}.
$$

Both [sequences](../../../../../sequence.md) approach zero through positive values. For arbitrary $a$, the same calculation gives $f_a(x_n)=x_n^a$ and $f_a(x'_n)=0$.
