<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Move the right-hand side to the left. The resulting [homogeneous linear system](../../../../../homogeneous-linear-system.md) has coefficient [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md)

$$
\begin{pmatrix}-2&1\\1&b-2\end{pmatrix}.
$$

A nonzero solution exists exactly when its [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) vanishes:

$$
\boxed{(-2)(b-2)-1=0}.
$$

Here the upper-left entry of the original [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) is $-1$, as verified in the original PDF; that entry is corrupted in the local OCR.
