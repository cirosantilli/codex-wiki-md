<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) condition reduces to $3-2b=0$, giving

$$
\boxed{b=\frac32}.
$$

As a check, the first [linear equation](../../../../../linear-equation.md) gives $y=2x$, and at this value of $b$ the second [linear equation](../../../../../linear-equation.md) is satisfied for every such pair, including nonzero pairs.
