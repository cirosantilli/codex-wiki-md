↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For the [parametric curve](../../../../../../parametric-curve.md), $\mathbf r'(t)=(2t,3t^2)^T$ and

$$
\mathbf F(\mathbf r(t))=(t^6-2t^2,\ 2t^5-t^3)^T.
$$

The [line integral](../../../../../../line-integral.md) is therefore

$$
\begin{aligned}
\int_C\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r
&=\int_0^1\mathbf F(\mathbf r(t))\cdot\mathbf r'(t)\,dt\\
&=\int_0^1(8t^7-3t^5-4t^3)\,dt\\
&=1-\frac12-1=\boxed{-\frac12}.
\end{aligned}
$$
