Applying the [quadratic formula](../../../../../quadratic-formula.md) to the [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) gives the two [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md)

$$
\boxed{\lambda=\frac{3+\sqrt5}{2}\quad\text{or}\quad\lambda=\frac{3-\sqrt5}{2}}.
$$
