<h1 id="9/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

An [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) makes the shifted [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) singular, so its [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) vanishes:

$$
\det\begin{pmatrix}1-\lambda&1\\1&2-\lambda\end{pmatrix}
=(1-\lambda)(2-\lambda)-1.
$$

Thus the [characteristic equation](../../../../../characteristic-equation-of-a-constant-coefficient-differential-equation.md) is

$$
\boxed{\lambda^2-3\lambda+1=0}.
$$
