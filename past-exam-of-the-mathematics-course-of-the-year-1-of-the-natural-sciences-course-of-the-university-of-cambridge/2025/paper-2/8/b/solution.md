<h1 id="8/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

By the Cartesian formula for the [curl](../../../../../curl.md),

$$
\nabla\times\mathbf F
=\left(\partial_yF_z-\partial_zF_y,\ \partial_zF_x-\partial_xF_z,\ \partial_xF_y-\partial_yF_x\right)^T
=\boxed{(0,0,-2xy)^T}.
$$
