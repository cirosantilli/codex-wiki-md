↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

The [product-to-sum formula](../../../../product-to-sum-formula.md) immediately gives the [Fourier series](../../../../fourier-series-split.md):

$$
\boxed{f(x)=\frac12\cos(2x)+\frac12\cos(8x)}.
$$

In the convention $f(x)=a_0/2+\sum_{n\geq1}(a_n\cos nx+b_n\sin nx)$, the only nonzero [Fourier coefficients](../../../../fourier-coefficient.md) are $a_2=a_8=1/2$. This is a finite [trigonometric polynomial](../../../../trigonometric-polynomial.md), and the identity holds at both endpoints as well.
