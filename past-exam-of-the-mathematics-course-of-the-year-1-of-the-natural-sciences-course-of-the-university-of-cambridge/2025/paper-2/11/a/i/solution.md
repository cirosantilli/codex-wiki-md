<h1 id="11/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Using the [complex conjugate](../../../../../../complex-conjugate.md), $b\bar z+\bar bz=2\operatorname{Re}(\bar bz)$. The [locus](../../../../../../locus-mathematics.md) is therefore the [straight line](../../../../../../straight-line.md)

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Re}(\bar bz)=-\frac c2}.
$$

In the [complex plane](../../../../../../complex-plane.md), the quantity $\operatorname{Re}(\bar bz)/|b|$ is the signed [scalar projection](../../../../../../scalar-projection.md) of $z$ in the direction $b$. Thus the [straight line](../../../../../../straight-line.md) is perpendicular to $b$ and passes through $-cb/(2|b|^2)$. This description uses no separation of $z$ into real coordinates.
