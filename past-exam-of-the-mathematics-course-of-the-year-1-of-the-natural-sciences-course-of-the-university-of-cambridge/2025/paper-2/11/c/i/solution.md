<h1 id="11/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Under the given [rotation](../../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md), $\bar z=\bar\alpha\bar w$ and $z\bar z=|\alpha|^2w\bar w=w\bar w$. Collecting terms gives

$$
a w\bar w+b\bar\alpha\bar w+\bar b\alpha w+c=0.
$$

Since $\overline{b\bar\alpha}=\bar b\alpha$, the transformed coefficients are

$$
\boxed{A=a,\qquad B=b\bar\alpha,\qquad C=c}.
$$
