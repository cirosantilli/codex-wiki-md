<h1 id="11/c/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

For the reciprocal [Möbius transformation](../../../../../../mobius-transformation.md), substitute $z=1/w$, $\bar z=1/\bar w$ and multiply by $w\bar w$, which is nonzero on the stipulated domain. This yields

$$
a+bw+\bar b\bar w+cw\bar w=0.
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{A=c,\qquad B=\bar b,\qquad C=a},
\qquad
\boxed{A=0\iff c=0}.
$$

For a nondegenerate original [circle](../../../../../../circle.md), $c=0$ says that it passes through the origin, and its image is a [straight line](../../../../../../straight-line.md). The restriction $w\ne0$ must be retained: multiplying through does not authorize adding that excluded point. Degenerate choices can give a constant or an identically zero equation instead of a [straight line](../../../../../../straight-line.md).
