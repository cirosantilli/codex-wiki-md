↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For the [translation](../../../../../../translation-geometry.md), expand the products of [complex numbers](../../../../../../complex-number.md) and collect the terms in $w\bar w$, $\bar w$, $w$ and the constant:

$$
a(w+\alpha)(\bar w+\bar\alpha)+b(\bar w+\bar\alpha)+\bar b(w+\alpha)+c=0.
$$

It follows that

$$
\boxed{A=a,\qquad B=a\alpha+b,\qquad C=a|\alpha|^2+b\bar\alpha+\bar b\alpha+c}.
$$

The last coefficient is real because $b\bar\alpha+\bar b\alpha=2\operatorname{Re}(\bar b\alpha)$.
