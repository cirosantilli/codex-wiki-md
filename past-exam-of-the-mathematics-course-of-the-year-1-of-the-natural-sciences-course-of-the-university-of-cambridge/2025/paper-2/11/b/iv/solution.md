<h1 id="11/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

There is a single point exactly when the [radius](../../../../../../radius.md) is zero:

$$
\boxed{c=|b|^2,\qquad z=-b}.
$$

Then $|z+b|^2=0$ forces this unique value of the [complex number](../../../../../../complex-number.md) $z$.
