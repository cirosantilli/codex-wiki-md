<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a constant [vector field](../../../../../vector-field.md), the [flux integral](../../../../../flux-integral.md) is its [dot product](../../../../../dot-product.md) with the [vector area](../../../../../vector-area.md). Thus

$$
\Phi=\mathbf F\cdot\mathbf S
=(1,1,-1)\cdot\left(-\frac12,-\frac12,-\frac12\right)
=\boxed{-\frac12}.
$$
