↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The prescribed [orientation](../../../../../orientation-of-a-simplex.md) orders the two edges from $A$ as $\mathbf c-\mathbf a$ followed by $\mathbf b-\mathbf a$. The [vector area](../../../../../vector-area.md) is half their [cross product](../../../../../cross-product.md):

$$
\mathbf S=\frac12(\mathbf c-\mathbf a)\times(\mathbf b-\mathbf a)
=\frac12(-1,0,1)^T\times(-1,1,0)^T
=\boxed{-\frac12(1,1,1)^T}.
$$

The sign agrees with the [right-hand rule](../../../../../right-hand-rule.md) for the traversal $A\to C\to B\to A$.
