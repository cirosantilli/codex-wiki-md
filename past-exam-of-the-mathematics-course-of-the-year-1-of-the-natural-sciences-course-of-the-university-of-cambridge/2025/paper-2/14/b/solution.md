<h1 id="14/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Substitute the [partial derivatives](../../../../../partial-derivative.md) obtained above. The first-order terms cancel exactly:

$$
\begin{aligned}
u_{xx}-\tfrac12u_y-yu_{yy}
&=U_{\xi\xi}+2U_{\xi\eta}+U_{\eta\eta}
-\frac{U_\xi-U_\eta}{2\sqrt y}\\
&\quad-\left(U_{\xi\xi}-2U_{\xi\eta}+U_{\eta\eta}
-\frac{U_\xi-U_\eta}{2\sqrt y}\right)\\
&=4U_{\xi\eta}.
\end{aligned}
$$

The transformed [partial differential equation](../../../../../partial-differential-equation-split.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{U_{\xi\eta}=0,\qquad \xi>\eta}.
$$

In these [characteristic coordinates](../../../../../characteristic-coordinate.md), the mixed [partial derivative](../../../../../partial-derivative.md) is the only surviving term.
