<h1 id="14/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

To distinguish the old and new coordinate descriptions, write $u(x,y)=U(\xi(x,y),\eta(x,y))$. Since $\xi_x=\eta_x=1$, the [multivariable chain rule](../../../../../../multivariable-chain-rule.md) gives

$$
\boxed{u_x=U_\xi+U_\eta}.
$$

Equivalently, the [differential operator](../../../../../../differential-operator.md) is $\partial_x=\partial_\xi+\partial_\eta$.
