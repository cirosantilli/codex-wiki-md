<h1 id="14/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Differentiate $u_y=y^{-1/2}(U_\xi-U_\eta)$, retaining the [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) of the prefactor. The [product rule](../../../../../../product-rule.md) and [multivariable chain rule](../../../../../../multivariable-chain-rule.md) give

$$
u_{yy}=-\frac1{2y^{3/2}}(U_\xi-U_\eta)
+\frac1y(U_{\xi\xi}-2U_{\xi\eta}+U_{\eta\eta}).
$$

Using $y=(\xi-\eta)^2/16$, this becomes

$$
\boxed{u_{yy}=\frac{16}{(\xi-\eta)^2}(U_{\xi\xi}-2U_{\xi\eta}+U_{\eta\eta})
-\frac{32}{(\xi-\eta)^3}(U_\xi-U_\eta)}.
$$

The last term is essential because the coordinate change is nonlinear.
