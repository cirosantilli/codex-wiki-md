<h1 id="14/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Apply $\partial_x=\partial_\xi+\partial_\eta$ once more. The [chain rule](../../../../../../chain-rule.md) and [symmetry of second derivatives](../../../../../../symmetry-of-second-derivatives.md) give

$$
\boxed{u_{xx}=U_{\xi\xi}+2U_{\xi\eta}+U_{\eta\eta}}.
$$

Here and in the next part the second [partial derivatives](../../../../../../partial-derivative.md) are understood with the usual [smoothness](../../../../../../smoothness.md) required for this [partial differential equation](../../../../../../partial-differential-equation-split.md).
