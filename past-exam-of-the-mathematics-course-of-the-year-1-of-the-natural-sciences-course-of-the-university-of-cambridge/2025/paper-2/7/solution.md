<h1 id="7/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [7](../7.md)

Use [spherical coordinates](../../../../spherical-coordinate-system.md) $x=s\sin\theta\cos\phi$, $y=s\sin\theta\sin\phi$, $z=s\cos\theta$. The [density](../../../../density.md) becomes $\rho_0s\sin\theta$, and the [Jacobian determinant](../../../../jacobian-determinant.md) supplies $dV=s^2\sin\theta\,ds\,d\theta\,d\phi$. Therefore the [mass](../../../../mass.md) is the [volume integral](../../../../volume-integral.md)

$$
M=\rho_0\int_0^{2\pi}d\phi\int_0^\pi\sin^2\theta\,d\theta\int_0^1s^3\,ds.
$$

Using $\sin^2\theta=(1-\cos2\theta)/2$, the three [integrals](../../../../integral-split.md) are $2\pi$, $\pi/2$ and $1/4$. Hence

$$
\boxed{M=\frac{\pi^2\rho_0}{4}}.
$$
