<h1 id="17/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Choose

$$
\boxed{f(z)=z},
$$

or add any constant. Then $f'(z)=1$, so the [vector field](../../../../../../vector-field.md) is exactly $\mathbf r$ and necessarily has the same [flux](../../../../../../flux.md) as in part (a). The formula from part (b)(i) confirms this:

$$
2\pi(z_0+1)+\pi z_0(1-z_0^2)
=\pi(2+3z_0-z_0^3).
$$
