<h1 id="6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

The [complementary function](../../../../homogeneous-solution.md) solves the associated [homogeneous linear differential equation](../../../../homogeneous-linear-differential-equation.md). Substituting $y=e^{mx}$ gives the [characteristic equation](../../../../characteristic-equation-of-a-constant-coefficient-differential-equation.md)

$$
m^2+m-6=(m-2)(m+3)=0.
$$

Its distinct [polynomial roots](../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md) give

$$
\boxed{y_{\mathrm c}(x)=C_1e^{2x}+C_2e^{-3x}}.
$$

The forcing affects the [particular solution](../../../../particular-solution.md), which is not needed for the [complementary function](../../../../homogeneous-solution.md).
