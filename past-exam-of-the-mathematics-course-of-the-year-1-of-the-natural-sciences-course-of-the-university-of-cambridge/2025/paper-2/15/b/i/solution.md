Here $y\geq0$, so the [absolute value](../../../../../../absolute-value.md) equals $y$. The [volume integral](../../../../../../volume-integral.md), evaluated in [Cartesian coordinates](../../../../../../cartesian-coordinate-system.md), is

$$
J=\int_0^1\int_0^x\int_0^{xy}xy\,dz\,dy\,dx.
$$

The integrand is constant with respect to $z$, giving

$$
J=\int_0^1\int_0^xx^2y^2\,dy\,dx
=\frac13\int_0^1x^5\,dx
=\boxed{\frac1{18}}.
$$
