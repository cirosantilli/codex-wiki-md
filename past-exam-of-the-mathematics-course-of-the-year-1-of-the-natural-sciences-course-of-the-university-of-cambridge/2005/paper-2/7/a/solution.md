<h1 id="7/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The required [Legendre transform](../../../../../convex-conjugate.md) is the [Helmholtz free energy](../../../../../helmholtz-free-energy.md), $\boxed{F=U-TS}$ up to an additive constant. Its differential is $dF=dU-TdS-SdT=-SdT-pdV$, so $F_T|_V=-S$ and $F_V|_T=-p$. Equality of mixed partial derivatives for a smooth thermodynamic state gives the [Maxwell relation](../../../../../maxwell-relations.md)

$$
\boxed{\left(\frac{\partial S}{\partial V}\right)_T=\left(\frac{\partial p}{\partial T}\right)_V.}
$$

Next use the [total differential](../../../../../total-differential.md) $dS=S_T|_VdT+S_V|_TdV$. Along a fixed-pressure path, divide by $dT$ to get $S_T|_p=S_T|_V+S_V|_T V_T|_p$. Substitute the Maxwell relation to obtain

$$
\boxed{\left(\frac{\partial S}{\partial T}\right)_p-\left(\frac{\partial S}{\partial T}\right)_V=\left(\frac{\partial p}{\partial T}\right)_V\left(\frac{\partial V}{\partial T}\right)_p.}
$$

Multiplying by $T$ gives the usual difference between the [heat capacity at constant pressure](../../../../../heat-capacity-at-constant-pressure.md) and the [heat capacity at constant volume](../../../../../heat-capacity-at-constant-volume.md).
