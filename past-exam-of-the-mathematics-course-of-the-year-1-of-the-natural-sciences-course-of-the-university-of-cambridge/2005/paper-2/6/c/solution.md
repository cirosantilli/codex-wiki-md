<h1 id="6/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [distance from a point to a plane](../../../../../distance-from-a-point-to-a-plane.md) is the absolute residual of the plane equation divided by its normal's length. Here $\mathbf n\cdot\mathbf c=11$ and $|\mathbf n|=3$, so

$$
\boxed{d=\frac{|11-20|}{3}=3.}
$$
