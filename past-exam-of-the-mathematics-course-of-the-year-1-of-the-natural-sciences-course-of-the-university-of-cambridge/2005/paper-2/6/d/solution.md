<h1 id="6/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The center of the [sphere-plane intersection](../../../../../sphere-plane-intersection.md) is the perpendicular projection of the sphere's center onto the plane:

$$
\boxed{\mathbf c_\circ=\mathbf c+\frac{20-\mathbf n\cdot\mathbf c}{|\mathbf n|^2}\mathbf n=(1,2,3)+(1,2,2)=(2,4,5).}
$$

The distance from $\mathbf c$ to the plane is $3<4$, so the intersection is a genuine circle. A [right triangle](../../../../../right-triangle.md) with hypotenuse equal to the sphere radius gives

$$
\boxed{\rho=\sqrt{4^2-3^2}=\sqrt7.}
$$

Its plane is the given plane; displacements within it are perpendicular to $\mathbf n$.
