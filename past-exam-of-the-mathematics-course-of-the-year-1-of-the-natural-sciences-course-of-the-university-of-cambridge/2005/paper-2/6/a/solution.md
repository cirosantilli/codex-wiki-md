The [sphere](../../../../../sphere.md) consists of points whose displacement from its center has fixed norm. Its vector and Cartesian equations are

$$
\boxed{|\mathbf r-(1,2,3)|=4,\qquad(x-1)^2+(y-2)^2+(z-3)^2=16.}
$$

Equivalently, $\mathbf r=(1,2,3)+4\mathbf n$ with $|\mathbf n|=1$.
