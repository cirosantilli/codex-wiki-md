<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The point $1+i$ has [modulus](../../../../../modulus.md) $\sqrt2$ and [argument](../../../../../argument-complex-analysis.md) $\pi/4$ modulo $2\pi$. Taking complex square roots halves the argument and takes the square root of the modulus:

$$
\boxed{|z|=2^{1/4},\qquad \arg z=\frac\pi8+k\pi\pmod{2\pi},\quad k=0,1.}
$$

The two roots are opposite points in the [complex plane](../../../../../complex-plane.md), explicitly $\pm[\sqrt{(\sqrt2+1)/2}+i\sqrt{(\sqrt2-1)/2}]$.
