<h1 id="5/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Use the exponential definitions of [complex sine function](../../../../../complex-sine-function.md) and [complex cosine](../../../../../complex-cosine.md). Multiplying the equation by $2i$ gives $e^{iz}-e^{-iz}=-2(e^{iz}+e^{-iz})$, hence $3e^{iz}+e^{-iz}=0$. Since exponentials never vanish, this is equivalent to $e^{2iz}=-1/3$. All branches of the [complex logarithm](../../../../../complex-logarithm.md) give

$$
2iz=-\ln3+i(\pi+2\pi n),\qquad\boxed{z=\frac\pi2+n\pi+\frac i2\ln3,\quad n\in\mathbb Z.}
$$

The derivation does not divide by $\cos z$, so it introduces no lost zero-denominator cases.
