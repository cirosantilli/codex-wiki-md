<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A homogeneous [linear system](../../../../../system-of-linear-equations.md) has a nonzero solution exactly when its coefficient [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) is singular. Expansion of the [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) gives

$$
\det M=5\alpha^2+3\alpha-8=(\alpha-1)(5\alpha+8).
$$

Thus the allowed values are $\boxed{\alpha=1\text{ or }-8/5}$. Solving the first two equations and checking the third gives

$$
\boxed{\alpha=1:\ x=\frac z5,\ y=-\frac{7z}5;\qquad \alpha=-\frac85:\ x=-5z,\ y=\frac{5z}2.}
$$

In each case $z\ne0$ gives a nonzero solution and spans the one-dimensional [null space](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md); $z=0$ gives only the zero vector.
