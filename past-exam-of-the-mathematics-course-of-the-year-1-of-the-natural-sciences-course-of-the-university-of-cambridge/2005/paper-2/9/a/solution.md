<h1 id="9/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A real [normal matrix](../../../../../normal-matrix.md) satisfies $MM^T=M^TM$. For a [symmetric matrix](../../../../../symmetric-matrix.md), $M^T=M$ and both products equal $M^2$. For an [antisymmetric matrix](../../../../../skew-symmetric-matrix.md), $M^T=-M$ and both equal $-M^2$. For an [orthogonal matrix](../../../../../orthogonal-matrix.md), both products are the identity. Thus **each of the three classes consists of normal matrices**.
