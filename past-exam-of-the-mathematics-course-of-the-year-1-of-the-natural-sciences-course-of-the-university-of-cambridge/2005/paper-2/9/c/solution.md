<h1 id="9/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [dot product](../../../../../dot-product.md) is $\mathbf u_+\cdot\mathbf u_-=-\cos\theta\sin\theta+\sin\theta\cos\theta=0$. Each planar vector is perpendicular to $\mathbf u_3$ because its third component vanishes. Each has norm one, so the three vectors form an [orthonormal eigenbasis](../../../../../orthonormal-eigenbasis.md). This also follows from the [spectral theorem](../../../../../spectral-theorem.md) for the real symmetric coefficient matrix.
