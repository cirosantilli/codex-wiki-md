<h1 id="7/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Separate the even factors from $1000!$:

$$
1000!=\left(1\cdot3\cdots999\right)\left(2\cdot4\cdots1000\right)=999!!\,2^{500}500!.
$$

The [double factorial](../../../../../double-factorial.md) therefore satisfies $\ln(999!!)=\ln(1000!)-500\ln2-\ln(500!)$. Applying the same fit consistently gives

$$
\boxed{\ln(1\cdot3\cdots999)\approx L(1000)-500\ln2-L(500)\simeq2954.2213.}
$$
