<h1 id="10/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Now $c=b$, so the forcing coincides with a homogeneous exponential and requires a [resonant exponential particular solution](../../../../../resonant-exponential-particular-solution.md). With $v=y'-by$ as above, $v'-av=e^{bx}$ and $v(0)=0$ give $v=(e^{ax}-e^{bx})/(a-b)$. Therefore

$$
(e^{-bx}y)'=\frac{e^{(a-b)x}-1}{a-b}.
$$

Integrating from zero yields

$$
\boxed{y(x)=\frac{e^{ax}-e^{bx}-(a-b)xe^{bx}}{(a-b)^2},\qquad a\ne b.}
$$

Its numerator starts at order $x^2$, ensuring both initial conditions. It is also the finite coalescing-rate limit $c\to b$ of the nonresonant [exponential forcing response with zero initial data](../../../../../exponential-forcing-response-with-zero-initial-data.md).
