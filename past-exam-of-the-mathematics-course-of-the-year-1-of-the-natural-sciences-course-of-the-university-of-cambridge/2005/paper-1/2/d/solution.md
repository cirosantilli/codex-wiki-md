<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

**True.** A [symmetric tensor](../../../../../symmetric-tensor.md) and an [antisymmetric tensor](../../../../../antisymmetric-tensor.md) are orthogonal under full index contraction. Renaming the two summed indices gives

$$
A_{ij}B_{ij}=A_{ji}B_{ji}=(-A_{ij})B_{ij}=-A_{ij}B_{ij},
$$

so $\boxed{A_{ij}B_{ij}=0}$. This is a scalar statement with both indices summed, not a claim that every individual entrywise product vanishes.
