<h1 id="11/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Put $u=x^2$, so $x\,dx=du/2$. Then [integration by substitution](../../../../../integration-by-substitution.md) gives

$$
\int_0^\infty(x+x^3)e^{-x^2}dx=\frac12\int_0^\infty(1+u)e^{-u}du=\frac12(1+1)=\boxed{1}.
$$

The last two integrals are elementary exponential moments, or values of the [Gamma function](../../../../../gamma-function.md).
