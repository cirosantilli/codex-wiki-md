<h1 id="3/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Under the independent uniform-birthday model, condition on the first person's birthday. Exactly one of the second person's $365$ equally likely dates matches, so

$$
\boxed{P(\text{these two birthdays match})=\frac1{365}.}
$$

This concerns one selected pair; the [birthday problem](../../../../../../birthday-problem.md) allowing any pair among all guests is a different event.
