<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For [matching one specified birthday](../../../../../../matching-one-specified-birthday.md), each of the $N-1$ other guests avoids the host's date with probability $364/365$. [Independence](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) gives

$$
\boxed{P(\text{at least one matches the host})=1-\left(\frac{364}{365}\right)^{N-1}.}
$$

For this to exceed $1/2$, the negative logarithm of $364/365$ reverses the inequality on division, giving $N-1>\ln(1/2)/\ln(364/365)\simeq252.652$. Thus **the smallest total attendance is $N=254$**, including the host.
