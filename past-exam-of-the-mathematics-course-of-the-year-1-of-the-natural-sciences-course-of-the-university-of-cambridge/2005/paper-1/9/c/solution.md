<h1 id="9/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Rearranging gives $y'=2x(y-1)^2$. First retain the [equilibrium](../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) $y=1$. For every other solution, [separation of variables](../../../../../separation-of-variables.md) gives $\int(y-1)^{-2}dy=\int2x\,dx$, so $-1/(y-1)=x^2+C$. Hence

$$
\boxed{y=1,\qquad\text{or}\qquad y=1-\frac1{x^2+C}.}
$$

A nonconstant solution is defined on any interval where its denominator is nonzero. Dividing by $(y-1)^2$ without retaining the equilibrium would lose one solution.
