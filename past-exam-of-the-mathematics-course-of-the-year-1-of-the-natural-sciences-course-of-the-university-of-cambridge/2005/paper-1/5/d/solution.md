<h1 id="5/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

With [normal vector](../../../../../normal-vector.md) $(1,0,1)$, the plane through $P$ has equation $x+z=3$, also satisfied by $Q,R$. Projecting the displacement from the origin onto the unit normal gives the [distance from a point to a plane](../../../../../distance-from-a-point-to-a-plane.md):

$$
\boxed{d=\frac{|3|}{\sqrt{1^2+0^2+1^2}}=\frac3{\sqrt2}.}
$$

The perpendicular foot is $(3/2,0,3/2)$, which lies in that plane and confirms the distance.
