<h1 id="6/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [divergence](../../../../../divergence.md) is $\nabla\cdot\mathbf F=1+2+3=6$. Since the [volume enclosed by a sphere](../../../../../volume-enclosed-by-a-sphere.md) is $4\pi a^3/3$,

$$
\boxed{\int_V\nabla\cdot\mathbf F\,dV=6\frac{4\pi a^3}{3}=8\pi a^3.}
$$

This agrees with the independently computed outward [surface integral](../../../../../surface-integral.md), as the [divergence theorem](../../../../../divergence-theorem.md) requires.
