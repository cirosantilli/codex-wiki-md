<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $V\subset\mathbb R^3$ be a bounded region with a piecewise smooth closed boundary $S=\partial V$, and let $\mathbf F$ have continuous first partial derivatives on a neighborhood of its closure. The [divergence theorem](../../../../../divergence-theorem.md) states

$$
\boxed{\int_V\nabla\cdot\mathbf F\,dV=\int_S\mathbf F\cdot\mathbf n\,dS,}
$$

where $\mathbf n$ is the outward unit [normal vector](../../../../../normal-vector.md). The right side is the outward [flux](../../../../../flux.md); reversing the orientation reverses its sign.
