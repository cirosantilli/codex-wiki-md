<h1 id="8/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For fixed $x\ne0$, the [chain rule](../../../../../../chain-rule.md) and the derivative of the [hyperbolic secant](../../../../../../hyperbolic-secant.md) give

$$
\frac{\partial}{\partial y}\left[-\frac1x\operatorname{sech}(xy)\right]=\operatorname{sech}(xy)\tanh(xy).
$$

Thus the stated antiderivative holds, with its integration constant independent of $y$ but allowed to depend on $x$. At $x=0$ the integrand is zero and the antiderivative is simply constant; the displayed formula with $1/x$ is meant for nonzero $x$.
