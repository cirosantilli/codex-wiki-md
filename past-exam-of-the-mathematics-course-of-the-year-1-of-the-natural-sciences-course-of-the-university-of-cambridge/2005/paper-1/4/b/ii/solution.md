<h1 id="4/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Assume $C,Q>0$. The expected net profit per item is $\Pi(\alpha)=Q\mathbb E[T]-C/\alpha^2=Q/\alpha-C/\alpha^2$. Put $u=1/\alpha>0$ and complete the square:

$$
\Pi=Qu-Cu^2=\frac{Q^2}{4C}-C\left(u-\frac Q{2C}\right)^2.
$$

This [quadratic function](../../../../../../quadratic-function.md) has a unique global maximum at $u=Q/(2C)$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\alpha_{\rm opt}=\frac{2C}{Q},\qquad\Pi_{\max}=\frac{Q^2}{4C}.}
$$

The calculation uses the expected operating time, rather than replacing lifetime by an arbitrary fixed time.
