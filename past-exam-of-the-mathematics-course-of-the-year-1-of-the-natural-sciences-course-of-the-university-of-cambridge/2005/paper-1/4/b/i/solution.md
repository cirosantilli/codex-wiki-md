↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $s,\tau\geq0$, use [conditional probability](../../../../../../conditional-probability.md) and the [survival function](../../../../../../survival-function.md):

$$
\boxed{P(T>s+\tau\mid T>s)=\frac{e^{-\alpha(s+\tau)}}{e^{-\alpha s}}=e^{-\alpha\tau}.}
$$

It is independent of $s$ and equals $P(T>\tau)$. This is the [memoryless property](../../../../../../memorylessness-of-the-exponential-distribution.md) of the [exponential distribution](../../../../../../exponential-distribution.md), equivalent here to a constant [hazard function](../../../../../../hazard-function.md) $\alpha$.
