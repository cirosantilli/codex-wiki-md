↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [probability density function](../../../../../../probability-density-function.md) is normalized because $\int_0^\infty\alpha e^{-\alpha t}dt=1$. [Integration by parts](../../../../../../integration-by-parts.md) gives $\mathbb E[T]=\int_0^\infty t\alpha e^{-\alpha t}dt=1/\alpha$ and $\mathbb E[T^2]=2/\alpha^2$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[T]=\frac1\alpha,\qquad\operatorname{Var}(T)=\mathbb E[T^2]-\mathbb E[T]^2=\frac1{\alpha^2}.}
$$

These are the mean and [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md) of the [exponential distribution](../../../../../../exponential-distribution.md) with rate $\alpha$.
