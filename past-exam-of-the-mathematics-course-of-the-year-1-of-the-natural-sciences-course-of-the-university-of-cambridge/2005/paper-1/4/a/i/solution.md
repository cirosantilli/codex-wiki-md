↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $t_0\geq0$, integration of the [exponential distribution](../../../../../../exponential-distribution.md) gives its [cumulative distribution function](../../../../../../cumulative-distribution-function.md):

$$
\boxed{P(0\leq T\leq t_0)=\int_0^{t_0}\alpha e^{-\alpha t}dt=1-e^{-\alpha t_0}.}
$$

For $t_0<0$ the event is empty and its probability is zero.
