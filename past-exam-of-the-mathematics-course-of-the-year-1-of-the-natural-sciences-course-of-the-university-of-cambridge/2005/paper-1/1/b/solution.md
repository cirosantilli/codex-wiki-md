<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [Taylor series](../../../../../taylor-series.md) of the [sine](../../../../../sine.md) and [arctangent](../../../../../inverse-tangent.md) give $\sin x-\arctan x=x^3/6+O(x^5)$, while $x^2\ln(1+x)=x^3(1-x/2+O(x^2))$. Dividing the two series gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{\sin x-\arctan x}{x^2\ln(1+x)}=\frac16+O(x).}
$$

The first nonzero term after cancellation is the constant $1/6$.
