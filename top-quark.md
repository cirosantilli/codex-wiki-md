# Top quark

↑ **Parent:** [Quark](quark.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Top_quark)

The top quark is the up-type member of the third [quark generation](quark-generation.md). Its mass exceeds the [W boson](w-boson.md) mass, so a real [W boson](w-boson.md) cannot decay into an on-shell top quark. A massless approximation for accessible decay products must not silently remove this channel threshold.
