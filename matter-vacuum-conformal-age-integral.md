# Matter-vacuum conformal age integral

↑ **Parent:** [Conformal time](conformal-time.md)

In a flat universe containing [pressureless matter](pressureless-matter.md) and a positive [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md), normalize the [scale factor](scale-factor-cosmology.md) to one today. The [Friedmann equation](friedmann-equations.md) and $d\tau=dt/a$ give

$$
\tau_0=H_0^{-1}\int_0^1\frac{da}{\sqrt{\Omega_m a+\Omega_\Lambda a^4}}
=H_0^{-1}\Omega_m^{-1/3}\Omega_\Lambda^{-1/6}
\int_0^{(\Omega_\Lambda/\Omega_m)^{1/3}}\frac{dy}{\sqrt{y(1+y^3)}}.
$$

The change of variable is $a=(\Omega_m/\Omega_\Lambda)^{1/3}y$. Including [radiation in cosmology](radiation-in-cosmology.md) makes this an approximation, with its omitted early contribution of order $H_0^{-1}\sqrt{\Omega_r}/\Omega_m$. When the upper limit is large, the integral approaches $I=\Gamma(1/6)\Gamma(1/3)/(3\sqrt\pi)$; this follows by setting $z=y^3$ and using the [beta function](beta-function.md) integral.
