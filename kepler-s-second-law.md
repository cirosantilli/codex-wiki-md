<h1 id="kepler-s-second-law">Kepler's second law</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Kepler's laws of planetary motion](kepler-s-laws-of-planetary-motion.md)

The radius vector from the force centre to an orbiting body sweeps out equal areas in equal times. Its [areal velocity](areal-velocity.md) is constant because a [central force](central-force.md) conserves [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md).
