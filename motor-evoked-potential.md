# Motor-evoked potential

↑ **Parent:** [Electromyography](electromyography.md)

A muscle response following stimulation of a motor pathway. Its amplitude indexes excitability under specified recording conditions, rather than directly measuring the activity of a single [mirror neuron](mirror-neuron.md).
