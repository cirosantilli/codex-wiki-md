# Ideal class group of Q of square root thirty-five

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal class group](ideal-class-group.md)

The [ring of integers](ring-of-integers.md) is $\mathbb Z[\sqrt{35}]$ and the [field discriminant](field-discriminant.md) is $140$. The [Minkowski bound for ideal classes](minkowski-s-bound.md) is $\sqrt{35}<6$. At three the defining [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) is irreducible, so the only [prime ideals](prime-ideal.md) needed to generate the [ideal class group](ideal-class-group.md) are $P_2=(2,\sqrt{35}-1)$ and $P_5=(5,\sqrt{35})$, of [ideal norms](ideal-norm.md) two and five. They satisfy $P_2^2=(2)$, $P_5^2=(5)$ and $P_2P_5=(5+\sqrt{35})$, so one class of [order of a group element](order-of-a-group-element.md) at most two generates. The [field norm](field-norm.md) equation $a^2-35b^2=\pm2$ is impossible modulo five, proving $P_2$ is not a [principal ideal](principal-ideal.md). Hence the [ideal class group](ideal-class-group.md) has exactly two elements.
