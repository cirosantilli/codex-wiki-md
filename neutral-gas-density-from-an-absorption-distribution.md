# Neutral gas density from an absorption distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Neutral-hydrogen column-density distribution](neutral-hydrogen-column-density-distribution.md)

Let $f_X=\partial^2\mathcal N/(\partial N\partial X)$ count neutral [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) absorbers per [column density](column-density.md) and [absorption distance](absorption-distance.md). The summed column per unit proper light-path length is the mean neutral [number density](number-density.md). Since $d\ell=c\,dz/[(1+z)H(z)]$ and $dX/dz=H_0(1+z)^2/H(z)$, that density is $(H_0/c)(1+z)^3\int Nf_X\,dN$. Dividing the proper [mass density](density.md) by $(1+z)^3$ gives the comoving density in the displayed formula. The denominator is today's [critical density](critical-density.md); including neutral helium requires a stated mass correction. For a distribution per redshift, replace $H_0f_X$ by $H(z)f_z/(1+z)^2$.
