# Relative cup product

↑ **Parent:** [Cup product](cup-product.md)

For open subsets $U,V$ of a [topological space](topological-space.md) $Y$, the relative [cup product](cup-product.md) gives

$$
H^p(Y,U;R)\otimes H^q(Y,V;R)\longrightarrow H^{p+q}(Y,U\cup V;R).
$$

Compatibility with the maps to absolute [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) identifies its image with the ordinary cup product. More generally the construction requires an excisive pair of subsets. The open-set case can be proved using subdivision to compute on chains subordinate to the open cover.
