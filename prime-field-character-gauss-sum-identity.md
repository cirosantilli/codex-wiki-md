# Prime-field character Gauss sum identity

↑ **Parent:** [Gauss sum of a Dirichlet character](gauss-sum-of-a-dirichlet-character.md)

For a nontrivial [Dirichlet character](dirichlet-character.md) modulo a [prime](prime-number.md) $p$ and $a\not\equiv0\pmod p$, set $\tau=\sum_{y\ne0}\chi(y)e(ay/p)$. Substituting $y=tz$ in $|\tau|^2$ gives $\sum_{t\ne0}\chi(t)\sum_{z\ne0}e(a(t-1)z/p)$. The inner sum is $p-1$ at $t=1$ and $-1$ otherwise. Since [character orthogonality](character-orthogonality.md) gives $\sum_{t\ne0}\chi(t)=0$, the result is $p$.
