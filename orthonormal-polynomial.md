# Orthonormal polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Orthogonal polynomial](orthogonal-polynomial-split.md)

An orthonormal family of [orthogonal polynomials](orthogonal-polynomial-split.md) also gives each member unit [norm](norm.md) in the chosen weighted [inner product](inner-product-split.md). Its pairwise inner products are the [Kronecker delta](kronecker-delta.md). Dividing every nonzero [orthogonal polynomial](orthogonal-polynomial-split.md) by its [norm](norm.md) produces such a family; the sign or complex phase still requires a convention, such as a positive real leading coefficient.
