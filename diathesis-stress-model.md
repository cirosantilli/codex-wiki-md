# Diathesis-stress model

↑ **Parent:** [Clinical psychology](clinical-psychology.md)

An account in which vulnerability and stressful circumstances jointly influence an outcome. A [psychological stressor](psychological-stressor.md) can increase risk without being universally necessary or sufficient, and the vulnerability can itself have developmental and environmental causes.
