# Exponential decay

↑ **Parent:** [Analysis](analysis-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exponential_decay)

A quantity $E(t)$ undergoes exponential decay with rate $\gamma>0$ when

$$
\frac{dE}{dt}=-\gamma E,
\qquad
E(t)=E(0)e^{-\gamma t}.
$$
