# CLE peptide

↑ **Parent:** [Signal transduction](signal-transduction-split.md)

A member of the CLAVATA3-EMBRYO SURROUNDING REGION-related peptide family. Selected root-produced CLE peptides participate in [autoregulation of nodulation](autoregulation-of-nodulation.md); the family has many other developmental functions.
