# Weyl complete reducibility theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Semisimple Lie algebra](semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weyl_complete_reducibility_theorem)

Every finite-dimensional representation of a complex [semisimple Lie algebra](semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md) is a direct sum of irreducible representations. Equivalently, every invariant subspace has an invariant complement.
