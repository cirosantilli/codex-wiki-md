# Skew Laurent extensions of domains are domains

↑ **Parent:** [Skew Laurent polynomial ring](skew-laurent-polynomial-ring.md)

Let $A$ have no zero divisors and $\sigma$ be an [automorphism](automorphism.md). For nonzero [Laurent polynomials](laurent-polynomial.md) $f=\sum a_ix^i$ and $g=\sum b_jx^j$ with greatest exponents $m,n$, the coefficient of $x^{m+n}$ in $fg$ is $a_m\sigma^m(b_n)$. No other exponent pair contributes to that highest degree. Both factors are nonzero, so their product is nonzero, proving the [noncommutative domain](noncommutative-domain.md) property in every characteristic.
