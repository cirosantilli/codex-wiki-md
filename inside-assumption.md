# InSIDE assumption

↑ **Parent:** [MR-Egger regression](mr-egger-regression.md)

InSIDE stands for instrument strength independent of direct effect. It requires the genetic associations with the [exposure](exposure.md) to be independent of the direct, pleiotropic effects of those [genetic variants](genetic-variant.md) on the outcome. It is a key identifying assumption for [MR-Egger regression](mr-egger-regression.md).
