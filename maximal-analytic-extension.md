# Maximal analytic extension

↑ **Parent:** [Spacetime extension](spacetime-extension.md)

A [maximal analytic extension](maximal-analytic-extension.md) of an analytic [spacetime](spacetime.md) is an analytic [spacetime extension](spacetime-extension.md) admitting no further proper analytic extension as a regular spacetime of the same dimension. It is different from a [maximal Cauchy development](maximal-cauchy-development.md), which can end at a smooth [Cauchy horizon](cauchy-horizon.md). Additional global choices or quotients can affect uniqueness. The simply connected extension of positive-mass [Schwarzschild spacetime](schwarzschild-spacetime.md) through both horizons is the [Kruskal spacetime](kruskal-spacetime.md); its [curvature singularities](curvature-singularity.md) remain boundaries.
