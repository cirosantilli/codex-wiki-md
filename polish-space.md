# Polish space

↑ **Parent:** [Complete metric space](complete-metric-space.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_space)

A Polish space is a [topological space](topological-space.md) whose topology can be induced by a complete separable metric. The completeness requirement concerns some compatible metric; it need not hold for every metric inducing the topology. Euclidean spaces are examples.
