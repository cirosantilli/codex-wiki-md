# Binomial likelihood-power annealing

↑ **Parent:** [Likelihood-power annealing](likelihood-power-annealing.md)

For $m$ independent [binomial distribution](binomial-distribution.md) observations with fixed trial count $n$ and total successes $s$, the likelihood-power [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) relative to $dp$ is $p^{s/T}(1-p)^{(mn-s)/T}$. Its normalized law is the displayed [Beta distribution](beta-distribution.md). Its mean tends to $s/(mn)$ and its variance tends to zero as $T\downarrow0$, including endpoint maxima. Energy for maximizing a [log-likelihood](log-likelihood.md) is its negative; the opposite sign reverses the optimization.
