# Base-excited oscillator

↑ **Parent:** [Forced oscillator](forced-oscillator.md)

A [base-excited oscillator](base-excited-oscillator.md) has a moving support rather than a directly applied periodic [force](force-split.md). If its [damping](damping.md) depends on velocity relative to the support, $z=x-y$ obeys $m\ddot z+b\dot z+kz=-m\ddot y$. A model with damping against absolute velocity instead obeys $m\ddot x+b\dot x+kx=ky$ after subtracting its static equilibrium. These conventions give different [transfer functions](transfer-function.md).
