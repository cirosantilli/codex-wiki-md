# Particle shower

↑ **Parent:** [Particle detection](particle-detection-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Particle_shower)

A cascade producing multiple secondary [particles](particle-physics.md) from an initial energetic interaction. Its observed multiplicity depends on which particles reach the detector and on [detection efficiency](detection-efficiency.md).
