<h1 id="expected-duration-of-biased-gambler-s-ruin">Expected duration of biased gambler's ruin</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Gambler's ruin](gambler-s-ruin.md)

For a nearest-neighbour fortune on $\{0,\ldots,N\}$, with win probability $p$, loss probability $q=1-p$ and $p\ne q$, put $\rho=q/p$. [First-step analysis](first-step-analysis.md) gives $m_i=1+pm_{i+1}+qm_{i-1}$, with zero endpoint values. Solving the recurrence gives the displayed mean duration. Its symmetric limit is $i(N-i)$.
