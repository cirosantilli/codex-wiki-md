# Helium-3

↑ **Parent:** [Helium](helium.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helium-3)

This stable [helium](helium.md) isotope contains two [protons](proton.md) and one [neutron](neutron-split.md). In solar proton-proton burning it is an intermediate: two such nuclei can form [helium-4](helium-4.md) and return two [protons](proton.md), or one can capture [helium-4](helium-4.md) to enter the second chain branch. The balance between production and destruction determines the [helium-3 equilibrium abundance](helium-3-equilibrium-abundance.md) and its [helium-3 relaxation time](helium-3-relaxation-time.md).
