# Friedel oscillations

↑ **Parent:** [Dielectric screening](dielectric-screening.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friedel_oscillations)

The sharp [Fermi surface](fermi-surface.md) gives a nonlocal oscillatory electronic response around an impurity. In a three-dimensional isotropic metal its far-field oscillations have characteristic wavevector $2k_F$ and algebraically decaying amplitude. They are absent from the purely exponential [Thomas-Fermi screening](thomas-fermi-screening.md) potential. The local screening formula is a useful long-wavelength model, not an exact asymptotic expression at every distance.
