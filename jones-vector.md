# Jones vector

↑ **Parent:** [Jones calculus](jones-calculus.md)

A [Jones vector](jones-vector.md) records the two transverse [complex amplitudes](complex-amplitude.md) of a completely polarized [monochromatic wave](monochromatic-wave.md). Multiplication by a common nonzero complex number preserves its [polarization of waves](polarization-waves.md), while changing overall amplitude and phase. With the convention $\boldsymbol\Psi(t)=\operatorname{Re}[\mathbf J e^{i\omega t}]$, $\mathbf J=(1,2i)^{\mathsf T}$ gives $(\cos\omega t,-2\sin\omega t)$, an [ellipse](ellipse.md) with the longer axis along $y$. Handedness labels require both a viewing direction and a naming convention.
