# Linear combination of jointly normal random variables

↑ **Parent:** [Multivariate Gaussian distribution](multivariate-gaussian-distribution.md)

Every linear combination $a^TX$ of the coordinates of a [jointly normal random vector](multivariate-gaussian-distribution.md) is a [normal random variable](gaussian-random-variable.md). Its mean is $a^T\mu$ and its variance is $a^T\Sigma a$.
