<h1 id="alfven-speed">Alfvén speed</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal magnetohydrodynamics](ideal-magnetohydrodynamics.md)

The Alfvén speed associated with magnetic-field magnitude $B$ and density $\rho$ is $v_A=B/\sqrt{\mu_0\rho}$.
