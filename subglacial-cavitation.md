# Subglacial cavitation

↑ **Parent:** [Basal sliding](basal-sliding.md)

A [water](water.md)-filled cavity opens where the normal contact [pressure](pressure-split.md) behind a bed obstacle would otherwise fall below subglacial [water pressure](fluid-pressure.md). Reduced [effective pressure in subglacial drainage](effective-pressure-in-subglacial-drainage.md) enlarges cavities and changes the resisting contact geometry. For a fixed bounded-slope bed, the basal shear stress is bounded by a bed-dependent multiple of effective [pressure](pressure-split.md); a sliding curve may saturate or weaken as cavities grow.
