# Sine-Gordon breather fusion amplitude

↑ **Parent:** [Sine-Gordon breather spectrum at reflectionless couplings](sine-gordon-breather-spectrum-at-reflectionless-couplings.md)

The second [breather](breather.md) is a bound pair of first [breathers](breather.md) with constituent shifts $\pm ia$. The [bound-state fusion of factorized S-matrices](bound-state-fusion-of-factorized-s-matrices.md) gives $S_{2,1}=S_{1,1}(\vartheta+ia)S_{1,1}(\vartheta-ia)$, which factors as displayed. The nearest physical-strip [pole](pole.md) at $\vartheta=ia$ is a crossed-channel exchange of the first [breather](breather.md). At $n=3$ the more distant center [pole](pole.md) is double, without changing that nearest-pole interpretation.
