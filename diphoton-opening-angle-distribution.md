# Diphoton opening-angle distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Relativistic two-body decay](relativistic-two-body-decay.md)

An [isotropic](isotropy.md) two-[photon](photon.md) decay of a massive parent with laboratory [Lorentz factor](lorentz-factor.md) $\gamma>1$ has the displayed normalized opening-angle [probability density](probability-density.md), with $\beta=\sqrt{1-\gamma^{-2}}$ and $2\arcsin(1/\gamma)<A<\pi$. To derive it in units $c=1$, the laboratory [photon](photon.md) [energies](energy-split.md) are $E_{1,2}=\gamma M(1\pm\beta z)/2$, where $z$ is uniform on $[-1,1]$. The [diphoton invariant mass](diphoton-invariant-mass.md) gives $\sin^2(A/2)=1/[\gamma^2(1-\beta^2z^2)]$. Apply the [change of variables for probability densities](change-of-variables-for-probability-densities.md) to both signs of $z$. Its minimum-angle peak is an [inverse-square-root singularity](inverse-square-root-singularity.md). At $\gamma=1$, the [probability distribution](probability-distribution-split.md) is concentrated at $A=\pi$.
