# Earthquake

↑ **Parent:** [Solid Earth](solid-earth.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earthquake)

A transient release of stored mechanical energy in the solid planet, often associated with fault motion. [Plate tectonics](plate-tectonics.md) is a major terrestrial driver, but earthquakes can also occur without an active global plate system.
