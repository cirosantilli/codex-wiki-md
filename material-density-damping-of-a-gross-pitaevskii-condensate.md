<h1 id="material-density-damping-of-a-gross-pitaevskii-condensate">Material-density damping of a Gross–Pitaevskii condensate</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Gross–Pitaevskii equation](gross-pitaevskii-equation.md)

Let $D_n=\partial_t+\mathbf v_n\cdot\nabla$ be the [material derivative](material-derivative.md) of the [normal fluid](normal-component-of-a-superfluid.md), and $\zeta>0$. The displayed phenomenological density damping preserves the condensate [continuity equation](continuity-equation.md) because its coefficient is real. Under a [Galilean boost](galilean-transformation.md), both velocities shift by the boost velocity and the field acquires the Schrödinger phase; $D_n|\psi|^2$ is invariant. Thus the normal-fluid derivative, rather than a derivative along the condensate flow, is essential to the model.
