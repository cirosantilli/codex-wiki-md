# Weingarten formula

↑ **Parent:** [Shape operator in a normal direction](shape-operator-in-a-normal-direction.md)

The [Weingarten formula](weingarten-formula.md) decomposes the ambient derivative of a normal field into a tangential [shape operator](shape-operator.md) term and the [normal connection](normal-connection.md). The minus sign corresponds to $\langle A_\xi X,Y\rangle=\langle II(X,Y),\xi\rangle$. For the outward unit normal of a unit [sphere](sphere.md), $A_\xi=-I$ with this convention.
