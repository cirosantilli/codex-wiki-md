# Geometric bound for omitted Haar details

↑ **Parent:** [Uniform hard-threshold convergence of normalized Haar expansions](uniform-hard-threshold-convergence-of-normalized-haar-expansions.md)

At each point there is at most one supported [Haar wavelet](haar-wavelet.md) per scale. If a detail coefficient is below $\delta$, its value there is at most $\delta2^{j/2}/\sqrt L$. Summing these bounds up to level $K$ gives the displayed geometric estimate. Selecting $K$ so the final term is of the order of the [modulus of continuity](modulus-of-continuity.md) controls all omitted fine details, even though arbitrary deletions from a uniformly convergent series need not preserve convergence.
