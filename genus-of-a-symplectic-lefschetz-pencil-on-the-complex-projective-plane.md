# Genus of a symplectic Lefschetz pencil on the complex projective plane

↑ **Parent:** [Lefschetz pencil](lefschetz-pencil.md)

Every symplectic smooth fiber in a Lefschetz pencil on $\mathbb{CP}^2$ represents $dH$ for an integer $d\geq1$. The [symplectic adjunction formula](symplectic-adjunction-formula.md) gives

$$
g=\frac{(d-1)(d-2)}2.
$$

Conversely, generic pencils of degree-$d$ complex plane curves realize every value in this list.
