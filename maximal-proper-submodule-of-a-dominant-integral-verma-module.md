# Maximal proper submodule of a dominant integral Verma module

↑ **Parent:** [Irreducible quotient of a Verma module](irreducible-quotient-of-a-verma-module.md)

If $\lambda$ is a [dominant integral weight](dominant-integral-weight.md), the maximal proper submodule of $M(\lambda)$ is generated by the simple-root [singular vectors](singular-vector.md)

$$
f_i^{\langle\lambda,\alpha_i^\vee\rangle+1}v_\lambda.
$$

The generator associated with $\alpha_i$ has weight $s_i(\lambda+\rho)-\rho$.
