# Closed unit ball

↑ **Parent:** [Unit ball](unit-ball.md)

The closed unit ball of a [normed vector space](normed-vector-space-split.md) is the set of vectors of [norm](norm.md) at most one. In a [continuous dual space](continuous-dual-space-split.md) it is compact in the [weak-star topology](weak-star-topology.md) by the [Banach-Alaoglu theorem](banach-alaoglu-theorem.md).
