# Real (p, p)-form

↑ **Parent:** [Differential form of type (p, q)](differential-form-of-type-p-q.md)

A [differential form of type (p, q)](differential-form-of-type-p-q.md) with equal holomorphic and antiholomorphic degrees is real when it is fixed by [complex conjugation](complex-conjugation.md). For such a form, $\overline{\bar\partial\eta}=\partial\eta$, so $\bar\partial\eta=0$ implies $d\eta=0$. The converse follows from the distinct types of $\partial\eta$ and $\bar\partial\eta$.
