# Monochromatic internal-wave overturning criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Plane internal gravity wave](plane-internal-gravity-wave.md)

For a [plane internal gravity wave](plane-internal-gravity-wave.md) with vertical displacement $\zeta$, linear density advection gives $\rho'=-\bar\rho_z\zeta$. In a constant stable gradient, $\rho_z=\bar\rho_z(1-\zeta_z)$, so static overturning begins when the maximum of $\zeta_z$ exceeds one. A monochromatic displacement has maximum $|mA_\zeta|$. The criterion predicts the failure of the small-amplitude field, rather than a valid continuation of linear theory beyond overturning.
