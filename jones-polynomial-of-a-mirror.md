# Jones polynomial of a mirror

↑ **Parent:** [Jones polynomial](jones-polynomial.md)

Reflecting a [link diagram](link-diagram.md) interchanges its two bracket smoothings and negates its [writhe of a link diagram](writhe-of-a-link-diagram.md). This replaces $A$ by $A^{-1}$ in the normalized [Kauffman bracket](kauffman-bracket.md), hence $t$ by $t^{-1}$. A [knot](knot.md) whose [Jones polynomial](jones-polynomial.md) differs from its reciprocal cannot be an [amphichiral knot](amphichiral-knot.md).
