# C2v molecular point group

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular symmetry](molecular-symmetry.md)

The four-element molecular [point group](point-group.md) with one twofold axis and two mirror planes containing that axis. [Water](water.md) has this symmetry at its equilibrium bent geometry.
