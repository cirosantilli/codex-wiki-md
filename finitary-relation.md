# Finitary relation

↑ **Parent:** [Set theory](set-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finitary_relation)

A [subset](subset.md) of a finite [Cartesian product](cartesian-product.md) specifies which [tuples](finite-tuple.md) of elements satisfy a relation. A [first-order structure](first-order-structure.md) interprets a relation symbol with $n$ argument places as such a [subset](subset.md) of the $n$-fold [Cartesian product](cartesian-product.md) of its [domain of a first-order structure](domain-of-a-first-order-structure.md). For two argument places this is a [binary relation](binary-relation.md); for three, the [set](set-split.md) of triples satisfying $x+y=z$ is an example involving [addition](addition.md).
