# Classification of connected abelian Lie groups

↑ **Parent:** [Abelian Lie group](abelian-lie-group.md)

For a connected [abelian Lie group](abelian-lie-group.md), the [Lie exponential map](exponential-map-of-a-lie-group.md) is an additive [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md) and a local [diffeomorphism](diffeomorphism.md) at zero. Its image is an open [subgroup](subgroup.md), hence the whole connected [group](group-split.md). Its kernel is a [discrete additive subgroup of a real vector space](discrete-additive-subgroup-of-a-real-vector-space.md). A basis of that kernel identifies it with $\mathbb Z^r$ inside $\mathbb R^n$, giving the displayed classification. The compact factors record periods of [one-parameter subgroups](one-parameter-subgroup.md).
