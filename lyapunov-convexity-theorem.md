# Lyapunov convexity theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Non-atomic measure](non-atomic-measure.md)

The range of a finite-dimensional, finite, countably additive, atomless vector measure is [compact](compact-space.md) and [convex](convex-function.md). In particular, an [atomless measure](non-atomic-measure.md) restricted to a measurable set $E$ can realize every mass between zero and $\mu(E)$. This allows exact balancing by small disjoint consumer cohorts in an [exchange economy](exchange-economy.md).
