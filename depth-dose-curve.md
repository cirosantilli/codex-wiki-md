# Depth-dose curve

↑ **Parent:** [Radiation therapy](radiation-therapy.md)

The variation of [absorbed dose](absorbed-dose.md) with depth for a specified beam, geometry and medium. A megavoltage photon beam has a secondary-electron buildup region followed by gradual dose reduction. A nearly monoenergetic proton beam instead exhibits a [Bragg peak](bragg-peak.md) near the end of its range.
