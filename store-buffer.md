# Store buffer

↑ **Parent:** [Computer memory](computer-memory-split.md)

Storage for pending writes, usually with their [memory addresses](memory-address.md), data and status. A younger [load instruction](load-instruction.md) must account for older overlapping buffered [store instructions](store-instruction.md), often by [store-to-load forwarding](store-to-load-forwarding.md).
