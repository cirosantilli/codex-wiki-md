# Complementary invariant masses in a sequential decay

↑ **Parent:** [Cascade-decay invariant-mass endpoint](cascade-decay-invariant-mass-endpoint.md)

For the sequential decay defining the [cascade-decay invariant-mass endpoint](cascade-decay-invariant-mass-endpoint.md), [momentum](momentum-split.md) conservation gives the displayed eventwise identity. Thus $m_{At}^2$ has minimum $m_A^2m_C^2/m_B^2$ and maximum $m_A^2+m_C^2-m_B^2$, attained at the opposite relative angles from the $s,t$ pair endpoints. The final A particle is massive, so its lower pair-mass bound need not vanish.
