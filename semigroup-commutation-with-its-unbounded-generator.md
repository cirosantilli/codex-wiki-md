# Semigroup commutation with its unbounded generator

↑ **Parent:** [Infinitesimal generator of a semigroup](infinitesimal-generator-of-a-semigroup.md)

For a [strongly continuous semigroup](c0-semigroup.md), apply the bounded operator $T_t$ to the difference quotient defining its [semigroup generator](infinitesimal-generator-of-a-semigroup.md). The limit proves domain invariance and commutation on the generator domain. This is not an assertion that the unbounded generator acts on every vector.
