<h1 id="prim-s-algorithm">Prim's algorithm</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Minimum spanning tree](minimum-spanning-tree.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prim's_algorithm)

Start from one [graph vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md). Repeatedly add the least-weight [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) joining the reached [graph vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) to an unreached [graph vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md). Each step grows a [tree](tree-graph-theory.md) and obeys the [minimum spanning tree cut property](minimum-spanning-tree-cut-property.md). On a finite connected [graph](graph-split.md), the result is a [minimum spanning tree](minimum-spanning-tree.md).
