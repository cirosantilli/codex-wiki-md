# Probability mass function

↑ **Parent:** [Probability distribution](probability-distribution.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Probability_mass_function)

For a [discrete probability distribution](discrete-probability-distribution-split.md), the [probability mass function](probability-mass-function.md) of its [random variable](random-variable-split.md) $X$ is

$$
p_X(x)=\mathbb P(X=x),\qquad p_X(x)\geq0,\qquad\sum_x p_X(x)=1.
$$

The sum ranges over its finite or [countable set](countable-set.md) of possible values. An event's probability is the sum of the masses of the included values. For the example under [probability distribution](probability-distribution.md), $\mathbb P(X\geq1)=p_X(1)+p_X(3)=0.8$. The [cumulative distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md) sums masses below a threshold; its jump at a value equals that value's [probability mass](probability-mass.md). This differs from a [probability density function](probability-density-function.md), whose height is not a point probability.
