# Half-plane capacity of a vertical slit

↑ **Parent:** [Half-plane capacity](half-plane-capacity.md)

For a vertical slit of height $h>0$ attached at $x\in\mathbb R$, the [mapping-out function of a compact H-hull](mapping-out-function-of-a-compact-h-hull.md) is $g(z)=x+\sqrt{(z-x)^2+h^2}$, with the branch asymptotic to $z-x$ at infinity. Its [half-plane capacity](half-plane-capacity.md) is $h^2/2$.
