# Spring-supported pendulum normal modes

↑ **Parent:** [Normal mode](normal-mode.md)

A pendulum bob and a horizontally moving support of equal [mass](mass.md) $m$, with support spring [spring constant](spring-constant.md) $k$, have the quadratic [potential energy](potential-energy.md) $kx_1^2/2+mg(x_2-x_1)^2/(2l)$. With $\kappa=k/m$, $q=g/l$, the [normal mode](normal-mode.md) [eigenvectors](eigenvector.md) can be normalized to $(1,r_\pm)$, where $r_\pm=(\kappa/2\mp\sqrt{\kappa^2/4+q^2})/q$. The lower mode is in phase and the upper mode in opposite phase. The positive [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) of the symmetric stiffness-per-mass [matrix](matrix.md) give the displayed squared [angular frequencies](angular-frequency.md).
