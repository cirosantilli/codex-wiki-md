# Neutral mode of the equal-width Hazel model

↑ **Parent:** [Hazel model](hazel-model.md)

The zero-[phase velocity](phase-velocity.md) ansatz $w=(\operatorname{sech}z)^k(\tanh z)^{1-k}$ solves the [Taylor–Goldstein equation](taylor-goldstein-equation.md) away from its [critical level of an internal gravity wave](critical-level-of-an-internal-gravity-wave.md) exactly when $J=k(1-k)$. Let $y=\tanh z$ and $a=1-k$. Then $w'/w=a/y-y$ and $w''/w=a(a-1)/y^2-a-1+2y^2$, so the differential-equation residual divided by $w$ is $[J-k(1-k)](y^{-2}-1)$. The curve has maximum $1/4$ at $k=1/2$. The endpoint $k=0$ does not decay at infinity; fractional powers at the critical level require a branch and regularity qualification.
