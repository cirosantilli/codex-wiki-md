# Early endosome

↑ **Parent:** [Endosome](endosome.md)

An [early endosome](early-endosome.md) is an early sorting compartment for internalized material. Delivery to it, recycling from it and onward trafficking are experimentally distinct from uptake across the [plasma membrane](cell-membrane-split.md).
