# Proper-eccentricity annulus

↑ **Parent:** [Eccentric debris ring](eccentric-debris-ring.md)

For a common [semi-major axis](semi-major-axis.md) $a$ and [proper eccentricity](proper-eccentricity.md) $e_p$, randomly distributed [proper longitudes of periapsis](proper-longitude-of-periapsis.md) give an [annulus](annulus-mathematics.md) of radii $a(1-e_p)$ and $a(1+e_p)$ about the point displaced from the [star](star.md) by minus $a$ times the [forced eccentricity](forced-eccentricity.md) vector, to first order in [orbital eccentricity](orbital-eccentricity.md).
