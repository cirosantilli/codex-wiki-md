# Stereographic energy of the O3 sigma model

↑ **Parent:** [O3 nonlinear sigma model](o3-nonlinear-sigma-model.md)

In the oriented [stereographic projection](stereographic-projection.md) $\phi=(2\operatorname{Re}w,2\operatorname{Im}w,1-|w|^2)/(1+|w|^2)$, the sphere's metric is $4|dw|^2/(1+|w|^2)^2$. For energy $V=\int|\nabla\phi|^2$, its [topological degree](topological-degree.md) is $N=\pi^{-1}\int\operatorname{Im}(\overline{w_{x_1}}w_{x_2})/(1+|w|^2)^2$. The inequality $|w_{x_1}|^2+|w_{x_2}|^2\geq2|\operatorname{Im}(\overline{w_{x_1}}w_{x_2})|$ gives $V\geq8\pi|N|$. [Holomorphic maps](holomorphic-map.md) and [antiholomorphic maps](antiholomorphic-function.md) that are [rational maps](rational-map-complex-analysis.md) saturate the respective signs. The more common energy with a prefactor $1/2$ has bound $4\pi|N|$ instead.
