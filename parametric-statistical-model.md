# Parametric statistical model

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical model](statistical-model-split.md)

A [statistical model](statistical-model-split.md) indexed by a parameter vector of fixed finite dimension is parametric. Examples include a [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md) with unknown mean and variance and a [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md) with unknown mean. Its [likelihood function](likelihood-function.md) is the joint sample density or mass function, regarded as a function of the parameter at the observed sample.
