# Atomic mass

↑ **Parent:** [Isotope](isotope.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atomic_mass)

The [rest mass](invariant-mass.md) of a [chemical atom](atom-physics.md) in its [ground state](ground-state.md), a [physical quantity](physical-quantity.md) expressed in [SI](international-system-of-units.md) [mass](mass.md) units or the [unified atomic mass unit](dalton-unit.md). Here $m_i$ means the [atomic mass](atomic-mass.md) of a neutral [chemical atom](atom-physics.md) of [isotope](isotope.md) $i$, not the [mass](mass.md) of its bare [atomic nucleus](atomic-nucleus.md) and not a population average.[atomic mass](iupac-compendium-of-chemical-terminology-fifth-edition-gold-book-by-international-union-of-pure-and-applied-chemistry/atomic-mass.md)

[Atomic mass](atomic-mass.md) includes [Electrons](electron.md) and depends on [nuclear binding energy](nuclear-binding-energy.md); it is not just [mass number](mass-number.md) times a universal [nucleon](nucleon.md) [mass](mass.md). The [atomic mass](atomic-mass.md) of neutral [carbon-12](carbon-12.md) in its [ground state](ground-state.md) is exactly $12\,\mathrm u$ by definition of the [unified atomic mass unit](dalton-unit.md). That exactness establishes the reference scale, rather than making the [atomic masses](atomic-mass.md) of all [isotopes](isotope.md) exact [integers](integer.md) in the same unit.
