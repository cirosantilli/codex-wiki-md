# Quadrupolar tidal forcing

↑ **Parent:** [Tidal tensor](tidal-tensor.md)

For a point-mass companion at separation $a$, subtract the constant and linear terms of its [Newtonian gravitational potential](newtonian-gravitational-potential.md) in a frame accelerating with the target's center. The leading residual is

$$
\Phi_{\mathrm{tide}}=-\frac{GM_2}{2a^3}\left[3(\mathbf n\cdot\mathbf r)^2-r^2\right].
$$

It is degree two in [spherical harmonics](spherical-harmonic.md). In a circular orbit with angular frequency $\omega_o$, its time-dependent $m=\pm2$ components force at $2\omega_o$ in a frame with no spin; there is also a static $m=0$ component. This is the leading approximation in $r/a$, and the tidal amplitude may be absorbed into a harmonic-dependent constant.
