<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A [tensor product of group representations](../../../../tensor-product-of-group-representations.md) has [character](../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) equal to the product of its factor [characters](../../../../character-of-a-representation.md). Multiplying the rows of the [character table](../../../../character-table.md) in [solution](../b/solution.md), using $s^2=2+t$, $t^2=2+s$ and $st=-1$, gives all the [Clebsch-Gordan decomposition of group representations](../../../../clebsch-gordan-decomposition-of-group-representations.md):

$$
\boxed{\begin{array}{c|cccc}
\otimes&R_0&R_1&R_2&R_3\\\hline
R_0&R_0&R_1&R_2&R_3\\
R_1&R_1&R_0&R_2&R_3\\
R_2&R_2&R_2&R_0\oplus R_1\oplus R_3&R_2\oplus R_3\\
R_3&R_3&R_3&R_2\oplus R_3&R_0\oplus R_1\oplus R_2
\end{array}}
$$

For example, the [character](../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) of $R_2\otimes R_2$ is $(4,s^2,t^2,0)=(4,2+t,2+s,0)$, the sum of the [characters](../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) of $R_0$, $R_1$ and $R_3$. Likewise $R_2\otimes R_3$ has [character](../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) $(4,-1,-1,0)=\chi_2+\chi_3$. The [trivial representation](../../../../trivial-representation.md) and $R_1\otimes R_1=R_0$ account for the remaining entries.
