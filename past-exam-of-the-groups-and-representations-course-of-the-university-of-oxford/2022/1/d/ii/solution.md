Now the doublet [invariant tensor](../../../../../invariant-tensor.md) would satisfy $D_1(Q)^T L D_2(Q)=L$. Its four entries acquire factors $\alpha^3$, $\alpha^{-1}$, $\alpha$, and $\alpha^{-3}$, respectively. None equals $1$, so $L=0$. Equivalently, $R_2\otimes R_3=R_2\oplus R_3$ has no [trivial representation](../../../../../trivial-representation.md). The mixed doublet and third-family terms are again forbidden because their [tensor product of group representations](../../../../../tensor-product-of-group-representations.md) contain no [trivial representation](../../../../../trivial-representation.md). Only the product of the two $R_1$ fields survives:

$$
\boxed{\lambda^{\mathrm{(ii)}}=
\begin{pmatrix}0&0&0\\0&0&0\\0&0&y_3\end{pmatrix},\qquad y_3\in\mathbb C.}
$$
