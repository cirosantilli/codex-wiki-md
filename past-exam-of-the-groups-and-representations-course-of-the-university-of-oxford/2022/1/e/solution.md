<h1 id="1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Let $v_H=\langle H\rangle$ be the [vacuum expectation value](../../../../vacuum-expectation-value.md) in the convention $M=v_H\lambda$. For canonically normalized [fermion](../../../../fermion.md) kinetic terms, physical [charged lepton](../../../../charged-lepton.md) masses are the nonnegative values in the [singular value decomposition](../../../../singular-value-decomposition.md) of $M$, equivalently the square roots of the [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md) of $M^\dagger M$.

In case (i),

$$
M^\dagger M=|v_H|^2\operatorname{diag}(|y|^2,|y|^2,|y_3|^2),\qquad
\boxed{\{m_1,m_2,m_3\}=|v_H|\{|y|,|y|,|y_3|\}.}
$$

The opposite algebraic [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md) $y$ and $-y$ of the doublet block do not represent different physical masses. The unavoidable twofold degeneracy conflicts with the three distinct nonzero measured [charged lepton](../../../../charged-lepton.md) masses, for any assignment of the three families.

In case (ii),

$$
\boxed{\{m_1,m_2,m_3\}=\{0,0,|v_Hy_3|\}.}
$$

One can fit the [tau lepton](../../../../tau-particle.md) mass by choosing $|v_Hy_3|$, but the [electron](../../../../electron.md) and [muon](../../../../muon.md) remain massless. Thus neither exact [dihedral group](../../../../dihedral-group.md) assignment with this single singlet [complex scalar field](../../../../complex-scalar-field.md) reproduces the observed spectrum. Additional symmetry-breaking [Yukawa interactions](../../../../yukawa-interaction.md) or other sources of mass are required; a basis change cannot remove the degeneracy or change the [matrix rank](../../../../matrix-rank.md).
