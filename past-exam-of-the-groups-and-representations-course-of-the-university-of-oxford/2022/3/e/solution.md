<h1 id="3/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The [symmetric square of a direct sum](../../../../symmetric-square-of-a-direct-sum.md) gives

$$
\operatorname{Sym}^2(A\oplus B)=\operatorname{Sym}^2A\oplus(A\otimes B)\oplus\operatorname{Sym}^2B.
$$

Now $\operatorname{Sym}^2\mathbf2=\mathbf3$ and $\operatorname{Sym}^2\mathbf3=\mathbf6$, rather than the singlet and antitriplet arising from [exterior squares](../../../../exterior-square.md). Thus

$$
\boxed{\mathbf{15}\downarrow=(\mathbf3,\mathbf1)_6\oplus(\mathbf2,\mathbf3)_1\oplus(\mathbf1,\mathbf6)_{-4}.}
$$

The dimensions are $3+6+6=15$.

By contrast, [solution](../c/solution.md) gives the following left-handed [Weyl fermion](../../../../weyl-spinor.md) content of $\overline{\mathbf5}\oplus\mathbf{10}$:

$$
L:(\mathbf2,\mathbf1)_{-3},\quad d^c:(\mathbf1,\overline{\mathbf3})_2,\quad
Q:(\mathbf2,\mathbf3)_1,\quad u^c:(\mathbf1,\overline{\mathbf3})_{-4},\quad
e^c:(\mathbf1,\mathbf1)_6.
$$

These are one [Standard Model](../../../../standard-model-split.md) family, with [weak hypercharge](../../../../hypercharge.md) $Y=q_c/6$. The $\mathbf{15}$ contains $Q$ but lacks $L$, $d^c$, $u^c$ and $e^c$; instead it contains an [SU(2)](../../../../su-2-group.md) triplet and an [SU(3)](../../../../su-3-group.md) sextet. Equality of total dimensions therefore does not give the same [representation](../../../../group-representation.md) content, and $\mathbf{15}$ cannot supply the family.
