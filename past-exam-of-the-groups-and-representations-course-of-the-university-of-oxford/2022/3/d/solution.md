<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [complexification of a Lie algebra](../../../../complexification-of-a-lie-algebra.md) in the [Adjoint representation](../../../../adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md) of [SU(5)](../../../../su-5.md) consists of traceless endomorphisms of $V=A\oplus B$. Splitting an endomorphism into its four blocks gives traceless endomorphisms of $A$ and $B$, one relative block-scalar direction, and the two off-diagonal spaces $\operatorname{Hom}(B,A)$ and $\operatorname{Hom}(A,B)$.

The first three pieces transform as $(\mathbf3,\mathbf1)_0$, $(\mathbf1,\mathbf8)_0$ and $(\mathbf1,\mathbf1)_0$. The off-diagonal pieces have [U(1) charges](../../../../u-1-charge.md) equal to output minus input charge, namely $3-(-2)=5$ and $-2-3=-5$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\mathbf{24}\downarrow=(\mathbf3,\mathbf1)_0\oplus(\mathbf1,\mathbf8)_0\oplus(\mathbf1,\mathbf1)_0
\oplus(\mathbf2,\overline{\mathbf3})_5\oplus(\mathbf2,\mathbf3)_{-5}.}
$$

The dimensions are $3+8+1+6+6=24$. The first three summands are precisely the [gauge fields](../../../../gauge-field.md) of the local [gauge group](../../../../gauge-group.md) $H\times U_c(1)$. The extra [gauge fields](../../../../gauge-field.md) of the [SU(5)](../../../../su-5.md) [gauge theory](../../../../gauge-theory.md) transform as

$$
\boxed{(\mathbf2,\overline{\mathbf3})_5\oplus(\mathbf2,\mathbf3)_{-5}.}
$$

Hermiticity relates the two off-diagonal blocks, so these describe twelve real [gauge fields](../../../../gauge-field.md), or six complex fields and their conjugates. Globally, the subgroup generated by $H$ and $U_c(1)$ inside [SU(5)](../../../../su-5.md) is $(SU(2)\times SU(3)\times U(1))/\mathbb Z_6$, since the parametrization $(U_2,U_3,e^{i\theta})\mapsto\operatorname{diag}(e^{3i\theta}U_2,e^{-2i\theta}U_3)$ has a six-element [kernel](../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md). This finite quotient does not alter the stated [branching of a representation](../../../../branching-of-a-representation.md).
