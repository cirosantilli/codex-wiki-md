<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Write the defining space as $V=A\oplus B$, where $A\cong\mathbb C^2$ and $B\cong\mathbb C^3$. Use ordered pairs for the [SU(2)](../../../../su-2-group.md) and [SU(3)](../../../../su-3-group.md) factors, in that order. The generator $X_c$ in [solution](../b/solution.md) gives [U(1) charges](../../../../u-1-charge.md) $3$ on $A$ and $-2$ on $B$, so

$$
\boxed{\mathbf5\downarrow=(\mathbf2,\mathbf1)_3\oplus(\mathbf1,\mathbf3)_{-2}.}
$$

Taking the [conjugate representation](../../../../conjugate-representation.md) reverses the [U(1) charges](../../../../u-1-charge.md). The [SU(2)](../../../../su-2-group.md) doublet is equivalent to its [dual representation](../../../../dual-representation.md), while the [SU(3)](../../../../su-3-group.md) triplet becomes the antitriplet:

$$
\boxed{\overline{\mathbf5}\downarrow=(\mathbf2,\mathbf1)_{-3}\oplus(\mathbf1,\overline{\mathbf3})_2.}
$$

For the [two-index antisymmetric representation](../../../../two-index-antisymmetric-representation.md), split the [antisymmetric tensor](../../../../antisymmetric-tensor.md) $T^{IJ}=-T^{JI}$ into $T^{ab}$, $T^{a\mu}$ and $T^{\mu\nu}$, with $a,b=1,2$ and $\mu,\nu=3,4,5$. The [exterior square of a direct sum](../../../../exterior-square-of-a-direct-sum.md) gives

$$
\Lambda^2(A\oplus B)=\Lambda^2A\oplus(A\otimes B)\oplus\Lambda^2B.
$$

The first term is an [SU(2)](../../../../su-2-group.md) singlet, the middle is $(\mathbf2,\mathbf3)$, and $\Lambda^2\mathbf3\cong\overline{\mathbf3}$ by contraction with the invariant alternating [tensor](../../../../tensor.md). Adding the [U(1) charges](../../../../u-1-charge.md) of the two indices gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbf{10}\downarrow=(\mathbf1,\mathbf1)_6\oplus(\mathbf2,\mathbf3)_1\oplus(\mathbf1,\overline{\mathbf3})_{-4}.}
$$

The dimensions $1+6+3=10$ check the [branching of a representation](../../../../branching-of-a-representation.md). In the convention $Q_{\rm em}=T_3+Y$, [weak hypercharge](../../../../hypercharge.md) is $Y=X_c/6$.
