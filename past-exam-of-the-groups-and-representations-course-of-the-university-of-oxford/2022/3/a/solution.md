<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The identity commutes with every $h\in H$, so the [centralizer](../../../../centralizer.md) $C_G(H)$ is nonempty. If $x,y\in C_G(H)$, then for every $h\in H$,

$$
(xy)h=x(yh)=x(hy)=(xh)y=(hx)y=h(xy).
$$

Thus it is closed under multiplication. From $xh=hx$, multiplication by $x^{-1}$ on both sides gives $hx^{-1}=x^{-1}h$, so $x^{-1}\in C_G(H)$. These are exactly the [subgroup](../../../../subgroup.md) conditions; hence

$$
\boxed{C_G(H)\leq G.}
$$

The proof works for the [centralizer of a subset](../../../../centralizer-of-a-subset.md) as well; it does not require that $H$ itself be a [subgroup](../../../../subgroup.md).
