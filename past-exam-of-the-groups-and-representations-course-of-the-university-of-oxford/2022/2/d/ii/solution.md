↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [two-index symmetric representation](../../../../../two-index-symmetric-representation.md) of [SU(3)](../../../../../su-3-group.md) is $\mathbf6$, with [Dynkin index](../../../../../dynkin-index.md) $5$. Thus $n$ copies have $c(r_S)=5n$, and the supplied [beta function](../../../../../beta-function-physics.md) is

$$
\beta(g)=-\frac{g^3}{192\pi^2}(132-5n).
$$

The strict sign condition for [asymptotic freedom](../../../../../asymptotic-freedom.md) is $n<132/5=26.4$, giving

$$
\boxed{n_{\max}=26.}
$$

Indeed $132-5(26)=2>0$, while $132-5(27)=-3<0$.
