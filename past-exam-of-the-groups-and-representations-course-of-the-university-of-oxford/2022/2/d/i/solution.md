<h1 id="2/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [Dynkin index](../../../../../dynkin-index.md) is additive over a [direct sum of group representations](../../../../../direct-sum-of-group-representations.md), so $n$ copies of the [fundamental representation](../../../../../fundamental-representation.md) have $c(r_S)=n$. With $c(\mathrm{ad})=6$, the supplied [beta function](../../../../../beta-function-physics.md) becomes

$$
\beta(g)=-\frac{g^3}{192\pi^2}(132-n).
$$

For positive [gauge coupling](../../../../../gauge-coupling.md) $g$, [asymptotic freedom](../../../../../asymptotic-freedom.md) at this order requires $\beta(g)<0$, hence $n<132$. For nonnegative integer $n$,

$$
\boxed{n_{\max}=131.}
$$

At $n=132$ the one-loop coefficient vanishes, so this formula alone does not establish [asymptotic freedom](../../../../../asymptotic-freedom.md). This count follows the scalar multiplicity convention of the printed [beta function](../../../../../beta-function-physics.md); changing how a real or complex [scalar field](../../../../../scalar-field.md) is counted requires changing its coefficient too.
