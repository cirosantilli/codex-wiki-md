<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Normalize the [simple roots](../../../../simple-root.md) to $(\alpha_i,\alpha_i)=2$. The [A2 root system](../../../../a2-root-system.md) has $(\alpha_1,\alpha_2)=-1$, so its [Dynkin diagram](../../../../dynkin-diagram.md) is two nodes joined by one bond:

$$
\underset{\alpha_1}{\circ}\! -\!\underset{\alpha_2}{\circ},\qquad
\boxed{A=\begin{pmatrix}2&-1\\-1&2\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

The [fundamental weights](../../../../fundamental-weight.md) satisfy $(\omega_i,\alpha_j)=\delta_{ij}$. Hence the [Gram matrix](../../../../gram-matrix.md) in the [fundamental weight](../../../../fundamental-weight.md) basis, the metric for [Dynkin label](../../../../dynkin-label.md) coordinates, is

$$
\boxed{G_{ij}=(\omega_i,\omega_j)=(A^{-1})_{ij}
=\frac13\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\1&2\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

For [weights of a representation](../../../../weight-of-a-representation.md) with [Dynkin labels](../../../../dynkin-label.md) $a$ and $b$, their [inner product](../../../../inner-product-split.md) is $a^TGb$. In the [simple root](../../../../simple-root.md) basis the metric is instead $A$ itself; stating the basis removes this apparent convention ambiguity.
