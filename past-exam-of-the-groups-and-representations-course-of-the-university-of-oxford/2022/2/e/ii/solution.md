<h1 id="2/e/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For the [fundamental representation](../../../../../fundamental-representation.md) and the [two-index symmetric representation](../../../../../two-index-symmetric-representation.md), the [Dynkin indices](../../../../../dynkin-index.md) are $1$ and $5$. Therefore the specified discrete [gauge anomaly](../../../../../gauge-anomaly.md) condition for $\mathbb Z_4$ is

$$
q_1+5q_2\equiv q_1+q_2\equiv0\pmod4.
$$

All ordered solutions are

$$
\boxed{(q_1,q_2)\in\{(0,0),(1,3),(2,2),(3,1)\}\pmod4.}
$$

Only $(1,3)$ and $(3,1)$ give a faithful $\mathbb Z_4$ action on this pair of [Weyl fermions](../../../../../weyl-spinor.md); faithfulness was not imposed. As in [solution](../i/solution.md), cancellation here concerns the stipulated discrete [gauge anomaly](../../../../../gauge-anomaly.md), not cancellation of the pure cubic [SU(3)](../../../../../su-3-group.md) [gauge anomaly](../../../../../gauge-anomaly.md). The integer normalization matters: one must not replace $c$ by a half-integer trace index inside an unchanged modular congruence.
