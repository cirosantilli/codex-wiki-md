<h1 id="2/e/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use the integer normalization $c(\mathbf3)=1$ from [solution](../../b/solution.md). For two [fundamental representations](../../../../../fundamental-representation.md), the specified discrete [gauge anomaly](../../../../../gauge-anomaly.md) coefficient is $\mathcal A=q_1+q_2$. Consequently all solutions in the [finite cyclic group](../../../../../finite-cyclic-group.md) of charges are

$$
\boxed{(q_1,q_2)=(q,-q)\pmod n,\qquad q\in\mathbb Z_n.}
$$

There are $n$ ordered charge pairs. The trivial pair is included: the problem does not require a faithful action. If a faithful action on these [Weyl fermions](../../../../../weyl-spinor.md) were additionally required, one would retain precisely the $q$ coprime to $n$.

This cancels the discrete [gauge anomaly](../../../../../gauge-anomaly.md) defined in the paper. Two left-handed [fundamental representations](../../../../../fundamental-representation.md) by themselves have a nonzero pure cubic [SU(3)](../../../../../su-3-group.md) [gauge anomaly](../../../../../gauge-anomaly.md); a complete [gauge theory](../../../../../gauge-theory.md) would need additional [fermions](../../../../../fermion.md) to cancel it. That separate condition does not alter the requested discrete congruence.
