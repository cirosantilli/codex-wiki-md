<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For the [special unitary group](../../../../special-unitary-group.md) $SU(N)$, denote a one-column [Young tableau](../../../../young-tableau.md) with $k$ empty boxes by $\mathcal C_k$. Explicitly the columns needed here are

$$
\mathcal C_1=\boxed{\phantom{a}},\quad
\mathcal C_2=\begin{array}{|c|}\hline\phantom{a}\\\hline\phantom{a}\\\hline\end{array},\quad
\mathcal C_3=\begin{array}{|c|}\hline\phantom{a}\\\hline\phantom{a}\\\hline\phantom{a}\\\hline\end{array},\quad
\mathcal C_4=\begin{array}{|c|}\hline\phantom{a}\\\hline\phantom{a}\\\hline\phantom{a}\\\hline\phantom{a}\\\hline\end{array},\quad
\mathcal C_5=\begin{array}{|c|}\hline\phantom{a}\\\hline\phantom{a}\\\hline\phantom{a}\\\hline\phantom{a}\\\hline\phantom{a}\\\hline\end{array}.
$$

A column $\mathcal C_k$ is the [exterior power](../../../../exterior-power.md) $\Lambda^kV$, with [highest weight](../../../../highest-weight-of-a-representation.md) $\omega_k$ and dimension $\binom Nk$. Contraction with the invariant volume [tensor](../../../../tensor.md) identifies $V^*\cong\Lambda^{N-1}V$ and $\Lambda^2V^*\cong\Lambda^{N-2}V$. This gives all the requested [Dynkin labels](../../../../dynkin-label.md), [Young tableaux](../../../../young-tableau.md), dimensions and [tensors](../../../../tensor.md):

$$
\boxed{\begin{array}{c|c|c|c|c}
\text{algebra}&R&\text{highest label}&\text{tableau}&\text{dimension and tensor}\\\hline
A_5&\mathbf6&(1,0,0,0,0)&\mathcal C_1&6:\ v^I\\
A_5&\overline{\mathbf6}&(0,0,0,0,1)&\mathcal C_5&6:\ v_I\\
A_5&\mathbf{15}&(0,1,0,0,0)&\mathcal C_2&15:\ T^{IJ}=-T^{JI}\\
A_5&\overline{\mathbf{15}}&(0,0,0,1,0)&\mathcal C_4&15:\ T_{IJ}=-T_{JI}\\\hline
A_4&\mathbf5&(1,0,0,0)&\mathcal C_1&5:\ v^i\\
A_4&\overline{\mathbf5}&(0,0,0,1)&\mathcal C_4&5:\ v_i\\
A_4&\mathbf{10}&(0,1,0,0)&\mathcal C_2&10:\ T^{ij}=-T^{ji}\\
A_4&\overline{\mathbf{10}}&(0,0,1,0)&\mathcal C_3&10:\ T_{ij}=-T_{ji}
\end{array}}
$$

Here $1\leq I,J\leq6$ and $1\leq i,j\leq5$. Lower indices denote the [dual representation](../../../../dual-representation.md), equivalent to the [conjugate representation](../../../../conjugate-representation.md) for these [compact groups](../../../../compact-group.md). The [SU(6)](../../../../su-6.md) $\mathbf{15}$ in this question is an [exterior square](../../../../exterior-square.md); the [SU(5)](../../../../su-5.md) $\mathbf{15}$ in [solution](../../3/e/solution.md) is a [symmetric square](../../../../symmetric-square.md). The ambient [group](../../../../group-split.md) is essential to distinguish them.
