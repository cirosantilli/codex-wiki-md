<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use the [A5 root system](../../../../a5-root-system.md) with [simple roots](../../../../simple-root.md) $\alpha_i=e_i-e_{i+1}$ for $1\leq i\leq5$, in the hyperplane $\sum_{i=1}^6x_i=0$. The [Dynkin diagram](../../../../dynkin-diagram.md) is the five-node chain

$$
\underset{1}{\circ}\!-\!\underset{2}{\circ}\!-\!\underset{3}{\circ}\!-\!\underset{4}{\circ}\!-\!\underset{5}{\circ}.
$$

Its [Cartan matrix](../../../../cartan-matrix.md) is

$$
\boxed{A_5=\begin{pmatrix}
2&-1&0&0&0\\-1&2&-1&0&0\\0&-1&2&-1&0\\0&0&-1&2&-1\\0&0&0&-1&2
\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

Deleting the final node leaves the [A4 root system](../../../../a4-root-system.md), with [simple roots](../../../../simple-root.md) $\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_4$. Concretely, the [Lie algebra homomorphism](../../../../lie-algebra-homomorphism.md) $\mathfrak{sl}_5(\mathbb C)\hookrightarrow\mathfrak{sl}_6(\mathbb C)$ sends $X$ to $\operatorname{diag}(X,0)$ and preserves the [Lie bracket](../../../../lie-bracket.md). This realizes the required [Lie subalgebra](../../../../lie-subalgebra.md) and fixes the embedding used below.
