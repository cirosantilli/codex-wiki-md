<h1 id="4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For the embedding in [solution](../a/solution.md), restriction retains the first four [Dynkin label](../../../../dynkin-label.md) coordinates, since the [simple coroots](../../../../simple-coroot.md) of $A_4$ are the first four of $A_5$. In a coordinate-free version of the same projection, let $\eta_i$ be the five defining [weights of a representation](../../../../weight-of-a-representation.md) of $A_4$. Restriction gives $\varepsilon_i\mapsto\eta_i$ for $1\leq i\leq5$, while $\varepsilon_6\mapsto0$.

For the [fundamental representation](../../../../fundamental-representation.md), its first five [weights of a representation](../../../../weight-of-a-representation.md) therefore form a $\mathbf5$ and the sixth is the zero [weight of a representation](../../../../weight-of-a-representation.md) of a [trivial representation](../../../../trivial-representation.md). Negating the [weights of a representation](../../../../weight-of-a-representation.md) gives the [conjugate representation](../../../../conjugate-representation.md):

$$
\boxed{\mathbf6\downarrow_{SU(5)}=\mathbf5\oplus\mathbf1,\qquad
\overline{\mathbf6}\downarrow_{SU(5)}=\overline{\mathbf5}\oplus\mathbf1.}
$$

For the [two-index antisymmetric representation](../../../../two-index-antisymmetric-representation.md), split the fifteen [weights of a representation](../../../../weight-of-a-representation.md) into the ten with $i<j\leq5$ and the five with $j=6$. Their restricted values are $\eta_i+\eta_j$ and $\eta_i$, respectively. These are precisely the [weight systems](../../../../weight-system.md) of $\mathbf{10}$ and $\mathbf5$. Negating again gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbf{15}\downarrow_{SU(5)}=\mathbf{10}\oplus\mathbf5,\qquad
\overline{\mathbf{15}}\downarrow_{SU(5)}=\overline{\mathbf{10}}\oplus\overline{\mathbf5}.}
$$

For instance the highest $A_5$ [weight of a representation](../../../../weight-of-a-representation.md) $(0,1,0,0,0)$ restricts to $(0,1,0,0)$ for the $\mathbf{10}$, while $(1,0,0,0,-1)$ restricts to $(1,0,0,0)$ for the $\mathbf5$. The restricted [weight systems](../../../../weight-system.md) are disjoint unions of multiplicity-one sets in these cases, and their dimensions check as $6=5+1$ and $15=10+5$.
