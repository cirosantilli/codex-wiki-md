<h1 id="4/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

One left-handed [Standard Model](../../../../standard-model-split.md) family requires the [SU(5)](../../../../su-5.md) [representation](../../../../group-representation.md) content $\mathbf{10}\oplus\overline{\mathbf5}$. No single [irreducible representation](../../../../irreducible-representation.md) in [solution](../b/solution.md) contains both. At the level of containing the required fields, the smallest choice is

$$
\boxed{\mathbf{15}\oplus\overline{\mathbf6}\downarrow
=(\mathbf{10}\oplus\overline{\mathbf5})\oplus\mathbf5\oplus\mathbf1.}
$$

The additional [SU(5)](../../../../su-5.md) multiplets are a $\mathbf5$ and a [trivial representation](../../../../trivial-representation.md). Another minimal choice using only two of the listed [irreducible representations](../../../../irreducible-representation.md) is

$$
\mathbf{15}\oplus\overline{\mathbf{15}}\downarrow
=(\mathbf{10}\oplus\overline{\mathbf5})\oplus(\overline{\mathbf{10}}\oplus\mathbf5).
$$

Its additional multiplets form the [conjugate representation](../../../../conjugate-representation.md) of a family. More generally, for multiplicities $(m_6,m_{\bar6},m_{15},m_{\overline{15}})$, [branching of a representation](../../../../branching-of-a-representation.md) gives

$$
(m_6+m_{15})\mathbf5\oplus(m_{\bar6}+m_{\overline{15}})\overline{\mathbf5}
\oplus m_{15}\mathbf{10}\oplus m_{\overline{15}}\overline{\mathbf{10}}
\oplus(m_6+m_{\bar6})\mathbf1.
$$

Thus containing a family requires $m_{15}\geq1$ and $m_{\bar6}+m_{\overline{15}}\geq1$.

For a consistent chiral [grand unified theory](../../../../grand-unified-theory.md), containment alone is insufficient. Normalize the cubic [gauge anomaly](../../../../gauge-anomaly.md) coefficient by $A(\mathbf6)=1$. For the [special unitary group](../../../../special-unitary-group.md) $SU(N)$, $A(\Lambda^2\mathbf N)=N-4$, so for [SU(6)](../../../../su-6.md) it is $2$. To verify this directly, take a traceless diagonal generator with entries $x_i$: its [exterior square](../../../../exterior-square.md) has entries $x_i+x_j$, and

$$
\sum_{i<j}(x_i+x_j)^3=(N-1)\sum_i x_i^3+3\sum_{i\ne j}x_i^2x_j
=(N-4)\sum_i x_i^3.
$$

The [conjugate representation](../../../../conjugate-representation.md) reverses this cubic [gauge anomaly](../../../../gauge-anomaly.md). The combination $\mathbf{15}\oplus\overline{\mathbf6}$ has coefficient $2-1=1$, so cannot alone be a quantum-consistent chiral [SU(6)](../../../../su-6.md) [gauge theory](../../../../gauge-theory.md). The minimal anomaly-free choice with one net [chiral gauge spectrum](../../../../chiral-gauge-spectrum.md) of a family is instead

$$
\boxed{\mathbf{15}\oplus2\overline{\mathbf6}\downarrow
=(\mathbf{10}\oplus\overline{\mathbf5})\oplus(\mathbf5\oplus\overline{\mathbf5})\oplus2\mathbf1.}
$$

The additional nonsinglet fields form a [vectorlike gauge spectrum](../../../../vectorlike-gauge-spectrum.md) that can acquire an [SU(5)](../../../../su-5.md)-invariant mass, and the two [gauge singlets](../../../../gauge-singlet.md) may serve as sterile [neutrinos](../../../../neutrino.md). More generally, precisely one net [chiral gauge spectrum](../../../../chiral-gauge-spectrum.md) of a family with vectorlike extra nonsinglets requires $m_{15}-m_{\overline{15}}=1$ and $m_{\bar6}-m_6=2$; these conditions also cancel the cubic [SU(6)](../../../../su-6.md) [gauge anomaly](../../../../gauge-anomaly.md). The $\mathbf{15}\oplus\overline{\mathbf{15}}$ choice above is anomaly-free but has zero net [chirality](../../../../chirality-physics.md).
