<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The squares of $I,-I,\pm A,\pm B$ are $I$, whereas $(\pm C)^2=-I$. Therefore $\chi_\rho(g^2)=(2,2,2,2,-2)$. The [symmetric square](../../../../symmetric-square.md) and [exterior square](../../../../exterior-square.md) formulas from [solution](../a/solution.md) give

$$
\boxed{\chi_{S^2\rho}=(3,3,1,1,-1),\qquad
\chi_{\wedge^2\rho}=(1,1,-1,-1,1).}
$$

Comparison with the [character table](../../../../character-table.md) gives the [Clebsch-Gordan decomposition of group representations](../../../../clebsch-gordan-decomposition-of-group-representations.md)

$$
\boxed{S^2\rho=L_{++}\oplus L_{+-}\oplus L_{-+},\qquad
\wedge^2\rho=L_{--}.}
$$

For explicit [bases](../../../../basis.md), put $e_1=(1,0)^T,e_2=(0,1)^T$. The [symmetric tensors](../../../../symmetric-tensor.md) $e_1\otimes e_1+e_2\otimes e_2$, $e_1\otimes e_2+e_2\otimes e_1$, and $e_1\otimes e_1-e_2\otimes e_2$ transform as $L_{++},L_{+-},L_{-+}$ respectively. The [antisymmetric tensor](../../../../antisymmetric-tensor.md) $e_1\otimes e_2-e_2\otimes e_1$ transforms as $L_{--}$, also the [determinant representation](../../../../determinant-representation.md) of $\rho$. Adding these [direct sums](../../../../direct-sum.md) recovers $\rho\otimes\rho=\bigoplus_{s,t}L_{st}$.
