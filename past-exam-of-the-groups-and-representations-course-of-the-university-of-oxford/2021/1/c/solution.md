<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Use the [conjugacy class](../../../../conjugacy-class.md) order of [solution](../b/solution.md). The defining [representation](../../../../group-representation.md) has [character](../../../../character-of-a-representation.md)

$$
\boxed{\chi_\rho=(2,-2,0,0,0).}
$$

Its [character inner product](../../../../character-inner-product.md) with itself is $(4+4)/8=1$, proving that it is an [irreducible representation](../../../../irreducible-representation.md). There is also a direct [invariant subspace](../../../../invariant-subspace.md) argument: $B$ has two distinct coordinate [eigenspaces](../../../../eigenspace.md), and $A$ exchanges them, so no line is invariant under both.

Let $L_{st}$ denote the [one-dimensional representation](../../../../one-dimensional-representation.md) with $L_{st}(A)=s$ and $L_{st}(B)=t$. Then $L_{st}(-I)=1$ and $L_{st}(C)=st$. The complete [character table](../../../../character-table.md) is

$$
\boxed{\begin{array}{c|rrrrr}
&I&-I&\pm A&\pm B&\pm C\\\hline
\text{class size}&1&1&2&2&2\\\hline
L_{++}&1&1&1&1&1\\
L_{+-}&1&1&1&-1&-1\\
L_{-+}&1&1&-1&1&-1\\
L_{--}&1&1&-1&-1&1\\
\rho&2&-2&0&0&0
\end{array}}
$$

The four scalar rows obey [character orthogonality](../../../../character-orthogonality.md), and the [dimensions](../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) exhaust the [group order](../../../../order-of-a-finite-group.md) as established in [solution](../b/solution.md).
