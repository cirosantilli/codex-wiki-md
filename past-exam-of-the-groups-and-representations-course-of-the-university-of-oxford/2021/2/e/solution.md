<h1 id="2/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

After the [scalar field](../../../../scalar-field.md) obtains a [vacuum expectation value](../../../../vacuum-expectation-value.md) $h$, the [fermion mass matrix](../../../../fermion-mass-matrix.md) in a canonically normalized [basis](../../../../basis.md) is $M=h\lambda$. Physical [masses](../../../../mass.md) are its [singular values](../../../../singular-value.md), not arbitrary entries or signed [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md). Thus

$$
\boxed{m_e=m_\mu=|ha|,\qquad m_\tau=|hb|.}
$$

The independent [Yukawa coupling](../../../../yukawa-interaction.md) $b$ can fit the [tau lepton](../../../../tau-particle.md) [mass](../../../../mass.md) relative to the doublet scale, but the [electron](../../../../electron.md) and [muon](../../../../muon.md) are predicted to have equal [masses](../../../../mass.md). The values supplied by the paper instead give $m_\mu/m_e\simeq105.7/0.511\simeq207$. Consequently exact unbroken $S_3$ with this [representation](../../../../group-representation.md) assignment cannot reproduce all three [masses](../../../../mass.md). Complex phases of $a,b$, or changes of [basis](../../../../basis.md), cannot split the two equal [singular values](../../../../singular-value.md). Additional breaking of the [symmetry](../../../../symmetry-physics.md) or a different field content would be needed; the discrepancy cannot be removed merely by choosing different invariant [Yukawa couplings](../../../../yukawa-interaction.md).
