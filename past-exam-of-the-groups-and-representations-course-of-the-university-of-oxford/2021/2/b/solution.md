<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For [permutations](../../../../permutation.md) $\sigma,\tau$, the prescribed [linear maps](../../../../linear-map.md) satisfy

$$
R(\sigma)R(\tau)e_k=R(\sigma)e_{\tau(k)}=e_{\sigma(\tau(k))}=R(\sigma\tau)e_k.
$$

Since this holds on a [basis](../../../../basis.md), $R(\sigma)R(\tau)=R(\sigma\tau)$. Also $R(e)=I$ and $R(\sigma)^{-1}=R(\sigma^{-1})$. Hence $R$ is a [representation](../../../../group-representation.md).

The [trace](../../../../matrix-trace.md) of a [permutation matrix](../../../../permutation-matrix.md) counts its fixed coordinate [basis vectors](../../../../basis-vector.md). There are respectively $3,1,0$ fixed vectors on the three [conjugacy classes](../../../../conjugacy-class.md), so $\chi_R=(3,1,0)=\chi_{\mathbf1}+\chi_D$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{R=\mathbf1\oplus D.}
$$

In particular the [sign representation](../../../../sign-representation.md) is absent.
