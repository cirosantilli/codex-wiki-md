<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

An unbroken [subgroup](../../../../subgroup.md) $H$ must fix the [vacuum expectation value](../../../../vacuum-expectation-value.md) itself. Thus a nonzero $H$-invariant [vacuum expectation value](../../../../vacuum-expectation-value.md) can exist only if the [restriction of a representation](../../../../restriction-of-a-representation.md) to $H$ contains a [trivial representation](../../../../trivial-representation.md). Applying the [singlet criterion for an unbroken subgroup](../../../../singlet-criterion-for-an-unbroken-subgroup.md) to [solution](../b/solution.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\begin{array}{c|cc}
R&\text{can preserve }H_2&\text{can preserve }H_3\\\hline
\mathbf4&\text{no}&\text{yes}\\
\mathbf6&\text{yes}&\text{no}\\
\mathbf{15}&\text{yes}&\text{yes}
\end{array}}
$$

The two “no” entries are forbidden for every nonzero [vacuum expectation value](../../../../vacuum-expectation-value.md). Each “yes” means that the indicated [subgroup](../../../../subgroup.md) can remain unbroken, subject to the [scalar potential](../../../../scalar-potential.md) choosing such a vacuum. It does **not** by itself prove that the indicated [subgroup](../../../../subgroup.md) is the entire [stabilizer](../../../../stabilizer-subgroup.md).

For exact [spontaneous symmetry breaking](../../../../spontaneous-symmetry-breaking.md) to the stated [subgroup](../../../../subgroup.md), the distinction matters. A complex $\mathbf4$ admits an exact $H_3$ [stabilizer](../../../../stabilizer-subgroup.md); a complex $\mathbf6$ admits an exact $H_2$ [stabilizer](../../../../stabilizer-subgroup.md). A single $\mathbf{15}$ preserving $H_2$ or $H_3$ necessarily preserves an additional continuous $U(1)$, as computed in [solution](../d/solution.md), so neither exact endpoint is possible with that single field. Also, if the $\mathbf6$ [scalar field](../../../../scalar-field.md) is constrained to the [real structure of the SU4 exterior square](../../../../real-structure-of-the-su4-exterior-square.md), its nonzero [stabilizer](../../../../stabilizer-subgroup.md) is larger than $H_2$. The paper does not specify whether this reality constraint is imposed, so both cases are distinguished below.
