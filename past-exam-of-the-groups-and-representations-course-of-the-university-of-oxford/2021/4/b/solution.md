<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Under the two-block [subgroup](../../../../subgroup.md) $H_2$, the defining [vector space](../../../../vector-space-split.md) splits into its first and last two coordinates. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
\mathbf4\downarrow_{H_2}&=(\mathbf2,\mathbf1)\oplus(\mathbf1,\mathbf2),\\
\mathbf6\downarrow_{H_2}&=(\mathbf1,\mathbf1)^{\oplus2}\oplus(\mathbf2,\mathbf2),\\
\mathbf{15}\downarrow_{H_2}&=(\mathbf3,\mathbf1)\oplus(\mathbf1,\mathbf3)
\oplus(\mathbf2,\mathbf2)^{\oplus2}\oplus(\mathbf1,\mathbf1).
\end{aligned}}
$$

For the [exterior square](../../../../exterior-square.md), the [exterior square of a direct sum](../../../../exterior-square-of-a-direct-sum.md) identity gives two within-block [determinant representations](../../../../determinant-representation.md), each trivial for $SU(2)$, and the cross-block [tensor product](../../../../tensor-product.md) $(\mathbf2,\mathbf2)$. For the [Adjoint representation](../../../../adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md), use $\operatorname{End}(\mathbb C^4)-\mathbf1$: each diagonal two-by-two block contributes a scalar and a traceless triplet, while each off-diagonal block gives $(\mathbf2,\mathbf2)$ because the [SU(2)](../../../../su-2-group.md) doublet is self-dual. Removing the overall [trace](../../../../matrix-trace.md) leaves one scalar. The [dimension](../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) counts are $2+2=4$, $1+1+4=6$, and $3+3+4+4+1=15$.

Under $H_3$, the first three coordinates carry the defining [SU(3)](../../../../su-3-group.md) [representation](../../../../group-representation.md) and the fourth is fixed. Hence

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
\mathbf4\downarrow_{H_3}&=\mathbf3\oplus\mathbf1,\\
\mathbf6\downarrow_{H_3}&=\overline{\mathbf3}\oplus\mathbf3,\\
\mathbf{15}\downarrow_{H_3}&=\mathbf8\oplus\mathbf3\oplus\overline{\mathbf3}\oplus\mathbf1.
\end{aligned}}
$$

Indeed $\wedge^2(\mathbf3\oplus\mathbf1)=\wedge^2\mathbf3\oplus\mathbf3=\overline{\mathbf3}\oplus\mathbf3$. The upper three-by-three block of a traceless [matrix](../../../../matrix.md) supplies the [Adjoint representation](../../../../adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md) $\mathbf8$ and one scalar, while the upper-right and lower-left blocks give $\mathbf3$ and $\overline{\mathbf3}$. This establishes the [branching of a representation](../../../../branching-of-a-representation.md) in each case without a dimension-only guess.
