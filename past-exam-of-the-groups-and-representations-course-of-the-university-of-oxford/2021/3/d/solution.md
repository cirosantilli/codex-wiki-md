<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [highest root](../../../../highest-root.md) is $\theta=2\alpha_1+3\alpha_2$. In the [Extended Dynkin diagram](../../../../extended-dynkin-diagram.md), the extra node $-\theta$ is long and is joined by a single bond to the long node $\alpha_1$; it is orthogonal to the short node $\alpha_2$. Deleting the short node therefore leaves the [A2 root system](../../../../a2-root-system.md) [Dynkin diagram](../../../../dynkin-diagram.md). Equivalently, the six long [roots](../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md) are a closed [root subsystem](../../../../root-subsystem.md), so their [root spaces](../../../../root-space.md) together with the [Cartan subalgebra](../../../../cartan-subalgebra.md) form an [A2 root system](../../../../a2-root-system.md) [Lie subalgebra](../../../../lie-subalgebra.md). The corresponding compact [subgroup](../../../../subgroup.md) is $SU(3)\subset G_2$.

For the particular projection in the paper, choose the [A2 root system](../../../../a2-root-system.md) [simple roots](../../../../simple-root.md) as $\beta_1=\alpha_1+3\alpha_2$ and $\beta_2=\alpha_1$. Since these are long, $\beta_1^\vee=\alpha_1^\vee+\alpha_2^\vee$ and $\beta_2^\vee=\alpha_1^\vee$. Consequently the [Dynkin labels](../../../../dynkin-label.md) restrict as

$$
\boxed{(a,b)^T\longmapsto P(a,b)^T=(a+b,a)^T,\qquad
P=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

Projecting the six short [roots](../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md) gives $\pm(1,0),\pm(0,1),\pm(1,-1)$. Group these into the three [weights](../../../../weight-representation-theory.md) of $\mathbf3$ and the three of $\overline{\mathbf3}$ listed in [solution](../a/solution.md). The zero [weight](../../../../weight-representation-theory.md) of $\mathbf7$ is left over, so

$$
\boxed{\mathbf7\downarrow_{SU(3)}=\mathbf3\oplus\overline{\mathbf3}\oplus\mathbf1.}
$$

The three positive long [roots](../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md) project as $(2,-3)\mapsto(-1,2)$, $(-1,3)\mapsto(2,-1)$ and $(1,0)\mapsto(1,1)$. Together with their negatives and the two zero [weights](../../../../weight-representation-theory.md), these are exactly the [Adjoint representation](../../../../adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md) $\mathbf8$. The remaining six short [roots](../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md) again give $\mathbf3\oplus\overline{\mathbf3}$. Hence the [branching of a representation](../../../../branching-of-a-representation.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathbf{14}\downarrow_{SU(3)}=\mathbf8\oplus\mathbf3\oplus\overline{\mathbf3}.}
$$
