<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For an [A2 root system](../../../../a2-root-system.md) [weight](../../../../weight-representation-theory.md) $w=(w_1,w_2)^T$, the specified [electric charge](../../../../electric-charge-split.md) and [flavour hypercharge](../../../../flavor-hypercharge.md) are the linear functionals

$$
q(w)=\frac{2w_1+w_2}{3},\qquad y(w)=\frac{w_1+2w_2}{3}.
$$

Apply these to the [meson octet](../../../../meson-octet.md) [weights](../../../../weight-representation-theory.md) in [solution](../a/solution.md). The results, with [electric charges](../../../../electric-charge-split.md) measured in units of the positive elementary [electric charge](../../../../electric-charge-split.md), are

$$
\boxed{\begin{array}{c|rr|c}
w&q&y&\text{pseudoscalar octet member}\\\hline
(2,-1)&1&0&\pi^+\\
(-2,1)&-1&0&\pi^-\\
(1,1)&1&1&K^+\\
(-1,2)&0&1&K^0\\
(-1,-1)&-1&-1&K^-\\
(1,-2)&0&-1&\bar K^0\\
(0,0)\text{ twice}&0&0&\pi^0,\eta_8
\end{array}}
$$

The table illustrates the [meson octet](../../../../meson-octet.md) of [pseudoscalar mesons](../../../../pseudoscalar-meson.md); the same [weight](../../../../weight-representation-theory.md) calculation applies to another [meson](../../../../meson.md) species arranged in the same [representation](../../../../group-representation.md). Here [flavour hypercharge](../../../../flavor-hypercharge.md) is $Y=B+S$, with $B$ the [baryon number](../../../../baryon-number.md) and $S$ the [strangeness](../../../../strangeness.md), rather than [weak hypercharge](../../../../hypercharge.md). If the full [quark model](../../../../quark-model.md) [tensor product](../../../../tensor-product.md) $\mathbf3\otimes\overline{\mathbf3}$ is retained, its additional [trivial representation](../../../../trivial-representation.md) is a ninth [meson](../../../../meson.md) with $(q,y)=(0,0)$. This singlet is separate from the octet and need not share its [mass](../../../../mass.md).
