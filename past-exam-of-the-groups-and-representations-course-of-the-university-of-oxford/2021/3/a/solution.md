<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use [Dynkin labels](../../../../dynkin-label.md) $w_i=\langle w,\alpha_i^\vee\rangle$, so the rows of the [Cartan matrix](../../../../cartan-matrix.md) are the [simple roots](../../../../simple-root.md) expressed in [fundamental weight](../../../../fundamental-weight.md) coordinates. For the [A2 root system](../../../../a2-root-system.md),

$$
\boxed{A_{A_2}=\begin{pmatrix}2&-1\\-1&2\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\alpha_1=(2,-1),\quad\alpha_2=(-1,2).}
$$

Starting at the [highest weight](../../../../highest-weight-of-a-representation.md) $(1,0)$ and subtracting successive allowed [simple roots](../../../../simple-root.md) gives $(1,0)\to(-1,1)\to(0,-1)$. Negating these [weights](../../../../weight-representation-theory.md) gives the [dual representation](../../../../dual-representation.md). Hence the two fundamental [weight systems](../../../../weight-system.md) are

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
\mathbf3=(1,0):&\quad (1,0),\ (-1,1),\ (0,-1),\\
\overline{\mathbf3}=(0,1):&\quad (0,1),\ (1,-1),\ (-1,0).
\end{aligned}}
$$

Each listed [weight](../../../../weight-representation-theory.md) has [weight multiplicity](../../../../weight-multiplicity.md) one. The [highest weight](../../../../highest-weight-of-a-representation.md) $(1,1)=\alpha_1+\alpha_2$ is the [highest root](../../../../highest-root.md), so its [representation](../../../../group-representation.md) is the [Adjoint representation](../../../../adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md). Its nonzero [weights](../../../../weight-representation-theory.md) are the six [roots](../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md), while the [Cartan subalgebra](../../../../cartan-subalgebra.md) supplies the zero [weight](../../../../weight-representation-theory.md) twice:

$$
\boxed{\mathbf8=(1,1):\quad
\pm(2,-1),\quad\pm(-1,2),\quad\pm(1,1),\quad(0,0)^{[2]}.}
$$

The superscript $[2]$ indicates [weight multiplicity](../../../../weight-multiplicity.md). This also follows from $\mathbf3\otimes\overline{\mathbf3}=\mathbf8\oplus\mathbf1$: the nine [tensor product](../../../../tensor-product.md) [weights](../../../../weight-representation-theory.md) comprise six differences of distinct fundamental [weights](../../../../weight-representation-theory.md) and three zeros, one zero belonging to the [trivial representation](../../../../trivial-representation.md).
