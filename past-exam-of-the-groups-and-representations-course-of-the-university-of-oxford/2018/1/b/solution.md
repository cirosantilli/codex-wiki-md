↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The additive [cyclic group](../../../../cyclic-group.md) $\mathbb Z_n$ is generated by $1$, with $n\cdot1=0$. Every complex [irreducible representation](../../../../irreducible-representation.md) is one-dimensional by [a](../a.md). If $R(1)=z$, the defining relation requires $z^n=1$. Conversely each such [root of unity](../../../../root-of-unity.md) defines a [group homomorphism](../../../../group-homomorphism.md). Hence the complete list is

$$
\boxed{\rho_k(g)=\exp\!\left(\frac{2\pi i kg}{n}\right),\qquad k=0,\ldots,n-1,\quad g\in\mathbb Z_n.}
$$

Changing the representative of $g$ changes the exponent by an integer multiple of $2\pi i$, so the formula is well-defined. These [representations](../../../../group-representation.md) are pairwise inequivalent because their values on the generator differ. The case $k=0$ is the [trivial representation](../../../../trivial-representation.md).
