<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The $x$ axis is an [invariant subspace](../../../../invariant-subspace.md) on which $r$ acts as $+1$ and $s$ as $-1$. The $yz$ plane carries the [irreducible representation](../../../../irreducible-representation.md) $E$ from [b](../b.md). Thus

$$
\boxed{R\cong\chi_{-,+}\oplus E,\qquad\chi_R=(3,-1,-1,1,-1).}
$$

The [character of a tensor product](../../../../character-of-a-tensor-product.md) is the product of the [characters](../../../../character-of-a-representation.md), so $\chi_{R\otimes R}=(9,1,1,1,1)$. Weighted [character inner products](../../../../character-inner-product.md) give

$$
\boxed{R\otimes R\cong
2\chi_{+,+}\oplus\chi_{+,-}\oplus\chi_{-,+}\oplus\chi_{-,-}\oplus2E.}
$$

For the trivial [character](../../../../character-of-a-representation.md), the multiplicity is $(9+1+2+2+2)/8=2$. For any other one-dimensional [character](../../../../character-of-a-representation.md), the weighted sum over the nonidentity classes is $-1$, giving $(9-1)/8=1$. For $E$, it is $(9\cdot2-2)/8=2$.

Equivalently, $E\otimes E$ is the sum of all four one-dimensional [representations](../../../../group-representation.md), and tensoring $E$ by $\chi_{-,+}$ gives the same two-dimensional [irreducible representation](../../../../irreducible-representation.md). The [dimensions](../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) in the boxed decomposition sum to nine, as required.
