<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The defining [SU(3)](../../../../su-3-group.md) [representation](../../../../group-representation.md) restricts to the given [matrix representation](../../../../matrix-representation.md). All its restricted [matrices](../../../../matrix.md) are real, so the [conjugate representation](../../../../conjugate-representation.md) restricts identically. Hence the first two [branchings](../../../../branching-rule.md) are

$$
\boxed{\mathbf3\downarrow_G=\chi_{-,+}\oplus E,\qquad
\overline{\mathbf3}\downarrow_G=\chi_{-,+}\oplus E.}
$$

For the [adjoint representation of SU(3)](../../../../adjoint-representation-of-su-3.md), use $\mathbf3\otimes\overline{\mathbf3}=\mathbf1\oplus\mathbf8$. This follows by splitting endomorphisms into scalar and traceless [matrices](../../../../matrix.md). Subtracting one trivial constituent from [c](../c.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbf8\downarrow_G=
\chi_{+,+}\oplus\chi_{+,-}\oplus\chi_{-,+}\oplus\chi_{-,-}\oplus2E.}
$$

As an independent check, its restricted [character](../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) is $|\chi_R|^2-1=(8,0,0,0,0)$, the [regular character](../../../../regular-character.md) of $G$. Thus this is precisely the [regular representation](../../../../regular-representation.md), whose [representation multiplicities](../../../../multiplicity-of-an-irreducible-representation.md) equal the corresponding [dimensions](../../../../dimension-vector-space.md).
