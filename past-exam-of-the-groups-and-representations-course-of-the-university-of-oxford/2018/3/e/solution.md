<h1 id="3/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The spinor [basis](../../../../basis.md) in [c](../c.md) is compatible with the supplied [gamma matrices](../../../../gamma-matrices.md). The rotation generator $r(J)$ is anti-Hermitian and commutes with $\gamma_0=-i\sigma_1$. The two boost generators are [Hermitian matrices](../../../../hermitian-operator.md) and anticommute with $\gamma_0$. It follows separately for all three generators, and hence for every real $T\in\mathfrak{so}(1,2)$, that

$$
\boxed{r(T)^\dagger\gamma_0+\gamma_0r(T)=0.}
$$

For the infinitesimal [Lorentz transformation](../../../../lorentz-transformation.md), $\delta\psi=r(T)\psi$ and $\delta\psi^\dagger=\psi^\dagger r(T)^\dagger$. Hence the [Dirac adjoint](../../../../dirac-adjoint.md) transforms contragrediently:

$$
\delta\overline\psi=\psi^\dagger r(T)^\dagger\gamma_0
=-\psi^\dagger\gamma_0r(T)=-\overline\psi\,r(T).
$$

The two contributions to the scalar variation cancel:

$$
\boxed{\delta(\overline\psi\psi)
=(\delta\overline\psi)\psi+\overline\psi\,\delta\psi=0.}
$$

This proves the [spinor invariant in three spacetime dimensions](../../../../spinor-invariant-in-three-spacetime-dimensions.md) has infinitesimal [Lorentz invariance](../../../../lorentz-invariance.md). Real transformation parameters matter because the [Hermitian adjoint](../../../../adjoint-operator.md) conjugates coefficients; this is not an invariance under arbitrary complexified parameters. For ordinary commuting spinor components, the bilinear is purely imaginary in this mostly-plus convention, and multiplying it by $i$ gives a real invariant. Its reality is separate from its invariance.
