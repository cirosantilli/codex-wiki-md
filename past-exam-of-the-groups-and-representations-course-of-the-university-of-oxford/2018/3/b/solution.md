<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In the [complexification of a Lie algebra](../../../../complexification-of-a-lie-algebra.md), define

$$
L_1=iK_1,\qquad L_2=iK_2,\qquad L_3=J.
$$

The relations in [a](../a.md) become

$$
[L_1,L_2]=L_3,\qquad[L_2,L_3]=L_1,\qquad[L_3,L_1]=L_2.
$$

The [special unitary Lie algebra](../../../../special-unitary-lie-algebra.md) $\mathfrak{su}(2)$ has real [basis](../../../../basis.md) $S_i=-i\sigma_i/2$, where the [Pauli matrices](../../../../pauli-matrices.md) satisfy $[\sigma_i,\sigma_j]=2i\epsilon_{ijk}\sigma_k$. Its [commutators](../../../../commutator.md) are the same: $[S_i,S_j]=\epsilon_{ijk}S_k$. The complex-linear map $L_i\mapsto S_i$ therefore preserves the bracket and maps a [basis](../../../../basis.md) to a [basis](../../../../basis.md). Consequently

$$
\boxed{\mathfrak{so}(1,2)_{\mathbb C}\cong\mathfrak{su}(2)_{\mathbb C}
\cong\mathfrak{sl}_2(\mathbb C).}
$$

The real [Lie algebras](../../../../lie-algebra-split.md) are not isomorphic: multiplying the boosts by $i$ changes their real form. For example, the [Adjoint representation of a Lie algebra](../../../../adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md) evaluated at $K_1$ has real [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md) $0,1,-1$, whereas the corresponding nonzero compact [SU(2)](../../../../su-2-group.md) generators have purely imaginary adjoint [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md).

Every complex [Lie algebra representation](../../../../lie-algebra-representation.md) of the real algebra extends uniquely by complex linearity to its [complexification of a Lie algebra](../../../../complexification-of-a-lie-algebra.md). The finite-dimensional complex [irreducible representations](../../../../irreducible-representation.md) are therefore classified by a nonnegative integer [highest weight](../../../../highest-weight-of-a-representation.md) $m$, just as for [SU(2)](../../../../su-2-group.md). There is one equivalence class for each

$$
\boxed{m=0,1,2,\ldots,\qquad \dim V_m=m+1=2j+1,\quad j=m/2.}
$$

These are statements about [Lie algebra representations](../../../../lie-algebra-representation.md). They do not assert positive-definite [unitarity](../../../../unitary-operator.md) for the noncompact real form, or automatic descent to the full [Lorentz group](../../../../lorentz-group.md). In particular, half-integer $j$ gives a sign under a $2\pi$ spatial [rotation](../../../../rotation-mathematics.md), so its integration needs a covering [group](../../../../group-split.md).
