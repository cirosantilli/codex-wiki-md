<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For $z\in U(1)$ and $U\in SU(n)$, the [matrix](../../../../matrix.md) $zU$ is unitary, since $(zU)^\dagger(zU)=|z|^2U^\dagger U=I_n$. Thus the map has its image in the [unitary group](../../../../unitary-group.md) $U(n)$. Scalar [matrices](../../../../matrix.md) commute with all [matrices](../../../../matrix.md), so the [direct product of groups](../../../../direct-product-of-groups.md) multiplication gives

$$
f((z,U)(w,V))=f(zw,UV)=zwUV=(zU)(wV)=f(z,U)f(w,V).
$$

Also $f(1,I_n)=I_n$. Hence

$$
\boxed{f:U(1)\times SU(n)\longrightarrow U(n),\quad(z,U)\longmapsto zU\text{ is a group homomorphism}.}
$$

The [special unitary group](../../../../special-unitary-group.md) condition $\det U=1$ imposes no requirement that $zU$ itself have unit [determinant](../../../../determinant.md); its [determinant](../../../../determinant.md) is $z^n$, as expected for a general element of the [unitary group](../../../../unitary-group.md).
