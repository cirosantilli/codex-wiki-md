<h1 id="3/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The first coordinate is fixed by the embedded [SU(5)](../../../../su-5.md), while the last five transform in its defining [representation](../../../../group-representation.md). Therefore $\mathbf6\downarrow_{SU(5)}=\mathbf1\oplus\mathbf5$, and taking the [dual representation](../../../../dual-representation.md) gives $\overline{\mathbf6}\downarrow_{SU(5)}=\mathbf1\oplus\overline{\mathbf5}$.

For the [symmetric square](../../../../symmetric-square.md), the fixed-fixed component is one [trivial representation](../../../../trivial-representation.md), the fixed-five mixed components give $\mathbf5$, and the symmetric five-five block is $\mathbf{15}$. For the [exterior square](../../../../exterior-square.md), the fixed-fixed component vanishes, the mixed components give $\mathbf5$, and the antisymmetric five-five block is $\mathbf{10}$. Finally restrict $\mathbf{35}=\mathbf6\otimes\overline{\mathbf6}-\mathbf1$, using $\mathbf5\otimes\overline{\mathbf5}=\mathbf{24}\oplus\mathbf1$.

The five [SU6 tensor branchings to SU5](../../../../su6-tensor-branchings-to-su5.md) are thus

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
\mathbf6&\downarrow=\mathbf5\oplus\mathbf1,\\
\overline{\mathbf6}&\downarrow=\overline{\mathbf5}\oplus\mathbf1,\\
\mathbf{21}&\downarrow=\mathbf{15}\oplus\mathbf5\oplus\mathbf1,\\
\mathbf{15}&\downarrow=\mathbf{10}\oplus\mathbf5,\\
\mathbf{35}&\downarrow=\mathbf{24}\oplus\mathbf5\oplus\overline{\mathbf5}\oplus\mathbf1.
\end{aligned}}
$$

The symbols $\mathbf{15}$ on the left and right refer to different [groups](../../../../group-split.md): the left-hand one is the [SU(6)](../../../../su-6.md) [exterior square](../../../../exterior-square.md), whereas the right-hand one is the [SU(5)](../../../../su-5.md) [symmetric square](../../../../symmetric-square.md). The [dimension](../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) checks are $6=5+1$, $21=15+5+1$, $15=10+5$ and $35=24+5+5+1$.

Explicitly, in the [Adjoint representation](../../../../adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md) the lower-right traceless five-by-five block gives $\mathbf{24}$; the lower-left column and upper-right row give $\mathbf5$ and $\overline{\mathbf5}$; and the diagonal direction $\operatorname{diag}(-5,1,1,1,1,1)$ gives the remaining [trivial representation](../../../../trivial-representation.md).
