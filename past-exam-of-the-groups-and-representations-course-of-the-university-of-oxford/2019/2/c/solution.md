<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [irreducible representations](../../../../irreducible-representation.md) can all be specified on the generators:

$$
\boxed{\begin{array}{c|cc}
&R(r)&R(s)\\\hline
\mathbf1&1&1\\
\varepsilon&1&-1\\
R_2&\begin{pmatrix}\alpha&0\\0&\alpha^2\end{pmatrix}&\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}
\end{array}}
$$

The first is the [trivial representation](../../../../trivial-representation.md) and the second is the [sign representation](../../../../sign-representation.md). To see directly that these exhaust the [one-dimensional representations](../../../../one-dimensional-representation.md), their scalar images commute, so the relation $srs=r^{-1}$ forces $r=r^{-1}$. Combining $r^2=1$ with $r^3=1$ gives $r=1$, and then $s$ may be either sign.

Taking [traces](../../../../matrix-trace.md) gives the [character table](../../../../character-table.md), in the [conjugacy class](../../../../conjugacy-class.md) order of [solution](../a/solution.md):

$$
\boxed{\begin{array}{c|rrr}
&I_2&r&s\\\hline
\text{class size}&1&2&3\\\hline
\mathbf1&1&1&1\\
\varepsilon&1&1&-1\\
R_2&2&-1&0
\end{array}}
$$

Here $\alpha+\alpha^2=-1$ gives the middle entry in the last row. The weighted [character inner products](../../../../character-inner-product.md) of distinct rows vanish, and the norm of each row is one. Together with [solution](../b/solution.md), this proves completeness of the table.
