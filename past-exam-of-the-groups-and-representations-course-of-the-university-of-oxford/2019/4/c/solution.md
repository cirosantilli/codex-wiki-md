<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let $r=V(0,1)$ be the four-dimensional [spinor representation](../../../../spin-representation.md). The [tensor product representation](../../../../tensor-product-of-group-representations.md) splits into its [symmetric square](../../../../symmetric-square.md) and [exterior square](../../../../exterior-square.md), with [dimensions](../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) ten and six. If $v$ is a [highest-weight vector](../../../../highest-weight-vector.md) of $r$, then $v\otimes v$ is a [highest-weight vector](../../../../highest-weight-vector.md) of [weight](../../../../weight-representation-theory.md) $(0,2)$ in the [symmetric square](../../../../symmetric-square.md). The [irreducible representation](../../../../irreducible-representation.md) with that [highest weight](../../../../highest-weight-of-a-representation.md) has [dimension](../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) ten by [solution](../b/solution.md), exhausting the [symmetric square](../../../../symmetric-square.md):

$$
S^2r\cong V(0,2)=\mathbf{10}.
$$

For the [exterior square](../../../../exterior-square.md), add each pair of distinct [weights](../../../../weight-representation-theory.md) from [solution](../b/solution.md). The resulting [multiset](../../../../multiset.md) of [weights](../../../../weight-representation-theory.md) is

$$
(1,0),\quad(-1,2),\quad(1,-2),\quad(-1,0),\quad(0,0)^{[2]}.
$$

Its maximal [weight](../../../../weight-representation-theory.md) $(1,0)$ is the [highest weight](../../../../highest-weight-of-a-representation.md) of a five-dimensional [irreducible representation](../../../../irreducible-representation.md). It accounts for the four nonzero [weights](../../../../weight-representation-theory.md) and one zero [weight](../../../../weight-representation-theory.md), leaving a single zero [weight](../../../../weight-representation-theory.md). By [complete reducibility of semisimple Lie algebra representations](../../../../weyl-s-theorem-on-complete-reducibility.md), that remaining one-dimensional summand is the [trivial representation](../../../../trivial-representation.md). Thus $\Lambda^2r\cong\mathbf5\oplus\mathbf1$, and the [spinor tensor square for so5](../../../../spinor-tensor-square-for-so5.md) is

$$
\boxed{r\otimes r\cong V(0,0)\oplus V(1,0)\oplus V(0,2)
=\mathbf1\oplus\mathbf5\oplus\mathbf{10}.}
$$

The [dimension](../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) check is $16=1+5+10$. The [trivial representation](../../../../trivial-representation.md) lies in the [exterior square](../../../../exterior-square.md), reflecting the invariant nondegenerate alternating [bilinear form](../../../../bilinear-form-split.md) of this [pseudoreal representation](../../../../pseudoreal-representation.md). Although $r$ itself does not descend to $SO(5)$, its [tensor square](../../../../tensor-square.md) does: the central element $-1\in\operatorname{Spin}(5)$ acts as $(-I_4)\otimes(-I_4)=I_{16}$.
