<h1 id="4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Choose the first $SU(2)$ as $SU_w(2)$ and embed $SU_c(3)$ in the upper-left three-by-three block of $SU(4)$. The [fundamental representations](../../../../fundamental-representation.md) then restrict as $\mathbf4\to\mathbf3\oplus\mathbf1$ and $\overline{\mathbf4}\to\overline{\mathbf3}\oplus\mathbf1$. Thus the [representation](../../../../group-representation.md) in [solution](../c/solution.md), forgetting [hypercharge](../../../../hypercharge.md), restricts to

$$
(\mathbf2,\mathbf3)\oplus(\mathbf2,\mathbf1)
\oplus2(\mathbf1,\overline{\mathbf3})\oplus2(\mathbf1,\mathbf1).
$$

This contains every required $SU_w(2)\times SU_c(3)$ [representation](../../../../group-representation.md), with one extra singlet.

For the [hypercharge](../../../../hypercharge.md) embedding, let $X=\operatorname{diag}(1,1,1,-3)$ in the $SU(4)$ fundamental [representation](../../../../group-representation.md), and $T_{3R}=\operatorname{diag}(1/2,-1/2)$ in the second $SU(2)$. These commute with $SU_w(2)\times SU_c(3)$. The integer normalization in the paper is

$$
\boxed{Y=X+6T_{3R}.}
$$

Explicitly, this [circle group](../../../../circle-group.md) embeds in the covering [group](../../../../group-split.md) by

$$
z\longmapsto\left(I_2,\begin{pmatrix}z^3&0\\0&z^{-3}\end{pmatrix},
\operatorname{diag}(z,z,z,z^{-3})\right).
$$

Both nontrivial blocks have determinant one, and the map is injective since the first three entries of the $SU(4)$ block recover $z$.

On $(\mathbf2,\mathbf1,\mathbf4)$, $T_{3R}=0$, so the [branching of a representation](../../../../branching-of-a-representation.md) gives $(\mathbf2,\mathbf3)_1\oplus(\mathbf2,\mathbf1)_{-3}$. On $(\mathbf1,\mathbf2,\overline{\mathbf4})$, the [U(1) charges](../../../../u-1-charge.md) under $X$ are $-1$ on $\overline{\mathbf3}$ and $3$ on $\mathbf1$, while $6T_{3R}$ takes values $\pm3$. Hence

$$
\begin{aligned}
(\mathbf1,\mathbf2,\overline{\mathbf4})&\longrightarrow
(\mathbf1,\overline{\mathbf3})_{-1+3}\oplus
(\mathbf1,\overline{\mathbf3})_{-1-3}\\
&\qquad\oplus(\mathbf1,\mathbf1)_{3+3}\oplus(\mathbf1,\mathbf1)_{3-3}.
\end{aligned}
$$

Combining the two summands gives

$$
\boxed{R\longrightarrow R_F\oplus(\mathbf1,\mathbf1)_0.}
$$

This is the [Pati-Salam charge embedding](../../../../pati-salam-charge-embedding.md), with $X=3(B-L)$. In terms of the common [Standard Model](../../../../standard-model-split.md) convention $Q_{\rm electric}=T_{3L}+Y_{\rm SM}$, the paper's integer [hypercharge](../../../../hypercharge.md) is $Y=6Y_{\rm SM}$.

The extra neutral singlet can be interpreted as the left-handed charge conjugate $\nu_R^c$ of a [right-handed neutrino](../../../../right-handed-neutrino.md). It is a [sterile neutrino](../../../../sterile-neutrino.md) under the [Standard Model](../../../../standard-model-split.md) [gauge group](../../../../gauge-group.md), and completes the sixteen [Weyl fermions](../../../../weyl-spinor.md) of one family in this [half-spin representation](../../../../half-spin-representation.md). Its neutrality permits a [Majorana mass term](../../../../majorana-mass-term.md) without violating the remaining [Standard Model](../../../../standard-model-split.md) [gauge group](../../../../gauge-group.md).
