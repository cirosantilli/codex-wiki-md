<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Schur's lemma](../../../../schur-s-lemma.md) states that an [intertwiner](../../../../intertwiner.md) $T:V\to W$ between [irreducible representations](../../../../irreducible-representation.md) is either zero or an [isomorphism](../../../../isomorphism.md). In particular, inequivalent [irreducible representations](../../../../irreducible-representation.md) have no nonzero [intertwiners](../../../../intertwiner.md). For a finite-dimensional [irreducible representation](../../../../irreducible-representation.md) over the [complex numbers](../../../../complex-number.md), every commuting [endomorphism](../../../../endomorphism.md) is scalar:

$$
\boxed{TR(g)=R(g)T\ \text{for all }g\quad\Longrightarrow\quad T=\lambda I.}
$$

Indeed, the [kernel](../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) and [image](../../../../image-of-a-function.md) of an [intertwiner](../../../../intertwiner.md) are [invariant subspaces](../../../../invariant-subspace.md), proving the first statement. For the second, choose an [eigenvalue](../../../../eigenvalue.md) $\lambda$ of $T$. The [intertwiner](../../../../intertwiner.md) $T-\lambda I$ has nonzero [kernel](../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md), so it must vanish. The scalar conclusion uses an algebraically closed coefficient [field](../../../../field.md): over the real numbers, planar rotation through $\pi/2$ gives an [irreducible](../../../../irreducible-representation.md) real [representation](../../../../group-representation.md) of $\mathbb Z_4$ and commutes with itself without being a real scalar [matrix](../../../../matrix.md).
