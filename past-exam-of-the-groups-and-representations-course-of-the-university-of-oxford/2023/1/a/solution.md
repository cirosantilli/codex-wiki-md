↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [subgroup](../../../../subgroup.md) $H$ of a [group](../../../../group-split.md) $G$ is a nonempty subset closed under the inherited multiplication and inverses; equivalently, $xy^{-1}\in H$ whenever $x,y\in H$. A [normal subgroup](../../../../normal-subgroup.md) satisfies $gHg^{-1}=H$ for every $g\in G$.

A [group representation](../../../../group-representation.md) on a [vector space](../../../../vector-space-split.md) $V$ is a [group homomorphism](../../../../group-homomorphism.md) $R:G\to\operatorname{GL}(V)$, so $R(gh)=R(g)R(h)$ and $R(e)=I_V$. An [irreducible representation](../../../../irreducible-representation.md) has $V\ne0$ and no [invariant subspaces](../../../../invariant-subspace.md) other than $0$ and $V$: there is no proper nonzero $W$ with $R(g)W\subseteq W$ for every $g\in G$. We use finite-dimensional [complex representations](../../../../complex-representation.md), with continuity for [Lie groups](../../../../lie-group.md), throughout.
