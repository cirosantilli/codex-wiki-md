<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let $\zeta=e^{2\pi i/n}$. The [group homomorphism](../../../../group-homomorphism.md) $\mathbb Z_n\to U(1)$ given by $[k]\mapsto\zeta^k$ is injective, identifying the [finite cyclic group](../../../../finite-cyclic-group.md) with the [subgroup](../../../../subgroup.md) of $n$th [roots of unity](../../../../root-of-unity.md) in the [circle group](../../../../circle-group.md).

The continuous [irreducible representations](../../../../irreducible-representation.md) of the [circle group](../../../../circle-group.md) are the [one-dimensional representations](../../../../one-dimensional-representation.md) $R_Q(e^{i\theta})=e^{iQ\theta}$ with $Q\in\mathbb Z$. One way to see this is to make the [representation](../../../../group-representation.md) [unitary](../../../../unitary-connection.md) by averaging, simultaneously diagonalize its commuting [unitary matrices](../../../../unitary-matrix.md), and impose $R_Q(e^{i(\theta+2\pi)})=R_Q(e^{i\theta})$. Its [restriction of a representation](../../../../restriction-of-a-representation.md) is

$$
R_Q(\zeta^k)=e^{2\pi iQk/n}=e^{2\pi iqk/n},\qquad
\boxed{q=Q\pmod n.}
$$

Thus each [irreducible representation](../../../../irreducible-representation.md) remains one-dimensional, and [representations](../../../../group-representation.md) whose integer labels differ by a multiple of $n$ become equivalent. Every [irreducible representation](../../../../irreducible-representation.md) of the [finite cyclic group](../../../../finite-cyclic-group.md) occurs this way.
