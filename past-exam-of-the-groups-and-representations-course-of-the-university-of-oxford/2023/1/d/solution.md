<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Choose a normalization in which the [circle group](../../../../circle-group.md) has period $2\pi$ and the [U(1) charges](../../../../u-1-charge.md) $\widehat Q_i$ of the [complex scalar fields](../../../../complex-scalar-field.md) are integers. By [restriction of a representation](../../../../restriction-of-a-representation.md),

$$
\boxed{\widehat q_i=\widehat Q_i\pmod n.}
$$

Write $v_i=\langle\phi_i\rangle$. A [vacuum expectation value](../../../../vacuum-expectation-value.md) $v_i\ne0$ is preserved by $e^{i\theta}$ precisely when $e^{i\widehat Q_i\theta}=1$. Therefore the specified [finite cyclic group](../../../../finite-cyclic-group.md) survives [spontaneous symmetry breaking](../../../../spontaneous-symmetry-breaking.md) exactly when every condensing [complex scalar field](../../../../complex-scalar-field.md) obeys

$$
\widehat Q_i\equiv0\pmod n.
$$

Only [U(1) charges](../../../../u-1-charge.md) of fields with nonzero [vacuum expectation values](../../../../vacuum-expectation-value.md) enter this test; in the paper all $N$ fields condense.

Suppose at least one condensing [U(1) charge](../../../../u-1-charge.md) is nonzero and put $d=\gcd(|\widehat Q_i|:v_i\ne0)$. Since each such [U(1) charge](../../../../u-1-charge.md) is a multiple of $d$, every $d$th [root of unity](../../../../root-of-unity.md) preserves the [vacuum expectation values](../../../../vacuum-expectation-value.md). Conversely, the [Bezout identity](../../../../bezout-s-identity.md) expresses $d$ as an integer combination of these [U(1) charges](../../../../u-1-charge.md), so a common stabilizer obeys $e^{id\theta}=1$. The [discrete remnant of a circle symmetry](../../../../discrete-remnant-of-a-circle-symmetry.md) is consequently

$$
\boxed{H_{\rm unbroken}=\mathbb Z_d,\qquad n_{\max}=d.}
$$

A nontrivial retained [finite cyclic group](../../../../finite-cyclic-group.md) exists exactly when $d>1$, and its possible orders are the divisors $n>1$ of $d$. For example, condensing [U(1) charges](../../../../u-1-charge.md) $6$ and $10$ leave $\mathbb Z_2$. If $d=1$, the [stabilizer](../../../../stabilizer-subgroup.md) is trivial. If all condensing [U(1) charges](../../../../u-1-charge.md) vanish, the entire [circle group](../../../../circle-group.md) is unbroken and there is no largest finite $n$; this case does not break the symmetry assumed in the question.
