↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

[Branching of a representation](../../../../branching-of-a-representation.md) describes the [irreducible representation](../../../../irreducible-representation.md) content of a [restriction of a representation](../../../../restriction-of-a-representation.md):

$$
R\big|_H\cong\bigoplus_\lambda m_\lambda S_\lambda.
$$

This direct decomposition applies when the [restriction of a representation](../../../../restriction-of-a-representation.md) is a [semisimple representation](../../../../semisimple-representation.md), as for [finite groups](../../../../finite-group.md) or continuous finite-dimensional [representations](../../../../group-representation.md) of [compact groups](../../../../compact-group.md). For an arbitrary [group](../../../../group-split.md) and [subgroup](../../../../subgroup.md), complete reducibility need not hold. A [vector subspace](../../../../vector-subspace.md) preserved by every $R(h)$ for $h\in H$ need not be preserved by the additional $R(g)$ for $g\notin H$, so an [irreducible representation](../../../../irreducible-representation.md) can become reducible.

For a concrete example, take the defining [representation](../../../../group-representation.md) of $G=SU(2)$ on $\mathbb C^2$ and $H=\{\operatorname{diag}(z,z^{-1}):|z|=1\}$, a [subgroup](../../../../subgroup.md) isomorphic to the [circle group](../../../../circle-group.md). The defining [representation](../../../../group-representation.md) is [irreducible](../../../../irreducible-representation.md): $SU(2)$ acts transitively on unit vectors, so no complex line is preserved by all its elements. Under $H$, the two coordinate lines are [invariant subspaces](../../../../invariant-subspace.md), and

$$
\boxed{\mathbf2\big|_{U(1)}=\mathbf1_{+1}\oplus\mathbf1_{-1}.}
$$

Here the two [one-dimensional representations](../../../../one-dimensional-representation.md) act by $z$ and $z^{-1}$.
