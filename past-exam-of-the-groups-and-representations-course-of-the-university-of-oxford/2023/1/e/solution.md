<h1 id="1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Normalize the [circle group](../../../../circle-group.md) as in [solution](../d/solution.md) and count all [Weyl fermions](../../../../weyl-spinor.md) as left-handed; a right-handed [Weyl fermion](../../../../weyl-spinor.md) is replaced by its left-handed charge conjugate. The [restriction of a representation](../../../../restriction-of-a-representation.md) gives

$$
\boxed{q_a=Q_a\pmod n.}
$$

The triangle coefficient of the [cubic U(1) gauge anomaly](../../../../cubic-u-1-gauge-anomaly.md) is proportional to $\sum_aQ_a^3$. In a theory with this single [gauge group](../../../../gauge-group.md), cancellation of its local pure [gauge anomaly](../../../../gauge-anomaly.md) requires

$$
\boxed{\sum_{a=1}^M Q_a^3=0.}
$$

If absence of the [mixed gauge-gravitational anomaly](../../../../mixed-gauge-gravitational-anomaly.md) is also required, as when coupling to a general gravitational background, one additionally needs $\sum_aQ_a=0$. Additional [gauge group](../../../../gauge-group.md) factors would require their mixed [gauge anomalies](../../../../gauge-anomaly.md) to cancel as well.

Choose integer representatives and write $Q_a=q_a+nk_a$. Expanding the cubic gives

$$
0=\sum_a Q_a^3
=\sum_aq_a^3+n\sum_a\bigl(3q_a^2k_a+3nq_ak_a^2+n^2k_a^3\bigr).
$$

Hence the inherited necessary congruence, and its gravitational counterpart when imposed, are

$$
\boxed{\sum_aq_a^3\equiv0\pmod n,\qquad
\sum_aq_a\equiv0\pmod n\quad\text{(if mixed gravitational cancellation is imposed)}.}
$$

These statements concern the same complete [fermion](../../../../fermion.md) spectrum used in the parent theory. They are consequences of an anomaly-free integer lift, rather than a full classification of intrinsic discrete [gauge anomalies](../../../../gauge-anomaly.md) or a sufficient test for such a lift. They also do not imply an ordinary equality for the chosen residues: for $n=3$, the anomaly-free [U(1) charges](../../../../u-1-charge.md) $(1,-1)$ have residues $(1,2)$, whose cubes sum to $9$, rather than zero. Integrating out heavy [fermions](../../../../fermion.md) can require extra contributions to the effective discrete [gauge anomaly](../../../../gauge-anomaly.md) accounting.
