<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [trivial representation](../../../../trivial-representation.md) and [sign of a permutation](../../../../sign-of-a-permutation.md) give

$$
R_0(\sigma)=1,\qquad R_1(\sigma)=\operatorname{sgn}(\sigma).
$$

They are [one-dimensional representations](../../../../one-dimensional-representation.md), hence [irreducible representations](../../../../irreducible-representation.md). Their [characters](../../../../character-of-a-representation.md), in the [conjugacy class](../../../../conjugacy-class.md) order of [solution](../a/solution.md), are

$$
\chi_0=(1,1,1,1,1),\qquad
\chi_1=(1,-1,1,1,-1).
$$

These are the only [one-dimensional representations](../../../../one-dimensional-representation.md). All transpositions are conjugate, so they have the same scalar image $t$ in any [one-dimensional representation](../../../../one-dimensional-representation.md); since a transposition squares to the identity, $t=\pm1$. Transpositions generate the [symmetric group](../../../../symmetric-group.md), fixing the whole [representation](../../../../group-representation.md) once $t$ is chosen.

By the [sum of squares of irreducible representation dimensions](../../../../sum-of-squares-of-irreducible-representation-dimensions.md), the three remaining dimensions satisfy $d_2^2+d_3^2+d_4^2=24-1-1=22$, with each $d_i\ge2$. No $d_i$ can be at least $4$, since $16+4+4>22$. Thus the only possible dimensions are

$$
\boxed{d_2=2,\qquad d_3=d_4=3.}
$$
