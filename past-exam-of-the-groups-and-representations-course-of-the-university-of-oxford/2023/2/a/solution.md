<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Conjugacy classes](../../../../conjugacy-class.md) in the [symmetric group](../../../../symmetric-group.md) are specified by [cycle type](../../../../cycle-type.md), equivalently a [partition](../../../../partition-of-an-integer.md) of $4$. If $m_j$ counts cycles of length $j$, the number of [permutations](../../../../permutation.md) of that [cycle type](../../../../cycle-type.md) is $4!/\prod_j(j^{m_j}m_j!)$: cyclic rotation of each cycle and interchange of equal-length cycles do not change the [permutation](../../../../permutation.md). This gives

$$
\begin{array}{c|ccccc}
\text{partition}&1^4&2\,1^2&2^2&3\,1&4\\\hline
\text{representative}&e&(12)&(12)(34)&(123)&(1234)\\
\text{class size}&1&6&3&8&6
\end{array}
$$

The sizes sum to $24$. Since the number of complex [irreducible representations](../../../../irreducible-representation.md) of a [finite group](../../../../finite-group.md) equals its number of [conjugacy classes](../../../../conjugacy-class.md), $S_4$ has $\boxed{5}$ inequivalent complex [irreducible representations](../../../../irreducible-representation.md).
