<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For the [A5 root system](../../../../a5-root-system.md), take [simple roots](../../../../simple-root.md) $\alpha_i=e_i-e_{i+1}$, $i=1,\ldots,5$, in the hyperplane $\sum_{i=1}^6x_i=0$. Their squared lengths are two, adjacent [simple roots](../../../../simple-root.md) have [inner product](../../../../inner-product-split.md) $-1$, and all other pairs are orthogonal. Hence the [An Dynkin diagram](../../../../an-dynkin-diagram.md) is the five-node single-edge chain

$$
\underset{1}{\circ}\! -\!\underset{2}{\circ}\! -\!\underset{3}{\circ}\! -\!\underset{4}{\circ}\! -\!\underset{5}{\circ}.
$$

Its [Cartan matrix](../../../../cartan-matrix.md) is

$$
\boxed{A(A_5)=\begin{pmatrix}
2&-1&0&0&0\\
-1&2&-1&0&0\\
0&-1&2&-1&0\\
0&0&-1&2&-1\\
0&0&0&-1&2
\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

The complex [Lie algebra](../../../../lie-algebra-split.md) is $\mathfrak{sl}_6(\mathbb C)$; the corresponding compact [special unitary group](../../../../special-unitary-group.md) is $SU(6)$, whose [Lie algebra](../../../../lie-algebra-split.md) is its compact real form.
