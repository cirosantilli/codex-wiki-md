<h1 id="1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Choose the doublet [bases](../../../../basis.md) so that the column of barred fields $B=(\bar\psi_L^1,\bar\psi_L^2)^T$ and $R=(\psi_R^1,\psi_R^2)^T$ both transform by the doublet [matrices](../../../../matrix.md) in [d](../d.md). This choice follows the source's assignment of the barred doublet itself. Since $H$ transforms in $\varepsilon$, its [Yukawa coupling](../../../../yukawa-interaction.md) must multiply a fermion bilinear in $\varepsilon$.

There is exactly one such bilinear in $\mathbf2\otimes\mathbf2$: the [Clebsch-Gordan coefficients](../../../../clebsch-gordan-coefficients.md) in [d](../d.md) give $B_1R_2-B_2R_1$. Equivalently, writing the upper block of the [Yukawa matrix](../../../../yukawa-matrix.md) as $A$, invariance requires

$$
D(r)^TAD(r)=A,\qquad D(s)^TAD(s)=-A.
$$

The first equation sets $A_{11}=A_{22}=0$, because $\alpha^2\ne1$; the second sets $A_{21}=-A_{12}$.

A mixed doublet-singlet bilinear remains a doublet after multiplication by $H$, so all four mixed entries vanish. Finally $\bar\psi_L^3\psi_R^3$ transforms in $\varepsilon\otimes\mathbf1=\varepsilon$, and multiplication by $H$ makes it invariant. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\lambda=\begin{pmatrix}0&a&0\\-a&0&0\\0&0&b\end{pmatrix},\qquad a,b\in\mathbb C.}
$$

The allowed [Yukawa interaction](../../../../yukawa-interaction.md) is $H\{a(\bar\psi_L^1\psi_R^2-\bar\psi_L^2\psi_R^1)+b\bar\psi_L^3\psi_R^3\}$, together with its [Hermitian conjugate](../../../../hermitian-conjugation.md) when forming a real [Lagrangian density](../../../../lagrangian-density.md).

The displayed texture depends on [basis](../../../../basis.md). If instead the unbarred left doublet is assigned $D$ in the same ordering as the right doublet, its barred doublet has the [dual representation](../../../../dual-representation.md) $D^*$ and the equivalent upper block is $a\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)$. Exchanging the two barred components converts between these descriptions. In either convention there are exactly two independent complex [Yukawa couplings](../../../../yukawa-interaction.md).
