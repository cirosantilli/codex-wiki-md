<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For any $h\in G$, left multiplication permutes the elements of the [finite group](../../../../finite-group.md). Consequently [averaging over a finite group](../../../../averaging-over-a-finite-group.md) gives

$$
R(h)Pv=\frac1{|G|}\sum_{g\in G}R(hg)v=\frac1{|G|}\sum_{k\in G}R(k)v=Pv.
$$

Thus $Pv$ belongs to the [fixed-point subspace of a group action](../../../../fixed-point-subspace-of-a-group-action.md) $V^G$; any nonzero such vector spans a [trivial representation](../../../../trivial-representation.md), or singlet. The zero vector is included in the invariance statement.

Conversely $Pw=w$ for every $w\in V^G$, so

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{im}P=V^G,\qquad P^2=P.}
$$

This is a [projection operator](../../../../projection-linear-algebra.md) onto all singlets, rather than necessarily onto a one-dimensional [vector subspace](../../../../vector-subspace.md). Its [trace](../../../../matrix-trace.md) is the singlet multiplicity $|G|^{-1}\sum_g\chi_R(g)$.
