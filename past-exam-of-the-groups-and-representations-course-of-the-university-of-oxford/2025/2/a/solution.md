<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a curve $O(t)=I+tX+O(t^2)$ in the [special orthogonal group](../../../../special-orthogonal-group.md), differentiate $O(t)^TO(t)=I$ at the identity. This gives $X^T+X=0$. Conversely, the [matrix exponential](../../../../matrix-exponential.md) of any real [skew-symmetric matrix](../../../../skew-symmetric-matrix.md) is orthogonal and has [determinant](../../../../determinant.md) $\det e^{tX}=e^{t\operatorname{tr}X}=1$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\mathfrak{so}(N)=\{X\in M_N(\mathbb R):X^T=-X\},\qquad [X,Y]=XY-YX.}
$$

A [basis](../../../../basis.md) is $L_{ij}=E_{ij}-E_{ji}$ for $1\le i<j\le N$, where $E_{ij}$ is a [matrix unit](../../../../matrix-unit.md). If $L_{ji}=-L_{ij}$ and $L_{ii}=0$, their [Lie brackets](../../../../lie-bracket.md) are

$$
[L_{ij},L_{kl}]=\delta_{jk}L_{il}-\delta_{jl}L_{ik}-\delta_{ik}L_{jl}+\delta_{il}L_{jk}.
$$

There are $\binom N2$ independent off-diagonal entries. A [maximal torus](../../../../maximal-torus.md) rotates independently in mutually orthogonal two-planes, generated by $L_{12},L_{34},\ldots$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\dim\mathfrak{so}(N)=\frac{N(N-1)}2,\qquad \operatorname{rank}\operatorname{SO}(N)=\left\lfloor\frac N2\right\rfloor.}
$$

The maximality follows by simultaneously decomposing commuting real [skew-symmetric matrices](../../../../skew-symmetric-matrix.md) into rotation two-planes, with at most one remaining fixed axis.
