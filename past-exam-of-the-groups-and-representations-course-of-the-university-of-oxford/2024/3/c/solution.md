<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The change of variables in [b](../b.md) diagonalizes the complexified action into $z\mapsto Uz$ and $\bar z\mapsto\bar U\bar z$. Consequently the conventional complex [branching rule](../../../../branching-rule.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathbf6_{SO(6)}\downarrow SU(3)=\mathbf3\oplus\bar{\mathbf3}.}
$$

Precisely, this is a statement about the [complexification of a real vector space](../../../../complexification-of-a-real-vector-space.md) carrying the real defining [representation](../../../../group-representation.md). Over the [real numbers](../../../../real-number.md), the six-dimensional space is the [realification of a complex representation](../../../../realification-of-a-complex-representation.md) $\mathbf3$; the conjugate summands above are not separate three-dimensional real [invariant subspaces](../../../../invariant-subspace.md).

The block embedding $SO(6)\hookrightarrow SO(7)$ fixes the seventh coordinate. It adds a [trivial representation](../../../../trivial-representation.md), giving

$$
\boxed{\mathbf7_{SO(7)}\downarrow SU(3)=\mathbf3\oplus\bar{\mathbf3}\oplus\mathbf1.}
$$

The dimensions are complex dimensions after [complexification of a real vector space](../../../../complexification-of-a-real-vector-space.md), or equivalently $6+1$ as real dimensions before it.
