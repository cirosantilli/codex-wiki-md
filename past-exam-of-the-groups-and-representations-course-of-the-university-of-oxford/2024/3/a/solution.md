<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a real linear change of coordinates $x'=Ax$, one has $dx'=A\,dx$. The [Euclidean metric](../../../../euclidean-metric.md) therefore transforms to

$$
g'=dx'^Tdx'=dx^TA^T A\,dx.
$$

It agrees with $dx^Tdx$ for every displacement precisely when $A^TA=I$. For example, equality of the associated [quadratic forms](../../../../quadratic-form-split.md) determines their symmetric coefficient [matrices](../../../../matrix.md) by [polarization identity](../../../../polarization-identity.md). The [orthogonal group](../../../../orthogonal-group.md) is defined by this condition, so

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Stab}_{\operatorname{GL}(6,\mathbb R)}(g)
=O(6)=\{A:A^TA=I\}.}
$$

Taking [determinants](../../../../determinant.md) gives $(\det A)^2=1$. Thus these [matrices](../../../../matrix.md) are invertible automatically; both signs of the [determinant](../../../../determinant.md) are allowed, since the [metric](../../../../metric.md) alone does not specify an orientation.
