<h1 id="2/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

A [meson](../../../../meson.md) has [quark model](../../../../quark-model.md) flavour content $q_i\bar q^{\,j}$, so four [quark flavours](../../../../quark-flavor.md) give sixteen flavour states in

$$
\boxed{\mathbf4\otimes\bar{\mathbf4}=\mathbf{15}\oplus\mathbf1.}
$$

This counts a flavour set for a specified spin and orbital assignment; it does not count every excited [meson](../../../../meson.md). Three [quark flavours](../../../../quark-flavor.md) instead give the familiar nine states $\mathbf3\otimes\bar{\mathbf3}=\mathbf8\oplus\mathbf1$.

Taking the fourth [quark](../../../../quark.md) to be the [charm quark](../../../../charm-quark.md), the first nine combinations $q_\alpha\bar q^{\,\beta}$, with $\alpha,\beta\in\{u,d,s\}$, are the light sector. The extra seven are $q_\alpha\bar c$ in $\mathbf3$, $c\bar q^{\,\alpha}$ in $\bar{\mathbf3}$, and $c\bar c$ in $\mathbf1$ under light-flavour $SU(3)$. For the lowest [pseudoscalar mesons](../../../../pseudoscalar-meson.md), the open-charm states are the three [D meson](../../../../d-meson.md)/[Ds meson](../../../../ds-meson.md) states and their antiparticles, and the hidden-charm state is the [eta c meson](../../../../eta-c-meson.md). The two neutral [trivial representation](../../../../trivial-representation.md) states can mix when the flavour symmetry is broken.

The intended limitation is the accuracy of flavour symmetry: the [charm quark](../../../../charm-quark.md) is much heavier than the three light [quarks](../../../../quark.md), so a [quark mass](../../../../quark-mass.md) term is far from $SU(4)$ invariant. Exact flavour $SU(4)$ would relate light and charmed states with the same spin, contrary to their large observed [mass](../../../../mass.md) splittings. However, saying that a four-flavour [quark model](../../../../quark-model.md) is never considered would be too strong: flavour $SU(4)$ is used as classification bookkeeping, with much stronger symmetry breaking than light-flavour $SU(3)$.[Section 15.5.2, printed p. 19: the large charm mass strongly breaks flavour SU(4), which remains useful for bookkeeping](../../../../quark-model-in-the-2024-review-of-particle-physics.md) This is a flavour [special unitary group](../../../../special-unitary-group.md), distinct from the three-colour [gauge group](../../../../gauge-group.md) of [Quantum chromodynamics](../../../../quantum-chromodynamics.md).
