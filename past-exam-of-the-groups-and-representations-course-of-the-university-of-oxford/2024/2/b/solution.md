<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use the Hermitian $H_a$ from [a](../a.md) to record real [weight](../../../../weight-representation-theory.md) coordinates: $H_a e_j=(w_j)_a e_j$. Reading off the diagonal entries gives

$$
\boxed{w_1=(1,0,0),\quad w_2=(-1,1,0),\quad
w_3=(0,-1,1),\quad w_4=(0,0,-1).}
$$

These are [weights](../../../../weight-representation-theory.md) in the simple-[coroot](../../../../coroot.md) coordinate system, not coordinates in an orthonormal [basis](../../../../basis.md). In the skew-Hermitian convention they mean $(iH_a)e_j=i(w_j)_ae_j$. Intrinsically, the [weight](../../../../weight-representation-theory.md) on $i\operatorname{diag}(h_1,h_2,h_3,h_4)$ is the functional $h\mapsto h_j$, with $\sum_jh_j=0$.

The [conjugate representation](../../../../conjugate-representation.md) acts by $U^*$, so a diagonal transformation with entries $e^{ih_j}$ becomes one with entries $e^{-ih_j}$. Thus its four [weights](../../../../weight-representation-theory.md) are

$$
\boxed{\bar w_1=(-1,0,0),\quad\bar w_2=(1,-1,0),\quad
\bar w_3=(0,1,-1),\quad\bar w_4=(0,0,1).}
$$

All have multiplicity one. In both [representations](../../../../group-representation.md) the sum of the [weights](../../../../weight-representation-theory.md) is zero, consistent with the vanishing [trace](../../../../matrix-trace.md) of every [Lie algebra generator](../../../../lie-algebra-generator.md).
