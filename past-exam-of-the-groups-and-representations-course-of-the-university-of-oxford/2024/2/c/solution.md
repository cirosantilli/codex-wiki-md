<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Write $[r_1,r_2,\ldots]$ for a [Young diagram](../../../../young-diagram.md) with row lengths $r_i$, and depict an empty box by $\square$. Appending the box of the [fundamental representation](../../../../fundamental-representation.md) to another single box gives a row or a column:

$$
\square\otimes\square
=(\square\,\square)\ \oplus\ \begin{array}{l}\square\\[-4pt]\square\end{array},
\qquad [1]\otimes[1]=[2]\oplus[1,1].
$$

The corresponding [symmetric tensors](../../../../symmetric-tensor.md) and [antisymmetric tensors](../../../../antisymmetric-tensor.md) have dimensions $4\cdot5/2=10$ and $4\cdot3/2=6$, so

$$
\boxed{\mathbf4\otimes\mathbf4=\mathbf{10}_{\mathrm{s}}\oplus\mathbf6_{\mathrm{a}}.}
$$

For the [conjugate representation](../../../../conjugate-representation.md), the invariant volume tensor identifies $\bar{\mathbf4}\cong\Lambda^3\mathbf4$, represented by a column of three boxes. Adding one box gives

$$
[1]\otimes[1,1,1]=[2,1,1]\oplus[1,1,1,1],
\qquad
\square\otimes\begin{array}{l}\square\\[-4pt]\square\\[-4pt]\square\end{array}
=\begin{array}{l}\square\,\square\\[-4pt]\square\\[-4pt]\square\end{array}
\ \oplus\ \begin{array}{l}\square\\[-4pt]\square\\[-4pt]\square\\[-4pt]\square\end{array}.
$$

A column of four boxes is the [determinant representation](../../../../determinant-representation.md), which is trivial on $SU(4)$. The remaining [Young diagram](../../../../young-diagram.md) has [Dynkin labels](../../../../dynkin-label.md) $(1,0,1)$ and dimension fifteen. For example the [hook-content formula](../../../../hook-content-formula.md) gives

$$
\dim[2,1,1]=\frac{4\cdot5\cdot3\cdot2}{4\cdot1\cdot2\cdot1}=15.
$$

Equivalently, a mixed [tensor](../../../../tensor.md) $M^i{}_j$ transforms by $M\mapsto UMU^\dagger$ and splits into its [trace](../../../../matrix-trace.md) and [traceless matrix](../../../../traceless-matrix.md) parts. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\mathbf4\otimes\bar{\mathbf4}=\mathbf1\oplus\mathbf{15},}
$$

where $\mathbf{15}$ is the [Adjoint representation](../../../../adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md).
