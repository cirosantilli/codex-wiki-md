<h1 id="4/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Among the [representations](../../../../group-representation.md) in [c](../c.md), the $\mathbf{10}$ of $SU(5)$ occurs in the $\mathbf{15}$ of $SU(6)$, while the simplest source of $\bar{\mathbf5}$ is $\bar{\mathbf6}$. Thus the economical [direct sum](../../../../direct-sum.md) containing the prescribed family is

$$
\boxed{R=\bar{\mathbf6}\oplus\mathbf{15}
\quad\downarrow SU(5)\quad
(\bar{\mathbf5}\oplus\mathbf{10})\oplus\mathbf5\oplus\mathbf1.}
$$

The extra $SU(5)$ [multiplets](../../../../multiplet.md) are one $\mathbf5$ and one [trivial representation](../../../../trivial-representation.md). Calling $R$ a [representation](../../../../group-representation.md) does not require it to be an [irreducible representation](../../../../irreducible-representation.md); none of the five individual choices in [c](../c.md) contains both required $SU(5)$ summands.

There is a further physical qualification if this is to be the entire chiral matter content of a consistent [grand unified theory](../../../../grand-unified-theory.md). Merely containing one [Standard Model](../../../../standard-model-split.md) family does not guarantee cancellation of the cubic $SU(6)$ [gauge anomaly](../../../../gauge-anomaly.md). In the convention $A(\mathbf6)=1$, one has $A(\bar{\mathbf6})=-1$ and $A(\mathbf{15})=2$, so the displayed sum has $A(R)=1$. With these types of [representations](../../../../group-representation.md), the minimal anomaly-free completion is instead

$$
\boxed{R_{\mathrm{af}}=2\bar{\mathbf6}\oplus\mathbf{15}
\quad\downarrow SU(5)\quad
(\bar{\mathbf5}\oplus\mathbf{10})\oplus(\mathbf5\oplus\bar{\mathbf5})\oplus2\mathbf1.}
$$

Beyond one family this adds a [vectorlike gauge spectrum](../../../../vectorlike-gauge-spectrum.md) $\mathbf5\oplus\bar{\mathbf5}$ and two [trivial representations](../../../../trivial-representation.md). The conjugate pair can receive a mass after the appropriate symmetry breaking, without removing the remaining family.[Section 3, equations (14)–(16) and the following discussion: the SU(6) anomaly coefficients are -1 and 2, requiring twice as many conjugate fundamentals as antisymmetric rank-two tensors](../../../../number-of-fermion-generations-from-a-novel-grand-unified-model-by-pritibhajan-byakti-and-others.md) The first boxed answer addresses representation containment; the second includes the extra consistency condition for a chiral [gauge theory](../../../../gauge-theory.md).
