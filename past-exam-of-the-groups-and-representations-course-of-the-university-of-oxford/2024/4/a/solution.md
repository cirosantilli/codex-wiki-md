<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The image is in the [unitary group](../../../../unitary-group.md), since $(zU)^\dagger(zU)=\bar z z\,U^\dagger U=I$ for $z\in U(1)$ and $U\in SU(n)$. The [direct product of groups](../../../../direct-product-of-groups.md) has multiplication $(z,U)(w,V)=(zw,UV)$. Scalar [matrices](../../../../matrix.md) commute with every [matrix](../../../../matrix.md), so

$$
\boxed{f\bigl((z,U)(w,V)\bigr)=zwUV=(zU)(wV)=f(z,U)f(w,V).}
$$

Thus $f$ is a [group homomorphism](../../../../group-homomorphism.md); in particular $f(1,I)=I$.
