<h1 id="4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Let index $0$ denote the first coordinate, which the embedded $SU(5)$ fixes, and let $a,b=1,\ldots,5$ denote the remaining coordinates. The [fundamental representation](../../../../fundamental-representation.md) and its [conjugate representation](../../../../conjugate-representation.md) immediately give

$$
\boxed{\mathbf6\downarrow SU(5)=\mathbf1\oplus\mathbf5,\qquad
\bar{\mathbf6}\downarrow SU(5)=\mathbf1\oplus\bar{\mathbf5}.}
$$

For a [symmetric tensor](../../../../symmetric-tensor.md), $S^{00}$ is a [trivial representation](../../../../trivial-representation.md), $S^{0a}=S^{a0}$ is a $\mathbf5$, and $S^{ab}$ is the fifteen-dimensional [symmetric square of a group representation](../../../../symmetric-square-of-a-group-representation.md) of $\mathbf5$. For an [antisymmetric tensor](../../../../antisymmetric-tensor.md), $A^{00}=0$, $A^{0a}=-A^{a0}$ is a $\mathbf5$, and $A^{ab}$ is the ten-dimensional [exterior power](../../../../exterior-power.md) $\Lambda^2\mathbf5$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\mathbf{21}\downarrow SU(5)=\mathbf{15}\oplus\mathbf5\oplus\mathbf1,\qquad
\mathbf{15}\downarrow SU(5)=\mathbf{10}\oplus\mathbf5.}
$$

Here the left-hand $\mathbf{15}$ is the [antisymmetric tensor](../../../../antisymmetric-tensor.md) of $SU(6)$, while the right-hand $\mathbf{15}$ in the first equation is the [symmetric tensor](../../../../symmetric-tensor.md) of $SU(5)$; equal dimensions alone do not identify [representations](../../../../group-representation.md) of different [groups](../../../../group-split.md).

Finally decompose the traceless block [matrix](../../../../matrix.md)

$$
M=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&D\end{pmatrix},\qquad a+\operatorname{tr}D=0.
$$

The traceless part of $D$ is the [Adjoint representation](../../../../adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md) $\mathbf{24}$, the column $c$ is $\mathbf5$, the row $b$ is $\bar{\mathbf5}$, and the one remaining diagonal [trivial representation](../../../../trivial-representation.md) is generated by $\operatorname{diag}(-5,1,1,1,1,1)$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\mathbf{35}\downarrow SU(5)=\mathbf{24}\oplus\mathbf5\oplus\bar{\mathbf5}\oplus\mathbf1.}
$$

All dimensions match: $6=1+5$, $21=15+5+1$, $15=10+5$ and $35=24+5+5+1$.
