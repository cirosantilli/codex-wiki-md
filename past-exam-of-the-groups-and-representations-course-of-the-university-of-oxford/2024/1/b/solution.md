<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Compose [permutations](../../../../permutation.md) from right to left. The three [transpositions](../../../../transposition-permutation.md) are $\tau_1=(23)$, $\tau_2=(13)$ and $\tau_3=(12)$. In [cycle notation](../../../../cycle-notation.md), direct composition gives

$$
\tau_1\tau_2=(123),\qquad \tau_2\tau_1=(132),\qquad \tau_i^2=e.
$$

The complete [symmetric group](../../../../symmetric-group.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{S_3=\{e,\tau_1,\tau_2,\tau_3,\tau_1\tau_2,\tau_2\tau_1\}.}
$$

[Conjugation](../../../../conjugation.md) relabels the entries of a [cycle](../../../../permutation-cycle.md), so the [conjugacy classes](../../../../conjugacy-class.md) are precisely

$$
\boxed{\{e\},\qquad\{\tau_1,\tau_2,\tau_3\},\qquad\{\tau_1\tau_2,\tau_2\tau_1\}.}
$$

Their sizes are $1,3,2$, respectively. The differing [orders of group elements](../../../../order-of-a-group-element.md) also show that these three sets cannot merge under [conjugation](../../../../conjugation.md).
