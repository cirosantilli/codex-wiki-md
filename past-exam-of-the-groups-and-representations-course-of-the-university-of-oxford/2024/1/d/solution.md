<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

All three displayed [transposition](../../../../transposition-permutation.md) [matrices](../../../../matrix.md) have [trace](../../../../matrix-trace.md) zero. Multiplying two of them gives

$$
R(\tau_1\tau_2)=\operatorname{diag}(\alpha,\alpha^{-1}),\qquad
R(\tau_2\tau_1)=\operatorname{diag}(\alpha^{-1},\alpha),
$$

since $\alpha^3=1$. Their [traces](../../../../matrix-trace.md) are $\alpha+\alpha^{-1}=-1$, whereas $\operatorname{tr}R(e)=2$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\chi_R=(2,0,-1),\qquad
\langle\chi_R,\chi_R\rangle=\frac{4+3\cdot0+2\cdot1}{6}=1.}
$$

By [character orthogonality](../../../../character-orthogonality.md), $R$ is the [irreducible representation](../../../../irreducible-representation.md) $\mathbf2$ from [c](../c.md).

There is also a direct [invariant subspace](../../../../invariant-subspace.md) argument. The two distinct [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md) of $R(\tau_1\tau_2)$ force any invariant line to be one of the two coordinate lines. But $R(\tau_3)$ exchanges those lines. Neither is invariant under the whole [group](../../../../group-split.md), so this two-dimensional [representation](../../../../group-representation.md) is irreducible.
