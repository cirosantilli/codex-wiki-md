<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

All three [vector spaces](../../../../vector-space-split.md) now have the same [dimension](../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) $n$. Each of the nine blocks of $P$ commutes with the common [irreducible representation](../../../../irreducible-representation.md), so the [Schur lemma](../../../../schur-s-lemma.md) gives

$$
\boxed{P=\begin{pmatrix}
a_{11}I_n&a_{12}I_n&a_{13}I_n\\
a_{21}I_n&a_{22}I_n&a_{23}I_n\\
a_{31}I_n&a_{32}I_n&a_{33}I_n
\end{pmatrix}=A\otimes I_n,\qquad A\in M_3(\mathbb C).}
$$

Conversely $R(g)=I_3\otimes R_1(g)$, so the [Kronecker product](../../../../kronecker-product.md) identity immediately verifies commutation for every $A$. The [commutant of a semisimple representation](../../../../commutant-of-a-semisimple-representation.md) acts arbitrarily on the three-dimensional multiplicity [vector space](../../../../vector-space-split.md) while acting as a scalar on each irreducible factor. Its complex [dimension](../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) is nine.
