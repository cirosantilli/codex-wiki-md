<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [complexification of a Lie algebra](../../../../complexification-of-a-lie-algebra.md) gives $\mathfrak{su}(4)_{\mathbb C}=\mathfrak{sl}_4(\mathbb C)$, of type [A3 root system](../../../../a3-root-system.md). Choose [simple roots](../../../../simple-root.md) $\alpha_1=e_1-e_2$, $\alpha_2=e_2-e_3$, $\alpha_3=e_3-e_4$, with squared lengths two. The [Cartan matrix](../../../../cartan-matrix.md) and [Dynkin diagram](../../../../dynkin-diagram.md) are

$$
\boxed{A=\begin{pmatrix}2&-1&0\\-1&2&-1\\0&-1&2\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\underset{1}{\circ}\! -\! \underset{2}{\circ}\! -\! \underset{3}{\circ}.}
$$

Each line represents a single bond. In the [Dynkin label](../../../../dynkin-label.md) convention $w_a=\langle w,\alpha_a^\vee\rangle$, the [simple roots](../../../../simple-root.md) have coordinates equal to the rows of $A$.

In the defining [representation](../../../../group-representation.md), apply the [simple coroots](../../../../simple-coroot.md) $E_{aa}-E_{a+1,a+1}$ to the four coordinate [basis vectors](../../../../basis-vector.md). Their [weights](../../../../weight-representation-theory.md) are

$$
\boxed{\mathbf4=(1,0,0):\quad
v_1=(1,0,0),\quad v_2=(-1,1,0),\quad v_3=(0,-1,1),\quad v_4=(0,0,-1).}
$$

Equivalently these are obtained by subtracting $\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_3$ successively from the [highest weight](../../../../highest-weight-of-a-representation.md). The [dual representation](../../../../dual-representation.md) has the negative [weights](../../../../weight-representation-theory.md), hence

$$
\boxed{\overline{\mathbf4}=(0,0,1):\quad
(-1,0,0),\quad(1,-1,0),\quad(0,1,-1),\quad(0,0,1).}
$$

All listed [weight multiplicities](../../../../weight-multiplicity.md) are one, so both [dimensions](../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) are four.

The [highest weight](../../../../highest-weight-of-a-representation.md) $(1,0,1)$ is $\alpha_1+\alpha_2+\alpha_3$, the [highest root](../../../../highest-root.md). Its [irreducible representation](../../../../irreducible-representation.md) is therefore the [Adjoint representation](../../../../adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md). Under the action on [traceless matrices](../../../../traceless-matrix.md) by conjugation, the off-diagonal [matrix unit](../../../../matrix-unit.md) $E_{ij}$ has [weight](../../../../weight-representation-theory.md) $v_i-v_j$, while the diagonal [Cartan subalgebra](../../../../cartan-subalgebra.md) has zero [weight](../../../../weight-representation-theory.md). Thus

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
\mathbf{15}=(1,0,1):\quad
&\pm(2,-1,0),\quad\pm(-1,2,-1),\quad\pm(0,-1,2),\\
&\pm(1,1,-1),\quad\pm(-1,1,1),\quad\pm(1,0,1),\\
&(0,0,0)^{[3]}.
\end{aligned}}
$$

The superscript $[3]$ indicates [weight multiplicity](../../../../weight-multiplicity.md) three. The twelve nonzero [weights](../../../../weight-representation-theory.md) each have [weight multiplicity](../../../../weight-multiplicity.md) one, giving $12+3=15$ in total. This also follows from the [tensor product representation](../../../../tensor-product-of-group-representations.md) $\mathbf4\otimes\overline{\mathbf4}=\mathbf{15}\oplus\mathbf1$: the sixteen differences $v_i-v_j$ contain four zero [weights](../../../../weight-representation-theory.md), and removing the identity [matrix](../../../../matrix.md) removes one of them.
