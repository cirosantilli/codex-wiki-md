<h1 id="4/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Compared with the complete three-flavour [meson](../../../../meson.md) [tensor product](../../../../tensor-product.md), the additional flavour states are exactly

$$
\boxed{\underbrace{(u\bar c,\ d\bar c,\ s\bar c)}_{\mathbf3},\qquad
\underbrace{(c\bar u,\ c\bar d,\ c\bar s)}_{\overline{\mathbf3}},\qquad
\underbrace{c\bar c}_{\mathbf1}.}
$$

Thus there are seven new flavour states. For the [pseudoscalar mesons](../../../../pseudoscalar-meson.md), the six open-charm states are $\bar D^0,D^-,D_s^-$ and $D^0,D^+,D_s^+$, respectively, using the [D meson](../../../../d-meson.md) and [Ds meson](../../../../ds-meson.md) conventions. A pure $c\bar c$ [pseudoscalar meson](../../../../pseudoscalar-meson.md) is the idealized flavour state associated with the [eta c meson](../../../../eta-c-meson.md). The [quark model](../../../../quark-model.md) flavour calculation alone does not specify spin or spatial excitations, so the same flavour labels also organize other [meson](../../../../meson.md) species.

If comparing the traceless [SU(4)](../../../../su-4-group.md) $\mathbf{15}$ with the traceless [SU(3)](../../../../su-3-group.md) [meson octet](../../../../meson-octet.md), the extra seven states are instead the same two triplets and the normalized [SU(3)](../../../../su-3-group.md) singlet

$$
\boxed{\eta_{15}=\frac{u\bar u+d\bar d+s\bar s-3c\bar c}{\sqrt{12}}.}
$$

Its coefficient [matrix](../../../../matrix.md) is traceless and has squared norm one. In particular, $c\bar c$ by itself is not a vector in the traceless $\mathbf{15}$; its coefficient [matrix](../../../../matrix.md) has [trace](../../../../matrix-trace.md) one.

To relate these two descriptions, let $S_4=(u\bar u+d\bar d+s\bar s+c\bar c)/2$ be the normalized [SU(4)](../../../../su-4-group.md) singlet and $S_3=(u\bar u+d\bar d+s\bar s)/\sqrt3$ the old [SU(3)](../../../../su-3-group.md) singlet. Then

$$
\boxed{S_3=\frac{\sqrt3}{2}S_4+\frac12\eta_{15},\qquad
c\bar c=\frac12S_4-\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\eta_{15}.}
$$

This orthonormal change of [basis](../../../../basis.md) shows explicitly why the complete [tensor product](../../../../tensor-product.md) contains two light-flavour singlets but adds only one state to the old light-flavour singlet sector.
