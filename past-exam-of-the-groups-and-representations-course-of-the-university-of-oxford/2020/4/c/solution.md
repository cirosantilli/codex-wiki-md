<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The projection deletes the third [Dynkin label](../../../../dynkin-label.md), so the four [weights](../../../../weight-representation-theory.md) $v_i$ of [solution](../b/solution.md) restrict to $(1,0),(-1,1),(0,-1),(0,0)$. The first three are the defining [SU(3)](../../../../su-3-group.md) [weight system](../../../../weight-system.md), and the fourth is a singlet. Negating them gives the [dual representation](../../../../dual-representation.md). Hence

$$
\boxed{\mathbf4\downarrow_{SU(3)}=\mathbf3\oplus\mathbf1,\qquad
\overline{\mathbf4}\downarrow_{SU(3)}=\overline{\mathbf3}\oplus\mathbf1.}
$$

For the [Adjoint representation](../../../../adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md), the projected nonzero [weights](../../../../weight-representation-theory.md) consist of the six [A2 root system](../../../../a2-root-system.md) [roots](../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md), the three defining [weights](../../../../weight-representation-theory.md) and their negatives. There are also three zero [weights](../../../../weight-representation-theory.md). Two zero [weights](../../../../weight-representation-theory.md) belong to the [SU(3)](../../../../su-3-group.md) [Adjoint representation](../../../../adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md), leaving one singlet. Therefore the [SU4 adjoint branching to SU3](../../../../su4-adjoint-branching-to-su3.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathbf{15}\downarrow_{SU(3)}=\mathbf8\oplus\mathbf3\oplus\overline{\mathbf3}\oplus\mathbf1.}
$$

One can see all four [invariant subspaces](../../../../invariant-subspace.md) directly by writing a [traceless matrix](../../../../traceless-matrix.md) as

$$
M=\begin{pmatrix}A&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix},\qquad \operatorname{tr}A+d=0.
$$

The traceless part of $A$ is $\mathbf8$, the column $b$ is $\mathbf3$, the row $c$ is $\overline{\mathbf3}$, and the remaining diagonal [matrix](../../../../matrix.md) is proportional to $\operatorname{diag}(1,1,1,-3)$ and is fixed by [SU(3)](../../../../su-3-group.md). These have [dimensions](../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) $8+3+3+1=15$. Equivalently restrict $\mathbf4\otimes\overline{\mathbf4}-\mathbf1$ and use $\mathbf3\otimes\overline{\mathbf3}=\mathbf8\oplus\mathbf1$.
