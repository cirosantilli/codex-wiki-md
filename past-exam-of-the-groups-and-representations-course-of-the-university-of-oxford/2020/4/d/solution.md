<h1 id="4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Take the [quarks](../../../../quark.md) $q=(u,d,s,c)^T$ in the defining $\mathbf4$ of flavour [SU(4)](../../../../su-4-group.md) and the [antiquarks](../../../../antiquark.md) in $\overline{\mathbf4}$. The flavour part of a [meson](../../../../meson.md) is a [quark](../../../../quark.md)-[antiquark](../../../../antiquark.md) pair $M^i{}_j=q^i\bar q_j$. It transforms as $M\mapsto UMU^{-1}$, so the complete [four-flavour meson multiplet](../../../../four-flavour-meson-multiplet.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathbf4\otimes\overline{\mathbf4}=\mathbf{15}\oplus\mathbf1.}
$$

Its traceless part $M-\tfrac14(\operatorname{tr}M)I_4$ is the [Adjoint representation](../../../../adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md) $\mathbf{15}$, the direct four-flavour analogue of the three-flavour [meson octet](../../../../meson-octet.md). The [trace](../../../../matrix-trace.md) supplies an additional [trivial representation](../../../../trivial-representation.md). Thus “the mesons” can refer to the traceless multiplet or to the complete [quark model](../../../../quark-model.md) [tensor product](../../../../tensor-product.md); keeping both explicit avoids dropping a state.

Using [solution](../c/solution.md), the [SU(3)](../../../../su-3-group.md) [representation](../../../../group-representation.md) contents are

$$
\boxed{\mathbf{15}\downarrow_{SU(3)}=\mathbf8\oplus\mathbf3\oplus\overline{\mathbf3}\oplus\mathbf1,\qquad
(\mathbf{15}\oplus\mathbf1)\downarrow_{SU(3)}=\mathbf8\oplus\mathbf3\oplus\overline{\mathbf3}\oplus2\mathbf1.}
$$

Directly, the upper-left block $q^i\bar q_j$ with $i,j\leq3$ is the old $\mathbf3\otimes\overline{\mathbf3}=\mathbf8\oplus\mathbf1$; the three entries $q^i\bar c$ form $\mathbf3$, the three entries $c\bar q_j$ form $\overline{\mathbf3}$, and $c\bar c$ is another singlet. The [dimensions](../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) are $9+3+3+1=16$. This is a classification by [flavour symmetry](../../../../flavor-symmetry.md), rather than colour or spin. The much larger [charm quark](../../../../charm-quark.md) [mass](../../../../mass.md) breaks the flavour [SU(4)](../../../../su-4-group.md) approximation strongly; it does not change this [representation](../../../../group-representation.md) counting.
