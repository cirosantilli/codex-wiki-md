<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The variables commute, so $x_ix_j=x_jx_i$. Thus $V$ has [dimension](../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) six, with [basis](../../../../basis.md) $x_1^2,x_2^2,x_3^2,x_1x_2,x_1x_3,x_2x_3$, even though nine coefficients occur in the redundant expression in the source. Its [representation](../../../../group-representation.md) is the [symmetric square](../../../../symmetric-square.md) of the three-dimensional [permutation representation](../../../../permutation-representation.md).

The identity fixes all six [monomials](../../../../monomial.md). A three-cycle fixes none. For a [transposition](../../../../transposition-permutation.md), say $(12)$, exactly $x_3^2$ and $x_1x_2$ are fixed; the remaining [monomials](../../../../monomial.md) occur in exchanged pairs. Therefore $\chi_{\widetilde R}=(6,0,2)$ in the [conjugacy class](../../../../conjugacy-class.md) order of [solution](../b/solution.md). The [character inner products](../../../../character-inner-product.md) give multiplicities

$$
n_{\mathbf1}=\frac{6+3\cdot2}{6}=2,\qquad
n_\varepsilon=\frac{6-3\cdot2}{6}=0,\qquad
n_D=\frac{6\cdot2}{6}=2.
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{\widetilde R\cong2\mathbf1\oplus2D.}
$$

There is also a concrete decomposition: the span of the three squares is one copy of the [permutation representation](../../../../permutation-representation.md). The span of the three mixed [monomials](../../../../monomial.md) is another, since $x_2x_3,x_1x_3,x_1x_2$ are permuted exactly like their omitted indices $1,2,3$. Within each, the coefficient-sum-zero [vector subspace](../../../../vector-subspace.md) gives a copy of $D$, and the sum of its three [basis](../../../../basis.md) [monomials](../../../../monomial.md) gives a [trivial representation](../../../../trivial-representation.md).

A fixed [quadratic polynomial](../../../../quadratic-polynomial.md) must have equal coefficients on each of the two [orbits](../../../../orbit-dynamical-system.md) of [monomials](../../../../monomial.md). Hence its fixed [vector subspace](../../../../vector-subspace.md) is

$$
\boxed{V^{S_3}=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb C}\{x_1^2+x_2^2+x_3^2,\quad x_1x_2+x_1x_3+x_2x_3\}.}
$$

The two displayed [polynomial invariants](../../../../polynomial-invariant.md) are linearly independent and account for both singlets. Equivalently, one may use $x_1^2+x_2^2+x_3^2$ and $(x_1+x_2+x_3)^2$ as a [basis](../../../../basis.md). No constant term belongs to this homogeneous degree-two [vector space](../../../../vector-space-split.md).
