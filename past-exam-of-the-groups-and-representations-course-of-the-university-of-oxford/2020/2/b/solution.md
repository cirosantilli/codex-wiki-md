<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

There are three complex [irreducible representations](../../../../irreducible-representation.md), because there are three [conjugacy classes](../../../../conjugacy-class.md). Two are the [trivial representation](../../../../trivial-representation.md) $\mathbf1$ and the [sign representation](../../../../sign-representation.md) $\varepsilon$: both send $r$ to $1$, while they send $s$ to $1$ and $-1$, respectively. These exhaust the [one-dimensional representations](../../../../one-dimensional-representation.md), since their scalar images must satisfy $r=r^{-1}$ and $r^3=1$, forcing $r=1$, and then $s^2=1$.

The defining two-dimensional [matrix representation](../../../../matrix-representation.md) $D$ has [character](../../../../character-of-a-representation.md)

$$
\chi_D(I)=2,\qquad \chi_D(r)=\chi_D(r^2)=\alpha+\alpha^2=-1,\qquad \chi_D(r^ks)=0.
$$

Its [character inner product](../../../../character-inner-product.md) with itself is

$$
\langle\chi_D,\chi_D\rangle=\frac{1}{6}(1\cdot4+2\cdot1+3\cdot0)=1,
$$

so it is an [irreducible representation](../../../../irreducible-representation.md). Directly, the two coordinate lines are the distinct [eigenspaces](../../../../eigenspace.md) of $r$, and $s$ exchanges them, leaving no common invariant line. The [sum of squares of irreducible representation dimensions](../../../../sum-of-squares-of-irreducible-representation-dimensions.md) is $1^2+1^2+2^2=6$, proving completeness. In the [conjugacy class](../../../../conjugacy-class.md) order of [solution](../a/solution.md), the [character table](../../../../character-table.md) is

$$
\boxed{\begin{array}{c|rrr}
&I&r&s\\\hline
\text{class size}&1&2&3\\\hline
\mathbf1&1&1&1\\
\varepsilon&1&1&-1\\
D&2&-1&0
\end{array}}
$$

Representatives $r,s$ in this table stand for their full [conjugacy classes](../../../../conjugacy-class.md). This lists all [irreducible representations](../../../../irreducible-representation.md) up to [equivalence of group representations](../../../../equivalence-of-group-representations.md).
