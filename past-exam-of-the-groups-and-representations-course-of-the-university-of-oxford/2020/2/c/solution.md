<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The specified [linear maps](../../../../linear-map.md) form a [permutation representation](../../../../permutation-representation.md), since

$$
R(\sigma)R(\tau)e_i=e_{\sigma(\tau(i))}=R(\sigma\tau)e_i.
$$

The [trace](../../../../matrix-trace.md) of a [permutation matrix](../../../../permutation-matrix.md) counts fixed coordinate [basis vectors](../../../../basis-vector.md). On the identity, three-cycles and [transpositions](../../../../transposition-permutation.md), respectively, this gives $\chi_R=(3,0,1)$. Comparing with [solution](../b/solution.md),

$$
\boxed{\chi_R=\chi_{\mathbf1}+\chi_D,\qquad R\cong\mathbf1\oplus D.}
$$

For an explicit [direct sum](../../../../direct-sum.md),

$$
\mathbb C^3=\mathbb C(e_1+e_2+e_3)\oplus
\{z_1e_1+z_2e_2+z_3e_3:z_1+z_2+z_3=0\}.
$$

Both summands are [invariant subspaces](../../../../invariant-subspace.md) under coordinate [permutations](../../../../permutation.md). The first is fixed pointwise and hence gives the [trivial representation](../../../../trivial-representation.md); the second has [character](../../../../character-of-a-representation.md) $(2,-1,0)$ and hence gives $D$. The [sign representation](../../../../sign-representation.md) has multiplicity zero.
