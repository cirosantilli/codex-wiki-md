<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Under the stated [SU(2)](../../../../su-2-group.md) embedding, the first two components of the defining [SU(3)](../../../../su-3-group.md) [representation](../../../../group-representation.md) form a doublet, while the third is fixed. Hence $\mathbf3\downarrow_{SU(2)}=\mathbf2\oplus\mathbf1$. Its [dual representation](../../../../dual-representation.md) restricts in the same way because the [SU(2)](../../../../su-2-group.md) doublet is self-dual.

For the [Adjoint representation](../../../../adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md), restrict $\mathbf8=\mathbf3\otimes\overline{\mathbf3}-\mathbf1$. The [SU(2)](../../../../su-2-group.md) [tensor product](../../../../tensor-product.md) $\mathbf2\otimes\mathbf2=\mathbf3\oplus\mathbf1$ gives

$$
\mathbf8\downarrow_{SU(2)}
=(\mathbf2\oplus\mathbf1)\otimes(\mathbf2\oplus\mathbf1)-\mathbf1
=\mathbf3\oplus\mathbf2\oplus\mathbf2\oplus\mathbf1.
$$

Here subtraction denotes removal of the [trivial representation](../../../../trivial-representation.md) generated by the identity [matrix](../../../../matrix.md); it is not a negative-dimensional summand. The first [SU(3)](../../../../su-3-group.md) factor is unchanged, so the requested [branching of a representation](../../../../branching-of-a-representation.md) rules are

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
(\mathbf3,\mathbf3)&\downarrow_H=(\mathbf3,\mathbf2)\oplus(\mathbf3,\mathbf1),\\
(\overline{\mathbf3},\mathbf1)&\downarrow_H=(\overline{\mathbf3},\mathbf1),\\
(\mathbf1,\mathbf3)&\downarrow_H=(\mathbf1,\mathbf2)\oplus(\mathbf1,\mathbf1),\\
(\mathbf1,\mathbf8)&\downarrow_H=(\mathbf1,\mathbf3)\oplus2(\mathbf1,\mathbf2)\oplus(\mathbf1,\mathbf1).
\end{aligned}}
$$

The [dimensions](../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) on the two sides are respectively $9=6+3$, $3=3$, $3=2+1$ and $8=3+2+2+1$.
