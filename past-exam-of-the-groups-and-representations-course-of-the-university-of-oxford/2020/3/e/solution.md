<h1 id="3/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The commuting direction in [solution](../d/solution.md) provides the correct candidate [Lie algebra](../../../../lie-algebra-split.md) for a [gauge group](../../../../gauge-group.md), $\mathfrak{su}(3)_c\oplus\mathfrak{su}(2)_L\oplus\mathfrak u(1)_Y$. This is a necessary structural condition for the [Standard Model](../../../../standard-model-split.md), but it does not by itself establish a viable [grand unified theory](../../../../grand-unified-theory.md).

The [representations](../../../../group-representation.md) in [solution](../c/solution.md) do not give a [Standard Model fermion](../../../../standard-model-fermion.md) generation with the correct [weak hypercharges](../../../../hypercharge.md). Use $Q_{\mathrm{electric}}=T_3+Y$. Identifying $(\mathbf3,\mathbf2)_1$ with the left-handed [quark doublet](../../../../quark-doublet.md), whose [weak hypercharge](../../../../hypercharge.md) is $1/6$, fixes $Y=X/6$. But then the doublet in $(\mathbf1,\mathbf3)$ has $Y=1/6$, whereas the left-handed [lepton](../../../../lepton.md) doublet requires $Y=-1/2$. Its singlet has $Y=-1/3$, whereas a right-handed [electron](../../../../electron.md) requires $Y=-1$. The $(\overline{\mathbf3},\mathbf1)_0$ is a further concrete mismatch: right-handed [quarks](../../../../quark.md) written as left-handed [antiquarks](../../../../antiquark.md) require $Y=-2/3$ or $1/3$, and a rescaling cannot turn zero into either value. These comparisons use the [electroweak representation and hypercharge table](../../../../electroweak-representation-and-hypercharge-table.md).

The charge-$-3$ doublet in $(\mathbf1,\mathbf8)$ does have the required lepton [weak hypercharge](../../../../hypercharge.md) under this normalization, but the same [Adjoint representation](../../../../adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md) also brings a doublet of the opposite charge and an extra triplet. Therefore merely adding that [representation](../../../../group-representation.md) does not supply the missing chiral generation without additional states and a mechanism for their [masses](../../../../mass.md).

Furthermore $SU(3)\times SU(3)$ is a product of two [Simple Lie groups](../../../../simple-lie-group.md), permitting two independent [gauge couplings](../../../../gauge-coupling.md); it does not enforce a common strong and electroweak [gauge coupling](../../../../gauge-coupling.md). Thus **the embedding exists, but the displayed matter content does not furnish a viable minimal unification of the Standard Model**. A more elaborate model would require additional [representations](../../../../group-representation.md), consistent chiral matter, [anomaly cancellation](../../../../anomaly-cancellation.md), and a specified [spontaneous symmetry breaking](../../../../spontaneous-symmetry-breaking.md) mechanism. The calculations here do not prove that every possible extension using this product [group](../../../../group-split.md) is impossible.
