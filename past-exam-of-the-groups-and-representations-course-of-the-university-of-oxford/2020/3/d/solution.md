<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

An element commuting with the full first [SU(3)](../../../../su-3-group.md) factor has its first component in that factor's [center of a group](../../../../center-of-a-group.md), which is discrete. In the second factor, commutation with every $\operatorname{diag}(U,1)$ forces the doublet block to be scalar and the mixed blocks to vanish, by the [Schur lemma](../../../../schur-s-lemma.md). Unitarity and unit [determinant](../../../../determinant.md) then give the connected commuting [subgroup](../../../../subgroup.md)

$$
\boxed{U(1)_X=\{(I_3,\operatorname{diag}(e^{i\theta},e^{i\theta},e^{-2i\theta})):\theta\in\mathbb R\},\qquad X=\operatorname{diag}(1,1,-2).}
$$

This is the [centralizer of the standard SU2 subgroup in SU3](../../../../centralizer-of-the-standard-su2-subgroup-in-su3.md) in the second factor; the full [centralizer](../../../../centralizer.md) in $G$ also includes the first factor's discrete [center of a group](../../../../center-of-a-group.md) $\mathbb Z_3$. The [Lie algebra](../../../../lie-algebra-split.md) generator is $iX$ in the [anti-Hermitian](../../../../skew-hermitian-matrix.md) convention. The opposite sign or a common nonzero rescaling changes the charge convention, not the commuting direction.

In the defining [representation](../../../../group-representation.md), the [U(1) charges](../../../../u-1-charge.md) are $1,1,-2$. In the [dual representation](../../../../dual-representation.md) they have the opposite signs. In the [Adjoint representation](../../../../adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md) a component $T^i{}_j$ has [U(1) charge](../../../../u-1-charge.md) $X_i-X_j$. Thus its upper-right doublet has charge $3$, its lower-left doublet has charge $-3$, and its diagonal blocks have charge zero. Adding the charges to [solution](../c/solution.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
(\mathbf3,\mathbf3)&\downarrow=(\mathbf3,\mathbf2)_{1}\oplus(\mathbf3,\mathbf1)_{-2},\\
(\overline{\mathbf3},\mathbf1)&\downarrow=(\overline{\mathbf3},\mathbf1)_{0},\\
(\mathbf1,\mathbf3)&\downarrow=(\mathbf1,\mathbf2)_{1}\oplus(\mathbf1,\mathbf1)_{-2},\\
(\mathbf1,\mathbf8)&\downarrow=(\mathbf1,\mathbf3)_{0}\oplus(\mathbf1,\mathbf2)_{3}\oplus(\mathbf1,\mathbf2)_{-3}\oplus(\mathbf1,\mathbf1)_{0}.
\end{aligned}}
$$

Each subscript denotes the [eigenvalue](../../../../eigenvalue.md) of $X$, so a state of charge $q$ acquires the phase $e^{iq\theta}$. The connected [subgroup](../../../../subgroup.md) generated by this circle and the second [SU(2)](../../../../su-2-group.md) is $(SU(2)\times U(1)_X)/\mathbb Z_2$: $\theta=\pi$ gives $\operatorname{diag}(-I_2,1)$, also the central element of the embedded [SU(2)](../../../../su-2-group.md). This finite intersection does not change the displayed [Lie algebra](../../../../lie-algebra-split.md) [branching of a representation](../../../../branching-of-a-representation.md) rules.
