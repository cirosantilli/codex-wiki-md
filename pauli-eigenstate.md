# Pauli eigenstate

↑ **Parent:** [Pauli operator](pauli-operator.md)

One of the six pure single-qubit eigenstates of $X,Y,Z$. Their [Bloch vectors](bloch-vector.md) are the six coordinate unit vectors with both signs. Their [convex hull](convex-hull.md) is the [stabilizer octahedron](stabilizer-octahedron.md).
