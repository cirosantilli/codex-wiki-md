# Free adjoint-scalar propagator in scalar-dependent gauge fixing

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar-dependent gauge fixing](scalar-dependent-gauge-fixing.md)

For [gauge fixing](gauge-fixing.md) with $F^a=\partial\cdot A^a+(n\cdot\partial\phi^a)/\sqrt2$, integrating out the [Nakanishi-Lautrup field](nakanishi-lautrup-field.md) produces gauge-scalar mixing. The [Schur complement](schur-complement.md) of the free gauge-field kernel cancels the extra $(n\cdot p)^2/2$ in the scalar kernel, leaving $\langle\phi^a(p)\phi^b(-p)\rangle=\delta^{ab}/p^2$. This is independent of the gauge vector $n$, but still depends on the full momentum $p$.
