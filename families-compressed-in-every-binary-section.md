# Families compressed in every binary section

↑ **Parent:** [Section compression in binary order](section-compression-in-binary-order.md)

If both sections for every coordinate are [binary initial segments](binary-initial-segment.md), then the family itself is a binary initial segment, apart from the possible family $(\mathcal P([n-1])\setminus\{[n-1]\})\cup\{\{n\}\}$. Any missing vertex earlier than a present vertex must disagree with it in every coordinate; the two are therefore complementary and consecutive in [binary order on the discrete cube](binary-order-on-the-discrete-cube.md), forcing this sole exceptional shape.
