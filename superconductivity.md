# Superconductivity

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical physics](statistical-physics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superconductivity)

[Superconductivity](superconductivity.md) is a phase with vanishing electrical resistance and expulsion of magnetic flux from the bulk, the [Meissner effect](meissner-effect.md). Conventional microscopic [BCS theory](bcs-theory.md) describes pairing of electrons into [Cooper pairs](cooper-pair.md).
