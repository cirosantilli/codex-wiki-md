# Real special linear group of degree two

↑ **Parent:** [Special linear group](special-linear-group.md)

This [Lie group](lie-group.md) consists of real two-by-two matrices of determinant one. Its [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) is the space of traceless real two-by-two matrices. Its [Exponential map of a Lie group](exponential-map-of-a-lie-group.md) is not onto: by the [Cayley-Hamilton theorem](cayley-hamilton-theorem.md), $A^2=-\det(A)I$ for traceless $A$, and its exponential has trace either $2\cosh s$ or $2\cos s$, hence never less than $-2$. The determinant-one matrix $\operatorname{diag}(-2,-1/2)$ is therefore outside its exponential image.
