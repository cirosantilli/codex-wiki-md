# Steric hindrance

↑ **Parent:** [Stereochemistry](stereochemistry-split.md)

[Steric hindrance](steric-hindrance.md) is the restriction of access to a reactive site or molecular arrangement by the spatial demands of surrounding groups. It can affect both relative stability and reaction barriers; these are distinct effects.
