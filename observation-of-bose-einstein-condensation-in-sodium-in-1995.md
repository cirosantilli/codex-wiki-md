# Observation of Bose-Einstein condensation in sodium in 1995

↑ **Parent:** [Dilute-gas Bose-Einstein condensation](dilute-gas-bose-einstein-condensation.md)

The 1995 [sodium](sodium.md) experiment combined [laser cooling](laser-cooling.md) with a magnetic quadrupole trap and a repulsive optical plug protecting its field zero. [Radio frequency](radio-frequency.md) evaporation preceded release and [absorption imaging](absorption-imaging.md) after [time of flight](time-of-flight.md). Lowering the evaporation cut produced broad thermal wings and a narrow core. Moving the plug changed the core's expansion orientation while the thermal distribution remained spherical: a control connecting the feature to confinement. Overlap of the two trapped pockets and residual switch-off acceleration limited quantitative shape comparisons; repulsive interactions affected expansion.[pp. 3969–3972, Figs. 1–4](bose-einstein-condensation-in-a-gas-of-sodium-atoms-by-k-b-davis-and-others.md)
