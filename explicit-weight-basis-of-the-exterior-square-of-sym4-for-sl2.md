# Explicit weight basis of the exterior square of Sym4 for sl2

↑ **Parent:** [sl2 Lie algebra](sl2-lie-algebra.md)

Let $e_i=x^{4-i}y^i$ and $w_{ij}=e_i\wedge e_j$ for $i<j$. For the [sl2 Lie algebra](sl2-lie-algebra.md), the raising and lowering operators are $E=x\partial_y$, $F=y\partial_x$, and $w_{ij}$ has [weight](weight-representation-theory.md) $8-2(i+j)$. A [weight basis](weight-basis.md) of the [irreducible representation](irreducible-representation.md) $L(6)$ is

$$
w_{01},\ w_{02},\ w_{03}+2w_{12},\ w_{04}+8w_{13},\ w_{14}+2w_{23},\ w_{24},\ w_{34}.
$$

A [weight basis](weight-basis.md) of the complementary $L(2)$ is

$$
w_{03}-3w_{12},\quad w_{04}-2w_{13},\quad w_{14}-3w_{23}.
$$

The first vector in each list is a [highest-weight vector](highest-weight-vector.md). Applying $F$ along the first list gives successive coefficients $3,2,1,12,5,2$; along the second it gives $1,2$. The final vectors are killed by $F$. At the overlapping [weights](weight-representation-theory.md) $2,0,-2$, the two listed vectors are independent, proving the [direct sum](direct-sum.md).
