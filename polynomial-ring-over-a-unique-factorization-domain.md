# Polynomial ring over a unique factorization domain

↑ **Parent:** [Gauss lemma for polynomials](gauss-lemma-for-polynomials.md)

If $R$ is a [unique factorization domain](unique-factorization-domain.md) with fraction field $F$, then $R[X]$ is a unique factorization domain. A primitive polynomial irreducible in $R[X]$ remains irreducible, hence prime, in the principal ideal domain $F[X]$; [Gauss lemma for polynomials](gauss-lemma-for-polynomials.md) brings divisibility back to $R[X]$.
