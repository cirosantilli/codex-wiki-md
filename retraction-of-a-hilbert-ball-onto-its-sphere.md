# Retraction of a Hilbert ball onto its sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Retraction](retraction.md)

The [closed unit ball](closed-unit-ball.md) $B$ in the infinite-dimensional real [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) $\ell^2$ admits a [retraction](retraction.md) onto its [unit sphere](unit-sphere.md). Write $S$ for the forward [unilateral shift operator](unilateral-shift-operator.md) and set $F(x)=\sqrt{1-\|x\|^2}\,e_0+Sx$. Then $\|F(x)\|=1$ and $F(x)\ne x$: equality would force all coordinates of $x$ to coincide, hence vanish, contradicting $F(0)=e_0$.

For $d=x-F(x)$, set

$$
r(x)=F(x)+\frac{2(1-\langle F(x),x\rangle)}{\|d\|^2}d.
$$

The nonvanishing denominator makes $r$ a [continuous map](continuous-map.md). Expanding its squared [norm](norm.md) gives $\|r(x)\|=1$. On $\|x\|=1$, the fraction is one, so $r(x)=x$. Thus the finite-dimensional [no-retraction theorem](no-retraction-theorem.md) does not extend to this [closed unit ball](closed-unit-ball.md).
