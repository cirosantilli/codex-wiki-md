# Cosine integral for a symmetric stable exponent

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetric stable distribution](symmetric-stable-distribution.md)

For $0<\alpha<2$, represent $u^{-1-\alpha}$ as $\Gamma(1+\alpha)^{-1}\int_0^\infty v^\alpha e^{-vu}\,dv$. The [Tonelli theorem](tonelli-theorem.md) applies to the nonnegative integrand. The inner [integral](integral.md) in $u$ is $1/[v(1+v^2)]$, leaving $\Gamma(1+\alpha)^{-1}\int_0^\infty v^{\alpha-1}/(1+v^2)\,dv$. Substitution $z=v^2$ and the [Euler beta function](beta-function.md) together with the [Gamma reflection formula](gamma-reflection-formula.md) give the result. The bounds at zero and infinity explain exactly the index range.
