# Minimum matching at a polytropic sonic point

↑ **Parent:** [Polytropic stellar wind](polytropic-stellar-wind.md)

Put $y=\rho r^{4/(\gamma+1)}$ and $\alpha=4(\gamma-1)/(\gamma+1)$. The [polytropic stellar wind](polytropic-stellar-wind.md) equations become $F(r)=Cr^\alpha+GMr^{\alpha-1}=G(y)=\gamma Ky^{\gamma-1}/(\gamma-1)+\dot M^2/(32\pi^2y^2)$. At a regular [sonic point](sonic-point.md) both derivatives vanish. Matching their minima allows the two positive-$y$ branches to join with finite slope; unequal minima either leave a forbidden radial interval or separate the subsonic and supersonic branches. This reduces a singular differential-equation crossing to a geometric minimum comparison.
