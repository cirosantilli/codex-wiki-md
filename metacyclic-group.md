# Metacyclic group

↑ **Parent:** [Group theory](group-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metacyclic_group)

A group is metacyclic when it has a cyclic normal subgroup whose quotient is cyclic. Every [dihedral group](dihedral-group.md) is metacyclic through its rotation subgroup.
