# Recessive affected-sib-pair likelihood

↑ **Parent:** [Affected-sib-pair analysis](affected-sib-pair-analysis.md)

For unaffected [genetic carriers](genetic-carrier.md) of a disease with complete [penetrance](penetrance.md) and [recessive inheritance](recessive-inheritance.md), suppose parental marker-disease [haplotype](haplotype.md) phases are equally likely and the marker is fully informative. Write $u=\theta(1-\theta)$ and $v=1/2-u$, where $\theta$ is the [recombination fraction](recombination-fraction.md). Conditional on sharing a parental marker copy, the probability that both siblings inherit that parent's disease allele is $[(1-\theta)^2+\theta^2]/2=v$; conditional on inheriting different marker copies it is $\theta(1-\theta)=u$. Independent paternal and maternal transmissions therefore give $L_0=u^2,L_1=uv,L_2=v^2$. Conditional instead on both siblings being affected, the IBD probabilities are $s^2,2s(1-s),(1-s)^2$, where $s=2u\in[0,1/2]$.
