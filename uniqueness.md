# Uniqueness

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical logic](mathematical-logic-split.md)

A solution is unique when any two objects satisfying the stated conditions are equal. This is an at-most-one assertion; [existence](existence.md) is a separate requirement. A common proof assumes two solutions, subtracts their equations, and proves that their difference vanishes. For a [linear differential equation](linear-differential-equation.md) this turns the task into a homogeneous problem with zero boundary or initial data.
