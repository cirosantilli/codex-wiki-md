# Clean-circle resolution of a Lagrangian surface immersion

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrangian surgery](lagrangian-surgery.md)

Perturb a clean self-intersection circle of a [Lagrangian immersion](lagrangian-immersion.md) by a [Morse function](morse-function.md) on $S^1$. Its minimum and maximum give two transverse double points. Resolving both by [Lagrangian surgery](lagrangian-surgery.md) adds two one-handles and changes the Euler characteristic by $-4$. One neck may be glued with the orientation-reversing choice, so the resolved surface is nonorientable.
