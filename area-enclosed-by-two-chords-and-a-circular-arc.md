# Area enclosed by two chords and a circular arc

↑ **Parent:** [Circular segment](circular-segment.md)

Take a [circle](circle.md) of [radius](radius.md) $R$, a vertex $A$ on its circumference, and two chords from $A$ symmetric about the diameter through $A$. If their [angle](angle.md) is $0\leq\theta\leq\pi$, choose the arc between their other endpoints that does not contain $A$. The [area](surface-area.md) bounded by the two chords and that arc is $R^2(\theta+\sin\theta)$. To calculate it, set $A=(0,R)$ and the other endpoints to $(\pm R\sin\theta,-R\cos\theta)$. Their [triangle](triangle.md) has area $R^2\sin\theta(1+\cos\theta)$, and the adjoining [circular segment](circular-segment.md), of central [angle](angle.md) $2\theta$, has area $R^2(\theta-\sin\theta\cos\theta)$. The sum gives the formula, including the limiting zero-area and full-disk cases.
