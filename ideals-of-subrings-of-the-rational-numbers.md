# Ideals of subrings of the rational numbers

↑ **Parent:** [Subring](subring.md)

Every [subring](subring.md) $R$ of the [rational numbers](rational-number.md) containing $\mathbb Z$ is a [principal ideal domain](principal-ideal-domain.md). If $a/b\in R$ is reduced, [Bézout's identity](bezout-s-identity.md) gives integers $u,v$ with $ua+vb=1$, hence $1/b=u(a/b)+v\in R$. Thus every reduced denominator occurring in $R$ is a [unit](unit-in-a-ring.md).

For an [ideal](ideal.md) $I$, write $I\cap\mathbb Z=d\mathbb Z$. Then $dR\subseteq I$. If $a/b\in I$ is reduced, $a=b(a/b)\in I\cap\mathbb Z$, so $a=dc$ for some integer $c$. Since $1/b\in R$, $a/b=d(c/b)\in dR$. Therefore $I=dR$, including the zero ideal. This is a concrete instance of extending and contracting [ideals](ideal.md) under [localization of a ring](localization-of-a-ring.md).
