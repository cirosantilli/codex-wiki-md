<h1 id="history-of-vieta-s-formulas">History of Vieta's formulas</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vieta's formulas](vieta-s-formulas.md)

[François Viète](francois-viete.md) studied the connection between positive [roots of a polynomial](root-of-a-polynomial.md) and the [coefficients](coefficient.md) of the powers of the unknown. His 1591 presentation of [algebraic notation](algebraic-notation.md) used letters for both known and unknown quantities, with consonants for known quantities and vowels for unknown quantities. That move makes it possible to express a relation for whole families of [polynomials](polynomial-split.md), rather than only a numerical instance. His historical setting concerned positive roots and expressions satisfying [dimensional homogeneity](dimensional-homogeneity.md); the modern [splitting field](splitting-field.md) formulation above is a broader statement.[MacTutor, François Viète: paragraphs on the 1591 algebraic notation, known and unknown quantities, and positive roots and coefficients](francois-viete-by-john-joseph-o-connor-and-edmund-frederick-robertson.md)
