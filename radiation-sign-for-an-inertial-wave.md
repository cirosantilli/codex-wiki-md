# Radiation sign for an inertial wave

↑ **Parent:** [Inertial wave](inertial-wave.md)

For fixed vertical [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) $k_z$, the [inertial-wave dispersion relation](inertial-wave-dispersion-relation.md) is $\omega^2=4\Omega^2k_z^2/(k_x^2+k_z^2)$. Its horizontal [group velocity](group-velocity.md) is the [derivative](derivative.md) displayed above, opposite in sign to $\omega k_x$. In a half-channel $x>0$, an outgoing propagating mode therefore requires $\omega k_x<0$. With time factor $e^{-i\omega t}$ and $q=|k_z|\sqrt{4\Omega^2/\omega^2-1}$, choose $e^{-i\operatorname{sgn}(\omega)qx}$. Above the inertial-[frequency](frequency.md) cutoff, the horizontal [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) is imaginary and the appropriate [radiation condition](radiation-condition.md) instead selects the exponentially decaying solution. Energy transport, rather than the direction of phase travel alone, determines the outgoing sign.
