# Planet-encounter relative velocity

↑ **Parent:** [Tisserand parameter](tisserand-parameter.md)

In units where a circular [planet](planet.md) has orbital radius and speed one, the particle's relative speed $u$ outside a close encounter satisfies $u^2=3-T$, to leading order in the [planet](planet.md)-to-[star](star.md) [mass](mass.md) ratio. This follows from the [vis-viva equation](vis-viva-equation.md) and $u^2=v^2+1-2h_z$ at the encounter radius. A short conservative encounter changes the direction of this relative velocity while preserving its magnitude.
