# Monotone circuit complexity

↑ **Parent:** [Circuit complexity](circuit-complexity.md)

Monotone circuit complexity studies Boolean circuits built only from AND and OR gates, without negations. Such circuits compute [monotone Boolean functions](monotone-boolean-function.md), but some monotone functions require much larger monotone circuits than unrestricted circuits.
