# Single-photon avalanche diode

↑ **Parent:** [Avalanche photodiode](avalanche-photodiode.md)

A [single-photon detector](single-photon-detector.md) based on a reverse-biased [photodiode](photodiode.md) operated above its breakdown voltage. A carrier generated by [photon](photon.md) absorption can trigger a self-sustaining [impact ionization](impact-ionization.md) avalanche. Quenching lowers the bias to stop that avalanche; recovery creates [detector dead time](detector-dead-time.md). Random internally generated carriers can also trigger indistinguishable pulses, producing [dark counts](dark-count.md). [Single-photon avalanche diodes](single-photon-avalanche-diode.md) ordinarily detect an event rather than resolve the incident [photon number](photon-number.md).
