# Hydrocarbon

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical compound](chemical-compound.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrocarbon)

A [hydrocarbon](hydrocarbon.md) contains only [carbon](carbon.md) and [hydrogen](hydrogen.md). A [fatty acid](fatty-acid.md) also contains [oxygen](oxygen.md); calling its long chain a [hydrocarbon](hydrocarbon.md) region does not make the whole [fatty acid](fatty-acid.md) a [hydrocarbon](hydrocarbon.md). See [IUPAC Gold Book: hydrocarbons](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/H02889).
