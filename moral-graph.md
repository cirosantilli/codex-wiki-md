# Moral graph

↑ **Parent:** [Directed acyclic graph](directed-acyclic-graph.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moral_graph)

The moral graph of a [Directed acyclic graph](directed-acyclic-graph.md) joins every pair of vertices with a common child and then replaces every directed edge by an undirected edge.
