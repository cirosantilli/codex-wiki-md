# Scatter plot

↑ **Parent:** [Descriptive statistics](descriptive-statistics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scatter_plot)

A [scatter plot](scatter-plot.md) represents paired quantitative observations as points in a coordinate plane. Group labels, uncertainty and transformations can reveal nonlinear patterns or heterogeneous associations that a single correlation coefficient conceals. A visual association does not establish causation.
