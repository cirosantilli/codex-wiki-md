# Risk difference

↑ **Parent:** [Epidemiology](epidemiology.md)

The difference between two outcome [probabilities](probability.md) over specified populations and follow-up periods. With intervention risk $p_1$ and comparison risk $p_0$, a beneficial reduction in an adverse outcome has $RD<0$ and absolute risk reduction $p_0-p_1$. A causal interpretation requires a suitable design or identification assumptions; a simple difference between selected observational groups is not automatically a treatment effect.
