# Semi-axis

↑ **Parent:** [Ellipsoid](ellipsoid.md)

Half the length of one principal diameter of an [ellipsoid](ellipsoid.md). For a centred [quadratic form](quadratic-form-split.md) $x^TSx=C$ with $S$ symmetric positive definite and $C>0$, its semi-axis lengths are $\sqrt{C/\lambda_i}$, where $\lambda_i$ are the [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) of $S$. The shortest centre-to-surface distance corresponds to the largest eigenvalue.
