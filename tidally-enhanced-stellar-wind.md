# Tidally enhanced stellar wind

↑ **Parent:** [Wind mass transfer in a binary star](wind-mass-transfer-in-a-binary-star.md)

A tidally enhanced stellar wind is a model in which a companion increases a giant's wind mass loss as the giant approaches its [Roche lobe](roche-lobe.md). Removing envelope mass before contact can reduce or reverse the mass ratio and make subsequent [Roche-lobe overflow](roche-lobe-overflow.md) more stable. This is one proposed route to wide systems with Algol-like reversed evolutionary ordering. [Van der Sluys's compact-binary study, section 5.7.1](https://www.astro.ru.nl/~sluys/Thesis/pdfs/vdSluys_thesis_screen.pdf) discusses the enhanced-wind scenario and its effect on mass-transfer stability.
