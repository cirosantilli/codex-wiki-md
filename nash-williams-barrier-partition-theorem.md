# Nash-Williams barrier partition theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Barrier in better-quasi-order theory](barrier-in-better-quasi-order-theory.md)

Every finite coloring of a [barrier in better-quasi-order theory](barrier-in-better-quasi-order-theory.md) has an infinite restriction on which all barrier members have the same color. It extends finite-dimensional [Ramsey's theorem](ramsey-s-theorem.md) from fixed-length subsets to variable-length initial segments. A proof proceeds by well-founded induction on the initial-segment tree and a fusion construction selecting successively nested infinite tails. It implies that every finite [set](set-split.md) with the equality order is a [better-quasi-ordering](better-quasi-ordering.md). The barrier/front partition proof is given in Section 3.2 of [https://arxiv.org/pdf/1604.05866](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1604.05866) .
