# Prevariety

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic variety](algebraic-variety.md)

In the classical convention over an [algebraically closed field](algebraically-closed-field.md) $k$, a prevariety is an [irreducible topological space](irreducible-topological-space.md) with a [sheaf of rings](sheaf-of-rings.md) which has a finite [open cover](open-cover.md) by [affine varieties](affine-algebraic-set.md), with compatible local identifications. A prevariety need not be separated. A classical [algebraic variety](algebraic-variety.md) is a separated prevariety: its [diagonal morphism](diagonal-morphism.md) has closed image. Some authors allow reducible prevarieties and varieties; the separation criterion and product construction remain the same under that convention.
