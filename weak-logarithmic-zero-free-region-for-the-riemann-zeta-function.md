# Weak logarithmic zero-free region for the Riemann zeta function

↑ **Parent:** [Zero-free region of the Riemann zeta function](zero-free-region-of-the-riemann-zeta-function.md)

The logarithm of the [Euler product](euler-product.md) and $3+4\cos\theta+\cos2\theta\ge0$ give $\zeta(\sigma)^3|\zeta(\sigma+it)|^4|\zeta(\sigma+2it)|\ge1$. At $\sigma=1+a/\log^9|t|$, upper bounds $\zeta(\sigma)\ll\log^9|t|/a$ and $|\zeta(\sigma+2it)|\ll\log|t|$ imply the lower bound $|\zeta|\gg a^{3/4}\log^{-7}|t|$. The [Cauchy estimate for derivatives](cauchy-estimate.md) gives $|\zeta'|\ll\log^2|t|$ nearby, allowing a sufficiently small leftward displacement of order $\log^{-9}|t|$. This elementary region is weaker than the classical logarithmic region but gives an explicit reciprocal bound by simple estimates.
