# Normal-incidence acoustic velocity reflection coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Acoustic reflection and transmission at an interface](acoustic-reflection-and-transmission-at-an-interface.md)

Fix a positive normal from medium 1 toward medium 2, and define all [velocity](velocity.md) amplitudes in that coordinate. A right-going linear [sound wave](acoustic-wave.md) satisfies $p'=Zu'$ and a left-going wave satisfies $p'=-Zu'$, with [acoustic impedance](acoustic-impedance.md) $Z=\rho c_s$. Continuity gives $u_i+u_r=u_t$ and $Z_1(u_i-u_r)=Z_2u_t$. Eliminating $u_t$ gives the displayed reflected-to-incident velocity ratio. The pressure reflection coefficient is its negative. A surface mass, surface restoring force or oblique incidence changes the boundary calculation.
