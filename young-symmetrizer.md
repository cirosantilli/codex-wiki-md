# Young symmetrizer

↑ **Parent:** [Young tableau](young-tableau.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Young_symmetrizer)

For a [Young tableau](young-tableau.md) $t$, let $r_t$ sum its row permutations and $c_t$ be the signed sum of its column permutations in the [group algebra](group-algebra.md) of the [symmetric group](symmetric-group.md). The product $h_t=c_tr_t$ is a Young symmetrizer. The other order is another conventional realization. It satisfies $h_t^2=H_\lambda h_t$, where $H_\lambda$ is the [hook product of a partition](hook-product-of-a-partition.md), so division by that scalar gives a primitive [idempotent](idempotent.md) in characteristic zero. Acting on a [tensor power](tensor-power.md) constructs a [Schur module](schur-module.md).
