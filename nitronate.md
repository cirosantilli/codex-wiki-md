# Nitronate

↑ **Parent:** [Ion](ion.md)

A [nitronate](nitronate.md) has negative charge delocalized between [carbon](carbon.md) and nitro oxygens. Nitroarene sigma-adducts can have [nitronate](nitronate.md)-like resonance descriptions during nucleophilic substitution.
