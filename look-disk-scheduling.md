# LOOK disk scheduling

↑ **Parent:** [Disk scheduling](disk-scheduling.md)

Sweep in one direction through pending requests, reversing at the last pending track rather than travelling to the disk's physical endpoint. It shares the directional sweep of [SCAN disk scheduling](scan-disk-scheduling.md) but can avoid movement into an empty end region.
