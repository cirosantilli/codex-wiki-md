# Nonintegrability of a radial triangular Fourier cutoff in three dimensions

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier multiplier](fourier-multiplier.md)

The inverse [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) of $(1-|\xi|)_+$ in three dimensions, with $a=2\pi|x|$, is $-4\pi\sin a/a^3+8\pi(1-\cos a)/a^4$. Its leading oscillatory $|x|^{-3}$ tail is not absolutely integrable against the radial [volume](volume.md) element $4\pi|x|^2d|x|$. Multiplying by a Gaussian [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) retains a nonzero boundary-[derivative](derivative.md) tail, so the radial cutoff need not map even [Schwartz functions](schwartz-function.md) into $L^1$. A [tensor product](tensor-product.md) of one-dimensional triangular cutoffs is a valid [approximate identity](approximate-identity.md), and must not be confused with this Euclidean radial cutoff.
