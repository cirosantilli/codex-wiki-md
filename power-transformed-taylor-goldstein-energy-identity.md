<h1 id="power-transformed-taylor-goldstein-energy-identity">Power-transformed Taylor–Goldstein energy identity</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Taylor–Goldstein equation](taylor-goldstein-equation.md)

For a mode with nonreal [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md) $c$, let $V=U-c$, choose a continuous branch of $V^a$, and put $\widehat w=V^aq$. Under impermeable boundary conditions, multiplying the transformed [Taylor–Goldstein equation](taylor-goldstein-equation.md) by $V^{2a}\overline q$ and applying [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) gives

$$
\int V^{2a}(|q'|^2+k^2|q|^2)dz=\int\left[\{N^2+a(a-1)(U')^2\}V^{2a-2}+(a-1)U''V^{2a-1}\right]|q|^2dz.
$$

Choosing $a=1/2$ gives the [Miles–Howard theorem](miles-howard-theorem.md); choosing $a=1$ makes the real [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md) of an unstable mode a weighted mean of $U$.
