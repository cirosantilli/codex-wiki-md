# Right Noetherian domain

↑ **Parent:** [Right Noetherian ring](right-noetherian-ring.md)

A [noncommutative domain](noncommutative-domain.md) whose [right ideals](right-ideal.md) satisfy the [ascending chain condition](ascending-chain-condition.md) is a right Noetherian domain. Its right regular [module](module-mathematics.md) is a [uniform module](uniform-module.md), so any two nonzero principal [right ideals](right-ideal.md) intersect. Thus its nonzero elements satisfy the [right Ore condition](right-ore-condition.md) and it embeds in a [division ring](division-ring.md) by [Ore localization](ore-localization.md).
