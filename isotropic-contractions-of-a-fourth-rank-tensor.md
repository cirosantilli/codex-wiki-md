# Isotropic contractions of a fourth-rank tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Isotropic tensor](isotropic-tensor.md)

Contract an [isotropic tensor](isotropic-tensor.md) with the [Kronecker delta](kronecker-delta.md) or the [Levi-Civita symbol](levi-civita-symbol.md), using invariance under proper rotations. The three displayed contractions have ranks one, two and three. The only isotropic vector is zero; an [isotropic second-rank tensor](isotropic-second-rank-tensor.md) is a scalar delta, and an [isotropic third-rank tensor](isotropic-third-rank-tensor.md) is a scalar epsilon. For $T_{ijkl}=\alpha\delta_{ij}\delta_{kl}+\beta\delta_{ik}\delta_{jl}+\gamma\delta_{il}\delta_{jk}$, direct contraction gives $\mu=3\alpha+\beta+\gamma$ and $\nu=\beta-\gamma$.
