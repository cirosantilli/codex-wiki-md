# Stokes equation

↑ **Parent:** [Stokes flow](stokes-flow-split.md)

For an incompressible Newtonian fluid with negligible inertia, the Stokes equations are

$$
-\nabla p+\mu\nabla^2\mathbf u+\mathbf f=0,
\qquad
\nabla\mathbin\cdot\mathbf u=0.
$$
