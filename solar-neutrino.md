# Solar neutrino

↑ **Parent:** [Sun](sun.md)

A solar neutrino is a [neutrino](neutrino.md) produced by a [nuclear reaction](nuclear-reaction.md) in the [Sun](sun.md), principally through the [proton–proton chain](proton-proton-chain.md) and [CNO cycle](cno-cycle.md). Its weak interactions allow it to escape directly from the [stellar core](stellar-core.md). Accounting for [neutrino oscillation](neutrino-oscillation.md) allows measurements of the different detected flavors to test the nuclear reaction power and rates independently of escaping [photons](photon.md).
