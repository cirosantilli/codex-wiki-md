# Dominant root-lattice highest weights have zero weight

↑ **Parent:** [Highest-weight representation](highest-weight-representation.md)

If a [dominant integral weight](dominant-integral-weight.md) $\lambda$ lies in the [root lattice](root-lattice.md), its finite-dimensional irreducible [highest-weight representation](highest-weight-representation.md) contains zero as a weight. First $\lambda$ is a nonnegative integral combination of [simple roots](simple-root.md): a negative part would have negative [inner product](inner-product.md) with $\lambda$, contradicting dominance. For any nonzero weight $\mu=\sum m_i\alpha_i$ with $m_i\geq0$, the identity $(\mu,\mu)=\sum m_i(\mu,\alpha_i)>0$ supplies an index with $m_i>0$ and $\langle\mu,\alpha_i^\vee\rangle>0$. By [Injectivity of sl2 lowering above weight zero](injectivity-of-sl2-lowering-above-weight-zero.md), applying the corresponding [lowering operator](lowering-operator.md) produces a nonzero vector of weight $\mu-\alpha_i$. Repetition reaches zero.
