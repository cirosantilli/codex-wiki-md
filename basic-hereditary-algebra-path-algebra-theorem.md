# Basic hereditary algebra path-algebra theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Hereditary ring](hereditary-ring.md)

Let $A$ be a finite-dimensional [basic algebra](basic-algebra.md) over an [algebraically closed field](algebraically-closed-field.md) and a [hereditary ring](hereditary-ring.md). The radicals of its indecomposable projectives are projective and satisfy $JP_i\cong\bigoplus_jP_j^{a_{ij}}$, where $a_{ij}$ are the [Ext quiver](ext-quiver.md) arrow multiplicities. Hence $\dim P_i=1+\sum_ja_{ij}\dim P_j$, forcing strict dimension decrease along arrows and therefore acyclicity. Lifting an arrow basis in $J/J^2$ gives a surjective [path algebra](path-algebra.md) homomorphism $kQ\to A$. The number of paths starting at $i$ satisfies the same dimension recursion. Equality of total dimensions proves the homomorphism is an isomorphism. Sums of vertex [idempotents](idempotent.md) over connected components are precisely the primitive [central idempotents](central-idempotent.md), so components give the blocks.
