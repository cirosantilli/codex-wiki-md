# Opposite intrinsic parities of a Dirac particle and antiparticle

↑ **Parent:** [Intrinsic parity](intrinsic-parity.md)

In the [Dirac representation of the gamma matrices](dirac-representation-of-the-gamma-matrices.md), the [parity symmetry in quantum field theory](parity-symmetry-in-quantum-field-theory.md) action $\psi_P(x)=\gamma^0\psi(x_P)$ and the [charge conjugation](charge-conjugation.md) action $\psi_C=i\gamma^2\psi^*$ obey $(\psi_P)_C(x)=-\gamma^0\psi_C(x_P)$, since $\gamma^0$ is real and anticommutes with $\gamma^2$. At rest, positive-frequency [Dirac spinors](dirac-spinor.md) have nonzero upper components and negative-frequency [Dirac spinors](dirac-spinor.md) have nonzero lower components; they are respectively $+1$ and $-1$ eigenvectors of $\gamma^0$. With the common conventional real intrinsic-parity phase, particle and antiparticle therefore have opposite [intrinsic parity](intrinsic-parity.md). The overall sign is conventional, whereas their relative sign is fixed.
