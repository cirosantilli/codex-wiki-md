# Thermal and electrical relaxation times in metals

↑ **Parent:** [Wiedemann-Franz law](wiedemann-franz-law.md)

For a degenerate isotropic parabolic electron band, [electrical conductivity](electrical-conductivity.md) is $\sigma=ne^2\tau_\sigma/m$ and electronic [thermal conductivity](thermal-conductivity.md) is $\kappa_e=\pi^2nk_B^2T\tau_\kappa/(3m)$. Their ratio is $\kappa_e/(\sigma T)=L_0\tau_\kappa/\tau_\sigma$, where $L_0=(\pi^2/3)(k_B/e)^2$ is the [Lorenz number](lorenz-number.md). Nearly elastic scattering that relaxes charge and heat currents similarly gives $\tau_\kappa\simeq\tau_\sigma$ and the [Wiedemann-Franz law](wiedemann-franz-law.md). Inelastic scattering can relax [heat current](heat-current.md) and directed [momentum](momentum-split.md) differently, invalidating the simplest identification even in a good [metal](metal.md).
