# Ideal-form correspondence for imaginary quadratic fields

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal class group](ideal-class-group.md)

For a negative fundamental discriminant $D$, primitive positive definite integral [binary quadratic forms](binary-quadratic-form.md) of discriminant $D$, modulo proper equivalence, correspond bijectively to the [ideal class group](ideal-class-group.md) of $\mathbb Q(\sqrt D)$. The displayed ideal gives one conventional choice of this correspondence. [Reduced positive definite binary quadratic forms](reduced-positive-definite-binary-quadratic-form.md) give unique class representatives with the usual boundary sign convention, making class-number computations finite.
