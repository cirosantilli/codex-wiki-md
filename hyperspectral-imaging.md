# Hyperspectral imaging

↑ **Parent:** [Remote sensing](remote-sensing.md)

Imaging in many narrow, usually adjacent wavelength bands. Relative to broad-band [multispectral imaging](multispectral-imaging.md), it can resolve narrower [mineral](mineral.md) absorption features in [spectral reflectance](spectral-reflectance.md), helping distinguish surface [lithologies](lithology.md), at the cost of data volume and often reduced signal per band.
