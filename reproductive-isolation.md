# Reproductive isolation

↑ **Parent:** [Speciation](speciation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reproductive_isolation)

[Reproductive isolation](reproductive-isolation.md) consists of barriers that reduce [gene flow](gene-flow.md) through [sexual reproduction](sexual-reproduction.md). Distinguish [premating isolation](premating-isolation.md) from barriers after mating. Barriers before [fertilization](fertilization.md) also include incompatible [gametes](gamete.md); barriers after [fertilization](fertilization.md) can reduce offspring survival or fertility. [§18.2, “Reproductive Isolation”, opening paragraphs, gametic barriers and hybrid inviability/sterility](biology-2e-by-mary-ann-clark-and-others.md)

An assay should state which step it measures. Observing fewer cross-group matings does not measure offspring fertility; observing [fertilization](fertilization.md) does not demonstrate that the offspring will reproduce. These distinctions make a partial barrier informative without declaring completed [speciation](speciation.md).
