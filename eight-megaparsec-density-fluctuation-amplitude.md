# Eight-megaparsec density fluctuation amplitude

↑ **Parent:** [Matter power spectrum](matter-power-spectrum.md)

The parameter $\sigma_8$ is the present linear rms [density contrast](density-contrast.md) averaged in a spherical top-hat of comoving radius $8h^{-1}\,\mathrm{Mpc}$, with $h=H_0/(100\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}\,Mpc^{-1}})$. Thus $\sigma_8^2=(2\pi^2)^{-1}\int_0^\infty k^2P_m(k,0)|W(kR_8)|^2\,dk$. It specifies an amplitude on a chosen scale rather than the complete [matter power spectrum](matter-power-spectrum.md); converting cluster abundance into this parameter also requires a spectral shape and background cosmology.
