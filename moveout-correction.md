# Moveout correction

↑ **Parent:** [Seismic reflection survey](seismic-reflection-survey.md)

Adjustment for the change in reflected [travel time](travel-time.md) with source–receiver separation. A horizontal-reflector approximation gives $t(x)^2=t_0^2+x^2/V_{\mathrm{NMO}}^2$ under appropriate assumptions; a [velocity model](velocity-model.md) is required.
