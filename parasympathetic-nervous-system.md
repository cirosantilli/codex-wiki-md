# Parasympathetic nervous system

↑ **Parent:** [Autonomic nervous system](autonomic-nervous-system.md)

A division of the [autonomic nervous system](autonomic-nervous-system.md) contributing to regulation of organs, including control of the [heart rate](heart-rate.md). Changes in cardiac activity can reflect interacting sympathetic and parasympathetic influences rather than a single scalar quantity called activation.
