# Geophysics

↑ **Parent:** [Branches of physics](branches-of-physics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geophysics)

Geophysics applies [physics](physics-split.md) and [mathematics](mathematics-split.md) to the structure, composition, and evolution of [planets](planet.md), including [Earth](earth.md). It connects measurements with mechanisms: [plate tectonics](plate-tectonics.md) explains moving crust, the [geodynamo](geodynamo.md) explains a planetary [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md), and [albedo](albedo.md) connects reflected sunlight to a [planetary surface energy balance](planetary-surface-energy-balance.md). Even a small model can reveal a useful organizing principle, such as the competition between [ice-ocean albedo feedback](ice-ocean-albedo-feedback.md) and stabilizing [thermal radiation](thermal-radiation.md).
