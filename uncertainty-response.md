# Uncertainty response

↑ **Parent:** [Metacognitive control](metacognitive-control.md)

An action permitting a subject to decline a difficult test, seek information or select an explicit uncertainty option. Reward, delay and task contingencies influence the action; an uncertainty response is an operational behaviour rather than a direct observation of subjective awareness.
