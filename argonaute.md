# Argonaute

↑ **Parent:** [RNA-binding protein](rna-binding-protein.md)

A [protein](protein-split.md) family that binds small guide [RNAs](ribonucleic-acid.md) and participates in sequence-directed regulation. Guide complementarity can identify targets, but cleavage activity varies among family members and pathways.
