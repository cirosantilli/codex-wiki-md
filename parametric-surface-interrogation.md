# Parametric surface interrogation

↑ **Parent:** [Parametric surface](parametric-surface.md)

Core numerical enquiries return a surface point and its [derivatives](derivative.md) through second order, together with the parameter domain, patch boundaries and regularity information. At a regular point these determine its [tangent plane](tangent-plane.md), [normal vector](normal-vector.md), first and [second fundamental forms](second-fundamental-form-split.md) and [curvatures](curvature.md). Subdivision and certified enclosing volumes provide spatial search and intersection support. Singular charts or discontinuity boundaries require one-sided or alternative-chart information; a zero [cross product](cross-product.md) does not define a unique [normal vector](normal-vector.md).
