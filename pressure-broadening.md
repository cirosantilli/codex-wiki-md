# Pressure broadening

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral line](spectral-line.md)

Collisions and fluctuating neighboring-particle electric fields alter atomic energy levels and coherence times, broadening a [spectral line](spectral-line.md). In a simple impact treatment the coherence-decay rate gives a Lorentzian width of order $\Gamma/(2\pi)$; the precise definition of full or half width must be stated. Unlike [Doppler broadening](doppler-broadening.md), this depends primarily on collision rates and perturber density. In stellar [opacity](opacity.md) calculations it alters line overlap and the low-opacity windows entering the [Rosseland mean opacity](rosseland-mean-opacity.md).
