# Poisson offspring clusters directed by their parent process

↑ **Parent:** [Cox process](cox-process.md)

When each parent has a Poisson number of independently displaced offspring, the conditional offspring process has the displayed directing measure. For disjoint target sets, [multinomial distribution](multinomial-distribution.md) splitting of a parent's Poisson count gives joint [probability generating function](probability-generating-function.md) $\exp(\mu\sum_j\kappa(A_j-z)(s_j-1))$. Multiply over [independent](independent-random-variables.md) parents to obtain [independent](independent-random-variables.md) Poisson counts conditional on the parents. Compactly supported displacement ensures local finiteness for a locally finite parent process.
