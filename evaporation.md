# Evaporation

↑ **Parent:** [Vaporization](vaporization.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evaporation)

[Evaporation](evaporation.md) transfers molecules from a [liquid](liquid.md) surface into the surrounding vapour. It can occur below the [boiling](boiling.md) temperature, and competes with condensation from the vapour. Its net rate depends on the surrounding [vapor pressure](vapor-pressure.md) and transport conditions. Removing [latent heat](latent-heat.md) through evaporation can cool the remaining liquid.
