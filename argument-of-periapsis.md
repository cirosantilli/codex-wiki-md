# Argument of periapsis

↑ **Parent:** [Orbital element](orbital-element.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argument_of_periapsis)

The [argument of periapsis](argument-of-periapsis.md) is the angle in the [orbital plane](orbital-plane.md) from the [ascending node](ascending-node.md) to [periapsis](periapsis.md), measured in the direction of motion. It differs from the [longitude of periapsis](longitude-of-periapsis.md) $\varpi=\Omega+\omega$. For an inclined orbit this sum is a conventional longitude, not generally the azimuth of the projected periapsis. The argument is undefined for a circular orbit and the node-based description is singular when the orbit and reference plane coincide.
