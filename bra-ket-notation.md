# Bra-ket notation

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum state](quantum-state.md)

A ket $|\psi\rangle$ denotes a [vector](vector.md) representing a [quantum state](quantum-state.md), and its bra $\langle\psi|$ is the linear functional taking $|\phi\rangle$ to $\langle\psi|\phi\rangle$. The map from a ket to its bra is conjugate-linear. In the physics convention, $\langle\psi|\phi\rangle$ is conjugate-linear in $\psi$ and linear in $\phi$. The [adjoint operator](adjoint-operator.md) is characterized by $\langle\psi|A\phi\rangle=\langle A^*\psi|\phi\rangle$. Products such as $|\psi\rangle\langle\phi|$ are rank-one operators, while $\langle\phi|\psi\rangle$ is a scalar [inner product](inner-product-split.md).
