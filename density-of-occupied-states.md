# Density of occupied states

↑ **Parent:** [Density of states](density-of-states.md)

The density of available one-particle states multiplied by their mean occupation. For equilibrium electrons, $Z(E)=D(E)f(E)$ with the [Fermi-Dirac distribution](fermi-dirac-distribution.md) $f$. The integral of $Z$ counts electrons; the integral of $D$ counts available states.
