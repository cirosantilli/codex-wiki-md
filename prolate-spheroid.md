# Prolate spheroid

↑ **Parent:** [Spheroid](spheroid.md)

A [prolate spheroid](prolate-spheroid.md) is an [ellipsoid](ellipsoid.md) with two equal shorter semiaxes and a longer symmetry-axis semiaxis. Its meridional section is an [ellipse](ellipse.md) rotated about its major axis. In the coordinates $R^2/(\lambda+\alpha)+z^2/(\lambda+\beta)=1$ with $\beta>\alpha$, a fixed regular $\lambda$ has equatorial radius $\sqrt{\lambda+\alpha}$ and polar radius $\sqrt{\lambda+\beta}$; its focal separation is independent of $\lambda$, giving a confocal family.
