# Cooling flow

↑ **Parent:** [Galaxy cluster](galaxy-cluster.md)

An inward flow driven by loss of thermal [pressure](pressure-split.md) as hot gas undergoes [radiative cooling](radiative-cooling.md). A classical unheated cluster model predicts substantial central cooling and deposition of cold material. [active-galactic-nucleus feedback](active-galactic-nucleus-feedback.md), transport and other heating can reduce the actual inflow; an [X-ray](x-ray.md) cooling [luminosity](luminosity.md) is not automatically a measured mass-deposition rate.
