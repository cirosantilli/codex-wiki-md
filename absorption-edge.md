# Absorption edge

↑ **Parent:** [X-ray attenuation](x-ray-attenuation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Absorption_edge)

An [absorption edge](absorption-edge.md) is an abrupt change in [photon absorption](photon-absorption.md) as incident [photon](photon.md) [energy](energy-split.md) crosses an electronic excitation threshold. For inner-shell [photoionization](photoionization.md), a new vacancy-production channel opens near the shell's [binding energy](binding-energy.md). This threshold concerns incident [energy](energy-split.md); a [Characteristic X-ray](characteristic-x-ray.md) concerns a subsequent difference of atomic [energies](energy-split.md). They are different observations. Barkla's lecture explicitly treats characteristic emission as requiring a critical excitation condition, while discussing characteristic radiation separately from scattering.[Nobel Lectures, Physics 1901–1921; Barkla, Characteristic Röntgen radiation, printed p. 395, PDF p. 135: shorter-wavelength excitation and critical condition](nobel-lectures-physics-1901-1921/characteristic-rontgen-radiation-by-charles-glover-barkla.md)

In the stipulated [X-ray fluorescence](x-ray-fluorescence.md) model, the K threshold is $10.0\,\mathrm{keV}$, whereas the K lines are $8.5$ and $9.7\,\mathrm{keV}$. An absorber made of a different material could have its own [absorption edge](absorption-edge.md) between those line [energies](energy-split.md), preferentially removing the upper line. Actual near-edge structure and attenuation values require material data; the following deliberately chosen coefficients are not such data.
