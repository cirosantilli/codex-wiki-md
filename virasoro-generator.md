# Virasoro generator

↑ **Parent:** [Virasoro algebra](virasoro-algebra.md)

[Virasoro generators](virasoro-generator.md) are the contour modes of the holomorphic stress tensor in a [two-dimensional conformal field theory](two-dimensional-conformal-field-theory.md). Their commutators are $[L_m,L_n]=(m-n)L_{m+n}+c(m^3-m)\delta_{m+n,0}/12$. A contour around an operator insertion extracts the corresponding coefficient of its stress-tensor [operator product expansion](operator-product-expansion.md). $L_{-1}$ generates translations, $L_0$ measures holomorphic weight, and all positive generators annihilate a [primary state](primary-state.md). An independent antiholomorphic copy acts on the other weight.
