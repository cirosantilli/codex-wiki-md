# Subgiant branch

↑ **Parent:** [Hertzsprung-Russell diagram](hertzsprung-russell-diagram.md)

The population sequence connecting the [main-sequence turnoff](main-sequence-turnoff.md) to the lower [red giant branch](red-giant-branch.md) in a coeval stellar population, tracing post-core-hydrogen-burning evolution before the large red-giant expansion. It is a feature of a population diagram associated with [subgiant](subgiant.md) stars, rather than a separate universal line followed by every stellar mass.
