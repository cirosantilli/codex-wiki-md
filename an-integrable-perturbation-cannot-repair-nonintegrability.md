# An integrable perturbation cannot repair nonintegrability

↑ **Parent:** [Lebesgue integrable function](lebesgue-integrable-function.md)

If $u$ is [Lebesgue integrable](lebesgue-integrable-function.md) and $v$ is measurable on the same [measure space](measure-space.md), then $u+v$ is integrable exactly when $v$ is integrable. One implication follows from $|u+v|\leq|u|+|v|$; the other follows from $|v|\leq|u+v|+|u|$. Thus adding a [function](function-split.md) with finite [integral](integral-split.md) of its [absolute value](absolute-value.md) cannot remove a failure of [absolute integrability](integrability.md). This is the [vector space](vector-space-split.md) property of [L1 space](l1-space.md) stated as a useful test for a sum of terms with different decay rates.
