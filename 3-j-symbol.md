# 3-j symbol

↑ **Parent:** [Clebsch-Gordan coefficients](clebsch-gordan-coefficients.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3-j_symbol)

A symmetrized form of the [Clebsch-Gordan coefficients](clebsch-gordan-coefficients.md) used for coupling three angular momenta. The relation is $\langle j_1m_1,j_2m_2|j_3m_3\rangle=(-1)^{j_1-j_2+m_3}\sqrt{2j_3+1}\begin{pmatrix}j_1&j_2&j_3\\m_1&m_2&-m_3\end{pmatrix}$. The symbol vanishes unless the magnetic indices sum to zero and the angular momenta obey the triangle and integrality conditions. Products of two such symbols express the [Gaunt integral](gaunt-integral.md); the zero-magnetic-index symbol enforces even total multipole for integer spherical harmonics.
