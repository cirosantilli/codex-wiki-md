# Cylinder in a simple shear Stokes flow

↑ **Parent:** [Stokes flow](stokes-flow-split.md)

For a stationary cylinder of radius $a$ in the far-field shear $(\Gamma y,0)$, the [stream function](stream-function.md) is $\psi=(\Gamma/4)[r^2-2a^2\log(r/a)-a^2-(r^2-2a^2+a^4/r^2)\cos2\varphi]$. It solves the [biharmonic equation](biharmonic-equation.md) and the [no-slip boundary condition](no-slip-boundary-condition.md). The fluid exerts torque $-2\pi\mu\Gamma a^2$ per unit length, because the angular part of the surface [traction](traction.md) averages to zero and the mean tangential traction is $-\mu\Gamma$.
