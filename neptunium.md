# Neptunium

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical element](chemical-element.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neptunium)

[Neptunium](neptunium.md) is the [chemical element](chemical-element.md) with [atomic number](atomic-number.md) 93. Its [isotope](isotope.md) [neptunium-239](neptunium-239.md) occurs between [uranium-239](uranium-239.md) and [plutonium-239](plutonium-239.md) in a neutron-capture and [beta decay](beta-decay.md) production chain.
