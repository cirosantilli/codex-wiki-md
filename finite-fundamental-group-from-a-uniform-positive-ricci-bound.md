# Finite fundamental group from a uniform positive Ricci bound

↑ **Parent:** [Myers's theorem](myers-s-theorem.md)

If a connected complete [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md) has $\operatorname{Ric}\ge(n-1)\kappa g$ with $\kappa>0$, its [universal cover](universal-cover.md) with the lifted metric is complete and satisfies the same bound. The [Bonnet-Myers theorem](myers-s-theorem.md) makes that cover compact. A covering fiber is closed and discrete and hence finite; the number of its elements equals the order of the [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md). Applying the diameter theorem only to the base would not prove this conclusion: the positive lower bound must also be used on the cover.
