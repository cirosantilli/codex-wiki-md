# Isotropic orientation fourth moment

↑ **Parent:** [Isotropic tensor](isotropic-tensor.md)

For a uniformly distributed [unit vector](unit-vector.md) in three dimensions, $\langle u_iu_ju_ku_l\rangle=(\delta_{ij}\delta_{kl}+\delta_{ik}\delta_{jl}+\delta_{il}\delta_{jk})/15$. Contracting indices fixes the coefficient. This identity is useful for incoherent scattering averages and random-axis material models.
