# Reflector

↑ **Parent:** [Reflective subcategory](reflective-subcategory.md)

The reflector of a [reflective subcategory](reflective-subcategory.md) is the left adjoint $L$ to its inclusion. Its unit $\eta_A:A\to LA$ is universal among morphisms from $A$ to objects of the subcategory.
