# Three-level no claims discount equilibrium

↑ **Parent:** [No claims discount system](no-claims-discount-system.md)

For a three-level [birth-death chain](birth-death-chain.md) whose down-or-stay claim probabilities are $p_1,p_2,p_3$, with each in $(0,1)$, [detailed balance](detailed-balance.md) is $\pi_1(1-p_1)=\pi_2p_2$ and $\pi_2(1-p_2)=\pi_3p_3$. Normalizing gives the displayed [stationary distribution](stationary-distribution.md). The finite chain is irreducible and has endpoint holding probabilities, so it is aperiodic and its level proportions converge to this distribution from any initial level.
