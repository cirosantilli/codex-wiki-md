# Alternating dyadic colouring excludes symmetric weighted pair sums

↑ **Parent:** [Dyadic interval](dyadic-interval.md)

Define a [finite colouring](finite-coloring.md) of the positive [integers](integer.md) by $c(n)=\lfloor\log_2 n\rfloor\pmod 2$. No increasing infinite [sequence](sequence.md) has all the numbers $x_i+2x_j$, $i\ne j$, in one [colour class](colour-class.md).

Write $\delta(y)=2^{\lfloor\log_2 y\rfloor+1}-y$. If $\delta$ is unbounded on the [sequence](sequence.md), fix a term $x$ and choose a later term $y>2x$ with $\delta(y)>2x$. With $L=2^{\lfloor\log_2 y\rfloor}$, the numbers $y+2x$ and $2y+x$ lie respectively in the adjacent [dyadic intervals](dyadic-interval.md) $[L,2L)$ and $[2L,4L)$, so their colours differ. If $\delta$ is bounded by $D$ on the [sequence](sequence.md), choose $x>2D$ and then $y>2x$. Now $y+2x=2L-\delta(y)+2x$ lies in $[2L,4L)$, whereas $2y+x=4L-2\delta(y)+x$ lies in $[4L,8L)$. Their colours again differ. The [finite colouring](finite-coloring.md) therefore excludes the symmetric weighted-pair configuration.
