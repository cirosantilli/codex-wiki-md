# Black-hole sphere of influence

↑ **Parent:** [Astrophysical black hole](astrophysical-black-hole.md)

The [black-hole sphere of influence](black-hole-sphere-of-influence.md) is a characteristic radius where the central [black hole](black-hole.md) appreciably changes surrounding stellar dynamics. Equating its [Newtonian gravitational potential](newtonian-gravitational-potential.md) scale $GM_\bullet/r$ to the ambient squared [velocity dispersion](velocity-dispersion.md) gives $r_{\rm inf}\simeq GM_\bullet/\sigma^2$. Dynamical mass measurements depend on angular resolution $r_{\rm inf}/D$, tracer brightness, and degeneracies in the orbital distribution, not solely on [black hole](black-hole.md) mass.
