# Totally bounded space

↑ **Parent:** [Compact metric space](compact-metric-space.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Totally_bounded_space)

A [metric space](metric-space.md) is totally bounded when, for every $\varepsilon>0$, it is covered by finitely many open balls of radius $\varepsilon$.

A [metric space](metric-space.md) is totally bounded when, for every $\varepsilon>0$, finitely many open balls of radius $\varepsilon$ cover it. Equivalently it has a finite $\varepsilon$-net at every positive scale.
