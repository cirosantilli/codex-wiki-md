# Negative spectra do not imply uniform semidiscrete stability

↑ **Parent:** [Eigenvalue stability analysis of a finite difference method](eigenvalue-stability-analysis-of-a-finite-difference-method.md)

Every eigenvalue of this [nonnormal matrix](non-normal-matrix.md) is $-1$, but $e^{tL_h}=e^{-t}\begin{pmatrix}1&t/h\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$. Applied to the second coordinate unit vector, its norm at $t=1$ is at least $1/(eh)$. Thus negative eigenvalues give no mesh-uniform semigroup bound by themselves. [Normal matrices](normal-matrix.md), uniformly conditioned eigenvector bases, or a direct dissipative energy estimate supply the missing control.
