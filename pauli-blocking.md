# Pauli blocking

↑ **Parent:** [Pauli exclusion principle](pauli-exclusion-principle.md)

Suppression of a transition when its required final [fermion](fermion.md) state is already occupied. In a low-temperature [Fermi gas](fermi-gas.md), an otherwise allowed collision must place outgoing particles in empty states; occupation restricts its available [phase space](phase-space.md). This does not mean that [fermions](fermion.md) cease interacting.
