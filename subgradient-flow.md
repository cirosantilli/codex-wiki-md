# Subgradient flow

↑ **Parent:** [Gradient flow](gradient-flow.md)

A nonsmooth [gradient flow](gradient-flow.md) driven by the [subdifferential](subdifferential.md) of a [convex function](convex-function.md). Explicit discretization gives the [forward subgradient step](forward-subgradient-step.md); implicit discretization gives the [proximal operator](proximal-operator.md). [Monotonicity of a convex subdifferential](monotonicity-of-a-convex-subdifferential.md) underlies uniqueness and contraction properties.
