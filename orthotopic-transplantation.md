# Orthotopic transplantation

↑ **Parent:** [Biology laboratory technique](biology-laboratory-technique.md)

Placement of a graft in the corresponding anatomical organ or site. It preserves more relevant local context than some ectopic grafts but still bypasses natural [tumour](tumour.md) initiation.
