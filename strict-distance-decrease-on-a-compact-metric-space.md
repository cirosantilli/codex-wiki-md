# Strict distance decrease on a compact metric space

↑ **Parent:** [Fixed-point theorem](fixed-point-theorem.md)

A self-map $f$ of a nonempty [compact metric space](compact-metric-space.md) satisfying the displayed strict distance inequality has exactly one [fixed point](fixed-point.md), although a uniform [contraction](contraction-mapping.md) constant need not exist. The inequality makes $f$ [Lipschitz continuous](lipschitz-continuity.md) with constant at most one, so $g(x)=d(fx,x)$ is continuous and attains a minimum at $p$. If $fp\ne p$, then $g(fp)<g(p)$, a contradiction. Two different [fixed points](fixed-point.md) contradict the same distance inequality.
