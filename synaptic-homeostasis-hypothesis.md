# Synaptic homeostasis hypothesis

↑ **Parent:** [Sleep](sleep.md)

The proposal that waking-associated strengthening is balanced by sleep-associated renormalization of overall strength of [synapses](synapse.md). Selective weakening could improve capacity and relative signal contrast while preserving important differences. It is compatible with some strengthening or reactivation, and is not the claim that sleep indiscriminately erases learning.
