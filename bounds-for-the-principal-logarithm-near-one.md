# Bounds for the principal logarithm near one

↑ **Parent:** [Principal complex logarithm](principal-complex-logarithm.md)

The [power series](power-series.md) $\operatorname{Log}(1-w)=-\sum_{j\ge1}w^j/j$ proves this estimate. There is no global bound $|\operatorname{Log}(1-w)|\le C|w|$, since the logarithm diverges as $w\to1$. The different estimate $\log|1-w|\le\log(1+|w|)\le|w|$ is valid wherever $w\ne1$, and is sufficient for growth bounds on [canonical products](canonical-product.md).
