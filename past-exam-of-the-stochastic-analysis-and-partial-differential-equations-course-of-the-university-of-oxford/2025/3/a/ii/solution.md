<h1 id="3/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $\lambda\ne\mu$, apply the [resolvent identity](../../../../../resolvent-identity.md) from part (i) in both orders:

$$
R_\lambda R_\mu=\frac{R_\lambda-R_\mu}{\mu-\lambda},\qquad
R_\mu R_\lambda=\frac{R_\mu-R_\lambda}{\lambda-\mu}.
$$

The two quotients are equal. For $\lambda=\mu$ the assertion is immediate. Hence

$$
\boxed{R_\lambda R_\mu=R_\mu R_\lambda\qquad(\lambda,\mu>0).}
$$

Thus [resolvent operators](../../../../../resolvent-of-an-operator.md) from the same [strongly continuous semigroup](../../../../../c0-semigroup.md) commute. This conclusion does not require commuting arbitrary [bounded linear operators](../../../../../continuous-linear-operator.md); it uses the fact that both arise from the same [semigroup generator](../../../../../infinitesimal-generator-of-a-semigroup.md).
