# Massless vector pole of the Veneziano amplitude

↑ **Parent:** [Veneziano amplitude pole residue](veneziano-amplitude-pole-residue.md)

The pole at $s=0$ has [residue](residue.md) $-(t+2)=-(t-u)/2$ for unit-slope tachyons. It is proportional to conserved scalar-pair vector currents and linear in the scattering-angle cosine, exhibiting a massless spin-one intermediate string state.
