# Childhood apraxia of speech

↑ **Parent:** [Apraxia of speech](apraxia-of-speech.md)

A developmental motor-speech condition affecting the planning or programming of [speech](speech.md) movements. [Language](language.md) skills and nonverbal cognition require separate assessment rather than being inferred from the severity of [speech](speech.md) output difficulty.
