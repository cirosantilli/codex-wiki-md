# Coherent reflection from a stationary rough surface

↑ **Parent:** [Small-height Dirichlet scattering](small-height-dirichlet-scattering.md)

Through second order in height, a normally incident acoustic plane wave has the displayed mean reflected [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) for a Dirichlet boundary. The propagating part of the [power spectrum of surface height](power-spectrum-of-surface-height.md) contributes a positive real correction to the negative flat reflection coefficient, while the evanescent part contributes an imaginary correction. The mean linear rough correction is zero.
