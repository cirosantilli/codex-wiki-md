# Endoplasmic reticulum lumen

↑ **Parent:** [Endoplasmic reticulum](endoplasmic-reticulum.md)

The [endoplasmic reticulum lumen](endoplasmic-reticulum-lumen.md) is the aqueous space enclosed by the ER membrane. A soluble lumenal cargo cannot directly contact a cytoplasmic coat across an intact membrane.
