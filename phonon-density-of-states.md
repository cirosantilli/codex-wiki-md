# Phonon density of states

↑ **Parent:** [Density of states](density-of-states.md)

The number of lattice-vibration modes per unit [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md). For a three-dimensional isotropic monatomic [crystal](crystal.md) with three linear acoustic branches of a common speed $v_s$, wavevector-shell counting gives $g(\omega)=3V\omega^2/(2\pi^2v_s^3)$ below a suitable cutoff. Different branch speeds replace $3v_s^{-3}$ by $v_L^{-3}+2v_T^{-3}$.
