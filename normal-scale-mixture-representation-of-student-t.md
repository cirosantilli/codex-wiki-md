# Normal scale mixture representation of Student t

↑ **Parent:** [Student's t-distribution](student-s-t-distribution.md)

For independent $Z\sim N(0,1)$ and $\Lambda\sim\chi_\nu^2$, the displayed ratio has the [Student t-distribution](student-s-t-distribution.md) with $\nu$ degrees of freedom. Equivalently, $T\mid\Lambda\sim N(0,\nu/\Lambda)$. Integrating the normal conditional density against the [chi-squared distribution](chi-squared-distribution.md) gives a density proportional to $(1+t^2/\nu)^{-(\nu+1)/2}$. A latent scale therefore supplies heavy tails while retaining convenient normal conditional calculations. For $\nu>2$, its variance is $\nu/(\nu-2)$, so its scale parameter is not its standard deviation.
