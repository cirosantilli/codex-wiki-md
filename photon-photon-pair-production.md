# Photon-photon pair production

↑ **Parent:** [Pair production](pair-production.md)

Two [photons](photon.md) meeting at angle $\alpha$ can produce an [electron](electron.md) and a [Positron](positron.md) when their combined [four-momentum](four-momentum.md) has sufficient [invariant mass](invariant-mass.md). For a head-on collision the threshold is $E_1E_2=m_e^2c^4$. Threshold means zero relative [momentum](momentum-split.md) in the [centre-of-momentum frame](center-of-momentum-frame.md), not necessarily in the laboratory.
