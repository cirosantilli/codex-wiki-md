# Critical Rayleigh number for an isothermal layer

↑ **Parent:** [Rayleigh-Bénard convection](rayleigh-benard-convection.md)

For fixed [temperatures](temperature-split.md) and stress-free impermeable boundaries, linear marginal [Rayleigh-Bénard convection](rayleigh-benard-convection.md) with dimensionless horizontal [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) $q$ obeys $\operatorname{Ra}(q)=(q^2+\pi^2)^3/q^2$. [Differentiation](differentiation.md) gives $q_c=\pi/\sqrt2$ and the displayed minimum. The threshold depends on the mechanical [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md): two rigid no-slip boundaries instead give approximately $1708$.
