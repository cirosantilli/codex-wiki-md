# Skumanich rotation law

↑ **Parent:** [Wind-driven magnetic braking of a solar-type star](wind-driven-magnetic-braking-of-a-solar-type-star.md)

If the moment of inertia I changes slowly and [wind-driven magnetic braking of a solar-type star](wind-driven-magnetic-braking-of-a-solar-type-star.md) has torque $-K\Omega^3$, then $\Omega^{-2}(t)=\Omega^{-2}(t_0)+2K(t-t_0)/I$. At sufficiently late times within this unsaturated approximation, rotation scales as $t^{-1/2}$. This empirical main-sequence trend is not a universal law for saturated rapid rotators, changing stellar structure or every magnetic geometry.
