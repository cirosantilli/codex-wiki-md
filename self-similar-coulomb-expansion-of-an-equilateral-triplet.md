# Self-similar Coulomb expansion of an equilateral triplet

↑ **Parent:** [Coulomb force](coulomb-force.md)

Three identical like [point charges](point-charge.md), initially at rest at an [equilateral triangle](equilateral-triangle.md)'s vertices, remain equilateral as they move away from the fixed [centroid](centroid.md). Each resultant [force](force-split.md) is radial by symmetry. If the centroid radius is $r$, its magnitude is $k_eq^2/(\sqrt3r^2)$. In a nonrelativistic model neglecting radiation, [energy conservation](conservation-of-energy.md) gives the final [speed](speed.md) $v=\sqrt{2k_eq^2/(ma)}$ for initial side $a$ and charge mass $m$.
