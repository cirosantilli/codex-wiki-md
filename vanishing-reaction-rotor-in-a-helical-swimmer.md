# Vanishing reaction rotor in a helical swimmer

↑ **Parent:** [Opposite-handed counterrotating helical swimmer](opposite-handed-counterrotating-helical-swimmer.md)

If the second rotor has zero drag, it cannot supply reaction torque. The single remaining [helix](helix.md) must be both [force-free](force-free.md) and [torque-free](torque-free.md); a positive-definite resistance matrix forces its translation and rotation to vanish. The relative motor motion is absorbed by the zero-resistance rotor.
