# Ground control point

↑ **Parent:** [Photogrammetry](photogrammetry.md)

An identifiable image feature or target with independently surveyed coordinates, used to georeference and constrain a [photogrammetry](photogrammetry.md) model. A check point is withheld from the fit to assess reconstruction accuracy. Distribution across the site, accurate survey and consistent height datums matter as well as the number of points.
