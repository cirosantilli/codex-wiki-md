# Cumulative hazard probability transformation

↑ **Parent:** [Cumulative hazard function](cumulative-hazard-function.md)

For a proper continuous event time and invertible [cumulative hazard function](cumulative-hazard-function.md) $H$, the [survivor function](survival-function.md) identity $F(t)=e^{-H(t)}$ gives $H(T)\sim\operatorname{Exp}(1)$.
