# Triplon

↑ **Parent:** [Magnon](magnon.md)

A mobile spin-triplet excitation above a singlet background in a quantum magnet. At low [density](density.md), an appropriate triplet branch can be described as bosonic quasiparticles with an [effective mass](band-effective-mass.md) and repulsive interaction. Applied [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) can lower one branch toward condensation. The term does not imply that the total excitation number is conserved on thermodynamic timescales.
