# Delusional conviction

↑ **Parent:** [Delusion](delusion.md)

The degree of confidence in a delusional [belief](belief.md), often measured dimensionally. A change in conviction is not automatically identical to categorical [recovery](clinical-recovery.md), reduced [distress](distress.md) or restored functioning.
