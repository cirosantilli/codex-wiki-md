# Up-and-in claim

↑ **Parent:** [Barrier option](barrier-option.md)

An [up-and-in claim](up-and-in-claim.md) activates when the [stock](stock.md) reaches an upper barrier $b>S_0$ during its lifetime. After activation its terminal payoff is the prescribed function $f(S_T)$; without activation it pays zero. Under continuous [stock](stock.md) paths the barrier event is described by the displayed running maximum.
