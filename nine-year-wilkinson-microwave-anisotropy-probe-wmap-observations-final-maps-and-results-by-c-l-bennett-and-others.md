# Nine-Year Wilkinson Microwave Anisotropy Probe (WMAP) Observations: Final Maps and Results by C. L. Bennett and others

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by Charles L. Bennett](work-by-charles-l-bennett.md)

The December 20, 2012 preprint was available before the January 2013 examination. Its abstract reports combined [cosmic microwave background](cosmic-microwave-background-split.md), [baryon](baryon.md) acoustic oscillation and Hubble-constant model fits, including spatial curvature and cosmic age. These combined-data constraints should not be attributed to the [cosmic microwave background](cosmic-microwave-background-split.md) alone.
