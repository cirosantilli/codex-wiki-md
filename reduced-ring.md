# Reduced ring

↑ **Parent:** [Commutative ring](commutative-ring.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reduced_ring)

A reduced ring is a [commutative ring](commutative-ring.md) with no nonzero [nilpotent element](nilpotent.md). Equivalently, its [nilradical](nilradical.md) is zero.
