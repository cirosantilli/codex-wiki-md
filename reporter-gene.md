# Reporter gene

↑ **Parent:** [Gene expression](gene-expression.md)

An experimentally introduced [gene](gene.md) whose measurable output reports a specified regulatory process. A change in [protein](protein-split.md) output alone cannot distinguish altered [transcription](transcription-biology.md), [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) decay and [biological translation](translation-biology.md).
