# Ultraviolet photoelectron spectroscopy

↑ **Parent:** [Photoemission spectroscopy](photoemission-spectroscopy.md)

[photoelectron](photoelectron.md) spectroscopy using ultraviolet [photons](photon.md) to probe occupied electronic states, often valence states. Empty states above the [Fermi level](fermi-level.md) require a different measurement or deliberate population.
