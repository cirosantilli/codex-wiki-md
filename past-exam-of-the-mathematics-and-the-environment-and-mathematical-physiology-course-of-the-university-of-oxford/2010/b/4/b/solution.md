<h1 id="b/4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Adding the [ordinary differential equations](../../../../../ordinary-differential-equation.md) for all [enzyme](../../../../../enzyme-split.md) states makes the net rates telescope:

$$
\frac{d}{dt}\sum_{i=0}^nC_i=-R_1+(R_1-R_2)+\cdots+(R_{n-1}-R_n)+R_n=0.
$$

With initially unbound [enzyme](../../../../../enzyme-split.md), this sum is $E_0$ for all time. Hence

$$
\boxed{C_0=E_0-\sum_{j=1}^nC_j.}
$$

This is [enzyme conservation](../../../../../enzyme-conservation.md), not conservation of each complex individually. The reaction changes site occupancy and makes [reaction product](../../../../../product-chemistry.md), but no event creates or destroys an [enzyme](../../../../../enzyme-split.md) molecule.
