<h1 id="b/4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use $S,C_i,P$ for [concentrations](../../../../../concentration.md) and define net complex-formation rates $R_i=k_iC_{i-1}S-(k_{-i}+k_i^+)C_i$, with $R_{n+1}=0$. The [law of mass action](../../../../../law-of-mass-action.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\dot C_0=-R_1,\quad \dot C_i=R_i-R_{i+1}\ (1\leq i<n),\quad\dot C_n=R_n,}
$$



$$
\boxed{\dot S=-\sum_{i=1}^n(k_iC_{i-1}S-k_{-i}C_i)=-\sum_{i=1}^n(R_i+k_i^+C_i),\quad \dot P=\sum_{i=1}^nk_i^+C_i.}
$$

Each binding event removes one free [substrate](../../../../../substrate-biochemistry.md) molecule; each unbinding returns one; each catalytic event releases one [reaction product](../../../../../product-chemistry.md) and reduces the number of occupied sites by one. Suitable initially unbound [enzyme](../../../../../enzyme-split.md) and [substrate](../../../../../substrate-biochemistry.md) data are $S(0)=S_0$, $P(0)=0$, $C_0(0)=E_0$, $C_i(0)=0$ for $i\geq1$. All rates are nonnegative on the boundary of the nonnegative [concentration](../../../../../concentration.md) region in the direction required to preserve it. These equations also conserve $S+P+\sum_{i=1}^niC_i=S_0$, accounting for bound [substrate](../../../../../substrate-biochemistry.md) as well as free [substrate](../../../../../substrate-biochemistry.md).
