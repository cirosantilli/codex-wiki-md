↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The common [vector](../../../../../vector.md) field $F$ represents an identical reference cell. The small cell-specific term $\epsilon G_i$ represents heterogeneity of cellular properties, such as differing ion-current parameters or intrinsic [oscillation](../../../../../oscillation.md) rates. It is not external random noise: the displayed model is deterministic.

The [Kronecker delta](../../../../../kronecker-delta.md) factors restrict coupling to the first state component, the [membrane potential](../../../../../membrane-potential-split.md). Substitution gives a coupling current in the [voltage](../../../../../voltage.md) equation proportional to $\epsilon\sum_jd_{ij}(u_j^1-u_i^1)$, while the other state components have no direct intercellular coupling. This is the linear voltage-difference response of [gap junctions](../../../../../gap-junction.md). Gating and chemical variables can still change indirectly through their own coupled intracellular dynamics. The zero diagonal removes self-coupling, which would contribute zero anyway. Physical reciprocal gap-junction coupling is often symmetric, but **zero diagonal alone does not impose reciprocity**; that distinction matters in the final part.
