<h1 id="a/3/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Set $G=\rho g(\sin\alpha-\cos\alpha H_x)$. For downstream shear $G\geq0$, [Glen's flow law](../../../../../glen-s-flow-law.md) and the [no-slip boundary condition](../../../../../no-slip-boundary-condition.md) give

$$
u(x,z)=A G^n\int_0^z(H-z')^n\,dz'=\frac{AG^n}{n+1}[H^{n+1}-(H-z)^{n+1}].
$$

Here $A$ is the [ice](../../../../../ice.md) rate factor, distinct from the cross-sectional [area](../../../../../surface-area.md) in the river problem. Integrating this [velocity profile](../../../../../velocity-profile.md) through the thickness gives the [volume flux](../../../../../volumetric-flow-rate.md) per unit valley width:

$$
Q=\frac{AG^n}{n+1}\left[H^{n+2}-\frac{H^{n+2}}{n+2}\right]=\boxed{A(\rho g\sin\alpha)^n(1-\cot\alpha H_x)^n\frac{H^{n+2}}{n+2}.}
$$

The factor $H^{n+2}$ combines the depth-dependent [shear stress](../../../../../shear-stress.md), integration to obtain [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md), and integration to obtain [flux](../../../../../flux.md). If reverse shear is admitted for noninteger $n$, the signed version of [Glen's flow law](../../../../../glen-s-flow-law.md) is $u_z=A|\tau|^{n-1}\tau$; the displayed unsigned [power](../../../../../power.md) presumes the downstream regime.
