<h1 id="a/2/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

First neglect [inertia](../../../../../inertia.md) relative to the $O(\epsilon)$ [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) correction, requiring $F\ll1$ for smooth fields on these scales. The positive [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) is

$$
u=A^{1/4}\bigl[1-\epsilon(\sqrt A)_x\bigr]^{1/2}=A^{1/4}-\frac\epsilon4A^{-1/4}A_x+O(\epsilon^2).
$$

Multiplying by [area](../../../../../surface-area.md) gives the [discharge](../../../../../discharge.md) $Q=A^{5/4}-(\epsilon/4)A^{3/4}A_x+O(\epsilon^2)$. Hence

$$
\boxed{A_t+(A^{5/4})_x=\frac\epsilon4\bigl(A^{3/4}A_x\bigr)_x.}
$$

The leading equation is a nonlinear [kinematic wave](../../../../../kinematic-wave.md) equation. The [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) correction has positive [diffusion coefficient](../../../../../diffusion-coefficient.md) for $A>0$ and smooths steep gradients. Dropped terms are $O(\epsilon^2)$ and inertial terms $O(\epsilon F)$ on the assumed scales; sufficiently thin fronts require a separate balance rather than an unrestricted [Taylor expansion](../../../../../taylor-expansion.md).
