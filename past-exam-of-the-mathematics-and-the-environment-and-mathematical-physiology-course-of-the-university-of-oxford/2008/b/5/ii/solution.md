<h1 id="b/5/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

[Mass action](../../../../../law-of-mass-action.md) gives

$$
\dot S=-k_1SE+k_{-1}C,\quad\dot E=-k_1SE+(k_{-1}+k_2)C,\quad
\dot C=k_1SE-(k_{-1}+k_2)C,\quad\dot P=k_2C.
$$

Thus $E+C=E_0$ and $S+C+P=S_0$ are conserved. Eliminating free [enzyme](../../../../../enzyme-split.md) yields the stated two-equation system.

With $s=S/S_0$, $c=C/E_0$ and $\tau=t/T$, substitution gives

$$
\boxed{\alpha=k_1E_0T,\quad\beta=\frac{k_{-1}E_0T}{S_0},\quad\gamma=k_1S_0T,\quad\delta=(k_{-1}+k_2)T.}
$$

The [dimensionless](../../../../../dimensionless-quantity.md) initial data are $\boxed{s(0)=1,\quad c(0)=0}$. The parameter named $\delta$ here is an [enzyme](../../../../../enzyme-split.md) kinetic coefficient and is unrelated to the loss parameter used in the later blood-cell question.
