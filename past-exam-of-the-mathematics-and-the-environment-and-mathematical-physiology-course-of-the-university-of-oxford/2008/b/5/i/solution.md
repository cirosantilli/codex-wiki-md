↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For an assay begun by mixing free [enzyme](../../../../../enzyme-split.md) and [substrate](../../../../../substrate-biochemistry.md), take $\boxed{[S](0)=S_0>0,\ [E](0)=E_0>0,\ [C](0)=[P](0)=0}$. The complex has not yet formed and no [product](../../../../../product-of-numbers.md) has been made. The later small-[enzyme](../../../../../enzyme-split.md) limit assumes $E_0\ll S_0$. Other preincubation protocols could require nonzero initial complex, but these [initial conditions](../../../../../initial-condition.md) are the ones used by the stated [inner solution](../../../../../inner-expansion.md).
