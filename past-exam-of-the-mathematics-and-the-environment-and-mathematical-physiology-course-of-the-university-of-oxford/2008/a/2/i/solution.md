Here $s$ is downstream distance, $A$ the wetted cross-sectional area, $u$ the section-averaged downstream [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md), and $M$ the lateral water input per channel length and time. Thus $A_t$ is local water storage and $(Au)_s$ the divergence of discharge. Their balance is [mass conservation](../../../../../mass-conservation.md).

The momentum terms $u_t$ and $uu_s$ are local and advective acceleration. The bed slope $S$ gives the downstream gravitational acceleration $gS$. [Bed shear stress](../../../../../wall-shear-stress.md) $\tau$ acting over wetted perimeter $l$ produces resisting force per unit mass $\tau l/(\rho A)$; the [hydraulic radius](../../../../../hydraulic-radius.md) is $R=A/l$. Finally $-g\overline h_s$ is the hydrostatic free-surface [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md)-gradient term in the chosen bed-following description. The Saint-Venant approximation depth-averages the flow, assumes a long shallow geometry and [hydrostatic pressure](../../../../../hydrostatic-pressure.md), and closes the boundary shear through a [roughness](../../../../../surface-roughness.md) law.
