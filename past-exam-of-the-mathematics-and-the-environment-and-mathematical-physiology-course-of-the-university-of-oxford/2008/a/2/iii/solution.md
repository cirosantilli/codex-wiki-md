↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For the supplied hillslope values,

$$
\boxed{u_0=\frac{0.01^{2/3}\sqrt{0.01}}{0.04}\simeq0.116\ {\rm m\,s^{-1}},\quad\epsilon=0.01,\quad F\simeq0.367,\quad\epsilon F^2\simeq1.35\times10^{-3}.}
$$

The [Froude number](../../../../../froude-number.md) is below one, but it is not extremely small numerically; the much smaller [products](../../../../../product-of-numbers.md) actually multiplying inertia and the depth-gradient term justify their leading neglect. Positive flow then satisfies $u=h^{2/3}$ and

$$
\boxed{h_t+(h^{5/3})_s=E.}
$$

In overland flow the source is net [rainfall](../../../../../rainfall.md) input after [infiltration](../../../../../soil-infiltration.md) into soil, with [evaporation](../../../../../evaporation.md) or other local losses included when important. Lateral inflow can also contribute. Negative net input must be limited by water availability once the surface dries.
