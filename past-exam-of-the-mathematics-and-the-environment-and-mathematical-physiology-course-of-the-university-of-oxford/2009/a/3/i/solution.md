Here $h$ is [water](../../../../../water.md) depth, $u$ the depth-averaged downstream [speed](../../../../../speed.md), $s$ bed elevation relative to the reference slope, and $\eta=s+h$ free-surface elevation relative to that reference. The constants $\rho_w,\rho_s$ are [water](../../../../../water.md) and grain densities, $S$ is the reference bed slope, $\tau$ bed [shear stress](../../../../../shear-stress.md), $\phi$ bed [porosity](../../../../../porosity.md), $c$ [suspended sediment](../../../../../suspended-sediment.md) [mass](../../../../../mass.md) [concentration](../../../../../concentration.md), $q_b$ bedload solid [volume flux](../../../../../volumetric-flow-rate.md) per unit width, and $v_E,v_D$ erosion and deposition velocities. The settling [speed](../../../../../speed.md) is denoted by $v_s$ in the constitutive laws.

Depth [integration](../../../../../integral-split.md) of incompressible [water](../../../../../water.md) conservation gives the depth-storage plus discharge-divergence balance. Depth-averaged [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) has local and advective acceleration on the left, and [gravity](../../../../../gravity.md) driven by the actual surface gradient together with bed drag on the right. [Hydrostatic pressure](../../../../../hydrostatic-pressure.md) and a [shallow-water approximation](../../../../../shallow-water-approximation.md) underlie this [Saint-Venant](../../../../../shallow-water-equations.md) description.

[Suspended sediment](../../../../../suspended-sediment.md) conservation first has the conservative form $(hc)_t+(huc)_x=\rho_s(v_E-v_D)$; the [water](../../../../../water.md) balance reduces it to the material-derivative form printed in the source. Solid bed [volume](../../../../../volume.md) per unit horizontal [area](../../../../../surface-area.md) is $(1-\phi)s$. Its storage plus bedload divergence equals deposition minus erosion, giving the Exner bed balance. Adding the suspended and bed balances after converting bed [volume](../../../../../volume.md) to grain [mass](../../../../../mass.md) cancels exchange terms: **erosion and deposition transfer sediment between the two reservoirs rather than create it**.
