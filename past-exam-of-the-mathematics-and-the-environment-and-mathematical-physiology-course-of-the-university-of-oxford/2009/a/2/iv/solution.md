<h1 id="a/2/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

[Taylor expansion](../../../../../taylor-expansion.md) gives $Q(A_{\rm eq}+a)=Q(A_{\rm eq})+cA_{\rm eq}^m a+O(a^2)$. Subtract the [equilibrium](../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) [equation](../../../../../equation.md) and write $V(s)=cA_{\rm eq}^m(s)$ to obtain $a_t+(Va)_s=0$. Set $b=Va$. Since $V$ is [time](../../../../../time-in-physics.md) independent, $b_t+Vb_s=0$. The [travel coordinate for a conservative transport perturbation](../../../../../travel-coordinate-for-a-conservative-transport-perturbation.md) $\xi(s)=\int_0^sV(r)^{-1}\,dr$ transforms this into $b_t+b_\xi=0$, and therefore

$$
\boxed{a(s,t)=\frac{\phi(\xi(s)-t)}{cA_{\rm eq}^m(s)}.}
$$

The conserved quantity along a [characteristic](../../../../../characteristic-of-a-field.md) is the [flux](../../../../../flux.md) perturbation $Va$, explaining the diminishing [area](../../../../../surface-area.md) [amplitude](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) as $V$ grows.

Differentiate the [equilibrium](../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) relation to get $A_{\rm eq}'=M/(cA_{\rm eq}^m)=M/V$. Thus the arrival [time](../../../../../time-in-physics.md) is explicitly

$$
\boxed{t_l=\xi(l)=\frac{A_{\rm eq}(l)-A_{\rm eq}(0)}M
=\frac1M\left(\frac{m+1}{c}\right)^{1/(m+1)}\left[(Q_{\rm in}+Ml)^{1/(m+1)}-Q_{\rm in}^{1/(m+1)}\right].}
$$

For zero upstream [flux](../../../../../flux.md) the [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md) remains finite, giving the limiting formula with its second term zero. However $V(0)=0$ then makes the displayed [amplitude](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) formula singular at the head; a uniformly small arbitrary [area](../../../../../surface-area.md) disturbance imposed exactly there is outside this [linearization](../../../../../linear-stability.md). A positive upstream [flux](../../../../../flux.md) avoids that degeneracy.
