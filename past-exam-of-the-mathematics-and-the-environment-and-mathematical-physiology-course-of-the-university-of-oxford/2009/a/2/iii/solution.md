<h1 id="a/2/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

At [equilibrium](../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) $Q_s=M$, so $Q=Q_{\rm in}+Ms$ for an upstream boundary [flux](../../../../../flux.md) $Q_{\rm in}$. Hence

$$
\boxed{A_{\rm eq}(s)=\left[\frac{m+1}{c}(Q_{\rm in}+Ms)\right]^{1/(m+1)}.}
$$

Without a specified upstream condition this [integration](../../../../../integral-split.md) constant must be retained. On a region of positive [area](../../../../../surface-area.md), the [characteristic](../../../../../characteristic-of-a-field.md) [speed](../../../../../speed.md) is $Q'(A)=cA^m>0$, increasing downstream along the [equilibrium](../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md). Small disturbances move downstream, spread in space as this [speed](../../../../../speed.md) increases, and decrease in [area](../../../../../surface-area.md) [amplitude](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) in the manner derived next. They are transported away from a fixed location; the model has no [diffusion](../../../../../diffusion.md) giving instantaneous smoothing. Finite-amplitude compressive disturbances can steepen into shocks because the [flux](../../../../../flux.md) is [convex](../../../../../convex-function.md), $Q''(A)=cmA^{m-1}>0$, so the small-disturbance picture is not a global no-shock assertion.
