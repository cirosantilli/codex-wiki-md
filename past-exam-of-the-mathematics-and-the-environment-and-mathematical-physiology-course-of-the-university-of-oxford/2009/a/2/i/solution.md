<h1 id="a/2/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The cross-sectional [area](../../../../../surface-area.md) $A(s,t)$ multiplied by the mean downstream [speed](../../../../../speed.md) $u(s,t)$ is the [volume flux](../../../../../volumetric-flow-rate.md) $Q=Au$. The term $A_t$ is local storage change per unit river [length](../../../../../length.md), $(Au)_s$ is net downstream export per unit [length](../../../../../length.md), and $M>0$ is lateral [volume](../../../../../volume.md) input per unit [length](../../../../../length.md) per unit [time](../../../../../time-in-physics.md), for example distributed tributary or runoff supply.

For a fixed reach $[s_1,s_2]$, [incompressibility](../../../../../incompressible-flow.md) and constant [density](../../../../../density.md) give

$$
\frac d{dt}\int_{s_1}^{s_2}A\,ds=Q(s_1,t)-Q(s_2,t)+\int_{s_1}^{s_2}M\,ds.
$$

Apply the [fundamental theorem of calculus](../../../../../fundamental-theorem-of-calculus.md) and localize the resulting [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md) identity to obtain $A_t+Q_s=M$. The [density](../../../../../density.md) cancels because every term transports the same [water](../../../../../water.md); no sediment source is included in this balance.
