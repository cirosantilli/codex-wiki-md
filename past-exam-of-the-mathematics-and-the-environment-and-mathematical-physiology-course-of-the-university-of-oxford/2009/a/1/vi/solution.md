<h1 id="a/1/vi/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vi](../vi.md)

The [characteristic](../../../../../characteristic-of-a-field.md) roots satisfy

$$
\lambda^2-\frac13\mu^{1/4}\lambda+4\mu^{7/6}=0.
$$

They are a nonreal [conjugate pair](../../../../../complex-conjugate.md) precisely when $\mu^{1/2}/9-16\mu^{7/6}<0$, or $\mu^{2/3}>1/144$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\mu_c=144^{-3/2}=\frac1{1728}.}
$$

In [lake-coupled Röthlisberger drainage instability](../../../../../lake-coupled-rothlisberger-drainage-instability.md), above this value the [real part](../../../../../real-part.md) is $\mu^{1/4}/6>0$, so nonzero linear perturbations are sinusoidal [oscillations](../../../../../oscillation.md) with an exponentially growing envelope. Their [angular frequency](../../../../../angular-frequency.md) is $[4\mu^{7/6}-\mu^{1/2}/36]^{1/2}$.

Physically the feedback represents repeated lake filling and [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) increase, channel enlargement by melting, rapid drainage, [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) reduction and [material creep](../../../../../creep-deformation.md) closure. Large drainage events are glacial lake outburst floods, or jökulhlaups. **The unbounded [amplitude](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) is a conclusion of the linearized [equations](../../../../../equation.md)**, not a proof of infinite physical discharge or an indefinitely growing nonlinear solution. Once perturbations are comparable with the [equilibrium](../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) scales, nonlinear dynamics and constraints such as lake depletion must be retained. At the threshold the two real roots coincide; below it the instability is nonoscillatory.
