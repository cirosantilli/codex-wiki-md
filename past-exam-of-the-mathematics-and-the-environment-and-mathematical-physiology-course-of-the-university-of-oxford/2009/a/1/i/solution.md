<h1 id="a/1/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For [Röthlisberger channel drainage](../../../../../rothlisberger-channel-drainage.md), $S$ is the water-filled cross-sectional [area](../../../../../surface-area.md) of the subglacial channel, $Q$ its [volume](../../../../../volume.md) discharge, $m$ the [mass](../../../../../mass.md) of [ice](../../../../../ice.md) melted per unit channel [length](../../../../../length.md) per unit [time](../../../../../time-in-physics.md), and $N=p_i-p_w$ the [effective pressure](../../../../../effective-pressure-in-subglacial-drainage.md), namely [ice](../../../../../ice.md) overburden [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) minus [water](../../../../../water.md) [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md). The first balance converts melt [mass](../../../../../mass.md) to [area](../../../../../surface-area.md) growth through division by [ice](../../../../../ice.md) [density](../../../../../density.md) $\rho_i$, and subtracts [material creep](../../../../../creep-deformation.md) closure $KSN^3$. The cubic [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) dependence comes from the adopted ice-flow law; $K$ includes its material and geometric factors.

The second relation balances the downslope hydraulic driving gradient $\rho_wg\sin\alpha$ against the adopted frictional [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) loss. It is an instantaneous discharge-area law, giving $Q=(\sin\alpha/f)^{1/2}S^{4/3}$ for positive downstream flow. The final balance equates [latent heat](../../../../../latent-heat.md) $mL$ needed to melt the channel walls with the gravitational [power](../../../../../power.md) dissipated per unit [length](../../../../../length.md), $\rho_wg\sin\alpha\,Q$. It assumes that this dissipated [power](../../../../../power.md) is available for melting rather than substantial sensible heating or other [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) losses. The model thus couples **melt opening, pressure-driven [material creep](../../../../../creep-deformation.md) closure and hydraulic discharge**.
