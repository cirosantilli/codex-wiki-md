<h1 id="a/1/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

With constant lake surface [area](../../../../../surface-area.md), raising its level by $dh$ changes its [volume](../../../../../volume.md) by $S_L\,dh$. The symbol $M_L$ in the proposed balance is an effective filling rate measured as lake-level rise per unit [time](../../../../../time-in-physics.md), so the [volume](../../../../../volume.md) inflow is $S_LM_L$. [Volume](../../../../../volume.md) conservation gives

$$
S_L\frac{dh}{dt}=S_LM_L-Q,\qquad\boxed{h_t=M_L-Q/S_L.}
$$

Thus the lake rises when its inflow exceeds channel discharge and falls in the opposite case. If an inflow were instead specified directly as a [volume](../../../../../volume.md) rate $I_L$, its contribution would be $I_L/S_L$; keeping these units distinct is necessary for the interpretation of $M_L$.
