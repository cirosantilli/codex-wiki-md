<h1 id="b/4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In [alternating-access carrier transport](../../../../../alternating-access-carrier-transport.md), the four reversible reaction pairs are $S_i+C_i\rightleftharpoons P_i$, $S_e+C_e\rightleftharpoons P_e$, $C_i\rightleftharpoons C_e$ and $P_i\rightleftharpoons P_e$. Binding and unbinding use $k_+,k_-$; both conformational pairs use $k$ in each direction. Define the net binding rates $B_i=k_+s_ic_i-k_-p_i$ and $B_e=k_+s_ec_e-k_-p_e$. [Mass action](../../../../../law-of-mass-action.md) and the change of each species in each reaction give

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
\dot s_i&=-B_i,&\dot s_e&=-B_e,\\
\dot p_i&=B_i+k(p_e-p_i),&\dot p_e&=B_e+k(p_i-p_e),\\
\dot c_i&=-B_i+k(c_e-c_i),&\dot c_e&=-B_e+k(c_i-c_e).
\end{aligned}}
$$

For example binding consumes one free interior [substrate](../../../../../substrate-biochemistry.md) and one free interior carrier, and produces one interior complex; loaded-carrier transformation then moves that bound [substrate](../../../../../substrate-biochemistry.md) across the [membrane](../../../../../cell-membrane-split.md). A bare-carrier transformation alone does not transport [substrate](../../../../../substrate-biochemistry.md). The [concentration](../../../../../concentration.md) convention implicitly uses compatible compartment-volume [normalization](../../../../../normalization.md), as required for the stated unweighted [conservation laws](../../../../../conservation-law.md).
