<h1 id="b/4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Adding the two complex and two free-carrier [equations](../../../../../equation.md) cancels binding and conformational exchange, giving

$$
\boxed{p_i+p_e+c_i+c_e=c_0.}
$$

Each free or bound carrier contains one [carrier protein](../../../../../carrier-protein.md), and the model neither synthesizes nor degrades it. Adding the two free-substrate and two bound-substrate [equations](../../../../../equation.md) likewise gives

$$
\boxed{s_i+s_e+p_i+p_e=s_0.}
$$

Binding stores [substrate](../../../../../substrate-biochemistry.md) temporarily and loaded conformational exchange relocates it; neither changes total [substrate](../../../../../substrate-biochemistry.md). The two invariants count distinct conserved chemical moieties. The constants $c_0$ and $s_0$ should not be interchanged: [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) and [substrate](../../../../../substrate-biochemistry.md) need not be present in equal amounts.
