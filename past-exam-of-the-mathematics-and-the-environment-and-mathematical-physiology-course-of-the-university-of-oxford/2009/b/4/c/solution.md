<h1 id="b/4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Replace only the free-substrate [equations](../../../../../equation.md) by

$$
\boxed{\dot s_i=-B_i-J,\qquad\dot s_e=-B_e+J.}
$$

The other four [equations](../../../../../equation.md) remain as above. The carrier invariant remains unchanged because no [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md) is added or removed. The two external [substrate](../../../../../substrate-biochemistry.md) terms cancel in the total-substrate balance, so the second invariant also remains unchanged. This cancellation describes a balanced source and sink, even though there is a nonzero throughput. A constant sink is physically meaningful only while [substrate](../../../../../substrate-biochemistry.md) [concentrations](../../../../../concentration.md) remain nonnegative; it is not a state-dependent removal law valid after an interior reservoir is exhausted.
