<h1 id="b/4/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

At [steady state](../../../../../../steady-state.md) the interior free-substrate balance gives $B_i=-J$. Substituting this into the interior free-carrier balance gives $0=J+k(c_e-c_i)$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{c_i=c_e+J/k.}
$$

For positive inward throughput there are more free carriers facing inward: release of transported [substrate](../../../../../../substrate-biochemistry.md) must be followed by outward return of empty carriers at net rate $k(c_i-c_e)=J$.
