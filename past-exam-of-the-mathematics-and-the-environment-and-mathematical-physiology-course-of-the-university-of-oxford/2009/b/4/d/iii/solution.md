<h1 id="b/4/d/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The exterior free-substrate [equation](../../../../../../equation.md) gives $B_e=J$ at [steady state](../../../../../../steady-state.md). Thus $k_+s_ec_e-k_-p_e=J$, and

$$
\boxed{p_e=\frac{s_ec_e}{K}-\frac J{k_-}.}
$$

The sign of the current term is opposite to the interior relation because [substrate](../../../../../../substrate-biochemistry.md) is supplied to the exterior and removed from the interior. Nonnegative occupancies are selected by the complete steady transport solution and compatible [substrate](../../../../../../substrate-biochemistry.md) totals.
