<h1 id="b/5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The cytoplasmic [concentration](../../../../../concentration.md) $c$ gains a constant influx $r$ from outside the cell and loses [calcium](../../../../../calcium.md) through a first-order extrusion term $kc$. The pump [flux](../../../../../flux.md) $J_+$ moves [calcium](../../../../../calcium.md) from [cytoplasm](../../../../../cytoplasm.md) into the [sarcoplasmic reticulum](../../../../../sarcoplasmic-reticulum.md), [saturating](../../../../../hill-function.md) with cytoplasmic [concentration](../../../../../concentration.md) through a [Hill function](../../../../../hill-function.md) with scale $K_1$, maximal rate $V_1$ and exponent $n$. The release [flux](../../../../../flux.md) $J_-$ returns stored [calcium](../../../../../calcium.md) to the [cytoplasm](../../../../../cytoplasm.md). It requires both [calcium](../../../../../calcium.md) in the store and calcium-dependent activation of release; its two [saturating](../../../../../hill-function.md) factors have scales $K_2,K_3$ and exponents $m,p$. This represents [calcium-induced calcium release](../../../../../calcium-induced-calcium-release.md). The leak $k_sc_s$ is passive return from the store.

Every internal transfer appears with opposite signs in the two balances, so $d(c+c_s)/dt=r-kc$ in the model's compatible [volume](../../../../../volume.md) [normalization](../../../../../normalization.md). Only influx and extrusion change the total. The nonlinear pump and activated release can redistribute this total rapidly and generate [oscillations](../../../../../oscillation.md) even with constant external input.
