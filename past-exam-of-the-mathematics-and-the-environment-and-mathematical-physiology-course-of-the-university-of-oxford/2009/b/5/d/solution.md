<h1 id="b/5/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For [Hopf thresholds in a two-pool calcium model](../../../../../hopf-thresholds-in-a-two-pool-calcium-model.md), with the unique [equilibrium](../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) parametrized by $u=\mu$, locate changes of stability by solving the two [equations](../../../../../equation.md)

$$
\boxed{f(\mu,v)=0,\qquad f_v(\mu,v)-\gamma f_u(\mu,v)=\epsilon.}
$$

If there are two simple roots $\mu_-<\mu_+$ with positive [trace](../../../../../matrix-trace.md) between them, the [equilibrium](../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) is a [repeller](../../../../../unstable-equilibrium.md) throughout that interval, since its [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) stays positive. At a nondegenerate crossing a [Hopf bifurcation](../../../../../hopf-bifurcation.md) is possible, with linear [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md) $[-f_v/\epsilon]^{1/2}$. A complete classification of criticality requires a nonlinear coefficient, not merely the [trace](../../../../../matrix-trace.md) calculation. At leading singular order the thresholds approach the folds determined by $f=0$ and $f_v-\gamma f_u=0$, equivalently $V'=-1/\gamma$.

Periodic solutions are also supported globally in the positive quadrant. The axes point inward for positive input, $v'\le(\beta-\delta v)/\epsilon$ bounds $v$, and the $u$ [equation](../../../../../equation.md) then bounds $u$ by a linear restoring term plus a bounded source. A large rectangle can thus be chosen [forward invariant](../../../../../invariant-set-of-a-measure-preserving-transformation.md). For an initial point distinct from the [repelling](../../../../../unstable-equilibrium.md) unique [equilibrium](../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md), its [omega-limit set](../../../../../omega-limit-set.md) cannot contain that [equilibrium](../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) or a boundary [equilibrium](../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md). The [Poincaré-Bendixson theorem](../../../../../poincare-bendixson-theorem.md) supplies a [periodic orbit](../../../../../periodic-orbit.md). In the strong separation regime it follows the two attracting branches and jumps between them, a [relaxation oscillation](../../../../../relaxation-oscillation.md).

The interval requires the [maximum](../../../../../maximum-of-a-subset-of-a-total-order.md) of $f_v-\gamma f_u$ along the [critical manifold](../../../../../critical-manifold.md) to exceed $\epsilon$; the listed scale hierarchy alone is not a substitute for that test for arbitrary remaining constants. As an explicit example, $\beta=0.1$, $\delta=10^{-5}$, $\epsilon=5\times10^{-4}$ and $\gamma=0.1$ give approximately $\mu_-=0.22618$ and $\mu_+=0.37350$. At $\mu=0.3$ the [trace](../../../../../matrix-trace.md) is positive, whereas at $0.03$ and $1$ it is negative. The figures use this example and label their singular-limit portions accordingly.
