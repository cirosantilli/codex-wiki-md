<h1 id="b/6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Chemoreceptors respond to [carbon-dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) levels, but feedback is delayed by blood transport to sensing sites, neural processing and the response of respiratory muscles. A fixed positive delay $\tau$ approximates their combined effect. The [ventilation](../../../../../pulmonary-ventilation.md) at the present instant therefore depends on the earlier [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md), $\dot V(t)=\mathcal V(P(t-\tau))$, not just on $P(t)$. This delayed [negative feedback](../../../../../negative-feedback.md) can overcorrect after the [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) has already changed, providing a mechanism for oscillatory breathing. The delay [equation](../../../../../equation.md) requires a [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) history over an interval of [length](../../../../../length.md) $\tau$, rather than a single initial value.
