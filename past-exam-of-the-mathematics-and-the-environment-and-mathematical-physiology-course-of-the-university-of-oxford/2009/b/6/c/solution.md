<h1 id="b/6/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [positive-part](../../../../../positive-part-of-a-real-valued-function.md) law $\mathcal V(P)=G[P-P_0]_+$ is a threshold-linear approximation to the ventilatory response. Below the apnoeic threshold $P_0$, this model assigns zero [ventilation](../../../../../pulmonary-ventilation.md); above it, each [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) increment produces a proportional [ventilation](../../../../../pulmonary-ventilation.md) increase, with gain $G>0$. Taking the [positive part](../../../../../positive-part-of-a-real-valued-function.md) ensures a nonnegative [ventilation](../../../../../pulmonary-ventilation.md) rate rather than the unphysical negative rate that unrestricted linear extrapolation would give. It is a local idealization of the response, not a claim that actual physiology lacks other respiratory drives or saturates nowhere. In the delayed model this response is evaluated at the delayed [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md).
