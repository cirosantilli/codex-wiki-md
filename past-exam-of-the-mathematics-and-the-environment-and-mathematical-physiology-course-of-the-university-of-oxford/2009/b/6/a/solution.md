<h1 id="b/6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In [delayed carbon-dioxide ventilation feedback](../../../../../delayed-carbon-dioxide-ventilation-feedback.md), $P$ is the effective [carbon-dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) [partial pressure](../../../../../partial-pressure.md) of the lumped body compartment. Its stored [carbon-dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) amount is proportional to $P$, with effective capacitance $K$, so $K\,dP/dT$ is its accumulation rate. Metabolism produces [carbon dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) at rate $M$. [Ventilation](../../../../../pulmonary-ventilation.md) removes it at a rate proportional to [partial pressure](../../../../../partial-pressure.md) [times](../../../../../time-in-physics.md) [ventilation](../../../../../pulmonary-ventilation.md), $P\dot V$, with gas-conversion constants absorbed into the model coefficients. The [equation](../../../../../equation.md) is therefore a one-compartment [carbon-dioxide](../../../../../carbon-dioxide.md) balance: **production minus respiratory removal equals accumulation**. It idealizes mixing and treats metabolic production as constant over the [time](../../../../../time-in-physics.md) scale of interest.
