<h1 id="b/6/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Distinguish physical [time](../../../../../../time-in-physics.md) $t$ from scaled [time](../../../../../../time-in-physics.md) $T=t/\tau$. Put

$$
P=P_0+P_s p,\qquad P_s=\frac M{GP_0},\qquad \dot V=\frac M{P_0}v,\qquad v(T)=[p(T-1)]_+.
$$

Then $K(P_s/\tau)p'=M-[P_0+P_sp](M/P_0)v$, giving

$$
\boxed{p'=\alpha[1-(1+\mu p)v],\quad\alpha=\frac{\tau GP_0}{K},\quad\mu=\frac M{GP_0^2},\quad p_1=p(T-1).}
$$

A [steady state](../../../../../../steady-state.md) must be positive, since otherwise [ventilation](../../../../../../pulmonary-ventilation.md) vanishes while production remains positive. It solves $\mu p_*^2+p_*-1=0$, so

$$
\boxed{p_*=\frac{2}{1+\sqrt{1+4\mu}}=1-\mu+2\mu^2+O(\mu^3).}
$$

The value $1/(1+\mu)=1-\mu+\mu^2+O(\mu^3)$ printed in the question agrees only through first order. Thus it is a small-$\mu$ approximation with error of order $\mu^2$, not an exact [equilibrium](../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) for nonzero $\mu$. The other quadratic root is negative and inadmissible for this steady [ventilation](../../../../../../pulmonary-ventilation.md) balance.
