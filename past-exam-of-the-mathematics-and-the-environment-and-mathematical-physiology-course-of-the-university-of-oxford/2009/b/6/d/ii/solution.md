<h1 id="b/6/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Since the [steady state](../../../../../../steady-state.md) is strictly positive, the [positive-part](../../../../../../positive-part-of-a-real-valued-function.md) [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) is differentiable in a neighbourhood of it. Write $p=p_*+q$. Retaining first-order terms gives

$$
q'(T)=-\alpha\mu p_*q(T)-\alpha(1+\mu p_*)q(T-1).
$$

Substitute the exponential mode $q=e^{\lambda T}$ to obtain

$$
\boxed{\lambda=-\beta-\gamma e^{-\lambda},\qquad\beta=\alpha\mu p_*,\quad\gamma=\alpha(1+\mu p_*)=\alpha/p_*.}
$$

Here the last equality uses the exact steady-state [equation](../../../../../../equation.md). For small $\mu$, $\beta=\alpha\mu+O(\alpha\mu^2)$ and $\gamma=\alpha(1+\mu)+O(\alpha\mu^2)$. Linearizing at the exact root avoids carrying a spurious constant residual from the printed approximate [equilibrium](../../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md).
