↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For an experiment beginning by mixing unbound [enzyme](../../../../../enzyme-split.md) with [substrate](../../../../../substrate-biochemistry.md), take $\boxed{[E](0)=e_0>0,\ [S](0)=s_0>0,\ [X](0)=[Y](0)=[E_i](0)=[P](0)=0}$. The [enzyme-substrate complexes](../../../../../enzyme-substrate-complex.md), inactivated enzyme and product are initially absent. Nonzero pre-existing product could be allowed without changing the dynamics here, since it has no feedback reaction in the specified scheme.
