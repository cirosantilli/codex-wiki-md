# 2009

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the mathematics and the environment and mathematical physiology course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

This [University of Oxford](../university-of-oxford-split.md) Part C paper combines Mathematics and the Environment in section A with Mathematical Physiology in section B. [Source release](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/maths_past_papers_3) The archive preserves the complete editable seven-page paper. Bounded searches of official sources found no advertised answers for this exact paper. The solutions distinguish linear and asymptotic conclusions from exact nonlinear claims, and identify the carrier-total typo and small-parameter respiratory approximations.
