# Pyrometer

↑ **Parent:** [Temperature measurement](temperature-measurement.md)

An instrument for estimating high [temperatures](temperature-split.md). The physical response and calibration must be specified; thermal expansion, a calorimetric transfer, electrical properties and emitted radiation lead to different methods and different limitations.
