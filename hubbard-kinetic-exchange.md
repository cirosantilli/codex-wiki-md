# Hubbard kinetic exchange

↑ **Parent:** [Two-site Hubbard dimer](two-site-hubbard-dimer.md)

At one [electron](electron.md) per site and $U\gg|t|$, the low-energy singlet of a repulsive [two-site Hubbard dimer](two-site-hubbard-dimer.md) mixes virtually with doubly occupied configurations. Its [energy](energy-split.md) is $-4t^2/U$ to leading order, while the three triplets have no such shift. With dimensionless spin-half operators this yields the displayed effective antiferromagnetic [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md). Restoring angular-momentum operators with units of $\hbar$ requires the corresponding $1/\hbar^2$ in the [spin](spin.md) product.
