# Translation continuity in Lp

↑ **Parent:** [Lp space](lp-space.md)

For $1\leq p<\infty$ and $f\in L^p(\mathbb R^d)$, translations converge to $f$ in the [Lp space](lp-space.md) [norm](norm.md). First prove this for continuous compactly supported [functions](function-split.md), using uniform continuity and a common bounded support. Their density in [Lp space](lp-space.md), together with the norm-preserving translation map and the [triangle inequality](triangle-inequality.md), proves the assertion for arbitrary $f$. It implies convergence of averages over shrinking intervals, a basic [approximate identity](approximate-identity.md) argument. The corresponding assertion fails for general $L^\infty$ functions, such as an [indicator function](indicator-function.md) with a jump.
