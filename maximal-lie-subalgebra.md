# Maximal Lie subalgebra

↑ **Parent:** [Lie subalgebra](lie-subalgebra.md)

A maximal [Lie subalgebra](lie-subalgebra.md) $\mathfrak h$ of $\mathfrak g$ is a proper [Lie subalgebra](lie-subalgebra.md) with no intermediate proper [Lie subalgebra](lie-subalgebra.md). A useful sufficient criterion is that the quotient [representation](group-representation.md) $\mathfrak g/\mathfrak h$ be an [irreducible representation](irreducible-representation.md) under the [adjoint action](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-group.md) of $\mathfrak h$: any intermediate [Lie subalgebra](lie-subalgebra.md) gives an [invariant subspace](invariant-subspace.md) of this quotient, hence must be either $\mathfrak h$ or $\mathfrak g$. For example, $\mathfrak{so}(p)\oplus\mathfrak{so}(q)\subset\mathfrak{so}(p+q)$ has quotient given by the [external tensor product of group representations](external-tensor-product-of-group-representations.md) of the two defining modules. For $p,q\ge3$ these are complex [irreducible representations](irreducible-representation.md), making the inclusion maximal.
