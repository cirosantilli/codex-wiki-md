# Mass multipole moment

↑ **Parent:** [Multipole moment](multipole-moment.md)

Spatial moments of a [mass density](density.md) distribution organize its far [gravitational field](gravitational-field.md). Symmetric trace-free parts isolate angular multipoles; the second moment gives the [mass quadrupole moment](mass-quadrupole-moment.md). In slow-motion [linearized gravity](linearized-gravity-split.md), changes of the quadrupole produce the leading [gravitational radiation](gravitational-wave-split.md); the conserved monopole and centre-of-mass dipole do not radiate independently.
