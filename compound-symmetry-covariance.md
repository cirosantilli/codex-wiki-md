# Compound-symmetry covariance

↑ **Parent:** [Covariance matrix](covariance-matrix.md)

A [compound-symmetry covariance](compound-symmetry-covariance.md) matrix has a common diagonal entry and a common off-diagonal entry: $\Sigma=aI+cJ$. For a group of size $k$, its within-group [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) is $a$ and its constant-direction [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) is $a+kc$. Thus $a\geq0$ and $a+kc\geq0$ characterize [positive semidefiniteness](positive-semidefinite-matrix.md). When $c\geq0$, an independent shared [random intercept](random-intercept.md) plus individual noise realizes this covariance. A group mean has [variance](variance-split.md) $c+a/k$; a difference of two disjoint subgroup means within the same group cancels $c$.
