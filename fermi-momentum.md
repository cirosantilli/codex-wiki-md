# Fermi momentum

↑ **Parent:** [Fermi energy](fermi-energy.md)

Fermi momentum is the momentum at the boundary of occupied one-particle states in a zero-temperature [Fermi gas](fermi-gas.md). For spin-one-half particles in three dimensions, counting the two spin states gives $p_F=\hbar(3\pi^2n)^{1/3}$, where $n$ is [number density](number-density.md).
