# Adiabatic apsidal condition

↑ **Parent:** [Adiabatic orbital expansion under isotropic mass loss](adiabatic-orbital-expansion-under-isotropic-mass-loss.md)

The signed instantaneous contribution of [apsidal precession](apsidal-precession.md) to azimuthal motion during [isotropic stellar mass loss](isotropic-stellar-mass-loss.md) is $\dot\varpi/\dot\theta=-(\dot\mu/\mu n)(1-e^2)^{3/2}\sin f/[e(1+e\cos f)^2]$. Its maximum absolute magnitude is small when $n|\mu/\dot\mu|\gg F(e)$, where $F(e)\sim1/e$ near a circular orbit and $F(e)\to3\sqrt3/4$ as $e\to1$. The low-[orbital eccentricity](orbital-eccentricity.md) coordinate singularity can be avoided by evolving the [eccentricity vector](eccentricity-vector.md).
