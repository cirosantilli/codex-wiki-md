# Wave-activity deposition

↑ **Parent:** [Eliassen–Palm flux](eliassen-palm-flux.md)

When waves dissipate or encounter a critical layer, their [Eliassen–Palm flux](eliassen-palm-flux.md) changes with position. Its divergence transfers wave pseudomomentum to the mean flow and produces wave drag.
