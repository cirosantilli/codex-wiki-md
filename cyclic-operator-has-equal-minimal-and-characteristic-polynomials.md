# Cyclic operator has equal minimal and characteristic polynomials

↑ **Parent:** [Cyclic vector](cyclic-vector.md)

On a $d$-dimensional [vector space](vector-space-split.md), a [cyclic vector](cyclic-vector.md) $v$ gives a [basis](basis.md) $v,Tv,\ldots,T^{d-1}v$. Write $T^dv=-\sum_{j<d}c_jT^jv$ and set $f(t)=t^d+\sum_{j<d}c_jt^j$. Then $f(T)v=0$. Since [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) in $T$ commute with $T$, $f(T)$ kills every basis vector, so $f(T)=0$. No nonzero polynomial of degree less than $d$ can kill $v$, by [linear independence](linear-independence.md). Hence the [minimal polynomial](minimal-polynomial.md) is $f$. The matrix in this basis is $C(f)$, and the [characteristic polynomial of a companion matrix](characteristic-polynomial-of-a-companion-matrix.md) is $f$ as well.
