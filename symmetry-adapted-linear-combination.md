# Symmetry-adapted linear combination

↑ **Parent:** [Linear combination of atomic orbitals](linear-combination-of-atomic-orbitals.md)

A [linear combination of atomic orbitals](linear-combination-of-atomic-orbitals.md) chosen to transform under an [irreducible representation](irreducible-representation.md) of the molecular [point group](point-group.md). Relative orbital signs must be transformed as well as the positions of their centres. Such combinations organize a [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) into symmetry blocks; combinations of the same symmetry generally still mix and need not themselves be [molecular orbitals](molecular-orbital.md).
