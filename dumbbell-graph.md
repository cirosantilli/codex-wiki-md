# Dumbbell graph

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic topology](algebraic-topology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dumbbell_graph)

A dumbbell graph consists of two cycles joined by a path. Its connecting path contains bridges, unlike a [Theta graph](theta-graph.md), although both the two-loop dumbbell and $\Theta_3$ have first homology $\mathbb Z^2$.
