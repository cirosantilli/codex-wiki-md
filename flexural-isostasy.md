# Flexural isostasy

↑ **Parent:** [Glacial isostatic adjustment](glacial-isostatic-adjustment.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flexural_isostasy)

Flexural isostasy models the lithosphere as an [elastic plate](elastic-plate.md) that bends over a buoyant substrate. For a sinusoidal deflection of wavenumber $k$, bending contributes a restoring stress proportional to $Bk^4$, where $B$ is the plate's bending stiffness.
