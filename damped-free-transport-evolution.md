# Damped free-transport evolution

↑ **Parent:** [Linear Boltzmann equation](linear-boltzmann-equation.md)

For nonnegative measurable $a$, define $(U_a(t,s)g)(x,v)=e^{-\int_s^t a(r,x-v(t-r),v)dr}g(x-v(t-s),v)$. The shear preserves [Lebesgue measure](lebesgue-measure.md) and the multiplier lies in $[0,1]$, so this is a contraction on [Lp spaces](lp-space.md). Extended nonnegative integrals use $e^{-\infty}=0$. Characteristic local integrability gives the usual continuous trace at the starting time.
