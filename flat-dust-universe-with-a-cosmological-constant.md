# Flat dust universe with a cosmological constant

↑ **Parent:** [Friedmann equations](friedmann-equations.md)

For [pressureless matter](pressureless-matter.md), put $C=8\pi G\rho_m a^3/3>0$ and use units $c=1$. The flat [Friedmann equation](friedmann-equations.md) is $\dot a^2=C/a+\Lambda a^2/3$. Substitution $u=a^{3/2}$ gives $\dot u^2=(9/4)(C+\Lambda u^2/3)$. With a [Big Bang](big-bang.md) at $t=0$, positive [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md) gives $a=(3C/\Lambda)^{1/3}\sinh^{2/3}(\sqrt{3\Lambda}\,t/2)$. Negative [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md) replaces the hyperbolic sine by $\sin(\sqrt{3|\Lambda|}\,t/2)$ and produces recollapse at $2\pi/\sqrt{3|\Lambda|}$. Zero [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md) gives $a=(9C/4)^{1/3}t^{2/3}$.
