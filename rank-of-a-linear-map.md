# Rank of a linear map

↑ **Parent:** [Linear map](linear-map.md)

The rank of a [linear map](linear-map.md) is the dimension of its [image of a linear map](image-of-a-linear-map.md). In finite-dimensional spaces it equals the [rank of a matrix](matrix-rank.md) representing the map in any bases.
