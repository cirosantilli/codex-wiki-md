# Transitive relation

↑ **Parent:** [Binary relation](binary-relation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transitive_relation)

A relation $\mathrel R$ is transitive when $x\mathrel R y$ and $y\mathrel R z$ imply $x\mathrel R z$.
