# Relative tautological line bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Holomorphic projectivization by lines](holomorphic-projectivization-by-lines.md)

On the [holomorphic projectivization by lines](holomorphic-projectivization-by-lines.md), take the line itself as the fibre of $S$. The evaluation inclusion makes it a holomorphic line subbundle of $p^*E$. Its fibre restriction is $\mathcal O(-1)$; the dual is the [relative hyperplane line bundle](relative-hyperplane-line-bundle.md).
