# Standing accretion shock instability

↑ **Parent:** [Core-collapse shock revival](core-collapse-shock-revival.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standing_accretion_shock_instability)

A nonradial instability of the stalled accretion [shock](shock-wave.md) and post-shock flow in a [core-collapse supernova](core-collapse-supernova.md). It can amplify large-scale sloshing or spiral motion through feedback between advected perturbations and acoustic disturbances. It can seed asymmetries relevant to explosion and remnant recoil, but is not itself a unique complete explanation of every neutron-star natal kick.
