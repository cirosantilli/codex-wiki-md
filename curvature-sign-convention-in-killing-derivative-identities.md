# Curvature-sign convention in Killing derivative identities

↑ **Parent:** [Second covariant derivative of a Killing vector](second-covariant-derivative-of-a-killing-vector.md)

Let $C^a{}_{bcd}=\partial_d\Gamma^a_{bc}-\partial_c\Gamma^a_{bd}-\Gamma^a_{ec}\Gamma^e_{bd}+\Gamma^a_{ed}\Gamma^e_{bc}$, with all other index positions obtained by the [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md). This convention is the negative of the convention $[\nabla_c,\nabla_d]V^a=R^a{}_{bcd}V^b$. The [Ricci identity](curvature-commutator-on-a-covariant-tensor.md) is $k_{a;bc}-k_{a;cb}=C_a{}^d{}_{bc}k_d$, with $k_{a;bc}=\nabla_c\nabla_b k_a$. For a [Killing vector](killing-vector-field.md), the [Killing equation](killing-equation.md) makes this second derivative antisymmetric in $a,b$. Applying the commutator three times gives $2k_{a;bc}=(C_a{}^d{}_{bc}+C_c{}^d{}_{ba}-C_b{}^d{}_{ac})k_d=-2C_{abc}{}^d k_d$, where the last step uses the [first Bianchi identity](first-bianchi-identity.md). Switching the sign of the [Riemann curvature tensor](riemann-curvature-tensor.md) switches the sign in this identity; moving a raised index by ordinary metric contraction does not switch it.
