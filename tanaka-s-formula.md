<h1 id="tanaka-s-formula">Tanaka's formula</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Itô's lemma](ito-s-lemma.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tanaka's_formula)

For a continuous semimartingale $X$,

$$
|X_t|=|X_0|+\int_0^t\operatorname{sgn}(X_s)\,dX_s+L_t^0(X),
$$

where $L^0(X)$ is the [local time of a semimartingale](local-time-of-a-semimartingale.md) at zero.
