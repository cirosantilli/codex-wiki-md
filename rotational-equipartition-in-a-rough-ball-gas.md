# Rotational equipartition in a rough-ball gas

↑ **Parent:** [Equipartition theorem](equipartition-theorem.md)

Rough elastic collisions can exchange rotational and translational [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md). For three-dimensional rigid balls with all three rotations thermalized, these six quadratic terms contribute $3k_BT$ per particle. A uniform gravitational height distribution adds $k_BT$, giving total mechanical mean energy $4k_BT$.
