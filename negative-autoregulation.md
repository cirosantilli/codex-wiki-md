# Negative autoregulation

↑ **Parent:** [Gene regulatory network](gene-regulatory-network.md)

A [gene](gene.md) product reduces its own production. This [negative feedback](negative-feedback.md) can stabilize its abundance and shorten the approach to a regulated level. In the strong [logic approximation for gene regulation](logic-approximation-for-gene-regulation.md), production switches off near a self-repression threshold; a smooth repression law avoids artificial infinitely rapid switching at that threshold.
