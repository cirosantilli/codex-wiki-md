# Hessian criterion for a flex

↑ **Parent:** [Inflection point of an algebraic plane curve](inflection-point-of-an-algebraic-plane-curve.md)

For a [plane curve](plane-curve.md) defined by a [homogeneous polynomial](homogeneous-polynomial.md) $F$ of degree $d\ge2$, assume the [field characteristic](characteristic-of-a-field.md) is different from two and $d-1$ is nonzero in the [field](field.md). A [smooth point of a variety](smooth-point-of-a-variety.md) is a [flex](inflection-point-of-an-algebraic-plane-curve.md) exactly when the [determinant](determinant.md) of the homogeneous [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) vanishes there, provided its [tangent line](tangent-line.md) is not a component. Put the point at $[0:0:1]$ with tangent $y=0$ and $F_y(p)\ne0$. The homogeneous identities give $F_{xz}(p)=F_{zz}(p)=0$ and $F_{yz}(p)=(d-1)F_y(p)$, so the determinant is $-(d-1)^2F_y(p)^2F_{xx}(p)$. In the completed [local ring](local-ring.md), write the curve as a [formal power series](formal-power-series.md) $y=h(x)$. Its linear coefficient is zero and its quadratic coefficient is $-F_{xx}(p)/(2F_y(p))$. Vanishing of that coefficient is exactly [intersection multiplicity](intersection-multiplicity.md) at least three with the tangent, proving the criterion in these positive characteristics as well as in characteristic zero.
