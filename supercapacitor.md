# Supercapacitor

↑ **Parent:** [Capacitor](capacitor.md)

An electrochemical capacitor storing charge through high-area [electric double layers](electric-double-layer.md), [pseudocapacitance](pseudocapacitance.md), or a combination. Its device energy depends on usable voltage, inactive mass and rate capability as well as electrode capacitance.
