# Silicate weathering

↑ **Parent:** [Geophysics](geophysics-split.md)

Chemical alteration of silicate minerals by [water](water.md) and dissolved [carbon dioxide](carbon-dioxide.md). Coupled to transport and subsequent carbonate burial, this provides a long-term geological sink for added carbon. Its response is much slower than atmospheric mixing or upper-ocean exchange; a rapid pulse can persist while this adjustment proceeds.
