# Newton forward interpolation

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial interpolation](polynomial-interpolation.md)

For equally spaced nodes $x_0,x_0+h,\ldots,x_0+dh$, write $t=(x-x_0)/h$. The interpolating [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) is $P(x)=\sum_{j=0}^d\binom tj\Delta_h^j f(x_0)$. A table of [forward differences](forward-difference.md) supplies its coefficients; for a [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) of degree at most $d$, the formula reproduces the function exactly.
