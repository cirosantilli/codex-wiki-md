# Kolmogorov two-thirds law

↑ **Parent:** [Kolmogorov second similarity hypothesis](kolmogorov-second-similarity-hypothesis.md)

The second-order [longitudinal structure function](longitudinal-velocity-structure-function.md) has the displayed form in the idealized [inertial range](inertial-range.md) of [Kolmogorov 1941 theory](kolmogorov-1941-theory.md). It predicts that the typical longitudinal [velocity increment](velocity-increment.md) grows as the cube root of separation. Unlike the [Kolmogorov four-fifths law](kolmogorov-four-fifths-law.md), its exponent and coefficient are not exact consequences of the [Navier-Stokes equation](navier-stokes-equation.md).
