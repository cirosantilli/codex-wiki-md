# Cantor part of a bounded-variation derivative

↑ **Parent:** [Function of bounded variation on a domain](function-of-bounded-variation-on-a-domain.md)

The [derivative](derivative.md) [measure](measure.md) of a [BV space](function-of-bounded-variation-on-a-domain.md) function decomposes into its absolutely continuous, jump and Cantor parts. The [Cantor part of a bounded-variation derivative](cantor-part-of-a-bounded-variation-derivative.md) is singular but not a jump [measure](measure.md) on a rectifiable hypersurface. The [special bounded-variation space](special-bounded-variation-space.md) excludes it; finite-length [image signal](image-signal.md) interfaces alone cannot describe it.
