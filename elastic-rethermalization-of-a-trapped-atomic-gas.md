# Elastic rethermalization of a trapped atomic gas

↑ **Parent:** [Forced evaporative cooling](forced-evaporative-cooling.md)

After energy-selective loss, [elastic collisions](elastic-collision.md) redistribute [energy](energy-split.md) and refill the escaping tail. This restores a nearly thermal distribution when redistribution is fast compared with the changing cutoff and unwanted atom loss. The collision rate depends on [number density](number-density.md), [cross section](cross-section-physics.md), and relative speed; slowing atoms alone does not guarantee continued equilibration.
