# Boltzmann superposition

↑ **Parent:** [Linear viscoelastic fluid](linear-viscoelastic-fluid.md)

In time-translation-invariant [linear response](linear-response-theory.md), each infinitesimal past strain step contributes its amplitude times the elapsed-time [relaxation modulus](relaxation-modulus.md). Adding those causal contributions gives the displayed stress-history integral for shear. An equivalent formulation convolves the [creep compliance](creep-compliance.md) with stress increments. This constitutive law assumes sufficiently small strains about a specified equilibrium or relaxed state; nonlinear deformation, aging and evolving material structure can invalidate that simple superposition.
