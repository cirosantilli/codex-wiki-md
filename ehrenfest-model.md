# Ehrenfest model

↑ **Parent:** [Markov chain](markov-chain-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ehrenfest_model)

A model of random exchange between two containers, associated with [Paul Ehrenfest](paul-ehrenfest.md) and [Tatyana Afanasyeva-Ehrenfest](tatyana-afanasyeva-ehrenfest.md). In the discrete-time [Ehrenfest urn model](ehrenfest-urn-model.md), choose one of $N$ balls uniformly at each step and change its container. The count in the first container decreases with probability $r/N$ and increases with probability $(N-r)/N$. A continuous-time version lets each particle change container independently at rate $\lambda$, giving [birth-death process](birth-death-process.md) transition rates $r\lambda$ and $(N-r)\lambda$. Its sequence of visited states is the discrete urn [Markov chain](markov-chain-split.md). Stopping when one container becomes empty produces an absorbing variant, rather than changing the transition rule of the unstopped model.
