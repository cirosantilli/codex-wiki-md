# Instanton translation zero mode

↑ **Parent:** [Instanton fluctuation prefactor](instanton-fluctuation-prefactor.md)

Differentiating the Euclidean classical equation for an [instanton](instanton.md) gives a zero eigenfunction proportional to its time derivative. Its norm satisfies $\mu\int\dot q_I^2d\tau=S_I$, so replacing the Gaussian zero-mode integral by the center coordinate gives $\sqrt{S_I/(2\pi\hbar)}d\tau_0$. The nonzero modes belong in the primed [functional determinant](functional-determinant.md). This removes the false divergence without deleting the center's integration volume.
