# NADPH

↑ **Parent:** [Nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide phosphate](nicotinamide-adenine-dinucleotide-phosphate.md)

[NADPH](nadph.md) is the reduced form of [nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide phosphate](nicotinamide-adenine-dinucleotide-phosphate.md). It carries [Electrons](electron.md) used in synthesis; in the [Calvin cycle](calvin-cycle.md) it becomes the oxidized form [NADP+](nadp-plus.md), available for reuse by [light-dependent reactions](light-dependent-reactions.md). [NADPH](nadph.md) and [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) serve different roles even though both connect [light](light.md) capture to [carbon fixation](carbon-fixation.md).
