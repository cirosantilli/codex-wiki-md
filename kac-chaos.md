# Kac chaos

↑ **Parent:** [Kac model](kac-model.md)

A permutation-symmetric sequence of probability laws is chaotic with one-particle law $f$ when, for every fixed $k$, its $k$-coordinate [marginal distributions](marginal-distribution.md) converge weakly to the product law $f^{\otimes k}$ as $N\to\infty$. This is an asymptotic independence condition, not a consequence of symmetry alone. A rigorous kinetic limit also requires propagation of this property under the dynamics and sufficient convergence to pass collision [integrals](integral.md).
