# Solar hydrogen and helium-3 abundance profiles

↑ **Parent:** [Proton-proton chain branches](proton-proton-chain-branches.md)

Present solar [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) is most depleted in the central burning region and rises toward the envelope. [helium-3](helium-3.md) is efficiently destroyed in the hot centre, increases outward as its equilibrium abundance rises, and develops an off-centre maximum where equilibration becomes slow. Still farther out its production becomes weak, leaving a smaller envelope abundance. Convective mixing tends to flatten the outer profiles. This qualitative pattern is not itself a quantitative solar model.
