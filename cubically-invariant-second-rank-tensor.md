# Cubically invariant second-rank tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Cartesian second-rank tensor](cartesian-second-rank-tensor.md)

A [Cartesian second-rank tensor](cartesian-second-rank-tensor.md) in three dimensions invariant under quarter-turn [rotations](rotation-mathematics.md) about all three coordinate axes is a scalar multiple of the [identity matrix](identity-matrix.md). Squaring the quarter-turns gives coordinate half-turns; invariance under these forces every off-diagonal component to vanish. The quarter-turns then equate the three diagonal components. This conclusion does not require the original [tensor](tensor.md) to be symmetric.
