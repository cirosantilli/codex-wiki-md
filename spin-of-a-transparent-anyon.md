# Spin of a transparent anyon

↑ **Parent:** [Transparent anyon](transparent-anyon.md)

Apply the [anyon balancing identity](anyon-balancing-identity.md) to the [vacuum anyon](vacuum-anyon.md) channel in $a\times\bar a$. Transparency gives $1=(\theta_a\theta_{\bar a})^{-1}=\theta_a^{-2}$. Thus a [transparent anyon](transparent-anyon.md) has bosonic or fermionic [topological spin](topological-spin.md). This does not require its [quantum dimension](quantum-dimension.md) to equal one.
