# Matching pennies

↑ **Parent:** [Bimatrix game](bimatrix-game.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matching_pennies)

Each player chooses heads or tails. One receives $1$ if the choices agree and $-1$ otherwise; the other receives the negative payoff. At every pure profile, the losing player can improve by switching, so there is no pure [Nash equilibrium](nash-equilibrium.md). Independent uniform randomization makes both pure choices equally valuable for each player and gives a mixed [Nash equilibrium](nash-equilibrium.md).
