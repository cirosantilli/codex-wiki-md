# Virasoro Verma module

↑ **Parent:** [Virasoro algebra](virasoro-algebra.md)

The [Virasoro Verma module](virasoro-verma-module.md) $M(c,h)$ is the universal [highest-weight representation](highest-weight-representation.md) with [central charge](central-charge.md) $c$ and ground weight $h$. The [Poincaré-Birkhoff-Witt theorem](poincare-birkhoff-witt-theorem.md) gives a basis $L_{-n_1}\cdots L_{-n_s}|h\rangle$ with $n_1\ge\cdots\ge n_s>0$. Its invariant Hermitian form is fixed by $L_n^\dagger=L_{-n}$ and the norm of the ground vector. Quotienting by its maximal proper submodule produces the irreducible highest-weight module; in the unitary cases this removes the null vectors.
