# Rigidity of a negative curve

↑ **Parent:** [Negative curve on a projective surface](negative-curve-on-a-projective-surface.md)

If an [effective divisor](effective-cartier-divisor.md) $D$ is [linearly equivalent](linear-equivalence-of-weil-divisors.md) to a [negative curve](negative-curve-on-a-projective-surface.md) $C$, then $D=C$. Otherwise $D\cdot C=C^2<0$ forces $C$ to be a component of $D$, since distinct irreducible curves have nonnegative [intersection multiplicities](intersection-multiplicity.md). Write $D=C+D'$ with $D'$ effective and linearly equivalent to zero. An [ample divisor](ample-cartier-divisor.md) intersects $D'$ positively unless $D'=0$, while linear equivalence forces that intersection to vanish.
