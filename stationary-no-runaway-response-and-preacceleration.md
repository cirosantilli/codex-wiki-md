# Stationary no-runaway response and preacceleration

↑ **Parent:** [Radiation reaction](radiation-reaction.md)

For positive $\varepsilon$, the real-axis frequency response of the [radiation-reaction oscillator pole structure](radiation-reaction-oscillator-pole-structure.md) excludes future runaway growth but retains an upper-half-plane pole. Closing its inverse [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md) upward at $t<0$ gives a nonzero response before the force. The fast pole contributes approximately $\varepsilon e^{t/\varepsilon}$ for negative $t$. A strictly causal retarded prescription instead includes an exponentially growing term for positive $t$ and requires a [Laplace transform](laplace-transform.md) contour. The pre-response is a consequence of imposing boundedness at future infinity, not an unavoidable property of all initial-value solutions of the differential equation.
