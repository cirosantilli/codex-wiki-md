# Linear polyhedron

↑ **Parent:** [Linear programming](linear-programming.md)

A linear polyhedron is an intersection of finitely many closed affine half-spaces in finite-dimensional real space. It may be empty, unbounded, or lower-dimensional. This usage differs from a three-dimensional geometric [polyhedron](polyhedron.md). The [strict separation of disjoint linear polyhedra](strict-separation-of-disjoint-linear-polyhedra.md) follows from [linear programming duality](linear-programming-duality.md).
