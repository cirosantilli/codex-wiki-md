# Cantilever spring constant

↑ **Parent:** [AFM cantilever](afm-cantilever.md)

The normal restoring-force coefficient of an [AFM cantilever](afm-cantilever.md), $k_c=F/\delta_c$, with units $\mathrm{N\,m^{-1}}$. A quantitative [force](force-split.md) measurement requires both this stiffness and the optical deflection sensitivity.
