# Optimal Selberg weights have modulus at most one

↑ **Parent:** [Selberg sieve diagonalization](selberg-sieve-diagonalization.md)

Let $k(d)=\prod_{p\mid d}g(p)/(1-g(p))$ on permitted [squarefree integers](squarefree-integer.md). The [Selberg sieve weights](selberg-sieve-weights.md) have

$$
|\rho_d|=\frac1{J\prod_{p\mid d}(1-g(p))}\sum_{\substack{v\leq L/d\\(v,d)=1}}k(v)\leq1.
$$

The numerator is the total weight of the distinct integers $ev\leq L$ with $e\mid d$ and $(v,d)=1$: [multiplicativity](multiplicativity-of-an-arithmetic-function.md) gives $\sum_{e\mid d}k(e)=\prod_{p\mid d}(1-g(p))^{-1}$. They are a subset of the terms in $J$.
