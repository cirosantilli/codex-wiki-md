# Computational-basis intercept-resend attack

↑ **Parent:** [Intercept-resend attack](intercept-resend-attack.md)

Measuring the transmitted [qubit](qubit.md) in the [computational basis](computational-basis.md) and forwarding its measured state produces a [dephasing channel](dephasing-channel.md) for users who do not know the outcome. For a [Bell state](bell-state-split.md) $|\Psi^+\rangle$, the reduced state becomes $(|01\rangle\langle01|+|10\rangle\langle10|)/2$. It retains perfect $Z$ anticorrelation while destroying $X$ correlation. Thus a single perfectly correlated key basis need not reveal an [intercept-resend attack](intercept-resend-attack.md); incompatible-basis correlation tests can.
