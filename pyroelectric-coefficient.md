# Pyroelectric coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Pyroelectricity](pyroelectricity.md)

The [temperature](temperature-split.md) derivative of a component of spontaneous [electric polarization](polarization-density.md) under stated mechanical/electrical constraints. An [electrode](electrode.md) of area $A$ has ideal short-circuit response $I=pA\,dT/dt$. Mechanical constraints distinguish primary and secondary contributions.
