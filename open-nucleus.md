# Open nucleus

↑ **Parent:** [Nucleus on a frame](nucleus-on-a-frame.md)

The [Heyting implication](heyting-implication.md) defines a [nucleus on a frame](nucleus-on-a-frame.md) whose [sublocale](sublocale.md) is the open determined by $a$. It is the least [nucleus on a frame](nucleus-on-a-frame.md) sending $a$ to $1$.
