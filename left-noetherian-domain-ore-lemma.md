# Left Noetherian domain Ore lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Left Ore set](left-ore-set.md)

In a [noncommutative domain](noncommutative-domain.md) that is a [left Noetherian ring](left-noetherian-ring.md), two nonzero principal [left ideals](left-ideal.md) always intersect nontrivially. If $Ra\cap Rb=0$, a relation $\sum_{i=0}^m u_iab^i=0$ first forces $u_0a\in Ra\cap Rb$, then $u_0=0$. Right cancellation of $b$ and repetition give all $u_i=0$. Hence $\sum_{i\geq0}Rab^i$ is an infinite [direct sum](direct-sum.md) of nonzero [left ideals](left-ideal.md), whose finite partial sums violate the [ascending chain condition](ascending-chain-condition.md). A nonzero member of $Rr\cap Rs$ supplies the equality $s'r=r's$ with $s'\ne0$, proving the [left Ore set](left-ore-set.md) condition for all nonzero elements.
