# Quadratic Stark shift of a polar linear rotor

↑ **Parent:** [Rotational Stark effect](rotational-stark-effect.md)

The dipole coupling $-pE\cos\theta$ connects only $J\pm1$ at fixed $m$. For $J\ge1$, second order gives $(pE)^2[J(J+1)-3m^2]/[2BJ(J+1)(2J-1)(2J+3)]$; the ground-state result is separately $-(pE)^2/(6B)$. Here $B$ has [energy](energy-split.md) units and $\theta$ is the [polar angle](polar-angle.md) to the field.
