# Cohomological Serre spectral sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Serre spectral sequence](serre-spectral-sequence.md)

For a [Serre fibration](serre-fibration.md), this first-quadrant spectral sequence has differential of bidegree $(r,1-r)$ and a fiber-cohomology local coefficient system on the base. A [simply connected](simply-connected-space.md) base makes that system constant. Its edge map is restriction to the fiber; a surviving fiber class is the restriction of a total-space class. For commutative ring coefficients it is multiplicative and its differentials are graded derivations.
