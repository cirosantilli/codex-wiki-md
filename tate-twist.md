# Tate twist

↑ **Parent:** [Cyclotomic character](cyclotomic-character.md)

The Tate twist $M(1)$ multiplies a [Galois representation](galois-representation.md)'s action by the [cyclotomic character](cyclotomic-character.md). Here $\mathbb Z_p(1)$ is the inverse limit of $p$-power [roots of unity](root-of-unity.md) under power maps. For a one-variable [Iwasawa module](iwasawa-module.md), twisting changes characteristic power series by the corresponding change in the generator's action.
