# String junction

↑ **Parent:** [(p,q) string](p-q-string.md)

At a junction of [(p,q) strings](p-q-string.md), their oriented charge vectors sum to zero. A static junction also balances the tension vectors. Supersymmetry further correlates spatial directions with charge phases so that the arms share [kappa symmetry projectors](kappa-symmetry-projector.md). Additional independent conditions reduce the common [Killing spinor](killing-spinor.md) space; an aligned dyonic string alone does not reduce its fraction relative to an aligned fundamental string.
