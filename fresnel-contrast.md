# Fresnel contrast

↑ **Parent:** [Electron phase contrast](electron-phase-contrast.md)

Defocus-dependent edge fringes caused by propagation and interference of the [electron](electron.md) wave. Their apparent width and sign can change with defocus, so they do not directly measure the thickness of a material shell.
