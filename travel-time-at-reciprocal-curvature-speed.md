# Travel time at reciprocal-curvature speed

↑ **Parent:** [Curvature of a plane curve](curvature-of-a-plane-curve.md)

If a regular plane curve has positive [curvature](curvature.md) $\kappa$ and a particle moves with [speed](speed.md) $v=1/\kappa$ in the chosen time units, its elapsed time is $\int\kappa\,ds$, the [total curvature](total-curvature.md) traversed. On a [logarithmic spiral](logarithmic-spiral.md) $r=ae^{b\theta}$, both $ds/d\theta$ and $1/\kappa$ equal $r\sqrt{1+b^2}$. Hence $dt=d\theta$ and every complete turn takes time $2\pi$, regardless of its radius.
