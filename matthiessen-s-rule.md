<h1 id="matthiessen-s-rule">Matthiessen's rule</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical resistivity](electrical-resistivity.md)

[Matthiessen's rule](matthiessen-s-rule.md) approximates the total [electrical resistivity](electrical-resistivity.md) of a [metal](metal.md) by the sum of separate scattering contributions. For a common parabolic [energy band](energy-band.md), approximately independent scattering mechanisms give $1/\tau=\sum_j1/\tau_j$ for the respective [momentum relaxation times](momentum-relaxation-time.md), and hence $\rho_{\rm el}=m/(ne^2\tau)=\sum_jm/(ne^2\tau_j)$. Correlated scattering, strongly angle-dependent processes or a complicated changing [energy band](energy-band.md) structure can invalidate the simple addition.
