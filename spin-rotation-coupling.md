# Spin-rotation coupling

↑ **Parent:** [Spin](spin.md)

The [spin-rotation coupling](spin-rotation-coupling.md) contribution to a rotating-frame [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) couples [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md) to [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md). When discussing [spin](spin.md) alone, identify which part of the total [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) enters the effective model.
