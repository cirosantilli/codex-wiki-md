# Resonance structure

↑ **Parent:** [Resonance (chemistry)](resonance-chemistry.md)

A [resonance structure](resonance-structure.md) is one [Lewis structure](lewis-structure.md) contributing to a [chemical resonance](resonance-chemistry.md) description. The contributors retain the same [atomic nuclei](atomic-nucleus.md) in the same positions, the same connections, and the same total [valence electron](valence-electron.md) count and [electric charge](electric-charge.md); allocations of [bond pairs](bond-pair.md), [lone pairs](lone-pair.md), and [formal charges](formal-charge.md) differ. They are alternative representations of one electronic structure, not distinct substances with different atomic arrangements. See [Chemistry 2e, 7.4: resonance forms](https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/7-4-formal-charges-and-resonance).
