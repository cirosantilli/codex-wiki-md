# Tail integral formula for expectation

↑ **Parent:** [Tail integral formula for moments](tail-integral-formula-for-moments.md)

For a nonnegative [random variable](random-variable-split.md), $Y=\int_0^\infty\mathbf1_{\{Y>t\}}dt$ pointwise, including when $Y=\infty$. [Tonelli's theorem](tonelli-theorem.md) permits taking [expectation](expected-value-split.md) under the integral and proves the formula in the extended nonnegative reals. This relates finiteness of a [Brownian exit time](brownian-exit-time.md) [expectation](expected-value-split.md) to integrability of its survival probability.
