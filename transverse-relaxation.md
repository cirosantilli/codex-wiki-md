# Transverse relaxation

↑ **Parent:** [Affine Bloch equation](affine-bloch-equation.md)

Decay of the off-diagonal coherence of a two-level [density operator](density-matrix-split.md). Population relaxation contributes half its total rate; additional [dephasing](dephasing-channel.md) contributes $\gamma_\phi\geq0$ in the usual completely positive two-level model. Thus transverse relaxation should not automatically be identified with pure dephasing alone.
