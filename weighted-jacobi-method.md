# Weighted Jacobi method

↑ **Parent:** [Jacobi method](jacobi-method.md)

With relaxation parameter $\omega$, weighted Jacobi updates by

$$
x^{(\nu+1)}
=x^{(\nu)}+\omega D^{-1}(b-Ax^{(\nu)}).
$$

Its [iteration matrix](iteration-matrix.md) is $H_\omega=I-\omega D^{-1}A$.
