# Boundary-side amplification for planar percolation

↑ **Parent:** [Percolation probability](percolation-probability.md)

If an [infinite percolation cluster](infinite-percolation-cluster.md) exists almost surely, the [probability](probability.md) that a large square box intersects one tends to one. The four rotationally symmetric exterior-side arm events are increasing. By the [Harris-FKG inequality](harris-fkg-inequality.md), their common [probability](probability.md) is at least $1-(1-P(D_N))^{1/4}$, where $D_N$ is their union. Thus a pair of specified opposite sides both has an infinite exterior arm with [probability](probability.md) at least $1-2(1-P(D_N))^{1/4}$, tending to one. The rays start at a boundary [graph vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) and immediately leave the [graph vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) box.
