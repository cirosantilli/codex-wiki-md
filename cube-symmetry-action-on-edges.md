# Cube symmetry action on edges

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetry group of a cube](symmetry-group-of-a-cube.md)

The full [symmetry group of a cube](symmetry-group-of-a-cube.md) acts transitively and faithfully on its twelve edges. For vertices $(\pm1,\pm1,\pm1)$, its [signed permutation matrices](signed-permutation-matrix.md) act transitively on the edges. The [stabilizer subgroup](stabilizer-subgroup.md) of $\{(1,1,z):-1\le z\le1\}$ consists of independently swapping $x,y$ and reversing $z$, and is a [Klein four-group](klein-four-group.md). The [orbit-stabilizer theorem](orbit-stabilizer-theorem.md) gives order $12\cdot4=48$. Fixing every edge fixes every vertex, as each vertex is the unique intersection of its three incident edges, so the [group action](group-action.md) is faithful. The resulting [subgroup](subgroup.md) of $S_{12}$ has index $12!/48$ and is not normal: central inversion has [cycle type](cycle-type.md) $2^6$, whose [conjugacy class](conjugacy-class.md) in $S_{12}$ has $12!/(2^6 6!)=10395>48$ elements.
