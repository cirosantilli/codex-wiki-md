# 2011

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the approximation of functions course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Original solutions to all three questions of the Approximation of Functions paper sat on 16 June 2011. Source: [released Mathematics papers](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/maths_past_papers_3), `Part C 2007 11.pdf`. Question 3 includes a counterexample to the unqualified printed endpoint exchange rule and the corrected rule. Statements are not reproduced.
