# Truncated tensor algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor algebra](tensor-algebra.md)

The truncated [tensor algebra](tensor-algebra.md) discards products of degree greater than $N$. Its multiplication is $(a\otimes b)^{(k)}=\sum_{j=0}^k a^{(j)}\otimes b^{(k-j)}$. Every element with scalar part $1$ is invertible by a finite geometric series in its positive-degree part. Truncated exponential and logarithm are finite polynomials.
