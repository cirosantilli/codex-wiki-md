# Phong reflection model

↑ **Parent:** [Computer graphics](computer-graphics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phong_reflection_model)

[Bui Tuong Phong](bui-tuong-phong.md)'s empirical local lighting model combines ambient illumination, cosine-weighted [Lambertian reflectance](lambertian-reflectance.md), and a specular highlight about the [specular reflection](specular-reflection.md) direction. The exponent of the specular lobe controls highlight width. It is distinct from interpolating [normal vectors](normal-vector.md) across a [triangle](triangle.md).
