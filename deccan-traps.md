# Deccan Traps

↑ **Parent:** [Large igneous province](large-igneous-province.md)

A mainly [basaltic](basaltic-rock.md) continental [large igneous province](large-igneous-province.md) in India, emplaced around the end of the [Cretaceous](cretaceous.md). Its surface deposits provide a major example of [volcanic loading](volcanic-loading.md). Deposited volume, loaded area and [density](density.md) must be distinguished when estimating [isostatic adjustment](isostatic-adjustment.md).
