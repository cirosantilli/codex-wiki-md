# D-instanton

↑ **Parent:** [D-brane](d-brane.md)

A Euclidean type-IIB [D-brane](d-brane.md) localized in all spacetime directions, coupling electrically to the RR scalar $C_0$. It is an [instanton](instanton.md), not a Lorentzian particle with negative spatial dimension. The magnetic partner of that scalar charge is a [D7-brane](d7-brane.md).
