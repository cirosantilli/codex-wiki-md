# Chain map

↑ **Parent:** [Chain complex](chain-complex.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chain_map)

A chain map $f:C\to C'$ is a family $f_i:C_i\to C'_i$ satisfying $d'_if_i=f_{i-1}d_i$.
