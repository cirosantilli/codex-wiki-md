# Shear wave

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic wave in an isotropic solid](elastic-wave-in-an-isotropic-solid.md)

A [shear wave](shear-wave.md) in an isotropic elastic material has [displacement](displacement.md) perpendicular to its [wavevector](wavevector.md), with zero divergence for a plane wave. The [Navier-Cauchy equation](navier-cauchy-equation.md) with [inertia](inertia.md) then reduces to $\rho\mathbf u_{tt}=\mu\Delta\mathbf u$, giving the displayed [wave speed](wave-speed.md). At a boundary it can be generated by mode conversion from a [compressional wave](compressional-wave.md), even when the incident [displacement](displacement.md) is purely longitudinal.
