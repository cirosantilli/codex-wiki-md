# Potential-vorticity inversion

↑ **Parent:** [Potential vorticity](potential-vorticity.md)

Potential-vorticity inversion recovers a balanced [velocity field](velocity-field.md) from [potential vorticity](potential-vorticity.md) and specified boundary and global data. In [two-dimensional vortex dynamics](two-dimensional-vortex-dynamics.md) this is a [Poisson equation](poisson-equation.md); [shallow-water quasi-geostrophic inversion](shallow-water-quasi-geostrophic-inversion.md) and [stratified quasi-geostrophic inversion](stratified-quasi-geostrophic-inversion.md) use different [elliptic boundary value problems](elliptic-boundary-value-problem-split.md). Advance the materially conserved scalar, then invert again to recover the evolving flow. Boundary [buoyancy](buoyancy.md) can induce flow with zero interior anomaly. The balance relation and [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md) are essential: unrestricted [gravity waves](gravity-wave-split.md) are additional degrees of freedom. [McIntyre's potential-vorticity account](https://pordlabs.ucsd.edu/wryoung/theorySeminar/pdf14/McIntyrePV.pdf) explains the general invertibility principle.
