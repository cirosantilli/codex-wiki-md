# Hydrogen iodide: Constants of diatomic molecules by Klaus P. Huber and others

↑ **Parent:** [Chemistry bibliography](chemistry-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by Gerhard H. Herzberg](work-by-gerhard-h-herzberg.md), [Work by Klaus P. Huber](work-by-klaus-p-huber.md)

By [Klaus P. Huber](klaus-p-huber.md) and [Gerhard H. Herzberg](gerhard-h-herzberg.md). NIST Chemistry WebBook compilation of diatomic constants.

[https://webbook.nist.gov/cgi/cbook.cgi?ID=C10034852&Mask=1000](https://webbook.nist.gov/cgi/cbook.cgi?ID=C10034852&Mask=1000)

Cited row: ground $X\,{}^1\Sigma^+$ state of [hydrogen iodide](hydrogen-iodide.md), $\omega_e=2309.014\ \mathrm{cm^{-1}}$ and $\omega_ex_e=39.6435\ \mathrm{cm^{-1}}$. Higher anharmonic coefficients are also tabulated, so the two-constant [Morse oscillator](morse-oscillator.md) extrapolation should not be confused with a measurement of the actual dissociation threshold.
