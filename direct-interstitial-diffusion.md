# Direct interstitial diffusion

↑ **Parent:** [Diffusion](diffusion.md)

[diffusion](diffusion.md) by hopping of an interstitial particle between interstitial sites without displacing a host lattice particle. Direction sums over neighbour vectors determine the [conductivity tensor](conductivity-tensor.md) in a dilute independent-jump model.
