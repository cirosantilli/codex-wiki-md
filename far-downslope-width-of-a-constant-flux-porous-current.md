# Far-downslope width of a constant-flux porous current

↑ **Parent:** [Depth-dependent inclined porous-current equation](depth-dependent-inclined-porous-current-equation.md)

In a slender steady [porous gravity current](porous-gravity-current.md), the dominant balance is $(h^\beta)_X=(h^\beta h_Y)_Y$. If its width and height scales are $W,H$, fixed downslope [volume flux](volumetric-flow-rate.md) gives $H^\beta W=\text{constant}$, and transverse spreading gives $W^2\sim HX$. Eliminating $H$ yields the displayed width exponent. The porosity exponent affects transient storage but not this steady balance.
