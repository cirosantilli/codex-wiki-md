# Brownian local time

↑ **Parent:** [Local time of a semimartingale](local-time-of-a-semimartingale.md)

For [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) with generator $\tfrac12\Delta$, its local time is the occupation density normalized by $\int_0^t h(B_s)ds=\int_{\mathbb R}h(a)L_t^a\,da$ for nonnegative Borel $h$. In the [Tanaka formula](tanaka-s-formula.md) it satisfies $|B_t-a|=|B_0-a|+\int_0^t\operatorname{sgn}(B_s-a)dB_s+L_t^a$. The symmetric-window approximation is $(2\varepsilon)^{-1}\int_0^t\mathbf1_{\{|B_s-a|<\varepsilon\}}ds$. The factor two is essential. [Occupation approximations for Brownian local time](occupation-approximations-for-brownian-local-time.md) give a direct derivation at level zero from smooth approximations and maximal martingale estimates.
