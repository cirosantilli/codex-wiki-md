# Trigonal planar molecular geometry

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular geometry](molecular-geometry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trigonal_planar_molecular_geometry)

A [molecular geometry](molecular-geometry.md) in which three attached [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) and their central atom lie in one plane. The ideal symmetric arrangement has three $120^\circ$ angles and [D3h molecular point group](d3h-molecular-point-group.md) symmetry, as in an isolated [boron trifluoride](boron-trifluoride.md) molecule.
