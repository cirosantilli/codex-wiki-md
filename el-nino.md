<h1 id="el-nino">El Niño</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [El Niño–Southern Oscillation](el-nino-southern-oscillation.md)

The warm phase of the coupled tropical Pacific [El Niño–Southern Oscillation](el-nino-southern-oscillation.md), with central and eastern equatorial Pacific surface warming and a weakened [Walker circulation](walker-circulation.md). Changes of [trade winds](trade-winds.md), [thermocline](thermocline.md) depth, and [equatorial Kelvin waves](equatorial-kelvin-wave.md) and [equatorial Rossby waves](equatorial-rossby-wave.md) participate in its evolution.
