# Equivalent sheared representations of the Klein four-group

↑ **Parent:** [Klein four-group](klein-four-group.md)

Let the [Klein four-group](klein-four-group.md) have generators $X,Y$ and $Z=XY$, with $X^2=Y^2=I$ and $XY=YX$. For every real $p$, the matrices

$$
D(X)=-I_2,\qquad D(Y)=\begin{pmatrix}-1&-p\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad D(Z)=-D(Y)
$$

define a [group representation](group-representation.md). Indeed, $D(Y)^2=I_2$, and $D(X)$ commutes with every matrix, so the group relations hold.

Set $S=\begin{pmatrix}1&-p/2\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$. A direct [change of basis](change-of-basis.md) gives

$$
S^{-1}D(Y)S=\operatorname{diag}(-1,1),\qquad S^{-1}D(Z)S=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1).
$$

Consequently all these [representations](group-representation.md) are [isomorphic representations](isomorphic-representations.md), with [character](character-of-a-representation.md) values $(2,-2,0,0)$ on $(I,X,Y,Z)$. Each is a [direct sum](direct-sum.md) of the two [one-dimensional representations](one-dimensional-representation.md) whose values on $X$ are $-1$ and whose values on $Y$ are $+1$ and $-1$. A [group representation](group-representation.md) need not be [orthogonal](orthogonal-vectors.md); nonzero $p$ changes the basis rather than the represented group.
