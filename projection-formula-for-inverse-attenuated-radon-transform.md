# Projection formula for inverse attenuated Radon transform

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral reconstruction of an attenuated Radon transform](spectral-reconstruction-of-an-attenuated-radon-transform.md)

Let $F(\rho,\tau,\theta)=\int_\tau^\infty f(s\boldsymbol d+\rho\boldsymbol n)ds$, $S=\mathcal Rf$, and $A=\int_{\mathbb R}e^{-F}g\,d\tau$ be the directed [attenuated Radon transform](attenuated-radon-transform.md). With the [signed Cauchy boundary operators](signed-cauchy-boundary-operators.md), set $J=e^F[e^{P^-S}P^-(e^{-P^-S}A)+e^{-P^+S}P^+(e^{P^+S}A)]$. An [integrating factor](integrating-factor.md) turns the attenuated equation into unattenuated directional transport; its spectral solution has jump $-J$ on the unit circle. A normalized [Cauchy integral formula](cauchy-integral-formula.md) and the coefficient at spectral infinity then prove the reconstruction formula. Known attenuation and full directed angular data determine the source; attenuation itself is not reconstructed by this pair.
