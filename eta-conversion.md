# Eta conversion

↑ **Parent:** [Untyped lambda calculus](untyped-lambda-calculus.md)

Eta conversion expresses extensionality for a [lambda abstraction](lambda-abstraction.md) whose fresh argument is only passed to a function. The side condition prevents removal of a genuine dependency. In a categorical model with $D\cong D^D$, the inverse laws for [currying](currying.md) and evaluation validate this conversion as well as [beta reduction](beta-reduction.md).
