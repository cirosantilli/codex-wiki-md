# Position-dependent resistive pulse

↑ **Parent:** [Resistive-pulse sensing](resistive-pulse-sensing.md)

A small insulating object of excluded [volume](volume.md) $\Omega$ perturbs an Ohmic pore most strongly where the pore cross-sectional [area](surface-area.md) $A$ is small. In a local weak-blockage model, replacing a small conducting [volume](volume.md) gives the displayed resistance scaling, with a shape-dependent coefficient. At fixed voltage, $\Delta I\simeq-I_0\Delta\mathcal R/\mathcal R_0$. A symmetric pore with narrow ends and a wide middle can therefore resolve two end features in a passage pulse. This is a conditional volume-exclusion model: charged-particle ion transport, surface interactions and bandwidth can change the observed sign, amplitude and shape.
