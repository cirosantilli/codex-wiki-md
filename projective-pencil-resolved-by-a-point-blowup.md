# Projective pencil resolved by a point blowup

↑ **Parent:** [Blowup of a complex manifold at a point](blowup-of-a-complex-manifold-at-a-point.md)

The [holomorphic map](holomorphic-map.md) $[Z_0:Z_1:Z_2]\mapsto[Z_1:Z_2]$ on $\mathbb{CP}^2\setminus\{[1:0:0]\}$ records the line through the missing point and the input point. Its graph closure is the incidence surface $Z_1b=Z_2a$ in $\mathbb{CP}^2\times\mathbb{CP}^1$, the [blowup of a complex manifold at a point](blowup-of-a-complex-manifold-at-a-point.md). Projection to $[a:b]$ extends the pencil holomorphically. On the [exceptional divisor](exceptional-divisor.md) it is the identity map on the projective tangent directions.
