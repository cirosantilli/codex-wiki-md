# Sodium iodide iodine-131

↑ **Parent:** [Radiopharmaceutical](radiopharmaceutical.md)

Radioiodide used in selected thyroid treatments because iodine uptake localizes activity in thyroid tissue. [Iodine-131](iodine-131.md) supplies local beta radiation and imageable gamma emission.
