# Topology axiom

↑ **Parent:** [Topological space](topological-space.md)

A family $\mathcal T$ of [subsets](subset.md) of a [set](set-split.md) $X$ is a topology when $\varnothing,X\in\mathcal T$, every [union](set-union.md) of members of $\mathcal T$ belongs to $\mathcal T$, and every finite [intersection](set-intersection.md) of members of $\mathcal T$ belongs to $\mathcal T$.
