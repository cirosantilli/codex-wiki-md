# Resonant topographic quasi-geostrophic wave

↑ **Parent:** [Quasi-geostrophic mountain wave](quasi-geostrophic-mountain-wave.md)

A stationary boundary corrugation can resonate with a [Rossby wave](rossby-wave.md) whose intrinsic propagation cancels the background current. For constant $N$, rigid upper height $H$, and $m=\pi/H$, define $\kappa^2=k^2+\ell^2+f_0^2m^2/N^2$. When $U=\beta/\kappa^2$, a lower corrugation proportional to $e^{ikx}\sin(\ell y)$ admits a disturbance proportional to $e^{ikx}\sin(\ell y)[Bt\cos(mz)+C(z-H)\sin(mz)]$, with $B=2ikf_0U^2/\beta$ and $C=N^2/(f_0m)$. Its [wave activity](wave-activity.md) grows quadratically with time while the upward [Eliassen–Palm flux](eliassen-palm-flux.md) grows linearly. The linear prediction applies only while the disturbance remains small.
