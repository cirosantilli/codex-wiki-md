# Halo-to-galaxy luminosity mapping

↑ **Parent:** [Baryon conversion efficiency of a halo](baryon-conversion-efficiency-of-a-halo.md)

A monotonic luminosity assignment to a counted halo population converts its differential abundance per mass into a [luminosity function](luminosity-function-astronomy.md) by a change of variable. If $n_h\propto M_h^{-a}$ and $L\propto M_h^p$, then $\Phi\propto L^{-1-(a-1)/p}$. This simple mapping assumes one counted galaxy per halo, no scatter and a fixed selection. A mass-dependent [baryon conversion efficiency of a halo](baryon-conversion-efficiency-of-a-halo.md), satellites and stellar-population differences modify it.
