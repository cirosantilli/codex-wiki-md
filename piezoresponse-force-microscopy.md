# Piezoresponse force microscopy

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic force microscopy](atomic-force-microscopy.md)

A contact measurement of electrically driven local surface displacement, detected by cantilever bending or torsion and often demodulated by a [lock-in amplifier](lock-in-amplifier.md). Electrostatic and contact-mechanical cross-talk must be separated from [converse piezoelectric effect](converse-piezoelectric-effect.md) response.
