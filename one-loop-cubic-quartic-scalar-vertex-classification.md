# One-loop cubic-quartic scalar vertex classification

↑ **Parent:** [One-particle-irreducible Feynman diagram](one-particle-irreducible-feynman-diagram.md)

For a connected real-scalar [Feynman diagram](feynman-diagram.md), degree counting and $L=I-V+1$ give $V_3+2V_4=E+2L-2$. A one-loop [one-particle-irreducible Feynman diagram](one-particle-irreducible-feynman-diagram.md) is a cycle: every vertex has two internal half-edges. For three labelled external legs, the possibilities are one cubic triangle with [symmetry factor](feynman-diagram-symmetry-factor.md) one and three mixed cubic-quartic bubbles with [symmetry factor](feynman-diagram-symmetry-factor.md) two. For four labelled external legs, there are three quartic bubbles with [symmetry factor](feynman-diagram-symmetry-factor.md) two, six mixed triangles with [symmetry factor](feynman-diagram-symmetry-factor.md) one, and three cubic boxes with [symmetry factor](feynman-diagram-symmetry-factor.md) one. Cyclic ordering modulo reversal explains the three boxes.
