# Cotunnelling

↑ **Parent:** [Coulomb blockade](coulomb-blockade.md)

A higher-order tunnelling process through intermediate virtual charge states. It can produce transport inside a region where sequential first-order tunnelling is suppressed by [Coulomb blockade](coulomb-blockade.md). Its relevance grows as junction coupling increases.
