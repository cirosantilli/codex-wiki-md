# Lattice-periodic plane wave

↑ **Parent:** [Reciprocal lattice](reciprocal-lattice.md)

A [plane wave](plane-wave.md) whose phase is unchanged by every direct lattice translation: $e^{i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf R}=1$ for all $\mathbf R\in\Lambda$. Its [wavevector](wavevector.md) is a [reciprocal lattice vector](reciprocal-lattice-vector.md). In one dimension $ka=2\pi m$.
