# Polymer brush

↑ **Parent:** [Grafted polymer chain](grafted-polymer-chain.md)

A layer of sufficiently crowded tethered [polymer](polymer-split.md) chains stretched away from their attachment surface. In a [good solvent](good-solvent.md), overlap increases the interaction contribution to [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md); extension relieves this crowding at a cost of chain [entropy](entropy-split.md). The mushroom-to-brush crossover occurs at $\sigma_gR^2\sim1$, where $R$ is an isolated coil size. In a simple good-solvent scaling model, the brush height is $H\sim Nb(v\sigma_g/b)^{1/3}$ for effective segment [excluded volume](excluded-volume.md) $v$ and statistical segment length $b$; if $v\sim b^3$, this becomes $H\sim Nb(\sigma_gb^2)^{1/3}$. The scaling requires strong stretching relative to the isolated coil but extension well below the contour length $Nb$.
