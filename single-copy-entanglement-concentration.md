# Single-copy entanglement concentration

↑ **Parent:** [Entanglement concentration](entanglement-concentration.md)

For one pure rank-two entangled state with descending squared [Schmidt coefficients](schmidt-coefficient.md) $(\lambda_0,\lambda_1)$, the success operator $F=\operatorname{diag}(\sqrt{\lambda_1/\lambda_0},1)$ gives a [Bell pair](bell-pair.md) with probability $2\lambda_1$. The complementary operator $\sqrt{I-F^\dagger F}$ makes a valid [quantum instrument](quantum-instrument.md). Optimality follows from the [Schmidt-tail entanglement monotone](schmidt-tail-entanglement-monotone.md): a successful Bell branch has smallest weight $1/2$, so its probability cannot exceed $2\lambda_1$. Failure branches have nonnegative monotone and cannot loosen this bound.
