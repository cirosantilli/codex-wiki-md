# Core and power-law elliptical galaxies

↑ **Parent:** [Elliptical galaxy](elliptical-galaxy.md)

Bright [elliptical galaxies](elliptical-galaxy.md) commonly have a shallow central surface-brightness cusp inside a break radius, whereas many less luminous [elliptical galaxies](elliptical-galaxy.md) have a steep cusp continuing inward. The latter can have many more luminous tracers at a fixed small radius, helping central [black hole](black-hole.md) dynamical measurements. This empirical distinction is not a universal classification by mass alone. [Measurements of central galaxy profiles](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/9610055) quantify the contrast.
