# Half-wave plate

↑ **Parent:** [Waveplate](waveplate.md)

A [half-wave plate](half-wave-plate.md) reverses the relative sign of the two components. For initial [linear polarization](linear-polarization.md) at [angle](angle.md) $\alpha$ to the fast axis, the outgoing line is at $-\alpha$ modulo $\pi$. The $45^\circ$ input therefore rotates by $90^\circ$.
