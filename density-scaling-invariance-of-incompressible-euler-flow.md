# Density scaling invariance of incompressible Euler flow

↑ **Parent:** [Euler equations for an inviscid fluid](euler-equations-for-an-inviscid-fluid-split.md)

A homogeneous fluid in a prescribed gravitational field obeys $D\mathbf u/Dt=-\nabla(p/\rho)+\mathbf g$ and $\nabla\cdot\mathbf u=0$. Multiplying both constant [mass density](density.md) and [pressure](pressure-split.md) by the same positive factor preserves the [velocity](velocity.md) solution, provided initial and boundary data remain compatible. Thus velocity-only observations do not determine density in this model. Independently prescribed pressure gradients or the fluid's own gravity can break this invariance.
