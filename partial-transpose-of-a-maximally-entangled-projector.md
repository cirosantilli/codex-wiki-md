# Partial transpose of a maximally entangled projector

↑ **Parent:** [Partial transpose](partial-transpose.md)

For $|\Phi_d\rangle=d^{-1/2}\sum_j|jj\rangle$, the [partial transpose](partial-transpose.md) of its [pure state](pure-state.md) projector is $S/d$, where $S$ is the [swap operator](swap-operator.md). Expand in matrix units: $|i\rangle\langle j|\otimes|i\rangle\langle j|$ becomes $|i\rangle\langle j|\otimes|j\rangle\langle i|$.
