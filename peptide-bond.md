# Peptide bond

↑ **Parent:** [Polypeptide](polypeptide.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peptide_bond)

A [peptide bond](peptide-bond.md) is the [covalent bond](covalent-bond.md) between the carboxyl [carbon](carbon.md) of one [amino acid residue](amino-acid-residue.md) and the amino [nitrogen](nitrogen.md) of the next. The displayed group shows the usual linkage, with the central carbon–nitrogen connection being the [peptide bond](peptide-bond.md). In an intact unbranched chain of $n$ [amino acid residues](amino-acid-residue.md), adjacent positions supply $n-1$ [peptide bonds](peptide-bond.md). Cutting one internal [peptide bond](peptide-bond.md) produces two fragments; merely changing the chain's shape need not cut it. See [Clark, Douglas and Choi, Biology 2e: peptide bonds and protein structure](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/3-4-proteins).
