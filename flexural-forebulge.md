# Flexural forebulge

↑ **Parent:** [Lithospheric flexure](lithospheric-flexure.md)

A region of upward deflection beyond the main downward flexure caused by loading an [elastic lithosphere](elastic-lithosphere.md). Its position and amplitude depend on [flexural rigidity](bending-stiffness.md), load geometry and boundary conditions. It is regional bending of the plate, rather than a separate buoyant load.
