# Moment determinacy

↑ **Parent:** [Moment](moment.md)

A [probability distribution](probability-distribution.md) is moment-determinate if its moments uniquely specify it among [probability distributions](probability-distribution.md). A [moment-generating function](moment-generating-function.md) finite in a neighbourhood of zero is a sufficient condition. The [standard normal distribution](standard-normal-distribution.md) is moment-determinate.
