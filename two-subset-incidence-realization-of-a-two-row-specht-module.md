# Two-subset incidence realization of a two-row Specht module

↑ **Parent:** [Specht module](specht-module.md)

Over a [field](field.md) of characteristic zero with $n\ge4$, let $V_2$ be the permutation representation on unordered two-element subsets and $V_1$ the representation on singletons. The equivariant incidence map $D:V_2\to V_1$ sends $e_{ij}$ to $e_i+e_j$. It is surjective: if coefficients $c_i$ annihilate its image, then $c_i+c_j=0$ for all distinct $i,j$, forcing all coefficients to vanish. Thus its kernel has dimension $\binom n2-n$.

A polytabloid of shape $(n-2,2)$, identified by its bottom-row pair, is an alternating four-edge vector $e_{cd}-e_{ad}-e_{cb}+e_{ab}$. Its incidence is zero. The [hook-length formula](hook-length-formula.md) gives the same dimension $\binom n2-n$ for this [Specht module](specht-module.md), proving the identity. [Maschke's theorem](maschke-s-theorem.md) yields $V_2\cong S^{(n-2,2)}\oplus V_1$.

For a Young subgroup with block sizes $\mu_1,\ldots,\mu_l$, there are $l$ singleton orbits and $\binom l2+\#\{i:\mu_i\ge2\}$ pair orbits. Taking fixed vectors and using [Frobenius reciprocity](frobenius-reciprocity.md) therefore gives the multiplicity of $S^{(n-2,2)}$ in $M^\mu$ as $\binom l2+\#\{i:\mu_i\ge2\}-l$. This derives the multiplicity without [Young's rule](young-s-rule.md); block sizes $(3,2,1)$ give exactly two.
