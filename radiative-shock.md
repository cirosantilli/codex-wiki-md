# Radiative shock

↑ **Parent:** [Shock wave](shock-wave.md)

A [shock wave](shock-wave.md) with significant radiation losses in its downstream evolution. Often a narrow dissipative jump initially heats the gas according to adiabatic [Rankine-Hugoniot conditions](rankine-hugoniot-conditions.md), followed by a much longer [radiative cooling](radiative-cooling.md) layer. The gas's [specific entropy](specific-entropy.md) increases at the jump and may then fall as radiation carries [entropy](entropy-split.md) away. If both distant states have equal [temperature](temperature-split.md), the combined [mass](mass.md) and [momentum](momentum-split.md) balances give an [isothermal shock](isothermal-shock.md); the gas's [energy](energy-split.md) flux is not conserved across the whole radiating region.
