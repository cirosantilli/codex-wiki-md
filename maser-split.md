# Maser

↑ **Parent:** [Optics](optics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maser)

A maser uses [stimulated emission](stimulated-emission.md) to amplify [microwaves](microwave.md). With [population inversion](population-inversion.md) and feedback it can sustain a [coherent wave field](coherent-wave-field.md) as an [oscillator](oscillator.md). The name abbreviates "microwave amplification by stimulated emission of radiation".[pp. 60–62, Basic maser principles](production-of-coherent-radiation-by-atoms-and-molecules-by-charles-hard-townes.md)

Why make [molecules](molecule-split.md) supply an [oscillator](oscillator.md)? Their [energy levels](energy-level.md) offer a reproducible [frequency](frequency.md) reference, while [stimulated emission](stimulated-emission.md) lets successive [molecules](molecule-split.md) reinforce one [coherent wave field](coherent-wave-field.md). In his 1964 lecture, [Charles Hard Townes](charles-hard-townes.md) traced his motivation to [microwave spectroscopy](microwave-spectroscopy.md): resolving molecular structure required useful coherent sources.[p. 58, opening discussion of microwave spectroscopy](production-of-coherent-radiation-by-atoms-and-molecules-by-charles-hard-townes.md) The interesting balance is between molecular amplification and escaping [energy](energy-split.md): even a beam with [population inversion](population-inversion.md) fails to oscillate if cavity losses win. This is the microwave counterpart of [laser threshold](laser-threshold.md).
