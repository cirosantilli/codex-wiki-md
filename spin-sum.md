# Spin sum

↑ **Parent:** [Dirac field](dirac-field.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spin_sum)

A spin sum adds a squared [scattering amplitude](scattering-amplitude.md) over the unobserved [spins](spin.md) of external particles. For an on-shell [Dirac spinor](dirac-spinor.md), the completeness relations $\sum_s u_s(p)\bar u_s(p)=\not p+m$ and $\sum_s v_s(p)\bar v_s(p)=\not p-m$ turn the sum into a [gamma-matrix trace](gamma-matrix-trace.md).
