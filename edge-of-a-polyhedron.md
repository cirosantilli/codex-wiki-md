# Edge of a polyhedron

↑ **Parent:** [Polyhedron](polyhedron.md)

A line segment along which two faces of a [polyhedron](polyhedron.md) meet. A [rectangular parallelepiped](rectangular-parallelepiped.md) has twelve edges, four parallel to each of its three side directions.
