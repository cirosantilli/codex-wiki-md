# Affine maps can collapse lines

↑ **Parent:** [Affine map](affine-map.md)

An [affine map](affine-map.md) need not be [injective](injective-function.md). For $G(x,y)=(x+y,0)$, the direction $(1,-1)$ belongs to the [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) of its [linear part of an affine map](linear-part-of-an-affine-map.md). Consequently the whole [line](straight-line.md) $y=-x$ maps to $(0,0)$, and every parallel [line](straight-line.md) $y=-x+c$ maps to $(c,0)$. These are collapsed lines, not images with a surviving direction. Assertions that an [affine map](affine-map.md) preserves distinct parallel lines require the appropriate noncollapse or [injectivity](injective-function.md) hypothesis.
