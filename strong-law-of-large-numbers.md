# Strong law of large numbers

↑ **Parent:** [Almost sure convergence](almost-sure-convergence.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strong_law_of_large_numbers)

If $(X_n)$ are [independent and identically distributed random variables](independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md) with $\mathbb E|X_1|<\infty$, then

$$
\frac1n\sum_{j=1}^nX_j\longrightarrow\mathbb E[X_1]
$$

almost surely.
