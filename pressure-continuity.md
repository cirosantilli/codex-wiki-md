# Pressure continuity

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamic boundary condition for an inviscid interface](dynamic-boundary-condition-for-an-inviscid-interface.md)

For an inviscid interface with no [surface tension](surface-tension.md), continuity of normal [traction](traction.md) reduces to continuity of [pressure](pressure.md). The same condition is used in a sharp [Darcy law](darcy-law.md) interface model that neglects [capillary pressure](capillary-pressure.md) and resolved interfacial viscous normal [stress](stress.md). For a general viscous interface, continuity of normal [traction](traction.md) balances the full [stress](stress.md) [tensor](tensor.md); it does not by itself imply continuity of [pressure](pressure.md).
