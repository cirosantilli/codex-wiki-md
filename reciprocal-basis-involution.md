# Reciprocal basis involution

↑ **Parent:** [Reciprocal basis](reciprocal-basis.md)

For a three-dimensional [basis](basis.md) with nonzero [scalar triple product](scalar-triple-product.md) $\Delta$, its [reciprocal basis](reciprocal-basis.md) satisfies $\widehat e_2\times\widehat e_3=e_1/\Delta$ and the cyclic analogues. Consequently its [scalar triple product](scalar-triple-product.md) is $1/\Delta$, and taking the [reciprocal basis](reciprocal-basis.md) again recovers the original [basis](basis.md). The coordinates of $V$ in the original [basis](basis.md) are $V\cdot\widehat e_i$; in the [reciprocal basis](reciprocal-basis.md) they are $V\cdot e_i$.
