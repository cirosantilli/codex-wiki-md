# Elastic-cover frequency ratio for water waves

↑ **Parent:** [Flexural-gravity wave](flexural-gravity-wave.md)

In the specified thin-sheet deep-water model, bending stiffness adds restoring force while sheet [mass](mass.md) adds inertia. Their ratio to an uncovered [gravity-capillary wave](gravity-capillary-wave.md) gives the displayed expression. Added inertia first lowers [frequency](frequency.md) at small [wavenumber](wavenumber.md); at formally large [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) bending produces a [frequency](frequency.md) ratio proportional to $k^{1/2}$. That formal asymptote must not be extrapolated past the range where a thin-sheet continuum description is valid.
