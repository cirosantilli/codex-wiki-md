# Killing energy with positive time signature

↑ **Parent:** [Killing energy](killing-energy.md)

With [metric signature](metric-signature.md) $(+---)$ and a future timelike stationary [Killing vector](killing-vector-field-split.md) $K$, positive [energy](energy-split.md) is $E=g(P,K)$ for future physical momentum $P$. A common $(-+++)$ convention instead uses $-g(P,K)$. The normalization of $K$ fixes the observer or asymptotic time scale.
