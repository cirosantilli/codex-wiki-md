<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [uniformization theorem](../../../../uniformization-theorem.md) says that every [simply connected](../../../../simply-connected-space.md) [Riemann surface](../../../../riemann-surfaces-split.md) is [biholomorphic](../../../../biholomorphism.md) to the [Riemann sphere](../../../../riemann-sphere.md), the [complex plane](../../../../complex-plane.md), or the [unit disc](../../../../unit-disc.md). Its consequence for a [compact Riemann surface](../../../../compact-riemann-surface.md) of [genus](../../../../genus-of-a-surface.md) at least two is that the [universal cover](../../../../universal-cover.md) is the [unit disc](../../../../unit-disc.md), equivalently the [hyperbolic plane](../../../../hyperbolic-plane.md).

To explain the restriction, a spherical [universal cover](../../../../universal-cover.md) would make the [fundamental group](../../../../fundamental-group.md) finite. Since a finite [covering map](../../../../covering-space.md) multiplies [Euler characteristic](../../../../euler-characteristic.md), $2=m(2-2g)$ is impossible for $g\geq2$. If the [universal cover](../../../../universal-cover.md) were the [complex plane](../../../../complex-plane.md), its [deck transformations](../../../../deck-transformation.md) would be [biholomorphisms](../../../../biholomorphism.md) $z\mapsto az+b$. A nonidentity such map with $a\ne1$ has a fixed point, impossible for a [deck transformation](../../../../deck-transformation.md). Thus the [deck transformation group](../../../../deck-transformation-group.md) would consist of translations. A [discrete subgroup](../../../../discrete-subgroup.md) of translations with [compact](../../../../compact-space.md) [quotient space](../../../../quotient-topological-space.md) is a rank-two [Euclidean lattice](../../../../euclidean-lattice.md), whose [quotient space](../../../../quotient-topological-space.md) is a [torus](../../../../torus.md), again incompatible with $g\geq2$.

On the [unit disc](../../../../unit-disc.md), the [Poincare metric on a Riemann surface](../../../../poincare-metric-on-a-riemann-surface.md)

$$
ds^2=\frac{4|dz|^2}{(1-|z|^2)^2}
$$

has [Gaussian curvature](../../../../gaussian-curvature.md) $-1$ and is invariant under every [biholomorphism](../../../../biholomorphism.md) of the [unit disc](../../../../unit-disc.md). It therefore descends through the [deck transformation group](../../../../deck-transformation-group.md) to a [hyperbolic metric](../../../../hyperbolic-metric.md) on $X$. Its local expression is a positive multiple of $|dz|^2$ in every holomorphic [coordinate chart](../../../../manifold-chart.md), so it is compatible with the given [complex structure](../../../../complex-structure.md). In fact, this [hyperbolic metric](../../../../hyperbolic-metric.md) is unique within that [conformal class](../../../../conformal-class-of-a-riemannian-metric.md).
