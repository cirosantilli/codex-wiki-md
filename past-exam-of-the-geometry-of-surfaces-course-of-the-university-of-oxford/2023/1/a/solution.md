<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [classification of compact surfaces](../../../../classification-of-compact-surfaces.md) says that every [compact](../../../../compact-space.md), [connected](../../../../connected-space.md) [topological surface](../../../../topological-surface-split.md) without [boundary](../../../../boundary-of-a-set.md) is [homeomorphic](../../../../homeomorphism.md) to exactly one of the following: the [sphere](../../../../sphere.md) $S^2$; a [connected sum of surfaces](../../../../connected-sum-of-surfaces.md) consisting of $g\geq1$ [tori](../../../../torus.md); or a [connected sum of surfaces](../../../../connected-sum-of-surfaces.md) consisting of $k\geq1$ [real projective planes](../../../../real-projective-plane.md). The first two cases are [orientable](../../../../orientable-surface.md); the last case is a [nonorientable surface](../../../../non-orientable-surface.md). Their [Euler characteristics](../../../../euler-characteristic.md) are

$$
\boxed{\chi(S^2)=2,\qquad\chi(\Sigma_g)=2-2g,\qquad\chi(N_k)=2-k.}
$$

Here $\Sigma_g$ has [genus](../../../../genus-of-a-surface.md) $g$, and $N_k$ has $k$ crosscaps; $N_1$ is the [real projective plane](../../../../real-projective-plane.md) and $N_2$ the [Klein bottle](../../../../klein-bottle.md). Thus [orientability](../../../../orientability.md) together with the [Euler characteristic](../../../../euler-characteristic.md) determines the closed [surface](../../../../topological-surface-split.md) up to [homeomorphism](../../../../homeomorphism.md).

If the convention permits a [surface with boundary](../../../../surface-with-boundary.md), also remove the interiors of $b\geq0$ disjoint [closed discs](../../../../closed-disc.md) from one of these closed [surfaces](../../../../topological-surface-split.md). The [Euler characteristic](../../../../euler-characteristic.md) decreases by $b$, giving $2-2g-b$ in the [orientable](../../../../orientable-surface.md) case and $2-k-b$ in the [nonorientable surface](../../../../non-orientable-surface.md) case. The number of [boundary](../../../../boundary-of-a-set.md) components is then an additional invariant.
