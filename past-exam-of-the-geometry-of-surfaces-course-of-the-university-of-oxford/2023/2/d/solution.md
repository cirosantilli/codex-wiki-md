<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For a [free action of a group](../../../../free-action-of-a-group.md), every [stabilizer subgroup](../../../../stabilizer-subgroup.md) is trivial. Hence every [ramification index of a holomorphic map](../../../../ramification-index-of-a-holomorphic-map.md) is one, and the projection is an unramified [covering map](../../../../covering-space.md) of [degree of a holomorphic map](../../../../degree-of-a-holomorphic-map.md) $|G|$. The [Riemann-Hurwitz formula](../../../../riemann-hurwitz-formula.md) reduces to

$$
\boxed{2g-2=|G|(2h-2),\qquad g-1=|G|(h-1).}
$$

This is also the multiplicativity of [Euler characteristic under a finite covering](../../../../euler-characteristic-under-a-finite-covering.md).
