<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

In a [cyclic group](../../../../cyclic-group.md) of [prime](../../../../prime-number-split.md) order $p$, a [stabilizer subgroup](../../../../stabilizer-subgroup.md) has order either one or $p$. The latter case consists exactly of points fixed by all of $G$. Thus the $n$ fixed points each have [ramification index of a holomorphic map](../../../../ramification-index-of-a-holomorphic-map.md) $p$, and every other point has index one. Applying the [Riemann-Hurwitz formula](../../../../riemann-hurwitz-formula.md) with [degree of a holomorphic map](../../../../degree-of-a-holomorphic-map.md) $p$ gives

$$
\boxed{2g-2=p(2h-2)+n(p-1).}
$$

Equivalently,

$$
\boxed{g=ph+\frac{(p-1)(n-2)}2.}
$$

The count $n$ concerns fixed points upstairs. Each is its own [group orbit](../../../../orbit-group-theory.md), so their images are also $n$ distinct [branch values of a holomorphic map](../../../../branch-value-of-a-holomorphic-map.md).
