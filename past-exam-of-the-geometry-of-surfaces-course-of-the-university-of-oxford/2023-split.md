# 2023

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the geometry of surfaces course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Original solutions to all three questions of the [Geometry of surfaces course of the University of Oxford](../geometry-of-surfaces-course-of-the-university-of-oxford.md) Part B examination, held on 7 June 2023. Source: [released examination PDF, bundle pages 214–217; paper pages 1–4](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/request_for_mathematics_examinat/response/3129786/attach/3/FOI%20202507%20740%20Data.pdf#page=214). The numbering follows the original paper. In 3(e), the displayed identity requires integration with multiplicity when the parameter interval wraps around the [surface](../topological-surface-split.md); the solution explains this omission in the native PDF.
