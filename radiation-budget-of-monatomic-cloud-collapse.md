# Radiation budget of monatomic cloud collapse

↑ **Parent:** [Virial theorem](virial-theorem.md)

For a hydrostatic monatomic [ideal gas](ideal-gas-split.md) with negligible external [pressure](pressure-split.md), $2U+\Omega_f=0$, where [gravitational potential energy](gravitational-energy.md) is negative. The final total [energy](energy-split.md) is therefore $\Omega_f/2$, giving the displayed radiated [energy](energy-split.md) when no [mass](mass.md) or other [energy](energy-split.md) carrier escapes. The common $-\Omega_f/2$ result additionally takes the initial total [energy](energy-split.md) to be negligible. Initial rest does not remove initial self-gravitational [binding energy](binding-energy.md).
