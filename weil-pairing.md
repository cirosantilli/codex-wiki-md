# Weil pairing

↑ **Parent:** [Torsion point of an elliptic curve](torsion-point-of-an-elliptic-curve.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weil_pairing)

The Weil pairing is a bilinear, alternating, nondegenerate pairing

$$
e_n:E[n]\times E[n]\longrightarrow\mu_n.
$$

For an isogeny $\phi$ and its dual, it satisfies $e_n(\phi P,Q)=e_n(P,\widehat\phi Q)$ whenever both sides are defined.
