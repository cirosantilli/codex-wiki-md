# Probabilistic phase-gate injection

↑ **Parent:** [Phase gate](phase-gate.md)

The magic state $|A_\theta\rangle=(|0\rangle+e^{i\theta}|1\rangle)/\sqrt2$ and a short [controlled-NOT gate](controlled-not-gate.md) circuit can apply either $P(\theta)$ or $P(-\theta)$ to an input, with a measured bit identifying the branch.
