# Weak convergence in a Hilbert space

↑ **Parent:** [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md)

A sequence $x_n$ converges weakly to $x$ when $\langle x_n,y\rangle\to\langle x,y\rangle$ for every $y$ in the Hilbert space.
