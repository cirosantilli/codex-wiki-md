# Credit tranche loss function

↑ **Parent:** [Synthetic CDO](synthetic-cdo.md)

For aggregate monetary loss $L$ and monetary attachment/exhaustion levels $A<D$, this is the loss allocated to the tranche. It is zero until attachment, rises with losses between $A$ and $D$, and is capped at $D-A$. Expected tranche losses and surviving tranche notional determine protection and premium legs. For basket products whose payoff occurs at a first [default](credit-default.md), the joint default-time distribution matters as well as the final loss distribution.
