<h1 id="schatten-norm-holder-inequality">Schatten norm Hölder inequality</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Trace-class operator](trace-class-operator.md)

The Schatten norm Hölder inequality gives $\lVert AB\rVert_r\leq\lVert A\rVert_p\lVert B\rVert_q$ when $1/r=1/p+1/q$. In particular, the product of two [Hilbert-Schmidt operators](hilbert-schmidt-operator.md) is trace class and

$$
\lVert AB\rVert_1\leq\lVert A\rVert_{\mathrm{HS}}\lVert B\rVert_{\mathrm{HS}}.
$$
