# Equal-expansion curvature overdensity

↑ **Parent:** [Linear cosmological density perturbation](linear-cosmological-density-perturbation-split.md)

Compare a flat background [Friedmann equation](friedmann-equations.md) with a nearly homogeneous curved region on a slicing of equal local [Hubble parameter](hubble-parameter.md). To first order in its curvature parameter $K$, subtraction gives $\delta\rho=3K/(8\pi Ga^2)$, or $\delta=K/(a^2H^2)$. Conserved curvature therefore gives $\delta\propto a^2$ in [radiation domination](radiation-domination.md) and $\delta\propto a$ in [matter domination](matter-domination.md). This construction specifies the slicing: two patches at equal proper time generally do not have the same [Hubble parameter](hubble-parameter.md). For growing dust perturbations, the [continuity equation](continuity-equation.md) gives $H_s-H=-\dot\delta/3$ to first order. These different time comparisons can share growth powers without sharing the same curvature-to-density coefficient.
