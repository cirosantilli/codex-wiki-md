# Flat direction of a scalar potential

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar potential](scalar-potential.md)

In vacuum analysis, an exact [flat direction of a scalar potential](flat-direction-of-a-scalar-potential.md) is a nonconstant continuous family of degenerate stationary vacua. The [scalar potential](scalar-potential.md) stays constant along this family and its [gradient](gradient.md) vanishes there. An ordinary level set does not suffice: $V(x,y)=x^2+y^2$ is constant on every circle, but its vacuum at the origin has no flat direction. A vanishing [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) of the quadratic [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) also does not suffice: $V(x)=x^4$ has zero quadratic mass at zero but no family of nearby vacua. Exact flat directions can be lifted by corrections absent from the classical [scalar potential](scalar-potential.md).
