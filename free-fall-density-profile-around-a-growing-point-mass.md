# Free-fall density profile around a growing point mass

↑ **Parent:** [Inside-out collapse of a singular isothermal sphere](inside-out-collapse-of-a-singular-isothermal-sphere.md)

Where a central [mass](mass.md) dominates and radial infall is locally steady, $|v_r|\simeq\sqrt{2GM_\star/r}$. A conserved [mass](mass.md) flux $\dot M=4\pi r^2\rho|v_r|$ then gives $\rho\simeq\dot M[4\pi\sqrt{2GM_\star}]^{-1}r^{-3/2}$. Constant accretion gives $M_\star\propto t$ and an inner normalization proportional to $t^{-1/2}$.
