# Pi backbonding

↑ **Parent:** [Coordinate covalent bond](coordinate-covalent-bond.md)

[Pi backbonding](pi-backbonding.md) is donation from occupied metal orbitals into ligand acceptor orbitals with pi [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md) about the metal–ligand axis. In a [carbonyl ligand](carbonyl-ligand.md), occupation of CO $\pi^*$ weakens C–O while strengthening metal–[carbon](carbon.md) bonding. Lower CO stretching [spectroscopic wavenumber](spectroscopic-wavenumber.md) is therefore a useful comparative probe, with electrostatic and mode-coupling qualifications.
