# Equal-endpoint Euclidean oscillator path

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean path integral](euclidean-path-integral.md)

For positive [mass](mass.md) $m$ and [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) $\omega$, the [Euclidean action](euclidean-action.md) $S_E=\tfrac m2\int_0^{\mathcal T}(\dot x^2+\omega^2x^2)\,d\tau$ with $x(0)=x(\mathcal T)=x$ has the unique minimizing path displayed. The [Euler-Lagrange equation](euler-lagrange-equation.md) is $\ddot x_0=\omega^2x_0$, and the positive [second variation](second-variation.md) proves uniqueness. On this path the integrand is $\tfrac m2\,d(x_0\dot x_0)/d\tau$, giving $S_E[x_0]=m\omega x^2\tanh(\omega\mathcal T/2)$. Increasing the interval of [imaginary time](imaginary-time.md) lets its interior approach the minimum of the [potential energy](potential-energy.md) even while both endpoints remain fixed.
