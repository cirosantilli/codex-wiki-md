# Schauder fixed-point theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Fixed-point theorem](fixed-point-theorem.md)

Every [continuous map](continuous-map.md) from a nonempty compact [convex set](convex-set-split.md) $K$ in a [Banach space](banach-space-split.md) to itself has a [fixed point](fixed-point.md). A useful equivalent form takes a nonempty closed bounded [convex set](convex-set-split.md) $C$ and a [continuous map](continuous-map.md) $T:C\to C$ whose image is [relatively compact](relatively-compact-subset.md). To obtain this version from the compact-domain version, restrict $T$ to $\overline{\operatorname{conv}}T(C)\subset C$ and use [closed convex hull of a compact set](closed-convex-hull-of-a-compact-set.md).

For a proof on $K$, the [Schauder approximation lemma](schauder-approximation-lemma.md) gives [continuous maps](continuous-map.md) $T_\varepsilon:K\to K\cap L_\varepsilon$ with finite-dimensional $L_\varepsilon$ and $\|T-T_\varepsilon\|_\infty\leq\varepsilon$. The [Brouwer fixed-point theorem](brouwer-fixed-point-theorem.md) on the nonempty compact [convex set](convex-set-split.md) $K\cap L_\varepsilon$ gives $x_\varepsilon=T_\varepsilon x_\varepsilon$, so $\|Tx_\varepsilon-x_\varepsilon\|\leq\varepsilon$. A [convergent subsequence](convergent-subsequence.md) as $\varepsilon\downarrow0$ and the [continuity](continuous-function.md) of $T$ give an exact [fixed point](fixed-point.md).
