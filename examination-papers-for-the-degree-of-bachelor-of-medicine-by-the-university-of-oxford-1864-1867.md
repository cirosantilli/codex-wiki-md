<h1 id="examination-papers-for-the-degree-of-bachelor-of-medicine-by-the-university-of-oxford-1864-1867">Examination papers for the Degree of Bachelor of Medicine by the University of Oxford (1864–1867)</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Biology bibliography](biology-bibliography-split.md)

A digitized volume of historical [University of Oxford](university-of-oxford-split.md) examinations. The First and Second Examination paper labels restart each year. Only the month and year are printed on the 1867 papers; page locators here refer to the PDF volume, not printed page numbers.

Original scan: [https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/3e/Examination_papers_for_the_Degree_of_Bachelor_of_medicine_-_1864-67_(IA_b24757810).pdf](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/3e/Examination_papers_for_the_Degree_of_Bachelor_of_medicine_-_1864-67_(IA_b24757810).pdf)

Catalogue: [https://wellcomecollection.org/works/d7b9zgnx](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/d7b9zgnx)

Wellcome Collection marks this digitized volume with the Public Domain Mark. The original was supplied by Royal College of Physicians, London. The original questions can therefore accompany the solutions here.[Licence and re-use: Public Domain Mark; credit and provider](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/d7b9zgnx/items?canvas=29&shouldScrollToCanvas=true)
