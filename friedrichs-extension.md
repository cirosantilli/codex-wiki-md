# Friedrichs extension

↑ **Parent:** [Self-adjoint operator](self-adjoint-operator.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friedrichs_extension)

A densely defined semibounded [symmetric operator](symmetric-operator.md) on a [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) has a canonical semibounded extension that is a [self-adjoint operator](self-adjoint-operator.md) obtained by closing its [quadratic form](quadratic-form.md). After shifting the lower bound to zero, complete the initial form domain in the norm $(\|u\|^2+q[u])^{1/2}$; the associated closed form determines the Friedrichs extension. For the [positive Laplace-Beltrami operator](positive-laplace-beltrami-operator.md) on a polygonal surface, this specifies the finite-energy realization at corners. Different choices of the initial form domain impose [Dirichlet boundary conditions](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md), [Neumann boundary conditions](neumann-boundary-condition.md), or mixed [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md); the extension does not choose the boundary condition independently of that domain.
