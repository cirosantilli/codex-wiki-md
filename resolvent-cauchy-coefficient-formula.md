# Resolvent Cauchy coefficient formula

↑ **Parent:** [Resolvent of an element](resolvent-of-an-element.md)

For a complex unital [Banach algebra](banach-algebra-split.md) and a circle enclosing its element's [Banach algebra spectrum](spectrum-of-an-element.md), the displayed identity holds for $n\geq0$. On a sufficiently large circle, expand the [Neumann series](neumann-series.md) and integrate it term by term. At any resolvent point the local series $R(\lambda_0+h)=\sum_{j\geq0}(-h)^jR(\lambda_0)^{j+1}$ proves norm analyticity. Apply continuous complex linear functionals and the scalar Cauchy theorem to deform the large circle to any radius greater than the maximum spectral modulus; the [Hahn-Banach theorem](hahn-banach-theorem.md) separates points and restores the vector integral identity. Its norm bound $\|x^n\|\leq R^{n+1}\max_{|\lambda|=R}\|R(\lambda)\|$ gives the reverse inequality in the [spectral radius formula](spectral-radius-formula.md).
