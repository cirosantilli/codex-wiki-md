# Fano stability

↑ **Parent:** [Fano-plane Turán theorem](fano-plane-turan-theorem.md)

For every $\rho>0$, some $\delta>0$ makes every sufficiently large Fano-free three-[uniform hypergraph](uniform-hypergraph.md) of density at least $3/4-\delta$ admit a partition with at most $\rho n^3$ internal triples. The argument compares four [hypergraph link graphs](hypergraph-link-graph.md) around a tetrahedron. Forbidden rainbow perfect matchings in their edge-coloured union force an approximate pattern with multiplicity two within the parts and four between them. A positive internal triple density would produce a complete tripartite six-vertex template, and its pairs together with a link vertex would complete a Fano plane.
