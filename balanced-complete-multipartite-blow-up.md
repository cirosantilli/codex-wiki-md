# Balanced complete multipartite blow-up

↑ **Parent:** [Complete multipartite graph](complete-multipartite-graph.md)

The balanced complete multipartite blow-up $K_r(t)$ replaces each [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) of the [complete graph](complete-graph.md) $K_r$ by an [independent set](independent-set-graph-theory.md) of $t$ [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md), placing every possible [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) between different sets and none inside a set. Every fixed [graph](graph-split.md) of [chromatic number](chromatic-number.md) at most $r$ embeds into $K_r(t)$ for sufficiently large $t$.
