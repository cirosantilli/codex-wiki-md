# Isolated ground sector of the Jaynes-Cummings model

↑ **Parent:** [Jaynes-Cummings model](jaynes-cummings-model.md)

Conservation of [excitation number](total-excitation-number.md) separates the [Jaynes-Cummings model](jaynes-cummings-model.md) into two-dimensional sectors $\{|e,n\rangle,|g,n+1\rangle\}$ for $n\geq0$, together with the isolated one-dimensional sector $|g,0\rangle$. The latter is annihilated by both interaction terms. Omitting it makes the often-stated paired-sector decomposition incomplete.
