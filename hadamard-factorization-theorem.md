# Hadamard factorization theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Weierstrass factorization theorem](weierstrass-factorization-theorem.md)

An [entire function](entire-function.md) of finite order factors as its canonical zero product times the exponential of a [polynomial](polynomial-split.md). For order at most one and nonzero value at zero, it has the form $f(z)=f(0)e^{Bz}\prod_j(1-z/a_j)e^{z/a_j}$. Genus-one factors converge when $\sum_j|a_j|^{-2}<\infty$. A zero-free function of this order is therefore the exponential of an affine [polynomial](polynomial-split.md).
