# Double-precision floating-point format

↑ **Parent:** [Floating-point arithmetic](floating-point-arithmetic-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Double-precision_floating-point_format)

A [floating-point representation](floating-point-representation.md) occupying $64$ [bits](bit.md), or $8$ bytes, with one sign bit, $11$ exponent bits and $52$ explicitly stored fraction bits. The implicit leading bit of a normal number gives $53$ bits of significand precision. Near one, adjacent numbers are separated by $2^{-52}$; rounding to nearest has [unit roundoff](unit-roundoff.md) $2^{-53}\approx1.11\times10^{-16}$ in the normal range. These are relative precision scales, distinct from underflow limits or a universal absolute error bound.[Chapter 2, IEEE Arithmetic Model and Double Format, Table 2-5](ieee-arithmetic-in-oracle-s-numerical-computation-guide.md)
