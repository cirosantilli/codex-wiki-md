# Quantized circulation

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum vortex](quantum-vortex.md)

Single-valuedness of the condensate field gives total [complex argument](argument-complex-analysis.md) change $2\pi q$ on a closed loop avoiding zeros, where $q$ is an integer [winding number](winding-number.md). Integrating the [superfluid velocity](superfluid-velocity.md) around that loop gives the displayed [circulation](circulation-physics.md). It is unchanged by smooth deformations of the loop that do not cross a [quantum vortex](quantum-vortex.md).
