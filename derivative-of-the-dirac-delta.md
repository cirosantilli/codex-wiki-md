# Derivative of the Dirac delta

↑ **Parent:** [Dirac delta function](dirac-delta-function.md)

The derivative of the Dirac delta is the [distributional derivative](distributional-derivative.md) defined by

$$
\langle\delta',\varphi\rangle=-\varphi'(0)
$$

for every [test function](test-function.md) $\varphi$.
