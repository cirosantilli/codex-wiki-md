# Ballistic streamline focusing by a moving star

↑ **Parent:** [Bondi–Hoyle accretion](bondi-hoyle-accretion.md)

In a cold pressureless stream of asymptotic speed $v$, conservation of [specific angular momentum](specific-angular-momentum.md) and the [eccentricity vector](eccentricity-vector.md) makes a streamline at impact parameter $d$ cross the downstream axis at $b=d^2v^2/(2GM)$. Its axial velocity there remains $v$, while opposing transverse velocities collide in a shock.
