# Polarized massless leptonic W decay

↑ **Parent:** [Massless leptonic W decay width](massless-leptonic-w-decay-width.md)

For a resting [W boson](w-boson.md) with real spatial [polarization vector](polarization-vector.md) along $z$, the [fermion spin sum](fermion-spin-sum.md) gives $\sum|\mathcal M|^2=g^2M_W^2\sin^2\theta/2$ for massless charged [lepton](lepton.md) and [antineutrino](antineutrino.md) momenta. The [two-body Lorentz-invariant phase space](two-body-lorentz-invariant-phase-space.md) gives the displayed angular [decay rate](decay-width.md). Since $\int\sin^2\theta\,d\Omega=8\pi/3$, the integrated width is $g^2M_W/(48\pi)$, equal to the unpolarized width. The [massive-vector spin average](massive-vector-spin-average.md) changes the angular distribution, not the total width. No initial [spin average](spin-average.md) is taken for a specified pure polarization.
