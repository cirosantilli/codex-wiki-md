# Oblique coupling of parallel Hertzian dipoles

↑ **Parent:** [Hertzian dipole](hertzian-dipole.md)

For lossless parallel Hertzian transmitting and receiving dipoles separated by distance $r$ at angle $\theta$ to their common axis, directional gains are each $G=3\sin^2\theta/2$. The emitted polarization matches the receiving axis's transverse projection. Matched-load [power](power.md) is therefore $P_L=P[3\sin^2\theta/2]^2[\lambda/(4\pi r)]^2$. One angular factor describes transmission and the other reception. This assumes reciprocal ideal dipoles, conjugate matching, far-field separation and negligible dissipative loss.
