# Small-loop magnetic radiation resistance

↑ **Parent:** [Radiation resistance](radiation-resistance.md)

A fixed electrically small loop of area A with peak sinusoidal current $I_0\cos\omega t$ has magnetic moment $m=AI$. The [magnetic dipole radiation](magnetic-dipole-radiation.md) law gives mean [power](power.md) $\mu_0A^2I_0^2\omega^4/(12\pi c^3)$. Defining it as $I_0^2R_{\rm rad}/2$ yields $R_{\rm rad}=\mu_0A^2\omega^4/(6\pi c^3)$. This equivalent resistance accounts for radiation, not heating in the conductor. Rotating the loop changes its vector moment and cannot in general be modelled by the same scalar current alone.
