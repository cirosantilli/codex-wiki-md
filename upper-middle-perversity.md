# Upper middle perversity

↑ **Parent:** [Perversity](perversity.md)

The [upper middle perversity](upper-middle-perversity.md) is $\bar n(k)=\lceil(k-2)/2\rceil$. It differs from the [lower middle perversity](lower-middle-perversity.md) by one at odd codimensions, and is complementary to it.
