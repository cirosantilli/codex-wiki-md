# Maximal clique

↑ **Parent:** [Clique (graph theory)](clique-graph-theory.md)

A maximal clique is a [clique](clique-graph-theory.md) not strictly contained in another clique. This is distinct from a largest clique, whose size equals the [clique number](clique-number.md). In a triangulated [probabilistic graphical model](probabilistic-graphical-model.md), maximal cliques are the natural nodes of a [junction tree](junction-tree.md).
