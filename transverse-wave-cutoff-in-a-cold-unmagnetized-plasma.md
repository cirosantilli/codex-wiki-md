# Transverse wave cutoff in a cold unmagnetized plasma

↑ **Parent:** [Plasma frequency](plasma-frequency.md)

With immobile ions, negligible collisions and electron density $n_e$, the [plasma frequency](plasma-frequency.md) is $\omega_p^2=n_ee^2/(m_e\epsilon_0)$. Combining inertial electron current with vacuum [displacement current](displacement-current.md) gives $\omega^2=\omega_p^2+c^2k^2$. Transverse fields are evanescent below $\omega_p$ and propagate above it.
