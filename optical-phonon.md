# Optical phonon

↑ **Parent:** [Phonon](phonon.md)

An optical phonon is a quantum of vibration on an [optical phonon branch](optical-phonon-branch.md), involving relative motion of atoms within a primitive cell. Its zone-center frequency can remain nonzero. Some such modes couple strongly to light when the relative displacement changes electric polarization; the name does not imply that every optical phonon is optically active. Their finite energies produce thresholds for electron emission and thermally suppressed absorption at low temperature.
