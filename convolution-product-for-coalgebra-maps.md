# Convolution product for coalgebra maps

↑ **Parent:** [Coalgebra](coalgebra.md)

For a [coalgebra](coalgebra.md) $C$ and a unital associative [algebra over a commutative ring](algebra-over-a-commutative-ring.md) $A$, maps $f,g:C\to A$ have convolution $f*g=m_A(f\otimes g)\Delta_C$, with unit $j_A\varepsilon_C$. The two-sided convolution inverse of $1_H$ is the [antipode](antipode.md).
