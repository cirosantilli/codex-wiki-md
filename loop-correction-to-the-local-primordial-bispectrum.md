# Loop correction to the local primordial bispectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Local-type primordial non-Gaussianity](local-type-primordial-non-gaussianity.md)

The exact [centered quadratic Gaussian transformation](centered-quadratic-gaussian-transformation.md) has a connected three-quadratic-vertex contribution $8\alpha^3\int d^3q\,P(q)P(|\mathbf k_1-\mathbf q|)P(|\mathbf k_2+\mathbf q|)/(2\pi)^3$. It is higher order than the usual tree-level local [primordial bispectrum](primordial-bispectrum.md). Infrared or ultraviolet regularization may be needed.
