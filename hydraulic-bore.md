# Hydraulic bore

↑ **Parent:** [Hydraulic jump](hydraulic-jump.md)

A hydraulic bore is a propagating abrupt depth change described as a moving [hydraulic jump](hydraulic-jump.md). In the [shock frame](shock-frame.md), an admissible rightward through-flow goes from [supercritical flow](supercritical-flow.md) to [subcritical flow](subcritical-flow.md), with the appropriate [characteristic curves](characteristic-curve.md) entering the jump.
