# Energy cascade

↑ **Parent:** [Turbulence](turbulence-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Energy_cascade)

An [energy cascade](energy-cascade.md) transfers [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) among scales through nonlinear motion. A forward cascade carries energy toward smaller spatial scales, where [kinematic viscosity](kinematic-viscosity.md) can dissipate it. Conservation and a mean scale-space flux do not uniquely prescribe the geometry or locality of the transfer mechanism.
