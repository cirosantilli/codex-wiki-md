# Exponential maximal bound for a symmetric random walk

↑ **Parent:** [Simple symmetric random walk](simple-symmetric-random-walk.md)

For a [simple symmetric random walk](simple-symmetric-random-walk.md), the estimate $\mathbb E e^{\theta S_n}\le e^{n\theta^2/2}$ and the exponential maximal inequality give $\mathbb P(\max_{k\le n}S_k\ge t\sqrt n)\le e^{-t^2/2}$ for $t\ge0$.
