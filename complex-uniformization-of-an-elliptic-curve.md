# Complex uniformization of an elliptic curve

↑ **Parent:** [Elliptic curve](elliptic-curve.md)

Every complex [elliptic curve](elliptic-curve.md), as a complex Lie group, is the quotient of $\mathbb C$ by a rank-two lattice. The coordinate functions are built from the [Weierstrass elliptic function](weierstrass-elliptic-function.md) and its derivative. Holomorphic group homomorphisms lift to scalar multiplication on $\mathbb C$, and their endomorphism condition is $aL\subseteq L$. This turns [complex multiplication](complex-multiplication.md) into a statement about lattice stabilizers.
