# Morse potential

↑ **Parent:** [Hamiltonian (quantum mechanics)](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morse_potential)

The Morse potential models an anharmonic molecular bond with dissociation energy $D_e>0$, equilibrium separation $x_e$, and inverse length $a>0$. Unlike a [quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md), it has finitely many bound levels and a dissociation continuum.
