# Nonfluent variant primary progressive aphasia

↑ **Parent:** [Primary progressive aphasia](primary-progressive-aphasia.md)

A [PPA](primary-progressive-aphasia.md) pattern characterised by effortful or agrammatic [language](language.md), often with [apraxia of speech](apraxia-of-speech.md). Motor [speech](speech.md) and syntactic comprehension should be measured separately; neither is established merely by slow output.
