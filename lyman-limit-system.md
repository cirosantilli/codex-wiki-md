# Lyman limit system

↑ **Parent:** [Lyman-alpha absorption](lyman-alpha-absorption.md)

A Lyman limit system has approximately unit or greater optical depth at the neutral-hydrogen [Lyman limit](lyman-limit.md) near $91.2\,\mathrm{nm}$. The threshold follows from $\tau_{912}=N_{\rm HI}\sigma_{912}$ with $\sigma_{912}\simeq6.3\times10^{-18}\,\mathrm{cm}^2$. Its strong continuum absorption distinguishes it from ordinary weak [Lyman-alpha forest](lyman-alpha-forest.md) lines. Classification conventions often separate [damped Lyman-alpha systems](damped-lyman-alpha-system.md) as the highest-column category.
