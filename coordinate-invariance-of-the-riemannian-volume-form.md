# Coordinate invariance of the Riemannian volume form

↑ **Parent:** [Riemannian volume form](riemannian-volume-form.md)

For positive coordinate charts put $J=\partial y/\partial x$. The [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md) transforms by $g_x=J^Tg_yJ$, giving $\det g_x=(\det J)^2\det g_y$. The top [wedge product of differential forms](wedge-product-of-differential-forms.md) transforms by $dy^1\wedge\cdots\wedge dy^n=(\det J)dx^1\wedge\cdots\wedge dx^n$. Since $\det J>0$, the two factors match. Thus the local expressions define one global [Riemannian volume form](riemannian-volume-form.md).
