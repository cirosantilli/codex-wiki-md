# Passive linear transformation of coherent states

↑ **Parent:** [Coherent state](coherent-state.md)

A lossless mode transformation maps a product of [coherent states](coherent-state.md) to another product of [coherent states](coherent-state.md) with transformed complex amplitudes. [unitarity](unitary-operator.md) preserves the total mean particle number. It does not generally turn a fixed-number input into [coherent states](coherent-state.md).
