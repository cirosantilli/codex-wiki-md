# Adjoint source-to-far-field operator

↑ **Parent:** [Source-to-far-field operator at fixed frequency](source-to-far-field-operator-at-fixed-frequency.md)

With complex [inner products](inner-product.md) linear in the first argument, the [adjoint operator](adjoint-operator.md) of the [source-to-far-field operator at fixed frequency](source-to-far-field-operator-at-fixed-frequency.md) is

$$
(T_k^*g)(\mathbf r)=-\frac1{4\pi}\int_{S_1}g(\boldsymbol\theta)e^{ik\boldsymbol\theta\cdot\mathbf r}\,dS(\boldsymbol\theta).
$$

It is a normalized [Herglotz wave function](herglotz-wave-function.md). Complex conjugation reverses the phase sign, while retaining the real factor $-1/(4\pi)$.
