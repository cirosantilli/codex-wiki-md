# Nonlinear optics

↑ **Parent:** [Optics](optics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonlinear_optics)

[Nonlinear optics](nonlinear-optics.md) studies the response of matter to [light](light.md) when the induced [electric polarization](polarization-density.md) is not proportional to the driving [electric field](electric-field-split.md). An intense [laser](laser-split.md) can make these additional terms observable. The striking consequence is that a material can emit [light](light.md) at a [frequency](frequency.md) absent from the incident beam: [second-harmonic generation](second-harmonic-generation.md) doubles it. This extends a [laser's](laser-split.md) usable [optical spectrum](optical-spectrum.md) without requiring a second lasing transition.

[Franken and colleagues frequency-doubling experiment](franken-and-colleagues-frequency-doubling-experiment.md) established an optical harmonic in 1961. [Nicolaas Bloembergen's](nicolaas-bloembergen.md) theoretical work developed the description of interacting [light](light.md) waves in nonlinear matter. His 1981 lecture connects a nonlinear constitutive relation with [Maxwell's equations](maxwell-equations-split.md) and explains compensation of [optical dispersion](dispersion-optics.md) by [birefringence](birefringence.md).[Nonlinear Optics and Spectroscopy, introduction and p. 19](nonlinear-optics-and-spectroscopy-by-nicolaas-bloembergen.md)
