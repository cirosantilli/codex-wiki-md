# Geostrophic adjustment

↑ **Parent:** [Geophysical fluid dynamics](geophysical-fluid-dynamics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geostrophic_adjustment)

Geostrophic adjustment is the radiation of inertia-gravity waves from an unbalanced disturbance, leaving a slower flow in [geostrophic balance](geostrophic-balance.md) while conserving linearized [potential vorticity](potential-vorticity.md).
