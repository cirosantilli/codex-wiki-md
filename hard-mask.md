# Hard mask

↑ **Parent:** [Etching](etching.md)

A durable patterned layer used to protect underlying material during an etch. Its thickness and [etch selectivity](etch-selectivity.md) must survive the intended depth; erosion and lateral bias can change final feature dimensions even when the starting pattern is correct.
