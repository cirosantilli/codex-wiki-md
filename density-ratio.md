# Density ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Probability density function](probability-density-function.md)

A [density ratio](density-ratio.md) compares two [probability density functions](probability-density-function.md) relative to the same underlying [measure](measure-split.md). More generally it is a [Radon-Nikodym derivative](radon-nikodym-derivative.md) when one [probability measure](probability-measure.md) has [absolute continuity of measures](absolute-continuity-of-measures.md) with respect to the other.
