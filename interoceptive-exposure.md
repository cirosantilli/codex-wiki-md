# Interoceptive exposure

↑ **Parent:** [Exposure therapy](exposure-therapy.md)

Exposure to appropriate bodily sensations relevant to a feared prediction. Assessment and supervision determine suitable exercises; the inference concerns a specific tested prediction, not the claim that all bodily symptoms are harmless.
