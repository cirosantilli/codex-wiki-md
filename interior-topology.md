# Interior (topology)

↑ **Parent:** [Topology](topology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interior_(topology))

The interior of a subset $A$ of a [topological space](topological-space.md) is the union of all [open sets](open-set.md) contained in $A$, equivalently the largest open subset of $A$.
