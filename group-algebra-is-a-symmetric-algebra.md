# Group algebra is a symmetric algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetric algebra (Frobenius algebra)](symmetric-algebra-frobenius-algebra.md)

For a finite group $G$, the coefficient of the identity in $xy$ defines a nondegenerate symmetric associative bilinear form on $kG$. Hence $kG\cong(kG)^*$ as bimodules and the [group algebra](group-algebra.md) is a [symmetric algebra](symmetric-algebra-frobenius-algebra.md).
