# Secant domination for expected utility derivatives

↑ **Parent:** [Expected utility maximization](expected-utility-maximization.md)

If a negative differentiable concave [utility function](utility-function-split.md) satisfies $\mathbb EU(\theta X)>-\infty$ on an open interval, outer secants at four points bound the sample derivative on a smaller compact interval. Negativity makes the endpoint values absolutely integrable. The [dominated convergence theorem](dominated-convergence-theorem.md) then gives $d\mathbb EU(\theta X)/d\theta=\mathbb E[XU'(\theta X)]$ and continuous derivative when $U'$ is continuous. This is a justification of differentiation, rather than an assumption of [integrability](integrability.md) of marginal utility.
