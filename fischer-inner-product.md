# Fischer inner product

↑ **Parent:** [Inner product](inner-product.md)

The Fischer inner product on [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) is $\langle x^\alpha,x^\beta\rangle_F=\alpha!\,\delta_{\alpha\beta}$. Multiplication by $x_i$ is adjoint to differentiation by $x_i$. Therefore multiplication by $|x|^2$ is adjoint to the ordinary [Laplacian](laplacian.md), yielding the [harmonic decomposition of homogeneous polynomials](harmonic-decomposition-of-homogeneous-polynomials.md) by finite-dimensional orthogonality.
