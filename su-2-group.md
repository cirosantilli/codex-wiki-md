<h1 id="su-2-group">SU(2) group</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Special unitary group](special-unitary-group.md)

The SU(2) group is the group of two-by-two unitary matrices of determinant one. It is a compact three-dimensional [Lie group](lie-group.md) whose underlying manifold is a three-sphere.
