# Helioseismology

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar oscillation](stellar-oscillation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helioseismology)

Helioseismology infers the [Sun](sun.md)'s structure and dynamics from its [stellar oscillations](stellar-oscillation.md). Spatial projection of resolved surface observations onto [spherical harmonics](spherical-harmonic.md) identifies angular degree and azimuthal order; a temporal [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) measures mode [frequencies](frequency.md). [Stellar acoustic modes](stellar-acoustic-mode.md) constrain the [adiabatic sound speed](adiabatic-sound-speed.md), while rotational splitting constrains [stellar rotation](stellar-rotation.md). Their finite wavelength, surface reflection phases and finite observational coverage limit inversion resolution.
