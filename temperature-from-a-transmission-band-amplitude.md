# Temperature from a transmission-band amplitude

↑ **Parent:** [Atmospheric spectral-feature amplitude](atmospheric-spectral-feature-amplitude.md)

For a thin hydrostatic [isothermal atmosphere](isothermal-atmosphere.md), $\Delta D\simeq2R_pN_HH/R_\star^2$ and $H=k_BT/(\mu m_ug)$. Hence $T\simeq\mu m_ug\Delta D R_\star^2/(2k_BR_pN_H)$. A measured [exoplanet transmission spectrum](exoplanet-transmission-spectrum.md) amplitude constrains $N_HH$, so gravity, mean molecular mass and the number $N_H$ of sampled [atmospheric scale heights](atmospheric-scale-height.md) are additional inputs. In a clear fixed-composition model, $N_H\simeq\log(\kappa_{\rm band}/\kappa_{\rm cont})$; clouds or abundance gradients can change it.
