# Minor galaxy merger

↑ **Parent:** [Dry galaxy merger](dry-galaxy-merger.md)

A minor galaxy merger has a small satellite-to-host mass ratio. Its stars are commonly deposited at large host radii after [tidal stripping](tidal-stripping.md) and [Chandrasekhar dynamical friction](chandrasekhar-dynamical-friction.md).
