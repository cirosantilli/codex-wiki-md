# Conjugate points

↑ **Parent:** [Geodesic](geodesic.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conjugate_points)

Two points along a [geodesic](geodesic.md) are conjugate when a nonzero [Jacobi field](jacobi-field.md) along it vanishes at both points. This detects singularity of the geodesic exponential map and governs loss of local length minimization.
