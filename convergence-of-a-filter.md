# Convergence of a filter

↑ **Parent:** [Filter (set theory)](filter-set-theory.md)

A [filter on a set](filter-set-theory.md) equipped with a [topology](topology-split.md) converges to $x$ if it contains every [neighbourhood](neighbourhood-mathematics.md) of $x$. A [Hausdorff space](hausdorff-space.md) gives every convergent filter at most one limit: disjoint neighbourhoods of distinct purported limits would have empty intersection in the filter.
