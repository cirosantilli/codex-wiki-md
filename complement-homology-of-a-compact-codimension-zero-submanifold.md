# Complement homology of a compact codimension-zero submanifold

↑ **Parent:** [Alexander duality](alexander-duality.md)

A compact smooth $n$-manifold with nonempty boundary embedded in $S^n$ is a proper compact locally contractible subset. [Alexander duality](alexander-duality.md) therefore expresses the [reduced homology](reduced-homology.md) of its complement by the reduced [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) of the abstract manifold. Thus these groups do not depend on its particular [smooth embedding](smooth-embedding.md).
