# Picard functor of a curve

↑ **Parent:** [Picard group](picard-group.md)

For a smooth projective geometrically connected [algebraic curve](algebraic-curve.md) $C/k$, this [functor](functor.md) assigns to a [scheme](scheme.md) $T$ the [line bundles](line-bundle.md) on $C\times T$ having degree $d$ on each geometric fibre, modulo bundles pulled back from $T$. One generally takes the associated sheaf for faithfully flat covers. A rational point of $C$ permits normalization of every [line bundle](line-bundle.md) along that point; normalized bundles have no automorphisms preserving their normalization and satisfy effective descent. The resulting [functor](functor.md) is represented by the degree-$d$ component of the [Picard scheme of a curve](picard-scheme-of-a-curve.md).
