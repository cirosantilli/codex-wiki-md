# Bialgebra cocycle twist

↑ **Parent:** [Bialgebra scalar cocycle](bialgebra-scalar-cocycle.md)

With the convention $m*\gamma=\gamma*n$ and invertible [bialgebra scalar cocycle](bialgebra-scalar-cocycle.md) $\gamma$ for $n$, the same [coalgebra](coalgebra.md) has $n=\bar\gamma*m*\gamma$, or equivalently $m=\gamma*n*\bar\gamma$. Thus $n(a,b)=\sum\bar\gamma(a_{(1)},b_{(1)})m(a_{(2)},b_{(2)})\gamma(a_{(3)},b_{(3)})$. Specifying the equation prevents opposite twist conventions from being conflated.
