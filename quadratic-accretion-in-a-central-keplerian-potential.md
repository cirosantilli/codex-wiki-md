# Quadratic accretion in a central Keplerian potential

↑ **Parent:** [Bondi–Hoyle accretion](bondi-hoyle-accretion.md)

In a fixed central [Newtonian gravitational potential](newtonian-gravitational-potential.md), pressureless [free fall](free-fall.md) has speed $u=aR^{-1/2}$, and randomly moving stars have typical gas-relative speed $V=bR^{-1/2}$. A steady, undepleted spherical [mass flux](mass-flux.md) $A$ gives $\rho=A R^{-3/2}/(4\pi a)$. The [Bondi--Hoyle--Lyttleton accretion rate](bondi-hoyle-lyttleton-accretion-rate.md) therefore becomes $\dot M=\lambda M^2$ with $\lambda=G^2A/(ab^3)$, independent of radius. Integrating $d(1/M)/dt=-\lambda$ gives the displayed solution before $\lambda M_0t=1$. Its finite-time divergence signals failure of the fixed potential, undepleted gas supply and negligible feedback assumptions; it is not a physically unlimited mass supply.
