# Deposit composition from sedimentation jump conditions

↑ **Parent:** [Bidisperse kinematic sedimentation](bidisperse-kinematic-sedimentation.md)

For a [bidisperse kinematic sedimentation](bidisperse-kinematic-sedimentation.md) model with normalized suspension concentrations $q_i$, isolated speeds $W_i$, and a stationary deposit with $p_1+p_2=1$, the upward deposit-front speed magnitude obeys $s(p_i-q_i)=W_iq_i(1-q_i)$. Adding gives $s=\sum_iW_iq_i(1-q_i)/(1-\sum_iq_i)$; substitution determines each deposited fraction. These are [Rankine-Hugoniot conditions](rankine-hugoniot-conditions.md) with a stationary compacted branch.
