# Foundation in the cumulative hierarchy without ambient Foundation

↑ **Parent:** [ZF without foundation](zf-without-foundation.md)

The [cumulative hierarchy](cumulative-hierarchy.md) is a transitive class even without the ambient [Axiom of foundation](axiom-of-regularity.md). If $a$ is a nonempty member, choose the least [ordinal](ordinal-split.md) $\alpha$ with $a\cap V_\alpha\ne\varnothing$. It is a successor $\beta+1$. An element $x$ of that intersection is a subset of $V_\beta$, while $a$ has no element in $V_\beta$, so $a\cap x=\varnothing$. This proves internal [Foundation](axiom-of-regularity.md). If the hierarchy also satisfies the other [ZF](zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory.md) axioms, restricting every [quantifier](quantifier.md) of a finite [ZF](zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory.md) proof to this definable class translates it into a proof from [ZF without foundation](zf-without-foundation.md). Thus adjoining [Foundation](axiom-of-regularity.md) preserves consistency relative to the remaining axioms.
