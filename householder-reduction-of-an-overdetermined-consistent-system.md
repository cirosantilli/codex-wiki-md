# Householder reduction of an overdetermined consistent system

↑ **Parent:** [Householder QR decomposition](householder-qr-decomposition.md)

For full column rank $A\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$, a full [QR factorization](qr-decomposition.md) gives $Q^TA=(R_1,0)^T$ with invertible upper triangular $R_1$. The equation $Ax=b$ is consistent exactly when the final $m-n$ entries of $Q^Tb$ vanish. If consistent, [back substitution](back-substitution.md) gives its unique solution. This separates consistency from uniqueness in overdetermined systems.
