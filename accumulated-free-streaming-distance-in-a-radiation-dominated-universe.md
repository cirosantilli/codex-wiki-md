# Accumulated free-streaming distance in a radiation-dominated universe

↑ **Parent:** [Free streaming](free-streaming.md)

Suppose a collisionless particle has [momentum](momentum.md) $p=T_r\propto a^{-1}$ during pure [radiation domination](radiation-domination.md). Since $a\propto t^{1/2}$, integrating $\chi=\int_0^t v(t')dt'/a(t')$ gives the displayed [comoving distance](comoving-radial-distance.md). The physical distance at observation is $a\chi=H^{-1}\operatorname{arsinh}(y)/y$, $y=m/T_r$. The printed expression without $a^{-1}$ is consequently a physical distance, or a comoving distance in coordinates normalized to $a=1$ at observation. Its ratio tends to one in the relativistic limit and to $\log(2y)/y$ in the [nonrelativistic limit](nonrelativistic-limit.md). The accumulated scale differs from an instantaneous velocity-over-expansion-rate estimate.
