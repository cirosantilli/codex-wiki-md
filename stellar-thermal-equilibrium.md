# Stellar thermal equilibrium

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar structure](stellar-structure-split.md)

In [stellar structure](stellar-structure-split.md), stellar thermal equilibrium means that internal heating balances the surface [luminosity](luminosity.md) and any energy loss through neutrinos, with negligible secular storage of thermal [energy](energy.md) on the timescale considered. A star can satisfy this balance while its interior is hotter than its surface and [heat](heat.md) flows outward. It is therefore different from equal-temperature [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium.md) between systems. Neglecting neutrino losses, the balance is $L_{\rm surface}=L_{\rm heat}$. In a slowly mass-losing [donor star](donor-star.md), a [stellar thermal equilibrium](stellar-thermal-equilibrium.md) radius sequence applies only when thermal adjustment is fast compared with the mass-loss timescale; rapid loss instead requires the adiabatic [stellar radius response exponent](stellar-radius-response-exponent.md).
