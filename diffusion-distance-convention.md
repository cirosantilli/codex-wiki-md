# Diffusion-distance convention

↑ **Parent:** [Diffusion](diffusion.md)

The scale $\sqrt{Dt}$ characterizes [diffusion](diffusion.md) over time $t$. For unrestricted isotropic [diffusion](diffusion.md), the rms displacement in one coordinate is $\sqrt{2Dt}$ and the total three-dimensional rms displacement is $\sqrt{6Dt}$. Stating the convention avoids spurious disagreement over a numerical prefactor.
