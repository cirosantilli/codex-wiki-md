# Epsilon induction

↑ **Parent:** [Axiom of regularity](axiom-of-regularity.md)

The principle of epsilon induction says that every progressive class is universal: if

$$
\forall x\left[
(\forall y\in x,\ \varphi(y))\Longrightarrow\varphi(x)
\right],
$$

then $\varphi(x)$ holds for every set $x$. Over the other axioms of ZF, it is equivalent to the [Axiom of foundation](axiom-of-regularity.md).
