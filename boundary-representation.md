# Boundary representation

↑ **Parent:** [Computer aided geometric design](computer-aided-geometric-design-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boundary_representation)

A [boundary representation](boundary-representation.md) specifies a solid through its oriented bounding faces and their edge and [vertex of a polyhedron](vertex-of-a-polyhedron.md) incidence. Supporting [planes](plane.md) and [coordinates](coordinate-linear-algebra.md) supply geometry; face loops and adjacency supply topology. A valid closed manifold model requires compatible orientation, two incident faces per edge and a single local fan at each [vertex of a polyhedron](vertex-of-a-polyhedron.md), together with an embedded boundary.
