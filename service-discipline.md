# Service discipline

↑ **Parent:** [Queueing theory](queueing-theory-split.md)

A service discipline specifies which customers receive the server's capacity and where newly arriving customers join the queue. [First come first served](first-come-first-served.md) and [processor sharing](processor-sharing.md) give different sample paths even when their equilibrium populations agree.
