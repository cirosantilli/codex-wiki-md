# Strong minimum principle for elliptic operators

↑ **Parent:** [Weak maximum principle for elliptic operators](weak-maximum-principle-for-elliptic-operators.md)

Let $L$ be uniformly elliptic with bounded coefficients and nonpositive zeroth-order coefficient. If $u\geq0$, $Lu\leq0$, and $u$ vanishes at an interior point of a connected domain, then $u$ vanishes identically. This is the minimum form of the [strong maximum principle](strong-maximum-principle-for-harmonic-functions.md).
