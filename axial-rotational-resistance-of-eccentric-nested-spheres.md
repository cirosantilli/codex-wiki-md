# Axial rotational resistance of eccentric nested spheres

↑ **Parent:** [Lubrication resistance](lubrication-resistance.md)

Rotation about the offset axis has azimuthal [Couette flow](couette-flow.md) and no pressure variation at leading [lubrication](lubrication-theory.md) order. Integrating the tangential traction gives torque $-\mathcal R_\Omega\Omega$ on the inner sphere. The concentric limit is $8\pi\mu a^4/(3\Delta)$ and the contact limit is finite, $4\pi\mu a^4/\Delta$, because the wall velocity vanishes at the closest pole.
