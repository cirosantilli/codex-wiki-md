# Finite-sample maximum bias

↑ **Parent:** [Breakdown point](breakdown-point.md)

Here $d_H$ counts the observations replaced. This curve measures the largest change after at most $m$ arbitrary replacements. Under the first-unbounded convention, the [replacement breakdown point](replacement-breakdown-point.md) is $n^{-1}\min\{m:b_n(m;y)=\infty\}$. The largest fraction still guaranteed bounded is one grid step smaller; those conventions must not be confused. A [sample mean](sample-mean.md) breaks after one replacement, while a scalar [sample median](sample-median.md) with a fixed ordinary tie convention needs about half the sample replaced.
