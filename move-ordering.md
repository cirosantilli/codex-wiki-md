# Move ordering

↑ **Parent:** [Alpha-beta pruning](alpha-beta-pruning.md)

The order in which legal child positions are searched. Good [move ordering](move-ordering.md) supplies useful alternatives early and increases [alpha-beta pruning](alpha-beta-pruning.md); poor [move ordering](move-ordering.md) can leave the worst-case [time complexity](time-complexity.md) of full [minimax search](minimax-search.md) unchanged.
