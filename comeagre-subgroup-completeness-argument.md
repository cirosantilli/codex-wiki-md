# Comeagre subgroup completeness argument

↑ **Parent:** [Baire category theorem](baire-category-theorem.md)

A dense additive subgroup of a complete metrizable topological group cannot be proper if it is [comeagre](comeagre-set.md). Every translate is comeagre and meets the subgroup, so every translating element is a difference of two subgroup elements. Applied to a [topologically complete](topological-completeness.md) normed space inside its completion, this proves that its original norm is complete.
