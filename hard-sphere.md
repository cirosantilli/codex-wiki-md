# Hard sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical physics](statistical-physics-split.md)

A [hard sphere](hard-sphere.md) has an impenetrable core and otherwise no pair interaction. Two identical spheres of diameter $d$ cannot have centre separation below $d$. Even without attractive [potential energy](potential-energy.md), crowding can produce fluid–crystal coexistence because available configuration space and [entropy](entropy-split.md) differ between the phases.
