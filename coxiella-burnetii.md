# Coxiella burnetii

↑ **Parent:** [Bacteria](bacteria.md)

[Coxiella burnetii](coxiella-burnetii.md) is a specialized intracellular bacterium whose replication was achieved in host-cell-free medium. Its natural intracellular niche must therefore be distinguished from an absolute biochemical requirement for a living host in all culture conditions.
