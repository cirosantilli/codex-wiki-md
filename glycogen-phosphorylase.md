# Glycogen phosphorylase

↑ **Parent:** [Enzyme](enzyme.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glycogen_phosphorylase)

[Glycogen phosphorylase](glycogen-phosphorylase.md) is an [enzyme](enzyme.md) involved in [glycogen](glycogen.md) breakdown. Its activity is the readout in the extract-transfer experiment under [second messenger](second-messenger.md); see that experiment's primary references. A change in [enzyme](enzyme.md) activity need not mean production of more [enzyme](enzyme.md) [protein](protein.md).
