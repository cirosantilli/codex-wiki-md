# Killing-normalized contraction of a cubic trace tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Cubic trace tensor of a Lie algebra representation](cubic-trace-tensor-of-a-lie-algebra-representation.md)

Lowering and raising [structure constants](structure-constant.md) with the [Killing form](killing-form.md) gives $c_a{}^{mn}c_{mn}{}^r=-\delta_a{}^r$. Since $c_a{}^{mn}$ is antisymmetric in $m,n$, only the [commutator](commutator.md) part of the [cubic trace tensor of a Lie algebra representation](cubic-trace-tensor-of-a-lie-algebra-representation.md) contributes, proving $c_a{}^{mn}B_{mnb}=-H_{ab}/2$. In an adapted compact basis with $\kappa_{ab}=-\delta_{ab}$ and $H_{ab}=-\mu\delta_{ab}$, the resulting sign is positive: $+\mu\delta_{ab}/2$. Defining the lowered structure constants with a positive Euclidean metric instead would reverse this sign.
