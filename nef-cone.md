# Nef cone

↑ **Parent:** [Real numerical divisor classes](real-numerical-divisor-classes.md)

The nef cone is the closed [convex cone](convex-cone.md) of [nef divisor](nef-line-bundle.md) classes. It is dual to the [closed cone of curves](closed-cone-of-curves.md). Its interior is the [ample cone](ample-cone.md) on a [projective scheme](projective-scheme.md).
