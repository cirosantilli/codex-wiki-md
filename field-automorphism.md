# Field automorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Galois theory](galois-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Field_automorphism)

A field automorphism is a bijective [ring homomorphism](ring-homomorphism.md) from a [field](field.md) to itself. The automorphisms fixing a specified subfield form a [Galois group](galois-group.md) when the extension is Galois.
