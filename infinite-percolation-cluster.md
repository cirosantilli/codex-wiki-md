# Infinite percolation cluster

↑ **Parent:** [Percolation cluster](percolation-cluster.md)

An infinite [percolation cluster](percolation-cluster.md) is an open [connected component of a graph](component-graph-theory.md) with infinitely many [graph vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md). On a countable translation-invariant lattice, a zero root [percolation probability](percolation-probability.md) implies that no [infinite percolation cluster](infinite-percolation-cluster.md) exists [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md), by the countable union over all [graph vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md). A positive root [probability](probability.md) implies almost-sure existence by [translation ergodicity of Bernoulli percolation](translation-ergodicity-of-bernoulli-percolation.md); uniqueness is an additional theorem.
