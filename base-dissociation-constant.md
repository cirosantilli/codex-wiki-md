# Base dissociation constant

↑ **Parent:** [Conjugate base](conjugate-base.md)

For the [conjugate base](conjugate-base.md) $\mathrm A^-$ accepting a [proton](proton.md) from [water](water.md),

$$
\mathrm A^-+\mathrm H_2\mathrm O\rightleftharpoons\mathrm{HA}+\mathrm{OH}^-,\qquad
K_b=\frac{a_{\mathrm{HA}}a_{\mathrm{OH}^-}}{a_{\mathrm A^-}}.
$$

This [base dissociation constant](base-dissociation-constant.md) uses the same [water](water.md) [thermodynamic activity](thermodynamic-activity.md) approximation and [standard state](standard-state.md) conventions as the [acid dissociation constant](acid-dissociation-constant.md). Multiplying the two relations cancels the partner [thermodynamic activities](thermodynamic-activity.md):

$$
K_aK_b=a_{\mathrm H^+}a_{\mathrm{OH}^-}=K_w,\qquad
\mathrm pK_a+\mathrm pK_b=\mathrm pK_w,\qquad
\mathrm pK_b=-\log_{10}K_b,\quad \mathrm pK_w=-\log_{10}K_w.
$$

Here $K_w$ is the aqueous ion product associated with [self-ionization of water](self-ionization-of-water.md). At fixed [temperature](temperature-split.md), a larger [acid dissociation constant](acid-dissociation-constant.md) gives a smaller [base dissociation constant](base-dissociation-constant.md) for its own [conjugate base](conjugate-base.md); this reciprocal rule compares matching [conjugate acid-base pairs](conjugate-acid-base-pair.md). A [weak acid](weak-acid.md) does not necessarily have a [conjugate base](conjugate-base.md) that reacts almost completely with [water](water.md). For example, stipulated $K_a=10^{-5}$ and $K_w=10^{-14}$ give $K_b=10^{-9}$.[Chemistry 2e by Paul Flowers and others; §14.3, Relative Strengths of Conjugate Acid-Base Pairs, cancellation giving Ka Kb = Kw](chemistry-2e-by-paul-flowers-and-others/14-3-relative-strengths-of-acids-and-bases.md)

The value of $K_w$ changes with [temperature](temperature-split.md); $10^{-14}$ is the familiar approximate value near $25\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$, not a universal constant. If [water](water.md) is retained explicitly in both full [equilibrium constants](equilibrium-constant.md), their product instead equals $a_{\mathrm H_3\mathrm O^+}a_{\mathrm{OH}^-}/a_{\mathrm H_2\mathrm O}^2$, the full [equilibrium constant](equilibrium-constant.md) for $2\mathrm H_2\mathrm O\rightleftharpoons\mathrm H_3\mathrm O^++\mathrm{OH}^-$. The [acid dissociation constant](acid-dissociation-constant.md), [base dissociation constant](base-dissociation-constant.md) and [self-ionization of water](self-ionization-of-water.md) convention must agree.
