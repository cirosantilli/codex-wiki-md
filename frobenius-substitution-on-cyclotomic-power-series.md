# Frobenius substitution on cyclotomic power series

↑ **Parent:** [Coleman power series](coleman-power-series.md)

On $R=\mathbb Z_p[[T]]$, this injective substitution reduces modulo $p$ to $f(T)\mapsto f(T^p)$. The ring is a free module of rank $p$ over $\varphi(R)$, with basis $1,(1+T),\ldots,(1+T)^{p-1}$. Prove this first modulo $p$, where the change of basis from $1,T,\ldots,T^{p-1}$ is triangular and invertible, and then lift coefficients successively modulo powers of $p$.
