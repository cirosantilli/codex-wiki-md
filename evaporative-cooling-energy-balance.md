# Evaporative cooling energy balance

↑ **Parent:** [Forced evaporative cooling](forced-evaporative-cooling.md)

For a classical three-dimensional harmonic trap of fixed frequencies, $E=3Nk_BT$. If each lost atom removes mean [energy](energy-split.md) $\epsilon_{\rm ev}=(\eta+\kappa)k_BT$, differential [energy](energy-split.md) balance gives $d\ln T/d\ln N=(\eta+\kappa-3)/3$. The result assumes rapid elastic equilibration and excludes separate work from changing trap frequencies.
