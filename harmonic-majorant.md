# Harmonic majorant

↑ **Parent:** [Subharmonic function](subharmonic-function.md)

A harmonic majorant of a real function $u$ is a real [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md) $h$ with $h\geq u$ pointwise. A least harmonic majorant lies below every other harmonic majorant. A continuous [subharmonic function](subharmonic-function.md) need not have one: $u(z)=(1-|z|^2)^{-1}$ on the [unit disk](unit-disk.md) has increasing circular means diverging to infinity, whereas any harmonic majorant would have the same finite mean on every centered circle.
