# Strict-range modification of a continuous extension

↑ **Parent:** [Tietze extension theorem](tietze-extension-theorem.md)

Suppose $Y$ is closed in a [normal space](normal-space.md) $X$ and $|f|<1$ on $Y$ for a [continuous](continuous-function.md) real-valued $f$ on $X$. The [closed set](closed-set.md) $Z=\{|f|\ge1\}$ misses $Y$. [Urysohn's lemma](urysohn-s-lemma.md) supplies $u:X\to[0,1]$ equal to zero on $Y$ and one on $Z$. Then $(1-u)f$ agrees with $f$ on $Y$ and has range in $(-1,1)$. This permits the inverse transform $s\mapsto s/(1-|s|)$ in the proof of unbounded Tietze extension.
