# Milne-Thomson circle theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Complex potential of a two-dimensional potential flow](complex-potential-of-a-two-dimensional-potential-flow.md)

The [Milne-Thomson circle theorem](milne-thomson-circle-theorem.md) constructs a circular impermeable boundary in a two-dimensional [potential flow](potential-flow.md). Suppose an incident [complex potential of a two-dimensional potential flow](complex-potential-of-a-two-dimensional-potential-flow.md) $f$ is analytic on and inside $|z|=a$, with its physical singularities outside that disk. Then the displayed $W$ has constant [stream function](stream-function.md) on $|z|=a$. The proof is the identity $a^2/\bar z=z$ on the circle: there $W=f(z)+\overline{f(z)}$ is real. Hence the circle is a [streamline](streamline.md) and satisfies the [no-penetration boundary condition](no-penetration-boundary-condition.md). The reflected term has its image singularities inside the obstacle. Additional imposed [circulation](circulation-physics.md) must be specified separately. The theorem is named after [Louis Melville Milne-Thomson](l-m-milne-thomson.md).
