# Limit inferior and limit superior

↑ **Parent:** [Sequence](sequence.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limit_inferior_and_limit_superior)

For a real [sequence](sequence.md) $(a_n)$, the [limit inferior](limit-inferior.md) is the limit of the infima of its tails and the [limit superior](limit-superior.md) is the limit of the suprema of its tails. These extended-real bounds describe its lowest and highest limiting behavior.
