# Variation of Levi-Civita connection

↑ **Parent:** [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md)

For a family of [Levi-Civita connections](levi-civita-connection.md), the [difference of affine connections is a tensor](difference-of-affine-connections-is-a-tensor.md). Dividing by the family parameter and taking the [limit](limit-of-a-function.md) shows that its [variation](variation.md) is [tensorial](tensoriality.md). Vary the [metric compatibility](metric-compatibility.md) equation and retain the symmetry of the two lower indices. Adding the two cyclic identities and subtracting the third gives the displayed result. In a normal [coordinate frame](coordinate-basis.md) it is the direct [derivative](derivative.md) of the Christoffel formula; its [tensorial](tensoriality.md) form then holds in every frame.
