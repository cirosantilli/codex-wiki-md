# Variation of metric volume density

↑ **Parent:** [Metric tensor](metric-tensor.md)

For a [nondegenerate](nondegenerate-bilinear-form.md) [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md) whose signature is fixed during the [variation](variation.md), the [Jacobi formula](jacobi-s-formula.md) gives $\delta\det g=(\det g)g^{ab}\delta g_{ab}$. Differentiating the square root of the absolute [determinant](determinant.md) proves the displayed identity. Expressed through the [variation of inverse metric](variation-of-inverse-metric.md), the formula is $-\sqrt{|\det g|}\,g_{ab}\delta g^{ab}/2$. Absolute values accommodate either Riemannian or Lorentzian signature.
