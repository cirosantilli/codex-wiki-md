# Counit

↑ **Parent:** [Comonoid](comonoid.md)

The structure map $\varepsilon:C\to I$ of a [comonoid](comonoid.md), with $(\varepsilon\otimes1)\Delta$ and $(1\otimes\varepsilon)\Delta$ equal to the inverse [unitors](unitor.md). This is distinct from the counit of an [adjunction](adjoint-functors.md).
