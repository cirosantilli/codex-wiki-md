# Period invariant for constant-fraction donor-wind mass loss

↑ **Parent:** [Donor-wind angular-momentum loss](donor-wind-angular-momentum-loss.md)

For a slowly evolving [circular orbit](circular-orbit.md) with constant retained fraction $f$ and [donor-wind angular-momentum loss](donor-wind-angular-momentum-loss.md), logarithmic differentiation of the [circular-binary orbital angular momentum](circular-binary-orbital-angular-momentum.md) gives $d\log P=-3f\,d\log M_d-3\,d\log M_a-2\,d\log(M_d+M_a)$. Integration gives the displayed invariant. At $f=1$ it becomes the [period-product invariant for conservative mass transfer](period-product-invariant-for-conservative-mass-transfer.md), while at $f=0$ it becomes $PM^2=\mathrm{constant}$. A varying retained fraction does not give this simple power-law invariant.
