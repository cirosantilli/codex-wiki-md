# Superlevel Caccioppoli inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Caccioppoli inequality](caccioppoli-inequality.md)

For a [weak solution](weak-solution.md) of $-\operatorname{div}(A\nabla u)=0$ with real symmetric coefficients satisfying the two-sided [uniform ellipticity](uniformly-elliptic-operator.md) bounds $\lambda I\leq A\leq\Lambda I$, the displayed [Caccioppoli inequality](caccioppoli-inequality.md) holds with $C=4\Lambda^2/\lambda^2$ for a smooth [cutoff function](cutoff-function.md) $\varphi$ of [compact support](compact-support.md). Put $w=(u-k)^+$ and test the [weak formulation](weak-formulation.md) with $\varphi^2w$. The [Sobolev chain rule](sobolev-chain-rule.md) gives $\nabla w=\mathbf1_{\{u>k\}}\nabla u$, and [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) yields $\lambda\int\varphi^2|\nabla w|^2\leq2\Lambda(\int\varphi^2|\nabla w|^2)^{1/2}(\int w^2|\nabla\varphi|^2)^{1/2}$. Divide and square. The constant is independent of the level $k$.
