# Product state

↑ **Parent:** [Reduced density matrix](reduced-density-matrix.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Product_state)

A bipartite [density operator](density-matrix-split.md) is a product state when it factors as $\rho_A\otimes\rho_B$. A pure product state factors as $|\psi_A\rangle\otimes|\psi_B\rangle$; its [reduced density matrices](reduced-density-matrix.md) have rank one and zero [entanglement entropy](entanglement-entropy.md). Mixed product states can have nonzero marginal [Von Neumann entropy](von-neumann-entropy-split.md).
