# Curved area of a spherical cap

↑ **Parent:** [Spherical cap](spherical-cap.md)

For a [sphere](sphere.md) of [radius](radius.md) $R>0$, the [spherical cap](spherical-cap.md) above $z=R-h$, with $0\leq h\leq2R$, has curved [surface area](surface-area.md) $2\pi Rh$. On the surface use $r(z)=\sqrt{R^2-z^2}$. The [surface of revolution](surface-of-revolution.md) area element is $2\pi r\sqrt{1+(dr/dz)^2}\,dz=2\pi R\,dz$. Integrating from $R-h$ to $R$ proves the formula. It includes only the spherical surface; if the planar closing disk is required, add $\pi(2Rh-h^2)$. The coordinate expression is interpreted by a [limit](limit-of-a-function.md) at a pole.
