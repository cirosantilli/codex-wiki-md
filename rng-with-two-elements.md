# Rng with two elements

↑ **Parent:** [Rng (algebra)](rng-algebra.md)

Up to an operation-preserving [bijection](bijection.md), there are two [rngs with two elements](rng-with-two-elements.md). Write the elements as $0,u$. The additive [abelian group](abelian-group.md) must satisfy $u+u=0$. The [distributive property](distributive-property.md) forces all products involving zero to vanish, leaving only the choice $u^2=0$ or $u^2=u$. The first gives a [zero-multiplication rng](zero-multiplication-rng.md); the second gives the [prime field](prime-field.md) $\mathbb F_2$, with identity $u$. Both satisfy the axioms, and they are not isomorphic because an isomorphism must send the unique nonzero element to the unique nonzero element and preserve its square. In particular, **there is just one unital [ring](ring.md) with two elements**.
