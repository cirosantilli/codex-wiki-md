# Band sum

↑ **Parent:** [Smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md)

A band sum joins two embedded spheres or links by a narrow tube along an arc, removing a small disk from each and joining their boundaries. In a [handle slide](handle-slide.md), the construction uses a parallel framed copy of the second [attaching sphere](attaching-sphere.md) and transports its [framing of an embedded sphere](framing-of-an-embedded-sphere.md) along the band.
