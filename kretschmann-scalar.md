# Kretschmann scalar

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann curvature tensor](riemann-curvature-tensor.md)

The Kretschmann scalar is the scalar [tensor contraction](tensor-contraction.md) of two copies of the [Riemann curvature tensor](riemann-curvature-tensor.md). In [Schwarzschild spacetime](schwarzschild-spacetime.md), with metric coefficient $1-2M/r$ and $G=c=1$, it is $48M^2/r^6$. Divergence of this scalar along a curve approaching a proposed regular point proves that there is no twice continuously differentiable, nondegenerate metric extension through that point: such an extension would have continuous finite curvature components and hence a finite scalar contraction. This gives a coordinate-independent test of a [curvature singularity](curvature-singularity.md). Boundedness of this single scalar does not prove regularity.
