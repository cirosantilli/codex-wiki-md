# Group of Hamiltonian diffeomorphisms

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectomorphism](symplectomorphism.md)

This [group](group-split.md) consists of time-one maps of [Hamiltonian isotopies](hamiltonian-isotopy.md) whose flows are defined throughout the specified time interval. With $\iota_{X_H}\omega=-dH$, composition of isotopies generated by $H_t,K_t$ has generator $H_t+K_t\circ\phi_t^{-1}$, where $\phi_t$ is the first isotopy. The inverse path has generator $-H_t\circ\phi_t$. These formulas prove closure under composition and inversion. On a closed [symplectic manifold](symplectic-manifold.md), every smooth time-dependent Hamiltonian has a complete flow over a bounded time interval.
