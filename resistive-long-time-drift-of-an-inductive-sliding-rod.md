# Resistive long-time drift of an inductive sliding rod

↑ **Parent:** [Inductive oscillation of a sliding rod on inclined rails](inductive-oscillation-of-a-sliding-rod-on-inclined-rails.md)

For positive loop resistance, coupled mechanical and circuit equations relax toward constant sliding [speed](speed.md) on sufficiently long rails. The steady current balances the downhill gravitational [force](force-split.md), and [motional electromotive force](motional-electromotive-force.md) balances the resistive [voltage](voltage.md). The undamped zero-resistance limit has no relaxation, so its late-time limit is singular.
