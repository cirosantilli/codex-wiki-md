# Invariant differential of a formal group law

↑ **Parent:** [Formal group law](formal-group-law.md)

For a one-dimensional [formal group law](formal-group-law.md), write $f(T)=\partial_YF(T,0)$. Its invariant differential is $dT/f(T)$. Differentiating associativity gives $\partial_YF(X,Y)f(Y)=f(F(X,Y))$, which expresses invariance under translation. In characteristic zero, integrating this differential constructs the [formal logarithm](formal-logarithm.md).
