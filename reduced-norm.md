# Reduced norm

↑ **Parent:** [Central simple algebra](central-simple-algebra.md)

The [reduced norm](reduced-norm.md) of an element of a [central simple algebra](central-simple-algebra.md) of degree $d$ is its [determinant](determinant.md) after any splitting isomorphism to $M_d$. [Inner automorphisms of a matrix algebra](inner-automorphisms-of-a-matrix-algebra.md) preserve that [determinant](determinant.md), while Galois invariance descends its polynomial coefficients to the center. It is a multiplicative homogeneous degree-$d$ polynomial, vanishing exactly on noninvertible elements. On a [central division algebra](central-division-algebra.md) it is anisotropic.
