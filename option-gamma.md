# Option gamma

↑ **Parent:** [Black-Scholes parameter sensitivities](black-scholes-parameter-sensitivities.md)

The second derivative of an option price with respect to its underlying price. It measures the change of [option delta](option-delta.md). A [convex](convex-function.md) terminal payoff in the [Black-Scholes model](black-scholes-model.md) has nonnegative option gamma before maturity.
