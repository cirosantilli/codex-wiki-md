# D2d molecular point group

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular symmetry](molecular-symmetry.md)

An eight-element molecular [point group](point-group.md) with classes $E,2S_4,C_2,2C_2^\prime,2\sigma_d$. The vector representation decomposes as $B_2+E$.
