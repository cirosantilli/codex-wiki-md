By [Mark Newman](mark-newman.md), credited as M. E. J. Newman. Published in Contemporary Physics 46, 323–351 (2005). The inspected arXiv version 3 was revised 29 May 2006; version 1 was submitted 1 December 2004. These preprint dates are distinct from the journal publication year.

Preprint: [https://arxiv.org/pdf/cond-mat/0412004](https://arxiv.org/pdf/cond-mat/0412004)

Cited passages: §II, pp. 4–5, equations (3)–(5), density and survival exponents and fitting caveat; §III.D, pp. 10–11, equations (24)–(28), fraction of a total held by an upper population fraction. Its density exponent is one greater than the positive survival exponent used for the [Pareto distribution](pareto-distribution.md).
