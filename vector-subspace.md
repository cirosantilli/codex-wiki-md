# Vector subspace

↑ **Parent:** [Vector space](vector-space-split.md)

A vector subspace is a nonempty subset of a [vector space](vector-space-split.md) closed under [vector addition](vector-addition.md) and [scalar multiplication](scalar-multiplication.md), and is therefore itself a [vector space](vector-space-split.md). Equivalently, whenever $u,v$ belong to the subset, so does $au+bv$ for all allowed [scalars](scalar.md) $a,b$. In particular it contains the [zero vector](zero-vector.md).

For example, the plane $W=\{(x,y,z):x+y+z=0\}\subseteq\mathbb R^3$ is a [vector subspace](vector-subspace.md), with [basis](basis.md) $(1,-1,0),(1,0,-1)$. Indeed every element is $(-y)(1,-1,0)+(-z)(1,0,-1)$. The parallel plane $x+y+z=1$ misses the [zero vector](zero-vector.md) and is an [affine subspace](affine-subspace.md) instead. Homogeneous constraints produce [vector subspaces](vector-subspace.md): $W$ is the [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) of the [linear map](linear-map.md) $(x,y,z)\mapsto x+y+z$. This lets us describe all solutions of a homogeneous [linear equation](linear-equation.md) through a [basis](basis.md) of its [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md).
