# Functional programming, design and analysis of algorithms examination paper by the University of Oxford (2014)

↑ **Parent:** [Computer science bibliography](computer-science-bibliography-split.md)

[Released examination archive](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/past_exam_papers_for_1st_year_of/response/1398416/attach/2/Fakour%20Zip%20Final%201.zip?cookie_passthrough=1), member `4208.pdf`. The original eight-page paper identifies the preliminary examination held on 17 June 2014. Questions 1–4 concern [functional programming](functional-programming.md) and Questions 5–8 concern [analysis of algorithms](analysis-of-algorithms.md). Question 5(b) permits signed [integers](integer-split.md) but prints a bound containing $\log_2 m$; for nonpositive inputs the meaningful extension uses $\log_2\max(2,|m|)$.
