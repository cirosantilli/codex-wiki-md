# Restriction ampleness implies semiampleness for an effective divisor

↑ **Parent:** [Semiample divisor](semiample-divisor.md)

If $E$ is an [effective Cartier divisor](effective-cartier-divisor.md) on a [projective scheme](projective-scheme.md) and $\mathcal O_E(E)$ is [ample](ample-line-bundle.md), then $E$ is [semiample](semiample-divisor.md). The [divisor restriction exact sequence](divisor-restriction-exact-sequence.md) and [Serre vanishing](serre-vanishing.md) make $H^1(X,(m-1)E)\to H^1(X,mE)$ surjective for large $m$. Their finite dimensions stabilize, so restriction on [global sections](global-section.md) is eventually surjective. Lift generators on $E$; off $E$, the canonical section of $mE$ generates. Together these generate $\mathcal O_X(mE)$, including on nonreduced $X$.
