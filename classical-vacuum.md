# Classical vacuum

↑ **Parent:** [Vacuum manifold](vacuum-manifold.md)

A spatially homogeneous field value minimizing the potential energy. For a [finite-energy field configuration](finite-energy-field-configuration.md) on an infinite domain, the potential is normally shifted so the relevant vacuum energy density is zero. Disconnected components of the [vacuum manifold](vacuum-manifold.md) allow a [scalar-field kink](scalar-field-kink.md) to connect distinct vacuum values.
