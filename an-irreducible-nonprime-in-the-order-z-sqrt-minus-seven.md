# An irreducible nonprime in the order Z sqrt minus seven

↑ **Parent:** [Irreducible element](irreducible-element.md)

The multiplicative [field norm](field-norm.md) $N(m+n\sqrt{-7})=m^2+7n^2$ is an integer. [Units](unit-in-a-ring.md) have [field norm](field-norm.md) one and are exactly $\pm1$. A nontrivial [factorization](factorization.md) of 2 would force both factors to have [field norm](field-norm.md) two, which the displayed quadratic form cannot represent. Thus 2 is an [irreducible element](irreducible-element.md). It is not a [prime element](prime-element.md), since it divides $(1+\sqrt{-7})(1-\sqrt{-7})=8$ but neither factor. This is an example in a nonmaximal quadratic order, rather than the full [ring](ring.md) of integers of $\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-7})$; the precise [ring](ring.md) matters for [factorization](factorization.md).
