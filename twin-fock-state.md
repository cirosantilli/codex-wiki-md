# Twin-Fock state

↑ **Parent:** [Fock state](fock-state.md)

A product of [number states](number-state.md) with equal fixed populations in two modes. Its total particle number is $2N$. It differs from a pair of [coherent states](coherent-state.md), whose number [distributions](distribution-mathematical-analysis.md) fluctuate.
