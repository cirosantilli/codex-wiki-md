# Muon-antimuon annihilation into two photons

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum electrodynamics](quantum-electrodynamics.md)

At tree level this [QED](quantum-electrodynamics.md) process has two internal [muon](muon.md) exchange diagrams, differing by the order of the two outgoing [photon](photon.md) vertices. Their propagator momenta are $p_1-k_1$ and $p_1-k_2$ for incoming [muon](muon.md) momentum $p_1$. The amplitude begins at order $e^2$ and obeys the [two-photon fermion Ward identity](two-photon-fermion-ward-identity.md). Identical final [photons](photon.md) require the usual factorial correction when integration over the full final-state [Lorentz-invariant phase space](lorentz-invariant-phase-space.md) would otherwise count them twice.
