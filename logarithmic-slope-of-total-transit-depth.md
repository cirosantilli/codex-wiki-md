# Logarithmic slope of total transit depth

↑ **Parent:** [Scattering slope of a transmission spectrum](scattering-slope-of-a-transmission-spectrum.md)

For $\delta=A+B(1-e^{-\tau})$ and $\tau\propto\lambda^{-p}$, the logarithmic slope is $-pB\tau e^{-\tau}/\delta$. The opaque planetary baseline generally dilutes the slope. In the [optically thin](optically-thin-medium.md) limit, the atmospheric excess $\delta-A$ has logarithmic slope $-p$.
