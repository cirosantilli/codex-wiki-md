# Spectral projection of the Stokes operator

↑ **Parent:** [Stokes operator](stokes-operator.md)

The spectral projection $P_N$ retains the first $N$ Stokes eigenfunctions. On its finite-dimensional range, one derivative costs at most $\lambda_N^{1/2}$ and all Sobolev norms are equivalent.
