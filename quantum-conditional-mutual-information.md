# Quantum conditional mutual information

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum mutual information](quantum-mutual-information.md)

For a tripartite [density operator](density-matrix.md), the quantum conditional mutual information is $I(A:C|B)=S(AB)+S(BC)-S(B)-S(ABC)$. It is nonnegative by [Strong subadditivity of Von Neumann entropy](strong-subadditivity-of-quantum-entropy.md). It measures the [coherent information](coherent-information.md) lost when a channel environment is discarded after an isometric dilation.
