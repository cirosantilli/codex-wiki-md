# Power of a Brinkman sheet

↑ **Parent:** [Brinkman swimming sheet](brinkman-swimming-sheet.md)

For $s=\sqrt{1+(\alpha/k)^2}$, the leading work per projected area on one side of a transverse [Brinkman swimming sheet](brinkman-swimming-sheet.md) is $\mu b^2k\omega^2s(s+1)/2$. Two fluid sides double it. Work includes [viscous dissipation](viscous-dissipation.md) and drag against the matrix, giving a greater fixed-stroke cost than in the unscreened fluid.
