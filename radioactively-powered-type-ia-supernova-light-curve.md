# Radioactively powered Type Ia supernova light curve

↑ **Parent:** [Type Ia supernova](type-ia-supernova.md)

The nickel-56 to cobalt-56 to iron-56 decay chain supplies gamma rays and charged particles that heat supernova ejecta. Early expansion and [photon](photon.md) diffusion delay the escaping optical light; after a peak on a scale of weeks, the [luminosity](luminosity.md) falls as decay power declines and gamma rays escape more readily. With comparable diffusion times and deposition efficiencies, peak [luminosity](luminosity.md) scales approximately with the nickel-56 [mass](mass.md). Using one fixed absolute [luminosity](luminosity.md) without accounting for composition-dependent yields can therefore bias standard-candle distances.
