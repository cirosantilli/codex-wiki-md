# Spectrification

↑ **Parent:** [Indexed prespectrum](indexed-prespectrum.md)

In the genuine indexed point-set model, [spectrification](spectrification.md) $L$ is the left adjoint to the forgetful functor from indexed spectra to [indexed prespectra](indexed-prespectrum.md). An indexed spectrum has adjoint structure maps $E(V)\to\Omega^{W\ominus V}E(W)$ that are homeomorphisms. Applying $L$ to a cellular [indexed prespectrum](indexed-prespectrum.md) supplies the associated genuine [topological spectrum](spectrum-topology.md).
