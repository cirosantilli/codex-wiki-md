# DNA methylation

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular genetics](molecular-genetics-split.md)

Covalent methyl-group modification of [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md), involved in regulation and cellular memory. Its relationship to transcription depends on genomic location and biological context.
