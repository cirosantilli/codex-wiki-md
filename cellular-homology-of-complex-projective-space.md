# Cellular homology of complex projective space

↑ **Parent:** [Complex projective space](complex-projective-space.md)

The standard [CW complex](cw-complex.md) structure on [Complex projective space](complex-projective-space.md) has one cell in every even dimension from zero through $2n$, and no odd-dimensional cells. Its [cellular chain complex](cellular-chain-complex.md) therefore has zero differential. The [fundamental classes](fundamental-class.md) of the inclusions $\mathbb{CP}^j\hookrightarrow\mathbb{CP}^n$ generate its even-degree [integral homology](integral-homology.md); all other groups vanish.
