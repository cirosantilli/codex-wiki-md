# Fourier-support almost orthogonality

↑ **Parent:** [Decoupling inequality](decoupling-inequality.md)

Functions with pairwise disjoint, or uniformly finitely overlapping, Fourier supports are almost orthogonal in $L^2$ by the [Plancherel theorem](plancherel-theorem.md).
