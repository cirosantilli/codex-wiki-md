# Proportional-infall model of galactic chemical evolution

↑ **Parent:** [Galactic chemical evolution](galactic-chemical-evolution.md)

With well-mixed [gas-phase metallicity](gas-phase-metallicity.md) $Z$, zero mass return, [star formation rate](star-formation-rate.md) $\psi$, pristine [galactic gas inflow](galactic-gas-inflow.md) $\nu\psi$, and constant [stellar yield](stellar-yield.md) $y$, [mass conservation](mass-conservation.md) and metal accounting give $\dot M=-(1-\nu)\psi$ and $M\dot Z=(y-\nu Z)\psi$. Thus, for $0<\nu<1$,

$$
Z=\frac y\nu+\left(Z_0-\frac y\nu\right)(M/M_0)^{\nu/(1-\nu)}.
$$

The initial [gas-phase metallicity](gas-phase-metallicity.md) $Z_0=0$ gives the usual pristine-infall expression. Neglecting returned bulk mass while retaining a nonzero metal yield is a trace-metal idealization.
