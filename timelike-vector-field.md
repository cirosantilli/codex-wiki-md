# Timelike vector field

↑ **Parent:** [Timelike vector](timelike-vector.md)

A [timelike vector field](timelike-vector-field.md) assigns a timelike vector continuously or smoothly to every event in its domain. A globally defined such field gives a [time orientation](time-orientation.md), but it need not be a [Killing vector field](killing-vector-field.md) or a [timelike geodesic vector field](timelike-geodesic-vector-field.md).
