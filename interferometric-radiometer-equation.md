# Interferometric radiometer equation

↑ **Parent:** [Radio interferometry](radio-interferometry.md)

For $N$ equal [antennas](antenna-radio.md), weak-source independent receiver noise, natural weighting and total on-source time $t$, the point-source rms in one [polarization of waves](polarization-waves.md) is the displayed value. There are $N(N-1)/2$ independent cross-correlation baselines, each with real-component error $\mathrm{SEFD}/\sqrt{2\Delta\nu t}$. Combining $n_{\rm pol}$ independent [polarization of waves](polarization-waves.md) estimates reduces the rms by $\sqrt{n_{\rm pol}}$. Correlation losses divide the sensitivity by an efficiency factor; weighting, flagging and an extended source change the effective sensitivity.[slides 7–12 and 20, baseline and image sensitivity](sensitivity-by-joan-wrobel.md)
