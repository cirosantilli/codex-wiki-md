# Dolbeault closedness of Chern curvature

↑ **Parent:** [Chern connection](chern-connection.md)

In a [holomorphic local frame](holomorphic-local-trivialization.md) the Chern matrix is $A=H^{-1}\partial H$. The identity $\partial A+A\wedge A=0$ gives [vector-bundle curvature](curvature-form.md) $\Theta=\bar\partial A$ of type $(1,1)$. Therefore $\bar\partial_{\operatorname{End}E}\Theta=0$, allowing the [vector-bundle curvature](curvature-form.md) to represent a bundle-valued Dolbeault class.
