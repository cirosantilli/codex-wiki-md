# Generator bound from a cyclic-factor series

↑ **Parent:** [Polycyclic group](polycyclic-group.md)

If a [group](group-split.md) has a cyclic-factor [subnormal series](subnormal-series.md) of length $n$, choose one lift of a generator of each nontrivial factor. Induction gives $G_i=\langle G_{i-1},g_i\rangle$, so the chosen lifts generate $G$. Thus at most $n$ generators are needed. Applying this to the intersection chain in [subgroups of polycyclic groups are polycyclic](subgroups-of-polycyclic-groups-are-polycyclic.md) bounds the number of generators of every subgroup by the same series length.
