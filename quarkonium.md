# Quarkonium

↑ **Parent:** [Meson](meson.md)

A [quarkonium](quarkonium.md) is a [bound state](bound-state.md) of a heavy [quark](quark.md) and its own [antiquark](antiquark.md). Examples are [charmonium](charmonium.md) and the bottom-antibottom states called [Upsilon mesons](upsilon-meson.md). Spectroscopic labels $n\,{}^{2S+1}L_J$ distinguish radial excitation, total quark [spin](spin.md), relative [orbital angular momentum](orbital-angular-momentum.md) and total [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md).
