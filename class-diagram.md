# Class diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Unified Modeling Language](unified-modeling-language.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Class_diagram)

A structural diagram showing [software classes](class-programming.md) or [object interfaces](object-interface.md), their attributes and operations, and relationships such as [class inheritance](class-inheritance.md) and associations. A hollow triangle points toward a more general classifier; a dashed realization line distinguishes implementation of an interface. A plain association does not assert exclusive lifetime ownership of the referenced object.
