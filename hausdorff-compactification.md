# Hausdorff compactification

↑ **Parent:** [Topology](topology-split.md)

A Hausdorff compactification is a compact [Hausdorff space](hausdorff-space.md) $K$ with a [topological embedding](topological-embedding.md) $j:X\to K$ whose image is dense. It retains the original topology on $X$ while adjoining boundary points. For a locally compact noncompact Hausdorff space, its [one-point compactification](alexandroff-extension.md) is an example. For every [Tychonoff space](completely-regular-hausdorff-space.md), the [Stone-Čech compactification](stone-cech-compactification.md) extends all continuous maps into compact Hausdorff targets.
