# Liouville height inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Absolute multiplicative Weil height](absolute-multiplicative-weil-height.md)

If $\gamma\ne0$ is algebraic of degree at most $D$, then every embedding satisfies

$$
|\gamma|\geq H(\gamma)^{-D}.
$$

Indeed, $H(\gamma^{-1})=H(\gamma)$ and the contribution of one Archimedean place to the height bounds $|\gamma|^{-1/D}$.
