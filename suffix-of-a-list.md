# Suffix of a list

↑ **Parent:** [List (abstract data type)](list-abstract-data-type.md)

A [list](list-abstract-data-type.md) obtained by dropping an initial segment. The whole list and, for finite lists, the empty list are suffixes. Proper suffixes exclude the whole list; nonempty suffixes exclude `[]`. In a [linked list](linked-list.md), suffixes can share the original nodes, permitting a list of all suffixes to be constructed with linear additional space.
