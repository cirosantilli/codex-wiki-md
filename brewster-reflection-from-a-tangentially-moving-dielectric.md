# Brewster reflection from a tangentially moving dielectric

↑ **Parent:** [Brewster's angle](brewster-s-angle.md)

A [vacuum](vacuum.md) ray incident normally on a [dielectric](dielectric.md) moving parallel to its surface becomes oblique in the material rest frame, with $\sin i=|\beta|$. For a transparent nonmagnetic [dielectric](dielectric.md), the rest-frame [Brewster angle](brewster-s-angle.md) satisfies $\tan i_B=n$, giving $|\beta_B|=n/\sqrt{1+n^2}$. The reflected [electric field](electric-field-split.md) is perpendicular to the plane containing the ray and the velocity. A real [refractive index](refractive-index.md) $n\ne1$ is assumed; [dispersion](wave-dispersion.md) makes the condition implicit through the rest-frame [frequency](frequency.md), and [absorption](absorption-electromagnetic-radiation.md) generally removes exact extinction.
