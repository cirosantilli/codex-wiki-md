# Coupled Ising fixed points near four dimensions

↑ **Parent:** [Epsilon expansion](epsilon-expansion.md)

The quartic interaction $g_1\phi_1^4+g_2\phi_2^4+\lambda\phi_1^2\phi_2^2$ includes the [Gaussian fixed point](gaussian-fixed-point.md), single and double [Ising model](ising-model.md) [Wilson-Fisher fixed points](wilson-fisher-fixed-point.md), and an [O(N) model](o-n-model.md) fixed point with $N=2$. A rotation of two decoupled identical [Ising models](ising-model.md) produces a second coordinate representation of their [renormalization-group fixed point](renormalization-group-fixed-point.md).
