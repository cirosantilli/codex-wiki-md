# Paranthropus boisei

↑ **Parent:** [Paranthropus](paranthropus.md)

An East African [hominin](hominin.md) with very large postcanine [teeth](tooth.md) and a robust feeding apparatus. [dental microwear](dental-microwear.md) and enamel carbon-isotope evidence complicate a simple inference of habitual hard-object feeding from gross anatomy. [PLoS ONE 3, e2044; seven specimens with preserved microwear and comparison with gross anatomy](dental-microwear-and-diet-of-the-plio-pleistocene-hominin-paranthropus-boisei-by-peter-s-ungar-and-others.md)
