# Hadley cell

↑ **Parent:** [Meteorology](meteorology.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hadley_cell)

A tropical atmospheric overturning circulation with rising air near the equator, poleward flow aloft, subtropical descent and an equatorward lower return. In its ideal upper branch, [conservation of angular momentum](conservation-of-angular-momentum.md) gives $u=\Omega a\sin^2\phi/\cos\phi$ for air initially at rest relative to the equatorial surface. [Eddies](eddy.md) and [friction](friction.md) modify this idealization.
