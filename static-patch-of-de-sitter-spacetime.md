# Static patch of de Sitter spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [de Sitter spacetime](de-sitter-spacetime.md)

This region $0\leq r<R$ of [de Sitter spacetime](de-sitter-spacetime.md) is static for the central observer, with a cosmological [Killing horizon](killing-horizon.md) at $r=R$. The central [proper time](proper-time.md) fixes the normalization of $\partial_t$. Static observers have acceleration $r/(R^2\sqrt{1-r^2/R^2})$. Horizon-crossing null coordinates remove the coordinate singularity, while the global extension has compact spherical spatial slices and spacelike past/future conformal infinity.
