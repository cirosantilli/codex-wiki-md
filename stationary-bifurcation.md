# Stationary bifurcation

↑ **Parent:** [Bifurcation theory](bifurcation-theory.md)

At a stationary bifurcation, a real [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) of the linearized system passes through zero. Nearby equilibria may then be created, destroyed, or exchange stability.
