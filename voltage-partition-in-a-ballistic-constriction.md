# Voltage partition in a ballistic constriction

↑ **Parent:** [Nonlinear conductance of a quantum point contact](nonlinear-conductance-of-a-quantum-point-contact.md)

A channel supporting [ballistic electron transport](ballistic-electron-transport.md) carries incident populations set by different source and drain [chemical potentials](chemical-potential-split.md), rather than an everywhere-equilibrated local distribution with an Ohmic [voltage](voltage.md) gradient. Its electrostatic barrier responds self-consistently to contacts, gates and [electrostatic screening](electrostatic-screening.md). A symmetric rigid-barrier model places reservoir energies at $E_F\pm eV/2$ relative to the barrier. Asymmetric partition or bias-dependent transmission changes the weights in the differential [electrical conductance](electrical-conductance.md), and must be included when extracting an [energy](energy-split.md) scale.
