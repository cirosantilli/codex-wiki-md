# Taxonomy (biology)

↑ **Parent:** [Biology](biology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taxonomy_(biology))

[Taxonomy](taxonomy-biology.md) names and classifies [organisms](organism-split.md). Classification can organize known diversity and make comparisons useful, but ranks and names are not themselves evidence of ancestry. [Phylogenetics](phylogenetics.md) infers that ancestry; a [clade](clade.md) includes an ancestor and all its descendants. A shared feature such as lacking a [cell nucleus](cell-nucleus.md) need not identify an exclusive [clade](clade.md). The [organism](organism-split.md) branch here organizes cell types for reading and is not a proposed [phylogenetic tree](phylogenetic-tree.md). See [OpenStax Biology 2e, 20.1: classification and evolutionary history](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/20-1-organizing-life-on-earth).
