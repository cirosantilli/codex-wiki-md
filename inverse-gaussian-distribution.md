# Inverse Gaussian distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Exponential family](exponential-family-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inverse_Gaussian_distribution)

For an [expected value](expected-value.md) parameter $\mu>0$ and a [shape parameter](shape-parameter.md) $\lambda>0$, the inverse Gaussian distribution has [probability density function](probability-density-function.md)

$$
f(x;\mu,\lambda)
=\left(\frac{\lambda}{2\pi x^3}\right)^{1/2}
\exp\left(-\frac{\lambda(x-\mu)^2}{2\mu^2x}\right),
\qquad x>0.
$$

Its mean is $\mu$ and its variance is $\mu^3/\lambda$.
