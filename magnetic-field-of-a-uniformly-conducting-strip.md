# Magnetic field of a uniformly conducting strip

↑ **Parent:** [Biot-Savart law](biot-savart-law.md)

For an infinitely long thin strip of width $w$ carrying uniform longitudinal current $I$, the [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) a perpendicular [distance](distance.md) $z>0$ above its centre has magnitude $B=\mu_0I\arctan(w/(2z))/(\pi w)$. Its near-sheet limit is $\mu_0I/(2w)$ and its distant line-current limit is $\mu_0I/(2\pi z)$.
