# Concavity of quantum conditional entropy

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum conditional entropy](quantum-conditional-entropy.md)

For bipartite [density operators](density-matrix.md),

$$
H(A|B)_{p\rho+(1-p)\sigma}\geq pH(A|B)_\rho+(1-p)H(A|B)_\sigma.
$$

This is a consequence of [Strong subadditivity of Von Neumann entropy](strong-subadditivity-of-quantum-entropy.md).
