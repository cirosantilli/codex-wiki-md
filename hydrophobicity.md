# Hydrophobicity

↑ **Parent:** [Physical chemistry](physical-chemistry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrophobicity)

[Hydrophobicity](hydrophobicity.md) describes unfavorable mixing with [water](water.md), as illustrated by long [hydrocarbon](hydrocarbon.md) regions. The [hydrophobic](hydrophobicity.md) regions of membrane-forming [phospholipids](phospholipid.md) face the interior of a [lipid bilayer](lipid-bilayer.md). This organization is explained by the [hydrophobic effect](hydrophobic-effect.md), rather than by imagining that chains actively repel [water](water.md). See [IUPAC Gold Book: hydrophobic interaction](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/H02907).
