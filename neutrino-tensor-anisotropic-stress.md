# Neutrino tensor anisotropic stress

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor cosmological perturbation](tensor-cosmological-perturbation.md)

For massless [neutrinos](neutrino.md) with a temperature perturbation $\Theta$ and background [energy density](energy-density.md) $\bar\rho_\nu$, the physical trace-free spatial [stress-energy tensor](stress-energy-tensor.md) is

$$
\pi_\nu^{ij}=4\bar\rho_\nu\int\frac{d\Omega_e}{4\pi}
\Theta\left(e^ie^j-\frac13\delta^{ij}\right).
$$

Some conventions define $\Pi^{ij}=-\pi_\nu^{ij}$ and correspondingly reverse the sign of the stress source in the gravitational-wave equation.
