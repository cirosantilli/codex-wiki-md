# 2-adic metric

↑ **Parent:** [2-adic absolute value](2-adic-absolute-value.md)

The 2-adic metric is $d_2(x,y)=|x-y|_2$, where $|x|_2=2^{-v_2(x)}$ for $x\ne0$ and $|0|_2=0$. Closeness means divisibility of the difference by a large power of two.
