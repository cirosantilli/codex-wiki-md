# Reexperiencing

↑ **Parent:** [Post-traumatic stress disorder](post-traumatic-stress-disorder.md)

Involuntary recurrence of aspects of a traumatic experience, potentially including sensory impressions and a sense of current occurrence. It should be distinguished from deliberate recollection, [rumination](rumination.md) and a future-oriented image.
