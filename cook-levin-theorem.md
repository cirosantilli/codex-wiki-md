# Cook-Levin theorem

↑ **Parent:** [NP-completeness](np-completeness.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cook–Levin_theorem)

Every [NP](np-complexity.md) verifier can be compiled into a polynomial-size [Boolean circuit](boolean-circuit.md) whose inputs encode its [certificate](certificate-complexity.md), and then into an equisatisfiable [conjunctive normal form](conjunctive-normal-form.md) by a [Tseitin transformation](tseytin-transformation.md). This gives a [polynomial-time many-one reduction](polynomial-time-many-one-reduction.md) from every [NP](np-complexity.md) language to [SAT](boolean-satisfiability-problem.md). Checking a guessed assignment proves membership, so [SAT](boolean-satisfiability-problem.md) is [NP-complete](np-completeness.md).
