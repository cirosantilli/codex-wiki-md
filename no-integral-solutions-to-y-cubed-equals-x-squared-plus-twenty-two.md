# No integral solutions to y cubed equals x squared plus twenty-two

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal class group of Q of square root of minus twenty-two](ideal-class-group-of-q-of-square-root-of-minus-twenty-two.md)

The equation $y^3=x^2+22$ has no integer solutions. Factoring it in $\mathbb Z[\sqrt{-22}]$, the two conjugate factors are coprime. [Unique factorization of ideals in a number field](unique-factorization-of-ideals-in-a-number-field.md) and the absence of three-torsion in the class group force $x+\sqrt{-22}$ to be a cube up to a unit. Writing it as $(a+b\sqrt{-22})^3$ makes its $\sqrt{-22}$ coefficient

$$
b(3a^2-22b^2)=1,
$$

which has no integer solution.
