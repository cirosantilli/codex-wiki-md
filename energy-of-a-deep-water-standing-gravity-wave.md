# Energy of a deep-water standing gravity wave

↑ **Parent:** [Deep-water gravity wave](deep-water-gravity-wave.md)

For elevation $h_0\sin(\omega t)\sin(kx)$, depth-integrated [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) per surface [area](surface-area.md) is $\rho h_0^2\omega^2\cos^2(\omega t)/(4k)$. Wavelength-averaged excess gravitational [energy](energy-split.md) is $\rho gh_0^2\sin^2(\omega t)/4$. Conservation gives $\omega^2=gk$ in the inviscid gravity-only model.
