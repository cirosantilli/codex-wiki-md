# SEC chromatogram

↑ **Parent:** [Size-exclusion chromatography](size-exclusion-chromatography.md)

A [SEC chromatogram](sec-chromatogram.md) plots a detector signal against elapsed time or elution [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md). At constant [volumetric flow rate](volumetric-flow-rate.md) $Q$, the volume delivered after injection is $Q(t-t_{\rm injection})$; subtraction of extra-column transport volume is needed for a column-only $V_e$. Real peak widths include injection volume, column dispersion, and tubing/detector spreading. Peak position, width, and area answer different questions.
