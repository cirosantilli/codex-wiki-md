# Dirichlet process

↑ **Parent:** [Bayesian statistics](bayesian-statistics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dirichlet_process)

A Dirichlet process is a distribution over probability measures such that the masses assigned to every finite measurable partition have a [Dirichlet distribution](dirichlet-distribution.md). Its draws are almost surely discrete.
