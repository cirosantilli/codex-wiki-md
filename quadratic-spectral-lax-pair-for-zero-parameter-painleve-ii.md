<h1 id="quadratic-spectral-lax-pair-for-zero-parameter-painleve-ii">Quadratic spectral Lax pair for zero-parameter Painlevé II</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Lax pair](lax-pair.md)

Let $A$ have diagonal entries $\pm(\lambda^2+w+z/2)$ and off-diagonal entries $u\lambda+u'$ and $-2w\lambda/u+2u'w/u^2$. Let $B$ have diagonal entries $\pm\lambda/2$ and off-diagonal entries $u/2,-w/u$. Substitution into the [isomonodromic deformation](isomonodromic-deformation.md) compatibility identity gives the displayed equations on domains with $u\ne0$. Thus $(w/u^2)'=0$, so $w=Cu^2$ and $u''+Cu^3+zu/2=0$, a rescaled zero-parameter [Painlevé II equation](painleve-ii-equation.md) when $C\ne0$. The leading coefficient $-2w/u$ is required by cancellation of the quadratic spectral terms.
