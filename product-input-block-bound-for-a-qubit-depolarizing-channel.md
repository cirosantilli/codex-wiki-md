# Product-input block bound for a qubit depolarizing channel

↑ **Parent:** [Holevo capacity of a qubit depolarizing channel](holevo-capacity-of-a-qubit-depolarizing-channel.md)

For $\Phi(\rho)=p\rho+(1-p)I/2$, every output qubit has [Von Neumann entropy](von-neumann-entropy-split.md) at least $h((1-p)/2)$. A product input therefore has output entropy at least $n h((1-p)/2)$. The average output has entropy at most $n$, so its [Holevo quantity](holevo-quantity.md) is bounded by the displayed expression. This allows correlated classical message labels and joint output measurements, but requires product quantum inputs. The [Holevo bound](holevo-s-theorem.md) and [Fano's inequality](fano-s-inequality.md) turn it into the corresponding asymptotic rate bound.
