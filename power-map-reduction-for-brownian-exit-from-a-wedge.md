# Power-map reduction for Brownian exit from a wedge

↑ **Parent:** [Conformal invariance of planar Brownian motion](conformal-invariance-of-planar-brownian-motion.md)

The map $z\mapsto z^{\pi/\alpha}$ takes a wedge of opening $\alpha$ to a half-plane and the circular boundary of radius $r$ to radius $r^{\pi/\alpha}$. [Conformal invariance of planar Brownian motion](conformal-invariance-of-planar-brownian-motion.md) preserves which boundary part is reached first, although it changes the clock. Use the branch defined by the wedge argument; the origin is a [polar point for planar Brownian motion](polar-point-for-planar-brownian-motion.md), so localization handles the vertex.
