# Rank-one connection

↑ **Parent:** [Deformation gradient](deformation-gradient.md)

Two constant [deformation gradients](deformation-gradient.md) are rank-one connected when their difference is an [outer product](outer-product.md) $a\otimes N$. For a nonzero jump its [matrix rank](matrix-rank.md) is one; equality $A=B$ is the trivial compatible case. The vector $N$ specifies a reference interface normal, and $a$ is the jump amplitude. The [Hadamard jump condition](hadamard-jump-condition.md) identifies this algebraic condition with continuity of an affine deformation across a planar interface. The normal can be normalized by rescaling $a$ without changing the product.
