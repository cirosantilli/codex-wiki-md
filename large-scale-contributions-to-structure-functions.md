# Large-scale contributions to structure functions

↑ **Parent:** [Longitudinal velocity structure function](longitudinal-velocity-structure-function.md)

A smooth velocity varying on a scale larger than $r$ contributes $\Delta U_L\simeq r\partial_LU_L$ to a velocity increment. Higher powers produce mixed [moments](moment.md) with smaller-scale increments. [longitudinal structure functions](longitudinal-velocity-structure-function.md) therefore attenuate, but do not exactly filter out, large-scale strain and intensity modulation.
