# Frankl-Wilson theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Extremal set theory](extremal-set-theory-split.md)

Let $p$ be a [prime number](prime-number.md), let $L\subseteq\mathbb F_p$ have $s\leq\min\{k,n-k\}$ elements, and suppose $\mathcal A\subseteq[n]^{(k)}$ satisfies $|A\cap B|\bmod p\in L$ for distinct members while $k\bmod p\notin L$. The Frankl-Wilson theorem gives

$$
|\mathcal A|\leq\binom ns.
$$

Its [polynomial method in combinatorics](polynomial-method-in-combinatorics.md) turns modular intersection restrictions into linearly independent functions represented by square-free monomials of degree $s$.
