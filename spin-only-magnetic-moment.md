# Spin-only magnetic moment

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic dipole moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md)

The [spin-only magnetic moment](spin-only-magnetic-moment.md) neglects orbital contributions and approximates $g$ by two: $\mu_{\mathrm{eff}}=g\sqrt{S(S+1)}\mu_B=\sqrt{n(n+2)}\mu_B$ for $n$ parallel unpaired [electrons](electron.md). Four unpaired [electrons](electron.md) give $\sqrt{24}\mu_B\simeq4.90\mu_B$.
