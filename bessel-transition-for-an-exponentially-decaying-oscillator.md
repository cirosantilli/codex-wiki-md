# Bessel transition for an exponentially decaying oscillator

↑ **Parent:** [WKB approximation for a slowly varying oscillator](wkb-approximation-for-a-slowly-varying-oscillator.md)

An oscillator with frequency $\omega(t)=e^{-\varepsilon t}$ leaves the [WKB approximation](wkb-approximation.md) when $|\omega_t|/\omega^2=\varepsilon e^{\varepsilon t}$ becomes order one. The shifted variable $T=\varepsilon t-\log(\varepsilon^{-1})$ changes its leading undamped equation to $Y_{TT}+e^{-2T}Y=0$. With $Z=e^{-T}$ this is the order-zero [Bessel differential equation](bessel-differential-equation.md). Large-$Z$ [Bessel functions](bessel-function.md) match the earlier oscillations, while the small-$Z$ constant and logarithmic solutions become a constant and a linear function of late time.
