# Triple-junction angle in isotropic segmentation

↑ **Parent:** [Segmentation interface curvature balance](segmentation-interface-curvature-balance.md)

At a freely moving regular junction of three smooth [image edges](image-edge.md) with equal isotropic length weights, the endpoint [first variation](first-variation.md) requires the sum of their outward unit tangent vectors to vanish. Three unit vectors with zero sum have pairwise angles of $120$ degrees. Different interface weights instead give a weighted force balance, and constrained or singular junctions require separate hypotheses.
