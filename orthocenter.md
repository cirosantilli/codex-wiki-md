# Orthocenter

↑ **Parent:** [Triangle](triangle.md)

The common intersection of a [triangle](triangle.md)'s three altitude lines. Each line passes through a vertex and is [orthogonal](orthogonal-vectors.md) to the opposite side. The intersection can lie inside or outside the triangle, or at its right-angle vertex. An [orthogonal projection onto an intercept plane](orthogonal-projection-onto-an-intercept-plane.md) supplies a concrete three-dimensional construction of one.
