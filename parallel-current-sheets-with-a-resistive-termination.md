# Parallel current sheets with a resistive termination

↑ **Parent:** [Current sheet](current-sheet.md)

Two broad perfect conductors of width $w$, separated by gap $d\ll w$, carry opposite steady [electric currents](electric-current.md) $I$ and connect through a terminating [resistor](resistor.md) $R$. Away from edges, [Ampère's circuital law](ampere-s-circuital-law.md) and the [superposition principle](superposition-principle.md) give gap [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) magnitude $\mu_0I/w$. Each conductor is an [equipotential](equipotential.md), and [Ohm's law](ohm-s-law.md) sets their [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) to $IR$. Planar [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md) and charge-free [Gauss's law](gauss-s-law.md) then give uniform gap [electric field](electric-field-split.md) magnitude $IR/d$. Injection parallel to the upper current makes the electric and magnetic [Lorentz forces](lorentz-force.md) opposite. Their cancellation selects [speed](speed.md) $Rw/(\mu_0d)$ independent of nonzero [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) and of operating current; nonrelativistic [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) is $m[Rw/(\mu_0d)]^2/2$.
