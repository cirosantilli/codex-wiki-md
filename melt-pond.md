# Melt pond

↑ **Parent:** [Sea ice](sea-ice.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melt_pond)

A melt pond is liquid water collected in a depression on melting ice. Its lower [albedo](albedo.md) than snow-covered ice can strengthen [ice-albedo feedback](ice-albedo-feedback.md). Pond drainage and connected pores can redistribute an [oil spill under sea ice](oil-spill-under-sea-ice.md) during warming.
