# Elementary reaction

↑ **Parent:** [Reaction mechanism](reaction-mechanism.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elementary_reaction)

An [elementary reaction](elementary-reaction.md) is a single step in a [reaction mechanism](reaction-mechanism.md). The [law of mass action](law-of-mass-action.md) models its directional [reaction rate](reaction-rate.md) using [reactant](reactant.md) [concentrations](concentration.md) raised to the step's stoichiometric multiplicities. Applying that rule requires evidence that the step is elementary; a net [chemical equation](chemical-equation.md) alone supplies no such evidence.
