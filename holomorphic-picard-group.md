# Holomorphic Picard group

↑ **Parent:** [Picard group](picard-group.md)

The [holomorphic Picard group](holomorphic-picard-group.md) consists of isomorphism classes of [holomorphic line bundles](holomorphic-line-bundle.md), with [tensor product](tensor-product.md) as its group law. The trivial bundle is the identity and dualization gives inverses. Transition functions identify it with $H^1(X,\mathcal O_X^*)$. On the [complex projective line](complex-projective-line.md) every class is $\mathcal O(n)$, so degree gives an isomorphism with $\mathbb Z$.
