# Subnormal series

↑ **Parent:** [Normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md)

A subnormal series is a finite chain of [subgroups](subgroup.md) in which each is a [normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md) of the next. A member need not be normal in the whole [group](group-split.md). Requiring the factors to be [cyclic groups](cyclic-group.md) defines a [polycyclic group](polycyclic-group.md); allowing finite factors as well defines a [poly-(cyclic or finite) group](poly-cyclic-or-finite-group.md).
