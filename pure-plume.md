# Pure plume

↑ **Parent:** [Top-hat plume model](top-hat-plume-model.md)

A pure plume is a similarity regime in which the source supplies [buoyancy flux](buoyancy-flux.md) but no dynamically persistent source volume or momentum flux. Its finite source region is represented by a [plume virtual origin](plume-virtual-origin.md).
