# Ortho-para conversion heat

↑ **Parent:** [Ortho-para conversion](ortho-para-conversion.md)

For [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) approximated as a [linear rigid rotor](linear-rigid-rotor.md), the $\ell=1$ to $\ell=0$ [energy](energy-split.md) decrease is $\hbar^2/I$, with [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) $I\simeq m_pr^2/2$. A sample of mass $M$ initially containing an [orthohydrogen](orthohydrogen.md) fraction $x_o$ therefore releases $Q\simeq x_oM\hbar^2/(m_p^2r^2)$ on complete conversion to the lowest [parahydrogen](parahydrogen.md) level. This estimate neglects the [electron](electron.md) contribution to molecular mass and changes of the rotational constant with environment. It describes an internal [heat](heat-split.md) load that external insulation cannot remove.
