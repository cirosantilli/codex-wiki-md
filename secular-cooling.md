# Secular cooling

↑ **Parent:** [Planetary mantle](planetary-mantle.md)

A long-term decline in mean interior [temperature](temperature-split.md), distinct from a short transient, a local cold anomaly or a change in the pressure used for a [potential temperature](potential-temperature.md). In a [mantle thermal budget](mantle-thermal-budget.md), cooling requires heat loss to exceed internal generation plus heat entering from below. The rate depends on both the heat sources and the efficiency of transport.
