# Repeat unit

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer](polymer-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Repeat_unit)

A [constitutional repeating unit](repeat-unit.md) is the smallest chemical unit whose repetition describes a regular chain. Its boundaries mark repetition, not necessarily the origin of the [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) from a single [monomer](monomer.md). See [IUPAC: constitutional repeating unit](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/C01286/plain).
