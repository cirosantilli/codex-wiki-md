# Statistical functional

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical inference](statistical-inference-split.md)

A statistical functional assigns a target value to each [probability measure](probability-measure.md) in a [statistical model](statistical-model-split.md). Examples include an [expected value](expected-value.md), a [quantile](quantile-function.md), and an [integral](integral.md) of a power of a [probability density function](probability-density-function.md). Its behavior along [statistical paths](statistical-path.md) determines whether first-order [influence-function representers](influence-function-representer.md) are available.
