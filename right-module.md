# Right module

↑ **Parent:** [Module (mathematics)](module-mathematics.md)

A right [module](module-mathematics.md) over a unital [ring](ring.md) $R$ is an [abelian group](abelian-group.md) $M$ with an additive scalar action on the right satisfying $(mr)s=m(rs)$, $m(r+s)=mr+ms$ and $m1=m$. The order of multiplication matters for a noncommutative [ring](ring.md). Equivalently it is a left [module](module-mathematics.md) over the [opposite ring](opposite-ring.md). Row vectors acted on by matrices on the right give a basic example.
