# Product-form stationary distribution of a loss network

↑ **Parent:** [Fixed routing](fixed-routing.md)

For a [loss network](loss-network.md) with feasible occupancies $An\leq C$, its [stationary distribution](stationary-distribution.md) is proportional to $\prod_r\alpha_r^{n_r}/n_r!$. It is the law of independent [Poisson random variables](poisson-distribution.md) conditioned on the resource constraints. With exponential holding times, [detailed balance for a continuous-time Markov chain](detailed-balance-for-a-continuous-time-markov-chain.md) proves the formula directly.
