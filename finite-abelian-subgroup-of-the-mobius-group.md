<h1 id="finite-abelian-subgroup-of-the-mobius-group">Finite abelian subgroup of the Möbius group</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Möbius group](mobius-group.md)

A finite [abelian subgroup](abelian-subgroup.md) of the [Möbius group](mobius-group.md) is cyclic or isomorphic to $C_2\times C_2$. If it contains an element of order greater than two, conjugate that element's two fixed points to $0,\infty$. Every commuting map must preserve these points individually: an interchange would conjugate the element to its inverse. All maps are then scalings, forming a cyclic finite subgroup of $\mathbb C^\times$. If every nonidentity element has order two, choose one with fixed points $0,\infty$; the subgroup acts on this pair with image of size at most two and kernel contained in $\{z,-z\}$. Its size is therefore at most four, giving the asserted possibilities.
