# Parallel plasma pressure

↑ **Parent:** [Pressure tensor](pressure-tensor.md)

The normal [stress](stress.md) along the local [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md), evaluated using the random parallel [velocity](velocity.md). At zero equilibrium mean flow, its linear perturbation is obtained from $m\int v_\parallel^2\delta f\,d^3v$ together with the perturbation of the [velocity-space measure](velocity-space-measure.md).
