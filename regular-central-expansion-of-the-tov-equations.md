# Regular central expansion of the TOV equations

↑ **Parent:** [Tolman–Oppenheimer–Volkoff equation](tolman-oppenheimer-volkoff-equation.md)

For a smooth centre with density $\rho_c$ and pressure $p_c$, the [Tolman–Oppenheimer–Volkoff equation](tolman-oppenheimer-volkoff-equation.md) gives $m(r)=4\pi\rho_cr^3/3+O(r^5)$ and $p(r)=p_c-2\pi(\rho_c+p_c)(\rho_c/3+p_c)r^2+O(r^4)$. A fixed [barotropic stellar equation of state](barotropic-stellar-equation-of-state.md) leaves one physical central parameter; the additive lapse constant fixes time normalization.
