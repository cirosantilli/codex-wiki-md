# Taylor remainder

↑ **Parent:** [Taylor's theorem](taylor-s-theorem.md)

The Taylor remainder is the difference between a function and a finite [Taylor polynomial](taylor-polynomial.md). Bounds on higher derivatives or their [Hölder continuity](holder-condition.md) control this error.
