# Flat band

↑ **Parent:** [Electronic band structure](electronic-band-structure.md)

A [flat band](flat-band.md) has energy independent of wavevector, and hence zero [group velocity](group-velocity.md). A nearly flat band has small dispersion and, near a parabolic extremum, large [band effective mass](band-effective-mass.md). Coupling a [flat band](flat-band.md) to a dispersive band can produce an [avoided crossing](avoided-crossing.md) and a [hybridization gap](hybridization-gap.md).
