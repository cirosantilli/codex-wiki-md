# Barrier-triggered put

↑ **Parent:** [American put option](american-put-option.md)

A put with a prescribed exercise rule: exercise at the first touch of $B<K$, or at expiry if no touch occurs. Its value is bounded above by the [American put option](american-put-option.md), which optimizes over exercise times. With zero interest rate, its value equals the [down-and-out put](down-and-out-put.md) value plus $(K-B)$ times a cash-at-expiry [one-touch option](one-touch-option.md) value. At positive interest rate the rebate is paid at touch, so replacing it by an expiry payment would generally be incorrect.
