# Nucleotide substitution model

↑ **Parent:** [Phylogenetics](phylogenetics.md)

A probabilistic model of sequence changes along a [phylogenetic tree](phylogenetic-tree.md). A [continuous-time Markov chain](continuous-time-markov-chain-split.md) rate matrix gives branch transition probabilities through a [matrix exponential](matrix-exponential.md); assumptions about composition and rate variation affect inference.
