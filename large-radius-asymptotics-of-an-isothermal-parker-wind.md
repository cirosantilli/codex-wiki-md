# Large-radius asymptotics of an isothermal Parker wind

↑ **Parent:** [Isothermal Parker wind](isothermal-parker-wind.md)

On the escaping supersonic [isothermal Parker wind](isothermal-parker-wind.md) branch, $w=u^2/c^2\to\infty$. Its integrated equation gives $w=4\ln x+\ln w-3+4/x$, so $w/(4\ln x)\to1$ and the title result follows. A further iteration yields $w=4\ln x+\ln(4\ln x)-3+o(1)$. The leading logarithmic growth holds for any supersonic branch with a fixed finite integration constant, but does not describe a [subsonic isothermal breeze](subsonic-isothermal-breeze.md). The unbounded speed is a formal consequence of indefinite isothermal heating in a prescribed central gravitational potential.
