# Solenoidal time-of-flight energy window

↑ **Parent:** [On-axis entry into a solenoidal tube](on-axis-entry-into-a-solenoidal-tube.md)

A nonrelativistic particle accepted only at a fixed flight time through an ideal uniform-field tube has fixed parallel [speed](speed.md) $v_\parallel=l/t_0$. On-axis entry and wall clearance bound the independent perpendicular [speed](speed.md) by $|q|Ba/(2m)$, provided a full gyration occurs before exit. Adding parallel and perpendicular [kinetic energies](kinetic-energy.md) gives the displayed ideal [energy](energy-split.md) window, where $K_0=mv_\parallel^2/2$. The upper endpoint is a grazing limit; strict wall exclusion makes it a [supremum](supremum.md).
