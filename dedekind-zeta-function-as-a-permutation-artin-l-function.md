# Dedekind zeta function as a permutation Artin L-function

↑ **Parent:** [Dedekind zeta function](dedekind-zeta-function.md)

Let $E$ be a finite normal closure of the [number field](number-field.md) $K$. The [permutation representation](permutation-representation.md) of its [Galois group](galois-group.md) on the embeddings of $K$ into $E$ has one trivial constituent, because the action is transitive. At a prime, the [inertia group](inertia-group.md) invariant basis consists of sums over inertia orbits of embeddings; [Frobenius automorphism](frobenius-automorphism.md) permutes them in cycles of lengths equal to the [residue degrees](residue-degree.md) of the primes of $K$. A cycle of length $f$ contributes $\det(1-T\rho(\operatorname{Frob}))=1-T^f$. Hence the local factors equal those of the [Dedekind zeta function](dedekind-zeta-function.md), including at ramified primes. If nontrivial irreducible [Artin L-functions](artin-l-function.md) are holomorphic and nonzero at one, this factorization and the simple pole of the [Riemann zeta function](riemann-zeta-function.md) show that $\zeta_K$ has a simple pole there.
