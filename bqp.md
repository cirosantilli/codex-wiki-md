# BQP

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum complexity theory](quantum-complexity-theory.md)

[BQP](bqp.md) is the class of [promise problems](promise-problem.md) decided by uniform polynomial-size [quantum circuits](quantum-circuit-split.md) with bounded error. Completeness $2/3$ and soundness $1/3$ are conventional; [BQP error reduction](bqp-error-reduction.md) shows that other fixed separated thresholds give the same class.
