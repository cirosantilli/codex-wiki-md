# Electrical susceptance

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical admittance](electrical-admittance.md)

In the $e^{i\omega t}$ [phasor](phasor.md) convention, [electrical admittance](electrical-admittance.md) is $Y=G+iB$, with conductance $G$ and [electrical susceptance](electrical-susceptance.md) $B$. An ideal [capacitor](capacitor.md) has $B=\omega C$ and an ideal [inductor](inductor.md) has $B=-1/(\omega L)$. [electrical susceptances](electrical-susceptance.md) add for parallel branches; their cancellation gives a purely resistive input admittance.
