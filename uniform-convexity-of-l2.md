# Uniform convexity of l2

↑ **Parent:** [L2 sequence space](l2-sequence-space.md)

The [l2 sequence space](l2-sequence-space.md) is a [uniformly convex Banach space](uniformly-convex-banach-space.md). If $\lVert x\rVert_2=\lVert y\rVert_2=1$, the [parallelogram law](parallelogram-law.md) gives

$$
\left\lVert\frac{x+y}{2}\right\rVert_2^2
=1-\frac14\lVert x-y\rVert_2^2.
$$

Thus $\lVert x-y\rVert_2\geq\varepsilon$ permits the modulus $\delta(\varepsilon)=1-\sqrt{1-\varepsilon^2/4}$.
