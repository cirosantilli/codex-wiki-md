# Clique count in a binomial random graph

↑ **Parent:** [Binomial random graph](binomial-random-graph.md)

If $X_t$ counts copies of the [complete graph](complete-graph.md) $K_t$ in $G(n,p)$, then

$$
\mathbb EX_t=\binom ntp^{\binom t2}.
$$

Consequently $p n^{2/(t-1)}\to0$ implies $\mathbb P(X_t>0)\to0$ by the [first moment method](first-moment-method.md).
