# Symplectic form on scalar-field solutions

↑ **Parent:** [Klein-Gordon field](klein-gordon-field.md)

For real [Klein-Gordon field](klein-gordon-field.md) solutions with suitable support or boundary conditions, $\Omega(\phi_1,\phi_2)=\int_\Sigma(\phi_1n^a\nabla_a\phi_2-\phi_2n^a\nabla_a\phi_1)d\Sigma$ is a conserved [symplectic form](symplectic-form.md). The field equation makes its current divergence-free. Its complexification gives $(u,v)_{KG}=i\Omega(u^*,v)$, the [Klein-Gordon inner product](klein-gordon-inner-product.md). A compatible [complex structure on the Klein-Gordon solution space](complex-structure-on-the-klein-gordon-solution-space.md) is additional data needed for a particle representation.
