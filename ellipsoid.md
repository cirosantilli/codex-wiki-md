# Ellipsoid

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean geometry](euclidean-geometry-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ellipsoid)

An ellipsoid is a level set $(x-c)^TA(x-c)=1$ for a real symmetric [positive-definite matrix](positive-definite-matrix.md) $A$. Its principal axes are the orthonormal eigenvectors of $A$, and an eigenvalue $\lambda$ gives semi-axis length $1/\sqrt\lambda$.
