# Bivector

↑ **Parent:** [Exterior algebra](exterior-algebra.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bivector)

A bivector is an antisymmetric contravariant tensor of degree two. A simple bivector is a single [exterior product](exterior-product.md) $u\wedge v$. A general bivector is a sum of such products; in four dimensions simplicity is equivalent to $w\wedge w=0$ for a nonzero bivector. Covariant counterparts are [differential two-forms](2-form.md).
