<h1 id="fejer-summation-is-a-uniform-norm-contraction">Fejér summation is a uniform-norm contraction</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Fejér sum](fejer-sum.md)

The [Fejér kernel](fejer-kernel.md) is nonnegative and has unit mass in its convolution normalization. The integral triangle inequality therefore bounds the [supremum norm](supremum-norm.md) of a [Fejér sum](fejer-sum.md) by that of the original function. In the half-kernel convention $F_n=(2n)^{-1}|\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}e^{ijt}|^2$, its integral is $\pi$ and the convolution prefactor is $1/\pi$.
