# Low-pass filter of a multiresolution analysis

↑ **Parent:** [Scaling refinement equation](scaling-refinement-equation.md)

For an [orthonormal](orthonormal-set.md) [scaling function](scaling-function.md) satisfying $\varphi(x)=\sqrt2\sum_kh_k\varphi(2x-k)$, its low-pass [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md) symbol is $m(\xi)=2^{-1/2}\sum_kh_ke^{-ik\xi}$. The [scaling refinement equation](scaling-refinement-equation.md) becomes $\widehat\varphi(2\xi)=m(\xi)\widehat\varphi(\xi)$. The symbol is initially an almost-everywhere defined periodic [function](function-split.md); the assumption that the [scaling function](scaling-function.md) is [Lebesgue integrable](lebesgue-integrable-function.md) alone does not make it [smooth](smooth-function.md). The [orthonormal](orthonormal-set.md) translates give the [quadrature mirror filter](quadrature-mirror-filter.md) identity.
