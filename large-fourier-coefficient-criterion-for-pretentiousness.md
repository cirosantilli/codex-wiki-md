# Large Fourier coefficient criterion for pretentiousness

↑ **Parent:** [Pretentious distance](pretentious-distance.md)

Let $X\geq3$, and let $f$ be a normalized [multiplicative arithmetic function](multiplicative-function.md) supported on [squarefree integers](squarefree-integer.md), with $|f(p)|\leq1$. Form the [finite measure](finite-measure.md) $\nu=\sum_n f(n)n^{-1-1/\log X}\delta_{\log n}$ using [Dirac measures](dirac-measure.md). Under the transform convention $\widehat\nu(t)=\int e^{-itu}\,d\nu(u)$, its [Euler product](euler-product.md) is $\prod_p(1+f(p)p^{-1-1/\log X-it})$. Expanding its logarithmic modulus, and replacing the damped [prime](prime-number.md) weights by $1/p$ up to $X$ with [Mertens first theorem](mertens-first-theorem.md), gives the displayed absolute bound, uniformly in real $t$. Hence $|\widehat\nu(t)|\geq\delta\log X$ forces $D(f,n^{it};X)^2\leq\log(C/\delta)$. The distance expression still defines a nonnegative pretentious score for [prime](prime-number.md) values bounded by one; its actual [metric](metric.md) interpretation uses unit-modulus values.
