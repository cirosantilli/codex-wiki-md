# Unit-ideal certificate from a principal affine cover

↑ **Parent:** [Cohomological criterion for affineness](cohomological-criterion-for-affineness.md)

If finitely many $X_{f_i}$ cover $X$, the morphism $\mathcal O_X^r\to\mathcal O_X$ with coefficients $f_i$ is onto. If its [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) has vanishing first [sheaf cohomology](sheaf-cohomology.md), the [long exact sequence in sheaf cohomology](long-exact-sequence-in-sheaf-cohomology.md) lifts the [global section](global-section.md) $1$ to coefficients $g_i$. This turns a geometric cover into a [unit ideal](unit-ideal.md) in the ring of [global sections](global-section.md).
