<h1 id="lloyd-s-mirror">Lloyd's mirror</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Interference (wave propagation)](interference-wave-propagation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lloyd's_mirror)

[Lloyd's mirror](lloyd-s-mirror.md) produces [interference](interference-wave-propagation.md) between light arriving directly from a narrow source and light reflected by a plane mirror, which appears to originate at the source's virtual image. With source distance $h$ from the mirror, the effective source separation is $2h$. A reflection [phase shift](phase-shift.md) close to $\pi$ reverses the bright/dark condition relative to two equally phased sources. Spatial filtering, suitable [temporal coherence](temporal-optical-coherence.md), and direct/reflected beam overlap remain necessary.
