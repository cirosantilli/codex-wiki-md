# Inverse Gaussian sum closure

↑ **Parent:** [Inverse Gaussian distribution](inverse-gaussian-distribution.md)

In the parametrization with density proportional to $y^{-3/2}\exp[-(\lambda/y+\phi y)/2]$, the [Inverse Gaussian distribution](inverse-gaussian-distribution.md) has [cumulant-generating function](cumulant-generating-function.md) $K(t)=\sqrt{\lambda\phi}-\sqrt{\lambda(\phi-2t)}$. For $n$ independent copies, the average has this same family with parameters $(n\phi,n\lambda)$, while the sum has parameters $(\phi,n^2\lambda)$. These assertions follow by multiplying [moment-generating functions](moment-generating-function.md) and scaling the density. The more usual mean parameter is $\mu=\sqrt{\lambda/\phi}$, so the sum has mean $n\mu$ and shape $n^2\lambda$.
