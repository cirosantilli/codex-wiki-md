# Digit agreement and p-adic distance

↑ **Parent:** [p-adic digit](p-adic-digit.md)

Two [p-adic integers](p-adic-integer.md) agree in their first $n$ low-order [p-adic digits](p-adic-digit.md) precisely when their difference is divisible by $p^n$. Equivalently,

$$
x\equiv y\pmod{p^n}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
v_p(x-y)\geq n\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
|x-y|_p\leq p^{-n}.
$$

If the first differing digit has index $j$, the remaining leading coefficient is nonzero modulo $p$, giving exact [P-adic valuation](p-adic-valuation.md) $j$ and [p-adic metric](p-adic-metric.md) $p^{-j}$. If no digit differs, the two elements are equal and their distance is zero. Truncating at $r_n$ therefore gives $|x-r_n|_p\leq p^{-n}$, with a stricter bound possible when further digits are zero.
