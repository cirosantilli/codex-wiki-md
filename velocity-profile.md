# Velocity profile

↑ **Parent:** [Viscous fluid flow](viscous-fluid-flow-split.md)

A velocity profile records the variation of [velocity](velocity.md) across a spatial coordinate, for example $u(z)$ across a parallel viscous layer. Its derivative sets the tangential [shear stress](shear-stress.md) in a [Newtonian fluid](newtonian-fluid.md), while its integral gives the [volume flux](volumetric-flow-rate.md).
