# Dark-matter halo merger

↑ **Parent:** [Dark matter halo](dark-matter-halo.md)

A dark-matter halo merger joins previously distinct [dark matter halos](dark-matter-halo.md). Orbital [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) can become internal halo spin, while escaping material carries away some mass and [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md). Halo mergers and accretion are important reasons that early [tidal torque theory](tidal-torque-theory.md) is only an approximate predictor of the spin of a later virialized remnant.
