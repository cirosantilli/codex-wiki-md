# Exponential-surjectivity obstruction from distinct negative eigenvalues

↑ **Parent:** [Exponential map of a matrix Lie group](exponential-map-of-a-matrix-lie-group.md)

A real matrix with at least one simple negative [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) cannot be a [matrix exponential](matrix-exponential.md) of a real matrix: a logarithm would commute with it and preserve each one-dimensional real negative-eigenvalue space, where exponentiation can only produce a positive [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md). In particular $\operatorname{diag}(-2,-1/2)$ belongs to the connected [special linear group](special-linear-group.md) $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb R)$ but has no real logarithm.
