# Long-read RNA sequencing

↑ **Parent:** [RNA sequencing](rna-sequencing.md)

Sequencing designed to span long portions of individual [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) molecules or derived [Complementary DNA](complementary-dna.md). Linking distant [exons](exon.md) on one molecule helps resolve complete [transcript isoforms](transcript-isoform.md), while per-base errors and sampling depth still require control.
