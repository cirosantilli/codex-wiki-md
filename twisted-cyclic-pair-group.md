# Twisted cyclic pair group

↑ **Parent:** [Semidirect product](semidirect-product.md)

Let $p$ be prime, $a\in\mathbb F_p^\times$, $x,y\in\mathbb Z/(p-1)\mathbb Z$ and $u,v\in\mathbb F_p$. [Fermat's little theorem](fermat-little-theorem.md) makes the displayed multiplication well defined. Its identity is $(0,0)$ and inverse is $(-x,-a^{-x}u)$; associativity follows by expanding both bracketings. Projection onto the first coordinate is a [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md) with normal cyclic kernel of order $p$. Under the change $U=a^{-x}u$, multiplication becomes $(x,U)(y,V)=(x+y,U+a^{-x}V)$, the [semidirect product](semidirect-product.md) of the additive prime field by the cyclic group of order $p-1$. The group is abelian precisely when $a=1$; $a$ need not be a generator of the multiplicative field group.
