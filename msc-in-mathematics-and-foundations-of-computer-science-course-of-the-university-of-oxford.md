# MSc in mathematics and foundations of computer science course of the University of Oxford

↑ **Parent:** [Master's course of the University of Oxford](master-s-course-of-the-university-of-oxford.md)

[Official course description](https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/graduate/courses/msc-mathematics-and-foundations-of-computer-science).

Basic-option schedule. The central description gives indicative choices including algebraic [topology](topology-split.md), [category theory](category-theory-split.md), [information theory](information-theory-split.md) and quantum processes and computation; annual availability follows the handbook. [2026–27 handbook](https://courses.maths.ox.ac.uk/mod/book/tool/print/index.php?id=70514). Advanced-option schedule. Indicative choices include algebraic [geometry](geometry.md), automata, logic and games, and axiomatic [set theory](set-theory-split.md). At least five lecture courses are required overall. Compulsory dissertation on a topic related to the taught material, undertaken in Trinity term and the summer.
