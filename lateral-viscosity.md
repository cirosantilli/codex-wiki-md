# Lateral viscosity

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamic viscosity](dynamic-viscosity-split.md)

An effective horizontal transport coefficient for [momentum](momentum-split.md), often representing unresolved [eddies](eddy.md) in ocean models. A simple constant-coefficient closure adds $\nu_h\nabla_h^2\mathbf u$ to the [Cauchy momentum equation](cauchy-momentum-equation.md) and $\nu_h\nabla_h^2\xi$ to the [vorticity equation](vorticity-equation.md). Its balance with the beta effect sets the width of a [Munk boundary layer](munk-boundary-layer.md).
