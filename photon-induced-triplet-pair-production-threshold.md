# Photon-induced triplet pair-production threshold

↑ **Parent:** [Photon fixed-target production threshold](photon-fixed-target-production-threshold.md)

For a [photon](photon.md) incident on a stationary [Electron](electron.md), producing an additional electron and positron leaves three massive particles in the final state. [Four-momentum conservation](four-momentum-conservation.md) gives $W^2=m_e^2c^4+2m_ec^2E_\gamma$ for the [centre-of-momentum energy](centre-of-momentum-energy.md). Its minimum is $3m_ec^2$, so the incident threshold is $4m_ec^2$. At threshold all final particles are at rest in their centre-of-momentum frame, which moves at $4c/5$ relative to the target laboratory frame. This differs from a heavy-target threshold because the electron target's recoil cannot be neglected.
