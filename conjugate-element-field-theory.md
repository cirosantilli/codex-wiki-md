# Conjugate element (field theory)

↑ **Parent:** [Field extension](field-extension.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conjugate_element_(field_theory))

The [algebraic conjugates](conjugate-element-field-theory.md) of an [algebraic element](algebraic-element.md) over a [field](field.md) $K$ are the [polynomial roots](root-of-a-polynomial.md) of its [algebraic minimal polynomial](minimal-polynomial-of-an-algebraic-element.md) over $K$ in an [algebraic closure](algebraic-closure.md).

The [algebraic conjugates](conjugate-element-field-theory.md) of an [algebraic number](algebraic-number.md) are the [polynomial roots](root-of-a-polynomial.md) of its [algebraic minimal polynomial](minimal-polynomial-of-an-algebraic-element.md), equivalently its images under [field embeddings](field-embedding.md) of its [number field](number-field.md) into an [algebraic closure](algebraic-closure.md).
