# Trace operator

↑ **Parent:** [Sobolev space](sobolev-space-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trace_operator)

On a bounded [Lipschitz domain](lipschitz-domain.md), the [Sobolev trace operator](trace-operator.md) is the continuous linear extension to $H^1(\Omega)$ of the boundary restriction of smooth functions. Continuity and density make this extension unique; its existence is the [Sobolev trace theorem](sobolev-trace-theorem.md). The kernel prescribes a homogeneous [Dirichlet boundary condition](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md). Vanishing [Sobolev trace](trace-operator.md) on only part of the boundary instead gives the form domain for a [mixed boundary condition](mixed-boundary-condition.md), with the natural [Neumann boundary condition](neumann-boundary-condition.md) on the remainder. On an assembly of congruent tiles, taking a [Sobolev trace](trace-operator.md) commutes with a constant matrix acting on the tile restrictions. This lets a [transplantation theorem](transplantation-theorem.md) be proved on [quadratic form](quadratic-form.md) domains even when corner eigenfunctions have singular classical derivatives.
