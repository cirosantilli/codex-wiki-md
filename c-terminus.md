# C-terminus

↑ **Parent:** [Protein](protein-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C-terminus)

The C-terminus is the carboxyl end of a [protein](protein-split.md) or [amino acid](amino-acid.md) chain. During [biological translation](translation-biology.md), each new residue is added at this end. The example under [gene expression](gene-expression.md) gives N–[methionine](methionine.md)–[alanine](alanine.md)–C, placing [alanine](alanine.md) at the [C-terminus](c-terminus.md); its [stop codon](stop-codon.md) supplies no further residue. [OpenStax Biology 2e, 3.4: polypeptide termini](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/3-4-proteins)
