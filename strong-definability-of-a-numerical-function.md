# Strong definability of a numerical function

↑ **Parent:** [Weak definability of a numerical function](weak-definability-of-a-numerical-function.md)

[Strong definability of a numerical function](strong-definability-of-a-numerical-function.md) adds internal uniqueness of the output at every standard input [tuple](finite-tuple.md). Together with a proved correct instance, this says the [first-order theory](first-order-theory.md) proves that the output [logical formula](logical-formula.md) is equivalent to [logical equality](logical-equality.md) with the correct numeral, for arbitrary potential outputs.
