# Positive energy theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Arnowitt-Deser-Misner energy](arnowitt-deser-misner-energy.md)

Complete asymptotically flat [spin](spin.md) initial data obeying the Einstein constraints and the [dominant energy condition](dominant-energy-condition.md) have future-causal ADM four-momentum, under the appropriate decay and boundary hypotheses. A spinorial proof solves the [Witten spinor equation](witten-spinor-equation.md) and integrates the [Nester two-form](nester-two-form.md). Its divergence is a sum of a nonnegative spinor-gradient term and a matter-energy contraction with a future-causal [Dirac current](dirac-current.md). Positivity for every asymptotic constant spinor yields the full inequality, rather than merely nonnegative energy in one frame.
