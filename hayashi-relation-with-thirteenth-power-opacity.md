# Hayashi relation with thirteenth-power opacity

↑ **Parent:** [Hayashi track](hayashi-track.md)

Match an efficient, [monatomic gas](monatomic-gas.md), fully convective star to the [grey-atmosphere convection onset with thirteenth-power opacity](grey-atmosphere-convection-onset-with-thirteenth-power-opacity.md). Its convective relation is $P=K_TT^{5/2}$. At the matching [temperature](temperature.md) $T_b\propto T_e$, the [stellar atmosphere](stellar-atmosphere.md) gives $K_T^2\propto ML^{-1}T_e^{-13}\propto MR^{13/2}L^{-17/4}$ using the [Stefan–Boltzmann law](stefan-boltzmann-law.md). The ideal-gas relation converts this to $P=K_\rho\rho^{5/3}$ with $K_\rho=(\mathcal R/\mu)^{5/3}K_T^{-2/3}$. The [polytropic mass-radius relation](polytropic-mass-radius-relation.md) at index $3/2$ gives $K_\rho\propto GM^{1/3}R$, hence $K_T^2\propto M^{-1}R^{-3}$. Equating the two expressions proves $L^{17/4}\propto M^2R^{19/2}$, or $L\propto M^{8/17}R^{38/17}$. The entropy coefficient is constant throughout the [convective envelope](convective-envelope.md), but can change between models.
