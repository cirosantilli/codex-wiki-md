# Disk scheduling

↑ **Parent:** [Hard disk drive](hard-disk-drive.md)

Choose the order of pending [input-output](input-output.md) requests. Physical head movement, [rotational latency](rotational-latency.md), request arrival times and fairness can favour different orders. A [seek distance](seek-distance.md) calculation is a simplified model; real [seek time](seek-time.md) is not necessarily linear in track displacement.
