# Time reversal of the squared diffusion-residual action

↑ **Parent:** [Squared diffusion-residual action](squared-diffusion-residual-action.md)

For a real scalar height field, [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) gives

$$
(h_t-\nu\nabla^2h)^2=h_t^2+\nu^2(\nabla^2h)^2+\nu\partial_t|\nabla h|^2-2\nu\nabla\cdot(h_t\nabla h).
$$

The bulk expression is even under [time reversal](t-symmetry.md). The original density is not pointwise even, but changes by a [total divergence](total-divergence.md); its stationary equation is time-reversal invariant. The first-order [diffusion equation](diffusion-equation-split.md) selected by zero residual is not invariant under ordinary time reversal at fixed positive $\nu$. Boundary conditions or the probabilistic interpretation can distinguish the two time directions even when the higher-order stationary equation does not.
