<h1 id="mobius-divisor-sum-identity">Möbius divisor-sum identity</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Möbius function](mobius-function.md)

For $n>1$, expand $\prod_{p\mid n}(1-1)=0$ over the subsets of its distinct prime divisors. The coefficient of a squarefree divisor is its [Möbius function](mobius-function.md) value; nonsquarefree divisors have value zero. At $n=1$ the empty product is one. This identity is the multiplicative cancellation behind [Möbius inversion](mobius-inversion-formula.md) and reciprocal [Dirichlet series](dirichlet-series.md).
