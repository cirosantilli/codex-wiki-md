# Glycogen synthase

↑ **Parent:** [Enzyme](enzyme-split.md)

[Glycogen synthase](glycogen-synthase.md) extends glycogen using activated glucose donors. Its activity is regulated by phosphorylation and metabolites, and its activation can be coordinated with inactivation of glycogen phosphorylase.
