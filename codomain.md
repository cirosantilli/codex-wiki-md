# Codomain

↑ **Parent:** [Function](function-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Codomain)

The [codomain](codomain.md) of a [function](function-split.md) is its specified target [set](set-split.md). The [image of a function](image-of-a-function.md) is a [subset](subset.md) of the [codomain](codomain.md), and equals it precisely for a [surjective function](surjective-function.md). Changing a [codomain](codomain.md) can change whether the same input-output rule is [surjective](surjective-function.md), as the [quadratic function](quadratic-function.md) examples under [function](function-split.md) show.
