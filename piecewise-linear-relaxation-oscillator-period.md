# Piecewise linear relaxation-oscillator period

↑ **Parent:** [Relaxation oscillation](relaxation-oscillation.md)

For $\epsilon\dot v=g(v)-w$, $\dot w=v$, let $g=-v$ below $\alpha$, $g=1-v$ above $\beta$, and join the endpoints by an increasing line, with $-1<\alpha<0<\beta<1$ and $\beta-\alpha<1$. The slow branches obey $\dot w=-w$ and $\dot w=1-w$ between $w_L=-\alpha$ and $w_R=1-\beta$. Their travel times are $\log[(1-\beta)/(-\alpha)]$ and $\log[(1+\alpha)/\beta]$. Fast horizontal jumps contribute vanishing time as $\epsilon\to0$, giving the displayed [relaxation oscillation](relaxation-oscillation.md) period.
