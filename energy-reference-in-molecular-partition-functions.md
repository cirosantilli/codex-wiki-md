# Energy reference in molecular partition functions

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular partition function](molecular-partition-function.md)

If energies are measured upward from a species reference $\varepsilon_0$, its absolute single-particle sum is $q_{\rm abs}=e^{-\beta\varepsilon_0}q$. The ideal-gas [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) is then $\varepsilon_0-k_BT\ln(q/N)$. A zero-point factor can be included either in $q$ or in the reference energy, but not both.
