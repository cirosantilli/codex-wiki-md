# Strong maximum principle for subharmonic functions

↑ **Parent:** [Maximum principle for subharmonic functions](maximum-principle-for-subharmonic-functions.md)

If the [canonical representative of a subharmonic distribution](canonical-representative-of-a-subharmonic-distribution.md) on a connected domain attains a finite global maximum at an interior point, it is constant. The ball mean inequality forces equality almost everywhere on a small ball around the maximum. The canonical representative then equals the maximum everywhere on that ball. The maximum set is open by this argument and closed by upper semicontinuity; [connectedness](connected-space.md) makes it the whole domain.
