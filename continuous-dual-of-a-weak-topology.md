# Continuous dual of a weak topology

↑ **Parent:** [Dual pair](dual-pair.md)

For a [separated dual pair](dual-pair.md), the continuous linear functionals for its [weak topology](weak-topology-split.md) are exactly evaluations by members of the paired space. Continuity bounds a functional on a neighbourhood defined by finitely many evaluations; scalar rescaling makes it vanish on their common kernel. It then factors through their finite-dimensional evaluation map and is a finite linear combination of them.
