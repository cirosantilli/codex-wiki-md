# Effective emission pressure

↑ **Parent:** [Grey atmosphere](grey-atmosphere.md)

For a source linear in downward effective [optical depth](optical-depth.md) $t\in[0,D]$, [two-stream approximation](two-stream-approximation.md) weighting $e^{-t}$ has normalized mean $t_{\rm eff}=1-D/(e^D-1)$. Constant mass opacity gives $p_{\rm rad}=p_s t_{\rm eff}/D$, tending to $p_s/2$ for small $D$ and $p_s/D$ for large $D$. The atmospheric flux is $(1-e^{-D})S(t_{\rm eff})$, separate from transmitted ground radiation. Exact angular transfer with literal vertical optical depth instead has weight $2E_2(t)$ and thick-limit mean $2/3$; the convention matters.
