# Stationary Rossby-wave ray envelope

↑ **Parent:** [Barotropic Rossby wave](barotropic-rossby-wave.md)

For a uniform eastward current $U>0$ on a [beta plane](beta-plane.md) with $\beta>0$, stationary nondivergent [Rossby waves](rossby-wave.md) have $k^2+l^2=\beta/U$. Their ground-frame [group velocity](group-velocity.md) is $2U(k^2,kl)/(k^2+l^2)$, tracing a circle centered at $(U,0)$ with radius $U$. Packets emitted by a localized source throughout $0<s<t$ occupy the disk shown in geometrical ray theory. This envelope is distinct from the full balanced near field. A westward current has no nontrivial stationary propagating branch in this model.
