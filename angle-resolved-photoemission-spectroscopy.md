# Angle-resolved photoemission spectroscopy

↑ **Parent:** [Photoemission spectroscopy](photoemission-spectroscopy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angle-resolved_photoemission_spectroscopy)

[Angle-resolved photoemission spectroscopy](angle-resolved-photoemission-spectroscopy.md) measures the kinetic energy and emission angle of [electrons](electron.md) removed from a solid by light. With photon energy $h\nu$, sample work function $\Phi$, and binding energy $E_B$ measured downwards from the [Fermi level](fermi-level.md), $E_{\rm kin}=h\nu-\Phi-E_B$. The measured parallel momentum is $k_\parallel=\sqrt{2m_eE_{\rm kin}}\sin\theta/\hbar$, modulo a surface reciprocal-lattice vector. Bright ridges reveal occupied [electronic band structure](electronic-band-structure.md); their slopes give the corresponding [group velocity](group-velocity.md). Extracting a [Fermi energy](fermi-energy.md) relative to a [Dirac point](dirac-point.md) requires a calibrated [Fermi level](fermi-level.md), not merely an unlabelled image boundary.
