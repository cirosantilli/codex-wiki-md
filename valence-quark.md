# Valence quark

↑ **Parent:** [Quark](quark.md)

A constituent used to specify the net [quark flavor](quark-flavor.md) content of a [hadron](hadron.md). The [quark model](quark-model.md) uses three [valence quarks](valence-quark.md) for an ordinary [baryon](baryon.md) and a [quark](quark.md)-[antiquark](antiquark.md) pair for an ordinary [meson](meson.md); this does not exclude additional sea pairs and [gluons](gluon.md) in the full state.
