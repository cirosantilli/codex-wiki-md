# Taylor number

↑ **Parent:** [Rotating fluid](rotating-fluid.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_number)

For a layer rotating at angular speed $\Omega$, of depth $d$ and [kinematic viscosity](kinematic-viscosity.md) $\nu$, the [Taylor number](taylor-number.md) compares the [Coriolis force](coriolis-force.md) to viscous damping through the squared ratio $(2\Omega d^2/\nu)^2$. Definitions in other geometries may include different geometric factors; this is the plane-layer convention.
