# Moduli space of Ricci-flat K3 metrics

↑ **Parent:** [K3 surface](k3-surface.md)

Locally, a unit-volume [Ricci flat](ricci-flat-manifold.md) metric on a [K3 surface](k3-surface.md) determines a positive three-plane in its real second [cohomology](cohomology-split.md), with the [K3 intersection lattice](k3-intersection-lattice.md) fixed. The corresponding Grassmannian has dimension $3\cdot19=57$. Allowing the volume gives one further positive real parameter; large diffeomorphisms identify lattice-equivalent descriptions. This is the same continuous moduli count as the three-torus [Narain moduli space](narain-moduli-space.md) plus its coupling.
