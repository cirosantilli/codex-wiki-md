# Stellar gravity

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar astrophysics](stellar-astrophysics-split.md)

Outside a spherical [star](star.md), its gravitational acceleration is $-GM_\star\mathbf r/r^3$. It provides the dominant central force for [Kepler orbits](kepler-orbit.md) in a star-dominated planetary system.
