# Mersenne number

↑ **Parent:** [Positive integer](positive-integer.md)

A [Mersenne number](mersenne-number.md) has the form $M_k=2^k-1$ for a [positive integer](positive-integer.md) $k$. Its [binary representation](binary-number.md) consists of $k$ ones. For example, $M_4=15$ is composite, while $M_5=31$ is [prime](prime-number-split.md). If $k=ab$ with $a,b>1$, the [finite geometric series](finite-geometric-series.md) factorization

$$
2^{ab}-1=(2^a-1)(1+2^a+\cdots+2^{a(b-1)})
$$

has two factors greater than one. Consequently a [Mersenne prime](mersenne-prime.md) requires a [prime](prime-number-split.md) exponent, but $M_{11}=23\cdot89$ shows that this condition alone is insufficient.
