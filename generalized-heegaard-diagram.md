# Generalized Heegaard diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Heegaard diagram](heegaard-diagram.md)

A generalized [Heegaard diagram](heegaard-diagram.md) permits [compression bodies](compression-body.md) and a nonempty negative boundary. Equivalently, a suitable [handlebody](handlebody.md) with [two-handles](two-handle.md) attached along only part of a compressing system can retain torus boundary components. Disk crossings supply generators and attaching-curve words supply relators for its [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md).
