# Phase reduction

↑ **Parent:** [Limit cycle](limit-cycle.md)

Near a [hyperbolic attracting limit cycle](hyperbolic-attracting-limit-cycle.md), a weak perturbation can be projected onto the neutral phase direction while transverse perturbations decay. Solving the periodic adjoint [variational equation](variational-equation.md) gives a [phase response function](phase-response-function.md). Averaging its inner product with weak forcing or coupling yields the first-order phase dynamics.
