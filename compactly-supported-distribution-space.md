# Compactly supported distribution space

↑ **Parent:** [Space of smooth functions](space-of-smooth-functions.md)

The [continuous dual](continuous-dual-space-split.md) $\mathcal E'(X)$ of $\mathcal E(X)$ is the space of [compactly supported distributions](compactly-supported-distribution.md) on $X$. Its weak topology is pointwise convergence on $\mathcal E(X)$.
