# Warm dark matter

↑ **Parent:** [Dark matter](dark-matter.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warm_dark_matter)

Warm dark matter has a primordial [collisionless free streaming](free-streaming.md) length large enough to suppress the formation of low-mass haloes, while remaining nonrelativistic early enough to cluster on galactic and larger scales.
