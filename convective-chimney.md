# Convective chimney

↑ **Parent:** [Open-ocean convection](open-ocean-convection.md)

A convective chimney is a localized deeply mixed region formed when surface [buoyancy](buoyancy.md) loss overcomes ocean [density stratification](density-stratification.md). Small plumes, compensating flow, rotation and lateral exchange maintain its structure; it need not be a permanently uniform descending column. [Brine rejection](brine-rejection.md) can increase its surface density forcing. A fresh summer cap can isolate a deep homogeneous core until winter forcing renews contact with the surface, so a surviving core need not imply continuous active convection.
