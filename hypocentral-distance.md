# Hypocentral distance

↑ **Parent:** [Hypocenter](hypocenter.md)

Distance from an [earthquake](earthquake.md) [hypocenter](hypocenter.md) to a receiver. In a uniform [velocity model](velocity-model.md) it is the straight ray-path length; in layered material an interphase delay instead constrains a model-dependent path.
