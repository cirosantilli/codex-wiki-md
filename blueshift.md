# Blueshift

↑ **Parent:** [Redshift](redshift.md)

A shift of spectral features to shorter [wavelengths](wavelength.md) and higher [frequencies](frequency.md). Relative approach produces a kinematic blueshift through the [Doppler effect](doppler-effect.md); gravitational and cosmological contributions can also affect an observed shift.
