# Stress-driven stagnation-point boundary layer

↑ **Parent:** [Boundary-layer equation](boundary-layer-equation.md)

For a fluid occupying $z<0$, match an outer [stagnation point](stagnation-point.md) flow $u=x,w=-z$ to a flat impermeable interface with dimensionless [shear stress](shear-stress.md) $\beta x$. With [boundary layer thickness](boundary-layer-thickness.md) $\delta=\mathrm{Re}^{-1/2}$, [inner variable](inner-variable.md) $\zeta=z/\delta$ and $\gamma=\delta\beta$, the leading [boundary-layer equation](boundary-layer-equation.md) admits $u=xf'(\zeta)$ and $w=-\delta f(\zeta)$. [Incompressibility](incompressible-flow.md) is automatic, and tangential [momentum conservation](momentum-conservation.md) gives the displayed [ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md), with $f(0)=0$, $f''(0)=\gamma$ and $f'\to1$ at $\zeta\to-\infty$. The sign of this far-field endpoint is fixed by the fluid domain. This is a prescribed-[traction](traction.md) interface; it does not impose a [no-slip boundary condition](no-slip-boundary-condition.md).
