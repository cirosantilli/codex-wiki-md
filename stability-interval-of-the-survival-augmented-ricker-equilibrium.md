# Stability interval of the survival-augmented Ricker equilibrium

↑ **Parent:** [Survival-augmented Ricker map](survival-augmented-ricker-map.md)

At the positive equilibrium,

$$
g'(x_*)=1-(1-r)\log\frac{k}{1-r}.
$$

The fixed point is strictly [linearly stable](linear-stability.md) when

$$
1-r<k<(1-r)e^{2/(1-r)}.
$$

The upper endpoint is also locally [asymptotically stable](asymptotic-stability.md), as the [nonlinear stability at the Ricker flip threshold](nonlinear-stability-at-the-ricker-flip-threshold.md) shows; hence the full local asymptotic-stability interval includes that endpoint. The lower endpoint has no positive equilibrium.
