# Shearing wave

↑ **Parent:** [Shearing sheet](shearing-sheet.md)

A shearing wave is a local disturbance whose wavevector evolves under background shear. In a Keplerian sheet, $k_x(t)=k_x(0)+(3/2)\Omega k_yt$.
