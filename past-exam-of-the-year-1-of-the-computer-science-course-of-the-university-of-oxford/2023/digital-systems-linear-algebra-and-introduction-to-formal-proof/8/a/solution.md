<h1 id="8/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

**The nonempty [simple graphs](../../../../../simple-graph.md) having an [independent vertex cover](../../../../../independent-vertex-cover.md) of size at most two.** The witnesses $x,y$ select the cover. The clause $\neg E(x,y)$ makes the selected [vertices](../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md) nonadjacent; the universal clause forbids every [edge](../../../../../edge-of-a-graph.md) whose two endpoints both lie outside the selected [set](../../../../../set-split.md).

The witnesses need not be distinct, so a one-[vertex](../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md) cover is permitted by choosing $x=y$. A nonempty edgeless [graph](../../../../../graph-split.md) also satisfies the formula. The empty [graph](../../../../../graph-split.md) does not, because the existential [quantifiers](../../../../../quantifier.md) have no witnesses. Here [independent vertex cover](../../../../../independent-vertex-cover.md) has its standard [vertex cover](../../../../../vertex-cover.md) meaning; 8(b)'s differing parenthetical definition is addressed separately below.
