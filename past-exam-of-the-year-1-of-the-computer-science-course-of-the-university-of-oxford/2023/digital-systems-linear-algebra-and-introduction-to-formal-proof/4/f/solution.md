↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

Expand the product and use (d) together with its [transpose](../../../../../transpose.md):

$$
MM^\top=(A+B)(A^\top+B^\top)
=AA^\top+AB^\top+BA^\top+BB^\top
=\boxed{AA^\top+BB^\top}.
$$

Indeed $AB^\top=0$ and $BA^\top=(AB^\top)^\top=0$.
