<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

If $A^\top x=0$, the [vector](../../../../../vector.md) $x$ belongs to the [null space](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) for which the columns of $L$ are a [basis](../../../../../basis.md). It is therefore a [linear combination](../../../../../linear-combination.md) of those columns, so $x=Ly$ for some $y\in\mathbb R^m$. Conversely, each column is annihilated by $A^\top$, hence $A^\top L=0$ and $x=Ly$ implies $A^\top x=0$. Thus

$$
\boxed{A^\top x=0\iff x=Ly\text{ for some }y\in\mathbb R^m.}
$$

The coordinate [vector](../../../../../vector.md) $y$ is unique because the columns of $L$ are [linearly independent](../../../../../linear-independence.md).
