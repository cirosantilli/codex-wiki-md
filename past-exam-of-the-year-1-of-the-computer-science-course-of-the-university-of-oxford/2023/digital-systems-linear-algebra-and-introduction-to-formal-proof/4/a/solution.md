<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The $m$ columns of $L$ form a [basis](../../../../../basis.md) of $\ker A^\top$, so that [null space](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) has [dimension](../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) $m$. The [rank-nullity theorem](../../../../../rank-nullity-theorem.md) applied to $A^\top:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^n$ gives $\operatorname{rank}A^\top=n-m$. A [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) and its [transpose](../../../../../transpose.md) have equal [matrix rank](../../../../../matrix-rank.md), hence

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{rank}A=n-m.}
$$
