The analogous [null space](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) statement is

$$
\boxed{Ax=0\iff x=Ry\text{ for some }y\in\mathbb R^m.}
$$

Here the [rank-nullity theorem](../../../../../rank-nullity-theorem.md) and (a) ensure that $\ker A$ also has [dimension](../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) $m$.
