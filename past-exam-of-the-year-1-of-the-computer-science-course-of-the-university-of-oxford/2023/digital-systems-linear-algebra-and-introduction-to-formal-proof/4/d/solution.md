<h1 id="4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The columnwise [null space](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) conditions say $A^\top L=0$ and $AR=0$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{A^\top B=A^\top LR^\top=0,\qquad
AB^\top=A(RL^\top)=(AR)L^\top=0.}
$$

Both right-hand sides are the $n\times n$ [zero matrix](../../../../../zero-matrix.md).
