<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Take $V=\mathbb R^2$ and the [linear map](../../../../../linear-map.md) $h(x,y)=(x,0)$. Then

$$
\mathcal R(h)=\operatorname{span}\{(1,0)\},\qquad
\mathcal N(h)=\operatorname{span}\{(0,1)\}.
$$

These two [vector subspaces](../../../../../vector-subspace.md) have zero [intersection](../../../../../set-intersection.md) and together span $\mathbb R^2$, so **$V=\mathcal R(h)\oplus\mathcal N(h)$** as a [direct sum of vector spaces](../../../../../direct-sum.md).
