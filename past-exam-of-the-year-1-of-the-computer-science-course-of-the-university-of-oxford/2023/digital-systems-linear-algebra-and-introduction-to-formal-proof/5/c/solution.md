<h1 id="5/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For $v\in\mathcal R(h^{m+k})$, choose $u$ with $v=h^{m+k}u=h^m(h^ku)$. Thus $v\in\mathcal R(h^m)$, proving

$$
\boxed{\mathcal R(h^{m+k})\subseteq\mathcal R(h^m).}
$$

This is the decreasing-image part of [stabilization of images of powers of a linear map](../../../../../stabilization-of-images-of-powers-of-a-linear-map.md). The convention $h^0=\operatorname{id}_V$ includes $m=0$ and $k=0$.
