<h1 id="6/g/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [G](../g.md)

The [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) is invariant under [transpose](../../../../../transpose.md), while multiplying all $n$ rows by $-1$ multiplies it by $(-1)^n$. Thus, for [odd integer](../../../../../odd-integer.md) $n$,

$$
\det A=\det A^\top=\det(-A)=(-1)^n\det A=-\det A.
$$

Over the [real numbers](../../../../../real-number.md), this forces **$\det A=0$**, so $A$ is a [singular matrix](../../../../../singular-matrix.md). This is the [odd-dimensional skew-symmetric singularity theorem](../../../../../odd-dimensional-skew-symmetric-singularity-theorem.md).
