<h1 id="6/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

If $A^\top=-A$, then the [dot product](../../../../../dot-product.md) satisfies

$$
(Ax)\cdot y=x^\top A^\top y=-x^\top Ay=-(Ay)\cdot x.
$$

Conversely, the assumed [dot product](../../../../../dot-product.md) identity gives $x^\top(A^\top+A)y=0$ for every $x,y$. Take $x=e_i$ and $y=e_j$ from the [standard basis](../../../../../standard-basis.md): every entry of $A^\top+A$ is zero. Hence **$A^\top=-A$**, proving the equivalence.
