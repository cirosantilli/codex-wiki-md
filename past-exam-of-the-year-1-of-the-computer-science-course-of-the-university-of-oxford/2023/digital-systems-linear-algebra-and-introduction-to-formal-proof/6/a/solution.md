↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $E_{ij}$ denote a [matrix unit](../../../../../matrix-unit.md), with its only nonzero entry equal to one in row $i$, column $j$. A [basis](../../../../../basis.md) for the [vector space](../../../../../vector-space-split.md) of real [skew-symmetric matrices](../../../../../skew-symmetric-matrix.md) is

$$
\boxed{\{E_{ij}-E_{ji}:1\leq i<j\leq n\},\qquad
\dim=\frac{n(n-1)}2.}
$$

Each upper-triangular entry is free, its reflected lower-triangular entry is its negative, and every diagonal entry is zero. This is the [basis of the space of real skew-symmetric matrices](../../../../../basis-of-the-space-of-real-skew-symmetric-matrices.md).
