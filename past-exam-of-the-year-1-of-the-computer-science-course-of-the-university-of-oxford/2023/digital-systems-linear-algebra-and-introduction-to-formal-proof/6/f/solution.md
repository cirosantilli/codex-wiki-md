<h1 id="6/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

For a real [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $\lambda$, choose a nonzero real [eigenvector](../../../../../eigenvector.md) $v$ with $Av=\lambda v$. By (e),

$$
0=v^\top Av=\lambda v^\top v.
$$

Since $v^\top v=\|v\|^2>0$, **$\lambda=0$**. If [eigenvectors](../../../../../eigenvector.md) were initially allowed to be complex, the real [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) $A-\lambda I$ has a nonzero real [null space](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) whenever it has a nonzero complex [null space](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md), so this choice of $v$ remains justified.
