<h1 id="6/h/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [H](../h.md)

If $(I+A)x=0$, take its [dot product](../../../../../dot-product.md) with $x$ and apply (e):

$$
0=x^\top(I+A)x=\|x\|^2+x^\top Ax=\|x\|^2.
$$

Hence $x=0$. A square [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) with zero [null space](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) is an [invertible matrix](../../../../../invertible-matrix.md) by the [rank-nullity theorem](../../../../../rank-nullity-theorem.md). Therefore **$I+A$ is nonsingular**. This proves [invertibility of identity plus a real skew-symmetric matrix](../../../../../invertibility-of-identity-plus-a-real-skew-symmetric-matrix.md).
