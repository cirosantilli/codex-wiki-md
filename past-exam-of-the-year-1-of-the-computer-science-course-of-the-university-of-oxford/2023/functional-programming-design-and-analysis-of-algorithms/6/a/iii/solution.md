↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [strongly connected components](../../../../../../strongly-connected-component.md) are

$$
\boxed{\{1,3,5\},\quad\{2\},\quad\{4\},\quad\{6\}.}
$$

The [directed cycle](../../../../../../directed-cycle.md) $1\to3\to5\to1$ makes its three vertices mutually reachable. Vertex $6$ has no outgoing edge. Vertex $2$ reaches that cycle but has no return path from it, and vertex $4$ reaches the other components but has no incoming path from them. These facts exclude every further merger.
