<h1 id="7/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Apply the [law of total expectation](../../../../../../law-of-total-expectation.md) to the bounded [random variable](../../../../../../random-variable-split.md) $R_{k+1}$. [Linearity of expectation](../../../../../../linearity-of-expectation.md) gives

$$
r_{k+1}=\mathbb E\!\left[\mathbb E[R_{k+1}\mid R_k]\right]
=\mathbb E\!\left[1+\left(1-\frac2n\right)R_k\right]
=\boxed{1+\left(1-\frac2n\right)r_k}.
$$

This also holds for the first step from $r_0=n$.
