For $\mathbb P(X=x)>0$, the [conditional probability mass function](../../../../../../conditional-probability-mass-function.md) is

$$
\boxed{p_{Y\mid X=x}(y)=\frac{\mathbb P(X=x,Y=y)}{\mathbb P(X=x)}}\quad(y\in S).
$$

The positive denominator makes the expression well-defined, and its [sum](../../../../../../sum.md) over $y$ is one.
