<h1 id="1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For the [pointwise order on real polynomials](../../../../../../pointwise-order-on-real-polynomials.md), $p(x)\le p(x)$ proves [reflexivity](../../../../../../reflexive-relation.md). Two opposite inequalities imply $p(x)=q(x)$ for every real $x$; the [polynomials](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) are therefore equal, proving the [antisymmetric relation](../../../../../../antisymmetric-relation.md) axiom. Chaining the inequalities at every $x$ proves [transitivity](../../../../../../transitive-relation.md).

To disprove [total order](../../../../../../total-order.md), take $p(x)=x$ and $q(x)=0$. The inequality $p\preceq q$ fails at $x=1$, whereas $q\preceq p$ fails at $x=-1$. **The order is partial but non-total.**
