A [total order](../../../../../../total-order.md) is a [partial order](../../../../../../partially-ordered-set.md) in which every pair is comparable: for every $a,b\in A$, at least one of $a\preceq b$ and $b\preceq a$ holds. Both hold exactly when $a=b$.
