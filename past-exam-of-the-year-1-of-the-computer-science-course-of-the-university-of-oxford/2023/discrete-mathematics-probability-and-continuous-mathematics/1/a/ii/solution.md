A [partial order](../../../../../../partially-ordered-set.md) is a [preorder](../../../../../../preorder.md) satisfying the [antisymmetric relation](../../../../../../antisymmetric-relation.md) axiom. In addition to [reflexivity](../../../../../../reflexive-relation.md) and [transitivity](../../../../../../transitive-relation.md), it satisfies $a\preceq b$ and $b\preceq a$ only when $a=b$.
