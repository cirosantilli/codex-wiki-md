<h1 id="1/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For every $x\in A$, membership in the relevant [equivalence class](../../../../../../equivalence-class.md) satisfies

$$
x\in[a]_{R\cap S}\iff a(R\cap S)x\iff (aRx\text{ and }aSx)\iff x\in[a]_R\cap[a]_S.
$$

Equality follows by [axiom of extensionality](../../../../../../axiom-of-extensionality.md). Thus **$[a]_{R\cap S}=[a]_R\cap[a]_S$**.
