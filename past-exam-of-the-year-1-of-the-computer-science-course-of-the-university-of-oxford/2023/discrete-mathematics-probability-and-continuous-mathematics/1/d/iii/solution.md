↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [floor function](../../../../../../floor-function.md) is constant at $j$ on the four consecutive [integers](../../../../../../integer-split.md) $4j,4j+1,4j+2,4j+3$. Consequently the [partition of a set](../../../../../../partition-of-a-set.md) is **$\{\{4j,4j+1,4j+2,4j+3\}:j\in\mathbb N\}$**. Different blocks are disjoint and every [nonnegative integer](../../../../../../natural-number.md) has exactly one quotient and remainder upon division by $4$.
