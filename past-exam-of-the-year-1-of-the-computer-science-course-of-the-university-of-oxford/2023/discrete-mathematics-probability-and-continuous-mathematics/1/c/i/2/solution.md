A [least upper bound](../../../../../../../least-upper-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md) of $S$ is an element $u\in A$ such that $s\preceq u$ for every $s\in S$, and such that $u\preceq v$ for every other [upper bound](../../../../../../../upper-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md) $v\in A$ of $S$. It need not belong to $S$. The [antisymmetric relation](../../../../../../../antisymmetric-relation.md) axiom makes it unique whenever it exists.
