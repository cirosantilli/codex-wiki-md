The [greatest element](../../../../../../../greatest-element.md) of $S$, also called its maximum, is an element $m\in S$ such that $s\preceq m$ for every $s\in S$. Membership in $S$ is essential; an [upper bound](../../../../../../../upper-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md) need not belong to $S$.
