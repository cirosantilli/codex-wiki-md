The [greatest common divisor](../../../../../../greatest-common-divisor.md) of positive [integers](../../../../../../integer-split.md) $a,b$ is **the largest positive [integer](../../../../../../integer-split.md) dividing both**, denoted $\gcd(a,b)$. The set of positive common divisors contains $1$ and is finite, so this definition supplies a value.
