<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The allowed [primes](../../../../../prime-number-split.md) are exactly $2,3,5,7$. Define

$$
\Phi(2^a3^b5^c7^d)=(a,b,c,d),\qquad a,b,c,d\in\mathbb N.
$$

The [fundamental theorem of arithmetic](../../../../../fundamental-theorem-of-arithmetic.md) makes this definition unambiguous and proves [injectivity](../../../../../injective-function.md). For every $(a,b,c,d)\in\mathbb N^4$, the positive [integer](../../../../../integer-split.md) $2^a3^b5^c7^d$ belongs to $S$ and maps to that tuple, proving [surjectivity](../../../../../surjective-function.md). The [inverse function](../../../../../inverse-function.md) is explicitly $\Phi^{-1}(a,b,c,d)=2^a3^b5^c7^d$. In particular, $1$ maps to $(0,0,0,0)$. This is the [prime-exponent parametrization of smooth numbers](../../../../../prime-exponent-parametrization-of-smooth-numbers.md).
