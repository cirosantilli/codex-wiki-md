**No.** Both $f(x)=x$ and $g(x)=-x$ are [affine functions](../../../../../affine-function.md), hence [convex functions](../../../../../convex-function.md). Their product $h(x)=-x^2$ is not [convex](../../../../../convex-function.md): at the midpoint of $-1$ and $1$, $h(0)=0>\tfrac12h(-1)+\tfrac12h(1)=-1$. Thus [products of convex functions need not be convex](../../../../../products-of-convex-functions-need-not-be-convex.md), even for smooth functions on all of $\mathbb R$.
