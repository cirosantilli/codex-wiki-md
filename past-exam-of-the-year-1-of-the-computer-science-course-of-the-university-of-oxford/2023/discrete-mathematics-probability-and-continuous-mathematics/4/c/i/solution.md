<h1 id="4/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Differentiating on the given domain yields

$$
j'(x)=\frac1{x\ln x},\qquad j''(x)=-\frac{\ln x+1}{x^2(\ln x)^2}<0\quad(x>1).
$$

The denominator and $\ln x+1$ are positive there. Thus **$j$ is strictly [concave](../../../../../../concave-function.md)**, and is not [convex](../../../../../../convex-function.md).
