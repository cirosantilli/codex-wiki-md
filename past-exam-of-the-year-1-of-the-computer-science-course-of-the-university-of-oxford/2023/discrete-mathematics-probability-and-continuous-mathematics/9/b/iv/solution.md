↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The nonnegative integer-valued [tail-sum formula for expectation](../../../../../../tail-sum-formula-for-expectation.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[\lfloor X\rfloor]=\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\mathbb P(\lfloor X\rfloor\ge k)=\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}k^{-(t-1)}=\zeta(t-1).}
$$

This is finite because $t-1>1$. Here $\zeta$ is the [Riemann zeta function](../../../../../../riemann-zeta-function.md) on its convergent real domain. The [floor function](../../../../../../floor-function.md) is nonnegative on the support, so the tail-sum identity applies directly.
