<h1 id="9/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [expected value](../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) is finite because $t>2$:

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[X]=(t-1)\int_1^{\infty}x^{1-t}\,dx=\frac{t-1}{t-2}.}
$$

The condition on $t$ is precisely the first [moment](../../../../../../moment.md) threshold for this parameterization of the [Pareto distribution](../../../../../../pareto-distribution.md).
