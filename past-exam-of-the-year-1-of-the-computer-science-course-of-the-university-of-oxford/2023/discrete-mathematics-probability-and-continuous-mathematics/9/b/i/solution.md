<h1 id="9/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Normalization of the [probability density function](../../../../../../probability-density-function.md) requires $1=c\int_1^{\infty}x^{-t}\,dx=c/(t-1)$. Hence **$c=t-1$**. It is positive for the given $t>2$, and the density is nonnegative, so this does define a [probability distribution](../../../../../../probability-distribution-split.md). It is a [Pareto distribution](../../../../../../pareto-distribution.md) with scale $1$ and shape $t-1$.
