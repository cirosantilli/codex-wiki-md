The [cumulative distribution function](../../../../../../cumulative-distribution-function.md) is **$F_X(x)=\mathbb P(X\le x)=\int_{-\infty}^{x}f(u)\,du$**. This formula uses the assumed [probability density function](../../../../../../probability-density-function.md); the density itself need not be a continuous function.
