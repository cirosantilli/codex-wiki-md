An [absolutely continuous probability distribution](../../../../../../absolutely-continuous-probability-distribution.md) assigns zero probability to a singleton, because its density has zero [integral](../../../../../../integral-split.md) over that set. Thus **$\mathbb P(X=x)=0$** for every real $x$, including points where a chosen version of $f$ is nonzero.
