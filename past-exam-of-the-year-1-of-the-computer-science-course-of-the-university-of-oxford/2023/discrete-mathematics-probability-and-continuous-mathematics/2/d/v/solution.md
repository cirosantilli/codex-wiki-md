<h1 id="2/d/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

Fix $b\in B$. Since $g\circ f$ is [surjective](../../../../../../surjective-function.md), there is $a\in A$ with $g(f(a))=g(b)$. [Injectivity](../../../../../../injective-function.md) of $g$ gives $f(a)=b$, proving that $f$ is [surjective](../../../../../../surjective-function.md). All five conclusions are instances of [injectivity and surjectivity under composition](../../../../../../injectivity-and-surjectivity-under-composition.md).
