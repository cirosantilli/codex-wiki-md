↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Suppose $(g\circ f)(a)=(g\circ f)(a')$. [Injectivity](../../../../../../injective-function.md) of $g$ gives $f(a)=f(a')$, and [injectivity](../../../../../../injective-function.md) of $f$ then gives $a=a'$. Thus the [function composition](../../../../../../function-composition.md) is [injective](../../../../../../injective-function.md).
