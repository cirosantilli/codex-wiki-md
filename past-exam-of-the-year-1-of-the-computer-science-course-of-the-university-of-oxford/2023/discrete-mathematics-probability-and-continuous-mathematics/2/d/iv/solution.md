<h1 id="2/d/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Suppose $g(b)=g(b')$. Since $f$ is [surjective](../../../../../../surjective-function.md), choose $a,a'\in A$ with $f(a)=b$ and $f(a')=b'$. Then $(g\circ f)(a)=(g\circ f)(a')$, so [injectivity](../../../../../../injective-function.md) gives $a=a'$ and therefore $b=b'$. Thus $g$ is [injective](../../../../../../injective-function.md). The use of [surjectivity](../../../../../../surjective-function.md) is what makes every point of $B$ accessible to this argument.
