<h1 id="2/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For any $c\in C$, [surjectivity](../../../../../../surjective-function.md) of $g\circ f$ supplies $a\in A$ with $g(f(a))=c$. Taking $b=f(a)\in B$ proves that $c$ has a preimage under $g$. Thus $g$ is [surjective](../../../../../../surjective-function.md).
