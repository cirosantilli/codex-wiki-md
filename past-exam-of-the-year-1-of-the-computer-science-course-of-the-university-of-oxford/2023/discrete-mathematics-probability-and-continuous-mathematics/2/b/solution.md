<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

At $n=2$, the product is $1-1/4=3/4$, as required. Suppose the formula holds at $n\ge2$. Appending the next factor gives

$$
\prod_{i=2}^{n+1}\left(1-\frac1{i^2}\right)
=\frac{n+1}{2n}\frac{n(n+2)}{(n+1)^2}
=\frac{n+2}{2(n+1)},
$$

which is exactly the formula at $n+1$. Thus [mathematical induction](../../../../../mathematical-induction.md) gives **$(n+1)/(2n)$** for every $n\ge2$. The factorization $1-i^{-2}=(i-1)(i+1)/i^2$ also explains the cancellation in this [telescoping product](../../../../../telescoping-product.md).
