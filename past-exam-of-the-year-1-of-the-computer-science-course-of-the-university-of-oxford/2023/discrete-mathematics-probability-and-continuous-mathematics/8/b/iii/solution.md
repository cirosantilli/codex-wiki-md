<h1 id="8/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Set $p_*=p/[p+(1-p)r]$. The preceding [probability generating function](../../../../../../probability-generating-function.md) becomes $p_*/[1-(1-p_*)s]$, identifying

$$
\boxed{Z\sim\operatorname{Geom}_0\!\left(\frac{p}{p+(1-p)r}\right).}
$$

Equivalently, for $z\in\mathbb N$, $\mathbb P(Z=z)=p_*[(1-p_*)]^z$. Since $0<p,r<1$, also $0<p_*<1$. This is the existing [geometric count under Bernoulli thinning](../../../../../../geometric-count-under-bernoulli-thinning.md), with the zero-based convention specified.
