↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

In [independent](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) [Bernoulli trials](../../../../../../bernoulli-trial.md) with success probability $p$, $N$ counts **failures before the first success**. The [event](../../../../../../event.md) $N=n$ requires $n$ failures followed by one success, giving $(1-p)^np$. This is the zero-based [geometric distribution](../../../../../../geometric-distribution.md), rather than the count of trials including the success.
