<h1 id="8/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Differentiate the [probability generating function](../../../../../../probability-generating-function.md) inside its radius of convergence:

$$
G_N'(s)=\frac{pq}{(1-qs)^2},\qquad G_N''(s)=\frac{2pq^2}{(1-qs)^3}.
$$

The first [moment](../../../../../../moment.md) and second factorial [moment](../../../../../../moment.md) are $\mathbb E[N]=G_N'(1)=q/p$ and $\mathbb E[N(N-1)]=G_N''(1)=2q^2/p^2$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[N]=\frac{1-p}{p},\qquad
\operatorname{Var}(N)=\frac{2q^2}{p^2}+\frac qp-\frac{q^2}{p^2}=\frac{1-p}{p^2}.}
$$
