<h1 id="5/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The origin is feasible and has objective zero, while a [Euclidean norm](../../../../../../euclidean-norm.md) is nonnegative. Thus **the [minimum](../../../../../../minimum-of-a-subset-of-a-total-order.md) is $0$**, and the complete [argmin](../../../../../../argmin.md) is

$$
\boxed{\ker A\cap\{\mathbf x:\|\mathbf x\|\le1\}.}
$$

If $A$ is [injective](../../../../../../injective-function.md), this set contains only the origin. If it has a nontrivial [kernel](../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md), every feasible [kernel](../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) vector is also a minimizer. No [Lagrange multipliers](../../../../../../lagrange-multiplier.md) are needed.
