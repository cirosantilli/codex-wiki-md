<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Eliminate the constraint by writing $y=1/x$, with $x>0$. The [logarithms](../../../../../logarithm.md) cancel, so the objective becomes $q(x)=1/x+2x$. Its [derivatives](../../../../../derivative.md) are $q'(x)=-1/x^2+2$ and $q''(x)=2/x^3>0$. Thus the unique stationary point is the unique global [minimum](../../../../../minimum-of-a-subset-of-a-total-order.md), at $x=1/\sqrt2$. Restoring $y$ gives

$$
\boxed{\operatorname*{arg\,min}=\{(1/\sqrt2,\sqrt2)\},\qquad\min=2\sqrt2.}
$$

As a direct global check, the [arithmetic-geometric mean inequality](../../../../../arithmetic-geometric-mean-inequality.md) gives $1/x+2x\ge2\sqrt2$, with equality exactly at the same point.
