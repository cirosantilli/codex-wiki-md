<h1 id="6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

[Taylor's theorem](../../../../../taylor-s-theorem.md) at zero gives $f(x)=-1000+x+\tfrac12x^2f''(\xi)$ for a point $\xi$ between zero and $x$. At $x=1000$, strict negativity of the second [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) gives $f(1000)<0$. At $x=2000$, its lower bound gives

$$
f(2000)>-1000+2000-\frac{2000^2}{8000}=500>0.
$$

By the [intermediate value theorem](../../../../../intermediate-value-theorem.md) there is a root strictly between these values. The given uniqueness identifies it as **$1000<x^*<2000$**.
