↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [loop invariant](../../../../../loop-invariant.md) is a property of the [program state](../../../../../program-state.md) that holds whenever control reaches the loop's condition, including the final failed test. Its main purpose is proving [partial correctness](../../../../../partial-correctness.md): establish it initially, prove one guarded iteration preserves it, then combine it with the negated condition to obtain the [postcondition](../../../../../postcondition.md). A separate [loop variant](../../../../../loop-variant.md) or another progress argument proves [algorithm termination](../../../../../algorithm-termination.md) and hence [total correctness](../../../../../total-correctness.md).
