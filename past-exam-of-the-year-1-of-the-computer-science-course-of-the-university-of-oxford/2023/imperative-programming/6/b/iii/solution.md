<h1 id="6/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**Permitted; returns `"AB"`.** A receiver of [static type](../../../../../../static-type.md) `B` exposes `show(A)` and `show(B)`, but the argument's [static type](../../../../../../static-type.md) `A` is not a [subtype](../../../../../../subtyping.md) of `B`. Only `show(A)` is applicable. Its effective implementation on the `C` receiver is `B.show(A)`, which concatenates the superclass result `"A"` and `"B"`.
