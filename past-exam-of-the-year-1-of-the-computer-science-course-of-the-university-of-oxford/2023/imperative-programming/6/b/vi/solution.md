↑ **Parent:** [Vi](../vi.md)

**Permitted; returns `"C"`.** Both receiver and argument have [static type](../../../../../../static-type.md) `C`, so the most-specific applicable overload is `show(C)`. That method is defined in `C` and returns its own one-letter result without calling `super`. The six results, in source order, are therefore **`AB, AB, AB, BC, BC, C`**.
