<h1 id="1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Because $A\subseteq\mathcal U$, the [set difference](../../../../../../set-difference.md) $A\setminus\mathcal U$ is empty and $\mathcal U\setminus A=\overline A$. Therefore the [symmetric difference](../../../../../../symmetric-difference.md) satisfies

$$
A\oplus\mathcal U=(A\setminus\mathcal U)\cup(\mathcal U\setminus A)=\varnothing\cup\overline A=\boxed{\overline A}.
$$

The [disjoint sets](../../../../../../disjoint-sets.md) $A$ and $\overline A$ have [union](../../../../../../set-union.md) $\mathcal U$, so

$$
A\oplus\overline A=(A\setminus\overline A)\cup(\overline A\setminus A)=A\cup\overline A=\boxed{\mathcal U}.
$$
