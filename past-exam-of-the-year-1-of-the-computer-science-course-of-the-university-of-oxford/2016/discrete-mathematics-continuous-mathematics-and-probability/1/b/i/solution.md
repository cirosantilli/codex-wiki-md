<h1 id="1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use the definition $A\oplus B=(A\setminus B)\cup(B\setminus A)$ of [symmetric difference](../../../../../../symmetric-difference.md). The algebraic laws for [set difference](../../../../../../set-difference.md) and the [empty set](../../../../../../empty-set.md) give

$$
A\oplus\varnothing=(A\setminus\varnothing)\cup(\varnothing\setminus A)=A\cup\varnothing=\boxed A,
$$

and

$$
A\oplus A=(A\setminus A)\cup(A\setminus A)=\varnothing\cup\varnothing=\boxed\varnothing.
$$
