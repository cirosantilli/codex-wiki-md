<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Apply [symmetric difference](../../../../../symmetric-difference.md) with $C$ to both sides, then use its [associativity](../../../../../associative-property.md) and the identities in (b)(i):

$$
(A\oplus C)\oplus C=(B\oplus C)\oplus C
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
A\oplus(C\oplus C)=B\oplus(C\oplus C)
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
A\oplus\varnothing=B\oplus\varnothing.
$$

The final identity gives **$A=B$**. This is cancellation by applying the same operation twice.
