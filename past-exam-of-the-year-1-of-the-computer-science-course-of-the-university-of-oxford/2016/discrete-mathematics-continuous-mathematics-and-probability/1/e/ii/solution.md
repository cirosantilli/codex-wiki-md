↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A [bijection](../../../../../../bijection.md) between [finite sets](../../../../../../finite-set.md) requires equal [cardinality](../../../../../../cardinality.md). When $m=n$, list the elements of $A$ and choose their distinct images in $n,n-1,\ldots,1$ ways. Thus

$$
\boxed{\#\{\text{bijections }A\to B\}=\begin{cases}n!&m=n,\\0&m\ne n.\end{cases}}
$$

The [factorial](../../../../../../factorial.md) convention $0!=1$ includes the unique [bijection](../../../../../../bijection.md) from the [empty set](../../../../../../empty-set.md) to itself.
