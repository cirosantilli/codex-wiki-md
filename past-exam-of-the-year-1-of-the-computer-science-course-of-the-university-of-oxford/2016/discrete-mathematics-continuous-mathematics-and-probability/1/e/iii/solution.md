<h1 id="1/e/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For a [surjection](../../../../../../surjective-function.md), the nonempty [fibres](../../../../../../fiber-of-a-function.md) $f^{-1}(b)$ form a [partition of a set](../../../../../../partition-of-a-set.md) $A$ into exactly $n$ blocks. Choose these unlabelled blocks in $S(m,n)$ ways, then assign the $n$ distinct elements of $B$ to them in $n!$ ways. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\#\{\text{surjections }A\to B\}=n!S(m,n).}
$$

It is zero when $m<n$. With $S(0,0)=1$ and $S(m,0)=0$ for $m>0$, the same formula also handles an [empty set](../../../../../../empty-set.md) codomain.
