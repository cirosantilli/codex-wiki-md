<h1 id="1/e/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Each of the $m$ elements of $A$ independently receives one of $n$ images in $B$. The [multiplication principle](../../../../../../rule-of-product.md) therefore gives **$n^m$ functions**. In particular an [empty set](../../../../../../empty-set.md) domain has exactly one function to any $B$, so here $0^0$ is interpreted as $1$; a nonempty domain has no function to an [empty set](../../../../../../empty-set.md) codomain.
