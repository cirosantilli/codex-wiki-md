<h1 id="1/e/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Choose the [image of a function](../../../../../../image-of-a-function.md) as a $j$-element [subset](../../../../../../subset.md) of $B$, then choose a [surjection](../../../../../../surjective-function.md) onto that selected image as in (iii). Therefore

$$
\boxed{\#\{f:A\to B:|\operatorname{Im}f|=j\}=\binom nj j!S(m,j)\quad(0\leq j\leq\min(m,n)).}
$$

The answer is zero when $j>n$. If $j=0$, it is one for $m=0$ and zero for $m>0$, as the [Stirling numbers of the second kind](../../../../../../stirling-numbers-of-the-second-kind.md) conventions require.
