<h1 id="5/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Apply [integration by parts](../../../../../../integration-by-parts.md) with $u=x$ and $dv=\sin(\mu x)\,dx$. Part (i) gives $v=-\cos(\mu x)/\mu$, so

$$
\int x\sin(\mu x)\,dx=-\frac{x\cos(\mu x)}\mu+\frac1\mu\int\cos(\mu x)\,dx
=\boxed{-\frac{x\cos(\mu x)}\mu+\frac{\sin(\mu x)}{\mu^2}+C.}
$$
