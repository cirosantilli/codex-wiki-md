In the [partially ordered set](../../../../../../partially-ordered-set.md) $(A,\preceq)$, the assertion $m=\operatorname{lub}(S)$ means both

$$
\boxed{(\forall s\in S,\ s\preceq m)\quad\text{and}\quad
(\forall u\in A,\ [\forall s\in S,\ s\preceq u]\Rightarrow m\preceq u).}
$$

The first condition makes $m$ an [upper bound](../../../../../../upper-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md); the second makes it the [least upper bound](../../../../../../least-upper-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md). Both conditions are necessary, and [antisymmetry](../../../../../../antisymmetry.md) makes such an element unique.
