<h1 id="6/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Write the derivative equation as $f'(x)=\Phi(x,f(x))$ with initial approximation $y_0$. The [forward Euler method](../../../../../../euler-method.md) uses the slope at the start of the step:

$$
\boxed{y_{i+1}=y_i+h\Phi(x_i,y_i),\qquad0\leq i<n.}
$$

If the derivative is a known function of $x$ alone, this is $y_{i+1}=y_i+hf'(x_i)$.
