<h1 id="6/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For the same uniform grid and even $n$, the [composite Simpson's rule](../../../../../../composite-simpson-s-rule.md) integrates a quadratic interpolant over each successive pair of strips:

$$
\boxed{S_n=\frac h3\left[f(x_0)+f(x_n)+4\sum_{j=1}^{n/2}f(x_{2j-1})+2\sum_{j=1}^{n/2-1}f(x_{2j})\right].}
$$

The weights are $1,4,2,4,\ldots,2,4,1$, which explains the even number of strips.
