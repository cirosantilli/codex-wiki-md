↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a nonnegative integer-valued [random variable](../../../../../../random-variable-split.md) $X$, its [probability generating function](../../../../../../probability-generating-function.md) is

$$
\boxed{G_X(s)=\mathbb E(s^X)=\sum_{j=0}^\infty\mathbb P(X=j)s^j,\qquad0\leq s\leq1.}
$$

At $s=0$, use $0^0=1$ so $G_X(0)=\mathbb P(X=0)$. The series also converges absolutely for complex $|s|\leq1$; for $s$ near zero, its coefficients recover the [probability mass function](../../../../../../probability-mass-function.md).
