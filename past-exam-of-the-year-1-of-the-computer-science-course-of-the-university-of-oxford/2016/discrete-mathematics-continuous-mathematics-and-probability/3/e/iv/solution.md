↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

For each fixed positive [integer](../../../../../../integer-split.md) $k$, part (i) gives $(f(n)+g(n))^k\leq(2h(n))^k=2^kh(n)^k$. This meets [big O notation](../../../../../../big-o-notation.md) with constant $2^k$ and threshold $1$. **True: $(f+g)^k=O(h^k)$ for fixed $k$.** The constant may depend on $k$; no claim uniform in a varying $k$ is required.
