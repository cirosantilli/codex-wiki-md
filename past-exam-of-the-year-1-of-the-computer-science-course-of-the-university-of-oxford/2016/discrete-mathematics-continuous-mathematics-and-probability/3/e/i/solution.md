↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Since $h(n)=\max\{f(n),g(n)\}$, both $f(n)\leq h(n)$ and $g(n)\leq h(n)$. Therefore $f(n)+g(n)\leq2h(n)$ for every positive $n$. The definition of [big O notation](../../../../../../big-o-notation.md) is satisfied with constant $2$ and threshold $1$. **True: $f+g=O(h)$.**
