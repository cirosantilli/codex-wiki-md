<h1 id="3/e/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Take $f(n)=g(n)=n^2$, so $h(n)=n^2$ but $(f\circ g)(n)=n^4$. The ratio $(f\circ g)(n)/h(n)=n^2$ is unbounded, precluding any eventual constant upper bound in [big O notation](../../../../../../big-o-notation.md). **False: in general $f\circ g\ne O(h)$.** This is a [counterexample](../../../../../../counterexample.md) involving [function composition](../../../../../../function-composition.md), not pointwise addition.
