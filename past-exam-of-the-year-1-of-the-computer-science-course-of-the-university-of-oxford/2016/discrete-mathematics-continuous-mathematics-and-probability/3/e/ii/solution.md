↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Positivity gives $\max\{f(n),g(n)\}\leq f(n)+g(n)$. Thus $h(n)\leq f(n)+g(n)$ for every positive $n$, meeting [big O notation](../../../../../../big-o-notation.md) with constant $1$ and threshold $1$. **True: $h=O(f+g)$.**
