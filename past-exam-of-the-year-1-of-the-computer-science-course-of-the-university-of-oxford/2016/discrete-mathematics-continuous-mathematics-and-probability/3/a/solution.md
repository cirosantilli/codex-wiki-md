<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

We prove the [contrapositive](../../../../../contrapositive.md). If $3\nmid a$, [Euclidean division](../../../../../euclidean-division.md) gives $a=3q+1$ or $a=3q+2$ for an [integer](../../../../../integer-split.md) $q$. Squaring gives respectively $a^2=3(3q^2+2q)+1$ or $a^2=3(3q^2+4q+1)+1$. Both have remainder $1$ upon division by $3$, so $3\nmid a^2$. Taking the [contrapositive](../../../../../contrapositive.md) yields **$3\mid a^2\Rightarrow3\mid a$**.
