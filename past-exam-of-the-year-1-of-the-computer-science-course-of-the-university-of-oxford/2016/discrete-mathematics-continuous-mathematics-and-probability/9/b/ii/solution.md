↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [law of the unconscious statistician](../../../../../../law-of-the-unconscious-statistician.md) and the finite [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md) give

$$
\mathbb E(Y)=\int_0^\infty xg(x)\,dx
=\frac1\mu\int_0^\infty x^2f(x)\,dx
=\frac{\mathbb E(X^2)}\mu
=\boxed{\mu+\frac{\sigma^2}\mu}.
$$

Thus [size bias](../../../../../../size-bias.md) increases the [mean](../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) by $\sigma^2/\mu$, in accord with preferentially selecting larger values.
