↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Summing the [probability mass function](../../../../../../probability-mass-function.md) of the [binomial distribution](../../../../../../binomial-distribution.md) up to the largest permitted integer gives

$$
\boxed{\sum_{0\leq k\leq\alpha n}\binom nkp^k(1-p)^{n-k}
=\mathbb P(S_n\leq\lfloor\alpha n\rfloor)
=\mathbb P(S_n/n\leq\alpha).}
$$

The floor is implicit in the original integer-indexed sum.
