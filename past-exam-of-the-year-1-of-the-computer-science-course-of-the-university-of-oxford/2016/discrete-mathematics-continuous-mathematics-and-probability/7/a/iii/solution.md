<h1 id="7/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

By [linearity of expectation](../../../../../../linearity-of-expectation.md), $\mathbb E(S_n)=\sum_{i=1}^n p=np$. Part (ii) gives $\operatorname{Var}(X_i)=\mathbb E(X_i^2)-[\mathbb E(X_i)]^2=p-p^2$. [Independent random variables](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) have zero pairwise [covariance](../../../../../../covariance.md), so the [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md) of their sum is the sum of their [variances](../../../../../../variance-split.md). Therefore

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E(S_n)=np,\qquad\operatorname{Var}(S_n)=np(1-p).}
$$
