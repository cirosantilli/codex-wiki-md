<h1 id="7/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The $X_i$ are independent [Bernoulli random variables](../../../../../../bernoulli-distribution.md) with success parameter $p$. Their sum has the **[binomial distribution](../../../../../../binomial-distribution.md) $S_n\sim\operatorname{Bin}(n,p)$**, whose [probability mass function](../../../../../../probability-mass-function.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(S_n=k)=\binom nk p^k(1-p)^{n-k},\qquad k=0,\ldots,n.}
$$

Choose the $k$ successful events in $\binom nk$ ways; [independence](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) gives the same product probability for each choice. At $p=0$ or $p=1$, interpret the formula by its degenerate distribution or by continuity.
