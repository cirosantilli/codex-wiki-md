<h1 id="7/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

An [indicator random variable](../../../../../../indicator-random-variable.md) equals $1$ with probability $p$ and $0$ with probability $1-p$, so $\mathbb E(X_i)=1\cdot p+0\cdot(1-p)=p$. Also $X_i^2=X_i$ pointwise, whence

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E(X_i)=\mathbb E(X_i^2)=p.}
$$
