<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Seek a [particular solution](../../../../../particular-solution.md) $y_p=ax+b$. Substitution into the [linear ordinary differential equation](../../../../../linear-ordinary-differential-equation-split.md) gives $6ax+2b=6x+2$, so $a=b=1$. Add the [complementary solution](../../../../../homogeneous-solution.md) from part (a):

$$
y=x+1+\frac A x+\frac B{x^2}.
$$

The two prescribed values yield $A+B=5$ and $A/2+B/4=3/2$. Thus $2A+B=6$, so $A=1$ and $B=4$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{y(x)=x+1+\frac1x+\frac4{x^2}\quad(x>0)}.
$$

Direct substitution gives $y(1)=7$ and $y(2)=9/2$, and the [homogeneous solution](../../../../../homogeneous-solution.md) contributes zero to the left-hand differential operator.
