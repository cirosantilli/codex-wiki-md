<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

This is the [pointwise order](../../../../../pointwise-order.md) on a [function space](../../../../../function-space.md). Each of the three [partial order](../../../../../partially-ordered-set.md) axioms follows from the corresponding axiom in $B$.

For [reflexivity](../../../../../reflexive-relation.md), $f(x)\preceq_B f(x)$ for every $x$, so $f\preceq_{\mathcal F}f$. For [antisymmetry](../../../../../antisymmetry.md), if $f\preceq_{\mathcal F}g$ and $g\preceq_{\mathcal F}f$, then $f(x)=g(x)$ for every $x\in A$; equality of [functions](../../../../../function-split.md) therefore gives $f=g$. For [transitivity](../../../../../transitive-relation.md), if $f\preceq_{\mathcal F}g\preceq_{\mathcal F}h$, then $f(x)\preceq_B g(x)\preceq_B h(x)$ for every $x$, so $f\preceq_{\mathcal F}h$.

Thus **$(\mathcal F,\preceq_{\mathcal F})$ is a [partially ordered set](../../../../../partially-ordered-set.md)**. The argument also covers an empty domain $A$, since then there is exactly one [function](../../../../../function-split.md) $A\to B$.
