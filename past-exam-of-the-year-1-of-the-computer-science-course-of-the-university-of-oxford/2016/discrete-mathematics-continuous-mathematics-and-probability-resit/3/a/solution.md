A [preorder](../../../../../preorder.md) is a [binary relation](../../../../../binary-relation.md) that is [reflexive](../../../../../reflexive-relation.md) and [transitive](../../../../../transitive-relation.md): $a\preceq a$, and $a\preceq b\preceq c$ implies $a\preceq c$.

A [partial order](../../../../../partially-ordered-set.md) is a [preorder](../../../../../preorder.md) that is also an [antisymmetric relation](../../../../../antisymmetric-relation.md): $a\preceq b$ and $b\preceq a$ together imply $a=b$.

A [linear order](../../../../../linear-order.md), also called a [total order](../../../../../total-order.md), is a [partial order](../../../../../partially-ordered-set.md) in which every two elements are comparable: **$a\preceq b$ or $b\preceq a$ for all $a,b\in A$**.
