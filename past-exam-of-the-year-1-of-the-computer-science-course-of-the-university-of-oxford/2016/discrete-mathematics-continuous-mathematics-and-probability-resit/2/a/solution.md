A [binary relation](../../../../../binary-relation.md) $R$ on $A$ is an [equivalence relation](../../../../../equivalence-relation.md) exactly when it is [reflexive](../../../../../reflexive-relation.md), [symmetric](../../../../../symmetric-relation.md), and [transitive](../../../../../transitive-relation.md):

$$
\begin{gathered}
aRa\quad(a\in A),\\
aRb\Longrightarrow bRa\quad(a,b\in A),\\
aRb\text{ and }bRc\Longrightarrow aRc\quad(a,b,c\in A).
\end{gathered}
$$

The [equivalence class](../../../../../equivalence-class.md) of $a\in A$ is **$[a]_R=\{b\in A:aRb\}$**. The [equivalence classes](../../../../../equivalence-class.md) partition $A$: every element belongs to its own class, and two classes are either equal or disjoint.
