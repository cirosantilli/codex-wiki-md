<h1 id="1/e/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Replace $x$ by $1/x$, which stays in the domain of the [functional equation](../../../../../../functional-equation.md). Writing $u=f(x)$ and $v=f(1/x)$ gives the [linear system](../../../../../../system-of-linear-equations.md)

$$
u+2v=x,\qquad 2u+v=\frac1x.
$$

Twice the second equation minus the first gives $3u=2/x-x$, so the only possible [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) is

$$
\boxed{f(x)=\frac{2/x-x}{3}\quad(x>0)}.
$$

To check existence as well as uniqueness, substitute this expression: $f(x)+2f(1/x)=(2/x-x+4x-2/x)/3=x$.
