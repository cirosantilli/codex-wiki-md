<h1 id="1/e/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The algebraic identity

$$
x^3+x^{-3}=(x+x^{-1})^3-3(x+x^{-1})
$$

suggests **$f(t)=t^3-3t$ for every $t>0$**, and substitution verifies the [functional equation](../../../../../../functional-equation.md).

In fact $x+1/x\geq2$, since $(x-1)^2/x\geq0$. Conversely, for each $t\geq2$, the [quadratic equation](../../../../../../quadratic-equation.md) $x^2-tx+1=0$ has a positive root $(t+\sqrt{t^2-4})/2$. Thus the [functional equation](../../../../../../functional-equation.md) forces $f(t)=t^3-3t$ exactly on $[2,\infty)$; its values on $(0,2)$ are arbitrary. The displayed [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) is one convenient choice on the full required domain.
