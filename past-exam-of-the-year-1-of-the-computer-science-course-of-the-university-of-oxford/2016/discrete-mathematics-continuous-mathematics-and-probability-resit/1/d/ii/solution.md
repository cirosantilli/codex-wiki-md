<h1 id="1/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For each $c\in C$, [surjectivity](../../../../../../surjective-function.md) of the [function composition](../../../../../../function-composition.md) gives $a\in A$ such that $g(f(a))=c$. Taking $b=f(a)\in B$ supplies a preimage of $c$ under $g$. Thus **$g$ is a [surjective function](../../../../../../surjective-function.md)**.
