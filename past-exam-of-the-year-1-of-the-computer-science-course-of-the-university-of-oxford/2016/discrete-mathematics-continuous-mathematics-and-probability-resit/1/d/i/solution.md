<h1 id="1/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [function composition](../../../../../../function-composition.md) $g\circ f:A\to C$ is defined by $(g\circ f)(a)=g(f(a))$. Suppose $f(a)=f(a')$. Applying $g$ gives $(g\circ f)(a)=(g\circ f)(a')$. Since the composite is an [injective function](../../../../../../injective-function.md), $a=a'$. Thus **$f$ is an [injective function](../../../../../../injective-function.md)**.
