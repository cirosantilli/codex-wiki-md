<h1 id="6/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

For $1<x_0<2$, the same [Newton's method](../../../../../newton-s-method-in-optimization.md) formula gives

$$
x_1-2=\frac{(x_0-2)^2}{2(x_0-1)}>0.
$$

Thus the first step **overshoots the root to $x_1>2$**. Part (e) then applies from index $1$: the remaining [sequence](../../../../../sequence.md) decreases to $2$. The first step can be very large when $x_0$ is close to $1$, where the derivative vanishes, but every starting value strictly between $1$ and $2$ still gives **convergence to $2$**. The entire sequence is not decreasing, since $x_1>2>x_0$.
