<h1 id="6/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The tangent line to $f$ at $x_n$ is $y=f(x_n)+f'(x_n)(x-x_n)$. Its intersection with the horizontal axis gives [Newton's method](../../../../../newton-s-method-in-optimization.md):

$$
\boxed{x_{n+1}=x_n-\frac{f(x_n)}{f'(x_n)}}.
$$

Each step requires $f'(x_n)\ne0$. Convergence to a [zero of a function](../../../../../zero-of-a-function.md) additionally depends on the starting point and the local behavior of $f$; the update formula alone is not a convergence guarantee.
