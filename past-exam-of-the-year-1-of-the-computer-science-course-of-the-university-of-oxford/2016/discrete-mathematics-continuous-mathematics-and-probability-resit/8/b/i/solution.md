↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For [independent random variables](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md), the [probability generating function of an independent sum](../../../../../../probability-generating-function-of-an-independent-sum.md) is the product of the individual [probability generating functions](../../../../../../probability-generating-function.md). Thus

$$
G_{X+Y}(s)=e^{\lambda(s-1)}e^{\mu(s-1)}=e^{(\lambda+\mu)(s-1)}.
$$

Expanding this last expression as $e^{-(\lambda+\mu)}\sum_{r\geq0}(\lambda+\mu)^rs^r/r!$ identifies each probability coefficient. Therefore

$$
\boxed{X+Y\sim\operatorname{Poisson}(\lambda+\mu)}.
$$
