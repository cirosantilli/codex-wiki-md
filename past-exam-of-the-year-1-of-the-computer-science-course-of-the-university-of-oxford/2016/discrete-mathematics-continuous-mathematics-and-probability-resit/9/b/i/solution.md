<h1 id="9/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Translate the inequality inside the [cumulative distribution function](../../../../../../cumulative-distribution-function.md):

$$
F_Y(y)=\mathbb P(1+X\leq y)=\mathbb P(X\leq y-1)=\boxed{F_X(y-1)}.
$$

Differentiating with the [chain rule](../../../../../../chain-rule.md) gives the [probability density function](../../../../../../probability-density-function.md)

$$
\boxed{f_Y(y)=f_X(y-1)}
$$

almost everywhere. Translation shifts the [probability distribution](../../../../../../probability-distribution-split.md) without changing its shape or its total probability.
