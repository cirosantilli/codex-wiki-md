The [cumulative distribution function](../../../../../../cumulative-distribution-function.md) is

$$
\boxed{F_X(x)=\mathbb P(X\leq x)}.
$$

For an [absolutely continuous probability distribution](../../../../../../absolutely-continuous-probability-distribution.md), the [probability density function](../../../../../../probability-density-function.md) is a nonnegative integrable [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) $f_X$ with

$$
\boxed{F_X(x)=\int_{-\infty}^{x}f_X(u)\,du,\qquad\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f_X(u)\,du=1}.
$$

Thus interval probabilities are [integrals](../../../../../../integral-split.md) of $f_X$, and $F_X'=f_X$ almost everywhere, in particular at continuity points of $f_X$.

The word “continuous” in the source is understood in this density sense. A merely continuous [cumulative distribution function](../../../../../../cumulative-distribution-function.md) need not possess a [probability density function](../../../../../../probability-density-function.md); it can instead describe a [singular continuous distribution](../../../../../../singular-continuous-distribution.md).
