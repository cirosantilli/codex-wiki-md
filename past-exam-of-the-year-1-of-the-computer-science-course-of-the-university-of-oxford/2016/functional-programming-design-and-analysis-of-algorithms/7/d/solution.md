[Dijkstra's algorithm](../../../../../dijkstra-algorithm.md) solves **the single-source [shortest path problem](../../../../../shortest-path-problem-split.md) in a finite weighted graph with nonnegative edge weights**. Given a source $s$, it computes for every [vertex](../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md) $v$ the minimum sum of edge weights on a path from $s$ to $v$, reporting $+\infty$ if $v$ is unreachable. Keeping a [shortest-path predecessor](../../../../../shortest-path-predecessor.md) also recovers the corresponding paths. Directed graphs are allowed; an undirected graph can be represented by two oppositely directed edges. Negative edge weights are outside the algorithm's standard correctness assumptions, while zero weights are allowed.
