A [divide-and-conquer algorithm](../../../../../divide-and-conquer-algorithm.md) **divides an input into smaller instances, solves those instances recursively, and combines their answers**. Directly solvable base cases terminate the [recursion](../../../../../recursion-computer-science.md). The total [time complexity](../../../../../time-complexity.md) includes both the recursive work and the work of dividing and combining; balanced sizes alone do not determine the running time.
