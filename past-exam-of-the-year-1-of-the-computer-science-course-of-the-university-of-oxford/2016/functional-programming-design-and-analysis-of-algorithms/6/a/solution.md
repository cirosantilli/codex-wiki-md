[Dynamic programming](../../../../../dynamic-programming.md) **solves a problem by storing solutions to overlapping subproblems and combining them according to a recurrence**. Each [dynamic programming state](../../../../../dynamic-programming-state.md) describes all information needed by subsequent choices. Evaluating states in an order where their dependencies are already available avoids recomputing the same subproblem. A typical optimization recurrence chooses the best transition; a decision recurrence instead combines feasible transitions with Boolean disjunction.
