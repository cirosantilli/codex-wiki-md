A [topological sort](../../../../../topological-sorting.md) is **a linear ordering of all [vertices](../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md) of a [Directed acyclic graph](../../../../../directed-acyclic-graph.md) in which every directed edge points from an earlier vertex to a later one**. For example, with $V=\{a,b,c\}$ and $E=\{(a,c),(b,c)\}$, both $(a,b,c)$ and $(b,a,c)$ are [topological sorts](../../../../../topological-sorting.md). They differ because neither edge nor directed path constrains the relative order of $a$ and $b$.
