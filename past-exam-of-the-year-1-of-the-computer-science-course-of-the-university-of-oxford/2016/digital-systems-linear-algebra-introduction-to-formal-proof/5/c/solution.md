<h1 id="5/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Use $-A=(-I_n)A$. The [diagonal matrix](../../../../../diagonal-matrix.md) $-I_n$ has $n$ diagonal entries equal to $-1$, so the product and [triangular matrix](../../../../../triangular-matrix.md) formulas from part (a) give

$$
\boxed{\det(-A)=\det(-I_n)\det(A)=(-1)^n\det(A).}
$$

Equivalently, multiplying each of the $n$ rows by $-1$ supplies one factor $-1$ per row.
