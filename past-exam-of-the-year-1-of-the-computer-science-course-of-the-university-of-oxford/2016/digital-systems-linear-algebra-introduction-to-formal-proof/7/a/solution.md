↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [implication introduction rule](../../../../../implication-introduction-rule.md) opens a [subproof](../../../../../subproof.md) with a [temporary assumption](../../../../../temporary-assumption.md) $\phi$, derives $\psi$, and discharges that assumption to conclude **$\phi\to\psi$**. The [negation introduction rule](../../../../../negation-introduction-rule.md) opens a [subproof](../../../../../subproof.md) with a [temporary assumption](../../../../../temporary-assumption.md) $\phi$, derives [logical falsity](../../../../../logical-falsity.md) $\bot$, and discharges it to conclude **$\neg\phi$**.

The boxes record the scopes and dependencies of the [temporary assumptions](../../../../../temporary-assumption.md). A result derived inside a box cannot be used outside it without the appropriate discharge rule; it may depend on the boxed assumption. Other, surrounding assumptions remain in force. This is how the [natural deduction](../../../../../natural-deduction.md) rules distinguish a conditional derivation from an unconditional assertion.
