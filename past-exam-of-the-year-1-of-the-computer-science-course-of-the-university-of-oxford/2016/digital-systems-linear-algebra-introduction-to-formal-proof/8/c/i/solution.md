↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

[Equality introduction rule](../../../../../../equality-introduction-rule.md) is reflexivity; [equality elimination rule](../../../../../../equality-elimination-rule.md) permits substitution of equals in a formula:

$$
\boxed{\frac{}{\Gamma\vdash t=t}\ (=\mathrm I),\qquad\frac{\Gamma\vdash s=t\quad\Gamma\vdash\phi(s)}{\Gamma\vdash\phi(t)}\ (=\mathrm E).}
$$

Terms substituted into $\phi$ must be [free for substitution](../../../../../../free-for-substitution.md), so no variable becomes captured. The notation $\phi(s),\phi(t)$ means substitution into the same designated free variable positions of one formula; other occurrences may remain fixed. These rules express [first-order equality](../../../../../../logical-equality.md) without assuming symmetry or transitivity as additional axioms.
