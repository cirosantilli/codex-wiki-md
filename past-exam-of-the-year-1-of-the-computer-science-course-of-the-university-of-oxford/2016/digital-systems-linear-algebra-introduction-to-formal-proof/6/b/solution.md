↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [vectors](../../../../../vector.md) $v_1,\ldots,v_n$ are [linearly independent](../../../../../linear-independence.md) if

$$
\alpha_1v_1+\cdots+\alpha_nv_n=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\alpha_1=\cdots=\alpha_n=0.
$$

They are [linearly dependent](../../../../../linear-dependence.md) if some such relation has **at least one nonzero coefficient**. All coefficients lie in the underlying [field](../../../../../field.md). Thus [linear independence](../../../../../linear-independence.md) means that only the trivial [linear combination](../../../../../linear-combination.md) gives zero; [linear dependence](../../../../../linear-dependence.md) means that a nontrivial one does.
