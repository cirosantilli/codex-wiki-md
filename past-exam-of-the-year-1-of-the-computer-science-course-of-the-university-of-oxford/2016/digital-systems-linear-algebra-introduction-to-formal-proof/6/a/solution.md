↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

An [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) and [eigenvector](../../../../../eigenvector.md) pair $(\lambda,v)$ for a square [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) $A$ satisfies

$$
\boxed{Av=\lambda v,\qquad v\ne0.}
$$

The [scalar](../../../../../scalar.md) $\lambda$ and the coordinates of $v$ belong to the chosen [field](../../../../../field.md), usually $\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$. The nonzero condition is essential: the zero [vector](../../../../../vector.md) satisfies the equation for every $\lambda$ and therefore cannot define an [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md).
