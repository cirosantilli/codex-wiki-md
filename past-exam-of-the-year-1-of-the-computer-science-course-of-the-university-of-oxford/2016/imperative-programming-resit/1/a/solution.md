<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Because $b>1$, [exponentiation](../../../../../exponentiation.md) to base $b$ is strictly increasing. The definition of the [floor function](../../../../../floor-function.md) gives $i\leq\log_b n<i+1$, so the equivalent characterization of the [integer logarithm](../../../../../integer-logarithm.md) is

$$
\boxed{b^i\leq n<b^{i+1}.}
$$

Here $i$ is a [nonnegative integer](../../../../../natural-number.md); $n=1$ gives $i=0$.
