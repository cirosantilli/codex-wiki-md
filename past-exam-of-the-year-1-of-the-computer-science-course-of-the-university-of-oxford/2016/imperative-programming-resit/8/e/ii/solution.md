**False as a general claim.** An appropriately chosen [software design pattern](../../../../../../software-design-pattern.md) can make expected variation easier: a new strategy or decorator can be supplied through an existing [object interface](../../../../../../object-interface.md). An unnecessary pattern can add indirection and make some changes harder, so applying one is not an automatic guarantee of flexibility either.
