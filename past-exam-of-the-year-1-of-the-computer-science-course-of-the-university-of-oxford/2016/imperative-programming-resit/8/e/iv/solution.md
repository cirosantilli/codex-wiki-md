↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

**True.** [Refactoring](../../../../../../refactoring.md) an existing implementation toward a [software design pattern](../../../../../../software-design-pattern.md) can be preferable once repeated structure and actual variation are visible. Existing behavior supplies a concrete contract to preserve, and tests can check that preservation. Starting from a pattern is appropriate when its problem is already established, but the pattern's name alone is not evidence that its extra structure is needed.
