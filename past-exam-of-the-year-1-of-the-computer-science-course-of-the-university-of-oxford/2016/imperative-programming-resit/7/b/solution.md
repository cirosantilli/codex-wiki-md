**This is the [Decorator pattern](../../../../../decorator-pattern.md), a [structural pattern](../../../../../structural-pattern.md).** `Packages` is a [decorator object](../../../../../decorator-object.md): it implements `CableTV` while holding another `CableTV`, to which its default queries delegate. `Movies` and `HDChannels` extend the price and description of the wrapped account. Because a wrapper itself satisfies `CableTV`, another wrapper can surround it; features combine without declaring a new [software class](../../../../../class-programming.md) for each possible package combination.
