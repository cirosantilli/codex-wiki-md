**[Creational patterns](../../../../../creational-pattern.md) organize object creation; [behavioral patterns](../../../../../behavioral-pattern.md) organize interactions and responsibility for behavior.** A [creational pattern](../../../../../creational-pattern.md) controls how concrete [software objects](../../../../../software-object.md) are selected, instantiated, or shared, so clients need not hard-code the entire construction policy. Examples include the [factory method pattern](../../../../../factory-method-pattern.md) and the [singleton pattern](../../../../../singleton-pattern.md).

A [behavioral pattern](../../../../../behavioral-pattern.md) describes how objects collaborate, receive requests, or distribute an [algorithm](../../../../../algorithm-split.md)'s behavior. For example the [observer pattern](../../../../../observer-pattern.md) lets a subject notify dependants after its state changes, and the [strategy pattern](../../../../../strategy-pattern.md) supplies interchangeable implementations of an operation. This distinction concerns the pattern's principal responsibility; a system can use both kinds together.
