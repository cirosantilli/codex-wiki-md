<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

By [unique prime factorization](../../../../../fundamental-theorem-of-arithmetic.md), a positive [divisor](../../../../../divisor.md) has precisely the form

$$
d=p_1^{e_1}\cdots p_k^{e_k},\qquad 0\leq e_j\leq i_j.
$$

There are $i_j+1$ independent choices for each [prime exponent](../../../../../p-adic-valuation.md); different choices give different [divisors](../../../../../divisor.md), and every [divisor](../../../../../divisor.md) occurs. The [multiplication principle](../../../../../rule-of-product.md) therefore gives **$\boxed{\tau(n)=\prod_{j=1}^{k}(i_j+1)}$**. For $n=1$, the empty [product](../../../../../product-of-numbers.md) is $1$, giving its one positive [divisor](../../../../../divisor.md).
