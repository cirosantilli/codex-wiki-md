↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Dynamic programming](../../../../../dynamic-programming.md) solves overlapping subproblems once and stores their values for reuse. The [principle of optimality](../../../../../principle-of-optimality.md) says that the remaining decisions within an optimal solution must solve the corresponding residual subproblem optimally; otherwise replacing them by a better residual solution would improve the original one. Here removing one chosen stamp leaves an optimal way to form the remaining postage.
