<h1 id="7/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [greedy algorithm](../../../../../greedy-algorithm.md) repeatedly takes the largest denomination no greater than the remaining postage. For denominations **$1,3,4$ and target $6$**, it returns $4+1+1$, using three stamps. The optimum is $3+3$, using two. Thus local preference for the largest stamp does not generally solve the [coin change problem](../../../../../coin-change-problem.md) optimally.
