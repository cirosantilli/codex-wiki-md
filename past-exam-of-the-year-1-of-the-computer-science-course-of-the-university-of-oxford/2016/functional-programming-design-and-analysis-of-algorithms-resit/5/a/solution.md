A [connected component](../../../../../connected-component.md) of an [undirected graph](../../../../../undirected-graph.md) is a maximal set of [vertices](../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md) such that each pair is joined by a [path](../../../../../continuous-path.md). Equivalently, components are the [equivalence classes](../../../../../equivalence-class.md) of the relation “reachable by a path”. The length-zero path ensures that an isolated [vertex](../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md) is a one-vertex component.
