↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

A [disjoint-set data structure](../../../../../disjoint-set-data-structure.md) maintains a [partition of a set](../../../../../partition-of-a-set.md) into pairwise disjoint sets. `Make-Set(v)` creates the singleton containing a new vertex. `Find-Set(v)` returns the [disjoint-set representative](../../../../../disjoint-set-representative.md) of its current set. `Union(u,v)` merges the two sets containing its arguments, preserving the partition; it has no effect if they are already the same set. A [disjoint-set forest](../../../../../disjoint-set-forest.md) can implement these operations without explicitly moving every member when sets are joined.
