↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Test **`Find-Set(u) == Find-Set(v)`**. Initially the sets are singleton [connected components](../../../../../connected-component.md) of the edgeless graph; processing an edge joins precisely the components it connects. By [induction](../../../../../mathematical-induction.md) over the processed edges, the final sets are exactly the [connected components](../../../../../connected-component.md) of $G$.
