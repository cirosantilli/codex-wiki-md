Use
```
type Point = (Double,Double)
type Polygon = [Point]
```
A valid [polygon](../../../../../polygon.md) is a finite list of at least three [vertices](../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md) in boundary order, with the final vertex connected back to the first. The first vertex is not repeated at the end. A simple [polygon](../../../../../polygon.md) also has no intersecting nonadjacent [edges](../../../../../edge-of-a-graph.md). Numerical routines below use finite coordinates; `NaN` and infinite coordinates are outside this representation's intended domain.
