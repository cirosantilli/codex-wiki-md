A [type synonym](../../../../../type-synonym.md) is a name for an existing type expression, introduced with `type`, for example `type Name = String`. It improves documentation and makes type signatures easier to maintain. It creates **no new distinct type**: a synonym and its expansion remain interchangeable. An [algebraic data type](../../../../../algebraic-data-type.md) declared with `data` or `newtype` has a different role.
