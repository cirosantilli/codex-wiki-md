↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [spanning tree](../../../../../spanning-tree.md) is a connected acyclic subgraph containing every [vertex](../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md). A [minimum spanning tree](../../../../../minimum-spanning-tree.md) minimizes the sum of edge weights over all spanning trees. In a three-vertex triangle with every edge weight 1, deleting any of the three edges gives a different spanning tree of total weight 2. Hence there are three minimum spanning trees; minimum total weight need not mean a unique tree.
