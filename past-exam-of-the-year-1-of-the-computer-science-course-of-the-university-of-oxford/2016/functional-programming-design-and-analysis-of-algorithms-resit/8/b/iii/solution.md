**False.** Take a triangle with $w(ab)=w(bc)=2$ and $w(ac)=3$. Its unique [minimum spanning tree](../../../../../../minimum-spanning-tree.md) uses $ab$ and $bc$, with total weight 4. The [shortest path](../../../../../../shortest-path.md) from $a$ to $c$ in the original graph is the direct edge of weight 3; the tree's only path has weight 4. A [minimum spanning tree](../../../../../../minimum-spanning-tree.md) optimizes total tree weight, not every pairwise path length.
