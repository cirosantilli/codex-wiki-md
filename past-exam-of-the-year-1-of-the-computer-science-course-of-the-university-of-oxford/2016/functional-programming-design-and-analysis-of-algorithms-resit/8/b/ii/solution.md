↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**True.** Every finite [tree](../../../../../../tree-graph-theory-split.md) on $|V|$ vertices has exactly $|V|-1$ edges. One proof removes a leaf and its incident edge, preserving a tree on one fewer vertex, and applies [induction](../../../../../../mathematical-induction.md) from the one-vertex tree. Therefore all [minimum spanning trees](../../../../../../minimum-spanning-tree.md) have the same edge count.
