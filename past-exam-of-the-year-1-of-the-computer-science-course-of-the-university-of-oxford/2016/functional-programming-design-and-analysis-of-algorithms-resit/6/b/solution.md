↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

**Yes**, if a merge takes from its left half when two [sort keys](../../../../../sort-key.md) compare equal. Within each half the [induction hypothesis](../../../../../induction-hypothesis.md) preserves equal-key order. Across the two halves every left-half occurrence originally preceded every right-half occurrence, and the tie rule preserves that order. Reversing the tie rule would lose [stable sorting](../../../../../stable-sorting.md).
