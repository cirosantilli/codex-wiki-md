[Device independence](../../../../../device-independence.md) lets an application request [input-output](../../../../../input-output.md) through a uniform interface without depending on a particular device's control registers, instruction protocol or geometry. A [device driver](../../../../../device-driver.md) translates the abstract requests into device-specific operations. Replacing one supported disk model with another can then preserve the application's file-reading interface.
