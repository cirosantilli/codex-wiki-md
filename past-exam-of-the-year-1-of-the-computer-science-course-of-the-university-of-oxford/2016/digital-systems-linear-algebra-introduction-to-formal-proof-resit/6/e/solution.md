<h1 id="6/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

A particular solution is $x_p=(1,0,-1,0)^T$, as direct substitution into all five equations verifies. Add any vector in the [null space](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) to obtain every solution:

$$
\boxed{x=\begin{pmatrix}1\\0\\-1\\0\end{pmatrix}+t\begin{pmatrix}-1\\1\\-1\\1\end{pmatrix},\qquad t\in\mathbb R.}
$$

Conversely, the difference of any two solutions is annihilated by $A$, so there are no further solutions. This is the [affine solution space](../../../../../affine-solution-space.md) of a consistent [linear system](../../../../../system-of-linear-equations.md).
