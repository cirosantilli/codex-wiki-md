↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For an $m\times n$ matrix, its [row space](../../../../../row-space.md) is the span of its rows, a subspace of $\mathbb R^n$; its [column space](../../../../../column-space.md) is the span of its columns, a subspace of $\mathbb R^m$. Its [null space](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) is $\ker A=\{x\in\mathbb R^n:Ax=0\}$. The [rank of a matrix](../../../../../matrix-rank.md) is the dimension of its column space, equal to the dimension of its row space. The [rank-nullity theorem](../../../../../rank-nullity-theorem.md) gives $\dim\ker A=n-\operatorname{rank}A$.
