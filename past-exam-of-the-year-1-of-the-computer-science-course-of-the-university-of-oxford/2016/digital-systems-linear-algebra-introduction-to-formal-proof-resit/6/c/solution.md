<h1 id="6/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The pivot columns are columns 1, 2 and 3 of the original matrix. A [basis](../../../../../basis.md) for its [column space](../../../../../column-space.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{\left\{\begin{pmatrix}2\\1\\1\\0\\1\end{pmatrix},\begin{pmatrix}2\\1\\2\\0\\0\end{pmatrix},\begin{pmatrix}1\\0\\1\\1\\0\end{pmatrix}\right\}.}
$$

Use the original columns, rather than the columns of the row-reduced matrix: [elementary row operations](../../../../../elementary-row-operation.md) preserve column dependence relations, but can change the column space as a subspace of $\mathbb R^5$.
