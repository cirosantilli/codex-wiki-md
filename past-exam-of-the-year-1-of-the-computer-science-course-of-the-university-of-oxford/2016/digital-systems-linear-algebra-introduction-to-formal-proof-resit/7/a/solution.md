[Implication introduction](../../../../../implication-introduction-rule.md) assumes $\phi$ temporarily, derives $\psi$, and discharges that assumption to conclude $\phi\to\psi$. [Disjunction elimination](../../../../../disjunction-elimination-rule.md) starts from $\phi\lor\psi$ and derives the same conclusion $\kappa$ in two separate branches, one assuming $\phi$ and the other assuming $\psi$.

The boxes delimit [subproofs](../../../../../subproof.md): a locally assumed formula is available inside its box but cannot be used unconditionally outside it. Discharging a box records exactly which dependency has been removed. The proofs below use a vertical bar to mark each active box.
