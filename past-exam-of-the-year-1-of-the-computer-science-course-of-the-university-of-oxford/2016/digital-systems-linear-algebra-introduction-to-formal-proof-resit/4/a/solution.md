<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

As written, the assertion needs a **nonzero-vector assumption**: the singleton set containing the [zero vector](../../../../../zero-vector.md) is mutually orthogonal but is [linearly dependent](../../../../../linear-dependence.md). For the [linear independence of nonzero orthogonal vectors](../../../../../linear-independence-of-nonzero-orthogonal-vectors.md), suppose $\sum_{i=1}^k\alpha_iq_i=0$. Taking the [inner product](../../../../../inner-product-split.md) with $q_j$ gives

$$
0=\sum_i\alpha_i\langle q_i,q_j\rangle=\alpha_j\lVert q_j\rVert^2.
$$

Since $q_j\ne0$, positive definiteness gives $\lVert q_j\rVert^2>0$, hence $\alpha_j=0$. This holds for every $j$, proving [linear independence](../../../../../linear-independence.md) under the necessary assumption.
