<h1 id="5/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) of the [unit lower triangular matrix](../../../../../unit-lower-triangular-matrix.md) $L$ is 1. There is one row swap, so $\det P=-1$, and the [upper triangular matrix](../../../../../upper-triangular-matrix.md) has determinant $1\cdot(-2)\cdot(3/2)\cdot2=-6$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\det A=\det P\det L\det U=6.}
$$
