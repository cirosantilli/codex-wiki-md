<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A value-bearing [perfect binary tree](../../../../../perfect-binary-tree.md) has size $w=2^{h+1}-1$, and the next size is $2w+1$. Suppose two size-$w$ trees have at least one smaller tree before them. The portion represented by these trees and that nonempty preceding portion has at least $2w+1$ elements. The greedy choice would have used a larger [perfect binary tree](../../../../../perfect-binary-tree.md) instead, contradicting the specified representation. Three size-$w$ trees are likewise impossible, since $3w\geq2w+1$. Therefore **the only possible repeated size is a pair at the beginning**. This is the representation invariant of a [skew-binary random-access list](../../../../../skew-binary-random-access-list.md).
