<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The zero [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) has the required pattern, and [matrix addition](../../../../../matrix-addition.md) and [scalar multiplication](../../../../../scalar-multiplication.md) preserve every prescribed zero entry. This proves the [vector subspace](../../../../../vector-subspace.md) condition. A [basis](../../../../../basis.md) consists of the [matrix units](../../../../../matrix-unit.md)

$$
\{E_{ii}:1\le i\le n\}\ \cup\ \{E_{i,i+1},E_{i+1,i}:1\le i<n\}.
$$

They independently select every permitted entry of a [tridiagonal matrix](../../../../../tridiagonal-matrix.md), so **the [dimension](../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) is $n+2(n-1)=3n-2$** for $n\ge1$.
