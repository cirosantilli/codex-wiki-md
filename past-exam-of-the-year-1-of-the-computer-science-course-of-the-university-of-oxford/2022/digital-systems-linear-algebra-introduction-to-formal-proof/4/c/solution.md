<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

With the two [diagonal matrices](../../../../../diagonal-matrix.md) from the preceding part, write $D_A=PAP^{-1}$ and $D_B=PBP^{-1}$. Their diagonal products agree entry by entry, so $D_AD_B=D_BD_A$. Therefore

$$
AB=P^{-1}D_AD_BP=P^{-1}D_BD_AP=BA.
$$

Hence [simultaneous diagonalization](../../../../../simultaneous-diagonalization.md) implies that $A,B$ are **[commuting operators](../../../../../commuting-operators.md)**.
