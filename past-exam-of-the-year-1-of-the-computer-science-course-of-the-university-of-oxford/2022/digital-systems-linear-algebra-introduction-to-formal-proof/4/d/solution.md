<h1 id="4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Using $BA=AB$ and [linearity](../../../../../linearity.md),

$$
\boxed{B(Av)=A(Bv)=A(\lambda v)=\lambda Av.}
$$

This establishes that [commuting maps preserve eigenspaces](../../../../../commuting-maps-preserve-eigenspaces.md). The [vector](../../../../../vector.md) $Av$ can be zero, so this step alone does not assert that $Av$ is an [eigenvector](../../../../../eigenvector.md).
