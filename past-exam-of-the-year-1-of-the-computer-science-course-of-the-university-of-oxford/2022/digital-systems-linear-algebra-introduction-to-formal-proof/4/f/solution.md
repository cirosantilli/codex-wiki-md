<h1 id="4/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

The $n$ independent [eigenvectors](../../../../../eigenvector.md) $v_1,\ldots,v_n$ of $B$ from part (e) are also [eigenvectors](../../../../../eigenvector.md) of $A$. Put $S=(v_1\ \cdots\ v_n)$ and $P=S^{-1}$. Then

$$
\boxed{PAP^{-1}=\operatorname{diag}(\mu_1,\ldots,\mu_n),\qquad PBP^{-1}=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_n).}
$$

This supplies the required [simultaneous diagonalization](../../../../../simultaneous-diagonalization.md) over the same [field](../../../../../field.md) as in part (e).
