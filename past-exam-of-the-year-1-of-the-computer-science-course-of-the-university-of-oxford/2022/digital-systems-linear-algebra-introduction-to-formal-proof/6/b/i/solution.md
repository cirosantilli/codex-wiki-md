<h1 id="6/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [intersection of vector subspaces](../../../../../../intersection-of-vector-subspaces.md) $W_1\cap W_2$ contains zero, so $(0,0)\in U$. If $(u,-u),(v,-v)\in U$ and $a,b$ are [scalars](../../../../../../scalar.md), then

$$
a(u,-u)+b(v,-v)=(au+bv,-(au+bv))\in U,
$$

because $au+bv\in W_1\cap W_2$ by the [vector subspace](../../../../../../vector-subspace.md) conditions. These pairs belong to $W_1\times W_2$, since both $u$ and $-u$ belong to the required factors. Thus **$U$ is a [vector subspace](../../../../../../vector-subspace.md) of $W_1\times W_2$**.
