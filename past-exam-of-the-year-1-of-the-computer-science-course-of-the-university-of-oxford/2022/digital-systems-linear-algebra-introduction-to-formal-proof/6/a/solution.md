<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Writing the given [bases](../../../../../basis.md) as $(u_1,\ldots,u_p)$ and $(v_1,\ldots,v_q)$, a [basis](../../../../../basis.md) of the [Cartesian product](../../../../../cartesian-product.md) with its componentwise [vector space](../../../../../vector-space-split.md) operations is

$$
\boxed{\bigl((u_1,0),\ldots,(u_p,0),(0,v_1),\ldots,(0,v_q)\bigr).}
$$

This is the [direct sum basis](../../../../../direct-sum-basis.md). Therefore **$\dim(W_1\times W_2)=p+q=\dim W_1+\dim W_2$**. The construction includes zero-dimensional factors, whose corresponding portion of the [basis](../../../../../basis.md) is empty.
