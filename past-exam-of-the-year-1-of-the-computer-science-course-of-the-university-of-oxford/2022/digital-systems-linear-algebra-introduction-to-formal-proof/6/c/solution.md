<h1 id="6/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Every output $u_1+u_2$ lies in the [sum of subspaces](../../../../../sum-of-vector-subspaces.md) $W_1+W_2$, since it is a [linear combination](../../../../../linear-combination.md) of members of the two [vector subspaces](../../../../../vector-subspace.md). For $u=(u_1,u_2)$, $v=(v_1,v_2)$ and [scalars](../../../../../scalar.md) $a,b$,

$$
g(au+bv)=(au_1+bv_1)+(au_2+bv_2)=a(u_1+u_2)+b(v_1+v_2)=ag(u)+bg(v).
$$

Therefore **$g$ is a [linear map](../../../../../linear-map.md)**. Every [vector](../../../../../vector.md) in $W_1+W_2$ is such an output: group the terms of any [linear combination](../../../../../linear-combination.md) from $W_1\cup W_2$ according to their originating [vector subspace](../../../../../vector-subspace.md). Thus the [addition map of subspaces](../../../../../addition-map-of-subspaces.md) is also [surjective](../../../../../surjective-function.md).
