<h1 id="8/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Express that the order is a [dense order](../../../../../dense-order.md):

$$
\boxed{\psi=\forall x\,\forall y\,\bigl(x<y\to\exists z\,(x<z\land z<y)\bigr).}
$$

In either real [interval](../../../../../interval-mathematics.md), $(x+y)/2$ lies strictly between any two distinct ordered elements and remains in the domain. In the [integers](../../../../../integer-split.md), $x=0,y=1$ have no intervening [integer](../../../../../integer-split.md), so this [first-order sentence](../../../../../first-order-sentence.md) is false. The endpoints of a closed [interval](../../../../../interval-mathematics.md) do not prevent a [dense order](../../../../../dense-order.md).
