<h1 id="8/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Express existence of a [minimum](../../../../../minimum-of-a-subset-of-a-total-order.md) using only the given strict-order relation:

$$
\boxed{\varphi=\exists m\,\forall x\,\neg(x<m).}
$$

In the closed [interval](../../../../../interval-mathematics.md) $[0,1]$, the witness is $m=0$. In the open [interval](../../../../../interval-mathematics.md) $(0,1)$, every possible witness $m$ has the smaller member $m/2$, so the [first-order sentence](../../../../../first-order-sentence.md) is false. These witnesses are explanations in the ambient [real numbers](../../../../../real-number.md), not additional symbols in the [first-order signature](../../../../../first-order-signature.md).
