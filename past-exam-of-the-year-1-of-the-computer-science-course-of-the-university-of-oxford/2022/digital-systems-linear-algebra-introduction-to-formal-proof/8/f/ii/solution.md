<h1 id="8/f/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**The term $x+z$ is [free for substitution](../../../../../../free-for-substitution.md) for $z$.** The only free occurrence of the target $z$ is in the outside disjunct $y<z$, beyond both [quantifier](../../../../../../quantifier.md) scopes. The occurrences under $\forall z$ are [bound variables](../../../../../../bound-variable.md) and are not replaced. Therefore no renaming is required, and

$$
\boxed{\varphi[x+z/z]=\bigl(\exists x\,(x<y\to\forall z\,(x<z+2))\bigr)\lor\bigl(y<x+z\bigr).}
$$

The new $x$ and $z$ in the last disjunct are free. [First-order substitution](../../../../../../first-order-substitution.md) replaces the original free occurrences once; it does not recursively substitute into the inserted term.
