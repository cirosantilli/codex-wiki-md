<h1 id="7/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The given [logical implication](../../../../../logical-implication.md) is false exactly when $A=B$ and $C\ne D$. For every [literal](../../../../../boolean-literal.md) on $A,B,C,D$, there is such a false [Boolean valuation](../../../../../boolean-valuation.md) making that [literal](../../../../../boolean-literal.md) true. For a prescribed value of $A$ or $B$, give the other the same value and choose $C\ne D$. For a prescribed value of $C$ or $D$, give the other the opposite value and choose $A=B$.

Consequently no [literal](../../../../../boolean-literal.md) implies the whole [propositional formula](../../../../../propositional-formula.md): **there is no one-[literal](../../../../../boolean-literal.md) [implicant](../../../../../implicant.md)**. If an equivalent [disjunctive normal form](../../../../../disjunctive-normal-form.md) contained a one-[literal](../../../../../boolean-literal.md) conjunction term $\ell$, then every [Boolean valuation](../../../../../boolean-valuation.md) satisfying $\ell$ would satisfy that [disjunctive normal form](../../../../../disjunctive-normal-form.md), contradicting its equivalence to the given [propositional formula](../../../../../propositional-formula.md). This proves the claim. Even if auxiliary [Boolean variables](../../../../../boolean-variable.md) are allowed in the equivalent expression, a one-[literal](../../../../../boolean-literal.md) term on one of them fails too: fix any false [Boolean valuation](../../../../../boolean-valuation.md) of the original four and set the auxiliary [literal](../../../../../boolean-literal.md) true. The word “conjunct” here refers to a conjunction term in the [disjunctive normal form](../../../../../disjunctive-normal-form.md), not a factor in a [conjunctive normal form](../../../../../conjunctive-normal-form.md).
