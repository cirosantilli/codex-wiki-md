↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Eliminating [logical implications](../../../../../logical-implication.md) gives

$$
(A\to C)\to(B\to C)\equiv\neg(\neg A\lor C)\lor\neg B\lor C\equiv(A\land\neg C)\lor\neg B\lor C.
$$

An absorption step simplifies this to the [disjunctive normal form](../../../../../disjunctive-normal-form.md)

$$
\boxed{A\lor\neg B\lor C.}
$$

Each one-[literal](../../../../../boolean-literal.md) term is a permitted [logical conjunction](../../../../../logical-conjunction.md) in a [disjunctive normal form](../../../../../disjunctive-normal-form.md).
