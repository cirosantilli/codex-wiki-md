<h1 id="4/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**A single header with `index == n`, and with both `next` and `prev` pointing to itself, represents the entirely zero vector.** It has no data nodes. The ignored header value can be `0.0`. The vector dimension remains $n$, even though the number of stored nonzero entries is zero.
