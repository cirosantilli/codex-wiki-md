<h1 id="8/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

**With the completed default [trait](../../../../../trait.md) from part (a), the expression is well-typed and creates the empty [complete multiset](../../../../../signed-multiset.md)**, provided the type `T` is in scope. Every member has a concrete default, `entries` is empty, and every count is zero.

**With only the originally printed abstract [trait](../../../../../trait.md), it is not a legal instantiation**, because abstract queries and operations remain unimplemented. The distinction is whether part (a)'s supplied defaults are in force, rather than any special treatment of anonymous objects.
