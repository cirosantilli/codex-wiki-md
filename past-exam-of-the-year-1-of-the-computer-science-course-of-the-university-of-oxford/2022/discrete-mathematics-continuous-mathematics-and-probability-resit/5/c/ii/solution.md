<h1 id="5/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

At $x=0$, the [gradient](../../../../../../gradient.md) from the preceding part vanishes and the [Hessian matrix](../../../../../../hessian-matrix.md) is zero. The second-order test is therefore inconclusive. Instead inspect the [eigenline](../../../../../../eigenline.md):

$$
h(tb)=(t^2b^TAb)(t\|b\|^2)=\tfrac12t^3.
$$

For arbitrarily small positive and negative $t$, these values have opposite signs. Thus **$0$ is neither a [local maximum](../../../../../../local-maximum.md) nor a [local minimum](../../../../../../local-minimum.md)**. In dimension at least two it is a degenerate [saddle point](../../../../../../saddle-point.md); in one dimension it is a stationary [inflection point](../../../../../../inflection-point.md). Looking only at the zero [Hessian matrix](../../../../../../hessian-matrix.md) would not determine this classification.
