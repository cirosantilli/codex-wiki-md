<h1 id="5/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Write $(x^Tb)^2=x^Tbb^Tx$ and put $C=A-bb^T$, which is symmetric. Differentiating this [quadratic form](../../../../../../quadratic-form-split.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\nabla g(x)=2Cx=2Ax-2(x^Tb)b,\qquad H_g(x)=2(A-bb^T).}
$$

Unlike $A$, $C$ need not be a [positive-definite matrix](../../../../../../positive-definite-matrix.md). Its behaviour must be checked before classifying the [stationary point](../../../../../../stationary-point.md).
