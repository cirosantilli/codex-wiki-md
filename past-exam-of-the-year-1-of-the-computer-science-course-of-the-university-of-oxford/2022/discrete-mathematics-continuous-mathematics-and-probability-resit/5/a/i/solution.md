<h1 id="5/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use compatible dimensions $A\in\mathbb R^{d\times d}$ and $b,x\in\mathbb R^d$; the printed “b-by-b” size is read consistently with the displayed products and domain. Take [derivatives](../../../../../../derivative.md) as column [gradients](../../../../../../gradient.md). [Symmetry](../../../../../../symmetry-physics.md) of $A$ gives

$$
\boxed{\nabla f(x)=2Ax+b,\qquad H_f(x)=2A.}
$$

For example, the directional [differential](../../../../../../differential-of-a-smooth-map.md) of $x^TAx$ is $dx^TAx+x^TAdx=2(Ax)^Tdx$. The linear term contributes $b$ to the [gradient](../../../../../../gradient.md) and zero to the [Hessian matrix](../../../../../../hessian-matrix.md).
