<h1 id="5/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [eigenvector](../../../../../../eigenvector.md) identity $Ab=\tfrac12b$ gives $\nabla f(-b)=-2Ab+b=0$. Since $H_f=2A$ is a [positive-definite matrix](../../../../../../positive-definite-matrix.md), this is a strict [local minimum](../../../../../../local-minimum.md). A global certificate follows by completing the [quadratic form](../../../../../../quadratic-form-split.md):

$$
f(x)=(x+b)^TA(x+b)-b^TAb=(x+b)^TA(x+b)-\tfrac12.
$$

[Positive definiteness](../../../../../../positive-definiteness.md) makes the first term nonnegative, with equality only at $x=-b$. Thus **$-b$ is the unique [global minimum](../../../../../../global-minimum.md)**, with value $-1/2$.
