<h1 id="4/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Both terms $x^6$ and $\tfrac32y^4$ are nonnegative, and they vanish simultaneously only at $(0,0)$. That point satisfies $x^4+2y^2\leq3$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{(x_*,y_*)=(0,0),\qquad F_*=0,}
$$

and the [global minimum](../../../../../global-minimum.md) is unique. No [Lagrange multiplier](../../../../../lagrange-multiplier.md) calculation is needed: nonnegativity gives a [lower bound](../../../../../lower-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md) attained at a [feasible point](../../../../../feasible-point.md).
