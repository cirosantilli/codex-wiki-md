↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

In the [probability density function](../../../../../../probability-density-function.md) convention used here, a [random variable](../../../../../../random-variable-split.md) $X$ has a nonnegative [probability density function](../../../../../../probability-density-function.md) $f$, with $\int_{\mathbb R}f(x)\,\mathrm dx=1$, such that for every [Borel set](../../../../../../borel-set.md) $A$,

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(X\in A)=\int_A f(x)\,\mathrm dx.}
$$

In particular, its [cumulative distribution function](../../../../../../cumulative-distribution-function.md) is $F(x)=\int_{-\infty}^x f(t)\,\mathrm dt$ and individual points have [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) zero. More precisely, this property is the statement that the distribution is an [absolutely continuous probability distribution](../../../../../../absolutely-continuous-probability-distribution.md); [continuity](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) of the [cumulative distribution function](../../../../../../cumulative-distribution-function.md) alone is a weaker property and need not imply a [probability density function](../../../../../../probability-density-function.md).
