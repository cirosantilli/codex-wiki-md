↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For a [probability density function](../../../../../../probability-density-function.md) $f$, the finite [expected value](../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[X]=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}x f(x)\,\mathrm dx,\qquad\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}|x|f(x)\,\mathrm dx<\infty.}
$$

The [absolute integrability](../../../../../../integrability.md) condition makes the signed integral unambiguous. One can also allow an extended [expectation](../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) when exactly one of the positive and negative parts has infinite integral, obtaining $+\infty$ or $-\infty$. If both parts have infinite integral, the [expectation](../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) is undefined; subtracting two infinities is not a definition.
