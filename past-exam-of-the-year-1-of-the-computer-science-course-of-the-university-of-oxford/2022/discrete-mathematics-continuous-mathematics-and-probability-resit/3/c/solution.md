↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [pigeonhole principle](../../../../../pigeonhole-principle.md) states that if more than $r$ objects are placed in $r$ boxes, with $r\geq1$, at least one box contains two or more objects. Equivalently, a [function](../../../../../function-split.md) from a finite [set](../../../../../set-split.md) of more than $r$ elements to a [set](../../../../../set-split.md) of $r$ elements cannot be injective: if every box held at most one object, there would be at most $r$ objects altogether.
