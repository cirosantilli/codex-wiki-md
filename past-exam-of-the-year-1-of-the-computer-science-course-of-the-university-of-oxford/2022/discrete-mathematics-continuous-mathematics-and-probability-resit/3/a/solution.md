<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [integer congruence](../../../../../integer-congruence.md) $x\equiv y\pmod n$ means $n\mid(x-y)$: the difference is an [integer](../../../../../integer-split.md) multiple of the positive modulus $n$. Write $x_1-x_2=na$ and $y_1-y_2=nb$ for [integers](../../../../../integer-split.md) $a,b$. Then

$$
(x_1+y_1)-(x_2+y_2)=n(a+b),
$$

so [addition](../../../../../addition.md) preserves [integer congruence](../../../../../integer-congruence.md). For [multiplication](../../../../../multiplication.md),

$$
x_1y_1-x_2y_2=y_1(x_1-x_2)+x_2(y_1-y_2)=n(ay_1+bx_2).
$$

The final coefficient is an [integer](../../../../../integer-split.md), so [multiplication](../../../../../multiplication.md) also preserves [integer congruence](../../../../../integer-congruence.md). These properties make [addition](../../../../../addition.md) and [multiplication](../../../../../multiplication.md) well defined on [residue classes](../../../../../residue-class.md).
