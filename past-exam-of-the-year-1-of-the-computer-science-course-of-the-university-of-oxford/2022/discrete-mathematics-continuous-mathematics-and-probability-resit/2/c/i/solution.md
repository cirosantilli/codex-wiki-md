↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**True.** For every [integer](../../../../../../integer-split.md) $n\geq1$, $n^2\leq n^3$ and $1\leq n^3$. Therefore

$$
0\leq n^3+1000n^2+96\leq1097n^3.
$$

The constants $C=1097$ and $N=1$ satisfy the definition of [Big O notation](../../../../../../big-o-notation.md). Whether the chosen convention for natural numbers includes zero is immaterial to this eventual assertion.
