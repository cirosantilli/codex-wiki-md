↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**True.** Rationalization gives, for $n\geq1$,

$$
\sqrt{4n^2+n}-2n
=\frac n{\sqrt{4n^2+n}+2n}
=\frac1{\sqrt{4+1/n}+2}.
$$

This lies between zero and $1/4$, so it is $O(1)$ in [Big O notation](../../../../../../big-o-notation.md). In fact it converges to $1/4$, rather than to zero. The rationalized form also avoids the [catastrophic cancellation](../../../../../../catastrophic-cancellation.md) of subtracting two nearby large quantities.
