<h1 id="2/c/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

**False.** On the unbounded [subsequence](../../../../../../subsequence.md) of odd positive [integers](../../../../../../integer-split.md), $f(n)/g(n)=n$. For any proposed constants $C>0,N$, choose an odd $n>\max(C,N)$. Then $|f(n)|=n>C|g(n)|=C$, contradicting the eventual [upper bound](../../../../../../upper-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md) required by [Big O notation](../../../../../../big-o-notation.md). The small ratio on even [integers](../../../../../../integer-split.md) cannot compensate for this failure on infinitely many odd [integers](../../../../../../integer-split.md).
