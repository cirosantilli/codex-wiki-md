<h1 id="probability/9/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The proposed [cumulative distribution function](../../../../../../../cumulative-distribution-function.md) satisfies $0<F(x)<1$ and is smooth. Its [derivative](../../../../../../../derivative.md) is

$$
F'(x)=e^{-x}\exp(-e^{-x})>0,
$$

so it is increasing and continuous, hence right-continuous. As $x\to-\infty$, $e^{-x}\to\infty$ and $F(x)\to0$; as $x\to\infty$, $e^{-x}\to0$ and $F(x)\to1$. Thus **it has all the required [cumulative distribution function](../../../../../../../cumulative-distribution-function.md) properties**, and defines the standard [Gumbel distribution](../../../../../../../gumbel-distribution.md).
