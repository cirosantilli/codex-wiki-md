<h1 id="probability/9/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Every [cumulative distribution function](../../../../../../../cumulative-distribution-function.md) is **nondecreasing, right-continuous, valued in $[0,1]$, and has limits $0$ at $-\infty$ and $1$ at $+\infty$**. For a continuous [random variable](../../../../../../../random-variable-split.md) it is continuous. When a [probability density function](../../../../../../../probability-density-function.md) exists, it is in fact absolutely continuous, with nonnegative [derivative](../../../../../../../derivative.md) almost everywhere.
