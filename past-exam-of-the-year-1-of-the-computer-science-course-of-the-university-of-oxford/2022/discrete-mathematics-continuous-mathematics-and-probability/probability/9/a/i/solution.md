<h1 id="probability/9/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [cumulative distribution function](../../../../../../../cumulative-distribution-function.md) is **$F_X(x)=\mathbb P(X\le x)$**. In the absolutely continuous setting of a [probability density function](../../../../../../../probability-density-function.md), the [probability density function](../../../../../../../probability-density-function.md) is a nonnegative integrable function $f_X$ satisfying

$$
\boxed{F_X(x)=\int_{-\infty}^x f_X(t)\,dt,\qquad\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f_X(t)\,dt=1.}
$$

Thus $\mathbb P(a<X\le b)=\int_a^b f_X(t)\,dt$ and $F_X'=f_X$ almost everywhere. A merely continuous [cumulative distribution function](../../../../../../../cumulative-distribution-function.md) need not possess a [probability density function](../../../../../../../probability-density-function.md); the density assumption means absolute continuity.
