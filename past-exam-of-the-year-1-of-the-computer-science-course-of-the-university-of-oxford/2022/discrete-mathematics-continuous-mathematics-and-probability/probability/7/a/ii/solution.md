↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For finite second moments, the [variance of a sum](../../../../../../../variance-of-a-sum.md) is

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{var}\!\left(\sum_{i=1}^nX_i\right)=\sum_{i=1}^n\operatorname{var}(X_i)+2\sum_{1\le i<j\le n}\operatorname{cov}(X_i,X_j)=\sum_{i,j=1}^n\operatorname{cov}(X_i,X_j).}
$$
