<h1 id="probability/7/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Assume finite second moments and set $Y_i=X_i-\mathbb E X_i$. By [linearity of expectation](../../../../../../../linearity-of-expectation.md), centring $X_1+X_2$ gives $Y_1+Y_2$. Expanding its square and using the definitions of [variance](../../../../../../../variance-split.md) and [covariance](../../../../../../../covariance.md),

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{var}(X_1+X_2)=\mathbb E(Y_1+Y_2)^2=\operatorname{var}(X_1)+\operatorname{var}(X_2)+2\operatorname{cov}(X_1,X_2).}
$$

No independence assumption is required for this [variance of a sum](../../../../../../../variance-of-a-sum.md) identity.
