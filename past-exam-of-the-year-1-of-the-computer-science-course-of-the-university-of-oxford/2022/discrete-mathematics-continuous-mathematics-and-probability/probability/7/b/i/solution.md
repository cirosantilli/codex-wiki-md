↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

In the [uniform balls-in-bins allocation](../../../../../../../uniform-balls-in-bins-allocation.md), each ball enters box 1 independently with [probability](../../../../probability.md) $1/n$. Thus **$M_1$ has the [binomial distribution](../../../../../../../binomial-distribution.md) $\operatorname{Bin}(n,1/n)$**, with [probability mass function](../../../../../../../probability-mass-function.md)

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(M_1=k)=\binom nk\left(\frac1n\right)^k\left(1-\frac1n\right)^{n-k},\quad k=0,\ldots,n,}
$$

and zero outside that range.
