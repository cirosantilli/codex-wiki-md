<h1 id="probability/7/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Write $p_n=(1-1/n)^n$, the [probability](../../../../probability.md) that all balls avoid a specified box. Each of the [empty-box indicators in uniform allocation](../../../../../../../empty-box-indicators-in-uniform-allocation.md) is an [indicator random variable](../../../../../../../indicator-random-variable.md) with success [probability](../../../../probability.md) $p_n$. Since $I_i^2=I_i$, its [expectation](../../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) and [variance](../../../../../../../variance-split.md) are

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E I_i=p_n,\qquad\operatorname{var}(I_i)=p_n(1-p_n).}
$$
