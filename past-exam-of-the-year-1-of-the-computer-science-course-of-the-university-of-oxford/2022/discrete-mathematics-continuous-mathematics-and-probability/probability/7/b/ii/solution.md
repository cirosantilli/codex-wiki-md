<h1 id="probability/7/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For both boxes to be empty, each ball must avoid two specified boxes. The individual avoidance [probability](../../../../probability.md) is $(n-2)/n$, and the choices of different balls are independent. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(M_1=0,M_2=0)=\left(1-\frac2n\right)^n.}
$$

This also covers $n=2$, when the answer is zero.
