↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

This is the [geometric distribution](../../../../../../../geometric-distribution.md) on $\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$ with success parameter $1/2$. Its [probability generating function](../../../../../../../probability-generating-function.md) is a [geometric series](../../../../../../../geometric-series.md):

$$
\boxed{G_N(s)=\frac12\sum_{k=0}^\infty\left(\frac s2\right)^k=\frac1{2-s},\qquad |s|<2.}
$$
