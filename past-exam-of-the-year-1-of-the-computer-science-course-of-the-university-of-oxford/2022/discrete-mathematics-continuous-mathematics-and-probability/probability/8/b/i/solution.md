<h1 id="probability/8/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The ancestor contributes one individual. Given its offspring count $N$, the descendant family rooted at each child is an independent [branching process](../../../../../../../branching-process.md) with the same [offspring distribution](../../../../../../../offspring-distribution-of-a-branching-process.md) as the original one. Its [total progeny](../../../../../../../total-progeny-of-a-branching-process.md) therefore has the same distribution as $Z$. The independent reproduction choices let these descendant [total progeny](../../../../../../../total-progeny-of-a-branching-process.md) variables be represented by independent copies $Z_1,Z_2,\ldots$, independent of $N$. Hence, on this family-tree construction,

$$
\boxed{Z=1+\sum_{i=1}^N Z_i,}
$$

where the sum is zero when $N=0$.
