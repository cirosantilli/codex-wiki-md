↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) is represented by the [gradient](../../../../../../gradient.md), and the [Hessian matrix](../../../../../../hessian-matrix.md) contains the second partial [derivatives](../../../../../../derivative.md):

$$
\boxed{\nabla f(x,y)=\begin{pmatrix}4x^3-4y\\4y-4x\end{pmatrix},\qquad H(f)(x,y)=\begin{pmatrix}12x^2&-4\\-4&4\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

Equivalently the [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) as a linear map is $Df(x,y)(u,v)=(4x^3-4y)u+(4y-4x)v$.
