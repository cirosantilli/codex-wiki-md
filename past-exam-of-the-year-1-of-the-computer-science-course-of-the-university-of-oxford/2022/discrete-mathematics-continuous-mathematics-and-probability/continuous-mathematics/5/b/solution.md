<h1 id="continuous-mathematics/5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

By [Taylor's theorem](../../../../../../taylor-s-theorem.md) with [Lagrange remainder](../../../../../../lagrange-remainder.md), for an increment $t$,

$$
\boxed{f(x+t)=f(x)+tf'(x)+\frac{t^2}{2}f''(x)+R_x(t),\qquad R_x(t)=\frac{t^3}{6}f'''(\xi_t),}
$$

where $\xi_t$ lies between $x$ and $x+t$. The given third [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) bound implies $|R_x(t)|\le |t|^3/6$.
