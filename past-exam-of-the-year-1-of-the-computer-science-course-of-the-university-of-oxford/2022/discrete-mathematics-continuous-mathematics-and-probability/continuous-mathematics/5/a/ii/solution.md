<h1 id="continuous-mathematics/5/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Use [composite Simpson's rule](../../../../../../../composite-simpson-s-rule.md) on this smooth, bounded one-dimensional interval. Its fourth [derivative](../../../../../../../derivative.md) is bounded on the interval, so the [error bound for composite Simpson's rule](../../../../../../../error-bound-for-composite-simpson-s-rule.md) gives **$O(N^{-4})$** in [big O notation](../../../../../../../big-o-notation.md) as the number of function evaluations increases.
