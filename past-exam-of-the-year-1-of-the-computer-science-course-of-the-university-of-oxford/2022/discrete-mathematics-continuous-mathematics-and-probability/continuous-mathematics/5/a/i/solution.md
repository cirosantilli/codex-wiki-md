<h1 id="continuous-mathematics/5/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use [Simpson's rule](../../../../../../../simpson-s-rule.md): the [cubic exactness of Simpson's rule](../../../../../../../cubic-exactness-of-simpson-s-rule.md) includes every quadratic integrand, so three function evaluations already give the exact answer. Thus **the error is zero**. For a general smooth integrand the corresponding [composite Simpson's rule](../../../../../../../composite-simpson-s-rule.md) has error $O(N^{-4})$ in [big O notation](../../../../../../../big-o-notation.md), but that bound is unnecessarily weak here.
