<h1 id="continuous-mathematics/5/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Use [tensor-product quadrature](../../../../../../../tensor-product-quadrature.md) with [composite Simpson's rule](../../../../../../../composite-simpson-s-rule.md) in each coordinate. If there are $m$ subintervals in each direction, then $N=(m+1)^2$ and the [quadrature rule](../../../../../../../quadrature-rule.md) error is $O(m^{-4})$: apply the one-dimensional [error bound for composite Simpson's rule](../../../../../../../error-bound-for-composite-simpson-s-rule.md) successively, using bounded fourth partial [derivatives](../../../../../../../derivative.md). Hence the error is **$O(N^{-2})$** in [big O notation](../../../../../../../big-o-notation.md). The rectangular domain makes the product grid convenient.
