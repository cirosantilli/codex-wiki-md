The [second-derivative criterion for convexity](../../../../../../../second-derivative-criterion-for-convexity.md) is

$$
\boxed{f\text{ is convex on }\mathbb R\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad f''(x)\ge0\text{ for every }x\in\mathbb R.}
$$

The non-strict inequality is necessary and sufficient; the stronger condition $f''>0$ is sufficient for [strict convexity](../../../../../../../strictly-convex-function.md).
