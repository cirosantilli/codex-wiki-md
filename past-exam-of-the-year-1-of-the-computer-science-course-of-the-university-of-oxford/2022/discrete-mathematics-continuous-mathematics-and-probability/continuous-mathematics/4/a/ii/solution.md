<h1 id="continuous-mathematics/4/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Expand before taking the [second derivative](../../../../../../../second-derivative.md):

$$
f(x)=e^{3x}+3e^x+3e^{-x}+e^{-3x},\qquad f''(x)=9e^{3x}+3e^x+3e^{-x}+9e^{-3x}>0.
$$

The [second-derivative criterion for convexity](../../../../../../../second-derivative-criterion-for-convexity.md) proves that **$f$ is [strictly convex](../../../../../../../strictly-convex-function.md)**, hence [convex](../../../../../../../convex-function.md).
