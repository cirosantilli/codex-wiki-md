<h1 id="continuous-mathematics/4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The map $u\mapsto u^2$ is [strictly increasing](../../../../../../strictly-increasing-function.md) on $[0,\infty)$: for $u,v\ge0$, $u\le v$ if and only if $u^2\le v^2$. Therefore a point has the smallest $f$-value if and only if it has the smallest $f^2$-value, and the same applies to $g$. By [minimizers under strictly increasing transformations](../../../../../../minimizers-under-strictly-increasing-transformations.md), **both asserted [argmin](../../../../../../argmin.md) equalities hold**, including when the [argmin](../../../../../../argmin.md) sets are empty. The differentiability assumptions are unnecessary for this order argument.
