↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [Hessian criterion for convexity](../../../../../../../hessian-criterion-for-convexity.md), in the usual twice-differentiable multivariate setting, is

$$
\boxed{g\text{ is convex on }\mathbb R^d\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad v^{\mathsf T}H(g)(x)v\ge0\text{ for all }x,v\in\mathbb R^d.}
$$

Thus the [Hessian matrix](../../../../../../../hessian-matrix.md) must be a [positive semidefinite matrix](../../../../../../../positive-semidefinite-matrix.md) everywhere. The criterion follows by applying the [second-derivative criterion for convexity](../../../../../../../second-derivative-criterion-for-convexity.md) to each line restriction $t\mapsto g(x+tv)$, whose [second derivative](../../../../../../../second-derivative.md) is $v^{\mathsf T}H(g)(x+tv)v$. Continuous second partials are a sufficient regularity hypothesis for this line-restriction argument.
