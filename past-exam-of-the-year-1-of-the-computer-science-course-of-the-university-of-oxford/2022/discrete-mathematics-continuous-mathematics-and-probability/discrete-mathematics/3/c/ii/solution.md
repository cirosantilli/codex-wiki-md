↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

This [binary relation](../../../../../../../binary-relation.md) is **not an [equivalence relation](../../../../../../../equivalence-relation.md)**, because it is not [symmetric](../../../../../../../symmetric-relation.md): $(0,0)R(0,1)$ since $0\le1$, but $(0,1)R(0,0)$ is false. In fact the injective encoding $(a,b)\mapsto10a+b$ gives a [total order](../../../../../../../total-order.md). There are no [equivalence classes](../../../../../../../equivalence-class.md) for this [binary relation](../../../../../../../binary-relation.md).
