<h1 id="discrete-mathematics/3/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Every nonzero integer $a$ has $a^2>0$, proving [reflexivity](../../../../../../../reflexive-relation.md). Because $ab=ba$, the relation is [symmetric](../../../../../../../symmetric-relation.md). If $ab>0$ and $bc>0$, then $ac=(ab)(bc)/b^2>0$, proving [transitivity](../../../../../../../transitive-relation.md). Thus this is an [equivalence relation](../../../../../../../equivalence-relation.md), whose **two [equivalence classes](../../../../../../../equivalence-class.md) are the positive integers and the negative integers**:

$$
\{1,2,3,\ldots\},\qquad\{-1,-2,-3,\ldots\}.
$$
