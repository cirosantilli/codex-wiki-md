<h1 id="discrete-mathematics/3/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Write $q(x,y)=x^2+y^2$. Equality of $q$-values defines an [equivalence relation induced by a function](../../../../../../../equivalence-relation-induced-by-a-function.md): equality is [reflexive](../../../../../../../reflexive-relation.md), [symmetric](../../../../../../../symmetric-relation.md), and [transitive](../../../../../../../transitive-relation.md), so all three properties hold for $R$. The [equivalence class](../../../../../../../equivalence-class.md) of $(x,y)$ is

$$
\boxed{[(x,y)]=\{(u,v):u^2+v^2=x^2+y^2\}.}
$$

For $(x,y)\ne(0,0)$ this is the [circle](../../../../../../../circle.md) centred at the origin with radius $\sqrt{x^2+y^2}$; the remaining [equivalence class](../../../../../../../equivalence-class.md) is the singleton $\{(0,0)\}$.
