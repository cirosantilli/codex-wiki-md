A [binary relation](../../../../../../../binary-relation.md) $R$ on $A$ is a [transitive relation](../../../../../../../transitive-relation.md) precisely when **$(aRb\ \text{and}\ bRc)\Rightarrow aRc$ for all $a,b,c\in A$**.
