↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For pairwise [disjoint](../../../../../../../disjoint-sets.md) [finite sets](../../../../../../../finite-set.md), the [rule of sum](../../../../../../../addition-principle.md) gives

$$
\left|\bigcup_{i=1}^n A_i\right|=\sum_{i=1}^n|A_i|.
$$

The password sets of different lengths are [disjoint](../../../../../../../disjoint-sets.md), so the required number is **$P_4+P_6+P_8$**.
