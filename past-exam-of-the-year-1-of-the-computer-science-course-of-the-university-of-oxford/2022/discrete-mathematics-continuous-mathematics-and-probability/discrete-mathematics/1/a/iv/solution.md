<h1 id="discrete-mathematics/1/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The [rule of product](../../../../../../../rule-of-product.md) counts $7^4$ unrestricted passwords and $6^4$ passwords containing no A. By the [rule of subtraction](../../../../../../../rule-of-subtraction.md), the answer is

$$
\boxed{7^4-6^4=2401-1296=1105.}
$$
