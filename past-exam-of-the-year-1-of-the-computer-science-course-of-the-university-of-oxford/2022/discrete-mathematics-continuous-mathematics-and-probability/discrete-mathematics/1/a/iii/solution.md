↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [rule of product](../../../../../../../rule-of-product.md) says that a sequence of $k$ choices, with $n_i$ possibilities at stage $i$ regardless of earlier choices, has **$\prod_{i=1}^k n_i$** possible outcomes. Applied to the [Cartesian product](../../../../../../../cartesian-product.md) of [finite sets](../../../../../../../finite-set.md), it gives $|A\times B|=|A||B|$. Here independence means that the available choices do not restrict one another; no [probability](../../../../probability.md) assumption is needed.
