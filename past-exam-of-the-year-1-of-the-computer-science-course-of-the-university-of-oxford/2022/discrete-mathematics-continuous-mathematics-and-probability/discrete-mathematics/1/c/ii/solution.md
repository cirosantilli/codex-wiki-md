<h1 id="discrete-mathematics/1/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $A_d$ contain the multiples of $d$ among $1,\ldots,999$. Its [cardinality](../../../../../../../cardinality.md) is $\lfloor999/d\rfloor$, using the [floor function](../../../../../../../floor-function.md). Because $3,7,11$ are pairwise [coprime](../../../../../../../coprime-integers.md), their pairwise intersections correspond to divisibility by $21,33,77$, and their triple intersection to divisibility by $231$. The [inclusion-exclusion principle](../../../../../../../inclusion-exclusion-principle.md) gives

$$
\boxed{333+142+90-47-30-12+4=480.}
$$
