For [finite sets](../../../../../../../finite-set.md), the [inclusion-exclusion principle](../../../../../../../inclusion-exclusion-principle.md) states

$$
\boxed{\left|\bigcup_{i=1}^n A_i\right|=\sum_{\varnothing\ne J\subseteq\{1,\ldots,n\}}(-1)^{|J|+1}\left|\bigcap_{j\in J}A_j\right|.}
$$

Thus single-set [cardinalities](../../../../../../../cardinality.md) are added, pairwise-intersection [cardinalities](../../../../../../../cardinality.md) subtracted, triple-intersection [cardinalities](../../../../../../../cardinality.md) added, and so on.
