↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [power set](../../../../../../../power-set.md) is $\mathcal P(A)=\{B:B\subseteq A\}$, the [set](../../../../../../../set-split.md) of all [subsets](../../../../../../../subset.md) of $A$. In this case,

$$
\boxed{\mathcal P(A)=\{\varnothing,\{a\},\{b\},\{c\},\{a,b\},\{a,c\},\{b,c\},\{a,b,c\}\}.}
$$
