<h1 id="discrete-mathematics/2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use [mathematical induction](../../../../../../mathematical-induction.md) on $n\ge0$. For $n=0$, the sum is $0!\,0=0=1!-1$. Suppose it is $(n+1)!-1$ at $n$. Then

$$
\sum_{i=0}^{n+1}i!\,i=(n+1)!-1+(n+1)!(n+1)=(n+2)!-1.
$$

The induction is complete, proving **$\sum_{i=0}^n i!\,i=(n+1)!-1$**. The [factorial](../../../../../../factorial.md) identity $i!\,i=(i+1)!-i!$ also explains the cancellation directly.
