↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [eigenspace](../../../../../../eigenspace.md) for 3 has [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) $\{(1,1,1)^T\}$. The [eigenspace](../../../../../../eigenspace.md) for 0 is the plane $v_1+v_2+v_3=0$, with [basis](../../../../../../basis.md)

$$
\boxed{E_3=\operatorname{span}\{(1,1,1)^T\},\qquad E_0=\operatorname{span}\{(1,0,-1)^T,(0,1,-1)^T\}.}
$$

The last two vectors are linearly independent and every vector in the plane equals $v_1(1,0,-1)^T+v_2(0,1,-1)^T$.
