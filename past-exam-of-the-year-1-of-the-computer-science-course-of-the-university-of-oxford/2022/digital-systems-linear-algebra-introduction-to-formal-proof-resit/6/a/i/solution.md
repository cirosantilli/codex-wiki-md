<h1 id="6/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $u=(1,1,1)^T$, the [all-ones matrix](../../../../../../all-ones-matrix.md) is $A=uu^T$, so $Au=3u$ and $Av=0$ whenever $v_1+v_2+v_3=0$. These subspaces have dimensions one and two and span $\mathbb R^3$. Thus **the [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are $3$ and $0$**.
