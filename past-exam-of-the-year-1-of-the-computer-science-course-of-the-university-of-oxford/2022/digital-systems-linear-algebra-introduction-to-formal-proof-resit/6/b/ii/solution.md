↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) of $E_n$ is $\{u\}$. For $n\geq2$,

$$
\boxed{E_0=\{v:\sum_jv_j=0\}=\operatorname{span}\{e_1-e_n,\ldots,e_{n-1}-e_n\}.}
$$

The displayed vectors are linearly independent by their first $n-1$ coordinates, and every vector with zero coordinate sum has exactly that expansion. At $n=1$ this list is empty and the [kernel](../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) is $\{0\}$; it is not an additional [eigenspace](../../../../../../eigenspace.md) associated with a present [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md). This is the [spectrum of an all-ones matrix](../../../../../../spectrum-of-an-all-ones-matrix.md).
