↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The decomposition into $\operatorname{span}\{u\}$ and the zero-sum [vector subspace](../../../../../../vector-subspace.md) gives [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) $t^{n-1}(t-n)$. For $n\geq2$, **$n$ has [algebraic multiplicity](../../../../../../algebraic-multiplicity.md) and [geometric multiplicity](../../../../../../geometric-multiplicity.md) one; zero has both multiplicities $n-1$**. At $n=1$, only the [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) 1 occurs, with both multiplicities one. Referring to a multiplicity zero for the absent root zero is merely a bookkeeping convention, not an assertion that it is an [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md).
