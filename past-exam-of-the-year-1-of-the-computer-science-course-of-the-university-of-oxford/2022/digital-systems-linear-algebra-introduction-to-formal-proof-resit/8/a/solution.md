<h1 id="8/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Using [first-order logic](../../../../../first-order-logic.md) with equality, one suitable sentence is

$$
\boxed{\exists e\,\bigl(Eq(e)\land\exists u\exists v\,[u\ne v\land Sol(e,u)\land Sol(e,v)\land\forall w\,(Sol(e,w)\to(w=u\lor w=v))]\bigr).}
$$

The distinct witnesses give at least two solutions, and the universal clause excludes every third solution. All [variables](../../../../../variable-mathematics.md) are bound, and the same equation variable $e$ is used in every solution predicate. This is an instance of a [first-order definition of exactly finitely many witnesses](../../../../../first-order-definition-of-exactly-finitely-many-witnesses.md).
