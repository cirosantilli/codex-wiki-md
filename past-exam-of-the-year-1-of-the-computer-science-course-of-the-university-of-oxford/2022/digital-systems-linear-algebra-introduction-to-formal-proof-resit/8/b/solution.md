<h1 id="8/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

On the universe $\mathbb Z$, interpret the [first-order signature](../../../../../first-order-signature.md) by

$$
\boxed{c_1^{\mathcal A}=c_2^{\mathcal A}=0,\quad f^{\mathcal A}(x,y)=x+y,\quad R_2^{\mathcal A}=\mathbb Z_{\geq0},\quad R_1^{\mathcal A}=\{(u,v)\in\mathbb Z^2:u\leq v\}.}
$$

This assigns both constants, a total binary function, a unary relation and a binary relation, so it specifies a complete [first-order structure](../../../../../first-order-structure.md). If $R_2(x)$ and $R_2(y)$ both hold, then $x,y\geq0$, and hence $f(c_1,c_2)=0\leq x+y=f(x,y)$, proving the consequent. If either premise fails, the implication is true. Thus the universally quantified implication holds for every pair of [integers](../../../../../integer-split.md), nonvacuously for every pair of nonnegative [integers](../../../../../integer-split.md). Distinct constant symbols need not denote distinct objects unless a separate axiom requires it.
