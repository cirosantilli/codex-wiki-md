<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The pull-down [Boolean function](../../../../../boolean-function.md) is $ab\lor bc=b(a\lor c)$. A conducting pull-down makes $z=0$, so

$$
\boxed{z=\neg\bigl(b\land(a\lor c)\bigr)=\neg b\lor(\neg a\land\neg c).}
$$

In particular, if $b=0$ the output is always high; if $b=1$, the output is high precisely when both other inputs are low. These cases give the [truth table](../../../../../truth-table.md) without confusing a network's conducting condition with the output voltage.
