<h1 id="7/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Reading the [abstract syntax tree](../../../../../abstract-syntax-tree.md) recursively gives the eight distinct [subformulas](../../../../../subformula.md)

$$
\boxed{A,\ B,\ C,\ \neg A,\ \neg B,\ C\to\neg A,\ \neg B\land(C\to\neg A),\ A\lor(\neg B\land(C\to\neg A)).}
$$

A [subformula](../../../../../subformula.md) includes the complete [propositional formula](../../../../../propositional-formula.md) itself as well as the operands of its connectives. There are nine occurrences in the [abstract syntax tree](../../../../../abstract-syntax-tree.md), because the atomic formula $A$ occurs twice, but only eight different [propositional formulas](../../../../../propositional-formula.md) in the set.
