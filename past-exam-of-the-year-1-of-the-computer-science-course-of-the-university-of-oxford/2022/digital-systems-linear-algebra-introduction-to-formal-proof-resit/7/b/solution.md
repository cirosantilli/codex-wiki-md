↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Eliminate the [logical implication](../../../../../logical-implication.md) and apply [De Morgan's laws](../../../../../de-morgan-s-laws.md):

$$
(A\lor B)\to(B\land C)\equiv\neg(A\lor B)\lor(B\land C)\equiv\boxed{(\neg A\land\neg B)\lor(B\land C).}
$$

This is [disjunctive normal form](../../../../../disjunctive-normal-form.md): a [logical disjunction](../../../../../logical-disjunction.md) of conjunctions of [literals](../../../../../boolean-literal.md). It has four [literal](../../../../../boolean-literal.md) occurrences, namely $\neg A,\neg B,B,C$, meeting the stated bound.
