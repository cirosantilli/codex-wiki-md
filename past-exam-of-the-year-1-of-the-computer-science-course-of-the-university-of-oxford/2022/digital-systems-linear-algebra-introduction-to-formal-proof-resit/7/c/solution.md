<h1 id="7/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Repeated use of $U\to V\equiv\neg U\lor V$ yields

$$
\varphi_n\equiv\neg A_n\lor\neg A_{n-1}\lor\cdots\lor\neg A_2\lor A_1.
$$

This [logical disjunction](../../../../../logical-disjunction.md) is false exactly when $A_2,\ldots,A_n$ are all true and $A_1$ is false. There is precisely one such [Boolean valuation](../../../../../boolean-valuation.md) out of the $2^n$ assignments to the $n$ independent propositional variables. Thus **the number of [satisfying assignments](../../../../../satisfying-assignment.md) is $2^n-1$**. This is the [satisfying-assignment count for nested implications](../../../../../satisfying-assignment-count-for-nested-implications.md); the implication brackets associate to the right here.
