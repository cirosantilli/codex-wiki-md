<h1 id="4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For an [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $t\in\{\varphi,\psi\}$, the equation $A(x,y)^T=t(x,y)^T$ gives $x=ty$ and $(t+1)y=t^2y$. The second identity holds because $t^2=t+1$. Taking $y=1$ gives the [eigenvectors](../../../../../eigenvector.md)

$$
\boxed{v_\varphi=\begin{pmatrix}\varphi\\1\end{pmatrix},\qquad v_\psi=\begin{pmatrix}\psi\\1\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

Each associated [eigenspace](../../../../../eigenspace.md) consists of all scalar multiples of its displayed vector. A zero vector is excluded when speaking of an [eigenvector](../../../../../eigenvector.md).
