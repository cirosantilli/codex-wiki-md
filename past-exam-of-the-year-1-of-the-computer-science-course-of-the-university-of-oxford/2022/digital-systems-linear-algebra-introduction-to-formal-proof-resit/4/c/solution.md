<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) is

$$
\det(tI-A)=\det\begin{pmatrix}t-1&-1\\-1&t\end{pmatrix}=t^2-t-1.
$$

Its two distinct real roots are the [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md)

$$
\boxed{\varphi=\frac{1+\sqrt5}{2}>0,\qquad \psi=\frac{1-\sqrt5}{2}<0.}
$$

Their product is $-1$, so $\psi=-1/\varphi$. Equivalently, $\varphi^2=\varphi+1$ implies $1/\varphi=\varphi-1$. The positive [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) is the [golden ratio](../../../../../golden-ratio.md).
