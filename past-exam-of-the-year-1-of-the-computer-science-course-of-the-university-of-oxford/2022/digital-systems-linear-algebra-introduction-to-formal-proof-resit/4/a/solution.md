<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The update must place the sum of the two current entries in the first coordinate and retain the old first coordinate in the second. Thus choose the [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md)

$$
\boxed{A=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

By [matrix multiplication](../../../../../matrix-multiplication.md), $A(L_n,L_{n-1})^T=(L_n+L_{n-1},L_n)^T=(L_{n+1},L_n)^T$ for every $n\geq1$, using the given [recurrence relation](../../../../../recurrence-relation.md). This is the companion-matrix representation of the [Lucas numbers](../../../../../lucas-number.md).
