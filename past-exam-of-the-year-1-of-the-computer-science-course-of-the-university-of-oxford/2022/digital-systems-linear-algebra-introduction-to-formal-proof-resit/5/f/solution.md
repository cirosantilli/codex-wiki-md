↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

An integer-entry example is

$$
\boxed{A=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&-1\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

Direct [matrix multiplication](../../../../../matrix-multiplication.md) gives $A^2=\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix}-1&1\\-1&0\end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$, so adding $A$ and $I_2$ gives the zero [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md). Equivalently, this is the [companion matrix](../../../../../companion-matrix.md) of $t^2+t+1$ and has the two nonreal cube roots of unity as [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md). The direct multiplication is already a complete proof. It also shows why part (e) does not extend to all real square [matrices](../../../../../matrix.md): with this $A$, choosing $B=0$ satisfies the original product identity.
