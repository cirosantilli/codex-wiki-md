<h1 id="5/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

For scalar $a=A\ne0$, part (c) gives

$$
B=\frac{a^2+a+1}{a}=\frac{(a+\tfrac12)^2+\tfrac34}{a}.
$$

The numerator is strictly positive for every real $a$, and the denominator is nonzero. Therefore **$B\ne0$ and hence $B$ is invertible**. This proof uses the order structure of the real scalars; it does not say that the [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) $A^2+A+I$ must be nonsingular in higher dimension.
