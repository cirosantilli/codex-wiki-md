<h1 id="5/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Multiply the original equation on the left by $A^{-1}$ to obtain

$$
B=A+I+A^{-1}.
$$

Each term on the right commutes with $A$. Thus

$$
\boxed{BA=A^2+A+I=AB.}
$$

No general commutativity of [matrix multiplication](../../../../../matrix-multiplication.md) is being assumed; it follows from this particular expression for $B$.
