<h1 id="5/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Differentiate the product $4(x-1)^{-2}(x+1)^{-1}$ using the [product rule](../../../../../../product-rule.md) and [chain rule](../../../../../../chain-rule.md):

$$
f'(x)=-8(x-1)^{-3}(x+1)^{-1}-4(x-1)^{-2}(x+1)^{-2}.
$$

Combining terms over a common denominator gives

$$
\boxed{f'(x)=-\frac{4(3x+1)}{(x-1)^3(x+1)^2},\qquad x\ne\pm1.}
$$
