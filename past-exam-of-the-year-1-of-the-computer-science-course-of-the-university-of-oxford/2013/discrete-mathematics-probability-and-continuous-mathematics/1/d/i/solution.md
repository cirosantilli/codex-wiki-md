↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [image of a function](../../../../../../image-of-a-function.md) is the [set](../../../../../../set-split.md) of values actually attained as its argument ranges over its [domain of a function](../../../../../../domain-of-a-function.md):

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{im}f=f(A)=\{f(a):a\in A\}=\{b\in B:\exists a\in A,\ f(a)=b\}.}
$$

It is a [subset](../../../../../../subset.md) of the [codomain](../../../../../../codomain.md) $B$, and need not equal $B$.
