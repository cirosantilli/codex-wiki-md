↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) is [surjective](../../../../../../surjective-function.md), also called onto, when every element of its [codomain](../../../../../../codomain.md) has at least one [preimage](../../../../../../preimage.md):

$$
\boxed{f(A)=B\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\forall b\in B\ \exists a\in A:\ f(a)=b.}
$$

The condition concerns the specified [codomain](../../../../../../codomain.md), not just the formula defining the [function](../../../../../../function-split.md).
