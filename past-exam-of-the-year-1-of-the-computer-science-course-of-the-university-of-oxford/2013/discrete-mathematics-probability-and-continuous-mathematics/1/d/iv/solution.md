A [bijection](../../../../../../bijection.md) is a [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) that is both [injective](../../../../../../injective-function.md) and [surjective](../../../../../../surjective-function.md). Each element of the [codomain](../../../../../../codomain.md) then has exactly one [preimage](../../../../../../preimage.md):

$$
\boxed{\forall b\in B\ \exists!a\in A:\ f(a)=b.}
$$

This unique-preimage property allows the [inverse function](../../../../../../inverse-function.md) $f^{-1}:B\to A$ to be defined on all of $B$.
