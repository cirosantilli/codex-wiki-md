The [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) is [injective](../../../../../../injective-function.md), also called one-to-one, when equal outputs force equal inputs:

$$
\boxed{\forall a,a'\in A,\quad f(a)=f(a')\Longrightarrow a=a'.}
$$

Equivalently, different elements of the [domain of a function](../../../../../../domain-of-a-function.md) always have different images.
