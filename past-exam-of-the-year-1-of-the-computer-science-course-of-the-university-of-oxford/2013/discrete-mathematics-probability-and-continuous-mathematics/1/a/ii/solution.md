<h1 id="1/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Apply the [distributive property](../../../../../../distributive-property.md) to the [intersection](../../../../../../set-intersection.md) with $\overline C$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
(A\cap B)\setminus C&=(A\cap B)\cap\overline C=(A\cap\overline C)\cap(B\cap\overline C),\\
(A\cup B)\setminus C&=(A\cup B)\cap\overline C=(A\cap\overline C)\cup(B\cap\overline C).
\end{aligned}
$$

In the first line the repeated factor $\overline C$ changes nothing. Rewriting in terms of [set differences](../../../../../../set-difference.md) yields

$$
\boxed{(A\cap B)\setminus C=(A\setminus C)\cap(B\setminus C),\quad(A\cup B)\setminus C=(A\setminus C)\cup(B\setminus C).}
$$
