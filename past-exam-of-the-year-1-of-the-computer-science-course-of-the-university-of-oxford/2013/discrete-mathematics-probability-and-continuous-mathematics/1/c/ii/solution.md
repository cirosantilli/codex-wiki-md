<h1 id="1/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Write the [set difference](../../../../../../set-difference.md) as an [intersection](../../../../../../set-intersection.md) and use the fact that taking the [complement of a set](../../../../../../complement-of-a-set.md) twice returns that [set](../../../../../../set-split.md). The [commutativity](../../../../../../commutativity.md) of [intersection](../../../../../../set-intersection.md) gives the reversible calculation

$$
\boxed{\overline A\setminus\overline B=\overline A\cap\overline{\overline B}=\overline A\cap B=B\cap\overline A=B\setminus A.}
$$
