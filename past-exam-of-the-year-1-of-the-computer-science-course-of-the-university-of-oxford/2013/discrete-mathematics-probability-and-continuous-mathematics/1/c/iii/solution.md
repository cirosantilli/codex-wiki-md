<h1 id="1/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Use the [symmetric difference](../../../../../../symmetric-difference.md) formula from part (b), followed by the [set difference](../../../../../../set-difference.md) identity from part (c)(ii):

$$
\begin{aligned}
\overline A\oplus\overline B
&=(\overline A\setminus\overline B)\cup(\overline B\setminus\overline A)\\
&=(B\setminus A)\cup(A\setminus B)
=A\oplus B.
\end{aligned}
$$

Taking both [complements](../../../../../../complement-of-a-set.md) swaps the two ways to belong to exactly one [set](../../../../../../set-split.md), leaving their [union](../../../../../../set-union.md) unchanged:

$$
\boxed{\overline A\oplus\overline B=A\oplus B.}
$$
