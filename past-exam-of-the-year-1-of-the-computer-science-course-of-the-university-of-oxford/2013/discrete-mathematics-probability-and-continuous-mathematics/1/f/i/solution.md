↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [function composition](../../../../../../function-composition.md) of two [bijections](../../../../../../bijection.md) is a [bijection](../../../../../../bijection.md). Its [inverse function](../../../../../../inverse-function.md) is two-sided, so

$$
\boxed{\text{True: }(f\circ g)\circ(f\circ g)^{-1}=\operatorname{id}.}
$$

This is exactly the defining cancellation property of an [inverse function](../../../../../../inverse-function.md), with the whole [function composition](../../../../../../function-composition.md) regarded as one [function](../../../../../../function-split.md).
