<h1 id="1/f/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Use the [associativity](../../../../../../associative-property.md) of [function composition](../../../../../../function-composition.md) to cancel adjacent [inverse functions](../../../../../../inverse-function.md) in the right order:

$$
f\circ g\circ g^{-1}\circ f^{-1}=f\circ\operatorname{id}\circ f^{-1}=\operatorname{id}.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{\text{True: }(f\circ g)\circ(g^{-1}\circ f^{-1})=\operatorname{id}.}
$$
