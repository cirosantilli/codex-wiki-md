<h1 id="1/f/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The inverse factors are generally in the wrong order. For a counterexample, take the [bijections](../../../../../../bijection.md) $f(x)=x+1$ and $g(x)=2x$, with [inverse functions](../../../../../../inverse-function.md) $f^{-1}(x)=x-1$ and $g^{-1}(x)=x/2$. Then

$$
((f\circ g)\circ(f^{-1}\circ g^{-1}))(x)=2(x/2-1)+1=x-1.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{\text{False in general; the displayed composition need not be }\operatorname{id}.}
$$

For particular [bijections](../../../../../../bijection.md) that commute the equality can hold; the claim fails as a universal assertion.
