↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

For the [bijections](../../../../../../bijection.md) $f(x)=x+1$ and $g(x)=2x$ used above, $f\circ g$ sends $x$ to $2x+1$. Its [inverse function](../../../../../../inverse-function.md) sends $x$ to $(x-1)/2$, whereas $f^{-1}\circ g^{-1}$ sends $x$ to $x/2-1$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\text{False in general: }(f\circ g)^{-1}\ne f^{-1}\circ g^{-1}.}
$$
