<h1 id="9/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The factor $2x-x^3=x(2-x^2)$ is positive on $(0,\sqrt2)$ and negative on $(\sqrt2,2)$. For every nonzero $\alpha$, the proposed [probability density function](../../../../../../probability-density-function.md) is therefore negative on an interval. The choice $\alpha=0$ has total integral zero. In fact,

$$
\int_0^2(2x-x^3)\,dx=\left[x^2-\frac{x^4}{4}\right]_0^2=0,
$$

so no scaling could normalise it even without the sign obstruction. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\text{No value of }\alpha\text{ gives a probability density function; no corresponding expectation exists}.}
$$
