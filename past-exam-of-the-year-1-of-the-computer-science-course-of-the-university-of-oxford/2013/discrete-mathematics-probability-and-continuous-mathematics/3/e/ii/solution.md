↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

This is equality of remainders in [modular arithmetic](../../../../../../modular-arithmetic-split.md). It is reflexive because $3\mid(n-n)=0$, symmetric because $3\mid(n-m)$ implies $3\mid(m-n)$, and transitive because $3\mid(n-m)$ and $3\mid(k-n)$ imply $3\mid(k-m)$ by addition. Thus

$$
\boxed{m\sim n\iff m\equiv n\pmod3\text{ is an equivalence relation}.}
$$

Its three [equivalence classes](../../../../../../equivalence-class.md) are

$$
\boxed{C_r=\{n\in\mathbb N:n\equiv r\pmod3\},\qquad r=0,1,2.}
$$

If zero is included in $\mathbb N$, it belongs to $C_0$; otherwise that class starts at three.
