↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [binary relation](../../../../../../binary-relation.md) $\leq$ is reflexive and transitive but is not a [symmetric relation](../../../../../../symmetric-relation.md). For example, $1\leq2$ while $2\nleq1$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\leq\text{ is not an equivalence relation on }\mathbb N.}
$$

There are consequently no [equivalence classes](../../../../../../equivalence-class.md) for this [binary relation](../../../../../../binary-relation.md) in the requested sense.
