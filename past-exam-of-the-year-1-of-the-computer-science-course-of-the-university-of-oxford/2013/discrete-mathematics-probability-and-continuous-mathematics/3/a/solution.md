[Mathematical induction](../../../../../mathematical-induction.md) has two obligations. Establish the base case $S(0)$ if the [natural numbers](../../../../../natural-number.md) start at zero, or $S(1)$ if they start at one. Then prove the induction step: for an arbitrary natural $k$ in the relevant range, assume $S(k)$ and deduce $S(k+1)$.

The base case starts the chain, and the step propagates truth to every subsequent [natural number](../../../../../natural-number.md):

$$
\boxed{S(n_0)\ \land\ \bigl(\forall k\geq n_0,\ S(k)\Rightarrow S(k+1)\bigr)\ \Longrightarrow\ \forall n\geq n_0,\ S(n).}
$$
