For a [discrete random variable](../../../../../../discrete-random-variable.md) taking nonnegative integer values, its [probability generating function](../../../../../../probability-generating-function.md) is

$$
\boxed{G_X(s)=\mathbb E[s^X]=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\mathbb P(X=k)s^k.}
$$

It is defined at least for $|s|\leq1$. The usual [probability generating function](../../../../../../probability-generating-function.md) is intended for nonnegative integer-valued [random variables](../../../../../../random-variable-split.md); a general discrete real-valued [random variable](../../../../../../random-variable-split.md) need not have this form. The coefficient of $s^k$ recovers $\mathbb P(X=k)$.
