↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [conditional probability](../../../../../../conditional-probability.md) rescales the [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) of the part of $A$ lying in $B$ by the [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) of $B$:

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(A\mid B)=\frac{\mathbb P(A\cap B)}{\mathbb P(B)},\qquad\mathbb P(B)>0.}
$$

The denominator makes $B$ itself have [conditional probability](../../../../../../conditional-probability.md) one.
