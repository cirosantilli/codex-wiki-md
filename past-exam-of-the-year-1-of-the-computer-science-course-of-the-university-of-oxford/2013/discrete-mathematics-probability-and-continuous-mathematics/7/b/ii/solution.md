<h1 id="7/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [events](../../../../../../event.md) $A\cap B$ and $A\cap B^c$ are disjoint and their [union](../../../../../../set-union.md) is $A$. Additivity of [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) gives

$$
\mathbb P(A)=\mathbb P(A\cap B)+\mathbb P(A\cap B^c).
$$

Both conditioning [events](../../../../../../event.md) have positive [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) because $0<\mathbb P(B)<1$. Apply the definition of [conditional probability](../../../../../../conditional-probability.md) to each term to obtain the [law of total probability](../../../../../../law-of-total-probability.md):

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(A)=\mathbb P(A\mid B)\mathbb P(B)+\mathbb P(A\mid B^c)\mathbb P(B^c).}
$$
