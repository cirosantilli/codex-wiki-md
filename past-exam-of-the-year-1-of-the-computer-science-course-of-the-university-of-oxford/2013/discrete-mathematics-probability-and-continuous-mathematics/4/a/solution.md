<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Take each [partial derivative](../../../../../partial-derivative.md) while holding the other coordinate fixed. Differentiating the [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md) term by term gives

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
f_x&=2x(y^4+y^2+1),\\
f_y&=4x^2y^3+3y^2+2x^2y-3,\\
f_{xx}&=2(y^4+y^2+1),\\
f_{xy}&=8xy^3+4xy,\\
f_{yy}&=12x^2y^2+6y+2x^2.
\end{aligned}}
$$

Since the [function](../../../../../function-split.md) is a [polynomial](../../../../../polynomial-split.md), both orders of the [mixed partial derivative](../../../../../mixed-partial-derivative.md) agree everywhere.
