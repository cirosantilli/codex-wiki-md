<h1 id="8/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Part (a) with $n=6$ gives $\mathbb E[S_1]=\mathbb E[S_2]=7/2$ and $\operatorname{var}(S_1)=\operatorname{var}(S_2)=35/12$. By [linearity of expectation](../../../../../../linearity-of-expectation.md),

$$
\mathbb E[X]=7,\qquad\mathbb E[Y]=0.
$$

[Independence of random variables](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) and $\operatorname{var}(-S_2)=\operatorname{var}(S_2)$ give the same [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md) for the sum and difference:

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[X]=7,\quad\mathbb E[Y]=0,\qquad\operatorname{var}(X)=\operatorname{var}(Y)=\frac{35}{6}.}
$$
