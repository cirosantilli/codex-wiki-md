<h1 id="8/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The identity $XY=(S_1+S_2)(S_1-S_2)=S_1^2-S_2^2$ gives $\mathbb E[XY]=0$, since the two rolls have the same [probability distribution](../../../../../../probability-distribution-split.md). Also $\mathbb E[X]\mathbb E[Y]=7\cdot0=0$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{cov}(X,Y)=\mathbb E[XY]-\mathbb E[X]\mathbb E[Y]=0.}
$$

The two [random variables](../../../../../../random-variable-split.md) are [uncorrelated random variables](../../../../../../uncorrelated-random-variables.md), but that alone does not establish [independence of random variables](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md).
