<h1 id="6/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For the homogeneous [linear ordinary differential equation](../../../../../linear-ordinary-differential-equation-split.md), the [characteristic equation](../../../../../characteristic-equation-of-a-constant-coefficient-differential-equation.md) is

$$
r^2+2r+2=0,\qquad r=-1\pm i.
$$

Its real solution is $y_h=e^{-x}(C_1\cos x+C_2\sin x)$. For a particular solution to the full [ordinary differential equation](../../../../../ordinary-differential-equation.md), try $y_p=ax+b$. Substitution gives

$$
2a+2ax+2b=8x+4,
$$

so $a=4$ and $b=-2$. Adding the homogeneous and particular solutions yields

$$
\boxed{y(x)=e^{-x}(C_1\cos x+C_2\sin x)+4x-2.}
$$

The two arbitrary real constants account for the second order of the [ordinary differential equation](../../../../../ordinary-differential-equation.md).
