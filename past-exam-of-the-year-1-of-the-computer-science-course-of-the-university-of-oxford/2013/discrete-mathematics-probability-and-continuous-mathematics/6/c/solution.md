<h1 id="6/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Divide by $x^2$, which is allowed because $x>0$:

$$
y'+\frac1xy=4e^{x^2}.
$$

The [integrating factor](../../../../../integrating-factor.md) for this [linear ordinary differential equation](../../../../../linear-ordinary-differential-equation-split.md) is $e^{\int dx/x}=x$. Multiplying through makes the left side a [product rule](../../../../../product-rule.md) derivative:

$$
(xy)'=4xe^{x^2}.
$$

Part (b) now gives $xy=2e^{x^2}+C$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{y(x)=\frac{2e^{x^2}+C}{x},\qquad x>0.}
$$
