The [Big O notation](../../../../../big-o-notation.md) asserts an eventual uniform bound on the size of $f$ by a constant multiple of the size of $g$:

$$
\boxed{f(n)=O(g(n))\iff\exists C>0\ \exists n_0\ \forall n\geq n_0:\ |f(n)|\leq C|g(n)|.}
$$

The constants $C,n_0$ may depend on the two [functions](../../../../../function-split.md) but cannot depend on $n$. Absolute values matter because the stated [functions](../../../../../function-split.md) can be real-valued with either sign.
