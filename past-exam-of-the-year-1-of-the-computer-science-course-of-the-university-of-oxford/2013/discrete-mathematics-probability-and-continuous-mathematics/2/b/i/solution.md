↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [logarithm](../../../../../../logarithm.md) identity $a^{\log_b n}=n^{\log_b a}$ rewrites the expression as $n^{\log_2 3}$. Since $3<4=2^2$, we have $\log_2 3<2$, and for $n\geq1$,

$$
3^{\log_2 n}=n^{\log_2 3}\leq n^2.
$$

Thus the [Big O notation](../../../../../../big-o-notation.md) bound holds with $C=1$:

$$
\boxed{\text{True: }3^{\log_2 n}=O(n^2).}
$$
