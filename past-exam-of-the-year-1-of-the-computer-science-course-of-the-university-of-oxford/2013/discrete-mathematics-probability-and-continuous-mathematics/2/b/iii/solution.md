<h1 id="2/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Divide by the proposed comparison [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) $2^n$:

$$
\frac{n^2+n2^n}{2^n}=\frac{n^2}{2^n}+n\geq n.
$$

The ratio is unbounded, so no constant in the definition of [Big O notation](../../../../../../big-o-notation.md) can work:

$$
\boxed{\text{False: }n^2+n2^n\ne O(2^n).}
$$
