<h1 id="2/b/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

Treat $a,b$ as fixed positive [integers](../../../../../../integer-split.md). For $n\geq1$, $n^2+a\leq(1+a)n^2$. Raising this inequality to the positive power $b$ gives

$$
(n^2+a)^b\leq(1+a)^b n^{2b}.
$$

The factor $(1+a)^b$ is independent of $n$, as required by [Big O notation](../../../../../../big-o-notation.md). Hence

$$
\boxed{\text{True for fixed }a,b:\quad(n^2+a)^b=O(n^{2b}).}
$$

A bound uniform over varying $a,b$ is not what the notation here asserts.
