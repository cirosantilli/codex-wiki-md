↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The [exponential function](../../../../../../exponential-function.md) eventually grows faster than the fixed [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md). A direct justification uses the ratio $r_n=4^n/n^4$:

$$
\frac{r_{n+1}}{r_n}=4\left(\frac n{n+1}\right)^4\longrightarrow4.
$$

For all sufficiently large $n$, this ratio exceeds $2$, so $r_n$ grows without bound. The proposed [Big O notation](../../../../../../big-o-notation.md) bound is therefore impossible:

$$
\boxed{\text{False: }4^n\ne O(n^4).}
$$
