<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For an $m\times n$ [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) $A$ over the [real numbers](../../../../../real-number.md), write its rows as $r_1,\ldots,r_m$ and its columns as $c_1,\ldots,c_n$. Then

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Row}(A)&=\operatorname{span}\{r_1,\ldots,r_m\}\subseteq\mathbb R^n,\\
\operatorname{Col}(A)&=\operatorname{span}\{c_1,\ldots,c_n\}\subseteq\mathbb R^m,\\
\operatorname{Null}(A)&=\{x\in\mathbb R^n:Ax=0\},\\
\operatorname{rank}A&=\dim\operatorname{Row}(A)=\dim\operatorname{Col}(A).
\end{aligned}}
$$

Thus the [row space](../../../../../row-space.md) and [column space](../../../../../column-space.md) consist of all [linear combinations](../../../../../linear-combination.md) of the corresponding rows and columns; the [null space](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) is the [kernel of a linear map](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) represented by $A$. The [matrix rank](../../../../../matrix-rank.md) counts independent row or column directions, with row rank equal to column rank.
