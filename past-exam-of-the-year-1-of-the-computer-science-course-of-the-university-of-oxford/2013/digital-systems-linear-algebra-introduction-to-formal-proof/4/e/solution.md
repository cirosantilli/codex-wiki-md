The [elementary row operations](../../../../../elementary-row-operation.md) preserve the [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md), and the diagonal product of the [upper triangular matrix](../../../../../upper-triangular-matrix.md) found above is

$$
\det B=(1-t^2)^3.
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{B\text{ is not invertible exactly when }t=1\text{ or }t=-1.}
$$

At $t=1$, all four rows are equal. At $t=-1$, successive rows alternate between one row and its negative. In either case the [matrix rank](../../../../../matrix-rank.md) is one, confirming the vanishing [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md).
