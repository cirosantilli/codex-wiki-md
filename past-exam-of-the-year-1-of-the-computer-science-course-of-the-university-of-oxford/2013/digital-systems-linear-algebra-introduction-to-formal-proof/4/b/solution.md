<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For either an [upper triangular matrix](../../../../../upper-triangular-matrix.md) or a [lower triangular matrix](../../../../../lower-triangular-matrix.md),

$$
\boxed{\det T=\prod_{i=1}^nT_{ii}.}
$$

In the permutation formula for the [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md), every contribution except the diagonal product includes an entry on the zero side of the diagonal. Equivalently, successive cofactor expansions along the first column of an [upper triangular matrix](../../../../../upper-triangular-matrix.md) prove the formula by [mathematical induction](../../../../../mathematical-induction.md).
