<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For $i\ne j$, the [elementary row operation](../../../../../elementary-row-operation.md) $R_i\gets R_i-kR_j$ leaves the [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) unchanged:

$$
\boxed{\det(\ldots,R_i-kR_j,\ldots,R_j,\ldots)=\det A.}
$$

By multilinearity in row $i$, the left side is $\det A$ minus $k$ times a [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) with row $j$ appearing twice. That second [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) is zero by alternation. No row scaling or row interchange is involved.
