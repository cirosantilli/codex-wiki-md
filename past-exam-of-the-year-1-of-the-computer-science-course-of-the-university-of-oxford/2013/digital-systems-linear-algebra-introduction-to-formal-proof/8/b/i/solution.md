↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [equality introduction rule](../../../../../../equality-introduction-rule.md) is reflexivity: any well-formed [first-order term](../../../../../../first-order-term.md) is equal to itself, with no premises. The [equality elimination rule](../../../../../../equality-elimination-rule.md) permits replacement of equals in a [first-order formula](../../../../../../first-order-formula.md):

$$
\boxed{\frac{}{T=T}\;{=I},\qquad\frac{S=T\qquad\phi(S)}{\phi(T)}\;{=E}.}
$$

In the second rule, write $\phi(S)$ and $\phi(T)$ as the results of substituting $S$ and $T$ into the same designated free occurrences of a variable in $\phi$. Both substitutions must avoid variable capture. The rule preserves the surrounding assumptions. This expresses [first-order equality](../../../../../../logical-equality.md) rather than treating equality as an arbitrary relation symbol.
