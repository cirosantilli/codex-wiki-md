<h1 id="8/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Use reflexivity to obtain $T_1=T_1$. In [equality elimination rule](../../../../../../equality-elimination-rule.md), take $\phi(z)$ to be $z=T_1$, and replace the designated first occurrence of $T_1$ using the premise $T_1=T_2$:

<a id="8/b/ii/table-symmetry-from-reflexivity-and-replacement"></a>
| Line | Open assumptions | Formula | Justification |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 | $T_1=T_2$ | Premise |
| 2 | 1 | $T_1=T_1$ | [equality introduction rule](../../../../../../equality-introduction-rule.md) |
| 3 | 1 | $T_2=T_1$ | [equality elimination rule](../../../../../../equality-elimination-rule.md) 1, 2 |

Therefore

$$
\boxed{T_2=T_1.}
$$

The second occurrence of $T_1$ in $T_1=T_1$ is part of the fixed parameter in $\phi(z)$ and is not replaced.
