<h1 id="6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a square [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) $M$, an [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md)–[eigenvector](../../../../../eigenvector.md) pair is a [scalar](../../../../../scalar.md) $\lambda$ and a nonzero [vector](../../../../../vector.md) $v$ satisfying

$$
\boxed{Mv=\lambda v,\qquad v\ne0.}
$$

Thus the [linear map](../../../../../linear-map.md) represented by $M$ scales this particular direction by $\lambda$. The nonzero condition excludes the zero [vector](../../../../../vector.md), which would otherwise satisfy the displayed equation for every [scalar](../../../../../scalar.md).
