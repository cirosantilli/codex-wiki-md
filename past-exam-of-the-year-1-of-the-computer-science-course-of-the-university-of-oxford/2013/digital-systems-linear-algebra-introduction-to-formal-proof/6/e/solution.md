<h1 id="6/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The factor $(\lambda-1)$ proves that one is an [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md). To find its normalized [eigenvector](../../../../../eigenvector.md), solve $Pv=v$. The second and third rows give

$$
\frac14v_1+\frac12v_2=v_2,\qquad\frac14v_1+\frac12v_3=v_3,
$$

so $v_2=v_3=v_1/2$. The normalization $v_1+v_2+v_3=1$ now gives

$$
\boxed{v=(1/2,1/4,1/4)^{\mathsf T}.}
$$

Its entries are nonnegative and sum to one, and direct [matrix multiplication](../../../../../matrix-multiplication.md) verifies $Pv=v$. As a [probability vector](../../../../../probability-vector.md) it is the [stationary distribution](../../../../../stationary-distribution.md); as resource shares it assigns half to the USA and a quarter to each other region.
