<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

With column [vectors](../../../../../vector.md), entry $P_{ij}$ is the fraction moving from region $j$ to region $i$. The first column is therefore $(1/2,1/4,1/4)^{\mathsf T}$, the second is $(1/2,1/2,0)^{\mathsf T}$, and the third is $(1/2,0,1/2)^{\mathsf T}$. Hence

$$
\boxed{A=\frac14,\qquad B=0,\qquad C=\frac12,\qquad P=\begin{pmatrix}1/2&1/2&1/2\\1/4&1/2&0\\1/4&0&1/2\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

Each column sums to one, as required for this convention for a [stochastic matrix](../../../../../stochastic-matrix.md). Multiplying by $P$ conserves the total resources.
