# Functional programming, design and analysis of algorithms

↑ **Parent:** [2013](split.md)

These solutions follow the original examination of 11 June 2013, code `4208`.[pp. 2–7, Questions 1–8](../../functional-programming-design-and-analysis-of-algorithms-examination-paper-by-the-university-of-oxford-2013.md) Each question's [Haskell](../../haskell.md) definitions form a separate namespace; hide redefined [Standard Prelude](../../standard-prelude.md) names when compiling them. Unless stated otherwise, arguments are finite and total. [Time complexity](../../time-complexity.md) counts primitive operations; the cost of comparing variable-length words and the growing bit cost of exact [integer](../../integer-split.md) arithmetic are stated separately where relevant.[§§3.1 and 4.1; Chapter 9, Standard Prelude](../../haskell-2010-language-report.md)
