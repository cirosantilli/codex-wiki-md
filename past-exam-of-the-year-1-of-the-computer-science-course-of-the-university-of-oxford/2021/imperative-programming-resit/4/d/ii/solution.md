With that display format the output is
```
20
10
10
Queue status: [30, 40, 15]
```
The first removal takes the newly prepended left value $20$. The right removal later takes the most recently appended $10$. The next left removal takes the earlier $10$, leaving the ordered [sequence](../../../../../../sequence.md) **$\langle30,40,15\rangle$**. The two equal values are different occurrences, and the displayed order follows the links rather than numerical sorting.
