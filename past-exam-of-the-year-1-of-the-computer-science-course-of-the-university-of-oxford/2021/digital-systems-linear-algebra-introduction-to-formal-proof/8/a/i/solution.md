<h1 id="8/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Apply the [universal quantifier elimination rule](../../../../../../universal-quantifier-elimination-rule.md) to the universal premise with term $c$, obtaining $P(c)\leftrightarrow Q(c)$. The left [biconditional elimination rule](../../../../../../biconditional-elimination-rule.md) gives $P(c)\to Q(c)$. Use the premise $P(c)$ and the [modus ponens rule](../../../../../../implication-elimination-rule.md) to derive $Q(c)$. The [existential quantifier introduction rule](../../../../../../existential-quantifier-introduction-rule.md) with witness $c$ yields **$\exists x\,Q(x)$**. Substitution of a constant is capture-free here, so both quantifier side conditions hold.
