↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [diagonal matrix entry](../../../../../../diagonal-matrix-entry.md) is $D_{ii}=\sum_{k=1}^N B_{ik}C_{ki}$. Only $k=i$ can contribute, so **$D_{ii}=B_{ii}C_{ii}$**. This also shows that the product is a [diagonal matrix](../../../../../../diagonal-matrix.md), with its diagonal obtained by entrywise multiplication of the two diagonals.
