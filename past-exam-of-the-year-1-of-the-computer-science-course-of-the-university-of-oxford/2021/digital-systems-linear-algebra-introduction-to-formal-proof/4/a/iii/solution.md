<h1 id="4/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Repeatedly applying the previous product rule gives

$$
\boxed{(B^p)_{ij}=\begin{cases}(B_{ii})^p&i=j,\\0&i\ne j.\end{cases}}
$$

Formally, the result holds for $p=1$; multiplication by $B$ preserves the zero [off-diagonal matrix entries](../../../../../../off-diagonal-matrix-entry.md) and multiplies each [diagonal matrix entry](../../../../../../diagonal-matrix-entry.md) by $B_{ii}$, proving the induction step.
