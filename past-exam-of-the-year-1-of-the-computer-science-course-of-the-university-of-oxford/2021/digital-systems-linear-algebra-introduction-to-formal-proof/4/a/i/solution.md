<h1 id="4/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

By [matrix multiplication](../../../../../../matrix-multiplication.md), $D_{ij}=\sum_{k=1}^N B_{ik}C_{kj}$. A potentially nonzero term requires $k=i$ because $B$ is a [diagonal matrix](../../../../../../diagonal-matrix.md), and $k=j$ because $C$ is a [diagonal matrix](../../../../../../diagonal-matrix.md). For $i\ne j$ these requirements cannot both hold. Thus every summand is zero and **$D_{ij}=0$ whenever $i\ne j$**.
