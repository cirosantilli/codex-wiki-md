<h1 id="5/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The $j$th column of $AE$ is $A(Ee_j)$. The [permutation matrix](../../../../../../permutation-matrix.md) fixes $e_1,e_2$ and interchanges $e_3,e_4$, so these columns are $Ae_1,Ae_2,Ae_4,Ae_3$. They are exactly the first, second, fourth and third columns of $A$. Thus **right multiplication by $E$ interchanges columns three and four**, leaving the others unchanged.
