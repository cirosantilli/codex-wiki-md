↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The second [pivot](../../../../../../pivotal-quantity.md) is two. Row three has second entry two, so subtract row two; row four has second entry minus two, so add row two. Therefore **$d=-1$, $e=1$**. Their trailing row pairs become $(0,4)$ and $(3,5)$, respectively; the zero in the third [pivot](../../../../../../pivotal-quantity.md) position is why a later [row interchange](../../../../../../row-interchange.md) is needed.
