<h1 id="5/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Let $e_{ij}$ denote the [matrix unit](../../../../../../matrix-unit.md) with one at position $(i,j)$ and zero elsewhere. The five inverses are

$$
\boxed{E_1^{-1}=I+e_{21},\quad E_2^{-1}=I+2e_{31},\quad E_3^{-1}=I-e_{41},\quad E_4^{-1}=I+e_{32},\quad E_5^{-1}=I-e_{42}.}
$$

Thus each is the [identity matrix](../../../../../../identity-matrix.md) with only the indicated [off-diagonal matrix entry](../../../../../../off-diagonal-matrix-entry.md) altered. These inverses reverse the corresponding row additions; no row scaling or permutation is involved.
