<h1 id="5/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Left multiplication by an [elementary matrix](../../../../../../elementary-matrix.md) adds a multiple of row one to its designated target row. The first-column entries below the [pivot](../../../../../../pivotal-quantity.md) are $1,2,-1$, so **$a=-1$, $b=-2$, $c=1$**. The corresponding [row operations](../../../../../../elementary-row-operation.md) are $R_2\leftarrow R_2-R_1$, $R_3\leftarrow R_3-2R_1$, and $R_4\leftarrow R_4+R_1$. Since none changes row one, their sequential application gives the required three eliminated first-column entries and the displayed remaining entries.
