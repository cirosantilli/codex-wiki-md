<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The initial input levels do not initialize the stored [bits](../../../../../bit.md). **The negative Start pulse asynchronously clears both outputs to zero**, making the experiment start from a known state even without any [clock edge](../../../../../clock-edge.md). Once Start is released, the circuit can recognize a fresh sequence. It also clears the history retained by the [JK flip-flops](../../../../../jk-flip-flop.md) between trials; merely lowering the data inputs would not do so.
