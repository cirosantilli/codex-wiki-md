↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The reduced row echelon form gives $u_1=2u_4/21$, $u_2=-3u_4/7$, and $u_3=-u_4/7$. Taking $u_4=21t$ gives every [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) in the [null space](../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) as $t(2,-9,-3,21)^\top$. Thus a [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) for the kernel of the [linear map](../../../../../../linear-map.md) is

$$
\boxed{\left\{\begin{pmatrix}2\\-9\\-3\\21\end{pmatrix}\right\}.}
$$

Direct multiplication gives $A(2,-9,-3,21)^\top=0$. The [rank-nullity theorem](../../../../../../rank-nullity-theorem.md) also checks the count: [matrix rank](../../../../../../matrix-rank.md) three plus [nullity](../../../../../../nullity-of-a-linear-map.md) one equals the domain [dimension](../../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) four.
