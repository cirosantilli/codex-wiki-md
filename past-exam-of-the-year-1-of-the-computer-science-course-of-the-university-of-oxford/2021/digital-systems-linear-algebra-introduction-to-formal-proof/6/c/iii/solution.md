**No.** The nonzero [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) $v=(2,-9,-3,21)^\top$ is in the kernel of the [linear map](../../../../../../linear-map.md), so $T(v)=T(0)=0$ although $v\ne0$. A [linear map](../../../../../../linear-map.md) is [injective](../../../../../../injective-function.md) exactly when its kernel consists only of zero.
