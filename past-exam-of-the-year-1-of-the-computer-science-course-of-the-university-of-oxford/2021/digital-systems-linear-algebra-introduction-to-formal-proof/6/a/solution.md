<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Replacing row one by $R_1-R_2-R_3-R_4$ does not change the [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md): adding a multiple of another row preserves a [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md). It produces first row $(-2,0,0,0)$, whose [cofactor](../../../../../cofactor.md) minor is the three-dimensional [identity matrix](../../../../../identity-matrix.md). Thus **the [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) is $-2$**.

The columns are homogeneous coordinate [vectors](../../../../../vector.md) $(1,v_i)^\top$ of the four vertices. Subtracting the first column from each other column preserves the [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) and reduces its magnitude to the absolute [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) of the three edge [vectors](../../../../../vector.md) based at $v_1$. That absolute [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) is the [parallelepiped](../../../../../parallelepiped.md) [volume](../../../../../volume.md). A [tetrahedron](../../../../../tetrahedron.md) occupies one sixth of that [parallelepiped](../../../../../parallelepiped.md), so **$V=|-2|/3!=1/3$** in cubic coordinate units.
