<h1 id="6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A [unit vector](../../../../../unit-vector.md) along the line is $d=(3,4)^\top/5$. Reflection preserves the [orthogonal projection](../../../../../orthogonal-projection.md) $dd^\top u$ onto that line and negates its perpendicular component. Therefore $Au=dd^\top u-(u-dd^\top u)$ and

$$
\boxed{A=2dd^\top-I=\frac1{25}\begin{pmatrix}-7&24\\24&7\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

As checks, $A(3,4)^\top=(3,4)^\top$, $A(-4,3)^\top=-(-4,3)^\top$, and $A^2=I$. Its [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) is minus one, as expected for a planar [reflection matrix](../../../../../reflection-matrix.md).
