<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Compute the two complements with the two [NOT gates](../../../../../not-gate.md), then form $t_1=a\land\neg b$ and $t_2=\neg a\land b$ with the two [AND gates](../../../../../and-gate.md). Feed those outputs to the [OR gate](../../../../../or-gate.md):

$$
\boxed{a\oplus b=(a\land\neg b)\lor(\neg a\land b).}
$$

Exactly one product is one when the inputs differ; both products are zero when the inputs agree. This uses precisely the allowed two [inverters](../../../../../not-gate.md), two two-input [AND gates](../../../../../and-gate.md) and one two-input [OR gate](../../../../../or-gate.md).
