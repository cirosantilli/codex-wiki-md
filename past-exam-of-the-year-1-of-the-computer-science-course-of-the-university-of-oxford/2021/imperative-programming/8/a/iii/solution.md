<h1 id="8/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**[Class inheritance](../../../../../../class-inheritance.md) offers a compact specialized implementation**: the scaled sum is also a `SumChannel` and can be used where that concrete type is required, while its inherited state handling is reused directly.

**The decorator supports run-time [object composition](../../../../../../object-composition.md) over any `Channel` implementation**, including an already constructed stateful [software object](../../../../../../software-object.md), without editing or subclassing each concrete [software class](../../../../../../class-programming.md). Its extra [software object](../../../../../../software-object.md) and delegation are a small cost. The two approaches therefore serve different reuse needs; the wrapper need not pretend to be the concrete sum-channel [software class](../../../../../../class-programming.md).
