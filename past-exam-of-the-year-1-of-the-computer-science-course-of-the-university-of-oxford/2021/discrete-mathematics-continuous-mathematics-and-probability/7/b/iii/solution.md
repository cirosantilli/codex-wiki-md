<h1 id="7/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For $Z=2(X+Y)+1$, [independence](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) gives

$$
G_Z(s)=\mathbb E[s^{2(X+Y)+1}]
=sG_X(s^2)G_Y(s^2)
=\boxed{s\exp\bigl((\lambda+\mu)(s^2-1)\bigr)}.
$$

The initial factor $s$ accounts for the shift by one; substituting $s^2$ accounts for doubling the [random variable](../../../../../../random-variable-split.md). Its support consists of positive odd [integers](../../../../../../integer-split.md).
