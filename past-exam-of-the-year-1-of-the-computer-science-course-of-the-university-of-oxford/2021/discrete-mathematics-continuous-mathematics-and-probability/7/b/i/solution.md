<h1 id="7/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [independent random variables](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) have a product [joint probability mass function](../../../../../../joint-probability-mass-function.md). For [integers](../../../../../../integer-split.md) $r,t\geq0$,

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(X=r,Y=t)=e^{-(\lambda+\mu)}\frac{\lambda^r\mu^t}{r!\,t!}.}
$$

It is zero for all other pairs. The factors are the respective [Poisson distribution](../../../../../../poisson-distribution.md) masses, with the product justified by [independence](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md).
