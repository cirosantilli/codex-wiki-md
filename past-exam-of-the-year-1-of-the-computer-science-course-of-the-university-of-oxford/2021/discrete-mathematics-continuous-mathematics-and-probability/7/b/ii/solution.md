<h1 id="7/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

By the definition of a [probability generating function](../../../../../../probability-generating-function.md) and the power series for the [exponential function](../../../../../../exponential-function.md),

$$
G_X(s)=\mathbb E[s^X]
=e^{-\lambda}\sum_{r=0}^{\infty}\frac{(\lambda s)^r}{r!}
=\boxed{e^{\lambda(s-1)}}.
$$

The [series](../../../../../../series-mathematics.md) is absolutely convergent for every finite real $s$; in particular the expression is finite for all $s>1$, as needed for the later tail estimate.
