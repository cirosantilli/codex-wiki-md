↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [expected value](../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) of an [indicator random variable](../../../../../../indicator-random-variable.md) is the [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) of its [event](../../../../../../event.md). For a [simple symmetric random walk](../../../../../../simple-symmetric-random-walk.md) absorbed at $0,k$, the [gambler's ruin](../../../../../../gambler-s-ruin.md) success [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) from $i$ is $i/k$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E_i[J]=\frac ik.}
$$

The boundary values are $0$ at the left exit and $1$ at chamber $k$.
