<h1 id="8/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Every position in the random [permutation](../../../../../../permutation.md) has the same marginal colour [probability](../../../../../../probability.md), so **$\mathbb P(\text{second red})=m/(m+n)$**. One can verify this with the [law of total probability](../../../../../../law-of-total-probability.md), conditioning on the first card:

$$
\frac mN\frac{m-1}{N-1}+\frac nN\frac m{N-1}
=\frac{m(m-1+n)}{N(N-1)}=\frac mN.
$$

Here $N\geq2$ because both colour counts are positive.
