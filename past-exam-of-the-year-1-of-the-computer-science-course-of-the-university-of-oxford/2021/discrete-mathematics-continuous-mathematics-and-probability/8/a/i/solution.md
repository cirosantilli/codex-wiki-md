<h1 id="8/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $N=m+n$. In a uniformly shuffled deck, each labelled card is equally likely to occupy the first position, and exactly $m$ cards are red. Therefore **$\mathbb P(\text{first red})=m/(m+n)$**. Equivalently, the red positions are a uniformly chosen $m$-element [subset](../../../../../../subset.md) of the $N$ positions.
