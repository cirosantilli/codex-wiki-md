<h1 id="8/a/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

Conditional on a contiguous red block, its $n+1$ possible starting positions remain equally likely because their original colour patterns had equal [probabilities](../../../../../../probability.md). The first card is red exactly when that block starts at position $1$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(\text{first red}\mid\text{all reds together})=\frac1{n+1}.}
$$

This also follows by dividing the [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) of the single favourable block position, $1/\binom{m+n}{m}$, by the [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) of all $n+1$ block positions.
