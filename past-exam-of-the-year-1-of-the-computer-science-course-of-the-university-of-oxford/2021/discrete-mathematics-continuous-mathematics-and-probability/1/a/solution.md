<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Reduction of both coefficients in [modular arithmetic](../../../../../modular-arithmetic-split.md) gives

$$
754=8\cdot91+26,\qquad -507=-6\cdot91+39.
$$

More explicitly,

$$
754x+507=91(8x+6)+(26x-39).
$$

The first expression is divisible by $91$ exactly when the last parenthesis is. By the definition of a [integer congruence](../../../../../integer-congruence.md), **the two congruences are equivalent**.
