<h1 id="1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [Euclidean algorithm](../../../../../../euclidean-algorithm.md) gives

$$
91=3\cdot26+13,\qquad26=2\cdot13,
$$

so $\gcd(754,91)=\gcd(26,91)=13$. Since $13\mid39$, the criterion for a [linear congruence](../../../../../../linear-congruence.md) guarantees a solution. One can also exhibit it directly: $26\cdot5+91(-1)=39$, or in the original coefficients,

$$
754\cdot5+91(-47)=-507.
$$

Thus **$x=5$ is a solution**, with an explicit [Bézout coefficient](../../../../../../bezout-coefficient.md) certificate.
