<h1 id="2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [contrapositive](../../../../../../contrapositive.md) is that an [integer](../../../../../../integer-split.md) [perfect square](../../../../../../square-number.md) has residue $0$ or $1$ modulo $4$. Let $n=t^2$ for an [integer](../../../../../../integer-split.md) $t$. If $t=2u$ is an [even number](../../../../../../even-number.md), then $t^2=4u^2\equiv0\pmod4$. If $t=2u+1$ is an [odd number](../../../../../../odd-number.md), then $t^2=4u(u+1)+1\equiv1\pmod4$. These exhaust the possible parities of an [integer](../../../../../../integer-split.md). Thus a residue of $2$ or $3$ excludes being a [perfect square](../../../../../../square-number.md), as required by the [contrapositive](../../../../../../contrapositive.md).
