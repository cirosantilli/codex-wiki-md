<h1 id="2/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Take $u=(1,1)$ and $v=(1,2)$. Their first coordinates are equal, so both $uRv$ and $vRu$ hold, although $u\ne v$. Thus $R$ is not an [antisymmetric relation](../../../../../../antisymmetric-relation.md), and hence **it is not a partial order**. The failure arises because its comparisons ignore the second coordinate entirely.
