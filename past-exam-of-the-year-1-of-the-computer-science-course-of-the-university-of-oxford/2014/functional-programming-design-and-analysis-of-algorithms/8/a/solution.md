↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a finite [connected graph](../../../../../connected-graph.md) $G=(V,E)$ with undirected [edges](../../../../../edge-of-a-graph.md) and [edge weights](../../../../../edge-weight.md) $w$, a [spanning tree](../../../../../spanning-tree.md) is a connected, acyclic subgraph containing all [vertices](../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md). It has $|V|-1$ [edges](../../../../../edge-of-a-graph.md). A [minimum spanning tree](../../../../../minimum-spanning-tree.md) is one minimizing the sum of its [edge weights](../../../../../edge-weight.md):

$$
\boxed{T\in\operatorname*{arg\,min}_{\substack{F\subseteq E\\(V,F)\text{ is a tree}}}\sum_{e\in F}w(e).}
$$

It minimizes total weight, rather than the distance of every route from a chosen root. Equal [edge weights](../../../../../edge-weight.md) may permit more than one [minimum spanning tree](../../../../../minimum-spanning-tree.md); negative weights do not invalidate the definition.
