<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

After division by $x>0$, the equation is linear with coefficient $-1/x$ on $y$. Its [integrating factor](../../../../../integrating-factor.md) is $1/x$, and the [product rule](../../../../../product-rule.md) gives

$$
\left(\frac yx\right)'=\frac{xy'-y}{x^2}=e^x\sin x.
$$

Using the [antiderivative](../../../../../antiderivative.md) from the preceding part,

$$
\boxed{y(x)=Cx+\frac{xe^x}{2}(\sin x-\cos x),\qquad x>0.}
$$

The arbitrary constant multiplies the homogeneous solution $x$.
