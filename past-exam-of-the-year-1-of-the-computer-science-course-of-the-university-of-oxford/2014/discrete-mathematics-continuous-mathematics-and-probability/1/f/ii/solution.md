<h1 id="1/f/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Set $y=f(x)$ and cube the equation. Solving for $x^3$ gives

$$
y^3=\frac{x^3}{1+x^3},\qquad
x^3=\frac{y^3}{1-y^3}.
$$

Consequently the [inverse function](../../../../../../inverse-function.md) is

$$
\boxed{f^{-1}(y)=\frac{y}{\sqrt[3]{1-y^3}},\qquad y\ne1.}
$$

The value $y=1$ is impossible in the original equation. Conversely, every real $y\ne1$ gives exactly one real $x$ by the displayed [cube root](../../../../../../cube-root.md), and this $x$ cannot equal $-1$. Direct substitution verifies both inverse identities. Thus the corrected [bijection](../../../../../../bijection.md) is $f:\mathbb R\setminus\{-1\}\to\mathbb R\setminus\{1\}$.
