**Function composition is not always commutative.** The reversed [function composition](../../../../../function-composition.md) may be undefined when the necessary [domain of a function](../../../../../domain-of-a-function.md) and [codomain](../../../../../codomain.md) do not match. A counterexample using [functions](../../../../../function-split.md) $\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ avoids that issue: let $f(x)=x+1$ and $g(x)=2x$. Then

$$
(g\circ f)(x)=2x+2,\qquad(f\circ g)(x)=2x+1.
$$

Both [function compositions](../../../../../function-composition.md) exist, and their values differ at every real $x$.
