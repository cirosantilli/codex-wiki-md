<h1 id="1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

For every $x\in\mathbb R$, the definition of [function composition](../../../../../function-composition.md) gives

$$
\bigl(h\circ(g\circ f)\bigr)(x)
=h\bigl((g\circ f)(x)\bigr)
=h(g(f(x)))
=\bigl((h\circ g)\circ f\bigr)(x).
$$

The [functions](../../../../../function-split.md) agree at every argument, so [function extensionality](../../../../../function-extensionality.md) proves the [associative property](../../../../../associative-property.md) of [function composition](../../../../../function-composition.md).
