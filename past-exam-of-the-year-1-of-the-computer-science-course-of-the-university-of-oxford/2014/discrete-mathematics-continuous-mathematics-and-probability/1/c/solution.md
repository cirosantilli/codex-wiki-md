<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Apply the two identities already proved with $A=\mathcal U$. Every $B_i$ is a [subset](../../../../../subset.md) of the common universe, and a [complement](../../../../../complement-of-a-set.md) is the [set difference](../../../../../set-difference.md) from that universe. Therefore

$$
\mathcal U\setminus\bigcup_{i=1}^nB_i
=\bigcap_{i=1}^n(\mathcal U\setminus B_i),\qquad
\mathcal U\setminus\bigcap_{i=1}^nB_i
=\bigcup_{i=1}^n(\mathcal U\setminus B_i).
$$

Replacing each [set difference](../../../../../set-difference.md) from $\mathcal U$ by its [complement](../../../../../complement-of-a-set.md) notation gives the claimed finite forms of [De Morgan's laws](../../../../../de-morgan-s-laws.md).
