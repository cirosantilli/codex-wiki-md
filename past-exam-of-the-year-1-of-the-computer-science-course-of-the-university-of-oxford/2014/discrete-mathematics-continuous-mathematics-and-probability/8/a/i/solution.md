<h1 id="8/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Put $q=1-p$. Summing the [geometric series](../../../../../../geometric-series.md) in the definition of the [probability generating function](../../../../../../probability-generating-function.md) gives

$$
\boxed{G_X(s)=\mathbb E[s^X]
=p\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}(qs)^k
=\frac{p}{1-qs},\qquad|s|<\frac1q.}
$$

This is the zero-based [geometric distribution](../../../../../../geometric-distribution.md), counting failures before the first success.
