<h1 id="8/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Differentiate the [probability generating function](../../../../../../probability-generating-function.md) to obtain

$$
G_X'(1)=\frac qp,\qquad G_X''(1)=\frac{2q^2}{p^2}.
$$

The first derivative is the [expected value](../../../../../../expected-value-split.md), and the second gives the [factorial moment](../../../../../../factorial-moment.md) $\mathbb E[X(X-1)]$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E X=\frac{1-p}{p},\qquad
\operatorname{Var}(X)=G_X''(1)+G_X'(1)-G_X'(1)^2
=\frac{1-p}{p^2}.}
$$

In particular, $\mathbb E[X^2]=q(2-p)/p^2$.
