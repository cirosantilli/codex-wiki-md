<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Partition the [power set](../../../../../power-set.md) into disjoint classes according to [subset](../../../../../subset.md) size. The class of size-$i$ [subsets](../../../../../subset.md) has [cardinality](../../../../../cardinality.md) $\binom ni$, while the whole [power set](../../../../../power-set.md) has [cardinality](../../../../../cardinality.md) $2^n$. Counting every [subset](../../../../../subset.md) once gives

$$
\boxed{\sum_{i=0}^n\binom ni=2^n.}
$$

This is also the [binomial theorem](../../../../../binomial-theorem.md) evaluated at $1+1$, but the counting proof follows directly from the earlier parts.
