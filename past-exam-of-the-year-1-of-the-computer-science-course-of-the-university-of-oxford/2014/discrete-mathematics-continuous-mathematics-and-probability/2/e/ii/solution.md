<h1 id="2/e/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For fixed $n\geq0$, use [mathematical induction](../../../../../../mathematical-induction.md) on $k$. At $k=0$, both sides equal one. If the [hockey-stick identity](../../../../../../hockey-stick-identity.md) holds at $k$, then [Pascal's identity](../../../../../../pascal-s-rule.md) gives

$$
\sum_{i=0}^{k+1}\binom{n+i}i
=\binom{n+k+1}k+\binom{n+k+1}{k+1}
=\binom{n+k+2}{k+1}.
$$

This is the required formula with $k$ replaced by $k+1$.
