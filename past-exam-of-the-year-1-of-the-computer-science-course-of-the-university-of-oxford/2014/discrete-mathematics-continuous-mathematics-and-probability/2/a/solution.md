↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

List the $n$ elements of the [finite set](../../../../../finite-set.md) $A$. A [subset](../../../../../subset.md) is determined by $n$ membership decisions, each with two possibilities. This is a [bijection](../../../../../bijection.md) from the [power set](../../../../../power-set.md) to the [set](../../../../../set-split.md) $\{0,1\}^n$, so

$$
\boxed{|\mathcal P(A)|=2^n.}
$$

The empty [set](../../../../../set-split.md) case is included: its [power set](../../../../../power-set.md) contains the single empty [subset](../../../../../subset.md).
