The requested [partial derivatives](../../../../../../partial-derivative.md) are

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
f_x&=3x^2+2x+2\alpha y+2\alpha,&
f_y&=2\alpha x+2y+2,\\
f_{xx}&=6x+2,&
f_{xy}&=2\alpha,&
f_{yy}&=2.
\end{aligned}}
$$

The last printed derivative has a misplaced superscript on $f$; the second-derivative list and subsequent [Taylor expansion](../../../../../../taylor-expansion.md) use $f_{yy}$.
