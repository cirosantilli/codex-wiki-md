↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The relation is [reflexive](../../../../../reflexive-relation.md) because $a-a=2\cdot0$. It is [symmetric](../../../../../symmetric-relation.md) because $a-b=2k$ implies $b-a=2(-k)$. It is [transitive](../../../../../transitive-relation.md) because $a-b=2k$ and $b-c=2\ell$ imply $a-c=2(k+\ell)$. It is therefore an [equivalence relation](../../../../../equivalence-relation.md), namely [congruence modulo an integer](../../../../../modular-congruence.md) for modulus two. Its two [equivalence classes](../../../../../equivalence-class.md) consist of the [even integers](../../../../../even-number.md) and the [odd integers](../../../../../odd-integer.md).
