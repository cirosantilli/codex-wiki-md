↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The notation means that $n$ divides $a_1-a_2$:

$$
\boxed{a_1\equiv a_2\pmod n\ \Longleftrightarrow\
\text{there exists }k\in\mathbb Z\text{ with }a_1-a_2=kn.}
$$

This is [congruence modulo an integer](../../../../../../modular-congruence.md); equivalently, the two [integers](../../../../../../integer-split.md) have the same remainder on division by the positive [integer](../../../../../../integer-split.md) $n$.
