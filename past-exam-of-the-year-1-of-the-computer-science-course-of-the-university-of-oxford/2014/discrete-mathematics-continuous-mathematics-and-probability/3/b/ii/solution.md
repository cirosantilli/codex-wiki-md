<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Write $a_1-a_2=kn$ with $k\in\mathbb Z$. The [difference of two squares](../../../../../../difference-of-two-squares.md) factors as

$$
a_1^2-a_2^2=(a_1-a_2)(a_1+a_2)=n\,k(a_1+a_2).
$$

The final factor is an [integer](../../../../../../integer-split.md), so $n$ divides the difference. By [congruence modulo an integer](../../../../../../modular-congruence.md), $\boxed{a_1^2\equiv a_2^2\pmod n}$.
