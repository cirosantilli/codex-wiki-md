<h1 id="3/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $n>0$,

$$
\frac{\sqrt{n^6+n^2}}{n^3}=\sqrt{1+n^{-4}}\longrightarrow1.
$$

Consequently $\boxed{\sqrt{n^6+n^2}=\Theta(n^3),\quad\text{in particular }O(n^3)}$ in [Big theta notation](../../../../../../big-theta-notation.md) and [Big O notation](../../../../../../big-o-notation.md).
