Here $a,b\geq2$ are fixed [integers](../../../../../../integer-split.md). The term $ab^n$ has [exponential growth](../../../../../../exponential-growth.md), whereas $an^b$ has fixed-degree [polynomial growth](../../../../../../polynomial-growth.md) and $nb^a$ is linear. Since a fixed-base exponential eventually dominates every fixed-degree polynomial,

$$
\frac{an^b+ab^n+nb^a}{b^n}\longrightarrow a.
$$

Hence the requested [Big O notation](../../../../../../big-o-notation.md) bound, with its matching lower order, is $\boxed{\Theta(b^n),\quad\text{in particular }O(b^n)}$.
