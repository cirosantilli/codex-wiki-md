<h1 id="3/c/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

For sufficiently large positive $n$, the [logarithm](../../../../../../logarithm.md) identities give

$$
n\log_2(5n)+\log_2(n^2)+4n
=n\log_2n+(\log_25+4)n+2\log_2n.
$$

After division by $n\log_2n$, the right-hand side tends to one. Thus $\boxed{\Theta(n\log n),\quad\text{in particular }O(n\log n)}$ gives the asymptotic order in [Big theta notation](../../../../../../big-theta-notation.md) and [Big O notation](../../../../../../big-o-notation.md).
