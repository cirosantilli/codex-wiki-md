<h1 id="7/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $(B_i)$ be a finite or countable [partition of a sample space](../../../../../../partition-of-a-sample-space.md) into [events](../../../../../../event.md) of positive [probability](../../../../../../probability.md). The [events](../../../../../../event.md) $A\cap B_i$ are disjoint and their [union](../../../../../../set-union.md) is $A$. [Countable additivity](../../../../../../countable-additivity.md) and the definition of [conditional probability](../../../../../../conditional-probability.md) therefore give the [law of total probability](../../../../../../law-of-total-probability.md)

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(A)=\sum_i\mathbb P(A\cap B_i)
=\sum_i\mathbb P(A\mid B_i)\mathbb P(B_i).}
$$

Cells of zero [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) contribute zero through $\mathbb P(A\cap B_i)$ and can be omitted rather than assigning an undefined [conditional probability](../../../../../../conditional-probability.md).
