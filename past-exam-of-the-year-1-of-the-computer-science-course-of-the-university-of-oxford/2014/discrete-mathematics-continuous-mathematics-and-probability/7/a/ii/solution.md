↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The two definitions of [conditional probability](../../../../../../conditional-probability.md) give

$$
\mathbb P(B\mid A)=\frac{\mathbb P(A\cap B)}{\mathbb P(A)},
\qquad
\mathbb P(A\mid B)\mathbb P(B)=\mathbb P(A\cap B).
$$

Substitution proves [Bayes' theorem](../../../../../../bayes-theorem.md):

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(B\mid A)=\frac{\mathbb P(A\mid B)\mathbb P(B)}{\mathbb P(A)}.}
$$

The positive [probabilities](../../../../../../probability.md) assumed in the question make both conditional expressions defined.
