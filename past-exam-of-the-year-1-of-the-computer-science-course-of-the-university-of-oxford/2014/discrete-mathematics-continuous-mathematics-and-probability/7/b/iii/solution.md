<h1 id="7/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Given the coin, the next toss is independent of its earlier tosses and has that coin's head [probability](../../../../../../probability.md). Averaging using the [posterior distribution](../../../../../../bayesian-posterior.md) gives the [posterior predictive probability](../../../../../../posterior-predictive-probability.md)

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(\text{next head}\mid H)
=\frac4{29}\frac13+\frac9{29}\frac12+\frac{16}{29}\frac23
=\frac{33}{58}.}
$$

Observing two heads raises the [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) of having chosen a high-bias coin.
