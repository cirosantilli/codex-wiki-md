↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $C_j$ identify the selected coin and let $H$ be the event of two heads. The prior coin [probabilities](../../../../../../probability.md) are equal, and the [conditional probabilities](../../../../../../conditional-probability.md) of $H$ are $1/9,1/4,4/9$. By [Bayes' theorem](../../../../../../bayes-theorem.md),

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(C_1\mid H)=
\frac{(1/3)(1/9)}{(1/3)(1/9+1/4+4/9)}
=\frac4{29}.}
$$

The full [posterior distribution](../../../../../../bayesian-posterior.md) over the three biases is $(4,9,16)/29$.
