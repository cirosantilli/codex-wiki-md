<h1 id="5/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The update in [Newton's method](../../../../../../newton-s-method-in-optimization.md) is exactly $x_n=g(x_{n-1})$. Positivity is preserved, and the [arithmetic-geometric mean inequality](../../../../../../arithmetic-geometric-mean-inequality.md) from part (a) applies to every update. Consequently

$$
\boxed{x_n\geq\sqrt a\quad\text{for every }n\geq1.}
$$

This holds even when $0<x_0<\sqrt a$: the first update moves to or above the positive [square root](../../../../../../square-root.md).
