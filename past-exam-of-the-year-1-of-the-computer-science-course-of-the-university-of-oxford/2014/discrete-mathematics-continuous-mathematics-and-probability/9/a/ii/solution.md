Integrating the [probability density function](../../../../../../probability-density-function.md) from the lower endpoint gives the [cumulative distribution function](../../../../../../cumulative-distribution-function.md)

$$
\boxed{F_X(x)=\begin{cases}
0,&x\leq1,\\
1-x^{-c},&x>1.
\end{cases}}
$$

The [cumulative distribution function](../../../../../../cumulative-distribution-function.md) is continuous at $x=1$ and tends to one at infinity.
