↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The [logarithm](../../../../../../logarithm.md) is strictly increasing, so $\mathbb P(Y\leq y)=\mathbb P(X\leq e^y)$. Since $X>1$ almost surely,

$$
\boxed{F_Y(y)=\begin{cases}0,&y\leq0,\\1-e^{-cy},&y>0,\end{cases}
\qquad
f_Y(y)=\begin{cases}ce^{-cy},&y>0,\\0,&y\leq0.\end{cases}}
$$

Thus the [logarithm of a Pareto random variable](../../../../../../logarithm-of-a-pareto-random-variable.md) has an [exponential distribution](../../../../../../exponential-distribution.md) with rate $c$. Its [probability density function](../../../../../../probability-density-function.md) value at the single endpoint can be chosen arbitrarily without changing the law.
