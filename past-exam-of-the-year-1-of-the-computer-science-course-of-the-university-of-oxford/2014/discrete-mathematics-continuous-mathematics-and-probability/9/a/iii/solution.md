<h1 id="9/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For $c>1$, the [expected value](../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) is the convergent improper [integral](../../../../../../integral-split.md)

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E X=\int_1^\infty x\frac{c}{x^{c+1}}\,dx
=c\int_1^\infty x^{-c}\,dx=\frac{c}{c-1}.}
$$

For completeness, when $0<c\leq1$ the same positive [integral](../../../../../../integral-split.md) diverges and the [expected value](../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) is infinite.
