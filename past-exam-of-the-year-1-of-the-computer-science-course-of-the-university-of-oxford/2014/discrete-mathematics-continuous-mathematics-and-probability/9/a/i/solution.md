<h1 id="9/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [probability density function](../../../../../../probability-density-function.md) must be nonnegative and integrate to one. For $c>0$,

$$
\int_1^\infty c x^{-c-1}\,dx
=\left[-x^{-c}\right]_1^\infty=1.
$$

At $c=0$ the function is identically zero, while $c<0$ makes it negative on $x>1$. Thus $\boxed{c>0}$ is necessary and sufficient. The resulting law is a [Pareto distribution](../../../../../../pareto-distribution.md) with scale one and shape $c$.
