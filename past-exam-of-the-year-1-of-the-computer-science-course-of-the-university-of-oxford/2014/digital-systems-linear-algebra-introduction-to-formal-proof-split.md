# Digital systems, linear algebra, introduction to formal proof

↑ **Parent:** [2014](split.md)

The original cover dates this paper to 20 June 2014. The solutions follow the printed numbering and use the supplied [natural deduction](../../natural-deduction.md) rules, including [double-negation elimination](../../double-negation-elimination.md). In the proof tables, the context column lists the numbered assumptions still in force; temporary assumptions are explicitly discharged by the indicated rule.[Cover and Figures 1–3, printed pages 1 and 9](../../digital-systems-linear-algebra-introduction-to-formal-proof-examination-paper-by-the-university-of-oxford-2014.md)
