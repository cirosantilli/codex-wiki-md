↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

An [inode](../../../../../inode.md) holds [file metadata](../../../../../file-metadata.md): object type, owner and group identities, access permissions, [file](../../../../../computer-file.md) size, access/modification/status-change timestamps, and the [hard link](../../../../../hard-link.md) count. It also holds the information locating the contents, traditionally direct addresses and [indirect block pointers](../../../../../indirect-block-pointer.md). Device-special [files](../../../../../computer-file.md) instead need device-identifying information. The [file](../../../../../computer-file.md)'s name and parent pathname belong to [directory entries](../../../../../directory-entry.md), not to the ordinary [inode](../../../../../inode.md).

$$
\boxed{\text{inode} = \text{metadata and content-location information}.}
$$
