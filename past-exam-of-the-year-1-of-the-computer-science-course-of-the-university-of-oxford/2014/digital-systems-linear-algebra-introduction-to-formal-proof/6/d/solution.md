<h1 id="6/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

A [triangular matrix](../../../../../triangular-matrix.md) has [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) equal to the product of its diagonal entries. Since $L$ is a [unit lower triangular matrix](../../../../../unit-lower-triangular-matrix.md), $\det L=1$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\det A=\det L\det U=1\cdot(1\cdot4\cdot(-1))=-4.}
$$

Equivalently, the elimination used only row additions, which leave the [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) unchanged; there were no row swaps or row scalings to account for.
