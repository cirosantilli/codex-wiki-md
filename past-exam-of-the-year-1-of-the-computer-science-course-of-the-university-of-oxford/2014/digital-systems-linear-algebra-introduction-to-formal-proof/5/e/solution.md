<h1 id="5/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The right-hand [vector](../../../../../vector.md) is in the [column space](../../../../../column-space.md), because

$$
2\begin{pmatrix}-1\\1\\2\end{pmatrix}+\begin{pmatrix}3\\2\\5\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}1\\4\\9\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Consequently the [linear system](../../../../../system-of-linear-equations.md) is consistent. A concrete solution is

$$
\boxed{x=(2,0,1,0,0)^{\mathsf T}.}
$$

As a compatibility check, the original rows satisfy $r_1+11r_2-5r_3=0$, and the right-hand entries satisfy the same relation $1+11\cdot4-5\cdot9=0$. There are infinitely many solutions, obtained by adding any [vector](../../../../../vector.md) in the [null space](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) to the displayed solution.
