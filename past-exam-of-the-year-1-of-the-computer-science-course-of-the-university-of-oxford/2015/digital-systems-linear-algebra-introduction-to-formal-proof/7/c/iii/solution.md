<h1 id="7/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

In [proof by contradiction](../../../../../../reductio-ad-absurdum.md), assume the [negation](../../../../../../negation.md) of the desired conclusion and derive [logical falsity](../../../../../../logical-falsity.md); discharge that assumption and infer the desired conclusion. Its [sequent](../../../../../../sequent.md) rule is

$$
\boxed{\frac{\Gamma,\neg\phi\vdash\bot}{\Gamma\vdash\phi}.}
$$

This is a rule of [classical propositional logic](../../../../../../classical-propositional-logic.md). It follows from the paper's [negation introduction rule](../../../../../../negation-introduction-rule.md), which first yields $\neg\neg\phi$, and [double-negation elimination](../../../../../../double-negation-elimination.md), which yields $\phi$. This is stronger than merely deriving $\neg\phi$ by showing that $\phi$ leads to [logical falsity](../../../../../../logical-falsity.md).
