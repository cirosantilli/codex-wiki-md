↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A common-context [sequent](../../../../../../sequent.md) version of the [disjunction elimination rule](../../../../../../disjunction-elimination-rule.md) is

$$
\boxed{\frac{\Gamma\vdash\phi\lor\psi\qquad\Gamma,\phi\vdash\kappa\qquad\Gamma,\psi\vdash\kappa}{\Gamma\vdash\kappa}.}
$$

The second and third premises record exactly the two boxed [subproofs](../../../../../../subproof.md) of the [natural deduction](../../../../../../natural-deduction.md) presentation. Their additional case assumptions do not occur in the conclusion: that is [assumption discharge](../../../../../../assumption-discharge.md). The surrounding context $\Gamma$ remains throughout. A formulation with different surrounding contexts is equivalent after retaining their union by [weakening](../../../../../../weakening.md).
