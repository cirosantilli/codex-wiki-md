<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For an $m\times n$ [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) $A$ with real entries, its [row space](../../../../../row-space.md) is the [linear span](../../../../../linear-span.md) of its rows, a [linear subspace](../../../../../vector-subspace.md) of $\mathbb R^n$. Its [column space](../../../../../column-space.md) is the [linear span](../../../../../linear-span.md) of its columns, a [linear subspace](../../../../../vector-subspace.md) of $\mathbb R^m$ and the [image of a linear map](../../../../../image-of-a-linear-map.md) $x\mapsto Ax$. Its [null space](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) is

$$
\ker A=\{x\in\mathbb R^n:Ax=0\},
$$

a [linear subspace](../../../../../vector-subspace.md) of $\mathbb R^n$. Finally,

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{rank}A=\dim\operatorname{Col}A=\dim\operatorname{Row}A.}
$$

Thus the [rank of a matrix](../../../../../matrix-rank.md) counts the number of [linearly independent](../../../../../linear-independence.md) columns, equivalently rows. The [nullity](../../../../../nullity-of-a-linear-map.md) is $\dim\ker A=n-\operatorname{rank}A$ by the [rank-nullity theorem](../../../../../rank-nullity-theorem.md).
