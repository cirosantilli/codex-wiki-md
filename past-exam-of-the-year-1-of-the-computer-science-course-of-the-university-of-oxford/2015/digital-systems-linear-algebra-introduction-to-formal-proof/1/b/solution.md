<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a square [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) $M$, an [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md)–[eigenvector](../../../../../eigenvector.md) pair consists of a [scalar](../../../../../scalar.md) $\lambda$ and a **nonzero** [vector](../../../../../vector.md) $v$ satisfying

$$
\boxed{Mv=\lambda v,\qquad v\ne0.}
$$

The [linear map](../../../../../linear-map.md) represented by $M$ therefore scales $v$ by $\lambda$. The exclusion of the zero [vector](../../../../../vector.md) matters: otherwise every [scalar](../../../../../scalar.md) would satisfy the equation trivially.
