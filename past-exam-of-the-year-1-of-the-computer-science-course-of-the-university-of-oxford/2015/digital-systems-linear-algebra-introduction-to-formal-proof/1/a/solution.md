↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [transition matrix](../../../../../stochastic-matrix.md) acts on a column [probability vector](../../../../../probability-vector.md) in the order Central, Headington, Botley. Thus column $j$ records the destinations of buses starting at station $j$. The Central-to-Headington, Headington-to-Botley and Botley-to-Central entries give

$$
\boxed{A=B=C=0.1,\qquad P=\begin{pmatrix}0.6&0.1&0.1\\0.1&0.8&0.2\\0.3&0.1&0.7\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

Each column sums to one, as required for this column convention for a [stochastic matrix](../../../../../stochastic-matrix.md).
