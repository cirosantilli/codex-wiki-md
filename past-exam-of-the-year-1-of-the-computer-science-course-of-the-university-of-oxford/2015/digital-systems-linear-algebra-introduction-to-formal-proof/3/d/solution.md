↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [elementary row operations](../../../../../elementary-row-operation.md) used above add multiples of other rows, so preserve the [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md). The [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) of an [upper triangular matrix](../../../../../upper-triangular-matrix.md) is the product of its diagonal entries. Hence

$$
\boxed{\det A=\det U=1\cdot2\cdot3=6.}
$$

Equivalently, $\det L=1$ and [multiplicativity of the determinant](../../../../../multiplicativity-of-the-determinant.md) gives $\det A=\det L\det U=6$.
