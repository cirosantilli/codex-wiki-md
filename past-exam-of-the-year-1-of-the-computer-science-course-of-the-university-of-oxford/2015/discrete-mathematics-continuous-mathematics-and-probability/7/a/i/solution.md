A [partition of a sample space](../../../../../../partition-of-a-sample-space.md) is a finite or countable family of nonempty [events](../../../../../../event.md) such that

$$
\boxed{B_i\cap B_j=\varnothing\quad(i\ne j),\qquad
\bigcup_iB_i=\Omega.}
$$

Thus exactly one event in the family occurs in each outcome. To use ordinary event-conditioned [conditional expectations](../../../../../../conditional-expectation.md), one usually assumes $\mathbb P(B_i)>0$; any zero-[probability](../../../../../../probability.md) blocks can instead be omitted from the expectation formula.
