<h1 id="9/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Assume a finite second [moment](../../../../../../moment.md), so the following [expectations](../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) are finite. Expand the square and use [linearity of expectation](../../../../../../linearity-of-expectation.md):

$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{var}(X)
&=\mathbb E[X^2-2\mu X+\mu^2]\\
&=\mathbb E[X^2]-2\mu\mathbb E[X]+\mu^2
=\mathbb E[X^2]-\mu^2.
\end{aligned}
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{var}(X)=\mathbb E[X^2]-(\mathbb E[X])^2.}
$$

The same equality holds in the extended sense when the [mean](../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) is finite and the second [moment](../../../../../../moment.md) is infinite.
