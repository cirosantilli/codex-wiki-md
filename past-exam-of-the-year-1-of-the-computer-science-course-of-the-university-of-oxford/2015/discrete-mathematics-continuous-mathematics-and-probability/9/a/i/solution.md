↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a [continuous random variable](../../../../../../continuous-random-variable.md) with [probability density function](../../../../../../probability-density-function.md) $f_X$, its [mean](../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mu=\mathbb E[X]=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}x f_X(x)\,dx,}
$$

provided $\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}|x|f_X(x)\,dx<\infty$. Its [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md) is the [expectation](../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) of the squared deviation from its [mean](../../../../../../expected-value-split.md):

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{var}(X)=\mathbb E[(X-\mu)^2]
=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}(x-\mu)^2f_X(x)\,dx.}
$$

This is finite when $\mathbb E[X^2]<\infty$; otherwise it may be $+\infty$ if the finite [mean](../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) exists.
