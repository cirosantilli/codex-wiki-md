↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [normalization of a probability distribution](../../../../../../normalization-of-a-probability-distribution.md) requires

$$
1=\int_0^b ax\,dx=\frac{ab^2}{2},
\qquad a=\frac2{b^2}.
$$

The prescribed [mean](../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) then gives

$$
1=\mathbb E[X]=\int_0^b x\cdot ax\,dx
=\frac{ab^3}{3}=\frac{2b}{3}.
$$

As $a,b>0$, these equations have the unique solution

$$
\boxed{b=\frac32,\qquad a=\frac89.}
$$
