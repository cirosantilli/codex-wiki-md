<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Assume the [homogeneous function](../../../../../homogeneous-function.md) is [differentiable](../../../../../differentiable-function.md). Applying the [multivariable chain rule](../../../../../multivariable-chain-rule.md) to $f(tx,ty)=t^mf(x,y)$ gives, for $t$ near $1$,

$$
x f_x(tx,ty)+y f_y(tx,ty)=mt^{m-1}f(x,y).
$$

Setting $t=1$ proves [Euler theorem for homogeneous functions](../../../../../euler-theorem-for-homogeneous-functions.md):

$$
\boxed{x\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}+y\frac{\partial f}{\partial y}=mf.}
$$

Only positive $t$ near $1$ are needed, so the derivation remains valid even when a noninteger $m$ makes the printed phrase “all real $t$” require qualification.
