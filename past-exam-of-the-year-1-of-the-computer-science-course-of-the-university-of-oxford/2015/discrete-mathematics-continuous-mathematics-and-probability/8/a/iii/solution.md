<h1 id="8/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Since $X^2=X(X-1)+X$, the [probability generating function](../../../../../../probability-generating-function.md) identities give

$$
\mathbb E[X^2]=G''(1)+G'(1).
$$

Subtracting the squared [mean](../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) yields the [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md)

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{var}(X)=G''(1)+G'(1)-\bigl(G'(1)\bigr)^2.}
$$
