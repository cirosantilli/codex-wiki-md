<h1 id="8/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Each of the $n$ distinguishable elements has $x$ independent choices of colour. By the [multiplication principle](../../../../../../rule-of-product.md), the number of [functions](../../../../../../function-split.md) from the $n$-element [set](../../../../../../set-split.md) to the $x$-element colour [set](../../../../../../set-split.md) is

$$
\boxed{x^n.}
$$
