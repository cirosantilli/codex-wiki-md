<h1 id="2/e/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [linearity](../../../../../../linearity.md) of the [Fibonacci recurrence](../../../../../../fibonacci-recurrence.md) allows a [linear combination](../../../../../../linear-combination.md)

$$
F_n=A\phi^n+B\psi^n.
$$

The [initial conditions](../../../../../../initial-condition.md) give $A+B=0$ and $A\phi+B\psi=1$. Since $\phi-\psi=\sqrt5$, we obtain $A=1/\sqrt5$ and $B=-1/\sqrt5$. Thus [Binet's formula](../../../../../../binet-formula.md) is

$$
\boxed{F_n=\frac{\phi^n-\psi^n}{\sqrt5}
=\frac1{\sqrt5}\left[\left(\frac{1+\sqrt5}{2}\right)^n-
\left(\frac{1-\sqrt5}{2}\right)^n\right].}
$$
