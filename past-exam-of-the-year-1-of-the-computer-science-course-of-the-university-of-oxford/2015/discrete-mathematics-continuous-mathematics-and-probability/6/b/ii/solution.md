<h1 id="6/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For the spatial mode with [eigenvalue](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $\kappa_n=(n\pi/L)^2$, the temporal [ordinary differential equation](../../../../../../ordinary-differential-equation.md) is

$$
\boxed{T_n'+D\left(\frac{n\pi}{L}\right)^2T_n=0.}
$$

Its [general solution](../../../../../../general-solution.md) is

$$
\boxed{T_n(t)=C_n\exp\left(-\frac{Dn^2\pi^2}{L^2}t\right),\qquad C_n\in\mathbb R.}
$$

The printed reference to “each solution $X(x)$ ... in part (b)” refers to (b)(i). The zero spatial mode gives $u=0$ irrespective of $T$ and contributes no additional nonzero solution.[Printed page 7, Question 6(b)(ii), internal part reference](../../../../../../discrete-mathematics-continuous-mathematics-and-probability-examination-2015-by-university-of-oxford.md)
