↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Solving the [linear ordinary differential equation](../../../../../../linear-ordinary-differential-equation-split.md) and imposing the [initial condition](../../../../../../initial-condition.md) gives

$$
\boxed{y(x)=2e^{-\lambda x}.}
$$

As $\lambda>0$, the requested [limit](../../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md) is

$$
\boxed{\lim_{\lambda\to\infty}y(1)=\lim_{\lambda\to\infty}2e^{-\lambda}=0.}
$$
