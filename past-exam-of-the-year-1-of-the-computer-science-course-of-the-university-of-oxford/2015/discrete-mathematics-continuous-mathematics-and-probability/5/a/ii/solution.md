<h1 id="5/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Use the [Taylor series](../../../../../../taylor-series.md) $e^u=1+u+u^2/2+\cdots$ with $u=x^3-x^5$. Since $u$ begins at cubic order and $u^2$ begins at sixth order, through cubic terms the [Taylor series](../../../../../../taylor-series.md) is

$$
\boxed{f(x)=1+x^3+O(x^5).}
$$

The linear and quadratic coefficients are zero. The stronger remainder $O(x^5)$, in [big O notation](../../../../../../big-o-notation.md), follows because the fourth-order term is also zero; the next term is $-x^5$.
