<h1 id="5/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Near zero, $x^3-x^5=x^3(1-x^2)$ has the sign of $x$, so $f(x)<1$ just to the left and $f(x)>1$ just to the right. Thus the [critical point](../../../../../../critical-point.md) is neither a [local minimum](../../../../../../local-minimum.md) nor a [local maximum](../../../../../../local-maximum.md). Moreover the [Taylor series](../../../../../../taylor-series.md) gives $f''(x)=6x+O(x^3)$, which changes sign at zero. Therefore

$$
\boxed{(0,1)\text{ is a stationary inflection point}.}
$$

This is the point called a “saddle point” in the question; [stationary inflection point](../../../../../../stationary-inflection-point.md) specifies the one-variable meaning.
