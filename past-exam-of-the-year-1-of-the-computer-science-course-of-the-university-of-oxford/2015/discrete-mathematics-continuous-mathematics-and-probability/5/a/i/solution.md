<h1 id="5/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [chain rule](../../../../../../chain-rule.md) gives

$$
f'(x)=e^{x^3-x^5}(3x^2-5x^4)
=e^{x^3-x^5}x^2(3-5x^2).
$$

The [exponential function](../../../../../../exponential-function.md) never vanishes, so all [critical points](../../../../../../critical-point.md) are

$$
\boxed{x=0,\qquad x=\sqrt{3/5},\qquad x=-\sqrt{3/5}.}
$$

In particular $f'(0)=0$.
