<h1 id="3/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Suppose the definition of [big O notation](../../../../../../big-o-notation.md) held with constants $C>0,N$. Choose an odd [integer](../../../../../../integer-split.md) $n>\max(C,N)$. At this input, $f(n)=n$ and $g(n)=1$, so the required [inequality](../../../../../../inequality-mathematics.md) would be $n\le C$, a contradiction. Thus

$$
\boxed{f\ne O(g).}
$$

The infinitely many even inputs, where $f(n)/g(n)=1/n$, cannot repair the failure on odd inputs because the bound must hold for **every** sufficiently large input.
