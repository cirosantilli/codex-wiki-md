↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [greatest common divisor](../../../../../../greatest-common-divisor.md) is the largest positive [integer](../../../../../../integer-split.md) dividing both inputs:

$$
\boxed{\gcd(a,b)=\max\{d\in\mathbb N_+:d\mid a\text{ and }d\mid b\}.}
$$

This [maximum](../../../../../../maximum-of-a-subset-of-a-total-order.md) exists because $1$ belongs to the [set](../../../../../../set-split.md) and all its elements are at most $b$.
