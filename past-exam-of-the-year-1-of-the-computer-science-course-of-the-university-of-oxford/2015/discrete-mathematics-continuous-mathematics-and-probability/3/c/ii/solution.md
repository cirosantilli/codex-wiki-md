<h1 id="3/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Write $a=qb+r$ using the [Euclidean division](../../../../../../euclidean-division.md). If $d\mid a$ and $d\mid b$, then $d\mid(a-qb)=r$. Conversely, if $d\mid b$ and $d\mid r$, then $d\mid(qb+r)=a$. Therefore $(a,b)$ and $(b,r)$ have exactly the same positive [common divisors](../../../../../../common-divisor.md), proving

$$
\boxed{\gcd(a,b)=\gcd(b,r).}
$$

For $r=0$, this agrees with $\gcd(b,0)=b$.
