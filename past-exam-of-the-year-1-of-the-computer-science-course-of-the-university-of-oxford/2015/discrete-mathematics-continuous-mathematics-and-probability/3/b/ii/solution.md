<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [Euclidean division](../../../../../../euclidean-division.md) gives $a=qb+r$ with $q\in\mathbb N_0$ and $0\le r<b$. Writing $a=nu,b=nv$ gives

$$
r=a-qb=n(u-qv).
$$

Since $u-qv$ is an [integer](../../../../../../integer-split.md), this proves the [divisibility](../../../../../../divisibility-split.md)

$$
\boxed{n\mid r,}
$$

including $r=0$.
