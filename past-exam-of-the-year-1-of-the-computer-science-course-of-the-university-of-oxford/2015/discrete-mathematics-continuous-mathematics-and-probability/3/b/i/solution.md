<h1 id="3/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

By [divisibility](../../../../../../divisibility-split.md), write $a=nu$ and $b=nv$ for positive [integers](../../../../../../integer-split.md) $u,v$. Then

$$
a^2-b^2=n^2(u^2-v^2).
$$

The factor $u^2-v^2$ is an [integer](../../../../../../integer-split.md), so

$$
\boxed{n^2\mid(a^2-b^2).}
$$
