<h1 id="1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Using [set difference](../../../../../../set-difference.md) as an [intersection](../../../../../../set-intersection.md) with a [complement of a set](../../../../../../complement-of-a-set.md), and using [double negation](../../../../../../double-negation.md), gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
\overline A\oplus\overline B
&=(\overline A\cap B)\cup(\overline B\cap A)\\
&=(B\setminus A)\cup(A\setminus B)
=A\oplus B.
\end{aligned}
$$

Interchanging the two [sets](../../../../../../set-split.md) simply interchanges the two terms of this [union](../../../../../../set-union.md), whose [commutativity](../../../../../../commutativity.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\overline A\oplus\overline B=A\oplus B,
\qquad A\oplus B=B\oplus A.}
$$
