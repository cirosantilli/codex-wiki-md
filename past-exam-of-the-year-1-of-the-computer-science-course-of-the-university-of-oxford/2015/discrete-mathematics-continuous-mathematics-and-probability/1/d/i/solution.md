<h1 id="1/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Seek an [affine function](../../../../../../affine-function.md) $f(x)=cx+d$. Its [function composition](../../../../../../function-composition.md) with itself is

$$
f(f(x))=c^2x+(c+1)d.
$$

Equating coefficients requires $c^2=a$ and $(c+1)d=b$. Because $a>0$ and $a\ne1$, either choice $c=\sqrt a$ or $c=-\sqrt a$ has $c+1\ne0$. Thus two valid [functions](../../../../../../function-split.md) are

$$
\boxed{f(x)=\sqrt a\,x+\frac{b}{1+\sqrt a}}
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\boxed{f(x)=-\sqrt a\,x+\frac{b}{1-\sqrt a}.}
$$

Substitution into $c^2x+(c+1)d$ verifies each answer. These provide the requested maps; no assumption that every possible solution is [affine function](../../../../../../affine-function.md) is needed.
