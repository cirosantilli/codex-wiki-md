<h1 id="1/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Try a [linear function](../../../../../../linear-function.md) $f(x)=cx$. The [functional equation](../../../../../../functional-equation.md) becomes $c^2x=(c+1)x$ for all $x$, so $c$ must be a [root of a polynomial](../../../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md) $c^2-c-1$. Its two [roots of a polynomial](../../../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md) give

$$
\boxed{f(x)=\frac{1+\sqrt5}{2}x}
\qquad\text{or}\qquad
\boxed{f(x)=\frac{1-\sqrt5}{2}x.}
$$

For either choice, $c^2=c+1$ proves the required equality directly. The positive coefficient is the [golden ratio](../../../../../../golden-ratio.md).
