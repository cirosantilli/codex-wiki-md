<h1 id="1/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Take the [function](../../../../../../function-split.md)

$$
\boxed{f:\mathbb R\longrightarrow\mathbb R,\qquad f(x)=-x.}
$$

Then $f(f(x))=-(-x)=x$ for every [real number](../../../../../../real-number.md) $x$, while $f(1)=-1\ne1$. Thus it is an [involution](../../../../../../involution.md) different from the [identity function](../../../../../../identity-function.md).
