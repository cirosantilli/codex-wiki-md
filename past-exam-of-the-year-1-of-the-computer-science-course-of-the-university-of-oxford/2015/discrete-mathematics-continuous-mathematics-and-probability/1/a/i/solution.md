The [set difference](../../../../../../set-difference.md) consists of elements belonging to the first [set](../../../../../../set-split.md) but not the second:

$$
\boxed{A\setminus B=\{x\in\mathcal U:x\in A\text{ and }x\notin B\}.}
$$

The [symmetric difference](../../../../../../symmetric-difference.md) consists of elements belonging to exactly one of the two [sets](../../../../../../set-split.md):

$$
\boxed{A\oplus B=(A\setminus B)\cup(B\setminus A).}
$$

In particular, the two [sets](../../../../../../set-split.md) in this [union](../../../../../../set-union.md) are [disjoint sets](../../../../../../disjoint-sets.md).
