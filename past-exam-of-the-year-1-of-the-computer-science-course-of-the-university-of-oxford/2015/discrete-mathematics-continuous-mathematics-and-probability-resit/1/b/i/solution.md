<h1 id="1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Since $x^2+1\geq2|x|$, the [image of a function](../../../../../../image-of-a-function.md) is contained in $[-1/2,1/2]$. Both endpoints occur, at $x=-1$ and $x=1$. By [continuity](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) and the [intermediate value theorem](../../../../../../intermediate-value-theorem.md) applied on $[-1,1]$, every intermediate value occurs too. Hence

$$
\boxed{f(\mathbb R)=[-1/2,1/2],\qquad f\text{ is neither onto nor one-to-one}.}
$$

It is not [surjective](../../../../../../surjective-function.md) onto $\mathbb R$ because, for example, $1$ is not attained. It is not [injective](../../../../../../injective-function.md) because $f(2)=f(1/2)=2/5$ although $2\ne1/2$.
