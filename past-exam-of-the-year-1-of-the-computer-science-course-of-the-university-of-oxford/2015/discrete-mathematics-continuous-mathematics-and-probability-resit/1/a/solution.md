↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [image of a function](../../../../../image-of-a-function.md) $f:A\to B$ is

$$
\boxed{f(A)=\{f(a):a\in A\}\subseteq B.}
$$

The [function](../../../../../function-split.md) is [surjective](../../../../../surjective-function.md), or onto, exactly when every $b\in B$ equals $f(a)$ for at least one $a\in A$, equivalently $f(A)=B$. It is [injective](../../../../../injective-function.md), or one-to-one, exactly when

$$
\boxed{\forall a,a'\in A,\quad f(a)=f(a')\Longrightarrow a=a'.}
$$

Thus [surjectivity](../../../../../surjective-function.md) concerns whether the entire [codomain](../../../../../codomain.md) is reached; [injectivity](../../../../../injective-function.md) concerns whether distinct inputs have distinct outputs.
