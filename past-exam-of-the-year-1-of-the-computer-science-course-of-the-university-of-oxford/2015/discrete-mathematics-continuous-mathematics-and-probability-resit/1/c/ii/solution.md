<h1 id="1/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Use [set difference](../../../../../../set-difference.md), associativity of [set intersection](../../../../../../set-intersection.md), and [De Morgan's laws](../../../../../../de-morgan-s-laws.md):

$$
\begin{aligned}
(A\setminus B)\setminus C
&=(A\cap B^c)\cap C^c\\
&=A\cap(B^c\cap C^c)\\
&=A\cap(B\cup C)^c\\
&=\boxed{A\setminus(B\cup C)}.
\end{aligned}
$$
