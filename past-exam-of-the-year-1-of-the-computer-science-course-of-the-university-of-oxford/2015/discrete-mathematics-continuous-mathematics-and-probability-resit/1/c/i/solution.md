<h1 id="1/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Take every [complement of a set](../../../../../../complement-of-a-set.md) relative to $\mathcal U$. By [set difference](../../../../../../set-difference.md), [De Morgan's laws](../../../../../../de-morgan-s-laws.md), and distribution of [set intersection](../../../../../../set-intersection.md) over [set union](../../../../../../set-union.md),

$$
\begin{aligned}
A\setminus(B\cap A\cap C)
&=A\cap(B^c\cup A^c\cup C^c)\\
&=(A\cap B^c)\cup(A\cap A^c)\cup(A\cap C^c)\\
&=A\cap(B^c\cup C^c)\\
&=\boxed{A\setminus(B\cap C)}.
\end{aligned}
$$

Here the middle term is the [empty set](../../../../../../empty-set.md), since a [set](../../../../../../set-split.md) and its [complement of a set](../../../../../../complement-of-a-set.md) are disjoint.
