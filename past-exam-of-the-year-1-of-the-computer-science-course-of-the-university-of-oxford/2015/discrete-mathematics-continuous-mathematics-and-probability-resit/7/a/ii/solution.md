<h1 id="7/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Since the $B_i$ form a [partition of a set](../../../../../../partition-of-a-set.md) of the [sample space](../../../../../../sample-space.md), the [events](../../../../../../event.md) $A\cap B_i$ are pairwise disjoint and their [set union](../../../../../../set-union.md) is $A$. By [countable additivity](../../../../../../countable-additivity.md) of [probability](../../../../../../probability.md), followed by the definition of [conditional probability](../../../../../../conditional-probability.md),

$$
\mathbb P(A)=\sum_{i=1}^{\infty}\mathbb P(A\cap B_i)
=\boxed{\sum_{i=1}^{\infty}\mathbb P(A\mid B_i)\mathbb P(B_i)}.
$$

Every ratio is legitimate because each $\mathbb P(B_i)>0$. This proves the countable [law of total probability](../../../../../../law-of-total-probability.md).
