<h1 id="8/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For each student, the [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) of both falling asleep and dreaming about [mathematics](../../../../../../mathematics-split.md) is $(1-p)q$. These indicators are independent across students, so the same [Poisson thinning](../../../../../../poisson-thinning.md) calculation gives, for the requested count $D$,

$$
\boxed{D\sim\operatorname{Poisson}(\lambda(1-p)q).}
$$

Explicitly $G_D(s)=\exp(\lambda(1-p)q(s-1))$, the [probability generating function](../../../../../../probability-generating-function.md) of that [Poisson distribution](../../../../../../poisson-distribution.md).
