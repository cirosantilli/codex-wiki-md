<h1 id="8/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $S_n=\sum_{i=1}^nX_i$. By [independence](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md), the [expectation of a product of independent random variables](../../../../../../expectation-of-a-product-of-independent-random-variables.md) factors, and the identical [distributions](../../../../../../distribution-mathematical-analysis.md) give

$$
\boxed{G_{S_n}(s)=\mathbb E\left[\prod_{i=1}^n s^{X_i}\right]
=\prod_{i=1}^n\mathbb E[s^{X_i}]=[G_X(s)]^n.}
$$

This is valid for $|s|\leq1$ and wherever the required absolute [expectations](../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) exist. It is the [probability generating function of an independent sum](../../../../../../probability-generating-function-of-an-independent-sum.md).
