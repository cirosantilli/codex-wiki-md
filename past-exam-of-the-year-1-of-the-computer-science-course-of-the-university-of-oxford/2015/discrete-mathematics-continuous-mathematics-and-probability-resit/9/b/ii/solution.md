<h1 id="9/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [cumulative distribution function](../../../../../../cumulative-distribution-function.md) is [continuous](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) and [strictly increasing](../../../../../../strictly-increasing-function.md) for $x>0$, so the [median](../../../../../../median.md) is the unique positive $c$ satisfying $F_X(c)=1/2$. Consequently $e^{-c^2/2}=1/2$, giving

$$
\boxed{c=\sqrt{2\log2}.}
$$

There is no atom at $c$, so $\mathbb P(X>c)=1-F_X(c)=1/2$ as required.
