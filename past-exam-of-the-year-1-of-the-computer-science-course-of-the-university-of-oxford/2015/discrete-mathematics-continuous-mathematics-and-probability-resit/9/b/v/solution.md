↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

The standard [normal distribution](../../../../../../normal-distribution.md) has [mean](../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) zero and [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md) one, so $\mathbb E[Z^2]=\operatorname{var}(Z)+(\mathbb E[Z])^2=1$. Part (iv) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[X]=\sqrt{\frac\pi2}.}
$$

Using the second [moment](../../../../../../moment.md) from part (iii),

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{var}(X)=\mathbb E[X^2]-(\mathbb E[X])^2=2-\frac\pi2=\frac{4-\pi}{2}.}
$$
