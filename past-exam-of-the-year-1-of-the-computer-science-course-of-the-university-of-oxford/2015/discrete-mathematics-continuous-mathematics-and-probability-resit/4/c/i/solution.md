<h1 id="4/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $y=1+x^2$, the [derivatives](../../../../../../derivative.md) are $y'=2x$ and $y''=2$. Its endpoint values are $y(0)=1$ and $y(2)=5$, so it satisfies both the [ordinary differential equation](../../../../../../ordinary-differential-equation.md) and the [boundary conditions](../../../../../../boundary-condition.md). Moreover any solution of $y''=2$ has the form $x^2+Ax+B$; the [boundary conditions](../../../../../../boundary-condition.md) force $B=1$ and $A=0$. Thus the unique solution of the [boundary value problem](../../../../../../boundary-value-problem-split.md) is

$$
\boxed{y(x)=1+x^2.}
$$
