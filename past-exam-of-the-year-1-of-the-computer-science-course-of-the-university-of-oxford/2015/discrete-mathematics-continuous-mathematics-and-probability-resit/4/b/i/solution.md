↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Taking the [partial derivatives](../../../../../../partial-derivative.md) of the [parabolic coordinates](../../../../../../parabolic-coordinates.md) transformation, holding the other coordinate fixed each time, gives

$$
\boxed{x_s=t,\qquad x_t=s,\qquad y_s=-s,\qquad y_t=t.}
$$

Thus its [Jacobian matrix](../../../../../../jacobian-matrix.md) is $J=\begin{pmatrix}t&s\\-s&t\end{pmatrix}$.
