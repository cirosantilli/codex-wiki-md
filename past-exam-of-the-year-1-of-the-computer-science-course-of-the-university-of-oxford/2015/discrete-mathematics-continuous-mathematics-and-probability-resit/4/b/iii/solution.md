↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Apply the multivariable [chain rule](../../../../../../chain-rule.md) to a differentiable [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) $f(s,t)$ on the chosen local branch of the [parabolic coordinates](../../../../../../parabolic-coordinates.md):

$$
\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}
=\frac{\partial f}{\partial s}\frac{\partial s}{\partial x}
+\frac{\partial f}{\partial t}\frac{\partial t}{\partial x}.
$$

Substituting part (ii) gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}=\frac{t f_s+s f_t}{s^2+t^2},\qquad(s,t)\ne(0,0).}
$$
