<h1 id="2/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

There are $n^2$ possible ordered pairs and hence $2^{n^2}$ [binary relations](../../../../../../binary-relation.md). A [reflexive relation](../../../../../../reflexive-relation.md) must contain the $n$ diagonal pairs, while its remaining $n^2-n$ pairs are unrestricted. Subtracting the [reflexive relations](../../../../../../reflexive-relation.md) gives

$$
\boxed{2^{n^2}-2^{n^2-n}}
$$

[binary relations](../../../../../../binary-relation.md) that are not [reflexive](../../../../../../reflexive-relation.md).
