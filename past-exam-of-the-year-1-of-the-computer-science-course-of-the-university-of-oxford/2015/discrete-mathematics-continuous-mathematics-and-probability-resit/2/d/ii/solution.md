<h1 id="2/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A [symmetric relation](../../../../../../symmetric-relation.md) has $n$ independent choices on the diagonal. For each of the $\binom n2$ unordered pairs $\{a,b\}$ of distinct elements, either both ordered pairs are included or neither is. Thus there are $2^{n+\binom n2}$ [symmetric relations](../../../../../../symmetric-relation.md), so the requested number is

$$
\boxed{2^{n^2}-2^{n+\binom n2}=2^{n^2}-2^{n(n+1)/2}.}
$$
