For a [binary relation](../../../../binary-relation.md) $R\subseteq A\times A$, [reflexivity](../../../../reflexive-relation.md) means $aRa$ for every $a\in A$; a [symmetric relation](../../../../symmetric-relation.md) satisfies $aRb\Rightarrow bRa$ for every $a,b\in A$; an [antisymmetric relation](../../../../antisymmetric-relation.md) satisfies $(aRb\text{ and }bRa)\Rightarrow a=b$ for every $a,b\in A$; [transitivity](../../../../transitive-relation.md) means $(aRb\text{ and }bRc)\Rightarrow aRc$ for every $a,b,c\in A$; an [irreflexive relation](../../../../irreflexive-relation.md) satisfies $\neg(aRa)$ for every $a\in A$. In particular, failure of [reflexivity](../../../../reflexive-relation.md) means at least one missing diagonal pair, whereas [irreflexivity](../../../../irreflexive-relation.md) requires every diagonal pair to be missing.
