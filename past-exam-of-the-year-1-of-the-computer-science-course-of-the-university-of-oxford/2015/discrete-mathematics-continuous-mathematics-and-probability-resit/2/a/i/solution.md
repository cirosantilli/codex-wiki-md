<h1 id="2/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $x_i$ be the number of objects in box $i$. The arrangements correspond exactly to nonnegative [integer](../../../../../../integer-split.md) solutions of $x_1+\cdots+x_k=n$. By [stars and bars](../../../../../../stars-and-bars-combinatorics.md), place $n$ stars and $k-1$ separating bars in a row. The number of stars between consecutive bars records the occupancy of each distinguishable box, with adjacent bars allowing an empty box. Choosing the bar positions gives

$$
\boxed{\binom{n+k-1}{k-1}}\qquad(k\geq1).
$$
