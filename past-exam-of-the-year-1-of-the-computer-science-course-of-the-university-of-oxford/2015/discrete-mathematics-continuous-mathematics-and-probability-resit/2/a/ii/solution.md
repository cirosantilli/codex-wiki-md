<h1 id="2/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Put one object into each box first. The remaining $n-k$ objects can be distributed without restrictions, so [stars and bars](../../../../../../stars-and-bars-combinatorics.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\binom{(n-k)+k-1}{k-1}=\binom{n-1}{k-1}.}
$$

Equivalently, the substitution $u_i=x_i-1$ gives a [bijection](../../../../../../bijection.md) between positive [integer](../../../../../../integer-split.md) solutions of $\sum_i x_i=n$ and nonnegative [integer](../../../../../../integer-split.md) solutions of $\sum_i u_i=n-k$.
