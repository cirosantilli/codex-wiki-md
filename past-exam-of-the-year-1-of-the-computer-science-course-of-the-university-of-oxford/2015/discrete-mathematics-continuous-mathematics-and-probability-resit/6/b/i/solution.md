<h1 id="6/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) factors as

$$
g'(x)=4x^3-24x^2+36x=4x(x-3)^2.
$$

The [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) is differentiable everywhere, so its [critical points](../../../../../../critical-point.md) occur precisely where this [derivative](../../../../../../derivative.md) vanishes. Hence

$$
\boxed{x=0\quad\text{and}\quad x=3.}
$$

Their values are $g(0)=5$ and $g(3)=32$.
