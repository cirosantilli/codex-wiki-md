<h1 id="6/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The relevant [derivatives](../../../../../../derivative.md) at $x=3$ are

$$
g(3)=32,\qquad g'(3)=0,\qquad
g''(3)=0,\qquad g'''(3)=24.
$$

Thus the cubic [Taylor polynomial](../../../../../../taylor-polynomial.md) is

$$
\boxed{T_3(x)=32+4(x-3)^3.}
$$

In fact the exact expansion is $g(x)=32+4(x-3)^3+(x-3)^4$, so the omitted term has order $O((x-3)^4)$ in [big O notation](../../../../../../big-o-notation.md).
