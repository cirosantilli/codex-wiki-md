<h1 id="6/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The left and right limits at zero are $-1$ and $1$. By [Dirichlet's theorem on Fourier series](../../../../../../dirichlet-s-theorem-on-fourier-series.md), the [Fourier series](../../../../../../fourier-series-split.md) converges to

$$
\boxed{\frac{f(0^-)+f(0^+)}2=\frac{-1+1}{2}=0.}
$$

Indeed every term $\sin((2j+1)\pi x)$ is zero at $x=0$.
