A [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ is [periodic](../../../../../../periodic-function.md) with period $\alpha>0$ when

$$
\boxed{f(x+\alpha)=f(x)\quad\text{for every }x\in\mathbb R.}
$$

The period need not be the smallest positive one; for example, every positive [integer](../../../../../../integer-split.md) multiple of a period is also a period.
