<h1 id="6/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

At $x=1$, the left limit is $1$. The right limit is $-1$ because of the period-two extension. Therefore [Dirichlet's theorem on Fourier series](../../../../../../dirichlet-s-theorem-on-fourier-series.md) gives the [Fourier series](../../../../../../fourier-series-split.md) limit

$$
\boxed{\frac{f(1^-)+f(1^+)}2=\frac{1-1}{2}=0.}
$$

Every [Fourier partial sum](../../../../../../fourier-partial-sum.md) is also exactly zero there because $\sin((2j+1)\pi)=0$.
