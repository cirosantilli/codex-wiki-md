The [characteristic equation](../../../../../../characteristic-equation-of-a-constant-coefficient-differential-equation.md) of the constant-coefficient [linear ordinary differential equation](../../../../../../linear-ordinary-differential-equation-split.md) is

$$
4r^2+8r+5=0,
$$

whose [roots of a polynomial](../../../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md) are $r=-1\pm i/2$. Taking real and imaginary parts of the corresponding [exponential functions](../../../../../../exponential-function.md) gives the real general solution

$$
\boxed{y_h(x)=e^{-x}\left(A\cos\frac x2+B\sin\frac x2\right),\qquad A,B\in\mathbb R.}
$$
