<h1 id="5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Since $x>-1$, divide by $x+1$ to obtain the first-order [linear ordinary differential equation](../../../../../linear-ordinary-differential-equation-split.md)

$$
y'-\frac3{x+1}y=(x+1)^4.
$$

An [integrating factor](../../../../../integrating-factor.md) is $\mu(x)=e^{-3\log(x+1)}=(x+1)^{-3}$. Multiplication by this factor gives

$$
\frac{d}{dx}\left[(x+1)^{-3}y\right]=x+1.
$$

After [integration](../../../../../integral-split.md), $(x+1)^{-3}y=\tfrac12(x+1)^2+C$, so

$$
\boxed{y(x)=\frac12(x+1)^5+C(x+1)^3,\qquad C\in\mathbb R,\quad x>-1.}
$$
