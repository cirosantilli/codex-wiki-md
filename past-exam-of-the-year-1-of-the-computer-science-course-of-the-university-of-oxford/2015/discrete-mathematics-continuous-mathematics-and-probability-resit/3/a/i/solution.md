↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Because $T$ is positive, the definition of [big O notation](../../../../../../big-o-notation.md) becomes

$$
\boxed{\exists C>0\ \exists n_0\in\mathbb N_+\ \forall n\geq n_0:\quad T(n)\leq Cn^2.}
$$

Here $C$ and $n_0$ are fixed independently of $n$.
