<h1 id="3/e/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Set $k=\lfloor x\rfloor$. The definition of the [floor function](../../../../../../floor-function.md) gives $k\leq x<k+1$. Adding the [integer](../../../../../../integer-split.md) $m$ yields

$$
k+m\leq x+m<k+m+1.
$$

Since $k+m$ is an [integer](../../../../../../integer-split.md), it is exactly the [floor](../../../../../../floor-function.md) of $x+m$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\lfloor x+m\rfloor=\lfloor x\rfloor+m.}
$$
