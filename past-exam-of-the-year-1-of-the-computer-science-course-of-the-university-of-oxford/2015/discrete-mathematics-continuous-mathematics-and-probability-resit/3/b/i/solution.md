<h1 id="3/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

By [modular arithmetic](../../../../../../modular-arithmetic-split.md), $a_1-b_1=nu$ and $a_2-b_2=nv$ for some [integers](../../../../../../integer-split.md) $u,v$. Adding gives

$$
(a_1+a_2)-(b_1+b_2)=n(u+v).
$$

Since $u+v$ is an [integer](../../../../../../integer-split.md), this proves

$$
\boxed{a_1+a_2\equiv b_1+b_2\pmod n.}
$$
