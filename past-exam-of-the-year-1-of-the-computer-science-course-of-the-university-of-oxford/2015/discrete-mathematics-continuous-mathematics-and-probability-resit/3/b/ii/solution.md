<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Using $a_i=b_i+nu_i$ with [integers](../../../../../../integer-split.md) $u_i$,

$$
\begin{aligned}
a_1a_2-b_1b_2
&=(b_1+nu_1)(b_2+nu_2)-b_1b_2\\
&=n(b_1u_2+b_2u_1+nu_1u_2).
\end{aligned}
$$

The quantity in parentheses is an [integer](../../../../../../integer-split.md), so the [divisibility](../../../../../../divisibility-split.md) criterion for [modular arithmetic](../../../../../../modular-arithmetic-split.md) gives

$$
\boxed{a_1a_2\equiv b_1b_2\pmod n.}
$$
