<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The four [CMOS inverters](../../../../../cmos-inverter.md) use $4\cdot2$ [transistors](../../../../../transistor.md), the four two-input [NAND gates](../../../../../nand-gate.md) use $4\cdot4$, and the four-input [NAND gate](../../../../../nand-gate.md) uses $8$. Therefore this particular design uses

$$
\boxed{N=8+16+8=32\text{ transistors}.}
$$
