↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [scalar](../../../../../scalar.md) $\lambda$ is an [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) of an $n\times n$ [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) $P$ if there is a nonzero [vector](../../../../../vector.md) $\mathbf v$ such that

$$
\boxed{P\mathbf v=\lambda\mathbf v,\qquad\mathbf v\ne\mathbf0.}
$$

Any such [vector](../../../../../vector.md) is an [eigenvector](../../../../../eigenvector.md) corresponding to $\lambda$. The requirement that an [eigenvector](../../../../../eigenvector.md) be nonzero is essential: the zero [vector](../../../../../vector.md) would satisfy the equation for every [scalar](../../../../../scalar.md) and would convey no information.
