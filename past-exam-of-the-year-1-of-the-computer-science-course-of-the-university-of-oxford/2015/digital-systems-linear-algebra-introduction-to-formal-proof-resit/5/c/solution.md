<h1 id="5/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Condition on the intermediate parent's height and use the [Markov property](../../../../../markov-property.md). The three mutually exclusive paths through Tall, Medium and Short contribute

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Pr(\text{short grandchild}\mid\text{tall grandparent})
&=0.6\cdot0.2+0.2\cdot0.1+0.2\cdot0.6\\
&=0.12+0.02+0.12.
\end{aligned}
$$

Equivalently, this is entry $(3,1)$ of the [matrix power](../../../../../matrix-power.md) $P^2$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\Pr=0.26=26\%.}
$$
