<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a real $m\times n$ [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) $A$, the [row space](../../../../../row-space.md) is the [linear span](../../../../../linear-span.md) of its rows in $\mathbb R^n$. The [column space](../../../../../column-space.md) is the [linear span](../../../../../linear-span.md) of its columns in $\mathbb R^m$, equivalently $\{A\mathbf x:\mathbf x\in\mathbb R^n\}$. The [null space](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) is

$$
\ker A=\{\mathbf x\in\mathbb R^n:A\mathbf x=\mathbf0\}.
$$

The [matrix rank](../../../../../matrix-rank.md) is the [dimension](../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) of the [column space](../../../../../column-space.md), which equals the [dimension](../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) of the [row space](../../../../../row-space.md). In particular,

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{rank}A=\dim\operatorname{Row}(A)=\dim\operatorname{Col}(A),\qquad
\dim\ker A=n-\operatorname{rank}A.}
$$

The last equality is the [rank-nullity theorem](../../../../../rank-nullity-theorem.md).
