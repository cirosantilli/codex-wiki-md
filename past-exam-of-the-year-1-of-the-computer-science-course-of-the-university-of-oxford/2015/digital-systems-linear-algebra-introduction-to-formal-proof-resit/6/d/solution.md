<h1 id="6/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [reduced row echelon form](../../../../../reduced-row-echelon-form.md) gives $x_1+x_4=0$, $x_2-x_4=0$, and $x_3+x_4=0$. With free parameter $x_4=t$,

$$
\mathbf x=t\begin{pmatrix}-1\\1\\-1\\1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The displayed nonzero [vector](../../../../../vector.md) therefore forms a [basis](../../../../../basis.md) for the [null space](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md):

$$
\boxed{\mathcal B_{\rm null}=\{(-1,1,-1,1)^T\},\qquad\dim\ker A=1.}
$$

This agrees with the [rank-nullity theorem](../../../../../rank-nullity-theorem.md): there are four columns and [matrix rank](../../../../../matrix-rank.md) three.
