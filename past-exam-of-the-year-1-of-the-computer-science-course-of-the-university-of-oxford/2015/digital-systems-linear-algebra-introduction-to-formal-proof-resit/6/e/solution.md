<h1 id="6/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The second, third and fourth [linear equations](../../../../../linear-equation.md) give

$$
x_1+x_2=2,\qquad x_2+x_3=0,\qquad x_3+x_4=-1.
$$

Setting $x_4=t$ yields $x_3=-1-t$, $x_2=1+t$ and $x_1=1-t$. The first [linear equation](../../../../../linear-equation.md) then gives $(1-t)+(1+t)+(-1-t)+t=1$, and the fifth gives $(1-t)+t=1$, so both remaining conditions hold for every real $t$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\mathbf x=\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\\-1\\0\end{pmatrix}
+t\begin{pmatrix}-1\\1\\-1\\1\end{pmatrix},\qquad t\in\mathbb R.}
$$

This is a particular solution plus the whole [null space](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md), an [affine line in a vector space](../../../../../affine-line-in-a-vector-space.md) of solutions. The derivation both produces solutions and shows that none have been omitted.
