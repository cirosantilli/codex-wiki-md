↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For transitivity of [first-order equality](../../../../../../logical-equality.md), take $\phi(v)$ to be $T_1=v$. The [equality elimination rule](../../../../../../equality-elimination-rule.md) applied to $T_2=T_3$ and $\phi(T_2)$ gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{T_2=T_3\qquad T_1=T_2}{T_1=T_3}\ =E.}
$$

For symmetry of [first-order equality](../../../../../../logical-equality.md), first obtain $T_1=T_1$ by the [equality introduction rule](../../../../../../equality-introduction-rule.md). Now take $\psi(v)$ to be $v=T_1$ and substitute using $T_1=T_2$:

$$
\boxed{\frac{T_1=T_2\qquad\dfrac{}{T_1=T_1}\ =I}{T_2=T_1}\ =E.}
$$

These choices of formula show exactly which occurrence is replaced, so neither argument assumes the property it is establishing.
