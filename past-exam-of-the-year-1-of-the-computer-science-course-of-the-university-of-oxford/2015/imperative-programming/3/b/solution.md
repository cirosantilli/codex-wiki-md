<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A [dummy header node](../../../../../sentinel-node.md) gives the first actual [linked list node](../../../../../linked-list-node.md) a predecessor, just like every other element. [Linked-list deletion](../../../../../linked-list-deletion.md) and insertion at the front can therefore update a predecessor's `tail` with the same code used in the middle. An empty [linked list](../../../../../linked-list.md) is represented by a header whose `tail` is `NIL`.

$$
\boxed{\text{A permanent predecessor removes special cases at the head.}}
$$
