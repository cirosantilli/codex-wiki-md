**No.** A [tree](../../../../../tree-graph-theory-split.md) consisting of a single edge $uv$ has the two [maximum independent sets](../../../../../maximum-independent-set.md) $\{u\}$ and $\{v\}$. Each has size one, and neither can be enlarged because $u,v$ are adjacent. Thus even a two-vertex [tree](../../../../../tree-graph-theory-split.md) gives a counterexample to uniqueness.
