<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Dynamic programming](../../../../../dynamic-programming.md) describes a problem by reusable subproblem states and a recurrence, computing each required state once through [memoization](../../../../../memoization.md) or a bottom-up order. Its benefit comes from sharing answers to overlapping subproblems.

The [principle of optimality](../../../../../principle-of-optimality.md) says that, after the information needed by a state has been fixed, the remaining part of an optimal solution is optimal for that state. Otherwise replacing it by a better subsolution would improve the whole solution. A correct state must retain every constraint that can affect later choices.
