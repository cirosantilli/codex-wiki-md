A [topological sort](../../../../../topological-sorting.md) of a [Directed acyclic graph](../../../../../directed-acyclic-graph.md) is a linear ordering of its vertices in which the tail of every directed edge precedes its head. Along a path $v_0\to v_1\to\cdots\to v_k$, this property successively gives

$$
v_0<v_1<\cdots<v_k
$$

in that ordering. Thus the vertices of every directed path occur in [topological order](../../../../../topological-order.md). The acyclicity condition is necessary, since a directed cycle would force its first vertex to precede itself.
