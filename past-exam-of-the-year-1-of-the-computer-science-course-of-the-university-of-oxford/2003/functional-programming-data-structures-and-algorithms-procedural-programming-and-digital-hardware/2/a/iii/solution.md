<h1 id="2/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

[Idempotence](../../../../../../idempotence.md) concerns applying an operation to the same _whole value_. It says more than saying that the equal-entry pairs are unchanged. Choose the [idempotent](../../../../../../idempotent.md) [binary operation](../../../../../../binary-operation.md) `min`. Then

$$
[1,2]\,X_{\min}\,[1,2]=[1,1,1,2]\ne[1,2].
$$

More generally, a finite [list](../../../../../../list-abstract-data-type.md) of length $m$ paired with itself produces $m^2$ entries, so length alone contradicts [idempotence](../../../../../../idempotence.md) when $m>1$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\text{False: the lifted operation need not be idempotent.}}
$$
