<h1 id="2/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

[Commutativity](../../../../../../commutativity.md) of the element [binary operation](../../../../../../binary-operation.md) does not remove the ordering of a [list comprehension](../../../../../../list-comprehension.md). For ordinary addition, take `xs = [1,2]` and `ys = [10,20]`. Then

$$
[1,2]\,X_+\,[10,20]=[11,21,12,22],\qquad
[10,20]\,X_+\,[1,2]=[11,12,21,22].
$$

The middle entries occur in opposite orders. Hence

$$
\boxed{\text{False: the lifted operation need not be commutative.}}
$$
