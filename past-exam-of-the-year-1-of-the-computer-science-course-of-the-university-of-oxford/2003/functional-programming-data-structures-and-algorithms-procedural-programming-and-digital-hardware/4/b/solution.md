For eventually nonnegative [functions](../../../../../function-split.md) $T,f,g$, [Big O notation](../../../../../big-o-notation.md) and [Big omega notation](../../../../../big-omega-notation.md) mean

$$
\boxed{T\in O(f)\iff\exists C>0\ \exists n_0\ \forall n\geq n_0:\ 0\leq T(n)\leq Cf(n),}
$$



$$
\boxed{T\in\Omega(g)\iff\exists c>0\ \exists n_0\ \forall n\geq n_0:\ 0\leq cg(n)\leq T(n).}
$$

These are asymptotic [upper bounds](../../../../../upper-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md) and [lower bounds](../../../../../lower-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md) up to positive constant factors. The notation $T(n)=O(f(n))$ conventionally expresses membership in the class $O(f)$, rather than equality of two numerical [functions](../../../../../function-split.md).
