<h1 id="10/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [D flip-flop](../../../../../../d-flip-flop.md) stores one [bit](../../../../../../bit.md). On each active [clock edge](../../../../../../clock-edge.md), it samples the input D and changes its stored output Q to that sampled value after the propagation delay. Between active edges, it holds the value. In the ideal discrete model,

$$
\boxed{Q_{k+1}=D_k,}
$$

where $D_k$ is the input sampled at edge $k$ and $Q_{k+1}$ is the state just after that edge. This assumes D meets the required [setup time](../../../../../../setup-time.md) and [hold time](../../../../../../hold-time.md); asynchronous set or reset inputs, if present, are inactive.
