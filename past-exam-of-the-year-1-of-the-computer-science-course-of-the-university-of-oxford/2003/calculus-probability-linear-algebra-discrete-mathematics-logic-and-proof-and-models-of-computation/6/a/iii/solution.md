<h1 id="6/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Equality of [remainders](../../../../../../remainder.md) after division by eight is a [Euclidean relation](../../../../../../euclidean-relation.md). If $x$, $y$ have the same remainder and $x$, $z$ have the same remainder, then $y$, $z$ have the same remainder. In the notation of [modular arithmetic](../../../../../../modular-arithmetic-split.md),

$$
x\equiv y\pmod8,\quad x\equiv z\pmod8\quad\Longrightarrow\quad y\equiv z\pmod8.
$$

Hence

$$
\boxed{\mathrm{MOD}\ 8\text{ is Euclidean}.}
$$
