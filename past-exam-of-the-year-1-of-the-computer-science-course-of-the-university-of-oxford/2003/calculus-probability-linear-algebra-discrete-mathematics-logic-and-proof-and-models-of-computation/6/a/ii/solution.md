The [divisibility](../../../../../../divisibility-split.md) [binary relation](../../../../../../binary-relation.md) on positive [natural numbers](../../../../../../natural-number.md) is **not** a [Euclidean relation](../../../../../../euclidean-relation.md). With $x=1$, $y=2$, $z=3$, we have $1\mid2$ and $1\mid3$ but $2\nmid3$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\mid\text{ is not Euclidean}.}
$$
