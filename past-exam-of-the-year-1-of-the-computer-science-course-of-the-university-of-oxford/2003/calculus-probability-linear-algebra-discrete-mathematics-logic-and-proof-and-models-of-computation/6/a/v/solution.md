↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

The “same age” [binary relation](../../../../../../binary-relation.md) is a [Euclidean relation](../../../../../../euclidean-relation.md), with age understood as one consistently measured value at a fixed time. If $x$ has the same age as both $y$ and $z$, the ages of $y$ and $z$ are equal. Hence

$$
\boxed{\text{same age is Euclidean}.}
$$

This is another instance of equality of the values of a [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) inducing an [equivalence relation](../../../../../../equivalence-relation.md).
