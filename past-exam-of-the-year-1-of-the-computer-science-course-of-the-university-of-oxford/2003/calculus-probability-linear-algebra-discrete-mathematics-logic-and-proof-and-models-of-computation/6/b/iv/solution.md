<h1 id="6/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

On the nonempty [set](../../../../../../set-split.md) $X=\{a\}$ take the empty [binary relation](../../../../../../binary-relation.md) $R=\varnothing$. There are no triples for which $xRy\land xRz$ holds. The defining implication is therefore true for every triple by [vacuous truth](../../../../../../vacuous-truth.md), making $R$ a [Euclidean relation](../../../../../../euclidean-relation.md).

But $aRa$ is false, so $R$ is not a [reflexive relation](../../../../../../reflexive-relation.md) and hence is not an [equivalence relation](../../../../../../equivalence-relation.md):

$$
\boxed{(X,R)=(\{a\},\varnothing)\text{ is a counterexample}.}
$$
