<h1 id="6/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Suppose $R$ is an [equivalence relation](../../../../../../equivalence-relation.md), and $xRy$, $xRz$. Since it is a [symmetric relation](../../../../../../symmetric-relation.md), $yRx$. Since it is a [transitive relation](../../../../../../transitive-relation.md), $yRx$ together with $xRz$ implies $yRz$. This proves

$$
\boxed{R\text{ an equivalence relation}\Longrightarrow R\text{ Euclidean}.}
$$
