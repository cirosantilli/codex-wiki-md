↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For [events](../../../../../../event.md) $A,B$ with $\mathbb P(B)>0$, the [conditional probability](../../../../../../conditional-probability.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(A\mid B)=\frac{\mathbb P(A\cap B)}{\mathbb P(B)}.}
$$

It measures the [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) of $A$ after restricting the [sample space](../../../../../../sample-space.md) to $B$ and renormalizing. This quotient does not define [conditional probability](../../../../../../conditional-probability.md) given an [event](../../../../../../event.md) of zero [probability](../../../../../../probability.md).
