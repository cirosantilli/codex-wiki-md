<h1 id="4/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For a square [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md), the corresponding changes in its [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) are

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
R_i\leftrightarrow R_j &: \quad \det\longmapsto-\det,\\
R_i\leftarrow cR_i &: \quad \det\longmapsto c\det,\\
R_i\leftarrow R_i+cR_j &: \quad \det\longmapsto\det.
\end{aligned}}
$$

These follow respectively from the alternating property, [multilinearity](../../../../../../multilinearity.md) in the rows, and the fact that a [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) with two equal rows vanishes.
