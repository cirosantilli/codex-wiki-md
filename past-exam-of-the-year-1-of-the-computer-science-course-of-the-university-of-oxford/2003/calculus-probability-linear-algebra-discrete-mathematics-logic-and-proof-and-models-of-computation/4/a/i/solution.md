↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The three [elementary row operations](../../../../../../elementary-row-operation.md) are:

- interchange two distinct rows, $R_i\leftrightarrow R_j$;
- multiply a row by a nonzero [scalar](../../../../../../scalar.md), $R_i\leftarrow cR_i$ with $c\ne0$;
- add a [scalar multiple](../../../../../../scalar-multiple.md) of another row, $R_i\leftarrow R_i+cR_j$ with $i\ne j$.

The nonzero requirement in the second operation ensures that each [elementary row operation](../../../../../../elementary-row-operation.md) is reversible.

$$
\boxed{R_i\leftrightarrow R_j;\qquad R_i\leftarrow cR_i\ (c\ne0);\qquad R_i\leftarrow R_i+cR_j\ (i\ne j).}
$$
