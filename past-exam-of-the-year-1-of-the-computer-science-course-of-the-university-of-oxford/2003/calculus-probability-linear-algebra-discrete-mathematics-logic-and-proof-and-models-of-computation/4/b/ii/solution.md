<h1 id="4/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A square [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) is an [invertible matrix](../../../../../../invertible-matrix.md) exactly when its [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) is nonzero. The preceding [recurrence relation](../../../../../../recurrence-relation.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{A_n\text{ is invertible}\iff n\not\equiv2\pmod3.}
$$
