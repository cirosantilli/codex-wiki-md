<h1 id="8/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

With all variables ranging over the domain of the [partially ordered set](../../../../../partially-ordered-set.md), the three [first-order logic](../../../../../first-order-logic.md) formulas are

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
\text{reflexivity: }&\forall x\;R(x,x),\\
\text{antisymmetry: }&\forall x\forall y\;((R(x,y)\land R(y,x))\Rightarrow x=y),\\
\text{transitivity: }&\forall x\forall y\forall z\;((R(x,y)\land R(y,z))\Rightarrow R(x,z)).
\end{aligned}}
$$

The middle formula defines an [antisymmetric relation](../../../../../antisymmetric-relation.md); it does not forbid $R(x,y)$ for distinct $x,y$, but forbids both directions simultaneously for distinct elements.
