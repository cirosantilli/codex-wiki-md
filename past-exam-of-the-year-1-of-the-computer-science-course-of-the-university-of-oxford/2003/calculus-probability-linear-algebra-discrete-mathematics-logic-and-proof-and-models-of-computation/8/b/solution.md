<h1 id="8/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $\mathbf A$ be the one-element [partially ordered set](../../../../../partially-ordered-set.md) $\{a\}$ with $R(a,a)$. Taking $x=a$ witnesses the [existential quantifier](../../../../../existential-quantification.md), and the only $y$ is comparable with $a$. Thus $\mathbf A\models\varphi$.

Let $\mathbf B$ be the two-element [antichain](../../../../../antichain.md) $\{a,b\}$, with $R$ consisting only of $(a,a)$ and $(b,b)$. This is a [partial order](../../../../../partially-ordered-set.md). If $x=a$, choose $y=b$, and if $x=b$, choose $y=a$. In either case both $R(x,y)$ and $R(y,x)$ are false. Therefore no witness for the [existential quantifier](../../../../../existential-quantification.md) exists:

$$
\boxed{\mathbf A=(\{a\},=)\models\varphi,\qquad\mathbf B=(\{a,b\},=)\not\models\varphi.}
$$
