<h1 id="10/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use the following [context-free grammar](../../../../../../context-free-grammar.md), with start symbol $S$:

$$
\boxed{S\longrightarrow\epsilon\mid S0\mid S1B0,\qquad B\longrightarrow\epsilon\mid B1B0.}
$$

The symbol $B$ generates the balanced members of the [suffix-nonnegative binary language](../../../../../../suffix-nonnegative-binary-language.md), while $S$ permits extra zeros. To prove the claim and prepare the ambiguity argument, reverse every generated [string](../../../../../../string.md). The reversed productions are

$$
U\longrightarrow\epsilon\mid0U\mid0D1U,\qquad D\longrightarrow\epsilon\mid0D1D.
$$

Here $U$ represents the reversals of the $S$ [strings](../../../../../../string.md), and $D$ represents the reversals of the $B$ [strings](../../../../../../string.md). A [string](../../../../../../string.md) belongs to the desired [formal language](../../../../../../formal-language.md) precisely when its reversal has no [string prefix](../../../../../../string-prefix.md) with more ones than zeros. Associate a path with each reversed [string](../../../../../../string.md), counting a zero as a step $+1$ and a one as a step $-1$.

The nonterminal $D$ generates exactly the paths that stay nonnegative and finish at zero. The production $0D1D$ consists of an initial up-step, a balanced path one level higher, a down-step to zero, and another balanced path. Conversely, any nonempty balanced nonnegative path has a first return to zero, and splitting there gives exactly this form. [Mathematical induction](../../../../../../mathematical-induction.md) on length proves the characterization.

The productions for $U$ generate nonnegative paths: $0U$ stays strictly above zero after its first step, while $0D1U$ first executes a balanced excursion and then another nonnegative path. Conversely, a nonempty nonnegative path starts with zero. If it never returns to height zero, removing that initial zero leaves a nonnegative path, giving $0U$. If it does return, split at its first return to obtain $0D1U$. These shorter components can be generated by [mathematical induction](../../../../../../mathematical-induction.md). This proves that the original [context-free grammar](../../../../../../context-free-grammar.md) generates exactly the required [formal language](../../../../../../formal-language.md).

For instance, in the original [context-free grammar](../../../../../../context-free-grammar.md),

$$
S\Rightarrow S1B0\Rightarrow S01B0\Rightarrow01B0\Rightarrow01B1B00\Rightarrow011B00\Rightarrow01100,
$$

while $S\Rightarrow S1B0\Rightarrow S1B01B0\Rightarrow^*1010$. The [string](../../../../../../string.md) $110$ is excluded since its entire length is a [string suffix](../../../../../../string-suffix.md) having two ones and only one zero.
