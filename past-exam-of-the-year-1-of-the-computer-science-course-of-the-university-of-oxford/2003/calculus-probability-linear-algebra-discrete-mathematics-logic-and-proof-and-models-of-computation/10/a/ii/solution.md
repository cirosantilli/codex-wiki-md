<h1 id="10/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Use start symbol $S$ and an auxiliary symbol $A$ in the [context-free grammar](../../../../../../context-free-grammar.md)

$$
\boxed{S\longrightarrow A1A1A1A,\qquad A\longrightarrow0A\mid1A\mid\epsilon.}
$$

The symbol $A$ generates exactly $\{0,1\}^*$, so every generated [string](../../../../../../string.md) has the three explicitly displayed ones. Conversely, any [string](../../../../../../string.md) with at least three ones can be split at any three of them as $u_0\,1\,u_1\,1\,u_2\,1\,u_3$, and each $u_j$ can be generated by $A$. This proves both inclusions. For example,

$$
S\Rightarrow A1A1A1A\Rightarrow^*00101110
$$

by taking the four $A$ substrings to be $00$, $0$, $1$, and $0$ respectively.
