<h1 id="3/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a [linear map](../../../../../../linear-map.md) $T:V\to W$, its [image of a linear map](../../../../../../image-of-a-linear-map.md) and [kernel of a linear map](../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) are

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{im}T=\{T(v):v\in V\}\subseteq W,\qquad \ker T=\{v\in V:T(v)=0_W\}\subseteq V.}
$$

Their [dimensions](../../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) define the [rank of a linear map](../../../../../../rank-of-a-linear-map.md) and [nullity](../../../../../../nullity-of-a-linear-map.md):

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{rank}T=\dim(\operatorname{im}T),\qquad\operatorname{nullity}T=\dim(\ker T).}
$$

The [image of a linear map](../../../../../../image-of-a-linear-map.md) measures which output [vectors](../../../../../../vector.md) can occur, while the [kernel of a linear map](../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) measures which input [vectors](../../../../../../vector.md) are sent to zero.
