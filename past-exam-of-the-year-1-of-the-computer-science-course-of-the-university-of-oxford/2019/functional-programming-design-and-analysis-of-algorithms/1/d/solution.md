Over [integers](../../../../../integer-split.md), choose **`s = (-)`, `c = flip (-)`, and `a = b = 0`**. The [left fold](../../../../../left-fold.md) and [right fold](../../../../../list-fold.md) both compute the negative of the [sum](../../../../../sum.md) of a finite [list](../../../../../list-abstract-data-type.md). The combining [functions](../../../../../function-split.md) differ: `s 1 2 = -1`, whereas `c 1 2 = 1`.
