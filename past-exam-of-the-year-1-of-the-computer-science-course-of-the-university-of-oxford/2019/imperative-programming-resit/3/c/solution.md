<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A permanent [dummy header node](../../../../../sentinel-node.md) gives even the first real [linked list node](../../../../../linked-list-node.md) a predecessor. Insertion into an empty [linked list](../../../../../linked-list.md) and deletion or movement of its first real [linked list node](../../../../../linked-list-node.md) then use the same link updates as internal cases. The header itself represents no contact, so it also removes the need to use a real name as a special end marker. A search still needs a null-end check.
