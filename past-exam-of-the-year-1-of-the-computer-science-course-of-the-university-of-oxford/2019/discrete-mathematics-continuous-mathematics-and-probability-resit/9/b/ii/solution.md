The missing axiom is **normalization: $\mathbb P(\Omega)=1$**. The zero [set function](../../../../../../set-function.md) satisfies both displayed requirements but is not a [probability measure](../../../../../../probability-measure.md), showing why this condition is essential.

The incomplete axiom needs **[countable additivity](../../../../../../countable-additivity.md)**: for every sequence of pairwise disjoint [events](../../../../../../event.md) $A_1,A_2,\ldots\in\mathcal F$,

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P\!\left(\bigcup_{j=1}^\infty A_j\right)=\sum_{j=1}^\infty\mathbb P(A_j).}
$$

The stated two-event version implies [finite additivity](../../../../../../finite-additivity-of-a-set-function.md), but does not supply this requirement for a countably infinite collection. Together with nonnegativity and normalization, this gives the usual [probability measure](../../../../../../probability-measure.md) axioms on the [sigma-algebra](../../../../../../sigma-algebra.md) $\mathcal F$.
