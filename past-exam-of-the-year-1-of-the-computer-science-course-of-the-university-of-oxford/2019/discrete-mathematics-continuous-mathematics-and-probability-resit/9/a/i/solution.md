↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a [Poisson distribution](../../../../../../poisson-distribution.md) with parameter $\lambda>0$, the **[probability mass function](../../../../../../probability-mass-function.md)** is

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(X=k)=e^{-\lambda}\frac{\lambda^k}{k!},\qquad k=0,1,2,\ldots,}
$$

and is zero at all other values. Its normalization follows from the [exponential series](../../../../../../exponential-series.md) $\sum_{k\geq0}\lambda^k/k!=e^\lambda$.
