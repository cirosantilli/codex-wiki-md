<h1 id="9/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

By the preceding [convolution](../../../../../../convolution.md), $Z=X_1+X_2$ has a [Poisson distribution](../../../../../../poisson-distribution.md) with parameter $\nu=\lambda+\mu$. Directly from its [probability mass function](../../../../../../probability-mass-function.md), shifting the index in the absolutely convergent [sums](../../../../../../sum.md) gives $\mathbb E[Z]=\nu$ and $\mathbb E[Z(Z-1)]=\nu^2$. Since $Z^2=Z(Z-1)+Z$, **$\mathbb E[(X_1+X_2)^2]=(\lambda+\mu)^2+(\lambda+\mu)$**. This is also the identity $\mathbb E[Z^2]=\operatorname{Var}(Z)+(\mathbb E[Z])^2$.
