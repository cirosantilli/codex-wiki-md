<h1 id="9/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Subtract the first three values of the [probability mass function](../../../../../../probability-mass-function.md) of the [Poisson distribution](../../../../../../poisson-distribution.md) from one:

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(X\geq3)=1-e^{-2}\left(1+2+\frac{2^2}{2!}\right)=1-5e^{-2}\approx0.323324.}
$$
