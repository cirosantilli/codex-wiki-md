<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a differentiable scalar [function](../../../../../function-split.md) of $n$ real variables, its [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) is the linear map

$$
Df(\mathbf x)[\mathbf h]=\sum_{i=1}^n\frac{\partial f}{\partial x_i}(\mathbf x)h_i=\nabla f(\mathbf x)^T\mathbf h,
$$

where the column [gradient](../../../../../gradient.md) is $\nabla f=(\partial f/\partial x_1,\ldots,\partial f/\partial x_n)^T$. Its **[Hessian](../../../../../hessian-matrix.md)** is the $n\times n$ [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) $H_f=(\partial^2 f/\partial x_i\partial x_j)_{i,j}$. For a $C^2$ [function](../../../../../function-split.md), equality of mixed [partial derivatives](../../../../../partial-derivative.md) makes this [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) symmetric.
