<h1 id="8/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $R_1,R_2$ be the [events](../../../../../../event.md) that the first and second birds are ringed. Under [sampling without replacement](../../../../../../sampling-without-replacement.md),

$$
\mathbb P(R_1\cap R_2)=\frac4{10}\frac39=\frac2{15}.
$$

Thus the [conditional probability](../../../../../../conditional-probability.md) is **$\displaystyle\mathbb P(R_1\mid R_2)=\frac{2/15}{2/5}=\frac13$**. Alternatively, once a particular ringed second bird is specified, three of the nine possible first birds are ringed.
