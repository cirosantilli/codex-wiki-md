<h1 id="8/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The two captures form a uniform ordered sample of distinct birds, that is, [sampling without replacement](../../../../../../sampling-without-replacement.md). By [exchangeability](../../../../../../exchangeable-random-variables.md), each of the ten birds has the same chance of occupying the second position, so **the [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) is $4/10=2/5$**. Equivalently, conditioning on the first capture gives $(4/10)(3/9)+(6/10)(4/9)=2/5$.
