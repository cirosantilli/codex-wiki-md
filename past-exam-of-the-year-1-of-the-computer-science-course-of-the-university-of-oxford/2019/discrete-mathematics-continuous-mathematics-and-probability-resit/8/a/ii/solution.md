<h1 id="8/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Using the [indicator random variable](../../../../../../indicator-random-variable.md), sum over the values of the [discrete random variable](../../../../../../discrete-random-variable.md) $X$:

$$
\mathbb E[X\mathbf1_B]=\sum_x x\,\mathbb P(\{X=x\}\cap B)=\mathbb P(B)\sum_x x\,\mathbb P(X=x\mid B).
$$

Dividing by the positive [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) $\mathbb P(B)$ proves **$\mathbb E[X\mid B]=\mathbb E[X\mathbf1_B]/\mathbb P(B)$**. On $B^c$ the product vanishes, so no additional contribution is omitted.
