<h1 id="8/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For an integrable [discrete random variable](../../../../../../discrete-random-variable.md), the **[conditional expectation](../../../../../../conditional-expectation.md) given an [event](../../../../../../event.md)** $B$ of positive [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[X\mid B]=\sum_x x\,\mathbb P(X=x\mid B)=\sum_x x\,\frac{\mathbb P(\{X=x\}\cap B)}{\mathbb P(B)}.}
$$

The [sum](../../../../../../sum.md) ranges over the possible values of $X$; integrability ensures absolute convergence.
