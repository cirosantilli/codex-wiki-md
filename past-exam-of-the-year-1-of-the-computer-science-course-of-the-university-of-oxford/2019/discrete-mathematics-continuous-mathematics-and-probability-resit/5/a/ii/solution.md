<h1 id="5/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Use $f(x)=x\log_2x-4$. Then $f(1)=-4$, $f(2)=-2$, and the [secant method](../../../../../../secant-method.md) gives

$$
x_2=2-(-2)\frac{2-1}{-2-(-4)}=3.
$$

Writing $L=\log_2 3$, the next step is

$$
x_3=3-\frac{(3L-4)(3-2)}{(3L-4)-(-2)}=3-\frac{3L-4}{3L-2}=2.725982457878719\ldots.
$$

Thus the requested approximation is **$x_3=2.73$ to three [significant figures](../../../../../../significant-figures.md)**. All intermediate [logarithms](../../../../../../logarithm.md) should be retained at higher precision before this final rounding.
