<h1 id="1/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Suppose $g(y)=g(y')$. Applying $f$ and using the [right inverse](../../../../../../right-inverse.md) identity gives

$$
y=(f\circ g)(y)=(f\circ g)(y')=y'.
$$

This is exactly [injectivity](../../../../../../injective-function.md), so **every [right inverse](../../../../../../right-inverse.md) is [injective](../../../../../../injective-function.md)**.
