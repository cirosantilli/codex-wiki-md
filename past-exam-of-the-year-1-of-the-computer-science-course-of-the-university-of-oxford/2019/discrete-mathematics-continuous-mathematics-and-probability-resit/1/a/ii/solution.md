A [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) $f:X\to Y$ is **[surjective](../../../../../../surjective-function.md)** if every element of its [codomain](../../../../../../codomain.md) has a [preimage](../../../../../../preimage.md): for every $y\in Y$, there is some $x\in X$ such that $f(x)=y$.
