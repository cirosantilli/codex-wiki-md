**Not [injective](../../../../../../injective-function.md); [surjective](../../../../../../surjective-function.md).** Indeed, $f(0)=f(-1)=-9$ although $0\ne-1$. This [polynomial](../../../../../../polynomial-split.md) is [continuous](../../../../../../continuous-function.md), with $f(x)\to-\infty$ as $x\to-\infty$ and $f(x)\to\infty$ as $x\to\infty$. For any prescribed real $y$, choose $a<b$ with $f(a)<y<f(b)$; the [intermediate value theorem](../../../../../../intermediate-value-theorem.md) supplies a [preimage](../../../../../../preimage.md) between $a$ and $b$.
