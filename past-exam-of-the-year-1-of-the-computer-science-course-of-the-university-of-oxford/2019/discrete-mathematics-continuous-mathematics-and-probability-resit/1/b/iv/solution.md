↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

**Not [injective](../../../../../../injective-function.md); [surjective](../../../../../../surjective-function.md).** The equal values $f(0)=f(2)=1$ disprove [injectivity](../../../../../../injective-function.md). For any $y>0$, take $x=1+y^{-1/2}>1$. This lies in the stated [domain of a function](../../../../../../domain-of-a-function.md), and

$$
\frac1{(1-|x|)^2}=\frac1{(-y^{-1/2})^2}=y.
$$

Consequently every member of the [codomain](../../../../../../codomain.md) has a [preimage](../../../../../../preimage.md).
