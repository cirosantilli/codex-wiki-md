**Both [injective](../../../../../../injective-function.md) and [surjective](../../../../../../surjective-function.md).** The [natural logarithm](../../../../../../natural-logarithm.md) is strictly increasing on the [positive real numbers](../../../../../../positive-real-number.md), so it is [injective](../../../../../../injective-function.md). For every $y\in\mathbb R$, $x=e^y>0$ satisfies $\ln x=y$; hence the [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) is [surjective](../../../../../../surjective-function.md), with the [exponential function](../../../../../../exponential-function.md) as its [inverse function](../../../../../../inverse-function.md).
