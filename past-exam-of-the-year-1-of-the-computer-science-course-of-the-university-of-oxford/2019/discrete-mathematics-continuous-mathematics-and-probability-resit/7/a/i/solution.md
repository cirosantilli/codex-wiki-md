<h1 id="7/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Call the normalization constant $K$ to distinguish it from the moment index used later. A [probability density function](../../../../../../probability-density-function.md) must have total [integral](../../../../../../integral-split.md) one, so

$$
1=K\int_c^\infty x^{-\theta-1}\,dx=\frac{Kc^{-\theta}}\theta.
$$

Therefore **$K=\theta c^\theta$**, the value denoted $k$ in the density. This is the [Pareto distribution](../../../../../../pareto-distribution.md) with scale $c$ and shape $\theta$.
