<h1 id="7/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Write $r$ for a positive [integer](../../../../../../integer-split.md) moment index. The [moment](../../../../../../moment.md) is

$$
\mathbb E[X^r]=\theta c^\theta\int_c^\infty x^{r-\theta-1}\,dx.
$$

This [improper integral](../../../../../../improper-integral.md) converges exactly when $r-\theta-1<-1$, namely $r<\theta$. For those indices,

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[X^r]=\frac{\theta c^r}{\theta-r},\qquad r\in\mathbb Z_{>0},\ r<\theta.}
$$

At $r=\theta$ the divergence is logarithmic; for $r>\theta$ it is a power divergence. Thus, in the source's moment notation, the positive [integers](../../../../../../integer-split.md) $k$ allowed are exactly $k<\theta$. If $\theta\leq1$, no positive integer [moment](../../../../../../moment.md) is finite.
