<h1 id="7/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $x\leq c$, the [cumulative distribution function](../../../../../../cumulative-distribution-function.md) is zero. For $x>c$, integrate the normalized [probability density function](../../../../../../probability-density-function.md):

$$
\boxed{F_X(x)=\begin{cases}0,&x\leq c,\\\displaystyle\int_c^x\theta c^\theta t^{-\theta-1}\,dt=1-\left(\frac cx\right)^\theta,&x>c.\end{cases}}
$$

It is continuous at $c$, increases to one, and is the [cumulative distribution function](../../../../../../cumulative-distribution-function.md) of the stated [Pareto distribution](../../../../../../pareto-distribution.md).
