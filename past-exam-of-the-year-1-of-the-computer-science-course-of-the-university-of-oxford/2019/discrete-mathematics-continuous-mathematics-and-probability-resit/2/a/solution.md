<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

There are $n$ choices for the first position, then $n-1$ choices for the second, and so on down to one choice. The [multiplication principle](../../../../../rule-of-product.md) therefore gives **$n!$ [permutations](../../../../../permutation.md)**. This also agrees with the empty [permutation](../../../../../permutation.md) convention $0!=1$.
