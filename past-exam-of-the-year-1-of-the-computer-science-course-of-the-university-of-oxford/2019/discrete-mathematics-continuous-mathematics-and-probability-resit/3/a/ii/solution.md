↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**False.** Set $a_n=4^n/n^4$. For $n\geq4$,

$$
\frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n}=4\left(\frac n{n+1}\right)^4\geq4\left(\frac45\right)^4=\frac{1024}{625}>1.
$$

Iteration gives a growing [geometric sequence](../../../../../../geometric-progression.md) as a lower bound for $a_n$. Hence $a_n\to\infty$ and no eventual constant can bound $4^n/n^4$, as [Big O notation](../../../../../../big-o-notation.md) would require.
