↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**True.** Rationalizing the [square root](../../../../../../square-root.md) gives, for $n\geq1$,

$$
0\leq\sqrt{n^2+n}-n=\frac n{\sqrt{n^2+n}+n}=\frac1{\sqrt{1+1/n}+1}\leq\frac12.
$$

The uniform bound is exactly the assertion $O(1)$ in [Big O notation](../../../../../../big-o-notation.md).
