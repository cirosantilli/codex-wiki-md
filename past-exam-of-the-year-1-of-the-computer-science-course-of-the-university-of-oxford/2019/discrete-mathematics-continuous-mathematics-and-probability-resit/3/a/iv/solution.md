<h1 id="3/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

**True.** The [logarithm](../../../../../../logarithm.md) identity gives

$$
\ln(e^n+n)-n=\ln(1+ne^{-n}).
$$

For $n\geq1$, $e^n\geq n$, so $0\leq ne^{-n}\leq1$. By [monotonicity](../../../../../../monotonic-function.md) of the [natural logarithm](../../../../../../natural-logarithm.md), the expression lies between $0$ and $\ln2$. It is therefore $O(1)$ in [Big O notation](../../../../../../big-o-notation.md).
