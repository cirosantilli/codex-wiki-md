↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

**False.** On every odd positive [integer](../../../../../../integer-split.md) $n$, $f(n)/g(n)=n$. Given any proposed [Big O notation](../../../../../../big-o-notation.md) constant $C$ and threshold $N$, choose an odd $n>\max(C,N)$. Then $f(n)>Cg(n)$, contradicting the required bound. The behavior on even [integers](../../../../../../integer-split.md) cannot repair this failure on an unbounded [subsequence](../../../../../../subsequence.md).
