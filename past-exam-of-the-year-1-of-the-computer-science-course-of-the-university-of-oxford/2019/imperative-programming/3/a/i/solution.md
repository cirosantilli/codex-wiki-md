<h1 id="3/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The first three subtractions are negative or return the already-seen zero, so addition is used. The fourth subtraction gives a new positive two; the next subtraction is negative. Thus **$r(1),\ldots,r(5)=1,3,6,2,7$**. These are the initial terms of [Recamán's sequence](../../../../../../recaman-s-sequence.md).[Initial terms, offset zero](../../../../../../oeis-entry-a005132.md)
