<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Iterator pattern](../../../../../iterator-pattern.md) gives clients a uniform way to inspect and consume the elements of a collection without exposing how that collection is stored. An [iterator](../../../../../iterator.md) has a traversal state separate from the abstract collection interface, so the same client loop can process an [array](../../../../../array-data-structure.md), a [linked list](../../../../../linked-list.md) or generated values. **It preserves [encapsulation](../../../../../encapsulation-computer-programming.md) and [representation independence](../../../../../representation-independence.md)**: changing the underlying [data structure](../../../../../data-structure-split.md) need not change the traversal client. Separate [iterator](../../../../../iterator.md) instances can track separate traversals; two aliases of one consuming [iterator](../../../../../iterator.md) instead share its progress.
