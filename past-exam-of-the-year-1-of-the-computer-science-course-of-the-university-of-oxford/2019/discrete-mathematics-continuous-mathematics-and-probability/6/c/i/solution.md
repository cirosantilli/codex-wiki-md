<h1 id="6/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use a column [gradient](../../../../../../gradient.md). By the multivariable [chain rule](../../../../../../chain-rule.md),

$$
\boxed{\nabla l(\mathbf x)=
\sum_{i=1}^n y_i f'(\mathbf a_i^{\mathsf T}\mathbf x)\,\mathbf a_i.}
$$

The scalar coefficients multiply the constant [vectors](../../../../../../vector.md) $\mathbf a_i$. In particular $f'(t)=-te^{-t^2/2}$, although explicit differentiation of $f$ is not required.
