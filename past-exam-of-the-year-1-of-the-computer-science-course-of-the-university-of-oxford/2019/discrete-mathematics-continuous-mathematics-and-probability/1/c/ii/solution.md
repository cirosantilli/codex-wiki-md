↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**It is neither [injective](../../../../../../injective-function.md) nor [surjective](../../../../../../surjective-function.md) onto $\mathbb R$, so it has no [inverse function](../../../../../../inverse-function.md) on the stated domain and codomain.** The distinct inputs $0$ and $\pi/3$ both lie in the domain and both give zero. All values lie in $[-1,1]$, so, for example, the real number $2$ has no preimage.
