**The [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) is [injective](../../../../../../injective-function.md) and [surjective](../../../../../../surjective-function.md), with [inverse function](../../../../../../inverse-function.md) $f^{-1}(y)=1-y$.** Equality $1-x=1-x'$ forces $x=x'$. For any $y\in(0,1)$, the input $x=1-y$ also lies in $(0,1)$ and satisfies $f(x)=y$. Applying the [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) twice returns the original input.
