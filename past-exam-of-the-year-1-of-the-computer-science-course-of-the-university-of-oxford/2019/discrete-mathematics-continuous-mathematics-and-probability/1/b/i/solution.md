↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [set difference](../../../../../../set-difference.md) $\varnothing\setminus\varnothing$ is the [empty set](../../../../../../empty-set.md). Its [power set](../../../../../../power-set.md) contains exactly one element, the [empty set](../../../../../../empty-set.md) itself. Therefore **the [cardinality](../../../../../../cardinality.md) is $1$**, not zero.
