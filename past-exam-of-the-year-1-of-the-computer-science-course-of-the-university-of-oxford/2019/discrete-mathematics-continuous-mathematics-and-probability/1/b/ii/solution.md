A [binary relation](../../../../../../binary-relation.md) on $A$ is any [subset](../../../../../../subset.md) of the [Cartesian product](../../../../../../cartesian-product.md) $A\times A$. There are $n^2$ ordered pairs, and each can independently be included or omitted. Hence **the number of [binary relations](../../../../../../binary-relation.md) is $2^{n^2}$**. This also gives one relation when $n=0$, namely the [empty set](../../../../../../empty-set.md).
