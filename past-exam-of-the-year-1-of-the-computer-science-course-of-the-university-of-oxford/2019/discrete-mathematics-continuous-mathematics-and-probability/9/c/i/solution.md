<h1 id="9/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Write the card values as $a_1,\ldots,a_{1000}$. Uniform selection gives

$$
\mathbb E[X]=\frac1{1000}\sum_i a_i=0,\qquad
\mathbb E[X^2]=\frac1{1000}\sum_i a_i^2=1.
$$

Thus **$\mathbb E[X]=0$ and $\operatorname{Var}(X)=1$**, by the [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md) identity $\operatorname{Var}(X)=\mathbb E[X^2]-\mathbb E[X]^2$.
