↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

\*\*[Chebyshev's inequality](../../../../../../chebyshev-s-inequality.md): if $X$ has finite mean $\mu$ and [variance](../../../../../../variance-split.md) $\sigma^2$, then for $a>0$,

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(|X-\mu|\ge a)\le\frac{\sigma^2}{a^2}.}
$$

Apply [Markov's inequality](../../../../../../markov-s-inequality.md) to the nonnegative [random variable](../../../../../../random-variable-split.md) $Y=(X-\mu)^2$ with threshold $a^2$. Since $\mathbb E[Y]=\sigma^2$ and $\{|X-\mu|\ge a\}=\{Y\ge a^2\}$, the desired statement follows immediately.
