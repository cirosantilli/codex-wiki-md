↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**True.** For every $n\ge4$, $2\le\sqrt n$, and raising both nonnegative sides to the $n$th power gives

$$
2^n\le n^{n/2}.
$$

Thus the [Big O notation](../../../../../../big-o-notation.md) condition holds with constant $C=1$ and threshold $n_0=4$.
