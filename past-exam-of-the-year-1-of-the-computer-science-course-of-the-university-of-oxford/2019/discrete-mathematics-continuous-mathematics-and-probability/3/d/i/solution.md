↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**False.** For $n\ge3$, every factor from $3$ through $n$ is at least $3$, so

$$
n!\ge2\cdot3^{n-2},\qquad
\frac{n!}{2^n}\ge\frac29\left(\frac32\right)^n\longrightarrow\infty.
$$

No fixed multiple of $2^n$ eventually bounds the [factorial](../../../../../../factorial.md), contradicting the definition of [Big O notation](../../../../../../big-o-notation.md). No use of [Stirling's approximation](../../../../../../stirling-formula.md) is required.
