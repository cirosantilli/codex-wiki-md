A [derangement](../../../../../../derangement-of-a-permutation.md) of a finite [set](../../../../../../set-split.md) $A$ is a [permutation](../../../../../../permutation.md) $\sigma:A\to A$ with **no fixed point**: $\sigma(a)\ne a$ for every $a\in A$. Thus every element is moved, although the [permutation](../../../../../../permutation.md) remains a [bijection](../../../../../../bijection.md).
