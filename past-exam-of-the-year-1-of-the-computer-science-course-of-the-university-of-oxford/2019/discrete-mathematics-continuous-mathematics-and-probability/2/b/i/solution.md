The [Fibonacci recurrence](../../../../../../fibonacci-recurrence.md), with the supplied initial values, is **$F_{n+2}=F_{n+1}+F_n$ for $n\ge0$**, with $F_0=0,F_1=1$. Two initial values are needed for this second-order [linear recurrence relation](../../../../../../linear-recurrence-relation.md).
