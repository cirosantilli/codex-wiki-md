↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**Use $G_1=0$ and $G_{n+1}=nG_n+n-1$ for $n\ge1$.** Indeed,

$$
G_{n+1}=n!-1=n\big((n-1)!-1\big)+n-1=nG_n+n-1.
$$

The initial value is $0!-1=0$, so the [recurrence relation](../../../../../../recurrence-relation.md) produces the specified [factorial](../../../../../../factorial.md) sequence.
