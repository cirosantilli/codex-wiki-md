↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Adding the [Fibonacci recurrence](../../../../../../fibonacci-recurrence.md) and the identical [recurrence relation](../../../../../../recurrence-relation.md) for the [Lucas numbers](../../../../../../lucas-number.md) gives

$$
\boxed{A_{n+2}=A_{n+1}+A_n,\qquad A_0=2,\quad A_1=2.}
$$

Linearity preserves this [linear recurrence relation](../../../../../../linear-recurrence-relation.md); the initial values come from $0+2$ and $1+1$.
