<h1 id="2/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $\phi=(1+\sqrt5)/2$, the [golden ratio](../../../../../../golden-ratio.md). Since $\phi^2=\phi+1$, multiplication by $\phi^n$ gives $\phi^{n+2}=\phi^{n+1}+\phi^n$. Adding this [recurrence relation](../../../../../../recurrence-relation.md) to the one for $A_n$ yields

$$
\boxed{B_{n+2}=B_{n+1}+B_n,\qquad B_0=3,\quad B_1=2+\phi.}
$$

Thus the extra exponential term changes the initial values, but not the [linear recurrence relation](../../../../../../linear-recurrence-relation.md).
