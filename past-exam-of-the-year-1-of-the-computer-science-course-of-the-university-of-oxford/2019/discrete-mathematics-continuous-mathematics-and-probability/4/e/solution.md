↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

**No: [Monte Carlo integration](../../../../../monte-carlo-integration.md) is poorly suited to this smooth one-dimensional problem at such a tight tolerance.** A sample-average [Monte Carlo estimator](../../../../../monte-carlo-estimator.md) has a standard error proportional to $N^{-1/2}$, whereas the deterministic [composite Simpson's rule](../../../../../composite-simpson-s-rule.md) bound decreases as $n^{-4}$ here and already certifies the target with tens of strips. Random sampling would require vastly more [function evaluations](../../../../../function-evaluation.md) and supplies a probabilistic error statement rather than the displayed deterministic guarantee.
