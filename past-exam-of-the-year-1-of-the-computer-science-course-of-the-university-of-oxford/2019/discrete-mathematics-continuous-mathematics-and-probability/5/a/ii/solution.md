<h1 id="5/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [extreme value theorem](../../../../../../extreme-value-theorem.md) guarantees extrema on the closed [interval](../../../../../../interval-mathematics.md). Compare its endpoints and the [stationary points](../../../../../../stationary-point.md) $0,\pm\sqrt{10/3}$:

$$
f(\pm3)=330,\qquad f(0)=6,\qquad
f\!\left(\pm\sqrt{10/3}\right)=-\frac{338}{27}.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{\operatorname*{arg\,max}_{[-3,3]}f=\{-3,3\},\qquad
\operatorname*{arg\,min}_{[-3,3]}f=
\left\{-\sqrt{\frac{10}{3}},\sqrt{\frac{10}{3}}\right\}.}
$$

The two-point answer in each case also follows from the [even function](../../../../../../even-function.md) symmetry.
