↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [Newton root-finding iteration](../../../../../../newton-root-finding-iteration.md) is

$$
\boxed{x_{k+1}=x_k-\frac{x_k^6-5x_k^4+6}{6x_k^5-20x_k^3}.}
$$

It is defined when $x_k\notin\{0,\pm\sqrt{10/3}\}$. Finding an exact [zero of a function](../../../../../../zero-of-a-function.md) is a stopping condition, rather than a reason to continue dividing.
