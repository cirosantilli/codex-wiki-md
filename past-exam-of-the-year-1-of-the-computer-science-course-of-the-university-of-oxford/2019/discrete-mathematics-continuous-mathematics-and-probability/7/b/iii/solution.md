<h1 id="7/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For $W=\max\{U,V\}$ and $0\le w\le1$, [independence](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) gives the [cumulative distribution function](../../../../../../cumulative-distribution-function.md)

$$
F_W(w)=\mathbb P(U\le w,V\le w)=w^2.
$$

The [cumulative distribution function](../../../../../../cumulative-distribution-function.md) is zero below $0$ and one above $1$. Differentiating on the interior gives

$$
\boxed{f_W(w)=2w\quad(0<w<1),\qquad f_W(w)=0\quad\text{otherwise},}
$$

up to irrelevant endpoint values. There are no endpoint atoms. This maximum is an [order statistic](../../../../../../order-statistic.md).
