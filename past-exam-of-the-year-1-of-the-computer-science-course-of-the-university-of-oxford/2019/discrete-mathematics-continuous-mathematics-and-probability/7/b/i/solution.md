↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

By [independence](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md), the [joint probability density](../../../../../../joint-probability-density.md) is the product of the two [uniform distribution](../../../../../../continuous-uniform-distribution.md) densities:

$$
\boxed{f_{U,V}(u,v)=\mathbf1_{\{0\le u\le1,\ 0\le v\le1\}}.}
$$

Changing its values on the boundary does not affect any [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) or [expected value](../../../../../../expected-value-split.md).
