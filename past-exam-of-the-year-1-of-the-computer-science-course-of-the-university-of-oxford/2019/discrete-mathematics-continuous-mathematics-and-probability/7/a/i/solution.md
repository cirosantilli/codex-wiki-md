<h1 id="7/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Multiplying the [beta distribution](../../../../../../beta-distribution.md) density by $x$ gives a constant multiple of the normalized density with parameters $(p+1,q)$:

$$
x f_{p,q}(x)=\frac p{p+q}f_{p+1,q}(x).
$$

Integrating and using the fact that the latter [probability density function](../../../../../../probability-density-function.md) integrates to one gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[X]=\frac p{p+q}.}
$$

This is a normalization-ratio argument, requiring no explicit integration of the polynomial density.
