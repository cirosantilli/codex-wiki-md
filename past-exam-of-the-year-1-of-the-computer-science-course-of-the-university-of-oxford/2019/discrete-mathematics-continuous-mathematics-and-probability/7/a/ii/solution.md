<h1 id="7/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $q>1$, decreasing the second parameter by one gives another valid [beta distribution](../../../../../../beta-distribution.md) density and

$$
\frac{f_{p,q}(x)}{1-x}
=\frac{p+q-1}{q-1}f_{p,q-1}(x).
$$

Normalization therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E\!\left[\frac1{1-X}\right]=\frac{p+q-1}{q-1}.}
$$

The restriction $q>1$ is essential for this finite [expected value](../../../../../../expected-value-split.md); when $q=1$, the singularity near $1$ instead makes the expectation infinite.
