<h1 id="6/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Write $D=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_m)$ and let $|\Lambda|=\max_i|\lambda_i|$. The stated [Euclidean norm](../../../../../../euclidean-norm.md) gives

$$
\|f_n\|^2=\sum_i|\lambda_i|^{2n}|(f_0)_i|^2
\le |\Lambda|^{2n}\sum_i|(f_0)_i|^2=|\Lambda|^{2n}\|f_0\|^2.
$$

Taking nonnegative square roots proves **$\|f_n\|\le|\Lambda|^n\|f_0\|$**. At $n=0$ this is equality. This is a contraction bound in the transformed coordinates; an arbitrary nonorthogonal $S$ does not give the same unit-constant bound for the original error [norm](../../../../../../norm.md).
