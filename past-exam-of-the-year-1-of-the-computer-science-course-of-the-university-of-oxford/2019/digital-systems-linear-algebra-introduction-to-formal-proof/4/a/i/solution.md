<h1 id="4/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For the [equal-cardinality basis criterion](../../../../../../equal-cardinality-basis-criterion.md), because $S$ [spans](../../../../../../linear-span.md) $V$, repeatedly remove a [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) whenever it is a [linear combination](../../../../../../linear-combination.md) of the others. Such a removal does not change the [span](../../../../../../linear-span.md). The finite process ends with a [linearly independent](../../../../../../linear-independence.md) spanning subset $T$, hence a [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) of $V$.

If the original $S$ were [linearly dependent](../../../../../../linear-dependence.md), at least one removal would occur and $|T|<n$. But a [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) of an $n$-dimensional [vector space](../../../../../../vector-space-split.md) has $n$ [vectors](../../../../../../vector.md). Equivalently, apply the allowed claim to the [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) $T$ and any fixed $n$-vector [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) of $V$: the latter would wrongly be [linearly dependent](../../../../../../linear-dependence.md). Thus no removal was possible. **The original [spanning set](../../../../../../spanning-set.md) is [linearly independent](../../../../../../linear-independence.md) and therefore a [basis](../../../../../../basis.md).**
