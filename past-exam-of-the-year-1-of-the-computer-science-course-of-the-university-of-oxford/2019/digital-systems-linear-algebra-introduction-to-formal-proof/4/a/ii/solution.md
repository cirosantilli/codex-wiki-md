<h1 id="4/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Suppose a [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) $v$ lies outside the [span](../../../../../../linear-span.md) of $S$. Then $S\cup\{v\}$ is [linearly independent](../../../../../../linear-independence.md): in an equation $\alpha v+\sum_i\beta_i s_i=0$, a nonzero $\alpha$ would express $v$ in that [span](../../../../../../linear-span.md), and with $\alpha=0$ the independence of $S$ forces every $\beta_i=0$.

This would give $n+1$ [linearly independent](../../../../../../linear-independence.md) [vectors](../../../../../../vector.md) in a space with an $n$-vector [basis](../../../../../../basis.md), contrary to the supplied claim. Therefore $S$ [spans](../../../../../../linear-span.md) $V$. **Independence plus spanning makes $S$ a [basis](../../../../../../basis.md).**
