<h1 id="4/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

A [triangular matrix](../../../../../../triangular-matrix.md) has [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) equal to the product of its diagonal entries, so $\det L=1$ and $\det U=3\cdot4\cdot(-11)=-132$. A [permutation matrix](../../../../../../permutation-matrix.md) exchanging two rows has [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) $-1$. Using multiplicativity of the [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md) in $PA=LU$ yields $(-1)\det A=-132$. Hence **$\det A=132$**.
