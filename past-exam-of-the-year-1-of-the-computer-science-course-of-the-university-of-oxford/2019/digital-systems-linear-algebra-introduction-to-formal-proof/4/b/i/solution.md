↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The product $AB$ is $m\times p$, so multiplying it by the $p\times q$ [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) $C$ gives an **$m\times q$ [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md)**. The product $BC$ is $n\times q$, so multiplying it on the left by the $m\times n$ [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) $A$ also gives an **$m\times q$ [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md)**. Both multiplications have matching inner [dimensions](../../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md).
