<h1 id="5/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Using the unit length of $q_i$,

$$
P_i^2=q_iq_i^Tq_iq_i^T=q_i(q_i^Tq_i)q_i^T=q_iq_i^T=P_i.
$$

Then ordinary [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) expansion gives $(I_n-P_i)^2=I_n-2P_i+P_i^2=I_n-P_i$. **Both $P_i$ and its complementary [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) are [projectors](../../../../../projection-linear-algebra.md).** The second calculation in fact holds for every [idempotent matrix](../../../../../idempotent-matrix.md), without requiring the [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) to equal its [transpose](../../../../../transpose.md).
