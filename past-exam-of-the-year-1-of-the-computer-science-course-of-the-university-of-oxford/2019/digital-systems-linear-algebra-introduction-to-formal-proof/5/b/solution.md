<h1 id="5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

[Associativity](../../../../../associative-property.md) of [matrix multiplication](../../../../../matrix-multiplication.md) identifies $(q_i^Tx)q_i=q_i(q_i^Tx)=(q_iq_i^T)x$. Thus each [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) **$P_i=q_iq_i^T$** has the required action and $x=\sum_iP_ix$.

Consequently $(\sum_iP_i-I_n)x=0$ for every $x$. Taking $x$ successively as the [standard basis](../../../../../standard-basis.md) [vectors](../../../../../vector.md) shows that every column of this difference is zero. Hence **$\sum_{i=1}^nP_i=I_n$**. Equality on one selected [vector](../../../../../vector.md) would not suffice; here it holds on all [vectors](../../../../../vector.md).
