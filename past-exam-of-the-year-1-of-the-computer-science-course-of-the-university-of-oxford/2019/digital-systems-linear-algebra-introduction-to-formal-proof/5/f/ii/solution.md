<h1 id="5/f/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [image of a linear map](../../../../../../image-of-a-linear-map.md) of $P_1$ is exactly the one-dimensional [span](../../../../../../linear-span.md) of $q_1$: its formula always returns a multiple of $q_1$, and $P_1q_1=q_1\ne0$. Hence **$\operatorname{rank}(P_1)=1$**.

The [rank-nullity theorem](../../../../../../rank-nullity-theorem.md) gives $\dim\mathcal N(P_1)=n-1$. Since $\mathcal C(I_n-P_1)=\mathcal N(P_1)$ by (e)(i), **$\operatorname{rank}(I_n-P_1)=n-1$**. This includes the case $n=1$, where the complementary [projector](../../../../../../projection-linear-algebra.md) is zero.
