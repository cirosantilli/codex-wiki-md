<h1 id="5/f/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a [sum of mutually orthogonal projectors](../../../../../../sum-of-mutually-orthogonal-projectors.md), [orthonormality](../../../../../../orthonormal-set.md) gives $q_i^Tq_j=0$ for $i\ne j$ and one for $i=j$. Therefore $P_iP_j=q_i(q_i^Tq_j)q_j^T$ equals zero for different indices and $P_i$ for equal indices. Expanding the [finite sum](../../../../../../finite-sum.md) yields

$$
\overline P_k^2=\sum_{i=1}^k\sum_{j=1}^kP_iP_j=\sum_{i=1}^kP_i=\overline P_k.
$$

Thus **every stated partial sum is a [projector](../../../../../../projection-linear-algebra.md)**. Its [image of a linear map](../../../../../../image-of-a-linear-map.md) is the [span](../../../../../../linear-span.md) of the first $k$ [orthonormal set](../../../../../../orthonormal-set.md) of [vectors](../../../../../../vector.md); arbitrary sums of [idempotent](../../../../../../idempotent.md) [matrices](../../../../../../matrix.md) would not have this conclusion.
