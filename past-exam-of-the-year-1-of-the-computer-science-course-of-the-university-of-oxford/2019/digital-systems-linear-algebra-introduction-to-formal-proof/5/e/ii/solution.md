<h1 id="5/e/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Set $Q=I-P$. Since $Q^2=I-2P+P^2=I-P=Q$, the previous result applies to this [projector](../../../../../../projection-linear-algebra.md) and gives $\mathcal N(Q)=\mathcal C(I-Q)$. Substituting $Q=I-P$ proves **$\mathcal N(I-P)=\mathcal C(P)$**. These are the [kernel-image identities for complementary projectors](../../../../../../kernel-image-identities-for-complementary-projectors.md).
