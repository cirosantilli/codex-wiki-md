<h1 id="5/e/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Prove both inclusions. If $v$ belongs to the [column space](../../../../../../column-space.md) of $I-P$, then $v=(I-P)u$ for some $u$ and $Pv=(P-P^2)u=0$. Thus $v\in\mathcal N(P)$.

Conversely, if $Pv=0$, then $v=(I-P)v$, which explicitly writes $v$ as an [image of a linear map](../../../../../../image-of-a-linear-map.md) under $I-P$. Hence $v\in\mathcal C(I-P)$. The two inclusions prove **$\mathcal N(P)=\mathcal C(I-P)$**. No [orthogonality](../../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) assumption was used.
