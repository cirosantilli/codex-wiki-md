<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write the [bit vector](../../../../../bit-vector.md) as $a=(a_{n-1},\ldots,a_0)$, with $a_0$ the [least significant bit](../../../../../least-significant-bit.md). Its [unsigned interpretation of a bit string](../../../../../unsigned-interpretation-of-a-bit-string.md) is **$\operatorname{bin}(a)=\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}a_i2^i$**, where each $a_i\in\{0,1\}$. The range is the [integers](../../../../../integer-split.md) from $0$ through $2^n-1$; a leading one contributes positive weight $2^{n-1}$, not a sign.
