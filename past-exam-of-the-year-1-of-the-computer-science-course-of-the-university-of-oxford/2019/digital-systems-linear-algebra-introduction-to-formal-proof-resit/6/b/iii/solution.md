<h1 id="6/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The supplied factors give $Q^Tb=(0,2)^T$. [Back substitution](../../../../../../back-substitution.md) in $Rx=Q^Tb$ yields $2y=2$ and $10x+4y=0$, so

$$
\boxed{x=-2/5,\qquad y=1.}
$$

Multiplying by the original [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) gives $(1,1,1,1)^T$, so this least-squares minimizer has residual zero: this particular overdetermined system is exactly consistent. [Full column rank](../../../../../../full-column-rank.md) still guarantees uniqueness of the minimizer.
