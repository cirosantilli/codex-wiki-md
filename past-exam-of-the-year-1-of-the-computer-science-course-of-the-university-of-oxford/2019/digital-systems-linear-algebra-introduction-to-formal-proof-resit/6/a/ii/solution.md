<h1 id="6/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Set $y=P^Tx$. Because $P$ is an [orthogonal matrix](../../../../../../orthogonal-matrix.md), $x=Py$ and $x=0$ exactly when $y=0$. The preceding diagonalization gives

$$
x^TAx=y^TDy=y_1^2+11y_2^2\ge y_1^2+y_2^2=\|x\|_2^2.
$$

Consequently **$x^TAx\ge0$, with equality only when $x=0$**. The [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) is a [symmetric positive-definite matrix](../../../../../../symmetric-positive-definite-matrix.md), not merely positive semidefinite. In the original coordinates the same identity is

$$
x^TAx=\frac{(3x_1-x_2)^2+11(x_1+3x_2)^2}{10},
$$

which also makes positivity transparent.
