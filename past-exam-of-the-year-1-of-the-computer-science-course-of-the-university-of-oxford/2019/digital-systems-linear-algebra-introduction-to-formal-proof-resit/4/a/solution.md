<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Subtract the first vertex from the other two, giving side [vectors](../../../../../vector.md) $(2,1)$ and $(3,4)$. Their [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) is $2\cdot4-3\cdot1=5$, the signed area of the corresponding [parallelogram](../../../../../parallelogram.md). The [triangle](../../../../../triangle.md) occupies half that area, hence **its area is $5/2$**. Taking the absolute value would remove any sign change caused by reversing the vertex order. This is the [determinant formula for triangle area](../../../../../determinant-formula-for-triangle-area.md).
