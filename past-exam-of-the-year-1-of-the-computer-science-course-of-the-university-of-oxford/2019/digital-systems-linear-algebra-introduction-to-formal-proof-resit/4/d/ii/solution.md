↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**True.** Using the same [transpose](../../../../../../transpose.md) identity and $X^T=-X$ gives

$$
(X^{-1})^T=(X^T)^{-1}=(-X)^{-1}=-X^{-1}.
$$

The final equality follows by multiplying $-X$ and $-X^{-1}$ in either order and obtaining the identity. Thus the inverse is a [skew-symmetric matrix](../../../../../../skew-symmetric-matrix.md) as well. The invertibility hypothesis is retained throughout.
