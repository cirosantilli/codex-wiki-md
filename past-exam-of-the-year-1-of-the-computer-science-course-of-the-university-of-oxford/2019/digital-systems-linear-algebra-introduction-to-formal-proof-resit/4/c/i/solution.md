<h1 id="4/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

If $A$ is $m\times n$, then $Y$ is $n\times m$. The equation $AY=I_m$ makes every standard basis [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) of $\mathbb R^m$ a linear combination of the columns of $A$: take the corresponding column of $Y$ as coefficients. Hence the [column space](../../../../../../column-space.md) is all of $\mathbb R^m$, giving **$\operatorname{rank}(A)=m$**. In particular, $n\ge m$ and $A$ has [full row rank](../../../../../../full-row-rank.md).
