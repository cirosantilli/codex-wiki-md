<h1 id="4/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**The system is consistent for every $b\in\mathbb R^m$:** take $x=Yb$, since $A(Yb)=(AY)b=b$. Thus $Y$ is a [right inverse](../../../../../../right-inverse.md) giving an explicit solution for every right-hand side.

All solutions are $Yb+u$ with $u$ in the [kernel of a linear map](../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) represented by $A$. The [rank-nullity theorem](../../../../../../rank-nullity-theorem.md) gives kernel dimension $n-m$; the solution is unique when $n=m$, and is nonunique when $n>m$.
