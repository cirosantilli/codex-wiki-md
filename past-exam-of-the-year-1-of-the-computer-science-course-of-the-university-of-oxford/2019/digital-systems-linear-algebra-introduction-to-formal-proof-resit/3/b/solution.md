<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The lower network pulls the output low exactly when either series branch conducts. Therefore the [Boolean function](../../../../../boolean-function.md) is

$$
\boxed{z=\neg(ab\lor cd)=(\neg a\lor\neg b)\land(\neg c\lor\neg d).}
$$

The inversion is important: $ab\lor cd$ describes connection to the low rail, not a high output. The [pull-up network](../../../../../pull-up-network.md) found in part a implements the complementary high condition.
