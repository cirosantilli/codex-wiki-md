<h1 id="5/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The two square products can have different sizes, but their [matrix traces](../../../../../matrix-trace.md) are both the same finite double sum:

$$
\operatorname{tr}(XY)=\sum_{i=1}^{l}(XY)_{ii}
=\sum_{i=1}^{l}\sum_{j=1}^{k}X_{ij}Y_{ji}
=\sum_{j=1}^{k}\sum_{i=1}^{l}Y_{ji}X_{ij}
=\operatorname{tr}(YX).
$$

Interchanging two finite sums and commuting their scalar factors is valid over the real numbers. No commutation of the [matrix](../../../../../matrix.md) products themselves is asserted. This proves the [cyclic property of the trace](../../../../../cyclic-property-of-the-trace.md) in the rectangular-factor case.
