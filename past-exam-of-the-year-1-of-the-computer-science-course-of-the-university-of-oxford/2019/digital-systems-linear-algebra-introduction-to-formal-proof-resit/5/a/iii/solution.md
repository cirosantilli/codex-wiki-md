↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**True.** A square [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) with [matrix rank](../../../../../../matrix-rank.md) $n$ is invertible, and its [transpose](../../../../../../transpose.md) has the same rank. Applying the [rank-nullity theorem](../../../../../../rank-nullity-theorem.md) to both $A$ and $A^T$ gives nullity zero in each case, so both kernels are $\{0\}$.
