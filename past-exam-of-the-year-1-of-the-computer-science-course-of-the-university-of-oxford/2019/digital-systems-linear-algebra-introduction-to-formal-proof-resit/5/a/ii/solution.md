↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**False.** Take $A=(1,0)^T$, an $m=2$, $n=1$ [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) of [matrix rank](../../../../../../matrix-rank.md) one, and $b=(0,1)^T$. Then $Ax=(x,0)^T$ never equals $b$. [Full column rank](../../../../../../full-column-rank.md) implies injectivity, not surjectivity onto a larger codomain.
