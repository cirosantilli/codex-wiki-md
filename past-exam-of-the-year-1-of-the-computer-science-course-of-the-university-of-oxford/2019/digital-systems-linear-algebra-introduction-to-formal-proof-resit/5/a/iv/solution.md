**False.** Triviality of $\mathcal N(A)$ gives $r=n$, while triviality of $\mathcal N(A^T)$ gives $r=m$ because [transpose](../../../../../../transpose.md) preserves [matrix rank](../../../../../../matrix-rank.md). Both conditions therefore force $m=n$, contradicting the stated unequal dimensions.
