<h1 id="6/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Since $B$ is [upper triangular](../../../../../../upper-triangular-matrix.md), its [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are its diagonal entries. Equivalently, its [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) is $\lambda(\lambda-3/8)$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\lambda_1=0,\qquad\lambda_2=3/8.}
$$
