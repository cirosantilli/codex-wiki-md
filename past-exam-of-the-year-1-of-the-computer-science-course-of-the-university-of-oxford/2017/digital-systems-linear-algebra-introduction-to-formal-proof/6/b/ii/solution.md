<h1 id="6/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

As $x_0=0$, solve $(D-L)x_1=b$. Its first row gives $4(x_1)_1=1$; its second gives $(x_1)_1+2(x_1)_2=-1$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{x_1=\begin{pmatrix}1/4\\-5/8\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

The second component uses the new first component $1/4$, distinguishing [Gauss-Seidel iteration](../../../../../../gauss-seidel-method.md) from [Jacobi iteration](../../../../../../jacobi-method.md).
