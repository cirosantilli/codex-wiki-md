<h1 id="5/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Reflection in $x=0$ sends $(x,y)$ to $(-x,y)$. Therefore its homogeneous-coordinate matrix is

$$
\boxed{J=\begin{pmatrix}-1&0&0\\0&1&0\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

Its square is the identity, as required for applying the same [reflection](../../../../../../reflection-mathematics.md) twice.
