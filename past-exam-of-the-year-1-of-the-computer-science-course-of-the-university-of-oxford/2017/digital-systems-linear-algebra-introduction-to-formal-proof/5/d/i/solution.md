↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

With column-vector [homogeneous coordinates](../../../../../../homogeneous-coordinate.md), the translation matrix is

$$
\boxed{T(a,b)=\begin{pmatrix}1&0&a\\0&1&b\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

It maps $(x,y,1)^T$ to $(x+a,y+b,1)^T$. The final coordinate incorporates the constant displacement into [matrix multiplication](../../../../../../matrix-multiplication.md).
