<h1 id="7/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

[Proof by contradiction](../../../../../../reductio-ad-absurdum.md) is the derived rule

$$
\boxed{\frac{\Gamma,\neg A\vdash\bot}{\Gamma\vdash A}\quad\mathrm{RAA}.}
$$

Given the subproof under $\neg A$, first discharge it by [negation introduction](../../../../../../negation-introduction-rule.md) to get $\Gamma\vdash\neg\neg A$. Apply [double-negation elimination](../../../../../../double-negation-elimination.md) to obtain $A$. This justification uses the stated classical rules; unrestricted RAA would not follow from intuitionistic rules alone.
