↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

An **[input-output-bound process](../../../../../i-o-bound-process.md)** spends much of its time waiting for [input-output](../../../../../input-output.md), often with short [CPU bursts](../../../../../cpu-burst.md) between waits. A **[CPU-bound process](../../../../../cpu-bound-process.md)** spends most of its time doing useful [CPU](../../../../../central-processing-unit.md) computation, with comparatively little blocking. These descriptions concern the bottleneck in the workload; an I/O-bound process still needs CPU time.
