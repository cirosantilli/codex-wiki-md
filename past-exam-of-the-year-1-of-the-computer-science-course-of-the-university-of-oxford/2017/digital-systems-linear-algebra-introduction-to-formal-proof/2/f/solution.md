<h1 id="2/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

The preemptive policy improves B's [scheduling turnaround time](../../../../../scheduling-turnaround-time.md) from 17 to 10 ms and the mean from 16.5 to 14 ms. A finishes later, at 18 rather than 16 ms, and the total completion time rises from 17 to 18 ms. Thus **preemption improves mean turnaround here; nonpreemption gives the shorter makespan here**.

The result illustrates different scheduling objectives, not a universal winner. These calculations assume negligible [context switch](../../../../../context-switch.md) overhead; charging for the additional switches could change the comparison. Both policies perform the same 15 ms of useful [CPU](../../../../../central-processing-unit.md) work.
