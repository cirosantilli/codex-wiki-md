<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Writing the columns as $a_1,a_2,a_3$, [matrix multiplication](../../../../../matrix-multiplication.md) gives

$$
c=Ay=y_1a_1+y_2a_2+y_3a_3.
$$

The [column space](../../../../../column-space.md) is the [linear span](../../../../../linear-span.md) of these columns, so this particular linear combination belongs to it. The assertion holds for every vector of coefficients $y$.
