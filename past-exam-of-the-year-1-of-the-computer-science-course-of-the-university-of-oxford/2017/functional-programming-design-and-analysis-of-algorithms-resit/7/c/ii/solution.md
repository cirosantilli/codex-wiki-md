**False.** The same ordered traversal gives a path $u\to x\to v$, but

$$
\boxed{d(v)=4>3=f(u).}
$$

A path from $u$ can pass through an already discovered ancestor, whose remaining edges are scanned only after $u$ finishes. A [reachability](../../../../../../reachability-in-a-graph.md) statement alone therefore does not impose the claimed finishing-time bound.
