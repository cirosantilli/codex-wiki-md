↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

**Bellman–Ford permits negative edge weights**, and can detect a reachable negative-weight cycle. The usual settled-label [Dijkstra's algorithm](../../../../../dijkstra-algorithm.md) requires nonnegative weights for its correctness argument. Negative edges are not themselves a problem for Bellman–Ford when no relevant negative cycle exists.
