# Functional programming, design and analysis of algorithms

↑ **Parent:** [2017](split.md)

This is the 13 June 2017 preliminary examination. Questions 1–4 concern [functional programming](../../functional-programming.md); Questions 5–8 concern [algorithm design](../../algorithm-design.md) and [analysis of algorithms](../../analysis-of-algorithms.md). All 35 printed subparts are answered. Source paper: [released examination archive](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/past_exam_papers_for_1st_year_of/response/1398416/attach/2/Fakour%20Zip%20Final%201.zip?cookie_passthrough=1), member `a10097w1-1.pdf`.

The [Haskell](../../haskell.md) definitions use separate question namespaces. For Question 1, hide the locally defined names with `import Prelude hiding ((.), zipWith, uncurry)`; for Question 4 use `import Prelude hiding (scanl)`. Alternative `solve` definitions are alternatives, not simultaneous duplicate declarations. Assume finite total puzzle inputs; infinite streams and partial values are treated explicitly where relevant. Operation counts separate numeric bit costs from list and comparison steps.[§5.6.2, hiding Prelude names; Chapter 9, Standard Prelude](../../haskell-2010-language-report.md)
