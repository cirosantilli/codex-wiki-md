↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [spanning tree](../../../../../spanning-tree.md) is a connected acyclic subgraph containing every vertex of the original undirected graph. For a graph with $|V|$ vertices it has $|V|-1$ edges. A [minimum spanning tree](../../../../../minimum-spanning-tree.md) minimizes the sum of its [edge weights](../../../../../edge-weight.md) over all spanning trees:

$$
\boxed{T\in\underset{\text{spanning trees }S}{\operatorname{argmin}}\sum_{e\in S}w(e).}
$$

Connectedness guarantees existence for a finite graph; equal weights can permit several minimizers.
