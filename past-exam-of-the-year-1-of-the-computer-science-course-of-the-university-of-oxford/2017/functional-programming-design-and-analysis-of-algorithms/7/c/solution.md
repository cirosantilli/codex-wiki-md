↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

It is a [greedy algorithm](../../../../../greedy-algorithm.md) because it commits at every step to the cheapest remaining edge that can be added without creating a [graph cycle](../../../../../cycle-in-a-graph.md), without revising earlier choices. The [minimum spanning tree cut property](../../../../../minimum-spanning-tree-cut-property.md) explains why these locally cheapest admissible choices can be extended to an optimal spanning tree.
