The most general [type signature](../../../../../type-signature.md) is **`scanl :: (b -> a -> b) -> b -> [a] -> [b]`**. The [left fold](../../../../../left-fold.md) step consumes an accumulator of type `b` and an input of type `a`, returning the next accumulator. A [left scan](../../../../../left-scan.md) retains all these accumulator values, including the initial one.
