The **[observer pattern](../../../../../observer-pattern.md) is a [behavioral design pattern](../../../../../behavioral-pattern.md)** defining a one-to-many dependency. When a subject changes relevant state, it notifies registered observers so they can update. The subject uses an observer interface rather than hard-coding each concrete dependent, allowing subscriptions to vary.
