<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For $i\geq1$, $k\geq1$, and integer $r\geq1$, all terms are nonnegative, so squaring preserves the inequalities:

$$
\boxed{i=\lfloor k\sqrt r\rfloor\iff i\leq k\sqrt r<i+1\iff i^2\leq rk^2<(i+1)^2.}
$$

This gives [integer-square membership in a square-root Beatty sequence](../../../../../integer-square-membership-in-a-square-root-beatty-sequence.md) without evaluating a floating-point square root. Since $\sqrt r\geq1$, any witnessing $k$ satisfies $k\leq i$.
