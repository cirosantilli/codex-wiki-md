↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The outermost braces form a [singleton set](../../../../../../singleton-mathematics.md), regardless of how its one element is nested. Its [cardinality](../../../../../../cardinality.md) is therefore **$1$**. A set containing the [empty set](../../../../../../empty-set.md) is different from the empty set itself.
