<h1 id="1/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [singleton set](../../../../../../singleton-mathematics.md) $\{\varnothing\}$ has cardinality one, so its [power set](../../../../../../power-set.md) has cardinality two. Taking the [Cartesian product](../../../../../../cartesian-product.md) with the singleton $\{\varnothing\}$ leaves that cardinality equal to two. The outer power set therefore has **$2^2=4$** elements.
