<h1 id="1/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A [Cartesian product](../../../../../../cartesian-product.md) with the [empty set](../../../../../../empty-set.md) is empty: there is no possible second coordinate. Thus the requested [cardinality](../../../../../../cardinality.md) is **$0$**, independently of the first factor's [power set](../../../../../../power-set.md).
