↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) of this [constant-coefficient differential equation](../../../../../constant-coefficient-differential-equation.md) is $r^2-r-2=(r-2)(r+1)$. Its distinct roots give

$$
\boxed{y(x)=C_1e^{2x}+C_2e^{-x}.}
$$
