<h1 id="2/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use [modular arithmetic](../../../../../../modular-arithmetic-split.md) modulo eight: $801\equiv1$, $32\cdot19\equiv0$, and $29\cdot13\equiv5\cdot5\equiv1$. The squared difference is consequently congruent to one, giving the remainder

$$
\boxed{2.}
$$
