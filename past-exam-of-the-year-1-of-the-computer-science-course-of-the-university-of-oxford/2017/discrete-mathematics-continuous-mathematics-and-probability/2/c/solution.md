<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [Euclidean algorithm](../../../../../euclidean-algorithm.md) gives

$$
1394=4(306)+170,\quad306=170+136,\quad170=136+34,\quad136=4(34).
$$

Thus the [greatest common divisor](../../../../../greatest-common-divisor.md) is $34$. Back-substitution, as in the [extended Euclidean algorithm](../../../../../extended-euclidean-algorithm.md), gives

$$
34=170-136=2(170)-306=2(1394)-9(306).
$$

Hence **$\gcd(306,1394)=34$, with $x=2$ and $y=-9$**.
