↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Because $T$ and $U$ are [independent random variables](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md), their product's [expected value](../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) factors:

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[TU]=\mathbb ET\,\mathbb EU=1.}
$$

The original independence assumption is essential to this factorization.
