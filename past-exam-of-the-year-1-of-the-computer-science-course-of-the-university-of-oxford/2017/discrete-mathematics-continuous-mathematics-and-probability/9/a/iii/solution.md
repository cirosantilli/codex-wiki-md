<h1 id="9/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For the overlapping products, factor using the independence of $T,U,V$:

$$
\operatorname{Cov}(TU,UV)=\mathbb E[TU^2V]-\mathbb E[TU]\mathbb E[UV]
=1\cdot2\cdot1-1\cdot1=1.
$$

This is the [covariance of overlapping independent products](../../../../../../covariance-of-overlapping-independent-products.md): the shared $U$ contributes its second moment. In contrast, $TU$ and $VW$ use disjoint independent factors and are themselves independent, giving

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Cov}(TU,UV)=1,\qquad\operatorname{Cov}(TU,VW)=0.}
$$
