<h1 id="9/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The rate-one [exponential distribution](../../../../../../exponential-distribution.md) has density $e^{-t}$ for $t\geq0$. [Integration by parts](../../../../../../integration-by-parts.md) gives $\mathbb ET=1$ and $\mathbb E[T^2]=2$, so

$$
\boxed{\mathbb ET=1,\qquad\operatorname{Var}(T)=2-1^2=1.}
$$
