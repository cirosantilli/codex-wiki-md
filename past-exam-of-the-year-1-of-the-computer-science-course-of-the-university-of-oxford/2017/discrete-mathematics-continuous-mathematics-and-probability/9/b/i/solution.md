<h1 id="9/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Assume finite second moments and write $Z_i=X_i-\mathbb EX_i$. Expanding the squared centered sum gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Var}(X_1+X_2)
&=\mathbb E[(Z_1+Z_2)^2]\\
&=\mathbb E[Z_1^2]+\mathbb E[Z_2^2]+2\mathbb E[Z_1Z_2]\\
&=\operatorname{Var}(X_1)+\operatorname{Var}(X_2)+2\operatorname{Cov}(X_1,X_2).
\end{aligned}
$$

This [variance of a sum](../../../../../../variance-of-a-sum.md) identity does not require independence; the [covariance](../../../../../../covariance.md) term records the contribution that would otherwise be lost.
