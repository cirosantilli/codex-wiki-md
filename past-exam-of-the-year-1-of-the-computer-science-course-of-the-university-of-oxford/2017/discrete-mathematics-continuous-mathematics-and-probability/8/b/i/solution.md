<h1 id="8/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Starting at $X$, the two possible two-step returns are $X\to X\to X$ and $X\to Y\to X$. They are [disjoint events](../../../../../../disjoint-events.md), with probabilities $1/4$ and $3/16$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{p_2=\frac14+\frac3{16}=\frac7{16}.}
$$

Each path probability uses the specified [Markov property](../../../../../../markov-property.md) to multiply its successive transition probabilities.
