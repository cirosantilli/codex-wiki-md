↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For a finite or countable measurable partition, the [law of total probability](../../../../../../law-of-total-probability.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(A)=\sum_{i:\,\mathbb P(B_i)>0}\mathbb P(A\mid B_i)\mathbb P(B_i).}
$$

Indeed, $A$ is the disjoint union of $A\cap B_i$, and the [conditional probability](../../../../../../conditional-probability.md) formula gives each positive-probability term. Zero-probability cells contribute zero; their conditionals need not be defined by a ratio. If all cells have positive probability, the sum simply runs over every $i\in I$.
