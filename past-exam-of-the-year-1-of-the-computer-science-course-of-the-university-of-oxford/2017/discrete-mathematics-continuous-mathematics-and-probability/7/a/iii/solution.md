<h1 id="7/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [expected value](../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) is the derivative of the [probability generating function](../../../../../../probability-generating-function.md) at one. The expression from part (ii) gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbb EX=G_X'(1)=\frac p{p^2}=\frac1p.}
$$

Its finiteness also follows directly from the exponentially decaying geometric probabilities.
