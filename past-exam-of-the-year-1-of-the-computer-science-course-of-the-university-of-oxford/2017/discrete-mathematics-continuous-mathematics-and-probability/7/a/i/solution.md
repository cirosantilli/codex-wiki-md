↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The variable has positive-integer support, so $\mathbb P(X>0)=1$. Subtract consecutive values of the [survival function](../../../../../../survival-function.md):

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(X=k)=\mathbb P(X>k-1)-\mathbb P(X>k)=p(1-p)^{k-1},\quad k\geq1.}
$$

This is the positive-support convention for the [geometric distribution](../../../../../../geometric-distribution.md).
