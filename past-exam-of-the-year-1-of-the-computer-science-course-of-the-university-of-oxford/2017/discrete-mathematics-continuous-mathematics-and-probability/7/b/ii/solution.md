<h1 id="7/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $0\leq s<1$, differentiate the [probability generating function](../../../../../../probability-generating-function.md) term by term and multiply by $s/\mu$:

$$
\boxed{G_{\widetilde Y}(s)=\sum_{k\geq1}\frac{kp_k}{\mu}s^k
=\frac s\mu\sum_{k\geq1}kp_ks^{k-1}=\frac s\mu G_Y'(s).}
$$

This is the [size-biased probability generating function](../../../../../../size-biased-probability-generating-function.md) identity. At $s=1$, the finite-mean assumption gives the limiting value one on both sides; at zero, positive support gives value zero.
