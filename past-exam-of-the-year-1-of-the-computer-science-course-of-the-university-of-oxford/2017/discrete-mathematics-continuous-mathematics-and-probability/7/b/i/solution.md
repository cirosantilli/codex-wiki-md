<h1 id="7/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Since $Y\geq1$, its finite [expected value](../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) satisfies $1\leq\mu<\infty$. Every $q_k=kp_k/\mu$ is nonnegative and

$$
\sum_{k\geq1}q_k=\frac1\mu\sum_{k\geq1}kp_k=\frac{\mathbb EY}{\mu}=1.
$$

Thus these values are a [probability mass function](../../../../../../probability-mass-function.md). They define the [size-biased distribution](../../../../../../size-biased-distribution.md) of $Y$: larger values receive proportionally more weight.
