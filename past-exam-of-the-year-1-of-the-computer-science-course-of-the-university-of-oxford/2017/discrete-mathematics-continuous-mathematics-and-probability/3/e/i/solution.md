↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [preorder](../../../../../../preorder.md) is **reflexive and transitive**: $xRx$ for every element, and $xRy,yRz$ imply $xRz$. A [partial order](../../../../../../partially-ordered-set.md) additionally requires **[antisymmetry](../../../../../../antisymmetry.md)**: $xRy$ and $yRx$ imply $x=y$. It need not compare every pair of distinct elements.
