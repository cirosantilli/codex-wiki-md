↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Over the underlying [field](../../../../../field.md) $F$, the [row space](../../../../../row-space.md) is the [linear span](../../../../../linear-span.md) of the row [vectors](../../../../../vector.md) of $A$, a [vector subspace](../../../../../vector-subspace.md) of $F^n$. The [column space](../../../../../column-space.md) is the [linear span](../../../../../linear-span.md) of its column [vectors](../../../../../vector.md), a [vector subspace](../../../../../vector-subspace.md) of $F^m$. The [null space](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) is

$$
\ker A=\{x\in F^n:Ax=0\},
$$

a [vector subspace](../../../../../vector-subspace.md) of the domain. The [column space](../../../../../column-space.md) is the [image of a linear map](../../../../../image-of-a-linear-map.md) represented by $A$; the [null space](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) is its [kernel](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md).
