The [rank](../../../../../../rank-one-quadratic-form.md) of $A$ is **the dimension of its [column space](../../../../../../column-space.md)**, equivalently the dimension of its [row space](../../../../../../row-space.md) or the number of [pivot columns](../../../../../../pivot-column.md) in a row-echelon form. For an $n\times n$ matrix it lies between zero and $n$.
