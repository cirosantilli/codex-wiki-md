<h1 id="4/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Each [elementary matrix](../../../../../../elementary-matrix.md) is obtained by applying one invertible elementary row operation to the [identity matrix](../../../../../../identity-matrix.md). Its inverse is the elementary matrix for the reverse operation. A product of [elementary matrices](../../../../../../elementary-matrix.md) is therefore invertible and has full [rank](../../../../../../rank-one-quadratic-form.md) $n$.

Conversely, if $\operatorname{rank}A=n$, [Gaussian elimination](../../../../../../gaussian-elimination.md) has a pivot in every column and reduces $A$ to the identity. Writing the successive row operations as left multiplication gives $E_r\cdots E_1A=I$. Hence $A=E_1^{-1}\cdots E_r^{-1}$, a product of [elementary matrices](../../../../../../elementary-matrix.md). This proves both directions of the [elementary-matrix factorization theorem](../../../../../../elementary-matrix-factorization-theorem.md).
