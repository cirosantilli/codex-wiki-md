<h1 id="4/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [pivot columns](../../../../../../pivot-column.md) of the reduced matrix in part (i) are columns one and two. Select those columns of the original matrix, giving the [column-space basis](../../../../../../column-space-basis.md)

$$
\boxed{\left\{\begin{pmatrix}-3\\-1\\2\end{pmatrix},\begin{pmatrix}4\\2\\1\end{pmatrix}\right\}.}
$$

They are independent, for example their first two entries have determinant $-2\ne0$. The remaining original column is the first plus twice the second. Taking the reduced matrix's columns instead would generally give the wrong [column space](../../../../../../column-space.md).
