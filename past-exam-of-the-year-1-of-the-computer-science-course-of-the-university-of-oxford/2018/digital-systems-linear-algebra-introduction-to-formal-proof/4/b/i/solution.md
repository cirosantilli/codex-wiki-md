↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

[Gaussian elimination](../../../../../../gaussian-elimination.md) gives

$$
\operatorname{rref}(A)=\begin{pmatrix}1&0&1\\0&1&2\\0&0&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Elementary row operations preserve the [row space](../../../../../../row-space.md), so **$\{(1,0,1),(0,1,2)\}$** is a [basis](../../../../../../basis.md). The two rows are [linearly independent](../../../../../../linear-independence.md) because their first two coordinates form the standard basis of $\mathbb R^2$.
