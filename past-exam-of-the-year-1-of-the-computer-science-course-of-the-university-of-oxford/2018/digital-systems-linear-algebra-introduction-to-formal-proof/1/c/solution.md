↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

With the encoding in part (b), **$y=A$** is a valid [Moore machine](../../../../../moore-machine.md) output equation. Among reachable states, $A=1$ exactly at $S_4$. This uses unused output-table entries as [don't-care conditions](../../../../../don-t-care-term.md). If one instead wants the output to be zero in every unused state, use $y=A\bar B\bar C$; that extra requirement does not change any output from a valid state.
