<h1 id="8/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A fresh variable is an arbitrary [eigenvariable](../../../../../eigenvariable.md) whose value is not constrained by the undischarged assumptions. For [universal introduction](../../../../../universal-quantifier-introduction-rule.md), it must not occur free in those assumptions; otherwise a fact about one constrained object could be incorrectly generalized to all objects. For [existential elimination](../../../../../existential-quantifier-elimination-rule.md), its temporary witness must additionally not occur free in the conclusion or in other undischarged assumptions outside the witness subproof. Choosing a completely unused variable is a sufficient convention. These are the [eigenvariable conditions](../../../../../eigenvariable-condition.md), and concern free occurrences rather than harmless reuse of bound names.
