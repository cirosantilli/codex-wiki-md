<h1 id="6/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A unit vector along the line is $v=(3,4)^T/5$. Reflection fixes its component parallel to $v$ and negates its perpendicular component, so the [reflection matrix](../../../../../../reflection-matrix.md) is

$$
\boxed{H=2vv^T-I=\frac1{25}\begin{pmatrix}-7&24\\24&7\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

Indeed $H(3,4)^T=(3,4)^T$, $H(-4,3)^T=-(-4,3)^T$ and $H^2=I$. These characterize the [orthogonal reflection](../../../../../../reflection-in-a-hyperplane.md) in that line.
