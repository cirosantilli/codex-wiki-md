↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

If $B$ is an invertible [upper triangular matrix](../../../../../../upper-triangular-matrix.md), $B^T$ is invertible and lower triangular. Part (ii) makes $(B^T)^{-1}$ lower triangular, while part (i) identifies it with $(B^{-1})^T$. Transposing gives that **$B^{-1}$ is upper triangular**. This deduction uses both the inverse-transpose identity and the lower-triangular result.
