<h1 id="5/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

A [linear map](../../../../../../linear-map.md) $T:V\to W$ is onto, or [surjective](../../../../../../surjective-function.md), when every $w\in W$ equals $T(v)$ for some $v\in V$; equivalently its [image](../../../../../../image-of-a-function.md) is all of $W$. Here **$T$ is not onto**, because its image has dimension two while its declared codomain $\mathbb R^3$ has dimension three. Indeed every image vector satisfies $x+y-3z=0$, so, for example, $(0,0,1)$ is not attained.
