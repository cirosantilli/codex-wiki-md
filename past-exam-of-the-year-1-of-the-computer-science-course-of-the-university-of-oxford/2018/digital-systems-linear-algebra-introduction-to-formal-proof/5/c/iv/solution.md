↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

A [linear map](../../../../../../linear-map.md) is one-to-one, or [injective](../../../../../../injective-function.md), when equal outputs imply equal inputs. Equivalently its [kernel](../../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) is $\{0\}$: subtracting inputs converts equality of outputs to a zero output. Here **$T$ is not one-to-one**, since its kernel has dimension two. Concretely, $T(-2,-1,1,0)=T(0)=0$ for distinct inputs.
