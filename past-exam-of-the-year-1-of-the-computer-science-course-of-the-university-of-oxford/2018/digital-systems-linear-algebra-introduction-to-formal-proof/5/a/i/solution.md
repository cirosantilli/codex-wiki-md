↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**False.** In the real [vector space](../../../../../../vector-space-split.md) $\mathbb R$, both $\{1\}$ and $\{2\}$ are [bases](../../../../../../basis.md): each is [linearly independent](../../../../../../linear-independence.md) and spans the whole space. A [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) need not be a unique set. What is fixed for a finite-dimensional [vector space](../../../../../../vector-space-split.md) is the number of basis elements, its [dimension](../../../../../../dimension-vector-space.md).
