↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**True.** Existence follows because the [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) spans $V$. If $v=\sum_i a_i s_i=\sum_i b_i s_i$, subtract to get $\sum_i(a_i-b_i)s_i=0$. The basis vectors' [linear independence](../../../../../../linear-independence.md) forces every $a_i-b_i=0$, proving uniqueness. For an infinite [basis](../../../../../../basis.md), both representations have finite support, so the same argument applies to their finite union. These coefficients are the [coordinates relative to a basis](../../../../../../coordinates-relative-to-a-basis.md).
