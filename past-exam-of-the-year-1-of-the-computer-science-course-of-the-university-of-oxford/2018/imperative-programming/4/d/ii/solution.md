↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The successive abstract [sequences](../../../../../../sequence.md) are $[10]$, $[20,10]$, $[20,10,30]$, $[10,30]$, $[10,30,40]$, $[10,30,40,10]$, $[10,30,40]$, $[10,30,40,15]$, and finally $[30,40,15]$. The three removals return $20$, $10$, and $10$, respectively. With the display convention in part (i), **the output is**
```
20
10
10
Queue status: [30, 40, 15]
```
The capacity-four bound is never exceeded. The abstract trace is independent of which physical [array](../../../../../../array-data-structure.md) slots wrap around.
