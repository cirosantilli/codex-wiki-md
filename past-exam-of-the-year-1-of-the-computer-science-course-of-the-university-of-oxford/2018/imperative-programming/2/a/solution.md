[Linear search](../../../../../linear-search.md) examines successive entries until a match or a stopping condition is reached; in the worst case it inspects $n$ entries. [Binary search](../../../../../binary-search.md) uses a [sorted array](../../../../../sorted-array.md) to discard about half of the remaining candidates after each comparison, requiring $O(\log(n+1))$ comparisons. Its logarithmic guarantee also requires constant-cost indexed access, as supplied by an [array](../../../../../array-data-structure.md); the same index operations on a [linked list](../../../../../linked-list.md) would not be constant-cost.
