↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**True.** Delete $e\in T$; the resulting two [connected components](../../../../../../connected-component.md) define a [cut of a graph](../../../../../../cut-graph-theory.md). If a crossing edge $f$ had $w(f)<w(e)$, the exchange $T-e+f$ would be a [spanning tree](../../../../../../spanning-tree.md) of smaller total weight, contradicting the [minimum spanning tree](../../../../../../minimum-spanning-tree.md) assumption. Thus $e$ is a [light edge crossing a cut](../../../../../../light-edge-crossing-a-cut.md) for this fundamental cut. It need not be uniquely light.
