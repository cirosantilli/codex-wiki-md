<h1 id="8/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**True.** A [tree](../../../../../../tree-graph-theory-split.md) has exactly one path between any two [vertices](../../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md). For $e\notin T$, that path between the endpoints of $e$, together with $e$, is a [fundamental cycle](../../../../../../fundamental-cycle.md). Every cycle in $T\cup\{e\}$ must contain $e$, since $T$ itself is [acyclic](../../../../../../forest.md); deleting $e$ from such a cycle leaves that same unique path. Therefore the cycle is unique. Minimality of the spanning tree's weight is not needed for this assertion.
