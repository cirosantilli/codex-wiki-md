A [cut of a graph](../../../../../../cut-graph-theory.md) is a partition $(S,V\setminus S)$ into two nonempty vertex sets. An [edge](../../../../../../edge-of-a-graph.md) crosses it when its endpoints lie on opposite sides. An [edge set respecting a cut](../../../../../../edge-set-respecting-a-cut.md) contains no crossing edge. A [light edge crossing a cut](../../../../../../light-edge-crossing-a-cut.md) has minimum weight among all crossing [edges](../../../../../../edge-of-a-graph.md); there can be several tied light edges. These definitions concern the graph's weighted edges, independently of any particular spanning tree.
