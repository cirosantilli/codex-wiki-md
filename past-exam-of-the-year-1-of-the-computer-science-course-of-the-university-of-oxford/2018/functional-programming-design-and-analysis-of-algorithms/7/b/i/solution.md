**False.** Take two [vertices](../../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md) $u,v$ with both directed [edges](../../../../../../edge-of-a-graph.md), and start [depth-first search](../../../../../../depth-first-search.md) at $v$. The timestamps are $d[v]=1$, $d[u]=2$, $f[u]=3$, $f[v]=4$. There is an [edge](../../../../../../edge-of-a-graph.md), hence a [directed path](../../../../../../directed-path.md), from $u$ to $v$, but $f[u]<f[v]$. Reaching a gray ancestor does not make the ancestor finish first.
