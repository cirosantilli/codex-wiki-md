<h1 id="6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The critical points of $f''$ occur where $f'''=0$, namely $-1,0,1$. Its sign on the four intervals separated by these points is, in order, positive, negative, positive, negative. Thus $f''$ has local maxima at $\pm1$ and a local minimum at zero. Their values are

$$
f''(-1)=f''(1)=\frac12,\qquad f''(0)=-2.
$$

Since $f''(x)\to0$ at both ends of the real line, these are also **the global maximum $1/2$ and the global minimum $-2$**. The sign analysis establishes extremality; merely solving $f'''=0$ would not.
