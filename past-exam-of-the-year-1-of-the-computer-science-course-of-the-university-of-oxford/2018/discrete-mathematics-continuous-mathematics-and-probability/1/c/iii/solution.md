↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**False.** Use the same two [equivalence relations](../../../../../../equivalence-relation.md) as in part (ii). They are both [symmetric relations](../../../../../../symmetric-relation.md). The composite contains $1S3$, since $1R_12$ and $2R_23$. It does not contain $3S1$, as shown there. Hence [composition of relations](../../../../../../composition-of-relations.md) need not preserve [symmetry of a relation](../../../../../../symmetric-relation.md), even when both factors are also [reflexive](../../../../../../reflexive-relation.md) and [transitive](../../../../../../transitive-relation.md).
