**True.** Fix $a\in A$. [Reflexivity](../../../../../../reflexive-relation.md) supplies $aR_1a$ and $aR_2a$. In the definition of [composition of relations](../../../../../../composition-of-relations.md), choose the intermediate element to be $a$ itself. Then $a(R_2\circ R_1)a$. This holds for every $a\in A$, so the composite [binary relation](../../../../../../binary-relation.md) is [reflexive](../../../../../../reflexive-relation.md).
