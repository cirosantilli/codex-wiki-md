↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**True.** The two operations retain complementary membership information. For $x\in C$, equality of the [intersections](../../../../../../set-intersection.md) makes $x\in A$ equivalent to $x\in B$. For $x\notin C$, equality of the [unions](../../../../../../set-union.md) makes $x\in A$ equivalent to $x\in B$. These cases exhaust every possible element. [Set extensionality](../../../../../../axiom-of-extensionality.md) therefore gives $A=B$. Equivalently, reconstruct either [set](../../../../../../set-split.md) from

$$
A=(A\cap C)\cup\bigl((A\cup C)\setminus C\bigr).
$$

Substituting the two given equalities into this expression gives $B$.
