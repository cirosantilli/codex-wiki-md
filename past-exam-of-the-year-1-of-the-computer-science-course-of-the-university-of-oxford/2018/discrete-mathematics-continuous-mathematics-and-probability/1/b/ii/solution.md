**False.** Take the [sets](../../../../../../set-split.md) $A=\{1\}$, $B=\varnothing$ and $C=\{1\}$. Both [unions](../../../../../../set-union.md) with $C$ equal $\{1\}$, although $A\ne B$. Taking a [union](../../../../../../set-union.md) with $C$ conceals which of its elements belonged to the original [set](../../../../../../set-split.md).
