**True.** For every [integer](../../../../../../integer-split.md) $n\geq1$, $n^{3/2}\leq n^2$ and $1\leq n^2$, so

$$
0\leq3n^2+5n^{3/2}+6\leq(3+5+6)n^2=14n^2.
$$

Thus $C=14$ and $N=1$ witness the required [Big O notation](../../../../../../big-o-notation.md) bound.
