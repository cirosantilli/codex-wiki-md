↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**False.** Set $r_n=n!/2^n$. The ratio of successive terms is

$$
\frac{r_{n+1}}{r_n}=\frac{n+1}{2}\geq2\qquad(n\geq3).
$$

Consequently $r_n\geq r_3\,2^{n-3}$ for $n\geq3$, which is unbounded. No constant can bound $n!$ by that constant times $2^n$ for all sufficiently large $n$. The [factorial](../../../../../../factorial.md) therefore fails the proposed [Big O notation](../../../../../../big-o-notation.md) bound, without needing [Stirling's formula](../../../../../../stirling-formula.md).
