↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

**False.** The [sum of squares of the first n positive integers](../../../../../../square-pyramidal-number.md) gives

$$
\frac1{n^2}\sum_{k=1}^n k^2=\frac{n(n+1)(2n+1)}{6n^2}\geq\frac n3.
$$

This ratio is unbounded, so it cannot satisfy the constant bound required by [Big O notation](../../../../../../big-o-notation.md). In fact the [sum](../../../../../../sum.md) has order $n^3$, as its leading term also shows.
