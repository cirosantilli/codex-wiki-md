<h1 id="5/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Substituting $y=-x$ cancels both the cubic and linear terms, leaving $f(x,-x)=0$ for every real $x$. Thus **the constrained maximum and minimum are both zero**, attained everywhere on the constraint line. The feasible [set](../../../../../set-split.md) is unbounded, but the restricted [function](../../../../../function-split.md) is constant; compactness is not needed here.
