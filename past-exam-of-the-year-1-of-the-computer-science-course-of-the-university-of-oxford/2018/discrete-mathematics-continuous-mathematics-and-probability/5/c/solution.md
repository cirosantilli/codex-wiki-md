<h1 id="5/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [gradient](../../../../../gradient.md) vanishes exactly when $x^2/4-1=0$ and $y^2/4-1=0$, giving the four points $(\pm2,\pm2)$. The [Hessian matrix](../../../../../hessian-matrix.md) is $\operatorname{diag}(x/2,y/2)$. It is positive definite at $(2,2)$, negative definite at $(-2,-2)$, and indefinite at the two mixed-sign points. Thus **$(2,2)$ is a strict local minimum, $(-2,-2)$ a strict local maximum, and $(2,-2),(-2,2)$ are [saddle points](../../../../../saddle-point.md)** by the [second-derivative test](../../../../../second-derivative-test.md). Their respective [function](../../../../../function-split.md) values are $-8/3$, $8/3$, and zero at both [saddle points](../../../../../saddle-point.md).
