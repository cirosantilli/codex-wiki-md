↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Independence gives the [probability generating function](../../../../../../probability-generating-function.md) of the sum as the product:

$$
G_{X+Y}(s)=\mathbb E[s^Xs^Y]=G_X(s)G_Y(s)=e^{(\mu+\lambda)(s-1)}.
$$

This is the [probability generating function](../../../../../../probability-generating-function.md) of the [Poisson distribution](../../../../../../poisson-distribution.md) with parameter $\mu+\lambda$. Uniqueness of the coefficients of a [power series](../../../../../../power-series.md) therefore gives **$X+Y\sim\operatorname{Poisson}(\mu+\lambda)$**. The product rule for these generating functions requires the stated independence.
