<h1 id="9/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The conditional mean of a [Poisson distribution](../../../../../../poisson-distribution.md) equals its parameter, so $\mathbb E[Z\mid X]=X$. The [law of total expectation](../../../../../../law-of-total-expectation.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E Z=\mathbb E X=\mu.}
$$

As a check, differentiating the [probability generating function](../../../../../../probability-generating-function.md) in part (iii) at one also gives $G'_Z(1)=\mu$.
