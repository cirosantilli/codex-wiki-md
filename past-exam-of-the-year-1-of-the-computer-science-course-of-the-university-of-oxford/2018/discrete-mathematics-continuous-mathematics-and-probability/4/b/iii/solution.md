↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Here the [integrating factor](../../../../../../integrating-factor.md) is $e^{2\sin x}$, since its [logarithmic derivative](../../../../../../logarithmic-derivative.md) is $2\cos x$. Multiplying the [differential equation](../../../../../../differential-equation-split.md) by it cancels the right-hand exponential and yields $(e^{2\sin x}y)'=1$. Hence

$$
\boxed{y(x)=(x+C)e^{-2\sin x},\qquad C\in\mathbb R.}
$$

This formula is actually defined for every real $x$.
