<h1 id="4/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [integrating factor](../../../../../../integrating-factor.md) is $e^{2x}$, so the [differential equation](../../../../../../differential-equation-split.md) becomes $(e^{2x}y)'=1$. Integration gives

$$
\boxed{y(x)=(x+C)e^{-2x},\qquad C\in\mathbb R.}
$$

The factor $x$ in the [particular solution](../../../../../../particular-solution.md) accounts for the forcing term being proportional to the [homogeneous solution](../../../../../../homogeneous-solution.md).
