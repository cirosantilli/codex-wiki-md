<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Choose the representative $1\leq a\leq p-1$. Since $p$ is [prime](../../../../../prime-number-split.md) and does not divide $a$, their [greatest common divisor](../../../../../greatest-common-divisor.md) is one. The [extended Euclidean algorithm](../../../../../extended-euclidean-algorithm.md) gives the [Bezout identity](../../../../../bezout-s-identity.md)

$$
ax+py=1
$$

for some [integers](../../../../../integer-split.md) $x,y$. Reducing modulo $p$ gives $ax\equiv1\pmod p$, so **the residue class of $x$ is a [multiplicative inverse](../../../../../multiplicative-inverse.md) of $a$**. The argument uses the nonzero condition to exclude the only residue with [greatest common divisor](../../../../../greatest-common-divisor.md) $p$.
