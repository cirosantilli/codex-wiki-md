<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use [modular exponentiation](../../../../../modular-exponentiation.md) by [repeated squaring](../../../../../exponentiation-by-squaring.md):

$$
27\equiv6\pmod7,\quad27^2\equiv6^2\equiv1\pmod7,\quad27^4\equiv27^8\equiv27^{16}\equiv1\pmod7.
$$

Since $17=16+1$, multiply the indicated powers to obtain **$27^{17}\equiv6\pmod7$**. Only the [binary expansion](../../../../../binary-expansion.md) of the exponent determines which squared powers are multiplied.
