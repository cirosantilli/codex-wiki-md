↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [probability axioms](../../../../../../kolmogorov-axioms.md) first give $\mathbb P(\varnothing)=0$: apply [countable additivity](../../../../../../countable-additivity.md) to $\Omega,\varnothing,\varnothing,\ldots$ and use normalization and nonnegativity. If $A\subseteq B$, then $B=A\mathbin{\dot\cup}(B\setminus A)$ is a disjoint [union](../../../../../../set-union.md) of events. Finite additivity, as a special case of [countable additivity](../../../../../../countable-additivity.md), gives

$$
\mathbb P(B)=\mathbb P(A)+\mathbb P(B\setminus A)\geq\mathbb P(A).
$$

Thus **a [probability measure](../../../../../../probability-measure.md) is monotone with respect to [set inclusion](../../../../../../set-inclusion.md)**. The difference event is measurable because the events belong to a [sigma-algebra](../../../../../../sigma-algebra.md).
