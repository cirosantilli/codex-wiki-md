The [probability measure](../../../../../../probability-measure.md) $\mathbb P$ maps the [sigma-algebra](../../../../../../sigma-algebra.md) $\mathcal F$ of events into $[0,1]$. Its [probability axioms](../../../../../../kolmogorov-axioms.md) are nonnegativity, normalization $\mathbb P(\Omega)=1$, and [countable additivity](../../../../../../countable-additivity.md): for every pairwise disjoint sequence of events $A_1,A_2,\ldots$,

$$
\mathbb P\left(\bigcup_{n=1}^\infty A_n\right)=\sum_{n=1}^\infty\mathbb P(A_n).
$$

The [sample space](../../../../../../sample-space.md) is $\Omega$, and $\mathcal F$ specifies which subsets are events. Requiring [countable additivity](../../../../../../countable-additivity.md), rather than only its finite version, is part of the [probability space](../../../../../../probability-space-split.md) definition.
