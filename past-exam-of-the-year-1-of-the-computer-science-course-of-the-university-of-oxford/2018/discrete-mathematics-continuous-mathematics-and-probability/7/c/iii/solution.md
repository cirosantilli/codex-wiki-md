<h1 id="7/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) of the homogeneous [linear recurrence](../../../../../../linear-recurrence-relation.md) is $(r-2)^2$, so its repeated-root solutions are $(A+Bn)2^n$. A constant [particular solution](../../../../../../particular-solution.md) is one. Thus $u_n=1+(A+Bn)2^n$. The initial conditions give $A=-1$ and $B=1$, hence

$$
\boxed{u_n=1+(n-1)2^n.}
$$

The factor $n$ is required by the repeated [root of a polynomial](../../../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md); omitting it would lose one independent solution.
