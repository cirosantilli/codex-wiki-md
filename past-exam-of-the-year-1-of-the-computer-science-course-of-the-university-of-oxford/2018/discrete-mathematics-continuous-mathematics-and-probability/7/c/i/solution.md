<h1 id="7/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [particular solution](../../../../../../particular-solution.md) of the [linear recurrence](../../../../../../linear-recurrence-relation.md) is $-n-1$, while its [homogeneous solution](../../../../../../homogeneous-solution.md) is $C2^n$. The initial value gives $C-1=1$, so

$$
\boxed{u_n=2^{n+1}-n-1.}
$$

Substitution verifies $u_{n+1}-2u_n=n$, and the recurrence plus $u_0$ determines all later terms uniquely.
