<h1 id="7/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) is $r^2-3r+2=(r-1)(r-2)$. Therefore $u_n=A+B2^n$. The initial values give $A+B=1$ and $A+2B=0$, yielding $A=2$, $B=-1$. Hence

$$
\boxed{u_n=2-2^n.}
$$

The two constants are fixed by the two initial conditions of this second-order [linear recurrence](../../../../../../linear-recurrence-relation.md).
