↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

By the even [probability density](../../../../../probability-density.md) of the [standard normal distribution](../../../../../standard-normal-distribution.md),

$$
\mathbb E|Z|=\frac2{\sqrt{2\pi}}\int_0^\infty z e^{-z^2/2}\,\mathrm dz=\frac2{\sqrt{2\pi}}\left[-e^{-z^2/2}\right]_0^\infty=\boxed{\sqrt{\frac2\pi}}.
$$

The integral is finite. Equivalently, $|Z|$ has the [half-normal distribution](../../../../../half-normal-distribution.md) with unit scale.
