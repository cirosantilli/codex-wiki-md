↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Normalization gives $c=8/3$. Integrating the [probability density](../../../../../../probability-density.md) from one to two gives

$$
\mathbb P(X\leq2)=\frac83\int_1^2x^{-3}\,\mathrm dx-\int_1^2x^{-4}\,\mathrm dx=\frac83\frac38-\frac7{24}=\boxed{\frac{17}{24}}.
$$

The [expected value](../../../../../../expected-value-split.md) exists, since the first-moment integrals converge, and equals

$$
\mathbb E X=\frac83\int_1^\infty x^{-2}\,\mathrm dx-\int_1^\infty x^{-3}\,\mathrm dx=\frac83-\frac12=\boxed{\frac{13}{6}}.
$$
