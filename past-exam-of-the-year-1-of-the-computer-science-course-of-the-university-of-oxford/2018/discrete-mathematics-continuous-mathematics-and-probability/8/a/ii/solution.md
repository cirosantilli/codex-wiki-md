<h1 id="8/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The all-red event $B$ is a [subset](../../../../../../subset.md) of the same-colour event $A$. Its probability is $(1/2)^3=1/8$, while the three disjoint monochromatic cases give

$$
\mathbb P(A)=\left(\frac12\right)^3+\left(\frac13\right)^3+\left(\frac16\right)^3=\frac16.
$$

Thus the [conditional probability](../../../../../../conditional-probability.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(B\mid A)=\frac{\mathbb P(B)}{\mathbb P(A)}=\frac34.}
$$

The monochromatic outcomes are not equally likely, so counting colours alone would give the wrong conditional answer.
