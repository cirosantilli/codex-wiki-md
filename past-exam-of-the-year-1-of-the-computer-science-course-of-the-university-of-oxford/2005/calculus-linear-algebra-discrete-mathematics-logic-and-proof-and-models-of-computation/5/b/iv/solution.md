<h1 id="5/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The [partial fraction decomposition](../../../../../../partial-fraction-decomposition.md)

$$
f(i)=\frac1{i(i+1)}=\frac1i-\frac1{i+1}
$$

makes the sum a [telescoping sum](../../../../../../telescoping-sum.md). All interior terms cancel:

$$
\boxed{\sum_{i=1}^nf(i)=\left(1-\frac12\right)+\left(\frac12-\frac13\right)+\cdots+\left(\frac1n-\frac1{n+1}\right)=1-\frac1{n+1}=\frac n{n+1}.}
$$
