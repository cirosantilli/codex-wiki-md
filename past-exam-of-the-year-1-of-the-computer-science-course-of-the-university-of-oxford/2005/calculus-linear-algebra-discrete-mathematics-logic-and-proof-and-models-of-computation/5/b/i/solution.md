↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [domain of a function](../../../../../../domain-of-a-function.md) is fixed by its declared type, so

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{dom}f=(0,\infty).}
$$

Although the rational expression also has values at some negative arguments, they are not in the specified [domain of a function](../../../../../../domain-of-a-function.md). Part (a) of the printed question only supplies the definition used in these four subparts.[Printed p. 4, Q5(a)–(b)](../../../../../../calculus-linear-algebra-discrete-mathematics-logic-and-proof-and-models-of-computation-examination-paper-by-the-university-of-oxford-2005.md)
