<h1 id="5/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For $s>0$, the denominator $s(s+1)$ is positive, so every value of $f$ is positive. Conversely, for every $r>0$, the positive number $s=(\sqrt{1+4/r}-1)/2$ satisfies $f(s)=r$. Thus its [image of a function](../../../../../../image-of-a-function.md), or range, is

$$
\boxed{f((0,\infty))=(0,\infty).}
$$

One can also see the full range from [continuity](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) and [monotonicity](../../../../../../monotonic-function.md): $f$ is a [continuous function](../../../../../../continuous-function.md) with $f'(s)=-(2s+1)/(s^2+s)^2<0$, and its endpoint [limits](../../../../../../limit-of-a-function.md) are $+\infty$ as $s\to0^+$ and $0$ as $s\to\infty$. The endpoints themselves are not attained.
