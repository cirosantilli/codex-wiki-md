<h1 id="8/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The first two clauses now require $x=2z$ and $y=2z$. Thus they already imply $x=y$. Choose, for example, $x=1,y=2$; no [natural number](../../../../../natural-number.md) $z$ can satisfy both equations. Consequently the [existential quantifier](../../../../../existential-quantification.md) fails for this pair, irrespective of the universally quantified final clause, and

$$
\boxed{\mathbf N''\not\models\psi.}
$$

In fact even $x=y=1$ fails, since $1=2z$ has no solution among the [natural numbers](../../../../../natural-number.md).
