<h1 id="8/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Every pair of [natural numbers](../../../../../natural-number.md) has a [maximum](../../../../../maximum-of-a-subset-of-a-total-order.md), itself a [natural number](../../../../../natural-number.md). Given arbitrary $x,y$, choose $z=\max\{x,y\}$. Then $x,y\leq z$, and any $w$ satisfying $x,y\leq w$ also satisfies $z\leq w$. All three clauses of $\phi$ hold. Thus

$$
\boxed{\mathbf N\models\psi.}
$$

This explicitly supplies the witness required by the [existential quantifier](../../../../../existential-quantification.md) for every pair chosen by the two [universal quantifiers](../../../../../universal-quantification.md).
