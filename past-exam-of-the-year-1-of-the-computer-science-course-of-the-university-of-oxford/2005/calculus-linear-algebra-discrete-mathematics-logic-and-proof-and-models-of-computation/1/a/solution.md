<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [image of a linear map](../../../../../image-of-a-linear-map.md) and [kernel of a linear map](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) are

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{im}T=\{T(v):v\in V\}\subseteq W,\qquad\ker T=\{v\in V:T(v)=0\}\subseteq V.}
$$

A [linear map](../../../../../linear-map.md) sends zero to zero, since $T(0)=T(0+0)=T(0)+T(0)$. Thus both displayed [sets](../../../../../set-split.md) contain zero. If $w_1=T(v_1)$ and $w_2=T(v_2)$ belong to the [image of a linear map](../../../../../image-of-a-linear-map.md), then for any real [scalars](../../../../../scalar.md) $a,b$,

$$
aw_1+bw_2=T(av_1+bv_2)\in\operatorname{im}T.
$$

If $v_1,v_2$ belong to the [kernel of a linear map](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md), then

$$
T(av_1+bv_2)=aT(v_1)+bT(v_2)=0,
$$

so $av_1+bv_2\in\ker T$. Containing zero and being closed under [linear combinations](../../../../../linear-combination.md) is the [vector subspace](../../../../../vector-subspace.md) criterion. Hence the [image of a linear map](../../../../../image-of-a-linear-map.md) is a [vector subspace](../../../../../vector-subspace.md) of $W$, and the [kernel of a linear map](../../../../../kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) is a [vector subspace](../../../../../vector-subspace.md) of $V$.
