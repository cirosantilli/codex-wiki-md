<h1 id="4/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The velocity contribution in [D'Alembert's formula](../../../../../../d-alembert-s-formula.md) is $\tfrac12\int_{x-t}^{x+t}1\,ds=t$. Hence, with arbitrary twice continuously differentiable $h$,

$$
\boxed{u(x,t)=\frac{h(x+t)+h(x-t)}2+t.}
$$

The added term has both second [partial derivatives](../../../../../../partial-derivative.md) zero and has [time derivative](../../../../../../time-derivative.md) one, so it supplies precisely the required [initial condition](../../../../../../initial-condition.md) while preserving the [wave equation](../../../../../../wave-equation-split.md).
