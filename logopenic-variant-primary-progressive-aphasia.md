# Logopenic variant primary progressive aphasia

↑ **Parent:** [Primary progressive aphasia](primary-progressive-aphasia.md)

A [PPA](primary-progressive-aphasia.md) pattern involving word-finding pauses and impaired repetition, with phonological-memory limitations often important. Sentence length and [syntactic complexity](syntactic-complexity.md) should be varied independently when testing comprehension.
