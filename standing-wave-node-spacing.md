# Standing wave node spacing

↑ **Parent:** [Standing wave](standing-wave.md)

A [node of a standing wave](node-physics.md) must have $y(x,t)=0$ for every time, not merely at one snapshot. For $y=2a\sin(kx)\cos(\omega t)$, with $a,k,\omega>0$, set $t=0$ to see that this requires $\sin(kx)=0$. Thus

$$
x_j=\frac{j\pi}{k}=\frac{j\lambda}{2},\qquad x_{j+1}-x_j=\frac{\lambda}{2},\qquad j\in\mathbb Z.
$$

An [antinode](antinode.md) instead maximizes the oscillation [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) $2a|\sin(kx)|$ and lies halfway between successive [nodes of a standing wave](node-physics.md), at $x=(2j+1)\lambda/4$. Neighbouring [antinodes](antinode.md) are also separated by $\lambda/2$; the distance from a [node of a standing wave](node-physics.md) to its nearest [antinode](antinode.md) is $\lambda/4$. These distances refer to the string's longitudinal coordinate, not its curved arc length.

Within one interval between [nodes of a standing wave](node-physics.md), all moving points have the same [oscillation phase](phase-waves.md). Adjacent intervals have opposite signs of $\sin(kx)$, so their [phase difference](phase-difference.md) is $\pi$: one loop rises while the next falls. The [oscillation phase](phase-waves.md) at an exact [node of a standing wave](node-physics.md) is undefined because its [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) is zero. A ruler measures the spacing of the fixed pattern; it does not track a moving [wave crest](wave-crest.md).
