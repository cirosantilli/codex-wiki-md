# Product rule

↑ **Parent:** [Derivative](derivative.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Product_rule)

If $f$ and $g$ are differentiable at $a$, then

$$
(fg)'(a)=f'(a)g(a)+f(a)g'(a).
$$
