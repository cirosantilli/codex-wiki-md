# Monotone operator

↑ **Parent:** [Convex optimization](convex-optimization-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monotone_operator)

An operator $F$ on an [inner product space](inner-product-space.md) is monotone when

$$
\langle F(v)-F(w),v-w\rangle\geq0
$$

for every $v,w$. The [gradient](gradient.md) of every differentiable [convex function](convex-function.md) is monotone.
