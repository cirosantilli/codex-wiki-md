# Dolichol phosphate

↑ **Parent:** [Lipid](lipid.md)

[dolichol phosphate](dolichol-phosphate.md) is a phosphorylated lipid carrier used in glycan assembly. Lipid-linked sugar intermediates permit some lumenal transfer steps that do not directly use free [nucleotide](nucleotide.md) sugars.
