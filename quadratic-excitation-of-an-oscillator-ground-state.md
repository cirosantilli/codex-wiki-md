# Quadratic excitation of an oscillator ground state

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md)

Multiplying the [quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md) [ground state](ground-state.md) by $x^2$ produces a [quantum superposition](quantum-superposition.md) of only its $n=0$ and $n=2$ [energy eigenstates](energy-eigenstate.md). In the conventional phase choice for the [creation and annihilation operators](creation-and-annihilation-operators.md), $X=\sqrt{\hbar/(2m\omega)}(b+b^\dagger)$ and $b|0\rangle=0$. Therefore

$$
X^2|0\rangle=\frac{\hbar}{2m\omega}(|0\rangle+\sqrt2\,|2\rangle).
$$

After [wavefunction normalization](wavefunction-normalization.md), the [energy measurement](energy-measurement.md) probabilities are $1/3$ at $\hbar\omega/2$ and $2/3$ at $5\hbar\omega/2$. Rephasing the second basis state can change the displayed relative coefficient's sign but cannot change these [probabilities](probability.md). For example, defining the unscaled [creation operator](creation-operator.md) as $a_+=P+im\omega X=i\sqrt{2m\hbar\omega}\,b^\dagger$ reverses the sign of the $n=2$ basis function generated from $(a_+)^2|0\rangle$.
