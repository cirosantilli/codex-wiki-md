# Permutation diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Permutation graph](permutation-graph.md)

A diagram of straight segments joining two parallel lines, with distinct endpoints on each line. Ordering the endpoints along the two lines encodes a [permutation](permutation.md); crossing segments define the associated [permutation graph](permutation-graph.md).
