# Galois character

↑ **Parent:** [Galois representation](galois-representation.md)

A Galois character is a continuous one-dimensional [Galois representation](galois-representation.md): the [absolute Galois group](absolute-galois-group.md) acts by multiplication by a scalar in a coefficient [field](field.md) $F$. A quadratic character over a coefficient field of characteristic different from two has values in $\{1,-1\}$; after identifying this group with $\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z$, its kernel fixes a [quadratic extension](quadratic-extension.md), or the base field for the trivial character. Its restriction to an [inertia group](inertia-group.md) is trivial exactly when that extension is unramified at the corresponding place.
