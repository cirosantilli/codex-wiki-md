# Witt algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Witt_algebra)

The Witt algebra is the [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) of vector fields on a circle with Laurent-polynomial coefficients. Its nontrivial [central extension of a Lie algebra](central-extension-of-a-lie-algebra.md) is the [Virasoro algebra](virasoro-algebra.md).
