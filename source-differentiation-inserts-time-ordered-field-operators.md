# Source differentiation inserts time-ordered field operators

↑ **Parent:** [Generating functional](generating-functional.md)

A [functional derivative](functional-derivative.md) of the source term in a Minkowski [scalar field path integral](scalar-field-path-integral.md) inserts $i\phi(x)$. Time-slicing identifies the resulting product with the [time-ordered product](time-ordered-product.md) of field operators. Dividing by the zero-source vacuum functional removes disconnected vacuum diagrams. The normalized formula has one factor $1/i$ for every insertion.
