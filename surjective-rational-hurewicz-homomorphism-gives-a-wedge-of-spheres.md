# Surjective rational Hurewicz homomorphism gives a wedge of spheres

↑ **Parent:** [Hurewicz homomorphism](hurewicz-homomorphism.md)

If $X$ is [simply connected](simply-connected-space.md) and $h_*:\pi_*(X)\otimes\mathbb Q\to\widetilde H_*(X;\mathbb Q)$ is onto, choose a homogeneous [homology](homology-split.md) [basis](basis.md) and representing sphere maps into $X_{\mathbb Q}$. Their [wedge sum](wedge-sum.md) gives a [rational homology](rational-homology.md) equivalence, hence a [rational homotopy equivalence](rational-homotopy-equivalence.md) by the [rational Whitehead theorem](rational-whitehead-theorem.md). All positive [cup products](cup-product.md) therefore vanish. In a [minimal model](sullivan-minimal-model.md), the induced map from [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) to the indecomposable quotient is injective; replacing a set of generators by cocycle representatives produces a [quasi-isomorphism](quasi-isomorphism.md) to the square-zero [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) algebra, proving [formality of a topological space](formal-space.md).
