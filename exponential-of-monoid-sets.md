# Exponential of monoid sets

↑ **Parent:** [Exponential object](exponential-object.md)

For left [M-sets](m-set.md), $B^A$ consists of [equivariant maps of monoid sets](equivariant-map-of-monoid-sets.md) $M\times A\to B$ for the diagonal action on the domain. Its action is $(m\cdot f)(w,a)=f(wm,a)$ and evaluation is $f(1,a)$. The curry of an equivariant $h:C\times A\to B$ is $\widehat h(c)(w,a)=h(w\cdot c,a)$. This construction can have noninjective action maps even when both $A$ and $B$ are decidable.
