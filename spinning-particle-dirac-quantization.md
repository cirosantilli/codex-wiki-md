# Spinning-particle Dirac quantization

↑ **Parent:** [Pseudoclassical spinning particle](pseudoclassical-spinning-particle.md)

Quantizing the odd [Dirac brackets](dirac-bracket.md) yields $\{\widehat\zeta^m,\widehat\zeta^n\}_+=\eta^{mn}$. The [constraint](constraint-mechanics.md) $p\cdot\zeta=0$ is then the [massless Dirac equation](massless-dirac-equation.md) on a spinor. Its square enforces $p^2=0$, matching the even [constraint](constraint-mechanics.md). The spin bilinear is represented by $-i[\gamma^m,\gamma^n]/4$ in this action convention.
