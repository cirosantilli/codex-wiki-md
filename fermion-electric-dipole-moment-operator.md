# Fermion electric dipole moment operator

↑ **Parent:** [CP violation](cp-violation.md)

The fermion electric dipole moment operator couples a fermion pseudotensor bilinear to the [electromagnetic field tensor](electromagnetic-field-tensor.md). Its nonrelativistic interaction is proportional to $\mathbf S\cdot\mathbf E$, so it is odd under parity and time reversal and signals CP violation.
