# Algebraic topology

↑ **Parent:** [Geometry and topology](geometry-and-topology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_topology)

[Algebraic topology](algebraic-topology-split.md) translates questions about [topological spaces](topological-space.md) and [continuous maps](continuous-map.md) into questions about [groups](group-split.md) and other structures from [algebra](algebra-split.md). A [topological invariant](topological-invariant.md) compresses the enormous amount of data in a space into something one can calculate. For example, the [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md) records which [based loops](based-loop.md) can be continuously deformed into one another. A filled [disk](disk-mathematics.md) has trivial [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md), whereas a [circle](circle.md) has [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md) $\mathbb Z$: an [integer](integer-split.md) remembers how many times a [loop](loop-topology.md) goes around.

This compression also respects maps. By [functoriality of the fundamental group](functoriality-of-the-fundamental-group.md), a [continuous map](continuous-map.md) preserving the chosen basepoints induces a [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md); successive maps induce successive [group homomorphisms](group-homomorphism.md). Thus geometry supplies arithmetic constraints. A proposed [homotopy equivalence](homotopy-equivalence.md) must induce an [isomorphism](isomorphism.md), so incompatible [fundamental groups](fundamental-group.md) rule it out before anyone attempts to construct the deformation.

The beauty is that a whole [annulus](annulus-mathematics.md) can retain exactly the same [homotopy type](homotopy-type.md) as its inner [circle](circle.md). Its thickness disappears under a [deformation retraction](deformation-retraction.md), while the obstruction to shrinking a [loop](loop-topology.md) survives. Equal [fundamental groups](fundamental-group.md) alone do not establish a [homeomorphism](homeomorphism.md) or a [homotopy equivalence](homotopy-equivalence.md) between arbitrary [topological spaces](topological-space.md).

This turns a visual question into a reusable test. Suppose a [continuous map](continuous-map.md) sends the inner [circle](circle.md) of an [annulus](annulus-mathematics.md) once around another [circle](circle.md). Could it extend over a filled [disk](disk-mathematics.md) spanning that boundary? Such an extension would supply a [homotopy](homotopy-split.md) shrinking the image [loop](loop-topology.md) to a point. Its [winding number](winding-number.md) would have to change from one to zero, which [homotopy invariance of winding number](homotopy-invariance-of-winding-number.md) forbids. Algebra rules out the extension even when no particular formula for it has been proposed.
