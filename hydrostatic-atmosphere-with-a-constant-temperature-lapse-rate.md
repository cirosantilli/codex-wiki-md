# Hydrostatic atmosphere with a constant temperature lapse rate

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrostatic balance](hydrostatic-balance.md)

For an [ideal gas](ideal-gas-split.md) with constant composition, set $\mathcal R=k_B/\bar m$, where $\bar m$ is the mean particle [mass](mass.md). Constant downward [gravitational acceleration](gravitational-acceleration.md) $g$ and $T=T_0-\alpha z>0$ give $p'=-(g/\mathcal R T)p$ by [hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md). Integration gives $p/p_0=(T/T_0)^{g/(\mathcal R\alpha)}$, and the [ideal gas law](ideal-gas-law.md) gives the displayed [mass density](density.md) ratio. The isothermal limit $\alpha\to0$ is $\rho/\rho_0=\exp[-gz/(\mathcal R T_0)]$.
