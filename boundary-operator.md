# Boundary operator

↑ **Parent:** [Chain complex](chain-complex.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boundary_operator)

The boundary operator of a [chain complex](chain-complex.md) is its degree-minus-one differential $\partial:C_i\to C_{i-1}$. The identity $\partial^2=0$ says that every [chain boundary](chain-boundary.md) is a [chain cycle](chain-cycle.md).
