# Nondivergence-form elliptic operator

↑ **Parent:** [Uniformly elliptic operator](uniformly-elliptic-operator.md)

A [nondivergence-form elliptic operator](nondivergence-form-elliptic-operator.md) applies its principal coefficients directly to the [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md): $Lu=a^{ij}D_{ij}u+b^iD_i u+cu$. Only the symmetric part of $a$ contributes to the second-derivative term. [Uniform ellipticity](uniformly-elliptic-operator.md) bounds its [quadratic form](quadratic-form.md) below by $\lambda|\xi|^2$ with a constant $\lambda>0$ independent of position.
