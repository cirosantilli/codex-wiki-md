# Horizontal convection

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal convection](thermal-convection.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horizontal_convection)

Horizontal convection is [buoyancy](buoyancy.md)-driven circulation forced by lateral variations of [temperature](temperature.md) along one horizontal boundary. Hot and cold surface regions drive a circulation even though all thermal forcing is applied at the same height. An insulated bottom and sides require zero net heat exchange through the remaining surface in steady state, but permit heat to enter its warmer region and leave its colder region.
