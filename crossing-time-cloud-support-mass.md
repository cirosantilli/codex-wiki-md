# Crossing-time cloud support mass

↑ **Parent:** [Jeans mass](jeans-mass.md)

Equate the [free-fall time of a uniform sphere](free-fall-time-of-a-uniform-sphere.md) $\sqrt{3\pi/(32G\rho)}$ to a radius-based [sound-crossing time](sound-crossing-time.md) $R/c_s$, then substitute $R$ in $M=4\pi\rho R^3/3$. This gives the displayed [mass](mass.md). It has the scaling of a [Jeans mass](jeans-mass.md), but its coefficient differs from the convention using a sphere of diameter equal to the [Jeans length](jeans-length.md). Equality of these two times is an approximate support comparison, not the exact marginal mode of an infinite-medium [Jeans instability](jeans-instability.md).
