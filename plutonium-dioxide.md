# Plutonium dioxide

↑ **Parent:** [Oxide nuclear fuel](oxide-nuclear-fuel.md)

A [plutonium](plutonium.md) oxide, $\mathrm{PuO_2}$, used as a component of mixed [oxide nuclear fuel](oxide-nuclear-fuel.md). Its use requires attention to [decay heat](decay-heat.md), chemical compatibility and the distribution of [fissile material](fissile-material.md); no fabrication recipe is specified.
