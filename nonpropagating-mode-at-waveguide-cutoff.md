# Nonpropagating mode at waveguide cutoff

↑ **Parent:** [Cutoff frequency of a membrane waveguide](cutoff-frequency-of-a-membrane-waveguide.md)

For a [membrane waveguide](membrane-waveguide.md) branch $\omega^2=v^2[k_x^2+(n\pi/b)^2]$, the exact cutoff has zero longitudinal [wavevector](wavevector.md) and zero [group velocity](group-velocity.md). It is not a traveling channel carrying finite longitudinal [energy flux](energy-flux.md). Modes strictly below cutoff are [evanescent](evanescent-wave.md); [amplitudes](wave-amplitude.md) of the permitted above-cutoff modes depend on the transverse excitation profile.
