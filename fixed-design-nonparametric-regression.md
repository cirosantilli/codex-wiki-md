# Fixed-design nonparametric regression

↑ **Parent:** [Nonparametric regression](nonparametric-regression.md)

The [fixed-design nonparametric regression](fixed-design-nonparametric-regression.md) model observes $Y_i=m(x_i)+\sigma\epsilon_i$ at deterministic design points $x_i$. For [independent and identically distributed random variables](independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md) $\epsilon_i$ with the standard [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md), comparing two mean functions has [Kullback-Leibler divergence](kullback-leibler-divergence.md) $\sum_i(m_1(x_i)-m_0(x_i))^2/(2\sigma^2)$.
