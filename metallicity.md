# Metallicity

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar chemical abundance](stellar-chemical-abundance.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metallicity)

The abundance of [chemical elements](chemical-element.md) heavier than [helium](helium.md) in astronomical material. A [metal mass fraction](metal-mass-fraction.md) measures their combined [mass](mass.md); spectroscopic abundance ratios are other measures of metallicity and need not be numerically identical to that fraction.
