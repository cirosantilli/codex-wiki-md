# Equalizer presentation by powers of a coseparator

↑ **Parent:** [Coseparator](coseparator.md)

In a [complete category](complete-category.md) that is a [locally small category](locally-small-category.md) with a [coseparator](coseparator.md) $S$, suppose every [monomorphism](monomorphism.md) is a [regular monomorphism](regular-monomorphism.md). The evaluation embedding $B\to S^I$, $I=\mathcal C(B,S)$, is an [equalizer](equaliser.md) of some pair into $Q$. Composing that pair with the evaluation embedding $Q\to S^J$ leaves the [equalizer](equaliser.md) unchanged. Here powers mean [products in a category](product-category-theory.md) indexed by sets, without assuming an [exponential object](exponential-object.md) exists.
