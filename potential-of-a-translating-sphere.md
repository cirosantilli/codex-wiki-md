# Potential of a translating sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Potential flow](potential-flow.md)

For a sphere translating through an unbounded inviscid incompressible fluid at rest far away, the laboratory fluid [velocity](velocity.md) written in coordinates centred on the moving sphere is the gradient of the displayed dipole potential. Its radial [velocity](velocity.md) at the surface equals $U\cos\theta$. The sphere-rest-frame [velocity](velocity.md) instead needs the additional uniform potential $-Ur\cos\theta$.
