# Laser pump threshold

↑ **Parent:** [Laser threshold](laser-threshold.md)

The pump power or pump rate at which an optical resonator's gain first compensates its round-trip loss. It depends on the population balance, mode overlap and loss budget; it is distinct from an unqualified value of the material gain coefficient.
