# Polar angle

↑ **Parent:** [Spherical coordinate system](spherical-coordinate-system.md)

In the usual physics convention for [spherical coordinates](spherical-coordinate-system.md), the polar angle is the [angle](angle.md) between the positive $z$ axis and the [position vector](position-vector.md). For $r=\sqrt{x^2+y^2+z^2}>0$, it satisfies $\cos\theta=z/r$ with $0\leq\theta\leq\pi$. The north and south axes have $\theta=0$ and $\theta=\pi$, respectively; the [plane](plane.md) $z=0$ has $\theta=\pi/2$. The angle is undefined at the origin. In [plane polar coordinates](plane-polar-coordinates.md), the same phrase usually denotes the oriented in-plane angle instead, so the reference axis and convention should always be stated. The [azimuth](azimuth.md) supplies the other angular coordinate in three dimensions.
