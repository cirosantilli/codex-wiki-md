# Usual conditions for a filtration

↑ **Parent:** [Filtered probability space](filtered-probability-space.md)

The usual conditions are completeness and right continuity of a [filtration](filtration-probability-theory.md): $\mathcal F_0$ contains all subsets of null events in the ambient [probability space](probability-space.md), and $\mathcal F_t=\bigcap_{s>t}\mathcal F_s$. They allow the standard continuous-time [martingale](martingale-split.md), [stopping time](stopping-time.md) and stochastic integration theorems to be used without repeated augmentation qualifications. An [absolute continuity of measures](absolute-continuity-of-measures.md) change preserves old null sets but can introduce new ones; the usual completion under the new measure may therefore be understood when needed.
