# Fourier space

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md)

Fourier space labels a field by the frequency or wavevector variables of its [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md), rather than by its original coordinates. Spatial gradients act by multiplication by $i\mathbf k$, and the negative [Laplacian](laplacian.md) acts by $k^2$.
