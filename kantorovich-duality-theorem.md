# Kantorovich duality theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Kantorovich optimal transport problem](kantorovich-optimal-transport-problem.md)

For [probability measures](probability-measure.md) defined as [Borel measures](borel-measure.md) on [Polish spaces](polish-space.md) and a nonnegative [sequentially lower semicontinuous](sequential-lower-semicontinuity.md) cost, the minimum cost over [transport plans](transport-plan.md) equals the supremum of $\int u\,d\mu+\int v\,d\nu$ over integrable [Kantorovich potentials](kantorovich-potential.md) satisfying $u(x)+v(y)\leq c(x,y)$. The primal minimum is attained; a dual maximum needs additional assumptions. Compact metric spaces and a finite continuous cost suffice for attainment of both extrema.
