# Diffusion generator

↑ **Parent:** [Infinitesimal generator (stochastic processes)](infinitesimal-generator-stochastic-processes.md)

For the one-dimensional Itô equation $dX=b(X)dt+\sigma(X)dW$, the infinitesimal generator on smooth test functions is $L=b\partial_x+\sigma^2\partial_{xx}/2$. The [Kolmogorov backward equation](kolmogorov-backward-equation.md) is $u_t=Lu$, while its formal adjoint produces the [Fokker-Planck equation](fokker-planck-equation.md).
