# Equality-constrained convex optimization

↑ **Parent:** [Convex optimization](convex-optimization-split.md)

An equality-constrained convex optimization problem has the form

$$
\min_x f(x)\quad\text{subject to }Ax=b,
$$

where $f$ is [convex](convex-function.md) and the constraint is [affine](affine-function.md). Its stationarity and feasibility equations can be combined into a monotone primal-dual operator.
