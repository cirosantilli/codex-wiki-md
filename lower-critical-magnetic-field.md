# Lower critical magnetic field

↑ **Parent:** [Ginzburg-Landau theory of superconductivity](ginzburg-landau-theory-of-superconductivity.md)

The applied bulk [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) at which introducing a vortex first becomes favorable in the ideal equilibrium type-II problem. Surface barriers, sample shape and [flux pinning](flux-pinning.md) can change the apparent entry field.
