# Force-tilted activation barrier

↑ **Parent:** [Transition state theory](transition-state-theory.md)

A constant tensile [force](force-split.md) changes a one-coordinate [free-energy landscape](free-energy-landscape.md) from $G_0(x)$ to $G_0(x)-Fx$. To first order, an escape barrier decreases by $F\Delta x$, where $\Delta x$ is the saddle-to-initial-minimum displacement at zero [force](force-split.md). An approximately fixed attempt prefactor gives the displayed rate law. Its characteristic [force](force-split.md) is $F_0=k_BT/\Delta x$. Large changes in saddle location or competing pathways invalidate a constant activation distance. The barrier is measured relative to the initial state; an absolute saddle [energy](energy-split.md) is not enough to determine an escape rate.
