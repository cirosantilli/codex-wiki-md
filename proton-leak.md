# Proton leak

↑ **Parent:** [Oxidative phosphorylation](oxidative-phosphorylation.md)

Proton return across a coupling membrane that is not coupled to productive [ATP synthase](atp-synthase.md) turnover. Its absolute rate, fraction of total proton return and [flux control coefficient](flux-control-coefficient.md) are different quantities.
