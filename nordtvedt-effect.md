# Nordtvedt effect

↑ **Parent:** [Strong equivalence principle](strong-equivalence-principle.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nordtvedt_effect)

A violation of the [strong equivalence principle](strong-equivalence-principle.md) can make the gravitational-to-inertial mass ratio depend on a body's negative gravitational self-energy $E_G$. Bodies with different self-binding fractions would then fall differently in the same external field, even after composition-dependent weak-equivalence effects are removed. For the Earth and Moon this produces a polarization of the lunar orbit toward the Sun, measurable by lunar laser ranging. [General relativity](general-relativity-split.md) predicts $\eta_N=0$.
