# Output (control theory)

↑ **Parent:** [Plant (control theory)](plant-control-theory.md)

A [plant output](output-control-theory.md) is a quantity exposed by the [controlled plant](plant-control-theory.md), often modeled as $y=h(x,u,t)$. In the illustrative model it is $y=x$. An [output](output-computing.md) of a computation has a different meaning.
