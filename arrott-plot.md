# Arrott plot

↑ **Parent:** [Landau theory](landau-theory.md)

An [Arrott plot](arrott-plot.md) presents squared magnetisation against field divided by magnetisation. For the uniform SI Landau equation $\mu_0H=aM+bM^3$, it is a straight line

$$
M^2=\frac{\mu_0}{b}\frac HM-\frac ab.
$$

The critical mean-field isotherm has $a=0$ and passes through the origin. Horizontal intercept $a/\mu_0$ changes sign across the transition; a positive vertical intercept estimates spontaneous magnetisation squared. The original criterion concerns internal [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) and the molecular-field regime. Demagnetizing fields and non-mean-field [critical exponents](critical-exponent.md) require additional care.[1957, abstract: cubic magnetisation proportional to internal field at the Curie point](criterion-for-ferromagnetism-from-observations-of-magnetic-isotherms-by-anthony-arrott.md)
