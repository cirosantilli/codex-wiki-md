# Radiometer thermal link

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical substitution heat balance](electrical-substitution-heat-balance.md)

A [radiometer thermal link](radiometer-thermal-link.md) is the intentional path for [thermal conduction](thermal-conduction.md) from receiver to reference block. Near a chosen operating point, write $Q_{\rm link}\simeq G(T-T_b)$ with local conductance $G$ in $\mathrm{W\,K^{-1}}$. A weaker link increases the steady [temperature](temperature-split.md) rise per added [power](power.md), $\delta T\simeq\delta P/G$, but also increases the uncontrolled relaxation [time](time-in-physics.md) $\tau=C/G$ when the link dominates other [heat transfer](heat-transfer.md). These are local linear approximations: actual [thermal conductivity](thermal-conductivity.md) depends on [temperature](temperature-split.md), and active [feedback control](closed-loop-control.md) introduces its own response. NIST explicitly describes the tradeoff between weaker links and smaller receiver [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md).[Key Design Features, final paragraph: response time proportional to receiver heat capacity and inverse thermal conductance](pico-watt-acr-by-national-institute-of-standards-and-technology.md)
