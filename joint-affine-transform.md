# Joint affine transform

↑ **Parent:** [Affine process](affine-process.md)

For a scalar discrete-time [affine process](affine-process.md), the conditional [cumulant-generating function](cumulant-generating-function.md) of $\sum_{j=1}^t\theta_jX_j$ is affine in $X_0$. Backward effective parameters $q_t=\theta_t$, $q_j=\theta_j+A(q_{j+1})$ give coefficients $A(q_1)$ and $\sum_jB(q_j)$.
