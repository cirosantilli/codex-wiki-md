# Ultrarelativistic longitudinal Doppler factor

↑ **Parent:** [Relativistic Doppler effect](relativistic-doppler-effect.md)

For a forward [photon](photon.md) and a positive collinear [Lorentz transformation](lorentz-transformation.md), the [Doppler factor](doppler-factor.md) is $D=\gamma(1+\beta)$. The identity $\gamma\beta=\sqrt{\gamma^2-1}$ gives the exact expression in the title. Expanding the square root by the [binomial series](binomial-series.md) gives $D=2\gamma-1/(2\gamma)-1/(8\gamma^3)+O(\gamma^{-5})$. A leading approximation $D\sim2\gamma$ has relative error of order $\gamma^{-2}$; retaining a purported subleading term requires expanding all dependent quantities consistently. For example, $\sqrt{4\gamma^2-1}$ has subleading term $-1/(4\gamma)$ and is not the exact longitudinal [Doppler factor](doppler-factor.md).
