# Free-electron Fermi circle in a square lattice

↑ **Parent:** [Fermi surface](fermi-surface.md)

For a two-dimensional [free electron gas](free-electron-model.md) with two spin states and $z$ [electrons](electron.md) per square cell of side $a$, counting occupied [wavevectors](wavevector.md) gives $z/a^2=2\pi k_F^2/(2\pi)^2$. The square [first Brillouin zone](first-brillouin-zone.md) has half-width $\pi/a$. A monovalent circle lies inside its faces; a divalent circle crosses its faces but not its corners. A real [periodic potential](periodic-potential.md) can deform and reconnect the crossings, as in [Fermi-surface reconstruction in a weak square potential](fermi-surface-reconstruction-in-a-weak-square-potential.md); valence alone is insufficient to prove insulation.
