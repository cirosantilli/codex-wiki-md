# Knudsen layer

↑ **Parent:** [Rarefied gas flow approximations](rarefied-gas-flow-approximations.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knudsen_layer)

A [Knudsen layer](knudsen-layer.md) is the gas region adjoining a boundary where the molecular distribution departs from the bulk near-equilibrium description over distances of order a few [mean free paths](mean-free-path.md). Continuum slip and jump conditions describe an effective boundary for the outer solution; they do not resolve this layer. [Rosner and Papadopoulos (1996), Jump, Slip, and Creep Boundary Conditions at Nonequilibrium Gas/Solid Interfaces](https://doi.org/10.1021/ie9600351)
