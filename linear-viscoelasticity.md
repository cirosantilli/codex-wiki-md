# Linear viscoelasticity

↑ **Parent:** [Viscoelasticity](viscoelasticity.md)

[stress](stress.md) is a linear causal functional of deformation history: in simple shear, $\sigma(t)=\int_{-\infty}^tG(t-t')\dot\gamma(t')dt'$. Superposition holds and [complex viscoelastic modulus](dynamic-modulus.md) is amplitude-independent within the small-deformation regime.
