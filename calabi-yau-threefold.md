# Calabi-Yau threefold

↑ **Parent:** [Kähler manifold](kahler-manifold.md)

Here a Calabi-Yau threefold is a compact complex three-dimensional [Kähler manifold](kahler-manifold.md) with trivial [canonical bundle](canonical-bundle.md) and $H^1(M,\mathbb C)=0$. The [Hodge decomposition theorem for compact Kähler manifolds](hodge-decomposition-theorem-for-compact-kahler-manifolds.md) gives $H^1(M,\mathcal O_M)=0$, and [Serre duality for compact complex manifolds](serre-duality-for-compact-complex-manifolds.md) then gives $H^2(M,\mathcal O_M)=0$. This supplies a positive integral Kähler class and hence a projective embedding.
