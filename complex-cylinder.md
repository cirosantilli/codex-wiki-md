# Complex cylinder

↑ **Parent:** [Complex manifold](complex-manifold.md)

A complex cylinder is a quotient $\mathbb C/\mathbb Z\tau$ by one nonzero complex translation. Unlike a compact [complex torus](complex-torus.md), it is biholomorphic to $\mathbb C^*$ through a suitable exponential map.
