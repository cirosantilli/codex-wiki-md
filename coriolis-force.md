# Coriolis force

↑ **Parent:** [Rotating reference frame](rotating-reference-frame.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coriolis_force)

In a frame rotating with angular velocity $\boldsymbol\Omega$, a moving particle of mass $m$ has apparent Coriolis force $-2m\boldsymbol\Omega\times\mathbf v$.
