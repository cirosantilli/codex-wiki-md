# Hydrostatic self-gravitating incompressible cylinder

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md)

For an infinitely long circular cylinder of uniform [mass density](density.md) $\rho$, the gravitational [Poisson equation](poisson-s-equation.md) and the [divergence theorem](divergence-theorem.md) give $d\Phi/dr=2\pi G\rho r$ internally. The [hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) equation $dp/dr=-\rho\,d\Phi/dr$ and boundary value $p(R)=p_{\rm ext}$ then give the title formula. This model neglects rotation, [surface tension](surface-tension-split.md) and end effects.
