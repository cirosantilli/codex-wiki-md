# Gaussianity of deterministic Brownian stochastic integrals

↑ **Parent:** [Itô integral](ito-integral.md)

For deterministic square-integrable $h$, approximate by step functions. Their [Itô integrals](ito-integral.md) are sums of independent normal [Brownian increments](brownian-increment.md). The [Itô isometry](ito-isometry.md) gives convergence in $L^2$, and the variances converge to $\int h^2$; convergence of [characteristic functions](characteristic-function.md) proves the normal limit. Each linear combination of finitely many such integrals is another deterministic stochastic integral, so they are jointly [Gaussian](normal-distribution.md).
