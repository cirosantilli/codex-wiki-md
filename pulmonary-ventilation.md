# Pulmonary ventilation

↑ **Parent:** [Physiology](physiology-split.md)

Movement of air into and out of the lungs. Changes in breathing can influence cardiac timing and can accompany [emotion](emotion.md) or nonemotional physical demands. Breathing must therefore be measured or controlled when interpreting appropriate [psychophysiology](psychophysiology.md) recordings.
