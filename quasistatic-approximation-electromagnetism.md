# Quasistatic approximation (electromagnetism)

↑ **Parent:** [Maxwell equations](maxwell-equations-split.md)

An approximation that keeps slow electromagnetic time variation while treating propagation across characteristic size $L$ as rapid compared with the variation time $1/\omega$. The dimensionless parameter $\omega L/c$ measures that separation. Which terms of the [Maxwell equations](maxwell-equations-split.md) can be dropped still depends on the physical scaling; small propagation delay alone does not justify deleting both [displacement current](displacement-current.md) and [electromagnetic induction](electromagnetic-induction.md). For a slowly driven [parallel-plate capacitor](parallel-plate-capacitor.md), a nearly uniform gap [electric field](electric-field-split.md) can coexist with the [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) generated by its changing [electric flux](electric-flux.md).
