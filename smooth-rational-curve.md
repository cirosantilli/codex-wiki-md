# Smooth rational curve

↑ **Parent:** [Smooth projective curve](smooth-projective-curve.md)

Over an algebraically closed field, a smooth projective rational curve is isomorphic to $\mathbb P^1$. Its [line bundles](line-bundle.md) are determined by degree, its degree-zero [line bundle](line-bundle.md) is trivial, and $H^1(\mathbb P^1,\mathcal O)=0$. An integral projective curve of [arithmetic genus](arithmetic-genus.md) zero is automatically smooth and rational by the normalization genus formula.
