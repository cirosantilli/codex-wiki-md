# Mean-energy neutron moderation count

↑ **Parent:** [Neutron moderation](neutron-moderation.md)

If every step retains the same conditional mean fraction $f$ of the incident kinetic energy, the mean after $n$ steps is $f^nE_i$. Solving for a target mean energy gives the displayed count. It describes an ensemble mean, not a guaranteed collision number for each neutron; averaging the logarithmic loss is another statistical convention.
