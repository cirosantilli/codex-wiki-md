# Projection formula

↑ **Parent:** [Pullback of a sheaf of modules](pullback-of-a-sheaf-of-modules.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Projection_formula)

For a morphism of [ringed spaces](ringed-space-split.md), there is a natural morphism

$$
f_*\mathcal F\otimes_{\mathcal O_Y}\mathcal E\longrightarrow f_*\bigl(\mathcal F\otimes_{\mathcal O_X}f^*\mathcal E\bigr).
$$

It is an isomorphism when $\mathcal E$ is a [locally free sheaf](locally-free-sheaf.md) of finite rank, because the claim is local and then reduces to distributivity over a finite direct sum.

The same formula holds for higher direct images: $R^if_*\mathcal F\otimes\mathcal E\cong R^if_*(\mathcal F\otimes f^*\mathcal E)$ when $\mathcal E$ is locally free of finite rank.
