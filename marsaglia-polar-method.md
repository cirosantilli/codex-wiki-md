# Marsaglia polar method

↑ **Parent:** [Rejection sampling](rejection-sampling.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marsaglia_polar_method)

Reject pairs of independent uniform coordinates from the square until their squared radius $S$ lies in $(0,1)$. Multiply each coordinate by $\sqrt{-2\log S/S}$ to obtain two independent variables with the [standard normal distribution](standard-normal-distribution.md). Squaring either gives a chi-squared variable with one degree of freedom.
