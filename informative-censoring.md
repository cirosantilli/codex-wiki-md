# Informative censoring

↑ **Parent:** [Censoring (statistics)](censoring-statistics.md)

[Informative censoring](informative-censoring.md) occurs when censoring carries information about the future event time beyond the variables conditioned on in the analysis. It can invalidate ordinary [Kaplan–Meier estimator](kaplan-meier-estimator.md) and survival-model residual interpretations.
