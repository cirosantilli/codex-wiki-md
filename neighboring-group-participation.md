# Neighboring group participation

↑ **Parent:** [Organic reaction](organic-reaction.md)

Intramolecular assistance by a group near a reacting centre. Temporary bridging can alter rate, connectivity, [stereochemistry](stereochemistry-split.md) or apparent retention; an intermediate with equivalent paths can erase an original [stereochemical](stereochemistry-split.md) distinction.
