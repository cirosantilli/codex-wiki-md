# Immunoglobulin heavy chain

↑ **Parent:** [Antibody](antibody.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Immunoglobulin_heavy_chain)

An [immunoglobulin heavy chain](immunoglobulin-heavy-chain.md) extends through an arm and the stem of the illustrated [IgG](immunoglobulin-g.md).

Its [IgG](immunoglobulin-g.md) [protein domains](protein-domain.md) run from the [N-terminus](n-terminus.md) as $V_H,C_{H1},C_{H2},C_{H3}$: the first pair contributes to [Fab fragments](fragment-antigen-binding.md), the last pair to the [Fc fragment](fragment-crystallizable-region.md).[Immunobiology: The Immune System in Health and Disease, 5th edition by Charles A. Janeway, Jr. and others; §§3-2–3-3: four heavy-chain domains and their allocation to fragments](immunobiology-the-immune-system-in-health-and-disease-5th-edition-by-charles-a-janeway-jr-and-others/the-structure-of-a-typical-antibody-molecule.md)
