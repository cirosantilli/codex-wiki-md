# Invariant mean

↑ **Parent:** [Amenable group](amenable-group.md)

An invariant mean on a group $G$ is a positive linear functional $m:\ell^\infty(G)\to\mathbb R$ with $m(1)=1$ and $m(gf)=m(f)$ for every $g\in G$. Applied to [indicator functions](indicator-function.md), it is a left-invariant [finitely additive probability measure](finitely-additive-probability-measure.md) on all subsets of $G$.
