# Attenuation

↑ **Parent:** [Electromagnetic radiation](electromagnetic-radiation-split.md)

Reduction of transmitted intensity by absorption or scattering out of the accepted beam. For constant linear coefficient $\mu$, a narrow unscattered beam follows $I(x)=I_0e^{-\mu x}$.
