# Two-out-of-three property for unitary structures

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic geometry](symplectic-geometry-split.md)

On $\mathbb R^{2n}$ let $J_0$ be the standard [complex structure](complex-structure.md), let $\omega_0$ be the standard [symplectic form](symplectic-form.md), and let $g_0(u,v)=\omega_0(u,J_0v)$ be the Euclidean [inner product](inner-product.md). A linear map preserving any two of $J_0$, $\omega_0$, and $g_0$ preserves the third. Equivalently,

$$
GL(n,\mathbb C)\cap Sp(2n,\mathbb R)
=Sp(2n,\mathbb R)\cap O(2n)
=O(2n)\cap GL(n,\mathbb C)
=U(n).
$$
