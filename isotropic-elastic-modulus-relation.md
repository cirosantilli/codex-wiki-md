# Isotropic elastic modulus relation

↑ **Parent:** [Linear elasticity](linear-elasticity.md)

Under uniform compressive [pressure](pressure-split.md) $p$, isotropic [Hooke's law](hooke-s-law.md) gives each normal [strain](strain.md) as $-p(1-2\nu)/E$. The volume strain is three times this, so the [bulk modulus](bulk-modulus.md) is $B=E/[3(1-2\nu)]$, where $E$ is [Young's modulus](young-s-modulus.md) and $\nu$ is [Poisson's ratio](poisson-s-ratio.md). Positivity of $E$ and finite $B$ gives $\nu<1/2$. Positive [shear modulus](shear-modulus.md) $G_s=E/[2(1+\nu)]$ adds the lower bound $\nu>-1$. Negative [Poisson's ratio](poisson-s-ratio.md) is allowed; the bulk relation alone does not supply the lower bound.
