# Proinsulin

↑ **Parent:** [Insulin](insulin.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proinsulin)

A single [polypeptide](polypeptide.md) precursor whose processing yields mature [insulin](insulin.md) and [C-peptide](c-peptide.md); it is not the two-chain mature [hormone](hormone.md). The biosynthetic distinction is described under [insulin](insulin.md).
