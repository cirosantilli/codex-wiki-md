# Entanglement swapping

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum teleportation](quantum-teleportation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entanglement_swapping)

Two initial maximally entangled pairs $AB_1$ and $B_2C$ can be turned into an entangled pair $AC$ by a [generalized Bell basis](generalized-bell-basis.md) measurement on $B_1B_2$, followed by a local correction at an endpoint using its classical label. This is [qudit teleportation](qudit-teleportation.md) of $B_1$, including its correlations with $A$, to $C$. The initial two pairs are consumed. Without the classical label, averaging the conditional endpoint states need not leave entanglement.
