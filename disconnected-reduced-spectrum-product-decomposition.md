# Disconnected reduced spectrum product decomposition

↑ **Parent:** [Spectrum of a commutative ring](spectrum-of-a-commutative-ring.md)

For a [reduced ring](reduced-ring.md) with a nonempty disconnected [spectrum of a commutative ring](spectrum-of-a-commutative-ring.md), choose a separation $V(I)\sqcup V(J)$. Disjointness gives $I+J=R$; covering the spectrum gives $IJ\subseteq\sqrt{(0)}=0$. For [comaximal ideals](comaximal-ideals.md), $I\cap J=IJ$. The [Chinese remainder theorem](chinese-remainder-theorem.md) then supplies a product of two nonzero quotient [rings](ring.md). Equivalently, if $i+j=1$ with $i\in I$, $j\in J$, then $i,j$ are nontrivial orthogonal [idempotents](idempotent.md). This connects topology of the spectrum to [ring product decomposition by an idempotent](ring-product-decomposition-by-an-idempotent.md) without a Noetherian assumption.
