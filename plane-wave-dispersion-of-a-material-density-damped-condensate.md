# Plane-wave dispersion of a material-density-damped condensate

↑ **Parent:** [Material-density damping of a Gross–Pitaevskii condensate](material-density-damping-of-a-gross-pitaevskii-condensate.md)

With the [normal fluid](normal-component-of-a-superfluid.md) at rest, linearize about the unit-amplitude [plane wave](plane-wave.md) $e^{i(\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf x-k^2t/2)}$. For amplitude and phase perturbations proportional to $e^{i\mathbf l\cdot\mathbf x+\sigma t}$, the [continuity equation](continuity-equation.md) gives $(\sigma+i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf l)R=l^2\Theta/2$, and the phase equation gives $(\sigma+i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf l)\Theta=-(2+l^2/2+2\zeta\sigma)R$. Their determinant is the displayed dispersion relation. The damping contains the laboratory exponent $\sigma$, not the Doppler-shifted exponent, because the reference flow is the normal component.
