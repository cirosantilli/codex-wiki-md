# Restoring force

↑ **Parent:** [Force](force-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Restoring_force)

A restoring force acts toward an [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) when a body is displaced from it. In one dimension, taking [displacement](displacement.md) $x=0$ at [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md), this means $xF(x)<0$ for sufficiently small nonzero $x$. An ideal linear [spring](spring.md) has $F(x)=-kx$ with positive [spring constant](spring-constant.md) $k$, producing [simple harmonic motion](simple-harmonic-motion.md) for a constant [mass](mass.md) without [damping](damping.md). More general restoring forces need not be linear.
