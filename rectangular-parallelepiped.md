# Rectangular parallelepiped

↑ **Parent:** [Parallelepiped](parallelepiped.md)

A rectangular parallelepiped is a [parallelepiped](parallelepiped.md) whose three edge directions at each vertex are mutually perpendicular. Its faces are [rectangles](rectangle.md); if its edge lengths are $a,b,c$, its [volume](volume.md) is $abc$. A [cube](cube.md) is the special case $a=b=c$.
