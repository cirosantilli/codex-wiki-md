# Limiting normal cone

↑ **Parent:** [Normal cone](normal-cone.md)

The limiting normal cone consists of limits $w_k\to w$ of vectors from the [Fréchet normal cone](frechet-normal-cone.md) $w_k\in\widehat N_C(z_k)$ with $z_k\to z$ in $C$. It need not be convex. Including normals from nearby graph pieces is essential to the [Mordukhovich criterion](mordukhovich-criterion.md).
