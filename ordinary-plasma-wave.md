# Ordinary plasma wave

↑ **Parent:** [Cold magnetized plasma](cold-magnetized-plasma.md)

For propagation perpendicular to an equilibrium [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md), an ordinary [plasma](plasma-physics.md) [wave](wave-split.md) has its [electric field](electric-field-split.md) parallel to that [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md), so the particle response involves no magnetic deflection. With immobile [ions](ion.md), the [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation-split.md) is $\omega^2=\omega_{pe}^2+c^2k^2$. It is a transverse [electromagnetic wave](electromagnetic-wave-split.md), despite its parallel particle motion.
