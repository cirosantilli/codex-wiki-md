<h1 id="p-q-five-brane">(p,q) five-brane</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Type IIB superstring theory](type-iib-superstring-theory.md)

The IIB five-brane doublet combines [NS5-brane](ns5-brane.md) and Ramond–Ramond five-brane charges. Its toroidal M-description involves wrapped [M5-branes](m5-brane.md) or [Kaluza-Klein monopoles](kaluza-klein-monopole.md), depending on whether the IIB circle lies along or transverse to the five-brane. Electric–magnetic charge bases fix the order and signs of the integer labels.
