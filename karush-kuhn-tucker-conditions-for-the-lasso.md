# Karush-Kuhn-Tucker conditions for the Lasso

↑ **Parent:** [Lasso](lasso.md)

For the [Lasso](lasso.md) criterion $\|Y-Xb\|_2^2/(2n)+\lambda\|b\|_1$, the displayed [subgradient optimality condition](subgradient-optimality-condition.md) is necessary and sufficient. A nonzero coefficient has residual score $\lambda\operatorname{sign}(b_k)$, and a zero coefficient has residual score in $[-\lambda,\lambda]$. The [subdifferential of the L1 norm](subdifferential-of-the-l1-norm.md) supplies both cases.
