# Logic approximation for gene regulation

↑ **Parent:** [Gene regulatory network](gene-regulatory-network.md)

A threshold approximation in which a regulatory input permits or blocks production according to whether a [transcription factor](transcription-factor.md) exceeds a specified concentration. With first-order removal, $\dot y=\beta g-\alpha y$ and $g\in\{0,1\}$. A zero-initialized rising response reaches $K<\beta/\alpha$ at $-\alpha^{-1}\ln(1-\alpha K/\beta)$. Boolean rules must specify how several inputs combine; equal rate constants do not determine thresholds.
