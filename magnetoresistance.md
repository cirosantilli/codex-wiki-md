# Magnetoresistance

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnetoresistance)

A change of [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) associated with an applied [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md), with material, [temperature](temperature-split.md), measuring [electric current](electric-current.md) and preparation specified. The response can depend on magnetic history as well as the instantaneous field. A magnetoresistance measurement therefore compares defined states, rather than treating every material as a single-valued function of field magnitude.
