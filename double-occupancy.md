# Double occupancy

↑ **Parent:** [Hubbard model](hubbard-model.md)

Two [electrons](electron.md) of opposite [spin](spin.md) occupying the same [spatial orbital](spatial-orbital.md). The mean occupation product measures its [probability](probability.md) at a specified site. In the two-electron dimer, the sum over sites is also the [probability](probability.md) of any doubly occupied site, since two sites cannot both be doubly occupied.
