# Filling fraction of an independent cosine band

↑ **Parent:** [Tight binding](tight-binding.md)

For a [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation-split.md) $E(k)=E_0+t\cos(ka)$, $t<0$, occupied states at zero [temperature](temperature-split.md) form the interval $|k|<k_F$ in the first [Brillouin zone](brillouin-zone.md). Its occupied fraction is $f=2k_F/(2\pi/a)=k_Fa/\pi$. A spin-degenerate band therefore holds $2f$ [electrons](electron.md) per site. If the band is independent of the other two wavevector coordinates, its [Fermi surface](fermi-surface.md) consists of two planes. For $t>0$ the occupied region is instead near the zone edges, bounded by $|k|=\pi(1-f)/a$.
