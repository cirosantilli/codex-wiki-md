# Benjamin-Feir stability condition

↑ **Parent:** [Complex Ginzburg–Landau equation](complex-ginzburg-landau-equation.md)

For $A_t=A+(1+ib)A_{xx}-(1+ic)|A|^2A$, the uniform oscillation $A=e^{-ict}$ has phase [diffusion](diffusion.md) coefficient $D=1+bc$. Its amplitude-phase [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation.md) is $\lambda^2+(2+2k^2)\lambda+2Dk^2+(1+b^2)k^4=0$. Thus $D<0$ gives a long-wavelength [modulational instability](modulational-instability.md), whereas $D>0$ gives [linear stability](linear-stability.md) of all nonzero [Fourier modes](fourier-mode.md). On a fixed periodic domain, instability requires an allowed wavenumber with $k^2<-2D/(1+b^2)$.
