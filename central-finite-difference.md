# Central finite difference

↑ **Parent:** [Finite difference method](finite-difference-method.md)

The centered unit-step approximation to a second derivative is

$$
f''(0)\approx f(-1)-2f(0)+f(1).
$$

The weights $1,-2,1$ are [finite difference coefficients](finite-difference-coefficient.md) for this centered second-derivative approximation.
