# Chiral projector

↑ **Parent:** [Chirality (physics)](chirality-physics.md)

The projectors $P_L=(1-\gamma^5)/2$ and $P_R=(1+\gamma^5)/2$ select the two [chirality](chirality-physics.md) eigenspaces of a [Dirac field](dirac-field.md). They satisfy $P_L+P_R=I$, $P_LP_R=0$, and $P_L^2=P_L$, $P_R^2=P_R$.
