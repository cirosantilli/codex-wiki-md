# Elastic energy uniqueness with restoring boundary springs

↑ **Parent:** [Energy uniqueness for traction-driven elasticity](energy-uniqueness-for-traction-driven-elasticity.md)

For a time-independent symmetric negative semidefinite boundary matrix $k$, the difference of two [linear elasticity](linear-elasticity.md) solutions with the same data has [traction](traction.md) $kw$. Integration by parts conserves the displayed nonnegative [energy](energy.md). Zero initial data force zero velocity and then zero displacement. The matrix $-k$ is the stiffness of boundary springs to a prescribed support; null directions are traction-free.
