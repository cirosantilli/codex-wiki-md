# Count-weighted Laplace functional of a Poisson random measure

↑ **Parent:** [Laplace functional of a Poisson random measure](laplace-functional-of-a-poisson-random-measure.md)

Let $M$ be a [Poisson random measure](poisson-random-measure.md) of intensity $\mu$, let $\mu(A)<\infty$, and let $f\geq0$ be [measurable](measurability.md). Apply the [Laplace functional of a Poisson random measure](laplace-functional-of-a-poisson-random-measure.md) to $f+t\mathbf1_A$ and differentiate from the right at $t=0$. On the random side, differentiation is dominated by the [integrable random variable](integrable-random-variable.md) $M(A)$; on the intensity side, by $\mathbf1_A$. This proves the displayed identity when $\int(1-e^{-f})d\mu$ is finite. When this integral is infinite, $e^{-M(f)}=0$ [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md), and the same identity holds with both sides zero.
