# Base-rate effect in a positive study

↑ **Parent:** [Posterior odds](posterior-odds.md)

When the [prior odds](prior-odds.md) of a real effect are $R$, a study's [Type I error](type-i-and-type-ii-errors.md) is $\alpha$, and its [statistical power](statistical-power.md) is $1-\beta$, a positive report updates those odds to $R(1-\beta)/\alpha$. This follows directly from [Bayes' theorem](bayes-theorem.md). A small false-positive rate does not itself make the effect's [posterior probability](posterior-probability.md) high: the prior odds and power also matter. The posterior exceeds one half exactly when $R>\alpha/(1-\beta)$.
