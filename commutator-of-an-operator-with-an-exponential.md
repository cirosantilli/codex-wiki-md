# Commutator of an operator with an exponential

↑ **Parent:** [Operator exponential](operator-exponential.md)

For bounded [linear operators](linear-operator.md), define $C(\beta)=[A,e^{-\beta B}]$. The [product rule](product-rule.md) gives $C'+BC=[B,A]e^{-\beta B}$ and $C(0)=0$. Multiplying by the [integrating factor](integrating-factor.md) $e^{\beta B}$ yields

$$
[A,e^{-\beta B}]
=e^{-\beta B}\int_0^\beta e^{\lambda B}[B,A]e^{-\lambda B}\,d\lambda.
$$

The identity is useful when the two [linear operators](linear-operator.md) do not commute: it preserves their order rather than incorrectly differentiating an [operator exponential](operator-exponential.md) as if all its factors were scalars. For unbounded [linear operators](linear-operator.md), the products and differentiations need a common justified [operator domain](operator-domain.md).
