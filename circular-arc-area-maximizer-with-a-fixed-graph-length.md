# Circular-arc area maximizer with a fixed graph length

↑ **Parent:** [Isoperimetric constraint](isoperimetric-constraint.md)

For a nonnegative graph joining $(0,0)$ and $(2a,0)$ with length between $2a$ and $\pi a$, the area-maximizing smooth graph is a circular arch. The [Euler-Lagrange equation](euler-lagrange-equation.md) integrates to the displayed [circle](circle.md) formula. Its radius satisfies $l=2R\arcsin(a/R)$ with $R>a$. [Concavity](concave-function.md) of $y-R\sqrt{1+(y^{\prime})^2}$ gives global maximality on the fixed-length class.
