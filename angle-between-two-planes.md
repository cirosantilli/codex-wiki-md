# Angle between two planes

↑ **Parent:** [Plane](plane.md)

The acute angle between two unoriented planes is the acute angle between their nonzero [normal vectors](normal-vector.md), giving the displayed formula. For an oriented convex polyhedron, the [interior dihedral angle of a convex polyhedron](interior-dihedral-angle-of-a-convex-polyhedron.md) is instead $\pi$ minus the angle between outward face normals. The distinction matters when a face normal is reversed: its unoriented plane is unchanged, but its vector angle is replaced by its supplement.
