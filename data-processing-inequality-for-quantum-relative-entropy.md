# Data-processing inequality for quantum relative entropy

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum relative entropy](quantum-relative-entropy.md)

For every [quantum channel](quantum-channel.md) $T$,

$$
D(T(\rho)\|T(\sigma))\leq D(\rho\|\sigma).
$$

For a [partial trace](partial-trace.md), this says that discarding a subsystem cannot make two [density operators](density-matrix.md) more distinguishable in [quantum relative entropy](quantum-relative-entropy.md).
