# Basic-function Laplacian identity

↑ **Parent:** [Riemannian submersion](riemannian-submersion.md)

For a [Riemannian submersion](riemannian-submersion.md) with [totally geodesic submanifolds](totally-geodesic-submanifold.md) as fibers, the [positive Laplace-Beltrami operator](positive-laplace-beltrami-operator.md) commutes with pullback of functions. Horizontal terms in the [Riemannian Hessian](riemannian-hessian.md) are pulled back from the base, and vertical terms vanish. Minimal fibers suffice because only the trace of the vertical [second fundamental form](second-fundamental-form-split.md) enters.
