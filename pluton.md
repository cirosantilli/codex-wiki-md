# Pluton

↑ **Parent:** [Magma intrusion](magma-intrusion.md)

A body of [igneous rock](igneous-rock.md) crystallized from [magma](magma.md) emplaced beneath the surface. [Granite](granite.md) is a common plutonic composition. A [pluton](pluton.md)'s [gravity anomaly](gravity-anomaly.md) depends on its [geophysical density contrast](geophysical-density-contrast.md) and geometry, rather than on the rock name alone.
