# Rabi cycle

↑ **Parent:** [Unitary time evolution](unitary-time-evolution.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rabi_cycle)

A [Rabi oscillation](rabi-cycle.md) is a coherent oscillation of populations in a driven two-level [quantum system](quantum-system-split.md).

A resonant drive can transfer a [quantum state](quantum-state.md) from one [energy eigenstate](energy-eigenstate.md) to another and then bring its population back. This is [unitary time evolution](unitary-time-evolution.md): the drive exchanges [energy](energy-split.md) with the selected [two-level system](two-level-system.md), while total [probability](probability.md) stays one. One complete [Rabi cycle](rabi-cycle.md) has population period $2\pi/\Omega$; halfway through, a [Rabi pi pulse](rabi-pi-pulse.md) transfers the whole population in the ideal model. This makes [Rabi oscillations](rabi-cycle.md) useful for calibrating [resonant quantum control](resonant-quantum-control.md), preparing [quantum superpositions](quantum-superposition.md) and supplying the pulses of [Ramsey interferometry](ramsey-interferometry.md).
