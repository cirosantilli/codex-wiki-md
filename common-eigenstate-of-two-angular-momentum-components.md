# Common eigenstate of two angular-momentum components

↑ **Parent:** [Angular momentum operator](angular-momentum-operator-split.md)

A common eigenstate of $J_x$ and $J_y$ must satisfy $J_z\psi=0$ by their [commutator](commutator.md). Applying the other angular-momentum [commutators](commutator.md) then forces both remaining [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) to vanish. Thus the state lies in a spin-zero sector. Noncommuting components do not possess a general common eigenbasis, but spin-zero states are an important exception to the claim that they can never share any eigenstate.
