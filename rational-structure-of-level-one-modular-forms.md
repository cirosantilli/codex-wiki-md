# Rational structure of level-one modular forms

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial ring of level-one modular forms](polynomial-ring-of-level-one-modular-forms.md)

A level-one [modular form](modular-form.md) with rational [Fourier coefficients](fourier-coefficient.md) is a weighted homogeneous polynomial with rational coefficients in the normalized [Eisenstein series](eisenstein-series.md) $E_4,E_6$. Subtract its constant coefficient times a same-weight monomial. The remainder is a [cusp form](cusp-form.md); divide it by $\Delta_0=(E_4^3-E_6^2)/1728=q+O(q^2)$. The [valence formula for the modular group](valence-formula-for-the-modular-group.md) shows that $\Delta_0$ has no interior zeros, so this quotient is holomorphic and has rational coefficients. Induction on weight proves the assertion. Euler's [Bernoulli number](bernoulli-number.md) formula for even zeta values then gives the corresponding rational weighted polynomial in the unnormalized [lattice Eisenstein sums](lattice-eisenstein-sum.md) $G_4,G_6$.
