# Twin prime

↑ **Parent:** [Prime number](prime-number.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twin_prime)

Two [primes](prime-number.md) differing by two form a twin-prime pair, for example $3,5$ or $11,13$. The [twin-prime upper bound using Bombieri–Vinogradov](twin-prime-upper-bound-using-bombieri-vinogradov.md) limits their number without proving there are infinitely many.
