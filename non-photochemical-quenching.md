# Non-photochemical quenching

↑ **Parent:** [Photosynthesis](photosynthesis.md)

[Non-photochemical quenching](non-photochemical-quenching.md) describes pathways that reduce chlorophyll fluorescence through routes other than photochemical charge separation. Rapid protective components can dissipate excitation as heat, but a fluorescence decrease alone does not uniquely identify the pathway.
