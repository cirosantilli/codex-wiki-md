# N-variable Lindenbaum algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Lindenbaum algebra](lindenbaum-algebra.md)

Take formulas whose [free variables](free-variable.md) lie among a fixed $n$-tuple and identify them when their universal equivalence follows from $T$. [Conjunction](conjunction-astronomy.md), disjunction and [negation](negation.md) induce a [Boolean algebra](boolean-algebra.md); its order is implication modulo $T$. [Complete types](complete-type.md) correspond to [ultrafilters](ultrafilter.md) and isolated [complete types](complete-type.md) to [atoms](atom-of-a-boolean-algebra.md). For a [complete theory](complete-theory.md) in a [countable](countable-set.md) language, finiteness of these algebras for every finite $n$ characterizes [countable](countable-set.md) categoricity.
