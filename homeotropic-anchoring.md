# Homeotropic anchoring

↑ **Parent:** [Nematic liquid crystal](nematic-liquid-crystal.md)

A surface boundary preference for the [nematic director](nematic-director.md) to align normal to the local surface. Around a sphere it favors radial orientation near the surface, which must match the far-field texture.
