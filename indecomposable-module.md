# Indecomposable module

↑ **Parent:** [Module theory](module-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indecomposable_module)

A nonzero [module](module-mathematics.md) is indecomposable if it cannot be expressed as a [direct sum](direct-sum.md) of two nonzero [submodules](submodule.md). The zero [module](module-mathematics.md) is excluded. A [module](module-mathematics.md) of finite [composition length](composition-length.md) is indecomposable exactly when its [endomorphism ring](endomorphism-ring.md) has no [idempotents](idempotent.md) other than zero and one: an [idempotent](idempotent.md) splits the [module](module-mathematics.md) into its image and [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md).
