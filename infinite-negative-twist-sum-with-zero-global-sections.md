# Infinite negative-twist sum with zero global sections

↑ **Parent:** [Cohomology of twisting sheaves on projective space](cohomology-of-twisting-sheaves-on-projective-space.md)

On the [projective line](projective-line.md), an infinite direct sum of copies of $\mathcal O(-1)$ is a [quasi-coherent sheaf](quasi-coherent-sheaf.md) but not a [coherent sheaf](coherent-sheaf.md): at any point its residue-vector-space dimension is infinite. Its [global sections](global-section.md) vanish because each summand has no sections, and sections commute with [direct sums](direct-sum.md) for the finite two-chart equalizer. Its [direct image sheaf](direct-image-sheaf.md) on $\operatorname{Spec}k$ is consequently zero and coherent. This shows that a nonaffine direct image can lose information needed to detect coherence.
