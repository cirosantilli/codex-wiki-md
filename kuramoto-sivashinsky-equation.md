# Kuramoto-Sivashinsky equation

↑ **Parent:** [Partial differential equation](partial-differential-equation-split.md)

The Kuramoto-Sivashinsky equation $u_t+uu_x+u_{xx}+u_{xxxx}=0$ combines destabilizing long-wave growth, stabilizing fourth-order [diffusion](diffusion.md), and nonlinear transport. Its potential form is $\Phi_t=-\Phi_{xx}-\Phi_{xxxx}+(\Phi_x)^2$, with $u=-2\Phi_x$. It arises as a long-wave phase [amplitude equation](amplitude-equation.md) near loss of the [Benjamin-Feir stability condition](benjamin-feir-stability-condition.md). On a domain of period $P$, the zero solution's [Fourier modes](fourier-mode.md) grow at rate $k^2-k^4$, $k=2\pi n/P$.
