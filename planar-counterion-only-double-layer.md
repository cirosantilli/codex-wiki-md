# Planar counterion-only double layer

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson-Boltzmann equation](poisson-boltzmann-equation.md)

A negatively charged plane with monovalent positive [counterions](counterion.md) in $z>0$ and no salt reservoir has the mean-field profile displayed above, where $b=e/(2\pi\ell_B|\sigma|)$ and [Bjerrum length](bjerrum-length.md) $\ell_B=e^2/(4\pi\varepsilon k_BT)$. Its potential relative to the wall is $2k_BT\log(1+z/b)/e$. The positive-ion [charge density](charge-density.md) integrates to $|\sigma|$, neutralizing the plane; the field decays algebraically. The potential has no finite zero-at-infinity reference, unlike the screened symmetric-reservoir problem.
