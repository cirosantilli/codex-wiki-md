# Foil areal number density

↑ **Parent:** [Thin-foil scattering counts](thin-foil-scattering-counts.md)

The number of target [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) per incident [area](surface-area.md) along the beam. For a uniform foil at normal incidence, [number density](number-density.md) $n$ and thickness $t$ give $n_s=nt$, in $\mathrm{m^{-2}}$. At oblique incidence the traversed path must replace $t$. The single-encounter count model requires the chance of two significant deflections to be negligible and [energy](energy-split.md) loss through the foil to be small. A small probability in one detector patch alone does not establish either condition.
