# Radial soft thresholding

↑ **Parent:** [Proximal operator](proximal-operator.md)

The [proximal map](proximal-operator.md) of $\tau\|\cdot\|_2$ sends $x$ to $(1-\tau/\|x\|_2)_+x$, with zero assigned at $x=0$. This removes vectors inside the radius-$\tau$ ball and shortens all larger vectors by a fixed radial distance. It is useful for groupwise sparsity.
