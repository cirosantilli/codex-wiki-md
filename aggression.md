# Aggression

↑ **Parent:** [Social psychology](social-psychology.md)

Behavior directed toward harming another who is motivated to avoid that harm. Anger, [emotional arousal](emotional-arousal.md), aggressive thoughts and scores on experimental tasks should not automatically be equated with enacted harm. Measurement must specify which component is observed.
