# Mean measure of Poisson-Dirichlet components

↑ **Parent:** [One-parameter Poisson-Dirichlet distribution](one-parameter-poisson-dirichlet-distribution.md)

For $PD(\theta)$, $\mathbb E\sum_i\varphi(p_i)=\int\varphi\,d\alpha_\theta$ whenever the absolute integral is finite. Apply the [Mecke formula for a Poisson random measure](mecke-formula-for-a-poisson-random-measure.md) to a selected jump $u$ and the remaining total $t$, whose density is $t^{\theta-1}e^{-t}/\Gamma(\theta)$. Changing variables to $s=u+t$ and $x=u/s$ factors out a [Gamma function](gamma-function.md) and gives the displayed measure. Weighting it by $x$ gives a [probability](probability.md) density $\theta(1-x)^{\theta-1}$, the [Beta distribution](beta-distribution.md) of a size-biased component.
