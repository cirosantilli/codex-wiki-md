# Particle horizon through radiation-matter equality

↑ **Parent:** [Comoving particle horizon](comoving-particle-horizon.md)

Neglecting curvature and [dark energy](dark-energy.md), integration of the [Friedmann equation](friedmann-equations.md) for [radiation in cosmology](radiation-in-cosmology.md) and [pressureless matter](pressureless-matter.md) gives the displayed [comoving particle horizon](comoving-particle-horizon.md). At [matter-radiation equality](matter-radiation-equality.md), $a_{\rm eq}=\Omega_{r,0}/\Omega_{m,0}$, this is $2(\sqrt2-1)$ times the radiation-only extrapolation $ca_{\rm eq}/(H_0\sqrt{\Omega_{r,0}})$. Its associated inverse length differs by an order-one factor from the [matter-radiation equality scale](matter-radiation-equality-scale.md) $a_{\rm eq}H_{\rm eq}/c$.
