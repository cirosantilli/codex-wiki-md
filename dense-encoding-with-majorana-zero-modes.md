# Dense encoding with Majorana zero modes

↑ **Parent:** [Majorana zero mode](majorana-zero-mode.md)

$2M$ Majorana zero modes define $M$ complex fermionic modes. Fixing total [fermion parity](fermion-parity.md) leaves a $2^{M-1}$-dimensional ground space, encoding $M-1$ qubits.
