# Burst transport under inverse-size radial drag

↑ **Parent:** [Poynting–Robertson drag](poynting-robertson-drag.md)

For grains of fixed diameter $D$ released instantaneously at $r_0$, the drift law $\dot r=-\gamma/(rD)$ integrates to the displayed size-radius map. The time to radius $r<r_0$ is $t_r(D)=D(r_0^2-r^2)/(2\gamma)$. Grains therefore arrive in increasing diameter order. This model of [Poynting–Robertson drag](poynting-robertson-drag.md) assumes nearly circular [Kepler orbits](kepler-orbit.md), fixed positive $\gamma$, and no grain loss or diameter changes before arrival. At a positive time, the map is monotone in $D$ and allows the initial [power-law size distribution](power-law-size-distribution.md) to be transformed into a spatial distribution or an arrival-time distribution.
