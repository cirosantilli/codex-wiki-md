# 2001 isolated attosecond pulse experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Attosecond streaking](attosecond-streaking.md)

The experiment involving [Ferenc Krausz](ferenc-krausz.md) combined a subfemtosecond soft-[X-ray](x-ray.md) pulse with a few-cycle visible [electric field](electric-field-split.md). Its delay-dependent [krypton](krypton.md) [photoelectron](photoelectron.md) spectra tested the field-controlled response. The reported pulse duration was $650\pm150$ attoseconds. Changing delay compared the emitted [electron](electron.md) distributions at different reference phases; the spectral-width trace supplied a further duration-sensitive observable.[Attosecond metrology, abstract and Figs.3–5: krypton spectra, delay scan and width](attosecond-metrology-by-michael-hentschel-and-others.md)

The distinction from [RABBITT](reconstruction-of-attosecond-beating-by-interference-of-two-photon-transitions.md) is the measurement regime and reconstruction, rather than merely calling one trace a clock. The [attosecond streaking](attosecond-streaking.md) [momentum](momentum-split.md) map is a limiting model; a finite-duration [wave packet](wave-packet.md) requires a quantum transition calculation. Inferring an emission delay also requires separating the illuminating pulse's [spectral phase](spectral-phase.md) from the target response. The measurement supports time-dependent [photoionization](photoionization.md) in a specified model, with no unique classical [electron](electron.md) trajectory inside an [atomic orbital](atomic-orbital.md) implied.
