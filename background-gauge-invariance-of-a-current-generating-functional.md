# Background gauge invariance of a current generating functional

↑ **Parent:** [Background gauge field](background-gauge-field.md)

For a nonanomalous [Dirac current](dirac-current.md) $j^\mu$, introduce $Z[B]=\int\mathcal D\bar\psi\mathcal D\psi\,e^{iS+i\int B_\mu j^\mu}$. The local phase change of variables has unit [functional Jacobian](functional-jacobian.md), while its kinetic variation cancels the shift of the source. Thus $Z[B+\partial\alpha]=Z[B]$. At first order, [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) yields $\partial_\mu\delta Z/\delta B_\mu=0$. Charged sources transform too when deriving the full [Ward-Takahashi identity](ward-identity.md) with [Ward identity contact terms](ward-identity-contact-terms.md).
