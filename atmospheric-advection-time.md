# Atmospheric advection time

↑ **Parent:** [Exoplanet atmospheric dynamics](exoplanet-atmospheric-dynamics.md)

The atmospheric advection time over length $L$ at wind speed $U$ is $\tau_{\rm adv}\sim L/U$. [Day-night heat redistribution](day-night-heat-redistribution.md) tends to be efficient when this time and the relevant wave-adjustment time are shorter than the [radiative relaxation time in a planetary atmosphere](radiative-relaxation-time-in-a-planetary-atmosphere.md).
