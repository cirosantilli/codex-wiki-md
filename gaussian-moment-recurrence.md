# Gaussian moment recurrence

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian integral](gaussian-integral.md)

For integers $n\geq2$, [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) gives

$$
I_n=\left[-\frac12x^{n-1}e^{-x^2}\right]_0^\infty+\frac{n-1}{2}I_{n-2}=\frac{n-1}{2}I_{n-2}.
$$

The boundary term vanishes because $n-1>0$ at zero and [Gaussian decay dominates every power](gaussian-decay-dominates-every-power.md) at infinity. The initial values are $I_0=\sqrt\pi/2$, the half-line [Gaussian integral](gaussian-integral.md), and $I_1=1/2$ by the [substitution rule](integration-by-substitution.md) $t=x^2$. More generally, for real $n>-1$, that substitution gives $I_n=\tfrac12\Gamma((n+1)/2)$, linking the recurrence to the [Gamma function](gamma-function.md).
