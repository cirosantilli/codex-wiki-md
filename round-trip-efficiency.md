# Round-trip efficiency

↑ **Parent:** [Energy storage](energy-storage.md)

The ratio of useful [energy](energy-split.md) recovered on discharge to the [energy](energy-split.md) supplied during charging for a complete storage cycle. Generation efficiency alone excludes the preceding charging losses.
