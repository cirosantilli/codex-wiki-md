# Apraxia of speech

↑ **Parent:** [Speech production](speech-production.md)

Impaired planning or programming of the coordinated movements needed for [speech](speech.md), distinguished from a primary muscle weakness or a deficit in grammatical knowledge. It can arise in developmental or acquired conditions.
