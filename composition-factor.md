# Composition factor

↑ **Parent:** [Composition series of a module](composition-series-of-a-module.md)

A composition factor is an irreducible successive quotient in a [composition series of a module](composition-series-of-a-module.md). For a central [Casimir operator](casimir-element.md), its scalar on a composition factor is an [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) of the operator on the original [Lie algebra representation](lie-algebra-representation.md).
