# Fricke transform of a primitive coprime twist

↑ **Parent:** [Primitive twist at coprime level](primitive-twist-at-coprime-level.md)

Use the [determinant-normalized slash operator](determinant-normalized-slash-operator.md) and $W_M=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\M&0\end{pmatrix}$. For units $u,v$ with $Nuv\equiv-1\pmod D$, the [matrix](matrix.md) identity $U_uW_{ND^2}=D\gamma_uW_NU_v$ has $\gamma_u=\begin{pmatrix}(1+Nuv)/D&u\\Nv&D\end{pmatrix}\in\Gamma_0(N)$. A positive scalar [matrix](matrix.md) acts trivially. The [nebentypus](nebentypus-character.md) supplies $\psi(D)$; reindexing gives $\overline\chi(u)=\chi(-N)\chi(v)$. Thus the factor is initially $\psi(D)\chi(-N)\tau(\chi)/\tau(\overline\chi)$. The primitive [Gauss sum of a Dirichlet character](gauss-sum-of-a-dirichlet-character.md) identity $\tau(\chi)\tau(\overline\chi)=\chi(-1)D$ gives the displayed equivalent factor. No assumption that $f$ itself is a Fricke eigenform is needed.
