# Spin crossover

↑ **Parent:** [Ligand field](ligand-field.md)

[Spin crossover](spin-crossover.md) interconverts electronic states with different total [spin](spin.md), often in response to [temperature](temperature-split.md). For octahedral [iron](iron.md)(II), $t_{2g}^4e_g^2$ and $t_{2g}^6$ correspond to $S=2$ and $S=0$. Removing [electrons](electron.md) from [metal](metal.md)–ligand antibonding $e_g$ orbitals shortens bonds in the low-[spin](spin.md) state. Crystal packing and [hydrogen bonds](hydrogen-bond.md) can affect transition temperatures.
