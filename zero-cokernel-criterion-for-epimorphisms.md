# Zero-cokernel criterion for epimorphisms

↑ **Parent:** [Abelian category](abelian-category.md)

In an [abelian category](abelian-category.md), a morphism is an [epimorphism](epimorphism.md) if and only if its [categorical cokernel](cokernel-in-a-category.md) object is zero. For the reverse implication, any difference of maps annihilating the morphism factors through its cokernel and is therefore zero. The [cokernel invariance under pushout](cokernel-invariance-under-pushout.md) then proves both preservation and reflection of epimorphisms by pushout.
