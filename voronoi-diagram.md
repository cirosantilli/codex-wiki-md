# Voronoi diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean geometry](euclidean-geometry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voronoi_diagram)

Given sites in a [Euclidean space](euclidean-norm.md), the Voronoi cell of a site is the set of points at least as close to it as to every other site. The Voronoi diagram is the subdivision formed by all of these cells.
