# Degree-one cup-square obstruction to ring isomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Cohomology ring](cohomology-ring.md)

The property that every degree-one element has square zero is invariant under a graded ring isomorphism. Over $\mathbb F_2$ it distinguishes the [cohomology ring of a torus](cohomology-ring-of-a-torus.md) from the [mod-two cohomology ring of an inversion mapping torus](mod-two-cohomology-ring-of-an-inversion-mapping-torus.md) of dimension at least two.
