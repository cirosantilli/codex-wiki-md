# Infrared cancellation in large-scale structure

↑ **Parent:** [One-loop matter power spectrum](one-loop-matter-power-spectrum.md)

At equal time, leading loop contributions from a very long-wavelength displacement cancel between perturbative diagrams. This is required by the [Equivalence principle](equivalence-principle.md), because a coherent long displacement merely translates short-scale structure.
