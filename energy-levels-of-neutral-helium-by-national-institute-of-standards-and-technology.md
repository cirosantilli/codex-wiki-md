# Energy Levels of Neutral Helium by National Institute of Standards and Technology

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by National Institute of Standards and Technology](work-by-national-institute-of-standards-and-technology.md)

By [National Institute of Standards and Technology](national-institute-of-standards-and-technology.md). Handbook of Basic Atomic Spectroscopic Data, neutral [helium](helium.md) He I table.

[https://physics.nist.gov/PhysRefData/Handbook/Tables/heliumtable5.htm](https://physics.nist.gov/PhysRefData/Handbook/Tables/heliumtable5.htm)

The table provides ground, singlet and triplet excitation energies as spectroscopic wavenumbers, and the He II ground-state ionization limit. Multiply by $hc$ with consistent inverse-length units to obtain excitation [energy](energy-split.md).
