# Forward difference

↑ **Parent:** [Finite difference](finite-difference-split.md)

The finite increment $\Delta_h f(x)=f(x+h)-f(x)$ for a specified step $h$. Higher forward differences are obtained by iterating this operation. For a [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) of degree $d$, its $(d+1)$st forward difference is zero.
