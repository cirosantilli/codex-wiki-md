# Complex viscosity

↑ **Parent:** [Linear viscoelastic fluid](linear-viscoelastic-fluid.md)

For the convention $e^{i\omega t}$, complex viscosity is the ratio of oscillatory [shear stress](shear-stress.md) amplitude to [shear rate](shear-rate.md) amplitude. A causal [relaxation modulus](relaxation-modulus.md) gives the displayed transform. Its associated complex shear modulus is $G^*=i\omega\eta^*=G'+iG''$, where $G'$ is the [storage modulus](storage-modulus.md) and $G''$ the [loss modulus](loss-modulus.md).
