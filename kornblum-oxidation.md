# Kornblum oxidation

↑ **Parent:** [Organic reaction](organic-reaction.md)

[oxidation](oxidation.md) of an alkyl halide or a related electrophile by [dimethyl sulfoxide](dimethyl-sulfoxide.md). An alkoxysulfonium intermediate undergoes base-promoted elimination to a [carbonyl compound](carbonyl-compound.md) and dimethyl sulfide.
