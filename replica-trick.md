# Replica trick

↑ **Parent:** [Quenched disorder](quenched-disorder.md)

For positive $Z$, the displayed identity follows by differentiating $Z^n=e^{n\log Z}$ at zero.

Integer powers of a [partition function](canonical-partition-function.md) represent independent copies with the same disorder. Integer moments alone do not justify [analytic continuation](analytic-continuation.md) or interchanging disorder averages and [thermodynamic limits](thermodynamic-limit.md).[Edwards, 1985 commentary, replica averaging and acknowledged mathematical limitations](citation-classic-commentary-on-theory-of-spin-glasses-by-sam-edwards.md)
