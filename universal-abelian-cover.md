# Universal abelian cover

↑ **Parent:** [Covering space](covering-space.md)

For a connected space, this is the connected covering corresponding to the kernel of the homomorphism from its [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md) to its [abelianization](abelianization.md). Its [deck transformation group](deck-transformation-group.md) is $H_1(Y;\mathbb Z)$. Lifted cells make its [cellular chain complex](cellular-chain-complex.md) a complex over the [group ring](group-ring.md) $\mathbb Z[H_1(Y)]$.
