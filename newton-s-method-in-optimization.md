<h1 id="newton-s-method-in-optimization">Newton's method in optimization</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical optimization](mathematical-optimization-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newton's_method_in_optimization)

Newton's method for minimizing a twice differentiable function uses

$$
x_{k+1}=x_k-[\nabla^2f(x_k)]^{-1}\nabla f(x_k).
$$
