# Regular natural exponential family

↑ **Parent:** [Natural exponential family](natural-exponential-family.md)

A [natural exponential family](natural-exponential-family.md) is regular when its parameter space is open. In a full regular family, differentiation under the normalizing integral is justified locally by exponential moments in a neighbourhood. Regularity does not guarantee that every possible data set has a finite [maximum-likelihood estimator](maximum-likelihood-estimator.md).
