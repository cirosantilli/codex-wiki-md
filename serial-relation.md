# Serial relation

↑ **Parent:** [Binary relation](binary-relation.md)

A [binary relation](binary-relation.md) is serial when every domain element has at least one outgoing related element. A [serial relation](serial-relation.md) may have cycles; combining seriality with an [irreflexive relation](irreflexive-relation.md) and a [transitive relation](transitive-relation.md) instead forces a nonempty domain to be infinite, by the [infinite-model property of a serial strict partial order](infinite-model-property-of-a-serial-strict-partial-order.md).
