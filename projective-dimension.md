# Projective dimension

↑ **Parent:** [Projective module](projective-module.md)

For a nonzero [module](module-mathematics.md), the projective dimension $\operatorname{pd}_RM$ is the shortest length of a [projective resolution](projective-resolution.md) of the [module](module-mathematics.md) $M$, or infinity if no finite [projective resolution](projective-resolution.md) exists. Equivalently, it is the supremum of degrees with nonzero $\operatorname{Ext}_R^n(M,N)$ as $N$ ranges over all [modules](module-mathematics.md). This equivalence follows by dimension shifting and the characterization of [projective modules](projective-module.md) by vanishing $\operatorname{Ext}^1$.
