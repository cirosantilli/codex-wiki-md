# Local topos

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric morphism](geometric-morphism.md)

A topos over sets is local when its [global sections functor](global-sections-functor.md) is itself an inverse image functor. For an idempotent-complete small indexing category, its [presheaf topos](presheaf-topos.md) is local exactly when the indexing category has a terminal object: then global sections are evaluation at that object.
