# Cyclotron resonance

↑ **Parent:** [Cyclotron frequency](cyclotron-frequency.md)

[Cyclotron resonance](cyclotron-resonance.md) is resonant absorption by charged particles in a [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) when an applied oscillating electric field drives their cyclotron motion. For a parabolic band, the resonance frequency is $\omega_c=|q|B/m^*$, so measuring it determines the [cyclotron effective mass](cyclotron-effective-mass.md). In the quantum description, transverse [electric dipole transitions](electric-dipole-transition.md) connect adjacent [Landau levels](landau-level.md), whose separation is $\hbar\omega_c$. For an electron and a field along $z$, only the circular polarization rotating in the electron's orbital sense drives the positive-frequency resonance. Scattering broadens the absorption peak; resolving it requires an orbital period short compared with the relaxation time.
