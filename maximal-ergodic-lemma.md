# Maximal ergodic lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Birkhoff ergodic theorem](birkhoff-ergodic-theorem.md)

For a [measure-preserving transformation](measure-preserving-transformation.md) and real integrable $f$, let $S_kf=\sum_{j<k}f\circ T^j$. For $E_N=\{\max_{1\leq k\leq N}S_kf>0\}$ the lemma asserts $\int_{E_N}f\,d\mu\geq0$. Passing to the increasing [union](set-union.md) gives the corresponding infinite-supremum inequality.
