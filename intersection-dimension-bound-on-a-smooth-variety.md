# Intersection dimension bound on a smooth variety

↑ **Parent:** [Intersection theory](intersection-theory.md)

For closed irreducible subvarieties $Y,Z$ of a smooth $d$-dimensional [algebraic variety](algebraic-variety.md) $X$, every [irreducible component](irreducible-component.md) of their intersection has dimension at least $\dim Y+\dim Z-d$. Locally the diagonal of $X\times X$ is cut out by $d$ parameters. Intersecting it with $Y\times Z$ and applying the [Krull height theorem](krull-height-theorem.md) gives the bound. The [three-dimensional affine quadric cone](three-dimensional-affine-quadric-cone.md) shows that the same lower bound can fail when $X$ is singular.
