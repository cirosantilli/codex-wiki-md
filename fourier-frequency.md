# Fourier frequency

↑ **Parent:** [Discrete Fourier transform](discrete-fourier-transform.md)

$\lambda_j=2\pi j/T$ is a frequency of the length-$T$ [discrete Fourier transform](discrete-fourier-transform.md). Distinct interior frequencies give orthogonal sine and cosine vectors; for a Gaussian [white noise](white-noise.md) record this makes the corresponding Fourier components independent.
