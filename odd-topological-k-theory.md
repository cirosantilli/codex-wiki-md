# Odd topological K-theory

↑ **Parent:** [Topological K-theory](topological-k-theory.md)

For compact metric $X$, odd topological K-theory is the [homotopy](homotopy.md) set of norm-continuous maps into unitaries equal to the identity modulo [compact operators](compact-operator-split.md) on a fixed separable infinite-dimensional [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md). Uniform finite-rank approximation over $X$ and over [homotopies](homotopy.md) gives the equivalent stable model $\operatorname{colim}_N[X,GL_N(\mathbb C)]$. Block sum gives an abelian group: a finite-dimensional unitary path swaps blocks, and a two-block rotation contracts $\operatorname{diag}(u,u^{-1})$ to the identity. On the circle the determinant winding identifies $K^1(S^1)$ with $\mathbb Z$; [Bott periodicity](bott-isomorphism.md) gives $K^1(S^n)=\mathbb Z$ in odd dimensions and zero in even dimensions.
