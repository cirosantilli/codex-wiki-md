# Kurie plot

↑ **Parent:** [Beta decay](beta-decay.md)

A [kurie plot](kurie-plot.md) removes known [electron](electron.md) phase-space and Coulomb factors from a beta spectrum and takes a square root. For negligible [neutrino](neutrino.md) [mass](mass.md)/recoil and constant nuclear shape factor, its ordinate is proportional to $Q-T$, giving a straight line whose kinetic-energy intercept is the beta endpoint. The precise factors depend on whether the measured spectrum is per [momentum](momentum-split.md) or per [energy](energy-split.md). Forbidden transitions generally need an additional shape correction.
