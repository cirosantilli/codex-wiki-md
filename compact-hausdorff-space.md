# Compact Hausdorff space

↑ **Parent:** [Compact space](compact-space.md)

A compact Hausdorff space is both [compact](compact-space.md) and [Hausdorff](hausdorff-space.md). Such a space is [normal](normal-space.md), and every real-valued [continuous function](continuous-function.md) on it is bounded.
