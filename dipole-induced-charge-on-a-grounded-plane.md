# Dipole-induced charge on a grounded plane

↑ **Parent:** [Image charge](image-charge.md)

A normal [electric dipole](electric-dipole.md) comprising $+q$ at height $h$ and $-q$ at height $h+d$, with $d\ll h$, induces the displayed leading [surface charge density](surface-charge-density.md) on a grounded plane. The [image charge](image-charge.md) construction first gives $\sigma_q(a)=-qh/[2\pi(a^2+h^2)^{3/2}]$ for one [point charge](point-charge.md); subtraction at heights $h$ and $h+d$ gives $-d\partial_h\sigma_q$ to first order. The central negative region ends at $a=\sqrt2h$, outside which the induced [surface charge density](surface-charge-density.md) is positive. Integrating over a disk gives $Q(<a)=-qd\,a^2/(a^2+h^2)^{3/2}$ to this order, so the total induced [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) is zero. At finite $d$, the exact sign-change radius obeys $a^2=h^{2/3}(h+d)^{2/3}[h^{2/3}+(h+d)^{2/3}]$.
