# Heyting implication

↑ **Parent:** [Heyting algebra](heyting-algebra.md)

In a [Heyting algebra](heyting-algebra.md), $a\to b$ is the greatest $x$ with $x\wedge a\leq b$. Thus $x\leq a\to b$ if and only if $x\wedge a\leq b$. Every [frame](complete-heyting-algebra.md) has this operation, obtained by taking the join of all such $x$. The [open nucleus](open-nucleus.md) attached to $a$ is $x\mapsto a\to x$.
