<h1 id="lehmann-scheffe-theorem">Lehmann–Scheffé theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Rao-Blackwell theorem](rao-blackwell-theorem.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lehmann–Scheffé_theorem)

An unbiased [estimator](estimator.md) that is a function of a [complete sufficient statistic](complete-sufficient-statistic.md) is the unique uniformly minimum-[variance](variance-split.md) unbiased [estimator](estimator.md), up to almost sure equality. [Rao-Blackwellization](rao-blackwellization.md) supplies [variance](variance-split.md) reduction, and completeness supplies uniqueness. The theorem applies to a conditional statistical experiment as well, when unbiasedness, sufficiency and completeness are all evaluated under that conditional family.
