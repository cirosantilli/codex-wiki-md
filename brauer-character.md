# Brauer character

↑ **Parent:** [Modular representation theory](modular-representation-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brauer_character)

Let $M$ be a representation over a splitting field $k$ of characteristic $p$. For a [p-regular element](p-regular-element.md) $g$, the eigenvalues of $g$ on $M$ have order prime to $p$. Lift them to characteristic-zero roots of unity by the [Teichmuller lift](teichmuller-representative.md); their sum is the Brauer character value $\chi_M(g)$.
