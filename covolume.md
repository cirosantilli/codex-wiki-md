# Covolume

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean lattice](euclidean-lattice.md)

The covolume of a full [Euclidean lattice](euclidean-lattice.md) is the [Lebesgue measure](lebesgue-measure.md) of a fundamental parallelepiped in its ambient [inner product space](inner-product-space.md). In orthonormal coordinates it is $|\det B|$ for a lattice basis matrix $B$. Scaling the lattice by $t>0$ multiplies the [covolume](covolume.md) by $t^n$; the [dual lattice](dual-lattice.md) has reciprocal [covolume](covolume.md).
