# First-order action for Abelian worldsheet duality

↑ **Parent:** [Buscher procedure](buscher-procedure.md)

Replace the derivative of an isometric coordinate by an independent one-form $A$ and impose $dA=0$ with a [Lagrange multiplier](lagrange-multiplier.md). In signature $(-,+)$ with $\epsilon^{\tau\sigma}=1$, its equation is $VA^\nu=\epsilon^{\mu\nu}\partial_\mu\widetilde z$. Eliminating $A$ from the full action, including the multiplier term, gives $\tfrac12V^{-1}\partial_\mu\widetilde z\partial^\mu\widetilde z$. Omitting the multiplier term during substitution would give the wrong sign. This derives the block-diagonal metric inversion in the [Buscher rules](buscher-rules.md).
