<h1 id="baker-campbell-hausdorff-formula">Baker--Campbell--Hausdorff formula</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix exponential](matrix-exponential.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baker–Campbell–Hausdorff_formula)

The Baker--Campbell--Hausdorff formula expresses the local [matrix logarithm](matrix-logarithm.md) of a product of exponentials as

$$
\log(e^Xe^Y)=X+Y+\frac12[X,Y]+\frac1{12}[X,[X,Y]]+\frac1{12}[Y,[Y,X]]+\cdots.
$$
