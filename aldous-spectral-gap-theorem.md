# Aldous spectral gap theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Interchange process](interchange-process.md)

On a finite connected weighted [graph](graph-split.md), the [interchange process](interchange-process.md) and the single-label [random walk](random-walk.md) with the same symmetric rates have equal [spectral gaps](spectral-gap.md). This is a substantial theorem, not just the elementary inclusion of the single-label [spectrum](spectrum-functional-analysis.md) in the full [spectrum](spectrum-functional-analysis.md). A proof is given in [Proof of Aldous' spectral gap conjecture](https://arxiv.org/abs/0906.1238), Theorem 1.1.
