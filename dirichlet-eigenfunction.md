# Dirichlet eigenfunction

↑ **Parent:** [Eigenfunction](eigenfunction.md)

A [Dirichlet eigenfunction](dirichlet-eigenfunction.md) is an [eigenfunction](eigenfunction.md) of a differential operator satisfying a homogeneous [Dirichlet boundary condition](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md). The term applies to more general boundary-value operators as well as the [Dirichlet Laplacian](dirichlet-laplacian.md).
