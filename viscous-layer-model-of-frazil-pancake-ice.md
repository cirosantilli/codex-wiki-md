# Viscous layer model of frazil-pancake ice

↑ **Parent:** [Wave attenuation in sea ice](wave-attenuation-in-sea-ice.md)

Represent disconnected [frazil ice](frazil-ice.md) and [pancake ice](pancake-ice.md) as an upper layer with effective density, thickness and viscosity, rather than a continuous [elastic plate](elastic-plate.md). Linear [Navier-Stokes equations](navier-stokes-equation.md) in that layer are coupled to underlying water through velocity and stress conditions. A complex [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) describes both dispersion and [wave attenuation in sea ice](wave-attenuation-in-sea-ice.md). A simpler conservative limit keeps ice mass loading but removes the elastic bending term; it does not predict viscous attenuation. The suspension model's effective viscosity is a fitted property, and a compact pancake cover need not behave like dilute frazil.
