# Adaptedness of a stopped right-continuous process

↑ **Parent:** [Adapted process](adapted-process.md)

For an [adapted process](adapted-process.md) with right-continuous paths and a [stopping time](stopping-time.md) $T$, $X^T_t=X_{t\wedge T}$ is [adapted](adapted-process.md). At fixed $t$, round $t\wedge T$ upward on finite partitions of $[0,t]$. Every approximating value is $\mathcal F_t$-[measurable](measurability.md), and right continuity gives the limit. Completeness handles a null exceptional set if right continuity holds only [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md). A [martingale](martingale-split.md) assumption and boundedness of $T$ are unnecessary.
