# Avogadro constant

↑ **Parent:** [Amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avogadro_constant)

The Avogadro constant converts [amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md) into a [dimensionless](dimensionless-quantity.md) entity count: $N=nN_{\mathrm A}$. Its value is exact under the [SI](international-system-of-units.md) definition of the [mole](mole-unit.md). [BIPM, exact Avogadro constant](https://www.bipm.org/en/si-base-units/mole)
