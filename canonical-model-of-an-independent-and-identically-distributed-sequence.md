# Canonical model of an independent and identically distributed sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Product measure](product-measure.md)

For a probability law $\nu$ on $E$, take the sequence space $E^{\mathbb N}$ with [product measure](product-measure.md) $\nu^{\otimes\mathbb N}$. The coordinate maps are an [independent and identically distributed sequence](independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md) with common law $\nu$, and they generate the product [sigma-algebra](sigma-algebra.md).
