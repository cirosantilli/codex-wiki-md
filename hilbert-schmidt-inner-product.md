# Hilbert-Schmidt inner product

↑ **Parent:** [Hilbert-Schmidt operator](hilbert-schmidt-operator.md)

For [Hilbert-Schmidt operators](hilbert-schmidt-operator.md) $A$ and $B$, the Hilbert-Schmidt inner product is $\langle A,B\rangle_{\mathrm{HS}}=\operatorname{tr}(A^*B)$. Its induced norm is the [Hilbert-Schmidt norm](hilbert-schmidt-norm.md).
