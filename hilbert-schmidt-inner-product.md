# Hilbert-Schmidt inner product

↑ **Parent:** [Hilbert-Schmidt operator](hilbert-schmidt-operator.md)

For [Hilbert-Schmidt operators](hilbert-schmidt-operator.md) $A$ and $B$, define the Hilbert-Schmidt inner product by $\langle A,B\rangle_{\mathrm{HS}}=\sum_k\langle Ae_k,Be_k\rangle$ for a [Hilbertian basis](hilbertian-basis.md) $(e_k)$. With an [inner product](inner-product-split.md) linear in its first argument this equals $\operatorname{tr}(B^*A)$; with an [inner product](inner-product-split.md) conjugate-linear in its first argument it equals $\operatorname{tr}(A^*B)$. Its induced [norm](norm.md) is the [Hilbert-Schmidt norm](hilbert-schmidt-norm.md), independently of that convention.
