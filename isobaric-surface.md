# Isobaric surface

↑ **Parent:** [Pressure coordinate](pressure-coordinate.md)

A surface of constant [pressure](pressure-split.md). Its intersection with a fixed-height horizontal plane is an [atmospheric isobar](atmospheric-isobar.md). An [isobaric surface](isobaric-surface.md) is generally not a [geopotential surface](geopotential-surface.md): the horizontal [geopotential](geopotential.md) variation on it determines the [geostrophic wind](geostrophic-wind.md).
