# Product of a quotient map with the unit interval

↑ **Parent:** [Quotient map](quotient-map.md)

If $q:E\to B$ is a [quotient map](quotient-map.md), then $q\times\operatorname{id}_{[0,1]}$ is a [quotient map](quotient-map.md). More generally the second factor may be any [locally compact space](locally-compact-space.md) that is [Hausdorff](hausdorff-space.md). To prove the assertion, let $W\subseteq B\times[0,1]$ have open preimage $O$, and take $(b,t)\in W$. Choose $e\in q^{-1}(b)$ and a compact [neighbourhood](neighbourhood-mathematics.md) $K$ of $t$ with $\{e\}\times K\subseteq O$. The set $E_K=\{u:\{u\}\times K\subseteq O\}$ is open by [compactness](compact-space.md) of $K$, and is saturated under $q$ because membership in $O$ depends only on $q(u)$ and the second coordinate. Hence $q(E_K)$ is open by the [quotient topology](quotient-topology.md). The rectangle $q(E_K)\times\operatorname{int}K$ is a [neighbourhood](neighbourhood-mathematics.md) of $(b,t)$ contained in $W$, proving that $W$ is open. This justifies parameterized [homotopies](homotopy-split.md) defined on [quotient spaces](quotient-topological-space.md).
