# Fixed-energy turbulent mixed layer

↑ **Parent:** [Fluid entrainment](fluid-entrainment.md)

In a slowly deepening mixed layer driven by a rotating boundary, a phenomenological balance between energy input, turbulent loss and acceleration of entrained fluid can keep total [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) approximately fixed. For a tank of radius $R$ this gives $u^2h=A\Omega^2R^3$, so velocity decreases as $h^{-1/2}$. The closure does not make the speed constant and is an additional physical assumption rather than a direct consequence of [mass conservation](mass-conservation.md).
