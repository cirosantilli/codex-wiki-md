# Airy power-series fundamental pair

↑ **Parent:** [Airy ordinary differential equation](airy-ordinary-differential-equation.md)

For the [Airy ordinary differential equation](airy-ordinary-differential-equation.md) $y''=xy$, a [power series](power-series.md) obeys $a_2=0$ and $a_{m+3}=a_m/[(m+3)(m+2)]$. Setting its first two coefficients to $(1,0)$ or $(0,1)$ gives

$$
y_1(x)=\sum_{k\geq0}\frac{x^{3k}}{\prod_{j=1}^k(3j)(3j-1)},\qquad
y_2(x)=\sum_{k\geq0}\frac{x^{3k+1}}{\prod_{j=1}^k(3j)(3j+1)}.
$$

Empty products are one. The [ratio test](ratio-test.md) proves convergence on the whole complex plane. Termwise differentiation verifies the equation, and the initial normalization gives [Wronskian](wronskian.md) one. By the [Abel identity](abel-s-identity.md), the [Wronskian](wronskian.md) remains one everywhere, so these solutions span the whole solution space.
