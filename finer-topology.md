# Finer topology

↑ **Parent:** [Topology](topology-split.md)

For two [topologies](topology-split.md) on the same [set](set-split.md) $X$, $\sigma$ is finer than $\tau$ when every $\tau$-[open set](open-set.md) is $\sigma$-[open](open-set.md). Equivalently, the [identity function](identity-function.md) $(X,\sigma)\to(X,\tau)$ is [continuous](continuous-function.md), by the definition using [preimages](preimage.md) of [open sets](open-set.md). The inclusion may be an equality; strictly finer means proper inclusion.
