# Vibroseis

↑ **Parent:** [Seismic source](seismic-source.md)

A controlled vibratory [seismic source](seismic-source.md) that transmits a known [frequency](frequency.md) sweep through a coupled ground contact. Repeated sweeps can improve useful signal strength, and [Vibroseis correlation](vibroseis-correlation.md) compresses the long source signal into a shorter wavelet. Access, ground coupling, finite source force, bandwidth and nonlinear harmonics affect the resulting survey data.
