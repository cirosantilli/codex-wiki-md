# Linear-density stellar model

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar structure](stellar-structure-split.md)

An imposed spherical profile $\rho=\rho_c(1-r/R)$ gives $M=\pi\rho_cR^3/3$ in [hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md). For an [ideal gas](ideal-gas.md) with zero surface pressure, $P=P_c(1-x)^2(1+2x-9x^2/5)$ and $T=T_c(1-x)(1+2x-9x^2/5)$, where $x=r/R$, $P_c=5GM^2/(4\pi R^4)$ and $T_c=5GM/(12\mathcal RR)$. The density has a central cusp and temperature initially rises outward, so positive outward [radiative diffusion](radiative-diffusion.md) with regular positive nuclear heating cannot satisfy this imposed profile near the centre.
