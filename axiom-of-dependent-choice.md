# Axiom of dependent choice

↑ **Parent:** [Axiom of choice](axiom-of-choice.md)

If a nonempty [set](set-split.md) carries a relation in which every point has a successor, the [axiom of dependent choice](axiom-of-dependent-choice.md) supplies an infinite [sequence](sequence.md) following that relation. Full [choice](axiom-of-choice.md) implies this by selecting a successor at every point and applying the [recursion theorem on the natural numbers](recursion-theorem-on-the-natural-numbers.md).
