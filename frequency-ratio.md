# Frequency ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Frequency](frequency.md)

The frequency ratio of two nonzero [frequencies](frequency.md) is a dimensionless [ratio](ratio.md). Ordinary and [angular frequencies](angular-frequency.md) give the same result because both are converted by the same factor $2\pi$. Rational ratios imply a common period for their sinusoidal motions, while the special ratio two means that one mode completes two cycles during one cycle of the other.
