# Quadratic exponential sum

↑ **Parent:** [Exponential sum](exponential-sum.md)

A quadratic exponential sum has a polynomial phase of degree two. Its multiplicative derivative at lag $h$ has the [linear phase](linear-phase.md) $2\alpha hn$ up to a constant factor. This degree reduction lets the [Van der Corput inequality for finite scalar sequences](van-der-corput-inequality-for-finite-scalar-sequences.md) reduce its size to estimates for [finite geometric series](finite-geometric-series.md).
