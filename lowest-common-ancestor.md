# Lowest common ancestor

↑ **Parent:** [Rooted tree](rooted-tree.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lowest_common_ancestor)

The [lowest common ancestor](lowest-common-ancestor.md) of two vertices is the common ancestor farthest from the root. In the ancestor [partial order](partially-ordered-set.md) with the root least, it is their infimum, also called their [greatest common ancestor](lowest-common-ancestor.md).
