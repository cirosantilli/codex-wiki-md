# Jarzynski equality

↑ **Parent:** [Crooks fluctuation theorem](crooks-fluctuation-theorem.md)

Integrating the [Crooks fluctuation theorem](crooks-fluctuation-theorem.md) over [work](work.md) and using normalization of the reverse [probability distribution](probability-distribution-split.md) gives

$$
\langle e^{-\beta W}\rangle_F=e^{-\beta\Delta F}.
$$

Consequently [Jensen's inequality](jensen-s-inequality.md) gives $\langle W\rangle_F\ge\Delta F$. Some realizations may nevertheless have $W<\Delta F$. This is a [work](work.md) relation with equilibrium endpoint preparations, while the [integral fluctuation theorem](integral-fluctuation-theorem.md) for total [entropy production](entropy-production.md) uses the actual final forward distribution. [Crooks, 1999, Eq. (3) and derivation from Eqs. (10)–(11)](the-entropy-production-fluctuation-theorem-and-the-nonequilibrium-work-relation-for-free-energy-differences-by-gavin-e-crooks.md)
