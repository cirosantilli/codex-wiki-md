# Local time of a semimartingale

↑ **Parent:** [Local time (mathematics)](local-time-mathematics.md)

Local time measures how intensely a semimartingale visits a level. For a continuous semimartingale it appears as the increasing correction term in the [Tanaka formula](tanaka-s-formula.md).
