# MSc in energy systems course of the University of Oxford

↑ **Parent:** [Master's course of the University of Oxford](master-s-course-of-the-university-of-oxford.md)

[Official course description](https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/graduate/courses/msc-energy-systems).

Three foundation modules: energy fundamentals and [thermodynamics](thermodynamics-split.md); energy infrastructure: power systems; energy demand. Seven further modules cover large fixed generation; distributed/renewable generation and storage; energy for development; energy and society; digitization, smart energy and communication; policy and governance; economics and markets. Small-group case study, whole-cohort exercise and a dissertation. Full-time study lasts one year; part-time study lasts two or three years.
