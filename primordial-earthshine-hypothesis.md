# Primordial Earthshine hypothesis

↑ **Parent:** [Lunar crustal dichotomy](lunar-crustal-dichotomy.md)

A proposed explanation of the lunar farside highlands in which irradiation from a nearby hot early [Earth](earth.md) imposes a thermal gradient on a synchronously rotating, accreting [Moon](moon.md). Preferential condensation and deposition of refractory crust-forming material toward the cooler farside seeds a lasting chemical and crustal-thickness asymmetry. It requires early [synchronous rotation](synchronous-rotation.md), appropriate transport times and incomplete homogenization by subsequent mixing. It is a testable formation hypothesis, distinct from ordinary present-day illumination of the lunar night side.[Sections 2–4, physical requirements and chemical-gradient mechanism](earthshine-on-a-young-moon-explaining-the-lunar-farside-highlands-by-arpita-roy-and-others.md)
