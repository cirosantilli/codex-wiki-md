# Flex configuration of the Hesse pencil

↑ **Parent:** [Hesse pencil](hesse-pencil.md)

For $\omega^3=1$ primitive and $k=0,1,2$, these nine points are the [inflection points](inflection-point.md) of every nonsingular finite member of the [Hesse pencil](hesse-pencil.md). On the cubic, substituting the equation into its [Hessian curve](hessian-curve-of-a-plane-curve.md) gives $-4(\lambda^3+1)xyz=0$. Nonsingularity forces one coordinate to vanish, and the remaining two cubes sum to zero, producing exactly the nine displayed points. They are the fixed base configuration of the pencil, independently of its smooth parameter.
