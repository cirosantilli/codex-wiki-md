# Subgroups and quotients of a cyclic group

↑ **Parent:** [Cyclic group](cyclic-group.md)

A nontrivial [subgroup](subgroup.md) $H$ of $\langle g\rangle$ is generated by $g^d$, where $d$ is the least positive exponent with $g^d\in H$. Divide any exponent $k$ with $g^k\in H$ by $d$; its remainder also gives an element in $H$ and must be zero. A [quotient group](quotient-group.md) by a [normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md) is generated by the coset of $g$, because every coset is a power of that coset. The trivial subgroup and quotient are also cyclic.
