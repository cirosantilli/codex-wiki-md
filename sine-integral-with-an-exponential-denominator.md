# Sine integral with an exponential denominator

↑ **Parent:** [Dominated convergence theorem](dominated-convergence-theorem.md)

For real $a$, the geometric-series expansion of $1/(e^x-1)$ gives an integrable [series](series-mathematics.md) after [multiplication](multiplication.md) by $\sin(ax)$: each absolute [integral](integral-split.md) is bounded by $|a|\int_0^\infty xe^{-nx}dx=|a|/n^2$. [Termwise integration](termwise-integration.md) is therefore justified and gives the [convergent series](convergent-series.md) $\sum_{n\ge1}a/(a^2+n^2)$.
