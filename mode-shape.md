# Mode shape

↑ **Parent:** [Normal mode](normal-mode.md)

The mode shape is the fixed spatial [eigenfunction](eigenfunction.md) multiplying the temporal factor of a [normal mode](normal-mode.md). It is determined by the governing [differential equation](differential-equation-split.md) and [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md), and may be normalized by a chosen amplitude or energy convention.
