# Phosphazene

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical compound](chemical-compound.md)

A [phosphazene](phosphazene.md) contains phosphorus–[nitrogen](nitrogen.md) bonding often represented with formal P=N units. Ionic bonding and [negative hyperconjugation](negative-hyperconjugation.md) provide a modern account; formal double-bond drawings do not require phosphorus $3d$ pi bonding.
