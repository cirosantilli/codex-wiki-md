# Separating family of seminorms

↑ **Parent:** [Seminorm topology](seminorm-topology.md)

A family of [seminorms](seminorm.md) separates points when every nonzero [vector](vector.md) $z$ has $p(z)>0$ for some member $p$. Equivalently, its [kernels of seminorms](kernel-of-a-seminorm.md) have intersection $\{0\}$, or any two distinct [vectors](vector.md) $x,y$ have $p(x-y)>0$ for some $p$. Individual [seminorms](seminorm.md) can have large [kernels of seminorms](kernel-of-a-seminorm.md); the whole family must have no common invisible direction.

The associated [seminorm topology](seminorm-topology.md) is [Hausdorff](hausdorff-space.md) if and only if the family separates points. Indeed, if $p(x-y)=a>0$, the [neighbourhoods](neighbourhood-mathematics.md) $\{u:p(u-x)<a/3\}$ and $\{v:p(v-y)<a/3\}$ are disjoint by the [triangle inequality](triangle-inequality.md). Conversely, if every $p$ vanishes on a nonzero $z$, then zero and $z$ belong to exactly the same open [sets](set-split.md) and cannot be separated. This criterion keeps separation distinct from [completeness](completeness.md): detecting all directions does not guarantee that all [Cauchy sequences](cauchy-sequence.md) have limits.
