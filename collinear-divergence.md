# Collinear divergence

↑ **Parent:** [Infrared divergence](infrared-divergence.md)

A singularity of a massless scattering or radiation integral when momenta of two particles become parallel. In [Quantum chromodynamics](quantum-chromodynamics.md), unresolved quark-gluon splittings cause such divergences. A mass regulator or [dimensional regularization](dimensional-regularization.md) exposes them; they cancel between suitable inclusive real and virtual contributions or are absorbed into factorized distributions where appropriate.
