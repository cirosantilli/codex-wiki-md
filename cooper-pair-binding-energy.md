# Cooper pair binding energy

↑ **Parent:** [Cooper problem](cooper-problem.md)

For shell width $D$, attraction $g>0$ and constant single-spin [density of states](density-of-states.md) $\rho$, $1=(g\rho/2)\log[(2D+B)/B]$ gives $B=2D/[\exp(2/(g\rho))-1]$. The pair [energy](energy-split.md) relative to the free threshold is $-B$. At weak coupling this scale is nonanalytic in $g$.
