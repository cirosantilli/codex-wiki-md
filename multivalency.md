# Multivalency

↑ **Parent:** [Protein-protein interaction](protein-protein-interaction.md)

The possession of several interaction sites in one molecule or assembly. Multiple contacts can give [avidity](avidity.md), crosslinking or collective assembly, depending on geometry and partner concentrations.
