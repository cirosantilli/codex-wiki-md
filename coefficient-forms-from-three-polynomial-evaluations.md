# Coefficient forms from three polynomial evaluations

↑ **Parent:** [Dual basis](dual-basis.md)

For $p(X)=a+bX+cX^2$, the three [point evaluation functionals](point-evaluation-functional.md) at $0,1,-1$ form a [basis](basis.md) of the [dual space](dual-space-split.md) of real [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) of degree at most two. The displayed recovery formulas express the coordinate functionals, hence the [dual basis](dual-basis.md) to $1,X,X^2$, as [linear combinations](linear-combination.md) of these evaluations. More generally distinct-node [polynomial interpolation](polynomial-interpolation.md) identifies values with [coefficient](coefficient.md) data through an invertible [Vandermonde matrix](vandermonde-matrix.md).
