# Cartesian category

↑ **Parent:** [Finite limit](finite-limit.md)

In the finite-limit convention, a [Cartesian category](cartesian-category.md) is a [category](category-split.md) with all [finite limits](finite-limit.md), equivalently a [terminal object](terminal-object.md) and all [pullbacks in a category](pullback-category-theory.md). Some authors use the term for finite products alone, so the convention matters in constructions requiring [equalizers](equaliser.md).
