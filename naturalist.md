# Naturalist

↑ **Parent:** [Natural history](natural-history.md)

A practitioner who observes, collects, describes or classifies [organisms](organism-split.md) and other natural objects. The historical role predates modern disciplinary divisions among [biology](biology-split.md), [geology](geology.md) and related subjects.
