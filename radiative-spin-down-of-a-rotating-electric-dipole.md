# Radiative spin-down of a rotating electric dipole

↑ **Parent:** [Electric dipole radiation](electric-dipole-radiation.md)

A permanent [electric dipole moment](electric-dipole-moment.md) of magnitude $p_0$ rotating perpendicular to a fixed axis emits $P=K\omega^4$ in the [electric-dipole approximation for radiation](electric-dipole-approximation-for-radiation.md), with $K=\mu_0p_0^2/(6\pi c)$. For fixed mechanical [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) $\mathcal I$, weak radiative loss gives $\mathcal I\omega\dot\omega=-K\omega^4$ and hence the displayed spin-down law. It assumes $|\dot\omega|\ll\omega^2$: the full dipole acceleration has squared magnitude $p_0^2(\omega^4+\dot\omega^2)$, whose second term is beyond this leading adiabatic approximation.
