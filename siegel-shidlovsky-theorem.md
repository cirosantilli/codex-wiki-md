<h1 id="siegel-shidlovsky-theorem">Siegel–Shidlovsky theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E-function](e-function.md)

Let a vector of [E-functions](e-function.md) satisfy $f'=A(z)f$ with $A\in\operatorname{Mat}_s(\overline{\mathbb Q}(z))$. At a nonzero algebraic point $\xi$ which is not a pole of $A$, the transcendence degree of the values over $\overline{\mathbb Q}$ equals the transcendence degree of the functions over $\overline{\mathbb Q}(z)$. In particular algebraically independent functions give algebraically independent values. The exponential system $f_j=e^{\gamma_jz}$, for rationally independent algebraic $\gamma_j$, recovers the [Lindemann–Weierstrass theorem](lindemann-weierstrass-theorem.md) by evaluating at one.
