# Embedding dimension distinguishes three-branch curves

↑ **Parent:** [Zariski tangent space](zariski-tangent-space.md)

Three coordinate axes in affine three-space have coordinate ideal $(xy,xz,yz)$; their common point has [embedding dimension](embedding-dimension.md) three. Three distinct coplanar lines meeting at one point have embedding dimension two there. An isomorphism preserves the unique point lying on all three irreducible components and preserves the [algebraic cotangent space](algebraic-cotangent-space.md), so these two configurations are not isomorphic despite identical component incidence.
