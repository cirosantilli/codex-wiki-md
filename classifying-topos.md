# Classifying topos

↑ **Parent:** [Grothendieck topos](grothendieck-topos.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classifying_topos)

A topos classifies a geometric theory when geometric morphisms into it from any [Grothendieck topos](grothendieck-topos.md) correspond naturally to internal models of that theory. Pulling back one generic model gives the corresponding model. For a finitary [algebraic theory](algebraic-theory.md), the classifying topos is the covariant functor category on finitely presented models.
