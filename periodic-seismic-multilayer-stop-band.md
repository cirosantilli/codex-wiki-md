# Periodic seismic multilayer stop band

↑ **Parent:** [Seismic layer transfer matrix](seismic-layer-transfer-matrix.md)

The two-layer cell [seismic layer transfer matrix](seismic-layer-transfer-matrix.md) has reciprocal [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) because its [determinant](determinant.md) is one. Writing them as $e^{\pm iKd}$ gives the displayed half-trace relation. At a real [frequency](frequency.md), an absolute half-trace exceeding one forces an exponentially growing/decaying pair instead of propagating cell modes. A long finite stack then suppresses transmission in this stop band, without dissipating [energy](energy.md): the missing transmitted flux is reflected. Quarter-wave layers of unequal [seismic impedance](seismic-impedance.md) give half-trace $-\tfrac12(q_1/q_2+q_2/q_1)<-1$.
