# Minimal-subtraction two-point counterterms in cubic scalar theory

↑ **Parent:** [One-loop two-point divergence in six-dimensional cubic scalar theory](one-loop-two-point-divergence-in-six-dimensional-cubic-scalar-theory.md)

With additive [counterterms](counterterm.md) $\tfrac12\delta Z(\partial\phi)^2+\tfrac12\delta m^2\phi^2$, their Euclidean propagator insertion is $-(\delta Zk^2+\delta m^2)$. These [minimal subtraction scheme](minimal-subtraction-scheme.md) coefficients cancel the cubic bubble insertion. A bare mass shift also subtracts $m^2\delta Z$ after [wavefunction renormalization](wave-function-renormalization.md); it is not the same coefficient as the additive mass counterterm.
