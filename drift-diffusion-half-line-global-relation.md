# Drift-diffusion half-line global relation

↑ **Parent:** [Global relation for a linear boundary value problem](global-relation-for-a-linear-boundary-value-problem.md)

For $q_t=q_{xx}+\alpha q_x$ on $x>0$, write $Q(k)=\int_0^\infty e^{-ikx}q(x,0)dx$ and $G_j(k,t)=\int_0^t e^{\omega(k)s}\partial_x^jq(0,s)ds$. Twice integrating by parts gives $e^{\omega(k)t}\widehat q(k,t)=Q(k)-G_1(k,t)-(ik+\alpha)G_0(k,t)$ in the lower transform half-plane. The [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation.md) is invariant under $k\mapsto i\alpha-k$. This [global relation](global-relation-for-a-linear-boundary-value-problem.md) eliminates one unknown boundary trace after the symmetry maps the inversion contour into the transform's analytic domain.
