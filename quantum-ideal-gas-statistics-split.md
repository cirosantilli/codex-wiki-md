# Quantum ideal-gas statistics

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical physics](statistical-physics-split.md)

An ideal quantum gas has mean occupation

$$
[\exp((E-\mu)/(k_BT))\mp1]^{-1},
$$

with one sign for bosons and the other for fermions.
