# Weak mixed Poisson problem

↑ **Parent:** [Lax-Milgram theorem](lax-milgram-theorem.md)

Let a positive-measure part $\Gamma_D$ of the boundary of a bounded connected domain carry a homogeneous [Dirichlet boundary condition](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md), and let the complementary part carry a homogeneous [Neumann boundary condition](neumann-boundary-condition.md). On

$$
V=\{v\in H^1(U):\operatorname{Tr}v|_{\Gamma_D}=0\},
$$

the weak Poisson problem is

$$
\int_U\nabla u\mathbin\cdot\nabla v=\int_Ufv
\qquad(v\in V).
$$

The [Poincare inequality with a partial Dirichlet boundary](poincare-inequality-with-a-partial-dirichlet-boundary.md) makes the left side coercive, so the [Lax-Milgram theorem](lax-milgram-theorem.md) gives a unique weak solution.
