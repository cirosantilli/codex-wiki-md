# Complex Gaussian exponential moment

↑ **Parent:** [Moment-generating function of a normal distribution](moment-generating-function-of-a-normal-distribution.md)

For a centered complex Gaussian variable $Z$ whose real and imaginary parts are jointly [Gaussian](normal-distribution.md), the displayed formula follows by analytic continuation of the real joint [moment-generating function](moment-generating-function.md). It uses the nonconjugated second moment $\mathbb E[Z^2]$, not the positive variance $\mathbb E[|Z|^2]$. This distinction matters for the coherent mean in the [Rytov approximation](rytov-approximation.md).
