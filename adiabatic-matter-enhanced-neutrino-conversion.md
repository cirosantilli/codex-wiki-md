# Adiabatic matter-enhanced neutrino conversion

↑ **Parent:** [Neutrino oscillation](neutrino-oscillation.md)

Flavor conversion in a slowly varying medium when an occupied instantaneous propagation eigenstate follows its eigenvalue branch without transitions to the other branch. For positive electron-neutrino matter potential and normal two-state ordering, a high-density [electron neutrino](electron-neutrino.md) follows the upper branch into the vacuum [mass eigenstate](mass-eigenstate.md) $\nu_2$. Detection in the electron flavor then has [probability](probability.md) $\sin^2\theta$, rather than the phase-averaged vacuum value $1-\tfrac12\sin^22\theta$. High production density alone does not guarantee adiabatic evolution.
