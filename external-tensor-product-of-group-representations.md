# External tensor product of group representations

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor product of group representations](tensor-product-of-group-representations.md)

For a $G$-[representation](group-representation.md) $V$ and an $H$-[representation](group-representation.md) $W$ over the same [field](field.md), $V\boxtimes W$ is the [representation](group-representation.md) of $G\times H$ on $V\otimes W$ given by $(g,h)(v\otimes w)=gv\otimes hw$. Its [character](character-of-a-representation.md) is $(g,h)\mapsto\chi_V(g)\chi_W(h)$. Over a splitting field in characteristic zero the external products of irreducible [representations](group-representation.md) form all irreducible representations of $G\times H$: character orthogonality gives irreducibility, and their squared degrees sum to $|G||H|$, proving exhaustion. Unlike the tensor product of two representations of one group, the two factors here act independently.
