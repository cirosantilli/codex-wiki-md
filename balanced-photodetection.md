# Balanced photodetection

↑ **Parent:** [Photodiode](photodiode.md)

Subtracting two [photocurrents](photocurrent.md) can suppress a shared classical intensity fluctuation when the optical responses and electronic gains are matched. Independent [shot noise](shot-noise.md) contributions do not cancel: their [variances](variance-split.md) add in the difference. With equal charge $q$ and mean currents $I_1,I_2$, the independent-arrival one-sided current [power spectral density](power-spectral-density.md) is $2q(I_1+I_2)$. Correlated optical inputs require their [covariance](covariance.md) as well.
