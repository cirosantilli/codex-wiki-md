# Standardization of a normal random variable

↑ **Parent:** [Standard normal distribution](standard-normal-distribution.md)

If $X\sim N(\mu,\sigma^2)$ with $\sigma>0$, then $Z=(X-\mu)/\sigma$ has the [standard normal distribution](standard-normal-distribution.md). The [change-of-variables formula for a probability density](change-of-variables-formula-for-a-probability-density.md) gives $f_Z(z)=\sigma f_X(\mu+\sigma z)=\phi(z)$, the [standard normal density](standard-normal-density.md). Thus [standardization of a normal random variable](standardization-of-a-normal-random-variable.md) removes both parameters without changing the [probabilities](probability.md) of corresponding [events](event.md).
