# Defective repeated eigenvalue in a cyclic conversion model

↑ **Parent:** [Three-state cyclic conversion model](three-state-cyclic-conversion-model.md)

When the displayed equality holds, the two nonzero [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) of the [three-state cyclic conversion model](three-state-cyclic-conversion-model.md) coincide at $\lambda=-(a+b+c)/2$. The positive cyclic rates force each [eigenspace](eigenspace.md) to have [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) at most one: if its $A$ coordinate vanished, the first eigenvector equation would force $C=0$, and then the third would force $B=0$. Thus the repeated [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) has only one [eigenvector](eigenvector.md) direction and the [matrix](matrix.md) is not [diagonalizable](diagonalizable-matrix.md). A length-two [Jordan chain](jordan-chain.md) produces both $e^{\lambda t}$ and $te^{\lambda t}$ transients. The rates $(a,b,c)=(1,4,1)$ give an explicit example with $\lambda=-3$.
