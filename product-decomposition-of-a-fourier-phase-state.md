# Product decomposition of a Fourier phase state

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum Fourier transform](quantum-fourier-transform.md)

Write the integer label in most-significant-bit-first order as $l=\sum_jb_j2^{n-j}$. The phase factors as $e^{i\phi l}=\prod_je^{i\phi b_j2^{n-j}}$, giving the displayed [product state](product-state.md). For dyadic Fourier phases this explains why the Fourier basis states are unentangled even though a general [quantum Fourier transform](quantum-fourier-transform.md) can create [entanglement](entangled-state.md).
