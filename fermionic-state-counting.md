# Fermionic state counting

↑ **Parent:** [Zero-temperature filling of finite fermion levels](zero-temperature-filling-of-finite-fermion-levels.md)

Choosing $N$ different complete [one-particle states](one-particle-state.md) from an [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) of size $M$ gives $\binom MN$ independent [Slater determinants](slater-determinant.md). This is the [binomial coefficient](binomial-coefficient-split.md) because only the occupied subset matters: permutations change the sign of the [Slater determinant](slater-determinant.md), not the physical [quantum state](quantum-state.md). Summing over particle numbers gives $2^M$ states in the finite [fermionic Fock space](fermionic-fock-space.md).

For the diagram's two [spatial orbitals](spatial-orbital.md) $A,B$, take energies $0,\Delta$ with $\Delta>0$, each independent of [spin](spin.md). In the complete-state order $(A\uparrow,A\downarrow,B\uparrow,B\downarrow)$, the six $N=2$ [occupation number](occupation-number.md) strings are $1100,1010,1001,0110,0101,0011$. Their total energies are $0,\Delta,\Delta,\Delta,\Delta,2\Delta$, giving energy multiplicities $1,4,1$. For $N=3$, the [ground state](ground-state.md) energy is $\Delta$ with two independent [ground states](ground-state.md), according to which [spin orbital](spin-orbital.md) in $B$ is occupied. Across $N=0,1,2,3,4$, the counts are $1,4,6,4,1$, summing to $16$. These are exact counts for this model, not measured atomic energies.
