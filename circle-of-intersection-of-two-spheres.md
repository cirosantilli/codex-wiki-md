# Circle of intersection of two spheres

↑ **Parent:** [Sphere](sphere.md)

Two [spheres](sphere.md) with centres $a,b$ and positive radii $p,q$ intersect in a nondegenerate [circle](circle.md) exactly under the displayed strict triangle inequalities. Put $d=|b-a|$. Subtracting $|r-a|^2=p^2$ and $|r-b|^2=q^2$ gives its plane,

$$
2(b-a)\cdot r=p^2-q^2+|b|^2-|a|^2.
$$

The circle centre is $a+\ell(b-a)/d$, where $\ell=(p^2-q^2+d^2)/(2d)$, and its squared radius is $p^2-\ell^2$. This is positive exactly under the strict inequalities. Equality gives internal or external tangency; coincident equal [spheres](sphere.md) have an entire sphere in common rather than a unique intersection circle.
