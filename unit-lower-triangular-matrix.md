# Unit lower triangular matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Lower triangular matrix](lower-triangular-matrix.md)

A [lower triangular matrix](lower-triangular-matrix.md) with every diagonal entry equal to one. Its [determinant](determinant.md) is one, and its inverse is again unit lower triangular. Such matrices store the elimination multipliers in an [LU decomposition](lu-decomposition.md).
