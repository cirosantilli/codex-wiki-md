# Galilean invariance

↑ **Parent:** [Principle of Galilean relativity](principle-of-galilean-relativity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galilean_invariance)

A [dynamical symmetry](dynamical-symmetry.md) under [Galilean transformations](galilean-transformation.md). For an isolated pair with constant [masses](mass.md), a [force law](force-law.md) depending only on relative [positions](position.md) and relative [velocities](velocity.md) has this invariance. Equal-and-opposite [forces](force-split.md) alone do not imply it: their magnitude can depend on [centre of mass](center-of-mass.md) [velocity](velocity.md) relative to a preferred frame.
