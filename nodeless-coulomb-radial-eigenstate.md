# Nodeless Coulomb radial eigenstate

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrogen atom](hydrogen-atom.md)

For the nonrelativistic point-Coulomb hydrogen [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md), the lowest radial state at fixed [orbital angular momentum](orbital-angular-momentum.md) $\ell$ has [principal quantum number](principal-quantum-number.md) $n=\ell+1$ and no positive-radius [wavefunction nodes](wavefunction-node.md). Substituting the displayed radial shape cancels the centrifugal inverse-square term and fixes $a_0=4\pi\epsilon_0\hbar^2/(me^2)$. With $b=a_0(\ell+1)$ and unit-normalized [spherical harmonic](spherical-harmonic.md), $A=(2/b)^{\ell+3/2}/\sqrt{(2\ell+2)!}$. Excited radial states require additional polynomial factors.
