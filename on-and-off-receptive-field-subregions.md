# ON and OFF receptive-field subregions

↑ **Parent:** [Receptive field](receptive-field.md)

[ON and OFF receptive-field subregions](on-and-off-receptive-field-subregions.md) distinguish locations by response polarity. In the classic mapping experiment, a bright spot in an ON region increased firing during illumination; illumination in an OFF region suppressed maintained firing and/or evoked a discharge when the light disappeared. If maintained firing was very low, suppression could be hard to see. The recorded decrease alone did not distinguish direct inhibition at a [synapse](synapse.md) from reduced excitation. [Hubel and Wiesel (1959), pp. 576–577: response polarity and inferential limits](https://xbio-live.s3.amazonaws.com/narratives/57358/Hubel%20and%20Wiesel%201959_Guided%20paper.pdf) These are functional regions in visual space, not compartments of a [cell body](soma-biology.md).
