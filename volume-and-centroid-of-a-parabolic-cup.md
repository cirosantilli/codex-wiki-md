# Volume and centroid of a parabolic cup

↑ **Parent:** [Elliptic paraboloid](elliptic-paraboloid.md)

The solid $0\leq z\leq h$, $x^2+y^2\leq az$, with $a,h>0$, has horizontal slice area $\pi az$. Thus its [volume](volume.md) is $\int_0^h\pi az\,dz=\pi ah^2/2$. For a positive constant [mass density](density.md), rotational symmetry puts its [centre of mass](center-of-mass.md) on the vertical axis, and

$$
\bar z=\frac{\int_0^h z(\pi az)dz}{\int_0^h\pi az\,dz}=\frac{2h}{3}.
$$

The curved [surface area](surface-area.md), excluding the horizontal opening, is $\pi a^2[(1+4h/a)^{3/2}-1]/6$. Parametrizing the [elliptic paraboloid](elliptic-paraboloid.md) by $(r\cos\theta,r\sin\theta,r^2/a)$ gives the [area element of a surface](area-element-of-a-surface.md) $r\sqrt{1+4r^2/a^2}\,dr\,d\theta$, with $0\leq r\leq\sqrt{ah}$, proving this last formula. It is also a translated and reflected specialization of [paraboloid sector surface measures](paraboloid-sector-surface-measures.md).
