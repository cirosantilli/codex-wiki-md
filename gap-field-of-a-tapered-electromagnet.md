# Gap field of a tapered electromagnet

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic circuit](magnetic-circuit.md)

A uniform-radius magnetic arc of [length](length.md) $L$ and [radius](radius.md) $R$, two linear tapers of [length](length.md) $s$ to [radius](radius.md) $r$, and an unfringed vacuum gap of [length](length.md) $h$ have the displayed gap field. Here $\mu_r=\mu/\mu_0$ and $B_g=\mu_0H_g$. Tapering concentrates flux, but at fixed winding current the gap-dominated limit is $H_g\simeq NI/h$ regardless of pole [area](surface-area.md). Saturation and leakage prevent unrestricted field enhancement.
