# Exponential sum

↑ **Parent:** [Analytic number theory](analytic-number-theory-split.md)

A finite [sum](sum.md) of [complex exponentials](complex-exponential-function.md), such as $S(\theta)=\sum_n a_ne(n\theta)$ with $e(t)=\exp(2\pi it)$, is an exponential sum. [Cancellation in an exponential sum](cancellation-in-an-exponential-sum.md) between its terms can make it much smaller than the [sum](sum.md) of their [absolute values](absolute-value.md).
