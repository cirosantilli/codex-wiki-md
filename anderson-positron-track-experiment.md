# Anderson positron track experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic rigidity](magnetic-rigidity.md)

The [Anderson positron track experiment](anderson-positron-track-experiment.md) identified a light positively [charged particle](charged-particle.md), now called the [Positron](positron.md), by combining magnetic bending, loss of [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) in [lead](lead.md), and [particle range](particle-range.md). Each observation answered a different question: bending constrained [momentum](momentum-split.md), the [lead](lead.md) supplied a direction of travel, and [particle range](particle-range.md) tested the proposed [mass](mass.md).

[Carl David Anderson](carl-david-anderson.md)'s [The Positive Electron](https://journals.aps.org/pr/pdf/10.1103/PhysRev.43.491) (1933), pp. 491–493, describes the 1932 discovery in a vertical [cloud chamber](cloud-chamber.md) with a $15{,}000\,\mathrm{gauss}$ [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md). A $6\,\mathrm{mm}$ [lead](lead.md) plate separated two photographed track segments. The effective viewing depth was $1\,\mathrm{cm}$ along the [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md), with $14\,\mathrm{cm}$ transverse diameter: this restricted out-of-plane motion. The photographic readout supplied [curvature](curvature.md) on both sides. Comparisons with [electron](electron.md) tracks under the same conditions constrained [ionization](ionization.md); specific [ionization](ionization.md) was not directly measured for these events. He tested accidental alignment of unrelated tracks and rejected a reversed [electron](electron.md) interpretation requiring an increase of [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) in [lead](lead.md). Assuming unit [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md), the [mass](mass.md) bound was about twenty [electron](electron.md) masses, rather than an exact [mass](mass.md) determination. The paper credits [Robert Andrews Millikan](robert-andrews-millikan.md) with the design and [Seth Henry Neddermeyer](seth-henry-neddermeyer.md) with assistance.[pp. 491–493, Fig. 1 and charge/mass discussion; p. 494, acknowledgment](the-positive-electron-by-carl-david-anderson.md)

Preparation used rapid piston expansion and a [water](water.md)–[alcohol](alcohol.md) mixture in the [cloud chamber](cloud-chamber.md); cooling permitted droplets to form along [ionization](ionization.md) trails. A camera photographed these droplets. Initially, expansions occurred at random, so many photographs contained no tracks. Later counter triggering improved the useful yield.[Biographical Memoirs: Volume 73; William H. Pickering, Carl David Anderson, pp. 27–28: preparation, photographs and timing](biographical-memoirs-volume-73/carl-david-anderson-by-william-hayward-pickering.md)

In [The Production and Properties of Positrons](https://www.nobelprize.org/uploads/2018/06/anderson-lecture.pdf), his 1936 lecture, [Carl David Anderson](carl-david-anderson.md) describes the chamber inside an [electromagnet](electromagnet.md) and explains why the [lead](lead.md) plate was decisive: after losing [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md), a particle bent more sharply. He describes a [proton](proton.md) hypothesis predicting only $5\,\mathrm{mm}$ of [particle range](particle-range.md), against an observed path longer than $50\,\mathrm{mm}$. These are historical values from the lecture, not the stipulated example below. The point was the incompatibility of [particle range](particle-range.md) with the [momentum](momentum-split.md) inferred from [curvature](curvature.md), rather than positive [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) alone.[Nobel Lectures, Physics 1922–1941; pp. 365–366, electromagnet, lead-plate direction test and proton range comparison](nobel-lectures-physics-1922-1941/the-production-and-properties-of-positrons-by-carl-david-anderson.md)
