# Hydrogen ground-state probability beyond the classical turning point

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrogen atom](hydrogen-atom.md)

The nonrelativistic hydrogen ground [energy](energy-split.md) is $-e^2/(8\pi\epsilon_0a_0)$. Requiring nonnegative classical [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) restricts radius to $r\leq2a_0$. Integrating the quantum ground-state radial [probability density](probability-density.md) beyond that radius instead gives $13e^{-4}\simeq0.238$. A bound-state tail can therefore have substantial integrated [probability](probability.md) outside the classically allowed region without violating [energy](energy-split.md) conservation.
