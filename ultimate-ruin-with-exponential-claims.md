# Ultimate ruin with exponential claims

↑ **Parent:** [Ultimate ruin probability](ultimate-ruin-probability.md)

For rate-$\alpha$ [exponential distribution](exponential-distribution.md) claims and $c>\lambda/\alpha$, the [ruin integro-differential equation](ruin-integro-differential-equation.md) reduces to $\psi''+(\alpha-\lambda/c)\psi'=0$. Differentiate the convolution and use its equation $A'=\alpha\psi-\alpha A$ to eliminate it. Positive [relative safety loading](relative-safety-loading.md) implies $\psi(u)\to0$ as $u\to\infty$, and the [zero-capital survival probability](zero-capital-survival-probability.md) gives $\psi(0)=\lambda/(\alpha c)$. These two conditions determine the displayed solution. The [adjustment coefficient](adjustment-coefficient.md) is $\alpha-\lambda/c$.
