# Payoff

↑ **Parent:** [Game theory](game-theory-split.md)

A payoff is the numerical outcome assigned to a player for a profile of [pure strategies](pure-strategy.md). In a finite [zero-sum game](zero-sum-game.md), one player's payoff is the negative of the other's. For [mixed strategies](mixed-strategy.md), the expected payoff is the probability-weighted average of the entries of the [payoff matrix](payoff-matrix.md).
