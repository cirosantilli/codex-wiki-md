# Payoff

↑ **Parent:** [Game theory](game-theory-split.md)

A [payoff](payoff.md) is the number assigned to a player's outcome at a [strategy profile](strategy-profile.md), with larger numbers preferred. In the usual [expected utility](expected-utility.md) model, [expected payoffs](expected-payoff.md) are [probability](probability.md)-weighted averages of these numbers. If a [payoff](payoff.md) is specified as money and a player has preferences involving risk, first apply the relevant [utility function](utility-function-split.md); maximizing expected money assumes [risk neutrality](risk-neutrality.md). In a two-player [zero-sum game](zero-sum-game.md), one player's [payoff](payoff.md) is the negative of the other's. General [bimatrix games](bimatrix-game.md) instead require a [payoff matrix](payoff-matrix.md) for each player.
