# Perfect field

↑ **Parent:** [Field](field.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perfect_field)

A field is perfect when every algebraic extension is separable. In positive characteristic $p$, this is equivalent to surjectivity of the [Frobenius endomorphism](frobenius-endomorphism.md) $x\mapsto x^p$; every [finite field](finite-field.md) is perfect.
