# Prime-to-residue-characteristic power map on principal units

↑ **Parent:** [Principal unit](principal-unit.md)

In a complete [discrete valuation ring](discrete-valuation-ring.md) of residue characteristic $p$, the power map $u\mapsto u^m$ is a bijection on $1+\mathfrak m$ when $p\nmid m$. For a [principal unit](principal-unit.md) $a$, the [Hensel lemma](hensel-s-lemma.md) lifts the simple residue root one of $T^m-a$ uniquely. This can absorb principal-unit factors in tame radical descriptions of [local fields](local-field.md).
