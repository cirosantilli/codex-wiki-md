# Coherent quantum feedback

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum control](quantum-control.md)

In [coherent quantum feedback](coherent-quantum-feedback.md), a quantum controller interacts with the controlled system without first converting the control signal into a classical measurement record. Optical feedback through a [beam splitter](beam-splitter.md) is one example. The input-output interconnection must preserve the physical quantum dynamics, including noise and commutation relations; a classical state-feedback formula does not automatically define a valid quantum controller.
