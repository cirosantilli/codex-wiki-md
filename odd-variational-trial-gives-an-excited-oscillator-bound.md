# Odd variational trial gives an excited oscillator bound

↑ **Parent:** [Rayleigh quotient](rayleigh-quotient.md)

An odd trial state is orthogonal to the even oscillator ground state. Its [Rayleigh quotient](rayleigh-quotient.md) therefore bounds the first excited energy from above, unlike an arbitrary trial state. The kinetic [quadratic form](quadratic-form.md) permits continuous compactly supported trials with [derivative](derivative.md) jumps.
