# Bit shift

↑ **Parent:** [Programming language](programming-language.md)

A fixed-width left shift discards high bits and inserts low zeros. A logical right shift inserts high zeros; an arithmetic right shift propagates the sign bit. In [Java](java-programming-language.md), `<<`, `>>>` and `>>` distinguish these operations. Left shift by $k$ is multiplication by $2^k$ modulo the word modulus.
