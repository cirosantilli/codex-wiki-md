# Center-of-mass energy

↑ **Parent:** [Four-momentum](four-momentum.md)

For future-directed particles with total [four-momentum](four-momentum.md) $P$ and [metric signature](metric-signature.md) $(-+++)$, the positive square root of $-P_\mu P^\mu$ is their energy in the frame where total spatial momentum vanishes. Its invariant definition remains valid at a collision in [spacetime](spacetime.md).
