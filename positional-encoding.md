# Positional encoding

↑ **Parent:** [Transformer model](transformer-model.md)

Information representing sequence position or relative position, supplied to input representations or to attention scores. It can be fixed or learned. Without order information or a position-dependent mask, a [self-attention](self-attention.md) layer using shared projections treats a simultaneous permutation of sequence positions equivariantly.
