# Primitive twist at coprime level

↑ **Parent:** [Character twist by rational translations of a cusp form](character-twist-by-rational-translations-of-a-cusp-form.md)

For a [primitive Dirichlet character](primitive-dirichlet-character.md) $\chi$ modulo $D$, its [Gauss sum of a Dirichlet character](gauss-sum-of-a-dirichlet-character.md) gives $f_\chi=\tau(\overline\chi)^{-1}\sum_u\overline\chi(u)f|U_u$, with $U_u=\begin{pmatrix}1&u/D\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$. If $\gamma=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix}\in\Gamma_0(ND^2)$, take $v\equiv d^2u\pmod D$. Then $U_u\gamma U_v^{-1}\in\Gamma_0(N)$ and its lower-right entry is congruent to $d$ modulo $N$. Reindexing the sum yields the character $\psi(d)\chi(d)^2$. Rational translations preserve vanishing at [modular cusps](cusp-of-a-modular-group.md), proving the stated cuspidality as well as the transformation law.
