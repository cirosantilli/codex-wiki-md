# Interior H2 regularity for continuous nondivergence coefficients

↑ **Parent:** [Coefficient-freezing interior second-derivative estimate](coefficient-freezing-interior-second-derivative-estimate.md)

Let $L=a^{ij}(x)D_{ij}$ be uniformly elliptic with continuous coefficients. The [coefficient-freezing interior second-derivative estimate](coefficient-freezing-interior-second-derivative-estimate.md) extends from smooth functions to the [graph norm](graph-norm.md) closure of $L$ on $H^1(U)$: if $u_k\to u$ in $H^1(U)$ and $Lu_k\to f$ in $L^2(U)$, then $(u_k)$ is Cauchy in $H^2(W)$ for every $W\Subset U$. Hence $u\in H^2_{\mathrm{loc}}(U)$ and $Lu=f$. Equivalently, the standard local regularization argument gives the same conclusion for [weak solutions](weak-solution.md).
