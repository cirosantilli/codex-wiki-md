<h1 id="hilbert-s-irreducibility-theorem">Hilbert's irreducibility theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Hilbertian field](hilbertian-field.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hilbert's_irreducibility_theorem)

The rational numbers, and every [number field](number-field.md), are [Hilbertian fields](hilbertian-field.md). The classical integral form supplies infinitely many integral specializations avoiding a prescribed finite exceptional set. The theorem preserves irreducibility, rather than asserting that every specialization works: $X^2-T$ becomes reducible at square parameters.
