# Right-triangle geometry of internal-wave velocities

↑ **Parent:** [Internal-wave phase and group velocity](internal-wave-phase-and-group-velocity.md)

Choose $k,m>0$, $K=(k^2+m^2)^{1/2}$ and the positive [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) $\omega=Nk/K$. Then the [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md) is $Nk(k,m)/K^3$ and the [group velocity](group-velocity.md) is $N(m^2,-km)/K^3$. Their [dot product](dot-product.md) vanishes and their sum is horizontal with magnitude $N/K$. Draw the [group velocity](group-velocity.md) from the tip of the [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md): the two form the perpendicular sides of a [right triangle](right-triangle.md). The horizontal hypotenuse is their sum, not the [horizontal phase velocity](horizontal-phase-velocity.md) $\omega/k$.
