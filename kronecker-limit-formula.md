# Kronecker limit formula

↑ **Parent:** [Completed nonholomorphic Eisenstein series](completed-nonholomorphic-eisenstein-series.md)

This normalization of the first Kronecker limit formula follows from the [Fourier expansion](fourier-series-split.md) of the [completed nonholomorphic Eisenstein series](completed-nonholomorphic-eisenstein-series.md). At $s=1$, $K_{1/2}(t)=\sqrt{\pi/(2t)}e^{-t}$, so the nonconstant terms sum to $-2\log|\prod_{m\geq1}(1-q^m)|$. The constant terms supply $\pi y/6-(\log y)/2+(\gamma-\log(4\pi))/2$. Combining them gives the [Dedekind eta function](dedekind-eta-function.md) expression, with $\gamma$ the [Euler--Mascheroni constant](euler-s-constant.md).
