# Szlenk index

↑ **Parent:** [Weak topology](weak-topology-split.md)

The Szlenk index measures how many transfinite [Szlenk derivations](szlenk-derivation.md) are needed to empty the dual unit ball at each positive norm-diameter scale. At a successor ordinal take another derivation; at a limit ordinal intersect the previous sets. Taking the supremum of the emptying ordinals over the positive scales gives an ordinal invariant of the Banach space.
