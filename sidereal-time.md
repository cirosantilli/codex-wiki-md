# Sidereal time

↑ **Parent:** [Astrophysics](astrophysics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sidereal_time)

Local [sidereal time](sidereal-time.md) is the [right ascension](right-ascension.md) on the upper [celestial meridian](celestial-meridian.md). A sidereal day is approximately $1436.07$ minutes; its angular rate is $2\pi/T_{\rm sid}$. Mean and apparent [sidereal time](sidereal-time.md) must be paired with corresponding [equatorial coordinate systems](equatorial-coordinate-system.md).
