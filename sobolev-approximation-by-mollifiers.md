# Sobolev approximation by mollifiers

↑ **Parent:** [Approximate identity](approximate-identity.md)

For $1\leq p<\infty$, an integer $k\geq0$, and a normalized nonnegative [mollifier](mollifier.md) $\rho\in C_c^\infty(\mathbb R^n)$, put $\rho_\varepsilon(x)=\varepsilon^{-n}\rho(x/\varepsilon)$. If $u\in W^{k,p}(\mathbb R^n)$, its [mollification](mollification.md) is smooth and converges to $u$ in the [Sobolev norm](sobolev-norm.md). The definition of a [weak derivative](weak-derivative.md) and [Fubini's theorem](fubini-s-theorem.md) give $\partial^\alpha(\rho_\varepsilon*u)=\rho_\varepsilon*\partial^\alpha u$ for every [multi-index](multi-index-notation.md) with $|\alpha|\leq k$. The [approximate identity](approximate-identity.md) theorem in the [Lp space](lp-space.md) $L^p$ applied to these finitely many [weak derivatives](weak-derivative.md) proves the claim. On a domain with boundary this direct [convolution](convolution.md) argument applies only away from the boundary unless an extension is first supplied.
