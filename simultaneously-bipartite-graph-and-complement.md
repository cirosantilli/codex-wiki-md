# Simultaneously bipartite graph and complement

↑ **Parent:** [Complement graph](complement-graph.md)

If a graph and its [complement graph](complement-graph.md) are both [bipartite](bipartite-graph.md), then the graph has at most four vertices. Indeed, each part of a bipartition of the original graph induces a clique in the complement and therefore has at most two vertices. The bound is attained by the four-vertex path, whose complement is another four-vertex path.
