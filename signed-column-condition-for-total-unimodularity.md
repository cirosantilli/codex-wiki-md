# Signed column condition for total unimodularity

↑ **Parent:** [Totally unimodular matrix](totally-unimodular-matrix.md)

A [matrix](matrix.md) with entries in $\{0,1,-1\}$ is a [totally unimodular matrix](totally-unimodular-matrix.md) if every column has at most two nonzero entries, with opposite signs when both occur. To prove this, induct on the size of a square submatrix. A zero column gives [determinant](determinant.md) zero, a one-entry column reduces by [cofactor](cofactor.md) expansion, and if every column has two entries then all column sums vanish, giving dependent rows. The [total unimodularity of directed incidence matrices](total-unimodularity-of-directed-incidence-matrices.md) is a central instance.
