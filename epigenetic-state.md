# Epigenetic state

↑ **Parent:** [Epigenetic inheritance](epigenetic-inheritance.md)

A regulatory state associated with [chromatin](chromatin.md), DNA modification or a self-maintaining regulatory process. Persistence through cell divisions and transmission through sexual reproduction are distinct properties.
