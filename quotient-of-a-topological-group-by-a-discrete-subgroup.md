# Quotient of a topological group by a discrete subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Covering space action](covering-space-action.md)

If $\Gamma$ is a discrete subgroup of a [topological group](topological-group-split.md) $G$, then left translation by $\Gamma$ is a covering-space action. Choose an identity neighbourhood $U$ with $UU^{-1}\cap\Gamma=\{1\}$; the translates $\gamma U$ are pairwise disjoint, and the quotient map $G\to\Gamma\backslash G$ restricts to a homeomorphism on each translate.
