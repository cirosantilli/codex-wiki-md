# Known-bit quantum money forgery

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum money](quantum-money.md)

If a known classical bit is independently encoded in a secret uniformly chosen X or Z eigenbasis, the averaged verification operator is $A_b=I/2+(-1)^b(X+Z)/4$. Its largest [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) is the displayed optimal acceptance [probability](probability.md) for a [counterfeit](known-bit-quantum-money-forgery.md) prepared without the original [qubit](qubit.md). The maximizing state bisects the two candidate Bloch directions. For $n$ independent secret bases, the maximal all-pass [probability](probability.md) is the $n$th [power](power.md), because the total acceptance operator is a [tensor product](tensor-product.md). Randomly choosing one of the legitimate encodings instead passes with [probability](probability.md) $3/4$ per bit.
