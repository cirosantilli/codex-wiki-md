# Fine-structure constant

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum electrodynamics](quantum-electrodynamics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fine-structure_constant)

In rationalized [natural units](natural-units.md), the [fine-structure constant](fine-structure-constant.md) is $\alpha=e^2/(4\pi)$. It measures the electromagnetic interaction strength. Its use in a specified perturbative calculation requires a consistent coupling and [renormalization scale](renormalization-scale.md) convention.
