# Right-invariant differential form

↑ **Parent:** [Lie group](lie-group.md)

A [differential form](differential-form-split.md) $\alpha$ on a [Lie group](lie-group.md) is right-invariant if $R_g^*\alpha=\alpha$ for every right translation $R_g(p)=pg$. A right [Maurer-Cartan form](maurer-cartan-form.md) is represented in a matrix group by $dg\,g^{-1}$, whereas the left version is $g^{-1}dg$. Its components supply a right-invariant coframe.
