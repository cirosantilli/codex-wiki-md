# Normal phonon collisions and heat-current relaxation

↑ **Parent:** [Phonon scattering](phonon-scattering.md)

Normal phonon collisions conserve the crystal momentum of the distribution, whereas [Umklapp scattering](umklapp-scattering.md) transfers momentum by a reciprocal vector. A normal collision can redistribute heat-carrying modes without relaxing a drifting distribution in the same manner as a momentum-relaxing event. Boundaries, impurities and Umklapp processes can then set the resistance. Counting all collision rates as interchangeable inverse transport times can miss collective phonon flow and nontrivial current weighting.
