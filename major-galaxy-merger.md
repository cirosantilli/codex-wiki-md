# Major galaxy merger

↑ **Parent:** [Galaxy merger](galaxy-merger.md)

A [major galaxy merger](major-galaxy-merger.md) joins galaxies with comparable masses. Rapid changes in the gravitational potential can redistribute stellar orbital energies through [violent relaxation](violent-relaxation.md), substantially disturbing preexisting [galactic disks](galactic-disk.md). The outcome depends on gas content, orbit and subsequent accretion; a major merger does not universally prohibit disk rebuilding.
