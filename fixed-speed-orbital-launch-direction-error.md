# Fixed-speed orbital launch-direction error

↑ **Parent:** [Orbital eccentricity](orbital-eccentricity.md)

Launching at the correct circular [orbital speed](orbital-speed.md) but at in-plane [angle](angle.md) $\alpha$ from the tangent leaves specific [orbital energy](orbital-energy.md) $-GM/(2R)$, hence [semi-major axis](semi-major-axis.md) $R$. The specific [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) becomes $\sqrt{GMR}\cos\alpha$, so $j^2=GMa(1-e^2)$ gives $e=|\sin\alpha|$. The actual periapsis and apoapsis are $R(1\mp e)$, equally far from the intended circular orbit. The maximum geometric radial separation is therefore $Re$. This is not an equal-time separation between two moving satellites.
