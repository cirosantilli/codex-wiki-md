# Past timelike infinity

↑ **Parent:** [Timelike infinity](timelike-infinity.md)

This is the past endpoint of complete inertial [timelike geodesics](timelike-geodesic.md) in the [Minkowski conformal compactification](minkowski-conformal-compactification.md), at $(T,R)=(-\pi,0)$. It is the time reversal of [future timelike infinity](future-timelike-infinity.md) and is reached at infinite physical [proper time](proper-time.md) to the past.
