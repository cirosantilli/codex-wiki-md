# Smoothing bandwidth

↑ **Parent:** [Kernel for nonparametric regression](kernel-for-nonparametric-regression.md)

A [smoothing bandwidth](smoothing-bandwidth.md) determines the spatial scale over which a [regression kernel](kernel-for-nonparametric-regression.md) averages data. Larger values reduce [variance](variance-split.md) and may increase the [bias of an estimator](bias-of-an-estimator.md).
