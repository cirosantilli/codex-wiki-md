<h1 id="so-3-vector-higgs-model">SO(3) vector Higgs model</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Higgs mechanism](higgs-mechanism.md)

A [Yang-Mills theory](yang-mills-theory.md) with [gauge group](gauge-group.md) $SO(3)$ and a real three-component [scalar field](scalar-field.md) in its defining vector representation. With a symmetry-breaking [scalar potential](scalar-potential.md), a nonzero vector vacuum leaves its $SO(2)$ [stabilizer subgroup](stabilizer-subgroup.md) unbroken. The two transverse internal directions produce massive [gauge bosons](gauge-boson.md).
