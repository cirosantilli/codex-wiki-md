# Selection model for informative longitudinal dropout

↑ **Parent:** [Missing not at random](missing-not-at-random.md)

Model the joint distribution by multiplying a response model by a sequential dropout model. For example, the [logit](logit.md) of dropout before visit $j$, conditional on remaining observed, can depend on the currently unobserved $Y_{ij}$ as well as previous responses and baseline [covariates](covariate.md). The observed-data [likelihood](likelihood-function.md) sums over the missing binary responses. Such a dependence parameter is usually weakly identified or unidentified from observed responses alone; [sensitivity analysis](sensitivity-analysis.md) must vary it over scientifically justified values.
