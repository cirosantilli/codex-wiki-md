# Elastic polarization

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic strain Green operator](elastic-strain-green-operator.md)

The [elastic polarization](elastic-polarization.md) is the excess [stress](stress.md) relative to a homogeneous comparison [elastic stiffness tensor](elastic-stiffness-tensor.md). In heterogeneous [linear elasticity](linear-elasticity.md), $\tau=(C-C^0)\varepsilon$. The comparison [static elastic equilibrium](static-elastic-equilibrium.md) equation gives the integral equation $\varepsilon=\varepsilon^0-\Gamma\tau$, where $\varepsilon^0$ is the reference [strain](strain.md) under the same external loading.
