# Local maximum

↑ **Parent:** [Critical point](critical-point.md)

A local maximum is a local form of an [extremum of a real-valued function](maximum-and-minimum.md).

A [function](function-split.md) has a local maximum at $x_0$ if $f(x)\leq f(x_0)$ throughout some neighbourhood of $x_0$. The inequality need not be strict. Negating the [function](function-split.md) exchanges local maxima and [local minima](local-minimum.md).
