# High-pressure limit of magnetosonic waves

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetosonic wave](magnetosonic-wave.md)

For [sound speed](speed-of-sound.md) $v_s$ much larger than [Alfvén speed](alfven-speed.md) $v_a$, and angle $\theta$ between the [wavevector](wavevector.md) and [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md), the [fast magnetosonic wave](fast-magnetosonic-wave.md) has $v_f^2=v_s^2+v_a^2\sin^2\theta+O(v_a^4/v_s^2)$. The [slow magnetosonic wave](slow-magnetosonic-wave.md) has $v_{\rm slow}^2=v_a^2\cos^2\theta[1-(v_a^2/v_s^2)\sin^2\theta+O(v_a^4/v_s^4)]$. The fast mode is predominantly longitudinal sound. The slow mode becomes almost incompressible, polarized in the plane of the [wavevector](wavevector.md) and [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md); the [Alfvén wave](alfven-wave.md) is polarized perpendicular to that plane. The latter two are predominantly magnetic-tension waves with equal leading [phase speeds](phase-speed.md).
