# Spherical surface-wave evanescence threshold

↑ **Parent:** [Seismic surface-wave dispersion](seismic-surface-wave-dispersion.md)

For surface phase [speed](speed.md) $c$ on a spherical [Earth](earth.md), the conserved angular ray parameter is $p=R_E/c$. The local horizontal [slowness](slowness.md) at radius $r$ is $p/r$, giving squared radial shear [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) $q_r^2=\omega^2[\beta(r)^{-2}-p^2/r^2]$ in ray theory. The displayed inequality makes it negative. A jump in shear [speed](speed.md) can turn a propagating wave above the interface into an [evanescent wave](evanescent-wave.md) below it. This is a local condition, not proof of evanescence at every greater depth.
