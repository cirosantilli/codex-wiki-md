# Weyl group of Dn

↑ **Parent:** [Dn root system](dn-root-system.md)

The [Weyl group](weyl-group.md) of the [Dn root system](dn-root-system.md) consists of coordinate permutations together with an even number of sign changes. Reflection in $\varepsilon_i-\varepsilon_j$ swaps coordinates $i,j$; reflection in $\varepsilon_i+\varepsilon_j$ swaps them and negates both. These generate all even signed permutations, giving order $2^{n-1}n!$.
