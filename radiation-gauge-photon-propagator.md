# Radiation-gauge photon propagator

↑ **Parent:** [Photon propagator](photon-propagator.md)

Time-ordering the [canonical transverse photon field](canonical-transverse-photon-field.md) gives a propagator with numerator equal to the spatial transverse projector and denominator $k^2+i0$. Its two energy poles encode forward propagation of the positive-frequency term and backward propagation of the negative-frequency term. This is the reduced free-field propagator; it is distinct from the covariant four-potential propagator.
