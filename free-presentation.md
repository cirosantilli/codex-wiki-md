# Free presentation

↑ **Parent:** [Group presentation](group-presentation.md)

A free presentation of a group is a quotient $G\cong F/R$ in which $F$ is a [free group](free-group.md) and $R$ is the [normal closure](normal-closure.md) of the chosen [relators](relator.md).
