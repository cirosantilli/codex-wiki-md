# Countable-test proof of ergodicity of conditional components

↑ **Parent:** [Ergodic component](ergodic-component.md)

Apply the [Birkhoff ergodic theorem](birkhoff-ergodic-theorem.md) to a countable generating algebra, and disintegrate the resulting common full-measure convergence set over the [invariant sigma-algebra](invariant-sigma-algebra.md). The orbit limits on a fiber are its conditional masses. Applying the theorem to that invariant component shows its invariant projection is constant on all generating indicators, hence on all $L^2$ functions by density. This proves [ergodicity](ergodicity.md) without an uncountable intersection of null sets.
