# Detachment fault

↑ **Parent:** [Normal fault](normal-fault.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Detachment_fault)

A long-lived low-angle [normal fault](normal-fault.md) that can exhume deep [crust](planetary-crust.md) or [mantle](planetary-mantle.md). Its mapped scarp need not be a plate-motion arrow.
