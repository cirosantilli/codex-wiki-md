# Reissner-Nordstrom Kretschmann scalar

↑ **Parent:** [Reissner-Nordstrom spacetime](reissner-nordstrom-spacetime.md)

For the [Reissner-Nordstrom metric](reissner-nordstrom-spacetime.md) with $F=1-2M/r+Q^2/r^2$, the [Kretschmann scalar](kretschmann-scalar.md) is

$$
\mathcal K=(F'')^2+4\left(\frac{F'}r\right)^2+4\left(\frac{1-F}{r^2}\right)^2
=\frac{48M^2}{r^6}-\frac{96MQ^2}{r^7}+\frac{56Q^4}{r^8}.
$$

The first expression follows by squaring the independent [Riemann curvature tensor](riemann-curvature-tensor-split.md) components in an [orthonormal frame](orthonormal-frame-in-spacetime.md). This [Kretschmann scalar](kretschmann-scalar.md) diverges at $r=0$ for any nonzero $M$ or $Q$, but is finite at every positive root of $F$. Regular [Eddington-Finkelstein coordinates](eddington-finkelstein-coordinates.md) show that those positive roots are [coordinate singularities](coordinate-singularity.md) of the static chart. In the extremal case $Q^2=M^2>0$, its horizon value is $8/M^4$.
