# S phase

↑ **Parent:** [Interphase](interphase.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S_phase)

During [S phase](s-phase.md), nuclear [DNA replication](dna-replication.md) produces paired [sister chromatids](sister-chromatids.md). [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) content rises from $2C$ to $4C$, while the [chromosome](chromosome.md) count remains $2n$.
