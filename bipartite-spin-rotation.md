# Bipartite spin rotation

↑ **Parent:** [Heisenberg antiferromagnet](heisenberg-antiferromagnet.md)

A [bipartite spin rotation](bipartite-spin-rotation.md) rotates the [spin](spin.md) on one sublattice by $\pi$, converting an alternating classical [Néel state](neel-state.md) to an all-up reference state. Rotation about $x$ sends $S^z\mapsto-S^z$ and $S^\pm\mapsto S^\mp$ on that sublattice. For an isotropic nearest-neighbor [Heisenberg antiferromagnet](heisenberg-antiferromagnet.md), a bond becomes $-S_i^zS_j^z+(S_i^+S_j^++S_i^-S_j^-)/2$. The transformation is unitary and preserves the [spin commutation relations](spin-commutation-relations.md); it changes the reference frame, not the model's spectrum.
