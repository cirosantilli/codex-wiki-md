# Chaikin basis function

↑ **Parent:** [Chaikin subdivision](chaikin-subdivision.md)

This centered [quadratic cardinal B-spline](quadratic-cardinal-b-spline.md) weights each original control ordinate at its uniformly spaced abscissa. Its translates sum to one. It and its first [derivative](derivative.md) match at half-integer knots, while the second [derivative](derivative.md) can jump. The limit graph is $\sum_i y_i\beta((x-x_i)/h)$; one ordinate influences exactly three original intervals. Refinement of a quadratic adds $ah^2/4$ in the limit, yielding this basis by extending each three-point neighborhood quadratically.
