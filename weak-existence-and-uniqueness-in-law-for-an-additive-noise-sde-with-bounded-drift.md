# Weak existence and uniqueness in law for an additive-noise SDE with bounded drift

↑ **Parent:** [Weak solution of a stochastic differential equation](weak-solution-of-a-stochastic-differential-equation.md)

If $b:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ is bounded and measurable, then on every finite time interval

$$
dX_t=b(X_t)dt+dW_t
$$

has a weak solution and [uniqueness in law](uniqueness-in-law.md). Starting with [Wiener measure](wiener-measure.md), the [Novikov condition](novikov-s-condition.md) and [Girsanov theorem](girsanov-theorem.md) add the drift. Applying the inverse change of measure to any weak solution recovers Wiener measure and identifies its law by the same pathwise density.
