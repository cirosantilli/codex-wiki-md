# Chromatic number of Euclidean space

↑ **Parent:** [Chromatic number](chromatic-number.md)

This is the least number of colors needed to color all points of [Euclidean space](euclidean-norm.md) so that points at [Euclidean distance](euclidean-distance.md) one receive different colors. Any finite unit-distance graph embedded in the space gives a lower bound. If $p$ is prime, the characteristic vectors of all $(2p-1)$-subsets of $[4p-1]$, divided by $\sqrt{2p}$, give such a graph: its edges are exactly pairs intersecting in $p-1$ points. The [prime-power modular intersection bound](prime-power-modular-intersection-bound.md) bounds an independent set by $\binom{4p-1}{p-1}$, and hence gives $\chi(\mathbb R^{4p-1})\geq\binom{4p-1}{2p-1}/\binom{4p-1}{p-1}$.
