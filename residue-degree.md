# Residue degree

↑ **Parent:** [Local field](local-field.md)

The degree of the [residue field](residue-field.md) extension induced by a finite extension of complete discretely valued fields. Along with the [ramification index](ramification-index.md), it satisfies $[L:K]=ef$. An [unramified extension](unramified-extension.md) additionally has separable residue extension; a [totally ramified extension](totally-ramified-extension.md) has residue degree one.
