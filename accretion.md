# Accretion

↑ **Parent:** [Astrophysics](astrophysics-split.md)

Growth of an astronomical object's [mass](mass.md) by capture of surrounding matter. During [planetary scattering](planetary-scattering.md), a collision can lead to [accretion](accretion.md) if material is retained by the [planet](planet.md); not every collision retains all material. An [accretion disk](accretion-disk.md) is one particular geometry supplying material.
