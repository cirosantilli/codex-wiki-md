<h1 id="brownian-rough-path-martingale-holder-bound">Brownian rough path martingale Hölder bound</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Nested polygonal approximation of Brownian rough paths](nested-polygonal-approximation-of-brownian-rough-paths.md)

Condition on the Brownian values at a dyadic mesh. First-level increments and antisymmetric area increments of its polygonal lift are conditional expectations of the corresponding Brownian increments. Conditional Jensen inequalities and the homogeneous degree-two norm give the displayed bound. The [Doob maximal inequality](doob-maximal-inequality-for-a-nonnegative-submartingale.md) makes the right-hand side uniformly finite almost surely when the Brownian Hölder norm has sufficiently many moments. Dense-time martingale convergence and equicontinuity then yield simultaneous convergence at every time.
