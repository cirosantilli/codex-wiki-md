# Comoving momentum

↑ **Parent:** [Free streaming](free-streaming.md)

[Spatial translation](spatial-translation.md) symmetry in a flat [FRW metric](friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md) conserves the covariant [canonical momentum](canonical-momentum.md) $q_i=ma^2dx^i/ds$ of a freely falling massive particle. An orthonormal spatial frame gives $p_{{\rm phys},i}=q_i/a$, so physical [momentum](momentum.md) [redshifts](redshift.md) as $a^{-1}$ even while the particle changes from relativistic to nonrelativistic motion. The local [energy](energy.md) is $E=\sqrt{m^2+q^2/a^2}$ and the coordinate [velocity](velocity.md) is $d\mathbf x/dt=\mathbf q/[a\sqrt{q^2+m^2a^2}]$.
