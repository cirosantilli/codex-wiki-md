# Conic bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Morphism of algebraic varieties](morphism-of-algebraic-varieties.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conic_bundle)

A flat proper [morphism of algebraic varieties](morphism-of-algebraic-varieties.md) whose geometric fibres are [plane conics](plane-conic.md), usually presented as a quadratic equation in a [projective bundle](projective-bundle.md) of relative dimension two. The generic fibre is normally required to be smooth; singular conics can occur in special fibres. A [conic bundle from a line on a smooth cubic surface](conic-bundle-from-a-line-on-a-smooth-cubic-surface.md) has five reducible fibres over the [projective line](projective-line.md).
