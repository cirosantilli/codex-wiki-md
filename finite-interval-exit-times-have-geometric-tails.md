# Finite-interval exit times have geometric tails

↑ **Parent:** [Birth-death chain](birth-death-chain.md)

For a nearest-neighbour [Markov chain](markov-chain.md) on $\{L,\ldots,U\}$ with positive right-step probabilities in its interior, let $m=U-L$ and $p_* >0$ be their minimum. From every interior state, at most $m$ consecutive right steps reach $U$, with probability at least $p_*^m$. The [Markov property](markov-property.md) then gives $\mathbb P(T>km)\leq(1-p_*^m)^k$ for the first boundary [hitting time](first-passage-time.md). Thus $T$ is almost surely finite and has finite expectation, regardless of the [holding probability of a Markov chain](holding-probability-of-a-markov-chain.md) at each interior state.
