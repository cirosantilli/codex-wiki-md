# Invariant measures of the two-dimensional voter model

↑ **Parent:** [Voter-model duality](voter-model-duality.md)

In a nearest-neighbor [voter model](voter-model.md) on the [square lattice](square-lattice.md), any two ancestral [coalescing random walks](coalescing-random-walks.md) meet almost surely. For an [invariant probability measure of a Markov process](invariant-probability-law-of-a-markov-process.md), the disagreement [probability](probability.md) is constant in time but bounded by the vanishing [probability](probability.md) of noncoalescence. Hence every pair of sites agrees almost surely. Countability concentrates the measure on the two constant configurations; both are [absorbing state](absorbing-state.md), so every mixture of their [Dirac measures](dirac-measure.md) is invariant. [Translation invariance](translation-invariance.md) need not be assumed.
