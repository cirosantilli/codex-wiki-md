# Supersymmetry transformation

↑ **Parent:** [Supersymmetry generator](supersymmetry-generator.md)

A [supersymmetry transformation](supersymmetry-transformation.md) is the infinitesimal action generated by an odd [supercharge](supersymmetry-generator.md), with constant anticommuting spinor parameter in a global theory. In [superspace](superspace.md) it acts as $\delta\mathcal S=(\epsilon Q+\bar\epsilon\bar Q)\mathcal S$, with possible conventional overall factors of $i$. On the fermion of a [chiral superfield](chiral-superfield.md), the transformation includes $\delta\psi=\sqrt2\epsilon F_{\mathrm{aux}}+i\sqrt2\sigma^\mu\bar\epsilon\,\partial_\mu\phi$. A nonzero vacuum [auxiliary field](auxiliary-field.md) therefore gives an inhomogeneous fermion shift, identifying the [Goldstino](goldstino.md) direction when global [supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md) is spontaneously broken.
