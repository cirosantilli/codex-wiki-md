# Saturated vapor column on a dry adiabat

↑ **Parent:** [Mass path](mass-path.md)

For a dilute saturated vapour with constant [latent heat](latent-heat.md) $L$ in a dry atmosphere, set $x=R_A/c_{pA}$, $\epsilon=R_A/R_B$ and $\beta=L/(R_BT_s)$. Its [mass path](mass-path.md) is $W=\epsilon e_s(T_s)e^\beta E_1(\beta)/(gx)$. Substituting $y=\beta e^{x\zeta}$ in the log-pressure column integral proves this identity. For $\beta\gg1$, $W\sim c_{pA}T_s e_s(T_s)/(gL)$ with relative correction $-1/\beta+O(\beta^{-2})$.
