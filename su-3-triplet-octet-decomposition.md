<h1 id="su-3-triplet-octet-decomposition">SU(3) triplet-octet decomposition</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor product of Lie algebra representations](tensor-product-of-lie-algebra-representations.md)

The summands have [Dynkin labels](dynkin-label.md) $(2,1)$, $(0,2)$ and $(1,0)$. Convolving the triplet and octet [weight diagrams](weight-diagram.md) and applying [highest-weight character subtraction](highest-weight-character-subtraction.md) removes these three characters in that order. Their dimensions $15+6+3=24$ match the product.
