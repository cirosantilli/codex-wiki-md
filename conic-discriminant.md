# Conic discriminant

↑ **Parent:** [Conic bundle](conic-bundle.md)

In characteristic different from two, a local equation for a [conic bundle](conic-bundle.md) is $z^{\mathsf T}Mz=0$ with $M$ symmetric. A fibre is singular exactly when the [determinant](determinant.md) vanishes. Changing the fibre coordinates multiplies that determinant by a nonzero square, so its zero locus is intrinsically defined even when the quadratic coefficients belong to twisting [line bundles](line-bundle.md).
