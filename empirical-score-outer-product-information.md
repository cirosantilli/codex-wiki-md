# Empirical score outer-product information

↑ **Parent:** [Fisher information matrix](fisher-information-matrix.md)

The empirical score outer-product matrix is

$$
i_n(\theta)=\frac1n\sum_{j=1}^n
\nabla_\theta\log f(X_j,\theta)
\nabla_\theta\log f(X_j,\theta)^T.
$$

Under regularity and consistency of $\widehat\theta$, $i_n(\widehat\theta)$ converges in probability to the [Fisher information matrix](fisher-information-matrix.md). This convention differs from the negative-Hessian convention for [Observed Fisher information](observed-fisher-information.md).
