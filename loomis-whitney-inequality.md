<h1 id="loomis-whitney-inequality">Loomis--Whitney inequality</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Uniform covers theorem](uniform-covers-theorem.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loomis–Whitney_inequality)

The sets $[n]\setminus\{i\}$ form an $(n-1)$-uniform cover, so the [uniform covers theorem](uniform-covers-theorem.md) gives

$$
|S|^{n-1}\leq\prod_{i=1}^n|S_{[n]\setminus\{i\}}|.
$$
