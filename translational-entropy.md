# Translational entropy

↑ **Parent:** [Entropy](entropy-split.md)

[entropy](entropy-split.md) associated with positions of independently translating objects. Connecting segments into one [polymer](polymer-split.md) leaves fewer independently translating objects than treating the same segments as separate molecules; this reduces mixing [entropy](entropy-split.md) per segment.
