# HeLa

↑ **Parent:** [Cell line](cell-line.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HeLa)

[HeLa](hela.md) is a human [continuous cell line](immortalised-cell-line.md) originating in [cervical cancer](cervical-cancer.md) [biological cells](biological-cell-split.md) collected from [Henrietta Lacks](henrietta-lacks.md) in 1951. Its name combines the beginning of her first and last names.[The Legacy of Henrietta Lacks, opening history and name explanation](the-legacy-of-henrietta-lacks-johns-hopkins-medicine.md)

ATCC's CCL-2 [HeLa](hela.md) preparation grows attached to a surface. Its product sheet specifies a [cell culture medium](cell-culture-medium.md) supplemented with [fetal bovine serum](fetal-bovine-serum.md), at $37\,{}^\circ\mathrm{C}$ with $5\%$ [carbon dioxide](carbon-dioxide.md) in air.[Characteristics, Growth properties; Handling information, Complete medium, Temperature and Atmosphere](hela-ccl-2-by-atcc.md) These are specified conditions for that preparation, rather than defining conditions for every [HeLa](hela.md) derivative.

Continuing access to related [biological cells](biological-cell-split.md) makes [HeLa](hela.md) useful for comparisons of [cell state](cell-state.md) and responses to interventions. But a [continuous cell line](immortalised-cell-line.md) is a laboratory model: its history and environment matter, and observations in it do not alone establish responses in an intact [organism](organism-split.md). [Cell counting](cell-counting.md) measures abundance; a [trypan blue exclusion test](trypan-blue-exclusion-test.md) measures a particular membrane-associated readout; neither proves [cell line](cell-line.md) identity.
