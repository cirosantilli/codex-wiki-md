# Center of symmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Point reflection](point-reflection.md)

A point $\mathbf c$ is a center of symmetry of a [set](set-split.md) $S$ in [Euclidean space](euclidean-norm.md) if [point reflection](point-reflection.md) $\mathbf x\mapsto2\mathbf c-\mathbf x$ maps $S$ onto itself. Every [ellipse](ellipse.md) has its [center of an ellipse](center-of-an-ellipse.md) as a center of symmetry. More generally, a nonempty bounded [set](set-split.md) has at most one: two such reflections compose to translation by twice the difference of their centers, and iterating a nonzero translation contradicts boundedness.
