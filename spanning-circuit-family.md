# Spanning circuit family

↑ **Parent:** [Directed cycle](directed-cycle.md)

A spanning circuit family is a collection of [directed cycles](directed-cycle.md) whose union contains every vertex of the [directed graph](directed-graph.md). Different circuits may overlap. This is distinct from a [cycle cover of a directed graph](cycle-cover-of-a-directed-graph.md), which requires vertex-disjoint cycles.
