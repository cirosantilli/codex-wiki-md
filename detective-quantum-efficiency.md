# Detective quantum efficiency

↑ **Parent:** [Electron microscope](electron-microscope.md)

[detective quantum efficiency](detective-quantum-efficiency.md) is the ratio of output to input squared [signal-to-noise ratio](signal-to-noise-ratio.md) for a specified measurement convention, often evaluated by [spatial frequency](spatial-frequency.md). It describes information preservation by a detector rather than just its pixel size.
