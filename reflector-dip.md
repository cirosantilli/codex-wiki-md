# Reflector dip

↑ **Parent:** [Seismic reflection](seismic-reflection.md)

The inclination of a reflecting interface to horizontal. It influences [seismic travel times](seismic-travel-time.md), offset sampling and [seismic migration](seismic-migration.md); a reflector is not necessarily a [fault plane](fault-plane.md).
