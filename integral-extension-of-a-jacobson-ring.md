# Integral extension of a Jacobson ring

↑ **Parent:** [Jacobson ring](jacobson-ring.md)

Every ring integral over a [Jacobson ring](jacobson-ring.md) is Jacobson. After quotienting by a prime, an integral equation for a nonzero element has nonzero constant term; choose a maximal ideal of the base avoiding that term and apply the [Lying-over theorem](lying-over-theorem.md).
