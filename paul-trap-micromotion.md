# Paul trap micromotion

↑ **Parent:** [Paul trap](paul-trap.md)

In the small-parameter regime, the slow coordinate $X_i$ describing [Paul trap secular motion](paul-trap-secular-motion.md) approximately obeys a [harmonic oscillator equation](simple-harmonic-motion.md) at [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) $\omega_i$. Its superposed fast motion is Paul trap micromotion. Substituting the leading fast displacement from the [RF-averaged trapping potential](rf-averaged-trapping-potential.md) gives the displayed expression in our phase convention. A small positional ripple can have substantial [velocity](velocity.md), because differentiating it brings down the large [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) $\Omega$. Precision [spectroscopy](spectroscopy.md) must therefore address both [Paul trap secular motion](paul-trap-secular-motion.md) and [Paul trap micromotion](paul-trap-micromotion.md); merely obtaining a bounded trajectory does not eliminate [Doppler shifts](doppler-effect.md).
