# Zero generalized weight space of a Cartan subalgebra

↑ **Parent:** [Generalized-weight decomposition for a nilpotent Lie algebra](generalized-weight-decomposition-for-a-nilpotent-lie-algebra.md)

Let $H$ be a [Cartan subalgebra](cartan-subalgebra.md) of a finite-dimensional complex [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) $L$. In the adjoint [generalized-weight decomposition for a nilpotent Lie algebra](generalized-weight-decomposition-for-a-nilpotent-lie-algebra.md), its zero summand satisfies $L^0=H$. Nilpotence gives $H\subseteq L^0$. If $L^0/H$ were nonzero, all adjoint operators of $H$ on this quotient would be nilpotent, so the [Engel theorem](engel-s-theorem.md) would supply a nonzero coset $x+H$ with $[H,x]\subseteq H$. That contradicts self-normalization. The derivation rule ensures that $L^0$ is a subalgebra, so the quotient action is well-defined.
