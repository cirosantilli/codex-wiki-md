# Volterra convolution of kernels

↑ **Parent:** [Volterra integral equation](volterra-integral-equation.md)

For time-dependent integral kernels on a [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md), composition also integrates over the intermediate point: $(A*B)(t,x,y)=\int_0^t\int_M A(t-s,x,z)B(s,z,y)\,dV_z\,ds$. It is associative under the usual absolute-integrability hypotheses. The integration simplex gives factorial bounds for iterated convolutions of bounded kernels.
