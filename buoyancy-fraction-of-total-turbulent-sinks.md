# Buoyancy fraction of total turbulent sinks

↑ **Parent:** [Flux Richardson number](flux-richardson-number.md)

With positive stable buoyancy destruction $B$ and positive dissipation $\epsilon$, this normalized sink fraction need not equal the [Flux Richardson number](flux-richardson-number.md) $B/P$ away from equilibrium. If $R=(B+\epsilon)/P$, then $J=\mathrm{Ri}_f/R$. In the [fixed-correlation equilibrium model for stratified turbulence](fixed-correlation-equilibrium-model-for-stratified-turbulence.md), a minimum of $R=a/q+bq$ has $B=\epsilon$, hence $J=1/2$. The actual flux Richardson number there is $R_{\min}/2$ and equals $1/2$ only when that minimum also satisfies the equilibrium condition $R=1$.
