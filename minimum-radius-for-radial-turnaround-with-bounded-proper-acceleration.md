# Minimum radius for radial turnaround with bounded proper acceleration

↑ **Parent:** [Schwarzschild static observer](schwarzschild-static-observer.md)

For radial motion in [Schwarzschild spacetime](schwarzschild-spacetime-split.md), $a^r=du^r/d\tau+GM/r^2$. At a prospective radial turning point $u^r=0$, bounded outward [proper acceleration](proper-acceleration.md) $g$ permits $du^r/d\tau\le g\sqrt{1-2GM/(c^2r)}-GM/r^2$. The equality $g=g_{\rm hover}$ defines a unique critical radius outside the horizon. A ship already falling inward cannot turn at or below it. The Newtonian proposed radius $\sqrt{GM/g}$ is strictly smaller and is therefore too late for reversal. A radius beyond the critical one is necessary, but braking must also remove the pre-existing inward [velocity](velocity.md).
