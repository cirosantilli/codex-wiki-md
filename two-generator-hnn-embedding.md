# Two-generator HNN embedding

↑ **Parent:** [Group embedding](group-embedding.md)

For $G=\langle g_i\mid R\rangle$, put $B=G*F(a,b)$, $u_0=a$, $v_0=b$, $u_i=b^{-i}ab^i$ and $v_i=g_i a^{-i}ba^i$ for $i\ge1$. The $u_i$ freely generate by [free conjugate bases in a rank-two free group](free-conjugate-bases-in-a-rank-two-free-group.md). The $v_i$ do too: projection killing $G$ maps them to a free conjugate basis, so no reduced word in them is trivial. The [HNN extension](hnn-extension.md) adjoining $t^{-1}u_it=v_i$ embeds $G$ by [Britton's lemma](britton-s-lemma.md). Since $b=t^{-1}at$ and $g_i=t^{-1}u_it(a^{-i}ba^i)^{-1}$, its generators reduce to $a,t$. For finitely many $g_i$ and $R$ this is a [finite group presentation](finite-group-presentation.md); for effective countably many generators and enumerable $R$ it is a two-generator [recursive presentation of a group](recursive-presentation-of-a-group.md).
