# Phosphatidylcholine

↑ **Parent:** [Glycerophospholipid](glycerophospholipid.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phosphatidylcholine)

[Phosphatidylcholine](phosphatidylcholine.md), abbreviated [PC](phosphatidylcholine.md), is a [glycerophospholipid](glycerophospholipid.md) with a [phosphate](phosphate.md)-[choline](choline.md) head. Its [zwitterionic](zwitterion.md) head illustrates why a [hydrophilic](hydrophilicity.md) region need not have a nonzero net [electric charge](electric-charge.md). See [Lodish et al.: membrane lipids](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK21726/).
