# Angle multiplication at a holomorphic critical point

↑ **Parent:** [Conformal map](conformal-map.md)

If a nonconstant [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function-split.md) has its first nonzero local Taylor coefficient at order $m$, small rays from $z_0$ have image arguments $\arg a+m\arg(z-z_0)$ to leading order. Their oriented angles are therefore multiplied by $m$ modulo $2\pi$. The case $m=1$ is locally [conformal](conformal-map.md); $m>1$ gives a [critical point](critical-point.md), and the local inverse has $m$ branches. For the [Joukowski map](joukowski-map.md) at $z_0=1$, $f(1+\zeta)=2+\zeta^2+O(\zeta^3)$, so $m=2$.
