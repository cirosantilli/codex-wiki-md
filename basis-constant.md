# Basis constant

↑ **Parent:** [Basis projection](basis-projection.md)

The basis constant of a [Schauder basis](schauder-basis.md) is $\sup_n\lVert P_n\rVert$, where $P_n$ are its [basis projections](basis-projection.md). Uniform boundedness makes this supremum finite.
