# Cross-product matrix spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Cross-product matrix](cross-product-matrix.md)

The [cross-product matrix](cross-product-matrix.md) $A\mathbf x=\mathbf n\times\mathbf x$ obeys $A\mathbf n=0$ and $A^2=\mathbf n\mathbf n^T-|\mathbf n|^2I$, by the [vector triple product](vector-triple-product.md). On the [orthogonal complement](orthogonal-complement.md) of a nonzero $\mathbf n$, this is $-|\mathbf n|^2I$; hence the two remaining complex [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are $\pm i|\mathbf n|$. In an orthonormal basis adapted to $\mathbf n$, $A$ is a planar quarter-turn multiplied by $|\mathbf n|$, with zero action along its axis.
