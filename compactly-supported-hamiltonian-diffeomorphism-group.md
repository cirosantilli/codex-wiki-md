# Compactly supported Hamiltonian diffeomorphism group

↑ **Parent:** [Group of Hamiltonian diffeomorphisms](group-of-hamiltonian-diffeomorphisms.md)

This subgroup uses Hamiltonians whose support in $[0,1]\times M$ is compact. Their flows are the identity outside a fixed compact set. On closed manifolds this support condition imposes no extra restriction. On an open [symplectic manifold](symplectic-manifold.md), it excludes nontrivial whole-fiber [cotangent fiber translations](cotangent-fiber-translation.md), even when their generating one-form is exact.
