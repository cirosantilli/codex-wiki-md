# Crystal polymorph

↑ **Parent:** [Polymorphism (materials science)](polymorphism-materials-science.md)

A [solid](solid.md) [thermodynamic phase](thermodynamic-phase.md) with a particular [crystal structure](crystal-structure.md), among distinct crystalline forms of the same chemical substance. [Crystal polymorphism](polymorphism-materials-science.md) names the existence of these alternatives; a [crystal polymorph](crystal-polymorph.md) names one of the forms.
