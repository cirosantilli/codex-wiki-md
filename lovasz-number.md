<h1 id="lovasz-number">Lovász number</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Graph coloring](graph-coloring.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lovász_number)

The Lovász number of a finite simple [graph](graph-split.md) is the [semidefinite program](semidefinite-programming.md) value

$$
\vartheta(G)=\max\{\operatorname{tr}(JB):B\succeq0,\ \operatorname{tr}B=1,\ B_{ij}=0\text{ for }ij\in E(G)\},
$$

where $J$ is the [all-ones matrix](all-ones-matrix.md). Its value on the [complement graph](complement-graph.md) gives a lower bound for the [chromatic number](chromatic-number.md).
