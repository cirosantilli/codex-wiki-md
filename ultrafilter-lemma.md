# Ultrafilter lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Filter (set theory)](filter-set-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultrafilter_lemma)

Every proper [filter on a set](filter-set-theory.md) is contained in an [ultrafilter](ultrafilter.md). Order its proper filter extensions by inclusion, use [Zorn lemma](zorn-s-lemma.md) to obtain a maximal extension, and observe that maximality forces it to contain exactly one of every set and its complement.
