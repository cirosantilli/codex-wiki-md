# Energy transport

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar structure](stellar-structure-split.md)

Transport of stellar [energy](energy-split.md) from the interior to escaping radiation, principally by [radiative diffusion](radiative-diffusion.md) and [convection](convection-split.md) in ordinary stellar models. Carrying a nonzero [luminosity](luminosity.md) generally requires a nonzero [temperature gradient](temperature-gradient.md).
