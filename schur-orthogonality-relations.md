# Schur orthogonality relations

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix coefficient](matrix-coefficient.md)

For inequivalent chosen [unitary irreducible representations](unitary-irreducible-representation.md) of a [finite group](finite-group.md), uniform [expectation](expected-value.md) gives

$$
\mathbb E_x\rho(x)_{ij}\overline{\sigma(x)_{kl}}
=\begin{cases}d_\rho^{-1}\delta_{ik}\delta_{jl},&\rho=\sigma,\\0,&\rho\ne\sigma.\end{cases}
$$

The [Kronecker deltas](kronecker-delta.md) in the first case refer to entries in the same chosen [basis](basis.md). Averaging an [intertwiner](intertwiner.md) and applying the [Schur lemma](schur-s-lemma.md) proves the formula; summing diagonal entries gives [character orthogonality](character-orthogonality.md).
