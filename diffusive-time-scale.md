# Diffusive time scale

↑ **Parent:** [Diffusion equation](diffusion-equation-split.md)

A concentration varying over length $L$ has Laplacian of order its amplitude divided by $L^2$. Balancing the time derivative with $\kappa\Delta\chi$ in the [diffusion equation](diffusion-equation-split.md) gives the displayed relaxation time. A [multiple-scale expansion](method-of-multiple-scales.md) with spatial scale $\varepsilon x$ therefore uses slow time $\varepsilon^2t$ when the effective [diffusivity](diffusion-coefficient.md) is of order one.
