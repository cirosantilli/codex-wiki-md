# Electron beam damage

↑ **Parent:** [Electron microscopy](electron-microscopy.md)

A specimen change caused by [electron](electron.md) irradiation, including atomic displacement, ionization-driven chemical changes, heating or contamination. Compare low-dose repeated observations and unexposed regions to test whether measured nanostructure is being altered by its measurement.
