# Skorokhod embedding theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Stopping time](stopping-time.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skorokhod_embedding_theorem)

The Skorokhod embedding theorem represents a centered [probability distribution](probability-distribution.md) $\mu$ on the [real numbers](real-number.md) with finite [variance](variance-split.md) as $B_T\sim\mu$ for a [stopping time](stopping-time.md) $T$ of [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md). One may choose $T$ so that the stopped process $(B_{t\wedge T})$ is [uniformly integrable](uniform-integrability.md) and $\mathbb ET=\int x^2\mu(dx)$.
