# Perfect adaptation

↑ **Parent:** [Signaling adaptation](signaling-adaptation.md)

A signalling output returns to its original [steady state](steady-state.md) after a persistent change of input. This concerns the final output, not absence of an initial response or invariance of the internal state. [Robust perfect adaptation](robust-perfect-adaptation.md) additionally requires that the return property persist over a range of parameters.
