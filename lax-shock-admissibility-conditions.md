# Lax shock admissibility conditions

↑ **Parent:** [System of conservation laws](system-of-conservation-laws.md)

For a strictly hyperbolic [system of conservation laws](system-of-conservation-laws.md) $U_t+F(U)_x=0$ with ordered [characteristic speeds](characteristic-speed.md) $\lambda_1<\cdots<\lambda_n$, a Lax family-$j$ shock has $\lambda_j(U_R)<s<\lambda_j(U_L)$. The remaining families must stay on the same side of $s$: $\lambda_{j-1}(U_L)<s$ and $s<\lambda_{j+1}(U_R)$ where those neighbors exist. These inequalities imply that the lower families have speed below $s$ on both sides and the higher families speed above $s$ on both sides. There are $n+1$ incoming characteristic directions: two from the compressed family and one from each other family. The [Rankine-Hugoniot conditions](rankine-hugoniot-conditions.md) supply the jump balance, while these inequalities supply admissibility. For a convex scalar flux this reduces to the [entropy shock](entropy-shock.md) condition.
