# Endergonic reaction

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical reaction](chemical-reaction-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endergonic_reaction)

An [endergonic reaction](endergonic-reaction.md) has positive molar reaction change in [Gibbs free energy](gibbs-free-energy.md) under the specified conditions. Sustained net forward conversion needs a driving process, such as [energy coupling](energy-coupling.md). See [OpenStax Biology 2e, 6.2: endergonic reactions](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/6-2-potential-kinetic-free-and-activation-energy).
