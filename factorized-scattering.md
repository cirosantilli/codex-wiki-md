# Factorized scattering

↑ **Parent:** [Integrable quantum field theory](integrable-quantum-field-theory.md)

Multiparticle [quantum scattering](quantum-scattering.md) is factorized when it decomposes into two-body scatterings without particle production or a new irreducible many-body amplitude. The independence of the answer from the order of exchanges imposes the [Yang-Baxter equation](yang-baxter-equation.md). Classical [pairwise additivity of soliton shifts](pairwise-additivity-of-soliton-shifts.md) is a corresponding property of integrable [soliton](soliton.md) collisions.
