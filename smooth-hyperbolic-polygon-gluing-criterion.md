# Smooth hyperbolic polygon gluing criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic surface](hyperbolic-surface.md)

Suppose a finite simple [polygon](polygon.md) in the curvature-minus-one [hyperbolic plane](hyperbolic-plane.md) has [hyperbolic geodesic](geodesic-in-the-poincare-half-plane-model.md) sides paired to produce a closed [topological surface](topological-surface-split.md). Its metric descends to a smooth [hyperbolic metric](hyperbolic-metric.md) exactly when paired [polygon sides](side-of-a-polygon.md) have equal [lengths](length.md) and the sum of internal [angles](angle.md) in each vertex [equivalence class](equivalence-class.md) is $2\pi$. Equal [lengths](length.md) allow [isometric](isometry.md) gluing at open edges; at vertices the sectors assemble into a hyperbolic disc precisely when their total [angle](angle.md) is $2\pi$. Other positive total [angles](angle.md) produce a [cone singularity of a surface metric](cone-singularity-of-a-surface-metric.md). The [area of a hyperbolic geodesic polygon](area-of-a-hyperbolic-geodesic-polygon.md) and the [Gauss-Bonnet theorem](gauss-bonnet-theorem.md) then give area $-2\pi\chi$ for the closed [surface](topological-surface-split.md).
