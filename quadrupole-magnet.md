# Quadrupole magnet

↑ **Parent:** [Beam optics](beam-optics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quadrupole_magnet)

A magnet whose transverse field varies linearly with displacement. Its Lorentz force focuses a charged-particle beam in one transverse plane and defocuses it in the other. Oppositely oriented elements provide [alternating-gradient focusing](strong-focusing.md).
