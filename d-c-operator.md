# d c operator

↑ **Parent:** [Complex manifold](complex-manifold.md)

With the convention $d^c=i(\bar\partial-\partial)$ and with $J$ acting on forms by applying the [complex structure](complex-structure.md) to every argument,

$$
d^c=J^{-1}dJ.
$$

Indeed, $J$ acts on a $(p,q)$-form by $i^{p-q}$; conjugating $d=\partial+\bar\partial$ therefore multiplies its two type components by $-i$ and $i$, respectively.
