# Reorder buffer

↑ **Parent:** [Out-of-order execution](out-of-order-execution.md)

An age-ordered structure holding issued instructions' results and completion or exception status until [instruction retirement](instruction-retirement.md). It permits execution to complete out of order while commitment follows program order.
