# Clonal interference

↑ **Parent:** [Population genetics](population-genetics-split.md)

Competition among independently arising beneficial lineages in a population with little or no [genetic recombination](genetic-recombination.md). A favorable [mutation](mutation.md) can be lost because a competing lineage spreads more successfully.
