# Borel integral with a simple pole

↑ **Parent:** [Borel summation](borel-summation.md)

If a [Borel summation](borel-summation.md) contour meets a simple pole, an upper or lower indentation fixes the exponentially small contribution. For the transform $(1-t^2)^{-1}$ on the positive ray, the relevant pole is $t=1$. A lower indentation gives the [Cauchy principal value](cauchy-principal-value.md) minus $i\pi e^{-z}/2$. Finite geometric expansion gives an exact remainder integral, which enables [optimal truncation](optimal-truncation.md) and [error-function smoothing of a Stokes multiplier](error-function-smoothing-of-a-stokes-multiplier.md).
