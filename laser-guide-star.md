# Laser guide star

↑ **Parent:** [Guide star (adaptive optics)](guide-star-adaptive-optics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laser_guide_star)

A laser guide star is an artificial atmospheric beacon for [adaptive optics](adaptive-optics.md). It improves sky coverage but samples a finite-distance cone rather than the full incoming stellar beam. Conventional systems still require a natural reference for absolute image motion.
