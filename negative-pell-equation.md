# Negative Pell equation

↑ **Parent:** [Pell equation](pell-equation.md)

The [Diophantine equation](diophantine-equation.md) $x^2-Dy^2=-1$, usually with a nonsquare [positive integer](positive-integer.md) $D$ and [integer](integer-split.md) unknowns. It need not have a solution. For $D=2$, multiplying $u+v\sqrt2$ by $3+2\sqrt2$ preserves norm $-1$ and gives $(u,v)\mapsto(3u+4v,2u+3v)$.
