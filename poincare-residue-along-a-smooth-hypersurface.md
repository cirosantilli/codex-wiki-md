<h1 id="poincare-residue-along-a-smooth-hypersurface">Poincaré residue along a smooth hypersurface</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical bundle](canonical-bundle.md)

If a meromorphic top form has at most a simple pole along a smooth [complex analytic hypersurface](complex-analytic-hypersurface.md) $Y=\{z=0\}$, write $\alpha=(dz/z)\wedge\beta$ with $\beta$ holomorphic. Its residue is $\beta|_Y$, a holomorphic section of the [canonical bundle](canonical-bundle.md) $K_Y$. A change of defining coordinate $t=az$, with $a$ holomorphic and nonzero, changes $dt/t$ by the holomorphic form $da/a$ and leaves this restriction invariant. Placing the normal factor first fixes the sign convention.
