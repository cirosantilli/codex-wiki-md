# Tricritical sextic beta function

↑ **Parent:** [Tricritical point](tricritical-point.md)

In three dimensions, after tuning the quadratic and quartic [relevant operators](relevant-operator.md), the positive sextic coupling has a [renormalization-group beta function](beta-function-physics.md) $d\lambda/ds=-C\lambda^2+\cdots$ with $C>0$ for increasing length scale $s$. The [Wick contractions](wick-contraction.md) connecting two sextic vertices with three propagators give the primitive logarithmic correction. The interaction is [marginally irrelevant](marginally-irrelevant-operator.md).
