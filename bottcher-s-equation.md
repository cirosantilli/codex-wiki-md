<h1 id="bottcher-s-equation">Böttcher's equation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Complex dynamics](complex-dynamics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Böttcher's_equation)

[Böttcher's equation](bottcher-s-equation.md) asks for a holomorphic coordinate conjugating a map with a superattracting fixed point to a monomial. If $f(z)=az^m+O(z^{m+1})$ with $m\geq2$ and $a\ne0$, a normalized local solution has $\phi(z)=bz+O(z^2)$ with $b^{m-1}=a$. The [Böttcher theorem](bottcher-theorem.md) asserts the existence and uniqueness of this local coordinate after choosing $b$.
