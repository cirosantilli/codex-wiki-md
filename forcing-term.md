# Forcing term

↑ **Parent:** [Inhomogeneous linear differential equation](inhomogeneous-linear-differential-equation.md)

For a [linear differential equation](linear-differential-equation.md) written $Ly=f$, the prescribed right-hand side $f$ is the forcing term. It may represent an external input or source, and can be constant or time dependent. Setting $f=0$ gives the associated [homogeneous linear differential equation](homogeneous-linear-differential-equation.md).
