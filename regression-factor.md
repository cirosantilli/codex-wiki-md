# Regression factor

↑ **Parent:** [Categorical variable](categorical-variable.md)

A [regression factor](regression-factor.md) is a [categorical variable](categorical-variable.md) encoded by indicator columns or [treatment contrasts](treatment-contrast.md) in a [statistical model](statistical-model-split.md). A factor with $k$ observed levels normally contributes $k-1$ [statistical degrees of freedom](statistical-degrees-of-freedom.md) when an intercept is present. The [reference level in a regression factor](reference-level-in-a-regression-factor.md) has coefficient zero under [treatment contrasts](treatment-contrast.md). Coding a year as a factor permits arbitrary year effects rather than imposing a linear trend.
