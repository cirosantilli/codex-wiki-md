# Reduced locus of a ring

↑ **Parent:** [Reduced ring](reduced-ring.md)

The subset of the [spectrum of a ring](spectrum-of-a-commutative-ring.md) where the [localization at a prime ideal](localization-at-a-prime-ideal.md) is [reduced](reduced-ring.md). Since the [nilradical commutes with localization](nilradical-commutes-with-localization.md), its complement is the [support of a module](support-of-a-module.md) $N=\operatorname{Nil}(R)$. When $N$ is a [finitely generated module](finitely-generated-module.md), [vanishing of a finitely generated module near a prime](vanishing-of-a-finitely-generated-module-near-a-prime.md) gives

$$
\mathcal R=\operatorname{Spec}R\setminus V(\operatorname{Ann}_R(N)),
$$

so the locus is a [Zariski-open subset](zariski-open-set.md). In particular this holds for every [Noetherian ring](noetherian-ring.md).
