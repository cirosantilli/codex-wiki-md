# Gravitational acceleration

↑ **Parent:** [Newtonian gravitational field](newtonian-gravitational-field.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gravitational_acceleration)

Gravitational acceleration is the acceleration of a freely falling test body in a [gravitational field](newtonian-gravitational-field.md). Near Earth's surface its magnitude is approximately constant on shallow vertical scales, and is conventionally denoted by $g$.
