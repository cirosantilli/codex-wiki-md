# Rankine cycle

↑ **Parent:** [Heat engine](heat-engine.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rankine_cycle)

A vapour-power cycle using a liquid pump, high-pressure heat addition, turbine expansion and low-pressure condensation. Ideal components are reversible apart from heat transfer through finite temperature differences. With states numbered pump inlet, pump outlet, turbine inlet and turbine outlet, the net [thermal efficiency](thermal-efficiency.md) is $[(h_3-h_4)-(h_2-h_1)]/(h_3-h_2)$. Liquid pumping avoids the impractical compression of a wet two-phase mixture in a steam realization of the [Carnot cycle](carnot-cycle.md). Named after [William John Macquorn Rankine](w-j-m-rankine.md).
