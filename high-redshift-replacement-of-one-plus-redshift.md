# High-redshift replacement of one plus redshift

↑ **Parent:** [Baryon catch-up approximation after recombination](baryon-catch-up-approximation-after-recombination.md)

Scale-factor ratios involve $(1+z)/(1+z_r)$ exactly. Replacing this by $z/z_r$ is accurate only when both redshifts are large; it cannot justify an exact zero-redshift identity.
