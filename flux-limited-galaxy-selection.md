# Flux-limited galaxy selection

↑ **Parent:** [Observational astronomy](observational-astronomy.md)

For a nearby Euclidean survey with limiting [flux](flux.md) $f_{\min}$, a [galaxy](galaxy-split.md) of [luminosity](luminosity.md) $L$ is detectable out to $d_{\max}=\sqrt{L/(4\pi f_{\min})}$. Its accessible volume is therefore proportional to $L^{3/2}$. With a [Schechter function](schechter-function.md) $\phi(L)\propto L^\alpha e^{-L/L_*}$, counts per logarithmic interval are proportional to $L^{\alpha+5/2}e^{-L/L_*}$ and peak at $(\alpha+5/2)L_*$ when $\alpha>-5/2$. This selection suppresses faint objects relative to a volume-limited census; finite survey boundaries and cosmological evolution modify the simple law.
