# Force on a cylinder from an external dipole

↑ **Parent:** [Blasius force theorem](blasius-force-theorem.md)

The [Milne-Thomson circle theorem](milne-thomson-circle-theorem.md) places an image dipole inside a [cylinder](cylinder-geometry.md) at the inverse point of an external dipole. In the [residue](residue.md) of the squared [complex velocity](complex-velocity.md), only the cross term between the analytic external [velocity](velocity.md) and the image [double pole](double-pole.md) contributes. The product of their conjugate dipole strengths is independent of orientation, so the [cylinder](cylinder-geometry.md) force is radial toward the external dipole.
