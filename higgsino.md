# Higgsino

↑ **Parent:** [Sparticle](sparticle.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higgsino)

A higgsino is the [Weyl spinor](weyl-spinor.md) partner of a Higgs [scalar field](scalar-field.md) in a [chiral superfield](chiral-superfield.md). After a [supersymmetric Higgs mechanism](supersymmetric-higgs-mechanism.md), its combination along a broken gauge direction mixes with the [gaugino](gaugino.md) in the resulting [massive N=1 vector multiplet](massive-n-1-vector-multiplet.md).
