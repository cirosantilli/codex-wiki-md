# Nilpotence of commutation by a nilpotent endomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Nilpotent linear map](nilpotent-linear-map.md)

For a [nilpotent endomorphism](nilpotent-linear-map.md) $N$ of a [vector space](vector-space-split.md), the [commutator](commutator.md) action $\operatorname{ad}N:T\mapsto NT-TN$ on its [endomorphisms](endomorphism.md) is nilpotent. The commuting left and right multiplication maps give $(\operatorname{ad}N)^r(T)=\sum_{j=0}^r(-1)^j\binom rjN^{r-j}TN^j$. If $N^q=0$ and $r\geq2q-1$, every summand vanishes. This works in every [characteristic of a field](characteristic-of-a-field.md) and is useful when applying [Engel theorem](engel-s-theorem.md) to an [Adjoint representation](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md).
