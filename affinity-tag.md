# Affinity tag

↑ **Parent:** [Protein](protein-split.md)

An [affinity tag](affinity-tag.md) is an engineered sequence or chemical attachment used to capture a [protein](protein-split.md) selectively. Tag placement and abundance need validation because the tag can alter [protein folding](protein-folding.md), [subcellular localization](subcellular-localization.md) or partner binding.
