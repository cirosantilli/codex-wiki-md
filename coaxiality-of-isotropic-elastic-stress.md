# Coaxiality of isotropic elastic stress

↑ **Parent:** [Material isotropy](material-isotropy.md)

The [polar decomposition in continuum mechanics](polar-decomposition-in-continuum-mechanics.md) and [material isotropy](material-isotropy.md) remove the reference rotation from an elastic [Cauchy stress tensor](cauchy-stress-tensor.md). Half-turns around principal stretch axes leave the diagonal [left stretch tensor](left-stretch-tensor.md) fixed, so [material frame indifference](material-frame-indifference.md) forces the stress's off-diagonal entries to vanish in those axes. Rotations within repeated-stretch eigenspaces also force isotropic stress there. Consequently stress and the [left Cauchy-Green deformation tensor](left-cauchy-green-deformation-tensor.md) are [coaxial symmetric tensors](coaxial-symmetric-tensors.md).
