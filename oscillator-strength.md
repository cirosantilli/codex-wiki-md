# Oscillator strength

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum mechanics](quantum-mechanics-split.md)

A dimensionless measure of the intensity of a specified optical transition. It combines the transition matrix element and transition energy; [selection rules](selection-rule.md), [Polarization](polarization-waves.md) and the distribution of electronic states determine which transitions carry appreciable absorption. Peak position alone does not give the absorbed fraction.
