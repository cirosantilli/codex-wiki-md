# Maximum projectile range from an elevated launch

↑ **Parent:** [Projectile range](projectile-range.md)

Under uniform gravity without [drag](drag-physics.md), a projectile launched at [speed](speed.md) $u$ from height $h$ reaches a maximum horizontal [projectile range](projectile-range.md) $u\sqrt{u^2+2gh}/g$. For horizontal position $X$, maximize $y=h+Xz-gX^2(1+z^2)/(2u^2)$ over $z=\tan\alpha$. The maximizer is $z=u^2/(gX)$ and its envelope meets the ground at the displayed distance. The optimum angle obeys $\tan\alpha=u/\sqrt{u^2+2gh}$.
