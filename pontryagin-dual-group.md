# Pontryagin dual group

↑ **Parent:** [Pontryagin duality](pontryagin-duality.md)

For a locally compact Hausdorff Abelian group $G$, its Pontryagin dual is the group of [continuous unitary characters](continuous-unitary-character.md), with pointwise multiplication and the [compact-open topology](compact-open-topology.md). Its topology agrees with the [Gelfand topology](gelfand-topology.md) of the [L1 convolution algebra](l1-convolution-algebra.md). It is again a locally compact Hausdorff Abelian group.
