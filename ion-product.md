# Ion product

↑ **Parent:** [Solubility product](solubility-product.md)

The [ion product](ion-product.md) evaluates the same dissolved-ion [thermodynamic activity](thermodynamic-activity.md) product as the [solubility product](solubility-product.md), but at the current composition rather than necessarily at [chemical equilibrium](chemical-equilibrium.md). For AB, $Q_{\mathrm{sp}}=a_{\mathrm A}a_{\mathrm B}$; its concentration counterpart is $Q_c=c_{\mathrm A}c_{\mathrm B}$. Compare $Q_c$ with $K_{\mathrm{sp},c}$ only under compatible [activity coefficient](activity-coefficient.md) assumptions.[Chemistry 2e, §15.1, Predicting Precipitation: comparison of reaction quotient and solubility product](chemistry-2e-by-paul-flowers-and-others/15-1-precipitation-and-dissolution.md)

For other [stoichiometries](stoichiometry.md), use the dissolution coefficients as powers: $\mathrm{AB}_2(s)\rightleftharpoons\mathrm A^{2+}(aq)+2\mathrm B^-(aq)$ gives $Q_{\mathrm{sp}}=a_{\mathrm A}a_{\mathrm B}^2$. A [solubility product](solubility-product.md) is not itself the [molar concentration](molar-concentration.md) dissolved; even in the ideal AB model in pure [water](water.md), that [concentration](concentration.md) is $\sqrt{K_{\mathrm{sp},c}}$.
