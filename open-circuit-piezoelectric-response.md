# Open-circuit piezoelectric response

↑ **Parent:** [Direct piezoelectric effect](direct-piezoelectric-effect.md)

For electrically isolated electrodes with unchanged free charge, the increment of normal [electric displacement field](electric-displacement-field.md) is approximately zero. A constitutive relation such as $D_3=d_{31}(\sigma_1+\sigma_2)+\epsilon E_3$ then gives an induced field that balances stress-induced displacement. Finite leakage and input capacitance modify the [voltage](voltage.md) response.
