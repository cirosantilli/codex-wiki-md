# TRV130

↑ **Parent:** [Biased agonism](biased-agonism.md)

An opioid receptor [ligand](ligand-biochemistry.md), also called [oliceridine](trv130.md), studied for differences between [G protein](g-protein.md) activation and [arrestin](arrestin.md) recruitment. Apparent signalling preference must be distinguished from low [intrinsic efficacy](intrinsic-efficacy.md).
