# Splitting a one-triangle adjunction idempotent

↑ **Parent:** [One-triangle adjunction idempotent](one-triangle-adjunction-idempotent.md)

The [one-triangle adjunction idempotent](one-triangle-adjunction-idempotent.md) splits if and only if $G$ has a [left adjoint](adjoint-functors.md). From a splitting $F\xrightarrow rH\xrightarrow sF$, define the new unit $Gr\,\eta$ and counit $\varepsilon s_G$; the absorption identities imply both [triangle identities for an adjunction](triangle-identities-for-an-adjunction.md). Conversely, for $H\dashv G$ with unit $\psi:1\Rightarrow GH$ and counit $\varphi:HG\Rightarrow1$, the splitting maps are $r=\varepsilon_HF\psi$ and $s=\varphi_FH\eta$. Their composites are $rs=1_H$ and $sr=e$.
