# Inverse hyperbolic cosine

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic cosine](hyperbolic-cosine.md)

For $x\geq1$, the inverse [hyperbolic cosine](hyperbolic-cosine.md) on its nonnegative real branch is $\operatorname{arcosh}x=\log(x+\sqrt{x^2-1})$. It is the real cosine member of the [inverse hyperbolic functions](inverse-hyperbolic-functions.md).
