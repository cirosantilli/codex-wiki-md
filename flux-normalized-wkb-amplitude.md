# Flux-normalized WKB amplitude

↑ **Parent:** [WKB approximation](wkb-approximation.md)

For a real scalar [wave equation](wave-equation-split.md) $E''+k^2E=0$, the next order of the [WKB approximation](wkb-approximation.md) gives $2ka'+k'a=0$. Thus $a=A/\sqrt k$, and the leading [Wronskian flux](wronskian-flux.md) of a right-travelling component is $|A|^2$ independently of the local [wavenumber](wavenumber.md).
