# Scalar-field momentum flux

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical stress-energy tensor](canonical-stress-energy-tensor.md)

For a real [scalar field](scalar-field.md) in one spatial dimension with metric signature $(+,-)$ and potential $V$, the [stress-energy conservation](stress-energy-conservation.md) law is $\partial_tT^{01}+\partial_xT^{11}=0$. The force on the field to the left of a fixed cut is $-T^{11}$ at the cut when the far-left boundary flux vanishes. [Finite-energy field configurations](finite-energy-field-configuration.md) also permit this conclusion by spatial cutoffs, without assuming pointwise decay of all derivatives.
