# Entanglement monotone

↑ **Parent:** [Entangled state](entangled-state.md)

An entanglement monotone quantifies [entanglement](entangled-state.md) without increasing on average under [local operations and classical communication](local-operations-and-classical-communication.md). The average is over recorded [measurement in quantum mechanics](quantum-measurement-split.md) outcomes: individual successful branches can have more entanglement, provided their probabilities compensate for that increase. This distinction makes an entanglement monotone useful for bounding probabilistic state conversion. A pure-state entanglement monotone need only satisfy this inequality on pure input states and pure conditional branches, with local measurement outcomes refined to individual [Kraus operators](kraus-operator.md).
