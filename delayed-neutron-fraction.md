# Delayed neutron fraction

↑ **Parent:** [Delayed neutron](delayed-neutron.md)

The fraction of fission-related neutron production assigned to [delayed neutrons](delayed-neutron.md) under the chosen averaging convention. Effective kinetics parameters can include spatial and spectral weighting; a small fraction can still have a large influence on population timescales.
