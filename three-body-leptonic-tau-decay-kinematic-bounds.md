# Three-body leptonic tau-decay kinematic bounds

↑ **Parent:** [Tau-pair electron-muon missing-energy signature](tau-pair-electron-muon-missing-energy-signature.md)

Combine the two massless [neutrinos](neutrino.md) in $\tau^-\to\ell^-\bar\nu_\ell\nu_\tau$ into $q$. In the parent rest frame, [four-momentum conservation](four-momentum-conservation.md) gives

$$
q^2=m_\tau^2+m_\ell^2-2m_\tau E_\ell^*,\qquad
0\leq q^2\leq(m_\tau-m_\ell)^2.
$$

The lower bound follows from the nonnegative [Minkowski norm](minkowski-norm.md) of the sum of two future-directed null [four-vectors](four-vector.md); the upper bound follows from $E_\ell^*\geq m_\ell$. Substituting gives the displayed [energy](energy-split.md) bounds. They are kinematic: they do not specify the [weak interaction](weak-interaction.md) [decay amplitude](decay-amplitude.md) or the [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) inside the interval.

If the charged daughter is also massless, put $x=2E_\ell^*/m_\tau\in[0,1]$. A parent with [Lorentz factor](lorentz-factor.md) $\gamma$ and [speed](speed.md) $\beta$ in [natural units](natural-units.md) produces

$$
E_\ell=\gamma\frac{m_\tau x}{2}(1+\beta\cos\theta^*),\qquad
0\leq E_\ell\leq\frac{E_\tau}{2}(1+\beta).
$$

Here $\theta^*$ is the daughter angle relative to the boost direction in the parent rest frame. Two opposite parent [momenta](momentum-split.md) therefore need not produce two opposite visible daughter [momenta](momentum-split.md): the invisible system can recoil differently in each [particle decay](particle-decay.md).
