# Complex Wishart matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Random matrix](random-matrix.md)

A complex Wishart [matrix](matrix.md) is the Gram [matrix](matrix.md) of an $n$-by-$m$ [matrix](matrix.md) of independent centered complex [Gaussian random variables](gaussian-random-variable.md) with an indicated [variance](variance-split.md) convention. It is a [positive semidefinite matrix](positive-semidefinite-matrix.md). In the standard convention with joint entry density proportional to $e^{-\operatorname{Tr}X^*X}$ and $n\geq m$, its [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) density contains the weight $x^{n-m}e^{-x}$ and a squared [Vandermonde determinant](vandermonde-determinant.md).
