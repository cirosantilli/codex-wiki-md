# Weak-pulse Ramsey spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Ramsey spectroscopy](ramsey-spectroscopy.md)

For two coherent square pulses of equal duration $\tau$, pulse-start separation $T\geq\tau$, resonant [Rabi angular frequency](rabi-angular-frequency.md) $\Omega$, and [Rabi detuning](rabi-detuning.md) $\delta$, the initially lower-state [transition probability](transition-probability.md) has the displayed leading weak-excitation form when $|\Omega|\tau\ll1$. Integrating the first-order [probability amplitude](probability-amplitude.md) over both windows gives a sum of two equal [Fourier transforms](fourier-transform-split.md), with relative [quantum phase](quantum-phase.md) $\delta T$. The factor four from coherent addition makes the central value $(\Omega\tau)^2$. The [rotating-wave approximation](rotating-wave-approximation.md) additionally requires many carrier cycles per pulse and near-resonant weak driving.
