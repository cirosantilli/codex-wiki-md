# Matrix power

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix](matrix.md)

For a square [matrix](matrix.md) $A$ and a [nonnegative integer](integer-split.md) $k$, the matrix power is the repeated product

$$
A^k=\underbrace{AA\cdots A}_{k\text{ factors}},
$$

with $A^0=I$.
