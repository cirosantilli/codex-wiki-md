# First-order theory

↑ **Parent:** [First-order logic](first-order-logic.md)

A first-order theory is a set of sentences in one first-order language. Depending on convention, the term may instead mean its deductive closure.

This is a [theory](theory-mathematical-logic.md) formulated in [first-order logic](first-order-logic.md).
