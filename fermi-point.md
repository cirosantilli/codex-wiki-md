# Fermi point

↑ **Parent:** [Fermi surface](fermi-surface.md)

A [Fermi point](fermi-point.md) is a point of the zero-temperature [Fermi surface](fermi-surface.md) in one-dimensional momentum space. For a spin-degenerate band with one occupied interval, its two boundaries are usually $-k_F$ and $+k_F$. Low-energy excitations near them form left- and right-moving branches. A perturbation at wavevector $2k_F$ can mix those branches, as in a [Peierls distortion](peierls-distortion.md).
