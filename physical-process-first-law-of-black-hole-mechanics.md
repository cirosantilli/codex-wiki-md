# Physical-process first law of black-hole mechanics

↑ **Parent:** [First law of black-hole mechanics](first-law-of-black-hole-mechanics.md)

The physical-process first law derives the area change caused by a small flux of matter through an initially and finally stationary horizon. Linearized [Null Raychaudhuri equation](null-raychaudhuri-equation.md) evolution converts the Killing-energy flux into $\Delta M=\kappa\Delta A/(8\pi)$.
