# Dinoflagellate viral nucleoprotein

↑ **Parent:** [Chromatin](chromatin.md)

A [dinoflagellate viral nucleoprotein](dinoflagellate-viral-nucleoprotein.md) is a member of the DVNP family of basic nuclear proteins found in dinoflagellates and related large DNA viruses. Comparative abundance and yeast perturbation experiments support interactions with DNA packaging, without fully reconstructing the evolutionary transition.
