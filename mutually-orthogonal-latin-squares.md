# Mutually orthogonal Latin squares

↑ **Parent:** [Latin square](latin-square.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mutually_orthogonal_Latin_squares)

[Latin squares](latin-square.md) on the same array are mutually [orthogonal](orthogonal-vectors.md) when every ordered pair of symbols from any two squares occurs exactly once on superposition. Pairwise balance makes the centered symbol-indicator spaces [orthogonal](orthogonal-vectors.md) in an additive [linear regression](linear-regression-split.md) model.
