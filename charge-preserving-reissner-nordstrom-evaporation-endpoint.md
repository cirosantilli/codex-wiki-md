# Charge-preserving Reissner-Nordstrom evaporation endpoint

↑ **Parent:** [Reissner-Nordstrom Hawking temperature](reissner-nordstrom-hawking-temperature.md)

In the ideal thermal-cutoff model where charged particles of [mass](mass.md) $m$ are emitted only for $T>m$, a sufficiently large initial [mass](mass.md) keeps charge fixed throughout evaporation exactly when $m$ is at least the [fixed-charge Reissner-Nordstrom temperature maximum](fixed-charge-reissner-nordstrom-temperature-maximum.md). Neutral radiation then approaches an extremal charged remnant of area $4\pi Q^2$. This conclusion does not include nonthermal electric-field discharge, nor establish finite-time attainment.
