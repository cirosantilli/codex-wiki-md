# Thorne spin limit

↑ **Parent:** [Black-hole spin-up by coherent accretion](black-hole-spin-up-by-coherent-accretion.md)

The Thorne spin limit is the equilibrium spin produced by competition between coherent thin-disc matter accretion and capture of disc photons by a [Kerr black hole](kerr-black-hole.md). Preferential capture of counterrotating photon angular momentum prevents the standard radiatively efficient model from reaching $a_*=1$. The commonly quoted limit is approximately $0.998$, with some dependence on emission and accretion assumptions. [Thorne's original calculation](https://articles.adsabs.harvard.edu/pdf/1974ApJ...191..507T) derives this result.
