# Weak measurement

↑ **Parent:** [Measurement in quantum mechanics](quantum-measurement-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weak_measurement)

A measurement with small information and disturbance per trial, often implemented by coupling an [observable](observable.md) weakly to a meter so that its eigenvalue-dependent pointer distributions overlap. Repeated observations can still accumulate substantial [measurement backaction](measurement-backaction.md). A [weak value](weak-value.md) additionally requires specified preparation and [postselection](postselection.md); a weak measurement without postselection need not involve one.
