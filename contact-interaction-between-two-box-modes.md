# Contact interaction between two box modes

↑ **Parent:** [Contact interaction (quantum mechanics)](contact-interaction-quantum-mechanics.md)

For distinct box modes $\varphi_1=\sqrt{2/L}\sin(p\pi x/L)$ and $\varphi_2=\sqrt{2/L}\sin(q\pi x/L)$, the displayed [contact interaction](contact-interaction-quantum-mechanics.md) overlaps produce self terms $3g\sum_jn_j(n_j-1)/(4L)$, a density term $2gn_1n_2/L$, and pair conversion $g(a_1^{\dagger2}a_2^2+a_2^{\dagger2}a_1^2)/(2L)$. Pair conversion preserves total [particle number](particle-number.md) but prevents diagonalization by individual [occupation numbers](occupation-number.md). The additional overlaps are $I_{1112}=-\delta_{q,3p}/(2L)$ and $I_{1222}=-\delta_{p,3q}/(2L)$, following from $\sin^3u=(3\sin u-\sin3u)/4$. Third-harmonic mode pairs therefore also permit single-particle transfer assisted by the other particles. For the first two box modes these additional terms vanish.
