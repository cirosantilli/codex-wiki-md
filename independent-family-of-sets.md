# Independent family of sets

↑ **Parent:** [Set theory](set-theory-split.md)

A family of [subsets](subset.md) of $X$ is independent when every finite choice of distinct members and their complements has nonempty [intersection](set-intersection.md). Write $A^1=A$ and $A^0=X\setminus A$ in the displayed condition. Every assignment of zero or one to the family then gives a collection with the [finite intersection property](finite-intersection-property.md), which can be extended to an [ultrafilter](ultrafilter.md).
