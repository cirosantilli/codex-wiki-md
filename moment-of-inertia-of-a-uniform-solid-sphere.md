# Moment of inertia of a uniform solid sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md)

A uniform solid sphere of mass $M$ and radius $a$ has the indicated [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) about any diameter. Integrating the squared perpendicular distance against uniform [mass density](density.md) gives $I=8\pi\rho a^5/15=2Ma^2/5$. It is different from the value for a thin spherical shell and determines the rotational contribution to [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) during [rolling without slipping](rolling-without-slipping.md).
