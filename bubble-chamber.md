# Bubble chamber

↑ **Parent:** [Particle detector](particle-detector.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bubble_chamber)

A [bubble chamber](bubble-chamber.md) photographs [vapor](vapor.md) bubbles along [charged particle](charged-particle.md) paths in a transparent [superheated liquid](superheated-liquid.md). The [liquid](liquid.md) serves as target and recording medium.[How it works, opening](how-it-works-bubble-chamber-cern-teaching-archive.md)

Compare the two phase changes: a [cloud chamber](cloud-chamber.md) forms [liquid](liquid.md) drops inside [supersaturated vapor](supersaturated-vapor.md), whereas a [bubble chamber](bubble-chamber.md) forms [vapor](vapor.md) cavities inside a [superheated liquid](superheated-liquid.md). Both use [metastability](metastability.md) to amplify a microscopic disturbance. The [photons](photon.md) reaching the [camera](camera.md) reveal the drops or bubbles. **The photographed trail's width is not the [particle](particle-physics.md)'s width**: it includes bubble growth, illumination and [spatial resolution](spatial-resolution.md).
