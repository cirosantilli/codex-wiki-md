# Standard Borel space

↑ **Parent:** [Measurable space](measurable-space.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standard_Borel_space)

A [standard Borel space](standard-borel-space.md) is a [measurable space](measurable-space.md) isomorphic to a [Borel set](borel-set.md) of a [Polish space](polish-space.md), with its Borel sigma-algebra. This measurable regularity permits the usual constructions of random samples and random counting measures, without requiring a particular compatible topology.
