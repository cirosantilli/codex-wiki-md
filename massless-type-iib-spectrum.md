# Massless type IIB spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Type IIB superstring theory](type-iib-superstring-theory.md)

The NS-NS fields are a [graviton](graviton.md), [Kalb–Ramond field](kalb-ramond-field.md) and [dilaton](dilaton.md), with $35+28+1$ physical polarizations. The RR fields are $C_0,C_2,C_4$, with $1+28+35$ polarizations; the five-form field strength of $C_4$ is self-dual. Two same-chirality Majorana-Weyl [gravitini](gravitino.md) contribute $112$ polarizations, and two Majorana-Weyl [dilatini](dilatino.md) of the opposite chirality contribute $16$. Potential components, dual potentials and physical polarizations are different counts.
