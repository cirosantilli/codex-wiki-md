# Covariance identity for continuous square-integrable martingales

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic covariation](quadratic-covariation.md)

For two zero-starting continuous martingales square-integrable at time $t$, the [Itô isometry](ito-isometry.md) gives $\mathbb E(M_t\pm N_t)^2=\mathbb E[M\pm N]_t$. The [polarization identity](polarization-identity.md) yields the displayed identity. Integrability of the cross-bracket follows from the [Kunita-Watanabe inequality](kunita-watanabe-inequality.md) and the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md).
