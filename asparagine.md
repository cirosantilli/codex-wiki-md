# Asparagine

↑ **Parent:** [Amino acid](amino-acid.md)

[asparagine](asparagine.md) is an amino acid with a side-chain amide. Its side-chain nitrogen is the usual attachment site in canonical N-linked [protein glycosylation](protein-glycosylation.md).
