# Geometric quantization

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic geometry](symplectic-geometry-split.md)

Geometric quantization associates quantum states and observables with a [symplectic manifold](symplectic-manifold.md). A first step uses a complex [line bundle](line-bundle.md) with a [connection on a vector bundle](connection-vector-bundle.md) whose curvature is proportional to the [symplectic form](symplectic-form.md) divided by $\hbar$; its existence imposes an integrality condition. A real polarization is an integrable half-dimensional distribution with [Lagrangian submanifolds](lagrangian-submanifold.md) as leaves. Requiring sections to be covariantly constant along the leaves reduces the variables to a configuration-space description. Restricting the curvature to a Lagrangian leaf gives zero, permitting local flat sections; global sections can require trivial holonomy. Exact Lagrangian graphs also describe local semiclassical phases through their generating functions.
