# Entanglement-assisted discrimination of a Pauli basis

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum channel discrimination](quantum-channel-discrimination.md)

The four [unitary operators](unitary-operator.md) $I,Z,X,iY$ can be identified with one use when an input [qubit](qubit.md) is entangled with a reference [ancilla qubit](ancilla-qubit.md). Applying the unknown operation to one half of $|\phi_+\rangle$ produces the four [orthogonal](orthogonal-vectors.md) [Bell states](bell-state-split.md), which a [Bell-basis measurement](bell-basis-measurement.md) distinguishes exactly. For operators $VU_jV^\dagger$ in any known [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md), prepare $(V\otimes I)|\phi_+\rangle$ and apply $V^\dagger$ after the unknown call. This reduces to the same [quantum channel discrimination](quantum-channel-discrimination.md) protocol. Direct expansion proves the four output identities; equivalently, their overlaps are $\operatorname{Tr}(U_j^\dagger U_k)/2=\delta_{jk}$.
