# Laws of black-hole mechanics

↑ **Parent:** [Black-hole thermodynamics](black-hole-thermodynamics.md)

The laws of black-hole mechanics relate [surface gravity](surface-gravity.md), horizon area, mass, angular momentum, and charge in close analogy with the laws of thermodynamics. Quantum theory identifies the analogy physically through [Hawking temperature](hawking-temperature.md) and [Bekenstein-Hawking entropy](bekenstein-hawking-entropy.md).
