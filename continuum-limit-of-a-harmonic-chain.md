# Continuum limit of a harmonic chain

↑ **Parent:** [Harmonic chain](harmonic-chain.md)

At wavelengths much longer than the lattice spacing, $u_{j+1}-u_j\simeq a\partial_xu$ gives $L=\int dx[\rho(\partial_tu)^2-K(\partial_xu)^2]/2$. The field obeys a [wave equation](wave-equation-split.md) with [sound speed](speed-of-sound.md) $v=\sqrt{K/\rho}$. Its conjugate [momentum](momentum-split.md) density is $\Pi=\rho\partial_tu$ and [canonical quantization](canonical-quantization.md) gives $[u(x),\Pi(x')]=i\hbar\delta(x-x')$. The lattice remains the [ultraviolet cutoff](ultraviolet-cutoff.md) of this long-wave theory.
