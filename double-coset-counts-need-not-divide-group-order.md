# Double-coset counts need not divide group order

↑ **Parent:** [Double coset](double-coset.md)

For the order-eight [dihedral group](dihedral-group.md) generated by $r$ of order four and $s$ of order two with $srs=r^{-1}$, let $H=K=\langle s\rangle$. There are exactly three [double cosets](double-coset.md): $H$, $Hr^2H$, and $HrH$, of sizes two, two, and four. Their number does not divide eight. This differs from the number of ordinary [cosets](coset.md), which divides group order by [Lagrange's theorem](lagrange-s-theorem.md).
