# Complementarity (physics)

↑ **Parent:** [Foundations of quantum mechanics](foundations-of-quantum-mechanics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complementarity_(physics))

The need for mutually exclusive experimental arrangements or incompatible observables to exhibit different aspects of a quantum system. For example, path discrimination and interference contrast obey a tradeoff, while sharp [Pauli operator](pauli-operator.md) $X$ and $Z$ measurements have incompatible eigenbases. [Which-path information](which-path-information.md) is an operational record, rather than a requirement that a conscious observer reads it.
