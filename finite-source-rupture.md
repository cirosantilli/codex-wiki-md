# Finite-source rupture

↑ **Parent:** [Earthquake](earthquake.md)

An [earthquake](earthquake.md) source with spatial extent and a history of [fault slip](fault-slip.md), rather than a single instantaneous point. Local slip rise time, advance of the rupture front and radiation travel times jointly control observed pulse shapes.
