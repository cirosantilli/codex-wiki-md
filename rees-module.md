# Rees module

↑ **Parent:** [Rees algebra](rees-algebra.md)

The Rees module of an $R$-[module](module-mathematics.md) $M$ with respect to an [ideal](ideal.md) $I$ is $\mathcal R_I(M)=\bigoplus_{j\ge0}I^jMt^j$, a [graded module](graded-module.md) over the [Rees ring](rees-algebra.md) $\mathcal R(I)$. Generators of $M$ in degree zero generate this module. For $N\subseteq M$, the graded [submodule](submodule.md) $\bigoplus_j(I^jM\cap N)t^j$ is finitely generated when $R$ is a [Noetherian ring](noetherian-ring.md); a bound on its generators' degrees yields the [Artin-Rees lemma](artin-rees-lemma.md).
