# Electromagnetic wave equation

↑ **Parent:** [Electromagnetic wave](electromagnetic-wave.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electromagnetic_wave_equation)

In source-free vacuum the [electric field](electric-field.md) and [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) obey

$$
\nabla^2\mathbf E-c^{-2}\partial_t^2\mathbf E=0,\qquad
\nabla^2\mathbf B-c^{-2}\partial_t^2\mathbf B=0.
$$

A brief derivation takes the [curl](curl.md) of [Faraday's law](faraday-s-law-of-induction.md) and substitutes the [Ampère-Maxwell equation](ampere-s-circuital-law.md). The [vector calculus](vector-calculus.md) identity $\nabla\times(\nabla\times\mathbf E)=\nabla(\nabla\cdot\mathbf E)-\nabla^2\mathbf E$, together with source-free [Gauss's law](gauss-s-law.md), leaves $\nabla^2\mathbf E=\mu_0\epsilon_0\partial_t^2\mathbf E$. The same argument gives the [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) equation. The operator $\nabla^2$ is the [Laplacian](laplacian.md), applied componentwise.
