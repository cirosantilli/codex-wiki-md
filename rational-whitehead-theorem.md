# Rational Whitehead theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Whitehead theorem](whitehead-theorem.md)

For maps between [simply connected](simply-connected-space.md) spaces, a rational [homology](homology-split.md) equivalence is a rational [homotopy equivalence](homotopy-equivalence.md). It follows from the relative Hurewicz theorem modulo torsion, or from rationalization and the [homological Whitehead theorem](homological-whitehead-theorem.md).
