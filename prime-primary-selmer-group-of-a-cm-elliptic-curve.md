# Prime-primary Selmer group of a CM elliptic curve

↑ **Parent:** [n-Selmer group](n-selmer-group.md)

For a split CM prime $\mathfrak p$, use $E[\mathfrak p^\infty]$ in the [Galois cohomology](galois-cohomology.md) definition of the [Selmer group of an elliptic curve](n-selmer-group.md), with local conditions given by its Kummer maps. The decomposition of the CM $p$-power torsion splits the full $p$-primary Selmer group into the two prime-primary pieces. Class-field descriptions must retain the CM-character twist and distinguish relaxed local conditions from the actual Selmer conditions.
