# Rectangle

↑ **Parent:** [Quadrilateral](quadrilateral.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rectangle)

A Euclidean [polygon](polygon.md) with four sides and four right angles. Opposite sides are parallel and have equal lengths. A rectangle is a [square](square.md) exactly when its adjacent side lengths agree.
