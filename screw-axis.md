# Screw axis

↑ **Parent:** [Crystal symmetry](crystal-symmetry.md)

A symmetry consisting of rotation about an axis and translation parallel to that axis. A $2_1$ operation combines a half-turn with half a lattice period; applying it twice gives a complete lattice translation. It is a spatial symmetry, distinct from a [screw dislocation](screw-dislocation.md).
