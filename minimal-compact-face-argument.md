# Minimal compact face argument

↑ **Parent:** [Krein-Milman theorem](krein-milman-theorem.md)

A nonempty compact [face of a convex set](face-of-a-convex-set.md) in a Hausdorff [locally convex space](locally-convex-space.md) contains an [extreme point](extreme-point.md). Chains of compact faces have nonempty compact intersection. A minimal face supplied by the [Zorn lemma](zorn-s-lemma.md) must be a singleton, since a separating [linear functional](linear-functional.md) would otherwise expose a proper maximizer face. This is the existence step in the [Krein-Milman theorem](krein-milman-theorem.md).
