# Abel-Jacobi map of a compact Riemann surface

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann surfaces](riemann-surfaces.md)

Integration of [holomorphic differential forms](holomorphic-differential-form.md) along cycles embeds the first [homology group](homology-group.md) as a full [Euclidean lattice](euclidean-lattice.md) in $H^0(C,K_C)^\vee$. Fixing a base point, sum the path integrals to the points of an effective [complex analytic divisor](divisor-on-a-complex-manifold.md). Different path choices differ by that lattice. The [Abel theorem for divisors](abel-theorem-for-divisors.md) identifies the fibres with [linear equivalence of divisors](linear-equivalence-of-divisors.md).
