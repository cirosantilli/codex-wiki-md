# On-axis entry into a solenoidal tube

↑ **Parent:** [Guiding center](guiding-center.md)

A particle entering a uniform axial [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) on the tube axis follows a transverse circle through that entry point. Its [guiding centre](guiding-center.md) is one [Larmor radius](gyroradius.md) from the axis, so the maximum radial displacement from the axis is twice the [Larmor radius](gyroradius.md). For tube [radius](radius.md) $a$, clearance requires $v_\perp\le |q|Ba/(2m)$ when the transit samples a complete gyration. If the transit is shorter, the actual sampled arc determines clearance instead. The axis is revisited after each full cyclotron [period](period-of-a-function.md), not after each half-period.
