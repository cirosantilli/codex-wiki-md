# Stigma (botany)

↑ **Parent:** [Flower](flower.md)

The [stigma](stigma-botany.md) is the [carpel](carpel.md)'s [pollen](pollen.md)-receiving region. Compatible [pollen](pollen.md) may form a [pollen tube](pollen-tube.md) that grows toward an [ovule](ovule.md); arrival alone does not establish [fertilization](fertilization.md).[Biology 2e, §32.2 Incompatibility Genes in Flowers and Double Fertilization](biology-2e-by-mary-ann-clark-and-others/32-2-pollination-and-fertilization.md)

A useful diagnostic question is what was actually observed: [pollen](pollen.md) on a [stigma](stigma-botany.md), a growing [pollen tube](pollen-tube.md), or a new [plant embryo](plant-embryo.md)? Each observation tests a different stage. A [pollen tube germination assay](pollen-tube-germination-assay.md) isolates the middle process for direct viewing.
