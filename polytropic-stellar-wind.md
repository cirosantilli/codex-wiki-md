# Polytropic stellar wind

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar wind](stellar-wind.md)

A steady spherical [polytropic flow](polytropic-flow.md) escaping a point-mass potential conserves the outward [mass loss rate](mass-loss-rate.md) and the [Bernoulli function](bernoulli-function.md) $C=u^2/2+c_s^2/(\gamma-1)-GM/r$. For positive terminal speed, a finite-radius smooth [sonic point](sonic-point.md) requires $C>0$ and $1<\gamma<5/3$. Its sound speed obeys $c_{s,c}^2=2C(\gamma-1)/(5-3\gamma)$, and evaluating the conserved flux there selects the transonic [mass loss rate](mass-loss-rate.md). Other parameter limits need separate treatment.
