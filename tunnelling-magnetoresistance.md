# Tunnelling magnetoresistance

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic tunnel junction](magnetic-tunnel-junction.md)

[tunnelling magnetoresistance](tunnelling-magnetoresistance.md) is the dependence of a [magnetic tunnel junction](magnetic-tunnel-junction.md)'s [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) on the relative [magnetizations](magnetization.md) of its electrodes. A common convention is $(R_{AP}-R_P)/R_P$, where the subscripts denote antiparallel and parallel alignment. Its magnitude and sign depend on effective spin-polarized transmission through the particular interfaces and barrier.
