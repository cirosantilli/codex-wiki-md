# Saddle point

↑ **Parent:** [Analysis](analysis-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saddle_point)

A saddle point of a differentiable real-valued function is a [critical point](critical-point.md) which is neither a [local maximum](local-maximum.md) nor a [local minimum](local-minimum.md). For example $f(x,y)=x^2-y^2$ has a saddle at the origin. For a nonconstant [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function-split.md), critical points of its real part supply the complex-phase application used by the [method of steepest descent](method-of-steepest-descent.md).
