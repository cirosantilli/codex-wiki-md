# Cosmic-ray muon time-dilation experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Time dilation](time-dilation.md)

Atmospheric [cosmic rays](cosmic-ray.md) generate [muons](muon.md) whose [mean lifetime](mean-lifetime-physics.md) at rest is about $2.2\,\mathrm{\mu s}$. For a selected speed $v$ and production-to-detection distance $h$ in the terrestrial frame, [special relativity](special-relativity-split.md) predicts survival fraction $e^{-h/(\gamma v\tau_0)}$, where $\gamma$ is the [Lorentz factor](lorentz-factor.md). Without [time dilation](time-dilation.md), the exponent would instead be $-h/(v\tau_0)$. Comparing altitude-dependent counts at controlled energies, allowing for acceptance and energy loss, tests these alternatives. In the [muon](muon.md) [rest frame](rest-frame.md) the terrestrial distance is shortened to $h/\gamma$ by [length contraction](length-contraction.md), so the proper flight time and survival fraction agree with the terrestrial calculation. This frame comparison does not turn the same decay measurement into two independent tests.
