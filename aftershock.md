# Aftershock

↑ **Parent:** [Earthquake](earthquake.md)

An [earthquake](earthquake.md) occurring in the region affected by an earlier event during its subsequent relaxation and [stress](stress.md) redistribution. A spatial [aftershock](aftershock.md) pattern can help identify a fault, but not every [aftershock](aftershock.md) must lie on the main rupture plane.
