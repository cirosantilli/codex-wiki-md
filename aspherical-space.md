# Aspherical space

↑ **Parent:** [Eilenberg–MacLane space](eilenberg-maclane-space.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aspherical_space)

A path-connected space is aspherical when its higher [homotopy groups](homotopy-group.md) vanish, equivalently when its universal cover is contractible. An aspherical space with fundamental group $G$ is a $K(G,1)$, so based homotopy classes of maps into it are controlled by homomorphisms of fundamental groups.
