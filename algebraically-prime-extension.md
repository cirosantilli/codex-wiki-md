# Algebraically prime extension

↑ **Parent:** [Model theory](model-theory.md)

For a [first-order theory](first-order-theory.md) $T$ and $A\models T_\forall$, an algebraically prime extension is a [structure embedding](structure-embedding.md) $i:A\to P\models T$ such that every embedding $j:A\to N\models T$ factors as $j=h\circ i$ for some [structure embedding](structure-embedding.md) $h:P\to N$. The embeddings need not be elementary or unique. A theory has algebraically prime models when every such $A$ has an extension of this kind.
