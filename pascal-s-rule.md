<h1 id="pascal-s-rule">Pascal's rule</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Binomial coefficient](binomial-coefficient.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pascal's_rule)

Pascal's identity is

$$
\binom nk=\binom{n-1}k+\binom{n-1}{k-1}.
$$
