# Mediodorsal thalamus

↑ **Parent:** [Thalamus](thalamus.md)

Thalamic region with prominent prefrontal connections, implicated in aspects of cognition and memory. It is anatomically distinct from the [anterior thalamic nuclei](anterior-thalamic-nuclei.md).
