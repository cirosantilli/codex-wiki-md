# Adapted process

↑ **Parent:** [Stochastic process](stochastic-process-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adapted_process)

A stochastic process $X$ is adapted to a [filtration](filtration-probability-theory.md) $(\mathcal F_t)$ when $X_t$ is $\mathcal F_t$-measurable at every time $t$. Its present value therefore depends only on information already available.
