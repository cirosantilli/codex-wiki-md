# Materials science

↑ **Parent:** [Engineering](engineering-split.md)

[Materials science](materials-science.md) relates the preparation and internal structure of materials to their properties and uses. [Crystal structure](crystal-structure.md), [elasticity](elasticity-physics-split.md) and [electrical conductivity](electrical-conductivity.md) describe different aspects of that connection.
