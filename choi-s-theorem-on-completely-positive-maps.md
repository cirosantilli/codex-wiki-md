<h1 id="choi-s-theorem-on-completely-positive-maps">Choi's theorem on completely positive maps</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Completely positive map](completely-positive-map.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Choi's_theorem_on_completely_positive_maps)

A finite-dimensional [linear map](linear-map.md) is [completely positive](completely-positive-map.md) if and only if its [Choi matrix](choi-matrix.md) is [positive semidefinite](positive-semidefinite-matrix.md). One direction applies the map tensored with the identity to a maximally entangled positive operator. For the converse, factor the positive Choi matrix into rank-one terms and reshape their vectors into matrices; the [spectral Kraus decomposition](spectral-kraus-decomposition.md) reconstructs the map as a [Kraus representation](kraus-representation.md), which is completely positive.
