# Projective scheme

↑ **Parent:** [Proper morphism](proper-morphism.md)

A projective scheme over a base $S$ is an $S$-scheme admitting a closed immersion into some projective space $\mathbb P_S^n$. Every projective morphism is [proper](proper-morphism.md).

Projectivity is a property of the scheme over its specified base, expressed by its structural [projective morphism](projective-morphism.md).
