# Ionization continuum

↑ **Parent:** [Ionization](ionization.md)

The continuous set of scattering states consisting of a residual ion and a free [electron](electron.md), beginning at the relevant ionization threshold. A bound Rydberg series approaches this threshold; the continuum is a set of states rather than the act of [ionization](ionization.md).
