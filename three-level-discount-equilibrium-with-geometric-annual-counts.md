# Three-level discount equilibrium with geometric annual counts

↑ **Parent:** [No claims discount system](no-claims-discount-system.md)

Suppose annual [claim counts](claim-count.md) are independent with [geometric distribution](geometric-distribution.md) mass $pq^j$, $j\ge0$. In a [no claims discount system](no-claims-discount-system.md) with discounts $0,\alpha,\beta$, a claim returns either of the first two levels to zero; at the top, one claim sends the level to $\alpha$ and two or more send it to zero. The [transition matrix](stochastic-matrix.md) is $\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix}q&p&0\\q&0&p\\q^2&pq&p\end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$. Solving $\pi P=\pi$ gives the displayed [stationary distribution](stationary-distribution.md). With full premium $c$, the stationary [expected value](expected-value.md) of the premium is $c[1-(\alpha pq+\beta p^2)/(1-p^2q)]$. Independent annual sampling is essential; a persistent random intensity does not justify this marginal transition matrix.
