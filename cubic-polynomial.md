# Cubic polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial](polynomial-split.md)

A [cubic polynomial](cubic-polynomial.md) in one [variable](variable-mathematics.md) has [polynomial degree](degree-of-a-polynomial.md) three. Over a [splitting field](splitting-field.md), its [factorization of polynomials](factorization-of-polynomials.md) contains three [polynomial factors](polynomial-factor.md) of [polynomial degree](degree-of-a-polynomial.md) one when [multiplicity of a root](multiplicity-of-a-root.md) is counted.
