# Proper-divisor sum

↑ **Parent:** [Sum-of-divisors function](sum-of-divisors-function.md)

The [proper-divisor sum](proper-divisor-sum.md), or [aliquot sum](proper-divisor-sum.md), adds the positive [proper divisors](proper-divisor.md):

$$
s(n)=\sum_{\substack{d\mid n\\d<n}}d=\sigma(n)-n.
$$

In particular $s(1)=0$, an [empty sum](empty-sum.md). Removing $n$ from the [sum-of-divisors function](sum-of-divisors-function.md) is essential: $\sigma(6)=12$, but its [proper-divisor sum](proper-divisor-sum.md) is $6$.
