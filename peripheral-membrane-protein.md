# Peripheral membrane protein

↑ **Parent:** [Membrane protein](membrane-protein.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peripheral_membrane_protein)

A [peripheral membrane protein](peripheral-membrane-protein.md) associates with a membrane face through noncovalent interactions, without spanning its [hydrophobic](hydrophobicity.md) interior. Association with another [membrane protein](membrane-protein.md) can suffice; peripheral describes attachment, not an unimportant function.[Molecular Biology of the Cell, 4th edition by Bruce Alberts and others; Membrane Proteins Can Be Associated with the Lipid Bilayer in Various Ways: noncovalent attachment and peripheral proteins](molecular-biology-of-the-cell-4th-edition-by-bruce-alberts-and-others/membrane-proteins.md)
