# Stochastic gradient noise floor

↑ **Parent:** [Stochastic gradient descent](stochastic-gradient-descent.md)

A residual accuracy level in a bound for [stochastic gradient descent](stochastic-gradient-descent.md) with a fixed [step size](step-size.md) and nonvanishing [gradient](gradient.md) noise. For a [convex function](convex-function.md) with an $L$-[Lipschitz gradient](lipschitz-gradient.md), [step size](step-size.md) $1/L$ and conditional total [variance](variance-split.md) at most $M$, typical bounds have floors $M$ for averaged squared [gradient](gradient.md) norms, $\sqrt M$ for averaged [gradient](gradient.md) norms, and $M/L$ for the best expected [objective function](objective-function.md) gap. Such floors are upper bounds depending on the analysis and assumptions, rather than claims that these exact values are attained or that every last iterate converges.
