# Electromagnetic shower

↑ **Parent:** [Particle shower](particle-shower.md)

A cascade driven primarily by [electron](electron.md) and [Positron](positron.md) bremsstrahlung and [photon](photon.md) pair production in matter. An [electromagnetic calorimeter](electromagnetic-calorimeter.md) estimates the incident [energy](energy-split.md) from the deposited cascade. Escaping shower particles and incomplete instrument coverage require containment and response corrections.
