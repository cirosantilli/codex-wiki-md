# Proton-lifetime bound on a product of R-parity-violating couplings

↑ **Parent:** [Squark-mediated proton decay](squark-mediated-proton-decay.md)

Exchanging a down-type [squark](squark.md) of mass $M$ between $LQD$ and $UDD$ vertices produces a dimension-six [effective operator](effective-operator.md) with coefficient $C\sim\lambda'\lambda''/M^2$. [Dimensional analysis](dimensional-analysis.md) then gives a [proton decay](proton-decay.md) width $\Gamma_p=c_h|\lambda'\lambda''|^2m_p^5/M^4$, with a dimensionless factor $c_h$ containing hadronic matrix elements and phase space. A lifetime bound $\tau_p>\tau_{\min}$ implies $|\lambda'\lambda''|<M^2/\sqrt{c_h\tau_{\min}m_p^5}$. The bound is on the relevant flavour product and depends on the exchanged mass; [dimensional analysis](dimensional-analysis.md) alone does not determine $c_h$.
