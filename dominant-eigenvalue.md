# Dominant eigenvalue

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral decomposition](spectral-decomposition.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominant_eigenvalue)

An [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) is dominant when its [modulus](modulus.md) is greater than or equal to that of every other eigenvalue. It is uniquely dominant when the inequality is strict.
