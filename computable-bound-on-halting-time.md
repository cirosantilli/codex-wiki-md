# Computable bound on halting time

↑ **Parent:** [Halting problem](halting-problem.md)

A machine's halting times admit a [total computable function](total-computable-function.md) as a bound on all inputs in its domain if and only if that domain is a [computable set](computable-set.md). A bound decides the domain by finite simulation; conversely, a domain decider allows returning zero outside the domain and the exactly simulated halting time inside it. In particular every machine computing a [total computable function](total-computable-function.md) has an exact computable running-time function, while nontotal machines may or may not have total bounds.
