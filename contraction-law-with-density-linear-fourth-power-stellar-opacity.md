# Contraction law with density-linear fourth-power stellar opacity

↑ **Parent:** [Convective contraction with power-law opacity](convective-contraction-with-power-law-opacity.md)

For a fixed-mass, fully convective [monatomic gas](monatomic-gas.md) [protostar](protostar.md), write $P=KT^{5/2}$ and $K=K_0\mu^{-5/2}M^{-1/2}R^{-3/2}$. Matching the [photosphere](photosphere.md) to $\kappa P=2g/3$ with $\kappa=\kappa_0\rho T^4$ gives $T_e^8=2G\mathcal R\mu^4M^2R/(3\kappa_0K_0^2)$. The [Stefan–Boltzmann law](stefan-boltzmann-law.md) gives $L=\pi ac[2G\mathcal R/(3\kappa_0)]^{1/2}K_0^{-1}\mu^2MR^{5/2}$. The [stellar virial theorem](stellar-virial-theorem.md) gives total energy $-3GM^2/(7R)$, so $L=-3GM^2\dot R/(7R^2)$. Integrating proves $C=(49\pi ac/6)[2\mathcal R/(3\kappa_0G)]^{1/2}K_0^{-1}$. This holds before [stellar nuclear fusion](stellar-nuclear-fusion.md), changing [opacity](opacity.md) or [electron degeneracy pressure](electron-degeneracy-pressure.md) invalidates the assumed contraction law.
