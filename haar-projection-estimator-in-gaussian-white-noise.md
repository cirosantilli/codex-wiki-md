# Haar projection estimator in Gaussian white noise

↑ **Parent:** [Haar projection](haar-projection.md)

In the [Gaussian white noise model](gaussian-white-noise-model.md), estimate a [Haar scaling function](haar-scaling-function.md) coefficient by $\int\phi_{J,k}\,dY$. The resulting [Haar projection](haar-projection.md) estimator is an [unbiased estimator](unbiased-estimator.md) with [independent](independent-random-variables.md) coefficient errors of [variance](variance-split.md) $1/n$. On each cell it is the observed path increment divided by the cell length. This gives a finite-dimensional estimator without imposing smoothness on the drift.
