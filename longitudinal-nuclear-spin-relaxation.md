# Longitudinal nuclear spin relaxation

↑ **Parent:** [Bloch equations](bloch-equations.md)

With no transverse drive, the [Bloch equations](bloch-equations.md) give

$$
M_z(t)=M_0+[M_z(0)-M_0]e^{-t/T_1}.
$$

[Longitudinal nuclear spin relaxation](longitudinal-nuclear-spin-relaxation.md) restores the population imbalance and thus the [magnetization](magnetization.md) parallel to the static [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md). It corresponds to [population relaxation](population-relaxation.md) for the two-level model. Starting from $M_z(0)=0$, waiting $T_1$ recovers $1-e^{-1}$ of $M_0$, whereas waiting $5T_1$ recovers about $99.3\%$. This is why repeated measurements require a recovery-time decision.
