# Quadratic variational principle for a symmetric positive operator

↑ **Parent:** [Positive definite symmetric operator](positive-definite-symmetric-operator.md)

For a symmetric [bounded bilinear form](bounded-bilinear-form.md) $B$ that is strictly positive on nonzero vectors, and a bounded [linear functional](linear-functional.md) $\ell$, set $I(v)=B(v,v)-2\ell(v)$. The [first variation](first-variation.md) is $DI(v)[w]=2(B(v,w)-\ell(w))$. A [weak solution](weak-solution.md) $u$ of $B(u,w)=\ell(w)$ satisfies $I(u+w)-I(u)=B(w,w)$, so it is the unique minimizer. Conversely, any minimizer solves the weak equation. A [coercive bilinear form](coercive-bilinear-form.md) gives existence by the [Lax-Milgram theorem](lax-milgram-theorem.md); strict positivity alone does not.
