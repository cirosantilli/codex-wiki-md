# Reliable recovery

↑ **Parent:** [Reliable improvement](reliable-improvement.md)

A service outcome combining a [reliable improvement](reliable-improvement.md) criterion with movement from baseline [caseness](clinical-caseness.md) below the relevant clinical cutoffs. The instruments and denominator must be specified; this is not automatically [remission](diagnostic-remission.md) established by diagnostic interview.
