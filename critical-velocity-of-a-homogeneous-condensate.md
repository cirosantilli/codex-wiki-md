# Critical velocity of a homogeneous condensate

↑ **Parent:** [Landau criterion](landau-criterion.md)

For a repulsive homogeneous condensate with Bogoliubov [energy](energy-split.md) $E(p)=\sqrt{(p^2/2m)(p^2/2m+2\mu)}$, the infimum of $E(p)/p$ is the [speed of sound](speed-of-sound.md) $\sqrt{\mu/m}$. This is energetic stability against the given bulk modes, not a universal measured dissipation threshold.
