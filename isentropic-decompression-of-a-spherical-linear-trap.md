# Isentropic decompression of a spherical linear trap

↑ **Parent:** [Classical ideal gas in a spherical linear trap](classical-ideal-gas-in-a-spherical-linear-trap.md)

At fixed atom number, the [entropy](entropy-split.md) of the [classical ideal gas in a spherical linear trap](classical-ideal-gas-in-a-spherical-linear-trap.md) depends on temperature and strength through $Z_1\propto T^{9/2}a^{-3}$. Reversible adiabatic change therefore holds this product constant, giving the displayed relation. Reducing trap strength lowers the temperature while increasing cloud size. Its peak classical phase-space occupation $N/Z_1$ remains constant, so this cooling alone does not make the gas more quantum degenerate. Heat exchange, particle loss or irreversible entropy production would change this relation.
