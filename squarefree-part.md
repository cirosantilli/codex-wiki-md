# Squarefree part

↑ **Parent:** [Square-free integer](square-free-integer.md)

The squarefree part of a [positive integer](positive-integer.md) $n$ is the unique [squarefree integer](square-free-integer.md) $s$ such that $n=s t^2$ for a [positive integer](positive-integer.md) $t$. For example, the squarefree part of $72=2^3 3^2$ is $2$. It retains precisely the [primes](prime-number-split.md) with odd [prime exponents](p-adic-valuation.md); the [radical of an integer](radical-of-an-integer.md) instead retains every distinct [prime factor](prime-factor.md).
