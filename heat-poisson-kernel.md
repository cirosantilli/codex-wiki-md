# Heat Poisson kernel

↑ **Parent:** [Heat kernel](heat-kernel.md)

For $x>0$ and the [heat equation](heat-equation.md) on a half-line, the zero-initial-data solution with prescribed [Dirichlet boundary condition](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) $g$ is $\int_0^tP(x,t-s)g(s)ds$, where

$$
P(x,t)=\frac{x}{2\sqrt\pi\,t^{3/2}}e^{-x^2/(4t)},\qquad\mathcal L_tP=e^{-x\sqrt p}.
$$

Its mass concentrates at $t=0$ as $x\downarrow0$, recovering the boundary trace.
