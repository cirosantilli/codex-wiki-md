# Edge of a graph

↑ **Parent:** [Graph theory](graph-theory-split.md)

An edge joins two vertices of a graph; in a simple graph it is an unordered pair of distinct vertices.

An [edge of a graph](edge-of-a-graph.md) joins endpoints in a [graph](graph-split.md). For a [simple graph](simple-graph.md) it is an unordered pair of distinct [vertices of a graph](vertex-graph-theory.md); for a [directed graph](directed-graph.md) its endpoints are ordered.
