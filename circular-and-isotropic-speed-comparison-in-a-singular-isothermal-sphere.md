# Circular and isotropic speed comparison in a singular isothermal sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Singular isothermal sphere](singular-isothermal-sphere.md)

For the $r^{-2}$ [singular isothermal sphere](singular-isothermal-sphere.md), the isotropic [Spherical Jeans equation](spherical-jeans-equation.md) gives $\sigma_r^2=\sigma_\theta^2=\sigma_\phi^2=v_0^2/2$, so $\langle v^2\rangle=3v_0^2/2$. Randomly oriented circular orbits instead have $\sigma_r^2=0$, each transverse second moment $v_0^2/2$, and total mean square speed $v_0^2$.
