# Geometric continuity

↑ **Parent:** [Computer aided geometric design](computer-aided-geometric-design-split.md)

Joined regular [parametric curves](parametric-curve.md) have order-$k$ [geometric continuity](geometric-continuity.md) when their local images admit compatible orientation-preserving regular parameter choices with matching [derivatives](derivative.md) through order $k$. At order one the nonzero one-sided tangent [vectors](vector.md) must be positive scalar multiples. At order two the oriented bending must also match: equality of unsigned [curvature](curvature.md) alone does not suffice if the [curves](curve.md) bend to opposite sides.
