# Radial sine-power kernel integrability

↑ **Parent:** [Radial power integrability criterion](radial-power-integrability-criterion.md)

Assign an arbitrary finite value at the origin. [Polar integration](polar-coordinates.md) and the [substitution rule](integration-by-substitution.md) $u=r^2$ reduce the joint [L1 norm](l1-norm.md) exactly to $\pi\int_0^\infty|\sin u|u^{-\alpha}\,du$. Near zero, $|\sin u|\leq u$ gives $u^{1-\alpha}$; at infinity, $|\sin u|\leq1$ gives $u^{-\alpha}$. These are integrable when $\alpha<2$ and $\alpha>1$, respectively. Their explicit [integrals](integral-split.md) give the displayed bound. This is a planar version of [sine power kernel integrability](sine-power-kernel-integrability.md), with the radial area element determining the exponents.
