# Traction

↑ **Parent:** [Stress](stress.md)

Traction is the [force](force.md) per unit area transmitted across an oriented surface. For unit [normal vector](normal-vector.md) $\mathbf n$, the [stress](stress.md) [tensor](tensor.md) gives $\mathbf t=\boldsymbol\sigma\mathbf n$. Its normal component is normal stress, and its tangential component is [shear stress](shear-stress.md).
