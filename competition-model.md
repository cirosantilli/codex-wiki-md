# Competition model

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical biology](mathematical-biology-split.md)

In a biological competition model, increasing one population reduces the growth of another. A two-species example is $\dot u=ru(1-u-v)$, $\dot v=v(1-\beta u)$, with both cross-effects negative. This asymmetric example has self-limitation only in $u$; disappearance of $u$ therefore permits unbounded growth of $v$. For $\beta>1$ its positive coexistence equilibrium is a saddle whose [stable manifold](stable-manifold.md) separates exclusion outcomes.
