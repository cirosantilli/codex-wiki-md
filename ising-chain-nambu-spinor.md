# Ising-chain Nambu spinor

↑ **Parent:** [Nambu spinor](nambu-spinor.md)

With Fourier convention $c_n=N^{-1/2}\sum_ke^{-ikn}c_k$, the [Transverse-field Ising model](transverse-field-ising-model.md) has $H=J\sum_k\Psi_k^\dagger[(g-\cos k)\sigma_z-\sin k\,\sigma_x]\Psi_k$. Expanding the spinor retains the constant $-JgN$ and pairs $k$ with $-k$. The physical [quasiparticle](quasiparticle.md) energy is twice the positive matrix [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) times $J$, because both [momentum](momentum.md) partners occur in the Nambu sum.
