# Pro-p group

↑ **Parent:** [Profinite group](profinite-group.md)

A pro-$p$ group is an inverse limit of finite $p$-groups. Every continuous action on a finite set or finite module factors through a finite $p$-group. In particular its action on $\mu_p$ is trivial when the field characteristic differs from $p$, because $\operatorname{Aut}(\mu_p)$ has order $p-1$.
