# Slew rate

↑ **Parent:** [Operational amplifier](operational-amplifier.md)

The [slew rate](slew-rate.md) bounds the large-signal output-voltage change per unit time. It is distinct from a small-signal [gain-bandwidth product](gain-bandwidth-product.md). A sinusoidal output of amplitude $V_p$ and [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) $\omega$ needs $\omega V_p$ below the available [slew rate](slew-rate.md) to avoid this source of distortion.
