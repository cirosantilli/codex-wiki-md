# Nonsplit zero-weight Verma extension for sl2

↑ **Parent:** [Reducibility of an sl2 Verma module](reducibility-of-an-sl2-verma-module.md)

The [Verma module](verma-module.md) $M(0)$ has basis $v_n=f^nv_0$, with $hv_n=-2nv_n$, $fv_n=v_{n+1}$ and $ev_n=n(1-n)v_{n-1}$. Its submodule spanned by $v_n$ for $n\geq1$ is $M(-2)$, and the quotient is the trivial one-dimensional module. A complementary submodule would be trivial and hence lie in the zero-weight space $\mathbb Cv_0$, but $fv_0=v_1\ne0$. Therefore the displayed extension does not split. This explains why the finite-dimensional hypothesis in the [Weyl complete reducibility theorem](weyl-complete-reducibility-theorem.md) is essential.
