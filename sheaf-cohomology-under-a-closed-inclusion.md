# Sheaf cohomology under a closed inclusion

↑ **Parent:** [Direct image sheaf](direct-image-sheaf.md)

For a closed inclusion $i:X\hookrightarrow Y$, [direct image sheaf](direct-image-sheaf.md) formation is exact: stalks on $X$ are unchanged and stalks outside $X$ are zero. It also preserves [flasque sheaves](flasque-sheaf.md). Pushing forward a [flasque resolution](flasque-resolution.md) thus gives a resolution computing cohomology on $Y$, and its complex of [global sections](global-section.md) is identical to the original one on $X$. This proves the displayed isomorphism in every nonnegative degree.
