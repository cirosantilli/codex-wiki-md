# Null directions

↑ **Parent:** [Light cone](light-cone.md)

Null directions are tangent directions of the [light cone](light-cone.md); their Minkowski squared norm is zero.

A direction represented by a nonzero [null vector](null-vector.md) has zero [Minkowski norm](minkowski-norm.md). Such directions form the generators of the [light cone](light-cone.md).
