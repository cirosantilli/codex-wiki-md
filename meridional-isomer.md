# Meridional isomer

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric isomer](geometric-isomer.md)

In an octahedral [meridional isomer](meridional-isomer.md), three selected like donors lie in a plane through the [metal](metal.md) and include one trans pair. For three unsymmetrical bidentate ligands, this changes both steric contacts and the identity of opposite donor pairs.
