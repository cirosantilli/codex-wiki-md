<h1 id="logarithmic-expansion-of-ramanujan-s-function">Logarithmic expansion of Ramanujan's function</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ramanujan's function](ramanujan-s-function.md)

The [Gamma reflection formula](gamma-reflection-formula.md) gives $(\sin\pi z/\pi)\Gamma(z)=1/\Gamma(1-z)$, analytic at zero with value one. A finite-parameter [Watson's lemma](watson-s-lemma.md) representation of [Ramanujan's function](ramanujan-s-function.md) therefore gives the displayed coefficients. In particular $c_0=1$, $c_1=-\gamma$ and $c_2=\gamma^2-\pi^2/6$, with $\gamma$ [Euler's constant](euler-s-constant.md). Obtaining this representation requires taking an imaginary part before sending its Mellin exponent to zero; the original complex [integral](integral.md) is divergent at that boundary.
