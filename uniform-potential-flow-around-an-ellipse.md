# Uniform potential flow around an ellipse

↑ **Parent:** [Potential flow](potential-flow.md)

For $0<a<1$, the [Joukowski map](joukowski-map.md) $z=\zeta+a^2/\zeta$ maps the exterior [unit circle](complex-unit-circle.md) to an [ellipse](ellipse.md) of horizontal and vertical semiaxes $1+a^2$ and $1-a^2$. The [Milne-Thomson circle theorem](milne-thomson-circle-theorem.md) gives $W(\zeta)=U(e^{-i\alpha}\zeta+e^{i\alpha}/\zeta)$ for a [uniform flow](uniform-flow.md) inclined by $\alpha$. The [chain rule](chain-rule.md) produces the displayed [complex velocity](complex-velocity.md) in the physical plane. Since $z\sim\zeta$, the far-field flow retains its speed and direction. Its two [stagnation points](stagnation-point.md) are

$$
z=\pm[(1+a^2)\cos\alpha+i(1-a^2)\sin\alpha],
$$

obtained from $\zeta=\pm e^{i\alpha}$. The map derivative is nonzero on and outside the [unit circle](complex-unit-circle.md), so these zeros are preserved without introducing a boundary singularity.
