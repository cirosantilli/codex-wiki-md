# Waiting time for a word in independent nonuniform symbols

↑ **Parent:** [Pattern waiting time](pattern-waiting-time.md)

Here $w$ is a finite [word over an alphabet](string.md), every letter of the word has positive probability, and $B(w)$ is the set of lengths of its [borders of a word](border-of-a-word.md), including its full length. Start a unit-capital gambler before every draw, betting successively on the word's letters and dividing surviving capital by the required letter probability. Total capital minus the number of entrants is a [martingale](martingale-split.md). At the first full match, the surviving gamblers correspond exactly to the borders, giving the displayed formula by [optional stopping](optional-sampling-theorem-for-a-supermartingale.md). Disjoint blocks of full word length give a [geometric tail bound from a uniform escape probability](geometric-tail-bound-from-a-uniform-escape-probability.md), so the waiting time is integrable; bounded word length and positive letter probabilities give bounded [martingale](martingale-split.md) increments and justify stopping.
