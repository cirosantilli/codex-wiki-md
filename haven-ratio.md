# Haven ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Ionic conductivity](ionic-conductivity.md)

With the explicitly stated convention $H_R=D_{\rm tr}/D_\sigma$, the tracer and collective [diffusion coefficients](diffusion-coefficient.md) are related by $D_\sigma=D_{\rm tr}/H_R$. The [Nernst-Einstein relation](nernst-einstein-relation.md) then gives $\sigma=nq^2D_{\rm tr}/(H_Rk_BT)$. State the convention, since reciprocal definitions can otherwise cause confusion.
