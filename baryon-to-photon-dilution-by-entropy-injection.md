# Baryon-to-photon dilution by entropy injection

↑ **Parent:** [Baryon-to-photon ratio](baryon-to-photon-ratio.md)

Thermal [photon number density](photon-number-density.md) is proportional to $T^3$. A sudden photon heating factor $(1+r)^{1/4}$ therefore raises photon number by $(1+r)^{3/4}$ and dilutes the [baryon-to-photon ratio](baryon-to-photon-ratio.md) by its inverse when baryon number is conserved. Comparing [Big Bang nucleosynthesis](big-bang-nucleosynthesis.md) and [cosmic microwave background](cosmic-microwave-background.md) baryon-density determinations can test entropy injection between the two epochs, subject to changes in the expansion rate and nuclear abundances.
