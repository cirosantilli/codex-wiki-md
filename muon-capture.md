# Muon capture

↑ **Parent:** [Muonic atom](muonic-atom.md)

A bound negative [muon](muon.md) can be absorbed by a [nucleus](atomic-nucleus-split.md) through the [weak charged current](charged-current.md), changing a [proton](proton.md) into a [neutron](neutron-split.md) and emitting a [muon neutrino](muon-neutrino.md). This competes with ordinary [muon decay](muon-decay.md) and changes the observable disappearance lifetime in a [muonic atom](muonic-atom.md).
