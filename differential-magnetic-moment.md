# Differential magnetic moment

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic dipole moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md)

With [energy](energy-split.md) convention $E=-\boldsymbol\mu\cdot\mathbf B$, the difference of physical moments is the negative derivative of a transition [energy](energy-split.md). Some trapping conventions instead call the positive [energy](energy-split.md) slope an effective magnetic moment. The sign convention must be stated; either gives the same absolute sensitivity of transition [frequency](frequency.md) to a field fluctuation.
