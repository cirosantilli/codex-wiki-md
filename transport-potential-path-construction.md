# Transport potential path construction

↑ **Parent:** [Strong c-monotonicity](strong-c-monotonicity.md)

Anchor a countable dense subset of a closed [c-cyclically monotone](c-cyclical-monotonicity.md) support. Take the infimum of accumulated differences $c(x_{j+1},y_j)-c(x_j,y_j)$ along chains ending at a variable point. Cyclical monotonicity bounds this potential below on the support projection. Its cost transform gives the other potential, with equality on the support. Continuity makes both potentials [upper semicontinuous](upper-semicontinuity.md) and thus Borel.
