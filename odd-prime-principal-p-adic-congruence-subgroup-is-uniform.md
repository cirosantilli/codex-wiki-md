# Odd-prime principal p-adic congruence subgroup is uniform

↑ **Parent:** [Uniform pro-p group](uniform-pro-p-group.md)

For an odd prime $p$, the [matrix logarithm](matrix-logarithm.md) and [matrix exponential](matrix-exponential.md) are inverse maps between $1+pM_d(\mathbb Z_p)$ and $pM_d(\mathbb Z_p)$. They imply that the $p$th-power map carries $U_m$ bijectively onto $U_{m+1}$. Direct multiplication gives $[U_i,U_j]\subseteq U_{i+j}$, so the [group](group-split.md) is a [powerful pro-p group](powerful-pro-p-group.md). The quotient $U_m/U_{m+1}$ is the additive [matrix](matrix.md) space over $\mathbb F_p$, with constant index $p^{d^2}$. The $d^2$ [matrices](matrix.md) $1+pE_{ij}$ topologically generate, by successive approximation through these quotients. The [lower p-series](lower-p-series.md) is exactly $U_m$, so the [group](group-split.md) is a [uniform pro-p group](uniform-pro-p-group.md) and a [torsion-free group](torsion-free-group.md). At $p=2$ the first congruence subgroup contains $-I$ and is not uniform.
