# Direct CP violation in two-pion kaon decay

↑ **Parent:** [CP violation](cp-violation.md)

Direct [CP violation](cp-violation.md) concerns the decay transition itself. At leading order, the charged and neutral two-[pion](pion.md) [decay amplitude](decay-amplitude.md) ratios separate a common $\epsilon$ from channel-dependent $\epsilon'$.[Cronin, pp.573–574, distinction between epsilon and epsilon-prime](https://www.nevis.columbia.edu/~haas/documents/physics_nobel_lectures/physics71-80-3.pdf)

For the corresponding double ratio of partial [decay widths](decay-width.md),

$$
R=\frac{\Gamma_L^{00}/\Gamma_S^{00}}{\Gamma_L^{+-}/\Gamma_S^{+-}}
\simeq\left|\frac{1-2x}{1+x}\right|^2
=1-6\operatorname{Re}x+O(|x|^2),\qquad x=\frac{\epsilon'}{\epsilon}.
$$

The expansion follows from $(1-2x)/(1+x)=1-3x+O(x^2)$. A single nonzero long-lived two-[pion](pion.md) yield does not isolate $x$: it can already occur with $\epsilon'=0$. Thus the 1964 evidence for [CP violation](cp-violation.md) must be distinguished from the later experimental establishment of direct [CP violation](cp-violation.md) through $\epsilon'$.
