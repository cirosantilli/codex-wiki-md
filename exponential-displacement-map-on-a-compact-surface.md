# Exponential displacement map on a compact surface

↑ **Parent:** [Degree modulo two](degree-modulo-two.md)

If $S$ is compact and $V$ is a smooth vector field, then

$$
\phi(p)=\exp_p(V(p))
$$

is smooth and well-defined by the [Hopf-Rinow theorem](hopf-rinow-theorem.md). The maps $\phi_t(p)=\exp_p(tV(p))$ form a smooth homotopy from the identity to $\phi$, so $\deg_2(\phi)=1$.
