# Point spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Spectrum (functional analysis)](spectrum-functional-analysis.md)

For a [bounded linear operator](continuous-linear-operator.md) $T:X\to X$ on a complex [Banach space](banach-space-split.md), the point spectrum is precisely its set of [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md):

$$
\sigma_p(T)=\{\lambda\in\mathbb C:\ker(T-\lambda I)\ne\{0\}\}.
$$

Here $I$ is the [identity operator](identity-operator.md). Thus membership requires an actual nonzero [eigenvector](eigenvector.md), whereas the [spectrum of a bounded operator](spectrum-of-a-bounded-operator.md) only requires failure of a [bounded inverse](bounded-inverse.md). Every [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) belongs to the [spectrum](spectrum-functional-analysis.md), but the inclusion can be strict. The [multiplication operator](multiplication-operator.md) discussed below has an entire interval in its [spectrum](spectrum-functional-analysis.md) and empty [point spectrum](point-spectrum.md).[HKUST Math 4063 notes, chapter 4, section 3: spectrum and point spectrum](https://www.math.hkust.edu.hk/~makyli/4063/fanotes2_150615.pdf#page=38)
