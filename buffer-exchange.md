# Buffer exchange

↑ **Parent:** [Dialysis (biochemistry)](dialysis-biochemistry.md)

[Buffer exchange](buffer-exchange.md) changes the small components surrounding a retained [macromolecule](macromolecule.md) toward those of a desired [buffer solution](buffer-solution.md). Components absent from fresh [dialysate](dialysate.md) tend to leave; supplied components can enter. Each permeant [solute](solute.md) has its own exchange problem.[Tech Tip 20, pp. 1-2, renewed gradients and Other Factors in Dialysis](dialysis-an-overview-pierce-biotechnology-technical-note.md)

For a dilute, nonbinding permeant [solute](solute.md), an ideal equilibrium calculation approaches the bath's specified [concentration](concentration.md). That approximation need not describe a [solute](solute.md) bound to a [protein](protein-split.md) or a charged mixture with additional electrical constraints. Retaining a [protein](protein-split.md) also does not by itself establish that its [protein structure](protein-structure.md) or catalytic function survives the change of [buffer solution](buffer-solution.md).
