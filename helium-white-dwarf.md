# Helium white dwarf

↑ **Parent:** [White dwarf](white-dwarf.md)

A helium white dwarf is a [white dwarf](white-dwarf.md) whose core is primarily helium. Stripping a low-mass evolved star before helium ignition can produce one on a much shorter timescale than isolated stellar evolution.
