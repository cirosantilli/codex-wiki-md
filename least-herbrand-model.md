# Least Herbrand model

↑ **Parent:** [Herbrand model](herbrand-model.md)

For a satisfiable equality-free universal [logical conjunction](logical-conjunction.md) of [Horn clauses](horn-clause.md), intersecting the sets of true ground atoms of its [Herbrand models](herbrand-model.md) gives another [Herbrand model](herbrand-model.md), contained in every one of them. A ground atom is true in this [least Herbrand model](least-herbrand-model.md) exactly when it holds in every [Herbrand model](herbrand-model.md). Positive [existential sentences](existential-sentence.md) are true in the [least Herbrand model](least-herbrand-model.md) exactly when they follow from the [Horn clauses](horn-clause.md); allowing negated atoms makes this equivalence fail. For example, the [least Herbrand model](least-herbrand-model.md) of $\forall x(P(x)\to Q(x))$ over a single constant $a$ makes both predicates empty and satisfies $\exists x\neg P(x)$, whereas the [first-order structure](first-order-structure.md) making both predicates universal is a [countermodel](countermodel.md) to that consequence.
