<h1 id="poly-a-polymerase">Poly(A) polymerase</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Enzyme](enzyme-split.md)

An [enzyme](enzyme-split.md) that adds adenosine residues to an [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) $3'$ end using [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md). Supplying a correctly pre-cleaved substrate separates the extension reaction from the preceding cleavage step.
