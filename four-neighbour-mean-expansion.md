# Four-neighbour mean expansion

↑ **Parent:** [Finite difference method](finite-difference-method.md)

Averaging a smooth [function](function-split.md) at the four points $x\pm he_1,x\pm he_2$ cancels odd [Taylor expansion](taylor-expansion.md) terms and leaves $h^2\Delta u/4$. The fourth-order correction is $h^4(u_{1111}+u_{2222})/48$. Equality to the center value at every sufficiently small radius therefore forces $\Delta u=0$ at that point, but does not establish harmonicity throughout a neighbourhood.
