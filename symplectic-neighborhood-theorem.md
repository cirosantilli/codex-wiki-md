# Symplectic neighborhood theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic normal bundle](symplectic-normal-bundle.md)

If a symplectomorphism between compact symplectic submanifolds lifts to an isomorphism of their [symplectic normal bundles](symplectic-normal-bundle.md), then it extends to a symplectomorphism between neighborhoods. Thus the germ of a symplectic neighborhood is determined by the restricted form and the symplectic normal bundle.
