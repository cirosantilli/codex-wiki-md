# Inertia group of a character

↑ **Parent:** [Restriction of a character](restriction-of-a-character.md)

For $N\triangleleft G$ and $\theta\in\operatorname{Irr}(N)$, set $\theta^g(n)=\theta(g^{-1}ng)$. Its inertia group is its stabilizer under this conjugation action and contains $N$. Write $\operatorname{Irr}(G\mid\theta)$ for the [irreducible characters](irreducible-character.md) of $G$ whose restriction to $N$ contains $\theta$.
