# Hexagonal ice crystal anisotropy

↑ **Parent:** [Crystal interface anisotropy](crystal-interface-anisotropy.md)

Ordinary atmospheric snow crystals are predominantly hexagonal [ice](ice.md) Ih. Rotating by sixty degrees around its crystallographic axis leaves the equilibrium orientation dependence unchanged, giving six equivalent basal-plane directions. [Temperature](temperature.md) and supersaturation determine whether plates, columns or [crystal dendrites](dendrite-crystal.md) develop. [Dendritic growth of a snowflake](dendritic-growth-of-a-snowflake.md) needs this crystal symmetry as well as the diffusion instability.
