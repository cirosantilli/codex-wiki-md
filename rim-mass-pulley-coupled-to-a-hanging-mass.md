# Rim-mass pulley coupled to a hanging mass

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrangian mechanics](lagrangian-mechanics-split.md)

A massless fixed-axis [pulley](pulley.md) carries a rim [point mass](point-mass.md) $M$ at radius $R$. A [mechanical string](mechanical-string.md) gripping its rim without slipping passes over a fixed guide [pulley](pulley.md) to a hanging [mass](mass.md) $m$. Choose the angle $\theta$ from downward vertical so the hanging [mass](mass.md) descends by $R\theta$. The [Lagrangian](lagrangian.md) is $(M+m)R^2\dot\theta^2/2+MgR\cos\theta+mgR\theta$. The stable [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) satisfies $\sin\theta_e=m/M$ with $\cos\theta_e>0$, and exists for $0<m<M$. Its small-amplitude [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) is $[Mg\cos\theta_e/((M+m)R)]^{1/2}$.
