# Uniform half-line Schrodinger representation by boundary lifting

↑ **Parent:** [Boundary lifting](boundary-lifting.md)

For the [free Schrodinger equation](free-schrodinger-equation.md) with compatible smooth initial and boundary data, subtract $g(t)e^{-x}$ before taking a [Fourier sine transform](fourier-sine-transform.md). The lifted initial datum vanishes at the endpoint, making its sine transform $O(k^{-2})$ if its second derivative is integrable. The transformed forcing is $k(ig-g')/(1+k^2)$; time integration followed by [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) makes its solution contribution uniformly $O(k^{-3})$. An integrable spectral majorant proves [uniform convergence](uniform-convergence.md) even at the compatible initial-boundary corner.
