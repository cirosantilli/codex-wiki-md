# Voronoi cell

↑ **Parent:** [Voronoi diagram](voronoi-diagram.md)

For centres $m_1,\ldots,m_r$ in [Euclidean space](euclidean-norm.md), the [Voronoi cell](voronoi-cell.md) of $m_j$ is the set of points at least as close to $m_j$ as to every other centre. Shared boundaries consist of points tied between nearest centres; assigning each such point to one centre makes the cells a [partition of a set](partition-of-a-set.md).

On the real line with distinct ordered centres, the [Voronoi cells](voronoi-cell.md) are [intervals](interval-mathematics.md), with boundaries at the midpoints of adjacent centres. This makes nearest-centre clusters contiguous blocks of sorted observations in [k-means clustering](k-means-clustering.md).
