# Normal natural-mean parameter ratio estimator

↑ **Parent:** [Exponential family](exponential-family-split.md)

For independent $N(\mu,v)$ observations with unknown mean and variance, the first natural parameter is $\phi_1=\mu/v$. Its [maximum-likelihood estimator](maximum-likelihood-estimator.md) is $\bar X/[n^{-1}\sum_i(X_i-\bar X)^2]$. The [delta method](delta-method.md) and independent asymptotic mean/variance errors give limiting variance $v^{-1}+2\mu^2v^{-2}$ for $\sqrt n(\widehat\phi_1-\phi_1)$. Knowing $v$ removes the second contribution and makes the estimator exactly normal with variance $1/(nv)$.
