# SEC light-scattering detection

↑ **Parent:** [Size-exclusion chromatography](size-exclusion-chromatography.md)

[SEC light-scattering detection](sec-light-scattering-detection.md) adds information from [scattering of light](scattering-of-light.md) to the separation. Static scattering with a [concentration](concentration.md) readout can infer a [weight-average molar mass](weight-average-molar-mass.md) for material in an eluting slice without the standard-family retention calibration. Dynamic scattering estimates a [diffusion coefficient](diffusion-coefficient.md) and hence a [hydrodynamic radius](hydrodynamic-radius.md). “Absolute” here means independent of a retention-to-[molar mass](molar-mass.md) standard curve; it still needs calibrated detectors, optical response parameters, and a valid sample model. Coeluting species produce a mixture-weighted result, rather than individually identified [molecules](molecule-split.md).

A concrete manufacturer test used a Bio SEC-3 $300\,\mathrm{\mathring A}$ column, $7.8\times300\,\mathrm{mm}$ with $3\,\mathrm{\mu m}$ particles, an Agilent 1260 Infinity G5611A quaternary pump and G5667A autosampler, a $10\,\mathrm{mm}$ diode-array flow cell, and dual-angle/dynamic scattering detectors. A [buffer solution](buffer-solution.md) at [pH](ph.md) $7.4$ flowed at $0.75\,\mathrm{mL\,min^{-1}}$; column and scattering detector were at $30\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. [Absorbance](absorbance.md) at $280\,\mathrm{nm}$ supplied a concentration-sensitive readout. [Dilution](dilution.md) and loading comparisons tested response: seventeen ovalbumin injections spanning $2$–$960\,\mathrm{\mu g}$ gave a reported average $47{,}198\,\mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$ with $2\%$ relative standard deviation. The note reports poorer separation of thyroglobulin from aggregates and an elevated mass estimate, illustrating the coelution limit. Its $0.2\,\mathrm{\mu m}$ sample filtration also limits interpretation of larger [protein aggregation](protein-aggregation.md) products removed before injection.[Sonja Schneider, Determination of Protein Molecular Weight and Size Using the Agilent 1260 Infinity Multi-Detector Bio-SEC Solution with Advanced Light Scattering Detection, 5991-3955EN, printed pp. 2–4: apparatus, Table 1, Figure 2 and Table 3](determination-of-protein-molecular-weight-and-size-using-the-agilent-1260-infinity-multi-detector-bio-sec-solution-with-advanced-light-scattering-detection-by-sonja-schneider.md)
