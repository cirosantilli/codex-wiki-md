# Coulomb WKB quantization with the Langer correction

↑ **Parent:** [Radial Schrodinger equation for the hydrogen atom](radial-schrodinger-equation-for-the-hydrogen-atom.md)

After the logarithmic radial coordinate $r=e^x$, the Langer replacement $l(l+1)\mapsto(l+1/2)^2$ turns the radial Coulomb problem into a two-turning-point [WKB approximation](wkb-approximation.md) problem. Its action integral gives

$$
E_{n,l}\sim-\frac{mV_0^2}{2\hbar^2(n+l+1)^2},
$$

which in this Coulomb case agrees with the exact dependence on the principal quantum number.
