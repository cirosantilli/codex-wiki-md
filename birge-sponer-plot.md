# Birge-Sponer plot

↑ **Parent:** [Morse oscillator](morse-oscillator.md)

A plot of successive vibrational spacings against a vibrational index. Extrapolation toward zero spacing estimates a dissociation limit under an assumed spacing law. The integration bounds and whether the energy is measured from the potential minimum or vibrational [ground state](ground-state.md) must be specified.
