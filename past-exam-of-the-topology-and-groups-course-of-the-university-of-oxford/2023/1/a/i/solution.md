<h1 id="1/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Two [continuous maps](../../../../../continuous-map.md) $f_0,f_1:X\to Y$ are [homotopic](../../../../../homotopy-split.md) if there is a [continuous map](../../../../../continuous-map.md)

$$
H:X\times I\longrightarrow Y,\qquad H(x,0)=f_0(x),\quad H(x,1)=f_1(x).
$$

Here the [Cartesian product](../../../../../cartesian-product.md) has the [product topology](../../../../../product-topology.md). Such an $H$ is a [homotopy](../../../../../homotopy-split.md), written $f_0\simeq f_1$.

The [topological spaces](../../../../../topological-space.md) $X,Y$ are [homotopy equivalent](../../../../../homotopy-inverse.md) if there are [continuous maps](../../../../../continuous-map.md) $f:X\to Y$ and $g:Y\to X$ such that

$$
\boxed{gf\simeq\operatorname{id}_X,\qquad fg\simeq\operatorname{id}_Y.}
$$

Each is then a [homotopy inverse](../../../../../homotopy-inverse.md) of the other.

For $A\subseteq X$ with its [subspace topology](../../../../../subspace-topology.md) with [inclusion map](../../../../../inclusion-map.md) $i:A\hookrightarrow X$, a [homotopy retract](../../../../../homotopy-retract.md), in the course's convention, is a [continuous map](../../../../../continuous-map.md) $r:X\to A$ with

$$
\boxed{ri=\operatorname{id}_A,\qquad ir\simeq\operatorname{id}_X.}
$$

Thus $r$ is an actual [retraction](../../../../../retraction.md), and $i,r$ give a [homotopy equivalence](../../../../../homotopy-equivalence.md). Requiring the latter [homotopy](../../../../../homotopy-split.md) to fix $A$ pointwise at all times gives the stronger condition of a [deformation retraction](../../../../../deformation-retraction.md) as used in this wiki. The course's definition of [homotopy retract](../../../../../homotopy-retract.md) requires no such restriction on that [homotopy](../../../../../homotopy-split.md).
