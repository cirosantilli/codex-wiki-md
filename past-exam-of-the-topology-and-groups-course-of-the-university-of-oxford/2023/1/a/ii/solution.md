↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**Yes.** Take $X=S^1$, the [circle](../../../../../circle.md), and $A=\{1\}\subset S^1$. The [constant function](../../../../../constant-function.md) $r(z)=1$ is a [continuous map](../../../../../continuous-map.md) and satisfies $ri=\operatorname{id}_A$, so $A$ is a [retract](../../../../../retract.md) of $X$.

However, their [fundamental groups](../../../../../fundamental-group.md) are

$$
\pi_1(S^1,1)\cong\mathbb Z,\qquad \pi_1(\{1\},1)=1.
$$

A [homotopy equivalence](../../../../../homotopy-equivalence.md) between [path-connected spaces](../../../../../path-connected-space.md) induces a [group isomorphism](../../../../../group-isomorphism.md) of their [fundamental groups](../../../../../fundamental-group.md), allowing the usual change of [base point](../../../../../base-point.md). The [infinite cyclic group](../../../../../infinite-cyclic-group.md) is not the [trivial group](../../../../../trivial-group.md). Therefore **a [retract](../../../../../retract.md) need not have the [homotopy type](../../../../../homotopy-type.md) of its ambient space**.
