<h1 id="1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Define

$$
r:M(f)\longrightarrow Y,\qquad r(q(x,t))=f(x),\quad r(i(y))=y.
$$

The two formulas agree on the attached end, so the [universal property of the quotient topology](../../../../../universal-property-of-the-quotient-topology.md) makes $r$ a [continuous map](../../../../../continuous-map.md). Also $ri=\operatorname{id}_Y$.

Slide each cylinder point towards its attached end using

$$
K(q(x,t),s)=q(x,t+s(1-t)),\qquad K(i(y),s)=i(y).
$$

At $t=1$ these formulas both give $i(f(x))$, so they define a [homotopy](../../../../../homotopy-split.md) on the [mapping cylinder](../../../../../mapping-cylinder.md). For continuity, the [product of a quotient map with the unit interval](../../../../../product-of-a-quotient-map-with-the-unit-interval.md) is a [quotient map](../../../../../quotient-map.md); the formulas before taking the [quotient topology](../../../../../quotient-topology.md) are [continuous maps](../../../../../continuous-map.md) and agree on every identified pair. Thus

$$
K(-,0)=\operatorname{id}_{M(f)},\qquad K(-,1)=ir.
$$

Moreover $Y$ stays fixed throughout. We have obtained the stronger conclusion

$$
\boxed{M(f)\text{ deformation retracts onto }Y.}
$$

In particular $r$ is the requested [homotopy retract](../../../../../homotopy-retract.md).
