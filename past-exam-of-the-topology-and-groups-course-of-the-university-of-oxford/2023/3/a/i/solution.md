↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [covering map](../../../../../covering-space.md) is a [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md) [surjection](../../../../../surjective-function.md) $p:\widetilde X\to X$ such that every $x\in X$ has an [open](../../../../../open-set.md) [neighbourhood](../../../../../neighbourhood-mathematics.md) $U$ for which

$$
\boxed{p^{-1}(U)=\bigsqcup_{\alpha\in A}U_\alpha,\qquad
p|_{U_\alpha}:U_\alpha\longrightarrow U\text{ a homeomorphism},}
$$

where the $U_\alpha$ are pairwise disjoint [open subsets](../../../../../open-set.md) of $\widetilde X$. Such a $U$ is an [evenly covered neighbourhood](../../../../../evenly-covered-neighbourhood.md), and the sets $U_\alpha$ are its [covering sheets](../../../../../covering-sheet.md). The index [set](../../../../../set-split.md) may be infinite. The requirement that every sheet map onto all of $U$ is part of the definition.
