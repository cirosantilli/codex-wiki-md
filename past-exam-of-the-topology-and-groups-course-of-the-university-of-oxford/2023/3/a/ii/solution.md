<h1 id="3/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For a [covering map](../../../../../covering-space.md) over a nonempty [connected space](../../../../../connected-space.md), the [degree of a covering map](../../../../../degree-of-a-covering-map.md) is

$$
\boxed{\deg p=|p^{-1}(x)|,}
$$

the [cardinality](../../../../../cardinality.md) of any [fibre of a function](../../../../../fiber-of-a-function.md). To show independence of $x$, choose an [evenly covered neighbourhood](../../../../../evenly-covered-neighbourhood.md) $U$. Each [covering sheet](../../../../../covering-sheet.md) contains exactly one point over every $y\in U$, so $|p^{-1}(y)|$ equals the number of [covering sheets](../../../../../covering-sheet.md) over $U$. The [fibre of a function](../../../../../fiber-of-a-function.md) cardinality is therefore [locally constant](../../../../../locally-constant-function.md). For a fixed [cardinal number](../../../../../cardinal-number-split.md) $\kappa$, the points with that cardinality form an [open subset](../../../../../open-set.md), and their complement is also open. [Connectedness](../../../../../connected-space.md) forces the value to be constant everywhere.

For a [path-connected](../../../../../path-connected-space.md) base there is also an explicit [fibre bijection by path lifting](../../../../../fibre-bijection-by-path-lifting.md): lift a [path](../../../../../continuous-path.md) from $x$ to $y$ starting at each point over $x$ and take its endpoint. The [path lifting theorem](../../../../../path-lifting-theorem.md) gives existence and uniqueness; [path reversal](../../../../../path-reversal.md) gives the inverse [bijection](../../../../../bijection.md). The bijection may depend on the [path](../../../../../continuous-path.md), but the resulting equality of [cardinalities](../../../../../cardinality.md) does not.

**A [connected](../../../../../connected-space.md) base is needed for one global degree.** Without it, use a degree on each [connected component](../../../../../connected-component.md) on which the cardinality is constant. For example, a [covering map](../../../../../covering-space.md) of a discrete two-point [topological space](../../../../../topological-space.md) can have one point over the first point and two over the second. Thus the unqualified printed definition needs the usual connected-base convention, or the explicit assumption that every [fibre of a function](../../../../../fiber-of-a-function.md) has the same cardinality.
