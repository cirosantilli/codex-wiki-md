<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Choose a [spanning tree](../../../../../spanning-tree.md) $T$ of the finite [connected graph](../../../../../connected-graph.md) $X$. It contains every [vertex](../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md) of $X$ and has $v(X)-1$ [edges](../../../../../edge-of-a-graph.md) by part (i). Collapsing $T$ to a point is a [homotopy equivalence](../../../../../homotopy-equivalence.md): $T$ is a [contractible](../../../../../contractible-space.md) [subcomplex](../../../../../simplicial-subcomplex.md), and [collapsing a contractible cofibration](../../../../../collapsing-a-contractible-cofibration.md) in a [CW complex](../../../../../cw-complex.md) preserves [homotopy type](../../../../../homotopy-type.md).

Every [edge](../../../../../edge-of-a-graph.md) outside $T$ then becomes a [circle](../../../../../circle.md) attached to the common point. Hence the [quotient space](../../../../../quotient-topological-space.md) $X/T$ is a [bouquet of circles](../../../../../bouquet-of-circles.md) with

$$
r=e(X)-e(T)=e(X)-v(X)+1
$$

petals. The [fundamental group of a bouquet of circles](../../../../../fundamental-group-of-a-bouquet-of-circles.md) is the [free group](../../../../../free-group.md) with one member of a [free basis of a group](../../../../../free-basis-of-a-group.md) for each petal. Therefore the [rank of the fundamental group of a finite connected graph](../../../../../rank-of-the-fundamental-group-of-a-finite-connected-graph.md) is

$$
\boxed{\pi_1(X)\cong F_{\,1-\chi(X)},\qquad r=1-\chi(X).}
$$

More concretely, choose a [vertex](../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md) as [base point](../../../../../base-point.md); follow the unique [path in a graph](../../../../../path-in-a-graph.md) in $T$ to one end of a non-tree [edge](../../../../../edge-of-a-graph.md), traverse it, and return through $T$. These [based loops](../../../../../based-loop.md) give the [free basis of a group](../../../../../free-basis-of-a-group.md). Thus $r$ is the number of free [group generators](../../../../../generator-of-a-group.md), equivalently the minimum size of a [generating set of a group](../../../../../generating-set-of-a-group.md), rather than the size of every possible redundant [generating set of a group](../../../../../generating-set-of-a-group.md). Minimality also follows by [abelianization](../../../../../abelianization.md), since $F_r$ has [abelianization](../../../../../abelianization.md) $\mathbb Z^r$.
