<h1 id="2/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Take the [free product](../../../../../free-product.md) $F=G_1*G_2$, with its natural [group homomorphisms](../../../../../group-homomorphism.md) $k_j:G_j\to F$, and form the [normal closure](../../../../../normal-closure.md)

$$
N=\left\langle\!\left\langle
k_1(\phi_1(h))\,k_2(\phi_2(h))^{-1}:h\in G_0
\right\rangle\!\right\rangle.
$$

The [pushout of groups](../../../../../pushout-of-groups.md) is the [quotient group](../../../../../quotient-group.md)

$$
\boxed{P=(G_1*G_2)/N,}
$$

equipped with the [group homomorphisms](../../../../../group-homomorphism.md) $j_1,j_2$ obtained by composing $k_1,k_2$ with the [quotient map of groups](../../../../../quotient-map-of-groups.md). The defining [relators](../../../../../relator.md) ensure

$$
j_1\phi_1=j_2\phi_2.
$$

Thus the two prescribed images of every [group element](../../../../../group-element.md) of $G_0$ agree in $P$. This construction works for arbitrary [group homomorphisms](../../../../../group-homomorphism.md) $\phi_1,\phi_2$; no [injectivity](../../../../../injective-function.md) is assumed, and $j_1,j_2$ need not be [injective](../../../../../injective-function.md). Its [universal property](../../../../../universal-property.md) is proved in the next part.
