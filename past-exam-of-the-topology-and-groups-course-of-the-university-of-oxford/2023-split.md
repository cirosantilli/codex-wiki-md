# 2023

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the topology and groups course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Solutions to all three questions of the [topology and groups course of the University of Oxford](../topology-and-groups-course-of-the-university-of-oxford.md) Part B examination, sat on 31 May 2023. Source: [original released PDF, bundle pages 221–223; paper pages 1–3](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/request_for_mathematics_examinat/response/3129786/attach/3/FOI%20202507%20740%20Data.pdf#page=221). The Mathematics and Statistics version is labelled B3.4. All eighteen parts are treated below. Question 3(a)(iii), as printed, needs an additional hypothesis such as [local path connectedness](../locally-path-connected-space.md); its solution includes both the intended argument and a counterexample to the unrestricted assertion.

Throughout Question 1, $I=[0,1]$ has its usual [topology](../topology-split.md), and the [mapping cylinder](../mapping-cylinder.md) uses the paper's convention that $(x,1)$ is attached to $f(x)$.
