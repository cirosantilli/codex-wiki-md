# Alpha effect

↑ **Parent:** [Mean-field dynamo](mean-field-dynamo.md)

The alpha effect is the part of a [mean-field electromotive force](mean-field-electromotive-force.md) linear in the mean [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) itself, $\boldsymbol{\mathcal E}=\boldsymbol\alpha\overline{\mathbf B}+\cdots$. The [alpha tensor](alpha-tensor.md) can be anisotropic; it need not be a scalar multiple of the identity. Its [curl](curl.md) can couple transverse mean-field components and produce an [alpha-squared dynamo](alpha-squared-dynamo.md).
