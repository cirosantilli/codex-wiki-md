# Data-processing inequality for coherent information

↑ **Parent:** [Coherent information](coherent-information.md)

A [quantum channel](quantum-channel.md) on the receiving system cannot increase [coherent information](coherent-information.md): $I(A\rangle B)_\rho\geq I(A\rangle B^{\prime})_\sigma$. Dilate the channel to a [linear isometry of Hilbert spaces](linear-isometry-of-hilbert-spaces.md) $B\to B^{\prime}E$. The difference is $I(A:E|B^{\prime})$ of the dilated state, nonnegative by [Strong subadditivity of Von Neumann entropy](strong-subadditivity-of-quantum-entropy.md).
