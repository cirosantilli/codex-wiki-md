# Regular epimorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Epimorphism](epimorphism.md)

A regular epimorphism is a morphism that is the [coequalizer](coequalizer.md) of some parallel pair. Every regular epimorphism is a [strong epimorphism](strong-epimorphism.md).
