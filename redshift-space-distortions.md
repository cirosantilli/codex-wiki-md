# Redshift-space distortions

↑ **Parent:** [Galaxy survey](galaxy-survey.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Redshift-space_distortions)

Redshift-space distortions arise because observed galaxy redshifts include [peculiar velocity](peculiar-velocity.md) as well as [cosmological redshift](cosmological-redshift.md). Distances inferred solely from expansion therefore distort clustering along the [line of sight](line-of-sight.md). Coherent large-scale motion and small-scale velocity dispersion produce different effects on the inferred [matter power spectrum](matter-power-spectrum.md).
