# Critical quadratic potential for the Ornstein-Uhlenbeck generator

↑ **Parent:** [Feynman-Kac formula](feynman-kac-formula.md)

For $\mathcal L=\tfrac12\Delta-\lambda x\cdot\nabla$ and potential $V=\lambda^2|x|^2/2$, the [Feynman-Kac formula](feynman-kac-formula.md) with initial value one gives $u=\mathbb E_x^{OU}e^{\int_0^tV(X_s)ds}$. The [Ornstein-Uhlenbeck likelihood relative to Wiener measure](ornstein-uhlenbeck-likelihood-relative-to-wiener-measure.md) cancels the quadratic time integral, leaving a Gaussian endpoint integral. Completing the square gives the displayed answer for $1+\lambda t>0$. It is finite for all nonnegative times when $\lambda\ge0$; when $\lambda<0$ the path expectation diverges at and beyond $t=-1/\lambda$.
