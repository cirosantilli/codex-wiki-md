# Post problem

↑ **Parent:** [Turing degree](turing-degree.md)

Post problem asks whether a [computably enumerable set](recursively-enumerable-set.md) can have a [Turing degree](turing-degree.md) strictly between the computable degree and the degree of the [halting problem](halting-problem.md). The [Friedberg–Muchnik theorem](friedberg-muchnik-theorem.md) answers yes by constructing two incomparable computably enumerable degrees.
