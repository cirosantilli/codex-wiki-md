# Negative binomial series

↑ **Parent:** [Binomial series](binomial-series.md)

For a nonnegative integer $L$ and $|z|<1$, this specialization of the [binomial series](binomial-series.md) sums the normalizing coefficients of a [negative binomial distribution](negative-binomial-distribution.md).
