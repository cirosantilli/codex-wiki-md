# Extremal length lower bound from a closed one-form

↑ **Parent:** [Extremal length](extremal-length.md)

Let $\alpha$ be a real [closed differential form](closed-differential-form.md) on a [Riemann surface](riemann-surfaces.md), of finite positive conformal energy $E=\int|\alpha|^2\,dA$. If its period on a loop [homotopy class](homotopy-class.md) $\gamma$ is $p$, then $\lambda(\gamma,X)\geq p^2/E$. Indeed the [conformal metric](conformal-metric.md) $\rho=|\alpha|$ has area $E$ and each loop in the class has length at least $|\int\alpha|=|p|$. Energy is independent of the auxiliary [smooth](smooth-function.md) [conformal metric](conformal-metric.md) used to compute the pointwise norm: the inverse scaling of the squared norm cancels the area scaling.
