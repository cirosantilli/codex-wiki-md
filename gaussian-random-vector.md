# Gaussian random vector

↑ **Parent:** [Multivariate normal distribution](multivariate-normal-distribution.md)

A Gaussian random vector has a [multivariate normal distribution](multivariate-normal-distribution.md); equivalently, every real [linear combination](linear-combination.md) of its coordinates is a [normal random variable](gaussian-random-variable.md), allowing zero [variance](variance-split.md). Its [expected value](expected-value.md) vector and [covariance matrix](covariance-matrix.md) determine its law. Subvectors are independent when their cross-covariances vanish, a feature particular to joint Gaussianity.
