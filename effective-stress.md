# Effective stress

↑ **Parent:** [Stress](stress.md)

The load carried by the solid skeleton or contacts after allowing for [pore fluid pressure](pore-fluid-pressure.md). In compression-positive form an elementary law is $\boldsymbol\sigma'=\boldsymbol\sigma^c-\alpha P\mathbf I$; the contact approximation often takes $\alpha=1$. Fixed-total-stress pressure changes translate a [Mohr circle](mohr-s-circle.md) without changing its radius.
