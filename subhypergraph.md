# Subhypergraph

↑ **Parent:** [Hypergraph](hypergraph-split.md)

A subhypergraph restricts the [vertex set](vertex-set.md) of a [hypergraph](hypergraph-split.md) and retains some of its edges contained in that vertex set. Retaining every such edge gives an [induced subhypergraph](induced-subhypergraph.md).
