# Torelli theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Jacobian variety](jacobian-variety.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torelli_theorem)

A [smooth projective curve](smooth-projective-curve.md) over the [complex numbers](complex-number.md) is determined up to isomorphism by its [Jacobian variety](jacobian-variety.md) together with its canonical principal [polarization of a complex torus](polarization-of-a-complex-torus.md). For genus at least three, the [branch locus of the theta Gauss map](branch-locus-of-the-theta-gauss-map.md) recovers the canonical curve by [projective biduality theorem](projective-biduality-theorem.md), or recovers the canonical [rational normal curve](rational-normal-curve.md) and the branch points of a [hyperelliptic curve](hyperelliptic-curve.md). In genus two the [theta divisor](theta-divisor.md) itself is the curve; in genus one the [Jacobian variety](jacobian-variety.md) is the curve after choosing an origin. The polarization is essential data in this statement.
