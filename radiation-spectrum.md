# Radiation spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Electromagnetic radiation](electromagnetic-radiation-split.md)

The distribution of emitted or received electromagnetic [power](power.md), [radiative flux](radiative-flux.md) or [specific intensity](spectral-radiance.md) across [frequency](frequency.md) or [wavelength](wavelength.md). The spectral quantity and interval convention must be specified: $F_\nu$ and $F_\lambda$ differ by the change-of-variable factor, while $\nu F_\nu$ gives energy flux per logarithmic frequency interval. This physical spectrum is distinct from the spectrum of a linear operator or a spectrum in topology.
