# One-dimensional rational characters of the general linear group

↑ **Parent:** [Determinant twist](determinant-twist.md)

Every one-dimensional rational $GL_m$ representation is $\det^r$ for one integer $r$. Its restriction to the diagonal torus is a [Laurent monomial](laurent-monomial.md). Invariance under conjugation by permutation matrices forces all its exponents to agree, and density of [diagonalizable](diagonalizable-matrix.md) invertible matrices gives the result on the whole group. For $GL_1$, the [character](character-of-a-representation.md) identity $p(zw)=p(z)p(w)$ directly forces a [Laurent polynomial](laurent-polynomial.md) to be one monomial with coefficient one.
