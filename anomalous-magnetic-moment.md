# Anomalous magnetic moment

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic dipole moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anomalous_magnetic_moment)

For a charged [spin one-half](spin-one-half.md) particle with the [Dirac equation](dirac-equation.md) reference value $g=2$, the [anomalous magnetic moment](anomalous-magnetic-moment.md) is conventionally expressed by the dimensionless quantity $a=(g-2)/2$. For the [electron](electron.md), write $a_e=(g_s-2)/2$. A stipulated $g_s=2.0024$ would therefore mean $a_e=0.0012$: a $0.12\%$ excess of the spin-component moment over one [Bohr magneton](bohr-magneton.md). This is a teaching input, not a quoted measurement.
