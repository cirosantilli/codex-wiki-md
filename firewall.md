# Firewall

↑ **Parent:** [Middlebox](middlebox.md)

A [firewall](firewall.md) enforces a communication policy, for example by filtering [packets](network-packet.md) or tracking permitted exchanges. Blocking unwanted incoming traffic is a policy decision; it is not the definition of [network address translation](network-address-translation.md).
