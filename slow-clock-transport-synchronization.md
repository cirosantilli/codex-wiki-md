# Slow clock transport synchronization

↑ **Parent:** [Einstein synchronisation](einstein-synchronisation.md)

For ideal [clocks](clock.md) in an [inertial frame](inertial-frame.md) separated by fixed [distance](distance.md) $L$, transporting a clock at small [speed](speed.md) $u$ produces lag $\delta=(L/u)(1-\sqrt{1-u^2/c^2})\sim Lu/(2c^2)$. Hence the limit agrees with [Einstein synchronisation](einstein-synchronisation.md), assuming the [clock hypothesis](clock-hypothesis.md). A relabelling $t_\kappa=t+\kappa x/c$ retains an endpoint offset $\kappa L/c$, so the result does not remove the choice of distant-time convention.
