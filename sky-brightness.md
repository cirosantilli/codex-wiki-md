# Sky brightness

↑ **Parent:** [Observational astronomy](observational-astronomy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sky_brightness)

Sky brightness is the diffuse radiance received away from an astronomical target. Ground-based observations include atmospheric airglow, scattered light, and [thermal radiation](thermal-radiation.md); extraterrestrial diffuse light also contributes. Its photon fluctuations contribute to the [signal-to-noise ratio in photon counting](signal-to-noise-ratio-in-photon-counting.md).
