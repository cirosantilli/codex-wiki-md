# Cartan theorem B

↑ **Parent:** [Stein manifold](stein-manifold.md)

On a [Stein manifold](stein-manifold.md) $X$, every [coherent analytic sheaf](coherent-analytic-sheaf.md) $\mathcal F$ has $H^q(X,\mathcal F)=0$ for $q>0$. In particular this applies to the [sheaf of holomorphic functions](structure-sheaf-of-a-complex-manifold.md). If every nonempty finite intersection in an [open cover](open-cover.md) is Stein, these vanishing results and the [acyclic cover theorem](leray-s-theorem.md) compute the corresponding [sheaf cohomology](sheaf-cohomology.md) from its [Čech cochain complex](cech-cochain-complex.md).
