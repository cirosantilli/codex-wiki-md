# Association constant

↑ **Parent:** [Equilibrium constant](equilibrium-constant.md)

For $A+B\rightleftharpoons AB$, a concentration [association constant](association-constant.md) is $K_a=[AB]/([A][B])$. Comparisons require matched solvent, temperature and standard-state conventions.
