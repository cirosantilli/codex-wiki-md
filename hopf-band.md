# Hopf band

↑ **Parent:** [Seifert surface](seifert-surface.md)

A Hopf band is an annulus with one full twist whose boundary is a [Hopf link](hopf-link.md). Plumbing it with another band adds a generator to the first [homology](homology-split.md) of a [Seifert surface](seifert-surface.md). Its core has self-[linking number](linking-number.md) $1$ or $-1$, depending on the twisting convention.
