# Second spherical moment integral

↑ **Parent:** [Surface integral](surface-integral.md)

For the outward unit normal on a three-dimensional [sphere](sphere.md), the off-diagonal integrals vanish by [reflection symmetry](reflection-symmetry.md). The diagonal integrals are equal by [rotational symmetry](rotational-symmetry.md), and their sum is $\int_{S^2}|\mathbf n|^2d\Omega=4\pi$. This proves the displayed [matrix](matrix.md) identity, where $\delta_{ij}$ is the [Kronecker delta](kronecker-delta.md). Contracting it with a constant [vector](vector.md) gives $\int_{S^2}(\mathbf U\cdot\mathbf n)\mathbf n\,d\Omega=(4\pi/3)\mathbf U$, useful for integrating far-field [stress](stress.md) forces.
