# O-linked glycosylation

↑ **Parent:** [Protein glycosylation](protein-glycosylation.md)

[O-linked glycosylation](o-linked-glycosylation.md) attaches a sugar through an oxygen atom on the acceptor, often a [protein](protein-split.md) hydroxyl group. Several different pathways and donor specificities fall under this description.
