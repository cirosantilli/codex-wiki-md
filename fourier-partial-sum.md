# Fourier partial sum

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md)

A [Fourier partial sum](fourier-partial-sum.md) retains the modes whose [integer](integer.md) frequency multipliers satisfy $|k|\leq n$ in a [complex Fourier series](complex-fourier-series.md):

$$
S_n f(x)=\sum_{k=-n}^{n}\widehat f_k e^{ikx}.
$$

For a real [function](function-split.md) of period $2\pi$, this is equivalently $a_0/2+\sum_{k=1}^{n}[a_k\cos(kx)+b_k\sin(kx)]$. It is a [trigonometric polynomial](trigonometric-polynomial.md), even when the original [function](function-split.md) is discontinuous. For a [square wave](square-wave.md), retaining $M$ nonzero odd sine modes gives $S_{2M-1}f$; all even-frequency coefficients are zero. The [Gibbs phenomenon](gibbs-phenomenon.md) illustrates why more retained modes need not reduce the maximum error.
