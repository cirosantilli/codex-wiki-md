# Transverse oscillation of the middle of three parallel currents

↑ **Parent:** [Simple harmonic motion](simple-harmonic-motion.md)

Two fixed outer wires at $x=\pm d$ attract a middle wire carrying the same [electric current](electric-current.md) $I$. If the middle wire is displaced in the perpendicular direction $y$, the net magnetic [force](force-split.md) per unit length is $-\mu_0I^2y/[\pi(d^2+y^2)]$. For $|y|\ll d$, the wire's linear [mass density](density.md) $\mu_m$ gives the displayed [simple harmonic motion](simple-harmonic-motion.md) [frequency](frequency.md). Motion towards either outer wire in the original plane is unstable. Gravity must be absent from or balanced in the oscillation direction.
