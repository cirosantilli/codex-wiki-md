# Absolute-vorticity flux tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Absolute vorticity](absolute-vorticity.md)

For a flow satisfying [incompressibility](incompressible-flow.md), this antisymmetric tensor writes advection and [vortex stretching](vortex-stretching.md) in conservative form. With its first index as transport direction, $(\nabla\cdot T)_j=\partial_iT_{ij}$.
