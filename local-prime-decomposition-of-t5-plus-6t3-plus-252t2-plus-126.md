# Local prime decomposition of T5 plus 6T3 plus 252T2 plus 126

↑ **Parent:** [Local factorization and extended absolute values](local-factorization-and-extended-absolute-values.md)

This polynomial is [Eisenstein](eisenstein-criterion.md) at $2$. Over $\mathbb Q_3$, its [Newton polygon](newton-polygon.md) has a single slope $-2/5$, so it is irreducible and a root generates a totally ramified extension of degree five. Over $\mathbb Q_7$, its factor degrees are $1,1,3$: the simple residue roots $1,-1$ lift, and the remaining roots have valuation $1/3$. Thus the degree-five [number field](number-field.md) generated by a root has one prime above $3$ and three above $7$.
