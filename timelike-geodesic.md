# Timelike geodesic

↑ **Parent:** [General relativity](general-relativity-split.md)

A [timelike geodesic](timelike-geodesic.md) has timelike tangent and vanishing covariant acceleration in an [affine parameter](affine-parameter.md). Choosing [proper time](proper-time.md) gives $g(\dot\gamma,\dot\gamma)=-1$.
