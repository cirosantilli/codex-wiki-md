# Gravity-restored centrifugal governor bifurcation

↑ **Parent:** [Centrifugal governor](centrifugal-governor.md)

For total ball mass $m$, arm length $l$ and imposed constant shaft speed $\Omega$, the reduced effective potential is $V_{\rm eff}/(ml^2)=-\Omega^2\sin^2\theta/2-\omega_0^2\cos\theta$. Its equilibrium equation is $\sin\theta(\omega_0^2-\Omega^2\cos\theta)=0$. The downward state is stable for $|\Omega|<\omega_0$ and loses linear stability above it; nonzero stable openings then satisfy the displayed relation. Extending the angle to signed values gives a [supercritical pitchfork bifurcation](supercritical-pitchfork-bifurcation.md). Squared [angular frequencies](angular-frequency.md) of [small oscillations](small-oscillation.md) are $\omega_0^2-\Omega^2$ at the downward state and $\Omega^2-\omega_0^4/\Omega^2$ at the open states. At threshold the quadratic term vanishes but the quartic potential is positive, so zero linear frequency does not imply finite-amplitude free motion.
