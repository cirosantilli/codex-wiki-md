# Spatial frequency

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier analysis](fourier-analysis-split.md)

[Spatial frequency](spatial-frequency.md) is the number of oscillation cycles per unit length. A ripple with spatial period $\Lambda$ has [frequency](frequency.md) $\nu=1/\Lambda$; its angular wavenumber is $k=2\pi\nu$. In two dimensions the spatial-frequency vector specifies the orientation and period of a plane-wave pattern.
