# Completeness of Lp spaces

↑ **Parent:** [Lp space](lp-space.md)

From an [Lp norm](lp-norm.md) Cauchy sequence choose a subsequence with successive difference norms at most $2^{-j}$. [Minkowski inequality](minkowski-inequality.md) and [monotone convergence theorem](monotone-convergence-theorem.md) make the sum of the absolute differences an $L^p$ function, hence finite almost everywhere. The telescoping series defines an $L^p$ limit; the geometric bound on its tail proves norm convergence of the subsequence, and the original Cauchy property proves convergence of the whole sequence. No sigma-finiteness assumption is needed.
