# Boolean topos

↑ **Parent:** [Elementary topos](elementary-topos.md)

A Boolean topos is an elementary topos in which every subobject has a complement, equivalently its internal truth-value [Heyting algebra](heyting-algebra.md) satisfies excluded middle. Its internal first-order logic is classical. Being Boolean alone does not imply that it has enough set-valued points.
