# Darcy velocity

↑ **Parent:** [Darcy law](darcy-law.md)

The Darcy velocity is liquid [volume flux](volumetric-flow-rate.md) relative to the solid skeleton, divided by total cross-sectional area. If [porosity](porosity.md) is $\epsilon$, liquid velocity is $\mathbf v_l$, and skeleton velocity is $\mathbf v_s$, then $\mathbf u=\epsilon(\mathbf v_l-\mathbf v_s)$. Thus pore-fluid speed relative to the skeleton is $\mathbf u/\epsilon$, rather than $\mathbf u$.
