# Radioactive decay

↑ **Parent:** [Physics](physics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radioactive_decay)

A population of unstable nuclei with decay constant $\lambda>0$ obeys $N'=-\lambda N$ and therefore decays as $N(t)=N(0)e^{-\lambda t}$.
