# Van der Waals gas

↑ **Parent:** [Van der Waals equation](van-der-waals-equation.md)

A mean-field fluid model with excluded-volume and attractive-interaction parameters, described by the [Van der Waals equation](van-der-waals-equation.md). Its pressure law alone does not fix every thermal property: the [caloric closure of the van der Waals equation](caloric-closure-of-the-van-der-waals-equation.md) supplies the additional internal-energy assumption.
