# Low-temperature thermal phonon number

↑ **Parent:** [Debye model](debye-model.md)

Integrating the [Bose-Einstein distribution](bose-einstein-distribution.md) against the three-dimensional [Debye model](debye-model.md) [phonon density of states](phonon-density-of-states.md) gives $N_{\mathrm{ph}}=9N(T/\Theta_D)^3\int_0^{\Theta_D/T}x^2/(e^x-1)\,dx$. For $T\ll\Theta_D$, expand $(e^x-1)^{-1}=\sum_{j\geq1}e^{-jx}$ and integrate termwise: the limiting integral is $2\sum_{j\geq1}j^{-3}=2\zeta(3)$. This counts thermal quanta, not available modes or half quanta of [zero-point energy](zero-point-energy.md).
