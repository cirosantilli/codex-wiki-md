<h1 id="ramond-ramond-sector">Ramond–Ramond sector</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Type II string theory](type-ii-string-theory.md)

The closed-string sector with [Ramond sector](ramond-sector.md) states on both chiral sides. The left and right spinor tensor product describes differential-form gauge fields. Opposite chiralities in [type IIA superstring theory](type-iia-superstring-theory.md) give the massless one-form and three-form, with transverse dimensions $8$ and $56$. Same chiralities instead give the type IIB form-field content.
