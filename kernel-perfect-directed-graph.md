# Kernel-perfect directed graph

↑ **Parent:** [Directed graph](directed-graph.md)

A [digraph kernel](kernel-of-a-directed-graph.md) is an [independent set](independent-set-graph-theory.md) $K$ of [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) such that each vertex outside $K$ has a [directed edge](directed-edge.md) from itself to a vertex in $K$. A [directed graph](directed-graph.md) is kernel-perfect if every [induced subgraph](induced-subgraph.md) has a [digraph kernel](kernel-of-a-directed-graph.md). Preference orientations of the [line graph](line-graph.md) of a [bipartite graph](bipartite-graph.md) are kernel-perfect: a [stable matching in a bipartite graph](stable-matching-in-a-bipartite-graph.md) is independent in the line graph, and stability supplies the required outgoing edge at every unmatched edge.
