# Strict local minimum

↑ **Parent:** [Local minimum](local-minimum.md)

A [local minimum](local-minimum.md) is strict when every other point of the [domain of a function](domain-of-a-function.md) in some [neighbourhood](neighbourhood-mathematics.md) has a larger value. Strictness is local: two separated [strict local minima](strict-local-minimum.md) can have the same value, as $x^4-2x^2$ at $\pm1$ demonstrates. Negating the [function](function-split.md) gives a [strict local maximum](strict-local-maximum.md).
