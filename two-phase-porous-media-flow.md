# Two-phase porous-media flow

↑ **Parent:** [Porous-media flow](porous-media-flow-split.md)

Immiscible fluids share pore space, with each phase's [Darcy flux](darcy-velocity.md) determined by its pressure, viscosity and [relative permeability](relative-permeability.md). [Fluid saturation](fluid-saturation.md) specifies how much pore space each phase occupies. A [capillary pressure](capillary-pressure.md) couples their pressures, while their individual [mass conservation](mass-conservation.md) laws determine displacement and saturation evolution.
