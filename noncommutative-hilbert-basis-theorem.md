# Noncommutative Hilbert basis theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Right Noetherian ring](right-noetherian-ring.md)

If an [algebra](algebra-split.md) $B$ is generated by a subalgebra $A$ that is a [right Noetherian ring](right-noetherian-ring.md) and one element $x$, and $A+xA=A+Ax$, then $B$ is a [right Noetherian ring](right-noetherian-ring.md). The proof uses $F_n=\sum_{i\leq n}Ax^i=\sum_{i\leq n}x^iA$ and, for each [right ideal](right-ideal.md) $I$, the ascending [right ideals](right-ideal.md) $L_n=\{a:ax^n\in I+F_{n-1}\}$. After these stabilize, finitely many lifts of generators for $L_0,\ldots,L_N$ generate $I$ by induction on degree. Neither unique normal forms nor an [automorphism](automorphism.md) moving coefficients is required.
