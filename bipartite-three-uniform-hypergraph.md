# Bipartite three-uniform hypergraph

↑ **Parent:** [Property B](property-b.md)

A three-[uniform hypergraph](uniform-hypergraph.md) is bipartite when its vertex set has a partition into two parts such that every [hyperedge](hyperedge.md) meets both parts. All such triples on parts of sizes $a,b$ number $a\binom b2+b\binom a2=ab(a+b-2)/2$. For fixed total order this is maximized by balanced parts.
