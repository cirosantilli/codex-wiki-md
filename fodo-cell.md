# FODO cell

↑ **Parent:** [Beam optics](beam-optics.md)

A repeating focusing-drift-defocusing-drift lattice cell, with opposite quadrupole focusing in the two transverse planes. Stability and [phase](phase-waves.md) advance depend on focal strengths and separations.
