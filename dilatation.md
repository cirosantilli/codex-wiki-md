# Dilatation

↑ **Parent:** [Scale invariance](scale-invariance.md)

A simultaneous rescaling of spacetime coordinates and a field with specified weight $\Delta$. Its infinitesimal variation at a fixed argument is $\bar\delta\phi=\alpha(\Delta\phi+x^\mu\partial_\mu\phi)$. For a canonical real [scalar field](scalar-field.md) in $D$ spacetime dimensions, $\Delta=(D-2)/2$ keeps the kinetic [action](action.md) invariant. Prescribing $\Delta=1$ therefore restricts kinetic invariance to $D=4$. In four dimensions the classical massless quartic [scalar field theory](scalar-field-theory-split.md) has a conserved [dilatation current](dilatation-current.md) $j^\mu=\phi\partial^\mu\phi+x_\nu T^{\mu\nu}$.
