# Discrete dividend budget equation

↑ **Parent:** [Budget constraint](budget-constraint.md)

When a productive [stock](stock.md) pays $d_t$ before trading at ex-dividend price $S_t$, an investor entering with $\theta_t$ shares receives $d_t\theta_t$ and sells or buys shares to leave with $\theta_{t+1}$. The displayed [budget constraint](budget-constraint.md) accounts for the available [consumption](consumption.md) and the cost of the new holding. Here $\theta_t$ is known before date $t$, while $C_t$ and $\theta_{t+1}$ may depend on the information revealed at that date.
