# Spin-valve read-head noise

↑ **Parent:** [Spin valve](spin-valve.md)

Fluctuations that limit the detectable field of a magnetoresistive read sensor. Thermal [magnetization](magnetization.md) noise, domain jumps, electronic [thermal noise](thermal-noise.md) and resistance fluctuations compete with the field-dependent signal; magnetic bias and adequate [anisotropy](anisotropy.md) stability are important.
