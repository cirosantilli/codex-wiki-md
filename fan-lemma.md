# Fan lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Set version of Menger theorem](set-version-of-menger-theorem.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fan_lemma)

In a $k$-connected graph, for a vertex $x$ and a set $Y$ of at least $k$ other vertices, there are $k$ paths from $x$ to distinct vertices of $Y$ that meet only at $x$. This follows from the [Set version of Menger theorem](set-version-of-menger-theorem.md) by separating $x$ from $Y$.
