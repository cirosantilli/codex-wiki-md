# Strong epimorphism in the category of small categories

↑ **Parent:** [Strong epimorphism](strong-epimorphism.md)

A functor between small categories is a strong epimorphism precisely when its image generates its codomain as a subcategory. It is a [regular epimorphism](regular-epimorphism.md) precisely when it is surjective on objects and full. Hence a functor can be strong without being regular when its image arrows generate additional composites that have no single preimage.
