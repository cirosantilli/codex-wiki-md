# Local time (mathematics)

↑ **Parent:** [Stochastic calculus](stochastic-calculus-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_time_(mathematics))

Local time records how much a [stochastic process](stochastic-process-split.md) visits a level. Its normalization depends on the process: when the [state space](state-space.md) is a [discrete space](discrete-space.md), it is occupation time at a state, while [local time of a semimartingale](local-time-of-a-semimartingale.md) is an occupation density weighted by [quadratic variation](quadratic-variation.md). [Brownian local time](brownian-local-time.md) is a special case.
