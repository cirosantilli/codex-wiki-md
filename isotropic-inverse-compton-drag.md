# Isotropic inverse-Compton drag

↑ **Parent:** [Inverse Compton scattering](inverse-compton-scattering.md)

An isotropic photon bath with total [radiation energy density](radiation-energy-density.md) $u_{\rm ph}$ exerts this drag on an electron in the [Thomson scattering](thomson-scattering.md) regime. Its [stress-energy tensor](stress-energy-tensor-split.md) has spatial diagonal entries $u_{\rm ph}/3$. A boost to the electron frame gives radiation [energy flux](energy-flux.md) $S'_\parallel=-(4/3)c\gamma^2\beta u_{\rm ph}$; multiplying its radiation force $\sigma_TS'_\parallel/c$ by the laboratory speed gives the electron's energy loss. This is net energy transferred, not all scattered photon energy. If only the magnetic wave-energy density is used, it is half of $u_{\rm ph}$ and the numerical coefficient doubles.
