# Photon pile-up

↑ **Parent:** [Photodetector](photodetector.md)

Two or more [photons](photon.md) detected within one unresolved spatial and temporal event cell can be registered as one event, have their energies combined, or be rejected by event grading. In an X-ray [CCD](charge-coupled-device.md), low mean occupancy per event island per frame is needed for reliable individual-photon [spectroscopy](spectroscopy.md). Reducing frame time or spreading the image can reduce this effect.
