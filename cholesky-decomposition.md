# Cholesky decomposition

↑ **Parent:** [Positive-definite matrix](positive-definite-matrix.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cholesky_decomposition)

Every real symmetric [positive-definite matrix](positive-definite-matrix.md) has a unique factorization $A=LL^T$ in which $L$ is a lower [triangular matrix](triangular-matrix.md) with positive diagonal entries.
