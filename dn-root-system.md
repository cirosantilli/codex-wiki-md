# Dn root system

↑ **Parent:** [Special orthogonal Lie algebra](special-orthogonal-lie-algebra.md)

For the complexified [Special orthogonal Lie algebra](special-orthogonal-lie-algebra.md) $\mathfrak{so}_{2n}$, $n\geq2$, choose positive [roots of a root system](root-of-a-root-system.md) $e_i-e_j$ and $e_i+e_j$ for $i<j$. Its [Weyl vector](half-sum-of-positive-roots.md) is $\rho=(n-1,n-2,\ldots,0)$. Dominant coordinates satisfy $\lambda_1\geq\cdots\geq\lambda_{n-1}\geq|\lambda_n|$. The [Weyl dimension formula](weyl-dimension-formula.md) becomes $\prod_{i<j}((\lambda_i+\rho_i)^2-(\lambda_j+\rho_j)^2)/(\rho_i^2-\rho_j^2)$. The case $D_2$ is two independent [A1 root systems](rank-one-root-system.md), while $D_3$ is isomorphic to the [A3 root system](a3-root-system.md).
