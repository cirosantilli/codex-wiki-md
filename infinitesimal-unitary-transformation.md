# Infinitesimal unitary transformation

↑ **Parent:** [Unitary operator](unitary-operator.md)

Differentiating a [unitary operator](unitary-operator.md) family at the identity gives an anti-Hermitian tangent. In the convention $U(\epsilon)=I+i\epsilon F+O(\epsilon^2)$ for real $\epsilon$, the generator $F$ is [Hermitian](hermitian-operator.md). This is the one-parameter version of the [Hermitian quantum generator convention](hermitian-quantum-generator-convention.md). A finite linear truncation need not itself be exactly unitary.
