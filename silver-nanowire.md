# Silver nanowire

↑ **Parent:** [Nanowire](nanowire.md)

A high-aspect-ratio [silver](silver.md) structure with nanoscale cross section. Low intrinsic metal resistivity and sparse network connectivity make such wires useful for flexible transparent conductors, but [nanowire junction resistance](nanowire-junction-resistance.md), insulating [ligands](ligand-biochemistry.md), tarnish and mechanical connectivity can dominate device performance.
