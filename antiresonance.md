# Antiresonance

↑ **Parent:** [Frequency response](frequency-response.md)

An [antiresonance](antiresonance.md) is a suppression of a specified measured response through cancellation of contributions, often between [normal modes](normal-mode.md). It does not generally mean that the entire system is motionless. In an ideal undamped two-oscillator system, the directly driven oscillator can have zero displacement while its partner still moves.
