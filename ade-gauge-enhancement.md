# ADE gauge enhancement

↑ **Parent:** [M-theory K3 duality](m-theory-k3-duality.md)

When appropriate negative-square two-cycles of a [K3 surface](k3-surface.md) collapse, wrapped [M2-branes](m2-brane.md) become massless. Their intersection pattern is an ADE root diagram, and the massless charged states enlarge the abelian gauge algebra to the corresponding simply laced [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md).
