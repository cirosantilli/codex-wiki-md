# Reflected impedance

↑ **Parent:** [Transformer (electrical engineering)](transformer-electrical-engineering.md)

The [reflected impedance](reflected-impedance.md) is the contribution of a coupled secondary load to a primary winding's [input impedance](input-impedance.md). For two ideal mutually coupled [inductors](inductor.md), choose winding signs so that positive [electric currents](electric-current.md) reinforce their [magnetic fluxes](magnetic-flux.md). If the secondary loop has [complex impedance](electrical-impedance.md) $Z_s$, its [Kirchhoff's voltage law](kirchhoff-cycle-law.md) gives $\widetilde I_s=-i\omega M\widetilde I_p/Z_s$. Substitution in $\widetilde V_p=i\omega L_p\widetilde I_p+i\omega M\widetilde I_s$ gives $Z_{\mathrm{in}}=i\omega L_p+\omega^2M^2/Z_s$. For $Z_s=R+i\omega L_s$, the reflected real part is $\omega^2M^2R/[R^2+(\omega L_s)^2]$, and primary mean input [power](power.md) equals secondary mean [Joule heating](joule-heating.md).
