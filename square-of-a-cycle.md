# Square of a cycle

↑ **Parent:** [Graph power](graph-power.md)

In a cycle square, each triple of consecutive [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) is a [clique](clique-graph-theory.md). A proper three-colouring therefore repeats with period three and closes exactly when the length is divisible by three. For all other cycle lengths at least four colours are needed; the five-cycle square is a complete five-vertex [graph](graph-split.md).
