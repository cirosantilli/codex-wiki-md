# Cauchy stress tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Stress](stress.md)

The stress tensor maps a surface normal to the [traction](traction.md) exerted across that surface. Its divergence is the force density in local momentum balance. For an incompressible Newtonian fluid it combines pressure with twice the viscosity times the symmetric strain-rate tensor.
