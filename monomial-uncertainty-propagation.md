# Monomial uncertainty propagation

↑ **Parent:** [Propagation of uncertainty](propagation-of-uncertainty.md)

For positive measured inputs and $f=C\prod_i x_i^{a_i}$, [logarithmic differentiation](logarithmic-differentiation.md) gives $df/f=\sum_i a_i\,dx_i/x_i$. Applying [propagation of uncertainty](propagation-of-uncertainty.md) yields the displayed [covariance](covariance.md) formula. For [independent](independent-random-variables.md) input errors it reduces to $\sigma_f/|f|\simeq[\sum_i(a_i\sigma_i/x_i)^2]^{1/2}$. If the input uncertainties are strict bounds rather than [standard deviations](standard-deviation.md), a first-order conservative bound is $|\Delta f|/|f|\lesssim\sum_i|a_i|\Delta x_i/|x_i|$. These are different statistical interpretations; a product formula alone does not choose between them.
