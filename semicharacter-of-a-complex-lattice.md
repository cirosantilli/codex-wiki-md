# Semicharacter of a complex lattice

↑ **Parent:** [Appell–Humbert theorem](appell-humbert-theorem.md)

For an integral alternating form $E$ arising as the imaginary part of a [Hermitian form](hermitian-form.md), a semicharacter satisfies $\alpha(\lambda+\mu)=e^{\pi iE(\lambda,\mu)}\alpha(\lambda)\alpha(\mu)$. The [Appell–Humbert theorem](appell-humbert-theorem.md) uses this datum together with the [Hermitian form](hermitian-form.md) to specify a [holomorphic line bundle](holomorphic-line-bundle.md) on a [complex torus](complex-torus.md). The ratio of two semicharacters is an ordinary unitary [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md). On the square [period lattice](period-lattice.md), with $H(z,w)=z\overline w$, all semicharacters are $\alpha(m+ni)=(-1)^{mn}u^m v^n$ for $u,v\in U(1)$.
