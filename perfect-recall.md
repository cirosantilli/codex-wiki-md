# Perfect recall

↑ **Parent:** [Game theory](game-theory-split.md)

A player has perfect recall when the [information sets](information-set.md) never erase that player's previously available information or earlier chosen actions. With perfect recall, randomizing once over complete [pure strategies](pure-strategy.md) and using suitable independent randomizations at information sets can induce the same outcome distributions.

Without perfect recall, one [information set](information-set.md) may be encountered twice on the same play path. A [behavioural strategy](behavioural-strategy.md) then uses the same probabilities with fresh draws at both encounters; its outcome distribution need not be attainable by any [mixed strategy](mixed-strategy.md) over deterministic plans.
