# Inhibitory postsynaptic potential

↑ **Parent:** [Synaptic potential](synaptic-potential.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inhibitory_postsynaptic_potential)

An [inhibitory postsynaptic potential](inhibitory-postsynaptic-potential.md) is a [synaptic potential](synaptic-potential.md) associated with decreased likelihood of a postsynaptic [action potential](action-potential.md). It can involve [hyperpolarization](hyperpolarization.md) or [depolarization](depolarization.md); an inhibitory conductance can even produce no measurable voltage change at rest. See [synaptic potential](synaptic-potential.md) and [shunting inhibition](shunting-inhibition.md) for worked examples and sources.
