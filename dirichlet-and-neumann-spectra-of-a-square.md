# Dirichlet and Neumann spectra of a square

↑ **Parent:** [Helmholtz equation](helmholtz-equation.md)

For $-\Delta\Phi=\lambda\Phi$ on the square, [separation of variables](separation-of-variables.md) gives [Dirichlet eigenfunctions](dirichlet-eigenfunction.md) $\sin(jx)\sin(ky)$ with $j,k\geq1$, and [Neumann eigenfunctions](neumann-eigenfunction.md) $\cos(jx)\cos(ky)$ with $j,k\geq0$. The [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) in both cases are $j^2+k^2$, with multiplicity equal to the number of ordered admissible pairs. In particular, an arbitrary expression $\lambda=m^2+p^2$ with integers $m,p$ need not be a [Dirichlet eigenvalue](dirichlet-eigenvalue.md) when one index is zero. The Neumann zero eigenvalue has the constant eigenfunction, whereas the Dirichlet zero problem has only the zero solution by [Dirichlet uniqueness for the Poisson equation](dirichlet-uniqueness-for-the-poisson-equation.md).
