# Orientable total space of the orientation line bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Orientation line bundle](orientation-line-bundle.md)

The total space of the [orientation line bundle](orientation-line-bundle.md) is orientable even when its base is not: an orientation reversal in a base coordinate transition is matched by a reversal in the line coordinate, so their product has positive sign. Fiberwise multiplication by $1-t$ gives a [deformation retraction](deformation-retraction.md) onto the zero section. For the real projective plane this total space is $(S^2\times\mathbb R)/((v,t)\sim(-v,-t))$, an oriented noncompact three-manifold homotopy equivalent to the plane.
