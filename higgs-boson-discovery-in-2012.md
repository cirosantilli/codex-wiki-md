# Higgs boson discovery in 2012

↑ **Parent:** [Higgs boson](higgs-boson.md)

The [ATLAS experiment](atlas-experiment.md) and [CMS experiment](compact-muon-solenoid.md) searched [proton](proton.md)-[proton](proton.md) collisions at the [Large Hadron Collider](large-hadron-collider.md) at collision [energies](energy-split.md) of $7$ and $8\,\mathrm{TeV}$. The [ATLAS experiment](atlas-experiment.md) used [silicon](silicon.md) tracking, a $2\,\mathrm T$ solenoid, liquid-argon [electromagnetic calorimeters](electromagnetic-calorimeter.md) and an external [muon](muon.md) spectrometer. Its July 2012 paper reported a combined local [statistical significance](statistical-significance.md) of $6.0$ [standard deviations](standard-deviation.md) near $126.5\,\mathrm{GeV}$; this paper result is distinct from the initial 4 July announcement.[abstract; Section 2; Section 9.2 and Table 7](observation-of-a-new-particle-in-the-search-for-the-standard-model-higgs-boson-with-the-atlas-detector-at-the-lhc-by-atlas-collaboration.md)

The [CMS experiment](compact-muon-solenoid.md) used [silicon](silicon.md) tracking in a $3.8\,\mathrm T$ solenoid, lead-tungstate [electromagnetic calorimeters](electromagnetic-calorimeter.md) and external [muon](muon.md) chambers. [Electrons](electron.md) combine tracks and [particle showers](particle-shower.md); [muons](muon.md) combine tracking and chamber hits; [photons](photon.md) supply shower [energies](energy-split.md) and directions. Selected $H\to\gamma\gamma$ and $H\to ZZ^*\to4\ell$ candidates, with $\ell=e,\mu$, yielded [invariant mass](invariant-mass.md) peaks. It checked lepton efficiencies with [Z boson](z-boson.md) decays, estimated fake-lepton backgrounds from control samples with relaxed identification, and fitted the smooth diphoton background from data. Its combined local [statistical significance](statistical-significance.md) was $5.0$ [standard deviations](standard-deviation.md) near $125\,\mathrm{GeV}$.[abstract; Sections 2, 5.1–5.2 and 7.1](observation-of-a-new-boson-at-a-mass-of-125-gev-with-the-cms-experiment-at-the-lhc-by-cms-collaboration.md)

The [ATLAS experiment](atlas-experiment.md) compared signal-plus-background and background-only hypotheses with a [profile likelihood](profile-likelihood.md), accounting for systematic uncertainties and correlations between [decay channels](decay-channel.md). Diphoton and four-lepton candidates gave precise [invariant masses](invariant-mass.md); $WW^*$ decays supplied additional evidence with poorer mass reconstruction because of escaping [neutrinos](neutrino.md). A local [p-value](p-value.md) concerns one specified mass; scanning masses requires a [look-elsewhere effect](look-elsewhere-effect.md) correction.[Sections 6–7 and 9.2](observation-of-a-new-particle-in-the-search-for-the-standard-model-higgs-boson-with-the-atlas-detector-at-the-lhc-by-atlas-collaboration.md)

Two-[photon](photon.md) decay excludes [spin](spin.md) one under the usual assumptions, but does not prove [spin](spin.md) zero. A mass peak does not determine every [Higgs boson](higgs-boson.md) coupling. Production rates, [branching fractions](branching-fraction.md) and angular correlations require additional tests.[Sections 7.1–7.3 and Summary](observation-of-a-new-boson-at-a-mass-of-125-gev-with-the-cms-experiment-at-the-lhc-by-cms-collaboration.md)
