# Energy expectation

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum measurement](quantum-measurement-split.md)

The first moment of the [energy measurement](energy-measurement.md) distribution. For discrete [energy eigenstates](energy-eigenstate.md) it is $\sum_n E_n|c_n|^2$ when finite; a normalizable state need not have a finite value.
