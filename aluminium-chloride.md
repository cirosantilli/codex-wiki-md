# Aluminium chloride

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical compound](chemical-compound.md)

The compound $\mathrm{AlCl_3}$. In an ideal fully dissociated aqueous-electrolyte bookkeeping model, formula concentration $c$ supplies $c$ of triply charged aluminium ions and $3c$ of singly charged chloride ions, giving [ionic strength](ionic-strength.md) $I=6c$. Hydrolysis, ion association and nonideal activities can make this simple model inadequate for concentrated real solutions.
