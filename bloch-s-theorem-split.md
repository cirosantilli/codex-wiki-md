# Bloch's theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum theory](quantum-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloch's_theorem)

In a periodic potential, energy eigenstates have the form $e^{ik\cdot x}u_k(x)$ with lattice-periodic $u_k$.
