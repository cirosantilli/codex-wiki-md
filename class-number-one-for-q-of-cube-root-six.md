# Class number one for Q of cube root six

↑ **Parent:** [Minkowski's bound](minkowski-s-bound.md)

For $\alpha^3=6$, the [ring of integers](ring-of-integers.md) is $\mathbb Z[\alpha]$ and its [field discriminant](field-discriminant.md) is $-972$. The [Minkowski bound for ideal classes](minkowski-s-bound.md) is $16\sqrt3/\pi<9$. Prime ideals of norm at most eight lie above $2,3,5,7$. The primes above two, three and the degree-one prime above five are generated by $\alpha-2$, $3+2\alpha+\alpha^2$ and $\alpha-1$, of norm magnitudes $2,3,5$. At seven the residue roots are $3,5,6$; the primes at roots six and three are generated by $\alpha+1$ and $\alpha^2+\alpha-5$, both of norm seven. Their product with the remaining prime is $(7)$, making that prime principal too. Every possible Minkowski representative is therefore principal.
