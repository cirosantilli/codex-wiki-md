# Exact quadratic basin boundary

↑ **Parent:** [Basin estimate from a Lyapunov sublevel set](basin-estimate-from-a-lyapunov-sublevel-set.md)

For $\dot z=Az+\|z\|^2z$, suppose a positive-definite [matrix](matrix.md) $P$ solves $A^TP+PA=-2I$. The quadratic function $V=z^TPz$ then obeys $\dot V=2\|z\|^2(V-1)$. Its unit ellipsoid separates decay toward the origin from escape. In a planar system with nonzero angular speed it is an invariant periodic orbit.
