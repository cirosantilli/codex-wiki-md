# State-dependent correlation investment problem

↑ **Parent:** [Investment-consumption problem](investment-consumption-problem.md)

When asset correlation varies with a traded market index, the index is both an asset and a state variable. Writing $m=\log M$ and optimizing [Brownian portfolio exposures](brownian-portfolio-exposures.md) accounts for the cross derivative between wealth and $m$. Power [homogeneity](homogeneity.md) reduces the [Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman equation](hamilton-jacobi-bellman-equation.md) to an ordinary differential equation in $m$.
