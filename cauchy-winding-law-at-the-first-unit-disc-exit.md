# Cauchy winding law at the first unit-disc exit

↑ **Parent:** [Planar Brownian motion](planar-brownian-motion.md)

For a start $Z_0=r_0e^{i\alpha_0}$ in the punctured [unit disc](unit-disc.md), the logarithmic lift $-i\log Z$ starts at $\alpha_0-i\log r_0$ in the [upper half-plane](upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md). Local [conformal invariance of planar Brownian motion](conformal-invariance-of-planar-brownian-motion.md) changes its clock by $\int|Z_s|^{-2}ds$. That clock is finite at the [disc](topological-disc.md) exit because the stopped path is [compact](compact-space.md) and avoids zero. Its terminal real coordinate is therefore the usual upper-half-plane [Cauchy exit law from a Brownian half-plane](cauchy-exit-law-from-a-brownian-half-plane.md). Subtracting $\alpha_0$ gives the displayed winding law. Its first absolute moment diverges logarithmically, even though the final point modulo angle lies on a [compact](compact-space.md) circle.
