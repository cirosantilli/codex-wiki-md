# Maxwellian beam threshold for a positive velocity slope

↑ **Parent:** [Landau damping](landau-damping.md)

A broad electron [Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution](maxwell-boltzmann-distribution.md) of width $\sigma_p$ plus a narrow beam of width $\sigma_b$ centred at speed $V$ can have a positive resonant velocity slope. The beam slope is largest at $V-\sigma_b$. Treating the background slope as constant over the beam width gives the displayed density threshold. Replacing the Maxwellian tail at $V-\sigma_b$ by its value at $V$ also requires $V\sigma_b/\sigma_p^2\ll1$; width separation alone does not ensure that exponentially sensitive approximation. A positive slope is the local resonant-drive criterion, while an exact unstable mode also requires the kinetic dispersion relation.
