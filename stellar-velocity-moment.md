# Stellar velocity moment

↑ **Parent:** [Galactic distribution function](galactic-distribution-function.md)

For a [galactic distribution function](galactic-distribution-function.md) $f(\mathbf x,\mathbf v)$ with tracer [number density](number-density.md) $\nu=\int f\,d^3v$, a velocity moment is the weighted [integral](integral.md) of a velocity monomial. The first moment gives the mean [velocity](velocity.md) $\overline v_i$; the second moment satisfies $\overline{v_iv_j}=\overline v_i\overline v_j+\sigma_{ij}^2$, separating ordered motion from the random [velocity dispersion](velocity-dispersion.md) tensor. Multiplying the [Collisionless Boltzmann equation](collisionless-boltzmann-equation.md) by $v_i$ and integrating yields the [Jeans equation](jeans-equation.md), with vanishing velocity-space boundary terms when the distribution decays sufficiently rapidly.
