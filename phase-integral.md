# Phase integral

↑ **Parent:** [Eikonal function](eikonal-function.md)

The accumulated phase along a one-dimensional ray. In an oscillatory [WKB approximation](wkb-approximation.md), the two travelling [waves](wave-split.md) have phases $\pm\int k\,dx$. In an evanescent region, the positive local decay rate $\kappa=\sqrt{-k^2}$ replaces $k$, producing exponents $\pm\int\kappa\,dx$.
