# Three-radialene

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrocarbon](hydrocarbon.md)

The [hydrocarbon](hydrocarbon.md) with a three-membered [carbon](carbon.md) ring and an exocyclic methylene [double bond](double-bond.md) at each vertex. The ideal planar structure belongs to the [D3h molecular point group](d3h-molecular-point-group.md).
