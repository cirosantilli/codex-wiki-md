# Change-of-type theory of disease

↑ **Parent:** [History of medicine](history-of-medicine.md)

A nineteenth-century explanation of changing therapeutic practice: inflammatory [diseases](disease.md) were said to have become less vigorous or more debilitating, requiring less depletion. It competed with explanations based on improved [pathology](pathology.md) and better evaluation of [treatment](treatment.md). This is a historical theory, not a general modern law. [pp. 1–3](the-bloodletting-controversy-1858-review.md)
