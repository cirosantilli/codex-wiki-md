# Equal superposition of the first two box states

↑ **Parent:** [Infinite square well](infinite-square-well.md)

For an [infinite square well](infinite-square-well.md) on $[0,L]$, choose $\phi_n=\sqrt{2/L}\sin(n\pi x/L)$ and $E_n=n^2E_1$, $E_1=\pi^2\hbar^2/(2mL^2)$. The state $\psi=(\phi_1e^{-iE_1t/\hbar}+\phi_2e^{-iE_2t/\hbar})/\sqrt2$ has constant [expectation value](expectation-value.md) $\langle E\rangle=5E_1/2$, but

$$
\langle x\rangle=\frac L2-\frac{16L}{9\pi^2}\cos\left(\frac{3E_1t}{\hbar}\right).
$$

Indeed, the diagonal position [matrix elements](matrix-element.md) are $L/2$, whereas the off-diagonal element is

$$
\langle1|x|2\rangle=\frac2L\int_0^L x\sin\frac{\pi x}L\sin\frac{2\pi x}L\,dx=-\frac{16L}{9\pi^2}.
$$

A different initial relative phase shifts the oscillation in time. The oscillation comes from [quantum interference](quantum-interference.md), while the constant mean energy follows from the fixed populations of the two [energy eigenstates](energy-eigenstate.md).
