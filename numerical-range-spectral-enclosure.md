# Numerical-range spectral enclosure

↑ **Parent:** [Numerical range of an operator](numerical-range-of-an-operator.md)

For a bounded [self-adjoint operator](self-adjoint-operator.md), $z$ outside the closed numerical range yields $\|(T-z)u\|\geq\operatorname{dist}(z,\overline{W(T)})\|u\|$. The same inequality for $T-\overline z$ makes the range dense, and the first makes it closed. Thus $z$ is in the resolvent. The enclosure remains valid for general bounded Hilbert-space operators by the analogous adjoint numerical-range argument.
