# Lossless coaxial line parameters

↑ **Parent:** [Coaxial cable](coaxial-cable.md)

For ideal coaxial conductors of radii $a<b$ filled with a homogeneous [permittivity](permittivity-split.md) $\epsilon$ and [magnetic permeability](permeability-electromagnetism.md) $\mu$, [Gauss's law](gauss-s-law.md) gives $E_r=\lambda/(2\pi\epsilon r)$ and hence $C'=2\pi\epsilon/\ln(b/a)$. The magnetic field $H_\varphi=I/(2\pi r)$ has energy per unit length $L'I^2/2$, giving $L'=\mu\ln(b/a)/(2\pi)$. These are the external-field parameters when internal magnetic penetration into the conductors is neglected. Their product is $L'C'=\mu\epsilon$, and the [characteristic impedance](characteristic-impedance.md) is $\sqrt{L'/C'}=\sqrt{\mu/\epsilon}\ln(b/a)/(2\pi)$.
