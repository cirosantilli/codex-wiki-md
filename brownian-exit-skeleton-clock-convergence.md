# Brownian exit-skeleton clock convergence

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian exit-time skeleton](brownian-exit-time-skeleton.md)

Unit-radius [Brownian exit time](brownian-exit-time.md) has finite [variance](variance-split.md), since independent unit-time increments give an exponential upper tail. The [Brownian exit-time skeleton](brownian-exit-time-skeleton.md) clock after $2^{2n}$ radius-$2^{-n}$ exits has mean $\mathbb E\sigma_1$ and [variance](variance-split.md) $2^{-2n}\operatorname{Var}(\sigma_1)$. [Chebyshev's inequality](chebyshev-inequality.md) and the [Borel-Cantelli first lemma](borel-cantelli-first-lemma.md) yield the displayed pathwise convergence. [Independence](independent-random-variables.md) between different rows indexed by $n$ is unnecessary.
