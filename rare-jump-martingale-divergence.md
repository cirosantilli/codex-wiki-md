# Rare-jump martingale divergence

↑ **Parent:** [Martingale](martingale-split.md)

A [martingale](martingale-split.md) can diverge to $+\infty$ [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md) when its increments are finite but not uniformly bounded. For independent [Bernoulli random variables](bernoulli-distribution.md) with $\mathbb P(Y_n=1)=2^{-n}$,

$$
X_n=\sum_{k=1}^n(1-2^kY_k)
$$

has centered integrable increments. The [First Borel-Cantelli lemma](borel-cantelli-first-lemma.md) makes the rare negative jumps occur only finitely often, after which every increment equals one. The expectation remains zero at every finite time despite this pathwise divergence.
