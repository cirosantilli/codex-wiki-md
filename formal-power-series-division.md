# Formal power series division

↑ **Parent:** [Formal power series](formal-power-series.md)

If the constant coefficient of $A$ is invertible, then for every [formal power series](formal-power-series.md) $B$ there is a unique quotient $Q$ satisfying $AQ=B$. Comparing coefficients determines $Q$ recursively; multiplying $B$ by the [formal power series reciprocal](formal-power-series-reciprocal.md) of $A$ computes the same quotient.
