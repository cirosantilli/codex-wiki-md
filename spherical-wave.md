# Spherical wave

↑ **Parent:** [Wave](wave-split.md)

For time dependence $e^{-i\omega t}$, the three-dimensional radial solutions $e^{ikr}/r$ and $e^{-ikr}/r$ describe outgoing and incoming [spherical waves](spherical-wave.md) away from the origin. Their inverse-radius amplitudes make their flux through spheres independent of radius. They form the asymptotic radial ingredients of [partial-wave scattering](partial-wave-scattering.md).
