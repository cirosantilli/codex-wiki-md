# Photon statistics

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum optics](quantum-optics-split.md)

[Photon statistics](photon-statistics.md) is the [probability distribution](probability-distribution-split.md) of the [number operator](number-operator.md) in specified field modes, or the distribution of registered counts in a specified gate. Those distributions agree only for an appropriate ideal measurement. Mode selection, losses, and [detector dead time](detector-dead-time.md) matter. For a one-mode [density matrix](density-matrix-split.md), $p_n=\langle n|\rho|n\rangle$ and

$$
\mu=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}np_n,\qquad
\langle N(N-1)\rangle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}n(n-1)p_n.
$$

These two sums already distinguish preparations with the same [expected value](expected-value-split.md).
