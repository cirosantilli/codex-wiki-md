# Endocytosis

↑ **Parent:** [Membrane transport](membrane-transport.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endocytosis)

[Endocytosis](endocytosis.md) takes material into a [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md) by inward budding and separation of part of its [cell membrane](cell-membrane-split.md). At a [chemical synapse](chemical-synapse.md), it helps recover membrane and [proteins](protein-split.md) after [synaptic vesicle](synaptic-vesicle.md) [exocytosis](exocytosis.md). This recovery is distinct from uptake of individual [neurotransmitter](neurotransmitter.md) [molecules](molecule-split.md) through [membrane transport proteins](membrane-transport-protein.md).
