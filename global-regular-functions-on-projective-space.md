# Global regular functions on projective space

↑ **Parent:** [Cohomology of twisting sheaves on projective space](cohomology-of-twisting-sheaves-on-projective-space.md)

The only [regular functions](regular-function.md) defined on all of a nonempty [projective space](projective-space-split.md) over a [field](field.md) $k$ are the constants:

$$
\Gamma(\mathbb P_k^n,\mathcal O)=H^0(\mathbb P_k^n,\mathcal O)=k.
$$

For $\mathbb P^1$, this also follows by gluing $k[t]$ and $k[t^{-1}]$ inside $k[t,t^{-1}]$: their intersection is $k$.
