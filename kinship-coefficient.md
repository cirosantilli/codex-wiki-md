# Kinship coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Identity by descent](identity-by-descent.md)

The kinship coefficient $\phi_{XY}$ is the [probability](probability.md) that one randomly selected [allele](allele.md) copy from each of $X,Y$ is [identical by descent](identity-by-descent.md). For a noninbred individual, $\phi_{XX}=1/2$, and more generally $\phi_{XX}=(1+F_X)/2$. For distinct individuals with parents $M_X,P_X$, [Mendelian segregation](mendelian-segregation.md) gives $\phi_{XY}=[\phi_{M_XY}+\phi_{P_XY}]/2$. The offspring's [inbreeding coefficient](inbreeding-coefficient.md) equals the parents' kinship coefficient.
