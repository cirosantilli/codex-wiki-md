# Sinusoidal viscous rebound

↑ **Parent:** [Postglacial rebound](postglacial-rebound.md)

A small sinusoidal relief on an incompressible Newtonian viscous half-space relaxes exponentially with the displayed timescale in the ideal free-surface model. Here $\lambda$ is [wavelength](wavelength.md), not automatically an ice-sheet radius or diameter. Ice-history, finite [mantle](planetary-mantle.md) layering, elastic [lithosphere](lithosphere.md) and self-gravity complicate real [glacial isostatic adjustment](glacial-isostatic-adjustment.md).
