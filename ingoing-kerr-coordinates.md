# Ingoing Kerr coordinates

↑ **Parent:** [Kerr black hole](kerr-black-hole.md)

[Ingoing Kerr coordinates](ingoing-kerr-coordinates.md) cancel the explicit inverse-$\Delta$ singularities of the [Boyer-Lindquist coordinates](boyer-lindquist-coordinates.md) metric. They give a smooth, nondegenerate [Kerr metric](kerr-metric.md) at the future outer horizon when $M>|a|$. Its stationary and axial [Killing vector fields](killing-vector-field.md) are $\partial_v$ and $\partial_\chi$. The advanced chart need not cover every horizon branch of the maximal extension.
