# Absolute grading in Heegaard Floer homology

↑ **Parent:** [Relative grading in Heegaard Floer homology](relative-grading-in-heegaard-floer-homology.md)

For a torsion [Spin-c structure](spin-c-structure.md), the relative grading of [Heegaard Floer homology](heegaard-floer-homology.md) lifts canonically to a rational absolute grading, normalized by the bottom plus generator of $S^3$ having degree zero. Multiplication by $U$ has degree $-2$, and a [Heegaard Floer cobordism map](heegaard-floer-cobordism-map.md) has degree $(c_1^2-2\chi-3\sigma)/4$. The correction term $d(Y,\mathfrak s)$ is the lowest grading of the image of $HF^\infty$ in $HF^+$ for a rational homology sphere. A pure tower then has bottom plus degree $d$ and top minus degree $d-2$ in the $i<0$ minus convention. Keeping that shift explicit prevents errors of two in surgery and mixed-invariant calculations.
