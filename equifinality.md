# Equifinality

↑ **Parent:** [Psychopathology](psychopathology.md)

Convergence of different starting conditions or processes on a similar outcome. [Equifinality](equifinality.md) cautions against inferring a unique mechanism from a [symptom](symptom.md) alone; it does not show that a syndrome-level classification is useless.
