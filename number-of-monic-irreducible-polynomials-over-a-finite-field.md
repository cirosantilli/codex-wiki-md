# Number of monic irreducible polynomials over a finite field

↑ **Parent:** [Irreducible factors of a finite-field Frobenius polynomial](irreducible-factors-of-a-finite-field-frobenius-polynomial.md)

Let $N_q(n)$ be the number of [monic](monic-polynomial.md) [irreducible polynomials](irreducible-polynomial.md) of [polynomial degree](degree-of-a-polynomial.md) $n$ over $\mathbb F_q$. Factoring $X^{q^n}-X$ into all such polynomials whose degrees divide $n$ gives

$$
q^n=\sum_{d\mid n}dN_q(d).
$$

The [Möbius inversion formula](mobius-inversion-formula.md) therefore gives

$$
N_q(n)=\frac1n\sum_{d\mid n}\mu(d)q^{n/d}.
$$
