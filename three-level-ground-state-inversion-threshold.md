# Three-level ground-state inversion threshold

↑ **Parent:** [Three-level laser](three-level-laser.md)

In a [three-level laser](three-level-laser.md) whose lower lasing level is the ground state, fast relaxation from a pumped higher level feeds a long-lived upper lasing level. With equal lasing-level degeneracies, negligible pump-level population and pump rate R per ground-state emitter, the no-light stationary rate equation gives $N_2/N_1=R\tau_{21}$. Positive [population inversion](population-inversion.md) therefore requires more than half the active emitters to leave the ground state. This is a transparency threshold; [optical resonator](optical-cavity.md) loss imposes an additional positive-gain requirement. Degenerate sublevels require population comparisons weighted by their statistical weights and transition strengths.
