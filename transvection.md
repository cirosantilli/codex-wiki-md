# Transvection

↑ **Parent:** [Linear map](linear-map.md)

For a nonzero [vector](vector.md) $v$ and nonzero [linear functional](linear-functional.md) $f$ vanishing on $v$, this invertible map fixes the [hyperplane](hyperplane.md) $\ker f$ pointwise and has determinant one. Its inverse is $I-vf$, since $(vf)^2=0$. A [symplectic transvection](symplectic-transvection.md) is obtained by taking $f=cB(-,v)$ for a nondegenerate [alternating bilinear form](alternating-bilinear-form.md).
