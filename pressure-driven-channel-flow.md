# Pressure-driven channel flow

↑ **Parent:** [Viscous fluid flow](viscous-fluid-flow-split.md)

A steady unidirectional incompressible viscous flow in a rigid channel, driven by $G=-dp/dz$, reduces the axial [momentum](momentum.md) equation to the displayed [Poisson equation](poisson-equation.md), with $w=0$ on the cross-sectional boundary. Circular-pipe flow is the [Hagen-Poiseuille flow](hagen-poiseuille-equation.md) special case; an arbitrary cross section need not have that parabolic radial profile.
