# Wittig methylenation

↑ **Parent:** [Wittig reaction](wittig-reaction.md)

A [Wittig reaction](wittig-reaction.md) with $\mathrm{Ph_3P{=}CH_2}$ that replaces a [carbonyl](carbonyl-group.md) [oxygen](oxygen.md) by $\mathrm{{=}CH_2}$. On a cyclic [ketone](ketone.md) it installs an exocyclic methylene without inserting a new ring atom.
