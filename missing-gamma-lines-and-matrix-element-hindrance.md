# Missing gamma lines and matrix-element hindrance

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear gamma-ray multipole selection rules](nuclear-gamma-ray-multipole-selection-rules.md)

An absent otherwise-fast gamma transition can support a [spin](spin.md) assignment that forbids it. It is not an absolute [selection rule](selection-rule.md): a nuclear [reduced matrix element](reduced-matrix-element.md) may be small even when [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) and [parity](parity.md) allow the mode. Meaningful limits require detection thresholds, branching normalization and competing-channel information. Assuming ordinary unhindered strengths can yield a useful conventional assignment, but that assumption must be distinguished from strict observed-line constraints.
