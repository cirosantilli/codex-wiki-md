<h1 id="su-6">SU(6)</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Special unitary group](special-unitary-group.md)

The [special unitary group](special-unitary-group.md) on $\mathbb C^6$ has dimension $35$. Its complexified [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) is $\mathfrak{sl}_6(\mathbb C)$, with the [A5 root system](a5-root-system.md). Its defining [representation](group-representation.md) and [exterior square](exterior-square.md) have dimensions $6$ and $15$.
