# Unsmoothed matter-density reduced skewness

↑ **Parent:** [Standard perturbation theory density kernel](standard-perturbation-theory-density-kernel.md)

For [Gaussian initial conditions](gaussian-initial-conditions.md) and the Einstein-de Sitter second-order [standard perturbation theory density kernel](standard-perturbation-theory-density-kernel.md), the unsmoothed [density contrast](density-contrast.md) obeys

$$
\langle\delta^3\rangle=\frac{34}{7}\sigma^4+O(\sigma^6),
\qquad \sigma^2=\langle\delta^2\rangle.
$$

There are three choices of the second-order field and two connected [Wick contractions](wick-contraction.md); the angular average of $F_2$ is $5/7+(2/7)/3=17/21$. Thus the reduced [skewness](skewness.md) is $6(17/21)=34/7$. Smoothing changes this result, and the unsmoothed calculation requires a finite [variance](variance-split.md).
