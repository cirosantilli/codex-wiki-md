# Entropy of an orthogonal quantum mixture

↑ **Parent:** [Von Neumann entropy](von-neumann-entropy-split.md)

For [density operators](density-matrix.md) supported on mutually orthogonal subspaces, the eigenvalues of their mixture are $p_i\lambda_{ij}$, where $\lambda_{ij}$ are the eigenvalues of $\rho_i$. Substitution into the definition of [Von Neumann entropy](von-neumann-entropy-split.md) gives the displayed decomposition into [Shannon entropy](information-entropy.md) of the label and the mean entropy within each block. Zero eigenvalues use $0\log0=0$.
