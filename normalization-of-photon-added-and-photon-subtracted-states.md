# Normalization of photon-added and photon-subtracted states

↑ **Parent:** [Photon statistics](photon-statistics.md)

Raw vectors $a^\dagger|\psi\rangle$ and $a|\psi\rangle$ have squared norms $\mu+1$ and $\mu$. Matrix elements of the [number operator](number-operator.md) in these vectors must be divided by their squared norms before calling them means. For an [optical squeezed vacuum](optical-squeezed-vacuum.md) with mean $s$, the raw moments are $(3s+1)(s+1)$ and $s(3s+1)$, while both normalized means equal $3s+1$ when subtraction is possible.
