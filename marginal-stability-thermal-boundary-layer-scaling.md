# Marginal-stability thermal boundary-layer scaling

↑ **Parent:** [Local Rayleigh-number closure](local-rayleigh-number-closure.md)

For constant [viscosity](dynamic-viscosity-split.md) and thermal properties, marginal local [Rayleigh number](rayleigh-number.md) gives $\rho g\alpha\theta\delta^3/(\eta\kappa)\sim R_c$. Conduction through the resulting [thermal boundary layer](thermal-boundary-layer.md) gives $E\sim k\theta/\delta\propto\theta^{4/3}$. This determines exponents, not an exact prefactor. The closure applies when the layer is thin compared with the convecting interior and its [temperature](temperature-split.md) contrast is positive.
