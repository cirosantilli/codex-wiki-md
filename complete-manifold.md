# Complete manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Riemannian geometry](riemannian-geometry-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complete_manifold)

A (pseudo-)[Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md) is geodesically complete when every inextendible affinely parametrized [geodesic](geodesic.md) is defined for all real parameter values. In the positive-definite case, the [Hopf-Rinow theorem](hopf-rinow-theorem.md) identifies this with metric completeness. Null-only completeness in spacetime is a separate weaker condition.
