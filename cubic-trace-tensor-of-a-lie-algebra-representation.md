# Cubic trace tensor of a Lie algebra representation

↑ **Parent:** [Trace form of a Lie algebra representation](trace-form-of-a-lie-algebra-representation.md)

The [cubic trace tensor of a Lie algebra representation](cubic-trace-tensor-of-a-lie-algebra-representation.md) is cyclic by the [cyclic property of the trace](cyclic-property-of-the-trace.md). It need not be fully antisymmetric. Its antisymmetric part in the first two arguments is fixed by the [Trace form of a Lie algebra representation](trace-form-of-a-lie-algebra-representation.md):

$$
B(X,Y,Z)-B(Y,X,Z)=H([X,Y],Z).
$$
