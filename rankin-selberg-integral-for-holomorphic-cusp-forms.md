<h1 id="rankin-selberg-integral-for-holomorphic-cusp-forms">Rankin–Selberg integral for holomorphic cusp forms</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Rankin–Selberg method](rankin-selberg-method.md)

For two weight-$k$ level-one [cusp forms](cusp-form.md), use the [primitive real-analytic Eisenstein series](primitive-real-analytic-eisenstein-series.md) and $d\mu=dx\,dy/y^2$. The [Rankin–Selberg unfolding](rankin-selberg-method.md) identity is

$$
I(f,g,w)=\frac{\Gamma(w+k-1)}{(4\pi)^{w+k-1}}\sum_{n\geq1}\frac{a_n\overline{b_n}}{n^{w+k-1}}.
$$

Initially this follows by absolute convergence, unfolding to $0\leq x<1$, and integrating the [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md) in $x$. Exponential cusp decay and polynomial growth of the [primitive real-analytic Eisenstein series](primitive-real-analytic-eisenstein-series.md) make the integral [holomorphic](complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md) for $\operatorname{Re}w>1$, continuing the [Dirichlet series](dirichlet-series.md) to $\operatorname{Re}s>k$.
