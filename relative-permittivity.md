# Relative permittivity

↑ **Parent:** [Wannier-Mott exciton](wannier-mott-exciton.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Relative_permittivity)

The dimensionless ratio of a medium's [permittivity](permittivity-split.md) to [vacuum permittivity](vacuum-permittivity.md). The constant scalar value used in a [Wannier-Mott exciton](wannier-mott-exciton.md) is an approximation to the relevant material response.
