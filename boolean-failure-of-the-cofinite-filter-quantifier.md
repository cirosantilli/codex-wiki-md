# Boolean failure of the cofinite-filter quantifier

↑ **Parent:** [Filter quantifier](filter-quantifier.md)

The even and odd integers are both absent from the [cofinite filter](cofinite-filter.md), but their union is present. Thus its [filter quantifier](filter-quantifier.md) need not turn a disjunction into the disjunction of quantified assertions, and failure of a quantified assertion need not imply truth of its quantified negation. An [ultrafilter](ultrafilter.md) restores both laws by deciding each set against its complement.
