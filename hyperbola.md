# Hyperbola

↑ **Parent:** [Conic section](conic-section.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperbola)

A hyperbola is a [conic section](conic-section.md) with two unbounded branches. In suitable coordinates its equation is $x^2/a^2-y^2/b^2=1$ with $a,b>0$. The unit hyperbola is parametrized by $x=\cosh t$, $y=\sinh t$ on its right branch, relating it to the [hyperbolic functions](hyperbolic-function.md).

For example, $x^2/9-y^2/16=1$ has [foci of a conic](focus-of-a-conic.md) at $(\pm5,0)$, because $5^2=3^2+4^2$. Its focal-distance difference has absolute value $6$, while its [eccentricity of a conic](eccentricity-of-a-conic.md) is $5/3>1$. At the point $(3,0)$, the focal distances are $8$ and $2$, verifying that difference. The [asymptotes](asymptote.md) are $y=\pm4x/3$; a distant point approaches those directions while remaining on one of the two branches. The focal-distance difference, rather than a constant sum, distinguishes this [hyperbola](hyperbola.md) from an [ellipse](ellipse.md).
