# Hyperbola

↑ **Parent:** [Conic section](conic-section.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperbola)

A hyperbola is a [conic section](conic-section.md) with two unbounded branches. In suitable coordinates its equation is $x^2/a^2-y^2/b^2=1$ with $a,b>0$. The unit hyperbola is parametrized by $x=\cosh t$, $y=\sinh t$ on its right branch, relating it to the [hyperbolic functions](hyperbolic-function.md).
