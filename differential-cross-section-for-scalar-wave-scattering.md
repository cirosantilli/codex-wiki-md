# Differential cross-section for scalar wave scattering

↑ **Parent:** [Wave scattering](wave-scattering.md)

If a unit incident [plane wave](plane-wave.md) has scattered field $\psi_s=e^{ikR}f_\infty(\widehat{\mathbf r},\widehat{\mathbf r}_0)/R+O(R^{-2})$, the differential wave scattering cross-section is

$$
 \frac{d\sigma}{d\Omega}=|f_\infty(\widehat{\mathbf r},\widehat{\mathbf r}_0)|^2.
$$

It is the far-field limit of $R^2$ times the scattered-to-incident [energy flux](energy-flux.md) ratio. The common physical amplitude normalization cancels when the incident and scattered waves are in the same background medium. The formula assumes that the $1/(4\pi)$ Green-function factor, if present, is already included in the [far-field pattern](far-field-pattern.md).
