# External-leg factorial cancellation at a quartic scalar vertex

↑ **Parent:** [Quartic interaction](quartic-interaction.md)

For a real [scalar field](scalar-field.md) with interaction $-\lambda\phi^4/4!$, the first [Dyson series](dyson-series.md) term is $-i\lambda\int\phi^4d^4x/4!$. Four labeled external legs can attach to the four fields in $4!$ ways, or in $\binom42\,2!\,2!=24$ ways after separating incoming and outgoing legs. The factorial cancels and spacetime integration supplies the [four-momentum conservation](four-momentum-conservation.md) delta function. This gives the connected tree [scattering amplitude](scattering-amplitude.md) $-\lambda$. The separate [identical final-state symmetry factor](identical-particle-factor-in-a-final-state-phase-space-integral.md) belongs in the event phase-space integral and is not another vertex factor.
