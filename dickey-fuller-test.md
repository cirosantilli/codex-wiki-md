<h1 id="dickey-fuller-test">Dickey–Fuller test</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Unit-root autoregressive process](unit-root-autoregressive-process.md)

The Dickey–Fuller test detects a [unit-root autoregressive process](unit-root-autoregressive-process.md) against a stationary causal alternative. Regress $\Delta X_t$ on $X_{t-1}$, with deterministic terms appropriate to the model. Under the null, the usual regression statistic has a nonnormal [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) functional limit, so ordinary normal critical values are inappropriate.
