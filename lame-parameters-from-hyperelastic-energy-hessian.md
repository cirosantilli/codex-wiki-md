<h1 id="lame-parameters-from-hyperelastic-energy-hessian">Lamé parameters from hyperelastic energy Hessian</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperelastic material](hyperelastic-material.md)

At a stress-free natural reference state, an objective [isotropic](isotropy.md) [hyperelastic material](hyperelastic-material.md) with energy $W$ per unit mass has $\rho_0[W(I+H)-W(I)]=\lambda(\operatorname{tr}e)^2/2+\mu e:e+O(|H|^3)$, where $e=\operatorname{sym}H$. Twice differentiating gives the displayed [Lamé parameters](lame-parameter.md) and the usual [elastic stiffness tensor](elastic-stiffness-tensor.md). A prestressed reference requires initial-stress corrections.
