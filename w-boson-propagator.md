# W boson propagator

↑ **Parent:** [W boson](w-boson.md)

In unitary gauge, the free massive [W boson](w-boson.md) has the displayed momentum-space propagator. At external momentum small compared with $M_W$, its leading term is $ig_{\mu\nu}/M_W^2$. Inserting it between two [weak charged current](charged-current.md) vertices produces the local [Fermi interaction](fermi-interaction.md), with $G_F/\sqrt2=g^2/(8M_W^2)$ when each vertex is written with $1-\gamma_5$. Higher momentum terms give higher-dimensional operators; other gauges distribute the longitudinal contribution differently while giving the same physical amplitudes.
