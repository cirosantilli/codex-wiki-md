# Quadratic-polynomial representation of the infinite dihedral group

↑ **Parent:** [Representation of the infinite dihedral group](representation-of-the-infinite-dihedral-group.md)

Let the rotation and reflection generators act on polynomials of degree at most two by

$$
(Tp)(x)=p(x+1),
\qquad
(Sp)(x)=p(-x).
$$

Then $S^2=I$ and $STS=T^{-1}$. The operator $T$ has one size-three unipotent [Jordan block](jordan-block.md), so this three-dimensional representation cannot split into invariant subspaces of dimension at most two.
