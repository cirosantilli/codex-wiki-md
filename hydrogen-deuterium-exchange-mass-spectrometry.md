# Hydrogen-deuterium exchange mass spectrometry

↑ **Parent:** [Mass spectrometry](mass-spectrometry.md)

[hydrogen-deuterium exchange mass spectrometry](hydrogen-deuterium-exchange-mass-spectrometry.md) follows exchange of exchangeable [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) with [deuterium](deuterium.md) and measures mass changes, often after digestion into [peptides](peptide.md). Protection can reflect [hydrogen bonds](hydrogen-bond.md), burial or restricted structural opening; altered exchange is not uniquely diagnostic of a binding interface.
