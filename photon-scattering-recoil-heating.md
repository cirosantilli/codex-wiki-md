# Photon-scattering recoil heating

↑ **Parent:** [Recoil energy](recoil-energy.md)

Absorption of [momentum](momentum-split.md) $\mathbf p$ followed by emission of $\mathbf p'$ changes a free atom's [momentum](momentum-split.md) by $\mathbf p-\mathbf p'$. For isotropic emission of fixed magnitude, its mean [vector](vector.md) is zero. The [energy](energy-split.md) change then separates into a directional [work](work.md) term and two positive [recoil](recoil.md) terms. If absorption directions are also isotropic and uncorrelated with the initial [momentum](momentum-split.md), the [work](work.md) term averages away. Equal incoming and outgoing [photon](photon.md) magnitudes give two [recoil](recoil.md) energies per cycle.
