# Direct-product decomposition of the cube symmetry group

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetry group of a cube](symmetry-group-of-a-cube.md)

The [symmetry group of a cube](symmetry-group-of-a-cube.md) splits into its central inversion and its [rotational symmetry group of a cube](rotational-symmetry-group-of-a-cube.md). Inversion has [determinant](determinant.md) $-1$, the rotation [subgroup](subgroup.md) has [determinant](determinant.md) $1$, and together they generate the full [group](group-split.md) with trivial intersection. The rotation [subgroup](subgroup.md) acts faithfully on the four body diagonals and is isomorphic to $S_4$. Consequently the full [group](group-split.md) is this [direct product of groups](direct-product-of-groups.md).
