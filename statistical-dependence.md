# Statistical dependence

↑ **Parent:** [Random variable](random-variable-split.md)

Two [random variables](random-variable-split.md) are statistically dependent when their joint [probability distribution](probability-distribution.md) differs from the product of their marginal [probability distributions](probability-distribution.md). Equivalently, some events determined by the two variables violate the product rule that defines [independence](independent-random-variables.md). Nonzero [covariance](covariance.md) implies statistical dependence when the moments exist, but zero [covariance](covariance.md) does not in general imply [independence](independent-random-variables.md).
