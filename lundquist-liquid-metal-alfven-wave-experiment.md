<h1 id="lundquist-liquid-metal-alfven-wave-experiment">Lundquist liquid-metal Alfvén wave experiment</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Alfvén wave](alfven-wave.md)

[Stig Lundquist](stig-lundquist.md), the author printed as S. Lundquist, reported an early laboratory test in 1949. The apparatus used [mercury](mercury-element.md) in a stainless-steel cylinder, a vibrating disk, and a floating mirror to indicate the motion at the liquid surface. The reproduced apparatus drawing also shows a scale; the accompanying account gives an applied [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) of $1.3\,\mathrm T$.[Andrey Sheyko, Numerical investigations of rotating MHD in a spherical shell (2014), p. 8, Figure 1.4 and following paragraph; reproduction of Lundquist's apparatus](numerical-investigations-of-rotating-mhd-in-a-spherical-shell-by-andrey-sheyko.md)

The imposed [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) ran along the cylinder, while the bottom disk oscillated to excite azimuthal motion. Varying the drive [frequency](frequency.md) sought a [standing wave](standing-wave.md) [resonance](resonance.md). A later authored experimental account describes this as an early hint: the [resonance](resonance.md) peak was not reached, and magnetic damping was substantial.[2019, Section 1, paragraph beginning “Only a few laboratory experiments”: mercury vessel, bottom crenelated disk, resonance search and unreached peak](torsional-alfven-waves-in-a-dipolar-magnetic-field-experiments-and-simulations-by-z-tigrine-and-others.md) The experiment therefore tested a bounded azimuthal shear disturbance, related to a [torsional Alfvén wave](torsional-alfven-wave.md), rather than an infinite [plane wave](plane-wave.md) or a compression-driven [magnetosonic wave](magnetosonic-wave.md).

**[Figure 2](#image-functional-chain-of-lundquist-s-early-mercury-alfven-wave-test). Functional chain of Lundquist's early mercury Alfvén-wave test**. An original functional schematic of the [mercury](mercury-element.md) vessel, axial [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md), bottom disk excitation and surface mirror readout. It is not a dimensional reconstruction. The [frequency](frequency.md) scan sought a [standing wave](standing-wave.md) [resonance](resonance.md); the early experiment did not establish a resolved peak.

The [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) provides the controlled physical parameter: the ideal [Alfvén wave](alfven-wave.md) prediction makes a propagation speed proportional to its strength, while a [resonance](resonance.md) also depends on the vessel's [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md). A quantitative test must compare the driven response with finite-[electrical conductivity](electrical-conductivity.md) theory and account for mechanical coupling and wall losses. An observed surface motion alone cannot distinguish all these mechanisms. The reproduced apparatus account gives neither a numerical drive [frequency](frequency.md) nor a field-off comparison. Lundquist's paper itself calls the experiment preliminary and proposes further investigations.[Abstract, final sentence: preliminary experiment and proposed investigations](experimental-investigations-of-magneto-hydrodynamic-waves-by-stig-lundquist.md)
