# Gauge-invariant electron Yukawa mass

↑ **Parent:** [Yukawa interaction](yukawa-interaction.md)

The [Standard Model](standard-model-split.md) lepton doublet $L=(\nu_L,e_L)^T$ has [hypercharge](hypercharge.md) $-1/2$, the electron singlet $e_R$ has $-1$, and the [Higgs doublet](higgs-field.md) has $1/2$. Therefore $-y_e\bar L\phi e_R+\mathrm{h.c.}$ is [gauge-invariant](gauge-invariance.md) even though a bare $\bar e_Le_R$ mass is not. The [vacuum expectation value](vacuum-expectation-value.md) generates a [Dirac mass](dirac-mass-term.md) $|y_e|v/\sqrt2$. In [unitary gauge](unitary-gauge.md), the [Higgs boson](higgs-boson.md) coupling is $-(m_e/v)h\bar ee$ after rephasing the electron.
