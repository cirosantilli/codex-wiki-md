# Universal enveloping algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_enveloping_algebra)

The universal enveloping algebra $U(\mathfrak g)$ is the associative algebra generated by $\mathfrak g$ subject to $xy-yx=[x,y]$. Its modules are the same as [Lie algebra representations](lie-algebra-representation.md).
