# One-loop diagram classes for Compton scattering

↑ **Parent:** [Compton scattering](compton-scattering.md)

At order $e^4$, the open electron line can carry an internal-electron [self-energy](self-energy.md), a correction at either photon vertex, or a virtual photon spanning both vertices. Include the crossed external-photon order for each class. External-electron self-energies and external-photon [vacuum polarization](vacuum-polarization.md) insertions appear in unamputated functions and are treated by [LSZ reduction](lsz-reduction-formula.md) and field residues in physical amplitudes. A closed loop with three photon vertices cancels between orientations by [Furry's theorem](furry-s-theorem.md). Renormalized calculations also include the mass, field and vertex [counterterms](counterterm.md) of the same perturbative order.
