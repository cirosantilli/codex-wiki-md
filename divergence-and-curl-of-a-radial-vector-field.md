# Divergence and curl of a radial vector field

↑ **Parent:** [Vector field](vector-field.md)

For $r=|x|>0$, the [gradient](gradient.md) satisfies $\partial_i r=x_i/r$. The [product rule](product-rule.md) and [Einstein notation](einstein-notation.md) then give the displayed [divergence](divergence.md) formula in dimension $n$. In dimension three, its derivative [matrix](matrix.md) is symmetric, so the [curl](curl.md) vanishes. Zero [divergence](divergence.md) forces $f(r)=Cr^{-n}$ on each positive radial interval. This formula does not assert regularity at the origin.
