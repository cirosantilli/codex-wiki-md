# Dipole levitation force above a superconducting plane

↑ **Parent:** [Superconducting plane image dipole](superconducting-plane-image-dipole.md)

For a small fixed-current loop with moment $m=I\pi R^2$ and $R\ll h$, the image field gives upward [force](force-split.md) $3\mu_0m^2/(32\pi h^4)$. Differentiating the external field holds the image position fixed; moving both source and image in that derivative would double the [force](force-split.md) incorrectly.
