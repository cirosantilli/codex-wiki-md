# Focal ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Optical instrument](optical-instrument.md)

The [focal ratio](focal-ratio.md) is the [focal length](focal-length.md) divided by the entrance [aperture](aperture.md) [diameter](diameter.md). It controls the cone [angle](angle.md) of a paraxial focused beam.
