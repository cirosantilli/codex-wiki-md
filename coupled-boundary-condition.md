# Coupled boundary condition

↑ **Parent:** [Boundary condition](boundary-condition.md)

A linear [boundary condition](boundary-condition.md) can relate traces at different endpoints rather than prescribe data independently at each endpoint. For a second-order [differential equation](differential-equation-split.md), examples are $a_1y(a)+b_1y'(a)+c_1y(b)+d_1y'(b)=0$ together with another independent relation. The resulting [operator domain](operator-domain.md) determines whether the boundary form vanishes. Coupling by itself guarantees neither [self-adjointness](self-adjoint-operator.md) nor its failure.
