# Charged current

↑ **Parent:** [Weak interaction](weak-interaction.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charged_current)

The weak charged current couples a [W boson](w-boson.md) to two members of a left-handed electroweak doublet. In the quark mass basis its flavor coefficients are entries of the [Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa matrix](cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md).
