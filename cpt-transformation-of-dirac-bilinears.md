# CPT transformation of Dirac bilinears

↑ **Parent:** [CPT theorem](cpt-theorem.md)

For a Hermitian [fermion bilinear](fermion-bilinear.md), an [antiunitary operator](antiunitary-operator.md) implementing [CPT](cpt-symmetry.md) conjugates numerical coefficients and exchanges particle and antiparticle fields at $-x$. Reordering the [fermions](fermion.md) then gives $\Theta:\overline\psi\Gamma\psi(x):\Theta^{-1}=:\overline\psi\gamma_5\Gamma\gamma_5\psi(-x):$ when $\Gamma^\dagger=\gamma^0\Gamma\gamma^0$. Thus scalar, pseudoscalar and tensor bilinears are even, while vector and axial-vector bilinears are odd. Each derivative contributes another minus sign. The graded reordering is essential; treating [Dirac fields](dirac-field.md) as commuting quantities gives the wrong transformation.
