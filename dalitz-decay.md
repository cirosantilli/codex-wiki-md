# Dalitz decay

↑ **Parent:** [Particle decay](particle-decay.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dalitz_decay)

A [Dalitz decay](dalitz-decay.md) replaces a radiated real [photon](photon.md) with a virtual [photon](photon.md) converting to a charged-lepton pair. The neutral-[pion](pion.md) example is $\pi^0\to\gamma e^+e^-$. [Double Dalitz decay](double-dalitz-decay.md) converts both virtual photons in the two-photon mode.
