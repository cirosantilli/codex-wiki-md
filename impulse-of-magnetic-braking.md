# Impulse of magnetic braking

↑ **Parent:** [Loop crossing a magnetic field boundary](loop-crossing-a-magnetic-field-boundary.md)

For a complete entry and exit of a small square loop, integrating the drag over two [distances](distance.md) $a$ gives [impulse](impulse.md) magnitude $2B^2a^3/R$. This is independent of [speed](speed.md) in the ideal sharp-edge, zero-inductance model, though dissipated [energy](energy-split.md) is not.
