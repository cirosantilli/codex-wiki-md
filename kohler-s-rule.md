<h1 id="kohler-s-rule">Kohler's rule</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetoresistance](magnetoresistance.md)

If one temperature-dependent relaxation scale controls a fixed carrier population and fixed orbital geometry, the [magnetoresistance ratio](magnetoresistance-ratio.md) can collapse onto a function of $B/\rho(0,T)$. This is [Kohler's rule](kohler-s-rule.md). For the equal-mobility [compensated two-carrier magnetoresistance](compensated-two-carrier-magnetoresistance.md) model, $[\rho(B)-\rho(0)]/\rho(0)=(\mu B)^2$ and $\mu=[2ne\rho(0)]^{-1}$. Therefore plotting against $[B/\rho(0)]^2$ gives a common straight line of slope $1/(4n^2e^2)$. A collapse tests the scaling relation; it does not by itself prove this particular microscopic carrier model. Multiple independently varying scattering times or carrier populations can spoil it.
