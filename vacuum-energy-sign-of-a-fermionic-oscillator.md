# Vacuum energy sign of a fermionic oscillator

↑ **Parent:** [Vacuum energy](vacuum-energy.md)

A fermionic mode with [canonical anticommutation relations](canonical-anticommutation-relations.md) $\{b,b^\dagger\}=1$ has the symmetrically ordered [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) $H=\tfrac E2(b^\dagger b-bb^\dagger)=E(b^\dagger b-1/2)$. Its empty-state zero-point contribution is $-E/2$. A [quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator.md) with bosonic [canonical commutation relations](canonical-commutation-relation.md) contributes $+E/2$ instead. For a [Dirac field](dirac-field.md), sum over both particle and [antiparticle](antiparticle.md) modes and over [spin](spin.md): there are four independent oscillators per spatial [momentum](momentum.md) in four spacetime dimensions. Comparing bosonic and fermionic [vacuum energies](vacuum-energy.md) requires a common [regularization in quantum field theory](regularization-in-quantum-field-theory.md), matching degrees of freedom and a stated vacuum-energy subtraction convention.
