# Unobserved heterogeneity

↑ **Parent:** [Latent variable](latent-variable.md)

Persistent unmeasured differences between individuals can generate outcome dependence after adjusting for observed [covariates](covariate.md). A [random intercept](random-intercept.md) is one representation. Conditional independence given that [latent variable](latent-variable.md) need not persist after it is integrated out; apparent history dependence can therefore occur without [true state dependence](true-state-dependence.md).
