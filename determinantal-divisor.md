# Determinantal divisor

↑ **Parent:** [Smith normal form](smith-normal-form.md)

For an integer [matrix](matrix.md) $M$, its $j$th determinantal divisor is the nonnegative greatest common divisor of all $j$-by-$j$ [matrix minors](minor-linear-algebra.md), with $\Delta_0=1$. If all these minors vanish, take $\Delta_j=0$. Unimodular row and column operations preserve these divisors. For nonzero invariant factors $d_1\mid\cdots\mid d_r$ in the [Smith normal form](smith-normal-form.md), $\Delta_j=d_1\cdots d_j$ for $j\leq r$, so $d_j=\Delta_j/\Delta_{j-1}$.
