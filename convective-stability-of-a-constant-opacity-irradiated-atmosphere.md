# Convective stability of a constant-opacity irradiated atmosphere

↑ **Parent:** [Radiative-convective boundary](radiative-convective-boundary.md)

A constant radiative-diffusion coefficient and constant gravity give $T^4=T_0^4+4AP/g$. Thus $\nabla_{\rm rad}=AP/(gT^4)<1/4$ for $T_0>0$. A diatomic [ideal gas](ideal-gas.md) has [adiabatic temperature gradient](adiabatic-temperature-gradient.md) $2/7$, so this idealized profile never becomes convective. A physical [radiative-convective boundary](radiative-convective-boundary.md) requires additional [opacity](opacity.md), flux, or thermodynamic variation.
