# Adenine nucleotide translocator

↑ **Parent:** [Inner mitochondrial membrane](inner-mitochondrial-membrane.md)

A carrier exchanging adenine nucleotides across the [inner mitochondrial membrane](inner-mitochondrial-membrane.md), supplying matrix [ADP](adenosine-diphosphate.md) and exporting synthesized [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) during ordinary oxidative phosphorylation.
