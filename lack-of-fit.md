# Lack of fit

↑ **Parent:** [Normal linear model](normal-linear-model.md)

Lack of fit is a mismatch between a regression model's mean function and the true mean relationship. Replicated predictor configurations can separate its sum of squares from [pure error](pure-error.md). A [residual-versus-fitted plot](residual-versus-fitted-plot.md) can suggest curvature or changing variance even without a formal replication-based test.
