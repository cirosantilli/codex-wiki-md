# Clopen sign approximation in the real Cantor unit ball

↑ **Parent:** [Extreme points of a real continuous-function unit ball](extreme-points-of-a-real-continuous-function-unit-ball.md)

Every real continuous function of supremum norm at most one on the [Cantor set](cantor-set.md) is a uniform limit of convex combinations of continuous sign functions. Approximate on a finite [Cantor cylinder](cantor-cylinder.md) partition by values $a_j\in[-1,1]$. The sign vector $s$ has weight $\prod_j(1+s_ja_j)/2$, whose coordinate means are $a_j$. This proves a [closed convex hull](closed-convex-hull.md) identity without weak compactness.
