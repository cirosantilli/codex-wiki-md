# Tetrahedral three-Skyrmion

↑ **Parent:** [Skyrmion](skyrmion.md)

The familiar low-energy $B=3$ [Skyrmion](skyrmion.md) has tetrahedral baryon-density symmetry $T_d$. Proper rotations form the [tetrahedral symmetry](tetrahedral-symmetry.md) group $T\cong A_4$, acting on the field with compensating [isorotations](isorotation.md). An angular approximation is $R(z)=(i\sqrt3z^2-1)/(z^3-i\sqrt3z)$. Its [Wronskian of a rational map](wronskian-of-a-rational-map.md) is proportional to $z^4+2i\sqrt3z^2+1$. The four branch directions are the face-hole directions of a tetrahedral shell. A [rational map](rational-map-complex-analysis.md) and a variational radial profile approximate the actual field, and baryon-density symmetry must be distinguished from invariance of the [pion](pion.md) field under an unaccompanied spatial rotation.
