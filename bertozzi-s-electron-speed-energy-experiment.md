<h1 id="bertozzi-s-electron-speed-energy-experiment">Bertozzi's electron speed-energy experiment</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Tests of special relativity](tests-of-special-relativity.md)

[William Bertozzi](william-bertozzi.md) compared [electron](electron.md) time-of-flight speeds with independently measured kinetic energies using electrostatic and linear acceleration. The 1964 report covers 0.5–15 MeV and uses calorimetry to check deposited [energy](energy-split.md). Speed approaches the light-speed limit while [energy](energy-split.md) continues rising, testing relativistic [energy](energy-split.md) against an unrestricted Newtonian extrapolation.[1964 abstract and experimental procedure](speed-and-kinetic-energy-of-relativistic-electrons-by-william-bertozzi.md)
