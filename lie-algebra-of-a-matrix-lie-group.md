# Lie algebra of a matrix Lie group

↑ **Parent:** [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md)

For a [Matrix Lie group](matrix-lie-group.md) $G$, its tangent space $\mathfrak g=T_I G$ is closed under the matrix [commutator](commutator.md). Indeed, for $X,Y\in\mathfrak g$, the group commutator

$$
e^{tX}e^{sY}e^{-tX}e^{-sY}
$$

lies in $G$, and the mixed derivative at $(0,0)$ of its matrix logarithm is $XY-YX$. Thus $[X,Y]=XY-YX$ belongs to $\mathfrak g$.
