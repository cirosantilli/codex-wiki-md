# Uniform material thinning under planar-sheet tension

↑ **Parent:** [Planar viscous-sheet stretching equations](planar-viscous-sheet-stretching-equations.md)

For a thin planar [viscous sheet](viscous-sheet.md) with zero effective gravity and prescribed tensile force $F(t)$ per unit transverse width, [force balance](force-balance.md) gives $4\mu h w_z=F(t)$. [Conservation of mass](mass-conservation.md) gives $D_th=-hw_z=-F/(4\mu)$, proving uniform subtraction from the initial material thickness. The material map satisfies $dz/dz_0=h_0/[h_0-\Delta]$. Thus the same material thinning creates greater extension wherever the initial sheet is thinner.
