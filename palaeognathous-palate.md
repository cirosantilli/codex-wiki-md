# Palaeognathous palate

↑ **Parent:** [Palate](palate.md)

An avian palatal arrangement with extensive bracing among the [vomer](vomer.md), [pterygoid bone](pterygoid-bone.md), [palatine bone](palatine-bone.md) and [skull](skull.md) base. It is associated with [Palaeognathae](palaeognathae.md); the name does not establish evolutionary primitiveness.
