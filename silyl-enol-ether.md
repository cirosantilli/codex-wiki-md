# Silyl enol ether

↑ **Parent:** [Enol](enol.md)

A [silyl enol ether](silyl-enol-ether.md) has the connectivity $\mathrm{C{=}C{-}O{-}SiR_3}$. Nucleophilic attack at [silicon](silicon.md) can cleave the Si–O bond and generate an [enolate](enolate.md) without adding to the original [carbonyl](carbonyl-group.md) [carbon](carbon.md).
