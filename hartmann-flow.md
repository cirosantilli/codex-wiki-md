# Hartmann flow

↑ **Parent:** [Resistive magnetohydrodynamics](resistive-magnetohydrodynamics.md)

Hartmann flow is a fully developed viscous conducting-fluid flow driven along a planar channel while a uniform [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) crosses the walls. Induction and the [Lorentz force density](lorentz-force-density.md) couple its velocity and tangential induced field. For no-slip, normal-field walls separated by $2L$, the velocity is a [hyperbolic cosine](hyperbolic-cosine.md) profile that approaches [plane Poiseuille flow](plane-poiseuille-flow.md) at zero imposed field and develops a uniform core with [Hartmann layers](hartmann-layer.md) at large [Hartmann number](hartmann-number.md).
