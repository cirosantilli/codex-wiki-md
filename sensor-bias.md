# Sensor bias

↑ **Parent:** [Measured output](measured-output.md)

A [sensor bias](sensor-bias.md) is a systematic offset between the [measured output](measured-output.md) and the physical [plant output](output-control-theory.md), modeled here as $y_m=y+b$ with constant $b$. Unlike zero-mean [measurement noise](measurement-noise.md), a [sensor bias](sensor-bias.md) persists when repeated readouts are averaged. A [sensor bias](sensor-bias.md) affects the physical meaning of a zero [tracking error](tracking-error.md); for a [temperature](temperature-split.md) readout, independent [thermometer calibration](thermometer-calibration.md) can detect it.
