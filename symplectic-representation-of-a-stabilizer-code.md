# Symplectic representation of a stabilizer code

↑ **Parent:** [Stabilizer code](stabilizer-code.md)

A [stabilizer group](stabilizer-group.md) with $r$ independent generators maps injectively to an r-dimensional isotropic subspace of the [binary symplectic space of Pauli labels](binary-symplectic-space-of-pauli-labels.md). For generator rows $(A\mid B)$, commutation is $AB^T+BA^T=0$ over $\mathbb F_2$. Its [stabilizer subspace](stabilizer-subspace.md) has dimension $2^{n-r}$. The labels of the [centralizer of a stabilizer group](centralizer-of-a-stabilizer-group.md) form $L^\perp$; labels in $L^\perp\setminus L$ act as nontrivial logical Pauli operators.
