# Binary test for quantum decoding success

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum binary hypothesis testing](quantum-binary-hypothesis-testing.md)

For $k$ equiprobable quantum code states decoded by a [POVM](positive-operator-valued-measure.md) $\{E_m\}$, testing whether the classical message label agrees with the decoded output gives a joint binary [POVM](positive-operator-valued-measure.md) with $F_0=\sum_m|m\rangle\langle m|\otimes E_m$. Its acceptance probability is the decoding success $1-\epsilon$ on the joint [classical-quantum state](classical-quantum-state.md) and $1/k$ on the product of its marginals. Applying [quantum relative entropy](quantum-relative-entropy.md) monotonicity to this test gives the [one-shot classical-quantum coding converse](one-shot-classical-quantum-coding-converse.md).
