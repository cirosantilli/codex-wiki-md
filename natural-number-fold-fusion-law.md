# Natural-number fold fusion law

↑ **Parent:** [Natural-number fold](natural-number-fold.md)

Suppose $f$ is a [strict function](strict-function.md), $f(a)=b$ and $f\circ g=h\circ f$. The zero case of the asserted equation is the base identity; the successor case rewrites $f(g(z))$ as $h(f(z))$ and applies [structural induction](structural-induction.md). At an undefined argument, the [strict function](strict-function.md) premise makes both sides the [bottom value](bottom-value.md). For pure definable [Haskell](haskell.md) functions, equality on finite partial approximations extends to infinite constructor chains by continuity in the information order. For finite total arguments alone, the zero and successor proof does not require strictness.
