# Tangent of a uniform angle has a Cauchy distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Cauchy distribution](cauchy-distribution.md)

If $s>0$ and $\Theta$ has the [continuous uniform distribution](continuous-uniform-distribution.md) on $(-\pi/2,\pi/2)$, then $Y=s\tan\Theta$ has the [Cauchy distribution](cauchy-distribution.md) with location zero and scale $s$. The [tangent function](tangent-function.md) is strictly increasing on this interval, so its [cumulative distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md) is

$$
P(Y\le y)=\frac12+\frac1\pi\arctan\frac ys.
$$

Differentiating gives the [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) $s/[\pi(s^2+y^2)]$. The [probability](probability.md) of either endpoint is zero, so including endpoints in the uniform interval does not alter this [probability distribution](probability-distribution-split.md). The slow quadratic decay makes the [expected value](expected-value-split.md) undefined: its positive and negative parts both have infinite integrals. Its second [moment](moment.md) is also infinite, so it has no finite [standard deviation](standard-deviation.md).
