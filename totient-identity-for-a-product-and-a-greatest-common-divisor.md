# Totient identity for a product and a greatest common divisor

↑ **Parent:** [Euler totient function](euler-totient-function.md)

The prime-power formula and [multiplicativity](multiplicativity-of-an-arithmetic-function.md) of [Euler's totient function](euler-totient-function.md) imply $\phi(mn)\phi(\gcd(m,n))=\phi(m)\phi(n)\gcd(m,n)$. For a [prime](prime-number-split.md) with positive exponents $a,b$ in the inputs, both local products equal $p^{a+b+\min(a,b)-2}(p-1)^2$. If one exponent is zero, the equality is immediate from $\phi(1)=1$.
