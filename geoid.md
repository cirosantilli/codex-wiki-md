# Geoid

↑ **Parent:** [Geophysics](geophysics-split.md)

An equipotential surface of [Earth](earth.md) [gravity](gravity.md), including the centrifugal potential, used as an idealized sea-level reference. [geoid](geoid.md) height responds to long-wavelength [density](density.md) anomalies and boundary deformation; local [gravity](gravity.md) and [geoid](geoid.md) height are related but distinct observables.
