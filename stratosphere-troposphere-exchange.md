# Stratosphere-troposphere exchange

↑ **Parent:** [Stratosphere](stratosphere.md)

Transport of air and trace species across the tropopause. Downward [ozone](ozone.md) transport can supply the [troposphere](troposphere.md) even when local tropospheric photochemical production is unchanged. Regional pathways, mixing and residence times determine the resulting burden.
