# Mechanism-based inhibition

↑ **Parent:** [Enzyme](enzyme-split.md)

In [mechanism-based inhibition](mechanism-based-inhibition.md), catalytic processing of an inhibitor generates the species responsible for sustained inhibition. The covalent bond can be between inhibitor and cofactor rather than to an amino acid side chain; persistence must be described on the relevant timescale.
