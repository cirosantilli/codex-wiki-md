# Exponential-mean confidence interval by pivot inversion

↑ **Parent:** [Confidence interval](confidence-interval.md)

For an [exponential distribution](exponential-distribution.md) with mean $\theta$, the [maximum-likelihood estimator](maximum-likelihood-estimator.md) is $\bar Y$ and $\sqrt n(\bar Y-\theta)/\theta$ converges to a standard [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md). Inverting the event that this pivot lies between $-z$ and $z$ gives the displayed [confidence interval](confidence-interval.md), provided $n>z^2$. Its two-sided [coverage probability](coverage-probability.md) has error $O(n^{-1})$, because the first [Edgeworth expansion](edgeworth-series.md) term is even and cancels between the two tails.
