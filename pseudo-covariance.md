# Pseudo-covariance

↑ **Parent:** [Complex covariance](complex-covariance.md)

For a centered complex [random variable](random-variable-split.md) $Z=A+iB$, the pseudo-covariance is $P=\mathbb EZ^2$. Together with $V=\mathbb E|Z|^2$ it determines the real [covariance matrix](covariance-matrix.md): $\mathbb EA^2=(V+\operatorname{Re}P)/2$, $\mathbb EB^2=(V-\operatorname{Re}P)/2$, and $\mathbb EAB=\operatorname{Im}P/2$.
