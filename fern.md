# Fern

↑ **Parent:** [Vascular plant](vascular-plant.md)

A member of a vascular-plant lineage generally reproducing through [spores](spore.md) rather than [seeds](seed.md). Most familiar fern bodies are [sporophytes](sporophyte.md); their [gametophytes](gametophyte.md) can have different environmental requirements. [heterospory](heterospory.md) and other life-cycle differences prevent treating every fern as one identical model.
