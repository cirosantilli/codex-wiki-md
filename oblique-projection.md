# Oblique projection

↑ **Parent:** [Projection (linear algebra)](projection-linear-algebra.md)

If two [closed subspaces of a Hilbert space](closed-subspace-of-a-hilbert-space.md) give $H=V\oplus W$, the projection onto $V$ along $W$ fixes $V$ and vanishes on $W$. It need not be an [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md). The [bounded inverse theorem](bounded-inverse-theorem.md) applied to the addition map $(v,w)\mapsto v+w$ on the complete component spaces proves boundedness of the component maps. With both complementary subspaces nonzero, its [operator norm](operator-norm.md) and that of $I-Q$ equal $\sec\theta_{V,W^\perp}$, with the [directed subspace angle](directed-subspace-angle.md) convention. Indeed, for fixed $v\in V$, the smallest possible [norm](norm.md) of $v+w$ over $w\in W$ is $\|P_{W^\perp}v\|$, which proves the formula for $\|Q\|$. Equality with $\|I-Q\|$ follows from the block representation $Q=\begin{pmatrix}I&B\\0&0\end{pmatrix}$ on $V\oplus V^\perp$: both squared [operator norms](operator-norm.md) are $1+\|B\|^2$. If a summand is zero, $Q$ is zero or the [identity operator](identity-operator.md), and one complementary [operator norm](operator-norm.md) is zero; those cases must be treated directly.
