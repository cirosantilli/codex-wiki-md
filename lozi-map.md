# Lozi map

↑ **Parent:** [Discrete dynamical system](discrete-dynamical-system-split.md)

A piecewise affine planar map whose [derivative](derivative.md) changes across $x=0$. Away from that line its Jacobian is $\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix}-a\operatorname{sgn}x&1\\b&0\end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$, with [determinant](determinant.md) $-b$. For $b\ne0$ it is invertible; at $b=0$ an iteration collapses the image onto a line. The switching line requires piecewise analysis rather than applying a smooth-map theorem across it without checking its hypotheses.
