# PCR primer

↑ **Parent:** [Polymerase chain reaction](polymerase-chain-reaction.md)

A [PCR primer](pcr-primer.md) is a short [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) strand that supplies a paired $3'$ end for a [DNA polymerase](dna-polymerase.md). Its [nucleotide sequence](nucleic-acid-sequence.md) determines where [base pairing](base-pairing.md) can initiate copying. Matching a [PCR primer](pcr-primer.md) at one site is insufficient: both [PCR primers](pcr-primer.md) must support opposed extension across a copyable interval for sustained selective [PCR amplification](polymerase-chain-reaction.md).
