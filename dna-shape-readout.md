# DNA shape readout

↑ **Parent:** [Transcription factor](transcription-factor.md)

[DNA shape readout](dna-shape-readout.md) is recognition through sequence-dependent geometry, deformability, electrostatics or hydration as well as, or instead of, direct contacts to exposed base-edge groups. Mutating a binding site can therefore change both its chemical pattern and its preferred shape.
