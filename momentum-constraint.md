# Momentum constraint

↑ **Parent:** [Hamiltonian formulation of general relativity](hamiltonian-formulation-of-general-relativity.md)

The momentum constraint is the equation obtained by varying the [shift vector](shift-vector.md) in the [Hamiltonian formulation of general relativity](hamiltonian-formulation-of-general-relativity.md). It expresses the tangential-normal projection of the [Einstein field equations](einstein-field-equations.md) and generates spatial coordinate transformations. Writing $p^{ij}=\pi^{ij}/\sqrt h$ avoids ambiguities about covariant derivatives of [tensor densities](tensor-density.md).
