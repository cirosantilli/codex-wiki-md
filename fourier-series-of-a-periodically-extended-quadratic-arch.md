# Fourier series of a periodically extended quadratic arch

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md)

The period-one extension of $x(1-x)$ on $[0,1]$ has [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md) $1/6-\pi^{-2}\sum_{n\ge1}n^{-2}\cos(2\pi nx)$. Endpoint agreement makes the periodic function continuous. Its derivative series is $(2/\pi)\sum_{n\ge1}n^{-1}\sin(2\pi nx)$, which equals $1-2x$ in the open unit interval and converges to zero at integers, the midpoint of the derivative's jump. [Termwise differentiation of a Fourier series](termwise-differentiation-of-a-fourier-series.md) applies locally away from the corners.
