# Single marked exponential jump process

↑ **Parent:** [Markov jump process](markov-jump-process.md)

Let $T$ be a rate-one [exponential random variable](exponential-distribution.md) independent of a [uniform random variable](uniform-random-variable.md) $U$ on $(0,1)$. In its natural [filtration](filtration-probability-theory.md), this [Markov jump process](markov-jump-process.md) jumps once from zero to its mark and then stays there. Its destination rate kernel is $q(x,dy)=\mathbf1_{\{x=0\}}\mathbf1_{(0,1)}(y)dy$. For a Borel mark set $B$, the [martingale](martingale-split.md) $\mathbf1_{\{T\leq t,U\in B\}}-|B|(t\wedge T)$ follows directly from the memoryless residual lifetime. Information about the future mark or lifetime must not be inserted into the initial filtration.
