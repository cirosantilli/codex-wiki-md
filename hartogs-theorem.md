# Hartogs theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Set theory](set-theory-split.md)

For every set $X$ there is a least ordinal $h(X)$ that does not inject into $X$.

The least ordinal supplied by the theorem is the [Hartogs number](hartogs-number.md).
