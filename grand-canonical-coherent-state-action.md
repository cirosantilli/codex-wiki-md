# Grand-canonical coherent-state action

↑ **Parent:** [Coherent-state time slicing](coherent-state-time-slicing.md)

Insert coherent-state completeness between short factors of $e^{-\beta(H-\mu N)}$. The adjacent bra and ket labels determine the normal-ordered symbol $H_N(\bar\psi_{j+1},\psi_j)$, while overlaps generate the derivative term. Bosonic labels are complex and periodic in imaginary time; fermionic labels are independent [Grassmann variables](grassmann-variable.md) and antiperiodic. The continuum shorthand is defined by that temporal ordering and measure, not an arbitrary substitution of commuting fields for operators.[§§3.1–3.2, coherent-state time slices and trace](physics-215c-quantum-field-theory-part-3-by-john-mcgreevy.md)
