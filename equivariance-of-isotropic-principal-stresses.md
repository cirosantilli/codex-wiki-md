# Equivariance of isotropic principal stresses

↑ **Parent:** [Coaxiality of isotropic elastic stress](coaxiality-of-isotropic-elastic-stress.md)

For an objective [material isotropy](material-isotropy.md) law, permutation of the [principal stretches](principal-stretch.md) permutes the labelled [principal stresses](principal-stress.md). This follows by conjugating the [left stretch tensor](left-stretch-tensor.md) with signed permutation [rotation matrices](rotation-matrix.md). Each principal stress is symmetric in the other two stretches, but need not be symmetric in all three. The law $\sigma=V^2$ provides the example $\sigma_i=\lambda_i^2$. In an invariant representation $\sigma=\beta_0I+\beta_1V+\beta_2V^2$, the scalar coefficients are symmetric functions of the unordered stretches.
