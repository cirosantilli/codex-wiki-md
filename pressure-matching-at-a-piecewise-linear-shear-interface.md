# Pressure matching at a piecewise-linear shear interface

↑ **Parent:** [Rayleigh equation for inviscid shear flow](rayleigh-equation-for-inviscid-shear-flow.md)

For a [normal mode](normal-mode.md) of an inviscid parallel flow, the perturbation [pressure](pressure.md) is $p=-(U-c)\phi'+U'\phi$. At an interface where $U$ is continuous but $U'$ jumps, continuity of normal velocity and [pressure](pressure.md) gives continuity of $\phi$ and the displayed flux. Consequently $(U-c)[\phi']=[U']\phi$. A [sinuous mode of a planar jet](sinuous-mode-of-a-planar-jet.md) may therefore have a cusp at a symmetric velocity-profile corner: even parity alone does not force its one-sided derivative to vanish.
