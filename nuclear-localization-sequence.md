# Nuclear localization sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear pore complex](nuclear-pore-complex.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_localization_sequence)

A [nuclear localization sequence](nuclear-localization-sequence.md) is an ordered stretch of [amino acid residues](amino-acid-residue.md) that can direct [protein](protein-split.md) import into a [cell nucleus](cell-nucleus.md). In the classical pathway, [importin](importin.md) α recognizes the [nuclear localization sequence](nuclear-localization-sequence.md), and [importin](importin.md) β mediates passage through the [nuclear pore complex](nuclear-pore-complex.md). Other recognition pathways exist. An exposed [nuclear localization sequence](nuclear-localization-sequence.md) is a targeting feature, not a guarantee of import under every condition.[Selective Transport of Proteins to and from the Nucleus, Figure 8.9; Regulation of Nuclear Protein Import](the-cell-a-molecular-approach-2nd-edition-by-geoffrey-m-cooper.md)

[RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) export is a different cargo pathway: [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) travels associated with [proteins](protein-split.md), as a [ribonucleoprotein](ribonucleoprotein.md), rather than being imported by its own [nuclear localization sequence](nuclear-localization-sequence.md). Export of [messenger RNA](messenger-rna.md) connects nuclear [transcription](transcription-biology.md) to cytoplasmic [biological translation](translation-biology.md).[Transport of RNAs, opening paragraphs](the-cell-a-molecular-approach-2nd-edition-by-geoffrey-m-cooper.md)
