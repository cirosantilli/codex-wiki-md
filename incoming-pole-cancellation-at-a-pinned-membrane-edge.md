# Incoming-pole cancellation at a pinned membrane edge

↑ **Parent:** [Fluid-loaded membrane edge scattering](fluid-loaded-membrane-edge-scattering.md)

Reconstructing the left scattered displacement in [fluid-loaded membrane edge scattering](fluid-loaded-membrane-edge-scattering.md) divides by $m\omega^2-Tk^2$. The pole at the lower bare-membrane wavenumber $b=\omega\sqrt{m/T}$ must cancel to preserve outgoing half-plane analyticity. This gives a linear condition determining the otherwise unknown endpoint slope $A$. The prescribed incident pole in the total field is a different singularity.
