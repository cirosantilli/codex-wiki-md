<h1 id="fehling-s-test">Fehling's test</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Reducing sugar](reducing-sugar.md)

A test named after [Hermann von Fehling](hermann-von-fehling.md). Heating with [Fehling's reagent](fehling-s-reagent.md) can produce [copper(I) oxide](copper-i-oxide.md) from [reducing sugars](reducing-sugar.md); several different substances can give a positive result.
