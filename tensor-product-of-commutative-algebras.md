# Tensor product of commutative algebras

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor product of modules](tensor-product-of-modules.md)

For commutative $R$-algebras, their module [tensor product](tensor-product.md) becomes a commutative algebra by $(a\otimes b)(a'\otimes b')=aa'\otimes bb'$. Its [universal property](universal-property.md) is that pairs of compatible $R$-algebra maps from $A$ and $B$ into a commutative algebra $C$ correspond to maps $A\otimes_RB\to C$. This coproduct of algebras gives, contravariantly, the [fibre product of schemes](fiber-product-of-schemes.md) on affine charts.
