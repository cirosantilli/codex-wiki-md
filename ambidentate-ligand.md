# Ambidentate ligand

↑ **Parent:** [Ligand in coordination chemistry](ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md)

An [ambidentate ligand](ambidentate-ligand.md) has alternative donor atoms through which it can attach, producing different binding modes. [Nitrite](nitrite.md) can bind through N or O; changing that attachment gives [linkage isomerism](linkage-isomerism.md).
