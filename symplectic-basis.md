# Symplectic basis

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic vector space](symplectic-vector-space.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symplectic_basis)

A [symplectic basis](symplectic-basis.md) organizes a [nondegenerate](nondegenerate-bilinear-form.md) [alternating bilinear form](alternating-bilinear-form.md) into independent pairs. Let $(V,\omega)$ be a [finite-dimensional vector space](finite-dimensional-vector-space.md) over a [field](field.md) $K$, with $\omega$ a [nondegenerate bilinear form](nondegenerate-bilinear-form.md) satisfying $\omega(v,v)=0$ for every [vector](vector.md) $v$. Its [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) is even, say $2n$, and a [symplectic basis](symplectic-basis.md) is an ordered [basis](basis.md) $\mathcal B=(e_1,\ldots,e_n,f_1,\ldots,f_n)$ such that

$$
\omega(e_i,e_j)=\omega(f_i,f_j)=0,\qquad
\omega(e_i,f_j)=\delta_{ij},\qquad
\omega(f_i,e_j)=-\delta_{ij}.
$$

Here $\delta_{ij}$ is the [Kronecker delta](kronecker-delta.md). Each $(e_i,f_i)$ is a [hyperbolic pair](hyperbolic-pair.md); different pairs have zero mutual pairing. These conventions apply in every [characteristic](characteristic-of-a-field.md). In [characteristic](characteristic-of-a-field.md) two, $-1=1$, but the condition $\omega(v,v)=0$ is still essential: a [symmetric bilinear form](symmetric-bilinear-form.md) need not be an [alternating bilinear form](alternating-bilinear-form.md). The existence theorem and the [characteristic](characteristic-of-a-field.md) caveat are given in [Bilinear Forms by Keith Conrad](bilinear-forms-by-keith-conrad.md) (Keith Conrad, Bilinear Forms, §1, Theorem 1.6; §5, Theorems 5.1 and 5.4 and Definition 5.3).

The benefit is a common coordinate language for every [symplectic vector space](symplectic-vector-space.md) of the same finite [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) over the same [field](field.md). The [span](linear-span.md) of all $e_i$, and separately of all $f_i$, is a [Lagrangian subspace](lagrangian-subspace.md); each paired [span](linear-span.md) is a [symplectic subspace](symplectic-subspace.md). In [Hamiltonian mechanics](hamiltonian-mechanics.md), the corresponding pairing of position and [momentum](momentum.md) coordinates describes [phase space](phase-space.md) and [canonical transformations](canonical-transformation.md). On a [symplectic manifold](symplectic-manifold.md), a [symplectic basis](symplectic-basis.md) describes one [tangent space](tangent-space.md); [Darboux coordinates](darboux-chart.md) additionally describe a whole local neighborhood.[§1.1, pp. 82–83: paired bases and Lagrangian spans; §1.5, pp. 90–91: Darboux coordinates](handbook-of-differential-geometry-volume-ii/symplectic-geometry.md)
