# Pion electron-to-muon decay ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Leptonic pion decay](leptonic-pion-decay.md)

At tree level with universal [weak charged current](charged-current.md) coupling and massless neutrinos, the [leptonic pseudoscalar decay width](leptonic-pseudoscalar-decay-width.md) gives the displayed ratio. The [pion decay constant](pion-decay-constant.md), Fermi constant and common quark mixing coefficient cancel. The charged-lepton mass factor is [helicity suppression](helicity-suppression.md): a spin-zero parent cannot emit the two massless helicities selected by the chiral weak interaction. The ratio tests this suppression and lepton universality; scalar or pseudoscalar four-fermion interactions can modify it. Radiative corrections must be included for a precision comparison.
