# Volume fluctuations of a closed gas bubble

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal fluctuation](thermal-fluctuation.md)

For fixed [particle number](particle-number.md) and [temperature](temperature-split.md), a bubble with [surface tension](surface-tension-split.md) $\Gamma$ and [area](surface-area.md) $\alpha V^{2/3}$ has effective [availability](exergy.md) $A(V)=F_{\mathrm{gas}}(T,V,N)+\Gamma\alpha V^{2/3}+p_RV$. Its stationary condition is $p_{\mathrm{gas}}-p_R=(2/3)\Gamma\alpha V^{-1/3}$. Its curvature is $A''=V^{-1}[\kappa_T^{-1}-(2/9)\Gamma\alpha V^{-1/3}]$. Positive curvature is required for stable [Gaussian fluctuation approximation](gaussian-fluctuation-approximation.md). The [variance](variance-split.md) follows from [availability curvature and equilibrium fluctuations](availability-curvature-and-equilibrium-fluctuations.md). For an [ideal gas](ideal-gas-split.md), the restoring denominator becomes $p_R+(4/9)\Gamma\alpha V^{-1/3}>0$ for positive $p_R$. Treating the internal [pressure](pressure-split.md) as externally fixed instead of fixing the gas [particle number](particle-number.md) changes the stability problem.
