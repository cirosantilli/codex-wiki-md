# Cosmological look-back time

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmological distance measure](cosmological-distance-measure.md)

The look-back time is the cosmic-time interval between emission at [redshift](redshift.md) $z$ and reception today. Differentiating $1+z=R_0/R(t)$ gives $dt/dz=-1/[(1+z)H]$ and hence the displayed [integral](integral-split.md). The radial comoving-distance [integral](integral-split.md) instead has denominator $H$ alone; elapsed time and present spatial separation answer different operational questions.
