# Constant random variable

↑ **Parent:** [Random variable](random-variable-split.md)

A [constant random variable](constant-random-variable.md) takes the same fixed [real number](real-number.md) $c$ at every outcome in its [sample space](sample-space.md). Its [probability distribution](probability-distribution-split.md) puts mass one at $c$. An [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md) constant [random variable](random-variable-split.md) may differ on an [event](event.md) of [probability](probability.md) zero, while having the same [probability distribution](probability-distribution-split.md).
