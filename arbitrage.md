# Arbitrage

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical finance](mathematical-finance-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arbitrage)

An arbitrage is a zero-cost [self-financing portfolio](self-financing-portfolio.md) whose terminal payoff is nonnegative in every state and strictly positive with positive probability.
