# Cocharacter lattice of an algebraic torus

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic torus](algebraic-torus.md)

The cocharacter lattice $N=\operatorname{Hom}(\mathbb G_m,T)$ is dual to the [character lattice of an algebraic torus](character-lattice-of-an-algebraic-torus.md) under composition:

$$
\langle m,n\rangle\in\operatorname{Hom}(\mathbb G_m,\mathbb G_m)\cong\mathbb Z.
$$
