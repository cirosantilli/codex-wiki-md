# Hypergeometric Frobenius recurrence

↑ **Parent:** [Gauss hypergeometric equation](gauss-hypergeometric-equation.md)

For the [Gauss hypergeometric equation](gauss-hypergeometric-equation.md) with parameters $\alpha,\beta,\gamma$, a [Frobenius solution](frobenius-solution.md) $y=x^\sigma\sum_{n\geq0}a_nx^n$, $a_0\ne0$, has [indicial roots](indicial-root.md) $\sigma=0$ and $\sigma=1-\gamma$. Matching each successive power gives the displayed undivided recurrence. The usual ratio formula is valid only when its left coefficient is nonzero. At integer-separated roots, a zero coefficient requires a compatibility check as in an [undetermined coefficient at Frobenius resonance](undetermined-coefficient-at-frobenius-resonance.md); otherwise a logarithmic companion may be necessary. For example, $\gamma=0,\sigma=0$ gives $0=\alpha\beta a_0$ at $n=1$, obstructing that exponent-zero series when $\alpha\beta\ne0$.
