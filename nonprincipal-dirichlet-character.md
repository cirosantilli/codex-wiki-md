# Nonprincipal Dirichlet character

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet character](dirichlet-character.md)

A [nonprincipal Dirichlet character](nonprincipal-dirichlet-character.md) differs from the [principal Dirichlet character](principal-dirichlet-character.md). Equivalently, it is a nontrivial character on the unit group, so its sum over one full residue period is zero by [character orthogonality](character-orthogonality.md). Its [Dirichlet L-function](dirichlet-l-function.md) is entire by [Mellin continuation of a nonprincipal Dirichlet L-function](mellin-continuation-of-a-nonprincipal-dirichlet-l-function.md).
