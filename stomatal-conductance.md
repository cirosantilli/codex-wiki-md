# Stomatal conductance

↑ **Parent:** [Photosynthesis](photosynthesis.md)

Conductance for gas exchange through [stomata](stoma.md), influencing carbon uptake and water loss. Responses to carbon dioxide and atmospheric dryness can affect growth and water-use efficiency.
