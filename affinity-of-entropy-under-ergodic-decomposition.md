# Affinity of entropy under ergodic decomposition

↑ **Parent:** [Ergodic decomposition](ergodic-decomposition.md)

If $\mu=\int\nu\,d\tau(\nu)$ is an [ergodic decomposition](ergodic-decomposition.md), then the [Kolmogorov-Sinai entropy](kolmogorov-sinai-entropy.md) satisfies $h_\mu(T)=\int h_\nu(T)\,d\tau(\nu)$.
