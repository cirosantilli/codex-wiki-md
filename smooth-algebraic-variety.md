# Smooth algebraic variety

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic variety](algebraic-variety.md)

An algebraic variety is smooth over its ground field when its structural morphism is smooth. Over an algebraically closed field its local rings are regular; on an irreducible variety its tangent spaces all have the variety's [dimension of a scheme](dimension-of-a-scheme.md).
