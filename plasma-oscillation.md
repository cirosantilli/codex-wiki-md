# Plasma oscillation

↑ **Parent:** [Plasmon](plasmon.md)

A [plasma oscillation](plasma-oscillation.md) is a collective oscillation of charge density and its restoring electric field. In a three-dimensional bulk charged superfluid, long-range Coulomb forces give the longitudinal collective mode a nonzero long-wave plasma [frequency](frequency.md). Different geometry and electromagnetic [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md) can change that limit.
