# Zeroth homology group

↑ **Parent:** [Homology group](homology-group.md)

The zeroth homology group is the free [abelian group](abelian-group.md) on the [path components](path-component.md) of $X$. In particular, if $X$ has finitely many path components, then

$$
H_0(X;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z^{\#\pi_0(X)}.
$$
