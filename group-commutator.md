# Group commutator

↑ **Parent:** [Group operation](group-operation.md)

The [group commutator](group-commutator.md) measures the failure of two elements of a [group](group-split.md) to commute. Under the convention $[x,y]=x^{-1}y^{-1}xy$, it is the identity exactly when $xy=yx$. The [commutator subgroup](commutator-subgroup.md) is generated by these elements. The alternative convention $xyx^{-1}y^{-1}$ generates the same [commutator subgroup](commutator-subgroup.md); individual formulas must specify their convention.
