# Jaklevic Josephson interference experiment

↑ **Parent:** [DC SQUID](dc-squid.md)

The 1964 report established quantum [interference](interference-wave-propagation.md) in superconducting tunneling. It is an experimental counterpart to the phase-sensitive current predicted for coupled superconductors.[Primary experimental report, 1964](quantum-interference-effects-in-josephson-tunneling-by-r-c-jaklevic-and-others.md)
