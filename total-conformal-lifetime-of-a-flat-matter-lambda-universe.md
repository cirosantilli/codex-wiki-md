# Total conformal lifetime of a flat matter-Lambda universe

↑ **Parent:** [Total comoving visibility radius](total-comoving-visibility-radius.md)

Use $c=1$, $a_0=1$, positive [pressureless matter](pressureless-matter.md) and positive [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md), with $\Omega_m+\Omega_\Lambda=1$ and no radiation. The [Friedmann equation](friedmann-equations.md) gives $\chi_{\rm max}=H_0^{-1}\int_0^\infty dy/(\Omega_\Lambda+\Omega_my^3)^{1/2}$. Set $y=(\Omega_\Lambda/\Omega_m)^{1/3}u$, then $v=u^3$. The remaining integral is $\tfrac13\int_0^\infty v^{-2/3}(1+v)^{-1/2}\,dv=\tfrac13B(1/3,1/6)$, proving the formula by the [beta function](beta-function.md) integral. Its two endpoints converge for positive densities, but the lifetime diverges in either pure-fluid limit.
