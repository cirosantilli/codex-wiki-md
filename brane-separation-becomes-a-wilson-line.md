# Brane separation becomes a Wilson line

↑ **Parent:** [T-duality of D-brane boundary conditions](t-duality-of-d-brane-boundary-conditions.md)

Under [T-duality](t-duality.md), the positions $y_i$ of [D-branes](d-brane.md) transverse to a circle of radius $R$ become diagonal [Wilson lines](wilson-line.md) on branes wrapping the dual circle $R'=\alpha'/R$. Their holonomies are $\exp(i2\pi R'A_i)=\exp(iy_i/R)$. An [Open string stretched between D-branes](open-string-stretched-between-d-branes.md) with displacement $y_j-y_i+2\pi Rw$ becomes a string with shifted compact momentum $w/R'+A_j-A_i$. The stretching contribution and the shifted-momentum contribution to its squared mass are identical.
