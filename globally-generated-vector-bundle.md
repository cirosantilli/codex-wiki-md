# Globally generated vector bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Vector bundle](vector-bundle.md)

A [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) is globally generated when its [global sections](global-section.md) span every fibre. The evaluation map in the display expresses this condition. On a projective curve its space of [global sections](global-section.md) is finite-dimensional, so a finite collection of sections suffices.
