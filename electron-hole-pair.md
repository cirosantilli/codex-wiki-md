# Electron-hole pair

↑ **Parent:** [Electron hole](electron-hole.md)

An [electron-hole pair](electron-hole-pair.md) consists of an excited [electron](electron.md) and the unoccupied valence state described as an [electron hole](electron-hole.md). Promoting a valence [electron](electron.md) across a [semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md) [band gap](band-gap.md) creates such a pair without changing the total [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md). The carriers can separate under an [electric field](electric-field-split.md), or undergo [electron-hole recombination](electron-hole-recombination.md). A bound electron-hole pair is an [exciton](exciton.md); an arbitrary unbound pair need not be an exciton.
