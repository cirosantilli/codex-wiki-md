# Flux average for a translating lubrication gap

↑ **Parent:** [Pressure recovery condition in lubrication flow](pressure-recovery-condition-in-lubrication-flow.md)

In dimensionless [Couette-Poiseuille flow in a thin gap](couette-poiseuille-flow-in-a-thin-gap.md) with lower-wall [velocity](velocity.md) one and stationary upper wall, the conserved [volume flux](volumetric-flow-rate.md) satisfies $Q=h/2-h^3p_x/12$. Equal pressures at the two ends imply $\int p_xdx=0$ and give the displayed expression. It is a weighted average of the positive gap height with weight $h^{-3}$, so $\min h\leq2Q\leq\max h$. The local [pressure gradient](pressure-gradient.md) has the sign of $h-2Q$, making the [flux](flux.md) constraint useful for locating [pressure](pressure-split.md) extrema without solving for the [pressure](pressure-split.md) explicitly.
