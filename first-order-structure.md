# First-order structure

↑ **Parent:** [First-order logic](first-order-logic.md)

A structure supplies a nonempty domain and an interpretation of every constant, operation, and relation symbol in its language.

This is a [structure](structure-mathematical-logic.md) for a [first-order language](first-order-language.md), with finitary interpretations of its nonlogical symbols.
