# Brittle-ductile transition

↑ **Parent:** [Creep (deformation)](creep-deformation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brittle-ductile_transition)

A change from deformation dominated by [brittle fracture](brittle-fracture.md) or frictional faulting to deformation dominated by ductile [material creep](creep-deformation.md), depending on [temperature](temperature-split.md), [strain rate](strain-rate.md), composition and fluids.
