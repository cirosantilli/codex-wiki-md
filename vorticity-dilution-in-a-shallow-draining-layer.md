# Vorticity dilution in a shallow draining layer

↑ **Parent:** [Shallow-water potential vorticity](shallow-water-potential-vorticity.md)

For inviscid nonrotating [shallow water equations](shallow-water-equations.md), columnar horizontal flow has material [vorticity](vorticity-split.md) equation $D\zeta/Dt=-\zeta\nabla_h\cdot\mathbf u_h$ and depth equation $Dh/Dt=-h\nabla_h\cdot\mathbf u_h$. Thus $\zeta/h$ is conserved, not $\zeta$ itself. Squashing a column to much smaller depth decreases its vertical vorticity. This permits a late draining flow to approximate an irrotational $u_\theta=C/r$ profile outside the drain, despite nonzero initial solid-body vorticity. At finite nonzero depth the residual vorticity need not be exactly zero. In a rotating frame, replace $\zeta$ by absolute vorticity $\zeta+f$ in the conserved ratio. [Kelvin's circulation theorem](kelvin-s-circulation-theorem.md) remains compatible with these changes because it follows a material loop, not a fixed spatial circle.
