# Activation barriers of the Adler phase equation

↑ **Parent:** [Adler phase equation](adler-phase-equation.md)

For $0<\omega<\epsilon$, set $\alpha=\arcsin(\omega/\epsilon)$ and $\kappa=\sqrt{\epsilon^2-\omega^2}$. The neighboring saddles of the [tilted washboard potential](tilted-washboard-potential.md) have barriers $\Delta V_+=2\kappa-\omega(\pi-2\alpha)$ and $\Delta V_-=2\kappa+\omega(\pi+2\alpha)$ above the minimum at $\alpha$. Their difference is $2\pi\omega$; both have curvature $-\kappa$, so forward and backward [Kramers escape rates](kramers-escape-rate.md) have equal leading prefactors.
