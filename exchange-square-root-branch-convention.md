# Exchange square-root branch convention

↑ **Parent:** [Square root of the SWAP gate](square-root-of-the-swap-gate.md)

Let $P_-$ project onto $(|01\rangle-|10\rangle)/\sqrt2$ and let $P_+=I-P_-$ project onto the symmetric sector. An isotropic [exchange interaction](exchange-interaction.md), with a chosen common energy offset, gives the continuous family displayed above. A full exchange has $\theta=\pi$ and equals [SWAP gate](swap-gate.md); the conventional half exchange has $\theta=\pi/2$ and equals $P_++iP_-$. Reversing the sign of the exchange gives the same full exchange at $\theta=-\pi$ but the inverse half exchange at $\theta=-\pi/2$. Thus even continuous time-independent [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) evolution and unchanged $|00\rangle,|11\rangle$ do not let the full-time truth table determine a unique half-time gate. A chosen generator or spectral branch is necessary. The half-exchange [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $1,i$ are the conventional principal scalar square-root choices for $1,-1$; no analytic matrix-function branch across the negative real axis is being assumed.
