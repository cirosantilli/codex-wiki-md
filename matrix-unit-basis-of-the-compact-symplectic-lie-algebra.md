# Matrix-unit basis of the compact symplectic Lie algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Compact symplectic Lie algebra](compact-symplectic-lie-algebra.md)

In its block form, a [matrix-unit basis of the unitary Lie algebra](matrix-unit-basis-of-the-unitary-lie-algebra.md) spans the anti-Hermitian block $A$, with the corresponding conjugate block below. Independent real and imaginary symmetric [matrix units](matrix-unit.md) span $B$, with lower block $-\overline B$. These give $n^2+n(n+1)$ independent real generators, including diagonal symmetric entries.
