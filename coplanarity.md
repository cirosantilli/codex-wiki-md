# Coplanarity

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean geometry](euclidean-geometry-split.md)

Points are coplanar when they belong to a common [plane](plane.md). For three vectors based at the origin of three-dimensional [Euclidean space](euclidean-norm.md), this means their span has [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) at most two. Equivalently their [scalar triple product](scalar-triple-product.md), and hence the [determinant](determinant.md) with those vectors as columns, is zero. This includes dependent or zero vectors.
