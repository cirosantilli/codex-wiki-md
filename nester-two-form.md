# Nester two-form

↑ **Parent:** [Positive energy theorem](positive-energy-theorem.md)

This antisymmetric spinor bilinear converts the [positive energy theorem](positive-energy-theorem.md) into a boundary-charge identity. Its divergence is $2(\nabla_\nu\bar\epsilon)\gamma^{\mu\nu\rho}\nabla_\rho\epsilon+G^{\mu\nu}\bar\epsilon\gamma_\nu\epsilon$ in the mostly-plus conventions specified here. On a spacelike hypersurface the first term is twice the difference between the squared spatial [covariant derivative](covariant-derivative.md) and the squared spatial Dirac operator. The [Witten spinor equation](witten-spinor-equation.md) removes the negative square, while the [dominant energy condition](dominant-energy-condition.md) controls the [curvature](curvature.md) term through the Einstein constraints.
