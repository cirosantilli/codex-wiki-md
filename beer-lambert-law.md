# Beer-Lambert law

↑ **Parent:** [Absorbance](absorbance.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beer–Lambert_law)

The [Beer-Lambert law](beer-lambert-law.md) relates [absorbance](absorbance.md) $A$ to an absorbing species' [concentration](concentration.md) $c$ and path length $l$ by $A=\varepsilon lc$ in a homogeneous medium using sufficiently narrow-band light. The coefficient $\varepsilon$ depends on the species, [wavelength](wavelength.md) and [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md) conditions. This makes [Ellman's reagent](ellman-s-reagent.md) useful for quantitative [thiol](thiol.md) assays once its released colored species has been calibrated. [IUPAC Gold Book: Beer-Lambert law and validity conditions](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/B00626)
