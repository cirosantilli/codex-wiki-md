# Derangement of a permutation

↑ **Parent:** [Permutation](permutation.md)

A [permutation](permutation.md) with no fixed points. Applying the [inclusion-exclusion principle](inclusion-exclusion-principle.md) to the events that each element is fixed gives $D_n=n!\sum_{k=0}^n(-1)^k/k!$. Therefore $D_n/n!\to e^{-1}$, and the alternating-series remainder is at most $1/(n+1)!$.
