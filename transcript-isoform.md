# Transcript isoform

↑ **Parent:** [Gene expression](gene-expression.md)

One of the distinct [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) products associated with a [gene](gene.md), differing through initiation, [RNA splicing](rna-splicing.md), end formation or other processing. Different [transcript isoforms](transcript-isoform.md) need not encode different [proteins](protein-split.md).
