# Symbolic dynamics

↑ **Parent:** [Topological dynamics](topological-dynamics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symbolic_dynamics)

Symbolic dynamics studies [dynamical systems](dynamical-systems-split.md) whose points are [sequences](sequence.md) of symbols from an [alphabet](alphabet.md) and whose evolution is a shift. A finite [alphabet](alphabet.md) with the [discrete topology](discrete-space.md) gives its [sequence](sequence.md) space a [product topology](product-topology.md).
