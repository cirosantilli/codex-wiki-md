# Uncompetitive inhibition

↑ **Parent:** [Reversible enzyme inhibition](reversible-enzyme-inhibition.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uncompetitive_inhibition)

The complementary limit of the branching model permits inhibitor binding only to the [enzyme-substrate complex](enzyme-substrate-complex.md): set $K_i\to\infty$, hence $\alpha=1$, with finite $K_i'$. Then both $V_{\max,\rm app}$ and $K_{M,\rm app}$ decrease by $\alpha'$, while their ratio stays fixed. This limit helps distinguish preferential binding to $ES$ in [mixed inhibition](mixed-inhibition.md) from exclusive binding to $ES$ in [uncompetitive inhibition](uncompetitive-inhibition.md).
