# Bosonic mode mixing implementability

↑ **Parent:** [Bogoliubov transformation](bogoliubov-transformation.md)

A bounded invertible bosonic [Bogoliubov transformation](bogoliubov-transformation.md) is unitarily implementable in the usual [bosonic Fock space](bosonic-fock-space.md) precisely when its creation-mixing part is a [Hilbert-Schmidt operator](hilbert-schmidt-operator.md). Without this condition, the transformation still preserves the algebraic [canonical commutation relations](canonical-commutation-relation.md), but its two vacua need not be vectors in a common [Fock space](fock-space.md). Equal nonzero squeezing in countably many independent modes is a simple counterexample.
