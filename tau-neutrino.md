# Tau neutrino

↑ **Parent:** [Neutrino](neutrino.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tau_neutrino)

The [neutrino flavor](neutrino-flavor.md) associated with the [tau lepton](tau-particle.md) in the [weak charged current](charged-current.md). Its direct detection through interactions producing a [tau lepton](tau-particle.md) was announced by the DONUT experiment in 2000.[July 21, 2000 announcement](physicists-find-first-direct-evidence-for-tau-neutrino-at-fermilab-by-fermilab.md)
