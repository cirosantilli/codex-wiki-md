# Torsion-free group

↑ **Parent:** [Group](group-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torsion-free_group)

A torsion-free group is a [group](group-split.md) in which the [identity element](identity-element.md) is the only element of finite [order of a group element](order-of-a-group-element.md).
