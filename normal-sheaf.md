# Normal sheaf

↑ **Parent:** [Conormal sheaf](conormal-sheaf.md)

For a [closed immersion](closed-immersion.md) $i:Z\hookrightarrow X$ with [ideal sheaf](ideal-sheaf-of-a-closed-subscheme.md) $\mathcal I$, the normal sheaf is $\mathcal N_{Z/X}=\mathcal Hom_{\mathcal O_Z}(\mathcal I/\mathcal I^2,\mathcal O_Z)$. It is the dual of the [conormal sheaf](conormal-sheaf.md). For a regular embedding it is a [locally free sheaf](locally-free-sheaf.md); in the smooth case it agrees with the [normal bundle](normal-bundle.md). Without regularity it need not be locally free, but its [global sections](global-section.md) still classify [first-order embedded deformations](first-order-embedded-deformation.md).
