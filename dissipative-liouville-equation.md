# Dissipative Liouville equation

↑ **Parent:** [Kinetic theory](kinetic-theory-split.md)

Conservation of [probability](probability.md) under a deterministic [characteristic flow map](characteristic-flow-map.md) with [velocity](velocity.md) $b(z,t)$ gives the displayed [continuity equation](continuity-equation.md) on [phase space](phase-space.md). Along a characteristic, $dP/dt=-P\nabla_z\cdot b$. Unlike the [Hamiltonian Liouville equation](hamiltonian-liouville-equation.md), it allows contraction of phase-space volume and increasing density along trajectories. It still preserves total [probability](probability.md) when boundary fluxes vanish.
