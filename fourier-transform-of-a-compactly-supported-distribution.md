# Fourier transform of a compactly supported distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Compactly supported distribution](compactly-supported-distribution.md)

For $u\in\mathcal E'(\mathbb R^n)$, its [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) is the [smooth function](smooth-function.md)

$$
\widehat u(\lambda)=\langle u(x),e^{-i\lambda\cdot x}\rangle.
$$

It has at most [polynomial growth](polynomial-growth.md), and its extension to complex frequency is controlled more precisely by the [Paley–Wiener–Schwartz theorem](paley-wiener-schwartz-theorem.md).
