# Gain narrowing

↑ **Parent:** [Gain bandwidth](gain-bandwidth.md)

Narrowing of a pulse spectrum when the central [frequencies](frequency.md) receive larger [optical gain](optical-gain.md) than its wings. Phase correction alone cannot restore the suppressed spectrum.
