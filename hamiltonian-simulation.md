# Hamiltonian simulation

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum theory](quantum-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamiltonian_simulation)

Hamiltonian simulation constructs a [quantum circuit](quantum-circuit-split.md) approximating the time-evolution operator $e^{-itH}$ generated by a [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) $H$, with cost controlled by the evolution time, requested precision, and structure of $H$.
