# Basis extension

↑ **Parent:** [Basis](basis.md)

Every [linearly independent](linear-independence.md) subset of a finite-dimensional [vector space](vector-space-split.md) extends to a [basis](basis.md). Starting with its vectors, add a vector outside their current [span](linear-span.md) until the span is the whole space. Each addition preserves [linear independence](linear-independence.md), and the process stops because the dimension is finite. This also lets a [linear functional](linear-functional.md) on a [vector subspace](vector-subspace.md) extend to the ambient space by assigning arbitrary values on the additional basis vectors.
