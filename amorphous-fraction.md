# Amorphous fraction

↑ **Parent:** [Radiation-induced amorphization](radiation-induced-amorphization.md)

The fraction of a material lacking the specified long-range crystalline order. It is a structural measure, not directly a dissolved [mass fraction](mass-fraction.md) or radionuclide release fraction.
