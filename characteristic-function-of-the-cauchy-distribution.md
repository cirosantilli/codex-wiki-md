# Characteristic function of the Cauchy distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Cauchy distribution](cauchy-distribution.md)

For $X\sim\operatorname{Cauchy}(x_0,\gamma)$,

$$
\mathbb E[e^{itX}]=e^{ix_0t-\gamma|t|}.
$$

In particular, the [Standard Cauchy distribution](standard-cauchy-distribution.md) has characteristic function $e^{-|t|}$.
