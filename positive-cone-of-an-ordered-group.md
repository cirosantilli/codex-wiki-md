# Positive cone of an ordered group

↑ **Parent:** [Partially ordered group](partially-ordered-group-split.md)

The nonnegative cone determines the [partial order](partially-ordered-set.md) by $a\le b\iff a^{-1}b\in G_{\ge1}$. It contains the [identity element](identity-element.md), is closed under products and [conjugation](conjugation.md), and intersects its inverse only in the identity. Conversely these conditions define a two-sided invariant [partial order](partially-ordered-set.md). Its strictly positive part omits the identity; conventions for $G^+$ differ, so specify which is intended.
