# Left shift

↑ **Parent:** [Full shift](full-shift.md)

On a two-sided [full shift](full-shift.md), the [left shift](left-shift.md) is the [homeomorphism](homeomorphism.md)

$$
(\mathcal Lz)(j)=z(j+1).
$$

Its inverse sends $z(j)$ to $z(j-1)$. On a one-sided [sequence](sequence.md) space, the same forward shift is generally not invertible.
