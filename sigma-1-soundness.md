# Sigma-1 soundness

↑ **Parent:** [Soundness of an arithmetic theory](soundness-of-an-arithmetic-theory.md)

A theory has Sigma-1 soundness if every [Sigma-1 formula](sigma-1-formula.md) that is a closed theorem is true in the standard natural-number structure. It is weaker than full [arithmetic soundness](soundness-of-an-arithmetic-theory.md). Under bounded completeness it is equivalent to [one-consistency](one-consistency.md). The standard interpretation matters: an existential statement can have a witness in a nonstandard model while having no standard witness.
