# Instability threshold of a gravitating particle lattice

↑ **Parent:** [Infinite gravitating particle lattice](infinite-gravitating-particle-lattice.md)

For $q=GmF(hk)/(h^3\Omega^2)$, the squared dimensionless growth rates are $s^2/\Omega^2=(q-1\pm\sqrt{9q^2-26q+1})/2$. Exponential growth begins for $q>(13-4\sqrt{10})/9$. Since the [gravitating lattice Fourier kernel](gravitating-lattice-fourier-kernel.md) is largest at $hk=\pi$, the full infinite lattice is exponentially unstable when $Gm/(h^3\Omega^2)>(13-4\sqrt{10})/[9F(\pi)]$. Marginal repeated imaginary roots and zero-frequency secular modes require separate treatment.
