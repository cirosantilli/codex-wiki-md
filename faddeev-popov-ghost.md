# Faddeev-Popov ghost

↑ **Parent:** [Faddeev-Popov determinant](faddeev-popov-determinant.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faddeev–Popov_ghost)

A Faddeev-Popov ghost field is a Grassmann-valued scalar field whose Gaussian functional integral represents the [Faddeev-Popov determinant](faddeev-popov-determinant.md). Ghosts occur only on internal lines and cancel unphysical gauge-field contributions.
