# Hessian criterion for convexity

↑ **Parent:** [Convex function](convex-function.md)

On an open [convex set](convex-set-split.md), a twice-differentiable [real-valued function](real-valued-function.md) is [convex](convex-function.md) if and only if its [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) is a [positive semidefinite matrix](positive-semidefinite-matrix.md) at every point. In particular continuous second partial [derivatives](derivative.md) suffice for the differentiability hypothesis. Restrict $g$ to any line segment: $\phi(t)=g(x+tv)$ has $\phi''(t)=v^{\mathsf T}H(g)(x+tv)v$. The [second-derivative criterion for convexity](second-derivative-criterion-for-convexity.md) proves both implications, because [convexity](convex-function.md) is equivalent to [convexity](convex-function.md) of all such restrictions.
