# Tetracode length-isospectral lattice construction

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean lattice](euclidean-lattice.md)

For the skew matrix $S=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&1&1&1\\-1&0&-1&1\\-1&1&0&-1\\-1&-1&1&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)$, both [Euclidean lattices](euclidean-lattice.md) reduce to the [tetracode](tetracode.md) modulo $3$. A reflection depending on the reduction coset matches their vectors coordinatewise in absolute value. Their weighted [length spectra](length-spectrum.md) agree for all positive diagonal axis weights. Rationally independent weights force any possible [linear isometry](linear-isometry-of-hilbert-spaces.md) to be a coordinate sign change; the code then forces an overall sign, which does not identify the two lattices.
