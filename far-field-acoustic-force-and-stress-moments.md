# Far-field acoustic force and stress moments

↑ **Parent:** [Lighthill acoustic analogy](lighthill-acoustic-analogy.md)

For compact [Lighthill stress tensor](lighthill-stress-tensor.md) and applied-force distributions, define $S_{ij}=\int T_{ij}\,d^3y$ and $F_i=\int f_i\,d^3y$. Differentiating the [retarded acoustic Green function](retarded-acoustic-green-function.md) at far distance gives the displayed leading radiation field, evaluated at $t-r/c_0$. Source compactness requires its size to be small compared with the acoustic wavelength, not merely with the observer distance.
