# Symplectic cylinder

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic embedding](symplectic-embedding.md)

The standard symplectic cylinder restricts one conjugate coordinate pair to a disk and leaves the remaining symplectic coordinates unrestricted. Its normalized [symplectic capacity](symplectic-capacity.md) is $\pi R^2$, despite its infinite [symplectic volume](symplectic-volume.md) when $n>1$.
