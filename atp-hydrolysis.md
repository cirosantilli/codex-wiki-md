# ATP hydrolysis

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrolysis](hydrolysis.md)

[ATP hydrolysis](atp-hydrolysis.md) converts [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) with [water](water.md) to products commonly including [ADP](adenosine-diphosphate.md) and inorganic [phosphate](phosphate.md). Its available [Gibbs free energy](gibbs-free-energy.md) depends on chemical conditions and can drive a coupled process; ATP binding and hydrolysis can have distinct structural roles.
