<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

In [inelastic scattering](../../../../../inelastic-scattering.md), the probe transfers [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) as well as [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) to the specimen. A neutron, X-ray or [electron](../../../../../electron.md) can create or annihilate a [phonon](../../../../../phonon.md), while electronic or magnetic excitations provide other channels. Measuring the incident and outgoing energies resolves the transfer $\hbar\omega$; their wavevectors determine [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) transfer $\hbar\mathbf Q$. In a crystal, phonon [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) is conserved modulo a [reciprocal lattice](../../../../../reciprocal-lattice.md) vector. Resolved spectra therefore probe excitation dispersions and lifetimes, rather than only average atomic positions.

[Thermal diffuse scattering](../../../../../thermal-diffuse-scattering.md) arises from thermally populated lattice displacements and their correlations. The equilibrium lattice produces sharp Bragg peaks, but displacements redistribute part of the scattering into diffuse intensity. To one-phonon order a mode contribution contains a displacement projection and thermal occupation factors of the form

$$
I_\nu(\mathbf Q)\propto\frac{|\mathbf Q\cdot\mathbf e_{\mathbf q\nu}|^2}{\omega_{\mathbf q\nu}}
\left[n_B(\omega_{\mathbf q\nu})+\frac12\right],
$$

with atomic scattering weights, phases and geometrical factors understood. Stokes and anti-Stokes energy-resolved components carry $n_B+1$ and $n_B$ respectively; the displayed combination describes their summed scale. At high [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), $n_B\simeq k_BT/(\hbar\omega)$, so soft modes and their [polarization](../../../../../polarization-waves.md) strongly influence the pattern. Zero-point motion also contributes at low [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md). An energy-integrating [diffraction](../../../../../diffraction.md) image can record this inelastic intensity as a diffuse background without resolving its small [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) transfers. Static disorder gives additional diffuse scattering, so diffuse intensity alone does not establish a thermal mechanism.
