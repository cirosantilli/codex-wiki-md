In [X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy](../../../../../x-ray-photoelectron-spectroscopy.md), the [electron](../../../../../electron.md) [binding energy](../../../../../binding-energy.md) relative to the chosen reference follows $E_B=h\nu-E_K-\phi_{\rm analyzer}$. A [core-level chemical shift](../../../../../core-level-chemical-shift.md) is a change in a core peak's [binding energy](../../../../../binding-energy.md) between environments. Initial-state contributions include charge redistribution, bonding, the local electrostatic potential and chemical-potential changes. Final-state contributions include relaxation and screening of the newly created core hole. The measured shift contains both; it is not a direct meter of an integer oxidation state.

For a related chemical series, [electron](../../../../../electron.md) withdrawal often raises a core [binding energy](../../../../../binding-energy.md), but screening and referencing can change that trend. A [surface core-level shift](../../../../../surface-core-level-shift.md) can separate surface atoms from bulk atoms because coordination and screening differ. Adsorption can introduce another component for chemically bound surface atoms. Spin-orbit doublets, multiplet or satellite features must be distinguished from separate chemical environments.

Reliable comparison needs a consistent Fermi-level or other justified [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) reference, compensation or diagnosis of sample charging, adequate [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) resolution and a stated line-shape/background model. Angle or photon-energy variation changes surface weighting through [electron](../../../../../electron.md) escape depth, helping distinguish surface and bulk assignments. A shifted, broader or overlapping peak alone does not uniquely identify a chemical species or its concentration.
