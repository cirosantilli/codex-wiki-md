# Astrophysical volume filling factor

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal bremsstrahlung](thermal-bremsstrahlung.md)

The fraction $f_V$ of a geometric volume occupied by a specified emitting gas phase is its volume filling factor. For approximately uniform [density](density.md) within that phase, a density-squared [luminosity](luminosity.md) scales as $n_e^2f_VV$. Without knowledge of $f_V$, emission gives an rms-density combination rather than a unique mean gas [density](density.md). This filling factor is distinct from the fraction of a [telescope](telescope.md) beam occupied on the sky.
