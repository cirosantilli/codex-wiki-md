# Complement of a normal subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md)

A group complement to a [normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md) $N\triangleleft G$ is a subgroup $H$ with $G=NH$ and $N\cap H=1$. Projection identifies $H$ with $G/N$, and conjugation gives $G=N\rtimes H$. This is a subgroup notion, not the set-theoretic complement of $N$.
