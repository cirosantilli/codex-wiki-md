# Single-channel current-voltage relation

↑ **Parent:** [Single-channel recording](single-channel-recording.md)

For an ideal open [ion channel](ion-channel.md) with constant [electrical conductance](electrical-conductance.md) $g$, [membrane potential](membrane-potential-split.md) $V$ and [reversal potential](reversal-potential.md) $E_{\mathrm{rev}}$, define outward [electric current](electric-current.md) as positive:

$$
i=g(V-E_{\mathrm{rev}}).
$$

This is an [Ohm's law](ohm-s-law.md) model of the open state, not a universal channel law or a rule for opening [probability](probability.md). In the synthetic figure, $g=10\,\mathrm{pS}$ and $E_{\mathrm{rev}}=0\,\mathrm{mV}$. Because $1\,\mathrm{pS}\cdot1\,\mathrm{mV}=10^{-3}\,\mathrm{pA}$, the open-state [electric currents](electric-current.md) at $-80$ and $-40\,\mathrm{mV}$ are $-0.8$ and $-0.4\,\mathrm{pA}$. Zero [electric current](electric-current.md) at the [reversal potential](reversal-potential.md) does not imply a closed [ion channel](ion-channel.md). Real [ion channels](ion-channel.md) can have nonlinear responses, so the straight line is an explicit idealization.
