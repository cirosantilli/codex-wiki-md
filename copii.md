# COPII

↑ **Parent:** [Membrane transport](membrane-transport.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COPII)

[COPII](copii.md) is a vesicle-coat system associated with export from the [endoplasmic reticulum](endoplasmic-reticulum.md). Sar1 recruits an inner Sec23/Sec24 layer, and Sec13/Sec31 forms an outer coat. Cargo selection and membrane deformation are coupled but experimentally separable.
