# Bacterial outer membrane

↑ **Parent:** [Cell envelope](cell-envelope.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bacterial_outer_membrane)

A [bacterial outer membrane](bacterial-outer-membrane.md) is an additional membrane outside the [peptidoglycan](peptidoglycan.md) in typical [Gram-negative bacteria](gram-negative-bacteria.md). It adds a barrier while the inner [cell membrane](cell-membrane.md) remains the boundary of the [cytoplasm](cytoplasm.md).
