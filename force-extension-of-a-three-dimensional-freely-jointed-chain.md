# Force-extension of a three-dimensional freely jointed chain

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal chain](ideal-chain.md)

Independent link orientations make the [canonical partition function](canonical-partition-function.md) $Z_1^N$, with the one-link integral defining the [Langevin function](langevin-function.md). Differentiating its logarithm with respect to force gives $L=Nb\mathscr L(Fb/(k_BT))$. At high extension, $Nb-L\sim Nk_BT/F$, so the force diverges inversely with remaining length deficit. This requires rigid links and excludes axial bond stretching.
