# Heat capacity for a two-dimensional linear fermion dispersion

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum ideal-gas statistics](quantum-ideal-gas-statistics-split.md)

For $g$ copies of a positive-energy branch $E=\hbar v|\mathbf k|$ in [area](surface-area.md) $A$, the [density of states](density-of-states.md) is $D(E)=gAE/(2\pi\hbar^2v^2)$. At zero [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md), the thermal [energy](energy-split.md) is $3g\zeta(3)A(k_BT)^3/(4\pi\hbar^2v^2)$, giving the displayed fixed-zero-chemical-potential [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md). If a symmetric filled negative-energy band also supplies holes, its excitation [energy](energy-split.md) contributes an equal amount. Counting cones, [spin](spin.md) species and holes explicitly is necessary before assigning an absolute numerical coefficient.
