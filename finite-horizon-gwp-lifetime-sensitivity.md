# Finite-horizon GWP lifetime sensitivity

↑ **Parent:** [Global warming potential](global-warming-potential.md)

For a gas pulse with unchanged [greenhouse gas radiative efficiency](greenhouse-gas-radiative-efficiency.md) and decay $e^{-t/\tau}$, its integrated forcing through $H$ is proportional to $\tau(1-e^{-H/\tau})$. Comparing two such lifetimes at fixed carbon-dioxide reference gives the ratio of these two factors. A fractional lifetime increase is not generally the same fractional GWP increase at finite $H$.
