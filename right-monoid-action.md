# Right monoid action

↑ **Parent:** [Monoid action](monoid-action.md)

A right [monoid action](monoid-action.md) has $x1=x$ and $(xm)n=x(mn)$. It is a contravariant [functor](functor.md) from the one-object category of the [monoid](monoid.md) to sets. The regular right action is multiplication on $M$ itself. Products of right actions use the diagonal action.
