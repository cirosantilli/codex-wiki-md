<h1 id="jurin-s-law">Jurin's law</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Capillary action](capillary-action.md)

For a circular capillary of inner radius $r$, balance the vertical force $2\pi r\gamma\cos\theta$ against the hydrostatic column weight $\rho g\pi r^2h$. The displayed signed height follows, neglecting meniscus-volume corrections. A wetting liquid with $\theta<\pi/2$ rises, whereas a nonwetting liquid with $\theta>\pi/2$ is depressed. The [contact angle](contact-angle.md) is measured through the liquid. The expression uses radius rather than diameter and presumes equilibrium at the specified wall chemistry.
