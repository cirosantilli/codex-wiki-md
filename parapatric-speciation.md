# Parapatric speciation

↑ **Parent:** [Speciation](speciation.md)

Divergence of adjacent populations into distinct [species](species.md) while some [gene flow](gene-flow.md) persists across their contact region. Spatially varying [natural selection](natural-selection.md) and the development of [reproductive isolation](reproductive-isolation.md) can maintain differences despite exchange. It is a geographical mode distinct from complete separation in [allopatric speciation](allopatric-speciation.md) and initial coexistence without such a barrier in [sympatric speciation](sympatric-speciation.md).
