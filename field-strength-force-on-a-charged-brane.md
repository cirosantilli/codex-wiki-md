# Field-strength force on a charged brane

↑ **Parent:** [Wess-Zumino brane coupling](wess-zumino-brane-coupling.md)

For $F=dA$ and an embedding variation $V$, the pullback [Lie derivative](lie-derivative-of-a-differential-form.md) and [Cartan's magic formula](cartan-s-magic-formula.md) give the displayed variation. Consequently the interior force from the [Wess-Zumino brane coupling](wess-zumino-brane-coupling.md) depends only on the gauge-invariant field strength. A change $A\mapsto A+d\Lambda$ changes the action only on the boundary, by the [Generalized Stokes theorem](generalized-stokes-theorem.md); boundaries require the appropriate fixed boundary data or additional boundary coupling.
