# Additive energy controls three-term progression mixing

↑ **Parent:** [Additive energy](additive-energy.md)

If a subset $A$ of a cyclic group has normalized [additive energy](additive-energy.md) at most $\alpha^4+\epsilon$, every nonconstant [Fourier coefficient on a finite abelian group](fourier-coefficient-on-a-finite-abelian-group.md) of its indicator has modulus at most $\epsilon^{1/4}$. Expanding the count of $a+c=2b$ gives the frequency product $\widehat{1_A}(r)\widehat{1_C}(r)\widehat{1_B}(-2r)$. The [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) and [Parseval identity on a finite group](parseval-identity-on-a-finite-group.md) bound its nonconstant part by the displayed quantity. Character doubling has multiplicity $\gcd(2,N)$, so no assumption that the modulus is odd is needed. The error is a normalized density error multiplied by $N^2$.
