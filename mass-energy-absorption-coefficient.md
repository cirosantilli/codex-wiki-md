# Mass energy-absorption coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Mass attenuation coefficient](mass-attenuation-coefficient.md)

An energy-weighted interaction coefficient for [photons](photon.md), accounting for the fraction transferred to charged particles after radiative losses. It differs from the removal-probability [mass attenuation coefficient](mass-attenuation-coefficient.md). With [charged-particle equilibrium](charged-particle-equilibrium.md), its product with [photon energy fluence](photon-energy-fluence.md) gives the [absorbed dose](absorbed-dose.md).
