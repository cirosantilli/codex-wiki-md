# Reflection theorem for definable hierarchies

↑ **Parent:** [Lévy reflection theorem](levy-reflection-theorem.md)

A finite collection of [first-order formulas](first-order-formula.md) has a [closed unbounded class of ordinals](closed-unbounded-class-of-ordinals.md) whose levels in a [definable continuous hierarchy](definable-continuous-hierarchy.md) agree with its union for all parameters in the level. Close the collection under subformulas, bound the least witness levels for existential instances on each set-sized stage by [Axiom schema of replacement](axiom-schema-of-replacement.md), and iterate these bounds countably. Continuity supplies the closed stages, and induction on formulas proves agreement. The [Tarski-Vaught test](tarski-vaught-test.md) describes the same witness criterion. For a hierarchy of length an uncountable [regular cardinal](regular-cardinal.md), bounds stay below that cardinal when every stage has smaller [cardinality](cardinality.md).
