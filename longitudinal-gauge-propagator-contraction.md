# Longitudinal gauge propagator contraction

↑ **Parent:** [Positive-sign covariant gauge-fixing inverse](positive-sign-covariant-gauge-fixing-inverse.md)

For the [positive-sign covariant gauge-fixing inverse](positive-sign-covariant-gauge-fixing-inverse.md), $p^aG_{ab}=\xi p_b/p^2$. It is not identically zero for fixed $\xi\ne0$ and is a nonzero vector at a nonzero non-null [four-momentum](four-momentum.md). Null [momenta](momentum.md) require the chosen pole prescription, and individual zero components are possible. The [covariant Landau gauge](landau-gauge-quantum-field-theory.md) limit suppresses this longitudinal part. The [Ward identity](ward-identity.md) removes longitudinal contributions from physical conserved-current amplitudes.
