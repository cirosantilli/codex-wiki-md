# Graded commutative algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Graded algebra](graded-algebra.md)

A [graded algebra](graded-algebra.md) is a graded commutative algebra if $uv=(-1)^{pq}vu$ for [homogeneous elements of a graded algebra](homogeneous-element-of-a-graded-algebra.md) of degrees $p,q$. In [characteristic](characteristic-of-a-field.md) not equal to two, every odd-degree element then squares to zero. The [exterior algebra](exterior-algebra.md) and [Hochschild cohomology](hochschild-cohomology.md) with the [Hochschild cup product](hochschild-cup-product.md) are examples.
