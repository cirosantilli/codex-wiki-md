# Circle-average process of the Gaussian free field is Brownian motion

↑ **Parent:** [Circle-average process of the Gaussian free field](circle-average-process-of-the-gaussian-free-field.md)

The circle-average process of a zero-boundary Gaussian free field in the unit disc has the law of a constant multiple of [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md). It is a continuous centered [Gaussian process](gaussian-process.md) with [stationary increments](stationary-increments.md) and [independent increments](independent-increments.md) and starts at zero.
