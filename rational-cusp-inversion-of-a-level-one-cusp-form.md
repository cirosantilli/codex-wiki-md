# Rational-cusp inversion of a level-one cusp form

↑ **Parent:** [Additive twist of a cusp-form L-function](additive-twist-of-a-cusp-form-l-function.md)

If $ad\equiv1\pmod N$, the determinant-one [matrix](matrix.md) with rows $(a,(ad-1)/N)$ and $(N,d)$ sends $\tau-d/N$ to $a/N-1/(N^2\tau)$. The transformation of the [cusp form](cusp-form.md) gives the displayed identity. It turns the small-height portion of an additive-twist [Mellin transform](mellin-transform.md) into exponentially decaying values at the inverse rational cusp.
