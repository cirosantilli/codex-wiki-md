<h1 id="representability-of-cohomology-by-eilenberg-maclane-spaces">Representability of cohomology by Eilenberg–MacLane spaces</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Eilenberg–MacLane space](eilenberg-maclane-space.md)

For a [CW complex](cw-complex.md) $X$, abelian $G$ and $n\geq1$, [homotopy](homotopy.md) classes of maps to the [Eilenberg–MacLane space](eilenberg-maclane-space.md) correspond to [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) classes by pullback of the [universal cohomology class of an Eilenberg–MacLane space](universal-cohomology-class-of-an-eilenberg-maclane-space.md). Pointed maps give the reduced version. This converts a [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) class into an actual map realizing its effect on [homotopy](homotopy.md).
