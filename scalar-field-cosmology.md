# Scalar-field cosmology

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmology](cosmology-split.md)

A homogeneous canonical [scalar field](scalar-field.md) contributes [energy density](energy-density.md) $\rho=\dot\varphi^2/2+V$ and [pressure](pressure-split.md) $P=\dot\varphi^2/2-V$. When it is the only source in a spatially flat [FLRW metric](friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md), the [Friedmann equation](friedmann-equations.md) is $3M_{\rm Pl}^2H^2=\rho$, and its evolution follows the [scalar field equation of motion](scalar-field-equation-of-motion.md). Positive [energy density](energy-density.md) implies $w\ge-1$ because $\rho+P=\dot\varphi^2\ge0$, even if the [scalar potential](scalar-potential.md) is negative.
