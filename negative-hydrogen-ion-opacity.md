# Negative hydrogen ion opacity

↑ **Parent:** [Opacity](opacity.md)

The [negative hydrogen ion](hydrogen-anion.md) supplies important continuum [opacity](opacity.md) in cool hydrogen-rich stellar atmospheres. A useful local approximate law is $\kappa=\kappa_0\rho^{1/2}T^9$; it is not valid over arbitrary temperatures and compositions. In an [ideal gas](ideal-gas.md) [power-law opacity radiative envelope](power-law-opacity-radiative-envelope.md) matched to $\nabla_{\rm phot}=1/8$, it gives $\nabla_{\rm rad}=-1/3+(11/24)(T/T_{\rm eff})^{9/2}$, rising rapidly toward [convective instability](stellar-convective-instability.md).
