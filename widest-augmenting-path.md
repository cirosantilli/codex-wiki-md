# Widest augmenting path

↑ **Parent:** [Augmenting path](augmenting-path.md)

An [augmenting path](augmenting-path.md) maximizing the possible increase in flow, namely its smallest [residual network](residual-network.md) edge capacity. If the remaining maximum flow is $F>0$ and the original undirected network has $m$ edges, a path has bottleneck at least $F/m$. Otherwise the vertices reachable through arcs of capacity at least $F/m$ define a cut of capacity strictly less than $F$, contradicting the [max-flow min-cut theorem](max-flow-min-cut-theorem.md). Only one directed arc per original crossing edge contributes to that cut.
