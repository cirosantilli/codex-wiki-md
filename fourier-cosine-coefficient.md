# Fourier cosine coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier cosine series](fourier-cosine-series.md)

On $[0,L]$, the [Fourier cosine series](fourier-cosine-series.md) convention $f(x)=a_0/2+\sum_{n\geq1}a_n\cos(n\pi x/L)$ uses the displayed coefficients, including $n=0$. [Orthogonality](orthogonal-vectors.md) of the [cosine](cosine.md) modes gives these formulas for [square-integrable functions](square-integrable-function.md); the expansion is interpreted in the [L2 norm](l2-norm.md) until stronger convergence is established.
