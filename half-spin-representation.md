# Half-spin representation

↑ **Parent:** [Spin group](spin-group.md)

For $\operatorname{Spin}(2m)$, $m\ge3$, the two complex half-spin [irreducible representations](irreducible-representation.md) have dimension $2^{m-1}$. Their [weights](weight-representation-theory.md) are $\tfrac12(\pm e_1\pm\cdots\pm e_m)$ with respectively even and odd numbers of minus signs, after a chirality convention is chosen; every [weight multiplicity](weight-multiplicity.md) is one. A model is the even or odd part of the [exterior algebra](exterior-algebra.md) of $\mathbb C^m$, where an occupied mode reverses the corresponding sign. Each is a [minuscule representation](minuscule-representation.md). These are [representations](group-representation.md) of the spin [group](group-split.md) and its [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md), but do not descend through the double covering to the [special orthogonal group](special-orthogonal-group.md).
