# Galaxy power spectrum shot noise

↑ **Parent:** [Galaxy survey](galaxy-survey.md)

Sampling with a homogeneous [Poisson point process](poisson-point-process.md) of mean [comoving number density](comoving-number-density.md) $\bar n$ adds a constant $1/\bar n$ to the dimensional galaxy [power spectrum](power-spectrum.md). Subtracting its expectation leaves sampling uncertainty. Correlated tracer populations and survey weights modify this simple formula; survey depth alone does not specify $\bar n$.
