# Impossibility of isolated massive-particle decay into one photon

↑ **Parent:** [Four-momentum](four-momentum.md)

Conservation of [four-momentum](four-momentum.md) forbids an isolated massive initial particle from decaying into a single [photon](photon.md): the initial [Minkowski norm](minkowski-norm.md) is $m^2c^2>0$, while the final norm would vanish. This argument is independent of [inertial frame](inertial-frame.md). A second final particle or external recoil changes the problem because the total final [four-momentum](four-momentum.md) need not be null.
