# Vanishing ideal

↑ **Parent:** [Affine algebraic set](affine-algebraic-set.md)

The vanishing ideal of $X\subseteq k^n$ is

$$
I(X)=\{f\in k[x_1,\ldots,x_n]:f(x)=0\text{ for every }x\in X\}.
$$
