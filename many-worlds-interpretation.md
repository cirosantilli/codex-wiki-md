# Many-worlds interpretation

↑ **Parent:** [Foundations of quantum mechanics](foundations-of-quantum-mechanics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Many-worlds_interpretation)

An interpretation retaining universal unitary evolution of the [quantum state](quantum-state.md), with definite measurement records described relative to outcome branches. [Quantum decoherence](quantum-decoherence.md) explains the practical isolation and stable record basis of emergent branches. It does not insert a physical collapse selecting just one component. The emergence of branch structure and justification of [Born rule](born-rule.md) weights involve additional interpretive arguments beyond writing the [Schrödinger equation](schrodinger-equation.md).
