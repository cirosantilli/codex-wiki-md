# Square discriminants in characteristic two

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial discriminant](polynomial-discriminant.md)

For a [separable polynomial](separable-polynomial.md) $f\in F[T]$ in characteristic two, the [Vandermonde determinant](vandermonde-determinant.md) of its roots is fixed by every [Galois automorphism](automorphism-of-a-field-extension.md): root [permutations](permutation.md) change it only by a sign, and $-1=1$ in $F$. It therefore lies in $F$. Its square, multiplied by the square of the appropriate leading-coefficient power for a nonmonic [polynomial](polynomial-split.md), is the [polynomial discriminant](polynomial-discriminant.md). Thus being a square does not detect containment of the [Galois group](galois-group.md) in the [alternating group](alternating-group.md). For example $T^2+T+1$ over $\mathbb F_2$ has discriminant one and [Galois group](galois-group.md) $C_2$, while $A_2$ is trivial. The [discriminant criterion for an alternating Galois group](discriminant-criterion-for-an-alternating-galois-group.md) requires characteristic different from two.
