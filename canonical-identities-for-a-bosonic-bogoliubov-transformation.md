# Canonical identities for a bosonic Bogoliubov transformation

↑ **Parent:** [Bogoliubov transformation](bogoliubov-transformation.md)

For mode mixing $\psi'_i=\sum_j(A_{ij}\psi_j+B_{ij}\overline\psi_j)$, the [Klein-Gordon inner product](klein-gordon-inner-product.md) extracts $a'_i=\sum_j(\overline A_{ij}a_j-\overline B_{ij}a_j^\dagger)$. The [canonical commutation relation](canonical-commutation-relation.md) is preserved exactly when $AA^\dagger-BB^\dagger=I$ and $AB^T=BA^T$. These follow by computing $[a'_i,a_k'{}^\dagger]$ and $[a'_i,a'_k]$; they are also the positive- and negative-frequency mode orthonormality identities. In infinitely many modes, convergence and the existence of a common [bosonic Fock space](bosonic-fock-space.md) representation require additional analysis; the expected total particle number is finite only if $B$ is a [Hilbert-Schmidt operator](hilbert-schmidt-operator.md).
