# Complex conjugate

↑ **Parent:** [Complex number](complex-number.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_conjugate)

The complex conjugate of $z=x+iy$ is $\overline z=x-iy$. It satisfies $z\overline z=|z|^2$.
