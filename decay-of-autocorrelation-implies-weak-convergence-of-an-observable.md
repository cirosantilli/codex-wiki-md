# Decay of autocorrelation implies weak convergence of an observable

↑ **Parent:** [Koopman operator](koopman-operator.md)

For a probability [measure-preserving system](measure-preserving-system.md), if $\langle U_T^nf,f\rangle\to|\int f\,d\mu|^2$, then $U_T^nf$ converges weakly in $L^2$ to $(\int f\,d\mu)\mathbf1$. Centre $v=f-(\int f)\mathbf1$; its autocorrelations tend to zero. The [isometry](isometry.md) identity gives the same decay against each $U_T^kv$, hence by approximation against their closed forward span. Against its orthogonal complement the correlations are exactly zero. [Orthogonal decomposition by a closed subspace](orthogonal-decomposition-by-a-closed-subspace.md) finishes the proof without invertibility.
