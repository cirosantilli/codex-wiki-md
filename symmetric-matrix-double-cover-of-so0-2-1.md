<h1 id="symmetric-matrix-double-cover-of-so0-2-1">Symmetric-matrix double cover of SO0(2,1)</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Spin group](spin-group.md)

Congruence $X\mapsto AXA^T$ on real symmetric two-by-two [matrices](matrix.md) preserves the [quadratic form](quadratic-form.md) $-\det X$ of signature $(2,1)$ when $\det A=1$. Its [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) is $\{\pm I\}$ and its derivative is injective: $aX+Xa^T=0$ for every symmetric $X$ implies $a=0$. Both [Lie groups](lie-group.md) have dimension three, so the connected image is the full [identity component](identity-component.md). Also $A^TJA=(\det A)J$ identifies $Sp(2,\mathbb R)$ with $SL(2,\mathbb R)$.
