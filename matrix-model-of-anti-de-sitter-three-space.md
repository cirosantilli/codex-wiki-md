# Matrix model of anti-de Sitter three-space

↑ **Parent:** [Anti-de Sitter spacetime](anti-de-sitter-spacetime.md)

For ambient [quadratic form](quadratic-form.md) $Q=-x_0^2-x_4^2+x_1^2+x_2^2$, associate $M=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}x_4+x_1&x_2+x_0\\x_2-x_0&x_4-x_1\end{smallmatrix}\right)$. Its [determinant](determinant.md) is $-Q$, so the unit [Anti-de Sitter spacetime](anti-de-sitter-spacetime.md) hyperboloid is the [special linear group](special-linear-group.md) of real two-by-two matrices. On its tangent space at the identity, the induced [bilinear form](bilinear-form.md) is $\tfrac12\operatorname{tr}(UV)$. The [Killing form](killing-form.md) is $4\operatorname{tr}(UV)$, and left and right matrix multiplication preserve the ambient form. Consequently the induced [bi-invariant pseudo-Riemannian metric](bi-invariant-pseudo-riemannian-metric.md) is $B/8$. This model has periodic time; its causally unwrapped version is the [universal covering Lie group](universal-covering-lie-group.md).
