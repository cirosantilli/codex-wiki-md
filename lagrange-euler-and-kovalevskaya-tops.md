# Lagrange, Euler, and Kovalevskaya tops

↑ **Parent:** [Rigid body dynamics](rigid-body-dynamics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lagrange,_Euler,_and_Kovalevskaya_tops)

The [Lagrange, Euler, and Kovalevskaya tops](lagrange-euler-and-kovalevskaya-tops.md) are three integrable cases of fixed-point [rigid body dynamics](rigid-body-dynamics.md). The Euler top has no external torque; the Lagrange top has axial symmetry and its center of mass on the symmetry axis; the Kovalevskaya top has two equal principal moments twice the third and its center of mass in the corresponding principal plane.
