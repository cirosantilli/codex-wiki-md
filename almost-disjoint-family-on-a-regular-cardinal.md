# Almost disjoint family on a regular cardinal

↑ **Parent:** [Set theory](set-theory-split.md)

A family of size-$\kappa$ [subsets](subset.md) of a set is almost disjoint at $\kappa$ when distinct members have intersection of size less than $\kappa$. Graphs of a [long chain under eventual domination](long-chain-under-eventual-domination.md) give such a family of size $\kappa^+$ on $\kappa\times\kappa$. Transport by a [bijection](bijection.md) gives a family on $\kappa$ itself.
