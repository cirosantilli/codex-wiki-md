# Normalized squared Cauchy kernel

↑ **Parent:** [H1 space on the unit disk](h1-space-on-the-unit-disk.md)

For $a\in\mathbb D$, this [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function.md) has [H1 space on the unit disk](h1-space-on-the-unit-disk.md) norm one: its boundary modulus is the unit-mass [Poisson kernel on the circle](poisson-kernel-on-the-circle.md). The radial integrals are bounded by that boundary integral by [subharmonic](subharmonic-function.md) comparison and tend to it by continuity. Choosing $a$ at depth comparable to an arc's length makes $|f_a|$ bounded below by the reciprocal length throughout its [hyperbolic geodesic cap](hyperbolic-geodesic-cap.md). It tests necessity in the [Carleson embedding theorem on the disk](carleson-embedding-theorem-on-the-disk.md).
