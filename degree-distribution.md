# Degree distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Graph theory](graph-theory-split.md)

A [degree distribution](degree-distribution.md) assigns the fraction or probability of [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) having each [degree of a vertex](degree-graph-theory.md). For an observed network, $P(k)=\#\{i:k_i=k\}/n$. Preserving every labelled degree preserves this distribution, but preserving only its mean does not.
