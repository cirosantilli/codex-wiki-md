# Kernel of a module homomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Module homomorphism](module-homomorphism.md)

The [kernel of a module homomorphism](kernel-of-a-module-homomorphism.md) $f:M\to N$ is the [submodule](submodule.md) of elements mapped to zero. Closure follows from $f(m+m')=f(m)+f(m')$ and $f(rm)=rf(m)$.
