# Weak formulation of the Robin Poisson problem

↑ **Parent:** [Robin boundary condition](robin-boundary-condition.md)

On a bounded [Lipschitz domain](lipschitz-domain.md), the [Poisson equation](poisson-s-equation.md) $-\Delta u=f$ with [Robin boundary condition](robin-boundary-condition.md) $\partial_nu+\beta u=0$ has the displayed [weak formulation](weak-formulation.md) in $H^1(\Omega)$ when $\beta$ is a bounded boundary coefficient. Boundary integrals use [Sobolev traces](trace-operator.md). It follows from [Green's first identity](green-s-first-identity.md), requiring only first [weak derivatives](weak-derivative.md) of $u$. If $\beta$ is uniformly positive, testing the difference of two solutions against itself proves uniqueness: its [gradient](gradient.md) and its boundary [Sobolev trace](trace-operator.md) vanish. The [Poincare inequality with a boundary trace](poincare-inequality-with-a-boundary-trace.md) also makes this a [coercive bilinear form](coercive-bilinear-form.md).
