# Jeans equation

↑ **Parent:** [Collisionless Boltzmann equation](collisionless-boltzmann-equation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeans_equation)

The Jeans equations are velocity moments of the [Collisionless Boltzmann equation](collisionless-boltzmann-equation.md). They relate density, mean motion, velocity dispersion, and gravitational acceleration without requiring a closed fluid equation of state.
