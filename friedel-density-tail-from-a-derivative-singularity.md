# Friedel density tail from a derivative singularity

↑ **Parent:** [Friedel oscillations](friedel-oscillations.md)

For the [radial induced-charge screening kernel](radial-induced-charge-screening-kernel.md), integrate the sine transform twice by parts, with $g(0)=g(\infty)=0$ and vanishing remaining endpoint terms. This gives $\rho_{\rm ind}=Ze\int g''(q)\sin(qr)dq/r^3$. A [Cauchy principal value](cauchy-principal-value.md) singularity at $Q>0$ contributes $C\pi\cos(Qr)$ to that integral: set $s=q-Q$ and use $\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\sin(sr)ds/s=\pi$ for $r>0$. Smoothly localizing around $Q$ justifies the large-$r$ extension of the limits. In a three-dimensional isotropic [Fermi gas](fermi-gas.md), $Q=2k_F$.
