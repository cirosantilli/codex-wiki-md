# Null line

↑ **Parent:** [Null geodesic](null-geodesic.md)

In a flat two-dimensional [Penrose diagram](penrose-diagram.md), a straight line tangent to a [null vector](null-vector.md); with radial [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md) $-dT^2+dX^2$ it is $T-X=\mathrm{constant}$ or $T+X=\mathrm{constant}$. Such lines represent radial [null geodesics](null-geodesic.md) after a [conformal transformation](conformal-map.md).
