# Dual Haar measure

↑ **Parent:** [Pontryagin dual group](pontryagin-dual-group.md)

The dual Haar measure is the scale of [Haar measure](haar-measure.md) on the [Pontryagin dual group](pontryagin-dual-group.md) compatible with [Fourier inversion on a locally compact abelian group](fourier-inversion-on-a-locally-compact-abelian-group.md). It can be constructed without first assuming inversion: for a compactly supported test function $\psi$, choose a finite sum of convolution squares $p$ with $\widehat p>0$ on its support and define $J(\psi)=\int\psi/\widehat p\,d\mu_p$. The [Bochner consistency identity for convolution squares](bochner-consistency-identity-for-convolution-squares.md) makes this independent of the choice. Modulation by a character shows $J$ is invariant under dual translation; the [Riesz representation on compactly supported continuous functions](riesz-representation-on-compactly-supported-continuous-functions.md) gives the measure. It satisfies $d\mu_p=\widehat p\,dm_{\widehat G}$ and scales reciprocally when $dm_G$ is rescaled.
