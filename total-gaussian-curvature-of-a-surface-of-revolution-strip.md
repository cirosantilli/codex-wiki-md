# Total Gaussian curvature of a surface-of-revolution strip

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian curvature of a surface of revolution](gaussian-curvature-of-a-surface-of-revolution.md)

For $a\leq x\leq b$, the curvature formula is an exact derivative and gives

$$
\int K\,dA
=-2\pi\left[
\frac{f'}{\sqrt{1+f'^2}}
\right]_{a}^{b}.
$$
