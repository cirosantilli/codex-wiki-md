# Uniform partial-sum criterion for a Schauder basis

↑ **Parent:** [Schauder basis](schauder-basis.md)

For a countable [minimal total family in a normed space](minimal-total-family-in-a-normed-space.md) in a [Banach space](banach-space-split.md), let $(x_n,f_n)$ be its unique [biorthogonal system](biorthogonal-system.md) and let $P_nx=\sum_{j\leq n}f_j(x)x_j$. Uniform boundedness of these projections implies $P_nx\to x$ for every $x$: approximate $x$ by a finite linear combination $y$, eventually fixed by $P_n$, and bound the error by $(1+\sup_n\|P_n\|)\|x-y\|$. Conversely, if every [vector](vector.md) has a norm-convergent expansion in this family, applying each continuous $f_j$ shows that its coefficients must be $f_j(x)$. Thus $P_nx\to x$, so the projections are pointwise bounded. The [Uniform boundedness principle](uniform-boundedness-principle.md) gives their uniform operator-norm bound. This proves the criterion and uniqueness of all expansions.
