# Non-algal particle

↑ **Parent:** [Ocean colour](ocean-colour.md)

An optically defined non-phytoplankton particle category, including detritus and suspended mineral material. Its absorption is often strongest in the blue; scattering can also strongly affect [remote-sensing reflectance](remote-sensing-reflectance.md).
