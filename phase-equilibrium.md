# Phase equilibrium

↑ **Parent:** [Thermodynamic equilibrium](thermodynamic-equilibrium.md)

Coexistence without a thermodynamic driving [force](force-split.md) for further exchange. Phases able to exchange heat and constituents have equal [temperature](temperature-split.md) and [chemical potentials](chemical-potential-split.md) of the exchanging species. Mechanical balance is also needed; interface [curvature](curvature.md) can require a pressure jump rather than equal bulk pressures.
