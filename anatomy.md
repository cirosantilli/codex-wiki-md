↑ **Parent:** [Tooth](#tooth)

[fossil](paleontology.md#fossil) tissue distributions and enamel-matrix [gene expression](molecular-genetics.md#gene-expression) support an ancestry in dermal coverings deeper than its familiar deployment on [teeth](#tooth).[Dermal distribution and inferred expansion into the mouth](ecology-and-evolution-bibliography.md#new-genomic-and-fossil-data-illuminate-the-origin-of-enamel-by-qingming-qu-and-others)

↑ **Parent:** [Enamel](#enamel)

### Dentition

↑ **Parent:** [Tooth](#tooth)

The arrangement and complement of teeth, including their classes, positions and replacement pattern.

### Tooth enamel

↑ **Parent:** [Tooth](#tooth)

A highly mineralized dental tissue. Its distribution and wear relative to other tissues help maintain functional tooth surfaces.

### Incisor

↑ **Parent:** [Tooth](#tooth)

An anterior tooth class specialized in diverse ways for cropping, cutting or gnawing.

### Molar

↑ **Parent:** [Tooth](#tooth)

A posterior tooth class important in many forms of food processing. Molar form varies with lineage, position and wear.

### Premolar

↑ **Parent:** [Tooth](#tooth)

A tooth class lying in front of the molars in the typical heterodont mammalian arrangement.

### Tribospheny

↑ **Parent:** [Tooth](#tooth)

An occlusal construction combining shearing and crushing, with the lower molar talonid basin interacting with an upper cusp. Similar functional geometry does not alone settle whether all examples share one evolutionary origin.

### Dental mesowear

↑ **Parent:** [Tooth](#tooth)

Assessment of macroscopic wear-related cusp shape and relief. It integrates a different temporal scale of food processing from [dental microwear](#dental-microwear).

### Dental microwear

↑ **Parent:** [Tooth](#tooth)

Small wear features on tooth surfaces used to infer food processing near the end of the wear record. Diet, external grit, preparation and post-mortem damage must be distinguished.

### Hypselodonty

↑ **Parent:** [Tooth](#tooth)

Continued growth of a tooth, distinguished from a tall but finite reserve crown in [hypsodonty](#hypsodonty).

### Hypsodonty

↑ **Parent:** [Tooth](#tooth)

A high-crowned tooth construction supplying a reserve against wear. It differs from [hypselodonty](#hypselodonty), and is not by itself proof of a grass diet.

### Selenodonty

↑ **Parent:** [Tooth](#tooth)

A dental construction with crescentic ridges, characteristic of many [ruminants](organism.md#ruminant).

### Lophodonty

↑ **Parent:** [Tooth](#tooth)

A dental construction in which cusps are connected into ridges. Ridge arrangement and wear influence the cutting and grinding performance of the surface.

### Carnassial

↑ **Parent:** [Tooth](#tooth)

A specialized shearing tooth. In [Carnivora](organism.md#carnivora) the principal opposing pair is the upper fourth premolar and lower first molar; other predatory lineages can specialize different teeth.

### Diphyodonty

↑ **Parent:** [Tooth](#tooth)

A replacement pattern with two successive generations for the replaced tooth classes. It should not be taken to mean every tooth in the jaw is replaced exactly once.

### Dental occlusion

↑ **Parent:** [Tooth](#tooth)

The relationship and contact of opposing [teeth](#tooth) during jaw closure and chewing. Precise correspondence of opposing surfaces permits specialized cutting and grinding.
