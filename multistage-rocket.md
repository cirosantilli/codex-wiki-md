# Multistage rocket

↑ **Parent:** [Rocket equation](rocket-equation.md)

A rocket discards exhausted stages, reducing the [mass](mass.md) accelerated by later burns. Apply the [rocket equation](rocket-equation.md) separately to each burn and add the signed [velocity](velocity.md) increments. Ideal jettison with no relative impulse leaves [velocity](velocity.md) continuous; it changes the mass ratio available to subsequent burns. Dry-stage mass must not be counted as propellant expelled at the [rocket exhaust speed](rocket-exhaust-speed.md).
