<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

[Theory-ladenness](../../../theory-ladenness.md) can affect the concepts used to describe an observation, the interpretation of an instrument and even which features attract attention. A detector's pulse height is not already a particle's [energy](../../../energy-split.md): the inference depends on [measurement calibration](../../../measurement-calibration.md), response assumptions and background subtraction. This defeats a particularly strong [empiricism](../../../empiricism.md) according to which every scientific claim rests on a completely theory-free observational foundation.

It does not follow that observation can never constrain theory. The crucial distinction is between relying on some background account and presupposing the truth of the particular target under test. One can calibrate a detector with independently characterized sources and compare measurements from detectors with different systematic errors. Agreement across these routes is more compelling than repeated use of one disputed reduction procedure. [Scientific replication](../../../scientific-replication.md) and tests with deliberately varied apparatus can expose shared assumptions rather than merely repeat them.

An [empiricist](../../../empiricist.md) can therefore adopt a fallible, public evidential practice. The [observable phenomenon](../../../observable-phenomenon.md) need not be linguistically uninterpreted to have an evidential role. Reports can be translated sufficiently for disagreement, and raw records can be retained so that rival analyses can be compared. Where all purported evidence is defined through the target theory itself, the circularity is a real problem; the reply must supply independent checks, not simply assert objectivity.

[Constructive empiricism](../../../constructive-empiricism.md) offers one further distinction: accepting a theory for its [empirical adequacy](../../../empirical-adequacy.md) does not require believing its full story about every [unobservable entity](../../../unobservable-entity.md). Its proponent can use a theoretical account to organize instruments without identifying use with unconditional belief.[sections II–III: instrumental detection and acceptance versus belief](../../../constructive-empiricism-now-by-bas-van-fraassen.md) [Theory-ladenness](../../../theory-ladenness.md) therefore undermines an infallible foundation, while leaving room for constrained, corrigible [empiricism](../../../empiricism.md).
