↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Karl Popper](../../../../karl-popper.md)'s [falsificationism](../../../../falsificationism.md) treats theories as conjectures to be exposed to serious attempted refutation. A universal claim entails observable restrictions and can conflict with a counterexample, whereas a finite collection of favourable cases does not deductively establish the universal. This emphasizes risky tests and distinguishes surviving criticism from proof. It also avoids pretending that the [problem of induction](../../../../problem-of-induction.md) is solved merely by collecting more confirming observations.[chapter 1 excerpt, numbered conclusions on confirmation and testability](../../../../science-conjectures-and-refutations-by-karl-popper.md)

The main difficulty is moving from this logical asymmetry to a complete account of practice. A prediction usually has the form $(T\land A)\Rightarrow E$, where $A$ includes [auxiliary hypotheses](../../../../auxiliary-hypothesis.md) about apparatus, initial conditions and background physics. Observing $\neg E$ implies $\neg T\lor\neg A$, not specifically $\neg T$. This [Duhem problem](../../../../duhem-problem.md) makes independent [measurement calibration](../../../../measurement-calibration.md) and discriminating follow-up [experiments](../../../../experiment.md) essential. An anomalous detector count can reveal a detector fault rather than a new fundamental interaction.

There are also statistical complications. Under a [probability model](../../../../probability-model.md), an unusual observation need not have zero [probability](../../../../probability.md); rejection at a chosen [significance level](../../../../significance-level.md) is fallible. Repeated severe tests can rationally alter comparative confidence even though they do not prove a theory. A methodology that merely labels all surviving theories unrefuted misses this evidential differentiation.

[Falsificationism](../../../../falsificationism.md) is therefore valuable as a critical discipline, especially its opposition to adjustments that insulate a theory from every conceivable result. It is incomplete as a rule requiring immediate abandonment after any anomaly, or as a whole theory of warranted acceptance. The better reconstruction combines explicit possible failures, justified [auxiliary hypotheses](../../../../auxiliary-hypothesis.md) and comparison of competing explanations, while retaining [fallibilism](../../../../fallibilism.md) about the outcome.
