↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

**A defensible demarcation evaluates a practice's accountability to evidence, rather than treating one syntactic feature of a claim as a complete test.** There are clear cases of [pseudoscience](../../../pseudoscience.md), even if there is no exceptionless rule that classifies every immature or disputed research programme immediately.

[Karl Popper](../../../karl-popper.md)'s [falsifiability](../../../falsifiability.md) criterion captures an essential insight: a claim has empirical content when some possible outcomes conflict with it. A prediction that a specified sample will always have the same [electric charge](../../../electric-charge-split.md) would exclude outcomes that a claim allowing every possible result does not. If apparent failures can always be redescribed as successes, accumulated favourable stories do not supply a demanding test. Popper also emphasizes risky predictions and warns that protective modifications can destroy a theory's testability.[chapter 1, section I, numbered conclusions on risky prediction, refutability and conventionalist rescue](../../../science-conjectures-and-refutations-by-karl-popper.md)

However, [falsifiability](../../../falsifiability.md) alone is not sufficient. A bare numerical prediction chosen without evidence may be perfectly falsifiable. So can a particular astrological claim. Testing such a claim seriously can be scientific work; a movement that advertises the claim while systematically ignoring its failure can still be [pseudoscience](../../../pseudoscience.md). Logical vulnerability and actual willingness to learn from tests are different properties. Moreover, a false theory can be scientific, while a true guess can lack a scientific basis. Demarcation is not identical to separating true from false statements.

Nor should every discrepancy lead to immediate rejection. Predictions depend on instruments, initial conditions and [auxiliary hypotheses](../../../auxiliary-hypothesis.md). A detector fault may explain a failed prediction more reasonably than rejection of a well-tested physical law. [Imre Lakatos](../../../imre-lakatos.md) therefore evaluates sequences of theories and their treatment of anomalies: a progressive programme generates new testable successes, whereas a degenerating one primarily adjusts to what is already known.[transcript discussion of Newtonian anomalies and the contrast between progressive and degenerating research programmes](../../../science-and-pseudoscience-by-imre-lakatos.md) This improves on assessing an isolated sentence, although deciding when a programme has had enough time is itself contestable.

A workable standard combines several mutually supporting demands. Claims should specify relevant evidence and conditions of application; tests should expose them to substantial risk of failure; data and methods should permit criticism and appropriate [scientific replication](../../../scientific-replication.md); and revisions should improve independently assessable performance. The crucial unit is a sustained practice presenting itself as scientific knowledge. Persistent selective reporting, explanations invented solely to absorb each failure, and refusal to permit competent scrutiny undermine that claim to authority.

For a concrete illustration, suppose a proposed material emits a new kind of signal. Initially poor understanding does not make the proposal [pseudoscience](../../../pseudoscience.md). Researchers could specify detector settings, compare blinded signal-present and signal-absent trials, publish failures, and seek independent [scientific replication](../../../scientific-replication.md). Contrast a practice that discloses only positive trials, changes the claimed conditions after each negative result, and gives no independent test of the revised conditions. The difference concerns the treatment of evidence, even if both practices use technical language and the same apparatus. The comparison is hypothetical; it does not depend on attributing misconduct to a named group.

This standard has borderline cases. A promising theory can temporarily lack accessible tests, and a programme can rationally survive a period of failure. It should then be represented with the corresponding uncertainty, rather than sold as established. Longstanding prestige, popularity and institutional affiliation are evidence about a community at most; they cannot replace examination of its methods.

Such a criterion is fallible and allows degrees, but that is compatible with useful discrimination. The best boundary identifies practices that systematically evade the evidential responsibilities they claim to meet. It preserves Popper's demand for genuine exposure to failure while incorporating the historical fact that responsible science tests and revises interconnected systems of claims.
