# Magnetic field-line refraction at a permeability interface

↑ **Parent:** [Electromagnetic boundary condition](electromagnetic-boundary-condition.md)

For linear isotropic media with no free sheet current, continuity of normal $B$ and tangential $H$ gives $\tan\theta_2/\tan\theta_1=\mu_2/\mu_1$, where the angles are measured from the normal. It is a consequence of the two different magnetic [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md), not continuity of every component of $B$.
