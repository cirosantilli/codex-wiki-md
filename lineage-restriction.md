# Lineage restriction

↑ **Parent:** [Cell lineage](cell-lineage.md)

A narrowing of the descendant [cell types](cell-type.md) available to a progenitor. It should be distinguished from failure to sample rare outputs and from changing [developmental competence](developmental-competence.md).
