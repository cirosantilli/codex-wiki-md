# Dorsal protein

↑ **Parent:** [Transcription factor](transcription-factor.md)

An NF-kappaB-family maternal [transcription factor](transcription-factor.md) whose ventral nuclear enrichment patterns the early [Drosophila melanogaster](drosophila-melanogaster.md) embryo. Its name does not mean its nuclear activity is highest dorsally.
