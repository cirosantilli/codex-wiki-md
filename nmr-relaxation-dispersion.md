# NMR relaxation dispersion

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear magnetic resonance](nuclear-magnetic-resonance-split.md)

[NMR relaxation dispersion](nmr-relaxation-dispersion.md) measures how an observed nuclear-spin relaxation rate changes with pulse timing or effective field conditions. Models can constrain exchange between molecular environments, including weakly populated conformations; rate and population estimates depend on the exchange model and information content.
