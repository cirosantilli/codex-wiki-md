# Calvin cycle

↑ **Parent:** [Carbon fixation](carbon-fixation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calvin_cycle)

The [Calvin cycle](calvin-cycle.md) couples [carbon fixation](carbon-fixation.md) to use of [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) and [NADPH](nadph.md). In a [chloroplast](chloroplast.md) it takes place in the [chloroplast stroma](chloroplast-stroma.md). Its [chemical reactions](chemical-reaction.md) incorporate [carbon dioxide](carbon-dioxide.md), use [NADPH](nadph.md)-supplied [Electrons](electron.md), and regenerate the [molecules](molecule.md) that accept further [carbon dioxide](carbon-dioxide.md).

In the standard [Calvin cycle](calvin-cycle.md) bookkeeping, three [carbon dioxide](carbon-dioxide.md) [molecules](molecule.md) require nine [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) and six [NADPH](nadph.md) for one net three-[carbon](carbon.md) [triose phosphate](triose-phosphate.md). A six-[carbon](carbon.md) [sugar](sugar.md) equivalent therefore corresponds to six [carbon dioxide](carbon-dioxide.md), eighteen [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) and twelve [NADPH](nadph.md); subsequent [sugar](sugar.md) synthesis is separate. These are pathway requirements, not a universal measured [photosynthesis](photosynthesis.md) yield. See [OpenStax Biology 2e, 8.3: Calvin cycle bookkeeping](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/8-3-using-light-energy-to-make-organic-molecules).

Calling the [Calvin cycle](calvin-cycle.md) light-independent means its individual [chemical reactions](chemical-reaction.md) do not directly capture [light](light.md); it does not mean the [Calvin cycle](calvin-cycle.md) runs only at night or independently of the supplies from [light-dependent reactions](light-dependent-reactions.md).
