# Principal vertex of a dual cube complex

↑ **Parent:** [Dual cube complex of a wallspace](dual-cube-complex-of-a-wallspace.md)

A point $x\in S$ determines a principal vertex by choosing, for every wall, the halfspace containing $x$. The combinatorial distance between the principal vertices of $x$ and $y$ is $d_{\mathcal W}(x,y)$.
