# Basal melt heat flux

↑ **Parent:** [Sea ice thermodynamics](sea-ice-thermodynamics.md)

Melting a basal ice thickness $\Delta h$ consumes energy $\rho_iL_f\Delta h$ per unit area, ignoring sensible warming and salinity corrections. The corresponding time-averaged [heat flux](heat-flux-density.md) is this energy divided by $\Delta t$. The actual [ocean heat flux](ocean-heat-flux.md) also includes heat conducted into the ice.
