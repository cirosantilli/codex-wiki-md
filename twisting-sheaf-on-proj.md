# Twisting sheaf on Proj

↑ **Parent:** [Sheaf associated with a graded module](sheaf-associated-with-a-graded-module.md)

With the [graded shift](graded-shift.md) convention $S(n)_d=S_{n+d}$, this is the [sheaf associated with a graded module](sheaf-associated-with-a-graded-module.md) $S(n)$. Under the [degree-one generation condition for Proj](degree-one-generation-condition-for-proj.md), it is an [invertible sheaf](line-bundle.md): on $D_+(f)$ with $\deg f=1$, multiplication by $f^n$ freely generates its local module for every integer $n$. Without that hypothesis, it need not be invertible. The [twisting sheaf on projective space](twisting-sheaf-on-projective-space.md) is the standard special case. [Stacks Project, Section 27.10](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/01MM) records the hypotheses and local trivializations.
