# Potential energy surface

↑ **Parent:** [Reaction coordinate](reaction-coordinate.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Potential_energy_surface)

A [potential energy surface](potential-energy-surface.md) assigns [potential energy](potential-energy.md) to the configurations of the reacting [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md), for a specified electronic description. A [reaction coordinate](reaction-coordinate.md) selects a path through this many-dimensional configuration space; a [potential-energy profile](potential-energy-profile.md) then records the [potential energy](potential-energy.md) along that path. Different paths can share endpoints but cross different barriers. See the [IUPAC Gold Book: potential-energy reaction surface](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/P04780).
