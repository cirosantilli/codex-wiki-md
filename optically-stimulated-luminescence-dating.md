# Optically stimulated luminescence dating

↑ **Parent:** [Geochronology](geochronology.md)

A trapped-charge dating method estimating time since suitable [mineral](mineral.md) grains last received sufficient light exposure. Luminescence measurements and environmental dose rate constrain burial age; incomplete bleaching and mixing can bias it. The measured clock is accumulated trapped charge rather than direct decay of a parent isotope.[The laboratory describes stored dose, sunlight resetting and age as equivalent dose divided by dose rate.](luminescence-dating-laboratory-by-united-states-geological-survey.md)
