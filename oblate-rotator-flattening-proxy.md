# Oblate-rotator flattening proxy

↑ **Parent:** [Rotation-anisotropy diagram](rotation-anisotropy-diagram.md)

The simplified moment prescription $q=\sigma_z^2/(\sigma_x^2+v_p^2)$, with $e=1-q$ and $\beta=1-\sigma_z^2/\sigma_x^2$, gives the displayed relation by solving for $v_p^2$. The prescription is an approximation, not a consequence of the [tensor virial theorem](tensor-virial-theorem.md) for arbitrary oblate density profiles. For constant azimuthal streaming $v_{\rm az}$, the one-component streaming second moment is $v_p^2=v_{\rm az}^2/2$.
