# Variational equation

↑ **Parent:** [Ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md)

Differentiating a smooth [characteristic flow map](characteristic-flow-map.md) in its initial point gives this linear equation, with $Y(s)=I$. The [Liouville formula for a fundamental matrix](liouville-formula-for-a-fundamental-matrix.md) then gives $\det Y(t)=\exp(\int_s^t\operatorname{div}b(r,X(r))\,dr)$. Incompressible [Hamiltonian flows](hamiltonian-flow.md) preserve [Lebesgue measure](lebesgue-measure.md) because this [divergence](divergence.md) vanishes.
