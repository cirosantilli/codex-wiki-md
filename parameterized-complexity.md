# Parameterized complexity

↑ **Parent:** [Computational complexity theory](computational-complexity-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parameterized_complexity)

[Parameterized complexity](parameterized-complexity.md) studies an [algorithm](algorithm-split.md)'s resource use as a function of both the encoded [input](input-computer-science.md) length $N$ and a distinguished [nonnegative integer](natural-number.md) parameter $k$. A small solution size, a dimension or a structural property of a [graph](graph-split.md) can serve as the parameter. [Fixed-parameter tractability](fixed-parameter-tractability.md) allows arbitrary computable dependence on $k$ while keeping the exponent of $N$ independent of $k$.
