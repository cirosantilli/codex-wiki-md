# Dehn algorithm

↑ **Parent:** [Dehn presentation](dehn-presentation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dehn_algorithm)

For a [Dehn presentation](dehn-presentation.md), repeatedly freely reduce a word or replace a [relator](relator.md) segment longer than half its perimeter by its shorter complementary segment. Length decreases strictly. A word represents the identity exactly when the procedure reaches the empty word: any nonempty terminal null word would contradict the defining Dehn property. The finite list of [relators](relator.md) makes each search effective.
