# C3v molecular point group

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular symmetry](molecular-symmetry.md)

The six-operation molecular [point group](point-group.md) with a threefold proper rotation axis and three vertical mirror planes. Its [rotational symmetry number](rotational-symmetry-number.md) is three because mirror operations do not count as proper spatial rotations.
