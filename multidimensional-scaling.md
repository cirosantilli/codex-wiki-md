# Multidimensional scaling

↑ **Parent:** [Unsupervised learning](unsupervised-learning.md)

Construct low-dimensional coordinates representing a [dissimilarity matrix](dissimilarity-matrix.md). Different methods preserve different features: [classical multidimensional scaling](classical-multidimensional-scaling.md) approximates a centered [Gram matrix](gram-matrix.md), while other methods minimize discrepancies between fitted and supplied distances or preserve distance ordering. The method and dimension determine what geometric claims the resulting display supports.
