# Line vortex

↑ **Parent:** [Vortex (fluid mechanics)](vortex-fluid-mechanics.md)

An ideal straight [line vortex](line-vortex.md) in a two-dimensional [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md) has [circulation](circulation-physics.md) $\Gamma$ and induces velocity

$$
\mathbf u(\mathbf r)=\frac{\Gamma}{2\pi}\frac{\mathbf e_z\times(\mathbf r-\mathbf r_0)}{|\mathbf r-\mathbf r_0|^2}.
$$

Its [stream function](stream-function.md), with $\mathbf u=(\psi_y,-\psi_x)$, is $\psi=-\Gamma\log|\mathbf r-\mathbf r_0|/(2\pi)$. Positive $\Gamma$ means counterclockwise [circulation](circulation-physics.md). In an ideal point-vortex description, the vortex position is advected by the regular velocity produced by the other vortices and the boundaries; its own singular velocity is excluded.
