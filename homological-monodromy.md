# Homological monodromy

↑ **Parent:** [Fiber monodromy](fiber-monodromy.md)

Homological monodromy is the induced automorphism of the [homology](homology-split.md) of a fiber. For a [fibered knot](fibered-knot.md), its action on the [first homology group](first-homology.md) presents the [Alexander module](alexander-module-of-a-knot.md).
