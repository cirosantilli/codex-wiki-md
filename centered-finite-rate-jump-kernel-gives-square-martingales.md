# Centered finite-rate jump kernel gives square martingales

↑ **Parent:** [Lévy kernel of a Markov jump process](levy-kernel-of-a-markov-jump-process.md)

With the displacement convention for a [Lévy kernel of a Markov jump process](levy-kernel-of-a-markov-jump-process.md), suppose its total rate is bounded, its first moment is zero and its second moment $v$ is bounded by $V$. The bounded rate prevents explosion. The compensated displacement integral is a square-integrable [martingale](martingale-split.md) with $\mathbb EX_t^2\le Vt$. The displayed process is the sum of $2\int X_{s-}dX_s$ and the compensated sum of squared jumps. The first term is square [integrable](integrability.md) since $4\mathbb E\int_0^tX_{s-}^2v(X_{s-})ds\le2V^2t^2$; the second is [integrable](integrability.md) because its expected total jump-square sum is at most $Vt$. Consequently the square compensation is a true [martingale](martingale-split.md), without a fourth-moment assumption.
