# Transverse momentum current of an elastic string

↑ **Parent:** [Noether current](noether-current.md)

For $\mathcal L=\rho(\dot\psi_y^2+\dot\psi_z^2)/2-F((\psi_y')^2+(\psi_z')^2)/2$, constant displacement symmetry in $y$ gives $j^0=\rho\dot\psi_y$ and $j^x=-F\psi_y'$. The [wave equation](wave-equation-split.md) implies $\partial_tj^0+\partial_xj^x=0$. Its integrated charge is physical transverse momentum; endpoint forces can change that charge on a finite segment.
