# Critical plasma density

↑ **Parent:** [Plasma (physics)](plasma-physics.md)

The [electron density](electron-density.md) at which the cold unmagnetized [electron](electron.md) [plasma frequency](plasma-frequency.md) equals an applied electromagnetic [frequency](frequency.md). In the collisionless linear model, transverse radiation below this cutoff does not propagate in a uniform bulk [plasma](plasma-physics.md).
