# Midpoint-plucked fixed-end string

↑ **Parent:** [Wave equation](wave-equation-split.md)

A string of length $L$ with fixed endpoints, initially displaced into a triangular tent of height $k$ at its midpoint and released from rest, has a [Fourier sine series](fourier-sine-series.md) with the coefficients in the title. Integrating the two linear halves gives $a_n$, and symmetry cancels the even modes. Each coefficient evolves as $a_n\cos(\omega_nt)$ under the [wave equation](wave-equation-split.md). The displacement vanishes everywhere at $t=L/(2c)$, reverses sign at $t=L/c$, and returns after $2L/c$. A straight configuration at an intermediate instant does not mean the string is at rest: its [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) is then nonzero. The initial slope corner gives a finite-energy [weak solution](weak-solution.md) whose corner lines propagate and reflect at the ends.
