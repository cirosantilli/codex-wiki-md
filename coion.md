# Coion

↑ **Parent:** [Electric double layer](electric-double-layer.md)

A [coion](coion.md) has [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) of the same sign as the reference interface or particle. Its [number density](number-density.md) is depleted near that interface in the simplest [Poisson-Boltzmann equation](poisson-boltzmann-equation.md) description. A [counterion](counterion.md) has the opposite sign. These roles depend on the reference charge: near a positively charged sphere in magnesium chloride, magnesium ions are coions and chloride ions are counterions.
