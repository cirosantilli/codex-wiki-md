# Series circuit

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical circuit](electrical-circuit-split.md)

A [series circuit](series-circuit.md) connects components along one unbranched path, so any [electric current](electric-current.md) through the connected components is the same. Oriented terminal [electric potential differences](electric-potential-difference.md) add along that path. In an open-circuit measurement, negligible [electric current](electric-current.md) permits the [electromotive forces](electromotive-force.md) of serially connected sources to add without appreciable internal [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) drops.
