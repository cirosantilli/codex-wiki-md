# Failure of concentration in fixed-dimensional grids

↑ **Parent:** [Lévy family of graphs](levy-family-of-graphs.md)

Equip $[n]^d$ with uniform [probability measure](probability-measure.md) and its nearest-neighbour [graph distance](distance-graph-theory.md) divided by $d(n-1)$, for fixed positive $d$. The [set](set-split.md) $\{x:x_1\leq\lceil n/2\rceil\}$ has at least half the vertices. Its radius-$\varepsilon d(n-1)$ [graph neighbourhood](graph-neighbourhood.md) still omits a proportion tending to $1/2-\varepsilon d$ when $0<\varepsilon<1/(2d)$. These grids therefore do not form a [Lévy family of graphs](levy-family-of-graphs.md).
