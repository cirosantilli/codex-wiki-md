# Zeeman energy

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic dipole moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md)

The energy of a magnetic dipole in an external field is $E=-\boldsymbol\mu\cdot\mathbf B$. For dimensionless angular-[momentum](momentum-split.md) projection $m$ and $\mu_z=-g\mu_Bm$, the energy is $g\mu_BBm$.
