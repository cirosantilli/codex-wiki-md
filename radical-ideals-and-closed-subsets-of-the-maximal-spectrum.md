# Radical ideals and closed subsets of the maximal spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Hilbert Nullstellensatz](hilbert-nullstellensatz.md)

For a [finitely generated algebra](finitely-generated-algebra.md) over an [algebraically closed field](algebraically-closed-field.md) $k$, [radical ideals](radical-ideal.md) correspond, with inclusion reversed, to closed subsets of the [maximal spectrum](maximal-spectrum.md). If $f\notin\sqrt I$, the nonzero algebra $(A/I)[1/f]$ is still finitely generated over $k$. Its maximal residue field is $k$ by the [Zariski lemma](zariski-s-lemma.md), so contracting a maximal ideal gives a maximal ideal of $A$ containing $I$ and avoiding $f$. Hence the intersection of all maximal ideals containing $I$ is $\sqrt I$. This proves the inverse correspondence, including the empty closed set and unit ideal.
