# Trivial zero of a Dirichlet L-function

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet L-function](dirichlet-l-function.md)

For a primitive nonprincipal [Dirichlet character](dirichlet-character.md) with parity $a$, the [gamma function](gamma-function.md) poles in its [completed Dirichlet L-function](completed-dirichlet-l-function.md) force zeros of $L(s,\chi)$ at $s=-a-2m$, $m=0,1,\ldots$. The functional equation and [nonvanishing of nonprincipal Dirichlet L-functions at one](nonvanishing-of-a-nonprincipal-dirichlet-l-function-at-one.md) show that these zeros are simple and cancel the poles, rather than remaining zeros of the completion. For the [Dirichlet beta function](dirichlet-beta-function.md), the odd-parity examples are $\beta(-1)=\beta(-3)=\cdots=0$. The primitive principal conductor-one case is different: its zeta trivial zeros start at $-2$ and the completion has poles at zero and one.
