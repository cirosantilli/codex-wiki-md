# Transonic flow

↑ **Parent:** [Compressible flow](compressible-flow-split.md)

A [compressible flow](compressible-flow-split.md) that passes between subsonic and supersonic regions. A smooth steady crossing of a [sonic point](sonic-point.md) requires the corresponding regularity condition; a discontinuous transition can instead involve a [shock wave](shock-wave.md).
