# Paul Broca

↑ **Parent:** [List of neuroanatomists](list-of-neuroanatomists.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Broca)

A [neuroanatomist](neuroanatomist.md) whose historical observations helped associate left frontal injury with impaired articulate [speech](speech.md). Their interpretation is reassessed by modern lesion imaging.[2007, historical cases and lesion extent](paul-broca-s-historic-cases-by-nina-f-dronkers-and-others.md)
