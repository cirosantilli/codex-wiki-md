# Telescoping product

↑ **Parent:** [Sequence and series](sequence-and-series-split.md)

A product in which numerator and denominator factors cancel between successive terms. For nonzero $b_m,\ldots,b_{n+1}$,

$$
\prod_{i=m}^{n}\frac{b_{i+1}}{b_i}=\frac{b_{n+1}}{b_m}.
$$

One can prove the identity directly by cancellation or by [mathematical induction](mathematical-induction.md) on $n$. Factoring a more complicated product into two such ratios often reveals an otherwise hidden closed expression.
