# MOS inversion threshold

↑ **Parent:** [Semiconductor inversion layer](semiconductor-inversion-layer.md)

For an ideal [MOSFET](mosfet.md) with a nondegenerate [p-type semiconductor](p-type-semiconductor.md) of acceptor density $N_A$, define $\phi_F=(k_BT/e)\ln(N_A/n_i)>0$, where $n_i$ is the [intrinsic carrier concentration](intrinsic-carrier-concentration.md). A positive surface [electric potential](electric-potential.md) $\psi_s$ lowers the local [conduction band](conduction-band.md) and [valence band](valence-band.md) by $e\psi_s$. The surface [electron](electron.md) density is $n_s=(n_i^2/N_A)\exp(e\psi_s/k_BT)$. Equality $n_s=N_A$ gives the conventional onset of strong inversion at $\psi_s=2\phi_F$. The earlier crossover $n_s=p_s=n_i$ occurs at $\psi_s=\phi_F$. Neither condition requires the [Fermi level](fermi-level.md) to enter the conduction band.
