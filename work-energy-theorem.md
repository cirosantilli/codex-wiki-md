# Work-energy theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Work](work.md)

The net [work](work.md) on a particle equals its change in [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md):

$$
\int_a^bF\mathbin{\cdot}dr=\Delta\left(\frac12mv^2\right).
$$
