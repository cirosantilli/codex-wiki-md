# Modified SOP model

↑ **Parent:** [SOP model](sop-model.md)

An extension allowing association changes involving associatively activated, physically absent cues. In the common symmetric formulation, matched $A_1/A_1$ and $A_2/A_2$ states support excitation, while mismatched $A_1/A_2$ and $A_2/A_1$ states support inhibition. The extra rules allow forms of [retrospective revaluation](retrospective-revaluation.md); neural reactivation alone does not verify their signs.
