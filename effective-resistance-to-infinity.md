# Effective resistance to infinity

↑ **Parent:** [Effective resistance](effective-resistance.md)

The effective resistance to infinity is the limit of the [effective resistance](effective-resistance.md) from a fixed vertex to the wired complement of increasing finite sets. It is finite exactly when the [finite-energy flow criterion for transience](finite-energy-flow-criterion-for-transience.md) holds.
