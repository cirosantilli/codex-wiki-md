# Envelope (mathematics)

↑ **Parent:** [Geometry and topology](geometry-and-topology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Envelope_(mathematics))

A [geometric envelope](envelope-mathematics.md) is a [curve](curve.md) tangent to the members of a smoothly varying family of [curves](curve.md). For a regular implicit family $F(x,y,s)=0$, candidate contact points also satisfy the [partial derivative](partial-derivative.md) condition $\partial_sF=0$; regularity is needed to turn these candidates into an actual envelope. The [Mach cone](mach-cone.md) is a three-dimensional analogue for expanding [spheres](sphere.md).
