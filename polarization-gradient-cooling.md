# Polarization-gradient cooling

↑ **Parent:** [Sub-Doppler cooling](sub-doppler-cooling.md)

Polarization-gradient cooling uses spatial variation in [electromagnetic polarization](polarization-of-an-electromagnetic-wave.md) together with [optical pumping](optical-pumping.md) between internal [quantum states](quantum-state.md) to cool moving [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md).

[Jean Dalibard](jean-dalibard.md) and [Claude Cohen-Tannoudji](claude-cohen-tannoudji.md) distinguish cooling by changing [elliptical polarization](elliptical-polarization.md) from cooling by rotating [linear polarization](linear-polarization.md). The hill picture below concerns the former.[§§2–3, pp. 2025–2029, Figs. 1–5](laser-cooling-below-the-doppler-limit-by-polarization-gradients-simple-theoretical-models-by-jean-dalibard-and-claude-cohen-tannoudji.md)
