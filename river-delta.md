# River delta

↑ **Parent:** [Sedimentary deposit](sedimentary-deposit.md)

A depositional body built where a sediment-bearing river enters a more slowly moving body of [water](water.md). Its internal structure and basin context distinguish it from other fan-shaped deposits.
