# Null twist vanishes exactly for hypersurface-orthogonal geodesics

↑ **Parent:** [Null twist](null-twist.md)

For an affine [null geodesic congruence](null-geodesic-congruence.md), $B_{ab}=\nabla_bU_a$ annihilates $U$ in both slots. Relative to a null partner, its antisymmetric part is its screen projection plus a term of the form $U_{[a}q_{b]}$. Hence zero [null twist](null-twist.md) implies $U\wedge dU=0$, and [Frobenius theorem](frobenius-theorem.md) makes $U_a$ locally proportional to a hypersurface gradient. Conversely, if $U_a=h\nabla_aS$, its antisymmetric derivative has a factor of $U$, so the screen projection vanishes. Geodesicity is essential to the converse from vanishing screen twist to the full Frobenius condition.
