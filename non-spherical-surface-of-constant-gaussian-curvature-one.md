# Non-spherical surface of constant Gaussian curvature one

↑ **Parent:** [Constant Gaussian curvature surfaces of revolution](constant-gaussian-curvature-surfaces-of-revolution.md)

For $0<A<1$, rotate the unit-speed profile

$$
Y(s)=A\cos s,
\qquad
Z'(s)=\sqrt{1-A^2\sin^2s}
$$

near $s=0$. Since $K=-Y''/Y$, the resulting regular [surface of revolution](surface-of-revolution.md) has $K=1$. At $s=0$ its [principal curvatures](principal-curvature.md) are $A$ and $A^{-1}$, so the point is not [umbilical](umbilical-point.md) when $A\ne1$ and no neighbourhood of it lies on a sphere.
