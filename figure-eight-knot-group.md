# Figure-eight knot group

↑ **Parent:** [Figure-eight knot](figure-eight-knot.md)

The [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md) of the [figure-eight knot](figure-eight-knot.md) complement has the displayed two-generator one-relator presentation. It follows from the [Artin automorphism of a braid](artin-automorphism-of-a-braid.md) for $(\sigma_1\sigma_2^{-1})^2$ by eliminating the third meridian from its fixed-generator presentation.
