# Mecke formula for a Poisson random measure

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson random measure](poisson-random-measure.md)

For nonnegative [measurable](measurability.md) $H$ and a [Poisson random measure](poisson-random-measure.md) of sigma-finite intensity $\rho$, removing the selected point from a sum leaves an [independent](independent-random-variables.md) copy of the original process under the integral. For finite intensity, condition on the Poisson count, represent the points as [independent](independent-random-variables.md) samples from normalized $\rho$, and shift the count index using $n/n!=1/(n-1)!$. For sigma-finite intensity, first select points in a finite-intensity set, condition on the [independent](independent-random-variables.md) outside process and then exhaust the space. Repetition gives the corresponding formula for ordered distinct tuples of points.
