# Average-reward optimal policy

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamic programming](dynamic-programming.md)

An average-reward optimal policy maximizes long-run expected reward per time step. In a finite recurrent [Markov chain](markov-chain.md), a stationary policy's gain is its expected one-step reward under the stationary distribution. Initial-state effects are encoded by a bias function rather than by the gain. In a communicating finite control problem, the [average-reward Bellman equation](average-reward-bellman-equation.md) certifies a policy that is optimal from every initial state.
