# Dirac propagator

↑ **Parent:** [Propagator](propagator.md)

The free Dirac propagator is $S_F(p)=i(\not p+m)/(p^2-m^2+i\epsilon)$ and inverts the Dirac kinetic operator with Feynman boundary conditions.
