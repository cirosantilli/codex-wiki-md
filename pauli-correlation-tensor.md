# Pauli correlation tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Density matrix](density-matrix.md)

For a two-qubit [density operator](density-matrix.md), the real three-by-three [matrix](matrix.md) $T$ encodes the spin-product [expectation values](expectation-value.md): unit measurement axes $\mathbf a,\mathbf b$ have $E(\mathbf a,\mathbf b)=\mathbf a^TT\mathbf b$. The tensor does not include local Bloch-vector data and alone need not specify the complete [density operator](density-matrix.md). The [CHSH optimum from the correlation tensor](chsh-optimum-from-the-correlation-tensor.md) depends on the two largest [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) of $T^TT$.
