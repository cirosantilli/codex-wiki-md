<h1 id="u-1-charge">U(1) charge</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Representation of the circle group](representation-of-the-circle-group.md)

For the [circle group](circle-group.md) parameterized by $e^{i\theta}$ with period $2\pi$, a [one-dimensional representation](one-dimensional-representation.md) acts as $e^{i\theta}\mapsto e^{iQ\theta}$, and its integer label $Q$ is its charge. Periodicity forces $Q\in\mathbb Z$. A [tensor product representation](tensor-product-of-group-representations.md) adds [U(1) charges](u-1-charge.md), a [dual representation](dual-representation.md) negates them, and restriction to the [finite cyclic group](finite-cyclic-group.md) generated by $e^{2\pi i/n}$ retains only $Q\bmod n$. Integer normalization must be fixed before discussing a largest finite subgroup left by a condensate.
