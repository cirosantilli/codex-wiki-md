# Shallow-water rarefaction from a receding wall

↑ **Parent:** [Rarefaction wave](rarefaction-wave.md)

A resting [shallow water](shallow-water-approximation.md) layer has depth $h_0$ and [wave speed](wave-speed.md) $c_0=\sqrt{gh_0}$. A vertical wall receding into negative $x$ at speed $0<V<2c_0$ creates a [simple wave](simple-wave.md) with invariant $u-2c=-2c_0$. Inside the expansion fan $x/t=u+c$, proving the displayed profiles. The wall-adjacent state is $u=-V$, $c=c_0-V/2$; the fan occupies $c_0-3V/2<x/t<c_0$, with the resting state ahead. The wall lies at $x/t=-V$, strictly to the left of the fan tail because $c_0-V/2>0$. Thus the assumption on wall speed is exactly the condition for positive depth and continued contact between fluid and wall. The depth follows from $h=c^2/g$.
