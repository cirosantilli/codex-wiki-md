# Newform multiplicity-one theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Newform](newform.md)

On the [new subspace of cusp forms](new-subspace-of-cusp-forms.md), a common [eigenspace](eigenspace.md) for the [Hecke operators](hecke-operator.md) away from the level has dimension one. Distinct [newforms](newform.md), even at different primitive levels, have different systems of these [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md). Consequently the commuting bad-prime operators also act by scalars on these lines. This is the multiplicity-one part of Atkin-Lehner-Li theory, not a consequence of [normal operator](normal-operator.md) diagonalization alone. See [Stein's statement of the theorem](https://wstein.org/books/modform/modform/newforms.html#atkin-lehner-li-theory).
