# Nilpotency class of a direct product

↑ **Parent:** [Nilpotency class](nilpotency-class.md)

[Group commutators](group-commutator.md) are taken coordinatewise in a [direct product of groups](direct-product-of-groups.md), giving $\Gamma_j(A\times B)=\Gamma_j(A)\times\Gamma_j(B)$. A finite direct product of [nilpotent groups](nilpotent-group.md) is nilpotent, with class the maximum of the factor classes.
