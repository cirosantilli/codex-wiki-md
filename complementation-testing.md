# Complementation testing

↑ **Parent:** [Functional complementation](functional-complementation.md)

A genetic test asking whether two mutant genomes together restore a function. Failure of recessive alleles to complement can support a shared affected [genetic locus](genetic-locus.md), subject to exceptions such as interacting loci and deletions.
