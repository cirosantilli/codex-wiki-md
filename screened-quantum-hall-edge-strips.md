# Screened quantum Hall edge strips

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum Hall edge state](quantum-hall-edge-state.md)

Self-consistent electrostatics allows a partially filled edge [Landau level](landau-level.md) to screen strongly and flatten its effective energy. Such compressible regions alternate with incompressible strips whose [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) lies in a gap and across which the potential changes. An externally prescribed unscreened smooth edge misses this redistribution. Temperature, disorder and interactions beyond the electrostatic approximation can change the strip profile.
