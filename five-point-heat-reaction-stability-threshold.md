# Five-point heat-reaction stability threshold

↑ **Parent:** [Five-point Laplacian](five-point-laplacian.md)

On a unit-square uniform mesh with $h=1/(M+1)$ and zero [Dirichlet boundary conditions](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md), the semidiscrete [five-point Laplacian](five-point-laplacian.md) has eigenvalues $-4h^{-2}[\sin^2(j\pi h/2)+\sin^2(k\pi h/2)]$. Adding reaction $\kappa I$ gives a contractive continuous-time grid evolution exactly under the displayed threshold. The threshold is $2\pi^2-\pi^4h^2/6+O(h^4)$, slightly below the continuum threshold. This is an all-time non-growth criterion; finite-time mesh-uniform bounds also hold for fixed larger reaction rates.
