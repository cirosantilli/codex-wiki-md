# Polarizability tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Electric polarizability](electric-polarizability.md)

The linear response relating an induced [electric dipole moment](electric-dipole-moment.md) to an applied [electric field](electric-field-split.md). In ordinary nonresonant [Raman spectroscopy](raman-spectroscopy.md), changes in its symmetric components during a [normal mode](normal-mode.md) determine activity. These components transform like $x^2,y^2,z^2,xy,xz,yz$.
