# Anticyclonic gradient-wind branches

↑ **Parent:** [Gradient wind](gradient-wind.md)

For a conventional Northern-Hemisphere anticyclone let $G=-p_r/\rho>0$. [Gradient wind](gradient-wind.md) balance gives $V^2/r-fV+G=0$, with $V_\pm=fr[1\pm\sqrt{1-4G/(f^2r)}]/2$. The minus branch approaches the [geostrophic wind](geostrophic-wind.md) at weak forcing; the plus branch is anomalous. Reality requires $G\le f^2r/4$, so a smooth central pressure maximum must have a radial pressure force vanishing at least linearly toward its centre.
