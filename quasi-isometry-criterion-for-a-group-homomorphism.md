# Quasi-isometry criterion for a group homomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Quasi-isometry](quasi-isometry.md)

For [finitely generated groups](finitely-generated-group.md) $G,H$, a [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md) $\phi:G\to H$ is a [quasi-isometry](quasi-isometry.md) exactly when its [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) is finite and its image is a [finite-index subgroup](finite-index-subgroup.md) of $H$. The finite kernel controls collapse of distances, and finite index is exactly the coarse-surjectivity condition.
