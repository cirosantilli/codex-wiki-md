# Acoustic tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Incremental elastic moduli](incremental-elastic-moduli.md)

The acoustic tensor determines the [plane waves](plane-wave.md) of a locally uniform incremental elastic medium. For a unit material direction $\nu$, its [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are $\rho_0c^2$ and its [eigenvectors](eigenvector.md) are displacement polarizations. Major symmetry of the [incremental elastic moduli](incremental-elastic-moduli.md) makes $Q$ a real [symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md). Positivity on every [rank-one matrix](rank-one-matrix.md) $m\otimes\nu$ makes all its wave speeds real and nonzero.
