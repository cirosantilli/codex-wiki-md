# Device driver

↑ **Parent:** [Operating system](operating-system.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Device_driver)

Software supplying device-specific operations behind an [operating system](operating-system.md) interface. For these examples its responsibilities include [DMA](direct-memory-access.md) setup and completion handling, or checking status for [polled input-output](polled-input-output.md). [Arpaci-Dusseau and Arpaci-Dusseau, OSTEP chapter 36, section 36.7: the driver abstraction](https://pages.cs.wisc.edu/~remzi/OSTEP/file-devices.pdf)
