# Dry deposition

↑ **Parent:** [Atmospheric chemistry](atmospheric-chemistry.md)

Removal of a gas or particle to a surface without precipitation. A simple parameterization uses downward flux $F=v_d C$, where $C$ is the [concentration](concentration.md) near the surface and $v_d$ the [deposition velocity](deposition-velocity.md). For a well-mixed layer of depth $H$, the loss is first order with coefficient $v_d/H$.
