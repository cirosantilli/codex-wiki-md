# Reduced Planck mass

↑ **Parent:** [Planck mass](planck-mass.md)

The reduced Planck mass is

$$
M_{\rm Pl}=\sqrt{\frac{\hbar c}{8\pi G}}.
$$

In natural units, Einstein's gravitational coupling is $M_{\rm Pl}^{-2}=8\pi G$.
