# Massive-vector spin average

↑ **Parent:** [Decay width](decay-width.md)

An unpolarized decay of a massive [spin](spin.md)-one particle averages the squared amplitude over three parent [spin](spin.md) states. Its polarization sum is $-\eta_{\mu\nu}+p_\mu p_\nu/m^2$. For a conserved external current the longitudinal $p_\mu p_\nu$ term drops out, but this does not replace the [spin](spin.md) average by $1/2$: the physical massive parent still has three states.
