# Heat-kernel convolution

↑ **Parent:** [Heat kernel](heat-kernel.md)

For the [heat equation](heat-equation.md) on $\mathbb R^n$ with diffusion coefficient one, the [heat kernel](heat-kernel.md) is $K_t(x)=(4\pi t)^{-n/2}e^{-|x|^2/(4t)}$. Its [convolution](convolution.md) with bounded initial data is smooth for positive time, solves the equation by differentiation under the integral, and returns [continuous](continuous-function.md) initial data locally uniformly through the [approximate identity](approximate-identity.md) property. It is [Gaussian filtering](gaussian-blur.md) with per-coordinate variance $2t$.
