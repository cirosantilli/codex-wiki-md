# Adjoint of a commutator

↑ **Parent:** [Adjoint operator](adjoint-operator.md)

When compositions and adjoints are defined on the common test domain, reversing products gives $[A,B]^*=[B^*,A^*]$. In particular a self-adjoint [Dolbeault Laplacian](dolbeault-laplacian.md) and the mutually adjoint Lefschetz operators satisfy $[\Delta_{\bar\partial},L]^*=[\Lambda,\Delta_{\bar\partial}]$.
