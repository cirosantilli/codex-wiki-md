# Feedback gain

↑ **Parent:** [Controller (control theory)](controller-control-theory.md)

A [feedback gain](feedback-gain.md) sets the strength of a [controller](controller-control-theory.md)'s response to its observations. For [proportional control](proportional-control.md), $u=ke$ means that a [tracking error](tracking-error.md) $e=0.1$ produces a [control input](control-input.md) $u=0.2$ when $k=2$.
