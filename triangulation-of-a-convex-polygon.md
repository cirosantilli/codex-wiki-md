# Triangulation of a convex polygon

↑ **Parent:** [Convex polygon](convex-polygon.md)

A [triangulation of a convex polygon](triangulation-of-a-convex-polygon.md) divides a [convex polygon](convex-polygon.md) into [triangles](triangle.md) using diagonals between its existing [vertices of a polytope](vertex-of-a-polytope.md), with no diagonal crossings in the interior. For $m\geq3$ vertices it uses $m-3$ diagonals and produces $m-2$ [triangles](triangle.md). Fixing one boundary edge, its adjacent [triangle](triangle.md) separates two smaller polygon regions; summing over the possible third [vertices of a polytope](vertex-of-a-polytope.md) gives the [Catalan recurrence](catalan-recurrence.md). Thus the number of [triangulations of a convex polygon](triangulation-of-a-convex-polygon.md) is $C_{m-2}$, with $C_n$ the [Catalan number](catalan-number.md). A quadrilateral has two choices and a pentagon has five.
