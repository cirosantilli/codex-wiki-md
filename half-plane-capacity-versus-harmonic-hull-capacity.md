# Half-plane capacity versus harmonic hull capacity

↑ **Parent:** [Half-plane capacity](half-plane-capacity.md)

The [Brownian representation of half-plane capacity](brownian-representation-of-half-plane-capacity.md) is $\operatorname{hcap}(K)=\lim_{y\to\infty}y\mathbb E_{iy}\operatorname{Im}B_T$. The [harmonic capacity from infinity in the upper half-plane](harmonic-capacity-from-infinity-in-the-upper-half-plane.md) uses $\operatorname{cap}(K)=\lim_{y\to\infty}\pi y\mathbb P_{iy}(B_T\in K)$. Since $0\leq\operatorname{Im}B_T\leq\operatorname{rad}(K)\mathbf1_{\{B_T\in K\}}$, the displayed bound follows. The normalization factor $\pi$ cannot be omitted. Independently, enclosure in a half-disc and [monotonicity of half-plane capacity](monotonicity-of-half-plane-capacity.md) give the sharp bound $\operatorname{hcap}(K)\leq\operatorname{rad}(K)^2$.
