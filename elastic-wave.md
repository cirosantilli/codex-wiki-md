# Elastic wave

↑ **Parent:** [Wave equation](wave-equation-split.md)

An elastic wave propagates a disturbance through an elastic solid. Small displacements in [linear elasticity](linear-elasticity.md) satisfy a vector [wave equation](wave-equation-split.md) governed by [mass density](density.md) and the two [Lamé parameters](lame-parameter.md).
