# Terminal-drift density law for compressible bubbles

↑ **Parent:** [Void fraction](void-fraction.md)

For steady upward bubbles in a stationary liquid, assume fixed bubble [mass](mass.md), quadratic [drag force](drag-physics.md), negligible gas weight and [inertia](inertia.md), and negligible wall force. Then $a\propto\rho_g^{-1/3}$. Hydrostatic mixture support and terminal [drag force](drag-physics.md) balance give $v^2\propto a(1-\alpha)$. The gas [mass flux](mass-flux.md) $J=\alpha\rho_gv$ is constant, hence $J^2\propto\alpha^2(1-\alpha)\rho_g^{5/3}$. Comparing at a reference point proves the displayed relation. The approximation requires a small gas/liquid [mass density](density.md) ratio and macroscopic variation scales; it need not hold near a gas-dominated endpoint or rapid acceleration region.
