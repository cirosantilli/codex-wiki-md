<h1 id="shannon-s-continuous-entropy-coordinate-convention">Shannon's continuous-entropy coordinate convention</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Differential entropy](differential-entropy.md)

In [A Mathematical Theory of Communication by Claude Shannon](a-mathematical-theory-of-communication-by-claude-shannon.md) (1948), section 20 extends the discrete entropy formula to continuous densities. Properties 8–9 explicitly discuss coordinate dependence, possible negative values and linear rescaling. [Claude Shannon](claude-shannon.md) relates this coordinate convention to the engineering use of entropy differences, rather than treating an isolated continuous entropy as an invariant count of messages.[section 20, definition and properties 8–9, reprint pp. 35 and 37–38](a-mathematical-theory-of-communication-by-claude-shannon.md)
