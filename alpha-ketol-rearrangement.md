# Alpha-ketol rearrangement

↑ **Parent:** [Organic reaction](organic-reaction.md)

A 1,2-carbon migration interconverts adjacent tertiary alpha-hydroxy [carbonyl compounds](carbonyl-compound.md). Formation of a new [carbonyl](carbonyl-group.md) at the original carbinol [carbon](carbon.md) accompanies conversion of the original [carbonyl](carbonyl-group.md) into an [alcohol](alcohol.md). Unlike dehydration, the net process preserves both [oxygens](oxygen.md).
