<h1 id="riemann-von-mangoldt-explicit-formula">Riemann–von Mangoldt explicit formula</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Second Chebyshev function](second-chebyshev-function.md)

For $x>1$ away from a jump of $\psi$,

$$
\psi(x)=x-\sum_\rho\frac{x^\rho}{\rho}
-\log(2\pi)-\frac12\log(1-x^{-2}),
$$

where the sum over [Nontrivial zeros of the Riemann zeta function](nontrivial-zero-of-the-riemann-zeta-function.md) is taken symmetrically. A finite-height version has an explicit truncation error controlled by $x/T$ and the distance from $x$ to the nearest prime power.
