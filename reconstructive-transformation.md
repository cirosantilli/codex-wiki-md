# Reconstructive transformation

↑ **Parent:** [Phase transition](phase-transition.md)

A structural phase change requiring substantial rearrangement of near-neighbor bonding or connectivity. Its activation barriers can make equilibrium transitions kinetically sluggish. It is distinguished from a cooperative [displacive transformation](displacive-transformation.md), rather than defined solely by the presence of a different final symmetry.
