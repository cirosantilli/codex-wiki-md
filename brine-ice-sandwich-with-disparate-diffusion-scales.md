# Brine-ice sandwich with disparate diffusion scales

↑ **Parent:** [Ice layer between freshwater and cold brine](ice-layer-between-freshwater-and-cold-brine.md)

For an [ice layer between freshwater and cold brine](ice-layer-between-freshwater-and-cold-brine.md), set $\epsilon=\sqrt{D/\kappa}$ and use solutal similarity positions $a=2\lambda_a\sqrt{Dt}$, $b=2\lambda_b\sqrt{Dt}$. A linear ice temperature, two [Stefan conditions](stefan-condition.md) and interfacial salt conservation determine both negative interface constants. As $\epsilon\to0$ with fixed [latent-to-sensible heat ratio](latent-to-sensible-heat-ratio.md) $S$, $\lambda_b=O(1)$ but $\lambda_a=O(\epsilon^{-1})$. Freezing above occurs over the thermal diffusion scale, while dilution-driven melting below has the much shorter solutal scale.
