# Empirical equivalence under uniform boosts

↑ **Parent:** [Empirical equivalence](empirical-equivalence.md)

For [Newtonian mechanics](newtonian-mechanics-split.md) with forces depending only on relative positions, a common [Galilean transformation](galilean-transformation.md) leaves relative positions and accelerations unchanged. Internal mechanical observations therefore cannot discriminate rival choices of a wholly uncoupled privileged uniform rest state. The qualification concerns uniform motion; it does not imply that rotation or acceleration is unobservable, or that [special relativity](special-relativity-split.md) makes identical predictions.
