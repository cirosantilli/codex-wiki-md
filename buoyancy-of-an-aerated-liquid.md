# Buoyancy of an aerated liquid

↑ **Parent:** [Buoyancy](buoyancy.md)

For a quasihydrostatic bubbly mixture with gas [void fraction](void-fraction.md) $\alpha$, volume averaging gives the displayed [mass density](density.md). A body displacing mixture volume $V$ has approximate [buoyant force](buoyancy.md) $\rho_{\rm mix}gV$. A floating body of mass $m$ therefore needs $V=m/\rho_{\rm mix}$; gas release can increase its draft until the required displacement exceeds its available watertight volume. This mechanism explains a possible loss of flotation without making sinking inevitable in every gas plume. Upward liquid flow, turbulence and nonuniform bubble distributions require additional hydrodynamic forces beyond this simple mixture model.
