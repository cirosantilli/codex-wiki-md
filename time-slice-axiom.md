# Time-slice axiom

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic quantum field theory](algebraic-quantum-field-theory.md)

The [time-slice axiom](time-slice-axiom.md) says that an algebra generated in an arbitrarily thin suitable neighbourhood of a Cauchy surface generates the algebra of its causal development. For $O$ such a neighbourhood in its development $D(O)$, this is $\mathcal A(O)=\mathcal A(D(O))$. Precise global and local versions can differ in the regions to which they apply. For a local hyperbolic field equation, later fields can be represented using the initial field and its conjugate momentum in the corresponding [domain of dependence](domain-of-dependence.md).
