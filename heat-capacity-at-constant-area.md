# Heat capacity at constant area

↑ **Parent:** [Heat capacity](heat-capacity.md)

For a two-dimensional system of fixed area $A$, the heat capacity at constant area is $C_A=(\partial E/\partial T)_{N,A}$ when the particle number and other conserved quantities are also fixed. It is the two-dimensional analogue of [heat capacity at constant volume](heat-capacity-at-constant-volume.md).
