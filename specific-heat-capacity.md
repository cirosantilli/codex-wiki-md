# Specific heat capacity

↑ **Parent:** [Heat capacity](heat-capacity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Specific_heat_capacity)

Specific heat capacity is [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) per unit mass. The volumetric heat capacity is $\rho c_p$, and $k/(\rho c_p)$ is the [thermal diffusivity](thermal-diffusivity.md) when $k$ is the [thermal conductivity](thermal-conductivity.md).
