# Specific heat capacity

↑ **Parent:** [Heat capacity](heat-capacity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Specific_heat_capacity)

Specific heat capacity is [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) per unit [mass](mass.md):

$$
c=\frac Cm,\qquad c_V=\frac{C_V}{m},\qquad c_p=\frac{C_p}{m}.
$$

Its units are $\mathrm{J\,kg^{-1}\,K^{-1}}$, and the constraint remains part of the definition. In particular, [specific heat capacity at constant volume](specific-heat-capacity-at-constant-volume.md) and [specific heat capacity at constant pressure](specific-heat-capacity-at-constant-pressure.md) are different responses. For homogeneous material, doubling the [mass](mass.md) doubles its [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) while leaving its [specific heat capacity](specific-heat-capacity.md) unchanged. [OpenStax, Specific heat and heating calculations](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/1-4-heat-transfer-specific-heat-and-calorimetry)

As an illustrative material model, give a fixed-[volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) body [mass](mass.md) $m=2\,\mathrm{kg}$ and constant [specific heat capacity at constant volume](specific-heat-capacity-at-constant-volume.md) $c_V=400\,\mathrm{J\,kg^{-1}\,K^{-1}}$. With no [phase transition](phase-transition.md) and no other [work](work.md), increasing its uniform [temperature](temperature.md) by $10\,\mathrm K$ requires

$$
C_V=mc_V=800\,\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}},\qquad
Q=mc_V\Delta T=8000\,\mathrm J=\Delta U.
$$

These stipulated values describe no measured substance. The right panel of the diagram in [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) shows the straight line $Q=mc_V\Delta T$: a [temperature](temperature.md) increase of $5\,\mathrm K$ takes $4000\,\mathrm J$ of [heat](heat.md). If the [specific heat capacity at constant volume](specific-heat-capacity-at-constant-volume.md) depends on [temperature](temperature.md), use $Q=m\int_{T_1}^{T_2}c_V(T)\,dT$ instead. The corresponding fixed-[pressure](pressure.md) calculation uses [specific heat capacity at constant pressure](specific-heat-capacity-at-constant-pressure.md) and gives $Q=\Delta H$ under the assumptions in [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md).

Choosing between [specific heat capacity](specific-heat-capacity.md), [molar heat capacity](molar-heat-capacity.md) and [volumetric heat capacity](volumetric-heat-capacity.md) is choosing whether to compare equal [mass](mass.md), equal [amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md) or equal [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md). A small sample with large [specific heat capacity](specific-heat-capacity.md) can still have less total [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) than a much larger sample.
