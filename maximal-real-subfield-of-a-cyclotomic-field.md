# Maximal real subfield of a cyclotomic field

↑ **Parent:** [Cyclotomic field](cyclotomic-field.md)

For $m\geq3$, put $\eta=\zeta_m+\zeta_m^{-1}$. Since $\eta$ is real and $\zeta_m$ is nonreal but satisfies $T^2-\eta T+1=0$, the [cyclotomic field](cyclotomic-field.md) $\mathbb Q(\zeta_m)$ has degree two over $\mathbb Q(\eta)$. [Complex conjugation](complex-conjugation.md) fixes $\eta$ and is nontrivial. The [Fundamental theorem of Galois theory](fundamental-theorem-of-galois-theory.md) therefore identifies its [fixed field](fixed-field.md) with $\mathbb Q(\eta)$. That [fixed field](fixed-field.md) is exactly $\mathbb Q(\zeta_m)\cap\mathbb R$, proving maximality among real [subfields](subfield.md).
