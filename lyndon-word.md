# Lyndon word

↑ **Parent:** [Combinatorics on words](combinatorics-on-words.md)

A nonempty finite [word](string.md) that is strictly smaller in [lexicographic order](lexicographic-order.md) than each of its nontrivial cyclic rotations. For example, $001$ is a Lyndon word, whereas $010$ is not because its rotation $001$ is smaller.
