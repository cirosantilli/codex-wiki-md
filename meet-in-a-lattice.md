# Meet in a lattice

↑ **Parent:** [Join and meet](join-and-meet.md)

The meet of two elements of a [lattice](lattice.md) is their [greatest lower bound](greatest-lower-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md). For [set families](set-family.md) ordered by inclusion, it is their intersection whenever that intersection lies in the specified [lattice](lattice.md).
