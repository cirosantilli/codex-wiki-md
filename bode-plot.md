# Bode plot

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous-time frequency response](continuous-time-frequency-response.md)

A [Bode plot](bode-plot.md) displays the gain and [oscillation phase](phase-waves.md) of a [frequency response](frequency-response.md) against a logarithmic [frequency](frequency.md) axis. Gain is often shown as $20\log_{10}|G(i\omega)|$ in decibels; phase can use degrees or [radians](radian.md). The [first-order low-pass filter](first-order-low-pass-filter.md) loses approximately $20$ decibels per factor of ten in [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) well above its corner. [Rowell, lecture 32, section 2: logarithmic plots](https://www.ocw.mit.edu/courses/2-004-dynamics-and-control-ii-spring-2008/2a0a97261561177bf1a91b07202bb09a_lecture_32.pdf)
