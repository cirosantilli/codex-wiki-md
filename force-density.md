# Force density

↑ **Parent:** [Force](force-split.md)

A [force density](force-density.md) is force per unit volume, measured in $\mathrm{N\,m^{-3}}$. [Pressure](pressure-split.md) gradients, the divergence of a viscous [stress](stress.md) and gravitational body force have this dimension in a local [momentum](momentum-split.md) balance.
