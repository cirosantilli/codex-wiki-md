# Half-reaction

↑ **Parent:** [Electrochemistry](electrochemistry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Half-reaction)

A [half-reaction](half-reaction.md) is a balanced [chemical equation](chemical-equation.md) for either the [oxidation](oxidation.md) or the [reduction](reduction-chemistry.md) part of a [redox](redox.md) [chemical reaction](chemical-reaction.md), explicitly including exchanged [Electrons](electron.md). Match the electron counts before adding the two [half-reactions](half-reaction.md); the net equation then contains no transferred [Electrons](electron.md). A [half-reaction](half-reaction.md) is bookkeeping, not necessarily an [elementary reaction](elementary-reaction.md). See [Florida State University, lab background: separate electrode reactions](https://www.chem.fsu.edu/chemlab/chm1lab/redox/background.html) and [Daniell cell](daniell-cell.md).
