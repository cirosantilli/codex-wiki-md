# Spherical harmonic matching with a derivative jump

↑ **Parent:** [Laplace equation](laplace-equation.md)

For a regular interior and a decaying exterior harmonic potential with continuous values on the unit sphere, the same coefficient multiplies the two radial branches of each [Legendre polynomial](legendre-polynomial.md) mode. Its exterior-minus-interior radial derivative is $-(2n+1)a_nP_n$. Thus an axisymmetric prescribed jump $j_nP_n(\cos\theta)$ produces coefficient $a_n=-j_n/(2n+1)$. This diagonal matching rule reduces a spherical interface problem to its angular-mode coefficients.
