# Singular prism operator

↑ **Parent:** [Homotopy invariance of homology](homotopy-invariance-of-homology.md)

Triangulate $\Delta^q\times I$ by the simplices with vertices $(v_0,0),\ldots,(v_i,0),(v_i,1),\ldots,(v_q,1)$ and coefficients $(-1)^i$. Composing with a [homotopy](homotopy.md) between f and g gives a degree-one map P on [singular chains](singular-chain.md). Shared faces cancel; the remaining faces give the displayed [chain homotopy](chain-homotopy.md) identity. Hence homotopic maps induce the same map on [singular homology](singular-homology.md).
