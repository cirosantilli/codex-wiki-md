# Cardinality of integer-periodic function spaces

↑ **Parent:** [Periodic function](periodic-function.md)

For fixed positive integer period $n$, integer-valued [periodic functions](periodic-function.md) on $\mathbb Z$ are in [bijection](bijection.md) with $\mathbb Z^n$ and hence form a [countable set](countable-set.md). Their union over periods is a [countable union of countable sets](countable-union-of-countable-sets.md). In contrast, period-one integer-valued [functions](function-split.md) on $\mathbb Q$ can prescribe arbitrary binary values on its infinitely many distinct classes modulo $\mathbb Z$. The [power set](power-set.md) of a countably infinite set injects into them, proving uncountability.
