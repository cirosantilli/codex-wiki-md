# Scalar primary operator

↑ **Parent:** [Conformal primary operator](conformal-primary-operator.md)

A scalar primary operator transforms without a spin rotation and is characterized by its [scaling dimension](scaling-dimension.md). Conformal symmetry fixes its two- and three-point functions up to constants, while a four-point function can depend on conformal cross-ratios.
