# Contact resistance

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md)

[Electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) introduced by a connection between conducting regions. Interface chemistry, contact geometry and microscopic barriers can dominate over the bulk resistance of the connected materials. Four-terminal measurements help separate contact drops from the resistance of a test section.
