# Bilinear degree pairing for elliptic-curve homomorphisms

↑ **Parent:** [Positive definiteness of degree on elliptic-curve homomorphisms](positive-definiteness-of-degree-on-elliptic-curve-homomorphisms.md)

With zero degree assigned to the zero element of the [homomorphism group of elliptic curves](homomorphism-group-of-elliptic-curves.md), the [degree parallelogram law](divisor-proof-of-the-degree-parallelogram-law.md) makes this symmetric pairing additive in both arguments. Its diagonal is the [degree of an isogeny](degree-of-an-isogeny.md); it is positive definite on every finite-dimensional rational span after extension to the [real numbers](real-number.md). The integral polarization $2B(\phi,\psi)$ is integer-valued, while $B$ can be half-integral. For instance, if an [elliptic curve](elliptic-curve.md) has an automorphism $\omega$ with $\omega^2+\omega+1=0$, then $1+\omega=-\omega^2$ has degree one, so $B(1,\omega)=-1/2$.
