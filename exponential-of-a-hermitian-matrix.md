# Exponential of a Hermitian matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix exponential](matrix-exponential.md)

A [Hermitian matrix](hermitian-operator.md) has a unitary [spectral decomposition](spectral-decomposition.md) $A=Q\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_j)Q^\dagger$ with real [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $\lambda_j$. The [matrix exponential respects similarity](matrix-exponential-respects-similarity.md), giving $e^A=Q\operatorname{diag}(e^{\lambda_j})Q^\dagger$. Its [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are positive and its [conjugate transpose](conjugate-transpose.md) equals itself. In particular $v^\dagger e^Av=\sum_j e^{\lambda_j}|(Q^\dagger v)_j|^2>0$ for $v\ne0$, so it is a [Hermitian positive-definite matrix](hermitian-positive-definite-matrix.md).
