# Hodge splitting of Euclidean two-forms

↑ **Parent:** [Hodge star operator](hodge-star-operator.md)

On an oriented [Euclidean metric](euclidean-metric.md) four-space, the [Hodge star operator](hodge-star-operator.md) on two-forms is an orthogonal involution. Its two eigenspaces each have dimension three. A [self-dual two-form](self-dual-differential-form.md) has eigenvalue $+1$, while an [anti-self-dual two-form](anti-self-dual-differential-form.md) has eigenvalue $-1$. The splitting uses the metric-normalized [Riemannian volume form](riemannian-volume-form.md); an arbitrarily rescaled volume in the defining wedge identity would rescale the operator itself.
