# Antibonding orbital

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular orbital](molecular-orbital.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antibonding_orbital)

Occupying an [antibonding molecular orbital](antibonding-orbital.md) opposes a [chemical bond](chemical-bond.md). For equivalent $1s$ [atomic orbitals](atomic-orbital.md) of the [hydrogen atom](hydrogen-atom.md), the difference $\sigma_u^*=\sigma_-$ vanishes on the plane halfway between the [protons](proton.md): every point there is equally far from both centres, so the amplitudes cancel. This nodal plane removes the contribution of this [molecular orbital](molecular-orbital.md) to [Electron](electron.md) density on that plane; it does not make an entire finite region empty. The $1s$-derived [antibonding molecular orbital](antibonding-orbital.md) lies above its [bonding molecular orbital](bonding-orbital.md) partner in [energy](energy.md). See [Field, MIT 5.61, lecture 25, pp. 1–2](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/5-61-physical-chemistry-fall-2017/e9f9413400be87f6a0a512e757d52e3c_MIT5_61F17_lec25.pdf).

In the $\mathrm{He}_2$ minimal model, $(\sigma_u^*)^2$ adds $N_a=2$ and cancels the [bond order](bond-order.md) contribution of $(\sigma_g)^2$. Opposite signs on the two lobes describe [probability amplitudes](probability-amplitude.md), while both lobes have nonnegative [probability density](probability-density.md) after squaring. The star is an orbital label, not an extra [Electron](electron.md) or an [electric charge](electric-charge.md).
