# Myofibril

↑ **Parent:** [Muscle](muscle.md)

A contractile assembly in a skeletal-muscle fibre consists of serial [sarcomeres](sarcomere.md). Their coordinated shortening contributes to shortening of the fibre; each sarcomere contains overlapping thin actin and thick myosin filaments.
