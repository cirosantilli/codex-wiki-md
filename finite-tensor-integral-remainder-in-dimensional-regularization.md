# Finite tensor-integral remainder in dimensional regularization

↑ **Parent:** [Dimensional regularization](dimensional-regularization.md)

Include the measure factor $(2\pi)^{-D}$ and a conventional [renormalization scale](renormalization-scale.md) factor in the displayed integral. The [Lorentz invariant](lorentz-invariance.md) replacement $k_\mu k_\nu\mapsto g_{\mu\nu}k^2/D$ must be performed before taking $D\to4$. For $D=4-\varepsilon$, its scalar integral has pole $i/(8\pi^2\varepsilon)$, while $4/D-1=\varepsilon/4+O(\varepsilon^2)$. Their product has the finite value shown. Setting $D=4$ before integration discards this value and can spoil a [Ward identity](ward-identity.md), even in a final ultraviolet-finite [scattering amplitude](scattering-amplitude.md). This is an elementary example of a vanishing regulator factor multiplying a divergent integral.
