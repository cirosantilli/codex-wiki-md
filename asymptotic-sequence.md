# Asymptotic sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Asymptotic expansion](asymptotic-expansion.md)

Functions $(\phi_n)$ form an asymptotic sequence at $x_0$ when $\phi_{n+1}=o(\phi_n)$. The notation

$$
f\sim\sum_{n\geq0}a_n\phi_n
$$

means that for every $N\geq0$,

$$
f-\sum_{n=0}^Na_n\phi_n=o(\phi_N).
$$

Successive division of the remainder by $\phi_N$ uniquely recovers every coefficient.
