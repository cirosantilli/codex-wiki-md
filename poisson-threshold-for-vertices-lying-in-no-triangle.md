# Poisson threshold for vertices lying in no triangle

↑ **Parent:** [Binomial random graph](binomial-random-graph.md)

For a fixed set of $r$ vertices, exclude all triangles meeting that set. Their total expectation is $r\log(n/c)+o(1)$. Distinct triangle pairs sharing an edge contribute $O_r(n^3p^5)=o(1)$ to the [Janson dependency sum](janson-dependency-sum.md). [Harris' inequality](harris-inequality.md) and [Janson inequality](janson-inequality.md) bound the avoidance probability between matching exponentials, giving $(c/n)^r(1+o(1))$. The [factorial-moment criterion for Poisson convergence](factorial-moment-criterion-for-poisson-convergence.md) then gives the displayed law.
