# Endpoint limits of power-law spherical accretion

↑ **Parent:** [Transonic spherical accretion rate in a power-law potential](transonic-spherical-accretion-rate-in-a-power-law-potential.md)

The dimensionless accretion factor $(1-q\delta)^{q-1/\delta}$ tends to $e^q$ as $\delta\to0$. For $q>0$, it tends to $1$ as $\delta\uparrow1/q$, although the [sonic point](sonic-point.md) moves to the origin. Writing the latter logarithm as $-qh\ln h/(1-h)$ makes the finite limit transparent. These limits connect [transonic spherical accretion rate in a power-law potential](transonic-spherical-accretion-rate-in-a-power-law-potential.md) to the [isothermal equation of state](globally-isothermal-equation-of-state.md) and the limiting critical [adiabatic exponent](heat-capacity-ratio.md).
