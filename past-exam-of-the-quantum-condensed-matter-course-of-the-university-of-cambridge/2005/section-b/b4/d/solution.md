<h1 id="section-b/b4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

In a [heavy fermion material](../../../../../heavy-fermion-material.md), correlated low-energy quasiparticles have a large electronic density of states and effective mass. A [Kondo lattice](../../../../../kondo-lattice.md) with hybridization between localized f-electron states and conduction states is an important setting. The large mass is a low-energy dynamical property, not a change in the electron's bare mass or the ionic mass.

Low-temperature calorimetry measures $C/T\simeq\gamma+\beta T^2$ in a regime where an electronic linear term and a Debye lattice term are appropriate. The large intercept $\gamma=(\pi^2k_B^2/3)D(E_F)$ measures the enhanced quasiparticle density of states, with volume or molar conventions kept consistent. Apply a known heat pulse to a thermally characterized sample platform and measure the temperature rise, then vary the base temperature to separate contributions.

In [low-temperature calorimetry and resistivity of CeAl3](../../../../../low-temperature-calorimetry-and-resistivity-of-ceal3.md), measurements below $0.2\,\mathrm K$ gave $\gamma=1.62\,\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}\,K^{-2}}$ and $\rho=\rho_0+AT^2$ with $A=35\,\mu\Omega\,\mathrm{cm\,K^{-2}}$. The large thermal coefficient and enhanced quadratic transport coefficient are complementary evidence for a strongly renormalized [Fermi liquid](../../../../../fermi-liquid-split.md).[Abstract: measured coefficients and sub-0.2 K range](../../../../../4f-virtual-bound-state-formation-in-ceal3-at-low-temperatures-by-klaus-andres-and-others.md) Enhanced spin susceptibility provides another probe. Where coherent quantum oscillations can be resolved, their thermal damping determines a [cyclotron effective mass](../../../../../cyclotron-effective-mass.md), giving a band-sensitive comparison with the thermodynamic mass inferred from heat capacity.
