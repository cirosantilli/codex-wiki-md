<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use physical fields proportional to the [real part](../../../../real-part.md) of $e^{-i\omega t}$. The [Drude frequency response](../../../../drude-frequency-response.md) is

$$
\sigma(\omega)=\frac{\sigma_0}{1-i\omega\tau},\qquad \boxed{|\sigma(\omega)|^2=\frac{\sigma_0^2}{1+\omega^2\tau^2}.}
$$

Thus the sketch is an even [Lorentzian line shape](../../../../lorentzian-line-shape.md), with maximum $\sigma_0^2$ at zero [frequency](../../../../frequency.md), half that value at $|\omega|=\tau^{-1}$, and an $\omega^{-2}$ tail. The positive-frequency half is shown in the preceding figure. Squaring the [modulus](../../../../modulus.md) differs from plotting either $|\sigma|$ or the [real part](../../../../real-part.md) of $\sigma$.

For a single carrier species, the [plasma frequency](../../../../plasma-frequency.md) and [electrical conductivity](../../../../electrical-conductivity.md) satisfy

$$
\omega_p^2=\frac{nq^2}{m\epsilon_0},\qquad \sigma_0=\frac{nq^2\tau}{m}=\epsilon_0\omega_p^2\tau.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{\omega_{1/2}=\frac1\tau=\frac{\epsilon_0\omega_p^2}{\sigma_0}=\frac{(8.854\times10^{-12})(5.6\times10^{15})^2}{9.9\times10^6}\simeq2.80\times10^{13}\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}.}
$$

The corresponding [relaxation time](../../../../relaxation-time.md) is $\tau\simeq3.57\times10^{-14}\,\mathrm s$. This is an [angular frequency](../../../../angular-frequency.md); the ordinary [frequency](../../../../frequency.md) is $\boxed{f_{1/2}=\omega_{1/2}/(2\pi)\simeq4.46\times10^{12}\,\mathrm{Hz}}$. The units of [electrical conductivity](../../../../electrical-conductivity.md) used in the calculation are $\mathrm{\Omega^{-1}m^{-1}}$, consistently with the SI plasma-frequency relation.
