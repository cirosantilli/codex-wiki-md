<h1 id="section-b/5/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

At nonzero [band hybridization](../../../../../band-hybridization.md), the crossing becomes an [avoided crossing](../../../../../avoided-crossing.md). The separation at $k_c$ is $2\alpha$. If $\alpha\ll E_{\rm fl}$, appreciable mixing occurs only where $|Ak^2-E_{\rm fl}|\lesssim\alpha$; away from that region each branch retains nearly its original orbital character. For $\alpha\sim E_{\rm fl}$, the avoided crossing is broad and both branches are substantially reshaped, as shown in the figure.

Use $E_-$ for the lower branch and $E_+$ for the upper one. For $\alpha>0$,

$$
E_-(k)<E_{\rm fl}<E_+(k),\qquad
\min_k E_+(k)=E_+(0)=\frac{E_{\rm fl}+\sqrt{E_{\rm fl}^2+4\alpha^2}}2.
$$

The lower branch increases towards $E_{\rm fl}$ from below. Thus an indirect gap exists in the exactly flat-band model. In the unbounded-parabola limit its lower bound is

$$
\boxed{\Delta_{\rm ind}=\frac{\sqrt{E_{\rm fl}^2+4\alpha^2}-E_{\rm fl}}2
\simeq\frac{\alpha^2}{E_{\rm fl}}\quad(\alpha\ll E_{\rm fl}).}
$$

A finite [Brillouin zone](../../../../../brillouin-zone.md) gives a finite maximum of $E_-$ below $E_{\rm fl}$ and hence a slightly larger indirect gap. This [hybridization gap](../../../../../hybridization-gap.md) differs from the local direct gap $2\alpha$.

The material can therefore be a [band insulator](../../../../../band-insulator.md) when the lower band is full and the upper band empty. With two [spin](../../../../../spin.md)-degenerate orbitals per [unit cell](../../../../../unit-cell.md), the required filling is

$$
\boxed{2\text{ electrons per unit cell, i.e. half of the combined four-state capacity}.}
$$

With spin degeneracy removed, replace two by one for each independent spin sector. Other fillings are metallic in this noninteracting model.
