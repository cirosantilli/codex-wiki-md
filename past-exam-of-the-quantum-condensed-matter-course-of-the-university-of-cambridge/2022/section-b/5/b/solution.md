A concrete example is **[samarium hexaboride](../../../../../samarium-hexaboride.md)**, $\mathrm{SmB}_6$: narrow, nearly localized $4f$-derived states mix with a much more dispersive $5d$-derived [conduction band](../../../../../conduction-band.md). Such [band hybridization](../../../../../band-hybridization.md) is the low-energy mechanism behind a bulk [Kondo insulator](../../../../../kondo-insulator.md) gap. The two-band [Hamiltonian](../../../../../hamiltonian.md) is an effective illustration; correlations help set its narrow-band energy and renormalized mixing.[Introduction: nearly localized flat f bands hybridize with a dispersive d-derived conduction band in SmB6](https://www.nature.com/articles/ncomms3991)
