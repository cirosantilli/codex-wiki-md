<h1 id="section-b/4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Take $q=-e$ as the signed electron charge. The [Lorentz oscillator model](../../../../../lorentz-oscillator-model.md) is a driven damped bound charge:

$$
\boxed{m\ddot{\mathbf u}+m\gamma\dot{\mathbf u}+m\omega_0^2\mathbf u=q\mathbf E(t).}
$$

The first term is inertia, the second is a phenomenological frictional loss of coherent motion to other degrees of freedom, and the third is the linear restoring force from binding to the atom. The driving term is the [electric force](../../../../../electric-force.md). The natural [angular frequency](../../../../../angular-frequency.md) $\omega_0$ represents the [electronic transition](../../../../../electronic-transition.md); this classical oscillator describes its near-resonant [linear response](../../../../../linear-response-theory.md). The friction convention is $m\gamma\dot u$, so no factor two is hidden in $\gamma$.
