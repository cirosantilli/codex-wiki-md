<h1 id="section-b/6/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Ordinary doped [semiconductor devices](../../../../../semiconductor-device.md) need a regime where their [donor impurities](../../../../../donor-impurity.md) or [acceptor impurities](../../../../../acceptor-impurity.md) supply mobile [charge carriers](../../../../../charge-carrier.md) while intrinsic thermal excitation remains sufficiently small.

At low [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), [donor impurities](../../../../../donor-impurity.md) or [acceptor impurities](../../../../../acceptor-impurity.md) may no longer be ionized. [Dopant freeze-out](../../../../../dopant-freeze-out.md) reduces the [carrier density](../../../../../carrier-density.md), raises [electrical resistance](../../../../../electrical-resistance.md) and changes the intended [carrier injection](../../../../../carrier-injection.md) and [p-n junction](../../../../../p-n-junction.md) response. At high [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) the [intrinsic carrier concentration](../../../../../intrinsic-carrier-concentration.md) grows as

$$
\boxed{n_i\propto T^{3/2}e^{-E_g/(2k_BT)}}
$$

when [band effective masses](../../../../../band-effective-mass.md) and [band gap](../../../../../band-gap.md) are treated as constant. Once it becomes comparable with the net doping density, it overwhelms the intended electron-hole imbalance; reverse leakage and dissipation increase and useful p/n control is lost.

The usual operating window lies between freeze-out and intrinsic conduction, with mobility and thermal management placing further practical limits. This is a statement about conventional doped-device operation, not a fundamental prohibition on engineered cryogenic or wide-bandgap devices.
