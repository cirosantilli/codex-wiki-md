<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A [self-assembled InAs quantum-dot single-photon source](../../../../self-assembled-inas-quantum-dot-single-photon-source.md) uses small [indium arsenide](../../../../indium-arsenide.md) islands inside a larger-gap [gallium arsenide](../../../../gallium-arsenide.md) host. Strain during epitaxial growth favors islands after a thin wetting layer has formed. Their characteristic diameter is of order $10$–$30\,\mathrm{nm}$ and their height a few nanometres, for example $2$–$5\,\mathrm{nm}$. Growth conditions determine the areal density: low-density samples suitable for isolating dots can be around $10^8$–$10^9\,\mathrm{cm^{-2}}$, while ordinary dense layers can be much denser. The particular electrically driven device underlying the supplied image had about $5\times10^8\,\mathrm{cm^{-2}}$. These are characteristic scales, not dimensions deducible from the emission image.[Device description and figure 1, printed p.103](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1066790)

[Quantum confinement](../../../../quantum-confinement.md) in the dot traps both [electrons](../../../../electron.md) and [electron holes](../../../../electron-hole.md) in three directions, giving discrete [energy levels](../../../../energy-level.md). In a junction device, injected carriers relax into these levels. [Electron-hole recombination](../../../../electron-hole-recombination.md) then emits a [photon](../../../../photon.md), and the dot must be repopulated before emitting again on that same transition. Spatial selection of one dot, spectral selection of one line, and well-separated injection pulses make this a [single-photon source](../../../../single-photon-source.md). [Photon antibunching](../../../../photon-antibunching.md), rather than low average brightness alone, establishes suppression of simultaneous [photons](../../../../photon.md). In particular, collecting both [photons](../../../../photon.md) of a cascade without selecting a line would not produce exactly one collected [photon](../../../../photon.md) per excitation.

The lower-current feature $X$ is the transition from one confined [exciton](../../../../exciton.md) to the empty dot. The higher-current feature $X_2$ is the first step of the [biexciton](../../../../biexciton.md) cascade $XX\to X\to0$: it becomes strong when the dot can contain two electron-hole pairs. In the weak-pumping limit the probabilities of one and two pairs scale approximately as $I$ and $I^2$, respectively. Coulomb and confinement energies shift the two transitions relative to one another; $X_2$ is not a [photon](../../../../photon.md) of twice the $X$ [energy](../../../../energy-split.md). In this particular image the $X_2$ [photon](../../../../photon.md) is about $4$–$5\,\mathrm{meV}$ higher in [energy](../../../../energy-split.md). With $B_{XX}=2E_X-E_{XX}$, this is a negative [biexciton](../../../../biexciton.md) [binding energy](../../../../binding-energy.md), since $E_{XX}-E_X=E_X-B_{XX}$.

For the mass estimate, the original plot places the $X$ line at approximately $E_X=1.3945\,\mathrm{eV}$, with graph-reading precision of order $0.5\,\mathrm{meV}$. Under the prescribed identification of [photon](../../../../photon.md) [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) with the full lowest [exciton](../../../../exciton.md) excitation [energy](../../../../energy-split.md),

$$
E_B=E_g-E_X\simeq1.415-1.3945=0.0205\,\mathrm{eV}.
$$

The three-dimensional screened [Wannier-Mott exciton](../../../../wannier-mott-exciton.md) has the hydrogenic [exciton binding energy](../../../../exciton-binding-energy.md)

$$
E_B=\frac{\mu e^4}{2(4\pi\epsilon_0\varepsilon_r)^2\hbar^2}=13.6057\,\mathrm{eV}\,\frac{\mu/m_0}{\varepsilon_r^2},\qquad \frac1\mu=\frac1{m_e^*}+\frac1{m_h^*}.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{\frac\mu{m_0}=\frac{(0.0205)(15)^2}{13.6057}\simeq0.339,\qquad \mu\simeq3.09\times10^{-31}\,\mathrm{kg}.}
$$

Reading the line as $1.395\,\mathrm{eV}$ instead gives $0.331m_0$; **about $0.33$–$0.34m_0$** is an appropriate precision. This inference uses the supplied pair-creation [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) and a three-dimensional screened hydrogenic model. In a real dot, strain and confinement also alter transition energies, so it is an effective model estimate and does not independently determine either individual carrier mass.
