<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A normal [Fermi liquid](../../../../fermi-liquid-split.md) has interacting fermions whose low-energy excitations can be followed continuously from those of a [Fermi gas](../../../../fermi-gas.md), provided no phase transition destroys that correspondence. A [Landau quasiparticle](../../../../landau-quasiparticle.md) is a particle together with the surrounding many-body response. It has the appropriate charge and [spin](../../../../spin.md), a renormalized dispersion and a long lifetime close to the [Fermi surface](../../../../fermi-surface.md). Interactions remain in both the [quasiparticle](../../../../quasiparticle.md) parameters and the interactions between [quasiparticles](../../../../quasiparticle.md); they have not been discarded.

Near an isotropic [Fermi surface](../../../../fermi-surface.md),

$$
\xi_{\mathbf k}=\varepsilon^*_{\mathbf k}-\mu\simeq\hbar v_F(k-k_F),\qquad v_F=\frac1\hbar\left.\frac{\partial\varepsilon^*_{\mathbf k}}{\partial k}\right|_{k_F},\qquad m^*=\frac{\hbar k_F}{v_F}.
$$

The last relation defines the local isotropic [Fermi-liquid effective mass](../../../../fermi-liquid-effective-mass.md). The [Landau quasiparticle energy functional](../../../../landau-quasiparticle-energy-functional.md) expresses how changing occupations shifts other [quasiparticle](../../../../quasiparticle.md) energies:

$$
\delta\varepsilon^*_{\mathbf k\sigma}=\sum_{\mathbf k'\sigma'}f_{\mathbf k\sigma,\mathbf k'\sigma'}\delta n_{\mathbf k'\sigma'},
$$

with normalization of the sum absorbed into $f$. Its angular components give [Landau parameters](../../../../landau-parameter.md) describing collective density and [spin](../../../../spin.md) response.

The [Pauli exclusion principle](../../../../pauli-exclusion-principle.md) severely restricts the available final states for low-energy collisions. In an ordinary three-dimensional [Fermi liquid](../../../../fermi-liquid-split.md) with regular interactions,

$$
\boxed{\tau^{-1}\propto\xi^2+(\pi k_BT)^2.}
$$

At zero [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) the decay width decreases faster than $|\xi|$, making [quasiparticles](../../../../quasiparticle.md) increasingly sharp near the [Fermi surface](../../../../fermi-surface.md). Their coherent spectral weight is the [quasiparticle residue](../../../../quasiparticle-residue.md) $0<Z\leq1$, and the true [electron](../../../../electron.md) [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) distribution has a discontinuity of height $Z$ at zero [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md). The rest of the spectral weight is incoherent. The [quasiparticle](../../../../quasiparticle.md) occupation itself still follows a [Fermi-Dirac distribution](../../../../fermi-dirac-distribution.md).

The low-temperature electronic [heat capacity](../../../../heat-capacity.md) is linear, $C_V/\mathcal V=(\pi^2/3)k_B^2N^*(0)T$, where $N^*(0)$ is the spin-summed [quasiparticle](../../../../quasiparticle.md) [density of states](../../../../density-of-states.md). In an isotropic spin-one-half model, the uniform [spin](../../../../spin.md) susceptibility and [compressibility](../../../../compressibility.md) take the forms

$$
\chi=\frac{\mu_0\mu_B^2N^*(0)}{1+F_0^a},\qquad \kappa=\frac{N^*(0)}{n^2(1+F_0^s)}.
$$

Thus enhanced effective mass and residual interactions can separately change observable responses. The familiar $T^2$ [electrical resistivity](../../../../electrical-resistivity.md) requires a mechanism relaxing electrical current, such as allowed Umklapp processes; collisions in a perfectly clean Galilean-invariant continuum conserve total [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) and do not alone produce a finite dc resistivity.

[Zero sound](../../../../zero-sound.md) is a collisionless collective density [oscillation](../../../../oscillation.md) sustained by the [quasiparticle](../../../../quasiparticle.md) interaction; ordinary first sound occurs when collisions establish local equilibrium during a cycle. Metals and normal liquid [helium-3](../../../../helium-3.md) supply important examples. The description fails in phases with no sufficiently sharp low-energy [quasiparticles](../../../../quasiparticle.md), such as generic interacting one-dimensional systems, and must be modified after pairing or another ordering transition.
