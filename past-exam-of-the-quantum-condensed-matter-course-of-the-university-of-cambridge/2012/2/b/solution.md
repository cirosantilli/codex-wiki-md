↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Different measurements reveal different aspects of [electronic band structure](../../../../electronic-band-structure.md); a convincing determination combines their constraints.

**Angle-resolved photoemission.** In [angle-resolved photoemission spectroscopy](../../../../angle-resolved-photoemission-spectroscopy.md), [photons](../../../../photon.md) of known [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) eject occupied [electrons](../../../../electron.md). Measuring their [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) and emission angle reconstructs [binding energy](../../../../binding-energy.md) and the component of [crystal momentum](../../../../crystal-momentum.md) parallel to the surface:

$$
E_B=h\nu-\Phi-E_{\rm kin},\qquad
k_\parallel=\frac{\sqrt{2m_eE_{\rm kin}}}{\hbar}\sin\theta.
$$

Here $\Phi$ is the sample [work function](../../../../work-function.md) when [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) is referenced to the nearby [vacuum](../../../../vacuum.md); an analyzer [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) scale requires its own work-function calibration. [Momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) is conserved modulo a surface [reciprocal lattice](../../../../reciprocal-lattice.md) vector, neglecting [photon](../../../../photon.md) [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md). Tracking the peaks maps occupied [dispersion relations](../../../../dispersion-relation-split.md) and the [Fermi surface](../../../../fermi-surface.md). The measured intensity also contains the [matrix element](../../../../matrix-element.md), the [Fermi-Dirac distribution](../../../../fermi-dirac-distribution.md) and the [spectral function](../../../../spectral-function.md), so an absent bright feature does not establish an absent band. For a three-dimensional solid, the perpendicular [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) requires a final-state model. [Inverse photoemission spectroscopy](../../../../inverse-photoemission-spectroscopy.md) probes unoccupied states by injecting [electrons](../../../../electron.md) and detecting emitted [photons](../../../../photon.md).

**Optical [spectroscopy](../../../../spectroscopy.md).** [Photon absorption](../../../../photon-absorption.md) and [ellipsometry](../../../../ellipsometry.md) measure [interband transitions](../../../../interband-transition.md), optical [selection rules](../../../../selection-rule.md) and the complex dielectric response. Because optical [photon](../../../../photon.md) [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) is small on the scale of the [Brillouin zone](../../../../brillouin-zone.md), direct transitions approximately preserve [crystal momentum](../../../../crystal-momentum.md). Their onset constrains a [direct band gap](../../../../direct-band-gap.md); a threshold involving a [phonon](../../../../phonon.md) can reveal an [indirect band gap](../../../../indirect-band-gap.md). Peak positions constrain [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) differences between bands, but the response also depends on the [joint density of states](../../../../joint-density-of-states.md) and optical [matrix elements](../../../../matrix-element.md), so it does not uniquely determine every $E_n(\mathbf k)$.

**Quantum [oscillations](../../../../oscillation.md) and [cyclotron resonance](../../../../cyclotron-resonance.md).** In clean samples at low [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md), the [de Haas-van Alphen effect](../../../../de-haas-van-alphen-effect.md) measures [oscillations](../../../../oscillation.md) in [magnetization](../../../../magnetization.md) and the [Shubnikov-de Haas effect](../../../../shubnikov-de-haas-effect.md) measures [oscillations](../../../../oscillation.md) in [electrical resistivity](../../../../electrical-resistivity.md) as the inverse [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) is varied. Their period in inverse [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) is $1/F$, where [Onsager quantization](../../../../onsager-quantization.md) relates $F$ to an extremal Fermi-surface area perpendicular to the field:

$$
F=\frac{\hbar}{2\pi e}A_{\rm ext}.
$$

Rotating the [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) maps the geometry of extremal orbits. The thermal [damping](../../../../damping.md) determines the [cyclotron effective mass](../../../../cyclotron-effective-mass.md),

$$
m_c=\frac{\hbar^2}{2\pi}\frac{\partial A}{\partial E},
$$

with the appropriate magnitude for a hole orbit. [Cyclotron resonance](../../../../cyclotron-resonance.md) supplies the corresponding dynamical mass through $\omega_c=eB/m_c$. These are detailed probes near the [Fermi level](../../../../fermi-level.md), rather than measurements of all occupied bands. [Diffraction](../../../../diffraction.md) supplies the [reciprocal lattice](../../../../reciprocal-lattice.md) needed to label the measured momenta, but [diffraction](../../../../diffraction.md) by itself is not a measurement of electronic dispersion.
