<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a [hydrogenic donor state](../../../../hydrogenic-donor-state.md), [dielectric screening](../../../../dielectric-screening.md) replaces $\epsilon_0$ by $\epsilon_r\epsilon_0$ and the [electron mass](../../../../electron-mass.md) by the [band effective mass](../../../../band-effective-mass.md). The characteristic orbital radius is therefore

$$
a^*=\frac{4\pi\epsilon_0\epsilon_r\hbar^2}{m^*e^2}
=\epsilon_r\frac{m_e}{m^*}a_0.
$$

Using $a_0=0.0529177\,\mathrm{nm}$ gives

$$
\boxed{a^*=120a_0=6.35\,\mathrm{nm}.}
$$

A [donor-state overlap estimate](../../../../donor-state-overlap-estimate.md) compares this radius with the mean dopant spacing $d\sim N_D^{-1/3}$. Taking $d\sim a^*$ yields

$$
\boxed{N_D\sim(a^*)^{-3}=3.91\times10^{24}\,\mathrm{m^{-3}}
=3.91\times10^{18}\,\mathrm{cm^{-3}}.}
$$

There is no sharp geometrical onset: hydrogenic [wavefunctions](../../../../wave-function-split.md) have exponential tails. If “overlap” is defined by neighbouring orbital _diameters_ touching, $d\sim2a^*$, the corresponding estimate is $4.88\times10^{23}\,\mathrm{m^{-3}}$, smaller by a factor of eight. These are conventions for the onset of appreciable [orbital overlap](../../../../orbital-overlap.md), not an exact critical density for a [metal-insulator transition](../../../../metal-insulator-transition.md).
