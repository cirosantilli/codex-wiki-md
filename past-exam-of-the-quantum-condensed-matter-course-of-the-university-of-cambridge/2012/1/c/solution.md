<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [band effective mass](../../../../band-effective-mass.md) is determined by the [second derivative](../../../../second-derivative.md) of the [dispersion relation](../../../../dispersion-relation-split.md):

$$
\frac1{m^*(k)}=\frac1{\hbar^2}\frac{d^2E}{dk^2}.
$$

With the stated sign convention and $t>0$, the band minimum is at $k=0$. Expanding the [cosine](../../../../cosine.md), or differentiating twice, gives

$$
E(k)=E_0-2t+ta^2k^2+O(k^4),\qquad
\left.\frac{d^2E}{dk^2}\right|_0=2ta^2.
$$

Comparison with a free [electron](../../../../electron.md)'s [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) $\hbar^2k^2/(2m_e)$ therefore requires

$$
\boxed{t=\frac{\hbar^2}{2m_ea^2}
=6.78\times10^{-20}\,\mathrm J
=0.423\,\mathrm{eV}.}
$$

The [hopping integral](../../../../hopping-integral.md) has units of [energy](../../../../energy-split.md). A negative $t$ would move the minimum to the edge of the [Brillouin zone](../../../../brillouin-zone.md) and give the same positive bottom-of-band [effective mass](../../../../band-effective-mass.md) with $|t|$ in this expression.
