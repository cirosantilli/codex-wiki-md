<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A positive [electric potential difference](../../../../electric-potential-difference.md) between gate and substrate attracts [electrons](../../../../electron.md) towards the insulating interface and repels the mobile [electron holes](../../../../electron-hole.md) of the [p-type semiconductor](../../../../p-type-semiconductor.md) substrate. The exposed, negatively charged [acceptor impurities](../../../../acceptor-impurity.md) form a [depletion region](../../../../depletion-region.md); the thin mobile [electron](../../../../electron.md) layer at its surface is the [semiconductor inversion layer](../../../../semiconductor-inversion-layer.md). The [conduction band](../../../../conduction-band.md) and [valence band](../../../../valence-band.md) bend _downwards_ towards the positively biased gate, because the [electron](../../../../electron.md) [potential energy](../../../../potential-energy.md) is $-e\psi$.

<a id="1/a/image-band-bending-and-charge-separation-at-the-conventional-mos-inversion-threshold"></a>
![](../../../../past-exam-of-the-quantum-condensed-matter-course-of-the-university-of-cambridge/2012-mos-inversion.png)

**[Figure 1](#1/a/image-band-bending-and-charge-separation-at-the-conventional-mos-inversion-threshold). Band bending and charge separation at the conventional MOS inversion threshold**.

To specify the conventional [MOS inversion threshold](../../../../mos-inversion-threshold.md), let $N_A$ be the ionized acceptor density, $n_i$ the [intrinsic carrier concentration](../../../../intrinsic-carrier-concentration.md), and

$$
\phi_F=\frac{k_BT}{e}\log\frac{N_A}{n_i}>0.
$$

In the neutral bulk the intrinsic level lies $e\phi_F$ above the [semiconductor](../../../../semiconductor-split.md) [chemical potential](../../../../chemical-potential-split.md). A positive surface potential $\psi_s$ lowers both band edges by $e\psi_s$, giving, in the nondegenerate approximation,

$$
p_s=N_Ae^{-e\psi_s/(k_BT)},\qquad
n_s=\frac{n_i^2}{N_A}e^{e\psi_s/(k_BT)}.
$$

At $\psi_s=\phi_F$, the surface is intrinsic: $n_s=p_s=n_i$. The usual onset of _strong inversion_ instead means

$$
\boxed{\psi_s\simeq2\phi_F,\qquad n_s\simeq N_A.}
$$

Thus the surface [electron density](../../../../electron-density.md) has grown to the bulk majority-carrier density. The [chemical potential](../../../../chemical-potential-split.md) need not actually enter the [conduction band](../../../../conduction-band.md). The phrase “just forms” can also refer to the earlier weak-inversion crossover; the diagram uses the conventional strong-inversion threshold and labels it explicitly.

Within the [depletion approximation](../../../../depletion-approximation.md) the negative [space charge](../../../../space-charge.md) is approximately $-eN_A$ over a width

$$
W_d=\sqrt{\frac{2\epsilon_s\psi_s}{eN_A}},\qquad
W_{d,\mathrm{th}}=\sqrt{\frac{4\epsilon_s\phi_F}{eN_A}},
$$

where $\epsilon_s$ is the absolute [semiconductor](../../../../semiconductor-split.md) [permittivity](../../../../permittivity-split.md). Its outer edge is where the bands become flat and mobile [electron holes](../../../../electron-hole.md) recover their bulk density. The [electrical insulator](../../../../electrical-insulator.md) blocks steady carrier transport while sustaining an [electric field](../../../../electric-field-split.md); the [metal](../../../../metal.md) gate supplies positive surface charge balancing depletion and inversion charge. Under gate bias, the gate and substrate [electrochemical potentials](../../../../electrochemical-potential.md) are different, so a single equilibrium [Fermi level](../../../../fermi-level.md) should not be drawn through the whole biased stack.
