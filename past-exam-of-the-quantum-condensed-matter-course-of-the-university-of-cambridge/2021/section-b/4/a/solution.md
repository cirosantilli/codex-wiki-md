<h1 id="section-b/4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a [Hamiltonian](../../../../../hamiltonian.md) with a lattice-periodic potential $V(\mathbf r+\mathbf R)=V(\mathbf r)$, its simultaneous [energy eigenstates](../../../../../energy-eigenstate.md) and lattice-translation eigenstates can be chosen in the [Bloch wave](../../../../../bloch-state.md) form

$$
\boxed{\psi_{n\mathbf k}(\mathbf r)=e^{i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf r}u_{n\mathbf k}(\mathbf r),\qquad
u_{n\mathbf k}(\mathbf r+\mathbf R)=u_{n\mathbf k}(\mathbf r).}
$$

Equivalently, $\psi_{n\mathbf k}(\mathbf r+\mathbf R)=e^{i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf R}\psi_{n\mathbf k}(\mathbf r)$. The index $n$ labels an [energy band](../../../../../energy-band.md); [crystal momentum](../../../../../crystal-momentum.md) $\hbar\mathbf k$ is defined modulo a [reciprocal lattice](../../../../../reciprocal-lattice.md) vector. These are the content of [Bloch's theorem](../../../../../bloch-s-theorem-split.md).
