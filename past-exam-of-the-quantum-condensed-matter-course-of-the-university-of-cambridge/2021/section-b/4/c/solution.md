<h1 id="section-b/4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A site in a [primitive cubic lattice](../../../../../primitive-cubic-lattice.md) has six nearest neighbours, at $\pm a\hat{\mathbf x}$, $\pm a\hat{\mathbf y}$ and $\pm a\hat{\mathbf z}$. Repeating the [Bloch state](../../../../../bloch-state.md) calculation with equal [hopping integrals](../../../../../hopping-integral.md) $t$ along all six bonds gives

$$
\boxed{E(\mathbf k)=E_0+2t[\cos(k_xa)+\cos(k_ya)+\cos(k_za)].}
$$

The cosine sum ranges from $-3$ to $3$ within the first [Brillouin zone](../../../../../brillouin-zone.md). Therefore

$$
E_{\min}=E_0-6|t|,\qquad E_{\max}=E_0+6|t|,\qquad
\boxed{W=E_{\max}-E_{\min}=12|t|.}
$$

For $t<0$ the minimum is at the zone centre; for $t>0$ the maximum is there.
