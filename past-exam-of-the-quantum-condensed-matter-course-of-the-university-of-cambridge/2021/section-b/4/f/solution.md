<h1 id="section-b/4/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

The [electronic bandwidth](../../../../../electronic-bandwidth.md) is $W(a)=24E_1e^{-a/a_1}$. With $a$ reduced from $a_1$ to $0.99a_1$,

$$
\boxed{\frac{\Delta W}{W}=\frac{W(0.99a_1)}{W(a_1)}-1
=e^{0.01}-1\simeq0.01005.}
$$

Thus the [energy band](../../../../../energy-band.md) widens by approximately **1.005 percent**, or **1 percent to first order**. Compression increases the orbital coupling.
