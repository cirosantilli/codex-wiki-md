<h1 id="section-b/4/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Taking $E_1>0$ so that the zone centre is a minimum, the [effective mass for exponentially decaying tight-binding hopping](../../../../../effective-mass-for-exponentially-decaying-tight-binding-hopping.md) is

$$
\boxed{m^*(a)=\frac{\hbar^2}{4E_1a^2}e^{a/a_1}.}
$$

Its logarithmic derivative is

$$
\frac1{m^*}\frac{dm^*}{da}=\frac1{a_1}-\frac2a.
$$

The curve decreases for $a<2a_1$, has a unique minimum at $a=2a_1$, and increases for $a>2a_1$:

$$
\boxed{m^*_{\min}=\frac{\hbar^2\exp(2)}{16E_1a_1^2}.}
$$

The formal limits are an $a^{-2}$ divergence as $a\to0$ and an exponential divergence as $a\to\infty$. The small-separation extrapolation eventually invalidates the localized-orbital [tight-binding model](../../../../../tight-binding.md); the sketch describes the specified formula.
