<h1 id="section-b/6/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

At equilibrium the [diffusion currents](../../../../../diffusion-current.md) and [drift currents](../../../../../drift-current.md) cancel separately for each carrier species. Electron diffusion is from n to p, while the [electric field](../../../../../electric-field-split.md) drives electrons from p to n; hole diffusion is from p to n, while hole drift is from n to p. The net current is zero, although these opposing microscopic fluxes are present. In conventional-current signs, both majority diffusion contributions point p to n and the balancing drift contributions point n to p.

[Forward bias](../../../../../forward-bias.md), $V_{\rm ext}>0$, reduces the barrier and enhances majority-carrier diffusion and [minority carrier](../../../../../minority-carrier.md) injection. The ideal low-injection [Shockley diode equation](../../../../../shockley-diode-equation.md) gives

$$
\boxed{J=J_s\left[e^{eV_{\rm ext}/(k_BT)}-1\right],}
$$

where positive current is p to n. For [reverse bias](../../../../../reverse-bias.md), majority diffusion is suppressed and the field sweeps thermally supplied minority carriers across the junction, giving approximately $J=-J_s$. This saturation is limited by carrier supply rather than a current proportional to the field. Depletion-layer generation, recombination and eventual [diode reverse breakdown](../../../../../diode-reverse-breakdown.md) modify the ideal result.
