<h1 id="section-a/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

For [state counting in a Bloch band](../../../../state-counting-in-a-bloch-band.md), take a [primitive cubic lattice](../../../../primitive-cubic-lattice.md) of spacing $a$ and impose [periodic boundary conditions](../../../../periodic-boundary-conditions.md) on lengths $L_i=N_i a$. The allowed [wavevectors](../../../../wavevector.md) are $k_i=2\pi l_i/L_i$. The first [Brillouin zone](../../../../brillouin-zone.md) has side $2\pi/a$, so it contains $N_i$ distinct values in each direction and hence $N_xN_yN_z=N$ wavevectors. Each orbital-derived [energy band](../../../../energy-band.md) has one state per wavevector per [spin](../../../../spin.md) orientation. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\text{states per band in the whole crystal}=2N,\qquad
\text{states per atom per band}=2.}
$$

The phrase “per atom” attached to $2N$ in the PDF is a counting error: $2N$ is the total, not the per-atom count.

By the [Pauli exclusion principle](../../../../pauli-exclusion-principle.md), a monovalent crystal supplies $N$ valence [electrons](../../../../electron.md) and half-fills a spin-degenerate band; its [Fermi level](../../../../fermi-level.md) lies within allowed states, giving a [metal](../../../../metal.md) in the independent-electron picture. Two electrons per atom can fill one band. If a [band gap](../../../../band-gap.md) separates it from all empty bands, the crystal is a [band insulator](../../../../band-insulator.md). An even valence count only permits insulation: overlapping bands can still produce a [metal](../../../../metal.md). Conversely, electron interactions can produce a [Mott insulator](../../../../mott-insulator.md), and symmetry breaking can permit insulation outside this simple spin-degenerate [electronic band structure](../../../../electronic-band-structure.md) criterion. The relevant count in a crystal with a basis is per [primitive unit cell](../../../../primitive-unit-cell.md).
