<h1 id="section-b/b1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [hydrogenic donor state](../../../../../hydrogenic-donor-state.md) radius is the [Bohr radius](../../../../../bohr-radius.md) rescaled by screening and [effective mass](../../../../../band-effective-mass.md):

$$
\boxed{a_B^*=a_0\frac{\varepsilon_r}{m^*/m_e}=(0.0529\,\mathrm{nm})\frac{16}{0.2}=4.23\,\mathrm{nm}.}
$$

The [donor-state overlap estimate](../../../../../donor-state-overlap-estimate.md) compares the mean spacing $n_D^{-1/3}$ to this radius. Taking the spacing as $a_B^*$ gives $n_D\sim(a_B^*)^{-3}=1.32\times10^{25}\,\mathrm{m^{-3}}=1.32\times10^{19}\,\mathrm{cm^{-3}}$. Taking it as the orbital diameter gives a factor eight less, $1.65\times10^{18}\,\mathrm{cm^{-3}}$. Thus a geometric estimate is **roughly $10^{18}$–$10^{19}\,\mathrm{cm^{-3}}$**, with its contact convention stated. The exponential tails do not have a sharp overlap boundary, and this estimate should not be identified with a precise metal-insulator transition threshold.
