<h1 id="section-b/b2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A partly filled one-dimensional [energy band](../../../../../energy-band.md) is especially sensitive to a lattice modulation at $2k_F$. At half filling, $2k_F=\pi/a$, so the [Peierls distortion](../../../../../peierls-distortion.md) alternates the bond lengths and doubles the primitive unit cell. Mixing states at the Fermi points opens a [band gap](../../../../../band-gap.md), lowering the energy of occupied states. In the ideal weak-coupling, zero-temperature chain, the electronic gain behaves as $-u^2\ln(1/|u|)$ while the elastic cost is proportional to $u^2$, favouring a small distortion. The [dimerized tight-binding chain](../../../../../dimerized-tight-binding-chain.md) gives an explicit band calculation.

The structural signature is extra diffraction intensity at the modulation wavevector; optical absorption can detect the gap and transport can detect loss of metallic conduction. A dynamic precursor is a [Kohn anomaly](../../../../../kohn-anomaly.md), with phonon softening near $2k_F$. [Inelastic neutron scattering of TTF-TCNQ](../../../../../inelastic-neutron-scattering-of-ttf-tcnq.md), using a deuterated sample over $60$–$300\,\mathrm K$, found an anomaly near $2k_F$ developing below about $150\,\mathrm K$ as the $54\,\mathrm K$ transition was approached. This probes a lattice mode coupled to the electronic instability rather than inferring distortion from resistance alone.[Abstract: inelastic neutron scattering, temperature range, 2kF anomaly and transition temperature](../../../../../phonon-dispersion-and-kohn-anomaly-in-tetrathiafulvalene-tetracyanoquinodimethane-ttf-tcnq-by-gen-shirane-and-others.md)

Away from half filling, the modulation need not simply double the lattice. Finite-temperature ordering in real quasi-one-dimensional solids also involves interchain coupling and fluctuations; the ideal isolated-chain argument alone does not establish a finite-temperature transition.
