↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In a [heavy fermion material](../../../../../heavy-fermion-material.md), interactions of narrow, often $f$-derived states with conduction electrons produce low-energy [quasiparticles](../../../../../quasiparticle.md) with very large thermodynamic [effective mass](../../../../../band-effective-mass.md). A coherent [Kondo lattice](../../../../../kondo-lattice.md) can form a narrow low-energy band, so the large mass is an interaction-renormalized response rather than a larger bare electron mass.

Two concrete experimental signatures are an enormous linear [electronic heat capacity coefficient](../../../../../electronic-heat-capacity-coefficient.md) and a strongly enhanced quadratic low-temperature [electrical resistivity](../../../../../electrical-resistivity.md). Calorimetry separates $C=\gamma T+\beta T^3$ by plotting $C/T$ against $T^2$; the intercept measures the electronic coefficient rather than a lattice contribution. Four-terminal transport tests the [Fermi liquid](../../../../../fermi-liquid-split.md) law $\rho=\rho_0+AT^2$. The [low-temperature calorimetry and resistivity of CeAl3](../../../../../low-temperature-calorimetry-and-resistivity-of-ceal3.md) found $\gamma=1.62\,\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}\,K^{-2}}$ and $A=35\,\mathrm{\mu\Omega\,cm\,K^{-2}}$ below $0.2\,\mathrm K$, making both signatures quantitative.[Abstract: specific heat and electrical resistivity below 0.2 K, with gamma and the quadratic resistivity coefficient](../../../../../4f-virtual-bound-state-formation-in-ceal3-at-low-temperatures-by-klaus-andres-and-others.md)

An enhanced nearly temperature-independent spin susceptibility and a large quantum-oscillation [cyclotron effective mass](../../../../../cyclotron-effective-mass.md) provide additional tests. Not every heavy-fermion compound has the same ordered ground state; magnetism or superconductivity can intervene before a simple normal Fermi-liquid limit is reached.
