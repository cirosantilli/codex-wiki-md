<h1 id="section-a/a3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [polymer overlap concentration](../../../../../polymer-overlap-concentration.md) corresponds to about one chain per coil volume. Approximating the occupied volume of one statistical segment by $a^3$ gives

$$
\boxed{\phi^*\sim\frac{Na^3}{R_\theta^3}=N^{-1/2}=0.0316.}
$$

Thus the site-volume model predicts overlap at a few percent volume fraction. A separately specified molecular segment volume or a coil-volume prefactor would change this numerical prefactor.
