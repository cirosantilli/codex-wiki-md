<h1 id="section-a/a3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For a [concentration blob](../../../../../concentration-blob.md) with $g$ ideal segments, $\xi\sim a\sqrt g$ and concentration matching gives $\phi\sim ga^3/\xi^3\sim g^{-1/2}$. Thus the [concentration-blob size in a theta solvent](../../../../../concentration-blob-size-in-a-theta-solvent.md) is

$$
\boxed{\xi\sim\frac a\phi=6.7\,\mathrm{nm},\qquad g\sim\phi^{-2}=100.}
$$

The whole chain contains about ten blobs, so the separation of scales is moderate. Below $\xi$, theta-solvent segments have [ideal chain](../../../../../ideal-chain.md) statistics; above it, screening also gives Gaussian random-walk statistics for the sequence of blobs. There is no good-solvent self-avoiding regime to insert in this particular theta-solvent calculation. Density correlations and segment dynamics can still cross over at the blob scale.
