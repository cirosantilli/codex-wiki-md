Here $R$ denotes a typical chain size, such as an rms [end-to-end distance of a polymer chain](../../../../../end-to-end-distance-of-a-polymer-chain.md), rather than the mean end-to-end vector of an isotropic solution, which is zero. The [Flory approximation](../../../../../flory-approximation.md) gives $R\sim aN^{3/5}$ in a [good solvent](../../../../../good-solvent.md), an [ideal chain](../../../../../ideal-chain.md) has $R\sim aN^{1/2}$ in a [theta solvent](../../../../../theta-solvent.md), and a compact [polymer globule](../../../../../polymer-globule.md) has size $R\sim aN^{1/3}$ in a strongly [poor solvent](../../../../../poor-solvent.md). These are scaling estimates with order-one prefactors omitted, treating the stated $N$ as the segment count associated with the supplied statistical length.
