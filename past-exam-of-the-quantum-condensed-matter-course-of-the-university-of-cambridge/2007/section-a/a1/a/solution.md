<h1 id="section-a/a1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a narrowly aligned [nematic liquid crystal](../../../../../nematic-liquid-crystal.md), the scalar [nematic order parameter](../../../../../nematic-order-parameter.md) is $S=\langle(3\cos^2\theta-1)/2\rangle\simeq1-\frac32\langle\theta^2\rangle$. Hence the [small-angle width of nematic orientation](../../../../../small-angle-width-of-nematic-orientation.md) is

$$
\boxed{\theta_{\rm rms}\simeq\sqrt{\frac{2(1-S)}3}=0.183\,\mathrm{rad}\simeq10.5^\circ.}
$$

This is a root-mean-square angular width about the unoriented [nematic director](../../../../../nematic-director.md). The single value of $S$ does not determine a complete distribution or its full width at half maximum. For an isotropic two-component Gaussian tilt, each transverse component would have standard deviation $\theta_{\rm rms}/\sqrt2$.
