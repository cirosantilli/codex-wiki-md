<h1 id="section-a/a4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a smooth homogeneous solid and a partially wetting liquid at equilibrium, tangential balance at the [contact line](../../../../../contact-line.md) gives [Young's equation](../../../../../young-s-equation.md)

$$
\boxed{\gamma_{SV}=\gamma_{SL}+\gamma_{LV}\cos\theta.}
$$

The angle is measured through the liquid. Replacing solid-vapour area by solid-liquid plus liquid-vapour area changes free energy by $-S$ per wetted area, where the [spreading coefficient](../../../../../spreading-coefficient.md) is

$$
\boxed{S=\gamma_{SV}-\gamma_{SL}-\gamma_{LV}.}
$$

For partial [wetting](../../../../../wetting.md), $S=\gamma_{LV}(\cos\theta-1)<0$. Positive $S$ favours a spreading film and zero macroscopic equilibrium [contact angle](../../../../../contact-angle.md); the finite-angle Young construction cannot be extrapolated to $\cos\theta>1$.
