<h1 id="section-a/a2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

In diffusion-controlled aggregation, particles meet through [Brownian motion](../../../../../brownian-motion-split.md) and stick with probability close to one on encounter. [Diffusion-limited cluster aggregation](../../../../../diffusion-limited-cluster-aggregation.md) allows already formed clusters to diffuse and join; [diffusion-limited aggregation](../../../../../diffusion-limited-aggregation.md) in its usual single-seed model instead adds diffusing individual particles to one growing cluster. First-contact sticking shields inner regions from later arrivals, favouring open branched structures. A [reaction-limited cluster aggregation](../../../../../reaction-limited-cluster-aggregation.md) model has a small sticking probability and permits many failed encounters before attachment.

For two dilute spherical species, the [Smoluchowski diffusion-controlled encounter rate](../../../../../smoluchowski-diffusion-controlled-encounter-rate.md) is $k_{12}=4\pi(D_1+D_2)(a_1+a_2)$. In relative coordinates, solve the steady [diffusion equation](../../../../../diffusion-equation-split.md) with an absorbing surface at $R=a_1+a_2$ and concentration $c_\infty$ at infinity: $c(r)=c_\infty(1-R/r)$. The inward [diffusive flux](../../../../../diffusive-flux.md) is $4\pi(D_1+D_2)Rc_\infty$, proving the rate coefficient. Interaction barriers and non-spherical aggregates require changes to this ideal encounter calculation.

Cluster growth can be tested by microscopy and [light scattering](../../../../../scattering-of-light.md). A [mass fractal dimension](../../../../../mass-fractal-dimension.md) $d_f$ relates particle number to cluster size through $N\propto R^{d_f}$; a scattering regime between the monomer and cluster length scales can reveal that scaling. The measured morphology depends on whether whole clusters move, on the sticking rule, and on possible bond rearrangement.
