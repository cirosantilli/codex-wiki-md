<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use a representative conduction-electron density $n\sim10^{29}\,\mathrm{m^{-3}}$, roughly one mobile [electron](../../../../electron.md) per atom in a dense [metal](../../../../metal.md), and the bare [electron](../../../../electron.md) [mass](../../../../mass.md) for this estimate. The [plasma frequency](../../../../plasma-frequency.md) is then

$$
\boxed{\omega_p=\sqrt{\frac{ne^2}{m_e\epsilon_0}}\simeq\sqrt{\frac{(10^{29})(1.602\times10^{-19})^2}{(9.109\times10^{-31})(8.854\times10^{-12})}}\simeq1.8\times10^{16}\,\mathrm{rad\,s^{-1}}.}
$$

This is an order-of-magnitude answer because no particular [metal](../../../../metal.md) or [carrier density](../../../../carrier-density.md) is specified. It corresponds to an ordinary [frequency](../../../../frequency.md) $f_p\simeq2.8\times10^{15}\,\mathrm{Hz}$, excitation [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) $\hbar\omega_p\simeq12\,\mathrm{eV}$, and free-space wavelength near $1.1\times10^{-7}\,\mathrm m$.

The physical [oscillation](../../../../oscillation.md) is collective motion of the [electron gas](../../../../electron-gas.md) against the positive ionic background. Displacing the [electrons](../../../../electron.md) relative to the [ions](../../../../ion.md) produces a restoring [electric field](../../../../electric-field-split.md). In the simplest uniform-displacement picture, $\ddot x+\omega_p^2x=0$: the [frequency](../../../../frequency.md) increases with the density of mobile [electric charge](../../../../electric-charge-split.md) and decreases with carrier inertia. Quantizing this longitudinal collective [oscillation](../../../../oscillation.md) gives a [plasmon](../../../../plasmon.md).

The same scale controls the ideal collisionless [Drude model](../../../../drude-model.md) optical response. Its [cold collisionless plasma dielectric response](../../../../cold-collisionless-plasma-dielectric-response.md) is $\epsilon_r(\omega)=1-\omega_p^2/\omega^2$. Below $\omega_p$, the negative [relative permittivity](../../../../relative-permittivity.md) makes a transverse [electromagnetic wave](../../../../electromagnetic-wave-split.md) evanescent, producing strong [reflection](../../../../reflection-mathematics.md); above it, the ideal model admits propagation. Real-metal collisions, interband [photon absorption](../../../../photon-absorption.md) and background [polarization](../../../../polarization-waves.md) modify this simple optical edge. Thus $\omega_p$ is a collective charge-response scale, not a single-electron scattering rate or a sharp transparency threshold for every real [metal](../../../../metal.md).
