<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A normal [Fermi liquid](../../../../fermi-liquid-split.md) describes an interacting fermion system whose low-energy excitations remain continuously connected to adding particles to or removing particles from a [Fermi gas](../../../../fermi-gas.md). The elementary long-lived excitation is a [Landau quasiparticle](../../../../landau-quasiparticle.md): adding a particle also changes the surrounding many-body state. Its [energy](../../../../energy-split.md), [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) and interactions are renormalized, even though its [electric charge](../../../../electric-charge-split.md) and [spin](../../../../spin.md) remain those appropriate to the excitation.

Near an isotropic [Fermi surface](../../../../fermi-surface.md),

$$
\xi_{\mathbf k}=\varepsilon^*_{\mathbf k}-\mu\simeq\hbar v_F(k-k_F),\qquad m^*=\frac{\hbar k_F}{v_F}.
$$

Residual interactions make the [quasiparticle](../../../../quasiparticle.md) [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) depend on the occupations of other [quasiparticles](../../../../quasiparticle.md), expressed by the [Landau quasiparticle energy functional](../../../../landau-quasiparticle-energy-functional.md). Its angular components give the [Landau parameters](../../../../landau-parameter.md) controlling density and [spin](../../../../spin.md) responses. Thus the successful particle picture does not mean that interactions have become zero.

Pauli blocking restricts the final states available for low-energy collisions. In an ordinary three-dimensional [Fermi liquid](../../../../fermi-liquid-split.md) with regular interactions, a representative decay law is

$$
\frac{\hbar}{\tau}=C\frac{\xi^2+(\pi k_BT)^2}{E_*},\qquad C>0.
$$

At $T=0$, the width vanishes faster than $|\xi|$, so an excitation becomes increasingly sharp as it approaches the [Fermi surface](../../../../fermi-surface.md). Its coherent spectral weight is the [quasiparticle residue](../../../../quasiparticle-residue.md) $Z$, and the physical [electron](../../../../electron.md) occupation has a discontinuity of height $Z$ at zero [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md). Incoherent spectral weight accounts for the remainder. The [quasiparticle](../../../../quasiparticle.md) occupations themselves obey a [Fermi-Dirac distribution](../../../../fermi-dirac-distribution.md).

Several leading thermodynamic signatures retain their gas-like form but have interaction-dependent coefficients. In particular,

$$
\frac{C_V}{\mathcal V}=\frac{\pi^2}{3}k_B^2N^*(0)T,\qquad \chi=\frac{\mu_0\mu_B^2N^*(0)}{1+F_0^a},\qquad \kappa=\frac{N^*(0)}{n^2(1+F_0^s)},
$$

for an isotropic spin-one-half model, with $N^*(0)$ the spin-summed [quasiparticle](../../../../quasiparticle.md) [density of states](../../../../density-of-states.md) per volume. [effective mass](../../../../band-effective-mass.md) changes $N^*(0)$, while the dimensionless interaction parameters $F_0^a,F_0^s$ separately change the [spin](../../../../spin.md) susceptibility and [compressibility](../../../../compressibility.md). Collisionless collective density motion can propagate as [zero sound](../../../../zero-sound.md), distinct from the single-particle excitations.

The $T^2$ [quasiparticle](../../../../quasiparticle.md) scattering rate often underlies a low-temperature $T^2$ contribution to metallic [electrical resistivity](../../../../electrical-resistivity.md), but a mechanism relaxing electrical [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md) is needed. In a clean Galilean-invariant continuum, electron-electron collisions conserve total [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) and do not alone give a finite dc [electrical resistivity](../../../../electrical-resistivity.md). [Crystal](../../../../crystal.md) Umklapp scattering or other current-relaxing processes change that conclusion.

Normal liquid [helium-3](../../../../helium-3.md) and many [metals](../../../../metal.md) motivate the Fermi-liquid framework. Pairing, magnetic ordering or other transitions can reconstruct the low-energy states. Generic interacting one-dimensional fermion systems instead form a different low-energy state, so the three-dimensional [quasiparticle](../../../../quasiparticle.md) conclusions should not simply be transferred to a chain.
