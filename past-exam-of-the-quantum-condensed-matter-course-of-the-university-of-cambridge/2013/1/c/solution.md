<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For one isotropic carrier species in the [Drude model](../../../../drude-model.md), the longitudinal [electrical resistivity](../../../../electrical-resistivity.md) at zero transverse [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md) is $\rho_{xx}=1/(ne\mu)$, and the Hall [electrical resistivity](../../../../electrical-resistivity.md) has magnitude $|\rho_{yx}|=B/(ne)$. Equivalently, the [electric field](../../../../electric-field-split.md) balances the magnetic deflection through $E_y=v_xB$, while $|v_x|=\mu|E_x|$. Therefore the magnitude of the [Hall angle](../../../../hall-angle.md) obeys

$$
\frac{|E_y|}{|E_x|}=\mu B.
$$

The required ratio gives

$$
\boxed{B=\frac{0.01}{0.2\,\mathrm{m^2\,V^{-1}\,s^{-1}}}=0.050\,\mathrm T=50\,\mathrm{mT}.}
$$

The [electron density](../../../../electron-density.md) cancels. With [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md) along $+x$ and field along $+z$, [electrons](../../../../electron.md) have negative [Hall coefficient](../../../../hall-coefficient.md) and hence $E_y<0$; the requested one-percent condition concerns the magnitude. This calculation assumes [electrons](../../../../electron.md) dominate transport and the transverse contacts draw no [electric current](../../../../electric-current.md), so a Hall field can develop.
