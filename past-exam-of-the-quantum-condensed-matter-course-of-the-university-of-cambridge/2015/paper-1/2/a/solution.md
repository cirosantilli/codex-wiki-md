[Heavy fermion materials](../../../../../heavy-fermion-material.md) are correlated metals whose low-energy [quasiparticles](../../../../../quasiparticle.md) have exceptionally large effective masses. Partly localized $f$-electron moments hybridize with broader conduction states. The [Kondo effect](../../../../../kondo-effect.md) and coherence below the [Kondo-lattice coherence temperature](../../../../../kondo-lattice-coherence-temperature.md) produce a narrow low-energy band, so a small energy change corresponds to a large momentum-space state density.

For the [electronic heat capacity of a heavy Fermi liquid](../../../../../electronic-heat-capacity-of-a-heavy-fermi-liquid.md), the resulting [density of states](../../../../../density-of-states.md) gives a large low-[temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) electronic specific-heat coefficient,

$$
\boxed{C_{\rm el}=\gamma T,\qquad\gamma=\frac{\pi^2k_B^2}{3}g(E_F),}
$$

and often an enhanced [magnetic susceptibility](../../../../../magnetic-susceptibility.md). Here $g$ includes both spins and must be expressed per sample, mole or volume consistently with $\gamma$. A coherent Fermi-liquid regime often has $\rho=\rho_0+AT^2$ with an enhanced coefficient $A$. Above coherence, scattering by local moments can instead give a Kondo-like resistivity upturn.

The competition between moment screening and intersite magnetic interactions can yield a heavy paramagnetic metal, magnetic order, quantum-critical behaviour or unconventional [superconductivity](../../../../../superconductivity-split.md). Ce- and U-based compounds supply familiar examples. “Heavy” describes the emergent electronic [quasiparticles](../../../../../quasiparticle.md), not the ionic mass; the simple heavy-band picture need not survive at a [quantum critical point](../../../../../quantum-critical-point.md).[Sections 1.1–1.3: heavy electrons, local moments and Kondo-lattice coherence](../../../../../heavy-fermions-and-the-kondo-lattice-a-21st-century-perspective-by-piers-coleman.md)
