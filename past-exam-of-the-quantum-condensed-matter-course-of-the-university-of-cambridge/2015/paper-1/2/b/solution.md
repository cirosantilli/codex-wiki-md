<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [Hubbard model](../../../../../hubbard-model.md) combines hopping with a local repulsion,

$$
H=-t\sum_{\langle ij\rangle,\sigma}(c_{i\sigma}^\dagger c_{j\sigma}+\mathrm{h.c.})+U\sum_i n_{i\uparrow}n_{i\downarrow},\qquad U>0.
$$

Hopping favours a broad itinerant band; the interaction penalizes opposite-spin [double occupancy](../../../../../double-occupancy.md). For [uniform Stoner decoupling of the Hubbard interaction](../../../../../uniform-stoner-decoupling-of-the-hubbard-interaction.md), let $s=n_\uparrow-n_\downarrow$ be the spin imbalance per site. The interaction is $U(n^2-s^2)/4$, so polarization lowers it by $Us^2/4$, at the expense of kinetic energy.

If $D(E_F)$ is the noninteracting [density of states](../../../../../density-of-states.md) **per spin per site**, small polarization costs $s^2/[4D(E_F)]$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\Delta E=\frac{s^2}{4}[D(E_F)^{-1}-U]+O(s^4),\qquad UD(E_F)>1}
$$

is the mean-field ferromagnetic instability criterion. The exchange splitting is $\Delta=Us$, and the paramagnetic response is enhanced as $\chi=\chi_0/[1-UD(E_F)]$. A [density of states](../../../../../density-of-states.md) including both spins changes the written factor in the criterion.

The model explains how itinerant bands can polarize without imposing pre-existing localized atomic spins. It is not a claim that every large-$U$ metal becomes ferromagnetic: near half filling the [Hubbard model](../../../../../hubbard-model.md) can instead form a [Mott insulator](../../../../../mott-insulator.md) with antiferromagnetic [superexchange](../../../../../superexchange.md) of scale $4t^2/U$. [Band filling](../../../../../band-filling.md), [bandwidth](../../../../../bandwidth-signal-processing.md) and fluctuations matter.[Section 3.6.6: the free-electron magnetic response](../../../../../quantum-gases-by-david-tong.md) The interaction-energy and kinetic-cost expansion above is the stated mean-field derivation of the enhancement.[Original local-repulsion model and the competition between atomic and itinerant limits](../../../../../electron-correlations-in-narrow-energy-bands-by-john-hubbard.md)
