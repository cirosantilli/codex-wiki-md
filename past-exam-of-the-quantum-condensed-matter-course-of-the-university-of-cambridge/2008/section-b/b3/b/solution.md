↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For an [acoustic phonon](../../../../../acoustic-phonon.md) with $\omega_q\simeq v_sq$, [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) and [crystal momentum](../../../../../crystal-momentum.md) conservation require $E_{\boldsymbol k'}-E_{\boldsymbol k}=\pm\hbar v_sq$ and $\boldsymbol k'-\boldsymbol k=\pm\boldsymbol q+\boldsymbol G$. The [sound speed](../../../../../speed-of-sound.md) is usually much smaller than the relevant [electron](../../../../../electron.md) velocity in a metal, so scattering can change the [electron](../../../../../electron.md)'s direction substantially while changing its [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) only slightly. It is approximately elastic for many transport calculations, though its finite [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) transfer is essential for cooling.

The middle panel gives an allowed backscattering example with a small [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) loss. In a degenerate metal, the [Pauli exclusion principle](../../../../../pauli-exclusion-principle.md) blocks occupied final states, restricting low-temperature scattering to a thermal neighborhood of the [Fermi surface](../../../../../fermi-surface.md). Phonon occupations also shrink on cooling. A normal process has $\boldsymbol G=0$; an [Umklapp process](../../../../../umklapp-scattering.md) transfers reciprocal-lattice momentum. In semiconductors, accessible valleys and their separation add further momentum constraints.
