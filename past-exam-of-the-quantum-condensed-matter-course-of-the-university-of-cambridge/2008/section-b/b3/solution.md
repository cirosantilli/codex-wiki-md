<h1 id="section-b/b3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B3](../b3.md)

Let $\alpha=\hbar^2/(2m^*)$, so the initial conduction-electron kinetic [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) is $E_1=\alpha k_1^2$. In the normal momentum-conserving process with the final hole fixed at zero [wavevector](../../../../wavevector.md), the two final conduction electrons obey $\boldsymbol k_1=\boldsymbol k_2+\boldsymbol k_3$. Their total kinetic [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) satisfies

$$
\alpha(k_2^2+k_3^2)=\frac{E_1}2+\frac\alpha2|\boldsymbol k_2-\boldsymbol k_3|^2\geq\frac{E_1}2.
$$

Energy conservation for [impact ionization](../../../../impact-ionization.md) is $E_1=E_g+\alpha(k_2^2+k_3^2)$. Consequently $E_1\geq E_g+E_1/2$, giving

$$
\boxed{E_{1,\min}=2E_g.}
$$

The bound is attained when $\boldsymbol k_2=\boldsymbol k_3=\boldsymbol k_1/2$; opposite spins permit the two electrons to share that [wavevector](../../../../wavevector.md). Fixing the hole momentum is essential: a mobile final hole gives a different threshold. This is the threshold for [impact ionization with a hole at zero momentum](../../../../impact-ionization-with-a-hole-at-zero-momentum.md).

The two hot electrons subsequently redistribute [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) by electron-electron collisions and transfer it to the lattice by [electron-phonon scattering](../../../../electron-phonon-scattering.md). Optical-phonon emission can provide large discrete losses while the electrons are hot; acoustic phonons dominate later small [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) exchanges. Electron-electron collisions alone conserve the [electron](../../../../electron.md) system's total [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) and do not cool it into the lattice. The electron-hole pair eventually recombines radiatively or through nonradiative channels, such as defect-assisted recombination or [Auger recombination](../../../../auger-recombination.md). Recombination is distinct from the preceding intraband cooling.
