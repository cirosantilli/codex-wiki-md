↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

An [optical phonon](../../../../../optical-phonon.md) often has approximately constant [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) $\hbar\omega_0$ over the relevant [wavevector](../../../../../wavevector.md) range. Its absorption or emission therefore moves the [electron](../../../../../electron.md) between [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) surfaces separated by this finite amount, with the [phonon](../../../../../phonon.md) supplying the associated [crystal momentum](../../../../../crystal-momentum.md) modulo a reciprocal vector. The last panel shows emission between two points on a parabolic [conduction band](../../../../../conduction-band.md).

Emission from a band with a lower edge requires at least $\hbar\omega_0$ of available kinetic [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) and an unoccupied final state. For nondegenerate hot carriers this produces a threshold and can lead to rapid [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) loss in successive [phonon](../../../../../phonon.md) emissions. Absorption is weighted by the [Bose-Einstein distribution](../../../../../bose-einstein-distribution.md) $n_B(\omega_0)$, while emission has the factor $n_B+1$; absorption is strongly suppressed at $k_BT\ll\hbar\omega_0$. In a metal, Pauli blocking also prevents an [electron](../../../../../electron.md) near the Fermi [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) from simply dropping into the occupied Fermi sea.
