<h1 id="section-b/b4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B4](../b4.md)

Only states within an [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) window of order $k_BT$ around the [Fermi energy](../../../../fermi-energy.md) can change occupation in a degenerate metal. A [Sommerfeld expansion](../../../../sommerfeld-expansion.md) gives electronic [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) above its zero-temperature value proportional to $g(E_F)(k_BT)^2$, hence $C_e=\gamma T$. Three-dimensional linear-dispersion [acoustic phonons](../../../../acoustic-phonon.md) have density of states proportional to $\omega^2$; integrating their Bose occupations gives thermal [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) proportional to $T^4$ and the [Debye heat capacity](../../../../debye-heat-capacity.md) $C_{
m ph}=\beta T^3$. Thus $C/T=\gamma+\beta T^2$ at sufficiently low [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) in the normal state.

Reading the straight low-temperature fit gives approximately $\gamma\simeq33\ \mathrm{mJ\,mol^{-1}K^{-2}}$ and $\beta\simeq3.8\ \mathrm{mJ\,mol^{-1}K^{-4}}$. These estimates agree with the reported fit to this material in the original experiment.[Figure 3 caption: low-temperature normal-state coefficients for Ba4Na2Ge25](../../../../superconductivity-in-the-filled-cage-compounds-ba6ge25-and-ba4na2ge25-by-friedrich-malte-grosche-and-others.md) Treating the graph values as approximate, the molar [density of states](../../../../density-of-states.md) is

$$
\boxed{g(E_F)=\frac{3\gamma}{\pi^2k_B^2}\simeq5.3\times10^{43}\ \mathrm{J^{-1}mol^{-1}}\simeq14\ \mathrm{eV^{-1}}\text{ per formula unit}.}
$$

The stipulated two electrons per formula unit give $n=2N_A/V_m\simeq2.62\times10^{27}\ \mathrm{m^{-3}}$. For a single spin-degenerate parabolic band, the molar density of states is $g(E_F)=3(2N_A)/(2E_F)=3N_A/E_F$. Hence

$$
\boxed{E_F\simeq3.4\times10^{-20}\ \mathrm J\simeq0.21\ \mathrm{eV},\qquad
k_F=(3\pi^2n)^{1/3}\simeq4.3\times10^9\ \mathrm{m^{-1}},\qquad
m^*=\frac{\hbar^2k_F^2}{2E_F}\simeq3.2m_e.}
$$

These are model-dependent effective parameters inferred from the measured heat capacity and assumed carrier count, rather than an independent determination of a unique band structure.

Using the Debye prefactor stated in the question gives

$$
\boxed{\Theta_D=\left(\frac{12\pi^4N_Ak_B}{5\beta}\right)^{1/3}\simeq80\ \mathrm K.}
$$

This prefactor counts three effective modes per formula unit. Consistently choosing the same count for the [Debye wavevector](../../../../debye-wavevector.md) gives $k_D=(6\pi^2N_A/V_m)^{1/3}\simeq4.3\times10^9\ \mathrm{m^{-1}}$. The corresponding average [sound speed](../../../../speed-of-sound.md) is

$$
\boxed{v_D=\frac{k_B\Theta_D}{\hbar k_D}\simeq2.5\times10^3\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}.}
$$

There is a normalization choice here: a formula unit has 31 atoms. With the conventional full atomic-mode count, the molar Debye coefficient contains an additional factor 31, and both $\Theta_D$ and $k_D$ increase by $31^{1/3}$. That convention gives $\Theta_D\simeq251\ \mathrm K$ and $k_D\simeq1.34\times10^{10}\ \mathrm{m^{-1}}$, while leaving the inferred [sound speed](../../../../speed-of-sound.md) unchanged. The [mode count in the Debye model](../../../../mode-count-in-the-debye-model.md) must be used consistently; mixing the printed [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) with an atomic-count [wavevector](../../../../wavevector.md) gives an erroneous [sound speed](../../../../speed-of-sound.md).

The upward curvature above about $3\ \mathrm K$ indicates low-energy vibrations missing from a pure acoustic $T^3$ model. In this cage compound, the original analysis fits an additional localized mode with $\hbar\Omega/k_B\simeq32\ \mathrm K$, assigned to a [rattling mode](../../../../rattling-mode.md) of a barium guest atom.[Page 247003-3 and Figure 3: extra Einstein-mode contribution](../../../../superconductivity-in-the-filled-cage-compounds-ba6ge25-and-ba4na2ge25-by-friedrich-malte-grosche-and-others.md) At temperatures high compared with all relevant [phonon](../../../../phonon.md) energies, the full lattice approaches the [Dulong-Petit law](../../../../dulong-petit-law.md), $C_{
m lattice}\to3(31)R=93R\simeq773\ \mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}K^{-1}}$. The reduced three-mode model alone approaches $3R$ and omits the other branches. An approximately linear electronic term can still be added while the carriers remain degenerate, but cannot be extrapolated beyond their [Fermi temperature](../../../../fermi-temperature.md).
