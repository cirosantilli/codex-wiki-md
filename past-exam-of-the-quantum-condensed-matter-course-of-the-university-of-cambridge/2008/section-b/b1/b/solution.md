<h1 id="section-b/b1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Put $N=N_xN_yN_z$ primitive cells in a periodic box. In each reciprocal-space direction, the allowed Bloch wavevectors are spaced by $2\pi/L_i$, giving $N_i$ independent values in the first [Brillouin zone](../../../../../brillouin-zone.md). There are consequently $N$ wavevectors per band and, including two [electron](../../../../../electron.md) spin states at each [wavevector](../../../../../wavevector.md),

$$
\boxed{2N\text{ states per band in the whole crystal},\qquad2\text{ states per atom}.}
$$

The total and per-atom counts are different; a factor $N$ cannot remain in the per-atom count for this monatomic lattice.

In independent-electron [band theory](../../../../../electronic-band-structure.md), a partially occupied band permits low-energy changes of occupancy and gives a metal. Filled bands separated from empty bands by a finite [band gap](../../../../../band-gap.md) give a [band insulator](../../../../../band-insulator.md) at zero [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md). An odd number of electrons per primitive cell cannot fill spin-degenerate bands, whereas an even number can; even filling alone does not ensure an insulator if bands overlap. Magnetic symmetry breaking and strong correlations, such as a [Mott insulator](../../../../../mott-insulator.md), go beyond this simple counting criterion.
