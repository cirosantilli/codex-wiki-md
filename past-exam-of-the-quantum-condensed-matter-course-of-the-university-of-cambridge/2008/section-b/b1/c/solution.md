<h1 id="section-b/b1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A [plasmon](../../../../../plasmon.md) is a quantum of a collective longitudinal oscillation of [electron](../../../../../electron.md) density against the positive background. A small charge displacement creates an electric restoring field; Newton's law then gives the plasma angular frequency. Assuming approximately one conduction [electron](../../../../../electron.md) per atom and spacing $d=3\ \text{\AA}$ gives $n\sim d^{-3}=3.7\times10^{28}\ \mathrm{m^{-3}}$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\omega_p=\sqrt{\frac{ne^2}{\varepsilon_0m_e}}\sim1.1\times10^{16}\ \mathrm{s^{-1}}.}
$$

The ordinary frequency is about $1.7\times10^{15}\ \mathrm{Hz}$, with [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) $\hbar\omega_p\sim7\ \mathrm{eV}$. A different conduction-electron count per atom changes this estimate by its square root; dielectric and band-structure corrections also modify the simplest free-electron result.
