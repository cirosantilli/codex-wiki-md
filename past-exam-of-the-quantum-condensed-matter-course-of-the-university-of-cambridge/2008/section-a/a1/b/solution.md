<h1 id="section-a/a1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For the symmetric [regular solution model](../../../../../regular-solution.md), the dimensionless mixing [free energy](../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) is $f/(k_BT)=\phi\log\phi+(1-\phi)\log(1-\phi)+\chi\phi(1-\phi)$. At the [thermodynamic critical point](../../../../../thermodynamic-critical-point.md), its second and third [derivatives](../../../../../derivative.md) in composition vanish. The third derivative gives $\phi_c=1/2$; the second then gives $4-2\chi_c=0$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\chi_c=2,\qquad T_c=600\ \mathrm K/2=300\ \mathrm K.}
$$

This uses the equal-size, composition-symmetric mixture model.
