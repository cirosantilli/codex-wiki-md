<h1 id="section-a/a4/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Here the [osmotic pressure](../../../../../osmotic-pressure.md) is a two-dimensional surface [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md), with units of [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) per area. In the dilute regime, independent whole chains provide the translational [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md), so

$$
\boxed{\Pi\sim\frac{k_BT}{\ell^2}\frac\phi N,\qquad\phi\ll\phi^*.}
$$

In the [semidilute polymer solution](../../../../../semidilute-polymer-solution.md), use a correlation blob containing $g$ monomers. Within it the chain still follows the planar good-solvent law $\xi\sim\ell g^{3/4}$. Its area fraction is $\phi\sim g\ell^2/\xi^2\sim g^{-1/2}$, so $g\sim\phi^{-2}$ and $\xi\sim\ell\phi^{-3/2}$. There is about one independent thermal contribution per blob area. The [semidilute osmotic pressure of a two-dimensional polymer solution](../../../../../semidilute-osmotic-pressure-of-a-two-dimensional-polymer-solution.md) is thus

$$
\boxed{\Pi\sim\frac{k_BT}{\xi^2}\sim\frac{k_BT}{\ell^2}\phi^3,\qquad\phi^*\ll\phi\ll1.}
$$

It is independent of total chain length once each chain contains many blobs. The two estimates agree at $\phi^*\sim N^{-1/2}$, where each gives $(k_BT/\ell^2)N^{-3/2}$.
