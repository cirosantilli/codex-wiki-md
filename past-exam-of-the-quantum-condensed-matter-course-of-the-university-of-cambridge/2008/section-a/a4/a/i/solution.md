<h1 id="section-a/a4/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For the planar [random walk](../../../../../../random-walk-split.md) with independent steps of length $b$, cross terms vanish and $\langle R^2\rangle=Nb^2$. Thus $R_{\rm rms}=bN^{1/2}$. At large $N$, the planar Gaussian end distribution gives $\langle R\rangle\simeq b\sqrt{\pi N}/2$.
