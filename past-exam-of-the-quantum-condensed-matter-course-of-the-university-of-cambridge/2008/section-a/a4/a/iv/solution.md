<h1 id="section-a/a4/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

A three-dimensional [self-avoiding walk](../../../../../../self-avoiding-walk.md) has swollen size $R\sim bN^\nu$, with $\nu\simeq0.59$; the three-dimensional [Flory approximation](../../../../../../flory-approximation.md) gives the nearby estimate $3/5$. Isotropic projection gives $\langle R_{\rm proj}^2\rangle=(2/3)\langle R^2\rangle$, preserving this exponent. For the mean distance rather than its root mean square, the orientation average instead gives $\langle R_{\rm proj}\rangle=(\pi/4)\langle R\rangle$. Neither prefactor changes its position between the ideal $N^{1/2}$ walks and the planar swollen $N^{3/4}$ walk.
