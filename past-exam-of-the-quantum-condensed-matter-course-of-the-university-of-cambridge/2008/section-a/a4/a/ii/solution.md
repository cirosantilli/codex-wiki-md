<h1 id="section-a/a4/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A planar [self-avoiding walk](../../../../../../self-avoiding-walk.md) cannot revisit its occupied sites. In the good-solvent scaling regime, the [two-dimensional Flory approximation](../../../../../../two-dimensional-flory-approximation.md) gives $R\sim bN^{3/4}$, larger at large $N$ than either ideal walk and the projected three-dimensional self-avoiding walk. This is a scaling statement, with microscopic prefactors dependent on the walk model.
