<h1 id="section-a/a4/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [polymer overlap concentration](../../../../../../polymer-overlap-concentration.md) is reached when the number of chains per coil area is of order one. If $n_c$ is the chain number per unit interfacial area, then $n_c^*R^2\sim1$. Equivalently, the monomer area fraction is $\phi^*\sim N\ell^2/R^2$, assuming an occupied monomer area of order $\ell^2$. Below this scale coils are mostly separate; above it they interpenetrate.
