<h1 id="section-a/a4/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For the planar good-solvent coil, $R\sim\ell N^{3/4}$. The [two-dimensional polymer overlap fraction](../../../../../../two-dimensional-polymer-overlap-fraction.md) is therefore

$$
\phi^*\sim\frac{N\ell^2}{\ell^2N^{3/2}}=N^{-1/2},\qquad
\boxed{\phi^*\sim10^{-2}=1\%.}
$$

This is a scaling estimate; the monomer geometry and choice of coil radius set order-one numerical factors.
