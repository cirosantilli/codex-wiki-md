<h1 id="section-a/a3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [creep compliance](../../../../../creep-compliance.md) is the [strain](../../../../../strain.md) per unit step [stress](../../../../../stress.md): if $\sigma(t)=\sigma_0H(t)$, then $\gamma(t)=\sigma_0J(t)$ for $t>0$. A [Newtonian fluid](../../../../../newtonian-fluid.md) obeys $\sigma=\eta\dot\gamma$, so under constant [stress](../../../../../stress.md) its initially zero [strain](../../../../../strain.md) grows as $\gamma=\sigma_0t/\eta$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{J(t)=t/\eta.}
$$
