<h1 id="section-a/a3/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

Under a step [stress](../../../../../stress.md), the Maxwell spring extends immediately by $\sigma_0/G_0$, while the dashpot adds $\sigma_0t/\eta$. Hence $J(t)=G_0^{-1}+t/\eta$, and the [microrheology of a Maxwell fluid](../../../../../microrheology-of-a-maxwell-fluid.md) relation gives

$$
\boxed{\langle\Delta r^2(t)\rangle=\frac{k_BT}{\pi aG_0}\left(1+\frac t\tau\right),\qquad\tau=\eta/G_0.}
$$

This idealized overdamped relation describes an elastic plateau followed by long-time [diffusion](../../../../../diffusion.md). Its nonzero extrapolated intercept is not the actual displacement variance at exactly zero lag, which is zero. Inertia or fast solvent relaxation must resolve the initial rise; those processes were omitted from the ideal spring-dashpot model.
