<h1 id="section-a/a3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For the convention $\gamma=\operatorname{Re}(\gamma_0e^{i\omega t})$, the [complex viscoelastic modulus](../../../../../dynamic-modulus.md) is $G^*=i\omega\int_0^\infty G(t)e^{-i\omega t}dt$. Thus

$$
G^*=\frac{i\omega\eta}{1+i\omega\tau},\qquad
\boxed{G'=G_0\frac{(\omega\tau)^2}{1+(\omega\tau)^2},\quad
G''=G_0\frac{\omega\tau}{1+(\omega\tau)^2}.}
$$

The [storage modulus](../../../../../storage-modulus.md) exceeds the [loss modulus](../../../../../loss-modulus.md) for $\omega\tau>1$, so the response is predominantly solid-like above the crossover

$$
\boxed{\omega_c=1/\tau,\qquad f_c=1/(2\pi\tau).}
$$

At low frequency $G^*\simeq i\omega\eta$; at high frequency $G^*\to G_0$.
