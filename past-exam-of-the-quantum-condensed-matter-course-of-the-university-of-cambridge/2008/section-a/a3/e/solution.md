<h1 id="section-a/a3/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

[Passive microrheology](../../../../../passive-microrheology.md) uses thermal bead motion as a probe of mechanical compliance. The [fluctuation-dissipation theorem](../../../../../fluctuation-dissipation-theorem.md) relates equilibrium displacement fluctuations to the same linear response measured by a small applied force. The stated thermal [stress](../../../../../stress.md) scale has units of [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) per bead volume; dividing the mean-square displacement by $a^2$ provides a dimensionless effective [strain](../../../../../strain.md). With that prescribed analogy,

$$
J(t)=\frac{\langle\Delta r^2(t)\rangle/a^2}{k_BT/(\pi a^3)},\qquad
\boxed{\langle\Delta r^2(t)\rangle=\frac{k_BT}{\pi a}J(t).}
$$

The displacement is relative to the starting position, and the variance here is the sum over three spatial coordinates. For a Newtonian fluid, substitution gives $\langle\Delta r^2\rangle=k_BTt/(\pi\eta a)=6Dt$, exactly the [Stokes–Einstein relation](../../../../../stokes-einstein-relation.md) $D=k_BT/(6\pi\eta a)$.

The continuum interpretation assumes a dilute no-slip spherical probe, thermal equilibrium, linear response and a homogeneous incompressible medium on the bead scale. The stated [stress](../../../../../stress.md) estimate is an effective scale for the analogy; a literal instantaneous [stress](../../../../../stress.md) variance would also require a microscopic or temporal averaging prescription.
