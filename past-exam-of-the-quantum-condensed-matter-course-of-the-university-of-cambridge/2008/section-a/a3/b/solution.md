<h1 id="section-a/a3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [Maxwell model](../../../../../linear-maxwell-fluid.md) has a spring of modulus $G_0$ in series with a [dashpot](../../../../../dashpot.md) of [viscosity](../../../../../dynamic-viscosity-split.md) $\eta$. Their stresses are equal and strains add, giving $\dot\gamma=\dot\sigma/G_0+\sigma/\eta$. After a step [strain](../../../../../strain.md) $\gamma_0$, the [strain](../../../../../strain.md) rate is zero for $t>0$, so $\dot\sigma=-\sigma/\tau$ with $\tau=\eta/G_0$. Initially the spring takes the step, $\sigma(0^+)=G_0\gamma_0$. Hence

$$
\boxed{G(t)=G_0e^{-t/\tau},\qquad\tau=\eta/G_0.}
$$
