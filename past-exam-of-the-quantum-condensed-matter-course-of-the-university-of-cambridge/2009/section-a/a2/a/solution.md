↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Flory interaction parameter](../../../../../flory-interaction-parameter.md) measures the energetic preference for unlike rather than like contacts in [Flory–Huggins solution theory](../../../../../flory-huggins-solution-theory.md). For polymer volume fraction $\phi$ and $N$ lattice sites per chain, the dimensionless mixing [Helmholtz free energy](../../../../../helmholtz-free-energy.md) per site is

$$
\frac{f_{\rm mix}}{k_BT}=\frac\phi N\log\phi+(1-\phi)\log(1-\phi)+\chi\phi(1-\phi).
$$

The logarithmic terms represent combinatorial [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md); polymer connectivity reduces the chain's translational mixing entropy by the factor $N$. In a simple contact model $\chi=z[\epsilon_{ps}-(\epsilon_{pp}+\epsilon_{ss})/2]/(k_BT)$, where $z$ is the coordination number. Positive $\chi$ penalizes polymer-solvent contacts. Increasing it can overcome mixing [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md) and cause [phase separation](../../../../../phase-separation.md); a negative second derivative of the mixing [free energy](../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) marks the [spinodal](../../../../../spinodal.md) region. For a long dilute chain, $\chi<1/2$ corresponds to a [good solvent](../../../../../good-solvent.md), $\chi=1/2$ to the idealized theta condition, and $\chi>1/2$ to a [poor solvent](../../../../../poor-solvent.md). Real effective parameters can depend on [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) and [concentration](../../../../../concentration.md), rather than being universal chemical constants.
