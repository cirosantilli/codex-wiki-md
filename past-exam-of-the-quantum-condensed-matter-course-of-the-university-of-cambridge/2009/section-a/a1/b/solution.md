<h1 id="section-a/a1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a spherical [micelle](../../../../../micelle.md) with $N$ tails, core radius $r$, molecular tail [volume](../../../../../volume.md) $v$ and head-group [area](../../../../../surface-area.md) $a$, volume and surface counting give $Nv=4\pi r^3/3$ and $Na=4\pi r^2$. Consequently $a=3v/r$. A tail cannot span a core radius larger than its maximum length $\ell$, so the [amphiphile packing parameter](../../../../../amphiphile-packing-parameter.md) condition is

$$
\boxed{a\geq\frac{3v}{\ell}=0.380\ \mathrm{nm^2}.}
$$

**This is a minimum area, not a maximum.** The printed request reverses the geometric inequality. The limiting value corresponds to fully extended tails with $r=\ell$; larger head groups permit smaller spherical aggregates in this simple packing argument. The supplied data alone impose no finite maximum head-group area, and geometry alone does not guarantee thermodynamic [micelle](../../../../../micelle.md) formation.
