<h1 id="section-a/a1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Stokes–Einstein relation](../../../../../stokes-einstein-relation.md) gives the translational [diffusion coefficient](../../../../../diffusion-coefficient.md)

$$
D=\frac{k_BT}{6\pi\eta R}=8.17\times10^{-11}\ \mathrm{m^2\,s^{-1}}
$$

at $T=298\ \mathrm K$. For isotropic three-dimensional [Brownian motion](../../../../../brownian-motion-split.md), the [mean squared displacement](../../../../../mean-squared-displacement.md) is $\langle|\Delta\mathbf r|^2\rangle=6Dt$. Thus after one hour the [root mean square](../../../../../root-mean-square.md) displacement is

$$
\boxed{\sqrt{6Dt}=1.33\ \mathrm{mm}.}
$$

The phrase “on average” needs a convention: the mean displacement vector is zero; the [mean displacement magnitude of three-dimensional Brownian motion](../../../../../mean-displacement-magnitude-of-three-dimensional-brownian-motion.md) is $\langle|\Delta\mathbf r|\rangle=4\sqrt{Dt/\pi}=1.22\ \mathrm{mm}$, obtained by integrating the three-dimensional [Gaussian distribution](../../../../../normal-distribution.md). These are displacements, not the total microscopic path length. The estimate assumes dilute unconfined water, a spherical hydrodynamic radius and constant [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md).
