<h1 id="section-b/6/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Replace the applied field in the doublet response by the [Weiss molecular field](../../../../../weiss-molecular-field.md) $B_E=\mu_0\lambda M$. At zero applied field,

$$
M=n\mu_B\tanh\left(\frac{\mu_0\lambda\mu_BM}{k_BT}\right).
$$

The equation is odd in $M$, so the two ordered orientations have equal magnitude. With $m=|M|/(n\mu_B)$, define

$$
\boxed{T_C=\frac{\mu_0\lambda n\mu_B^2}{k_B},\qquad t=\frac{T}{T_C}.}
$$

The [mean-field self-consistency equation](../../../../../self-consistency-equation.md) becomes

$$
\boxed{m=\tanh(m/t).}
$$

Plot $y=m$ and $y=\tanh(m/t)$. The second has slope $1/t$ at zero and is concave for positive $m$. For $0<t<1$ it starts above the straight line but is below it by $m=1$, giving one positive intersection. For $t\geq1$, $\tanh(m/t)<m$ for every $m>0$. Hence

$$
\boxed{m>0\text{ exists for }0<T<T_C;\qquad m=0\text{ for }T\geq T_C.}
$$

At $T=0$ the limiting solution is $m=1$. The onset of [spontaneous magnetization](../../../../../spontaneous-magnetization.md) is the mean-field [Curie temperature](../../../../../curie-temperature.md).
