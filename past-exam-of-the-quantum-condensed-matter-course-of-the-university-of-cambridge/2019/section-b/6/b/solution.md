<h1 id="section-b/6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

At weak field or high temperature, $\mu_BB\ll k_BT$ and $\tanh x\simeq x$, so

$$
M\simeq\frac{n\mu_B^2B}{k_BT}.
$$

Using $B\simeq\mu_0H$ for the applied-field response and neglecting demagnetizing corrections, the SI [volume magnetic susceptibility](../../../../../volume-magnetic-susceptibility.md) is

$$
\boxed{\chi=\left.\frac{\partial M}{\partial H}\right|_{H=0}
=\frac{C}{T},\qquad C=\frac{\mu_0n\mu_B^2}{k_B}.}
$$

This is [Curie's law](../../../../../curie-s-law.md). If susceptibility is instead defined as $\partial M/\partial B$, its Curie constant is $n\mu_B^2/k_B$ without $\mu_0$; stating which field is used prevents a units error.
