<h1 id="section-b/4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Write $U=V_g$ for the coupling Fourier coefficient, with $|U|=|V_0|$ for the supplied cosine potential. Projecting the [Schrödinger equation](../../../../../schrodinger-equation.md) onto the two retained [plane waves](../../../../../plane-wave.md) gives

$$
\begin{pmatrix}\epsilon_{q+g/2}&U\\U^*&\epsilon_{q-g/2}\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}A\\B\end{pmatrix}
=E\begin{pmatrix}A\\B\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The diagonal potential average is zero. The [characteristic equation](../../../../../characteristic-equation-of-a-constant-coefficient-differential-equation.md) is

$$
(E-\epsilon_{q+g/2})(E-\epsilon_{q-g/2})-|U|^2=0,
$$

so the [nearly-free electron dispersion near a one-dimensional band gap](../../../../../nearly-free-electron-dispersion-near-a-one-dimensional-band-gap.md) is

$$
\boxed{E_\pm(q)=\frac{\epsilon_{q+g/2}+\epsilon_{q-g/2}}2
\pm\frac12\sqrt{(\epsilon_{q+g/2}-\epsilon_{q-g/2})^2+4V_0^2}.}
$$

Only the coupling magnitude enters the energies; its sign changes the standing-wave eigenvectors. The two-wave truncation is controlled near this crossing when $|V_0|\ll\epsilon_{g/2}$, since the other [plane waves](../../../../../plane-wave.md) are separated by much larger kinetic energies.
