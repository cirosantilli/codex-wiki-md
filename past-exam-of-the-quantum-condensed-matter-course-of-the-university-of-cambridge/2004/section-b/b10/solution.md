↑ **Parent:** [B10](../b10.md)

[Exchange interactions](../../../../exchange-interaction.md) arise from quantum electronic motion, Coulomb interactions and fermionic antisymmetry. A localized-spin description can be written $H=\sum_{i<j}J_{ij}\mathbf S_i\cdot\mathbf S_j$. In this explicit plus-sign convention $J<0$ favors parallel [spins](../../../../spin.md) and $J>0$ favors antiparallel [spins](../../../../spin.md); many texts put a minus sign into the Hamiltonian and reverse that convention.

[Direct exchange](../../../../direct-exchange.md) involves overlapping orbitals and their Coulomb exchange matrix elements. The [Pauli exclusion principle](../../../../pauli-exclusion-principle.md) correlates [spin](../../../../spin.md) symmetry with spatial symmetry, changing the electronic [energy](../../../../energy-split.md). Orbital occupation and competing kinetic processes determine which [spin](../../../../spin.md) state is favored; the preference is not universally ferromagnetic.

[Superexchange](../../../../superexchange.md) couples moments through virtual hopping, often via a nonmagnetic ligand. For two half-filled orbitals in the simple repulsive one-band model, the antiferromagnetic coupling is $J\simeq4|t|^2/U$: virtual double occupation lowers the antiparallel-spin state more effectively. Orbital geometry and intermediate-state interactions can change the sign in other configurations. Real charge transport can remain insulating while this magnetic coupling acts.

[Double exchange](../../../../double-exchange-mechanism.md) instead uses actual carrier motion between mixed-valence sites. Local [Hund's coupling](../../../../hund-s-coupling.md) ties the mobile [electron](../../../../electron.md)'s [spin](../../../../spin.md) to a core moment. Parallel neighboring cores allow a larger effective hopping amplitude and a lower carrier [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md), favoring ferromagnetism. The original model explicitly connected conduction and magnetic order in manganese perovskites.[1951 abstract: correlation of electrical conduction with ferromagnetism and its double-exchange interpretation](../../../../interaction-between-the-d-shells-in-the-transition-metals-ii-ferromagnetic-compounds-of-manganese-with-perovskite-structure-by-clarence-zener.md)

[Conduction-electron-mediated exchange](../../../../conduction-electron-mediated-exchange.md) couples localized moments through a metal's electronic [spin](../../../../spin.md) response. Its strength and sign depend on separation and the [Fermi surface](../../../../fermi-surface.md); in a simple three-dimensional asymptotic model the range factor oscillates as $\cos(2k_Fr)/r^3$. The original nuclear-moment calculation was motivated by resonance-line broadening in silver.[1954 abstract and introduction: nuclear moments coupled through conduction-electron hyperfine interactions](../../../../indirect-exchange-coupling-of-nuclear-magnetic-moments-by-conduction-electrons-by-m-a-ruderman-and-charles-kittel.md) In an itinerant metal, an alternative description compares the kinetic-energy cost of unequal [spin](../../../../spin.md) populations with the exchange-energy gain; the [Stoner criterion](../../../../stoner-criterion.md) expresses when spontaneous polarization is favorable.

The resulting order also depends on the lattice and competing couplings. In [ferromagnetism](../../../../ferromagnetism.md), moments align to give a nonzero moment within a domain. In [antiferromagnetism](../../../../antiferromagnetism.md), opposing sublattices cancel their moments. In [ferrimagnetism](../../../../ferrimagnetism.md), oppositely ordered sublattices have unequal moments and leave a net one. A bulk zero magnetization alone cannot distinguish antiferromagnetic order from a disordered state or canceling [magnetic domains](../../../../magnetic-domain.md).

The [manganese monoxide neutron diffraction experiment](../../../../manganese-monoxide-neutron-diffraction-experiment.md) makes that distinction experimentally. A monochromatic neutron beam scatters from powdered material, and a counter records angular intensity. Comparing the same compound at room [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) and $80\,\mathrm K$ reveals additional low-temperature magnetic reflections, indexed by a larger magnetic unit cell, while nuclear reflections persist. That spatial periodicity is evidence of alternating order even though the net moment cancels.[1949, Fig. 1 and its room-temperature/80 K comparison](../../../../detection-of-antiferromagnetism-by-neutron-diffraction-by-clifford-glenwood-shull-and-j-samuel-smart.md)
