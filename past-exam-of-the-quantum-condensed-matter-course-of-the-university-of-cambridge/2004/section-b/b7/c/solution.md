<h1 id="section-b/b7/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

This relates an [thermodynamic equilibrium](../../../../../thermodynamic-equilibrium.md) [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) to the local stiffness of an appropriate [thermodynamic free energy](../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md). At fixed bath [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), suppose the effective [thermodynamic free energy](../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) $A(x)$ includes the constraints and the measure for a chosen coordinate. Its [thermodynamic equilibrium](../../../../../thermodynamic-equilibrium.md) [probability density function](../../../../../probability-density-function.md) is proportional to $e^{-A(x)/(k_BT)}$. Near a stable minimum $x_0$, write

$$
A(x)\simeq A(x_0)+\frac12A''(x_0)(x-x_0)^2,\qquad A''(x_0)>0.
$$

The resulting [normal distribution](../../../../../normal-distribution.md) gives the [Gaussian fluctuation approximation](../../../../../gaussian-fluctuation-approximation.md)

$$
\boxed{\langle(x-x_0)^2\rangle\simeq\frac{k_BT}{A''(x_0)}.}
$$

This is exact for a quadratic [thermodynamic free energy](../../../../../thermodynamic-free-energy.md) with the stated flat coordinate measure; in general it is a small-fluctuation result. A smaller curvature means a softer restoring response and larger fluctuations. Near vanishing curvature, higher-order terms become important, so the quadratic approximation cannot alone predict an actual infinite variance.

For example [optical trap Langevin calibration](../../../../../optical-trap-langevin-calibration.md) uses the Brownian displacement of a trapped bead: $A=\kappa(x-x_0)^2/2$ gives $\kappa=k_BT/\langle\Delta x^2\rangle$. A camera or position detector records the fluctuations. Finite exposure averages rapid motion and can bias the measured variance; the measurement protocol matters as well as the [thermodynamic equilibrium](../../../../../thermodynamic-equilibrium.md) identity.
