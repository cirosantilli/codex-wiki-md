<h1 id="section-b/b8/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B8](../b8.md)

[Entropy](../../../../entropy-split.md) is a thermodynamic [state function](../../../../state-function.md). For a reversible [heat](../../../../heat-split.md) transfer, $dS=\delta Q_{\rm rev}/T$; the [Clausius inequality](../../../../clausius-inequality.md) generalizes this to irreversible processes. The combined [entropy](../../../../entropy-split.md) of a system and its surroundings does not decrease. A subsystem can lose [entropy](../../../../entropy-split.md) by exporting [heat](../../../../heat-split.md) or matter, so the second-law statement must specify the whole isolated combination. In a simple compressible system,

$$
dU=T\,dS-P\,dV+\mu\,dN,
$$

which identifies [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md), [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md) and [chemical potential](../../../../chemical-potential-split.md) as derivatives of the [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) representation of the state function. [Thermodynamic equilibrium](../../../../thermodynamic-equilibrium.md) maximizes [entropy](../../../../entropy-split.md) under fixed total [energy](../../../../energy-split.md), [volume](../../../../volume.md) and particle number. A [heat](../../../../heat-split.md) reservoir instead leads to minimizing the [Helmholtz free energy](../../../../helmholtz-free-energy.md) at fixed $T,V,N$, or the [Gibbs free energy](../../../../gibbs-free-energy.md) at fixed $T,P,N$.

Statistical mechanics explains the state count. In a [microcanonical ensemble](../../../../microcanonical-ensemble.md), equally probable compatible microstates give [Boltzmann's entropy formula](../../../../boltzmann-s-entropy-formula.md), $S=k_B\ln\Omega$. With general discrete probabilities, the [Gibbs entropy](../../../../gibbs-entropy.md) is

$$
S=-k_B\sum_jp_j\ln p_j.
$$

The microcanonical formula follows by setting every nonzero probability to $1/\Omega$. For a [canonical ensemble](../../../../canonical-ensemble-split.md), $p_j=e^{-\beta E_j}/Z$, so

$$
\boxed{S=k_B\ln Z+\frac UT,\qquad F=U-TS=-k_BT\ln Z.}
$$

This turns microscopic [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) levels and their populations into a measurable thermodynamic quantity. Classical phase-space counting needs a consistent microscopic normalization and indistinguishability convention; quantum state counting supplies that normalization. Independent systems have multiplicative state counts and therefore additive entropies.

For an [ideal gas](../../../../ideal-gas-split.md) freely expanding from [volume](../../../../volume.md) $V$ to $2V$ in an insulated enclosure, [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) remains fixed while each particle gains twice the accessible position [volume](../../../../volume.md). The state count increases by $2^N$, giving $\boxed{\Delta S=Nk_B\ln2}$. The actual irreversible process transfers no [heat](../../../../heat-split.md). A reversible isothermal comparison path transfers [heat](../../../../heat-split.md) $Nk_BT\ln2$ and computes the same [entropy](../../../../entropy-split.md) change because the initial and final [thermodynamic equilibrium](../../../../thermodynamic-equilibrium.md) states are the same.

Calorimetry supplies another connection to experiment: at fixed [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md) and within one phase, $S(T)-S(0)=\int_0^T C_P(T')\,dT'/T'$, with latent-heat contributions $\mathcal L/T$ at first-order transitions. The [third law of thermodynamics](../../../../third-law-of-thermodynamics.md) fixes $S(0)=0$ for an ideal perfect crystal with a unique ground state. Degeneracy or frozen disorder can produce [residual entropy](../../../../residual-entropy.md). [Entropy](../../../../entropy-split.md) thus links heat-capacity measurements, phase [thermodynamic equilibrium](../../../../thermodynamic-equilibrium.md) and the number of accessible microscopic configurations.
