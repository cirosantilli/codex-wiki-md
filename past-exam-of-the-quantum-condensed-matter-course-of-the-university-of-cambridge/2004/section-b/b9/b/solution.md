<h1 id="section-b/b9/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Generation increases the mobile [electron](../../../../../electron.md) or hole populations; interband excitation creates an [electron-hole pair](../../../../../electron-hole-pair.md). Thermal excitation and absorbed photons are important sources, and [impact ionization](../../../../../impact-ionization.md) can multiply carriers in a strong field. Ordinary single-photon interband absorption needs enough photon [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) to bridge the [band gap](../../../../../band-gap.md), with the required momentum balance.

[Electron-hole recombination](../../../../../electron-hole-recombination.md) removes a pair. [Radiative electron-hole recombination](../../../../../radiative-electron-hole-recombination.md) emits a photon, with simple rate $Bnp$; [trap-assisted electron-hole recombination](../../../../../trap-assisted-electron-hole-recombination.md) uses localized defect levels and transfers [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) to the lattice; [Auger recombination](../../../../../auger-recombination.md) transfers the [electron-hole recombination](../../../../../electron-hole-recombination.md) [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) to another carrier. The last process becomes especially important at high injection because its rate contains products such as $n^2p$ and $np^2$. The detailed band structure and available defects determine which channel dominates.

At thermal [thermodynamic equilibrium](../../../../../thermodynamic-equilibrium.md), generation and [electron-hole recombination](../../../../../electron-hole-recombination.md) continue at equal rates. For the simple radiative model the net [electron-hole recombination](../../../../../electron-hole-recombination.md) rate is $B(np-n_i^2)$, enforcing the [semiconductor mass-action law](../../../../../semiconductor-mass-action-law.md). Under weak excitation with the majority density nearly fixed, an excess minority density often decays as $\Delta n(t)=\Delta n(0)e^{-t/\tau}$, defining its [minority carrier lifetime](../../../../../minority-carrier-lifetime.md).

A short light pulse followed by time-resolved [electrical conductivity](../../../../../electrical-conductivity.md) or luminescence measures the relaxation. Spatial [diffusion](../../../../../diffusion.md), trapping and instrumental response must be separated from [electron-hole recombination](../../../../../electron-hole-recombination.md) before identifying a single fitted decay constant with the intrinsic lifetime. These processes control photodetector response and light-emitting-device efficiency.
