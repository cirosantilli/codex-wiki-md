↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Charge carrier mobility](../../../../../charge-carrier-mobility.md) measures weak-field drift speed per [electric field](../../../../../electric-field-split.md). For [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) and [electron holes](../../../../../electron-hole.md),

$$
\mathbf v_e=-\mu_e\mathbf E,\qquad\mathbf v_h=+\mu_h\mathbf E,\qquad\boxed{\sigma=e(n\mu_e+p\mu_h).}
$$

Both mobilities are positive, with SI units $\mathrm{m^2\,V^{-1}s^{-1}}$; the opposite drift signs reflect the opposite carrier charges. In a simple isotropic relaxation-time model, [electron mobility](../../../../../electron-mobility.md) is $\mu_e=e\tau_e/m_e^*$ and [hole mobility](../../../../../hole-mobility.md) is $\mu_h=e\tau_h/m_h^*$. These are momentum relaxation times, not the [minority carrier lifetime](../../../../../minority-carrier-lifetime.md) for [electron-hole recombination](../../../../../electron-hole-recombination.md).

Scattering by [phonons](../../../../../phonon.md), ionized impurities and defects limits mobility. Increasing [phonon](../../../../../phonon.md) populations commonly lowers mobility at high [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md); ionized-impurity scattering can dominate at low [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) and high [semiconductor doping](../../../../../semiconductor-doping.md). Approximate addition of independent inverse relaxation times is the [Matthiessen's rule](../../../../../matthiessen-s-rule.md). At sufficiently strong field, velocity saturation invalidates the constant-mobility drift law.

The [Hall effect](../../../../../hall-effect.md) provides a measurement: a current-carrying bar in a perpendicular [magnetic field](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) develops a transverse voltage. In a one-carrier model $|R_H|=1/(en)$ and $\mu=\sigma|R_H|$, combining the transverse voltage with longitudinal [electrical conductivity](../../../../../electrical-conductivity.md). Multiple carrier types and scattering-dependent Hall factors require a more detailed interpretation.
