<h1 id="section-a/4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

A straight line in $C/T$ against $T^2$ means $C/T=\gamma+\beta T^2$. Reading its intercept and slope gives approximately $\gamma\simeq2.1\,\mathrm{mJ\,mol^{-1}K^{-2}}$ and $\beta\simeq2.7\,\mathrm{mJ\,mol^{-1}K^{-4}}$; the graphical precision is only a few percent. Hence the molar [heat capacity](../../../../heat-capacity.md) is approximately

$$
\boxed{C(T)\simeq(2.1\times10^{-3}\,\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}K^{-2}})T+(2.7\times10^{-3}\,\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}K^{-4}})T^3.}
$$

The linear [electronic heat capacity](../../../../electronic-heat-capacity.md) comes from conduction [electrons](../../../../electron.md) within [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) of order $k_BT$ of the [Fermi energy](../../../../fermi-energy.md); the [Pauli exclusion principle](../../../../pauli-exclusion-principle.md) prevents deeply occupied states from contributing freely. The cubic lattice term is the low-temperature [Debye heat capacity](../../../../debye-heat-capacity.md) of three-dimensional acoustic [phonons](../../../../phonon.md), whose low-frequency [density of states](../../../../density-of-states.md) is proportional to [frequency](../../../../frequency.md) squared. These are molar coefficients, so the [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) powers carry the extra kelvin factors indicated.
