<h1 id="section-a/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Treat $\alpha$ as a constant and use the [canonical ensemble](../../../../canonical-ensemble-split.md) identity $F=-k_BT\ln Z$. The given formal [partition function](../../../../canonical-partition-function.md) gives $F=\alpha k_BT^3N^2/V$. The [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md) is its negative [volume](../../../../volume.md) derivative at fixed $T,N$:

$$
\boxed{P=-\left(\frac{\partial F}{\partial V}\right)_{T,N}=\alpha k_BT^3\frac{N^2}{V^2},\qquad PV^2=\alpha k_BT^3N^2.}
$$

Equivalently $P=k_BT(\partial\ln Z/\partial V)_{T,N}$. The explicit [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) dependence in $\ln Z$ must be retained; this is not the ideal-gas [pressure](../../../../pressure-split.md) law.
