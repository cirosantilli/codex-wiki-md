<h1 id="section-a/a2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For two species of chains with the same polymerization degree $N$, the number of independently translating objects per occupied site is reduced by $N$. The [symmetric Flory-Huggins polymer blend](../../../../../symmetric-flory-huggins-polymer-blend.md) has

$$
\frac f{k_BT}=\frac{\phi\ln\phi+(1-\phi)\ln(1-\phi)}N+\chi\phi(1-\phi),\qquad \frac{f''}{k_BT}=\frac1{N\phi(1-\phi)}-2\chi.
$$

The symmetry still places the [thermodynamic critical point](../../../../../thermodynamic-critical-point.md) at $\phi_c=1/2$, but now

$$
\boxed{\chi_c=\frac2N.}
$$

The [entropy of mixing](../../../../../entropy-of-mixing.md) per monomer is much smaller than for simple liquids, so even a weak unfavorable interaction can cause [phase separation](../../../../../phase-separation.md) for long chains. If the energetic interaction parameter is $\chi=A/T$ with $A>0$, this model gives $T_c=AN/2$; the conclusion relies on that specified temperature law.
