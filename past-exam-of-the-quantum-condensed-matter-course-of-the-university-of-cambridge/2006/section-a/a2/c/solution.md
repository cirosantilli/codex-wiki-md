<h1 id="section-a/a2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

With polymer degree $N_A$ and single-site solvent molecules, [Flory–Huggins solution theory](../../../../../flory-huggins-solution-theory.md) gives $f/(k_BT)=(\phi/N_A)\ln\phi+(1-\phi)\ln(1-\phi)+\chi\phi(1-\phi)$. Expanding the solvent entropy at low concentration gives the [dilute-polymer free-energy expansion](../../../../../dilute-polymer-free-energy-expansion.md)

$$
\boxed{\frac f{k_BT}=\frac\phi{N_A}\ln\phi+(\chi-1)\phi+\left(\frac12-\chi\right)\phi^2+\frac{\phi^3}{6}+O(\phi^4).}
$$

The linear term sets a reference chemical potential. The quadratic term controls the leading effective two-segment [excluded volume](../../../../../excluded-volume.md), $v_{\rm eff}=v_0(1-2\chi)$ for site volume $v_0$.

In a [good solvent](../../../../../good-solvent.md), $\chi<1/2$ and positive [excluded volume](../../../../../excluded-volume.md) swells a dilute chain: the [Flory approximation](../../../../../flory-approximation.md) gives $R\sim bN_A^{3/5}$ in three dimensions. In a [poor solvent](../../../../../poor-solvent.md), $\chi>1/2$ favors segment contacts and a sufficiently long isolated chain forms a [polymer globule](../../../../../polymer-globule.md), with compact scaling $R\sim bN_A^{1/3}$ up to density-dependent factors.

At the [theta temperature](../../../../../theta-temperature.md), the leading two-segment interaction cancels, $\chi=1/2$ in this model, and the long-chain conformation is approximately an [ideal chain](../../../../../ideal-chain.md), $R\sim b\sqrt{N_A}$. The positive cubic term remains. The [critical point of a Flory–Huggins polymer solution](../../../../../critical-point-of-a-flory-huggins-polymer-solution.md) at finite $N_A$ has $\chi_c=\tfrac12(1+N_A^{-1/2})^2$; its limiting value, rather than every finite-chain critical point, is the theta condition.
