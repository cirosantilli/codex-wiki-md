<h1 id="section-a/a2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Write $z_c$ for the lattice coordination number and $\epsilon_{AA},\epsilon_{BB},\epsilon_{AB}$ for contact energies. Counting the excess of unlike contacts relative to the unmixed reference gives the [Flory interaction parameter](../../../../../flory-interaction-parameter.md)

$$
\boxed{\chi=\frac{z_c}{k_BT}\left[\epsilon_{AB}-\frac{\epsilon_{AA}+\epsilon_{BB}}2\right].}
$$

Unlike contacts that are less favorable than the mean like contact give $\chi>0$. For temperature-independent contact energies, **$\chi$ is proportional to $1/T$**, so heating weakens this energetic preference relative to the [entropy of mixing](../../../../../entropy-of-mixing.md). Effective parameters in real solutions can also include entropic terms, often represented schematically by $A/T+B$; the pure contact-energy model does not explain every possible temperature dependence.
