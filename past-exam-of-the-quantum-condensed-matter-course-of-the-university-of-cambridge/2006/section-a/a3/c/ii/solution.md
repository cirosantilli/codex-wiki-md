<h1 id="section-a/a3/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Multiplying each ion number density by its signed charge gives the local [charge density](../../../../../../charge-density.md)

$$
\boxed{\rho(s)=q[n_+(s)-n_-(s)]=-2qn_0\sinh\!\frac{q\psi(s)}{k_BT}.}
$$

The fixed surface charge is supplied separately as a boundary condition; it is not part of this mobile charge density in the liquid.
