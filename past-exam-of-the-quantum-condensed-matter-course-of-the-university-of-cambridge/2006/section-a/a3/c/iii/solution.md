<h1 id="section-a/a3/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Substitution into [Poisson's equation](../../../../../../poisson-s-equation.md), $\nabla^2\psi=-\rho/\varepsilon$, gives the [Poisson-Boltzmann equation](../../../../../../poisson-boltzmann-equation.md)

$$
\boxed{\nabla^2\psi=\frac{2qn_0}{\varepsilon}\sinh\!\frac{q\psi}{k_BT}.}
$$

For a plane, $\nabla^2\psi=d^2\psi/ds^2$; for a sphere, it is $r^{-2}d(r^2\psi')/dr$. The bulk conditions are $\psi\to0$ and $n_\pm\to n_0$. At a planar wall with negligible internal field and normal pointing into the liquid, [Gauss's law](../../../../../../gauss-s-law.md) imposes $-\varepsilon\psi'(0)=\sigma_s$; alternatively, a prescribed surface potential gives $\psi(0)=\psi_0$.
