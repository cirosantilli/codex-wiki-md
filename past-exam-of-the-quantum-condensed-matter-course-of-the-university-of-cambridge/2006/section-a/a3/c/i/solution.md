<h1 id="section-a/a3/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Constancy of each ionic [electrochemical potential](../../../../../../electrochemical-potential.md) gives the [Boltzmann distribution](../../../../../../boltzmann-distribution.md) in the local [electric potential](../../../../../../electric-potential.md):

$$
\boxed{n_+(s)=n_0e^{-q\psi(s)/(k_BT)},\qquad n_-(s)=n_0e^{q\psi(s)/(k_BT)}.}
$$

Positive potential repels positive ions and attracts negative [counterions](../../../../../../counterion.md). The coordinate $s$ measures distance from a locally planar surface; the same local relations hold for a spherical potential with its appropriate radial coordinate.
