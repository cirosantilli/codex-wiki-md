↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

In the [quantum Hall effect](../../../../../quantum-hall-effect.md), a two-dimensional electron system at low temperature in a strong perpendicular [magnetic field](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) has transverse conductivity of quantized magnitude $|\sigma_{xy}|=\nu e^2/h$. On a plateau with negligible longitudinal conductivity, [quantum Hall conductivity tensor inversion](../../../../../quantum-hall-conductivity-tensor-inversion.md) gives $|\rho_{xy}|=h/(\nu e^2)$ and $\rho_{xx}\to0$; the Hall sign depends on the carrier and orientation convention. The [filling factor](../../../../../filling-factor-quantum-hall-effect.md) is $\nu=n_{2D}h/(eB)$ when all spin-resolved states are counted.

For the [integer quantum Hall effect](../../../../../integer-quantum-hall-effect.md), filled [Landau levels](../../../../../landau-level.md) and a mobility gap give integer $\nu$. Disorder-localized states allow the density or field to vary without changing the number of extended occupied channels, producing a [quantum Hall plateau](../../../../../quantum-hall-plateau.md) rather than quantization only at isolated fields. [Quantum Hall edge states](../../../../../quantum-hall-edge-state.md) provide an equivalent transport picture. In a [Hall bar](../../../../../hall-bar.md), separate voltage leads measure transverse and longitudinal voltages while a known current flows; sweeping the field reveals both the plateau and the resistance minimum. The [von Klitzing-Dorda-Pepper quantum Hall experiment](../../../../../von-klitzing-dorda-pepper-quantum-hall-experiment.md) established this integer quantization.

The [fractional quantum Hall effect](../../../../../fractional-quantum-hall-effect.md) requires electron correlations within a partially filled [Landau level](../../../../../landau-level.md). An incompressible correlated state can occur at fractions such as $\nu=1/3$; a noninteracting partially filled level does not account for the associated gap. The [Laughlin wavefunction](../../../../../laughlin-wavefunction.md) at $\nu=1/m$, odd $m$, contains a factor $\prod_{i<j}(z_i-z_j)^m$, suppressing close electron approaches while retaining fermionic antisymmetry. Its charged collective excitations can carry magnitude $e/m$; the constituent electrons still have charge $-e$. The [Tsui-Störmer-Gossard fractional Hall experiment](../../../../../tsui-stormer-gossard-fractional-hall-experiment.md) observed the fractional Hall feature together with a longitudinal-resistance minimum, providing the experimental distinction from an arbitrary rational filling of an ordinary conducting state.
