<h1 id="section-b/b4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In an isolated [energy band](../../../../../energy-band.md), semiclassical motion satisfies $\hbar\dot k=F$ and $v=\hbar^{-1}dE/dk$. The band is periodic in [crystal momentum](../../../../../crystal-momentum.md), so a constant force sweeps the state through equivalent points of the [Brillouin zone](../../../../../brillouin-zone.md). Its velocity repeats rather than increasing without bound. For an electron in a constant [electric field](../../../../../electric-field-split.md), $F=-e\mathcal E$, and the [Bloch oscillation](../../../../../bloch-oscillation.md) period is

$$
\boxed{T_B=\frac{2\pi\hbar}{e|\mathcal E|a},\qquad\omega_B=\frac{e|\mathcal E|a}{\hbar}.}
$$

For $E(k)=E_0-2J\cos ka$, $v=(2Ja/\hbar)\sin ka$ and integration gives a bounded position oscillation of amplitude $2J/|F|$ about its mean. A filled band has no corresponding net transport oscillation after summing uniformly over all $k$.

Observation requires scattering times longer than $T_B$ and negligible [interband tunnelling](../../../../../interband-tunnelling.md). Ordinary crystals often scatter too rapidly before a full zone traversal; larger-period structures and clean atom-optical lattices help. In the [cold-atom Bloch oscillation experiment](../../../../../cold-atom-bloch-oscillation-experiment.md), accelerating a standing-wave lattice supplied a controlled force, and releasing the atoms at different times revealed an oscillating momentum distribution.[Physical Review Letters 76, 4508–4511 (1996), lattice acceleration and momentum measurements](../../../../../bloch-oscillations-of-atoms-in-an-optical-potential-by-maxime-dahan-and-others.md)
