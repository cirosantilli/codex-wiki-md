# Inward transport by an angular-momentum-enhanced disk wind

↑ **Parent:** [Wind-loaded Keplerian disk evolution](wind-loaded-keplerian-disk-evolution.md)

For zero viscosity, $S=f(r)\Sigma$ and constant $q>1$, set $\beta=2(q-1)$. On intervals where f\>0, the [wind-loaded Keplerian disk evolution](wind-loaded-keplerian-disk-evolution.md) equation becomes $\Sigma_t=\beta rf\Sigma_r+[\beta(2f+rf')-f]\Sigma$. Multiplication by $fr^{2-1/\beta}$ and the displayed travel-time coordinate reduce it to constant-speed [advection](advection.md). Its profile is $g'=F(x'+t)$ and its characteristics move inward with $dr/dt=-\beta rf$. The weight accounts for mass loss as well as radial compression.
