# Traceless second-rank tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor](tensor.md)

A second-rank Cartesian [tensor](tensor.md) is traceless when the contraction $T_{ii}$ vanishes. For a [symmetric second-rank tensor](symmetric-second-rank-tensor.md), this is equivalent to its [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) summing to zero. The orientational [nematic order parameter](nematic-order-parameter.md) is both symmetric and traceless, excluding an isotropic scalar contribution.
