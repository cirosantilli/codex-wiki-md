# Excellent three-manifold

↑ **Parent:** [3-manifold](3-manifold.md)

In the orientable case an excellent compact three-manifold is an [irreducible three-manifold](irreducible-three-manifold.md) and a [boundary-irreducible three-manifold](boundary-irreducible-three-manifold.md), is not a ball, contains a two-sided properly embedded [incompressible surface](incompressible-surface.md), and has every properly embedded incompressible zero-[Euler characteristic](euler-characteristic.md) surface a [boundary-parallel surface](boundary-parallel-surface.md). In particular it has no essential [annulus](annulus-mathematics.md) or [torus](torus.md). These properties give lower bounds on the [Thurston norm](thurston-norm.md) of classes with essential boundary.
