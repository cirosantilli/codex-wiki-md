# Thin galactic disk

↑ **Parent:** [Galactic disk](galactic-disk.md)

A thin galactic disk is a stellar component whose vertical extent is small compared with its radial size. Small vertical [velocity dispersion](velocity-dispersion.md) and substantial [specific angular momentum](specific-angular-momentum.md) are characteristic. Its survival constrains cumulative [galactic disk heating](galactic-disk-heating.md), but its stars need not all have the same formation epoch.
