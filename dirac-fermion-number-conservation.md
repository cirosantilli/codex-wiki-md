# Dirac fermion number conservation

↑ **Parent:** [Dirac field](dirac-field.md)

A [Dirac field](dirac-field.md) theory invariant under $\psi\mapsto e^{i\alpha}\psi$, $\bar\psi\mapsto e^{-i\alpha}\bar\psi$ has a conserved global charge, subject to the quantum theory preserving the [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md). In particle language this charge counts particles minus [antiparticles](antiparticle.md). When the [S-matrix](s-matrix.md) commutes with it, a [scattering amplitude](scattering-amplitude.md) between states of different charge vanishes. A neutral scalar and a [Yukawa interaction](yukawa-interaction.md) preserve the charge, so a process with one incoming Dirac particle and only one outgoing Dirac antiparticle plus neutral particles is forbidden. This net charge is distinct from a sum of positive occupation numbers for all particle and antiparticle modes.
