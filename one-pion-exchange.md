# One-pion exchange

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear force](nuclear-force-split.md)

Although the [pion](pion.md) has [spin](spin.md) zero, [one-pion exchange](one-pion-exchange.md) depends on [nucleon](nucleon.md) [spin](spin.md) and [isospin](isospin.md) and includes a [nuclear tensor force](nuclear-tensor-force.md). Shorter-range and multiple-[nucleon](nucleon.md) interactions also matter. The scalar [Yukawa potential](yukawa-potential.md) isolates a range mechanism rather than specifying the complete [nuclear force](nuclear-force-split.md).[Modern Theory of Nuclear Forces, inspected preprint sections I.B and II.B, especially equation (2.11) and Fig. 12](modern-theory-of-nuclear-forces-by-evgeny-epelbaum-and-others.md)
