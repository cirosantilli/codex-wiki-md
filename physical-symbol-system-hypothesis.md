# Physical symbol system hypothesis

↑ **Parent:** [Symbolic computation](symbolic-computation.md)

A proposal that structured symbolic representation and manipulation provide a basis for general intelligent action. It is a claim about computation to be evaluated empirically, rather than a mathematical theorem equating every particular symbolic program with intelligence.[Communications of the ACM 19, pp. 113–126, physical symbol systems and search](computer-science-as-empirical-inquiry-symbols-and-search-by-allen-newell-and-others.md)
