# Global generation of positive twists on a smooth curve

↑ **Parent:** [Globally generated vector bundle](globally-generated-vector-bundle.md)

For a [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) of finite positive rank let $B$ be an upper degree bound for the [line subbundles](line-subbundle.md) of $E^*\otimes K_C$. If $m-1>B$, no nonzero map $\mathcal O_C(mp_0-p)\to E^*\otimes K_C$ can exist for any point $p$: its saturated image would have degree at least $m-1$. [Serre duality](serre-duality.md) then gives $H^1(E(mp_0-p))=0$. The evaluation [exact sequence](exact-sequence.md) shows that $H^0(E(mp_0))\to E(mp_0)|_p$ is [surjective](surjective-function.md) at every point, proving the assertion with a uniform bound in $p$.
