# Marked-lattice model of a modular form

↑ **Parent:** [Gamma 1 level structure on a complex lattice](gamma-1-level-structure-on-a-complex-lattice.md)

Choose an oriented lattice basis with $t=\omega_2/N$ modulo the lattice. Changing such a basis is exactly the [Gamma 1 congruence subgroup](gamma-1-congruence-subgroup.md) action, so the weight law makes the displayed function independent of the choice. It satisfies $F(uL,ut)=u^{-k}F(L,t)$. Holomorphy on the half-plane and at all [modular cusp](cusp-of-a-modular-group.md) degenerations identifies the functions coming from [modular forms](modular-form.md).
