# Lie algebra of a quadratic-form stabilizer

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix Lie group](matrix-lie-group.md)

For a real [quadratic form](quadratic-form.md) $Q(v)=v^TAv$ with $A$ a [symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md), its stabilizer is $G_Q=\{g\in\operatorname{GL}_n(\mathbb R):g^TAg=A\}$. Its [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) consists exactly of $X$ satisfying $X^TA+AX=0$. Differentiation proves necessity; the [matrix exponential](matrix-exponential.md) proves sufficiency because $e^{tX^T}Ae^{tX}=A$. The result holds even when the [quadratic form](quadratic-form.md) is degenerate.
