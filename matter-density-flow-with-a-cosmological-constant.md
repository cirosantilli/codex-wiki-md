# Matter density flow with a cosmological constant

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmological density parameter flow](cosmological-density-parameter-flow.md)

For separately conserved [pressureless matter](pressureless-matter.md), $\rho_m\propto a^{-3}$. The [deceleration parameter](deceleration-parameter.md) identity $\dot H/H^2=-(1+q)$ and $\Omega_m\propto\rho_m/H^2$ give $\dot\Omega_m=H\Omega_m(2q-1)$. A dust-plus-vacuum universe has $q=\Omega_m/2-\Omega_\Lambda$; conversion to conformal time yields the displayed flow. The familiar matter-only equation requires negligible [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md). In a spatially flat dust-plus-vacuum universe, $\Omega_m+\Omega_\Lambda=1$ and $\Omega_m'=-3\mathcal H\Omega_m\Omega_\Lambda$, so flatness does not require $\Omega_m=1$.
