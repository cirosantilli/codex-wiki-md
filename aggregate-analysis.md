# Aggregate analysis

↑ **Parent:** [Amortized analysis](amortized-analysis.md)

[Aggregate analysis](aggregate-analysis.md) bounds the total cost of a whole [sequence](sequence.md) directly, then divides by its length to obtain an amortized bound. Summing the copied capacities as a [geometric series](geometric-series.md) in [dynamic array doubling](dynamic-array-doubling.md) is an example.
