# Three-body problem

↑ **Parent:** [Celestial mechanics](celestial-mechanics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three-body_problem)

The gravitational motion of three interacting bodies under their coupled [Newtonian mechanics](newtonian-mechanics-split.md) equations. When one body's [mass](mass.md) is negligible, the [restricted three-body problem](restricted-three-body-problem.md) neglects its influence on the other two while retaining both forces on its trajectory.
