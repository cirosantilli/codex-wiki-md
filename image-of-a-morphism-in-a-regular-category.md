# Image of a morphism in a regular category

↑ **Parent:** [Regular category](regular-category.md)

In a [regular category](regular-category.md), the image of $f:A\to B$ is the monomorphism in its essentially unique factorization

$$
A\twoheadrightarrow\operatorname{im}f\hookrightarrow B
$$

as a [regular epimorphism](regular-epimorphism.md) followed by a [monomorphism](monomorphism.md). Images are stable under pullback.
