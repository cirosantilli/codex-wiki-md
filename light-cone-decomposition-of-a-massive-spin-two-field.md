# Light-cone decomposition of a massive spin-two field

↑ **Parent:** [Massive spin-two field](massive-spin-two-field.md)

When $\partial_-$ is invertible, divergence determines components with a plus index from $h_{--},h_{-I},h_{IJ}$. The [trace](matrix-trace.md) constraint fixes the [trace](matrix-trace.md) of $h_{IJ}$. The remaining transverse [group representations](group-representation.md) are a [symmetric traceless square](symmetric-trace-free-square-of-the-defining-orthogonal-representation.md), a [vector representation](vector-representation.md), and a scalar.
