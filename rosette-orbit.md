# Rosette orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Central force](central-force.md)

A planar [orbit](orbit-dynamical-system.md) whose [radius](radius.md) oscillates as its [polar angle](polar-angle.md) increases, producing successive lobes around the force center. For nonconstant $r$ with fundamental angular radial period $2\pi/\nu$ and nonzero [specific angular momentum](specific-angular-momentum.md), the full [position](position.md) and [velocity](velocity.md) repeat precisely when $\nu$ is a [rational number](rational-number.md), provided the [radius](radius.md) is bounded above and away from zero. If $\nu=p/q$ in lowest terms, $p$ radial periods advance the angle by $2\pi q$; $dt=r^2\,d\theta/h$ then gives a finite time period. This proves closure rather than merely a spatial self-intersection.
