# Axisymmetric clamped-plate deflection

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic plate](elastic-plate.md)

A circular [elastic plate](elastic-plate.md) with [bending stiffness](bending-stiffness.md) $B$, constant excess pressure $p$ and [clamped boundary conditions](clamped-boundary-condition.md) at radius $R$ obeys $B\nabla_r^4h=p$, where $\nabla_r^2=r^{-1}\partial_r(r\partial_r)$. Its regular solution is $h=p(R^2-r^2)^2/(64B)$. Consequently its enclosed volume is $V=\pi pR^6/(192B)$, and its edge [curvature](curvature.md) is $h_{rr}(R)=pR^2/(8B)=24V/(\pi R^4)$.
