# Carbon price

↑ **Parent:** [Carbon pricing](carbon-pricing.md)

The monetary charge per unit emitted, for example currency per tonne of carbon dioxide. Multiplying by an activity's emissions intensity gives its direct carbon-cost increment per unit output. A common accounting basis must distinguish tonnes of carbon from tonnes of carbon dioxide.
