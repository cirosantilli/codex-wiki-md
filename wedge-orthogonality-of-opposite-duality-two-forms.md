# Wedge orthogonality of opposite-duality two-forms

↑ **Parent:** [Hodge splitting of Euclidean two-forms](hodge-splitting-of-euclidean-two-forms.md)

The [Hodge star operator](hodge-star-operator.md) is self-adjoint on real two-forms in four Euclidean dimensions, so its opposite eigenspaces are orthogonal. Since $H\wedge G=\langle H,*G\rangle\operatorname{vol}_g$, a [self-dual two-form](self-dual-differential-form.md) and an [anti-self-dual two-form](anti-self-dual-differential-form.md) also have zero [wedge product of differential forms](wedge-product-of-differential-forms.md). Applied to Lie-algebra components with an invariant pairing, this identity eliminates mixed terms from the [Yang-Mills action](yang-mills-action.md).
