# Gaussian Bayes classifier

↑ **Parent:** [Bayes classifier](bayes-classifier.md)

For two [multivariate normal densities](multivariate-normal-density.md), the Bayes log posterior odds are a quadratic polynomial. Equal covariance matrices cancel the quadratic term and give [linear discriminant analysis](linear-discriminant-analysis.md); unequal covariance matrices give [quadratic discriminant analysis](quadratic-discriminant-analysis.md).
