# Cubic series reversion

↑ **Parent:** [Series reversion](series-reversion.md)

Suppose $f=a_1g+a_2g^2+a_3g^3+O(g^4)$ with $a_1\ne0$. Substitute $g=b_1f+b_2f^2+b_3f^3+O(f^4)$ and compare coefficients. The three equations are $a_1b_1=1$, $a_1b_2+a_2b_1^2=0$, and $a_1b_3+2a_2b_1b_2+a_3b_1^3=0$. Solving them proves the title formula. This is [series reversion](series-reversion.md) for a local [inverse function](inverse-function.md); analyticity guarantees a convergent inverse series sufficiently near the origin.
