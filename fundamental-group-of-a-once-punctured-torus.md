# Fundamental group of a once-punctured torus

↑ **Parent:** [Fundamental group of a surface](fundamental-group-of-a-surface.md)

Removing an embedded [open disk](open-disk.md) from the [torus](torus.md) leaves a [topological surface](topological-surface-split.md) with one boundary [circle](circle.md). In the square model, radial expansion away from the removed [disk](disk-mathematics.md) gives a [deformation retraction](deformation-retraction.md) onto the square boundary with opposite sides identified, a [wedge of two circles](wedge-of-two-circles.md). Its [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md) is therefore the [free group](free-group.md) on two [group generators](generator-of-a-group.md).
