# Poloidal magnetic field

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetohydrodynamics](magnetohydrodynamics.md)

A poloidal [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) lies in meridional planes through the symmetry axis, in contrast to a [toroidal magnetic field](toroidal-magnetic-field.md). For an [axisymmetric vector field](axisymmetric-vector-field.md), it is represented by a [poloidal magnetic flux function](poloidal-magnetic-flux-function.md) as $\mathbf B_p=R^{-1}\nabla\Psi\times\mathbf e_\phi$.
