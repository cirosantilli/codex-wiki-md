# Perchlorate

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical compound](chemical-compound.md)

An anion containing chlorine in [oxidation state](oxidation-state.md) $+7$, or a salt containing it. Historical ferric “perchloride” is a [chloride](chloride.md), not a [perchlorate](perchlorate.md).
