# Marked length spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Length spectrum](length-spectrum.md)

For a closed [hyperbolic surface](hyperbolic-surface.md) with a specified marking of its [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md), assign to each nontrivial conjugacy class the length of its unique closed [geodesic](geodesic.md). Retaining the group label is stronger than retaining only the unmarked length multiset: it also records which lengths belong to intersecting curves or powers of particular curves.
