# Kakeya maximal conjecture

↑ **Parent:** [Kakeya maximal function](kakeya-maximal-function.md)

The maximal estimate used in the classical [Kakeya set](kakeya-set.md) problem asks, for every $\varepsilon>0$, for a bound from $L^n(\mathbb R^n)$ to $L^n(S^{n-1})$ with loss at most $\delta^{-\varepsilon}$. Constants may depend on dimension and $\varepsilon$, but not tube thickness or the function. Applied to an [indicator function](indicator-function.md) of a neighborhood of a [Kakeya set](kakeya-set.md), it gives the [Kakeya Minkowski dimension conjecture](kakeya-minkowski-dimension-conjecture.md).
