# Unramified residue generator integral basis

↑ **Parent:** [Unramified generator criterion with separable reduction](unramified-generator-criterion-with-separable-reduction.md)

For an [unramified extension](unramified-extension.md) of [local fields](local-field.md) $L/K$, an integral element whose residue generates $k_L/k_K$ generates $L/K$: its degree is at least the [residue degree](residue-degree.md) $f=[L:K]$. The reduced powers $1,\bar\alpha,\ldots,\bar\alpha^{f-1}$ are a [basis](basis.md) of the [residue field](residue-field.md) extension. If $x=\sum c_j\alpha^j$, factor out a coefficient of least [valuation](valuation.md). Reduction of the remaining sum is nonzero by that residue [basis](basis.md), so $v_L(x)=\min_j v_K(c_j)$. Consequently $x$ is integral exactly when all $c_j$ are integral.
