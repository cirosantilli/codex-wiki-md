<h1 id="sydenham-s-chorea">Sydenham's chorea</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Chorea](chorea.md)

An immune-mediated childhood syndrome associated with [acute rheumatic fever](acute-rheumatic-fever.md). The name commemorates [Thomas Sydenham](thomas-sydenham.md). The neurological syndrome can emerge after the preceding infection is no longer demonstrable.
