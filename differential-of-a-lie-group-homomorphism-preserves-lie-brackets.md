# Differential of a Lie group homomorphism preserves Lie brackets

↑ **Parent:** [Lie algebra homomorphism](lie-algebra-homomorphism.md)

For a smooth homomorphism of [Lie groups](lie-group.md), naturality gives $f(\exp X)=\exp(df_eX)$. Therefore $\log(f(g))=df_e(\log g)$ locally. Apply this to the [group commutator](group-commutator.md) and differentiate its two curve parameters to obtain the displayed identity for the [Lie bracket from local group commutators](lie-bracket-from-local-group-commutators.md). In particular the differential of a group representation is a [Lie algebra representation](lie-algebra-representation.md).
