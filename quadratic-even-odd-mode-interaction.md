# Quadratic even-odd mode interaction

↑ **Parent:** [Normal form (dynamical systems)](normal-form-dynamical-systems.md)

A [reflection](reflection-mathematics.md) acting as $(A,B)\mapsto(A,-B)$ forces an [equivariant](equivariant-map.md) [vector field](vector-field.md) to have $\dot A$ even and $\dot B$ odd in $B$. With the zero solution preserved, its quadratic [normal form](normal-form-dynamical-systems.md) is $\dot A=\lambda_1A+a_1A^2+a_2B^2$, $\dot B=\lambda_2B+a_3AB$. For positive coefficients, every mixed [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) with $B\ne0$ has [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md) $-2a_2a_3B^2<0$, hence is a [saddle equilibrium](saddle-equilibrium.md). The two invariant-axis equilibria meet in a [transcritical bifurcation](transcritical-bifurcation.md), and the mixed branches meet them in [pitchfork bifurcations](pitchfork-bifurcation-normal-form.md).
