# Positive contraction

↑ **Parent:** [Positive operator](positive-operator.md)

A positive contraction is a [positive operator](positive-operator.md) whose [operator norm](operator-norm.md) is at most one. Its [spectrum](spectrum-functional-analysis.md) lies in $[0,1]$, so the [spectral theorem for normal operators](spectral-theorem-for-normal-operators.md) gives $M^2\leq M$. Positive contractions are the effects in a [POVM](positive-operator-valued-measure.md) and occur in the [pure-state gentle measurement bound](pure-state-gentle-measurement-bound.md).
