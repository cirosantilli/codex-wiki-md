# Rank-one Kraus representation of an entanglement-breaking channel

↑ **Parent:** [Entanglement-breaking channel](entanglement-breaking-channel.md)

An [entanglement-breaking channel](entanglement-breaking-channel.md) admits a [Kraus representation](kraus-representation.md) with rank-one [Kraus operators](kraus-operator.md) $K_j=|a_j\rangle\langle b_j|$. Its [Choi state](choi-state.md) is a sum of positive [tensor-product operators](tensor-product-operator.md), hence separable. Conversely, spectral decompositions of each prepared state and each [POVM](positive-operator-valued-measure.md) effect in a [measure-and-prepare channel](measure-and-prepare-channel.md) produce rank-one Kraus operators. Trace preservation imposes $\sum_j\lVert a_j\rVert^2|b_j\rangle\langle b_j|=I$.
