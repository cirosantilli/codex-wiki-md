# Feller semigroup

↑ **Parent:** [Contraction semigroup](contraction-semigroup.md)

On a compact [metric space](metric-space.md), a conservative Feller semigroup is a [strongly continuous semigroup](c0-semigroup.md) of positive unital operators on $C(X)$. The [Riesz-Markov-Kakutani representation theorem](riesz-markov-kakutani-representation-theorem.md) associates a [Markov kernel](markov-kernel.md) to each operator. On noncompact spaces the standard definition uses $C_0(X)$; conservativity then refers to the corresponding kernels having mass one, even when $1\notin C_0(X)$.
