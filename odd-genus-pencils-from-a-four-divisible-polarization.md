# Odd-genus pencils from a four-divisible polarization

↑ **Parent:** [Lefschetz pencil](lefschetz-pencil.md)

On a [smooth projective surface](smooth-projective-surface.md), fix an [ample divisor](ample-cartier-divisor.md) $H$. For sufficiently large $m$, a general pencil in $|mH|$ is a [Lefschetz pencil](lefschetz-pencil.md). The [adjunction formula](adjunction-formula.md) gives the displayed genus. If $m$ is divisible by four, both terms added to $1$ are even integers, so the genus is odd.
