# Local formula for the Chern connection on a line bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Chern connection](chern-connection.md)

Let $e$ be a [holomorphic local frame](holomorphic-local-trivialization.md) of a Hermitian holomorphic line bundle and put $h=h(e,e)$. The [Chern connection](chern-connection.md) and its curvature are locally

$$
\nabla e=(\partial\log h)e,
\qquad
F_\nabla=\bar\partial\partial\log h=-\partial\bar\partial\log h.
$$

Thus $F_\nabla$ has type $(1,1)$. Because the connection is [unitary](unitary-connection.md), $\bar F_\nabla=-F_\nabla$, so $iF_\nabla$ is a [real (1, 1)-form](real-1-1-form.md).
