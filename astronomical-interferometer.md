# Astronomical interferometer

↑ **Parent:** [Interferometer](interferometer.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomical_interferometer)

An [astronomical interferometer](astronomical-interferometer.md) combines observations from separated collecting locations to infer angular source structure. Its projected [interferometric baseline](interferometric-baseline.md) determines the angular spatial frequency sampled. Amplitude and intensity interferometers measure different coherence observables; a [Michelson stellar interferometer](michelson-stellar-interferometer.md) combines optical field amplitudes.
