# Ground-space perturbation bound

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral gap](spectral-gap.md)

Suppose $H\geq0$ has ground energy zero and positive [spectral gap](spectral-gap.md) at least $g$, and $0\leq V\leq I$. Let $\nu$ be the minimum of $V$ restricted to the [ground space](ground-state-subspace.md). Splitting a normalized vector into ground and excited components bounds its energy below by $\delta\nu+(g-\delta)y^2-2\delta y$, with $y$ the excited norm. Completing the square yields the displayed bound for $0<\delta<g$. The error is controlled by the gap and cannot be treated as a uniform $O(\delta^2)$ when $g$ closes.
