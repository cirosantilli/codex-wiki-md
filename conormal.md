# Conormal

↑ **Parent:** [Unit normal](unit-normal.md)

Along the [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md) of an [oriented surface](oriented-surface.md), the outward unit [conormal](conormal.md) lies in the [tangent plane](tangent-plane.md) of the [surface](topological-surface-split.md) and is perpendicular to the [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md) [unit tangent vector](unit-tangent-vector.md). If $\mathbf n$ is the chosen [unit normal vector](unit-normal.md), the positively oriented [unit tangent vector](unit-tangent-vector.md) is $\mathbf n\times\boldsymbol\nu$.
