# Weakly intersecting family in a product alphabet

↑ **Parent:** [Extremal set theory](extremal-set-theory-split.md)

Here two words must both have symbols greater than one in some common coordinate, without requiring the symbols to agree. Their supports $S(x)$ therefore form an [intersecting family](intersecting-family.md). The fibre of a support of size $r$ contains $(k-1)^r$ words, relating this problem to a weighted [set family](set-family.md) problem.
