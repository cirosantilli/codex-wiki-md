<h1 id="su-2-as-the-three-sphere">SU(2) as the three-sphere</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Special unitary group](special-unitary-group.md)

Every element of $SU(2)$ has the unique form

$$
\begin{pmatrix}z_1&-\overline z_2\\z_2&\overline z_1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad |z_1|^2+|z_2|^2=1.
$$

Thus $SU(2)$ is diffeomorphic to the unit three-sphere in $\mathbb C^2$ and is a [parallelizable manifold](parallelizable-manifold.md).
