# Reachability-total layering criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Condensation graph](condensation-graph.md)

A positive-[integer](integer-split.md) labeling with $a\leadsto b\Rightarrow\ell(a)\leq\ell(b)$ and $a\not\leadsto b\Rightarrow\ell(a)>\ell(b)$ exists exactly when the [condensation graph](condensation-graph.md) is totally ordered by reachability. Mutual paths force equal labels, and equal labels force mutual paths. Two incomparable components would require strict inequalities in both directions. Conversely number the components in [topological order](topological-order.md); total comparability forces every earlier component to reach every later one, proving both conditions. Compute the labeling in linear time. Reachability here includes length-zero paths.
