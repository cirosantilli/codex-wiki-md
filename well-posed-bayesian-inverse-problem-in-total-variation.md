# Well-posed Bayesian inverse problem in total variation

↑ **Parent:** [Bayesian inverse problem](bayesian-inverse-problem.md)

A Bayesian inverse problem is well posed in total variation when a unique posterior exists for every datum and the map from data to posterior is continuous in [total variation distance](total-variation-distance.md).
