# Bose integral

↑ **Parent:** [Gamma function](gamma-function.md)

For $\operatorname{Re}s>1$, expansion of $(e^x-1)^{-1}$ as a [geometric series](geometric-series.md) and termwise integration give

$$
\int_0^\infty\frac{x^{s-1}}{e^x-1}\,dx
=\Gamma(s)\zeta(s).
$$
