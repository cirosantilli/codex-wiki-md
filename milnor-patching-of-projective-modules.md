# Milnor patching of projective modules

↑ **Parent:** [Milnor square](milnor-square.md)

A compatible triple $(P_1,P_2,\phi)$ patches to $\{(x_1,x_2):\phi(\bar x_1)=\bar x_2\}$. It is finitely generated projective and recovers $P_i$ after [extension of scalars](extension-of-scalars.md). For [free modules](free-module.md) glued by $g$, the block identity $\operatorname{diag}(g,g^{-1})\in E_\infty(A_0)$ lifts through the surjective side and makes the [direct sum](direct-sum.md) with inverse gluing free. Complements reduce general projective triples to that free case. Conversely an [idempotent](idempotent.md) over $A$ is exactly a compatible pair of [idempotents](idempotent.md) over its two factors.
