# Enone

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical compound](chemical-compound.md)

An [enone](enone.md) has a carbonyl conjugated to a [carbon](carbon.md)–[carbon](carbon.md) double bond, $\mathrm{O{=}C{-}C{=}C}$. Its beta [carbon](carbon.md) can undergo [conjugate addition](conjugate-addition.md).
