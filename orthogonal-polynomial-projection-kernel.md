# Orthogonal polynomial projection kernel

↑ **Parent:** [Finite-rank projection kernel](finite-rank-projection-kernel.md)

For real [monic orthogonal polynomials](monic-orthogonal-polynomial.md) $p_j$ with squared weighted norms $h_j$ under a positive weight $w$, the [functions](function-split.md) $p_j\sqrt{w/h_j}$ form an [orthonormal set](orthonormal-set.md) for the unweighted reference measure. Their [finite-rank projection kernel](finite-rank-projection-kernel.md) turns the squared [weighted Vandermonde determinant](weighted-vandermonde-determinant.md) into a kernel [determinant](determinant.md). Normalization on the full labelled configuration space is $1/m!$.
