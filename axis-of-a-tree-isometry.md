# Axis of a tree isometry

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic isometry of a tree](hyperbolic-isometry-of-a-tree.md)

The axis of a hyperbolic tree isometry $g$ is its unique invariant line. The isometry acts on this line by translation through its translation length.
