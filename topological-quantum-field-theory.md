# Topological quantum field theory

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum field theory](quantum-field-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topological_quantum_field_theory)

A [quantum field theory](quantum-field-theory-split.md) assigning [vector spaces](vector-space-split.md) to closed spatial [manifolds](topological-manifold-split.md) and linear maps to interpolating spacetime [cobordisms](cobordism.md), compatibly with composition and disjoint union. Its [TQFT partition function](topological-quantum-field-theory-partition-function.md) on $\Sigma\times S^1$ is the [trace](matrix-trace.md) of the identity of the state space on $\Sigma$, hence its [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md).
