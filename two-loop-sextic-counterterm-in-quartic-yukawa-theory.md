# Two-loop sextic counterterm in quartic Yukawa theory

↑ **Parent:** [Quartic Yukawa interaction in three spacetime dimensions](quartic-yukawa-interaction-in-three-spacetime-dimensions.md)

Four quartic Yukawa vertices on a [fermion loop](fermion-loop.md) with one internal scalar chord give six external scalar legs, two loops and overall logarithmic degree. The coefficient is nonzero: in a constant scalar background $v$, the induced cubic [Yukawa coupling](yukawa-interaction.md) is $y=gv$ and the fermion mass is $\mu=m+gv^2/2$. The two-loop [sunset diagram](sunset-diagram.md) in the [quantum effective potential](quantum-effective-potential.md) has a logarithmic pole proportional to $y^2(4\mu^2-\mathcal B^2)$, where $\mathcal B^2$ is the background scalar squared mass. Its $v^6$ term is $g^4v^6$ when the tree sextic coupling vanishes. Thus zero renormalized sextic coupling at one scale does not eliminate the sextic [counterterm](counterterm.md) or make that zero stable under [renormalization](renormalization.md).
