# Measurement of Subpicosecond Time Intervals between Two Photons by Interference by C. K. Hong and others

🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by C. K. Hong](work-by-c-k-hong.md), [Work by Leonard Mandel](work-by-leonard-mandel.md), [Work by Z. Y. Ou](work-by-z-y-ou.md)

[https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.59.2044](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.59.2044)

Primary-paper scan hosted by Caltech: [https://painterlab.caltech.edu/wp-content/uploads/2019/06/iqd_measurement_subpicosecond_time_two_photons.pdf](https://painterlab.caltech.edu/wp-content/uploads/2019/06/iqd_measurement_subpicosecond_time_two_photons.pdf)

Physical Review Letters 59, 2044–2046. The experiment combines down-converted [photon](photon.md) pairs at a [beam splitter](beam-splitter.md), scans relative path delay and records coincidences after accounting for accidental background; the ideal balanced, indistinguishable limit suppresses the coincidence output.
