# Geometric syntactic topology

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric syntactic category](geometric-syntactic-category.md)

A family of definable arrows covers a formula when the theory proves that their images jointly exhaust that formula. Such covers encode disjunction and existential quantification. The resulting [site](site-category-theory.md) presents the [classifying topos](classifying-topos.md) of the [geometric theory](geometric-theory.md). Adding geometric axioms adds covering sieves, yielding the [duality between geometric quotients and subtoposes](duality-between-geometric-quotients-and-subtoposes.md).
