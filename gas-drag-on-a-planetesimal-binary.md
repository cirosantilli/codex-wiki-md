# Gas drag on a planetesimal binary

↑ **Parent:** [Binary planetesimal](binary-planetesimal.md)

For linear [gas drag](gas-drag.md) with [aerodynamic stopping times](aerodynamic-stopping-time.md) $t_1,t_2$, masses $m_1,m_2$, total mass $m=m_1+m_2$, and relative velocity $\mathbf v$, define

$$
\kappa=\frac{m_1/t_2+m_2/t_1}{m},\qquad
\kappa_b=\frac{m_1/t_1+m_2/t_2}{m},\qquad
\Delta=\frac1{t_2}-\frac1{t_1}.
$$

If $\mathbf w$ is the gas velocity relative to the [centre of mass](center-of-mass.md), the relative drag acceleration is $-\kappa\mathbf v+\Delta\mathbf w$. The first term dissipates internal [specific orbital energy](specific-orbital-energy.md); the second is a differential headwind that vanishes for equal [aerodynamic stopping times](aerodynamic-stopping-time.md).
