# Self-avoiding polygon

↑ **Parent:** [Self-avoiding walk](self-avoiding-walk.md)

A [self-avoiding polygon](self-avoiding-polygon.md) on a lattice is a closed nearest-neighbour walk whose vertices are distinct except for the final repetition of the starting point. On the square lattice a nondegenerate polygon has even perimeter at least four, since each step changes the parity of the sum of the coordinates. One can generate such a polygon by generating a self-avoiding walk with $M-1$ edges, retaining it only when its endpoint is a neighbour of its origin, and appending the closing edge. The resulting distribution inherits the bias of the walk sampler unless an additional correction is applied.
