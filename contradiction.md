# Contradiction

↑ **Parent:** [Propositional logic](propositional-logic.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contradiction)

A contradiction is an assertion or collection of assumptions from which [logical falsity](logical-falsity.md) follows. Deriving both a statement $P$ and its [logical negation](negation.md) is a standard example. A contradiction under a temporary assumption can establish that assumption's negation; classical [proof by contradiction](reductio-ad-absurdum.md) additionally uses this to establish positive conclusions.
