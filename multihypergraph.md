# Multihypergraph

↑ **Parent:** [Hypergraph](hypergraph-split.md)

A multihypergraph has a [multiset](multiset.md) of hyperedges, allowing the same subset of vertices to appear more than once. Its [hypergraph vertex degrees](hypergraph-vertex-degree.md) and [hypergraph codegrees](hypergraph-codegree.md) count multiplicity. Such multiplicity is important when several original hyperedges produce the same link in a [hypergraph container theorem](hypergraph-container-theorem.md).
