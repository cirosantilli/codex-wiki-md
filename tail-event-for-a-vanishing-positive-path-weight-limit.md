# Tail event for a vanishing positive path-weight limit

↑ **Parent:** [Positive random-environment path-weight martingale](positive-random-environment-path-weight-martingale.md)

Delete the first $m$ environment factors from the path-weight expectation and call the result $Z_T^{(m)}$. Pointwise, $(1-\varepsilon)^mZ_T^{(m)}\le Z_T\le(1+\varepsilon)^mZ_T^{(m)}$. Thus $\{\liminf_TZ_T=0\}$ equals $\{\liminf_TZ_T^{(m)}=0\}$ and belongs to every future-time environment sigma-algebra. The [Kolmogorov zero-one law](kolmogorov-s-zero-one-law.md) makes its probability zero or one. If the nonnegative [martingale](martingale-split.md) limit has [mean](expected-value.md) one, the probability cannot be one, so its limit is strictly positive almost surely. The pointwise comparison establishes a genuine [tail event](tail-event.md), rather than merely invariance outside an unspecified exceptional set.
