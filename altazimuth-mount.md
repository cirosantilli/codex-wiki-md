# Altazimuth mount

↑ **Parent:** [Optical telescope](optical-telescope.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Altazimuth_mount)

An [altazimuth mount](altazimuth-mount.md) points an [optical telescope](optical-telescope.md) using [altitude](altitude.md) and [azimuth](azimuth.md) rotations. A mount restricted to elevations no greater than the [zenith](zenith.md) must change its [azimuth](azimuth.md) by approximately half a turn when a target crosses the [zenith](zenith.md).
