# Base change of a pullback square

↑ **Parent:** [Pullback (category theory)](pullback-category-theory.md)

Given a [pullback in a category](pullback-category-theory.md) $A=B\times_D C$ and a [morphism](morphism.md) $D'\to D$, assume the indicated [pullbacks in a category](pullback-category-theory.md) exist. The four base-changed objects form a [pullback in a category](pullback-category-theory.md). A compatible pair of [morphisms](morphism.md) into $B\times_D D'$ and $C\times_D D'$ gives [morphisms](morphism.md) into $B,C$ and a common [morphism](morphism.md) into $D'$. The first two [morphisms](morphism.md) factor uniquely through $A$, and their common image in $D$ permits unique factorization through $A\times_D D'$. This proves the displayed canonical [isomorphism](isomorphism.md) without an assumption that the whole [category](category-split.md) is complete.
