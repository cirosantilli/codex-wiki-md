# Figure-eight knot

↑ **Parent:** [Knot](knot.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Figure-eight_knot)

The figure-eight knot is the unique knot of [crossing number of a knot](crossing-number-of-a-knot.md) four. It is prime, alternating, and has [Alexander polynomial of a knot](alexander-polynomial.md) $-t+3-t^{-1}$ up to a unit.
