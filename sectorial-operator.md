# Sectorial operator

↑ **Parent:** [Closed linear operator](closed-linear-operator.md)

A densely defined [closed operator](closed-linear-operator.md) is sectorial of angle $\theta$ if its [spectrum](spectrum-functional-analysis.md) lies in $\{z:|\arg z|\leq\theta\}\cup\{0\}$ and, for every larger angle $\phi<\pi$, its [resolvent](resolvent-of-an-operator.md) outside that larger sector satisfies $\|(z-A)^{-1}\|\leq C_\phi/|z|$. If $\theta<\pi/2$, then $-A$ generates an [analytic semigroup](analytic-semigroup.md). A real shift of the generator allows exponential growth without changing the short-time smoothing estimates.
