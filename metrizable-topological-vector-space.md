# Metrizable topological vector space

↑ **Parent:** [Topological vector space](topological-vector-space-split.md)

A topological vector space is metrizable when its topology is induced by a [metric](metric.md). At the origin it then has a countable neighborhood basis, so continuity of a [linear map](linear-map.md) can be tested on sequences.
