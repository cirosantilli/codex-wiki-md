# Analytic germ projection

↑ **Parent:** [Space of germs of holomorphic functions](space-of-germs-of-holomorphic-functions.md)

The projection $[f]_z\mapsto z$ is a local [biholomorphism](biholomorphism.md) for the sheet topology on the [space of germs of holomorphic functions](space-of-germs-of-holomorphic-functions.md). It need not be a topological [covering map](covering-space.md): branches may end at singularities even when another branch remains over that base point.
