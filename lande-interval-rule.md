<h1 id="lande-interval-rule">Landé interval rule</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Addition of angular momentum](addition-of-angular-momentum.md)

For dimensionless [angular momentum operators](angular-momentum-operator-split.md) and a [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) $H=a\mathbf I\cdot\mathbf J$ at fixed $I,J$, [addition of angular momentum](addition-of-angular-momentum.md) gives $E_F=a[F(F+1)-I(I+1)-J(J+1)]/2$. Subtracting neighbouring [energy levels](energy-level.md) proves the displayed rule. For [fine structure](fine-structure.md) substitute $\mathbf L,\mathbf S$ and total $J$; for magnetic-dipole [hyperfine structure](hyperfine-structure.md) use electronic $J$, [nuclear spin](nuclear-spin.md) $I$ and total $F$. Electric-quadrupole interactions and mixing with nearby [energy levels](energy-level.md) can violate the rule.
