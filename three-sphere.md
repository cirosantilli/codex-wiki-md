# Three-sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Sphere](sphere.md)

The three-sphere is the unit sphere in four-dimensional [Euclidean space](euclidean-norm.md):

$$
S^3=\{x\in\mathbb R^4:\lVert x\rVert=1\}.
$$

Identifying $\mathbb R^4$ with $\mathbb C^2$ realizes it as $\{(z_1,z_2):|z_1|^2+|z_2|^2=1\}$.
