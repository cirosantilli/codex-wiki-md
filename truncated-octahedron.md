# Truncated octahedron

↑ **Parent:** [Polyhedron](polyhedron.md)

The Archimedean solid obtained by truncating an [octahedron](octahedron.md), with eight hexagonal faces and six square faces. In an fcc nanocrystal the analogous shape can expose both $\{111\}$ and $\{100\}$ [facets](facet.md); the relative [facet](facet.md) areas need not equal those of the regular Archimedean solid.
