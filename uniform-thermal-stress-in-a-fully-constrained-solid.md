# Uniform thermal stress in a fully constrained solid

↑ **Parent:** [Thermoelasticity](thermoelasticity.md)

For isotropic [linear elasticity](linear-elasticity.md) with volumetric expansion coefficient $\alpha$, the [stress](stress.md)-free [thermal strain](thermal-strain.md) is $e^{\rm th}=\alpha(T-T_0)I/3$. Thus the [thermal stress](thermal-stress.md) term is $-K\alpha(T-T_0)I$, where $K=\lambda+2\mu/3$ is the [bulk modulus](bulk-modulus.md). A uniformly heated body with its full boundary fixed has zero [displacement](displacement.md) and the displayed compressive [stress](stress.md). For spatially varying temperature and zero [body force](body-force.md), the [displacement](displacement.md) instead solves $\mu\Delta u+(\lambda+\mu)\nabla\operatorname{div}u=K\alpha\nabla T$ with its boundary values; the uniform formula is not automatic. A [body force](body-force.md) per volume $+K\alpha\nabla T$ cancels this thermal forcing in the [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) equation, but does not by itself cancel the [stress](stress.md) or its boundary tractions.
