# Pythagorean theorem in an inner-product space

↑ **Parent:** [Inner product](inner-product.md)

If $u$ and $v$ are orthogonal, then

$$
\lVert u+v\rVert^2=\lVert u\rVert^2+\lVert v\rVert^2.
$$
