# Sharp three-transitivity

↑ **Parent:** [Sharp t-transitivity](sharp-t-transitivity.md)

Exactly one element sends each ordered triple of distinct points to each other such triple. On a [projective line](projective-line.md), representatives of the first two target points form a [basis](basis.md); scaling them so their sum represents the third point gives the unique projective transformation.
