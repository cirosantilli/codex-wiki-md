# Weak-decoupling temperature estimate

↑ **Parent:** [Neutrino decoupling](neutrino-decoupling.md)

In [natural units](natural-units.md) with unreduced [Planck mass](planck-mass.md) $M_P=G^{-1/2}$, the radiation-era [Hubble parameter](hubble-parameter.md) scales as $\sqrt{g_*}T^2/M_P$, while a low-energy [weak interaction](weak-interaction.md) rate scales as $G_F^2T^5$. Their equality gives a decoupling scale of order one MeV. Distinct weak processes have distinct rate coefficients: [neutrino decoupling](neutrino-decoupling.md) and [cosmological weak freeze-out](cosmological-weak-freeze-out.md) of neutron-proton conversion are nearby events, not an identical precise temperature inferred from the power laws alone.
