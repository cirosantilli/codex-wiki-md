# Discrete Green convolution for the period-six recurrence

↑ **Parent:** [Inhomogeneous linear recurrence](inhomogeneous-linear-recurrence.md)

The unit-response sequence $a_1=a_2=1$, $a_{n+2}-a_{n+1}+a_n=0$ is six-periodic. In the displayed [convolution](convolution.md), all older terms cancel under $P_{k+2}-P_{k+1}+P_k$ by this recurrence; the two new boundary terms reduce to $f_{k+2}$. Thus it gives a particular solution of the [inhomogeneous linear recurrence](inhomogeneous-linear-recurrence.md) with forcing $f_{k+2}$. Add the two-dimensional homogeneous span of $\cos(k\pi/3)$ and $\sin(k\pi/3)$ for the general solution. This is a discrete [Green function](green-s-function.md) construction.
