# Additive energy produces a large subspace in a fourfold difference set

↑ **Parent:** [Bogolyubov lemma](bogolyubov-lemma.md)

For fixed $c>0$ and prime $p$, if $A\subseteq\mathbb F_p^N$ has at least $c|A|^3$ [additive quadruples](additive-quadruple.md), then $2A-2A$ contains a vector subspace of size at least $c'(c,p)|A|$. Combine the [Balog-Szemerédi-Gowers theorem](balog-szemeredi-gowers-theorem.md), the [Freiman-Ruzsa theorem over a finite field](freiman-ruzsa-theorem-over-a-finite-field.md), and the [Finite-field Bogolyubov lemma](finite-field-bogolyubov-lemma.md).
