# Harmonic-trap energy-zero convention

↑ **Parent:** [Bose condensation in a three-dimensional harmonic trap](bose-condensation-in-a-three-dimensional-harmonic-trap.md)

The exact three-dimensional oscillator ground [energy](energy-split.md) is $3\hbar\omega/2$ above the potential minimum. Subtracting it sets the condensate [energy](energy-split.md) to zero. The commonly used $g(\epsilon)=\epsilon^2/[2(\hbar\omega)^3]$ is the leading continuum term; it does not include the finite-level corrections from shifting the [energy](energy-split.md) origin.
