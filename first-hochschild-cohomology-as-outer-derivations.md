# First Hochschild cohomology as outer derivations

↑ **Parent:** [Inner derivation](inner-derivation.md)

The degree-one Hochschild cocycles are exactly the maps that are a [derivation into a bimodule](derivation-into-a-bimodule.md), while degree-one coboundaries are exactly the [inner derivations](inner-derivation.md). Therefore

$$
HH^1(R,M)\cong\operatorname{Der}_k(R,M)/\operatorname{Inn}(R,M).
$$
