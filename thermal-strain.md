# Thermal strain

↑ **Parent:** [Thermoelasticity](thermoelasticity.md)

[Thermal strain](thermal-strain.md) is the [strain](strain.md) that heating would produce without mechanical [stress](stress.md). In isotropic linear thermoelasticity it is a pure dilation: a volumetric expansion coefficient $\alpha_v$ corresponds to a linear expansion coefficient $\alpha_v/3$. The constitutive law acts on the mechanical [strain](strain.md) $e-e^{\rm th}$. Spatially varying [thermal strain](thermal-strain.md) need not be globally compatible with a [stress](stress.md)-free [displacement](displacement.md).
