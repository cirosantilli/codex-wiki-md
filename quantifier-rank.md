# Quantifier rank

↑ **Parent:** [First-order formula](first-order-formula.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantifier_rank)

The quantifier rank of a [first-order formula](first-order-formula.md) is the maximum nesting depth of quantifiers. Atomic formulas have rank zero; negation preserves rank, binary Boolean connectives take the maximum, and either quantifier increases rank by one. It is not the total number of quantifiers.
