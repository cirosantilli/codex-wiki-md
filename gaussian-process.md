# Gaussian process

↑ **Parent:** [Stochastic process](stochastic-process-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaussian_process)

A Gaussian process is a [stochastic process](stochastic-process-split.md) whose every finite vector of values has a [multivariate normal distribution](multivariate-normal-distribution.md). Its law is determined by its mean and covariance functions.
