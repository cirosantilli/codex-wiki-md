# Spinor Lorentz generator commutator

↑ **Parent:** [Spinor representation of the Lorentz group](spinor-representation-of-the-lorentz-group.md)

Define $\sigma^{\mu\nu}=\frac i2[\gamma^\mu,\gamma^\nu]$ using the [gamma matrix](gamma-matrices.md) [Clifford algebra](clifford-algebra.md). Moving $\gamma^\rho$ through the two factors with $\{\gamma^\mu,\gamma^\nu\}=2g^{\mu\nu}$ proves the displayed [operator commutator](operator-commutator.md). For real antisymmetric $\omega_{\mu\nu}$ and $S=1-\frac i4\sigma^{\mu\nu}\omega_{\mu\nu}$, it gives $S^{-1}\gamma^\rho S=\gamma^\rho+\omega^\rho{}_{\nu}\gamma^\nu+O(\omega^2)$. This is the infinitesimal [Lorentz covariance](lorentz-covariance.md) identity required by the [Dirac equation](dirac-equation.md).
