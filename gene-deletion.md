# Gene deletion

↑ **Parent:** [Mutation](mutation.md)

A [gene deletion](gene-deletion.md) removes a chosen [gene](gene.md) sequence from a [genome](genome.md). An experimental [gene deletion](gene-deletion.md) tests the consequences of that removal in a specified [genetic background](genetic-background.md) and environment; lack of an observed [phenotype](phenotype.md) does not prove that the [gene](gene.md) has no function under other conditions.
