# Scalar-QED four-point vertex pole in transverse gauge

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar quantum electrodynamics Ward identity](scalar-quantum-electrodynamics-ward-identity.md)

In $D=4-\epsilon$, the two surviving zero-momentum seagull bubbles contribute $-4e_D^4\int_lP^{\mu\nu}(l)/[l^2(l^2-m^2)]+(\mu\leftrightarrow\nu)$. Tensor averaging gives $(D-1)/D$, and the scalar integral has pole $i/(8\pi^2\epsilon)$. Cancellation against $2ie_D^2(Z_4-1)g^{\mu\nu}$ gives the displayed result. A positive scalar [mass](mass.md) separates the ultraviolet pole from the scaleless massless limit. The [scalar QED Ward identity](scalar-quantum-electrodynamics-ward-identity.md) gives $Z_4=Z_2$ in this convention.
