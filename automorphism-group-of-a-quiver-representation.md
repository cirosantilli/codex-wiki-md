# Automorphism group of a quiver representation

↑ **Parent:** [Representation of a quiver](representation-of-a-quiver.md)

This is $\operatorname{End}_Q(X)^\times$, a connected [linear algebraic group](linear-algebraic-group.md) by the [units of a finite-dimensional algebra](units-of-a-finite-dimensional-algebra.md) argument. It is the stabilizer under base change. For $X=\bigoplus_aM_a^{\oplus m_a}$, reduction modulo the radical gives quotient $\prod_a\operatorname{GL}_{m_a}(k)$.
