# Antigen

↑ **Parent:** [Antibody](antibody.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antigen)

In [antibody](antibody.md) binding, an [antigen](antigen.md) is material bearing a recognized [epitope](epitope.md). An [antigen](antigen.md) need not be a [protein](protein.md); [polysaccharides](polysaccharide.md) can also carry [epitopes](epitope.md). One [antigen](antigen.md) may present several different [epitopes](epitope.md), so recognizing one feature does not imply recognizing every part of the target.
