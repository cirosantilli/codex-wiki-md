# Fiber of a function

↑ **Parent:** [Function](function-split.md)

For a [function](function-split.md) $f:X\to Y$ and $y\in Y$, its fiber is the [set](set-split.md) $f^{-1}(y)=\{x\in X:f(x)=y\}$. Fibers partition the domain after empty fibers are omitted. A function is [injective](injective-function.md) exactly when every fiber has at most one element. Quantum [hidden subgroup problem](hidden-subgroup-problem.md) algorithms use functions whose fibers are [cosets](coset.md).
