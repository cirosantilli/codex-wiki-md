# Essential submodule

↑ **Parent:** [Submodule](submodule.md)

A [submodule](submodule.md) $N\subseteq M$ is an [essential submodule](essential-submodule.md) if it intersects every nonzero [submodule](submodule.md) of $M$ nontrivially. A nonzero [module](module-mathematics.md) is a [uniform module](uniform-module.md) precisely when every nonzero [submodule](submodule.md) is an [essential submodule](essential-submodule.md).
