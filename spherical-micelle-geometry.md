# Spherical micelle geometry

↑ **Parent:** [Micelle](micelle.md)

In a sharp-core spherical [micelle](micelle.md) of aggregation number $n$, incompressible tail [volume](volume.md) $v$ per molecule and effective head-group [area](surface-area.md) $a_0$ give $nv=4\pi R^3/3$ and $na_0=4\pi R^2$. Division gives the displayed radius and aggregation number. If the maximum tail extension is $l_c$, the model requires $R\leq l_c$, equivalently the [amphiphile packing parameter](amphiphile-packing-parameter.md) $v/(a_0l_c)\leq1/3$. Input dimensions violating that inequality cannot describe an exact spherical filled core without modifying the molecular geometry assumptions.
