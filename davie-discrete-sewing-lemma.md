# Davie discrete sewing lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Rough differential equation](rough-differential-equation.md)

For a finite time grid, suppose a nonnegative error is zero on neighboring points and obeys $a_{s,t}\leq a_{s,u}+a_{u,t}+L\omega(s,t)^{1/p}a_{s,u}+A\omega(s,t)^\theta$. For a [control function for rough paths](control-function-for-rough-paths.md) and $\theta>1$, sufficiently small control intervals have the displayed bound with constants independent of the grid. Splitting near half the control size makes the higher-order errors contract, and handles a crossing single grid interval by its zero error. Applying this to local Euler composition errors yields compactness of approximate pathlevel solutions.
