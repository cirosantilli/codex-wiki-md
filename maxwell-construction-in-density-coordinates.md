# Maxwell construction in density coordinates

↑ **Parent:** [Maxwell construction](maxwell-construction.md)

The equal-area condition is naturally $\int(p-p_{\rm coex})\,dv=0$ in volume per particle $v$. In number density $n=1/v$, it becomes $\int_{n_g}^{n_l}[p(n)-p_{\rm coex}]\,dn/n^2=0$. Unweighted area in a [pressure](pressure-split.md)-versus-density plot is not the [Maxwell construction](maxwell-construction.md).
