# Zeeman shift

↑ **Parent:** [Zeeman splitting](zeeman-splitting.md)

The change of an [energy level](energy-level.md) in a [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md). The displayed weak-field form applies to a [hyperfine level](hyperfine-level.md) with magnetic projection $m_F$, when $g_F$ is the corresponding [Landé g-factor](lande-g-factor.md). A transition shift is the difference of the two level shifts divided by [Planck constant](planck-constant.md). A [clock transition](clock-transition.md) can have zero first-order transition shift even when its two individual level slopes are nonzero. For $m_F=0$ [hyperfine levels](hyperfine-level.md), the linear level shift vanishes and quadratic terms remain.
