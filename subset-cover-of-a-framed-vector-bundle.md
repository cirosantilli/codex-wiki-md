# Subset cover of a framed vector bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Unordered Stiefel bundle](unordered-stiefel-bundle.md)

A global ordered framing selects the displayed $\binom nk$-sheeted cover inside the [unordered Stiefel bundle](unordered-stiefel-bundle.md). It is a trivial cover because the frame labels are global. Selecting subsets of a direct-sum framing decomposes the cover as the disjoint union of fiber products of the two summands' subset covers.
