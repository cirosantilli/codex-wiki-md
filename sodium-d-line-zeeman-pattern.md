# Sodium D-line Zeeman pattern

↑ **Parent:** [Anomalous Zeeman effect](anomalous-zeeman-effect.md)

Neglect [hyperfine structure](hyperfine-structure.md) and use a [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) weak compared with the [spin-orbit interaction](spin-orbit-interaction.md). The [sodium](sodium.md) D doublet has transitions $3p\,{}^2P_{1/2}\to3s\,{}^2S_{1/2}$ and $3p\,{}^2P_{3/2}\to3s\,{}^2S_{1/2}$. Their [Landé g-factors](lande-g-factor.md) are $2/3$, $4/3$ and $2$ respectively. With $m_u,m_l$ denoting upper and lower magnetic labels, the photon shift is

$$
\delta E_\gamma=\mu_BB(g_um_u-2m_l),\qquad m_u-m_l=0,\pm1.
$$

The first transition has four allowed shifts $\mu_BB\{\pm2/3,\pm4/3\}$; the second has six, $\mu_BB\{\pm1/3,\pm1,\pm5/3\}$. The most blue-shifted component of the second is $m_u=1/2\to m_l=-1/2$, with shift $5\mu_BB/3$. The apparently larger shift $m_u=3/2\to m_l=-1/2$ is excluded by the [electric-dipole selection rule](electric-dipole-selection-rule.md). The $3p\,{}^2P_{3/2}$ level lies above $3p\,{}^2P_{1/2}$, so in this weak-field model the $5/3$ component also has the shortest wavelength of the whole doublet. [NIST, Energy Levels of Neutral Sodium, ordering of the two 3p fine-structure levels](https://physics.nist.gov/PhysRefData/Handbook/Tables/sodiumtable5.htm)
