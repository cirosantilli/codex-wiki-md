# Distinct-positive-integer subset-sum lower bound

↑ **Parent:** [Sumset](sumset.md)

For $0<s_1<\cdots<s_n$, adding the largest integer creates at least $n$ new [sums of subsets](sum-of-a-subset.md). If $P=\sum_{i<n}s_i$ is the previous maximum, the values $P+s_n$ and $P+s_n-s_i$ for $i<n$ are distinct new sums above $P$. Induction gives the displayed bound. Equality holds for $s_i=i$, whose subset sums fill the interval of integers from zero to $n(n+1)/2$.
