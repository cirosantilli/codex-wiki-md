# Foundations of mathematics

↑ **Parent:** [Area of mathematics](area-of-mathematics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foundations_of_mathematics)

Foundations of mathematics asks what our objects mean, what we assume about them, and why an argument establishes its conclusion. [Set theory](set-theory-split.md) gives a common language for collections and [functions](function-split.md); [mathematical logic](mathematical-logic-split.md) makes the structure of statements and deductions precise. A [mathematical definition](mathematical-definition.md) fixes a meaning, an [axiom](axiom.md) supplies an assumption, and a [mathematical proof](mathematical-proof.md) explains what follows. Separating these roles makes results reusable: once the [group](group-split.md) axioms are enough for a conclusion, that conclusion applies to every [group](group-split.md), regardless of how its members are represented.

The assumptions can change the answer to a familiar calculation. Over the [real numbers](real-number.md), $x^2=x$ factors as $x(x-1)=0$, so $x$ is zero or one. The same reasoning holds in any [integral domain](integral-domain.md), because a product can be zero only when a factor is zero. In the [ring](ring.md) of [integers](integer-split.md) modulo six, however, $3^2\equiv3$ and $4^2\equiv4\pmod6$: there are four solutions, represented by $0,1,3,4$. The extra solutions are [idempotents](idempotent.md), and the failed step involves [zero divisors](zero-divisor.md). Writing the domain is part of explaining the answer.

This precision also reveals surprising possibilities. A two-dimensional grid of [natural numbers](natural-number.md) is a [countable set](countable-set.md), while all infinite [binary sequences](bitstream.md) form an [uncountable set](uncountable-set.md). The [Cantor pairing function](cantor-pairing-function.md) supplies a complete enumeration of the first; [Cantor's diagonal argument](cantor-s-diagonal-argument.md) defeats every proposed enumeration of the second. Both arguments are short, yet distinguish two sizes of infinity. The subject's appeal is this ability to turn a vague question into one whose assumptions, construction, and limits can all be inspected.
