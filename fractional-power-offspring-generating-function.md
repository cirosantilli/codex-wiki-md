# Fractional-power offspring generating function

↑ **Parent:** [Offspring distribution of a branching process](offspring-distribution-of-a-branching-process.md)

For $0<\alpha,\beta<1$, this [probability generating function](probability-generating-function.md) has probabilities $p_0=1-\alpha$ and $p_k=\alpha\beta(1-\beta)\cdots(k-1-\beta)/k!$ for $k\ge1$, where the product after $\beta$ is empty for $k=1$. All probabilities are positive and sum to one, while the [expected value](expected-value.md) is infinite. In a [Galton-Watson process](galton-watson-process.md) starting with one individual, the generation-$n$ [probability generating function](probability-generating-function.md) is $G_n(s)=1-\alpha^{(1-\beta^n)/(1-\beta)}(1-s)^{\beta^n}$. The [branching extinction probability](extinction-probability-of-a-branching-process.md) is $1-\alpha^{1/(1-\beta)}$, obtained by taking the limit of $G_n(0)$ and using that extinction by generation $n$ is an increasing sequence of [events](event.md).
