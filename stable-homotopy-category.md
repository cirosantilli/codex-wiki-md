# Stable homotopy category

↑ **Parent:** [Stable homotopy theory](stable-homotopy-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stable_homotopy_category)

The [stable homotopy category](stable-homotopy-category.md) is obtained from [topological spectra](spectrum-topology.md) by inverting [stable equivalences of spectra](stable-equivalence-of-spectra.md). Its morphisms are abelian groups; for a cellular source and fibrant target they are [spectrum homotopy](spectrum-homotopy.md) classes of point-set [maps of topological spectra](map-of-topological-spectra.md).
