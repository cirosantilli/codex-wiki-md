# Regular perturbation of a simple algebraic root

↑ **Parent:** [Dominant balance for algebraic roots](dominant-balance-for-algebraic-roots.md)

If $P(\lambda_0,0)=0$ and $P_\lambda(\lambda_0,0)\ne0$, the [implicit function theorem](implicit-function-theorem.md) gives a local root branch. For analytic coefficients it has an ordinary power series in the perturbation parameter. Its corrections follow by substituting that series into the polynomial and comparing coefficients. A vanishing leading root may require its first nonzero correction to capture a slow exponential mode.
