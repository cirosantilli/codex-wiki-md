# Missing-data mechanism

↑ **Parent:** [Missing data](missing-data.md)

The mechanism assigns probabilities to observation patterns $R$ conditional on complete measurements $D$: $p(R\mid D)$. Whether it depends on missing values distinguishes [missing completely at random](missing-completely-at-random.md), [missing at random](missing-at-random.md) and [missing not at random](missing-not-at-random.md).
