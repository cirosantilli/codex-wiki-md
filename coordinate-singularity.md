# Coordinate singularity

↑ **Parent:** [General relativity](general-relativity-split.md)

A [coordinate singularity](coordinate-singularity.md) is a failure of a coordinate description which disappears in a regular chart. The horizon singularity of the [Schwarzschild metric](schwarzschild-spacetime.md) in $(t,r)$ coordinates is removable; its $r=0$ [curvature singularity](curvature-singularity.md) is not.
