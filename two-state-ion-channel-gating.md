# Two-state ion-channel gating

↑ **Parent:** [Voltage-gated ion channel](voltage-gated-ion-channel.md)

For a gate that switches independently between closed and open states, its open probability obeys the displayed balance equation. At fixed voltage, $X_\infty=\alpha_X/(\alpha_X+\beta_X)$ and $\tau_X=1/(\alpha_X+\beta_X)$. A channel requiring several independent permissive gates to be open has an open probability equal to their product. In the [Hodgkin-Huxley model](hodgkin-huxley-model.md), sodium and potassium conductances are $\bar g_{\rm Na}m^3h$ and $\bar g_{\rm K}n^4$ respectively.
