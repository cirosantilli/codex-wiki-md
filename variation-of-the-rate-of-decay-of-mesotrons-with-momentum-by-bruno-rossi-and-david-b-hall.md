# Variation of the Rate of Decay of Mesotrons with Momentum by Bruno Rossi and David B. Hall

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by Bruno Benedetto Rossi](work-by-bruno-benedetto-rossi.md), [Work by David B. Hall](work-by-david-b-hall.md)

[Bruno Rossi](bruno-benedetto-rossi.md) and [David B. Hall](david-b-hall.md), Physical Review 59, 223–228 (1941). The primary experiment compared more and less penetrating cosmic-ray particle populations and found momentum-dependent decay rates compatible with relativistic moving-clock rates.
