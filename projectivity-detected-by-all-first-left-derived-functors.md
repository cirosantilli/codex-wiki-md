# Projectivity detected by all first left derived functors

↑ **Parent:** [Projective object](projective-object.md)

In an [abelian category](abelian-category.md) with enough projectives, a projective object has a resolution concentrated in degree zero, so every first [left derived functor](left-derived-functor.md) vanishes on it. Conversely choose $0\to K\to P\to A\to0$ with $P$ projective and use the additive [functor](functor.md) $F=\mathcal A(-,K)$ with target the opposite of the [category](category-split.md) of [abelian groups](abelian-group.md). This [functor](functor.md) is [right exact](right-exact-additive-functor.md) in that target. If $L_1F(A)=0$, the derived exact sequence makes $F(K)\to F(P)$ monic, meaning that $\operatorname{Hom}(P,K)\to\operatorname{Hom}(K,K)$ is surjective in [abelian groups](abelian-group.md). Extending $1_K$ gives a retraction of $K\to P$, so the sequence splits and $A$ is a [direct summand](direct-summand.md) of the projective $P$.
