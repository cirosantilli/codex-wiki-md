# Positive-pivot Givens QR factorization of an orthogonal matrix

↑ **Parent:** [QR decomposition by Givens rotations](qr-decomposition-by-givens-rotations.md)

Use determinant-one [Givens rotations](givens-rotation.md) and choose every nonzero eliminated pair to leave a positive pivot. If the input square [matrix](matrix.md) $A$ is orthogonal, its resulting [upper triangular](upper-triangular-matrix.md) factor $R$ is orthogonal too, and hence diagonal with entries of absolute value one. Positivity fixes the first $n-1$ entries to one, while [determinant](determinant.md) preservation fixes the last to $\det A$. A negative last entry is unavoidable when the input reverses orientation.
