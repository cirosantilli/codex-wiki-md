# Affine iteration

↑ **Parent:** [Fixed-point iteration](fixed-point-iteration.md)

Iterating an [affine map](affine-map.md) gives $x_k=M^{k-1}x_1+\sum_{j=0}^{k-2}M^jc$. A [fixed point](fixed-point.md) exists exactly when $c$ is in the [image of a linear map](image-of-a-linear-map.md) $I-M$. If the [spectral radius](spectral-radius.md) of $M$ is below one, its [matrix powers](matrix-power.md) tend to zero and every orbit converges to the unique [fixed point](fixed-point.md) $(I-M)^{-1}c$.
