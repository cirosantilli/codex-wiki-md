# Specific internal energy

↑ **Parent:** [Internal energy](internal-energy.md)

Specific [internal energy](internal-energy.md) is [internal energy](internal-energy.md) per unit mass. For a [perfect gas](ideal-gas.md) with constant [specific-heat ratio](heat-capacity-ratio.md), $e=p/[(\gamma-1)\rho]$. Its material balance is $\rho De/Dt=-p\nabla\cdot\mathbf u+Q$, where $Q$ is [heat](heat.md) supplied per unit volume per unit time.
