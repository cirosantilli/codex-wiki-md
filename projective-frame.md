# Projective frame

↑ **Parent:** [Projective space](projective-space-split.md)

An ordered projective frame consists of $n+2$ [projective points](projective-point.md) such that any $n+1$ representative vectors are linearly independent. After mapping the first $n+1$ points to the coordinate axes, the last point has every coordinate nonzero; rescaling those axes sends it to $[1:\cdots:1]$. This proves existence and uniqueness of the [projective transformation](projective-linear-transformation.md) carrying one ordered frame to another. In the [projective plane](projective-plane.md) a frame consists of four points, no three collinear.
