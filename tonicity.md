# Tonicity

↑ **Parent:** [Osmosis](osmosis.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tonicity)

[Tonicity](tonicity.md) describes how a surrounding [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md) changes cellular [volume](volume.md) through [osmosis](osmosis.md). It depends on which [solutes](solute.md) remain effectively excluded on the timescale of interest, not simply on the amount of dissolved material. The following comparisons take a functional [cell membrane](cell-membrane-split.md), an initially unpressurized [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md), and effectively excluded [solutes](solute.md) as the reference; rising [turgor pressure](turgor-pressure.md) can oppose entry in a walled [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md).

Operationally, [hypotonic solutions](hypotonic-solution.md) cause sustained swelling, [hypertonic solutions](hypertonic-solution.md) sustained shrinking, and [isotonic solutions](isotonic-solution.md) no sustained [volume](volume.md) change in the specified passive comparison. State the reference [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md), membrane properties and observation interval. A [permeating solute](permeating-solute.md) can transiently change [water potential](water-potential.md) while crossing; after its [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) equilibrates, its initial unequal [concentrations](concentration.md) cannot be treated as a permanently retained osmotic load. Thus equal [osmolarities](osmotic-concentration.md) need not imply [isotonic solutions](isotonic-solution.md). Conversely, a stationary walled [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md) may have unequal [osmolarities](osmotic-concentration.md) balanced by [turgor pressure](turgor-pressure.md).[Cell Membranes—Structure and Transport, Osmosis: paragraphs beginning Here is another way and final Note; permeating solutes and transient effects](osmosis-cell-membranes-structure-and-transport-byu-idaho.md)
