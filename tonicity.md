# Tonicity

↑ **Parent:** [Osmosis](osmosis.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tonicity)

[Tonicity](tonicity.md) describes how a surrounding [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md) changes cellular [volume](volume.md) through [osmosis](osmosis.md). It depends on which [solutes](solute.md) remain effectively excluded on the timescale of interest, not simply on the amount of dissolved material. The following comparisons take a functional [cell membrane](cell-membrane.md), an initially unpressurized [biological cell](biological-cell.md), and effectively excluded [solutes](solute.md) as the reference; rising [turgor pressure](turgor-pressure.md) can oppose entry in a walled [biological cell](biological-cell.md).
