# First Chern class formula for a symplectic blowup

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic blowup](symplectic-blowup.md)

For a [symplectic blowup](symplectic-blowup.md) of a $2n$-dimensional [symplectic manifold](symplectic-manifold.md), the displayed formula gives its [First Chern class](first-chern-class.md). The exceptional divisor has [normal bundle](normal-bundle.md) $\mathcal O(-1)$; the discrepancy coefficient is $n-1$. In dimension four it is one.
