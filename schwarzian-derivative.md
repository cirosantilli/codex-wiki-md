# Schwarzian derivative

↑ **Parent:** [Prolongation of a vector field](prolongation-of-a-vector-field.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schwarzian_derivative)

For a thrice differentiable function with $u'\ne0$, the Schwarzian derivative is

$$
S[u]=\frac{u'''}{u'}-\frac32\left(\frac{u''}{u'}\right)^2.
$$

It vanishes exactly for fractional linear functions $u(x)=(ax+b)/(cx+d)$ on intervals where the denominator does not vanish. In a [two-dimensional conformal field theory](two-dimensional-conformal-field-theory.md), it is the inhomogeneous term in the finite conformal transformation of a stress-energy tensor with nonzero central charge.
