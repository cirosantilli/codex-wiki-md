# Brownian covariance kernel

↑ **Parent:** [Covariance kernel](covariance-kernel.md)

The covariance kernel of standard [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) on $[0,1]$ is $c(s,t)=\min(s,t)$. Its integral operator has eigenfunctions $\sqrt2\sin((k-\tfrac12)\pi t)$ and eigenvalues $((k-\tfrac12)\pi)^{-2}$.
