# Simultaneity judgment

↑ **Parent:** [Psychophysics](psychophysics.md)

A judgment of whether two events occurred together. Its response curve across onset differences depends on timing noise and acceptance criteria; it need not equal the [psychometric function](psychometric-function.md) of temporal order.
