# Represented cohomology theory

↑ **Parent:** [Generalized cohomology theory](generalized-cohomology-theory.md)

A [topological spectrum](spectrum-topology.md) $E$ represents the reduced [generalized cohomology theory](generalized-cohomology-theory.md) $\widetilde E^q(X)=[\Sigma^\infty X,\Sigma^qE]_{\mathrm{st}}$. For an [Omega-spectrum](omega-spectrum.md) this equals $[X,E_q]_*$, taking $E_q=\Omega^{-q}E_0$ for negative $q$. It is contravariant in $X$.
