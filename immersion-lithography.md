# Immersion lithography

↑ **Parent:** [Photolithography](photolithography.md)

Projection lithography using a liquid between the final lens and wafer to increase [numerical aperture](numerical-aperture.md) through its refractive index, with stringent optical and chemical compatibility requirements.
