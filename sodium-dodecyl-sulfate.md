# Sodium dodecyl sulfate

↑ **Parent:** [Surfactant](surfactant.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sodium_dodecyl_sulfate)

An anionic [surfactant](surfactant.md) with a twelve-carbon [hydrocarbon](hydrocarbon.md) tail and a negatively charged sulfate head group, accompanied by a [sodium](sodium.md) [counterion](counterion.md). This separation of [hydrophobic](hydrophobicity.md) and [hydrophilic](hydrophilicity.md) regions makes it an [amphiphile](amphiphile.md). It forms aqueous [micelles](micelle.md) under suitable concentration and temperature conditions and is used in [sodium dodecyl sulfate polyacrylamide gel electrophoresis](sds-page.md).
