# Eigenvalue counting function

↑ **Parent:** [Compact elliptic spectral theorem](compact-elliptic-spectral-theorem.md)

For a nonnegative [self-adjoint operator](self-adjoint-operator.md) with [compact resolvent](compact-resolvent.md), this function counts [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) up to a given level with their multiplicities. For the [positive Laplace-Beltrami operator](positive-laplace-beltrami-operator.md) on a closed $n$-dimensional [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md), $N(\Lambda)\leq C(1+\Lambda)^{n/2}$. To prove this bound, multiply each vector in the low-eigenvalue subspace by a finite set of chart cutoffs and record its local [Fourier coefficients](fourier-coefficient.md) of frequency at most $R$. The total number of retained coefficients is $O(R^n)$, while the sum of discarded squared norms is at most $CR^{-2}(1+\Lambda)\|u\|_2^2$ by [Parseval identity](parseval-identity.md) and the energy bound. For $R$ a sufficiently large multiple of $\sqrt{1+\Lambda}$, vanishing of all recorded coefficients forces $u=0$. The recording map is injective and proves the dimension bound. Inverting this estimate gives $\lambda_j\geq c(j+1)^{2/n}-1$.
