# Countable intersection

↑ **Parent:** [Set intersection](set-intersection.md)

The countable intersection of a [sequence](sequence.md) of [sets](set-split.md) contains exactly the elements belonging to every set. [De Morgan's laws](de-morgan-s-laws.md) identify its [relative complement](set-difference.md) with the union of their complements.
