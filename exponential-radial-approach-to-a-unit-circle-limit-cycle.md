# Exponential radial approach to a unit-circle limit cycle

↑ **Parent:** [Unit-circle attracting limit cycle](unit-circle-attracting-limit-cycle.md)

On the punctured plane, the [vector field](vector-field.md) $\dot x=(1/r-1)x-y$, $\dot y=(1/r-1)y+x$ has the displayed polar equations. Its exact trajectories satisfy $r(t)=1+(r(0)-1)e^{-t}$ and $\theta(t)=\theta(0)+t$. Every forward [trajectory](trajectory.md) approaches the [unit circle](complex-unit-circle.md), rotating counterclockwise, from inside or outside according to its initial radius. The circle is an attracting [limit cycle](limit-cycle.md) with least period $2\pi$. The origin is not an [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md): the original [vector field](vector-field.md) is undefined there. The singularity prevents an application of a whole-plane [Bendixson-Dulac criterion](bendixson-dulac-theorem.md) to this [periodic orbit](periodic-orbit.md).
