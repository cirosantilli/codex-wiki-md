# Velocity ellipsoid

↑ **Parent:** [Velocity dispersion](velocity-dispersion.md)

The [velocity ellipsoid](velocity-ellipsoid.md) represents the positive [velocity](velocity.md) [covariance](covariance.md) tensor by its orthogonal principal axes and eigenvalue square roots. The uncentered second-moment tensor differs from [covariance](covariance.md) when a population streams. Vanishing mixed [second moments](second-moment.md) align the second-moment [ellipsoid](ellipsoid.md) with the chosen frame; for pure azimuthal streaming in a phase-mixed axisymmetric model, the off-diagonal [covariance](covariance.md) also vanishes.
