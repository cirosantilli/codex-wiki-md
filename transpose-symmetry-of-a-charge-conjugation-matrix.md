# Transpose symmetry of a charge-conjugation matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Charge conjugation of a Dirac field](charge-conjugation-of-a-dirac-field.md)

If $C\gamma^{\mu T}C^{-1}=-\gamma^\mu$, transposition shows that $C^TC^{-1}$ commutes with every [gamma matrix](gamma-matrices.md). Irreducibility of the complex Dirac representation and the [Schur lemma](schur-s-lemma.md) make it scalar. Transposing $C^T=zC$ again yields $z^2=1$, so $C^T=\pm C$.
