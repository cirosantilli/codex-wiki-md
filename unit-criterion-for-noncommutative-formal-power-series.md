# Unit criterion for noncommutative formal power series

↑ **Parent:** [Noncommutative formal power series ring](noncommutative-formal-power-series-ring.md)

A [noncommutative formal power series](noncommutative-formal-power-series.md) over a [field](field.md) is a [unit](unit-in-a-ring.md) exactly when its [constant term](constant-term.md) is nonzero. Necessity follows because the [constant term](constant-term.md) is a [ring homomorphism](ring-homomorphism.md) to the coefficient [field](field.md). For sufficiency, write $F=c(1+h)$ with $c\ne0$ and $h$ of zero [constant term](constant-term.md). The formal [geometric series](geometric-series.md) $\sum_{j\geq0}(-h)^j$ is well-defined because only $j\leq d$ can contribute to a [word](string.md) of length $d$. Its left and right products with $1+h$ are $1$, by the finite geometric-series identity in each fixed degree. Multiplying by $c^{-1}$ gives the inverse of $F$.
