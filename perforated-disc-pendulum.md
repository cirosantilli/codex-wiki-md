# Perforated-disc pendulum

↑ **Parent:** [Physical pendulum](physical-pendulum.md)

For a plane lamina suspended at O, treat removed pieces as negative [masses](mass.md) and attached pieces as positive ones. Add their [moments of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) about O using the [parallel axis theorem](parallel-axis-theorem.md), and add their first moments to locate the [centre of mass](center-of-mass.md). If the first moments lie along one axis, the gravitational restoring coefficient is $g|\sum_i m_i x_i|$. Dividing it by the total inertia gives the small-[oscillation](oscillation.md) angular frequency squared. Both the restoring moment and inertia change when material is moved.
