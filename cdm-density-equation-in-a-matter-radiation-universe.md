# CDM density equation in a matter-radiation universe

↑ **Parent:** [Nonrelativistic density equation in synchronous gauge](nonrelativistic-density-equation-in-synchronous-gauge.md)

In [Synchronous gauge in cosmology](synchronous-gauge-in-cosmology.md) with coordinates comoving with [cold dark matter](cold-dark-matter.md), the linearized [Einstein field equations](einstein-field-equations.md) and matter conservation give the displayed density equation. Primes denote [conformal time](conformal-time.md) derivatives. The radiation term contains its pressure contribution and cannot be dropped on [superhorizon scales](superhorizon-scale.md) merely because radiation oscillates after [cosmological horizon entry](cosmological-horizon-entry.md).
