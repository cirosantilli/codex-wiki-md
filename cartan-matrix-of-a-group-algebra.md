# Cartan matrix of a group algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Decomposition matrix (modular representation theory)](decomposition-matrix-modular-representation-theory.md)

The Cartan matrix records composition-factor multiplicities in the projective indecomposable modules. For a split modular group algebra, it is

$$
C=D^TD,
$$

where $D$ is the [decomposition matrix](decomposition-matrix-modular-representation-theory.md).
