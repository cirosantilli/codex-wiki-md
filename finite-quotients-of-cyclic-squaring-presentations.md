# Finite quotients of cyclic squaring presentations

↑ **Parent:** [Higman group](higman-group.md)

A [group presentation](group-presentation.md) with a cyclic list of generators $a_i$ and relations $a_i a_{i+1}a_i^{-1}=a_{i+1}^2$ has no nontrivial [finite quotient of a group](finite-quotient-of-a-group.md). In a finite image, all generator orders are odd. Choose the least prime $p$ dividing any nontrivial generator order and let $x$ be the predecessor of a generator $y$ whose order is divisible by $p$. Conjugation by $x$ acts as squaring on $\langle y\rangle$, so the [multiplicative order](multiplicative-order.md) of $2$ modulo $p$ divides the order of $x$. That multiplicative order is greater than one and divides $p-1$, giving a prime divisor smaller than $p$ in the order of $x$, a contradiction. This argument applies to any cycle length; it asserts absence of finite quotients, not infinitude of the presented group.
