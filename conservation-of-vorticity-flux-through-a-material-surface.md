# Conservation of vorticity flux through a material surface

↑ **Parent:** [Barotropic vorticity transport](barotropic-vorticity-transport.md)

For smooth inviscid [barotropic fluid](barotropic-fluid.md) with potential body forces, [barotropic vorticity transport](barotropic-vorticity-transport.md) and $\nabla\cdot\boldsymbol\omega=0$ give $D\boldsymbol\omega/Dt=(\boldsymbol\omega\cdot\nabla)\mathbf u-\boldsymbol\omega\nabla\cdot\mathbf u$. Combine this with [material surface element evolution](material-surface-element-evolution.md) to obtain $D(\boldsymbol\omega\cdot d\mathbf S)/Dt=0$. Integrating over a [material surface](material-surface.md) proves the flux law. Its boundary must move with the fluid; flux through a fixed open surface is generally not conserved. By [Stokes theorem](stokes-theorem.md) the same statement is [Kelvin's circulation theorem](kelvin-s-circulation-theorem.md) around the material boundary.
