# Memorylessness of the geometric distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric distribution](geometric-distribution.md)

For the positive-integer [geometric distribution](geometric-distribution.md) with success [probability](probability.md) $0<p\leq1$, the displayed identity holds for nonnegative [integers](integer.md) $m,r$ whenever the conditioning [event](event.md) has positive [probability](probability.md). The first $m$ attempts have failed; future [independent](independent-random-variables.md) attempts use the same success [probability](probability.md). Equivalently, divide the tail $(1-p)^{m+r}$ by $(1-p)^m$. For a zero-based failures count $F=T-1$, use $\mathbb P(F\geq m+r\mid F\geq m)=\mathbb P(F\geq r)$. Constant success [probability](probability.md) and [independence](independent-random-variables.md) are essential: accumulated wear or learning would require a different model.
