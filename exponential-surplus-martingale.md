# Exponential surplus martingale

↑ **Parent:** [Lundberg inequality](lundberg-inequality.md)

In a [classical risk model](classical-risk-model.md), an [adjustment coefficient](adjustment-coefficient.md) $R$ makes $\lambda(M_X(R)-1)-cR=0$. The [random-sum transform identity](random-sum-transform-identity.md) and [independent increments](independent-increments.md) show that $\mathbb E[Z_t\mid\mathcal F_s]=Z_s$. This nonnegative [martingale](martingale-split.md) starts at one. At the ruin [stopping time](stopping-time.md) $\tau$, $Z_\tau>e^{Ru}$. Applying the [optional stopping theorem](optional-sampling-theorem-for-a-supermartingale.md) only to $\tau\wedge t$ yields $\mathbb P(\tau\le t)\le e^{-Ru}$; passage to increasing horizons proves the [Lundberg inequality](lundberg-inequality.md) without an unjustified expectation identity at an unbounded stopping time.
