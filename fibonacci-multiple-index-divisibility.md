# Fibonacci multiple-index divisibility

↑ **Parent:** [Fibonacci number](fibonacci-number.md)

For positive [integers](integer-split.md) $m,n$, the [Fibonacci numbers](fibonacci-number.md) satisfy $F_m\mid F_{mn}$. The [Fibonacci addition formula](fibonacci-addition-formula.md) gives $F_{mn+m}=F_{m-1}F_{mn}+F_mF_{mn+1}$, so [mathematical induction](mathematical-induction.md) on $n$ proves the assertion. This explains why every fifth [Fibonacci number](fibonacci-number.md) is divisible by $F_5=5$.
