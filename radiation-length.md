# Radiation length

↑ **Parent:** [Particle detection](particle-detection-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radiation_length)

The characteristic thickness over which a high-[energy](energy-split.md) [electron](electron.md) loses all but a fraction $1/e$ of its [energy](energy-split.md) through [bremsstrahlung](bremsstrahlung.md). At fixed thickness $x$, smaller $X_0$ increases the radiative loss; the relevant dimensionless measure is $x/X_0$. Low-[energy](energy-split.md) charged particles can instead be dominated by collisional [stopping power](stopping-power.md).[Sections 34.4.1–34.4.4: collision losses, radiation length and critical energy](review-of-particle-physics-by-particle-data-group-2022.md)
