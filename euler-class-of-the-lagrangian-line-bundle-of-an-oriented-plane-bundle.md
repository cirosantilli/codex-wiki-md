# Euler class of the Lagrangian-line bundle of an oriented plane bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrangian Grassmannian bundle](lagrangian-grassmannian-bundle.md)

If $E\to B$ is an oriented real plane bundle, then $\mathcal LGr(E)=\mathbb P(E)$ is an oriented [circle bundle](circle-bundle.md). Its transition rotations have twice the angle of those of $E$, so

$$
e(\mathcal LGr(E))=2e(E).
$$
