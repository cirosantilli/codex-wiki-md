# Relativistic Doppler effect

↑ **Parent:** [Doppler effect](doppler-effect.md)

The [Lorentz transformation](lorentz-transformation.md) changes the [photon](photon.md) [four-momentum](four-momentum.md) and therefore its frequency, producing the displayed formula for a boost along the first axis. Here the primed ray angle is measured in the emitting frame and $\beta=v/c$. The angle and frequency transforms must be used together: [aberration of light](relativistic-aberration.md) changes the direction, and a photon-number angular fraction need not equal the corresponding radiant-energy fraction.

Here $c$ is the [vacuum](vacuum.md) [speed of light](speed-of-light.md), $\gamma$ is the [Lorentz factor](lorentz-factor.md), and the two [frequencies](frequency.md) are measured in different inertial [reference frames](reference-frame.md). Unlike the [classical Doppler effect for sound](classical-doppler-effect-for-sound.md), there is no material rest [reference frame](reference-frame.md) for [light](light.md) in [vacuum](vacuum.md). For small radial recession speed $v_r\ll c$, the [wavelength](wavelength.md) shift satisfies $\Delta\lambda/\lambda\simeq v_r/c$. This small-speed limit underlies [Doppler spectroscopy](doppler-spectroscopy.md); it is not an exact sound formula.
