# Relativistic Doppler effect

↑ **Parent:** [Doppler effect](doppler-effect.md)

The [Lorentz transformation](lorentz-transformation.md) changes the [photon](photon.md) [four-momentum](four-momentum.md) and therefore its frequency, producing the displayed formula for a boost along the first axis. Here the primed ray angle is measured in the emitting frame and $\beta=v/c$. The angle and frequency transforms must be used together: [aberration of light](relativistic-aberration.md) changes the direction, and a photon-number angular fraction need not equal the corresponding radiant-energy fraction.
