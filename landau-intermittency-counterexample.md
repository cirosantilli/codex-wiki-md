# Landau intermittency counterexample

↑ **Parent:** [Integral-scale intermittency](integral-scale-intermittency.md)

Mixing turbulent populations with differing [viscous dissipation](viscous-dissipation.md) gives $S_p=\beta_pr^{p/3}\langle\epsilon_a^{p/3}\rangle$. Relative to the same power of the mean [viscous dissipation](viscous-dissipation.md), the coefficient changes with the intensity distribution except at $p=3$. Thus universality of all unconditional coefficients is incompatible with arbitrary [integral-scale intermittency](integral-scale-intermittency.md).
