# Real (1, 1)-form

↑ **Parent:** [Hermitian manifold](hermitian-manifold.md)

A [differential form of type (p, q)](differential-form-of-type-p-q.md) $\varphi$ of type $(1,1)$ is real when $\bar\varphi=\varphi$. Locally this is equivalent to

$$
\varphi=i\sum_{j,k}h_{j\bar k}\,dz^j\wedge d\bar z^k
$$

for a Hermitian matrix $(h_{j\bar k})$.
