# Balance of linear momentum

↑ **Parent:** [Continuum mechanics](continuum-mechanics-split.md)

For a body occupying $V$, the rate of change of $\int_V\rho\mathbf v\,dV$ equals the [body forces](body-force.md) and surface [tractions](traction.md). With the [Cauchy stress tensor](cauchy-stress-tensor.md), the local form is $\rho D\mathbf v/Dt=\nabla\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma+\rho\mathbf b$.
