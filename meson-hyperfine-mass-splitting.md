# Meson hyperfine mass splitting

↑ **Parent:** [Meson](meson.md)

In a simple constituent [quark model](quark-model.md), a spin-dependent contribution $A\mathbf S_1\cdot\mathbf S_2/(m_1m_2)$ splits a spin-zero and spin-one [meson](meson.md) having the same spatial configuration. For two spin-half constituents, [addition of angular momentum](addition-of-angular-momentum.md) gives $\mathbf S_1\cdot\mathbf S_2=[S(S+1)-3/2]/2$, equal to $-3/4$ for $S=0$ and $+1/4$ for $S=1$. Thus

$$
M_0=M_{\rm base}-\frac{3A}{4m_1m_2},\qquad M_1=M_{\rm base}+\frac{A}{4m_1m_2},\qquad M_1-M_0=\frac{A}{m_1m_2}.
$$

The spin-averaged mass is $(M_0+3M_1)/4=M_{\rm base}$. Positive $A$ places the vector state above the pseudoscalar. Determining $A$ from just $M_0$ needs an additional assumption about $M_{\rm base}$; setting it equal to $m_1+m_2$ neglects all other spin-independent binding terms. A splitting below the [pion](pion.md) mass closes a one-pion strong decay to the lower meson, often making the allowed electromagnetic transition with [photon](photon.md) emission dominant.
