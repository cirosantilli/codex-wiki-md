# Blaschke factorization of a bounded holomorphic function

↑ **Parent:** [Blaschke product](blaschke-product.md)

Every nonzero bounded [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function.md) on the [unit disc](unit-disc.md) has this factorization, with $B$ the [Blaschke product](blaschke-product.md) of its zeros and $g$ zero-free and [holomorphic](complex-differentiability-at-a-point.md). The [Blaschke condition](blaschke-condition.md) follows from [Jensen's formula](jensen-s-formula.md). Dividing by any finite partial zero product gives a [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function.md); the [maximum modulus principle](maximum-modulus-principle.md) and the finite product's boundary modulus one bound that quotient by $\|f\|_\infty$. [Locally uniform convergence](locally-uniform-convergence.md) of the partial products gives the same bound for $g$, with removable extensions at their zeros. This factorization alone does not make $g$ constant or a full inner-outer factorization.
