# Constant-coefficient elliptic second-derivative estimate

↑ **Parent:** [Uniformly elliptic operator](uniformly-elliptic-operator.md)

If the constant [symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md) $A$ satisfies $A\xi\mathbin\cdot\xi\geq\theta|\xi|^2$, then every [smooth function](smooth-function.md) $u$ with [compact support](compact-support.md) satisfies

$$
\theta\|D^2u\|_2\leq\|A^{ij}D_{ij}u\|_2.
$$

Indeed, the [Fourier transform of a derivative](fourier-transform-of-a-derivative.md) and the [Plancherel theorem](plancherel-theorem.md) turn the squared norms into integrals with [Fourier multipliers](fourier-multiplier.md) $|\xi|^4$ and $(A\xi\mathbin\cdot\xi)^2$.
