# Pairwise coprimality

↑ **Parent:** [Coprime integers](coprime-integers.md)

A list of [integers](integer-split.md) $a_1,\ldots,a_r$, with $r\ge2$, has [pairwise coprimality](pairwise-coprimality.md) when every two entries in distinct positions are [coprime](coprime-integers.md). This implies [joint coprimality](joint-coprimality.md). For [positive integers](positive-integer.md), it means no [prime factor](prime-factor.md) occurs in two different entries. For example, $4,9,25$ satisfy this condition.
