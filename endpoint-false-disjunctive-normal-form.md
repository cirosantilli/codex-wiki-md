# Endpoint-false disjunctive normal form

↑ **Parent:** [Disjunctive normal form](disjunctive-normal-form.md)

A [propositional formula](propositional-formula.md) false on the all-true and all-false [Boolean valuations](boolean-valuation.md) has a [disjunctive normal form](disjunctive-normal-form.md) in which every [minterm](minterm.md) contains a positive [literal](boolean-literal.md) and a negative [literal](boolean-literal.md). Grouping the positive [propositional variables](propositional-variable.md) into $C$ and the negated [propositional variables](propositional-variable.md) into the [negation](negation.md) of a [logical disjunction](logical-disjunction.md) $D$ writes each [minterm](minterm.md) as $\neg(C\to D)$. [De Morgan's laws](de-morgan-s-laws.md) therefore give the [negation](negation.md) of a [logical conjunction](logical-conjunction.md) of such [logical implications](logical-implication.md). For a [contradiction](contradiction.md) use $\neg(p\to p)$.
