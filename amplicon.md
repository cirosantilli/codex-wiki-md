# Amplicon

↑ **Parent:** [Polymerase chain reaction](polymerase-chain-reaction.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amplicon)

An [amplicon](amplicon.md) is a [nucleic acid](nucleic-acid.md) product of amplification. In ordinary [PCR amplification](polymerase-chain-reaction.md) without added primer tails, its boundaries eventually correspond to the outer ends of the two [PCR primer](pcr-primer.md) binding sites. First-cycle extensions on long starting templates can run beyond the opposite site; exact-length single strands appear in cycle 2 and exact-length duplex products in cycle 3 under complete extension. This explains why the bounded product becomes dominant even though the initial extensions need not have its length.
