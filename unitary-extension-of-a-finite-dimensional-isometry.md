# Unitary extension of a finite-dimensional isometry

↑ **Parent:** [Linear isometry of Hilbert spaces](linear-isometry-of-hilbert-spaces.md)

A [linear isometry](linear-isometry-of-hilbert-spaces.md) from a subspace of a finite-dimensional [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) into the same ambient space extends to a [unitary operator](unitary-operator.md). Complete an [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) of the input subspace and its isometric image to full [orthonormal bases](orthonormal-basis.md), then map the first to the second. The equal complement dimensions make this possible. Orthogonality of the specified image columns, not normalization alone, is essential. In an infinite-dimensional space arbitrary isometries need not be surjective; the finite-dimensional same-space hypothesis cannot be dropped.
