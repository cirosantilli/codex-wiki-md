# Simes test

↑ **Parent:** [Simes inequality](simes-inequality.md)

For the intersection of $m$ null hypotheses, reject when $\min_i mp_{(i)}/i\le\alpha$. The [Simes inequality](simes-inequality.md) establishes a level-$\alpha$ test under independence of valid true-null [p-values](p-value.md). The displayed quantity is its combined [p-value](p-value.md).
