# Hernquist model

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar dynamics](stellar-dynamics.md)

The [Hernquist model](hernquist-model.md) has [relative potential](relative-potential.md) $\psi=GM/(r+a)$ and density $\rho=Ma/[2\pi r(r+a)^3]$. It has enclosed [mass](mass.md) $Mr^2/(r+a)^2$, a central $r^{-1}$ cusp and an outer $r^{-4}$ tail. Since $r\rho=a\psi^3/(2\pi G^3M^2)$, the [half-anisotropic distribution function](half-anisotropic-distribution-function.md) is $f(E,L)=3aE^2/(4\pi^3G^3M^2L)$ for $0<E<GM/a$. Its moments are $\langle v_r^2\rangle=\psi/4$ and each tangential moment $\psi/8$. Isotropic and other admissible anisotropic distributions can also support this same density; the [gravitational potential](newtonian-potential-of-a-point-mass.md) does not select a unique [velocity](velocity.md) anisotropy.
