# Invariant factor of a finitely generated module

↑ **Parent:** [Structure theorem for finitely generated modules over a principal ideal domain](structure-theorem-for-finitely-generated-modules-over-a-principal-ideal-domain.md)

For a [finitely generated module](finitely-generated-module.md) over a [principal ideal domain](principal-ideal-domain.md), its invariant factors are nonzero nonunits $d_i$ in a decomposition $M\cong R^r\oplus\bigoplus_iR/(d_i)$, ordered by $d_i\mid d_{i+1}$. They are unique up to multiplication by units and describe the [torsion submodule](torsion-submodule.md). Over $F[t]$, making $t$ act as a [linear operator](linear-operator.md) recovers the [invariant factors of a linear operator](invariant-factors-of-a-linear-operator.md) used in its [rational canonical form](rational-canonical-form.md).
