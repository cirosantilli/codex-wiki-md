# Symbolic potential

↑ **Parent:** [Thermodynamic formalism](thermodynamic-formalism.md)

A symbolic potential assigns a real weight to an infinite symbolic sequence. Its orbit sum gives the energy in a weighted [partition function](canonical-partition-function.md). Agreement on the first $r$ symbols defines the variation $\operatorname{var}_rU$; summable variations control sensitivity to a remote history. Geometric potentials encode interval contraction, while other potentials can assign different statistical weights to the same symbolic dynamics.
