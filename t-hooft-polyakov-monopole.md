# 't Hooft-Polyakov monopole

↑ **Parent:** [Gauge-theory soliton](gauge-theory-soliton.md)

A smooth finite-energy monopole in an $SU(2)$ [Yang-Mills theory](yang-mills-theory.md) with an adjoint [Higgs field](higgs-field.md) uses the normalized Higgs direction at infinity to define a map $S^2_\infty\to S^2$. Its nonzero degree supplies the magnetic topological sector. Unlike a singular Abelian Dirac monopole, the non-Abelian gauge and Higgs fields can have a regular core. In the zero-potential limit the [Bogomolny-Prasad-Sommerfield monopole](bogomolny-prasad-sommerfield-monopole.md) equations are $B_i=\pm D_i\Phi$.
