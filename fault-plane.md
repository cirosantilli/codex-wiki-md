# Fault plane

↑ **Parent:** [Earthquake](earthquake.md)

A [fault plane](fault-plane.md) is an idealized planar surface across which rock has slipped. A curved fault can be described by local [fault planes](fault-plane.md). Its orientation is specified by [fault strike](fault-strike.md) and [fault dip](fault-dip.md), and the slip direction within it by [fault rake](fault-rake.md).
