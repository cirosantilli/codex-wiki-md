# Absorption-Line Probes of Gas and Dust in Galactic Superwinds by Timothy M. Heckman and others

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by Timothy M. Heckman](work-by-timothy-m-heckman.md)

The primary survey compares interstellar sodium absorption with galaxy systemic velocities in infrared-bright starbursts. Blueshifts and a viewing-angle dependence support a minor-axis outflow interpretation. Saturation, [ionization](ionization.md) and geometry limit conversion of the absorption into total mass-loss rates.

[https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0002526](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0002526)
