# Semisimple category

↑ **Parent:** [Additive category](additive-category.md)

In the finite-dimensional setting, a semisimple category is an [additive category](additive-category.md) over a [field](field.md) in which every object is a finite [biproduct](biproduct.md) of simple objects, different simple objects have zero [morphism](morphism.md) spaces, and the [endomorphism](endomorphism.md) algebra of each simple object is a [division algebra](division-algebra.md). Such categories have finite [matrix](matrix.md) blocks and all their [idempotents](idempotent.md) split. The [semisimplicity of the reduced Temperley-Lieb category](semisimplicity-of-the-reduced-temperley-lieb-category.md) gives an example whose simple objects are the surviving [Jones-Wenzl colors](jones-wenzl-color.md).
