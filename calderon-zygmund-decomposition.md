<h1 id="calderon-zygmund-decomposition">Calderón–Zygmund decomposition</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Harmonic analysis](harmonic-analysis.md)

For $f\in L^1(\mathbb R^d)$ and a positive threshold $\lambda$, take maximal dyadic cubes with mean of $|f|$ exceeding $\lambda$. They are disjoint, have total volume at most $\|f\|_1/\lambda$, and their means are at most $2^d\lambda$. Replace $f$ inside each selected cube by its mean to obtain $g$; write $b_Q=(f-f_Q)\mathbf1_Q$. Then $\|g\|_\infty\leq2^d\lambda$, $\|g\|_1\leq\|f\|_1$, $\int b_Q=0$, and $\sum_Q\|b_Q\|_1\leq2\|f\|_1$. The outside bound follows from the [Lebesgue differentiation theorem](lebesgue-differentiation-theorem.md). This separates a bounded part from localized cancellation errors.
