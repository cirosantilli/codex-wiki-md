# Lebesgue constant of interpolation

↑ **Parent:** [Uniform approximation](uniform-approximation-split.md)

If interpolation at distinct sites $x_i$ in a finite-dimensional space of [continuous functions](continuous-function.md) is unique, its cardinal functions satisfy $\ell_i(x_j)=\delta_{ij}$. The interpolation [linear map](linear-map.md) is $Pf=\sum_if(x_i)\ell_i$ and has [operator norm](operator-norm.md) $\Lambda_{\mathbf x}$ in the [supremum norm](supremum-norm.md). The upper bound is the [triangle inequality](triangle-inequality.md). For the reverse bound choose node values to be the signs of the cardinal functions at a point maximizing their absolute sum, and extend those values by a [continuous function](continuous-function.md) of [norm](norm.md) one. The [polynomial reproduction error bound](polynomial-reproduction-error-bound.md) gives $\|f-Pf\|_\infty\le(1+\Lambda_{\mathbf x})\inf_{s\in\operatorname{range}P}\|f-s\|_\infty$.
