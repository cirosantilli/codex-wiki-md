# Capped simplex

↑ **Parent:** [Convex set](convex-set.md)

For an integer $0\leq k\leq n$, the capped simplex

$$
P_k=\{v\in\mathbb R^n:0\leq v_i\leq1,\ \sum_i v_i=k\}
$$

is the [convex hull](convex-hull.md) of the zero-one [vectors](vector.md) having exactly $k$ entries equal to one. It is a [convex polytope](convex-polytope.md) whose [extreme points](extreme-point.md) are precisely these [vectors](vector.md).

If a feasible [vector](vector.md) has a fractional coordinate, the integer coordinate sum forces at least two fractional coordinates. Perturbing one upward and the other downward by a sufficiently small amount gives two distinct feasible [vectors](vector.md) whose midpoint is the original [vector](vector.md). It is therefore not an [extreme point](extreme-point.md). Conversely, a zero-one [vector](vector.md) cannot be a nontrivial [convex combination](convex-combination.md) of points of $[0,1]^n$, since each coordinate is already at a bound.
