# Electron-pair arrow

↑ **Parent:** [Reaction mechanism](reaction-mechanism.md)

A curved arrow whose tail marks an electron pair and whose head marks its destination in a proposed reaction step. The pair may start as a [lone pair](lone-pair.md) or a bond pair; a full arrowhead represents two electrons.
