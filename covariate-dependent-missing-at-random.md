# Covariate-dependent missing at random

↑ **Parent:** [Missing at random](missing-at-random.md)

For each fixed observation pattern, the probability of that pattern may depend on observed response components and fully observed [covariates](covariate.md), but not on the response components missing under that pattern. Conditional [missing completely at random](missing-completely-at-random.md) is the special case with no dependence on observed response components either.
