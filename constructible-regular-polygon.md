# Constructible regular polygon

↑ **Parent:** [Constructible polygon](constructible-polygon.md)

A [constructible regular polygon](constructible-regular-polygon.md) is a [regular polygon](regular-polygon.md) admitting a [compass-and-straightedge construction](straightedge-and-compass-construction-split.md). The [Gauss-Wantzel theorem](gauss-wantzel-theorem.md) classifies its possible numbers of sides.
