# Quadratic form of a squared cross product

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrange identity for the cross product](lagrange-identity-for-the-cross-product.md)

For real three-dimensional vectors, [Lagrange's identity](lagrange-identity-for-the-cross-product.md) gives $|a\times b|^2=a^TMa$ with symmetric matrix $M=|b|^2I-bb^T$. For $b\ne0$, the normal line spanned by $b$ is its [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) and the perpendicular plane is its $|b|^2$ [eigenspace](eigenspace.md). Consequently $M$ is [positive semidefinite](positive-semidefinite-matrix.md), has rank two and has zero [determinant](determinant.md). For $b=0$, it is the zero matrix.
