# Buoyant density

↑ **Parent:** [Isopycnic centrifugation](isopycnic-centrifugation.md)

[Buoyant density](buoyant-density.md) is the surrounding liquid [density](density.md) at which a species has no net centrifugal drift under the stated conditions. It depends on interactions with the medium, including associated solvent, rather than necessarily equaling the dry substance's [density](density.md). Equal [buoyant densities](buoyant-density.md) can give overlapping bands even for differently sized [proteins](protein.md) or [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) fragments.[An Introduction to Ultracentrifugation, Isopycnic Centrifugation: buoyant density depends on the surrounding medium](https://www.beckman.com/resources/technologies/centrifugation/ultracentrifugation/)
