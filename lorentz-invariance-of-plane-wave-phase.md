# Lorentz invariance of plane-wave phase

↑ **Parent:** [Four-wavevector](four-wavevector.md)

The [wave phase](phase-waves.md) labels a physical stage of an oscillation at a [spacetime](spacetime.md) event. Treating it as a [Lorentz scalar](lorentz-scalar.md) makes its [gradient](gradient.md) $K_\mu=\partial_\mu\Phi$ a [covector](covector.md); raising the index gives the [four-wavevector](four-wavevector.md) $K^\mu=(\omega/c,\mathbf k)$. A boost along $x$ gives $\omega'=\gamma(\omega-vk_x)$ and $k_x'=\gamma(k_x-v\omega/c^2)$. Thus [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) and [wavevector](wavevector.md) transform together rather than independently.
