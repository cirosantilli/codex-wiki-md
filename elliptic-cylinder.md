# Elliptic cylinder

↑ **Parent:** [Quadric surface](quadric-surface.md)

For $a,b>0$, an elliptic cylinder is a [quadric surface](quadric-surface.md) with the displayed equation in suitable [Cartesian coordinates](cartesian-coordinate-system.md), independent of the third coordinate. It is formed by translating an [ellipse](ellipse.md) parallel to a line perpendicular to its plane. The generator direction is the zero-eigenvalue direction of its quadratic part, while the transverse semiaxes are $a,b$. It is circular if $a=b$.
