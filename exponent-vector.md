# Exponent vector

↑ **Parent:** [Factor base](factor-base.md)

Relative to an ordered [factor base](factor-base.md) $(p_1,\ldots,p_B)$, a positive [integer](integer-split.md) $r=\prod_jp_j^{e_j}$ has exponent vector $(e_1,\ldots,e_B)$. [Multiplication](multiplication.md) of such [integers](integer-split.md) adds their exponent vectors. Reducing every coordinate modulo two gives the parity vector used to find [congruences of squares](congruence-of-squares.md). The ordering and any sign coordinate must be used consistently across all rows.
