# Inclined-bed sediment threshold

↑ **Parent:** [Sediment entrainment threshold](sediment-entrainment-threshold.md)

For locally bed-tangent [drag force](drag-physics.md) and an upslope angle $a$, a spherical-grain [force balance](force-balance.md) gives $\tau_{\mathrm{th}}(a)=\tau_{\mathrm{th},0}(\cos a+\sin a/\mu_s)$. The small-slope term is $\delta\tau_{\mathrm{th}}=(\tau_{\mathrm{th},0}/\mu_s)\eta_x$. A horizontal [drag force](drag-physics.md) instead changes the normal contact [force](force.md) and gives a different slope correction.
