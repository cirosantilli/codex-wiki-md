# Maximum growth rate of ideal magnetorotational instability

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal magnetorotational dispersion relation](ideal-magnetorotational-dispersion-relation.md)

For local cylindrical shear $q=-d\ln\Omega/d\ln r$ with $0<q<2$, maximizing the positive squared-growth root of the [ideal magnetorotational dispersion relation](ideal-magnetorotational-dispersion-relation.md) gives the displayed formulas. For a Keplerian disk these are $s_{\max}=3\Omega/4$ and $\omega_A^2=15\Omega^2/16$. Field strength selects the fastest wavelength, while the maximum ideal rate is set by shear. Finite domains and magnetic diffusion can exclude that wavelength.
