# Frobenius algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Associative algebra](associative-algebra-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frobenius_algebra)

A finite-dimensional unital [associative algebra](associative-algebra-split.md) over a [field](field.md) admitting a [linear functional](linear-functional.md) $\varepsilon$ whose pairing $(a,b)\mapsto\varepsilon(ab)$ is nondegenerate. The [dual numbers](dual-number.md) with functional extracting the nilpotent coefficient are an example; semisimplicity is unnecessary.
