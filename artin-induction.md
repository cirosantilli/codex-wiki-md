# Artin induction

↑ **Parent:** [Character theory](character-theory.md)

Every complex [character of a representation](character-of-a-representation.md) of a [finite group](finite-group.md) $G$ is a rational linear combination of [characters](character-of-a-representation.md) of [induced representations](induced-representation.md) from one-dimensional [group representations](group-representation.md) of [cyclic subgroups](cyclic-subgroup.md) $C_j$. One-dimensionality follows because the [irreducible representations](irreducible-representation.md) of a finite [cyclic group](cyclic-group.md) over the complex numbers are one-dimensional. The rational coefficients distinguish this statement from [Brauer induction](brauer-induction.md), which permits integer coefficients by allowing more general subgroups. For a rational-valued [character](character-of-a-representation.md), another form of Artin induction uses rational combinations of [induced representations](induced-representation.md) of trivial [group representations](group-representation.md) of [cyclic subgroups](cyclic-subgroup.md).
