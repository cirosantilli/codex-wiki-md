# Solar magnetic field

↑ **Parent:** [Sun](sun.md)

The solar magnetic field is generated and maintained by electrically conducting flows through [solar dynamo](solar-dynamo.md) action. It includes a large-scale approximately dipolar component near activity minimum and strong, intermittent fields in active regions and [sunspots](sunspot.md). Its organized polarity reversals define the magnetic [solar cycle](solar-cycle.md). Magnetic stresses modify the generating flows, so a saturated dynamo requires nonlinear feedback as well as magnetic induction.
