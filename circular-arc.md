# Circular arc

↑ **Parent:** [Circle](circle.md)

A [circular arc](circular-arc.md) is a connected portion of a [circle](circle.md). For [radius](radius.md) $R>0$ and subtended central [angle](angle.md) $0<\theta<2\pi$ measured in [radians](radian.md), its [arc length](arc-length.md) is $R\theta$. Two distinct endpoints determine two [circular arcs](circular-arc.md): when they are not opposite, the minor arc has $\theta<\pi$ and the major arc has $\theta>\pi$. Opposite endpoints determine two [semicircles](semicircle.md). The [straight line](straight-line.md) segment joining the endpoints is a [chord](chord-of-a-circle.md), rather than the curved [circular arc](circular-arc.md) itself.
