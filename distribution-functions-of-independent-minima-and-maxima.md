# Distribution functions of independent minima and maxima

↑ **Parent:** [Order statistic](order-statistic.md)

Independence factors the events that both variables exceed a threshold or both lie below it. This gives the two [cumulative distribution functions](cumulative-distribution-function.md) above, including for distributions with atoms. The minimum formula is most naturally expressed through survival [probabilities](probability.md), while the maximum formula uses the CDFs directly.
