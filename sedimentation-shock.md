# Sedimentation shock

↑ **Parent:** [Kinematic sedimentation](kinematic-sedimentation.md)

A moving discontinuity of [particle volume fraction](particle-volume-fraction.md) in [kinematic sedimentation](kinematic-sedimentation.md). Conservation of particle [volume](volume.md) gives the [Rankine-Hugoniot condition](rankine-hugoniot-conditions.md) $U[\phi]=[j]$ in a consistently oriented coordinate. A clearing front and a deposit front can both be [sedimentation shocks](sedimentation-shock.md). A stationary deposit uses zero particle flux; its compacted branch need not obey the suspension flux law.
