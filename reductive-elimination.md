# Reductive elimination

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical reaction](chemical-reaction-split.md)

In a standard two-ligand [reductive elimination](reductive-elimination.md), two metal-bound anionic fragments couple to form a bond while metal [oxidation state](oxidation-state.md) decreases by two. The reacting ligands commonly need adjacent positions; favorable bond formation and metal reduction energetics determine whether the formal step actually occurs.
