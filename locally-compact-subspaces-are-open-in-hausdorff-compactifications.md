# Locally compact subspaces are open in Hausdorff compactifications

↑ **Parent:** [Hausdorff compactification](hausdorff-compactification.md)

If a locally [compact](compact-space.md) [Hausdorff space](hausdorff-space.md) is densely embedded in a [Hausdorff space](hausdorff-space.md), its image is [open](open-set.md). Around a point choose an ambient [open set](open-set.md) whose trace lies in a [compact](compact-space.md) [neighborhood](neighbourhood-mathematics.md) $K$. Density puts that ambient [open set](open-set.md) inside the ambient [closure](closure-topology.md) of its trace, which lies in $K$ because [compact subsets](compact-space.md) of a [Hausdorff space](hausdorff-space.md) are [closed](closed-set.md). Thus the ambient [open set](open-set.md) lies in the image.
