# Skellam distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md)

The distribution of $N_+-N_-$ for independent [Poisson random variables](poisson-distribution.md) with means $\lambda_+,\lambda_-$ is the Skellam distribution. Its [characteristic function](characteristic-function.md) is $\exp(\lambda_+(e^{iu}-1)+\lambda_-(e^{-iu}-1))$, its mean is $\lambda_+-\lambda_-$ and its variance is $\lambda_++\lambda_-$. For equal means $\lambda>0$,

$$
\mathbb P(N_+-N_-=k)=e^{-2\lambda}\sum_{j=0}^\infty\frac{\lambda^{2j+|k|}}{j!(j+|k|)!},\qquad k\in\mathbb Z.
$$

This is the fixed-time law of a [symmetric Poisson difference process](symmetric-poisson-difference-process.md).
