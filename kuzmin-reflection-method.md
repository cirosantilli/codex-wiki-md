# Kuzmin reflection method

↑ **Parent:** [Kuzmin disk](kuzmin-disk.md)

Reflect a vacuum [gravitational potential](newtonian-potential-of-a-point-mass.md) in the upper half-space evenly across $z=0$. For the convention in which [gravitational acceleration](gravitational-acceleration.md) is $\nabla\psi$, the derivative jump is $2\partial_z\psi(R,0^+)=-4\pi G\Sigma(R)$. Thus a point mass $m$ below the plane produces a [Kuzmin disk](kuzmin-disk.md), and superposing point masses with weight $\mu(b)\,db$ produces $\Sigma(R)=(2\pi)^{-1}\int_0^\infty b\mu(b)(R^2+b^2)^{-3/2}\,db$. Positive weights give a nonnegative [surface density](surface-density-of-a-disk.md) wherever the integral converges.
