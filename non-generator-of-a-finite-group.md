# Non-generator of a finite group

↑ **Parent:** [Frattini subgroup](frattini-subgroup.md)

An element that can always be omitted from a generating family. These are exactly the elements of the [Frattini subgroup](frattini-subgroup.md): a proper generated subgroup is contained in a maximal subgroup, while an element outside a maximal subgroup generates the group together with it.
