# Scattering

↑ **Parent:** [Physics](physics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scattering)

An interaction that changes the state or propagation of an incident [particle](particle-physics.md) or [wave](wave-split.md). Distinguish [elastic scattering](elastic-scattering.md) from [inelastic scattering](inelastic-scattering.md), and the general concept from its specifically quantum treatment in [quantum scattering](quantum-scattering-split.md).
