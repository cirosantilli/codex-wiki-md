# Euler strut

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic beam](elastic-beam.md)

An [Euler strut](euler-strut.md) is an initially straight slender elastic column loaded in axial compression. Its lateral stability depends on [bending stiffness](bending-stiffness.md), [length](length.md) and endpoint constraints. [Euler buckling](euler-s-critical-load.md) predicts a loss of straight-configuration stability before material failure only in the appropriate slender elastic regime; it is distinct from crushing or plastic yielding of a short column.
