# Radiative recombination

↑ **Parent:** [Ionization](ionization.md)

A free [electron](electron.md) can be captured by an ion while a photon carries away binding and kinetic energy. This [radiative recombination](radiative-recombination.md) process contributes free-bound radiation to plasma cooling and balances [collisional ionization](collisional-ionization.md) in a collisional-equilibrium plasma.
