# Lie group homomorphism determined by its differential

↑ **Parent:** [Lie group homomorphism](lie-group-homomorphism.md)

A [Lie group homomorphism](lie-group-homomorphism.md) intertwines [one-parameter subgroups](one-parameter-subgroup.md) and therefore obeys the displayed identity. A connected [Lie group](lie-group.md) is generated by an identity neighbourhood in an exponential chart, so its [homomorphism](homomorphism.md) is uniquely determined by the differential at the identity. On a disconnected [group](group-split.md), only the restriction to its [identity component](identity-component.md) is determined this way; the images of the other components require extra data.
