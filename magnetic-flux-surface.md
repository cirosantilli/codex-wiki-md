# Magnetic flux surface

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetohydrodynamics](magnetohydrodynamics-split.md)

A surface everywhere tangent to the [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md). Nested toroidal flux surfaces provide a useful approximate organization of [tokamak](tokamak.md) confinement: ideal guiding motion follows the field rather than directly crossing the surfaces. Finite orbit width, collisions and fluctuating fields can still produce transport across them.
