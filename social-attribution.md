# Social attribution

↑ **Parent:** [Attribution theory](attribution-theory.md)

An inference about the causes of another person's conduct or a social event. [Social attribution](social-attribution.md) can assign different weights to dispositional and situational explanations; its accuracy depends on available evidence and justified causal assumptions.
