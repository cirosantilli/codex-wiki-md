# Signed gluing-map reduction

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetric homoclinic gluing bifurcation](symmetric-homoclinic-gluing-bifurcation.md)

For $x'=-\mu\operatorname{sgn}(x)+A\operatorname{sgn}(y)|x|^\delta$, $y'=\operatorname{sgn}(x)$, the invariant sign sheet has $y=\pm1$ after one iterate. There $z=-xy$ obeys the displayed one-dimensional map. A negative fixed $z$ lifts to two symmetry-related [fixed points](fixed-point.md), while a positive fixed $z$ lifts to one [period-two orbit](period-two-orbit.md). The origin of the [return map](poincare-map.md) represents the [separatrix](separatrix.md), where the flow return time diverges, and is not an ordinary finite-period [orbit of a group action](orbit-of-a-group-action.md).
