# Sine-Gordon breather spectrum at reflectionless couplings

↑ **Parent:** [Sine-Gordon theory](sine-gordon-theory.md)

At renormalized coupling $\gamma=8\pi/n$, the kink-antikink transmission [poles](pole.md) lie at $\vartheta=i\pi(1-k/n)$. The [relativistic bound-state mass from a rapidity pole](relativistic-bound-state-mass-from-a-rapidity-pole.md) gives the displayed increasing [breather](breather.md) [masses](mass.md). The [kink](scalar-field-kink.md) [mass](mass.md) is $M=mn/\pi$, and $k=n$ is an excluded threshold state. No [breathers](breather.md) occur at $n=1$; scattering involving a physical second [breather](breather.md) requires $n\geq3$.
