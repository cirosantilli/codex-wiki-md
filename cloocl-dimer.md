# ClOOCl dimer

↑ **Parent:** [Chlorine monoxide](chlorine-monoxide.md)

The [chlorine](chlorine.md) peroxide structure formed by association of two ClO radicals with collisional stabilization. Its low-temperature stability and [photodissociation](photodissociation.md) provide a route for catalytic [ozone](ozone.md) destruction. In a laboratory flow system, changing among three ClO precursors and examining infrared spectra helped distinguish the predominant ClOOCl product from an alternative ClOClO isomer. Product assignment and [ultraviolet](ultraviolet.md) absorption support the proposed intermediate, but do not alone measure an entire atmospheric cycle's ozone-loss rate.[NASA abstract: precursors, 220–240 K flow system, distinct infrared signatures and [ultraviolet](ultraviolet.md) spectrum](production-of-chlorine-oxide-cl2o2-from-the-self-reaction-of-the-chlorine-oxide-clo-radical-by-l-t-molina-and-m-j-molina.md)
