# Periodic Potts chain

↑ **Parent:** [Potts model](potts-model.md)

The ferromagnetic [Potts model](potts-model.md) on a labelled ring has $Q$ possible site states, $J>0$ and $\sigma_{L+1}=\sigma_1$. Its [spin-chain transfer matrix](transfer-matrix-for-a-classical-spin-chain.md) is $T=(e^{\beta J}-1)I+P$, with $P$ the $Q$-dimensional [all-ones matrix](all-ones-matrix.md). The [canonical partition function](canonical-partition-function.md) is $(e^{\beta J}+Q-1)^L+(Q-1)(e^{\beta J}-1)^L$. The convention counts two bonds when $L=2$.
