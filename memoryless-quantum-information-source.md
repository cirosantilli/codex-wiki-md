# Memoryless quantum information source

↑ **Parent:** [Schumacher compression](schumacher-compression.md)

A pure-state source emits independent states from a fixed [quantum state ensemble](quantum-state-ensemble.md). Its average block [density operator](density-matrix.md) is the tensor power of the one-use average. Unassisted [reliable quantum source compression](reliable-quantum-source-compression.md) preserves the emitted quantum states, rather than only their classical labels. [Schumacher compression](schumacher-compression.md) gives the optimum asymptotic qubit rate $S(\rho)$ for such a source, with every larger rate achievable. Nonorthogonal equally likely pure states need not have entropy one: their classical labels have entropy one but their average density operator can have lower [Von Neumann entropy](von-neumann-entropy-split.md).
