# Electron pair geometry

↑ **Parent:** [Valence shell electron pair repulsion theory](valence-shell-electron-pair-repulsion-theory.md)

[Electron pair geometry](electron-pair-geometry.md) describes the arrangement of all [electron domains](electron-domain.md) in the [VSEPR model](valence-shell-electron-pair-repulsion-theory.md), including [lone pairs](lone-pair.md). [Molecular geometry](molecular-geometry.md) instead describes the arrangement of [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md). Four domains have a tetrahedral ideal arrangement, but keeping only the bonded neighbours gives the different [molecular geometries](molecular-geometry.md) of [methane](methane.md), [ammonia](ammonia.md) and [water](water.md).
