# Deuteron D-state admixture

↑ **Parent:** [Deuteron](deuteron.md)

The [deuteron](deuteron.md) has a dominant $\,{}^3S_1$ component and a smaller $\,{}^3D_1$ component coupled by the [nuclear tensor force](nuclear-tensor-force.md). The [electric quadrupole moment](electric-quadrupole-moment.md) rules out a purely isotropic [S wave](s-wave-quantum-scattering.md) spatial state. A D-state probability depends on the chosen nuclear Hamiltonian and current approximation; it is not an independently model-free observable.
