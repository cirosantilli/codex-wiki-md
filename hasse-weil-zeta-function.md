<h1 id="hasse-weil-zeta-function">Hasse–Weil zeta function</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [L-function](l-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hasse–Weil_zeta_function)

The Hasse–Weil zeta function of an [algebraic variety](algebraic-variety.md) over a [number field](number-field.md) is assembled from local point-counting factors at finite places. At a good place with residue field of size $q$, the factor is $Z(V_q,q^{-s})$, where $Z(V_q,T)=\exp(\sum_{n\geq1}\#V_q(\mathbb F_{q^n})T^n/n)$. Defining all bad-place factors requires a specified integral model or convention. The [zeta function of an elliptic curve over a finite field](zeta-function-of-an-elliptic-curve-over-a-finite-field.md) is a local factor, rather than this global product.
