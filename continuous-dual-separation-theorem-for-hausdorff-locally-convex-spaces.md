# Continuous-dual separation theorem for Hausdorff locally convex spaces

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous dual space](continuous-dual-space-split.md)

In a Hausdorff [locally convex space](locally-convex-space.md), a separating family of continuous [seminorms](seminorm.md) has $p(x)>0$ for any chosen nonzero $x$ and some $p$. Define a [linear functional](linear-functional.md) on its one-dimensional span attaining $p(x)$ and extend it under that [seminorm](seminorm.md) by the real [Hahn-Banach theorem](hahn-banach-theorem.md). The extension is continuous because it is seminorm-bounded, and distinguishes $x$ from zero. The Hausdorff condition is essential.
