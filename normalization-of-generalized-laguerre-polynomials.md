# Normalization of generalized Laguerre polynomials

↑ **Parent:** [Generalized Laguerre polynomial](generalized-laguerre-polynomial.md)

For integers $m,n\geq0$, the standard [Rodrigues' formula](rodrigues-formula.md) $L_n^{(m)}=e^xx^{-m}D^n(x^{n+m}e^{-x})/n!$ has the endpoint value and squared norm displayed above. The lowest-power term after $n$ differentiations is $(n+m)!x^m/m!$, giving the endpoint value after division by $n!x^m$. For the norm, integrate by parts $n$ times against the same [polynomial](polynomial-split.md); its leading coefficient is $(-1)^n/n!$, so the final [derivative](derivative.md) is $(-1)^n$. All boundary terms vanish and the remaining [Gamma function](gamma-function.md) integral is $(n+m)!/n!$. Normalizing the value at zero to one divides the [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) by $\binom{n+m}{n}$ and its squared norm by the square of that factor. Only when $m=0$ or $n=0$ do these normalizations already coincide.
