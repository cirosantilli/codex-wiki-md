# Extrusion invariance of Loop subdivision

↑ **Parent:** [Loop subdivision surface](loop-subdivision-surface.md)

On an infinite regular triangular grid, height data constant along one edge direction remain constant along that direction under [Loop subdivision](loop-subdivision-surface.md). Across rows the induced rule is $g'_{2j}=(g_{j-1}+6g_j+g_{j+1})/8$, $g'_{2j+1}=(g_j+g_{j+1})/2$, the centered [Cardinal cubic B-spline](cardinal-cubic-b-spline.md) rule.
