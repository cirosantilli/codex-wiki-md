# Kantorovich potential

↑ **Parent:** [Kantorovich duality theorem](kantorovich-duality-theorem.md)

Kantorovich potentials are the functions in the dual of the [Kantorovich optimal transport problem](kantorovich-optimal-transport-problem.md). A feasible pair satisfies $u(x)+v(y)\leq c(x,y)$ and provides a lower bound $\int u\,d\mu+\int v\,d\nu$ for every [transport plan](transport-plan.md). An optimal pair attaining the dual value provides an optimality certificate.
