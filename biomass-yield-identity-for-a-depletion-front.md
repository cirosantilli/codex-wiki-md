# Biomass yield identity for a depletion front

↑ **Parent:** [Growth-factor depletion travelling wave](growth-factor-depletion-travelling-wave.md)

With inhibited [bacterial](bacteria.md) growth $b_t=b_{xx}+gb(1-\eta b)$ and unchanged depletion $g_t=-gb$, a positive [travelling wave](travelling-wave.md) leaving [density](number-density.md) $b_1$ obeys $cb_1=c-\eta\int_{\mathbb R}gb^2\,dy$. This follows by [integrating](integral-split.md) both [travelling-wave equations](travelling-wave.md) between their constant endpoint states. Thus $b_1<1$ for $\eta>0$: the yield behind the [front](travelling-wave.md) is smaller than for uninhibited growth.
