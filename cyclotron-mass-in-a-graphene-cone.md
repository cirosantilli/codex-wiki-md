# Cyclotron mass in a graphene cone

↑ **Parent:** [Dirac valleys in graphene](dirac-valleys-in-graphene.md)

A massless-cone description permits a nonzero [cyclotron effective mass](cyclotron-effective-mass.md). For a circular constant-[energy](energy-split.md) contour of [area](surface-area.md) $S(E)=\pi E^2/(\hbar v_F)^2$, the semiclassical definition gives

$$
m_c=\frac{\hbar^2}{2\pi}\left|\frac{dS}{dE}\right|=\frac{|E_F|}{v_F^2}=\frac{\hbar\sqrt{\pi|n|}}{v_F}.
$$

The vanishing mass parameter in a [Dirac Hamiltonian](dirac-hamiltonian.md) and the finite cyclotron response away from neutrality are different statements. This makes the [temperature](temperature-split.md) dependence of [Shubnikov-de Haas oscillations](shubnikov-de-haas-effect.md) a useful test of the dispersion rather than a measurement of an [electron](electron.md)'s vacuum [rest mass](invariant-mass.md).
