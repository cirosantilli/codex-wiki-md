# Minimum excludant

↑ **Parent:** [Natural number](natural-number.md)

The minimum excludant of a finite set of [nonnegative integers](natural-number.md) is the smallest [nonnegative integer](natural-number.md) absent from the set. For example, $\operatorname{mex}\{0,1,3\}=2$.
