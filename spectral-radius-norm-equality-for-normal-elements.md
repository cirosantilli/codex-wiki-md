# Spectral radius norm equality for normal elements

↑ **Parent:** [Normal element of a C-star algebra](normal-element-of-a-c-star-algebra.md)

For a [Normal element of a C-star algebra](normal-element-of-a-c-star-algebra.md), the [C-star identity](c-star-identity.md) gives $\|a^2\|=\|a\|^2$, and iteration gives $\|a^{2^n}\|=\|a\|^{2^n}$. The [spectral radius formula](spectral-radius-formula.md) then gives $r(a)=\|a\|$. This proves the isometry of the [Gelfand transform](gelfand-representation.md) for a commutative [C-star algebra](c-star-algebra.md) directly from its defining norm identity.
