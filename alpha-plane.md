# Alpha-plane

↑ **Parent:** [Twistor correspondence](twistor-correspondence.md)

An alpha-plane in the compact [twistor correspondence](twistor-correspondence.md) is the set of two-planes containing a fixed line $\ell$ in [twistor space](twistor-space.md). Its affine portion has tangent vectors $u^A\pi^{A'}$ for fixed primed spinor $\pi$, so any two tangent vectors are orthogonal and null. Two distinct compact alpha-planes meet at the unique two-plane spanned by their defining lines; that point can lie at conformal infinity.
