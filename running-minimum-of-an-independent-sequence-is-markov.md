# Running minimum of an independent sequence is Markov

↑ **Parent:** [Markov chain](markov-chain.md)

For [independent random variables](independent-random-variables.md) with a common law, $L_n=\min_{j\leq n}X_j$ satisfies $L_{n+1}=\min(L_n,X_{n+1})$. The next-state law therefore depends on the past only through $L_n$.
