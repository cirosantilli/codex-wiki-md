# Lowest light-cone levels of an open bosonic string

↑ **Parent:** [Open bosonic string mass spectrum](open-bosonic-string-mass-spectrum.md)

The first levels are the [oscillator vacuum](oscillator-vacuum.md), $\alpha_{-1}^I|0\rangle$, and $\alpha_{-2}^I|0\rangle,\alpha_{-1}^I\alpha_{-1}^J|0\rangle$. At level two the transverse [vector representation](vector-representation.md) and [symmetric square](symmetric-square.md) assemble into the massive [symmetric traceless square](symmetric-trace-free-square-of-the-defining-orthogonal-representation.md) of one extra rotation dimension.
