# Polchinski equation

↑ **Parent:** [Wilsonian effective action](wilsonian-effective-action.md)

The [Polchinski equation](polchinski-equation.md) changes an interaction action with its regulated Gaussian covariance so that the normalized [functional integral](functional-measure.md) stays unchanged. In operator notation, $\dot e^{-S_1}=-\dot C:\delta^2e^{-S_1}/2$. The resulting action flow has a product of first [functional derivatives](functional-derivative.md), joining two vertices, and a second derivative, closing two legs into a loop. It generates a [Wilsonian effective action](wilsonian-effective-action.md), which is not the same functional as the [one-particle-irreducible correlation functions](one-particle-irreducible-correlation-function.md) encoded by the [quantum effective action](effective-action.md).
