<h1 id="fundamental-doublet-breaking-of-su-2">Fundamental-doublet breaking of SU(2)</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [SU(2) gauge theory](su-2-gauge-theory.md)

A single [complex scalar field](complex-scalar-field.md) in the two-dimensional [fundamental representation](fundamental-representation.md) with potential $\lambda(\phi^\dagger\phi-v^2/2)^2$, $\lambda,v>0$, has a three-sphere [vacuum manifold](vacuum-manifold.md). The stabilizer in $SU(2)$ of a chosen nonzero vector is trivial, so all three [gauge bosons](gauge-boson.md) acquire the common [mass](mass.md) $gv/2$. In [unitary gauge](unitary-gauge.md), $\phi=(0,v+h)^T/\sqrt2$ leaves one physical radial scalar of squared [mass](mass.md) $2\lambda v^2$. Its couplings are $g^2vhA^2/4+g^2h^2A^2/8-\lambda vh^3-\lambda h^4/4$. The three angular fields become longitudinal vector [polarizations](polarization-waves.md) through the [Higgs mechanism](higgs-mechanism.md).
