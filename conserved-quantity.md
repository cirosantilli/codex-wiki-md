# Conserved quantity

↑ **Parent:** [Integral of motion](integral-of-motion.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conserved_quantity)

A conserved quantity is a quantity that remains constant along the time evolution of a system. In [Hamiltonian mechanics](hamiltonian-mechanics-split.md), a possibly time-dependent phase-space function $F$ is conserved exactly when $\partial_tF+\{F,H\}=0$.
