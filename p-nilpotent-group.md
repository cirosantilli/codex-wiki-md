# p-nilpotent group

↑ **Parent:** [Normal p-complement](normal-p-complement.md)

A finite group is p-nilpotent when it has a [normal p-complement](normal-p-complement.md). This terminology is distinct from having a normal Sylow p-subgroup. A finite group is [nilpotent](nilpotent.md) exactly when it is p-nilpotent for every prime dividing its order.
