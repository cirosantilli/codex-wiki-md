# Selective-area epitaxy

↑ **Parent:** [Epitaxy](epitaxy.md)

Epitaxial crystal growth confined to exposed substrate regions defined by a growth-inhibiting mask. Pattern position and pitch can control wire [nucleation](nucleation.md), while transport and facet-dependent incorporation influence [growth rate](growth-rate.md) and composition.
