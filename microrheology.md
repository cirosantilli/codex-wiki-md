# Microrheology

↑ **Parent:** [Rheology](rheology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microrheology)

Microrheology infers local mechanical response from microscopic probes embedded in a material. Active methods apply a calibrated force and measure displacement; [passive microrheology](passive-microrheology.md) uses thermal fluctuations. Probe size, boundary conditions at the probe, temporal resolution and material heterogeneity determine which continuum response can be inferred.
