# Integrated square of Brownian motion

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md)

The quadratic functional $Q=\int_0^1W_t^2dt$ is finite almost surely. The [Brownian half-integer sine expansion](brownian-half-integer-sine-expansion.md) writes it as a sum of independent scaled squared standard normals. Its mean is $1/2$, and its Laplace transform is determined by a hyperbolic cosine product.
