# Method-of-mixtures calorimetry

↑ **Parent:** [Calorimetry](calorimetry-split.md)

Infer an unknown [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) by thermal contact with a calibrated receiver and a measured common final [temperature](temperature-split.md). The energy lost by hotter components equals the energy gained by colder components, with the [calorimeter](calorimeter.md) and surrounding [heat transfer](heat-transfer.md) included. For appreciably varying heat capacities, use temperature integrals rather than constant $mc\Delta T$ terms.
