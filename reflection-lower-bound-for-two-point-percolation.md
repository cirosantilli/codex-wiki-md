# Reflection lower bound for two-point percolation

↑ **Parent:** [Percolation two-point connection probability](percolation-two-point-connection-probability.md)

For [bond percolation](bond-percolation-split.md) on the [square lattice](square-lattice.md), write $h_n=\mathbb P_p(0\leftrightarrow(n,0))$ and $g_k=\mathbb P_p(0\leftrightarrow\partial[-k,k]^2)$. A maximal-probability boundary [graph vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) can be placed at $(k,j)$ by symmetry. Reflection about $x=k$ identifies its connection [probability](probability.md) from $0$ with that from $(2k,0)$. The [Harris-FKG inequality](harris-fkg-inequality.md) yields $(g_k/(8k))^2\leq h_{2k}\leq g_{2k}$ for $k\geq1$.
