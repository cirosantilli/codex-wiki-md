# Group multiplier automaton

↑ **Parent:** [Automatic structure for a group](automatic-structure-for-a-group.md)

A [group multiplier automaton](group-multiplier-automaton.md) accepts exactly the [padded convolutions of words](padded-convolution-of-words.md) $u\otimes v$ with $u,v\in L$ and $\overline u x=\overline v$. The case $x=\varepsilon$ recognizes equality in the [group](group-split.md), rather than equality of literal [words over an alphabet](string.md).
