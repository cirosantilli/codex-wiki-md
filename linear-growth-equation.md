# Linear growth equation

↑ **Parent:** [Linear growth factor](linear-growth-factor.md)

For pressureless matter on subhorizon scales, the [linear growth factor](linear-growth-factor.md) obeys

$$
\ddot D+2H\dot D-4\pi G\bar\rho_mD=0.
$$

The second term is dilution by [cosmic expansion](expansion-of-the-universe.md), while the final term drives [Jeans instability](jeans-instability.md).
