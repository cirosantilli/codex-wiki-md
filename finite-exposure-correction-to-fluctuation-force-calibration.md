# Finite-exposure correction to fluctuation force calibration

↑ **Parent:** [Transverse-fluctuation calibration of a tensioned tether](transverse-fluctuation-calibration-of-a-tensioned-tether.md)

For an equilibrium overdamped harmonic coordinate with covariance $\langle x(t)x(0)\rangle=V e^{-|t|/\tau}$, a camera exposure $\Delta$ records the average over that interval. Integrating the covariance twice gives $\operatorname{Var}(\bar x)=2V\Delta^{-2}\int_0^\Delta(\Delta-u)e^{-u/\tau}du$. The displayed reduction follows with $s=\Delta/\tau$. In the tether calibration $F=k_BTh/V$, ignoring exposure averaging overestimates the inferred [force](force-split.md). Independent localization-noise [variance](variance-split.md) instead adds to the measured [variance](variance-split.md) and biases it in the opposite direction. Other filters require their actual response functions.
