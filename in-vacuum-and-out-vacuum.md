# In-vacuum and out-vacuum

↑ **Parent:** [Bogoliubov transformation](bogoliubov-transformation.md)

If a background is stationary in the remote past and future, positive-frequency modes in those two regions define an in-vacuum and an out-vacuum. The intervening time dependence generally makes the two mode bases differ by a [Bogoliubov transformation](bogoliubov-transformation.md).
