# Differentiation

↑ **Parent:** [Derivative](derivative.md)

Differentiation computes the [derivative](derivative.md) of a [function](function-split.md). A [difference quotient](difference-quotient.md) approximates a [derivative](derivative.md) using finite increments; its [limit](limit-of-a-function.md) is the [derivative](derivative.md) when the [function](function-split.md) is [differentiable](differentiable-function.md).
