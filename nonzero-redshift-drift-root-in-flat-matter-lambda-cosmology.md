# Nonzero redshift-drift root in flat matter-Lambda cosmology

↑ **Parent:** [Redshift drift](redshift-drift.md)

For positive matter and vacuum fractions with $\Omega_m+\Omega_\Lambda=1$, [redshift drift](redshift-drift.md) vanishes when $s^2=\Omega_ms^3+\Omega_\Lambda$, $s=1+z$. Factoring out the trivial observer root $s=1$ leaves $\Omega_ms^2-\Omega_\Lambda s-\Omega_\Lambda=0$, giving the displayed positive [root of a polynomial](root-of-a-polynomial.md). It lies at a positive [redshift](redshift.md) exactly when $\Omega_m<2/3$. It is independent of the [Hubble constant](hubble-constant.md) at fixed density fractions and is not the [redshift](redshift.md) of the acceleration-to-deceleration transition.
