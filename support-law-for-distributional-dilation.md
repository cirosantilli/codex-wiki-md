# Support law for distributional dilation

↑ **Parent:** [Dilation of a distribution](dilation-of-a-distribution.md)

For $t>0$, the [support of a distribution](support-of-a-distribution.md) satisfies

$$
\operatorname{supp}(\delta_tu)=t^{-1}\operatorname{supp}u.
$$

The duality formula $\langle\delta_tu,\varphi\rangle=t^{-n}\langle u,\varphi(\mathord\cdot/t)\rangle$ proves one inclusion by transporting [test functions](test-function.md); applying the inverse dilation proves the other. For a compact support, the [Paley–Wiener–Schwartz theorem](paley-wiener-schwartz-theorem.md) gives another proof: $\widehat{\delta_tu}(\zeta)=t^{-n}\widehat u(\zeta/t)$ rescales the exponential growth indicator by $1/t$.
