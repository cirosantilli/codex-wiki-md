# Direct optical transition

↑ **Parent:** [Interband transition](interband-transition.md)

A photon-induced [interband transition](interband-transition.md) that approximately preserves the electron's crystal wavevector because the photon momentum is small compared with reciprocal-lattice momenta. Its strength depends on the dipole matrix element and the [joint density of states](joint-density-of-states.md). A [direct band gap](direct-band-gap.md) permits such a transition between the band-edge states, but optical selection rules can still suppress it.
