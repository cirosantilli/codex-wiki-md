# Inertia tensor of a sheared rectangular lamina

↑ **Parent:** [Inertia tensor](inertia-tensor.md)

For a uniform planar lamina with $|x_1-sx_2|\leq a$ and $|x_2|\leq b$, put $u=x_1-sx_2$, $v=x_2$. The [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md) is one, the mass is $M=4ab\sigma$, and symmetry puts the centre of mass at zero. Uniform rectangular moments give $\langle u^2\rangle=a^2/3$, $\langle v^2\rangle=b^2/3$, $\langle uv\rangle=0$. Substitution into $I_{ij}=\int(|x|^2\delta_{ij}-x_ix_j)dm$ yields the displayed [inertia tensor](inertia-tensor.md). The in-plane [principal moments of inertia](principal-moment-of-inertia.md) are $M[a^2+(1+s^2)b^2\pm\sqrt{(a^2+(s^2-1)b^2)^2+4s^2b^4}]/6$; the perpendicular moment is their sum. This separates the geometric shear from the elementary rectangular integrals.
