# Gaussian empirical Gram matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Gram matrix](gram-matrix.md)

For a matrix $G$ with [independent](independent-random-variables.md) standard normal entries, $G^TG/n$ is a [Gaussian empirical Gram matrix](gaussian-empirical-gram-matrix.md) and has [expectation](expected-value.md) equal to the identity. With mean known to be zero it is the raw empirical second moment. Subtracting a sample mean would give a different estimator. On any fixed unit vector its [quadratic form](quadratic-form.md) is a chi-squared variable divided by $n$.
