# Compatible triple

↑ **Parent:** [Compatible almost complex structure](compatible-almost-complex-structure.md)

A compatible triple consists of a [symplectic form](symplectic-form.md), a [compatible almost complex structure](compatible-almost-complex-structure.md), and the [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md) $g(u,v)=\omega(u,Jv)$. Equivalently $g(Ju,v)=\omega(u,v)$.
