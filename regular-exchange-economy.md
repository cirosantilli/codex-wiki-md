# Regular exchange economy

↑ **Parent:** [Exchange economy](exchange-economy.md)

An [exchange economy](exchange-economy.md) is regular when the derivative of its reduced [aggregate excess demand](aggregate-excess-demand.md) has full rank at every equilibrium. For two goods and normalized prices $(p,1)$, this means $Z_p(p)\ne0$ at every zero of first-good excess demand. Regular equilibria are locally isolated and persist smoothly under small parameter changes.
