# Two hinged rods sliding on a floor

↑ **Parent:** [Rigid body dynamics](rigid-body-dynamics.md)

Two equal uniform rods of length $2l$ and mass $M$, one pivoted to the floor and the other endpoint sliding on it, have arch coordinates $B=(2l\cos\theta,2l\sin\theta)$ and $C=(4l\cos\theta,0)$. The [kinetic energy decomposition about the center of mass](kinetic-energy-decomposition-about-the-center-of-mass.md) gives $T=\tfrac43Ml^2(1+3\sin^2\theta)\dot\theta^2$, while gravitational [potential energy](potential-energy.md) is $2Mgl\sin\theta$. [Conservation of energy](conservation-of-energy.md) from rest at angle $\alpha$ gives the displayed equation and hinge impact [speed](speed.md) $\sqrt{6gl\sin\alpha}$, provided contact is maintained. The endpoint's normal reaction becomes negative during this constrained fall if $\sin\alpha>35/54$; a unilateral floor then permits detachment and the constrained formula no longer describes the entire motion.
