# Particle extinction coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Atmospheric extinction](atmospheric-extinction.md)

The total extinction cross-section per unit volume of particles is a length-inverse coefficient. Its path integral is [optical depth](optical-depth.md). It combines scattering and absorption and differs from the dimensionless imaginary part of a [complex refractive index](complex-refractive-index.md). For liquid droplets with size-independent $Q_{\rm ext}$, $\beta_{\rm ext}=3Q_{\rm ext}w/(4\rho_wr_e)$.
