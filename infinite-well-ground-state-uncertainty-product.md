# Infinite-well ground-state uncertainty product

↑ **Parent:** [Infinite square well](infinite-square-well.md)

For the normalized [ground state](ground-state.md) $\sqrt{2/L}\sin(\pi x/L)$ on $0<x<L$, direct position [integrals](integral-split.md) give $\Delta x^2=L^2(1/12-1/(2\pi^2))$. The kinetic [momentum](momentum-split.md) second moment gives $\Delta p^2=\pi^2\hbar^2/L^2$. Their product is about $0.568\hbar$, strictly above the [Heisenberg uncertainty principle](heisenberg-uncertainty-relation.md) bound. Infinite walls do not make this non-Gaussian state a minimum-uncertainty wave packet.
