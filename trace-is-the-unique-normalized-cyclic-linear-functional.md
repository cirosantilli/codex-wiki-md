# Trace is the unique normalized cyclic linear functional

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix trace](matrix-trace.md)

Over $\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$, every [linear functional](linear-functional.md) on all $n\times n$ [matrices](matrix.md) satisfying $f(AB)=f(BA)$ is a scalar multiple of the [trace](matrix-trace.md). The [matrix units](matrix-unit.md) show that the functional vanishes off the diagonal and has equal values on every diagonal unit. The normalization $f(I)=n$ fixes the scalar to one.
