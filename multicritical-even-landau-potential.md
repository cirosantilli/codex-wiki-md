# Multicritical even Landau potential

↑ **Parent:** [Landau free energy](landau-free-energy.md)

For $n\geq2$ and $\lambda_n>0$, this [Landau free energy](landau-free-energy.md) has [mean-field critical exponents](mean-field-critical-exponent.md) $\beta=1/[2(n-1)]$ and $\alpha=(n-2)/(n-1)$ when $r$ crosses zero linearly with [temperature](temperature.md). For $n>2$, obtaining this behavior requires tuning the lower even interactions to zero.
