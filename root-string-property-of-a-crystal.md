# Root-string property of a crystal

↑ **Parent:** [Crystal basis](crystal-basis.md)

For each simple root $\alpha_i$, the $i$-colored edges of a crystal form strings. Along such a string, $\widetilde e_i$ raises the weight by $\alpha_i$ and $\widetilde f_i$ lowers it by $\alpha_i$.
