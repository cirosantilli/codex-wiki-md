# Farkas' lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Conic optimization](conic-optimization.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Farkas'_lemma)

Exactly one of the two displayed systems is feasible. [Closedness of finitely generated cones](closedness-of-finitely-generated-cones.md) and the [Fenchel-Moreau theorem](fenchel-moreau-theorem.md) applied to a cone's [indicator functional](indicator-functional-of-a-constraint-set.md) provide a proof through its polar cone. It yields a nonnegative multiplier certificate when a finite system of linear inequalities is inconsistent.
