# Wavenumber selection by a moving observer of deep-water waves

↑ **Parent:** [Deep-water gravity wave](deep-water-gravity-wave.md)

A deep-water [gravity wave](gravity-wave-split.md) has dispersion $\omega(k)=\sqrt{g|k|}$. An observer moving with nonzero speed $V$ samples Fourier phases $kV\pm\omega(k)$. The [stationary phase method](stationary-phase-method.md) selects the points where their [derivatives](derivative.md) vanish, which gives the displayed wavenumbers. Their [group velocity](group-velocity.md) matches the observer's [velocity](velocity.md). For real even initial displacement with a nonzero Fourier coefficient there, the resulting signal has leading size $t^{-1/2}$ and phase $gt/(4|V|)-\pi/4$. If the initial coefficient vanishes, that leading term is absent; a stationary observer does not select a finite nonzero [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) by this rule.
