# Distributional Cauchy-Riemann lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Cauchy-Riemann equations](cauchy-riemann-equations.md)

A [distribution](distribution-mathematical-analysis.md) $u$ on a plane [open set](open-set.md) with $\partial_{\bar z}u=0$ is represented by a [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function-split.md). Indeed $\Delta u=4\partial_z\partial_{\bar z}u=0$, and [elliptic regularity](elliptic-regularity.md) makes $u$ smooth. For a direct proof, mollify locally with a radial [mollifier](mollifier.md) $\eta_\varepsilon$. The resulting [smooth functions](smooth-function.md) are harmonic. Their [mean value property](mean-value-property-for-harmonic-functions.md) gives $(u*\eta_\varepsilon)*\eta_r=u*\eta_\varepsilon$ on a smaller ball for fixed small $r$. Passing to the [weak convergence of distributions](weak-convergence-of-distributions.md) limit gives $u*\eta_r=u$ there. This proves smoothness without assuming [local integrability](locally-integrable-function.md) beforehand. The ordinary [Cauchy-Riemann equations](cauchy-riemann-equations.md) now prove holomorphy.

The equation $\partial_zu=0$ similarly characterizes [antiholomorphic functions](antiholomorphic-function.md). On the whole plane, the homogeneous solutions are respectively arbitrary [entire functions](entire-function.md) $h(z)$ and the functions $h(\bar z)$ with $h$ entire. Consequently any two [fundamental solutions](fundamental-solution-of-a-linear-differential-operator.md) for one of these [Wirtinger derivatives](wirtinger-derivatives.md) differ by the corresponding homogeneous solution.
