# Periodically reversing Parker dynamo coupling

↑ **Parent:** [Parker dynamo wave](parker-dynamo-wave.md)

For $\dot A=D^2f(t)B$ and $\dot B=iA$, with $f$ alternating between $+1$ and $-1$ for intervals of length $T$, the [monodromy matrix of a periodic linear system](monodromy-matrix-of-a-periodic-linear-system.md) has determinant one and trace $\cosh(\sqrt2DT)+\cos(\sqrt2DT)>2$. Its reciprocal [Floquet multipliers](floquet-multiplier.md) give positive dominant [Floquet growth rate](floquet-growth-rate.md) for $D,T>0$, despite the zero mean of $f$. An exceptional initial state in the stable [eigenspace](eigenspace.md) decays.
