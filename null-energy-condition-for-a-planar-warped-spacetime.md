# Null energy condition for a planar warped spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [Null energy condition](null-energy-condition.md)

In a [planar warped spacetime](planar-warped-spacetime.md), contraction of the [Ricci tensor](ricci-tensor.md) with a [null vector](null-vector.md) in an [orthonormal frame](orthonormal-frame-in-spacetime.md) gives $R_{ab}k^ak^b=-2(\log A)''(k^3)^2$. The [Einstein field equations](einstein-field-equations.md) identify this with $8\pi T_{ab}k^ak^b$; metric terms, including a cosmological constant, vanish in a null contraction. Thus the [null energy condition](null-energy-condition.md) is equivalent to concavity of $\log A$ in the proper transverse coordinate $z$. Null directions confined to a planar slice saturate the condition.
