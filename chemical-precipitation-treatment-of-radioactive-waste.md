# Chemical precipitation treatment of radioactive waste

↑ **Parent:** [Radioactive liquid waste treatment](radioactive-liquid-waste-treatment.md)

Transfer of dissolved contaminants to separable solids by [precipitation](precipitation-chemistry.md), [coprecipitation](coprecipitation.md) or surface uptake. The resulting sludge requires separation and conditioning; residual dissolved [radioactive activity](radioactive-activity.md) must be measured.
