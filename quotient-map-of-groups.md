# Quotient map of groups

↑ **Parent:** [Quotient group](quotient-group.md)

The [quotient map of groups](quotient-map-of-groups.md) sends each [group element](group-element.md) to its [coset](coset.md) modulo a [normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md). It is a [surjective group homomorphism](surjective-group-homomorphism.md), with [group kernel](kernel-of-a-group-homomorphism.md) exactly $N$. In the additive six-element example, it sends $0,3$ to $N$, $1,4$ to $1+N$, and $2,5$ to $2+N$. The [quotient map of groups](quotient-map-of-groups.md) records the identification that produces the new [group](group-split.md).
