# Gauge equivalence of principal connections

↑ **Parent:** [Connection (principal bundle)](connection-principal-bundle.md)

A gauge transformation is an automorphism of a [principal bundle](principal-bundle.md) covering the identity on the base and commuting with its right group action. Pullback relates gauge-equivalent [principal connections](connection-principal-bundle.md). Locally it acts by the displayed formula; the [gauge curvature](gauge-field-strength.md) transforms by conjugation. Gauge functions on overlapping charts satisfy the bundle's transition compatibility. Infinitesimally $g=e^\varepsilon$ gives $\delta A=D_A\varepsilon$.
