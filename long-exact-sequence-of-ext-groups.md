# Long exact sequence of Ext groups

↑ **Parent:** [Ext functor](ext-functor.md)

Applying the contravariant [Hom functor](hom-functor.md) $\operatorname{Hom}_R(-,W)$ to $0\to I\to X\to C\to0$ gives

$$
0\to\operatorname{Hom}(C,W)\to\operatorname{Hom}(X,W)\to\operatorname{Hom}(I,W)\to\operatorname{Ext}^1(C,W)\to\operatorname{Ext}^1(X,W)\to\operatorname{Ext}^1(I,W)\to\operatorname{Ext}^2(C,W)\to\cdots.
$$

The connecting map sends $I\to W$ to its [pushout of a module extension](pushout-of-a-module-extension.md). Its vanishing means that map extends to $X\to W$. For a [hereditary ring](hereditary-ring.md), $\operatorname{Ext}^2(C,W)=0$, making the indicated restriction on first [extension groups](extension-group.md) surjective. The covariant variable has its corresponding long exact sequence.
