# Noncausal stationary autoregression

↑ **Parent:** [Autoregressive model](autoregressive-model.md)

A [weakly stationary process](weakly-stationary-process.md) satisfying an [autoregressive model](autoregressive-model.md) can depend on future driving [white noise](white-noise.md). For $X_t=\phi X_{t-1}+\varepsilon_t$ with $|\phi|>1$, the convergent in the sense of [mean-square convergence](convergence-in-l2.md) solution is $X_t=-\sum_{j\geq1}\phi^{-j}\varepsilon_{t+j}$. Its [autocovariance](autocovariance.md) is $\sigma^2\phi^{-|h|}/(\phi^2-1)$. This is different from a [causal time series](causal-time-series.md).
