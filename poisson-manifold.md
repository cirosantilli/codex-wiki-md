# Poisson manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic geometry](symplectic-geometry-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poisson_manifold)

A Poisson manifold is a smooth manifold with a bilinear bracket on smooth functions that is antisymmetric, obeys the [Jacobi identity](jacobi-identity.md), and is a derivation in each argument. Its Poisson bivector may have nonconstant rank, unlike the inverse of a symplectic form.
