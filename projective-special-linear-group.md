# Projective special linear group

↑ **Parent:** [Special linear group](special-linear-group.md)

The quotient of the [special linear group](special-linear-group.md) by its scalar center, acting faithfully on [projective space](projective-space-split.md). It is a [simple group](simple-group.md) for $n\ge3$, or for $n=2$ and $|F|>3$. [Elementary transvection matrices](elementary-transvection-matrix.md) give generation and perfectness, and the [Iwasawa simplicity lemma](iwasawa-simplicity-lemma.md) applies to the abelian subgroup of transvections with a fixed center.
