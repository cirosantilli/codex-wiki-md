# Ohmic conductor

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical conductivity](electrical-conductivity.md)

A material whose [current density](current-density.md) is proportional to its [electric field](electric-field.md), with proportionality given by its [electrical conductivity](electrical-conductivity.md).
