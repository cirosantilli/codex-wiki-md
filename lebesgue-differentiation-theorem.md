# Lebesgue differentiation theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Measure density](measure-density.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebesgue_differentiation_theorem)

If $f$ is locally [Lebesgue integrable](lebesgue-integrable-function.md) on $\mathbb R^n$, then for [Lebesgue almost every](almost-everywhere.md) $x$,

$$
\lim_{r\downarrow0}\frac1{|B_r(x)|}\int_{B_r(x)}f(y)\,dy=f(x).
$$
