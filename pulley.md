# Pulley

↑ **Parent:** [Classical mechanics](classical-mechanics-split.md)

A wheel guiding a rope or cable so that it can redirect a [tension](tension.md) force. An ideal fixed pulley changes the force direction without multiplying its magnitude. A freely rotating pulley with negligible [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) and friction has equal rope tensions on its two sides; a finite [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) instead relates their difference to its angular [acceleration](acceleration.md) through the net [torque](torque.md).
