# Theory of torsion-free divisible Abelian groups allowing the trivial group

↑ **Parent:** [Theory of nontrivial torsion-free divisible Abelian groups](theory-of-nontrivial-torsion-free-divisible-abelian-groups.md)

Remove the nonzero-model axiom from [DAG](theory-of-nontrivial-torsion-free-divisible-abelian-groups.md). The inclusion $\{0\}\subseteq\mathbb Q$ is then an inclusion of models but is not a [simple closure](simple-closure.md), because $x\ne0$ has a witness only in the larger model. The theory does not have [quantifier elimination](quantifier-elimination.md): all closed group terms are zero, so no quantifier-free sentence distinguishes the two models.
