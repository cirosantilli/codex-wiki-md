# Endpoint force derivative of clamped-free beam energy

↑ **Parent:** [Clamped-free beam under uniform load and endpoint force](clamped-free-beam-under-uniform-load-and-endpoint-force.md)

For the [clamped-free beam under uniform load and endpoint force](clamped-free-beam-under-uniform-load-and-endpoint-force.md), the additional minimized internal energy is

$$
\mathcal E(F)=\frac{F^2L^3}{6A}.
$$

Its [derivative](derivative.md) is the endpoint [displacement](displacement.md) caused by the endpoint [force](force.md):

$$
\frac{d\mathcal E}{dF}=\frac{FL^3}{3A}=h.
$$
