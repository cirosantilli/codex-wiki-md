# Steric stabilization

↑ **Parent:** [Colloid](colloid.md)

[Steric stabilization](steric-stabilization.md) is resistance to particle aggregation supplied by attached or adsorbed molecular layers. Bringing two coated particles close compresses or overlaps the layers, increasing their [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md) through conformational and osmotic effects. It differs from the [depletion force](depletion-force.md) of nonadsorbing dissolved chains.
