# Power Gauss sum over a prime field

↑ **Parent:** [Exponential sum](exponential-sum.md)

For $a\ne0$ in the [finite field](finite-field.md) $\mathbb F_p$, put $d=\gcd(k,p-1)$. The [multiplicative group of a finite field is cyclic](multiplicative-group-of-a-finite-field-is-cyclic.md), and [character orthogonality](character-orthogonality.md) expresses the number of $k$th roots of $y\ne0$ as $\sum_{\chi^d=1}\chi(y)$. The trivial [character](character-of-a-representation.md) contributes $-1$, cancelling the $x=0$ term. Each of the remaining $d-1$ [Gauss sums of Dirichlet characters](gauss-sum-of-a-dirichlet-character.md) has modulus $\sqrt p$, by the [prime-field character Gauss sum identity](prime-field-character-gauss-sum-identity.md). Consequently $|G_{a,p}|\le(d-1)/\sqrt p\le k/\sqrt p$.
