# Limestone

↑ **Parent:** [Sedimentary deposit](sedimentary-deposit.md)

A [sedimentary rock](sedimentary-rock.md) composed mainly of [calcium carbonate](calcium-carbonate.md), commonly [calcite](calcite.md). Many [limestones](limestone.md) form through biological or chemical carbonate accumulation in marine settings, while others form in lakes. Burial, cementation, dissolution and fractures can strongly alter their [porosity](porosity.md) and [porous permeability](permeability-of-a-porous-medium.md).
