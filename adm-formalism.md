# ADM formalism

↑ **Parent:** [Numerical relativity](numerical-relativity-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ADM_formalism)

The Arnowitt-Deser-Misner formalism expresses [general relativity](general-relativity-split.md) as constrained Hamiltonian evolution of a spatial metric and its conjugate momentum after a [3+1 decomposition of spacetime](3-plus-1-decomposition-of-spacetime.md). Lapse and shift enforce the Hamiltonian and momentum constraints. For an asymptotically flat spacetime its boundary Hamiltonian gives the [Arnowitt-Deser-Misner energy](arnowitt-deser-misner-energy.md).
