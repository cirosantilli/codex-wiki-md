# Modal logic

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical logic](mathematical-logic-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modal_logic)

A [modal logic](modal-logic.md) extends propositional connectives by operators such as $\Box$ and its dual $\Diamond A=\neg\Box\neg A$. An interpretation determines their meaning: necessity, knowledge and formal provability lead to different axiom systems. In a relational semantics, $\Box A$ holds at a world when $A$ holds at every accessible world. [Provability logic](provability-logic.md) interprets the box through arithmetically coded proofs.
