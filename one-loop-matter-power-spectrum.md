# One-loop matter power spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Standard perturbation theory in cosmology](standard-perturbation-theory-in-cosmology.md)

For Gaussian linear perturbations, the one-loop matter power spectrum is

$$
P_{\rm 1-loop}=a^2P+a^4(P_{22}+2P_{13}),
$$

where $P_{22}$ correlates two second-order fields and $P_{13}$ correlates a linear field with a third-order field.
