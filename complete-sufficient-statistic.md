# Complete sufficient statistic

↑ **Parent:** [Sufficient statistic](sufficient-statistic.md)

A [sufficient statistic](sufficient-statistic.md) is complete when every integrable function of it with [expectation](expected-value.md) zero for every parameter is almost surely zero for every parameter. Full regular one-parameter [exponential families](exponential-family-split.md) with natural parameter ranging over an open interval provide a standard completeness result. Completeness makes an unbiased function of a [sufficient statistic](sufficient-statistic.md) unique, giving the [Lehmann–Scheffé theorem](lehmann-scheffe-theorem.md).
