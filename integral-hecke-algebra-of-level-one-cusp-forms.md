# Integral Hecke algebra of level-one cusp forms

↑ **Parent:** [Hecke operator](hecke-operator.md)

This is the subring generated by the [Hecke operators](hecke-operator.md) $T_n$ and the integer scalars acting on level-one [cusp forms](cusp-form.md). It preserves the lattice of [cusp forms](cusp-form.md) with integral [Fourier coefficients of a composite-index Hecke operator](fourier-coefficients-of-a-composite-index-hecke-operator.md). It is distinct from the generic [Hecke algebra](hecke-algebra.md) attached to a [Coxeter system](coxeter-system.md).
