# Orthogonal invariance of the Euclidean norm

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean norm](euclidean-norm.md)

For an [orthogonal matrix](orthogonal-matrix.md) $Q$, the identity $Q^TQ=I$ gives $\|Qx\|_2^2=x^TQ^TQx=x^Tx$. Thus multiplication by $Q$ preserves the [Euclidean norm](euclidean-norm.md). Applying this on both input and output sides proves $\|UAV^T\|_2=\|A\|_2$ for [orthogonal matrices](orthogonal-matrix.md) $U,V$ and the Euclidean [operator norm](operator-norm.md). The analogous invariance of the [Frobenius norm](frobenius-norm.md) follows by summing column lengths or by a [trace](matrix-trace.md) calculation.
