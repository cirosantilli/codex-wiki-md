# L2 norm

↑ **Parent:** [Lp norm](lp-norm.md)

The $L^2$ norm is

$$
\lVert f\rVert_2=\left(\int|f|^2\right)^{1/2}.
$$

It is induced by the $L^2$ inner product and is the continuous analogue of the [Euclidean norm](euclidean-norm.md).
