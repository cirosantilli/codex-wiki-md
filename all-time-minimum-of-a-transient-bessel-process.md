# All-time minimum of a transient Bessel process

↑ **Parent:** [Bessel power local martingale](bessel-power-local-martingale.md)

For a [Bessel process](bessel-process.md) of dimension $\delta>2$ started at $x>0$, its [scale function of a one-dimensional diffusion](scale-function-stochastic-processes.md) gives $\mathbb P_x(\tau_r<\infty)=(r/x)^{\delta-2}$ for $0<r<x$. In particular, $\inf_{t\geq0}X_t>0$ almost surely. The minimum is attained because the process tends to infinity; the hitting formula alone already implies positivity of the infimum.
