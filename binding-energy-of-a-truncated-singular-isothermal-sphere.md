# Binding energy of a truncated singular isothermal sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Singular isothermal sphere](singular-isothermal-sphere.md)

For $\rho=k/(2\pi Gr^2)$ truncated at $r_0$, the [enclosed mass](enclosed-mass.md) is $M(r)=2kr/G$. The shell-assembly integral gives $W=-\int GM(r)\,dM/r=-GM_0^2/r_0$. The origin has divergent density but finite total mass and [binding energy](binding-energy.md).
