# Shear-wave velocity

↑ **Parent:** [Seismic velocity](seismic-velocity.md)

The propagation [speed](speed.md) of a shear [elastic wave](elastic-wave.md). In an ideal isotropic elastic medium it is $V_s=\sqrt{\mu/\rho}$, where $\mu$ is [shear modulus](shear-modulus.md) and $\rho$ [mass density](density.md). [Seismic anisotropy](seismic-anisotropy.md), frequency-dependent relaxation and [melt](geological-melt.md) can complicate the inference from an observed [shear-wave velocity](shear-wave-velocity.md) to [temperature](temperature-split.md) or composition.
