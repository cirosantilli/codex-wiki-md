<h1 id="line-plane-incidence-in-plucker-coordinates">Line-plane incidence in Plücker coordinates</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Plücker coordinates](plucker-coordinates.md)

The intersection of a line in [Plücker coordinates](plucker-coordinates.md) with a plane covector $F$ is the first displayed homogeneous vector: it lies in the span of the defining points and its contraction with $F$ is zero by antisymmetry. A zero vector means the whole line is contained in the plane. Dually, the plane through the line and a point or ideal direction $D$ is given by the second formula; it vanishes if $D$ is already on the line. These operations require no Euclidean lengths or angles.
