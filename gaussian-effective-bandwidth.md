# Gaussian effective bandwidth

↑ **Parent:** [Effective bandwidth](effective-bandwidth.md)

For a [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md) $N(\lambda,\sigma^2)$, the logarithm of its [moment-generating function](moment-generating-function.md) is $s\lambda+s^2\sigma^2/2$. Dividing by $s>0$ gives its [effective bandwidth](effective-bandwidth.md). Independent demands add these bandwidths. For total mean $m$ and positive total [variance](variance-split.md) $v$, optimizing a [Chernoff bound](chernoff-bound.md) with target $e^{-\gamma}$, $\gamma>0$, yields the sufficient margin $C\geq m+\sqrt{2\gamma v}$.
