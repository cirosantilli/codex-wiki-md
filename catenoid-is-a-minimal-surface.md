# Catenoid is a minimal surface

↑ **Parent:** [Catenoid](catenoid.md)

For the standard catenoid, the two [principal curvatures](principal-curvature.md) are opposite, so its [mean curvature](mean-curvature.md) vanishes. Its [Gaussian curvature](gaussian-curvature.md) is $K=-\operatorname{sech}^4u<0$, which also shows that no neighbourhood is [locally isometric](local-isometry.md) to a plane, cylinder, or regular part of a cone, all of which have zero Gaussian curvature.
