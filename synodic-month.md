# Synodic month

↑ **Parent:** [Lunar phase](lunar-phase.md)

A [synodic month](synodic-month.md) is the [synodic period](synodic-period.md) of the [Moon](moon.md) relative to the [Sun](sun.md), about $29.53059$ days on average. A [lunation](synodic-month.md) is commonly measured from one new Moon to the next. Actual [lunations](synodic-month.md) vary, so the constant-speed relation derived in [lunar phase](lunar-phase.md) is useful for understanding the mean cycle rather than predicting exact dates. [Fred Espenak, NASA: the synodic month and lunation](https://eclipse.gsfc.nasa.gov/SEhelp/moonorbit.html).
