# Particle and antiparticle occupation operators of a Dirac field

↑ **Parent:** [Mode expansion of a Dirac field](mode-expansion-of-a-dirac-field.md)

The positive occupation operators are $N_e=\sum_s\int d^3p\,a_{\mathbf p}^{s\dagger}a_{\mathbf p}^s/(2\pi)^3$ and $N_{\bar e}=\sum_s\int d^3p\,b_{\mathbf p}^{s\dagger}b_{\mathbf p}^s/(2\pi)^3$. Both commute with the free Hamiltonian. The [normal-ordered](normal-ordering.md) local density integral $\int:\psi^\dagger\psi:\,d^3x$ is their difference, not their sum. It generates the global phase symmetry. For electron charge $-e$, electric charge is $-eQ$. Pair creation can change total occupation while preserving this net charge.
