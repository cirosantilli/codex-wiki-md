# Electron Yukawa suppression of Higgs production

↑ **Parent:** [Higgs boson](higgs-boson.md)

With the conventional electroweak vacuum value $v\simeq246\,\mathrm{GeV}$, the [electron](electron.md) [Yukawa coupling](yukawa-interaction.md) is $y_e\simeq2.9\times10^{-6}$ and the physical Higgs-electron vertex coefficient is $m_e/v\simeq2.1\times10^{-6}$. Direct s-channel Higgs production in [electron-positron annihilation](electron-positron-annihilation.md) is consequently very small away from a compensating resonance enhancement. This suppression belongs to the coupling at the initial vertex, not to a prohibition on the [Higgs boson](higgs-boson.md) coupling to [W bosons](w-boson.md).
