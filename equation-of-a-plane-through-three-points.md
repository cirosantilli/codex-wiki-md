# Equation of a plane through three points

↑ **Parent:** [Plane](plane.md)

For three non-collinear points with position [vectors](vector.md) $\mathbf a,\mathbf b,\mathbf c$, a nonzero [normal vector](normal-vector.md) is

$$
\mathbf N=(\mathbf b-\mathbf a)\times(\mathbf c-\mathbf a).
$$

The [plane](plane.md) is the set of $\mathbf r$ satisfying $(\mathbf r-\mathbf a)\cdot\mathbf N=0$. Expanding the [cross product](cross-product.md) and using the [scalar triple product](scalar-triple-product.md) gives

$$
\mathbf r\cdot(\mathbf a\times\mathbf b+\mathbf b\times\mathbf c+\mathbf c\times\mathbf a)
=\mathbf a\cdot(\mathbf b\times\mathbf c).
$$

Non-collinearity is essential: otherwise $\mathbf N=0$ and the equation fails to select a unique plane.
