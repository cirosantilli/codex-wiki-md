# Puppe sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Mapping cone (topology)](mapping-cone-topology.md)

Successive mapping cones of a based map give this sequence up to [homotopy](homotopy.md). Applying based maps into a space gives an exact sequence of [pointed sets](pointed-set.md) in the opposite direction. The exactness statement at $[X,Y]$ says that a map whose restriction to $A$ is [null-homotopic](null-homotopic-map.md) extends over $C_i$ using the chosen null-homotopy.
