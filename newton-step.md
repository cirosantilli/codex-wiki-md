# Newton step

↑ **Parent:** [Newton's method in optimization](newton-s-method-in-optimization.md)

A Newton step for [Newton's method in optimization](newton-s-method-in-optimization.md) is the [displacement](displacement.md) $s$ solving the displayed [linear system](system-of-linear-equations.md), where $H$ is the [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) and $\nabla f$ the [gradient](gradient.md). The next iterate is $p+s$. This makes the first order [Taylor expansion](taylor-expansion.md) of the [gradient](gradient.md) at the new point vanish. An invertible [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) determines a unique step; it need not give a descent direction when the [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) is indefinite.
