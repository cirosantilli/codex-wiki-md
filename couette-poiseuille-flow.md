# Couette-Poiseuille flow

↑ **Parent:** [Couette flow](couette-flow.md)

[Couette-Poiseuille flow](couette-poiseuille-flow.md) superposes wall-driven shear and pressure-driven parabolic flow between no-slip parallel plates. With one plate moving at $U$, separation $a$, and $dp/dx=-\rho\nu G$, the displayed profile has volume flux $Ua/2+Ga^3/12$ per unit width. Requiring zero flux gives $G=-6U/a^2$ and $u=U[3(y/a)^2-2y/a]$, with a pressure gradient induced by return-flow constraints.
