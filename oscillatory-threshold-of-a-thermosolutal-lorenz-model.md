# Oscillatory threshold of a thermosolutal Lorenz model

↑ **Parent:** [Lorenz model of thermosolutal convection](lorenz-model-of-thermosolutal-convection.md)

Linearization of the [Lorenz model of thermosolutal convection](lorenz-model-of-thermosolutal-convection.md) at conduction yields $(\lambda+\sigma)(\lambda+1)(\lambda+\tau)-\sigma r(\lambda+\tau)+\sigma r_s(\lambda+1)=0$. A [Hopf bifurcation](hopf-bifurcation.md) requires the cubic coefficient identity $a_3=a_1a_2$ and positive frequency squared. Its threshold is the stated $r_H$, with $\omega^2=\sigma r_s(1-\tau)/(\sigma+1)-\tau^2>0$. If $r_s=\beta^2\tau$ and $\delta=\sigma/(1+\sigma)$, then $r_H=1+\tau/\delta+\beta^2\tau\delta+O(\tau^2)$. Comparing with the [steady fold of a thermosolutal Lorenz model](steady-fold-of-a-thermosolutal-lorenz-model.md) gives a leading nonnegative difference $\tau(\beta\delta-1)^2/\delta$ for $\beta\ge0$.
