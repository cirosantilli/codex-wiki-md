# Pauli multiplication identity

↑ **Parent:** [Pauli matrices](pauli-matrices.md)

Direct multiplication of the three [Pauli matrices](pauli-matrices.md) gives $X^2=Y^2=Z^2=I$, $XY=iZ$ and their cyclic permutations, with reversed products changing sign. Consequently $(\mathbf a\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma)(\mathbf b\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma)=(\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b)I+i(\mathbf a\times\mathbf b)\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma$. In particular $(\mathbf s\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma)^2=|\mathbf s|^2I$, which gives the [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $(1\pm|\mathbf s|)/2$ of a qubit [density matrix](density-matrix-split.md).
