# Many-one reduction

↑ **Parent:** [Computability theory](computability-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Many-one_reduction)

A many-one reduction $A\leq_mB$ is a total computable $f$ satisfying $x\in A$ exactly when $f(x)\in B$.
