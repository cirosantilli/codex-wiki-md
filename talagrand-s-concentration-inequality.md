<h1 id="talagrand-s-concentration-inequality">Talagrand's concentration inequality</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Concentration inequality](concentration-inequality.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talagrand's_concentration_inequality)

[Talagrand's concentration inequality](talagrand-s-concentration-inequality.md) gives strong concentration in product spaces using convex distance. If $A$ has positive product probability, its convex-distance enlargement has Gaussian tails. A [Talagrand concentration inequality for certifiable functions](talagrand-concentration-inequality-for-certifiable-functions.md) follows by bounding this distance using bounded differences and small certificates.
