# Curvature-blowup criterion for inextendibility of a surface

↑ **Parent:** [Geodesic incompleteness](geodesic-incompleteness.md)

Let $S\subset\mathbb R^3$ be a smooth surface. Suppose its [Gaussian curvature](gaussian-curvature.md) tends to infinity in absolute value along every [maximal geodesic](maximal-geodesic.md) with a finite affine-parameter endpoint. Then $S$ is an [inextendible embedded surface](inextendible-embedded-surface.md): a smooth extension would let some geodesic reach the extension boundary in finite time while its curvature remained locally bounded.
