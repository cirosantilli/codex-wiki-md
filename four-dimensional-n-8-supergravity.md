<h1 id="four-dimensional-n-8-supergravity">Four-dimensional N=8 supergravity</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Supergravity](supergravity.md)

Four-dimensional $\mathcal N=8$ [supergravity](supergravity.md) has eight independent [Weyl spinor](weyl-spinor.md) [supercharges](supersymmetry-generator.md). Its [supergravity multiplet](supergravity-multiplet.md) has [helicities](helicity.md) from $2$ to $-2$, with multiplicities $1,8,28,56,70,56,28,8,1$.
