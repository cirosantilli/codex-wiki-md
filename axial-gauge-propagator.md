# Axial-gauge propagator

↑ **Parent:** [Axial gauge](axial-gauge.md)

In strict [axial gauge](axial-gauge.md), the [gauge-boson propagator](gauge-boson-propagator.md) obeys $n^\mu D_{\mu\nu}(k)=0$ and contains poles at $n\cdot k=0$. Its contraction with conserved external currents removes the gauge-choice terms. Consistent calculations require an [axial-gauge pole prescription](axial-gauge-pole-prescription.md).
