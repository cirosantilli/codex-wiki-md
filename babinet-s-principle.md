<h1 id="babinet-s-principle">Babinet's principle</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar diffraction theory](scalar-diffraction-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Babinet's_principle)

In linear [scalar diffraction theory](scalar-diffraction-theory.md), complementary aperture transmissions $t$ and $1-t$ give complex fields $U_t+U_{1-t}=U_0$, where $U_0$ is the unobstructed field under identical illumination. Away from the direct beam in [Fraunhofer diffraction](fraunhofer-diffraction.md), their diffracted fields differ by a sign and have equal intensity. Intensities do not generally add: in particular, complementary on-axis [Fresnel diffraction](fresnel-diffraction.md) patterns need not have equal intensity. The ideal scalar statement does not incorporate vector boundary details at a real conducting edge.
