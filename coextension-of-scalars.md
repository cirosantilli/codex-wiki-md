# Coextension of scalars

↑ **Parent:** [Change of rings](change-of-rings.md)

For a [ring homomorphism](ring-homomorphism.md) $R\to A$ and a left $R$-[module](module-mathematics.md) $M$, the left $A$-action on $\operatorname{Hom}_R(A,M)$ is $(a\cdot h)(b)=h(ba)$. This construction is right adjoint to [restriction of scalars](restriction-of-scalars.md).
