# John Schulman

↑ **Parent:** [List of computer scientists](list-of-computer-scientists.md)

[Computer scientist](computer-scientist.md) and coauthor of [Gradient Estimation Using Stochastic Computation Graphs by John Schulman and others](gradient-estimation-using-stochastic-computation-graphs-by-john-schulman-and-others.md). The 2015 paper gives a common framework for [score-function gradient estimators](score-function-gradient-estimator.md) and [pathwise gradients](reparameterization-gradient.md).
