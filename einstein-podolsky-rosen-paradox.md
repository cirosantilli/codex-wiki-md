<h1 id="einstein-podolsky-rosen-paradox">Einstein–Podolsky–Rosen paradox</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Foundations of quantum mechanics](foundations-of-quantum-mechanics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Einstein–Podolsky–Rosen_paradox)

The EPR argument uses correlations of an entangled state and a locality assumption to challenge the completeness of the quantum description. Its [Einstein–Podolsky–Rosen criterion of reality](einstein-podolsky-rosen-criterion-of-reality.md) identifies quantities predictable with certainty without disturbing a distant system as elements of physical reality.
