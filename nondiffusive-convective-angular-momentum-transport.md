# Nondiffusive convective angular momentum transport

↑ **Parent:** [Reynolds stress](reynolds-stress.md)

Rotating anisotropic [convection](convection.md) can produce an azimuthal-meridional [velocity](velocity.md) correlation even without a gradient of the mean rotation rate. Its [Reynolds stress](reynolds-stress.md) transports [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) and can drive [differential rotation](differential-rotation.md), whereas a purely viscous closure only erases shear. Energy comes from the thermally driven [convection](convection.md); the [Coriolis force](coriolis-force.md) changes the correlations but does no work on a parcel.
