# Creatine kinase

↑ **Parent:** [Enzyme](enzyme-split.md)

[Creatine kinase](creatine-kinase.md) catalyses reversible transfer between phosphocreatine and ADP to yield creatine and ATP. Inferring free ADP from this reaction requires an appropriate equilibrium model and information about pH, magnesium and the creatine pool.
