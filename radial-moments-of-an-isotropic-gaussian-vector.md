# Radial moments of an isotropic Gaussian vector

↑ **Parent:** [Chi-squared distribution](chi-squared-distribution.md)

For $d$ independent centered [normal random variables](gaussian-random-variable.md) of common [variance](variance-split.md) $v$, $|\mathbf X|^2/v$ has a [chi-squared distribution](chi-squared-distribution.md) with $d$ degrees of freedom. Its density gives the displayed moments by a [Gamma integral](gamma-integral.md). In three dimensions, $\mathbb E|\mathbf X|^{2n}=(2n+1)!!v^n$. Radial odd moments need not vanish, although any odd Cartesian product does.
