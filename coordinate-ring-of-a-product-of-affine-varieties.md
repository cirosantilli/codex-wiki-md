# Coordinate ring of a product of affine varieties

↑ **Parent:** [Affine algebraic set](affine-algebraic-set.md)

For [affine varieties](affine-algebraic-set.md) $X,Y$ over an [algebraically closed field](algebraically-closed-field.md) $k$, their [product in a category](product-category-theory.md) is affine, with [coordinate ring](coordinate-ring.md) $A\otimes_k B$, where $A=k[X]$ and $B=k[Y]$. Presenting $A=k[x]/J$ and $B=k[y]/L$ identifies this ring with $k[x,y]/(J,L)$. If both varieties are irreducible, this is an [integral domain](integral-domain.md): express a nonzero tensor $F$ using linearly independent second factors. The points $x$ where its specialization $F_x$ is nonzero form a nonempty open subset of $X$. For two nonzero tensors $F,G$, choose $x$ in the intersection of these open subsets; then $F_xG_x\ne0$ in the domain $B$. The [Hilbert Nullstellensatz](hilbert-nullstellensatz.md) identifies $(J,L)$ with the defining ideal of $X\times Y$. The universal property follows from the universal property of the [tensor product of modules](tensor-product-of-modules.md) as a tensor product of commutative algebras.
