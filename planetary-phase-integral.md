# Planetary phase integral

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric albedo](geometric-albedo.md)

The phase integral converts a monochromatic [geometric albedo](geometric-albedo.md) into the corresponding reflected power fraction. The bolometric [Bond albedo](bond-albedo.md) is the incident-spectrum-weighted average of $q_\lambda A_{g,\lambda}$.
