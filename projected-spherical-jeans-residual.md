# Projected spherical Jeans residual

↑ **Parent:** [Spherical Jeans equation](spherical-jeans-equation.md)

For a spherical nonrotating tracer with [luminosity density](luminosity-density.md) $\lambda$, radial [velocity dispersion](velocity-dispersion.md) $\sigma_r$, enclosed [luminosity](luminosity.md) $L(r)$, and constant [mass-to-light ratio](mass-to-light-ratio.md) $\Upsilon$, let $q=\lambda\sigma_r^2$ and $\gamma=G\Upsilon$. Define

$$
A(R)=\int_R^\infty\frac{q(r)r\,dr}{\sqrt{r^2-R^2}},\qquad p(R)=\mu(R)\sigma_p^2(R)-\gamma R^2\int_R^\infty\frac{\lambda(r)L(r)\,dr}{r^2\sqrt{r^2-R^2}}.
$$

The [Spherical Jeans equation](spherical-jeans-equation.md) and the projected second [stellar velocity moment](stellar-velocity-moment.md) give $p=2A+RA'$. To differentiate without a singular lower-end contribution, write $A(R)=\int_0^\infty q(\sqrt{R^2+z^2})\,dz$. If $R^2A$ vanishes at both endpoints, $\int_0^\infty Rp(R)\,dR=0$. A finite outer aperture instead retains its boundary stress term.
