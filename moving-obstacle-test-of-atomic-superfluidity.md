# Moving-obstacle test of atomic superfluidity

↑ **Parent:** [Superfluidity](superfluidity.md)

A repulsive optical obstacle probes dissipation by being moved through a condensate. Heating as a function of speed tests low-speed flow stability; stationary-obstacle and thermal-cloud controls help separate other heating mechanisms. The measured onset need not equal the homogeneous [critical velocity of a homogeneous condensate](critical-velocity-of-a-homogeneous-condensate.md) because density gradients, obstacle shape and vortex nucleation matter.
