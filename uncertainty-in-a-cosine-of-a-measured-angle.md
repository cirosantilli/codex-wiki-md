# Uncertainty in a cosine of a measured angle

↑ **Parent:** [Propagation of uncertainty](propagation-of-uncertainty.md)

For $S=A\cos(q\theta)$ with [independent](independent-random-variables.md) [standard uncertainties](standard-uncertainty.md), linear propagation gives $\sigma_S^2=\cos^2(q\theta)\sigma_A^2+[qA\sin(q\theta)]^2\sigma_\theta^2$. Angular uncertainty must be in radians when using this derivative. [Covariance](covariance.md) adds cross terms for correlated inputs. Strict error bounds instead add absolute first-order contributions; they do not justify Gaussian significance tests.
