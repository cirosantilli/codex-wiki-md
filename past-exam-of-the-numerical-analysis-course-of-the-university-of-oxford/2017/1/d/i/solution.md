At $q=2$, the preceding [tensor-product quadrature](../../../../../tensor-product-quadrature.md) gives

$$
\boxed{Q(g)=\frac{1+1}{36}=\frac1{18}=I(g).}
$$

Both factors of the [separable integrand](../../../../../separable-integrand.md) are [quadratic polynomials](../../../../../quadratic-polynomial.md), so exactness follows directly from the construction of [Simpson's rule](../../../../../simpson-s-rule.md).
