# 2021

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the numerical analysis course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Source: [Oxford Part A, A7 Numerical Analysis, A13759W1, 24 June 2021; parent PDF pages 128–131, paper pages 1–4](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/bsc_mathematics_part_a_examinati/response/2820674/attach/3/FOI%20202410%20944%20Data.pdf#page=128). These original solutions cover all three questions, all nine lettered parts and all twenty-one answer units, including every optional question. Each question carries 25 marks; the examination counted the best two answers. The [local truncation error](../local-truncation-error.md) in Question 3(a) is divided by the [step size](../step-size.md). In Question 3(c), the leading [coefficient](../coefficient.md) of a genuine $k$-step [linear multistep method](../linear-multistep-method-split.md) is understood to be nonzero.
