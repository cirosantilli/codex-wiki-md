# 2023

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the numerical analysis course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Source: [Oxford Part A, A7 Numerical Analysis, A13759W1, 23 June 2023; parent PDF pages 135–137, paper pages 1–3](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/bsc_mathematics_part_a_examinati/response/2820674/attach/3/FOI%20202410%20944%20Data.pdf#page=135). These original solutions cover all three questions and all eleven lettered subparts. In Question 1(d), the unrefined [Gershgorin discs](../gershgorin-disc.md) alone are inconclusive for the shift $5$; a further exact calculation resolves it. In Question 2(c), clearing denominators produces a [linear system](../system-of-linear-equations.md) whose solution need not define a [rational interpolant](../rational-interpolant.md) at every [interpolation node](../interpolation-node.md). Question 3 uses the paper's normalized convention for the [local truncation error](../local-truncation-error.md).
