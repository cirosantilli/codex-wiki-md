↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [norm](../../../../norm.md) induced by the specified [inner product](../../../../inner-product-split.md) is the [L2 norm](../../../../l2-norm.md)

$$
\boxed{\|u\|=\sqrt{(u,u)}=\left(\int_{-1}^1u(x)^2\,dx\right)^{1/2}.}
$$

A [polynomial](../../../../polynomial-split.md) $p_n\in\Pi_n$ is a best [polynomial least-squares approximation](../../../../polynomial-least-squares-approximation.md) when

$$
\boxed{\|u-p_n\|=\min_{q\in\Pi_n}\|u-q\|.}
$$

Minimizing this [norm](../../../../norm.md) is equivalent to minimizing its square, the integrated squared error. Continuity ensures that a function with zero squared [norm](../../../../norm.md) is identically zero: a nonzero value would give a neighbourhood on which the square has a positive integral.
