<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

By [parity preservation by polynomial least-squares projection](../../../../parity-preservation-by-polynomial-least-squares-projection.md), $p_2(x)=\beta x$: the constant and quadratic coefficients vanish. The remaining [normal equation](../../../../normal-equation.md) is

$$
0=\int_{-1}^1(x^{1969}-\beta x)x\,dx=\frac2{1971}-\beta\frac23.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{p_2(x)=\frac3{1971}x=\frac{x}{657}.}
$$

The residual is automatically [orthogonal](../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) to $1$ and $x^2$ by parity and to $x$ by construction, so it is orthogonal to every element of $\Pi_2$. This checks the full [linear least squares](../../../../linear-least-squares.md) condition, rather than only a necessary coefficient equation.
