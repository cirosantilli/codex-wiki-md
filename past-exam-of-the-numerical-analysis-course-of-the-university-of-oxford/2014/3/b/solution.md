↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The second theorem, or [Gershgorin eigenvalue counting](../../../../gershgorin-eigenvalue-counting.md), states: **if a union of $k$ indexed Gershgorin discs is disjoint from all remaining discs, it contains exactly $k$ eigenvalues, counted with algebraic multiplicity.** In particular, an isolated [Gershgorin disc](../../../../gershgorin-disc.md) contains exactly one simple [eigenvalue](../../../../eigenvalue.md). The separation condition is essential; merely drawing overlapping [discs](../../../../topological-disc.md) does not assign one [eigenvalue](../../../../eigenvalue.md) to each.
