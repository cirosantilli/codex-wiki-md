<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [determinant](../../../../determinant.md) is invariant under [transpose](../../../../transpose.md). Therefore the [characteristic polynomials](../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) coincide:

$$
\boxed{\det(\lambda I-A^T)=\det((\lambda I-A)^T)=\det(\lambda I-A).}
$$

Their [polynomial roots](../../../../root-of-a-polynomial.md), including their [algebraic multiplicities](../../../../algebraic-multiplicity.md), are the same, so $A$ and $A^T$ have the same [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md). This allows column sums as well as row sums to supply [Gershgorin discs](../../../../gershgorin-disc.md) for the same [spectrum](../../../../spectrum-functional-analysis.md).
