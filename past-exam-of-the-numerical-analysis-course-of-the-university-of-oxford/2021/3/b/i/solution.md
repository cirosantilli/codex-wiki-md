<h1 id="3/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $f(x,y)=-xy^2$, the [explicit Euler method](../../../../../euler-method.md) evaluates the right-hand side at the known point $(x_n,y_n)$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{y_{n+1}=y_n-hx_ny_n^2=y_n(1-hx_ny_n),\qquad y_0=1.}
$$

This update requires no [root finding](../../../../../root-finding.md).
