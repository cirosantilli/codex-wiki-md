<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A real square [matrix](../../../../matrix.md) $Q$ is an [orthogonal matrix](../../../../orthogonal-matrix.md) when $Q^TQ=I$, equivalently its columns form an [orthonormal basis](../../../../orthonormal-basis.md). Here $Q^T$ is the [transpose](../../../../transpose.md) and $I$ the [identity matrix](../../../../identity-matrix.md). For every [vector](../../../../vector.md) $x$,

$$
\|Qx\|_2^2=(Qx)^T(Qx)=x^TQ^TQx=x^Tx=\|x\|_2^2.
$$

Taking nonnegative [square roots](../../../../square-root.md) proves

$$
\boxed{\|Qx\|_2=\|x\|_2.}
$$

Thus an [orthogonal matrix](../../../../orthogonal-matrix.md) preserves the [Euclidean norm](../../../../euclidean-norm.md).
