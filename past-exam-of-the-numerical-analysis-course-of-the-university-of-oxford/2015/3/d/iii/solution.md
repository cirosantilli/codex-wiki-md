<h1 id="3/d/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [orthonormal](../../../../../orthonormal-set.md) columns satisfy $\|a\|_2=\|b\|_2=1$ and $a^Tb=0$. By part (i), $Q_1a=e_1$. A product of [orthogonal matrices](../../../../../orthogonal-matrix.md) is an [orthogonal matrix](../../../../../orthogonal-matrix.md), since $Q_1^TQ_1=I$. Consequently

$$
0=a^Tb=(Q_1a)^T(Q_1b)=e_1^TQ_1b.
$$

The last expression is the first coordinate of the transformed second column. Thus **the first row is**

$$
\boxed{(Q_1A)_{1,:}=(1,0).}
$$

The one in the first entry uses the nonnegative retained-coordinate convention. A different consistent sign convention could give $(-1,0)$ instead; the second entry must still be zero by preservation of the [inner product](../../../../../inner-product-split.md).
