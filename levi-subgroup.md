# Levi subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Parabolic subgroup](parabolic-subgroup.md)

A [Levi subgroup](levi-subgroup.md) appears in a group version of a [Levi decomposition](levi-decomposition.md). A Levi subgroup is a reductive complement to the unipotent radical of a parabolic subgroup.
