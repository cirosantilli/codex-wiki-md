# Daniel Gottesman

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum mechanics physicist](quantum-mechanics-physicist-split.md)

ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Gottesman)

[Physicist](physicist-split.md) whose work includes the stabilizer formalism for [quantum error correction](quantum-error-correction-split.md). His [The Heisenberg Representation of Quantum Computers by Daniel Gottesman](the-heisenberg-representation-of-quantum-computers-by-daniel-gottesman.md) develops the [Pauli group](pauli-group.md) description of [Clifford circuits](clifford-circuit.md). [Verified institutional profile](https://quics.umd.edu/people/daniel-gottesman).
