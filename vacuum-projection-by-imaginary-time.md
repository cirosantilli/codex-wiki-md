# Vacuum projection by imaginary time

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar field path integral](scalar-field-path-integral.md)

Long imaginary-time evolution multiplies an [energy eigenstate](energy-eigenstate.md) by $e^{-TE_n}$. After normalization it projects onto the lowest energy component with nonzero initial overlap, or onto the lowest-energy subspace if the vacuum is degenerate. This provides vacuum boundary conditions for a [path integral](path-integral.md) and is related to the [Feynman i-epsilon prescription](feynman-i-epsilon-prescription.md).
