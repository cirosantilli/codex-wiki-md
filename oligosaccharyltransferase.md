# Oligosaccharyltransferase

↑ **Parent:** [Enzyme](enzyme-split.md)

[oligosaccharyltransferase](oligosaccharyltransferase.md) transfers an assembled oligosaccharide from a lipid-linked donor to a [protein](protein-split.md) acceptor. Recognition of an accessible acceptor sequence is distinct from synthesis of the donor.
