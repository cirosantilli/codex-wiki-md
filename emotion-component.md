# Emotion component

↑ **Parent:** [Emotion](emotion.md)

An aspect of an emotional episode, such as [cognitive appraisal](cognitive-appraisal.md), felt experience, bodily regulation, [facial movement](facial-movement.md) or readiness to act. Components can vary partly independently. A theory must state which components its evidence actually measures.
