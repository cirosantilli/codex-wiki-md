# Queue (queueing theory)

↑ **Parent:** [Queueing theory](queueing-theory-split.md)

A queue consists of customers or jobs waiting for or receiving service. An arrival process adds customers and a service rule specifies which waiting customer is served next. A stochastic model of these events is studied in [queueing theory](queueing-theory-split.md); its queue length is the number of customers waiting or in service, according to the stated convention.
