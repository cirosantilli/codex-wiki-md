# Poynting flux and local heating in a skin layer

↑ **Parent:** [Poynting theorem](poynting-theorem.md)

For a good conductor with surface electric-field peak amplitude $E_0$, the [surface impedance of a good conductor](surface-impedance-of-a-good-conductor.md) gives the displayed time-averaged [Poynting vector](poynting-vector.md) component into the conductor. The factor one half in $\langle\mathbf S\rangle=\operatorname{Re}(\mathbf E\times\mathbf H^*)/2$ is required for peak phasors. Its loss with depth obeys $-d\langle S_z\rangle/dz=\sigma E_0^2e^{-2z/\delta}/2=\langle\mathbf j\cdot\mathbf E\rangle$. Electromagnetic energy becomes [Joule heating](joule-heating.md) locally; averaged field-energy storage has zero time derivative.
