# Gysin ring splitting for an odd-dimensional sphere bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Gysin sequence of a sphere bundle](gysin-sequence-of-a-sphere-bundle.md)

Let $E$ be an oriented real [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) of positive even rank $r$, with zero [Euler class](euler-class-of-a-vector-bundle.md), and suppose the integral [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) of $B$ is torsion-free. The [Gysin sequence](gysin-sequence-of-a-sphere-bundle.md) gives $0\to H^j(B)\to H^j(S(E))\to H^{j-r+1}(B)\to0$. Choose an odd-degree class $a\in H^{r-1}(S(E))$ with $\pi_!a=1$. The [projection formula for sphere bundle integration](projection-formula-for-sphere-bundle-integration.md) identifies the cohomology additively with $\pi^*H^*(B)\oplus\pi^*H^*(B)a$. It is torsion-free, so [graded commutativity of the cup product](graded-commutativity-of-the-cup-product.md) gives $a^2=0$ from $2a^2=0$. These facts establish the displayed ring isomorphism, not just a decomposition of groups.
