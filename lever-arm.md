# Lever arm

↑ **Parent:** [Torque](torque.md)

The [lever arm](lever-arm.md) of a nonzero [force](force.md) about an origin is the shortest distance from that origin to the line through the application point parallel to the [force](force.md). Its length is $\ell=|\mathbf r\times\widehat{\mathbf F}|$, with [unit vector](unit-vector.md) $\widehat{\mathbf F}=\mathbf F/|\mathbf F|$. Thus [torque](torque.md) magnitude is $F\ell$, maximal at a right [angle](angle.md) for fixed $r$ and $F$, and zero if the line passes through the origin. Sliding the application point along that line leaves [torque](torque.md) unchanged, since $(\mathbf r+s\widehat{\mathbf F})\times\mathbf F=\mathbf r\times\mathbf F$. The same perpendicular-distance construction gives [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) magnitude $p\ell$ for a line parallel to nonzero [momentum](momentum.md) through the particle.
