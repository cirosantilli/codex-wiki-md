# Axisymmetric magnetic field near a regular axis

↑ **Parent:** [Gauss's law for magnetism](gauss-s-law-for-magnetism.md)

Let a smooth [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) with [axisymmetry](axisymmetric-vector-field.md) have axial value $B_0(z)$ and no line singularity on its axis. [Gauss's law for magnetism](gauss-s-law-for-magnetism.md) gives $\partial_r(rB_r)=-r\partial_zB_z$. Since $B_z(r,z)=B_0(z)+O(r^2)$, integration from the regular axis gives the displayed expansion. A falling axial field therefore implies outward field lines above a positive-source loop. In a current-free region, [Ampère's law](ampere-s-circuital-law.md) also gives $B_z(r,z)=B_0(z)-r^2B_0''(z)/4+O(r^4)$, which follows by equating $\partial_zB_r=\partial_rB_z$.
