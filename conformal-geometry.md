# Conformal geometry

↑ **Parent:** [Conformal map](conformal-map.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conformal_geometry)

[Conformal geometry](conformal-geometry.md) studies a [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md) only up to multiplication by a nowhere-zero positive scalar function on a real slice. A conformal class determines angles in Riemannian signature and null directions in Lorentzian signature. The complexified flat four-dimensional conformal geometry is encoded by the [Klein quadric](klein-quadric.md), whose incidence relation describes null separation.
