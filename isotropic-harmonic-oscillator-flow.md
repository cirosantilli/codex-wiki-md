# Isotropic harmonic oscillator flow

↑ **Parent:** [Hamiltonian flow](hamiltonian-flow.md)

For $H_\omega=(|v|^2+\omega^2|x|^2)/2$, the displayed linear [Hamiltonian flow](hamiltonian-flow.md) solves $\dot X=V$, $\dot V=-\omega^2X$. Its inverse is $S_{-t}$, its determinant is one and it preserves $H_\omega$. The zero-frequency limit is the shear $(x,v)\mapsto(x+tv,v)$.
