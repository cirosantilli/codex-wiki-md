# Hadamard space

↑ **Parent:** [CAT(0) space](cat-0-space.md)

A [Hadamard space](hadamard-space.md) is a complete [CAT(0) space](cat-0-space.md). Equivalently, every pair has a midpoint $m$ satisfying the midpoint inequality $d(z,m)^2\leq\tfrac12d(z,x)^2+\tfrac12d(z,y)^2-\tfrac14d(x,y)^2$ for every $z$. This inequality makes the midpoint unique. Iterating midpoints and using completeness constructs the unique [geodesic](geodesic.md) joining the pair. The squared-distance inequality extends to every point of that [geodesic](geodesic.md) by dyadic approximation and implies Euclidean triangle comparison and [geodesic convexity](geodesic-convexity.md) of closed balls.
