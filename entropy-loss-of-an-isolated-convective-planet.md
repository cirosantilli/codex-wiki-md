# Entropy loss of an isolated convective planet

↑ **Parent:** [Intrinsic planetary luminosity](intrinsic-planetary-luminosity.md)

With negligible nuclear or external heating, the [stellar structure equations](stellar-structure-equations.md) give $L_{\rm int}=-\int_0^M T\,\partial s/\partial t\,dm$. A nearly isentropic convective interior therefore has $\dot s=-L_{\rm int}/\int T\,dm<0$ whenever it radiates. Decreasing [specific entropy](specific-entropy.md) does not require temperature at every depth to decrease immediately: contraction of an [ideal gas](ideal-gas.md) can raise its central temperature while total energy is lost.
