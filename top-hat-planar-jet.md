# Top-hat planar jet

↑ **Parent:** [Kelvin-Helmholtz instability](kelvin-helmholtz-instability.md)

A top-hat planar jet has one constant streamwise velocity in a layer and another constant velocity outside it. In the inviscid model, the two layer boundaries are parallel [vortex sheets](vortex-sheet.md).
