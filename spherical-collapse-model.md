# Spherical-collapse model

↑ **Parent:** [Dark matter halo](dark-matter-halo.md)

The spherical-collapse model applies a spherical [top-hat filter](top-hat-filter.md) to an overdense region and predicts collapse when its linearly extrapolated smoothed overdensity exceeds the threshold $\delta_c\simeq1.686$ in an Einstein-de Sitter universe.
