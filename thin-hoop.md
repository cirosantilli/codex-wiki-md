# Thin hoop

↑ **Parent:** [Rigid body dynamics](rigid-body-dynamics-split.md)

An ideal circular ring whose [mass](mass.md) lies at a single radius $R$ from its symmetry axis. About that axis, the [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) is $\int R^2\,dm=MR^2$. The result assumes negligible radial and axial thickness; it is different from the $MR^2/2$ of a uniform solid [disk](disk-mathematics.md).
