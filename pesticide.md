# Pesticide

↑ **Parent:** [Chemistry](chemistry-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pesticide)

An agent used to prevent, suppress or destroy a target pest. Its intended function does not establish ecological selectivity or the absence of effects on other [organisms](organism-split.md).
