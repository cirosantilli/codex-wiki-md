# Descent lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Lipschitz gradient](lipschitz-gradient.md)

A differentiable function with an $L$-[Lipschitz gradient](lipschitz-gradient.md) obeys $f(v)\leq f(u)+\langle\nabla f(u),v-u\rangle+(L/2)\|v-u\|^2$. Integrate the gradient along the line segment and bound the deviation from its initial value. The lemma does not require convexity.
