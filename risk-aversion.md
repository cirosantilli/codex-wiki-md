# Risk aversion

↑ **Parent:** [Utility function](utility-function-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Risk_aversion)

For increasing [expected utility](expected-utility.md) preferences, an investor is risk-averse when a sure mean is at least as desirable as the corresponding lottery. A concave [utility function](utility-function-split.md) gives this preference by the [Jensen inequality](jensen-s-inequality.md); a strictly concave utility gives strict preference for nondegenerate risks. Local risk aversion is measured by $-U''/U'$ and its relative counterpart $-WU''/U'$.
