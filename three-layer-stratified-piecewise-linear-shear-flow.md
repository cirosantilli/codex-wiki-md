# Three-layer stratified piecewise-linear shear flow

↑ **Parent:** [Inviscid parallel shear flow](inviscid-parallel-shear-flow.md)

This three-layer [shear flow](shear-flow.md) has $U=\Delta Uz/h$ and density $\rho_0$ between $z=\pm h/2$. Above and below, the [velocity](velocity.md) is the corresponding constant $\pm\Delta U/2$, and the [mass density](density.md) is $\rho_0\mp\Delta\rho/2$. The density drops and [vorticity](vorticity.md) jumps coincide at both interfaces. Its [hydrodynamic stability](hydrodynamic-stability-split.md) involves coupled [gravity-vorticity interface waves](gravity-vorticity-interface-wave.md), unlike a profile with uniform shear extending through all three layers.
