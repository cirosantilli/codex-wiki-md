# Cobounded group action

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric group theory](geometric-group-theory-split.md)

An isometric [group action](group-action.md) on a [metric space](metric-space.md) is cobounded if some orbit is within a fixed distance $R$ of every point. Equivalently, translates of one bounded ball cover the space. Coboundedness does not require finite stabilizers, a proper space, or a finitely generated group. Every action on a point is cobounded.
