# Phosphorylation

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical reaction](chemical-reaction-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phosphorylation)

[Phosphorylation](phosphorylation.md) attaches a [phosphate](phosphate.md) group to a [molecule](molecule-split.md). Transfer from [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) to a [substrate](substrate-biochemistry.md) or [enzyme](enzyme-split.md) can participate in [energy coupling](energy-coupling.md); the name alone does not specify the complete [reaction mechanism](reaction-mechanism.md). See [OpenStax Biology 2e, 6.4: phosphorylation](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/6-4-atp-adenosine-triphosphate).
