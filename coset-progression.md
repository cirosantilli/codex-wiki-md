# Coset progression

↑ **Parent:** [Generalized arithmetic progression](generalized-arithmetic-progression.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coset_progression)

A coset progression is a [sumset](sumset.md) $H+P$, where $H$ is a finite [subgroup](subgroup.md) of an [abelian group](abelian-group.md) and $P$ is a [generalized arithmetic progression](generalized-arithmetic-progression.md).
