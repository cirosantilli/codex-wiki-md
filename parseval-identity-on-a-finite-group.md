# Parseval identity on a finite group

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier transform on a finite group](fourier-transform-on-a-finite-group.md)

For the [Fourier transform on a finite group](fourier-transform-on-a-finite-group.md), the displayed identity uses uniform [expectation](expected-value.md) in the original function space and a weighted [Hilbert-Schmidt inner product](hilbert-schmidt-inner-product.md) in the matrix components. In particular $\|f\|_2^2=\sum_\rho d_\rho\|\widehat f(\rho)\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2$. For an [abelian group](abelian-group.md), all $d_\rho=1$, and the identity becomes a sum of squared scalar coefficients.
