# Collision theory

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical kinetics](chemical-kinetics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collision_theory)

[Collision theory](collision-theory.md) estimates a gas-phase [reaction rate](reaction-rate.md) by combining encounter frequency with energetic and geometrical requirements for reaction. For distinct hard spheres with contact separation $R$, the collision cross section is $\pi R^2$, and the mean relative speed is $\sqrt{8kT/(\pi\mu)}$. A reactive barrier and orientation restrictions reduce the fraction of collisions that react.
