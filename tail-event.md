# Tail event

↑ **Parent:** [Tail sigma-algebra](tail-sigma-algebra.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tail_event)

A tail event is an [event](event.md) $A\in\mathcal T$, where $\mathcal T$ is the [tail sigma-algebra](tail-sigma-algebra.md) of the chosen [sequence](sequence.md) of [random variables](random-variable-split.md). It concerns information available arbitrarily far along the [sequence](sequence.md). In the measurable coordinate models described in [finite-prefix invariance of tail events](finite-prefix-invariance-of-tail-events.md), any finite edit preserves whether it occurs.

This is stronger than merely needing a long waiting time. On $\{0,1\}^{\mathbb N}$, with $1$ denoting heads, let $K=\inf\{j\geq1:X_j=1\}$, taking $K=\infty$ if there is no head. The [event](event.md)

$$
\{K=3\}=\{X_1=0,X_2=0,X_3=1\}
$$

is not a [tail event](tail-event.md): changing only $X_1$ from zero to one changes membership. Under fair [independent](independent-random-variables.md) [Bernoulli trials](bernoulli-trial.md) its [probability](probability.md) is $1/8$. Even the [event](event.md) $\{K<\infty\}$, of [probability](probability.md) one in this model, is not a [tail event](tail-event.md) as a literal [set](set-split.md): the all-zero [sequence](sequence.md) and the [sequence](sequence.md) with just one initial head differ in one coordinate. A [probability](probability.md) of zero or one does not characterize [tail events](tail-event.md).
