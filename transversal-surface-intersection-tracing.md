# Transversal surface intersection tracing

↑ **Parent:** [Continuation method](continuation-method.md)

For a [parametric surface](parametric-surface.md) $F(u,v)$ and a scalar implicit equation $f(P)=0$, set $g=f\circ F$. At a regular transversal intersection, $\nabla g\ne0$, and $(-g_v,g_u)$ is a parameter [tangent vector](tangent-vector.md). Normalize its image under $DF$ to choose physical [arc length](arc-length.md) steps. A predictor followed by [Newton method](newton-s-method-in-optimization.md) correction of $g=0$ and a transverse plane equation gives a local [continuation method](continuation-method.md). Certified patch bounds are useful for finding seeds; corner signs alone do not detect a closed intersection entirely inside a patch.
