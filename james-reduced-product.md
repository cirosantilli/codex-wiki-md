# James reduced product

↑ **Parent:** [Loop space](loop-space.md)

Finite words in a based space, with occurrences of its basepoint deleted. Word concatenation defines a multiplication. For connected based CW spaces, the natural map to $\Omega\Sigma X$ is a [weak homotopy equivalence](weak-homotopy-equivalence.md). With free integral [homology](homology-split.md), the [Bott–Samelson theorem](bott-samelson-theorem.md) identifies its [homology](homology-split.md) algebra with the [tensor algebra](tensor-algebra.md) on reduced [homology](homology-split.md).
