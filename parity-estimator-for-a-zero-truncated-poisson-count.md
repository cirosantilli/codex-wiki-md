# Parity estimator for a zero-truncated Poisson count

↑ **Parent:** [Zero-truncated Poisson distribution](zero-truncated-poisson-distribution.md)

For the [zero-truncated Poisson distribution](zero-truncated-poisson-distribution.md), $2\mathbf1_{\{Y\text{ even}\}}$ is the unique integrable nonrandomized [unbiased estimator](unbiased-estimator.md) of $p=1-e^{-\theta}$. Indeed unbiasedness gives $\sum_{y\ge1}t(y)\theta^y/y!=e^\theta+e^{-\theta}-2$, and [power series](power-series.md) coefficients force the parity values. Its [variance](variance-split.md) is $2p-p^2$; clipping it to $\mathbf1_{\{Y\text{ even}\}}$ gives [mean squared error](mean-squared-error.md) $p/2$, strictly smaller for $0<p<1$.
