# Standard chemical potential

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md)

The [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) assigned to a specified [standard state](standard-state.md). With dimensionless [thermodynamic activity](thermodynamic-activity.md) $a$, the per-particle convention is $\mu=\mu^\circ+k_BT\ln a$; a molar convention uses $RT$. The [standard state](standard-state.md) changes both $\mu^\circ$ and the activity definition without changing observable [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md).
