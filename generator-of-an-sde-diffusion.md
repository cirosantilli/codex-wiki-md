# Generator of an SDE diffusion

↑ **Parent:** [Itô diffusion](ito-diffusion.md)

The Itô formula identifies the operator of an SDE with drift $b$ and noise vector fields $\sigma_k$ as the displayed second-order operator. With bounded measurable coefficients, every solution satisfies the true martingale identity for $C_b^2$ test functions, hence is an [L-diffusion](diffusion-martingale-problem.md) in the martingale-problem sense.
