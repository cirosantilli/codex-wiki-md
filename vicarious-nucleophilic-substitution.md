# Vicarious nucleophilic substitution

↑ **Parent:** [Nucleophilic aromatic substitution](nucleophilic-aromatic-substitution.md)

[Vicarious nucleophilic substitution](vicarious-nucleophilic-substitution.md) replaces aromatic [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) using a [nucleophile](nucleophile.md) that carries its own leaving group. In nitroarenes, reversible carbanion addition gives a [sigma-H adduct](sigma-h-adduct.md); base-induced elimination of the [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) and the [nucleophile](nucleophile.md)-borne leaving group, followed by [protonation](protonation.md), gives substitution without expelling hydride.
