# Velocity in polar coordinates

↑ **Parent:** [Plane polar coordinates](plane-polar-coordinates.md)

For a differentiable path in [plane polar coordinates](plane-polar-coordinates.md) with $r>0$, the moving [unit vectors](unit-vector.md) are $\widehat{\mathbf r}=(\cos\theta,\sin\theta)$ and $\widehat{\boldsymbol\theta}=(-\sin\theta,\cos\theta)$. Differentiating the [position](position.md) $\mathbf r=r\widehat{\mathbf r}$ and using $\dot{\widehat{\mathbf r}}=\dot\theta\widehat{\boldsymbol\theta}$ proves the displayed [velocity](velocity.md). Its signed radial and transverse [vector components](coordinate-linear-algebra.md) are $\dot r$ and $r\dot\theta$. A second differentiation gives the [acceleration in polar coordinates](acceleration-in-polar-coordinates.md). The complex representatives of the two moving [unit vectors](unit-vector.md) are $e^{i\theta}$ and $ie^{i\theta}$.
