# Manometer

↑ **Parent:** [Fluid mechanics](fluid-mechanics-split.md)

An instrument for measuring [pressure](pressure-split.md) relative to another [pressure](pressure-split.md). A liquid-column [manometer](manometer.md) uses [hydrostatic pressure](hydrostatic-pressure.md); a digital [manometer](manometer.md) uses a [pressure transducer](pressure-transducer.md).

The appeal of a [liquid](liquid.md)-column [manometer](manometer.md) is that a [pressure measurement](pressure-measurement.md) becomes a visible [length](length.md) measurement: [mass density](density.md) and [gravitational acceleration](gravitational-acceleration.md) supply the conversion. It can compare two [pressure tappings](pressure-tapping.md) in a flowing [fluid](fluid.md), even though the measuring [liquid](liquid.md) itself must settle into [hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md). The reference connection determines which [pressure](pressure-split.md) difference is being measured.
