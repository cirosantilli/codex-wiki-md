# A cycle and a transposition generate the symmetric group exactly at coprime separation

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetric group](symmetric-group.md)

For $n\geq2$ and $1\leq k<n$, an $n$-cycle $c=(1\,2\,\ldots\,n)$ and $t=(1\,1+k)$ generate $S_n$ exactly when $\gcd(n,k)=1$. Their conjugate [transpositions](transposition-permutation.md) connect labels differing by $k$ modulo $n$. This [graph](graph-split.md) is connected exactly at [coprime](coprime-integers.md) separation, so [transpositions on a connected graph generate the symmetric group](transpositions-on-a-connected-graph-generate-the-symmetric-group.md). Otherwise residue classes modulo $\gcd(n,k)$ form a nontrivial [block system](block-system.md) preserved by both generators.
