# Principal minor resolvent trace bound

↑ **Parent:** [Stieltjes matrix resolvent](stieltjes-matrix-resolvent.md)

The [eigenvalue interlacing](eigenvalue-interlacing.md) of a [Hermitian matrix](hermitian-operator.md) and a principal minor bounds the difference of their resolvent traces by a constant times $|\operatorname{Im}z|^{-1}$. When both traces are normalized by the original dimension $N$, the bound acquires $N^{-1}$. One proof writes the difference using the interlacing counting [functions](function-split.md), whose difference is at most one, and bounds the integral of $|t-z|^{-2}$ by $\pi/|\operatorname{Im}z|$. This gives the admissible absolute constant $c=\pi$.
