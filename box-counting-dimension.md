# Box-counting dimension

↑ **Parent:** [Fractal geometry](fractal-geometry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Box-counting_dimension)

For a nonempty bounded subset $E$ of [Euclidean space](euclidean-norm.md), let $N_\delta(E)$ be the least number of radius-$\delta$ balls covering $E$. Its lower and upper box-counting dimensions are the lower and upper limits of $\log N_\delta(E)/\log(1/\delta)$ as $\delta\downarrow0$. If these limits agree, their common value is its [Minkowski dimension](box-counting-dimension.md). A cover by $N_\delta(E)$ balls implies $|E_\delta|\lesssim_n\delta^nN_\delta(E)$ for the open $\delta$-neighborhood. Conversely, a maximal separated subset gives the reverse comparison up to fixed changes of scale. This connects small-scale covering counts with neighborhood [Lebesgue measure](lebesgue-measure.md).
