# Effective mechanical temperature of released rough balls

↑ **Parent:** [Rotational equipartition in a rough-ball gas](rotational-equipartition-in-a-rough-ball-gas.md)

If each initially stationary ball is released at height $h$ and the subsequent isolated dilute assembly reaches effective classical equilibrium, energy conservation gives $4k_BT=mgh$. The height-tail probability above the release height is then $e^{-4}$. The effective [temperature](temperature-split.md) refers to mechanical motion, not the material's internal [temperature](temperature-split.md).
