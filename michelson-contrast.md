# Michelson contrast

↑ **Parent:** [Optical transfer function](optical-transfer-function.md)

For a periodic intensity distribution, $C=(I_{\max}-I_{\min})/(I_{\max}+I_{\min})$. It should not be confused with a resist contrast parameter.
