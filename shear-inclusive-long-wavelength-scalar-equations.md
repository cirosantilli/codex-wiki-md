# Shear-inclusive long-wavelength scalar equations

↑ **Parent:** [Long-wavelength approximation in cosmology](long-wavelength-approximation-in-cosmology.md)

For zero [shift vector](shift-vector.md), negative-expansion extrinsic curvature $K=-3H$ and $S^i{}_j=\widetilde K^i{}_j$, retain the leading spatial-gradient equations of a minimally coupled scalar. They give $\dot\phi=N\Pi$, $\dot\Pi=-N(3H\Pi+V_{,\phi})$, $\dot H=-4\pi GN\Pi^2-NS^i{}_jS^j{}_i/2$, and $\dot S^i{}_j=-3NH S^i{}_j$. The first-gradient [momentum constraint](momentum-constraint.md) is $\partial_iH=-4\pi G\Pi\partial_i\phi-\nabla_jS^j{}_i/2$. Shear decays as $a^{-3}$ during expansion. Its neglect after inflationary damping yields the equations underlying conservation of the [nonlinear curvature covector](nonlinear-curvature-covector.md).
