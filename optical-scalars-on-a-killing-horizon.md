# Optical scalars on a Killing horizon

↑ **Parent:** [Killing horizon](killing-horizon.md)

The [null expansion](null-expansion.md), [null shear](null-shear.md) and [null twist](null-twist.md) of a congruence tangent to the generators of a [Killing horizon](killing-horizon.md) all vanish there. If $k$ is its Killing generator and $\ell=fk$ is an affine generator, the [screen-space projector](screen-space-projector.md) kills every term proportional to $k$. The symmetric part of the projected derivative of $\ell$ therefore vanishes by the [Killing equation](killing-equation.md). The antisymmetric part vanishes by [Frobenius theorem](frobenius-theorem.md), since $\ell$ is hypersurface-orthogonal. These conclusions extend through a regular [bifurcation surface](bifurcation-surface.md) by continuity.
