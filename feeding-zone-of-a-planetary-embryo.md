# Feeding zone of a planetary embryo

↑ **Parent:** [Oligarchic growth](oligarchic-growth.md)

The region of a disk from which a [planetary embryo](planetary-embryo.md) can efficiently accrete solids. Its width depends on gravitational encounters and the surrounding orbital population. Approximating that width by a multiple of the [Hill radius](hill-radius.md) gives a useful local [isolation mass](isolation-mass.md).
