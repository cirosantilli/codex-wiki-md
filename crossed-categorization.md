# Crossed categorization

↑ **Parent:** [Social categorization](social-categorization.md)

An arrangement in which group boundaries on different dimensions do not coincide. A former [outgroup](social-outgroup.md) member may share another valued identity with the observer. This can soften a single division, but need not eliminate exclusion of people outside both categories.
