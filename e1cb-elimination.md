# E1cb elimination

↑ **Parent:** [Elimination reaction](elimination-reaction.md)

In an [E1cb elimination](e1cb-elimination.md), deprotonation first produces a conjugate-base intermediate, followed by leaving-group loss. Dehydration of an appropriate aldol product can form a conjugated enone.
