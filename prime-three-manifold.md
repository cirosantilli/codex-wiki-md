# Prime three-manifold

↑ **Parent:** [3-manifold](3-manifold.md)

A closed connected [orientable smooth manifold](orientable-smooth-manifold.md) of dimension three is prime when a decomposition as a [connected sum](connected-sum-of-oriented-manifolds.md) forces at least one summand to be the [three-sphere](three-sphere.md). Unlike an [irreducible three-manifold](irreducible-three-manifold.md), it may contain a nonseparating embedded two-sphere.
