# Degeneracy test for a fully ionized light-mass donor gas

↑ **Parent:** [Semiconductor doping](semiconductor-doping.md)

Before using a Maxwell–Boltzmann chemical-potential formula in a doped [semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md), compare the carrier density to its conduction-band effective density of states. When n is much larger than $N_c$, a nondegenerate logarithmic estimate is inconsistent. Use Fermi–Dirac statistics or a controlled degenerate expansion instead. A small effective mass strongly reduces $N_c$ and raises the Fermi energy at fixed density, so even an otherwise familiar donor concentration can put the chemical potential inside the conduction band.
