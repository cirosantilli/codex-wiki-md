<h1 id="3/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Under fixed phase representatives, two [unitary operators](../../../../../../unitary-operator.md) on the same space are **$\varepsilon$-close when $\|U-V\|_{\rm op}\le\varepsilon$**, where the [operator norm](../../../../../../operator-norm.md) is

$$
\|U-V\|_{\rm op}=\sup_{\|\psi\|_2=1}\|(U-V)|\psi\rangle\|_2.
$$

Thus every input [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) is approximated uniformly, not merely one selected input. Under the phase-insensitive convention, replace this bound by $\min_\theta\|U-e^{i\theta}V\|_{\rm op}\le\varepsilon$. The minimization uses one phase for the whole operator, not a separately chosen phase inside the [supremum](../../../../../../supremum.md) for each input. This is [operator-norm distance between unitary gates](../../../../../../operator-norm-distance-between-unitary-gates.md) with the stated global-phase identification.
