<h1 id="1/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Choose **$|\psi_0\rangle=|+\rangle$** and its [orthogonal](../../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) companion $|\psi_1\rangle=|-\rangle$. Since $X|+\rangle=|+\rangle$, either branch of `RandomFlip` returns the same [pure state](../../../../../../pure-state.md). Alice therefore obtains label 0 with certainty, and

$$
\boxed{\Pr(1\mid\texttt{RandomFlip})=0.}
$$

The alternative choice $|-\rangle$ also works: its sign change under $X$ is only a [global phase](../../../../../../global-phase.md). The useful property is being a [Pauli eigenstate](../../../../../../pauli-eigenstate.md) of the [Pauli X gate](../../../../../../pauli-x-gate.md).
