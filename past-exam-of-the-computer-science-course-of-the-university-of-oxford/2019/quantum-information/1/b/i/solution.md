<h1 id="1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The columns are $i|+\rangle$ and $-i|-\rangle$. Their [norms](../../../../../../norm.md) are one and their [inner product](../../../../../../inner-product-split.md) is zero, since $|+\rangle,|-\rangle$ are [orthonormal](../../../../../../orthonormal-set.md). Thus **$M_1$ is a [unitary matrix](../../../../../../unitary-matrix.md)**. Equivalently,

$$
M_1=iHZ,\qquad M_1^\dagger M_1=Z H^\dagger H Z=I,
$$

using the [Hadamard gate](../../../../../../hadamard-gate.md) and [Pauli Z gate](../../../../../../pauli-z-gate.md). The different column phases do not destroy [orthonormality](../../../../../../orthonormal-set.md).
