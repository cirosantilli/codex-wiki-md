<h1 id="1/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Set $A=U_A\otimes I_B$ and $B=I_A\otimes U_B$. They are [self-adjoint operators](../../../../../../self-adjoint-operator.md) and satisfy $A^2=B^2=I$, because the original factors are both self-adjoint and unitary. They also commute: each acts on a different tensor factor. Hence

$$
M_3^\dagger M_3=\frac12(A-iB)(A+iB)=\frac12(A^2+B^2+iAB-iBA)=I.
$$

The same calculation in the opposite order gives $M_3M_3^\dagger=I$. Thus **$M_3$ is a [unitary matrix](../../../../../../unitary-matrix.md) for every allowed pair of factors**. Commutation cancels the cross terms, while their relative imaginary coefficient supplies the opposite signs. This is the [commuting-involution unitary combination](../../../../../../commuting-involution-unitary-combination.md).
