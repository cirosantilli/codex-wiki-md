<h1 id="1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [Pauli Y gate](../../../../../../pauli-y-gate.md) satisfies $Y^\dagger=Y$ and $Y^2=I$. With $c=\cos(\pi/6)=\sqrt3/2$ and $s=\sin(\pi/6)=1/2$, the given [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) is [Hermitian](../../../../../../hermitian-operator.md), and

$$
M_2^\dagger M_2=(cI+sY)^2=(c^2+s^2)I+2csY=I+\frac{\sqrt3}{2}Y\ne I.
$$

Therefore **$M_2$ is not a [unitary matrix](../../../../../../unitary-matrix.md)**. Its [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are $c+s$ and $c-s$, neither of which has modulus one. An expression $\cos\theta\,I+i\sin\theta\,Y$ would behave differently, but the printed [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) has no such factor of $i$.
