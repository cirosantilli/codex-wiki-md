<h1 id="2/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Suppose $|W\rangle=(U_A\otimes U_B\otimes U_C)|GHZ\rangle$. Measure C in the [orthonormal basis](../../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) $\{U_C|0\rangle,U_C|1\rangle\}$. Its two AB conditional [vectors](../../../../../../vector.md) would be proportional to

$$
(U_A|0\rangle)\otimes(U_B|0\rangle),\qquad (U_A|1\rangle)\otimes(U_B|1\rangle),
$$

respectively. Both are [product states](../../../../../../product-state.md), because measuring the original GHZ state's C factor in the [computational basis](../../../../../../computational-basis.md) selects $|00\rangle$ or $|11\rangle$. This contradicts part (i), which applies to every [basic measurement](../../../../../../basic-measurement.md) basis of the W state's C factor. Therefore **no local [unitary transformation](../../../../../../unitary-operator.md) maps GHZ to W**.

An additional invariant makes the distinction visible without measurement records. Across the partition AB versus C, the GHZ [reduced density matrix](../../../../../../reduced-density-matrix.md) on C has [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) $1/2,1/2$, whereas the W state's are $2/3,1/3$. By [local-unitary invariance of reduced-state spectra](../../../../../../local-unitary-invariance-of-reduced-state-spectra.md), these spectra cannot be changed by local unitaries. This supplies the same impossibility and emphasizes that the proposed transformation is a product of unitaries, not arbitrary postselected local processing.
