<h1 id="2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The joint [Born rule](../../../../../../born-rule.md) uses the [tensor product](../../../../../../tensor-product.md) of the two outcome bras. Its amplitude is

$$
(\langle0|\otimes\langle\theta_0|)|\psi\rangle_{AB}=\frac45\langle\theta_0|0\rangle=\frac45\cos\theta.
$$

The other source component vanishes on contraction with $\langle0|$ on A. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\Pr(A=0,B=\theta_0)=\frac{16}{25}\cos^2\theta.}
$$

This is a joint [probability](../../../../../../probability.md): $\Pr(A=0)=16/25$, while $\Pr(B=\theta_0\mid A=0)=\cos^2\theta$. Dropping the first factor would give only the [conditional probability](../../../../../../conditional-probability.md).
