↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Here $\langle+y|=(\langle0|-i\langle1|)/\sqrt2$. Keeping that [complex conjugation](../../../../../../complex-conjugation.md), the joint amplitude is

$$
(\langle+|\otimes\langle+y|)|\psi\rangle_{AB}=\frac1{2\sqrt2}\bigl(1+(-i)(-i)\bigr)=0.
$$

Hence **$\Pr(A=+,B=+y)=0$**. The cancellation is destructive [quantum interference](../../../../../../quantum-interference.md) between the two source components, not the absence of either local outcome. Each local outcome individually has [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) $1/2$, since each [reduced density matrix](../../../../../../reduced-density-matrix.md) is $I/2$. Omitting the conjugation in the second bra would turn the cancellation into reinforcement and give the wrong answer.
