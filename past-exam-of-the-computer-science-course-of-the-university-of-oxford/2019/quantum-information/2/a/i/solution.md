↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A normalized bipartite [pure state](../../../../../../pure-state.md) is **entangled when it cannot be written as a [product state](../../../../../../product-state.md)** $|a\rangle_A\otimes|b\rangle_B$. Equivalently, its [Schmidt rank](../../../../../../schmidt-rank.md) exceeds one. This definition concerns the specified partition into systems A and B; the mixed-state definition of separability requires a different convex-mixture condition.
