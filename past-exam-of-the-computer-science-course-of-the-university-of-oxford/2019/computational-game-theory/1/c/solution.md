<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

**Yes to both questions, for both players.** Site one is [strictly dominated](../../../../../strict-dominance.md) by site two. Against opposing site $y$, the [payoff](../../../../../payoff.md) difference is

$$
u(2,y)-u(1,y)=\begin{cases}k-2,&y=1\text{ or }2,\\1,&3\leq y\leq k.\end{cases}
$$

Every difference is positive for $k\geq4$. By reflection, site $k$ is [strictly dominated](../../../../../strict-dominance.md) by site $k-1$. These endpoint actions are therefore also [weakly dominated strategies](../../../../../weakly-dominated-strategy.md), since [strict dominance](../../../../../strict-dominance.md) is stronger than the requirement of being no worse everywhere and better somewhere.
