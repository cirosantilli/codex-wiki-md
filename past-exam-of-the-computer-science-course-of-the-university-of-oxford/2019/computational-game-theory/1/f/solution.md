<h1 id="1/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

**There is no pure [Nash equilibrium](../../../../../nash-equilibrium.md).** If the firms choose different sites, $S$ serves at least its own building, so $C$ receives less than $2k$. By moving onto $S$'s site, $C$ makes every customer equidistant from the two shops and wins every tie, obtaining $2k$. Thus any separated profile has a profitable [unilateral deviation](../../../../../unilateral-deviation.md) by $C$.

At any co-located profile, $S$ receives zero. It can move to a different site and capture at least that site's customers, obtaining at least two. Thus every co-located profile also has a profitable [unilateral deviation](../../../../../unilateral-deviation.md). These cases exhaust all pure [strategy profiles](../../../../../strategy-profile.md).
