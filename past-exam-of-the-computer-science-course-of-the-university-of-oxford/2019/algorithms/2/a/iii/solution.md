<h1 id="2/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Let $p$ be a [vertex of a linear polyhedron](../../../../../../vertex-of-a-linear-polyhedron.md) and choose $n$ independent [active constraints](../../../../../../active-constraint.md), written $Bp=d$, with $B$ invertible. Suppose $p=\lambda u+(1-\lambda)v$, where $u,v\in P$ and $0<\lambda<1$. Each selected inequality has $B_iu\leq d_i$ and $B_iv\leq d_i$, while

$$
d_i=B_ip=\lambda B_iu+(1-\lambda)B_iv.
$$

Since both weights are positive, equality in this weighted upper bound forces $B_iu=B_iv=d_i$ for every selected row. Hence $Bu=Bv=d$, and invertibility gives $u=v=p$. Therefore **every [vertex of a linear polyhedron](../../../../../../vertex-of-a-linear-polyhedron.md) is an [extreme point](../../../../../../extreme-point.md)**.
