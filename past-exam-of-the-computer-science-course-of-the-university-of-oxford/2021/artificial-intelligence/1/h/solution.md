<h1 id="1/h/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [H](../h.md)

**Take $\varphi_1=p$ for an $A$ win and $\varphi_2=p\land\neg p$ for a $B$ win.** In the first case $A$ assigns $p$ true and play terminates immediately. In the second, either value of $p$ completes an assignment making the [contradiction](../../../../../contradiction.md) false, so $B$ wins regardless of $A$'s move. These examples force termination and do not rely on a stalling convention.

For the subsequent parts, adopt the explicit convention that an infinite play is a draw, with [payoff](../../../../../payoff.md) $0$, between an $A$ win at $+1$ and a $B$ win at $-1$. The source does not assign a value to nontermination. Different conventions can change values of cyclic positions; the forced finite wins above remain valid.
