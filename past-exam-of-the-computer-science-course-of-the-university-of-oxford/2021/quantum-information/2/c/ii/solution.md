↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**Either experiment is again possible.** The sequential experiment with $U=I$ repeats its first measurement outcome in every basis: the first [projective measurement](../../../../../../projective-measurement.md) has prepared one of that basis's [eigenstates](../../../../../../eigenstate.md), and the identity leaves it unchanged.

For the two-[qubit](../../../../../../qubit.md) experiment choose

$$
|\psi\rangle=|\Phi^+\rangle=\frac{|00\rangle+|11\rangle}{\sqrt2}
=\frac{|++\rangle+|--\rangle}{\sqrt2}.
$$

The two expansions exhibit identical outcomes in both specified bases. Each allowed equal-label record has [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) $1/2$. These are fixed choices of $U$ and $|\psi\rangle$, respectively; neither depends on which of the two bases Alice selects.
