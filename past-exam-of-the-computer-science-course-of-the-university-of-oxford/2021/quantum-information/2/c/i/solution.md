↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**Either experiment is possible.** In the sequential experiment choose $U=X$. The first [computational basis](../../../../../../computational-basis.md) measurement on $I/2$ returns a uniform [bit](../../../../../../bit.md) $j$ and prepares $|j\rangle$. The [Pauli X gate](../../../../../../pauli-x-gate.md) changes it to $|1-j\rangle$, so the second result is always opposite. The two possible records have [probabilities](../../../../../../probability.md) $\Pr(0,1)=\Pr(1,0)=1/2$.

In the two-[qubit](../../../../../../qubit.md) experiment choose the fixed [pure state](../../../../../../pure-state.md)

$$
|\psi\rangle=|\Psi^+\rangle=\frac{|01\rangle+|10\rangle}{\sqrt2}.
$$

The [Born rule](../../../../../../born-rule.md) gives the same two possible records, again with [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) $1/2$ each. The observed [computational basis](../../../../../../computational-basis.md) anticorrelation alone does not identify whether the correlation was generated sequentially or shared between two [qubits](../../../../../../qubit.md).
