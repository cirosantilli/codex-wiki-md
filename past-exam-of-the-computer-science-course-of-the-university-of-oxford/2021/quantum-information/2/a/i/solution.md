<h1 id="2/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Factor the sum before making an [entanglement](../../../../../../entangled-state.md) claim:

$$
|\Phi\rangle=\frac12\bigl(|+\rangle+|-\rangle\bigr)\otimes\bigl(|0\rangle+|1\rangle\bigr)
=\boxed{|0\rangle\otimes|+\rangle}.
$$

**The state is not entangled:** it is a [product state](../../../../../../product-state.md). A sum written with several tensor-product terms need not be entangled; those terms may combine into a single [tensor product](../../../../../../tensor-product.md).
