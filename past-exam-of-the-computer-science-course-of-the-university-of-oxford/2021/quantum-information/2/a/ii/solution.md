<h1 id="2/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The displayed [computational basis](../../../../../../computational-basis.md) expansion is already a [Schmidt decomposition](../../../../../../schmidt-decomposition.md), with coefficients $\sqrt{1-p}$ and $\sqrt p$. For $0<p<1$ both are nonzero, so the [Schmidt rank](../../../../../../schmidt-rank.md) is two. A pure [product state](../../../../../../product-state.md) has [Schmidt rank](../../../../../../schmidt-rank.md) one. **The state is therefore entangled throughout the specified open interval.**

Equivalently, the [reduced density matrix](../../../../../../reduced-density-matrix.md) is

$$
\rho_A=\begin{pmatrix}1-p&0\\0&p\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\operatorname{Tr}(\rho_A^2)=1-2p(1-p)<1.
$$

The reduced state is a [mixed state](../../../../../../mixed-state.md), which is impossible for one factor of a pure [product state](../../../../../../product-state.md). At the excluded endpoints the [Schmidt rank](../../../../../../schmidt-rank.md) drops to one, giving $|00\rangle$ or $|11\rangle$.
