<h1 id="3/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The required [superposition](../../../../../../superposition-principle.md) is the [Bell state](../../../../../../bell-state-split.md)

$$
\frac{|++\rangle+|--\rangle}{\sqrt2}
=\frac{|00\rangle+|11\rangle}{\sqrt2}=|\Phi^+\rangle.
$$

**Prepare $|00\rangle$, apply $H$ to the first [qubit](../../../../../../qubit.md), then apply CNOT from the first to the second.** The intermediate state is $(|00\rangle+|10\rangle)/\sqrt2$, and the [controlled-NOT gate](../../../../../../controlled-not-gate.md) gives the required [Bell state](../../../../../../bell-state-split.md). No final basis change is needed: the two displayed expressions represent exactly the same [vector](../../../../../../vector.md).
