<h1 id="1/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Prepare Bob in $|0\rangle_B$ and encode the [bit](../../../../../../bit.md) using Alice's preparation $|m\rangle_A$. The [controlled-NOT gate](../../../../../../controlled-not-gate.md) gives

$$
\operatorname{CNOT}_{A\to B}|m\rangle_A|0\rangle_B
=|m\rangle_A|m\rangle_B.
$$

**Bob measures in the [computational basis](../../../../../../computational-basis.md) and reads $m$ with [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) one.** Alice's final measurement is unnecessary. The interaction transmits a [classical bit](../../../../../../bit.md); it does not clone an arbitrary unknown [qubit](../../../../../../qubit.md).
