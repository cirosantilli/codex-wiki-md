<h1 id="1/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

In an orthonormal [computational basis](../../../../../../computational-basis.md), the squared [norm](../../../../../../norm.md) is the sum of the squared [moduli](../../../../../../modulus.md) of the coefficients:

$$
\langle\phi|\phi\rangle=\left|\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\right|^2+\left|\frac{1+i\sqrt3}{4}\right|^2
=\frac34+\frac{1+3}{16}=\boxed{1}.
$$

Thus the [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) is normalized and represents a [pure state](../../../../../../pure-state.md) of a [qubit](../../../../../../qubit.md). The [imaginary part](../../../../../../imaginary-part.md) contributes positively to the squared [modulus](../../../../../../modulus.md); squaring the coefficient without [complex conjugation](../../../../../../complex-conjugation.md) would give the wrong [norm](../../../../../../norm.md).
