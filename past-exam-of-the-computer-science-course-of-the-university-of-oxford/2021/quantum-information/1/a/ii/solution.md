<h1 id="1/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For the [Hadamard basis](../../../../../../hadamard-basis.md), $|\pm\rangle=(|0\rangle\pm|1\rangle)/\sqrt2$. The [Born rule](../../../../../../born-rule.md) gives

$$
\Pr(\pm)=|\langle\pm|\phi\rangle|^2
=\frac12\left|\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\pm\frac{1+i\sqrt3}{4}\right|^2.
$$

Writing the two [computational basis](../../../../../../computational-basis.md) amplitudes as $a,b$, [normalization](../../../../../../normalization.md) gives $\Pr(\pm)=\frac12\pm\operatorname{Re}(a^*b)$, and $\operatorname{Re}(a^*b)=\sqrt3/8$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\Pr(+)=\frac{4+\sqrt3}{8},\qquad\Pr(-)=\frac{4-\sqrt3}{8}.}
$$

These [probabilities](../../../../../../probability.md) sum to one. The cross term is [interference](../../../../../../interference-wave-propagation.md) between [probability amplitudes](../../../../../../probability-amplitude.md), so the [probabilities](../../../../../../probability.md) cannot be obtained by treating the input as an incoherent mixture of the [computational basis](../../../../../../computational-basis.md) alternatives.
