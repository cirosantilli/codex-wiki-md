# Quantum information

↑ **Parent:** [2021](split.md)

Source paper: [released examination archive](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/ba_computer_science_past_papers/response/2577745/attach/2/202402%20183%20CS%20Papers.zip?cookie_passthrough=1), member `2020/T/a16608w1.pdf`. The printed sitting is 27 May 2021; the ZIP directory names 2020. All three questions and all 21 marked subparts are answered. This is the canonical CS paper; the raster-identical Physics-held copy is a source reference, while Mathematics C7.4 is a different examination.[Cover and printed pages 2–5; all three questions](../../quantum-information-examination-paper-a16608w1-2021.md)

An identical copy is available in [the released combined PDF, pages 111–115](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/oxford_physics_past_papers_2/response/2340696/attach/9/Physics%20Papers%202020.pdf?cookie_passthrough=1#page=111). Its historical folder and attachment name do not change the Computer Science cover identity.

Tensor factors follow their displayed subsystem order, and the rightmost operator acts first. Computational labels are $0,1$; Hadamard- and Pauli-Y-basis labels are the corresponding plus/minus [eigenvalue](../../eigenvalue.md) labels. The exact correlations in Question 2 are interpreted as ideal [probability](../../probability.md)-one predictions, not certainty inferred from a finite experimental sample.
