A [simple cooperative game](../../../../../simple-cooperative-game.md) is a [transferable utility game](../../../../../transferable-utility-game.md) $(N,v)$ with

$$
\boxed{v:2^N\to\{0,1\},\quad v(\varnothing)=0,\quad v(N)=1,\quad
S\subseteq T\Longrightarrow v(S)\le v(T).}
$$

A [coalition](../../../../../coalition-game-theory.md) of value $1$ is a [winning coalition](../../../../../winning-coalition.md); a value-$0$ [coalition](../../../../../coalition-game-theory.md) loses. The displayed definition uses the usual monotone, nontrivial convention: the empty [coalition](../../../../../coalition-game-theory.md) loses and the grand [coalition](../../../../../coalition-game-theory.md) wins.
