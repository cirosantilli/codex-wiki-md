<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [phase oracle](../../../../../phase-oracle.md) multiplies an unmarked [basis](../../../../../basis.md) [vector](../../../../../vector.md) by $+1$ and a marked one by $-1$. Thus the state after the oracle is

$$
\boxed{V_f|e_0\rangle=\frac1{\sqrt N}\bigl(|A_0\rangle-|A_1\rangle\bigr).}
$$

Its squared [norm](../../../../../norm.md) is $(S_0+S_1)/N=1$. All computational outcomes would still have [probability](../../../../../probability.md) $1/N$ if measured at this point: the oracle has changed the [relative phase](../../../../../relative-quantum-phase.md), not the magnitudes of the amplitudes. The following reflection converts that phase information into a changed [probability distribution](../../../../../probability-distribution-split.md) through [quantum interference](../../../../../quantum-interference.md).
