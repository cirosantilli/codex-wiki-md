↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Summing part (i) over the $S_0$ unmarked indices gives $\langle e_0|A_0\rangle=S_0/\sqrt N$. The [Grover diffusion operator](../../../../../../grover-diffusion-operator.md) therefore acts as

$$
W|A_0\rangle=2|e_0\rangle\langle e_0|A_0\rangle-|A_0\rangle=\frac{2S_0}{\sqrt N}|e_0\rangle-|A_0\rangle=\boxed{\frac{2S_0}N|J\rangle-|A_0\rangle.}
$$

This is an identity for an unnormalized subset sum; it does not require replacing its [norm](../../../../../../norm.md) by one.
