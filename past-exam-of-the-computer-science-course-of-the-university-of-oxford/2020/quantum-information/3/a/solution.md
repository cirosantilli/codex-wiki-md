<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Substitute the promised cardinalities:

$$
\frac{2(S_0-S_1)}N=\frac2N\left(\frac{3N}4-\frac N4\right)=\frac2N\frac N2=\boxed{1}.
$$

The marked and unmarked subsets also exhaust the [computational basis](../../../../../computational-basis.md), so $S_0+S_1=N$; this will ensure that the uniform initial state has unit [norm](../../../../../norm.md).
