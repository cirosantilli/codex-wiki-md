<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Swap the first two computational [vectors](../../../../../vector.md) and fix the third:

$$
\boxed{U=|1\rangle\langle0|+|0\rangle\langle1|+|2\rangle\langle2|=\begin{pmatrix}0&1&0\\1&0&0\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

This [permutation matrix](../../../../../permutation-matrix.md) has [orthonormal](../../../../../orthonormal-set.md) columns; equivalently, $U^\dagger=U$ and $U^2=I$, proving [unitarity](../../../../../unitary-operator.md). Its first column gives $U|0\rangle=|1\rangle=|\phi_1\rangle$, as required. A unitary must also specify what happens to the rest of the [basis](../../../../../basis.md), which is why a single outer product alone would not suffice.
