For an [orthonormal basis](../../../../orthonormal-basis.md), choosing coefficients with magnitudes $\sqrt{p_j}$ gives the required [Born rule](../../../../born-rule.md) [probabilities](../../../../probability.md). More generally, $\sum_j\sqrt{p_j}e^{i\theta_j}|\phi_j\rangle$ realizes the same distribution for arbitrary phases.
