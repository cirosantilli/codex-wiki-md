<h1 id="1/e/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Directly applying the [Hadamard gate](../../../../../../hadamard-gate.md) gives

$$
H|+i\rangle=\frac12\{(1+i)|0\rangle+(1-i)|1\rangle\}=e^{i\pi/4}|-i\rangle,
$$

while

$$
H|-i\rangle=\frac12\{(1-i)|0\rangle+(1+i)|1\rangle\}=e^{-i\pi/4}|+i\rangle.
$$

Hence **the two states are interchanged up to the displayed [global phases](../../../../../../global-phase.md)**. Their relative factors $-i$ and $+i$ are retained. They are the two [Pauli eigenstates](../../../../../../pauli-eigenstate.md) of the [Pauli Y gate](../../../../../../pauli-y-gate.md), and the [Hadamard interchange of Y eigenstates](../../../../../../hadamard-interchange-of-y-eigenstates.md) is consistent with $HYH=-Y$.
