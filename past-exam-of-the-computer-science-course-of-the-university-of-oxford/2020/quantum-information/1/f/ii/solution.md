Here $H^2=I$. Both remaining gates fix the all-zero computational [vector](../../../../../../vector.md): the [T gate](../../../../../../t-gate.md) fixes $|0\rangle$, and the [controlled-NOT gate](../../../../../../controlled-not-gate.md) fixes $|00\rangle$. Therefore every finite circuit, on any number of initially zero wires, leaves $|0\cdots0\rangle$ unchanged.

It cannot even approximate $|+\rangle\otimes|0\cdots0\rangle$, whose squared overlap with that unchanged [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) is $1/2$. Thus **the gate set is not universal**. The needed [quantum superposition](../../../../../../quantum-superposition.md) cannot be created from this input by [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) permutations and diagonal phases alone.
