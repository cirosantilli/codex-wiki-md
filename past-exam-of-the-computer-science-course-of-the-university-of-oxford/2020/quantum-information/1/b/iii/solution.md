<h1 id="1/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Choose

$$
\boxed{|\psi\rangle=\frac{|\phi_0\rangle+|\phi_1\rangle}{\sqrt2}=\frac12|0\rangle+\frac1{\sqrt2}|1\rangle+\frac i2|2\rangle.}
$$

[Orthonormality](../../../../../../orthonormal-set.md) makes this a [unit vector](../../../../../../unit-vector.md). Its [probability amplitudes](../../../../../../probability-amplitude.md) in the given measurement [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) are $(1/\sqrt2,1/\sqrt2,0)$, so the required [probabilities](../../../../../../probability.md) are $(1/2,1/2,0)$.
