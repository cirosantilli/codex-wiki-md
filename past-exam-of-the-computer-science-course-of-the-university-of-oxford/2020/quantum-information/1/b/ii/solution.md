<h1 id="1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Choose **$|\psi\rangle=|\phi_2\rangle$**. Its [inner products](../../../../../../inner-product-split.md) with the three measurement [vectors](../../../../../../vector.md) are $(0,0,1)$, so the [Born rule](../../../../../../born-rule.md) gives certainty for the outcome labelled 2 and zero [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) for the other two outcomes. This uses the measurement [basis](../../../../../../basis.md), rather than assuming that its label 2 means the initial state must be the computational [vector](../../../../../../vector.md) $|2\rangle$.
