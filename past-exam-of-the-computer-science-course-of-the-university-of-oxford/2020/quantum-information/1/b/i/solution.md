<h1 id="1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Choose **$|\psi\rangle=|0\rangle$**. Since $|0\rangle=(|\phi_0\rangle+|\phi_2\rangle)/\sqrt2$, the [probability amplitudes](../../../../../../probability-amplitude.md) in the measurement [basis](../../../../../../basis.md) are $(1/\sqrt2,0,1/\sqrt2)$. Their squared moduli give $(1/2,0,1/2)$ by the [Born rule](../../../../../../born-rule.md).
