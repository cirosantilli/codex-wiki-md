<h1 id="2/d/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

A [local unitary transformation](../../../../../../local-unitary-operation.md) preserves a product factorization:

$$
(U\otimes V)(|a\rangle\otimes|b\rangle)=(U|a\rangle)\otimes(V|b\rangle).
$$

The output is therefore another [product state](../../../../../../product-state.md). If an [entangled state](../../../../../../entangled-state.md) could be locally mapped to a [product state](../../../../../../product-state.md), applying the inverse $U^\dagger\otimes V^\dagger$ would make the original [entangled state](../../../../../../entangled-state.md) a product as well, a contradiction. Hence **a [product state](../../../../../../product-state.md) and an [entangled state](../../../../../../entangled-state.md) are never [local-unitary equivalent](../../../../../../local-unitary-equivalence.md)**.

In terms of a [tensor-product coefficient matrix](../../../../../../tensor-product-coefficient-matrix.md), $C$ changes to $UCV^T$, with an ordinary [transpose](../../../../../../transpose.md) on the second factor. Both multiplying [matrices](../../../../../../matrix.md) are invertible, so the [matrix rank](../../../../../../matrix-rank.md), equivalently the [Schmidt rank](../../../../../../schmidt-rank.md), is unchanged. This supplies an algebraic form of the same argument: a nonzero [pure state](../../../../../../pure-state.md) is a [product state](../../../../../../product-state.md) exactly when that rank is one.
