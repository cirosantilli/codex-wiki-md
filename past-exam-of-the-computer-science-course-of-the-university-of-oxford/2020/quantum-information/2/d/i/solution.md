<h1 id="2/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Fix the two subsystem dimensions. For [reflexivity](../../../../../../reflexive-relation.md), $|\Psi\rangle=(I_d\otimes I_{d'})|\Psi\rangle$, and both [identity operators](../../../../../../identity-operator.md) are unitary.

For symmetry, if $|\Psi\rangle=(U\otimes V)|\Phi\rangle$, apply the inverse [local unitary transformation](../../../../../../local-unitary-operation.md):

$$
|\Phi\rangle=(U^\dagger\otimes V^\dagger)|\Psi\rangle.
$$

The [conjugate transposes](../../../../../../conjugate-transpose.md) of [unitary operators](../../../../../../unitary-operator.md) are unitary, so the reverse relation holds.

For [transitivity](../../../../../../transitive-relation.md), if additionally $|\Phi\rangle=(R\otimes S)|\Theta\rangle$, the [tensor product](../../../../../../tensor-product.md) multiplication rule gives

$$
|\Psi\rangle=(U\otimes V)(R\otimes S)|\Theta\rangle=(UR\otimes VS)|\Theta\rangle.
$$

Both products are unitary, for example $(UR)^\dagger(UR)=R^\dagger U^\dagger UR=I$. Thus **[local-unitary equivalence](../../../../../../local-unitary-equivalence.md) is reflexive, symmetric and transitive**, and hence is an [equivalence relation](../../../../../../equivalence-relation.md).
