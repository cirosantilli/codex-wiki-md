<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Measure the first [qutrit](../../../../../qutrit.md) in the [computational basis](../../../../../computational-basis.md) and the second in the [orthonormal basis](../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) $\{|\phi_0\rangle,|\phi_1\rangle,|\phi_2\rangle\}$ from Question 1(a), with those labels as outcomes. For first-party outcome 0, the unnormalized conditional [vector](../../../../../vector.md) of the second party is $|0\rangle/\sqrt3$, so this outcome occurs with [probability](../../../../../probability.md) $1/3$ and leaves the normalized state $|0\rangle$.

The second-party [Born rule](../../../../../born-rule.md) [probabilities](../../../../../probability.md) are then $|\langle\phi_0|0\rangle|^2=1/2$, $|\langle\phi_1|0\rangle|^2=0$, and $|\langle\phi_2|0\rangle|^2=1/2$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\Pr(B=0\mid A=0)=\Pr(B=2\mid A=0)=\tfrac12,\qquad\Pr(B=1\mid A=0)=0.}
$$

The relevant joint [probabilities](../../../../../probability.md) are $1/6,0,1/6$, each divided by $\Pr(A=0)=1/3$. This distinguishes the conditional statement from an unconditional outcome [probability](../../../../../probability.md). More generally, [conjugate-state steering from a maximally entangled pair](../../../../../conjugate-state-steering-from-a-maximally-entangled-pair.md) explains how the first local measurement determines the second party's conditional [vector](../../../../../vector.md); the [basis](../../../../../basis.md) choice here makes that [vector](../../../../../vector.md) especially simple.
