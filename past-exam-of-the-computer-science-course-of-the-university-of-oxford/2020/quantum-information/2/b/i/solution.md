<h1 id="2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Factor the coefficients:

$$
|\Psi_1\rangle=\left(\frac{|0\rangle-|1\rangle}{\sqrt2}\right)\otimes\left(\frac{|0\rangle-|1\rangle}{\sqrt2}\right)=|-\rangle\otimes|-\rangle.
$$

Therefore **the first state is a [product state](../../../../../../product-state.md), not [entangled](../../../../../../entangled-state.md)**. Both factors have unit [norm](../../../../../../norm.md), so this factorization also checks [normalization](../../../../../../normalization.md).
