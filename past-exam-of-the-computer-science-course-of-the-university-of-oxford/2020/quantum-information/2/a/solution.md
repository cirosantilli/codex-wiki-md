<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write the [product state](../../../../../product-state.md) as $|\Psi\rangle=|a\rangle\otimes|b\rangle$. The two [partial inner products](../../../../../partial-inner-product.md) are

$$
|\psi'_j\rangle=(\langle\psi_j|a\rangle)|b\rangle=c_j|b\rangle,\qquad j=1,2.
$$

Both [vectors](../../../../../vector.md) therefore lie in the same one-dimensional [vector subspace](../../../../../vector-subspace.md), possibly with one or both equal to zero. If $(c_1,c_2)\ne(0,0)$, choose $\lambda_1=c_2$ and $\lambda_2=c_1$; then $\lambda_1|\psi'_1\rangle=\lambda_2|\psi'_2\rangle$, and the multipliers are not both zero. If both $c_j$ vanish, choose $(\lambda_1,\lambda_2)=(1,0)$.

Thus **the conditional [vectors](../../../../../vector.md) are [linearly dependent](../../../../../linear-dependence.md)**, with a nontrivial pair of multipliers in every case. The printed existential condition does not explicitly exclude $(0,0)$, which would make it vacuous for arbitrary [vectors](../../../../../vector.md); the argument establishes the stronger intended statement. These unnormalized [vectors](../../../../../vector.md) need not themselves be valid unit state [vectors](../../../../../vector.md), and a zero [vector](../../../../../vector.md) corresponds to a measurement outcome of [probability](../../../../../probability.md) zero. This is the forward direction of the [conditional-vector product-state criterion](../../../../../conditional-vector-product-state-criterion.md).
