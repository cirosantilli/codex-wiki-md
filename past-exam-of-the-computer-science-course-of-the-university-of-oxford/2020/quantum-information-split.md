# Quantum information

↑ **Parent:** [2020](split.md)

Solutions to all three questions of the 11 June 2020 paper. [Released Oxford examination archive](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/ba_computer_science_past_papers/response/2577745/attach/2/202402%20183%20CS%20Papers.zip?cookie_passthrough=1), member `2019/T/a16608w1.pdf`. The dated cover establishes 2020 despite the archive directory. All displayed state [vectors](../../vector.md) are normalized unless described as unnormalized conditional [vectors](../../vector.md). We use column [vectors](../../vector.md), $|ij\rangle=|i\rangle\otimes|j\rangle$, and the left tensor factor as the first wire. In a product of gates, the rightmost gate acts first. A [global phase](../../global-phase.md) is physically irrelevant, but all relative signs and phases below are retained.
