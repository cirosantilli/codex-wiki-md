<h1 id="2/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For normalized vector representatives in the same [Hilbert space](../../../../../../hilbert-space-split.md), two [pure states](../../../../../../pure-state.md) are $\varepsilon$-close in [pure-state norm distance](../../../../../../pure-state-norm-distance.md) when

$$
\boxed{\bigl\||\psi\rangle-|\phi\rangle\bigr\|_2\leq\varepsilon.}
$$

Here $\|\cdot\|_2$ is the [Euclidean norm](../../../../../../euclidean-norm.md) of the coefficient vector in any [orthonormal basis](../../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md). Equivalently, $2-2\operatorname{Re}\langle\psi|\phi\rangle\leq\varepsilon^2$. If the convention identifies vector representatives differing by a [global phase](../../../../../../global-phase.md), first minimize over that phase, giving $\min_\gamma\||\psi\rangle-e^{i\gamma}|\phi\rangle\|_2=\sqrt{2-2|\langle\psi|\phi\rangle|}$; the vector-norm convention above specifies the representatives.
