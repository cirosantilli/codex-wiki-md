An [entangling gate](../../../../../../entangling-gate.md) is a [unitary operator](../../../../../../unitary-operator.md) on two [quantum systems](../../../../../../quantum-system-split.md) for which **some pure product input is mapped to an entangled state**:

$$
\boxed{\exists\,|u\rangle_A,|v\rangle_B:\quad
U(|u\rangle_A\otimes|v\rangle_B)\ \text{is entangled}.}
$$

It need not create [quantum entanglement](../../../../../../entangled-state.md) from every [product state](../../../../../../product-state.md). For example, a [controlled-NOT gate](../../../../../../controlled-not-gate.md) entangles $|+\rangle|0\rangle$ but leaves $|00\rangle$ a [product state](../../../../../../product-state.md).
