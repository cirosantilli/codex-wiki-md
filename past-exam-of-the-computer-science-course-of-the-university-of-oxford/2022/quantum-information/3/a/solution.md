↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The two conditions on a [density matrix](../../../../../density-matrix-split.md) are **positive semidefiniteness and unit trace**:

$$
\boxed{\rho\geq0,\qquad\operatorname{Tr}\rho=1.}
$$

Here [positive semidefinite](../../../../../positive-semidefinite-matrix.md) includes being a [Hermitian matrix](../../../../../hermitian-operator.md), with $v^\dagger\rho v\geq0$ for every vector $v$. Equivalently, a [density matrix](../../../../../density-matrix-split.md) is [Hermitian](../../../../../hermitian-operator.md) with nonnegative [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) summing to one.
