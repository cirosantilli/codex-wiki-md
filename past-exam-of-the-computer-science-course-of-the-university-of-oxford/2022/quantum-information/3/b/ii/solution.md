<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Since $H=(X+Z)/\sqrt2$, the [Hadamard gate](../../../../../../hadamard-gate.md) is, up to a [global phase](../../../../../../global-phase.md), the [rotation gate](../../../../../../rotation-gate.md)

$$
e^{-i\pi(X+Z)/(2\sqrt2)}=-iH.
$$

Thus its [Bloch sphere](../../../../../../bloch-sphere.md) action is a **rotation through $\pi$ about the axis $(1,0,1)/\sqrt2$**, halfway between the positive $x$ and $z$ axes:

$$
\boxed{(r_x,r_y,r_z)\longmapsto(r_z,-r_y,r_x).}
$$

The identities $HXH=Z$, $HYH=-Y$ and $HZH=X$ verify this [Bloch vector](../../../../../../bloch-vector.md) transformation directly.
