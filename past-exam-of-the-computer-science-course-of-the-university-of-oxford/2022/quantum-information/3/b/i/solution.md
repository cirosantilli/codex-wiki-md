The [Pauli Z gate](../../../../../../pauli-z-gate.md) gives a **rotation through $\pi$ about the $z$ axis** of the [Bloch sphere](../../../../../../bloch-sphere.md). In [Bloch vector](../../../../../../bloch-vector.md) coordinates,

$$
\boxed{(r_x,r_y,r_z)\longmapsto(-r_x,-r_y,r_z).}
$$

For example, this follows from $ZXZ=-X$, $ZYZ=-Y$ and $ZZZ=Z$ in the [Pauli matrix](../../../../../../pauli-matrices.md) expansion $\rho=(I+r_xX+r_yY+r_zZ)/2$.
