<h1 id="3/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**The state is separable.** The off-diagonal terms of the two [Bell state](../../../../../../bell-state-split.md) projectors have opposite signs and cancel:

$$
\boxed{\frac12|\phi_+\rangle\langle\phi_+|+\frac12|\phi_-\rangle\langle\phi_-|
=\frac12|00\rangle\langle00|+\frac12|11\rangle\langle11|.}
$$

This is a [quantum state ensemble](../../../../../../quantum-state-ensemble.md) of two [pure states](../../../../../../pure-state.md) that are [product states](../../../../../../product-state.md), with [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) $1/2$ each, establishing a [separable quantum state](../../../../../../separable-quantum-state.md). Its correlations are classical; an ensemble decomposition into [entangled states](../../../../../../entangled-state.md) does not itself establish [quantum entanglement](../../../../../../entangled-state.md) of the [density matrix](../../../../../../density-matrix-split.md).
