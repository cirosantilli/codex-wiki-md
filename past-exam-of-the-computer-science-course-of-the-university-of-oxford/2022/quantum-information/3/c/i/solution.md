<h1 id="3/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**The state is separable.** The [identity matrix](../../../../../../identity-matrix.md) resolves into the four projectors of the [computational basis](../../../../../../computational-basis.md), giving the explicit [quantum state ensemble](../../../../../../quantum-state-ensemble.md)

$$
\boxed{\frac{I_4}{4}=\frac14\sum_{i,j=0}^1
\bigl(|i\rangle\langle i|\bigr)_A\otimes\bigl(|j\rangle\langle j|\bigr)_B
=\frac{I_2}{2}\otimes\frac{I_2}{2}.}
$$

Each member is a [pure state](../../../../../../pure-state.md) that is a [product state](../../../../../../product-state.md), so this is a [separable quantum state](../../../../../../separable-quantum-state.md) by its defining decomposition.
