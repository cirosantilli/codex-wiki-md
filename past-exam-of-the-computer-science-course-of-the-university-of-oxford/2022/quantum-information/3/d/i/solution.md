<h1 id="3/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For input $|0\rangle_S$, the [controlled-NOT gate](../../../../../../controlled-not-gate.md) fixes $|00\rangle_{SC}$, which is the ideal two-copy [pure state](../../../../../../pure-state.md). Thus its [global cloning fidelity](../../../../../../global-cloning-fidelity.md) is $F_0=1$. For input $|+\rangle_S$, the actual output is the [Bell state](../../../../../../bell-state-split.md) $|\phi_+\rangle$, whereas the desired output is $|++\rangle$. Their overlap is

$$
\langle++|\phi_+\rangle=\frac1{\sqrt2},\qquad F_+=\frac12.
$$

With the given equal input [probabilities](../../../../../../probability.md),

$$
\boxed{F_0=1,\qquad F_+=\frac12,\qquad
\overline F=\frac12\left(1+\frac12\right)=\frac34.}
$$

These values use the specified [squared quantum fidelity](../../../../../../squared-quantum-fidelity.md) for the full output, rather than a one-copy marginal fidelity.
