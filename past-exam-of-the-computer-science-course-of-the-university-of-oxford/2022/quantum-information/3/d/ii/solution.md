<h1 id="3/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Assume perfect cloning of both specified [pure states](../../../../../../pure-state.md). Allowing irrelevant output [global phases](../../../../../../global-phase.md), this would require

$$
U|00\rangle=e^{i\alpha}|00\rangle,
\qquad U|+0\rangle=e^{i\beta}|++\rangle.
$$

A [unitary operator](../../../../../../unitary-operator.md) preserves [inner products](../../../../../../inner-product-split.md), and therefore their magnitudes. But the input overlap and required output overlap have different magnitudes:

$$
\boxed{|\langle00|+0\rangle|=\frac1{\sqrt2}
\ne\frac12=|\langle00|++\rangle|.}
$$

This contradiction proves that **no such two-qubit unitary exists**, without assuming the [no-cloning theorem](../../../../../../no-cloning-theorem.md).
