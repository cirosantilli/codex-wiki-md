<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [basic measurement](../../../../../basic-measurement.md) of a finite-dimensional [quantum system](../../../../../quantum-system-split.md) is specified by an [orthonormal basis](../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) $\{|b_j\rangle\}_j$ of its [Hilbert space](../../../../../hilbert-space-split.md). For a normalized input [pure state](../../../../../pure-state.md) $|\chi\rangle$, the [Born rule](../../../../../born-rule.md) gives

$$
\boxed{p_j=|\langle b_j|\chi\rangle|^2,\qquad
|\chi_j\rangle=\frac{|b_j\rangle\langle b_j|\chi\rangle}{\sqrt{p_j}}
\quad(p_j>0).}
$$

The [post-measurement state](../../../../../post-measurement-state.md) is therefore $|b_j\rangle$ up to a [global phase](../../../../../global-phase.md). An outcome of zero [probability](../../../../../probability.md) has no conditional [post-measurement state](../../../../../post-measurement-state.md). For a general input [density matrix](../../../../../density-matrix-split.md) $\rho$, the same [basic measurement](../../../../../basic-measurement.md) has $p_j=\langle b_j|\rho|b_j\rangle$ and conditional [density matrix](../../../../../density-matrix-split.md) $|b_j\rangle\langle b_j|$ whenever $p_j>0$.
