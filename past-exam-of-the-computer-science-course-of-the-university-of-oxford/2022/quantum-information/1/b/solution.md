<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use the [Pauli X eigenstates](../../../../../pauli-x-eigenstate.md) $|\pm\rangle=(|0\rangle\pm|1\rangle)/\sqrt2$ and $\cos\theta+i\sin\theta=e^{i\theta}$. Their [probability amplitudes](../../../../../probability-amplitude.md) are

$$
\langle\pm|\phi\rangle=\frac{\sqrt2\pm e^{i\theta}}{\sqrt6}.
$$

The [Born rule](../../../../../born-rule.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\Pr(+)=\frac12+\frac{\sqrt2}{3}\cos\theta,\qquad
\Pr(-)=\frac12-\frac{\sqrt2}{3}\cos\theta.}
$$

The two [probabilities](../../../../../probability.md) sum to one. Their dependence on the [relative quantum phase](../../../../../relative-quantum-phase.md) is the [quantum interference](../../../../../quantum-interference.md) between the two components in the [computational basis](../../../../../computational-basis.md).
