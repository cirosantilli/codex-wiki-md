↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a real [random variable](../../../../../random-variable-split.md) $X$ with finite [expectation](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) $\mu=\mathbb E X$, its [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) is

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Var}(X)=\mathbb E[(X-\mu)^2].}
$$

When the [second moment](../../../../../second-moment.md) is finite, expansion and [linearity of expectation](../../../../../linearity-of-expectation.md) give $\operatorname{Var}(X)=\mathbb E[X^2]-\mu^2$. The definition also permits infinite [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) when the [expectation](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) is finite but the [second moment](../../../../../second-moment.md) is infinite.
