↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

[Chebyshev's inequality](../../../../../chebyshev-s-inequality.md) assumes a real [random variable](../../../../../random-variable-split.md) $X$ with finite [expectation](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) $\mu$ and finite [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) $\sigma^2$. For every real $a>0$,

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(|X-\mu|\geq a)\leq\frac{\sigma^2}{a^2}.}
$$

No [independence](../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) assumption is needed. Applying [Markov's inequality](../../../../../markov-s-inequality.md) to the nonnegative [random variable](../../../../../random-variable-split.md) $(X-\mu)^2$ proves the formula, including the zero-[variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) case.
