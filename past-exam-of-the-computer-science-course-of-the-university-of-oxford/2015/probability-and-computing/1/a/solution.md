The [expectation](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) of a real [random variable](../../../../../random-variable-split.md) $X$ on a [probability space](../../../../../probability-space-split.md) is its [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md) against the [probability measure](../../../../../probability-measure.md):

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E X=\int_\Omega X(\omega)\,d\mathbb P(\omega).}
$$

For a discrete [random variable](../../../../../random-variable-split.md), this becomes $\mathbb E X=\sum_x x\mathbb P(X=x)$; for a [random variable](../../../../../random-variable-split.md) with [probability density function](../../../../../probability-density-function.md) $f$, it becomes $\mathbb E X=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}xf(x)\,dx$. A finite [expectation](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) requires $\mathbb E|X|<\infty$, making $X$ an [integrable random variable](../../../../../integrable-random-variable.md). An extended [expectation](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) can also be defined when just one of the positive and negative parts has finite [expectation](../../../../../expected-value-split.md); the indeterminate difference $\infty-\infty$ is not allowed.
