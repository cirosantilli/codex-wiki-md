<h1 id="2/g/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [G](../g.md)

The [expectation](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) from part (f) is the average of $|S(\sigma)|$ over the finite collection of [permutations](../../../../../permutation.md). At least one value is as large as its average. By part (e), the corresponding $S(\sigma)$ is an [independent set](../../../../../independent-set-graph-theory.md), so

$$
\boxed{\alpha(G)\geq\sum_{i=1}^n\frac1{d_i+1}.}
$$

Here $\alpha(G)$ is the [independence number](../../../../../independence-number.md). Since its value is an [integer](../../../../../integer-split.md), one can equivalently round the right side up with the [ceiling function](../../../../../ceiling-function.md). This application of the [probabilistic method](../../../../../probabilistic-method.md) is the [Caro-Wei bound](../../../../../caro-wei-bound.md).
