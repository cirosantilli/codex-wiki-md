<h1 id="3/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**Always true.** An [all-different constraint](../../../../../../all-different-constraint.md) of arity $k$ requires $k$ distinct values. If $|D|<k$, the [pigeonhole principle](../../../../../../pigeonhole-principle.md) makes even one such constraint impossible. Since $m>0$, at least one is present, and consequently

$$
\boxed{|D|<k\ \Longrightarrow\ \operatorname{Sol}(P)=\varnothing.}
$$
