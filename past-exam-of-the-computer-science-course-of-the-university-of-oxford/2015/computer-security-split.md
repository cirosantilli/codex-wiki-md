# Computer security

↑ **Parent:** [2015](split.md)

Solutions to all three questions of the examination dated 15 June 2015. The cover permits candidates to answer two questions.[Cover, A12073W1; questions on printed pages 2–7](../../computer-security-examination-paper-by-the-university-of-oxford-2015.md)

The [authentication](../../authentication.md) claims in Question 2 require particular care: the printed third message permits substitution of Bob's key ticket after his [cryptographic nonce](../../cryptographic-nonce.md) becomes public. The solutions distinguish the intended freshness argument from the [Dolev-Yao model](../../dolev-yao-model.md) attack that defeats Bob's [authentication](../../authentication.md). This is a property of the printed [protocol](../../communication-protocol.md), rather than a missing part of the source.
