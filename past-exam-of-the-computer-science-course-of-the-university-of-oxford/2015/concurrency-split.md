# Concurrency

↑ **Parent:** [2015](split.md)

Solutions to all three questions of the examination dated 1 June 2015. The cover permits answers to two questions.[Cover; questions on printed pp. 2–5](../../concurrency-examination-paper-by-the-university-of-oxford-2015.md)

Throughout, $\Sigma$ is the visible [CSP event](../../csp-event.md) alphabet, $\tau$ is a [CSP internal action](../../csp-internal-action.md), and $\mathrm{STOP}$ has no transitions. There is no successful termination event. Write $S\sqsubseteq_M I$ when implementation $I$ refines specification $S$ in model $M$: the implementation's recorded behaviours are contained in the specification's. We use $T$, $F$ and $FD$ for the [CSP traces model](../../csp-traces-model.md), [CSP stable-failures model](../../csp-stable-failures-model.md) and [CSP failures-divergences model](../../csp-failures-divergences-model.md).
