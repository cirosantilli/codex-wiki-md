# Computational complexity

↑ **Parent:** [2015](split.md)

Solutions to all three questions of the [University of Oxford](../../university-of-oxford-split.md) [computer science](../../computer-science-split.md) finals paper held on 3 June 2015, Trinity Term 2015. The printed cover gives paper code A12071W1. Questions 2 and 3 are marked corrected in the source.[Cover and printed pp. 2–4, Questions 1–3](../../computational-complexity-examination-paper-of-the-university-of-oxford-2015.md)

The solutions use the original printed notation; the [integer parity](../../parity-mathematics.md) convention needed in Question 3(e) is stated there explicitly.
