<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Take an infinite supply of variables and the two distinguished constants of [combinatory logic](../../../../../combinatory-logic.md), the [K combinator](../../../../../k-combinator.md) and the [S combinator](../../../../../s-combinator.md). The [combinatory logic terms](../../../../../combinatory-logic-term.md) are exactly the finite expressions generated by

$$
\boxed{T::=x\mid\mathbf K\mid\mathbf S\mid(TT).}
$$

Thus every variable and each distinguished constant is a [combinatory logic term](../../../../../combinatory-logic-term.md), and the application of two [combinatory logic terms](../../../../../combinatory-logic-term.md) is a [combinatory logic term](../../../../../combinatory-logic-term.md). Nothing else is a [combinatory logic term](../../../../../combinatory-logic-term.md). Application associates to the left, so $ABC$ means $(AB)C$; it is not assumed to be associative. The [I combinator](../../../../../i-combinator.md) can be used as the abbreviation $\mathbf I\equiv\mathbf{SKK}$, without adding a new primitive constant.

The basic equality is the least relation containing the two axiom schemes

$$
\boxed{\mathbf KAB=A,\qquad\mathbf SABC=AC(BC)}
$$

for all [combinatory logic terms](../../../../../combinatory-logic-term.md) $A,B,C$, and closed under the rules of [equational reasoning](../../../../../equational-reasoning.md):

$$
\frac{}{A=A},\qquad
\frac{A=B}{B=A},\qquad
\frac{A=B\quad B=C}{A=C},
\qquad
\frac{A=B\quad C=D}{AC=BD}.
$$

The final rule permits replacing equal [combinatory logic terms](../../../../../combinatory-logic-term.md) inside either side of an application; repeated use permits replacement at any depth. Uniform [substitution](../../../../../substitution-algebra.md) of [combinatory logic terms](../../../../../combinatory-logic-term.md) for variables preserves equality, either included as a rule or proved by induction on these derivations, since the displayed axioms are schemes. No rule equating two [combinatory logic terms](../../../../../combinatory-logic-term.md) merely because they agree after an extra argument belongs to this basic theory. Its equality is the symmetric, reflexive and transitive closure of [weak combinatory reduction](../../../../../weak-combinatory-reduction.md).
