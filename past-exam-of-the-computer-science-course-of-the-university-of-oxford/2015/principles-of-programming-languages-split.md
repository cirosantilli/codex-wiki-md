# Principles of programming languages

↑ **Parent:** [2015](split.md)

Solutions to all three questions of the paper dated 11 June 2015.[Cover and pp. 2–8, A12079W1](../../principles-of-programming-languages-examination-paper-by-the-university-of-oxford-2015.md) In the equations below, $r_m$ and $\mathbin{\triangleright_m}$ denote the base [computational monad](../../computational-monad.md)'s result and sequencing operations. All equalities of computations are extensional: apply both sides to the same remaining arguments. [Haskell](../../haskell.md) fragments use `result` and `bind` for the paper's $\operatorname{result}$ and $\mathbin{\triangleright}$. The `FunMem` programs use the language's own syntax.
