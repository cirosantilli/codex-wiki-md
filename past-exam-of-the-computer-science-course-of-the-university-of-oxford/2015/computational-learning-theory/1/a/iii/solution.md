The [shattering coefficient](../../../../../../shattering-coefficient.md), also called the [growth function of a hypothesis class](../../../../../../shattering-coefficient.md), counts the largest number of labellings obtainable on $m$ inputs:

$$
\boxed{\Pi_H(m)=\sup_{x_1,\ldots,x_m\in X}\left|\{(h(x_1),\ldots,h(x_m)):h\in H\}\right|.}
$$

When $X$ has at least $m$ points, the same value is obtained by maximizing $|\{h|_S:h\in H\}|$ over $m$-point [sets](../../../../../../set-split.md) $S$. Always $\Pi_H(m)\leq2^m$, with equality exactly when some $m$-point [set](../../../../../../set-split.md) is shattered.
