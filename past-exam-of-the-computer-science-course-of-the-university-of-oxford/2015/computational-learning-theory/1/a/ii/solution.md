The [VC dimension](../../../../../../vc-dimension.md) is

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{VC}(H)=\sup\{|S|:S\subseteq X\text{ is finite and is shattered by }H\}.}
$$

It is infinite if arbitrarily large finite [sets](../../../../../../set-split.md) are shattered. A [hypothesis class](../../../../../../concept-class.md) has [VC dimension](../../../../../../vc-dimension.md) zero when it shatters no one-point [set](../../../../../../set-split.md); for a nonempty [hypothesis class](../../../../../../concept-class.md), the empty [set](../../../../../../set-split.md) is always shattered.
