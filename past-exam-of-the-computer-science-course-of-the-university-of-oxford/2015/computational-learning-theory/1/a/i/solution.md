<h1 id="1/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [hypothesis class](../../../../../../concept-class.md) $H$ [shatters a set](../../../../../../set-shattering.md) $S$ when it realizes every binary labelling of $S$: for every [function](../../../../../../function-split.md) $g:S\to\{0,1\}$ there is an $h\in H$ with $h|_S=g$. Identifying each [classifier](../../../../../../classifier.md) with its positive [set](../../../../../../set-split.md), this says

$$
\boxed{\{S\cap h:h\in H\}=\mathcal P(S),}
$$

where $\mathcal P(S)$ is the [power set](../../../../../../power-set.md). Different labellings may require different members of the [hypothesis class](../../../../../../concept-class.md).
