# Databases

↑ **Parent:** [2015](split.md)

Solutions to all three questions of the examination dated 3 June 2015. The cover permits answers to two questions.[Cover and printed pp. 2–4](../../databases-examination-paper-by-the-university-of-oxford-2015.md)

Use classical [relational algebra](../../relational-algebra.md) with [set semantics](../../set-semantics.md). Write $C,S,U,P$ for the paper's `Car`, `Sales`, `Customer` and `Salesperson` [database relations](../../database-relation.md). A [natural join](../../natural-join.md) between $C$ and $S$ matches `serialnumber`; joins involving a salesperson explicitly match `salespersonid` to `empid`. Identifiers, rather than names, distinguish customers and salespeople. The calculations use the ordinary non-null [relational model](../../relational-model.md); the [SQL](../../sql.md) aggregate below also explains its empty-input behavior.
