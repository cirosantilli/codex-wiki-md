↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Using the supplied counting function, an appropriate [first-order sentence](../../../../../../first-order-sentence.md) is

$$
\boxed{\forall x\Bigl(\bigl(\mathit{FacultyMember}(x)\land\mathit{GreaterOrEqual}(\mathit{CountSupervise}(x),2)\land\exists y\,\mathit{Teach}(x,y)\bigr)\to\mathit{Busy}(x)\Bigr).}
$$

Here the counting comparison expresses the threshold and the [existential quantification](../../../../../../existential-quantification.md) expresses teaching at least one course. The word “if” supplies this direction of [logical implication](../../../../../../logical-implication.md); it does not assert that these are the only reasons a faculty member can be busy. With the intended meanings of the nonlogical symbols, no further counting axioms are needed for this translation. If those meanings were unspecified, the displayed [first-order sentence](../../../../../../first-order-sentence.md) alone would not define arithmetic or connect the counting function to the supervision relation.
