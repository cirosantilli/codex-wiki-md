<h1 id="1/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The supervisor can depend on the student, so the [existential quantification](../../../../../../existential-quantification.md) belongs inside the [universal quantification](../../../../../../universal-quantification.md):

$$
\boxed{\forall x\bigl((\mathit{Undergrad}(x)\lor\mathit{Masters}(x))\to\exists y\,\mathit{SupervisedBy}(x,y)\bigr).}
$$

The [first-order sentence](../../../../../../first-order-sentence.md) permits different students to share a supervisor. It requires neither that the supervisor be different from the student nor that the supervisor be a faculty member, since neither additional condition was given.
