<h1 id="1/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For resolution, a [first-order sentence](../../../../../../first-order-sentence.md) is in [clausal normal form](../../../../../../clausal-form.md) when it is a universally quantified [logical conjunction](../../../../../../logical-conjunction.md) of [first-order clauses](../../../../../../first-order-clause.md):

$$
\boxed{\forall\bar x\,\bigwedge_{i=1}^{m}\bigvee_{j=1}^{k_i}L_{ij},}
$$

where every $L_{ij}$ is a [first-order literal](../../../../../../first-order-literal.md), meaning an [atomic formula](../../../../../../atomic-formula.md) or the [logical negation](../../../../../../negation.md) of an [atomic formula](../../../../../../atomic-formula.md). The quantifiers are often left implicit and the [first-order clauses](../../../../../../first-order-clause.md) treated as a set. The empty [first-order clause](../../../../../../first-order-clause.md) represents falsity; an empty set of [first-order clauses](../../../../../../first-order-clause.md) represents truth.

A convention that allows an arbitrary quantifier prefix followed by a conjunctive matrix is weaker: that is a [prenex normal form](../../../../../../prenex-normal-form.md) with a conjunctive matrix. The elimination of existential quantifiers by [Skolemization](../../../../../../skolemization.md) is the extra step relevant to resolution and to part (ii).
