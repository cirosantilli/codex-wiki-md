<h1 id="1/c/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

In [ALC](../../../../../../alc.md), $\boxed{\text{only (ii)}}$ is representable over the given symbols. It is the [general concept inclusion](../../../../../../general-concept-inclusion.md)

$$
\boxed{\mathit{Undergrad}\sqcup\mathit{Masters}\sqsubseteq\exists\mathit{SupervisedBy}.\top.}
$$

Equivalently, use one [general concept inclusion](../../../../../../general-concept-inclusion.md) for each category. Unary predicates become [description logic concepts](../../../../../../description-logic-concept.md) and the supervision predicate becomes a [description logic role](../../../../../../description-logic-role.md).

Sentence (i) depends on a ternary predicate evaluated at two named constants. Basic [ALC](../../../../../../alc.md) has unary [description logic concepts](../../../../../../description-logic-concept.md) and binary [description logic roles](../../../../../../description-logic-role.md), with no constructor for that course-and-year slice of a ternary predicate. Sentence (iii) uses a counting function and a comparison relation that basic [ALC](../../../../../../alc.md) cannot express. Replacing these conditions by freshly named [description logic concepts](../../../../../../description-logic-concept.md) would give an informal encoding, but would not define their meanings or establish equivalence in the supplied vocabulary.
