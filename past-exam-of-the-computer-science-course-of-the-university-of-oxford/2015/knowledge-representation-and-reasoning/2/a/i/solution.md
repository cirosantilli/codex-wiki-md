An initial split into an acyclic definition and the remaining [general concept inclusions](../../../../../../general-concept-inclusion.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathcal T_u=\{C\equiv\exists P.\top\},\qquad \mathcal T_g=\{(1),(2),(3),(4)\}.}
$$

The definition of $C$ is suitable for [TBox unfolding](../../../../../../tbox-unfolding.md): its right-hand side does not depend on $C$, and $C$ has only this defining left-hand side. The other displayed [general concept inclusions](../../../../../../general-concept-inclusion.md) do not initially have an atomic defined left-hand side. In particular, $A\sqcap B\sqsubseteq\bot$ can be rewritten for [TBox absorption](../../../../../../tbox-absorption.md), but is not already an atomic definition.
