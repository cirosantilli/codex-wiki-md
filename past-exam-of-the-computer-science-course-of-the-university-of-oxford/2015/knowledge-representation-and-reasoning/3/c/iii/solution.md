<h1 id="3/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Add the [ground atom](../../../../../../ground-atom.md)

$$
\boxed{\mathit{Year}(N).}
$$

Together with the three rules above, this is the [Datalog with negation](../../../../../../datalog-with-negation.md) [knowledge base](../../../../../../knowledge-base.md) $\mathcal K$. There are no supplied divisibility facts and no other year facts. In particular, nothing in this [knowledge base](../../../../../../knowledge-base.md) asserts $\mathit{DivisibleBy}(N,4)$ or $\mathit{DivisibleBy}(N,100)$.
