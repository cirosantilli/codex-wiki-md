<h1 id="1/g/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [G](../g.md)

Insert the union subtree from part (f) as another child of the original intersection:

$$
\boxed{P_{\mathrm{rounded}}=
H_5\cap H_6\cap H_4\cap H_1\cap(H_2\cup H_3)\cap(H_g\cup D_r).}
$$

Thus the [constructive solid geometry tree](../../../../../constructive-solid-geometry-tree.md) has one intersection root, four original [half-space](../../../../../half-space.md) leaves and two union children. The first union handles the inward corner at $C$; the second embeds the circular [fillet](../../../../../fillet.md) at $B$. The [line segments](../../../../../line-segment.md) $AB$ and $BC$ now end at $M$ and start at $N$, with the chosen [circular arc](../../../../../circular-arc.md) between them. All other boundary [line segments](../../../../../line-segment.md) are retained.
