<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Solving the [implicit equation](../../../../../implicit-equation.md) for $y$ when $\Delta x\ne0$ gives

$$
y=y_A+\frac{\Delta y}{\Delta x}(x-x_A),\qquad
\boxed{m=\frac{y_B-y_A}{x_B-x_A}.}
$$

Here “gradient” means the [slope](../../../../../slope.md) $dy/dx$, rather than the [gradient](../../../../../gradient.md) $(a,b)$ of an implicit defining [function](../../../../../function-split.md). If $\Delta x=0$ and $A\ne B$, the [straight line](../../../../../straight-line.md) is vertical and has no finite [slope](../../../../../slope.md). If $\Delta y=0$ and $\Delta x\ne0$, its [slope](../../../../../slope.md) is zero.
