<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Set $\Delta x=x_B-x_A$ and $\Delta y=y_B-y_A$. A displacement from $A$ lies along the [straight line](../../../../../straight-line.md) precisely when its two-dimensional [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) with $(\Delta x,\Delta y)$ vanishes. Thus an [implicit equation](../../../../../implicit-equation.md) is

$$
\boxed{\Delta y(x-x_A)-\Delta x(y-y_A)=0.}
$$

Equivalently, write $ax+by+c=0$, with $a=\Delta y$, $b=-\Delta x$ and $c=\Delta x\,y_A-\Delta y\,x_A$. The [vector](../../../../../vector.md) $(a,b)$ is a [normal vector](../../../../../normal-vector.md). The formula handles vertical and horizontal [straight lines](../../../../../straight-line.md) without division. It presupposes $A\ne B$: coincident [points](../../../../../point-geometry.md) do not determine a unique [straight line](../../../../../straight-line.md).
