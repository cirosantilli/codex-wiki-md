# Probability and computing

↑ **Parent:** [2015](split.md)

Solutions to the paper dated 1 June 2015.[Cover, code A14681W1; printed pp. 2–3, Questions 1–3](../../probability-and-computing-examination-paper-by-the-university-of-oxford-2015.md) Every numbered subpart is treated. **The original assertions in 2(b) and 2(c) are false:** the two-bin [repeated singleton-removal allocation](../../repeated-singleton-removal-allocation.md) gives exact [counterexamples](../../counterexample.md). Those answers explain the defect and the intended conditional calculation; 2(d) also supplies an independent, valid bound for the actual process.
