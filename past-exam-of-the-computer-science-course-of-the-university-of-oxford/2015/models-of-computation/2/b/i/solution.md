<h1 id="2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [context-free grammar](../../../../../../context-free-grammar.md) is **an [ambiguous grammar](../../../../../../ambiguous-grammar.md) if some [word](../../../../../../string.md) in its generated [formal language](../../../../../../formal-language.md) has two distinct [parse trees](../../../../../../parse-tree.md)**. Equivalently, that [word](../../../../../../string.md) has two distinct [leftmost derivations](../../../../../../leftmost-derivation.md) from the start [nonterminal symbol](../../../../../../nonterminal-symbol.md). A [leftmost derivation](../../../../../../leftmost-derivation.md) always expands the leftmost remaining [nonterminal symbol](../../../../../../nonterminal-symbol.md).

Two arbitrary [grammar derivations](../../../../../../grammar-derivation.md) of the same [word](../../../../../../string.md) do not by themselves prove ambiguity: expanding independent [nonterminal symbols](../../../../../../nonterminal-symbol.md) in a different order can describe the same [parse tree](../../../../../../parse-tree.md). Requiring distinct [parse trees](../../../../../../parse-tree.md), or distinct [leftmost derivations](../../../../../../leftmost-derivation.md), avoids that issue.

$$
\boxed{\text{Ambiguity means two distinct parse trees for at least one word}.}
$$
