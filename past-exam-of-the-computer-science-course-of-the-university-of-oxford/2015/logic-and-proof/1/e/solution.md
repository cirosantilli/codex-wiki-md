The [propositional compactness theorem](../../../../../propositional-compactness-theorem.md) states that an arbitrary set $S$ of [propositional formulas](../../../../../propositional-formula.md) is satisfiable if and only if every finite [subset](../../../../../subset.md) of $S$ is satisfiable:

$$
\boxed{S\text{ has a model}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\text{every finite }S_0\subseteq S\text{ has a model}.}
$$

The satisfying [Boolean valuation](../../../../../boolean-valuation.md) may depend on $S_0$ on the right; the conclusion on the left provides one [Boolean valuation](../../../../../boolean-valuation.md) satisfying all of $S$ simultaneously.
