<h1 id="3/a/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The same person $y$ must both love $x$ and be loved by $x$:

$$
\boxed{\forall x\,[(\exists y\,(L(y,x)\land L(x,y)))\to H(x)].}
$$

Equivalently, use $\forall x\forall y\,[(L(y,x)\land L(x,y))\to H(x)]$. There is no restriction that $x$ and $y$ be distinct; this matters when the later [ground resolution](../../../../../../ground-resolution.md) proof considers Lucy loving herself.
