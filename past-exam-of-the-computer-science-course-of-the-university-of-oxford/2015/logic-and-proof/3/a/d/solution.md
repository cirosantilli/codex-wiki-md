<h1 id="3/a/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Lucy's happiness is a single antecedent, while the conclusion applies to everyone:

$$
\boxed{H(\ell)\to\forall x\,M(x).}
$$

Since $x$ does not occur in the antecedent, this is equivalent to $\forall x\,[H(\ell)\to M(x)]$. The [universal quantifier](../../../../../../universal-quantification.md) does not assert that everyone is happy; it is the merriness conclusion that is universal.
