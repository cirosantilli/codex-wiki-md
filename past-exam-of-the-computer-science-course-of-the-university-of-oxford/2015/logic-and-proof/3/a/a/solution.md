The [existential quantifier](../../../../../../existential-quantification.md) supplies the person loved, with a choice that may depend on $x$:

$$
\boxed{\forall x\,[H(x)\lor\exists y\,L(x,y)].}
$$

The outer [universal quantifier](../../../../../../universal-quantification.md) ranges over everyone. The [logical disjunction](../../../../../../logical-disjunction.md) is inclusive; it permits a person both to be happy and to love somebody.
