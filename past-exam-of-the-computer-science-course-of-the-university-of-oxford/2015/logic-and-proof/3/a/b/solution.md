↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The property of loving at least one person is the antecedent:

$$
\boxed{\forall x\,[(\exists y\,L(x,y))\to L(x,\ell)].}
$$

An equivalent [first-order sentence](../../../../../../first-order-sentence.md), useful for [Skolemization](../../../../../../skolemization.md), is $\forall x\forall y\,[L(x,y)\to L(x,\ell)]$. The person loved in the antecedent need not be Lucy; the conclusion concerns Lucy specifically.
