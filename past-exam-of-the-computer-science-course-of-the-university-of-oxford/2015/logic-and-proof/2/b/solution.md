<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A [first-order theory](../../../../../first-order-theory.md) $T$ admits [quantifier elimination](../../../../../quantifier-elimination.md) when every [first-order formula](../../../../../first-order-formula.md) $\varphi(\bar x)$ has a [quantifier-free formula](../../../../../quantifier-free-formula.md) $\psi(\bar x)$, in the same [first-order signature](../../../../../first-order-signature.md) and with no additional [free variables](../../../../../free-variable.md), such that

$$
\boxed{T\models\forall\bar x\,[\varphi(\bar x)\leftrightarrow\psi(\bar x)].}
$$

Thus all [first-order models](../../../../../model-of-a-first-order-theory.md) of $T$ agree that the two [first-order formulas](../../../../../first-order-formula.md) define the same tuples. This definition concerns [first-order formulas](../../../../../first-order-formula.md) with [free variables](../../../../../free-variable.md), as well as [first-order sentences](../../../../../first-order-sentence.md); merely deciding closed assertions would be weaker. The existence of an effective procedure is an additional issue, supplied by the explicit construction in part (d).
