# Advanced data structures and algorithms

↑ **Parent:** [2015](split.md)

Solutions to the paper dated 5 June 2015.[Cover, formal code A10399W1; question pages 2–3](../../advanced-data-structures-and-algorithms-examination-paper-by-the-university-of-oxford-2015.md) All thirteen numbered subparts are treated. The original cover and both question pages have been checked, including the directions and labels of the [flow network](../../flow-network-split.md) diagram. The [binary counter](../../binary-counter.md) and [decimal counter](../../decimal-counter.md) analyses use the conventional all-zero initial state; the effect of allowing a different initial state is stated explicitly.
