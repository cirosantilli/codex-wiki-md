<h1 id="1/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

First, the [Bell-LaPadula model](../../../../../../bell-lapadula-model.md) is a [information confidentiality](../../../../../../information-confidentiality.md) policy. It permits a low-level subject to overwrite a more highly classified object by blind writing. That can destroy a high-level file or introduce false data without disclosing its old contents. Thus it does not supply a [data integrity](../../../../../../data-integrity.md) policy, and it also does not ensure [service availability](../../../../../../service-availability.md). Modern [multiuser operating systems](../../../../../../multiuser-operating-system.md) require protection against modification and resource exhaustion as well as disclosure.

Second, a single hierarchy of classifications cannot express ordinary independent user ownership and selective collaboration. Two users at the same level may still need different permissions on their respective files; conversely, an authorised publication may deliberately release selected information to lower levels. The simple model lacks those owner-specific permissions and controlled release decisions. Adding [discretionary access control](../../../../../../discretionary-access-control.md), categories and explicit [declassification](../../../../../../declassification.md) mechanisms is substantial additional policy.

$$
\boxed{\text{It omits integrity requirements and cannot express ordinary selective sharing.}}
$$
