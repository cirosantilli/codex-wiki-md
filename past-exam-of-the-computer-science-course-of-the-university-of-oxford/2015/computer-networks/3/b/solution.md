[Flow control](../../../../../flow-control.md) prevents a sender from overrunning the receiving [endpoint](../../../../../communication-endpoint.md)'s capacity to buffer and consume data. [Congestion control](../../../../../congestion-control.md) prevents the aggregate traffic from overloading the intervening [computer network](../../../../../computer-network-split.md), especially shared links and [router](../../../../../router.md) queues. A fast receiver can still sit beyond a congested path; an uncongested path can still lead to a slow receiver. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\text{flow control protects the receiver; congestion control protects the network}.}
$$
