# Logic and proof

↑ **Parent:** [2015](split.md)

Solutions to the three questions of the [University of Oxford](../../university-of-oxford-split.md) [computer science](../../computer-science-split.md) Finals examination held on 10 June 2015. Question 3(d) has a false assertion as printed; its solution gives a [countermodel](../../countermodel.md) and explains the additional hypotheses under which the usual [least Herbrand model](../../least-herbrand-model.md) argument works.[Cover and printed pp. 2–4, Questions 1–3; the unrestricted quantifier-free matrix in Question 3(d) is visible on printed p. 4](../../logic-and-proof-examination-paper-of-the-university-of-oxford-2015.md)
