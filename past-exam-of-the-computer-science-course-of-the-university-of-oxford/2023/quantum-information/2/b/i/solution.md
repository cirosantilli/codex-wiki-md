<h1 id="2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Initialize one [qubit](../../../../../../qubit.md) in $|0\rangle$, then apply a [Hadamard gate](../../../../../../hadamard-gate.md), a [T gate](../../../../../../t-gate.md), and a [Hadamard gate](../../../../../../hadamard-gate.md), in that temporal order. Defining the [Pauli X eigenstates](../../../../../../pauli-x-eigenstate.md) $|\pm\rangle=(|0\rangle\pm|1\rangle)/\sqrt2$, the successive [quantum states](../../../../../../quantum-state.md) are

$$
|0\rangle\xrightarrow{H}|+\rangle
\xrightarrow{T}\frac{|0\rangle+e^{i\pi/4}|1\rangle}{\sqrt2}
\xrightarrow{H}\boxed{\frac{|+\rangle+e^{i\pi/4}|-\rangle}{\sqrt2}.}
$$
