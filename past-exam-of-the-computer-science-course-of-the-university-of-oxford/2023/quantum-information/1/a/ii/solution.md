↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A [basic measurement](../../../../../../basic-measurement.md) is specified by an [orthonormal basis](../../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) $\{|b_j\rangle\}_{j=0}^{d-1}$. The [Born rule](../../../../../../born-rule.md) for the [density matrix](../../../../../../density-matrix-split.md) $\rho$ gives the outcome [probabilities](../../../../../../probability.md)

$$
\boxed{p(j)=\langle b_j|\rho|b_j\rangle=\operatorname{Tr}\bigl(\rho|b_j\rangle\langle b_j|\bigr).}
$$

These [probabilities](../../../../../../probability.md) are nonnegative by [positive semidefiniteness](../../../../../../positive-semidefinite-matrix.md) and sum to one by the [resolution of the identity](../../../../../../resolution-of-the-identity.md) and unit [trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md). In the [computational basis](../../../../../../computational-basis.md), the answers are simply the diagonal entries $\rho_{jj}$.[§2.3, Definition 2.34, measurement probabilities](../../../../../../the-theory-of-quantum-information-by-john-watrous/basic-notions-of-quantum-information.md)
