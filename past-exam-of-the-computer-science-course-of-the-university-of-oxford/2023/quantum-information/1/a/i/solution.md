A [density matrix](../../../../../../density-matrix-split.md) is a complex $d\times d$ [Hermitian matrix](../../../../../../hermitian-operator.md) that is [positive semidefinite](../../../../../../positive-semidefinite-matrix.md) and has unit [trace](../../../../../../matrix-trace.md):

$$
\boxed{\rho=\rho^\dagger,\qquad v^\dagger\rho v\geq0\ \text{for every }v\in\mathbb C^d,\qquad\operatorname{Tr}\rho=1.}
$$

Equivalently, its [eigenvalues](../../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are nonnegative and sum to one, with an [orthonormal basis](../../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) of [eigenvectors](../../../../../../eigenvector.md).[§1.1.2, printed pp. 17–18, equations (1.94)–(1.97)](../../../../../../the-theory-of-quantum-information-by-john-watrous/mathematical-preliminaries.md)
