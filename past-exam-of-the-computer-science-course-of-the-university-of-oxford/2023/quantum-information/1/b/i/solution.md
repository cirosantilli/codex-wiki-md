<h1 id="1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Expand the [density matrix](../../../../../../density-matrix-split.md) of the [Bell state](../../../../../../bell-state-split.md) and take the [partial trace](../../../../../../partial-trace.md) over Alice's [qubit](../../../../../../qubit.md):

$$
|\phi_+\rangle\langle\phi_+|=\frac12\sum_{j,k=0}^1|j\rangle\langle k|_A\otimes|j\rangle\langle k|_B,
\qquad
\rho_B=\frac12\sum_{j,k=0}^1\langle k|j\rangle|j\rangle\langle k|.
$$

The [orthonormality](../../../../../../orthonormal-set.md) of the [computational basis](../../../../../../computational-basis.md) removes the off-diagonal terms. Thus Bob's [reduced density matrix](../../../../../../reduced-density-matrix.md) is the [maximally mixed state](../../../../../../maximally-mixed-state.md)

$$
\boxed{\rho_B=\frac12\bigl(|0\rangle\langle0|+|1\rangle\langle1|\bigr)=\frac I2.}
$$
