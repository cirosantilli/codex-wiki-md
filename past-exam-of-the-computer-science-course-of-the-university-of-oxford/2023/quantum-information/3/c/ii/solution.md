<h1 id="3/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Grouping by the first [qubit](../../../../../../qubit.md) gives

$$
\frac12\bigl(|00\rangle+|01\rangle+|10\rangle-|11\rangle\bigr)
=\frac{|0\rangle|+\rangle+|1\rangle|-\rangle}{\sqrt2}.
$$

The vectors on both sides form [orthonormal bases](../../../../../../orthonormal-basis.md), so this is a [Schmidt decomposition](../../../../../../schmidt-decomposition.md) with two nonzero [Schmidt coefficients](../../../../../../schmidt-coefficient.md) $1/\sqrt2$. Hence **the state is maximally entangled**. Independently, its coefficient [matrix](../../../../../../matrix.md) has [determinant](../../../../../../determinant.md)

$$
\det\left[\frac12\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{pmatrix}\right]=-\frac12\ne0,
$$

which excludes a [product state](../../../../../../product-state.md) by the [two-qubit product-state determinant criterion](../../../../../../two-qubit-product-state-determinant-criterion.md). Each [reduced density matrix](../../../../../../reduced-density-matrix.md) is $I/2$.
