<h1 id="3/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The sign factor separates as $(-1)^{x+y}=(-1)^x(-1)^y$. Consequently the [quantum state](../../../../../../quantum-state.md) factors into a [tensor product of quantum systems](../../../../../../tensor-product-of-quantum-systems-split.md):

$$
\frac12\sum_{x,y=0}^1(-1)^{x+y}|x\rangle|y\rangle
=\left(\frac{|0\rangle-|1\rangle}{\sqrt2}\right)\otimes\left(\frac{|0\rangle-|1\rangle}{\sqrt2}\right)
=\boxed{|-\rangle\otimes|-\rangle.}
$$

It is a [product state](../../../../../../product-state.md), so **it is not entangled**.
