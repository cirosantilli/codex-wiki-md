A bipartite [pure state](../../../../../pure-state.md) is **entangled exactly when it is not a product state**: there are no normalized [quantum states](../../../../../quantum-state.md) $|u\rangle_A,|v\rangle_B$ such that

$$
|\psi\rangle_{AB}=|u\rangle_A\otimes|v\rangle_B.
$$

Equivalently, its [Schmidt rank](../../../../../schmidt-rank.md) exceeds one, or either [reduced density matrix](../../../../../reduced-density-matrix.md) is a [mixed state](../../../../../mixed-state.md). This definition concerns [quantum entanglement](../../../../../entangled-state.md) across the specified division into systems $A$ and $B$.
