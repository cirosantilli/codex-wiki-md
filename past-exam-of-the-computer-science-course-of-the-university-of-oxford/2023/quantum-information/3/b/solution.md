An [entangling gate](../../../../../entangling-gate.md) is a [unitary transformation](../../../../../unitary-operator.md) $U$ on two [quantum systems](../../../../../quantum-system-split.md) for which **at least one pure product input has an entangled output**:

$$
\boxed{\exists\,|u\rangle_A,|v\rangle_B:\quad
U(|u\rangle_A\otimes|v\rangle_B)\ \text{is entangled}.}
$$

It need not create [quantum entanglement](../../../../../entangled-state.md) on every [product state](../../../../../product-state.md). For example, the [controlled-NOT gate](../../../../../controlled-not-gate.md) leaves $|00\rangle$ a [product state](../../../../../product-state.md) but maps $|+\rangle|0\rangle$ to a [Bell state](../../../../../bell-state-split.md), so it is an [entangling gate](../../../../../entangling-gate.md).
