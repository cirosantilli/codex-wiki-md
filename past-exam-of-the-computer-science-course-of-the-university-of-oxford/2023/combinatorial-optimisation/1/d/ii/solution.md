**No.** Take the [hypergraph](../../../../../../hypergraph-split.md) with $V=\{a,b\}$ and its single [hyperedge](../../../../../../hyperedge.md) $\{a,b\}$ of weight $1$. Then $\{a\}\subseteq V$ but $f(\{a\})=1>0=f(V)$, violating the [monotone set function](../../../../../../monotone-set-function.md) property. This example already has strictly positive weights.
