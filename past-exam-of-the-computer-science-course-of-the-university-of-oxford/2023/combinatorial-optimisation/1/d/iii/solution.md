**No for arbitrary signed weights.** Give the same single [hyperedge](../../../../../../hyperedge.md) weight $-1$. For $A=\{a\}$ and $B=\{b\}$,

$$
f(A)+f(B)=-2<0=f(A\cap B)+f(A\cup B).
$$

This directly violates the defining inequality for a [submodular set function](../../../../../../submodular-set-function.md).
