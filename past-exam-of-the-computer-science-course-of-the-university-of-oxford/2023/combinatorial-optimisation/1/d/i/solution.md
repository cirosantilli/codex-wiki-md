**Yes, for arbitrary signed weights.** A [hyperedge](../../../../../../hyperedge.md) meets both $S$ and $V\setminus S$ exactly when it meets both $V\setminus S$ and $S$. Therefore $\delta(S)=\delta(V\setminus S)$, and their weighted sums agree. The [hypergraph cut function](../../../../../../hypergraph-cut-function.md) is a [symmetric set function](../../../../../../symmetric-set-function.md) regardless of the signs of its weights.
