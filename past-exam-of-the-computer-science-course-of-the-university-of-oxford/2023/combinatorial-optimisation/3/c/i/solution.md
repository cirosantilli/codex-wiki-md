The [maximum-weight matching](../../../../../../maximum-weight-matching.md) problem asks for a [matching in a graph](../../../../../../matching-graph-theory-split.md) $M\subseteq E$ maximizing its total weight:

$$
\boxed{\max_{M\text{ a matching}}w(M),\qquad w(M)=\sum_{e\in M}w(e).}
$$

The requirement is on weight; a [maximum cardinality matching](../../../../../../maximum-cardinality-matching.md) need not solve this [optimization](../../../../../../mathematical-optimization-split.md) problem.
