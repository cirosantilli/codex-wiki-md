A [matching in a graph](../../../../../matching-graph-theory-split.md) is a [subset](../../../../../subset.md) $M\subseteq E$ whose [edges](../../../../../edge-of-a-graph.md) have pairwise disjoint endpoints. It is a [perfect matching](../../../../../perfect-matching.md) when every [vertex](../../../../../vertex-graph-theory.md) is incident to exactly one [edge](../../../../../edge-of-a-graph.md) in $M$.
