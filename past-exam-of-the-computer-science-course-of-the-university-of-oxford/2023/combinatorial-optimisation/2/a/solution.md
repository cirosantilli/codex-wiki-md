A [matroid](../../../../../matroid.md) is a pair $(X,\mathcal I)$, where $X$ is a finite [ground set](../../../../../ground-set.md) and $\mathcal I\subseteq2^X$ is its collection of [matroid independent sets](../../../../../independent-set-of-a-matroid.md), satisfying the [matroid independence axioms](../../../../../matroid-independence-axioms.md):

- $\varnothing\in\mathcal I$.
- If $I\in\mathcal I$ and $J\subseteq I$, then $J\in\mathcal I$.
- If $I,J\in\mathcal I$ and $|I|<|J|$, some $x\in J\setminus I$ satisfies $I\cup\{x\}\in\mathcal I$.

The last axiom is augmentation. In particular, every maximal [matroid independent set](../../../../../independent-set-of-a-matroid.md) within a given [subset](../../../../../subset.md) has maximum [cardinality](../../../../../cardinality.md) there: otherwise augmentation would enlarge it.
