↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

If $x\in Y$, then $Y+x=Y$, so $r(Y+x)=r(Y)$. By the definition of the [closure of a matroid](../../../../../../closure-of-a-matroid.md), $x\in\sigma(Y)$. Hence **$Y\subseteq\sigma(Y)$**.
