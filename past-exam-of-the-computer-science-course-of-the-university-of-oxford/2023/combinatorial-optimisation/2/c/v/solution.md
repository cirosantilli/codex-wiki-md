<h1 id="2/c/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

Choose a maximal [matroid independent set](../../../../../../independent-set-of-a-matroid.md) $B\subseteq Y$. Its [cardinality](../../../../../../cardinality.md) is $r(Y)=r(Z)$, so it is also maximal in $Z$. Apply (iv) first inside $Y$ and then inside $Z$:

$$
\boxed{\sigma(Y)=\sigma(B)=\sigma(Z).}
$$

This proves that nested [subsets](../../../../../../subset.md) of equal [rank of a matroid](../../../../../../rank-of-a-matroid.md) have the same [closure of a matroid](../../../../../../closure-of-a-matroid.md).
