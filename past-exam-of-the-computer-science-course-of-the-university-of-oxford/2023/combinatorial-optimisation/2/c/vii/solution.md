<h1 id="2/c/vii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vii](../vii.md)

By (ii), $Y\subseteq\sigma(Y)$, and by (vi) these [subsets](../../../../../../subset.md) have equal [rank of a matroid](../../../../../../rank-of-a-matroid.md). Apply (v) to this pair to obtain

$$
\boxed{\sigma(\sigma(Y))=\sigma(Y).}
$$

Thus the [closure of a matroid](../../../../../../closure-of-a-matroid.md) is an [idempotent function](../../../../../../idempotent-function.md).
