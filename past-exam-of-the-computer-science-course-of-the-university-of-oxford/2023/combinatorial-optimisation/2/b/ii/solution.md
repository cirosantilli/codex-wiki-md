<h1 id="2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Up to [matroid isomorphism](../../../../../../matroid-isomorphism.md), the numbers for $n=1,2,3$ are **$2,4,8$**. A [matroid isomorphism](../../../../../../matroid-isomorphism.md) may relabel elements but preserves which [subsets](../../../../../../subset.md) are [matroid independent sets](../../../../../../independent-set-of-a-matroid.md).

For $n=1$, the [loop of a matroid](../../../../../../loop-of-a-matroid.md) and the [nonloop](../../../../../../nonloop-of-a-matroid.md) are different types. For $n=2$, there is one type each at [rank of a matroid](../../../../../../rank-of-a-matroid.md) zero and two, and two types at [rank of a matroid](../../../../../../rank-of-a-matroid.md) one, distinguished by having one or two [nonloops](../../../../../../nonloop-of-a-matroid.md). Thus there are four.

For $n=3$, the seven labelled examples of [rank of a matroid](../../../../../../rank-of-a-matroid.md) one reduce to three types, distinguished by having one, two or three [nonloops](../../../../../../nonloop-of-a-matroid.md). The seven labelled examples of [rank of a matroid](../../../../../../rank-of-a-matroid.md) two also reduce to three types: one [loop of a matroid](../../../../../../loop-of-a-matroid.md) and two independent elements; no [matroid loops](../../../../../../loop-of-a-matroid.md) and one pair of [parallel elements of a matroid](../../../../../../parallel-elements-of-a-matroid.md); or the [uniform matroid](../../../../../../uniform-matroid.md) $U_{2,3}$. Different choices of labels within each description are isomorphic. Together with the unique types of [rank of a matroid](../../../../../../rank-of-a-matroid.md) zero and three, this gives $1+3+3+1=8$. The [rank of a matroid](../../../../../../rank-of-a-matroid.md), number of [matroid loops](../../../../../../loop-of-a-matroid.md) and presence of [parallel elements of a matroid](../../../../../../parallel-elements-of-a-matroid.md) distinguish every type just listed.
