↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

**Yes: choose $Y=4/X$.** The equilibrium [expected payoffs](../../../../../expected-payoff.md) are opposite because this is a [zero-sum game](../../../../../zero-sum-game.md). They are equal exactly when both are zero. Part (b)'s value vanishes exactly when $XY=4$, and $4/X>0$ for every allowed $X>0$. This establishes both existence and the unique suitable positive choice of $Y$.
