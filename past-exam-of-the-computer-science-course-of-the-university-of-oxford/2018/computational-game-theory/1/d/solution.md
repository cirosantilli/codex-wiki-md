<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Under the intended positive rewards $0<h<d$, a hunter at the all-hare [strategy profile](../../../../../strategy-profile.md) receives $h$. A unilateral switch to deer leaves only one participant. Since the integer threshold satisfies $k>n/2$ with $n\geq3$, we have $k\geq2$, so that deer hunt fails and pays zero. Thus no player can gain by switching, and **the all-hare profile is a [Nash equilibrium](../../../../../nash-equilibrium.md)**. At $h=0$ it remains an equilibrium through indifference; negative $h$ would invalidate the claim.
