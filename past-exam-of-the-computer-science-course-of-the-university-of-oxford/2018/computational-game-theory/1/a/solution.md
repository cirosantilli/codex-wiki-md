<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a finite [normal-form game](../../../../../normal-form-game.md), a [mixed strategy](../../../../../mixed-strategy.md) $\sigma_i$ is a [probability distribution](../../../../../probability-distribution-split.md) over player $i$'s [pure strategies](../../../../../pure-strategy.md). Independent randomization by the players defines an [expected payoff](../../../../../expected-payoff.md) $u_i(\sigma)$. A [strategy profile](../../../../../strategy-profile.md) $\sigma$ is a [Nash equilibrium](../../../../../nash-equilibrium.md) if

$$
u_i(\sigma_i,\sigma_{-i})\geq u_i(\tau_i,\sigma_{-i})
$$

for every player $i$ and every alternative [mixed strategy](../../../../../mixed-strategy.md) $\tau_i$. By linearity in a player's own distribution, it is enough to check all pure deviations. Equivalently, every [pure strategy](../../../../../pure-strategy.md) in the [support of a mixed strategy](../../../../../support-of-a-mixed-strategy.md) must be a [best response](../../../../../best-response.md) to the other players' choices.
