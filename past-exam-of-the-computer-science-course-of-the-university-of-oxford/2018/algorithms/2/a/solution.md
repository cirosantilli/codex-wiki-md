For a finite [undirected graph](../../../../../undirected-graph.md) $G=(V,E)$, a [vertex cover](../../../../../vertex-cover.md) is a [subset](../../../../../subset.md) $C\subseteq V$ meeting every [edge](../../../../../edge-of-a-graph.md): for every $\{u,v\}\in E$, at least one of $u,v$ belongs to $C$. **The optimization problem asks for a [vertex cover](../../../../../vertex-cover.md) of minimum [cardinality](../../../../../cardinality.md).** Its usual decision version also supplies an [integer](../../../../../integer-split.md) $k$ and asks whether one exists with $|C|\leq k$.
