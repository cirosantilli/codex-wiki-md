# Stress inoculation training

↑ **Parent:** [Cognitive behavioral therapy](cognitive-behavioral-therapy.md)

Structured [coping](coping.md)-skills training with practice and application to appropriate challenges. The intervention is distinct from the hypothesis that naturally occurring [adversity](adversity.md) causes later protection; it does not mean deliberately imposing harmful experiences.
