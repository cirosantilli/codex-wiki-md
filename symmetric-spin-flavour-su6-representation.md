# Symmetric spin-flavour SU6 representation

↑ **Parent:** [Pauli constraint on three-quark flavour multiplets](pauli-constraint-on-three-quark-flavour-multiplets.md)

Three light [quarks](quark.md) have a six-dimensional single-quark spin-flavour space $\mathbb C^3\otimes\mathbb C^2$. A symmetric spatial ground state and the antisymmetric [three-quark colour singlet](three-quark-colour-singlet.md) require the symmetric cube of that space. Its dimension is $\binom83=56$, and restriction to flavour $SU(3)$ and spin $SU(2)$ gives a [baryon decuplet](baryon-decuplet.md) of spin $3/2$ and a [baryon octet](baryon-octet.md) of spin $1/2$. The two mixed-permutation multiplicity spaces pair once in the symmetric sector, explaining why the two flavour-octet copies in the unconstrained [tensor](tensor.md) cube do not represent two distinct ground-state octets.
