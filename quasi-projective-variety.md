# Quasi-projective variety

↑ **Parent:** [Quasi-projective algebraic set](quasi-projective-algebraic-set.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quasi-projective_variety)

An [irreducible variety](irreducible-variety.md) which admits a locally closed embedding into [projective space](projective-space-split.md). Open subsets of [affine varieties](affine-algebraic-set.md) and [blowups of algebraic varieties](blowing-up-algebraic-geometry.md) along closed centres give important examples.
