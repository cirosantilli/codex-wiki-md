# Spherical Neumann dipole images

↑ **Parent:** [Method of images](method-of-images.md)

For a [current dipole](current-dipole.md) in a homogeneous insulating sphere of radius $a$, let $\lambda=r^2/a^2$ and let $\Psi$ be its primary [electric potential](electric-potential.md). The displayed representation solves the interior [Neumann boundary condition](neumann-boundary-condition.md) up to an additive constant. It rewrites the spherical [Kelvin transform](kelvin-transform.md) correction and a distributed dipole correction using the [dipole line-integral identity](dipole-line-integral-identity.md). The image parameters depend on the observation radius; this is an exact parametrized representation rather than a fixed collection of image source positions.
