# Joint endpoint and maximum law for drifted Brownian motion

↑ **Parent:** [Finite-horizon maximum of Brownian motion with drift](finite-horizon-maximum-of-brownian-motion-with-drift.md)

For $W_t^\nu=B_t+\nu t$, $M_t^\nu=\sup_{s\le t}W_s^\nu$, $a>0$ and $x\le a$, the joint distribution function is $\Phi((x-\nu t)/\sqrt t)-e^{2a\nu}\Phi((x-2a-\nu t)/\sqrt t)$. The [Brownian reflection principle](reflection-principle-wiener-process.md) gives killed endpoint density $\phi_t(y)-\phi_t(2a-y)$ at zero drift. Multiplication by $e^{\nu y-\nu^2t/2}$ and completion of squares gives the formula.
