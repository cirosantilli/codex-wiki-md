# Two-dimensional vortex dynamics

↑ **Parent:** [Vortex dynamics](vortex-dynamics.md)

For inviscid planar [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md), write [velocity](velocity.md) as $(-\psi_y,\psi_x)$ and [vorticity](vorticity.md) as $\zeta=\nabla^2\psi$. Taking [curl](curl.md) of the [Euler equations for an inviscid fluid](euler-equations-for-an-inviscid-fluid.md) removes [pressure](pressure.md) and gives the displayed transport equation, with $J(\psi,\zeta)=\psi_x\zeta_y-\psi_y\zeta_x$. The [Poisson equation](poisson-equation.md) then recovers the [streamfunction](stream-function.md) at each instant. A velocity tangent to the plane cannot stretch its perpendicular [vorticity](vorticity.md) vector.
