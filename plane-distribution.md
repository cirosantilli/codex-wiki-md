# Plane distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Distribution (differential geometry)](distribution-differential-geometry.md)

A plane distribution on a three-manifold is a rank-two [distribution](distribution-mathematical-analysis.md). Locally it is the kernel of a nowhere-zero [differential 1-form](one-form.md) $\alpha$.
