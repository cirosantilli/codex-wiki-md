# Newtonian gravitational potential energy

↑ **Parent:** [Newtonian gravitational field](newtonian-gravitational-field.md)

Two point masses $m_1,m_2$ separated by distance $r$ have [potential energy](potential-energy.md)

$$
V(r)=-\frac{Gm_1m_2}{r}.
$$

The corresponding attractive force is $-\nabla V$.

This is the Newtonian point-mass specialization of [gravitational energy](gravitational-energy.md), with the reference value chosen at infinite separation.
