# Endosome

↑ **Parent:** [Organelle](organelle.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endosome)

An [endosome](endosome.md) is a membrane-enclosed sorting compartment in the [endocytosis](endocytosis.md) pathway. Cargo may return to the [cell membrane](cell-membrane-split.md) or proceed toward [lysosomes](lysosome.md); entry therefore does not by itself establish a digestive fate.[The Cell: A Molecular Approach, 2nd edition by Geoffrey M. Cooper; Figure 9.36: early endosomes, recycling and onward maturation](the-cell-a-molecular-approach-2nd-edition-by-geoffrey-m-cooper/lysosomes.md)
