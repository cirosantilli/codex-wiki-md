# Intrinsic carrier concentration

↑ **Parent:** [Intrinsic semiconductor](intrinsic-semiconductor.md)

For nondegenerate bands separated by $E_g$, the [intrinsic carrier concentration](intrinsic-carrier-concentration.md) is $n_i=\sqrt{N_cN_v}\,e^{-E_g/(2k_BT)}$, using the [effective density of states](effective-density-of-states.md) $N_c,N_v$. For fixed parabolic [band effective masses](band-effective-mass.md) and fixed gap, $n_i\propto T^{3/2}e^{-E_g/(2k_BT)}$.
