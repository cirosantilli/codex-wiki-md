# Nearest-centroid decision hyperplane

↑ **Parent:** [K-means clustering](k-means-clustering.md)

For two distinct [centroids](centroid.md) in a [Euclidean space](euclidean-norm.md), equal squared [Euclidean distances](euclidean-distance.md) expand to the displayed affine [hyperplane](hyperplane.md). It is their [perpendicular bisector](perpendicular-bisector.md); each closed [half-space](half-space.md) consists of points at least as close to the centroid on that side. This proves weak [linear separability](linear-separability.md) after a two-centroid nearest-assignment step, allowing ties on the boundary. Coincident [centroids](centroid.md) are an essential exception: every point ties and the distance comparison supplies no nonzero normal vector.
