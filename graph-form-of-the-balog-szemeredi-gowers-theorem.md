<h1 id="graph-form-of-the-balog-szemeredi-gowers-theorem">Graph form of the Balog-Szemerédi-Gowers theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Balog-Szemerédi-Gowers theorem](balog-szemeredi-gowers-theorem.md)

There are absolute $c,C>0$ such that finite sets $A,B$ of size $n$, a [bipartite graph](bipartite-graph.md) of density at least $\delta$, and a [restricted sumset](restricted-sumset.md) of size at most $Kn$ yield subsets $A',B'$ with $|A'|,|B'|\geq c\delta^Cn$ and $|A'+B'|\leq C\delta^{-C}K^Cn$. A graph formed by popular representations of sums turns this into the usual large-[additive energy](additive-energy.md) form of the [Balog-Szemerédi-Gowers theorem](balog-szemeredi-gowers-theorem.md).
