# Cross-spectrum of two stationary time series

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral density of a stationary process](spectral-density-of-a-stationary-process.md)

For zero-mean real [weakly stationary processes](weakly-stationary-process.md) whose cross-covariances are summable, the cross-spectrum is the [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md) of their lagged [covariance](covariance.md), with the displayed convention. Reality gives $f_{XY}(-\lambda)=\overline{f_{XY}(\lambda)}$, but the cross-spectrum need not be real or even. Exchanging the two series gives $f_{YX}(\lambda)=\overline{f_{XY}(\lambda)}$. The entries form a Hermitian [spectral density of a stationary process](spectral-density-of-a-stationary-process.md) matrix.
