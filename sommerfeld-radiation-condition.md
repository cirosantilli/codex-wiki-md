# Sommerfeld radiation condition

↑ **Parent:** [Far-field pattern](far-field-pattern.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sommerfeld_radiation_condition)

The Sommerfeld radiation condition selects outgoing solutions of the exterior [Helmholtz equation](helmholtz-equation.md). In three dimensions it requires $r(\partial_r u-iku)\to0$ as $r\to\infty$ uniformly in direction.
