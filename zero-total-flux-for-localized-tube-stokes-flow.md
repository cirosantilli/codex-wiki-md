# Zero total flux for localized tube Stokes flow

↑ **Parent:** [Stokes flow](stokes-flow-split.md)

Extend the [velocity](velocity.md) rigidly through a moving [sphere](sphere.md) in an incompressible tube flow. The extended [velocity](velocity.md) is continuous and divergence-free, so its total flux is independent of height. Quiescent far-field conditions make this flux zero. The [sphere](sphere.md) contributes $V_zA_s$ through a cross-section; the rotational contribution integrates to zero over its circular slice. Thus the fluid flux compensates the solid displacement. A descending [sphere](sphere.md) generates upward fluid flux in every section cutting its interior. This statement assumes a localized flow with no imposed throughflow and negligible tube-end effects.
