# Existential witness

↑ **Parent:** [Existential quantification](existential-quantification.md)

An object, or a term denoting an object, that satisfies the predicate asserted by an [existential quantification](existential-quantification.md). In a [natural deduction](natural-deduction.md) use of the [existential quantifier elimination rule](existential-quantifier-elimination-rule.md), a fresh [eigenvariable](eigenvariable.md) represents an otherwise unspecified witness inside a [subproof](subproof.md). The [eigenvariable condition](eigenvariable-condition.md) prevents it from occurring freely in the exported conclusion or the other undischarged assumptions; violating this restriction is [witness escape](witness-escape.md).
