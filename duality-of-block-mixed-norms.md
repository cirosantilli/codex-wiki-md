# Duality of block mixed norms

↑ **Parent:** [Block mixed norm](block-mixed-norm.md)

For a [linear map](linear-map.md) $A$ between finite-dimensional real [inner product spaces](inner-product-space.md),

$$
\lambda\|Au\|_{q,1}=\sup_{\max_i\|p_i\|_{q'}\le\lambda}\langle A^*p,u\rangle.
$$

Thus the penalty is the [support function](support-function.md) of the [compact](compact-space.md) [convex set](convex-set.md) $A^*\{p:\max_i\|p_i\|_{q'}\le\lambda\}$. The [Moreau decomposition](moreau-decomposition.md) makes its [proximal operator](proximal-operator.md) the identity minus the [Euclidean projection onto a convex set](euclidean-projection-onto-a-convex-set.md).
