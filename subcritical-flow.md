# Subcritical flow

↑ **Parent:** [Froude number](froude-number.md)

A shallow [gravity current](gravity-current.md) is subcritical when its [Froude number](froude-number.md) is less than one: a gravity disturbance can propagate upstream relative to the bed. This definition uses the characteristic speed $\sqrt{g'h}$ appropriate to the stated model.
