# Algol binary

↑ **Parent:** [Roche-lobe overflow](roche-lobe-overflow.md)

An Algol binary is a [semidetached binary](semidetached-binary.md) in which an evolved, less massive [donor star](donor-star.md) fills its [Roche lobe](roche-lobe.md) while its more massive [mass gainer](mass-gaining-star.md) remains near the [main sequence](main-sequence.md). [Binary mass-ratio reversal](binary-mass-ratio-reversal.md) resolves the [Algol paradox](algol-paradox.md). Eclipsing members commonly have [Algol variable](algol-variable.md) light curves, but that observational class also includes detached systems.
