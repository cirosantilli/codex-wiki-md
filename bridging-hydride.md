# Bridging hydride

↑ **Parent:** [Hydride](hydride.md)

A [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) ligand bonded to more than one skeletal centre. A B–H–B bridge in a [borane](borane.md) is commonly represented as a [three-centre two-electron bond](three-centre-two-electron-bond.md); it is not two independent ordinary B–H single bonds.
