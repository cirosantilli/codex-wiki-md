# Straightening theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Local flow](local-flow.md)

Near a point where a smooth [vector field](vector-field.md) is nonzero, there are coordinates in which it is $\partial/\partial x_1$. Flow a transverse coordinate slice by the [local flow](local-flow.md); the [inverse function theorem](inverse-function-theorem.md) makes the resulting map a coordinate chart. This is the first step of the [coordinate proof of the Frobenius theorem](coordinate-proof-of-the-frobenius-theorem.md).
