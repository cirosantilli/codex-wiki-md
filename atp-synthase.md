# ATP synthase

↑ **Parent:** [Enzyme](enzyme-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ATP_synthase)

[ATP synthase](atp-synthase.md) is a [molecular motor](molecular-motor.md) that couples downhill [proton](proton.md) movement across a [cell membrane](cell-membrane-split.md) to formation of [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) from [ADP](adenosine-diphosphate.md) and [inorganic phosphate](inorganic-phosphate.md). In [oxidative phosphorylation](oxidative-phosphorylation.md), this is the machinery that makes [chemiosmosis](chemiosmosis.md) productive: an [electrochemical gradient](electrochemical-gradient.md) becomes reusable chemical driving capacity.[Watanabe, Rotary catalysis of FoF1-ATP synthase, 2013, p. 51, opening paragraphs](rotary-catalysis-of-fof1-atp-synthase-by-rikiya-watanabe.md)

Its usefulness is a conversion between currencies. A membrane-spanning [proton-motive force](proton-motive-force.md) can power a local machine, while newly formed [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) can participate in [energy coupling](energy-coupling.md) elsewhere. The [enzyme](enzyme-split.md) supplies a route between these forms of [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md); it cannot make an energetically uphill total cycle favourable merely by accelerating its chemistry.
