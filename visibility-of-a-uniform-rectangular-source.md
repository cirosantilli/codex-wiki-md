# Visibility of a uniform rectangular source

↑ **Parent:** [Complex visibility](complex-visibility.md)

With constant [surface brightness](surface-brightness.md) over a centred angular rectangle of widths $\Theta_x,\Theta_y$, and a nearly uniform [primary beam](primary-beam.md) over that rectangle, the [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md) separates into two one-dimensional integrals. The result is $V(u,v)=S_\nu\operatorname{sinc}(\pi\Theta_xu)\operatorname{sinc}(\pi\Theta_yv)$, where $\operatorname{sinc}x=\sin x/x$. The zero-spacing value is the integrated [spectral flux density](spectral-flux-density.md), while the first axial null is at $|u|=1/\Theta_x$. Long baselines therefore do not measure the total flux of a resolved rectangle.
