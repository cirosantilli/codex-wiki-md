# Rotational invariance of the Laplacian

↑ **Parent:** [Laplacian](laplacian.md)

For a constant [orthogonal matrix](orthogonal-matrix.md) $R$, the [Laplacian](laplacian.md) is unchanged by the coordinate transformation $\xi=Rx$. If $u$ is twice continuously differentiable, the [chain rule](chain-rule.md) gives

$$
\sum_i\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial x_i^2}=\sum_{i,j,k}R_{ji}R_{ki}\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial\xi_j\partial\xi_k}=\sum_j\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial\xi_j^2},
$$

because $\sum_iR_{ji}R_{ki}=\delta_{jk}$ by [orthogonality](orthogonal-vectors.md) of the rows. Here $\delta_{jk}$ is the [Kronecker delta](kronecker-delta.md). In two dimensions, the [rotation matrix](rotation-matrix.md) $\begin{pmatrix}a&-b\\b&a\end{pmatrix}$ with $a^2+b^2=1$ makes the mixed [partial derivatives](partial-derivative.md) cancel explicitly. The same argument works for reflections and translations, so the [Laplacian](laplacian.md) depends on the [Euclidean geometry](euclidean-geometry-split.md) rather than the orientation or origin of the coordinates.
