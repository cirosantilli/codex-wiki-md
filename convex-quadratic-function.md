# Convex quadratic function

↑ **Parent:** [Convex function](convex-function.md)

A [convex quadratic function](convex-quadratic-function.md) on $\mathbb R^n$ has the form $q(x)=\tfrac12x^THx+b^Tx+c$ with a symmetric [positive semidefinite matrix](positive-semidefinite-matrix.md) $H$. Its Hessian is $H$, and $q(y)-q(x)-\nabla q(x)\cdot(y-x)=\tfrac12(y-x)^TH(y-x)\geq0$, proving convexity directly. A stationary point is a global minimum. On a compact polytope, a minimum either is stationary in the interior or minimizes a restriction to a boundary face.
