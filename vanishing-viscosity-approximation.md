# Vanishing viscosity approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Viscous scalar conservation law](viscous-scalar-conservation-law.md)

This approximation studies the limit of a [viscous scalar conservation law](viscous-scalar-conservation-law.md) as its positive diffusion coefficient tends to zero. Under hypotheses giving compactness and appropriate convergence, the viscous entropy dissipation selects an [entropy solution](entropy-solution.md) of the inviscid [scalar conservation law](scalar-conservation-law.md). Fixed-phase decreasing [travelling waves](travelling-wave.md) for a strictly convex flux converge to an [entropy shock](entropy-shock.md). Translating each profile differently can change or destroy the limit, so phase or initial data must specify the family.
