# Optical lattice

↑ **Parent:** [Optics](optics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optical_lattice)

An optical lattice is a periodic [optical trap](optical-tweezers.md) produced by interfering light fields. Its [periodic potential](periodic-potential.md) gives atomic [Bloch states](bloch-state.md) and [energy bands](energy-band.md). For the displayed one-dimensional lattice, the [recoil energy](recoil-energy.md) is $E_R=\hbar^2\pi^2/(2md^2)$ and $s$ measures the depth in recoil units.
