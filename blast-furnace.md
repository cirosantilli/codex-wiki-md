# Blast furnace

↑ **Parent:** [Iron extraction](iron-extraction.md)

A shaft furnace with a blast of hot air, in which descending ore and ascending gases exchange [heat](heat-split.md) and react. In [iron](iron.md) extraction it supplies molten [pig iron](pig-iron.md) and [slag](slag.md).
