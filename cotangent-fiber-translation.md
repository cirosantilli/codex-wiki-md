# Cotangent fiber translation

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical one-form on a cotangent bundle](canonical-one-form-on-a-cotangent-bundle.md)

Translation by a [differential one-form](one-form.md) $\beta$ is $(x,\xi)\mapsto(x,\xi+\beta_x)$. It changes the [canonical one-form on a cotangent bundle](canonical-one-form-on-a-cotangent-bundle.md) by $\pi^*\beta$. A [closed differential form](closed-differential-form.md) gives a [symplectomorphism](symplectomorphism.md); an [exact differential form](exact-differential-form.md) gives a [Hamiltonian diffeomorphism](hamiltonian-isotopy.md) when compact support is not required.
