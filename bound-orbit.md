# Bound orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Orbit (dynamical system)](orbit-dynamical-system.md)

An [orbit](orbit-dynamical-system.md) that remains within a bounded spatial region for all times for which the motion is defined. In an attractive inverse-distance [central potential](central-potential.md) with zero [potential energy](potential-energy.md) at infinity, a noncolliding [orbit](orbit-dynamical-system.md) is bound when its total [mechanical energy](mechanical-energy.md) is negative. That sign test depends on the potential and its energy origin; it is not a universal definition of bounded motion. A [closed orbit](closed-orbit.md) repeats its position and velocity, whereas a general [bound orbit](bound-orbit.md) can precess without closing.
