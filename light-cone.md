# Light cone

↑ **Parent:** [Minkowski metric](minkowski-metric.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Light_cone)

The light cone through an event consists of null displacement vectors. In one spatial dimension its two directions satisfy $x=\pm ct$.
