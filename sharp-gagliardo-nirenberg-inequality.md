# Sharp Gagliardo-Nirenberg inequality

↑ **Parent:** [NLS ground state](nls-ground-state.md)

In dimension $d$, the mass-critical sharp inequality is

$$
\|u\|_{2+4/d}^{2+4/d}
\leq\frac{d+2}{d\|Q\|_2^{4/d}}
\|u\|_2^{4/d}\|\nabla u\|_2^2,
$$

and equality holds exactly on the phase, translation, and scaling orbit of the [NLS ground state](nls-ground-state.md) $Q$. For $d=2$ this becomes $\|u\|_4^4\leq2\|Q\|_2^{-2}\|u\|_2^2\|\nabla u\|_2^2$.
