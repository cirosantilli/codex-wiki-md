# Three-region calcium nullcline

↑ **Parent:** [Two-pool calcium model](two-pool-calcium-model.md)

For $J=\beta u^2/(1+u^2)$ and $K=u^4/(1+u^4)$ with small leak $\delta$, the positive [nullcline](nullcline.md) is unique at each $u$ because its [derivative](derivative.md) with respect to $v$ is strictly negative. Its asymptotic regions are a high-store balance $v\simeq(J-K)/\delta$, a crossing balance near $J(u_c)=K(u_c)$, and a negligible-leak balance $v^2/(1+v^2)\simeq J/K$. The crossing relation is $(K_c'-J_c')(u-u_c)\simeq K_c/v^2-\delta v$. These describe one continuous curve, not three distinct equilibria at a fixed cytoplasmic [concentration](concentration.md).
