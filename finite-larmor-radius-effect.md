# Finite Larmor radius effect

↑ **Parent:** [Gyrokinetics](gyrokinetics.md)

Modification of the [plasma](plasma-physics.md) response because particles sample fields around a finite [cyclotron orbit](gyromotion.md). Effects become order one at $k_\perp\rho_s=O(1)$; even at $k_\perp\rho_s\ll1$, the leading polarization correction can be essential to a slow [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation-split.md).
