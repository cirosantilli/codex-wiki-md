# Metacognition

↑ **Parent:** [Animal behaviour](animal-behaviour-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metacognition)

Monitoring or control of one's own [cognition](cognition.md). [Metacognitive monitoring](metacognitive-monitoring.md) estimates aspects of current or future performance, while [metacognitive control](metacognitive-control.md) uses such estimates to guide behaviour. [Metamemory](metamemory.md) applies these functions to remembering; [uncertainty responses](uncertainty-response.md) provide a way to study some of them without verbal reports. Successful behaviour alone need not establish [introspection](introspection.md) or subjective awareness.
