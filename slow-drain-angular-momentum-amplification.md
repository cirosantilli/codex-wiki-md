# Slow-drain angular-momentum amplification

↑ **Parent:** [Vortex dynamics](vortex-dynamics.md)

Axisymmetric [inviscid flow](inviscid-flow.md) has no azimuthal pressure torque, so each parcel conserves specific axial [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) $j=ru_\theta=r^2\omega$. If late draining fluid originally lay near the rim $R$ of a uniformly rotating pool, then $j\simeq\omega_0R^2$ and $\omega\simeq\omega_0R^2/r^2$. The late-origin assumption is essential; torque conservation alone gives $j=\omega_0r_0^2$ for each parcel's own initial radius $r_0$. Quasistatic radial and vertical pressure balance then gives free-surface depression $h(r)=\omega_0^2R^4(r^{-2}-R^{-2})/(2g)$. The singular profile applies outside a finite drain or vortex core, not at the rotation axis.
