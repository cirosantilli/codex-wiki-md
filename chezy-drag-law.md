<h1 id="chezy-drag-law">Chézy drag law</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Hydraulic radius](hydraulic-radius.md)

A quadratic boundary-drag model relates [shear stress](shear-stress.md) to mean flow [velocity](velocity.md), with dimensionless resistance coefficient $c_d>0$. Uniform [steady flow](steady-flow.md) down slope $S$ balances $\rho gAS=\tau\ell$, giving $|u|=\sqrt{gRS/c_d}$. Its sign is chosen so drag opposes the motion.
