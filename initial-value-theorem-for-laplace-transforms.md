# Initial value theorem for Laplace transforms

↑ **Parent:** [Laplace transform](laplace-transform.md)

For a [function](function-split.md) continuous at zero and of exponential order, the displayed limit recovers its initial value from its [Laplace transform](laplace-transform.md). Substitute $u=pt$ to obtain $p\mathcal Lf(p)=\int_0^\infty e^{-u}f(u/p)\,du$ and apply the [dominated convergence theorem](dominated-convergence-theorem.md); an exponential-order bound controls the tail. The limit along positive real $p$ suffices for this version.
