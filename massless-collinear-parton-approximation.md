# Massless collinear parton approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Parton model](parton-model.md)

The leading high-energy approximation neglects [quark](quark.md) and target masses and transverse constituent momentum. A lightlike reference momentum is used for the [hadron](hadron.md); this does not describe a massless particle at rest.
