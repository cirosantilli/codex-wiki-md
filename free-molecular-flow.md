# Free molecular flow

↑ **Parent:** [Rarefied gas flow approximations](rarefied-gas-flow-approximations.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_molecular_flow)

In [free molecular flow](free-molecular-flow.md), gas–gas [collisions](collision.md) are negligible over the trajectories relevant to the observable. Molecules still encounter walls, whose scattering of [momentum](momentum-split.md) and [energy](energy-split.md) must be modeled. In a long narrow tube, $\ell_{\mathrm{mfp}}$ exceeding the diameter does not by itself prove that every complete transmission path is collision-free: repeated wall reflections can create much longer paths. The approximation must be checked against those paths as well as the width. [Fitzpatrick, Molecular Flow, wall encounters in a long tube](https://farside.ph.utexas.edu/teaching/355/Surveyhtml/node223.html)
