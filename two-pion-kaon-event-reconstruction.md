# Two-pion kaon event reconstruction

↑ **Parent:** [CP violation](cp-violation.md)

A two-track [invariant mass](invariant-mass.md) and a direction test answer different questions. In units $c=1$, assign each track the [pion](pion.md) [mass](mass.md) $m_\pi$ and reconstruct its [momentum](momentum-split.md) before magnetic deflection. With $p_\pm=|\mathbf p_\pm|$ and opening angle $\alpha$,

$$
E_\pm=\sqrt{p_\pm^2+m_\pi^2},\qquad
M_{\pi\pi}^2=2m_\pi^2+2(E_+E_--p_+p_-\cos\alpha).
$$

This follows by expanding the squared sum of the two [four-momenta](four-momentum.md). The angle $\theta$ between the reconstructed parent [momentum](momentum-split.md) $\mathbf P=\mathbf p_++\mathbf p_-$ and the neutral [particle beam](particle-beam.md) direction $\hat{\mathbf n}$ satisfies

$$
\cos\theta=\frac{\mathbf P\cdot\hat{\mathbf n}}{|\mathbf P|}.
$$

A fully reconstructed two-body [decay channel](decay-channel.md) gives the parent [mass](mass.md) and $\theta\simeq0$, within experimental resolution. A three-body [decay channel](decay-channel.md) with an unseen [neutrino](neutrino.md) generally misses both constraints; an accidental correct [invariant mass](invariant-mass.md) alone is insufficient. Notice that the track opening angle $\alpha$ need not be small even when the parent direction angle $\theta$ vanishes.

The 1964 analysis selected $490<M_{\pi\pi}<510$ MeV and remeasured events with $\cos\theta>0.9995$, comparing adjacent [mass](mass.md) bands.[p.139, Figs.2–3 and remeasurement selection](evidence-for-the-2-pi-decay-of-the-k%E2%82%82%E2%81%B0-meson-by-james-h-christenson-and-others.md)

For an original dimensionless example, take $m_\pi=1$, $\hat{\mathbf n}=(0,0,1)$ and $\mathbf p_\pm=(\pm\sqrt3,0,3)$. Each energy is $\sqrt{13}$; the opening angle is $60$ degrees since $\cos\alpha=(9-3)/(9+3)=1/2$. Yet $\mathbf P=(0,0,6)$ points exactly along the beam, and $M_{\pi\pi}^2=4(13)-36=16$. Thus a parent of [mass](mass.md) $4$ passes both ideal tests. These chosen numbers illustrate [four-momentum conservation](four-momentum-conservation.md), not detector performance.
