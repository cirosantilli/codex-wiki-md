# Second-order cone programming

↑ **Parent:** [Second-order cone](second-order-cone.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second-order_cone_programming)

A second-order cone program has a linear objective and affine constraints taking values in products of [second-order cones](second-order-cone.md) and nonnegative orthants. Norm epigraphs have the form $(t,z)\in\mathcal Q$; quadratic epigraphs admit the affine lift $((q+1)/2,(q-1)/2,a)\in\mathcal Q_3$, equivalent to $q\geq a^2$. Product-cone [self-concordant barriers](self-concordant-barrier.md) give [interior-point methods](interior-point-method.md).
