# Lagrangian connected sum

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrangian surgery](lagrangian-surgery.md)

If two embedded Lagrangian surfaces meet transversely in one point, [Lagrangian surgery](lagrangian-surgery.md) at that point produces their connected sum. For orientable surfaces $\Sigma_{g_1}$ and $\Sigma_{g_2}$, the result is $\Sigma_{g_1+g_2}$.
