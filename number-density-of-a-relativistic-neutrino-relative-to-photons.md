# Number density of a relativistic neutrino relative to photons

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmic neutrino background](cosmic-neutrino-background.md)

With negligible [chemical potential](chemical-potential.md), the [Fermi-Dirac distribution](fermi-dirac-distribution.md) has $3/4$ of the boson number density per degree of freedom at equal temperature. A species with $g_\nu$ populated states therefore has $n_\nu/n_\gamma=(3g_\nu/8)(T_\nu/T_\gamma)^3$, since [photons](photon.md) have two states. This remains the number-density ratio after the [neutrino](neutrino.md) becomes massive if its comoving particle number is conserved.
