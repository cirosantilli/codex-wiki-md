# Dual-process theory

↑ **Parent:** [Cognitive psychology](cognitive-psychology.md)

A family of accounts distinguishing relatively automatic or intuitive processing from more controlled, demanding processing. Specific theories differ about conflict detection, engagement and correction; a biased final response does not establish that statistical information was never processed.
