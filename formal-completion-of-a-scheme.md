# Formal completion of a scheme

↑ **Parent:** [Formal scheme](formal-scheme.md)

For a [closed subscheme](closed-subscheme.md) with [ideal sheaf](ideal-sheaf-of-a-closed-subscheme.md) $\mathcal I$ in a locally [Noetherian scheme](noetherian-scheme.md), the completion is the [formal scheme](formal-scheme.md) on $|Y|$ with topological structure sheaf $\varprojlim_n(\mathcal O_X/\mathcal I^{n+1})|_{|Y|}$. On a Noetherian [affine scheme](affine-scheme.md) $\operatorname{Spec}A$, it is $\operatorname{Spf}(\varprojlim_nA/I^{n+1})$. Its [adic topology](adic-topology.md) remembers every [infinitesimal neighbourhood](infinitesimal-neighbourhood-of-a-closed-subscheme.md).
