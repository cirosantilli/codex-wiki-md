# Reflexive relation

↑ **Parent:** [Binary relation](binary-relation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reflexive_relation)

A relation $\mathrel R$ on $X$ is reflexive when $x\mathrel R x$ for every $x\in X$.
