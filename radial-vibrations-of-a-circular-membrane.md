# Radial vibrations of a circular membrane

↑ **Parent:** [Wave equation](wave-equation-split.md)

A fixed circular [elastic membrane](elastic-membrane.md) of radius $a$ has radial [normal modes](normal-mode.md) satisfying the displayed [Sturm-Liouville problem](sturm-liouville-problem.md), with weight $r$, $R(a)=0$ and regularity at the origin. The regular solutions are [Bessel functions of the first kind](bessel-function-of-the-first-kind.md) $J_0(\sqrt\lambda r)$. Integration by parts gives the positive [Rayleigh quotient](rayleigh-quotient.md) $\lambda=\int_0^a r|R'|^2dr/\int_0^a r|R|^2dr$. Distinct modes are orthogonal in the [weighted inner product](weighted-inner-product.md) $\int_0^a r\overline R S\,dr$.
