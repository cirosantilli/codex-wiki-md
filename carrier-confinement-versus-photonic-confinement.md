# Carrier confinement versus photonic confinement

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum confinement](quantum-confinement.md)

Carrier confinement quantizes electronic and hole states and changes transition energies, [joint density of states](joint-density-of-states.md) and envelope overlaps. A [waveguide](waveguide.md) or cavity confines electromagnetic [modes](mode-statistics.md) and changes optical overlap, resonances or emission rates. Both can occur in a semiconductor light source, but a small electronic island is not automatically a designed [optical cavity](optical-cavity.md).
