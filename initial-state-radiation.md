# Initial-state radiation

↑ **Parent:** [Particle detection](particle-detection-split.md)

[radiation](radiation.md) emitted by an incoming particle before the hard collision. At a hadron collider an energetic recoil [jet](jet-particle-physics.md) can help trigger and measure events whose nearly degenerate parent-daughter decays otherwise yield soft visible particles.
