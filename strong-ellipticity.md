# Strong ellipticity

↑ **Parent:** [Acoustic tensor](acoustic-tensor.md)

The elastic stiffness satisfies $c_{ijkl}a_ip_ja_kp_l>0$ for every nonzero [polarization](polarization-waves.md) $a$ and propagation [vector](vector.md) $p$. It makes the [elastic acoustic tensor](acoustic-tensor.md) positive definite, ensuring positive squared wave [speeds](speed.md). Positive [strain](strain.md) [energy](energy-split.md) is a sufficient stronger condition in ordinary [linear elasticity](linear-elasticity.md).
