# Quark rephasing and CP conservation

↑ **Parent:** [Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa matrix](cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md)

For nondegenerate [quark masses](quark-mass.md), changing the constant [quantum phases](quantum-phase.md) of [quark fields](quark-field.md) preserves their diagonal mass terms. Adopt the convention

$$
V'_{ij}=e^{-i\alpha_i}V_{ij}e^{i\beta_j}.
$$

Such [quantum field rephasings](quantum-field-rephasing.md) alter individual entries of the [CKM matrix](cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md) without altering physics. Physical [CP symmetry](cp-symmetry.md) in this mixing sector means that some allowed convention makes the [CKM matrix](cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md) real. [Toshihide Maskawa](toshihide-maskawa.md) emphasized the distinction between complex entries and an irremovable [CP-violating phase](cp-violating-phase.md).[pp. 1029–1030, Eqs. (1)–(2) and phase counting](nobel-lecture-what-does-cp-violation-tell-us-by-toshihide-maskawa.md)

Here is a constructive two-generation argument. For a generic $2\times2$ [unitary matrix](unitary-matrix.md), use the three effective row/column phases to make $V_{11},V_{12},V_{22}$ real and positive. Column [orthogonality](orthogonal-vectors.md) then forces $V_{21}$ to be real and negative. Row normalization leaves just the real rotation specified by the [Cabibbo angle](cabibbo-angle.md). Zero entries are limiting cases and introduce no [weak phase](weak-phase-cp-violation.md). Thus two-generation [quark mixing](quark-mixing.md) cannot violate [CP symmetry](cp-symmetry.md) through this mechanism, even when its entries initially look complex.

For three [quark generations](quark-generation.md), the [Jarlskog invariant](jarlskog-invariant.md) provides an obstruction to repeating that construction: all four phase factors cancel from its defining quartet. If its imaginary part is nonzero, no choice of [quantum field rephasings](quantum-field-rephasing.md) can make every entry real. With nondegenerate [quark masses](quark-mass.md), this proves physical [CP violation](cp-violation.md) in the mixing sector. This argument concerns the [weak interaction](weak-interaction.md); it does not dispose of the separate [Strong CP problem](strong-cp-problem.md).
