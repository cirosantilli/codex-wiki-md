# Quark rephasing and CP conservation

↑ **Parent:** [Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa matrix](cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md)

For nondegenerate quark masses, independent phase changes of the up- and down-quark mass eigenfields alter the numerical [CKM matrix](cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md) by row and column phases without changing physics. Thus [CP symmetry](cp-symmetry.md) requires that some allowed phase convention makes the matrix real, rather than requiring real entries in every convention. With $N$ generic families, removing $2N-1$ phases from a unitary matrix leaves $(N-1)(N-2)/2$ physical CP-violating phases and $N(N-1)/2$ mixing angles.
