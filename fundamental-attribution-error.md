# Fundamental attribution error

↑ **Parent:** [Attribution theory](attribution-theory.md)

Underweighting situational determinants relative to personal determinants when explaining behavior. [Correspondence bias](correspondence-bias.md) concerns the more specific inference of a corresponding disposition from observed conduct. Neither term establishes that a particular inference is mistaken without a justified comparison.
