# Fourfold graphene Hall sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum Hall effect](quantum-hall-effect.md)

With unresolved [spin](spin.md) and valley splittings, each [graphene](graphene.md) [Landau level](landau-level.md) has areal degeneracy $4e|B|/h$. Counting carriers from a half-filled zero level yields integer Hall plateaux at filling factors $\pm2,\pm6,\pm10,\ldots$. For fixed positive density $n$, plateau-centre fields are $nh/(e\nu)$ and ideal extended-level centres have $B_j=nh/(4ej)$. Localization and level broadening determine finite plateau widths; degeneracy counting does not determine them.
