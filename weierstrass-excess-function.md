# Weierstrass excess function

↑ **Parent:** [Calculus of variations](calculus-of-variations-split.md)

This excess measures the height of the slope-dependent integrand above its tangent line at the reference slope $p$, evaluated at a trial slope $q$. A [convex function](convex-function.md) of slope has nonnegative excess. At a smooth strong local minimizer, localized needle variations force nonnegative excess along the minimizing curve for every trial slope. It detects fast-gradient competitors that the [second variation](second-variation.md) around a fixed derivative can miss.
