# Three-body orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Three-body problem](three-body-problem.md)

A trajectory whose dynamics require gravitational forces from more than one attracting body. Around a synchronously rotating moon, a maintained trajectory involving the planet–moon system is distinct from a simple moon-centred stationary [Kepler orbit](kepler-orbit.md); the moon's [Hill sphere](hill-sphere.md) limits the latter approximation.
