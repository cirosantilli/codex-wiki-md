# Decibel

↑ **Parent:** [System of units](system-of-units.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decibel)

A comparison using a [logarithm](logarithm.md) of two [powers](power.md) is expressed as $10\log_{10}(P_2/P_1)\,\mathrm{dB}$. Positive loss instead uses $10\log_{10}(P_{\rm in}/P_{\rm out})\,\mathrm{dB}$. For [wave amplitudes](wave-amplitude.md), a factor twenty is appropriate only when the corresponding [power](power.md) ratio equals the square of the amplitude ratio.
