# Left derived functor

↑ **Parent:** [Homological algebra](homological-algebra.md)

For an additive right-exact [functor](functor.md) on module categories, apply it to a [projective resolution](projective-resolution.md) and take [homology](homology-split.md). Lifts of module maps exist by projectivity; any two lifts are [chain homotopic](chain-homotopy.md), giving independence and functoriality. The zeroth derived functor is naturally $F$, positive degrees vanish on projective modules, and the [horseshoe lemma](horseshoe-lemma.md) produces a long exact homology sequence from each short exact sequence. A [Tor functor](tor-functor.md) is the left derived functor of a [tensor product of modules](tensor-product-of-modules.md) functor.
