# Allotropy

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical element](chemical-element.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allotropy)

The existence of different structural forms of the same [chemical element](chemical-element.md). [Diamond](diamond.md) and [graphite](graphite.md) are crystalline examples for [carbon](carbon.md); molecular oxygen and ozone illustrate that [allotropy](allotropy.md) need not consist only of different packing arrangements of identical molecules.
