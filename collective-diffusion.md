# Collective diffusion

↑ **Parent:** [Diffusion](diffusion.md)

Relaxation of a [density](density.md) or composition fluctuation involving many constituents. Its coefficient can depend on osmotic compressibility and collective [mobility](order-parameter-mobility.md), and need not equal the tagged-particle [self-diffusion](self-diffusion.md) coefficient at nonzero [concentration](concentration.md).
