# Adiabatic bulk modulus

↑ **Parent:** [Bulk modulus](bulk-modulus.md)

The [pressure](pressure-split.md) response to compression at fixed entropy, $K_S=\rho(\partial P/\partial\rho)_S$. Seismic compressions are commonly approximately adiabatic; a background [density](density.md) gradient follows the corresponding derivative only when composition and entropy assumptions also apply.
