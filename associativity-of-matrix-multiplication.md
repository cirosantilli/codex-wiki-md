# Associativity of matrix multiplication

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix multiplication](matrix-multiplication.md)

For dimension-compatible [matrices](matrix.md), $(AB)C=A(BC)$. In coordinates both sides have $(i,j)$ entry $\sum_{k,l}A_{ik}B_{kl}C_{lj}$; distributing and regrouping the finite [sums](sum.md) proves equality. This permits accumulating a product left to right even when a recursive [algorithm](algorithm-split.md) groups the same transformations from the right. It does not permit changing factor order, because [matrix multiplication](matrix-multiplication.md) is generally not [commutative](commutativity.md).
