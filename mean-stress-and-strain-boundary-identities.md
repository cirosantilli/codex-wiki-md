# Mean stress and strain boundary identities

↑ **Parent:** [Linear elasticity](linear-elasticity.md)

Apply the [divergence theorem](divergence-theorem.md) to $\partial_k(x_i\sigma_{jk})$ and use momentum balance $\sigma_{jk,k}=\rho(a_j-b_j)$ and stress symmetry to obtain the displayed mean [stress](stress.md) identity. Applying the same theorem to the [infinitesimal strain tensor](infinitesimal-strain-tensor.md) gives $V\overline e_{ij}=\frac12\int_S(u_in_j+u_jn_i)\,dS$. The acceleration term cannot be dropped during a transient.
