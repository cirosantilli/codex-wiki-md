# Stable word length

↑ **Parent:** [Word metric](word-metric.md)

For a [word metric](word-metric.md) from a finite generating set, the [stable word length](stable-word-length.md) is

$$
\tau_S(g)=\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{|g^n|_S}{n}=\inf_{n\geq1}\frac{|g^n|_S}{n}.
$$

Subadditivity and the [Fekete lemma](fekete-s-lemma.md) give the limit. Conjugating $g^n$ changes its length by at most a constant, so [stable word length](stable-word-length.md) is conjugacy invariant. It satisfies $\tau_S(g^k)=|k|\tau_S(g)$. Finite-order elements have zero [stable word length](stable-word-length.md); in a [hyperbolic group](hyperbolic-group.md), every infinite-order element has positive [stable word length](stable-word-length.md).
