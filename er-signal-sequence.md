# ER signal sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Rough endoplasmic reticulum](rough-endoplasmic-reticulum.md)

An [ER signal sequence](er-signal-sequence.md) is [polypeptide chain](polypeptide.md) targeting information recognized by machinery delivering a [protein](protein-split.md) to the [endoplasmic reticulum](endoplasmic-reticulum.md). Many soluble cargoes have a removable [N-terminus](n-terminus.md) signal; membrane insertion can use internal signals. An [ER signal sequence](er-signal-sequence.md) alone does not specify the final destination.[Molecular Biology of the Cell, 4th edition by Bruce Alberts and others; The ER Signal Sequence Is Removed from Most Soluble Proteins After Translocation; In Single-Pass Transmembrane Proteins, a Single Internal ER Signal Sequence Remains in the Lipid Bilayer as a Membrane-spanning α Helix](molecular-biology-of-the-cell-4th-edition-by-bruce-alberts-and-others/the-endoplasmic-reticulum.md)

For an original counting example, stipulate an unbranched precursor [polypeptide chain](polypeptide.md) of $180$ [amino acid residues](amino-acid-residue.md) whose first $20$ [amino acid residues](amino-acid-residue.md) form a cleaved [ER signal sequence](er-signal-sequence.md). With no further processing, the remaining chain has $180-20=160$ [amino acid residues](amino-acid-residue.md) and $159$ [peptide bonds](peptide-bond.md). The initial chain had $179$ [peptide bonds](peptide-bond.md); the detached signal has $19$, so $159+19=178$ remain after one bond is cut. The check counts adjacent positions: an unbranched chain of $n$ [amino acid residues](amino-acid-residue.md) has $n-1$ [peptide bonds](peptide-bond.md). These stipulated lengths are not a natural sequence, a measured size or evidence that a particular sequence functions as an [ER signal sequence](er-signal-sequence.md).
