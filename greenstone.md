# Greenstone

↑ **Parent:** [Metamorphic rock](metamorphic-rock.md)

A common descriptive name for low-grade metamorphosed mafic volcanic [rock](rock-geology.md), often containing greenish [minerals](mineral.md). A [greenstone belt](greenstone-belt.md) can contain these rocks together with other volcanic and [sedimentary rocks](sedimentary-rock.md); the belt name does not imply uniform composition or [density](density.md).
