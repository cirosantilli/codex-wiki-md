# Rock salt structure

↑ **Parent:** [Ionic crystal](ionic-crystal.md)

The [rock salt structure](rock-salt-structure.md) of ordinary [sodium chloride](sodium-chloride.md) has a [coordination number](coordination-number.md) of six for each kind of [ion](ion.md): six nearest neighbours of the opposite sign surround each [ion](ion.md) along three perpendicular directions. Its conventional cubic [unit cell](unit-cell.md) contains four [formula units](formula-unit.md); sharing corner, face, and edge sites between adjacent cells must be included in that count. Sixfold local coordination is compatible with $1:1$ overall [stoichiometry](stoichiometry.md).[§7.1, Figure 7.3; §10.6, “The Structures of Ionic Crystals”, NaCl unit-cell accounting](chemistry-2e-by-paul-flowers-and-others.md)

The checkerboard plane in the diagram is a slice through this three-dimensional arrangement. It leaves out the two nearest neighbours above and below the plane. Repeating a complete even-by-even patch gives equal numbers of signs; the displayed odd-by-odd crop has a boundary imbalance and is not a complete neutral [ionic crystal](ionic-crystal.md) or a [unit cell](unit-cell.md). Other [salts](salt-chemistry.md) need not have the [rock salt structure](rock-salt-structure.md) or the same [coordination number](coordination-number.md).
