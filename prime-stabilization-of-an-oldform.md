# Prime stabilization of an oldform

↑ **Parent:** [Oldform by argument dilation](oldform-by-argument-dilation.md)

For a nonzero positive-weight eigenform at a good prime, the two oldforms $f(\tau),f(p\tau)$ are independent. At level multiplied by $p$, the [Hecke operator](hecke-operator.md) is the bad-prime operator, with [matrix](matrix.md) $\begin{pmatrix}\lambda&1\\-\chi(p)p^{k-1}&0\end{pmatrix}$. Distinct roots $\alpha,\beta$ of its [characteristic polynomial](characteristic-polynomial.md) give eigenforms $f-\beta f(p\tau)$ and $f-\alpha f(p\tau)$. Weight-zero constants are an exception to the independence assertion.
