# Bohmian circulation of an angular-momentum eigenstate

↑ **Parent:** [de Broglie-Bohm theory](de-broglie-bohm-theory.md)

For the [separation of a two-dimensional central-potential eigenstate](separation-of-a-two-dimensional-central-potential-eigenstate.md), the [guidance equation](guidance-equation.md) gives zero radial velocity and constant-radius circular motion, with circulation $2\pi\hbar k/m$. A real superposition of the degenerate $k$ and $-k$ [eigenstates](eigenstate.md) has zero current away from nodes instead. The speed depends on the actual [wavefunction](wave-function.md), not just its energy.
