# First-Rytov coherent mean

↑ **Parent:** [Rytov approximation](rytov-approximation.md)

If the first logarithmic perturbation $\chi_1$ is a centered linear functional of a [Gaussian random field](gaussian-random-field.md), the [complex Gaussian exponential moment](complex-gaussian-exponential-moment.md) gives the displayed mean of the first [Rytov approximation](rytov-approximation.md) $\psi_R=\psi_i e^{\mu\chi_1}$. This is the mean of the truncated logarithmic model. A nonzero mean of the omitted second logarithmic perturbation can change the true field mean at order $\mu^2$.
