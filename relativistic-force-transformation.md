# Relativistic force transformation

↑ **Parent:** [Special relativity](special-relativity-split.md)

For a particle instantaneously at rest in a primed frame moving at $v\widehat{\mathbf x}$ relative to the laboratory, the ordinary three-force transforms as $F_\parallel=F'_\parallel$ and $F_\perp=F'_\perp/\gamma$. The spatial components of the [four-force](four-force.md) are $\gamma_u\mathbf F$, whereas its time component is $\gamma_u\mathbf F\cdot\mathbf u/c$. For a co-moving material segment, [length contraction](length-contraction.md) also changes its laboratory length, so transforming total force is not the same as transforming [force per unit length](force-per-unit-length.md).
