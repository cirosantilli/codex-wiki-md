# Harper theorem implies the Kruskal-Katona theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Vertex-isoperimetric inequality in the discrete cube](vertex-isoperimetric-inequality-in-the-discrete-cube.md)

Adjoin all lower levels to a [uniform set family](uniform-set-family.md) before applying [Harper theorem](vertex-isoperimetric-inequality-in-the-discrete-cube.md). Its remaining neighbourhood contribution is the [upper shadow](upper-shadow.md), so lexicographic [initial segments](initial-segment.md) minimize upper shadows. Complementation and reversal of coordinates turn this into [colexicographic order](colexicographic-order.md) minimizing the [lower shadow](lower-shadow.md), giving the [Kruskal-Katona theorem](kruskal-katona-theorem.md).
