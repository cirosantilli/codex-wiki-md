# Degree-zero de Rham cohomology

↑ **Parent:** [de Rham cohomology](de-rham-cohomology.md)

There are no exact forms in degree zero. A [smooth function](smooth-function.md) has zero [exterior derivative](exterior-derivative.md) precisely when it is [locally constant](locally-constant-function.md), so $H^0_{\mathrm{dR}}(M)$ is the space of [locally constant functions](locally-constant-function.md). On a connected nonempty [smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md), it is $\mathbb R$. An overlapping cover by two connected open subsets therefore does not give the direct-sum formula for [de Rham cohomology of a finite disjoint union](de-rham-cohomology-of-a-finite-disjoint-union.md): restriction sends a constant to equal constants on both pieces.
