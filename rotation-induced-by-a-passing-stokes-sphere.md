# Rotation induced by a passing Stokes sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Faxén's rotational law](faxen-s-rotational-law.md)

Let a [sphere](sphere.md) of radius $a$ descend along the $z$ axis through a stationary unbounded [Newtonian fluid](newtonian-fluid.md). A second [sphere](sphere.md) with centre $(\ell,0,0)$ is held against translation but has zero applied [torque](torque.md). In the prescribed isolated-sphere incident flow, [Faxén's rotational law](faxen-s-rotational-law.md) and the [vorticity of translating-sphere Stokes flow](vorticity-of-translating-sphere-stokes-flow.md) give

$$
\Omega_y=-\frac{3aU\ell}{4(\ell^2+Z^2)^{3/2}},\qquad\dot Z=-U.
$$

The speed $U$ cancels on integrating along the complete passage. Since $\int_{-\infty}^\infty dZ/(\ell^2+Z^2)^{3/2}=2/\ell^2$, the result is the displayed angle about $-\mathbf e_y$. The target radius is absent from the rotational Faxén response to this harmonic incident [vorticity](vorticity-split.md). This is a one-way incident-flow calculation; nonoverlap alone does not justify ignoring mutual [hydrodynamic interactions](hydrodynamic-interaction.md) between finite spheres. A fully coupled encounter requires the [method of reflections for Stokes flow](method-of-reflections-for-stokes-flow.md) or an exact two-body solution.
