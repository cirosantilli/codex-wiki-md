# Monodromy of the complex inverse sine

↑ **Parent:** [Branches of the complex inverse sine](branches-of-the-complex-inverse-sine.md)

Let $w$ be a local value of the [complex inverse sine](complex-inverse-sine.md). Since the limiting values at the two [branch points](branch-point.md) are $\pi/2$ at $z=1$ and $-\pi/2$ at $z=-1$, [monodromy reflection at a square-root branch point](monodromy-reflection-at-a-square-root-branch-point.md) gives

$$
R_+(w)=\pi-w,
\qquad
R_-(w)=-\pi-w.
$$

Following a counterclockwise loop that encloses both branch points in the convention where $\sqrt{1-z^2}$ is positive just above $(-1,1)$ applies the two reflections in the order that gives

$$
R_-\circ R_+(w)=w-2\pi.
$$

The reverse orientation gives $w+2\pi$. Thus the translational part of the monodromy is generated by $2\pi$.
