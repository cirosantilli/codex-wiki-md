# Field norm

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic number theory](algebraic-number-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Field_norm)

The field norm $N_{L/K}(x)$ is the determinant of multiplication by $x$, equivalently the product of its conjugates when the extension is separable.
