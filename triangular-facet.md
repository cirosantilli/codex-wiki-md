# Triangular facet

↑ **Parent:** [Geomorphology](geomorphology.md)

A roughly triangular planar mountain face bounded by incised valleys and a basal front. Repeated [normal faulting](normal-faulting.md), [tectonic uplift](tectonic-uplift.md) and erosion can maintain arrays of [triangular facets](triangular-facet.md). Their geometry is suggestive but does not alone date an individual [earthquake](earthquake.md).
