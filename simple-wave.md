# Simple wave

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann invariant](riemann-invariant.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simple_wave)

A simple wave is a region of a hyperbolic flow in which one [Riemann invariant](riemann-invariant.md) is constant throughout, leaving the other invariant to determine all fluid variables.
