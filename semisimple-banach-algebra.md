# Semisimple Banach algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Banach algebra](banach-algebra-split.md)

A [Banach algebra](banach-algebra-split.md) is semisimple when its [Jacobson radical](jacobson-radical.md) is zero. This does not assert that every [quasinilpotent element](quasinilpotent-element.md) is zero. For example, the algebra of bounded operators on a [Banach space](banach-space-split.md) of dimension greater than one is semisimple, but contains nonzero square-zero rank-one operators. This notion also does not require the finite-dimensional direct-sum decomposition associated with a [semisimple algebra](semisimple-algebra.md).
