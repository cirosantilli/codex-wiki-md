# Holomorphic vector bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Complex manifold](complex-manifold.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holomorphic_vector_bundle)

A holomorphic vector bundle is a complex [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) whose local trivializations have holomorphic transition functions.
