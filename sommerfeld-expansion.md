# Sommerfeld expansion

↑ **Parent:** [Fermi-Dirac distribution](fermi-dirac-distribution.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sommerfeld_expansion)

The [Sommerfeld expansion](sommerfeld-expansion.md) approximates integrals weighted by the [Fermi-Dirac distribution](fermi-dirac-distribution.md) when $k_BT\ll\mu$ and $f$ is smooth near $\mu$. Applying it to the number integral at fixed particle number gives $\mu-E_F=-(\pi^2/6)(k_BT)^2g'(E_F)/g(E_F)+\cdots$. The sign depends on the slope of the [density of states](density-of-states.md). For an unbounded parabolic band, $g(E)\propto E^{d/2-1}$, yielding opposite quadratic shifts in one and three dimensions. In two dimensions the leading algebraic shift vanishes, but the finite band-bottom cutoff still gives an exponentially small negative shift.
