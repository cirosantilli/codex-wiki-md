# Worldsheet conformal Killing group

↑ **Parent:** [Worldsheet moduli](worldsheet-moduli.md)

The worldsheet conformal Killing group is the connected residual group of conformal automorphisms of the chosen compact [Riemann surface](riemann-surfaces.md). Its infinitesimal vectors satisfy the [Conformal Killing equation](conformal-killing-equation.md), or $P_1v=0$ for the [worldsheet diffeomorphism ghost operator](worldsheet-diffeomorphism-ghost-operator.md). Its complex dimensions are three on a sphere, one on a torus, and zero for [genus](genus-of-a-surface.md) at least two. The sphere group consists of [Möbius transformations](mobius-transformation.md), while the torus's connected group is translation. Additional discrete automorphisms are handled by the moduli quotient rather than counted as continuous zero modes.
