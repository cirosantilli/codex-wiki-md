# Voltage-gated potassium channel

↑ **Parent:** [Voltage-gated ion channel](voltage-gated-ion-channel.md)

A [voltage-gated potassium channel](voltage-gated-potassium-channel.md) couples a voltage-sensing region to a potassium-selective pore. The sensor determines voltage-dependent opening, whereas filter coordination chemistry contributes to which ions permeate.
