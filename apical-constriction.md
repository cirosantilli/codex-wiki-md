# Apical constriction

↑ **Parent:** [Morphogenesis](morphogenesis.md)

Reduction of a cell's apical surface area, often through localized [actomyosin contractility](actomyosin-contractility.md). Coordinated constriction can bend an epithelial sheet when forces are transmitted through appropriate attachments.
