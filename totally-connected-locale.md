# Totally connected locale

↑ **Parent:** [Open locale](open-locale.md)

A positive [open locale](open-locale.md) is totally connected when every [positive open of a locale](positive-open-of-a-locale.md) is connected. Under classical logic its positive opens form a [completely prime filter](completely-prime-filter.md), hence specify a dense point. Positivity of the whole locale is essential: the empty locale satisfies the condition on positive opens vacuously but has no point.
