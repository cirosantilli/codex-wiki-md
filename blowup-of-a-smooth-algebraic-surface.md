# Blowup of a smooth algebraic surface

↑ **Parent:** [Birational variety](birational-variety.md)

Over an algebraically closed [field](field.md), the [blowup of a smooth algebraic surface](blowup-of-a-smooth-algebraic-surface.md) at a closed point replaces the point by an exceptional [smooth rational curve](smooth-rational-curve.md) $E\cong\mathbb P^1$, with $E^2=-1$. Locally it is the [blowup of the affine plane at the origin](blowup-of-the-affine-plane-at-the-origin.md). It is a proper [birational morphism](birational-morphism.md), an isomorphism off the exceptional curve, and the blown-up surface is again smooth and projective when the original is projective.
