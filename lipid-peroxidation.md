# Lipid peroxidation

↑ **Parent:** [Lipid](lipid.md)

[lipid peroxidation](lipid-peroxidation.md) oxidatively damages lipids, particularly susceptible unsaturated chains. Oxidation products can alter membrane properties and chemical stability; higher unsaturation therefore creates potential tradeoffs as well as functional opportunities.
