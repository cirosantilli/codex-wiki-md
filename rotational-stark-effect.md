# Rotational Stark effect

↑ **Parent:** [Stark effect](stark-effect.md)

An electric-field shift or splitting of molecular rotational levels. The observed line shift is the difference of upper- and lower-level shifts, subject to polarization-dependent [selection rules](selection-rule.md); shifting only one endpoint gives an incorrect spectral splitting.
