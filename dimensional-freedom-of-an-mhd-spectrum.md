# Dimensional freedom of an MHD spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetohydrodynamic turbulence](magnetohydrodynamic-turbulence.md)

In a local isotropic hydrodynamic inertial-range model with only $\epsilon$ and $k$, dimensional analysis fixes the [Kolmogorov 1941 theory](kolmogorov-1941-theory.md) spectrum. A mean [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) supplies the independent [Alfvén speed](alfven-speed.md), permitting the arbitrary dimensionless function in the displayed formula even before anisotropy adds parallel scales. Cascade-time assumptions such as weak decorrelated interactions or [critical balance](critical-balance.md) are therefore necessary to select an MHD spectrum.
