<h1 id="a/3/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [weak solution](../../../../../../weak-solution.md) belongs to the [zero-boundary Sobolev space](../../../../../../zero-boundary-sobolev-space.md) $H_0^1(\Omega)$ and satisfies

$$
\boxed{\int_\Omega\nabla u\cdot\nabla\varphi\,dx+\int_\Omega(b\cdot\nabla u)\varphi\,dx=\int_\Omega f\varphi\,dx\quad(\varphi\in H_0^1(\Omega)).}
$$

The boundedness of $b$ and the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](../../../../../../cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) make the drift term integrable. This [weak formulation](../../../../../../weak-formulation.md) moves one derivative from the [Laplacian](../../../../../../laplacian.md) onto the [test function](../../../../../../test-function.md), while retaining the first-order drift term.
