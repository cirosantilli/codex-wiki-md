↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The function $v$ is the [weak derivative](../../../../../../weak-derivative.md) $D^\alpha u$ if

$$
\boxed{\int_\Omega v\varphi\,dx=(-1)^{|\alpha|}\int_\Omega uD^\alpha\varphi\,dx\quad\text{for every }\varphi\in C_0^\infty(\Omega).}
$$

The integrals exist by local integrability and the compact support of the [test function](../../../../../../test-function.md). This is the definition of a [distributional derivative](../../../../../../distributional-derivative.md) represented by a locally integrable function.
