↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

An integrable example on the whole real line is the tent function

$$
\boxed{u(x)=(1-|x|)_+,\qquad D u(x)=\mathbf1_{(-1,0)}(x)-\mathbf1_{(0,1)}(x).}
$$

It is not [differentiable](../../../../../../differentiable-function.md) at $-1,0,1$. For a [test function](../../../../../../test-function.md) $\varphi$, split the integral into $(-1,0)$ and $(0,1)$ and use [integration by parts](../../../../../../integration-by-parts.md). The endpoint terms at $-1,1$ vanish because $u=0$, and those at zero cancel because $u$ is continuous. The result is $\int u\varphi'=-\int(Du)\varphi$. Thus the corners obstruct classical differentiability but not this [weak derivative](../../../../../../weak-derivative.md).
