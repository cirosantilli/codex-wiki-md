<h1 id="a/2/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For one coordinate, [integration by parts](../../../../../../integration-by-parts.md) gives $\int_\Omega(\partial_ju)\varphi=-\int_\Omega u\,\partial_j\varphi$: the [test function](../../../../../../test-function.md) vanishes outside a compact subset of $\Omega$, so there is no boundary contribution. Repeat this operation once for each of the $|\alpha|$ coordinate differentiations in the [multi-index](../../../../../../multi-index-notation.md) $\alpha$. Each operation contributes a factor $-1$, giving

$$
\boxed{\int_\Omega D^\alpha u\,\varphi\,dx=(-1)^{|\alpha|}\int_\Omega uD^\alpha\varphi\,dx.}
$$

All intermediate functions are continuous on the relevant compact support, since $u\in C^k$ and $|\alpha|\leq k$.
