Here [uniform ellipticity](../../../../../../uniformly-elliptic-operator.md) means that there is a constant $\lambda>0$, independent of $x$, such that

$$
\boxed{\xi^Ta(x)\xi\geq\lambda|\xi|^2\quad\text{for almost every }x\in\Omega\text{ and every }\xi\in\mathbb R^n.}
$$

The assumption that the entries are [essentially bounded functions](../../../../../../essentially-bounded-function.md) also gives an upper bound $|a(x)\xi|\leq\Lambda|\xi|$ with a finite constant $\Lambda$. For this symmetric divergence-form operator, the lower bound makes its energy control the squared [Euclidean norm](../../../../../../euclidean-norm.md) of the [gradient](../../../../../../gradient.md).
