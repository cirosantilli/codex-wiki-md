<h1 id="a/1/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

A [weak solution](../../../../../../weak-solution.md) is a function $u\in H_0^1(\Omega)$ satisfying the [weak formulation](../../../../../../weak-formulation.md)

$$
\boxed{\int_\Omega a\nabla u\cdot\nabla\varphi\,dx=\int_\Omega f\varphi\,dx\quad\text{for every }\varphi\in H_0^1(\Omega).}
$$

Multiplication by a [test function](../../../../../../test-function.md) and [integration by parts](../../../../../../integration-by-parts.md) give this identity from the classical equation. Conversely it is the equation in the sense of [distributions](../../../../../../distribution-mathematical-analysis.md), with the zero boundary condition encoded by the [zero-boundary Sobolev space](../../../../../../zero-boundary-sobolev-space.md). Testing only against $C_0^\infty(\Omega)$ is equivalent, by its [density](../../../../../../density.md) in $H_0^1$ and continuity of both sides.
