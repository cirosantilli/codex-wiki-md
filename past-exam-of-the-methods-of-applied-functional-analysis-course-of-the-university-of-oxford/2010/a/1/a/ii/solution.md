The [space of test functions](../../../../../../space-of-test-functions.md) is $\boxed{C_0^\infty(\Omega)=\{\varphi\in C^\infty(\Omega):\operatorname{supp}\varphi\text{ is a compact subset of }\Omega\}}$. The [Sobolev space](../../../../../../sobolev-space-split.md) is

$$
\boxed{H^1(\Omega)=\{u\in L^2(\Omega):D_ju\in L^2(\Omega),\ j=1,\ldots,n\},}
$$

where $D_ju$ denotes a [weak derivative](../../../../../../weak-derivative.md), and its [Sobolev norm](../../../../../../sobolev-norm.md) is $\|u\|_{H^1}^2=\|u\|_2^2+\sum_j\|D_ju\|_2^2$. The [zero-boundary Sobolev space](../../../../../../zero-boundary-sobolev-space.md) is $\boxed{H_0^1(\Omega)=\overline{C_0^\infty(\Omega)}^{\,H^1}}$. This closure defines the boundary condition even when the boundary has insufficient regularity for a classical boundary value.
