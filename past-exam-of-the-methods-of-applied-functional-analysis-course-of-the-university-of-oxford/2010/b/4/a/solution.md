<h1 id="b/4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Brouwer fixed-point theorem](../../../../../brouwer-fixed-point-theorem.md) says that every continuous self-map of a closed ball in $\mathbb R^n$ has a [fixed point](../../../../../fixed-point.md).

Suppose $g$ has no zero in $\overline B_R$. Then

$$
T(x)=-R\frac{g(x)}{|g(x)|}
$$

is a continuous map from $\overline B_R$ into its boundary, hence into $\overline B_R$. The [Brouwer fixed-point theorem](../../../../../brouwer-fixed-point-theorem.md) gives $T(x_0)=x_0$. This forces $|x_0|=R$ and

$$
g(x_0)\cdot x_0=-R|g(x_0)|<0,
$$

contradicting the assumed boundary inequality. Therefore $\boxed{g(x_0)=0\text{ for some }|x_0|\leq R}$. This is the [outward vector field zero theorem](../../../../../outward-vector-field-zero-theorem.md).
