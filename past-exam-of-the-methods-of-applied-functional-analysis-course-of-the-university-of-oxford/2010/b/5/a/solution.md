Here $T$ is a [nonlinear compact operator](../../../../../nonlinear-compact-operator.md): it is continuous and sends bounded subsets of $M$ to [relatively compact subsets](../../../../../relatively-compact-subset.md) of $X$. Equivalently, the images of any bounded input [sequence](../../../../../sequence.md) have a norm-convergent [subsequence](../../../../../subsequence.md).

The [Schauder fixed-point theorem](../../../../../schauder-fixed-point-theorem.md) states that if $M$ is a nonempty closed bounded [convex set](../../../../../convex-set-split.md) in a [Banach space](../../../../../banach-space-split.md) and $T:M\to M$ is continuous and compact, then $\boxed{T\text{ has a fixed point in }M}$. Linearity, a contraction estimate, and uniqueness are not assumptions or conclusions of this theorem.
