↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Using the [dual pairing](../../../../../dual-pairing.md), [monotonicity](../../../../../monotone-operator.md) means

$$
\boxed{\langle A(u)-A(v),u-v\rangle\ge0\qquad(u,v\in X).}
$$

[Strict monotonicity](../../../../../strictly-monotone-operator.md) requires strict positivity whenever $u\ne v$. Coercivity with respect to zero, in the growth sense used for monotone-operator existence theory, means

$$
\boxed{\frac{\langle A(v),v\rangle}{\|v\|_X}\longrightarrow+\infty\qquad\text{as }\|v\|_X\to\infty.}
$$

This is a condition on all [sequences](../../../../../sequence.md) escaping to [infinity](../../../../../infinity.md), not just each fixed direction with a bound depending on that direction. [Strict monotonicity](../../../../../strictly-monotone-operator.md) concerns pairs of distinct arguments; it does not by itself imply this uniform large-norm growth.
