# Volume of a nef divisor

↑ **Parent:** [Nef line bundle](nef-line-bundle.md)

For a [nef divisor](nef-line-bundle.md) on an integral projective variety of dimension $n$,

$$
h^0(X,mD)=\frac{D^n}{n!}m^n+O(m^{n-1}).
$$

Indeed, combine [asymptotic Riemann–Roch](asymptotic-riemann-roch.md) with [cohomology growth for nef twists](cohomology-growth-for-nef-twists.md). Thus its normalized section-growth [limit](limit-of-a-function.md) is $D^n/n!$, and it is big exactly when $D^n>0$.
