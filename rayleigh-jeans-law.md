# Rayleigh-Jeans law

↑ **Parent:** [Planck function](planck-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rayleigh-Jeans_law)

The Rayleigh-Jeans law is the long-wavelength, low-frequency limit of [Planck law](planck-s-law.md). When $hc/(\lambda k_BT)\ll1$,

$$
B_\lambda(T)\simeq\frac{2ck_BT}{\lambda^4}.
$$
