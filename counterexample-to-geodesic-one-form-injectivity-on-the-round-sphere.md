# Counterexample to geodesic one-form injectivity on the round sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Geodesic X-ray transform](geodesic-x-ray-transform.md)

The displayed one-form on the unit round sphere has zero integral on every closed [geodesic](geodesic.md). Indeed, the [antipodal map](antipodal-map.md) pulls it back to its negative, and the second half of an oriented great circle is the antipodal image of the first. Nevertheless $d\theta=2x_3\,dx_3\wedge dx_1$ is nonzero on an open set, so the one-form is not a [closed differential form](closed-differential-form.md) and cannot be an [exact differential form](exact-differential-form.md). Thus a curvature-free extension of one-form injectivity fails.
