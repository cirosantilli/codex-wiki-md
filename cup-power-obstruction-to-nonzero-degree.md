# Cup-power obstruction to nonzero degree

↑ **Parent:** [Degree of a map between oriented manifolds](degree-of-a-map-between-oriented-manifolds.md)

Let $M,N$ be closed connected oriented manifolds of the same dimension, and suppose $a^r$ generates the top integral [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) of $N$. If every class of degree $|a|$ on $M$ has zero rth [cup power](cup-power.md), every [continuous map](continuous-map.md) $f:M\to N$ has [mapping degree](degree-of-a-continuous-mapping.md) zero. Indeed, $f^*(a^r)=(f^*a)^r=0$, and evaluation on the [fundamental class](fundamental-class.md) gives $\deg f=0$. For $S^2\times S^4\to\mathbb{CP}^3$, the degree-two class is a multiple of the sphere class $u$, whose square vanishes.
