# Gauche interaction

↑ **Parent:** [Conformation](conformation.md)

A [gauche interaction](gauche-interaction.md) concerns groups separated by an approximately $60^\circ$ torsion about a bond. Its energetic effect depends on the groups and electronic environment; not every gauche geometry is unfavorable.
