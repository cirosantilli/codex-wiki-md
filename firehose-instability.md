# Firehose instability

↑ **Parent:** [Pressure anisotropy](pressure-anisotropy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Firehose_instability)

Parallel particle [pressure](pressure-split.md) can overcome [magnetic tension](magnetic-tension.md). In the long-[wavelength](wavelength.md) gyrotropic [plasma](plasma-physics.md) limit, the [Alfvén wave](alfven-wave.md) has $\omega^2=k_\parallel^2[B^2/(4\pi)+p_\perp-p_\parallel]/\rho$, so the condition in the title makes it unstable. The detailed kinetic threshold at shorter scales requires additional physics.
