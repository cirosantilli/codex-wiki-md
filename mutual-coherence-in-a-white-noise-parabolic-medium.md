# Mutual coherence in a white-noise parabolic medium

↑ **Parent:** [Parabolic wave equation](parabolic-wave-equation.md)

A [parabolic wave equation](parabolic-wave-equation.md) driven by longitudinal [Gaussian white noise](gaussian-white-noise.md) with transverse [covariance kernel](covariance-kernel.md) $C$ has the displayed closed [mutual coherence](coherence-of-a-normalized-matrix.md) equation. The noise is the derivative of a [longitudinal Brownian field with transverse covariance](longitudinal-brownian-field-with-transverse-covariance.md); the physical smooth-noise limit uses the [Stratonovich integral](stratonovich-integral.md). Conversion to the [Itô integral](ito-integral.md) supplies the attenuation drift and the cross variation supplies the $C(\mathbf s_1-\mathbf s_2)$ term. For unit plane-wave incidence and transverse [statistical homogeneity](statistical-homogeneity.md), $\Gamma=\exp[-k^2\mu^2x(C(0)-C(\mathbf s_1-\mathbf s_2))]$. General Gaussian colored media do not have this local closure.
