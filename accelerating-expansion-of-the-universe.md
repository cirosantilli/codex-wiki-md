# Accelerating expansion of the universe

↑ **Parent:** [Deceleration parameter](deceleration-parameter.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accelerating_expansion_of_the_universe)

An accelerating universe has $\ddot a>0$, equivalently $q<0$ while expansion continues.

Here $a$ is the [scale factor](scale-factor-cosmology.md) and $q=-a\ddot a/\dot a^2$ the [deceleration parameter](deceleration-parameter.md). [Accelerating expansion](accelerating-expansion-of-the-universe.md) means $\dot a>0$ and $\ddot a>0$; the [Hubble parameter](hubble-parameter.md) $H=\dot a/a$ can still decrease. The observational challenge is to reconstruct that history from a [past light cone](past-light-cone.md), rather than watch a distant [galaxy](galaxy-split.md) speed up over the observing campaign.
