# Primitive ontology

↑ **Parent:** [Philosophy of physics](philosophy-of-physics.md)

The material variables that a physical theory identifies with the distribution of stuff in ordinary space or spacetime, and through which it describes apparatus and recorded events. In particle [Bohmian mechanics](de-broglie-bohm-theory.md), actual particle configurations play this role; the [wave function](wave-function-split.md) guides them. This is a way to specify an interpretation's account of matter, rather than a theorem that only particle ontologies are admissible.[On the Common Structure of Bohmian Mechanics and the Ghirardi-Rimini-Weber Theory by Valia Allori and others](on-the-common-structure-of-bohmian-mechanics-and-the-ghirardi-rimini-weber-theory-by-valia-allori-and-others.md)
