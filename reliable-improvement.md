# Reliable improvement

↑ **Parent:** [Reliable change index](reliable-change-index.md)

Improvement exceeding a specified measurement-error criterion, with any additional no-deterioration rule made explicit. It differs from crossing a [symptom](symptom.md) cutoff and from improvement attributable to treatment.
