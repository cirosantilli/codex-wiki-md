# Discrete circuit

↑ **Parent:** [Integrated circuit](integrated-circuit.md)

An [electrical circuit](electrical-circuit-split.md) assembled from separately made components, such as individual [transistors](transistor.md), [resistors](resistor.md) and [capacitors](capacitor.md). Several functions can share a package without forming a [monolithic integrated circuit](monolithic-integrated-circuit.md); packaging alone does not establish how the components were fabricated.
