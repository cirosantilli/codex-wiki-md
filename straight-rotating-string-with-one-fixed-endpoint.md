# Straight rotating string with one fixed endpoint

↑ **Parent:** [Rotating Nambu–Goto string](rotating-nambu-goto-string.md)

A [Nambu–Goto action](nambu-goto-action.md) solution with $X^0=t$ is $\boldsymbol X=L\sin(\sigma/L)(\cos(t/L),\sin(t/L))$, for $0\leq\sigma\leq\pi L/2$. It solves the [wave equation](wave-equation-split.md), has a fixed endpoint at the origin, and obeys the [free-end string boundary condition](free-end-string-boundary-condition.md) at the other end. Its [proper length](proper-length.md) is $L$ and its free endpoint has angular speed $1/L$ and spatial speed one.
