# Bias voltage

↑ **Parent:** [Electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md)

An applied potential difference that sets a device operating condition or shifts reservoir electrochemical potentials. In a multi-terminal device the grounded lead, biased lead and gate reference must be stated; they determine the relation between measured slopes and [capacitances](capacitance.md).
