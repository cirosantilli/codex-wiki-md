# Barkhausen effect

↑ **Parent:** [Ferromagnetism](ferromagnetism.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barkhausen_effect)

The [Barkhausen effect](barkhausen-effect.md) consists of abrupt [magnetization](magnetization.md) changes during a smooth applied [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) sweep. Its inductive [signal](signal.md) is [Barkhausen noise](barkhausen-effect.md), often arising from collective [magnetic domain wall](magnetic-domain-wall.md) motion.[§I, p. 2: wall-motion interpretation](the-barkhausen-effect-by-gianfranco-durin-and-stefano-zapperi.md)

The distinction is between a smooth drive and an intermittent material response. A smooth macroscopic [hysteresis](hysteresis.md) curve can hide smaller jumps. By [Faraday's law of induction](faraday-s-law-of-induction.md), a [pickup coil](pickup-coil.md) responds to the rate of change of linked [magnetic flux](magnetic-flux.md), so a rapid jump can be conspicuous even when the total change is small. A pulse is not one [chemical atom](atom-physics.md) flipping: neither its height nor the number of audible clicks counts individual [electron](electron.md) [spins](spin.md). Qualitative crackling alone does not establish quantum [spin](spin.md); collective switching also admits a classical model with trapped interfaces. Compare the distinct mechanical readout of [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) transfer in the [Einstein-de Haas effect](einstein-de-haas-effect.md).
