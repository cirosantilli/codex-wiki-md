# Simple machine

↑ **Parent:** [Classical mechanics](classical-mechanics-split.md)

A mechanism that trades the magnitude or direction of an applied [force](force-split.md) against the displacement through which it acts. An ideal [simple machine](simple-machine.md) conserves [work](work.md); it does not multiply [energy](energy-split.md).
