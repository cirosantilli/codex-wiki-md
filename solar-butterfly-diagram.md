# Solar butterfly diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Solar cycle](solar-cycle.md)

A solar butterfly diagram plots sunspot occurrence or magnetic flux against latitude and time. New-cycle sunspot belts appear at middle latitudes and migrate toward the equator, producing successive wing-shaped patterns. Magnetic butterfly diagrams additionally show poleward flux transport and polar-field reversals. The diagram constrains both [Parker dynamo wave](parker-dynamo-wave.md) propagation and magnetic transport in [flux-transport solar dynamos](flux-transport-solar-dynamo.md).
