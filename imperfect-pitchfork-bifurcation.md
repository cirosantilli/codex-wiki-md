# Imperfect pitchfork bifurcation

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetry-forced pitchfork bifurcation](symmetry-forced-pitchfork-bifurcation.md)

Adding a small symmetry-breaking term to a [pitchfork bifurcation](pitchfork-bifurcation-normal-form.md) disconnects its two symmetric daughter branches and generically leaves one or more [saddle-node bifurcations](saddle-node-bifurcation.md). This unfolding is called an imperfect pitchfork.
