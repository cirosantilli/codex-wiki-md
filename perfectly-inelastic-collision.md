# Perfectly inelastic collision

↑ **Parent:** [Inelastic collision](inelastic-collision.md)

A [perfectly inelastic collision](perfectly-inelastic-collision.md) is an [inelastic collision](inelastic-collision.md) in which the bodies stick and share one outgoing [velocity](velocity.md). For two isolated [point masses](point-mass.md), [momentum conservation](momentum-conservation.md) gives $v=(m_1u_1+m_2u_2)/(m_1+m_2)$. At fixed total [momentum](momentum-split.md), common [velocity](velocity.md) minimizes the outgoing translational [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md), so sticking gives the greatest loss of that [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) for the specified incoming state.
