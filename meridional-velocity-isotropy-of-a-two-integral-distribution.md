# Meridional velocity isotropy of a two-integral distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Two-integral galactic distribution function](two-integral-galactic-distribution-function.md)

At fixed position a [two-integral distribution](two-integral-galactic-distribution-function.md) depends on $v_R,v_z$ only through $v_R^2+v_z^2$. Swapping these integration variables makes their second moments equal. Their odd moments and mixed second moments vanish by reflection symmetry. For an even function of $L_z$ the azimuthal [velocity](velocity.md) is also centered, so all mixed entries of the [velocity ellipsoid](velocity-ellipsoid.md) vanish.
