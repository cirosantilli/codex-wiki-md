# Exact cover

↑ **Parent:** [Set partition](set-partition.md)

Given a [set](set-split.md) $U$ and a family of its [subsets](subset.md), an [exact cover](exact-cover.md) selects members of that family so that each element of $U$ belongs to exactly one selected member. Thus the selected members are [disjoint sets](disjoint-sets.md) whose [set union](set-union.md) is $U$. Anchoring a recursive choice at the smallest uncovered element avoids counting a cover in different orders.
