# Weyl denominator formula

↑ **Parent:** [Weyl character formula](weyl-character-formula.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weyl_denominator_formula)

For a positive root system $\Phi^+$,

$$
\sum_{w\in W}(-1)^{\ell(w)}e^{w\rho}
=e^\rho\prod_{\alpha\in\Phi^+}(1-e^{-\alpha}).
$$
