# Vector potential

↑ **Parent:** [Curl](curl.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vector_potential)

A vector potential for a divergence-free vector field $F$ is a vector field $A$ satisfying $F=\nabla\times A$. It is not unique because adding a [gradient](gradient.md) leaves its curl unchanged.
