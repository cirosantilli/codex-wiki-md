# RNA tethering assay

↑ **Parent:** [Post-transcriptional regulation](post-transcriptional-regulation.md)

Artificial recruitment of a candidate effector to a chosen [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) through a separate recognition module. It tests activity after recruitment while bypassing native recognition; physiological targeting and necessity require independent evidence.
