# Binding energy

↑ **Parent:** [Energy](energy-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Binding_energy)

[Binding energy](binding-energy.md) is the [energy](energy-split.md) required to separate a [bound state](bound-state.md) into specified free constituents. For an [electron](electron.md) bound in a [chemical atom](atom-physics.md), neglecting this cost is an assumption of the free-[electron](electron.md) [Compton scattering](compton-scattering.md) model.
