# Infinite sites mutation model

↑ **Parent:** [Population genetics](population-genetics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infinite_sites_mutation_model)

The infinite-sites model assumes that every mutation occurs at a previously unmutated site, so each mutation on the sample genealogy produces a distinct segregating site. If mutation rate per lineage is $\theta/2$ in standard coalescent time units, a tree of total length $L$ has a [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md) of mutation count with mean $\theta L/2$.
