# Rigidity of a scalene triangle

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean isometry](euclidean-isometry.md)

The vertex [set](set-split.md) of a triangle with three distinct side lengths has trivial setwise [stabilizer subgroup](stabilizer-subgroup.md) in the plane [isometry group](isometry-group.md). Each vertex is characterized by the unordered pair of lengths of its incident edges, so a setwise-preserving [Euclidean isometry](euclidean-isometry.md) fixes every vertex. An [isometry](isometry.md) fixing three noncollinear plane points is the identity: squared [Euclidean distances](euclidean-distance.md) to them determine every other point, since subtracting two of the [Euclidean distance](euclidean-distance.md) equations gives two independent linear equations. For example, $(0,0),(1,0),(0,2)$ have side lengths $1,2,\sqrt5$.
