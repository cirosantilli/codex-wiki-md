# Parton convolution normalization for a hadronic tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Parton model](parton-model.md)

In the incoherent [massless collinear parton approximation](massless-collinear-parton-approximation.md), $f_f(\xi)d\xi$ is the number of quarks in a momentum-fraction interval, not a covariantly normalized external state weight. A single-particle response divided by its external-state normalization is proportional to $w/(2k^0)$; the hadron normalization is $2P^0$. Thus converting a number-weighted parton sum to the target tensor contributes $P^0/k^0=1/\xi$. This gives the displayed convolution, including antiquarks. The [spin-averaged electromagnetic parton tensor](spin-averaged-electromagnetic-parton-tensor.md) and $\delta(2\xi P\cdot q-Q^2)=\delta(\xi-x)/(2P\cdot q)$ then give $F_1=\tfrac12\sum_fQ_f^2(f_f+\bar f_f)$ and $F_2=x\sum_fQ_f^2(f_f+\bar f_f)$, proving the [Callan-Gross relation](callan-gross-relation.md) at this order. Target masses, transverse momentum and radiative effects are beyond this leading approximation.
