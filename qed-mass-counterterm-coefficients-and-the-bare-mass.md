# QED mass counterterm coefficients and the bare mass

↑ **Parent:** [Mass counterterm](mass-counterterm.md)

With renormalized [Dirac fields](dirac-field.md), the coefficient of $-\bar\psi\psi$ in the [counterterm Lagrangian](counterterm-lagrangian.md) is $\delta m_L=Z_2m_0-m$, whereas the bare-parameter shift is $m_0-m=\delta m_L-m\delta Z_2$ at first order. In [Feynman gauge](feynman-gauge.md) and $d=4-\epsilon$, [QED](quantum-electrodynamics.md) has $\delta Z_2=-e^2/(8\pi^2\epsilon)$ and $\delta m_L=-4me^2/(8\pi^2\epsilon)$. Their difference gives $m_0-m=-3me^2/(8\pi^2\epsilon)$. Defining the insertion and the [mass](mass.md) coefficient before quoting its sign avoids confusing these two [counterterms](counterterm.md).
