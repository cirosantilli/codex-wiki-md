# Carleson measure

↑ **Parent:** [Harmonic analysis](harmonic-analysis.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carleson_measure)

A positive [Borel measure](borel-measure.md) near a domain boundary is a Carleson measure when its mass in boundary neighborhoods is bounded by a constant times their boundary measure. On the [unit disc](unit-disc.md), this becomes the displayed bound for every [Carleson box](carleson-box.md). The equivalent [hyperbolic geodesic cap](hyperbolic-geodesic-cap.md) version follows from the [geodesic-cap and Carleson-box comparison](geodesic-cap-and-carleson-box-comparison.md). The [Carleson embedding theorem on the disk](carleson-embedding-theorem-on-the-disk.md) explains why this geometric growth condition controls integration of analytic [Hardy spaces](hardy-space.md).
