# Electrical conductor

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical conductivity](electrical-conductivity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_conductor)

An electrical conductor supports mobile charge carriers and hence an electric current under a field. In a band description, a partially filled band or available overlapping bands supply nearby accessible states. The contrast with a [band insulator](band-insulator.md) depends on band filling as well as spectral gaps; [semiconductors](semiconductor.md) provide carriers through thermal excitation or doping.
