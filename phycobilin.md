# Phycobilin

↑ **Parent:** [Accessory pigment](accessory-pigment.md)

A light-absorbing chromophore of particular antenna pigment systems. Different phycobilin-containing complexes absorb different visible wavelengths, including green and orange regions poorly absorbed by [chlorophyll a](chlorophyll-a.md).
