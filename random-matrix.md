# Random matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Probability theory](probability-theory-split.md)

A random [matrix](matrix.md) is a [matrix](matrix.md) whose entries are [random variables](random-variable-split.md). Its [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) form a random configuration, and its [empirical spectral measure](empirical-spectral-measure.md) records their distribution with equal mass on each [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md). Entry assumptions and normalization determine the spectral regime.
