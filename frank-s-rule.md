<h1 id="frank-s-rule">Frank's rule</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Dislocation](dislocation.md)

The energy criterion associated with [Frederick Charles Frank](frederick-charles-frank.md) states that, for dislocations of comparable length with approximately common line-energy factors, a reaction conserving [Burgers vector](burgers-vector.md) is energetically favourable when the displayed inequality holds. The rule is an energy estimate, not a proof that the geometry or kinetic barriers permit the reaction. Edge/screw character, elastic anisotropy and changed lengths refine it.
