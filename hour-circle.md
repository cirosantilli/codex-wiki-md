# Hour circle

↑ **Parent:** [Equatorial coordinate system](equatorial-coordinate-system.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hour_circle)

An [hour circle](hour-circle.md) is a [great circle](great-circle.md) through both reference [celestial poles](celestial-pole.md) and a chosen direction. Its two halves have [right ascensions](right-ascension.md) differing by $12^{\rm h}$; choose the half containing the direction when measuring [declination](declination.md). The observer's [celestial meridian](celestial-meridian.md) is the particular [hour circle](hour-circle.md) through the [zenith](zenith.md). [Jeremy Tatum: Section 6.3, hour circles and declination circles](https://www.astro.uvic.ca/~tatum/celmechs/celm6.pdf).
