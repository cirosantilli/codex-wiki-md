<h1 id="principal-eilenberg-maclane-fibration">Principal Eilenberg–MacLane fibration</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Postnikov tower](postnikov-tower.md)

For an abelian group $A$, a principal fibration with fibre $K(A,n)$ is a [homotopy pullback](homotopy-pullback.md) of the [path fibration](path-space-fibration.md) $PK(A,n+1)\to K(A,n+1)$. It is therefore the [homotopy fibre](homotopy-fiber.md) of a map $k:B\to K(A,n+1)$ and is classified by $[k]\in H^{n+1}(B;A)$. The fibre acts by translation up to [homotopy](homotopy.md); in this untwisted description the [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md) acts trivially on $A$. This is the principal-fibration form used for [simply connected](simply-connected-space.md) [Postnikov towers](postnikov-tower.md).
