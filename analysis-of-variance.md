# Analysis of variance

↑ **Parent:** [Linear regression](linear-regression-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Analysis_of_variance)

Analysis of variance decomposes variation into contributions associated with model terms and residual error. For nested [normal linear models](normal-linear-model.md), differences of residual sums of squares give sequential or partial [F-tests](f-test.md).
