# Black-hole no-hair theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Black-hole uniqueness theorem](black-hole-uniqueness-theorem.md)

The black-hole no-hair principle says that radiation carries away the higher multipole structure of a collapsing isolated body, leaving a stationary black hole specified by conserved asymptotic charges such as [mass](mass.md), [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md), and [electric charge](electric-charge.md).
