# Improving Access to Psychological Therapies

↑ **Parent:** [Access to mental health care](access-to-mental-health-care.md)

The historical English programme expanding evidence-based psychological care for [depression](depression.md) and [anxiety](anxiety.md). Sessional outcome measurement made service results unusually complete and publicly inspectable. The clinical effects of particular feedback arrangements should be distinguished from the feasibility and transparency of collecting data.[Lancet 391, 679–686; 98% outcome coverage among treated patients, organisational predictors and between-year analyses](transparency-about-the-outcomes-of-mental-health-services-iapt-approach-an-analysis-of-public-data-by-david-m-clark-and-others.md)
