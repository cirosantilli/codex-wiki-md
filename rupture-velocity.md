# Rupture velocity

↑ **Parent:** [Finite-source rupture](finite-source-rupture.md)

The [speed](speed.md) at which the rupture front advances along a [fault plane](fault-plane.md). It is distinct from the local particle slip [velocity](velocity.md) and the propagating [seismic wave](seismic-wave.md) speeds. Length equals $V_rT$ only for the stated approximately constant-speed propagation geometry.
