# Electrochemical gradient

↑ **Parent:** [Electrochemical potential](electrochemical-potential.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrochemical_gradient)

An [electrochemical gradient](electrochemical-gradient.md) combines spatial changes in [chemical potential](chemical-potential.md) and [electric potential](electric-potential.md) for a charged species. [Passive transport](passive-transport.md) proceeds downhill in [electrochemical potential](electrochemical-potential.md) when an appropriate route is available; a [concentration](concentration.md) difference alone does not determine the direction for an [ion](ion.md).
