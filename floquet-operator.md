# Floquet operator

↑ **Parent:** [Time-evolution operator](time-evolution-operator.md)

For a periodic [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) $H(t+T)=H(t)$, the [Floquet operator](floquet-operator.md) is the [unitary operator](unitary-operator.md) evolving a [quantum state](quantum-state.md) through one complete period, at a fixed starting phase of the drive. The composition law gives $U(NT,0)=U_F^N$. Choosing a different starting phase conjugates $U_F$ by a [unitary operator](unitary-operator.md), so its [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are unchanged.
