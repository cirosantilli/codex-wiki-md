# Fixed-L4 minimization in the harmonic-oscillator energy space

↑ **Parent:** [Confining-potential energy space](confining-potential-energy-space.md)

In two dimensions, the quadratic functional

$$
\int|\nabla u|^2+\int|u|^2+\frac\eta4\int|x|^2|u|^2
$$

attains its minimum subject to $\|u\|_4^4=M>0$. A bounded minimizing sequence is weakly compact in the harmonic-oscillator energy space and strongly compact in $L^4$: local [Rellich-Kondrachov compactness theorem](rellich-kondrachov-theorem.md) and the weighted tail bound prevent escape to infinity.
