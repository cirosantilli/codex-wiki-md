# Charge continuity equation

↑ **Parent:** [Charge conservation from Maxwell equations](charge-conservation-from-maxwell-equations.md)

The local form of [conservation of electric charge](charge-conservation.md) relates [charge density](charge-density.md) to [current density](current-density.md). Taking the [divergence](divergence.md) of the [Ampère-Maxwell equation](ampere-s-circuital-law.md) and substituting [Gauss's law](gauss-s-law.md) proves the equation, using that the [divergence of a curl is zero](divergence-of-a-curl-is-zero.md).
