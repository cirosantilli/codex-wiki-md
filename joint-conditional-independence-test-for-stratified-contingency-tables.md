# Joint conditional independence test for stratified contingency tables

↑ **Parent:** [Pearson's chi-squared test](pearson-s-chi-squared-test.md)

For $K$ [independent](independent-random-variables.md) strata, each containing an $r\times c$ [contingency table](contingency-table.md), test [independence](independent-random-variables.md) of the two table variables within every stratum. Under adequate expected counts and the usual [multinomial distribution](multinomial-distribution.md) sampling assumptions, each stratum's [Pearson chi-squared statistic](pearson-chi-squared-statistic.md) has asymptotic [chi-squared distribution](chi-squared-distribution.md) with $(r-1)(c-1)$ [statistical degrees of freedom](statistical-degrees-of-freedom.md). Their sum has asymptotic [chi-squared distribution](chi-squared-distribution.md) with $K(r-1)(c-1)$ [statistical degrees of freedom](statistical-degrees-of-freedom.md), by the additivity of [independent](independent-random-variables.md) [chi-squared distributions](chi-squared-distribution.md). This is a test of conditional [independence](independent-random-variables.md), not of equality of a common effect across strata; the latter needs an [interaction contrast](interaction-contrast.md) or another specified homogeneity test.
