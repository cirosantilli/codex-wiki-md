# Confocal optical resonator

↑ **Parent:** [Optical cavity](optical-cavity.md)

A symmetric [confocal optical resonator](confocal-optical-resonator.md) has two concave mirrors of equal curvature $R$, separated by $R$. Its symmetric fundamental [Gaussian beam](gaussian-beam.md) has a central [beam waist](beam-waist.md) and [Rayleigh range](rayleigh-length.md) $R/2$. Its ideal transverse spectrum has degeneracies, so transmission at one resonance need not certify exclusive excitation of the fundamental [normal mode](normal-mode.md).
