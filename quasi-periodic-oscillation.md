# Quasi-periodic oscillation

↑ **Parent:** [Black-hole accretion](black-hole-accretion.md)

An oscillatory feature with finite width in a source's variability [power spectrum](power-spectrum.md), rather than a perfectly periodic clock. Orbital, precession and disc-mode interpretations connect different [frequencies](frequency.md) to the same compact object. Identifying an observed peak with an [innermost stable circular orbit](innermost-stable-circular-orbit.md) orbital [frequency](frequency.md) is therefore a model assumption, not a direct black-hole [mass](mass.md) or spin measurement.
