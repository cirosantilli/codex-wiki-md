# Units of Q of square root ten

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet's unit theorem](dirichlet-s-unit-theorem.md)

For $K=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{10})$,

$$
\mathcal O_K^\times
=\{\mathord\pm(3+\sqrt{10})^n:n\in\mathbb Z\}.
$$

The fundamental unit $3+\sqrt{10}$ has norm minus one.
