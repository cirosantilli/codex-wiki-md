# Sensory modality

↑ **Parent:** [Sensory system](sensory-system.md)

A category of sensory input and processing, such as [vision](vision.md), [hearing](hearing.md) or [gustation](gustation.md). Receptor type, pathway and experienced quality provide related classifications, but should not be assumed to define identical boundaries.
