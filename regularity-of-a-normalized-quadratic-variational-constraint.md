# Regularity of a normalized quadratic variational constraint

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrange multiplier](lagrange-multiplier.md)

An integral constraint need not admit a normal [Lagrange multiplier](lagrange-multiplier.md) unless its [first variation](first-variation.md) is nonzero. For the quadratic normalization in the title, the variation in direction $y$ has derivative $2\int wy^2=2$, so the constraint is always regular at an admissible function. With free endpoint values this justifies the [Euler-Lagrange equation](euler-lagrange-equation.md) for $F-\lambda wy^2$ and its natural endpoint conditions. For $F=py'^2-qy^2$, multiplication of the differential equation by $y$ gives $\lambda=J[y]$.
