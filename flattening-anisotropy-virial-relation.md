<h1 id="flattening-anisotropy-virial-relation">Flattening–anisotropy virial relation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor virial theorem](tensor-virial-theorem.md)

For a pressure-supported scale-free tracer $\rho\propto(R^2+z^2/q^2)^{-\gamma/2}$ in a spherical [Newtonian gravitational potential](newtonian-gravitational-potential.md), the [tensor virial theorem](tensor-virial-theorem.md) constrains integrated directional [velocity dispersions](velocity-dispersion.md). Modest oblate flattening and a decreasing radial [mass density](density.md) require greater in-plane random support per direction. The displayed expansion assumes finite global virial integrals; the corresponding ratio of force moments also has a cutoff-independent angular interpretation.
