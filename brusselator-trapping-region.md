# Brusselator trapping region

↑ **Parent:** [Brusselator](brusselator.md)

For the spatially homogeneous Brusselator with parameters $r,s>0$, set $\alpha=r/(1+s)$. The quadrilateral

$$
\alpha\leq x,\qquad
0\leq y\leq\frac s\alpha,\qquad
x+y\leq r+\frac s\alpha
$$

is a [trapping region](trapping-region.md). The four inward-pointing tests use $\dot x\geq0$ on $x=\alpha$, $\dot y\geq0$ on $y=0$, $\dot y\leq0$ on $y=s/\alpha$, and $\dot x+\dot y=r-x\leq0$ on the sloping edge.
