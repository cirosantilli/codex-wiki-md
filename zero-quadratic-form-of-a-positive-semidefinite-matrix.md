# Zero quadratic form of a positive semidefinite matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Positive semidefinite matrix](positive-semidefinite-matrix.md)

If $P$ is a real [positive semidefinite matrix](positive-semidefinite-matrix.md), then $x^TPx=0$ if and only if $Px=0$. Indeed $x^TPx=\|\sqrt P\,x\|^2$ by the [principal square root of a positive semidefinite matrix](principal-square-root-of-a-positive-semidefinite-matrix.md), so a zero [quadratic form](quadratic-form.md) puts $x$ in the [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) of $\sqrt P$ and hence of $P$.
