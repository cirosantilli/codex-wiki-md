# First-order excitation in a linearly expanding infinite well

↑ **Parent:** [Moving-wall derivative coupling](moving-wall-derivative-coupling.md)

For $L(t)=vt$ and $\alpha=3\pi^2\hbar/(2mv^2)$, first-order excitation out of the instantaneous ground state has the displayed oscillatory [integral](integral-split.md). Its lower endpoint means the limit of a ground-state preparation at positive time and positive width; no normalized state exists in a zero-width well. Substitution $u=\alpha/s$ makes convergence explicit, and $P_{1\to2}\sim16t^2/(9\alpha^2)$ as $t/\alpha\to0$. Large excitation invalidates the first-order approximation.
