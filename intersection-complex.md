# Intersection complex

↑ **Parent:** [Intersection cohomology](intersection-cohomology.md)

The degree-zero normalized Deligne [intersection complex](intersection-complex.md) extends the constant [sheaf](sheaf-mathematics.md) from the regular stratum by successive derived direct images and cohomological truncations at degree $\bar p(k)$ when codimension-$k$ strata are added. Its [hypercohomology](hypercohomology.md) defines [intersection cohomology](intersection-cohomology.md). On a cone with vertex of codimension $k$, its local [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) is the link [intersection cohomology](intersection-cohomology.md) in degrees at most $\bar p(k)$ and zero above.
