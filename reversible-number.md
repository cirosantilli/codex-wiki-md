# Reversible number

↑ **Parent:** [Positive integer](positive-integer.md)

A [positive integer](positive-integer.md) whose sum with its digit reversal has only odd [numerical digits](numerical-digit.md), with leading zeros forbidden in both the original and reversed representations. For example, $36+63=99$. Studying the [arithmetic carries](carry-arithmetic.md) of mirrored digit pairs allows whole lengths to be counted without enumerating all [integers](integer-split.md) of that length.
