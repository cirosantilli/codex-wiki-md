# Mean-square orbital radius

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic orbital](atomic-orbital.md)

For a normalized spatial [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md), $\langle r^2\rangle=\int r^2|\psi(\mathbf r)|^2d^3r$. Its square root is the root-mean-square radius, which differs from the exponential decay length. A spherical [hydrogenic donor state](hydrogenic-donor-state.md) with $\psi\propto e^{-r/a}$ has $\langle r^2\rangle=3a^2$, obtained by dividing the radial integrals of $r^4e^{-2r/a}$ and $r^2e^{-2r/a}$.
