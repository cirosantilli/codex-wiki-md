# Complex Proca Hamiltonian

↑ **Parent:** [Proca field](proca-field.md)

For [Minkowski metric](minkowski-metric.md) $(+---)$ and $\mathcal L=-G_{\mu\nu}^*G^{\mu\nu}/2+M^2B_\mu^*B^\mu$, use coordinates $B^\mu,B^{*\mu}$. The spatial [canonical momenta](canonical-momentum.md) are $\pi_i=G^{*0i}$, $\pi_i^*=G^{0i}$; the temporal momenta vanish. Constraint preservation gives $\partial_i\pi_i=M^2B_0^*$. Eliminating $B_0$ and its conjugate gives the positive reduced [Hamiltonian density](hamiltonian-density.md) displayed above, up to a spatial surface term. In particular the massive [Gauss law](gauss-s-law.md) has a mass-dependent right-hand side; setting it to zero removes a physical longitudinal [particle polarization](particle-polarization.md).
