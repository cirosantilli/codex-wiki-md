# Charge relaxation in an isolated conducting cylinder

↑ **Parent:** [Charge relaxation](charge-relaxation.md)

A uniform cylindrical volume charge relaxes exponentially under [Ohm's law](ohm-s-law.md) and the [continuity equation for electric charge](charge-continuity-equation.md). In a cylinder of radius $R$ initially carrying no surface charge, the radial conduction current deposits $\rho_s(t)=R\rho_0(1-e^{-\sigma t/\epsilon_0})/2$ on its surface. Total charge per unit length remains $\pi R^2\rho_0$, so the exterior radial [electric field](electric-field.md) is time independent even though the interior field decays. Neglecting the deposited surface charge violates charge conservation.
