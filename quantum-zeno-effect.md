# Quantum Zeno effect

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum measurement](quantum-measurement-split.md)

Inhibition of a transition by increasingly frequent ideal [projective measurements](projective-measurement.md) that check whether a [quantum system](quantum-system-split.md) remains in its initial [quantum state](quantum-state.md). For a normalized vector $\psi$, a fixed [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) $H$ and the [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md) $P=|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|$, the [probability](probability.md) of passing every check at $N$ equally spaced times up to fixed time $T$ is

$$
\mathbb P_N(T)=\left|\langle\psi,e^{-iHT/(N\hbar)}\psi\rangle\right|^{2N}.
$$

For a finite-dimensional [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md), the [quadratic short-time quantum survival probability](quadratic-short-time-quantum-survival-probability.md) implies $\mathbb P_N(T)\to1$. This is an ideal limit with instantaneous measurements and [unitary time evolution](unitary-time-evolution.md) between them.
