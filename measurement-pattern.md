# Measurement pattern

↑ **Parent:** [Measurement-based quantum computation](measurement-based-quantum-computation.md)

A measurement pattern specifies the resource [quantum state](quantum-state.md), the measured [qubits](qubit.md), their measurement bases, outcome-dependent basis choices and output corrections or classical relabellings. The dependence of a basis on earlier outcomes determines the [logical depth of a measurement pattern](logical-depth-of-a-measurement-pattern.md). Preparing the resource and processing the final classical results are separate costs.
