# Horizontal cell

↑ **Parent:** [Neuron](neuron-split.md)

A retinal interneuron contributing lateral interactions among [photoreceptor](photoreceptor.md) signals and spatial context. Its participation in a circuit does not imply that every perceptual surround effect originates at that one cell type.
