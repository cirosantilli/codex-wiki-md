# Affine lift of a holomorphic map between complex tori

↑ **Parent:** [Complex torus](complex-torus.md)

Every [holomorphic map](holomorphic-map.md) $V_1/\Lambda_1\to V_2/\Lambda_2$ lifts to $z\mapsto Az+x$, where $A$ is a [complex-linear map](complex-linear-map.md) satisfying $A\Lambda_1\subseteq\Lambda_2$. Period differences of a lift lie in the discrete target [Euclidean lattice](euclidean-lattice.md), so its derivative is periodic. Boundedness on a compact fundamental domain and the [Liouville theorem](liouville-theorem.md) make this derivative constant. The linear map is unique, and the translation vector is determined modulo $\Lambda_2$.
