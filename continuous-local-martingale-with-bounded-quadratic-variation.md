# Continuous local martingale with bounded quadratic variation

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous local martingale](continuous-local-martingale.md)

A [continuous local martingale](continuous-local-martingale.md) $M$ with $M_0=0$ and deterministically bounded [quadratic variation](quadratic-variation.md) on every finite horizon is a [square-integrable](square-integrable-function.md) [martingale](martingale-split.md), with $\mathbb EM_t^2=\mathbb E[M]_t$. Localization, the [Doob L2 maximal inequality](doob-l2-maximal-inequality.md), and the resulting integrable bound on the squared supremum justify removing the [localizing sequence](localizing-sequence.md).
