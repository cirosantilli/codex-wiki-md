<h1 id="andrews-s-carbon-dioxide-experiment">Andrews's carbon dioxide experiment</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Van der Waals equation](van-der-waals-equation.md)

The 1869 [The Bakerian Lecture: On the Continuity of the Gaseous and Liquid States of Matter by Thomas Andrews](the-bakerian-lecture-on-the-continuity-of-the-gaseous-and-liquid-states-of-matter-by-thomas-andrews.md) describes dried [carbon dioxide](carbon-dioxide.md) trapped above [mercury](mercury-element.md) in a sealed [capillary tube](capillary-tube.md). A steel screw pressurized [water](water.md) in a strong [copper](copper.md) tube; movable [mercury](mercury-element.md) transmitted the load. A second [capillary tube](capillary-tube.md) held air as a [pressure](pressure.md) gauge. Flowing [water](water.md) around the visible [capillary tubes](capillary-tube.md) controlled [temperature](temperature.md); a [thermometer](thermometer.md) read the bath. A [cathetometer](cathetometer.md) read column positions, converted to [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) readings using weighed-[mercury](mercury-element.md) calibration.[Andrews, pp. 576–578 and Plate LXIII, figures 3 and 5–7: sample preparation, screw, coupled capillaries, water jackets and optical readout](the-bakerian-lecture-on-the-continuity-of-the-gaseous-and-liquid-states-of-matter-by-thomas-andrews.md)

Below the critical [temperature](temperature.md), compression produced a visible [liquid](liquid.md)–[gas](gas.md) boundary; above it, large density changes occurred without that boundary. Three trials gave $30.92\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ as his critical [temperature](temperature.md). Purity checks detected residual air; [mercury](mercury-element.md) height corrections accounted for unequal [hydrostatic pressures](hydrostatic-pressure.md). He explicitly left air nonideality and pressure-induced tube-capacity corrections unresolved, so his air-gauge [pressures](pressure.md) were approximate. These readouts support a [liquid-gas critical point](liquid-gas-critical-point.md); they do not measure a homogeneous unstable loop or establish universal quantitative accuracy of the later [Van der Waals equation](van-der-waals-equation.md).[Andrews, pp. 576, 578–580 and 583: residual air, approximate air-gauge pressure, hydrostatic corrections and three critical-temperature trials](the-bakerian-lecture-on-the-continuity-of-the-gaseous-and-liquid-states-of-matter-by-thomas-andrews.md)
