# Static spherical Komar mass in reciprocal radial gauge

↑ **Parent:** [Komar mass](komar-mass.md)

For $ds^2=-f(r)dt^2+dr^2/f(r)+R(r)^2d\Omega^2$ and $K=\partial_t$ normalized at infinity, the [Komar mass](komar-mass.md) on a round sphere is $R^2f'/(2G)$. The future unit normal to a static slice is $f^{-1/2}K$, and the outward unit radial normal is $f^{1/2}\partial_r$; their contraction with $\nabla_aK_b$ gives $f'/2$, whose integral over the sphere yields the formula. The asymptotic mass is its limit at infinity. In the presence of matter, the finite-radius integral need not be constant.
