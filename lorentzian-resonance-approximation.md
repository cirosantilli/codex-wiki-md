# Lorentzian resonance approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Dirac delta function](dirac-delta-function.md)

For $\gamma>0$, the kernels $\gamma/[\pi(x^2+\gamma^2)]$ have unit integral and concentrate at zero as $\gamma\to0^+$. Splitting the integral into a neighborhood of zero and its complement proves convergence against bounded continuous test functions. Thus $[(kv-\omega)^2+\gamma^2]^{-1}\simeq\pi\delta(kv-\omega)/|\gamma|$ under an [integral](integral-split.md), with $\delta(kv-\omega)=\delta(v-\omega/k)/|k|$. This is the narrow-width limit of a [Cauchy distribution](cauchy-distribution.md), not a pointwise replacement away from the peak.
