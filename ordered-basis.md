# Ordered basis

↑ **Parent:** [Basis](basis.md)

An ordered basis is a [basis](basis.md) equipped with an ordering of its [basis vectors](basis-vector.md). The order fixes which position of a [coordinate vector](coordinate-vector.md) corresponds to each [basis vector](basis-vector.md).
