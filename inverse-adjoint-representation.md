# Inverse-adjoint representation

↑ **Parent:** [Group representation](group-representation.md)

For a complex finite-dimensional [group representation](group-representation.md), the displayed matrices give another [group representation](group-representation.md): taking inverses and taking the [conjugate transpose](conjugate-transpose.md) each reverse multiplication order, so their composition preserves it. This operation is involutive. With the standard [Hermitian inner product](hermitian-inner-product.md), an invariant [vector subspace](vector-subspace.md) $W$ for $D^\sharp$ has [orthogonal complement](orthogonal-complement.md) invariant for $D$, because $D(g)^\dagger W=D^\sharp(g^{-1})W\subseteq W$. Thus $\langle w,D(g)v\rangle=\langle D(g)^\dagger w,v\rangle=0$ for $v\in W^\perp,w\in W$. It follows that $D^\sharp$ is an [irreducible representation](irreducible-representation.md) exactly when $D$ is. No finiteness assumption on the [group](group-split.md) or unitarity assumption on the [group representation](group-representation.md) is needed.
