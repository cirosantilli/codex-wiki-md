# Near-axis radial magnetic field

↑ **Parent:** [Gauss's law for magnetism](gauss-s-law-for-magnetism.md)

For a smooth axisymmetric [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md), write $B_z(\rho,z)=B_0(z)+O(\rho^2)$. The absence of magnetic monopoles gives $\rho^{-1}\partial_\rho(\rho B_\rho)=-B_0'(z)+O(\rho^2)$. Integrating from the axis and excluding a singular $1/\rho$ term proves the displayed relation. An axial field decreasing along $+z$ has an outward radial component, even when the field points purely axially on the axis itself.
