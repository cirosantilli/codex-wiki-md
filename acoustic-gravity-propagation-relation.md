# Acoustic-gravity propagation relation

↑ **Parent:** [Linear adiabatic stellar oscillation equations](linear-adiabatic-stellar-oscillation-equations.md)

In the [Cowling approximation](cowling-approximation.md) and local short-wavelength limit, the radial wave number satisfies $k_r^2\simeq(\omega^2-S_\ell^2)(\omega^2-N^2)/(c_s^2\omega^2)$. The [Lamb frequency](lamb-frequency.md) $S_\ell$ and [stellar buoyancy frequency](stellar-buoyancy-frequency.md) $N$ delimit acoustic and gravity-wave cavities. This leading interior relation does not replace the near-surface acoustic cutoff or the full gravitational boundary problem.
