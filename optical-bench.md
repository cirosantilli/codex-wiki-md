# Optical bench

↑ **Parent:** [Image formation](image-formation.md)

An [optical bench](optical-bench.md) is a rail or aligned platform carrying an object, [optical lens](lens-optics.md) elements and a screen or detector at readable positions. It turns [image formation](image-formation.md) into measurements of position and [transverse magnification](transverse-magnification.md). Read distances between the optical planes rather than arbitrary holder boundaries; keep heights and [optical axis](optical-axis.md) alignment controlled. [PASCO, Object and Image Distances Thin Lens: bench apparatus and measurements](https://www.pasco.com/resources/lab-experiments/484).
