# Symmetric Flory-Huggins polymer blend

↑ **Parent:** [Flory–Huggins solution theory](flory-huggins-solution-theory.md)

For two equal-site-volume polymers of chain length $N$, the mixing [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md) per lattice site is $f/(k_BT)=[\phi\ln\phi+(1-\phi)\ln(1-\phi)]/N+\chi\phi(1-\phi)$. Each chain is one translating object, reducing the [entropy of mixing](entropy-of-mixing.md) per monomer by $N$ relative to the [regular solution model](regular-solution.md). Its curvature is $f''/(k_BT)=1/[N\phi(1-\phi)]-2\chi$. The symmetric [thermodynamic critical point](thermodynamic-critical-point.md) has $\phi_c=1/2$ and $\chi_c=2/N$. Long chains can therefore demix under a very small unfavorable [Flory interaction parameter](flory-interaction-parameter.md).
