# William Bertozzi

↑ **Parent:** [List of physicists](list-of-physicists.md)

An experimental nuclear physicist whose [electron](electron.md) speed-and-energy measurement provides a direct test of relativistic kinematics. [Bertozzi's electron speed-energy experiment](bertozzi-s-electron-speed-energy-experiment.md) measures flight time and calorimetric [energy](energy-split.md) independently.

Official profile: [https://physics.mit.edu/faculty/william-bertozzi/](https://physics.mit.edu/faculty/william-bertozzi/)
