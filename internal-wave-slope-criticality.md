# Internal-wave slope criticality

↑ **Parent:** [Reflection of an internal-wave ray](reflection-of-an-internal-wave-ray.md)

Let an [internal gravity wave](internal-wave.md) energy ray make angle $\theta$ with the horizontal, so that $\sin\theta=\omega/N$. A boundary of local slope magnitude $s$ is subcritical, critical, or supercritical according as $s$ is smaller than, equal to, or larger than $\tan\theta$. At criticality the inviscid reflected wavelength tends to zero.
