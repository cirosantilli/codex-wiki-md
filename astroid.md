# Astroid

↑ **Parent:** [Plane curve](plane-curve.md)

An astroid is the four-cusped [plane curve](plane-curve.md) parametrized by $x=a\cos^3t$, $y=a\sin^3t$ for $a>0$. It appears as the relative trajectory in the [astroid motion of a harmonic oscillator in a rotating frame](astroid-motion-of-a-harmonic-oscillator-in-a-rotating-frame.md). The parametrization has zero first derivative at the cusps while remaining smooth as a function of its parameter.
