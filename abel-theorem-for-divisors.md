# Abel theorem for divisors

↑ **Parent:** [Abel-Jacobi map of a compact Riemann surface](abel-jacobi-map-of-a-compact-riemann-surface.md)

A degree-zero [complex analytic divisor](divisor-on-a-complex-manifold.md) $E$ on a [compact Riemann surface](compact-riemann-surface.md) is principal exactly when its [Abel-Jacobi map of a compact Riemann surface](abel-jacobi-map-of-a-compact-riemann-surface.md) is zero. Normalize a [differential of the third kind](differential-of-the-third-kind.md) with residues equal to the coefficients of $E$. The [Riemann bilinear relations for a compact surface](riemann-bilinear-relations-for-a-compact-surface.md) express its $b$-periods as $2\pi i$ times the Abel integrals. If those integrals are in the period lattice, subtract a suitable integral multiple of the normalized [holomorphic differential forms](holomorphic-differential-form.md); all periods then lie in $2\pi i\mathbb Z$, so exponentiation gives the required [meromorphic function](meromorphic-function.md). Conversely, the logarithmic differential of a [meromorphic function](meromorphic-function.md) has integer residues and periods in $2\pi i\mathbb Z$, giving a zero Abel class by the same calculation.
