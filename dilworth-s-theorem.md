<h1 id="dilworth-s-theorem">Dilworth's theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Partially ordered set](partially-ordered-set.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dilworth's_theorem)

Every finite [partially ordered set](partially-ordered-set.md) can be partitioned into as many [chains in a partial order](chain-in-a-partial-order.md) as its largest [antichain](antichain.md). Reachability in an acyclic [directed graph](directed-graph.md) is a [partial order](partially-ordered-set.md), so the theorem supplies chains which can be expanded into directed paths, possibly overlapping at intervening vertices.
