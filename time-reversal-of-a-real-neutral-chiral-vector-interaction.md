# Time reversal of a real neutral chiral vector interaction

↑ **Parent:** [Time reversal of a Dirac field](time-reversal-of-a-dirac-field.md)

Let $B=\gamma^5C$ with the unitary [charge-conjugation matrix](charge-conjugation-matrix.md) C, and $t_0=1,t_i=-1$. Hermitian-adjoint gamma identities give $C\gamma^{\mu*}C^{-1}=-\gamma^0\gamma^\mu\gamma^0$, and multiplication by $\gamma^5$ gives the displayed identities. An [antiunitary operator](antiunitary-operator.md) conjugates the explicit i in $\gamma^5=i\gamma^0\gamma^1\gamma^2\gamma^3$, which is essential to the second identity. Both vector and axial currents therefore have the same time-reversal component signs; their contraction with a similarly transforming neutral vector is invariant for real couplings.
