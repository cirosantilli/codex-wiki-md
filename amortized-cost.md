# Amortized cost

↑ **Parent:** [Potential method](potential-method.md)

In the [potential method](potential-method.md), an operation's [amortized cost](amortized-cost.md) is $\widehat c_i=c_i+\Phi_i-\Phi_{i-1}$. It can exceed or fall below its actual cost $c_i$. The endpoint potentials determine how the total charge compares with the total actual cost.
