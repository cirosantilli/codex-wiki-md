# Quantifier

↑ **Parent:** [First-order logic](first-order-logic.md)

A [quantifier](quantifier.md) binds a [free variable](free-variable.md) in a [first-order formula](first-order-formula.md) and specifies how its truth depends on elements of a [first-order structure](first-order-structure.md): [universal quantification](universal-quantification.md) $\forall$ requires every element, while [existential quantification](existential-quantification.md) $\exists$ requires at least one.
