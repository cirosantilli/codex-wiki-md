# Good filtration for the order filtration

↑ **Parent:** [Good filtration of a module](good-filtration-of-a-module.md)

For the [order filtration of a Weyl algebra](order-filtration-of-a-weyl-algebra.md), a [filtration of a module](filtration-of-a-module.md) is good if it is increasing, exhaustive, zero in sufficiently negative degrees, compatible with multiplication, and has [associated graded module](associated-graded-module.md) finite over the [associated graded ring](associated-graded-ring.md). Equivalently it has the displayed finite shifted-generating description. Choose homogeneous generators of the [associated graded module](associated-graded-module.md), lift them to $m_\ell$, and successively remove the top symbol of any element; the lower bound on the [filtration of a module](filtration-of-a-module.md) makes this process terminate. Conversely, the displayed description makes the symbols of the $m_\ell$ generate. Each $M_j$ is finite over the degree-zero [polynomial ring](polynomial-ring.md) $\mathbb C[x_1,\ldots,x_n]$; it need not be a [finite-dimensional vector space](finite-dimensional-vector-space.md) over $\mathbb C$.
