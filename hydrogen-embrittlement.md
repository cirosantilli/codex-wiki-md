# Hydrogen embrittlement

↑ **Parent:** [Materials science](materials-science.md)

Loss of resistance to cracking associated with hydrogen in a material. Mechanisms are material-dependent; brittle hydride precipitation is particularly relevant to [zirconium alloys](zirconium-alloys.md).
