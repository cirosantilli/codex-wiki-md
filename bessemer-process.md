# Bessemer process

↑ **Parent:** [Steelmaking](steelmaking.md)

Conversion of molten carbon-rich [iron](iron.md) by blowing air through it. [Oxidation](oxidation.md) of constituents supplies [heat](heat-split.md); early acidic converters required low-phosphorus feed.
