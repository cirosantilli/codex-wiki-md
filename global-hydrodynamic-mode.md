# Global hydrodynamic mode

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrodynamic stability](hydrodynamic-stability-split.md)

A global hydrodynamic mode is a separated solution $a(x)e^{-i\omega t}$ satisfying the complete spatial [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md) of a linearized flow problem. Its [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) depends on the spatial domain and variable coefficients, rather than solely on a local [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation.md).
