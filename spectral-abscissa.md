# Spectral abscissa

↑ **Parent:** [Eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md)

The [spectral abscissa](spectral-abscissa.md) is the largest real part of a [matrix](matrix.md)'s [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md). It describes asymptotic exponential rates of its [matrix exponential](matrix-exponential.md), but a [non-normal matrix](non-normal-matrix.md) can also have transient amplification. It is generally distinct from the [Euclidean logarithmic norm](euclidean-logarithmic-norm.md), which gives an immediate Euclidean energy-growth bound.
