# Inviscid instability of a piecewise-linear mixing layer

↑ **Parent:** [Rayleigh equation for inviscid shear flow](rayleigh-equation-for-inviscid-shear-flow.md)

For $U=-1$ below $z=-1$, $U=z$ between $z=-1$ and $z=1$, and $U=1$ above $z=1$, decaying [normal modes](normal-mode.md) solve $\phi''-k^2\phi=0$ in each region. [Pressure matching at a piecewise-linear shear interface](pressure-matching-at-a-piecewise-linear-shear-interface.md) gives continuity of $\phi$ and $(U-c)\phi'-U'\phi$. The two corner conditions yield the displayed [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation.md). At $k=1/2$ the wave speeds are $c=\pm i/e$, so one mode grows exponentially with rate $1/(2e)$.
