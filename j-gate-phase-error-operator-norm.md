# J-gate phase-error operator norm

↑ **Parent:** [J gate in measurement-based quantum computation](j-gate-in-measurement-based-quantum-computation.md)

The [J gate](j-gate-in-measurement-based-quantum-computation.md) is $J(\alpha)=HP(\alpha)$, where $P(\alpha)=\operatorname{diag}(1,e^{i\alpha})$: the [phase gate](phase-gate.md) acts first and the [Hadamard gate](hadamard-gate.md) acts second. Unitary invariance of the [operator norm](operator-norm.md) reduces its angle error to the single nonzero diagonal difference of the [phase gate](phase-gate.md). Its norm is $|e^{i\delta}-1|=2|\sin(\delta/2)|\leq|\delta|$. The [quantum circuit gate-error telescoping bound](quantum-circuit-gate-error-telescoping-bound.md) then makes an angle tolerance of $\epsilon/k$ sufficient for $k$ such imperfect gates and exact other gates.
