# Simple shear

↑ **Parent:** [Finite strain theory](finite-strain-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simple_shear)

Simple shear displaces one coordinate direction in proportion to a transverse coordinate while preserving volume. Its [deformation gradient](deformation-gradient.md) has a single off-diagonal component $\gamma$. [Simple shear flow](simple-shear-flow.md) describes the corresponding velocity field rather than the finite deformation.
