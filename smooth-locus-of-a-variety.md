# Smooth locus of a variety

↑ **Parent:** [Zariski tangent space](zariski-tangent-space.md)

The smooth locus consists of points $P$ for which $\dim T_{X,P}=\dim X$. For an irreducible variety over a perfect field it is a nonempty [Zariski-open subset](zariski-open-set.md) and therefore dense.
