# Longitudinal solenoid field under an axial boost

↑ **Parent:** [Lorentz transformation of electromagnetic fields](lorentz-transformation-of-electromagnetic-fields.md)

An ideal long [solenoid](solenoid.md) has $B=\mu_0nI$, with axial turn [density](density.md) n. Under an axial boost, turn [density](density.md) becomes $n'=\gamma n$. The transverse circulation path is unchanged while a turn's charge-circulation rate, measured relative to its moving material, becomes $I'=I/\gamma$. Thus $n'I'=nI$ and the axial [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) remains unchanged. Equivalently, the ideal transverse current-sheet [density](density.md) is invariant under this boost. End effects are excluded.
