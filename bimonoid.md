# Bimonoid

↑ **Parent:** [Monoid object](monoid-object.md)

An object that is both a [monoid object](monoid-object.md) and a [comonoid](comonoid.md) in a [braided monoidal category](braided-monoidal-category.md), with $\Delta,\varepsilon$ preserving multiplication and unit. The product on its tensor square uses the ambient [braiding](braiding.md).
