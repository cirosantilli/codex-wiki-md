# Matrix 2-norm of a normal matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Normal matrix](normal-matrix.md)

For every finite-dimensional normal matrix $A$, the [matrix 2-norm](matrix-2-norm.md) equals the [spectral radius](spectral-radius.md):

$$
\lVert A\rVert_2=\max_{\lambda\in\sigma(A)}|\lambda|=\rho(A).
$$

This follows by [unitary diagonalization of a normal matrix](unitary-diagonalization-of-a-normal-matrix.md) and unitary invariance of the matrix 2-norm.
