# Optical rotation

↑ **Parent:** [Optical activity](optical-activity.md)

[Optical rotation](optical-rotation.md) is the signed [angle](angle.md) measured in [polarimetry](polarimetry.md). Positive rotation is clockwise for an observer facing the incoming [light](light.md); negative rotation is counterclockwise. State the viewing convention, [wavelength](wavelength.md), [temperature](temperature-split.md), [solvent](solvent.md), [concentration](concentration.md) and path length when comparing measurements. [R and S configuration](r-and-s-configuration.md) specifies spatial arrangement, and does not predict the sign of [optical rotation](optical-rotation.md).[McMurry, Organic Chemistry, §5.5, discussion after Figure 5.10: no simple relation between R/S and rotation sign](organic-chemistry-by-john-mcmurry/5-5-sequence-rules-for-specifying-configuration.md)

Here the observer stands beyond the sample, on the detector side, looking back toward the source. Measure both the [polarimeter analyzer](polarimeter-analyzer.md) [angle](angle.md) $\theta$ and the [optical rotation](optical-rotation.md) $\alpha$ clockwise from the blank's polarization direction. Looking from the source end reverses the apparent sense. A line has no distinguished arrowhead, so an intensity scan alone determines its orientation only modulo $180^\circ$; continuity or additional information must choose the appropriate rotation branch.
