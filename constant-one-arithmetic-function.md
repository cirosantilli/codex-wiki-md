# Constant-one arithmetic function

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet convolution](dirichlet-convolution.md)

The [constant-one arithmetic function](constant-one-arithmetic-function.md) has $\mathbf1(n)=1$ for every [positive integer](positive-integer.md) $n$, including $\mathbf1(1)=\mathbf1(12)=1$. It is a [completely multiplicative function](completely-multiplicative-function.md). Its [Dirichlet convolution](dirichlet-convolution.md) with an [arithmetic function](arithmetic-function-split.md) $f$ is the [divisor sum](divisor-sum.md) $(\mathbf1*f)(n)=\sum_{d\mid n}f(d)$. Thus $\mathbf1*\mathbf1=\tau$, the [divisor function](divisor-function.md). Its [pointwise product of arithmetic functions](pointwise-product-of-arithmetic-functions.md) with $f$ is $f$ itself; its [Dirichlet convolution](dirichlet-convolution.md) with $f$ generally is not. The [Identity for Dirichlet convolution](identity-for-dirichlet-convolution.md) is instead $\varepsilon$.
