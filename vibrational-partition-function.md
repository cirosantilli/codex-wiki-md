# Vibrational partition function

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical partition function](canonical-partition-function.md)

A [vibrational partition function](vibrational-partition-function.md) sums over molecular vibrational [energy levels](energy-level.md). A single [quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md), with energies measured relative to its ground level and vibrational temperature $\theta_v=h\nu/k$, has $q_{\rm vib}=(1-e^{-\theta_v/T})^{-1}$. Retaining its absolute [zero-point energy](zero-point-energy.md) multiplies this by $e^{-\theta_v/(2T)}$. That factor shifts the [internal energy](internal-energy-split.md) but contributes no [entropy](entropy-split.md).
