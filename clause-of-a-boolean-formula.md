# Clause of a Boolean formula

↑ **Parent:** [Boolean formula](boolean-formula.md)

A clause is an expression $\ell_1\lor\cdots\lor\ell_k$ formed from [Boolean literals](boolean-literal.md), true when at least one literal is true. The empty clause is false. Repeating a literal does not change the truth value. A [conjunctive normal form](conjunctive-normal-form.md) formula is a conjunction of these clauses.
