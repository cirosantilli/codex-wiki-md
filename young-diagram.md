# Young diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Partition of an integer](partition-of-an-integer.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Young_diagram)

The Young diagram of $\lambda$ is the set of cells $(i,j)$ with $1\leq j\leq\lambda_i$.
