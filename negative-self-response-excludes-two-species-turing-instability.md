# Negative self-response excludes two-species Turing instability

↑ **Parent:** [Turing instability](turing-instability.md)

For a reaction-stable two-species [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) with both [Jacobian matrix](jacobian-matrix.md) diagonal entries negative and positive diagonal diffusivities, each spatial mode has more negative [trace](matrix-trace.md) after [diffusion](diffusion.md) is added. Its [determinant](determinant.md) is the positive reaction [determinant](determinant.md) plus positive coefficients of $k^2$ and $k^4$. Hence every mode stays stable. Unequal [diffusion](diffusion.md) alone is insufficient for a [Turing instability](turing-instability.md); the reaction feedback must also permit a destabilizing weighted diagonal sum.
