# Harmonic mean

↑ **Parent:** [Arithmetic mean](arithmetic-mean.md)

The [harmonic mean](harmonic-mean.md) of positive numbers $x_1,\ldots,x_n$ is $H=n/(\sum_i1/x_i)$. The [Jensen inequality](jensen-s-inequality.md) for $1/x$ gives $H\le\overline x$, with equality exactly when all entries coincide.
