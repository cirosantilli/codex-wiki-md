# Rest energy

↑ **Parent:** [Four-momentum](four-momentum.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rest_energy)

The rest energy of a particle of invariant mass $m$ is $mc^2$, the energy measured in its rest frame.
