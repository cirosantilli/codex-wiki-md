# Bulk heterojunction

↑ **Parent:** [Organic solar cell](organic-solar-cell.md)

An interpenetrating donor-acceptor morphology in a photovoltaic absorber. Small domains facilitate [exciton](exciton.md) access to interfaces, while continuous pathways in each phase allow separated charges to reach selective contacts.
