# Body-centered tetragonal lattice

↑ **Parent:** [Bravais lattice](bravais-lattice.md)

A tetragonal [Bravais lattice](bravais-lattice.md) represented by conventional-cell points $(0,0,0)$ and $(1/2,1/2,1/2)$, with $a=b\ne c$ and right angles. A motif attached to each point determines the atomic structure and can give more than two atoms per conventional cell.
