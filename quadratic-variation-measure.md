# Quadratic-variation measure

↑ **Parent:** [Itô isometry](ito-isometry.md)

For a [L2-bounded continuous martingale](l2-bounded-continuous-martingale.md) $M$, define a finite [measure](measure.md) on the [predictable sigma-algebra](predictable-sigma-algebra.md) by $\nu_M(A)=\mathbb E\int_0^\infty\mathbf1_A\,d[M]$. Its total mass is $\mathbb E[M]_\infty=\mathbb E M_\infty^2-\mathbb E M_0^2$. The [Lebesgue space](lp-space.md) $L^2(\nu_M)$ identifies [predictable processes](predictable-process.md) that agree $\nu_M$-almost everywhere. Integration against $M$ is an isometry from this space into [L2-bounded continuous martingales](l2-bounded-continuous-martingale.md) starting at zero by the [Itô isometry](ito-isometry.md). This gives the correct integrand space even when the [quadratic variation](quadratic-variation.md) is random or is not absolutely continuous in time.
