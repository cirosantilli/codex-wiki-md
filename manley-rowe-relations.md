# Manley-Rowe relations

↑ **Parent:** [Three-wave mixing](three-wave-mixing.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manley–Rowe_relations)

Conservation relations for resonant, lossless wave mixing. For [sum-frequency generation](sum-frequency-generation.md) with $\omega_3=\omega_1+\omega_2$, the intensities obey $I_1'/\omega_1=I_2'/\omega_2=-I_3'/\omega_3$. Thus $I_1/\omega_1+I_3/\omega_3$ and $I_2/\omega_2+I_3/\omega_3$ are constant. Their difference gives a third, dependent invariant. Adding the intensity derivatives yields [conservation of energy](conservation-of-energy.md).

The exchange interpretation divides each intensity by its [photon](photon.md) energy $\hbar\omega_j$. One conversion removes a photon from each lower-frequency mode and adds one to the upper mode. For classical coupled-wave equations, differentiating the squared [complex amplitudes](complex-amplitude.md) gives the same common exchange rate multiplied by $(\omega_1,\omega_2,-\omega_3)$. Loss or externally replenished pumps require corresponding source terms.
