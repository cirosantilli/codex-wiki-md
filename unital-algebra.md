# Unital algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Associative algebra](associative-algebra-split.md)

An [associative algebra](associative-algebra-split.md) with a two-sided multiplicative identity. For a [subalgebra](subalgebra.md) of [bounded operators](continuous-linear-operator.md), the term [unital](unital-algebra.md) here means that it contains the identity of the represented [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md).
