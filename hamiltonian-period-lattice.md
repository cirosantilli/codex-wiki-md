# Hamiltonian period lattice

↑ **Parent:** [Integrable Hamiltonian system](integrable-hamiltonian-system.md)

For a compact connected regular level of $n$ independent [Poisson-commuting functions](poisson-commuting-functions.md), the periods of their joint flow form a full-rank discrete subgroup $\Lambda\subset\mathbb R^n$. The level is $\mathbb R^n/\Lambda$. A local smooth basis of these periods determines [action integrals](action-integral.md) and the normalized periodic fields used to construct [action-angle variables](action-angle-variables.md).
