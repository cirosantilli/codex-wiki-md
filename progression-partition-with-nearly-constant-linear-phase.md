# Progression partition with nearly constant linear phase

↑ **Parent:** [Linear phase](linear-phase.md)

For a [linear phase](linear-phase.md) $e^{2\pi i\theta x}$ on $[n]$, set $Q=\lfloor\sqrt n\rfloor$. The [Dirichlet approximation theorem](dirichlet-s-approximation-theorem.md) gives $1\leq q\leq Q$ with $\|q\theta\|_{\mathbb R/\mathbb Z}\leq Q^{-1}$. If $1\leq L$ and $2L\leq Q$, split each residue chain modulo $q$ into [arithmetic progressions](arithmetic-progression.md) of lengths between $L$ and $2L-1$, merging the final remainder into the preceding block. Each chain has at least $Q$ points, so this is possible. On each resulting [arithmetic progression](arithmetic-progression.md), the [linear phase](linear-phase.md) varies from its first value by at most $4\pi L/Q$. This converts a large [Fourier coefficient](fourier-coefficient.md) of a zero-sum real [function](function-split.md) into positive mean on one block: freezing the [linear phase](linear-phase.md) controls the weighted block sums, and their positive and negative total masses agree.
