# Xenon poisoning

↑ **Parent:** [Neutron poison](neutron-poison.md)

The reduction of [reactor reactivity](reactivity-nuclear-engineering.md) caused by neutron absorption by xenon-135. In a simplified model its abundance is fed by radioactive iodine decay, while its removal is by [radioactive decay](radioactive-decay.md) and [neutron capture](neutron-capture.md). Changes in these pathways can delay its response to a change of [neutron flux](neutron-flux.md).
