# Compactified approximation from endpoint asymptotics

↑ **Parent:** [Asymptotic expansion](asymptotic-expansion.md)

Suppose a continuous function $F(\lambda)$ on $[0,\infty)$ has a known power-law tail $F(\lambda)\sim a\lambda^{-p}$. Put $u=\lambda/(1+\lambda)$ and $h(u)=(1+\lambda)^pF(\lambda)$. When $h$ extends continuously to $u=1$, [polynomial approximation](polynomial-approximation.md) on $[0,1]$ gives a global approximation $F(\lambda)\approx(1+\lambda)^{-p}h_N(u)$ using only elementary operations. [Taylor series](taylor-series.md) at zero and the tail [asymptotic expansion](asymptotic-expansion.md) can constrain endpoint coefficients. Agreement at the two endpoints alone does not guarantee accuracy in the intervening interval; uniform error control or intermediate calibration is needed.
