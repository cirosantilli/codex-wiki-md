# Conditional latent event-time density under competing risks

↑ **Parent:** [Competing risks model](competing-risks-model.md)

For independent continuous event times, conditioning on $T_A<T_B$ weights the density of $T_A$ by the [survivor function](survival-function.md) of $T_B$. Conditioning on $T_B<T_A$ instead weights it by one minus that [survivor function](survival-function.md). These are distributions of the latent $T_A$, not the distribution of the observed first event in every case.
