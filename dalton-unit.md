# Dalton (unit)

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic mass](atomic-mass.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dalton_(unit))

The [unified atomic mass unit](dalton-unit.md) and [dalton](dalton-unit.md) are names for the same [mass](mass.md) unit:

$$
1\,\mathrm u=1\,\mathrm{Da}=\frac{m_{\rm a}({}^{12}\mathrm C)}{12},
$$

where the [carbon-12](carbon-12.md) [chemical atom](atom-physics.md) is free, at rest, and in its nuclear and electronic [ground state](ground-state.md). Its conversion to [SI](international-system-of-units.md) [mass](mass.md) units is determined experimentally. See [The International System of Units (SI), ninth edition, SI Brochure](the-international-system-of-units-si-ninth-edition-si-brochure.md) (Table 8, footnote (f), printed p. 146: dalton and unified atomic mass unit).
