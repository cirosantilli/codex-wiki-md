# Orientability

↑ **Parent:** [Topology](topology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orientability)

Orientability is the existence of a consistent choice of orientation across a [manifold](topological-manifold.md). For a [smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md), the transition maps of an oriented atlas have positive Jacobian determinant; for a surface this rules out a Möbius-band neighborhood. The [orientable smooth manifold](orientable-smooth-manifold.md) condition is its differentiable formulation.
