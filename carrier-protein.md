# Carrier protein

↑ **Parent:** [Membrane transport protein](membrane-transport-protein.md)

A membrane [carrier protein](carrier-protein.md) binds a [solute](solute.md) and changes its exposed binding site from one side of a [cell membrane](cell-membrane-split.md) to the other. Depending on its mechanism and coupling, it can mediate [passive transport](passive-transport.md) or [active transport](active-transport.md).
