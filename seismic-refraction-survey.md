# Seismic refraction survey

↑ **Parent:** [Seismology](seismology.md)

An acquisition and interpretation method using [seismic travel times](seismic-travel-time.md), commonly first arrivals, to constrain a [velocity model](velocity-model.md). [Seismic head waves](seismic-head-wave.md) can constrain faster layers, but missing velocity contrasts, hidden thin layers and low-velocity layers limit simple interpretations.
