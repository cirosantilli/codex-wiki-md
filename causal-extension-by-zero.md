# Causal extension by zero

↑ **Parent:** [Laplace transform](laplace-transform.md)

Extend a [function](function-split.md) given for nonnegative time to the [real line](real-line.md) by setting it to zero before the starting time. This turns unilateral [convolution](convolution.md) into whole-line [convolution](convolution.md) and identifies a [Laplace transform](laplace-transform.md) slice with a [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) after [exponential function](exponential-function.md) weighting.
