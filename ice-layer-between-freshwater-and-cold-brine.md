# Ice layer between freshwater and cold brine

↑ **Parent:** [Saline Stefan problem](saline-stefan-problem.md)

In a planar no-flow model with pure [ice](ice.md), a stable cold brine below freshwater can support an [ice](ice.md) layer whose upper [phase boundary](phase-boundary.md) freezes freshwater while its lower boundary melts into diluted brine. [Heat conduction](thermal-conduction.md) and salt [diffusion](diffusion.md) obey different similarity scales. Local [liquidus](liquidus.md) equilibrium, salt conservation and two [Stefan conditions](stefan-condition.md) determine both boundary positions. The heat lost into the brine supports net [ice](ice.md) growth even while some basal [ice](ice.md) melts.
