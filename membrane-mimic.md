# Membrane mimic

↑ **Parent:** [Membrane protein](membrane-protein.md)

An experimental environment designed to shield hydrophobic [protein](protein-split.md) surfaces while retaining relevant [membrane](cell-membrane-split.md) properties. Detergent [micelles](micelle.md), [bicelles](bicelle.md) and [lipid bilayer nanodiscs](lipid-bilayer-nanodisc.md) have different limitations.
