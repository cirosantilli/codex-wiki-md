# Two-exponential-mixture survival probability

↑ **Parent:** [Ruin integro-differential equation](ruin-integro-differential-equation.md)

In the [classical risk model](classical-risk-model.md) with density $3e^{-4x}+e^{-2x}/2$ and [relative safety loading](relative-safety-loading.md) $3/5$, the claim mean is $5/16$ and $\lambda/c=2$. The convolution terms $A_a(u)=\int_0^u\phi(u-x)e^{-ax}\,dx$ satisfy $A_a'=\phi-aA_a$. Applying $(D+4)(D+2)$ to $\phi'=2\phi-6A_4-A_2$ yields $\phi^{(3)}+4\phi''+3\phi'=0$. Its solution is fixed by $\phi(\infty)=1$, $\phi(0)=3/8$ and $\phi'(0)=3/4$, giving the displayed probability. The derivative condition comes from the original integral equation and excludes spurious solutions of the higher-order equation.
