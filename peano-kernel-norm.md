# Peano kernel norm

↑ **Parent:** [Peano kernel](peano-kernel.md)

The [Peano kernel theorem](peano-kernel-theorem.md) writes a derivative-based error functional as $L(u)=\int K(t)u^{(r)}(t)\,dt$, so its bound in the displayed derivative [seminorm](seminorm.md) is at most the [L1 norm](l1-norm.md) of the kernel. This is the sharp constant: continuous derivatives can approximate the sign of $K$ in the weighted integral, and integrating them $r$ times produces admissible test functions. If $K$ has one sign, a constant $r$th [derivative](derivative.md) already attains equality; in particular $\int K=L[x^r/r!]$. This provides a direct exact check on proposed error constants before estimating individual polynomial pieces.
