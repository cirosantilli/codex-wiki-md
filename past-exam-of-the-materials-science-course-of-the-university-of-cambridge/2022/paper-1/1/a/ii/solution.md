**A motif can have fivefold [symmetry](../../../../../../symmetry-physics.md).** For example, repeat an identically oriented regular pentagonal molecular motif at every point of a square translation lattice. Each isolated motif has fivefold [rotational symmetry](../../../../../../rotational-symmetry.md), while the [crystal structure](../../../../../../crystal-structure.md) as a whole does not: rotating about one motif fails to map the other motif centres onto [lattice points](../../../../../../lattice-point.md). The [symmetry](../../../../../../symmetry-physics.md) of an [atomic basis of a crystal](../../../../../../atomic-basis-of-a-crystal.md) need not be a [symmetry](../../../../../../symmetry-physics.md) of its [Bravais lattice](../../../../../../bravais-lattice.md).
