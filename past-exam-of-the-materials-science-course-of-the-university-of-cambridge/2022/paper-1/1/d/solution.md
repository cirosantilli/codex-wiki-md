<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

In the [tetragonal crystal system](../../../../../tetragonal-crystal-system.md), physical squared distances use $a^2(\Delta x^2+\Delta y^2)+c^2\Delta z^2$. Two Ti–O distances from the corner Ti have length $\sqrt2ua$; the other four have length $\sqrt{2a^2(1/2-u)^2+c^2/4}$. Equating both to $r$ gives

$$
a=\frac{r}{\sqrt2u},\qquad c=2r\sqrt{1-\frac{(1/2-u)^2}{u^2}}.
$$

Thus, within the equal-bond model,

$$
\boxed{a=4.596\,\mathrm{\mathring A},\qquad c=2.907\,\mathrm{\mathring A}}.
$$

The [rutile structure](../../../../../rutile-structure.md) remains tetragonal and its octahedral angles remain distorted; these numbers are not a claim about measured ambient-pressure lattice parameters.
