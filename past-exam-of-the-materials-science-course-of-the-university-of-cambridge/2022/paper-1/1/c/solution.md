<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Each Ti has [coordination number](../../../../../coordination-number.md) **six**, with oxygen neighbours forming a distorted octahedron. Each oxygen has [coordination number](../../../../../coordination-number.md) **three**, with titanium neighbours forming a planar triangle. For oxygen at $(u,u,0)$, the nearest Ti positions can be taken as $(0,0,0)$ and $(1/2,1/2,\pm1/2)$; all three lie in the plane $x=y$. The triangle is not generally equilateral. Equal Ti–O bond lengths in the following calculation do not make every angle ideal.
