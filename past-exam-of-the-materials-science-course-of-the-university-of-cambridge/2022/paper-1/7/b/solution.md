<h1 id="7/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use [mass density](../../../../../density.md) $\rho=ZM/(N_Aa^3)$, with $M=74.55\,\mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$. The [rock salt structure](../../../../../rock-salt-structure.md) has $Z=4$ [formula units](../../../../../formula-unit.md) per conventional cell; the [CsCl structure](../../../../../caesium-chloride-structure.md) has $Z=1$. In [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) order the densities are approximately

$$
\boxed{2099,\ 2172,\ 2208,\ 2527,\ 2571,\ 2614\ \mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}}.
$$

The increase across the transition is approximately $14.5\%$, much greater than the smooth compression within either branch. Comparing $a^3$ directly without correcting the different $Z$ would create a fictitious [volume](../../../../../volume.md) discontinuity.
