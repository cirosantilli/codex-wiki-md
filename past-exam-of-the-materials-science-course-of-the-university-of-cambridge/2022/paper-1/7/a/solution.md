A [diamond anvil cell](../../../../../diamond-anvil-cell.md) concentrates [force](../../../../../force-split.md) onto tiny opposing culets, reaching very high [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) with small samples and useful optical access through diamond; its small [volume](../../../../../volume.md), [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) gradients, alignment demands and difficulties with some heating or sample environments can limit experiments. A [multi-anvil press](../../../../../multi-anvil-press.md) supports a much larger sample and is useful for high-temperature synthesis and bulk measurements, but usually reaches lower ultimate pressures and has more restricted optical access. Hydrostaticity, attainable [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) and calibration depend on the actual apparatus and [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) medium; neither design guarantees uniform [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md).
