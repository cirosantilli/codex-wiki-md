<h1 id="7/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Within each cubic phase, increasing [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) decreases $a$, so $d_{hkl}=a/\sqrt{h^2+k^2+l^2}$ decreases and [Bragg's law](../../../../../bragg-s-law.md) moves a given reflection to higher $2\theta$. Near $2.55\,\mathrm{GPa}$, peaks of the [rock salt structure](../../../../../rock-salt-structure.md) disappear as those of the [CsCl structure](../../../../../caesium-chloride-structure.md) appear, potentially with a coexistence interval. The former has an FCC translation selection rule, with $h,k,l$ all even or all odd; mixed-parity reflections vanish kinematically. The latter has primitive-cubic translations and basis factor $f_K+(-1)^{h+k+l}f_{Cl}$, so it has no FCC extinction rule. Odd-sum reflections may nevertheless be weak because the two ionic scattering factors are similar. Peak spacings, relative intensities and the cell [volume](../../../../../volume.md) per formula unit change discontinuously. A simple uniform shift of the entire original pattern cannot describe the structural transition.
