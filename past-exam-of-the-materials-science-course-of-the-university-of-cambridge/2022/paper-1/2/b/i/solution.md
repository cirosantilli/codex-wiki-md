<h1 id="2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

There are four cation sites per conventional [unit cell](../../../../../../unit-cell.md), hence $0.4$ Gd substitutions per cell on average. Two substitutions of Gd$^{3+}$ for Ce$^{4+}$ require one [oxygen vacancy](../../../../../../oxygen-vacancy.md) for [charge neutrality](../../../../../../charge-neutrality.md), so the cell contains $0.2$ vacancies. Equivalently, the composition is $\mathrm{Ce_{0.9}Gd_{0.1}O_{1.95}}$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{n_v=\frac{0.2}{a^3}=3.461\times10^{27}\,\mathrm{m^{-3}}}.
$$

This uses the supplied lattice parameter literally and treats it as the conventional fluorite-cell edge.
