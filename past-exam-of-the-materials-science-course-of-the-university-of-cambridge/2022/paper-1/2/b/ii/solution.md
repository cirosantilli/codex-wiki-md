<h1 id="2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

An [oxygen vacancy](../../../../../../oxygen-vacancy.md) has effective carrier charge $q_v=+2e$. Combining its density with its own [diffusion coefficient](../../../../../../diffusion-coefficient.md) in the [Nernst-Einstein relation](../../../../../../nernst-einstein-relation.md) gives

$$
\sigma_{\rm ion}=\frac{n_v(2e)^2D_v}{k_BT},\qquad
\boxed{\sigma_{\rm ion}=2.00\,\mathrm{S\,m^{-1}}}
$$

at $T=873.15\,\mathrm K$. The oxygen ions move oppositely to the vacancies and carry the same conventional current. This estimate assumes independently migrating vacancies, negligible correlation corrections, and negligible electronic contribution to the reported [ionic conductivity](../../../../../../ionic-conductivity.md).
