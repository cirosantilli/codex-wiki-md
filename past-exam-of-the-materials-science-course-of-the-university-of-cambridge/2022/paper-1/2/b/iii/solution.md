<h1 id="2/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Write the [Arrhenius equation](../../../../../../arrhenius-equation.md) as $D=D_0e^{-Q/(k_BT)}$. Eliminating the prefactor with two temperatures gives

$$
Q=\frac{k_B\ln(D_{600}/D_{300})}{1/T_{300}-1/T_{600}}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\boxed{Q=0.795\,\mathrm{eV}=76.7\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}}.
$$

Use absolute temperatures $573.15$ and $873.15\,\mathrm K$. Because the input is a vacancy [diffusion coefficient](../../../../../../diffusion-coefficient.md), this is a migration [activation energy](../../../../../../activation-energy.md) inferred from that diffusivity; an additional vacancy-formation energy should not automatically be added.
