<h1 id="3/b/i/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

For mixed parity, one of the three pair sums is even and two are odd. Thus their phase factors sum to $-1$, giving

$$
\boxed{F_{hkl}=f_{\rm Sc}-f_{\rm Al}}.
$$

These [superlattice reflections](../../../../../../../superlattice-reflection.md) reveal chemical ordering. Their kinematic intensity is proportional to $|f_{\rm Sc}-f_{\rm Al}|^2$, and vanishes in the hypothetical limit of equal species scattering factors.
