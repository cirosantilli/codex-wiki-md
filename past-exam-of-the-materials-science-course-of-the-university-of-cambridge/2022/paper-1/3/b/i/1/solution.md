For the [L12 structure](../../../../../../../l12-structure.md), the [crystal basis structure factor](../../../../../../../crystal-basis-structure-factor.md) is

$$
F_{hkl}=f_{\rm Sc}+f_{\rm Al}\left[e^{i\pi(h+k)}+e^{i\pi(h+l)}+e^{i\pi(k+l)}\right].
$$

Equal parity of $h,k,l$ makes all three phase factors $+1$, hence

$$
\boxed{F_{hkl}=f_{\rm Sc}+3f_{\rm Al}}.
$$

The [electron](../../../../../../../electron.md) scattering factors are evaluated at the reflection's scattering vector.
