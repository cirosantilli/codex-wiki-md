↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The cosine of the angle between [Burgers vector](../../../../../../burgers-vector.md) and line direction is $1/3$, so the angle is $70.53^\circ$: **the [dislocation](../../../../../../dislocation.md) is mixed**. Both vectors must lie in its [dislocation glide](../../../../../../dislocation-glide.md) plane. Their [cross product](../../../../../../cross-product.md) is proportional to

$$
(1,-1,1)\times(1,1,1)=(-2,0,2),
$$

so the plane is **$(10\bar1)$**, a member of $\{110\}$. The screw component magnitude is $a/(2\sqrt3)$ and the edge component magnitude is $a\sqrt{2/3}$.
