<h1 id="5/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

At small load the deformation is elastic: $\varepsilon_{\parallel}=\sigma/E$, and, using isotropic elasticity with $E=2G(1+\nu)$, $\nu=0.30$ gives lateral contraction $\varepsilon_\perp=-0.30\varepsilon_\parallel$. At maximum load,

$$
\boxed{\sigma_{\max}=\frac{12.6\,\mathrm{kN}}{\pi(2\,\mathrm{mm})^2}=1.003\,\mathrm{GPa}}.
$$

For the specified [slip system](../../../../../../slip-system.md), the tensile direction is proportional to $(-2,-3,5)$, the slip direction to $(1,-1,1)$ and the plane normal to $(1,0,-1)$. Hence the [Schmid factor](../../../../../../schmid-factor.md) is

$$
m=\frac{|6|}{\sqrt{38}\sqrt3}\frac{|-7|}{\sqrt{38}\sqrt2}
=0.4512,\qquad \boxed{|\tau_{\max}|=0.452\,\mathrm{GPa}}.
$$

[Schmid's law](../../../../../../schmid-s-law.md) predicts glide when this reaches the [critical resolved shear stress](../../../../../../critical-resolved-shear-stress.md). Before that, unloading reverses the [strain](../../../../../../strain.md); after activation, glide produces permanent [strain](../../../../../../strain.md) and may generate [work hardening](../../../../../../work-hardening.md) and additional slip. **The supplied data do not fix the yield load.** Neither the adjective “ultra-strong” nor $E/G$ supplies a critical shear [stress](../../../../../../stress.md) or an absolute modulus. Other [slip systems](../../../../../../slip-system.md) must also be checked before claiming which activates first. In particular, the specimen remains elastic through the maximum load if every relevant system stays below its activation threshold.
