<h1 id="6/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Check stiffness and tip deflection using [Young's modulus](../../../../../young-s-modulus.md), failure from existing cracks using [fracture toughness](../../../../../fracture-toughness.md) or the supplied critical energy-release rate, local crushing at supports, shear failure, buckling, fatigue and joint design. In a simple linear-elastic crack model, $K_c^2\simeq E'G_c$, so [toughness](../../../../../toughness.md) cannot be inferred from strength alone. Wood also has grain-direction dependence, moisture sensitivity, natural defects, property scatter and time-dependent creep; the design must control grain alignment and compression-side stability. Metals raise issues of corrosion, [mass](../../../../../mass.md), joining, machining, residual [stress](../../../../../stress.md) and heat-treatment state. Cost, availability, reproducibility, service [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) and end-of-life treatment can decide between otherwise feasible designs. A high merit index is a screening result, not a complete safety specification.
