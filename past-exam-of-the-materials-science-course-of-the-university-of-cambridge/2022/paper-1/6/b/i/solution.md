<h1 id="6/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The rectangular [second moment of area](../../../../../../second-moment-of-area.md) is $I=wh^3/12$. The maximum bending [stress](../../../../../../stress.md) in the [cantilever](../../../../../../cantilever.md) is $FL(h/2)/I=6FL/(wh^2)$. If $h$ is fixed, choose the minimum allowed width $w=6FL/(\sigma_fh^2)$. Its [mass](../../../../../../mass.md) is then

$$
m_A=\rho Lwh=\frac{6FL^2}{h}\frac{\rho}{\sigma_f}.
$$

Thus the [material performance index](../../../../../../material-performance-index.md) to maximize is

$$
\boxed{M_A=\frac{\sigma_f}{\rho}}.
$$

For unequal tensile and compressive strengths, use the smaller permissible [stress](../../../../../../stress.md) in this symmetric section. The derivation assumes bending-strength control, not a separate deflection, shear, buckling or existing-crack constraint.
