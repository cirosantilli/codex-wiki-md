<h1 id="6/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

With $w$ fixed, bending strength requires $h=\sqrt{6FL/(w\sigma_f)}$. Substitution into the [cantilever](../../../../../../cantilever.md) [mass](../../../../../../mass.md) gives

$$
m_B=L\sqrt{6FLw}\frac{\rho}{\sqrt{\sigma_f}}.
$$

The [material performance index](../../../../../../material-performance-index.md) to maximize is therefore

$$
\boxed{M_B=\frac{\sqrt{\sigma_f}}{\rho}}.
$$

Changing the free geometric variable changes the strength exponent. A stiffness-limited problem would instead involve [Young's modulus](../../../../../../young-s-modulus.md); the supplied modulus does not enter this strength-only index.
