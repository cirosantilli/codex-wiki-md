↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The fixed support supplies the end shear [force](../../../../../force-split.md) and the largest bending moment, $FL$. In elementary beam bending, the greatest longitudinal tensile and compressive [stresses](../../../../../stress.md) lie on opposite outer faces of the section at the **fixed end**, with zero longitudinal bending [stress](../../../../../stress.md) on the neutral axis. For a downward tip load on a horizontal [cantilever](../../../../../cantilever.md), the upper face is in tension and the lower face in compression. Transverse shear [stress](../../../../../stress.md) has a different distribution, largest near the neutral axis for a rectangular section; “highest [force](../../../../../force-split.md)” should therefore be interpreted with the [stress](../../../../../stress.md) component specified.
