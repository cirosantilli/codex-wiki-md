↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

At $227^\circ\mathrm C\simeq500\,\mathrm K$, $1000/T\simeq2$. Reading the [carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) line gives $D_C\sim2\times10^{-15}\,\mathrm{m^2s^{-1}}$ to graphical accuracy. With $t=60\,\mathrm s$, the characteristic [diffusion length](../../../../../diffusion-length.md) is

$$
\boxed{\sqrt{Dt}\sim0.35\,\mathrm{\mu m}}.
$$

If “distance” means a one-coordinate root-mean-square displacement, use $\sqrt{2Dt}\sim0.49\,\mathrm{\mu m}$; the three-dimensional radial root-mean-square is $\sqrt{6Dt}\sim0.85\,\mathrm{\mu m}$. The graph does not justify precision between these conventions. Equal [diffusion](../../../../../diffusion.md) distance in equal time requires equal [diffusion coefficients](../../../../../diffusion-coefficient.md). The [copper](../../../../../copper.md) line reaches this diffusivity around $1000/T\simeq0.96$, giving **$T_{\rm Cu}\sim1.0\times10^3\,\mathrm K$, about $770^\circ\mathrm C$**. [Carbon](../../../../../carbon.md) moves interstitially through [ferrite](../../../../../ferrite.md), whereas [copper](../../../../../copper.md) self-diffusion is substitutional and vacancy-mediated, with a much higher effective activation barrier.
