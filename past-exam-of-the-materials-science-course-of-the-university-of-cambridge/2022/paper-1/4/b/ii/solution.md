<h1 id="4/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

As-quenched [martensite](../../../../../../martensite.md) generally has greater [hardness](../../../../../../hardness.md) and strength, but lower ductility and [toughness](../../../../../../toughness.md), than ordinary pearlitic steel. Its supersaturated [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) and distorted lattice impede [dislocation glide](../../../../../../dislocation-glide.md); quench [stresses](../../../../../../stress.md) also promote cracking. [Pearlite](../../../../../../pearlite.md) combines hard [cementite](../../../../../../cementite.md) lamellae with more deformable [ferrite](../../../../../../ferrite.md), with finer spacings generally giving greater strength. **Austenitize, quench to [martensite](../../../../../../martensite.md), then temper** to obtain a useful compromise between indentation resistance and resistance to fast fracture. Tempering below the austenitizing range relieves [stresses](../../../../../../stress.md) and develops a fine carbide-containing microstructure. It trades some maximum [hardness](../../../../../../hardness.md) for improved [toughness](../../../../../../toughness.md); the [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) and time must suit the required balance.
