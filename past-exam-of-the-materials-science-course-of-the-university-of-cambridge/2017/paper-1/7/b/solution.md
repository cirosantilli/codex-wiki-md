<h1 id="7/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

At turbine operating [temperatures](../../../../../temperature-split.md), [grain boundaries](../../../../../grain-boundary.md) provide paths for rapid [diffusion](../../../../../diffusion.md), [grain-boundary sliding](../../../../../grain-boundary-sliding.md) and cavity formation, encouraging [creep](../../../../../creep-deformation.md) rupture. A [single-crystal turbine blade](../../../../../single-crystal-turbine-blade.md) removes these boundaries, especially boundaries unfavourably oriented across the tensile load. It retains [dislocations](../../../../../dislocation.md) and can still [creep](../../../../../creep-deformation.md); eliminating boundaries is useful in this high-temperature regime even though fine [crystal grains](../../../../../grain-in-a-polycrystal.md) can strengthen other materials at lower [temperatures](../../../../../temperature-split.md).

Use [directional solidification](../../../../../directional-solidification.md) of the cast alloy through a controlled thermal gradient. A suitably oriented seed or a narrow [grain selector](../../../../../grain-selector.md) lets one crystal survive and grow into the blade mould while suppressing competing [nucleation](../../../../../nucleation.md). A spiral selector is a common way to exclude competing [crystal grains](../../../../../grain-in-a-polycrystal.md). The blade is generally oriented near a $\langle001\rangle$ axis for the intended [anisotropic](../../../../../anisotropy.md) properties. [crystal grain](../../../../../grain-in-a-polycrystal.md) selection is experimentally verified by orientation mapping, rather than assuming a directionally elongated casting is automatically a [single crystal](../../../../../single-crystal.md).[Sections 1–3, directional casting, selector geometry and electron-backscatter diffraction of surviving grains](../../../../../grain-selection-in-a-high-efficiency-2d-grain-selector-during-casting-of-single-crystal-superalloys-by-xintao-zhu-and-others.md)
