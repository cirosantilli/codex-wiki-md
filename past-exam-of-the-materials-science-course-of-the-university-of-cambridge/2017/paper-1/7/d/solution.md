<h1 id="7/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The main strengthening architecture is a high [volume fraction](../../../../../volume-fraction.md) of fine, thermally stable ordered [gamma prime phase](../../../../../gamma-prime-phase.md) precipitates in the ductile nickel-rich matrix. Their nearly matching crystal parameters allow [coherent crystal interfaces](../../../../../coherent-crystal-interface.md), but coherence does not make them transparent to a moving [dislocation](../../../../../dislocation.md): cutting disrupts chemical order and costs antiphase-boundary energy. Pairing of cutting [dislocations](../../../../../dislocation.md) and their interactions with narrow matrix channels raise the [stress](../../../../../stress.md) needed for deformation. For particles that are bypassed, bowing and high-temperature climb are also impeded by the precipitate spacing and interface [dislocation](../../../../../dislocation.md) structures.

[coherency strain](../../../../../coherency-strain.md) and differences in elastic response provide additional obstacles. Refractory substitutional solutes contribute [solid-solution strengthening](../../../../../solid-solution-strengthening.md) and can slow diffusion-controlled deformation. Single-crystal manufacture suppresses grain-boundary [creep](../../../../../creep-deformation.md) and [grain-boundary cavitation](../../../../../grain-boundary-cavitation.md). Together these mechanisms permit useful strength at [temperatures](../../../../../temperature-split.md) where an unstrengthened [nickel](../../../../../nickel.md) matrix would deform much faster. Protective oxide scales and coatings preserve load-bearing material against environmental attack; they complement, rather than replace, the internal strengthening mechanisms.

A concrete microscopic observation is the paired [dislocation](../../../../../dislocation.md) loops around fine precipitates in the Cambridge C263 [creep](../../../../../creep-deformation.md) micrograph: the observed defect structure supports the importance of precipitate–[dislocation](../../../../../dislocation.md) interactions, though one image does not by itself separate every strengthening contribution.[creep-tested C263 micrograph and accompanying paired-loop interpretation](../../../../../nickel-based-superalloy-dislocation-structure-by-h-k-d-h-bhadeshia.md) In-situ diffraction during stress-jump tests of a rafted AM1 [single crystal](../../../../../single-crystal.md) also resolves evolving load partition and deformation in matrix channels, demonstrating why channel width and interface interactions matter.[abstract, Sections 2–4: transmission diffraction, loading transients and channel-deformation analysis](../../../../../dislocation-densities-and-velocities-within-the-gamma-channels-of-an-sx-superalloy-during-in-situ-high-temperature-creep-tests-by-t-schenk-and-others.md) Long exposure can coarsen or change the [precipitate morphology](../../../../../precipitate-morphology.md) and alter channel widths, so high strength is conditional on [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), time and microstructural stability.
