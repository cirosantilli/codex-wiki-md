<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Each [calcium](../../../../../calcium.md) [cation](../../../../../cation.md) has eight [fluoride](../../../../../fluoride.md) neighbours at the vertices of a [cube](../../../../../cube.md): [cubic coordination](../../../../../cubic-coordination.md). Each [fluoride](../../../../../fluoride.md) [anion](../../../../../anion.md) has four [calcium](../../../../../calcium.md) neighbours in [tetrahedral coordination](../../../../../tetrahedral-coordination.md). Both unlike-neighbour [Euclidean distances](../../../../../euclidean-distance.md) are $\sqrt3a/4$, with $a$ the conventional cubic [lattice parameter](../../../../../lattice-constant.md).

Apply the [Goldschmidt packing principle](../../../../../goldschmidt-packing-principle.md) at its limiting eight-coordinate geometry: the eight surrounding [anions](../../../../../anion.md) just touch one another and also touch the central [cation](../../../../../cation.md). The [anions](../../../../../anion.md) form a [cube](../../../../../cube.md) of edge $a/2$, so $2r_a=a/2$ and $r_a+r_c=\sqrt3a/4$. Eliminating $a$ gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{r_a}{r_c}=\frac1{\sqrt3-1}=\frac{\sqrt3+1}{2}\simeq1.366.}
$$

Equivalently, $r_c/r_a=0.732$. This is a limiting [radius ratio rule](../../../../../radius-ratio-rule.md), rather than a measured ionic-radius ratio. It does not assume that the [calcium](../../../../../calcium.md) sublattice consists of mutually touching [spheres](../../../../../sphere.md): the [calcium](../../../../../calcium.md) positions have face-centred cubic symmetry, but that symmetry alone does not specify which [ions](../../../../../ion.md) touch. A different construction with touching [calcium](../../../../../calcium.md) [spheres](../../../../../sphere.md) and [fluoride](../../../../../fluoride.md) just filling their [tetrahedral interstitial sites](../../../../../tetrahedral-interstitial-site.md) would instead give $r_a/r_c=\sqrt{3/2}-1=0.225$; that is the opposite contact limit, not the eight-coordinate anion-contact ideal used here.
