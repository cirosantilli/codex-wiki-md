<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Using the contact model of (b), $r_a=a/4$ and $r_c=(\sqrt3-1)a/4$. Divide the [volume](../../../../../volume.md) of four [cation](../../../../../cation.md) [spheres](../../../../../sphere.md) and eight [anion](../../../../../anion.md) [spheres](../../../../../sphere.md) by $a^3$ to obtain the [packing fraction](../../../../../packing-fraction.md):

$$
\eta=\frac{4\pi}{3a^3}(4r_c^3+8r_a^3)=\frac\pi{12}\left[(\sqrt3-1)^3+2\right]=\boxed{\frac\pi6(3\sqrt3-4)\simeq0.6263.}
$$

For one-radius [cubic close-packed structure](../../../../../cubic-close-packed-structure.md), face-diagonal contact gives $4r=\sqrt2a$, hence $\eta_{\rm ccp}=4(4\pi r^3/3)/a^3=\pi/(3\sqrt2)\simeq0.7405$. The limiting fluorite value is **lower than the one-radius close-packed value**. Its two sizes, stoichiometry and eight-to-four coordination impose a different geometry: the [fluoride](../../../../../fluoride.md) sites form a simple-cubic array, and the [calcium](../../../../../calcium.md) sites are too far apart for [calcium](../../../../../calcium.md)–[calcium](../../../../../calcium.md) contact. A face-centred arrangement of one species is therefore not sufficient to infer a close-packed whole crystal.
