<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [fluorite structure](../../../../../fluorite-structure.md) consists of a [face-centered cubic lattice](../../../../../face-centered-cubic-lattice.md) of [calcium](../../../../../calcium.md) sites with all eight [tetrahedral interstitial sites](../../../../../tetrahedral-interstitial-site.md) occupied by [fluoride](../../../../../fluoride.md). In fractional coordinates of the conventional cubic [unit cell](../../../../../unit-cell.md), the [calcium](../../../../../calcium.md) basis is $(0,0,0),(0,\tfrac12,\tfrac12),(\tfrac12,0,\tfrac12),(\tfrac12,\tfrac12,0)$, and [fluoride](../../../../../fluoride.md) occupies all combinations $(x,y,z)$ with $x,y,z\in\{\tfrac14,\tfrac34\}$. The projection therefore superposes two [fluoride](../../../../../fluoride.md) sites at each quarter-coordinate position. Labels in the original diagram give fractional depth along $[001]$; periodic boundary sites are displayed on both edges.

![](../../../../../past-exam-of-the-materials-science-course-of-the-university-of-cambridge/2017/paper-1-fluorite.png)

**[Figure 1](#1/a/image-original-fluorite-projection-along-the-cubic-z-axis-with-fractional-depths-and-the-limiting-anion-contact-geometry). Original fluorite projection along the cubic z axis with fractional depths and the limiting anion-contact geometry**.

Counting shared sites gives $8/8+6/2=4$ [calcium](../../../../../calcium.md) sites and eight interior [fluoride](../../../../../fluoride.md) sites: **four [formula units](../../../../../formula-unit.md) per conventional cell**.
