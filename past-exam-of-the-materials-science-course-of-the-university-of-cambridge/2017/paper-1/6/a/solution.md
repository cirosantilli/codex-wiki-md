<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Make the implicit fixed-crack-geometry assumption explicit by writing $\sigma_f=C\sqrt{EG_c}$, where $C$ is common to the compared specimens and includes the crack-size dependence needed for dimensional consistency. [linear elasticity](../../../../../linear-elasticity.md) gives

$$
\varepsilon_f=\frac{\sigma_f}{E}=C\sqrt{\frac{G_c}{E}}.
$$

The appropriate [materials merit index](../../../../../material-performance-index.md) is therefore **$G_c/E$**, or its monotonically equivalent square root. This comparison holds crack geometry and the brittle-failure model fixed; changing crack sizes or yielding before fracture changes the selection problem.
