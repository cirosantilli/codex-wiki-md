<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For identical thicknesses, compare $d/\kappa$ using the supplied coefficients:

$$
\frac d\kappa=0.02667,\quad0.2105,\quad0.6053\quad\mathrm{pC\,N^{-1}}
$$

for [barium titanate](../../../../../barium-titanate.md), [lead zirconate titanate](../../../../../lead-zirconate-titanate.md) and [quartz](../../../../../quartz.md) respectively. Thus **[barium titanate](../../../../../barium-titanate.md), then [lead zirconate titanate](../../../../../lead-zirconate-titanate.md), then [quartz](../../../../../quartz.md)** is the increasing [voltage sensitivity of a piezoelectric sensor](../../../../../voltage-sensitivity-of-a-piezoelectric-sensor.md) order. A large charge coefficient alone does not guarantee a large voltage: the large [permittivity](../../../../../permittivity-split.md) of a ferroelectric also increases its [capacitance](../../../../../capacitance.md).
