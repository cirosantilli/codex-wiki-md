The sensor must survive the [stress](../../../../../stress.md) range and repeated loading without fracture, fatigue or excessive mechanical compliance. The [tensor](../../../../../tensor.md) character of the [piezoelectric coefficient](../../../../../piezoelectric-coefficient.md) makes [crystal orientation](../../../../../crystal-orientation.md) important; a ceramic's [poling](../../../../../ferroelectric-poling.md) quality and domain stability also matter. Compare linearity, [hysteresis](../../../../../hysteresis.md), reproducibility, aging and the usable frequency bandwidth, including [resonance](../../../../../resonance.md) and dynamic response.

Electrical design is equally important: leakage resistance and readout input impedance determine low-frequency loss, and cable/input [capacitance](../../../../../capacitance.md) loads a voltage output. A [charge amplifier](../../../../../charge-amplifier.md) may favour a different material ranking from a passive open-circuit voltage measurement. [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) changes can alter the coefficients, produce a pyroelectric signal in a polar material and depole a ferroelectric near its transition. Humidity, [electrode](../../../../../electrode.md) bonding, electrical breakdown, manufacturability, cost and environmental constraints such as lead content also affect a practical choice. Calibration should separate applied [stress](../../../../../stress.md) from [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) drift and readout loading.
