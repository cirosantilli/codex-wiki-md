<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Take the longitudinal scalar [piezoelectric coefficient](../../../../../piezoelectric-coefficient.md) appropriate to the load and [electrode](../../../../../electrode.md) direction. For a uniformly stressed slab of face [area](../../../../../surface-area.md) $A$ and thickness $t$, the source charge is $\Delta Q=d\Delta F=dA\Delta\sigma$. Its [capacitance](../../../../../capacitance.md) is $C=\epsilon_0\kappa A/t$, so the ideal initially uncharged, electrically isolated sensor gives

$$
\boxed{\left|\frac{\Delta V}{\Delta\sigma}\right|=\frac{dt}{\epsilon_0\kappa}.}
$$

The sign depends on the [poling](../../../../../ferroelectric-poling.md) direction and [electrode](../../../../../electrode.md) convention. Equivalently, the incremental constitutive law is $\Delta D=d\Delta\sigma+\epsilon_0\kappa\Delta E$; an ideal [open circuit](../../../../../open-circuit.md) keeps free [electrode](../../../../../electrode.md) charge, hence $D$, fixed and gives $\Delta E=-d\Delta\sigma/(\epsilon_0\kappa)$. [area](../../../../../surface-area.md) cancels because both generated charge and [capacitance](../../../../../capacitance.md) scale with [area](../../../../../surface-area.md). Thickness does not cancel: the material comparison is through $d/\kappa$ only when geometry is fixed. Here $\kappa$ must be the [relative permittivity](../../../../../relative-permittivity.md) under the relevant mechanical and frequency conditions.
