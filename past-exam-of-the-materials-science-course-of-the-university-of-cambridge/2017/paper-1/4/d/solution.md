<h1 id="4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The disc [volume](../../../../../volume.md) is $\pi d^2h/4$, the combined [area](../../../../../surface-area.md) of its two flat faces is $\pi d^2/2$, and its cylindrical edge [area](../../../../../surface-area.md) is $\pi dh$. With the signed bulk driving term $\Delta G$ per [volume](../../../../../volume.md),

$$
\boxed{W_n=\frac\pi4d^2h\Delta G+\frac\pi2d^2\gamma_f+\pi dh\gamma_e.}
$$

For favourable transformation $\Delta G$ is negative and the interfacial terms are positive. This [classical nucleation theory](../../../../../classical-nucleation-theory.md) expression neglects a separate elastic term; if [coherency strain](../../../../../coherency-strain.md) is important, its appropriate energy must be added or included explicitly in the effective [volume](../../../../../volume.md) term. $W_n$ has energy units, whereas $\Delta G$ here has energy-per-volume units.
