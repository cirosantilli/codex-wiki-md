The basal close-packed planes of a [hexagonal close-packed structure](../../../../../../hexagonal-close-packed-structure.md) are **$\{0001\}$** in four-index [Miller-Bravais index](../../../../../../miller-bravais-index.md), or $\{001\}$ with three hexagonal indices. Their stacking is **$ABAB\ldots$**: alternate layers return above the same lateral positions.
