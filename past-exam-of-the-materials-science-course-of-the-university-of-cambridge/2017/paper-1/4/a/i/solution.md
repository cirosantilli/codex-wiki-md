The close-packed planes of a [cubic close-packed structure](../../../../../../cubic-close-packed-structure.md) are **$\{111\}$**, with **$ABCABC\ldots$** stacking. Each layer is a triangular array; the third layer occupies the remaining set of hollows rather than returning above the first.
