<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Measure ring centers and [radii](../../../../../radius.md) against the printed scale, rather than comparing raw squared [radii](../../../../../radius.md). A representative reading of the four rings is $R\simeq6.0,7.0,10.6,13.2\,\mathrm{mm}$, with broad photographic lines. For a flat plate at distance $L$,

$$
2\theta=\arctan(R/L),\qquad
\sin^2\theta=\frac12\left(1-\frac{L}{\sqrt{L^2+R^2}}\right).
$$

With $L=16\,\mathrm{mm}$ the $\sin^2\theta$ ratios are approximately $1:1.33:2.67:3.67$, giving

$$
\boxed{111,\quad200,\quad220,\quad311;\qquad\text{face-centered cubic lattice}.}
$$

For identical atoms at the four conventional FCC positions,

$$
F_{hkl}=f\{1+(-1)^{h+k}+(-1)^{h+l}+(-1)^{k+l}\}.
$$

The [structure factor](../../../../../structure-factor.md) is $4f$ for indices all even or all odd, and zero for mixed parity. Thus, for example, 100, 110 and 210 are [systematic absences](../../../../../systematic-absence.md). The allowed squared-index sums begin $3,4,8,11,12,16,\ldots$. Only the first four rings fit within the displayed field; 222 and later allowed reflections are not forbidden merely because they are outside the image. The central shadow and its holder are not extra [diffraction](../../../../../diffraction.md) peaks.
