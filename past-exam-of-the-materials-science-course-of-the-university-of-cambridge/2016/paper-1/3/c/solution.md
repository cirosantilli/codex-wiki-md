<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For any indexed cubic reflection,

$$
a=\frac{\lambda\sqrt{h^2+k^2+l^2}}{2\sin[\tfrac12\arctan(R/L)]}.
$$

Use $\lambda_{\rm Mo}\simeq0.07107\,\mathrm{nm}$ for the unresolved [molybdenum](../../../../../molybdenum.md) $K_\alpha$ doublet; the tabulated components are approximately $0.0709300$ and $0.0713590\,\mathrm{nm}$. These are external spectral constants, not an assertion about the unavailable historical Data Book.[Table V, molybdenum entries; physical PDF page19](../../../../../x-ray-wavelengths-and-x-ray-atomic-energy-levels-by-j-a-bearden.md)

Reading broad ring centers in the supplied image more closely gives representative [radii](../../../../../radius.md) $5.96,6.99,10.63,13.23\,\mathrm{mm}$. Their corresponding estimates are about $0.3471,0.3473,0.3478,0.3480\,\mathrm{nm}$. Their small scatter understates possible common scale and camera-distance errors. In particular,

$$
\left|\frac{\delta a}{a}\right|\simeq
\frac{\cot\theta}{2[1+(R/L)^2]}
\left|\frac{\delta R}{L}-\frac{R\,\delta L}{L^2}\right|
$$

for a first-order signed perturbation; independent uncertainties are combined in quadrature instead. A reading uncertainty of order $0.2\,\mathrm{mm}$, plus the printed-scale and setup uncertainty, justifies a deliberately modest precision:

$$
\boxed{a\simeq0.35\pm0.01\,\mathrm{nm}.}
$$

**[Nickel](../../../../../nickel.md) is a plausible identification**, since it is FCC with room-temperature edge about $0.352\,\mathrm{nm}$.[Abstract, pure-nickel lattice parameter at25°C](../../../../../thermal-expansion-of-nickel-and-iron-and-the-influence-of-nitrogen-on-the-lattice-parameter-of-iron-at-the-curie-temperature-by-jinn-wen-hwang.md) The source photograph alone does not chemically identify the specimen; nearby elemental lattice constants and the scale uncertainty preclude a rigorous unique assignment. The estimate is not reported with artificial precision inherited from pixel fitting.
