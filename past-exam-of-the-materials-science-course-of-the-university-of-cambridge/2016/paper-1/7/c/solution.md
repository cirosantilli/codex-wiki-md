<h1 id="7/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

At height $z$ above the free lower end, a uniform vertical filament carries the weight of length $z$. With cross-sectional area $A$,

$$
F(z)=\rho A g z,\qquad\sigma(z)=\rho g z,
\qquad\boxed{\sigma_{\max}=\rho g L.}
$$

**The maximum [stress](../../../../../stress.md) is at the top and is independent of diameter.** The diameter cancels because both [mass](../../../../../mass.md) and load-bearing area scale with $A$. Neglecting self-heating gradients, evaporation and initial fabrication [stress](../../../../../stress.md), this top point sets the first creep-strain limit.

For a clearly labelled external reference estimate, take $\rho=1.93\times10^4\,\mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$. Then $\sigma_{\max}=1.89\times10^4\,\mathrm{Pa}=0.0189\,\mathrm{MPa}$; the area is $3.14\times10^{-8}\,\mathrm{m^2}$ and the total suspended [mass](../../../../../mass.md) is about $6.06\times10^{-5}\,\mathrm{kg}$. The [density](../../../../../density.md) is independently tabulated, not invented as a supplied 2016 value.[2009 table, density entry](../../../../../atomic-and-nuclear-properties-of-tungsten-by-the-particle-data-group.md)

Use the original map's top [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) axis at $2500^\circ\mathrm C$ and its **right-hand tensile-stress axis**, so no uncertain shear-stress conversion or additional [shear modulus](../../../../../shear-modulus.md) is required. The point lies in C, between the $10^{-9}$ and $10^{-8}\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$ contours, nearer $10^{-8}$. A logarithmic interpolation of the scanned contours gives about $6\times10^{-9}\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$, with only graphical precision. Thus

$$
t_f\simeq\frac{0.05}{\dot\varepsilon_{\max}}
\simeq8\times10^6\,\mathrm s
\simeq2.3\times10^3\,\mathrm h,
$$

or **of order three months in this steady-rate, reference-density map estimate**. Reading only the bracketing contours gives the appropriately broad interval $5\times10^6$ to $5\times10^7\,\mathrm s$; the graph does not support an exact service-life number.

For the exact prescribed input, the answer remains

$$
\boxed{t_f\simeq\frac{0.05}{\dot\varepsilon(2500^\circ\mathrm C,\rho gL)}}.
$$

This estimate uses the cited reference [density](../../../../../density.md) and the grain-size assumptions of the supplied map. [Thermal expansion](../../../../../thermal-expansion.md) lowers [density](../../../../../density.md), and an evolved area or tertiary [material creep](../../../../../creep-deformation.md) changes the rate; a steady initial-rate estimate is not a guaranteed lifetime. In particular, a different filament [grain size](../../../../../grain-size.md) could require a different map. Other failure mechanisms could shorten useful life.
