<h1 id="7/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

**Lower the operating [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) or [stress](../../../../../stress.md), suppress [diffusion](../../../../../diffusion.md), and remove or stabilize the paths that accommodate [material creep](../../../../../creep-deformation.md).** Increasing the melting [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) can reduce $T/T_m$ at a specified operating [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md). Chemistry that reduces diffusivity or impedes [dislocation climb](../../../../../dislocation-climb.md) can reduce the [material creep](../../../../../creep-deformation.md) rate; stable precipitates or dispersoids can resist glide and climb, provided they do not dissolve or coarsen rapidly in service.

Large grains reduce both Nabarro-Herring and [Coble creep](../../../../../coble-creep.md), according to their $d^{-2}$ and $d^{-3}$ dependencies. Directional [solidification](../../../../../freezing.md) can reduce boundaries normal to the dominant tensile [stress](../../../../../stress.md), while a single crystal removes grain-boundary transport and sliding from the ordinary polycrystal mechanism inventory. It does not remove bulk [diffusion](../../../../../diffusion.md) or [dislocation](../../../../../dislocation.md) [material creep](../../../../../creep-deformation.md).

This is why room-temperature grain-refinement strengthening is not automatically a high-temperature [material creep](../../../../../creep-deformation.md) strategy: fine grains create more boundary transport paths. Any protective [microstructure](../../../../../microstructure.md) must remain stable at the service [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), and the design must also avoid [stress](../../../../../stress.md) concentrations and unwanted thermal loading.
