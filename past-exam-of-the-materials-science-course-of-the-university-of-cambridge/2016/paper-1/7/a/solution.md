<h1 id="7/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

**[Material creep](../../../../../creep-deformation.md) is time-dependent permanent deformation under sustained [stress](../../../../../stress.md).** Its rate is often appreciable at a large [homologous temperature](../../../../../homologous-temperature.md) $T/T_m$, because [diffusion](../../../../../diffusion.md) and obstacle-escape processes become rapid. In an activated regime a useful form is

$$
\dot\varepsilon=A\sigma^n d^{-p}\exp[-Q_c/(RT)],
$$

with additional elastic, geometric and [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) prefactors absorbed into $A$ for the chosen approximation. The [activation energy](../../../../../activation-energy.md) belongs to the rate-controlling process: bulk [diffusion](../../../../../diffusion.md), grain-boundary [diffusion](../../../../../diffusion.md) and [dislocation](../../../../../dislocation.md) motion need not have the same value.

- **A: power-law [dislocation](../../../../../dislocation.md) [material creep](../../../../../creep-deformation.md).** [Dislocations](../../../../../dislocation.md) glide and climb around obstacles; vacancy transport enables climb and recovery. The rate is strongly stress-dependent, typically with $n$ several times greater than one. It dominates the higher-stress, hot field below the glide-strength boundary.
- **B: [Nabarro-Herring creep](../../../../../nabarro-herring-creep.md).** Stress-directed [vacancy diffusion](../../../../../vacancy-diffusion.md) through the grain interiors transfers material between differently stressed boundaries, extending grains along tension. In the simple equiaxed-grain model $\dot\varepsilon\propto D_V\sigma/(Td^2)$. It occupies the high-temperature, low-stress field.
- **C: [Coble creep](../../../../../coble-creep.md).** The analogous transport runs predominantly along [grain boundaries](../../../../../grain-boundary.md). The characteristic scaling is $\dot\varepsilon\propto\delta D_{GB}\sigma/(Td^3)$, where $\delta$ is an effective boundary thickness. The lower activation cost of boundary [diffusion](../../../../../diffusion.md) makes it competitive at lower [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) than bulk-diffusion [material creep](../../../../../creep-deformation.md).

Boundary sliding can accommodate grain-shape changes during diffusional [material creep](../../../../../creep-deformation.md); it is not a reason to identify either [diffusion](../../../../../diffusion.md) field as [dislocation glide](../../../../../dislocation-glide.md). The supplied map's A/B/C placement, including the boundary between B and C, determines these assignments. Its numerical contours apply to the material state used to construct that map, not to arbitrary [grain sizes](../../../../../grain-size.md).
