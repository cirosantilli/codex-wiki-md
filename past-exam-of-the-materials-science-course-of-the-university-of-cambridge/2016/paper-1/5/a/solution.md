<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The alloy shear yield [stress](../../../../../stress.md) can be roughly $70/0.78\simeq90$ times the stated pure-aluminium [critical resolved shear stress](../../../../../critical-resolved-shear-stress.md). Strengthening raises the [stress](../../../../../stress.md) required for [dislocation glide](../../../../../dislocation-glide.md) rather than requiring homogeneous shearing of a perfect crystal.

- **[Solid-solution strengthening](../../../../../solid-solution-strengthening.md):** substitutional solutes create elastic and chemical disturbances that interact with [dislocations](../../../../../dislocation.md). A moving [dislocation](../../../../../dislocation.md) must escape or drag these disturbances.
- **[Precipitation hardening](../../../../../precipitation-hardening.md):** suitably spaced second-phase particles obstruct glide. Small shearable precipitates resist cutting; larger nonshearable obstacles require [dislocation](../../../../../dislocation.md) bowing and bypass. Particle spacing and coherency matter, so overaging can reduce strength.
- **[Work hardening](../../../../../work-hardening.md):** prior plastic deformation increases [dislocation density](../../../../../dislocation-density.md). Intersections, junctions and forest [dislocations](../../../../../dislocation.md) impede subsequent glide; a common scaling is $\Delta\tau\sim\alpha Gb\sqrt{\rho_d}$.
- **[Grain boundary strengthening](../../../../../grain-boundary-strengthening.md):** smaller grains shorten the distance over which [dislocations](../../../../../dislocation.md) can pile up and make slip transfer across differently oriented grains more difficult. In its ordinary regime, the [Hall-Petch relation](../../../../../hall-petch-relation.md) is $\sigma_y=\sigma_0+k_y d^{-1/2}$.

These mechanisms can coexist. The numerical comparison is an order-of-magnitude comparison of shear strengths; it is not a conversion of the alloy's shear yield [stress](../../../../../stress.md) directly into a tensile yield [stress](../../../../../stress.md).
