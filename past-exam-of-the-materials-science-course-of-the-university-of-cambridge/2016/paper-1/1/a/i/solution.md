<h1 id="1/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**This is a [C-centered orthorhombic lattice](../../../../../../c-centered-orthorhombic-lattice.md).** The translation $(1/2,1/2,0)$ takes each A molecule to another A molecule and each B molecule to another B molecule. Thus the conventional cell has two [lattice points](../../../../../../lattice-point.md).

A two-molecule [crystal motif](../../../../../../crystal-motif.md) can be chosen as A at $(0,0,0)$ and B at $(1/2,0,1/2)$. Attaching that same motif to both C-centered [lattice points](../../../../../../lattice-point.md) produces all four molecules, hence **eight [iodine](../../../../../../iodine.md) atoms per conventional cell**.

The centers alone appear face-centered, but $(1/2,0,1/2)$ exchanges A and B orientations. It is not a translation symmetry of the oriented molecular solid. An F-centered assignment would discard essential motif information.
