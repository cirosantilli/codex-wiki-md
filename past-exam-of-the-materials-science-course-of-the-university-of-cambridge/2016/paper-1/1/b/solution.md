<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Count atoms before comparing cell [volumes](../../../../../volume.md). The molecular conventional cell has eight atoms; the metallic face-centered cubic cell has four. Thus

$$
v_{\rm mol}=\frac{0.726(0.480)(0.978)}8
=0.04260168\,\mathrm{nm^3},\qquad
v_{\rm met}=\frac{0.51^3}{4}=0.03316275\,\mathrm{nm^3}.
$$

Relative to the molecular phase,

$$
\boxed{100\frac{v_{\rm met}-v_{\rm mol}}{v_{\rm mol}}=-22.16\%.}
$$

The transformation therefore produces **about 22.2% contraction per atom** on the stated extrapolated-volume comparison. Conversely, the molecular [volume](../../../../../volume.md) is about $28.5\%$ larger than the metallic [volume](../../../../../volume.md); these percentages have different denominators. Compressibility would be needed to turn the extrapolated comparison into exact [volumes](../../../../../volume.md) at the transformation [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md).
