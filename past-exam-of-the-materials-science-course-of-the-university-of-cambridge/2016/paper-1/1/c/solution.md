<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

At fixed [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), a phase's [Gibbs free energy](../../../../../gibbs-free-energy.md) satisfies $\mathrm dG=V\,\mathrm dp$. For the metallic-minus-molecular difference,

$$
\left(\frac{\partial\Delta G}{\partial p}\right)_T=\Delta V<0.
$$

**Increasing [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) favors the smaller-volume metallic phase.** With a pressure-independent [volume](../../../../../volume.md) approximation, $\Delta G(p)\simeq\Delta G(p_0)+(p-p_0)\Delta V$, so [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) can overcome an initially positive free-energy difference. The [volume](../../../../../volume.md) comparison explains the direction of stabilization; it does not by itself determine the transition [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) or the electronic origin of [metallic bonding](../../../../../metallic-bonding.md).
