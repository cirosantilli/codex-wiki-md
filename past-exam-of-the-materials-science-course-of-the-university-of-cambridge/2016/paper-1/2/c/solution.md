<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The conventional cubic cell has [volume](../../../../../volume.md)

$$
a^3=(0.52\times10^{-9})^3=1.40608\times10^{-28}\,\mathrm{m^3}.
$$

Consequently the same effective carrier count is

$$
\boxed{N_{\rm eff}=n_{\rm eff}a^3=0.9236\quad\text{carriers per cell}.}
$$

A noninteger mean is normal for a doped crystal: it describes an ensemble average, not a requirement that every cell contain the same number of defects. The [fluorite structure](../../../../../fluorite-structure.md) has four cation sites and eight oxygen sites in this conventional cell.
