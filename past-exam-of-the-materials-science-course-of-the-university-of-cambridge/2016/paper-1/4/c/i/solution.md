<h1 id="4/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Apply the [lever rule](../../../../../../lever-rule.md) on the $779^\circ\mathrm C$ solid tie line. All compositions are [copper](../../../../../../copper.md) [mass](../../../../../../mass.md) percentages:

$$
\boxed{w_\alpha=\frac{92-28}{92-9}=\frac{64}{83}=0.7711,
\qquad w_\beta=\frac{28-9}{92-9}=\frac{19}{83}=0.2289.}
$$

Thus the equilibrium material is **77.1% $\alpha$ and 22.9% $\beta$ by [mass](../../../../../../mass.md)** just below the [eutectic temperature](../../../../../../eutectic-temperature.md). These are phase fractions; converting them to [volume fractions](../../../../../../volume-fraction.md) would require [densities](../../../../../../density.md).
