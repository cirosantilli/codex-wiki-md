Original schematic Ag-Cu phase diagram constrained by the supplied endpoints and eutectic tie line; unprovided curve shapes and intermediate temperatures are illustrative**.

The horizontal axis is [copper](../../../../../copper.md) [mass](../../../../../mass.md) percentage. The two [liquidus](../../../../../liquidus.md) branches meet at $(28\%,779^\circ\mathrm C)$ and connect to pure [silver](../../../../../silver.md) at $960^\circ\mathrm C$ and pure [copper](../../../../../copper.md) at $1083^\circ\mathrm C$. The terminal solid-solution boundaries at the [eutectic temperature](../../../../../eutectic-temperature.md) are $9\%$ Cu for $\alpha$ and $92\%$ Cu for $\beta$. Label the single-phase fields $L,\alpha,\beta$, the two liquid-plus-solid fields $L+\alpha,L+\beta$, and the lower two-solid field $\alpha+\beta$.

The [solidus](../../../../../solidus.md) boundaries link the pure-component melting points to their terminal solubility limits; the eutectic isotherm spans the three-phase tie line. Below it, the [solvus](../../../../../solvus.md) branches approach pure Ag and pure Cu at room [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md). The supplied data determine this topology and the marked points, **not the exact shapes of every boundary**. The drawing therefore does not invent a numerical [liquidus](../../../../../liquidus.md) [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) for an intermediate composition.
