<h1 id="4/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The cooling curve first falls in the entirely liquid field. On crossing the [liquidus](../../../../../liquidus.md), primary Ag-rich $\alpha$ begins to grow. [Latent heat](../../../../../latent-heat.md) release makes the descent shallower; the remaining liquid becomes richer in [copper](../../../../../copper.md) and approaches the [eutectic composition](../../../../../eutectic-composition.md).

At $779^\circ\mathrm C$, the remaining liquid solidifies as $\alpha+\beta$, giving an **isothermal arrest** under the ideal equilibrium, heat-extraction model. After the liquid is exhausted, [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) falls through the two-solid field. Continued loss of solid solubility causes additional redistribution and precipitation; it does not require another invariant liquid-freezing plateau.

Immediately before the eutectic reaction, the remaining liquid fraction is

$$
w_L=\frac{20-9}{28-9}=\frac{11}{19}=0.5789;
\qquad w_{\alpha,\rm primary}=\frac8{19}=0.4211.
$$

The arrest represents freezing of roughly $58\%$ of the sample by [mass](../../../../../mass.md). Its duration cannot be calculated without the heat-extraction rate and [latent heat](../../../../../latent-heat.md), and the onset [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) cannot be calculated exactly from the supplied phase-diagram points alone.
