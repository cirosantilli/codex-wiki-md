<h1 id="4/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

**At equilibrium at room [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) the phases are pure Ag and pure Cu, with [mass fractions](../../../../../mass-fraction.md) $0.80$ and $0.20$.** A representative [solidification](../../../../../freezing.md) structure contains primary Ag-rich grains or dendrites surrounded by a finer two-phase eutectic aggregate. Subsequent equilibrium solute rejection modifies both constituents as they cool.

<a id="4/e/image-original-schematic-primary-silver-and-lamellar-eutectic-arrangement-geometrical-drawing-areas-do-not-assert-mass-or-volume-fractions"></a>
![](../../../../../past-exam-of-the-materials-science-course-of-the-university-of-cambridge/2016/paper-1-ag-cu-microstructure.png)

**[Figure 5](#4/e/image-original-schematic-primary-silver-and-lamellar-eutectic-arrangement-geometrical-drawing-areas-do-not-assert-mass-or-volume-fractions). Original schematic primary-silver and lamellar eutectic arrangement; geometrical drawing areas do not assert mass or volume fractions**.

The $42.1\%$ primary-$\alpha$ and $57.9\%$ eutectic-constituent fractions calculated at the eutectic arrest describe how the liquid froze. They are **not** the final pure-Ag and pure-Cu phase fractions. For example, just below the arrest the total copper-rich phase fraction is $(20-9)/(92-9)=11/83=0.1325$; it rises to $0.20$ as solubility is lost on further equilibrium cooling. The distinction separates a microstructural constituent from a [thermodynamic phase](../../../../../thermodynamic-phase.md).

Faster [solidification](../../../../../freezing.md) usually refines the dendrite and eutectic spacings. If solid-state [diffusion](../../../../../diffusion.md) cannot keep up, composition gradients, supersaturated solids and delayed precipitation can persist. Their exact fractions then depend on the thermal history; faster cooling does not change the alloy's overall $20\%$ [copper](../../../../../copper.md) inventory. Complete long-term equilibration would restore the same equilibrium phase compositions and [mass fractions](../../../../../mass-fraction.md).
