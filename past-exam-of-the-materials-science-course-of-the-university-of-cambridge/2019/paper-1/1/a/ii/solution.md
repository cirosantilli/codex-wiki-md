[Barium titanate](../../../../../../barium-titanate.md) has a high-temperature cubic [perovskite structure](../../../../../../perovskite-structure.md), with Ti centred in its [oxygen](../../../../../../oxygen.md) octahedron on average. This phase is [paraelectric](../../../../../../paraelectricity.md). Near room [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) the structure is tetragonal, with relative ionic displacements that give a spontaneous [electric polarization](../../../../../../polarization-density.md). This phase is [ferroelectric](../../../../../../ferroelectricity.md): an applied [electric field](../../../../../../electric-field-split.md) can switch between differently polarized domains. Both phases have the same composition, BaTiO$_3$; the transition near $120\,{}^\circ\mathrm{C}$ changes [crystal symmetry](../../../../../../crystal-symmetry.md) and electrical response.
