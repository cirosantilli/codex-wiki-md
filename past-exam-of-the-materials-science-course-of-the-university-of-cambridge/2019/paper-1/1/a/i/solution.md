In [crystal polymorphism](../../../../../../polymorphism-materials-science.md), the same [chemical composition](../../../../../../chemical-composition.md) has different [crystal structures](../../../../../../crystal-structure.md); for an [chemical element](../../../../../../chemical-element.md), this is a form of [allotropy](../../../../../../allotropy.md). [Diamond](../../../../../../diamond.md) has a three-dimensional tetrahedral network of [covalent bonds](../../../../../../covalent-bond-split.md), giving high [hardness](../../../../../../hardness.md) and a wide [band gap](../../../../../../band-gap.md). [Graphite](../../../../../../graphite.md) has strongly bonded planar sheets, with weaker interactions between sheets. Sliding between sheets makes it a useful [solid](../../../../../../solid.md) [lubricant](../../../../../../lubricant.md); mobile electronic states give good electrical conduction within the sheets. Their different properties come from different bonding and structures, despite both being [carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md).
