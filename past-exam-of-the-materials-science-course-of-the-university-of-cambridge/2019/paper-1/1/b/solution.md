↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [zinc blende structure](../../../../../zinc-blende-structure.md) has an FCC [sulfur](../../../../../sulfur.md) sublattice and [zinc](../../../../../zinc.md) on half its tetrahedral sites. One conventional [unit cell](../../../../../unit-cell.md) uses S at $(0,0,0),(1/2,1/2,0),(1/2,0,1/2),(0,1/2,1/2)$ and Zn at those positions displaced by $(1/4,1/4,1/4)$, reduced modulo one. The [wurtzite structure](../../../../../wurtzite.md) has ABAB [sulfur](../../../../../sulfur.md) layers, instead of ABCABC layers. One hexagonal-cell convention uses S at $(0,0,0),(2/3,1/3,1/2)$ and Zn at $(0,0,u),(2/3,1/3,1/2+u)$, with ideal $u=3/8$. Looking along $[001]$ superposes S and Zn in that latter coordinate convention; the height labels distinguish them.

**[Figure 1](#1/b/image-two-by-two-zinc-sulfide-unit-cell-projections-along-the-stacking-axes). Two-by-two zinc sulfide unit-cell projections along the stacking axes**. Original coordinate constructions. Heights are fractional coordinates along the viewing axis. The hexagonal plan uses basal vectors at 120 degrees.

The closest-packed [sulfur](../../../../../sulfur.md) planes are **$\{111\}$, with four distinct plane orientations**, for [zinc blende structure](../../../../../zinc-blende-structure.md), and **$\{001\}$, with one orientation**, for [wurtzite structure](../../../../../wurtzite.md). Opposite normals describe the same unoriented plane orientation. Hexagonal four-index notation writes the latter basal family as $\{0001\}$.
