<h1 id="5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Applying [Schmid's law](../../../../../schmid-s-law.md) at first yield,

$$
\boxed{\tau_{\rm CRSS}=83.5\frac{48}{42\sqrt6}=38.96\,\mathrm{MPa}\simeq39.0\,\mathrm{MPa}.}
$$

This infers the [critical resolved shear stress](../../../../../critical-resolved-shear-stress.md) for the initially active system, assuming the supplied tensile [stress](../../../../../stress.md) is the relevant uniaxial [stress](../../../../../stress.md) at yield and the twelve FCC systems have comparable initial slip resistance.
