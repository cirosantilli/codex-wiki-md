<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

An FCC crystal slips on $\{111\}\langle110\rangle$. For each [slip system](../../../../../slip-system.md), [Schmid's law](../../../../../schmid-s-law.md) gives $\tau=\sigma m$, with $m=|\hat{\mathbf l}\cdot\hat{\mathbf n}|\,|\hat{\mathbf l}\cdot\hat{\mathbf s}|$. Enumerating the twelve systems for $\mathbf l=(5,1,4)$ gives the maximum at

$$
\boxed{(1\bar11)[110].}
$$

Here $\mathbf n=(1,-1,1)$ and $\mathbf s=(1,1,0)$ are perpendicular, while $\mathbf l\cdot\mathbf n=8$, $\mathbf l\cdot\mathbf s=6$ and $|\mathbf l|^2=42$. Hence $m=48/(42\sqrt6)=0.46657$. Reversing both directed slip conventions describes the same physical system.
