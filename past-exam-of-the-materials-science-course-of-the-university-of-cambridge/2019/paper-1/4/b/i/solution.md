↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

At $200\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$, moving from Bi-rich to Sn-rich composition gives **$(\mathrm{Bi})$, $(\mathrm{Bi})+L$, $L$, $L+(\beta\mathrm{Sn})$, $(\beta\mathrm{Sn})$**. Approximate [liquid](../../../../../../liquid.md) coexistence compositions read from the diagram are $x_{\rm Sn}\simeq0.33$ on the left and $0.87$ on the right; the coexisting solids lie near their respective pure ends. The stable [Gibbs free energy](../../../../../../gibbs-free-energy.md) follows [solid](../../../../../../solid.md) curves near the endpoints, two [common-tangent constructions](../../../../../../common-tangent-construction.md) in the coexistence intervals, and the [liquid](../../../../../../liquid.md) curve between them.
