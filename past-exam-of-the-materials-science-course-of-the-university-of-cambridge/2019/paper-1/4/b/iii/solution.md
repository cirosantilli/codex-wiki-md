↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

At $100\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$, the stable sequence is **$(\mathrm{Bi})$, $(\mathrm{Bi})+(\beta\mathrm{Sn})$, $(\beta\mathrm{Sn})$**. The stable two-solid [common tangent](../../../../../../common-tangent.md) lies below the [liquid](../../../../../../liquid.md) curve. There is no stable [liquid](../../../../../../liquid.md) interval, although a metastable extrapolated [liquid](../../../../../../liquid.md) [Gibbs free energy](../../../../../../gibbs-free-energy.md) can still be drawn.
