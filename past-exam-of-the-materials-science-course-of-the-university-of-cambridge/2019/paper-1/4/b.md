For a mixture with phase compositions $x_1,x_2$, its molar [Gibbs free energy](../../../../gibbs-free-energy.md) is the straight-line combination $g=(1-f)g_1(x_1)+fg_2(x_2)$ at overall $x=(1-f)x_1+fx_2$. Minimizing over phase compositions gives equal slopes and equal tangent intercepts: the [common-tangent construction](../../../../common-tangent-construction.md) expresses equality of both component [chemical potentials](../../../../chemical-potential-split.md). The equilibrium curve is the lower [convex envelope](../../../../convex-envelope.md) of the candidate phase curves. Phase fractions follow the [lever rule](../../../../lever-rule.md).
