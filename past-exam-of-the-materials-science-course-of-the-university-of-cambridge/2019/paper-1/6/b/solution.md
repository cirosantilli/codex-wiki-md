<h1 id="6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Energetic [neutron](../../../../../neutron-split.md) collisions create [displacement cascades](../../../../../displacement-cascade.md) and [Frenkel pairs](../../../../../frenkel-pair.md): displaced atoms become [self-interstitials](../../../../../self-interstitial.md), leaving [vacancies](../../../../../vacancy.md). During migration, some opposite defects recombine, while others cluster or reach sinks. Interstitials can condense into extra atomic planes bounded by [dislocation loops](../../../../../dislocation-loop.md); [vacancy](../../../../../vacancy.md) clustering can instead give missing-plane loops. Their subsequent growth is driven by defect absorption and [dislocation climb](../../../../../dislocation-climb.md).

Randomly oriented loops do not select a macroscopic shear direction in an initially [isotropic](../../../../../isotropy.md) polycrystal. That does **not** prove that every external dimension remains unchanged: interstitial and [vacancy](../../../../../vacancy.md) loops have opposite relaxation-volume contributions, and an imbalance can change the volume. Macroscopic [void swelling](../../../../../void-swelling.md) requires the additional vacancy-cavity mechanism; it must not be identified automatically with the mere presence of loops. Texture, stress-biased absorption and unequal loop populations can also give anisotropic dimensional change.
