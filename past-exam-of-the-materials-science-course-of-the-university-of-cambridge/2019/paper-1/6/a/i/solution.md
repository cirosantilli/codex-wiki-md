The [nuclear fuel](../../../../../../nuclear-fuel.md) supplies nuclei whose [nuclear fission](../../../../../../nuclear-fission.md) releases energy. Most of that energy becomes [heat](../../../../../../heat-split.md) in the fuel and neighbouring material. The reactor transfers this [heat](../../../../../../heat-split.md) to a working fluid; the fuel's chemical form and geometry must also remain compatible with its thermal and irradiation environment.
