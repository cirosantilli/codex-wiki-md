The [coolant](../../../../../../coolant.md) removes [heat](../../../../../../heat-split.md) from the fuel region and carries it to a [heat](../../../../../../heat-split.md) exchanger or directly to a turbine system. It can also act as a [neutron moderator](../../../../../../neutron-moderator.md) in some reactor types, but cooling and moderation are distinct functions and need not be performed by the same material.
