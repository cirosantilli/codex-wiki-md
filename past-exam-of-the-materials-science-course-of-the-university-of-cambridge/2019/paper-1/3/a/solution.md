<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a disordered [face-centered cubic lattice](../../../../../face-centered-cubic-lattice.md), equal average scattering factors on the four sites give reflections only when $h,k,l$ are all even or all odd. [Bragg's law](../../../../../bragg-s-law.md) also requires $h^2+k^2+l^2\leq4a^2/\lambda^2$. With Fe K$\alpha$ [wavelength](../../../../../wavelength.md) $\lambda\simeq0.1936\,\mathrm{nm}$, the upper bound is $15.41$. The allowed sums are $3,4,8,11,12$, giving

$$
\boxed{111,\quad200,\quad220,\quad311,\quad222.}
$$

The next allowed family, $400$, has squared-index sum $16$ and cannot satisfy the diffraction condition for this [wavelength](../../../../../wavelength.md). This list assumes the full geometrically accessible angular range, rather than an instrument-specific cutoff.
