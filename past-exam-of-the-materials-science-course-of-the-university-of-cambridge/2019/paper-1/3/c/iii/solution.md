<h1 id="3/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The first peak is $001$, with $d_{001}=c$; the second is $110$, with $d_{110}=a/\sqrt2$. Applying [Bragg's law](../../../../../../bragg-s-law.md) with $\lambda=0.193604\,\mathrm{nm}$ gives

$$
\boxed{c=\frac\lambda{2\sin15.30^\circ}=0.3669\,\mathrm{nm},\qquad a=b=\frac\lambda{\sqrt2\sin20.24^\circ}=0.3957\,\mathrm{nm}.}
$$

Thus $c/a=0.927$, consistent with the approximate ratio used to order the reflections. These are the lengths of the supplied larger basal cell; the primitive basal [lattice parameter](../../../../../../lattice-constant.md) is $a/\sqrt2=0.2798\,\mathrm{nm}$.
