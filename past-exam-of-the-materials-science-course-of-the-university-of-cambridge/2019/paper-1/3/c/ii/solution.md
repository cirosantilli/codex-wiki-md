<h1 id="3/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Ordering reflections by $d_{hkl}^{-2}=(h^2+k^2)/a^2+l^2/c^2$ with $c\simeq0.9a$, the first three nonzero families are **$001$, $110$ and $111$**. The $100$ family is extinct. Their [structure factors](../../../../../../structure-factor.md) are

$$
\boxed{F_{001}=2(f_{\rm Au}-f_{\rm Cu}),\quad F_{110}=2(f_{\rm Au}-f_{\rm Cu}),\quad F_{111}=2(f_{\rm Au}+f_{\rm Cu}).}
$$

The [atomic scattering factors](../../../../../../atomic-scattering-factor.md) in each expression are evaluated at that reflection's scattering vector, so equal symbolic forms need not mean equal numerical amplitudes at different angles. In particular, the low-angle superlattice peaks need not be the strongest peaks.
