<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Successive planes normal to $[001]$ are chemically ordered Au and Cu layers. The supplied four-site cell is a convenient doubled description of the **[primitive tetragonal lattice](../../../../../primitive-tetragonal-lattice.md)**: valid primitive translations are

$$
\mathbf a_1=\tfrac a2(1,1,0),\quad
\mathbf a_2=\tfrac a2(-1,1,0),\quad
\mathbf a_3=(0,0,c).
$$

They give a square basal cell of side $a/\sqrt2$, containing an Au basis atom at the origin and Cu at fractional $(1/2,1/2,1/2)$. That last vector exchanges chemical species and is not a Bravais translation. Thus a face-centred-looking atomic arrangement does not make the chemically ordered material an FCC [Bravais lattice](../../../../../bravais-lattice.md). This is the [L10 crystal structure](../../../../../l10-crystal-structure.md); the following indices retain the larger cell specified in the question.
