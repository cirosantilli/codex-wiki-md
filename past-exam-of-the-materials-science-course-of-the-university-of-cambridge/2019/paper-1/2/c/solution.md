<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

At distance $x$ from its tip, the wedge thickness is $t_q=x\tan\theta_q$. Its [optical path difference](../../../../../optical-path-difference.md) is therefore

$$
\Gamma_q=\Delta n_q x\tan\theta_q
=0.009(5\times10^{-3})\tan(0.5^\circ)
=3.927\times10^{-7}\,\mathrm m.
$$

The perpendicular slow directions make the two retardances subtract. A white-light black compensation fringe is the zero-order cancellation, so **the film retardance has magnitude approximately $393\,\mathrm{nm}$**, with its slow direction along the rods. This interpretation assumes the observed fringe is the white-light compensation fringe; a single monochromatic dark fringe alone would have an integer-order ambiguity. Without the wedge, the [wavelength](../../../../../wavelength.md) dependence of $\sin^2(\pi\Gamma/\lambda)$ selectively transmits visible wavelengths and gives the film its [interference colour](../../../../../interference-colour.md).
