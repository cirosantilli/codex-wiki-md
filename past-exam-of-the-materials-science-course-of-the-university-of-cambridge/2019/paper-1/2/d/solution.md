<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The local slow direction follows the radial rods. Between crossed [polarizers](../../../../../polarizer.md), the angular factor is $\sin^2(2\varphi)$, so radial directions parallel to either polarizer are dark. The pattern is a **[Maltese cross in polarized-light microscopy](../../../../../maltese-cross-in-polarized-light-microscopy.md)**, with four bright sectors separated by dark arms parallel to the polarizer directions. Its colours depend on the local [optical path difference](../../../../../optical-path-difference.md); tapering rods or changing fill fraction can make this vary with radius. The cross belongs to the local radial-axis pattern and does not imply a [conoscopic interference figure](../../../../../conoscopic-interference-pattern.md).
