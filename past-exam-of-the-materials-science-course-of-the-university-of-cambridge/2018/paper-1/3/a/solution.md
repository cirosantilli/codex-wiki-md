<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a cubic phase, $d_{hkl}=a/\sqrt{h^2+k^2+l^2}$ and [Bragg's law](../../../../../bragg-s-law.md) gives

$$
\sin^2\theta=\frac{\lambda^2}{4a^2}(h^2+k^2+l^2).
$$

The first six FCC-allowed squared-index sums are $3,4,8,11,12,16$, from indices all odd or all even. **The labels1–6 are therefore**

$$
\boxed{111,\quad200,\quad220,\quad311,\quad222,\quad400.}
$$

Remember that the plot reports $2\theta$, not $\theta$. The first peak gives

$$
a_{111}=\frac{\sqrt3(0.1542)}{2\sin(43.1^\circ/2)}
\simeq0.36356\,\mathrm{nm}.
$$

The last peak provides an independent estimate,

$$
a_{400}=\frac{4(0.1542)}{2\sin(115.9^\circ/2)}
\simeq0.36386\,\mathrm{nm}.
$$

Thus **$a\simeq0.3638\,\mathrm{nm}$** is appropriate to the supplied precision. The two estimates differ by about $0.08\%$; high-angle data are less sensitive to a fixed angular error, so the $400$ estimate is useful for the composition calculation. The printed angles do not justify many more significant figures or an exact fit of all six positions.
