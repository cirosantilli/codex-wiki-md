<h1 id="2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a conventional initially random, untextured piezoceramic, orientational averaging suppresses the macroscopic linear response. Electrical poling of [ferroelectric domains](../../../../../../ferroelectric-domain.md) is the usual way to generate a lasting net response. **Under that conventional preparation assumption, a ferroelectric constituent is the expected route.**

However, the word polycrystalline alone does not logically require [ferroelectricity](../../../../../../ferroelectricity.md). A deliberately textured aggregate of nonswitchable piezoelectric grains can have a nonzero effective tensor if their orientations and polar directions do not cancel. [Ferroelectricity](../../../../../../ferroelectricity.md) means electrically switchable spontaneous polarization, whereas [piezoelectricity](../../../../../../piezoelectricity.md) means linear [stress](../../../../../../stress.md)–charge coupling. Thus the unconditional statement that every polycrystalline piezoelectric must be ferroelectric is too strong.
