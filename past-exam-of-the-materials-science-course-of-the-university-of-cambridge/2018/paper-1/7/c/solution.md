<h1 id="7/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Read the [coesite](../../../../../coesite.md)–[stishovite](../../../../../stishovite.md) boundary at approximately $(400\,{}^\circ\mathrm C,7.2\,\mathrm{GPa})$ and $(2800\,{}^\circ\mathrm C,13.5\,\mathrm{GPa})$. The slope is therefore

$$
\frac{dp}{dT}\simeq\frac{(13.5-7.2)\times10^9}{2800-400}
=2.625\times10^6\,\mathrm{Pa\,K^{-1}}.
$$

A Celsius [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) difference equals the corresponding kelvin difference. The graph does not justify treating the endpoints as exact measurements.

For [stishovite](../../../../../stishovite.md), the tetragonal cell volume divided by two gives

$$
v_{{\rm formula},s}=\frac{a^2c}{2}
=0.0232486\,\mathrm{nm^3}.
$$

For monoclinic [coesite](../../../../../coesite.md) the cell volume is $abc\sin\beta$, so

$$
v_{{\rm formula},c}=\frac{(0.7143)^2(1.2383)\sin120^\circ}{16}
=0.0341978\,\mathrm{nm^3}.
$$

Using $M_{\mathrm{SiO_2}}\simeq0.060084\,\mathrm{kg\,mol^{-1}}$ and Avogadro's constant, the mass-specific volumes are

$$
v_s\simeq2.3302\times10^{-4},\qquad
v_c\simeq3.4276\times10^{-4}\quad\mathrm{m^3\,kg^{-1}}.
$$

For the direction **[stishovite](../../../../../stishovite.md) to [coesite](../../../../../coesite.md)**,

$$
\boxed{\Delta s_{s\to c}=(v_c-v_s)\frac{dp}{dT}
\simeq+2.88\times10^2\,\mathrm{J\,kg^{-1}\,K^{-1}}.}
$$

For the reverse, [coesite](../../../../../coesite.md) to [stishovite](../../../../../stishovite.md), the [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md) change is **about $-288\,\mathrm{J\,kg^{-1}\,K^{-1}}$**. [Stishovite](../../../../../stishovite.md) is the denser phase: a positive boundary slope and its smaller volume imply a lower [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md) on the high-pressure side. Stating the direction prevents an otherwise avoidable sign ambiguity.
