<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

**The 020 reflection is extinguished by the diamond basis, even though it satisfies the face-centered lattice parity rule.** Write the diamond structure as an FCC lattice with identical atoms at basis positions $(0,0,0)$ and $(1/4,1/4,1/4)$.

The [structure factor](../../../../../structure-factor.md) separates into lattice and basis terms:

$$
F_{hkl}=f\left[1+(-1)^{h+k}+(-1)^{h+l}+(-1)^{k+l}\right]
\left[1+e^{i\pi(h+k+l)/2}\right].
$$

At $(h,k,l)=(0,2,0)$, all indices are even, so the first bracket is $4$. The second bracket is $1+e^{i\pi}=0$. The two identical sublattices scatter with opposite phases and cancel:

$$
\boxed{F_{020}=0,\qquad I_{020}\propto|F_{020}|^2=0.}
$$

For all-even reflections of diamond, the additional allowed condition is $h+k+l$ divisible by four. This is a [systematic absence](../../../../../systematic-absence.md), not an absence of the corresponding family of geometrical planes.
