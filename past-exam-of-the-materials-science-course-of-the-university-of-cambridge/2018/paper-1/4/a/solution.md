**[Pearlite](../../../../../pearlite.md) is a microstructural arrangement of two phases, [ferrite](../../../../../ferrite.md) and [cementite](../../../../../cementite.md), rather than a single phase.** [Ferrite](../../../../../ferrite.md) is an iron-rich [solid solution](../../../../../solid-solution.md); [cementite](../../../../../cementite.md) is the carbide $\mathrm{Fe_3C}$. A [pearlite](../../../../../pearlite.md) colony contains alternating lamellae of these constituents formed by transformation of [austenite](../../../../../austenite.md).

A [thermodynamic phase](../../../../../thermodynamic-phase.md) has a specified [crystal structure](../../../../../crystal-structure.md) and composition range with locally homogeneous properties on the appropriate scale. The word [pearlite](../../../../../pearlite.md) instead describes how distinct phases are arranged. Its lamellar spacing and orientation can change while the identities of its two phases remain the same.
