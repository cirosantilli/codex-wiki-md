<h1 id="4/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

**[Ferrite](../../../../../../ferrite.md) etches faster in [Nital](../../../../../../nital.md)**, so it recedes and leaves [cementite](../../../../../../cementite.md) standing as the higher ridges. The identification is based on the surface-relief preparation, not on assuming that every bright feature in every microscopy mode is [cementite](../../../../../../cementite.md). In the stated AFM image, brightness is a displayed height contrast.
