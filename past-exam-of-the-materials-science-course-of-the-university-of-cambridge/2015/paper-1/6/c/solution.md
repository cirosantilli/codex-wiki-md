<h1 id="6/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

[Diamond](../../../../../diamond.md) can be **metastable**: its [Gibbs free energy](../../../../../gibbs-free-energy.md) is higher than [graphite](../../../../../graphite.md)'s under ambient conditions, but transformation is kinetically inhibited. Conversion requires substantial rearrangement of the strong carbon-bond network and the formation and growth of a new [thermodynamic phase](../../../../../thermodynamic-phase.md), with an activation and [nucleation barrier](../../../../../nucleation-barrier.md).

At room temperature the rate can be extremely small even though the free-energy change favors [graphite](../../../../../graphite.md). Thermodynamic stability specifies the preferred equilibrium state, not the time needed to reach it. Persistence of [diamond](../../../../../diamond.md) therefore does not contradict [graphite](../../../../../graphite.md)'s ambient equilibrium stability.
