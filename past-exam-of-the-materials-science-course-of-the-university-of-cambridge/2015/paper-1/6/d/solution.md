<h1 id="6/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Use the same molar amount for both [thermodynamic phases](../../../../../thermodynamic-phase.md). A hexagonal cell built from two basal lattice vectors at120degrees has volume $(\sqrt3/2)a^2c$, not the volume of a three-times-larger full hexagonal prism. With four atoms per stated [graphite](../../../../../graphite.md) cell,

$$
V_{m,g}=\frac{N_A}{4}\frac{\sqrt3}{2}
(0.246\times10^{-9})^2(0.671\times10^{-9})
\simeq5.2944\times10^{-6}\ {\rm m^3\,mol^{-1}}.
$$

[Diamond](../../../../../diamond.md) has eight atoms per conventional cubic cell, so

$$
V_{m,d}=\frac{N_A}{8}(0.357\times10^{-9})^3
\simeq3.4250\times10^{-6}\ {\rm m^3\,mol^{-1}}.
$$

For [graphite](../../../../../graphite.md) transforming to [diamond](../../../../../diamond.md),

$$
\Delta V_m\simeq-1.8693\times10^{-6}\ {\rm m^3\,mol^{-1}},
\qquad
\Delta S_m=2.45-5.69=-3.24\ {\rm J\,K^{-1}\,mol^{-1}}.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{\frac{\mathrm dP}{\mathrm dT}
\simeq1.73\times10^6\ {\rm Pa\,K^{-1}}
=1.73\ {\rm MPa\,K^{-1}}.}
$$

The positive slope reflects [diamond](../../../../../diamond.md)'s smaller [entropy](../../../../../entropy-split.md) and volume. Higher temperature favors the higher-entropy [graphite](../../../../../graphite.md) [thermodynamic phase](../../../../../thermodynamic-phase.md), requiring greater pressure to compensate in favor of the smaller-volume [diamond](../../../../../diamond.md) [thermodynamic phase](../../../../../thermodynamic-phase.md).

This estimate treats the supplied ambient molar [entropies](../../../../../entropy-split.md) and volumes as applicable near coexistence and neglects their changes through [thermal expansion](../../../../../thermal-expansion.md), [compressibility](../../../../../compressibility.md) and [heat capacity](../../../../../heat-capacity.md). It gives an approximate gradient, not the transition pressure or a complete high-pressure [thermodynamic phase](../../../../../thermodynamic-phase.md) boundary; an intercept would require an additional free-energy datum.
