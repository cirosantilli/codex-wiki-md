In [tempered martensite](../../../../../../tempered-martensite.md), the metastable carbon-supersaturated martensitic matrix can evolve toward a lower-carbon ferritic matrix containing fine carbides. [Carbon](../../../../../../carbon.md) segregation and precipitation reduce [supersaturation](../../../../../../supersaturation.md) and tetragonal lattice distortion; recovery can reduce internal [strain](../../../../../../strain.md) while the original plate or lath pattern remains recognizable. This is tempering, rather than a fresh austenite-to-pearlite transformation.[Introduction and Virgin Microstructure, carbon redistribution and retained plate structure](../../../../../../tempered-martensite-by-h-k-d-h-bhadeshia.md)

A generic sketch consequently replaces a uniform highly strained plate interior by a lower-carbon matrix with dispersed carbide particles, as in the third panel above. This provides a qualitative materials explanation for a changed fracture response. It does not specify a manufacturing optimization, and tempering is not a guarantee of monotonic toughness improvement in every [steel](../../../../../../steel.md): carbide morphology, impurities and retained [austenite](../../../../../../austenite.md) matter. The source reports the observed outcome, so the explanation must not be turned into a universal process recommendation.
