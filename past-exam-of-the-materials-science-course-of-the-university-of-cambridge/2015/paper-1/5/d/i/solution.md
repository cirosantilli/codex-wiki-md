<h1 id="5/d/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

On a timescale short compared with $\tau=\eta/E$, or for $\omega\tau\gg1$, the [dashpot](../../../../../../dashpot.md) strongly resists rapid deformation. The imposed oscillating [stress](../../../../../../stress.md) produces a small [strain](../../../../../../strain.md), of amplitude approximately $\sigma_0/(\eta\omega)$, nearly90degrees behind the [stress](../../../../../../stress.md). **The network appears stiff and predominantly viscous in this stress-controlled high-frequency limit.** It is not an ideal perfectly rigid body at finite frequency.
