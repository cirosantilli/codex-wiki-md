<h1 id="5/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For a slowly varying load with $\omega\tau\ll1$, the [dashpot](../../../../../../dashpot.md) [stress](../../../../../../stress.md) is small relative to the [spring](../../../../../../spring.md) [stress](../../../../../../stress.md). The [strain](../../../../../../strain.md) approaches $\sigma/E$ almost in [phase](../../../../../../phase-waves.md), so **the low-frequency response is predominantly elastic**. A constant load approaches the finite equilibrium [strain](../../../../../../strain.md) $\sigma_0/E$; it does not sustain steady viscous flow in this parallel network.
