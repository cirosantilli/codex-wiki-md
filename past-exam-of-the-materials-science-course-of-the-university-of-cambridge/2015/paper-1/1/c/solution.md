<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The four FCC translations give a lattice [structure factor](../../../../../structure-factor.md) that is nonzero only when $h,k,l$ are all even or all odd. The identical two-atom motif then multiplies it by

$$
1+\exp\left[\frac{i\pi}{2}(h+k+l)\right].
$$

For $(200)$ the FCC part is allowed, but the motif factor is $1+e^{i\pi}=0$. Hence

$$
\boxed{F_{200}=0.}
$$

In the projection, equal numbers of atoms occur at $x/a=0,1/4,1/2,3/4$. Their $(200)$ [phases](../../../../../phase-waves.md) are respectively $+1,-1,+1,-1$. The paired layers cancel exactly for equal [silicon](../../../../../silicon.md) [scattering amplitudes](../../../../../scattering-amplitude.md). This [diamond-basis diffraction extinction](../../../../../diamond-basis-diffraction-extinction.md) is additional to the FCC centring rule; it is not evidence that no atoms lie on the nominal reflecting planes.
