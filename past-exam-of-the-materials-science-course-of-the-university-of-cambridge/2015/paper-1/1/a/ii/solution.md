[Bond-centred inversion in diamond cubic](../../../../../../bond-centred-inversion-in-diamond-cubic.md) gives one [inversion centre](../../../../../../inversion-centre.md) at the midpoint of a nearest-neighbor bond:

$$
\boxed{\mathbf c=(\tfrac18,\tfrac18,\tfrac18).}
$$

Mark its projected point $(1/8,1/8)$ and label its height **$z/a=1/8$**. Inversion about this point sends $\mathbf r$ to $(1/4,1/4,1/4)-\mathbf r$. It exchanges the two identical FCC sublattices and therefore preserves the crystal. An individual atom at the chosen origin is not an [inversion centre](../../../../../../inversion-centre.md).
