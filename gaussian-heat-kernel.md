# Gaussian heat kernel

↑ **Parent:** [Heat kernel](heat-kernel.md)

For standard [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) in $\mathbb R^d$, $p_t(y-x)$ is the transition density from $x$ to $y$. It is the [heat kernel](heat-kernel.md) for generator $\tfrac12\Delta$. For every unit vector $e$, $\int|\partial_e p_t(x)|dx=\sqrt{2/\pi}/\sqrt t$, which bounds the change in this density under a spatial translation and proves the [Gaussian heat-kernel proof of the harmonic Liouville theorem](gaussian-heat-kernel-proof-of-the-harmonic-liouville-theorem.md).
