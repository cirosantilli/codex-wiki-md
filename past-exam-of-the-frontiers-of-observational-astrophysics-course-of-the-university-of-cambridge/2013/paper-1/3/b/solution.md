The [thermal Sunyaev-Zeldovich effect](../../../../../thermal-sunyaev-zeldovich-effect.md) is inverse-Compton scattering of [cosmic microwave background](../../../../../cosmic-microwave-background-split.md) [photons](../../../../../photon.md) by hot [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) in the [intracluster medium](../../../../../intracluster-medium.md). [Electron](../../../../../electron.md) thermal [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) shifts [photons](../../../../../photon.md) toward higher frequencies, changing the spectrum relative to neighboring sky. In the nonrelativistic, small-optical-depth limit its amplitude is controlled by the Compton parameter

$$
\boxed{y=\frac{\sigma_T}{m_ec^2}\int n_e k_BT_e\,dl.}
$$

It measures line-of-sight [electron](../../../../../electron.md) [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md). At low frequencies the apparent Rayleigh-Jeans [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) changes by $\Delta T/T_{\rm CMB}\simeq-2y$. The thermal spectral distortion crosses through zero near $217\,\mathrm{GHz}$ for the usual CMB [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) and then becomes an increment; relativistic corrections matter for hot gas. Multi-frequency measurements distinguish this spectrum from dust, radio sources and an ordinary CMB [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) fluctuation.

The surface-brightness contrast has no ordinary $(1+z)^{-4}$ dimming for the same cluster [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) integral: the incident CMB [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) and the observation's [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md) redshift scale together. Angular extent and integrated flux still depend on distance. This makes the effect useful for selecting distant clusters and mapping their thermal [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md). The solid-angle integral $Y=\int y\,d\Omega$ scales with total thermal [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) divided by angular-diameter distance squared, providing a [mass](../../../../../mass.md) proxy when its gas-physics calibration is specified.[cluster survey and integrated-SZ selection context](../../../../../planck-2013-results-xxix-planck-catalogue-of-sunyaev-zeldovich-sources-by-planck-collaboration.md)

X-ray surface brightness depends roughly on $\int n_e^2\Lambda(T_e)dl$, whereas $y$ depends on $\int n_eT_e dl$. Combining X-ray spectra, brightness and resolved SZ data constrains density and line-of-sight depth. With a justified geometry, comparison to angular size gives a distance estimate; clumping and elongation can bias it. Cluster abundances and their redshift evolution test cosmological structure growth after selection and [mass](../../../../../mass.md) calibration are modeled.

The [kinetic Sunyaev-Zeldovich effect](../../../../../kinetic-sunyaev-zeldovich-effect.md) additionally probes bulk line-of-sight motion, with leading $\Delta T/T_{\rm CMB}\simeq-\tau_e v_\parallel/c$ in a stated velocity-sign convention. Its approximately blackbody [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) spectrum differs from the thermal distortion. These are complementary measurements of thermal [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) and gas motion, requiring foreground and instrumental control.
