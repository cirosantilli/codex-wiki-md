<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

[annual stellar parallax](../../../../../stellar-parallax.md) uses the known Earth-orbit baseline, with **$d(\mathrm{pc})=1/\varpi(\mathrm{arcsec})$**. Repeated positions separate parallax from [proper motion](../../../../../proper-motion.md); [calibration](../../../../../calibration-differential-geometry.md) offsets, binarity and noisy-parallax selection require checks. Nearby [stars](../../../../../star.md) calibrate absolute [luminosities](../../../../../luminosity.md). Extinction-corrected [distance modulus](../../../../../distance-modulus.md) then gives

$$
\boxed{m_\lambda-M_\lambda=5\log_{10}(d/10\,\mathrm{pc})+A_\lambda}.
$$

[main-sequence fitting](../../../../../main-sequence-fitting.md) compares a cluster sequence with nearby calibrated [stars](../../../../../star.md); age, [metallicity](../../../../../metallicity.md), reddening and unresolved binaries limit the match. [Cepheid variables](../../../../../cepheid-variable.md) extend the range to nearby [galaxies](../../../../../galaxy-split.md): measure periods and mean multiband magnitudes, calibrate the [Cepheid period-luminosity relation](../../../../../cepheid-period-luminosity-relation.md), and correct for [extinction](../../../../../extinction.md), [metallicity](../../../../../metallicity.md), crowding and pulsation mode. [RR Lyrae stars](../../../../../rr-lyrae-star.md) and the [tip of the red giant branch](../../../../../tip-of-the-red-giant-branch.md) provide complementary population-II routes with their own composition and [calibration](../../../../../calibration-differential-geometry.md) dependence.

Some anchors bypass intermediate rungs. An [eclipsing-binary distance](../../../../../eclipsing-binary-distance.md) starts from a double-lined [eclipsing binary](../../../../../eclipsing-binary.md): velocities give $a\sin i$; eclipses give inclination $i$ and relative radii $R/a$, hence physical radii. An independently calibrated surface-brightness/colour relation gives angular radii and distance; alternatively corrected bolometric flux satisfies $F_{\rm bol}=\sigma T_{\rm eff}^4(R/d)^2$ for a component. [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), [extinction](../../../../../extinction.md), third light and surface-brightness [calibration](../../../../../calibration-differential-geometry.md) remain systematic errors. Eight late-type binaries gave a 2.2%-accuracy [LMC](../../../../../large-magellanic-cloud.md) anchor in 2013.[abstract, Nature 495, 76–79; photometric and orbital information](../../../../../an-eclipsing-binary-distance-to-the-large-magellanic-cloud-accurate-to-two-per-cent-by-g-pietrzynski-and-others.md)

A [maser-disk geometric distance](../../../../../maser-disk-geometric-distance.md) uses water [masers](../../../../../maser-split.md) orbiting in the nearly edge-on nuclear disk of [NGC 4258](../../../../../ngc-4258.md). [very-long-baseline interferometry](../../../../../very-long-baseline-interferometry.md), Doppler velocities, [proper motions](../../../../../proper-motion.md) and accelerations constrain its orbit scale and distance. In an ideal circular, matched-radius example $R=v^2/a_{\rm orb}$ and $d=R/\theta$; real fits account for disk warp and different [maser](../../../../../maser-split.md) locations. This geometric method calibrated an extragalactic distance independently of stellar [luminosities](../../../../../luminosity.md).[abstract; orbital proper motions and accelerations, 1999](../../../../../a-4-percent-geometric-distance-to-the-galaxy-ngc4258-from-orbital-motions-in-a-nuclear-gas-disk-by-j-r-herrnstein-and-others.md) These overlapping anchors cross-check the nearby-galaxy ladder; no Hubble-flow fit is needed here.
