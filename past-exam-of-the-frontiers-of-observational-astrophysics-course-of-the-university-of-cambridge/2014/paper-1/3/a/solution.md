The [thermal Sunyaev-Zeldovich effect](../../../../../thermal-sunyaev-zeldovich-effect.md) is [inverse Compton scattering](../../../../../inverse-compton-scattering.md) of [cosmic microwave background](../../../../../cosmic-microwave-background-split.md) [photons](../../../../../photon.md) by hot cluster [electrons](../../../../../electron.md). For an [optically thin](../../../../../optically-thin-medium.md), nonrelativistic thermal [plasma](../../../../../plasma-physics.md),

$$
y=\frac{\sigma_T}{m_ec^2}\int n_ek_BT_e\,dl,\qquad
\Delta I_\nu=I_0y\frac{x^4e^x}{(e^x-1)^2}\left[x\coth(x/2)-4\right],
\quad x=\frac{h\nu}{k_BT_{\rm CMB}},\quad I_0=\frac{2(k_BT_{\rm CMB})^3}{h^2c^2}.
$$

The [Compton y parameter](../../../../../compton-y-parameter.md) measures the [electron](../../../../../electron.md) [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) column. The distortion is a decrement below roughly **217 GHz**, an increment above it, and has $\Delta T/T=-2y$ in the [Rayleigh-Jeans law](../../../../../rayleigh-jeans-law.md) limit. Relativistic [electron](../../../../../electron.md) corrections modify the spectrum and null.[abstract and relativistic thermal-distortion calculation](../../../../../relativistic-corrections-to-the-sunyaev-zel-dovich-effect-by-a-d-challinor-and-a-n-lasenby.md)

Multifrequency maps separate this spectrum from primary [Cosmic microwave background anisotropy](../../../../../cosmic-microwave-background-anisotropy-split.md), radio sources and dust; atmospheric subtraction, beams and [calibration](../../../../../calibration-differential-geometry.md) require control. The [kinetic Sunyaev-Zeldovich effect](../../../../../kinetic-sunyaev-zeldovich-effect.md) has $\Delta T/T\simeq-\tau v_{\rm los}/c$ and a CMB-temperature-like spectrum, so it is both a contaminant and a peculiar-velocity probe. Planck demonstrated blind multifrequency cluster detection with external confirmation and a measured selection function.[abstract; multifrequency detection and catalogue validation](../../../../../planck-2013-results-xxix-planck-catalogue-of-sunyaev-zeldovich-sources-by-planck-collaboration.md)

For a fixed physical [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) column, $y$ has **no ordinary [cosmological surface-brightness dimming](../../../../../cosmological-surface-brightness-dimming.md)**: the distorted spectrum and CMB redshift together. The [integrated Compton parameter](../../../../../integrated-compton-parameter.md) $Y=\int y\,d\Omega$ still scales as $D_A^{-2}$ for fixed integrated physical [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md). SZ observations therefore find distant clusters and measure thermal [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md); integrated $Y$ is a useful mass proxy, requiring [calibration](../../../../../calibration-differential-geometry.md) and a selection model for cosmological counts. X-ray emission scales approximately as $\int n_e^2\Lambda(T_e)dl$, while SZ scales as $\int n_eT_e dl$. Combining them and a measured [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) constrains [density](../../../../../density.md), depth and, with angular geometry, distance. [gas clumping factor](../../../../../gas-clumping-factor.md), asphericity, [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) structure and nonthermal support limit that inference.
