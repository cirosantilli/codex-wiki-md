<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

An [astronomical interferometer](../../../../../astronomical-interferometer.md) combines light from collectors separated by projected [interferometric baseline](../../../../../interferometric-baseline.md) $\mathbf B$. For a distant, narrowband, spatially incoherent source, the [Van Cittert-Zernike theorem](../../../../../van-cittert-zernike-theorem.md) gives normalized [complex visibility](../../../../../complex-visibility.md)

$$
\mathcal V(\mathbf B/\lambda)=\frac{\int I(\boldsymbol\theta)e^{-2\pi i\mathbf B\cdot\boldsymbol\theta/\lambda}\,d^2\theta}{\int I(\boldsymbol\theta)\,d^2\theta}.
$$

The resolving scale is **$\lambda/B$**, while the individual collector and beam combiner restrict the field of view. Delay lines compensate geometric path differences. To see broadband fringes, residual [optical path length](../../../../../optical-path-length.md) mismatch must lie within the [coherence length](../../../../../optical-coherence-length.md) of order $\lambda^2/\Delta\lambda$; [fringe tracking](../../../../../fringe-tracking.md) follows rapidly changing [atmospheric piston in interferometry](../../../../../atmospheric-piston-in-interferometry.md). Baseline changes and [Earth-rotation aperture synthesis](../../../../../earth-rotation-aperture-synthesis.md) sample additional [spatial frequencies](../../../../../spatial-frequency.md).

For an ideal uniform circular [star](../../../../../star.md) of full [angular diameter](../../../../../angular-diameter.md) $\Theta$,

$$
\mathcal V=\frac{2J_1(x)}x,\qquad x=\frac{\pi B\Theta}{\lambda},\qquad
\boxed{\Theta=1.2197\,\lambda/B_{\rm first\ null}}.
$$

Real [limb darkening](../../../../../limb-darkening.md) changes the conversion. Several baselines constrain binaries, [stellar radii](../../../../../stellar-radius.md) and circumstellar structure; orbital angular separations combined with [radial velocities](../../../../../radial-velocity.md) can give physical orbital scales. [calibration](../../../../../calibration-differential-geometry.md) [stars](../../../../../star.md) test contrast losses, alongside [wavelength](../../../../../wavelength.md), photometric imbalance, [polarization](../../../../../polarization-waves.md) and [bandwidth](../../../../../bandwidth-signal-processing.md) controls. For three collectors the [closure phase](../../../../../closure-phase.md) cancels telescope-based phase errors: $\phi_{12}+\phi_{23}+\phi_{31}$ retains source asymmetry information. Sparse baseline coverage still leaves image/model ambiguities.

In [stellar intensity interferometry](../../../../../stellar-intensity-interferometry.md), thermal-light correlations instead give $g^{(2)}(0)-1=|g^{(1)}|^2$ for an ideal single-polarization resolved temporal mode. The Sirius experiment correlated separate detector outputs rather than optically combining the beams.[Nature 178, 1046–1048 (1956)](../../../../../a-test-of-a-new-type-of-stellar-interferometer-on-sirius-by-robert-hanbury-brown-and-richard-quentin-twiss.md) Pure atmospheric phase shifts do not alter local [intensities](../../../../../intensity-physics.md), but finite detector [bandwidth](../../../../../bandwidth-signal-processing.md) dilutes the correlation and [photon](../../../../../photon.md) statistics limit sensitivity; the two-detector observable also loses the complex phase.
