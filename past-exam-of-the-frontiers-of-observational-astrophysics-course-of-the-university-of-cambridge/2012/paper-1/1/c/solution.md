The [wavelength](../../../../../wavelength.md) corresponds to about $15\,\mathrm{GHz}$, in the low-frequency decrement part of the [thermal Sunyaev-Zeldovich effect](../../../../../thermal-sunyaev-zeldovich-effect.md). Hot cluster [electrons](../../../../../electron.md) inverse-Compton scatter CMB [photons](../../../../../photon.md). In the Rayleigh-Jeans limit,

$$
\frac{\Delta T}{T_{\rm CMB}}\simeq-2y,\qquad
y=\int n_e\sigma_T\frac{k_BT_e}{m_ec^2}\,dl.
$$

The signal probes integrated [electron](../../../../../electron.md) [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md). Short baselines give sensitivity to extended arcminute-scale cluster structure, while the primary field covers the cluster and surrounding sky. The array's several-arcminute resolution suits many such structures; it is not equally well matched to every cluster size.

Simultaneous cross-correlations reject uncorrelated receiver [noise](../../../../../noise-signal-processing.md) and provide spatial information; compact radio sources can be constrained with the relatively longer baselines and separated from extended structure. Broad [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md) coverage helps sensitivity provided channel smearing is controlled. Correlated atmospheric emission, [calibration](../../../../../calibration-differential-geometry.md) error, primordial CMB fluctuations and radio-source contamination remain relevant. The lack of zero and very short spacings also [means](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) that total integrated cluster [flux](../../../../../flux.md) requires a model or complementary data. An interferometer is therefore useful for the spatially resolved decrement, rather than automatically measuring every cluster's full [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) integral.
