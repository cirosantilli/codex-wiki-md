<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Atmospheric refractive-index fluctuations distort the incoming wavefront. In a Kolmogorov model, the long-exposure seeing width is approximately $0.98\lambda/r_0$, where the [Fried parameter](../../../../../fried-parameter.md) scales as $r_0\propto\lambda^{6/5}$. High-quality sites and longer [wavelengths](../../../../../wavelength.md) can improve the observing conditions, but do not eliminate the distorted phase. Short coherence times also require rapid correction or exposure.

[Adaptive optics](../../../../../adaptive-optics.md) uses a guide source, [wavefront sensor](../../../../../wavefront-sensor.md), [deformable mirror](../../../../../deformable-mirror.md) and fast feedback loop. Tip-tilt correction removes image wander; higher-order actuators correct finer spatial distortions. Finite actuator spacing, sensor [noise](../../../../../noise-signal-processing.md), delay and changing turbulence leave residual errors. A nearby natural [guide star](../../../../../guide-star-adaptive-optics.md) limits sky coverage, while a laser beacon supplies higher-order reference information over more directions. Conventional laser-guide-star systems still need independent image-motion information, commonly from a natural star.[ESO, tip-tilt information and sky-coverage limitation](../../../../../laser-guide-star-for-adaptive-optics-by-european-southern-observatory.md) [Anisoplanatism](../../../../../anisoplanatism.md) limits the corrected field; laser finite-altitude sampling and variable [PSFs](../../../../../point-spread-function-split.md) need their own [calibration](../../../../../calibration-differential-geometry.md).

[Lucky imaging](../../../../../lucky-imaging.md) selects favorable short exposures and aligns them, trading exposure efficiency and [photon](../../../../../photon.md) statistics for reduced blur. [Speckle interferometry](../../../../../speckle-interferometry.md) uses short-exposure spatial-frequency information and suitable phase/statistical reconstruction instead of simply averaging away the speckles. Both need adequate time resolution, detector performance and [calibration](../../../../../calibration-differential-geometry.md); selection cannot restore information never recorded. Interferometric methods can also recover fine structure with phase control or closure information. These approaches correct or infer wavefront effects; they do not make clouds, absorption or every atmospheric amplitude fluctuation disappear.[wavefront sensing, deformable-mirror correction and atmospheric limitations](../../../../../adaptive-optics-by-european-southern-observatory.md)
