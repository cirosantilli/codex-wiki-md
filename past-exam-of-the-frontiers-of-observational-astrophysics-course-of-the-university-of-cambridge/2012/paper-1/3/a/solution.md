<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Thermal bremsstrahlung](../../../../../thermal-bremsstrahlung.md) arises from [electron](../../../../../electron.md) acceleration in [ion](../../../../../ion.md) Coulomb fields. For a thermal [plasma](../../../../../plasma-physics.md) its optically thin spectral emissivity has the dependence

$$
j_\nu\propto n_e\sum_iZ_i^2n_i\,T_e^{-1/2}e^{-h\nu/(k_BT_e)}g_{\rm ff}(\nu,T_e).
$$

The exponential high-energy slope helps constrain [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) in X-ray spectra, with composition, absorption and other emission components included. At radio frequencies with $h\nu\ll k_BT_e$, the thin spectrum is much flatter and does not alone determine a unique [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md); the [Gaunt factor](../../../../../gaunt-factor.md) supplies a weak [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md) dependence.

For an approximately isothermal layer, [radiative transfer](../../../../../radiative-transfer.md) gives $I_\nu=B_\nu(T_e)(1-e^{-\tau_\nu})$. Radio free-free opacity scales approximately as $\tau_\nu\propto\mathrm{EM}\,T_e^{-3/2}\nu^{-2}g_{\rm ff}$, where the hydrogen-plasma [emission measure](../../../../../emission-measure.md) is $\mathrm{EM}=\int n_e^2dl$. A resolved optically thick brightness [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) approaches $T_e$; an unresolved filling factor lowers the observed value. The turnover between thin and thick regimes helps constrain [emission measure](../../../../../emission-measure.md).

An angular extent and distance give a physical size, and an assumed geometry supplies a line-of-sight depth. Combining that depth with [emission measure](../../../../../emission-measure.md) estimates an rms [density](../../../../../density.md), $n_{e,\rm rms}\sim\sqrt{\mathrm{EM}/\ell}$. Clumping, filling factor, composition and unknown depth preserve a [density-depth degeneracy of free-free radiation](../../../../../density-depth-degeneracy-of-free-free-radiation.md). These measurements constrain different combinations of [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), [density](../../../../../density.md) and size rather than independently fixing all three without assumptions.
