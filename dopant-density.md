# Dopant density

↑ **Parent:** [Semiconductor doping](semiconductor-doping.md)

The number of dopant sites per volume. For a roughly homogeneous three-dimensional distribution, the characteristic site separation is of order $n^{-1/3}$. This counts introduced sites rather than automatically counting free [conduction band](conduction-band.md) [electrons](electron.md): [semiconductor carrier freeze-out](semiconductor-carrier-freeze-out.md) or compensation can make the mobile carrier [density](density.md) much smaller.
