# Vacuum boundary obstruction for an isothermal sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Isothermal sphere](isothermal-sphere.md)

An [isothermal sphere](isothermal-sphere.md) with constant positive [isothermal sound speed](isothermal-sound-speed.md) $c$ obeys the title [hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) equation. Between two positive finite radii with bounded enclosed mass, its right-hand side has a finite integral. A positive starting density therefore remains positive at any proposed finite outer radius. It cannot meet a vacuum boundary with $p=c^2\rho=0$ there without an additional confining structure or a change of equation of state. The [singular isothermal sphere](singular-isothermal-sphere.md) makes the obstruction explicit: $\rho=c^2/(2\pi Gr^2)$ and $p=c^4/(2\pi Gr^2)$ never vanish at finite radius, and its mass grows as $2c^2r/G$ without bound at infinity.
