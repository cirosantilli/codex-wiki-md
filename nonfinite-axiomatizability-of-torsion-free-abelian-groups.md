# Nonfinite axiomatizability of torsion-free abelian groups

↑ **Parent:** [Torsion-free abelian group](torsion-free-abelian-group.md)

The class of [torsion-free](torsion-free-group.md) [abelian groups](abelian-group.md) has an infinite first-order axiomatization but no [finite axiomatization](finite-axiomatizability.md). Add $\forall x(nx=0\to x=0)$ for every positive [integer](integer-split.md) $n$ to the [abelian group](abelian-group.md) [axioms](axiom.md). If an equivalent finite theory existed, [compactness](compactness-theorem.md) would make it follow from finitely many of these conditions. A [cyclic group](cyclic-group.md) of [prime](prime-number-split.md) order larger than the finitely many integers mentioned satisfies those conditions but has [torsion](torsion-subgroup.md), a contradiction.
