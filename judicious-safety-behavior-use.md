# Judicious safety-behavior use

↑ **Parent:** [Safety behavior](safety-behavior.md)

Use of a precaution in a way intended to support engagement while preserving useful [learning](learning.md). Its value cannot be settled from its label; prediction testing, attribution of safety, replacement precautions and longer-term transfer should be measured. An undergraduate contamination experiment showed why reduction in [symptoms](symptom.md) need not be logically impossible in the presence of a [safety behavior](safety-behavior.md).[Journal of Behavior Therapy and Experimental Psychiatry 42, 397–404; 80 undergraduates, exposure with or without hygienic wipes, two-session analogue](reducing-contamination-by-exposure-plus-safety-behaviour-by-stanley-rachman-and-others.md)
