# Hecke operator on lattice functions

↑ **Parent:** [Lattice model of a modular form](lattice-model-of-a-modular-form.md)

One standard normalization is

$$
(T_nF)(\Lambda)=\frac1n\sum_{\Lambda'\supset\Lambda\atop[\Lambda':\Lambda]=n}F(\Lambda').
$$

With [lattice model of a modular form](lattice-model-of-a-modular-form.md) homogeneity $F(uL)=u^{-k}F(L)$, the factor $1/n$ gives the usual weight-$k$ [Hecke operator](hecke-operator.md). For prime $p$, the overlattices contribute $f((\tau+b)/p)$ and $p^kf(p\tau)$; division by $p$ produces the standard Fourier coefficients.
