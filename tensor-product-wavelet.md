# Tensor-product wavelet

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor-product wavelet basis](tensor-product-wavelet-basis.md)

A tensor-product wavelet is a product of one-dimensional [wavelets](wavelet.md) and [scaling functions](scaling-function.md), with at least one wavelet factor. In two dimensions, the three simultaneous-scale types are $\psi\otimes\varphi$, $\varphi\otimes\psi$ and $\psi\otimes\psi$. Their translates and normalized dilates form a [tensor-product wavelet basis](tensor-product-wavelet-basis.md).
