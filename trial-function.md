# Trial function

↑ **Parent:** [Rayleigh-Ritz method](rayleigh-ritz-method.md)

A trial function is a chosen candidate used to approximate a solution or evaluate a [variational principle](variational-principle.md). For an [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) upper bound from a [Rayleigh quotient](rayleigh-quotient.md), it must be nonzero, have finite numerator and denominator, and lie in the appropriate variational domain. In a [Dirichlet problem](dirichlet-problem.md) it satisfies the fixed endpoint values. Natural boundary conditions need not always be imposed on a trial in the quadratic-form domain, but a candidate violating an essential [boundary condition](boundary-condition.md) does not give the stated bound.
