# Contraction semigroup

↑ **Parent:** [C0-semigroup](c0-semigroup.md)

A contraction semigroup is a [strongly continuous semigroup](c0-semigroup.md) of bounded [linear operators](linear-operator.md) satisfying $\|S(t)\|\leq1$ for every $t\geq0$. The [Hille-Yosida theorem](hille-yosida-theorem.md) characterizes its [infinitesimal generator of a semigroup](infinitesimal-generator-of-a-semigroup.md) by dense domain, closedness, and the [resolvent](resolvent-of-an-operator.md) estimates $\|(\lambda-A)^{-n}\|\leq\lambda^{-n}$ for real $\lambda>0$ and integers $n\geq1$.
