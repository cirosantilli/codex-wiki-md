↑ **Parent:** [HNN extension](hnn-extension.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baumslag–Solitar_group)

The Baumslag-Solitar group is

$$
\operatorname{BS}(m,n)=\langle a,t\mid ta^mt^{-1}=a^n\rangle.
$$
