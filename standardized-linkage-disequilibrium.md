# Standardized linkage disequilibrium

↑ **Parent:** [Linkage disequilibrium](linkage-disequilibrium.md)

For positive [linkage disequilibrium](linkage-disequilibrium.md) $D$, take $D_{\max}=\min(p_A(1-p_B),(1-p_A)p_B)$; for negative $D$, take $D_{\max}=\min(p_Ap_B,(1-p_A)(1-p_B))$ and retain its negative sign in $D'$. Thus $|D'|\le1$. Its magnitude measures the fraction of the largest disequilibrium possible at the specified allele frequencies. High $|D'|$ need not imply high [allelic correlation](allelic-correlation.md), so it does not alone guarantee good marker prediction.
