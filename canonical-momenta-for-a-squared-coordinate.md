# Canonical momenta for a squared coordinate

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic cotangent lift with a closed momentum shift](symplectic-cotangent-lift-with-a-closed-momentum-shift.md)

On either branch $q_1\ne0$, all local [canonical momenta](canonical-momentum.md) for these coordinates have the form $P_1=(p_1-p_2)/(2q_1)+\partial_{Q_1}S$ and $P_2=p_2+\partial_{Q_2}S$, with arbitrary smooth $S(Q_1,Q_2)$. The mixed coordinate–[momentum](momentum-split.md) brackets fix the linear [momentum](momentum-split.md) terms, while the [momentum](momentum-split.md)–[momentum](momentum-split.md) bracket requires the remaining shift one-form to be closed. It is locally a [gradient](gradient.md). The transformation is singular at $q_1=0$ and is not globally one-to-one across both signs of $q_1$.
