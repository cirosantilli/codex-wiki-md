# Neutron-induced fission threshold

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear fission](nuclear-fission.md)

In a simple compound-nucleus picture, compare the [neutron-capture excitation energy](neutron-capture-excitation-energy.md) to the [fission barrier](fission-barrier.md) of the capture product. If its [neutron separation energy](neutron-separation-energy.md) already exceeds the barrier, slow-neutron capture can lead to prompt fission without a positive kinetic-energy threshold. If it lies below, finite neutron kinetic energy is needed for appreciable over-barrier fission; sub-barrier tunnelling and detailed resonance widths soften a literal sharp-threshold picture.
