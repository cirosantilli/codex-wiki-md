# Tensor product of Lie algebra representations

↑ **Parent:** [Lie algebra representation](lie-algebra-representation.md)

The [tensor product](tensor-product.md) of [Lie algebra representations](lie-algebra-representation.md) has action $x(v\otimes w)=xv\otimes w+v\otimes xw$. The [Lie algebra representation](lie-algebra-representation.md) identity follows because operators on the two different tensor factors commute. This action descends to [exterior powers](exterior-power.md) and [symmetric powers](symmetric-power.md).
