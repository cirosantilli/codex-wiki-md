# Measurable cardinal

↑ **Parent:** [Large cardinal](large-cardinal.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Measurable_cardinal)

An uncountable cardinal $\kappa$ is measurable when it carries a nonprincipal $\kappa$-complete ultrafilter.
