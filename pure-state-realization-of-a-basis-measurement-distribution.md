# Pure-state realization of a basis measurement distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Born rule](born-rule.md)

For an [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) $\{|e_j\rangle\}$ and nonnegative numbers $p_j$ summing to one, the displayed [vector](vector.md) is a [unit vector](unit-vector.md) and gives measurement [probabilities](probability.md) $p_j$ by the [Born rule](born-rule.md). Conversely, every [pure state](pure-state.md) with those [probabilities](probability.md) has coefficients of these magnitudes, with arbitrary phases on the nonzero coefficients. A common change of all phases is a [global phase](global-phase.md); differences between them are [relative phases](relative-quantum-phase.md) that can affect measurements in another [basis](basis.md). Zero-probability coefficients vanish, so their phases have no effect.
