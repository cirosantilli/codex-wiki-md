# Logarithmic metallicity distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Metallicity distribution function](metallicity-distribution-function.md)

Changing from linear [stellar metallicity](stellar-metallicity.md) to its logarithm multiplies the [metallicity distribution function](metallicity-distribution-function.md) by its [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md). A flat distribution in linear metallicity rises exponentially in logarithmic metallicity. The commonly used iron abundance coordinate is equivalent to total-metallicity scaling only if relative element abundances are fixed.
