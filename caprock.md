# Caprock

↑ **Parent:** [Geology](geology.md)

A low-[porous permeability](permeability-of-a-porous-medium.md) sealing layer that impedes fluid escape from a reservoir. Seal effectiveness depends on continuity, connected fractures, [capillary entry pressure](capillary-entry-pressure.md) and [effective stress](effective-stress.md), rather than simply the geological name of the layer.
