# Stopped inverse radial power martingale classification

↑ **Parent:** [Bessel power local martingale](bessel-power-local-martingale.md)

For [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) started at radius $x>0$, the displayed process is a [continuous local martingale](continuous-local-martingale.md). It is a bounded true [martingale](martingale-split.md) when $0<a\leq x$, but a [strict local martingale](strict-local-martingale.md) when $a=0$ or $a>x$. In the latter case the boundary is outside the starting point, so stopping does not remove the singularity at the origin. Brownian scaling gives $\mathbb E\|B_t\|^{2-d}=O(t^{-(d-2)/2})$; when $a>x$, the stopped expectation tends to $a^{2-d}<x^{2-d}$.
