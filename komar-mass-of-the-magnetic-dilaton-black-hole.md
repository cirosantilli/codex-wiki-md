# Komar mass of the magnetic dilaton black hole

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetically charged dilaton black hole](magnetically-charged-dilaton-black-hole.md)

For the [magnetically charged dilaton black hole](magnetically-charged-dilaton-black-hole.md), the metric-dual one-form of $k=\partial_t$ is $k^\flat=-Vdt$. With orientation $dt\wedge dr\wedge d\theta\wedge d\phi$, its [Hodge star operator](hodge-star-operator.md) gives $\star dk^\flat=-V'R^2\sin\theta\,d\theta\wedge d\phi$. Therefore the [Komar mass](komar-mass.md) is $R^2V'/2=r_+/2-P^2/r$. Its radial dependence is allowed because the [Ricci tensor](ricci-tensor.md) is nonzero; the divergence of $dk^\flat$ is sourced by $R_{ab}k^b$. At the horizon $M_K(r_+)=(r_+-r_-)/2=\kappa A_H/(4\pi)$.
