# Sections of the degree-one line bundle on the projective line

↑ **Parent:** [Holomorphic line bundle](holomorphic-line-bundle.md)

A global [holomorphic section](holomorphic-section.md) of $\mathcal O(1)$ on $\mathbb{CP}^1$ is a linear homogeneous polynomial in the two projective coordinates. On one patch its coefficient is an entire [function](function-split.md) $p(\lambda)$; regularity on the other patch says $p(\lambda)/\lambda$ is regular at infinity. Hence $p$ has at most linear growth. [Cauchy estimates](cauchy-estimate.md) make its second and higher [derivatives](derivative.md) zero, so $p=a+b\lambda$. The same argument entrywise applies to matrix-valued sections and makes the logarithmic [derivative](derivative.md) in the [Penrose-Ward correspondence](penrose-ward-correspondence.md) linear in the primed [two-component spinor](two-component-spinor.md).
