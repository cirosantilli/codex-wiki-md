# Globus pallidus

↑ **Parent:** [Basal ganglia](basal-ganglia.md)

A component of the [basal ganglia](basal-ganglia.md). Bilateral pallidal injury has been associated with severe disturbances of spontaneous activity, though clinical [brain lesions](brain-lesion.md) and toxic exposures are not anatomically selective experiments.
