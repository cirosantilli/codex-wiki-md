# Affine upper envelope

↑ **Parent:** [Compact metrizable convex set](compact-metrizable-convex-set.md)

For a bounded real [function](function-split.md) on a compact convex set, this envelope is the infimum of its continuous affine majorants. It is finite, concave and [upper semicontinuous](upper-semicontinuity.md), and dominates the [function](function-split.md). For continuous [functions](function-split.md) it describes the maximal integral among [probability measures](probability-measure.md) with a specified [barycenter](barycenter.md). Its homogeneity, subadditivity and affine-translation identities give the [supporting measure lemma for affine upper envelopes](supporting-measure-lemma-for-affine-upper-envelopes.md).
