# Barium titanate

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical compound](chemical-compound.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barium_titanate)

An oxide with a [perovskite structure](perovskite-structure.md). Near room temperature its tetragonal phase has switchable [electric polarization](polarization-density.md) and is [ferroelectric](ferroelectricity.md). Its higher-temperature cubic phase is [paraelectric](paraelectricity.md).
