# MSc in mathematical sciences course of the University of Oxford

↑ **Parent:** [Master's course of the University of Oxford](master-s-course-of-the-university-of-oxford.md)

[Official course description](https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/graduate/courses/msc-mathematical-sciences).

Six to eight taught units from the Part C [mathematics](mathematics-split.md) and [statistics](statistics.md) menus. Shared branches include [Category theory course of the University of Oxford](category-theory-course-of-the-university-of-oxford.md), [Algebraic topology course of the University of Oxford](algebraic-topology-course-of-the-university-of-oxford.md), [model theory](model-theory-split.md) and [elliptic curves](elliptic-curve.md). [Current departmental catalogue](https://courses.maths.ox.ac.uk/). Compulsory dissertation worth two units.
