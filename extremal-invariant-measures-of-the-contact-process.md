# Extremal invariant measures of the contact process

↑ **Parent:** [Contact process](contact-process.md)

The [graphical representation of the contact process](graphical-representation-of-the-contact-process.md) preserves coordinatewise order. Its law from the all-infected state decreases in [stochastic domination](stochastic-domination-of-probability-measures.md) with time, and its limit is invariant by the [Feller semigroup](feller-semigroup.md) property. The all-healthy state is absorbing. Any invariant probability law lies between these two laws. [Duality of the contact process](duality-of-the-contact-process.md) gives $\overline\nu(\eta(x)=1)=\theta(\lambda)$ when the recovery rate is one. Consequently the two extremal laws coincide exactly when the [survival probability of the contact process](survival-probability-of-the-contact-process.md) is zero.
