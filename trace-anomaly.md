# Trace anomaly

↑ **Parent:** [Anomaly (physics)](anomaly-physics.md)

A [trace anomaly](trace-anomaly.md) is a quantum contribution to the [trace](matrix-trace.md) of a [stress-energy tensor](stress-energy-tensor-split.md) which was traceless [on shell](on-shell.md) in the classical theory. Running [renormalized couplings](renormalized-coupling.md) can supply such a contribution through their [beta functions](beta-function.md); curvature-dependent contributions can occur as well. Classical [stress-energy tensor improvement](stress-energy-tensor-improvement.md) alone does not remove quantum scale dependence.
