# PTBP1

↑ **Parent:** [PTBP protein](ptbp-protein.md)

A polypyrimidine-tract-binding [protein](protein-split.md) with four [RNA recognition motifs](rna-recognition-motif.md). Its splicing effects depend on site position, [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) geometry and other [proteins](protein-split.md); it is not exclusively a repressor.
