# Free-surface pressure condition of an incompressible disk

↑ **Parent:** [Axisymmetric waves of a homogeneous incompressible disk](axisymmetric-waves-of-a-homogeneous-incompressible-disk.md)

Vertical [hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) gives $p_0=\rho\Omega_z^2(H^2-z^2)/2$. At a vacuum interface, the [Lagrangian pressure perturbation](lagrangian-pressure-perturbation.md) vanishes: $p'+\xi_z\partial_zp_0=0$. The vertical equation gives $\xi_z=p'_z/(\rho\omega^2)$, hence

$$
p'-\frac{\Omega_z^2}{\omega^2}zp'_z=0\quad\text{at }z=\pm H.
$$

The moving interface makes this different from setting the [Eulerian perturbation of a fluid variable](eulerian-perturbation-of-a-fluid-variable.md) $p'$ to zero on the original surface.
