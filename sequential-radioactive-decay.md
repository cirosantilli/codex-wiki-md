# Sequential radioactive decay

↑ **Parent:** [Radioactive decay](radioactive-decay.md)

In a chain $N_1\to N_2\to N_3$ with distinct decay constants, the [Bateman equation](bateman-equation.md) expresses each population as a [linear combination](linear-combination.md) of the [exponential functions](exponential-function.md) $e^{-\lambda_i t}$. When two consecutive constants coalesce to $\lambda$, the repeated [Laplace transform](laplace-transform.md) pole produces a term proportional to $t e^{-\lambda t}$.
