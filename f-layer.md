# F layer

↑ **Parent:** [Ionosphere](ionosphere.md)

The F layer is a higher main region of the [ionosphere](ionosphere.md), above the [E layer](e-layer.md). Its different [electron density](electron-density.md) and persistence can permit [skywave](skywave.md) propagation when a probing [radio wave](radio-wave.md) passes through the lower [E layer](e-layer.md).
