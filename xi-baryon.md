# Xi baryon

↑ **Parent:** [Baryon](baryon.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xi_baryon)

The [baryon octet](baryon-octet.md) contains the [isospin](isospin.md) doublet $\Xi^0,\Xi^-$. Each has [strangeness](strangeness.md) $-2$, with valence [quark](quark.md) content $uss$, $dss$.
