# Fredholm lacunary series

↑ **Parent:** [Mahler method](mahler-method.md)

This lacunary series is analytic for $|z|<1$ and satisfies $f_l(z^l)=f_l(z)-z$. At roots of unity of $l$-power order, its radial tail diverges, giving a dense set of singularities on the unit circle and excluding algebraicity as a function. The [Mahler method](mahler-method.md) proves transcendence of its values at every algebraic $z$ with $0<|z|<1$. At $z=1/2$, reduced truncations have denominators $2^{l^m}$ and error less than twice their $-l$th power; for $l\geq3$, the [Roth theorem](roth-s-theorem.md) gives a shorter proof.
