# Daily mean solar irradiance

↑ **Parent:** [Solar constant](solar-constant.md)

At latitude $\phi$ and solar declination $\delta$, integrate $S_0\max(0,\sin\phi\sin\delta+\cos\phi\cos\delta\cos H)$ over hour angle $H$. Sunset occurs at $H_0=\arccos(-\tan\phi\tan\delta)$ when this lies in $[0,\pi]$, with clipping for polar day or night. The displayed daily mean is at the top of the atmosphere; local absorbed [heat flux](heat-flux-density.md) also includes transmission and [surface albedo](surface-albedo.md).
