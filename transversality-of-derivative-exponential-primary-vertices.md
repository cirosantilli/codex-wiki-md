# Transversality of derivative-exponential primary vertices

↑ **Parent:** [Primary field](primary-field.md)

For free embedding bosons, the [operator product expansion](operator-product-expansion.md) of the stress tensor with $\zeta_\mu:\partial X^\mu e^{ipX}:$ has a cubic pole $-i\alpha'(p\cdot\zeta):e^{ipX}:/2$. Its vanishing is precisely the primary condition, with weight $1+\alpha'p^2/4$. The two-derivative closed-string vertex has the two displayed transversality conditions from its holomorphic and antiholomorphic cubic poles. Being primary does not require $p^2=0$; that extra condition follows when a physical integrated vertex must have [conformal weights](conformal-weight.md) $(1,1)$.
