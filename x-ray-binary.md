# X-ray binary

↑ **Parent:** [Binary star](binary-star.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/X-ray_binary)

An X-ray binary has a compact [mass-gaining star](mass-gaining-star.md) or [black hole](black-hole-split.md) accreting from a companion, so that much of its emitted power appears as [X-rays](x-ray.md). Gravitational binding energy supplies an [accretion luminosity](accretion-luminosity.md) of order $GM\dot M/R$ when there is a surface or an appropriate inner disk radius. The [donor star](donor-star.md) may feed an [accretion disk](accretion-disk.md) through [Roche-lobe overflow](roche-lobe-overflow.md) or a [stellar wind](stellar-wind.md).
