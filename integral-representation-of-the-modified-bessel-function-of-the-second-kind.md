# Integral representation of the modified Bessel function of the second kind

↑ **Parent:** [Modified Bessel function of the second kind](modified-bessel-function-of-the-second-kind.md)

For real $x>0$ and $\nu$, one useful representation is

$$
K_\nu(x)=\frac12\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}e^{-x\cosh t+\nu t}\,dt.
$$

It turns large-order asymptotics into a [Laplace integral](laplace-integral.md) with a moving maximum.
