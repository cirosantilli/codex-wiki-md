# Existence of minimal primes over a proper ideal

↑ **Parent:** [Minimal prime ideal](minimal-prime-ideal.md)

Every proper [ideal](ideal.md) of a unital commutative [ring](ring.md) is contained in a minimal prime over that [ideal](ideal.md). A [maximal ideal](maximal-ideal.md) supplies a prime containing it, and an intersection of a decreasing chain of primes is still prime. [Zorn's lemma](zorn-s-lemma.md), with reverse inclusion, therefore supplies a minimal member. No [Noetherian](noetherian-ring.md) assumption is needed.
