# Photon statistics of a squeezed vacuum

↑ **Parent:** [Optical squeezed vacuum](optical-squeezed-vacuum.md)

The [single-mode squeeze operator](single-mode-squeeze-operator.md) changes occupation in pairs. Consequently an [optical squeezed vacuum](optical-squeezed-vacuum.md) has only even [photon numbers](photon-number.md), with mean $s=\sinh^2r$ and [variance](variance-split.md) $2s(s+1)$. Its large number fluctuations are compatible with [quadrature squeezing](quadrature-squeezing.md): they concern different observables.
