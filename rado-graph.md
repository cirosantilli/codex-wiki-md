# Rado graph

↑ **Parent:** [Random graph](random-graph.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rado_graph)

The Rado graph is the unique countably infinite [graph](graph-split.md) with the two-uniform [hypergraph extension property](hypergraph-extension-property.md). Equivalently, for any disjoint finite vertex sets $U,W$, some new vertex is adjacent to every member of $U$ and no member of $W$. The [back-and-forth method](back-and-forth-method.md) proves uniqueness. Independent edge sampling on the [natural numbers](natural-number.md) with any fixed $p\in(0,1)$ produces this graph [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md). It contains both edges and nonedges and therefore is not a [complete graph](complete-graph.md). Its first-order axioms constitute the [theory of the random graph](theory-of-the-random-graph.md).
