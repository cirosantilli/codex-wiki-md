# Symmetric polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial](polynomial-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symmetric_polynomial)

A symmetric polynomial is unchanged by permutations of its variables. The [Fundamental theorem of symmetric polynomials](fundamental-theorem-of-symmetric-polynomials.md) expresses it as a polynomial in the [elementary symmetric polynomials](elementary-symmetric-polynomial.md). The square of the [Vandermonde determinant](vandermonde-determinant.md) is an important example.
