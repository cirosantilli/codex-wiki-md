# Hahn decomposition

↑ **Parent:** [Signed measure](signed-measure.md)

A [Hahn decomposition](hahn-decomposition.md) of a [signed measure](signed-measure.md) partitions its space into [measurable sets](measurable-set.md) $P,N$ such that every measurable subset of $P$ has nonnegative [measure](measure-split.md) and every measurable subset of $N$ has nonpositive [measure](measure-split.md). For finite [total variation measure](variation-measure.md), set $\mu^+=(|\mu|+\mu)/2$ and choose a $\mu^+$-[null set](null-set.md) $N$ maximizing $|\mu|(N)$: a [countable union](countable-union.md) of approximating [null sets](null-set.md) attains the [supremum](supremum.md). If some subset of its complement has negative signed mass, successively remove disjoint subsets with positive mass at least half the [supremum](supremum.md) still available. Their masses tend to zero because their sum is bounded by total variation. The remainder has no positive-[measure](measure-split.md) subset, has negative mass, and is $\mu^+$-null, contradicting maximality after adjoining it to $N$. On $P$ one has $|\mu|=\mu$ and on $N$ one has $|\mu|=-\mu$. Consequently $d\mu^+/d|\mu|=\mathbf1_P$, an explicit [Radon-Nikodym derivative](radon-nikodym-derivative.md).
