# Pangaea

↑ **Parent:** [Paleogeography](paleogeography.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pangaea)

The [supercontinent](supercontinent.md) assembled by the late [Paleozoic](paleozoic.md) and fragmented during the [Mesozoic](mesozoic.md). Restored geological belts, [fossil assemblages](fossil-assemblage.md), [glacial deposits](glacial-deposit.md), [paleomagnetism](paleomagnetism.md) and the younger [seafloor spreading](seafloor-spreading.md) record provide complementary constraints on its history.
