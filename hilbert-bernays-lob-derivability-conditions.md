<h1 id="hilbert-bernays-lob-derivability-conditions">Hilbert-Bernays-Löb derivability conditions</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Arithmetical provability predicate](arithmetical-provability-predicate.md)

For an [arithmetical provability predicate](arithmetical-provability-predicate.md) write $\Box A=\operatorname{Pr}_T(\ulcorner A\urcorner)$. The conditions are: if $T\vdash A$ then $T\vdash\Box A$; $T\vdash\Box(A\to B)\to(\Box A\to\Box B)$; and $T\vdash\Box A\to\Box\Box A$. The first is an external rule, while the latter two are internal theorems. They formalize proof verification, closure under [modus ponens rule](implication-elimination-rule.md) and positive introspection. None licenses reflection from a proved sentence $\Box A$ to $A$.
