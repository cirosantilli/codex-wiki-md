# munmap

↑ **Parent:** [mmap](mmap.md)

Removes a mapping from an [operating-system process](process-computing.md)'s [address space](address-space.md). Mapping lifetime is separate from [file descriptor](file-descriptor.md) lifetime; removal also does not supply the completed writeback promised by [memory-mapped file synchronization](memory-mapped-file-synchronization.md).
