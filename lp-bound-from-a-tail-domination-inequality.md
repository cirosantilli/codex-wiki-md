# Lp bound from a tail domination inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Layer cake representation](layer-cake-representation.md)

Let $F,G\geq0$, $1<p<\infty$, and $G\in L^p$. Assume the displayed tail inequality and finite positive-threshold superlevel measures. First split $G$ at $a/2$ to obtain $a\mu\{F>2a\}\leq\int_{\{G>a\}}G$. [Layer cake representation](layer-cake-representation.md) and [Tonelli theorem](tonelli-theorem.md) imply $F\in L^p$. Integrating the original inequality against $pa^{p-2}$ then gives $\|F\|_p^p\leq q\int GF^{p-1}\leq q\|G\|_p\|F\|_p^{p-1}$, with $q=p/(p-1)$. This is the analytic estimate behind the [Doob Lp maximal inequality](doob-lp-maximal-inequality.md).
