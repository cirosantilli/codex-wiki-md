# Modular lambda function

↑ **Parent:** [Modular function](modular-function-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modular_lambda_function)

The nonzero even theta constants and the [Jacobi abstruse identity](jacobi-abstruse-identity.md) give $\lambda(-1/\tau)=1-\lambda(\tau)$ and $\lambda(\tau+1)=\lambda(\tau)/(\lambda(\tau)-1)$. It is invariant under the principal [congruence subgroup](congruence-subgroup.md) of level two. This parameter puts the associated complex [elliptic curve](elliptic-curve.md) in Legendre form $y^2=x(x-1)(x-\lambda)$; its [Klein j-invariant](klein-j-invariant.md) is $256(1-\lambda+\lambda^2)^3/[\lambda^2(1-\lambda)^2]$.
