# Burn-in

↑ **Parent:** [Markov chain Monte Carlo](markov-chain-monte-carlo.md)

The initial segment of a [Markov chain Monte Carlo](markov-chain-monte-carlo.md) run discarded to reduce dependence on its starting state. A fixed discarded count is a choice to assess using [Markov chain Monte Carlo convergence diagnostics](markov-chain-monte-carlo-convergence-diagnostics.md), not a proof that the retained sample has reached its [stationary distribution](stationary-distribution.md).
