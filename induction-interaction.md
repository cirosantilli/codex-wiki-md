# Induction interaction

↑ **Parent:** [Intermolecular force](intermolecular-force.md)

An existing charge distribution polarizes a neighbouring molecule, changing its [electron density](electron-density.md) and interaction energy. The resulting interaction depends on the molecular [polarizability](electric-polarizability.md) and local [electric field](electric-field-split.md).
