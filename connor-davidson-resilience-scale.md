# Connor-Davidson Resilience Scale

↑ **Parent:** [Measurement of psychological resilience](measurement-of-psychological-resilience.md)

A [self-report](self-report.md) measure of [coping](coping.md)-related qualities developed by [Kathryn M. Connor](kathryn-m-connor.md) and [Jonathan R. T. Davidson](jonathan-r-t-davidson.md). The original version has 25 items scored from zero to four. [Measurement reliability](measurement-reliability.md) and treatment-associated change concern the measured [self-reports](self-report.md); they do not substitute for prospective evidence of adaptation to a documented [adversity](adversity.md).[2003, original scale and validation](development-of-a-new-resilience-scale-by-kathryn-m-connor-and-jonathan-r-t-davidson.md)
