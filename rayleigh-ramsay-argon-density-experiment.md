# Rayleigh-Ramsay argon density experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Argon](argon.md)

In his 1895 lecture, [Lord Rayleigh](john-william-strutt-3rd-baron-rayleigh.md) compared [gas](gas.md) masses in a globe under matched [pressure](pressure-split.md) and [temperature](temperature-split.md). Removing [oxygen](oxygen.md) from air left gas about half a percent heavier than [nitrogen](nitrogen.md) prepared entirely from chemical sources. Several preparation routes reproduced the difference; [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) contamination was a concern. An eight-month storage test re-established the filling conditions: simply reweighing a sealed globe could not test a change in [molar mass](molar-mass.md). [William Ramsay](william-ramsay.md) concentrated the residual gas by circulating the mixture over hot [magnesium](magnesium.md), which consumed [nitrogen](nitrogen.md). A spectral test checked remaining [nitrogen](nitrogen.md). Agreement of spectra from this route and the alternative spark-and-alkali separation supported a common residual gas.[Argon, Royal Institution Proceedings 14 (1895), 524–538: gas-weight table; storage test; magnesium circulation; nitrogen spectrum test and comparison of isolation routes](argon-by-lord-rayleigh.md)

Original functional diagram of the [Rayleigh-Ramsay argon density experiment](rayleigh-ramsay-argon-density-experiment.md), not a scale reconstruction. The weighing comparison and subsequent chemical separation answer different questions. The [gas density](gas-density.md) comparison requires common [pressure](pressure-split.md) and [temperature](temperature-split.md); the hot-[magnesium](magnesium.md) treatment concentrates the unconsumed component. The diagram shows operations, not a laboratory construction plan.

The density difference alone identifies neither a unique [chemical element](chemical-element.md) nor its concentration. A heavier contaminant, a lighter reference contaminant, or unmatched filling conditions can all shift a weighing. A [separation process](separation-process.md) and an [optical spectrum](optical-spectrum.md) provide additional observations. This combination makes the experiment powerful: the anomaly motivates another operation whose result can be checked independently.
