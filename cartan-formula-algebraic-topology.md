# Cartan formula (algebraic topology)

↑ **Parent:** [Steenrod algebra](steenrod-algebra.md)

The Cartan product formula says that total [Steenrod squares](steenrod-square.md) and total [Steenrod reduced powers](steenrod-reduced-power.md) preserve the [cup product](cup-product.md):

$$
\operatorname{Sq}^k(ab)=\sum_{i+j=k}\operatorname{Sq}^i(a)\operatorname{Sq}^j(b),
\qquad
P^k(ab)=\sum_{i+j=k}P^i(a)P^j(b).
$$

It is named after Henri Cartan and is distinct from [Cartan's magic formula](cartan-s-magic-formula.md) for the [Lie derivative of a differential form](lie-derivative-of-a-differential-form.md), named after Élie Cartan.
