# Monodromy reflection at a square-root branch point

↑ **Parent:** [Branch point](branch-point.md)

Let

$$
W(z)=\int_{z_0}^z\frac{q(t)}{\sqrt{P(t)}}\,dt,
$$

where $P$ has a simple zero at $a$, and let $A$ be the limiting value of $W$ at $a$ on one sheet. [Analytic continuation](analytic-continuation.md) once around $a$ changes the sign of the square root and hence of $W'$. The continued primitive agrees at $a$ with the original one, so it is

$$
W\longmapsto 2A-W.
$$
