<h1 id="zeckendorf-s-theorem">Zeckendorf's theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Fibonacci number](fibonacci-number.md)

Every [positive integer](positive-integer.md) is a unique sum of nonconsecutive [Fibonacci numbers](fibonacci-number.md), using the sequence $1,2,3,5,8,\ldots$ with one copy of $1$. The greedy procedure selects the largest allowable [Fibonacci number](fibonacci-number.md) and repeats on the remainder.
