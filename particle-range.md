# Particle range

↑ **Parent:** [Particle detection](particle-detection-split.md)

A [particle range](particle-range.md) is the distance a [charged particle](charged-particle.md) travels through a specified material before stopping; it depends on [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md), particle identity, and the material.
