# Relative product in topological K-theory

↑ **Parent:** [Relative topological K-theory](relative-topological-k-theory.md)

The [relative topological K-theory](relative-topological-k-theory.md) product

$$
K^i(X,A)\otimes K^j(X,A)\longrightarrow K^{i+j}(X,A)
$$

is induced by the reduced diagonal $X/A\to(X/A)\wedge(X/A)$. If $X/A$ is a positive-dimensional [sphere](sphere.md), this diagonal is [null-homotopic](null-homotopic-map.md), so the kernel of $K^0(X)\to K^0(A)$ is square-zero.
