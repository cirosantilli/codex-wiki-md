<h1 id="craig-s-theorem">Craig's theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical logic](mathematical-logic-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Craig's_theorem)

[Craig's theorem](craig-s-theorem.md) says that a theory with a computably enumerable axiomatization has an equivalent decidable axiomatization. The [Craig trick](craig-trick.md) gives a constructive proof by encoding an enumeration index in repeated conjunctions of the enumerated axiom.
