# Double pendulum with an end-to-end spring

↑ **Parent:** [Double pendulum](double-pendulum.md)

With equal rod lengths $\ell$ and absolute downward-vertical [angles](angle.md) $\theta,\phi$, the endpoint separation is $s^2=2\ell^2[1+\cos(\theta-\phi)]$. A [zero-rest-length spring](zero-rest-length-spring.md) between the fixed support and the final [mass](mass.md) contributes $-\kappa\ell^2[1+\cos(\theta-\phi)]$ to the [Lagrangian](lagrangian.md). It favours bending away from maximum extension and can reduce the gravitational bending stiffness.
