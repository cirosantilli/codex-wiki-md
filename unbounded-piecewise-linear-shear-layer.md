# Unbounded piecewise-linear shear layer

↑ **Parent:** [Inviscid parallel shear flow](inviscid-parallel-shear-flow.md)

A uniform-density inviscid [shear flow](shear-flow.md) with $U=U_0z/d$ for $|z|<d$ and $U=\pm U_0$ outside has two [vorticity](vorticity.md) jumps but no walls. Decaying [normal modes](normal-mode.md) satisfy $w''-k^2w=0$ in each region. Continuity of $w$ and [pressure matching at a piecewise-linear shear interface](pressure-matching-at-a-piecewise-linear-shear-interface.md) gives a two-by-two determinant whose diagonal entries are $1-2kd\pm2kd\,c/U_0$ and off-diagonal entries $e^{-2kd}$. Its vanishing gives the displayed [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation.md). The [Kelvin-Helmholtz instability](kelvin-helmholtz-instability.md) band is $0<kd<\alpha_s$, where $2\alpha_s-1=e^{-2\alpha_s}$ has its unique positive root above $1/2$, approximately $0.63923227$. Short waves decouple the two edges and remain neutral; long waves approach the [vortex sheet](vortex-sheet.md) instability with $c=\pm iU_0$.
