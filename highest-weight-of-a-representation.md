# Highest weight of a representation

↑ **Parent:** [Highest-weight representation](highest-weight-representation.md)

The highest weight of a highest-weight representation is the weight of a generating vector annihilated by every positive-root operator. Finite-dimensional irreducible representations of a complex semisimple Lie algebra are classified by dominant integral highest weights.
