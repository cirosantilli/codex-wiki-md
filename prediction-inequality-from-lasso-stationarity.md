# Prediction inequality from Lasso stationarity

↑ **Parent:** [Basic inequality for the Lasso](basic-inequality-for-the-lasso.md)

For [Lasso](lasso.md) loss $\|Y-X\beta\|_2^2/(2n)+\lambda\|\beta\|_1$ and $Y=X\beta^0+\eta$, stationarity with a penalty [subgradient](subgradient.md) gives $\|X\delta\|_2^2/n\leq\eta^TX\delta/n+\lambda(\|\beta^0\|_1-\|\widehat\beta\|_1)$, $\delta=\widehat\beta-\beta^0$. This follows by multiplying the [Karush-Kuhn-Tucker conditions](karush-kuhn-tucker-conditions.md) by $\delta$ and using $z^T\widehat\beta=\|\widehat\beta\|_1$ and $z^T\beta^0\leq\|\beta^0\|_1$.
