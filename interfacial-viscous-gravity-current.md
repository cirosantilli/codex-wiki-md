# Interfacial viscous gravity current

↑ **Parent:** [Gravity current](gravity-current.md)

A viscous current with density between those of two ambient fluids spreads along their interface. Vertical buoyancy divides its thickness above and below the undisturbed interface. When its internal shear and in-plane extension are both negligible, resistance from the two ambient [Stokes flows](stokes-flow-split.md) controls its spreading.
