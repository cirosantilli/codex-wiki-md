# Symmetric-top rotational spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Rotational spectroscopy](rotational-spectroscopy.md)

For a polar rigid [symmetric top](symmetric-top.md) with its permanent dipole on the [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md) axis, electric-dipole pure rotation has $\Delta J=\pm1$, $\Delta K=0$. The $A-B$ term cancels within each transition; [centrifugal distortion](centrifugal-distortion.md) can lift the rigid-model coincidence of different K components.
