# One-dimensional extension of a rational-linear map

↑ **Parent:** [Rational-linear map](rational-linear-map.md)

Let $D$ be a rational [vector subspace](vector-subspace.md) of the [real numbers](real-number.md) and let $t\notin D$. Every element of $D+\mathbb Qt$ has a unique expression $d+qt$. For any prescribed [real number](real-number.md) $a$, the rule $h(d+qt)=f(d)+qa$ extends a [rational-linear map](rational-linear-map.md) $f$ on $D$ and remains rational-linear. The uniqueness follows because a nonzero rational multiple of $t$ cannot lie in $D$.
