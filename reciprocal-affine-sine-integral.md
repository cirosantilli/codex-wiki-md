# Reciprocal affine sine integral

↑ **Parent:** [Contour integration](contour-integration.md)

For real $a>b>0$, put $d=\sqrt{a^2-b^2}$. The [change of variables](change-of-variables-formula.md) $z=e^{i\theta}$ changes the integral to $\oint_{|z|=1}(2/b)\,dz/[z^2+2i(a/b)z-1]$. The roots are $-i(a-d)/b$ and $-i(a+d)/b$, with only the first inside the circle because $a-d=b^2/(a+d)$. The [residue](residue.md) there is $1/(id)$, and the [residue theorem](residue-theorem.md) proves the displayed result. A shift of the angular variable replaces sine by cosine. More generally for real $a>|b|$, the same answer follows by shifting by $\pi$ when $b<0$, and the case $b=0$ follows directly.
