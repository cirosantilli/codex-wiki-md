# Finite-energy condition for planar exterior potential flow

↑ **Parent:** [Potential flow](potential-flow.md)

For a planar [potential flow](potential-flow.md) outside a bounded body, suppose its single-valued [complex velocity](complex-velocity.md) $q=u-iv$ is holomorphic near infinity and tends to zero. Its exterior [Laurent series](laurent-series.md) begins $q=b_1/z+O(z^{-2})$. The outward [volume flux](volumetric-flow-rate.md) $Q$ and counterclockwise [circulation](circulation-physics.md) $\Gamma$ satisfy $\Gamma+iQ=2\pi i b_1$. Thus both vanish precisely when the leading term is absent. A nonzero $b_1$ gives $|q|^2\sim|b_1|^2/r^2$ and logarithmically divergent exterior [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md). When $b_1=0$, $q=O(r^{-2})$ and the far-field energy integral converges. Zero [circulation](circulation-physics.md) alone does not exclude the radial source field $\nabla\log r$.
