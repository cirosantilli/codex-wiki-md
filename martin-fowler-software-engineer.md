# Martin Fowler (software engineer)

↑ **Parent:** [List of software engineers](list-of-software-engineers.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Fowler_(software_engineer))

A [software engineer](software-engineer.md) and author writing about [refactoring](refactoring.md) and software design. His short essay [Code Smell by Martin Fowler](code-smell-by-martin-fowler.md) explains the distinction between noticing a symptom and establishing the design problem behind it.
