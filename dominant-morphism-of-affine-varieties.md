# Dominant morphism of affine varieties

↑ **Parent:** [Coordinate ring](coordinate-ring.md)

A morphism $f:X\to Y$ of affine varieties is dominant when its image is [Zariski dense](zariski-dense-subset.md) in $Y$. It is dominant exactly when the pullback homomorphism

$$
f^*:k[Y]\to k[X]
$$

is injective. When $X$ and $Y$ are irreducible, this injection extends to their function fields and gives $\dim X\geq\dim Y$.

This is the dense-image case of a [morphism of algebraic varieties](morphism-of-algebraic-varieties.md).
