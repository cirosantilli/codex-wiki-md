# Electrocardiography

↑ **Parent:** [Biophysics](biophysics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrocardiography)

Measurement and interpretation of cardiac electrical activity using body-surface electrodes. The resulting trace is an [electrocardiogram](electrocardiogram.md); waveform timing reflects the coordinated activation and recovery of cardiac tissue.
