# Centrosome

↑ **Parent:** [Organelle](organelle.md)

A microtubule-organizing structure in relevant animal cells. Positioning centrosomes relative to a niche can prepare the orientation of a later mitotic spindle. Budding yeast instead has a [spindle pole body](spindle-pole-body.md).
