# Pressure gradient force

↑ **Parent:** [Pressure gradient](pressure-gradient.md)

A spatially varying [pressure](pressure-split.md) exerts net [force](force-split.md) per unit volume $-\nabla p$ on a small fluid element. Its contribution to [acceleration](acceleration.md) is $-\nabla p/\rho$. Pressure increases in the direction opposite to this force.
