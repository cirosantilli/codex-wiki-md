# Block-copolymer sphere phase

↑ **Parent:** [Microphase separation](microphase-separation.md)

An ordered morphology in which a minority block forms approximately spherical domains in a continuous majority-block matrix. The lattice packing and phase stability depend on segregation strength, composition and chain statistics. The label S in a [phase diagram](phase-diagram.md) denotes a morphology, not a spherical shape of the entire specimen.
