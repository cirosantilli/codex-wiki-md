# Thin-disk potential-density inversion

↑ **Parent:** [Razor-thin disk Poisson kernel](razor-thin-disk-poisson-kernel.md)

For an isolated [razor-thin disk](razor-thin-disk-approximation.md), define the two-dimensional [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) by $\widetilde g(\mathbf k)=\int e^{-i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf x}g(\mathbf x)d^2x$. At $k>0$, the [razor-thin disk Poisson kernel](razor-thin-disk-poisson-kernel.md) gives the displayed inversion. Since the transform of $1/|\mathbf x|$ is $2\pi/k$, inversion also gives $\Sigma=(4\pi^2G)^{-1}(1/|\mathbf x|)*\Delta_2\Phi$. The forward relation is $\Phi=-G(1/|\mathbf x|)*\Sigma$. An additive potential constant is irrelevant; a uniform infinite sheet and scale-free divergent potentials require separate zero-mode or potential-difference treatment rather than an arbitrary decaying boundary condition.
