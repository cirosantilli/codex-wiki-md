# Sigma-only octahedral molecular orbital model

↑ **Parent:** [Ligand field](ligand-field.md)

A model in which six donor orbitals of an octahedral complex form $a_{1g}+e_g+t_{1u}$ symmetry-adapted combinations. [metal](metal.md) s, p and two [d orbitals](d-orbital.md) supply matching symmetry partners; the other three [d orbitals](d-orbital.md) remain nonbonding $t_{2g}$ orbitals. The model neglects ligand [pi donation](pi-donation.md), backbonding and departures from octahedral symmetry.
