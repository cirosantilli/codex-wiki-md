# Late-time scalar gradient vertex integral

↑ **Parent:** [Vacuum prescription for inflationary in-in integrals](vacuum-prescription-for-inflationary-in-in-integrals.md)

For $K=k_1+k_2+k_3$, $s_2=k_1k_2+k_1k_3+k_2k_3$ and $s_3=k_1k_2k_3$, the convergent [in-in formalism](keldysh-formalism.md) integral is $I=\int_{-\infty(1-i0)}^{0^-}\tau^{-2}\prod_i(1-ic_sk_i\tau)e^{ic_sK\tau}d\tau$. Its real endpoint divergence drops out of the expectation value, but the finite imaginary endpoint term $-c_sK$ must be kept. The remaining terms contribute $c_ss_2/K+c_ss_3/K^2$. Reversing the exponential requires conjugating both the contour and the outside prefactor.
