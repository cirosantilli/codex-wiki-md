# Density of the smooth locus

↑ **Parent:** [Smooth locus of a variety](smooth-locus-of-a-variety.md)

If $X\subseteq\mathbb A^n$ is irreducible of dimension $d$, choose a nonzero $(n-d)$-rowed minor of a Jacobian matrix at one point of minimum tangent dimension. Its nonvanishing locus is a nonempty [Zariski-open set](zariski-open-set.md) on which the Jacobian has rank $n-d$, so every point there is smooth. Irreducibility makes every nonempty [Zariski-open set](zariski-open-set.md) dense.
