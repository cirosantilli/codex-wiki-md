# Deformation gradient

↑ **Parent:** [Finite strain theory](finite-strain-theory.md)

The [Jacobian matrix](jacobian-matrix.md) of a [deformation map](deformation-map.md) carries an infinitesimal reference line element to its current image. Its [determinant](determinant.md) $J=\det F$ is the local volume ratio. An orientation-preserving deformation has $J>0$, and [incompressibility](incompressible-flow.md) imposes $J=1$.
