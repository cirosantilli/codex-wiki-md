# Base change of a morphism of schemes

↑ **Parent:** [Fiber product of schemes](fiber-product-of-schemes.md)

Given $f:X\to Y$ and $g:Y'\to Y$, the base change of $f$ along $g$ is the projection

$$
X\times_YY'\longrightarrow Y'.
$$

Properties such as being [proper](proper-morphism.md) and [flat](flat-morphism.md) are stable under arbitrary base change.
