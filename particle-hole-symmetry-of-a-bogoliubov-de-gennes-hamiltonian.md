<h1 id="particle-hole-symmetry-of-a-bogoliubov-de-gennes-hamiltonian">Particle-hole symmetry of a Bogoliubov--de Gennes Hamiltonian</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Bogoliubov--de Gennes Hamiltonian](bogoliubov-de-gennes-hamiltonian.md)

Particle-hole symmetry relates $H_{\rm BdG}(k)$ to $-H_{\rm BdG}^*(-k)$. Consequently every energy $E$ at momentum $k$ is paired with energy $-E$ at momentum $-k$.
