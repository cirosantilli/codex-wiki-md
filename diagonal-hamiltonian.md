# Diagonal Hamiltonian

↑ **Parent:** [Local Hamiltonian](local-hamiltonian.md)

A diagonal Hamiltonian is diagonal in a specified product basis, usually the computational basis. Every basis state is therefore an [energy eigenstate](energy-eigenstate.md), and optimization of its diagonal entries becomes minimization of the corresponding classical cost function.
