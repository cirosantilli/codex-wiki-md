# Conducting sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Electrostatics](electrostatics-split.md)

A spherical [electrical conductor](electrical-conductor.md) has constant [electric potential](electric-potential.md) throughout its connected interior in [electrostatic equilibrium](electrostatic-equilibrium.md). For an isolated sphere in a homogeneous medium, spherical symmetry and [Gauss's law](gauss-s-law.md) give the exterior field of a point [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) at its center.
