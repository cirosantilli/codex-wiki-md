# Spectrum of a flat torus

↑ **Parent:** [Flat torus](flat-torus.md)

With the nonnegative [Laplace-Beltrami operator](laplace-beltrami-operator.md), a frequency $w$ in the [dual lattice](dual-lattice.md) gives the [eigenfunction](eigenfunction.md) $e^{2\pi i\langle w,x\rangle}$ and [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) $4\pi^2|w|^2$. Its multiplicity is the number of dual vectors with that norm. [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md) prove completeness. In dimension two the shortest vector, shortest independent vector and covolume determine a reduced [Gram matrix](gram-matrix.md), proving [spectral rigidity](spectral-rigidity.md).
