<h1 id="eliashberg-gromov-rigidity-theorem">Eliashberg–Gromov rigidity theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic capacity](symplectic-capacity.md)

A locally uniform limit of [symplectomorphisms](symplectomorphism.md) that is a smooth diffeomorphism is symplectic. By [capacity preservation under uniform limits](capacity-preservation-under-uniform-limits.md) and [linear capacity rigidity](linear-capacity-rigidity.md), its derivative is symplectic or [anti-symplectic](anti-symplectic-map.md). Repeat this argument after taking the product of every map with the identity on a symplectic plane. An anti-symplectic derivative on the original factor and a symplectic derivative on the added factor would give neither sign on the product, a contradiction. The argument uses [symplectic capacities](symplectic-capacity.md) in the original and stabilized dimensions.
