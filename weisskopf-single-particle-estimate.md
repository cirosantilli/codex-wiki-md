# Weisskopf single-particle estimate

↑ **Parent:** [Gamma decay](gamma-decay.md)

Named for [Victor Weisskopf](victor-weisskopf.md), a Weisskopf estimate is a benchmark electromagnetic nuclear transition strength based on a simple single-particle size model. It supplies a scale against which enhancement or hindrance is measured, rather than a universal rate. The partial rate contains both the photon-energy power $E_\gamma^{2L+1}$ and the actual reduced nuclear strength. Electric and magnetic multipoles at different energies cannot be universally ranked by order alone.[Appendix C, printed pp.60–67](guidelines-for-nuclear-structure-evaluators-by-murray-j-martin.md)
