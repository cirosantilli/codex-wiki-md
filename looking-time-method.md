# Looking-time method

↑ **Parent:** [Psychophysics](psychophysics.md)

An experimental method using the duration of looking as a dependent measure. A difference can reflect novelty, familiarity, surprise or processing demands, so psychological interpretation depends on the design and controls rather than on duration alone.
