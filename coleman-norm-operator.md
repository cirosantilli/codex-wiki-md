# Coleman norm operator

↑ **Parent:** [Coleman power series](coleman-power-series.md)

The determinant norm for the finite free extension $R/\varphi(R)$ defines $\mathcal N$ on units. After adjoining the p-th [roots of unity](root-of-unity.md), the norm is the displayed product of conjugates. A [Coleman power series](coleman-power-series.md) of a norm-compatible unit sequence satisfies $\mathcal Nf=f$. For symmetric cyclotomic units, the product relation follows directly from $\prod_{\xi^p=1}(1-\xi^aX^a)=1-X^{ap}$ when $p\nmid a$.
