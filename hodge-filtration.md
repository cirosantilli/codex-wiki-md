# Hodge filtration

↑ **Parent:** [Hodge structure](hodge-structure.md)

A decreasing filtration defines a [Hodge structure](hodge-structure.md) when $H_{\mathbb C}=F^p\oplus\overline{F^{w-p+1}}$ for every $p$. The decomposition is recovered as $H^{p,q}=F^p\cap\overline{F^q}$. This filtration is the holomorphic datum that varies in a [variation of Hodge structure](variation-of-hodge-structure.md).
