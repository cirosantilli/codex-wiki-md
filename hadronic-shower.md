# Hadronic shower

↑ **Parent:** [Particle shower](particle-shower.md)

A cascade of nuclear interactions and secondary [hadrons](hadron.md) produced when an energetic [hadron](hadron.md) traverses matter. Neutral-pion decay supplies an electromagnetic component. Nuclear breakup, unobserved low-energy particles and leakage affect the visible response of a hadronic [calorimeter](calorimeter.md).
