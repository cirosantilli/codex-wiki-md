# Energy identity for a J-holomorphic curve

↑ **Parent:** [Pseudoholomorphic curve](pseudoholomorphic-curve.md)

For compatible metrics on a Riemann surface and symplectic target,

$$
E(u)=\frac12\int_\Sigma|du|^2
=\int_\Sigma u^*\omega+\int_\Sigma|\bar\partial_Ju|^2.
$$

Thus a J-holomorphic curve minimizes energy among maps in its homology class.
