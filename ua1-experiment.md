# UA1 experiment

↑ **Parent:** [W and Z discovery experiments](w-and-z-discovery-experiments.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UA1_experiment)

The 1983 [UA1 experiment](ua1-experiment.md) paper used [proton](proton.md)–[antiproton](antiproton.md) collisions at $\sqrt{s}=540\,\mathrm{GeV}$. A high-[transverse momentum](transverse-momentum.md) [electron](electron.md) candidate required a track matched to an electromagnetic [particle shower](particle-shower.md), with little energy behind it in the hadronic [particle calorimeter](calorimeter-particle-physics.md). Test beams checked [electron](electron.md) and [pion](pion.md) responses; ordinary events and [particle jet](jet-particle-physics.md) samples checked the imbalance resolution. Two searches, beginning with isolated [electrons](electron.md) and with missing transverse energy, converged on the same candidates. Conversion pairs, hadrons and heavy-flavor decays were examined as backgrounds.[1983 paper, sections 2–4, 6–10, Table 1, Figs.1, 5, 8–9](experimental-observation-of-isolated-large-transverse-energy-electrons-with-associated-missing-energy-at-sqrt-s-540-gev-by-g-arnison.md)
