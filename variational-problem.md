# Variational problem

↑ **Parent:** [Calculus of variations](calculus-of-variations-split.md)

A variational problem seeks a stationary point or minimizer of a [functional](functional.md) on a specified [function space](function-space.md). For a symmetric [coercive bilinear form](coercive-bilinear-form.md) $a$ and bounded [linear functional](linear-functional.md) $\ell$ on a real [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md), minimizing $J(v)=a(v,v)/2-\ell(v)$ is equivalent to the [weak formulation](weak-formulation.md) $a(u,v)=\ell(v)$ for every test vector. The [Lax-Milgram theorem](lax-milgram-theorem.md) gives a unique solution, and $J(u+w)-J(u)=a(w,w)/2$ proves unique minimization. Specifying the admissible space and its boundary constraints is part of the problem.
