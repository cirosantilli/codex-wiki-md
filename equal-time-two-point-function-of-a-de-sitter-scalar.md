# Equal-time two-point function of a de Sitter scalar

↑ **Parent:** [Scale-invariant inflationary power spectrum](scale-invariant-inflationary-power-spectrum.md)

For a light scalar in the [Bunch-Davies vacuum](bunch-davies-vacuum.md) with metric perturbations neglected, the mode $e^{-ik\tau}(1-i/(k\tau))/\sqrt{2k}$ gives the displayed equal-time spectrum. The dimensionless power tends to $H^2/(4\pi^2)$ on [superhorizon scales](superhorizon-scale.md). The exact massless infinite-volume correlation has an infrared divergence; a finite-volume or finite-duration infrared regulator, and smearing for coincident products, makes the correlators well defined.
