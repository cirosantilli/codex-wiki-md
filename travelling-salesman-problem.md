# Travelling salesman problem

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical optimization](mathematical-optimization-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Travelling_salesman_problem)

Given finitely many locations and pairwise travel costs, find a cyclic visiting order minimizing the sum of its costs. For [Euclidean distance](euclidean-distance.md) this becomes the [Euclidean travelling salesman tour](euclidean-travelling-salesman-tour.md) problem.
