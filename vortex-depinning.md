# Vortex depinning

↑ **Parent:** [Flux pinning](flux-pinning.md)

Motion of a previously pinned [Abrikosov vortex](abrikosov-vortex.md) when drive, thermal activation or other fluctuations overcome its trapping. The resulting flux motion can generate an [electric field](electric-field-split.md), so a transport critical current depends on the measurement's voltage and time criteria.
