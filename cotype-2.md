# Cotype 2

↑ **Parent:** [Cotype of a Banach space](cotype-of-a-banach-space.md)

This is [cotype of a Banach space](cotype-of-a-banach-space.md) with $q=2$. The [Lp space](lp-space.md) $L^1$ has cotype 2 with constant at most $\sqrt2$: the triangle inequality for the Euclidean norm gives $(\sum\|f_j\|_1^2)^{1/2}\le\int(\sum|f_j(t)|^2)^{1/2}\,dt$, and the [sharp Rademacher second-moment inequality](sharp-rademacher-second-moment-inequality.md), followed by [Fubini theorem](fubini-s-theorem.md), bounds this by $\sqrt2\,\mathbb E\|\sum\varepsilon_jf_j\|_1$.
