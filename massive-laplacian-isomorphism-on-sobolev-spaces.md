# Massive Laplacian isomorphism on Sobolev spaces

↑ **Parent:** [Sobolev space](sobolev-space-split.md)

For every real $s$,

$$
-\Delta+1:H^{s+2}(\mathbb R^n)\to H^s(\mathbb R^n)
$$

is an isomorphism. Its inverse is the Fourier multiplier $(1+|\xi|^2)^{-1}$.
