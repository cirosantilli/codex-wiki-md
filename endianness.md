# Endianness

↑ **Parent:** [Computer memory](computer-memory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endianness)

The ordering of the bytes of a multi-byte value in addressed memory. Little-endian storage puts the least significant byte at the lowest address; big-endian storage puts the most significant byte there. Logical shifts of a value already held in a [processor register](processor-register.md) act on bit significance, independently of that storage convention.
