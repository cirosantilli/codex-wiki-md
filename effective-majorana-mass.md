# Effective Majorana mass

↑ **Parent:** [Neutrinoless double beta decay](neutrinoless-double-beta-decay.md)

The coherent electron-flavor [mass](mass.md) combination entering the usual light-neutrino-exchange amplitude. Its phases can cancel contributions, so a null double-beta result does not imply vanishing individual [masses](mass.md).
