# Isobaric thermal stability of a two-level coolant

↑ **Parent:** [Isochoric thermal stability of a collisionally excited line](isochoric-thermal-stability-of-a-collisionally-excited-line.md)

At fixed [pressure](pressure-split.md) and fixed composition, [density](density.md) varies as $T^{-1}$. With [temperature](temperature-split.md)-independent heating per particle, low-[density](density.md) cooling per particle scales as $T^{-3/2}e^{-\theta/T}$ and is restoring for $T<2\theta/3$. For constant heating per volume instead, the cooling scales as $T^{-5/2}e^{-\theta/T}$ and the threshold is $2\theta/5$. The heating normalization and perturbation constraint must be specified.
