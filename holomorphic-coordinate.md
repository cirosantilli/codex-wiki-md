# Holomorphic coordinate

↑ **Parent:** [Complex manifold](complex-manifold.md)

A holomorphic coordinate chart on a [complex manifold](complex-manifold.md) is a [biholomorphism](biholomorphism.md) from an open subset of the manifold to an open subset of $\mathbb C^n$. Its component functions are holomorphic coordinates.
