<h1 id="frohlich-canonical-transformation">Fröhlich canonical transformation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Fröhlich Hamiltonian](frohlich-hamiltonian.md)

An [anti-Hermitian](skew-hermitian-matrix.md) generator $S$ is chosen so $[S,H_0]=-H_{e\text{-}ph}$. Expanding $e^SH e^{-S}$ leaves $H_0+[S,H_{e\text{-}ph}]/2$ to second order. Eliminating virtual [phonons](phonon.md) produces an effective electron-electron interaction, plus [energy](energy-split.md) shifts. Resonant denominators and higher orders limit this perturbative elimination.
