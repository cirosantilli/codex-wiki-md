# Minkowski conformal compactification

↑ **Parent:** [Conformal completion](conformal-completion.md)

For four-dimensional [Minkowski spacetime](minkowski-spacetime.md), set $p=\arctan((t-r)/L)$ and $q=\arctan((t+r)/L)$, with $L>0$, then $T=p+q$, $R=q-p$. Multiplication by the square of the [conformal factor](conformal-factor.md) $2\cos p\cos q/L$ yields the displayed metric on $R\geq0$, $|T|+R<\pi$. Its radial [Penrose diagram](penrose-diagram.md) is a triangle: the timelike edge $R=0$ is the ordinary centre, while the two sloping edges are [future null infinity](future-null-infinity.md) and [past null infinity](past-null-infinity.md). A signed Cartesian spatial coordinate in two dimensions gives a full diamond instead.
