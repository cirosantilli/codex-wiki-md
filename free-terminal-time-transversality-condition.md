# Free-terminal-time transversality condition

↑ **Parent:** [Pontryagin maximum principle](pontryagin-maximum-principle.md)

For a smooth normal [optimal control](optimal-control.md) problem with freely variable terminal time, fixed terminal state independent of that time and terminal cost $\Phi$, the optimized [Hamiltonian of an optimal-control problem](hamiltonian-of-an-optimal-control-problem.md) at completion satisfies $H(T)+\Phi_T=0$. It follows by varying the terminal time in the augmented objective; restrictions on terminal time or a moving endpoint introduce additional terms or inequalities.
