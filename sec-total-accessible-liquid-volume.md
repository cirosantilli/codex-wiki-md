# SEC total accessible liquid volume

↑ **Parent:** [Size-exclusion chromatography](size-exclusion-chromatography.md)

The [SEC total accessible liquid volume](sec-total-accessible-liquid-volume.md) is $V_t=V_0+V_i$: interparticle plus pore liquid. A fully included nonbinding tracer estimates it. Geometric bed volume instead includes solid matrix, $V_c=V_t+V_{\rm matrix}$.[GE Healthcare, Size Exclusion Chromatography: Principles and Methods, revision 18102218 AM, printed pp. 15–16, Figure 1.7: liquid, pore and geometric volumes](size-exclusion-chromatography-principles-and-methods-by-ge-healthcare.md)
