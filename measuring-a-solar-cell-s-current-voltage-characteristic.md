<h1 id="measuring-a-solar-cell-s-current-voltage-characteristic">Measuring a solar cell's current-voltage characteristic</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Solar cell](solar-cell.md)

Keep the illumination and cell [temperature](temperature-split.md) approximately constant. Connect an [ammeter](ammeter.md) and an adjustable [resistor](resistor.md) as a [series circuit](series-circuit.md) across the [solar cell](solar-cell.md); connect a high-resistance [voltmeter](voltmeter.md) across its terminals. Vary the [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) and record pairs of [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) $V$ and delivered [electric current](electric-current.md) $I$. This measures the illuminated [current-voltage characteristic](current-voltage-characteristic.md).

A load close to a [short circuit](short-circuit.md) estimates $I_{\rm sc}$ at $V\simeq0$. With the load disconnected, an [open circuit](open-circuit.md) measurement using a high-resistance [voltmeter](voltmeter.md) estimates $V_{\rm oc}$ at $I\simeq0$. Neither endpoint delivers useful [power](power.md): $P=VI$ vanishes there. The intermediate readings locate the maximum of $VI$ and determine the [solar-cell fill factor](solar-cell-fill-factor.md). Holding illumination fixed matters because the photogenerated [electric current](electric-current.md) changes with [light intensity](light-intensity.md); holding [temperature](temperature-split.md) fixed matters because the [diode saturation current](diode-saturation-current.md) and junction response change with it.

This experiment distinguishes producing an [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) from delivering [energy](energy-split.md) to a load. To determine conversion efficiency as well, measure the incident [power](power.md) on the illuminated area, rather than equating it to a lamp's electrical rating.
