# Monodisperse

↑ **Parent:** [Suspension (chemistry)](suspension-chemistry-split.md)

A [monodisperse](monodisperse.md) [particle suspension](suspension-chemistry-split.md) has a narrow distribution of particle sizes. Treating its particles as having a single [radius](radius.md) simplifies the [Perrin sedimentation experiment](perrin-sedimentation-experiment.md) model; residual size variation can still affect the inferred [Avogadro constant](avogadro-constant.md).
