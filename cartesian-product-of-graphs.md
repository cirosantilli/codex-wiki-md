# Cartesian product of graphs

↑ **Parent:** [Graph](graph-split.md)

For two [graphs](graph-split.md) $G,H$, their [Cartesian product of graphs](cartesian-product-of-graphs.md) has [graph vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) $(g,h)\in V(G)\times V(H)$. Two [graph vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) are adjacent if either their $G$ coordinates agree and their $H$ coordinates are adjacent, or conversely their $H$ coordinates agree and their $G$ coordinates are adjacent. The [ladder graph](ladder-graph.md) is the product of a [graph path](path-in-a-graph.md) with $K_2$.
