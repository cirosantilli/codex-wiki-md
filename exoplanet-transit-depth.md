# Exoplanet transit depth

↑ **Parent:** [Exoplanet transit photometry](exoplanet-transit-photometry.md)

The fractional decrease in [observed radiative flux](observed-radiative-flux.md) during an [exoplanet transit](exoplanet-transit.md), relative to the out-of-transit baseline. For a uniform [star](star.md), a dark [planet](planet.md), full disc overlap, and no [transit dilution](transit-dilution.md), the [exoplanet transit depth](exoplanet-transit-depth.md) is $(R_p/R_\star)^2$. For a nonuniform [star](star.md) it depends on the blocked [specific intensity](spectral-radiance.md) as well as the [radius](radius.md) ratio.
