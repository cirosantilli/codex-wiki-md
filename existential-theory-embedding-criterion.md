# Existential-theory embedding criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Method of diagrams](method-of-diagrams.md)

A finite [conjunction](conjunction-astronomy.md) from the [signed atomic diagram](diagram-mathematical-logic.md) of $A$ becomes, after existentially closing its new names, a sentence in its existential [theory](first-order-theory.md). Joint satisfiability with $T$ therefore makes the full diagram finitely satisfiable with $T$. Compactness gives the [embedding](structure-embedding.md), with inequalities ensuring injectivity and signed relation literals ensuring [reflection](full-local-reflection-in-a-one-sentence-extension-is-inconsistent.md). The reverse implication is preservation of existential truth under [embeddings](structure-embedding.md).
