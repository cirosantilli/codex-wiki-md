# Axisymmetric pure plume

↑ **Parent:** [Pure plume](pure-plume.md)

For a top-hat axisymmetric pure plume of radius $r$, speed $U$, and reduced gravity $g'$, the conserved fluxes are $Q=\pi r^2U$, $M=\pi r^2U^2$, and $B=\pi r^2Ug'$. The [Batchelor entrainment hypothesis](batchelor-entrainment-hypothesis.md) gives $Q\propto B^{1/3}z^{5/3}$.
