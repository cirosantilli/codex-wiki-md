# Hyperplane divisor

↑ **Parent:** [Cartier divisor](cartier-divisor-split.md)

A hyperplane divisor on [projective space](projective-space-split.md) is the effective [Cartier divisor](cartier-divisor-split.md) cut out by one nonzero linear homogeneous polynomial. Its class generates $\operatorname{Cl}(\mathbb P_k^n)\cong\mathbb Z$.
