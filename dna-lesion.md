# DNA lesion

↑ **Parent:** [Deoxyribonucleic acid](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md)

A [DNA lesion](dna-lesion.md) is physical or chemical damage to [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md), such as an altered [nucleobase](nucleotide-base.md) or strand break. [DNA repair](dna-repair.md) can restore the sequence before a lasting [mutation](mutation.md) occurs.
