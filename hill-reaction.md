# Hill reaction

↑ **Parent:** [Chloroplast](chloroplast.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hill_reaction)

The [Hill reaction](hill-reaction.md) couples [light](light.md)-driven [electron](electron.md) transfer from [water](water.md) to an added [electron acceptor](electron-acceptor.md) with [molecular oxygen](molecular-oxygen.md) release in an isolated photosynthetic preparation. The [chloroplast dye-reduction experiment](chloroplast-dye-reduction-experiment.md) uses [DCPIP](dichlorophenolindophenol.md).[Investigating the light dependent reaction in photosynthesis, introductory and Teaching notes: artificial acceptor assay](investigating-the-light-dependent-reaction-in-photosynthesis-at-practical-biology.md)

In 1937, [Robin Hill](robin-hill-biochemist.md)'s Oxygen Evolved by Isolated Chloroplasts described a spectroscopic [molecular oxygen](molecular-oxygen.md) measurement in illuminated isolated [chloroplasts](chloroplast.md): oxygenation of muscle [hemoglobin](hemoglobin.md) provided the indicator. This was an oxygen-binding readout. The publicly accessible first paragraph establishes that method; it does not specify enough preparation or control details to reconstruct the entire historical experiment here.[Hill, Nature 139, 881–882 (22 May 1937), public first-paragraph preview: hemoglobin oxygenation and spectroscopic saturation measurement](oxygen-evolved-by-isolated-chloroplasts-by-robin-hill.md)

The experimental idea is powerful because isolation asks how much of [photosynthesis](photosynthesis.md) remains functional outside an intact [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md). Breaking a [chloroplast envelope](chloroplast-envelope.md) does not logically imply destroying every internal [thylakoid membrane](thylakoid-membrane.md). Conversely, finding an active preparation cannot establish that its [chloroplast envelope](chloroplast-envelope.md) remains intact. Activity and structural integrity require different evidence.
