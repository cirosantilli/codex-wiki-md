# Abscisic acid

↑ **Parent:** [Plant hormone](plant-hormone.md)

A [plant hormone](plant-hormone.md) involved in water-stress responses, seed dormancy and other processes. In guard cells, ABA-dependent receptor inhibition of phosphatases permits kinase activation and [stomatal closure](stomatal-closure.md).
