# Hopkins-Levitzki theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Right Artinian ring](right-artinian-ring.md)

A [right Artinian ring](right-artinian-ring.md) is a [right Noetherian ring](right-noetherian-ring.md). Its right regular [module](module-mathematics.md) therefore has finite [composition length](composition-length.md). Its [Jacobson radical](jacobson-radical.md) is a [nilpotent ideal](nilpotent-ideal.md), and its quotient by that [Jacobson radical](jacobson-radical.md) is a [semisimple ring](semisimple-ring.md). In particular every finitely generated right [module](module-mathematics.md) over such a [ring](ring.md) has finite [composition length](composition-length.md).
