# Protein structure

↑ **Parent:** [Protein](protein.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protein_structure)

[Protein structure](protein-structure.md) describes both the chain's chemical organization and its spatial arrangement. Four levels answer different questions: [protein primary structure](protein-primary-structure.md) gives the order; [protein secondary structure](protein-secondary-structure.md) gives recurring local patterns; [protein tertiary structure](protein-tertiary-structure.md) gives one chain's overall arrangement; [protein quaternary structure](protein-quaternary-structure.md) gives the assembly of multiple chains. These are levels of description, not four mandatory stages that every [protein](protein.md) must pass through. See [Clark, Douglas and Choi: four levels of protein structure](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/3-4-proteins).
