# Specific enthalpy per particle

↑ **Parent:** [Perfect fluid in general relativity](perfect-fluid-in-general-relativity.md)

For [number density](number-density.md) $n$, rest-frame [energy density](energy-density.md) $\rho$ and [pressure](pressure.md) $p$, the enthalpy per particle is $\epsilon=(\rho+p)/n$. In an isentropic [barotropic fluid](barotropic-fluid.md), $d\rho=\epsilon\,dn$ and $dp=n\,d\epsilon$. It differs from enthalpy per unit mass by the particle mass when that mass is fixed. Using $\epsilon$ as thermodynamic variable gives $n=p'(\epsilon)$ and $\rho=\epsilon p'(\epsilon)-p(\epsilon)$.
