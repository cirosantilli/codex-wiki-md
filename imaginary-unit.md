# Imaginary unit

↑ **Parent:** [Complex number](complex-number.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imaginary_unit)

The imaginary unit is the complex number $i$ satisfying $i^2=-1$. Its integer powers repeat with period four.
