# Postulates of special relativity

↑ **Parent:** [Special relativity](special-relativity-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postulates_of_special_relativity)

The first postulate is [Einstein's principle of relativity](einstein-s-principle-of-relativity.md): nongravitational laws have the same form in every [inertial frame](inertial-frame.md). The second states that light in vacuum propagates at the same finite [speed of light](speed-of-light.md) $c$ in every [inertial frame](inertial-frame.md), independently of the source's motion. Together with the usual spacetime symmetries and calibrated coordinates, these postulates yield the [Lorentz transformation](lorentz-transformation.md). The relativity principle alone also admits the [Galilean transformation](galilean-transformation.md) with its infinite invariant-speed limit.
