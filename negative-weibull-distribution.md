# Negative Weibull distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Extreme value theory](extreme-value-theory.md)

For $\alpha>0$, the standard [negative Weibull distribution](negative-weibull-distribution.md) has [distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md) $\Psi_\alpha(x)=e^{-(-x)^\alpha}$ for $x<0$ and one for $x\geq0$. This is the [probability distribution](probability-distribution.md) of the negative of a standard positive [Weibull distribution](weibull-distribution.md) variable.
