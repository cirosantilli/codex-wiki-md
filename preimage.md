# Preimage

↑ **Parent:** [Image and preimage of a function](image-and-preimage-of-a-function.md)

For a [function](function-split.md) $f:X\to Y$ and a [subset](subset.md) $B\subseteq Y$, the preimage $f^{-1}(B)$ consists of points of $X$ mapped into $B$. This notation does not require $f$ to have an [inverse function](inverse-function.md). Preimages preserve [unions](set-union.md), [intersections](set-intersection.md) and complements, which makes them useful in defining [continuous functions](continuous-function.md) and [measurable functions](measurable-function.md).
