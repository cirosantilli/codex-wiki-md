# D7-brane

↑ **Parent:** [D-brane](d-brane.md)

A type-IIB [D-brane](d-brane.md) with seven spatial dimensions. Its RR charge is magnetic with respect to $C_0$, or electric with respect to the dual eight-form potential. Its transverse geometry is codimension two, so the simple asymptotically flat $r^{p-7}$ harmonic formula for lower-dimensional D-branes does not apply unchanged.
