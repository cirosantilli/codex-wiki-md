# Normal scalar-field momentum

↑ **Parent:** [3+1 decomposition of spacetime](3-plus-1-decomposition-of-spacetime.md)

For a [minimally coupled scalar field](minimally-coupled-scalar-field.md) with spatial [induced metric](induced-metric.md) $h_{ij}$, the field's derivative along the future [unit normal](unit-normal.md) is $\Pi$. If the [shift vector](shift-vector.md) enters as $dx^i-N^idt$, then $\Pi=(\dot\phi+N^i\partial_i\phi)/N$. The canonical momentum of the density is $\pi_\phi=\sqrt h\,\Pi$. Confusing this density with the normal derivative loses a volume factor.
