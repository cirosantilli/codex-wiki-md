# Coronagraph

↑ **Parent:** [Optics](optics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coronagraph)

A coronagraph suppresses the light of a bright central object to improve detection of nearby faint sources. In [exoplanet direct imaging](exoplanet-direct-imaging.md), its inner working angle and residual stellar leakage constrain accessible separations and contrasts.
