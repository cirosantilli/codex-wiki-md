<h1 id="einstein-s-principle-of-relativity">Einstein's principle of relativity</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Special relativity](special-relativity-split.md)

The laws of physics have the same form in all [inertial frames](inertial-frame.md); uniform absolute motion cannot be identified by experiments confined to an inertial laboratory. The invariant vacuum speed of light is a separate postulate. Together they motivate the [Lorentz transformations](lorentz-transformation.md) of [special relativity](special-relativity-split.md).
