# Entropy density of a photon gas

↑ **Parent:** [Photon gas](photon-gas.md)

For a [photon gas](photon-gas.md), the [first law of thermodynamics](first-law-of-thermodynamics.md) gives $TdS=d(a_RT^4V)+(a_RT^4/3)dV$, hence $dS=(4a_R/3)d(T^3V)$. Choosing zero [entropy](entropy.md) at zero [temperature](temperature.md) gives $S=4a_RT^3V/3$. Adiabatic expansion with fixed relativistic species therefore preserves $T^3V$; changing species or producing entropy requires a more general entropy balance.
