# Magnetic susceptibility tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic susceptibility](magnetic-susceptibility.md)

A [magnetic susceptibility tensor](magnetic-susceptibility-tensor.md) maps a small change of internal [magnetic field intensity](magnetic-field-intensity.md) to a change of [magnetization](magnetization.md):

$$
\delta M_i=\sum_j\chi_{ij}\,\delta H_{{\rm int},j}.
$$

This [tensor](tensor.md) reduces to $\chi\delta_{ij}$ for [isotropy](isotropy.md). With [anisotropy](anisotropy.md), even a linear response need not point along the applied [magnetic field intensity](magnetic-field-intensity.md). As a stipulated example, $\boldsymbol\chi=\operatorname{diag}(1,1,3)\times10^{-4}$ sends $(H,0,H)$ to $(H,0,3H)\times10^{-4}$. State the axes and bias field when reporting its components. A constant [tensor](tensor.md) describes linear response; in a nonlinear material it is the [Jacobian matrix](jacobian-matrix.md) of the response at the chosen operating point.
