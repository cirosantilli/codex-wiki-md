# Nobel Prize in Physics in 2013

↑ **Parent:** [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics-split.md)

Awarded in equal shares to [François Englert](francois-englert.md) and [Peter W. Higgs](peter-higgs.md) for the theoretical discovery of a mechanism explaining the origin of [mass](mass.md) in [subatomic particles](subatomic-particle.md), confirmed by discovery of the predicted particle in the [ATLAS experiment](atlas-experiment.md) and [CMS experiment](compact-muon-solenoid.md) at the [Large Hadron Collider](large-hadron-collider.md).[Individual official summary: Englert and Higgs one half each; theoretical mass mechanism and its experimental confirmation.](the-nobel-prize-in-physics-2013-by-nobel-prize-outreach.md)
