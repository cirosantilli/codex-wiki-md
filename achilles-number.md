# Achilles number

↑ **Parent:** [Powerful number](powerful-number.md)

A [powerful number](powerful-number.md) that is not a [perfect power](perfect-power.md). If $n=\prod_i p_i^{e_i}$ is its [prime factorization](fundamental-theorem-of-arithmetic.md), the conditions are $e_i\ge2$ for every $i$ and $\gcd(e_1,\ldots,e_k)=1$.
