# Electron recoil

↑ **Parent:** [Particle detection](particle-detection-split.md)

An [energy](energy-split.md) deposition in a target's electronic degrees of freedom, as opposed to a [nuclear recoil](nuclear-recoil.md). [gamma rays](gamma-ray.md) and [beta decay](beta-decay.md) can produce backgrounds in this channel. Detector discrimination is statistical because the measured light and charge distributions have finite widths.
