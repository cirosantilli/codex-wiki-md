# Symmetric tensor vacuum stabilizer

↑ **Parent:** [Spontaneous symmetry breaking](spontaneous-symmetry-breaking.md)

A real [symmetric tensor](symmetric-tensor.md) of the [special orthogonal group](special-orthogonal-group.md) transforms as $Q\mapsto OQO^T$. If its [vacuum expectation value](vacuum-expectation-value.md) has distinct [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) with multiplicities $m_1,\ldots,m_p$, its [stabilizer subgroup](stabilizer-subgroup.md) is $S(O(m_1)\times\cdots\times O(m_p))$: each transformation preserves each [eigenspace](eigenspace.md) and the product of the block [determinants](determinant.md) is one. Its [identity component](identity-component.md) is $\prod_iSO(m_i)$. Thus a scalar multiple of the identity preserves the whole [group](group-split.md), whereas a tensor with simple spectrum preserves only diagonal signs with total sign one. A tracelessness condition constrains the eigenvalues but does not change this stabilizer formula.
