# Affine open subscheme

↑ **Parent:** [Affine scheme](affine-scheme.md)

An affine open subscheme is an [open subset](open-set.md) of a [scheme](scheme.md) with its restricted [structure sheaf](structure-sheaf-of-a-scheme.md), which is itself an [affine scheme](affine-scheme.md). Every scheme has an [open cover](open-cover.md) by such subschemes. On an affine scheme, the [principal open subschemes](principal-open-subscheme.md) give an [open basis](basis-of-a-topology.md).
