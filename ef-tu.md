# EF-Tu

↑ **Parent:** [Translation (biology)](translation-biology.md)

A bacterial translational GTPase delivering [aminoacyl-tRNA](aminoacyl-trna.md) to the ribosome. Correct decoding promotes its hydrolysis cycle; [EF-Ts](ef-ts.md) accelerates nucleotide exchange for reuse.
