# Zero-inflated Poisson distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md)

A zero-inflated Poisson distribution is a mixture of a point mass at zero and a [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md). If the Poisson component has weight $\pi$ and mean $\lambda$, then its mean is $\pi\lambda$ and its variance is $\pi\lambda+\pi(1-\pi)\lambda^2$.
