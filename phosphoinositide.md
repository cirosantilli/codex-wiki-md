# Phosphoinositide

↑ **Parent:** [Phospholipid](phospholipid.md)

A [phosphoinositide](phosphoinositide.md) is a phosphorylated derivative of [phosphatidylinositol](phosphatidylinositol.md). Phosphate position changes the recognition surface of the headgroup, allowing distinct species to recruit different membrane-associated [proteins](protein-split.md).
