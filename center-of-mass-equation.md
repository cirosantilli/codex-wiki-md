# Center-of-mass equation

↑ **Parent:** [Center of mass](center-of-mass.md)

For a closed system with fixed total [mass](mass.md), summing the constituent [Newton's second law](newton-s-second-law.md) equations makes pairwise internal forces cancel when [Newton's third law](newton-s-third-law.md) applies. The remaining resultant external [force](force-split.md) is the derivative of total [momentum](momentum-split.md), equal to $M\ddot{\mathbf r}_{\rm CM}$. This governs translation of the [center of mass](center-of-mass.md); external [torque](torque.md) additionally governs changes of [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md).
