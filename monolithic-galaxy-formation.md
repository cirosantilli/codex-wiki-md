# Monolithic galaxy formation

↑ **Parent:** [Hierarchical galaxy formation](hierarchical-galaxy-formation.md)

Monolithic galaxy formation idealizes a [galaxy](galaxy-split.md) as forming in one early rapid dissipative collapse, followed mainly by passive evolution. Rapid formation of the stars is distinct from early assembly of all those stars into their final object; [hierarchical galaxy formation](hierarchical-galaxy-formation.md) can assemble old stellar populations later.
