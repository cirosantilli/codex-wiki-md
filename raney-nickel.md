# Raney nickel

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrogenation](hydrogenation.md)

A porous nickel catalyst prepared by selective removal of aluminium from a nickel–aluminium alloy. It can catalyse [hydrogenations](hydrogenation.md); its use does not guarantee that every other reducible functional group will remain unchanged.
