# SNO solar-neutrino flavor experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Sudbury Neutrino Observatory](sudbury-neutrino-observatory.md)

In 2001, $240.95$ live days, reconstructed radius below $5.50\,\mathrm m$ and effective [electron](electron.md) kinetic [energy](energy-split.md) above $6.75\,\mathrm{MeV}$ gave a CC [neutrino flux](neutrino-flux.md) of $1.75\times10^6\,\mathrm{cm}^{-2}\mathrm{s}^{-1}$ for the assumed [boron](boron.md)-8 spectrum. Source calibrations measured $1.4\%$ instrumental-cut signal loss. SNO's own CC–ES difference was $1.6\sigma$; comparison with [Super-Kamiokande](super-kamiokande.md)'s precise ES flux gave $3.3\sigma$ evidence for non-electron active [neutrinos](neutrino.md). This was not an NC measurement.[PDF pp. 2–5: cuts, calibration and CC–ES inference; central flux](measurement-of-the-rate-of-nu-e-plus-d-%E2%86%92-p-plus-p-plus-e-minus-interactions-produced-by-8b-solar-neutrinos-at-the-sudbury-neutrino-observatory-by-q-r-ahmad-and-others.md)

In 2002 the pure-[heavy water](heavy-water.md) analysis used $306.4$ live days and a $5\,\mathrm{MeV}$ reconstructed kinetic-[energy](energy-split.md) threshold. NC [neutrons](neutron-split.md) captured on [deuterons](deuteron.md) emitted $6.25\,\mathrm{MeV}$ [gamma rays](gamma-ray.md), producing secondary [electron](electron.md) light. The NC reaction threshold was $2.2\,\mathrm{MeV}$, a different quantity. [Californium](californium.md)-252 calibrated $29.9\%$ capture efficiency, but only $14.4\%$ detection efficiency after fiducial and [energy](energy-split.md) cuts. Assays constrained backgrounds from [radioactive decay](radioactive-decay.md) and photodisintegration.[PDF pp. 2–3: neutron calibration, thresholds and backgrounds](direct-evidence-for-neutrino-flavor-transformation-from-neutral-current-interactions-in-the-sudbury-neutrino-observatory-by-q-r-ahmad-and-others.md)

Energy, radius and direction relative to the [Sun](sun.md) statistically separated channels. Assuming the standard [boron](boron.md)-8 spectral shape, the NC total active [neutrino flux](neutrino-flux.md) was $5.09^{+0.44}_{-0.43}\,\mathrm{(stat.)}^{+0.46}_{-0.43}\,\mathrm{(syst.)}\times10^6\,\mathrm{cm}^{-2}\mathrm{s}^{-1}$, consistent with [stellar structure](stellar-structure-split.md) predictions. The non-electron active component differed from zero by $5.3\sigma$. This established active flavour conversion, without individually tagging [muon neutrinos](muon-neutrino.md) and [tau neutrinos](tau-neutrino.md). It did not exclude every sterile admixture.[PDF pp. 4–5, Fig. 3: constrained-spectrum fluxes and non-electron component](direct-evidence-for-neutrino-flavor-transformation-from-neutral-current-interactions-in-the-sudbury-neutrino-observatory-by-q-r-ahmad-and-others.md)
