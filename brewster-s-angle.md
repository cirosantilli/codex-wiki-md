<h1 id="brewster-s-angle">Brewster's angle</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Fresnel equations](fresnel-equations.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brewster's_angle)

For an interface between two lossless, [isotropic](isotropy.md), nonmagnetic media with unequal [refractive index](refractive-index.md) values $n_1,n_2$, Brewster's angle is the incidence angle at which the reflected amplitude in [transverse magnetic polarization](transverse-magnetic-polarization.md) vanishes. The [Fresnel equations](fresnel-equations.md) and [Snell's law](snell-s-law.md) give $\tan\theta_B=n_2/n_1$; the reflected and transmitted rays are perpendicular there. The usual formula assumes equal [magnetic permeabilities](permeability-electromagnetism.md).
