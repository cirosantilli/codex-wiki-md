# Jacobson ring

↑ **Parent:** [Jacobson radical](jacobson-radical.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacobson_ring)

A commutative ring $R$ is Jacobson when every [prime ideal](prime-ideal.md) is the intersection of the maximal ideals containing it. Equivalently, for every ideal $I$, its [radical](radical-of-an-ideal.md) is the intersection of the maximal ideals containing $I$.
