# Tip-change artefact

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic force microscopy](atomic-force-microscopy.md)

An apparent image change caused by alteration of the effective probe apex during acquisition. A shifted height baseline may be corrected using a suitable reference, but changed geometric access, duplicated features and lost data are not generally repaired by [AFM image flattening](afm-image-flattening.md).
