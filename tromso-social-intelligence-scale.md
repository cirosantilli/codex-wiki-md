<h1 id="tromso-social-intelligence-scale">Tromsø Social Intelligence Scale</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Social intelligence](social-intelligence.md)

A 21-item [self-report](self-report.md) instrument developed with dimensions labeled social information processing, [social skills](social-skills.md) and [social awareness](social-awareness.md). Its internal structure is evidence about this operationalization; it does not by itself settle the organization of all social abilities.
