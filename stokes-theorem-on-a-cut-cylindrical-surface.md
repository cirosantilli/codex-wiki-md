# Stokes theorem on a cut cylindrical surface

↑ **Parent:** [Stokes theorem](stokes-theorem.md)

[Stokes theorem](stokes-theorem.md) applies to an [oriented surface](oriented-surface.md) with several boundary components. Cutting a cylindrical side along one generator produces a parameter rectangle: the two copies of the cut are traversed in opposite directions and their [line integrals](line-integral.md) cancel for a single-valued smooth [vector field](vector-field.md). The upper and lower rims retain opposite directions when viewed from the same side. This proves why cylindrical topology does not require a modification of [Stokes theorem](stokes-theorem.md); it requires inclusion of the entire oriented boundary.
