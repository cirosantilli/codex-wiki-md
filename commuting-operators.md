# Commuting operators

↑ **Parent:** [Linear operator](linear-operator.md)

Two [linear operators](linear-operator.md) $A$ and $B$ commute when $AB=BA$. Their [matrix exponentials](matrix-exponential.md) then satisfy $e^{A+B}=e^Ae^B=e^Be^A$.
