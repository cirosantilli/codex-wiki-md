# Simplicial mapping space

↑ **Parent:** [Simplicial set](simplicial-set.md)

The simplicial mapping space is defined by

$$
\underline{\operatorname{Hom}}(A,X)_n
=\operatorname{Hom}(A\times\Delta^n,X).
$$

If $A\hookrightarrow B$ is a monomorphism and $X$ is a [Kan complex](kan-complex.md), restriction $\underline{\operatorname{Hom}}(B,X)\to\underline{\operatorname{Hom}}(A,X)$ is a Kan fibration.
