# State-counting and helicity factors in pion decay

↑ **Parent:** [Leptonic pion decay](leptonic-pion-decay.md)

In a box-normalized treatment of negative-[pion](pion.md) two-body decay with a massless [antineutrino](antineutrino.md), $dp/dE_0=E_\ell/m_\pi$. The simple [density of states](density-of-states.md) is therefore proportional to $p_\ell^2E_\ell/m_\pi$. The [spin-zero two-body helicity selection](spin-zero-two-body-helicity-selection.md) requires positive charged-lepton helicity, whose left-chiral weight is $(1-\beta_\ell)/2$. Since $1-\beta_\ell=2m_\ell^2/(m_\pi E_\ell)$, the energy factors cancel in the product and give the standard relative rate proportional to $m_\ell^2p_\ell^2$. A [Lorentz-invariant phase-space measure](lorentz-invariant-phase-space-measure.md) distributes normalization factors differently but gives the same observable ratio when the amplitude is normalized consistently.
