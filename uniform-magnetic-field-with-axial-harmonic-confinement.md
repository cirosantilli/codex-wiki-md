# Uniform magnetic field with axial harmonic confinement

↑ **Parent:** [Charged particle in a uniform magnetic field](charged-particle-in-a-uniform-magnetic-field.md)

The cylindrical [vector](vector.md) potential $A_\theta=\mu r$ gives a uniform axial [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) $2\mu$, while [scalar potential](scalar-potential.md) $\lambda z^2$ supplies harmonic axial confinement for positive charge and positive $\lambda$. Transverse motion is cyclotron motion about a fixed guiding centre, and axial motion is an independent harmonic [oscillation](oscillation.md). A circle centred on the cylindrical origin has $\dot\theta=-\Omega_c$ for positive charge.
