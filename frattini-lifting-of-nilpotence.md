# Frattini lifting of nilpotence

↑ **Parent:** [Frattini subgroup](frattini-subgroup.md)

Let $\Phi=\Phi(G)$ in a [finite group](finite-group.md). If $H\trianglelefteq G$ contains $\Phi$ and $H/\Phi$ is a [nilpotent group](nilpotent-group.md), every [Sylow subgroup](sylow-subgroup.md) $P$ of $H$ has $P\Phi\trianglelefteq G$. The [Frattini argument](frattini-argument.md) gives $G=(P\Phi)N_G(P)=\Phi N_G(P)$, and the defining non-generator property of $\Phi$ forces $N_G(P)=G$. Hence $H$ is a [nilpotent group](nilpotent-group.md).
