<h1 id="elsasser-variable">Elsässer variable</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal magnetohydrodynamics](ideal-magnetohydrodynamics.md)

For an [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md) with constant [mass density](density.md), the [Elsässer variables](elsasser-variable.md) are $\mathbf z^\pm=\mathbf u\pm\mathbf v_a$, where $\mathbf v_a$ is the [Alfvén velocity](alfven-velocity.md). Adding and subtracting the [Euler momentum equation](euler-equations-for-an-inviscid-fluid.md) and [ideal magnetohydrodynamic induction equation](ideal-magnetohydrodynamic-induction-equation.md) yields $\partial_t\mathbf z^\pm+(\mathbf z^\mp\cdot\nabla)\mathbf z^\pm=-\nabla\psi$, with $\psi=p/\rho+\Phi+|\mathbf v_a|^2/2$ and $\nabla\cdot\mathbf z^\pm=0$. Thus each field is transported by the other, a useful way to expose interactions of oppositely directed [Alfvén waves](alfven-wave.md).
