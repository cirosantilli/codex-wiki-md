# Guided parent-delivered cognitive behavioral therapy

↑ **Parent:** [Parent involvement in youth anxiety treatment](parent-involvement-in-youth-anxiety-treatment.md)

A therapist supports a caregiver in delivering structured [CBT](cognitive-behavioral-therapy.md) activities with their [child](child.md). Guidance includes reviewing practice and overcoming barriers, rather than merely distributing information. Evidence for a specified guided programme does not establish that an unsupported manual has the same effect.[British Journal of Psychiatry 203, 436–444; 194 children, full/brief parent guidance and waiting list; primary-carer eligibility](treatment-of-child-anxiety-disorders-via-guided-parent-delivered-cognitive-behavioural-therapy-by-kerstin-thirlwall-and-others.md)
