# Anisotropic quantum harmonic oscillator

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md)

For a positive diagonal quadratic potential $V=m\sum_i\omega_i^2x_i^2/2$, coordinate [Hamiltonians](hamiltonian.md) commute and [wavefunctions](wave-function-split.md) separate into one-dimensional oscillator factors. Equality of two squared [frequencies](frequency.md) gives [rotation](rotation-mathematics.md) [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md) in their plane; equality of all three gives full [rotation](rotation-mathematics.md) [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md). Equalities create [energy](energy-split.md) degeneracies and extra conserved [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md). Merely finding an accidental equality between particular level energies does not establish a global [rotational symmetry](rotational-symmetry.md).
