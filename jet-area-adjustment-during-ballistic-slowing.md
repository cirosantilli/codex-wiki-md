# Jet-area adjustment during ballistic slowing

↑ **Parent:** [Apex-target condition for a ballistic jet](apex-target-condition-for-a-ballistic-jet.md)

Steady incompressible [mass flow rate](mass-flow-rate.md) stays constant along a jet, while speed and cross-sectional area can change under gravity. At a horizontal apex the arriving speed is the initial horizontal component. The force on a target that stops this horizontal momentum is $\dot m v_{\rm hit}$, using the conserved nozzle mass flux. Substituting the nozzle area into $\rho A_{\rm hit}v_{\rm hit}^2$ without adjusting the area gives the wrong result.
