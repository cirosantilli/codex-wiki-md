# Endo selectivity

↑ **Parent:** [Diels-Alder reaction](diels-alder-reaction.md)

A preference for the cycloaddition orientation in which relevant [dienophile](dienophile.md) substituents lie under the [diene](diene.md) framework in the transition geometry. It is distinct from regiochemistry and is not an unconditional guarantee of one product.
