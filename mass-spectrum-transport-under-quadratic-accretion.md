# Mass-spectrum transport under quadratic accretion

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic accretion in a central Keplerian potential](quadratic-accretion-in-a-central-keplerian-potential.md)

If stars are neither created nor lost and all follow [quadratic accretion in a central Keplerian potential](quadratic-accretion-in-a-central-keplerian-potential.md), conservation of number gives $F(M,t)dM=F_0(M_0)dM_0$. Inverting the trajectory gives $M_0=M/(1+\lambda tM)$ and its [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md) is $(1+\lambda tM)^{-2}$. This proves the displayed mass-density-in-mass-space transformation. In a broad high-mass range with $\lambda tM\gg1$, whose inverse images remain in a narrow interval where $F_0$ varies little, the [power law](power-law.md) is $F\propto M^{-2}$. A strictly monochromatic initial population remains monochromatic. Finite initial support imposes a finite upper cutoff before the earliest formal blow-up, so the power law is an intermediate asymptotic subject to the accretion model's limits.
