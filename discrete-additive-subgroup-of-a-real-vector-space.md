# Discrete additive subgroup of a real vector space

↑ **Parent:** [Discrete subgroup](discrete-subgroup.md)

A discrete additive [subgroup](subgroup.md) of $\mathbb R^n$ is generated by real-linearly independent vectors. Indeed, isolation of zero gives uniform separation, so compact sets meet the [subgroup](subgroup.md) finitely. Choose a real basis from the [subgroup](subgroup.md) for its real span. Its [integer](integer.md) span has finite index, since reduction into a compact fundamental parallelepiped gives finitely many [coset](coset.md) representatives. The [structure theorem for finitely generated abelian groups](fundamental-theorem-of-finitely-generated-abelian-groups.md) then gives a free [integer](integer.md) basis, whose size is the dimension of the real span. Thus the [subgroup](subgroup.md) is a [Euclidean lattice](euclidean-lattice.md) in its span, although it need not have full rank in the ambient space.
