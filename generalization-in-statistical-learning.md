# Generalization in statistical learning

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical learning](statistical-learning-split.md)

Generalization is the ability of a fitted prediction rule to perform well on new observations under a specified target [probability distribution](probability-distribution.md). It is assessed through [prediction error](prediction-error.md) rather than [training error](training-error.md). The target distribution and use of data are part of the question: [dataset shift](dataset-shift.md) can change performance, and [data leakage](data-leakage.md) can make an apparent assessment misleading.
