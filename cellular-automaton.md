# Cellular automaton

↑ **Parent:** [Computer science](computer-science-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cellular_automaton)

A [cellular automaton](cellular-automaton.md) assigns one of finitely many states to each cell of a regular grid and updates cells in discrete generations using a fixed local rule. In the synchronous convention, each new state depends only on old states in a finite neighborhood. [Conway's Game of Life](conway-game-of-life.md) supplies a concrete example with two states and eight neighbors; its global update is a [function](function-split.md) on complete configurations, with [deterministic evolution](deterministic-evolution.md).[Johnston and Greene, chapter 1: Life and cellular automata](https://github.com/nathanieljohnston/game-of-life-book/blob/master/early_life.tex)
