# Wave travel time on a hanging uniform cable

↑ **Parent:** [Transverse wave](transverse-wave.md)

At distance $x$ below the support of a flexible uniform cable of length $L$, the equilibrium [tension](tension.md) is $\mu g(L-x)$, with [linear mass density](linear-mass-density.md) $\mu$. The local transverse speed is $\sqrt{g(L-x)}$. Integrating its reciprocal from zero to $L$ gives the displayed travel time. The speed vanishes at the free end, but the travel-time singularity is integrable. With variable [tension](tension.md), the small-amplitude [wave equation](wave-equation-split.md) is $\mu y_{tt}=\partial_x(Ty_x)$; its characteristic speed is still $\sqrt{T/\mu}$.
