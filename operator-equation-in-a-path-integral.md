# Operator equation in a path integral

↑ **Parent:** [Path integral](path-integral.md)

An [operator equation in a path integral](operator-equation-in-a-path-integral.md) $\mathcal O(x)=0$ asserts that insertions of $\mathcal O(x)$ vanish in every [time-ordered correlation function](time-ordered-correlation-function.md) with the other insertion points separated from $x$. Derivatives of [time ordering](time-ordering.md) can produce [Ward identity contact terms](ward-identity-contact-terms.md) at coincidence. For example, conservation of a [Dirac current](dirac-current.md) gives a vanishing separated-point divergence, while a charged insertion contributes a delta-function contact term fixed by its transformation. A vanishing one-point expectation value alone is insufficient.
