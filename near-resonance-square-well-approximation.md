# Near-resonance square-well approximation

↑ **Parent:** [S-wave bound-state threshold in a spherical square well](s-wave-bound-state-threshold-in-a-spherical-square-well.md)

The exact [spherical square well](spherical-square-well.md) matching equation is $k'\cot(k'a)=k\cot(ka+\delta_0)$. For $E\ll V_0$ near a threshold peak, neglecting $ka$ relative to the chosen [scattering phase shift](scattering-phase-shift.md) and replacing $k'$ by $\kappa$ gives $\sigma_{\rm res}\simeq(4\pi/k^2)E/[E+V_0\cot^2(\kappa a)]$. Its maxima approach the [S-wave bound-state threshold in a spherical square well](s-wave-bound-state-threshold-in-a-spherical-square-well.md). Its formal zeros at $\kappa a=n\pi$ are outside that approximation's validity: the exact zero-energy [scattering length](scattering-length-from-a-partial-wave-s-matrix.md) is then $a$, whereas genuine leading-order zeros satisfy $\tan(\kappa a)=\kappa a$.
