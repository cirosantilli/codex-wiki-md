# Gauge-invariant operator

↑ **Parent:** [Gauge invariance](gauge-invariance.md)

A local field combination is a gauge-invariant operator when its gauge representations and charges combine to a singlet. In an [effective field theory](effective-field-theory.md), such operators may be multiplied by [Wilson coefficients](wilson-coefficient.md) and inverse powers of a heavy scale. Multiplication by $\phi^\dagger\phi$ preserves the invariance of an existing Higgs Yukawa operator.
