# Pushout of a module extension

↑ **Parent:** [Module extension](module-extension.md)

Pushing an inclusion $i:A\to B$ along $f:A\to A\prime$ gives a new [module extension](module-extension.md) with kernel $A\prime$ and the same quotient. This [pushout](pushout.md) realizes the covariance of the degree-one [Ext functor](ext-functor.md) in its second argument.
