# Strebel infinite-index subgroup theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Poincare duality group](poincare-duality-group.md)

An infinite-index subgroup of an integral dimension-$d$ [Poincare duality group](poincare-duality-group.md) has integral [cohomological dimension](integral-cohomological-dimension-of-a-group.md) at most $d-1$. No finiteness assumption on the subgroup is needed for this inequality. In dimension three it places every infinite-index subgroup in cohomological dimension at most two.
