# Riesz representation on compactly supported continuous functions

↑ **Parent:** [Riesz-Markov-Kakutani representation theorem](riesz-markov-kakutani-representation-theorem.md)

A [positive linear functional](positive-linear-functional.md) on the [space of continuous compactly supported functions](space-of-continuous-compactly-supported-functions.md) of a locally compact Hausdorff space has a unique representing positive [Radon measure](radon-measure.md). Positivity gives boundedness on fixed compact supports: choose a compactly supported continuous cutoff $p\geq1$ on the support $K$, then $|I(f)|\leq\|f\|_\infty I(p)$ for real $f$ supported in $K$. The resulting measure is finite on compact sets but need not have finite total mass.
