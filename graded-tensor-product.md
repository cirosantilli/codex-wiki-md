# Graded tensor product

↑ **Parent:** [Graded algebra](graded-algebra.md)

The tensor product of graded algebras uses the displayed multiplication, incorporating the sign for interchanging homogeneous factors. This is the algebra structure in the cohomological [Künneth theorem](kunneth-theorem.md), and explains the signs of mixed intersection products on product manifolds.
