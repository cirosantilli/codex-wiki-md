# Optimal phase of a two-state rotary motor

↑ **Parent:** [Driven two-state rotary motor](driven-two-state-rotary-motor.md)

For the [driven two-state rotary motor](driven-two-state-rotary-motor.md) with a triangular bound potential of height $U_0$, the [torque](torque.md) scale is $T_0=U_0/\pi$. Differentiating the phase-averaged torque gives $\overline T'(s)=T_0[2P_A(s)-1]/\pi$. The decreasing crossing of $P_A=1/2$ therefore gives $s_{\max}=\ln(2\alpha)/\beta$ and $\overline T_{\max}=T_0\ln\cosh(\pi\beta/2)/(\pi\beta)$. The slow-speed limit is $T_0/2$; the fast-speed limit vanishes as $T_0\pi k_0/(8\omega)$.
