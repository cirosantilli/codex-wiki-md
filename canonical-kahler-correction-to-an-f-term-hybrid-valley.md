<h1 id="canonical-kahler-correction-to-an-f-term-hybrid-valley">Canonical-Kähler correction to an F-term hybrid valley</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F-term hybrid inflation](f-term-hybrid-inflation.md)

The [supergravity F-term potential](supergravity-f-term-potential.md) on the inflationary branch has the displayed exact form. Its expansion is $|g\zeta|^2(1+x^2/2+O(x^3))$, so the inflaton quadratic mass cancels at tree level for this canonical model. For $x>0$ its derivative is $|g\zeta|^2e^xx(1+x)>0$; the branch is therefore a transverse valley, not a stationary supersymmetry-breaking vacuum.
