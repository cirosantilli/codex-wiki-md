# Polarization-selective spontaneous emission between conducting plates

↑ **Parent:** [Spontaneous emission](spontaneous-emission.md)

Wide perfectly conducting plates with $d\ll c/\omega_0$ admit only a normal-electric-field [transverse electromagnetic mode](transverse-electromagnetic-mode.md) at the transition frequency. The [electric-dipole interaction](electric-dipole-interaction.md) therefore couples to the normal component of the atomic transition dipole. A normal dipole has enhanced available mode density; an in-plane dipole has no allowed electric-dipole radiation channel below the higher-mode cutoffs. The conclusion assumes negligible changes to the atom and negligible absorption, leakage and symmetry-breaking mixing.
