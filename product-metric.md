# Product metric

↑ **Parent:** [Metric space](metric-space.md)

For [metric spaces](metric-space.md) $(X_1,d_1)$ and $(X_2,d_2)$, the sum metric on $X_1\times X_2$ is the displayed formula. Nonnegativity, symmetry and the [triangle inequality](triangle-inequality.md) follow by adding the respective inequalities in the two factors, and zero distance forces equality in both coordinates. It induces the finite [product topology](product-topology.md), as does the [maximum product metric](maximum-product-metric.md), since their distances satisfy $d_{\max}\leq d_{\mathrm{sum}}\leq2d_{\max}$. A sequence converges for this [metric](metric.md) exactly when both coordinate sequences converge. Two successive extractions of a [convergent subsequence](convergent-subsequence.md) therefore show that the product of two [sequentially compact spaces](sequentially-compact-space.md) is sequentially compact.
