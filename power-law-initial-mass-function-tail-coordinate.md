# Power-law initial-mass-function tail coordinate

↑ **Parent:** [Initial mass function](initial-mass-function.md)

For an [initial mass function](initial-mass-function.md) proportional to $M^{-p}$ on $M\ge M_{\min}$ with $p>1$, integration and normalization give the displayed upper-tail fraction. The [probability integral transform](probability-integral-transform.md) makes $X$ uniform on $[0,1]$ for a randomly sampled birth mass. Evolutionary lifetime regions can then be measured as ordinary areas when the independent age coordinate is also uniform.
