<h1 id="neel-temperature">Néel temperature</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Antiferromagnetism](antiferromagnetism.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Néel_temperature)

The [Néel temperature](neel-temperature.md) marks loss of long-range [antiferromagnetism](antiferromagnetism.md). A [Curie temperature](curie-temperature.md) concerns [ferromagnetism](ferromagnetism.md) or [ferrimagnetism](ferrimagnetism.md); [magnetic compensation temperature](magnetic-compensation-temperature.md) instead denotes cancellation within an ordered [ferrimagnet](ferrimagnetism.md).[§5, pp.324–326; §§8,11–12: ordering versus compensation](magnetism-and-the-local-molecular-field-by-louis-neel.md)

In the simple collinear description, compare $|\mathbf L|$ across $T_N$, rather than expecting $|\mathbf M|$ to switch from nonzero to zero. Zero-field [magnetization](magnetization.md) may be zero on both sides. A nonzero [magnetic susceptibility](magnetic-susceptibility.md) is also compatible with [antiferromagnetism](antiferromagnetism.md): an applied [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) can disturb the cancellation. The [uniform response of a two-sublattice Ising antiferromagnet](uniform-response-of-a-two-sublattice-ising-antiferromagnet.md) provides a model calculation.
