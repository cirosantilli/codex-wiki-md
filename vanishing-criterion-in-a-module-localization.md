# Vanishing criterion in a module localization

↑ **Parent:** [Localization of a module](localization-of-a-module.md)

The fraction relation defining [localization of a module](localization-of-a-module.md) gives this criterion. For localization at powers of a single element $f$, a fraction vanishes exactly when some power of $f$ annihilates its numerator. Finitely many such vanishing relations can be cleared with one common exponent. This is distinct from detecting global zero by localizing at every prime ideal.
