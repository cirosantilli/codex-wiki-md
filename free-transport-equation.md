# Free transport equation

↑ **Parent:** [Transport equation](transport-equation.md)

Particles keep their velocity and move along straight trajectories. The [method of characteristics](method-of-characteristics.md) gives $f(t,x,v)=f_0(x-tv,v)$. The shear preserves [Lebesgue measure](lebesgue-measure.md) and every [Lp norm](lp-norm.md). Compact initial [support](support.md) remains compact at each finite time, but need not remain in one compact set for all times.
