# Solidification attachment mobility

↑ **Parent:** [Kinetic undercooling](kinetic-undercooling.md)

The attachment mobility $\mathcal M>0$ is the coefficient converting interfacial undercooling into a [solidification](freezing.md) speed. In the linear isotropic law, $U=\mathcal M(T_{\rm eq}-T_i)$, and capillarity makes $T_{\rm eq}=T_m-\gamma_T\mathcal K$. Thus $T_i=T_m-U/\mathcal M-\gamma_T\mathcal K$. This kinetic coefficient refers to a moving [phase boundary](phase-boundary.md), rather than an order-parameter relaxation coefficient. The [Gibbs--Thomson relation](gibbs-thomson-relation.md) determines $\gamma_T=T_m\gamma/(\rho L)$ for isotropic [solid-liquid surface energy](solid-liquid-surface-energy.md) $\gamma$.
