# Euclidean logarithmic norm

↑ **Parent:** [Operator norm](operator-norm.md)

The [Euclidean logarithmic norm](euclidean-logarithmic-norm.md) is the largest [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) of a [matrix](matrix.md)'s Hermitian part. Differentiating $\|y\|_2^2$ along $y'=Ay$ gives $\|e^{tA}\|_2\leq e^{t\mu_2(A)}$. It is the least exponent for such a bound with prefactor one, as a first-order expansion at zero shows. It equals the [spectral abscissa](spectral-abscissa.md) for a [normal matrix](normal-matrix.md), but need not do so otherwise. A scalar rational [stability function](stability-function.md) does not generally inherit a bound by its value at this real number.
