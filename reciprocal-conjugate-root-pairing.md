# Reciprocal-conjugate root pairing

↑ **Parent:** [Conjugate symmetry of trigonometric polynomial coefficients](conjugate-symmetry-of-trigonometric-polynomial-coefficients.md)

For a nonzero real-on-the-circle [trigonometric polynomial](trigonometric-polynomial.md) of actual order $d$, the [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) $P(z)=z^dp(z)$ satisfies $P(z)=z^{2d}\overline{P(1/\overline z)}$. Its constant term is the nonzero conjugate of its leading [coefficient](coefficient.md). Hence its nonzero [roots of a polynomial](root-of-a-polynomial.md) occur in pairs $\zeta,1/\overline\zeta$ with the same [multiplicity of a root](multiplicity-of-a-root.md). The [coefficient](coefficient.md) reversal and [complex conjugation](complex-conjugation.md) preserve the exponents of the paired factors. Fixed [roots of a polynomial](root-of-a-polynomial.md) on the [unit circle](complex-unit-circle.md) require nonnegativity, rather than this symmetry alone, to have even multiplicity.
