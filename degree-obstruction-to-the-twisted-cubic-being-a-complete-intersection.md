# Degree obstruction to the twisted cubic being a complete intersection

↑ **Parent:** [Twisted cubic](twisted-cubic.md)

The twisted cubic is a [nondegenerate projective variety](nondegenerate-projective-variety.md), so every hypersurface containing it has degree at least two. At least two hypersurfaces are needed to cut out a curve in $\mathbb P^3$, making their degree product at least four rather than the curve's degree three.
