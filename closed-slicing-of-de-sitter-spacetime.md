# Closed slicing of de Sitter spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [de Sitter spacetime](de-sitter-spacetime.md)

The vacuum [Einstein field equations](einstein-field-equations.md) with positive [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md) admit a closed spherical slicing with scale factor $a(t)=\ell\cosh[(t-t_0)/\ell]$. The time-symmetric slice is at $t=t_0$. The substitution $T=2\arctan(e^\tau)-\pi/2$ gives $dT/d\tau=\operatorname{sech}\tau$ and $\cos T=\operatorname{sech}\tau$, so the metric is $\ell^2\sec^2T[-dT^2+d\Omega_3^2]$. The conformal metric is a finite-time slab of the [Einstein static universe](einstein-static-universe.md), with spacelike conformal infinities $T=\pm\pi/2$.
