# Concurrence of a two-qubit Werner state

↑ **Parent:** [Two-qubit Werner state](two-qubit-werner-state.md)

For any normalized pure two-[qubit](qubit.md) vector, its squared overlap with a [maximally entangled state](maximally-entangled-state.md) is at most $(1+C)/2$: in a [Schmidt decomposition](schmidt-decomposition.md) the overlap is bounded by $(\sqrt\lambda+\sqrt{1-\lambda})/\sqrt2$. Averaging gives $C(W_F)\geq2F-1$. Equality follows from the six-state ensemble $\sqrt F\,|\Psi_-\rangle\pm\sqrt{1-F}\,|t_j\rangle$, with equal weights, where $t_1=\Psi_+$, $t_2=\Phi_-$ and $t_3=i\Phi_+$. The coherences cancel and each coefficient determinant gives pure concurrence $2F-1$.
