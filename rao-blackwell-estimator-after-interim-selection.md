# Rao-Blackwell estimator after interim selection

↑ **Parent:** [Conditional selection bias after futility continuation](conditional-selection-bias-after-futility-continuation.md)

For two independent stage means $X,Y\sim N(\delta,s^2)$ and continuation $X\ge c$, let $S=(X+Y)/2$ and $v=s/\sqrt2$. The second-stage mean is conditionally unbiased. The [Rao-Blackwell theorem](rao-blackwell-theorem.md) gives $E(Y\mid S,X\ge c)=S-v\lambda((c-S)/v)$, using $X\mid S\sim N(S,v^2)$ and truncation. This is a [conditionally unbiased estimator](conditionally-unbiased-estimator.md) using both stages through their combined [sample mean](sample-mean.md).
