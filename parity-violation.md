# Parity violation

↑ **Parent:** [Weak interaction](weak-interaction.md)

[Parity violation](parity-violation.md) means that a physical law fails to be invariant under the [spatial reflection](spatial-reflection.md) represented by the [parity operator](parity-operator.md). A process and its fully inverted counterpart can then have different [probabilities](probability.md). For [beta-minus decay](beta-minus-decay.md) with polarized parent [atomic nuclei](atomic-nucleus-split.md), a nonzero correlation between nuclear [spin](spin.md) and emitted [electron](electron.md) [momentum](momentum-split.md) is such a test. [Rotational symmetry](rotational-symmetry.md) alone allows this correlation: a [pseudoscalar](pseudoscalar.md) is unchanged by proper [rotations](rotation-mathematics.md) but changes sign under [spatial reflection](spatial-reflection.md).
