# C-type inactivation

↑ **Parent:** [Ion-channel inactivation](ion-channel-inactivation.md)

An availability change associated with the outer pore and selectivity filter of certain [potassium](potassium.md) [ion channels](ion-channel.md). Its kinetics and structural origin differ from [ball-and-chain inactivation](ball-and-chain-inactivation.md), although the two processes can interact.
