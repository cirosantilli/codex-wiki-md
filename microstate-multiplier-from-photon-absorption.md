# Microstate multiplier from photon absorption

↑ **Parent:** [Boltzmann's entropy formula](boltzmann-s-entropy-formula.md)

A single [photon](photon.md) thermalized in a macroscopic body adds [energy](energy-split.md) $hc/\lambda$. If its [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) is sufficiently large that the [temperature](temperature-split.md) change is negligible, the [entropy](entropy-split.md) increase is $hc/(\lambda T)$. The [Boltzmann entropy](boltzmann-s-entropy-formula.md) then gives the displayed ratio of accessible [microstates](microstate.md). The exponential can be enormous even though the deposited [energy](energy-split.md) is macroscopically tiny.
