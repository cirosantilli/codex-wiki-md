# Isotropic-to-areal weak-field radius change

↑ **Parent:** [Linearized Schwarzschild metric](linearized-schwarzschild-metric.md)

The area of a symmetry sphere in the weak-field isotropic metric is $4\pi\rho^2(1+2GM/\rho)$. Equating it to $4\pi r^2$ defines the [areal radius](areal-radius.md) and gives the displayed change. To first order $dr=d\rho$, and replacing $1/\rho$ by $1/r$ changes the perturbation only at second order. This explains why the isotropic angular correction disappears in the [linearized Schwarzschild metric](linearized-schwarzschild-metric.md) without discarding spatial curvature.
