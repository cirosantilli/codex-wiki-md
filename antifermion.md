# Antifermion

↑ **Parent:** [Antiparticle](antiparticle.md)

The [antiparticle](antiparticle.md) of a [fermion](fermion.md). In a [Dirac field](dirac-field.md), its external wavefunctions are the negative-frequency $v$ [spinors](spinor.md), while its physical [energy](energy-split.md) is positive. Fermion-number arrows on [Feynman diagrams](feynman-diagram.md) run opposite an antifermion's physical [momentum](momentum-split.md).
