# Airfoil

↑ **Parent:** [Fluid mechanics](fluid-mechanics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airfoil)

An airfoil is a body profile designed to generate [lift](lift-force.md) in a surrounding flow. In two-dimensional [potential flow](potential-flow.md), its circulation-dependent lift per unit span is described by the [Kutta–Joukowski theorem](kutta-joukowski-theorem.md).
