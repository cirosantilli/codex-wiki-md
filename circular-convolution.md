# Circular convolution

↑ **Parent:** [Discrete convolution](discrete-convolution.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circular_convolution)

Circular convolution of two length-$N$ [sequences](sequence.md) uses [modular arithmetic](modular-arithmetic-split.md) for their indices. Extending both inputs periodically gives the displayed formula. Equivalently, fold a finite [discrete convolution](discrete-convolution.md) into residue classes modulo $N$ by adding entries whose indices are congruent. The [discrete Fourier transform](discrete-fourier-transform-split.md) of the circular convolution is the product of the two [discrete Fourier transforms](discrete-fourier-transform-split.md), with the usual unnormalized forward transform and $1/N$ inverse convention.
