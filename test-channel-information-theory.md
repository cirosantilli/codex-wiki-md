# Test channel (information theory)

↑ **Parent:** [Rate-distortion function](rate-distortion-function.md)

A [test channel](test-channel-information-theory.md) is a proposed [conditional distribution](conditional-distribution.md) of a reconstruction given the source symbol. It describes a candidate joint [probability distribution](probability-distribution-split.md) for evaluating [mutual information](mutual-information.md) and [expected distortion](expected-distortion.md). It need not be a physical transmission [channel](communication-channel.md), or an implementable one-symbol [encoder](channel-encoder.md); the [Shannon rate-distortion theorem](shannon-s-rate-distortion-theorem.md) relates this optimization to long [block codes](block-code.md).
