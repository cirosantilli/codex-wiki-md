# Crooks fluctuation theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Fluctuation theorem](fluctuation-theorem.md)

For a system coupled to a [thermal reservoir](heat-bath.md) at fixed [temperature](temperature.md) $T$, prepare the forward process in the initial [canonical ensemble](canonical-ensemble.md) and the reverse process in the final control parameter's [canonical ensemble](canonical-ensemble.md). Reverse the protocol, assume [microscopic reversibility](microscopic-reversibility.md) and matching path [probability support](support-of-a-probability-distribution.md), and count $W$ as [work](work.md) done on the system. With [Helmholtz free energy](helmholtz-free-energy.md) difference $\Delta F=F_{\rm final}-F_{\rm initial}$ and $\beta=1/(k_BT)$,

$$
\frac{P_F(W)}{P_R(-W)}=e^{\beta(W-\Delta F)}.
$$

The final forward distribution need not already be in [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium.md). Its reverse preparation is prescribed equilibrium preparation, rather than the actual forward final distribution used in [finite trajectory entropy production](finite-trajectory-entropy-production.md). Thus the paired path [logarithm](logarithm.md) $\beta(W-\Delta F)$ is dissipated [work](work.md) in thermal units, not automatically the instantaneous total stochastic [entropy production](entropy-production.md) at the end of an arbitrary driven protocol.
