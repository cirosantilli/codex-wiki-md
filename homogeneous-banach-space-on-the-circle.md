# Homogeneous Banach space on the circle

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier analysis](fourier-analysis-split.md)

A [Banach space](banach-space-split.md) $B\subseteq L^1(\mathbb T)$ is homogeneous when its inclusion into $L^1$ is continuous, circle translations act isometrically, and each orbit is continuous in the $B$ norm. One commonly normalizes $\|f\|_1\leq\|f\|_B$. The [continuous functions](continuous-function.md) with the [supremum norm](supremum-norm.md), and $L^p(\mathbb T)$ for $1\leq p<\infty$, are examples. Full $L^\infty$ fails translation continuity. If $g$ is continuous, the [Bochner integral](bochner-integral.md) $f*g=\int g(t)\tau_tf\,dt/(2\pi)$ belongs to $B$ and satisfies $\|f*g\|_B\leq\|f\|_B\|g\|_1$. Approximation by [continuous functions](continuous-function.md) extends this to every $g\in L^1$.
