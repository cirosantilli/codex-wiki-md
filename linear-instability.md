# Linear instability

↑ **Parent:** [Linearization of a dynamical system](linearization-of-a-dynamical-system.md)

[Linear instability](linear-instability.md) is growth of small disturbances in the linearized dynamics. An eigenmode with positive temporal [growth rate](growth-rate.md) is sufficient to establish it. In a non-normal system, finite transient amplification is a different issue from the existence of an exponentially growing eigenmode. For [normal modes](normal-mode.md) written as $e^{i\alpha(x-ct)}$, the temporal rate is $\alpha\operatorname{Im}c$.
