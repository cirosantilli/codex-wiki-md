# Two-dimensional modified Helmholtz fundamental solution

↑ **Parent:** [Modified Helmholtz equation](modified-helmholtz-equation.md)

For $m>0$, the decaying fundamental solution of $-\Delta+m^2$ in two dimensions is $K_0(mr)/(2\pi)$, with $K_0$ the [Modified Bessel function of the second kind](modified-bessel-function-of-the-second-kind.md). [Green second identity](green-second-identity.md) reconstructs an interior solution from its [Dirichlet boundary condition](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) $f$ and outward [normal derivative](normal-derivative.md) $h$ as $q(r)=\int_{\partial\Omega}(\Gamma_m h-f\partial_n\Gamma_m)ds$.
