# Bisimulation

↑ **Parent:** [Rooted directed graph](rooted-directed-graph.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bisimulation)

A bisimulation relates the roots of two [rooted directed graphs](rooted-directed-graph.md) and matches every successor move in either graph with a successor move in the other leading to related vertices. Multiple successors may be related to the same vertex. This local matching is weaker than graph isomorphism.
