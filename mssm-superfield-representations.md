# MSSM superfield representations

↑ **Parent:** [Minimal supersymmetric Standard Model](minimal-supersymmetric-standard-model.md)

In the [MSSM](minimal-supersymmetric-standard-model.md), each [fermion generation](fermion-generation.md) has [chiral superfields](chiral-superfield.md) $Q:(3,2,1/6)$, $U^c:(\bar3,1,-2/3)$, $D^c:(\bar3,1,1/3)$, $L:(1,2,-1/2)$, and $E^c:(1,1,1)$. This uses [hypercharge](hypercharge.md) normalized by $Q_{\rm electric}=T^3+Y$ and only left-handed [Weyl spinors](weyl-spinor.md). Two [Higgs chiral doublets](higgs-chiral-doublet.md) have charges $\pm1/2$. The three [vector superfields](vector-superfield.md) transform as $(8,1,0)$, $(1,3,0)$ and $(1,1,0)$, supplying the [gauge bosons](gauge-boson.md) and [gauginos](gaugino.md).
