# CoGeNT experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Dark-matter direct detection](dark-matter-direct-detection.md)

A low-threshold point-contact [germanium](germanium.md) [ionization](ionization.md) search. The small readout capacitance supports sensitivity to low-energy interactions. Bulk/surface charge collection, internal lines, threshold response and time dependence must be controlled before interpreting an excess or modulation as a [WIMP](weakly-interacting-massive-particle.md) recoil.[detector and event-population analysis](search-for-an-annual-modulation-in-a-p-type-point-contact-germanium-dark-matter-detector-by-c-e-aalseth-and-others.md)
