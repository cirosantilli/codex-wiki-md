# Second isomorphism theorem for groups

↑ **Parent:** [Group theory](group-theory-split.md)

For a [subgroup](subgroup.md) $H\leq G$ and a [normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md) $N\trianglelefteq G$, the map $h\mapsto hN$ from $H$ onto $HN/N$ is a [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md) with kernel $H\cap N$. The [first isomorphism theorem](first-isomorphism-theorem.md) gives the displayed [group isomorphism](group-isomorphism.md). The product $HN$ is a [subgroup](subgroup.md) because $N$ is normal. In particular, if $HN=G$ and $H\cap N=1$, then $H\cong G/N$.
