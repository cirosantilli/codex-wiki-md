# Alpha-Omega dynamo

↑ **Parent:** [Mean-field dynamo](mean-field-dynamo.md)

An alpha-Omega [mean-field dynamo](mean-field-dynamo.md) couples toroidal-field generation by the [Omega effect](omega-effect.md) to poloidal-field regeneration by the [alpha effect](alpha-effect.md). A simple Fourier model is $\dot A=\alpha_0 f(t)B-\eta k^2A$, $\dot B=ik\Omega A-\eta k^2B$, where $A$ is a component of the [vector potential](vector-potential.md) for the poloidal field and $B$ is a toroidal-field amplitude. For bounded $|f|\leq1$, the [bounded-modulation alpha-Omega growth estimate](bounded-modulation-alpha-omega-growth-estimate.md) controls the maximum amplification.
