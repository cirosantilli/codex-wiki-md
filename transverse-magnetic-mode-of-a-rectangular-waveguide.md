# Transverse magnetic mode of a rectangular waveguide

↑ **Parent:** [Rectangular electromagnetic waveguide](rectangular-electromagnetic-waveguide.md)

In a hollow [rectangular electromagnetic waveguide](rectangular-electromagnetic-waveguide.md) with [perfect conductor](perfect-conductor.md) walls and axis $z$, a [transverse magnetic polarization](transverse-magnetic-polarization.md) has $B_z=0$ and a nonzero longitudinal [electric field](electric-field-split.md) $E_z$. The wall [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md) impose $E_z=0$ around the transverse rectangle. [Separation of variables](separation-of-variables.md) therefore gives $E_z=E_0\sin(n\pi x/a)\sin(m\pi y/b)e^{i(k_z z-\omega t)}$, with $n,m\geq1$. Setting either index to zero makes the entire field vanish. Thus there is no $\mathrm{TM}_{n0}$ or $\mathrm{TM}_{0m}$ branch. The transverse [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) is $k_\perp^2=(n\pi/a)^2+(m\pi/b)^2$, with [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation-split.md) $\omega^2/c^2=k_z^2+k_\perp^2$. This exclusion is specific to this geometry and these [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md).
