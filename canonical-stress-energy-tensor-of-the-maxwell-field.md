# Canonical stress-energy tensor of the Maxwell field

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical stress-energy tensor](canonical-stress-energy-tensor.md)

For signature $(+,-,-,-)$, translation invariance of the [Maxwell Lagrangian](maxwell-lagrangian.md) gives this tensor. It is generally neither symmetric nor gauge invariant. The [antisymmetric superpotential improvement](antisymmetric-superpotential-improvement.md) $-\partial_\rho(F^{\rho\mu}A^\nu)$ produces, on the source-free equations of motion, the symmetric [electromagnetic stress-energy tensor](electromagnetic-stress-energy-tensor.md) $\Theta^{\mu\nu}=-F^{\mu\rho}F^\nu{}_{\rho}+\eta^{\mu\nu}F^2/4$, which is traceless in four spacetime dimensions.
