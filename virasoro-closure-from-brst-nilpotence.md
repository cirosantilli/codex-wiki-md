# Virasoro closure from BRST nilpotence

↑ **Parent:** [BRST operator](brst-operator.md)

If $L_m=\{b_m,Q\}$, nilpotence gives $[L_m,Q]=0$. The [graded Jacobi identity](graded-jacobi-identity.md) and $[b_m,L_n]=(m-n)b_{m+n}$ then imply the displayed algebra. Thus a quantum central extension in the total generators obstructs [BRST nilpotence](brst-nilpotence.md).
