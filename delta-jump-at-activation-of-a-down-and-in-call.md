# Delta jump at activation of a down-and-in call

↑ **Parent:** [Down-and-in claim](down-and-in-claim.md)

For strike $c>b$, write $C(s,c,\tau)$ for the ordinary [European call option](european-call-option.md) value. Before activation the [down-and-in claim](down-and-in-claim.md) is $I(s,\tau)=(b/s)^\alpha C(b^2/s,c,\tau)$, where $\alpha=2\rho/\sigma^2-1$. Consequently its left-limit [option delta](option-delta.md) at the barrier is $-\alpha C(b,c,\tau)/b-C_s(b,c,\tau)$, whereas after activation it is $C_s(b,c,\tau)$. Their difference is strictly positive for $\tau>0$: differentiating the killed normal transition density at its boundary gives the integral of a strictly positive function against the positive call payoff. The value is continuous, so changing the bank holding finances the [stock](stock.md) rebalance.
