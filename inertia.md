# Inertia

↑ **Parent:** [Classical mechanics](classical-mechanics-split.md)

[Inertia](inertia.md) is the resistance of a body's motion to change under applied force. For a particle, [Newton's second law](newton-s-second-law.md) relates force to the rate of change of momentum; at constant mass it is $F=ma$. In an overdamped approximation the inertial acceleration term is small compared with drag over the time scales considered.
