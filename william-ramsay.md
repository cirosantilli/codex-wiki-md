# William Ramsay

↑ **Parent:** [List of chemists](list-of-chemists.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Ramsay)

A [chemist](chemist.md) who led the chemistry department at University College London from 1887 to 1913. His isolation of [argon](argon.md) followed [John William Strutt, 3rd Baron Rayleigh](john-william-strutt-3rd-baron-rayleigh.md)'s observation that atmospheric [nitrogen](nitrogen.md) had greater [density](density.md) than chemically prepared [nitrogen](nitrogen.md).[History of the Department, Ramsay and the nobel gases: chemistry professorship, headship dates and argon isolation](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/mathematical-physical-sciences/chemistry/about-us/history-department)
