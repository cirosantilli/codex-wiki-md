# Henkin omission extension lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Omitting types theorem](omitting-types-theorem.md)

Let $p(\mathbf x)$ be a [nonprincipal partial type](nonprincipal-partial-type.md) over a consistent [first-order theory](first-order-theory.md) $T$. Suppose $T\cup\{\sigma(\mathbf c)\}$ is consistent, with $\sigma$ a finite condition in finitely many new constants, and let $\mathbf t(\mathbf c)$ be a tuple of closed terms. Some $\psi\in p$ leaves $T\cup\{\sigma(\mathbf c),\neg\psi(\mathbf t(\mathbf c))\}$ consistent. Otherwise the original-language [first-order formula](first-order-formula.md)

$$
\theta(\mathbf x)=\exists\mathbf z\bigl(\sigma(\mathbf z)\land\mathbf x=\mathbf t(\mathbf z)\bigr)
$$

is consistent with $T$ and entails every member of $p$, contradicting nonprincipality. Replace all new constants occurring in the condition or terms by variables. This lemma makes the omission requirements dense in a countable [Henkin construction](henkin-construction.md).
