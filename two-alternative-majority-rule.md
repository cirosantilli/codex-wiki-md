# Two-alternative majority rule

↑ **Parent:** [Social choice function](social-choice-function.md)

With an odd number of agents and two alternatives, select the alternative ranked first by a strict majority. The rule is onto, [strategyproof](strategyproofness.md), and for at least three agents has no [dictator in social choice](dictator-in-social-choice.md). A pivotal agent already gets its preferred alternative by reporting truthfully; a nonpivotal agent cannot change the outcome alone.
