# Time-reversal parity of a fermion electric dipole operator

↑ **Parent:** [Fermion electric dipole moment operator](fermion-electric-dipole-moment-operator.md)

For real $a$, the Hermitian effective interaction $ia\bar\psi\sigma^{\mu\nu}\gamma^5\psi F_{\mu\nu}$ is odd under [time-reversal symmetry](t-symmetry.md). The spinor tensor and [gauge field strength](gauge-field-strength.md) both acquire $-t_\mu t_\nu$, while the [chirality matrix](chirality-matrix.md) is even. Their signs cancel, leaving the minus sign from the explicit $i$ under [antiunitarity](antiunitary-operator.md). By the [CPT theorem](cpt-theorem.md) this is also [CP violation](cp-violation.md). It is not a renormalizable tree-level [Standard Model](standard-model-split.md) term, but a radiatively generated low-energy dipole is allowed because of the [CP-violating phase](cp-violating-phase.md) in the [CKM matrix](cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md).
