# Correlogram

↑ **Parent:** [Sample autocorrelation function](sample-autocorrelation-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Correlogram)

A correlogram plots the estimated [autocorrelation function](autocorrelation.md) against lag. The plot helps distinguish the finite [autocorrelation](autocorrelation.md) cutoff of a [moving-average model](moving-average-model.md) from the decaying dependence of a causal [autoregressive model](autoregressive-model.md). Estimated cutoffs are diagnostics, not exact finite-sample identities.
