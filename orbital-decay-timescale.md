# Orbital decay timescale

↑ **Parent:** [Circular gravitational-wave inspiral](circular-gravitational-wave-inspiral.md)

An instantaneous [orbital decay timescale](orbital-decay-timescale.md) is positive even when the orbital period decreases. For a constant-mass circular binary with $\dot A=-C/A^3$ and $P\propto A^{3/2}$, $\tau_P=2A^4/(3C)$. Integrating to formal zero separation instead gives $t_{\rm merge}=A^4/(4C)=3\tau_P/8$. A local logarithmic decay time is not generally the remaining lifetime.
