# Maximum-norm bound for spline projection

↑ **Parent:** [Spline approximation](spline-approximation.md)

For the orthogonal [B-spline](b-spline.md) projector $P$, the normal equations in the [mixed-normalization spline Gram matrix](mixed-normalization-spline-gram-matrix.md) give $Ga=r$, with $r_i=(M_i,f)$. Positivity and unit integral of $M_i$ imply $\|r\|_{\ell^\infty}\leq\|f\|_\infty$. Nonnegativity and subpartition of unity of $N_i$ give $\|\sum_ia_iN_i\|_\infty\leq\|a\|_{\ell^\infty}$. Hence $\|P\|_\infty\leq\|G^{-1}\|_{\ell^\infty}$, where the matrix norm is maximum absolute row sum. The range is the spline space, which lies in $C[0,1]$ only for continuous splines.
