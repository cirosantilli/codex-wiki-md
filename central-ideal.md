# Central ideal

↑ **Parent:** [Center of a Lie algebra](center-of-a-lie-algebra.md)

A central ideal is a [linear subspace](vector-subspace.md) of the [center of a Lie algebra](center-of-a-lie-algebra.md). It is a [Lie algebra ideal](ideal-of-a-lie-algebra.md) because its [Lie bracket](lie-bracket.md) with every element is zero. Passing to the [quotient Lie algebra](quotient-lie-algebra.md) removes this central subspace. For $\mathfrak{sl}_p$ in [characteristic](characteristic-of-a-field.md) $p$, the identity matrix has zero [trace](matrix-trace.md) and spans a central ideal.
