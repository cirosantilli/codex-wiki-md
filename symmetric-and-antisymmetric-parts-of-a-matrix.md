# Symmetric and antisymmetric parts of a matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Linear algebra](linear-algebra-split.md)

Every square matrix decomposes uniquely as $A=(A+A^T)/2+(A-A^T)/2$, a symmetric part plus an antisymmetric part.
