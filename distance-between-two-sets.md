# Distance between two sets

↑ **Parent:** [Distance to a set](distance-to-a-set.md)

The set distance is the infimum of pairwise [metric](metric.md) distances. Two nonempty disjoint [compact sets](compact-space.md) have positive set distance: continuity attains the minimum on their [Cartesian product](cartesian-product.md), and a zero minimum would give a common point. Disjoint closed sets without compactness need not have positive separation.
