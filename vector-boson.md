# Vector boson

↑ **Parent:** [Boson](boson.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vector_boson)

A [boson](boson.md) with spin one. A massive [vector boson](vector-boson.md) has three physical [particle polarizations](particle-polarization.md). A complex [Proca field](proca-field.md) describes a charged particle and its antiparticle, each with three [particle polarizations](particle-polarization.md); the free classical field has three complex dynamical components after its [constraints](constraint-mechanics.md) are enforced.
