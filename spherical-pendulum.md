# Spherical pendulum

↑ **Parent:** [Simple pendulum](simple-pendulum-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spherical_pendulum)

A [point mass](point-mass.md) constrained to a fixed [distance](distance.md) $r$ from a pivot and free to move in three dimensions under uniform [gravitational acceleration](gravitational-acceleration.md). With the vertical axis downward and [polar angle](polar-angle.md) $\theta$ measured from it, the [Lagrangian](lagrangian.md) is the expression in the title. The [azimuth](azimuth.md) $\phi$ is a [cyclic coordinate](cyclic-coordinate.md), so $mr^2\sin^2\theta\dot\phi$ is conserved [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) about the vertical axis. A rigid massless rod enforces the constraint without the loss-of-tension limitation of a string.
