<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Differentiate the argument through the nonvanishing curve, using $g'=\operatorname{Im}(\beta'/\beta)$. Since $\beta'=-ie^{i\theta}$,

$$
g'(\theta)=\operatorname{Im}\frac{-ie^{i\theta}(\overline w-e^{-i\theta})}{|w-e^{i\theta}|^2}
=\frac{1-w_1\cos\theta-w_2\sin\theta}{|w-e^{i\theta}|^2}.
$$

Also $|w-e^{i\theta}|^2=1+|w|^2-2(w_1\cos\theta+w_2\sin\theta)$. Subtracting one therefore gives

$$
\boxed{-1+2g'(\theta)=\frac{1-|w|^2}{|w-e^{i\theta}|^2}>0.}
$$

Thus the lifted boundary angle $\phi(\theta)=-\theta+2g(\theta)$ is strictly increasing. Its total change around the circle is $2\pi$, because the [disk automorphism](../../../../automorphism-of-the-unit-disk.md) has boundary degree one. The same total change follows directly from the [winding number](../../../../winding-number.md) one of $w-e^{i\theta}$. This is the [boundary-angle derivative of a disk automorphism](../../../../boundary-angle-derivative-of-a-disk-automorphism.md).
