↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Apply part (a) to the entire function $f(z)=e^{i\theta}z$. Its derivative has modulus one, so its [conformal Brownian clock](../../../../../conformal-brownian-clock.md) is $A_t=t$. Thus

$$
e^{i\theta}Z_t=e^{i\theta}Z_0+\widetilde Z_t,
$$

which is a [planar Brownian motion](../../../../../planar-brownian-motion.md) starting from the rotated initial point. In particular, if the initial point is zero, its law is unchanged. This is [rotational invariance of planar Brownian motion](../../../../../rotational-invariance-of-planar-brownian-motion.md).
