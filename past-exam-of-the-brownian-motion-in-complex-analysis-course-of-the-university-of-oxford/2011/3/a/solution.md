<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A bounded [holomorphic function](../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) of planar Brownian motion is a local martingale up to exit, because its real and imaginary parts are harmonic and [Itô's formula](../../../../ito-s-lemma.md) has zero drift. Stop before the given $S<T_D$. Localization inside compact subdisks gives the local martingale $f(Z_{t\wedge S}^z)$, and $|f|<1$ makes it bounded. The [bounded local martingale criterion](../../../../bounded-local-martingale-criterion.md) therefore gives a true martingale.

Exit from the unit disk is finite almost surely, so $S<T_D$ is finite too. Taking $t\to\infty$ and using the [dominated convergence theorem](../../../../dominated-convergence-theorem.md) yields

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[f(Z_S^z)]=f(z).}
$$

The same proof works for any bounded [holomorphic function](../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md), after rescaling if needed; it does not require the stopping time itself to be bounded.
