# Greedy colouring by removing independent sets

↑ **Parent:** [Graph coloring](graph-coloring.md)

If every [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) subset of size at least $m$ contains an [independent set](independent-set-graph-theory.md) of size $r$, repeatedly colour and remove an $r$-element [independent set](independent-set-graph-theory.md) while at least $m$ [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) remain. Giving each remaining [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) its own colour uses at most $|V|/r+m$ colours.
