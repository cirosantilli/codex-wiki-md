# Eddy buoyancy flux

↑ **Parent:** [Transformed Eulerian mean](transformed-eulerian-mean.md)

The local meridional eddy [buoyancy](buoyancy.md) flux is the covariance of disturbance meridional [velocity](velocity.md) with disturbance [buoyancy](buoyancy.md). In a [Boussinesq approximation](boussinesq-approximation.md), $b'=-g\rho'/\rho_0$. This is a local transport [density](density.md), distinct from a plume's cross-section-integrated [buoyancy flux](buoyancy-flux.md). In the [transformed Eulerian mean](transformed-eulerian-mean.md), division by the background [buoyancy frequency](buoyancy-frequency.md) squared gives an eddy-induced [meridional overturning streamfunction](meridional-overturning-streamfunction.md).
