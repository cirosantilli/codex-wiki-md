# Almost sure submartingale convergence theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Martingale convergence theorem](martingale-convergence-theorem.md)

A [submartingale](submartingale.md) with $\sup_n\mathbb E Y_n^+<\infty$ converges almost surely to a finite [integrable random variable](integrable-random-variable.md). The positive-part bound also bounds negative parts because $\mathbb E Y_n\geq\mathbb E Y_0$. The result specializes to an $L^1$-bounded [martingale](martingale-split.md), and hence to every nonnegative [martingale](martingale-split.md). It does not by itself give [convergence in L1](convergence-in-l1.md); [uniform integrability](uniform-integrability.md) supplies that stronger conclusion.
