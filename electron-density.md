# Electron density

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum chemistry](quantum-chemistry.md)

The electron density describes the expected number of [electrons](electron.md) per unit [volume](volume.md) at position $\mathbf r$. For a system with a fixed number $N$ of [electrons](electron.md), $\int n(\mathbf r)\,d^3\mathbf r=N$. A [Lewis structure](lewis-structure.md) allocates electron counts without specifying this spatial density.
