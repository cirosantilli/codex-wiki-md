# Adaptive rejection sampling

↑ **Parent:** [Rejection sampling](rejection-sampling.md)

Adaptive rejection sampling builds a piecewise linear upper envelope for a differentiable [log-concave probability density](log-concave-probability-density.md), samples from its piecewise exponential form, and accepts with the target-to-envelope ratio. Adding evaluated points improves the envelope without changing the target distribution. Initial tangent slopes must yield integrable tails. The accepted draws are exact samples, unlike a finite run of [Markov chain Monte Carlo](markov-chain-monte-carlo.md).
