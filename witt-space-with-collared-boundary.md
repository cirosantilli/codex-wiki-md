# Witt space with collared boundary

↑ **Parent:** [Witt space](witt-space.md)

A compact [stratified space](stratified-space.md) with boundary is a [Witt space](witt-space.md) with collared boundary when its interior and boundary are [Witt spaces](witt-space.md) and a stratified neighbourhood of the boundary is $\partial X\times[0,\varepsilon)$, with product stratification. [Orientations](orientation-of-a-simplex.md) are carried by the regular strata and induce the usual boundary [orientation](orientation-of-a-simplex.md).
