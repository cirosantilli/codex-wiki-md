# Supremum convergence for decreasing continuous functions

↑ **Parent:** [Compact Hausdorff space](compact-hausdorff-space.md)

On a nonempty [compact Hausdorff space](compact-hausdorff-space.md), let continuous nonnegative functions decrease pointwise. Their suprema decrease to a finite number $L$. For every $t<L$, the sets $\{f_n\geq t\}$ are nonempty nested closed sets; compactness gives a common point with limit value at least $t$. Thus the supremum of the pointwise limit is $L$. The limit need not be continuous, and this conclusion does not claim uniform convergence as in the [Dini theorem](dini-s-theorem.md).
