# Scaleless integral

↑ **Parent:** [Dimensional regularization](dimensional-regularization.md)

A [Feynman integral](feynman-integral.md) with no nonzero [mass](mass.md) or external [momentum](momentum-split.md) scale is assigned zero by [dimensional regularization](dimensional-regularization.md). This does not imply separate absence of [ultraviolet divergences](ultraviolet-divergence.md) and [infrared divergences](infrared-divergence.md). For a logarithmically scaleless example, zero can encode cancellation of the two pole contributions. Keeping a scale or using [auxiliary-mass extraction of ultraviolet poles](auxiliary-mass-extraction-of-ultraviolet-poles.md) is necessary when only the ultraviolet part is sought.
