# Elastic beam

↑ **Parent:** [Elasticity (physics)](elasticity-physics-split.md)

An [elastic beam](elastic-beam.md) is a slender body that supports bending moments and transverse loads. In the small-slope Euler-Bernoulli approximation its bending moment is $M=EIy_{xx}$, and transverse momentum balance gives $\rho Ay_{tt}+EIy_{xxxx}=q$ for a uniform beam under distributed force per length $q$. Here $I$ is the [second moment of area](second-moment-of-area.md); boundary conditions specify the end constraints and applied moments and forces.
