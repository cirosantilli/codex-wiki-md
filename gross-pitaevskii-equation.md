<h1 id="gross-pitaevskii-equation">Gross–Pitaevskii equation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Bose-Einstein condensation](bose-einstein-condensation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gross–Pitaevskii_equation)

The [Gross–Pitaevskii equation](gross-pitaevskii-equation.md) describes a dilute interacting condensate through a complex [order parameter](order-parameter.md) $\psi$, normalized so that $|\psi|^2$ is [number density](number-density.md). Repulsive contact interactions have $g>0$. In a frame rotating at the condensate frequency, the operator includes $-\mu$, with [chemical potential](chemical-potential.md) $\mu$. It is generated by a [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) [functional](functional.md); its homogeneous positive-density state has $n_0=\mu/g$ when $g,\mu>0$.
