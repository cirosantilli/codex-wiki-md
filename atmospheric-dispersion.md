# Atmospheric dispersion

↑ **Parent:** [Atmospheric refraction](atmospheric-refraction.md)

Atmospheric dispersion is the wavelength dependence of [atmospheric refraction](atmospheric-refraction.md). It spreads a broadband stellar image along the vertical direction toward the zenith.
