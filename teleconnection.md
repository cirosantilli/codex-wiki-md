# Teleconnection

↑ **Parent:** [Meteorology](meteorology.md)

Correlated atmospheric variability between geographically separated regions. Wave propagation, mean-flow changes and coupled atmosphere–ocean forcing can produce physical connections, while statistical correlation alone does not identify a unique cause. A [Rossby-wave teleconnection](rossby-wave-teleconnection.md) is an important large-scale pathway rather than an exhaustive definition of all teleconnections.
