# Tectonic plate

↑ **Parent:** [Plate tectonics](plate-tectonics.md)

A region of [lithosphere](lithosphere.md) approximated as rigid on the spatial and temporal scale of a plate-kinematic model. Its instantaneous motion on a [sphere](sphere.md) is a [rotation](rotation-mathematics.md); deformation within boundary zones limits the rigid-plate approximation.
