# Symmetrization map in characteristic two

↑ **Parent:** [Transpose](transpose.md)

Over a [field](field.md) of [characteristic two](characteristic-two.md), the [linear map](linear-map.md) $T:M_n(F)\to M_n(F)$, $A\mapsto A+A^T$, has [kernel of a linear map](kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) the [symmetric matrices](symmetric-matrix.md), of [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) $n(n+1)/2$. Its [image of a linear map](image-of-a-linear-map.md) is the [linear subspace](vector-subspace.md) of [symmetric matrices](symmetric-matrix.md) with zero diagonal, of [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) $n(n-1)/2$. Indeed, $T(A)=0$ means $A=A^T$, and every symmetric zero-diagonal [matrix](matrix.md) is the image of the [matrix](matrix.md) obtained by retaining just its entries above the diagonal. Thus the two [dimensions](dimension-vector-space.md) still add to $n^2$, as required by the [rank-nullity theorem](rank-nullity-theorem.md).
