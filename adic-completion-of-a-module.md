# Adic completion of a module

↑ **Parent:** [Adic topology](adic-topology.md)

The adic completion is the [inverse limit](inverse-limit.md) of the [quotient modules](quotient-module.md) $M/I^nM$. An element is a compatible family of residue classes at every order. For $R=k[t]$ and $M=V\otimes_k k[t]$, this gives the [formal power series module](formal-power-series-module.md) $V[[t]]$. The canonical map from $M$ need not be injective in general: its [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) is $\bigcap_n I^nM$. It is injective for this polynomial example because a nonzero polynomial has finite degree. Successive changes of coordinates in [formal rigidity from vanishing second Hochschild cohomology](formal-rigidity-from-vanishing-second-hochschild-cohomology.md) converge in this completion because the order-$r$ change is the identity modulo $t^r$.
