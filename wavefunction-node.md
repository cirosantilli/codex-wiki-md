# Wavefunction node

↑ **Parent:** [Node (physics)](node-physics.md)

A point or surface at which a [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) vanishes. In one dimension a simple [node](node-dynamical-systems.md) of a real stationary [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) changes its sign. A [node](node-dynamical-systems.md) is not generally the same as a [classical turning point](classical-turning-point.md); for a smooth finite potential, the stationary [Schrödinger equation](schrodinger-equation.md) also makes the second [derivative](derivative.md) vanish at a [wavefunction node](wavefunction-node.md).
