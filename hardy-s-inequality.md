<h1 id="hardy-s-inequality">Hardy's inequality</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Real analysis](real-analysis-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hardy's_inequality)

[Hardy's inequality](hardy-s-inequality.md) bounds averages or weighted functions by their original size or [derivatives](derivative.md). For $p>1$ and $f\geq0$, its integral form is $\int_0^\infty(x^{-1}\int_0^xf(t)\,dt)^p\,dx\leq(p/(p-1))^p\int_0^\infty f(x)^p\,dx$. Spatial weighted forms include the [Hardy inequality in Euclidean space](hardy-inequality-in-euclidean-space.md).
