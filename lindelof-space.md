<h1 id="lindelof-space">Lindelöf space</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Topological space](topological-space.md)

A [Lindelöf space](lindelof-space.md) is a [topological space](topological-space.md) in which every [open cover](open-cover.md) has a [countable](countable-set.md) subcover. A [closed discrete subset](closed-discrete-subset.md) of a [Lindelöf space](lindelof-space.md) must be [countable](countable-set.md): cover each of its points by an [open set](open-set.md) meeting it at only that point and add the [open](open-set.md) [complement](complement-of-a-set.md). In a [metrizable space](metrizable-space.md), the [Lindelöf](lindelof-space.md) property is equivalent to [topological separability](separable-topological-space.md).
