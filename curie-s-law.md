<h1 id="curie-s-law">Curie's law</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic susceptibility](magnetic-susceptibility.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curie's_law)

For independent moments of angular momentum $J$ at small $\mu_B|B|/(k_BT)$, the zero-field [variance](variance-split.md) is $\langle m^2\rangle=J(J+1)/3$. Differentiating the canonical mean gives the [magnetic susceptibility](magnetic-susceptibility.md)

$$
\chi=\frac{N\mu_B^2J(J+1)}{3k_BT}.
$$

Thus susceptibility is inversely proportional to temperature. The small-field or high-temperature condition distinguishes this law from the exponentially vanishing fixed-field susceptibility of a saturated paramagnet at low temperature.
