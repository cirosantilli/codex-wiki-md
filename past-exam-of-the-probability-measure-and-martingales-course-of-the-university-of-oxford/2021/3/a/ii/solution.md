<h1 id="3/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [product measure](../../../../../product-measure.md) $\mu\otimes\nu$ is the unique [probability measure](../../../../../probability-measure.md) on the [sigma-algebra](../../../../../sigma-algebra.md) generated by measurable rectangles for which

$$
\boxed{(\mu\otimes\nu)(C\times D)=\mu(C)\nu(D)
\quad\text{for all }C,D\in\mathcal B(\mathbb R).}
$$

Here the printed product notation $\mathcal B(\mathbb R)\times\mathcal B(\mathbb R)$ denotes the generated [product sigma-algebra](../../../../../product-sigma-algebra.md), conventionally written $\mathcal B(\mathbb R)\otimes\mathcal B(\mathbb R)$; it is not just the collection of rectangles. On $\mathbb R^2$ it equals $\mathcal B(\mathbb R^2)$: coordinate projections show that rectangles are [Borel sets](../../../../../borel-set.md), while open rectangles with rational endpoints give every open [set](../../../../../set-split.md) as a [countable union](../../../../../countable-union.md) and hence generate the [Borel sigma-algebra](../../../../../borel-sigma-algebra.md).

Existence is the [product measure](../../../../../product-measure.md) theorem for finite [measures](../../../../../measure-split.md), which are in particular [sigma-finite measures](../../../../../sigma-finite-measure.md). Uniqueness follows because the rectangles form a [pi-system](../../../../../pi-system.md) generating the [product sigma-algebra](../../../../../product-sigma-algebra.md), and two finite [measures](../../../../../measure-split.md) agreeing there agree everywhere by the [Dynkin lemma](../../../../../dynkin-lemma.md).[§1.7, Theorem 1.7.1, printed p. 38: existence and characterization on rectangles](../../../../../probability-theory-and-examples-fifth-edition-by-rick-durrett.md)
