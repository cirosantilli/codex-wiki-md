<h1 id="3/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [filtration](../../../../../filtration-probability-theory.md) is generated by the successive [independent random variables](../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) $X_k$. Each $S_n$ is [adapted](../../../../../adapted-process.md), and $|S_n|\leq n$ makes it an [integrable random variable](../../../../../integrable-random-variable.md). Since [independence](../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) gives $\mathbb E[X_{n+1}\mid\mathcal F_n]=0$,

$$
\mathbb E[S_{n+1}\mid\mathcal F_n]=S_n.
$$

Thus $(S_n)$ is a [martingale](../../../../../martingale-split.md).

Write the accumulated number of visits to zero as

$$
L_n=\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}\mathbf1_{\{S_k=0\}},\qquad L_0=0.
$$

It is [adapted](../../../../../adapted-process.md), and $0\leq L_n\leq n$. Thus $M_n=|S_n|-L_n$ is also [adapted](../../../../../adapted-process.md) and an [integrable random variable](../../../../../integrable-random-variable.md). For every integer $s$,

$$
\frac{|s+1|+|s-1|}{2}-|s|
=\begin{cases}1,&s=0,\\0,&s\ne0.\end{cases}
$$

Conditioning on $S_n$ and using [independence](../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) of the next step therefore gives

$$
\mathbb E[|S_{n+1}|-|S_n|\mid\mathcal F_n]
=\mathbf1_{\{S_n=0\}}
=L_{n+1}-L_n.
$$

The drift of the absolute value is cancelled exactly by the visit count, so $\mathbb E[M_{n+1}-M_n\mid\mathcal F_n]=0$. Hence **both $(S_n)$ and $(M_n)$ are martingales**. The latter is the [absolute value martingale of a simple symmetric random walk](../../../../../absolute-value-martingale-of-a-simple-symmetric-random-walk.md), a [discrete Tanaka formula](../../../../../discrete-tanaka-formula.md) in which visits to zero supply the correction at the corner of the absolute-value [function](../../../../../function-split.md).
