↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

A family $(\xi_i)_{i\in I}$ of [integrable random variables](../../../../../integrable-random-variable.md) is [uniformly integrable](../../../../../uniform-integrability.md) when

$$
\boxed{\lim_{K\to\infty}\sup_{i\in I}\mathbb E\bigl[|\xi_i|\mathbf1_{\{|\xi_i|>K\}}\bigr]=0.}
$$

The [Lipschitz condition](../../../../../lipschitz-continuity.md) gives, on $\{X<1\}$,

$$
|Z_n|=2^n|f(X_n+2^{-n})-f(X_n)|\leq2^nL2^{-n}=L.
$$

Our chosen zero value on $\{X=1\}$ satisfies the same bound. Hence the [random variables](../../../../../random-variable-split.md) $(Z_n)$ are [integrable](../../../../../integrability.md), and for $K\geq L$ every tail [expectation](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) in the definition vanishes. Therefore **the family is uniformly integrable**.
