For this part and part (c), take $X$ to be $[0,1]$-valued everywhere. The [uniform distribution](../../../continuous-uniform-distribution.md) gives $\mathbb P(X=1)=0$, but the paper's expression for $Z_n$ is undefined there because $X_n+2^{-n}>1$. Define $Z_n=0$ on $\{X=1\}$. Keep $X_n=\lfloor2^nX\rfloor/2^n$ exactly as printed, including $X_n=1$ on that [null set](../../../null-set.md); this preserves the exact [sigma-algebra](../../../sigma-algebra.md) equality in part (ii). Every slope formula below is asserted on $\{X<1\}$, hence [almost surely](../../../almost-sure-convergence.md).
