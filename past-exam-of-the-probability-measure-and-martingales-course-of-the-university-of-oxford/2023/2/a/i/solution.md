<h1 id="2/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [sigma-algebra generated by a random variable](../../../../../sigma-algebra-generated-by-a-random-variable.md) $X$ is the smallest [sigma-algebra](../../../../../sigma-algebra.md) on $\Omega$ making $X$ a [measurable function](../../../../../measurable-function.md) into $(\mathbb R,\mathcal B(\mathbb R))$. Equivalently, it is the [intersection](../../../../../set-intersection.md) of all [sigma-algebras](../../../../../sigma-algebra.md) containing the [inverse images](../../../../../preimage.md) $X^{-1}(C)$ of [Borel sets](../../../../../borel-set.md) $C$.

Let $\mathcal D=\{X^{-1}(C):C\in\mathcal B(\mathbb R)\}$. This collection is already a [sigma-algebra](../../../../../sigma-algebra.md): $X^{-1}(\mathbb R)=\Omega$,

$$
\bigl(X^{-1}(C)\bigr)^c=X^{-1}(C^c),\qquad
\bigcup_{j=1}^\infty X^{-1}(C_j)=X^{-1}\!\left(\bigcup_{j=1}^\infty C_j\right).
$$

The right-hand [sets](../../../../../set-split.md) are in $\mathcal D$, since the [Borel sigma-algebra](../../../../../borel-sigma-algebra.md) is closed under the indicated operations. Any [sigma-algebra](../../../../../sigma-algebra.md) making $X$ [measurable](../../../../../measurability.md) must contain every member of $\mathcal D$. Conversely $\mathcal D$ makes $X$ [measurable](../../../../../measurability.md) by definition. Hence

$$
\boxed{\sigma(X)=\{X^{-1}(C):C\in\mathcal B(\mathbb R)\}.}
$$

This equality holds exactly as a collection of [sets](../../../../../set-split.md), without completing the [sigma-algebra](../../../../../sigma-algebra.md).
