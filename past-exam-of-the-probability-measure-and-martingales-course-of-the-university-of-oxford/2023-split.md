# 2023

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the probability, measure and martingales course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Solutions to every part of all three questions of the [probability, measure and martingales course of the University of Oxford](../probability-measure-and-martingales-course-of-the-university-of-oxford.md) Part B examination, sat on 5 June 2023. Source: [original released PDF, bundle pages 288–291; paper pages 1–4](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/request_for_mathematics_examinat/response/3129786/attach/3/FOI%20202507%20740%20Data.pdf#page=288). The native PDF confirms the endpoint defect in Question 2: its slope formula evaluates $f$ outside $[0,1]$ when $X=1$. We specify values on that [null set](../null-set.md) below. Question 1(b)'s literal [measurability](../measurability.md) assertion uses everywhere sign-valued [random variables](../random-variable-split.md); for arbitrary versions with the stated [probability distributions](../probability-distribution-split.md), it holds up to [null sets](../null-set.md), as explained in the solution.
