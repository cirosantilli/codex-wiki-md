# Ultrafilter characterization of closure of an image

↑ **Parent:** [Pushforward filter](pushforward-filter.md)

A point $b$ lies in $\overline{f[A]}$ exactly when some [ultrafilter](ultrafilter.md) containing $A$ has pushforward converging to $b$. For the forward implication, the [sets](set-split.md) $A\cap f^{-1}[V]$ for [neighborhoods](neighbourhood-mathematics.md) $V$ of $b$ form a [proper filter](proper-filter.md) [topological basis](basis-of-a-topology.md); extend to an [ultrafilter](ultrafilter.md). For the reverse implication each such [intersection](set-intersection.md) belongs to a [proper filter](proper-filter.md) and is nonempty.
