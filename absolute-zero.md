# Absolute zero

↑ **Parent:** [Temperature](temperature-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Absolute_zero)

[Absolute zero](absolute-zero.md) is the zero of [thermodynamic temperature](thermodynamic-temperature.md), corresponding to $-273.15\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$. For a system with a nondegenerate [ground state](ground-state.md), equilibrium [entropy](entropy-split.md) approaches zero as [temperature](temperature-split.md) approaches [absolute zero](absolute-zero.md). This does not mean that every quantum [degree of freedom](degree-of-freedom.md) loses its [zero-point energy](zero-point-energy.md). The [entropy](entropy-split.md) statement and the operational [unattainability of absolute zero](unattainability-of-absolute-zero.md) require their respective physical assumptions.
