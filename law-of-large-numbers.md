# Law of large numbers

↑ **Parent:** [Convergence of random variables](convergence-of-random-variables-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_of_large_numbers)

Laws of large numbers give conditions under which [sample means](sample-mean.md) approach a deterministic [expected value](expected-value.md). For [independent and identically distributed random variables](independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md) with $\mathbb E|X_1|<\infty$, the [strong law of large numbers](strong-law-of-large-numbers.md) gives [almost sure convergence](almost-sure-convergence.md), while the [weak law of large numbers](weak-law-of-large-numbers.md) gives [convergence in probability](convergence-in-probability.md). These conclusions specify different modes of [convergence of random variables](convergence-of-random-variables-split.md), rather than a universal numerical accuracy guarantee. The [central limit theorem](central-limit-theorem.md) is a separate result about the remaining fluctuations after rescaling.
