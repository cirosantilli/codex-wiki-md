# Acceptor impurity

↑ **Parent:** [Semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md)

An impurity that can accept an electron from the valence band, leaving a mobile hole. A shallow acceptor creates an [impurity level in a crystal](impurity-level-in-a-crystal.md) near and above the valence-band edge. Donor and acceptor levels are descriptions of appropriate dopants, not universal features of every impurity species.
