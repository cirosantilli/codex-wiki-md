# 2023

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the numerical solutions of partial differential equations course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Original solutions to all three questions of the [numerical solutions of partial differential equations course of the University of Oxford](../numerical-solutions-of-partial-differential-equations-course-of-the-university-of-oxford.md) Part B examination, held on 6 June 2023. Source: [original released PDF, bundle pages 292–295; paper pages 1–4](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/request_for_mathematics_examinat/response/3129786/attach/3/FOI%20202507%20740%20Data.pdf#page=292). The numbering follows the paper. In 3(e), the growth constant must be allowed to depend on the mesh; the literal mesh-independent assertion in the native PDF is false. The corrected result and the intended failure of [practical stability of a finite difference scheme](../practical-stability-of-a-finite-difference-scheme.md) are proved below.
