<h1 id="doob-s-martingale-convergence-theorems">Doob's martingale convergence theorems</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Martingale](martingale-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doob's_martingale_convergence_theorems)

These are convergence results for martingales and submartingales under appropriate one-sided or absolute-integrability bounds. They distinguish almost-sure convergence from convergence in mean: uniform integrability gives the latter. The [martingale convergence theorem](martingale-convergence-theorem.md) stated for an L1-bounded martingale is one standard member of the family.
