# Spin magnetization

↑ **Parent:** [Spin model](spin-model.md)

The spin magnetization is the average signed spin of a discrete spin system. For $S_i\in\{-1,1\}$, $k$ positive spins give $M=-1+2k/n$. Multiplying by the magnetic moment per spin and the spin density relates this dimensionless average to physical [magnetization](magnetization.md).
