# Heartbeat perception

↑ **Parent:** [Interoception](interoception.md)

Perception or estimation of one's heartbeat. A [mental tracking](heartbeat-perception.md) score can depend on timing, [beliefs](belief.md) about ordinary [heart rate](heart-rate.md) and response strategy as well as sensitivity to actual cardiac signals; such a score is not automatically a pure measure of interoceptive accuracy.[Behaviour Research and Therapy 42, 439–448; 79 children aged 8–11, mental tracking and symptom/sensitivity measures](heart-beat-perception-panic-somatic-symptoms-and-anxiety-sensitivity-in-children-by-thalia-c-eley-and-others.md)
