# Contour inversion for call prices

↑ **Parent:** [European call option](european-call-option.md)

The [Mellin transform of call prices](mellin-transform-of-call-prices.md) can be inverted along a vertical contour with real part $x_0>1$: $C(k)=\frac1{2\pi i}\int_{x_0-i\infty}^{x_0+i\infty}\mathbb Ee^{z\xi}/(z(z-1)e^{(z-1)k})\,dz$. A finite [exponential moment](exponential-moment.md) at $x_0$ and the quadratic denominator give absolute convergence.
