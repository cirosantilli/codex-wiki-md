# Magnetic-field-aligned rolls evade steady magnetic inhibition

↑ **Parent:** [Horizontal-field magnetoconvection dispersion relation](horizontal-field-magnetoconvection-dispersion-relation.md)

At a fixed horizontal [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) magnitude, the steady neutral curve in the [horizontal-field magnetoconvection dispersion relation](horizontal-field-magnetoconvection-dispersion-relation.md) is minimized by $m=0$. The disturbance is then constant along the imposed [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md); it induces no field bending and incurs no [magnetic tension](magnetic-tension.md) penalty. Minimizing $(k^2+\pi^2)^3/k^2$ gives the displayed stationary threshold for every $Q\geq0$. These [convection rolls](convection-roll.md) have axes parallel to the imposed field. This assertion concerns steady onset.
