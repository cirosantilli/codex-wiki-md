# Conditional mode of Gaussian random effects

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian linear mixed model](gaussian-linear-mixed-model.md)

In a [Gaussian linear mixed model](gaussian-linear-mixed-model.md) with $V=ZDZ^T+R$, the [conditional multivariate normal distribution](conditional-multivariate-normal-distribution.md) of $b$ given $Y$ has mean $DZ^TV^{-1}(Y-X\beta)$ and [covariance matrix](covariance-matrix.md) $D-DZ^TV^{-1}ZD$. When nonsingular, its mean is also its mode. Estimated parameters yield empirical conditional modes and shrink group deviations toward zero. At a zero variance component the associated effect is degenerate at zero; the covariance formula still applies, whereas formulas involving $D^{-1}$ require limits.
