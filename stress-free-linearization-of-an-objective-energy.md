# Stress-free linearization of an objective energy

↑ **Parent:** [Material frame indifference](material-frame-indifference.md)

For an objective smooth [strain energy density](strain-energy-density.md) $W(QF)=W(F)$, differentiation along $Q=e^{tK}$ shows $DW(F)F^T$ is symmetric. Hence the associated [Cauchy stress tensor](cauchy-stress-tensor.md) is symmetric. At a stress-free reference $DW(I)=0$, differentiation of this identity shows $D^2W(I)[H]$ is symmetric for every $H$. In components this gives output minor symmetry. The [Hessian](hessian-matrix.md) has pair-interchange symmetry, which then gives the input minor symmetry and the displayed annihilation of skew increments. The stress-free condition matters: at a prestressed reference the derivative includes the extra term $DW(I)H^T$.
