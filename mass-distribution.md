# Mass distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Mass](mass.md)

A description of how [mass](mass.md) is assigned to positions, expressed for example by [point masses](point-mass.md), a [mass density](density.md), or a [measure](measure-split.md). Writing $dm$ allows [centre of mass](center-of-mass.md) and [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) formulas to cover both discrete and continuous cases.
