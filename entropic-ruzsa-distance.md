# Entropic Ruzsa distance

↑ **Parent:** [Information theory](information-theory-split.md)

For finitely supported random variables $X,Y$ in a [finite additive group](finite-additive-group.md), let $X',Y'$ be independent variables with their respective marginal distributions. The entropic Ruzsa distance is

$$
d_R(X,Y)=H(X'-Y')-\frac12H(X')-\frac12H(Y').
$$

It is symmetric and nonnegative, but can have $d_R(X,X)>0$.
