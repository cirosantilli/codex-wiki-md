# Wave reflection

↑ **Parent:** [Wave](wave-split.md)

[Wave reflection](wave-reflection.md) returns part of an incident [wave](wave-split.md) into the medium from which it arrived, as a consequence of an interface or spatial variation of material properties. An amplitude [reflection coefficient](reflection-coefficient.md) compares the reflected and incident [waves](wave-split.md); its squared [modulus](modulus.md) gives their [wave intensity](intensity-physics.md) ratio when both use the same medium and normalization. Reflected [energy](energy-split.md) has not necessarily undergone [wave absorption](wave-absorption.md).
