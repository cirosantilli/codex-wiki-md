# Alexander matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Alexander polynomial](alexander-polynomial.md)

Given a presentation $\langle x_1,\ldots,x_n\mid r_1,\ldots,r_m\rangle$ of a knot group, apply abelianization to the [Fox derivatives](fox-calculus.md) $\partial r_j/\partial x_i$. The resulting matrix over $\mathbb Z[t^{\pm1}]$ is an Alexander matrix; suitable maximal minors recover the [Alexander polynomial of a knot](alexander-polynomial.md).
