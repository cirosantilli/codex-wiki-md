# Hydraulic resistance

↑ **Parent:** [Fluid mechanics](fluid-mechanics-split.md)

[Hydraulic resistance](hydraulic-resistance.md) relates the [pressure](pressure.md) drop across a resistive fluid element to its [volumetric flow rate](volumetric-flow-rate.md). In a linear model, $\Delta p=RQ$ with $R>0$, so dissipated mechanical power is $\Delta p\,Q=RQ^2$. A peripheral circulatory bed can be represented by such a resistance when its detailed dynamics are not retained. This terminal [pressure](pressure.md)-to-flow resistance differs from the area-dependent friction rate multiplying [velocity](velocity.md) in a distributed tube momentum equation.
