# Perturbation theory (quantum mechanics)

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum mechanics](quantum-mechanics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perturbation_theory_(quantum_mechanics))

[Perturbation theory](perturbation-theory-quantum-mechanics.md) expands a quantum problem around a solvable Hamiltonian. [Time-independent perturbation theory](time-independent-perturbation-theory.md) expands stationary energies and eigenstates; [time-dependent perturbation theory](time-dependent-perturbation-theory.md) expands dynamical amplitudes and transitions.
