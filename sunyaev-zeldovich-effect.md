# Sunyaev-Zeldovich effect

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmic microwave background](cosmic-microwave-background.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunyaev-Zeldovich_effect)

Hot [Electrons](electron.md) in [galaxy](galaxy-split.md) clusters scatter [photons](photon.md) of the [cosmic microwave background](cosmic-microwave-background.md), producing a characteristic spectral distortion. The thermal contribution measures a line-of-sight integral of [Electron](electron.md) [pressure](pressure.md), whereas X-ray emission depends approximately on the integral of [Electron](electron.md) density squared times a temperature-dependent emissivity. Combining the two with a geometrical model can constrain a cluster's physical size and hence its [angular diameter distance](angular-diameter-distance.md). [Temperature](temperature.md) structure and departures from the assumed geometry introduce systematic uncertainty.
