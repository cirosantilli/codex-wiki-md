# Survival renewal equation for a classical risk model

↑ **Parent:** [Classical risk model](classical-risk-model.md)

The ultimate [survival probability](survival-probability.md) satisfies $\varphi(u)=\varphi(0)+(\lambda/c)\int_0^u\varphi(u-x)(1-F(x))\,dx$. Condition on the first arrival of the [Poisson process](poisson-process.md), differentiate the resulting exponentially weighted integral, and integrate the convolution derivative equation from zero. [Tonelli theorem](tonelli-theorem.md) converts the claim-density convolution to the tail convolution. The kernel mass is $\lambda\mu/c<1$, so this is a [defective renewal equation](defective-renewal-equation.md).
