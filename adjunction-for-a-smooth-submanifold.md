# Adjunction for a smooth submanifold

↑ **Parent:** [Adjunction formula](adjunction-formula.md)

For a smooth complex submanifold $V\subset M$, the tangent exact sequence gives $\det TM|_V=\det TV\otimes\det N_V$. Dualizing yields $K_V=K_M|_V\otimes\det N_V$. This form of [adjunction formula](adjunction-formula.md) applies in any codimension.
