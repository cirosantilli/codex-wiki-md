# Elliptic cone

↑ **Parent:** [Quadric surface](quadric-surface.md)

An elliptic cone is a real [quadric surface](quadric-surface.md) consisting of the two nappes $u=\pm\sqrt{(bv^2+cw^2)/a}$, joined at the vertex. Its intersections with planes $u=u_0\ne0$ are [ellipses](ellipse.md). It is the zero level of an indefinite [quadratic form](quadratic-form-split.md) of [signature](signature-of-a-quadratic-form.md) $(1,2)$. Changing the level to a positive value produces a [two-sheeted hyperboloid](two-sheeted-hyperboloid.md); changing it to a negative value produces a [one-sheet hyperboloid](one-sheet-hyperboloid.md), when the positive coefficient is assigned to the axial coordinate $u$.
