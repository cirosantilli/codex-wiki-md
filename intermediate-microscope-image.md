# Intermediate microscope image

↑ **Parent:** [Optical microscope](optical-microscope.md)

The [real image](real-image.md) of the specimen supplied to the [microscope eyepiece](microscope-eyepiece.md) or a recording surface. In the example above, a bare detector placed at $x=60\,\mathrm{mm}$ records an image five times the specimen's [length](length.md), inverted. It does not record a fifty-times image: that number compares viewing [angles](angle.md) under the stated reference convention. An [optical camera](optical-camera.md) receiving the eyepiece's outgoing [light](light.md) needs its own [optical lens](lens-optics.md) to form a further [real image](real-image.md), with a recording scale determined by that added [image formation](image-formation.md).
