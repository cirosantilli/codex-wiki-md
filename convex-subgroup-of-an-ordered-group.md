# Convex subgroup of an ordered group

↑ **Parent:** [Partially ordered group](partially-ordered-group-split.md)

A [subgroup](subgroup.md) $C$ is convex if $c_1\le g\le c_2$ with $c_1,c_2\in C$ implies $g\in C$. In a [linearly ordered group](linearly-ordered-group.md), convex subgroups form a chain under inclusion: positive elements witnessing incomparability would be comparable and force the smaller one into the other subgroup by convexity.
