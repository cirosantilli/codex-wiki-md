<h1 id="exponential-form-of-levy-characteristic-functions">Exponential form of Lévy characteristic functions</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Characteristic exponent of a Lévy process](characteristic-exponent-of-a-levy-process.md)

[Independent increments](independent-increments.md) and [stationary increments](stationary-increments.md) make a [Lévy process](levy-process.md)'s [characteristic functions](characteristic-function.md) multiplicative in time. Together with [continuity of Lévy characteristic functions](continuity-of-levy-characteristic-functions.md) and the value one at time zero, this forces an exponential. A continuous local [complex logarithm](complex-logarithm.md) is additive: its failure of additivity would be a continuous integer multiple of $2\pi i$, hence zero. The [Cauchy functional equation](cauchy-s-functional-equation.md) and subdivision then give the exponential at every time. This construction of the [characteristic exponent of a Lévy process](characteristic-exponent-of-a-levy-process.md) precedes the [Lévy–Khintchine formula](levy-khintchine-formula.md) describing the exponent's possible form.
