# Scattering cross-section of a harmonically bound charge

↑ **Parent:** [Lorentz oscillator model](lorentz-oscillator-model.md)

For a charge $q$ of [mass](mass.md) $m$ bound by spring constant $k=m\omega_0^2$, the undamped equation $m\ddot x+kx=qE_0e^{-i\omega t}$ has [complex amplitude](complex-amplitude.md) $x_0=qE_0/[m(\omega_0^2-\omega^2)]$. The induced [electric dipole moment](electric-dipole-moment.md) is $p_0=qx_0$. Averaged [electric dipole radiation](electric-dipole-radiation.md) [power](power.md), divided by incident [light intensity](light-intensity.md), gives

$$
\sigma(\omega)=\frac{q^4\omega^4}{6\pi\epsilon_0^2c^4(k-m\omega^2)^2}
=\sigma_T\frac{\omega^4}{(\omega_0^2-\omega^2)^2}.
$$

The [Rayleigh scattering](rayleigh-scattering.md) limit $\omega\ll\omega_0$ gives $\sigma\sim\sigma_T(\omega/\omega_0)^4$, proportional to $\lambda^{-4}$. For $\omega_0\ll\omega\ll mc^2/\hbar$, the charge behaves as free and the [Thomson cross-section](thomson-cross-section.md) is recovered. At resonance the undamped steady-state formula is invalid; a finite [damping](damping.md) mechanism or [radiation reaction](radiation-reaction.md) must be included. The formula describes independent weakly driven scatterers, not arbitrary coherent scattering from an extended medium.
