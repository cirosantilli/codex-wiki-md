# Folded sine approximation to a Dirac delta

↑ **Parent:** [Dirac delta function](dirac-delta-function.md)

As [distributions](distribution-mathematical-analysis.md) on the line, $m\sin(m|t|)\to2\delta_0$. Pairing with a [test function](test-function.md) $A$ folds the integral to $\int_0^\infty m\sin(ms)[A(s)+A(-s)]\,ds$. One [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) yields $2A(0)$ plus a cosine integral tending to zero by the [Riemann-Lebesgue lemma](riemann-lebesgue-lemma.md). Pointwise convergence is unnecessary.
