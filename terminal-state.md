# Terminal state

↑ **Parent:** [Transition system](transition-system.md)

A [state](state-computer-science.md) with no successor in the [transition relation](transition-relation.md). Assuming no [terminal states](terminal-state.md) makes every finite [path in a transition system](path-in-a-transition-system.md) extendible and avoids choosing a separate convention for interpreting infinite-path [temporal logic](temporal-logic.md) at a dead end.
