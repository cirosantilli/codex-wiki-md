# Parameter-dependent coordinate shift in a transcritical bifurcation

↑ **Parent:** [Transcritical bifurcation](transcritical-bifurcation.md)

A [transcritical bifurcation](transcritical-bifurcation.md) need not already have its persistent branch at coordinate zero. The smooth parameter-dependent shift $u=y-\mu$ turns the displayed crossing of $y=\pm\mu$ into the standard form with parameter $-2\mu$. The crossing branches exchange [dynamical stability](stability-theory.md). A mere nonsmooth relabeling of stable and unstable branches as $\pm|\mu|$ would conceal this smooth exchange.
