# Local minimum

↑ **Parent:** [Critical point](critical-point.md)

A local minimum is a local form of an [extremum of a real-valued function](maximum-and-minimum.md).

A function has a local minimum at $x_0$ when $f(x)\geq f(x_0)$ throughout some neighbourhood of $x_0$. For a twice differentiable function, its [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) at an interior local minimum is [positive semidefinite](positive-semidefinite-matrix.md).
