# Weighted one-norm on l2

↑ **Parent:** [L1 norm](l1-norm.md)

For $0<c\leq w_k<\infty$, interpret the nonnegative sum as an extended-valued functional on the [l2 sequence space](l2-sequence-space.md). Finiteness implies $u\in\ell^1$, but unbounded weights can make it infinite even there. It is proper and convex. Every finite partial sum is norm-continuous, so their supremum is [sequentially lower semicontinuous](sequential-lower-semicontinuity.md) and also weakly lower semicontinuous. This is useful as a sparsity penalty in [variational regularization](variational-regularization.md).
