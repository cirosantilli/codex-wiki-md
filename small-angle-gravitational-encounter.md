# Small-angle gravitational encounter

↑ **Parent:** [Two-body relaxation](two-body-relaxation.md)

For a [mass](mass.md) $m$ passing at relative [speed](speed.md) $u$ and [impact parameter](impact-parameter.md) $b$, [integration](integral.md) of $Gmb/(b^2+u^2t^2)^{3/2}$ gives the transverse kick $2Gm/(bu)$. Weak deflection requires $b\gg Gm/u^2$, up to the equal-mass order-one factor. This scale supplies a small-impact cutoff for the weak-scattering diffusion approximation, not a physical exclusion of stronger encounters.
