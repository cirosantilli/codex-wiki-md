# Qutrit linear-function identification

↑ **Parent:** [Bernstein-Vazirani phase kickback](bernstein-vazirani-phase-kickback.md)

A [modular-addition quantum oracle](modular-addition-quantum-oracle.md) for a linear [function](function-split.md) over the three-element [finite field](finite-field.md) reveals its two coefficients in one query. Prepare the answer [qutrit](qutrit.md) in $\operatorname{QFT}_3|2\rangle$, whose unit shift [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) is $e^{2\pi i/3}$. [Quantum phase kickback](phase-kickback.md) produces a product of Fourier states labelled by the coefficients; inverse [quantum Fourier transforms](quantum-fourier-transform.md) read both coefficients exactly.
