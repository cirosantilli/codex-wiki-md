# Cubic smoothing spline

↑ **Parent:** [Smoothing spline](smoothing-spline.md)

For $\lambda>0$ and at least two distinct design points, the unique $C^2[a,b]$ minimizer of squared residuals plus $\lambda J(g)$ is $N(I+\lambda\Gamma)^{-1}\mathbf Y$. The [minimum roughness property of the natural cubic spline interpolant](minimum-roughness-property-of-the-natural-cubic-spline-interpolant.md) reduces the optimization to the value vector. The [matrix](matrix.md) $I+\lambda\Gamma$ is a [positive-definite matrix](positive-definite-matrix.md), so the normal equations have a unique solution; the interpolation equality case gives uniqueness as a function. Linear tails are part of the natural boundary convention.
