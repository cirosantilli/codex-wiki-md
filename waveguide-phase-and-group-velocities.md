# Waveguide phase and group velocities

↑ **Parent:** [Waveguide cutoff frequency](waveguide-cutoff-frequency.md)

Above a mode's [waveguide cutoff frequency](waveguide-cutoff-frequency.md) $\omega_c$, its [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md) is $c/[1-(\omega_c/\omega)^2]^{1/2}$ and its [group velocity](group-velocity.md) is $c[1-(\omega_c/\omega)^2]^{1/2}$. Differentiate $\omega^2=c^2k^2+\omega_c^2$ to obtain the latter. Phase travels faster than $c$ while the wave packet travels slower, so the phase speed does not imply superluminal information transport.
