# Single-particle orbital

↑ **Parent:** [Wave function](wave-function-split.md)

A single-particle [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) used as a mode of a many-particle description. An [orbital](single-particle-orbital.md) can include spatial and [spin](spin.md) variables; a [spatial orbital](spatial-orbital.md) specifies the spatial part. Orthonormal [orbitals](single-particle-orbital.md) define [creation and annihilation operators](creation-and-annihilation-operators.md). Occupation of one normalized [orbital](single-particle-orbital.md) by every [boson](boson.md) gives a [Hartree product state](hartree-product-state.md), while antisymmetrized occupation by [fermions](fermion.md) gives a [Slater determinant](slater-determinant.md).
