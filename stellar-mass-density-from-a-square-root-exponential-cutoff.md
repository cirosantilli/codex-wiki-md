# Stellar mass density from a square-root exponential cutoff

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar mass function with a square-root exponential cutoff](stellar-mass-function-with-a-square-root-exponential-cutoff.md)

Integrating [galactic stellar mass](galaxy-stellar-mass.md) against a $x^{-7/4}e^{-\sqrt x}$ mass function requires $\int_0^\infty x^{-3/4}e^{-\sqrt x}dx$. Substitution $u=\sqrt x$ turns it into $2\Gamma(1/2)=2\sqrt\pi$. Thus the stellar density is $2\sqrt\pi$ times characteristic number density times characteristic [galactic stellar mass](galaxy-stellar-mass.md), in the same volume convention as the mass function.
