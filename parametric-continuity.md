# Parametric continuity

↑ **Parent:** [Computer aided geometric design](computer-aided-geometric-design-split.md)

Joined pieces have order-$k$ [parametric continuity](parametric-continuity.md) when their positions and one-sided [derivatives](derivative.md) through order $k$ agree in the actual common parameter. Changing parameter-interval lengths changes the required derivative scale by the [chain rule](chain-rule.md). For regular [curves](curve.md), [parametric continuity](parametric-continuity.md) implies the corresponding [geometric continuity](geometric-continuity.md).
