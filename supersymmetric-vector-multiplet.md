# Supersymmetric vector multiplet

↑ **Parent:** [Supermultiplet](supermultiplet.md)

A supersymmetric vector multiplet contains a [gauge boson](gauge-boson.md) and its [gaugino](gaugino.md) partners, with additional [scalar fields](scalar-field.md) for [extended supersymmetry](extended-supersymmetry.md). Its [gauge group representation](gauge-group-representation.md) is the adjoint [group representation](group-representation.md).
