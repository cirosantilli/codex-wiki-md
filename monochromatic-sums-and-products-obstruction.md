# Monochromatic sums-and-products obstruction

↑ **Parent:** [Ramsey theory](ramsey-theory-split.md)

There is a [finite coloring](finite-coloring.md) of the [positive integers](positive-integer.md) for which no infinite set has all pairwise sums and pairwise products in one color. Refining this coloring by the parity of the [2-adic valuation](2-adic-valuation.md) also prevents a constant infinite sequence from evading the obstruction.
