# Riboswitch

↑ **Parent:** [Gene expression](gene-expression.md)

A [riboswitch](riboswitch.md) is a structured RNA regulatory element whose ligand-binding state changes gene expression through a coupled expression platform. Depending on the system, the immediate output can be translation-initiation accessibility or transcription termination.
