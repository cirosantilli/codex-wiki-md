# Gradient of a dipole potential

↑ **Parent:** [Harmonic function](harmonic-function.md)

Away from the origin, a dipole potential $(d\cdot x)/|x|^3$ is [harmonic](harmonic-function.md). Its [gradient](gradient.md) is angularly nonuniform, decays as $r^{-3}$, and has zero spherical mean because $\langle n\otimes n\rangle=I/3$. That cancellation distinguishes a self-dipole field from the regular incident field that excites a small inclusion.
