# The Science and Philosophy of the Organism by Hans Driesch

↑ **Parent:** [Cell biology bibliography](cell-biology-bibliography-split.md)

[https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/44388/pg44388-images.html](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/44388/pg44388-images.html)

Primary participant retrospective, based on the 1907 lectures. Part I, section B.1, Experimental Morphology, recounts Roux's 1888 experiment and the author's work three years later. The account and its vitalist interpretation must be distinguished from contemporaneous reports of the original trials.
