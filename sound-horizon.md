# Sound horizon

↑ **Parent:** [Photon-baryon fluid](photon-baryon-fluid.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sound_horizon)

The comoving sound horizon is the distance traveled by an acoustic wave,

$$
r_s(\eta)=\int_0^\eta c_s(\eta')\,d\eta'.
$$
