# Index of a planar periodic orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Poincaré index](poincare-index.md)

A planar [periodic orbit](periodic-orbit.md), oriented by the flow, has [Poincaré index](poincare-index.md) $+1$. The index theorem equates this with the sum of the indices of the isolated equilibria enclosed by the orbit. A hyperbolic equilibrium has index $+1$ when its [Jacobian matrix](jacobian-matrix.md) has positive determinant and index $-1$ when it is a [saddle equilibrium](saddle-equilibrium.md).
