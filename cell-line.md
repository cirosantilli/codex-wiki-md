# Cell line

↑ **Parent:** [Biological cell](biological-cell-split.md)

A [cell line](cell-line.md) comprises descendants after a [primary cell culture](primary-cell-culture.md)'s first [subculture](subculture.md). Further division can be finite or continuous; one founder [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md) is not required.[Glossary, Cell line, Clone and Continuous cell culture](animal-cell-culture-guide-by-atcc.md)

The useful continuity is ancestry across successive [subcultures](subculture.md): descendants supply the next experiment. Individual [biological cells](biological-cell-split.md) can divide or die while their [cell line](cell-line.md) persists. A common origin helps compare experiments, but does not imply that every descendant has identical [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md), [gene expression](gene-expression.md) or [cell state](cell-state.md).
