# Gyrocompass

↑ **Parent:** [Gyroscope](gyroscope.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gyrocompass)

A [gyrocompass](gyrocompass.md) uses a spinning [gyroscope](gyroscope.md) and [Earth's rotation](earth-s-rotation.md) to identify geographical north. In an ideal model whose axle is constrained to the local horizontal plane but free to turn about the vertical, the northward direction of the spin [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) is stable away from the poles; the opposite spin direction is unstable. A real settling instrument also requires damping. This is different from merely preserving an initial inertial orientation with a freely mounted [gyroscope](gyroscope.md).
