# Coulomb logarithm

↑ **Parent:** [Plasma (physics)](plasma-physics.md)

The [logarithm](logarithm.md) of the ratio of effective maximum to minimum impact parameter in weakly coupled Coulomb collision transport. Screening and short-distance scattering determine the cutoffs; it encodes many small-angle encounters.
