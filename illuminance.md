# Illuminance

↑ **Parent:** [Photometry (optics)](photometry-optics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Illuminance)

[Illuminance](illuminance.md) is incident [luminous flux](luminous-flux.md) per receiving [area](surface-area.md), measured in [lux](lux.md). It is the visually weighted counterpart of [irradiance](irradiance.md). Uniform illumination of an aperture of [area](surface-area.md) $A$ gives collected [luminous flux](luminous-flux.md) $AE_v$. An aperture measurement alone does not give a source's total emitted [luminous flux](luminous-flux.md).[Radiometry and Photometry: Review for Vision Optics](osa-handbook-of-optics-volume-iii-visual-optics-and-vision/radiometry-and-photometry-review-for-vision-optics.md)
