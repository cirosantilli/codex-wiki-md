# Quantum torus

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum plane](quantum-plane.md)

The two-dimensional quantum torus is the [algebra](algebra-split.md) with invertible generators $X,Y$ and relation $YX=qXY$, for $q\in k^\times$. Its [basis](basis.md) consists of $X^iY^j$ with $i,j\in\mathbb Z$, with the same multiplication rule as the [quantum plane](quantum-plane.md). It is a [right Noetherian ring](right-noetherian-ring.md): successive adjunction of $X^{-1}$, $Y$, and $Y^{-1}$ satisfies the hypotheses of the [noncommutative Hilbert basis theorem](noncommutative-hilbert-basis-theorem.md).
