# Fundamental identity for prime decomposition

↑ **Parent:** [Ramification index of a prime ideal](ramification-index-of-a-prime-ideal.md)

Let $B$ be the [integral closure](integral-closure.md) of a [Dedekind domain](dedekind-domain.md) $R$ in a finite separable extension $L/K$ of degree $n$. For a nonzero [prime ideal](prime-ideal.md) $\mathfrak p$ of $R$, write

$$
\mathfrak pB=\prod_{\mathfrak q\mid\mathfrak p}\mathfrak q^{e_{\mathfrak q/\mathfrak p}}
$$

and let $f_{\mathfrak q/\mathfrak p}=[B/\mathfrak q:R/\mathfrak p]$. Then

$$
n=\sum_{\mathfrak q\mid\mathfrak p}e_{\mathfrak q/\mathfrak p}f_{\mathfrak q/\mathfrak p}.
$$
