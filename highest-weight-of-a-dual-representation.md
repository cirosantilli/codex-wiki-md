# Highest weight of a dual representation

↑ **Parent:** [Dual Lie algebra representation](dual-lie-algebra-representation.md)

For a finite-dimensional irreducible representation $L(\lambda)$ of a complex [semisimple Lie algebra](semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md), its [dual Lie algebra representation](dual-lie-algebra-representation.md) has [highest weight](highest-weight-of-a-representation.md) $-w_0\lambda$, where $w_0$ is the [longest Weyl-group element](longest-weyl-group-element.md). Indeed duality negates all [weights](weight-representation-theory.md), and $w_0\lambda$ is the lowest weight of the original module. In particular $w_0=-I$ makes every such irreducible module self-dual.
