# Probability sampling

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical inference](statistical-inference-split.md)

Probability sampling chooses observational units by a specified random mechanism with known inclusion [probabilities](probability.md). This separates design-based representativeness from the convenience of obtaining particular observations. [Simple random sampling](simple-random-sampling.md), [stratified sampling](stratified-sampling.md) and [cluster sampling](cluster-sampling.md) are different designs with different precision and cost properties.
