# Omega-stability of a theory

↑ **Parent:** [Stable theory](stable-theory.md)

A [complete theory](complete-theory.md) in a [countable](countable-set.md) [first-order language](first-order-language.md) is omega-stable if over every [countable set](countable-set.md) of parameters it has only countably many [complete one-types](complete-one-type.md). The [theory of a free bijection](theory-of-a-free-bijection.md) is an example: a new point is either a specified [integer](integer-split.md) translate of a parameter or lies in a new orbit. It is omega-stable but not omega-categorical.
