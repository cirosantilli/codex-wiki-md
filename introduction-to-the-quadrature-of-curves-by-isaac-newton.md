# Introduction to the Quadrature of Curves by Isaac Newton

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematics bibliography](mathematics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by Isaac Newton](work-by-isaac-newton.md)

Introduction to the quadrature treatise appended to the 1704 Opticks. The linked text reproduces [John Harris](john-harris-writer.md)'s 1710 English translation, edited by [David R. Wilkins](david-r-wilkins.md). Archive context: [https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Newton/Quadratura/](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Newton/Quadratura/) . Cited opening account of generated quantities, fluxions and first ratios.

Online source: [https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Newton/Quadratura/HarrisIQ.pdf](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/pub/HistMath/People/Newton/Quadratura/HarrisIQ.pdf)
