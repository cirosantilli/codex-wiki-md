# Diproton

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear force](nuclear-force-split.md)

A two-[proton](proton.md) system. Its low-energy spin-singlet strong channel does not give a vacuum [bound state](bound-state.md), and electrostatic repulsion further opposes binding. This is distinct from [proton](proton.md) pairing correlations within an [atomic nucleus](atomic-nucleus-split.md).
