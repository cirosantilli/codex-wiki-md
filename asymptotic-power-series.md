# Asymptotic power series

↑ **Parent:** [Asymptotic expansion](asymptotic-expansion.md)

The displayed condition for every fixed $N$ defines an [asymptotic expansion](asymptotic-expansion.md) in powers about $a$ along a specified domain. It need not converge away from $a$. The coefficients are uniquely recovered by successive limits after subtracting all lower-order powers. A convergent [power series](power-series.md) is asymptotic because each fixed finite truncation has a higher-order tail, but a factorially growing formal series can be asymptotic while having zero radius of convergence.
