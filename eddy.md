# Eddy

↑ **Parent:** [Fluid mechanics](fluid-mechanics-split.md)

A coherent or fluctuating swirling region of a [velocity field](velocity-field.md). [Eddies](eddy.md) transport [momentum](momentum-split.md), [heat](heat-split.md) and material constituents, and their averaged fluxes can modify a mean current. They need not be nonlinear: a linear [wave packet](wave-packet.md) can also have nonzero averaged quadratic fluxes.
