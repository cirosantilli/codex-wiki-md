# Burstiness of queue input

↑ **Parent:** [Queue (queueing theory)](queue-queueing-theory.md)

The size and frequency of unusually large arrivals or sustained overload episodes. It depends on the time and threshold scales being examined, rather than just on the one-slot [variance](variance-split.md). A cap can leave smaller rare deviations unchanged while removing larger positive bursts.
