# P-star approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Saddlepoint density approximation](saddlepoint-density-approximation.md)

For a regular model with a suitable [ancillary statistic](ancillary-statistic.md) $a$, the displayed expression approximates the conditional density of the [maximum-likelihood estimator](maximum-likelihood-estimator.md). The normalizing factor includes $(2\pi)^{-p/2}$ for a $p$-dimensional estimator, depending on convention. The [observed information](observed-fisher-information.md) is evaluated at the sample's own fitted value. In a pure scale model the factor is proportional to $1/\widehat\theta$; this [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md) can make the normalized approximation coincide with the exact conditional density.
