# Borel summation

↑ **Parent:** [Asymptotic expansion](asymptotic-expansion.md)

For a formal inverse-power series $\sum_{n\geq0}a_nz^{-n-1}$, divide its coefficients by $n!$ to form a Borel transform $\sum a_nt^n/n!$ and integrate it against $e^{-zt}$. Singularities on the integration ray require a specified contour prescription. Different lateral contours can produce exponentially small differences and the [Stokes phenomenon](stokes-phenomenon.md).
