# Coastal vorticity tendency from variable surface drag

↑ **Parent:** [Seasonal land-heating vorticity tendency](seasonal-land-heating-vorticity-tendency.md)

Spatially varying drag has a vorticity tendency beyond simple damping of existing relative vorticity. The cross-gradient term depends on wind direction and the gradient of surface resistance. Increasing roughness on moving from ocean to land does not alone specify a universal cyclonic or anticyclonic sign. Seasonal heating also changes stratification and isentropic mass thickness, so it cannot generally be replaced by adiabatic PV conservation.
