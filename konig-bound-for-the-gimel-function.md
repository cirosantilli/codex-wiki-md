<h1 id="konig-bound-for-the-gimel-function">König bound for the Gimel function</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Gimel function](gimel-function.md)

Choose cofinal [ordinals](ordinal-split.md) $\alpha_i<\kappa$, indexed by $\mu=\operatorname{cf}(\kappa)$, and set $a_i=|\alpha_i|$. Then $\sum_i a_i=\kappa$ and $a_i^+\leq\kappa$. The [König theorem for cardinal numbers](konig-s-theorem-set-theory.md) gives $\kappa<\prod_i a_i^+\leq\kappa^\mu$. The estimate holds for every infinite [cardinal number](cardinal-number-split.md), regular or singular, under the usual [axiom of choice](axiom-of-choice.md) convention for [cardinal arithmetic](cardinal-arithmetic.md).
