# Schwarzschild time-translation Killing field in Kruskal coordinates

↑ **Parent:** [Kruskal–Szekeres coordinates](kruskal-szekeres-coordinates.md)

For null coordinates normalized by $UV=(2m-r)e^{r/(2m)}$, the displayed field agrees with exterior Schwarzschild time translation. Its flow rescales $U$ and $V$ inversely, preserving their product, the [radius](radius.md) and $dU\,dV$. It therefore preserves the extended [metric](metric.md) across both horizons. Its squared norm is $1-2m/r$: it is timelike in both exterior regions, spacelike in the black-hole and white-hole interiors, and nonzero null on the horizons away from the bifurcation two-[sphere](sphere.md). At that [sphere](sphere.md) it vanishes. The [zero vector](zero-vector.md) should not be mistaken for a nonzero null generator. This gives an explicit symmetry that survives where the original time coordinate ceases to be regular.
