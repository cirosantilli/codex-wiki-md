# Radiative pion decay

↑ **Parent:** [Leptonic pion decay](leptonic-pion-decay.md)

A charged [pion](pion.md) decays to a charged [antilepton](antilepton.md), its associated [neutrino](neutrino.md), and a [photon](photon.md). Inner [bremsstrahlung](bremsstrahlung.md) from charged external states interferes with structure-dependent radiation. An experimentally resolved [photon](photon.md) needs an [energy](energy-split.md) cut: the real-radiation rate alone diverges in the soft limit, whereas a suitably inclusive combination with virtual [radiative corrections](radiative-correction.md) is finite.[Section 62.1, especially equations (62.1)–(62.4)](radiative-pion-and-kaon-decay-form-factors-in-the-2025-review-of-particle-physics.md)
