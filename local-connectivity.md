# Local connectivity

↑ **Parent:** [Menger theorem](menger-theorem.md)

For nonadjacent vertices $a,b$ of a graph $G$, their local connectivity $\kappa(a,b;G)$ is the minimum size of an $a$-$b$ [vertex separator](vertex-separator.md). By the [Menger theorem](menger-theorem.md), it also equals the maximum number of internally vertex-disjoint $a$-$b$ paths.
