# Bond-length alternation

↑ **Parent:** [Conjugated system](conjugated-system.md)

Alternation between shorter and longer [chemical bonds](chemical-bond-split.md) along a [conjugated system](conjugated-system.md). In the [Hückel method](huckel-method.md), unequal nearest-neighbour couplings split the frontier [molecular orbitals](molecular-orbital.md). An electronic [energy](energy-split.md) gain must be compared at a stated constraint on the mean coupling and against the geometric distortion cost.
