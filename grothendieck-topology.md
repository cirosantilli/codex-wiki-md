# Grothendieck topology

↑ **Parent:** [Presheaf category](presheaf-category.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grothendieck_topology)

A Grothendieck topology assigns covering sieves to each object. The maximal sieve covers; pulling back a covering sieve gives a covering sieve; and a sieve locally covering along all arrows of a covering sieve itself covers. It defines the [sheaves on a site](sheaf-on-a-site.md). Families of arrows generate sieves and can be used to generate the topology.
