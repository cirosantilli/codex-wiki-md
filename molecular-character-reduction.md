# Molecular character reduction

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular symmetry](molecular-symmetry.md)

For a finite molecular [point group](point-group.md), the multiplicity of irrep $\lambda$ in a representation with character $\chi$ is $n_\lambda=|G|^{-1}\sum_C |C|\chi(C)\chi_\lambda(C)^*$. The sum is over [conjugacy classes](conjugacy-class.md).
