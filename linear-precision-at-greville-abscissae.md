# Linear precision at Greville abscissae

↑ **Parent:** [Greville abscissa](greville-abscissa.md)

For an open clamped [spline knot sequence](spline-knot-sequence.md) and degree $d\ge1$, the [Greville abscissae](greville-abscissa.md) $\xi_i=d^{-1}\sum_{r=1}^dt_{i+r}$ reproduce the parameter. The [B-spline differentiation formula](b-spline-differentiation-formula.md) gives derivative coefficients $d(\xi_i-\xi_{i-1})/(t_{i+d}-t_i)=1$. Their lower-degree [partition of unity](partition-of-unity.md) makes the derivative of the weighted sum equal to one. The left endpoint value is the left parameter endpoint, fixing the integration constant to zero. This proves [linear precision](linear-precision-of-a-geometric-basis.md) even with unequal knot intervals.
