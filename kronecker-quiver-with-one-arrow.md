# Kronecker quiver with one arrow

↑ **Parent:** [Quiver](quiver.md)

The [quiver](quiver.md) has two vertices and one arrow. Splitting a representation's kernel, image and complementary target decomposes it into source-only and sink-only simples and copies of the identity $k\to k$. These are its three indecomposables. Fixed-dimension isomorphism classes are [rank orbits of a matrix under left-right multiplication](rank-orbit-of-a-matrix-under-left-right-multiplication.md).
