# Vortex tube

↑ **Parent:** [Vorticity](vorticity.md)

A [vortex tube](vortex-tube.md) is a bundle of vortex lines bounded by a surface to which [vorticity](vorticity.md) is tangent. [Circulation](circulation-physics.md) measures its [vorticity](vorticity.md) flux. Stretching a nearly incompressible tube reduces its cross-sectional area and increases its [vorticity](vorticity.md). The [Burgers vortex](burgers-vortex.md) is a local model in which this concentration balances viscous diffusion.
