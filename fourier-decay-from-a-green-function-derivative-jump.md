# Fourier decay from a Green-function derivative jump

↑ **Parent:** [One-dimensional modified Helmholtz Green function](one-dimensional-modified-helmholtz-green-function.md)

If $H$ is continuous, piecewise twice differentiable, and has derivative jump $[H']=H'(y+)-H'(y-)$ at $y$, then its distributional second [derivative](derivative.md) is $H''_{\mathrm{reg}}+[H']\delta_y$. If the regular part is integrable and the boundary terms vanish, two [integrations by parts](integration-by-parts.md) give the displayed [asymptotic expansion](asymptotic-expansion.md) by the [Riemann-Lebesgue lemma](riemann-lebesgue-lemma.md). A jump $[H]$ in $H$ itself instead produces $[H]e^{-iqy}/(iq)$ at leading order. This distinguishes the different decay rates of the [streamfunction](stream-function.md) and streamwise [velocity](velocity.md) in [linear inviscid damping in unbounded Couette flow](linear-inviscid-damping-in-unbounded-couette-flow.md).
