# Critical ionizing photon rate of a cored power-law nebula

↑ **Parent:** [Recombination integral for a power-law nebula](recombination-integral-for-a-power-law-nebula.md)

For the [cored power-law density profile](cored-power-law-density-profile.md) that is constant inside $r_c$ and falls as $r^{-\beta}$ outside, with $\beta>3/2$, the maximum integrated [Case B recombination](case-b-recombination.md) rate is

$$
Q_{\rm crit}=4\pi\alpha n_0^2r_c^3\left(\frac13+\frac1{2\beta-3}\right).
$$

A [hydrogen-ionizing photon production rate](hydrogen-ionizing-photon-production-rate.md) greater than this has no finite radius at [photoionization equilibrium](photoionization-equilibrium.md). Equality requires an infinite ionized radius. Other core shapes change the coefficient but retain the convergence condition $\beta>3/2$.
