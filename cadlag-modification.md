<h1 id="cadlag-modification">Càdlàg modification</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Modification of a stochastic process](modification-of-a-stochastic-process.md)

A [càdlàg modification](cadlag-modification.md) of a [stochastic process](stochastic-process-split.md) is a [modification of a stochastic process](modification-of-a-stochastic-process.md) whose paths are [càdlàg](cadlag.md) on one event of probability one. Equality [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md) at every separately fixed time does not imply [indistinguishability of stochastic processes](indistinguishability-of-stochastic-processes.md) when the original process lacks path regularity. A [stochastic process](stochastic-process-split.md) starting at zero, with [independent increments](independent-increments.md), [stationary increments](stationary-increments.md), and [stochastic continuity](stochastic-continuity.md), has a [càdlàg modification](cadlag-modification.md) and is a [Lévy process](levy-process.md) under the intrinsic definition.
