# Contact distribution symplectic form

↑ **Parent:** [Contact distribution](contact-distribution.md)

For a [contact form](contact-form.md) on a $(2r+1)$-manifold, $d\alpha|_{\ker\alpha}$ is [nondegenerate](nondegenerate-bilinear-form.md): the contact condition says its top exterior power is nowhere zero on the hyperplanes. This fiberwise [symplectic form](symplectic-form.md) identifies contact-plane vectors with covectors on that plane and determines the [horizontal generator of contact stability](horizontal-generator-of-contact-stability.md).
