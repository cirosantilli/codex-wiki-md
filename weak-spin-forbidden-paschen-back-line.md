# Weak spin-forbidden Paschen-Back line

↑ **Parent:** [Paschen-Back effect](paschen-back-effect.md)

At large but finite [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md), opposite-spin admixtures in approximate product [eigenstates](eigenstate.md) let nominally spin-forbidden electric-dipole channels borrow [intensity](intensity-physics.md). Their amplitudes scale as [spin-orbit interaction](spin-orbit-interaction.md) [energy](energy-split.md) divided by Zeeman [energy](energy-split.md); their intensities therefore vanish quadratically in that ratio.
