# Bernoulli formula for zeta values at nonpositive integers

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann zeta function](riemann-zeta-function.md)

For every integer $n\geq1$, the [Riemann zeta function](riemann-zeta-function.md) satisfies $\zeta(1-n)=(-1)^{n-1}B_n/n$. One proof applies [meromorphic continuation of a Mellin transform from an asymptotic expansion](meromorphic-continuation-of-a-mellin-transform-from-an-asymptotic-expansion.md) to $(e^y-1)^{-1}$, whose transform is the [Bose integral](bose-integral.md) $\Gamma(s)\zeta(s)$, and divides residues by the [residues of the Gamma function](residues-of-the-gamma-function.md). The formula includes $\zeta(0)=-1/2$.
