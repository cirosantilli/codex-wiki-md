# Freeness criterion for a subgroup of a polygon-cover deck group

↑ **Parent:** [Paired-polygon construction of finite surface covers](paired-polygon-construction-of-finite-surface-covers.md)

In the [paired-polygon construction of finite surface covers](paired-polygon-construction-of-finite-surface-covers.md), a [subgroup](subgroup.md) $U$ acts freely precisely when the displayed intersection is trivial for every vertex word $C_j$ and every $g\in T$. The quotient map $M\to U\backslash M$ is then a [normal covering map](normal-covering-map.md) with deck [group](group-split.md) $U$ and $\chi(U\backslash M)=\chi(M)/|U|$. Normality of $U$ in $T$ is instead the criterion for the intermediate [orbifold](orbifold.md) cover $U\backslash M\to T\backslash M$ to be normal. These are different [covering maps](covering-space.md).
