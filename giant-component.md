# Giant component

↑ **Parent:** [Random graph](random-graph.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giant_component)

A giant component in a sequence of [random graphs](random-graph.md) on $n$ [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) has order proportional to $n$. Its existence can change at a [threshold function for a monotone graph property](threshold-function-for-a-monotone-graph-property.md). A [giant component](giant-component.md) need not contain every [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md), and its uniqueness requires proof for the model in question.
