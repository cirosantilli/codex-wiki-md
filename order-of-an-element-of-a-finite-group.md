# Order of an element of a finite group

↑ **Parent:** [Group theory](group-theory-split.md)

The order of an element is the least positive power equal to the identity and divides the order of a finite group.

This is the [order of a group element](order-of-a-group-element.md) in the finite setting, one use of [order (group theory)](order-group-theory.md).
