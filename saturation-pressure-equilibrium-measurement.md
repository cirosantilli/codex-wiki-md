# Saturation-pressure equilibrium measurement

↑ **Parent:** [Clausius-Clapeyron relation](clausius-clapeyron-relation.md)

A concrete experiment turns the [phase coexistence curve](phase-coexistence-curve.md) into pairs of [temperature](temperature-split.md) and [saturation pressure](vapor-pressure.md). A 1969 paper reports [water](water.md) measurements made in 1942 and 1947–1949 using a boiler and precision [mercury](mercury-element.md) [manometer](manometer.md). [Platinum resistance thermometers](platinum-resistance-thermometer.md) read the boiler [temperature](temperature-split.md) through a [resistance bridge](resistance-bridge.md). The [pressure](pressure-split.md)-transfer line passed through an ice-water condenser and a dry-ice trap; [helium](helium.md) filled connecting sections. Corrected [mercury](mercury-element.md) column height gave the [pressure](pressure-split.md) at the thermometer coils, including [hydrostatic pressure](hydrostatic-pressure.md) contributions from [water](water.md) vapor, [helium](helium.md), [mercury](mercury-element.md), and [mercury](mercury-element.md) vapor.[Stimson, §§1–2, pp. 493–495: boiler, bridge thermometry, and fluid-head corrections](some-precise-measurements-of-the-vapor-pressure-of-water-in-the-range-from-25-to-100-%C2%B0c-by-h-f-stimson.md)

Controls included normal/reversed-lead readings, readings at two thermometer [electric currents](electric-current.md) extrapolated to zero current to remove self-heating, repeated bridge calibration, and daily [triple point](triple-point.md) [thermometer calibration](thermometer-calibration.md) checks. Cellar [temperature](temperature-split.md) measurements corrected fluid-column and gauge-block [thermal expansion](thermal-expansion.md). One measurement series was excluded because a trapped gas bubble was suspected of corrupting the [mercury](mercury-element.md) head. The paper adjusted measurements to round [temperatures](temperature-split.md) using previously tabulated slopes; those adjusted values therefore are not independent measurements of those slopes.[Stimson, §2, pp. 494–495, and §3, p. 495: corrections and suspected gas-bubble bias](some-precise-measurements-of-the-vapor-pressure-of-water-in-the-range-from-25-to-100-%C2%B0c-by-h-f-stimson.md)

The thermodynamic inference requires [liquid](liquid.md) and [gas](gas.md) in [phase coexistence](phase-coexistence.md), a well-defined common [temperature](temperature-split.md), and a [pressure](pressure-split.md) corrected to that same location. Leaks, contamination, or a [temperature gradient](temperature-gradient.md) can spoil that interpretation. A separate [calorimetry](calorimetry-split.md) measurement of [latent heat](latent-heat.md), together with the two [specific volumes](specific-volume.md), allows a test of the full [Clapeyron equation](clausius-clapeyron-relation.md). Converting a measured [phase coexistence curve](phase-coexistence-curve.md) slope directly into [latent heat](latent-heat.md) instead assumes that equation; it is not an independent test.
