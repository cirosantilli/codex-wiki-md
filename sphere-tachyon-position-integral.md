# Sphere tachyon position integral

↑ **Parent:** [Closed-string scattering amplitude](closed-string-scattering-amplitude.md)

The tree-level [Polyakov path integral](polyakov-path-integral.md) for closed-string [tachyons](tachyon.md) reduces in [conformal gauge](conformal-gauge.md) to their vertex-position integral on the [Riemann sphere](riemann-sphere.md). Three positions are fixed by the residual [Möbius group](mobius-group.md); the other $N-3$ complex positions are integrated. The embedding-field Gaussian integral produces [momentum conservation](momentum-conservation.md) and the [Koba-Nielsen factor](koba-nielsen-factor.md).
