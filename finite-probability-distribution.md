# Finite probability distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Probability distribution](probability-distribution-split.md)

A [probability mass function](probability-mass-function.md) with finite positive-mass support, nonnegative masses and total mass one. Listing its support means listing exactly positive-mass outcomes once, excluding zero-[probability](probability.md) endpoint outcomes. A computational interface returning floating-point masses generally approximates exact rational or real probabilities.
