# Equivalent terminal and maximal norms for L2-bounded continuous martingales

↑ **Parent:** [L2-bounded continuous martingale](l2-bounded-continuous-martingale.md)

For a [L2-bounded continuous martingale](l2-bounded-continuous-martingale.md), the terminal and maximal [norms](norm.md) satisfy

$$
\|M_\infty\|_2\leq\left\|\sup_{t\geq0}|M_t|\right\|_2\leq2\|M_\infty\|_2.
$$

The first inequality follows from [almost sure convergence](almost-sure-convergence.md). Apply the [Doob L2 maximal inequality](doob-l2-maximal-inequality.md) on $[0,T]$ and then the [monotone convergence theorem](monotone-convergence-theorem.md) as $T\to\infty$ for the second. These are [equivalent norms](equivalent-norms.md) on the space modulo [indistinguishability of stochastic processes](indistinguishability-of-stochastic-processes.md); neither inequality needs a zero initial value.
