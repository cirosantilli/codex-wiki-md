# Representation ring of a semisimple Lie algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Lie algebra representation](lie-algebra-representation.md)

The representation ring of finite-dimensional modules for a complex [semisimple Lie algebra](semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md) is the abelian group generated by module classes, with $[V\oplus U]=[V]+[U]$, and multiplication $[V][U]=[V\otimes U]$. [Weyl complete reducibility theorem](weyl-complete-reducibility-theorem.md) makes the classes of [irreducible representations](irreducible-representation.md) a free integral basis. The [formal character](formal-character-of-a-weight-module.md) map identifies this ring with the [Weyl group](weyl-group.md) invariant part of the [group ring of a weight lattice](group-ring-of-a-weight-lattice.md). Injectivity follows from distinct [highest weights](highest-weight-of-a-representation.md); surjectivity follows by subtracting the character with each maximal dominant weight, whose highest-weight coefficient is one, from a Weyl-invariant finite sum.
