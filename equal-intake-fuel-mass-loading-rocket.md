# Equal-intake-fuel mass-loading rocket

↑ **Parent:** [Rocket equation with stationary mass accretion](rocket-equation-with-stationary-mass-accretion.md)

A Newtonian rocket collecting stationary matter at rate $\mu$ and combining it with the same rate of carried fuel ejects [mass](mass.md) at rate $2\mu$. If relative exhaust [speed](speed.md) is $u_0$, [momentum conservation](momentum-conservation.md) gives the title equation and [mass conservation](mass-conservation.md) gives $\dot m=-\mu$. With constant $\mu$ and initial rest, $m=m_i-\mu t$ and $u=2u_0\mu t/m_i$. The limiting [speed](speed.md) $2u_0$ would require zero remaining [mass](mass.md) in this ideal solution; positive dry [mass](mass.md) terminates it sooner. A constant collection rate per time is distinct from a constant collection cross-section through a uniform medium.
