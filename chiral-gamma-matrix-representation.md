# Chiral gamma-matrix representation

↑ **Parent:** [Gamma matrices](gamma-matrices.md)

For signature $+---$, use $\gamma^0=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&I_2\\I_2&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)$ and $\gamma^i=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&\sigma^i\\-\sigma^i&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)$, where $\sigma^i$ are [Pauli matrices](pauli-matrices.md). Their [Clifford algebra](clifford-algebra.md) follows directly from the Pauli [anticommutator](anticommutator.md). The [chirality matrix](chirality-matrix.md) is $\gamma^5=\operatorname{diag}(-I_2,I_2)$, separating the two chiral components of a [Dirac spinor](dirac-spinor.md).
