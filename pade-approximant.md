<h1 id="pade-approximant">Padé approximant</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Rational function](rational-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Padé_approximant)

A Padé approximant is a [rational function](rational-function.md) with numerator degree at most $m$ and denominator degree at most $n$, normalized by $Q_n(0)=1$, such that $Q_n(z)f(z)-P_m(z)=O(z^{m+n+1})$ near zero, when such an approximant exists. For the [exponential function](exponential-function.md), its $[1/1]$ approximant is $(1+z/2)/(1-z/2)$. Applying this rational function to a matrix gives the [Crank-Nicolson method](crank-nicolson-method.md). Diagonal and subdiagonal approximants also give stability functions of [Gauss collocation methods](gauss-legendre-method.md) and [Radau IIA methods](radau-iia-method.md) respectively.
