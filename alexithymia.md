# Alexithymia

↑ **Parent:** [Emotion](emotion.md)

Difficulty identifying and describing one’s own [emotions](emotion.md), often accompanied by an externally oriented thinking style. It is not synonymous with absence of [emotion](emotion.md), [autism](autism.md) or inability to care about others.
