# Photon-number parity

↑ **Parent:** [Parity operator](parity-operator.md)

Even and odd occupation sectors have [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $+1$ and $-1$. Pair creation preserves parity, whereas one [photon addition](photon-addition.md) or [photon subtraction](photon-subtraction.md) reverses it. An [optical squeezed vacuum](optical-squeezed-vacuum.md) is even and a [squeezed one-photon state](squeezed-one-photon-state.md) is odd.
