# Cup length

↑ **Parent:** [Cup product](cup-product.md)

The largest number of positive-degree [cohomology classes](cohomology-class.md) whose [cup product](cup-product.md) is nonzero, with a specified coefficient ring $R$; it can be infinite. It is zero if there is no nonzero positive-degree class. The [vanishing cup product from an open cover](vanishing-cup-product-from-an-open-cover.md) argument implies that [Lusternik–Schnirelmann category](lusternik-schnirelmann-category.md) is at least cup length plus one. For [Complex projective space](complex-projective-space.md) $\mathbb{CP}^r$, its integral cup length is $r$.
