<h1 id="kahler-identities">Kähler identities</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Lefschetz operator of a Kähler manifold](lefschetz-operator-of-a-kahler-manifold.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kähler_identities)

For the [Lefschetz operator of a Kähler manifold](lefschetz-operator-of-a-kahler-manifold.md) and its adjoint, the Kähler identities include

$$
[\Lambda,\partial]=i\bar\partial^*,\qquad
[\Lambda,\bar\partial]=-i\partial^*,\qquad
[L,\partial^*]=i\bar\partial,\qquad
[L,\bar\partial^*]=-i\partial.
$$
