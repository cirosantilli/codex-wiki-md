# Errors-in-variables model

↑ **Parent:** [Measurement error](measurement-error.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Errors-in-variables_model)

An errors-in-variables model distinguishes a latent true predictor from its noisy measurement and models both the measurement and the response. For example, $W_i\mid U_i\sim N(U_i,s^2)$ and $Y_i\mid U_i\sim N(a+bU_i,v^2)$, with a population distribution for $U_i$, propagate predictor uncertainty into inference on $b$. Treating $W_i$ as exact can create [attenuation bias from classical measurement error](attenuation-bias-from-classical-measurement-error.md). Identifiability requires appropriate information about the measurement process or latent predictor distribution.
