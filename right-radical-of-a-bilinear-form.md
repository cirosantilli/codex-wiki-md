# Right radical of a bilinear form

↑ **Parent:** [Radical of a bilinear form](radical-of-a-bilinear-form.md)

The [right radical](right-radical-of-a-bilinear-form.md) is $\{w\in V:B(v,w)=0\text{ for every }v\in V\}$. If $A$ is the [matrix of a bilinear form](matrix-of-a-bilinear-form.md), this condition is $Aw=0$. For $B(v,w)=v_1w_2$ from the [left radical](left-radical-of-a-bilinear-form.md) example, the [right radical](right-radical-of-a-bilinear-form.md) is $\operatorname{span}\{(1,0)\}$. The [left radical](left-radical-of-a-bilinear-form.md) and [right radical](right-radical-of-a-bilinear-form.md) differ here because the [bilinear form](bilinear-form-split.md) is neither symmetric nor alternating. For a [symmetric bilinear form](symmetric-bilinear-form.md) or an [alternating bilinear form](alternating-bilinear-form.md), reversing a zero pairing preserves zero and the two [linear subspaces](vector-subspace.md) coincide.
