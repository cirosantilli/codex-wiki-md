# Holonomy groups of closed orientable flat three-manifolds

↑ **Parent:** [Flat manifold](flat-manifold.md)

These are precisely the faithful linear holonomy groups of closed orientable Euclidean three-manifolds. Cyclic cases arise from planar lattice rotations combined with axial screw translations. The noncyclic case is generated by two perpendicular half-turn screws. The list refers to the maximal translation lattice, not to arbitrary finite quotients of a [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md).
