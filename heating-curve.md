# Heating curve

↑ **Parent:** [Heat capacity](heat-capacity.md)

A heating curve records [temperature](temperature-split.md) against accumulated supplied [heat](heat-split.md) under a specified constraint. Within one [thermodynamic phase](thermodynamic-phase.md), its slope is $dT/dQ=1/C_X$, where $C_X$ is the constrained [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md). A [latent heat](latent-heat.md) plateau instead records changing [phase fraction](phase-fraction.md) at the [phase coexistence](phase-coexistence.md) [temperature](temperature-split.md). The [sensible heat](sensible-heat.md) example combines two sloping intervals and a [melting](melting.md) plateau in one heating budget.
