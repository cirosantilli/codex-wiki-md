# Recursive presentation of a structure

↑ **Parent:** [Model theory](model-theory.md)

A recursive presentation identifies the domain of a countable structure with a [computable set](computable-set.md), with its basic operations given by [total computable functions](total-computable-function.md) and its basic relations decidable. In a fixed finite signature this makes evaluation of [atomic formulas](atomic-formula.md) effective. A countable structure may have a [recursive presentation of a structure](recursive-presentation-of-a-structure.md) only for some choices of presentation, or none at all.
