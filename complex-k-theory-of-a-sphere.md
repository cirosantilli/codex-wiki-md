# Complex K-theory of a sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Bott isomorphism](bott-isomorphism.md)

Complex Bott periodicity gives

$$
\widetilde K^i(S^d)\cong
\begin{cases}
\mathbb Z,&i-d\text{ is even},\\
0,&i-d\text{ is odd}.
\end{cases}
$$
