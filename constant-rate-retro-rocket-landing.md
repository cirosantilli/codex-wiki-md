# Constant-rate retro-rocket landing

↑ **Parent:** [Rocket equation](rocket-equation.md)

With downward [speed](speed.md) $v$, downward relative exhaust [speed](speed.md) $v_E>0$ and [mass](mass.md) $M=M_0-\alpha t$, the ideal vertical equation is $\dot v=g-\alpha v_E/M$. Integration gives $v=v_0+gt+v_E\log(M/M_0)$. Height follows from $\dot h=-v$; its touchdown condition must be imposed together with the final fuel fraction.
