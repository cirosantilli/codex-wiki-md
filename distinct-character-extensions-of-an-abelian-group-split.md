# Distinct-character extensions of an abelian group split

↑ **Parent:** [Group representation](group-representation.md)

A two-dimensional representation of an [abelian group](abelian-group.md) fitting into an extension of two distinct [linear characters](linear-character.md) splits. Choose an element on which the [linear characters](linear-character.md) differ. Its matrix has two distinct [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md), and all commuting group operators preserve its two eigenlines. Those eigenlines supply the two invariant [linear character](linear-character.md) summands. The argument does not apply when the two [linear characters](linear-character.md) coincide.
