# Universal quotient bundle need not have a nowhere-zero section

↑ **Parent:** [Universal quotient bundle on a real Grassmannian](universal-quotient-bundle-on-a-real-grassmannian.md)

Over $\operatorname{Gr}_1(\mathbb R^2)=\mathbb{RP}^1$, the universal quotient is the orthogonal-complement line bundle, a [Möbius line bundle](mobius-line-bundle.md). Writing a section as $a(\theta)(-\sin\theta,\cos\theta)$ forces $a(\theta+\pi)=-a(\theta)$. Continuity then forces a zero, so no [nowhere-zero section](nowhere-zero-section.md) exists.
