# Polymers at interfaces

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer physics](polymer-physics.md)

An interface changes [polymer](polymer-split.md) conformations through segment-surface affinity, [solvent](solvent.md) selectivity and [confinement](confinement.md). Adsorbed chains make bound trains, intervening loops and free tails; end-grafted chains cross from isolated mushrooms to overlapping brushes as coverage rises. Surface binding [energy](energy-split.md) competes with chain [conformational entropy](conformational-entropy.md) and crowding.
