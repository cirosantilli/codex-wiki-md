# Metal mass fraction

↑ **Parent:** [Metallicity](metallicity.md)

The fraction of total [mass](mass.md) in [chemical elements](chemical-element.md) heavier than [helium](helium.md), so the [hydrogen mass fraction](hydrogen-mass-fraction.md), [helium mass fraction](helium-mass-fraction.md) and metal mass fraction satisfy $X+Y+Z=1$. Despite the astronomical name, this includes elements such as [carbon](carbon.md) and [oxygen](oxygen.md) that are not chemical metals.
