# Surface gravity wave forced by a traveling bottom

↑ **Parent:** [Airy wave theory](airy-wave-theory.md)

Consider fluid at rest with mean depth $d$ and a bottom displacement $a\sin(k(x-ct))$. The [linearized free-surface boundary conditions](linearized-free-surface-boundary-conditions.md) at $y=0$ and moving-bottom [kinematic boundary condition](kinematic-boundary-condition.md) at $y=-d$ give a periodic particular response $\eta=b\sin(k(x-ct))$ and [velocity potential](velocity-potential.md)

$$
\phi=-\frac b{kc}[g\cosh(ky)+kc^2\sinh(ky)]\cos(k(x-ct)).
$$

The bottom derivative fixes the signed response coefficient to the title formula. Here $k>0$, $c\ne0$, and the forcing must avoid $k^2c^2=gk\tanh(kd)$. This equality is [resonance](resonance.md) with the natural finite-depth [surface gravity wave](surface-gravity-wave.md); nonzero undamped resonant forcing has no bounded periodic solution. The response reverses phase below resonance. General initial data can add free [surface gravity waves](surface-gravity-wave.md), which do not decay in this inviscid model. Near resonance the resulting [wave steepness](wave-steepness.md) and particle excursions, rather than the bottom displacement alone, determine whether the [linearization](linearization.md) remains valid.
