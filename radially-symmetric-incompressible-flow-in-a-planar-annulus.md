# Radially symmetric incompressible flow in a planar annulus

↑ **Parent:** [Velocity potential](velocity-potential.md)

For liquid occupying $a(t)<r<b(t)$, radial symmetry and [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md) imply

$$
u_r(r,t)=\frac{a\dot a}{r},
\qquad
\phi(r,t)=a\dot a\log r+C(t).
$$

The [kinematic boundary condition](kinematic-boundary-condition.md) at the outer interface gives

$$
b^2-a^2=\text{constant},
$$

which expresses conservation of the liquid's area.
