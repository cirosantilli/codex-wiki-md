<h1 id="bernoulli-s-principle">Bernoulli's principle</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Euler equations for an inviscid fluid](euler-equations-for-an-inviscid-fluid.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernoulli's_principle)

The [Bernoulli principle](bernoulli-s-principle.md) relates [pressure](pressure.md), [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) per unit volume and [Newtonian gravitational potential](newtonian-gravitational-potential.md) along a streamline of steady [inviscid flow](inviscid-flow.md). For constant [mass density](density.md), the [Bernoulli equation](bernoulli-equation.md) gives $p/\rho+u^2/2+\Phi$ constant along that streamline. Constancy across different streamlines requires further hypotheses, such as [irrotational flow](irrotational-flow.md).
