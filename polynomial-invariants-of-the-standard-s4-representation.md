# Polynomial invariants of the standard S4 representation

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial invariant ring](polynomial-invariant-ring.md)

Realize the [standard representation of the symmetric group](standard-representation-of-the-symmetric-group.md) on $W=\{x\in\mathbb C^4:\sum_ix_i=0\}$. The symmetric power sums $p_k=\sum_ix_i^k$, for $k=2,3,4$, freely generate its [polynomial invariant ring](polynomial-invariant-ring.md). To justify the description, average any polynomial lift from $W$ to $\mathbb C^4$ over the [symmetric group](symmetric-group.md); the resulting symmetric lift is a polynomial in the [elementary symmetric polynomials](elementary-symmetric-polynomial.md) $e_1,e_2,e_3,e_4$, and restriction sets $e_1=0$. The remaining three generators are algebraically independent. The [Newton identities](newton-s-identities.md) express them equivalently through $p_2,p_3,p_4$ over the [complex numbers](complex-number.md). In coordinates $x=(a,b,c,-a-b-c)$, one has $p_2=a^2+b^2+c^2+(a+b+c)^2$ and $p_3=-3(a+b)(b+c)(c+a)$.
