# Oxide-film transport limitation

↑ **Parent:** [Passivation](passivation.md)

Slowing of electrochemical film growth as the deposited oxide restricts [electron](electron.md) or ion transport. Increasing thickness increases the transport barrier even while dissolved precursor remains available.
