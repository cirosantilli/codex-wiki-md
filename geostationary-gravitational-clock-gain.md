# Geostationary gravitational clock gain

↑ **Parent:** [Gravitational time dilation](gravitational-time-dilation.md)

For spherical weak-field [gravity](gravity.md), a stationary clock at [radius](radius.md) $r_g>R$ gains the displayed [proper time](proper-time.md) relative to a surface clock over coordinate interval $t$. A [geostationary orbit](geostationary-orbit.md) has $r_g=(GM/\Omega_\oplus^2)^{1/3}$. This is the potential contribution only. Combining it with orbital and surface rotational special-relativistic terms requires a consistent reference frame and must not be silently included in a gravitational-only comparison.
