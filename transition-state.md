# Transition state

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical kinetics](chemical-kinetics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transition_state)

A [transition state](transition-state.md) describes configurations at the barrier between [reactants](reactant.md) and [reaction products](product-chemistry.md) in an [elementary reaction](elementary-reaction.md). It is a crossing region, rather than a stable substance that accumulates in a bottle. The IUPAC definition characterizes these configurations by equal probabilities of proceeding toward [reactants](reactant.md) or [reaction products](product-chemistry.md): [IUPAC Gold Book: transition state](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/T06468). Lowering the barrier relative to the starting configuration can increase a [reaction rate](reaction-rate.md), even when the overall change in [Gibbs free energy](gibbs-free-energy.md) is unchanged.
