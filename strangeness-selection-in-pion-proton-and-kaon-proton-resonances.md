# Strangeness selection in pion-proton and kaon-proton resonances

↑ **Parent:** [Meson-baryon scattering](meson-baryon-scattering.md)

The $\pi^+p$ channel has baryon number one, strangeness zero and $I_3=3/2$, matching the ordinary $uuu$ [Delta baryon](delta-baryon.md). The $K^+p$ channel has strangeness plus one, which no three-light-quark baryon can have because each strange quark contributes minus one. A resonance in that channel would require exotic content such as four quarks and an antiquark; the ordinary low-energy qqq multiplets therefore explain the contrast.
