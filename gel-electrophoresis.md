# Gel electrophoresis

↑ **Parent:** [Biology](biology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gel_electrophoresis)

Separation of charged [molecules](molecule.md) through a gel in an [electric field](electric-field.md). Denaturing conditions suppress folded structures, helping compare [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) lengths; matching migration alone does not establish an identical [nucleotide sequence](nucleic-acid-sequence.md) or identical [covalent bonds](covalent-bond.md). In the [ribozyme](ribozyme.md) experiments, labelled [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) was detected after separation by exposing photographic film.
