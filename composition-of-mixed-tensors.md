# Composition of mixed tensors

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor](tensor.md)

A type-$(1,1)$ [tensor](tensor.md) identifies with an [endomorphism](endomorphism.md) of a finite-dimensional [vector space](vector-space-split.md). Composing two such maps produces another type-$(1,1)$ [tensor](tensor.md), because $(X,\eta)\mapsto\eta(B(A(X)))$ is a [multilinear map](multilinear-map.md). In output-first components the result is $C^a{}_b=B^a{}_cA^c{}_b$; the contracted index implements composition, and a basis change conjugates the corresponding matrices.
