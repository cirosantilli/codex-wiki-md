# One minus a nilpotent element is a unit

↑ **Parent:** [Unit in a ring](unit-in-a-ring.md)

If $z$ is a [nilpotent element](nilpotent.md) of a [ring](ring.md), choose $m\geq1$ with $z^m=0$. The finite [geometric series](geometric-series.md) identity gives

$$
(1-z)(1+z+\cdots+z^{m-1})=1-z^m=1,
$$

and the factors commute, so $1-z$ is a [unit](unit-in-a-ring.md). In a [commutative ring](commutative-ring.md), every multiple of a [nilpotent element](nilpotent.md) is again [nilpotent](nilpotent.md). Consequently an equality $x=ax^2$ with $x$ [nilpotent](nilpotent.md) forces $x=0$: rewrite it as $(1-ax)x=0$ and multiply by the inverse of $1-ax$.
