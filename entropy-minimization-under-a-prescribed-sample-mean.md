# Entropy minimization under a prescribed sample mean

↑ **Parent:** [Information projection](information-projection.md)

For positive probabilities $p_i$ on a finite alphabet with numerical values $a_i$, impose $\sum_iq_ia_i=m$. The [relative entropy](kullback-leibler-divergence.md) is minimized by the displayed [exponential tilting](exponential-tilting.md), with $\theta$ chosen to give mean $m$. For any other feasible distribution $r$, direct substitution gives $D(r\Vert p)=D(r\Vert q)+\theta m-\log\sum_jp_je^{\theta a_j}$. [Relative entropy nonnegativity](relative-entropy-nonnegativity.md) proves optimality and uniqueness. The [method of types](method-of-types.md) makes this the typical empirical distribution conditioned on the rare sample-mean constraint, on the leading exponential scale.
