# NMOS transistor

↑ **Parent:** [MOSFET](mosfet.md)

In the ideal digital switch model, an enhancement-mode [NMOS transistor](nmos-transistor.md) controlled by a high gate signal conducts, and a low gate signal disconnects its channel. Series devices implement [logical conjunction](logical-conjunction.md) of conducting conditions; parallel devices implement [logical disjunction](logical-disjunction.md). An [NMOS transistor](nmos-transistor.md) [pull-down network](pull-down-network.md) connects a [CMOS](cmos.md) output to the low supply rail.
