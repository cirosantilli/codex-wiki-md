# Vector cosmological perturbation

↑ **Parent:** [Linear cosmological perturbation theory](linear-cosmological-perturbation-theory-split.md)

A vector cosmological perturbation is the transverse-vector sector of linear metric and matter perturbations. For a [Fourier mode](fourier-mode.md) with nonzero wavevector $\mathbf k$, transversality removes the component parallel to $\mathbf k$, leaving two [helicity](helicity.md) components. In a gauge with $ds^2=a^2[-d\eta^2+2B_i d\eta dx^i+\delta_{ij}dx^i dx^j]$, $\partial_iB_i=0$ describes the vector metric perturbation.
