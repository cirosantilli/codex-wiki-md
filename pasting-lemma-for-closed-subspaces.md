# Pasting lemma for closed subspaces

↑ **Parent:** [Topology](topology-split.md)

If a [topological space](topological-space.md) is the union of finitely many [closed subspaces](closed-subspace.md) and [continuous maps](continuous-map.md) on those subspaces agree on every overlap, they combine into a [continuous map](continuous-map.md) on the whole space. For a [closed set](closed-set.md) in the target, its [preimage](preimage.md) is a [finite union](finite-union.md) of [closed sets](closed-set.md), proving continuity.
