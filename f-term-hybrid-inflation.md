# F-term hybrid inflation

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmic inflation](cosmic-inflation-split.md)

The bilinear hybrid [superpotential](superpotential.md) couples an inflaton $S$ to two waterfall fields $u,v$. In global [supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md), $V=|g|^2(|uv-\zeta|^2+|S|^2(|u|^2+|v|^2))$. The branch $u=v=0$ breaks supersymmetry and is transversely stable for $|S|^2>|\zeta|$, while the zero-energy branch $S=0$, $uv=\zeta$ is supersymmetric. A canonical [Kähler potential](kahler-potential.md) in [supergravity](supergravity.md) slightly lifts the inflationary valley. It must not be described as two isolated stationary minima of the full potential.
