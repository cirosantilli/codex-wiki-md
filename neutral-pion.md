# Neutral pion

↑ **Parent:** [Pion](pion.md)

The [neutral pion](neutral-pion.md) is the neutral member of the [pion](pion.md) isospin triplet, with leading flavour wavefunction $(u\bar u-d\bar d)/\sqrt2$. Its dominant decay is to two [photons](photon.md).
