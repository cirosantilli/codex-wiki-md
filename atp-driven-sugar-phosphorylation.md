# ATP-driven sugar phosphorylation

↑ **Parent:** [Biochemistry](biochemistry-split.md)

[Hexokinase](hexokinase.md) provides a concrete connection between [sugar](sugar.md) chemistry and [energy coupling](energy-coupling.md): it transfers a terminal [phosphate](phosphate.md) group from [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) to [glucose](glucose.md), forming a phosphorylated [sugar](sugar.md) and [adenosine diphosphate](adenosine-diphosphate.md). The [enzyme](enzyme-split.md) accelerates the transfer, while consuming [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) changes the net [chemical reaction](chemical-reaction-split.md) being carried out. The donated group reaches the [substrate](substrate-biochemistry.md) through the catalytic route.[§7.2, First Half of Glycolysis, step 1: hexokinase and ATP-dependent glucose phosphorylation](biology-2e-by-mary-ann-clark-and-others.md)

For a symbolic teaching model, write $S$ for an unphosphorylated [sugar](sugar.md), $S\text{-}P$ for its phosphorylated form, and $P_i$ for [inorganic phosphate](inorganic-phosphate.md). These are labels for chemical states, not complete elemental formulas. Using one consistent reference convention at fixed [pH](ph.md) and [temperature](temperature-split.md), stipulate the following molar changes in [Gibbs free energy](gibbs-free-energy.md):

$$
\begin{aligned}
S+P_i&\longrightarrow S\text{-}P+\mathrm{H_2O},
&\Delta G_1^{\circ\prime}&=+12\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}},\\
\mathrm{ATP}+\mathrm{H_2O}&\longrightarrow\mathrm{ADP}+P_i,
&\Delta G_2^{\circ\prime}&=-32\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}},\\
S+\mathrm{ATP}&\longrightarrow S\text{-}P+\mathrm{ADP},
&\Delta G_{\mathrm{net}}^{\circ\prime}&=-20\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}.
\end{aligned}
$$

All three numbers are chosen illustrative values, not measured properties of [glucose](glucose.md) or a particular [enzyme](enzyme-split.md). The prime marks a biochemical reference convention with [pH](ph.md) held fixed; explicit proton and metal-binding bookkeeping is suppressed consistently. The first two lines are a thermodynamic decomposition: cancelling [water](water.md) and [inorganic phosphate](inorganic-phosphate.md) gives the net transfer. They do not assert that the [reaction mechanism](reaction-mechanism.md) first releases free [inorganic phosphate](inorganic-phosphate.md) and then recaptures it.

Original symbolic reaction diagram, with a schematic catalytic cycle and a separate thermodynamic sum. $S$ and $S\text{-}P$ label hypothetical [sugar](sugar.md) states. The cycle groups many possible microscopic steps and draws no molecular structure. The chosen $+12-32=-20\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$ refers to one consistent reference convention; it is not a measured energy profile or an [activation energy](activation-energy.md) barrier.

Because [Gibbs free energy](gibbs-free-energy.md) is a [state function](state-function.md), consistently defined reaction changes add. A working [reaction mechanism](reaction-mechanism.md) must also connect the desired conversion to [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) consumption; independently allowing [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) [hydrolysis](hydrolysis.md) beside the [sugar](sugar.md) does not establish [energy coupling](energy-coupling.md). The general distinction is developed under [energy coupling](energy-coupling.md). [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md)-dependent [phosphorylation](phosphorylation.md) of a membrane [protein](protein-split.md) can likewise connect a chemical change to [active transport](active-transport.md).[§6.4, Energy Coupling, Figure 6.14: transfer to the sodium-potassium pump and its conformational cycle](biology-2e-by-mary-ann-clark-and-others.md)

Actual [Gibbs free energy](gibbs-free-energy.md) changes depend on [thermodynamic activities](thermodynamic-activity.md). Even a favourable reference sum can be overcome by accumulation of [reaction products](product-chemistry.md) or depletion of [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md); it supplies no prediction of the [reaction rate](reaction-rate.md). A living [biological cell](biological-cell-split.md) also regenerates [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md). Sustained operation therefore requires a connected [metabolism](metabolism.md), not an inexhaustible stock of a special [molecule](molecule-split.md).
