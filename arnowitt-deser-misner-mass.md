# Arnowitt-Deser-Misner mass

↑ **Parent:** [Arnowitt-Deser-Misner energy](arnowitt-deser-misner-energy.md)

For an asymptotically flat stationary solution in its asymptotic rest frame, the [ADM mass](arnowitt-deser-misner-mass.md) is the gravitational mass measured by the [Arnowitt-Deser-Misner energy](arnowitt-deser-misner-energy.md). It is fixed by the asymptotic metric falloff, not by a coordinate parameter's name. If spatial ADM momentum is present, distinguish the asymptotic energy component from the invariant mass obtained from the energy-momentum norm. For a static [Schwarzschild-Tangherlini metric](schwarzschild-tangherlini-metric.md) in $D$ dimensions with $f=1-\mu/r^{D-3}$, the mass is $(D-2)\Omega_{D-2}\mu/(16\pi G_D)$; the dimensional normalization is also reproduced by the [Komar mass normalization in higher dimensions](komar-mass-normalization-in-higher-dimensions.md).
