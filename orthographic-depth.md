# Orthographic depth

↑ **Parent:** [Orthography](orthography.md)

The degree of complexity or inconsistency in mappings between written and spoken forms. Its consequences depend on the units and outcomes assessed; early pronunciation accuracy, fluency and comprehension need not show identical patterns.
