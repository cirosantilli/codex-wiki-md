# Retro hetero-Diels-Alder reaction

↑ **Parent:** [Cycloreversion](cycloreversion.md)

A [retro hetero-Diels-Alder reaction](retro-hetero-diels-alder-reaction.md) is the reverse of a [Diels-Alder reaction](diels-alder-reaction.md) involving a heteroatom-containing partner. For a six-membered ring with sequence $O-A-B=C-D-E$, cleavage of $O-A$ and $D-E$ can give $A=B-C=D$ and $E=O$. Whether the [cycloreversion](cycloreversion.md) is concerted or proceeds through ionic intermediates depends on the substrate and conditions.
