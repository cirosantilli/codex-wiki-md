# Cooling-controlled planetary envelope growth

↑ **Parent:** [Core accretion](core-accretion.md)

Gravitational binding is necessary but not sufficient for [gas](gas.md) capture. A hydrostatic envelope contracts by losing energy on a Kelvin-Helmholtz scale $t_{\rm KH}\sim|E|/L$. Solid accretion heats it, while radiative transport depends on [opacity](opacity.md) and [convection](convection-split.md) carries energy when the radiative gradient is unstable. The disk supply and outer tidal/thermal boundaries also matter.
