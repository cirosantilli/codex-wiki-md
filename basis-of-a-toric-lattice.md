# Basis of a toric lattice

↑ **Parent:** [Lattice in toric geometry](lattice-in-toric-geometry.md)

A lattice basis $v_1,\ldots,v_n$ expresses each vector of the [toric lattice](lattice-in-toric-geometry.md) uniquely as $\sum_i a_iv_i$ with $a_i\in\mathbb Z$. Relative to any fixed integral basis, vectors form another lattice basis exactly when their determinant is $\pm1$. A primitive vector is one whose integer span is saturated; it can be completed to such a basis by the Euclidean algorithm.
