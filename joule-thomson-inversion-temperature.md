# Joule-Thomson inversion temperature

↑ **Parent:** [Joule-Thomson inversion curve](joule-thomson-inversion-curve.md)

A [Joule-Thomson inversion temperature](joule-thomson-inversion-temperature.md) is a [temperature](temperature-split.md) at which the [Joule-Thomson coefficient](joule-thomson-coefficient.md) is zero at a specified [pressure](pressure-split.md). There can be more than one such [temperature](temperature-split.md) at that [pressure](pressure-split.md); distinguish the upper and lower branches from a maximum or a dilute-gas limiting value.
