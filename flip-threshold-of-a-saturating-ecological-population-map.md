# Flip threshold of a saturating ecological population map

↑ **Parent:** [Fixed point stability for an iteration](fixed-point-stability-for-an-iteration.md)

For $\alpha>\beta>0$ the positive [fixed point](fixed-point.md) is $(\alpha-\beta)^{1/\gamma}$ with multiplier $1-\gamma(1-\beta/\alpha)$. It is stable for $1<\gamma<\gamma_*$ and crosses through minus one at the displayed threshold. Since $\gamma_*>2$, every positive [fixed point](fixed-point.md) is stable when $1<\gamma\leq2$. The increasing power controls overcompensatory [density dependence](density-dependence.md) at high abundance. A multiplier crossing identifies the local flip mechanism; a separate nonlinear calculation determines its criticality and any resulting attractor.
