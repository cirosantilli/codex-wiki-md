# On-shell divergence of a quartic scalar dilation current

↑ **Parent:** [Dilatation current](dilatation-current.md)

For $\mathcal L=(\partial\phi)^2/2-\lambda\phi^4$ and the displayed-weight dilation variation $\delta\phi=\phi+x\cdot\partial\phi$, the full divergence identity is $(4-n)\mathcal L+\delta\phi(\Box\phi+4\lambda\phi^3)$. The field equation removes the second term. In four [spacetime](spacetime.md) dimensions the [density](density.md) changes by a total divergence, so the [Noether current](noether-current.md) includes the subtraction $-x^\mu\mathcal L$. This is a classical statement about generic solutions and this particular current.
