# Coalescing random walks

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous-time random walk](continuous-time-random-walk.md)

Particles perform independent [continuous-time random walks](continuous-time-random-walk.md) until they meet, after which they share one path. A finite initial particle set remains finite and its cardinality cannot increase. They arise as the common ancestors of sites in a [voter model](voter-model.md). On the [square lattice](square-lattice.md) every pair eventually coalesces, because the difference walk is [recurrent Markov chain](recurrent-markov-chain.md).
