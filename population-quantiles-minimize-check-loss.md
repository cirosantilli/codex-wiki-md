# Population quantiles minimize check loss

↑ **Parent:** [Check loss](check-loss.md)

For an integrable response with continuous strictly increasing [distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md) $F$, the [population risk](population-risk.md) for the [check loss](check-loss.md) has derivative $F(q)-\tau$. Bounded difference quotients justify differentiation by [dominated convergence](dominated-convergence-theorem.md). Integrating the derivative between the unique [quantile](quantile-function.md) and another location proves strict optimality. This proves [identifiability](identifiability.md) without requiring a positive density.
