# Coleman power series

↑ **Parent:** [Class-field unit sequence](class-field-unit-sequence.md)

A Coleman power series encodes a norm-compatible sequence of local units in a cyclotomic tower by a single integral power series evaluated at $\zeta_{p^n}-1$. A logarithmic derivative and trace correction give a measure, relating the local images of [cyclotomic units](cyclotomic-unit.md) to [p-adic L-functions](p-adic-l-function.md).
