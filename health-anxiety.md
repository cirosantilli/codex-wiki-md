# Health anxiety

↑ **Parent:** [Anxiety](anxiety.md)

Concern about having or acquiring illness. Its intensity, interpretation, persistence and effects on functioning vary; [health anxiety](health-anxiety.md) can coexist with physical illness, and concern alone does not establish a disorder.
