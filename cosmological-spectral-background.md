# Cosmological spectral background

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmology](cosmology-split.md)

The [cosmological spectral background](cosmological-spectral-background.md) is received radiant power per detector area, received [frequency](frequency.md) interval and [solid angle](solid-angle.md), summed over a cosmological source population. Its intensity depends on emitted spectra, their cosmic evolution, the expansion history and absorption. A finite radial [comoving distance](comoving-radial-distance.md) alone does not guarantee a finite background when high-[redshift](redshift.md) sources are sampled at increasingly high emitted [frequencies](frequency.md).
