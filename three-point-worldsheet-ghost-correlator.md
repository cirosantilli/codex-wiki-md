# Three-point worldsheet ghost correlator

↑ **Parent:** [Worldsheet ghost field](worldsheet-ghost-field.md)

On the [Riemann sphere](riemann-sphere.md), three holomorphic reparameterization ghost zero modes correspond to the three infinitesimal [Möbius transformations](mobius-transformation.md). Three $c$ insertions saturate their [Grassmann integral](berezin-integral.md). In a basis $1,z,z^2$, their integral is the Vandermonde determinant, giving $z_{12}z_{13}z_{23}$ up to an ordering-dependent sign. Including the antiholomorphic factor gives $|z_{12}z_{13}z_{23}|^2$, the Jacobian in [sphere gauge fixing for four string vertices](sphere-gauge-fixing-for-four-string-vertices.md).
