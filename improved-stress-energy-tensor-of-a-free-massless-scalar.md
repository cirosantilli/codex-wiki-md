# Improved stress-energy tensor of a free massless scalar

↑ **Parent:** [Stress-energy tensor improvement](stress-energy-tensor-improvement.md)

For $d>1$, the displayed [stress-energy tensor](stress-energy-tensor.md) is symmetric and conserved on the free massless [scalar field](scalar-field.md) equation $\Box\phi=0$. Its improvement is identically divergence-free. Since $\Box\phi^2=2(\partial\phi)^2$ on shell, its trace vanishes. Contracting it with a [Conformal Killing vector field](conformal-killing-vector-field.md) therefore produces a conserved conformal current. In $d=2$ the improvement coefficient is zero: the canonical tensor already has zero trace.
