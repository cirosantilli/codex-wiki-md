# Derjaguin approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Colloid](colloid.md)

For gently curved interacting surfaces whose range and closest gap are small compared with their radii, integrate the local planar interaction over nearly parallel surface elements. With $W(s)$ the planar interaction [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md) per area and $\Pi(s)=-W'(s)$, the local gap for two spheres is $s\simeq h+r^2/(2R_{\rm eff})$, where $R_{\rm eff}^{-1}=a_1^{-1}+a_2^{-1}$. Thus $F=2\pi\int_0^\infty r\Pi(s)dr=2\pi R_{\rm eff}\int_h^\infty\Pi(s)ds=2\pi R_{\rm eff}W(h)$ when $W(\infty)=0$. Equal spheres have $R_{\rm eff}=a/2$; a sphere and plane have $R_{\rm eff}=a$. Use consistent surface boundary conditions when supplying the planar interaction.
