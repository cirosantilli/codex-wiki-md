# Discharge

↑ **Parent:** [Volumetric flow rate](volumetric-flow-rate.md)

River [discharge](discharge.md) is [volume flux](volumetric-flow-rate.md) across a channel section. For approximately uniform cross-sectional [velocity](velocity.md) it equals flow [area](surface-area.md) times mean [velocity](velocity.md). Its time dependence at a fixed station is a [hydrograph](hydrograph.md).
