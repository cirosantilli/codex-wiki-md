# Measurement independence

↑ **Parent:** [Local hidden-variable theory](local-hidden-variable-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Measurement_independence)

Measurement independence says that the distribution of the hidden state is independent of the measurement settings: $\rho(\lambda|a,b)=\rho(\lambda)$. Its failure is the measurement-dependence loophole in a [Bell test](bell-theorem.md).
