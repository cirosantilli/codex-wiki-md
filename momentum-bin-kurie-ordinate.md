# Momentum-bin Kurie ordinate

↑ **Parent:** [Kurie plot](kurie-plot.md)

For counts $N(p)dp$ per [electron](electron.md) [momentum](momentum-split.md) interval, the allowed spectrum is proportional to $p^2F(p)(Q-T)^2dp$ when recoil and [neutrino](neutrino.md) [mass](mass.md) are neglected. Thus the displayed ordinate is linear in [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) under a constant-shape assumption. Counts per kinetic-energy interval instead have the factor $pE_e$ in [natural units](natural-units.md); the Jacobian $dT/dp=p/E_e$ accounts for the difference. Mixing those two binning conventions changes the inferred ordinate.
