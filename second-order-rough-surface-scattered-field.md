# Second-order rough-surface scattered field

↑ **Parent:** [Small-height Dirichlet scattering](small-height-dirichlet-scattering.md)

For normal plane-wave incidence, the second-order [Dirichlet boundary condition](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) gives $\psi_s^{[2]}=-h\partial_z\psi_s^{[1]}$ at the mean plane, since $\partial_z^2\psi^{[0]}$ vanishes there. The [Dirichlet-to-Neumann map for a Helmholtz half-space](dirichlet-to-neumann-map-for-a-helmholtz-half-space.md) then yields $2k h\mathcal B h$. This nonlocal expression is not generally $2k^2h^2$; that local limit needs roughness varying slowly compared with the wavelength.
