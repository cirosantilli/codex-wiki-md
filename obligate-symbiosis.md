# Obligate symbiosis

↑ **Parent:** [Symbiosis](symbiosis.md)

A close biological association on which at least one partner depends for persistence or reproduction under the specified conditions. Obligacy does not itself imply mutual benefit, reciprocity or [coevolution](coevolution.md).
