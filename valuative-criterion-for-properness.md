# Valuative criterion for properness

↑ **Parent:** [Proper morphism](proper-morphism.md)

For a finite-type morphism of [Noetherian schemes](noetherian-scheme.md), properness is equivalent to existence and uniqueness in every lifting problem from the generic point $\operatorname{Spec}K$ of a [valuation ring](valuation-ring.md) $R$ to $\operatorname{Spec}R$.

This is the existence-and-uniqueness part of the [valuative criterion](valuative-criterion.md).
