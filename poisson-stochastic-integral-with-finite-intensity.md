# Poisson stochastic integral with finite intensity

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson random measure](poisson-random-measure.md)

For a [Poisson random measure](poisson-random-measure.md) on $E$ with finite intensity $\nu(E)$, its integral of a finite-valued measurable mark function is the finite sum of the marks at its random atoms. Its [characteristic function](characteristic-function.md) is $\mathbb E\exp(iu\int f\,dN)=\exp(\int(e^{iuf}-1)\,d\nu)$. On a time-mark space with intensity $ds\,\nu(dz)$ and finite mark intensity, restriction to each bounded time interval has finite intensity and defines a [Compound Poisson process](compound-poisson-process.md). Zero-valued marks do not affect the integral and need not appear in its [Lévy measure](levy-measure.md).
