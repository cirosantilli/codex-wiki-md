# Boltzmann encoder demonstration

↑ **Parent:** [Boltzmann machine](boltzmann-machine.md)

[David H. Ackley](david-h-ackley.md), [Geoffrey Hinton](geoffrey-e-hinton.md) and [Terrence J. Sejnowski](terrence-j-sejnowski.md) tested their 1985 algorithm on a simulated 4–2–4 encoder. Two four-unit visible groups communicated through two [hidden units](hidden-unit.md), with no direct connections between the groups. Preparation set weights to zero and supplied corresponding one-active-unit patterns. Noisy clamping and annealed settling generated pair-coactivation readouts, compared with freely running readouts to choose weight changes. Learned hidden codes were the outcome. Equal sampling effort in clamped and free conditions supplied the comparison; noise limited weight growth. Finite settling was assumed to reach equilibrium, limiting the evidence for exact [likelihood](likelihood-function.md) learning.[1985, sec. 4 and sec. 4.1, pp. 157–160, Figs. 2–3; finite settling assumption p. 159](a-learning-algorithm-for-boltzmann-machines-by-david-h-ackley-and-others.md)
