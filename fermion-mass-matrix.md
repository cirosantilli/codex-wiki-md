# Fermion mass matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Yukawa interaction](yukawa-interaction.md)

After a scalar vacuum expectation value, a [Yukawa matrix](yukawa-matrix.md) produces a bilinear $-\bar f_LM_ff_R+\mathrm{h.c.}$. Independent unitary left/right rotations diagonalize $M_f$ with nonnegative masses. Extra scalar-dependent operators can make the physical scalar coupling matrix fail to diagonalize with the mass matrix.
