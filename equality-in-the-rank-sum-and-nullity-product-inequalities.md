# Equality in the rank-sum and nullity-product inequalities

↑ **Parent:** [Rank-nullity theorem](rank-nullity-theorem.md)

For [endomorphisms](endomorphism.md) $S,T$ of a finite-dimensional space of [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) $d$, $r(S+T)\le r(S)+r(T)$ and $n(ST)\le n(S)+n(T)$. If both equalities hold, the first gives $r(S)+r(T)\le d$, while the second gives $2d-r(S)-r(T)\le d$. Thus the [rank](rank-one-quadratic-form.md) sum is $d$, $S+T$ is an [isomorphism](isomorphism.md), and $ST=0$. Conversely, $ST=0$ implies $\operatorname{im}T\subseteq\ker S$ and hence [rank](rank-one-quadratic-form.md) sum at most $d$, whereas invertibility of $S+T$ gives [rank](rank-one-quadratic-form.md) sum at least $d$. This proves both equalities.
