# Gamma decay

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear de-excitation](nuclear-de-excitation.md)

[Gamma decay](gamma-decay.md) is electromagnetic [nuclear de-excitation](nuclear-de-excitation.md) by [photon](photon.md) emission. It preserves [mass number](mass-number.md) and [atomic number](atomic-number.md), while changing the nuclear state. [Angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) and [parity](parity.md) constrain the allowed radiation multipoles. The [gamma ray](gamma-ray.md) topic is broader than this nuclear process.
