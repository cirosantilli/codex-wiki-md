# Galilean velocity addition

↑ **Parent:** [Galilean transformation](galilean-transformation.md)

With parallel inertial axes and absolute time, differentiating a [Galilean transformation](galilean-transformation.md) adds relative [velocities](velocity.md) as ordinary [vectors](vector.md). This is the low-speed limit of [relativistic velocity addition](velocity-addition-formula.md). At speeds comparable with the [speed of light](speed-of-light.md), absolute time is no longer the appropriate frame transformation, and the relativistic denominator cannot be neglected.
