# Galactic Bridges and Tails by Alar Toomre and Juri Toomre

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by Alar Toomre](work-by-alar-toomre.md), [Work by Juri Toomre](work-by-juri-toomre.md)

Original restricted-particle calculations of tidal structures in close [galaxy](galaxy-split.md) encounters. SectionII constructs examples with rotating particle rings and nearly parabolic companion orbits; the model deliberately neglects disc [self-gravity](self-gravity.md). Original paper: [https://articles.adsabs.harvard.edu/pdf/1972ApJ...178..623T](https://articles.adsabs.harvard.edu/pdf/1972ApJ...178..623T) .
