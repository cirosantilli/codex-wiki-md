# Buoyancy production in a particle-laden current

↑ **Parent:** [Particle-laden gravity current](particle-laden-gravity-current.md)

The integrated budget is $\partial_t(g^{\prime} h)+B_x=S_B-v_sg^{\prime}$. A steady spatial increase needs a source $S_B$ exceeding deposition, for example [sediment resuspension](sediment-resuspension.md) or lateral particle input. In unsteady flow, local depletion can produce $B_x>0$ even without particle addition.
