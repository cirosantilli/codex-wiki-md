# Character prime sums in the Chebotarev proof

↑ **Parent:** [Chebotarev density theorem](chebotarev-density-theorem.md)

The logarithm from the [Euler product](euler-product.md) of an [Artin L-function](artin-l-function.md) has first-prime-power term $\sum\chi(g_{\mathfrak p})(N\mathfrak p)^{-s}$. Higher powers are uniformly bounded as real $s\downarrow1$: $|\chi(g)|\leq\chi(1)$ and $\sum_{\mathfrak p}\sum_{m\geq2}(N\mathfrak p)^{-ms}/m\leq2\sum_{\mathfrak p}(N\mathfrak p)^{-2}<\infty$. The finitely many ramified local factors also have bounded logarithms. The [Artin L-function order at one](artin-l-function-order-at-one.md) therefore proves the displayed estimate. Expanding a conjugacy-class indicator by [character orthogonality](character-orthogonality.md) leaves only the trivial character's logarithmic term, with coefficient $|C|/|G|$. This proves [Dirichlet density](dirichlet-density.md) directly; a natural-density assertion needs additional analytic arguments.
