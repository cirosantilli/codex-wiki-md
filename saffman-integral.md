# Saffman integral

↑ **Parent:** [Isotropic turbulence](isotropic-turbulence.md)

The [Saffman integral](saffman-integral.md) is $L=\int\langle\mathbf u(\mathbf x)\cdot\mathbf u(\mathbf x+\mathbf r)\rangle\,d^3r$. With integrable trace correlation it equals the large-volume limit of $\langle|\int_V\mathbf u\,dV|^2\rangle/|V|$. It is nonnegative and measures [momentum](momentum.md)-fluctuation density. Under appropriate unforced far-field flux conditions it is invariant.
