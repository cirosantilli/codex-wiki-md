# Copositive matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copositive_matrix)

A real [symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md) $A$ is copositive if its [quadratic form](quadratic-form.md) satisfies $x^TAx\geq0$ for every $x$ in the [nonnegative orthant](nonnegative-orthant.md). Every [positive semidefinite matrix](positive-semidefinite-matrix.md) and every symmetric [nonnegative matrix](nonnegative-matrix.md) is copositive, as is their sum. The converse fails for the [Horn copositive matrix](horn-copositive-matrix.md).
