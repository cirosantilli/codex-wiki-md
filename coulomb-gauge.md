# Coulomb gauge

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic vector potential](magnetic-vector-potential.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coulomb_gauge)

The Coulomb gauge imposes

$$
\nabla\cdot A=0
$$

on the [magnetic vector potential](magnetic-vector-potential.md). A [gauge transformation](gauge-transformation.md) $A\mapsto A+\nabla\chi$ reaches this gauge when $\chi$ solves

$$
\nabla^2\chi=-\nabla\cdot A.
$$
