# Hartree-Fock-Bogoliubov theory

↑ **Parent:** [Mean-field theory](mean-field-theory.md)

A self-consistent quadratic approximation to an interacting many-particle system that retains both normal one-particle densities and anomalous pair averages. Normal contractions supply Hartree and Fock fields, while anomalous contractions supply the [pair potential](pair-potential.md). A [Bogoliubov transformation](bogoliubov-transformation.md) preserving the appropriate [fermion](fermion.md) anticommutators or [boson](boson.md) commutators diagonalizes the resulting [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md); the densities must then be recomputed to satisfy self-consistency and the particle-number constraint.
