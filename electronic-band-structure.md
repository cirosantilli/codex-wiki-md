# Electronic band structure

↑ **Parent:** [Bloch's theorem](bloch-s-theorem-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_band_structure)

Electronic band structure is the collection of allowed electron energy bands and gaps in a periodic medium, usually represented as energy versus crystal momentum. An [energy band](energy-band.md) is one branch of that structure.
