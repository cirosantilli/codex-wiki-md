# Rising factorial

↑ **Parent:** [Factorial](factorial.md)

For a nonnegative integer $n$, the rising factorial is the product of $n$ consecutive increments starting at $a$, with $(a)_0=1$. Another notation is $a^{\overline n}$; the [falling factorial](falling-factorial.md) uses descending factors. It compactly expresses the coefficient recurrence of a [confluent hypergeometric function of the first kind](confluent-hypergeometric-function-of-the-first-kind.md).
