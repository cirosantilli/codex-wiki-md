# Nuclear particle-hole excitation

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear shell model](nuclear-shell-model.md)

Promoting a nucleon from an occupied orbital to an unoccupied orbital creates a particle-hole configuration. Its possible angular momenta come from coupling the particle and hole, and its [parity](parity.md) relative to the reference configuration is the product of their orbital [parities](parity.md). Residual interactions split and mix configurations; a measured nuclear [excitation energy](excitation-energy.md) is not generally just an uncorrected independent-orbital spacing.
