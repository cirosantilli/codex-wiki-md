# Geometric morphism induced by a functor

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric morphism](geometric-morphism.md)

A functor $F:\mathcal C\to\mathcal D$ induces a [geometric morphism](geometric-morphism.md) from the presheaf topos on $\mathcal C$ to that on $\mathcal D$. Its inverse image is precomposition with $F^{\mathrm{op}}$, its direct image is [Right Kan extension](right-kan-extension.md) and its extra left adjoint is [left Kan extension](left-kan-extension.md). The extra left adjoint sends $yC$ to $y(FC)$.
