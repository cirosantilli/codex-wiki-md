# Tadpole subtraction

↑ **Parent:** [Tadpole diagram](tadpole-diagram.md)

A linear [counterterm](counterterm.md) can be chosen so that the renormalized one-point function, or equivalently the chosen [vacuum expectation value](vacuum-expectation-value.md), is zero. This cancels attached [tadpole diagrams](tadpole-diagram.md) in the perturbative expansion about that vacuum. In a massive [six-dimensional cubic scalar field theory](six-dimensional-cubic-scalar-field-theory.md), the one-loop proper tadpole is $t=gI_1/2$; without subtraction the stationary background shifts by $v=-t/m^2+O(g^3)$ at one-loop order. One must specify this convention when comparing reducible exchange diagrams or mass corrections.
