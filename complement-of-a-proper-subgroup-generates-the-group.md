# Complement of a proper subgroup generates the group

↑ **Parent:** [Generating set of a group](generating-set-of-a-group.md)

For a proper [subgroup](subgroup.md) $H$ of any [group](group-split.md) $G$, its complement is a [generating set of a group](generating-set-of-a-group.md). Choose $x\notin H$. For every $h\in H$, also $xh\notin H$, and $h=x^{-1}(xh)$ lies in the [subgroup](subgroup.md) generated by the complement. That [subgroup](subgroup.md) consequently contains both $H$ and its complement. No finiteness assumption is needed.
