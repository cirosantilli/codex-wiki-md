# Black hole information paradox

↑ **Parent:** [Hawking radiation](hawking-radiation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_hole_information_paradox)

The black hole information paradox is the apparent conflict between unitary quantum evolution and the semiclassical prediction that complete black-hole evaporation leaves only thermal [Hawking radiation](hawking-radiation.md). A resolution must explain how information is preserved or why one of the assumptions behind the conflict fails.
