# Normal variety

↑ **Parent:** [Normal scheme](normal-scheme.md)

An [algebraic variety](algebraic-variety.md) whose [local rings](local-ring.md) are [integrally closed domains](integrally-closed-domain.md). In particular an [irreducible variety](irreducible-variety.md) that is a [normal variety](normal-variety.md) has normal affine [coordinate rings](coordinate-ring.md), enabling [codimension-two extension of regular functions on a normal variety](codimension-two-extension-of-regular-functions-on-a-normal-variety.md).
