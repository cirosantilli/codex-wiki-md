# Inclusion body

↑ **Parent:** [Protein folding](protein-folding.md)

An [inclusion body](inclusion-body.md) in recombinant protein production is an insoluble aggregate-rich fraction containing the expressed material. Abundance does not demonstrate native folding; any recovered refolded product needs independent identity, assembly and activity checks.
