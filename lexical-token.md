# Lexical token

↑ **Parent:** [Lexical analysis](lexical-analysis.md)

A categorized piece of program text, such as a [program identifier](program-identifier.md), a numeral denoting a [literal value](literal-value.md), or an operator symbol. Its spelling and category help a [parser](parser.md) recognize the larger construct.
