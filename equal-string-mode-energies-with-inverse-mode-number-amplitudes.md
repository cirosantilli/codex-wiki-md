# Equal string-mode energies with inverse-mode-number amplitudes

↑ **Parent:** [Modal energy of a string](modal-energy-of-a-string.md)

For a uniform fixed-end [mechanical string](mechanical-string.md) of length $l$ and [tension](tension.md) $T$, a single sine [normal mode](normal-mode.md) of amplitude $A_n$ has conserved [energy](energy-split.md) $E_n=n^2\pi^2TA_n^2/(4l)$. Hence amplitudes proportional to $1/n$ give equal energies, even though their displacement magnitudes differ. In particular $A_2=A_1/2$ makes the first two modal energies equal. Orthogonality removes cross terms in the integrated [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) and [potential energy](potential-energy.md), so the modal energies add.
