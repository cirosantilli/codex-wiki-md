# Schur complement formula for a diagonal resolvent entry

↑ **Parent:** [Schur complement](schur-complement.md)

For a [Hermitian matrix](hermitian-operator.md), let $x_i$ be its $i$th column without the diagonal entry and let $G^{(i)}=(X^{(i)}-zI)^{-1}$. Solve the two block equations of the inverse against the $i$th coordinate vector to obtain this formula. Both full and minor inverses must exist; nonreal $z$ guarantees that. In the real symmetric case $x_i^*$ may be replaced by $x_i^T$.
