# Noise operator on the Boolean hypercube

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier-Walsh transform](fourier-walsh-transform.md)

The noise operator averages $f(y)$ over a random $y$ correlated with $x$ by $\rho$. It acts diagonally on the [Fourier-Walsh transform](fourier-walsh-transform.md):

$$
T_\rho f=\sum_S\rho^{|S|}\widehat f(S)\chi_S.
$$
