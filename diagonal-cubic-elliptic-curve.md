# Diagonal cubic elliptic curve

↑ **Parent:** [Elliptic curve](elliptic-curve.md)

For $d\in\mathbb Q^\times$, the [smooth projective curve](smooth-projective-curve.md) $X^3+Y^3+dZ^3=0$, with identity $O=(1:-1:0)$, is a genus-one [elliptic curve](elliptic-curve.md). Its partial derivatives have no common projective zero. The invertible linear [projective transformation](projective-linear-transformation.md)

$$
(\mathcal X:\mathcal Y:\mathcal Z)=(-12dZ:-36d(X-Y):X+Y)
$$

sends it to $\mathcal Y^2\mathcal Z=\mathcal X^3-432d^2\mathcal Z^3$, taking $O$ to $(0:1:0)$. Indeed $(X+Y)^3+3(X+Y)(X-Y)^2=4(X^3+Y^3)=-4dZ^3$. In affine coordinates $u=X/Z,v=Y/Z$, this gives $x=-12d/(u+v)$ and $y=-36d(u-v)/(u+v)$, with inverse $u=(y-36d)/(6x)$, $v=(-y-36d)/(6x)$. Thus diagonal sums of two cubes are naturally studied through [elliptic-curve addition formula](elliptic-curve-addition-formula.md).
