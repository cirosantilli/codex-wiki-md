# Constant-intersection family bound

↑ **Parent:** [Extremal set theory](extremal-set-theory-split.md)

If distinct members of a [set family](set-family.md) on $[n]$ have intersection size $\lambda$, then its size is at most $n$ for $\lambda>0$, and at most $n+1$ for $\lambda=0$, assuming $n\geq1$. In the positive case, use the [Gram matrix](gram-matrix.md) of the [characteristic vectors of sets](characteristic-vector-of-a-set.md); a member of size $\lambda$ is handled by disjoint remainders. In the zero case nonempty members are disjoint.
