# Protein secondary structure

↑ **Parent:** [Protein structure](protein-structure.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protein_secondary_structure)

[Protein secondary structure](protein-secondary-structure.md) consists of recurring local arrangements of the [protein backbone](protein-backbone.md), notably the [alpha helix](alpha-helix.md) and [beta sheet](beta-sheet.md). [Backbone hydrogen bonds](backbone-hydrogen-bond.md) help stabilize these patterns. A [beta sheet](beta-sheet.md) can bring together stretches far apart in the [protein primary structure](protein-primary-structure.md); local refers to the pattern of backbone geometry, not necessarily neighboring sequence positions. See [Alberts et al.: alpha helices, beta sheets and backbone interactions](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK26830/).
