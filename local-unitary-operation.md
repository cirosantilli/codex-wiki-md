# Local unitary operation

↑ **Parent:** [Local operations and classical communication](local-operations-and-classical-communication.md)

An operation that applies a [unitary operator](unitary-operator.md) to one party's subsystem and the identity to the other is a local unitary operation. Several separated parties may each apply a known unitary, resulting in a product of their local unitaries. Such operations preserve the squared [Schmidt coefficients](schmidt-coefficient.md) across the relevant bipartition.
