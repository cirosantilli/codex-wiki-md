# Isotropic etch

↑ **Parent:** [Etching](etching.md)

An etch that removes a material in different directions without a strongly preferred geometrical direction. It can undercut an overhang and release a [sacrificial layer](sacrificial-layer.md), but can also widen a patterned opening. Access and transport can make the realized undercut nonuniform even with locally isotropic chemistry.
