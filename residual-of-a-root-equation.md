# Residual of a root equation

↑ **Parent:** [Root finding](root-finding.md)

The [residual of a root equation](residual-of-a-root-equation.md) $f(x)=0$ at a proposed solution $\widehat x$ is the value $f(\widehat x)$. It measures violation of the equation, while the forward error $|\widehat x-x_*|$ measures distance from a chosen exact [root](root-of-a-root-system.md). Rescaling $f$ rescales the [residual of a root equation](residual-of-a-root-equation.md) without moving its [roots](root-of-a-root-system.md), so a small residual needs a scale or a [derivative](derivative.md) bound before it certifies a small forward error.
