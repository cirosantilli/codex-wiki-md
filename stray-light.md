# Stray light

↑ **Parent:** [Optical instrument](optical-instrument-split.md)

[Stray light](stray-light.md) reaches a [photodetector](photodetector.md) by an unintended path or from outside the intended [wavelength](wavelength.md) band. It can bias [spectrophotometry](spectrophotometry.md) by adding received [radiant power](radiant-flux.md).
