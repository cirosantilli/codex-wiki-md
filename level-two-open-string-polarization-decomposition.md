# Level-two open-string polarization decomposition

↑ **Parent:** [Covariant quantization of the bosonic string](covariant-quantization-of-the-bosonic-string.md)

For $d=26$, $q^2=-2$ and the [physical string state](physical-string-state.md) $[\zeta_{\mu\nu}\alpha_{-1}^\mu\alpha_{-1}^\nu+b_\mu\alpha_{-2}^\mu]|p\rangle$, the [Virasoro constraints](virasoro-constraint.md) give $b_\nu=-q^\mu\zeta_{\mu\nu}$ and $t=\zeta^\mu{}_{\mu}=-2q\cdot b$. Every solution has the displayed decomposition, where $q\cdot A=0$ and $c$ is symmetric, traceless and transverse to $q$. The vector piece is $L_{-1}(2A\cdot\alpha_{-1}|p\rangle)$; the scalar piece is $t(L_{-2}+3L_{-1}^2/2)|p\rangle/10$. These are [null string states](null-string-state.md), leaving the transverse traceless massive tensor as the physical polarization.
