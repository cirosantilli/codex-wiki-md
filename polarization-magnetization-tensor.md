# Polarization-magnetization tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Relativistic electromagnetism](relativistic-electromagnetism-split.md)

An [antisymmetric tensor](antisymmetric-tensor.md) combines the [electric polarization](polarization-density.md) and [magnetization](magnetization.md) of a medium. For [Minkowski metric](minkowski-metric.md) $(-,+,+,+)$ and units with light speed one, choose $M^{i0}=P_i$ and $M^{ij}=\epsilon_{ijk}M_k$, together with $F_{i0}=E_i$ and $F_{ij}=\epsilon_{ijk}B_k$. Then the [Lorentz scalar](lorentz-scalar.md) interaction is $M^{\mu\nu}F_{\mu\nu}/2=\mathbf P\cdot\mathbf E+\mathbf M\cdot\mathbf B$. The [bound charge and bound current](bound-charge-and-bound-current.md) have four-current $j_b^\mu=\partial_\nu M^{\mu\nu}$, giving $\rho_b=-\nabla\cdot\mathbf P$ and $\mathbf J_b=\partial_t\mathbf P+\nabla\times\mathbf M$. This current is identically conserved because partial derivatives commute and $M$ is antisymmetric.
