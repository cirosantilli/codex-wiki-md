# Intermolecular force

↑ **Parent:** [Force](force-split.md)

A force between [molecules](molecule-split.md), including effects of [electrostatics](electrostatics-split.md), [polarizability](electric-polarizability.md) and [London dispersion forces](london-dispersion-force.md). [Hydrogen bonds](hydrogen-bond.md) describe a directional interaction pattern rather than an additional fundamental force to add independently to all those components.
