# Decay of a Fourier mode in a third-order spatial evolution equation

↑ **Parent:** [Separation of variables](separation-of-variables.md)

A separated [Fourier mode](fourier-mode.md) with initial value $e^{iky}$ at $x=0$ obeys $X'=-k^3X/2$ and $X(0)=1$. Its amplitude decays for $x\to+\infty$ precisely when $k>0$. The zero mode stays constant and negative modes grow. Thus a decay requirement on a half-space imposes a sign condition on the real wave number; a formal separated expression alone does not establish compatibility of both [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md).
