# Macromolecule

↑ **Parent:** [Molecule](molecule-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macromolecule)

A [molecule](molecule-split.md) with a high [relative molecular mass](relative-molecular-mass.md), typically built from many smaller units joined by [covalent bonds](covalent-bond-split.md). [Proteins](protein-split.md) and [nucleic acids](nucleic-acid.md) are examples. In [dialysis](dialysis-biochemistry.md), a [semipermeable membrane](semipermeable-membrane.md) can retain these large [molecules](molecule-split.md) while smaller [solutes](solute.md) diffuse through.
