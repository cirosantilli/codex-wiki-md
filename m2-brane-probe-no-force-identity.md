# M2-brane probe no-force identity

↑ **Parent:** [M2-brane supergravity solution](m2-brane-supergravity-solution.md)

For $F=\operatorname{vol}_{012}\wedge dH^{-1}$ and $F=dA$, choose $A=(1-H^{-1})\operatorname{vol}_{012}$. A parallel probe then has $q=-1$ in the [supermembrane action](supermembrane-action.md), so its induced-volume term $-T_2H^{-1}$ and potential term $-T_2(1-H^{-1})$ add to a position-independent constant. This proves the cancellation without a weak-field approximation. An oppositely charged probe has no such cancellation.
