<h1 id="6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

The selfish-invasion hypothesis concerns the origin and spread of [introns](../../../../intron.md), whereas current function concerns the consequences of their presence today. A sequence can begin by improving its own transmission, later become dependent on host machinery, and subsequently acquire a host-beneficial function. Conversely, present-day persistence need not mean that every [intron](../../../../intron.md) was retained because it helps the organism.

[Group II introns](../../../../group-ii-intron.md) provide a mechanistic model for mobile, self-splicing ancestors of spliceosomal [introns](../../../../intron.md). Their structural and catalytic similarities to the [spliceosome](../../../../spliceosome.md) support a historical relationship, although the detailed route and timing of [eukaryotic](../../../../eukaryote.md) [intron](../../../../intron.md) origins remain reconstructed hypotheses. In ordinary spliceosomal [introns](../../../../intron.md), many activities needed for [RNA](../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) processing are supplied in trans by a shared cellular machinery rather than by a complete mobile apparatus encoded in each [intron](../../../../intron.md). This dependence limits autonomy but does not demonstrate that all selfish proliferation has ended.

[Purifying selection](../../../../purifying-selection.md) constrains the burden. Insertions that disrupt coding information, essential regulation or reliable splicing can reduce host fitness. [Intron](../../../../intron.md) loss can remove transcriptional, processing and mutational costs, and selection preserves splice signals needed to produce functional [RNA](../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md). The balance depends on [effective population size](../../../../effective-population-size.md), [mutation](../../../../mutation.md) and loss rates, [gene](../../../../gene.md) expression and the effect of a particular insertion. Weakly deleterious [introns](../../../../intron.md) can persist when drift overwhelms selection; a large genome-wide [intron](../../../../intron.md) count is therefore insufficient evidence for either universal adaptation or unrestricted selfish spread.

The host has also harnessed intronic sequence and the processing step. [Alternative splicing](../../../../alternative-splicing.md) can connect one [gene](../../../../gene.md) to several regulated transcripts, while [exon shuffling](../../../../exon-shuffling.md) can rearrange [protein](../../../../protein-split.md) modules. [Introns](../../../../intron.md) can contain enhancers, [microRNAs](../../../../microrna.md) and [small nucleolar RNAs](../../../../small-nucleolar-rna.md); splicing can be coupled to [RNA export](../../../../rna-export.md) and surveillance. Some alternative events regulate transcript abundance through [nonsense-mediated decay](../../../../nonsense-mediated-decay.md) rather than producing a stable alternative [protein](../../../../protein-split.md). These examples establish possible functions, not that every alternative isoform or every intronic base has been selected for such a function.

Specific experiments demonstrate functions that an origin story alone cannot predict. [Julie Parenteau](../../../../julie-parenteau.md) and colleagues found that deleting [introns](../../../../intron.md) from budding-yeast [genes](../../../../gene.md) often impaired performance during nutrient depletion and connected [intron](../../../../intron.md) presence with control of growth-related expression. [Jeffrey T. Morgan](../../../../jeffrey-t-morgan.md) and colleagues identified excised [introns](../../../../intron.md) that become stable linear RNAs under particular growth-inhibitory conditions and contribute to growth regulation. These are evidence for regulated intron-derived activity in defined conditions, not a license to regard all excised [introns](../../../../intron.md) as functional.[Intron-deletion phenotypes during nutrient depletion](../../../../introns-are-mediators-of-cell-response-to-starvation-by-julie-parenteau-and-others.md)[Stabilized excised introns and growth control](../../../../excised-linear-introns-regulate-growth-in-yeast-by-jeffrey-t-morgan-and-others.md)

To distinguish function from a correlated consequence, an [intron](../../../../intron.md) deletion must be assessed for effects on expression, local [DNA](../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) regulation and splicing, with rescue or appropriate sequence controls. Comparative conservation, turnover and experimentally measured fitness costs help determine whether host-level selection maintains a given feature. The extent of containment is thus substantial but incomplete and lineage dependent: shared processing and host selection constrain the elements, while retained [introns](../../../../intron.md) span functional, tolerated and potentially deleterious cases.
