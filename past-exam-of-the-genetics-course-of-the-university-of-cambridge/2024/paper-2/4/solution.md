<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

**[Bicoid](../../../../bicoid.md) and [Nanos](../../../../nanos.md) are opposed maternal inputs with different principal molecular outputs: [Bicoid](../../../../bicoid.md) directly helps activate anterior zygotic [transcription](../../../../transcription-biology.md), whereas [Nanos](../../../../nanos.md) prevents inappropriate posterior translation of maternal [hunchback messenger RNA](../../../../hunchback-messenger-rna.md).** They cooperate with, rather than replace, other maternal and zygotic regulators. It is misleading to describe them as two otherwise equivalent [transcription](../../../../transcription-biology.md)-factor gradients.

The first shared feature is spatial control of a maternally produced transcript. During [oogenesis](../../../../oogenesis.md), [bicoid messenger RNA](../../../../bicoid-messenger-rna.md) is supplied from the maternal [germline](../../../../germline.md) and concentrated at the anterior of the [oocyte](../../../../oocyte.md) through cis-acting localization information and transport/anchoring machinery. Its translation after egg activation establishes an anterior source of [Bicoid](../../../../bicoid.md) [protein](../../../../protein-split.md). Specific [antibody](../../../../antibody.md) detection showed nuclear accumulation and an approximately declining anterior-to-posterior distribution, with [mutant](../../../../mutant.md) [embryos](../../../../embryo.md) providing specificity controls.[1988, pp. 83–93, protein detection, mutant controls and spatial distribution](../../../../a-gradient-of-bicoid-protein-in-drosophila-embryos-by-wolfgang-driever-and-christiane-nusslein-volhard.md) Nuclear localization is functionally relevant because [Bicoid](../../../../bicoid.md) is a [homeodomain](../../../../homeodomain.md) [DNA-binding protein](../../../../dna-binding-protein.md), acting on [enhancers](../../../../enhancer.md) in the [syncytial blastoderm](../../../../syncytial-blastoderm.md) before [cellularization](../../../../cellularization.md).

[Nanos](../../../../nanos.md) is also maternally supplied, but its effective source is posterior. [Oskar](../../../../oskar.md)-organized [germ plasm](../../../../germ-plasm.md) provides a posterior localization and translational environment for [nanos messenger RNA](../../../../nanos-messenger-rna.md). [RNA](../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) retained elsewhere is normally translationally repressed, preventing a broadly distributed transcript population from producing an inappropriate [embryo](../../../../embryo.md)-wide [Nanos](../../../../nanos.md) signal. Posterior localization should not be reduced to directed transport of every molecule: live imaging of endogenous [nanos messenger RNA](../../../../nanos-messenger-rna.md) demonstrated [diffusion](../../../../diffusion.md) and entrapment as an important mechanism.[2003, live-RNA imaging and posterior trapping](../../../../live-imaging-of-endogenous-rna-reveals-a-diffusion-and-entrapment-mechanism-for-nanos-mrna-localization-in-drosophila-by-kevin-m-forrest-and-elizabeth-r-gavis.md) Thus transcript localization, local translation and repression outside the source together determine where [Nanos](../../../../nanos.md) [protein](../../../../protein-split.md) is produced. [Bicoid](../../../../bicoid.md) and [Nanos](../../../../nanos.md) gradients are [protein](../../../../protein-split.md)/activity distributions, not claims that the entire maternal [RNA](../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) pool is itself a matching exponential gradient.

**Their main readouts occur at different regulatory levels.** [Bicoid](../../../../bicoid.md) binds [enhancer](../../../../enhancer.md) [DNA](../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) and contributes [concentration](../../../../concentration.md)-dependent activation of anterior targets, including zygotic [hunchback gene](../../../../hunchback-gene.md) expression. Different target responses depend on binding-site organization, other [transcription factors](../../../../transcription-factor.md), [chromatin accessibility](../../../../chromatin-accessibility.md) and time available for [transcription](../../../../transcription-biology.md). A nucleus does not merely read one [Bicoid](../../../../bicoid.md) [concentration](../../../../concentration.md) in isolation: maternal [Hunchback](../../../../hunchback.md), gap-[gene](../../../../gene.md) interactions and temporal integration sharpen or modify its response. [Bicoid](../../../../bicoid.md) also represses translation of [caudal messenger RNA](../../../../caudal-messenger-rna.md) in the anterior by recognizing [RNA](../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) regulatory information, helping generate an opposing [Caudal](../../../../caudal.md) [protein](../../../../protein-split.md) distribution from broadly available maternal [RNA](../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md). Reporter and [RNA](../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md)-binding experiments establish this second, post-transcriptional role of a [homeodomain](../../../../homeodomain.md) [protein](../../../../protein-split.md).[1996, pp. 694–699, caudal RNA recognition and translation-initiation assays; publisher-linked corrigendum](../../../../rna-recognition-and-translational-regulation-by-a-homeodomain-protein-by-josh-dubnau-and-gary-struhl.md)

[Nanos](../../../../nanos.md) is a [zinc finger](../../../../zinc-finger.md) [RNA-binding protein](../../../../rna-binding-protein.md) rather than a sequence-specific transcriptional activator of posterior [gap genes](../../../../gap-gene.md). In cooperation with [Pumilio](../../../../pumilio.md) and other repression machinery, it acts on [Nanos response elements](../../../../nanos-response-element.md) in the [Hunchback](../../../../hunchback.md) [3′ untranslated region](../../../../3%E2%80%B2-untranslated-region.md), reducing maternal [Hunchback](../../../../hunchback.md) production posteriorly. Repression can involve inhibition of translation and regulated [RNA](../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) stability; measuring [protein](../../../../protein-split.md) alone would not distinguish their contributions. Response-element [mutation](../../../../mutation.md) and transfer into reporters test whether regulation depends on a particular [RNA](../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) sequence rather than on posterior position alone. Such experiments showed that the quality and number of response elements influence [Nanos](../../../../nanos.md)-dependent regulation.[1991, pp. 955–967, necessary/sufficient response elements and concentration-dependent reporter regulation](../../../../rna-regulatory-elements-mediate-control-of-drosophila-body-pattern-by-the-posterior-morphogen-nanos-by-robin-p-wharton-and-gary-struhl.md)

The distinction between maternal and zygotic [Hunchback](../../../../hunchback.md) explains how the two inputs fit together. Maternal [hunchback messenger RNA](../../../../hunchback-messenger-rna.md) is broadly present, but posterior [Nanos](../../../../nanos.md) prevents it from giving broadly high [Hunchback](../../../../hunchback.md) [protein](../../../../protein-split.md). Anteriorly, where [Nanos](../../../../nanos.md) is low, maternal [Hunchback](../../../../hunchback.md) remains available and [Bicoid](../../../../bicoid.md) activates additional zygotic [hunchback gene](../../../../hunchback-gene.md) [transcription](../../../../transcription-biology.md). Posteriorly, suppression of maternal [Hunchback](../../../../hunchback.md) removes an inhibitory influence on abdominal gap-[gene](../../../../gene.md) programmes, while [Caudal](../../../../caudal.md) and other inputs support posterior patterning. [Bicoid](../../../../bicoid.md)'s repression of anterior [Caudal](../../../../caudal.md) is complementary: an anterior transcriptional activator also helps keep a posterior-promoting [protein](../../../../protein-split.md) out of the wrong region. These interactions build distinct regulatory environments at the two ends, rather than assigning each end with one independent positive instruction.

An idealized [concentration](../../../../concentration.md) model makes the difference between a gradient and its readout explicit. With a localized [Bicoid](../../../../bicoid.md) source, uniform [diffusion](../../../../diffusion.md) coefficient $D$ and first-order loss $k$, a one-dimensional steady-state approximation away from the source gives

$$
DB^{\prime\prime}-kB=0,\qquad B(x)=B_0e^{-x/\lambda},\qquad\lambda=\sqrt{D/k}.
$$

A hypothetical target responding at threshold $B_*$ has boundary $x_*=\lambda\log(B_0/B_*)$. If the source amplitude doubles without changing the other parameters, the boundary shifts by $\lambda\log2$. This is a model prediction, not a fitted measurement from the exam. Source-distribution changes, nuclear trapping, [protein](../../../../protein-split.md) lifetime, non-steady dynamics and [enhancer](../../../../enhancer.md) regulation can invalidate its simplest assumptions. The expression makes clear why source dosage can move a boundary without changing the identity of the target [enhancer](../../../../enhancer.md). In the classic tests, genetic changes in [Bicoid](../../../../bicoid.md) source amount or distribution altered the [protein](../../../../protein-split.md) gradient and displaced anterior fate-map positions, supporting an instructive [concentration](../../../../concentration.md)-dependent role.[1988, pp. 95–104, source manipulation and fate-map shifts](../../../../the-bicoid-protein-determines-position-in-the-drosophila-embryo-in-a-concentration-dependent-manner-by-wolfgang-driever-and-christiane-nusslein-volhard.md)

[Nanos](../../../../nanos.md) positional activity can instead be read through the fraction of [Hunchback](../../../../hunchback.md) transcripts repressed at a given position. Its effect depends on [protein](../../../../protein-split.md) [concentration](../../../../concentration.md), [RNA](../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md)-element affinity and abundance of its partners. Calling it a [morphogen](../../../../morphogen.md) is compatible with an indirect transcriptional outcome: its graded activity is translated first into an opposing [Hunchback](../../../../hunchback.md) distribution, which then influences zygotic [transcription](../../../../transcription-biology.md). That does not imply that [Nanos](../../../../nanos.md) supplies the same number of independently interpreted [concentration](../../../../concentration.md) thresholds as [Bicoid](../../../../bicoid.md), or that it alone specifies every abdominal identity.

**Both inputs are necessary in the normal regulatory context because they correct different problems.** Without maternal [Bicoid](../../../../bicoid.md), normal anterior zygotic activation and anterior repression of [Caudal](../../../../caudal.md) fail, causing major anterior-pattern defects. Without [Nanos](../../../../nanos.md), maternal [Hunchback](../../../../hunchback.md) persists posteriorly and interferes with abdominal development despite the presence of [Bicoid](../../../../bicoid.md) anteriorly. The decisive genetic test is that reducing maternal [Hunchback](../../../../hunchback.md) can suppress the posterior-pattern defect of [Nanos](../../../../nanos.md) [mutants](../../../../mutant.md): this places much of [Nanos](../../../../nanos.md)'s abdominal function in removal of an inhibitor rather than direct activation of every posterior target.[1989, pp. 646–648, maternal-hunchback genetic interaction](../../../../the-drosophila-posterior-group-gene-nanos-functions-by-repressing-hunchback-activity-by-vivian-irish-and-others.md) Such suppression does not restore every [Nanos](../../../../nanos.md)-dependent function, particularly its separate roles in germ-[cell](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) development.

A further discriminating design would compare localized wild-type [RNAs](../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) with localization-defective but equally expressed versions; use separate [enhancer](../../../../enhancer.md) and 3′-UTR reporters; quantify target [RNA](../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) and [protein](../../../../protein-split.md) simultaneously; and test target-site [mutants](../../../../mutant.md). Ectopic posterior [Bicoid](../../../../bicoid.md) should impose inappropriate anterior-like regulatory activity where competent targets remain, while ectopic anterior [Nanos](../../../../nanos.md) should repress its susceptible maternal targets rather than operate as a conventional [DNA](../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md)-binding anterior repressor. Appropriate controls include [RNA](../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) abundance, [protein](../../../../protein-split.md) stability, maternal [genotype](../../../../genotype.md) and developmental stage. **Their complementarity is therefore an anterior transcriptional programme reinforced by selective [translational repression](../../../../translational-repression.md) at both ends, with [Nanos](../../../../nanos.md) additionally protecting a posterior environment in which abdominal patterning can proceed.**
