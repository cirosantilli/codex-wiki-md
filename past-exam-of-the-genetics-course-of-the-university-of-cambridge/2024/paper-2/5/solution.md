↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

**[gastrulation](../../../../gastrulation.md) is coordinated at tissue scale but implemented through changes in individual [cells](../../../../biological-cell-split.md): motility, adhesion, polarity and shape. “Individual” and “collective” describe different aspects of the mechanism, not mutually exclusive types of [embryo](../../../../embryo.md).** A [cell](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) can move relative to its neighbours while transmitting force or receiving directional information through those same neighbours. Conversely, nearby [cells](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) can share a trajectory because they are passively carried by a moving tissue.

The principal outcomes are internalization and rearrangement of germ-layer progenitors, establishment of their relative positions and elongation or spreading of the embryonic axis. [Ingression](../../../../ingression.md) internalizes individual [cells](../../../../biological-cell-split.md), whereas [invagination](../../../../invagination.md) bends a connected epithelial sheet; involution rolls tissue across a margin. [Convergent extension](../../../../convergent-extension.md) changes tissue proportions, while [epiboly](../../../../epiboly.md) spreads a tissue over a larger surface. These geometrical descriptions should be distinguished from the force-generating mechanisms. For example, two [embryos](../../../../embryo.md) can both extend but use different mixtures of oriented intercalation, migration, shape change and growth.

**Individual motility is especially apparent after [cells](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) leave an [epithelium](../../../../epithelium.md).** In mouse, [epiblast](../../../../epiblast.md) [cells](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) approach the [primitive streak](../../../../primitive-streak.md), reorganize junctions, cross a locally permissive [basement membrane](../../../../basement-membrane.md) and undergo an [epithelial-to-mesenchymal transition](../../../../epithelial-to-mesenchymal-transition.md). Once internalized, mesodermal [cells](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) migrate between [epiblast](../../../../epiblast.md) and [endoderm](../../../../endoderm.md). Protrusions, cytoskeletal force production and transient attachment to neighbouring [cells](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) or [extracellular matrix](../../../../extracellular-matrix.md) produce movement. This allows descendants to disperse to distinct destinations and respond to local signals. Downregulation of stable epithelial adhesion can facilitate departure, but productive migration still requires regulated interactions: complete loss of coupling can prevent traction or integration.

[FGFR1](../../../../fgfr1.md) illustrates the connection between regulatory state and [cell](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) behaviour. Its perturbation affects mesodermal [gene expression](../../../../gene-expression.md) as well as [Snail](../../../../snail.md)/[E-cadherin](../../../../e-cadherin.md) regulation and movement from the mouse streak.[2001, pp. 37–49, separate differentiation and epithelial-to-mesenchymal-transition readouts](../../../../fgf-signaling-regulates-mesoderm-cell-fate-specification-and-morphogenetic-movement-at-the-primitive-streak-by-brian-ciruna-and-janet-rossant.md) Therefore accumulation of [cells](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) at a streak cannot automatically be interpreted as a pure motility defect. I would determine whether they acquired the expected identity, whether they detached from the [epithelium](../../../../epithelium.md), and whether viable detached [cells](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) can migrate. A normal motility [protein](../../../../protein-split.md) does not restore [gastrulation](../../../../gastrulation.md) if the progenitor population was never specified.

**Collective [invagination](../../../../invagination.md) coordinates local contractions through a mechanically continuous sheet.** In the [Drosophila](../../../../drosophila.md) ventral furrow, mesodermal [cells](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) constrict their apical surfaces and become wedge-shaped, bending the [epithelium](../../../../epithelium.md) inward. Apical [actomyosin](../../../../actomyosin.md) contraction is transmitted to neighbours through [adherens junctions](../../../../adherens-junction.md); surrounding tissue and basal [cell](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) properties constrain the resulting deformation. Live imaging showed pulses of medial apical [actin](../../../../actin.md)–[myosin](../../../../myosin.md) contraction rather than a universally continuous circumferential purse string. Stabilization between pulses produces a ratchet-like reduction in apical area; [Snail](../../../../snail.md) and [Twist](../../../../twist.md) perturbations separate contraction initiation from stabilization.[2009, Figures 1–4, live myosin dynamics, apical-area changes and transcription-factor perturbations](../../../../pulsed-contractions-of-an-actin-myosin-network-drive-apical-constriction-by-adam-c-martin-and-others.md) Asynchronous pulses in individual [cells](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) can thus contribute to a coherent tissue fold. Collective behaviour does not require every [cell](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) to contract simultaneously, and a [cell](../../../../biological-cell-split.md)'s change in shape need not equal its net translational displacement.

**Collective extension often requires [cells](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) to exchange neighbours rather than preserve one fixed group arrangement.** During Xenopus dorsal [mesoderm](../../../../mesoderm.md) convergence and extension, [cells](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) polarize, make biased protrusions and intercalate mediolaterally. The tissue narrows across its width and extends along the body axis without requiring a corresponding increase in [cell](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) number or total volume. Coordinated [planar cell polarity](../../../../planar-cell-polarity.md) signalling coordinates this orientation. [Dishevelled](../../../../dishevelled.md) perturbation disrupts polarized behaviour and extension even when the relevant [cell fates](../../../../cell-fate.md) remain, supporting a role in [morphogenesis](../../../../morphogenesis.md) separable from specification.[2000, pp. 81–85, time-lapse imaging, polarity-defective perturbations and fate controls](../../../../dishevelled-controls-cell-polarity-during-xenopus-gastrulation-by-john-b-wallingford-and-others.md) Individual [cells](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) are active force-generators, but their useful orientations and contact rearrangements depend on tissue organization. Merely increasing [cell](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) speed would not necessarily restore directional intercalation.

A kinematic check illustrates the distinction from growth. If a roughly rectangular tissue of constant thickness and area changes from width $W$ and length $L$ to $W/2$ and $2L$, it has undergone strong convergence and extension while preserving $WL$. Actual gastrulating tissues can also change thickness, proliferate and exchange [cells](../../../../biological-cell-split.md), so this is an illustrative conservation argument rather than an assertion of strict area conservation. Quantifying all three dimensions and [cell](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) number is required before attributing axis elongation entirely to intercalation.

The [zebrafish](../../../../zebrafish.md) [prechordal plate](../../../../prechordal-plate.md) provides a particularly clear experiment distinguishing individual movement from collective guidance. Isolated prechordal [cells](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) remain polarized and motile but lose their normal directional migration toward the [animal](../../../../animal.md) pole. Directionality returns when they contact the endogenous plate, with [E-cadherin](../../../../e-cadherin.md), Wnt/planar-[cell](../../../../biological-cell-split.md)-polarity components and [Rac1](../../../../rac1.md) implicated in the response. Even an isolated group is not automatically oriented correctly.[2012, pp. 16945–16950, isolated-cell/group transplantation and contact-dependent orientation](../../../../collective-mesendoderm-migration-relies-on-an-intrinsic-directionality-signal-transmitted-through-cell-contacts-by-julien-g-dumortier-and-others.md) Thus collective movement is more than the sum of many [cells](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) independently sensing the same external attractant. Nor does the experiment prove that the frontmost [cell](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) alone is a permanently designated leader. The informative observation is retention of motility but loss and restoration of directional information.

**[epiboly](../../../../epiboly.md) demonstrates how an adjacent tissue can supply a substantial driving force.** In [zebrafish](../../../../zebrafish.md), the superficial [enveloping layer](../../../../enveloping-layer.md) spreads over the yolk while deeper populations rearrange. Here, [radial intercalation](../../../../radial-intercalation.md) helps thin and spread deep tissue. Contractile [actomyosin](../../../../actomyosin.md) in the [yolk syncytial layer](../../../../yolk-syncytial-layer.md) contributes pulling at the epithelial margin. Force and flow analysis showed that retrograde [actomyosin](../../../../actomyosin.md) flow can generate pulling through resistance, in addition to circumferential contraction; spreading can proceed when circumferential constriction alone would be unproductive.[2012, pp. 257–260, actomyosin flow, mechanical analysis and epithelial spreading](../../../../forces-driving-epithelial-spreading-in-zebrafish-gastrulation-by-martin-behrndt-and-others.md) Observing a [cell](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) move vegetally therefore does not establish that its own crawling supplies the principal force. Collective [morphogenesis](../../../../morphogenesis.md) can couple embryonic and extraembryonic compartments.

The movement classes are related developmentally. A coherent [epithelium](../../../../epithelium.md) can first bend, then release [cells](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) that migrate individually, which can later establish new collective arrangements. Partial retention of contacts can permit a mesenchymal population to remain coordinated. Such [cell](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) rearrangement changes exposure to inducing signals and the mechanical environment, so movement can influence fate as well as execute a prior fate decision. The comparison should therefore ask where force originates, how it is transmitted, what gives direction and which contacts persist, rather than assign one universal movement mode to [gastrulation](../../../../gastrulation.md).

I would test these alternatives with simultaneous nuclear, membrane, junctional and [actomyosin](../../../../actomyosin.md) imaging. Trajectories should be measured relative to neighbouring [cells](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) and matrix landmarks to subtract [tissue advection](../../../../tissue-advection.md). Speed, directional persistence, neighbour exchange, [cell](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) shape and spatial velocity correlations answer different questions; a common external pull can correlate velocities without contact-mediated guidance. Sparse mosaics distinguish a [cell](../../../../biological-cell-split.md)-intrinsic motility defect from a surrounding-tissue defect. Isolated-[cell](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) versus group transplants test whether direction requires neighbours. Acute, local perturbation after fate specification, followed by rescue, reduces the ambiguity of early patterning [mutants](../../../../mutant.md). Local [laser](../../../../laser-split.md) severing and recoil can reveal prestress, but recoil also depends on dissipation and is not an absolute force measurement without a mechanical model. Matching viability, proliferation, adhesion and differentiation controls is essential. **The strongest explanation connects an individual behaviour to a measurable tissue deformation and shows, by perturbation, which interactions make that behaviour productive.**
