<h1 id="2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

The first task is to map the [DNA](../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) associated with the centromeric [histone](../../../../histone.md) variant [CENP-A](../../../../cenp-a.md); the second is to determine how that occupancy is maintained. These require different evidence. A shared [DNA](../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) sequence is not sufficient evidence for an inherited centromeric state.

Use validated anti-[CENP-A](../../../../cenp-a.md) [chromatin immunoprecipitation sequencing](../../../../chromatin-immunoprecipitation-sequencing.md) with input [DNA](../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) and an appropriate negative immunoprecipitation control. Check antibody recognition separately in human, horse and chicken; an endogenous epitope-tagged allele can provide an orthogonal control if its chromosome-segregation function is retained. Native digestion can recover CENP-A-containing [nucleosomes](../../../../nucleosome.md), whereas crosslinking can retain indirect associations. [CUT&RUN](../../../../cut-and-run.md) provides a complementary occupancy assay with different recovery biases. Establish centromeric function by colocalization with [kinetochore](../../../../kinetochore.md) [proteins](../../../../protein-split.md) and by normal [chromosome segregation](../../../../chromosome-segregation.md), not merely by calling a sequencing peak.

Mapping repetitive [DNA](../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) is the main analytical obstacle. Use centromere-resolved assemblies where possible, retain information from multimapping reads, and analyse satellite-family enrichment as well as uniquely mappable sequence. Absence of a peak in an incomplete reference is not evidence of absent [CENP-A](../../../../cenp-a.md). Long-read sequence assemblies, informative [single-nucleotide polymorphisms](../../../../single-nucleotide-polymorphism.md), and [DNA fluorescence in situ hybridization](../../../../dna-fluorescence-in-situ-hybridization.md) can connect a recovered sequence to a particular [chromosome](../../../../chromosome.md) and distinguish homologues.

For precise positioning, compare enriched domains with input-normalized background at several resolutions. Map nucleosome-protected fragments and use fibre immunofluorescence or sequence probes to check domain extent and heterogeneity. A broad population peak may represent alternative positions in different cells or homologues, rather than one broad [CENP-A](../../../../cenp-a.md) domain on every [chromosome](../../../../chromosome.md).

The expected outcome is **a common centromeric function without a universal centromeric [DNA](../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) sequence**. Human [CENP-A](../../../../cenp-a.md) commonly occupies selected portions of alpha-satellite arrays, but human [neocentromeres](../../../../neocentromere.md) show that such arrays are not obligatory. Horse [chromosome](../../../../chromosome.md) 11 provides a normal satellite-free [centromere](../../../../centromere.md). Chicken [chromosomes](../../../../chromosome.md) include both repeat-rich [centromeres](../../../../centromere.md) and comparatively short non-tandem-repetitive centromeric regions.[Comparative chicken centromere mapping](../../../../chickens-possess-centromeres-with-both-extended-tandem-repeats-and-short-non-tandem-repetitive-sequences-by-wei-hao-shang-and-others.md)[Satellite-free mammalian centromeres and inherited positional variation](../../../../birth-evolution-and-transmission-of-satellite-free-mammalian-centromeric-domains-by-solomon-g-nergadze-and-others.md)

To test mitotic stability, start from single-cell clones and remap [CENP-A](../../../../cenp-a.md) on the same haplotype after successive divisions, while checking that the underlying [DNA](../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) has not changed. Pulse labelling of old versus newly incorporated [CENP-A](../../../../cenp-a.md) can distinguish retention from replenishment. Follow both [chromosome](../../../../chromosome.md) identity and domain position: stable centromeric function does not require an invariant nucleotide boundary. To test meiotic transmission, use informative pedigrees or gamete-and-offspring material and allele-resolved mapping, with matched cell types where possible. An inherited positional state, or bounded movement of that state on unchanged [DNA](../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md), supports [epigenetic centromere inheritance](../../../../epigenetic-centromere-inheritance.md). Artificial [centromere](../../../../centromere.md) deletion and neocentromere recovery provide an additional perturbation test, but establishment after deletion is different from ordinary meiotic inheritance.[Chicken neocentromere formation and CENP-A domain mapping](../../../../chromosome-engineering-allows-the-efficient-isolation-of-vertebrate-neocentromeres-by-wei-hao-shang-and-others.md)
