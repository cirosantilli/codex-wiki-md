[Genomic instability](../../../../genomic-instability.md) means an elevated propensity to acquire genetic alterations. It includes changes in nucleotide sequence, repeat length, [chromosome](../../../../chromosome.md) structure and [chromosome](../../../../chromosome.md) number. A genome carrying one fixed alteration is not, by that fact alone, genomically unstable: instability concerns the process that generates further alterations.

At the sequence level, [DNA replication](../../../../dna-replication.md) introduces mismatches and small insertion-deletion errors. Polymerase proofreading and [DNA mismatch repair](../../../../dna-mismatch-repair.md) normally suppress their fixation. Failure of mismatch repair is particularly evident as [microsatellite instability](../../../../microsatellite-instability.md). Damaged bases, stalled replication forks and collisions with [transcription](../../../../transcription-biology.md) can increase [replication stress](../../../../replication-stress.md); unrepaired or improperly repaired lesions can produce substitutions and rearrangements. [Homologous recombination](../../../../homologous-recombination.md) and [non-homologous end joining](../../../../non-homologous-end-joining.md) solve different repair problems, and inappropriate use or incomplete repair can alter genome structure.

At the [chromosome](../../../../chromosome.md) level, failed chromosome-end protection can allow end fusion. Subsequent separation of fused [chromosomes](../../../../chromosome.md) can initiate [breakage-fusion-bridge cycles](../../../../breakage-fusion-bridge-cycle.md), producing repeated rearrangement and copy-number change. Faulty [kinetochore](../../../../kinetochore.md) attachment, weakened [sister chromatid cohesion](../../../../sister-chromatid-cohesion.md) or abnormal spindle organization can cause [chromosome](../../../../chromosome.md) missegregation and [aneuploidy](../../../../aneuploidy.md). The resulting imbalances may themselves disturb cell physiology and [DNA replication](../../../../dna-replication.md). [DNA](../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) confined in an aberrant micronucleus can experience defective replication and fragmentation; repair of clustered fragments is one route to [chromothripsis](../../../../chromothripsis.md), rather than every rearrangement arising through that mechanism.[Live-cell tracking linked to sequencing of descendants](../../../../chromothripsis-from-dna-damage-in-micronuclei-by-cheng-zhong-zhang-and-others.md)

The experimental signatures distinguish mechanisms. [Mutation](../../../../mutation.md) spectra and mismatch-repair status address sequence errors; repeat-length distributions address [microsatellite instability](../../../../microsatellite-instability.md); [karyotyping](../../../../karyotyping.md), copy-number profiles and live [chromosome](../../../../chromosome.md) imaging address numerical or structural [chromosome](../../../../chromosome.md) instability. Single-cell or lineage-resolved measurements can show ongoing production of new states. A mixed endpoint population alone cannot establish whether variation arose rapidly during the experiment or was selected from pre-existing diversity.

In [cancer](../../../../cancer.md), instability can generate selectable combinations that promote growth, escape from controls or resistance to treatment. It is neither automatically beneficial to the tumour nor sufficient for malignant transformation: excessive instability can impair proliferation or trigger [apoptosis](../../../../apoptosis.md) and [cellular senescence](../../../../cellular-senescence.md). Examples of mechanistic connections include mismatch-repair deficiency in [Lynch syndrome](../../../../lynch-syndrome.md), homologous-recombination defects involving [BRCA1](../../../../brca1.md) or [BRCA2](../../../../brca2.md), and inherited replication or repair disorders. [Chromosome](../../../../chromosome.md) missegregation can also contribute to developmental disease, pregnancy loss and infertility; instability in long-lived somatic tissues can impair function without producing a tumour.

A useful causal analysis asks what fails first, what alterations it produces, and which cellular responses restrain or select the affected cells. Restoring a repair factor or correcting a segregation defect should reduce the appropriate new-error rate, not merely change which clone grows best. The same reasoning explains why a genomic-instability [phenotype](../../../../phenotype.md) can be informative in disease while remaining insufficient to diagnose a particular condition from morphology alone.
