<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

Both checkpoints couple cell-cycle timing to spatial organization, but they act at different transitions. The budding-yeast [spindle position checkpoint](../../../../spindle-position-checkpoint.md) prevents premature mitotic exit when the anaphase spindle has not been appropriately placed across the mother-bud axis. The fly [centrosome orientation checkpoint](../../../../centrosome-orientation-checkpoint.md) delays entry into mitosis until interphase [centrosomes](../../../../centrosome.md) have the orientation needed for a properly oriented division.

In [Saccharomyces cerevisiae](../../../../saccharomyces-cerevisiae.md), one product of division must enter the bud. If both spindle poles and [chromosome](../../../../chromosome.md) masses remain in the mother, cytokinesis would generate the wrong nuclear distribution. The [mitotic exit network](../../../../mitotic-exit-network.md) promotes sustained activation of [Cdc14](../../../../cdc14.md) and the decline of mitotic CDK activity. Its upstream [GTPase](../../../../gtpase.md) [Tem1](../../../../tem1.md) is restrained by the [Bub2-Bfa1 complex](../../../../bub2-bfa1-complex.md). When the spindle is mispositioned, [Kin4](../../../../kin4.md) helps preserve that inhibition and opposes the checkpoint-inactivating action of [Cdc5](../../../../cdc5.md) on Bfa1. Thus spindle mispositioning is connected to continued mitotic arrest rather than simply to an attempt to pull harder on [chromosomes](../../../../chromosome.md).[Kin4-dependent inhibition of mitotic exit](../../../../the-protein-kinase-kin4-inhibits-exit-from-mitosis-in-response-to-spindle-position-defects-by-katharine-e-d-aquino-and-others.md)

Spatial regulation makes this pathway sensitive to which compartment a spindle pole occupies. [Kin4](../../../../kin4.md) activity is associated with the mother-cell side, whereas the bud environment favours release of the inhibitory state when a pole enters appropriately. [Elm1](../../../../elm1.md) contributes to activation of [Kin4](../../../../kin4.md). This is a regulated network involving localization and phosphorylation, rather than an infallible single switch at the bud neck. It is also distinct from the [spindle checkpoint](../../../../spindle-checkpoint.md), which monitors [kinetochore](../../../../kinetochore.md) attachment and restrains anaphase; a spindle can have attached [chromosomes](../../../../chromosome.md) yet be in the wrong position.[Kin4 activation and regulation of Bub2-Bfa1](../../../../elm1-kinase-activates-the-spindle-position-checkpoint-kinase-kin4-by-ayse-koca-caydasi-and-others.md)

In male [Drosophila melanogaster](../../../../drosophila-melanogaster.md) [germline stem cells](../../../../germline-stem-cell.md), contact with the hub defines the [stem-cell niche](../../../../stem-cell-niche.md). Normally one [centrosome](../../../../centrosome.md) is retained toward the hub and the other moves away, preparing a division in which one daughter retains niche contact. Live imaging showed that misoriented [centrosomes](../../../../centrosome.md) can delay division until orientation is restored, including in aged or dedifferentiated cells. Counting misoriented interphase [centrosomes](../../../../centrosome.md) alone therefore overestimates the frequency of misoriented mitoses.[Interphase misorientation, delayed cycling and reorientation before mitosis](../../../../centrosome-misorientation-reduces-stem-cell-division-during-aging-by-jun-cheng-and-others.md)

The fly checkpoint involves [Centrosomin](../../../../centrosomin.md), [Par-1](../../../../par-1.md), and regulation of [cyclin A](../../../../cyclin-a.md) localization. [Par-1](../../../../par-1.md) and the germline [spectrosome](../../../../spectrosome.md) help restrain the mitotic programme; perturbing this organization can uncouple orientation from mitotic entry. [Bazooka](../../../../bazooka.md) provides a cortical platform associated with [centrosome](../../../../centrosome.md) anchoring. These observations support spatial control of mitotic competence, not identity of the molecular pathway with yeast [Tem1](../../../../tem1.md) signalling.[Par-1, spectrosome-associated cyclin A and checkpoint defects](../../../../regulation-of-cyclin-a-localization-downstream-of-par-1-function-is-critical-for-the-centrosome-orientation-checkpoint-in-drosophila-male-germline-stem-cells-by-hebao-yuan-and-others.md)[Baz-centrosome association and Par-1-dependent regulation](../../../../the-polarity-protein-baz-forms-a-platform-for-the-centrosome-orientation-during-asymmetric-stem-cell-division-in-the-drosophila-male-germline-by-mayu-inaba-and-others.md)

A decisive experimental comparison measures both position and transition timing in live cells. A pure orientation defect raises misorientation and prolongs the relevant pause; loss of the checkpoint permits the transition despite misorientation. Rescue with an appropriate wild-type component helps establish specificity. In yeast, score mitotic exit and nuclear inheritance; in fly [stem cells](../../../../stem-cell.md), score entry into mitosis and daughter contact with the niche. This separates the mechanism that constructs the correct geometry from the mechanism that waits for it.
