The structural implication is a spectrum of arrangements, not a universal regular fibre. Local [nucleosome](../../../../../nucleosome.md) packing, irregular accessible chains, compact domains and interactions between differently modified regions can coexist, and their balance depends on preparation and cellular context. [Nucleosome breathing](../../../../../nucleosome-breathing.md) changes the sites available for intermolecular contacts and hence the connectivity of an array assembly; it is not merely a uniform change in the size of a rigid bead.[Comparison of breathing and artificially constrained nucleosomes](../../../../../nucleosome-plasticity-is-a-critical-element-of-chromatin-liquid-liquid-phase-separation-and-multivalent-nucleosome-interactions-by-stephen-e-farr-and-others.md)

Reconstituted-array experiments separate contributions that are difficult to disentangle in nuclei. Fluorescent arrays, microscopy, droplet fusion and molecular exchange measurements test condensation and dynamics; changing tails, linker organization or acetylation tests the interaction mechanism. [Bryan A. Gibson](../../../../../bryan-a-gibson.md) and colleagues found that [histone H1](../../../../../histone-h1.md) and [nucleosome](../../../../../nucleosome.md) spacing modulate condensation, while acetylation and reader [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md) can produce different condensed states.[Reconstituted arrays and regulation of condensate properties](../../../../../organization-of-chromatin-by-intrinsic-and-regulated-phase-separation-by-bryan-a-gibson-and-others.md)

In cells, combine imaging with contact maps and accessibility assays. [Hi-C](../../../../../hi-c.md) can reveal preferential contacts and [chromatin compartments](../../../../../chromatin-compartment.md), but does not establish liquid behaviour. [Fluorescence recovery after photobleaching](../../../../../fluorescence-recovery-after-photobleaching.md) measures recovery kinetics, but recovery alone does not prove equilibrium liquid-liquid demixing. Conversely, an in-vitro droplet is not proof that every nuclear domain forms by the same mechanism. The strongest case combines material behaviour with controlled molecular perturbations and measurements of the resulting organization.
