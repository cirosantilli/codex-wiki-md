[Chromatin](../../../../../chromatin.md) is a composite of charged [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md), folded [histone](../../../../../histone.md) cores, flexible [histone](../../../../../histone.md) tails and associated [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md). These parts differ in charge, hydrophobicity, flexibility, interaction partners and chemical modifications. It is therefore not a chemically uniform chain with one identical contact energy at every position.

Diversity also occurs along a [chromosome](../../../../../chromosome.md): [nucleosome](../../../../../nucleosome.md) occupancy, [linker DNA](../../../../../linker-dna.md) length, [histone variants](../../../../../histone-variant.md), [histone acetylation](../../../../../histone-acetylation.md) and other [histone modifications](../../../../../histone-modification.md) change the local interaction pattern. Positively charged tails can contact [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) or neighbouring [nucleosome](../../../../../nucleosome.md) surfaces; an acetylated lysine has different electrostatic properties and can recruit a modification-reading [protein](../../../../../protein-split.md). The number, arrangement and lifetime of contacts matter as well as their average strength. A coarse-grained interaction parameter can summarize one preparation but should not be mistaken for a universal chemical constant of all [chromatin](../../../../../chromatin.md).
