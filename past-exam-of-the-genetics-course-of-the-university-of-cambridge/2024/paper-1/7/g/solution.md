<h1 id="7/g/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [G](../g.md)

Physicochemical diversity provides ways to regulate the same [DNA](../../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) without changing its sequence. [Histone modifications](../../../../../histone-modification.md), variants and recruited [proteins](../../../../../protein-split.md) can alter local contacts, accessibility and the partitioning of regulatory factors. This can couple [chromatin organization](../../../../../chromatin-organization.md) to [transcription](../../../../../transcription-biology.md), [DNA replication](../../../../../dna-replication.md) and [DNA repair](../../../../../dna-repair.md), while allowing distinct genomic regions to behave differently in one nucleus.

For example, changing [histone acetylation](../../../../../histone-acetylation.md) can weaken some intrinsic contacts while favouring binding of specific acetylation readers. A local state can therefore change both how strongly a region associates with itself and which partners it recruits. Reversible, multivalent interactions allow organization to respond to signals without requiring an irreversible solid assembly.

These mechanisms also allow memory: feedback between a chromatin-associated mark, its reader and modifying machinery can help restore a local state after replication. Such [epigenetic inheritance](../../../../../epigenetic-inheritance.md) still needs evidence for maintenance and re-establishment; a stable-looking condensed region alone does not establish it. Likewise, correlations between compaction and reduced [transcription](../../../../../transcription-biology.md) do not prove that condensation is always the cause of repression. Perturbations must separate altered material properties from direct effects on transcriptional machinery.
