↑ **Parent:** [Paper 2](paper-2.md)

<h3 id="1/solution">Solution</h3>

↑ **Parent:** [1](#1)

**Both animals extend their posterior axis by coordinated [biological tissue](../../biology.md#tissue-biology) production and rearrangement, but the balance between these processes differs markedly.** In the [house mouse](../../organism.md#house-mouse), sustained expansion of posterior [progenitor cells](../../biological-cell.md#progenitor-cell) contributes substantially to new [biological tissue](../../biology.md#tissue-biology); in the [zebrafish](../../organism.md#zebrafish), much early elongation redistributes an existing population, with additional growth occurring in already formed axial [biological tissues](../../biology.md#tissue-biology). Calling both posterior regions a growth zone can therefore conceal the mechanism that actually increases length.

During [gastrulation](../../biology.md#gastrulation), the [house mouse](../../organism.md#house-mouse) supplies posterior [biological tissues](../../biology.md#tissue-biology) through the [primitive streak](../../biology.md#primitive-streak); later the [tailbud](../../biology.md#tailbud) maintains posterior contributions. [neuromesodermal progenitors](../../biological-cell.md#neuromesodermal-progenitor) near the node–streak border and caudal lateral [epiblast](../../biology.md#epiblast) contribute to [spinal cord](../../neuroscience.md#spinal-cord) and [paraxial mesoderm](../../biology.md#paraxial-mesoderm). Their characteristic combination of [Sox2](../../molecular-genetics.md#sox2) and [Brachyury](../../molecular-genetics.md#brachyury) reflects an unresolved neural–mesodermal regulatory state. [Wnt signalling](../../signal-transduction.md#wnt-signalling) and [FGF signalling](../../signal-transduction.md#fgf-signalling) help maintain posterior identity and [progenitor cells](../../biological-cell.md#progenitor-cell), while increased mesodermal commitment involves [Tbx6](../../molecular-genetics.md#tbx6). Neural differentiation involves resolution toward the [Sox2](../../molecular-genetics.md#sox2) programme. A shared marker combination is evidence about [cell state](../../biological-cell.md#cell-state); the stronger claim that a particular [biological cell](../../biological-cell.md) is bipotent requires [clonal lineage tracing](../../biological-cell.md#clonal-lineage-tracing) showing descendants in both [biological tissues](../../biology.md#tissue-biology). Retrospective clonal analysis in the [house mouse](../../organism.md#house-mouse) provided evidence for persistent common neural–mesodermal progenitors during axial formation.[Developmental Cell 17, pp. 365–376; retrospective clonal analysis of neural and mesodermal descendants](../../biology-bibliography.md#redefining-the-progression-of-lineage-segregations-during-mammalian-embryogenesis-by-clonal-analysis-by-elena-tzouanacou-and-others) Sustained [cell proliferation](../../biological-cell.md#cell-proliferation) supplies material as descendants leave the posterior pool, but the pool is heterogeneous and does not provide every posterior [biological tissue](../../biology.md#tissue-biology).

In the [zebrafish](../../organism.md#zebrafish), [convergent extension](../../biology.md#convergent-extension) during [gastrulation](../../biology.md#gastrulation) narrows the forming body and lengthens it through directed movements and [cell intercalation](../../biological-cell.md#cell-intercalation). Posterior [biological tissue](../../biology.md#tissue-biology) continues to receive [biological cells](../../biological-cell.md) from lateral regions, and rearrangement as [biological cells](../../biological-cell.md) leave the [tailbud](../../biology.md#tailbud) lengthens the axis without requiring equivalent local volume production. The [notochord](../../biology.md#notochord), [neural tube](../../biology.md#neural-tube) and flanking [paraxial mesoderm](../../biology.md#paraxial-mesoderm) are mechanically coupled; they cannot be understood as independent rods. At later stages, growth within the [spinal cord](../../neuroscience.md#spinal-cord) and [notochord](../../biology.md#notochord) also contributes. Direct comparative measurements found that the unsegmented posterior region of the [zebrafish](../../organism.md#zebrafish) does not itself generate the extra volume predicted by a simple posterior-growth model; inhibiting [FGF signalling](../../signal-transduction.md#fgf-signalling) impaired [biological tissue](../../biology.md#tissue-biology) convergence rather than posterior volume growth.[Abstract; Development 143, pp. 1732–1741: tissue influx, convergence–extension, late axial-tissue growth and FGF inhibition](../../biology-bibliography.md#species-specific-contribution-of-volumetric-growth-and-tissue-convergence-to-posterior-body-elongation-in-vertebrates-by-ben-steventon-and-others)

The distinction is made precise by an illustrative geometric identity. Approximate a defined [biological tissue](../../biology.md#tissue-biology) region by length $L$, mean cross-sectional area $A$ and volume $V=AL$. Then

$$
\frac{\dot L}{L}=\frac{\dot V}{V}-\frac{\dot A}{A}.
$$

Thus elongation can result from increasing volume, decreasing cross-sectional area, or both. At constant volume, a 20% reduction of area gives $L_{\mathrm{new}}/L_{\mathrm{old}}=1/0.8=1.25$, a 25% length increase. This is a constructed example, not an embryonic measurement. An anatomically defined region is an open system: [cell migration](../../biological-cell.md#cell-migration) across its boundary changes its volume even without growth of its resident [biological cells](../../biological-cell.md). For such a region, separate local volume production from inflow and outflow before interpreting $\dot V$. Likewise, [cell proliferation](../../biological-cell.md#cell-proliferation) increases [biological cell](../../biological-cell.md) number but is not itself a measurement of tissue-volume growth.

The two animals nevertheless share posterior [biological cells](../../biological-cell.md) with neuromesodermal competence. [clonal lineage tracing](../../biological-cell.md#clonal-lineage-tracing) and whole-embryo [light-sheet fluorescence microscopy](../../fluorescence.md#light-sheet-fluorescence-microscopy) in the [zebrafish](../../organism.md#zebrafish) identified early common neural–mesodermal ancestry and a later posterior population with delayed [biological tissue](../../biology.md#tissue-biology) allocation. Tracking the latter population revealed predominantly single-fate contributions over the observed interval rather than the sustained bifated self-renewing behaviour often inferred from the [house mouse](../../organism.md#house-mouse). **Conserved competence need not imply conserved realised lineage behaviour.** A clone with both fates can reflect an earlier common ancestor; conversely, a single-fate clone sampled late does not prove that its ancestor was never competent for both.[Abstract and Results: genetic clone tracing, early versus late allocation, and retrospective assignment of tailbud cell fates](../../biology-bibliography.md#neuromesodermal-progenitors-are-a-conserved-source-of-spinal-cord-with-divergent-growth-dynamics-by-andrea-attardi-and-others)

A useful direct comparison would combine [clonal lineage tracing](../../biological-cell.md#clonal-lineage-tracing), live nuclear and membrane labels, three-dimensional [biological tissue](../../biology.md#tissue-biology) segmentation, [DNA synthesis labeling with EdU](../../cell-cycle.md#dna-synthesis-labeling-with-edu) and measurements of [biological cell](../../biological-cell.md) volume and [apoptosis](../../biological-cell.md#apoptosis). Follow material landmarks as well as fixed anatomical compartments, so [biological tissue](../../biology.md#tissue-biology) influx is distinguishable from expansion. Quantify axial length, cross-sectional area, total [biological tissue](../../biology.md#tissue-biology) volume, [biological cell](../../biological-cell.md) number and displacement fields at matched developmental landmarks, such as [somite](../../biology.md#somite) number. Match imaging depth and mounting constraints and include untreated imaged and non-imaged controls, because confinement or [phototoxicity](../../fluorescence.md#phototoxicity) can alter movements. Short, stage-specific inhibition of [cell proliferation](../../biological-cell.md#cell-proliferation) and independent perturbation of [convergent extension](../../biology.md#convergent-extension) would ask which process is necessary during each interval; verify the intended perturbation and exclude [biological cell](../../biological-cell.md) death or broad developmental arrest. A shortened [embryo](../../biology.md#embryo) after a pathway inhibitor alone does not distinguish loss of [biological tissue](../../biology.md#tissue-biology) identity, impaired movement and reduced growth.

The most informative conclusion is therefore mechanistic: the [house mouse](../../organism.md#house-mouse) couples posterior [progenitor cell](../../biological-cell.md#progenitor-cell) maintenance and substantial growth to elongation, whereas the rapidly developing [zebrafish](../../organism.md#zebrafish) makes particularly extensive use of convergence, rearrangement and delayed allocation, followed by growth of axial [biological tissues](../../biology.md#tissue-biology). Both must coordinate this supply and movement with [somitogenesis](../../biology.md#somitogenesis), so equal final body architecture can emerge through different cellular dynamics.

<h3 id="2/solution">Solution</h3>

↑ **Parent:** [2](#2)

**The joint eggshell and embryonic phenotype makes defective communication between the oocyte and follicle [biological cells](../../biological-cell.md) a strong hypothesis, but it does not identify the mutated [gene](../../genetics.md#gene).** In [Drosophila melanogaster](../../organism.md#drosophila-melanogaster), an eggshell is produced by maternal [follicle cells](../../biology.md#follicle-cell), whereas the [embryo](../../biology.md#embryo) interprets maternal positional information later. The experimental aim is to determine inheritance, the [biological tissue](../../biology.md#tissue-biology) requiring the wild-type product, the first abnormal molecular event, and the causal sequence connecting it to ventralisation.

First establish a reproducible [phenotype](../../genetics.md#phenotype). Backcross the stock to control its [genetic background](../../genetics.md#genetic-background); score independent mothers and several egg collections at matched temperature and maternal age. Image dorsal appendage number, position and fusion, and examine embryonic [insect cuticle](../../biology.md#insect-cuticle) and [biological tissue](../../biology.md#tissue-biology) markers. Record unhatched and fertilised eggs separately. In [embryos](../../biology.md#embryo), quantify the spatial extent of [Twist](../../molecular-genetics.md#twist) and [Snail](../../molecular-genetics.md#snail) expression, the distribution of nuclear [Dorsal protein](../../molecular-genetics.md#dorsal-protein), and dorsal [Decapentaplegic](../../signal-transduction.md#decapentaplegic) expression. Expanded ventral molecular identities, rather than lethality or abnormal shape alone, establish embryonic ventralisation. Appendage defects alone are insufficient because their formation has additional regulatory requirements.

Perform reciprocal crosses between mutant and wild-type flies and score both the mothers' eggs and the offspring [genotypes](../../genetics.md#genotype). For a recessive [maternal-effect mutation](../../genetics.md#maternal-effect-mutation), homozygous mutant mothers produce affected eggs and [embryos](../../biology.md#embryo) even when the father is wild type; heterozygous mothers can produce normally patterned [embryos](../../biology.md#embryo) that are themselves homozygous mutant. A [zygotic-effect mutation](../../genetics.md#zygotic-effect-mutation) instead tracks the [embryo](../../biology.md#embryo)'s [genotype](../../genetics.md#genotype). Test [dominance](../../genetics.md#dominance-genetics) with heterozygous mothers, score [penetrance](../../genetics.md#penetrance) and severity, and distinguish maternal rescue from a paternal contribution by following the next generation. Do not impose a simple Mendelian ratio if the lesion is sex-linked, semidominant or affects fertility.

Map the [mutation](../../genetics.md#mutation) using [genetic linkage](../../genetics.md#genetic-linkage), informative [genetic markers](../../genetics.md#genetic-marker) and deficiencies, followed by [DNA sequencing](../../molecular-genetics.md#dna-sequencing). [complementation testing](../../genetics.md#complementation-testing) against known candidates can distinguish a new [allele](../../genetics.md#allele) of an established [genetic locus](../../genetics.md#genetic-locus) from a different locus, although failure to complement needs confirmation because interacting loci and deletions can complicate interpretation. Compare the mutant over a deficiency with the homozygote, and test [gene](../../genetics.md#gene) dosage to distinguish reduced function from a dominant activating or interfering effect. The decisive causal package is rescue by a suitably expressed wild-type genomic copy, recreation of the candidate sequence change in a clean background, and reversal by precise repair. Check that rescue restores both eggshell and embryonic pattern, rather than merely viability.

The normal information flow supplies a series of discriminating readouts. Localised [Gurken](../../protein.md#gurken) from the dorsal-anterior [oocyte](../../biology.md#oocyte) activates the [epidermal growth factor receptor](../../protein.md#epidermal-growth-factor-receptor) in neighbouring dorsal [follicle cells](../../biology.md#follicle-cell). This represses [Pipe](../../protein.md#pipe) there, leaving its activity ventrally restricted.[Cell 95, pp. 471–481; follicle-cell pipe expression and experimental repatterning of embryonic polarity](../../biology-bibliography.md#spatially-restricted-expression-of-pipe-in-the-drosophila-egg-chamber-defines-embryonic-dorsal-ventral-polarity-by-jonaki-sen-and-others) The modified extracellular environment biases a [proteolytic cascade](../../protein.md#proteolytic-cascade) that activates [Spätzle](../../protein.md#spatzle); this [ligand](../../protein.md#ligand-biochemistry) activates [Toll receptor](../../protein.md#toll-receptor) ventrally. Loss of the inhibitory action of [Cactus protein](../../protein.md#cactus-protein) allows high ventral nuclear [Dorsal protein](../../molecular-genetics.md#dorsal-protein), which activates ventral targets and represses dorsal ones. Therefore loss of the early dorsalising input can produce expanded ventral identity, whereas ordinary loss of the downstream [Toll receptor](../../protein.md#toll-receptor) pathway produces dorsalisation. [genetic mosaics](../../genetics.md#genetic-mosaic) established the distinct ovarian requirements for [Gurken](../../protein.md#gurken) and the [epidermal growth factor receptor](../../protein.md#epidermal-growth-factor-receptor).[Abstract; Cell 49, pp. 699–707: germline mosaics distinguish gurken and torpedo requirements](../../biology-bibliography.md#germ-line-and-soma-cooperate-during-oogenesis-to-establish-the-dorsoventral-pattern-of-egg-shell-and-embryo-in-drosophila-melanogaster-by-trudi-schupbach)

Use [genetic mosaics](../../genetics.md#genetic-mosaic) to make mutant germ [biological cells](../../biological-cell.md) within wild-type ovarian soma, and mutant [follicle cell](../../biology.md#follicle-cell) clones around wild-type germ [biological cells](../../biological-cell.md). Mark clones independently and verify their size and location. A requirement in the maternal germline supports defects in [Gurken](../../protein.md#gurken) production, localisation or secretion; a requirement in dorsal [follicle cells](../../biology.md#follicle-cell) supports reception or interpretation of that signal. Include wild-type clones generated by the same procedure and tissue-specific wild-type rescue. If both compartments contribute, test whether the locus has two functions rather than forcing it into one category.

Then localise the first defect during [oogenesis](../../biology.md#oogenesis). Determine the [oocyte](../../biology.md#oocyte) nuclear position and [Gurken](../../protein.md#gurken) [messenger RNA](../../polymer.md#messenger-rna) and [protein](../../protein.md) distributions; measure receptor-pathway activity by phosphorylated [ERK](../../signal-transduction.md#erk) staining and map [Pipe](../../protein.md#pipe) expression. Correct [Gurken](../../protein.md#gurken) localisation with absent dorsal receptor output favours reception or downstream transduction. Reduced localised [Gurken](../../protein.md#gurken) with an otherwise responsive receptor pathway favours defective [ligand](../../protein.md#ligand-biochemistry) production or positioning. Expanded [Pipe](../../protein.md#pipe) despite normal dorsal receptor activity points further downstream. Use sense-probe or probe-omission controls where appropriate, validated [antibodies](../../protein.md#antibody), common staining batches and known pathway mutants. Temporal staging matters: a late normal-looking pattern cannot exclude an earlier transient failure.

Apply [epistasis](../../genetics.md#epistasis) and bypass tests with explicit predictions. If the lesion acts upstream of receptor activation, activated receptor in [follicle cells](../../biology.md#follicle-cell) should restore dorsal pathway output and repress ectopic [Pipe](../../protein.md#pipe), provided downstream machinery is intact. If ectopic [Pipe](../../protein.md#pipe) causes embryonic ventralisation, removing [Pipe](../../protein.md#pipe) should suppress that ventralisation and usually yield the downstream loss-of-function dorsalised state; it need not repair the eggshell. Similarly, remove [Toll receptor](../../protein.md#toll-receptor) or maternal [Dorsal protein](../../molecular-genetics.md#dorsal-protein) function to test whether embryonic ventralisation requires the normal ventral effector pathway. Persistence after a verified downstream block demands an alternative mechanism or multiple lesions. Conversely, an activated [Toll receptor](../../protein.md#toll-receptor) allele bypasses many upstream inputs and can ventralise an [embryo](../../biology.md#embryo) while leaving the earlier eggshell programme unaffected. **Suppression of an embryonic defect and restoration of the eggshell are different rescue criteria.**

Finally, quantify nuclear-to-cytoplasmic [Dorsal protein](../../molecular-genetics.md#dorsal-protein) around the embryonic circumference, alongside [Twist](../../molecular-genetics.md#twist), [Snail](../../molecular-genetics.md#snail) and [Decapentaplegic](../../signal-transduction.md#decapentaplegic) boundaries. Normal ovarian [Gurken](../../protein.md#gurken) signalling with uniform high nuclear [Dorsal protein](../../molecular-genetics.md#dorsal-protein) instead suggests ectopic extracellular activation, constitutive [Toll receptor](../../protein.md#toll-receptor) activity or loss of its intracellular restraint. A reproducible result from tissue-specific rescue, first-defect imaging and reciprocal [epistasis](../../genetics.md#epistasis) would distinguish these explanations. Neither resemblance to a known mutant nor a candidate sequence variant alone establishes how this novel lesion acts.

<h3 id="3/solution">Solution</h3>

↑ **Parent:** [3](#3)

**Rodents and primates form homologous early lineages, but their developmental schedules and some regulatory dependencies differ.** The [house mouse](../../organism.md#house-mouse) is a useful rodent reference; human and [marmoset](../../organism.md#marmoset) [embryos](../../biology.md#embryo) provide primate comparisons. Neither one rodent nor one primate represents every member of its group. Comparisons should align developmental state as well as time since [fertilization](../../biology.md#fertilization).

Both begin with [cleavage divisions](../../cell-cycle.md#cleavage-division), initially using maternal [RNA](../../polymer.md#ribonucleic-acid) and [proteins](../../protein.md), and then undergo [zygotic genome activation](../../molecular-genetics.md#zygotic-genome-activation). The major activation wave occurs at the two-cell stage in the [house mouse](../../organism.md#house-mouse), whereas in humans it is spread over the four- to eight-cell interval with a prominent eight-cell programme; [marmoset](../../organism.md#marmoset) activation is likewise later than in the [house mouse](../../organism.md#house-mouse). Minor earlier [transcription](../../molecular-genetics.md#transcription-biology) makes an absolute statement that the [embryo](../../biology.md#embryo) is transcriptionally silent before these stages misleading. Comparative [single-cell RNA sequencing](../../molecular-genetics.md#single-cell-rna-sequencing) identifies both conserved lineage programmes and species-specific regulators, including differences between human and [marmoset](../../organism.md#marmoset) [embryos](../../biology.md#embryo).[Development 145, dev167833; early cleavage-stage comparisons and lineage-specific transcription-factor analyses](../../biology-bibliography.md#single-cell-transcriptome-analysis-of-human-marmoset-and-mouse-embryos-reveals-common-and-divergent-features-of-preimplantation-development-by-thorsten-boroviak-and-others)

The timing difference can be demonstrated with stage-resolved nascent-[RNA](../../polymer.md#ribonucleic-acid) labeling, intronic [RNA](../../polymer.md#ribonucleic-acid) measurements and [transcription](../../molecular-genetics.md#transcription-biology) inhibition, alongside maternal-transcript decay measurements. Increased total transcript abundance alone cannot distinguish new synthesis from altered stability. An inhibitor-induced cleavage arrest is informative only if uptake, target engagement and viability are checked; maternal stores may sustain divisions after inhibition. Species-specific rates of division also mean that two-cell versus eight-cell activation cannot be translated into a fixed hour difference without direct measurement.

The first major lineage separation distinguishes outer [trophectoderm](../../biology.md#trophectoderm), which contributes to extraembryonic support, from the [inner cell mass](../../biology.md#inner-cell-mass). In the [house mouse](../../organism.md#house-mouse), [biological cell](../../biological-cell.md) position and apical [cell polarity](../../biological-cell.md#cell-polarity) regulate [Hippo signalling](../../signal-transduction.md#hippo-signalling): inner [biological cells](../../biological-cell.md) restrain nuclear [YAP](../../molecular-genetics.md#yap), while outer polarised [biological cells](../../biological-cell.md) permit [YAP](../../molecular-genetics.md#yap)–[TEAD](../../molecular-genetics.md#tead) activity and expression of [trophectoderm](../../biology.md#trophectoderm) regulators such as [CDX2](../../molecular-genetics.md#cdx2). [OCT4](../../molecular-genetics.md#oct4), [Sox2](../../molecular-genetics.md#sox2) and [NANOG](../../molecular-genetics.md#nanog) participate in pluripotent identity. Human [embryos](../../biology.md#embryo) also use polarity-dependent regulation and form the same broad lineage distinction, but marker onset and restriction are less interchangeable with mouse stages. A single [OCT4](../../molecular-genetics.md#oct4)-positive or [CDX2](../../molecular-genetics.md#cdx2)-positive [biological cell](../../biological-cell.md) is therefore insufficient to classify all [species](../../biology.md#species) at every early stage; use multiple markers, position and developmental trajectory.[Nature 587, pp. 443–447; comparative polarity, Hippo-pathway activity and trophectoderm initiation](../../biology-bibliography.md#initiation-of-a-conserved-trophectoderm-program-in-human-cow-and-mouse-embryos-by-claudia-gerri-and-others)

The [inner cell mass](../../biology.md#inner-cell-mass) subsequently separates into [epiblast](../../biology.md#epiblast), which supplies the [embryo](../../biology.md#embryo) proper, and [hypoblast](../../biology.md#hypoblast), also called [primitive endoderm](../../biology.md#hypoblast), which supplies extraembryonic endodermal [biological tissues](../../biology.md#tissue-biology). In the [house mouse](../../organism.md#house-mouse), initially overlapping [NANOG](../../molecular-genetics.md#nanog) and [GATA6](../../molecular-genetics.md#gata6) expression resolves into intermingled prospective [epiblast](../../biology.md#epiblast) and [hypoblast](../../biology.md#hypoblast) [biological cells](../../biological-cell.md), followed by sorting into their appropriate positions. Thus initial specification is not simply imposed by which [biological cell](../../biological-cell.md) first contacts the cavity. [FGF4](../../signal-transduction.md#fgf4) from the prospective [epiblast](../../biology.md#epiblast) acts through [FGF signalling](../../signal-transduction.md#fgf-signalling) and [ERK](../../signal-transduction.md#erk) to promote the [hypoblast](../../biology.md#hypoblast) programme, including later [GATA4](../../molecular-genetics.md#gata4) and [SOX17](../../molecular-genetics.md#sox17) expression. Reduced pathway output favours [epiblast](../../biology.md#epiblast); increased output can favour [hypoblast](../../biology.md#hypoblast). Loss of the [Grb2](../../signal-transduction.md#grb2) adaptor provides genetic evidence for the importance of this pathway in mouse lineage segregation.[Developmental Cell 10, pp. 615–624: Grb2 mutants, NANOG/GATA6 segregation and lineage allocation](../../biology-bibliography.md#early-lineage-segregation-between-epiblast-and-primitive-endoderm-in-mouse-blastocysts-through-the-grb2-mapk-pathway-by-claire-chazaud-and-others)

For the evidence available at the June 2025 examination, the central contrast was that [hypoblast](../../biology.md#hypoblast) markers persisted in human [embryos](../../biology.md#embryo) exposed to [FGF receptor](../../protein.md#fgf-receptor) or [MEK](../../signal-transduction.md#mek) inhibitors under conditions that strongly redirected rodent [inner cell mass](../../biology.md#inner-cell-mass) allocation. This supported a difference in experimentally demonstrated pathway dependence, rather than proof that humans never use [FGF signalling](../../signal-transduction.md#fgf-signalling). Inhibition at one node may be incomplete, signals can converge downstream, and an already specified [hypoblast](../../biology.md#hypoblast) [biological cell](../../biological-cell.md) can persist after an initiating input is removed. The original comparative inhibitor experiments should therefore be interpreted in their stage and culture context.[Figure 2 and accompanying Results: human versus mouse and rat responses to FGF/MEK inhibition](../../biology-bibliography.md#human-hypoblast-formation-is-not-dependent-on-fgf-signalling-by-mila-roode-and-others)

There are additional primate features. Human [epiblast](../../biology.md#epiblast) expresses a distinctive pluripotency network, with [KLF17](../../molecular-genetics.md#klf17) among its useful markers, and [Nodal signalling](../../signal-transduction.md#nodal-signalling) has been implicated in maintaining its [NANOG](../../molecular-genetics.md#nanog) programme. Perturbation of [TGF-beta signalling](../../signal-transduction.md#tgf-beta-signalling) in cultured human [embryos](../../biology.md#embryo) reduced [NANOG](../../molecular-genetics.md#nanog), supporting an input into human [epiblast](../../biology.md#epiblast) maintenance; it does not show that this pathway alone specifies the lineage. Nor should mouse stem-cell dependence on [LIF signalling](../../signal-transduction.md#lif-signalling), or human cultured stem-cell dependence on particular media, be substituted for an in-embryo lineage-specification experiment.[Results: human epiblast-enriched transcription and TGF-beta signalling inhibition in human embryos](../../biology-bibliography.md#defining-the-three-cell-lineages-of-the-human-blastocyst-by-single-cell-rna-seq-by-paul-blakeley-and-others)

Morphogenesis also diverges. The [house mouse](../../organism.md#house-mouse) establishes a cup-shaped postimplantation [epiblast](../../biology.md#epiblast) within an egg-cylinder organisation; primates form a flatter embryonic disc, with different extraembryonic geometry and early [amnion](../../biology.md#amnion) development. Such geometry changes which [biological tissues](../../biology.md#tissue-biology) can deliver signals to competent recipients. It is therefore unsafe to transfer a signalling-source map directly between [species](../../biology.md#species) merely because the [ligand](../../protein.md#ligand-biochemistry) and responding [transcription factors](../../molecular-genetics.md#transcription-factor) are conserved.

A rigorous comparison would use several developmental windows, independent inhibitors at receptor and downstream levels, genetic perturbations where feasible, [ligand](../../protein.md#ligand-biochemistry) addition, washout and rescue. Measure pathway activity inside the [embryo](../../biology.md#embryo), not only in a cultured reference [cell line](../../biological-cell.md#cell-line). Count total [inner cell mass](../../biology.md#inner-cell-mass) [biological cells](../../biological-cell.md) and multiple lineage markers, and score [cell proliferation](../../biological-cell.md#cell-proliferation) and [apoptosis](../../biological-cell.md#apoptosis). A smaller [hypoblast](../../biology.md#hypoblast) population could reflect failed specification, death or delayed maturation. Analyse [embryos](../../biology.md#embryo) as independent [biological replicates](../../design-of-experiments.md#biological-replicate), with [biological cells](../../biological-cell.md) nested within [embryos](../../biology.md#embryo); compare human and [marmoset](../../organism.md#marmoset) separately rather than pooling them as primates.

A later result sharpens the caveat. Published on 28 July 2025, after this examination, direct [ERK](../../signal-transduction.md#erk) inhibition with [ulixertinib](../../biology.md#ulixertinib) strongly reduced human [hypoblast](../../biology.md#hypoblast) and favoured an [epiblast](../../biology.md#epiblast)-like [inner cell mass](../../biology.md#inner-cell-mass). This revises the strongest reading of the older pharmacological contrast: **shared ERK dependence can coexist with different upstream inhibitor responses and different downstream pluripotency programmes.** It is a subsequent scientific update, not evidence a June candidate was expected to know.[Publication date; Figure 2 and Results, Suppression of ERK signalling blocks hypoblast formation in the human blastocyst](../../biology-bibliography.md#suppression-of-erk-signalling-promotes-pluripotent-epiblast-in-the-human-blastocyst-by-claire-s-simon-and-others)

<h3 id="4/solution">Solution</h3>

**Time patterns an axis by controlling which signals [biological cells](../../biological-cell.md) encounter, how long they interpret them, and when a changing regulatory state becomes fixed in space.** Repeated segments, regional identities and competence windows are distinct temporal problems. An [embryo](../../biology.md#embryo) may solve them with interacting timers; there is no requirement for one universal developmental clock.

In [vertebrate](../../organism.md#vertebrate) [somitogenesis](../../biology.md#somitogenesis), [presomitic mesoderm](../../biology.md#presomitic-mesoderm) shows cyclic [gene expression](../../molecular-genetics.md#gene-expression), including [Hes/Her transcription factors](../../molecular-genetics.md#hes-her-transcription-factor). Delayed [negative feedback](../../signal-transduction.md#negative-feedback) can produce repeated activity, while [Notch signalling](../../signal-transduction.md#notch-signalling) couples neighbouring [biological cells](../../biological-cell.md) so their phases remain coherent. In a [clock and wavefront model](../../biology.md#clock-and-wavefront-model), these oscillations meet a moving maturation boundary associated with changing [FGF signalling](../../signal-transduction.md#fgf-signalling), [Wnt signalling](../../signal-transduction.md#wnt-signalling) and anterior differentiation. A boundary is fixed once per effective clock cycle. For a steady relative wavefront speed $v$ and period $T$, the model predicts

$$
s\simeq vT,\qquad N\simeq \frac{\Delta t}{T},
$$

where $s$ is segment spacing and $N$ is the number formed during an active interval $\Delta t$. The speed is relative to the [biological tissue](../../biology.md#tissue-biology), not necessarily the measured displacement of the tail tip. Units check: length/time multiplied by time gives length. These are approximations: changing [biological tissue](../../biology.md#tissue-biology) strain, gradients or periods can invalidate constant-parameter predictions.

The classic chick evidence distinguished periodic expression from movement. Paired halves of posterior [biological tissue](../../biology.md#tissue-biology) were compared after different culture intervals, revealing recurrent [hairy gene](../../genetics.md#hairy-gene) expression patterns with a period matching [somite](../../biology.md#somite) formation. The reported approximately 90-minute period belongs to those chick conditions and should not be assigned to every [vertebrate](../../organism.md#vertebrate). A travelling expression wave need not be a travelling cohort of [biological cells](../../biological-cell.md).[Cell 91, pp. 639–648; cyclic c-hairy1 expression and comparison of temporal expression patterns](../../biology-bibliography.md#avian-hairy-gene-expression-identifies-a-molecular-clock-linked-to-vertebrate-segmentation-and-somitogenesis-by-isabel-palmeirim-and-others)

Tests should separately alter clock period, intercellular coupling and [biological tissue](../../biology.md#tissue-biology) maturation. An experimentally lengthened period predicts wider spacing if relative wavefront speed remains constant; slowing maturation can offset that effect. Disrupting coupling may produce irregular boundaries even while isolated [biological cells](../../biological-cell.md) continue oscillating. Record live reporters together with [biological cell](../../biological-cell.md) tracking, rather than inferring a clock solely from differently staged fixed [embryos](../../biology.md#embryo). Matched temperature, developmental stage and reporter maturation are essential controls: a slow fluorescent reporter can smooth or phase-shift the underlying [transcription](../../molecular-genetics.md#transcription-biology).

Regional identity has another temporal component. [Hox genes](../../genetics.md#hox-gene) are often activated in temporal order in posterior [progenitor cells](../../biological-cell.md#progenitor-cell); earlier descendants leave with a different regulatory history from later descendants, helping produce nested anterior–posterior expression domains. Experiments in chick linked sequential [Hoxb gene](../../genetics.md#hoxb-gene) activation to the timing of mesodermal ingression, connecting temporal activation to spatial deployment.[Nature 442, pp. 568–571: temporal activation in epiblast precursors and effects on ingression](../../biology-bibliography.md#collinear-activation-of-hoxb-genes-during-gastrulation-is-linked-to-mesoderm-cell-ingression-by-tadahiro-iimura-and-olivier-pourquie) The [Hox gene](../../genetics.md#hox-gene) programme assigns regional character, whereas the [segmentation clock](../../mathematical-biology.md#segmentation-clock) chiefly organises repetition. A [somite](../../biology.md#somite) boundary and a cervical-versus-thoracic identity are therefore not the same output. Temporal collinearity is also not a universal property of all [Hox gene](../../genetics.md#hox-gene) clusters in all animals.

Signal duration supplies a further mechanism. A recipient can integrate a time-dependent [morphogen](../../biology.md#morphogen) exposure. In an illustrative threshold model, let $c(t)$ denote local signal activity and define

$$
I(t)=\int_0^t c(\tau)\,\mathrm d\tau.
$$

A decision at $I=I_*$ depends on both strength and duration. This model is a hypothesis to test, not a claim that every [morphogen](../../biology.md#morphogen) is integrated linearly. Give equal-duration pulses of different strengths and equal-total-exposure pulses with different timing, then assay final [cell fate](../../organism.md#cell-fate). If equal integrals yield different outcomes, adaptation, changing competence or threshold memory matters. Pulse-and-washout experiments during [gastrulation](../../biology.md#gastrulation) can therefore separate an early induction requirement from a later maintenance requirement.

In the long-germ insect [Drosophila melanogaster](../../organism.md#drosophila-melanogaster), most trunk segments are initially patterned together rather than added one by one from a posterior zone. Maternal inputs feed [gap genes](../../genetics.md#gap-gene), then [pair-rule genes](../../genetics.md#pair-rule-gene), then [segment-polarity genes](../../genetics.md#segment-polarity-gene). This is still a dynamic process: domains shift and the regulatory interactions change with developmental stage. The temporally regulated factor [Odd-paired](../../molecular-genetics.md#odd-paired) reorganises the [pair-rule gene regulatory network](../../mathematical-biology.md#pair-rule-gene-regulatory-network), enabling a transition from double-segment to single-segment periodicity. Loss of that temporal input prevents the normal network transition even though the earlier spatial information exists.[eLife 5, e18215; Results: Odd-paired-dependent network changes and frequency doubling](../../biology-bibliography.md#odd-paired-controls-frequency-doubling-in-drosophila-segmentation-by-altering-the-pair-rule-gene-regulatory-network-by-erik-clark-and-michael-akam)

In the short-germ beetle [Tribolium castaneum](../../organism.md#tribolium-castaneum), much segmentation proceeds sequentially from a posterior [segment addition zone](../../biology.md#segment-addition-zone). Temporal changes are converted to positions as [biological tissue](../../biology.md#tissue-biology) leaves that zone. Comparative expression and perturbation evidence implicates a sequence involving [Caudal](../../molecular-genetics.md#caudal), [Dichaete](../../molecular-genetics.md#dichaete) and [Odd-paired](../../molecular-genetics.md#odd-paired), with their distributions associated with successive segmentation phases. This links the apparently simultaneous and sequential modes through changing regulatory competence, without establishing that the complete insect and [vertebrate](../../organism.md#vertebrate) oscillators are homologous.[Development 145, dev155580; Results: candidate timing factors and comparison between Drosophila and Tribolium](../../biology-bibliography.md#evidence-for-the-temporal-regulation-of-insect-segmentation-by-a-conserved-sequence-of-transcription-factors-by-erik-clark-and-andrew-d-peel)

Finally, [heterochrony](../../biology.md#heterochrony) can change how many repetitions fit within the period of axial production, which identities are assigned to them, or the order in which [biological tissues](../../biology.md#tissue-biology) become competent. These possibilities require separate tests. Counting extra segments cannot by itself distinguish a faster clock from prolonged posterior activity; shifting a regional boundary cannot by itself distinguish altered [Hox gene](../../genetics.md#hox-gene) activation from altered [biological cell](../../biological-cell.md) deployment. The strongest explanation of temporal patterning therefore connects a measured history, a manipulated timing variable, and a lasting spatial or functional outcome.

<h3 id="5/solution">Solution</h3>

↑ **Parent:** [5](#5)

**Identify specification by changing a [biological cell](../../biological-cell.md)'s environment and asking what it can still become.** A normal [fate map](../../organism.md#fate-map) reports what [biological cells](../../biological-cell.md) do in an intact [embryo](../../biology.md#embryo); it does not reveal their developmental potential. [cell specification](../../organism.md#cell-specification) is operationally demonstrated when a [biological cell](../../biological-cell.md) adopts a fate in an appropriate neutral environment, whereas [cell determination](../../organism.md#cell-determination) is the stronger ability to retain that fate in a conflicting but viable environment. [Cell differentiation](../../organism.md#cellular-differentiation) is the execution of specialised properties. None should be inferred from one early marker alone.

Begin with a stage-resolved reference. Image [embryos](../../biology.md#embryo) from [fertilization](../../biology.md#fertilization) through the first stable [biological tissue](../../biology.md#tissue-biology) identities, using minimally perturbing nuclear and membrane labels. Mark individual [blastomeres](../../biological-cell.md#blastomere) by [photoconversion](../../fluorescence.md#photoconversion) or inducible [genetic lineage tracing](../../biological-cell.md#genetic-lineage-tracing) and follow all descendants. Combine this with spatial marker panels and [single-cell RNA sequencing](../../molecular-genetics.md#single-cell-rna-sequencing) at selected stages to distinguish transient states from stable endpoints. Register positions, division orientation, neighbours and final [cell fate](../../organism.md#cell-fate). Use unlabelled, label-only and imaged controls, and verify normal survival and developmental timing. Independent [embryos](../../biology.md#embryo), not multiple [biological cells](../../biological-cell.md) from one [embryo](../../biology.md#embryo), are the primary [biological replicates](../../design-of-experiments.md#biological-replicate).

Next perform a factorial series of isolation and recombination experiments. Isolate identified [blastomeres](../../biological-cell.md#blastomere) at progressively later stages and culture them separately in several defined media chosen to maintain viability without intentionally imposing a candidate fate. Use intact [embryos](../../biology.md#embryo), sham-manipulated [embryos](../../biology.md#embryo) and known committed [biological tissue](../../biology.md#tissue-biology) as controls. If an isolated [biological cell](../../biological-cell.md) repeatedly develops according to its normal fate, it is already specified under those conditions. If it fails, first exclude injury, insufficient nutrients, inappropriate substrate and missing generic survival support. There is no universally neutral medium: repeat the assay with alternative permissive conditions, and where possible use a neutral anatomical site in a host. Agreement across environments makes the inference stronger.

Test an [autonomous specification](../../organism.md#autonomous-specification) hypothesis by tracking asymmetric maternal [RNA](../../polymer.md#ribonucleic-acid), [proteins](../../protein.md) or [organelles](../../biological-cell.md#organelle) through cleavage and asking whether their inheritance predicts fate after isolation. Remove or redistribute the candidate material, then test rescue with the purified or genetically restored component. Transfer it to an otherwise non-producing [biological cell](../../biological-cell.md) to test sufficiency. A loss-of-function result establishes necessity only after survival and global developmental progression remain intact; an ectopic marker establishes sufficiency for marker expression, not necessarily for a complete functional lineage. Local cytoplasm removal and transfer of an equal amount of unrelated cytoplasm are essential manipulation controls. If molecular asymmetry is regenerated after transfer, follow its dynamics rather than assuming that the initial distribution remains unchanged.

Test [conditional specification](../../organism.md#conditional-specification) by altering contacts while preserving the isolated [biological cell](../../biological-cell.md)'s physiological condition. Remove an identified neighbour before the putative induction window, recombine the recipient with that neighbour, and compare unrelated [biological tissue](../../biology.md#tissue-biology). Rescue by the correct neighbour implicates an interaction. A permeable barrier or conditioned medium can distinguish a diffusible signal from a requirement for direct contact, but failure of conditioned medium is inconclusive if the signal is unstable, matrix-bound or requires a local concentration. A positive result through a barrier must also exclude passage of intact [biological cells](../../biological-cell.md). Label sender and receiver separately so recipient conversion is not confused with expansion of contaminating donor [biological cells](../../biological-cell.md).

Build a causal sender–signal–receiver chain. Delete a candidate [ligand](../../protein.md#ligand-biochemistry) specifically in the sender, delete its [receptor](../../protein.md#receptor-biochemistry) specifically in the recipient, and monitor an immediate pathway reporter before the terminal fate readout. Ligand addition should rescue [ligand](../../protein.md#ligand-biochemistry) loss when the receptor is present; it should fail after verified receptor loss. Activated downstream machinery in the recipient can test bypass. Demonstrate a dose range that changes fate without changing survival, and use pulse-and-washout experiments to map a [competence window](../../organism.md#competence-window). Loss of an endpoint after late treatment may indicate maintenance or differentiation failure rather than the original specification event. Independent genetic and pharmacological interventions reduce the risk of an off-target explanation.

Perform reciprocal heterotopic [cell transplantation](../../biology.md#cell-transplantation) at several stages. Move a labelled prospective A [biological cell](../../biological-cell.md) into a B environment and a B [biological cell](../../biological-cell.md) into an A environment, with same-position grafts as surgical controls. Verify integration, survival and exposure to the host signal. If an early donor adopts the host fate, its potential is conditional at that stage. If a later donor retains its original fate while the host remains permissive and the graft survives, that supports [cell determination](../../organism.md#cell-determination). Retention in a host that never contacts or signals to the graft is uninformative. Follow mature [morphology](../../biology.md#morphology-biology) and function as well as markers; a hybrid marker state can represent incomplete conversion rather than either stable fate.

Regulative capacity needs another test. Ablate a mapped precursor and trace the source of replacement [biological cells](../../biological-cell.md). Normal final anatomy is stronger evidence of [regulative development](../../biology.md#regulative-development) if a previously distinct neighbour demonstrably adopts the missing fate; it is weaker if a few unablated members of the original lineage simply proliferate. Include sham ablation and damage-matched ablation elsewhere, quantify [biological cell](../../biological-cell.md) death and measure final [biological cell](../../biological-cell.md) numbers. Conversely, persistent loss of a structure after ablation does not prove [mosaic development](../../biology.md#mosaic-development): the [embryo](../../biology.md#embryo) may have lost an essential signal, physical scaffold or sufficient [biological cell](../../biological-cell.md) mass rather than an irreplaceable determinant.

A third mechanism, [syncytial specification](../../biology.md#syncytial-specification), must be tested if early divisions produce many [cell nuclei](../../biological-cell.md#cell-nucleus) in shared [cytoplasm](../../biological-cell.md#cytoplasm) before [cellularization](../../biology.md#cellularization). Image membranes to establish whether true cellular boundaries exist. Map nuclear position, local [morphogen](../../biology.md#morphogen) activity and nascent [transcription](../../molecular-genetics.md#transcription-biology); move [cell nuclei](../../biological-cell.md#cell-nucleus) relative to the gradient or shift the signal source while retaining nuclear identity. If equivalent [cell nuclei](../../biological-cell.md#cell-nucleus) adopt different programmes according to position in shared [cytoplasm](../../biological-cell.md#cytoplasm), that supports positional specification before [biological cell](../../biological-cell.md) boundaries. Perturbing [cellularization](../../biology.md#cellularization) alone is a poor test because it can damage several later processes.

An explicit interpretation matrix prevents overclaiming: retention after isolation supports specification; switching after a viable heterotopic graft shows continuing plasticity; retention after a verified conflicting graft supports determination; fate following a manipulated inherited determinant supports autonomy; and fate following a manipulated neighbour signal supports induction. These modes can coexist. An asymmetric maternal determinant might set competence for an external signal, and an induced fate can later become autonomous through [positive feedback](../../signal-transduction.md#positive-feedback) in a [gene regulatory network](../../mathematical-biology.md#gene-regulatory-network). **The final product should be a map of mechanisms and commitment times for particular lineages, not a single label for the entire organism.**

**Germ plasm inheritance places germline-promoting material into selected [biological cells](../../biological-cell.md); induction establishes germline identity through signalling to competent [biological cells](../../biological-cell.md).** Both must protect the future [germline](../../biological-cell.md#germline) from inappropriate somatic differentiation. They differ in how that programme begins and in how broadly early [transcription](../../molecular-genetics.md#transcription-biology) is repressed. [primordial germ cells](../../biological-cell.md#primordial-germ-cell) are precursors of future [gametes](../../biology.md#gamete), not simply [biological cells](../../biological-cell.md) expressing one widely shared germ-cell marker.

In [Drosophila melanogaster](../../organism.md#drosophila-melanogaster), maternal [oskar messenger RNA](../../polymer.md#oskar-messenger-rna) is transported to the posterior [oocyte](../../biology.md#oocyte) and locally translated. [Oskar](../../protein.md#oskar) organises [germ plasm](../../biological-cell.md#germ-plasm) containing [RNA](../../polymer.md#ribonucleic-acid) and [proteins](../../protein.md), including [Vasa](../../protein.md#vasa), [Tudor](../../protein.md#tudor) and [Nanos](../../protein.md#nanos). Posterior [cell nuclei](../../biological-cell.md#cell-nucleus) encounter this specialised [cytoplasm](../../biological-cell.md#cytoplasm) and cellularise as [pole cells](../../biological-cell.md#pole-cell). Necessity and positional sufficiency are distinguishable: loss of [Oskar](../../protein.md#oskar) disrupts assembly, while experimentally mislocalised [oskar messenger RNA](../../polymer.md#oskar-messenger-rna) can induce anterior [pole cells](../../biological-cell.md#pole-cell). The latter result shows that the organising material can redirect where the programme begins; it does not mean that [Oskar](../../protein.md#oskar) acts without the other cellular components.[Nature 358, pp. 387–392; anterior oskar localisation and ectopic germ-cell formation](../../biology-bibliography.md#induction-of-germ-cell-formation-by-oskar-by-anne-ephrussi-and-ruth-lehmann)

Assembly and inheritance also should not be equated with one universal granule mechanism. A [germ plasm](../../biological-cell.md#germ-plasm) is a spatially organised collection of functional components; the visible granule and the whole regulatory activity are not necessarily identical. Test assembly by tracking recruitment order, deleting candidate organisers and rescuing them at physiological levels. Test specification separately by permanent [lineage tracing](../../biological-cell.md#lineage-tracing) and later contribution to the gonad and, where experimentally feasible, functional [gametes](../../biology.md#gamete). Disrupting a visible aggregate without abolishing the relevant biochemical activity is not evidence that all germline specification has failed.

In fly [pole cells](../../biological-cell.md#pole-cell), a direct route to [transcriptional quiescence](../../molecular-genetics.md#transcriptional-quiescence) is the small [Pgc protein](../../protein.md#pgc-protein), translated from inherited germ-plasm [RNA](../../polymer.md#ribonucleic-acid). It binds [P-TEFb](../../protein.md#p-tefb), a [protein kinase](../../enzyme.md#protein-kinase) complex needed for productive [RNA polymerase II](../../molecular-genetics.md#rna-polymerase-ii) elongation, and prevents its normal recruitment to transcriptional sites. Reduced [phosphorylation](../../chemical-reaction.md#phosphorylation) of the polymerase's C-terminal domain at serine 2 accompanies repression. Loss, ectopic expression, physical interaction and recruitment assays connect [Pgc protein](../../protein.md#pgc-protein) to this mechanism rather than merely correlating a silent [biological cell](../../biological-cell.md) with a granule.[Abstract and Results: Pgc loss and ectopic expression, P-TEFb interaction and chromatin recruitment](../../biology-bibliography.md#drosophila-pgc-protein-inhibits-p-tefb-recruitment-to-chromatin-in-primordial-germ-cells-by-kazuko-hanyu-nakamura-and-others)

The nematode [Caenorhabditis elegans](../../organism.md#caenorhabditis-elegans) illustrates another inherited route. Maternal [PIE-1](../../protein.md#pie-1) segregates into the germline blastomeres and inhibits the somatic [transcription](../../molecular-genetics.md#transcription-biology) programme. A repression domain with similarity to the [RNA polymerase II](../../molecular-genetics.md#rna-polymerase-ii) C-terminal repeat provided a mechanistic link to the transcriptional apparatus; mutations that damage repression also weaken in-embryo rescue. Thus distinct inherited repressors can converge on productive [RNA polymerase II](../../molecular-genetics.md#rna-polymerase-ii) activity. It is unnecessary to claim that [PIE-1](../../protein.md#pie-1) and [Pgc protein](../../protein.md#pgc-protein) are the same molecule or that every inherited germline component acts by this mechanism.[Genes & Development 13, pp. 202–212; repression-domain assays and rescue with altered PIE-1](../../biology-bibliography.md#transcriptional-repression-by-the-caenorhabditis-elegans-germ-line-protein-pie-1-by-ceri-batchelder-and-others)

In the [house mouse](../../organism.md#house-mouse), [primordial germ cells](../../biological-cell.md#primordial-germ-cell) are induced from competent postimplantation [epiblast](../../biology.md#epiblast) rather than assigned by an already localised maternal [germ plasm](../../biological-cell.md#germ-plasm). [BMP4](../../signal-transduction.md#bmp4) from extraembryonic [biological tissues](../../biology.md#tissue-biology) supplies an essential inductive input. [BMP4](../../signal-transduction.md#bmp4) null [embryos](../../biology.md#embryo) lack [primordial germ cells](../../biological-cell.md#primordial-germ-cell), and chimera analysis identified an extraembryonic source requirement; fewer founders in heterozygotes showed a dosage-sensitive effect on establishment rather than simply later expansion.[Abstract and chimera analysis; Genes & Development 13, pp. 424–436](../../biology-bibliography.md#bmp4-is-required-for-the-generation-of-primordial-germ-cells-in-the-mouse-embryo-by-kirstie-a-lawson-and-others) Necessity of this signal does not imply that every exposed [epiblast](../../biology.md#epiblast) [biological cell](../../biological-cell.md) becomes germline: competence, position and competing inputs constrain the response.

The induced programme uses [BLIMP1](../../molecular-genetics.md#blimp1), [PRDM14](../../molecular-genetics.md#prdm14) and [TFAP2C](../../molecular-genetics.md#tfap2c) to repress somatic regulatory networks and establish germline identity and [epigenetic reprogramming](../../organism.md#epigenetic-reprogramming). [BLIMP1](../../molecular-genetics.md#blimp1) binds and represses somatic and proliferation-associated targets, while the factors also activate germline-associated programmes. This is the crucial contrast in interpreting quiescence: **an induced germline must transcribe its specification programme, so selective repression of somatic [genes](../../genetics.md#gene) is not equivalent to a universal shutdown of transcription.** Chromatin and [DNA methylation](../../molecular-genetics.md#dna-methylation) are subsequently remodelled; a hypomethylated state by itself is not evidence that the genome is transcriptionally inactive.[Abstract and Results: direct BLIMP1 targets, cooperative transcription-factor activity and induction of PGC-like cells](../../biology-bibliography.md#a-tripartite-transcription-factor-network-regulates-primordial-germ-cell-specification-in-mice-by-erna-magnusdottir-and-others)

Induction also differs among mammals. Human [primordial germ cell-like cells](../../biological-cell.md#primordial-germ-cell-like-cell) require [SOX17](../../molecular-genetics.md#sox17) as an important upstream specifier, with [BLIMP1](../../molecular-genetics.md#blimp1) helping suppress alternative somatic programmes. This comes from experimentally induced [biological cells](../../biological-cell.md) compared with available germ-cell references; it should not be presented as direct observation of every event in an intact human [embryo](../../biology.md#embryo). It demonstrates why the exact mouse hierarchy cannot simply be renamed human.[Cell 160, pp. 253–268; SOX17 perturbation and human primordial germ cell-like cell induction](../../biology-bibliography.md#sox17-is-a-critical-specifier-of-human-primordial-germ-cell-fate-by-naoko-irie-and-others)

For either mode, measure nascent [RNA](../../polymer.md#ribonucleic-acid) incorporation together with serine-2-phosphorylated [RNA polymerase II](../../molecular-genetics.md#rna-polymerase-ii), total polymerase and multiple somatic and germline transcripts. Maternal [messenger RNA](../../polymer.md#messenger-rna) persistence makes total [RNA](../../polymer.md#ribonucleic-acid) abundance an unreliable measure of current synthesis. Include somatic [biological cells](../../biological-cell.md) in the same [embryo](../../biology.md#embryo) as positive controls, a validated transcription-inhibited sample as a low-signal control, and viability measurements. Timed perturbation after specification distinguishes a requirement for establishing identity from one for maintaining quiescence. Rescue should recover appropriate molecular identity and later lineage function, not merely lower a staining signal. These experiments would reveal the shared aim of suppressing inappropriate differentiation while preserving the different transcriptional requirements of inheritance and induction.

<h3 id="7/solution">Solution</h3>

**The [New Head hypothesis](../../biology.md#new-head-hypothesis) offers a plausible developmental explanation for [vertebrate](../../organism.md#vertebrate) cranial innovation: migratory neural crest [biological cells](../../biological-cell.md) helped build a feeding and sensory apparatus that could diversify.** Its strongest form is a hypothesis about integration of several innovations, not a claim that [neural crest](../../biology.md#neural-crest) alone produced every part of the [vertebrate](../../organism.md#vertebrate) head or directly proves evolutionary success.

[neural crest](../../biology.md#neural-crest) arises near the border of the neural and non-neural [ectoderm](../../biology.md#ectoderm). Cells undergo an [epithelial-to-mesenchymal transition](../../organism.md#epithelial-to-mesenchymal-transition), migrate and generate diverse derivatives. Cranial populations contribute substantially to facial and pharyngeal [cartilage](../../biology.md#cartilage), [bone](../../biology.md#bone) and connective [biological tissues](../../biology.md#tissue-biology); other populations supply peripheral [neurons](../../neuroscience.md#neuron), [glial cells](../../neuroscience.md#glial-cell) and pigment [biological cells](../../biological-cell.md). These are regionally restricted potentials, not a claim that every crest [biological cell](../../biological-cell.md) makes every derivative. Importantly, [neural crest](../../biology.md#neural-crest) remains ectodermal in origin even when it makes [biological tissues](../../biology.md#tissue-biology) often associated with [mesoderm](../../biology.md#mesoderm).

The hypothesis proposed by [Carl Gans](../../biology.md#carl-gans) and [R. Glenn Northcutt](../../biology.md#r-glenn-northcutt) linked the new head to a transition from passive suspension feeding toward more active food acquisition, with an expanded cranial skeleton, sensory systems and muscularised pharyngeal apparatus. [neural crest](../../biology.md#neural-crest), [ectodermal placodes](../../biology.md#ectodermal-placode) and mesodermal muscle contributions were complementary. A support skeleton allows feeding surfaces and sensory organs to be positioned; sensory organs guide action; muscles supply force. Their integration gives a more convincing functional explanation than a list of crest derivatives alone.[Science 220, pp. 268–273; proposed relationship between cranial embryonic sources and active predation](../../biology-bibliography.md#neural-crest-and-the-origin-of-vertebrates-a-new-head-by-carl-gans-and-r-glenn-northcutt)

Several distinctions keep this argument anatomically accurate. The [brain](../../neuroscience.md#brain) largely develops from the [neural tube](../../biology.md#neural-tube); [ectodermal placodes](../../biology.md#ectodermal-placode) contribute important cranial sensory components; much cranial muscle comes from [mesoderm](../../biology.md#mesoderm). [neural crest](../../biology.md#neural-crest) provides connective and skeletal organisation around those [biological tissues](../../biology.md#tissue-biology) and contributes to their interactions. The [pharyngeal arches](../../biology.md#pharyngeal-arch) are multicellular constructions involving [neural crest](../../biology.md#neural-crest), [mesoderm](../../biology.md#mesoderm), [ectoderm](../../biology.md#ectoderm) and [endoderm](../../biology.md#endoderm). Thus loss of cranial [neural crest](../../biology.md#neural-crest) can secondarily disrupt [biological tissues](../../biology.md#tissue-biology) it does not itself become. Fate mapping distinguishes cellular contribution, while selective ablation and rescue test developmental necessity and interaction.

There is direct experimental support for a role in diversification. Reciprocal [cell transplantation](../../biology.md#cell-transplantation) of prospective beak [neural crest](../../biology.md#neural-crest) between quail and duck shifted beak [morphology](../../biology.md#morphology-biology) toward the donor [species](../../biology.md#species) and altered nearby host [biological tissues](../../biology.md#tissue-biology). This connects intrinsic properties of the crest population with instructions to its environment, explaining how changes in one interacting population can reshape a complex organ. Same-species grafts, developmental-stage matching and permanent donor labels are needed to distinguish patterning information from surgical effects or simple replacement. The result supports a mechanism for morphological variation; it does not by itself establish which ancestral feeding behaviour was favoured by [natural selection](../../biology.md#natural-selection).[Science 299, pp. 565–568; reciprocal quail–duck grafts, donor-like beaks and effects on host tissue](../../biology-bibliography.md#the-cellular-and-molecular-origins-of-beak-morphology-by-richard-a-schneider-and-jill-a-helms)

Comparative evidence also qualifies an abrupt invention narrative. Neural-border regulatory and sensory programmes have antecedents outside [vertebrates](../../organism.md#vertebrate). In [Ciona](../../organism.md#ciona), a pigment-cell lineage with parts of a crest-like regulatory programme can acquire migratory behaviour after experimental manipulation. Such a rudiment supports stepwise assembly of properties; a migrating pigment precursor is not automatically equivalent to the full multipotent, skeletogenic [vertebrate](../../organism.md#vertebrate) [neural crest](../../biology.md#neural-crest). Compare origin, regulatory dependencies, migration and derivative range separately.[Nature 492, pp. 104–107; Ciona pigment-cell regulatory programme and experimentally induced migration](../../biology-bibliography.md#identification-of-a-neural-crest-rudiment-in-a-non-vertebrate-chordate-by-philip-barron-abitua-and-others)

Jawless [vertebrates](../../organism.md#vertebrate) are another important logical control. Their existence prevents equating the origin of [neural crest](../../biology.md#neural-crest) with the later origin of jaws: cranial contributions preceded the particular jawed-vertebrate feeding apparatus. Later modifications of an inherited developmental system can support new structures without repeating its original evolutionary origin. Conversely, retention of homologous [genes](../../genetics.md#gene) does not establish identical [biological cell](../../biological-cell.md) types; comparative experiments must test how those [genes](../../genetics.md#gene) interact and what the [biological cells](../../biological-cell.md) actually produce.

A strong research programme would combine [lineage tracing](../../biological-cell.md#lineage-tracing) and selective perturbation across [vertebrate](../../organism.md#vertebrate) and non-vertebrate [chordates](../../organism.md#chordate), compare homologous regulatory elements and ask whether reconstructed changes alter migration, skeletal competence or [biological tissue](../../biology.md#tissue-biology) interactions. [Fossils](../../biology.md#fossil) constrain when hard cranial structures appeared, but rarely identify the embryonic source directly. Modern ablation establishes a present-day requirement, not the sequence of ancestral events. Likewise, [species](../../biology.md#species) richness is influenced by many ecological and historical factors, so it is not a direct experimental readout of one developmental innovation.

The defensible conclusion is that **neural crest supplied a versatile developmental substrate for cranial construction and diversification, acting with placodes and other [biological tissues](../../biology.md#tissue-biology)**. Experimental grafting and comparative developmental evidence make that causal contribution substantial. The specific ecological transition proposed in the [New Head hypothesis](../../biology.md#new-head-hypothesis) remains an evolutionary reconstruction whose details require evidence beyond the existence of crest derivatives.
