<h1 id="1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

**Both animals extend their posterior axis by coordinated [biological tissue](../../../../tissue-biology.md) production and rearrangement, but the balance between these processes differs markedly.** In the [house mouse](../../../../house-mouse.md), sustained expansion of posterior [progenitor cells](../../../../progenitor-cell.md) contributes substantially to new [biological tissue](../../../../tissue-biology.md); in the [zebrafish](../../../../zebrafish.md), much early elongation redistributes an existing population, with additional growth occurring in already formed axial [biological tissues](../../../../tissue-biology.md). Calling both posterior regions a growth zone can therefore conceal the mechanism that actually increases length.

During [gastrulation](../../../../gastrulation.md), the [house mouse](../../../../house-mouse.md) supplies posterior [biological tissues](../../../../tissue-biology.md) through the [primitive streak](../../../../primitive-streak.md); later the [tailbud](../../../../tailbud.md) maintains posterior contributions. [neuromesodermal progenitors](../../../../neuromesodermal-progenitor.md) near the node–streak border and caudal lateral [epiblast](../../../../epiblast.md) contribute to [spinal cord](../../../../spinal-cord.md) and [paraxial mesoderm](../../../../paraxial-mesoderm.md). Their characteristic combination of [Sox2](../../../../sox2.md) and [Brachyury](../../../../brachyury.md) reflects an unresolved neural–mesodermal regulatory state. [Wnt signalling](../../../../wnt-signalling.md) and [FGF signalling](../../../../fgf-signalling.md) help maintain posterior identity and [progenitor cells](../../../../progenitor-cell.md), while increased mesodermal commitment involves [Tbx6](../../../../tbx6.md). Neural differentiation involves resolution toward the [Sox2](../../../../sox2.md) programme. A shared marker combination is evidence about [cell state](../../../../cell-state.md); the stronger claim that a particular [biological cell](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) is bipotent requires [clonal lineage tracing](../../../../clonal-lineage-tracing.md) showing descendants in both [biological tissues](../../../../tissue-biology.md). Retrospective clonal analysis in the [house mouse](../../../../house-mouse.md) provided evidence for persistent common neural–mesodermal progenitors during axial formation.[Developmental Cell 17, pp. 365–376; retrospective clonal analysis of neural and mesodermal descendants](../../../../redefining-the-progression-of-lineage-segregations-during-mammalian-embryogenesis-by-clonal-analysis-by-elena-tzouanacou-and-others.md) Sustained [cell proliferation](../../../../cell-proliferation.md) supplies material as descendants leave the posterior pool, but the pool is heterogeneous and does not provide every posterior [biological tissue](../../../../tissue-biology.md).

In the [zebrafish](../../../../zebrafish.md), [convergent extension](../../../../convergent-extension.md) during [gastrulation](../../../../gastrulation.md) narrows the forming body and lengthens it through directed movements and [cell intercalation](../../../../cell-intercalation.md). Posterior [biological tissue](../../../../tissue-biology.md) continues to receive [biological cells](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) from lateral regions, and rearrangement as [biological cells](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) leave the [tailbud](../../../../tailbud.md) lengthens the axis without requiring equivalent local volume production. The [notochord](../../../../notochord.md), [neural tube](../../../../neural-tube.md) and flanking [paraxial mesoderm](../../../../paraxial-mesoderm.md) are mechanically coupled; they cannot be understood as independent rods. At later stages, growth within the [spinal cord](../../../../spinal-cord.md) and [notochord](../../../../notochord.md) also contributes. Direct comparative measurements found that the unsegmented posterior region of the [zebrafish](../../../../zebrafish.md) does not itself generate the extra volume predicted by a simple posterior-growth model; inhibiting [FGF signalling](../../../../fgf-signalling.md) impaired [biological tissue](../../../../tissue-biology.md) convergence rather than posterior volume growth.[Abstract; Development 143, pp. 1732–1741: tissue influx, convergence–extension, late axial-tissue growth and FGF inhibition](../../../../species-specific-contribution-of-volumetric-growth-and-tissue-convergence-to-posterior-body-elongation-in-vertebrates-by-ben-steventon-and-others.md)

The distinction is made precise by an illustrative geometric identity. Approximate a defined [biological tissue](../../../../tissue-biology.md) region by length $L$, mean cross-sectional area $A$ and volume $V=AL$. Then

$$
\frac{\dot L}{L}=\frac{\dot V}{V}-\frac{\dot A}{A}.
$$

Thus elongation can result from increasing volume, decreasing cross-sectional area, or both. At constant volume, a 20% reduction of area gives $L_{\mathrm{new}}/L_{\mathrm{old}}=1/0.8=1.25$, a 25% length increase. This is a constructed example, not an embryonic measurement. An anatomically defined region is an open system: [cell migration](../../../../cell-migration.md) across its boundary changes its volume even without growth of its resident [biological cells](../../../../biological-cell-split.md). For such a region, separate local volume production from inflow and outflow before interpreting $\dot V$. Likewise, [cell proliferation](../../../../cell-proliferation.md) increases [biological cell](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) number but is not itself a measurement of tissue-volume growth.

The two animals nevertheless share posterior [biological cells](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) with neuromesodermal competence. [clonal lineage tracing](../../../../clonal-lineage-tracing.md) and whole-embryo [light-sheet fluorescence microscopy](../../../../light-sheet-fluorescence-microscopy.md) in the [zebrafish](../../../../zebrafish.md) identified early common neural–mesodermal ancestry and a later posterior population with delayed [biological tissue](../../../../tissue-biology.md) allocation. Tracking the latter population revealed predominantly single-fate contributions over the observed interval rather than the sustained bifated self-renewing behaviour often inferred from the [house mouse](../../../../house-mouse.md). **Conserved competence need not imply conserved realised lineage behaviour.** A clone with both fates can reflect an earlier common ancestor; conversely, a single-fate clone sampled late does not prove that its ancestor was never competent for both.[Abstract and Results: genetic clone tracing, early versus late allocation, and retrospective assignment of tailbud cell fates](../../../../neuromesodermal-progenitors-are-a-conserved-source-of-spinal-cord-with-divergent-growth-dynamics-by-andrea-attardi-and-others.md)

A useful direct comparison would combine [clonal lineage tracing](../../../../clonal-lineage-tracing.md), live nuclear and membrane labels, three-dimensional [biological tissue](../../../../tissue-biology.md) segmentation, [DNA synthesis labeling with EdU](../../../../dna-synthesis-labeling-with-edu.md) and measurements of [biological cell](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) volume and [apoptosis](../../../../apoptosis.md). Follow material landmarks as well as fixed anatomical compartments, so [biological tissue](../../../../tissue-biology.md) influx is distinguishable from expansion. Quantify axial length, cross-sectional area, total [biological tissue](../../../../tissue-biology.md) volume, [biological cell](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) number and displacement fields at matched developmental landmarks, such as [somite](../../../../somite.md) number. Match imaging depth and mounting constraints and include untreated imaged and non-imaged controls, because confinement or [phototoxicity](../../../../phototoxicity.md) can alter movements. Short, stage-specific inhibition of [cell proliferation](../../../../cell-proliferation.md) and independent perturbation of [convergent extension](../../../../convergent-extension.md) would ask which process is necessary during each interval; verify the intended perturbation and exclude [biological cell](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) death or broad developmental arrest. A shortened [embryo](../../../../embryo.md) after a pathway inhibitor alone does not distinguish loss of [biological tissue](../../../../tissue-biology.md) identity, impaired movement and reduced growth.

The most informative conclusion is therefore mechanistic: the [house mouse](../../../../house-mouse.md) couples posterior [progenitor cell](../../../../progenitor-cell.md) maintenance and substantial growth to elongation, whereas the rapidly developing [zebrafish](../../../../zebrafish.md) makes particularly extensive use of convergence, rearrangement and delayed allocation, followed by growth of axial [biological tissues](../../../../tissue-biology.md). Both must coordinate this supply and movement with [somitogenesis](../../../../somitogenesis.md), so equal final body architecture can emerge through different cellular dynamics.
