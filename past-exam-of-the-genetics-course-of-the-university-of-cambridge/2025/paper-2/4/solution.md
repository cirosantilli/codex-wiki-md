↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

**Time patterns an axis by controlling which signals [biological cells](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) encounter, how long they interpret them, and when a changing regulatory state becomes fixed in space.** Repeated segments, regional identities and competence windows are distinct temporal problems. An [embryo](../../../../embryo.md) may solve them with interacting timers; there is no requirement for one universal developmental clock.

In [vertebrate](../../../../vertebrate.md) [somitogenesis](../../../../somitogenesis.md), [presomitic mesoderm](../../../../presomitic-mesoderm.md) shows cyclic [gene expression](../../../../gene-expression.md), including [Hes/Her transcription factors](../../../../hes-her-transcription-factor.md). Delayed [negative feedback](../../../../negative-feedback.md) can produce repeated activity, while [Notch signalling](../../../../notch-signalling.md) couples neighbouring [biological cells](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) so their phases remain coherent. In a [clock and wavefront model](../../../../clock-and-wavefront-model.md), these oscillations meet a moving maturation boundary associated with changing [FGF signalling](../../../../fgf-signalling.md), [Wnt signalling](../../../../wnt-signalling.md) and anterior differentiation. A boundary is fixed once per effective clock cycle. For a steady relative wavefront speed $v$ and period $T$, the model predicts

$$
s\simeq vT,\qquad N\simeq \frac{\Delta t}{T},
$$

where $s$ is segment spacing and $N$ is the number formed during an active interval $\Delta t$. The speed is relative to the [biological tissue](../../../../tissue-biology.md), not necessarily the measured displacement of the tail tip. Units check: length/time multiplied by time gives length. These are approximations: changing [biological tissue](../../../../tissue-biology.md) strain, gradients or periods can invalidate constant-parameter predictions.

The classic chick evidence distinguished periodic expression from movement. Paired halves of posterior [biological tissue](../../../../tissue-biology.md) were compared after different culture intervals, revealing recurrent [hairy gene](../../../../hairy-gene.md) expression patterns with a period matching [somite](../../../../somite.md) formation. The reported approximately 90-minute period belongs to those chick conditions and should not be assigned to every [vertebrate](../../../../vertebrate.md). A travelling expression wave need not be a travelling cohort of [biological cells](../../../../biological-cell-split.md).[Cell 91, pp. 639–648; cyclic c-hairy1 expression and comparison of temporal expression patterns](../../../../avian-hairy-gene-expression-identifies-a-molecular-clock-linked-to-vertebrate-segmentation-and-somitogenesis-by-isabel-palmeirim-and-others.md)

Tests should separately alter clock period, intercellular coupling and [biological tissue](../../../../tissue-biology.md) maturation. An experimentally lengthened period predicts wider spacing if relative wavefront speed remains constant; slowing maturation can offset that effect. Disrupting coupling may produce irregular boundaries even while isolated [biological cells](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) continue oscillating. Record live reporters together with [biological cell](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) tracking, rather than inferring a clock solely from differently staged fixed [embryos](../../../../embryo.md). Matched temperature, developmental stage and reporter maturation are essential controls: a slow fluorescent reporter can smooth or phase-shift the underlying [transcription](../../../../transcription-biology.md).

Regional identity has another temporal component. [Hox genes](../../../../hox-gene.md) are often activated in temporal order in posterior [progenitor cells](../../../../progenitor-cell.md); earlier descendants leave with a different regulatory history from later descendants, helping produce nested anterior–posterior expression domains. Experiments in chick linked sequential [Hoxb gene](../../../../hoxb-gene.md) activation to the timing of mesodermal ingression, connecting temporal activation to spatial deployment.[Nature 442, pp. 568–571: temporal activation in epiblast precursors and effects on ingression](../../../../collinear-activation-of-hoxb-genes-during-gastrulation-is-linked-to-mesoderm-cell-ingression-by-tadahiro-iimura-and-olivier-pourquie.md) The [Hox gene](../../../../hox-gene.md) programme assigns regional character, whereas the [segmentation clock](../../../../segmentation-clock.md) chiefly organises repetition. A [somite](../../../../somite.md) boundary and a cervical-versus-thoracic identity are therefore not the same output. Temporal collinearity is also not a universal property of all [Hox gene](../../../../hox-gene.md) clusters in all animals.

Signal duration supplies a further mechanism. A recipient can integrate a time-dependent [morphogen](../../../../morphogen.md) exposure. In an illustrative threshold model, let $c(t)$ denote local signal activity and define

$$
I(t)=\int_0^t c(\tau)\,\mathrm d\tau.
$$

A decision at $I=I_*$ depends on both strength and duration. This model is a hypothesis to test, not a claim that every [morphogen](../../../../morphogen.md) is integrated linearly. Give equal-duration pulses of different strengths and equal-total-exposure pulses with different timing, then assay final [cell fate](../../../../cell-fate.md). If equal integrals yield different outcomes, adaptation, changing competence or threshold memory matters. Pulse-and-washout experiments during [gastrulation](../../../../gastrulation.md) can therefore separate an early induction requirement from a later maintenance requirement.

In the long-germ insect [Drosophila melanogaster](../../../../drosophila-melanogaster.md), most trunk segments are initially patterned together rather than added one by one from a posterior zone. Maternal inputs feed [gap genes](../../../../gap-gene.md), then [pair-rule genes](../../../../pair-rule-gene.md), then [segment-polarity genes](../../../../segment-polarity-gene.md). This is still a dynamic process: domains shift and the regulatory interactions change with developmental stage. The temporally regulated factor [Odd-paired](../../../../odd-paired.md) reorganises the [pair-rule gene regulatory network](../../../../pair-rule-gene-regulatory-network.md), enabling a transition from double-segment to single-segment periodicity. Loss of that temporal input prevents the normal network transition even though the earlier spatial information exists.[eLife 5, e18215; Results: Odd-paired-dependent network changes and frequency doubling](../../../../odd-paired-controls-frequency-doubling-in-drosophila-segmentation-by-altering-the-pair-rule-gene-regulatory-network-by-erik-clark-and-michael-akam.md)

In the short-germ beetle [Tribolium castaneum](../../../../tribolium-castaneum.md), much segmentation proceeds sequentially from a posterior [segment addition zone](../../../../segment-addition-zone.md). Temporal changes are converted to positions as [biological tissue](../../../../tissue-biology.md) leaves that zone. Comparative expression and perturbation evidence implicates a sequence involving [Caudal](../../../../caudal.md), [Dichaete](../../../../dichaete.md) and [Odd-paired](../../../../odd-paired.md), with their distributions associated with successive segmentation phases. This links the apparently simultaneous and sequential modes through changing regulatory competence, without establishing that the complete insect and [vertebrate](../../../../vertebrate.md) oscillators are homologous.[Development 145, dev155580; Results: candidate timing factors and comparison between Drosophila and Tribolium](../../../../evidence-for-the-temporal-regulation-of-insect-segmentation-by-a-conserved-sequence-of-transcription-factors-by-erik-clark-and-andrew-d-peel.md)

Finally, [heterochrony](../../../../heterochrony.md) can change how many repetitions fit within the period of axial production, which identities are assigned to them, or the order in which [biological tissues](../../../../tissue-biology.md) become competent. These possibilities require separate tests. Counting extra segments cannot by itself distinguish a faster clock from prolonged posterior activity; shifting a regional boundary cannot by itself distinguish altered [Hox gene](../../../../hox-gene.md) activation from altered [biological cell](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) deployment. The strongest explanation of temporal patterning therefore connects a measured history, a manipulated timing variable, and a lasting spatial or functional outcome.
