<h1 id="7/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [7](../7.md)

**The [New Head hypothesis](../../../../new-head-hypothesis.md) offers a plausible developmental explanation for [vertebrate](../../../../vertebrate.md) cranial innovation: migratory neural crest [biological cells](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) helped build a feeding and sensory apparatus that could diversify.** Its strongest form is a hypothesis about integration of several innovations, not a claim that [neural crest](../../../../neural-crest.md) alone produced every part of the [vertebrate](../../../../vertebrate.md) head or directly proves evolutionary success.

[neural crest](../../../../neural-crest.md) arises near the border of the neural and non-neural [ectoderm](../../../../ectoderm.md). Cells undergo an [epithelial-to-mesenchymal transition](../../../../epithelial-to-mesenchymal-transition.md), migrate and generate diverse derivatives. Cranial populations contribute substantially to facial and pharyngeal [cartilage](../../../../cartilage.md), [bone](../../../../bone.md) and connective [biological tissues](../../../../tissue-biology.md); other populations supply peripheral [neurons](../../../../neuron.md), [glial cells](../../../../glial-cell.md) and pigment [biological cells](../../../../biological-cell-split.md). These are regionally restricted potentials, not a claim that every crest [biological cell](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) makes every derivative. Importantly, [neural crest](../../../../neural-crest.md) remains ectodermal in origin even when it makes [biological tissues](../../../../tissue-biology.md) often associated with [mesoderm](../../../../mesoderm.md).

The hypothesis proposed by [Carl Gans](../../../../carl-gans.md) and [R. Glenn Northcutt](../../../../r-glenn-northcutt.md) linked the new head to a transition from passive suspension feeding toward more active food acquisition, with an expanded cranial skeleton, sensory systems and muscularised pharyngeal apparatus. [neural crest](../../../../neural-crest.md), [ectodermal placodes](../../../../ectodermal-placode.md) and mesodermal muscle contributions were complementary. A support skeleton allows feeding surfaces and sensory organs to be positioned; sensory organs guide action; muscles supply force. Their integration gives a more convincing functional explanation than a list of crest derivatives alone.[Science 220, pp. 268–273; proposed relationship between cranial embryonic sources and active predation](../../../../neural-crest-and-the-origin-of-vertebrates-a-new-head-by-carl-gans-and-r-glenn-northcutt.md)

Several distinctions keep this argument anatomically accurate. The [brain](../../../../brain.md) largely develops from the [neural tube](../../../../neural-tube.md); [ectodermal placodes](../../../../ectodermal-placode.md) contribute important cranial sensory components; much cranial muscle comes from [mesoderm](../../../../mesoderm.md). [neural crest](../../../../neural-crest.md) provides connective and skeletal organisation around those [biological tissues](../../../../tissue-biology.md) and contributes to their interactions. The [pharyngeal arches](../../../../pharyngeal-arch.md) are multicellular constructions involving [neural crest](../../../../neural-crest.md), [mesoderm](../../../../mesoderm.md), [ectoderm](../../../../ectoderm.md) and [endoderm](../../../../endoderm.md). Thus loss of cranial [neural crest](../../../../neural-crest.md) can secondarily disrupt [biological tissues](../../../../tissue-biology.md) it does not itself become. Fate mapping distinguishes cellular contribution, while selective ablation and rescue test developmental necessity and interaction.

There is direct experimental support for a role in diversification. Reciprocal [cell transplantation](../../../../cell-transplantation.md) of prospective beak [neural crest](../../../../neural-crest.md) between quail and duck shifted beak [morphology](../../../../morphology-biology.md) toward the donor [species](../../../../species.md) and altered nearby host [biological tissues](../../../../tissue-biology.md). This connects intrinsic properties of the crest population with instructions to its environment, explaining how changes in one interacting population can reshape a complex organ. Same-species grafts, developmental-stage matching and permanent donor labels are needed to distinguish patterning information from surgical effects or simple replacement. The result supports a mechanism for morphological variation; it does not by itself establish which ancestral feeding behaviour was favoured by [natural selection](../../../../natural-selection.md).[Science 299, pp. 565–568; reciprocal quail–duck grafts, donor-like beaks and effects on host tissue](../../../../the-cellular-and-molecular-origins-of-beak-morphology-by-richard-a-schneider-and-jill-a-helms.md)

Comparative evidence also qualifies an abrupt invention narrative. Neural-border regulatory and sensory programmes have antecedents outside [vertebrates](../../../../vertebrate.md). In [Ciona](../../../../ciona.md), a pigment-cell lineage with parts of a crest-like regulatory programme can acquire migratory behaviour after experimental manipulation. Such a rudiment supports stepwise assembly of properties; a migrating pigment precursor is not automatically equivalent to the full multipotent, skeletogenic [vertebrate](../../../../vertebrate.md) [neural crest](../../../../neural-crest.md). Compare origin, regulatory dependencies, migration and derivative range separately.[Nature 492, pp. 104–107; Ciona pigment-cell regulatory programme and experimentally induced migration](../../../../identification-of-a-neural-crest-rudiment-in-a-non-vertebrate-chordate-by-philip-barron-abitua-and-others.md)

Jawless [vertebrates](../../../../vertebrate.md) are another important logical control. Their existence prevents equating the origin of [neural crest](../../../../neural-crest.md) with the later origin of jaws: cranial contributions preceded the particular jawed-vertebrate feeding apparatus. Later modifications of an inherited developmental system can support new structures without repeating its original evolutionary origin. Conversely, retention of homologous [genes](../../../../gene.md) does not establish identical [biological cell](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) types; comparative experiments must test how those [genes](../../../../gene.md) interact and what the [biological cells](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) actually produce.

A strong research programme would combine [lineage tracing](../../../../lineage-tracing.md) and selective perturbation across [vertebrate](../../../../vertebrate.md) and non-vertebrate [chordates](../../../../chordate.md), compare homologous regulatory elements and ask whether reconstructed changes alter migration, skeletal competence or [biological tissue](../../../../tissue-biology.md) interactions. [Fossils](../../../../fossil.md) constrain when hard cranial structures appeared, but rarely identify the embryonic source directly. Modern ablation establishes a present-day requirement, not the sequence of ancestral events. Likewise, [species](../../../../species.md) richness is influenced by many ecological and historical factors, so it is not a direct experimental readout of one developmental innovation.

The defensible conclusion is that **neural crest supplied a versatile developmental substrate for cranial construction and diversification, acting with placodes and other [biological tissues](../../../../tissue-biology.md)**. Experimental grafting and comparative developmental evidence make that causal contribution substantial. The specific ecological transition proposed in the [New Head hypothesis](../../../../new-head-hypothesis.md) remains an evolutionary reconstruction whose details require evidence beyond the existence of crest derivatives.
