<h1 id="5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

**Identify specification by changing a [biological cell](../../../../biological-cell-split.md)'s environment and asking what it can still become.** A normal [fate map](../../../../fate-map.md) reports what [biological cells](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) do in an intact [embryo](../../../../embryo.md); it does not reveal their developmental potential. [cell specification](../../../../cell-specification.md) is operationally demonstrated when a [biological cell](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) adopts a fate in an appropriate neutral environment, whereas [cell determination](../../../../cell-determination.md) is the stronger ability to retain that fate in a conflicting but viable environment. [Cell differentiation](../../../../cellular-differentiation.md) is the execution of specialised properties. None should be inferred from one early marker alone.

Begin with a stage-resolved reference. Image [embryos](../../../../embryo.md) from [fertilization](../../../../fertilization.md) through the first stable [biological tissue](../../../../tissue-biology.md) identities, using minimally perturbing nuclear and membrane labels. Mark individual [blastomeres](../../../../blastomere.md) by [photoconversion](../../../../photoconversion.md) or inducible [genetic lineage tracing](../../../../genetic-lineage-tracing.md) and follow all descendants. Combine this with spatial marker panels and [single-cell RNA sequencing](../../../../single-cell-rna-sequencing.md) at selected stages to distinguish transient states from stable endpoints. Register positions, division orientation, neighbours and final [cell fate](../../../../cell-fate.md). Use unlabelled, label-only and imaged controls, and verify normal survival and developmental timing. Independent [embryos](../../../../embryo.md), not multiple [biological cells](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) from one [embryo](../../../../embryo.md), are the primary [biological replicates](../../../../biological-replicate.md).

Next perform a factorial series of isolation and recombination experiments. Isolate identified [blastomeres](../../../../blastomere.md) at progressively later stages and culture them separately in several defined media chosen to maintain viability without intentionally imposing a candidate fate. Use intact [embryos](../../../../embryo.md), sham-manipulated [embryos](../../../../embryo.md) and known committed [biological tissue](../../../../tissue-biology.md) as controls. If an isolated [biological cell](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) repeatedly develops according to its normal fate, it is already specified under those conditions. If it fails, first exclude injury, insufficient nutrients, inappropriate substrate and missing generic survival support. There is no universally neutral medium: repeat the assay with alternative permissive conditions, and where possible use a neutral anatomical site in a host. Agreement across environments makes the inference stronger.

Test an [autonomous specification](../../../../autonomous-specification.md) hypothesis by tracking asymmetric maternal [RNA](../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md), [proteins](../../../../protein-split.md) or [organelles](../../../../organelle.md) through cleavage and asking whether their inheritance predicts fate after isolation. Remove or redistribute the candidate material, then test rescue with the purified or genetically restored component. Transfer it to an otherwise non-producing [biological cell](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) to test sufficiency. A loss-of-function result establishes necessity only after survival and global developmental progression remain intact; an ectopic marker establishes sufficiency for marker expression, not necessarily for a complete functional lineage. Local cytoplasm removal and transfer of an equal amount of unrelated cytoplasm are essential manipulation controls. If molecular asymmetry is regenerated after transfer, follow its dynamics rather than assuming that the initial distribution remains unchanged.

Test [conditional specification](../../../../conditional-specification.md) by altering contacts while preserving the isolated [biological cell](../../../../biological-cell-split.md)'s physiological condition. Remove an identified neighbour before the putative induction window, recombine the recipient with that neighbour, and compare unrelated [biological tissue](../../../../tissue-biology.md). Rescue by the correct neighbour implicates an interaction. A permeable barrier or conditioned medium can distinguish a diffusible signal from a requirement for direct contact, but failure of conditioned medium is inconclusive if the signal is unstable, matrix-bound or requires a local concentration. A positive result through a barrier must also exclude passage of intact [biological cells](../../../../biological-cell-split.md). Label sender and receiver separately so recipient conversion is not confused with expansion of contaminating donor [biological cells](../../../../biological-cell-split.md).

Build a causal sender–signal–receiver chain. Delete a candidate [ligand](../../../../ligand-biochemistry.md) specifically in the sender, delete its [receptor](../../../../receptor-biochemistry.md) specifically in the recipient, and monitor an immediate pathway reporter before the terminal fate readout. Ligand addition should rescue [ligand](../../../../ligand-biochemistry.md) loss when the receptor is present; it should fail after verified receptor loss. Activated downstream machinery in the recipient can test bypass. Demonstrate a dose range that changes fate without changing survival, and use pulse-and-washout experiments to map a [competence window](../../../../competence-window.md). Loss of an endpoint after late treatment may indicate maintenance or differentiation failure rather than the original specification event. Independent genetic and pharmacological interventions reduce the risk of an off-target explanation.

Perform reciprocal heterotopic [cell transplantation](../../../../cell-transplantation.md) at several stages. Move a labelled prospective A [biological cell](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) into a B environment and a B [biological cell](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) into an A environment, with same-position grafts as surgical controls. Verify integration, survival and exposure to the host signal. If an early donor adopts the host fate, its potential is conditional at that stage. If a later donor retains its original fate while the host remains permissive and the graft survives, that supports [cell determination](../../../../cell-determination.md). Retention in a host that never contacts or signals to the graft is uninformative. Follow mature [morphology](../../../../morphology-biology.md) and function as well as markers; a hybrid marker state can represent incomplete conversion rather than either stable fate.

Regulative capacity needs another test. Ablate a mapped precursor and trace the source of replacement [biological cells](../../../../biological-cell-split.md). Normal final anatomy is stronger evidence of [regulative development](../../../../regulative-development.md) if a previously distinct neighbour demonstrably adopts the missing fate; it is weaker if a few unablated members of the original lineage simply proliferate. Include sham ablation and damage-matched ablation elsewhere, quantify [biological cell](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) death and measure final [biological cell](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) numbers. Conversely, persistent loss of a structure after ablation does not prove [mosaic development](../../../../mosaic-development.md): the [embryo](../../../../embryo.md) may have lost an essential signal, physical scaffold or sufficient [biological cell](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) mass rather than an irreplaceable determinant.

A third mechanism, [syncytial specification](../../../../syncytial-specification.md), must be tested if early divisions produce many [cell nuclei](../../../../cell-nucleus.md) in shared [cytoplasm](../../../../cytoplasm.md) before [cellularization](../../../../cellularization.md). Image membranes to establish whether true cellular boundaries exist. Map nuclear position, local [morphogen](../../../../morphogen.md) activity and nascent [transcription](../../../../transcription-biology.md); move [cell nuclei](../../../../cell-nucleus.md) relative to the gradient or shift the signal source while retaining nuclear identity. If equivalent [cell nuclei](../../../../cell-nucleus.md) adopt different programmes according to position in shared [cytoplasm](../../../../cytoplasm.md), that supports positional specification before [biological cell](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) boundaries. Perturbing [cellularization](../../../../cellularization.md) alone is a poor test because it can damage several later processes.

An explicit interpretation matrix prevents overclaiming: retention after isolation supports specification; switching after a viable heterotopic graft shows continuing plasticity; retention after a verified conflicting graft supports determination; fate following a manipulated inherited determinant supports autonomy; and fate following a manipulated neighbour signal supports induction. These modes can coexist. An asymmetric maternal determinant might set competence for an external signal, and an induced fate can later become autonomous through [positive feedback](../../../../positive-feedback.md) in a [gene regulatory network](../../../../gene-regulatory-network.md). **The final product should be a map of mechanisms and commitment times for particular lineages, not a single label for the entire organism.**
