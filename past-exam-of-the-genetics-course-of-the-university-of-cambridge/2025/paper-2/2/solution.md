<h1 id="2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

**The joint eggshell and embryonic phenotype makes defective communication between the oocyte and follicle [biological cells](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) a strong hypothesis, but it does not identify the mutated [gene](../../../../gene.md).** In [Drosophila melanogaster](../../../../drosophila-melanogaster.md), an eggshell is produced by maternal [follicle cells](../../../../follicle-cell.md), whereas the [embryo](../../../../embryo.md) interprets maternal positional information later. The experimental aim is to determine inheritance, the [biological tissue](../../../../tissue-biology.md) requiring the wild-type product, the first abnormal molecular event, and the causal sequence connecting it to ventralisation.

First establish a reproducible [phenotype](../../../../phenotype.md). Backcross the stock to control its [genetic background](../../../../genetic-background.md); score independent mothers and several egg collections at matched temperature and maternal age. Image dorsal appendage number, position and fusion, and examine embryonic [insect cuticle](../../../../insect-cuticle.md) and [biological tissue](../../../../tissue-biology.md) markers. Record unhatched and fertilised eggs separately. In [embryos](../../../../embryo.md), quantify the spatial extent of [Twist](../../../../twist.md) and [Snail](../../../../snail.md) expression, the distribution of nuclear [Dorsal protein](../../../../dorsal-protein.md), and dorsal [Decapentaplegic](../../../../decapentaplegic.md) expression. Expanded ventral molecular identities, rather than lethality or abnormal shape alone, establish embryonic ventralisation. Appendage defects alone are insufficient because their formation has additional regulatory requirements.

Perform reciprocal crosses between mutant and wild-type flies and score both the mothers' eggs and the offspring [genotypes](../../../../genotype.md). For a recessive [maternal-effect mutation](../../../../maternal-effect-mutation.md), homozygous mutant mothers produce affected eggs and [embryos](../../../../embryo.md) even when the father is wild type; heterozygous mothers can produce normally patterned [embryos](../../../../embryo.md) that are themselves homozygous mutant. A [zygotic-effect mutation](../../../../zygotic-effect-mutation.md) instead tracks the [embryo](../../../../embryo.md)'s [genotype](../../../../genotype.md). Test [dominance](../../../../dominance-genetics.md) with heterozygous mothers, score [penetrance](../../../../penetrance.md) and severity, and distinguish maternal rescue from a paternal contribution by following the next generation. Do not impose a simple Mendelian ratio if the lesion is sex-linked, semidominant or affects fertility.

Map the [mutation](../../../../mutation.md) using [genetic linkage](../../../../genetic-linkage.md), informative [genetic markers](../../../../genetic-marker.md) and deficiencies, followed by [DNA sequencing](../../../../dna-sequencing.md). [complementation testing](../../../../complementation-testing.md) against known candidates can distinguish a new [allele](../../../../allele.md) of an established [genetic locus](../../../../genetic-locus.md) from a different locus, although failure to complement needs confirmation because interacting loci and deletions can complicate interpretation. Compare the mutant over a deficiency with the homozygote, and test [gene](../../../../gene.md) dosage to distinguish reduced function from a dominant activating or interfering effect. The decisive causal package is rescue by a suitably expressed wild-type genomic copy, recreation of the candidate sequence change in a clean background, and reversal by precise repair. Check that rescue restores both eggshell and embryonic pattern, rather than merely viability.

The normal information flow supplies a series of discriminating readouts. Localised [Gurken](../../../../gurken.md) from the dorsal-anterior [oocyte](../../../../oocyte.md) activates the [epidermal growth factor receptor](../../../../epidermal-growth-factor-receptor.md) in neighbouring dorsal [follicle cells](../../../../follicle-cell.md). This represses [Pipe](../../../../pipe.md) there, leaving its activity ventrally restricted.[Cell 95, pp. 471–481; follicle-cell pipe expression and experimental repatterning of embryonic polarity](../../../../spatially-restricted-expression-of-pipe-in-the-drosophila-egg-chamber-defines-embryonic-dorsal-ventral-polarity-by-jonaki-sen-and-others.md) The modified extracellular environment biases a [proteolytic cascade](../../../../proteolytic-cascade.md) that activates [Spätzle](../../../../spatzle.md); this [ligand](../../../../ligand-biochemistry.md) activates [Toll receptor](../../../../toll-receptor.md) ventrally. Loss of the inhibitory action of [Cactus protein](../../../../cactus-protein.md) allows high ventral nuclear [Dorsal protein](../../../../dorsal-protein.md), which activates ventral targets and represses dorsal ones. Therefore loss of the early dorsalising input can produce expanded ventral identity, whereas ordinary loss of the downstream [Toll receptor](../../../../toll-receptor.md) pathway produces dorsalisation. [genetic mosaics](../../../../genetic-mosaic.md) established the distinct ovarian requirements for [Gurken](../../../../gurken.md) and the [epidermal growth factor receptor](../../../../epidermal-growth-factor-receptor.md).[Abstract; Cell 49, pp. 699–707: germline mosaics distinguish gurken and torpedo requirements](../../../../germ-line-and-soma-cooperate-during-oogenesis-to-establish-the-dorsoventral-pattern-of-egg-shell-and-embryo-in-drosophila-melanogaster-by-trudi-schupbach.md)

Use [genetic mosaics](../../../../genetic-mosaic.md) to make mutant germ [biological cells](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) within wild-type ovarian soma, and mutant [follicle cell](../../../../follicle-cell.md) clones around wild-type germ [biological cells](../../../../biological-cell-split.md). Mark clones independently and verify their size and location. A requirement in the maternal germline supports defects in [Gurken](../../../../gurken.md) production, localisation or secretion; a requirement in dorsal [follicle cells](../../../../follicle-cell.md) supports reception or interpretation of that signal. Include wild-type clones generated by the same procedure and tissue-specific wild-type rescue. If both compartments contribute, test whether the locus has two functions rather than forcing it into one category.

Then localise the first defect during [oogenesis](../../../../oogenesis.md). Determine the [oocyte](../../../../oocyte.md) nuclear position and [Gurken](../../../../gurken.md) [messenger RNA](../../../../messenger-rna.md) and [protein](../../../../protein-split.md) distributions; measure receptor-pathway activity by phosphorylated [ERK](../../../../erk.md) staining and map [Pipe](../../../../pipe.md) expression. Correct [Gurken](../../../../gurken.md) localisation with absent dorsal receptor output favours reception or downstream transduction. Reduced localised [Gurken](../../../../gurken.md) with an otherwise responsive receptor pathway favours defective [ligand](../../../../ligand-biochemistry.md) production or positioning. Expanded [Pipe](../../../../pipe.md) despite normal dorsal receptor activity points further downstream. Use sense-probe or probe-omission controls where appropriate, validated [antibodies](../../../../antibody.md), common staining batches and known pathway mutants. Temporal staging matters: a late normal-looking pattern cannot exclude an earlier transient failure.

Apply [epistasis](../../../../epistasis.md) and bypass tests with explicit predictions. If the lesion acts upstream of receptor activation, activated receptor in [follicle cells](../../../../follicle-cell.md) should restore dorsal pathway output and repress ectopic [Pipe](../../../../pipe.md), provided downstream machinery is intact. If ectopic [Pipe](../../../../pipe.md) causes embryonic ventralisation, removing [Pipe](../../../../pipe.md) should suppress that ventralisation and usually yield the downstream loss-of-function dorsalised state; it need not repair the eggshell. Similarly, remove [Toll receptor](../../../../toll-receptor.md) or maternal [Dorsal protein](../../../../dorsal-protein.md) function to test whether embryonic ventralisation requires the normal ventral effector pathway. Persistence after a verified downstream block demands an alternative mechanism or multiple lesions. Conversely, an activated [Toll receptor](../../../../toll-receptor.md) allele bypasses many upstream inputs and can ventralise an [embryo](../../../../embryo.md) while leaving the earlier eggshell programme unaffected. **Suppression of an embryonic defect and restoration of the eggshell are different rescue criteria.**

Finally, quantify nuclear-to-cytoplasmic [Dorsal protein](../../../../dorsal-protein.md) around the embryonic circumference, alongside [Twist](../../../../twist.md), [Snail](../../../../snail.md) and [Decapentaplegic](../../../../decapentaplegic.md) boundaries. Normal ovarian [Gurken](../../../../gurken.md) signalling with uniform high nuclear [Dorsal protein](../../../../dorsal-protein.md) instead suggests ectopic extracellular activation, constitutive [Toll receptor](../../../../toll-receptor.md) activity or loss of its intracellular restraint. A reproducible result from tissue-specific rescue, first-defect imaging and reciprocal [epistasis](../../../../epistasis.md) would distinguish these explanations. Neither resemblance to a known mutant nor a candidate sequence variant alone establishes how this novel lesion acts.
