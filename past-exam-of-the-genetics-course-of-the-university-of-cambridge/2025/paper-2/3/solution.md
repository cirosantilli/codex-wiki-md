<h1 id="3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

**Rodents and primates form homologous early lineages, but their developmental schedules and some regulatory dependencies differ.** The [house mouse](../../../../house-mouse.md) is a useful rodent reference; human and [marmoset](../../../../marmoset.md) [embryos](../../../../embryo.md) provide primate comparisons. Neither one rodent nor one primate represents every member of its group. Comparisons should align developmental state as well as time since [fertilization](../../../../fertilization.md).

Both begin with [cleavage divisions](../../../../cleavage-division.md), initially using maternal [RNA](../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) and [proteins](../../../../protein-split.md), and then undergo [zygotic genome activation](../../../../zygotic-genome-activation.md). The major activation wave occurs at the two-cell stage in the [house mouse](../../../../house-mouse.md), whereas in humans it is spread over the four- to eight-cell interval with a prominent eight-cell programme; [marmoset](../../../../marmoset.md) activation is likewise later than in the [house mouse](../../../../house-mouse.md). Minor earlier [transcription](../../../../transcription-biology.md) makes an absolute statement that the [embryo](../../../../embryo.md) is transcriptionally silent before these stages misleading. Comparative [single-cell RNA sequencing](../../../../single-cell-rna-sequencing.md) identifies both conserved lineage programmes and species-specific regulators, including differences between human and [marmoset](../../../../marmoset.md) [embryos](../../../../embryo.md).[Development 145, dev167833; early cleavage-stage comparisons and lineage-specific transcription-factor analyses](../../../../single-cell-transcriptome-analysis-of-human-marmoset-and-mouse-embryos-reveals-common-and-divergent-features-of-preimplantation-development-by-thorsten-boroviak-and-others.md)

The timing difference can be demonstrated with stage-resolved nascent-[RNA](../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) labeling, intronic [RNA](../../../../ribonucleic-acid.md) measurements and [transcription](../../../../transcription-biology.md) inhibition, alongside maternal-transcript decay measurements. Increased total transcript abundance alone cannot distinguish new synthesis from altered stability. An inhibitor-induced cleavage arrest is informative only if uptake, target engagement and viability are checked; maternal stores may sustain divisions after inhibition. Species-specific rates of division also mean that two-cell versus eight-cell activation cannot be translated into a fixed hour difference without direct measurement.

The first major lineage separation distinguishes outer [trophectoderm](../../../../trophectoderm.md), which contributes to extraembryonic support, from the [inner cell mass](../../../../inner-cell-mass.md). In the [house mouse](../../../../house-mouse.md), [biological cell](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) position and apical [cell polarity](../../../../cell-polarity.md) regulate [Hippo signalling](../../../../hippo-signalling.md): inner [biological cells](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) restrain nuclear [YAP](../../../../yap.md), while outer polarised [biological cells](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) permit [YAP](../../../../yap.md)–[TEAD](../../../../tead.md) activity and expression of [trophectoderm](../../../../trophectoderm.md) regulators such as [CDX2](../../../../cdx2.md). [OCT4](../../../../oct4.md), [Sox2](../../../../sox2.md) and [NANOG](../../../../nanog.md) participate in pluripotent identity. Human [embryos](../../../../embryo.md) also use polarity-dependent regulation and form the same broad lineage distinction, but marker onset and restriction are less interchangeable with mouse stages. A single [OCT4](../../../../oct4.md)-positive or [CDX2](../../../../cdx2.md)-positive [biological cell](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) is therefore insufficient to classify all [species](../../../../species.md) at every early stage; use multiple markers, position and developmental trajectory.[Nature 587, pp. 443–447; comparative polarity, Hippo-pathway activity and trophectoderm initiation](../../../../initiation-of-a-conserved-trophectoderm-program-in-human-cow-and-mouse-embryos-by-claudia-gerri-and-others.md)

The [inner cell mass](../../../../inner-cell-mass.md) subsequently separates into [epiblast](../../../../epiblast.md), which supplies the [embryo](../../../../embryo.md) proper, and [hypoblast](../../../../hypoblast.md), also called [primitive endoderm](../../../../hypoblast.md), which supplies extraembryonic endodermal [biological tissues](../../../../tissue-biology.md). In the [house mouse](../../../../house-mouse.md), initially overlapping [NANOG](../../../../nanog.md) and [GATA6](../../../../gata6.md) expression resolves into intermingled prospective [epiblast](../../../../epiblast.md) and [hypoblast](../../../../hypoblast.md) [biological cells](../../../../biological-cell-split.md), followed by sorting into their appropriate positions. Thus initial specification is not simply imposed by which [biological cell](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) first contacts the cavity. [FGF4](../../../../fgf4.md) from the prospective [epiblast](../../../../epiblast.md) acts through [FGF signalling](../../../../fgf-signalling.md) and [ERK](../../../../erk.md) to promote the [hypoblast](../../../../hypoblast.md) programme, including later [GATA4](../../../../gata4.md) and [SOX17](../../../../sox17.md) expression. Reduced pathway output favours [epiblast](../../../../epiblast.md); increased output can favour [hypoblast](../../../../hypoblast.md). Loss of the [Grb2](../../../../grb2.md) adaptor provides genetic evidence for the importance of this pathway in mouse lineage segregation.[Developmental Cell 10, pp. 615–624: Grb2 mutants, NANOG/GATA6 segregation and lineage allocation](../../../../early-lineage-segregation-between-epiblast-and-primitive-endoderm-in-mouse-blastocysts-through-the-grb2-mapk-pathway-by-claire-chazaud-and-others.md)

For the evidence available at the June 2025 examination, the central contrast was that [hypoblast](../../../../hypoblast.md) markers persisted in human [embryos](../../../../embryo.md) exposed to [FGF receptor](../../../../fgf-receptor.md) or [MEK](../../../../mek.md) inhibitors under conditions that strongly redirected rodent [inner cell mass](../../../../inner-cell-mass.md) allocation. This supported a difference in experimentally demonstrated pathway dependence, rather than proof that humans never use [FGF signalling](../../../../fgf-signalling.md). Inhibition at one node may be incomplete, signals can converge downstream, and an already specified [hypoblast](../../../../hypoblast.md) [biological cell](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) can persist after an initiating input is removed. The original comparative inhibitor experiments should therefore be interpreted in their stage and culture context.[Figure 2 and accompanying Results: human versus mouse and rat responses to FGF/MEK inhibition](../../../../human-hypoblast-formation-is-not-dependent-on-fgf-signalling-by-mila-roode-and-others.md)

There are additional primate features. Human [epiblast](../../../../epiblast.md) expresses a distinctive pluripotency network, with [KLF17](../../../../klf17.md) among its useful markers, and [Nodal signalling](../../../../nodal-signalling.md) has been implicated in maintaining its [NANOG](../../../../nanog.md) programme. Perturbation of [TGF-beta signalling](../../../../tgf-beta-signalling.md) in cultured human [embryos](../../../../embryo.md) reduced [NANOG](../../../../nanog.md), supporting an input into human [epiblast](../../../../epiblast.md) maintenance; it does not show that this pathway alone specifies the lineage. Nor should mouse stem-cell dependence on [LIF signalling](../../../../lif-signalling.md), or human cultured stem-cell dependence on particular media, be substituted for an in-embryo lineage-specification experiment.[Results: human epiblast-enriched transcription and TGF-beta signalling inhibition in human embryos](../../../../defining-the-three-cell-lineages-of-the-human-blastocyst-by-single-cell-rna-seq-by-paul-blakeley-and-others.md)

Morphogenesis also diverges. The [house mouse](../../../../house-mouse.md) establishes a cup-shaped postimplantation [epiblast](../../../../epiblast.md) within an egg-cylinder organisation; primates form a flatter embryonic disc, with different extraembryonic geometry and early [amnion](../../../../amnion.md) development. Such geometry changes which [biological tissues](../../../../tissue-biology.md) can deliver signals to competent recipients. It is therefore unsafe to transfer a signalling-source map directly between [species](../../../../species.md) merely because the [ligand](../../../../ligand-biochemistry.md) and responding [transcription factors](../../../../transcription-factor.md) are conserved.

A rigorous comparison would use several developmental windows, independent inhibitors at receptor and downstream levels, genetic perturbations where feasible, [ligand](../../../../ligand-biochemistry.md) addition, washout and rescue. Measure pathway activity inside the [embryo](../../../../embryo.md), not only in a cultured reference [cell line](../../../../cell-line.md). Count total [inner cell mass](../../../../inner-cell-mass.md) [biological cells](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) and multiple lineage markers, and score [cell proliferation](../../../../cell-proliferation.md) and [apoptosis](../../../../apoptosis.md). A smaller [hypoblast](../../../../hypoblast.md) population could reflect failed specification, death or delayed maturation. Analyse [embryos](../../../../embryo.md) as independent [biological replicates](../../../../biological-replicate.md), with [biological cells](../../../../biological-cell-split.md) nested within [embryos](../../../../embryo.md); compare human and [marmoset](../../../../marmoset.md) separately rather than pooling them as primates.

A later result sharpens the caveat. Published on 28 July 2025, after this examination, direct [ERK](../../../../erk.md) inhibition with [ulixertinib](../../../../ulixertinib.md) strongly reduced human [hypoblast](../../../../hypoblast.md) and favoured an [epiblast](../../../../epiblast.md)-like [inner cell mass](../../../../inner-cell-mass.md). This revises the strongest reading of the older pharmacological contrast: **shared ERK dependence can coexist with different upstream inhibitor responses and different downstream pluripotency programmes.** It is a subsequent scientific update, not evidence a June candidate was expected to know.[Publication date; Figure 2 and Results, Suppression of ERK signalling blocks hypoblast formation in the human blastocyst](../../../../suppression-of-erk-signalling-promotes-pluripotent-epiblast-in-the-human-blastocyst-by-claire-s-simon-and-others.md)
