<h1 id="5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

**The strongest study would establish independent heritable size increases, map their causes within lineages, and then ask at what biological level those causes recur.** Similar adult sizes alone do not establish independent [adaptive substitutions](../../../../adaptive-substitution.md), and a repeated change in the same [gene](../../../../gene.md) need not be the same causal [mutation](../../../../mutation.md).

Begin with dense sampling of island and mainland relatives across the [clade](../../../../clade.md), including small-bodied island lineages where available. Reconstruct a [phylogenetic tree](../../../../phylogenetic-tree.md) from many genomic regions, estimate colonization relationships, and compare body-size histories with uncertainty in ancestral states. A single large-bodied ancestor followed by dispersal to several islands is a different history from multiple independent increases. Discordant local trees require attention to [incomplete lineage sorting](../../../../incomplete-lineage-sorting.md) and [introgression](../../../../introgression.md). Record island area, resource availability, predators, competitors and colonization history, because these can explain why [natural selection](../../../../natural-selection.md) favored size, or why some apparent replicates share the same historical cause. The number of independently evolved shifts, rather than the number of animals sampled, determines replication for a claim about [convergent evolution](../../../../convergent-evolution.md).

Define the [phenotype](../../../../phenotype.md) carefully. Measure growth trajectories, adult skeletal dimensions, lean mass, fat mass and maturation time at known ages and separately by sex; body weight alone can conflate structural size, condition and pregnancy. Measure individuals blind to ancestry. Rear descendants from several independent families in a [common garden experiment](../../../../common-garden-experiment.md), randomizing cages and controlling diet, density, temperature and pathogen exposure. Include more than one rearing environment to test [genotype-environment interaction](../../../../genotype-environment-interaction.md). Reciprocal crosses and cross-fostering can help distinguish inherited [genetic variants](../../../../genetic-variant.md) from maternal provisioning and early rearing effects. Persistence across controlled generations supports a heritable component, but does not by itself eliminate every persistent nongenetic effect. Field pedigrees and [heritability](../../../../heritability.md) estimates can complement, rather than substitute for, these controls.

Generate comparable [genome](../../../../genome.md) data. High-quality assemblies from representative island and mainland animals help detect [structural variants](../../../../structural-variant.md) and [gene duplication](../../../../gene-duplication.md); population resequencing estimates [allele frequencies](../../../../allele-frequency.md) and [haplotypes](../../../../haplotype.md). Mapping every lineage only to a mainland reference can preferentially miss island sequence, so use alternative references or a [pangenome](../../../../pangenome.md) where feasible and compare mapping quality across groups. Sequence both sexes and retain [sex chromosomes](../../../../sex-chromosome.md) and [mitochondrial genomes](../../../../mitochondrial-genome.md) when technically possible. Design sampling and coverage using a [statistical power](../../../../statistical-power.md) analysis, with multiple unrelated individuals per lineage and independent cohorts for replication; one [genome](../../../../genome.md) per island is inadequate for within-population inference.

Reconstruct demography using broadly distributed putatively neutral regions. Island founding can create a [population bottleneck](../../../../population-bottleneck.md), increased [inbreeding](../../../../inbreeding.md) and long-range [linkage disequilibrium](../../../../linkage-disequilibrium.md). These processes can imitate aspects of a [selective sweep](../../../../selective-sweep.md). Compare candidate regions with null simulations that reproduce plausible colonization sizes, [gene flow](../../../../gene-flow.md), [recombination rates](../../../../recombination-rate.md) and sequencing ascertainment; propagate uncertainty in those quantities. High [FST](../../../../fixation-index.md) can reflect reduced within-population diversity rather than an exceptional accumulation of adaptive differences, so examine absolute divergence and diversity alongside it. A high ratio of nonsynonymous to synonymous changes can also reflect relaxed [purifying selection](../../../../purifying-selection.md), especially after a decline in [effective population size](../../../../effective-population-size.md). No single outlier statistic identifies a body-size mechanism.

Where fertile crosses are possible, perform [quantitative trait locus mapping](../../../../quantitative-trait-locus-mapping.md) using island–mainland intercrosses, with enough recombinants to resolve several causal regions. Measure longitudinal growth and test sex-dependent effects, [dominance](../../../../dominance-genetics.md) and [epistasis](../../../../epistasis.md), while controlling family and shared rearing. Natural [association mapping](../../../../association-mapping.md) can help when large samples segregate relevant [alleles](../../../../allele.md); account for [population stratification](../../../../population-stratification.md) and relatedness with an appropriate [Gaussian linear mixed model](../../../../gaussian-linear-mixed-model.md). Fixed island–mainland differences cannot be mapped within an island where they no longer vary, and a [genome-wide association study](../../../../genome-wide-association-study.md) that simply contrasts [species](../../../../species.md) confounds ancestry with body size. As an empirical precedent, Gough Island mouse intercrosses identified multiple regions affecting body weight and growth rate, illustrating that rapid extreme size [evolution](../../../../evolution.md) can have a distributed genetic basis.[Genetics 201, pp. 213–228; intercross design and separate body-weight and growth-rate QTL analyses](../../../../genetics-of-rapid-and-extreme-size-evolution-in-island-mice-by-melissa-m-gray-and-others.md) These regions are starting points for resolution, not automatically individual causal [genes](../../../../gene.md).

Integrate mapping with measurements in relevant tissues and developmental windows. [RNA sequencing](../../../../rna-sequencing.md) of growth plate, muscle or endocrine tissue may identify candidate changes in [gene expression](../../../../gene-expression.md), but match developmental stage, sex, diet and cellular composition so that a larger animal's different tissue mixture is not mistaken for a regulatory cause. [allele-specific expression](../../../../allele-specific-expression.md) in an informative first-generation cross can distinguish some effects of [cis-regulatory evolution](../../../../cis-regulatory-evolution.md) from shared trans-acting influences, provided reference-mapping bias is controlled. Test accessible [enhancers](../../../../enhancer.md) or coding changes suggested jointly by mapping, sequence and tissue evidence. Growth pathways are reasonable hypotheses, but discovering a familiar growth-related annotation is not causal validation and should not exclude unexpected [genetic loci](../../../../genetic-locus.md).

Resolve the level of convergence explicitly. Independent lineages might share the same new [nucleotide substitution](../../../../nucleotide-substitution.md), different changes in the same [gene](../../../../gene.md), different [genes](../../../../gene.md) in one pathway, or many changes distributed across a [polygenic trait](../../../../polygenic-trait.md). Shared long [haplotypes](../../../../haplotype.md) and local ancestry can reveal reuse through [introgression](../../../../introgression.md) or [standing genetic variation](../../../../standing-genetic-variation.md). Distinguish these from recurrent [mutations](../../../../mutation.md) using local genealogies, surrounding sequence and age estimates. For pathway enrichment, control [gene](../../../../gene.md) length, annotation density, [mutation rate](../../../../mutation-rate.md), [genetic linkage](../../../../genetic-linkage.md) and multiple testing; a shared [gene](../../../../gene.md) annotation is not an independent replicate for every linked site. Failure to find repeated individual hits does not exclude convergence at a broader biological level.

Finally, replace candidate [alleles](../../../../allele.md) or [enhancers](../../../../enhancer.md) in controlled [genetic backgrounds](../../../../genetic-background.md), ideally in both directions, and measure their effects on growth and its costs. Use unedited siblings, sham-edited lines, independent edited founders and a rescue or precise reversion to address manipulation and off-target effects. Test combinations if mapping indicates [epistasis](../../../../epistasis.md). Cell assays or another model [species](../../../../species.md) can establish biochemical activity, but need not reproduce the original mammal's whole-body effect. To call the change adaptive, also connect size and the candidate [alleles](../../../../allele.md) to survival and [reproductive success](../../../../reproductive-success.md) under relevant island conditions; mainland and island environments provide a useful reciprocal comparison. **A persuasive result connects independent history, heritable size, specific causal variants and [genotype fitness](../../../../genotype-fitness.md) consequences, while allowing convergence to involve different genes or reuse of ancestral variation.**
