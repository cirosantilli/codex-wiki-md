[phenotypic plasticity](../../../../phenotypic-plasticity.md) is the capacity of a [genotype](../../../../genotype.md) to produce different [phenotypes](../../../../phenotype.md) in different environments. It can change an individual's performance within a generation, before any change in [allele frequency](../../../../allele-frequency.md). **[phenotypic plasticity](../../../../phenotypic-plasticity.md) can buy time for evolutionary [adaptation](../../../../adaptation.md) and generate new targets for selection, but an induced response is neither necessarily beneficial nor evidence of inherited evolutionary change.**

A [reaction norm](../../../../reaction-norm.md) makes the distinction precise. Write an individual's [phenotypic trait](../../../../phenotypic-trait.md) as $z=a+bC$, where $C$ is an environmental cue and the [genotype](../../../../genotype.md) helps determine the intercept $a$ and responsiveness $b$. Differences in $a$ represent different baseline [phenotypic traits](../../../../phenotypic-trait.md); differences in $b$ represent genetic variation in sensitivity, a form of [genotype-environment interaction](../../../../genotype-environment-interaction.md). If the [phenotype](../../../../phenotype.md) maximizing [genotype fitness](../../../../genotype-fitness.md) in the later selective environment $E$ is $z^*=kE$, an informative cue can move individuals nearer that optimum immediately. Appropriate changes in developmental timing or physiology can therefore prevent demographic collapse and preserve the variation and numbers needed for subsequent [natural selection](../../../../natural-selection.md).

The importance of cue reliability follows from a simple model. Suppose [genotype fitness](../../../../genotype-fitness.md) loss is proportional to squared mismatch, the cue and environment have finite second moments, and we ignore the costs of responding. Minimizing $\mathbb E[(a+bC-kE)^2]$ over $a,b$, with $\operatorname{Var}(C)>0$, gives

$$
\boxed{b^*=k\frac{\operatorname{Cov}(E,C)}{\operatorname{Var}(C)},\qquad
a^*=k\mathbb E[E]-b^*\mathbb E[C].}
$$

This is a model calculation, not a universal optimal [reaction norm](../../../../reaction-norm.md). It shows why reliable prediction of the selective environment can favor responsiveness, whereas an uninformative cue gives $b^*=0$. A response evolved under one cue–environment relation can become harmful when that relation changes. Temporal delays, irreversible [biological development](../../../../biological-development.md), limited response speed and energetic costs further constrain the advantage. Stress-induced loss of growth, for example, is not evidence of adaptive [phenotypic plasticity](../../../../phenotypic-plasticity.md) unless it improves an appropriate measure of [genotype fitness](../../../../genotype-fitness.md) relative to a feasible alternative.

[phenotypic plasticity](../../../../phenotypic-plasticity.md) can affect later [evolution](../../../../evolution.md) in several ways. It can expose previously unexpressed [genetic variation](../../../../genetic-variation.md) when a novel environment changes developmental pathways. It can let a lineage enter a new habitat where [natural selection](../../../../natural-selection.md) then improves performance. It can also reduce the mismatch so effectively that [natural selection](../../../../natural-selection.md) on the underlying constitutive [phenotypic trait](../../../../phenotypic-trait.md) weakens, slowing genetic change. Even maladaptive initial [phenotypic plasticity](../../../../phenotypic-plasticity.md) can intensify [natural selection](../../../../natural-selection.md) for compensatory change, provided the [biological population](../../../../population-biology.md) survives. Thus a rapid phenotypic response, rapid genetic [adaptation](../../../../adaptation.md) and long-term diversification are three distinct outcomes, not interchangeable measures of success.

[genetic accommodation](../../../../genetic-accommodation.md) is evolutionary change in an environmentally responsive [phenotypic trait](../../../../phenotypic-trait.md) or its regulation. It can change the mean, threshold, magnitude or specificity of the response and can preserve or increase [phenotypic plasticity](../../../../phenotypic-plasticity.md). [genetic assimilation](../../../../genetic-assimilation.md) is the particular case in which a formerly induced [phenotype](../../../../phenotype.md) becomes expressed without the original inducing condition. Neither process requires the environment to write a beneficial change directly into the inherited [DNA](../../../../deoxyribonucleic-acid.md); [natural selection](../../../../natural-selection.md) or imposed breeding can sort existing and newly arising variation affecting the response. Assimilation need not occur when environments continue to fluctuate, because maintaining a responsive [reaction norm](../../../../reaction-norm.md) may remain advantageous.

The classic experimental demonstration selected fruit flies for a wing-vein [phenotype](../../../../phenotype.md) induced by heat treatment, eventually obtaining expression without that treatment.[Evolution 7, pp. 118–126; heat-induced crossveinless phenotype and selection for its expression](../../../../genetic-assimilation-of-an-acquired-character-by-conrad-hal-waddington.md) The key observation is the changed relation between environment and [phenotype](../../../../phenotype.md) across selected generations. It demonstrates the feasibility of [genetic assimilation](../../../../genetic-assimilation.md), not that losing the vein was an adaptive response in nature, nor that acquired bodily modifications were directly copied into the [genome](../../../../genome.md). A modern replication should include heat-exposed unselected lines, untreated lines, replicated selection lines, controlled rearing and [genotype fitness](../../../../genotype-fitness.md) assays, and should distinguish segregating variation from a new major-effect [mutation](../../../../mutation.md).

For diversification, [phenotypic plasticity](../../../../phenotypic-plasticity.md) can permit use of several resources or produce alternative specialist forms. Repeated exposure to a new resource can increase the frequency with which one form is expressed, giving [natural selection](../../../../natural-selection.md) more opportunity to refine it. In spadefoot tadpoles, experiments compared the competitive performance of an induced carnivore form among [biological populations](../../../../population-biology.md) differing in how often they produce it; the association between expression frequency and competitive refinement supports one prediction of plasticity-led [evolution](../../../../evolution.md).[Proceedings of the Royal Society B 286, article 20182754; carnivore-frequency comparisons and competition experiments](../../../../plasticity-led-evolution-evaluating-the-key-prediction-of-frequency-dependent-adaptation-by-nicholas-a-levis-and-david-w-pfennig.md) Such comparisons do not directly observe the ancestor or establish that every difference arose after induction rather than from prior genetic divergence.

Alternative forms can lead to [ecological speciation](../../../../ecological-speciation.md) if specialization causes habitat-associated mating, divergent [natural selection](../../../../natural-selection.md), and reduced [gene flow](../../../../gene-flow.md). A mating preference coupled to the adopted habitat could help turn ecological divergence into [premating isolation](../../../../premating-isolation.md). But the opposite outcome is also plausible: one flexible [genotype](../../../../genotype.md) can succeed in both habitats, reducing divergent [natural selection](../../../../natural-selection.md) and maintaining [gene flow](../../../../gene-flow.md). A difference in [reaction norms](../../../../reaction-norm.md) is consequently not itself evidence of [speciation](../../../../speciation.md). A plastic radiation can persist without producing new [species](../../../../species.md), and a radiation can occur through ordinary heritable divergence with little contribution from [phenotypic plasticity](../../../../phenotypic-plasticity.md).

Testing the causal historical claim requires more than comparing present-day induced and constitutive forms. Use related lineages and explicit ancestral-state uncertainty, raise multiple [genotypes](../../../../genotype.md) in crossed environments, and measure both [reaction norms](../../../../reaction-norm.md) and [genotype fitness](../../../../genotype-fitness.md). Split-family rearing controls ancestry; cross-fostering and controlled generations help identify maternal carry-over; experimental manipulation of the cue separates induction from resource quality. Replicate [evolution](../../../../evolution.md) with and without access to the cue and quantify survival, subsequent genetic response and reproductive barriers. An extant sister lineage is an imperfect proxy for an ancestor, and correlations between [phenotypic plasticity](../../../../phenotypic-plasticity.md) and [species](../../../../species.md) richness can reflect reverse causation or ecological opportunity. **[phenotypic plasticity](../../../../phenotypic-plasticity.md) contributes most convincingly when a demonstrated cue-dependent [genotype fitness](../../../../genotype-fitness.md) benefit precedes heritable refinement, and any proposed diversification is independently connected to reduced [gene flow](../../../../gene-flow.md).**
