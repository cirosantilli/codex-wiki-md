# Monoamine oxidase

↑ **Parent:** [Enzyme](enzyme-split.md)

An enzyme class involved in metabolism of [monoamines](monoamine.md). Inhibiting it can increase monoamine availability, but that pharmacological fact does not establish a universal deficiency in untreated illness.
