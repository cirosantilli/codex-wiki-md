# Euler class of a complex vector bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Euler class of a vector bundle](euler-class-of-a-vector-bundle.md)

A complex rank-$r$ [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) has a canonical real orientation, and its [Euler class](euler-class-of-a-vector-bundle.md) equals its top [Chern class](chern-class.md). For a complex line this is $e(L_{\mathbb R})=c_1(L)$. The [splitting principle for complex vector bundles](splitting-principle-for-complex-vector-bundles.md) gives an injective cohomology pullback on which the bundle splits into lines. The [Whitney product formula for Euler classes](whitney-product-formula-for-euler-classes.md) and [Whitney sum formula for Chern classes](whitney-sum-formula-for-chern-classes.md) then identify both sides with the product of those first Chern classes, proving the general equality.
