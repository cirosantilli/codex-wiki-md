# Countable-ordinal correctness under constructibility

↑ **Parent:** [Uncountable transitive set model has uncountable ordinal height](uncountable-transitive-set-model-has-uncountable-ordinal-height.md)

Assuming $V=L$, every uncountable [transitive model](transitive-model.md) of [ZFC](zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory-with-choice.md) contains all ambient [countable ordinals](countable-ordinal.md) and witnesses their countability internally. Its ordinal height is at least $\omega_1$, and [absoluteness of constructible levels](absoluteness-of-constructible-levels.md) puts $L_{\omega_1}$ inside it. Every countability witness for a countable ordinal can be chosen in $L_{\omega_1}$ by [hereditarily countable constructible sets appear below omega-one](hereditarily-countable-constructible-sets-appear-below-omega-one.md).
