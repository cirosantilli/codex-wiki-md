# Fractional uniform cover

↑ **Parent:** [Uniform cover](uniform-cover.md)

Nonnegative weights $\lambda_A$ on coordinate subsets form a fractional uniform cover when the displayed equality holds for each coordinate. Rational weights can be multiplied by a common denominator to become an integer [uniform cover](uniform-cover.md). The [uniform covers theorem](uniform-covers-theorem.md) then gives $\log|S|\leq\sum_A\lambda_A\log|S_A|$. Exact equality of coordinate coverage matters when projection measures may be less than one.
