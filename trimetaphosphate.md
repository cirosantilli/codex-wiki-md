# Trimetaphosphate

↑ **Parent:** [Phosphate](phosphate.md)

The cyclic [anion](anion.md) $\mathrm{P_3O_9^{3-}}$ comprising three tetrahedral $\mathrm{PO_4}$ groups linked by three P–O–P bridges. Each [phosphorus](phosphorus.md) has two terminal [oxygen](oxygen.md) atoms. It can bind a [metal](metal.md) through one terminal [oxygen](oxygen.md) from each [phosphorus](phosphorus.md).
