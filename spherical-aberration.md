# Spherical aberration

↑ **Parent:** [Seidel aberration](seidel-aberration.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spherical_aberration)

Spherical aberration makes rays at different pupil radii focus at different axial positions. Its primary pupil dependence is quartic; the balanced [Zernike spherical mode](zernike-spherical-mode.md) also contains quadratic and constant terms to remove defocus and piston.
