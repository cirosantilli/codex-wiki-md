# Quantum time-reversal operator

↑ **Parent:** [Time-reversal symmetry in quantum mechanics](time-reversal-symmetry-in-quantum-mechanics.md)

A quantum time-reversal operator is an [antiunitary operator](antiunitary-operator.md) reversing spatial [momentum](momentum.md) and [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md). For a time-reversal-invariant [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md), its conjugation of $i$ makes it intertwine forward and backward [unitary time evolution](unitary-time-evolution.md) without reversing the [energy](energy.md) spectrum. A scalar [momentum eigenstate](momentum-eigenstate.md) may transform as $\hat T|\boldsymbol p\rangle=e^{i\chi(\boldsymbol p)}|-\boldsymbol p\rangle$. With unit-modulus phases and a complete normalized basis, expansion of arbitrary states proves [antiunitarity](antiunitary-operator.md).
