# Numerical method

↑ **Parent:** [Numerical analysis](numerical-analysis-split.md)

A [numerical method](numerical-method.md) is an [algorithm](algorithm-split.md) for computing an exact or approximate solution to a mathematical problem through finitely many [arithmetic operations](arithmetic-operation.md) and other prescribed steps. For example, [Gaussian elimination](gaussian-elimination.md) solves a [linear system](system-of-linear-equations.md) exactly in exact arithmetic, whereas finite precision can introduce [roundoff error](round-off-error.md).
