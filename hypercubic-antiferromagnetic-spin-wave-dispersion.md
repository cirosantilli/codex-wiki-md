# Hypercubic antiferromagnetic spin-wave dispersion

↑ **Parent:** [Antiferromagnetic spin wave](antiferromagnetic-spin-wave.md)

For a nearest-neighbour [Heisenberg antiferromagnet](heisenberg-antiferromagnet.md) on a [hypercubic lattice](cubic-lattice.md), the [linear spin-wave approximation](linear-spin-wave-approximation.md) gives $\gamma_{\mathbf k}=d^{-1}\sum_j\cos(k_ja)$ and the displayed frequency. Near a gapless point its [spin-wave velocity](spin-wave-velocity.md) is $2JSa\sqrt d$. Its ground-state boson occupation is $[1/\sqrt{1-\gamma_{\mathbf k}^2}-1]/2$, producing [quantum depletion of Néel order](quantum-depletion-of-neel-order.md). Exact zero modes need an infrared regulator or separate global-rotation treatment.
