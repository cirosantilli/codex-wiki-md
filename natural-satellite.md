# Natural satellite

↑ **Parent:** [Planetary science](planetary-science-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_satellite)

A [natural satellite](natural-satellite.md) is a naturally occurring body orbiting a [planet](planet.md) or another nonstellar body. The [Moon](moon.md) is [Earth](earth.md)'s [natural satellite](natural-satellite.md); its [synchronous rotation](synchronous-rotation.md) provides a familiar example of [tidal locking](tidal-locking.md).
