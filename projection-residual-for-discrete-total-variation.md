# Projection residual for discrete total variation

↑ **Parent:** [Discrete isotropic total variation](discrete-isotropic-total-variation.md)

For $C=D^*\{p:|p_i|_2\le\lambda\}$, discrete [total variation denoising](total-variation-denoising.md) is the [proximal operator](proximal-operator.md) of the [support function](support-function.md) of $C$. The [Moreau decomposition](moreau-decomposition.md) gives the displayed projection residual. It is generally not itself a [metric projection onto a closed convex set](euclidean-projection-onto-a-convex-set.md): such a projection is idempotent, but TV denoising shrinks a sufficiently large two-level jump again when reapplied.
