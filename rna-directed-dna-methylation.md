# RNA-directed DNA methylation

↑ **Parent:** [Plant epigenetics](plant-epigenetics.md)

A [plant](plant.md) silencing pathway in which small RNAs guide methylation at homologous [chromatin](chromatin.md). Canonical [RdDM](rna-directed-dna-methylation.md) involves [Pol IV](rna-polymerase-iv.md), [RDR2](rdr2.md), [DCL3](dcl3.md), Argonaute and [Pol V](rna-polymerase-v.md) scaffold [transcription](transcription-biology.md).
