# Radial quadratic oscillation defect in two dimensions

↑ **Parent:** [Oscillating point-mass defect](oscillating-point-mass-defect.md)

For $a>0$, polar-coordinate reduction and [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) give $m\sin(m||x|^2-a|)=2\delta(|x|^2-a)-\pi\cos(ma)\delta_0+o_{\mathcal D'}(1)$ in two dimensions. The circle term comes from the cusp in the folded phase, while the origin term is a radial endpoint contribution. A punctured-domain limit therefore need not extend across the quadratic critical point.
