# Wardrop equilibrium

↑ **Parent:** [Stochastic network](stochastic-network.md)

A Wardrop equilibrium assigns positive traffic only to routes with minimum delay for their source-sink pair. With fixed demands and continuous increasing link delays, it minimizes the [Beckmann potential](beckmann-potential.md). Strictly increasing delays make link throughputs unique, while route flows can remain nonunique.
