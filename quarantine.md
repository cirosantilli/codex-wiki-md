# Quarantine

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical epidemiology](mathematical-epidemiology.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quarantine)

[Quarantine](quarantine.md) restricts contacts of exposed individuals whose infection status is uncertain, with monitoring to detect illness. It differs from [isolation](isolation-health-care.md) of recognized infected or ill individuals. An [incubation period](incubation-period.md) [quantile](quantile-function.md) can enter a duration calculation, but assumptions about exposure time, symptom detection and infectiousness are needed before the calculation describes transmission risk.
