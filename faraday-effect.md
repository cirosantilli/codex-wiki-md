# Faraday effect

↑ **Parent:** [Polarization of an electromagnetic wave](polarization-of-an-electromagnetic-wave.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faraday_effect)

The [Faraday effect](faraday-effect.md) is [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md)-induced [circular birefringence](circular-birefringence.md): opposite [circular polarizations](circular-polarization.md) acquire different [wave phases](phase-waves.md), rotating transmitted [linear polarization](linear-polarization.md). For a transparent medium with [isotropy](isotropy.md) before applying the [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md), in the linear magnetic-response regime at fixed [vacuum](vacuum.md) [wavelength](wavelength.md) $\lambda_0$ and [temperature](temperature-split.md) $T$,

$$
\theta=V(\lambda_0,T)\int_0^L B_\parallel(\ell)\,d\ell.
$$

Here $V$ is the [Verdet constant](verdet-constant.md), $L$ is the material path length, and $B_\parallel$ is the applied [magnetic flux density](magnetic-field-split.md) component along the ray. This $\mathbf B$ is the external bias, distinct from the oscillating [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) of the [light](light.md) itself. [Michael Faraday](michael-faraday.md) discovered the effect in 1845.[RP Photonics Encyclopedia, Faraday Effect, Rüdiger Paschotta: What is the Faraday Effect?, rotation law and discovery](rp-photonics-encyclopedia-by-rudiger-paschotta/faraday-effect.md)

Fix the sign before using the formula. On the forward path choose a right-handed [Cartesian coordinate system](cartesian-coordinate-system.md), propagation along $+z$, and measure $\theta$ from $+x$ toward $+y$ for the [electric field](electric-field-split.md)'s oscillation line. Positive $\theta$ is counterclockwise in the $x$-right, $y$-up end view seen from $z>0$, looking toward the source. Then $B_\parallel=B_z$ and positive $V$ means positive rotation for positive $B_z$. This convention differs from the clockwise-positive convention in [optical rotation](optical-rotation.md). A [linear polarization](linear-polarization.md) line is unchanged by $180^\circ$; a measured orientation determines accumulated rotation only modulo $\pi$, unless continuity selects a branch.

For uniform $B_\parallel$, the formula reduces to $\theta=VBL$. Its appeal is that a weak change of [refractive index](refractive-index.md) becomes a visible change of orientation. [Magnetic reversal measurement of Faraday rotation](magnetic-reversal-measurement-of-faraday-rotation.md) turns this into an inference of $V$ or of the [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) path integral. [Faraday rotation on a round trip](faraday-rotation-on-a-round-trip.md) explains the directional behavior used by an [optical isolator](optical-isolator.md). In an astronomical cold [plasma](plasma-physics.md), the high-[frequency](frequency.md) limit instead leads to the [rotation measure](rotation-measure.md) law $\theta=\mathrm{RM}\lambda_0^2$; that [wavelength](wavelength.md) dependence is not a universal law for transparent solids.
