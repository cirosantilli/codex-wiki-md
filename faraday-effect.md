# Faraday effect

↑ **Parent:** [Polarization of an electromagnetic wave](polarization-of-an-electromagnetic-wave.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faraday_effect)

The Faraday effect rotates the orientation of linear [polarization of an electromagnetic wave](polarization-of-an-electromagnetic-wave.md) during propagation through a magnetized medium. The two opposite [circular polarizations](circular-polarization.md) accumulate different phases; their recombination rotates the linear polarization. In a sufficiently high-frequency cold [plasma](plasma-physics.md), this angle varies as the squared [wavelength](wavelength.md) times a [rotation measure](rotation-measure.md).
