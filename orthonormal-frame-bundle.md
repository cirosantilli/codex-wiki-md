# Orthonormal frame bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Principal bundle](principal-bundle.md)

For a metric of signature $(r,s)$, the orthonormal frame bundle consists of all linear isometries $u:\mathbb R^{r,s}\to T_xM$. Right composition by $O(r,s)$ makes it a [principal bundle](principal-bundle.md). Its standard [associated vector bundle](associated-vector-bundle.md) is the [tangent bundle](tangent-bundle.md), via $[u,v]\mapsto u(v)$. A global orthonormal frame is a global section and therefore trivializes this principal bundle. A [Lie group](lie-group.md) with a left-invariant metric has such a frame by left translating an orthonormal basis at the identity.
