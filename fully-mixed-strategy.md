# Fully mixed strategy

↑ **Parent:** [Mixed strategy](mixed-strategy.md)

A [mixed strategy](mixed-strategy.md) assigning strictly positive [probability](probability.md) to every available [pure strategy](pure-strategy.md). Its [support of a mixed strategy](support-of-a-mixed-strategy.md) is the entire action [set](set-split.md). A [mixed strategy](mixed-strategy.md) can be non-pure without being fully mixed when there are more than two actions.
