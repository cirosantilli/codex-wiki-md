# Electromagnetic stress-energy divergence identity

↑ **Parent:** [Electromagnetic stress-energy tensor](electromagnetic-stress-energy-tensor.md)

For signature $(+---)$ in Gaussian electromagnetic units, $T^{ab}=(4\pi)^{-1}[-F^a{}_cF^{bc}+g^{ab}F_{cd}F^{cd}/4]$. The [Electromagnetic Bianchi identity](electromagnetic-bianchi-identity.md) gives $F^{bc}\nabla_bF^a{}_c=\nabla^a(F_{cd}F^{cd})/4$, so differentiating the stress tensor proves the displayed identity. Stress conservation implies the source-free [Maxwell equations](maxwell-equations.md) if the matrix $F^a{}_c$ is invertible; otherwise it only forces the field divergence into that matrix's kernel. Conversely source-free Maxwell equations always imply stress conservation.
