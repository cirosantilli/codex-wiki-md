# Helical motion in a uniform magnetic field

↑ **Parent:** [Charged particle in a uniform magnetic field](charged-particle-in-a-uniform-magnetic-field.md)

In a uniform [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md), the [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md) leaves the component of [velocity](velocity.md) parallel to the field constant and rotates the perpendicular component at the nonrelativistic [cyclotron frequency](cyclotron-frequency.md) $|q|B/m$. The resulting path is a [helix](helix.md) around a field line.
