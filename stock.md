# Stock

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical finance](mathematical-finance-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stock)

A stock is a traded ownership interest in a company. In a [mathematical finance](mathematical-finance-split.md) model, its price is a [stochastic process](stochastic-process-split.md) and a [self-financing portfolio](self-financing-portfolio.md) may hold positive or negative numbers of its shares.
