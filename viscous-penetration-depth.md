# Viscous penetration depth

↑ **Parent:** [Stokes' second problem](stokes-second-problem.md)

The [viscous penetration depth](viscous-penetration-depth.md) is the distance over which a time-periodic shear [velocity](velocity.md) driven by an oscillating boundary decays by $1/e$ in a semi-infinite [Newtonian fluid](newtonian-fluid.md). The [momentum diffusion](momentum-diffusion.md) equation gives $u=U_0e^{-z/\delta}\cos(\omega t-z/\delta)$. Both attenuation and phase lag are controlled by $z/\delta$. The corresponding fluid-displacement amplitude has the same spatial decay, because dividing each velocity amplitude by the common [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) does not change their ratio.
