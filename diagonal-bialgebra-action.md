# Diagonal bialgebra action

↑ **Parent:** [Bialgebra](bialgebra.md)

The tensor of left [modules](module-mathematics.md) over a [bialgebra](bialgebra.md) has action $h(u\otimes v)=\sum(h_{(1)}u)\otimes(h_{(2)}v)$. The unit module $k$ has action through the [counit](counit.md).
