# Weight multiplicity

↑ **Parent:** [Weight space](weight-space.md)

The multiplicity of a weight $\mu$ is the dimension of its [weight space](weight-space.md).

Thus it is a dimension attached to a [Weight](weight-representation-theory.md) and its [weight space](weight-space.md).
