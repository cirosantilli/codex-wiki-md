# Tonelli existence theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Direct method in the calculus of variations](direct-method-in-the-calculus-of-variations.md)

For a scalar fixed-endpoint problem on a bounded interval, suppose the continuous integrand $f(x,y,p)$ is convex in $p$ and satisfies $f(x,y,p)\geq\Theta(|p|)-C$, where $\Theta$ is nonnegative convex and $\Theta(r)/r\to\infty$. If there is one admissible absolutely continuous function of finite energy, a global minimizer exists in $W^{1,1}$.

The lower bound gives uniform integrability of derivatives in a minimizing sequence. Fixed endpoint values then give boundedness and equicontinuity of the functions. Extract uniform convergence of the functions and weak $L^1$ convergence of their derivatives; the limit retains the endpoint values. Convexity in the derivative gives [weak lower semicontinuity of convex gradient energies](weak-lower-semicontinuity-of-convex-gradient-energies.md), so the limit attains the infimum. Strict convexity of the full integrand on a convex admissible class additionally gives uniqueness.
