# Biopolymer

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer](polymer-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biopolymer)

A biopolymer is a [polymer](polymer-split.md) produced by a living system, including [proteins](protein-split.md), [nucleic acids](nucleic-acid.md) and [polysaccharides](polysaccharide.md). Local chemical structure, sequence and interactions can give it stiffness or preferred conformations beyond those of an [ideal chain](ideal-chain.md). [Biopolymer mechanics](biopolymer-mechanics.md) uses models such as the [worm-like chain](worm-like-chain.md) and the [helix-coil transition model](helix-coil-transition-model.md) to represent those features.
