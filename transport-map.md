# Transport map

↑ **Parent:** [Monge optimal transport problem](monge-optimal-transport-problem.md)

A transport map from $\mu$ to $\nu$ is a [measurable function](measurable-function.md) $T$ satisfying $T_\#\mu=\nu$. Its graph defines the [transport plan](transport-plan.md) $(\operatorname{Id},T)_\#\mu$.
