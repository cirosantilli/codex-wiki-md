# Nucleosynthesis

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear physics](nuclear-physics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nucleosynthesis)

[Nucleosynthesis](nucleosynthesis.md) is the production of [atomic nuclei](atomic-nucleus.md) through [nuclear reactions](nuclear-reaction.md). It includes [Big Bang nucleosynthesis](big-bang-nucleosynthesis.md), [stellar nucleosynthesis](stellar-nucleosynthesis.md) and production in explosive or compact-remnant environments. Making nuclei and delivering them to the [interstellar medium](interstellar-medium.md) are separate steps in chemical enrichment.
