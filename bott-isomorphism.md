# Bott isomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Topological K-theory](topological-k-theory.md)

Multiplication by the [Bott element](bott-element.md) $\beta\in\widetilde K^0(S^2)$ gives the Bott isomorphism

$$
K^i(X)\xrightarrow{\ \cong\ }\widetilde K^i(S^2\wedge X)
\cong K^{i-2}(X).
$$
