# Dimension of a Weyl algebra module for the order filtration

↑ **Parent:** [Order filtration of a Weyl algebra](order-filtration-of-a-weyl-algebra.md)

For a nonzero [finitely generated module](finitely-generated-module.md) over a complex [Weyl algebra](weyl-algebra.md), choose a [good filtration for the order filtration](good-filtration-for-the-order-filtration.md) and take the [Krull dimension](krull-dimension.md) of its [support of a module](support-of-a-module.md) over $\operatorname{gr}R=\mathbb C[x_1,\ldots,x_n,\xi_1,\ldots,\xi_n]$. This is the [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) of its [order characteristic variety of a Weyl algebra module](order-characteristic-variety-of-a-weyl-algebra-module.md). One may set $d(0)=-\infty$.

For independence from the good module filtration, any two such [filtrations of a module](filtration-of-a-module.md) are bounded shifts of one another, by their finite shifted-generating descriptions. If a homogeneous symbol $\sigma(a)$ of degree $d$ annihilates one [associated graded module](associated-graded-module.md), then $aF_jM\subseteq F_{j+d-1}M$ and its $q$-th power lowers the expected degree by $q$. For shift bound $c$ and $q>2c$, comparison shows that $\sigma(a)^q$ annihilates the other [associated graded module](associated-graded-module.md). Reverse the roles to obtain equality of the [radicals of ideals](radical-of-an-ideal.md) of their [annihilators](annihilator-ring-theory.md), hence equality of their [support of a module](support-of-a-module.md) and [Krull dimension](krull-dimension.md). This argument holds the algebra filtration fixed.
