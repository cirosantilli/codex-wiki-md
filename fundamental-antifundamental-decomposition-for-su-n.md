<h1 id="fundamental-antifundamental-decomposition-for-su-n">Fundamental-antifundamental decomposition for SU(N)</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor product of group representations](tensor-product-of-group-representations.md)

Identify the product with endomorphisms of the defining space. [Special unitary group](special-unitary-group.md) conjugation fixes scalar matrices and preserves the complementary traceless subspace. For $N\ge2$, the latter is the complex [Adjoint representation of a Lie algebra](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md), which is irreducible because $\mathfrak{sl}_N$ is simple. With fundamental [Dynkin index](dynkin-index.md) $1/2$, the trace-Casimir identity gives $C_2(\mathbf N)=(N^2-1)/(2N)$; the [tensor-product Casimir trace identity](tensor-product-casimir-trace-identity.md) then gives $C_2(\mathbf{Adj})=N$.
