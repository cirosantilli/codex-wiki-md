# Mass defect

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear binding energy](nuclear-binding-energy.md)

A difference between the mass of a bound system and an appropriate sum of reference constituent masses. For a nuclear transformation, the initial-minus-final mass difference gives the available [Q value](q-value.md) after multiplication by $c^2$, with atomic electrons counted consistently when using neutral-atom masses.
