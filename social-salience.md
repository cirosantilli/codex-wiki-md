# Social salience

↑ **Parent:** [Social perception](social-perception.md)

The degree to which a person, feature or category captures [attention](attention.md) or is readily available for interpretation in a social setting. Salience helps explain what enters a [social attribution](social-attribution.md), but does not by itself determine its causal validity.
