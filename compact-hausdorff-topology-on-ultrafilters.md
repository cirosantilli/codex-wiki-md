# Compact Hausdorff topology on ultrafilters

↑ **Parent:** [Stone-Čech compactification of the natural numbers](stone-cech-compactification-of-the-natural-numbers.md)

For $A\subseteq\mathbb N$, the basic set $\widehat A=\{U:A\in U\}$ in the [Stone-Čech compactification of the natural numbers](stone-cech-compactification-of-the-natural-numbers.md) is a [clopen set](clopen-set.md), since its complement is $\widehat{\mathbb N\setminus A}$. Distinct [ultrafilters](ultrafilter.md) are separated by complementary basic sets. A basic cover with no finite subcover would give complements with the [finite intersection property](finite-intersection-property.md), whose generated [filter on a set](filter-set-theory.md) extends to an [ultrafilter](ultrafilter.md) missing the entire cover. Thus the space is a [compact Hausdorff space](compact-hausdorff-space.md).
