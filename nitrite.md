# Nitrite

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical compound](chemical-compound.md)

[Nitrite](nitrite.md) is the bent anion $\mathrm{NO_2^-}$. It can act as an [ambidentate ligand](ambidentate-ligand.md), giving N-bound [nitro ligands](nitro-ligand.md) or O-bound [nitrito ligands](nitrito-ligand.md).
