# Supersymmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Branches of physics](branches-of-physics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supersymmetry)

Supersymmetry extends spacetime symmetry by odd [supercharges](supersymmetry-generator.md) that interchange [boson](boson.md) and [fermion](fermion.md) states. Their [Super-Poincaré algebra](super-poincare-algebra.md) pairs positive-energy states into [supermultiplets](supermultiplet.md) and gives [energy positivity in global supersymmetry](energy-positivity-in-global-supersymmetry.md). [Superspace](superspace.md) and [superfields](superfield.md) package the component fields into representations of this symmetry.
