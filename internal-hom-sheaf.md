# Internal Hom sheaf

↑ **Parent:** [Sheaf of modules](sheaf-of-modules.md)

For [sheaves of modules](sheaf-of-modules.md) $\mathcal F,\mathcal G$ on a [ringed space](ringed-space-split.md), the internal Hom sheaf assigns to an open $U$ the module of [sheaf morphisms](morphism-of-sheaves.md) $\mathcal F|_U\to\mathcal G|_U$ respecting $\mathcal O_U$. Compatible morphisms glue, so this is a sheaf. Its [global sections](global-section.md) are precisely the global module-sheaf morphisms. For a [locally free sheaf](locally-free-sheaf.md) $\mathcal F$ of finite rank it is $\mathcal F^\vee\otimes\mathcal G$.
