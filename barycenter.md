# Barycenter

↑ **Parent:** [Compact metrizable convex set](compact-metrizable-convex-set.md)

The [barycenter](barycenter.md) of a [probability measure](probability-measure.md) on a compact convex set is the point satisfying the displayed affine integral identities. It exists by approximating the [measure](measure.md) by finitely supported [measures](measure.md) and using compactness of their finite convex combinations. It is unique because continuous affine [functions](function-split.md) separate points. In a Banach-space setting it agrees with the appropriate vector integral when that integral is defined.
