# Streamfunction advection bracket

↑ **Parent:** [Incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md)

For the two-dimensional [streamfunction](stream-function.md) convention $u=\psi_z$, $w=-\psi_x$, the advective derivative of $\theta$ is $u\theta_x+w\theta_z=J(\psi,\theta)$. This antisymmetric [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md) bracket organizes nonlinear interactions in convection and vorticity equations.
