# Harringtonine

↑ **Parent:** [Translation initiation](translation-initiation.md)

A [biological translation](translation-biology.md) inhibitor used to enrich [ribosomes](ribosome.md) near initiation sites while much ongoing elongation runs off. Exposure time and trapping kinetics affect inferred initiation positions.
