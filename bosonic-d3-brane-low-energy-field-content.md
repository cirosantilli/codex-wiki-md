# Bosonic D3-brane low-energy field content

↑ **Parent:** [Coincident-D-brane gauge enhancement](coincident-d-brane-gauge-enhancement.md)

For coincident [D3-branes](d3-brane.md) in the critical 26-dimensional [bosonic string theory](bosonic-string-theory.md), the massless open-string oscillator has a four-dimensional gauge vector and 22 transverse [scalar fields](scalar-field.md). [Chan-Paton factors](chan-paton-factor.md) put all these fields in the adjoint of $U(k)$ for $k$ coincident branes. Ignoring the [tachyon](tachyon.md), their leading interactions are the [dimensional reduction](dimensional-reduction.md) of 26-dimensional [Yang-Mills theory](yang-mills-theory.md). This contains a Yang-Mills sector but is not pure four-dimensional [Yang-Mills theory](yang-mills-theory.md) and is not the supersymmetric six-scalar D3-brane theory of the ten-dimensional superstring.
