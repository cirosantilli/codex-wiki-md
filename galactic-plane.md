# Galactic plane

↑ **Parent:** [Milky Way](milky-way.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galactic_plane)

The plane of a disk-shaped [galaxy](galaxy-split.md) contains most of its stellar disk. In ordinary sky-coordinate usage the galactic plane refers to the [Milky Way](milky-way.md)'s disk plane. A concentration of [interstellar dust](interstellar-dust.md) near that plane produces a prominent component of [galactic foreground](galactic-foreground.md) emission.
