# External gravity wave

↑ **Parent:** [Inertia-gravity wave](inertia-gravity-wave.md)

An external gravity wave displaces a fluid's [free surface](free-surface.md) and moves nearly uniformly through the depth in the long-wave limit. In a layer of depth $H$, its leading [shallow-water approximation](shallow-water-approximation.md) speed is $\sqrt{gH}$.
