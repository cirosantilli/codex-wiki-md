# Epidermal growth factor receptor

↑ **Parent:** [Receptor tyrosine kinase](receptor-tyrosine-kinase.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epidermal_growth_factor_receptor)

In an illustrative [EGFR](epidermal-growth-factor-receptor.md) activation route, [EGF](epidermal-growth-factor.md) stabilizes an associated pair of [receptor](receptor-biochemistry.md) chains. Asymmetric contact between their [protein kinase](protein-kinase.md) [protein domains](protein-domain.md) activates one catalytic partner without requiring its activation-loop [phosphorylation](phosphorylation.md). Subsequent [phosphorylation](phosphorylation.md) of cytoplasmic tails creates recruitment sites. Preassociated inactive [EGFR](epidermal-growth-factor-receptor.md) populations can also occur: the cartoon's separated starting chains are not a universal initial condition.[Lemmon and Schlessinger, “Mechanisms of Receptor Activation” and “Allosteric activation of TKDs”, Figure 2F](cell-signaling-by-receptor-tyrosine-kinases-by-mark-a-lemmon-and-joseph-schlessinger.md)
