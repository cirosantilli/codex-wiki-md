# Prefix-freeness of fully parenthesized formulas

↑ **Parent:** [Unique readability theorem](unique-readability-theorem.md)

In the grammar $\phi::=p_i\mid\neg\phi\mid(\phi\to\phi)$, no [logical formula](logical-formula.md) is a proper initial segment of another. A recursive [parser](parser.md) reads one [variable](logical-variable.md), or a [negation](negation.md) followed by one [formula](logical-formula.md), or a parenthesized pair of [formulas](logical-formula.md) separated by [implication](logical-implication.md). [Structural induction](structural-induction.md) shows that this [parser](parser.md) consumes precisely one whole [formula](logical-formula.md). Its deterministic stopping position proves [prefix-freeness](prefix-freeness-of-fully-parenthesized-formulas.md). Consequently any [formula](logical-formula.md) substring in a valid [formula](logical-formula.md) coincides with the [formula](logical-formula.md) of a syntax subtree.
