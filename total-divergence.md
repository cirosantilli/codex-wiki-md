# Total divergence

↑ **Parent:** [Divergence](divergence.md)

A total divergence has the form $\nabla\mathbin\cdot\mathbf F$. The [divergence theorem](divergence-theorem.md) converts its volume integral into a boundary flux, which vanishes when the field has suitable decay or boundary conditions.
