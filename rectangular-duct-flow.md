# Rectangular duct flow

↑ **Parent:** [Pressure-driven channel flow](pressure-driven-channel-flow.md)

[Rectangular duct flow](rectangular-duct-flow.md) is [steady flow](steady-flow.md) of a [Newtonian fluid](newtonian-fluid.md) down a straight rectangular pipe under a constant axial [pressure gradient](pressure-gradient.md) $-G$. For stationary walls and [unidirectional flow](unidirectional-flow.md), [incompressibility](incompressible-flow.md) eliminates axial dependence and the [Navier-Stokes equation](navier-stokes-equation.md) becomes a two-dimensional [Poisson equation](poisson-s-equation.md) with zero [Dirichlet boundary conditions](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md). Subtracting a [plane Poiseuille flow](plane-poiseuille-flow.md) profile leaves a [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md) that repairs the side-wall values using [separation of variables](separation-of-variables.md). Uniqueness follows from [Green's first identity](green-s-first-identity.md): the difference of two solutions has zero boundary values and zero [integral](integral-split.md) of the squared [gradient](gradient.md), and hence vanishes.
