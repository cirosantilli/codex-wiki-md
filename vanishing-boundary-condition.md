# Vanishing boundary condition

↑ **Parent:** [Boundary condition](boundary-condition.md)

A vanishing boundary condition requires the relevant field, variation, or flux to tend to zero on the boundary. It removes boundary terms produced by [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md).
