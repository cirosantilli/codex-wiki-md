# Saturated line subbundle on a smooth curve

↑ **Parent:** [Locally free sheaf](locally-free-sheaf.md)

A rank-one subsheaf of a [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) on a [smooth algebraic curve](smooth-algebraic-curve.md) is saturated when the quotient is torsion-free. Intersecting a one-dimensional subspace of the generic fibre with each stalk constructs a saturated subsheaf. The [local ring of a smooth algebraic curve](local-ring-of-a-smooth-algebraic-curve.md) is a [discrete valuation ring](discrete-valuation-ring.md), so the subsheaf and its torsion-free quotient are [locally free](locally-free-sheaf.md). Saturating a nonzero map from a [line bundle](line-bundle.md) enlarges that line bundle by an [effective divisor](effective-cartier-divisor.md), recording the map's zeros.
