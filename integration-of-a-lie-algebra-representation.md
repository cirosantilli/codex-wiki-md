# Integration of a Lie-algebra representation

↑ **Parent:** [Lie algebra representation](lie-algebra-representation.md)

For a finite-dimensional real [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md), a finite-dimensional [Lie algebra representation](lie-algebra-representation.md) integrates uniquely to a smooth representation of the connected [simply connected](simply-connected-space.md) [Lie group](lie-group.md) with that [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md). For another connected group with the same [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md), integration is possible exactly when the representation of its universal cover is trivial on the covering kernel. This separates local Lie-algebra classification from global representation restrictions.
