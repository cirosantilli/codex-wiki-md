# Overspill

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical logic](mathematical-logic-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Overspill)

[Overspill](overspill.md) is the principle that an internal subset of a nonstandard extension containing every standard natural number must contain some nonstandard natural number. The [overspill lemma](overspill-lemma.md) is its definable-set instance in nonstandard arithmetic.
