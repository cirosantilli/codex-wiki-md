# Functoriality of chosen representations

↑ **Parent:** [Representation of a functor](representation-of-a-functor.md)

If $H:\mathcal A^{\mathrm{op}}\times\mathcal B\to\mathbf{Set}$ has chosen representations $\psi_B:\mathcal A(-,GB)\cong H(-,B)$, the [Yoneda lemma](yoneda-lemma.md) determines a unique [functor](functor.md) $G$ on morphisms by $\mathcal A(-,Gb)=\psi_{B'}^{-1}H(-,b)\psi_B$. This makes the representing [natural isomorphisms](natural-isomorphism.md) natural in $B$.
