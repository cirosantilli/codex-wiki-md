# ANOVA stratum

↑ **Parent:** [Analysis of variance](analysis-of-variance.md)

An [ANOVA stratum](anova-stratum.md) is an [orthogonal](orthogonal-vectors.md) response subspace representing one level of experimental variation, such as between blocks or within blocks. In a covariance model scalar on each such subspace, its [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) supplies the common variance scale for its projected coordinates. Treatment and residual [sums of squares in ANOVA](sum-of-squares-in-anova.md) must be compared within appropriate strata.
