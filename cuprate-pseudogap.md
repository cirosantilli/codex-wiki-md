# Cuprate pseudogap

↑ **Parent:** [Cuprate superconductor](cuprate-superconductor.md)

A partial, momentum-dependent suppression of low-energy electronic spectral weight observed in underdoped cuprates above the superconducting transition. It is not simply a full insulating band gap, and its observation alone does not establish either preformed pairs or a unique competing order. A spectral suppression and a thermodynamic phase boundary are different pieces of evidence. [2005 perspective: phenomenology and competing interpretations](the-pseudogap-friend-or-foe-of-high-tc-by-michael-r-norman-and-others.md)
