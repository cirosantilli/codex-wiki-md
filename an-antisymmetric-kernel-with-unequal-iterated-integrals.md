# An antisymmetric kernel with unequal iterated integrals

↑ **Parent:** [Fubini's theorem](fubini-s-theorem.md)

The function $(x-y)/(x+y)^3$ on the [unit square](unit-square.md) is not [Lebesgue integrable](lebesgue-integrable-function.md). On $0<y<x/2$, its absolute value is at least $4/(27x^2)$, whose [area](surface-area.md) [integral](integral-split.md) diverges logarithmically. Its two signed [iterated integrals](iterated-integral.md) nevertheless exist, ignoring null boundary sections, and equal $1/2$ and $-1/2$. This illustrates why [absolute integrability](integrability.md) is required for [Fubini's theorem](fubini-s-theorem.md).
