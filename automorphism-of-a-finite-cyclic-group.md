# Automorphism of a finite cyclic group

↑ **Parent:** [Automorphism group](automorphism-group.md)

Write the [finite cyclic group](finite-cyclic-group.md) as $C_n=\langle r\rangle$. A [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md) is determined by $r\mapsto r^a$; it is a [group automorphism](group-automorphism.md) exactly when $r^a$ generates $C_n$, equivalently $\gcd(a,n)=1$. Composition multiplies the exponents modulo $n$, proving $\operatorname{Aut}(C_n)\cong(\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z)^\times$. Its [group order](order-of-a-finite-group.md) is therefore [Euler's totient function](euler-totient-function.md) $\phi(n)$. A [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md) $C_2\to\operatorname{Aut}(C_n)$ is determined by a [unit](unit-in-a-ring.md) $a$ with $a^2\equiv1\pmod n$.
