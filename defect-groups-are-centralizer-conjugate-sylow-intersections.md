# Defect groups are centralizer-conjugate Sylow intersections

↑ **Parent:** [Defect group of a block](defect-group-of-a-block.md)

Let $P$ be any [Sylow subgroup](sylow-subgroup.md) containing a [defect group of a block](defect-group-of-a-block.md) $D$. Regard the [block of a group algebra](block-of-a-group-algebra.md) as a [permutation module](permutation-module.md) summand for $G\times G$, acting by left and right multiplication. Its vertex is $\Delta D$. Restricting to $P\times P$ retains a summand with this vertex. Transitive [permutation modules](permutation-module.md) for a [finite p-group](finite-p-group.md) are indecomposable, with their point stabilizers as vertices. Thus some orbit in $G$ has stabilizer $\Delta D$ at a suitably chosen point $c$. This means $c$ centralizes $D$ and $P\cap{}^cP=D$. Choosing $P$ to contain a Sylow subgroup of $N_G(D)$ shows that $D=O_p(N_G(D))$; every normal [p-subgroup](p-subgroup.md) of $G$ is contained in both Sylow factors and hence in $D$.
