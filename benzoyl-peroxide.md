# Benzoyl peroxide

↑ **Parent:** [Peroxide](peroxide.md)

An organic peroxide that can initiate [radical polymerization](radical-polymerization.md) by O–O bond homolysis. Benzoyloxy radicals, and radicals formed by their further reaction, can initiate monomer addition.
