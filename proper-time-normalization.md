# Proper-time normalization

↑ **Parent:** [Four-velocity](four-velocity.md)

With signature $(-,+,+,+)$ and units $c=1$, parametrizing a timelike worldline by [proper time](proper-time.md) makes its [four-velocity](four-velocity.md) satisfy $U^aU_a=-1$.
