# Witt null-bordism in dimensions one two and three

↑ **Parent:** [Witt bordism](witt-bordism.md)

Every odd-dimensional closed oriented [Witt space](witt-space.md) bounds its closed cone: the new vertex has odd-dimensional link, so introduces no Witt obstruction. For a closed oriented two-dimensional pseudomanifold, normalize to a [surface](topological-surface.md), fill each [surface](topological-surface.md) component by a [handlebody](handlebody.md), and attach a collar of the original space using the finite normalization map. Along the singular collar arcs the links are unions of circles; at their interior starting points the links are wedges of two-spheres. These links have zero middle [intersection homology](intersection-homology.md), so the construction is a three-dimensional Witt null-bordism.
