# One-time pad

↑ **Parent:** [Coding theory](coding-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One-time_pad)

For messages in a [finite additive group](finite-additive-group.md), a one-time pad encrypts $X$ as $C=X+K$, where the key $K$ is uniform and [independent](independent-random-variables.md) of $X$, as long as the message, and never reused. Then $C$ is uniform and [independent](independent-random-variables.md) of $X$, giving [perfect secrecy](perfect-secrecy.md).
