# Potential-vorticity evolution equation

↑ **Parent:** [Potential vorticity](potential-vorticity.md)

A [potential vorticity](potential-vorticity.md) budget has the form

$$
\frac{Dq}{Dt}=S_q.
$$

The [material derivative](material-derivative.md) follows the fluid motion and $S_q$ collects forcing or dissipation. With $S_q=0$ it becomes [potential-vorticity conservation](potential-vorticity-conservation.md). For a [quasi-geostrophic streamfunction](quasi-geostrophic-streamfunction.md), horizontal advection is $J(\psi,q)=\psi_xq_y-\psi_yq_x$.
