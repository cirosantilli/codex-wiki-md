# Piola-Kirchhoff stress tensors

↑ **Parent:** [Stress](stress.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piola–Kirchhoff_stress_tensors)

These [stress](stress.md) measures refer force or area to the [reference configuration](reference-configuration.md). Different index-order conventions distinguish a [first Piola-Kirchhoff stress tensor](first-piola-kirchhoff-stress-tensor.md) from its transposed [nominal stress tensor](nominal-stress-tensor.md). The [second Piola-Kirchhoff stress tensor](second-piola-kirchhoff-stress-tensor.md) has two material indices.
