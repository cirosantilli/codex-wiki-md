# Twin plane

↑ **Parent:** [Martensite](martensite.md)

A twin plane is a compatible planar interface between differently oriented crystal variants. In a piecewise homogeneous elastic deformation its reference normal is the $N$ in a [rank-one connection](rank-one-connection.md) between the two variant deformation gradients. Continuity of the deformation holds along this plane even though its derivative changes. Other material planes crossing the interface can kink after deformation.
