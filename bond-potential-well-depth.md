# Bond potential-well depth

↑ **Parent:** [Bond-dissociation energy](bond-dissociation-energy.md)

For a [diatomic molecule](diatomic-molecule.md), let $V(r)$ be the electronic [potential energy](potential-energy.md) at [bond length](bond-length.md) $r$, with the [atomic nuclei](atomic-nucleus-split.md) treated as fixed while finding the electronic [energy](energy-split.md). Choose zero at infinitely separated [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) in the specified electronic [quantum states](quantum-state.md). If the minimum occurs at $r_e$, the [bond potential-well depth](bond-potential-well-depth.md) is

$$
D_e=V(\infty)-V(r_e)=-V(r_e).
$$

The bottom of this curve describes an equilibrium [bond length](bond-length.md), not an attainable motionless vibrational [quantum state](quantum-state.md). Changing the zero of [potential energy](potential-energy.md) shifts both endpoints equally and leaves $D_e$ unchanged. A [quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md) approximates the curve near its minimum but rises without limit, so that local approximation alone cannot supply a dissociation asymptote. A [Morse potential](morse-potential.md) supplies both a minimum and a finite separated-fragment limit.[definition: subscripts e and 0 distinguish the potential-energy minimum and ground state](iupac-compendium-of-chemical-terminology-gold-book-by-international-union-of-pure-and-applied-chemistry/dissociation-energy.md)
