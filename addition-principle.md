# Addition principle

↑ **Parent:** [Combinatorics](combinatorics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Addition_principle)

The [rule of sum](addition-principle.md) counts alternatives in disjoint finite [sets](set-split.md) by adding their sizes. For a [disjoint union](disjoint-union.md) $A\sqcup B$, each element belongs to exactly one alternative, so $|A\sqcup B|=|A|+|B|$. Overlapping alternatives require accounting for repeated elements, as in the [inclusion-exclusion principle](inclusion-exclusion-principle.md). The groups indexed by first-return position in the [Catalan recurrence](catalan-recurrence.md) are disjoint alternatives.
