# Euclidean metric

↑ **Parent:** [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md)

The Euclidean metric is the constant [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md) on $\mathbb R^n$ given in Cartesian coordinates by $g=\sum_i(dx^i)^2$. Its [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md) has zero [Christoffel symbols](christoffel-symbol.md) in those coordinates, so it is flat and its geodesics are straight lines. The [Euclidean norm](euclidean-norm.md) is the pointwise length determined by this metric.
