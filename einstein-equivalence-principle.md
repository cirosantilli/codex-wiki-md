# Einstein equivalence principle

↑ **Parent:** [Equivalence principle](equivalence-principle.md)

The [Einstein equivalence principle](einstein-equivalence-principle.md) combines the [weak equivalence principle](weak-equivalence-principle.md) with local Lorentz invariance and local position invariance of nongravitational experiments. In a sufficiently small freely falling laboratory, their results agree with [special relativity](special-relativity-split.md) independently of its location and velocity. The [strong equivalence principle](strong-equivalence-principle.md) extends this claim to local gravitational experiments.
