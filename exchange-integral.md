# Exchange integral

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum chemistry](quantum-chemistry.md)

For two orthonormal spatial [atomic orbitals](atomic-orbital.md), the normalized symmetric and antisymmetric two-particle factors have Coulomb energies $J_{ab}+K_{ab}$ and $J_{ab}-K_{ab}$. With common orbitals, the fermionic [singlet state](singlet-state.md) and [triplet state](spin-one-half-triplet-state.md) therefore differ by $2K_{ab}$. This is an [exchange interaction](exchange-interaction.md) resulting from the state symmetry and the Coulomb operator, rather than a separate classical force.
