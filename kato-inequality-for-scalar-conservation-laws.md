# Kato inequality for scalar conservation laws

↑ **Parent:** [Doubling of variables for scalar conservation laws](doubling-of-variables-for-scalar-conservation-laws.md)

For bounded [entropy solutions](entropy-solution.md) of the same scalar law, $Q(u,v)=\operatorname{sgn}(u-v)(f(u)-f(v))$ satisfies the displayed distributional inequality, with initial value $|u_0-v_0|$ in its test-function form. The [doubling of variables for scalar conservation laws](doubling-of-variables-for-scalar-conservation-laws.md), local translation continuity and the [averaged initial trace of an entropy solution](averaged-initial-trace-of-an-entropy-solution.md) prove it. Its significance is comparison between two solutions rather than an inequality against a constant state.
