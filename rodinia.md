# Rodinia

↑ **Parent:** [Paleogeography](paleogeography.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rodinia)

A [Proterozoic](proterozoic.md) [supercontinent](supercontinent.md) assembled principally around 1.1–0.9 billion years ago and dispersed in stages later in the [Proterozoic](proterozoic.md). Its detailed [continental configuration](continental-configuration.md) is less tightly constrained than that of [Pangaea](pangaea.md), because later deformation and recycling have removed much of the original evidence.
