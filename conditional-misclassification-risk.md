# Conditional misclassification risk

↑ **Parent:** [Misclassification risk](misclassification-risk.md)

For a random [classifier](classifier.md) $C$ determined by training information $\mathcal T$, and an [independent](independent-random-variables.md) test pair $(X,Y)$, its conditional [misclassification risk](misclassification-risk.md) is $L(C)=\mathbb P(C(X)\ne Y\mid\mathcal T)$. It is itself a training-dependent random variable. The [tower property of conditional expectation](law-of-total-expectation.md) gives $\mathbb E L(C)=\mathbb P(C(X)\ne Y)$.
