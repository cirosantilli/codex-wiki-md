# Centrifugal pump

↑ **Parent:** [Pump](pump.md)

A rotating-impeller [pump](pump.md) that transfers mechanical energy to a fluid. At a fixed speed its head varies with flow; its characteristic curve and the connected system determine the [pump operating point](pump-operating-point.md).
