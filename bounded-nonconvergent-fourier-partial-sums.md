# Bounded nonconvergent Fourier partial sums

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier partial sum](fourier-partial-sum.md)

A continuous complex-valued [function](function-split.md) can have uniformly bounded [Fourier partial sums](fourier-partial-sum.md) which fail to converge at one point. Normalize harmonic sine polynomials by their harmonic coefficient sums, modulate them into disjoint high-frequency intervals and choose their degrees so that the complete norms are summable. At zero, complete blocks vanish while their midpoint prefixes have a common nonzero value. The [frequency-separated Fourier block series](frequency-separated-fourier-block-series.md) gives a uniform bound on every partial sum as well as two distinct subsequential values.
