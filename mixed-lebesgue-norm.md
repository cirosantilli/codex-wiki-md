# Mixed Lebesgue norm

↑ **Parent:** [Lp space](lp-space.md)

A [mixed Lebesgue norm](mixed-lebesgue-norm.md) first takes the [Lp norm](lp-norm.md) in one variable and then the norm in another. For finite $p,q$, $\|f\|_{L_x^pL_v^q}=(\int(\int|f(x,v)|^q\,dv)^{p/q}dx)^{1/p}$; an infinite exponent replaces that integral norm by an [essential supremum](essential-supremum.md). The order matters: generally $L_x^pL_v^q$ and $L_v^qL_x^p$ are different spaces. This distinction is crucial in [free transport dispersion](free-transport-dispersion.md).
