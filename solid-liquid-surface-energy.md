# Solid-liquid surface energy

↑ **Parent:** [Phase boundary](phase-boundary.md)

The excess free energy per area of a solid-liquid [phase boundary](phase-boundary.md). Curvature changes the equilibrium [temperature](temperature.md) through the [Gibbs--Thomson relation](gibbs-thomson-relation.md). Crystal anisotropy can make this energy and its associated surface stiffness depend on orientation.
