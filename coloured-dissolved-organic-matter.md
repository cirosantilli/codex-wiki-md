# Coloured dissolved organic matter

↑ **Parent:** [Ocean colour](ocean-colour.md)

The light-absorbing fraction of dissolved organic material. Its visible absorption commonly decreases approximately exponentially from blue to red, and can confound [chlorophyll retrieval](chlorophyll-retrieval.md).
