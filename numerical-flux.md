# Numerical flux

↑ **Parent:** [Finite volume method](finite-volume-method.md)

A numerical flux approximates the transported quantity through a cell interface using nearby states. For a two-state flux $F(a,b)$ approximating a [scalar conservation law](scalar-conservation-law.md) with flux $f$, consistency requires $F(u,u)=f(u)$. A [monotone conservative scheme](monotone-conservative-scheme.md) uses a flux nondecreasing in its left state and nonincreasing in its right state, with a time step making the complete update monotone.
