# Oriented frame bundle of de Sitter spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [Orthonormal frame bundle](orthonormal-frame-bundle.md)

Represent unit-radius [de Sitter spacetime](de-sitter-spacetime.md) by the spacelike unit [hyperboloid](hyperboloid.md) in five-dimensional [Minkowski spacetime](minkowski-spacetime.md). Appending its position vector as the last column of an oriented [pseudo-orthonormal frame](pseudo-orthonormal-frame.md) produces a matrix in $SO(4,1)$. Projection to that last column identifies $SO(4,1)$ with the oriented [orthonormal frame bundle](orthonormal-frame-bundle.md), a [principal bundle](principal-bundle.md) with group $SO(3,1)$. The full bundle allowing both orientations is $O(4,1)$ with group $O(3,1)$. Requiring a time orientation as well selects the corresponding identity components.
