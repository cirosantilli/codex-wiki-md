# Gnomonic projection

↑ **Parent:** [Sphere](sphere.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gnomonic_projection)

The gnomonic projection intersects rays from a [sphere](sphere.md)’s centre with a tangent plane. If $\mathbf b$ is its unit normal and $\mathbf e,\mathbf n$ are tangent basis vectors, a direction $\mathbf s$ maps to $f(\mathbf s\cdot\mathbf e,\mathbf s\cdot\mathbf n)/(\mathbf s\cdot\mathbf b)$. It maps [great circle](great-circle.md) arcs to straight lines and is singular at the tangent-plane horizon.
