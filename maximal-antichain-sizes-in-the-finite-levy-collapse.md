<h1 id="maximal-antichain-sizes-in-the-finite-levy-collapse">Maximal-antichain sizes in the finite Lévy collapse</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Finite-condition Lévy collapse](finite-condition-levy-collapse.md)

For a regular uncountable $\kappa$, the finite-condition collapse has the $\kappa$ [chain in a partial order](chain-in-a-partial-order.md) condition, by a regular-cardinal [Delta-system lemma](delta-system-lemma.md) and thinning to identical assignments on the finite root. Thus every maximal [forcing antichain](forcing-antichain.md) has ground-model size less than $\kappa$. There is no fixed size: for any nonzero [cardinal](cardinal-number.md) $\mu<\kappa$, assigning all values below $\mu$ at the one coordinate $(\mu,0)$ is a maximal [forcing antichain](forcing-antichain.md) of size $\mu$.
