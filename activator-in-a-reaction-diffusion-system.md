# Activator in a reaction-diffusion system

↑ **Parent:** [Reaction–diffusion system](reaction-diffusion-system.md)

An activator in an activator-inhibitor [reaction–diffusion system](reaction-diffusion-system.md) is a species whose local feedback enhances activation. Near a homogeneous equilibrium, positive self-feedback is reflected in the corresponding diagonal entry of the reaction [Jacobian matrix](jacobian-matrix.md). Cross-coupling to an [inhibitor](inhibitor-in-a-reaction-diffusion-system.md) and differing diffusion rates can generate a [Turing instability](turing-instability.md).
