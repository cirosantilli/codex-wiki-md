# Half-value layer

↑ **Parent:** [X-ray attenuation](x-ray-attenuation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Half-value_layer)

The [half-value layer](half-value-layer.md) is the thickness reducing the specified transmitted signal by one half. For constant monoenergetic [linear attenuation coefficient](linear-attenuation-coefficient.md) $\mu$, solving $e^{-\mu x_{1/2}}=1/2$ gives $x_{1/2}=\ln2/\mu$. The stipulated $\mu=0.40\,\mathrm{mm^{-1}}$ gives $x_{1/2}\simeq1.73\,\mathrm{mm}$; two such layers transmit one quarter. A broad [frequency spectrum](frequency-spectrum.md) need not retain the same [half-value layer](half-value-layer.md) after filtering.
