# Determinant of a block diagonal matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Determinant](determinant.md)

In the [Leibniz formula for a determinant](leibniz-formula-for-determinants.md) of a [block diagonal matrix](block-diagonal-matrix.md), a term is zero unless its [permutation](permutation.md) maps every block's row indices onto that same block's column indices. The surviving permutations are precisely independent permutations within the blocks; their signs and products of entries factor over the blocks. Summing therefore gives the product of the individual [determinants](determinant.md). Applied to $tI-T$, this also proves that the [characteristic polynomial](characteristic-polynomial.md) of a block diagonal [linear operator](linear-operator.md) is the product of the characteristic polynomials of its blocks.
