# Finite-horizon Robin spectral elimination

↑ **Parent:** [Linearizable Robin scattering for defocusing NLS](linearizable-robin-scattering-for-defocusing-nls.md)

Here $\Gamma=B^\sharp/(ad)$ and $\Delta=\mathcal D/(2k+ic)$. The difference follows algebraically from the [half-line NLS global relation](half-line-nls-global-relation.md) and the Robin symmetry. Multiplication by the spatial-temporal jump factor gives $e^{2ikx-4ik^2(T-t)}$, decaying in the second quadrant for $t<T$. A triangular analytic change of the [Riemann-Hilbert problem](riemann-hilbert-problem.md) removes this finite-horizon defect while retaining the reconstruction and transferring all genuine pole information to the known determinant. No unproved large-time decay of the boundary values is needed.
