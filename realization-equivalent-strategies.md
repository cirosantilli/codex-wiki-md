# Realization-equivalent strategies

↑ **Parent:** [Extensive-form game](extensive-form-game.md)

Two strategies for the same player are realization-equivalent when they induce the same [probability distribution](probability-distribution-split.md) on terminal histories against every fixed opposing [strategy profile](strategy-profile.md). The requirement is stronger than having equal [expected payoff](expected-payoff.md) against one particular opponent. For a finite [extensive-form game](extensive-form-game.md) with [perfect recall](perfect-recall.md), [Kuhn's theorem](kuhn-s-theorem.md) gives this equivalence between appropriate [mixed strategies](mixed-strategy.md) and [behavioural strategies](behavioural-strategy.md).
