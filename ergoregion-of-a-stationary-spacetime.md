# Ergoregion of a stationary spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [Stationary spacetime](stationary-spacetime.md)

For a chosen stationary time-translation [Killing vector field](killing-vector-field.md) $k$, this is the region where $k$ is spacelike and observers following its orbits cannot be timelike. In an asymptotically flat geometry, the usual choice is the nonrotating time translation at infinity. The stationary-limit boundary has $g(k,k)=0$, but need not be an [event horizon](event-horizon.md). The [Kerr ergoregion](kerr-ergoregion.md) is a particular rotating-black-hole example: its outer boundary touches the horizon only on the axis for nonzero subextremal spin.
