# Clonal development

↑ **Parent:** [Multicellular organism](multicellular-organism.md)

[Clonal development](clonal-development.md) builds a group through successive divisions of founding [biological cells](biological-cell.md), rather than by gathering previously separate lineages. In [snowflake yeast](snowflake-yeast.md), daughter [biological cells](biological-cell.md) remain attached after [budding](budding.md). Common ancestry does not imply that every descendant remains genetically identical forever: new [mutations](mutation.md) can arise. See the 2015 study cited under [multicellular organism](multicellular-organism.md).
