# Biadjacency matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Bipartite graph](bipartite-graph.md)

For a [bipartite graph](bipartite-graph.md) with ordered parts $X,Y$, its biadjacency matrix has rows indexed by $X$ and columns indexed by $Y$, with entry one for an [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) and zero otherwise. It has size $|X|$ by $|Y|$, rather than the square full [bipartite adjacency matrix](bipartite-adjacency-matrix.md) on $X\sqcup Y$, which is the block [matrix](matrix.md) $\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&M\\M^{\mathsf T}&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)$. Its [singular values](singular-value.md) describe the [normalized adjacency operator of a bipartite graph](normalized-adjacency-operator-of-a-bipartite-graph.md) after division by $\sqrt{|X||Y|}$.
