# Van Cittert-Zernike theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Spatial optical coherence](spatial-optical-coherence.md)

For a distant spatially incoherent quasimonochromatic source in the small-angle regime, the equal-delay mutual coherence across projected baseline $\mathbf b$ is the [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md) of its angular [intensity](intensity-physics.md). With matched collector response, the normalized degree is $g(\mathbf b)=\int I(\boldsymbol\theta)e^{-i2\pi\mathbf b\cdot\boldsymbol\theta/\lambda}d^2\theta/\int I(\boldsymbol\theta)d^2\theta$. Equal collector [intensities](intensity-physics.md) and matched polarizations make fringe visibility $|g|$. Finite bandwidth and unmatched optical paths introduce additional temporal-coherence averaging.
