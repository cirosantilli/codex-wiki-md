# Von Neumann algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Operator algebra](operator-algebra.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Von_Neumann_algebra)

A [unital](unital-algebra.md) [star-subalgebra](star-subalgebra.md) of the [bounded operators](continuous-linear-operator.md) on a [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) that is closed in the [weak operator topology](weak-operator-topology.md), equivalently in the [strong operator topology](strong-operator-topology.md). The [Von Neumann double commutant theorem](von-neumann-double-commutant-theorem.md) makes these conditions equivalent to equality with the double [commutant of an operator algebra](commutant-of-an-operator-algebra.md). It is [norm](norm.md) closed and hence a [C-star algebra](c-star-algebra.md).
