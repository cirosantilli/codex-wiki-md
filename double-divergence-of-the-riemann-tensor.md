# Double divergence of the Riemann tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Contracted Bianchi identity](contracted-bianchi-identity.md)

For the [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md), the [second Bianchi identity](second-bianchi-identity.md) gives $\nabla_iR^{ik}{}_{mn}=\nabla_mR^k{}_n-\nabla_nR^k{}_m$. Applying another [covariant derivative](covariant-derivative.md) and the [contracted Bianchi identity](contracted-bianchi-identity.md) reduces its divergence to a commuting scalar Hessian plus curvature commutators on the [Ricci tensor](ricci-tensor.md). The latter are the difference of symmetric tensors: a square of the [Ricci tensor](ricci-tensor.md) and $R^{ab}R_{anbm}$, which is symmetric in $m,n$ by curvature pair exchange and Ricci symmetry. Both differences vanish. This identity does not require vacuum field equations and is independent of the overall curvature sign convention.
