# Closed curve

↑ **Parent:** [Curve](curve.md)

A [curve](curve.md) $\gamma:[0,1]\to X$ whose endpoints agree, $\gamma(0)=\gamma(1)$. This is the same unbased notion as a [loop](loop-topology.md); self-intersections are allowed.
