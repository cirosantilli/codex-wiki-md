# Uniform square recurrence on the circle

↑ **Parent:** [Equidistribution of a quadratic polynomial with irrational leading coefficient](equidistribution-of-a-quadratic-polynomial-with-irrational-leading-coefficient.md)

For rational $a$, a multiple of its denominator gives exact integer recurrence. For irrational $a$, [Weyl differencing](weyl-differencing.md) and the [Weyl criterion](weyl-criterion.md) give an [equidistributed sequence](equidistributed-sequence.md) $am^2$ modulo one. Thus every parameter belongs to one of the open sets $\{a:\|am^2\|<\epsilon\}$. Compactness of the [circle group](circle-group.md) gives a finite subcover, hence a bound $N$ uniform in $a$; no explicit rate follows from this compactness step.
