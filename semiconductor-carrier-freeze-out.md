# Semiconductor carrier freeze-out

↑ **Parent:** [Semiconductor doping](semiconductor-doping.md)

At sufficiently low [temperature](temperature-split.md), thermal ionization of shallow dopants becomes inefficient, reducing the mobile [carrier density](carrier-density.md). An acceptor-doped sample may then have many neutral acceptors and few mobile [electron holes](electron-hole.md). Extracting an activation [energy](energy-split.md) requires a model of dopant ionization and compensation; the mobile density need not have the same simple activation exponent in every compensation regime.
