# Radial probability density

↑ **Parent:** [Probability density](probability-density.md)

The [radial probability density](radial-probability-density.md) $P(r)$ is defined so that $P(r)\,dr$ is the probability of finding a particle between radii $r$ and $r+dr$. The spherical volume element gives $P(r)=r^2\int|\psi(r,\Omega)|^2d\Omega$. For a spherically symmetric [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md), $P(r)=4\pi r^2|\psi(r)|^2$; for $\psi=R(r)Y_l^m(\Omega)$ with normalized [spherical harmonics](spherical-harmonic.md), $P(r)=r^2|R(r)|^2$. Its maximum need not coincide with the maximum of the density per unit volume: a hydrogenic $1s$ state has its largest spatial density at the origin but its most probable radius at $a_0/Z$.
