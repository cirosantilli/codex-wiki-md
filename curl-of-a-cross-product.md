# Curl of a cross product

↑ **Parent:** [Divergence and curl of a cross product](divergence-and-curl-of-a-cross-product.md)

The displayed [vector calculus](vector-calculus.md) identity follows by contracting the two alternating tensors in the component formula for [curl](curl.md). If both fields have zero [divergence](divergence.md), only the two directional derivatives remain. It rewrites the transport terms in the [adjoint linearized Navier-Stokes evolution](adjoint-linearized-navier-stokes-evolution.md).
