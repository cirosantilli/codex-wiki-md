# Perfect Lie algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Semisimple Lie algebra](semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md)

A [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) $L$ is perfect when $[L,L]=L$. Every semisimple Lie algebra over a field of characteristic zero is perfect.

Perfectness is a property of a [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md), defined by equality with its commutator algebra; semisimplicity is a sufficient condition in characteristic zero.
