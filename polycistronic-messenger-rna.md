# Polycistronic messenger RNA

↑ **Parent:** [Messenger RNA](messenger-rna.md)

A [polycistronic messenger RNA](polycistronic-messenger-rna.md) contains multiple protein-coding regions. Their translation can be regulated independently or coupled through local RNA structure and ribosome movement.
