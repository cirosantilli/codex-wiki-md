# Adenylate kinase

↑ **Parent:** [Enzyme](enzyme-split.md)

An [enzyme](enzyme-split.md) interconverting [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md), [ADP](adenosine-diphosphate.md) and [AMP](adenosine-monophosphate.md) through $2\mathrm{ADP}\rightleftharpoons\mathrm{ATP}+\mathrm{AMP}$. Near equilibrium, [AMP](adenosine-monophosphate.md):[ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) is proportional to the square of [ADP](adenosine-diphosphate.md):[ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) under fixed conditions.
