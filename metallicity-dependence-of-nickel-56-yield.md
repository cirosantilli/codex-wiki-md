# Metallicity dependence of nickel-56 yield

↑ **Parent:** [Type Ia supernova](type-ia-supernova.md)

Approximate a fixed [mass](mass.md) of iron-group freeze-out ashes by nickel-56 and iron-54, with no further weak neutronization and negligible residual light particles. Their electron fractions are $1/2$ and $26/54$. Conservation of the initial [electron fraction in stellar matter](electron-fraction-in-stellar-matter.md), $1/2-X_{22}/22$, then gives the displayed [nickel](nickel.md) [mass](mass.md) fraction. Its argument is the initial neon-22 [mass](mass.md) fraction, which need not equal the earlier [nitrogen](nitrogen.md) metallicity. The two-isotope mass-budget approximation is not an exact full nuclear equilibrium with all free-particle densities set to zero.
