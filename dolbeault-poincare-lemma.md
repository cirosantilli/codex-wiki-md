<h1 id="dolbeault-poincare-lemma">Dolbeault-Poincaré lemma</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Dolbeault operator](dolbeault-operator.md)

On a sufficiently small polydisc in a [complex manifold](complex-manifold.md), every $\bar\partial$-closed $(p,q)$-form with $q>0$ is locally $\bar\partial$-exact. A coefficientwise one-variable integral operator and induction over the coordinates prove the result.
