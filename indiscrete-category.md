# Indiscrete category

↑ **Parent:** [Category of small categories](category-of-small-categories.md)

The indiscrete category on a [set](set-split.md) has exactly one morphism between each ordered pair of objects. Every morphism is invertible. A functor into it is determined by its object assignment.
