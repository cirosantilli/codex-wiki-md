<h1 id="rotation-invariant-kahler-potential-on-the-complex-plane">Rotation-invariant Kähler potential on the complex plane</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Local real potential for a closed (1,1)-form](local-real-potential-for-a-closed-1-1-form.md)

A smooth real rotation-invariant potential is constant on every positive-radius circle, so its logarithmic-radius function $u$ is smooth on $\mathbb R$. Off the origin, $f_{z\bar z}=e^{-t}u''(t)$. Since $f$ is smooth on the entire plane, this coefficient extends smoothly through the origin. A finite [Taylor expansion](taylor-expansion.md), rather than a convergent series assumption, gives $f(z)=f(0)+b|z|^2+O(|z|^4)$.
