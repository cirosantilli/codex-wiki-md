# Faddeev-Zamolodchikov algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Factorized scattering](factorized-scattering.md)

Particle operators obey a rapidity-dependent exchange rule $A_i(\theta_1)A_j(\theta_2)=S_{ij}^{kl}(\theta_1-\theta_2)A_l(\theta_2)A_k(\theta_1)$, with repeated species indices summed. A consistent [associative algebra](associative-algebra-split.md) must give the same coefficients when a triple product is brought to decreasing or increasing [rapidity](rapidity.md) order by either sequence of adjacent exchanges. This is the [Faddeev-Zamolodchikov associativity constraint](faddeev-zamolodchikov-associativity-constraint.md). Rapidity ordering here is distinct from the creation/annihilation convention of ordinary [normal ordering](normal-ordering.md).
