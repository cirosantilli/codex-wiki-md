# Protein phosphatase

↑ **Parent:** [Enzyme](enzyme-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protein_phosphatase)

A [protein phosphatase](protein-phosphatase.md) is an [enzyme](enzyme-split.md) that removes a [phosphate](phosphate.md) group from a modified [protein](protein-split.md) through [hydrolysis](hydrolysis.md). This restores a modifiable site rather than regenerating [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) by simply running [protein kinase](protein-kinase.md) chemistry backward.
