# Parseval identity for Fourier series

↑ **Parent:** [Parseval identity](parseval-identity.md)

For a [square-integrable function](square-integrable-function.md) of [period](period-of-a-function.md) $T$, use [Fourier coefficients](fourier-coefficient.md) $u_r=T^{-1}\int_0^Tf(t)e^{-2\pi irt/T}\,dt$. [Fourier orthogonality](fourier-orthogonality.md) proves the displayed identity for a finite [trigonometric polynomial](trigonometric-polynomial.md). The [mean-square convergence](convergence-in-l2.md) of the [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md) then passes it to the limit. It includes the zero-frequency contribution $|u_0|^2$; excluding that contribution gives the variance rather than the full [mean-square signal power](signal-power.md).
