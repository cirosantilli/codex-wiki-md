# Ricci soliton

↑ **Parent:** [Riemannian geometry](riemannian-geometry-split.md)

A [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md) satisfying the displayed equation for a [vector field](vector-field.md) $X$ and constant $\lambda$. A gradient Ricci soliton has $X=\nabla f$; it is steady when $\lambda=0$.
