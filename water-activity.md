# Water activity

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md)

Water activity measures the chemical potential of water relative to a chosen pure-water standard state. Under ideal-vapour conditions above a flat solution, it is the equilibrium vapour pressure divided by that above pure liquid water at the same temperature. Dissolved solute usually lowers activity. [Raoult's law](raoult-s-law.md) gives the ideal dilute-solution limit; nonideal interactions require an activity model.
