# Projective unitary representation

↑ **Parent:** [Wigner's theorem](wigner-s-theorem.md)

Because state vectors differing by a [quantum phase](quantum-phase.md) represent the same ray, a unitary realization of a group action need only satisfy

$$
U(g_1)U(g_2)=e^{i\phi(g_1,g_2)}U(g_1g_2).
$$

A [projective unitary representation](projective-unitary-representation.md) is a [projective representation](projective-representation.md) whose chosen representatives are unitary operators. The phase factors disappear on rays.
