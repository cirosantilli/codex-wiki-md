# G-optimal design

↑ **Parent:** [Optimal experimental design](optimal-experimental-design.md)

A G-optimal design minimizes the maximum [design sensitivity function](design-sensitivity-function.md) over the full design region. Thus it minimizes the worst [variance of a fitted regression mean](variance-of-a-fitted-regression-mean.md), excluding the separate noise [variance](variance-split.md) of a new observation. The sensitivity average is $p$, so its supremum is at least $p$.
