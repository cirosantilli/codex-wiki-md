# Hecke operators are self-adjoint for the Petersson inner product

↑ **Parent:** [Petersson inner product](petersson-inner-product.md)

On level-one [cusp forms](cusp-form.md), determinant-normalized [Hecke operators](hecke-operator.md) satisfy $\langle T_nf,g\rangle=\langle f,T_ng\rangle$. Unfolding their finite correspondences transfers a matrix to its inverse; its adjugate has the same determinant and reverses the correspondence. Since these operators commute, the cusp space has a common [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) of [Hecke eigenforms](hecke-eigenform.md), with real eigenvalues.
