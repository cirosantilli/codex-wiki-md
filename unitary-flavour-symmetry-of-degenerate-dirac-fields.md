# Unitary flavour symmetry of degenerate Dirac fields

↑ **Parent:** [Internal symmetry of a classical field theory](internal-symmetry-of-a-classical-field-theory.md)

For $N$ [Dirac fields](dirac-field.md) with common [mass](mass.md), $\mathcal L=\bar\Psi(i\not\partial-m\mathbf1_N)\Psi$ is invariant under constant complex-linear rotations $\Psi\mapsto U\Psi$ with $U\in U(N)$. A Hermitian flavour generator $T^A$ has [Noether current](noether-current.md) $j_A^\mu=\bar\Psi\gamma^\mu T^A\Psi$. A nondegenerate [fermion mass matrix](fermion-mass-matrix.md) restricts the symmetry to unitary matrices commuting with it. If the theory is entirely free and rotations into [charge-conjugate fields](charge-conjugate-field.md) are allowed, its [enhanced internal symmetry of free Dirac fields](enhanced-internal-symmetry-of-free-dirac-fields.md) can be larger than this subgroup.
