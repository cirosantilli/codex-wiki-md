# Salt-advection feedback

↑ **Parent:** [Two-box overturning model](two-box-overturning-model.md)

A circulation change alters the [salinity](salinity.md) contrast and hence the [density](density.md) difference that supports the circulation. In a thermally dominated positive branch, weaker exchange removes the imposed salinity contrast less effectively, making the high-latitude reservoir fresher and weakening the circulation further. This positive feedback permits [bistability](bistability.md) and a [saddle-node bifurcation](saddle-node-bifurcation.md).
