# Superhorizon adiabatic CDM mode in radiation domination

↑ **Parent:** [CDM density equation in a matter-radiation universe](cdm-density-equation-in-a-matter-radiation-universe.md)

During [radiation domination](radiation-domination.md), $a\propto\tau$ and $8\pi G\rho_Ra^2=3/\tau^2$. The [adiabatic initial conditions](adiabatic-initial-conditions.md) reduce the [CDM density equation in a matter-radiation universe](cdm-density-equation-in-a-matter-radiation-universe.md) to $\delta_C''+\delta_C'/\tau-4\delta_C/\tau^2=0$. Its [Euler-Cauchy equation](euler-cauchy-equation.md) powers are two and minus two. The growing solution is the displayed one, in the stated CDM-comoving [synchronous gauge](synchronous-gauge-in-cosmology.md).
