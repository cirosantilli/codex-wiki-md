# Tate module

↑ **Parent:** [Galois representation](galois-representation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tate_module)

For an [abelian variety](abelian-variety-split.md) $A$ and a [prime number](prime-number.md) $\ell$ different from the characteristic, the inverse limit of its geometric $\ell^n$-torsion, with transition multiplication by $\ell$, is its Tate module. For an [elliptic curve](elliptic-curve.md), it is free of rank two over the [p-adic integers](p-adic-integer.md) and satisfies $T_\ell(E)/\ell^nT_\ell(E)\simeq E[\ell^n]$. The [absolute Galois group](absolute-galois-group.md) acts continuously and commutes with all base-defined [endomorphisms](endomorphism.md).
