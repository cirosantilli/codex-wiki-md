# Bosonic superexchange

↑ **Parent:** [Schwinger boson representation](schwinger-boson-representation.md)

Effective exchange of internal states through virtual [boson](boson.md) hopping into energetically costly occupation sectors. For two [bosons](boson.md) initially one per site and charging energy $U\Delta^2/4$, the second-order operator is $-(t^2/U)(3+4\mathbf s_a\cdot\mathbf s_b)$ for dimensionless [spins](spin.md). The triplet is lowered and the singlet is unchanged in this example; signs for other energy functions require their actual denominators.
