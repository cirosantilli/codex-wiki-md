# Directional derivative of a convex function is sublinear

↑ **Parent:** [Directional derivative](directional-derivative.md)

For a [locally Lipschitz function](locally-lipschitz-function.md) that is [convex](convex-function.md) near $x$, increasing secant slopes have a finite right limit. Positive rescaling of $t$ gives positive homogeneity in $y$. The [convexity](convex-function.md) bound $f(x+t(y+z))\leq[f(x+2ty)+f(x+2tz)]/2$ gives subadditivity after subtracting $f(x)$, dividing by $t$ and taking the limit. Thus $p_x$ is a finite [sublinear functional](sublinear-function.md), although it need not be a [linear functional](linear-functional.md).
