# Annihilator of the valuation ring

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier transform over a local field](fourier-transform-over-a-local-field.md)

For an [additive character](additive-character.md) $\psi$ of a non-Archimedean [local field](local-field.md) $F$,

$$
\mathcal O_F^\perp
=\{y\in F:\psi(xy)=1\text{ for every }x\in\mathcal O_F\}.
$$

For the character induced from the standard character of $\mathbb Q_p$, this annihilator is the [inverse different](inverse-different.md).
