# Eta meson

↑ **Parent:** [Eta and eta prime mesons](eta-and-eta-prime-mesons.md)

The lighter physical neutral [pseudoscalar meson](pseudoscalar-meson.md) in the [eta and eta prime mesons](eta-and-eta-prime-mesons.md) system. The pure [Eta octet state](eta-octet-state.md) is a useful approximation to its flavor content, not its exact physical [mass](mass.md) eigenstate.
