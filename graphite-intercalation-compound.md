# Graphite intercalation compound

↑ **Parent:** [Graphite](graphite.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graphite_intercalation_compound)

A layered material with guest species inserted between [graphite](graphite.md) sheets. Alkali-metal guests can donate [electron](electron.md) density to graphitic bands, altering carrier [concentration](concentration.md), spacing and scattering.
