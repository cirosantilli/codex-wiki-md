# Hydrogen radial Hamiltonian factorization

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrogen atom](hydrogen-atom.md)

With [Bohr radius](bohr-radius.md) $a$, define $A_l=a[i p_r/\hbar-(l+1)/r+1/((l+1)a)]/\sqrt2$. For regular radial [bound states](bound-state.md) the radial inner product and compatible domains give the displayed [factorization](factorization.md). Expanding the first-order factors produces the centrifugal term $l(l+1)\hbar^2/(2mr^2)$ and Coulomb term $-\hbar^2/(mar)$. [factorization](factorization.md) converts a second-order spectral problem into a positivity bound and first-order terminating-state equation.
