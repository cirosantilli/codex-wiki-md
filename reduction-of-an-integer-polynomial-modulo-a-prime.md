# Reduction of an integer polynomial modulo a prime

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial ring](polynomial-ring.md)

Coefficientwise reduction defines a [ring homomorphism](ring-homomorphism.md)

$$
\mathbb Z[X]\longrightarrow\mathbb F_p[X].
$$

It preserves sums, products, and divisibility. The [Frobenius endomorphism](frobenius-endomorphism.md) gives $\overline{g(X^p)}=\bar g(X)^p$.
