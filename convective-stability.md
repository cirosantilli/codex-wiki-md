# Convective stability

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal convection](thermal-convection.md)

A stable stratification gives a restoring [buoyancy](buoyancy.md) force and positive squared [buoyancy frequency](buoyancy-frequency.md) for an adiabatically displaced parcel. An adverse [specific entropy](specific-entropy.md) gradient can instead drive [convection](convection.md); other forces such as rotation can modify the full stability criterion.
