# Rank of an observation

↑ **Parent:** [Order statistic](order-statistic.md)

The rank of an observation in a sample is its position after sorting from smallest to largest. A tied block occupying positions $a,\ldots,b$ assigns each member the average rank $(a+b)/2$. Without omissions, the ranks sum to $n(n+1)/2$, including under this tie rule. [Wilcoxon signed-rank tests](wilcoxon-signed-rank-test.md) rank the absolute nonzero paired differences, while [Wilcoxon rank sum tests](mann-whitney-u-test.md) rank the pooled observations from two samples; these are different rankings.
