# Hadronic tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Deep inelastic scattering](deep-inelastic-scattering.md)

The inclusive product of hadronic-current [matrix elements](matrix-element.md), summed over final states with a [Lorentz-invariant phase-space measure](lorentz-invariant-phase-space-measure.md). Electromagnetic [current conservation](conserved-current.md) makes both indices transverse to the exchanged [four-momentum](four-momentum.md).
