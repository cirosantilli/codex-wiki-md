# Evaporation truncation parameter

↑ **Parent:** [Forced evaporative cooling](forced-evaporative-cooling.md)

The ratio of an evaporation cutoff to thermal [energy](energy-split.md). Keeping the cutoff fixed while lowering [temperature](temperature-split.md) increases this ratio and reduces population in the escaping tail.
