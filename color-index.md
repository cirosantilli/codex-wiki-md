# Color index

↑ **Parent:** [Photometric system](photometric-system.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Color_index)

A [colour index](color-index.md) is the difference of an object's measured [apparent magnitudes](apparent-magnitude.md) in two [photometric passbands](photometric-passband.md), such as $B-V$. Since magnitude is logarithmic, this difference measures a flux ratio after the respective [magnitude zero points](magnitude-zero-point.md) are accounted for. It describes spectral shape rather than the object's overall brightness, and changes with both intrinsic radiation properties and [interstellar extinction](interstellar-extinction.md).
