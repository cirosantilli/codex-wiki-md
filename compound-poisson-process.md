# Compound Poisson process

↑ **Parent:** [Lévy process](levy-process.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compound_Poisson_process)

A compound Poisson process has the form

$$
X_t=\sum_{n=1}^{N_t}Y_n,
$$

where $N$ is a [Poisson process](poisson-process.md) and the independent identically distributed marks $Y_n$ are independent of $N$. It is a [Lévy process](levy-process.md) with a finite Lévy measure.
