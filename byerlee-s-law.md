<h1 id="byerlee-s-law">Byerlee's law</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Frictional sliding](frictional-sliding.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Byerlee's_law)

An empirical relationship summarizing the high friction measured for many rock surfaces as a function of effective normal load. Weak [fault gouge](fault-gouge.md) or high [pore pressure](pore-pressure.md) can produce different effective fault strengths. Named for [James Byerlee](james-byerlee.md).
