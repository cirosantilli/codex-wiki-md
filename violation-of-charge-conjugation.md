# Violation of charge conjugation

↑ **Parent:** [Charge conjugation](charge-conjugation.md)

A theory violates [charge conjugation](charge-conjugation.md) when conjugating particles to antiparticles changes a physical interaction or its transition probabilities. This is different from [CP violation](cp-violation.md): an interaction can violate [charge conjugation](charge-conjugation.md) and [parity symmetry in quantum field theory](parity-symmetry-in-quantum-field-theory.md) separately while preserving their combination.
