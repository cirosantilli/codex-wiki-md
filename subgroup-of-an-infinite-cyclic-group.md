# Subgroup of an infinite cyclic group

↑ **Parent:** [Infinite cyclic group](infinite-cyclic-group.md)

For $G=\langle g\rangle$ infinite, every [subgroup](subgroup.md) is $\langle g^m\rangle$ for a unique integer $m\geq0$. This is the classification of subgroups of the [additive group of integers](additive-group-of-integers.md) transported through a [group isomorphism](group-isomorphism.md). For $m>0$ the [subgroup index](index-of-a-subgroup.md) is $m$ and the subgroup is again an [infinite cyclic group](infinite-cyclic-group.md); for $m=0$ it is trivial and has infinite [subgroup index](index-of-a-subgroup.md).
