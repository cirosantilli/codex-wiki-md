# Syntax tree of a formula

↑ **Parent:** [Abstract syntax tree](abstract-syntax-tree.md)

The [syntax tree](syntax-tree-of-a-formula.md) of a [logical formula](logical-formula.md) records its [recursive construction](structural-induction.md). A [propositional variable](propositional-variable.md) is a leaf, [negation](negation.md) has one child, and [binary implication](logical-implication.md) has an [ordered pair](ordered-pair.md) of children. A [subformula occurrence](subformula.md) corresponds to a node together with the subtree rooted there. Different occurrences may have identical [formula strings](logical-formula.md).
