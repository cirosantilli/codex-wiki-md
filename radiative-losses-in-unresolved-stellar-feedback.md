# Radiative losses in unresolved stellar feedback

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar feedback](stellar-feedback.md)

Depositing [supernova](supernova.md) [energy](energy-split.md) into dense unresolved gas can radiate it away before a numerically resolved expansion develops. Equal injected [energy](energy-split.md) therefore need not imply equal outflow [momentum](momentum-split.md) or heating at different resolution. A feedback model should represent the coupling and cooling phase it intends to approximate, and comparisons should include gas recycling and the resulting stellar and circumgalactic properties.
