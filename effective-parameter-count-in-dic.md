# Effective parameter count in DIC

↑ **Parent:** [Deviance information criterion](deviance-information-criterion.md)

The [DIC](deviance-information-criterion.md) effective parameter count compares mean [Bayesian deviance](bayesian-deviance.md) with deviance at the [posterior mean](posterior-mean.md). It need not be an integer or equal the number of coefficients. Interpretation depends on the parameterization and the chosen observational likelihood; nuisance intercepts count when they appear in that likelihood.
