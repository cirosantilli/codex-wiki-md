# Conformal automorphism of the upper half-plane

↑ **Parent:** [Upper half-plane (complex analysis)](upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md)

A [conformal bijection](biholomorphism.md) of the [complex upper half-plane](upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md) to itself is a real [Möbius transformation](mobius-transformation.md) with $ad-bc>0$. Three distinct boundary values determine it uniquely. Its real derivative is positive away from its pole, and $\operatorname{Im}\phi(z)=(ad-bc)\operatorname{Im}z/|cz+d|^2$.
