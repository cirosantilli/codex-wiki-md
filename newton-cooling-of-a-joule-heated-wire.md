# Newton cooling of a Joule-heated wire

↑ **Parent:** [Joule-heated cylindrical wire](joule-heated-cylindrical-wire.md)

The surface flux of a uniformly heated cylinder is $Ha/2$. Equating it to the Newton-cooling flux $\alpha[T(a)-T_{\rm air}]$ gives the displayed surface [temperature](temperature-split.md). The full [temperature](temperature-split.md) profile adds the internal parabolic excess to this surface value. Electrical power per length equals the surface [heat](heat-split.md) loss per length in steady state.
