# Optical gradient force

↑ **Parent:** [Optical tweezers](optical-tweezers.md)

An optical gradient force arises from spatial variation in illumination. In a usual [optical trap](optical-tweezers.md) for a suitable transparent particle with higher [refractive index](refractive-index.md) than its surroundings, it draws the particle towards high [light intensity](light-intensity.md). It supplies a [restoring force](restoring-force.md) near a stable trap, but three-dimensional confinement also depends on the [optical scattering force](optical-scattering-force.md). The direction and size depend on the particle and field; attraction to the brightest point is not universal. [Ashkin and colleagues, single-beam gradient optical trap, 1986](https://doi.org/10.1364/OL.11.000288).
