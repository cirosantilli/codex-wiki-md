# Free fall in a fixed singular isothermal sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Gravitational collapse](gravitational-collapse.md)

A test particle released from rest at $r_0$ in a fixed spherical [mass density](density.md) $\rho(r)=\rho_0(r_0/r)^2$ obeys $\ddot r=-4\pi G\rho_0r_0^2/r$. Integrating once gives $\dot r^2=8\pi G\rho_0r_0^2\ln(r_0/r)$. The substitution $u=\sqrt{\ln(r_0/r)}$ then gives $t(r)=(8G\rho_0)^{-1/2}\operatorname{erf}(u)$, and the arrival time at the centre is $(8G\rho_0)^{-1/2}$. This is [free fall](free-fall.md) in a frozen [singular isothermal sphere](singular-isothermal-sphere.md), not the self-consistent pressureless collapse of the background. An initially resting material shell with the same initial enclosed [mass](mass.md) instead arrives after $\sqrt{\pi/(32G\rho_0)}$, because its enclosed [mass](mass.md) stays fixed while the cloud contracts.
