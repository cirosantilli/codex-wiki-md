# Achromatic lens

↑ **Parent:** [Chromatic aberration](chromatic-aberration.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Achromatic_lens)

An [achromatic lens](achromatic-lens.md) combines [optical lens](lens-optics.md) elements with different [optical dispersion](dispersion-optics.md) so that two specified [wavelengths](wavelength.md) have the same [focal length](focal-length.md) in the [paraxial approximation](paraxial-approximation.md). This brings their parallel incoming [light rays](light-ray.md) to a common [optical focus](focus-optics.md), helping an [optical camera](optical-camera.md) form a sharper image under mixed-colour illumination. A common implementation is an [achromatic doublet](achromatic-doublet.md): a positive [crown glass](crown-glass-optics.md) element paired with a negative [flint glass](flint-glass.md) element. [Fitzpatrick, Chromatic aberration, compound lenses](https://farside.ph.utexas.edu/teaching/302l/lectures/node142.html).

The [achromatic doublet](achromatic-doublet.md) does not require either material to have zero [optical dispersion](dispersion-optics.md). Instead, the positive element gains positive [optical power](optical-power.md) toward blue, while the negative element becomes more negative. Suitable [Abbe numbers](abbe-number.md) and signed [optical powers](optical-power.md) let these changes cancel even though their reference [optical powers](optical-power.md) do not cancel. For example, the [achromatic doublet power balance](achromatic-doublet-power-balance.md) calculation below combines $+10\,\mathrm D$ with $-5\,\mathrm D$ to obtain $+5\,\mathrm D$, or [focal length](focal-length.md) $0.2\,\mathrm m$, with zero primary F–C [optical power](optical-power.md) difference. The smaller [Abbe number](abbe-number.md) of the negative element makes its weaker reference [optical power](optical-power.md) sufficient to oppose the positive element's colour change.

An [achromatic lens](achromatic-lens.md) is corrected at two chosen [wavelengths](wavelength.md), not necessarily at every [wavelength](wavelength.md) between them. The remaining [focal length](focal-length.md) variation is the [secondary spectrum](secondary-spectrum.md). Correcting [axial chromatic aberration](axial-chromatic-aberration.md) also does not automatically remove [lateral chromatic aberration](lateral-chromatic-aberration.md) throughout an imaging system, [spherical aberration](spherical-aberration.md), or [diffraction](diffraction.md). Actual designs therefore need [ray tracing](ray-tracing.md) with the intended [wavelengths](wavelength.md), element thicknesses, separations and [apertures](aperture.md). [Nikon, ED glass and secondary spectrum](https://imaging.nikon.com/sport-optics/guide/fieldscopes/choosing/choosing_03/); [Caltech AY 105, Optical aberrations, 2011, Part A: doublet ray tracing and through-focus comparison](ay-105-lab-experiment-3-optical-aberrations-by-california-institute-of-technology.md).
