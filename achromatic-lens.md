# Achromatic lens

↑ **Parent:** [Chromatic aberration](chromatic-aberration.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Achromatic_lens)

An achromatic lens combines elements with different [Abbe numbers](abbe-number.md) to make two specified [wavelengths](wavelength.md) share a [focal length](focal-length.md). Other wavelengths can retain secondary chromatic error.
