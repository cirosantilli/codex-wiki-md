# Bell-basis measurement

↑ **Parent:** [Bell state](bell-state-split.md)

A Bell-basis measurement is the four-outcome projective measurement whose projectors are onto the four [Bell states](bell-state-split.md). It jointly measures two qubits and is the sender's measurement in [quantum teleportation](quantum-teleportation.md).
