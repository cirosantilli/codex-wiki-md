# Open planar sector

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean ray](euclidean-ray.md)

The open planar sector between two distinct [Euclidean rays](euclidean-ray.md) of positive slope consists of nonzero points whose directions lie strictly between their directions. Sectors between consecutive members of an ordered collection of [Euclidean rays](euclidean-ray.md) are disjoint.
