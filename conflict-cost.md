# Conflict cost

↑ **Parent:** [Cognitive control](cognitive-control.md)

A performance contrast between conditions with greater and smaller response conflict. For [reaction time](reaction-time.md), $C=\overline{RT}_{\rm incongruent}-\overline{RT}_{\rm congruent}$. A group advantage specific to conflict requires a difference of such contrasts, not just faster responses in both conditions.
