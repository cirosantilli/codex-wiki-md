# Fokas method

↑ **Parent:** [Boundary value problem](boundary-value-problem.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fokas_method)

The Fokas method uses a [global relation for a linear boundary value problem](global-relation-for-a-linear-boundary-value-problem.md) between transformed boundary traces, complex spectral symmetries, and [contour integration](contour-integration.md) to eliminate unknown traces or construct a numerical boundary scheme. The distinction between the domains of analyticity of the transforms and the decay sectors of spectral exponentials controls valid contour deformations.
