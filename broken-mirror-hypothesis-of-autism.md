# Broken mirror hypothesis of autism

↑ **Parent:** [Autism](autism.md)

A proposal that dysfunction of action observation–execution matching explains [autistic](autism.md) social differences. Its broad form requires direct evidence connecting the mechanism to the relevant behaviour, not merely atypical activation or an [imitation](imitation.md) score.
