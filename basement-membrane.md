# Basement membrane

↑ **Parent:** [Extracellular matrix](extracellular-matrix.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basement_membrane)

A [basement membrane](basement-membrane.md) is a specialized extracellular layer associated with the basal surface of an epithelium and other tissue interfaces. Breaching it is a mechanically and biologically distinct step from moving across a culture surface.
