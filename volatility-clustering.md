# Volatility clustering

↑ **Parent:** [Autoregressive conditional heteroscedasticity](autoregressive-conditional-heteroscedasticity.md)

Volatility clustering means that large absolute observations tend to be followed by further large absolute observations, and small ones by small ones. In an [ARCH process](autoregressive-conditional-heteroscedasticity.md), persistence is in the [conditional variance](conditional-variance.md) rather than necessarily in the signed observations.
