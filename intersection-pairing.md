# Intersection pairing

↑ **Parent:** [Intersection form](intersection-form.md)

An intersection pairing counts signed intersections of transverse representatives of complementary [homology classes](homology-class.md), using one relative representative when the [manifold](topological-manifold.md) has boundary. With $\mathbb F_2$ coefficients it is defined without [orientation](orientation-of-a-simplex.md) choices. [Poincare-Lefschetz duality](lefschetz-duality.md) makes this a [perfect pairing](perfect-pairing.md) for compact [manifolds](topological-manifold.md).
