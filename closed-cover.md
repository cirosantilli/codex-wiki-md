# Closed cover

↑ **Parent:** [Topology](topology-split.md)

A closed cover of a [topological space](topological-space.md) is a family of [closed sets](closed-set.md) whose union is the whole space. A finite closed cover can be used in separation and antipodal arguments: in a [metric space](metric-space.md), vanishing distance to a closed member means actual membership. Unlike an [open cover](open-cover.md), a closed cover does not automatically have a partition of unity subordinate to its members.
