# Viscous Burgers step solution with negative flux

↑ **Parent:** [Viscous Burgers equation](viscous-burgers-equation.md)

For positive diffusivity, the [Cole-Hopf transformation](cole-hopf-transformation.md) with $f=2\alpha\partial_\theta\log\psi$ converts the negative-flux equation into the [heat equation](heat-equation.md). Step data give a ratio of two Gaussian-tail integrals $J$. At $\theta=-UZ/2$, $J=1$ and $f=U/2$. Its [vanishing-viscosity limit](vanishing-viscosity-limit.md) selects the shock or [rarefaction wave](rarefaction-wave.md) in the [Burgers Riemann problem with negative flux](burgers-riemann-problem-with-negative-flux.md), depending on the sign of $U$.
