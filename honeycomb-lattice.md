# Honeycomb lattice

↑ **Parent:** [Planar graph](planar-graph-split.md)

The honeycomb lattice is the periodic degree-three [planar graph](planar-graph-split.md) consisting of the vertices and edges of a regular hexagonal tiling. It has two vertex classes; every edge joins opposite classes. Its [planar dual graph](planar-dual-graph.md) is the [triangular lattice](triangular-lattice.md).
