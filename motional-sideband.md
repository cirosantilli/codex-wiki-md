# Motional sideband

↑ **Parent:** [Trapped-ion internal and motional states](trapped-ion-internal-and-motional-states.md)

A motional sideband is a transition that changes internal excitation and a motional [quantum number](quantum-number.md) together. Define the internal [energy](energy-split.md) gap as $\hbar\omega_0>0$. A carrier drives $|g,n\rangle\leftrightarrow|e,n\rangle$; the first red sideband drives $|g,n\rangle\leftrightarrow|e,n-1\rangle$; the first blue sideband drives $|g,n\rangle\leftrightarrow|e,n+1\rangle$. Their resonant drive [angular frequencies](angular-frequency.md) follow by subtracting these [energy levels](energy-level.md). For a [Raman transition](raman-transition.md), the drive is a beam-difference [frequency](frequency.md) with a sign fixed by the selected internal transition.

Choose a real carrier [Rabi angular frequency](rabi-angular-frequency.md) $\Omega$ defined so that resonant carrier excitation is $\sin^2(\Omega t/2)$. In the [Lamb-Dicke regime](lamb-dicke-regime.md), isolated first-sideband [Hamiltonians](hamiltonian.md) in a rotating frame can be written, after choosing the pulse [phase](phase-waves.md), as

$$
H_r=\frac{\hbar\eta\Omega}{2}(\sigma_+a+\sigma_-a^\dagger),\qquad
H_b=\frac{\hbar\eta\Omega}{2}(\sigma_+a^\dagger+\sigma_-a),\qquad
\sigma_+=|e\rangle\langle g|,\quad\sigma_-=\sigma_+^\dagger.
$$

These formulas use a [rotating-wave approximation](rotating-wave-approximation.md): neglected carrier and other-sideband terms oscillate rapidly compared with the retained coupling. The drive's effective width and sideband coupling must be small compared with $\nu$; sufficient suppression of off-resonant carrier excitation must also be checked. The red interaction is a [Jaynes-Cummings model](jaynes-cummings-model.md) with a motional quantum replacing a cavity [photon](photon.md). The blue interaction creates internal excitation and a motional quantum together; the external [laser](laser-split.md) supplies [energy](energy-split.md). The driven-system rotating-frame [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) alone is not the total laboratory [energy](energy-split.md).
