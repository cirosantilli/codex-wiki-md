# Power put option

↑ **Parent:** [Put option](put-option.md)

A power put has [financial payoff](contingent-claim-payoff.md) $(K-S)^n$ below the [strike price](strike-price.md) $K$ and zero at or above it, where $n$ is a positive [integer](integer-split.md). For $n=1$ this is the ordinary [put option](put-option.md). For every $n\geq1$ the payoff is decreasing and is a [convex function](convex-function.md) of the [stock](stock.md) price. A [European option](european-contingent-claim.md) permits exercise only at expiry; a [perpetual power put option](perpetual-power-put-option.md) permits exercise at any finite time without a fixed expiry.
