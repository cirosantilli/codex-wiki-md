# Holonomy representation of a flat connection

↑ **Parent:** [Flat connection](flat-connection.md)

The [holonomy](holonomy.md) of a [flat connection](flat-connection.md) is invariant under based homotopy of loops, giving a [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md) from the [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md) into the structure group after choosing a frame at the base point. Changing that frame conjugates the representation. Its associated bundle is constructed from the universal cover and this monodromy. It differs from the natural representation of a Riemannian holonomy group on a tangent fiber.
