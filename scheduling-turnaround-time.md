# Scheduling turnaround time

↑ **Parent:** [Process scheduling](process-scheduling.md)

The duration from a task's arrival $a_i$ until its completion $c_i$: $T_i=c_i-a_i$. It includes execution and all intervening waits. For A under [round-robin scheduling](round-robin-scheduling.md) in [process scheduling](process-scheduling.md), $T_A=9-0=9$, despite a [scheduling response time](scheduling-response-time.md) of zero.
