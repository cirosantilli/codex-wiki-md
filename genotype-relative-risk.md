# Genotype relative risk

↑ **Parent:** [Genetic association](genetic-association.md)

The genotype relative risk compares the [penetrance](penetrance.md) for [genotype](genotype.md) $g$ with that of a chosen baseline $g_0$. The common baseline factor cancels when computing [conditional probabilities](conditional-probability.md) of transmitted genotypes given an affected offspring. It is a ratio of disease probabilities, unlike an [odds ratio](odds-ratio.md).
