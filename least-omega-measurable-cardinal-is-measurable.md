# Least omega-measurable cardinal is measurable

↑ **Parent:** [Omega-measurable cardinal](omega-measurable-cardinal.md)

If $\kappa$ is the least [omega-measurable cardinal](omega-measurable-cardinal.md) and $U$ witnesses it, failure of $\kappa$-completeness partitions a member of $U$ into fewer than $\kappa$ fibres, none belonging to $U$. The [pushforward ultrafilter](pushforward-ultrafilter.md) under the fibre-index map is then a nonprincipal countably complete measure on a smaller cardinal, a contradiction. Thus $U$ is $\kappa$-complete and $\kappa$ is a [measurable cardinal](measurable-cardinal.md).
