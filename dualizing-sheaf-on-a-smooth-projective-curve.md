# Dualizing sheaf on a smooth projective curve

↑ **Parent:** [Serre duality](serre-duality.md)

For [vector bundles](vector-bundle.md) on a [smooth projective curve](smooth-projective-curve.md), a dualizing sheaf is a [line bundle](line-bundle.md) $D_C$ with a trace $H^1(C,D_C)\to k$ making the evaluation pairings $H^i(C,E)\times H^{1-i}(C,E^\vee\otimes D_C)\to k$ perfect for $i=0,1$. Choose a finite generically separable map to the [projective line](projective-line.md). The trace-dual module for this finite map, tensored with $\Omega^1_{\mathbb P^1}$, is $\Omega^1_C$ by the different–differential identity. Finite pushforward and [residue duality on the projective line](residue-duality-on-the-projective-line.md) then prove the pairings, rather than assuming the curve-duality theorem to establish them.
