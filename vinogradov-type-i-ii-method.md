<h1 id="vinogradov-type-i-ii-method">Vinogradov Type I–II method</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Bilinear sum](bilinear-sum.md)

This method uses cancellation in [Type I sums](type-i-sum.md) and [Type II sums](type-ii-sum.md) to control a sum weighted by the [Von Mangoldt function](von-mangoldt-function.md). The [Vaughan identity](vaughan-s-identity.md) separates the short-factor contributions from bilinear ones. [Fourier separation of interval cutoffs](fourier-separation-of-interval-cutoffs.md) permits a hyperbolic product cutoff. [Ben Green's 2007 notes, Sections 2.3–2.4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0710.0823) give a version adapted to binary digit sums.
