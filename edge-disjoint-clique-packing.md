# Edge-disjoint clique packing

↑ **Parent:** [Clique (graph theory)](clique-graph-theory.md)

An edge-disjoint clique packing is a collection of $r$-[cliques](clique-graph-theory.md) no two of which share an [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md); they may share [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md). Its maximum cardinality $Z_r(G)$ changes by at most one when a single [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) is toggled. Removing that [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) destroys at most one packed [clique](clique-graph-theory.md), which makes this variable suitable for an [edge-exposure martingale](edge-exposure-martingale.md).
