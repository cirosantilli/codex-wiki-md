# Interior moment matching in a finite exponential family

↑ **Parent:** [Mean parameter of an exponential family](mean-parameter-of-an-exponential-family.md)

For a finite support whose [convex hull](convex-hull.md) has full dimension, every interior target mean is realized by a unique natural parameter. Minimize the [coercive function](coercive-function.md) $\log\sum_ne^{\theta\cdot n}-\theta\cdot\mu$; its [gradient](gradient.md) is the mean discrepancy and its [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) is the positive-definite [covariance matrix](covariance-matrix.md) of the support.
