# Classical Virasoro constraint algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Virasoro constraint](virasoro-constraint.md)

With the [oscillator symplectic form of an open string](oscillator-symplectic-form-of-an-open-string.md), the quadratic generators $L_n=\sum_k\alpha_k\cdot\alpha_{n-k}/2$ obey $\{\alpha_k,L_n\}=-ik\alpha_{k+n}$. Their [Poisson brackets](poisson-bracket.md) close without a central term, so they are [first-class constraints](first-class-constraint.md). Quantization requires [normal ordering](normal-ordering.md) and can produce the [Virasoro central extension](virasoro-central-extension.md).
