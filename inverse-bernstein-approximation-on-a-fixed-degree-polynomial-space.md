# Inverse Bernstein approximation on a fixed-degree polynomial space

↑ **Parent:** [Bernstein polynomial](bernstein-polynomial.md)

On the fixed finite-dimensional space of polynomials of degree at most $d$, uniform convergence of $B_n$ on each monomial and [equivalence of norms in finite dimensions](equivalence-of-norms-in-finite-dimensions.md) imply $\varepsilon_n=\|B_n-I\|_{\rm op}\to0$. A [Neumann series](neumann-series.md) then gives $\|B_n^{-1}-I\|_{\rm op}\le\varepsilon_n/(1-\varepsilon_n)$ for sufficiently large $n$. Consequently the displayed convergence holds. A strictly positive polynomial has positive minimum on the compact interval, so its inverse Bernstein approximants are also strictly positive for all sufficiently large $n$.
