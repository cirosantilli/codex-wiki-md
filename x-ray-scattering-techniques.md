# X-ray scattering techniques

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum scattering](quantum-scattering-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/X-ray_scattering_techniques)

[X-ray scattering techniques](x-ray-scattering-techniques.md) use the change in a scattered [photon](photon.md)'s direction and, in inelastic measurements, its [energy](energy-split.md) to probe matter. [Inelastic X-ray scattering](inelastic-x-ray-scattering.md) transfers energy to excitations such as [phonons](phonon.md); elastic methods probe spatial structure without that energy transfer.
