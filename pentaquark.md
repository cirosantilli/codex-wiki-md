# Pentaquark

↑ **Parent:** [Baryon](baryon.md)

A [baryon](baryon.md) with constituent content four [quarks](quark.md) and one [antiquark](antiquark.md), whose net [baryon number](baryon-number.md) is one. Such content admits more flavour representations than three quarks alone. A representation in the tensor product is not evidence by itself of a bound state.
