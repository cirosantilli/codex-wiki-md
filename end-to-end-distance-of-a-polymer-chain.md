# End-to-end distance of a polymer chain

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer physics](polymer-physics.md)

The end-to-end vector joins the two ends of a [polymer](polymer-split.md) chain. In an isotropic unforced [polymer solution](polymer-solution.md), its vector [expected value](expected-value-split.md) is zero; a useful measure of chain size is its root-mean-square length. For an [ideal chain](ideal-chain.md) with $N$ independent isotropic steps of length $b$, the cross terms in the square of the step sum have zero [expected value](expected-value-split.md), so $R_{\mathrm{rms}}^2=Nb^2$. For a long [Gaussian chain](gaussian-chain.md), its [radius of gyration](radius-of-gyration.md) obeys $R_g^2=R_{\mathrm{rms}}^2/6$. If $N$ counts chemical [repeat units](repeat-unit.md) instead of independent [Kuhn length](kuhn-length.md) segments, the statistical length used in $R_{\mathrm{rms}}^2=Nb^2$ must be calibrated to that choice of $N$; it is not automatically the physical bond length or the [Kuhn length](kuhn-length.md) itself.
