# Finite codimension in a Banach space

↑ **Parent:** [Banach space](banach-space-split.md)

For a closed [linear subspace](vector-subspace.md) $Y$ of a [Banach space](banach-space-split.md) $X$, finite codimension means the vector quotient $X/Y$ has finite [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md). This definition does not subtract two infinite dimensions. A local [stable manifold](stable-manifold.md) has the codimension of its tangent subspace. If the linearized phase space has a [direct sum](direct-sum.md) decomposition $X=E^s\oplus E^u$ with $\dim E^u=1$, then $X/E^s$ is isomorphic to $E^u$, so a local stable manifold tangent to $E^s$ has codimension one.
