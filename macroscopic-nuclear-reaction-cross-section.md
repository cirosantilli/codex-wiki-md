# Macroscopic nuclear reaction cross section

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear reaction cross-section](nuclear-reaction-cross-section.md)

A reaction coefficient formed from target [number densities](number-density.md) and microscopic [nuclear reaction cross-sections](nuclear-reaction-cross-section.md). It has inverse-length units. Multiplying it by the scalar [neutron flux](neutron-flux.md) gives the corresponding reaction rate per volume, under the stated energy averaging.
