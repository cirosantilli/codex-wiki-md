# Oxygenic photosynthesis

↑ **Parent:** [Photosynthesis](photosynthesis.md)

[Oxygenic photosynthesis](oxygenic-photosynthesis.md) uses [water](water.md) as an [Electron](electron.md) source and releases [molecular oxygen](molecular-oxygen.md). [Plants](plant.md), [algae](algae.md) and [cyanobacteria](cyanobacteria.md) provide examples. The defining distinction from [anoxygenic photosynthesis](anoxygenic-photosynthesis.md) is [molecular oxygen](molecular-oxygen.md) release, not merely capture of [light](light.md).
