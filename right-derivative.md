# Right derivative

↑ **Parent:** [Derivative](derivative.md)

The [right derivative](right-derivative.md) is the displayed one-sided [limit](limit-of-a-function.md), when it exists as a finite number. It describes differentiation at the left endpoint of a [closed interval](closed-real-interval.md) without requiring the [function](function-split.md) to be defined to the left. An ordinary [derivative](derivative.md) at an interior point requires matching left and right [derivatives](derivative.md). For example, the [absolute value](absolute-value.md) [function](function-split.md) has right derivative one at zero and left derivative minus one, so it has no ordinary [derivative](derivative.md) there.
