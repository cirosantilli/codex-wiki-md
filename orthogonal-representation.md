# Orthogonal representation

↑ **Parent:** [Group representation](group-representation.md)

An [orthogonal representation](orthogonal-representation.md) is a real [group representation](group-representation.md) preserving a positive-definite [inner product](inner-product.md), equivalently a [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md) into an [orthogonal group](orthogonal-group.md). Every finite-dimensional [continuous](continuous-function.md) [real representation](real-representation.md) of a [compact group](compact-group.md) has this form after averaging an [inner product](inner-product.md) over [Haar measure](haar-measure.md), by [orthogonalization of a compact-group representation](orthogonalization-of-a-compact-group-representation.md).
