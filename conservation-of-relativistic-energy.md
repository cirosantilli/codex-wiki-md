# Conservation of relativistic energy

↑ **Parent:** [Energy–momentum relation](energy-momentum-relation.md)

In an isolated relativistic collision, total four-momentum is conserved; its time component states conservation of total relativistic energy.

This is the relativistic form of [conservation of energy](conservation-of-energy.md), expressed through the conserved [four-momentum](four-momentum.md).
