<h1 id="richardson-s-ether-spray">Richardson's ether spray</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Local anaesthesia](local-anaesthesia.md)

A historical [local anaesthesia](local-anaesthesia.md) method described by [Benjamin Ward Richardson](benjamin-ward-richardson.md) in 1866: an atomizer distributes [diethyl ether](diethyl-ether.md), whose rapid [vaporization](vaporization.md) cools the surface. The physical cooling mechanism and the author's historical account of [nerve](nerve.md) signalling should be distinguished.[1866, apparatus and mechanism](on-a-new-and-ready-mode-of-producing-local-anaesthesia-by-benjamin-ward-richardson.md)
