# Gauge-scalar global symmetry of covariantly fixed QED

↑ **Parent:** [Global symmetry in field theory](global-symmetry-in-field-theory.md)

Adding a free real [scalar field](scalar-field.md) $\phi$ to covariantly gauge-fixed [QED](quantum-electrodynamics.md) gives an infinitesimal global invariance $\delta A_\mu=\varepsilon\partial_\mu\phi$, $\delta\psi=-ie\varepsilon\phi\psi$, $\delta\phi=-\varepsilon(\partial\cdot A)/\xi$. The matter terms transform as an ordinary [gauge transformation](gauge-transformation.md). The scalar kinetic variation combines with the [gauge fixing](gauge-fixing.md) variation into $\delta\mathcal L=-(\varepsilon/\xi)\partial_\mu[(\partial\cdot A)\partial^\mu\phi]$. Consequently the action is invariant under boundary conditions removing this total derivative, without using field equations.
