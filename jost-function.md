# Jost function

↑ **Parent:** [S-matrix](s-matrix.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jost_function)

For radial [quantum scattering](quantum-scattering.md), the incoming coefficient of the regular solution defines the Jost function, with convention $S(k)=F(-k)/F(k)$. A zero at $k=i\kappa$, $\kappa>0$, removes the exponentially growing component and produces a [bound state](bound-state.md), subject to regularity and normalizability. Poles of the outgoing numerator alone need not produce [bound states](bound-state.md).
