<h1 id="cassini-s-identity">Cassini's identity</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Fibonacci number](fibonacci-number.md)

For the [Fibonacci numbers](fibonacci-number.md) $F_0=0$, $F_1=1$, the identity $F_{n+1}F_{n-1}-F_n^2=(-1)^n$ holds for $n\ge1$. It follows by [induction](mathematical-induction.md): replacing each term using the [Fibonacci recurrence](fibonacci-recurrence.md) negates the left-hand side when $n$ increases by one, and the value at $n=1$ is $-1$.
