# Instantaneous-reheating horizon matching

↑ **Parent:** [Number of e-folds](number-of-e-folds.md)

Start with an inflationary patch of physical [radius](radius.md) $H_i^{-1}$ in units $c=1$. After $N$ e-folds and subsequent expansion, its present [radius](radius.md) is $H_i^{-1}e^Na_0/a_{\rm reh}$. Requiring it to cover the present [Hubble radius](hubble-radius.md) gives the displayed inequality. With instantaneous conversion to [radiation](radiation.md), fixed relativistic degrees of freedom and a fixed ratio $H_i/H_{\rm end}$, [energy](energy-split.md) conservation gives $H_i\propto T_{\rm reh}^2$, whereas adiabatic subsequent expansion gives $a_0/a_{\rm reh}\propto T_{\rm reh}$. Thus the required $N$ increases approximately as $\log T_{\rm reh}$. Holding $H_i$ fixed while varying the end [energy](energy-split.md) independently is a different comparison and reverses that simple temperature-only trend. The inflationary [energy](energy-split.md) history and post-inflationary [entropy](entropy-split.md) history must be specified.[Section II, equations 2 and 6–8](how-long-before-the-end-of-inflation-were-observable-perturbations-produced-by-andrew-r-liddle-and-samuel-m-leach.md)
