# Polarization tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Massless closed-string vertex operator](massless-closed-string-vertex-operator.md)

A closed-string polarization tensor specifies the target-spacetime tensor state multiplying $\partial X^\mu\bar\partial X^\nu e^{ik\cdot X}$. Its decomposition into symmetric trace-free, antisymmetric and trace parts separates the graviton, [Kalb–Ramond field](kalb-ramond-field.md) and [dilaton](dilaton.md) polarizations.
