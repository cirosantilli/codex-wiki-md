# Lagrangian pressure perturbation

↑ **Parent:** [Eulerian and Lagrangian fluid perturbations](eulerian-and-lagrangian-fluid-perturbations.md)

The [Lagrangian pressure perturbation](lagrangian-pressure-perturbation.md) is the change in [pressure](pressure.md) experienced by a displaced [fluid element](fluid-element.md): $\Delta p=\delta p+\boldsymbol\xi\cdot\nabla p_0$. For a free surface against fixed external [pressure](pressure.md), $\Delta p=0$, rather than $\delta p=0$ at the original surface.
