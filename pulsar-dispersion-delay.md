# Pulsar dispersion delay

↑ **Parent:** [Pulsar](pulsar.md)

A [radio wave](radio-wave.md) traversing a dilute cold [plasma](plasma-physics.md) has a [group velocity](group-velocity.md) that depends on its [frequency](frequency.md). In the ideal unmagnetized limit, the [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation-split.md) is $\omega^2=c^2k^2+\omega_p^2$, where $\omega_p$ is the angular [plasma frequency](plasma-frequency.md). For $\omega\gg\omega_p$, differentiate to obtain $v_g=c\sqrt{1-\omega_p^2/\omega^2}$, then expand $1/v_g$ to first order. The excess travel time over a vacuum path is

$$
t(f)-t(\infty)\simeq\frac{e^2}{8\pi^2\epsilon_0m_ec}\frac{1}{f^2}\int n_e\,dl.
$$

Here $f=\omega/(2\pi)$ is ordinary [frequency](frequency.md), $e$ is the magnitude of the [elementary charge](elementary-charge.md), $m_e$ is the [electron mass](electron-mass.md), $\epsilon_0$ is the [vacuum permittivity](vacuum-permittivity.md), $n_e$ is [electron](electron.md) number density, and $c$ is the [speed of light](speed-of-light.md). This approximation neglects magnetic corrections, collisions and multipath scattering; its positive sign means lower [frequencies](frequency.md) arrive later.
