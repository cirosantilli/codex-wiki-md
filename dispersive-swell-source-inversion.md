# Dispersive swell source inversion

↑ **Parent:** [Deep-water gravity wave](deep-water-gravity-wave.md)

For an impulsive distant source of [deep-water gravity waves](deep-water-gravity-wave.md), a component of period $T$ travels at [group velocity](group-velocity.md) $gT/(4\pi)$. Its arrival at distance $L$ obeys the displayed law, so inverse period increases linearly with arrival time. Two arrivals determine both $L$ and $t_0$. Finite source duration, currents, refraction and passage through [sea ice](sea-ice.md) can invalidate the simple inversion.
