# Understanding POWER Multiprocessors (2011)

↑ **Parent:** [Computer science bibliography](computer-science-bibliography-split.md)

Primary research article: [https://www.cl.cam.ac.uk/~pes20/ppc-supplemental/pldi105-sarkar.pdf](https://www.cl.cam.ac.uk/~pes20/ppc-supplemental/pldi105-sarkar.pdf)

Section 2.1 introduces store buffering and message passing. Section 2.2 explains barriers and dependencies, including why a genuine [address dependency](address-dependency.md) can order reads while a bare [control dependency](control-dependency.md) does not. This source corroborates the general [memory model](memory-model.md) discussion, not the availability of examination answers.
