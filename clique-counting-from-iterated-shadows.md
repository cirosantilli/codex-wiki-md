# Clique counting from iterated shadows

↑ **Parent:** [Iterated lower shadow](iterated-lower-shadow.md)

For the [uniform set family](uniform-set-family.md) of vertex sets of $r$-cliques, its $(r-2)$-fold [lower shadow](lower-shadow.md) consists of graph edges. Repeated use of the [Kruskal-Katona theorem](kruskal-katona-theorem.md) bounds clique count from edge count. Sixteen four-cliques have at least twenty-three triples in their shadow and at least eighteen edges in their second shadow, by the expansions $16=\binom64+\binom33$ and $23=\binom63+\binom32$. Hence a graph with fifteen edges has at most fifteen four-cliques, attained by the [complete graph](complete-graph.md) on six vertices.
