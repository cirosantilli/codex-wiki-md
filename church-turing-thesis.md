<h1 id="church-turing-thesis">Church–Turing thesis</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Computability theory](computability-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Church–Turing_thesis)

The Church–Turing thesis asserts that every effectively calculable function is computable by a formal model equivalent to a [Turing machine](turing-machine.md). It identifies an informal notion of effective procedure with a mathematical class rather than stating a theorem within one formal system.
