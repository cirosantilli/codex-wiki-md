# Group manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Lie group](lie-group.md)

The [group manifold](group-manifold.md) is the underlying [smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md) of a [Lie group](lie-group.md), with smooth multiplication and inversion. Its global topology need not be determined by its [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md): the [SU(2) group](su-2-group.md) and [SO(3) group](so-3-group.md) have isomorphic [Lie algebras](lie-algebra-split.md) but different [fundamental groups](fundamental-group.md).
