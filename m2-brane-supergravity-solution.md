# M2-brane supergravity solution

↑ **Parent:** [M2-brane](m2-brane.md)

The transverse [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md) $H$ solves the field equations away from membrane sources. Positive centres contribute $Q_a/|y-y_a|^6$ in eight dimensions. Its [Killing spinors](killing-spinor.md) are $H^{-1/6}\epsilon_0$ with one oriented membrane projector, giving sixteen supersymmetries. The three-form's sign and membrane orientation must be chosen together.
