# Futility boundary

↑ **Parent:** [Group sequential design](group-sequential-design.md)

A futility boundary specifies an interim result below which a [clinical trial](clinical-trial.md) stops without rejecting the [null hypothesis](null-hypothesis.md). Removing only potential rejection paths cannot increase the [Type I error](type-i-and-type-ii-errors.md). With a finite boundary and a nonsingular joint normal test-statistic distribution, it strictly reduces that error relative to the corresponding fixed-final-boundary test.
