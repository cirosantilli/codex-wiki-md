# Deubiquitinating enzyme

↑ **Parent:** [Ubiquitin](ubiquitin.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deubiquitinating_enzyme)

A [deubiquitinating enzyme](deubiquitinating-enzyme.md) cleaves ubiquitin from a substrate or edits ubiquitin chains. Removing a modification can rescue a substrate from recognition, recycle ubiquitin during degradation, or change a signalling outcome.
