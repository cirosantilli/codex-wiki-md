# Physiological effector

↑ **Parent:** [Homeostasis](homeostasis.md)

A [physiological effector](physiological-effector.md) performs the action that changes the [regulated physiological variable](regulated-physiological-variable.md). In [thermoregulation](thermoregulation.md), [sweat glands](sweat-gland.md), changes in [skin](skin.md) blood flow, and [skeletal muscle](skeletal-muscle.md) [shivering](shivering.md) contribute different actions. The command, the action and the resulting [temperature](temperature.md) are three distinct readouts.
