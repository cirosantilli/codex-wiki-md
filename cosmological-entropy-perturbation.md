# Cosmological entropy perturbation

↑ **Parent:** [Non-adiabatic pressure perturbation](non-adiabatic-pressure-perturbation.md)

A cosmological entropy perturbation changes the relative composition of cosmic fluids while leaving the total curvature initially unchanged. For [cold dark matter](cold-dark-matter.md) and photons, a common relative entropy variable is

$$
S_{c\gamma}=\delta_c-\frac34\delta_\gamma.
$$
