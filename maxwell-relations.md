# Maxwell relations

↑ **Parent:** [Thermodynamics](thermodynamics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maxwell_relations)

A Maxwell relation equates mixed derivatives of a thermodynamic potential. For $dF=-S\,dT-P\,dV$, equality of mixed derivatives gives $(\partial S/\partial V)_T=(\partial P/\partial T)_V$. For $dG=-S\,dT+V\,dP$, it gives $(\partial S/\partial P)_T=-(\partial V/\partial T)_P$. These identities connect experimentally accessible response functions to [heat capacities](heat-capacity.md) and [compressibility](compressibility.md).
