# Principal-value reciprocal distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Multiplication of a distribution by a smooth function](multiplication-of-a-distribution-by-a-smooth-function.md)

The [Cauchy principal value](cauchy-principal-value.md)

$$
\left\langle\operatorname{pv}\frac1x,\varphi\right\rangle
=\lim_{\varepsilon\downarrow0}\int_{|x|>\varepsilon}\frac{\varphi(x)}x\,dx
$$

defines a [distribution](distribution-mathematical-analysis.md). Near zero the odd constant contribution cancels, and the remaining numerator is $O(x)$. It satisfies $x\operatorname{pv}(1/x)=1$. Hence all solutions of $xv=1$ are $v=\operatorname{pv}(1/x)+c\delta_0$ by the [kernel of multiplication by a coordinate](kernel-of-multiplication-by-a-coordinate.md).
