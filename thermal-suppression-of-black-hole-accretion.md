# Thermal suppression of black-hole accretion

↑ **Parent:** [Active-galactic-nucleus feedback](active-galactic-nucleus-feedback.md)

Heating a gas reservoir reduces its [Bondi accretion](bondi-accretion.md) rate because $\dot M_B\propto\rho_\infty c_s^{-3}$. At fixed density, increasing the [sound speed](speed-of-sound.md) by ten reduces the rate by about a thousand and the gravitational capture radius by a hundred. Expansion and density loss can strengthen this negative feedback. Neither the density nor the cold pre-heating accretion rate is determined by the temperature change alone.
