# Photonic entanglement-swapping experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Bell test](bell-test.md)

In 1998, [Jian-Wei Pan](jian-wei-pan.md), [Dik Bouwmeester](dirk-bouwmeester.md), [Harald Weinfurter](harald-weinfurter.md) and [Anton Zeilinger](anton-zeilinger.md) created two [photon](photon.md) pairs by two passages of a pulsed pump through a [crystal](crystal.md) using [spontaneous parametric down-conversion](spontaneous-parametric-down-conversion.md). Interfering the inner [photons](photon.md) at a [nonpolarizing beam splitter](nonpolarizing-beam-splitter.md) implemented a partial [Bell-basis measurement](bell-basis-measurement.md). Narrow [optical filters](optical-filter.md) and spatial filtering removed distinguishing information; changing the outer [polarizer](polarizer.md) bases checked the conditional correlations. Fourfold detections selected the [entanglement swapping](entanglement-swapping.md) events. The reported interference supported conditional [quantum entanglement](entangled-state.md), but the paper reserved a statistically significant [Bell inequality](bell-inequality.md) violation for improved visibility; it was not a demonstration closing the [detection loophole](detection-loophole.md).[pp. 3892-3894, Eq. (3), Figs. 2-3; visibility discussion](experimental-entanglement-swapping-entangling-photons-that-never-interacted-by-jian-wei-pan-and-others.md)

The ideal calculation makes the distinction between conditional and unconditional [quantum states](quantum-state.md) explicit. Start with [spin singlet states](spin-singlet-state.md) $|\psi^-\rangle_{12}|\psi^-\rangle_{34}$, where $|\psi^-\rangle=(|HV\rangle-|VH\rangle)/\sqrt2$ and $H,V$ denote orthogonal [photon polarizations](photon-polarization.md). Taking the [inner product](inner-product-split.md) with $\langle\psi^-|_{23}$ gives $-|\psi^-\rangle_{14}/2$: expanding the four terms leaves precisely the two antisymmetric outer terms. That [Bell-basis measurement](bell-basis-measurement.md) outcome has [probability](probability.md) $1/4$ and leaves the outer pair in a [spin singlet state](spin-singlet-state.md). Ignoring the inner outcome instead leaves the outer [density matrix](density-matrix-split.md) $I_4/4$, a [maximally mixed state](maximally-mixed-state.md). A conditional preparation flag is useful for later [Bell tests](bell-test.md) only with the timing and setting conditions in [setting-independent heralding preserves Bell locality](setting-independent-heralding-preserves-bell-locality.md).
