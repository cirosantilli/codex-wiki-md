# Magnetic field of a rotating charged annulus

↑ **Parent:** [Biot-Savart law](biot-savart-law.md)

An [annulus](annulus-mathematics.md) with uniform [surface charge density](surface-charge-density.md) $\sigma$, radii $a,b$ and rigid [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md) $\Omega$ carries ring [electric current](electric-current.md) $dI=\sigma\Omega r\,dr$. The centre [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) of each ring is $\mu_0dI/(2r)$ by the [Biot-Savart law](biot-savart-law.md). Integrating gives $\mathbf B(0)=\mu_0\sigma\Omega(b-a)\hat{\mathbf n}/2$, equivalent to the title expression with $Q=\pi\sigma(b^2-a^2)$. The oriented normal follows the positive rotation convention, with the sign of $Q$ retained.
