# Relative vorticity from a variable-depth transport streamfunction

↑ **Parent:** [Ocean transport streamfunction](ocean-transport-streamfunction.md)

The [ocean transport streamfunction](ocean-transport-streamfunction.md) convention $Hu=-\psi_y$, $Hv=\psi_x$ gives [relative vorticity](relative-vorticity.md) $\zeta=H^{-1}\nabla^2\psi-H^{-2}\nabla H\cdot\nabla\psi$. This identity is exact for variable positive depth and does not assume that depth variations are small.
