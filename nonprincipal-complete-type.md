# Nonprincipal complete type

↑ **Parent:** [Complete type](complete-type.md)

A [nonprincipal complete type](nonprincipal-complete-type.md) has no [first-order consistent](first-order-consistency.md) [first-order formula](first-order-formula.md) implying all its members. In a complete [countable](countable-set.md) [first-order theory](first-order-theory.md) such a [partial type](partial-type.md) can be omitted in a [countable](countable-set.md) [first-order model](model-of-a-first-order-theory.md) by the [omitting types theorem](omitting-types-theorem.md). A [partial type](partial-type.md) realized by a parameter-free definable [tuple](finite-tuple.md) is principal.
