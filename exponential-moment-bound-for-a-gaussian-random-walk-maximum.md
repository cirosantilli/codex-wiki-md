# Exponential moment bound for a Gaussian random-walk maximum

↑ **Parent:** [Exponential martingale of a random walk](exponential-martingale-of-a-random-walk.md)

For [independent and identically distributed random variables](independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md) with law $N(-1,1)$, $e^{2S_n}$ is a nonnegative [martingale](martingale-split.md), since the increment's [moment-generating function](moment-generating-function.md) equals one at parameter two. The [Doob maximal inequality](doob-maximal-inequality-for-a-nonnegative-submartingale.md) gives $\mathbb P(Z>t)\leq e^{-2t}$ for the all-time maximum $Z\geq0$. Integrating the tail gives the displayed bound. For $b\leq0$, $e^{bZ}\leq1$. The [strong law of large numbers](strong-law-of-large-numbers.md) guarantees finiteness and attainment of the maximum because $S_n\to-\infty$.
