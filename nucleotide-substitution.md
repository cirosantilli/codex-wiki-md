# Nucleotide substitution

↑ **Parent:** [Point mutation](point-mutation.md)

A [nucleotide substitution](nucleotide-substitution.md) replaces a [nucleotide](nucleotide.md) at a specified position without changing sequence length. In a fixed [reading frame](reading-frame.md), a single substitution can preserve an [amino acid](amino-acid.md) assignment, change it or introduce a [stop codon](stop-codon.md); the [mutation](mutation.md) ledger exhibits all three possibilities.
