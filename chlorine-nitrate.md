# Chlorine nitrate

↑ **Parent:** [Atmospheric chemistry](atmospheric-chemistry.md)

The reservoir species $\mathrm{ClONO_2}$, formed from ClO and NO2 with third-body stabilization. Heterogeneous reaction with [hydrogen chloride](hydrogen-chloride.md) can produce molecular [chlorine](chlorine.md) and [nitric acid](nitric-acid.md), activating [chlorine](chlorine.md) while removing reactive [nitrogen](nitrogen.md) from the gas phase. It belongs to the wider reactive [nitrogen](nitrogen.md) family, not to the narrow NO-plus-NO2 definition of NOx.
