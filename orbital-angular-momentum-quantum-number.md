# Orbital angular momentum quantum number

↑ **Parent:** [Angular momentum quantum number](angular-momentum-quantum-number.md)

The nonnegative integer $l$ for which a [spherical harmonic](spherical-harmonic.md) has squared [orbital angular momentum](orbital-angular-momentum.md) $\hbar^2l(l+1)$. The angular part of a regular scalar [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) has $l=0,1,2,\ldots$ and [magnetic quantum number](magnetic-quantum-number.md) $m=-l,\ldots,l$.
