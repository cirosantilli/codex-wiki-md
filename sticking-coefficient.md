# Sticking coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Adsorption](adsorption.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sticking_coefficient)

The probability that an impinging species is retained at a surface under specified conditions. Incident flux alone does not fix net surface accumulation when sticking is incomplete or [desorption](desorption.md) occurs.
