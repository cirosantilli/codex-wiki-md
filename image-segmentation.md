# Image segmentation

↑ **Parent:** [Image processing](image-processing.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Image_segmentation)

[Image segmentation](image-segmentation.md) divides an [image signal](image-signal.md) domain into regions according to a chosen criterion, such as approximately constant or smoothly varying intensity. The [Mumford–Shah segmentation model](mumford-shah-functional.md) balances fidelity, within-region smoothness and [image edge](image-edge.md) length. An intensity segmentation need not coincide with semantic object identities.
