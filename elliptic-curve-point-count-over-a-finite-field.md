# Elliptic-curve point count over a finite field

↑ **Parent:** [Frobenius isogeny of an elliptic curve](frobenius-isogeny-of-an-elliptic-curve.md)

If $\alpha,\beta$ are the roots of $T^2-aT+q$, where $a=q+1-\#E(\mathbb F_q)$, then

$$
\#E(\mathbb F_{q^r})=q^r+1-\alpha^r-\beta^r.
$$
