# Mostly male theory

↑ **Parent:** [Evolutionary developmental biology](evolutionary-developmental-biology.md)

A specific historical hypothesis in which ectopic ovules arose on a predominantly male precursor during flower evolution. It combines gene-history and morphological arguments but remains distinct from a demonstrated ancestral developmental sequence.[Historical proposal and evidence considered](the-mostly-male-theory-of-flower-evolutionary-origins-from-genes-to-fossils-by-michael-w-frohlich-and-david-s-parker.md)
