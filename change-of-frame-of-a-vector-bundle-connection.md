# Change of frame of a vector-bundle connection

↑ **Parent:** [Connection (vector bundle)](connection-vector-bundle.md)

With local frame written as a row $e$ and $D(eu)=e(du+Au)$, a change $e'=eg$ gives $A'=g^{-1}Ag+g^{-1}dg$. The [vector-bundle curvature](curvature-form.md) transforms by conjugation. This convention makes the inhomogeneous derivative term and every subsequent [vector-bundle curvature](curvature-form.md) sign explicit.
