# Small-height Dirichlet scattering

↑ **Parent:** [Acoustics](acoustics.md)

[Small-height Dirichlet scattering](small-height-dirichlet-scattering.md) expands the acoustic boundary condition about a mean plane for a fixed regular surface profile. The zeroth-order field includes the flat reflection; higher orders solve outgoing [Helmholtz equation](helmholtz-equation.md) problems with induced boundary data. Small $|kh|$ controls the incident-field Taylor expansion, but rapidly varying roughness can require additional control of normal derivatives and spectral moments.
