# Discovery of asymptotic freedom and the 2004 Nobel Prize

↑ **Parent:** [Asymptotic freedom](asymptotic-freedom.md)

[David Gross](david-j-gross.md) and [Frank Wilczek](frank-wilczek.md), and independently [H. David Politzer](hugh-david-politzer.md), published their one-[loop order](loop-order.md) [Non-Abelian gauge theory](yang-mills-theory.md) results in adjacent 1973 Physical Review Letters papers. Both established a high-[energy](energy-split.md) route to [weak coupling](weak-coupling.md); extrapolating into the low-[energy](energy-split.md) region was a separate dynamical problem.[1973, p. 1345, acknowledgment of the independent calculation](ultraviolet-behavior-of-non-abelian-gauge-theories-by-david-j-gross-and-frank-wilczek.md)[1973, p. 1348, low-energy hypotheses versus the asymptotic result](reliable-perturbative-results-for-strong-interactions-by-hugh-david-politzer.md)

The three shared the 2004 Nobel Prize in Physics for discovering [asymptotic freedom](asymptotic-freedom.md) in the theory of the [strong interaction](strong-interaction.md). [David Gross](david-j-gross.md)'s Nobel lecture describes the unexpected sign and their comparison of independent calculations.[2005 reprint, p. 837, award footnote; p. 843, discovery narrative](the-discovery-of-asymptotic-freedom-and-the-emergence-of-qcd-by-david-j-gross.md) In his lecture The Dilemma of Attribution, [H. David Politzer](hugh-david-politzer.md) emphasizes that the prize marks a wider community's work and that a tidy prize-centered history hides how discovery actually proceeds.[Lecture of 8 December 2004, pp. 1–2 and 6–9](the-dilemma-of-attribution-by-hugh-david-politzer.md)
