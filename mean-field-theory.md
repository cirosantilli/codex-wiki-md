# Mean-field theory

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical physics](statistical-physics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mean-field_theory)

[Mean-field theory](mean-field-theory.md) replaces fluctuating interactions with a self-consistent average field. For example, an [Ising spin](ising-spin-variable.md) feels a field determined by the average neighboring [spin magnetization](spin-magnetization.md); a [self-consistency equation](self-consistency-equation.md) approximates the interacting [Ising model](ising-model.md). Neglecting correlated fluctuations can fail near a continuous [phase transition](phase-transition.md). The [mean-field approximation](mean-field-approximation.md) in [Landau theory](landau-theory.md) is a particular use of this general framework.
