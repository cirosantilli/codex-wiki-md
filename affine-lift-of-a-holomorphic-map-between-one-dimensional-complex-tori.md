# Affine lift of a holomorphic map between one-dimensional complex tori

↑ **Parent:** [Complex torus](complex-torus.md)

Let $\pi_\Lambda:\mathbb C\to\mathbb C/\Lambda$ and $\pi_{\Lambda'}:\mathbb C\to\mathbb C/\Lambda'$ be the [universal covering maps](universal-cover.md) of two one-dimensional [complex tori](complex-torus.md). Every [holomorphic map](holomorphic-map.md) $f:\mathbb C/\Lambda\to\mathbb C/\Lambda'$ has a [holomorphic lift between one-dimensional complex tori](affine-lift-of-a-holomorphic-map-between-one-dimensional-complex-tori.md) $F:\mathbb C\to\mathbb C$ satisfying $\pi_{\Lambda'}\circ F=f\circ\pi_\Lambda$, and every such lift is an [affine function](affine-function.md)

$$
F(z)=az+b
$$

with $a\Lambda\subseteq\Lambda'$. Indeed, for $\lambda\in\Lambda$, the continuous function $F(z+\lambda)-F(z)$ takes values in the discrete set $\Lambda'$ and is therefore constant. Hence $F'$ is $\Lambda$-periodic. It is bounded on a compact [fundamental parallelogram](fundamental-parallelogram-of-a-period-lattice.md) and therefore on the [complex plane](complex-plane.md), so the [Liouville theorem](liouville-theorem.md) makes $F'$ constant. If $f$ preserves the identity and the lift is chosen with $F(0)=0$, then $b=0$ and the lift is a [linear map](linear-map.md).
