# Single-observation location minimax bound

↑ **Parent:** [Location family](location-family.md)

For a known mean-zero noise law with finite [variance](variance-split.md) $\sigma^2$, observing only $Y=a+\epsilon$ with unrestricted $a\in\mathbb R$ has [minimax risk](minimax-risk.md) $\sigma^2$ under [squared-error loss](squared-error-loss.md). The observation itself attains this risk. For the lower bound, use a flat [prior distribution](prior-probability.md) on $[-A,A]$ and condition an easier experiment on $|\epsilon|\leq M$. Away from the prior endpoints, the [Bayesian posterior](bayesian-posterior.md) noise has its original truncated law. Let $A\to\infty$ and then $M\to\infty$ in the resulting [Bayes risk](bayes-risk.md) lower bound. No [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md) or density for the noise is required.
