# Polytropic accretion in a mixed inverse-power potential

↑ **Parent:** [Barotropic spherical accretion equation](barotropic-spherical-accretion-equation.md)

For $p=K\rho^{1+1/m}$, the [barotropic enthalpy function](barotropic-enthalpy-function.md) is $mc_s^2$. In $\Phi=-\lambda/R^2-\mu/R$, a regular [sonic point](sonic-point.md) obeys $c_{s,s}^2=\lambda/R_s^2+\mu/(2R_s)$, and its Bernoulli integral yields the displayed quadratic. For $K,\lambda,\mu>0$ and the usual positive polytropic index, its unique positive root exists precisely when $m>1/2$. The [mass accretion rate](mass-accretion-rate.md) is $4\pi R_s^2\rho_\infty c_{s,s}(c_{s,s}^2/c_\infty^2)^m$. If indices with $0<1+1/m<1$ are admitted, $m<-1$ also permits a positive sonic root; negative compressibility indices do not.
