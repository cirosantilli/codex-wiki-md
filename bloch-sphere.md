# Bloch sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Bloch vector](bloch-vector.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloch_sphere)

The [Bloch sphere](bloch-sphere.md) is the unit sphere of [Bloch vectors](bloch-vector.md), corresponding to pure [qubit](qubit.md) states through $\rho=(I+\mathbf r\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma)/2$. A local unitary change of qubit basis induces a rotation of this sphere, with its common phase physically irrelevant. Mixed states occupy the interior of the unit ball.
