# String prefix

↑ **Parent:** [Prefix of a list](prefix-of-a-list.md)

An initial segment of a [string](string.md), including the empty [string](string.md) or the whole [string](string.md). Two [strings](string.md) share a prefix exactly when their initial characters agree up to its length. The [longest common prefix](longest-common-prefix.md) is the longest such shared initial segment.
