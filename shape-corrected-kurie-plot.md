# Shape-corrected Kurie plot

↑ **Parent:** [Kurie plot](kurie-plot.md)

If a beta spectrum has nonconstant shape factor $C(T)$, divide by it as well as by the Coulomb and phase-space factors before taking a square root. A leading unique first-forbidden shape contains $p_e^2+p_\nu^2$ in [natural units](natural-units.md), with Coulomb and nuclear corrections. Dividing only by a Coulomb [Fermi function](fermi-function.md) does not remove that shape. A straight uncorrected line must therefore be treated as an approximation, not an exact consequence of forbidden-transition [selection rules](selection-rule.md).
