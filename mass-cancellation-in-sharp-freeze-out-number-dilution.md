# Mass cancellation in sharp-freeze-out number dilution

↑ **Parent:** [Sharp-freeze-out relic dilution](sharp-freeze-out-relic-dilution.md)

For an imposed equilibrium cutoff $mc^2=Pk_BT_f$ and density $n_f=6[mc^2k_BT_f/(\hbar c)^2]^{3/2}e^{-P}$, dilution by $(T_0/T_f)^3$ cancels the explicit [mass](mass.md) in the final [number density](number-density.md). The rest-energy density still scales with particle [mass](mass.md). This result assumes constant [entropy degrees of freedom](entropy-degrees-of-freedom.md) and no post-cutoff [annihilation](annihilation.md); [entropy](entropy-split.md) conservation adds a factor $g_{*s,0}/g_{*s,f}$ when these assumptions are relaxed.
