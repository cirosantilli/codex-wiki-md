# Rossby wave in a log-pressure atmosphere

↑ **Parent:** [Baroclinic Rossby wave](baroclinic-rossby-wave.md)

For constant [buoyancy frequency](buoyancy-frequency.md) $N$, the [log-pressure quasi-geostrophic potential vorticity](log-pressure-quasi-geostrophic-potential-vorticity.md) equation has modes $\psi=e^{z/(2H)}e^{i(kx+mz-\omega t)}$ with

$$
\omega=-\frac{\beta k}{k^2+(f_0^2/N^2)[m^2+1/(4H^2)]}.
$$

The prefactor compensates the density decrease in the energy weight. For $\beta,k>0$, vertical propagation occurs for $-\beta k/[k^2+f_0^2/(4N^2H^2)]<\omega<0$. An outgoing [radiation condition](radiation-condition.md) selects the root with positive vertical [group velocity](group-velocity.md); bounded weighted amplitude alone does not select between two propagating roots.
