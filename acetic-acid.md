# Acetic acid

↑ **Parent:** [Carboxylic acid](carboxylic-acid.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acetic_acid)

[Acetic acid](acetic-acid.md) is a [weak acid](weak-acid.md) that partly transfers [protons](proton.md) to [water](water.md), supplying [hydronium](hydronium.md) and negative [ions](ion.md). It is therefore a [weak electrolyte](weak-electrolyte.md) in [water](water.md).
