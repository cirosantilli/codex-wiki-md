# Log-call option

↑ **Parent:** [European contingent claim](european-contingent-claim.md)

A [European option](european-contingent-claim.md) paying the [positive part](positive-part-of-a-real-valued-function.md) of the [logarithm](logarithm.md) of terminal [stock](stock.md) price divided by a positive strike $K$. In the dividend-free [Black-Scholes model](black-scholes-model.md), that [logarithm](logarithm.md) has a [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md) under the [risk-neutral measure](risk-neutral-measure.md). The [Gaussian positive-part expectation](gaussian-positive-part-expectation.md) gives $V=e^{-r\tau}[m\Phi(m/a)+a\phi(m/a)]$, where $m=\log(S/K)+(r-\sigma^2/2)\tau$ and $a=\sigma\sqrt\tau$. This differs from the untruncated logarithmic payoff and from the payoff $S_T\log S_T$.
