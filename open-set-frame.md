# Open-set frame

↑ **Parent:** [Complete Heyting algebra](complete-heyting-algebra.md)

The [open subsets](open-set.md) of a [topological space](topological-space.md) form a [frame](complete-heyting-algebra.md). Arbitrary joins are unions, finite meets are intersections, and an arbitrary meet is the interior of an intersection. A [continuous map](continuous-map.md) induces a [frame homomorphism](frame-homomorphism.md) in the opposite direction by inverse image.
