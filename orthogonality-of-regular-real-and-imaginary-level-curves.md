# Orthogonality of regular real and imaginary level curves

↑ **Parent:** [Cauchy-Riemann equations](cauchy-riemann-equations.md)

For a [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function-split.md) $f=u+iv$, the [Cauchy-Riemann equations](cauchy-riemann-equations.md) give the displayed [gradient](gradient.md) identities. Where $f'\ne0$, both [level sets](level-set.md) are regular curves; their nonzero [normal vectors](normal-vector.md) are perpendicular, so their tangents are perpendicular as well. The qualification is necessary. At a [critical point](critical-point.md) both [gradients](gradient.md) vanish and the zero [level sets](level-set.md) may have several tangent branches. For $f(z)=z^2$ at zero, $u=0$ consists of two diagonals while $v=0$ consists of the two coordinate axes; there is no general orthogonality assertion at that point.
