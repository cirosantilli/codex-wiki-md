# Hilbert subset

↑ **Parent:** [Hilbertian field](hilbertian-field.md)

For a finite family of irreducible separable [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) $f_i(T,X)$ and a nonzero exclusion [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) $h(T)$, this is the set of parameters $a\in K$ with $h(a)\ne0$ for which every specialization remains irreducible of its original degree. A [Hilbertian field](hilbertian-field.md) has infinitely many points in each such set. Denominators, leading coefficients and discriminants can be included in $h$.
