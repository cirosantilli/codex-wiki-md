# Inverse photoemission spectroscopy

↑ **Parent:** [Spectroscopy](spectroscopy.md)

[Inverse photoemission spectroscopy](inverse-photoemission-spectroscopy.md) injects an [electron](electron.md) of known [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) into a sample and detects the [photon](photon.md) emitted as it enters an unoccupied electronic state. [Conservation of energy](conservation-of-energy.md), together with incident-angle information and an appropriate surface model, probes the unoccupied [electronic band structure](electronic-band-structure.md). Ordinary [angle-resolved photoemission spectroscopy](angle-resolved-photoemission-spectroscopy.md) instead removes [electrons](electron.md) from occupied states.
