# Design sensitivity function

↑ **Parent:** [Information matrix of an experimental design](information-matrix-of-an-experimental-design.md)

The sensitivity function is the normalized [variance of a fitted regression mean](variance-of-a-fitted-regression-mean.md). Its average under the design is the number $p$ of model coefficients, since $\int d\,d\xi=\operatorname{tr}(M^{-1}M)=p$. The directional derivative of $\log\det M$ towards a point mass at $x$ is $d(x,\xi)-p$.
