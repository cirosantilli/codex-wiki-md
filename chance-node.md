# Chance node

↑ **Parent:** [Game tree](game-tree.md)

A [game tree](game-tree.md) node whose successor is chosen according to a specified [probability distribution](probability-distribution-split.md). Its [expected utility](expected-utility.md) is a weighted sum of successor values, rather than the maximum or minimum used at a player's decision node.
