# sl2R Lie algebra

↑ **Parent:** [sl2 Lie algebra](sl2-lie-algebra.md)

The real traceless two-by-two matrices form a three-dimensional [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md). A basis adapted to [conformal mechanics](conformal-mechanics.md) obeys $\{D,E\}=-E$, $\{D,K\}=K$, $\{E,K\}=2D$.
