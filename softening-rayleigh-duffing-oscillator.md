# Softening Rayleigh-Duffing oscillator

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamical systems](dynamical-systems-split.md)

The softening restoring force and cubic velocity damping give the energy $E=(z')^2/2+z^2/2-z^4/4$ with $\dot E=-A(z')^2-(z')^4$. The origin and the two saddle points $z=\pm1$ persist for all parameters. The origin undergoes a [supercritical Hopf bifurcation](supercritical-hopf-bifurcation.md) at $A=0$. A [heteroclinic cycle](heteroclinic-cycle.md) and a subsequent [saddle-node bifurcation of periodic orbits](saddle-node-bifurcation-of-periodic-orbits.md) are distinct global changes in its [softening Rayleigh-Duffing phase portrait](softening-rayleigh-duffing-phase-portrait.md).
