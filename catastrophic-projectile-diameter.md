# Catastrophic projectile diameter

↑ **Parent:** [Catastrophic disruption threshold](catastrophic-disruption-threshold.md)

For equal-density spherical [planetesimals](planetesimal.md), an impactor much smaller than target diameter $D$ causes a [catastrophic planetesimal collision](catastrophic-planetesimal-collision.md) when its [specific impact energy](specific-impact-energy.md) reaches $Q_D^*$. Neglecting [gravitational focusing](gravitational-focusing.md),

$$
D_{\rm cc}=D\left(\frac{2Q_D^*}{v_{\rm rel}^2}\right)^{1/3}.
$$

When encounter speed is much smaller than the target's [escape velocity](escape-velocity.md), impact speed is approximately $v_{\rm esc}=D\sqrt{2\pi G\rho/3}$. For $Q_D^*=Q_bD^b$, the focusing-dominated threshold is $D_{\rm cc}=(3Q_b/\pi G\rho)^{1/3}D^{(1+b)/3}$. This assumes the disruption threshold uses impact energy per target mass and neglects energy-coupling efficiency variations.
