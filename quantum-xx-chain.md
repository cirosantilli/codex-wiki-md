# Quantum XX chain

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum magnetism](quantum-magnetism.md)

The spin-one-half [quantum XX chain](quantum-xx-chain.md) has equal nearest-neighbour transverse exchange. Its continuous [spin rotation](spin-rotation.md) [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md) conserves total longitudinal [magnetization](magnetization.md). The [Jordan–Wigner transformation](jordan-wigner-transformation.md) maps it to number-conserving free [fermions](fermion.md). This quantum chain is distinct from the classical [XY model](xy-model.md).
