# Quintuple bond

↑ **Parent:** [Covalent bond](covalent-bond-split.md)

A formal [bond order](bond-order.md) of five. The common d-metal model has one sigma, two pi and two delta bonding orbitals occupied, $\sigma^2\pi^4\delta^4$, with the corresponding antibonding orbitals empty.
