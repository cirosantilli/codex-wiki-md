# Normal likelihood

↑ **Parent:** [Normal distribution](normal-distribution.md)

The [likelihood function](likelihood-function.md) of independent observations from the same [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md). For fixed positive [variance](variance-split.md) $\sigma^2$, its unique maximizing mean is the [sample mean](sample-mean.md): $\sum_i(x_i-\mu)^2=\sum_i(x_i-\overline x)^2+n(\mu-\overline x)^2$. This algebraic identity proves global optimality without relying only on a stationary derivative.
