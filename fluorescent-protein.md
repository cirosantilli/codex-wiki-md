# Fluorescent protein

↑ **Parent:** [Protein](protein-split.md)

A genetically encodable [protein](protein-split.md) that emits [fluorescence](fluorescence-split.md) after appropriate excitation. Fusion constructs can label cellular structures, but expression and illumination must be controlled for effects on the process being observed.
