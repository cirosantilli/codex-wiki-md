# Black-hole spin

↑ **Parent:** [Astrophysical black hole](astrophysical-black-hole.md)

Black-hole spin is the angular momentum of a black hole, commonly expressed by the dimensionless Kerr parameter $a_*=cJ/(GM^2)$. It changes the [innermost stable circular orbit](innermost-stable-circular-orbit.md), accretion efficiency, and relativistic frame dragging.
