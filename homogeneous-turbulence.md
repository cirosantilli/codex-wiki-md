# Homogeneous turbulence

↑ **Parent:** [Turbulence](turbulence-split.md)

[Homogeneous turbulence](homogeneous-turbulence.md) has velocity statistics unchanged by translating all observation points. Mean spatial divergences vanish when the [moments](moment.md) exist and averaging commutes with derivatives. Homogeneity is distinct from isotropy: it does not require equal statistics in different directions.
