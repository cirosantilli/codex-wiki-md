# Symplectic leaf

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson manifold](poisson-manifold.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symplectic_leaf)

A symplectic leaf is a maximal connected immersed submanifold on which the Poisson bivector has constant full rank and therefore inverts to a [symplectic form](symplectic-form.md).
