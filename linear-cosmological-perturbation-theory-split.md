# Linear cosmological perturbation theory

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmology](cosmology-split.md)

This is the first-order sector of [cosmological perturbation theory](cosmological-perturbation-theory.md).

Linear cosmological perturbation theory expands the metric and matter fields to first order about a homogeneous and isotropic [Friedmann-Lemaître-Robertson-Walker metric](friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md).
