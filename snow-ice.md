# Snow ice

↑ **Parent:** [Sea ice](sea-ice.md)

[Snow ice](snow-ice.md) forms when flooded [snow](snow.md) or a seawater-snow mixture freezes. Snow loading of a thin floe can depress the ice surface below water level and initiate flooding; wave overwash is another route.
