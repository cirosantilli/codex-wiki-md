# Ambondro mahabo

↑ **Parent:** [Mammaliaform](mammaliaform.md)

A Middle [Jurassic](jurassic.md) Malagasy [mammaliaform](mammaliaform.md) known from lower teeth in a jaw fragment. Its tribosphenic-like construction bears on competing hypotheses for the origin of [tribospheny](tribospheny.md). [Nature 401, pp. 57–60; Ambondro lower jaw, approximately 167 ± 2 million years old](a-middle-jurassic-mammal-from-madagascar-by-john-j-flynn-and-others.md)
