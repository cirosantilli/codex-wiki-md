# Photometer

↑ **Parent:** [Photometry (optics)](photometry-optics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photometer)

A [photometer](photometer.md) measures a visually weighted radiation quantity. A filtered [photodiode](photodiode.md) approximates the [photopic luminous efficiency function](photopic-luminous-efficiency-function.md) with its [spectral responsivity](spectral-responsivity.md); calibration establishes how its [electric current](electric-current.md) maps to [illuminance](illuminance.md) or collected [luminous flux](luminous-flux.md). NIST's detector-based realization traces the [spectral responsivity](spectral-responsivity.md) to a [cryogenic radiometer](cryogenic-radiometer.md) and the aperture [area](surface-area.md) to length measurements.
