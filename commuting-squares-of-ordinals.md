# Commuting squares of ordinals

↑ **Parent:** [Ordinal multiplication](ordinal-multiplication.md)

Two [ordinals](ordinal.md) commute under [ordinal multiplication](ordinal-multiplication.md) if and only if their squares commute. One implication follows from [associativity](associative-property.md). For the converse, squaring is strictly increasing: $A<B$ implies $A^2\leq BA<B^2$. If $\alpha\beta<\beta\alpha$, monotonicity gives

$$
\alpha^2\beta^2\leq(\alpha\beta)^2<(\beta\alpha)^2\leq\beta^2\alpha^2,
$$

a contradiction to commuting squares. Swap the factors to exclude the reverse inequality; zero factors cause no exception.
