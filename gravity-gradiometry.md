# Gravity gradiometry

↑ **Parent:** [Tidal tensor](tidal-tensor.md)

[Gravity gradiometry](gravity-gradiometry.md) measures spatial differences of [gravitational acceleration](gravitational-acceleration.md). Two simultaneous measurements along a [unit vector](unit-vector.md) $\mathbf e$, separated by $L\mathbf e$, give

$$
\frac{\mathbf e\cdot[\mathbf g(\mathbf R+L\mathbf e)-\mathbf g(\mathbf R)]}{L}\simeq\mathbf e^{\mathsf T}T\mathbf e.
$$

This measures one directional component of the [tidal tensor](tidal-tensor.md), not its whole [matrix](matrix.md). Any identical platform [acceleration](acceleration.md) cancels in the difference. Unequal instrument responses, changing geometry and [rotating reference frames](rotating-reference-frame.md) can spoil that cancellation. For a large baseline, the quotient is an average of the directional [tidal tensor](tidal-tensor.md) along the baseline, rather than its exact value at one point.
