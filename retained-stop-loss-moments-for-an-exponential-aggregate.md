# Retained stop loss moments for an exponential aggregate

↑ **Parent:** [Aggregate stop loss reinsurance](aggregate-stop-loss-reinsurance.md)

If $S$ has [exponential distribution](exponential-distribution.md) with [expected value](expected-value.md) $\mu$, the [tail integral formula for moments](tail-integral-formula-for-moments.md) gives $\mathbb E\min(S,M)=\mu(1-e^{-m})$ and $\operatorname{Var}(\min(S,M))=\mu^2(1-2me^{-m}-e^{-2m})$, where $m=M/\mu$. At matching retained [expected value](expected-value.md), the excess [variance](variance-split.md) under [quota share reinsurance](quota-share-reinsurance.md) is $2\mu^2e^{-m}(m-1+e^{-m})\geq0$.
