# Functoriality of abelian image factorization

↑ **Parent:** [Image factorization in an abelian category](image-factorization-in-an-abelian-category.md)

A commuting square $vf=f'u$ induces a unique map between their images by $I(u,v)p=p'u$ and $i'I(u,v)=vi$. The [cokernel in a category](cokernel-in-a-category.md) property supplies existence, and cancellation of the epimorphic $p$ supplies uniqueness. These equations prove identity and composition laws, giving a [functor](functor.md) from the [arrow category](arrow-category.md) to the [abelian category](abelian-category.md).
