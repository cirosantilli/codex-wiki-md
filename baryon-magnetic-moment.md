# Baryon magnetic moment

↑ **Parent:** [Baryon](baryon.md)

In the additive ground-state [quark model](quark-model.md), a [baryon](baryon.md) [magnetic moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md) is the expectation of $\sum_i Q_i e\boldsymbol\sigma_i/(2m_i)$ in its spin-and-flavor [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md), with constituent [quark masses](quark-mass.md) $m_i$. For a spin-up [proton](proton.md) and [neutron](neutron-split.md) this gives $\mu_p=(4\mu_u-\mu_d)/3$ and $\mu_n=(4\mu_d-\mu_u)/3$. Approximately equal up and down constituent [masses](mass.md) imply $\mu_u\simeq-2\mu_d$ and $\mu_p/\mu_n\simeq-3/2$. This is evidence for the [valence quark](valence-quark.md) model, not proof that sea [quark](quark.md)-[antiquark](antiquark.md) pairs are absent.
