# Near-pencil

↑ **Parent:** [Finite linear space](finite-linear-space.md)

A near-pencil on $v$ points has one line containing $v-1$ points and $v-1$ two-point lines joining the remaining point to each point of the large line.

This is a particular [finite linear space](finite-linear-space.md) configuration, rather than a synonym for every pencil of lines.
