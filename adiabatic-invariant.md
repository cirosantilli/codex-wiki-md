# Adiabatic invariant

↑ **Parent:** [Hamiltonian mechanics](hamiltonian-mechanics.md)

A quantity conserved to leading order when a parameter varies slowly compared with the natural motion period. The [action variable](action-variable.md) of a regular periodic Hamiltonian orbit is a standard example, as explained by [adiabatic invariance of the action](adiabatic-invariance-of-the-action.md).
