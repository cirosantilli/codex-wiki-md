# Jump part of a BV derivative

↑ **Parent:** [Decomposition of a BV derivative](decomposition-of-a-bv-derivative.md)

This [derivative](derivative.md) component is carried by the [jump set of a BV function](jump-set-of-a-bounded-variation-function.md). Swapping the two [BV traces on a hypersurface](bv-trace-on-a-hypersurface.md) and reversing the normal leaves it unchanged. In one dimension it consists of the point [measure atoms](atom-measure-theory.md), each weighted by the difference between the right and left limits.
