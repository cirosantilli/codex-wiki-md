# Cryptic species

↑ **Parent:** [Species delimitation](species-delimitation.md)

Distinct evolutionary lineages or reproductively isolated [species](species.md) that are difficult to distinguish using the morphological characters traditionally examined. Similar appearance can therefore conceal [species](species.md) boundaries. Genetic evidence alone also requires interpretation, because population structure need not imply complete [reproductive isolation](reproductive-isolation.md).
