# Markov inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Probability inequality](probability-inequality-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Markov_inequality)

For a nonnegative random variable $Y$ and $a>0$,

$$
\Pr(Y\geq a)\leq\frac{\mathbb EY}{a}.
$$
