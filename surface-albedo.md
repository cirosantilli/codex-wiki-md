# Surface albedo

↑ **Parent:** [Planetary surface energy balance](planetary-surface-energy-balance.md)

Surface albedo is the fraction of incident solar [energy flux](energy-flux.md) reflected by a surface, averaged over the specified spectrum and illumination geometry. The locally absorbed fraction is $1-\alpha$. It is distinct from [geometric albedo](geometric-albedo.md), which measures brightness in a particular viewing configuration.
