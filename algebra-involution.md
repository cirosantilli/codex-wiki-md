# Algebra involution

↑ **Parent:** [Associative algebra](associative-algebra-split.md)

An algebra involution on a complex [associative algebra](associative-algebra-split.md) is a [conjugate-linear map](antilinear-map.md) satisfying $(xy)^*=y^*x^*$ and $(x^*)^*=x$. It fixes the identity when one exists. A star algebra is an algebra equipped with such a map. These algebraic identities do not themselves impose [norm](norm.md) [continuity](continuous-function.md) or the [C-star identity](c-star-identity.md).
