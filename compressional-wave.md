# Compressional wave

↑ **Parent:** [Longitudinal wave](longitudinal-wave.md)

A compressional wave is a [longitudinal wave](longitudinal-wave.md) whose propagation produces changes of volume or density. In an isotropic elastic solid its [displacement](displacement.md) is parallel to the [wavevector](wavevector.md); substituting that polarization into the dynamic [Navier-Cauchy equation](navier-cauchy-equation.md) gives the displayed speed. In a fluid without static shear stiffness the analogous speed is $\sqrt{K/\rho}$, with the appropriate compressional [bulk modulus](bulk-modulus.md). Reflection at a rigid elastic boundary can convert part of its energy into a [shear wave](shear-wave.md).
