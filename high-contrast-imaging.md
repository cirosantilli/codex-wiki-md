# High-contrast imaging

↑ **Parent:** [Optical instrument](optical-instrument.md)

High-contrast imaging seeks faint objects near a bright source. Residual [point spread functions](point-spread-function.md) and [speckle patterns](speckle-pattern.md) can dominate [photon shot noise](photon-shot-noise.md); [angular differential imaging](angular-differential-imaging.md) and [simultaneous spectral differential imaging](simultaneous-spectral-differential-imaging.md) use source motion or spectral diversity to distinguish them.
