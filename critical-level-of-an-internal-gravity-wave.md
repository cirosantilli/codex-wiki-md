# Critical level of an internal gravity wave

↑ **Parent:** [Intrinsic frequency](intrinsic-frequency.md)

A critical level is a height at which the [intrinsic frequency](intrinsic-frequency.md) of an [internal gravity wave](internal-wave.md) vanishes. For finite positive [buoyancy frequency](buoyancy-frequency.md), its local vertical [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) diverges as $\widehat\omega\to0$. The inviscid [Taylor–Goldstein equation](taylor-goldstein-equation.md) is singular there; viscosity, diffusion or nonlinear wave breaking can regularize the resulting small scales.
