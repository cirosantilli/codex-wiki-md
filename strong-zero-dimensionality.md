# Strong zero-dimensionality

↑ **Parent:** [Zero-dimensional space](zero-dimensional-space.md)

In a normal Hausdorff space, strong zero-dimensionality means that any disjoint closed sets can be separated by a [clopen set](clopen-set.md), equivalently by disjoint clopen neighborhoods. For general [Tychonoff spaces](completely-regular-hausdorff-space.md), the usual definition asks this for completely separated sets. In the normal setting [Urysohn's lemma](urysohn-s-lemma.md) makes the formulations agree. The condition is equivalent to zero-dimensionality of the [Stone-Čech compactification](stone-cech-compactification.md).
