# Normalization of the Poincare disc metric

↑ **Parent:** [Poincaré disk model](poincare-disk-model.md)

Transferring the upper half-plane [hyperbolic metric](hyperbolic-metric.md) $|dw|^2/(\operatorname{Im}w)^2$ through the [Cayley transform](cayley-transform-complex-analysis.md) gives $ds^2=4|dz|^2/(1-|z|^2)^2$ on the unit disc. Radial integration gives distance $2\operatorname{artanh}r$ from zero to $r$. The alternative convention $d\widetilde s^2=|dz|^2/(1-|z|^2)^2$ divides the metric tensor by four and every length by two, giving $\operatorname{artanh}r$. Both have the same [isometries](isometry.md) and unparametrized [geodesics](geodesic.md), but their [Gaussian curvatures](gaussian-curvature.md) are respectively minus one and minus four. In general scaling a [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric-split.md) by $c>0$ scales lengths by $\sqrt c$ and [curvature](curvature.md) by $1/c$.
