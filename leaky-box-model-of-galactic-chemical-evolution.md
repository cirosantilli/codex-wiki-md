# Leaky-box model of galactic chemical evolution

↑ **Parent:** [Closed-box model of galactic chemical evolution](closed-box-model-of-galactic-chemical-evolution.md)

The leaky-box model adds a [galactic outflow](galactic-outflow.md) to the closed box. If the outflow has the ambient gas metallicity and constant [mass-loading factor](mass-loading-factor.md) $\lambda$, its effective yield is reduced to $y/(1+\lambda)$ when returned fractions are absorbed into the definitions.
