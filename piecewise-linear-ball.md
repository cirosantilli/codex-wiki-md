# Piecewise linear ball

↑ **Parent:** [Topological manifold](topological-manifold.md)

A piecewise linear ball is a [simplicial complex](simplicial-complex.md) admitting a [homeomorphism](homeomorphism.md) to an $n$-[simplex](simplex.md) that is linear on the [simplexes](simplex.md) of suitable finite [simplicial subdivisions](simplicial-subdivision.md). Its [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md) is a piecewise linear [sphere](sphere.md).
