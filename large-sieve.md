# Large sieve

↑ **Parent:** [Sieve theory](sieve-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Large_sieve)

The large sieve bounds how much an [exponential sum](exponential-sum.md) can concentrate at separated points of the [circle group](circle-group.md). Its [variance form of the large sieve](variance-form-of-the-large-sieve.md) also bounds simultaneous concentration in [residue classes](residue-class.md) modulo many [primes](prime-number.md).
