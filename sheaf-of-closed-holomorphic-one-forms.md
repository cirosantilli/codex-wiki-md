# Sheaf of closed holomorphic one-forms

↑ **Parent:** [Holomorphic de Rham complex](holomorphic-de-rham-complex.md)

The sheaf is $\mathcal Z_M^1=\ker(\partial:\Omega_M^1\to\Omega_M^2)$. The [holomorphic Poincaré lemma](holomorphic-poincare-lemma.md) gives $0\to\underline{\mathbb C}\to\mathcal O_M\xrightarrow{\partial}\mathcal Z_M^1\to0$. On a compact complex surface its [long exact sequence in sheaf cohomology](long-exact-sequence-in-sheaf-cohomology.md) yields $0\to H^0(M,\Omega_M^1)\to H^1(M,\mathbb C)\to H^1(M,\mathcal O_M)$.
