# Complex velocity

↑ **Parent:** [Complex potential of a two-dimensional potential flow](complex-potential-of-a-two-dimensional-potential-flow.md)

For a [complex potential of a two-dimensional potential flow](complex-potential-of-a-two-dimensional-potential-flow.md) $W=\phi+i\psi$ in the coordinate $z=x+iy$, the [Cauchy-Riemann equations](cauchy-riemann-equations.md) give $W'=\phi_x-i\phi_y=u-iv$. A material point with physical [velocity](velocity.md) $(u,v)$ therefore satisfies $\dot z=\overline{W'(z)}$. Complex conjugation matters when deriving a [pathline](pathline.md) from a [complex potential of a two-dimensional potential flow](complex-potential-of-a-two-dimensional-potential-flow.md).
