# Conditional characteristic-function criterion for Brownian increments

↑ **Parent:** [Exponential test-function characterization of Brownian motion](exponential-test-function-characterization-of-brownian-motion.md)

For an adapted continuous process starting at zero, the displayed conditional [characteristic function](characteristic-function.md) identifies every increment as $N(0,t-s)$ and makes it independent of the preceding sigma-field. Multiply by the indicator of an event in that sigma-field, then use the [uniqueness theorem for characteristic functions](uniqueness-theorem-for-characteristic-functions.md) for finite measures. Thus the process is a [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) in the given filtration.
