<h1 id="exciton-absorption-experiment-in-copper-i-oxide">Exciton absorption experiment in copper(I) oxide</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Exciton optical transition](exciton-optical-transition.md)

In [Giant Rydberg Excitons in Cuprous Oxide by Tomasz Kazimierczuk and others](giant-rydberg-excitons-in-cuprous-oxide-by-tomasz-kazimierczuk-and-others.md), a polished $34\,\mathrm{\mu m}$ [copper(I) oxide](copper-i-oxide.md) crystal was mounted without strain at $1.2\,\mathrm K$. A tunable single-frequency dye [laser](laser-split.md) scanned [photon](photon.md) [energy](energy-split.md); balanced detection compared transmitted light with a reference beam. Transmission dips resolved [spectral lines](spectral-line.md) assigned to bound [excitons](exciton.md) through [principal quantum number](principal-quantum-number.md) $n=25$. Their convergence towards an unbound threshold supports a hydrogen-like internal series, with small systematic deviations.[pp. 3–5, figures 1–2; supplement, Experimental setup, pp. 3–5 and figure S2](giant-rydberg-excitons-in-cuprous-oxide-by-tomasz-kazimierczuk-and-others.md)

Controls mattered: restricting broadband illumination improved resolution of high-$n$ [excitons](exciton.md); lowering [laser](laser-split.md) power or expanding its spot reduced interaction-induced suppression. Balanced detection suppressed source fluctuations. The measurement probes optical resonances, not individual [electron](electron.md)-[electron hole](electron-hole.md) trajectories or directly imaged radii. [Electric-dipole selection rules](electric-dipole-selection-rule.md) favor P-envelope states in this material, unlike the hypothetical permitted lowest-state transition above. Finite resolution and crystal inhomogeneity affect [spectral lines](spectral-line.md); the inferred binding series requires a continuum threshold and a model.[pp. 3–5; supplement pp. 2–4 and 6–7, figure S3: selection rules, illumination tests, line corrections and deviations](giant-rydberg-excitons-in-cuprous-oxide-by-tomasz-kazimierczuk-and-others.md)
