# Buoyancy frequency of an ideal-gas stratification

↑ **Parent:** [Buoyancy frequency](buoyancy-frequency.md)

For uniform composition, upward coordinate $z$ and downward acceleration $g>0$, a pressure-equilibrated adiabatic parcel in a hydrostatic [ideal gas](ideal-gas-split.md) has $\ddot\zeta=-N^2\zeta$ with $N^2=g[(d\ln p/dz)/\gamma-d\ln\rho/dz]$. A positive value gives restoring [buoyancy](buoyancy.md); for a calorically [perfect gas](ideal-gas-split.md) it is also $(g/c_p)ds/dz$. Heat or composition exchange requires a modified stability test.
