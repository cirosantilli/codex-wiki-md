# Photon Boltzmann hierarchy

↑ **Parent:** [Free-streaming photon Boltzmann equation](free-streaming-photon-boltzmann-equation.md)

Expanding the direction-dependent photon temperature in [Legendre polynomials](legendre-polynomial.md) turns its transport equation into an infinite hierarchy. Free streaming couples each multipole $\Theta_\ell$ only to its neighbours $\Theta_{\ell-1}$ and $\Theta_{\ell+1}$.
