# Massive-fermion chiral weight at fixed helicity

↑ **Parent:** [Chirality (physics)](chirality-physics.md)

For a positive-energy [Dirac spinor](dirac-spinor.md) normalized to unit probability and with positive [helicity](helicity.md), the left-chiral component has squared weight $(1-\beta)/2$, where $\beta=|\mathbf p|/E$ in [natural units](natural-units.md). Its right-chiral weight is $(1+\beta)/2$; for negative helicity the two are exchanged. In an ultrarelativistic positive-helicity particle, the left-chiral amplitude is suppressed by a mass-to-energy ratio.
