# Inverse-radius viscous disk similarity

↑ **Parent:** [Viscous evolution of an accretion disk](viscous-evolution-of-an-accretion-disk.md)

For a [Keplerian accretion disk](keplerian-accretion-disk.md) with $\bar\nu\propto r^{5/2}$, dimensionless $g=\bar\nu\Sigma/(\bar\nu_0\Sigma_0)$ satisfies $g_\tau=4x^{5/2}g_{xx}+6x^{3/2}g_x$. In $y=x^{-1/2}$ this is $g_\tau=y g_{yy}$. The ansatz $g=G(y/\tau)$ gives $G=A+B e^{-y/\tau}$; finite disk [mass](mass.md) selects $A=0$. Its nonzero profile has logarithmically infinite disk [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) on an infinite radial domain.
