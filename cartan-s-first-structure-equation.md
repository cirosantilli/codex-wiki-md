<h1 id="cartan-s-first-structure-equation">Cartan's first structure equation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Connection 1-form](connection-1-form-split.md)

Cartan's first structure equation is

$$
T^a=d\theta^a+\omega^a{}_b\wedge\theta^b.
$$

For the torsion-free [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md), $T^a=0$.
