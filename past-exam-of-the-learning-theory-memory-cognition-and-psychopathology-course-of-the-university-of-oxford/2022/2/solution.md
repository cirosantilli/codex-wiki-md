**[Aberrant salience](../../../aberrant-salience.md) is a useful account of how some psychotic experiences become compelling, but it is a [hypothesis](../../../hypothesis.md) about a process contributing to symptoms, not a complete explanation of [schizophrenia](../../../schizophrenia.md).** Salience concerns what captures [attention](../../../attention.md) and motivates interpretation or action. It is distinct from whether a stimulus is physically intense, pleasurable or objectively informative.

In [Shitij Kapur](../../../shitij-kapur.md)'s original framework, dysregulated [dopamine](../../../dopamine.md) gives inappropriate motivational significance to ordinary events or internally generated representations. A coincidental glance can consequently feel urgent and personally directed. A [delusion](../../../delusion.md) may then be an attempt to explain that experience using the person's existing [beliefs](../../../belief.md) and circumstances. The model connects neurobiology with the subjective onset of [psychosis](../../../psychosis.md) rather than treating an unusual [belief](../../../belief.md) as a direct chemical readout. It also predicts that dampening salience can reduce the compelling quality of experiences before their interpretation has been revised. This is a theoretical synthesis, not an experiment proving the entire causal chain.[American Journal of Psychiatry 160, 13–23; original framework linking biology, phenomenology and pharmacology](../../../psychosis-as-a-state-of-aberrant-salience-by-shitij-kapur.md)

The framework makes two separable predictions: inappropriate significance should attach to uninformative cues, and relevant cues may fail to gain appropriate significance. They should be measured independently. In the [Salience Attribution Test](../../../salience-attribution-test.md) used by [Jonathan P. Roiser](../../../jonathan-p-roiser.md) and colleagues, visual cues varied along colour and form dimensions, with only one dimension predicting monetary [reinforcement](../../../reinforcement.md). Reaction-time speeding and explicit [probability](../../../probability.md) ratings indexed responding to relevant versus irrelevant features. Patients showed reduced adaptive salience; importantly, the diagnostic groups did not differ overall in [aberrant salience](../../../aberrant-salience.md). Greater explicit [aberrant salience](../../../aberrant-salience.md) occurred in patients with [delusions](../../../delusion.md) relative to patients without them. The small, predominantly medicated sample, subgroup analysis and multiple comparisons prevent presenting this as a universal [schizophrenia](../../../schizophrenia.md) deficit.[Psychological Medicine 39, 199–209; Salience Attribution Test, Results and study limitations](../../../do-patients-with-schizophrenia-exhibit-aberrant-salience-by-jonathan-p-roiser-and-others.md)

A useful measurement caution follows directly. If equally uninformative features receive noisy ratings $R_1,R_2$, the absolute difference $|R_1-R_2|$ is generally positive even when both estimates are unbiased. For independent normal errors of [variance](../../../variance-split.md) $\sigma^2$, its expectation is $2\sigma/\sqrt\pi$. A high score can therefore reflect rating noise or poor [probability](../../../probability.md) discrimination as well as genuinely assigned significance. Repeated trials, reliability assessment, explicit task comprehension and measurements of adaptive [learning](../../../learning.md) are needed to interpret the score.

Biological evidence is compatible with, but does not uniquely identify, the [hypothesis](../../../hypothesis.md). [Oliver D. Howes](../../../oliver-d-howes.md) and colleagues used fluorodopa [PET](../../../photoinduced-electron-transfer.md) to compare people with prodromal symptoms, patients with [schizophrenia](../../../schizophrenia.md) and controls. Elevated synthesis-related uptake in the associative [striatum](../../../striatum.md) in the prodromal group links [dopamine](../../../dopamine.md) dysfunction to a stage before established psychotic illness. This was a case-control study; uptake is not a recording of cue-specific phasic [dopamine](../../../dopamine.md), and a high-risk group does not establish that every individual will develop [schizophrenia](../../../schizophrenia.md). It supplies an upstream biological constraint, not a demonstration that a particular neutral cue caused a [delusion](../../../delusion.md).[Archives of General Psychiatry 66, 13–20; case-control fluorodopa PET](../../../elevated-striatal-dopamine-function-linked-to-prodromal-signs-of-schizophrenia-by-oliver-d-howes-and-others.md)

Several explanatory steps remain. An account must specify why significance is assigned to this event, how a persistent interpretation is selected, and why contradictory evidence fails to revise it. [Attention](../../../attention.md), [memory retrieval](../../../memory-retrieval.md), prior [beliefs](../../../belief.md) and the social environment can supply those constraints. [Dopamine](../../../dopamine.md) dysfunction need not explain all negative symptoms or cognitive difficulties. [Antipsychotic](../../../antipsychotic.md) effects cannot alone establish the original cause: altering a downstream component can improve symptoms arising through several pathways. Nor should a medication-associated decrease in ordinary [motivation](../../../motivation.md) automatically be taken as recovery of selective salience.

A similar perspective is particularly productive for [substance use disorder](../../../substance-use-disorder.md). [Incentive salience](../../../incentive-salience.md) distinguishes cue-triggered wanting from pleasure. [Cindy L. Wyvell](../../../cindy-l-wyvell.md) and colleagues separately trained a sucrose-seeking response and a reward-predictive cue. Accumbens amphetamine enhanced cue-triggered responding in a test without [reward](../../../reward.md) [reinforcement](../../../reinforcement.md), without enhancing sucrose liking measured by taste reactivity. A later sensitization experiment found amplified cue-triggered seeking in a drug-free test after prior exposure. The experiments distinguish [motivation](../../../motivation.md) from hedonic impact; they do not themselves demonstrate every feature of human addiction.[Journal of Neuroscience 20, 8122–8130; separate instrumental and cue training, extinction test and taste reactivity](../../../intra-accumbens-amphetamine-increases-the-conditioned-incentive-salience-of-sucrose-reward-by-cindy-l-wyvell-and-others.md)[Journal of Neuroscience 21, 7831–7840; drug-free test ten days after sensitization](../../../incentive-sensitization-by-previous-amphetamine-exposure-by-cindy-l-wyvell-and-others.md)

This suggests how drug cues can capture behaviour even when enjoyment has declined or the person intends abstinence. Unlike [psychosis](../../../psychosis.md), however, these cues were genuinely associated with [reward](../../../reward.md). The shared issue is excessive or poorly regulated motivational control, not necessarily significance attached to statistically irrelevant events.

[Anxiety disorders](../../../anxiety-disorder.md) offer a second, more limited analogy: threat-related cues can monopolise [attention](../../../attention.md), avoidance can maintain their significance, and safe cues may be treated as dangerous. In [panic disorder](../../../panic-disorder.md), catastrophic interpretation can make ordinary bodily sensations feel threatening. Such accounts concern [threat appraisal](../../../threat-appraisal.md) and discrimination and do not justify assuming identical [dopamine](../../../dopamine.md) abnormalities across diagnoses. **[Aberrant salience](../../../aberrant-salience.md) is most informative as a testable, transdiagnostic process whose computational meaning, neural implementation and clinical consequences must each be specified.**
