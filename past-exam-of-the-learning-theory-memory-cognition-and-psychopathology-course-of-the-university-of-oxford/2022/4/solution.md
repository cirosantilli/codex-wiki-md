↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

**Psychology has developed interventions that reduce some criminal and violent behaviour in particular populations; the evidence supports conditional effectiveness rather than a single [treatment](../../../treatment.md) for criminality.** Criminal behaviour is a legal category covering different actions and contexts, not a diagnosis. An arrest, a conviction, an episode of [aggression](../../../aggression.md) and removal from home are different outcomes. Their measurement and the comparison service matter to any effectiveness claim.

One approach is [cognitive behavioral therapy](../../../cognitive-behavioral-therapy.md) directed at decisions in provocative situations. Its theoretical basis is that rapidly selected interpretations and practised responses can become inappropriate when circumstances change. Work on identifying automatic appraisals, considering alternatives, rehearsing a pause and practising nonviolent responses aims to improve action selection before escalation. [Social learning](../../../social-learning.md) supplies modelling and rehearsal; [reinforcement](../../../reinforcement.md) helps establish an alternative response. The [hypothesis](../../../hypothesis.md) concerns modifiable processes rather than an assumption that all offending reflects deficient intelligence or deliberate calculation.

The [Becoming a Man](../../../becoming-a-man.md) programme evaluated by [Sara B. Heller](../../../sara-b-heller.md) and colleagues provides unusually strong evidence because two school-based studies randomly allocated access. The authors reported programme-participation estimates of 28–35% fewer total arrests and 45–50% fewer violent-crime arrests during intervention periods, together with improved school engagement. Their mechanism evidence was suggestive of slowing down automatic interpretations, rather than establishing mediation by a measured trait. These participation estimates should not be confused with the raw effect of being offered a place. The randomised offer supplies the clean intention-to-treat contrast; estimating participation effects requires additional assumptions about uptake.[Quarterly Journal of Economics 132, 1–54; two Becoming a Man randomized trials and mechanism analyses](../../../thinking-fast-and-slow-some-field-experiments-to-reduce-crime-and-dropout-in-chicago-by-sara-b-heller-and-others.md)

Such results support a specific intervention in disadvantaged male youth in Chicago. They do not show that the same programme treats all adults with convictions, nor that the benefit must persist after sessions end. School membership, facilitator skill and local exposure to [violence](../../../violence.md) can alter effectiveness. Administrative arrest records avoid some [self-report](../../../self-report.md) biases but also depend on policing; reduced arrests are not a perfect measure of the total number or severity of offences. Where feasible, independent victimisation or [self-report](../../../self-report.md) measures and school outcomes should complement those records.

A second approach, [multisystemic therapy](../../../multisystemic-therapy.md), treats the adolescent's behaviour within interacting family, peer, school and community settings. A functional analysis asks what precedes and maintains a specific behaviour. [Treatment](../../../treatment.md) may strengthen consistent parental monitoring, reinforce prosocial activity, reduce access to reinforcing antisocial peer interactions and help families solve practical barriers to attendance. [Social learning](../../../social-learning.md) and [operant conditioning](../../../operant-conditioning.md) explain these components; the wider systems perspective explains why changing an isolated individual may fail if the surrounding contingencies remain unchanged. Delivery in the family's environment is part of the intervention, not merely a change of consultation venue.

In [Charles M. Borduin](../../../charles-m-borduin.md)'s randomised study of 176 serious juvenile offenders, [multisystemic therapy](../../../multisystemic-therapy.md) outperformed individual therapy on family-related measures and on rearrest outcomes over four years. This is evidence beyond improvement on a therapist's symptom scale. Nevertheless, a small developer-associated efficacy study against one comparator does not establish the size of benefit under routine services in another country.[Journal of Consulting and Clinical Psychology 63, 569–578; randomized 176-youth comparison and four-year rearrest follow-up](../../../multisystemic-treatment-of-serious-juvenile-offenders-long-term-prevention-of-criminality-and-violence-by-charles-m-borduin-and-others.md)

The larger English START trial led by [Peter Fonagy](../../../peter-fonagy.md) makes this limitation concrete. It allocated 684 families to [multisystemic therapy](../../../multisystemic-therapy.md) followed by management as usual or to management as usual alone. At 18 months, the primary outcome, out-of-home placement, occurred in 43/340 versus 36/335 participants with available outcome data: approximately 13% versus 11%, with a reported [odds ratio](../../../odds-ratio.md) of 1.25 and 95% [confidence interval](../../../confidence-interval.md) 0.77–2.05. This did not demonstrate superiority. Placement is not identical to reoffending, but this pragmatic result prevents presenting the programme as reliably better than established local provision.[Lancet Psychiatry 5, 119–133; Methods, primary outcome and Findings at 18 months](../../../multisystemic-therapy-versus-management-as-usual-in-the-treatment-of-adolescent-antisocial-behaviour-start-by-peter-fonagy-and-others.md)

Both approaches illustrate why theoretical plausibility and outcome evidence have separate roles. Skills rehearsal can be the stated mechanism while the actual benefit arises through a supportive adult, structured activity or changes in peer exposure. Testing mechanism requires measurement before outcome change and, ideally, experimental variation of the proposed component. A favourable post-treatment association between parenting and offending is insufficient by itself.

A defensible assessment would prespecify serious harm and [recidivism](../../../recidivism.md) outcomes, use random allocation with an adequate active comparator, document [treatment fidelity](../../../treatment-fidelity.md) and uptake, and track participants after services stop. It would assess costs, school and family functioning, unintended exclusion and whether effects differ by need. Coercive participation also makes meaningful consent and independent outcome measurement particularly important. **There is evidence that structured cognitive and family-based interventions can reduce offending, but [treatment](../../../treatment.md) choice must follow population-specific comparative trials rather than the programme label or its theoretical appeal alone.**
