# Stemward slippage

↑ **Parent:** [Taphonomic bias](taphonomic-bias.md)

Erroneous placement toward the base of a lineage when loss of diagnostic characters through [carcass decay](carcass-decay.md) is interpreted as an ancestral biological absence. Coding unobservable characters as unknown and comparing preservation across specimens can reduce the problem.
