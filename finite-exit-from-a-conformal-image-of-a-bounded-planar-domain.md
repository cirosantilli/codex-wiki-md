# Finite exit from a conformal image of a bounded planar domain

↑ **Parent:** [Conformal Brownian clock](conformal-brownian-clock.md)

A [planar Brownian motion](planar-brownian-motion.md) exits a conformal image of a bounded planar domain in finite time [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md). If the image lifetime were infinite, the inverse derivative is bounded away from zero on a small interior disc. [Recurrence of planar Brownian motion](recurrence-of-planar-brownian-motion.md) and the [Strong Markov property](strong-markov-property.md) give infinite [Brownian occupation time](brownian-occupation-time.md) there, forcing the inverse [conformal Brownian clock](conformal-brownian-clock.md) to diverge. It cannot exceed the finite original exit time. The expected image exit time can nevertheless be infinite.
