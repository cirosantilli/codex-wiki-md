# Analytical chemistry

↑ **Parent:** [Chemistry](chemistry-split.md)

The branch of [chemistry](chemistry-split.md) concerned with identifying substances in a sample and measuring their quantities or [concentrations](concentration.md). A [bicinchoninic acid assay](bicinchoninic-acid-assay.md), for example, estimates [protein](protein-split.md) content through an optical measurement.
