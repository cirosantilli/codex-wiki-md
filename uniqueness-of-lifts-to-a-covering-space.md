# Uniqueness of lifts to a covering space

↑ **Parent:** [Covering space](covering-space.md)

Let $p:\widetilde X\to X$ be a covering map and let $Y$ be path-connected. If two lifts $\widetilde f_1,\widetilde f_2:Y\to\widetilde X$ of the same map $f:Y\to X$ agree at one point, they agree everywhere. Join that point to any other by a path and apply uniqueness in the [path lifting theorem](path-lifting-theorem.md).
