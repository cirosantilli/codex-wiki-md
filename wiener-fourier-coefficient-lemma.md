# Wiener Fourier coefficient lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Bochner–Herglotz theorem](bochner-herglotz-theorem.md)

For a finite positive [Borel measure](borel-measure.md) on the circle, averaged squared [Fourier coefficients](fourier-coefficient.md) tend to the sum of squares of its atomic masses. Express each squared coefficient as a double integral and average the circle character. The averaged character is bounded by one, tends to one on the diagonal and to zero elsewhere, so the [dominated convergence theorem](dominated-convergence-theorem.md) gives the diagonal mass. In particular an atomless measure has vanishing averaged absolute coefficients by the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md).
