# Completion of an algebraic closure of a p-adic field is algebraically closed

↑ **Parent:** [Krasner's lemma](krasner-s-lemma.md)

The completion $\mathbb C_p$ of an [algebraic closure](algebraic-closure.md) of $\mathbb Q_p$ is an [algebraically closed field](algebraically-closed-field.md). Approximate a polynomial over $\mathbb C_p$ by one over $\overline{\mathbb Q}_p$, use [continuity of roots over a non-Archimedean field](continuity-of-roots-over-a-non-archimedean-field.md) to obtain a nearby algebraic root, and then use [Krasner's lemma](krasner-s-lemma.md) to show that the original root already belongs to $\mathbb C_p$.
