# Brownian wedge-exit probability

↑ **Parent:** [Power-map reduction for Brownian exit from a wedge](power-map-reduction-for-brownian-exit-from-a-wedge.md)

A [planar Brownian motion](planar-brownian-motion.md) starting at $1$ exits the disk of radius $r>1$ before leaving a wedge of opening $\alpha\in(0,2\pi]$, centered on the positive axis, with probability $(4/\pi)\arctan(r^{-\pi/\alpha})$. The [power-map reduction for Brownian exit from a wedge](power-map-reduction-for-brownian-exit-from-a-wedge.md), the [reflection identity for Brownian exit from a half-disc](reflection-identity-for-brownian-exit-from-a-half-disc.md) and the [Möbius calculation of circular Brownian exit](mobius-calculation-of-circular-brownian-exit.md) give the formula. Its large-radius decay exponent is $\pi/\alpha$.
