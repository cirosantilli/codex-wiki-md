# Homogeneous hyperelastic material

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperelastic material](hyperelastic-material.md)

A [hyperelastic material](hyperelastic-material.md) is homogeneous when its [strain energy density](strain-energy-density.md) $W(F)$ has no explicit dependence on the [reference configuration](reference-configuration.md) position. Its first [Piola-Kirchhoff stress tensor](piola-kirchhoff-stress-tensors.md) is $T=DW(F)$. Homogeneity of the material does not require the [deformation gradient](deformation-gradient.md) to be spatially constant.
