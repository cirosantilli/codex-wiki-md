# Population mean

↑ **Parent:** [Expected value](expected-value-split.md)

The [expected value](expected-value-split.md) of a numerical characteristic under the target population's [probability distribution](probability-distribution-split.md), when it exists. A [sample mean](sample-mean.md) estimates this fixed [statistical parameter](statistical-parameter.md) from observed data; it is not itself the population value.
