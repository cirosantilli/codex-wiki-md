# Block basic sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Schauder basis](schauder-basis.md)

Relative to a [Schauder basis](schauder-basis.md) $(e_j)$, a block basic sequence consists of nonzero finite combinations supported on successive disjoint coordinate intervals. In the [l-p sequence space](l-p-sequence-space.md), disjointly supported vectors $u_n$ of norm one satisfy $\|\sum a_nu_n\|_p^p=\sum|a_n|^p$. Their closed span is consequently isometric to the [l-p sequence space](l-p-sequence-space.md).
