# C2 root system

↑ **Parent:** [Cn root system](cn-root-system.md)

In an orthonormal basis $(\varepsilon_1,\varepsilon_2)$, the roots are $\pm(\varepsilon_1-\varepsilon_2)$, $\pm(\varepsilon_1+\varepsilon_2)$, $\pm2\varepsilon_1$, and $\pm2\varepsilon_2$. Choose [simple roots](simple-root.md) $\alpha_1=\varepsilon_1-\varepsilon_2$ and $\alpha_2=2\varepsilon_2$, with $\alpha_1$ a [short root](short-root.md). The [fundamental weights](fundamental-weight.md) are $\omega_1=\varepsilon_1$ and $\omega_2=\varepsilon_1+\varepsilon_2$.
