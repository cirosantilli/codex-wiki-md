# Osmium tetroxide

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md)

[Osmium tetroxide](osmium-tetroxide.md) adds to an [alkene](alkene.md) to form a [cyclic osmate ester](cyclic-osmate-ester.md). Hydrolysis releases a vicinal diol; regeneration of Os(VIII) requires a reoxidant in a catalytic cycle.
