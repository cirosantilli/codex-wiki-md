# Radial derivative

↑ **Parent:** [Directional derivative](directional-derivative.md)

Away from the origin, the radial derivative is the [directional derivative](directional-derivative.md) along the outward radial unit vector. For a [Sobolev space](sobolev-space-split.md) function it is the same scalar product with the [weak gradient](weak-gradient.md), defined [almost everywhere](almost-everywhere.md). On an [annulus](annulus-mathematics.md), it has no singular direction at the origin.
