# Folded wave-number counting for a monatomic chain

↑ **Parent:** [Monatomic-chain dispersion relation](monatomic-chain-dispersion-relation.md)

A periodic chain of $N$ sites and length $Na$ has allowed signed [wave numbers](wavenumber.md) separated by $2\pi/(Na)$. The continuum state density on the signed axis is $Na/(2\pi)$. Combining opposite propagation directions onto $0<|q|<\pi/a$ doubles it to $Na/\pi$. This counts the same $N$ modes, not two independent [acoustic phonon branches](acoustic-phonon-branch.md).
