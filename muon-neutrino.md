# Muon neutrino

↑ **Parent:** [Neutrino](neutrino.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muon_neutrino)

The muon neutrino is the neutral [lepton](lepton.md) coupled to a [muon](muon.md) by the [weak charged current](charged-current.md). It carries [lepton number](lepton-number.md) $+1$; its antineutrino has the opposite [lepton number](lepton-number.md).
