# Medical imaging

↑ **Parent:** [Physics](physics-split.md)

[Computed tomography](computed-tomography.md), [positron emission tomography](positron-emission-tomography.md) and [single-photon emission computed tomography](single-photon-emission-computed-tomography.md) use different signal-generation and reconstruction physics.
