<h1 id="tensor-square-of-the-su-3-adjoint-representation">Tensor square of the SU(3) adjoint representation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Adjoint representation of SU(3)](adjoint-representation-of-su-3.md)

For traceless matrices $T,S$ transforming by conjugation under [SU(3)](su-3-group.md), the [trace](matrix-trace.md) $\operatorname{tr}(TS)$ supplies a singlet, the [commutator](commutator.md) $[T,S]$ an antisymmetric octet, and the traceless anticommutator a symmetric octet. Symmetrizing the two upper and two lower indices and removing traces supplies the 27. Contracting an antisymmetric lower pair with the invariant alternating tensor and symmetrizing the remaining three upper indices supplies the 10, and the conjugate construction supplies the conjugate 10. These nonzero equivariant maps exhaust the dimensions: the symmetric part has $36=1+8+27$, the antisymmetric part $28=8+10+10$.
