# Gravitational rms escape and collapse speeds

↑ **Parent:** [Gravitational radius](gravitational-radius.md)

For identical point masses of total mass $M$, let $W=-GM^2/R_g$ exclude self-interactions. The [virial theorem](virial-theorem.md) gives $\langle v_{\rm eq}^2\rangle=GM/R_g$. Since the sum of individual potential energies counts each pair twice, the mass-weighted mean frozen-potential [escape velocity](escape-velocity.md) squared is $-4W/M$. A dissipation-free collapse from zero total [energy](energy.md) instead has kinetic energy $-W$, hence mean squared speed $-2W/M$. These are three-dimensional speeds relative to the [centre of mass](center-of-mass.md); a coherent collapse speed is not a local random [velocity dispersion](velocity-dispersion.md).
