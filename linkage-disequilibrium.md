# Linkage disequilibrium

↑ **Parent:** [Population genetics](population-genetics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linkage_disequilibrium)

Linkage disequilibrium is nonindependence of allelic states at different loci in population [haplotypes](haplotype.md). For alleles $A,B$, $D=P(AB)-P(A)P(B)$. It is a population-frequency property, distinct from [genetic linkage](genetic-linkage.md), which concerns transmission within meioses.
