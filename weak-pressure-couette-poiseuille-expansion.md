# Weak-pressure Couette-Poiseuille expansion

↑ **Parent:** [Differential shear viscosity](differential-shear-viscosity.md)

Perturb the [Couette flow](couette-flow.md) between walls moving at +-U by a small pressure gradient -G. The mean shear rate remains U/h, and the stress variation across the gap is -Gy. Linearizing the monotone [generalized Newtonian fluid](generalized-newtonian-fluid.md) law divides this variation by differential viscosity, giving the displayed velocity and flux per unit width. Require $Gh\ll(U/h)\eta_d(U/h)$ and a smooth constitutive law on the perturbed rates. For a [power-law fluid](power-law-fluid.md), the denominator is $nk(U/h)^{n-1}$.
