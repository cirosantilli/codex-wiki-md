# Sequence space

↑ **Parent:** [Vector space](vector-space-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sequence_space)

A sequence space is a [vector space](vector-space-split.md) of scalar [sequences](sequence.md), closed under coordinatewise addition and scalar multiplication. Examples include the [l-p sequence space](l-p-sequence-space.md), the [l-infinity sequence space](l-infinity-sequence-space.md) and spaces of convergent sequences.
