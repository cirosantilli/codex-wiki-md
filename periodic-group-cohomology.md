# Periodic group cohomology

↑ **Parent:** [Group cohomology](group-cohomology.md)

For a finite group, the usual integral notion asks for a class $\Delta\in H^n(G;\mathbb Z)$ whose [cup product](cup-product.md) gives the displayed isomorphisms for every coefficient module $M$ and every $i>0$. The analogous claim for just trivial coefficients over a fixed field is weaker and should be stated as such. A free cohomologically orientation-preserving sphere action supplies that field-coefficient version through [periodic group cohomology from a free sphere action](periodic-group-cohomology-from-a-free-sphere-action.md).
