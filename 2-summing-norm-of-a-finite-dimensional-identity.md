# 2-summing norm of a finite-dimensional identity

↑ **Parent:** [2-summing operator](2-summing-operator.md)

For a finite-dimensional [normed vector space](normed-vector-space.md) $E$, represent a finite family by its column operator $A:\ell_2^m\to E$. Its operator norm is the family's weak square norm. Since $A=AQ$ for the [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md) onto $(\ker A)^\perp$, summing $\|Ae_j\|^2\le\|A\|^2\|Qe_j\|_2^2$ proves the upper bound $\sqrt{\dim E}$. In coordinates adapted to a maximal inscribed Euclidean or Hermitian [ellipsoid](ellipsoid.md), the [John contact decomposition](john-contact-decomposition.md) gives $\sum_r c_ru_ru_r^*=I$ and $\sum_rc_r=\dim E$. The vectors $\sqrt{c_r}u_r$ have total squared norm $\dim E$ and weak square norm one, giving equality. This holds over both real and complex scalars.
