# Brownian rough path small-ball positivity

↑ **Parent:** [Enhanced Brownian motion](enhanced-brownian-motion.md)

For every $2<p<3$ and every positive radius, the canonical Brownian enhancement has positive probability of lying in the displayed [rough path metric](rough-path-metric.md) ball. This controls both displacement and area. Together with [Cameron-Martin quasi-invariance of enhanced Brownian motion](cameron-martin-quasi-invariance-of-enhanced-brownian-motion.md), it puts every lifted Cameron-Martin control in the [support of enhanced Brownian motion](support-of-enhanced-brownian-motion.md).
