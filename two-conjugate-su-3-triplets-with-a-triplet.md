<h1 id="two-conjugate-su-3-triplets-with-a-triplet">Two conjugate SU(3) triplets with a triplet</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Fundamental representations of sl3](fundamental-representations-of-sl3.md)

Split the two dual factors into antisymmetric and symmetric [tensors](tensor.md). The invariant [Levi-Civita symbol](levi-civita-symbol.md) identifies the antisymmetric pair with $\mathbf3$, giving $\mathbf6\oplus\overline{\mathbf3}$ after multiplying by the remaining triplet. On the symmetric part, contraction $T^a{}_{ba}$ maps onto $\overline{\mathbf3}$; the equivariant splitting is $T^a{}_{bc}=(\delta^a_bv_c+\delta^a_cv_b)/4$. Its trace-free kernel has dimension $18-3=15$ and highest weight with [Dynkin labels](dynkin-label.md) $(1,2)$. The [Weyl dimension formula](weyl-dimension-formula.md) gives dimension $15$, so this is exactly the irreducible kernel. It is conjugate to the other fifteen-dimensional module with labels $(2,1)$; dimension alone does not distinguish the two.
