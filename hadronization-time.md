# Hadronization time

↑ **Parent:** [Hadronization](hadronization.md)

A characteristic proper timescale for strong bound-state formation, estimated by [dimensional analysis](dimensional-analysis.md) from the [QCD scale](qcd-scale.md). A scale of a few tenths of a GeV corresponds to a few $10^{-24}\,\mathrm s$. It is an order-of-magnitude guide rather than a universal sharply defined formation time.
