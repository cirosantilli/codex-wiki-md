# Fluid approximation of slotted ALOHA

↑ **Parent:** [Slotted ALOHA](slotted-aloha.md)

When backlog $N$ and attempt-control scale $S$ are large with $N/S\approx\kappa$, idle and success probabilities approach $e^{-\kappa}$ and $\kappa e^{-\kappa}$. Expected update increments then motivate a [fluid model](fluid-model.md) described by [ordinary differential equations](ordinary-differential-equation.md).
