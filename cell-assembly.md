# Cell assembly

↑ **Parent:** [Neural coding](neural-coding.md)

A group of [neurons](neuron-split.md) whose coordinated activity and learned interactions can represent or participate in an experience. An assembly can overlap with assemblies for other events; it need not assign one private [neuron](neuron-split.md) to each memory.
