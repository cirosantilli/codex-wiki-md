# Induced character

↑ **Parent:** [Induced representation](induced-representation.md)

For a character $\psi$ of $H\leq G$, the induced character is

$$
(\operatorname{Ind}_H^G\psi)(g)=\frac1{|H|}\sum_{x\in G:\,x^{-1}gx\in H}\psi(x^{-1}gx).
$$

This is the [character of a representation](character-of-a-representation.md) of $\mathbb C[G]\otimes_{\mathbb C[H]}V_\psi$. Substituting this expression into the character inner product and putting $g=xhx^{-1}$ proves [Frobenius reciprocity](frobenius-reciprocity.md).
