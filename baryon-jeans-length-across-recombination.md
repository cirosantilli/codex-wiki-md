# Baryon Jeans length across recombination

↑ **Parent:** [Comoving Jeans length](comoving-jeans-length.md)

Before [cosmological recombination](recombination-cosmology.md), [photon-baryon sound speed](photon-baryon-sound-speed.md) controls the baryonic [comoving Jeans length](comoving-jeans-length.md). The tightly coupled [photon-baryon fluid](photon-baryon-fluid.md) has $c_s^2=c^2/[3(1+R_b)]$, where $R_b=3\bar\rho_b/(4\bar\rho_\gamma)\propto a$. Using total background density in the instantaneous dynamical Jeans estimate, the scale grows as $a$ during [radiation domination](radiation-domination.md), approaches a plateau in strongly baryon-loaded [matter domination](matter-domination.md), and drops when [photon decoupling](photon-decoupling.md) removes photon pressure support. Subsequently, adiabatic monatomic-gas cooling gives $T_b\propto a^{-2}$ and a [comoving Jeans length](comoving-jeans-length.md) proportional to $a^{-1/2}$. Residual [Compton scattering](compton-scattering.md) can delay that cooling law.
