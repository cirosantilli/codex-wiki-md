# Spin inference from magnetic-dipole hyperfine intervals

↑ **Parent:** [Landé interval rule](lande-interval-rule.md)

Consecutive positive gaps obey $\nu_F=|A|F/h$ for an isolated [hyperfine multiplet](hyperfine-multiplet.md) governed by the [magnetic-dipole hyperfine interaction](magnetic-dipole-hyperfine-interaction.md). Their common difference estimates $|A|/h$, and the first gap divided by that scale identifies the first higher F. The endpoints of the resulting F ladder constrain I and J. Gap magnitudes alone do not fix the sign of A or an absolute nuclear g-factor.
