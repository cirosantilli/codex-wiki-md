# Allocation concealment

↑ **Parent:** [Randomization](randomization.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allocation_concealment)

Allocation concealment prevents those deciding whether to enroll an eligible participant from knowing the upcoming randomized assignment. It protects the [randomized controlled trial](randomized-controlled-trial.md) against selection based on that assignment. It concerns access to the assignment before enrollment; keeping participants or assessors unaware after assignment is a separate design feature.
