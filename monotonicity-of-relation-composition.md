# Monotonicity of relation composition

↑ **Parent:** [Composition of relations](composition-of-relations.md)

If $R\subseteq R'$ and $S\subseteq S'$, then $R\circ S\subseteq R'\circ S'$. A witness joining a pair through $S$ and then $R$ also joins it through the larger relations. If both $R$ and $S$ are [reflexive relations](reflexive-relation.md) on the same [set](set-split.md), inserting an identity step gives $R\cup S\subseteq R\circ S$, so monotonicity also gives $(R\cup S)^2\subseteq(R\circ S)^2$.
