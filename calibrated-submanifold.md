# Calibrated submanifold

↑ **Parent:** [Calibrated geometry](calibrated-geometry.md)

An oriented submanifold is calibrated by a [calibration](calibration-differential-geometry.md) when its tangent planes attain the comass bound at every point. Closedness then compares its volume with homologous competitors by [Stokes theorem](stokes-theorem.md).
