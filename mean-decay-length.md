# Mean decay length

↑ **Parent:** [Proper mean lifetime](proper-mean-lifetime.md)

For a freely moving unstable [particle](particle-physics.md) of fixed [speed](speed.md) $v=\beta c$ and [proper mean lifetime](proper-mean-lifetime.md) $\tau_0$, the mean travelled [distance](distance.md) before decay is $\ell_d=v\gamma\tau_0$. Survival beyond [distance](distance.md) $L$ has [probability](probability.md) $e^{-L/\ell_d}$, since elapsed [proper time](proper-time.md) is $L/(\gamma v)$. For [rest mass](invariant-mass.md) $m$ and [three-momentum](three-momentum.md) magnitude $p$, $\ell_d=p\tau_0/m$.
