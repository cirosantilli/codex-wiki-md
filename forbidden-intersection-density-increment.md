# Forbidden-intersection density increment

↑ **Parent:** [Extremal set theory](extremal-set-theory-split.md)

For two [set families](set-family.md) forbidding all cross-intersections in $[a,b]$, split into absent and present coordinate sections. The section pairs $(\mathcal F_1,\mathcal G_1)$, $(\mathcal F_0,\mathcal G_0\cup\mathcal G_1)$, and $(\mathcal F_1,\mathcal G_0\cap\mathcal G_1)$ forbid, respectively, $[a-1,b-1]$, $[a,b]$, and $[a-1,b]$ in one fewer dimension. For $0<\delta\leq1/10$, either one of the first two pairs, allowing exchange of the [set family](set-family.md) names, increases the density product by at least $1+\delta$, or the third widens the interval and retains at least $1-\delta-2\delta^2$ of that product. This converts a single forbidden intersection into a long forbidden interval while recording every density gain and loss.
