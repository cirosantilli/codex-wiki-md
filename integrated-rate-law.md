# Integrated rate law

↑ **Parent:** [Rate law](rate-law.md)

An [integrated rate law](integrated-rate-law.md) relates [concentration](concentration.md) to elapsed time after solving a [rate equation](rate-equation.md) with an [initial condition](initial-condition.md). Its validity retains the assumptions of the underlying [rate law](rate-law.md), including fixed [rate constants](rate-constant.md) and appropriate [stoichiometry](stoichiometry.md). The [second-order reaction](second-order-reaction.md) reciprocal-[concentration](concentration.md) relation is one example; a different [rate equation](rate-equation.md) generally requires a different [integrated rate law](integrated-rate-law.md).[§12.4, opening paragraph and “Second-Order Reactions”: concentration as a function of elapsed time](chemistry-2e-by-paul-flowers-and-others.md)
