# Sum of Lp spaces

↑ **Parent:** [Lp space](lp-space.md)

The sum consists of [measurable functions](measurable-function.md) $f=g+h$ with $g\in L^{p_0}$ and $h\in L^{p_1}$, with [norm](norm.md) $\inf_{f=g+h}(\|g\|_{p_0}+\|h\|_{p_1})$. It is a [Banach space](banach-space-split.md). One construction identifies it with the quotient of $L^{p_0}\oplus_1L^{p_1}$ by the closed kernel of the addition map. Bounded endpoint operators are therefore bounded on the corresponding sum spaces.
