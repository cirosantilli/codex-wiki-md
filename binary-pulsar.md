# Binary pulsar

↑ **Parent:** [Pulsar](pulsar.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Binary_pulsar)

A binary pulsar is a [pulsar](pulsar.md) in a [binary star](binary-star.md): the companion's [gravity](gravity.md) changes the emitting [neutron star](neutron-star.md)'s motion and hence its pulse arrival times. The companion need not itself be a detectable [pulsar](pulsar.md). [Pulsar timing](pulsar-timing.md) turns that motion into a measurable [orbit](orbit-dynamical-system.md), making the system useful for [stellar mass determination](stellar-mass-determination.md) and tests of [general relativity](general-relativity-split.md).
