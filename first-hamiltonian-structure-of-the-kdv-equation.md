# First Hamiltonian structure of the KdV equation

↑ **Parent:** [Korteweg-De Vries equation](korteweg-de-vries-equation.md)

One normalization of the first [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) structure uses the constant differential [Poisson operator](poisson-operator.md) $J=-2\partial_x$. For [functionals](functional.md) with [variational derivatives](variational-derivative.md) $f,g$, its [Poisson bracket](poisson-bracket.md) is $\int fJg\,dx=-2\int fg'\,dx$ under periodic or vanishing-boundary conventions. Thus the field bracket is $\{u(x),u(y)\}=-2\partial_x\delta(x-y)$. The factor and sign change with normalization; the constant first-order operator is the invariant structural feature.
