# Convexity of a European call price in strike

↑ **Parent:** [European call option](european-call-option.md)

For any fixed maturity, a [European call option](european-call-option.md) price is convex in its strike because $K\mapsto(s-K)^+$ is convex for every terminal stock value $s$. Specifically, for $0\leq a\leq1$, $(s-aK_1-(1-a)K_2)^+\leq a(s-K_1)^++(1-a)(s-K_2)^+$. Multiplication by the positive discount factor and expectation under the pricing measure preserve this inequality. This also explains why a violation of convexity creates a butterfly-spread arbitrage.
