# Multiparticle parity

↑ **Parent:** [Parity](parity.md)

The parity of a two-particle centre-of-mass state with intrinsic parities $\eta_1,\eta_2$ and relative [orbital angular momentum](orbital-angular-momentum.md) $\ell$ is

$$
\eta_1\eta_2(-1)^\ell.
$$
