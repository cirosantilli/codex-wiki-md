# Magnetohydrodynamic phase mixing

↑ **Parent:** [Alfvén wave](alfven-wave.md)

Neighboring magnetic surfaces with different [Alfvén speeds](alfven-speed.md) carry the same axial waveform at different rates. For a traveling profile $f(R,z+v(R)t)$, differentiation produces the displayed term, so transverse gradients grow even without axial wave steepening. Small [magnetic diffusivity](magnetic-diffusivity.md) or [viscosity](dynamic-viscosity.md) can eventually act strongly on these gradients. The ideal process redistributes phase rather than directly dissipating wave energy.
