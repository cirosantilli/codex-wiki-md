# Early stopping of Landweber iteration

↑ **Parent:** [Landweber iteration](landweber-iteration.md)

The [Landweber spectral filter](landweber-spectral-filter.md) progressively admits smaller singular-value components. Its approximation bias tends to zero on exact admissible data, but its noise amplification grows. With $\|A\|\leq1$, a sufficient [a priori regularization parameter choice](a-priori-regularization-parameter-choice.md) is $n(\delta)\to\infty$ and $\sqrt{n(\delta)}\,\delta\to0$. Taking regularization parameter $\alpha=1/n$ expresses this as $\alpha\to0$ and $\delta/\sqrt\alpha\to0$; the [noise-bias decomposition for linear regularization](noise-bias-decomposition-for-linear-regularization.md) proves convergence.
