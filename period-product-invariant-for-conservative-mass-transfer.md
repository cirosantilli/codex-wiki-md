# Period-product invariant for conservative mass transfer

↑ **Parent:** [Conservative binary mass transfer](conservative-binary-mass-transfer.md)

Negligible spin and a circular orbit give $J=M_1M_2\sqrt{Ga/M}$. Fixed $M$ and $J$ imply $a(M_1M_2)^2$ is constant. [Kepler's third law](kepler-s-third-law.md) then gives the period-product invariant $P(M_1M_2)^3$.
