# Three-momentum

↑ **Parent:** [Four-momentum](four-momentum.md)

[Three-momentum](three-momentum.md) is the spatial part of a particle's [four-momentum](four-momentum.md), $p^\mu=(E/c,\mathbf p)$. Its components depend on the reference frame, while $p_\mu p^\mu=m^2c^2$ is a [Lorentz invariant](lorentz-invariance.md).
