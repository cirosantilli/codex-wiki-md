# Hydrogen-ionization disk instability

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal stability of an accretion disk](thermal-stability-of-an-accretion-disk.md)

Partial [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) [ionization](ionization.md) changes disk cooling and permits cold and hot stable thermal branches separated by unstable equilibria. A [dwarf nova](dwarf-nova.md) can cycle between a cold matter-storing disk and a hot, rapidly accreting disk. The cycle is described by the evolution of local [surface density](surface-density-of-a-disk.md) and [temperature](temperature.md), coupled to [viscous evolution of an accretion disk](viscous-evolution-of-an-accretion-disk.md).
