# Absorbed-power bandwidth of a viscously damped oscillator

↑ **Parent:** [Q factor](q-factor.md)

For a constant-amplitude [force](force-split.md) on $m\ddot x+b\dot x+kx=F_0\cos\Omega t$, the cycle-averaged absorbed [power](power.md) is $bF_0^2\Omega^2/[2((k-m\Omega^2)^2+b^2\Omega^2)]$. It peaks at $\omega_0=\sqrt{k/m}$. Its half-power [frequencies](frequency.md) are $\Omega_\pm=(\sqrt{b^2+4mk}\pm b)/(2m)$, hence their separation is exactly $b/m$ and $Q=\omega_0/\Delta\Omega$. A displacement-amplitude [resonance](resonance.md) has different half-power [frequencies](frequency.md) away from weak [damping](damping.md); the measured observable must be specified.
