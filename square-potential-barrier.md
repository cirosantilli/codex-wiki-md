# Square potential barrier

↑ **Parent:** [Potential barrier](potential-barrier.md)

A piecewise constant barrier of finite width. Exact scattering follows by matching the [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) and its derivative at its two interfaces; below its height the internal wave number is imaginary.
