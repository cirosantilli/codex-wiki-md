# Star-forming galaxy

↑ **Parent:** [Galaxy](galaxy-split.md)

A star-forming galaxy is a [galaxy](galaxy-split.md) with ongoing [star formation](star-formation.md). Its hot young stars can supply hydrogen-ionizing radiation to the [intergalactic medium](intergalactic-medium.md), with the contribution controlled by the [ionizing photon escape fraction](ionizing-photon-escape-fraction.md).
