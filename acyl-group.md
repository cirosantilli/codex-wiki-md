# Acyl group

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical substance](chemical-substance.md)

For the [carboxylic acid](carboxylic-acid.md)-derived examples here, an [acyl group](acyl-group.md) is the group remaining after formal removal of $\mathrm{OH}$ from $\mathrm{R{-}C(=O){-}OH}$. It includes the [oxygen](oxygen.md) at the end of the [fatty acid](fatty-acid.md)-derived chain, so it is more than just a [hydrocarbon](hydrocarbon.md) region. See [IUPAC class names: carboxylic acids and derivatives](https://iupac.qmul.ac.uk/class/carba.html).
