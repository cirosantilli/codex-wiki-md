# Harmonic forcing of a horizontally driven pendulum

↑ **Parent:** [Pendulum with a prescribed translating support](pendulum-with-a-prescribed-translating-support.md)

For small [angles](angle.md) about the downward vertical and prescribed pivot [position](position.md) $x=A\cos\omega t$, a [simple pendulum](simple-pendulum-split.md) obeys the displayed forced-oscillator equation. Off resonance, its harmonic particular solution is $[A\omega^2/(l(\omega_0^2-\omega^2))]\cos\omega t$, with $\omega_0^2=g/l$. At exact resonance, a pendulum initially vertical with zero angular [velocity](velocity.md) has $\theta=(A\omega_0/(2l))t\sin\omega_0t$ in the undamped linear model. The growing envelope eventually violates the small-angle assumption; [damping](damping.md) and nonlinear frequency shifts matter in a real pendulum.
