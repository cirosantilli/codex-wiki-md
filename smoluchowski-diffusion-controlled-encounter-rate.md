# Smoluchowski diffusion-controlled encounter rate

↑ **Parent:** [Diffusion-limited cluster aggregation](diffusion-limited-cluster-aggregation.md)

For two dilute spherical species with absorbing contact and negligible interaction barriers, relative [diffusion](diffusion.md) has coefficient $D=D_1+D_2$ and contact radius $R=a_1+a_2$. The steady concentration outside one target is $c(r)=c_\infty(1-R/r)$, solving the radial [Laplace equation](laplace-equation.md) with zero contact concentration. Its inward diffusive flux is $4\pi DRc_\infty$, proving the displayed bimolecular rate coefficient. For identical particles, converting this encounter coefficient into a rate of loss of cluster number additionally needs the factor that prevents counting the same pair twice.
