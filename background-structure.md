# Background structure

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical physics](mathematical-physics-split.md)

Geometric or field data held fixed when a theory's dynamical degrees of freedom vary. The [Minkowski metric](minkowski-metric.md) is background structure in ordinary [special relativity](special-relativity-split.md); the spacetime [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md) is dynamical in [general relativity](general-relativity-split.md). A fixed timelike vector field can introduce a preferred direction even when an equation is written using [tensor notation](index-notation.md).
