# Pointwise periodic self-map

↑ **Parent:** [Function](function-split.md)

A pointwise periodic self-map returns each point to itself after a positive number of iterates which may depend on the point. It is a [bijection](bijection.md): each finite cycle supplies predecessors; equality of two images can be undone by an iterate with an exponent one below a common multiple of the two periods. The [set](set-split.md) consequently decomposes into disjoint finite [permutation cycles](permutation-cycle.md). Pointwise periodicity does not imply that one iterate is the identity on an infinite [set](set-split.md).
