# Nonvertical qualification in the steady-top double-root condition

↑ **Parent:** [Steady precession of a Lagrange top](steady-precession-of-a-lagrange-top.md)

After fixing axial and vertical [angular momenta](angular-momentum.md), the heavy symmetric top has [energy](energy-split.md) $A\dot\theta^2/2+V_{\rm eff}(\cos\theta)$. A fixed angle strictly between zero and $\pi$ requires both $E=V_{\rm eff}(u_0)$ and $V_{\rm eff}'(u_0)=0$. Thus $P(u)=2A(1-u^2)(E-V_{\rm eff}(u))$ has a repeated interior root. A vertical axis belongs to the excluded endpoints: the upright stationary top has $P(u)=2Amga(1-u)^2(1+u)$, whose only repeated root is one. An assertion of an interior common root therefore needs the nonvertical hypothesis.
