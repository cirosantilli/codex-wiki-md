# History of physics

↑ **Parent:** [Physics](physics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_physics)

A discovery becomes more intelligible when its motivating question, proposed explanation and actual measurement can be followed together. The [photoelectric effect](photoelectric-effect.md) connects [Albert Einstein](albert-einstein.md)'s light-quantum reasoning with the [Millikan photoelectric experiment](millikan-photoelectric-experiment.md). The [Schrödinger equation](schrodinger-equation.md) and the [Davisson–Germer experiment](davisson-germer-experiment.md) connect mathematical [wave mechanics](wave-mechanics.md) with a laboratory test of [matter waves](matter-wave.md).

The [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics-split.md) provides one route through these subjects. Following the original papers and the people who built the apparatus also brings collaborators into view: [Lester Halbert Germer](lester-halbert-germer.md) appears alongside [Clinton J. Davisson](clinton-davisson.md) in the [Davisson–Germer experiment](davisson-germer-experiment.md). The history belongs in the articles on those people and concepts as well as in award records.
