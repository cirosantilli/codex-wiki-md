# Anaphase-promoting complex

↑ **Parent:** [Cell cycle](cell-cycle-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anaphase-promoting_complex)

The [anaphase-promoting complex](anaphase-promoting-complex.md) is a regulated [ubiquitin ligase](ubiquitin-ligase.md) that uses regulatory cofactors to target selected [cell cycle](cell-cycle-split.md) [proteins](protein-split.md) for destruction. Degradation of mitotic cyclins helps reset kinase activity during exit, while other substrates coordinate [chromosome](chromosome.md) separation.
