# Snowflake yeast

↑ **Parent:** [Clonal development](clonal-development.md)

[Snowflake yeast](snowflake-yeast.md) are cluster-forming [Saccharomyces cerevisiae](saccharomyces-cerevisiae.md) with branching connections between mothers and attached daughter [biological cells](biological-cell-split.md). They provide an experimental system for studying early transitions to simple [multicellular organisms](multicellular-organism.md); see the protocol, observations and limits under [multicellular organism](multicellular-organism.md).
