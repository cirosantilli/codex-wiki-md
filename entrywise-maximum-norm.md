# Entrywise maximum norm

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix](matrix.md)

The entrywise maximum norm is the largest absolute value of a matrix entry. For compatible vectors $x$ and $y$,

$$
|x^TAy|\leq\|A\|_{\max}\|x\|_1\|y\|_1.
$$
