# Characteristic of a rng

↑ **Parent:** [Rng (algebra)](rng-algebra.md)

The [characteristic of a rng](characteristic-of-a-rng.md) is the least positive [integer](integer-split.md) annihilating every element of its additive [abelian group](abelian-group.md), or zero if no such integer exists. In a unital [ring](ring.md) this equals the [characteristic of a ring](characteristic-of-a-ring.md): $n1=0$ implies $nr=(n1)r=0$, and annihilating every element in particular annihilates $1$. In a finite rng it is the [exponent of a finite group](exponent-of-a-finite-group.md) of the additive group.
