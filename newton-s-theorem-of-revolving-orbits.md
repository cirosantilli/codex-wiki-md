<h1 id="newton-s-theorem-of-revolving-orbits">Newton's theorem of revolving orbits</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Central potential](central-potential.md)

Adding an inverse-cube [force](force-split.md) can preserve radial motion while rescaling angular motion. For potential $-A/r+B/r^2$ and [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) $L$, the radial [effective potential](effective-potential.md) equals that of $-A/r$ with effective [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) $\sqrt{L^2+2mB}$ when this is real.
