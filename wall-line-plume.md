# Wall line plume

↑ **Parent:** [Line plume](line-plume.md)

A wall line plume is a one-sided [line plume](line-plume.md) adjoining a solid wall. Under a top-hat pure-plume model, $d(bW)/ds=\alpha W$ because only its outer edge entrains ambient fluid.
