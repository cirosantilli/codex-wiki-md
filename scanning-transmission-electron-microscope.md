# Scanning transmission electron microscope

↑ **Parent:** [Scanning transmission electron microscopy](scanning-transmission-electron-microscopy.md)

An [electron microscope](electron-microscope.md) that scans a focused probe across an electron-transparent specimen and records transmitted-electron signals at each scan position. Its image [magnification](magnification.md) is set by the displayed-to-scanned length ratio, separately from angular detector collection.
