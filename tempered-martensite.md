# Tempered martensite

↑ **Parent:** [Tempering (metallurgy)](tempering-metallurgy.md)

A microstructure in which [carbon](carbon.md) redistribution and carbide formation reduce the [supersaturation](supersaturation.md) of a martensitic matrix. Plate or lath morphology can remain while local [strain](strain.md) and [thermodynamic phase](thermodynamic-phase.md) composition evolve. The term describes a material state, not a universal guarantee of a particular toughness.
