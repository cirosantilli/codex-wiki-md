# Optical power meter

↑ **Parent:** [Optical instrument](optical-instrument-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optical_power_meter)

An [optical power meter](optical-power-meter.md) measures collected [radiant flux](radiant-flux.md) using a calibrated detector and readout. With a [photodiode](photodiode.md), its conversion to [watts](watt.md) depends on [spectral responsivity](spectral-responsivity.md), as in [calibrated radiant-flux measurement](calibrated-radiant-flux-measurement.md).
