# Martingale decomposition of a density-dependent jump process

↑ **Parent:** [Density-dependent Markov jump process](density-dependent-markov-jump-process.md)

With jumps $\ell/N$ at rates $N\beta_\ell(z)$, compensate each jump count by its integrated rate. The drift is $F(z)=\sum_\ell\ell\beta_\ell(z)$ and the [predictable quadratic variation](predictable-quadratic-variation.md) matrix of the [martingale](martingale-split.md) term is $N^{-1}\int_0^t\sum_\ell\ell\ell^{\mathsf T}\beta_\ell(Z_s^N)ds$. On a compact region with bounded rates this bracket is of order $1/N$. A Lipschitz drift and the [Gronwall inequality](gronwall-inequality.md) transfer martingale concentration to the [fluid limit](fluid-limit.md).
