# Projective-space quotient as a Thom space of conjugate tautological lines

↑ **Parent:** [Thom space](thom-space.md)

Decompose the ambient vector space as $V\oplus W$, of dimensions $n+1,m+1$. A projective line outside $\mathbb P(V)$ is the graph of a unique linear map from a line in $W$ to $V$. Thus the complement is the total space of $\gamma^*\otimes V$, which is complex-isomorphic to $\bar\gamma^{\oplus(n+1)}$ after choosing a [Hermitian metric](hermitian-metric-on-a-holomorphic-vector-bundle.md). Collapsing $\mathbb P(V)$ gives its [one-point compactification](alexandroff-extension.md), hence the [Thom space](thom-space.md) because the base is compact.
