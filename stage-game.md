# Stage game

↑ **Parent:** [Repeated game](repeated-game.md)

The [normal-form game](normal-form-game.md) played once in each round of a [repeated game](repeated-game.md). Its actions and [payoffs](payoff.md) are distinct from the repeated interaction's history-dependent [pure strategies](pure-strategy.md) and aggregate [payoffs](payoff.md). The same [stage game](stage-game.md) can produce different incentive constraints under a finite sum, discounting or [long-run average payoff](long-run-average-payoff.md) criterion.
