# Constant term of a nonholomorphic Eisenstein series

↑ **Parent:** [Nonholomorphic Eisenstein series](nonholomorphic-eisenstein-series.md)

For the series over all nonzero integer pairs, the constant [Fourier coefficient](fourier-coefficient.md) is $2\zeta(2s)y^s+2\sqrt\pi\,\Gamma(s-1/2)\zeta(2s-1)y^{1-s}/\Gamma(s)$. Integrating over one real period unfolds the translated intervals and then uses the [Gaussian integral](gaussian-integral.md). Multiplication by $\pi^{-s}\Gamma(s)$ expresses it as $2\Lambda(2s)y^s+2\Lambda(2s-1)y^{1-s}$ in terms of the [completed Riemann zeta function](completed-riemann-zeta-function.md).
