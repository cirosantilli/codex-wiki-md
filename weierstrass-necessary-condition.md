# Weierstrass necessary condition

↑ **Parent:** [Weierstrass excess function](weierstrass-excess-function.md)

At a [strong local minimizer of a variational integral](strong-local-minimizer-of-a-variational-integral.md), almost every reference slope $u'(x)$ is a [point of convexity](point-of-convexity.md) of $v\mapsto f(x,u(x),v)$. Equivalently its [Weierstrass excess function](weierstrass-excess-function.md) is nonnegative for every trial slope. Uniformly small variations can have large derivatives, which is why this condition tests all trial slopes rather than only nearby ones. It is a necessary condition along the minimizing curve; the [strengthened Weierstrass condition](strengthened-weierstrass-condition.md) additionally controls a neighborhood of that curve and its slopes.
