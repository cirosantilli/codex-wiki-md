# Sobolev algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Sobolev space](sobolev-space-split.md)

On a sufficiently regular domain in $\mathbb R^n$, $H^s$ is a Banach algebra when $s>n/2$:

$$
\|uv\|_{H^s}\leq C\|u\|_{H^s}\|v\|_{H^s}.
$$
