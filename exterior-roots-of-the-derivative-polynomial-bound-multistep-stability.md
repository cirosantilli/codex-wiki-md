# Exterior roots of the derivative polynomial bound multistep stability

↑ **Parent:** [Amplification polynomial of a multistep method](amplification-polynomial-of-a-multistep-method.md)

If the derivative [characteristic polynomial](characteristic-polynomial.md) $\sigma$ of a [linear multistep method](linear-multistep-method.md) has a simple root outside the [unit disk](unit-disk.md) and keeps its degree in the limit, then its [absolute stability](linear-stability-domain.md) region is bounded. Otherwise a stable sequence with $|z|\to\infty$ would give amplification polynomials $\sigma-\rho/z$ tending coefficientwise to $\sigma$. A root near the exterior root would persist by continuity, contradicting the [root condition for a multistep method](root-condition-for-a-multistep-method.md). This argument excludes stability at every sufficiently large complex $z$, not only along the negative real axis.
