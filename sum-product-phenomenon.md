# Sum-product phenomenon

↑ **Parent:** [Additive combinatorics](additive-combinatorics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sum-product_phenomenon)

The sum-product phenomenon says that a finite subset of a [field](field.md) cannot usually have both a small [sumset](sumset.md) and a small product set unless it has additional subfield-like structure.
