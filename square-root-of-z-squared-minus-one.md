# Square root of z squared minus one

↑ **Parent:** [Holomorphic square root](holomorphic-square-root.md)

The [branch points](branch-point.md) are $\pm1$; infinity has two unbranched meromorphic choices. On the [complex plane](complex-plane.md) slit along $[-1,1]$, normalization $f(z)\sim z$ at infinity selects a [holomorphic square root](holomorphic-square-root.md) with boundary values $f(x+i0)=i\sqrt{1-x^2}$ and $f(x-i0)=-i\sqrt{1-x^2}$ for $-1<x<1$. On the exterior real intervals, $f(x)=\operatorname{sgn}(x)\sqrt{x^2-1}$. A counterclockwise [contour integral](contour-integral.md) around the finite [branch cut](branch-cut.md) satisfies $\oint d\zeta/f(\zeta)=2\pi i$: the upper lip runs right to left, the lower lip left to right, and the endpoint arcs contribute $O(\sqrt\varepsilon)$. The opposite global branch reverses these signs.
