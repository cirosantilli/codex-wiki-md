# Superset

↑ **Parent:** [Subset](subset.md)

A [set](set-split.md) $B$ is a [superset](superset.md) of $A$ when $A$ is a [subset](subset.md) of $B$, meaning that every element of $A$ belongs to $B$. Equality is permitted; a proper [superset](superset.md) additionally differs from $A$.
