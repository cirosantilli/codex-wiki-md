# Fixed-charge response to added divalent chloride

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson-Boltzmann equation](poisson-boltzmann-equation.md)

For an ideal planar [electric double layer](electric-double-layer.md) at fixed [surface charge density](surface-charge-density.md), let $v=e\phi_0/(k_BT)$. Adding bulk divalent chloride concentration $\delta c$ adds two monovalent coions per counterion. Differentiating the squared [Grahame equation](grahame-equation.md) gives the displayed expression, with all denominator concentrations evaluated at the surface. The numerator concentrations are bulk increments. Dropping depleted coions at strongly negative potential gives the leading counterion approximation. A large finite increment instead requires solving the fixed-charge equation at the new composition; exponentiating a first-order voltage estimate does not make that estimate exact.
