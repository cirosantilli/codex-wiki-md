# Lifting residue-field embeddings of unramified extensions

↑ **Parent:** [Unramified extension](unramified-extension.md)

For an [unramified extension](unramified-extension.md) $L/K$ of non-Archimedean [local fields](local-field.md) and any finite extension $M/K$, reduction is a bijection between $K$-[field homomorphisms](field-homomorphism.md) $L\to M$ and $k_K$-[field homomorphisms](field-homomorphism.md) $k_L\to k_M$. Choose an integral generator with separable irreducible reduction. Each specified residue image lifts uniquely to a root in $M$ by [Hensel lemma](hensel-s-lemma.md). Conversely a $K$-embedding preserves the unique extended [valuation](valuation.md) and therefore induces the residue embedding. The same argument applies when $M$ is any non-Archimedean local extension with the given compatible embedding of $K$.
