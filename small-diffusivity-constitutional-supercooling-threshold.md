# Small-diffusivity constitutional-supercooling threshold

↑ **Parent:** [Constitutional supercooling](constitutional-supercooling.md)

For a [saline Stefan problem](saline-stefan-problem.md) with common thermal properties, $\epsilon=\sqrt{D/\kappa}\ll1$, and fixed [latent-to-sensible heat ratio](latent-to-sensible-heat-ratio.md), set $T_0=-mC_0$, $\theta_-=T_0-T_{-\infty}$ and $\theta_+=T_\infty-T_0>0$. A finite-$\lambda$ [similarity solution](similarity-solution.md) has $T_i=(T_\infty+T_{-\infty})/2+O(\epsilon)$. At the onset of [constitutional supercooling](constitutional-supercooling.md), $\lambda=O(\epsilon)$ and the error in this mean is $O(\epsilon^2)$. The [salt-rejection function for a saline Stefan front](salt-rejection-function-for-a-saline-stefan-front.md) then gives $m\lambda C_i=(\theta_--\theta_+)/(2\sqrt\pi)+O(\epsilon^2)$, whereas $\sqrt{Dt}\,T_{l,x}(a^+)=\epsilon\theta_+/\sqrt\pi+O(\epsilon^2)$. Comparing the actual [temperature gradient](temperature-gradient.md) to the [liquidus](liquidus.md) [gradient](gradient.md) gives the displayed onset, with supercooling on the side of greater $\theta_-$. For a freezing front at $a\ge0$ and $D<\kappa$, the ratio of these two [gradients](gradient.md) decreases into the liquid, so their interface comparison also decides whether a supercooled interval occurs anywhere ahead. The formula is an asymptotic criterion; finite-$\epsilon$ onset requires the full [Stefan condition](stefan-condition.md) and salt balance.
