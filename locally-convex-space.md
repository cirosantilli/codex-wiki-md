# Locally convex space

↑ **Parent:** [Topological vector space](topological-vector-space-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Locally_convex_space)

A locally convex space is a [topological vector space](topological-vector-space-split.md) with a [neighbourhood basis](neighbourhood-basis.md) at zero consisting of [convex sets](convex-set-split.md). Equivalently, its [topology](topology-split.md) is a [seminorm topology](seminorm-topology.md) generated by some family $\mathcal P$ of [seminorms](seminorm.md). We allow non-[Hausdorff](hausdorff-space.md) spaces here; a [separating family of seminorms](separating-family-of-seminorms.md) gives precisely the [Hausdorff](hausdorff-space.md) case. Every [normed vector space](normed-vector-space-split.md) is locally convex, but [smooth convergence](smooth-convergence-on-a-compact-interval.md) generally requires a whole family of measurements.

The geometric and measurement descriptions fit together: [seminorm](seminorm.md) sublevel sets are [convex](convex-function.md), while the [Minkowski functional](minkowski-functional.md) of an open, convex zero [neighbourhood](neighbourhood-mathematics.md) invariant under all [scalar multiplications](scalar-multiplication.md) with $|\lambda|\leq1$ is a [continuous](continuous-function.md) [seminorm](seminorm.md). Such [neighbourhoods](neighbourhood-mathematics.md) can be chosen as a [neighbourhood basis](neighbourhood-basis.md). [Peter Philip, Functional Analysis, Theorem 1.40, equivalence of local convexity and seminorm topologies](https://www.math.lmu.de/~philip/publications/lectureNotes/philipPeter_FunctionalAnalysis.pdf).

Local convexity makes [Hahn-Banach theorem](hahn-banach-theorem.md) and [separation of a point and an open convex set](separation-of-a-point-and-an-open-convex-set.md) available. It turns geometric distinctions into [continuous linear functionals](continuous-linear-functional.md), connecting [convex sets](convex-set-split.md) to the [continuous dual space](continuous-dual-space-split.md). For a [seminorm topology](seminorm-topology.md) generated by $\mathcal P$, a [linear functional](linear-functional.md) $\ell$ is [continuous](continuous-function.md) exactly when there are $p_1,\ldots,p_m\in\mathcal P$ and $C>0$ with

$$
|\ell(x)|\leq C\max_{1\leq i\leq m}p_i(x)\qquad(x\in E).
$$

Thus one [continuous linear functional](continuous-linear-functional.md) needs only finitely many of the available measurements. For example, [point evaluation](point-evaluation-functional.md) of a [derivative](derivative.md) on the [space of smooth functions on a compact interval](space-of-smooth-functions-on-a-compact-interval.md) is controlled by a suitable [derivative seminorm](derivative-seminorms-on-a-compact-interval.md).
