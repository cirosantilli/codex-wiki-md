# S-to-P conversion

↑ **Parent:** [Mode conversion at a planar elastic interface](mode-conversion-at-a-planar-elastic-interface.md)

Conversion of an incident [S wave](s-wave.md) into a [P wave](p-wave.md) at a material contrast. It provides a complementary [receiver function](receiver-function.md) constraint because its travel-time ordering and interference with reverberations differ from [P-to-S conversion](p-to-s-conversion.md).
