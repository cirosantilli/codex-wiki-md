<h1 id="su-3-lie-algebra">SU(3) Lie algebra</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Unitary Lie algebra](unitary-lie-algebra.md)

The compact real [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) $\mathfrak{su}(3)$ consists of traceless anti-Hermitian $3\times3$ matrices. Physicists often specify Hermitian generators $t_a$ instead, so the genuine [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) elements are $it_a$. The compact real algebra and its complexification $\mathfrak{sl}_3(\mathbb C)$ should be distinguished.
