# Global spin-rotation symmetry about one axis

↑ **Parent:** [Spin rotation](spin-rotation.md)

If the [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) commutes with total $S^z$, simultaneous spin rotations $e^{-i\phi S^z_{\mathrm{tot}}}$ form a [circle group](circle-group.md) [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md). In a state of definite total $S^z$, the transverse one-point averages vanish because $S^x\pm iS^y$ change that eigenvalue. A chosen classical orientation or symmetry-breaking limit requires separate care when constructing a [linear spin-wave approximation](linear-spin-wave-approximation.md).
