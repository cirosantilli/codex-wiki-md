# Homotopy excision theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Relative homotopy group](relative-homotopy-group.md)

For a based CW triad with connected intersection $A$, if $(B,A)$ is $p$-connected and $(C,A)$ is $q$-connected, $p,q\ge1$, the comparison map is an [isomorphism](isomorphism.md) for $i<p+q$ and a surjection for $i=p+q$. This connectivity form of the Blakers-Massey theorem controls the failure of ordinary relative [homotopy](homotopy.md) to satisfy unrestricted excision.
