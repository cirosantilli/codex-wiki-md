# One-photon spin-zero transition prohibition

↑ **Parent:** [Spin-zero two-body helicity selection](spin-zero-two-body-helicity-selection.md)

A spin-zero particle cannot decay into a spin-zero particle and one on-shell real [photon](photon.md). In the parent rest frame the two final [helicities](helicity.md) must be equal for total [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) zero, whereas the [photon](photon.md) has [helicity](helicity.md) $\pm1$ and the scalar has [helicity](helicity.md) zero. The prohibition is rotational and independent of charge-conjugation conservation. An off-shell [photon](photon.md) producing additional particles is a different process.
