# Fitting ideal

↑ **Parent:** [Finitely presented module](finitely-presented-module.md)

For a [module](module-mathematics.md) over a commutative ring presented by a [matrix](matrix.md) with n generators, its zeroth Fitting ideal is generated by all n-by-n minors of the relation [matrix](matrix.md). It is independent of the chosen finite presentation. Over a [unique factorization domain](unique-factorization-domain.md), the greatest common divisor of these minors defines the [module](module-mathematics.md) order up to a unit. This does not require a Smith normal form over the ring. The square [Seifert-matrix presentation of the Alexander module](seifert-matrix-presentation-of-the-alexander-module.md) makes the order its determinant.
