# Steady rotating-frame Bernoulli integral

↑ **Parent:** [Bernoulli equation](bernoulli-equation.md)

For [steady flow](steady-flow.md) of constant-density fluid in a uniformly rotating frame with conservative body forces, the displayed quantity is constant along each relative [streamline](streamline.md). The [centrifugal potential](centrifugal-potential.md) contributes the negative quadratic term, and the [Coriolis force](coriolis-force.md) contributes no work because it is perpendicular to the relative velocity. The result follows by dotting the rotating [Euler equations for an inviscid fluid](euler-equations-for-an-inviscid-fluid.md) with that velocity.
