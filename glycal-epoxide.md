# Glycal epoxide

↑ **Parent:** [Epoxide](epoxide.md)

A 1,2-anhydro sugar derivative obtained by epoxidizing a glycal. Its anomeric carbon can undergo direct nucleophilic opening or ionization assisted by the ring oxygen.
