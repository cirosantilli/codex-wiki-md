# Presentation of a localization at one element

↑ **Parent:** [Localization of a ring](localization-of-a-ring.md)

The [localization of a ring](localization-of-a-ring.md) $A$ at the powers of $f$ is generated over $A$ by $1/f$. The presentation $A_f\cong A[t]/(ft-1)$ follows from the [universal property of localization](universal-property-of-localization.md): both sides universally receive a [ring homomorphism](ring-homomorphism.md) from $A$ making $f$ invertible. This proof works with [zero divisors](zero-divisor.md) and includes the [zero ring](zero-ring.md) when $f$ is [nilpotent](nilpotent.md). In particular, a [principal open subscheme](principal-open-subscheme.md) of an [affine scheme](affine-scheme.md) has an inclusion which is a [morphism of finite type](morphism-of-finite-type.md).
