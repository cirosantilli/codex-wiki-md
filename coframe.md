# Coframe

↑ **Parent:** [Frame of a vector bundle](frame-of-a-vector-bundle.md)

A coframe dual to a local frame of the [tangent bundle](tangent-bundle.md) is the corresponding local frame of the [cotangent bundle](cotangent-bundle.md). Its elements are [differential one-forms](one-form.md). Applying the coframe to covariant derivatives of the frame extracts the [connection one-forms](connection-one-form.md); applying it to the [torsion tensor](torsion-tensor.md) extracts the [torsion forms](torsion-form.md).
