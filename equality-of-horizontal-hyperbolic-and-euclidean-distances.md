# Equality of horizontal hyperbolic and Euclidean distances

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic distance in the Poincare half-plane](hyperbolic-distance-in-the-poincare-half-plane.md)

For points $ib$ and $a+ib$ with $a,b>0$, the [hyperbolic distance](hyperbolic-distance.md) is $2\operatorname{arsinh}(a/(2b))$. It equals their Euclidean distance $a$ exactly when the displayed relation holds. The function of $a$ decreases continuously from one to zero: its derivative has the sign of $\sinh(a/2)-(a/2)\cosh(a/2)<0$. Hence every height $b\in(0,1)$ has exactly one positive matching horizontal separation.
