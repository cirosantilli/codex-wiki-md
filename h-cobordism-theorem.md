# H-cobordism theorem

↑ **Parent:** [H-cobordism](h-cobordism.md)

A compact smooth [h-cobordism](h-cobordism.md) of dimension at least six between connected simply connected closed [manifolds](topological-manifold.md) is [diffeomorphic](diffeomorphism.md) to a product, relative to its incoming boundary. The boundary [manifolds](topological-manifold.md) consequently are [diffeomorphic](diffeomorphism.md). The dimension and simple-connectivity hypotheses are sufficient conditions, not necessary properties of a product cylinder. The two boundary inclusions being [homotopy equivalences](homotopy-equivalence.md) are necessary for a [trivial cobordism](trivial-cobordism.md).
