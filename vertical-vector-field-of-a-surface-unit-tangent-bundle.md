# Vertical vector field of a surface unit tangent bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical coframe of a surface unit tangent bundle](canonical-coframe-of-a-surface-unit-tangent-bundle.md)

On an oriented [Riemannian surface](riemannian-surface.md), the vertical vector field generates positive rotation in each fibre of its [unit tangent bundle](unit-tangent-bundle.md): $Vf(x,v)=\left.\partial_t f(x,\cos t\,v+\sin t\,iv)\right|_{t=0}$. Hence $V(v)=iv$ and $V(iv)=-v$. In the canonical frame, $[V,X]=H$, $[V,H]=-X$ and $[X,H]=KV$.
