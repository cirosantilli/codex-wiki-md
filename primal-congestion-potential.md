# Primal congestion potential

↑ **Parent:** [Congestion control](congestion-control.md)

This potential subtracts integrated resource prices from [weighted logarithmic utility](weighted-logarithmic-utility.md). Continuous nonnegative nondecreasing prices make it strictly concave on positive rates. If every route meets a resource with a nonzero price function, its superlevel sets are compact and its maximizer is unique. The rate adjustment $\dot x_r=\kappa_rx_r\partial_rU$ increases it strictly away from equilibrium.
