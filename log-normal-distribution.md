# Log-normal distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Normal distribution](normal-distribution.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Log-normal_distribution)

A positive random variable is log-normal when its logarithm has a [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md). If $Z\sim N(0,1)$ and $X=e^Z$, then $\mathbb E[X^n]=e^{n^2/2}$.
