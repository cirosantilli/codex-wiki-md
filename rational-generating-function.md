# Rational generating function

↑ **Parent:** [Generating function](generating-function.md)

A rational generating function is a [formal power series](formal-power-series.md) equal to a quotient of two [polynomials](polynomial-split.md), with denominator nonzero at the origin. Multiplying by the denominator shows that its coefficients eventually satisfy a [linear recurrence](linear-recurrence-relation.md) with constant coefficients.
