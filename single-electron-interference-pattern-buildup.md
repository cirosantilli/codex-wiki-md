# Single-electron interference pattern buildup

↑ **Parent:** [Double-slit experiment](double-slit-experiment.md)

A sequence of individual localized [electron](electron.md) detections can accumulate a two-path [interference](interference-wave-propagation.md) distribution when the apparatus maintains coherence. The 1989 experiment led by [Akira Tonomura](akira-tonomura.md) used a field-emission source, an [electron](electron.md) biprism, [electron](electron.md) lenses and a position-sensitive [electron](electron.md) counter. It realized two coherent paths without literal machined double slits. Low occupation of the instrument excludes interactions between different [electrons](electron.md) as the origin of the fringes.[section III and conclusion](demonstration-of-single-electron-buildup-of-an-interference-pattern-by-akira-tonomura-and-others.md)
