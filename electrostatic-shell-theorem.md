# Electrostatic shell theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Gauss's law](gauss-s-law.md)

Outside a bounded [spherically symmetric charge distribution](spherically-symmetric-charge-distribution.md) in vacuum, the [electric field](electric-field-split.md) is that of a central [point charge](point-charge.md) with the same total [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md). With zero [electric potential](electric-potential.md) at infinity, the exterior [electric potential](electric-potential.md) is $Q/(4\pi\epsilon_0r)$. A uniform [surface charge](surface-charge.md) on a [sphere](sphere.md) has zero [electric field](electric-field-split.md) inside.
