# Symmetric positive-definite matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md)

A real [symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md) is positive definite when its [quadratic form](quadratic-form-split.md) is positive on every nonzero vector. By the [spectral theorem](spectral-theorem.md), this is equivalent to all its [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) being positive. It has a unique symmetric positive-definite square root.
