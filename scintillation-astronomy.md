# Scintillation (astronomy)

↑ **Parent:** [Atmospheric turbulence](atmospheric-turbulence.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scintillation_(astronomy))

Atmospheric scintillation is the fluctuation of received stellar intensity produced by propagation through [atmospheric turbulence](atmospheric-turbulence.md). A phase-only [deformable mirror](deformable-mirror.md) does not generally remove these amplitude fluctuations.
