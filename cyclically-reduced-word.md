# Cyclically reduced word

↑ **Parent:** [Freely reduced word](freely-reduced-word.md)

A [freely reduced word](freely-reduced-word.md) is cyclically reduced if its first and last letters are not inverse, with the empty word also allowed. Every cyclic permutation of a nonempty [cyclically reduced word](cyclically-reduced-word.md) is freely reduced, represents a conjugate, and has the same length. Positive powers are freely reduced because no cancellation occurs at copy boundaries.
