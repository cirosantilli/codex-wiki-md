# Uniqueness of the Neumann problem

↑ **Parent:** [Neumann boundary-value problem](neumann-boundary-value-problem.md)

On a connected bounded domain, two harmonic potentials with the same normal [derivative](derivative.md) differ by a constant. Their difference $u$ has $\nabla^2u=0$ and $\partial_nu=0$. [Green's first identity](green-s-first-identity.md) gives $\int|\nabla u|^2dV=\int_{\partial\Omega}u\partial_nu\,dA=0$, so the [gradient](gradient.md) vanishes. In a multiply connected magnetic core, also fix the [circulation](circulation-physics.md) around each independent hole: a curl-free [magnetic field intensity](magnetic-field-intensity.md) with nonzero [circulation](circulation-physics.md) need not be the [gradient](gradient.md) of a single-valued scalar potential. After those [circulations](circulation-physics.md) have been matched, apply the scalar-potential uniqueness argument to the difference field.
