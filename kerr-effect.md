# Kerr effect

↑ **Parent:** [Nonlinear optics](nonlinear-optics.md)

The [Kerr effect](kerr-effect.md) is a change in a material's [refractive index](refractive-index.md) quadratic in an applied [electric field](electric-field-split.md). A static applied [electric field](electric-field-split.md) can produce [linear birefringence](linear-birefringence.md); the optical field itself can also produce an intensity-dependent [refractive index](refractive-index.md) in [nonlinear optics](nonlinear-optics.md).
