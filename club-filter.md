# Club filter

↑ **Parent:** [Club set](club-set.md)

On a regular uncountable [cardinal number](cardinal-number.md) $\kappa$, the club filter consists of all subsets containing a [club set](club-set.md). It is a [kappa-complete filter](kappa-complete-filter.md) by [club filter completeness](club-filter-completeness.md). Its members are [stationary sets](stationary-set.md), although a [stationary set](stationary-set.md) need not be a filter member, and a filter member need not itself be closed.
