# Sprague-Grundy theory

↑ **Parent:** [Impartial game](impartial-game.md)

Sprague-Grundy theory studies finite acyclic [impartial games](impartial-game.md) under the [normal-play convention](normal-play-convention.md) by assigning each position a [Grundy number](grundy-number.md). The assignment recursively takes the [minimum excludant](minimum-excludant.md) of the values of all legal followers. The [Sprague-Grundy theorem](sprague-grundy-theorem.md) then reduces a [disjunctive sum of games](disjunctive-sum-of-games.md) to the [bitwise exclusive or](exclusive-or.md) of its component values. This framework explains both why a zero value is losing and how independently understood games can be combined without exploring the entire product of their state spaces.
