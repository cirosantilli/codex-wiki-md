# Mass resolution

↑ **Parent:** [Mass spectrometry](mass-spectrometry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mass_resolution)

[Mass resolving power](mass-resolution.md) compares a peak coordinate $x$ with a stated width or separation criterion $\Delta x$. With $\Delta x$ taken as [full width at half maximum](full-width-at-half-maximum.md), a [Gaussian function](gaussian-function.md) of [standard deviation](standard-deviation.md) $\sigma$ has

$$
\Delta x=2\sqrt{2\log2}\,\sigma,\qquad R=\frac{x}{\Delta x}.
$$

At the [neon-20](neon-20.md) coordinate in the synthetic [mass spectrum](mass-spectrum.md), $\sigma=0.04$ gives $\Delta x\approx0.0942$ and $R\approx212$, while $\sigma=0.70$ gives $\Delta x\approx1.648$ and $R\approx12.1$. The width criterion and relative peak areas both matter when judging whether a small neighboring [isotope](isotope.md) can be distinguished. [Mass resolution](mass-resolution.md) also differs from the accuracy of a calibrated peak position.
