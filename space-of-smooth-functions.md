# Space of smooth functions

↑ **Parent:** [Distribution theory](distribution-theory-split.md)

For an [open set](open-set.md) $X\subseteq\mathbb R^n$, the space $\mathcal E(X)=C^\infty(X)$ carries the [Fréchet topology](frechet-space.md) in which $f_j\to f$ exactly when every derivative converges uniformly on every [compact set](compact-space.md) contained in $X$.
