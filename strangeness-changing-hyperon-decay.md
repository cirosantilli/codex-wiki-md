# Strangeness-changing hyperon decay

↑ **Parent:** [Strangeness](strangeness.md)

Strong and [electromagnetic interactions](electromagnetic-interaction.md) conserve net [strangeness](strangeness.md); a decay changing it requires the [weak interaction](weak-interaction.md). A strange [baryon](baryon.md) decay into a less-strange [baryon](baryon.md) and a [pion](pion.md) is an example. The [weak interaction](weak-interaction.md) does not impose a [parity](parity.md) [selection rule](selection-rule.md), so a final orbital [partial wave](partial-wave.md) cannot by itself determine the parent's [parity](parity.md). Qualitative lifetime classes do not provide an exact lifetime without an [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) calculation or measurement.
