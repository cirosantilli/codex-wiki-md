# Marginally outer trapped surface

↑ **Parent:** [Trapped surface](trapped-surface.md)

A closed spacelike two-surface is marginally outer trapped when its future outgoing [null expansion](null-expansion.md) is zero. For the future black-hole type, the ingoing [null expansion](null-expansion.md) is negative. An [apparent horizon](apparent-horizon.md) on a chosen spatial slice is ordinarily its outermost such surface under suitable regularity assumptions. The notion depends on the slice and is not the global definition of an [event horizon](event-horizon.md).
