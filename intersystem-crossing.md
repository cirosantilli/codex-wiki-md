# Intersystem crossing

↑ **Parent:** [Excited state](excited-state.md)

A nonradiative transition between electronic states of different [spin multiplicity](spin-multiplicity.md), enabled by interactions such as spin–orbit coupling. A constant intersystem-crossing channel contributes to the unquenched excited-state lifetime.
