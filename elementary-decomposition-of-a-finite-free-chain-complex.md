# Elementary decomposition of a finite free chain complex

↑ **Parent:** [Chain complex](chain-complex.md)

Over the principal ideal domain $\mathbb Z$, [Smith normal form](smith-normal-form.md) decomposes a chain complex of finitely generated free modules into direct sums of one-term complexes $\mathbb Z$ and two-term complexes

$$
0\longrightarrow\mathbb Z\xrightarrow{m}\mathbb Z\longrightarrow0.
$$

The one-term summands record free homology, the summands with $m>1$ record cyclic torsion, and those with $m=1$ are acyclic.
