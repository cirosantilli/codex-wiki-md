# DNA hairpin

↑ **Parent:** [Stem-loop](stem-loop.md)

A [DNA hairpin](dna-hairpin.md) is a [stem-loop](stem-loop.md) formed within one DNA strand. Complementary arms can pair while intervening bases form a loop; bases outside the paired arms remain overhangs.
