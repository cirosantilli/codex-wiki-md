# Mod-two homology of a submanifold complement

↑ **Parent:** [Alexander duality](alexander-duality.md)

For a closed connected $k$-manifold smoothly embedded in $S^n$, with $k<n$ and $n\geq1$, its complement has

$$
\widetilde H_i(S^n\setminus M;\mathbb F_2)\cong H_{i+k+1-n}(M;\mathbb F_2)\quad(0\leq i\leq n-2),
$$

and zero reduced homology for $i\geq n-1$, where negative-index groups are zero. The [Thom isomorphism theorem](thom-isomorphism-theorem.md) for the normal bundle and the pair's exact sequence give this formula. The top ambient [fundamental class](fundamental-class.md) maps isomorphically to the fundamental class of $M$, canceling the exceptional top term.
