# CSP semantic model

↑ **Parent:** [Communicating sequential processes](communicating-sequential-processes.md)

A representation of a [CSP process](csp-process.md) by specified kinds of observable behaviour. The [CSP traces model](csp-traces-model.md), [CSP stable-failures model](csp-stable-failures-model.md) and [CSP failures-divergences model](csp-failures-divergences-model.md) record progressively more information about refusals and internal activity.
