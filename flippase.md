# Flippase

↑ **Parent:** [Membrane transport protein](membrane-transport-protein.md)

A [flippase](flippase.md) catalyzes directional movement of selected [lipids](lipid.md) between [membrane leaflets](membrane-leaflet.md). Such activity can establish or maintain lipid asymmetry, subject to specificity and energy coupling.
