# C2 field

↑ **Parent:** [Ci field](ci-field.md)

A $C_2$ field is a [Ci field](ci-field.md) with the bound $N>d^2$. Function fields of curves over [finite fields](finite-field.md) are examples. The extra-variable construction in [surjectivity of reduced norms over C2 fields](surjectivity-of-reduced-norms-over-c2-fields.md) makes this polynomial-solvability property useful for [division algebras](division-algebra.md).
