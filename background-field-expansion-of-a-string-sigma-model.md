# Background field expansion of a string sigma model

↑ **Parent:** [String nonlinear sigma model](string-nonlinear-sigma-model.md)

Expand the [string embedding map](string-embedding-map.md) along target geodesics as $X=\exp_{\bar X}Y$. The quadratic Euclidean fluctuation action has a [covariant derivative](covariant-derivative.md) term and a curvature term $-R_{acbd}Y^aY^b\partial\bar X^c\partial\bar X^d$. The logarithmically divergent coincident contraction is proportional to $\alpha'g^{ab}$; its curvature contraction is the [Ricci tensor](ricci-tensor.md). A compensating metric [counterterm](counterterm.md) gives $\beta^g_{ab}=\alpha'R_{ab}+O(\alpha'^2)$. This is why the target gravitational equation arises from quantum [worldsheet Weyl anomaly](worldsheet-weyl-anomaly.md) cancellation, rather than from the classical worldsheet metric variation. Infrared regulation and removal of equation-of-motion terms by field redefinitions specify the usual one-loop scheme.
