# Lq inclusion implies finite measure on a Euclidean Borel subset

↑ **Parent:** [Lp inclusion on a finite measure space](lp-inclusion-on-a-finite-measure-space.md)

Let $X\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ be Borel. If $L^q(X)\subseteq L^p(X)$ for $1\leq p<q\leq\infty$, the [closed graph theorem](closed-graph-theorem.md) makes the inclusion continuous. Applying its norm bound to indicators of $X\cap B(0,R)$ shows that their measures are uniformly bounded, hence $\mu(X)<\infty$.
