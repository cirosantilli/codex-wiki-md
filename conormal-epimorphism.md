# Conormal epimorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Pointed category](pointed-category.md)

A conormal epimorphism is an [epimorphism](epimorphism.md) that is the [cokernel in a category](cokernel-in-a-category.md) of some morphism. It is the concept dual to a [normal monomorphism](normal-monomorphism.md).
