# Quarter-plane oblique boundary spectral functions

↑ **Parent:** [Rational-angle oblique derivative problem on a quadrant](rational-angle-oblique-derivative-problem-on-a-quadrant.md)

For real [harmonic](harmonic-function.md) $q$, prescribe $-\sin\beta_1q_x+\cos\beta_1q_y=h_1$ on the vertical axis and $\cos\beta_2q_x-\sin\beta_2q_y=h_2$ on the horizontal axis. Their orthogonal derivatives $j_1,j_2$ determine the missing gradient components. If $H_1,J_1$ use the kernel $e^{ky}$ and $H_2,J_2$ use $e^{-ikx}$, the clockwise spectra $\widehat q_1=\int_0^{i\infty}e^{-ikz}q_z\,dz$, $\widehat q_2=-\int_0^\infty e^{-ikz}q_z\,dz$ have the displayed expressions. Their [global relation](global-relation-for-a-linear-boundary-value-problem.md) is $\widehat q_1+\widehat q_2=0$ in the third spectral quadrant.
