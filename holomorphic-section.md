# Holomorphic section

↑ **Parent:** [Holomorphic vector bundle](holomorphic-vector-bundle.md)

A section of a [holomorphic vector bundle](holomorphic-vector-bundle.md) is holomorphic when its coordinate functions in every [holomorphic local trivialization](holomorphic-local-trivialization.md) are holomorphic.

It is a [section of a fiber bundle](section-fiber-bundle.md) satisfying holomorphicity.
