# Primitive element of a Hopf algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Hopf algebra](hopf-algebra.md)

An element $x$ of positive degree is primitive when $\Delta x=x\otimes1+1\otimes x$. Graded [commutators](commutator.md) of primitives are primitive, so they form a [graded Lie algebra](graded-lie-algebra.md). In a [universal enveloping algebra](universal-enveloping-algebra.md) over $\mathbb Q$, the [primitive elements of a Hopf algebra](primitive-element-of-a-hopf-algebra.md) are exactly its given [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md): the associated graded algebra of the word-length filtration is symmetric, where no nonzero homogeneous polynomial of length greater than one is additive under the diagonal.
