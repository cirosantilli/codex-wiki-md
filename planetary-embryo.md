# Planetary embryo

↑ **Parent:** [Planet formation](planet-formation.md)

A growing solid protoplanet dynamically important compared with the surrounding smaller [planetesimals](planetesimal.md). The term identifies a formation stage, rather than a single sharply specified [mass](mass.md).
