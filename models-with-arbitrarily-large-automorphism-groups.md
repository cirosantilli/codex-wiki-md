# Models with arbitrarily large automorphism groups

↑ **Parent:** [Ehrenfeucht-Mostowski theorem](ehrenfeucht-mostowski-theorem.md)

Every first-order theory with an infinite model has models whose [automorphism groups](automorphism-group-of-a-first-order-structure.md) have arbitrarily large cardinality. Choose a total order with many order automorphisms and apply the [Ehrenfeucht-Mostowski theorem](ehrenfeucht-mostowski-theorem.md).
