# Cut vertex

↑ **Parent:** [Graph theory](graph-theory-split.md)

A vertex whose deletion increases the number of connected components of a [graph](graph-split.md). A [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) graph has no cut vertex: deleting a vertex from a [Hamilton cycle](hamilton-cycle.md) leaves a spanning path.
