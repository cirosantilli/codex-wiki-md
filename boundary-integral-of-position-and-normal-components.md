# Boundary integral of position and normal components

↑ **Parent:** [Divergence theorem](divergence-theorem.md)

For a bounded sufficiently regular region, the [divergence theorem](divergence-theorem.md) applied to the vector field $x_i e_j$ gives the displayed identity. Contracting with constant vectors gives $\int_{\partial\Omega}(b\cdot x)(c\cdot n)\,dS=|\Omega|b\cdot c$. Contracting with the [Kronecker delta](kronecker-delta.md) or the [Levi-Civita symbol](levi-civita-symbol.md) respectively gives $\int x\cdot n\,dS=3|\Omega|$ and $\int x\times n\,dS=0$ in three dimensions.
