# Ammonium nitrate

↑ **Parent:** [Inorganic chemistry](inorganic-chemistry.md)

A salt containing [ammonium](ammonium.md) and [nitrate](nitrate.md). Its [thermal decomposition](thermal-decomposition.md) has condition-dependent pathways; the familiar [nitrous oxide](nitrous-oxide.md) pathway is not universal.
