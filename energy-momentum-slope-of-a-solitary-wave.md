<h1 id="energy-momentum-slope-of-a-solitary-wave">Energy–momentum slope of a solitary wave</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Gross–Pitaevskii solitary wave](gross-pitaevskii-solitary-wave.md)

With $E=\frac12\int[|\nabla\psi|^2+\frac12(1-|\psi|^2)^2]$ and the [renormalized momentum of a condensate](renormalized-momentum-of-a-condensate.md), a [Gross–Pitaevskii solitary wave](gross-pitaevskii-solitary-wave.md) is a critical point of $E-vp_x$. Along a differentiable solution family, fixed bulk normalization and vanishing variation boundary terms give $dE/d\lambda=v\,dp_x/d\lambda$. Therefore $dE/dp_x=v$ wherever $dp_x/d\lambda\ne0$. At a cusp or turning point the parametrized identity is the appropriate statement; one must not assume a globally single-valued dispersion branch.
