# Hydrogen spectral series

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrogen atom](hydrogen-atom.md)

A family of hydrogen emission lines terminating at a fixed lower [principal quantum number](principal-quantum-number.md) $n_f$. In the nonrelativistic [Bohr model](bohr-model.md), their inverse [wavelengths](wavelength.md) are $1/\lambda=R_H(1/n_f^2-1/n_i^2)$ for integers $n_i>n_f$. Holding $n_f$ fixed and increasing $n_i$ gives lines approaching the series limit $1/\lambda=R_H/n_f^2$. [Reduced mass](reduced-mass.md) shifts the Rydberg constant, while additional interactions produce corrections to this gross spectral pattern.
