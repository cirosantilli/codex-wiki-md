# Pebble accretion

↑ **Parent:** [Core accretion](core-accretion.md)

Capture of small drifting solids by a [planetary embryo](planetary-embryo.md), aided by dissipation through gas [drag](drag-physics.md). Its efficiency depends on the [Stokes number](stokes-number.md), particle-layer thickness, embryo [mass](mass.md) and the supply of solids; it is not simply geometric sweeping of every passing particle.
