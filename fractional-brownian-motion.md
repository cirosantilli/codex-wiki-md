# Fractional Brownian motion

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian process](gaussian-process.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fractional_Brownian_motion)

A fractional Brownian motion with [Hurst exponent](hurst-exponent.md) $H\in(0,1)$ is a centered [Gaussian process](gaussian-process.md) with the displayed standard [covariance function](covariance-function.md) and a [continuous modification](continuous-modification.md). Its increment [variance](variance-split.md) is $|s-t|^{2H}$. Some sources omit the factor $1/2$, multiplying the process by $\sqrt2$; the corresponding increment [variance](variance-split.md) is then $2|s-t|^{2H}$. Its [canonical pseudometric of a Gaussian process](canonical-pseudometric-of-a-gaussian-process.md) is proportional to $|s-t|^H$, which gives finite [expected value](expected-value.md) of its absolute [supremum](supremum.md) on compact intervals through the [Dudley entropy integral](dudley-entropy-integral.md).
