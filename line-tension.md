# Line tension

↑ **Parent:** [Lipid bilayer](lipid-bilayer.md)

[Line tension](line-tension.md) is energy per unit length of a boundary, such as the edge between two phases of a [lipid bilayer](lipid-bilayer.md). Positive [line tension](line-tension.md) tends to minimize the perimeter at fixed domain area. Its dimensions and geometry differ from [surface tension](surface-tension.md), which is energy per unit area.
