# Osmometry

↑ **Parent:** [Osmotic pressure](osmotic-pressure.md)

Measure the [pressure](pressure-split.md) difference across a solvent-permeable barrier that retains the solute, at known [temperature](temperature-split.md). For dilute [polymers](polymer-split.md), the zero-concentration intercept of reduced [pressure](pressure-split.md) determines the [number-average molar mass](number-average-molar-mass.md). Leakage, aggregation and ionic contributions can change the inferred molecular population.
