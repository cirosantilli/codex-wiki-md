# Vector area of a polygonal boundary

↑ **Parent:** [Vector area](vector-area.md)

For an oriented closed polygon, triangulate with a common vertex $P$. Its total [vector area](vector-area.md) is $\tfrac12\sum_i(B_i-P)\times(B_{i+1}-P)$; the terms involving $P$ telescope, giving the displayed boundary-only formula. Its signed projected [area](surface-area.md) along a unit vector $V$ is $V\cdot\mathbf A$. The maximum is $|\mathbf A|$, in direction $\mathbf A/|\mathbf A|$ if nonzero, and zero projections have directions perpendicular to $\mathbf A$. If $\mathbf A=0$, every direction has zero signed projection despite possible nonzero unsigned surface area.
