# Second-order differential equation

↑ **Parent:** [Ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md)

A second-order [ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md) involves a second [derivative](derivative.md) as its highest derivative. In explicit form $y''=f(x,y,y')$, introducing $v=y'$ converts it into the first-order system $y'=v$, $v'=f(x,y,v)$. Under local regularity and [Lipschitz continuity](lipschitz-continuity.md) in the dependent variables, an [initial value problem](initial-value-problem.md) is specified by both $y(x_0)$ and $y'(x_0)$. The linear case is a [second-order linear differential equation](second-order-linear-differential-equation.md).
