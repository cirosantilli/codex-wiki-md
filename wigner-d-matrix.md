# Wigner D-matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Irreducible spin representation](irreducible-spin-representation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wigner_D-matrix)

For active rotations with $U=e^{-i\phi J_3}e^{-i\theta J_2}e^{-i\psi J_3}$, the [irreducible spin representation](irreducible-spin-representation.md) [matrix](matrix.md) is $D^{(j)}_{mm'}=e^{-im\phi}d^{(j)}_{mm'}(\theta)e^{-im'\psi}$, where $d^{(j)}_{mm'}=\langle j,m|e^{-i\theta J_2}|j,m'\rangle$. Its spin-one-half middle [matrix](matrix.md) is $\begin{pmatrix}\cos(\theta/2)&-\sin(\theta/2)\\\sin(\theta/2)&\cos(\theta/2)\end{pmatrix}$. A $2\pi$ rotation acts by $(-1)^{2j}$; only integer-spin representations descend to the [special orthogonal group](special-orthogonal-group.md) in three [dimensions](dimension-vector-space.md).
