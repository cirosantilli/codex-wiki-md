<h1 id="cayley-s-matrix-memoir">Cayley's matrix memoir</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Cayley-Hamilton theorem](cayley-hamilton-theorem-split.md)

[Arthur Cayley](arthur-cayley.md)'s 1858 paper A Memoir on the Theory of Matrices motivates [matrices](matrix.md) as an abbreviated way to write [systems of linear equations](system-of-linear-equations.md). Its opening already connects the characteristic equation with reducing [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) in a [matrix](matrix.md) to lower degree. In §21 he works out the two-by-two identity; in §23 he says he has verified the three-by-three case but does not give a formal proof for every size. Thus this source distinguishes the general statement from a general proof.[Printed pp. 17–18, opening motivation; pp. 23–24, §§21–25, characteristic equation and reduction of matrix functions; received 10 December 1857, read 14 January 1858, published 1858](a-memoir-on-the-theory-of-matrices-by-arthur-cayley.md) The theorem's name also commemorates [William Rowan Hamilton](william-rowan-hamilton.md).
