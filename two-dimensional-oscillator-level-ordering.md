# Two-dimensional oscillator level ordering

↑ **Parent:** [Anisotropic quantum harmonic oscillator](anisotropic-quantum-harmonic-oscillator.md)

For a two-dimensional [anisotropic quantum harmonic oscillator](anisotropic-quantum-harmonic-oscillator.md) in the indicated range, the six lowest [number states](number-state.md), counted with multiplicity, have occupations $(0,0),(1,0),(0,1),(2,0),(1,1),(0,2)$. Their excitation [energies](energy-split.md), divided by $\hbar$, are $0,\omega_1,\omega_2,2\omega_1,\omega_1+\omega_2,2\omega_2$. These are ordered because $\omega_2<2\omega_1$ and $2\omega_2<3\omega_1$; every omitted state has at least three quanta and costs at least $3\hbar\omega_1$. At equal [angular frequencies](angular-frequency.md), the [two-dimensional isotropic harmonic oscillator](two-dimensional-isotropic-harmonic-oscillator.md) has [energy eigenvalue](energy-eigenvalue-split.md) $\hbar\omega(N+1)$ and [quantum degeneracy](degenerate-energy-levels.md) $N+1$, since there are $N+1$ ordered nonnegative pairs with sum $N$.
