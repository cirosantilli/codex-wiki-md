# Nuclear matter saturation

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear force](nuclear-force-split.md)

The approximately bounded [binding energy per nucleon](binding-energy-per-nucleon.md) and approximately constant interior density of well-bound medium and heavy nuclei. The short effective range and many-body character of the [nuclear force](nuclear-force-split.md) prevent each nucleon from binding independently to every other nucleon with an unsuppressed long-range attraction.
