# Hamming code of length seven

↑ **Parent:** [Hamming code](hamming-code.md)

The [binary linear code](binary-linear-code.md) known as the [Hamming code](hamming-code.md) of length seven is the [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md) of a $3\times7$ [parity-check matrix](parity-check-matrix.md) whose columns are all the nonzero vectors of $\mathbb F_2^3$. Its [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) is four. No one-column or two-column sum vanishes, but some three-column sum does, so its [minimum Hamming distance](minimum-distance-of-a-code.md) is three. The [syndrome](syndrome.md) of a one-bit error is its column, uniquely identifying the erroneous position.
