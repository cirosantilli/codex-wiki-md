# Interpolation property of weighted least-squares polynomial approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial interpolation](polynomial-interpolation.md)

Let $f$ be a real [continuous function](continuous-function.md) on a nondegenerate compact interval and let $p_n$ be its [best approximation](best-approximation-in-a-normed-space.md) among [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) of [polynomial degree](degree-of-a-polynomial.md) at most $n$ for a positive integrable [weighted inner product](weighted-inner-product.md). Then $f-p_n$ has at least $n+1$ distinct zeros. Indeed, if it had at most $n$, multiply it by the [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) with simple zeros at precisely its interior sign changes. The product has constant nonzero sign away from finitely many zeros, so its weighted integral is nonzero, contradicting [orthogonality](orthogonal-vectors.md) of $f-p_n$ to all [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) of [polynomial degree](degree-of-a-polynomial.md) at most $n$.
