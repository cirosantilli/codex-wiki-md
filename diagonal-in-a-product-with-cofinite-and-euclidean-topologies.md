# Diagonal in a product with cofinite and Euclidean topologies

↑ **Parent:** [Cofinite topology](cofinite-topology.md)

Let $Z$ be the underlying [set](set-split.md) of [real numbers](real-number.md) with the [cofinite topology](cofinite-topology.md). The diagonal $W=\{(t,t):t\in\mathbb R\}$ in $Z\times\mathbb R$ with its [subspace topology](subspace-topology.md) is [homeomorphic](homeomorphism.md) to the usual [real line](real-line.md). Indeed, $t\mapsto(t,t)$ is a [continuous bijection](continuous-bijection.md): its first coordinate is [continuous](continuous-function.md) because usual [finite sets](finite-set.md) are [closed](closed-set.md), and its second coordinate is the identity. The inverse is the second [coordinate projection](coordinate-projection.md) restricted to $W$. Consequently $W$ is not [compact](compact-space.md), whereas $Z$ is [compact](compact-space.md) by the fact that [every subset of a cofinite space is compact](every-subset-of-a-cofinite-space-is-compact.md). Thus $W$ is not [homeomorphic](homeomorphism.md) to $Z$.
