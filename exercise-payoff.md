# Exercise payoff

↑ **Parent:** [Contingent claim payoff](contingent-claim-payoff.md)

The [financial payoff](contingent-claim-payoff.md) obtained by exercising a [financial option](option-finance.md) at a permitted [stopping time](stopping-time.md) $\tau$. An [American option](american-option.md) compares this immediate payoff with the value of keeping the exercise right, whereas a [European option](european-contingent-claim.md) restricts exercise to expiry.
