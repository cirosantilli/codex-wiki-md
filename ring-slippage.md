# Ring slippage

↑ **Parent:** [Hapticity](hapticity.md)

A change in the number of adjacent ring atoms coordinating a [metal](metal.md), such as $\eta^5\to\eta^3$. It changes the [coordination ligand](ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md) donation and can open a site without loss of the entire ring [coordination ligand](ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md).
