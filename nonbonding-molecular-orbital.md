# Nonbonding molecular orbital

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular orbital](molecular-orbital.md)

A [molecular orbital](molecular-orbital.md) whose occupation changes bonding between the selected centres relatively little. In a real complex, weak bonding or antibonding contributions may replace exact nonbonding character.
