# Carbonyl stretching frequency as a probe of backbonding

↑ **Parent:** [Carbonyl ligand](carbonyl-ligand.md)

For a series of comparable [metal](metal.md) carbonyls, greater metal-to-CO [pi backbonding](pi-backbonding.md) populates CO [antibonding orbitals](antibonding-orbital.md), weakens its internal bond and lowers its infrared stretching frequency. Coupled [normal modes](normal-mode.md) and changing geometry must be considered when comparing observed bands.
