# Creation of limits up to isomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Limit-creating functor](limit-creating-functor.md)

An equivalence-invariant lifting property permits the underlying cone of the lift to be identified with the chosen cone by an isomorphism, rather than requiring literal equality. Lifts are unique up to a unique isomorphism compatible with that identification. A [monadic adjunction](monadic-adjunction.md) defined by equivalence of its comparison functor has this property by [monad algebra forgetful functor creates limits](monad-algebra-forgetful-functor-creates-limits.md). Literal creation follows if the comparison is an isomorphism over the base, or suitable strict transport of objects is part of the convention. An equivalence alone does not guarantee a literally prescribed underlying object lies in the image.
