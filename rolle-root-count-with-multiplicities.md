# Rolle root count with multiplicities

↑ **Parent:** [Rolle theorem](rolle-theorem.md)

If a nonzero real [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) has $N$ roots in an open real interval, counted with [multiplicity of a root](multiplicity-of-a-root.md), its derivative has at least $N-1$ roots in that interval, also counted with multiplicity, provided $N\ge1$. Each root of multiplicity $m$ becomes a root of the derivative of multiplicity $m-1$. The [Rolle theorem](rolle-theorem.md) supplies one further root between each pair of distinct consecutive roots. Thus $k$ distinct roots of multiplicities $m_j$ give at least $\sum_j(m_j-1)+(k-1)=N-1$ derivative roots.
