# Lagrangian foliation

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrangian submanifold](lagrangian-submanifold.md)

A foliation of a [symplectic manifold](symplectic-manifold.md) is Lagrangian when every leaf has half the ambient dimension and the restricted [symplectic form](symplectic-form.md) vanishes. The tangent distribution is a real [polarization in geometric quantization](polarization-in-geometric-quantization.md). Restricted prequantum connections are flat on individual leaves, but nontrivial [holonomy](holonomy.md) can obstruct global parallel sections.
