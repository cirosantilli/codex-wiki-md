# Order of an algebraic differential operator

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic differential operator ring](algebraic-differential-operator-ring.md)

The least index in the commutator filtration of the [algebraic differential operator ring](algebraic-differential-operator-ring.md) containing a nonzero operator. The zero operator has order $-\infty$ by convention. Equivalently every $(j+1)$-fold iterated commutator with multiplication maps vanishes for an operator of order at most $j$.
