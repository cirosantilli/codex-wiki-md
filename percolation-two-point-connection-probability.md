# Percolation two-point connection probability

↑ **Parent:** [Bond percolation](bond-percolation-split.md)

The two-point [percolation two-point connection probability](percolation-two-point-connection-probability.md) $\tau_p(x,y)=\mathbb P_p(x\leftrightarrow y)$ is the [probability](probability.md) that two fixed [graph vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) belong to the same [percolation cluster](percolation-cluster.md). On a translation-invariant lattice it depends on their displacement. Restricting the permitted connecting [graph paths](path-in-a-graph.md) to a subgraph gives the corresponding confined [percolation two-point connection probability](percolation-two-point-connection-probability.md). The [Harris-FKG inequality](harris-fkg-inequality.md) implies $\tau_p(x,z)\geq\tau_p(x,y)\tau_p(y,z)$.
