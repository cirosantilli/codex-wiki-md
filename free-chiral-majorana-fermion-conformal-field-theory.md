# Free chiral Majorana fermion conformal field theory

↑ **Parent:** [Two-dimensional conformal field theory](two-dimensional-conformal-field-theory.md)

A real chiral [Majorana fermion](majorana-spinor.md) with contraction $\psi(z)\psi(w)\sim1/(z-w)$ has stress tensor $T=-:\psi\partial\psi:/2$. Fermionic [Wick contractions](wick-contraction.md) give $T(z)\psi(w)\sim\psi(w)/(2(z-w)^2)+\partial\psi(w)/(z-w)$. Double contractions in $TT$ give $1/(4(z-w)^4)$, so the [central charge](central-charge.md) is $1/2$. Fermion bilinears of different species are weight-one currents; equal-species undifferentiated bilinears vanish by antisymmetry.
