# Wang sequence over a sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Wang homomorphism](wang-homomorphism.md)

For a fibration $F\to E\to S^n$, the [Wang homomorphism](wang-homomorphism.md) belongs to the exact sequence

$$
\cdots\to H_j(F)\to H_j(E)\to H_{j-n}(F)
\xrightarrow{\Delta}H_{j-1}(F)\to\cdots.
$$
