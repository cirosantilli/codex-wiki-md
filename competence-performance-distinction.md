# Competence-performance distinction

↑ **Parent:** [Cognitive psychology](cognitive-psychology.md)

The distinction between a proposed underlying ability or representation and its expression under a particular task’s demands. An easier response format may reveal an ability missed by another format, while scaffolded success need not establish independent, fully general deployment.
