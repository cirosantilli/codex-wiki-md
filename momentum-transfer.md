# Momentum transfer

↑ **Parent:** [Scattering amplitude](scattering-amplitude.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Momentum_transfer)

The momentum transfer in a scattering process is the difference between the incoming and outgoing [wavevectors](wavevector.md), commonly $Q=k-k'$ up to a sign convention and a factor of $\hbar$.
