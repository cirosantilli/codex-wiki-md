# Complete splitting is preserved by Galois closure

↑ **Parent:** [Primes in an intermediate field as double cosets](primes-in-an-intermediate-field-as-double-cosets.md)

If $M/K$ is the [Galois closure](galois-closure-of-a-field-extension.md) of a finite extension $L/K$, a prime splits completely in $L$ exactly when it splits completely in $M$. Complete splitting means every right action of $D$ on $H\backslash G$ fixes each coset, so $D$ lies in the [subgroup core](core-group-theory.md) of $H$. That core is trivial because the conjugates of $L$ generate $M$.
