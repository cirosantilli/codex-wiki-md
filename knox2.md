# KNOX2

↑ **Parent:** [Transcription factor](transcription-factor.md)

A class of plant developmental regulators implicated in the distinction between [gametophyte](gametophyte.md) and [sporophyte](sporophyte.md) programs. Deleting KNOX2 regulators in a moss produced gametophyte bodies from [diploid](diploid.md) embryos without intervening [meiosis](meiosis.md).[Deletion experiments](knox2-genes-regulate-the-haploid-to-diploid-morphological-transition-in-land-plants-by-keiko-sakakibara-and-others.md)
