# Regular-epimorphism-monomorphism factorization from kernel pairs

↑ **Parent:** [Strong epimorphism](strong-epimorphism.md)

Suppose a category has pullbacks and coequalizers and every pullback of a [regular epimorphism](regular-epimorphism.md) is epic. Coequalizing the kernel pair of any morphism produces a factorization as a regular epimorphism followed by a [monomorphism](monomorphism.md). Consequently every [strong epimorphism](strong-epimorphism.md) is regular.
