<h1 id="dirichlet-s-approximation-theorem">Dirichlet's approximation theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Diophantine approximation](diophantine-approximation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dirichlet's_approximation_theorem)

For every [irrational number](irrational-number.md) $\alpha$ and [positive integer](positive-integer.md) $N$, there are [integers](integer.md) $p,q$ with $1\leq q\leq N$ such that

$$
\left|\alpha-\frac pq\right|\leq\frac1{qN}.
$$

The proof applies the [pigeonhole principle](pigeonhole-principle.md) to the $N+1$ [fractional parts](fractional-part.md) of $0,\alpha,\ldots,N\alpha$.
