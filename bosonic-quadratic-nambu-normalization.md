# Bosonic quadratic Nambu normalization

↑ **Parent:** [Nambu spinor](nambu-spinor.md)

For real even coefficients, $\Psi_k=(a_k,a_{-k}^\dagger)^{\mathsf T}$ and a full-zone sum, this expression equals $C+\sum_k[A_ka_k^\dagger a_k+B_k(a_ka_{-k}+a_k^\dagger a_{-k}^\dagger)/2]$. The constant subtraction follows from $a_{-k}a_{-k}^\dagger=1+a_{-k}^\dagger a_{-k}$. Pairing redundancy and [normal ordering](normal-ordering.md) determine both factors together. Switching to half a zone requires grouping both members of each pair; changing only a diagonal factor is inconsistent.
