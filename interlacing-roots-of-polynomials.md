# Interlacing roots of polynomials

↑ **Parent:** [Root of a polynomial](root-of-a-polynomial.md)

Two real [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) with only real [roots of a polynomial](root-of-a-polynomial.md) interlace when their ordered roots alternate, counting [multiplicity of a root](multiplicity-of-a-root.md). For equal [polynomial degrees](degree-of-a-polynomial.md), one ordering is the displayed chain; the opposite ordering is also permitted. Consecutive degrees give the analogous chain with one extra root at each end for the higher-degree [polynomial](polynomial-split.md). Strict interlacing uses strict inequalities. Weak interlacing permits common roots and follows as a [limit](limit-of-a-function.md) of strict interlacing.
