# Syn and anti pentalene coordination

↑ **Parent:** [Pentalene](pentalene.md)

Two [metal](metal.md) fragments attached to the fused ring system are syn when they lie on the same face and anti when they lie on opposite faces. A direct [metal](metal.md)–[metal](metal.md) bond between adjacent fragments generally requires the syn arrangement.
