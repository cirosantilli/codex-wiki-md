# Factorial moment densities of Poisson-Dirichlet components

↑ **Parent:** [One-parameter Poisson-Dirichlet distribution](one-parameter-poisson-dirichlet-distribution.md)

This density is supported where all $x_i>0$ and $\sum_i x_i<1$, and gives the expected sum over ordered distinct components of $PD(\theta)$. Apply the iterated [Mecke formula for a Poisson random measure](mecke-formula-for-a-poisson-random-measure.md) to $m$ selected jumps $u_i$ and the remaining gamma total $t$. With $s=t+\sum_i u_i$ and $x_i=u_i/s$, the Jacobian is $s^m$ and the integral in $s$ is $\Gamma(\theta)$, giving the density. It describes joint counts of large components, beyond the first-moment measure.
