# Singular chain complex

↑ **Parent:** [Singular homology](singular-homology.md)

The [chain complex](chain-complex.md) whose degree-$n$ group is freely generated over $R$ by the [singular simplices](singular-simplex.md) $\sigma:\Delta^n\to X$. Its boundary is the alternating sum of restrictions to codimension-one faces. The [homology](homology-split.md) of this complex is [singular homology](singular-homology.md).
