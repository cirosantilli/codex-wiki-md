# Alpha-amino acid

↑ **Parent:** [Amino acid](amino-acid.md)

An [alpha-amino acid](alpha-amino-acid.md) has amino functionality on the [carbon](carbon.md) next to its carboxyl [carbon](carbon.md). The skeleton in [amino acid](amino-acid.md) shows this adjacency explicitly. Numbering the carboxyl [carbon](carbon.md) as C1 makes the adjacent position C2, also called the alpha position.
