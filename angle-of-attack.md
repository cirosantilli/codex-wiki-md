# Angle of attack

↑ **Parent:** [Airfoil](airfoil.md)

The signed [angle](angle.md) between a lifting surface's chord and its incoming relative flow. Positive incidence in the planar convention used in [airfoil](airfoil.md) raises the leading edge relative to the trailing edge in a horizontal left-to-right flow. It is distinct from orientation relative to the ground; an induced [velocity](velocity.md) can change the local effective incidence.
