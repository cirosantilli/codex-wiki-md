# Near-origin probability in a maximal-angular-momentum hydrogen state

↑ **Parent:** [Radial Schrodinger equation for the hydrogen atom](radial-schrodinger-equation-for-the-hydrogen-atom.md)

For a normalized [maximal-angular-momentum hydrogen state](maximal-angular-momentum-hydrogen-state.md), the probability inside a small radius $r_p$ is the integral of $|R|^2r^2$. Since $R$ behaves as $r^l$ near zero, it equals $2^{2l+3}(r_p/a)^{2l+3}/[(2l+3)!(l+1)^{2l+3}]$ times $1+O(r_p/a)$. Increasing angular momentum strongly suppresses penetration toward the Coulomb centre.
