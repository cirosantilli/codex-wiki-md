# Prediction and coefficient error bound for compatible Lasso

↑ **Parent:** [Fast-rate Lasso prediction bound under compatibility](fast-rate-lasso-prediction-bound-under-compatibility.md)

With objective $\|Y-Xb\|_2^2/(2n)+\lambda\|b\|_1$, the [Gaussian score event for the Lasso](gaussian-score-event-for-the-lasso.md) $\|X^T\epsilon/n\|_\infty\leq\lambda/2$ gives $Q+\lambda\|\delta_{S^c}\|_1\leq3\lambda\|\delta_S\|_1$, where $Q=\|X\delta\|_2^2/n$. The [Lasso cone condition](lasso-cone-condition.md) activates the [Compatibility condition for the Lasso](compatibility-condition-for-the-lasso.md). Then $Q+\lambda\|\delta\|_1\leq4\lambda\sqrt{sQ}/\phi_0\leq Q/2+8\lambda^2s/\phi_0^2$. Thus even $Q+2\lambda\|\delta\|_1\leq16\lambda^2s/\phi_0^2$. This controls prediction and coefficient error together, including a design with correlated columns.
