# Impulse approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Galaxy dynamics](galaxy-dynamics.md)

In a rapid encounter, the impulse approximation holds stellar positions fixed while integrating an external acceleration over time. A stellar velocity kick is $\Delta\mathbf v=\int\mathbf a_{\rm tidal}(t)\,dt$. If the encounter time $p/v$ is much shorter than the orbital time $\Omega^{-1}$, this gives reliable leading [tidal heating](tidal-heating.md) for a weak, distant encounter. Slow perturbations require [adiabatic shielding of tidal encounters](adiabatic-shielding-of-tidal-encounters.md) instead.
