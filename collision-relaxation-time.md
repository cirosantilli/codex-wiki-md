# Collision relaxation time

↑ **Parent:** [BGK model](bgk-model.md)

The timescale on which a kinetic relaxation model damps departures from a matching [local Maxwellian](local-maxwellian.md). In the spatially homogeneous [BGK model](bgk-model.md) with constant $\tau$, conserved [velocity moments](velocity-moment.md) fix $\mathcal M$, and $f(t)=\mathcal M+[f(0)-\mathcal M]e^{-t/\tau}$. The parameter is of the order of an effective mean time between collisions rather than an exact universal microscopic collision time.
