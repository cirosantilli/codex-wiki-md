# Entropy monotonicity under independent addition

↑ **Parent:** [Information entropy](information-entropy.md)

If $U,V$ are [independent random variables](independent-random-variables.md) taking values in a countable additive group, conditioning on $V$ makes $U+V$ a translate of $U$. Consequently $H(U+V\mid V)=H(U)$, and [conditional entropy](conditional-entropy.md) gives $H(U+V)\geq H(U)$. If the two [information entropies](information-entropy.md) are finite, equality holds exactly when $U+V$ and $V$ are independent.
