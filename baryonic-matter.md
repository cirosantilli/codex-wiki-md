# Baryonic matter

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmology](cosmology-split.md)

The ordinary matter component whose [mass](mass.md) is predominantly in [baryons](baryon.md), including stars and gas. Cosmological baryonic-matter budgets conventionally include the associated [electrons](electron.md). Its cosmic fraction among total matter is $\Omega_b/\Omega_m$, rather than $\Omega_b$ alone.
