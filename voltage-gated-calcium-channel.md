# Voltage-gated calcium channel

↑ **Parent:** [Voltage-gated ion channel](voltage-gated-ion-channel.md)

A [voltage-gated calcium channel](voltage-gated-calcium-channel.md) couples membrane voltage to a calcium-permeable pore. In a pancreatic beta cell, depolarization-dependent calcium entry can connect metabolic sensing to insulin-granule exocytosis.
