# Image-length measure of a strictly increasing continuous function

↑ **Parent:** [Absolute continuity of measures](absolute-continuity-of-measures.md)

Let $h:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ be [continuous](continuous-function.md), [strictly increasing](strictly-increasing-function.md), and satisfy [Lusin condition N](lusin-condition-n.md). Then

$$
\nu(A)=\lambda(h(A))
$$

defines a [measure](measure.md) on the [Lebesgue measurable sets](lebesgue-measurable-set.md), and $\nu\ll\lambda$. Moreover,

$$
\nu([a,b])=h(b)-h(a)
$$

for $a\leq b$.
