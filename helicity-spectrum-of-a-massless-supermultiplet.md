# Helicity spectrum of a massless supermultiplet

↑ **Parent:** [Massless supermultiplet](massless-supermultiplet.md)

For a four-dimensional [massless supermultiplet](massless-supermultiplet.md) with $\mathcal N$ independent [supercharges](supersymmetry-generator.md) and highest [helicity](helicity.md) $\lambda$, level $k$ has [helicity](helicity.md) $\lambda-k/2$ and multiplicity $\binom{\mathcal N}{k}$. The total is $2^{\mathcal N}$ states and the [helicity](helicity.md) range has width $\mathcal N/2$, before any necessary [CPT completion of a supermultiplet](cpt-completion-of-a-supermultiplet.md).
