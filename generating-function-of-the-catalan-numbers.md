# Generating function of the Catalan numbers

↑ **Parent:** [Catalan recurrence](catalan-recurrence.md)

The [ordinary generating function](ordinary-generating-function.md) $C(x)=\sum_{n\geq0}C_nx^n$ packages the [Catalan recurrence](catalan-recurrence.md). Its [Cauchy product](cauchy-product.md) gives the [formal power series](formal-power-series.md) identity

$$
C(x)=1+xC(x)^2.
$$

The constant term is $C_0=1$. Solving the [quadratic equation](quadratic-equation.md) chooses

$$
C(x)=\frac{1-\sqrt{1-4x}}{2x},
$$

with the [formal power series](formal-power-series.md) square root having constant term one. The apparent quotient at $x=0$ has constant term one after cancellation; the other quadratic solution has a pole and cannot be this [ordinary generating function](ordinary-generating-function.md). The equation records the empty object or an outer unit with two ordered smaller objects.
