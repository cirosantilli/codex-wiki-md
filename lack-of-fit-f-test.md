# Lack-of-fit F-test

↑ **Parent:** [Lack of fit](lack-of-fit.md)

For $n$ independent normal responses of common [variance](variance-split.md) at $g$ distinct predictor settings, a rank-$p$ [normal linear model](normal-linear-model.md) has residual degrees $n-p$. The saturated setting-mean model has $g$ parameters and [pure error](pure-error.md) degrees $n-g$. Splitting orthogonal residual spaces gives [lack of fit](lack-of-fit.md) degrees $g-p$. Under the fitted mean model, the two squared lengths divided by the common variance are independent [chi-squared](chi-squared-distribution.md) variables; their ratio of mean squares has the [F-distribution](f-distribution.md) shown. This test is unavailable without repeated settings or positive lack-of-fit degrees.
