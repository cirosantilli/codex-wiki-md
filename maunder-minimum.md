# Maunder minimum

↑ **Parent:** [Solar grand minimum](solar-grand-minimum.md)

The Maunder minimum is the interval approximately 1645–1715 when recorded sunspot activity was exceptionally low. It is the best-known historical [solar grand minimum](solar-grand-minimum.md). Historical coverage and cosmogenic-isotope records require care in inferring its precise magnetic amplitude; evidence for weak modulation need not imply that the normal spot-producing dynamo disappeared completely.
