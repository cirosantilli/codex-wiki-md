# Highest-weight character subtraction

↑ **Parent:** [Formal character of a weight module](formal-character-of-a-weight-module.md)

For a finite-dimensional representation of a complex [semisimple Lie algebra](semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md), select a maximal remaining [dominant weight](dominant-weight.md), subtract that multiplicity of the corresponding irreducible [formal character](formal-character-of-a-weight-module.md), and repeat. The [Weyl complete reducibility theorem](weyl-complete-reducibility-theorem.md) guarantees nonnegative residual multiplicities and termination. Checking the full character or the exhausted total dimension confirms that no summands remain.
