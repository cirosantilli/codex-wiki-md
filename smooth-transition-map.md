# Smooth transition map

↑ **Parent:** [Smooth atlas](smooth-atlas.md)

On overlapping [manifold charts](manifold-chart.md), a transition map changes one coordinate representation to the other. In a [smooth atlas](smooth-atlas.md) it and its inverse are smooth on the corresponding open coordinate domains.
