# Signed cyclic three-mode amplitude equations

↑ **Parent:** [Amplitude equation](amplitude-equation.md)

Independent sign changes of three amplitudes and a cyclic coordinate permutation force each cubic component to be its own amplitude times a cyclic permutation of one quadratic polynomial. One convenient parametrization is $\dot x=x(\mu-aX-cy^2+ez^2)$ with cyclic companions and $X=x^2+y^2+z^2$. The axial and diagonal isotropy types give primary branches; two-coordinate branches can emerge in secondary [pitchfork bifurcations](pitchfork-bifurcation-normal-form.md) when cross-couplings change sign.
