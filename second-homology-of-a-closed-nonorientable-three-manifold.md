# Second homology of a closed nonorientable three-manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Poincare duality with the orientation local system](poincare-duality-with-the-orientation-local-system.md)

Zero [Euler characteristic](euler-characteristic.md) and $H_3=0$ give free rank $b_1-1$. The [universal coefficient theorem for cohomology](universal-coefficient-theorem-for-cohomology.md) identifies $H^3(M;\mathbb Z)=\mathbb Z/2$ with $\operatorname{Ext}(H_2(M;\mathbb Z),\mathbb Z)$, so the only torsion in $H_2$ is one copy of $\mathbb Z/2$. In particular $b_1\geq1$.
