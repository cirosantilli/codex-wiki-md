# Stellar thermal-wind balance

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar rotation](stellar-rotation.md)

Taking the [curl](curl.md) of axisymmetric [hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) with centrifugal acceleration gives $\varpi\partial_z\Omega^2=(\nabla p\times\nabla\rho)_\phi/\rho^2$. For an approximately spherical [ideal gas](ideal-gas.md) with constant composition, use $\nabla\rho=\nabla p/c_s^2-\rho\nabla S/c_p$ to obtain the displayed relation. Latitudinal [entropy](entropy.md) differences can support noncylindrical [differential rotation](differential-rotation.md). A [barotropic fluid](barotropic-fluid.md) gives cylindrical rotation in this leading balance; the relation does not determine the transport stresses or circulation rate.
