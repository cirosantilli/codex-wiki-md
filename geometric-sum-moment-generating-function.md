# Geometric-sum moment-generating function

↑ **Parent:** [Random sum of independent claims](random-sum-of-independent-claims.md)

For a [geometric distribution](geometric-distribution.md) count on the positive integers, independent of [independent and identically distributed random variables](independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md) $X_i$, the [moment-generating function](moment-generating-function.md) of $S=\sum_{i=1}^NX_i$ is the displayed expression. Condition on the count and sum a [geometric series](geometric-series.md). For real $t$, the finite domain requires $M_X(t)<\infty$ and $(1-p)M_X(t)<1$. Positive summands always permit $t\leq0$, even when no positive exponential moment exists; this gives a [Laplace transform of a nonnegative random variable](laplace-transform-of-a-nonnegative-random-variable.md). The aggregate has no mass at zero when all summands are strictly positive.
