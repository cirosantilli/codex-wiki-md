# Holomorphic projectivization by lines

↑ **Parent:** [Projective bundle](projective-bundle.md)

For a [holomorphic vector bundle](holomorphic-vector-bundle.md), projectivize each fibre using lines. The transition $g_{ij}(x)$ acts holomorphically by $[v]\mapsto[g_{ij}(x)v]$. Thus local products $U_i\times\mathbb P^{r-1}$ define a [complex manifold](complex-manifold.md) with holomorphic projection to the base. The lines convention gives the [relative tautological line bundle](relative-tautological-line-bundle.md) of fibre degree $-1$, and its dual has degree $+1$.
