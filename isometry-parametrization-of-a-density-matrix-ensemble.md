# Isometry parametrization of a density-matrix ensemble

↑ **Parent:** [Hughston–Jozsa–Wootters theorem](hughston-jozsa-wootters-theorem.md)

For the nonzero spectral decomposition $\rho=\sum_{a=1}^r\lambda_a|e_a\rangle\langle e_a|$, every pure-state [quantum state ensemble](quantum-state-ensemble.md) of $\rho$ is specified by the displayed column [linear isometry](linear-isometry-of-hilbert-spaces.md). To prove this, write $w_j=\sqrt{p_j}\phi_j$. Positivity forces each $w_j$ into the support of $\rho$, and the coefficients $U_{ja}=\langle e_a|w_j\rangle/\sqrt{\lambda_a}$ have orthonormal columns because $\sum_j|w_j\rangle\langle w_j|=\rho$. Conversely this column identity reconstructs $\rho$; the squared vector norms are the [probabilities](probability.md) and sum to one. Complete the columns to unitary bases after adding zero ensemble vectors to obtain the usual unitary-mixing formulation.
