**[Delay-period activity](../../../delay-period-activity.md) explains important properties of [working memory](../../../working-memory.md) when its information content and dynamics predict behavior; elevated activity alone is not a complete storage mechanism.** An explanation must account for maintaining an absent stimulus, limited [working memory capacity](../../../working-memory-capacity.md), variable precision, interference and flexible prioritization. Different measurements reveal different aspects: [extracellular single-unit recording](../../../extracellular-single-unit-recording.md) measures individual [neurons](../../../neuron-split.md), [electroencephalography](../../../electroencephalography.md) measures aggregated electrical signals, and [functional magnetic resonance imaging](../../../functional-magnetic-resonance-imaging.md) measures a slower hemodynamic response. None directly reads a unitary store.

The classic case for [persistent neural activity](../../../persistent-neural-activity.md) came from delayed-response tasks. In [Shintaro Funahashi](../../../shintaro-funahashi.md) and colleagues' 1989 experiment, monkeys maintained fixation after a briefly presented peripheral cue and later made a memory-guided [saccade](../../../saccade.md). [Dorsolateral prefrontal cortex](../../../dorsolateral-prefrontal-cortex.md) [neurons](../../../neuron-split.md) could maintain location-selective firing during the intervening blank interval; [delay-period activity](../../../delay-period-activity.md) was reduced or absent on some error trials. This connects a maintained code to a remembered location rather than merely to stimulus presence. It also supplies a plausible bridge between a short-lived sensory event and a later response. However, the remembered location and intended eye movement covaried, so the experiment does not isolate human mnemonic storage from prospective action coding.[1989, Summary and Conclusions; oculomotor delayed-response task](../../../mnemonic-coding-of-visual-space-in-the-monkey-s-dorsolateral-prefrontal-cortex-by-shintaro-funahashi-and-others.md)

More explanatory leverage comes from linking population dynamics to the _pattern_ of errors. A [bump attractor](../../../bump-attractor.md) maintains a localized pattern through recurrent excitation and inhibition. Noise can move its center while leaving a memory trace present, predicting increasing uncertainty and trial-specific directional errors. [Klaus Wimmer](../../../klaus-wimmer.md) and colleagues tested such predictions against monkey prefrontal recordings and memory-guided eye movements: neural variability was related to fine-grained response errors in ways predicted by a diffusing representation. This is stronger than a generic activation–accuracy association because competing models need to explain that structured relationship. Nevertheless, it is evidence from a trained spatial task in monkeys; it does not by itself explain verbal rehearsal, binding errors or every source of human forgetting.[2014, abstract and tests of model-predicted neural–behavioral relationships](../../../bump-attractor-dynamics-in-prefrontal-cortex-explains-behavioral-precision-in-spatial-working-memory-by-klaus-wimmer-and-others.md)

Human recordings link delay signals to [working memory capacity](../../../working-memory-capacity.md). In [Edward K. Vogel](../../../edward-k-vogel.md) and colleagues' visual-memory experiments, people remembered arrays on one side of fixation while ignoring the other. The [contralateral delay activity](../../../contralateral-delay-activity.md) increased with memory load and approached an asymptote associated with each person's behavioral [working memory capacity](../../../working-memory-capacity.md). Its usefulness is the relation between a physiological load function and an independently estimated behavioral limit. It does not prove that the [brain](../../../brain.md) has exactly a fixed number of universal slots: stimulus complexity, filtering, allocation and the measurement's sensitivity can influence the signal. A plateau is evidence constraining a [working memory capacity](../../../working-memory-capacity.md) mechanism, not the mechanism itself.[2004, load-dependent electrophysiology and individual capacity differences](../../../neural-activity-predicts-individual-differences-in-visual-working-memory-capacity-by-edward-k-vogel-and-others.md)

[Delay-period activity](../../../delay-period-activity.md) also contains specific sensory information. [Stephenie A. Harrison](../../../stephenie-a-harrison.md) and [Frank Tong](../../../frank-tong.md) presented oriented patterns and cued which orientation to remember. [Multivariate pattern analysis](../../../multivariate-pattern-analysis.md) of early [visual cortex](../../../visual-cortex.md) [functional magnetic resonance imaging](../../../functional-magnetic-resonance-imaging.md) could identify the remembered orientation during the delay, even when mean activation was weak. This supports [sensory recruitment](../../../sensory-recruitment.md) and helps explain why visual memories retain feature-specific detail and may compete with new sensory input. Decoding establishes accessible information, however, not necessarily a local storage site: top-down feedback, [attention](../../../attention.md) or task preparation could generate a decodable pattern. Its slow hemodynamics cannot establish uninterrupted moment-to-moment firing.[2009, orientation-memory task and delay-period decoding](../../../decoding-reveals-the-contents-of-visual-working-memory-in-early-visual-areas-by-stephenie-a-harrison-and-frank-tong.md)

The temporal and priority structure of [working memory](../../../working-memory.md) challenges an exclusively tonic account. [Mikael Lundqvist](../../../mikael-lundqvist.md) and colleagues recorded monkey prefrontal spiking and [local field potentials](../../../local-field-potential.md) and found brief [gamma oscillation](../../../gamma-oscillation.md) and [beta oscillation](../../../beta-oscillation.md) bursts rather than uniformly sustained oscillatory activity. A smooth trial average can conceal intermittent events. This finding qualifies the interpretation of averaged persistence; it does not show that every [neuron](../../../neuron-split.md) becomes silent between bursts, or that recurrent activity is irrelevant.[2016, abstract; trial-level prefrontal bursts and sensory reactivation](../../../gamma-and-beta-bursts-underlie-working-memory-by-mikael-lundqvist-and-others.md)

Human perturbation experiments distinguish current priority from continued retention. In [Nathan S. Rose](../../../nathan-s-rose.md) and colleagues' task, a temporarily unattended item could cease to be decodable yet remain relevant for a later probe. A [transcranial magnetic stimulation](../../../transcranial-magnetic-stimulation.md) pulse reinstated a detectable representation under appropriate relevance conditions. In [Michael J. Wolff](../../../michael-j-wolff.md) and colleagues' experiment, a neutral visual impulse during the delay evoked an [electroencephalography](../../../electroencephalography.md) response carrying information about remembered items, including temporarily unattended ones; instructed forgetting and temporary inattention had different consequences, and the hidden-state measure related to [working memory precision](../../../working-memory-precision.md). These results explain how an item may remain available without occupying the same active state as the current focus of [attention](../../../attention.md).[2016, retrocue and perturbation experiments](../../../reactivation-of-latent-working-memories-with-transcranial-magnetic-stimulation-by-nathan-s-rose-and-others.md)[2017, impulse-response decoding, instructed forgetting and behavioral precision](../../../dynamic-hidden-states-underlying-working-memory-guided-behavior-by-michael-j-wolff-and-others.md)

An [activity-silent working memory](../../../activity-silent-working-memory.md) interpretation proposes temporarily altered network responsiveness, potentially involving [short-term synaptic plasticity](../../../short-term-synaptic-plasticity.md). Neither an absent decoding result nor a successful perturbation identifies that molecular mechanism uniquely: weak or recoded activity can evade a particular measurement, and perturbation can recruit distributed networks. The strongest conclusion is therefore pluralistic. [Persistent neural activity](../../../persistent-neural-activity.md) offers a plausible mechanism for some maintenance and precision effects, while intermittent and latent states help explain prioritization and retained but unattended information. A successful theory must jointly predict errors, load effects and perturbation consequences at the appropriate time scale. **[Delay-period activity](../../../delay-period-activity.md) provides substantial mechanistic constraints, but human [working memory](../../../working-memory.md) is better explained by interacting representations and control states than by equating storage with continuously elevated firing.**
