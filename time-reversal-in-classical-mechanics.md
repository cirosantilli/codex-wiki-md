# Time reversal in classical mechanics

↑ **Parent:** [Classical mechanics](classical-mechanics-split.md)

[Time reversal in classical mechanics](time-reversal-in-classical-mechanics.md) reverses the order of a trajectory's states and the sign of its velocities. A coordinate is time-even, while its velocity is time-odd. [Microscopic reversibility](microscopic-reversibility.md) compares a path with this physical reversal, including the correct equilibrium initial weights.
