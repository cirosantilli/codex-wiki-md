# Countable generating algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Algebra of sets](algebra-of-sets.md)

A countable [algebra of sets](algebra-of-sets.md) generating the intended sigma-algebra. Standard Borel spaces admit such algebras by taking finite Boolean combinations from a countable topological base. Finite linear combinations of its indicators are dense in $L^2$ for every Borel [probability measure](probability-measure.md), by the [Monotone class theorem](monotone-class-theorem.md). Countability permits a common full-measure convergence set for all test indicators.
