# Weight-space decomposition

↑ **Parent:** [Weight space](weight-space.md)

A weight-space decomposition expresses a [Lie algebra representation](lie-algebra-representation.md) as a [direct sum](direct-sum.md) of simultaneous [eigenspaces](eigenspace.md) for a [Cartan subalgebra](cartan-subalgebra.md). Each summand is the [weight space](weight-space.md) for its [weight](weight-representation-theory.md). In complex finite-dimensional representations of a [semisimple Lie algebra](semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md), the simple [coroots](coroot.md) act as commuting diagonalizable operators, giving this decomposition.
