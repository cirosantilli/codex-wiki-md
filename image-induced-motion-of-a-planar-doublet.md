# Image-induced motion of a planar doublet

↑ **Parent:** [Complex potential of a two-dimensional potential flow](complex-potential-of-a-two-dimensional-potential-flow.md)

For a [two-dimensional potential doublet](two-dimensional-potential-doublet.md) of fixed complex strength $M$ outside a stationary circle of radius $a$, the [Milne-Thomson circle theorem](milne-thomson-circle-theorem.md) gives the image contribution $\bar M z/(a^2-\bar b z)$. If the singularity is stipulated to follow the regular fluid [velocity](velocity.md) at its position, differentiating this term and using the [complex velocity](complex-velocity.md) convention gives the displayed motion law. Thus $\operatorname{Im}(b\bar M)$ is constant: the trajectory is a straight line parallel to $M$. With $M=me^{i\alpha}$ and initial location $b_0>a$ real, its [polar coordinates](polar-coordinates.md) satisfy $r\sin(\theta-\alpha)=-b_0\sin\alpha$. This statement assumes fixed orientation; the translational rule alone supplies no equation for orientation evolution.
