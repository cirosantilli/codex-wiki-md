# Liquid-gas critical point

↑ **Parent:** [Critical phenomenon](critical-phenomenon-split.md)

The endpoint of a gas-liquid [phase coexistence curve](phase-coexistence-curve.md), where the distinct liquid and gas states merge. For a smooth fluid [equation of state](equation-of-state.md), the critical isotherm has $p_v=p_{vv}=0$ at this point; this thermodynamic meaning differs from a stationary [critical point](critical-point.md) of an arbitrary function.
