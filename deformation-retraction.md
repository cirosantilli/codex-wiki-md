# Deformation retraction

↑ **Parent:** [Retract](retract.md)

A deformation retraction of $X$ onto $A$ is a homotopy from $\operatorname{id}_X$ to a retraction $r:X\to A$ that keeps $A$ inside itself throughout. It makes the inclusion $A\hookrightarrow X$ a [homotopy equivalence](homotopy-equivalence.md).
