# Euler-Lagrange field equation with second derivatives

↑ **Parent:** [Euler-Lagrange field equation](euler-lagrange-field-equation.md)

For an [action](action.md) $S=\int\mathcal L(\phi,\partial\phi,\partial^2\phi)d^{d+1}x$, vary the field with compact support, or fix both $\delta\phi$ and its first derivatives on the boundary. One [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) on the first-derivative term and two on the second-derivative term give the displayed [Euler-Lagrange field equation](euler-lagrange-field-equation.md). With ordered repeated indices, define the second-derivative coefficient symmetrically so that $\delta\mathcal L=\mathcal L_\phi\delta\phi+\mathcal L_{\phi_{,\mu}}\partial_\mu\delta\phi+\mathcal L_{\phi_{,\mu\nu}}\partial_\mu\partial_\nu\delta\phi$. This convention avoids an unintended factor of two in mixed derivative terms. Higher spatial derivatives alone do not require higher time derivatives.
