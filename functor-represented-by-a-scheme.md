# Functor represented by a scheme

↑ **Parent:** [Scheme](scheme.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Functor_represented_by_a_scheme)

The functor of points describes a [scheme](scheme.md) through its morphisms from test [schemes](scheme.md). On [commutative rings](commutative-ring.md), it is covariant because a [ring homomorphism](ring-homomorphism.md) reverses the corresponding affine-scheme map. A functor on [rings](ring.md) is represented by $X$ in the algebraic-geometric sense when it is naturally isomorphic to $h_X$. The representing [scheme](scheme.md) need not be affine; this is different from demanding a [representable functor](representable-functor.md) in the category of [rings](ring.md) itself.
