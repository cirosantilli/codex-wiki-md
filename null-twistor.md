# Null twistor

↑ **Parent:** [Twistor Hermitian form](twistor-hermitian-form.md)

A nonzero twistor null for the [twistor Hermitian form](twistor-hermitian-form.md). Its projective class determines a real [null geodesic](null-geodesic.md) in compactified Minkowski space. For nonzero $\pi$, its affine real solutions of the [twistor incidence relation](twistor-incidence-relation.md) have the form $x=x_0+r\overline\pi^A\pi^{A'}$, $r\in\mathbb R$. Twistors with $\pi=0$ encode generators at infinity rather than finite affine solutions.
