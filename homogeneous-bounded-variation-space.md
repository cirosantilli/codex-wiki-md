# Homogeneous bounded-variation space

↑ **Parent:** [Function of bounded variation on a domain](function-of-bounded-variation-on-a-domain.md)

In the $L^2$ formulation of [total variation denoising](total-variation-denoising.md) on the entire plane, one may allow finite distributional [total variation seminorm on a domain](total-variation-seminorm-on-a-domain.md) without requiring global $L^1$ membership. Its extended penalty is a supremum of [continuous](continuous-function.md) test-function pairings and hence is [sequentially lower semicontinuous](sequential-lower-semicontinuity.md). This domain can be larger than $BV(\mathbb R^2)\cap L^2$, since the usual [bounded-variation space](function-of-bounded-variation-on-a-domain.md) includes an $L^1$ condition.
