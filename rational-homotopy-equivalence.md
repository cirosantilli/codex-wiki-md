# Rational homotopy equivalence

↑ **Parent:** [Rationalization of a topological space](rationalization-of-a-topological-space.md)

A map between [simply connected](simply-connected-space.md) spaces is a [rational homotopy equivalence](rational-homotopy-equivalence.md) when it induces isomorphisms $\pi_n(X)\otimes\mathbb Q\to\pi_n(Y)\otimes\mathbb Q$ in every degree. Equivalently it induces an equivalence of their rationalizations, or a [rational homology](rational-homology.md) isomorphism by the [rational Whitehead theorem](rational-whitehead-theorem.md). Spaces have the same [rational homotopy type](rational-homotopy-type.md) when they are connected by a zigzag of such maps. This is distinct from [rational homotopy equivalence](rational-homotopy-equivalence.md) of algebraic cycles.
