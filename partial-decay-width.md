# Partial decay width

↑ **Parent:** [Decay width](decay-width.md)

The contribution of a specified decay channel to the total [decay width](decay-width.md). In an exponential-decay description, its probability rate is $\lambda_i=\Gamma_i/\hbar$, the total width is $\Gamma=\sum_i\Gamma_i=\hbar/\tau$, and its [branching fraction](branching-fraction.md) is $\Gamma_i/\Gamma$. Channels must be specified consistently, including any inclusive treatment of unresolved radiation. A [partial decay width](partial-decay-width.md) has [energy](energy-split.md) units rather than inverse-time units.
