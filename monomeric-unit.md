# Monomeric unit

↑ **Parent:** [Monomer](monomer.md)

An incorporated [monomeric unit](monomeric-unit.md) is the largest constitutional unit identifiable as derived from one [monomer molecule](monomer-molecule.md). It need not be a whole unreacted [molecule](molecule-split.md) or the smallest [constitutional repeating unit](repeat-unit.md). See [IUPAC: monomeric unit](https://doi.org/10.1515/pac-2023-0304).
