# Inverse infinity-norm bound from diagonal dominance

↑ **Parent:** [Strictly diagonally dominant matrix](strictly-diagonally-dominant-matrix.md)

If $\delta=\min_i(|a_{ii}|-\sum_{j\ne i}|a_{ij}|)>0$, then $A$ is invertible and its [operator norm](operator-norm.md) on $\ell^\infty$ satisfies the displayed bound. For $Ax=b$, choose $i$ maximizing $|x_i|$. The [reverse triangle inequality](reverse-triangle-inequality.md) gives $|b_i|\ge\delta\|x\|_{\ell^\infty}$. Taking $b=0$ proves injectivity and hence invertibility in finite dimensions; taking arbitrary $b$ proves the bound. The estimate is useful for converting [strict diagonal dominance](strictly-diagonally-dominant-matrix.md) into quantitative [matrix inverse](matrix-inverse.md) stability.
