# Neutral axis

↑ **Parent:** [Bending moment](bending-moment.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neutral_axis)

The [neutral axis](neutral-axis.md) is the line in the cross-section of an [elastic beam](elastic-beam.md) where longitudinal bending [strain](strain.md) vanishes. For a homogeneous beam in pure bending, zero resultant longitudinal [force](force-split.md) gives $\int_Az\,dA=0$, so the [neutral axis](neutral-axis.md) passes through the [centroid](centroid.md). Fibres on its opposite sides are stretched and compressed. The [second moment of area](second-moment-of-area.md) about this axis controls the relation between [bending moment](bending-moment.md) and [curvature](curvature.md).
