# Spherical cap area upper bound

↑ **Parent:** [Spherical cap](spherical-cap.md)

On the [unit sphere](unit-sphere.md) $S^{n-1}$, let $D_\rho$ be a [spherical cap](spherical-cap.md) of angular radius $0<\rho<\pi/2$. If $\sin\rho\geq1/\sqrt n$, its [surface area](surface-area.md) is at most $s_{n-1}\sin^n\rho$. This includes $\rho\geq\pi/4$ for every $n\geq2$. The bound follows by comparing the cap-area derivative with the derivative of $\sin^n\rho$ and checking the two endpoint values.
