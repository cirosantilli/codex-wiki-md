# Secular term

↑ **Parent:** [Method of multiple scales](method-of-multiple-scales.md)

A secular term grows in a nominal perturbation correction and eventually invalidates an otherwise ordered expansion. In the [method of multiple scales](method-of-multiple-scales.md), a solvability condition removes resonant forcing that would generate such growth.
