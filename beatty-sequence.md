# Beatty sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Sequence](sequence.md)

The sequence $\lfloor n\alpha\rfloor$ for $n=1,2,\ldots$, with a fixed positive [irrational number](irrational-number.md) $\alpha$. Complementary [Beatty sequences](beatty-sequence.md) for $\alpha,\beta>1$ partition the [positive integers](positive-integer.md) when $1/\alpha+1/\beta=1$.
