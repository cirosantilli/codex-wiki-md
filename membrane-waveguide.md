# Membrane waveguide

↑ **Parent:** [Wave equation](wave-equation-split.md)

A strip of membrane with fixed edges $y=0,b$ supports transverse [normal modes](normal-mode.md) proportional to $\sin(n\pi y/b)$, $n\geq1$. Separation of the two-dimensional [wave equation](wave-equation-split.md) gives the displayed [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation-split.md). Confinement produces longitudinal [wave dispersion](wave-dispersion.md) even when the membrane's underlying [wave speed](wave-speed.md) $v$ is independent of [frequency](frequency.md).
