# Stopping angle under constant resisting torque

↑ **Parent:** [Torque](torque.md)

A rigid body's axial [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) $I$ and a constant resisting [torque](torque.md) $\tau_f>0$ give constant [angular acceleration](angular-acceleration.md) $-\tau_f/I$. Hence $t_{\rm stop}=I\omega_0/\tau_f$ and the [angular displacement](angular-displacement.md) is the displayed expression. Equivalently, the lost [rotational kinetic energy](rotational-kinetic-energy.md) $I\omega_0^2/2$ equals the resisting work $\tau_f\Delta\phi$. The total number of revolutions is $\Delta\phi/(2\pi)$; the number of whole revolutions completed is its [floor function](floor-function.md).
