# Christoffel trace identity

↑ **Parent:** [Christoffel symbol](christoffel-symbol.md)

For a nondegenerate [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md) and its [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md), contracting the [Christoffel symbol](christoffel-symbol.md) formula gives $\Gamma^\rho{}_{\rho\mu}=\tfrac12g^{ab}\partial_\mu g_{ab}$. The [Jacobi determinant derivative formula](jacobi-determinant-derivative-formula.md) identifies this with $\partial_\mu\log\sqrt{|\det g|}$. The volume density, rather than the determinant without an absolute value, works in every fixed metric signature.
