# Ferrel cell

↑ **Parent:** [Atmospheric circulation](atmospheric-circulation.md)

Named after [William Ferrel](william-ferrel.md), the thermally indirect middle-latitude mean atmospheric overturning: near-surface flow is poleward and the upper return equatorward. It is maintained largely by [eddy](eddy.md) forcing and surface stress. Its [Eulerian mean](eulerian-mean-flow.md) thermal transport can oppose the poleward [eddy](eddy.md) contribution; it is not a second thermally direct [Hadley cell](hadley-cell.md).
