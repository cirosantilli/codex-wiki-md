# Decidable well-order

↑ **Parent:** [Well-order](well-order.md)

A [decidable well-order](decidable-well-order.md) on the [natural numbers](natural-number.md) is a [well-order](well-order.md) whose comparison relation is computable. Comparing two codes is an effective finite task; the proof that the relation has no infinite descending chain is a separate mathematical assertion. [Computable Cantor normal form notation](computable-cantor-normal-form-notation.md) gives such an order of type [epsilon zero](epsilon-zero.md).
