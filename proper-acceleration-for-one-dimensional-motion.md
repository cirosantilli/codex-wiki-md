# Proper acceleration for one-dimensional motion

↑ **Parent:** [Proper acceleration](proper-acceleration.md)

For motion on a fixed axis, the signed spatial [four-acceleration](four-acceleration.md) in the instantaneous rest [inertial frame](inertial-frame.md) is $a=\gamma^3\dot u$. Its magnitude is the scalar [proper acceleration](proper-acceleration.md). Constant signed $a$ from rest gives $\gamma u=at$ and the [hyperbolic motion](hyperbolic-motion.md) worldline $x=(c^2/a)(\sqrt{1+a^2t^2/c^2}-1)$.
