# Hemiketal

↑ **Parent:** [Hemiacetal](hemiacetal.md)

A [hemiketal](hemiketal.md) is the addition product of an [alcohol](alcohol.md) and a [ketone](ketone.md), with [hydroxy group](hydroxy-group.md) and alkoxy substituents on the former [carbonyl](carbonyl-group.md) [carbon](carbon.md). Intramolecular formation can temporarily tether an [alcohol](alcohol.md) to a [ketone](ketone.md) before another [chemical reaction](chemical-reaction-split.md) changes the carbon skeleton.
