# Explosion radiation in an isotropic elastic solid

↑ **Parent:** [Seismic moment tensor](seismic-moment-tensor.md)

An isotropic point moment is a dilatational gradient source. Its radial projection gives direction-independent [P wave](p-wave.md) radiation, whereas its transverse projection vanishes. Thus it produces no far-field [S wave](s-wave.md) in a homogeneous [isotropic](isotropy.md) solid; conversion at an interface can change that conclusion.
