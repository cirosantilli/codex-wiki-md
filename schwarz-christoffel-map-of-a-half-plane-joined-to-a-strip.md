# Schwarz-Christoffel map of a half-plane joined to a strip

↑ **Parent:** [Schwarz-Christoffel mapping](schwarz-christoffel-mapping.md)

The [upper half-plane](upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md) joined to the downward strip $-1<x<1$ has reentrant corners at $\pm1$. The [Schwarz-Christoffel formula](schwarz-christoffel-mapping.md) gives $z'(Z)=2R/(\pi Z)$, with $R\sim Z$ at upper infinity. Since $(R+\tan^{-1}(1/R))'=R/Z$, integration gives the displayed map. The [inverse trigonometric function](inverse-trigonometric-functions.md) is continued analytically in the [upper half-plane](upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md) so its corner values are $\pm\pi/2$. The origin is the lower channel end and infinity the upper open end. The complex potentials $2Z/\pi$ and $2\log Z/\pi$ respectively normalize unit horizontal [velocity](velocity.md) in the upper region and unit upward [velocity](velocity.md) in the channel.
