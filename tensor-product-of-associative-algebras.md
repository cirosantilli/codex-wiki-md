# Tensor product of associative algebras

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor product](tensor-product.md)

For two unital [associative algebras](associative-algebra-split.md) over a [field](field.md) $k$, their [tensor product](tensor-product.md) carries the multiplication in the title, extended by [bilinearity](bilinearity.md), with identity $1\otimes1$. Choosing a [basis](basis.md) $(b_j)$ of the second factor gives the [basis of a module](basis-of-a-module.md) $(1\otimes b_j)$ for the resulting left $A$-[module](module-mathematics.md); [dual basis](dual-basis.md) functionals recover the coefficients uniquely.
