# Tsen theorem

↑ **Parent:** [C1 field](c1-field.md)

The function field of a curve over an [algebraically closed field](algebraically-closed-field.md) is a [C1 field](c1-field.md). Consequently its [Brauer group](brauer-group.md) is zero. This is the input for [cyclic splitting by extension of constants](cyclic-splitting-by-extension-of-constants.md) over a finite constant field.
