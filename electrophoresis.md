# Electrophoresis

↑ **Parent:** [Physical chemistry](physical-chemistry-split.md)

[Electrophoresis](electrophoresis.md) is motion of charged dispersed species relative to a surrounding medium under an applied [electric field](electric-field-split.md). The drift depends on electrical driving and friction, including the surrounding ionic atmosphere. [Gel electrophoresis](gel-electrophoresis.md) adds a porous matrix that makes migration sensitive to molecular size and conformation.
