<h1 id="spearman-s-rank-correlation-coefficient">Spearman's rank correlation coefficient</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical association](statistical-association.md)

A [correlation coefficient](pearson-correlation-coefficient.md) computed from ranks, measuring monotone association. Ties need a stated ranking convention. A strong nonlinear but monotone relationship can have a strong [Spearman rank correlation](spearman-s-rank-correlation-coefficient.md) even when a straight-line summary in the original units is inappropriate.
