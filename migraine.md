# Migraine

↑ **Parent:** [Neurology](neurology.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Migraine)

Migraine is a neurological disorder that can be an outcome in a [clinical trial](clinical-trial.md) or an [observational study](observational-study.md). Its statistical association with an [exposure](exposure.md) does not alone establish a [causal effect](causal-effect.md).
