# Value in a lattice-ordered group

↑ **Parent:** [Convex lattice subgroup](convex-lattice-subgroup.md)

A value of $g\ne1$ is a [convex lattice subgroup](convex-lattice-subgroup.md) maximal among those omitting $g$. [Zorn's lemma](zorn-s-lemma.md) supplies a value: the union of a chain of convex lattice subgroups omitting $g$ still omits $g$. Such subgroups are prime. Their unique cover is the smallest convex lattice subgroup properly containing them.
