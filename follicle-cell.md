# Follicle cell

↑ **Parent:** [Oogenesis](oogenesis.md)

A somatic cell surrounding an [oocyte](oocyte.md). In [Drosophila melanogaster](drosophila-melanogaster.md), ovarian follicle cells interpret oocyte signals and contribute to eggshell pattern and later embryonic positional information.
