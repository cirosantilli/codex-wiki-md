# Physiological integrator

↑ **Parent:** [Homeostasis](homeostasis.md)

A [physiological integrator](physiological-integrator.md) combines regulatory inputs to select a response; it need not perform the mathematical [integral action](integral-action.md) used in [control theory](control-theory-split.md). In [thermoregulation](thermoregulation.md), the [hypothalamus](hypothalamus.md) participates in combining internal and [skin](skin.md) signals. The term describes a role, which can be distributed across interacting [biological cells](biological-cell.md), rather than a mandatory single comparator.
