# Snow insulation and summer ice protection

↑ **Parent:** [Maykut-Untersteiner model](maykut-untersteiner-model.md)

At fixed boundary temperatures, a snow cover decreases winter conductive heat loss through the resistance $h_s/k_s+h_i/k_i$ and inhibits [sea ice](sea-ice.md) growth. Its high [albedo](albedo.md) and persistence into summer reduce ablation. Therefore annual equilibrium thickness has competing snow sensitivities. The near cancellation at modest snow depth and thickening with persistent summer snow in the original [Maykut-Untersteiner model](maykut-untersteiner-model.md) are forcing-dependent responses, not a universal monotonically decreasing snow-thickness relation.
