# Parker instability

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic buoyancy instability](magnetic-buoyancy-instability.md)

[Parker instability](parker-instability.md) is an undular [magnetic buoyancy instability](magnetic-buoyancy-instability.md) of a gravitationally stratified atmosphere supported partly by [magnetic pressure](magnetic-pressure.md). A bent horizontal [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) can permit gas to drain along its [magnetic field lines](magnetic-field-line.md), leaving buoyant rising regions. [Magnetic tension](magnetic-tension.md) resists bending, so wavelengths and [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md) affect the instability threshold. For a [magnetized isothermal atmosphere](magnetized-isothermal-atmosphere.md), the unrestricted-wavevector threshold is given by the [magnetic buoyancy energy criterion](magnetic-buoyancy-energy-criterion.md).
