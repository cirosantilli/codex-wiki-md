# Infinitude of intermediate-height prime ideals

↑ **Parent:** [Prime ideals between a three-prime chain](prime-ideals-between-a-three-prime-chain.md)

If a Noetherian ring has finite [Krull dimension](krull-dimension.md) $d$, then it has infinitely many prime ideals of each height $n$ with $0<n<d$. In a prime chain of maximal length, apply [prime ideals between a three-prime chain](prime-ideals-between-a-three-prime-chain.md) to the terms of heights $n-1$, $n$, and $n+1$.
