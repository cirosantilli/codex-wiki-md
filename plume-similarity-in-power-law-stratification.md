# Plume similarity in power-law stratification

↑ **Parent:** [Boussinesq top-hat plume in a stratified ambient](boussinesq-top-hat-plume-in-a-stratified-ambient.md)

For $N^2=Kz^\beta$ and positive pure-power fluxes, the [similarity solution](similarity-solution.md) exponents are $q=3+\beta/2$, $m=4+\beta$ and $f=4+3\beta/2$. Increasing volume and momentum and decreasing positive [buoyancy flux](buoyancy-flux.md) require $-4<\beta<-8/3$. At fixed nonzero $K$ the endpoint coefficient limits are singular.
