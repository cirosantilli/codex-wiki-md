# Intersection cohomology

↑ **Parent:** [Intersection homology](intersection-homology.md)

In the Deligne-sheaf convention, $IH_{\bar p}^i(X)=\mathbb H^i(X,IC_{\bar p})$, with $IC_{\bar p}$ normalized in degree zero on the regular stratum. For a compact oriented $N$-dimensional pseudomanifold over a field, $IH_{\bar p}^i(X)\cong IH_{N-i}^{\bar p}(X)$ and it is dual to $IH_i^{\bar q}(X)$, where $\bar p(k)+\bar q(k)=k-2$. Thus the dual of lower-middle [singular chains](singular-chain.md) uses the upper-middle Deligne [sheaf](sheaf-mathematics.md). The two middle conventions agree on [Witt spaces](witt-space.md).
