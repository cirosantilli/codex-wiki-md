# Sudden approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum mechanics](quantum-mechanics-split.md)

When a [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) changes instantaneously by a finite potential step, the [quantum state](quantum-state.md) is continuous across the switching time. Its coefficients in the new energy eigenbasis are its overlaps with those eigenstates; the [Born rule](born-rule.md) gives their squared moduli as probabilities. The state need not remain an energy eigenstate of the new Hamiltonian.
