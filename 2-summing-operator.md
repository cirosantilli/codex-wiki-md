# 2-summing operator

↑ **Parent:** [Absolutely p-summing operator](absolutely-p-summing-operator.md)

A [bounded linear operator](continuous-linear-operator.md) $T:E\to F$ is 2-summing if $\sum_j\|Tx_j\|^2\le C^2\sup_{\phi\in B_{E^*}}\sum_j|\phi(x_j)|^2$ for every finite family; $\pi_2(T)$ is the least $C$. For operators between [Hilbert spaces](hilbert-space-split.md), this is equivalent to being a [Hilbert-Schmidt operator](hilbert-schmidt-operator.md), with $\pi_2(T)=\|T\|_{\mathrm{HS}}$. The upper bound follows by applying the [Hilbert-Schmidt norm](hilbert-schmidt-norm.md) ideal inequality to the column operator of the family; testing finite orthonormal families gives the reverse bound.
