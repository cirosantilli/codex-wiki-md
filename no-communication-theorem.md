# No-communication theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Reduced density matrix](reduced-density-matrix.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No-communication_theorem)

A trace-preserving operation on subsystem $B$ cannot change the [reduced density matrix](reduced-density-matrix.md) of a disjoint subsystem $A$ when its unobserved outcomes are averaged. Local measurements on $A$ therefore cannot reveal which operation was chosen on $B$.
