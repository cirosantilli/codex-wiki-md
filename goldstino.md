# Goldstino

↑ **Parent:** [Supersymmetry breaking](supersymmetry-breaking.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goldstino)

A [goldstino](goldstino.md) is the massless spin-one-half mode associated with spontaneously broken global [supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md). For [F-term](f-term.md) breaking, the [fermion](fermion.md) in a multiplet with nonzero [auxiliary field](auxiliary-field.md) transforms by an inhomogeneous term, $\delta G\supset\sqrt2\epsilon F$. In [supergravity](supergravity.md), the corresponding mode supplies the longitudinal degrees of freedom of a massive gravitino.
