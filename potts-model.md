# Potts model

↑ **Parent:** [Spin model](spin-model.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Potts_model)

The ferromagnetic [Potts model](potts-model.md) on a finite [graph](graph-split.md) assigns $q$ possible states to each [graph vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) and rewards neighbouring equal states by the displayed weight, for $\beta\geq0$. The interaction sum counts each unoriented edge once. The [Edwards-Sokal coupling](edwards-sokal-coupling.md) identifies its spin clusters with the [random-cluster model](random-cluster-model.md) at $p=1-e^{-\beta}$ and integer $q\geq2$.
