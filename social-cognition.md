# Social cognition

↑ **Parent:** [Social psychology](social-psychology.md)

The processes through which people represent other individuals, relationships and groups. [Social cognition](social-cognition.md) includes [social attribution](social-attribution.md), [emotion recognition](emotion-recognition.md) and [theory of mind](theory-of-mind.md), which need not rely on identical mechanisms.
