# Equal-characteristic complete discretely valued field

↑ **Parent:** [Teichmuller expansion](teichmuller-expansion.md)

If a complete discretely valued field $K$ has positive characteristic and perfect residue field $k$, its [Teichmuller lift](teichmuller-representative.md) is the unique ring section $k\to\mathcal O_K$. A choice of [uniformizer](uniformizer.md) $t$ then gives

$$
\mathcal O_K\cong k[[t]],
\qquad K\cong k((t)).
$$
