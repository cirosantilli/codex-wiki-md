# Projectile range under a ceiling

↑ **Parent:** [Projectile range](projectile-range.md)

A [projectile](projectile.md) launched from and returning to the floor beneath a horizontal ceiling of height $H$ has maximum [projectile range](projectile-range.md) $\sqrt{8H(u^2/g-2H)}$ when $0\leq H\leq u^2/(4g)$. For higher ceilings the maximum is $u^2/g$. This assumes uniform [gravitational acceleration](gravitational-acceleration.md), no [air resistance](air-resistance.md) and fixed launch [speed](speed.md) $u$.

Indeed, with $z=\sin^2\theta$, the apex condition is $z\leq2gH/u^2$ and $R^2=4u^4z(1-z)/g^2$. This concave [quadratic function](quadratic-function.md) is maximized at the admissible value nearest $1/2$. Thus the lower-ceiling expression must not be extrapolated past its regime. If touching the ceiling is excluded, the limiting value is a supremum instead of an attained maximum.
