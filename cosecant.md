# Cosecant

↑ **Parent:** [Trigonometric function](trigonometric-function.md)

The cosecant is $\csc z=1/\sin z$, wherever the [sine](sine.md) denominator is nonzero. The square of the [cosecant](cosecant.md) occurs in the derivative $d(\cot z)/dz=-\csc^2z$ of the [cotangent](cotangent.md).
