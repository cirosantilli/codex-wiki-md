# Circular-binary quadrupole radiation

↑ **Parent:** [Far-zone quadrupole strain](far-zone-quadrupole-strain.md)

Two equal masses $m$ orbiting their [centre of mass](center-of-mass.md) at radius $R$ have separation $2R$. [Newton's law of universal gravitation](newton-s-law-of-universal-gravitation.md) gives the displayed orbital frequency. Their [mass quadrupole moment](mass-quadrupole-moment.md) varies at twice that frequency, producing [gravitational radiation](gravitational-wave.md) with two [gravitational wave polarizations](gravitational-wave-polarization.md). For inclination $\iota$, its leading plus and cross amplitudes are proportional to $1+\cos^2\iota$ and $2\cos\iota$, respectively. Face-on radiation is circularly polarized; edge-on radiation is linearly polarized.
