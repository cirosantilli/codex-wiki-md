# Axisymmetric stresslet from a Stokeslet pair

↑ **Parent:** [Force-dipole flow](force-dipole-flow.md)

Opposite forces $\pm F\mathbf e$ applied to the fluid at $\pm(a/2)\mathbf e$ have no net force or torque. Their leading [Stokeslet](stokeslet.md) difference is $-Fa(\mathbf e\cdot\nabla)[J(\mathbf r)\mathbf e]$, equal to the axial [stresslet](force-dipole-flow.md) with strength $S=Fa$. Positive $S$ drives outward flow on the axis and inward flow in the equatorial plane, corresponding to a [pusher microswimmer](pusher-microswimmer.md); negative $S$ gives a [puller microswimmer](puller-microswimmer.md).
