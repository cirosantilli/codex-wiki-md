# Root-multiplicity criterion for unstable binary forms

↑ **Parent:** [Hilbert-Mumford criterion for the affine null cone](hilbert-mumford-criterion-for-the-affine-null-cone.md)

For $\operatorname{diag}(t,t^{-1})$, the monomial $x^{d-i}y^i$ has weight $d-2i$. Positive weights force divisibility by $x^{\lfloor d/2\rfloor+1}$. Conjugating the subgroup replaces $x$ by any nonzero linear factor. Thus a nonzero [binary form](binary-form.md) is unstable exactly when a projective root has multiplicity greater than half its degree. Equality gives zero weight and does not imply instability.
