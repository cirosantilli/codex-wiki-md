# Total influence

↑ **Parent:** [Influence of a variable](influence-of-a-variable.md)

The total influence is

$$
\mathbf I(f)=\sum_i\operatorname{Inf}_i(f)
=\sum_{S\subseteq[n]}|S|\widehat f(S)^2.
$$

This [Fourier-Walsh transform](fourier-walsh-transform.md) identity uses $\{-1,1\}$-valued $f$ and the flip-probability convention for [influence of a variable](influence-of-a-variable.md). For $\{0,1\}$-valued $f$, the right-hand side is multiplied by four. Indeed [Parseval's identity](parseval-identity.md) applied to the [discrete derivative of a Boolean function](discrete-derivative-of-a-boolean-function.md) gives $\|D_i f\|_2^2=\sum_{S\ni i}\widehat f(S)^2$; sum over coordinates and apply the chosen normalization.
