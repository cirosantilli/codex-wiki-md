# Carbon-nanotube Dirac mass

↑ **Parent:** [Nanotube zone folding](nanotube-zone-folding.md)

Circumferential momentum quantization of a [graphene](graphene.md) [Dirac cone](dirac-cone.md) produces nanotube dispersion $E_\pm(q_\parallel)=\pm\sqrt{(\hbar v_Fq_\parallel)^2+(\hbar v_Fq_\perp)^2}$. Comparing with a massive one-dimensional [Dirac Hamiltonian](dirac-hamiltonian.md) identifies the displayed [effective mass](band-effective-mass.md) and gap $2m_{\rm eff}v_F^2$. This is an emergent band mass, not a changed vacuum [electron](electron.md) rest mass. A quantization line through the valley gives zero mass in the ideal zone-folding model; curvature, strain and interactions can change that idealization.
