# Gradient catastrophe

↑ **Parent:** [Shock formation by characteristic intersection](shock-formation-by-characteristic-intersection.md)

A smooth solution undergoes a [gradient catastrophe](gradient-catastrophe.md) when a spatial [derivative](derivative.md) becomes unbounded while the solution itself remains bounded. For $u_t+q'(u)u_x=0$, [characteristics](characteristic-of-a-field.md) have $x=s+tq'(u_0(s))$ and $u=u_0(s)$; thus $u_x=u_0'(s)/[1+tq''(u_0(s))u_0'(s)]$. Vanishing of the [characteristic](characteristic-of-a-field.md) projection [derivative](derivative.md) gives the first such time. A [weak solution](weak-solution.md) with an [entropy condition](entropy-condition.md) can continue after this breakdown of the smooth description.
