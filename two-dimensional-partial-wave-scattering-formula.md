# Two-dimensional partial-wave scattering formula

↑ **Parent:** [Partial-wave scattering](partial-wave-scattering.md)

For a real central potential and the outgoing convention $e^{i\pi/4}f(\theta)e^{ikr}/\sqrt{kr}$, the [Partial-wave S-matrix](partial-wave-s-matrix.md) is $S_m=e^{2i\delta_m}$. The cosine form of the [Jacobi-Anger expansion](jacobi-anger-expansion.md) fixes $w_0=1$ and $w_m=2$ for $m\geq1$. Comparing the outgoing part of each phase-shifted radial cosine with the incident [plane wave](plane-wave.md) gives the displayed [scattering amplitude](scattering-amplitude.md). Integrating $|f|^2/k$ gives the displayed [scattering cross-section](scattering-cross-section.md), since the constant angular mode has squared norm $2\pi$ and each positive-order cosine has squared norm $\pi$. Reversing the multiplicities would already fail to reproduce the free [plane wave](plane-wave.md) at zero [scattering phase shift](scattering-phase-shift.md).
