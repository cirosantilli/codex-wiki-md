# Ideal Bose-gas heat capacity below condensation

↑ **Parent:** [Homogeneous ideal Bose gas condensation](homogeneous-ideal-bose-gas-condensation.md)

For quadratic three-dimensional dispersion below condensation, the excited-state thermal [energy](energy-split.md) is proportional to $T^{5/2}$. Differentiating at fixed particle number and volume gives the displayed [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md), with limiting value approximately $1.926Nk_B$ at $T_c^-$. The macroscopic ground occupation carries no excitation [energy](energy-split.md) in the shifted reference. The exponent differs from the linear-phonon result of an interacting gas.
