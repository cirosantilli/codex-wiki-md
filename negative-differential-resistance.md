# Negative differential resistance

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md)

A local negative slope $dV/dI<0$ of a [current-voltage characteristic](current-voltage-characteristic.md), where the inverse slope exists. This differs from negative $V/I$: a [tunnel diode](tunnel-diode.md) can have positive [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) and positive [electric current](electric-current.md) while $dV/dI<0$.
