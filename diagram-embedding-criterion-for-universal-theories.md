# Diagram embedding criterion for universal theories

↑ **Parent:** [Method of diagrams](method-of-diagrams.md)

A [first-order structure](first-order-structure.md) $A$ embeds into a model of $T$ exactly when $A\models T_\forall$. For the nontrivial direction apply the [compactness theorem](compactness-theorem.md) to $T\cup\operatorname{Diag}(A)$. If a finite diagram conjunction were inconsistent with $T$, its universal negation after replacing the new constants by variables would belong to $T_\forall$ and contradict its truth in $A$.
