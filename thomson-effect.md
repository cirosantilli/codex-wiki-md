# Thomson effect

↑ **Parent:** [Thermoelectric effect](thermoelectric-effect-split.md)

The [Thomson effect](thomson-effect.md) is distributed reversible heating or cooling when an [electric current](electric-current.md) flows through a homogeneous material with a [temperature gradient](temperature-gradient.md). It is distinct from junction [Peltier effect](peltier-effect.md) heating and even-in-current [Joule heating](joule-heating.md).
