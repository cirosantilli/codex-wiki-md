# Electric battery

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical circuit](electrical-circuit.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electric_battery)

An [electric battery](electric-battery.md) supplies [energy](energy.md) to an [electrical circuit](electrical-circuit.md) by moving [electric charge](electric-charge.md) through a terminal [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md). In the ideal fixed-voltage model used in [fixed-voltage capacitor energy balance](fixed-voltage-capacitor-energy-balance.md), it maintains a prescribed terminal [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) while [electric charge](electric-charge.md) flows to or from the [capacitor](capacitor.md).
