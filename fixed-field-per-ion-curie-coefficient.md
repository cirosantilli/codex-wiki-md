# Fixed-field per-ion Curie coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Curie's law](curie-s-law.md)

For independent localized angular-momentum-$J$ ions at fixed field, the weak-polarization mean moment is $\langle\mu_z\rangle=C_\mu/T$. Unlike the volume-susceptibility [Curie constant](curie-constant.md), this coefficient includes the magnetic field and no ion number density. The expansion requires $g\mu_B|B|J\ll k_BT$; the moment saturates toward $g\mu_BJ$ beyond that regime.
