# Cylindrical KdV equation

↑ **Parent:** [Korteweg-De Vries equation](korteweg-de-vries-equation.md)

The [cylindrical KdV equation](cylindrical-kdv-equation.md) in this normalization is $q_t+q_{xxx}+qq_x+q/(3t)=0$, for $t\ne0$. The additional time-dependent term distinguishes it from the ordinary [KdV equation](korteweg-de-vries-equation.md). A scaling-invariant solution has $q=t^{-2/3}F(xt^{-1/3})$. Substitution gives $F^{(3)}+FF'-(\xi F)'/3=0$. [Integration](integral.md) reduces the similarity problem to $F''+F^2/2-\xi F/3=C$. The [integration](integral.md) constant depends on supplementary conditions and cannot be set to zero without them. Use real cube roots separately on either sign of $t$.
