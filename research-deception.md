# Research deception

↑ **Parent:** [Research ethics](research-ethics.md)

Intentionally misleading participants or withholding relevant information about a study. Its necessity and risks require justification and appropriate review; less deceptive alternatives should be considered. [Research debriefing](research-debriefing.md) can explain what was withheld but does not automatically remove all consequences.
