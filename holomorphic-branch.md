# Holomorphic branch

↑ **Parent:** [Branch of a multivalued function](branch-of-a-multivalued-function.md)

A single-valued choice from a multivalued relation which is a [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function-split.md) on its [domain](domain-mathematical-analysis.md). For example, a [holomorphic branch](holomorphic-branch.md) of the [square root](square-root.md) of a nonvanishing [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function-split.md) $h$ is a [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function-split.md) $g$ satisfying $g^2=h$. On a [domain](domain-mathematical-analysis.md), any two such choices differ by a fixed sign, since their [continuous](continuous-function.md) quotient takes values in the [discrete set](discrete-subset.md) $\{1,-1\}$.
