# Cyclic splitting by extension of constants

↑ **Parent:** [Tsen theorem](tsen-theorem.md)

For the function field $K$ of a curve over a finite full constant field $k$, extend constants to $\overline k$. The resulting curve function field is $C_1$ by the [Tsen theorem](tsen-theorem.md), so every [central simple algebra](central-simple-algebra.md) splits there. The finite collection of coefficients of a splitting isomorphism descends to some finite constant extension $k^{\prime}/k$. Regularity makes $Kk^{\prime}/K$ cyclic, giving a finite cyclic splitting field.
