# Infinite-horizon crossing probability for Brownian motion with negative drift

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian motion with drift](brownian-motion-with-drift.md)

For $a,y>0$,

$$
\mathbb P\!\left(\sup_{u\geq0}(W_u-au)>y\right)=e^{-2ay}.
$$
