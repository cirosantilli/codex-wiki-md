# Photoionization rate

↑ **Parent:** [Photoionization](photoionization.md)

For isotropic [specific intensity](spectral-radiance.md) $J_\nu$, absorption cross-section $\sigma_\nu$, and threshold frequency $\nu_0$, the photoionization rate per target atom is

$$
\Gamma=4\pi\int_{\nu_0}^\infty\frac{J_\nu\sigma_\nu}{h\nu}\,d\nu.
$$

For a directed incident flux, replace $4\pi J_\nu$ by its [spectral flux density](spectral-flux-density.md). The factor $h\nu$ converts energy flux into photon flux. This rate alone does not imply [photoionization equilibrium](photoionization-equilibrium.md).
