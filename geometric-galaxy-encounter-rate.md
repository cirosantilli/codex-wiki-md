# Geometric galaxy-encounter rate

↑ **Parent:** [Galaxy merger](galaxy-merger.md)

A target [galaxy](galaxy-split.md) with geometric encounter cross section $\pi R^2$ in a population of number density $N$ has encounter rate $\Gamma=\pi R^2N\langle v_{\mathrm{rel}}\rangle$. A [Poisson process](poisson-process.md) gives merger probability $1-e^{-\Gamma T}$, approximated by $\Gamma T$ only for rare encounters. Counts of distinct pairs across the whole population additionally require a factor of one-half.
