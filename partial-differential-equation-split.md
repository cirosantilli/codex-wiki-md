# Partial differential equation

↑ **Parent:** [Analysis](analysis-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equation)

A [partial differential equation](partial-differential-equation-split.md) relates an unknown [function](function-split.md) of several independent variables to its [partial derivatives](partial-derivative.md). For $u(x,t)$, the notation $u_t=\partial u/\partial t$ means changing time while holding position fixed; $u_{xx}=\partial^2u/\partial x^2$ measures how its spatial slope changes. Unlike an [ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md), a [partial differential equation](partial-differential-equation-split.md) can describe an entire evolving profile rather than a single trajectory.

The [heat equation](heat-equation.md) $u_t=D u_{xx}$ smooths spatial variations, the [transport equation](transport-equation-split.md) $u_t+c u_x=0$ moves them, and [Laplace's equation](laplace-equation.md) $\Delta u=0$ describes a spatial equilibrium. Here $D>0$ is a [diffusivity](diffusion-coefficient.md), $c$ is a [velocity](velocity.md), and $\Delta$ is the [Laplacian](laplacian.md). The [periodic heat and transport comparison](periodic-heat-and-transport-comparison.md) makes the difference visible using identical [initial conditions](initial-condition.md).

A [partial differential equation](partial-differential-equation-split.md) usually supplies a local rule rather than a complete answer. For example, every $u(x,t)=A+Bx$ satisfies the [heat equation](heat-equation.md) with zero [PDE source term](source-term-in-a-partial-differential-equation.md); data select which profile is relevant. A complete problem specifies its [domain of a function](domain-of-a-function.md), coefficients, [initial conditions](initial-condition.md), [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md), and meaning of [solution of a partial differential equation](solution-of-a-partial-differential-equation.md). A [well-posed problem](well-posed-problem.md) has existence, uniqueness, and [continuous dependence on initial data](continuous-dependence-on-initial-data.md) in stated spaces; the equation alone guarantees none of these.[Victor Ivrii, Initial and Boundary Value Problems](https://www.math.toronto.edu/ivrii/PDE-textbook/Chapter1/S1.2.html)
