# Affine toric variety

↑ **Parent:** [Toric variety](toric-variety.md)

For a rational strongly convex cone $\sigma\subseteq N_{\mathbb R}$, the affine toric variety is

$$
U_\sigma=\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb C[\sigma^\vee\cap M].
$$
