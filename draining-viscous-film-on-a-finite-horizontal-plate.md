# Draining viscous film on a finite horizontal plate

↑ **Parent:** [Lubrication theory](lubrication-theory.md)

With $K=\rho g/(3\mu)$ and formal edge conditions $h(\pm L,t)=0$, the [lubrication theory](lubrication-theory.md) equation is $h_t=K(h^3h_x)_x$. Its positive separated long-time profile has

$$
h(x,t)=\left(\frac{L^2}{Kt}\right)^{1/3}H(x/L),\qquad
(H^3H')'=-\frac H3.
$$

Writing $C=B(4/5,1/2)$ in terms of the [beta function](beta-function.md), the centre height is $h_0^3=10\mu L^2/(\rho gC^2t)$. The [edge region of a draining viscous film](edge-region-of-a-draining-viscous-film.md) determines how the singular outer profile matches the flow over the physical edge.
