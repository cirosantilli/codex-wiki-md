# Corestriction functor for comodules

↑ **Parent:** [Comodule](comodule.md)

A [comonoid morphism](comonoid-morphism.md) $f:H\to K$ induces a [functor](functor.md) $f_*$ on right [comodules](comodule.md), replacing $\rho$ by $(1\otimes f)\rho$ and preserving underlying objects and arrows. For [bimonoids](bimonoid.md), it is strict monoidal exactly when $f$ is also a [monoid morphism](monoid-morphism.md).
