# Survival probability from an absorbing transition density

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet heat kernel on an interval](dirichlet-heat-kernel-on-an-interval.md)

A transition density with absorbing boundaries counts the joint event of survival up to [time](time-in-physics.md) $t$ and a specified final [position](position.md). Its integrated [mass](mass.md) is [survival probability](survival-probability.md), not generally one. Dividing by that [mass](mass.md) gives the endpoint density conditional on survival, a different object.
