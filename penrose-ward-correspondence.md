# Penrose-Ward correspondence

↑ **Parent:** [Twistor correspondence](twistor-correspondence.md)

A [holomorphic vector bundle](holomorphic-vector-bundle.md) on an appropriate twistor domain, trivial on its space-time incidence lines, determines a complex anti-self-dual gauge [gauge connection](connection-vector-bundle.md). Split its patching [matrix](matrix.md) on each line and apply the tangent vector fields $\pi^{A'}\partial_{AA'}$. They annihilate the unsplit twistor patching [matrix](matrix.md), so the two logarithmic [derivatives](derivative.md) of the factors agree and define a global degree-one [function](function-split.md) of $\pi$. It is consequently $\pi^{A'}A_{AA'}$. The inverse splitting [bundle frame](frame-of-a-vector-bundle.md) is parallel along each [alpha-plane](alpha-plane.md); commuting these parallel equations proves that the primed [gauge curvature](gauge-field-strength.md) [two-component spinor](two-component-spinor.md) vanishes. Reality conditions recover unitary Euclidean [gauge fields](gauge-field.md); Euclidean [instanton](instanton.md) bundles need triviality on the real [twistor lines](twistor-line.md), not on every possible complex line.
