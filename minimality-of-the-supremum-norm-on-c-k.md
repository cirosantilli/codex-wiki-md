<h1 id="minimality-of-the-supremum-norm-on-c-k">Minimality of the supremum norm on C(K)</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Algebra norm](algebra-norm.md)

Any submultiplicative [algebra norm](algebra-norm.md) on all of $C(K)$ dominates its [supremum norm](supremum-norm.md), even when the new norm is incomplete. Every [evaluation character](evaluation-character.md) extends continuously to the completion: the restrictions of the completion's [character space](character-space-of-an-algebra.md) form a closed subset of $K$, and an omitted point would give a nonzero function annihilated by an invertible function in the completion.
