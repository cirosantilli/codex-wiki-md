# Symmetric top

↑ **Parent:** [Rigid body dynamics](rigid-body-dynamics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symmetric_top)

A symmetric top is a [rigid body](rigid-body-dynamics-split.md) with two equal principal moments of inertia, conventionally $I_1=I_2$. Its third principal axis is its symmetry axis.
