# Lower half-plane

↑ **Parent:** [Complex analysis](complex-analysis-split.md)

The [lower half-plane](lower-half-plane.md) is the set of complex numbers with negative imaginary part. It is the reflection of the [complex upper half-plane](upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md) across the real axis.
