# Compatible nonlinear shear energy

↑ **Parent:** [Compatibility condition for pure nonlinear transverse waves](compatibility-condition-for-pure-nonlinear-transverse-waves.md)

An explicit objective [isotropic](isotropy.md) example is $\mathcal W=\mu(S-1)/2+h(S-1)^2/4+(\lambda+\mu)(J-1)^2/2$, with $J=\det A$, $S=\operatorname{tr}(A^{\mathsf T}A)-2J$, and $\lambda,\mu>0$, $h\ge0$. On $A=[(a,q,0)^{\mathsf T},e_2,e_3]$, $S-1=(a-1)^2+q^2$ and $J=a$, so $P_{11}=0$ at $a=1$ while the shear [nominal stress](nominal-stress-tensor.md) is the displayed cubic. The natural-state quadratic energy has [Lamé parameters](lame-parameter.md) $\lambda,\mu$, and the shear speed is $\sqrt{(\mu+3hq^2)/\rho_0}$.
