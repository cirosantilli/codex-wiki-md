# Fourier sample

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum Fourier transform](quantum-fourier-transform.md)

A Fourier sample is a [computational-basis measurement](quantum-measurement-in-the-computational-basis.md) outcome after applying a [quantum Fourier transform](quantum-fourier-transform.md) $F$ to a [quantum state](quantum-state.md) $|\psi\rangle$. Its distribution is given by the squared Fourier amplitudes. For a uniform periodic coset of spacing $r\mid N$, samples are the multiples $sN/r$ with uniform $s\in\mathbb Z_r$. Samples need not individually reveal the whole period: a [greatest common divisor](greatest-common-divisor.md) can remove factors from a single reduced denominator, motivating [two-sample exact period recovery](two-sample-exact-period-recovery.md).
