# Endomorphism ring of an elliptic curve

↑ **Parent:** [Isogeny of elliptic curves](isogeny-of-elliptic-curves.md)

The endomorphism ring of an elliptic curve consists of its self-isogenies together with the zero map, with pointwise addition and composition as multiplication.
