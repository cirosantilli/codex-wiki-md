# Hydrophone

↑ **Parent:** [Seismometer](seismometer.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrophone)

An underwater acoustic [pressure](pressure-split.md) sensor. A [hydrophone](hydrophone.md) streamer records fluid [P waves](p-wave.md); observing ground [S waves](s-wave.md) directly instead requires coupled solid-motion sensors.
