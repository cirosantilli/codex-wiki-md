# Dual connection

↑ **Parent:** [Connection (vector bundle)](connection-vector-bundle.md)

The [dual connection](dual-connection.md) is induced by a [connection on a vector bundle](connection-vector-bundle.md) through the evaluation pairing. A connection on $E$ induces one on $E^*$ by

$$
(\nabla_X\alpha)(s)=X(\alpha(s))-\alpha(\nabla_Xs).
$$

Its connection matrices are the negatives of the transposes of those for $E$.
