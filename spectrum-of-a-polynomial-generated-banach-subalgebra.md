# Spectrum of a polynomial-generated Banach subalgebra

↑ **Parent:** [Banach subalgebra generated by one element](banach-subalgebra-generated-by-one-element.md)

For $B=\overline{\mathbb C[1,x]}$ inside $A$, the displayed [set](set-split.md) is the [full spectrum](full-spectrum.md) of $x$ in $A$. On a bounded complementary component, the [maximum modulus principle](maximum-modulus-principle.md) gives $|p(\lambda)|\le\max_{\sigma_A(x)}|p|\le\|p(x)\|$. Evaluation $p(x)\mapsto p(\lambda)$ therefore extends to a [character of an algebra](character-of-an-algebra.md) on $B$, showing $\lambda\in\sigma_B(x)$. Conversely the unbounded resolvent component survives by [resolvent components in a closed unital subalgebra](resolvent-components-in-a-closed-unital-subalgebra.md). In particular, if zero lies in that component, $x^{-1}$ belongs to the [norm](norm.md) [closure](closure-topology.md) of [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) in $x$.
