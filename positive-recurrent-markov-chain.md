# Positive recurrent Markov chain

↑ **Parent:** [Recurrent Markov chain](recurrent-markov-chain.md)

An [irreducible Markov chain](irreducible-markov-chain.md) is positive recurrent when its mean return times are finite. For a [nonexplosive Markov chain](nonexplosive-markov-chain.md) on a countable state space, this is equivalent to the existence of a [stationary distribution](stationary-distribution.md) on its irreducible class. For continuous time, a return cycle includes departure and return, rather than counting immediate residence as a return.
