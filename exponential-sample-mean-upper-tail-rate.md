# Exponential sample-mean upper-tail rate

↑ **Parent:** [Cramér's theorem](cramer-s-theorem.md)

For [independent](independent-random-variables.md) unit-rate [exponential random variables](exponential-distribution.md), the [cumulant-generating function](cumulant-generating-function.md) is $-\log(1-\theta)$ for $\theta<1$. Its [Legendre transform of a cumulant-generating function](legendre-transform-of-a-cumulant-generating-function.md) is $I(x)=x-1-\log x$ for $x>0$ and infinite otherwise. The [Cramér theorem](cramer-s-theorem.md) gives the upper-tail logarithmic rate $-I(a)$ for $a>1$, and zero for $0\leq a\leq1$. At the mean threshold the infima on both the open and closed upper half-lines are zero; below the mean the [strong law of large numbers](strong-law-of-large-numbers.md) applies.
