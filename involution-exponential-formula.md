# Involution exponential formula

↑ **Parent:** [Eigenspace decomposition of a linear involution](eigenspace-decomposition-of-a-linear-involution.md)

For a linear operator with $A^2=I$, every even power is $I$ and every odd power is $A$. Separating the absolutely convergent [matrix exponential](matrix-exponential.md) series into those powers proves the displayed identity without a Hermiticity assumption. If $A$ is also a [Hermitian operator](hermitian-operator.md), the exponential is a [unitary operator](unitary-operator.md).
