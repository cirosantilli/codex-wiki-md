# Inclusion map

↑ **Parent:** [Topological space](topological-space.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inclusion_map)

For a subspace $A\subseteq X$, the inclusion map is the [injective function](injective-function.md) $i:A\to X$ given by $i(a)=a$.
