# MINOS

↑ **Parent:** [Neutrino oscillation](neutrino-oscillation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MINOS)

A long-baseline [neutrino oscillation](neutrino-oscillation.md) experiment using a Fermilab beam, near and far detectors, and magnetized iron/scintillator sampling [calorimeters](calorimeter.md). A [charged current](charged-current.md) [muon neutrino](muon-neutrino.md) event gives a negative [muon](muon.md) and a [hadronic shower](hadronic-shower.md); their reconstructed [energies](energy-split.md) estimate the incident [neutrino](neutrino.md) [energy](energy-split.md).
