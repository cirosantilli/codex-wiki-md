# Outlet layer in a deep unconfined aquifer

↑ **Parent:** [Unconfined aquifer with depth-dependent permeability](unconfined-aquifer-with-depth-dependent-permeability.md)

Even when most of an [unconfined aquifer with depth-dependent permeability](unconfined-aquifer-with-depth-dependent-permeability.md) is deep, its height vanishes at an absorbing outlet. Near that outlet, finite outward [volume flux per unit width](volume-flux-per-unit-width.md) $Q$ gives $u_bhh_x\simeq Q$ and hence $h\simeq(2Qx/u_b)^{1/2}$. The high-depth outer approximation instead has $h\simeq[6Qx/(u_b\beta)]^{1/3}$. Their crossover lies at height of order $1/\beta$ and distance of order $u_b/(\beta^2Q)$; the thin inner [boundary layer](boundary-layer.md) transmits the same leading discharge.
