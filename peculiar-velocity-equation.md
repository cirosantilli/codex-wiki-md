# Peculiar-velocity equation

↑ **Parent:** [Peculiar velocity](peculiar-velocity.md)

For a fluid in an expanding universe, the peculiar-velocity equation is the [Euler equations for an inviscid fluid](euler-equations-for-an-inviscid-fluid.md) after decomposing physical velocity into [Hubble flow](hubble-flow.md) and [peculiar velocity](peculiar-velocity.md). It contains the Hubble-drag term $H\mathbf v$ and force $-\nabla\Phi/a$ from the [peculiar gravitational potential](peculiar-gravitational-potential.md).
