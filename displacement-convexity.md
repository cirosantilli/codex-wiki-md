# Displacement convexity

↑ **Parent:** [Displacement interpolation](displacement-interpolation.md)

An energy has $\lambda$-displacement convexity when the displayed inequality holds along every [displacement interpolation](displacement-interpolation.md) between endpoints in its domain. The case $\lambda=0$ is displacement convexity; $\lambda>0$ gives uniform convexity along these [metric geodesics](metric-geodesic.md). This is distinct from [convexity](convex-function.md) along ordinary linear mixtures of [probability measures](probability-measure.md). On subintervals, the inequality is equivalent to [convexity](convex-function.md) of $t\mapsto E(\mu_t)-\lambda W_2(\mu_0,\mu_1)^2t^2/2$; if the energy is twice differentiable, it is equivalent to the corresponding [second derivative](second-derivative.md) lower bound. Without that regularity the lower bound is interpreted using [distributions](distribution-mathematical-analysis.md).
