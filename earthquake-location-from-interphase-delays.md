# Earthquake location from interphase delays

↑ **Parent:** [Earthquake location by arrival times](earthquake-location-by-arrival-times.md)

In a homogeneous medium, two phases along the same geometric path have delay $\Delta t=KR$, where $K=1/v_B-1/v_A$. The squared delay is a quadratic in station position. Its vertex locates the [epicentre](epicenter.md), while the ratio of intercept to quadratic coefficient gives squared depth. These spatial parameters do not depend on a particular assigned speed ratio if both speeds are inferred from the same data. Local clock offsets cancel, although uncertainties in the velocity model do not.
