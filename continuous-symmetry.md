# Continuous symmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetry (physics)](symmetry-physics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuous_symmetry)

A continuous symmetry belongs to a group whose parameters can vary continuously. Spontaneous breaking of such a symmetry can produce [Goldstone bosons](goldstone-boson.md).
