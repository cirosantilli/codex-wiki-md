# Clique conflict graph

↑ **Parent:** [Edge-disjoint clique packing](edge-disjoint-clique-packing.md)

The clique conflict graph has one [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) for each $r$-[clique](clique-graph-theory.md) of a fixed [graph](graph-split.md), and joins two when they share an [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md). Its [independent sets](independent-set-graph-theory.md) correspond exactly to [edge-disjoint clique packings](edge-disjoint-clique-packing.md). The [Caro-Wei bound](caro-wei-bound.md) converts clique-count and overlap estimates into a packing lower bound.
