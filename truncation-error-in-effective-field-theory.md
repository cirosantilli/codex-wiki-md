# Truncation error in effective field theory

↑ **Parent:** [Power counting in quantum field theory](power-counting-in-quantum-field-theory.md)

The [truncation error in effective field theory](truncation-error-in-effective-field-theory.md) is the uncertainty from omitted orders of a stated energy, derivative or loop expansion. It is commonly estimated from the first omitted terms using an explicit assumption about [Wilson coefficients](wilson-coefficient.md). Small energy alone does not justify ignoring an anomalously large coupling or a nearby omitted pole.
