# Polymer melt

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer physics](polymer-physics.md)

A polymer melt is a dense fluid of [polymer](polymer-split.md) chains with little or no solvent. For a long flexible chain in a homogeneous melt, other chains screen its long-range [excluded volume](excluded-volume.md) interactions: distant segments of the chosen chain displace statistically similar segments belonging to its neighbours. On scales above local stiffness, equilibrium chain dimensions are consequently approximately those of an [ideal chain](ideal-chain.md), even though monomers still cannot physically overlap. Entanglements can strongly affect dynamics without changing this leading large-scale equilibrium size law. Crystallinity, strong orientational order or unusual topological constraints require separate treatment.
