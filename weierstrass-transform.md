# Weierstrass transform

↑ **Parent:** [Integral transform](integral-transform.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weierstrass_transform)

The [Weierstrass transform](weierstrass-transform.md) is [Gaussian](normal-distribution.md) [convolution](convolution.md), $Wf(x)=(4\pi)^{-1/2}\int_{\mathbb R}e^{-(x-y)^2/4}f(y)\,dy$. It smooths appropriate functions and is the [heat semigroup](heat-semigroup.md) at unit time. The [Brownian transition semigroup](brownian-transition-semigroup.md) uses variance-$t$ Gaussian kernels and therefore supplies the scaled time family.
