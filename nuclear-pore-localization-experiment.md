# Nuclear pore localization experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear pore complex](nuclear-pore-complex.md)

In a 1982 experiment, purified [nucleoplasmin](nucleoplasmin.md) was labeled with radioactive [iodine](iodine.md) and cleaved by [proteolysis](proteolysis.md). [Microinjection](microinjection.md) delivered cargo into [Xenopus laevis](xenopus-laevis.md) [oocyte](oocyte.md) [cytoplasm](cytoplasm.md). Manually separated [cell nuclei](cell-nucleus.md) and [cytoplasm](cytoplasm.md) were compared by [gel electrophoresis](gel-electrophoresis.md) and [autoradiography](autoradiography.md); section [autoradiography](autoradiography.md) checked internal localization. Tail-bearing cargo entered the [cell nucleus](cell-nucleus.md); tailless cores did not. Direct nuclear [microinjection](microinjection.md) retained the cores: the tails mattered for entry, but the assay did not identify a receptor.[Cell 30, 449–458: Figures 2–3, 5 and 7; The Trypsin-resistant Core Remains in the Nucleus after Nuclear Injection](a-polypeptide-domain-that-specifies-migration-of-nucleoplasmin-into-the-nucleus-by-colin-dingwall-and-others.md)
