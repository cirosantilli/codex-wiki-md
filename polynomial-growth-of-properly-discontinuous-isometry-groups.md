# Polynomial growth of properly discontinuous isometry groups

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial growth of a group](polynomial-growth-of-a-group.md)

A finitely generated group of [isometries](isometry.md) acting [properly discontinuously](properly-discontinuous-group-action.md) on a complete connected $n$-dimensional [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md) with nonnegative [Ricci curvature](ricci-curvature.md) has [polynomial growth of a group](polynomial-growth-of-a-group.md) of degree at most $n$. Choose $\rho>0$ so distinct points of an orbit are separated by more than $2\rho$. Let $L$ be the largest displacement of the base point by a generator. The radius-$m$ [word metric](word-metric.md) ball maps into the metric ball of radius $Lm$, with fibers of size the finite stabilizer $|\Gamma_p|$. Disjoint radius-$\rho$ balls around these orbit points have equal volume and fit inside the ball of radius $Lm+\rho$. The [Bishop-Gromov inequality](bishop-gromov-inequality.md) bounds their number by $(1+Lm/\rho)^n$, giving the displayed estimate.
