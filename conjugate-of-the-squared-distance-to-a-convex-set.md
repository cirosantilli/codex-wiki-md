# Conjugate of the squared distance to a convex set

↑ **Parent:** [Squared distance to a convex set](squared-distance-to-a-convex-set.md)

The [squared distance to a convex set](squared-distance-to-a-convex-set.md) is the [infimal convolution](infimal-convolution.md) of the set's [indicator functional of a constraint set](indicator-functional-of-a-constraint-set.md) and the half-squared [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) [norm](norm.md). Its [convex conjugate](convex-conjugate.md) is the sum of the half-squared [norm](norm.md) and the [support function](support-function.md) of the set.
