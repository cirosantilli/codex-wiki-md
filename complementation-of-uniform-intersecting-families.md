# Complementation of uniform intersecting families

↑ **Parent:** [T-intersecting family](t-intersecting-family.md)

Complementing all members preserves [cardinality](cardinality.md) and gives $|A^c\cap B^c|=n-2r+|A\cap B|$. Thus complementation bijects [t-intersecting families](t-intersecting-family.md) of $r$-sets with $(n-2r+t)$-intersecting families of $(n-r)$-sets. It swaps left and right [coordinate shifts of a set family](coordinate-shifts-of-a-set-family.md). When the transformed intersection parameter is positive, extremal families and their generating-support lemmas can therefore be compared in opposite compression directions.
