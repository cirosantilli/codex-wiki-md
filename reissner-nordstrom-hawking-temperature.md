# Reissner-Nordstrom Hawking temperature

↑ **Parent:** [Reissner-Nordstrom spacetime](reissner-nordstrom-spacetime.md)

For $f=(r-r_+)(r-r_-)/r^2$, near-horizon Euclidean geometry has polar form $d\rho^2+[f'(r_+)/2]^2\rho^2d\tau^2$. Smoothness gives inverse [temperature](temperature.md) $4\pi/f'(r_+)$ and the displayed formula. This assumes a nondegenerate [Killing horizon](killing-horizon.md) and time normalized at infinity. Its nonextremal limit is zero at $M=|Q|$.
