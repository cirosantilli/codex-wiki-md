# Ion-dipole attraction

↑ **Parent:** [Solvation shell](solvation-shell.md)

An [ion-dipole attraction](ion-dipole-attraction.md) couples an [ion](ion.md)'s [electric field](electric-field-split.md) to a [molecule](molecule-split.md)'s [electric dipole moment](electric-dipole-moment.md). In the elementary point-[electric dipole](electric-dipole.md) approximation, the interaction [potential energy](potential-energy.md) is $U=-\mathbf p\cdot\mathbf E$. An orientation with the [electric dipole moment](electric-dipole-moment.md) along the [electric field](electric-field-split.md) lowers this [energy](energy-split.md). For [water](water.md), the [oxygen](oxygen.md) end preferentially faces a [cation](cation.md), and the [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) end an [anion](anion.md). At nonzero [temperature](temperature-split.md), fluctuations and competing [hydrogen bonds](hydrogen-bond.md) prevent perfect alignment of every [molecule](molecule-split.md).[Chemistry 2e by Paul Flowers and others; “Ionic Electrolytes”, Figure 11.7 and its explanatory paragraph](chemistry-2e-by-paul-flowers-and-others/11-2-electrolytes.md)
