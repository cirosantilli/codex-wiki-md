# Waterflooding

↑ **Parent:** [Two-phase porous-media flow](two-phase-porous-media-flow.md)

Injecting [water](water.md) to displace oil through a porous reservoir. Spatial permeability contrasts cause preferential flow and early water breakthrough, while unswept slower regions retain oil. The recovery also depends on [fractional flow](fractional-flow.md), [capillary pressure](capillary-pressure.md) and phase mixing; a passive streamline model alone does not capture all two-phase effects.
