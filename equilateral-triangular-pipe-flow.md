# Equilateral triangular pipe flow

↑ **Parent:** [Viscous fluid flow](viscous-fluid-flow-split.md)

For a straight pipe with cross-section $0<x<a$, $|y|<x/\sqrt3$, steady unidirectional [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md) reduces the [Navier-Stokes equations](navier-stokes-equation.md) to $\mu\nabla_\perp^2w=p_z$, with constant axial [pressure gradient](pressure-gradient.md). The displayed cubic [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) vanishes on every wall, and its transverse [Laplacian](laplacian.md) is $p_z/\mu$. The axial drag per unit length on each of the three equal walls is $-p_za^2/(3\sqrt3)$. This follows by integrating the viscous [traction](traction.md) on one wall; summing the three [forces](force-split.md) equals the [pressure gradient](pressure-gradient.md) times the triangular [area](surface-area.md).
