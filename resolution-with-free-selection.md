# Resolution with free selection

↑ **Parent:** [First-order resolution](first-order-resolution.md)

A family of resolution calculi for [first-order Horn clauses](first-order-horn-clause.md). A selection function chooses either the positive head atom alone or a nonempty set of body atoms. Permitted inferences respect those choices; fair saturation is refutationally complete. Function symbols need not be excluded, so completeness does not by itself guarantee termination.
