# Supportive Parenting for Anxious Childhood Emotions

↑ **Parent:** [Parent involvement in youth anxiety treatment](parent-involvement-in-youth-anxiety-treatment.md)

A parent-based treatment that targets [accommodation](family-accommodation.md) and supportive responses to a [child](child.md)'s [anxiety](anxiety.md). A [randomised](randomization.md) trial compared it with [child CBT](cognitive-behavioral-therapy.md) using prespecified [noninferiority](noninferiority-trial.md) criteria; the tested age range and margins constrain the conclusion.[Journal of the American Academy of Child and Adolescent Psychiatry 59, 362–372; SPACE noninferiority trial, ages 7–14 and accommodation outcomes](parent-based-treatment-as-efficacious-as-cognitive-behavioral-therapy-for-childhood-anxiety-a-randomized-noninferiority-study-of-supportive-parenting-for-anxious-childhood-emotions-by-eli-r-lebowitz-and-others.md)
