# Buser finite length spectrum theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Length spectrum](length-spectrum.md)

For fixed genus $g\ge2$ and systole lower bound $\varepsilon>0$, a uniform finite length cutoff $L$ determines the entire unmarked [length spectrum](length-spectrum.md) of a closed [hyperbolic surface](hyperbolic-surface.md) in the thick part, including multiplicities. Equality up to $L$ forces equality everywhere. This is a finite-determination statement for spectra and does not claim that all isospectral surfaces are isometric.
