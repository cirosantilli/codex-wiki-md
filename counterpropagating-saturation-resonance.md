# Counterpropagating saturation resonance

↑ **Parent:** [Doppler-free saturation spectroscopy](doppler-free-saturation-spectroscopy.md)

Take a single isolated optical transition of [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) $\omega_0$. Both beams have laboratory [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) $\omega_L$, with [laser detuning](laser-detuning.md) $\delta=\omega_L-\omega_0$. Let the pump travel along $+z$, the probe along $-z$, $v$ denote an atom's $z$-[velocity](velocity.md), and $k=\omega_0/c$ be the approximate vacuum [wavenumber](wavenumber.md); $c$ is the [speed of light](speed-of-light.md). To first order in $v/c$, the [laser detunings](laser-detuning.md) in the atom's frame are

$$
\Delta_{\rm pump}=\delta-kv,\qquad \Delta_{\rm probe}=\delta+kv.
$$

The pump addresses atoms near $v=\delta/k$, while the probe addresses atoms near $v=-\delta/k$. For the same transition, simultaneous exact resonance requires both expressions to vanish: adding and subtracting gives $\delta=0$ and $v=0$. The selected atoms can still have substantial transverse [velocity](velocity.md). Finite [natural linewidth](natural-linewidth.md) admits a narrow interval of longitudinal [velocities](velocity.md); a warm gas has not been cooled.

This is velocity selection, not cancellation of each atom's [Doppler shift](doppler-effect.md) within a single absorption event. Changing [laser detuning](laser-detuning.md) selects different atoms for ordinary [photon absorption](photon-absorption.md), but only near line centre do these two selected classes overlap. [Laser cooling](laser-cooling.md) instead changes the distribution of the atoms' [velocities](velocity.md) through repeated momentum transfer.
