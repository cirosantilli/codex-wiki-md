# Subspace supplement with a prescribed intersection

↑ **Parent:** [Sum of vector subspaces](sum-of-vector-subspaces.md)

For [vector subspaces](vector-subspace.md) $U\subseteq W\subseteq V$ of a finite-dimensional [vector space](vector-space-split.md), such a $W'$ always exists. Extend a [basis](basis.md) of $U$ to one of $W$, then extend again to one of $V$. Let $W'$ be spanned by the original [basis](basis.md) of $U$ together with the vectors added in the second extension. Uniqueness of coordinates gives the displayed intersection and sum. Equivalently, choose a [direct sum](direct-sum.md) complement $C$ with $V=W\oplus C$ and put $W'=U\oplus C$. Necessarily $\dim W'=\dim V-\dim W+\dim U$. In $M_2(\mathbb R)$, the skew-symmetric subspace $U$ inside the traceless subspace $W$ admits $W'=\operatorname{span}\{I,\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\-1&0\end{pmatrix}\}$.
