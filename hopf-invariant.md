# Hopf invariant

↑ **Parent:** [Homotopy group](homotopy-group.md)

For $\phi:S^{2q-1}\to S^q$, $q>1$, its mapping cone has integral [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) generators $a$ in degree $q$ and $b$ in degree $2q$. The integer defined by $a\smile a=H(\phi)b$, with fixed cell orientations, is the Hopf invariant. It measures how an attaching map changes multiplication without changing the additive groups. The complex [Hopf fibration](hopf-fibration.md) has invariant one because its mapping cone is $\mathbb{CP}^2$; a constant attaching map has invariant zero.
