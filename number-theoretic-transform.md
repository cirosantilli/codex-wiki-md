# Number-theoretic transform

↑ **Parent:** [Discrete Fourier transform](discrete-fourier-transform-split.md)

A number-theoretic transform is a [discrete Fourier transform](discrete-fourier-transform-split.md) computed in [modular arithmetic](modular-arithmetic-split.md). In a [finite field](finite-field.md) with $p$ elements, choose a [primitive root of unity](primitive-root-of-unity.md) $\omega$ of order $L$ dividing $p-1$. The transform above is inverted using $\omega^{-1}$ and $L^{-1}$. With sufficient zero padding, it computes [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) products exactly modulo $p$, avoiding [floating-point arithmetic](floating-point-arithmetic-split.md) rounding.
