# Coprime-degree determinant extension theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Character extension](character-extension.md)

Suppose $N\triangleleft G$, $G/N$ is solvable, $\theta\in\operatorname{Irr}(N)$ is invariant under $G$, and $\theta(1)$ is coprime to $[G:N]$. If a [linear character](linear-character.md) $\mu$ of $G$ restricts to the [determinant character](determinant-character.md) $\det\theta$, then there is a unique [character extension](character-extension.md) $\chi\in\operatorname{Irr}(G)$ with determinant $\mu$. This is the solvable-quotient extension result supplied for the exam's application.
