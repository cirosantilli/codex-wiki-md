<h1 id="hua-s-lemma">Hua's lemma</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Weyl differencing](weyl-differencing.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hua's_lemma)

For $1\le j\le k$ and every $\eta>0$, the displayed estimate bounds a mean value of a polynomial [exponential sum](exponential-sum.md). For cubes its eighth-moment bound is $O_\eta(n^{5+\eta})$. It complements pointwise [Weyl differencing](weyl-differencing.md) estimates: a ninth moment over a region is bounded by its supremum there times this eighth moment. [Leiden lecture notes, Section 8.1](https://pub.math.leidenuniv.nl/~evertsejh/ant20-8.pdf) give the cubic formulation.
