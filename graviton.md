# Graviton

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum gravity](quantum-gravity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graviton)

A graviton is the massless spin-two quantum of the gravitational field. It occurs in the symmetric trace-free transverse sector of the first massless [closed string](closed-string.md) level.
