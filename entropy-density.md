# Entropy density

↑ **Parent:** [Entropy](entropy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entropy_density)

For a homogeneous equilibrium system with [energy density](energy-density.md) $\rho$, [pressure](pressure.md) $P$, [chemical potential](chemical-potential.md) $\mu$, and [number density](number-density.md) $n$, the entropy density is

$$
s=\frac{\rho+P-\mu n}{T}.
$$
