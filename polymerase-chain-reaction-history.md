# Polymerase chain reaction history

↑ **Parent:** [Polymerase chain reaction](polymerase-chain-reaction.md)

The 1993 Nobel chemistry announcement credits [Kary Mullis](kary-mullis.md) with inventing the [polymerase chain reaction](polymerase-chain-reaction.md) method and states that the technique was first presented in 1985. The reusable-product principle explains its power: each successfully copied interval can help specify the next generation. The award attribution is a historical claim; it does not imply that every improvement in [DNA polymerases](dna-polymerase.md), [thermal cyclers](thermal-cycler.md) or [PCR controls](pcr-controls.md) came from one person.[13 October 1993 announcement, award citation and introductory account of PCR first presented in 1985](press-release-the-1993-nobel-prize-in-chemistry-by-royal-swedish-academy-of-sciences.md)
