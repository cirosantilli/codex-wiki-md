# Exchange symmetry of two identical spin-one-half fermions

↑ **Parent:** [Fermion](fermion.md)

For two identical spin-one-half fermions with no additional antisymmetric internal factor, the antisymmetric [spin-one-half singlet state](spin-one-half-singlet-state.md) combines with an even-$\ell$ symmetric orbital state, while the symmetric [spin-one-half triplet state](spin-one-half-triplet-state.md) combines with an odd-$\ell$ antisymmetric orbital state.
