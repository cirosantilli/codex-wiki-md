<h1 id="gisin-s-theorem">Gisin's theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [CHSH inequality](chsh-inequality.md)

Every entangled pure state of two qubits violates a [CHSH inequality](chsh-inequality.md) for a suitable choice of local measurement axes. If its two [Schmidt coefficients](schmidt-coefficient.md) are $c_0,c_1>0$, the optimal construction already gives a value $2\sqrt{1+(2c_0c_1)^2}>2$.
