# Level sets of a linear numerator over a radial quadratic

↑ **Parent:** [Level set](level-set.md)

For a fixed nonzero $a\in\mathbb R^n$, a nonzero [level set](level-set.md) $f(x)=h$ is the sphere

$$
\left\|x-\frac a{2h}\right\|^2=\frac{\|a\|^2}{4h^2}-1.
$$

This follows by completing the square after multiplying by the positive denominator. Nonzero levels exist only for $|h|\leq\|a\|/2$. The zero level is the [hyperplane](hyperplane.md) $a\cdot x=0$; the extreme levels shrink to points $x=\pm a/\|a\|$. The [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) gives $|f(x)|\leq\|a\|\|x\|/(1+\|x\|^2)\leq\|a\|/2$, so these extrema are global. At them the [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) is respectively $-\|a\|I/2$ and $+\|a\|I/2$. In two dimensions the spheres are circles whose centres all lie on the axis spanned by $a$.
