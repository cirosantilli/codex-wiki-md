# Raman sideband cooling experiment of 1995

↑ **Parent:** [Resolved sideband cooling](resolved-sideband-cooling.md)

In 1995, [Christopher Monroe](christopher-monroe.md) and collaborators cooled one $^9\mathrm{Be}^+$ [ion](ion.md) in a ring/endcap [Paul trap](paul-trap.md). They used $313\ \mathrm{nm}$ [Doppler cooling](doppler-cooling.md) and [optical pumping](optical-pumping.md) into $|F,m_F\rangle=|2,2\rangle$. Red-sideband [Raman transitions](raman-transition.md) transferred population to $|1,1\rangle$, followed by dissipative recycling. Repeating across three directions yielded a reported three-dimensional [ground state](ground-state.md) population of about $92\%$.[Monroe and others, Resolved-Sideband Raman Cooling of a Bound Atom to the 3D Zero-Point Energy, 1995, pp. 4011–4013, Fig. 1 and Table I](resolved-sideband-raman-cooling-of-a-bound-atom-to-the-3d-zero-point-energy-by-christopher-monroe-and-others.md)

A variable-frequency [Raman transition](raman-transition.md) probe, an exchange pulse and a cycling detection beam converted state populations into [fluorescence](fluorescence-split.md) collected through $f/2$ optics onto a position-sensitive [photomultiplier](photomultiplier.md). Red/blue asymmetry inferred motional occupation; varying probe duration tested the thermal assumption. Off-resonant processes and independently observed heating limited cooling.[pp. 4012–4014, Fig. 2, Table I and final heating discussion](resolved-sideband-raman-cooling-of-a-bound-atom-to-the-3d-zero-point-energy-by-christopher-monroe-and-others.md)
