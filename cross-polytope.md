# Cross-polytope

↑ **Parent:** [Convex polytope](convex-polytope.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cross-polytope)

The [convex hull](convex-hull.md) of the positive and negative coordinate [unit vectors](unit-vector.md). Its boundary triangulates a [sphere](sphere.md), and each face contains at most one member of each antipodal pair of coordinate [unit vectors](unit-vector.md). This makes its [barycentric subdivision](barycentric-subdivision.md) useful for triangulating [Real projective space](real-projective-space.md).
