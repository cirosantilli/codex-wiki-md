# Characteristic zero

↑ **Parent:** [Characteristic of a field](characteristic-of-a-field.md)

A [field](field.md) has characteristic zero when no positive integer multiple of its identity vanishes. Equivalently, its [prime field](prime-field.md) is isomorphic to $\mathbb Q$. Every [irreducible polynomial](irreducible-polynomial.md) over such a field is a [separable polynomial](separable-polynomial.md).
