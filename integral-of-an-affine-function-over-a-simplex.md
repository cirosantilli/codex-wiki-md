# Integral of an affine function over a simplex

↑ **Parent:** [Centroid](centroid.md)

For a nondegenerate $d$-dimensional [simplex](simplex.md) $S$ with vertices $v_0,\ldots,v_d$ and an [affine function](affine-function.md) $f$, the integral equals the volume times the value at the [centroid](centroid.md) $c=(v_0+\cdots+v_d)/(d+1)$. To prove this, write $x=\sum_i\lambda_i(x)v_i$ in a [barycentric coordinate system](barycentric-coordinate-system.md). On the standard simplex, symmetry permuting the vertices gives equal integrals of all $\lambda_i$; since $\sum_i\lambda_i=1$, each is $\operatorname{vol}(S)/(d+1)$. An invertible [affine map](affine-map.md) transfers this result to any simplex. Affineness gives $f(x)=\sum_i\lambda_i(x)f(v_i)$, so [linearity of integration](linearity-of-integration.md) proves the formula. For a tetrahedron the factor is one quarter. The rule is exact for affine integrands; a nonlinear integrand generally needs additional moments.
