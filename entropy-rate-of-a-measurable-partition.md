# Entropy rate of a measurable partition

↑ **Parent:** [Entropy of a finite measurable partition](entropy-of-a-finite-measurable-partition.md)

The entropy rate of $\xi$ is

$$
h_\mu(T,\xi)=\lim_{N\to\infty}\frac1N
H_\mu\left(\bigvee_{n=0}^{N-1}T^{-n}\xi\right).
$$

The numerator is a [subadditive sequence](subadditive-sequence.md), so the limit exists and equals the infimum of the displayed ratios.
