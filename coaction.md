# Coaction

↑ **Parent:** [Comodule](comodule.md)

The structure map of a [comodule](comodule.md); a right coaction satisfies $(\rho\otimes1)\rho=(1\otimes\Delta)\rho$ with coherent parentheses, and $(1\otimes\varepsilon)\rho$ is the inverse right [unitor](unitor.md).
