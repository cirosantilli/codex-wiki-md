# Velocity-dependent collisional cascade

↑ **Parent:** [Collisional cascade](collisional-cascade.md)

For [catastrophic disruption threshold](catastrophic-disruption-threshold.md) $Q_D^*=Q_bD^b$, encounter speed $v_{\rm rel}=v_pD^p$, fixed belt volume, and small impactors, the [catastrophic projectile diameter](catastrophic-projectile-diameter.md) scales as $D_{\rm cc}\propto D^{1+(b-2p)/3}$. If $\alpha>1$, small destructive impactors dominate the [catastrophic planetesimal collision rate](catastrophic-planetesimal-collision-rate.md), giving

$$
R_{\rm cc}(D)\propto D^{2+p}D_{\rm cc}^{1-\alpha}.
$$

Mass per logarithmic bin scales as $D^{4-\alpha}$. [Constant mass flux in a collisional cascade](constant-mass-flux-in-a-collisional-cascade.md) then gives

$$
\alpha=\frac{21+b+p}{6+b-2p}.
$$

Size-independent speed and strength recover the [Dohnanyi collisional cascade](dohnanyi-collisional-cascade.md) slope $7/2$. A size-dependent belt thickness changes this derivation. The broader coupled size-and-velocity problem is studied by [Pan and Schlichting](https://arxiv.org/abs/1111.0667).
