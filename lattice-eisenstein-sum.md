# Lattice Eisenstein sum

↑ **Parent:** [Eisenstein series](eisenstein-series.md)

For a [period lattice](period-lattice.md) $\Lambda\subset\mathbb C$ and an integer $k>2$, this sum converges absolutely. Pairing $\omega$ and $-\omega$ makes it zero when $k$ is odd. For even weights it supplies the [Laurent coefficients of the Weierstrass elliptic function](laurent-coefficients-of-the-weierstrass-elliptic-function.md). Scaling the [period lattice](period-lattice.md) gives $G_k(a\Lambda)=a^{-k}G_k(\Lambda)$.
