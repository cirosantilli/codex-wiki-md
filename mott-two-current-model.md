# Mott two-current model

↑ **Parent:** [Giant magnetoresistance](giant-magnetoresistance.md)

When [spin-flip scattering](spin-flip-scattering.md) is weak, two spin populations have separately defined current responses and share a terminal electric field. Their [electrical conductivities](electrical-conductivity.md) add: $\sigma=\sigma_\uparrow+\sigma_\downarrow$, so $\rho=\rho_\uparrow\rho_\downarrow/(\rho_\uparrow+\rho_\downarrow)$. Spin-dependent band structure and scattering set the channel parameters; being the majority population does not universally imply lower channel resistivity. The model is named for [Nevill Francis Mott](nevill-francis-mott.md) and is the independent-channel basis for many [giant magnetoresistance](giant-magnetoresistance.md) models.
