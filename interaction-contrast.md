# Interaction contrast

↑ **Parent:** [Treatment contrast](treatment-contrast.md)

An [interaction contrast](interaction-contrast.md) compares a treatment difference across levels of another factor. A nonzero difference of differences shows that an additive representation of those cell means is inadequate. Its [standard error](standard-error.md) must use the error stratum in which that [interaction term](interaction-term.md) is randomized.
