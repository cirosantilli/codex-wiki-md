# Monodromy of hyperplane sections of a smooth curve

↑ **Parent:** [Monodromy action of a covering space](monodromy-action-of-a-covering-space.md)

For a nondegenerate embedded smooth [algebraic curve](algebraic-curve.md) over the [complex numbers](complex-number.md), the cover that chooses one point of a transverse hyperplane section has transitive [monodromy](monodromy.md). The analogous incidence space for two distinct ordered points is irreducible, giving two-transitivity. A loop around an ordinary tangent hyperplane exchanges two intersection points. Two-transitivity conjugates this transposition to every transposition, so the [monodromy](monodromy.md) is the full [symmetric group](symmetric-group.md). In particular its action on fixed-size subsets is transitive.
