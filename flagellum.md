# Flagellum

↑ **Parent:** [Biological cell](biological-cell-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flagellum)

An appendage used for movement or other functions. Bacterial flagella are driven by a [bacterial flagellar motor](bacterial-flagellar-motor.md); their construction differs from eukaryotic motile [cilia](cilium.md).
