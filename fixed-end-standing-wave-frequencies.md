# Fixed-end standing wave frequencies

↑ **Parent:** [Standing wave](standing-wave.md)

For the ideal uniform [wave equation on a string](wave-equation-on-a-string.md), the [wave speed](wave-speed.md) is $c=\sqrt{\tau/\mu}$, where $\tau$ is constant [tension](tension.md) and $\mu$ is [linear mass density](linear-mass-density.md). Assume small transverse slopes, negligible bending stiffness and no [damping](damping.md). Fix both ends at zero [displacement](displacement.md), giving the homogeneous [Dirichlet boundary conditions](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) $y(0,t)=y(L,t)=0$. Our [standing wave](standing-wave.md) already satisfies the first. For a nonzero [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md), the second requires $\sin(kL)=0$, hence

$$
k_n=\frac{n\pi}{L},\qquad \lambda_n=\frac{2L}{n},\qquad \omega_n=ck_n,\qquad f_n=\frac{\omega_n}{2\pi}=\frac{nc}{2L},\qquad n=1,2,3,\ldots.
$$

The value $n=0$ gives zero [displacement](displacement.md) in this sine family, not a vibrating [normal mode](normal-mode.md). Each positive $n$ fits $n$ half-[wavelengths](wavelength.md) into the string: it has $n$ [antinodes](antinode.md), $n-1$ interior [nodes of a standing wave](node-physics.md), and two endpoint [nodes of a standing wave](node-physics.md). For $L=1\,\mathrm m$ and $c=20\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$, $f_n=10n\,\mathrm{Hz}$; the third [normal mode](normal-mode.md) has $f_3=30\,\mathrm{Hz}$ and [wavelength](wavelength.md) $2/3\,\mathrm m$.

The equally spaced [eigenfrequencies](eigenfrequency.md) follow from this string's uniform [wave speed](wave-speed.md) and fixed-end [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md). They are not a universal spectrum for every vibrating object. Changing an endpoint constraint changes the allowed [mode shapes](mode-shape.md); changing [tension](tension.md) changes [wave speed](wave-speed.md). At fixed $L$ and $\mu$, quadrupling $\tau$ doubles every $f_n$. The more general construction of arbitrary initial motion belongs to [normal mode](normal-mode.md) and [wave equation on a string](wave-equation-on-a-string.md).
