# Muscovite

↑ **Parent:** [Silicate mineral](silicate-mineral.md)

A mica [mineral](mineral.md) with an idealized endmember composition $\mathrm{KAl_3Si_3O_{10}(OH)_2}$. Its formula contains one tetrahedral Al and two octahedral Al per formula unit. Under ideal independent [mixing on crystallographic sites](mixing-on-crystallographic-sites.md), an endmember [thermodynamic activity](thermodynamic-activity.md) includes all mixed sites rather than only a bulk [mole fraction](mole-fraction.md).
