# Frobenius monoid

↑ **Parent:** [Monoid object](monoid-object.md)

A [monoid object](monoid-object.md) $A$ with a [morphism](morphism.md) $\varepsilon:A\to I$ such that $\varepsilon m$ and a suitable coevaluation make $(A,A)$ a [dual pair in a monoidal category](dual-pair-in-a-monoidal-category.md). In a category of [vector spaces](vector-space-split.md) this is a [Frobenius algebra](frobenius-algebra.md). This condition alone is not the usual separability condition.
