# Nucleophile

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical reaction](chemical-reaction-split.md)

A chemical species or reactive group that donates an electron pair to form a new [covalent bond](covalent-bond-split.md). In serine-dependent [hydrolysis](hydrolysis.md), an activated oxygen can attack a carbonyl carbon.
