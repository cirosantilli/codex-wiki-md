# Kolchin theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Unipotent algebraic group](unipotent-algebraic-group.md)

Every unipotent algebraic subgroup of $\operatorname{GL}_n$ is conjugate to a subgroup of the upper unitriangular group. The filtration by vanishing initial superdiagonals then proves that every unipotent algebraic group is a [nilpotent group](nilpotent-group.md).
