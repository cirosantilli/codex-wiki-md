# Local conserved-charge hierarchy of sine-Gordon theory

↑ **Parent:** [Bäcklund generating current for sine-Gordon conserved charges](backlund-generating-current-for-sine-gordon-conserved-charges.md)

The Bäcklund expansion produces local differential-polynomial currents. In the coordinates $\xi=m(x+t)/2$, $\eta=m(x-t)/2$, their charges are $\int(P_j+Q_j)dx$ with vanishing boundary flux. Derivative improvements contribute no new charge. The first nontrivial higher current can be written $P=u_{\xi\xi}^2-u_\xi^4/4$, $Q=\cos u\,u_\xi^2$. Continuing, and exchanging light-cone directions, produces the infinite higher-spin hierarchy characterizing [classical integrability](classical-integrability.md).
