# Convex lattice subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Lattice-ordered group](lattice-ordered-group.md)

A [convex subgroup of an ordered group](convex-subgroup-of-an-ordered-group.md) that is also closed under [join](least-upper-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md) and [meet](greatest-lower-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md). A normal convex lattice subgroup is the kernel of the quotient [lattice-ordered group homomorphism](lattice-ordered-group-homomorphism.md).
