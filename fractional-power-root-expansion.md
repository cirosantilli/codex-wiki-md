# Fractional-power root expansion

↑ **Parent:** [Puiseux series](puiseux-series.md)

An algebraic root can depend on a parameter through fractional powers even when the polynomial coefficients have ordinary power series. For example, the roots of $t^3=\epsilon^2$ are on the scale $\epsilon^{2/3}$. [Dominant balance for algebraic roots](dominant-balance-for-algebraic-roots.md) identifies the exponent, and local expansion of the scaled root supplies successive coefficients.
