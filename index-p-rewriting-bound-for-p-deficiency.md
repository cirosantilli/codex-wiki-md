# Index-p rewriting bound for p-deficiency

↑ **Parent:** [P-deficiency](p-deficiency.md)

If $H$ is a [normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md) of index $p$ in a group presented by $\mathcal P$, the [Reidemeister–Schreier theorem](reidemeister-schreier-theorem.md) produces a presentation $\mathcal Q$ with

$$
\operatorname{def}_p(\mathcal Q)-1\geq p\bigl(\operatorname{def}_p(\mathcal P)-1\bigr).
$$

The preimage of $H$ in the [free group](free-group.md) has rank $1+p(d-1)$. A relator $w^{p^k}$ produces at most $p$ conjugate relators of weight $p^{-k}$ if its root lies in that preimage. If the root lies outside, $k\geq1$, and the coset conjugates are redundant up to conjugation; one relator of weight at most $p^{-(k-1)}$ suffices. Thus the total weighted relator cost grows by at most $p$. Convergent infinite sums are handled term by term.
