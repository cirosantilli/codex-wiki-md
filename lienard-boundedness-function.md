<h1 id="lienard-boundedness-function">Liénard boundedness function</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Lyapunov function](lyapunov-function.md)

For the Liénard system $\dot x=z-F(x)$, $\dot z=-g(x)$ with $F=x^3/3-2x$, $g=x^3-x$, and $V'=g$, the displayed function is coercive up to a constant. Differentiation gives $\dot W=-gF-z^2+Fz+xg\leq-z^2/2-5x^6/18+8x^4/3-x^2$. Its [derivative](derivative.md) is negative outside a [compact set](compact-space.md), providing bounded forward dynamics even though the original oscillator has negative damping near the origin. The coordinate $z=y+F(x)$ exposes this restoring and damping balance.
