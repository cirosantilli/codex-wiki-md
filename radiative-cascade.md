# Radiative cascade

↑ **Parent:** [Spontaneous emission](spontaneous-emission.md)

A radiative cascade is a sequence of downward photon-emitting transitions following excitation or recombination. Cascades feed lower excited levels and change their emitted line intensities. An [effective recombination coefficient](effective-recombination-coefficient.md) includes all relevant cascade paths under its stated optical-depth and density assumptions.
