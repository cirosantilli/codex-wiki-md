# Unitary flat holomorphic vector bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Holomorphic vector bundle](holomorphic-vector-bundle.md)

A [unitary representation](unitary-representation.md) of the [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md) of a connected [complex manifold](complex-manifold.md) gives a bundle $(\widetilde X\times V)/\pi_1X$ with locally constant unitary transition matrices. Its induced flat [unitary connection](unitary-connection.md) has $(0,1)$ part equal to its [Dolbeault operator](dolbeault-operator.md), so it is the [Chern connection](chern-connection.md) for the induced Hermitian metric. On a [compact Riemann surface](compact-riemann-surface.md), zero curvature gives degree zero; the subbundle curvature inequality makes the bundle semistable.
