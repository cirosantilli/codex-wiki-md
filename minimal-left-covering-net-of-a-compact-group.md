# Minimal left covering net of a compact group

↑ **Parent:** [Haar measure](haar-measure.md)

For an open identity neighbourhood $V$ in a compact [topological group](topological-group-split.md), a finite left covering net is a finite set $F$ such that the left translates $fV$, $f\in F$, cover the group. Compactness gives such finite sets; minimum cardinality gives a minimal one. Left translation preserves minimality because $G=FV$ implies $G=gFV$.
