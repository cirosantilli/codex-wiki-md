# Unit-circle root

↑ **Parent:** [Root of a polynomial](root-of-a-polynomial.md)

A [root of a polynomial](root-of-a-polynomial.md) lying on the complex [unit circle](complex-unit-circle.md). For a numerical [amplification polynomial](amplification-polynomial-of-a-multistep-method.md), a simple such root gives an undamped oscillatory mode. A repeated one supplies polynomial growth in the step index and fails the [root condition for a multistep method](root-condition-for-a-multistep-method.md).
