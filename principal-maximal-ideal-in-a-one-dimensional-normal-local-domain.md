# Principal maximal ideal in a one-dimensional normal local domain

↑ **Parent:** [Dedekind domain](dedekind-domain.md)

The maximal ideal of an integrally closed Noetherian local domain of Krull dimension one is principal. Hence every such local ring is a [discrete valuation ring](discrete-valuation-ring.md).
