# Circuit satisfiability problem

↑ **Parent:** [Boolean circuit](boolean-circuit.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circuit_satisfiability_problem)

The circuit satisfiability problem asks whether a [Boolean circuit](boolean-circuit.md) has an input on which its designated output is one. It is [NP-complete](np-completeness.md).
