# Contralateral delay activity

↑ **Parent:** [Electroencephalography](electroencephalography.md)

A sustained lateralized electrophysiological signal during visual-memory delays, typically measured as a difference between signals contralateral and ipsilateral to the attended visual field. Its load dependence and relation to behavioral capacity constrain models without fixing a universal storage count.
