# Restricted direct sum of groups

↑ **Parent:** [Direct product of groups](direct-product-of-groups.md)

The restricted direct sum of a family of [groups](group-split.md) $G_i$ is the subgroup of their [direct product of groups](direct-product-of-groups.md) consisting of tuples with only finitely many nonidentity coordinates. Multiplication is coordinatewise. If every factor is a [finite group](finite-group.md), each [subgroup](subgroup.md) that is a [finitely generated group](finitely-generated-group.md) lies in a finite subproduct, so the restricted direct sum is a [locally finite group](locally-finite-group.md). This gives concrete infinite [torsion groups](torsion-group.md) without a uniform bound on element orders.
