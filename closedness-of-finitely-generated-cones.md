# Closedness of finitely generated cones

↑ **Parent:** [Finitely generated cone](finitely-generated-cone.md)

A [finitely generated cone](finitely-generated-cone.md) is a [closed convex cone](closed-convex-cone.md). For a convergent sequence of represented vectors, use linearly independent active generators and pass to a subsequence with the same active set. Its coefficients converge through a fixed left inverse and retain nonnegativity. General linear images of closed cones need not be closed; finiteness of the generators supplies the missing argument.
