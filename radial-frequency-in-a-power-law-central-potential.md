# Radial frequency in a power-law central potential

↑ **Parent:** [Central force](central-force.md)

For [central potential](central-potential.md) $V(r)=\beta r^k$ and nonzero [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) $J$, a [circular orbit](circular-orbit.md) satisfies $\beta k r_0^{k+2}=J^2/m$, so attraction requires $\beta k>0$. Twice differentiating the radial [effective potential](effective-potential.md) $V+J^2/(2mr^2)$ gives $V_{\mathrm{eff}}''(r_0)=(k+2)J^2/(mr_0^4)$. Its [harmonic approximation](harmonic-approximation.md) therefore has radial [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) $\omega_r=\sqrt{k+2}|J|/(mr_0^2)$, whereas $|\omega_\theta|=|J|/(mr_0^2)$. This proves the ratio and shows stability precisely when $k>-2$. At $k=-2$ the tuned [effective potential](effective-potential.md) is flat; at $k<-2$ the radial disturbance grows exponentially. Rationality of this local [frequency](frequency.md) ratio is only a necessary condition for all nearby [orbits](orbit-dynamical-system.md) to close; [Bertrand's theorem](bertrand-s-theorem.md) makes a stronger finite-amplitude assertion.
