# Dirac membrane action

↑ **Parent:** [Brane](brane.md)

A relativistic membrane sweeps out a three-dimensional [worldvolume](worldvolume.md) with [induced worldvolume metric](induced-worldvolume-metric.md) $h_{ij}=\partial_iX^m\partial_jX^n\eta_{mn}$. Its leading geometric [action](action.md) is tension times its invariant volume. In static gauge in four-dimensional spacetime, one transverse field $Y$ gives $h_{ij}=\eta_{ij}+\partial_iY\partial_jY$, and the [matrix determinant lemma](matrix-determinant-lemma.md) gives $S=-T\int\sqrt{1+\partial_iY\partial^iY}$. Expanding supplies the translation-zero-mode kinetic term. A [domain wall](domain-wall.md) of finite thickness also has higher-curvature corrections outside the long-wavelength limit.
