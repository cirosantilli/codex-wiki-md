# Seifert matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Seifert form](seifert-form.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seifert_matrix)

A Seifert matrix represents the [Seifert form](seifert-form.md) in an integral basis of $H_1(F)$. Its skew-symmetrization $A-A^T$ represents the intersection form of the surface and is unimodular.
