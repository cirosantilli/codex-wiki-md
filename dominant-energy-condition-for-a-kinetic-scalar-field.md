# Dominant energy condition for a kinetic scalar field

↑ **Parent:** [Dominant energy condition](dominant-energy-condition.md)

For a [scalar field](scalar-field.md) with $X=-q^2/2$, Lagrangian $F(X)$ and [stress-energy tensor](stress-energy-tensor.md) $T_{ab}=F\prime q_aq_b+Fg_{ab}$, the inequalities $A=F\prime\ge0$ and $Y=XF\prime-F\ge0$ imply the [dominant energy condition](dominant-energy-condition.md) for any gradient type. In a local [Lorentz frame](lorentz-frame.md), $\rho=A(q_0^2+\sum_iq_i^2)/2+Y\ge0$ and $\rho^2-\sum_iT_{0i}^2=A^2X^2+A(q_0^2+\sum_iq_i^2)Y+Y^2\ge0$.
