# Stellar halo

↑ **Parent:** [Galaxy](galaxy-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stellar_halo)

A [stellar halo](stellar-halo.md) is the extended, approximately spheroidal [stellar population](stellar-population.md) surrounding a [galaxy](galaxy-split.md). It differs from the total [dark matter halo](dark-matter-halo.md). If its outer luminosity density is $r^{-p}$, its projected light follows the [projection of a spherical power-law emissivity](projection-of-a-spherical-power-law-emissivity.md) and scales as $R^{1-p}$ away from breaks and outer truncation.
