# Uniform space charge in a grounded spherical cavity

↑ **Parent:** [Cavity in a conductor](cavity-in-a-conductor.md)

A spherical cavity of radius $a$ filled with fixed uniform [charge density](charge-density.md) $\rho$ and surrounded by a grounded [conductor](electrical-conductor.md) has [electric field](electric-field-split.md) $\rho r\hat{\mathbf r}/(3\epsilon_0)$ inside and zero [electric field](electric-field-split.md) outside the conductor when no external sources are present. [Gauss's law](gauss-s-law.md) supplies the interior [electric field](electric-field-split.md); integration from $V(a)=0$ gives the displayed [electric potential](electric-potential.md). Its stored [electrostatic energy](electrostatic-energy.md) is $2\pi\rho^2a^5/(45\epsilon_0)$, obtained by integrating $\epsilon_0|\mathbf E|^2/2$ over the cavity. Grounding permits the conductor's net [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) to change.
