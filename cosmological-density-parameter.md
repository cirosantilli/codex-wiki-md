# Cosmological density parameter

↑ **Parent:** [Critical density](critical-density.md)

The density parameter is $\Omega=\rho/\rho_{\rm crit}$. In a Friedmann universe, its departure from one measures the spatial-curvature term relative to the density or expansion term.

The [Friedmann equation](friedmann-equations.md) relates this ratio to spatial curvature and the expansion rate; the parameter and the evolution equations are distinct concepts.
