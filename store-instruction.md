# Store instruction

↑ **Parent:** [Machine instruction](machine-instruction.md)

A [machine instruction](machine-instruction.md) that writes data to [computer memory](computer-memory-split.md). A [store buffer](store-buffer.md) can separate local completion from visibility to other [CPU cores](cpu-core.md).
