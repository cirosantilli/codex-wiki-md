# Starvation freedom

↑ **Parent:** [Liveness property](liveness-property.md)

For acquisition of a [mutex](mutex.md), the guarantee that every persistent requester eventually succeeds, under specified scheduling assumptions and with owners eventually releasing. Some [software threads](thread-computing.md) repeatedly succeeding while another waits forever violates [starvation freedom](starvation-freedom.md) without violating [mutual exclusion](mutual-exclusion.md). A finite bound on the wait, and service in arrival order, are additional requirements.
