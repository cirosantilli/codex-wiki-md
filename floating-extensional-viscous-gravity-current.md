# Floating extensional viscous gravity current

↑ **Parent:** [Gravity current](gravity-current.md)

A thin layer of very viscous liquid floating on an effectively inviscid denser liquid has negligible shear traction on both faces. Its leading horizontal [velocity](velocity.md) is a plug, and [incompressibility](incompressible-flow.md) gives vertical strain $w_z=-u_x$. Normal-stress balance shifts the [pressure](pressure.md) by $-2\mu u_x$, so the longitudinal excess stress is $4\mu u_x$. Combining depth-integrated momentum balance with hydrostatic [reduced gravity](reduced-gravity-split.md) gives the displayed extensional equations. This is distinct from a no-slip, shear-driven thin-film gravity current.
