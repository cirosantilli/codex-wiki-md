# Banach algebra exponential

↑ **Parent:** [Holomorphic functional calculus](holomorphic-functional-calculus.md)

The exponential series converges absolutely in a unital [Banach algebra](banach-algebra-split.md). Multiplication of absolutely convergent series gives $e^xe^{-x}=1$, and gives $e^{x+y}=e^xe^y$ when $x,y$ commute. Termwise differentiation gives $e^{tx}=1+tx+O(t^2)$ near zero. In a [C-star algebra](c-star-algebra.md), if $h=h^*$ and $t$ is real, taking adjoints termwise gives $(e^{ith})^*=e^{-ith}$, so this exponential is a [Unitary element of a C-star algebra](unitary-element-of-a-c-star-algebra.md) and has norm one. These facts let a norm bound on a functional recover its real values on Hermitian elements.
