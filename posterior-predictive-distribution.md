# Posterior predictive distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Bayesian posterior](bayesian-posterior.md)

The distribution of a new or missing quantity integrates its model over the [Bayesian posterior](bayesian-posterior.md): $p(y^*\mid y)=\int p(y^*\mid\phi,y)p(\phi\mid y)d\phi$. Both parameter and residual uncertainty should enter predictive draws used for [multiple imputation](multiple-imputation.md).
