# Eigenstrain

↑ **Parent:** [Elasticity (physics)](elasticity-physics-split.md)

An [eigenstrain](eigenstrain.md) is a local preferred deformation in the absence of [stress](stress.md). Constraints or incompatibility can make the actual [strain](strain.md) differ from it, generating [stress](stress.md). [Magnetostrictive strain](magnetostrictive-strain.md) is one example.
