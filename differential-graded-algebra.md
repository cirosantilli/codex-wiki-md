# Differential graded algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Graded algebra](graded-algebra.md)

A graded algebra equipped with a degree-one differential satisfying $d(ab)=d(a)b+(-1)^{|a|}a\,d(b)$ and $d^2=0$. Its [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) inherits a graded algebra structure. In rational [homotopy](homotopy.md), a [Sullivan minimal model](sullivan-minimal-model.md) is a free graded-commutative differential graded algebra with decomposable generator differentials.
