# Default intensity

↑ **Parent:** [Default time](default-time.md)

In a deterministic-intensity model, $\lambda(t)$ is the instantaneous conditional [default](credit-default.md) rate given survival and $G(t)$ is the survival [probability](probability.md). Risk-neutral intensity is calibrated for pricing and can differ from the physical intensity used for forecasting losses. More general stochastic-intensity models condition on the underlying filtration and require joint [expectations](expected-value.md) with discounting and recovery.
