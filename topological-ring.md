# Topological ring

↑ **Parent:** [Ring](ring.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topological_ring)

A topological ring is a [ring](ring.md) equipped with a [topology](topology-split.md) making addition, additive inversion and multiplication [continuous functions](continuous-function.md). An [adic topology](adic-topology.md) is obtained from powers of an [ideal](ideal.md).
