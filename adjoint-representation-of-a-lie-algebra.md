# Adjoint representation of a Lie algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Lie algebra representation](lie-algebra-representation.md)

The adjoint representation is $\operatorname{ad}_x(y)=[x,y]$. For a semisimple [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md), its nonzero weights are the roots and its zero-weight space is the [Cartan subalgebra](cartan-subalgebra.md).
