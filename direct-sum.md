# Direct sum

↑ **Parent:** [Vector space](vector-space-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Direct_sum)

The direct sum $U\oplus W$ of two [vector spaces](vector-space-split.md) consists of pairs $(u,w)$ with componentwise operations. A vector space is an internal direct sum of subspaces $U$ and $W$ exactly when every vector has a unique expression $u+w$ with $u\in U$ and $w\in W$.
