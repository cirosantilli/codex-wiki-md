# Sonic boom

↑ **Parent:** [Acoustic wave](acoustic-wave.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sonic_boom)

A [sonic boom](sonic-boom.md) is the sound associated with [shock waves](shock-wave.md) from a body moving at a [supersonic](supersonic-flow.md) [Mach number](mach-number.md). The weak-wave [Mach cone](mach-cone.md) describes a propagation geometry rather than the finite [pressure](pressure-split.md) signature determining the sound strength.
