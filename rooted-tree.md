# Rooted tree

↑ **Parent:** [Tree (graph theory)](tree-graph-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rooted_tree)

A rooted tree is a [tree](tree-graph-theory.md) with a distinguished vertex called the root. Its levels consist of the vertices at a fixed [graph distance](distance-graph-theory.md) from the root.
