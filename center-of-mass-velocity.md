# Center-of-mass velocity

↑ **Parent:** [Center of mass](center-of-mass.md)

For fixed classical particle [masses](mass.md), differentiating the [center of mass](center-of-mass.md) position gives the displayed mass-weighted [velocity](velocity.md). The total [momentum](momentum-split.md) is $M\mathbf V_{\rm CM}$. The relativistic zero-momentum frame instead has velocity $c^2\mathbf P/E$; treating rest mass as the only weight there generally fails.
