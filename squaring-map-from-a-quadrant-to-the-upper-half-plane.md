# Squaring map from a quadrant to the upper half-plane

↑ **Parent:** [Conformal map](conformal-map.md)

The map $z\mapsto z^2$ is a bijective [conformal map](conformal-map.md) from the open first quadrant to the [complex upper half-plane](upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md). In polar coordinates it doubles arguments from $(0,\pi/2)$ to $(0,\pi)$, and its derivative $2z$ never vanishes inside the quadrant. Its inverse is the branch of the [square root](square-root.md) with argument in $(0,\pi/2)$. By [conformal invariance of harmonicity](conformal-invariance-of-harmonicity.md), a [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md) $f$ on the half-plane pulls back to $g(x,y)=f(x^2-y^2,2xy)$. The two boundary rays map to the positive and negative real axes, respectively.
