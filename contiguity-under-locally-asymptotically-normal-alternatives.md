# Contiguity under locally asymptotically normal alternatives

↑ **Parent:** [Local asymptotic normality](local-asymptotic-normality.md)

Under [local asymptotic normality](local-asymptotic-normality.md), a fixed $h/\sqrt n$ shift has a [likelihood ratio](likelihood-ratio.md) limit $\exp(W-v/2)$ with $W\sim N(0,v)$. This is positive with mean one. [Le Cam first lemma](le-cam-s-first-lemma.md) gives [mutual contiguity](mutual-contiguity.md) of the product laws, so [consistency](consistency-statistics.md) at the central parameter transfers to these local alternatives.
