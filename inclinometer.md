# Inclinometer

↑ **Parent:** [Altitude](altitude.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inclinometer)

An [inclinometer](inclinometer.md) measures tilt relative to the vertical established by [gravity](gravity.md), usually reporting an [angle](angle.md) from the perpendicular horizontal [plane](plane.md). A weighted-string [inclinometer](inclinometer.md) compares a sighting tube with a freely hanging reference; its scale must be oriented so a level sight gives zero [altitude](altitude.md). A raw protractor may instead indicate the complementary [angle](angle.md), so check the markings rather than assuming that every instrument has the same scale.
