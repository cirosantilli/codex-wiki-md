# Specific enthalpy

↑ **Parent:** [Enthalpy](enthalpy-split.md)

Specific enthalpy is [enthalpy](enthalpy-split.md) per unit mass. For a simple compressible fluid of fixed composition, the [first law of thermodynamics](first-law-of-thermodynamics-split.md) gives

$$
dh=T\,ds+\frac{dp}{\rho},
$$

where $T$ is [temperature](temperature-split.md), $s$ is [specific entropy](specific-entropy.md), $p$ is [pressure](pressure-split.md), and $\rho$ is [mass density](density.md). Along an [isentropic flow](isentropic-flow.md), $dh=dp/\rho$. For a [perfect gas](ideal-gas-split.md) with constant [specific-heat ratio](heat-capacity-ratio.md) $\gamma$, $h=\gamma p/[(\gamma-1)\rho]$.
