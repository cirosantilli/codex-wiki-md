# Weak Boltzmann collision identity

↑ **Parent:** [Nonlinear Boltzmann collision operator](nonlinear-boltzmann-collision-operator.md)

Here $\Delta\psi=\psi(v')+\psi(v_*')-\psi(v)-\psi(v_*)$ is the pairwise collision change, not a [Laplace operator](laplace-operator.md). Average over the two particle labels and use collision reversal to obtain the formula. Every [collision invariant](collision-invariant.md) has $\Delta\psi=0$, giving mass, [momentum](momentum.md) and [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) conservation. A second symmetrization replaces $ff_*\Delta\psi/2$ by $(f'f_*'-ff_*)(-\Delta\psi)/4$.
