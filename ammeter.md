# Ammeter

↑ **Parent:** [Electric current](electric-current.md)

An [ammeter](ammeter.md) measures [electric current](electric-current.md) through a branch of an [electrical circuit](electrical-circuit.md). Connect it in series; its own finite [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) changes the measured branch's [electric current](electric-current.md) unless that effect is negligible or corrected.[Measuring Current with an Ammeter, Figure 10.35 and following paragraph: connection and loading](university-physics-volume-2-by-samuel-j-ling-and-others/10-4-electrical-measuring-instruments.md)
