<h1 id="cu-111-surface-state-standing-wave-experiment">Cu(111) surface-state standing-wave experiment</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Standing-wave scanning tunneling spectroscopy](standing-wave-scanning-tunneling-spectroscopy.md)

Low-temperature [scanning tunneling spectroscopy](scanning-tunneling-spectroscopy.md) resolves energy-dependent [interference](interference-wave-propagation.md) in the surface electronic [density of states](density-of-states.md) near scattering features. Its coexistence with a structural map distinguishes electronic corrugation from atomic positions. The 1993 Cu(111) observation provides an early example.
