# Lazy reversal of a doubly linked list

↑ **Parent:** [Doubly linked list](doubly-linked-list.md)

Keep the physical [doubly linked list](doubly-linked-list.md) unchanged and represent the logical [sequence](sequence.md) in either forward or backward order according to a [Boolean](boolean-data-type.md) flag. Toggling the flag reverses the logical contents in constant [time complexity](time-complexity.md). Endpoint insertion and indexed access must interpret their positions using the flag, and the [abstraction function](abstraction-function.md) explicitly accounts for this choice.
