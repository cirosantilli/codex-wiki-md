# Classical integrability

↑ **Parent:** [Integrable systems](integrable-systems-split.md)

For a finite-dimensional [Hamiltonian system](hamiltonian-system.md), integrability is normally expressed by enough independent first integrals in mutual [Poisson bracket](poisson-bracket.md) involution. In classical field theories, an infinite hierarchy of compatible [conserved quantities](conserved-quantity.md) is the corresponding structure. For [Sine-Gordon theory](sine-gordon-theory.md), [Bäcklund transformations](backlund-transformation.md) generate local [conservation laws](conservation-law.md) and explicit soliton solutions. Conservation of arbitrary quantities alone should not be confused with proof of their [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) involution.
