# Population hazard under exponential frailty

↑ **Parent:** [Frailty distribution among survivors](frailty-distribution-among-survivors.md)

For unit-rate [exponential distribution](exponential-distribution.md) frailty, the population survival and hazard in a [proportional frailty model](proportional-frailty-model.md) are

$$
\overline S(t)=\frac1{1+H_0(t)},
\qquad
\overline h(t)=\frac{h_0(t)}{1+H_0(t)}.
$$

The surviving frailty distribution is exponential with rate $1+H_0(t)$.
