# Carrier injection

↑ **Parent:** [Minority carrier](minority-carrier.md)

Introducing excess mobile [charge carriers](charge-carrier.md) into a [semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md), for example through a contact under [biasing](biasing.md). Their subsequent motion and [electron-hole recombination](electron-hole-recombination.md) can change the [electrical conductivity](electrical-conductivity.md) and an output [electric current](electric-current.md).
