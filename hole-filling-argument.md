# Hole-filling argument

↑ **Parent:** [Energy estimate](energy-estimate.md)

An estimate $E(R)\leq C(E(2R)-E(R))$ bounds the energy in a ball by the energy in the surrounding [annulus](annulus-mathematics.md). Moving the smaller-ball energy to the left gives the strict contraction $E(R)\leq\theta E(2R)$ with $\theta=C/(1+C)<1$. Iteration turns this contraction into decay at small scales.
