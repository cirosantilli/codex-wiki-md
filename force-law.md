# Force law

↑ **Parent:** [Force](force-split.md)

A specified relation giving a [force](force-split.md) from relevant positions, velocities or other physical variables. Combined with [Newton's laws of motion](newton-s-laws-of-motion.md), it constrains the possible [motion](motion.md).
