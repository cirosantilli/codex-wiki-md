<h1 id="siegel-walfisz-theorem">Siegel–Walfisz theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet's theorem on arithmetic progressions](dirichlet-s-theorem-on-arithmetic-progressions.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siegel–Walfisz_theorem)

For fixed $A,C>0$,

$$
\psi(x;q,a)=\frac{x}{\phi(q)}+O_{A,C}\left(\frac{x}{\log^Ax}\right)
$$

uniformly for $q\leq\log^Cx$ and $(a,q)=1$. The implied constant may be ineffective. This small-modulus input complements the [large sieve](large-sieve.md) in the proof of the [Bombieri–Vinogradov theorem](bombieri-vinogradov-theorem.md).
