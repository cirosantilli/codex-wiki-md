# Regular part at a simple pole

↑ **Parent:** [Simple pole](simple-pole.md)

For holomorphic $N,D$ with $N(z_0)D(z_0)\ne0$, write the quotient as $h(z)/(z-z_0)$ where $h=N/D$. Its [Taylor series](taylor-series.md) immediately gives the displayed [Laurent series](laurent-series.md). The [residue](residue.md) is $h(z_0)$ and the constant regular term is $h'(z_0)$; the latter requires differentiating both numerator and remaining denominator. Higher regular coefficients follow from the higher Taylor coefficients of $h$.
