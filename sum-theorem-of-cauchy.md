# Sum theorem of Cauchy

↑ **Parent:** [Uniform convergence](uniform-convergence.md)

The continuity assertion for sums of continuous [functions](function-split.md) in the [Cours d'analyse by Augustin-Louis Cauchy](cours-d-analyse-by-augustin-louis-cauchy.md), Chapter VI, §I. Under a modern pointwise reading the assertion needs stronger control: $x^n$ on $[0,1]$ has a discontinuous [pointwise limit](pointwise-limit.md). [Uniform convergence](uniform-convergence.md) supplies a sufficient hypothesis through the [uniform limit theorem](uniform-limit-theorem.md).
