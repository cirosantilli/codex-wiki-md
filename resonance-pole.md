# Resonance pole

↑ **Parent:** [Scattering resonance](scattering-resonance.md)

A pole of the continued [scattering matrix](s-matrix.md) associated with outgoing boundary conditions and an unstable trapped state. A pole below the real energy axis gives time dependence $e^{-iE_Rt/\hbar}e^{-\Gamma t/(2\hbar)}$, so the probability lifetime is $\hbar/\Gamma$. Bound-state poles instead lie on the positive imaginary wavenumber axis and produce square-integrable decaying spatial waves.
