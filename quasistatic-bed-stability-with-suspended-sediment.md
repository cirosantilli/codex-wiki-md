# Quasistatic bed stability with suspended sediment

↑ **Parent:** [Exner equation](exner-equation.md)

A leading fixed-discharge, level-surface approximation to a shallow erodible-bed flow gives perturbation [equations](equation.md) $C_x=4U-C$ and $U_t+3\beta U_x=-4U+C$. The displayed Fourier growth rate has negative [real part](real-part.md) for every nonzero [wave number](wavenumber.md), independently of bedload parameter $\beta$. Bedload changes the propagation [speed](speed.md) but not this damping. The zero-wave-number mode is neutral, so absence of growing modes should not be confused with strict asymptotic decay of every spatially uniform perturbation.
