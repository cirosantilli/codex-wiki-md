# Spherical top-hat collapse model

↑ **Parent:** [Spherical-collapse model](spherical-collapse-model.md)

A uniform overdensity evolves as a separate spherical pressureless region until [shell crossing](shell-crossing.md). In an Einstein-de Sitter background, formal collapse has linearly extrapolated threshold $\delta_c=(3/5)(3\pi/2)^{2/3}\simeq1.686$. This is distinct from the actual nonlinear [density](density.md). Virialization and nonspherical tides require further physics.
