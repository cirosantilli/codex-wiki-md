# Closed-hyperplane proof of Lp duality

↑ **Parent:** [Duality of Lp spaces](duality-of-lp-spaces.md)

Let $1<p<\infty$ and $q=p/(p-1)$. For a nonzero [bounded linear functional](continuous-linear-functional.md) $\ell$ on an [Lp space](lp-space.md), choose $f_0$ with $\ell(f_0)=1$ and a closest point $h$ in its closed kernel. Put $z=f_0-h$. The variational condition is $\int |z|^{p-2}\overline z\,k=0$ for every kernel element $k$. Since $f-\ell(f)z$ lies in the kernel, $\ell(f)=\int fg$ with $g=|z|^{p-2}\overline z/\|z\|_p^p\in L^q$. [Hölder's inequality](holder-s-inequality.md) gives the upper norm bound; testing against $|g|^{q-2}\overline g$ gives equality. Thus the representation is an isometric linear isomorphism when the pairing is bilinear.
