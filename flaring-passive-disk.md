# Flaring passive disk

↑ **Parent:** [Disk flaring](disk-flaring.md)

Assume a [passive disk](passive-disk.md) has local [isothermal sound speed](isothermal-sound-speed.md) squared $c_s^2=aT$, [disk scale height](disk-scale-height.md) $H=c_s/\Omega_K$, and irradiated height $z_s=fH$ with constant $f$. Its [irradiation grazing angle of a disk](irradiation-grazing-angle-of-a-disk.md) and [Stefan–Boltzmann law](stefan-boltzmann-law.md) give $q'=Cq^8/R^3$ for $q=z_s/R$, where $C=4\pi\sigma(GM/a)^4/(L_*f^8)$. Integrating gives $q^{-7}=R_0^2/R^2+B$, with $R_0^2=7C/2$. Where the first term dominates and the [thin disk](thin-disk.md) assumptions hold, $q\simeq(R/R_0)^{2/7}$, $H\propto R^{9/7}$ and $T\propto R^{-3/7}$. The law cannot be extrapolated into a geometrically thick region using the same grazing-angle approximation.
