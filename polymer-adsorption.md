# Polymer adsorption

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer physics](polymer-physics.md)

[Polymer adsorption](polymer-adsorption.md) binds a [polymer](polymer-split.md) near an attractive interface through contacts of some of its [monomers](monomer.md). The binding [energy](energy-split.md) competes with the loss of conformational [entropy](entropy-split.md). A chain need not lie flat: noncontacting loops and tails can extend into the [solvent](solvent.md). In a weak-adsorption blob model, the layer thickness exceeds the [Kuhn length](kuhn-length.md) and local chain statistics remain approximately those of the free chain below that thickness.
