# Elliptic fibration of the rational elliptic surface

↑ **Parent:** [Rational elliptic surface](rational-elliptic-surface.md)

Projection to $[s:t]$ gives an elliptic fibration with connected cubic fibers. Each of the nine [exceptional divisors](exceptional-divisor.md) maps isomorphically to $\mathbb{CP}^1$ and is therefore a holomorphic section.
