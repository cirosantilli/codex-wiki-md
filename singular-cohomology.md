# Singular cohomology

↑ **Parent:** [Cohomology](cohomology-split.md)

For a [topological space](topological-space.md) $X$ and [abelian group](abelian-group.md) $A$, singular cohomology is the [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) of the [cochain complex](cochain-complex.md) $C^n(X;A)=\operatorname{Hom}(C_n(X;\mathbb Z),A)$, where $C_n$ is the [singular chain group](singular-chain-group.md). Its differential is dual to the singular boundary. This theory has [relative cohomology](relative-cohomology.md), [long exact sequences](long-exact-sequence.md) and the [Excision theorem](excision-theorem.md). On spaces that are not locally contractible it can differ from [Čech cohomology](cech-cohomology.md).
