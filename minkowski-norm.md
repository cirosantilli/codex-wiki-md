# Minkowski norm

↑ **Parent:** [Minkowski metric](minkowski-metric.md)

The Minkowski norm is the Lorentz-invariant quadratic form $U\cdot U=\eta_{\mu\nu}U^\mu U^\nu$.

This quadratic form belongs to the geometry of [Minkowski spacetime](minkowski-spacetime.md); the spacetime itself is a broader concept than this squared norm.
