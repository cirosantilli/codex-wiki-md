# Distant view of gravitational collapse

↑ **Parent:** [Gravitational collapse](gravitational-collapse.md)

In a spherical collapse model a freely falling surface can cross its [event horizon](event-horizon.md) after finite [proper time](proper-time.md), while outgoing signals received far away have increasing delay and redshift. Schwarzschild-coordinate freezing does not mean collapse physically stops or leaves a permanently bright surface at the horizon.
