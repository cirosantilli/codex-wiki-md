# Chemotactic drift

↑ **Parent:** [Chemotaxis](chemotaxis.md)

[Chemotactic drift](chemotactic-drift.md) is the directed velocity associated with sensing a chemical gradient. In a simple continuum model the drift is $\chi\nabla c$; multiplying by cell density gives the [chemotactic flux](chemotactic-flux.md).
