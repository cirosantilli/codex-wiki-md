# Caratheodory boundary extension theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann mapping theorem](riemann-mapping-theorem.md)

For a bounded [simply connected domain](simply-connected-domain.md) $D$ and a [conformal bijection](biholomorphism.md) $f:\mathbb D\to D$, $f$ extends continuously to the closed disc if and only if $\partial D$ is a [locally connected space](locally-connected-space.md). A Jordan [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md) gives a homeomorphism of closed discs, but [local connectedness](locally-connected-space.md) alone need not give injectivity on the [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md): a slit has two [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md) approaches. The same statement applies on the sphere to unbounded [domains](domain-mathematical-analysis.md) after a suitable change of coordinates. This [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md) theorem is different from the [Caratheodory extension theorem](caratheodory-s-extension-theorem.md) for measures.
