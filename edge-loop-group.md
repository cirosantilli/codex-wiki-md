# Edge-loop group

↑ **Parent:** [Fundamental group](fundamental-group.md)

Two based [edge loops](edge-loop.md) are equivalent under insertion or deletion of stationary steps, cancellation or insertion of a backtracking spur, and replacement of two sides of a triangle by its third side. The [edge-loop group](edge-loop-group.md) consists of the resulting classes, with concatenation as product and reversal as inverse. Each elementary move can be performed inside a longer concatenation, so multiplication is well defined. Associativity follows from concatenation, the stationary loop is the identity, and a loop followed by its reversal cancels by spurs.

Realization induces a homomorphism to the [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md) of the geometric realization, because each move is a homotopy in an edge or a triangle. The relative [Simplicial approximation theorem](simplicial-approximation-theorem.md) applied to based circles makes this map surjective. The full edge-path theorem additionally proves injectivity by approximating homotopies; that stronger assertion is not needed for the group construction or the surjectivity argument.
