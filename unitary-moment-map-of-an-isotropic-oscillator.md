# Unitary moment map of an isotropic oscillator

↑ **Parent:** [Moment map](moment-map.md)

On the canonical [cotangent bundle](cotangent-bundle.md) $T^*\mathbb R^n$, $\omega=i\sum dz_j\wedge d\bar z_j$ and $H=z^\dagger z$. The [unitary group](unitary-group.md) acts by $z\mapsto Uz$, preserving both. For a Hermitian $A$, the generator $-iA$ has Hamiltonian $\mu_A=z^\dagger Az$. The [Poisson bracket](poisson-bracket.md) satisfies $\{\mu_A,\mu_B\}=\mu_{-i[A,B]}$, with no central term. The central generator has moment map $H$; imaginary antisymmetric matrices give ordinary [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md), while real symmetric matrices give quadratic observables mixing position and momentum.
