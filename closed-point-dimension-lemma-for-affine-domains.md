# Closed-point dimension lemma for affine domains

↑ **Parent:** [Krull dimension of an affine variety](krull-dimension-of-an-affine-variety.md)

If $A$ is a finite-type [integral domain](integral-domain.md) over an [algebraically closed field](algebraically-closed-field.md) and $\dim A=d$, every [maximal ideal](maximal-ideal.md) $\mathfrak m$ has [height of a prime ideal](height-of-a-prime-ideal.md) $d$. Choose a [Noether normalization](noether-normalization.md) $k[z_1,\ldots,z_d]\subset A$. The contraction of $\mathfrak m$ is a [maximal ideal](maximal-ideal.md) of the [polynomial ring](polynomial-ring.md) and has height $d$; [going-down theorem](going-down-theorem.md) lifts its full chain since the [polynomial ring](polynomial-ring.md) is [integrally closed domain](integrally-closed-domain.md). The upper bound is $\dim A$. A [principal open subset](principal-open-subscheme.md) containing a [closed point](closed-point.md) consequently has dimension $d$, because the whole chain under that point survives [localization](localization-of-a-ring.md).
