# Homologous adiabatic stellar stability

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar homology](stellar-homology.md)

A mass-preserving [stellar homology](stellar-homology.md) with scale $x$ has [mass density](density.md) proportional to $x^{-3}$. For an [adiabatic process](adiabatic-process.md) with constant [adiabatic index](heat-capacity-ratio.md) $\gamma$, its [internal energy](internal-energy.md) scales as $x^{-3(\gamma-1)}$ and its [Newtonian gravitational potential energy](newtonian-gravitational-potential-energy.md) as $x^{-1}$. Writing $a=3(\gamma-1)$, stationarity at $x=1$ requires $W_0=-aU_0$, and the second scale derivative is $a(a-1)U_0$. Thus this particular displacement is restoring for $\gamma>4/3$, destabilizing for $1<\gamma<4/3$, and marginal at $\gamma=4/3$. This is a test of homologous radial stability; it does not by itself test every stellar displacement.
