# Newlander-Nirenberg theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Integrable almost complex structure](integrable-almost-complex-structure.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newlander-Nirenberg_theorem)

The Newlander-Nirenberg theorem says that a smooth [almost complex structure](almost-complex-manifold.md) is [integrable](integrable-almost-complex-structure.md) if and only if its [Nijenhuis tensor](nijenhuis-tensor.md) vanishes.
