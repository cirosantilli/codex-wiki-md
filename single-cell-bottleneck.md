# Single-cell bottleneck

↑ **Parent:** [Multicellularity](multicellularity.md)

Passage through a single founding cell during a life cycle. It increases initial genetic similarity among descendants and restricts transmission of competing cell lineages, without preventing later [mutation](mutation.md).
