# Tensor contractions in the SU2 Clebsch-Gordan decomposition

↑ **Parent:** [Clebsch-Gordan decomposition for SU2](clebsch-gordan-decomposition-for-su2.md)

Realize $V_n=\operatorname{Sym}^n\mathbb C^2$. Contract $r$ pairs of indices, one from each [symmetric tensor](symmetric-tensor.md), with the alternating [invariant tensor](invariant-tensor.md) $\epsilon$, and symmetrize the remaining indices. This is a nonzero [intertwining operator](intertwining-operator.md) into $V_{m+n-2r}$. In the bihomogeneous polynomial realization, $(z_1w_2-z_2w_1)^rz_1^{m-r}w_1^{n-r}$ is a [highest-weight vector](highest-weight-vector.md) of weight $m+n-2r$. Its lowering string has length $m+n-2r+1$. These inequivalent irreducible summands exhaust the tensor product, since their dimensions sum to $(m+1)(n+1)$.
