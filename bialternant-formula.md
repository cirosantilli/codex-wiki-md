# Bialternant formula

↑ **Parent:** [Schur polynomial](schur-polynomial.md)

Let $a_\gamma=\det[x_j^{\gamma_i}]$ and $\delta=(N-1,\ldots,0)$. Multiply the [Jacobi–Trudi identity](jacobi-trudi-identity.md) [matrix](matrix.md) by $B_{jk}=(-1)^{N-j}e_{N-j}(X\setminus x_k)$. The generating-function identity $H(t)\prod_{i\ne k}(1-x_it)=(1-x_kt)^{-1}$ makes the product [matrix](matrix.md) $[x_k^{\lambda_i+N-i}]$. Setting $\lambda=0$ shows $\det B=a_\delta$, proving the formula as a [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) identity.
