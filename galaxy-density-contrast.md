# Galaxy density contrast

↑ **Parent:** [Galaxy clustering](galaxy-clustering.md)

The [galaxy density contrast](galaxy-density-contrast.md) measures a fractional departure of galaxy number density from its ensemble or survey mean. Linear [galaxy bias](galaxy-bias.md) relates its large-scale three-dimensional component to the matter [density contrast](density-contrast.md) by $\delta_g=b_g\delta_m$. An angular sample averages this field with its normalized redshift selection; a [probability density](probability-density.md) $p(z)$ per unit [cosmological redshift](cosmological-redshift.md) corresponds to a distance kernel $H(z)p(z)/c$ in a spatially flat background.
