# Antagonist

↑ **Parent:** [Pharmacology](pharmacology-split.md)

A ligand that reduces [receptor](receptor-biochemistry.md) activation by an [agonist](agonist.md) or endogenous signal under specified conditions. Behavioural selectivity cannot be inferred solely from an in-vitro affinity label.
