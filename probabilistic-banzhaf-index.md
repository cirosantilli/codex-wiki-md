# Probabilistic Banzhaf index

↑ **Parent:** [Transferable utility game](transferable-utility-game.md)

The [probabilistic Banzhaf index](probabilistic-banzhaf-index.md) averages a player's [marginal contribution](marginal-contribution.md) over all [coalitions](coalition-game-theory.md) not containing that player, each given equal weight. In a [simple cooperative game](simple-cooperative-game.md), it is the [probability](probability.md) of changing a losing [coalition](coalition-game-theory.md) into a winning one when every other player joins independently with [probability](probability.md) $1/2$. Its [sum](sum.md) over players need not equal the grand-[coalition](coalition-game-theory.md) value: in a three-player majority game each player has index $1/2$, giving total $3/2$ despite $v(N)=1$. This convention differs from both the raw count of pivotal [coalitions](coalition-game-theory.md) and the [normalized Banzhaf index](normalized-banzhaf-index.md).

The name refers to [John Banzhaf](john-banzhaf.md), whose 1965 paper addresses the mathematical analysis of weighted voting.[Author's reference page, citation to Rutgers Law Review 19 (1965), starting p. 317](weighted-voting-doesn-t-work-a-mathematical-analysis-by-john-banzhaf.md)
