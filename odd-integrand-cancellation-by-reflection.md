# Odd-integrand cancellation by reflection

↑ **Parent:** [Multiple integral](multiple-integral.md)

If a region is invariant under one coordinate reflection and an integrable function changes sign under that reflection, its [multiple integral](multiple-integral.md) is zero. Apply the [change of variables formula](change-of-variables-formula.md) to the reflection, whose absolute [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md) is one: the integral equals its negative. This applies to odd coordinate powers in symmetric volumes and to the off-diagonal entries of an [inertia tensor](inertia-tensor.md) when the [mass density](density.md) shares the coordinate-reflection symmetries.
