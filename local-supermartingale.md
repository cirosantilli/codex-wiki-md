# Local supermartingale

↑ **Parent:** [Supermartingale](supermartingale.md)

A [local supermartingale](local-supermartingale.md) becomes a [supermartingale](supermartingale.md) after stopping along an increasing sequence of [stopping times](stopping-time.md) tending to infinity. Localization allows an [Itô formula](ito-s-lemma.md) comparison before global integrability is established. Additional lower bounds or uniform integrability are needed to pass from the local comparison to an unrestricted expectation inequality.
