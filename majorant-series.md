# Majorant series

↑ **Parent:** [Power series](power-series.md)

A power series $g(x)=\sum_\alpha b_\alpha x^\alpha$ with nonnegative coefficients majorizes $f(x)=\sum_\alpha a_\alpha x^\alpha$ when $|a_\alpha|\leq b_\alpha$ for every [multi-index](multi-index-notation.md) $\alpha$. Coefficientwise majorization turns estimates for an analytic equation into estimates for a simpler positive-coefficient equation.
