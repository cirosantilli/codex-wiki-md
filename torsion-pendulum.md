# Torsion pendulum

↑ **Parent:** [Torsion balance](torsion-balance.md)

A [harmonic oscillator](simple-harmonic-motion.md) in which a restoring [torque](torque.md) is proportional to angular [displacement](displacement.md); its [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) and [torsion constant](torsion-constant.md) set the natural [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md).
