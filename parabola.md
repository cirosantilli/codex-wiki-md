# Parabola

↑ **Parent:** [Conic section](conic-section.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parabola)

A parabola is a [plane curve](plane-curve.md) whose points are equally distant from a focus and a directrix. In suitable coordinates it has equation $y=ax^2$, with $a\ne0$. Its quadratic geometry can also describe a [contour deformation](contour-deformation.md) through a [saddle point](saddle-point.md).
