# Parabola

↑ **Parent:** [Conic section](conic-section.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parabola)

A parabola is a [plane curve](plane-curve.md) whose points are equally distant from a focus and a directrix. In suitable coordinates it has equation $y=ax^2$, with $a\ne0$. Its quadratic geometry can also describe a [contour deformation](contour-deformation.md) through a [saddle point](saddle-point.md).

For example, take a [focus of a conic](focus-of-a-conic.md) at $(1,0)$ and a [directrix of a conic](directrix-of-a-conic.md) $x=-1$. Equality of squared [Euclidean distances](euclidean-distance.md) gives $(x-1)^2+y^2=(x+1)^2$, hence $y^2=4x$. The point $(1,2)$ has distance $2$ to both the focus and the directrix. Its [eccentricity of a conic](eccentricity-of-a-conic.md) is one. Unlike an [ellipse](ellipse.md), a [parabola](parabola.md) is unbounded and has only one matched focus-directrix description.
