# Lamb shift

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic physics](atomic-physics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lamb_shift)

A [quantum electrodynamics](quantum-electrodynamics.md) correction separates [hydrogen atom](hydrogen-atom.md) levels such as $2s_{1/2}$ and $2p_{1/2}$ that coincide in the simplest model.

The Lamb shift measures an [energy level](energy-level.md) displacement beyond a specified [Dirac equation](dirac-equation.md) reference. In the point-[proton](proton.md), infinite-nuclear-[mass](mass.md) [Coulomb potential](coulomb-potential-energy.md) model, $2S_{1/2}$ and $2P_{1/2}$ share [principal quantum number](principal-quantum-number.md) $n=2$ and total electronic [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) $j=1/2$, hence [energy](energy-split.md), despite differing [orbital angular momentum](orbital-angular-momentum.md) values.[Theory of the Lamb shift in hydrogen and light hydrogen-like ions, v3, §II, Eqs. (2)–(4)](theory-of-the-lamb-shift-in-hydrogen-and-light-hydrogen-like-ions-by-vladimir-yerokhin-and-others.md)

The [Lamb-Retherford experiment](lamb-retherford-experiment.md) converts a small [energy](energy-split.md) difference into a large detector change: prepare a long-lived [quantum state](quantum-state.md), transfer population into a short-lived one, and read the surviving beam.
