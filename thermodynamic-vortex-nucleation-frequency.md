# Thermodynamic vortex-nucleation frequency

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum vortex](quantum-vortex.md)

A [quantum vortex](quantum-vortex.md) becomes energetically favourable in a [rotating reference frame](rotating-reference-frame.md) when $\Delta E-\Omega\Delta L_z<0$. For a singly [quantized vortex](quantum-vortex.md) in a long radially Thomas–Fermi condensate, the cutoff-shell estimate is

$$
\Omega_c\simeq\frac{2\hbar}{mR_r^2}\left[\log\frac{R_r}{a_0}-\frac12\right].
$$

Integrating the trapped [number density](number-density.md) against $u_\theta^2$ produces the [logarithm](logarithm.md) and constant; integrating it against [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) per particle gives $\Delta L_z\simeq\pi Hn_0\hbar R_r^2/2$. Resolved-core corrections can alter the nonlogarithmic constant. This criterion compares equilibrium rotating-frame energies and does not remove a dynamical vortex-entry barrier.
