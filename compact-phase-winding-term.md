# Compact phase winding term

↑ **Parent:** [Density-phase action of a Bose gas](density-phase-action-of-a-bose-gas.md)

A periodic complex field permits $\phi(\beta,\mathbf r)-\phi(0,\mathbf r)=2\pi w(\mathbf r)$. Its uniform-density first-order term is therefore a winding-dependent imaginary action, not generally a removable global constant. It vanishes in the smooth zero-winding sector used for Gaussian [phonons](phonon.md). Density defects and vortices require more than that smooth-sector approximation.
