# Orientation of a monopole lift

↑ **Parent:** [Dimensional reduction of a Yang-Mills instanton to a monopole](dimensional-reduction-of-a-yang-mills-instanton-to-a-monopole.md)

Use $\epsilon_{123}=1$ and orientation $d\tau\wedge dx^1\wedge dx^2\wedge dx^3$. A $\tau$-independent [gauge curvature](gauge-field-strength.md) has mixed component $\mathcal F_{i\tau}=D_i\Phi$. If $B_i=D_i\Phi$, its two-form is $\sum_{\rm cyclic}B_i(dx^j\wedge dx^k-d\tau\wedge dx^i)$. The [Hodge star operator](hodge-star-operator.md) interchanges the two displayed basis forms, so $*\mathcal F=-\mathcal F$. With orientation $dx^1\wedge dx^2\wedge dx^3\wedge d\tau$ the same lift is self-dual; changing the sign of its $d\tau$ component instead recovers anti-self-duality. Orientation must therefore accompany any stated sign in this reduction.
