# Automorphism of a field extension

↑ **Parent:** [Field automorphism](field-automorphism.md)

An automorphism of a [field extension](field-extension.md) $K/F$ is a [field automorphism](field-automorphism.md) $\sigma:K\to K$ fixing every element of $F$. Such maps form the [field automorphism group](field-automorphism-group.md) $\operatorname{Aut}_F(K)$ under composition. For a [Finite Galois extension](finite-galois-extension.md) this is the [Galois group](galois-group.md) $\operatorname{Gal}(K/F)$. Specifying the fixed base [field](field.md) distinguishes these maps from arbitrary [field automorphisms](field-automorphism.md) of $K$.
