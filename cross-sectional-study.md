# Cross-sectional study

↑ **Parent:** [Observational study](observational-study.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cross-sectional_study)

A [cross-sectional study](cross-sectional-study.md) measures different individuals during one observation period. Differences between ages can reflect [confounding](confounding.md) or birth-cohort differences as well as changes within individuals; a [longitudinal study](longitudinal-study.md) is needed to measure the latter directly.
