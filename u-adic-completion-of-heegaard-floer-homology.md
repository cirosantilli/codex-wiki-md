# U-adic completion of Heegaard Floer homology

↑ **Parent:** [Variants of Heegaard Floer homology](variants-of-heegaard-floer-homology.md)

Completing the minus [chain complex](chain-complex.md) in powers of $U$ replaces $\mathbb Z[U]$ by the [formal power series ring](formal-power-series.md) $\mathbb Z[[U]]$; its homology is written $\mathbf{HF}^-$. This permits infinite sums of [Heegaard Floer cobordism maps](heegaard-floer-cobordism-map.md) whose exponents tend to infinity, as occur in surgery formulas. A series with constant coefficient $\pm1$ is a unit: its inverse is found recursively, or by the geometric series for its positive-order part. Such a series need not have a polynomial inverse, which is why exact triangles involving sums over all Spin-c extensions require a completion in the minus theory. Individual homogeneous cobordism maps are still defined on the ordinary minus groups.
