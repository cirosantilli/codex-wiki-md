<h1 id="real-embedding-of-su-n-in-so-2n">Real embedding of SU(n) in SO(2n)</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Realification of a complex representation](realification-of-a-complex-representation.md)

Writing complex coordinates as $z=x+iy$ converts the [unitary representation](unitary-representation.md) $U=A+iB$ into the indicated real [matrix](matrix.md). It preserves the [Euclidean metric](euclidean-metric.md) because $U^\dagger U=I$, and its real [determinant](determinant.md) is $|\det_{\mathbb C}U|^2=1$. Thus even $U(n)$ embeds in $SO(2n)$. The complexified defining real [representation](group-representation.md) restricts as $\mathbf n\oplus\bar{\mathbf n}$, whereas the underlying real space is the [realification of a complex representation](realification-of-a-complex-representation.md) of dimension $n$.
