# Eggleton Roche-lobe radius approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Roche lobe](roche-lobe.md)

For a circular synchronized [binary star](binary-star.md), this approximates the volume-equivalent [Roche lobe](roche-lobe.md) radius, where $q$ is the enclosed star's [mass](mass.md) divided by its companion's [mass](mass.md). It describes critical equipotential geometry; it does not give the star's radius response to [mass](mass.md) loss. The primary calculation reports radius errors below about one percent across the mass-ratio range.[Printed p. 368, equation 2 and accompanying comparison](approximations-to-the-radii-of-roche-lobes-by-peter-p-eggleton.md)
