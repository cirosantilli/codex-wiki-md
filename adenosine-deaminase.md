# Adenosine deaminase

↑ **Parent:** [Enzyme](enzyme-split.md)

[Adenosine deaminase](adenosine-deaminase.md) catalyzes conversion of [adenosine](adenosine.md) and [water](water.md) into [inosine](inosine.md) and [ammonia](ammonia.md). Its zinc-containing [active site](active-site.md) activates [water](water.md) and stabilizes the reacting configuration; [proton transfers](proton-transfer.md) assist addition and leaving-group departure. Mutation kinetics constrain catalytic roles but do not uniquely locate every proton.
