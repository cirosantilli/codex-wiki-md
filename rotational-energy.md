# Rotational energy

↑ **Parent:** [Rigid rotor](rigid-rotor.md)

For a [linear rigid rotor](linear-rigid-rotor.md) with [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) $I$, the [energy eigenvalue](energy-eigenvalue-split.md) at [angular momentum quantum number](angular-momentum-quantum-number.md) $J$ is $\hbar^2J(J+1)/(2I)$. Each level has [statistical weight](statistical-weight.md) $2J+1$ before additional symmetry or nuclear-[spin](spin.md) restrictions.
