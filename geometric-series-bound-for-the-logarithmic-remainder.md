# Geometric-series bound for the logarithmic remainder

↑ **Parent:** [Logarithm](logarithm.md)

For $0<u<1$, the [power series](power-series.md) for the [logarithm](logarithm.md) gives $-\log(1-u)-u=\sum_{n=2}^\infty u^n/n<\frac12\sum_{n=2}^\infty u^n=u^2/[2(1-u)]$. The inequality is strict because $1/n<1/2$ for every $n\geq3$.
