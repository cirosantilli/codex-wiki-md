# Superallowed beta decay

↑ **Parent:** [Allowed beta decay](allowed-beta-decay.md)

An especially strong [allowed beta decay](allowed-beta-decay.md), conventionally including pure [Fermi transitions](fermi-transition.md) between $0^+$ [isobaric analog states](isobaric-analog-state.md). Those states have closely related spatial and spin structure, so the [isospin](isospin.md) ladder operator has a large overlap. A $0^+\to0^+$ assignment alone fixes the allowed Fermi character, but does not establish analog-state overlap or a large rate; a small [comparative half-life](comparative-half-life.md) supplies additional evidence.
