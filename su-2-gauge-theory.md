<h1 id="su-2-gauge-theory">SU(2) gauge theory</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Yang-Mills theory](yang-mills-theory.md)

An SU(2) gauge theory is a [Yang-Mills theory](yang-mills-theory.md) with gauge group $SU(2)$. In its defining representation the generators are $T^a=\sigma^a/2$, and its structure constants are the Levi-Civita symbols $\epsilon^{abc}$.
