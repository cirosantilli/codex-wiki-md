# Linear-time multiplication in an automatic structure

↑ **Parent:** [Group multiplier automaton](group-multiplier-automaton.md)

Fix the first tape of a [group multiplier automaton](group-multiplier-automaton.md) and use [dynamic programming](dynamic-programming.md) over automaton states and output-padding status, retaining predecessor pointers. The [pumping of a group multiplier automaton](pumping-of-a-group-multiplier-automaton.md) bounds the search by $|u|+N$ layers, each of fixed size. This constructs a representative of $\overline u x$ in time $O(|u|+1)$ without assuming uniqueness of representatives.
