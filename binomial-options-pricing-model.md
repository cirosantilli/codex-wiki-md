# Binomial options pricing model

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical finance](mathematical-finance-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Binomial_options_pricing_model)

The binomial options pricing model values a [contingent claim](contingent-claim.md) on a two-outcome asset-price tree using [risk-neutral probability](risk-neutral-probability.md) and [backward option pricing](backward-option-pricing.md). Its underlying [discrete-time binomial market](discrete-time-binomial-market.md) distinguishes the market assumptions from the pricing procedure.
