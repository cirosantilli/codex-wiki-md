# Weyl reflection lift in an sl2 representation

↑ **Parent:** [sl2 Lie algebra](sl2-lie-algebra.md)

In a finite-dimensional [sl2 Lie algebra](sl2-lie-algebra.md) representation, raising and lowering operators are [nilpotent operators](nilpotent-linear-map.md), so their exponentials are finite sums. In the defining two-dimensional representation, the displayed product is $\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\-1&0\end{pmatrix}$. It sends $h$ to $-h$ by conjugation, lifting the nontrivial [Weyl group](weyl-group.md) reflection. Its square acts on the irreducible highest-weight module $L(n)=\operatorname{Sym}^n\mathbb C^2$ by $(-1)^n$. Thus a Weyl reflection can have a representative of order four on a representation.
