# Random unitary channel

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum channel](quantum-channel.md)

A random unitary channel has $T(\rho)=\sum_jp_jU_j\rho U_j^\dagger$. Its Choi matrix is a convex combination of maximally entangled pure states.
