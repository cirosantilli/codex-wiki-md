# Equiangular direction in least angle regression

↑ **Parent:** [Least angle regression](least-angle-regression.md)

With signed active [covariate](covariate.md) columns $X_A^s=(s_jx_j)_{j\in A}$ and their positive-definite [Gram matrix](gram-matrix.md) $G_A=(X_A^s)^TX_A^s$, the displayed direction has unit [norm](norm.md) and $(X_A^s)^Tu=\alpha1$. Thus it makes equal angles with unit-length signed active [covariates](covariate.md), and movement along it reduces their signed residual correlations at the common rate $\alpha$. The [regression coefficient](regression-coefficient.md) direction is $d_A=\operatorname{diag}(s_A)w$, with inactive components zero.
