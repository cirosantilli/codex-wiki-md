# Uniformly most powerful test for an exponential rate

↑ **Parent:** [Neyman-Pearson lemma](neyman-pearson-lemma.md)

For independent samples from an [exponential distribution](exponential-distribution.md), a test of rate at most $\lambda_0$ against rate greater than $\lambda_0$ rejects for small sample sums. All simple-alternative [likelihood ratios](likelihood-ratio.md) against $\lambda_0$ decrease with that sum; its [gamma distribution](gamma-distribution.md) calibrates the size at $\lambda_0$. Monotonicity of the rejection probability in the rate proves that this is a [uniformly most powerful test](uniformly-most-powerful-test.md) for the composite hypotheses.
