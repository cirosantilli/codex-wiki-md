# Multinomial deviance

↑ **Parent:** [Multinomial likelihood](multinomial-likelihood.md)

For counts with a [multinomial distribution](multinomial-distribution.md), this is twice the log likelihood ratio of the saturated cell-probability estimate to fixed probabilities $p_i$. Zero-count terms have limiting value zero. Simulation under the specified probabilities gives a direct reference distribution when asymptotic approximations are unsuitable.
