# Harmonic oscillator transition kernel

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator.md)

For [action](action.md) $\frac m2\int(\dot q^2-\omega^2q^2)dt$, the kernel is $\sqrt{m\omega/(2\pi i\sin\omega T)}\exp\{im\omega[(q_f^2+q_i^2)\cos\omega T-2q_fq_i]/(2\sin\omega T)\}$. The [Dirichlet oscillator determinant ratio](dirichlet-oscillator-determinant-ratio.md) supplies its prefactor, while the classical boundary [action](action.md) supplies its exponent. The [Feynman i-epsilon prescription](feynman-i-epsilon-prescription.md) specifies its continuation through caustics.
