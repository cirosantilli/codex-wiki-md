# Gamma-five convention without an imaginary unit

↑ **Parent:** [Gamma matrices](gamma-matrices.md)

For the mostly-minus [Minkowski metric](minkowski-metric.md), this product anticommutes with every [gamma matrix](gamma-matrices.md) and is anti-Hermitian in the standard representation. The conventional Hermitian [chirality](chirality-physics.md) matrix is $iG$. Nevertheless $\bar\psi G\psi$ is Hermitian, because $\gamma^0G\gamma^0=-G$. Therefore a real interaction coefficient in $-g\phi\bar\psi G\psi$ is compatible with [Hermitian](hermitian-operator.md) dynamics and gives the [Feynman vertex](interaction-vertex.md) $-igG$.
