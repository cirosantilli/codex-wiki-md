# Toeplitz index theorem for continuous symbols

↑ **Parent:** [Toeplitz operator](toeplitz-operator.md)

On the [Hardy space of the circle](hardy-space-of-the-circle.md), a continuous symbol gives a [Fredholm](fredholm-operator.md) Toeplitz operator exactly when it never vanishes. Nonvanishing gives a parametrix $T_{1/f}$ modulo compact semicommutators. Write $f(z)=z^ne^{h(z)}$, where $n$ is its counterclockwise [winding number](winding-number.md) and $h$ is continuous. Homotopy through nonvanishing symbols and [local constancy of the Fredholm index](local-constancy-of-the-fredholm-index.md) reduce the index to that of the shift $T_{z^n}$, namely $-n$. If $f$ vanishes at a point, normalized Hardy reproducing kernels concentrating there are weakly null unit vectors on which $T_f$ tends to zero, contradicting a Fredholm parametrix.
