# Uniform convergence of Bernstein polynomial derivatives

↑ **Parent:** [Derivatives of Bernstein polynomials](derivatives-of-bernstein-polynomials.md)

For fixed $r$ and $f\in C^r[0,1]$, uniform convergence of normalized [finite differences](finite-difference-split.md) to $f^{(r)}$, uniform continuity of $f^{(r)}$, and $(n)_r/n^r\to1$ imply $g_{n,r}\to f^{(r)}$ uniformly. Positivity and partition of unity make [Bernstein polynomials](bernstein-polynomial.md) contractions in the [supremum norm](supremum-norm.md). Consequently $\|B_{n-r}g_{n,r}-f^{(r)}\|_\infty\le\|g_{n,r}-f^{(r)}\|_\infty+\|B_{n-r}f^{(r)}-f^{(r)}\|_\infty\to0$. The differentiability assumption is essential; continuity alone does not define the target derivative.
