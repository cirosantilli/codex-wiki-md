# Antilepton

↑ **Parent:** [Lepton](lepton.md)

An [antilepton](antilepton.md) is the [antiparticle](antiparticle.md) of a [lepton](lepton.md), with opposite additive quantum numbers. A [Positron](positron.md) is the charged [antilepton](antilepton.md) corresponding to an [electron](electron.md).
