# Clinical recovery

↑ **Parent:** [Clinical outcome](clinical-outcome.md)

Meeting an explicitly specified [recovery](clinical-recovery.md) criterion in a clinical context. The criterion can concern [symptoms](symptom.md), [diagnosis](clinical-diagnosis.md) or functioning; published [recovery](clinical-recovery.md) percentages cannot be interpreted without knowing that definition, timing and denominator.
