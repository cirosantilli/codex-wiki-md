# Planetary mantle

↑ **Parent:** [Geophysics](geophysics-split.md)

The [planetary mantle](planetary-mantle.md) is the predominantly silicate rock region beneath a rocky planet's [crust](planetary-crust.md). On geological timescales, solid rock can undergo [material creep](creep-deformation.md) and support [mantle convection](mantle-convection.md) without wholesale melting.
