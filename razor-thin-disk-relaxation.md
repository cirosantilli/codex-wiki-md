# Razor-thin disk relaxation

↑ **Parent:** [Two-body relaxation](two-body-relaxation.md)

In an unsoftened [plane](plane.md) with number surface density $n_2$ and relative [speed](speed.md) $u$, an encounter strip has area $2u\,dt\,db$. Thus the [velocity](velocity.md) diffusion rate is $8G^2m^2n_2/u\int db/b^2$, dominated by the small-impact cutoff. Setting this cutoff to $Gm/u^2$ and requiring a change of order $u^2$ gives $t_{\rm rel}\sim u/(Gmn_2)$. With $u=\lambda v$, $n_2\sim N/R^2$ and $v^2\sim GNm/R$, this is of order $\lambda R/v$, independent of $N$ at fixed total [mass](mass.md). The point-particle, zero-thickness limit is singular; fixed softening or nonzero thickness changes it. Numerical coefficients are not controlled by extending weak kicks down to order-one deflections.
