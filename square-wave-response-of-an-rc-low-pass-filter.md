# Square-wave response of an RC low-pass filter

↑ **Parent:** [RC low-pass filter](rc-low-pass-filter.md)

For an equal-duty [square wave](square-wave.md) alternating between $\pm A$ with period $T$, the steady periodic [capacitor](capacitor.md) [voltage](voltage.md) has extrema $\pm v_p$. A positive half-period maps $-v_p$ to $A-(A+v_p)e^{-T/(2RC)}=v_p$, proving the title formula. The negative half-period is its sign-reversed copy. Short periods give a small nearly triangular output; long periods give input-like plateaux with exponential transitions.
