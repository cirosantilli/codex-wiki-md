# Inertial Brownian displacement with zero initial velocity

↑ **Parent:** [Underdamped Langevin dynamics](underdamped-langevin-dynamics.md)

A free particle following [Langevin dynamics](langevin-dynamics.md) initialized at zero position and velocity has relaxation time $\tau=m/\zeta$ and thermal [diffusivity](diffusion-coefficient.md) $D=k_BT/\zeta$. Its position is the convolution of the random force with $[1-e^{-t/\tau}]/\zeta$. The [fluctuation-dissipation relation for a Langevin particle](fluctuation-dissipation-relation-for-a-langevin-particle.md) gives force covariance amplitude $2\zeta k_BT$, and integrating the squared kernel yields the displayed [variance](variance-split.md). At short times it grows as $2Dt^3/(3\tau^2)$ because the initial velocity is cold; at long times it grows as $2Dt$. A Maxwellian initial velocity produces a different short-time transient.
