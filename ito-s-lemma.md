<h1 id="ito-s-lemma">Itô's lemma</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Stochastic calculus](stochastic-calculus-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Itô's_lemma)

For an Itô process $dX_t=b_t\,dt+\sigma_t\,dB_t$ and a twice differentiable function $f$,

$$
df(X_t)=f'(X_t)\,dX_t+\frac12f''(X_t)\sigma_t^2\,dt.
$$

The second-order term reflects the nonzero quadratic variation of [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md).
