# Musiela forward-curve equation

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian forward-rate field](gaussian-forward-rate-field.md)

For time-to-maturity coordinates $g_t(x)=f(t,t+x)$ and time-homogeneous deterministic [Heath-Jarrow-Morton model](heath-jarrow-morton-model.md) volatility, $A(x)=\langle\sigma(x),\int_0^x\sigma(u)du\rangle$. The displayed evolution contains the maturity shift. Its mild solution is $g_t(x)=g_0(x+t)+\int_0^t A(x+s)ds+\int_0^t\langle\sigma(x+t-s),dW_s\rangle$. The whole curve is a time-homogeneous [Markov process](markov-process-split.md) under suitable function-space well-posedness conditions; the current [short rate](short-rate.md) alone need not be a sufficient state.
