<h1 id="m-g-1-queue">M/G/1 queue</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Queueing theory](queueing-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M/G/1_queue)

An $M/G/1$ queue has Poisson arrivals, [independent identically distributed](independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md) service times, and one server. If service has mean $m$ and $\lambda m<1$, its mean busy period is

$$
\frac{m}{1-\lambda m}.
$$

This follows by decomposing the busy period into the first service and the busy-period descendants of arrivals during that service.
