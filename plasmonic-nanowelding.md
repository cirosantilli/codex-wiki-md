# Plasmonic nanowelding

↑ **Parent:** [Welding](welding.md)

Localized optical heating at a nanoscale metal junction can fuse a contact. In the demonstrated silver-wire process, the initial gap concentrates light; once electrical/physical contact forms, the optical hotspot changes and limits further heating. Comparing local morphology and [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) with unwelded or uniformly heated controls tests whether contacts improve without broad substrate damage.[Abstract and junction-localized heating mechanism](self-limited-plasmonic-welding-of-silver-nanowire-junctions-by-erik-c-garnett-and-others.md)
