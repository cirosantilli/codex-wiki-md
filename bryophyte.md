# Bryophyte

↑ **Parent:** [Plant](plant.md)

A member of the land-plant lineages comprising [mosses](moss.md), [liverworts](liverwort.md) and [hornworts](hornwort.md). Their persistent [gametophytes](gametophyte.md) support attached [sporophytes](sporophyte.md); physiological independence should not be inferred merely from a green sporophyte.
