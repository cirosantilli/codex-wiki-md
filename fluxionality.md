# Fluxionality

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular geometry](molecular-geometry.md)

Interconversion of molecular arrangements that exchanges chemically distinguishable sites. In [NMR spectroscopy](nuclear-magnetic-resonance-split.md), exchange fast compared with the relevant frequency difference can merge signals, so observed equivalence need not be a static [molecular symmetry](molecular-symmetry.md).
