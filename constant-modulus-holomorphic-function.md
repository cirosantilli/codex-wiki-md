# Constant-modulus holomorphic function

↑ **Parent:** [Holomorphic function](holomorphic-function-split.md)

A [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function-split.md) with constant modulus on a connected [domain](domain-mathematical-analysis.md) is constant. If $h=u+iv$ and $u^2+v^2=c^2>0$, differentiation and the [Cauchy-Riemann equations](cauchy-riemann-equations.md) give $uu_x+vv_x=0$ and $-uv_x+vu_x=0$. Their coefficient [determinant](determinant.md) is $-c^2$, so $u_x=v_x=0$, and all first [derivatives](derivative.md) vanish. When $c=0$, the conclusion is immediate. Consequently two nonvanishing [holomorphic functions](holomorphic-function-split.md) with equal moduli differ by a single constant phase on a connected [domain](domain-mathematical-analysis.md).
