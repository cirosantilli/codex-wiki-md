# Gravitational wake

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamical friction](dynamical-friction.md)

The stellar density and [Newtonian gravitational potential](newtonian-gravitational-potential.md) perturbation induced around a moving massive object. The stellar response includes both a forced component that follows the object and switch-on collective transients. In a homogeneous stable damped model, each late-time spatial harmonic is modified by the [gravitational dielectric function](gravitational-dielectric-function.md) at the object's forcing [frequency](frequency.md). The wake's [force](force-split.md) back on the object produces [dynamical friction](dynamical-friction.md).
