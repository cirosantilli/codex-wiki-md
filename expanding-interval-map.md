# Expanding interval map

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamical system](dynamical-system.md)

A piecewise smooth interval map is uniformly expanding when every smooth branch has derivative magnitude at least $\lambda>1$. A finite branch partition gives a symbolic itinerary and cylinders with diameters at most a constant times $\lambda^{-n}$. Expansion alone does not make every symbolic [Gibbs measure](gibbs-measure.md) absolutely continuous; the geometric potential and suitable branch regularity are essential.
