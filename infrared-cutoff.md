# Infrared cutoff

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier analysis](fourier-analysis-split.md)

A lower included [wavenumber](wavenumber.md). A finite periodic patch of size $L$ has smallest nonzero [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) of order $2\pi/L$; supports and [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md) can change the factor. The cutoff limits large-scale fluctuations.
