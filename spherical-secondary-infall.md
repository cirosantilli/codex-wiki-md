# Spherical secondary infall

↑ **Parent:** [Einstein-de Sitter universe](einstein-de-sitter-universe.md)

Spherical secondary infall follows concentric collisionless shells around a primordial overdensity. Each shell initially expands with the Hubble flow, turns around, collapses, and subsequently crosses other shells.
