# Quantum superposition

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum state](quantum-state.md)

A normalized [quantum state](quantum-state.md) is a [linear combination](linear-combination.md) of [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) vectors in a [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md). Its [density operator](density-matrix.md) is $|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|$. When at least two basis amplitudes are nonzero, it has off-diagonal terms and differs from the [mixed state](mixed-state.md) $\sum_j|a_j|^2|j\rangle\langle j|$. [Quantum gates](quantum-logic-gate.md) act linearly on this superposition and preserve its phase information.
