# Thermal stress

↑ **Parent:** [Thermoelasticity](thermoelasticity.md)

[Thermal stress](thermal-stress.md) is [stress](stress.md) generated when [thermal expansion](thermal-expansion.md) or contraction is constrained or incompatible. A uniformly heated isotropic body whose entire boundary is fixed has $e=0$ and $\tau=-K\alpha_v(T-T_0)I$. For a nonuniform temperature field, interior [displacement](displacement.md) generally remains nonzero even with a fixed outer boundary; [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) and compatibility must be solved together. Cancelling the divergence of a [thermal stress](thermal-stress.md) term with a [body force](body-force.md) is not the same as making that [stress](stress.md) vanish.
