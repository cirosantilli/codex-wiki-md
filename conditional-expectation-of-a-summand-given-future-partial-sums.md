# Conditional expectation of a summand given future partial sums

↑ **Parent:** [Conditional expectation](conditional-expectation.md)

For [independent and identically distributed random variables](independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md) $X_j$ that are [integrable random variables](integrable-random-variable.md), with $S_n=\sum_{j=1}^nX_j$, symmetry gives $\mathbb E[X_1\mid S_n,S_{n+1},\ldots]=S_n/n$. The decreasing [sigma-algebras](sigma-algebra.md) generated by these future sums permit application of the [reverse martingale convergence theorem](reverse-martingale-convergence-theorem.md).
