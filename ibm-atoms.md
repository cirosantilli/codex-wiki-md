# IBM (atoms)

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic manipulation](atomic-manipulation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IBM_(atoms))

A deliberately assembled xenon-atom surface pattern illustrating controlled [atomic manipulation](atomic-manipulation.md). [Don Eigler](don-eigler.md) and [Erhard Schweizer](erhard-schweizer.md) reported individual xenon positioning on single-crystal nickel at about $4\,\mathrm K$ in their 1990 paper.[abstract](positioning-single-atoms-with-a-scanning-tunnelling-microscope-by-don-eigler-and-erhard-schweizer.md)
