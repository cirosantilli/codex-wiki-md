# Diisobutylaluminium hydride

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md)

$\mathrm{(iBu)_2AlH}$, a hydride reducing reagent. Low-temperature, controlled-stoichiometry reduction can convert an [ester](ester.md) into an [aldehyde](aldehyde.md) or a [lactone](lactone.md) into a [lactol](lactol.md); conditions must prevent further reduction.
