# Opposite-handed counterrotating helical swimmer

↑ **Parent:** [Microswimmer](microswimmer.md)

Two coaxial opposite-handed [helices](helix.md) linked by an internal motor share a translation speed but counterrotate. In an additive local-drag model, a length ratio $n$ multiplies the second [helix](helix.md)'s diagonal resistances by $n$ and reverses its coupling. With $\Omega_1-\Omega_2=\omega$, force and torque balance give $U=-2nBD\omega/[AD(1+n)^2-B^2(1-n)^2]$.
