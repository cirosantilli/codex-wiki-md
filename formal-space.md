# Formal space

↑ **Parent:** [Sullivan minimal model](sullivan-minimal-model.md)

A space is formal over $\mathbb Q$ if its rational cochain commutative [differential graded algebra](differential-graded-algebra.md) is joined to $(H^*(X;\mathbb Q),0)$ by [quasi-isomorphisms](quasi-isomorphism.md). Equivalently in the usual [simply connected](simply-connected-space.md) Sullivan-model setting, its [minimal model](sullivan-minimal-model.md) has a [quasi-isomorphism](quasi-isomorphism.md) to its [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) algebra with zero differential. This says its [rational homotopy type](rational-homotopy-type.md) can be recovered from the [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) algebra; zero [cup products](cup-product.md) alone do not imply [formality of a topological space](formal-space.md) without an additional argument.
