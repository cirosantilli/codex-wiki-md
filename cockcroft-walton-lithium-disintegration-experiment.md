# Cockcroft-Walton lithium disintegration experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Electrostatic accelerator](electrostatic-accelerator.md)

In 1932 [John Douglas Cockcroft](john-douglas-cockcroft.md) and [Ernest Thomas Sinton Walton](ernest-thomas-sinton-walton.md) accelerated [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) [ions](ion.md) from a discharge source through an evacuated tube onto [lithium](lithium.md) at $45^\circ$. A side-facing [zinc sulfide](zinc-sulfide.md) screen, viewed by microscope, showed [particle scintillations](scintillation-physics.md). Mica stopped scattered [protons](proton.md); switching off or blocking the beam removed the flashes. An external screen allowed absorption-range measurements.[Experiments with High Velocity Positive Ions. II. The Disintegration of Elements by High Velocity Protons, 1932, pp. 230–232, sections 2–3, Fig. 1; primary text inspected in the Scribd transcription](experiments-with-high-velocity-positive-ions-ii-the-disintegration-of-elements-by-high-velocity-protons-by-john-douglas-cockcroft-and-ernest-thomas-sinton-walton.md)

Magnetic deflection checked voltage calibration. Opposite screens and independent observers found excess coincidences for a thin [lithium](lithium.md) target, supporting paired [alpha particle](alpha-particle.md) emission; geometry and screen efficiency limited detection.[pp. 231, 237–238, section 4, Figs. 5–6: corrected voltage calibration, paired screens and random-coincidence comparison](experiments-with-high-velocity-positive-ions-ii-the-disintegration-of-elements-by-high-velocity-protons-by-john-douglas-cockcroft-and-ernest-thomas-sinton-walton.md)

[John Douglas Cockcroft](john-douglas-cockcroft.md)'s 1951 lecture recalls detection at $125\,\mathrm{kV}$ and an absorption range about $8.4\,\mathrm{cm}$, then refined [alpha particle](alpha-particle.md) energies about $8.6\,\mathrm{MeV}$ each. He places the work under [Ernest Rutherford](ernest-rutherford.md)'s direction. The inferred [nuclear reaction](nuclear-reaction.md) was [lithium-7](lithium-7.md) plus a [proton](proton.md) producing two [alpha particles](alpha-particle.md).[Experiments on the interaction of high-speed nucleons with atomic nuclei, lecture delivered 11 December 1951, pp. 167–170, Figs. 2–4; primary text inspected in the Scribd transcription](experiments-on-the-interaction-of-high-speed-nucleons-with-atomic-nuclei-by-john-douglas-cockcroft.md)

The logic has several independent links. Beam blocking tests whether the flashes depend on bombardment. An absorber tests whether scattered beam particles could reach the screen. A [particle range](particle-range.md) tests product identity and [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md), while coincidences test whether two products belong to one event. These tests support a reaction interpretation jointly; counting flashes alone is not an isotope identification, and a range-to-energy conversion needs calibration. The [lithium proton reaction energy](lithium-proton-reaction-energy.md) below is a modern calculation, separate from those historical measurements.
