# Active spectrum of the power method

↑ **Parent:** [Power method](power-method.md)

For a [diagonalizable matrix](diagonalizable-matrix.md), the active spectrum consists of [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) whose [eigenspaces](eigenspace.md) have nonzero components of the starting [vector](vector.md) in its [spectral decomposition](spectral-decomposition.md). These components are [orthogonal projections](orthogonal-projection.md) for a [self-adjoint operator](self-adjoint-operator.md), but need not be orthogonal projections for a general [diagonalizable matrix](diagonalizable-matrix.md). With a unique largest [modulus](modulus.md) in this active spectrum, the [power method](power-method.md) selects its eigendirection; an exactly absent dominant component is never created in exact arithmetic.
