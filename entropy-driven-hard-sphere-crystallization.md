# Entropy-driven hard-sphere crystallization

↑ **Parent:** [Hard sphere](hard-sphere.md)

Crystallization favored by greater total accessible configurational [entropy](entropy-split.md), despite increased spatial order. Ordered cages at sufficiently high [packing fraction](packing-fraction.md) can allow more positional/free-volume configurations than a disordered crowded fluid.
