# Smoothed explicit formula for the Chebyshev function

↑ **Parent:** [First integral of the Chebyshev function](first-integral-of-the-chebyshev-function.md)

For $x>1$, moving the Mellin contour past the [pole](pole.md) at one, the [Nontrivial zeros of the Riemann zeta function](nontrivial-zero-of-the-riemann-zeta-function.md), and the remaining poles gives

$$
\Psi_1(x)=\frac{x^2}{2}-\sum_\rho\frac{x^{\rho+1}}{\rho(\rho+1)}-x\log(2\pi)+\frac{\zeta'(-1)}{\zeta(-1)}-\sum_{k\ge1}\frac{x^{1-2k}}{2k(2k-1)}.
$$

Zeros are included with multiplicity. The [Riemann–von Mangoldt formula](riemann-von-mangoldt-formula.md) makes the zero sum absolutely convergent. If every nontrivial zero has real part below one, division by $x^2$ and [dominated convergence](dominated-convergence-theorem.md) give $\Psi_1(x)\sim x^2/2$. Monotonicity of $\psi$ then recovers $\psi(x)\sim x$ by upper and lower difference quotients.
