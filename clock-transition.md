# Clock transition

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperfine structure](hyperfine-structure.md)

A transition whose [frequency](frequency.md) is stationary to first order in a specified perturbation at an operating point. For magnetic sensitivity, the derivative shown vanishes, but second and higher derivatives can remain nonzero. A [clock transition](clock-transition.md) need not have zero individual [energy](energy-split.md) slopes: equal slopes suffice. Optical light shifts and other perturbations require their own sensitivity analysis.
