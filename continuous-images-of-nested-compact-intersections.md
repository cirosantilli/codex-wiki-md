# Continuous images of nested compact intersections

↑ **Parent:** [Compact space](compact-space.md)

Let $X$ be a [Hausdorff space](hausdorff-space.md), let $K_n$ be a nested sequence of nonempty compact subsets of $X$, and let $f:X\to Y$ be a [continuous map](continuous-map.md) into a [T1 space](t1-space.md). Then the displayed equality holds. The forward inclusion is true for every map. For the reverse, fix $y\in\bigcap_nf(K_n)$. Each $F_n=K_n\cap f^{-1}(\{y\})$ is nonempty and closed in the compact set $K_1$: the $K_n$ are closed by the Hausdorff assumption, while the fiber is closed because $Y$ is T1 and $f$ is continuous. The nested sets $F_n$ have the [finite intersection property](finite-intersection-property.md), so compactness gives a point in their intersection. Its image is $y$.

The proof only needs the relevant fibers to be closed in $K_1$, rather than all of $Y$ to be T1. A [Hausdorff space](hausdorff-space.md) as codomain is a familiar sufficient special case. Mere continuity into an arbitrary space does not suffice.
