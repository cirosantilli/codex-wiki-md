# Completely positive matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Positive semidefinite matrix](positive-semidefinite-matrix.md)

A real [symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md) admitting a finite sum of nonnegative [rank-one matrices](rank-one-matrix.md) $x_jx_j^T$. It is both a [positive semidefinite matrix](positive-semidefinite-matrix.md) and a [nonnegative matrix](nonnegative-matrix.md), but these two properties alone need not imply membership in the [completely positive cone](completely-positive-cone.md) in arbitrary [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md). This [matrix](matrix.md) notion is distinct from a [completely positive map](completely-positive-map.md).
