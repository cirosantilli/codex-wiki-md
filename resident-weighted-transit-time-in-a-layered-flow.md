# Resident-weighted transit time in a layered flow

↑ **Parent:** [Porous-media flow](porous-media-flow-split.md)

Without transverse exchange, a particle chosen uniformly from pore volume samples each streamline's transit time with resident-volume weight. This differs from the [flux-weighted residence time in a porous layer](flux-weighted-residence-time-in-a-porous-layer.md), which samples at the inlet proportionally to $v$. For constant [porosity](porosity.md) and a linear positive [velocity](velocity.md) $v=v_0+(v_1-v_0)y/H$, the two means are $L\log(v_1/v_0)/(v_1-v_0)$ and $2L/(v_0+v_1)$, respectively. Their limits agree for uniform [velocity](velocity.md).
