# Three-dimensional Schoenflies theorem

↑ **Parent:** [3-manifold](3-manifold.md)

A smooth, piecewise-linear or locally flat embedded two-sphere in the [three-sphere](three-sphere.md) has two complementary components, each with closure homeomorphic to a three-ball. Equivalently a tame embedded two-sphere in $\mathbb R^3$ bounds a ball on its compact side. Local flatness is essential; arbitrary wild sphere embeddings are excluded.
