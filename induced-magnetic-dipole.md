# Induced magnetic dipole

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic dipole moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md)

A small linearly magnetizable sample has a [magnetic dipole moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md) induced by its applied field. In a fixed principal direction, write $m=\alpha_B B_0$; this convention gives $\alpha_B$ units $\mathrm{A\,m^2\,T^{-1}}$. A [uniformly magnetized ellipsoid](uniformly-magnetized-ellipsoid.md) of volume $V$ has $\alpha_B=V\chi/[\mu_0(1+N_d\chi)]$. The effective interaction [potential energy](potential-energy.md) at fixed source current is $-\alpha_BB_0^2/2$. The factor one half arises because the moment must be induced as the field is raised; differentiating $-m(B_0)B_0$ as though it described a permanent moment would count the response twice. The response model excludes remanence, nonlinear saturation and hysteresis.
