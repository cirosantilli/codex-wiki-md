# Compact Linear Collider

↑ **Parent:** [Linear collider](linear-collider.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compact_Linear_Collider)

A proposed electron–positron [linear collider](linear-collider.md) using normal-conducting RF structures powered through a separate drive-beam deceleration system. The 2012 conceptual design studied multi-TeV operation and lower-energy stages; its $3$ TeV design used approximately $100$ MV/m main-linac gradients. Historical design targets should be distinguished from later staging revisions and from operating measurements. [CLIC Conceptual Design Report, CERN-2012-007, abstract and accelerator concept](https://cds.cern.ch/record/1500095)
