# Absolute Galois group of the complex Laurent series field

↑ **Parent:** [Finite extensions of the complex Laurent series field](finite-extensions-of-the-complex-laurent-series-field.md)

Inside an algebraic closure, choose compatible roots $t^{1/n}$. The fields $\mathbb C((t^{1/n}))$ exhaust all finite extensions by [finite extensions of the complex Laurent series field](finite-extensions-of-the-complex-laurent-series-field.md). Restriction from a degree-$m$ field to a degree-$n$ field for $n\mid m$ sends an exponent modulo $m$ to its reduction modulo $n$. The [absolute Galois group](absolute-galois-group.md) is their [inverse limit](inverse-limit.md), namely the [profinite completion](profinite-completion.md) of $\mathbb Z$. This is a procyclic group, rather than the direct-limit torsion group of all complex roots of unity.
