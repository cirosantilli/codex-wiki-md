# Polynomial growth of a finitely generated two-step nilpotent group

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial growth of a group](polynomial-growth-of-a-group.md)

Every finitely generated [two-step nilpotent group](two-step-nilpotent-group.md) has [polynomial growth of a group](polynomial-growth-of-a-group.md). If $s_1,\ldots,s_k$ generate it, centrality of the commutators collects every word of length at most $n$ into

$$
s_1^{a_1}\cdots s_k^{a_k}\prod_{i<j}[s_i,s_j]^{b_{ij}},
$$

where $|a_i|\leq n$ and $|b_{ij}|=O(n^2)$. There are therefore only polynomially many possible collected words.
