# Frobenius conjugate

↑ **Parent:** [Finite-field Frobenius automorphism](finite-field-frobenius-automorphism.md)

Over $\mathbb F_q$, the roots of the [minimal polynomial](minimal-polynomial.md) of $\alpha\in\mathbb F_{q^n}$ are its distinct iterates $\alpha,\alpha^q,\alpha^{q^2},\ldots$ under the [finite-field Frobenius automorphism](finite-field-frobenius-automorphism.md).
