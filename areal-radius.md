# Areal radius

↑ **Parent:** [General relativity](general-relativity-split.md)

In a spherically symmetric [spacetime](spacetime.md), the [areal radius](areal-radius.md) $r$ is defined by the area $A=4\pi r^2$ of each symmetry sphere. It is independent of which radial coordinate chart is used.
