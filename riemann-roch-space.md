# Riemann-Roch space

↑ **Parent:** [Divisor on an algebraic curve](divisor-on-an-algebraic-curve.md)

The Riemann-Roch space of a divisor $D$ is the finite-dimensional vector space

$$
L(D)=\{f\in k(X)^\times:(f)+D\geq0\}\cup\{0\}.
$$

Its elements are the [rational functions](rational-function.md) whose poles are bounded by the negative coefficients of $D$.
