# Weierstrass construction from Riemann-Roch in genus one

↑ **Parent:** [Weierstrass equation of an elliptic curve](weierstrass-equation-of-an-elliptic-curve.md)

Let $E$ be a [genus one curve](genus-one-curve.md) and choose $O\in E$. Since a canonical divisor is linearly equivalent to zero, [Riemann--Roch](riemann-roch-theorem.md) gives $\ell(nO)=n$ for $n\geq1$. Choose $x\in L(2O)\setminus L(O)$ and $y\in L(3O)\setminus L(2O)$. Their pole orders at $O$ are two and three. The seven functions

$$
1,x,y,x^2,xy,x^3,y^2\in L(6O)
$$

are linearly dependent because $\ell(6O)=6$, and comparison of pole orders puts the relation in [Weierstrass form](weierstrass-equation-of-an-elliptic-curve.md). The complete linear system $|3O|$ is very ample and realizes this relation as a smooth plane cubic. In characteristic other than two and three, completing the square and translating $x$ produce a [Short Weierstrass form](short-weierstrass-form.md).
