<h1 id="weil-chatelet-group">Weil–Châtelet group</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Galois cohomology](galois-cohomology.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weil–Châtelet_group)

For an [abelian variety](abelian-variety-split.md) $A$ over a field $K$, its Weil–Châtelet group is the [Galois cohomology](galois-cohomology.md) group $H^1(K,A(K^{\rm sep}))$, classifying torsors under $A$. Such an [algebraic-group torsor](algebraic-group-torsor.md) becomes $A$ after passing to a separable closure but need not have a $K$-point. A chosen geometric point identifies the torsor with $A$ and its Galois descent with a cocycle; changing that point adds a coboundary. The zero class is precisely the torsor with a rational point. For an [elliptic curve](elliptic-curve.md), these torsors are genus-one curves with a specified action and Jacobian. Over a number field the kernel of restriction to every completion is the [Tate–Shafarevich group](tate-shafarevich-group.md).
