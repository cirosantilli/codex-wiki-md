# Massless little-group generators

↑ **Parent:** [Massless particle little group](massless-particle-little-group.md)

For a standard null momentum $(E,0,0,E)$ in signature $(+---)$, define $K_i=M^{0i}$ and $J_i=\epsilon_{ijk}M^{jk}/2$. The stabilizing generators are $J_3,N_1,N_2$. The [Poincare algebra](poincare-algebra.md) gives $[J_3,N_1]=iN_2$, $[J_3,N_2]=-iN_1$ and $[N_1,N_2]=0$, the Lie algebra of planar Euclidean motions. Evaluating the [Pauli-Lubanski pseudovector](pauli-lubanski-pseudovector.md) gives $W^2=-E^2(N_1^2+N_2^2)$.
