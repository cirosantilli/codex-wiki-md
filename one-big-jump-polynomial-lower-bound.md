# One-big-jump polynomial lower bound

↑ **Parent:** [Pareto distribution](pareto-distribution.md)

For nonnegative [independent](independent-random-variables.md) identically distributed summands with polynomial upper tail $\mathbb P(X_1\geq x)=x^{-\beta}$ for $x\geq1$, positivity gives $\mathbb P(S_n\geq na)\geq(na)^{-\beta}$ for fixed $a>0$ and large $n$. Since this [probability](probability.md) is also at most one, its logarithm divided by $n$ tends to zero. No positive exponential moment exists, so the exponential-moment form of the [Cramér theorem](cramer-s-theorem.md) cannot be applied. The bound proves a logarithmic rate and does not assert an exact tail asymptotic.
