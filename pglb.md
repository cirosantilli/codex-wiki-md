# PglB

↑ **Parent:** [Enzyme](enzyme-split.md)

[PglB](pglb.md) is a bacterial oligosaccharyltransferase whose structural characterization supplies a model for protein-acceptor recognition. Its donor and complete pathway should not be assumed identical to those of eukaryotic ER glycosylation.
