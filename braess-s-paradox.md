<h1 id="braess-s-paradox">Braess's paradox</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Wardrop equilibrium](wardrop-equilibrium.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Braess's_paradox)

Adding a link to a nonatomic traffic [flow network](flow-network.md) can increase the equilibrium travel delay. Extra routes change the incentives defining a [Wardrop equilibrium](wardrop-equilibrium.md), so the new equilibrium need not retain an earlier good allocation. The system-optimal delay cannot worsen when the old allocation remains feasible; the paradox concerns selfish equilibrium.
