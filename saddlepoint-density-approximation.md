# Saddlepoint density approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Cumulant-generating function](cumulant-generating-function.md)

For independent identically distributed continuous random variables, let $K(t)=\log\mathbb E e^{tY}$ be finite near zero. At a mean value $x$ reached by $K'(\widehat t)=x$ in the interior of that domain, the displayed leading approximation describes the density of the sum $S_n$. [Exponential tilting](exponential-tilting.md) by $\widehat t$ puts the sum at its tilted mean; a local normal approximation gives the denominator, and undoing the tilt gives the exponential factor. Under suitable smooth nonlattice regularity, at fixed interior $x$ the exact density is the displayed quantity times $1+O(n^{-1})$. This statistical approximation is an application of the [saddle-point approximation](saddle-point-approximation.md) to a transform inversion integral. It requires more than the existence of a few ordinary moments.
