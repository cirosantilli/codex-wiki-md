# Green et al. Paranoid Thoughts Scale

↑ **Parent:** [Psychological assessment](psychological-assessment.md)

A [self-report](self-report.md) measure used to assess paranoid thinking, including ideas of reference and persecution. The total score and a persecution subscale should not be substituted for each other after a trial has specified its [primary outcome](primary-outcome.md).[JAMA Psychiatry 78, 714–725; 361 in analysis, prespecified 24-week GPTS total endpoint and secondary outcomes](effects-of-slowmo-a-blended-digital-therapy-targeting-reasoning-on-paranoia-among-people-with-psychosis-a-randomized-clinical-trial-by-philippa-garety-and-others.md)
