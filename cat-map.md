# Cat map

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic toral automorphism](hyperbolic-toral-automorphism.md)

The [cat map](cat-map.md) is the [area-preserving](area-preserving-map.md) [toral automorphism](toral-automorphism.md) induced by the [integer matrix](integer-matrix.md) $A=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix}$. Its [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are $(3\pm\sqrt5)/2$, giving one expanding and one contracting [eigendirection](eigenvector.md). Cutting its image [parallelogram](parallelogram.md) by the [integer](integer-split.md) grid and translating the pieces [modulo one](modular-arithmetic-split.md) gives a concrete geometric construction on the [unit square](square.md).
