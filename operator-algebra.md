# Operator algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Functional analysis](functional-analysis-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operator_algebra)

An [operator algebra](operator-algebra.md) is an algebra of [linear operators](linear-operator.md), with multiplication given by composition. In quantum examples it can be generated by creation and annihilation operators subject to the [canonical commutation relations](canonical-commutation-relation.md) or [canonical anticommutation relations](canonical-anticommutation-relations.md). Its analytic realization requires specifying a common domain for unbounded operators.
