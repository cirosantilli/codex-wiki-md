# Chiral spinor curvature operator

↑ **Parent:** [Two-component spinor calculus](two-component-spinor-calculus.md)

The [spinor covariant derivative](spinor-covariant-derivative.md) commutator is a [differential two-form](2-form.md) in its derivative indices, so the [two-component spinor calculus](two-component-spinor-calculus.md) decomposition defines symmetric curvature operators $\Delta_{AB}$ and $\Delta_{A'B'}$. With $\epsilon^{AB}\epsilon_{AB}=2$, the unprimed projection is $\Delta_{AB}=\epsilon^{A'B'}[\nabla_{AA'},\nabla_{BB'}]/2$, and the primed projection is analogous. For a [torsion-free connection](torsion-free-connection.md) these are algebraic curvature derivations: they annihilate scalar functions and obey the [Leibniz rule](leibniz-rule.md) on tensor products.
