# Category of finite sets

↑ **Parent:** [Category of sets](category-of-sets.md)

The [category](category-split.md) whose objects are [finite sets](finite-set.md) and whose [morphisms](morphism.md) are all [functions](function-split.md) between them. Finite [categorical limits](categorical-limit.md) and [colimits](colimit.md) are the ordinary set constructions. It is essentially small: a [skeleton of a category](skeleton-of-a-category.md) consists of the sets $\{0,\ldots,n-1\}$ for $n\in\mathbb N$. Its [Yoneda embedding](yoneda-embedding.md) preserves limits but generally does not preserve coproducts: evaluating $y1\amalg y1$ at a two-element set gives two elements, while evaluating $y2$ gives four.
