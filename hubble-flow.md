# Hubble flow

↑ **Parent:** [Hubble parameter](hubble-parameter.md)

The homogeneous expansion velocity is proportional to physical position:

$$
\mathbf u=H\mathbf r=aH\mathbf x=\dot a\,\mathbf x.
$$

The velocity-distance relation is [Hubble's law](hubble-s-law.md); a peculiar velocity is a departure from this homogeneous flow.
