# Goldstone directions in the scalar mass matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Goldstone theorem](goldstone-theorem.md)

For a smooth invariant [scalar potential](scalar-potential.md), differentiating $\partial_I V\,(it^a\phi)^I=0$ at a [classical vacuum](classical-vacuum.md) gives $M^2(it^a\phi_0)=0$. The symmetry tangent space has dimension $\dim G-\dim H_0$, where $H_0$ is the full [stabilizer subgroup](stabilizer-subgroup.md). With a positive nonsingular scalar [kinetic term](kinetic-term.md), these are massless directions of the quadratic theory. Further zero [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are possible unless the [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) is positive on the normal complement.
