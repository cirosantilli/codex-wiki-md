# Fubini-Study form

↑ **Parent:** [Fubini-Study metric](fubini-study-metric.md)

The Fubini-Study form is the standard Kähler form on complex projective space. With the integral normalization, $[\omega_{FS}/(2\pi)]=c_1(\mathcal O(1))$.

The two-form is associated with the [Fubini-Study metric](fubini-study-metric.md).
