# Monodromy matrix of a periodic linear system

↑ **Parent:** [Floquet theory](floquet-theory.md)

For $\dot Y=K(t)Y$ with coefficient period $P$, the monodromy matrix maps $Y(0)$ to $Y(P)$. Its [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are the [Floquet multipliers](floquet-multiplier.md). For piecewise constant coefficients it is the time-ordered product of the segment [matrix exponentials](matrix-exponential.md), with the earliest segment on the right. Its determinant is $\exp(\int_0^P\operatorname{tr}K(t)\,dt)$.
