# Condensation energy of a superconductor

↑ **Parent:** [Superconductivity](superconductivity-split.md)

The [condensation energy of a superconductor](condensation-energy-of-a-superconductor.md) is the free-energy lowering on entering the superconducting state, with sign convention specified. For a local Landau density $a|\Delta|^2+b|\Delta|^4/2$ and $a<0$, its minimum is $-a^2/(2b)$ relative to the normal-state value. The positive [energy](energy-split.md) required to destroy the condensate is the opposite sign.
