# Metabolic flux

↑ **Parent:** [Metabolic pathway](metabolic-pathway.md)

A [metabolic flux](metabolic-flux.md) is the rate of material flow through a specified reaction or pathway. Pool size, transcript abundance and maximal [enzyme](enzyme-split.md) activity do not individually determine it.
