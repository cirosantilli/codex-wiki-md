# Polygonal connectivity of a finite-line complement

↑ **Parent:** [Path-connected space](path-connected-space.md)

The complement of finitely many lines in $\mathbb R^3$ is [path-connected](path-connected-space.md), and any two points can be joined by two line segments. For endpoints $a,b$ outside the lines, exclude the finitely many affine planes through each endpoint and each removed line. Choose a bend point $c$ outside all those planes. If either segment met a removed line, the bend point would lie in its associated excluded plane, a contradiction.
