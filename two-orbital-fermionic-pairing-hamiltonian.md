# Two-orbital fermionic pairing Hamiltonian

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic fermion Hamiltonian](quadratic-fermion-hamiltonian.md)

For distinct [fermionic](fermion.md) orbitals obeying the full [canonical anticommutation relations](canonical-anticommutation-relations.md), fix the [Fock state](fock-state.md) order $|11\rangle=a^\dagger b^\dagger|0\rangle$. With real $\varepsilon,\Delta>0$, the even-[fermion parity](fermion-parity.md) [matrix](matrix.md) is $\begin{pmatrix}0&-\Delta\\-\Delta&2\varepsilon\end{pmatrix}$, since $ab|11\rangle=-|0\rangle$. The two odd-[fermion parity](fermion-parity.md) [energy eigenvalues](energy-eigenvalue-split.md) are both $\varepsilon$. The even-[fermion parity](fermion-parity.md) [energy eigenvalues](energy-eigenvalue-split.md) are $\varepsilon\pm\sqrt{\varepsilon^2+\Delta^2}$. This finite model isolates the relative sign and constant shift required in a fermionic [Bogoliubov transformation](bogoliubov-transformation.md) without any momentum sum or particle-hole doubling factor.
