# Annual stellar aberration amplitude

↑ **Parent:** [Relativistic aberration](relativistic-aberration.md)

At a sightline perpendicular to [Earth](earth.md)'s instantaneous orbital [velocity](velocity.md), a heliocentrically transverse ray acquires an apparent angular displacement towards the observer's motion. A [telescope](telescope.md) of [length](length.md) $L$ moves transversely by $v_\oplus L/c$ during the light crossing, giving the first-order result. The exact transverse Lorentz boost gives $\sin\delta=v_\oplus/c$. For a circular orbital [radius](radius.md) $R$ and year $Y$, $v_\oplus=2\pi R/Y$. The [amplitude](wave-amplitude.md) is half the full opposite-season excursion for a suitable sightline; it is not annual parallax, which depends on stellar distance.
