# Relative entropy bound for the unnormalized Hellinger distance

↑ **Parent:** [Hellinger distance](hellinger-distance.md)

The scalar inequality $-\log u\geq1-u$, applied to $u=\sqrt{q/p}$ and integrated against $P$, gives $K(P,Q)\geq2-2\int\sqrt{pq}\,d\mu$. The right side is the unnormalized squared [Hellinger distance](hellinger-distance.md). Infinite divergence and zero density regions are handled by the usual extended-integral conventions.
