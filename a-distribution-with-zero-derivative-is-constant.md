# A distribution with zero derivative is constant

↑ **Parent:** [Distributional derivative](distributional-derivative.md)

If $v'=0$ on $\mathbb R$, then $v$ is a constant [distribution](distribution-mathematical-analysis.md). Every [test function](test-function.md) of integral zero is the derivative of its compactly supported primitive, so $v$ vanishes on those test functions. Choosing one test function $\eta$ of integral one gives $\langle v,\varphi\rangle=\langle v,\eta\rangle\int\varphi$. Iteration shows that a distribution with $m$th derivative zero is a [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) of degree at most $m-1$.
