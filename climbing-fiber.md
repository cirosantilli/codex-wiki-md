# Climbing fiber

↑ **Parent:** [Axon](axon.md)

An axon from the inferior olive with powerful input to a [Purkinje cell](purkinje-cell.md). Its activity can evoke a complex spike and participate in an instructive pathway for [cerebellar error-driven learning](cerebellar-error-driven-learning.md).
