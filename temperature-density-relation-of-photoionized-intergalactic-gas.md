# Temperature-density relation of photoionized intergalactic gas

↑ **Parent:** [Intergalactic medium](intergalactic-medium.md)

Diffuse gas in [photoionization equilibrium](photoionization-equilibrium.md) is often approximately described by the displayed [temperature](temperature.md)-[density contrast](density-contrast.md) relation. Photoheating and expansion cooling produce its thermal history. It is an empirical model for low-density gas, not the adiabatic equation of state of every intergalactic phase. [Lyman-alpha forest](lyman-alpha-forest.md) line widths constrain both the temperature at a reference density and the slope through their lower envelope.
