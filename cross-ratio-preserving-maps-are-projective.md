# Cross-ratio preserving maps are projective

↑ **Parent:** [Cross-ratio normalization of four projective points](cross-ratio-normalization-of-four-projective-points.md)

An [injective](injective-function.md) self-map of the complex [projective line](projective-line.md) preserving all [cross-ratios](cross-ratio.md) is a [projective transformation](projective-linear-transformation.md); neither continuity nor prior surjectivity is needed. Choose the unique projectivity agreeing with the map on three distinct [projective points](projective-point.md) and compose with its inverse. The resulting map fixes those three points. Normalize them to $\infty,0,1$. Preservation of the [cross-ratio](cross-ratio.md) $(\infty\,0:1\,z)=z$ now fixes every other point as well, proving that the normalized map is the [identity map](identity-function.md). Undoing the composition proves the assertion.
