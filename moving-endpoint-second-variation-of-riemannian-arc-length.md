# Moving-endpoint second variation of Riemannian arc length

↑ **Parent:** [Second variation of Riemannian arc length](second-variation-of-riemannian-arc-length.md)

For a variation of a unit-speed [geodesic](geodesic.md), let $T$ be its tangent, $J$ its [variation vector field](variation-vector-field.md) and $A=\nabla_sJ$ its variation acceleration. The [second variation of Riemannian arc length](second-variation-of-riemannian-arc-length.md) has the displayed endpoint term in addition to the [Riemannian index form](riemannian-index-form.md) of $J^\perp=J-\langle J,T\rangle T$. Differentiating the speed twice gives $|D_tJ|^2-\langle D_tJ,T\rangle^2+\langle D_sD_tJ,T\rangle$. Commuting derivatives and integrating $D_tA$ by parts gives the formula. The term cannot be discarded unless the endpoints are fixed or an appropriate endpoint condition makes it zero.
