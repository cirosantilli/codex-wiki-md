# Altitude of a triangle

↑ **Parent:** [Triangle](triangle.md)

An altitude of a [triangle](triangle.md) is the whole [line](straight-line.md) through one vertex that is [orthogonal](orthogonal-vectors.md) to the [line](straight-line.md) through the opposite side. For vertex $A$ and opposite vertices $B,C$, its [equation](equation.md) in [position vectors](position-vector.md) is $(\mathbf x-\mathbf a)\cdot(\mathbf c-\mathbf b)=0$. The three such [lines](straight-line.md) meet at the [orthocenter](orthocenter.md); they need not meet inside the [triangle](triangle.md).
