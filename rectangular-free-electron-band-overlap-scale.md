# Rectangular free-electron band-overlap scale

↑ **Parent:** [Nearly-free electron model](nearly-free-electron-model.md)

For rectangular real-space periods $a>b$, the free first-zone corner has [energy](energy-split.md) $\hbar^2\pi^2(a^{-2}+b^{-2})/(2m)$, whereas the free second-zone minimum is $\hbar^2\pi^2/(2ma^2)$. Their difference is the displayed overlap. A divalent spin-degenerate system is a [band insulator](band-insulator.md) only when the actual first-band maximum lies below the second-band minimum. A rigid symmetric splitting $\Delta$ removes the overlap if $\Delta>\Delta_{\mathrm{overlap}}$, but an arbitrary [periodic potential](periodic-potential.md) does not shift every corner and face by the same amount. The exact criterion uses the [indirect band gap](indirect-band-gap.md), not a geometry-only local direct-gap width.
