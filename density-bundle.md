# Density bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Vector bundle](vector-bundle.md)

On an $n$-dimensional [smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md), the density bundle $|\Lambda^nT^*M|$ is the real [line bundle](line-bundle.md) whose coordinate transformation uses the absolute value of the [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md). A density can therefore be integrated without an [orientation of a smooth manifold](orientation-of-a-smooth-manifold.md). A [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md) gives the positive density $\sqrt{\det(g_{ij})}|dx^1\cdots dx^n|$, including on a nonorientable manifold.
