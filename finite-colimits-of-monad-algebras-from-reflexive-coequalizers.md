# Finite colimits of monad algebras from reflexive coequalizers

↑ **Parent:** [Coproduct presentation for monad algebras](coproduct-presentation-for-monad-algebras.md)

If $\mathcal C$ has finite [colimits](colimit.md) and a [monad](monad.md) $T$ preserves [reflexive coequalizers](reflexive-coequalizer.md), its [Eilenberg-Moore category](eilenberg-moore-category.md) has finite colimits. The forgetful [functor](functor.md) creates reflexive coequalizers preserved by $T$. The [coproduct presentation for monad algebras](coproduct-presentation-for-monad-algebras.md) yields binary coproducts, and $F0$ is initial. Any pair $a,b:X\rightrightarrows Y$ can then be replaced by the [reflexive pair](reflexive-pair.md) $[a,1],[b,1]:X+Y\rightrightarrows Y$, with identical coequalizers. Finite coproducts and coequalizers give all finite colimits.
