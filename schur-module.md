# Schur module

↑ **Parent:** [Schur functor](schur-functor.md)

A Schur [module](module-mathematics.md) is the value of a [Schur functor](schur-functor.md) on a [vector space](vector-space-split.md). Over the [complex numbers](complex-number.md) it is either zero or an irreducible [polynomial representation](polynomial-representation-of-the-general-linear-group.md) of the [general linear group](general-linear-group.md). Its [character](character-of-a-representation.md) is the [Schur polynomial](schur-polynomial.md) $s_\lambda$ in the [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md), and its highest weight is $\lambda$ padded with zeros.
