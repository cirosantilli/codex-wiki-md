# Sullivan minimal-model classification

↑ **Parent:** [Sullivan minimal model](sullivan-minimal-model.md)

For [simply connected](simply-connected-space.md) [rational spaces](rational-space.md) of finite type, isomorphic minimal Sullivan models are equivalent to the same [rational homotopy type](rational-homotopy-type.md). A [quasi-isomorphism](quasi-isomorphism.md) between [minimal models](sullivan-minimal-model.md) is an isomorphism, so a zigzag of cochain [quasi-isomorphisms](quasi-isomorphism.md) produces the same [minimal model](sullivan-minimal-model.md). Existence and uniqueness come from adjoining closed generators to represent missing [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) and generators with prescribed decomposable differential to kill kernel classes, degree by degree.
