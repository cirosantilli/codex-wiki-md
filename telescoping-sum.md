# Telescoping sum

↑ **Parent:** [Summation](summation.md)

A [telescoping sum](telescoping-sum.md) cancels intermediate terms, leaving boundary terms. For a finite [sequence](sequence.md) $a_0,\ldots,a_n$, the displayed identity follows because every $a_1,\ldots,a_{n-1}$ occurs once with each sign. Applying it to an infinite [series](series-mathematics.md) also requires checking the [limit](limit-of-a-function.md) of the boundary terms; finite cancellation alone does not prove that the [series](series-mathematics.md) is a [convergent series](convergent-series.md).
