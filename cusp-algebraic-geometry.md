# Cusp (algebraic geometry)

↑ **Parent:** [Singular point of an algebraic variety](singular-point-of-an-algebraic-variety.md)

A unibranch [singular point of an algebraic variety](singular-point-of-an-algebraic-variety.md) on a plane [algebraic curve](algebraic-curve.md). The ordinary cusp has local equation $y^2=x^3$ and parametrization $(x,y)=(t^2,t^3)$; its branch has a repeated tangent and a vanishing first derivative at the singular point. Higher-order cusps may have different local equations. A [cuspidal cubic](cuspidal-cubic.md) gives the basic projective example.
