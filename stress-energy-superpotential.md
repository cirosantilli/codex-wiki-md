# Stress-energy superpotential

↑ **Parent:** [Stress-energy tensor improvement](stress-energy-tensor-improvement.md)

A [stress-energy superpotential](stress-energy-superpotential.md) is an antisymmetric tensor $B^{\rho\mu\nu}=-B^{\mu\rho\nu}$ whose divergence supplies a [stress-energy tensor improvement](stress-energy-tensor-improvement.md). In the [Maxwell field](electromagnetic-field.md), the potential-dependent expression $B^{\rho\mu}{}_{\nu}=F^{\mu\rho}A_\nu$ produces the difference between canonical and gauge-invariant [stress-energy tensors](stress-energy-tensor.md) [on shell](on-shell.md). Antisymmetry ensures identically conserved improvement terms.
