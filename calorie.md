# Calorie

↑ **Parent:** [Energy](energy-split.md)

An [energy](energy-split.md) unit with several historical definitions. The thermochemical small [calorie](calorie.md) is exactly $4.184$ [joules](joule.md); a kilocalorie is one thousand such [calorie](calorie.md) units. A calorimetric calculation must specify its unit convention.
