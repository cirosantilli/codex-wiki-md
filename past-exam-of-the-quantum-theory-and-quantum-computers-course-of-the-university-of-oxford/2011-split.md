# 2011

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the quantum theory and quantum computers course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

This is the standalone Part C paper for Quantum Theory and Quantum Computers, held on Wednesday 15 June 2011, 9.30am–11.00am. Its three questions match questions 4–6 of section B in the combined Part B paper B7.1/C7.1, formal code `DMAB 2686`. The original solutions are reused here with the standalone question numbering, including the correction to the missing Fourier normalization factor.

Source release: [Oxford examination archive release thread](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/maths_past_papers_3), Part C bundle pages 438–440.

Answer availability checked on 28 October 2026: bounded official-source searches by year, title, module and formal code found no exact full-paper or selected-part solution advertisement. The check was independent of any release policy for later years.
