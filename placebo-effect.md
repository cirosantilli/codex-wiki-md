# Placebo effect

↑ **Parent:** [Psychological research methods](psychological-research-methods.md)

A change associated with treatment context or expectation rather than the specific active component being tested. A comparison with no treatment and appropriate masking is needed to distinguish a [placebo effect](placebo-effect.md) from spontaneous change or reporting differences.
