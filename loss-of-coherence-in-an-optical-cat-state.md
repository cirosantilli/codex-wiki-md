# Loss of coherence in an optical cat state

↑ **Parent:** [Schrödinger cat state](schrodinger-cat-state.md)

A pure-loss channel of transmissivity $\eta$ maps the two branches to system amplitudes $\pm\sqrt\eta\alpha$ and environment amplitudes $\pm\sqrt{1-\eta}\alpha$. Tracing over the environment multiplies their cross terms by its branch overlap, the factor displayed. Large cat states can therefore lose interference rapidly even while their mean [intensities](intensity-physics.md) change only moderately.
