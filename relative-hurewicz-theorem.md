# Relative Hurewicz theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Hurewicz theorem](hurewicz-theorem.md)

If $X$ and $A$ are [simply connected](simply-connected-space.md) and the pair is $(r-1)$-connected, with $r\geq3$, its first possible nonzero relative [homotopy group](homotopy-group.md) maps isomorphically to relative integral [homology](homology-split.md). This lets a basis of a free relative [homology](homology-split.md) group be realized by attaching disks.
