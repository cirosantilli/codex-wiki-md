# Exponential-disk asymmetric drift

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar asymmetric drift](stellar-asymmetric-drift.md)

For a stationary axisymmetric tracer with isotropic [velocity dispersion](velocity-dispersion.md), vanishing tilted stress, density $\nu\propto e^{-R/R_d}$, and $\sigma^2\propto e^{-R/R_\sigma}$, the radial [Jeans equation](jeans-equation.md) gives the displayed expression. More generally its right side is $-\sigma^2 d\ln(\nu\sigma^2)/d\ln R$. It requires a nonnegative streaming-speed square; the density and dispersion laws must describe the same tracer and the chosen height or consistent vertical average.
