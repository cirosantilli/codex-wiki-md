# Eccentricity and semimajor-axis invariant of a truncated resonance

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrange planetary equations](lagrange-planetary-equations.md)

If retained [Lagrange planetary equations](lagrange-planetary-equations.md) have $\dot a=-2(p+q)ae^q C_r\sin\phi$ and $\dot e=-qe^{q-1}C_r\sin\phi$, then $d(e^2)/d\log a=q/(p+q)$. Thus $e^2-[q/(p+q)]\log(a/a_{\rm ref})$ is constant. A resonantly oscillating [orbital eccentricity](orbital-eccentricity.md)–[semi-major axis](semi-major-axis.md) trajectory retraces one curve rather than enclosing area in that projection.
