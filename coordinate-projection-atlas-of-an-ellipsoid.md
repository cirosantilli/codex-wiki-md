# Coordinate-projection atlas of an ellipsoid

↑ **Parent:** [Ellipsoid](ellipsoid.md)

For nonzero real $a_i$, scale the ambient coordinates by $q_i=a_i r_i$. On the half where $\varepsilon q_i>0$, omitting $q_i$ is a [manifold chart](manifold-chart.md) onto the [open unit ball](open-unit-ball.md); its inverse restores $q_i=\varepsilon\sqrt{1-\sum_{j\ne i}q_j^2}$. These $2(n+1)$ charts form a [smooth atlas](smooth-atlas.md) of the [ellipsoid](ellipsoid.md), including for negative $a_i$.
