# Transported addition on the multiplicative natural-number monoid

↑ **Parent:** [Commutative-monoid enrichment](commutative-monoid-enrichment.md)

For a multiplicative [monoid automorphism](monoid-automorphism.md) $\phi:\mathbb N\to\mathbb N$ fixing $0$, define $a\oplus b=\phi^{-1}(\phi(a)+\phi(b))$. This transports a [semiring](semiring.md) structure to the fixed underlying multiplication, hence a [commutative-monoid enrichment](commutative-monoid-enrichment.md) on its one-object category. Swapping prime $2$ with an [odd prime](odd-prime.md) $p$ gives $1\oplus1=p$. Infinitely many such primes therefore give infinitely many distinct additions, although the transported enriched categories are isomorphic.
