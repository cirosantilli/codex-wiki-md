# Exponential of right monoid actions

↑ **Parent:** [Right monoid action](right-monoid-action.md)

Give $M\times X$ the diagonal [right monoid action](right-monoid-action.md). On its [equivariant maps](equivariant-map.md) into $Y$, define $(ek)(g,x)=e(kg,x)$. The [evaluation map of an exponential object](evaluation-map-of-an-exponential-object.md) is $e(1,x)$ and [currying](currying.md) of $h:Z\times X\to Y$ is $\widehat h(z)(g,x)=h(zg,x)$. Both maps are equivariant, and they are inverse under evaluation. The left multiplication $kg$ in this right action is essential in a noncommutative [monoid](monoid.md).
