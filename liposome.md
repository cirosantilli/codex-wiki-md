# Liposome

↑ **Parent:** [Lipid vesicle](lipid-vesicle.md)

A [liposome](liposome.md) is a closed artificial [lipid vesicle](lipid-vesicle.md) enclosing an aqueous compartment. It may contain one [lipid bilayer](lipid-bilayer.md) or several concentric bilayers. A single-bilayer spherical model distinguishes the inner aqueous radius, the boundary between opposing tail regions, and the outer head-group radius; finite molecular packing constrains all three.
