# Mechanical string

↑ **Parent:** [Continuum mechanics](continuum-mechanics-split.md)

A slender flexible body modeled by a moving curve that transmits [tension](tension.md) while its bending stiffness is neglected. For a uniform stretched string with constant [tension](tension.md) $T$ and [linear mass density](linear-mass-density.md) $\mu$, small transverse [oscillations](oscillation.md) obey $\mu y_{tt}=Ty_{xx}$; see the [wave equation on a string](wave-equation-on-a-string.md).
