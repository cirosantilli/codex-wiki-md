# Foucault pendulum

↑ **Parent:** [Simple pendulum](simple-pendulum-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foucault_pendulum)

A [Foucault pendulum](foucault-pendulum.md) is a long [simple pendulum](simple-pendulum-split.md) whose suspension allows the bob to swing in both independent horizontal directions. Its slowly changing swing direction relative to the building reveals [Earth's rotation](earth-s-rotation.md). A hinge restricting the bob to one vertical [plane](plane.md) would prevent this measurement: the support would impose the very direction we want the pendulum to select freely. [Léon Foucault](leon-foucault.md) demonstrated the effect in 1851.[The Foucault pendulum, Does the Earth turn?: 1851 demonstration](the-foucault-pendulum-by-joe-wolfe.md)

The bob's back-and-forth [oscillation](oscillation.md) and the swing direction's [precession](precession.md) are different motions. The bob traverses its arc in seconds, while successive arcs change direction over hours. In a [conical pendulum](conical-pendulum.md) the bob itself circulates around the vertical; a nearly planar [Foucault pendulum](foucault-pendulum.md) instead reverses along an almost straight horizontal trace, whose orientation changes slowly. The measured quantity is that orientation against a fixed floor or wall reference, rather than the bob's instantaneous [azimuth](azimuth.md).
