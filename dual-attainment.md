# Dual attainment

↑ **Parent:** [Strong duality](strong-duality.md)

[Dual attainment](dual-attainment.md) means that a feasible choice of [Lagrange multipliers](lagrange-multiplier.md) achieves the optimal value of the [Lagrangian dual problem](lagrangian-dual-problem.md). Equality of primal and dual optimal values alone does not imply [dual attainment](dual-attainment.md).
