# Inversion criterion with intrinsic planetary flux

↑ **Parent:** [Inversion (meteorology)](inversion-meteorology.md)

For the usual [semi-grey irradiated atmosphere](semi-grey-irradiated-atmosphere.md) with downward thermal [optical depth](optical-depth.md) $\tau$, an inversion occurs where $f_rT_{\rm irr}^4(\gamma^2-1)e^{-\sqrt3\gamma\tau}>T_{\rm int}^4$, with $\gamma$ the visible-to-thermal [opacity](opacity.md) ratio. At negligible intrinsic flux the threshold reduces to $\gamma>1$.
