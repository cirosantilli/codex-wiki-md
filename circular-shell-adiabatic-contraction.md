# Circular-shell adiabatic contraction

↑ **Parent:** [Adiabatic invariant](adiabatic-invariant.md)

In a slowly changing spherical [gravitational potential](newtonian-potential-of-a-point-mass.md), [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) is exactly conserved and the [radial action](radial-action.md) is an [adiabatic invariant](adiabatic-invariant.md). An initially [circular orbit](circular-orbit.md) remains circular in the adiabatic limit, so $J_r=0$ and $L^2=GrM(r)$ give the displayed shell relation. If dark-matter shells do not cross, their enclosed dark mass is also conserved. A density profile alone does not specify this orbital model: eccentric-orbit populations require their full action distribution.
