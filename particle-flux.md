# Particle flux

↑ **Parent:** [Particle beam](particle-beam.md)

Here [particle flux](particle-flux.md) means the number of [particles](particle-physics.md) crossing a surface per unit [area](surface-area.md) and unit [time](time-in-physics.md), with the area taken perpendicular to the incident [particle beam](particle-beam.md). Multiplying by the target interaction [cross section](cross-section-physics.md) gives an event rate per target particle in the ideal independent-target model.
