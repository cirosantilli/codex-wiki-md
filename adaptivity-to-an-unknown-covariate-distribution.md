# Adaptivity to an unknown covariate distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Semiparametric statistics](semiparametric-statistics.md)

If the parametric [score function](informant-function.md) has zero [conditional expectation](conditional-expectation.md) given a [covariate](covariate.md) $X$, it is orthogonal to the centered [functions](function-split.md) of $X$ that form the covariate-density [nuisance tangent space](nuisance-tangent-space.md). Its [efficient score](efficient-score.md) then equals its parametric [score function](informant-function.md), and the unknown covariate distribution causes no loss of [Fisher information](fisher-information-matrix.md). This applies to [Gaussian regression scores](gaussian-regression-score.md) and more generally to regular conditional models with unrestricted covariate distribution and no additional nuisance components.
