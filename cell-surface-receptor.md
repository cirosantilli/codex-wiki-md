# Cell-surface receptor

↑ **Parent:** [Receptor (biochemistry)](receptor-biochemistry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell-surface_receptor)

A [cell-surface receptor](cell-surface-receptor.md) detects an extracellular signal at the [cell membrane](cell-membrane.md). Its changed state affects the intracellular side, coupling reception to [signal transduction](signal-transduction.md) or directly to an [ion channel](ion-channel.md)'s opening.
