# Operator-path-integral equivalence

↑ **Parent:** [Path integral](path-integral.md)

With a time-slice regulator, inserting field-coordinate resolutions of identity in evolution kernels produces a bosonic phase-space [path integral](path-integral.md); integrating the [canonical momentum](canonical-momentum.md) gives the configuration-space [action](action.md). For [fermions](fermion.md), resolutions in [fermionic coherent states](fermionic-coherent-state.md) instead produce a [Berezin integral](berezin-integral.md) and the first-order kinetic term. Vacuum endpoint projection supplies the [Feynman i-epsilon prescription](feynman-i-epsilon-prescription.md). Both constructions reproduce the same free oscillator correlators and their [Wick theorem](wick-s-theorem.md) expansions, and thus agree order by order in a regulated [perturbation series](perturbation-series.md).
