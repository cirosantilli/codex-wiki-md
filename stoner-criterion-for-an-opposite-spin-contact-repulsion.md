# Stoner criterion for an opposite-spin contact repulsion

↑ **Parent:** [Stoner criterion](stoner-criterion.md)

For an interaction-energy density $U n_\uparrow n_\downarrow$, let $n_\uparrow=n/2+\delta$, $n_\downarrow=n/2-\delta$ at fixed total [carrier density](carrier-density.md). If $g_s(E_F)$ is the unpolarized [single-spin density of states](single-spin-density-of-states.md) per unit volume, the kinetic-energy cost of the two occupation shifts is $\delta^2/g_s(E_F)$, whereas the interaction changes by $-U\delta^2$. Therefore

$$
\Delta\mathcal E=\left[\frac1{g_s(E_F)}-U\right]\delta^2+O(\delta^4),\qquad
U_c=\frac1{g_s(E_F)}=\frac2{g_{\rm tot}(E_F)}.
$$

For the three-dimensional [free electron model](free-electron-model.md), $g_{\rm tot}(E_F)=3n/(2E_F)$ and $U_c=4E_F/(3n)$. Both the interaction normalization and the spin counting in the [density of states](density-of-states.md) must be specified when quoting a [Stoner criterion](stoner-criterion.md).
