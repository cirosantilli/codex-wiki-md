# First minimum of a finite slit array

↑ **Parent:** [Diffraction grating](diffraction-grating.md)

For $N$ equally spaced, coherently illuminated narrow slits at normal incidence, the [phasor](phasor.md) sum is proportional to $\sin(N\delta/2)/\sin(\delta/2)$, where $\delta=2\pi d\sin\theta/\lambda$. The first zero around the central maximum has $d\sin\theta=\lambda/N$. Principal maxima are removable zero-over-zero limits, not [intensity](intensity-physics.md) zeros.
