# Variational characterization of quantum conditional entropy

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum relative entropy](quantum-relative-entropy.md)

The [quantum conditional entropy](quantum-conditional-entropy.md) has the relative-entropy representation

$$
-H(A|B)_\rho=\min_{\xi_B}D(\rho_{AB}\|I_A\otimes\xi_B),
$$

where the minimum ranges over [density operators](density-matrix.md) on $B$.
