# Chloride ligand

↑ **Parent:** [Ligand in coordination chemistry](ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md)

A [chloride ligand](chloride-ligand.md) is coordinated $\mathrm{Cl^-}$. It is an anionic two-electron donor in ionic [electron counting](electron-counting.md), with occupied orbitals that can contribute pi donation.
