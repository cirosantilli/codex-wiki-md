# Action symmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Noether conserved quantity for a mechanical point symmetry](noether-conserved-quantity-for-a-mechanical-point-symmetry.md)

An infinitesimal transformation that preserves the [action](action.md) up to endpoint terms. For $\delta t=\varepsilon\phi$ and $\delta q_i=\varepsilon\psi_i$, invariance of $L\,dt$ yields the [Noether charge](noether-charge.md) $\sum_ip_i\psi_i-H\phi$, less any boundary-term generator.
