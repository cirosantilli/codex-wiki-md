# Invariance of domain

↑ **Parent:** [Topological manifold](topological-manifold.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invariance_of_domain)

[Invariance of domain](invariance-of-domain.md) says that a [continuous map](continuous-map.md) that is an [injective function](injective-function.md) between [topological manifolds](topological-manifold.md) without boundary of the same dimension is an [open map](open-map.md). It is useful in the [Penrose singularity theorem](penrose-singularity-theorem.md): projecting a [compact](compact-space.md) [achronal boundary](achronal-boundary.md) along timelike curves onto a [Cauchy hypersurface](cauchy-surface.md) gives a nonempty subset that is both [open](open-set.md) and [closed](closed-set.md), forcing that [connected](connected-space.md) [Cauchy hypersurface](cauchy-surface.md) to be [compact](compact-space.md).
