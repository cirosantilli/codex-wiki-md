# Filtered colimits commute with finite limits in sets

↑ **Parent:** [Filtered category](filtered-category.md)

In the [Category of sets](category-of-sets.md), filtered colimits commute with [finite limits](finite-limit.md). Elements in a finite limiting diagram involve only finitely many representatives and finitely many equalities, all of which can be realized at one common stage of a filtered diagram.
