# Two-vessel effusive mixing

↑ **Parent:** [Effusion](effusion.md)

For a species $s$ in two well-mixed vessels of equal [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) $V$ joined by a thin opening of [area](surface-area.md) $A_h$, the [effusion](effusion.md) law gives $\dot n_{s,L}=-(A_h/4V)(\overline v_{s,L}n_{s,L}-\overline v_{s,R}n_{s,R})$. Each species' total inventory is conserved. With fixed [temperatures](temperature-split.md) and total density sum $n_{s,L}+n_{s,R}=n_{s,\Sigma}$, the left density relaxes exponentially with [time](time-in-physics.md) constant $\tau_s=4V/[A_h(\overline v_{s,L}+\overline v_{s,R})]$ to $n_{s,L}^{\infty}=n_{s,\Sigma}\sqrt{T_R}/(\sqrt{T_L}+\sqrt{T_R})$.

At equal [temperatures](temperature-split.md), $\tau_s=2V/(A_h\overline v_s)\propto\sqrt{m_s}$, and initially separated species mix at different rates. At unequal [temperatures](temperature-split.md), the final density ratio is $n_{s,L}/n_{s,R}=\sqrt{T_R/T_L}$ for every species, while the partial-pressure ratio is $p_{s,L}/p_{s,R}=\sqrt{T_L/T_R}$. The mass-independent final ratios do not imply mass-independent transient rates. Maintaining different [temperatures](temperature-split.md) requires external [heat](heat-split.md) exchange even after net [particle](particle-physics.md) transfer vanishes.
