# Empirical characteristic function

↑ **Parent:** [Characteristic function](characteristic-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Empirical_characteristic_function)

For a sample $X_1,\ldots,X_n$, $\varphi_n(u)=n^{-1}\sum_j e^{iuX_j}$ is the [characteristic function](characteristic-function.md) of the sample's [empirical measure](empirical-measure.md). For [independent and identically distributed random variables](independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md), $\mathbb E\varphi_n(u)=\varphi(u)$ and $\operatorname{Var}_{\mathbb C}\varphi_n(u)=(1-|\varphi(u)|^2)/n$, without any moment condition on the observations.
