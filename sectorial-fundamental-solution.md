# Sectorial fundamental solution

↑ **Parent:** [Stokes phenomenon](stokes-phenomenon.md)

At an unramified [irregular singular point](irregular-singular-point.md) with distinct leading [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md), a sectorial fundamental solution is a [fundamental matrix](fundamental-matrix-of-a-linear-differential-equation.md) whose entries are [holomorphic functions](holomorphic-function.md) asymptotic to a prescribed formal [matrix](matrix.md) on a sector of the [logarithm](logarithm.md) cover. For competing cubic exponentials, the usual construction uses six overlapping sectors with basic angle $\pi/3$. A [formal power series](formal-power-series.md) need not converge, and two sectorial realizations may differ by exponentially small terms across a sector boundary.
