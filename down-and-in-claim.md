# Down-and-in claim

↑ **Parent:** [Barrier option](barrier-option.md)

A [down-and-in claim](down-and-in-claim.md) with lower barrier $b$ pays a specified terminal payoff only if the [stock](stock.md) reaches $b$ before maturity. Before activation its value solves a killed-domain pricing problem; at activation its value equals the ordinary terminal-payoff claim value.
