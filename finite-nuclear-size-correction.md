# Finite nuclear size correction

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear charge distribution](nuclear-charge-distribution.md)

Spreading a point nuclear [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) over a small spherical [nuclear charge distribution](nuclear-charge-distribution.md) weakens the central attraction and raises electronic [energy levels](energy-level.md). In nonrelativistic first-order [time-independent perturbation theory](time-independent-perturbation-theory.md), a slowly varying $s$-state contact density gives the displayed shift. For a uniform sphere, $\langle r^2\rangle_{\rm ch}=3R^2/5$, and integration of the difference between spherical and point [Coulomb potentials](coulomb-potential-energy.md) gives $\Delta E_{1s}=(4/5)Z^4\mathrm{Ry}(R/a_0)^2$ for a [hydrogen-like ion](hydrogen-like-ion.md). A nonrelativistic $p$ [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) vanishes at the origin, suppressing its leading shift to order $R^4$. Heavy [atomic nuclei](atomic-nucleus-split.md) require relativistic and realistic-charge-distribution corrections. An observed transition [frequency](frequency.md) shifts by the difference of the two level corrections divided by the [Planck constant](planck-constant.md).
