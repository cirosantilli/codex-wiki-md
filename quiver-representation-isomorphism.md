# Quiver representation isomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Quiver representation morphism](quiver-representation-morphism.md)

A [quiver representation morphism](quiver-representation-morphism.md) is an isomorphism exactly when all its vertex maps are invertible. Choosing bases turns this into the [base change action on quiver representations](base-change-action-on-quiver-representations.md), whose orbits coincide with isomorphism classes.
