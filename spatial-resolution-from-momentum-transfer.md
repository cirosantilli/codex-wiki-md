# Spatial resolution from momentum transfer

↑ **Parent:** [Momentum transfer](momentum-transfer.md)

In [quantum scattering](quantum-scattering-split.md), a spatial feature on scale $\ell$ contributes Fourier components with [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) of order $1/\ell$. A [momentum transfer](momentum-transfer.md) of magnitude $q$ supplies [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) $q/\hbar$, giving the characteristic estimate $\ell\sim\hbar/q$. With the energy-like quantity $Q=qc$, this becomes $\ell\sim\hbar c/Q$. The full [de Broglie wavelength](de-broglie-wavelength.md) uses $h/p$ and differs from the corresponding reduced wavelength by $2\pi$. Beam [energy](energy-split.md) only bounds accessible [momentum transfer](momentum-transfer.md); it does not determine the resolution of every event.
