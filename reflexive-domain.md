# Reflexive domain

↑ **Parent:** [Lambda model](lambda-model.md)

A [pointed complete partial order](pointed-complete-partial-order.md) $D$ is reflexive when there are [Scott continuous maps](scott-continuous-map.md) $e:[D\to D]\to D$ and $p:D\to[D\to D]$ with $p\circ e=\mathrm{id}$. Define $a\cdot b=p(a)(b)$ and interpret [lambda abstraction](lambda-abstraction.md) using $e$. The inverse law gives beta soundness; $e\circ p=\mathrm{id}$ additionally gives eta soundness.
