<h1 id="burgi-dunitz-angle">Bürgi-Dunitz angle</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Nucleophilic addition](nucleophilic-addition.md)

The [Bürgi-Dunitz angle](burgi-dunitz-angle.md) describes the oblique approach of a nucleophile toward a carbonyl carbon, conventionally measured relative to C=O. A typical trajectory is near $107^\circ$, with the optimum depending on the system.
