# Canonical ensemble

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical physics](statistical-physics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canonical_ensemble)

The [canonical ensemble](canonical-ensemble.md) is the [probability distribution](probability-distribution.md) for a system in [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium.md) with a [heat bath](heat-bath.md) at fixed [temperature](temperature.md) $T$, with fixed particle number and external parameters. It allows [energy](energy.md) exchange, so individual [energy](energy.md) measurements fluctuate. With [Boltzmann constant](boltzmann-constant.md) $k_B$ and [inverse temperature](inverse-temperature.md) $\beta=1/(k_BT)$,

$$
p_s=\frac{e^{-\beta E_s}}{Z},\qquad Z=\sum_s e^{-\beta E_s}.
$$

The [heat bath](heat-bath.md) is idealized as large enough that exchanges barely change its [temperature](temperature.md); coupling must permit equilibration while contributing negligible interaction [energy](energy.md). A finite [heat bath](heat-bath.md) approximates this description only when its [temperature](temperature.md) change is negligible over the relevant exchanges. Strong coupling or a small [heat bath](heat-bath.md) can require a different description.

For one [two-level system](two-level-system.md) at positive [temperature](temperature.md), the [Boltzmann weights](boltzmann-factor.md) are $1,e^{-\beta\Delta}$, hence

$$
Z_1=1+e^{-\beta\Delta},\qquad p=\frac{e^{-\beta\Delta}}{Z_1}=\frac1{1+e^{\beta\Delta}}.
$$

At $\beta\Delta=\ln3$, $Z_1=4/3$ and $p=1/4$. This is a probabilistic thermal [mixed state](mixed-state.md). For quantum levels, its [density matrix](density-matrix.md) is $\rho=(1-p)|0\rangle\langle0|+p|1\rangle\langle1|$. The [pure state](pure-state.md) $\sqrt{1-p}|0\rangle+\sqrt p|1\rangle$ has the same [energy measurement](energy-measurement.md) [probabilities](probability.md), but also has [quantum coherence](quantum-coherence-in-a-specified-basis.md) between the [energy eigenstates](energy-eigenstate.md). Matching those [probabilities](probability.md) does not make the states equivalent.
