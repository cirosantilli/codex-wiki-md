# Radiative-zero disk surface

↑ **Parent:** [Homologous vertical structure of a constant-opacity alpha disk](homologous-vertical-structure-of-a-constant-opacity-alpha-disk.md)

For a constant-opacity, gas-pressure-supported radiative disk surface at $z=H$, imposing $p=T=0$ does not impose $F=0$. If the outgoing surface [flux](flux.md) is finite and positive, dividing hydrostatic balance by the [temperature](temperature.md) gradient gives $dp/dT\sim16\sigma\Omega^2HT^3/(3\kappa F_s)$. Consequently $p\propto T^4$, $\rho\propto T^3$, and the diffusion equation gives $T\propto H-z$. Thus the [mass density](density.md) also vanishes regularly while the surface radiates a finite [flux](flux.md). This ideal boundary replaces the finite-temperature photospheric transition; its accuracy is not controlled solely by the disk aspect ratio.
