# Compact closed-ball image forces finite rank on c0

↑ **Parent:** [Compact operator](compact-operator-split.md)

Suppose a linear map $S:c_0\to Y$ has compact [image](image-of-a-function.md) of the closed unit ball. It is bounded, and each $g\in Y^*$ attains its maximum on this compact [image](image-of-a-function.md). Thus $S^*g$ attains its [norm](norm.md), so [norm attainment on the vanishing sequence space](norm-attainment-on-the-vanishing-sequence-space.md) implies $S^*(Y^*)\subseteq c_{00}$. The [Baire category theorem](baire-category-theorem.md) applied to the inverse images of the first $n$ coordinate spans makes this adjoint finite rank: these are closed [linear subspaces](vector-subspace.md) covering the complete [continuous dual space](continuous-dual-space-split.md) $Y^*$, so one has interior and is all of $Y^*$. Finally the [Hahn-Banach theorem](hahn-banach-theorem.md) identifies $\ker S$ as the common kernel of $S^*(Y^*)$, proving finite rank of $S$. Ordinary [compact operators](compact-operator-split.md) require only relative compactness of the ball [image](image-of-a-function.md), so they need not have finite rank.
