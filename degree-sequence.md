# Degree sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Degree (graph theory)](degree-graph-theory.md)

The degree sequence of a labelled [graph](graph-split.md) records the [degree of a vertex](degree-graph-theory.md) at each label. The [handshaking lemma](degree-sum-formula.md) gives $\sum_i d_i=2|E|$. A nonincreasing list forgets the assignment of degrees to labels unless a particular ordering is specified.
