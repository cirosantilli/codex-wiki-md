<h1 id="spatial-damping-of-a-driven-alfven-wave">Spatial damping of a driven Alfvén wave</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Resistive damping of an Alfvén wave](resistive-damping-of-an-alfven-wave.md)

For fixed real positive [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) $\omega$, a transverse [Alfvén wave](alfven-wave.md) proportional to $e^{i(kz-\omega t)}$ in a uniform fluid has $k^2=\omega^2/(v_A^2-i\eta_m\omega)$ when [magnetic diffusivity](magnetic-diffusivity.md) $\eta_m$ is retained and [viscosity](dynamic-viscosity-split.md) is neglected. Choose the branch with positive real and imaginary parts for propagation and attenuation along $+z$. For $\epsilon\ll1$, $k=(\omega/v_A)(1+i\epsilon/2+O(\epsilon^2))$. The amplitude attenuation length is $L_A=1/\operatorname{Im}k$, whereas the [energy](energy-split.md) attenuation length is $L_E=1/(2\operatorname{Im}k)$. With wavelength $\lambda=2\pi/\operatorname{Re}k$, $L_A/\lambda\sim1/(\pi\epsilon)$ and $L_E/\lambda\sim1/(2\pi\epsilon)$. Fixed-real-[wavenumber](wavenumber.md) temporal [Resistive damping of an Alfvén wave](resistive-damping-of-an-alfven-wave.md) is a different boundary-value prescription.
