# Natural emittance

↑ **Parent:** [Beam emittance](beam-emittance.md)

The equilibrium horizontal [beam emittance](beam-emittance.md) set by synchrotron-radiation excitation and damping in an uncoupled storage ring. The radiation-integral relation is $\varepsilon_{x0}=C_q\gamma^2I_5/(J_xI_2)$; similar cell optics give a cubic dependence on bending angle.
