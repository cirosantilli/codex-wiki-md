# Ultramafic rock

↑ **Parent:** [Rock (geology)](rock-geology.md)

A [rock](rock-geology.md) dominated by magnesium- and iron-rich [minerals](mineral.md), with lower silica content than typical [mafic rocks](mafic-rock.md). Alteration can strongly change its magnetic properties.
