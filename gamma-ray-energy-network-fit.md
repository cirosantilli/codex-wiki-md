# Gamma-ray energy-network fit

↑ **Parent:** [Weighted least squares](weighted-least-squares.md)

For a proposed gamma-transition graph, fix the [ground-state energy](ground-state-energy.md) and fit the remaining level energies with a linear incidence [matrix](matrix.md). Loops give consistency constraints and correlated excitation-energy errors. Different transition graphs can have the same minimized residuals if they rearrange the same energy-sum identities; a small chi-squared is not then a unique level assignment.
