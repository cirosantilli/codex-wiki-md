# NIST trace-gas FTMW validation experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier transform microwave spectroscopy](fourier-transform-microwave-spectroscopy.md)

The NIST validation report used $1\%$ [carbonyl sulfide](carbonyl-sulfide.md) in [argon](argon.md) to illustrate the readout. A pulsed nozzle injected a [molecular beam](molecular-beam.md) along a two-mirror cavity. Approximately $1\,\mathrm{\mu s}$ excitation prepared [quantum coherence](quantum-coherence-in-a-specified-basis.md); a receiver digitized the decaying [electric field](electric-field-split.md), then a [Fourier transform](fourier-transform-split.md) produced [spectral lines](spectral-line.md). Counterpropagating cavity waves produced a [Doppler shift](doppler-effect.md) doublet; its midpoint represents one rest-frame transition.[The Validation of Fourier-Transform Microwave Spectroscopy for Trace-Gas Analysis, printed pp. 25–26, Figures 2–3: coaxial apparatus, excitation and OCS readout; PDF pages 31–32](manufacturing-technology-the-road-ahead-nistir-6816/the-validation-of-fourier-transform-microwave-spectroscopy-for-trace-gas-analysis.md)

An original arrangement of the documented sample, cavity, excitation and readout functions; no published figure is copied. The frequency-reference branch summarizes the later portable electronics described in the NIST historical account. The sample fraction is a reported preparation, not a synthetic measured spectrum.

The portable electronics used a common microwave source and a $30\,\mathrm{MHz}$ offset derived from a $10\,\mathrm{MHz}$ reference. Cavity tuning selects the accessible band; it does not independently calibrate the line's [frequency](frequency.md). Absolute accuracy also requires a characterized reference and receiver offset.[§5, printed pp. 282–283, Figure 20: source splitting, frequency reference, single-sideband modulation, mixer and digitizer](evolution-of-microwave-spectroscopy-at-the-national-bureau-of-standards-nbs-and-the-national-institute-of-standards-and-technology-nist-by-f-j-lovas-and-others.md)

Reported controls included nozzle off/on, carrier-gas changes, and a blind [nitrogen](nitrogen.md) mixture. Assigned components were correctly identified. Single-component standards supplied preliminary [concentration](concentration.md) calibration; one standard per quantified component left response validation incomplete. Condensation and departure from [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium-split.md) in the nozzle were acknowledged limitations.[printed pp. 26–27: nozzle/carrier comparison in Figure 6, blind mixture in Table II, one-standard calibration and nozzle-expansion limitations](manufacturing-technology-the-road-ahead-nistir-6816/the-validation-of-fourier-transform-microwave-spectroscopy-for-trace-gas-analysis.md)

The lesson is that [rotational spectral line assignment](rotational-spectral-line-assignment.md) and [concentration](concentration.md) inference answer different questions. Reproducible [frequencies](frequency.md) support molecular identity; converting signal size into [concentration](concentration.md) requires calibrated response under the same transfer and excitation conditions. The thermal absorption example above does not model a nozzle's nonequilibrium populations or the cavity's pulsed response.
