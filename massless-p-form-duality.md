# Massless p-form duality

↑ **Parent:** [Massless p-form gauge field](massless-p-form-gauge-field.md)

For a free [massless p-form gauge field](massless-p-form-gauge-field.md) with $0\le p\le D-2$, its field equation makes $*dA$ closed. The [Poincaré lemma](poincare-lemma.md) writes it locally as $dB$, giving a dual potential of degree $D-p-2$. The original [Bianchi identity for an Abelian p-form](bianchi-identity-for-an-abelian-p-form.md) becomes the dual field equation, and the original equation becomes the dual Bianchi identity. The two descriptions have equal [little group](little-group.md) counts $\binom{D-2}{p}$. Global fluxes and top forms require separate treatment.
