# Letter to Foscarini by Robert Bellarmine

↑ **Parent:** [History of science bibliography](history-of-science-bibliography.md)

[https://inters.org/Bellarmino-Letter-Foscarini](https://inters.org/Bellarmino-Letter-Foscarini)

Primary letter in English translation, with its three numbered arguments. The date is 12 April 1615. Hypothetical mathematical use, scriptural interpretation and an actual physical [demonstration](demonstration-in-early-modern-natural-philosophy.md) are distinguished. The examination prints 1616; that is not the date of this document.
