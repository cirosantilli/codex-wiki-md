# Torsional waves in a solid circular rod

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic wave](elastic-wave.md)

For small torsional displacement $\phi(x,t)$, the transmitted [torque](torque.md) is $C=GJ_p\phi_x$. A slice of length $dx$ receives net torque $GJ_p\phi_{xx}dx$ and has axial [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) $\rho J_pdx$. Its angular equation therefore gives the displayed [wave equation](wave-equation-split.md), with wave speed $\sqrt{G/\rho}$. The same geometric factor cancels from inertia and stiffness, making this ideal torsional speed independent of rod radius. This long-wave circular-rod model neglects additional elastic modes and dissipation.
