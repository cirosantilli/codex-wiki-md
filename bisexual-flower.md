# Bisexual flower

↑ **Parent:** [Flower](flower.md)

A [flower](flower.md) containing both male and female reproductive organs, [stamens](stamen.md) and [carpels](carpel.md). It differs from [monoecy](monoecy.md), in which male and female structures occur separately on the same individual.
