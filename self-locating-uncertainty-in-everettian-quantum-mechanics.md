# Self-locating uncertainty in Everettian quantum mechanics

↑ **Parent:** [Many-worlds interpretation](many-worlds-interpretation.md)

Uncertainty about which observer-centred situation is one's own, even conditional on a known global [wave function](wave-function-split.md). An example is a post-measurement observer who has not yet consulted a decohered record. This proposal distinguishes uncertainty about the experienced record from randomness in the universal dynamics. A further argument is needed to connect its credences to [branch weights](branch-weight.md); not every decision-theoretic Everettian account must interpret its weights as ignorance of a uniquely selected future branch.
