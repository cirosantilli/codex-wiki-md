# Heterozygote

↑ **Parent:** [Genotype](genotype.md)

A heterozygote carries different [alleles](allele.md) at a diploid locus. Under ordinary Mendelian segregation each is transmitted to an offspring with probability $1/2$. Only heterozygous parents provide a comparison between alternative transmissions in the [transmission disequilibrium test](transmission-disequilibrium-test.md).
