# Minimal Morse critical count on a circle times an orientable surface

↑ **Parent:** [Product of Morse functions](product-of-morse-functions.md)

For a closed connected [orientable surface](orientable-surface.md) of [genus](genus-of-a-surface.md) $g$, the [Künneth theorem](kunneth-theorem.md) gives the [Betti numbers](betti-number.md) of $S^1\times\Sigma_g$ as $(1,2g+1,2g+1,1)$. The weak [Morse inequalities](morse-inequalities.md) give the displayed lower bound. A surface [Morse function](morse-function.md) with one minimum, $2g$ saddles and one maximum exists from its standard [handle decomposition](handle-decomposition.md). Adding a positive multiple of the cosine [Morse function](morse-function.md) on the [circle](circle.md) makes a [product of Morse functions](product-of-morse-functions.md), whose critical counts are precisely these four Betti numbers. The lower bound is attained.
