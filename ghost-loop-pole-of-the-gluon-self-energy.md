# Ghost-loop pole of the gluon self-energy

↑ **Parent:** [Gluon self-energy](gluon-self-energy.md)

With insertion convention $i\Pi$, [ghost propagator](ghost-propagator.md) $i/p^2$, and oriented ghost-gluon [Feynman rule](feynman-rule.md) $gf^{abc}p^\mu$ with $p$ pointing away along the ghost arrow, the [ghost loop](ghost-loop.md) is $i\Pi_{\rm gh}=-g^2C_A\delta^{ab}\int d^Dl\,(2\pi)^{-D}(l+k)^\mu l^\nu/[l^2(l+k)^2]$. In [dimensional regularization](dimensional-regularization.md) with $D=4-\epsilon$, a [Feynman parameter](feynman-parameter.md) and a shift give the displayed [ultraviolet pole](ultraviolet-pole.md). Its contraction is $g^2C_A\delta^{ab}k^2k^\nu/(32\pi^2\epsilon)$; it cancels the nontransverse part of the two-three-gluon-vertex contribution. A different insertion or ghost-vertex convention must be translated before comparing signs.
