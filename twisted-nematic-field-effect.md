# Twisted nematic field effect

↑ **Parent:** [Nematic liquid crystal](nematic-liquid-crystal.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twisted_nematic_field_effect)

The [twisted nematic field effect](twisted-nematic-field-effect.md) switches a [nematic liquid crystal](nematic-liquid-crystal.md) between a twisted optically rotating state and a largely field-aligned state. With [adiabatic optical following in a twisted nematic](adiabatic-optical-following-in-a-twisted-nematic.md) and crossed [polarizers](polarizer.md) aligned to the two planar surface directors, a quarter-turn cell passes light at low voltage and suppresses it at high voltage. A rear reflector permits reflective operation with little power expended on light generation.
