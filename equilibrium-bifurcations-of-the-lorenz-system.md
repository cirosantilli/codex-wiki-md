# Equilibrium bifurcations of the Lorenz system

↑ **Parent:** [Lorenz system](lorenz-system.md)

For positive $\sigma,b,r$, the origin of the [Lorenz system](lorenz-system.md) loses stability at $r=1$ and two equilibria $(\pm\sqrt{b(r-1)},\pm\sqrt{b(r-1)},r-1)$ emerge stably. Center-manifold projection gives $\dot q=\sigma(r-1)q/(\sigma+1)-\sigma q^3/[b(\sigma+1)]+\cdots$, proving a supercritical pitchfork. The nonzero-branch characteristic polynomial is $\lambda^3+(\sigma+b+1)\lambda^2+b(\sigma+r)\lambda+2\sigma b(r-1)$. If $\sigma\leq b+1$ these equilibria remain stable for all $r>1$. Otherwise their transverse Hopf crossing occurs at the displayed [parameter](parameter.md), with frequency squared $b(\sigma+r_H)$. Its cubic Hopf coefficient is positive, so this Hopf is subcritical, producing an unstable small [periodic orbit](periodic-orbit.md) on the stable-equilibrium side. A zero [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) detects the pitchfork, while the Hopf condition uses the Routh-Hurwitz equality; neither alone determines nonlinear criticality without the corresponding normal-form calculation.
