# Fourier inversion on a locally compact abelian group

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier transform on a locally compact abelian group](fourier-transform-on-a-locally-compact-abelian-group.md)

For the [dual Haar measure](dual-haar-measure.md), the formula first follows for continuous compactly supported convolution squares from the [Bochner theorem](bochner-s-theorem.md). More generally, if $f\in L^1(G)$ and $\widehat f\in L^1(\widehat G)$, the right-hand side is a continuous representative of $f$; equality holds everywhere if $f$ is continuous, and otherwise at every point where the chosen representative is continuous. Convolution with normalized small-support positive convolution squares proves the extension: the spatial convolutions approach $f$ in $L^1$, while their transforms approach $\widehat f$ in $L^1$ by [equicontinuity of compact families of characters](equicontinuity-of-compact-families-of-characters.md) and an integrable-tail split.
