# Lane-Emden equation

↑ **Parent:** [Emden-Fowler transformation](emden-fowler-transformation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lane–Emden_equation)

The classical [Lane-Emden equation](lane-emden-equation.md) is the dimensionless equation

$$
u''+\frac{2}{r}u'+u^p=0
$$

for a [spherically symmetric](spherical-symmetry.md) [polytrope](polytrope.md) in three dimensions. The [Generalized Lane-Emden equation](generalized-lane-emden-equation.md) extends it to arbitrary dimensions and to functions without radial symmetry.
