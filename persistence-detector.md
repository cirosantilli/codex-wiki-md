# Persistence detector

↑ **Parent:** [Coherent feed-forward loop](coherent-feed-forward-loop.md)

A [persistence detector](persistence-detector.md) responds strongly to a sustained input and weakly to short isolated inputs. In an activating [coherent feed-forward loop](coherent-feed-forward-loop.md), an intermediate [protein](protein-split.md) accumulates toward a threshold while the target also requires the direct input. Eliminating that intermediate by an instantaneous [quasi-steady-state approximation](quasi-steady-state-approximation.md) removes its accumulation delay.
