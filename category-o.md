# Category O

↑ **Parent:** [Lie algebra representation](lie-algebra-representation.md)

For a finite-dimensional complex [semisimple Lie algebra](semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md) with a fixed [triangular decomposition of a Lie algebra](triangular-decomposition-of-a-lie-algebra.md), category $\mathcal O$ consists of finitely generated [universal enveloping algebra](universal-enveloping-algebra.md) modules that are direct sums of Cartan weight spaces and are locally finite under the positive nilpotent subalgebra. Their [weight spaces](weight-space.md) are finite-dimensional and their weights lie in a finite union of downward positive-root cones. [Verma modules](verma-module.md), finite-dimensional modules, submodules and quotients belong to this category. Closure under submodules uses the noetherian property of the enveloping algebra, obtained from the [Poincaré-Birkhoff-Witt theorem](poincare-birkhoff-witt-theorem.md). The category condition does not require the module to be finite-dimensional.
