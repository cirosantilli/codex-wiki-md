# Unitary operator

↑ **Parent:** [Linear operator](linear-operator.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unitary_operator)

A unitary operator $U$ on a complex vector space with an [inner product](inner-product.md) satisfies $U^*U=UU^*=I$ and therefore preserves inner products.
