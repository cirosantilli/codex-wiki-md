# Plane internal gravity wave

↑ **Parent:** [Internal wave](internal-wave.md)

A monochromatic [internal gravity wave](internal-wave.md) in uniform stratification has a spatially linear phase. In the nonrotating inviscid [Boussinesq approximation](boussinesq-approximation.md), its [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation.md) is $\omega^2=N^2|\mathbf k_h|^2/(|\mathbf k_h|^2+m^2)$. The [wave vector](wavevector.md) is normal to its phase planes, while the [group velocity](group-velocity.md) lies along them.
