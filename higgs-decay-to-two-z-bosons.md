# Higgs decay to two Z bosons

↑ **Parent:** [Higgs boson coupling to Z bosons](higgs-boson-coupling-to-z-bosons.md)

For $m_H>2m_Z$, the [Higgs boson coupling to Z bosons](higgs-boson-coupling-to-z-bosons.md) gives $\mathcal M=(2m_Z^2/v)\epsilon_1^*\cdot\epsilon_2^*$. The [massive vector polarization sum](polarization-sum-for-a-massive-vector-boson.md) gives $\sum|\mathcal M|^2=(m_H^4/v^2)(1-4x+12x^2)$, with $x=m_Z^2/m_H^2$. The [two-body decay phase space](two-body-decay-phase-space.md) and [identical final-state symmetry factor](identical-particle-factor-in-a-final-state-phase-space-integral.md) $1/2$ give

$$
\boxed{\Gamma(H\to ZZ)=\frac{m_H^3}{32\pi v^2}\sqrt{1-4x}(1-4x+12x^2).}
$$

The contraction uses $k_1\cdot k_2=(m_H^2-2m_Z^2)/2$. The on-shell rate vanishes at threshold; below threshold an off-shell calculation is required.
