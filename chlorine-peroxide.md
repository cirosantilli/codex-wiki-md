# Chlorine peroxide

↑ **Parent:** [Chlorine monoxide dimer cycle](chlorine-monoxide-dimer-cycle.md)

The peroxy-linked [chlorine](chlorine.md) oxide $\mathrm{ClOOCl}$, often written $\mathrm{Cl_2O_2}$ in a [chlorine monoxide dimer cycle](chlorine-monoxide-dimer-cycle.md). Thermal return to two [chlorine monoxide](chlorine-monoxide.md) [radicals](radical-chemistry.md) competes with a photolytic pathway that ultimately yields two [chlorine](chlorine.md) atoms. The formula alone should not be taken as specifying every possible dichlorine-dioxide isomer.
