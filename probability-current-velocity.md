# Probability current velocity

↑ **Parent:** [Fokker-Planck probability current](fokker-planck-probability-current.md)

Where a [probability density](probability-density.md) $\psi$ is positive, its [Fokker-Planck probability current](fokker-planck-probability-current.md) can be written $\mathbf J=\psi\mathbf V$, giving a continuity equation $\partial_t\psi+\nabla\cdot(\psi\mathbf V)=0$. This velocity transports probability. It is not a differentiable instantaneous velocity of an overdamped [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) path. In the [Hookean dumbbell model](hookean-dumbbell-model.md), its osmotic term is $-(2k_BT/\zeta)\nabla\log\psi$.
