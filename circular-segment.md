# Circular segment

↑ **Parent:** [Circle](circle.md)

A [circular segment](circular-segment.md) is the part of a disk bounded by a chord and one of the two [circular arcs](circular-arc.md) joining its endpoints. For [radius](radius.md) $R$ and the chosen arc's central [angle](angle.md) $0\leq\phi\leq2\pi$ in [radians](radian.md), its [area](surface-area.md) is $R^2(\phi-\sin\phi)/2$. Subtract the signed central [triangle](triangle.md) of area $R^2\sin\phi/2$ from the [circular sector](circular-sector.md) of area $R^2\phi/2$. Angles below $\pi$ give the smaller segment and angles above $\pi$ the larger one.
