# Mechanical system

↑ **Parent:** [Classical mechanics](classical-mechanics-split.md)

A collection of bodies and specified interactions whose [configuration](mechanical-configuration.md), [velocities](velocity.md) and evolution are studied in [mechanics](mechanics.md). [Generalized coordinates](generalized-coordinate.md) describe its allowed configurations, while [constraints](constraint-mechanics.md) restrict them. A model must specify which bodies and surroundings are included before applying [conservation of momentum](momentum-conservation.md) or [conservation of energy](conservation-of-energy.md).
