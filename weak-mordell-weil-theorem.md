# Weak Mordell-Weil theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Mordell-Weil group](mordell-weil-group.md)

For an elliptic curve over a number field and an integer $n\geq2$, the quotient $E(K)/nE(K)$ is finite. Combining this finite quotient with a height descent proves finite generation of $E(K)$.
