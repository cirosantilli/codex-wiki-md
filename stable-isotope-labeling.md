# Stable isotope labeling

↑ **Parent:** [Isotope](isotope.md)

[stable isotope labeling](stable-isotope-labeling.md) uses nonradioactive [isotopes](isotope.md) to distinguish molecular populations. Paired labeled and unlabeled samples can be compared by [mass spectrometry](mass-spectrometry.md), subject to labeling completeness, mixing and measurement-response controls.
