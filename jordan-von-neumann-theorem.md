# Jordan-von Neumann theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Parallelogram law](parallelogram-law.md)

A complex [normed vector space](normed-vector-space-split.md) has an inducing [inner product](inner-product-split.md) exactly when its [norm](norm.md) satisfies the [parallelogram law](parallelogram-law.md). Define $B(u,v)=[\|u+v\|^2-\|u-v\|^2]/4$. Subtract two parallelogram identities to obtain $B(u+w,v)+B(u-w,v)=2B(u,v)$. Taking $w=u$ gives doubling, and then replacing $u,w$ by the half-sum and half-difference gives additivity. Integer scaling and division give rational homogeneity; continuity gives real homogeneity. Thus $B$ is a symmetric real-bilinear form. The complex formula $P(u,v)=B(u,v)+iB(u,iv)$ satisfies $P(iu,v)=iP(u,v)$, because $B(iu,v)=-B(u,iv)$ and $B(iu,iv)=B(u,v)$. Symmetry gives [conjugate symmetry](conjugate-symmetry.md) of $P$, and $P(u,u)=\|u\|^2$ gives positive definiteness. The reverse implication follows by expanding squared [norms](norm.md). [Completeness](completeness.md) is needed only for the resulting [inner product](inner-product-split.md) space to be a [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md).
