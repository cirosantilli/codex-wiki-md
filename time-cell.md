# Time cell

↑ **Parent:** [Neural coding](neural-coding.md)

A [neuron](neuron-split.md) active preferentially at a particular part of a temporally extended task or interval. Temporal selectivity can coexist with selectivity for location, behavior and sequence identity; it is not a claim that the cell is an isolated clock.
