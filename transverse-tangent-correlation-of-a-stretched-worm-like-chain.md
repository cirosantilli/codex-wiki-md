# Transverse tangent correlation of a stretched worm-like chain

↑ **Parent:** [High-force worm-like chain elasticity](high-force-worm-like-chain-elasticity.md)

The bulk tangent [covariance](covariance.md) is twice the inverse [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) of $k_BT/(Aq^2+f)$, one term for each transverse direction. Equivalently the inverse of $-A\partial_r^2+f$ is $e^{-|r|/\xi}/(2\sqrt{Af})$. This gives the displayed correlation and identifies a force-dependent [correlation length](correlation-length.md), different from the zero-force [persistence length](persistence-length.md). Finite chains require endpoint conditions or a periodic convention.
