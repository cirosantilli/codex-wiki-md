# Complementary log-log link

↑ **Parent:** [Link function](link-function.md)

The [complementary log-log link](complementary-log-log-link.md) maps $p\in(0,1)$ to $\ln[-\ln(1-p)]$, with inverse $p=1-\exp(-e^\eta)$. For constant infection hazard $\lambda$ and exposure $e$, $p=1-e^{-\lambda e}$ implies $\operatorname{cloglog}(p)=\ln\lambda+\ln e$. Thus a [binomial regression](binomial-regression.md) with a log-exposure [offset](generalized-linear-model-offset.md) estimates the log hazard as its intercept.
