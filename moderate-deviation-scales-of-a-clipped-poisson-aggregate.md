# Moderate deviation scales of a clipped Poisson aggregate

↑ **Parent:** [Bufferless queue output](bufferless-queue-output.md)

Clip [Poisson](poisson-distribution.md) input of mean $L\lambda$ at $L\lambda+C L^{(1+\beta)/2}$. The [Poisson moderate deviation principle](poisson-moderate-deviation-principle.md) and [contraction principle for large deviations](contraction-principle-for-large-deviations.md) give the displayed output rate at the clipping scale. At smaller exponents the output retains the original rate by [moderate deviations below a bufferless queue cap](moderate-deviations-below-a-bufferless-queue-cap.md). At larger positive-deviation scales every overflow event is eventually impossible because the requested threshold exceeds the cap.
