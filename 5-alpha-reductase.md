<h1 id="5-alpha-reductase">5α-reductase</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Enzyme](enzyme-split.md)

Steroid [5α-reductase](5-alpha-reductase.md) reduces the conjugated double bond of particular steroids using [NADPH](nadph.md). Human type 2 can convert [testosterone](testosterone.md) to [dihydrotestosterone](dihydrotestosterone.md) and generate an inhibitory cofactor adduct from [finasteride](finasteride.md).
