# Two-photon decay

↑ **Parent:** [Particle decay](particle-decay.md)

Decay of a massive parent into exactly two [photons](photon.md). In its rest frame, [momentum conservation](momentum-conservation.md) requires opposite photon momenta of equal magnitude, so each photon has energy $Mc^2/2$. A boost along their common axis gives laboratory photon energies $\gamma Mc^2(1\pm\beta)/2$. Their product stays $(Mc^2/2)^2$ in that collinear configuration; other emission angles require the corresponding angular [Lorentz transformation](lorentz-transformation.md).
