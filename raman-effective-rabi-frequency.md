# Raman effective Rabi frequency

↑ **Parent:** [Stimulated Raman transition](stimulated-raman-transition.md)

Eliminating a common far-detuned intermediate state with single-photon couplings $\hbar\Omega_1/2$ and $\hbar\Omega_2/2$ gives effective [Rabi angular frequency](rabi-angular-frequency.md) of magnitude $|\Omega_1\Omega_2|/(2|\Delta|)$. The complex [laser](laser-split.md) phases set the coupling phase, and differential [AC Stark shifts](ac-stark-shift.md) change the two-photon resonance.
