# Classification of Weinstein-functional minimizers

↑ **Parent:** [Weinstein functional](weinstein-functional.md)

Every minimizer of the [Weinstein functional](weinstein-functional.md) has the form

$$
u(x)=aQ(b(x-x_0)),
\qquad
a\in\mathbb C\setminus\{0\},\quad b>0,\quad x_0\in\mathbb R^d,
$$

where $Q$ is the positive radial [NLS ground state](nls-ground-state.md). The [Euler-Lagrange equation](euler-lagrange-equation.md) first reduces a minimizer to a rescaled ground-state equation; the equality cases in the diamagnetic and rearrangement inequalities give the constant phase, translation, and radial profile.
