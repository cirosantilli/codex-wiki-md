# Factorial derivative criterion for real analyticity

↑ **Parent:** [Real analytic function](real-analytic-function.md)

A [smooth function](smooth-function.md) on a real open interval is [real analytic](real-analytic-function.md) exactly when, near each point, there are constants $C,A>0$ such that $|f^{(n)}(x)|\leq CA^n n!$ for every $n$ and every $x$ in that neighborhood. The bound must hold on a neighborhood, not only at its center. The [Taylor theorem with Lagrange remainder](taylor-theorem-with-lagrange-remainder.md) proves sufficiency; differentiating a convergent [power series](power-series.md) on a smaller interval proves necessity. A [flat function](flat-function.md) shows why pointwise bounds alone are insufficient.
