# Diffusive large-scale pair dispersion

↑ **Parent:** [Richardson pair dispersion](richardson-pair-dispersion.md)

At separations well above the [integral scale of turbulence](integral-scale-of-turbulence.md), two fluid velocities can decorrelate spatially. If the relative-velocity [autocorrelation](autocorrelation.md) $R_{\mathrm{rel}}$ is stationary and integrable, the displacement identity used in [Taylor turbulent dispersion](taylor-turbulent-dispersion.md) gives the displayed long-time linear mean-square growth. With speed $U$ and [Lagrangian integral time](lagrangian-integral-time.md) of order $L/U$, its relative [diffusivity](diffusion-coefficient.md) is of order $UL$. The characteristic separation grows as $t^{1/2}$, unlike inertial-range [Richardson pair dispersion](richardson-pair-dispersion.md).
