# Regulator of an elliptic curve

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical height of an elliptic curve](canonical-height-of-an-elliptic-curve.md)

The regulator is the determinant of the canonical-height pairing on a basis of the free part of the Mordell-Weil group. A unimodular basis change leaves it invariant.
