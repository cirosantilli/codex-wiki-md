# Fundamental system of solutions

↑ **Parent:** [Linear ordinary differential equation](linear-ordinary-differential-equation-split.md)

A fundamental system for a homogeneous [linear ordinary differential equation](linear-ordinary-differential-equation-split.md) of order $n$ on an interval is a [basis](basis.md) of its [vector space](vector-space-split.md) of [homogeneous solutions](homogeneous-solution.md). Every solution has a unique expression $\sum_{j=1}^nc_jy_j$ with constant [coefficients](coefficient.md). Under the usual [continuity](continuous-function.md) assumptions and a nonvanishing leading [coefficient](coefficient.md), a nonzero [Wronskian](wronskian.md) at one point proves that $n$ solutions form such a system: their initial-value [vectors](vector.md) are independent and span all possible initial data, and the [Picard-Lindelöf theorem](picard-lindelof-theorem.md) supplies uniqueness. For a first-order [vector](vector.md) system, the corresponding columns form a [fundamental matrix](fundamental-matrix-of-a-linear-differential-equation.md).
