# Boundary-incompressible surface

↑ **Parent:** [Incompressible surface](incompressible-surface.md)

A properly embedded [topological surface](topological-surface.md) is boundary-incompressible when no disk in the ambient [three-manifold](3-manifold.md) joins an essential arc on the [topological surface](topological-surface.md) to an arc on the ambient boundary, with the disk's interior disjoint from both.
