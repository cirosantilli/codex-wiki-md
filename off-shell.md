# Off shell

↑ **Parent:** [Euler-Lagrange field equation](euler-lagrange-field-equation.md)

An [off shell](off-shell.md) field configuration is not required to satisfy the [Euler-Lagrange field equation](euler-lagrange-field-equation.md). An external [four-momentum](four-momentum.md) is [off shell](off-shell.md) if it is not constrained to its physical [mass shell](mass-shell.md). [On shell](on-shell.md) identities can therefore fail for general untransformed [correlation functions](correlation-function.md), even when the associated [S-matrix](s-matrix.md) is unchanged by a [field redefinition](field-redefinition.md).
