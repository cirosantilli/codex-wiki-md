# Central pressure

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar structure](stellar-structure-split.md)

The [pressure](pressure-split.md) at the centre of a star or planet. A uniform-density Newtonian sphere with negligible surface [pressure](pressure-split.md) has $P_c=3GM^2/(8\pi R^4)$, obtained by integrating [hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md).
