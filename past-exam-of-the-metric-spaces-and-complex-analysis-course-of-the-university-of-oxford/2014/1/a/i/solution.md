<h1 id="1/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [homeomorphism](../../../../../homeomorphism.md) $f:M\to N$ is a [bijection](../../../../../bijection.md) for which both $f$ and $f^{-1}$ are [continuous maps](../../../../../continuous-map.md), using the respective [metric topologies](../../../../../metric-topology.md).

$$
\boxed{f\text{ homeomorphism}\iff f\text{ bijective and }f,f^{-1}\text{ continuous}.}
$$

[Continuity](../../../../../continuous-function.md) of a [bijection](../../../../../bijection.md) alone is insufficient: [continuity](../../../../../continuous-function.md) of the inverse is a separate requirement.
