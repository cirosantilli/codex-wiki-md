<h1 id="1/b/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

**True.** For every finite partial sum,

$$
\sum_{j=0}^N|x_j|^2\leq\left(\sum_{j=0}^N|x_j|\right)^2\leq\|x\|_1^2.
$$

The nondecreasing partial sums on the left are bounded, so the square series converges. Hence $\ell^1\subseteq\ell^2$ and

$$
\boxed{\|x\|_2\leq\|x\|_1.}
$$

Applying this [norm](../../../../../norm.md) inequality to $x-y$ proves $\|f(x)-f(y)\|_2\leq\|x-y\|_1$. Thus the inclusion is a [Lipschitz continuous](../../../../../lipschitz-continuity.md) map, in particular [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md), and it is well-defined on every element of [l1 sequence space](../../../../../absolutely-summable-sequence-space.md).
