<h1 id="1/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

**True.** For $u,v\in f(M)$ choose preimages $x,y\in M$. A [path](../../../../../continuous-path.md) $\gamma:[0,1]\to M$ joining $x$ to $y$ exists because $M$ is [path-connected](../../../../../path-connected-space.md). The [composition of continuous functions](../../../../../composition-of-continuous-functions.md) $f\circ\gamma$ is a [path](../../../../../continuous-path.md) in $f(M)$ from $u$ to $v$.

$$
\boxed{M\text{ path-connected}\Longrightarrow f(M)\text{ path-connected}.}
$$

[Surjectivity](../../../../../surjective-function.md) onto a larger target is unnecessary because the conclusion concerns the image itself.
