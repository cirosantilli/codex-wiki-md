<h1 id="1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**False.** Removing a point from the [real line](../../../../../real-line.md) leaves two disjoint nonempty open rays, so the remainder is disconnected. Removing a point from the [Euclidean plane](../../../../../euclidean-plane.md) leaves a [path-connected space](../../../../../path-connected-space.md): join any two remaining points by a [line segment](../../../../../line-segment.md) if it avoids the removed point, or by two segments through a point not on either of the two lines through the removed point and the chosen endpoints.

A proposed [homeomorphism](../../../../../homeomorphism.md) would restrict to a [homeomorphism](../../../../../homeomorphism.md) between these punctured spaces and preserve [connectedness](../../../../../connected-space.md), which is impossible. Hence

$$
\boxed{\mathbb R^2\not\cong\mathbb R.}
$$
