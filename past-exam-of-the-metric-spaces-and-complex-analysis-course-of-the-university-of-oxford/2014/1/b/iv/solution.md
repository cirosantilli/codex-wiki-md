<h1 id="1/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

**True.** For any [continuous map](../../../../../continuous-map.md), $f(\overline A)\subseteq\overline{f(A)}$: every open neighbourhood of $f(x)$ pulls back to an open neighbourhood of $x\in\overline A$, and therefore meets $A$ in its preimage.

Apply the same inclusion to the [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md) inverse of the [homeomorphism](../../../../../homeomorphism.md):

$$
f^{-1}(\overline{f(A)})\subseteq\overline{f^{-1}(f(A))}=\overline A.
$$

Applying $f$ gives the reverse inclusion, proving

$$
\boxed{\overline{f(A)}=f(\overline A).}
$$

The closures are taken in their respective ambient spaces.
