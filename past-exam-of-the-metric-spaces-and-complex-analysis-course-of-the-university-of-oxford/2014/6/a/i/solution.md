<h1 id="6/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The line through the north [pole](../../../../../pole.md) and $p=(a,b,c)$ is

$$
(0,0,1)+t(a,b,c-1).
$$

Its intersection with the equatorial complex plane has third coordinate zero, giving $t=1/(1-c)$ when $p$ is not the north [pole](../../../../../pole.md). The first two coordinates are therefore $a/(1-c)$ and $b/(1-c)$, hence

$$
\boxed{\pi(a,b,c)=\frac{a+bi}{1-c}.}
$$

The north [pole](../../../../../pole.md) maps to the point at infinity. This is [stereographic projection](../../../../../stereographic-projection.md) onto the [Riemann sphere](../../../../../riemann-sphere.md).
