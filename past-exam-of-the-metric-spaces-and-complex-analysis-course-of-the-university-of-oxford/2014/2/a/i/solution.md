↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Nonnegativity and symmetry follow from the corresponding [metric](../../../../../metric.md) axioms in the two factors. The maximum is zero exactly when both coordinate distances are zero, hence exactly when the ordered pairs are equal.

For the [triangle inequality](../../../../../triangle-inequality.md), put $p=(m_1,n_1)$, $q=(m_2,n_2)$ and $r=(m_3,n_3)$. Then

$$
\begin{aligned}
\delta(p,r)&\leq\max\{d_M(m_1,m_2)+d_M(m_2,m_3),\ d_N(n_1,n_2)+d_N(n_2,n_3)\}\\
&\leq\max\{d_M(m_1,m_2),d_N(n_1,n_2)\}+\max\{d_M(m_2,m_3),d_N(n_2,n_3)\}\\
&=\delta(p,q)+\delta(q,r).
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore **the maximum formula defines a [metric](../../../../../metric.md)**, the [maximum product metric](../../../../../maximum-product-metric.md).
