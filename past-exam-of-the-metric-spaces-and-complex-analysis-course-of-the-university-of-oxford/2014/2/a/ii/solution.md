<h1 id="2/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Each coordinate distance is at most the maximum, so convergence of the pairs implies convergence of both coordinates. Conversely, for any $\epsilon>0$, coordinate convergence gives indices $N_M,N_N$ after which the respective distances are below $\epsilon$. Beyond their maximum, both are below $\epsilon$, and so is the [maximum product metric](../../../../../maximum-product-metric.md).

$$
\boxed{(x_n,y_n)\to(x,y)\iff x_n\to x\text{ and }y_n\to y.}
$$

The same distance comparison will also handle [Cauchy sequences](../../../../../cauchy-sequence.md).
