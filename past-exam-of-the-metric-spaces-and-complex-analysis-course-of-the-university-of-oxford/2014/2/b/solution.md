<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For coordinate differences $a,b$, the elementary inequalities

$$
\max(|a|,|b|)\leq\sqrt{a^2+b^2}\leq\sqrt2\max(|a|,|b|)
$$

give

$$
\boxed{\delta(p,q)\leq d_2(p,q)\leq\sqrt2\,\delta(p,q).}
$$

The identity map between the two versions of the square is bijective, and both it and its inverse are [Lipschitz continuous](../../../../lipschitz-continuity.md) maps by these inequalities. It is therefore a [homeomorphism](../../../../homeomorphism.md). In fact the same comparison works on all of $\mathbb R^2$; restriction to the square causes no difficulty.
