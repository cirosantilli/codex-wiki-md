<h1 id="4/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For the usual piecewise smooth complex [path](../../../../../continuous-path.md) $\gamma$, let $\ell(\gamma)$ denote its finite length. The [estimation lemma](../../../../../estimation-lemma.md) gives

$$
\left|\int_\gamma f\,dz-\int_\gamma f_n\,dz\right|
\leq\ell(\gamma)\sup_{z\in\gamma}|f(z)-f_n(z)|.
$$

By [uniform convergence](../../../../../uniform-convergence.md), the supremum tends to zero, so

$$
\boxed{\int_\gamma f\,dz=\lim_{n\to\infty}\int_\gamma f_n\,dz.}
$$

The uniform limit is [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md) on the [path](../../../../../continuous-path.md), so its integral exists. This is [uniform convergence and contour integration](../../../../../uniform-convergence-and-contour-integration.md); the [path](../../../../../continuous-path.md) length is fixed while the functions vary.
