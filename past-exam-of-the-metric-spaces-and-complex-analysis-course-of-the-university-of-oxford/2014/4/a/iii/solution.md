<h1 id="4/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Let the common domain be an [open set](../../../../../open-set.md) $U$. The [uniform limit theorem](../../../../../uniform-limit-theorem.md) makes the limit $f$ [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md). For every triangle whose closed interior lies in $U$, [Cauchy's integral theorem](../../../../../cauchy-s-integral-theorem.md) gives $\int_{\partial T}f_n=0$. Part (ii) passes this identity to the limit, giving $\int_{\partial T}f=0$. [Morera's theorem](../../../../../morera-s-theorem.md) now proves that **$f$ is [holomorphic](../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md)**.

The same reasoning works for [locally uniform convergence](../../../../../locally-uniform-convergence.md), since each triangle boundary is [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md). No passage of derivatives through a limit has been assumed.
