[Morera's theorem](../../../../../morera-s-theorem.md) says that a [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md) complex-valued [function](../../../../../function-split.md) $f$ on an [open set](../../../../../open-set.md) $U\subseteq\mathbb C$ is [holomorphic](../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) if

$$
\boxed{\int_{\partial T}f(z)\,dz=0}
$$

for every triangle $T$ whose closed interior is contained in $U$. Triangle boundaries are traversed with positive orientation. The [continuity](../../../../../continuous-function.md) hypothesis matters: the zero-integral condition is combined with [continuity](../../../../../continuous-function.md), not used in isolation.
