<h1 id="3/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Write $\alpha=a+ib$ with $b>0$. Then

$$
\boxed{f_\alpha(z)=a+bz.}
$$

It preserves the [upper half-plane](../../../../../upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md), has inverse $(z-a)/b$, and sends $i$ to $\alpha$. Its nonzero [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) $b$ verifies that this [bijection](../../../../../bijection.md) is conformal.
