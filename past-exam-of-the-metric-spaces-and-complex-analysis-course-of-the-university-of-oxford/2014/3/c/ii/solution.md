<h1 id="3/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The map $h(z)=e^{\pi z/2}$ is a [conformal bijection](../../../../../biholomorphism.md) from the width-two strip to the [upper half-plane](../../../../../upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md), and $h(i)=i$. Its inverse there is $h^{-1}(w)=2L(w)/\pi$.

Write $e^{\pi\beta/2}=A+iB$, where $B>0$. Conjugate the preceding affine half-plane map by $h$:

$$
\boxed{g_\beta(z)=\frac2\pi L\left(A+B e^{\pi z/2}\right).}
$$

Every argument of $L$ lies in the [upper half-plane](../../../../../upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md). The formula is therefore a conformal [bijection](../../../../../bijection.md) of the strip onto itself, and

$$
g_\beta(i)=\frac2\pi L(A+iB)=\frac2\pi L(e^{\pi\beta/2})=\beta.
$$

The final equality uses $0<\operatorname{Im}(\pi\beta/2)<\pi$, so no multiple of $2\pi i$ is lost. This is a [strip automorphism with a prescribed image of its midpoint](../../../../../strip-automorphism-with-a-prescribed-image-of-its-midpoint.md).
