<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A [holomorphic primitive](../../../../../holomorphic-primitive.md) on the slit domain is $P(z)=zL(z)-z$, since the [product rule](../../../../../product-rule.md) and $L'=1/z$ give $P'=L$. The [line segment](../../../../../line-segment.md) stays in that domain. The [fundamental theorem for line integrals](../../../../../fundamental-theorem-for-line-integrals.md) therefore gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
\int_{[1,i]}L(z)\,dz&=P(i)-P(1)\\
&=\left(i\frac{\pi i}{2}-i\right)-(-1).
\end{aligned}
$$

Hence

$$
\boxed{\int_{[1,i]}L(z)\,dz=1-\frac\pi2-i.}
$$

The value $L(i)=\pi i/2$ is fixed by the chosen branch.
