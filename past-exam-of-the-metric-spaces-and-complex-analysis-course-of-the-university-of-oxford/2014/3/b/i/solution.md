<h1 id="3/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use the [principal complex logarithm](../../../../../principal-complex-logarithm.md):

$$
\boxed{L(z)=\log|z|+i\operatorname{Arg}z,\qquad-\pi<\operatorname{Arg}z<\pi.}
$$

The excluded nonpositive real ray allows a [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md) choice of argument, and $L(1)=0$. For $z=x+iy$, its [real part](../../../../../real-part.md) is $u=\tfrac12\log(x^2+y^2)$. Differentiating the chosen local argument gives

$$
u_x=\frac{x}{x^2+y^2},\quad u_y=\frac{y}{x^2+y^2},\quad
v_x=-\frac{y}{x^2+y^2},\quad v_y=\frac{x}{x^2+y^2}.
$$

These [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md) partial derivatives satisfy the [Cauchy-Riemann equations](../../../../../cauchy-riemann-equations.md), proving [holomorphy](../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) on the slit domain. Its [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) is

$$
\boxed{L'(z)=u_x+iv_x=\frac{x-iy}{x^2+y^2}=\frac1z.}
$$
