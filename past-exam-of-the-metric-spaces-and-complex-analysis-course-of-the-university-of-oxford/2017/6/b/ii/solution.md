<h1 id="6/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

On the first quadrant choose the holomorphic logarithm with $0<\arg w<\pi/2$. It maps $w=\rho e^{i\theta}$ to $\log\rho+i\theta$, so its image is precisely the strip $0<\operatorname{Im}\zeta<\pi/2$. Its inverse there is the [exponential function](../../../../../exponential-function.md); both derivatives are nonzero. Composing with part (i) gives

$$
\boxed{F(z)=\operatorname{Log}\!\left((1+i)\frac{1+z}{1-z}\right).}
$$

This is a bijective [conformal map](../../../../../conformal-map.md) from the lens onto the strip, with inverse

$$
z=\frac{e^\zeta-(1+i)}{e^\zeta+(1+i)}.
$$

The logarithm's branch is fixed by its argument range on the quadrant. Using a different branch would shift the strip vertically by an integer multiple of $2\pi$. This is the [lens-to-strip logarithmic conformal map](../../../../../lens-to-strip-logarithmic-conformal-map.md).
