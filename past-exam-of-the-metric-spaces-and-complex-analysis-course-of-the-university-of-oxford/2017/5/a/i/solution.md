↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

[Laurent's theorem](../../../../../laurent-theorem.md) states that a function holomorphic on $R<|z-a|<S$ has a unique expansion

$$
f(z)=\sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty}c_n(z-a)^n,
$$

converging uniformly on every compact subannulus. Its coefficients are

$$
\boxed{c_n=\frac1{2\pi i}\int_{|\zeta-a|=\rho}\frac{f(\zeta)}{(\zeta-a)^{n+1}}\,d\zeta,\qquad R<\rho<S,}
$$

with counterclockwise orientation. The value is independent of the chosen intermediate radius. The nonnegative powers form a power series convergent inside the outer radius, and the negative powers form the [principal part of a Laurent series](../../../../../principal-part-of-a-laurent-series.md).
