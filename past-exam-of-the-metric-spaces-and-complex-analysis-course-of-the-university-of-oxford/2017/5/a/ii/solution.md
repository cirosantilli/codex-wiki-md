↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [residue](../../../../../residue.md) $\operatorname{res}(f;a)$ is **the coefficient $c_{-1}$ of $(z-a)^{-1}$** in the [Laurent series](../../../../../laurent-series.md) at the isolated singularity. Equivalently it is $(2\pi i)^{-1}$ times the integral around a sufficiently small counterclockwise circle enclosing $a$.

The [residue theorem](../../../../../residue-theorem.md) states that if $f$ is holomorphic on a neighborhood of a positively oriented simple closed contour and its interior except for finitely many isolated singularities $a_j$ inside, then

$$
\boxed{\int_\gamma f(z)\,dz=2\pi i\sum_j\operatorname{res}(f;a_j).}
$$

No singularity may lie on the integration contour. A more general contour weights each residue by its winding number.
