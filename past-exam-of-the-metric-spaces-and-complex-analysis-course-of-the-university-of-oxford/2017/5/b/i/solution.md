<h1 id="5/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For $z=x+iy$, $e^{iz}=e^{-y}e^{ix}$, so $|e^{iz}|=e^{-y}$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{|e^{iz}|\leq1\iff\operatorname{Im}z\geq0,}
$$

the closed [upper half-plane](../../../../../upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md). Equality holds on the real axis.
