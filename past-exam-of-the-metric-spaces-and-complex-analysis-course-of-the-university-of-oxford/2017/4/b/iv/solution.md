<h1 id="4/b/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Take **$g_n(z)=z^n/n$ and $g(z)=0$**, for $n\geq1$. These functions are entire and

$$
\sup_{|z|\leq1}|g_n(z)|=1/n\longrightarrow0,
$$

so convergence is uniform on the closed unit disc. But $g_n'(z)=z^{n-1}$, giving

$$
\boxed{\sup_{|z|\leq1}|g_n'(z)-g'(z)|=1}
$$

for every $n$. In particular $g_n'(1)=1$ never approaches zero. On every strictly smaller disc the [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) does converge uniformly, consistent with part (iii). This is [uniform holomorphic convergence without boundary derivative convergence](../../../../../uniform-holomorphic-convergence-without-boundary-derivative-convergence.md).
