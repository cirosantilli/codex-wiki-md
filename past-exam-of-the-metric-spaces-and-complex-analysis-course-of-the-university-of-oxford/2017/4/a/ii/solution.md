↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**Yes:** take the [unnormalized sinc function](../../../../../unnormalized-sinc-function.md) with its removable value at zero,

$$
\boxed{g(z)=\begin{cases}\sin z/z,&z\ne0,\\1,&z=0.\end{cases}}
$$

Its [power series](../../../../../power-series.md) is $\sum_{m=0}^\infty(-1)^mz^{2m}/(2m+1)!$, with infinite [radius of convergence](../../../../../radius-of-convergence.md), so it is an [entire function](../../../../../entire-function.md). At $z=1/n$ it gives $n\sin(1/n)$ as required. The [identity theorem](../../../../../identity-theorem.md) also proves this entire solution is unique.
