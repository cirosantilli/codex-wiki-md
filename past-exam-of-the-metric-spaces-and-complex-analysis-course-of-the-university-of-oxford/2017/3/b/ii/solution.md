↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [Cauchy integral theorem](../../../../../cauchy-s-integral-theorem.md) for a [star-shaped domain](../../../../../star-shaped-domain.md) says: if $U$ is open and star-shaped and $F$ is holomorphic on $U$, then **$\int_\gamma F(z)\,dz=0$ for every closed contour $\gamma$ in $U$**. Equivalently, $F$ has a [complex antiderivative](../../../../../complex-antiderivative.md) on $U$. Star-shaped means that there is one point of $U$ from which every straight segment to another point of $U$ remains in $U$.
