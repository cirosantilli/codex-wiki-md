<h1 id="3/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For $\phi(t)=re^{it}$, $\phi'(t)=ire^{it}$ and $|\phi'(t)|=r$. The [triangle inequality for an integral](../../../../../integral-triangle-inequality.md) applied to the parametrized [contour integral](../../../../../contour-integral.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\left|\int_\phi f(z)\,dz\right|=\left|\int_0^{2\pi}f(re^{it})ire^{it}\,dt\right|\leq r\int_0^{2\pi}|f(re^{it})|\,dt.}
$$

This is the circular-contour instance of the [estimation lemma](../../../../../estimation-lemma.md).
