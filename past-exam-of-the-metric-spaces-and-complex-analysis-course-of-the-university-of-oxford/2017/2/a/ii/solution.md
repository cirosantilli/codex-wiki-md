<h1 id="2/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A space is [path-connected](../../../../../path-connected-space.md) if for every pair $x_0,x_1$ it contains a [continuous path](../../../../../continuous-path.md) $\gamma:[0,1]\to X$ with $\gamma(0)=x_0$ and $\gamma(1)=x_1$. For any $y_0,y_1\in f(X)$ choose preimages $x_0,x_1$. A path between those preimages maps to $f\circ\gamma$, a continuous path from $y_0$ to $y_1$ staying in $f(X)$. Thus **a [continuous image of a path-connected space](../../../../../continuous-image-of-a-path-connected-space.md) is [path-connected](../../../../../path-connected-space.md)**. Surjectivity onto the ambient codomain $Y$ is not needed; the conclusion concerns $f(X)$.
