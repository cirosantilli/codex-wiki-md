<h1 id="2/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Suppose a [path-connected](../../../../../path-connected-space.md) space had a separation $X=U\cup V$ as above. Choose $u\in U$, $v\in V$, and a continuous path $\gamma$ joining them. The nonempty disjoint sets $\gamma^{-1}(U)$ and $\gamma^{-1}(V)$ would separate $[0,1]$, contradicting [connectedness of an interval](../../../../../connectedness-of-an-interval.md). Hence **[path-connectedness implies connectedness](../../../../../path-connectedness-implies-connectedness.md)**. Equivalently, the connected path image from part (i) cannot meet both sides of a separation.
