# 2023

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the metric spaces and complex analysis course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Solutions to all six questions of the [University of Oxford](../university-of-oxford-split.md) Part A [metric space](../metric-space.md) and [complex analysis](../complex-analysis-split.md) paper, sat on 12 June 2023. Source: [released examination PDF, compilation pages 65–68; paper pages 1–4](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/bsc_mathematics_part_a_examinati/response/2820674/attach/3/FOI%20202410%20944%20Data.pdf#page=65). The solutions retain the paper's labels. In 2(b)(i), the printed zero case $m\ne0$ is inconsistent with its one case; the solution explicitly uses the intended $m\ne n$. The extra closing parenthesis in the printed definition of the [supremum norm](../supremum-norm.md) metric has no mathematical effect.
