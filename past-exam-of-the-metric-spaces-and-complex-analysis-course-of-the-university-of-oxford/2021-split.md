# 2021

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the metric spaces and complex analysis course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Solutions to all six questions of the [University of Oxford](../university-of-oxford-split.md) Part A [metric space](../metric-space.md) and [complex analysis](../complex-analysis-split.md) paper, sat on 14 June 2021. Source: [released examination PDF, compilation pages 54–58; paper pages 1–5](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/bsc_mathematics_part_a_examinati/response/2820674/attach/3/FOI%20202410%20944%20Data.pdf#page=54). The examination counted the best four answers; all six are covered here. The printed request in 6(c)(i) is impossible for the stated planar domain: its solution proves this and gives the precise map obtained when the missing point at infinity is accounted for.
