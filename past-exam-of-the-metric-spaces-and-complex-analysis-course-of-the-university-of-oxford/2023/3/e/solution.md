<h1 id="3/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [e](../e.md)

Define $F(z)=\overline{f(\overline z)}$. To apply the [identity theorem](../../../../identity-theorem.md), we first verify that $F$ is [holomorphic](../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md); mere [complex conjugation](../../../../complex-conjugation.md) of the values of $f$ would not suffice. For $h\ne0$,

$$
\frac{F(z+h)-F(z)}h
=\overline{\frac{f(\overline z+\overline h)-f(\overline z)}{\overline h}}
\longrightarrow\overline{f'(\overline z)}.
$$

Thus $F$ is an [entire function](../../../../entire-function.md), just as $f$ is. At each positive integer $n$, the point $1/n$ is real and $f(1/n)$ is real, so $F(1/n)=f(1/n)$. These distinct points accumulate at $0\in\mathbb C$. The [identity theorem](../../../../identity-theorem.md) on the [connected](../../../../connected-space.md) [complex plane](../../../../complex-plane.md) gives

$$
\boxed{f(z)=\overline{f(\overline z)}\quad(z\in\mathbb C)}.
$$

This is [conjugate reflection of a holomorphic function](../../../../conjugate-reflection-of-a-holomorphic-function.md), with the reality data forcing equality to its reflection.
