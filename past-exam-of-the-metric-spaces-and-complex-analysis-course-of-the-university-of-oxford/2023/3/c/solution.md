<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [c](../c.md)

The function $h(z)=1/(z+1)$ is [holomorphic](../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) on $U$ and has

$$
\frac1{z+1}=\frac{x+1-iy}{(x+1)^2+y^2}.
$$

Its imaginary part is exactly the prescribed $v$. Thus $f-h$ is a real-valued [holomorphic function](../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md). The [Cauchy-Riemann equations](../../../../cauchy-riemann-equations.md) force both partial derivatives of its real part to vanish. Since $\mathbb C\setminus\{-1\}$ is a [connected space](../../../../connected-space.md), $f-h$ is a single real constant. All possibilities are therefore

$$
\boxed{f(z)=\frac1{z+1}+C,\qquad C\in\mathbb R}.
$$

Conversely every such function has the stated imaginary part. The data determine $f$ only up to this real additive constant.
