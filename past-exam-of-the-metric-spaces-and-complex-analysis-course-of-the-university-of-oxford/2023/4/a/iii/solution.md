A [holomorphic function](../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) has a zero of order $n$ at $a$ when it is [holomorphic](../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) on a neighbourhood of $a$ and factors as

$$
\boxed{f(z)=(z-a)^n h(z),\qquad h\text{ holomorphic},\quad h(a)\ne0}.
$$

Equivalently, $f(a)=f'(a)=\cdots=f^{(n-1)}(a)=0$ and $f^{(n)}(a)\ne0$. The [order of a zero of a holomorphic function](../../../../../order-of-a-zero-of-a-holomorphic-function.md) is the degree of the first nonzero term of its [Taylor series](../../../../../taylor-series.md).
