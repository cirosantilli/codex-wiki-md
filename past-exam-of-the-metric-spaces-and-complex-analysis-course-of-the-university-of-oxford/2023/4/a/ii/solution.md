↑ **Parent:** [ii](../ii.md)

An [isolated singularity](../../../../../isolated-singularity-split.md) at $a$ is a [removable singularity](../../../../../removable-singularity.md) when there is a [holomorphic function](../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) $F$ on a full disc $B(a,r)$ agreeing with $f$ away from $a$. Equivalently,

$$
\boxed{\lim_{z\to a}f(z)=L\in\mathbb C,\qquad F(a)=L}.
$$

The [Riemann removable singularity theorem](../../../../../riemann-removable-singularity-theorem.md) also characterizes this by boundedness of $f$ on a sufficiently small punctured disc. A preassigned incorrect value at $a$ is replaced by $L$.
