↑ **Parent:** [ii](../ii.md)

A [subset](../../../../../subset.md) $F\subseteq X$ is a [closed set](../../../../../closed-set.md) in $X$ when its [complement](../../../../../complement-of-a-set.md) is an [open set](../../../../../open-set.md) in the [metric topology](../../../../../metric-topology.md). Equivalently,

$$
\boxed{x\in X\setminus F\ \Longrightarrow\ \exists r>0:\ B(x,r)\cap F=\varnothing}.
$$

The [open ball](../../../../../open-ball.md) is taken inside the ambient [metric space](../../../../../metric-space.md) $X$; closedness always depends on that ambient space.
