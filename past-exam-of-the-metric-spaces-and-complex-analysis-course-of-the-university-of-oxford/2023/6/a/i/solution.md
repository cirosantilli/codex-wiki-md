↑ **Parent:** [i](../i.md)

A [conformal map](../../../../../conformal-map.md) at a point $z_0$ is a map that preserves oriented angles between regular differentiable curves through $z_0$. In the differential formulation, it is real differentiable at $z_0$ and its derivative is an orientation-preserving similarity: a rotation followed by multiplication of lengths by a positive constant. The map is conformal on $U$ when it has this property at every point:

$$
\boxed{D f(z_0)=\lambda R_\alpha,\qquad\lambda>0}.
$$

Here $R_\alpha$ is a planar rotation. Conformality is a local property and does not itself require global injectivity. We use the oriented convention standard in [complex analysis](../../../../../complex-analysis-split.md); a reflection reverses oriented angles.
