↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A [metric space](../../../../../metric-space.md) is [path-connected](../../../../../path-connected-space.md) if every pair $x,y$ can be joined by a [path](../../../../../continuous-path.md): a [continuous map](../../../../../continuous-map.md) $\gamma:[0,1]\to X$ with $\gamma(0)=x$ and $\gamma(1)=y$.

$$
\boxed{\forall x,y\in X\ \exists\gamma:[0,1]\to X\text{ continuous joining }x\text{ to }y.}
$$

The [path](../../../../../continuous-path.md) must remain in $X$, including at its endpoints.
