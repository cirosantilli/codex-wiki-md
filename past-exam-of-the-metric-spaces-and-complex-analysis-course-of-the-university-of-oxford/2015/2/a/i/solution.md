↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [metric space](../../../../../metric-space.md) is [connected](../../../../../connected-space.md) when it has no [separation of a topological space](../../../../../separation-of-a-topological-space.md): it cannot be written as the union of two disjoint nonempty [open subsets](../../../../../open-set.md). Equivalently, the only [subsets](../../../../../subset.md) that are both [open](../../../../../open-set.md) and [closed](../../../../../closed-set.md) are the [empty set](../../../../../empty-set.md) and the whole space.

$$
\boxed{X\text{ connected}\iff\text{no two disjoint nonempty open sets cover }X.}
$$

The [open sets](../../../../../open-set.md) here are those of the [metric topology](../../../../../metric-topology.md).
