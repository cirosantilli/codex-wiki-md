<h1 id="2/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Only the right-hand ball is [closed](../../../../../closed-set.md), but the union is still **[path-connected](../../../../../path-connected-space.md) and [connected](../../../../../connected-space.md)**. Its right-hand [closed ball](../../../../../closed-ball.md) contains zero, and every point $z$ of the left-hand [open ball](../../../../../open-ball.md) can be joined to zero by $\gamma(t)=(1-t)z$.

To check the endpoint carefully, write $a=(-1,0)$. For $0\leq t<1$, [convexity](../../../../../convex-function.md) and the [triangle inequality](../../../../../triangle-inequality.md) give

$$
\lVert(1-t)z-a\rVert
\leq(1-t)\lVert z-a\rVert+t\lVert a\rVert<1.
$$

At $t=1$, the [path](../../../../../continuous-path.md) reaches zero in the other, closed ball. Points in that ball also join zero by [line segments](../../../../../line-segment.md), completing the construction.
