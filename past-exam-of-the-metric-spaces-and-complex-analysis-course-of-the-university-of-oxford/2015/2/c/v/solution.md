<h1 id="2/c/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

Let $D$ be this union of rational-coordinate horizontal and vertical [lines](../../../../../straight-line.md). A point $(x,y)\in D$ has either rational $x$, in which case it moves vertically to $(x,0)$ then horizontally to $(0,0)$, or rational $y$, in which case it moves horizontally to $(0,y)$ then vertically to $(0,0)$. Every segment remains on a [line](../../../../../straight-line.md) with a rational fixed coordinate.

Thus **$D$ is [path-connected](../../../../../path-connected-space.md) and [connected](../../../../../connected-space.md)**. It is not necessary for both coordinates to be rational.
