<h1 id="2/c/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Each vertical [line segment](../../../../../line-segment.md) over a rational coordinate meets the horizontal [line](../../../../../straight-line.md) at height one. A point on such a segment can move vertically to the top, then horizontally along that [line](../../../../../straight-line.md); points already at height one use only the horizontal motion. Concatenating these motions joins any two points.

Thus the [rational comb with a deleted attachment point](../../../../../rational-comb-with-a-deleted-attachment-point.md) construction, before deletion, is **[path-connected](../../../../../path-connected-space.md) and hence [connected](../../../../../connected-space.md)**. The infinitely many vertical pieces cause no difficulty because each individual [path](../../../../../continuous-path.md) uses only finitely many segments.
