<h1 id="2/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Each [closed ball](../../../../../closed-ball.md) is a [convex set](../../../../../convex-set-split.md) and contains the origin. Join any point to zero by a [line segment](../../../../../line-segment.md) inside its ball, then join zero to the other point. Concatenating the two segments gives a [path](../../../../../continuous-path.md) in the union. It is therefore **[path-connected](../../../../../path-connected-space.md) and [connected](../../../../../connected-space.md)**.
