<h1 id="2/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The two [open balls](../../../../../open-ball.md) are disjoint: a common point would have distances to their centres summing to less than two, contradicting the [triangle inequality](../../../../../triangle-inequality.md). Both are nonempty and relatively [open](../../../../../open-set.md), and they cover the union. They therefore give a [separation](../../../../../axiom-schema-of-specification.md), so the union is **neither [connected](../../../../../connected-space.md) nor [path-connected](../../../../../path-connected-space.md)**.
