<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Suppose a [path-connected space](../../../../path-connected-space.md) had a [separation](../../../../axiom-schema-of-specification.md) $X=U\cup V$. Choose $u\in U$, $v\in V$, and a [path](../../../../continuous-path.md) $\gamma$ from $u$ to $v$. The [inverse images](../../../../preimage.md) $\gamma^{-1}(U)$ and $\gamma^{-1}(V)$ are disjoint [open sets](../../../../open-set.md) in the [subspace topology](../../../../subspace-topology.md) of $[0,1]$, cover it, and are nonempty because they contain its opposite endpoints. This contradicts the [connectedness](../../../../connected-space.md) of an [interval](../../../../interval-mathematics.md).

Therefore **every [path-connected space](../../../../path-connected-space.md) is [connected](../../../../connected-space.md)**. Notice that it is the [subspace topology](../../../../subspace-topology.md) on $[0,1]$ that is used in the inverse-image argument.
