<h1 id="6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Put $w=1/z$, so $z=1/w$ and $w\ne0$. Substitute into the equation in part (a) and multiply by $|w|^2$:

$$
\frac{A}{|w|^2}+\frac{\overline B}{w}+\frac B{\overline w}+C=0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\boxed{C|w|^2+Bw+\overline B\overline w+A=0.}
$$

This is the [reciprocal transformation of a generalized circle](../../../../reciprocal-transformation-of-a-generalized-circle.md). In the original coefficient convention the new triple is $(C,\overline B,A)$: the linear coefficient is conjugated, which reflects the distinction between $1/z$ and [inversion in a circle](../../../../inversion-in-a-circle.md) $1/\overline z$.

**A [circle](../../../../circle.md) through zero.** Here $A\ne0$, $C=0$, and nondegeneracy forces $|B|^2>0$. Its image satisfies

$$
2\operatorname{Re}(Bw)+A=0.
$$

This is a [straight line](../../../../straight-line.md), and $w=0$ does not satisfy it because $A\ne0$. Conversely every point of this [straight line](../../../../straight-line.md) has a nonzero reciprocal on the original [circle](../../../../circle.md). Thus the original [circle](../../../../circle.md) with zero omitted maps onto the full finite [straight line](../../../../straight-line.md).

**A [circle](../../../../circle.md) missing zero.** Now $A\ne0$, $C\ne0$ and $\Delta=|B|^2-AC>0$. Applying part (a) to the transformed coefficients gives centre $-\overline B/C$ and [radius](../../../../radius.md) $\sqrt\Delta/|C|$, so the image is again a genuine [circle](../../../../circle.md). It misses zero, because the transformed equation has nonzero constant $A$. The inverse $z=1/w$ proves that the whole of this [circle](../../../../circle.md) is attained.

**A [straight line](../../../../straight-line.md) through zero.** Here $A=C=0$ and $B\ne0$. The transformed equation is $2\operatorname{Re}(Bw)=0$, another [straight line](../../../../straight-line.md) through zero. Precisely, the finite map takes the punctured original [straight line](../../../../straight-line.md) onto the punctured image [straight line](../../../../straight-line.md), since neither the domain nor the image of $1/z$ contains zero.

In the [Riemann sphere](../../../../riemann-sphere.md) convention $0\leftrightarrow\infty$, all three become exact statements about whole [generalized circles](../../../../generalized-circle-under-a-mobius-transformation.md):

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
\text{circle through }0&\longmapsto\text{line missing }0,\\
\text{circle missing }0&\longmapsto\text{circle missing }0,\\
\text{line through }0&\longmapsto\text{line through }0.
\end{aligned}}
$$

Finite [straight lines](../../../../straight-line.md) acquire their point at infinity in this convention. Stating it avoids accidentally adding $w=0$ merely because it satisfies a multiplied equation.
