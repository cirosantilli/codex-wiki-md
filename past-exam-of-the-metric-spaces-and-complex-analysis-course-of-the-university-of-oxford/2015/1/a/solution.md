The [Heine-Borel theorem](../../../../heine-borel-theorem.md) says that a [subset](../../../../subset.md) $K$ of finite-dimensional [Euclidean space](../../../../euclidean-norm.md) is [compact](../../../../compact-space.md) exactly when it is both [closed](../../../../closed-set.md) and [bounded](../../../../boundedness.md):

$$
\boxed{K\subseteq\mathbb R^n\text{ compact}\iff K\text{ closed and bounded}.}
$$

Here [compactness](../../../../compact-space.md) refers to the [subspace topology](../../../../subspace-topology.md) induced by the usual [Euclidean metric](../../../../euclidean-metric.md), and [closedness](../../../../closedness.md) and [boundedness](../../../../boundedness.md) are measured in the ambient [Euclidean space](../../../../euclidean-norm.md). This characterization does not hold for arbitrary [metric spaces](../../../../metric-space.md).
