↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [unit circle](../../../../../complex-unit-circle.md) is the inverse image of the [closed set](../../../../../closed-set.md) $\{1\}$ under the [continuous map](../../../../../continuous-map.md) $(x,y)\mapsto x^2+y^2$, so it is [closed](../../../../../closed-set.md). It is also [bounded](../../../../../boundedness.md), since every point has [Euclidean norm](../../../../../euclidean-norm.md) one. The [Heine-Borel theorem](../../../../../heine-borel-theorem.md) gives **[compactness](../../../../../compact-space.md)**.
