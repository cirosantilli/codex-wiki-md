<h1 id="1/c/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

The points $(n,0)$ belong to the indicated [subset](../../../../../subset.md) for every positive integer $n$. They form an [unbounded](../../../../../unbounded.md) [sequence](../../../../../sequence.md), so this [metric space](../../../../../metric-space.md) is not [bounded](../../../../../boundedness.md). Therefore **it is not [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md)**. Shrinking the vertical width does not bound its horizontal extent.
