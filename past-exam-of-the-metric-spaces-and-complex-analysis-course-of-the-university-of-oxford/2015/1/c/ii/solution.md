<h1 id="1/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Take an [irrational number](../../../../../irrational-number.md) $a\in(0,1)$. The [density of the rational numbers](../../../../../density-of-the-rational-numbers.md) provides a [sequence](../../../../../sequence.md) in $\mathbb Q\cap[0,1]$ converging to $a$, which is outside that [subset](../../../../../subset.md). Thus the [subset](../../../../../subset.md) is not [closed](../../../../../closed-set.md) in $\mathbb R$. By the [Heine-Borel theorem](../../../../../heine-borel-theorem.md), **it is not [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md)**, despite being [bounded](../../../../../boundedness.md).
