↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The open [unit disk](../../../../../unit-disk.md) is [bounded](../../../../../boundedness.md) but not [closed](../../../../../closed-set.md): $(1-1/n,0)$ lies in it for $n\geq2$ and converges to the omitted point $(1,0)$. By the [Heine-Borel theorem](../../../../../heine-borel-theorem.md), **it is not [compact](../../../../../compact-space.md)**.
