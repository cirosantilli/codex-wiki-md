<h1 id="5/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Start with the [exponential function](../../../../../exponential-function.md) definition of the [hyperbolic sine](../../../../../hyperbolic-sine.md) and use [Euler's formula](../../../../../euler-s-formula.md):

$$
\begin{aligned}
\sinh(x+iy)&=\frac{e^xe^{iy}-e^{-x}e^{-iy}}2\\
&=\frac{e^x(\cos y+i\sin y)-e^{-x}(\cos y-i\sin y)}2\\
&=\frac{e^x-e^{-x}}2\cos y+i\frac{e^x+e^{-x}}2\sin y.
\end{aligned}
$$

Identifying the [hyperbolic sine](../../../../../hyperbolic-sine.md) and [hyperbolic cosine](../../../../../hyperbolic-cosine.md) in the final line gives

$$
\boxed{\sinh(x+iy)=\sinh x\cos y+i\cosh x\sin y.}
$$
