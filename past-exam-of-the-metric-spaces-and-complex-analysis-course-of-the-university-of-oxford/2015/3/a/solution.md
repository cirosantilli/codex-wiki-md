<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

One standard form of the [Cauchy integral formula](../../../../cauchy-integral-formula.md) is this: if $f$ is [holomorphic](../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) on an [open set](../../../../open-set.md) containing a [contour](../../../../complex-integration-contour.md) that is a [simple closed curve](../../../../simple-closed-curve.md) $\Gamma$ and its interior, and $\Gamma$ has [positive orientation of a contour](../../../../positive-orientation-of-a-contour.md), then for every point $w$ inside it,

$$
\boxed{f(w)=\frac1{2\pi i}\int_\Gamma\frac{f(z)}{z-w}\,dz.}
$$

The hypothesis includes the contour itself; [singularities](../../../../complex-singularity.md) on the contour are excluded. In particular, the formula applies on a [circle](../../../../circle.md) whose closed [disk](../../../../disk-mathematics.md) is contained in the domain of [holomorphy](../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md).
