<h1 id="1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [i](../i.md)

The [inverse tangent](../../../../../inverse-tangent.md) $T(x)=\arctan x$ is a [homeomorphism](../../../../../homeomorphism.md) from the [real line](../../../../../real-line.md) with its [Euclidean metric](../../../../../euclidean-metric.md) onto $I=(-\pi/2,\pi/2)$ with its [subspace topology](../../../../../subspace-topology.md): $T$ and its inverse $\tan$ are [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md). It is also an [isometry](../../../../../isometry.md) from $(\mathbb R,\rho)$ onto $I$, because $\rho(x,y)=|T(x)-T(y)|$. Consequently, for $U\subseteq\mathbb R$,

$$
\boxed{U\text{ is }d\text{-open}\iff T(U)\text{ is open in }I\iff U\text{ is }\rho\text{-open}.}
$$

For a direct [open ball](../../../../../open-ball.md) interpretation, $B_\rho(x,r)=T^{-1}((T(x)-r,T(x)+r)\cap I)$ is $d$-open by [continuity](../../../../../continuous-function.md) of $T$. Conversely, [continuity](../../../../../continuous-function.md) of $\tan$ at $T(x)$ supplies a sufficiently small $\rho$-ball inside any prescribed $d$-ball about $x$. Thus both [metrics](../../../../../metric.md) generate exactly the same [open sets](../../../../../open-set.md).
