↑ **Parent:** [i](../i.md)

A [norm](../../../../../norm.md) on a real [vector space](../../../../../vector-space-split.md) $X$ is a map $\|\cdot\|:X\to[0,\infty)$ such that, for $x,y\in X$ and $\lambda\in\mathbb R$,

$$
\|x\|=0\iff x=0,\qquad \|\lambda x\|=|\lambda|\|x\|,\qquad \|x+y\|\leq\|x\|+\|y\|.
$$

The last property is the [triangle inequality](../../../../../triangle-inequality.md); the middle property is [absolute homogeneity of a norm](../../../../../absolute-homogeneity-of-a-norm.md). The induced [metric](../../../../../metric.md) is

$$
\boxed{d(x,y)=\|x-y\|.}
$$

Positivity and separation follow from the [norm](../../../../../norm.md) axioms, symmetry from [absolute homogeneity of a norm](../../../../../absolute-homogeneity-of-a-norm.md) with $\lambda=-1$, and the [triangle inequality](../../../../../triangle-inequality.md) from $x-z=(x-y)+(y-z)$.
