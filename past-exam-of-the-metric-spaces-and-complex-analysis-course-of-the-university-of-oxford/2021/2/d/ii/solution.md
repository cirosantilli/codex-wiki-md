<h1 id="2/d/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [ii](../ii.md)

If $f$ is [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md) at $a$, then for every $\varepsilon>0$ there is $r_0>0$ such that $d(x,a)<r_0$ implies $|f(x)-f(a)|<\varepsilon$. In particular, for $0<r\leq r_0$,

$$
f(a)\leq\sup_{x\in B(a,r)}f(x)\leq f(a)+\varepsilon.
$$

The [squeeze theorem](../../../../../squeeze-theorem.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\lim_{r\downarrow0}\sup_{x\in B(a,r)}f(x)=f(a),}
$$

which implies condition (1). Thus [continuity](../../../../../continuous-function.md) implies [upper semicontinuity](../../../../../upper-semicontinuity.md).
