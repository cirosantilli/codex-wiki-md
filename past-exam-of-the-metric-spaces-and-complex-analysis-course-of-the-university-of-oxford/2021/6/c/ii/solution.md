<h1 id="6/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [ii](../ii.md)

Use the same [principal square root](../../../../../principal-square-root-of-a-complex-number.md) $S(z)=\sqrt{(z-a)/(z-b)}$, which is [holomorphic](../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) on $A$ because its argument avoids the nonpositive [real axis](../../../../../real-axis.md). Then

$$
\boxed{g(z)=(z-b)\sqrt{\frac{z-a}{z-b}},\qquad z\in A,}
$$

is a [holomorphic function](../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) satisfying

$$
g(z)^2=(z-b)^2\frac{z-a}{z-b}=(z-a)(z-b).
$$

Thus it is a [holomorphic branch](../../../../../holomorphic-branch.md) of the requested [square root](../../../../../square-root.md). This construction does not require a global [holomorphic logarithm](../../../../../holomorphic-logarithm.md) of $z-a$ or $z-b$ separately. Its sign is fixed by $S(z)\to1$ at infinity, so $g(z)/z\to1$. The negative of this [function](../../../../../function-split.md) is the other [holomorphic branch](../../../../../holomorphic-branch.md); any two branches have a [continuous](../../../../../continuous-function.md) quotient taking values in $\{1,-1\}$, which is constant because $A$ is [connected](../../../../../connected-space.md).
