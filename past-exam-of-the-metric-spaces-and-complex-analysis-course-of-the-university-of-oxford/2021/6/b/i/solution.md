<h1 id="6/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [i](../i.md)

Write $\Delta=ad-bc>0$. For real $a,b,c,d$ and $z\in H$, the denominator cannot vanish: if $c\ne0$ its zero $-d/c$ is real, and if $c=0$ then $d\ne0$. Taking the [imaginary part](../../../../../imaginary-part.md) after multiplying by $\overline{cz+d}$ gives

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Im}\frac{az+b}{cz+d}=\frac{\Delta\operatorname{Im}z}{|cz+d|^2}>0.}
$$

Thus $f(H)\subseteq H$. Also

$$
f'(z)=\frac\Delta{(cz+d)^2}\ne0,
$$

so it is [conformal](../../../../../conformal-map.md). Cross-multiplication shows that $f(z_1)=f(z_2)$ implies $\Delta(z_1-z_2)=0$, proving [injectivity](../../../../../injective-function.md).

The inverse [Möbius transformation](../../../../../mobius-transformation.md) is

$$
f^{-1}(w)=\frac{dw-b}{-cw+a}.
$$

Its coefficients are real and its [determinant](../../../../../determinant.md) is again $\Delta>0$, so the same [imaginary part](../../../../../imaginary-part.md) identity shows that it maps $H$ into $H$. Therefore every $w\in H$ has a preimage in $H$, proving [surjectivity](../../../../../surjective-function.md). Hence $f$ maps $H$ one-to-one and onto itself.
