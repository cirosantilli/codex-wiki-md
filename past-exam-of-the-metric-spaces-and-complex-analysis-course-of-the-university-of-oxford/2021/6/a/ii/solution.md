<h1 id="6/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [ii](../ii.md)

Use the [univalent functions](../../../../../univalent-function.md) on $D$, namely the [univalent functions](../../../../../univalent-function.md) $F:D\to\mathbb C$. Each has nonzero [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md), so its [image](../../../../../image-of-a-function.md) $F(D)$ is a [domain](../../../../../domain-mathematical-analysis.md) [conformally equivalent](../../../../../conformal-equivalence.md) to $D$, and is therefore [simply connected](../../../../../simply-connected-space.md). This [image](../../../../../image-of-a-function.md) is proper: otherwise $F^{-1}:\mathbb C\to D$ would be a bounded nonconstant [entire function](../../../../../entire-function.md), contrary to the [Liouville theorem](../../../../../liouville-theorem.md).

Conversely, every proper [simply connected domain](../../../../../simply-connected-domain.md) $G\subset\mathbb C$ is $F(D)$ for some such $F$, by taking the inverse of a [Riemann mapping theorem](../../../../../riemann-mapping-theorem.md) bijection. Thus these [functions](../../../../../function-split.md) parameterize all proper [simply connected domains](../../../../../simply-connected-domain.md) by their images. The [complex plane](../../../../../complex-plane.md) itself is the exceptional [simply connected domain](../../../../../simply-connected-domain.md) and must be listed separately.

The parameterization has redundancy: two [univalent functions](../../../../../univalent-function.md) $F_1,F_2$ have the same [image](../../../../../image-of-a-function.md) if and only if $F_2=F_1\circ\phi$ for an [automorphism of the unit disk](../../../../../automorphism-of-the-unit-disk.md) $\phi$. All such [automorphisms](../../../../../automorphism.md) have the form

$$
\phi(z)=e^{i\theta}\frac{z-\alpha}{1-\overline\alpha z},\qquad |\alpha|<1,\quad\theta\in\mathbb R.
$$

If an interior point $w_0\in G$ is prescribed, the normalization $F(0)=w_0$ and $F'(0)>0$ real removes this redundancy. Equivalently one can describe these maps through their convergent [Taylor series](../../../../../taylor-series.md) on $D$, imposing [univalence](../../../../../univalent-function.md) on the resulting [holomorphic functions](../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md).
