↑ **Parent:** [i](../i.md)

The [Riemann mapping theorem](../../../../../riemann-mapping-theorem.md) gives a [conformal bijection](../../../../../biholomorphism.md) $G\to D$ exactly when

$$
\boxed{G\ne\mathbb C,}
$$

given the hypothesis that $G$ is a [simply connected domain](../../../../../simply-connected-domain.md). A [domain](../../../../../domain-mathematical-analysis.md) is nonempty, [open](../../../../../open-set.md), and [connected](../../../../../connected-space.md), so no extra nonemptiness qualification is needed.

For necessity, a [conformal bijection](../../../../../biholomorphism.md) $\mathbb C\to D$ would be a bounded nonconstant [entire function](../../../../../entire-function.md), contradicting the [Liouville theorem](../../../../../liouville-theorem.md). For sufficiency, $G$ is a proper [simply connected domain](../../../../../simply-connected-domain.md), exactly the hypothesis of the [Riemann mapping theorem](../../../../../riemann-mapping-theorem.md).
