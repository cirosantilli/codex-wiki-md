<h1 id="3/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [iv](../iv.md)

Let $F(z)=\overline{\sinh z}$. More generally, for a [holomorphic function](../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) $h$, the increment expansion at $z_0$ gives

$$
\frac{\overline{h(z_0+w)}-\overline{h(z_0)}}w=\overline{h'(z_0)}\frac{\overline w}w+o(1).
$$

Along real increments $w$ the first factor $\overline w/w$ is $1$, while along purely imaginary increments it is $-1$. Thus [complex differentiability](../../../../../complex-differentiability.md) of $\overline h$ requires $h'(z_0)=0$. Conversely, if $h'(z_0)=0$, the displayed expression tends to zero for arbitrary complex increments, so the [complex derivative](../../../../../complex-derivative.md) exists and equals zero.

For $h=\sinh$, this condition is $\cosh z_0=0$, equivalent to $e^{2z_0}=-1$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{F\text{ is complex differentiable exactly at }z_0=(k+\tfrac12)\pi i,\quad F'(z_0)=0.}
$$

These points form a [discrete subset](../../../../../discrete-subset.md) of the [complex plane](../../../../../complex-plane.md) and contain no nonempty [open set](../../../../../open-set.md). Every [neighbourhood](../../../../../neighbourhood-mathematics.md) of each one contains points where $F$ is not [complex differentiable](../../../../../complex-differentiability.md). Therefore $F$ is [holomorphic](../../../../../holomorphic-function-split.md) on no nonempty [open set](../../../../../open-set.md), even though it has [complex derivatives](../../../../../complex-derivative.md) at the specified points.
