# Kolmogorov second similarity hypothesis

↑ **Parent:** [Kolmogorov 1941 theory](kolmogorov-1941-theory.md)

In the [inertial range](inertial-range.md), [velocity increment](velocity-increment.md) statistics also become independent of [kinematic viscosity](kinematic-viscosity.md). Combined with the [Kolmogorov first similarity hypothesis](kolmogorov-first-similarity-hypothesis.md), this gives $S_p(r)=\beta_p(\epsilon r)^{p/3}$ by [dimensional analysis](dimensional-analysis.md). Signed integer [moments](moment.md) and absolute noninteger [moments](moment.md) must be distinguished; the signed third-order coefficient is negative in a forward [energy cascade](energy-cascade.md).
