# Squirmer

↑ **Parent:** [Microswimmer](microswimmer.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Squirmer)

A squirmer is an idealized spherical [microswimmer](microswimmer.md) propelled by a prescribed tangential [surface slip velocity](surface-slip-velocity.md). The [Lorentz reciprocal theorem for Stokes flow](lorentz-reciprocal-theorem-for-stokes-flow.md) determines its rigid translation and rotation without solving its complete exterior flow.
