# Nonsingular point of an algebraic curve

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic curve](algebraic-curve.md)

For an affine plane [algebraic curve](algebraic-curve.md) $F(x,y)=0$, a point over an [algebraic closure](algebraic-closure.md) is nonsingular when the displayed condition holds. Its [Zariski tangent space](zariski-tangent-space.md) has dimension one, and a local coordinate can be chosen in the direction of a nonzero [partial derivative](partial-derivative.md). A [smooth algebraic curve](smooth-algebraic-curve.md) has all points nonsingular. Over a complete [local field](local-field.md), the [Hensel lemma](hensel-s-lemma.md) lifts a nonsingular residue point by solving for a coordinate with unit [partial derivative](partial-derivative.md).
