# Almost-sure growth rate of independent products

↑ **Parent:** [Strong law of large numbers](strong-law-of-large-numbers.md)

If the positive factors $U_j$ are [independent and identically distributed](independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md) and $\mathbb E|\log U_1|<\infty$, the [strong law of large numbers](strong-law-of-large-numbers.md) applied to their [logarithms](logarithm.md) gives the displayed [almost sure convergence](almost-sure-convergence.md). A negative limit forces the products to tend to zero; a positive limit forces them to tend to infinity. A zero limit alone gives neither conclusion. For a [uniform distribution](continuous-uniform-distribution.md) on $(0,b)$, the logarithmic [expected value](expected-value-split.md) is $\log b-1$, so the products tend to zero [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md) for $b<e$ and to infinity [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md) for $b>e$.
