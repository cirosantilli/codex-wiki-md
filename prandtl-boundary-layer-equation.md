# Prandtl boundary-layer equation

↑ **Parent:** [Viscous boundary layer](viscous-boundary-layer.md)

For a steady two-dimensional incompressible boundary layer with tangential velocity $u(x,y)$, normal velocity $v(x,y)$, outer tangential velocity $U(x)$, and [kinematic viscosity](kinematic-viscosity.md) $\nu$,

$$
u u_x+v u_y=U\frac{dU}{dx}+\nu u_{yy},
\qquad
u_x+v_y=0.
$$

The pressure is uniform across the layer, and the outer Euler equation supplies $-p_x/\rho=U\,dU/dx$.
