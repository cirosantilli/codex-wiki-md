# Logarithm from a spectral slit in a Banach algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Holomorphic functional calculus](holomorphic-functional-calculus.md)

If a [compact](compact-space.md) [spectrum of an element](spectrum-of-an-element.md) avoids zero and zero belongs to its unbounded complementary component, choose a simple arc from zero to infinity avoiding the [Banach algebra spectrum](spectrum-of-an-element.md). Its complement is a [simply connected](simply-connected-space.md) neighborhood of the [Banach algebra spectrum](spectrum-of-an-element.md), admitting a [holomorphic logarithm](holomorphic-logarithm.md). Apply [holomorphic functional calculus](holomorphic-functional-calculus.md) to that logarithm and the [composition rule for holomorphic functional calculus](composition-rule-for-holomorphic-functional-calculus.md) to the exponential to obtain the displayed identity. An invertible [matrix](matrix.md) over the [complex numbers](complex-number.md) always satisfies the hypothesis because its finite [Banach algebra spectrum](spectrum-of-an-element.md) cannot separate zero from infinity.
