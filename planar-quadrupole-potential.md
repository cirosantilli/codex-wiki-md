# Planar quadrupole potential

↑ **Parent:** [Electric quadrupole](electric-quadrupole.md)

This [electric potential](electric-potential.md) is harmonic and independent of $z$. Its [electric field](electric-field-split.md) is linear in transverse position, with a saddle at the origin. For $A>0$, field lines approach along the $x$ axis and leave along the $y$ axis; off-axis field lines satisfy $xy=\mathrm{constant}$. Equipotentials satisfy $x^2-y^2=\mathrm{constant}$. This is a local planar [electric quadrupole](electric-quadrupole.md) geometry, not the decaying far field of an isolated three-dimensional quadrupole.
