# Zone-centre bands of a zinc-blende III-V semiconductor

↑ **Parent:** [III-V semiconductor](iii-v-semiconductor.md)

A common unstrained cubic III-V band ordering consists of an s-like $\Gamma_{6c}$ conduction doublet, a p-like $\Gamma_{8v}$ quartet and a lower spin-orbit split-off $\Gamma_{7v}$ doublet. In the cubic limit the quartet gives [heavy-hole and light-hole bands](heavy-hole-and-light-hole-bands.md) that meet at the [Gamma point](gamma-point.md); different curvatures and direction-dependent mixing distinguish them away from it. The [split-off valence band](split-off-valence-band.md) is separated by $\Delta_{\rm so}$. This common ordering is a model to check against the actual material, not a consequence of chemical stoichiometry alone.
