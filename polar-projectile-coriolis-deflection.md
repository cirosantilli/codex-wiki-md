# Polar projectile Coriolis deflection

↑ **Parent:** [Coriolis force](coriolis-force.md)

In a flat uniform-gravity model at the North pole, [Earth's rotation](earth-s-rotation.md) is vertical. To first order in angular [speed](speed.md), the horizontal component $u\cos\theta$ gives constant transverse [Coriolis acceleration](coriolis-acceleration.md) $-2\Omega u\cos\theta$ toward the right of the aiming direction. The return flight time is $2u\sin\theta/g$, proving the displayed miss distance. At the maximum unperturbed range, $\theta=45^\circ$, it becomes $\sqrt2\Omega u^3/g^2$. [curvature](curvature.md), varying gravity, air drag and second-order rotational terms are excluded.
