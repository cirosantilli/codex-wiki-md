# Gaussian even-moment pairing count

↑ **Parent:** [Isserlis's theorem](isserlis-s-theorem.md)

For a centered scalar [normal random variable](gaussian-random-variable.md) of [variance](variance-split.md) $v$, the [Isserlis theorem](isserlis-s-theorem.md) sums complete pairings of $2n$ factors. The first factor has $2n-1$ partners, then $2n-3$ choices remain for the first unpaired factor, giving $(2n-1)!!=(2n)!/(2^nn!)$. Odd moments vanish. Summing the [moment-generating function](moment-generating-function.md) gives $\exp(vs^2/2)$. This is a scalar formula, not a formula for the length of a vector.
