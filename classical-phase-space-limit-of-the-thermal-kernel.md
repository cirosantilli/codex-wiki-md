# Classical phase-space limit of the thermal kernel

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal trace as a closed Euclidean path integral](thermal-trace-as-a-closed-euclidean-path-integral.md)

For $H=p^2/(2m)+V(x)$ and a smooth potential on scales larger than the [thermal de Broglie wavelength](thermal-de-broglie-wavelength.md) $\lambda_T=\sqrt{2\pi\beta\hbar^2/m}$, the diagonal thermal kernel approaches $\langle x|e^{-\beta H}|x\rangle\simeq\lambda_T^{-d}e^{-\beta V(x)}$. Integration over its diagonal gives the classical [phase space](phase-space.md) integral with one factor $(2\pi\hbar)^{-1}$ per coordinate-momentum pair. Exchange and boundary corrections must be small; a positive imaginary-time weight by itself does not make an arbitrary quantum system classical.
