# Gaussian semivariogram

↑ **Parent:** [Semivariogram](semivariogram.md)

For $\phi>0$ and nonnegative [variances](variance-split.md), $\gamma(h)=\tau^2\mathbf1_{\{h\ne0\}}+\sigma^2(1-e^{-\phi^2\|h\|^2})$ admits [covariance](covariance.md) $C(h)=\sigma^2e^{-\phi^2\|h\|^2}+\tau^2\mathbf1_{\{h=0\}}$. The [Gaussian kernel](gaussian-kernel.md) is positive definite because it is the [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) of the density of a [Gaussian distribution](normal-distribution.md). Adding independent [white noise](white-noise.md) supplies the [nugget effect](nugget-effect.md). Its scale is $a=1/\phi$. For $\sigma^2>0$, its exact [range of a semivariogram](range-of-a-semivariogram.md) is infinite. If $\sigma^2=0$, it is a pure [nugget effect](nugget-effect.md) with no nontrivial structured range.
