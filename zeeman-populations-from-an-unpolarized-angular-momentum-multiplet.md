# Zeeman populations from an unpolarized angular-momentum multiplet

↑ **Parent:** [Zeeman splitting](zeeman-splitting.md)

An equal incoherent mixture within a fixed total-$J$ multiplet has [density operator](density-matrix-split.md) $\rho=P_J/(2J+1)$, where $P_J$ is its [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md). If the applied-field [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) is proportional to $L_z+2S_z$ and no [spin-orbit interaction](spin-orbit-interaction.md) is retained, its [energy eigenstates](energy-eigenstate.md) are the uncoupled states $|m_L,m_S\rangle$. The [Born rule](born-rule.md) gives their populations by the formula above, or equivalently by squared [Clebsch-Gordan decomposition](clebsch-gordan-decomposition.md) coefficients. Equal populations of coupled states need not give equal populations of magnetic [energy eigenvalues](energy-eigenvalue-split.md).

For $L=1,S=1/2,J=3/2$, the nonextremal coupled states have weights $2/3$ and $1/3$ in their two uncoupled components. The five dimensionless magnetic energies $-2,-1,0,1,2$ then have populations $1/4,1/6,1/6,1/6,1/4$; the zero energy contains two independent states, each with population $1/12$. In a different, weak-field approximation with a dominant [spin-orbit interaction](spin-orbit-interaction.md), projecting into the isolated multiplet gives the [Landé g-factor](lande-g-factor.md) spectrum instead. The full operator and its projection must not be interchanged.
