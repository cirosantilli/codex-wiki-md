# Solar wind

↑ **Parent:** [Solar system](solar-system.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_wind)

The solar wind is the outward plasma flow from the Sun, carrying an interplanetary [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md). Its pressure and field compress intrinsic planetary [magnetospheres](magnetosphere.md), form magnetotails, and can drive an [induced magnetosphere](induced-magnetosphere.md) around a planet with no appreciable intrinsic field.
