# Pointwise anchoring of a holomorphic normal family

↑ **Parent:** [Normal family](normal-family.md)

For a holomorphic [normal family](normal-family.md), limits are holomorphic or identically infinity. A uniform bound at one interior point excludes infinity on its [connected](connected-space.md) domain. Every sequence therefore has a finite locally uniform subsequential limit. The [Cauchy integral formula](cauchy-integral-formula.md) makes the [derivatives](derivative.md) converge locally uniformly too, so the [derivative](derivative.md) family is normal. Without the point bound this can fail: $n(1+z^2/2)$ tends uniformly to infinity on the unit disk, whereas its [derivatives](derivative.md) $nz$ are not normal at zero.
