# Protoplast

↑ **Parent:** [Cell wall](cell-wall.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protoplast)

A [protoplast](protoplast.md) is the living contents of a [biological cell](biological-cell.md) bounded by its [cell membrane](cell-membrane.md) after removal of the [cell wall](cell-wall.md). Removing the [cell wall](cell-wall.md) need not immediately destroy the [cell membrane](cell-membrane.md); stability depends on the preparation and surroundings. [Lysozyme](lysozyme.md) acts on susceptible bacterial [peptidoglycan](peptidoglycan.md), not on [cellulose](cellulose.md) or [chitin](chitin.md), so it is not a universal wall-removal [enzyme](enzyme.md).
