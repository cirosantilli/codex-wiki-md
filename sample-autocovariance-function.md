# Sample autocovariance function

↑ **Parent:** [Autocovariance](autocovariance.md)

For a known zero mean, $\widehat\gamma_n(h)=n^{-1}\sum_{t=1}^{n-h}Y_tY_{t+h}$ for $0\le h<n$. [Weakly stationary process](weakly-stationary-process.md) assumptions give [expectation](expected-value.md) $(1-h/n)\gamma(h)$. Dividing by $n-h$ instead gives an unbiased estimate at each individual lag, although the resulting collection need not form a positive semidefinite [covariance](covariance.md) sequence.
