# Canonical bundle of projective space

↑ **Parent:** [Twisting sheaf on projective space](twisting-sheaf-on-projective-space.md)

Over any field, the [canonical bundle](canonical-bundle.md) of [projective space](projective-space-split.md) is $\mathcal O(-n-1)$. Indeed, dualizing the [Euler sequence](euler-sequence.md) gives $0\to\Omega^1_{\mathbf P^n}\to\mathcal O(-1)^{\oplus(n+1)}\to\mathcal O\to0$; taking determinants gives the result. Hence [Serre duality](serre-duality.md) identifies $H^i(\mathbf P^n,\mathcal O(m))$ with the dual of $H^{n-i}(\mathbf P^n,\mathcal O(-m-n-1))$.
