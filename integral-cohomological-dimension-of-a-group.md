# Integral cohomological dimension of a group

↑ **Parent:** [Group cohomology](group-cohomology.md)

The integral cohomological dimension is the least length of a [projective resolution](projective-resolution.md) of the trivial [group ring](group-ring.md) module $\mathbb Z$, equivalently the largest degree in which [group cohomology](group-cohomology.md) with some module can be nonzero. A group containing torsion has infinite integral cohomological dimension. For $\mathbb Z^r$ the dimension is $r$, realized by its [torus](torus.md) classifying space and detected by its top cohomology.
