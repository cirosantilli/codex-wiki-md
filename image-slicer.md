# Image slicer

↑ **Parent:** [Integral field spectrograph](integral-field-spectrograph.md)

An image slicer divides a two-dimensional image into narrow strips and optically rearranges them into a long pseudo-slit. A [spectrograph](spectrograph.md) then disperses each strip while preserving its position along the slit.
