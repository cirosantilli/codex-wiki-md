# Quasilinear diffusion

↑ **Parent:** [Quasilinear approximation](quasilinear-approximation.md)

The diffusive evolution of an averaged [phase-space distribution function](phase-space-distribution-function.md) caused by weak fluctuating fields in the [quasilinear approximation](quasilinear-approximation.md). In a stationary homogeneous one-dimensional electrostatic problem, if the potential's temporal [power spectral density](power-spectral-density.md) is $S_{\varphi,k}(\omega)$ with covariance $\int S_{\varphi,k}(\omega)e^{-i\omega\tau}d\omega/(2\pi)$, then

$$
D(v)=\frac{e^2}{2m_e^2}\sum_k k^2S_{\varphi,k}(kv).
$$

This follows from the real part of the one-sided time correlation along an unperturbed particle trajectory. Both signs of the spatial [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) are included. The action-space counterpart for regular [stellar orbits](stellar-orbit.md) is [quasilinear diffusion in action space](quasilinear-diffusion-in-action-space.md).
