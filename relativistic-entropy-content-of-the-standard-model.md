# Relativistic entropy content of the Standard Model

↑ **Parent:** [Effective number of relativistic degrees of freedom](effective-number-of-relativistic-degrees-of-freedom.md)

In the ideal-gas high-temperature limit, the [Standard Model](standard-model-split.md) has 28 [boson](boson.md) states: 16 [gluon](gluon.md) states, eight electroweak [gauge boson](gauge-boson.md) states and four [Higgs field](higgs-field.md) states. Its 90 [fermion](fermion.md) states are 72 [quark](quark.md) states, 12 charged [lepton](lepton.md) states and six [neutrino](neutrino.md) states. Counting a relativistic [fermion](fermion.md) state with weight $7/8$ gives the displayed [effective number of relativistic entropy degrees of freedom](effective-number-of-relativistic-degrees-of-freedom.md). The [effective number of relativistic energy degrees of freedom](effective-number-of-relativistic-energy-degrees-of-freedom.md) has the same value when all species share a temperature. Mass thresholds and interactions change the ideal-gas count.
