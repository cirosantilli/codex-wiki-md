# Local isotropy of turbulence

↑ **Parent:** [Isotropic turbulence](isotropic-turbulence.md)

Small-scale joint [velocity increment](velocity-increment.md) statistics become approximately invariant under [rotations](rotation-mathematics.md) of the observation configuration, even when the energy-containing flow is anisotropic. This is a scale-restricted hypothesis; it does not make the entire [velocity field](velocity-field.md) isotropic or remove [internal intermittency](internal-intermittency.md).
