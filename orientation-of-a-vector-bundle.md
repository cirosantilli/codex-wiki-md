# Orientation of a vector bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Vector bundle](vector-bundle.md)

This is a fiberwise [orientation](orientation-of-a-simplex.md) compatible with local trivializations of a [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md). An $R$-orientation of a rank-$d$ vector bundle is a coherent choice of generator of $H^d(E_b,E_b\setminus\{0\};R)$ for every fiber, equivalently a [Thom class](thom-class.md) with the corresponding fiberwise restriction.
