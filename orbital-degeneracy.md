# Orbital degeneracy

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular orbital](molecular-orbital.md)

[Orbital degeneracy](orbital-degeneracy.md) means distinct spatial orbitals have equal energy within the specified model or symmetry. Two [electrons](electron.md) in degenerate orbitals need not pair; exchange can favor a [triplet state](spin-one-half-triplet-state.md).
