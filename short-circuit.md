# Short circuit

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical circuit](electrical-circuit-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Short_circuit)

An ideal [short circuit](short-circuit.md) fixes zero [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) across a terminal pair, corresponding to zero load [electrical impedance](electrical-impedance.md). At the end of a lossless [transmission line](transmission-line.md), its voltage-wave [reflection coefficient](reflection-coefficient.md) is $-1$ and the terminal [voltage](voltage.md) is zero. The available current is still limited by source and circuit impedances; real short circuits can dissipate substantial [Joule heating](joule-heating.md).
