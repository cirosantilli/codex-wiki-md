# Functional completeness

↑ **Parent:** [Boolean function](boolean-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Functional_completeness)

A [set](set-split.md) of [Boolean functions](boolean-function.md) is functionally complete if [function composition](function-composition.md) using those operations can express every positive-arity [Boolean function](boolean-function.md). The [NAND gate](nand-gate.md) alone suffices: tie its inputs to obtain [negation](negation.md), negate its output to obtain [logical conjunction](logical-conjunction.md), and apply [De Morgan's laws](de-morgan-s-laws.md) to obtain [logical disjunction](logical-disjunction.md); then use [disjunctive normal form](disjunctive-normal-form.md).
