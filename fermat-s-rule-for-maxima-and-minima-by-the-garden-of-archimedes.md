<h1 id="fermat-s-rule-for-maxima-and-minima-by-the-garden-of-archimedes">Fermat's rule for maxima and minima by the Garden of Archimedes</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematics bibliography](mathematics-bibliography-split.md)

University of Florence mathematical-museum exhibition, Analytical geometry and the problem of tangents. It gives a translation and reconstruction of [Pierre de Fermat](pierre-de-fermat.md)'s [parabola](parabola.md) example, using the letters B, C, D, E, I, O of the exam image. The text distinguishes circulation in the 1630s from the 1679 publication. Its final displayed label contains a typographical anomaly; the derivation and geometry give $CE=2CD$.

Online source: [https://archimede.dimai.unifi.it/archimede/archimede_NEW_inglese/mostra_calcolo/guida/node7.html](https://archimede.dimai.unifi.it/archimede/archimede_NEW_inglese/mostra_calcolo/guida/node7.html)
