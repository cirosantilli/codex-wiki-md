# Total degree of a polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Degree of a polynomial](degree-of-a-polynomial.md)

The total degree of a multivariate [monomial](monomial.md) $x_1^{a_1}\cdots x_n^{a_n}$ is $a_1+\cdots+a_n$. The total degree of a nonzero [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) is the largest total degree of one of its monomials with nonzero coefficient.
