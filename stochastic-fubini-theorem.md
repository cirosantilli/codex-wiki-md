# Stochastic Fubini theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Stochastic calculus](stochastic-calculus-split.md)

A theorem interchanging a parameter integral with an [Itô integral](ito-integral.md), under appropriate measurability and integrability hypotheses. For a bounded deterministic integrand on a finite parameter-time rectangle, the square-integrability conditions hold; triangular domains can be handled by an indicator of the domain.
