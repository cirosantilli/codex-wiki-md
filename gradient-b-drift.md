# Gradient-B drift

↑ **Parent:** [Drift kinetics](drift-kinetics.md)

The transverse [guiding center](guiding-center.md) drift produced by a [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) strength [gradient](gradient.md), with [first adiabatic invariant](first-adiabatic-invariant.md) $\mu=v_\perp^2/(2B)$ and signed [cyclotron frequency](cyclotron-frequency.md) $\Omega_s$. Together with [magnetic curvature drift](magnetic-curvature-drift.md), it produces velocity-dependent transverse transport.
