# Convective contraction with power-law opacity

↑ **Parent:** [Hayashi track](hayashi-track.md)

For a monatomic fully convective [ideal gas](ideal-gas.md) star, uniform [specific entropy](specific-entropy.md) gives $P=KT^{5/2}$ with $K\propto\mu^{-5/2}M^{-1/2}R^{-3/2}$. Combining this with the [stellar surface boundary condition](stellar-surface-boundary-condition.md) $\kappa P=2g/3$ and $\kappa=\kappa_0\rho^aT^b$ gives

$$
T_e^{(3a+5)/2+b}\propto
\mu^{(3a+5)/2}M^{(a+3)/2}R^{(3a-1)/2}.
$$

All composition comparisons here hold $\kappa_0$ fixed. For $a=1,b=4$, $T_e^8\propto\mu^4M^2R$, and the [Stefan–Boltzmann law](stefan-boltzmann-law.md) gives $L\propto\mu^2MR^{5/2}$. The [stellar virial theorem](stellar-virial-theorem.md) and [Kelvin-Helmholtz contraction](kelvin-helmholtz-mechanism.md) then give $R^{-7/2}-R_0^{-7/2}\propto\mu^2M^{-1}(t-t_0)$, with a positive coefficient. Once the initial-radius term is negligible, $R\propto\mu^{-4/7}M^{2/7}(t-t_0)^{-2/7}$.
