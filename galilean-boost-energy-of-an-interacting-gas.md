# Galilean boost energy of an interacting gas

↑ **Parent:** [Galilean transformation](galilean-transformation.md)

For a resting uniform continuum gas with pair interactions depending on relative positions, shifting every particle [momentum](momentum-split.md) by $\mathbf p_0$ leaves interaction [energy](energy-split.md) unchanged and changes the microscopic [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) by $\mathbf p_0\cdot\mathbf P/m+Np_0^2/(2m)$. The resting [ground state](ground-state.md) has total [momentum](momentum-split.md) $\mathbf P=0$. The resulting boost [energy](energy-split.md) uses the bare [mass](mass.md) even in an interacting [Fermi liquid](fermi-liquid-split.md); matching this with the [Landau quasiparticle energy functional](landau-quasiparticle-energy-functional.md) constrains its interaction kernel.
