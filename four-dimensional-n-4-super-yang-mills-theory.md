<h1 id="four-dimensional-n-4-super-yang-mills-theory">Four-dimensional N=4 super Yang-Mills theory</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Supersymmetric gauge theory](supersymmetric-gauge-theory.md)

A four-dimensional $\mathcal N=4$ [supersymmetric gauge theory](supersymmetric-gauge-theory.md) has one [supersymmetric vector multiplet](supersymmetric-vector-multiplet.md) containing a [gauge boson](gauge-boson.md), four [Weyl spinors](weyl-spinor.md), and six [real scalar fields](real-scalar-field.md) in the adjoint [group representation](group-representation.md). Its massless [helicity](helicity.md) multiplicities are $1,4,6,4,1$ from [helicity](helicity.md) $1$ to $-1$.
