# Unitary transitions of the tautological line over the projective line

↑ **Parent:** [Complex tautological line bundle](complex-tautological-line-bundle.md)

For $\zeta=z_2/z_1$ and $\eta=1/\zeta$, the tautological frames $(1,\zeta)$ and $(\eta,1)$ differ by $1/\zeta$. Normalize their ambient [Hermitian form](hermitian-form.md) norms; the resulting frames differ by the displayed phase. The unit circle total space is $S^3\subset\mathbb C^2$ and its projection to the [complex projective line](complex-projective-line.md) is the [Hopf fibration](hopf-fibration.md). The transition of the dual line is the reciprocal phase.
