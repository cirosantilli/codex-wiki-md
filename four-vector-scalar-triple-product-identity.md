# Four-vector scalar triple product identity

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar triple product](scalar-triple-product.md)

For four [vectors](vector.md) in three-dimensional real [Euclidean space](euclidean-norm.md), write $[a,b,c]=a\cdot(b\times c)$. Expand the same [cross product](cross-product.md) in two ways using the [vector triple product identity](vector-triple-product.md):

$$
(a\times b)\times(c\times d)
=[a,b,d]c-[a,b,c]d
=[a,c,d]b-[b,c,d]a.
$$

Rearrangement gives the displayed identity. It expresses [linear dependence](linear-dependence.md) of four [vectors](vector.md) in three [dimensions](dimension-vector-space.md) with explicit [determinant](determinant.md) coefficients and remains valid when some or all of those coefficients vanish.
