# Punctured plane is not conformally equivalent to the punctured unit disc

↑ **Parent:** [Conformal equivalence](conformal-equivalence.md)

A conformal map from $\mathbb C^*$ into the punctured unit disc would be bounded near its isolated singularity at zero, hence extend to a bounded entire function. The [Liouville theorem](liouville-theorem.md) would make it constant.
