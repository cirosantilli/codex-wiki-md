# Heralded Bell-pair projection with two polarization parity filters

↑ **Parent:** [Entanglement swapping](entanglement-swapping.md)

Start from three normalized [Bell states](bell-state-split.md) $\Phi^+_{12}\Phi^+_{34}\Phi^+_{56}$. A [polarizing beam splitter](polarizing-beam-splitter.md) parity filter $\Pi_Z^{25}$ followed by $X$-basis outcome $++$, and a second filter $\Pi_X^{46}$ followed by $Z$-basis outcome $00$, have effective bras $\langle\Phi^+|_{25}/\sqrt2$ and $\langle\Phi^+|_{46}/\sqrt2$. Their contraction leaves $\Phi^+_{13}/8$. The herald [probability](probability.md) is therefore $1/64$ per ideal three-pair input, or $1/16$ conditional on both parity-routing filters having succeeded. This distinction prevents renormalizing away the optical [postselection](postselection.md) cost.
