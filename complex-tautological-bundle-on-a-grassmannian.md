# Complex tautological bundle on a Grassmannian

↑ **Parent:** [Tautological bundle](tautological-bundle.md)

Over $\operatorname{Gr}_k(\mathbb C^N)$ this rank-k [complex vector bundle](complex-vector-bundle.md) has fibre W at the point W. In a chart of planes that are graphs of maps from a fixed k-plane, $(A,w)\mapsto(\operatorname{graph}A,w+Aw)$ gives a [local trivialization](local-trivialization.md). The direct-limit bundle over the infinite [Grassmannian](grassmannian.md) classifies rank-k bundles over compact Hausdorff spaces.
