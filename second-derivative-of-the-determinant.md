# Second derivative of the determinant

↑ **Parent:** [Derivative of the determinant](derivative-of-the-determinant.md)

The [derivative of the determinant](derivative-of-the-determinant.md) at an invertible [matrix](matrix.md) is $D\det_A(K)=\det A\operatorname{tr}(A^{-1}K)$. At $A=I+H$, use $\det(I+H)=1+\operatorname{tr}H+O(\|H\|^2)$ and $(I+H)^{-1}=I-H+O(\|H\|^2)$. Differentiating the first derivative gives the displayed symmetric [bilinear map](bilinear-map.md). More generally $D^2\det_A(H,K)=\det A[\operatorname{tr}(A^{-1}H)\operatorname{tr}(A^{-1}K)-\operatorname{tr}(A^{-1}HA^{-1}K)]$.
