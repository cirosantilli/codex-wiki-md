<h1 id="henon-heiles-system">Hénon-Heiles system</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Hamiltonian system](hamiltonian-system.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hénon–Heiles_system)

The Hénon-Heiles system is a [Hamiltonian system](hamiltonian-system.md) with two [degrees of freedom](degree-of-freedom.md) and [Hamiltonian function](hamiltonian-function.md)

$$
H=\frac12(p_x^2+p_y^2)+V(x,y),\qquad V(x,y)=\frac12(x^2+y^2)+x^2y-\frac13y^3.
$$

Its [Hamilton's equations](hamilton-s-equations.md) give $\ddot x=-x-2xy$ and $\ddot y=-y-x^2+y^2$. Its [potential energy](potential-energy.md) combines an isotropic quadratic minimum with a cubic interaction.
