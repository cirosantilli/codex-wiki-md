# Transvection conjugacy over a finite field

↑ **Parent:** [Transvection](transvection.md)

Every nonidentity [transvection](transvection.md) has an adapted basis in which it is $I+E_{12}$. Thus all such maps are conjugate in the [general linear group](general-linear-group.md). In dimension at least three, a scaling of a third basis vector centralizes this matrix and has arbitrary nonzero determinant; adjusting a conjugator by this scaling proves conjugacy in the [special linear group](special-linear-group.md) as well.
