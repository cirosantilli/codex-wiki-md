# GSO projection

↑ **Parent:** [Spinning string](spinning-string.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GSO_projection)

The Gliozzi–Scherk–Olive projection selects consistent worldsheet fermion-number and chirality sectors in an RNS string theory. In suitable supersymmetric choices it removes the tachyonic [Neveu–Schwarz sector](neveu-schwarz-sector.md) ground state and keeps a chosen [Ramond sector](ramond-sector.md) chirality. Together with the consistent sector combinations it produces spacetime [fermions](fermion.md) and, for theories such as [Type II superstring theory](type-ii-string-theory.md), a tachyon-free spectrum. The mere presence of classical [worldsheet supersymmetry](worldsheet-supersymmetry.md) does not specify this projection or guarantee tachyon removal.
