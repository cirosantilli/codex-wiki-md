# Minimum magnetic-equilibrium spin period

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic spin equilibrium](magnetic-spin-equilibrium.md)

With [magnetic spin equilibrium](magnetic-spin-equilibrium.md) at the [magnetospheric truncation radius](magnetospheric-truncation-radius.md), $T\propto R_M^{3/2}M^{-1/2}\propto B_*^{6/7}R_*^{18/7}\dot M^{-3/7}$ at fixed [mass](mass.md). Inserting the [surface accretion Eddington rate](surface-accretion-eddington-rate.md) changes the radius power to $15/7$. The relation assumes the boundary remains outside the star; very weak fields instead encounter surface-accretion and [mass-shedding limits](mass-shedding-limit.md).
