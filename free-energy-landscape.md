# Free-energy landscape

↑ **Parent:** [Energy landscape](energy-landscape.md)

At fixed environmental constraints, integrate out unobserved coordinates to obtain an equilibrium probability density for a chosen reaction coordinate $x$. Relative to a specified coordinate measure, the displayed [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md) reproduces that density. A change of coordinate can add a Jacobian term, so the profile is not independent of its coordinate convention. Minima identify probable basins, while barriers relative to an initial basin contribute to activated kinetics; a kinetic prefactor still requires dynamics. A conjugate [force](force-split.md) tilts the profile by $-Fx$.
