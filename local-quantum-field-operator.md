# Local quantum field operator

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum field theory](quantum-field-theory-split.md)

A local quantum field operator is supported at a spacetime point, with operator-valued distributions understood by smearing. Products at the same point generally require [renormalization](renormalization.md); these give [composite operators](composite-operator.md). This is distinct from the topos-theoretic meaning of [local operator](lawvere-tierney-topology.md).
