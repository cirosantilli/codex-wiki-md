# Centre-of-momentum energy

↑ **Parent:** [Center-of-momentum frame](center-of-momentum-frame.md)

The centre-of-momentum energy is the total energy measured in the [centre-of-momentum frame](center-of-momentum-frame.md). For a scattering process with total four-momentum $P$, it is the Lorentz-invariant quantity $\sqrt{P^2}=\sqrt s$ in the mostly-minus convention.
