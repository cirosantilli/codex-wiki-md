# Fermi liquid

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical physics](statistical-physics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fermi_liquid)

Why can a strongly interacting liquid resemble a gas? Its low-energy excitations can be long-lived [Landau quasiparticles](landau-quasiparticle.md) near a [Fermi surface](fermi-surface.md). Normal liquid [helium-3](helium-3.md) motivates this [Fermi liquid](fermi-liquid-split.md) description: interactions survive in effective excitation [energies](energy-split.md) and interactions.[p.920 and section 1](the-theory-of-a-fermi-liquid-by-lev-davidovich-landau.md)

Interacting one-dimensional [fermion](fermion.md) systems often form [Luttinger liquids](luttinger-liquid.md); [non-Fermi liquids](non-fermi-liquid.md) also occur in higher dimensions. A [Cooper instability](cooper-instability.md) can produce a paired phase.[Sections IV E, VI D and XI](renormalization-group-approach-to-interacting-fermions-by-ramamurti-shankar.md)

The [Fermi-surface renormalization-group scaling](fermi-surface-renormalization-group-scaling.md) below organizes the low-energy interactions; [zero sound](zero-sound.md) tests whether their collective dynamics survives even when collisions are rare.
