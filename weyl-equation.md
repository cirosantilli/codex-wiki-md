# Weyl equation

↑ **Parent:** [Weyl spinor](weyl-spinor.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weyl_equation)

The free [massless](massless-particle.md) relativistic field equation for one chiral two-component [spinor](spinor.md). With mostly-minus metric, the left-chiral equation has the minus sign and the right-chiral equation the plus sign. The positive-energy [particle](particle-physics.md) [helicities](helicity.md) are respectively $-1/2$ and $+1/2$; antiparticle [helicities](helicity.md) are opposite.
