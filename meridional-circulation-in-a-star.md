# Meridional circulation in a star

↑ **Parent:** [Uniformly rotating stellar hydrostatic equilibrium](uniformly-rotating-stellar-hydrostatic-equilibrium.md)

Slow circulation in planes containing the rotation axis can transport entropy through a rotating star while the leading mechanical balance remains nearly [hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md). Its steady mass flux satisfies the [continuity equation](continuity-equation.md). It is distinct from assuming an exactly motionless radiative interior, and from turbulent [convection](convection.md).
