# Semilocally simply connected space

↑ **Parent:** [Covering space](covering-space.md)

A [topological space](topological-space.md) is semilocally simply connected if every point has an open neighbourhood whose loops based at that point become [null-homotopic](null-homotopic-map.md) in the whole space. The neighbourhood itself need not be [simply connected](simply-connected-space.md). Together with [path connectedness](path-connected-space.md) and [local path connectedness](locally-path-connected-space.md), this condition gives the classical existence and classification of connected [covering spaces](covering-space.md) by [subgroups](subgroup.md) of the [fundamental group](fundamental-group.md).
