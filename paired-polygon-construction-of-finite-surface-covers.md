# Paired-polygon construction of finite surface covers

↑ **Parent:** [Orbifold fundamental group](orbifold-fundamental-group.md)

Pair the $2s$ edges of an oriented [hyperbolic polygon](hyperbolic-polygon.md) by length-preserving identifications reversing their [boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md) directions. Label the pairs by generating elements $A_1,\ldots,A_s$ of a finite [group](group-split.md) $T$. Glue the corresponding edge of a copy labelled $g$ to its mate in the copy labelled $gA_k$. Left multiplication of labels defines the $T$ action. A [vertex cycle of a paired polygon](vertex-cycle-of-a-paired-polygon.md) has angle sum $\theta_j$ and accumulated gluing word $C_j\in T$ of order $q_j$; above it lie $|T|/q_j$ vertices, each of [cone angle](cone-angle.md) $q_j\theta_j$. The action is free away from vertices, and the vertex stabilizers are conjugates of $\langle C_j\rangle$. If the base cone has integer order $m_j=2\pi/\theta_j$ and $q_j\mid m_j$, this is an [orbifold](orbifold.md) covering, with lifted cone order $m_j/q_j$. The lift is smooth exactly when $q_j=m_j$.
