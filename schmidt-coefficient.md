# Schmidt coefficient

↑ **Parent:** [Schmidt decomposition](schmidt-decomposition.md)

The Schmidt coefficients of a normalized bipartite pure state are the nonnegative numbers $s_j$ in its [Schmidt decomposition](schmidt-decomposition.md); they satisfy $\sum_js_j^2=1$ and their squares are the nonzero eigenvalues of either [reduced density matrix](reduced-density-matrix.md).
