# Correlation-volume derivation of the scalar Ginzburg ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Ginzburg criterion](ginzburg-criterion.md)

For the dimensionless scalar [Landau-Ginzburg theory](landau-ginzburg-theory.md) with stiffness $c$, quadratic coefficient $r<0$ and quartic term $u\phi^4/4$, the [mean-field approximation](mean-field-approximation.md) gives $\phi_0^2=|r|/u$ and [correlation length](correlation-length.md) $\xi\asymp(c/|r|)^{1/2}$. Restricting the Gaussian [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) covariance $(cq^2+2|r|)^{-1}$ to $|q|\lesssim\xi^{-1}$ estimates the variance of the field averaged over a [correlation volume](correlation-volume.md) as $c^{-d/2}|r|^{(d-2)/2}$, up to a dimension-dependent constant. Dividing by $\phi_0^2$ proves the displayed [Ginzburg criterion](ginzburg-criterion.md) ratio. It diverges below four dimensions, vanishes above four and is marginal at four. Short-distance fluctuations renormalize microscopic coefficients and should not be confused with this long-distance critical test.
