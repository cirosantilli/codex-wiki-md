# Anxiety disorder

↑ **Parent:** [Mental disorder](mental-disorder.md)

A diagnostic grouping characterized by persistent or excessive fear or [anxiety](anxiety.md) and associated impairment. Specific disorders differ in their feared outcomes and behavioral patterns, so a shared maintaining process is a hypothesis to assess rather than a universal explanation.
