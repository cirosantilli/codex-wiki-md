# Group algebra of a p-group in characteristic p is local

↑ **Parent:** [Modular representation theory](modular-representation-theory.md)

If $G$ is a finite $p$-group and $k$ has characteristic $p$, then the [augmentation ideal](augmentation-ideal.md) is the [Jacobson radical](jacobson-radical.md) of $kG$ and $kG/J(kG)\cong k$. Thus $kG$ is a [local ring](local-ring.md), has only the trivial simple module, and its regular module is indecomposable.
