# Twisted boundary condition

↑ **Parent:** [Periodic boundary conditions](periodic-boundary-conditions.md)

A twisted [boundary condition](boundary-condition.md) identifies the ends of an interval up to a fixed unit-modulus [complex number](complex-number.md). For the kinetic [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) $-\hbar^2\partial_x^2/(2m)$, one also imposes $\psi'(x+L)=e^{i\theta}\psi'(x)$. In [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md), the endpoint terms then cancel, giving the usual [self-adjoint](self-adjoint-operator.md) ring realization. The allowed [plane waves](plane-wave.md) have momenta $\hbar(2\pi n+\theta)/L$.

A constant [magnetic vector potential](magnetic-vector-potential.md) can be removed from $(p-a)^2/(2m)$ locally by $\widetilde\psi=e^{-iax/\hbar}\psi$. If $\psi$ is periodic, $\widetilde\psi$ has twist $\theta=-aL/\hbar$. Thus the [Aharonov-Bohm effect](aharonov-bohm-effect.md) survives a locally vanishing vector potential as a global condition on the [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md).
