# Wigner rotation

↑ **Parent:** [Lorentz boost](lorentz-boost.md)

Two noncollinear [Lorentz boosts](lorentz-boost.md) produce a [Lorentz transformation](lorentz-transformation.md) with both a boost and a rotation. In the [SL(2,C)](complex-special-linear-group-in-dimension-two.md) lift, the product of two positive Hermitian boost [matrices](matrix.md) generally ceases to be Hermitian. Its [polar decomposition of an invertible complex matrix](polar-decomposition-of-an-invertible-complex-matrix.md) separates a positive Hermitian boost factor and a unitary rotation factor. The rotation depends on both boosts and their order, and is called the Wigner rotation.
