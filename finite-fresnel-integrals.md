# Finite Fresnel integrals

↑ **Parent:** [Fresnel integral](fresnel-integral.md)

In the unscaled convention, the finite [Fresnel integrals](fresnel-integral.md) are $C(\lambda)=\int_0^\lambda\cos(t^2)dt$ and $S(\lambda)=\int_0^\lambda\sin(t^2)dt$. Their [Taylor series](taylor-series.md) follow by termwise integration. At infinity both tend to $\sqrt{\pi/8}$, and their oscillatory tails have [asymptotic expansions](asymptotic-expansion.md) obtained by [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md). This convention differs from the convention with phase $\pi t^2/2$.
