# Rough-data damping distinction for the theta method

↑ **Parent:** [Theta diffusion stability criterion](theta-diffusion-stability-criterion.md)

At $a=1/2$, arbitrarily stiff heat modes approach amplification $-1$: the [Crank-Nicolson method](crank-nicolson-method.md) is contractive but does not rapidly damp unresolved rough components. The [Backward Euler method](backward-euler-method.md) instead has limit zero. Thus unconditional linear stability and strong stiff decay are different requirements. High smooth-data consistency order alone does not imply a uniform high-order estimate down to the initial instant for arbitrary [L2 initial conditions](l2-initial-condition.md).
