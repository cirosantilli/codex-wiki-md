# Periplasm

↑ **Parent:** [Bacterial outer membrane](bacterial-outer-membrane.md)

The [periplasm](periplasm.md) is the compartment between the cytoplasmic and outer membranes in a typical Gram-negative bacterial envelope. Its components and the outer membrane can gate access of external electron acceptors.
