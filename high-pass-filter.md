# High-pass filter

↑ **Parent:** [Signal processing](signal-processing.md)

A [high-pass filter](high-pass-filter.md) attenuates low [frequencies](frequency.md) relative to a specified higher-frequency pass-band. A simple first-order RC high-pass has the displayed [transfer function](transfer-function.md), with a zero at zero [frequency](frequency.md) and a [pole](pole.md) at $s=-1/\tau$. Combining a high-pass with a low-pass makes a band-pass, but its attainable selectivity depends on [pole](pole.md) structure rather than gain alone.
