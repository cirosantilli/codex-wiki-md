# Limit cardinal

↑ **Parent:** [Cardinal number](cardinal-number.md)

An infinite [cardinal number](cardinal-number.md) is a limit cardinal if it is not a [successor cardinal](successor-cardinal.md); equivalently it is an [aleph number](aleph-number.md) with zero or a [limit ordinal](limit-ordinal.md) as index. Uncountable limit cardinals are either [singular cardinals](singular-cardinal.md) or [weakly inaccessible cardinals](weakly-inaccessible-cardinal.md). This concerns succession among cardinals, rather than merely being a [limit ordinal](limit-ordinal.md).
