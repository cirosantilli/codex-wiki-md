# Ion temperature gradient

↑ **Parent:** [Temperature gradient](temperature-gradient.md)

Spatial variation of the [ion](ion.md) [temperature](temperature-split.md). The inverse logarithmic scale in the title is positive for an outward-decreasing profile. A nonzero value is a possible [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md) source; it does not by itself establish [ion-temperature-gradient instability](ion-temperature-gradient-instability.md) without the appropriate dynamics and threshold.
