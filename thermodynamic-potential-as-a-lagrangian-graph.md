# Thermodynamic potential as a Lagrangian graph

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrangian submanifold](lagrangian-submanifold.md)

For a smooth function $M(q)$, its differential defines a [Lagrangian submanifold](lagrangian-submanifold.md) of the [cotangent bundle](cotangent-bundle.md): pullback of the [canonical one-form on a cotangent bundle](canonical-one-form-on-a-cotangent-bundle.md) is $dM$, so pullback of the [symplectic form](symplectic-form.md) $-d\theta$ is zero. The graph has half the ambient dimension. With $q=(S,V,N)$ and momentum coordinates $(T,-P,\mu)$, the [first law of thermodynamics](first-law-of-thermodynamics.md) identifies the equilibrium graph with $dM$. This geometric statement does not require extensivity or thermodynamic stability.
