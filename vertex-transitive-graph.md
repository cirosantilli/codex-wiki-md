# Vertex-transitive graph

↑ **Parent:** [Graph](graph-split.md)

A [graph](graph-split.md) whose automorphisms act transitively on its vertices: any chosen [vertex of a graph](vertex-graph-theory.md) can be carried to any other by a symmetry preserving adjacency. Consequently rooted [self-avoiding walk](self-avoiding-walk.md) counts are independent of the chosen root.
