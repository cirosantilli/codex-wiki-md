# Hall mobility

↑ **Parent:** [Charge carrier mobility](charge-carrier-mobility.md)

A transport quantity obtained by combining longitudinal [electrical conductivity](electrical-conductivity.md) and a [Hall coefficient](hall-coefficient.md). It equals single-carrier drift mobility only under the corresponding one-carrier and scattering assumptions. This does not independently determine [effective mass](band-effective-mass.md) and scattering time.
