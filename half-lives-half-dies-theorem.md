# Half-lives-half-dies theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Intersection pairing](intersection-pairing.md)

For a compact [three-manifold](3-manifold.md) $W$, let $L=\ker(H_1(\partial W;\mathbb F_2)\to H_1(W;\mathbb F_2))$. The boundary [intersection pairing](intersection-pairing.md) satisfies $L^\perp=L$: the [long exact sequence in relative homology](long-exact-sequence-in-relative-homology.md) identifies $L$ with boundaries of relative surfaces, and the boundary-interior adjoint identity identifies its orthogonal complement with the same kernel. Hence $2\dim L=\dim H_1(\partial W;\mathbb F_2)$. This also implies that a closed surface with odd first mod-two [Betti number](betti-number.md), such as $\mathbb{RP}^2$, cannot be the entire boundary of a compact [three-manifold](3-manifold.md).
