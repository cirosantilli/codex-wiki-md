# Polynomial reduction of a slashed vector

↑ **Parent:** [Feynman slash notation](feynman-slash-notation.md)

The [Clifford algebra](clifford-algebra.md) gives $(\not p)^2=p^2I$. Splitting a scalar-coefficient [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) into even and odd powers proves the displayed reduction. For an on-shell [fermion spin sum](fermion-spin-sum.md) assumed to be such a [polynomial](polynomial-split.md), $(\not p-m)P=0$ implies $P=A(\not p+m)$; the normalization $\operatorname{tr}(P\gamma^0)=4p^0$ fixes $A=1$ in four spacetime dimensions.
