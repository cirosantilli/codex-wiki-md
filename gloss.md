# Gloss

↑ **Parent:** [Material perception](material-perception.md)

A surface appearance associated with directional [optical reflection](optical-reflection.md) and its image structure. Highlights, their relation to diffuse shading and their behavior across views can contribute; bright pixels alone are not a unique physical gloss measure.
