# Charge reversal

↑ **Parent:** [Electric double layer](electric-double-layer.md)

Overcompensation of an initial interfacial charge by adsorbed species or correlated [ions](ion.md), so the charge or potential inferred outside the compact interfacial region has the opposite sign. Screening of a fixed boundary charge alone is not the same mechanism.
