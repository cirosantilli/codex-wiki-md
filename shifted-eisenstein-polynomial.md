# Shifted Eisenstein polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Eisenstein polynomial](eisenstein-polynomial.md)

A [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) may fail the [Eisenstein polynomial](eisenstein-polynomial.md) test while a translate $f(T+a)$ passes it. Translation does not change the generated extension: if $f(\alpha)=0$, then $f(T+a)$ vanishes at $\alpha-a$. For $f(T)=T^3-m$, the translate by $m$ is Eisenstein at $3$ whenever $3\nmid m$ and $m\not\equiv\pm1\pmod9$.
