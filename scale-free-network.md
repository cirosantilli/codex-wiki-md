# Scale-free network

↑ **Parent:** [Graph theory](graph-theory-split.md)

A [scale-free network](scale-free-network.md) has a [degree distribution](degree-distribution.md) with an approximately power-law tail over a meaningful range. Evidence requires degrees from enough [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) to assess that range and compare alternative tail models. Large [clustering coefficients](clustering-coefficient.md) or short [graph distances](distance-graph-theory.md) alone do not establish this property.
