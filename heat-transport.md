# Heat transport

↑ **Parent:** [Heat transfer](heat-transfer.md)

Transfer of thermal [energy](energy-split.md) between regions. In a constant-property thermal [Boussinesq approximation](boussinesq-approximation.md), the advective [heat flux](heat-flux-density.md) per unit area is $\rho_0c_pT\mathbf u$ and transport across a section is its normal component integrated over that section. With zero net volume transport, adding a constant reference [temperature](temperature-split.md) does not change that integral. Otherwise the reference and the return-flow budget must be specified. Atmospheric [energy](energy-split.md) transport also includes [latent heat](latent-heat.md) and [gravitational potential energy](gravitational-energy.md).
