# Pieri rule

↑ **Parent:** [Littlewood–Richardson rule](littlewood-richardson-rule.md)

In the induction ring of ordinary symmetric-group [characters](character-of-a-representation.md), $[\lambda][r]$ is the sum, with multiplicity one, of $[\nu]$ for which $\nu/\lambda$ is a [horizontal strip](horizontal-strip.md) of $r$ cells. Here $[r]$ is the [trivial representation](trivial-representation.md) of $S_r$. In the [Littlewood–Richardson rule](littlewood-richardson-rule.md), content $(r)$ means every skew entry is $1$; strict column increase is possible exactly when there is at most one new cell in every column. This proves the rule.
