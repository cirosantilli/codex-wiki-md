# Charged oscillator basis of a scalar doublet

↑ **Parent:** [Internal rotation symmetry of two real scalar fields](internal-rotation-symmetry-of-two-real-scalar-fields.md)

For the [internal rotation symmetry of two real scalar fields](internal-rotation-symmetry-of-two-real-scalar-fields.md) with $\delta\phi_1=\alpha\phi_2$ and $\delta\phi_2=-\alpha\phi_1$, the [Noether charge](noether-charge.md) is $Q=i\int(a_1^\dagger a_2-a_2^\dagger a_1)d^3p/(2\pi)^3$. Its [commutators](commutator.md) satisfy $[Q,b_\pm^\dagger]=\pm b_\pm^\dagger$, so these [creation operators](creation-operator.md) produce charge [eigenstates](eigenstate.md) in the [one-particle state](one-particle-state.md) sector. Substitution gives $Q=N_+-N_-$, converting the rotation of real components into opposite charges in a complex basis.
