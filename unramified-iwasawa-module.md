# Unramified Iwasawa module

↑ **Parent:** [Iwasawa module](iwasawa-module.md)

For a [Zp-extension](zp-extension.md), let $L_\infty$ be the maximal unramified abelian pro-$p$ extension of $F_\infty$. Its [Galois group](galois-group.md) is a compact [Iwasawa module](iwasawa-module.md), identifiable by [Artin reciprocity](artin-reciprocity-law.md) with the inverse limit of the $p$-primary [ideal class groups](ideal-class-group.md) of finite layers under norms.
