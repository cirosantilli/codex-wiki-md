# Mass-to-charge ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Mass spectrometry](mass-spectrometry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mass-to-charge_ratio)

The ratio of an [ion](ion.md)'s [mass](mass.md) to its [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md); in [SI](international-system-of-units.md) it has units $\mathrm{kg/C}$. A convenient numerical [mass spectrum](mass-spectrum.md) coordinate is $x=(m/\mathrm u)/z$, with $q=ze$ and positive integer $z$ here. This $x$ is [dimensionless](dimensionless-quantity.md). Doubling $z$ at fixed [mass](mass.md) halves $x$, so the [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) state must be identified before assigning an [isotope](isotope.md).
