# 2022

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the episodic and semantic memory course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Original solutions to all six options in the [Episodic and semantic memory course of the University of Oxford](../episodic-and-semantic-memory-course-of-the-university-of-oxford.md) examination held on 3 June 2022. Question 1 retains its two printed Roman labels. The arguments use research available before the examination; the explanatory diagrams and counterexamples are original constructions.

Source: [Oxford examination release](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/experimental_psychology_past_exa/response/2757831/attach/3/New%20Compressed%20zipped%20Folder.zip?cookie_passthrough=1), member a10975w1.pdf. The cover supplies the code and date; Part II is the frozen audited course classification.
