<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

**The theories can be reconciled at several explanatory levels, but a strong restriction of medial temporal regions to [declarative memory](../../../declarative-memory.md) conflicts with evidence for their contribution to demanding online [perception](../../../perception.md).** Compatibility should be assessed between explicit claims, rather than achieved by renaming every deficit relational or mnemonic.

The [medial temporal lobe declarative memory system theory](../../../medial-temporal-lobe-declarative-memory-system-theory.md), associated with [Larry Squire](../../../larry-squire.md) and [Stuart Zola-Morgan](../../../stuart-zola-morgan.md), identifies a system important for rapidly establishing long-term memories for facts and events. Their 1991 account emphasizes binding distributed cortical representations and a time-limited dependence during consolidation. It explains why substantial impairment in acquiring new declarative knowledge can coexist with preserved skills or other [learning](../../../learning.md). The proposal specifies a memory system and its temporal role; it does not by itself specify every representation or [learning](../../../learning.md) rule inside each component.[1991, anatomical components, declarative learning, cortical binding and consolidation](../../../the-medial-temporal-lobe-memory-system-by-larry-squire-and-others.md)

[Relational memory theory](../../../relational-memory-theory.md), associated with [Howard Eichenbaum](../../../howard-eichenbaum.md) and supported by work including [Norbert J. Fortin](../../../norbert-j-fortin.md)'s, addresses the organization and flexible use of relationships. Items may be connected by location, order or another shared element. The account therefore explains why [hippocampal](../../../hippocampus.md) damage can affect more than a spatial map. Its proposal that related experiences support flexible queries supplies a candidate computation within a wider declarative system, rather than necessarily competing with the existence of that system.[1999, systematic organization and flexible expression of memories](../../../the-hippocampus-and-mechanisms-of-declarative-memory-by-howard-eichenbaum.md)

The [representational hierarchy theory](../../../representational-hierarchy-theory.md) associated with [Timothy J. Bussey](../../../timothy-j-bussey.md) and [Mark J. Buckley](../../../mark-j-buckley.md) asks what conjunctions a region represents. A simple feature can occur in several objects and be insufficient for distinguishing them. Perirhinal object-level representations can reduce such ambiguity. The same representation can support recognizing an object after a delay or distinguishing it among simultaneously visible alternatives; the representational demand crosses the usual [perception](../../../perception.md)–memory boundary.

Two experiments sharpen this claim. [Timothy J. Bussey](../../../timothy-j-bussey.md) and colleagues in 2002 held the number of object pairs constant while varying [feature ambiguity](../../../feature-ambiguity.md) in visual discrimination. Perirhinal lesion effects were related to the conjunction demand, supporting a more specific account than simply more objects or more difficult [learning](../../../learning.md). [Mark J. Buckley](../../../mark-j-buckley.md) and colleagues in 2001 used simultaneous touchscreen oddity tasks. Lesioned macaques were impaired when object-level distinctions were required, with spared performance on difficult discriminations based on simple color, shape or size differences. Difficulty alone therefore does not explain the pattern.[2002, fixed number of pairs and manipulated feature ambiguity](../../../perirhinal-cortex-resolves-feature-ambiguity-in-complex-visual-discriminations-by-timothy-j-bussey-and-others.md)[2001, object oddity and difficult simple-feature controls](../../../selective-perceptual-impairments-after-perirhinal-cortex-ablation-by-mark-j-buckley-and-others.md)

A constructive reconciliation follows. The declarative account identifies a broad system and its role in long-term [learning](../../../learning.md); the relational account specifies organization and flexible expression; a representational hierarchy specifies which conjunctions its components can supply. Perirhinal object representations and contextual representations can participate in [hippocampal](../../../hippocampus.md) binding, and [learning](../../../learning.md) can stabilize those relations. Item/source differences in [Lila Davachi](../../../lila-davachi.md)'s encoding study are consistent with differentiated contributions within a common system. Consistency is weaker than a unique confirmation of this particular synthesis.[2003, differentiated subsequent item and source memory effects](../../../multiple-routes-to-memory-by-lila-davachi-and-others.md)

A real disagreement remains. If an original strong declarative-only position says that these regions have no necessary role in [perception](../../../perception.md) or immediate processing, a selective deficit on simultaneous object discrimination is problematic. Calling every across-fixation comparison memory would protect that position only by changing what it excludes. Conversely, simultaneous display does not eliminate [working memory](../../../working-memory.md), [attention](../../../attention.md), task-rule [learning](../../../learning.md) or all incidental encoding. The strongest adjudication manipulates representational complexity separately from retention demand and matches sensory and motor requirements. A selective conjunction deficit at negligible imposed delay supports the representational claim more directly than a generic difficult-task impairment.

The theories also need not agree about eventual [hippocampal](../../../hippocampus.md) [independence](../../../independent-random-variables.md) of detailed remote episodes. Systems-level consolidation, relational organization and perceptual conjunctions are different issues; adopting one does not logically settle the others. Each retained commitment should generate a prediction that could differ from an alternative, including the kind of error expected after a selective lesion.

**A coherent account can combine a differentiated medial temporal system, relational binding and hierarchical representations. It must explicitly revise a strict memory-only boundary if complex online [perception](../../../perception.md) requires the same representations; that tension cannot be removed merely by saying that all three theories describe different levels.**
