# Proper quark-gluon vertex colour factors

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum chromodynamics](quantum-chromodynamics.md)

For Hermitian [fundamental representation](fundamental-representation.md) generators normalized by $\operatorname{tr}(T^aT^b)=\delta^{ab}/2$, the Abelian-shaped one-loop [Feynman diagram](feynman-diagram.md) has [colour factor](color-factor.md) $\sum_bT^bT^aT^b=(C_F-C_A/2)T^a=-T^a/(2N)$. Expand $\sum_b[T^b,[T^b,T^a]]=C_AT^a$ to prove the identity. With oriented structure tensor $f^{abc}$, the three-gluon graph has $f^{abc}T^bT^c=iC_AT^a/2$ by antisymmetrizing the generator product. The factor $i$ and its sign must be combined consistently with the chosen three-gluon [Feynman rule](feynman-rule.md). For $SU(3)$, the two real coefficients are $-1/6$ and $3/2$.
