# Convection cell

↑ **Parent:** [Convection](convection.md)

An organized region of buoyant rising fluid and compensating descending flow that transports heat or composition. [Convection rolls](convection-roll.md) are a simple planar example; [solar granules](solar-granule.md) are evolving compressible examples. A cellular pattern does not require every material trajectory to form a closed streamline, especially near a radiating free surface.
