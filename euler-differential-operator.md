# Euler differential operator

↑ **Parent:** [Differential operator](differential-operator.md)

The [Euler differential operator](euler-differential-operator.md) differentiates in logarithmic position: if $r=\log x$ and $V(r)=v(e^r)$, then $Dv=V'$. In particular $D(x^n)=nx^n$, so a polynomial $p(D)$ acts on the power law $x^n$ by multiplication by $p(n)$. This converts a [Cauchy-Euler equation](cauchy-euler-equation.md) into a constant-coefficient [linear differential equation](linear-differential-equation.md). Repeated roots give additional logarithmic factors, as ordinary constant-coefficient equations give polynomial factors multiplying exponentials.
