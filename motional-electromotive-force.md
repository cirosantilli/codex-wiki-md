# Motional electromotive force

↑ **Parent:** [Electromotive force](electromotive-force.md)

Motional electromotive force is the contribution caused by a conductor moving through a [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md). The magnetic part of the [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md) separates charges within the conductor. For a bar of length $L$ moving at velocity $v$ perpendicular to a uniform field, its magnitude is $BL|v|$; the sign follows the chosen circuit orientation. Together with the electric-field contribution it enters [Faraday's law](faraday-s-law-of-induction.md) for moving circuits.
