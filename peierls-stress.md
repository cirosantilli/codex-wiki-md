# Peierls stress

↑ **Parent:** [Dislocation glide](dislocation-glide.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peierls_stress)

A lattice-friction stress for [dislocation glide](dislocation-glide.md), associated with [Rudolf Peierls](rudolf-peierls.md) and [Frank Nabarro](frank-nabarro.md). A simple model gives $\tau_P\sim3G\exp(-2\pi w/b)$, where $w$ is core width and $b$ the [Burgers vector](burgers-vector.md) magnitude. Spreading slip over many atomic rows reduces the periodic energy barrier; narrower cores are harder to move in this model.
