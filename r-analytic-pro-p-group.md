# R-analytic pro-p group

↑ **Parent:** [Pro-p group](pro-p-group.md)

For a [pro-p ring](pro-p-ring.md) $R$, an $R$-analytic [pro-p group](pro-p-group.md) is a [pro-p group](pro-p-group.md) equipped with an analytic manifold structure locally modelled on open subsets of a finite free $R$-module, such that multiplication and inversion are given locally by convergent [power series](power-series.md). The number of local coordinates is its analytic [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md). The [Nottingham group](nottingham-group.md) admits no such structure over any pro-$p$ ring: its hereditary just infiniteness, unbounded finite cyclic torsion and sub-base-three derived-series growth exclude both alternatives in the analytic just-infinite structure theorem.
