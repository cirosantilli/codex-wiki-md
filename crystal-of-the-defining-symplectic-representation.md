# Crystal of the defining symplectic representation

↑ **Parent:** [Crystal basis](crystal-basis.md)

For the [symplectic Lie algebra](symplectic-lie-algebra.md) $\mathfrak{sp}_{2n}$, the defining [crystal basis](crystal-basis.md) is the chain $1,2,\ldots,n,\bar n,\ldots,\bar1$, with [weights](weight-representation-theory.md) $\varepsilon_1,\ldots,\varepsilon_n,-\varepsilon_n,\ldots,-\varepsilon_1$. Its successive lowering-edge colors are $1,2,\ldots,n-1,n,n-1,\ldots,1$. Every step subtracts its color's [simple root](simple-root.md). Applying the [crystal tensor-product rule](crystal-tensor-product-rule.md) to two copies gives a complete grid description of the tensor-square crystal. For $n\ge2$, its only highest vertices are $1\otimes1$, $1\otimes2$, and $1\otimes\bar1$, of [highest weights](highest-weight-of-a-representation.md) $2\omega_1$, $\omega_2$, and zero. For $n=1$, the middle vertex is absent and only the other two highest vertices remain.
