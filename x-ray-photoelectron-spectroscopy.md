# X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy

↑ **Parent:** [Photoemission spectroscopy](photoemission-spectroscopy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/X-ray_photoelectron_spectroscopy)

[X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy](x-ray-photoelectron-spectroscopy.md) uses [X-ray](x-ray.md) excitation to investigate [electron](electron.md) removal energies, including those of [core electrons](core-electron.md). The common name [electron spectroscopy for chemical analysis](x-ray-photoelectron-spectroscopy.md) emphasizes its use in distinguishing chemical surroundings. The whole recorded spectrum can also contain [electrons](electron.md) produced by processes other than direct [photoionization](photoionization.md); a peak assignment must identify the emission process as well as the [chemical element](chemical-element.md).

In a modern implementation, a [quartz](quartz.md) crystal selects [aluminum](aluminium.md) characteristic [X-rays](x-ray.md) according to [Bragg's law](bragg-s-law.md) and focuses them onto the sample. [Vacuum](vacuum.md) reduces gas collisions and contamination; a separately pumped entry chamber limits exposure to air. Lenses collect [photoelectrons](photoelectron.md); an electrostatic [electron energy analyzer](electron-energy-analyzer.md) transmits a narrow [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) band. Stepping that band and counting detector pulses produces the spectrum. An electron flood source can compensate positive [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) on insulating samples. Ion cleaning can alter vulnerable samples.[Sections Ultra-high vacuum system; XPS X-ray source; Electron analyzer; Ion sources; Charge neutralization](x-ray-photoelectron-spectroscopy-instrumentation-features-by-thermo-fisher-scientific.md)

For a scan with count $N_j$ and dwell [time](time-in-physics.md) $t_j$ at setting $j$, compare rates $N_j/t_j$, rather than raw counts when dwell times differ. Under an independent [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md) model, the count [standard deviation](standard-deviation.md) is approximately $\sqrt{N_j}$ for a sufficiently populated bin. Longer acquisition reduces this relative counting [measurement uncertainty](measurement-uncertainty.md); it does not automatically improve separation of overlapping peaks. [Photoelectron peak overlap and resolution](photoelectron-peak-overlap-and-resolution.md) gives an example in which the same chemical separation produces either two maxima or one.
