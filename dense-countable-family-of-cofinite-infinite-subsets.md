# Dense countable family of cofinite infinite subsets

↑ **Parent:** [Ordinary topology on infinite subsets](ordinary-topology-on-infinite-subsets.md)

In the [ordinary topology on infinite subsets](ordinary-topology-on-infinite-subsets.md), the family $D$ of [cofinite sets](cofinite-set.md) is a [countable set](countable-set.md), a [dense](dense-set.md) and a [meagre set](meagre-set.md). Countability follows by indexing its members by their finite complements. Each singleton is closed with empty interior, while every basic cylinder with finite initial segment $s$ contains $s\cup\{n:n>\max s\}$. Consequently $D$ is not a [nowhere dense set](nowhere-dense-set.md). This example uses the ordinary cylinder topology, rather than the [Ramsey cone topology](ramsey-cone-topology.md).
