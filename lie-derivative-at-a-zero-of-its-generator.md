# Lie derivative at a zero of its generator

↑ **Parent:** [Lie derivative of a tensor field](lie-derivative-of-a-tensor-field.md)

The [tensor Lie derivative](lie-derivative-of-a-tensor-field.md) need not vanish at a point where its generating [vector field](vector-field.md) vanishes. For $X=x\partial_x$, the local flow fixes zero but its differential rescales tangent vectors; $\mathcal L_Xdx=dx$ even there. Straightening coordinates exist only at nonzero generator points. The flow definition remains valid at zeros and includes derivatives of the generator acting on tensor slots.
