# Oscillator uncertainty and allocation of classical energy

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md)

For a one-dimensional oscillator eigenstate, [parity](parity.md) gives $\langle x\rangle=0$ and its coordinate [variance](variance-split.md) is the displayed expression. A classical oscillator with that coordinate [energy](energy-split.md) has turning amplitude $A=\sqrt{2E_x/(m\omega^2)}$, so $\Delta x=A/\sqrt2$. In several dimensions the total [energy](energy-split.md) does not specify a given coordinate's classical amplitude until its [energy](energy-split.md) allocation is fixed. [zero-point energies](zero-point-energy.md) in the other quantum coordinates must not silently be assigned to that one classical mode.
