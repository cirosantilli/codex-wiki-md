# Subsonic flow

↑ **Parent:** [Mach number](mach-number.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subsonic_flow)

A subsonic flow has [Mach number](mach-number.md) less than one, so its speed is below the local [sound speed](speed-of-sound.md).

For uniform [gas](gas.md) moving at $U\mathbf e_x$ past a resting body, an upstream-directed [acoustic wave](acoustic-wave.md) has body-frame [velocity](velocity.md) $(U-c)\mathbf e_x$. When $0\leq M<1$, this [velocity](velocity.md) points upstream. This is why the body can influence the upstream [fluid flow](fluid-flow.md) through small [pressure](pressure-split.md) disturbances.
