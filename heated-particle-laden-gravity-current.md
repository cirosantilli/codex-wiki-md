# Heated particle-laden gravity current

↑ **Parent:** [Particle-laden gravity current](particle-laden-gravity-current.md)

A [heated particle-laden layer](heated-particle-laden-layer.md) released along a bed forms a [gravity current](gravity-current.md) while $g'=g(\gamma\phi-\theta)>0$. In a [gravity-current box model](gravity-current-box-model.md), heating can make $g'$ vanish in finite time, whereas [settling velocity](settling-velocity.md) alone can give finite [runout length of a gravity current](runout-length-of-a-gravity-current.md) approached asymptotically.
