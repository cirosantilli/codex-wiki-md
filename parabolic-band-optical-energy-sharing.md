# Parabolic-band optical energy sharing

↑ **Parent:** [Direct optical transition](direct-optical-transition.md)

A vertical direct transition at a common [wavevector](wavevector.md) puts an [electron](electron.md) above the conduction edge and leaves a hole below the valence edge. Their parabolic kinetic energies are inversely proportional to their effective masses. The excess [photon](photon.md) [energy](energy-split.md) is therefore split as $K_e/K_h=m_h^*/m_e^*$, rather than equally unless the masses match. The initial valence [electron](electron.md) [energy](energy-split.md) is $-K_h$ relative to its band maximum; the final conduction [energy](energy-split.md) is $E_g+K_e$.
