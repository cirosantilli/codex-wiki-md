# Sobolev characterization by bounded difference quotients

↑ **Parent:** [First-order Sobolev space](first-order-sobolev-space.md)

For $f\in H^1(\mathbb R)$, its [difference quotient](difference-quotient.md) equals $D_hf=\int_0^1f'(\cdot+\theta h)\,d\theta$ in [L2 space](l2-space-is-a-hilbert-space.md). Thus $\|D_hf\|_2\leq\|f'\|_2$, and [translation of a function](translation-of-a-function.md) gives $D_hf\to f'$ in [L2 space](l2-space-is-a-hilbert-space.md). Conversely, bounded difference quotients have a weakly convergent subsequence as $h\to0$. Against a [test function](test-function.md), $\int D_hf\,v\to-\int f v'$, so that weak limit is the [weak derivative](weak-derivative.md) of $f$. It lies in [L2 space](l2-space-is-a-hilbert-space.md), which is precisely the [first-order Sobolev space](first-order-sobolev-space.md) condition.
