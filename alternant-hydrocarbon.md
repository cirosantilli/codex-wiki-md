# Alternant hydrocarbon

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrocarbon](hydrocarbon.md)

A conjugated [hydrocarbon](hydrocarbon.md) whose pi-site graph is bipartite: its sites can be divided into two sets with every retained resonance interaction joining opposite sets. The definition refers to the selected nearest-neighbour Hückel model, not simply to a visually alternating Lewis drawing.
