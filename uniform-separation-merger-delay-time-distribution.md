# Uniform-separation merger delay-time distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Neutron star merger](neutron-star-merger.md)

If a circular compact binary has a [gravitational wave](gravitational-wave-split.md) coalescence delay $t=BA_0^4$ and $dN/dA_0$ is constant, then $dN/dt=(dN/dA_0)|dA_0/dt|\propto t^{-3/4}$. More generally $dN/dA_0\propto A_0^\beta$ gives a [delay-time distribution](delay-time-distribution.md) proportional to $t^{(\beta-3)/4}$. A log-uniform separation distribution instead gives $t^{-1}$.
