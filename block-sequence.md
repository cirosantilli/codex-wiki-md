# Block sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Schauder basis](schauder-basis.md)

Relative to a [Schauder basis](schauder-basis.md), a block sequence consists of nonzero finitely supported vectors with successive disjoint supports: $\max\operatorname{supp}x_i<\min\operatorname{supp}x_{i+1}$. It is a [basic sequence](basic-sequence.md), with [basis constant](basis-constant.md) at most that of the original basis.
