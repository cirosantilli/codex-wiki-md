# Gaussian Bianchi group

↑ **Parent:** [Kleinian group](kleinian-group.md)

The Gaussian Bianchi group is the [Kleinian group](kleinian-group.md) of determinant-one two-by-two matrices over the [Gaussian integers](gaussian-integer.md), modulo $\{\pm I\}$. Its matrix entries lie in a discrete subring of $\mathbb C$, so it is a discrete subgroup of $PSL_2(\mathbb C)$. The [Borromean link group](borromean-link-group.md) is a torsion-free subgroup of index 24.
