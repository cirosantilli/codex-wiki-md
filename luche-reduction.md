# Luche reduction

↑ **Parent:** [Carbonyl reduction](carbonyl-reduction.md)

A cerium(III)-assisted borohydride reduction often used to favour 1,2-reduction of an enone to an allylic [alcohol](alcohol.md). Actual selectivity remains substrate- and condition-dependent.
