# Eunotosaurus

↑ **Parent:** [Sauropsida](sauropsida.md)

A [fossil](fossil.md) reptile proposed as an early [turtle](turtle.md) relative. Its [skull](skull.md) [anatomy](anatomy.md), ontogeny and phylogenetic placement have been used to test the history of temporal roof closure.
