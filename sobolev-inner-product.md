# Sobolev inner product

↑ **Parent:** [Sobolev norm](sobolev-norm.md)

The displayed [inner product](inner-product-split.md) on the first-order [Sobolev space](sobolev-space-split.md) induces its usual [Sobolev norm](sobolev-norm.md). A continuous reaction-diffusion [weak formulation](weak-formulation.md) with unit diffusion and unit reaction has this exact [bilinear form](bilinear-form-split.md), so its conforming [Galerkin method](galerkin-method.md) gives a [best approximation](best-approximation-in-a-normed-space.md) with constant one in the first-order [Sobolev norm](sobolev-norm.md).
