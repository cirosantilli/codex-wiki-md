# Pressure-limited spherical freezing in a porous medium

↑ **Parent:** [Stefan problem](stefan-problem.md)

A growing ice-filled sphere expels liquid because ice is less dense than water. With [porosity](porosity.md) $\phi$, $\epsilon=1-\rho_s/\rho$, and pressure-induced [freezing-point depression](freezing-point-depression.md) $T_i=T_m-\gamma p_i$, the [Darcy velocity](darcy-velocity.md) gives $p_i=\mu\phi\epsilon a\dot a/\Pi$. Quasi-steady spherical [thermal conduction](thermal-conduction.md) gives $\phi\rho_sLa\dot a=k(T_i-T_\infty)$. Hence $a\dot a=k(T_m-T_\infty)/[\phi\rho_sL(1+\Lambda)]$, where $\Lambda=k\gamma\mu\epsilon/(\Pi\rho_sL)$. The interface approaches the far-field temperature at low [permeability of a porous medium](permeability-of-a-porous-medium.md), while its pressure tends to a finite equilibrium value.
