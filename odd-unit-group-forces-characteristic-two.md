# Odd unit group forces characteristic two

↑ **Parent:** [Characteristic of a ring](characteristic-of-a-ring.md)

In a nonzero unital [ring](ring.md) with finite odd [unit group](unit-group.md), the [unit](unit-in-a-ring.md) $-1$ has order dividing two. By [Lagrange's theorem](lagrange-s-theorem.md), that order also divides $|U(R)|$, so $-1=1$ and the [characteristic of a ring](characteristic-of-a-ring.md) is two. Consequently $\overline a\mapsto a1_R$ defines a unital [ring homomorphism](ring-homomorphism.md) $\mathbb F_2\to R$. This map still exists for the [zero ring](zero-ring.md), whose characteristic is one under the least-positive-integer convention.
