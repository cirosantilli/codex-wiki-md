# Number

↑ **Parent:** [Arithmetic](arithmetic-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Number)

Numbers are mathematical objects used to express quantities and carry out operations. Familiar families include the [natural numbers](natural-number.md), [integers](integer-split.md), [rational numbers](rational-number.md), [real numbers](real-number.md), and [complex numbers](complex-number.md); specifying the family determines which operations and solutions are available.
