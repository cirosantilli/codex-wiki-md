# Position measurement

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum measurement](quantum-measurement-split.md)

An ideal position measurement of a normalized [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) $\psi$ assigns a [probability](probability.md) to each spatial region $A$ through the [Born rule](born-rule.md): $P(A)=\int_A|\psi(x)|^2\,dx$. This specifies outcome statistics; it does not by itself specify the [post-measurement state](post-measurement-state.md). A physical detector has finite spatial resolution, which must be included in its measurement model.
