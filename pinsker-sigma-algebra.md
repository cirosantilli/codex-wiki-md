# Pinsker sigma-algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Kolmogorov-Sinai entropy](kolmogorov-sinai-entropy.md)

The Pinsker sigma-algebra consists of the measurable sets $A$ whose binary partition $\{A,X\setminus A\}$ has entropy rate zero. It is a sigma-algebra and is the largest invariant factor with zero [Kolmogorov-Sinai entropy](kolmogorov-sinai-entropy.md).
