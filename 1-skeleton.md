# 1-skeleton

↑ **Parent:** [CW complex](cw-complex.md)

The one-skeleton $X^{(1)}$ of a [CW complex](cw-complex.md) is the union of its zero-cells and one-cells. It is a graph. The unit-edge path metric measures the infimum of lengths of paths in this graph; it is different from metrics that allow paths through two-cell interiors.
