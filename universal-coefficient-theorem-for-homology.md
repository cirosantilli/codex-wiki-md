# Universal coefficient theorem for homology

↑ **Parent:** [Homology (mathematics)](homology-split.md)

For an abelian coefficient group $G$, there is a split short exact sequence

$$
0\to H_i(X;\mathbb Z)\otimes G
\to H_i(X;G)
\to\operatorname{Tor}(H_{i-1}(X;\mathbb Z),G)\to0.
$$

For $G=\mathbb Q$, the Tor term vanishes.

This is the homology case of the [universal coefficient theorem](universal-coefficient-theorem.md); its cohomology case uses Hom and Ext rather than tensor product and Tor.
