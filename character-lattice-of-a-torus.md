# Character lattice of a torus

↑ **Parent:** [Maximal torus](maximal-torus.md)

For a [compact](compact-space.md) [torus](torus.md) $T\cong U(1)^r$, its [continuous](continuous-function.md) one-dimensional [characters](character-of-a-representation.md) form a [free abelian group](free-abelian-group.md) of rank $r$. They are precisely $z\mapsto z_1^{m_1}\cdots z_r^{m_r}$ with integer exponents. This follows from the classification of [representations of the circle group](representation-of-the-circle-group.md), applied to each factor. These [characters](character-of-a-representation.md) are the integral [group](group-split.md) [weights](weight-representation-theory.md) occurring upon restricting a [group representation](group-representation.md) to a [maximal torus](maximal-torus.md); global integrality must be distinguished from integrality against coroots for a [simply connected](simply-connected-space.md) semisimple covering [group](group-split.md).
