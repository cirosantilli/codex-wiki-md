# No-write-allocate cache

↑ **Parent:** [Cache (computing)](cache-computing.md)

A [memory cache](cache-computing.md) policy that sends a write miss onward without allocating the missed [cache line](cache-line.md) in that level. Different levels of a [cache hierarchy](cache-hierarchy.md) may use different write policies.
