# Electron removal spectral function

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral function](spectral-function.md)

The [electron](electron.md) single-particle [spectral function](spectral-function.md) $A(\mathbf k,\epsilon)=-\operatorname{Im}G^R(\mathbf k,\epsilon)/\pi$, in a convention normalized to unit energy integral per orbital and spin, describes addition and removal energies. For the fermionic retarded [Green function](green-s-function-split.md), $G^R(\mathbf k,t)=-i\Theta(t)\langle\{c_{\mathbf k}(t),c_{\mathbf k}^\dagger(0)\}\rangle$, energy is referenced to the [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md). The occupied removal spectrum is $f(\epsilon)A(\mathbf k,\epsilon)$, with [Fermi-Dirac distribution](fermi-dirac-distribution.md) $f$. In the noninteracting limit, $A$ is a delta peak at the band energy; interactions can shift, broaden, and redistribute its weight.
