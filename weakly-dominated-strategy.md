# Weakly dominated strategy

↑ **Parent:** [Dominated strategy](dominated-strategy.md)

A strategy is weakly dominated if another gives at least as much payoff against every opponent strategy and strictly more against at least one. Unlike a [strictly dominated strategy](strict-dominance.md), it may remain a [best response](best-response.md) to opponents who never choose a strategy where the comparison is strict. Thus [weak domination does not exclude equilibrium strategies](weak-domination-does-not-exclude-equilibrium-strategies.md).
