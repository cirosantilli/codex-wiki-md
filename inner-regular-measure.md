# Inner regular measure

↑ **Parent:** [Measure](measure.md)

A [measure](measure.md) is inner regular on its measurable sets if their measures are suprema of the measures of contained [compact sets](compact-space.md). Every [Borel probability measure](borel-probability-measure.md) on a [compact metric space](compact-metric-space.md) is inner regular and has [outer regularity](outer-regular-measure.md). Combining the two properties allows approximation of an [indicator function](indicator-function.md) by a [continuous function](continuous-function.md) in the $L^1$ norm.
