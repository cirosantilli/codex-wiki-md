# Isotropic fourth moment of a ball

↑ **Parent:** [Isotropic tensor](isotropic-tensor.md)

Here $V_d$ is the volume of the unit ball in $d$-dimensional [Euclidean space](euclidean-norm.md). Rotational invariance makes the integral an [isotropic tensor](isotropic-tensor.md), while complete symmetry in its four indices equates the three coefficients. Contracting $i=j$ and $k=l$ gives $d(d+2)$ times their common value. The same contraction of the integral is $\int_{|x|\le R}|x|^4\,d^dx=dV_dR^{d+4}/(d+4)$, proving the formula. For $d=3,R=1$ the coefficient is $4\pi/105$. This is the fourth-order counterpart of the [isotropic second moment of a ball](isotropic-second-moment-of-a-ball.md).
