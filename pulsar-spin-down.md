# Pulsar spin-down

↑ **Parent:** [Pulsar](pulsar.md)

Loss of a rotating [neutron star](neutron-star.md)'s [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) and rotational [energy](energy-split.md) through electromagnetic torque and a magnetized outflow. For constant [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) $I$, differentiating $E_{\rm rot}=I(2\pi/P)^2/2$ gives the displayed positive loss rate when $\dot P>0$. It is not the same as the measured [radiation](radiation.md) [luminosity](luminosity.md) in any one observing band.
