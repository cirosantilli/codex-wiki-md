# Method of types

↑ **Parent:** [Type (information theory)](type-information-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Method_of_types)

For a finite [alphabet](alphabet.md) $A$, there are at most $(n+1)^{|A|}$ length-$n$ types, and under an [i.i.d.](independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables.md) law $P$ the probability of the type class $T_R$ is at most $2^{-nD(R\Vert P)}$ when logarithms use base two.
