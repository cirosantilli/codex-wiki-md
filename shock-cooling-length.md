# Shock cooling length

↑ **Parent:** [Radiative shock](radiative-shock.md)

The distance a downstream fluid element travels while losing a substantial fraction of its thermal [energy](energy-split.md). Multiplying the immediate downstream [velocity](velocity.md) by a [radiative gas cooling time](optically-thin-gas-cooling-time.md) gives an order-of-magnitude length. A resolved layer instead uses $\ell=\int u(t)\,dt$, because [mass density](density.md), [temperature](temperature-split.md) and speed change during cooling. It is distinct from the microscopic dissipative thickness of the [shock wave](shock-wave.md).
