# Babcock-Leighton mechanism

↑ **Parent:** [Solar dynamo](solar-dynamo.md)

The Babcock-Leighton mechanism regenerates large-scale poloidal solar flux through the emergence and dispersal of systematically tilted bipolar active regions. With [Joy's law](joy-s-law.md) tilt, opposite polarities have different latitudes: cancellation near the equator and poleward transport of surviving following-polarity flux alter and reverse the polar field. Thus a toroidal field that produces rising bipolar regions feeds back into a [poloidal magnetic field](poloidal-magnetic-field.md). The source is intrinsically nonlocal between the interior flux reservoir and surface emergence and is stochastic because active-region fluxes and tilts vary.
