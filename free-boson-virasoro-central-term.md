# Free-boson Virasoro central term

↑ **Parent:** [Virasoro central extension](virasoro-central-extension.md)

For $d$ free target coordinates, [normal ordering](normal-ordering.md) of $L_m=\sum_n:\alpha_{m-n}\cdot\alpha_n:/2$ gives a matter [central charge](central-charge.md) $d$. On the formal zero-momentum vacuum, the double contractions yield $\langle L_mL_{-m}\rangle=(d/2)\sum_{r=1}^{m-1}r(m-r)=d(m^3-m)/12$. Two target-metric contractions give $\eta_{ab}\eta^{ab}=d$, so timelike target coordinates do not subtract from this [central charge](central-charge.md). Transverse-only and covariant generators must not be confused.
