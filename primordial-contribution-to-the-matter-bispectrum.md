# Primordial contribution to the matter bispectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Local-type primordial non-Gaussianity](local-type-primordial-non-gaussianity.md)

If the linear [density contrast](density-contrast.md) obeys $\delta(\mathbf k)=\alpha(k)\varphi(\mathbf k)$, then [local-type primordial non-Gaussianity](local-type-primordial-non-gaussianity.md) contributes

$$
B_m^{\rm prim}=2f_{\rm NL}\left[\frac{\alpha_3}{\alpha_1\alpha_2}P_m(k_1)P_m(k_2)+\text{cyclic terms}\right].
$$

In a [squeezed bispectrum configuration](squeezed-bispectrum-configuration.md) and for a nearly scale-invariant potential, the leading term is $4f_{\rm NL}P_m(k_L)P_m(k_S)/\alpha(k_L)$. This excludes the [standard perturbation theory in cosmology](standard-perturbation-theory-in-cosmology.md) contribution from nonlinear gravity.
