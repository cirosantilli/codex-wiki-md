# Cotranslational protein translocation

↑ **Parent:** [Organelle protein import](organelle-protein-import.md)

[Cotranslational protein translocation](cotranslational-protein-translocation.md) is passage of a growing [polypeptide chain](polypeptide.md) across a membrane during [biological translation](translation-biology.md). [SRP](signal-recognition-particle.md) recognizes an exposed [hydrophobic](hydrophobicity.md) [ER signal sequence](er-signal-sequence.md). The [SRP receptor](signal-recognition-particle-receptor.md) enables handoff to the [Sec61 complex](sec61-complex.md). [Biological translation](translation-biology.md) can slow during delivery; the [ribosome](ribosome.md) remains on the [cytosol](cytosol.md) side.[Shao and Hegde, Membrane Protein Insertion at the Endoplasmic Reticulum, 2011; “Cotranslational Targeting”, Figure 3](membrane-protein-insertion-at-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-by-sichen-shao-and-ramanujan-s-hegde.md)

The useful engineering feature is coordination: a targeting instruction can become accessible while the rest of its cargo is still being made. Recognition, docking and crossing remain distinct operations. A defect in any one can prevent successful import even when the [protein primary structure](protein-primary-structure.md) contains an appropriate signal.

This route is not universal. Many [tail-anchored proteins](tail-anchored-protein.md) use delivery after [biological translation](translation-biology.md), and their membrane-spanning segment lies near the [C-terminus](c-terminus.md). Thus [protein targeting](protein-targeting.md) signals need not be at the [N-terminus](n-terminus.md).[Shao and Hegde, 2011; “Posttranslational Membrane Protein Topogenesis”, Figure 6](membrane-protein-insertion-at-the-endoplasmic-reticulum-by-sichen-shao-and-ramanujan-s-hegde.md)
