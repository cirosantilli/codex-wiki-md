# Euler-Lagrange equation for a second-derivative functional

↑ **Parent:** [Calculus of variations](calculus-of-variations-split.md)

For a sufficiently smooth [functional](functional.md) $I[y]=\int_a^b F(x,y,y',y'')\,dx$, the [first variation](first-variation.md) in direction $\eta$ is $\int(F_y\eta+F_{y'}\eta'+F_{y''}\eta'')dx$. Twice applying [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) gives the interior coefficient $F_y-(F_{y'})'+(F_{y''})''$ and boundary expression $[(F_{y'}-(F_{y''})')\eta+F_{y''}\eta']_a^b$. Variations supported in the interior and the [fundamental lemma of the calculus of variations](fundamental-lemma-of-the-calculus-of-variations.md) force the displayed equation. The remaining boundary terms give the [natural boundary conditions for second-derivative functionals](natural-boundary-conditions-for-second-derivative-functionals.md).
