# Lp conservation for incompressible transport

↑ **Parent:** [Linear transport equation](linear-transport-equation.md)

Scalar advection preserves every finite [Lp norm](lp-norm.md) if its complete differentiable characteristic flow preserves volume and the initial datum belongs to the corresponding [Lp space](lp-space.md). In general the $p$th power of the [norm](norm.md) is the initial $|f_0|^p$ weighted by the [phase-space flow Jacobian](phase-space-flow-jacobian.md). Completeness alone preserves the [essential supremum](essential-supremum.md), but does not preserve finite-$p$ [norms](norm.md) for compressible transport.
