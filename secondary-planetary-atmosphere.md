# Secondary planetary atmosphere

↑ **Parent:** [Exoplanet atmosphere](exoplanet-atmosphere.md)

A secondary planetary atmosphere is supplied or replenished after the original nebular-envelope stage, through outgassing, volatile delivery and processing. The present atmospheres of Earth, Venus and Mars are predominantly secondary. The distinction from a [primary planetary atmosphere](primary-planetary-atmosphere.md) concerns origin, not merely current molecular weight.
