# Stable sorting

↑ **Parent:** [Sorting algorithm](sorting-algorithm.md)

[Stable sorting](stable-sorting.md) preserves the original relative order of entries with equal keys. The entries may carry different names or other information even when their keys coincide. For example, sorting records $(2,\mathrm{Ada}),(1,\mathrm{Bo}),(2,\mathrm{Cy})$ by the numerical key gives $(1,\mathrm{Bo}),(2,\mathrm{Ada}),(2,\mathrm{Cy})$: Ada remains before Cy. This is useful when a previous ordering contains information worth retaining, such as arrival order among equally ranked requests.
