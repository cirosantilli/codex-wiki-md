# On Poisoning by Diseased Pork by Julius Althaus

↑ **Parent:** [Microbiology and infectious disease bibliography](microbiology-and-infectious-disease-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by Julius Althaus](work-by-julius-althaus.md)

[https://wellcomecollection.org/works/dm2gtp39](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/dm2gtp39)

London: John Churchill and Sons, 1864, 34 pages. Full title continues “being an essay on trichinosis, or flesh-worm disease, its prevention and cure”. Original scan: [https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/0d/On_poisoning_by_diseased_pork_-_being_an_essay_on_trichinosis%2C_or_flesh-worm_disease%2C_its_prevention_and_cure_%28IA_b22267372%29.pdf.](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/0d/On_poisoning_by_diseased_pork_-_being_an_essay_on_trichinosis%2C_or_flesh-worm_disease%2C_its_prevention_and_cure_%28IA_b22267372%29.pdf.) The opening discussion connects pork, trichinae and microscopic examination; the historical remedies are not present treatment advice.
