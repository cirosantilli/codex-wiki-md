# Weighing balance

↑ **Parent:** [Experimental apparatus](experimental-apparatus.md)

An instrument that compares quantities through their mechanical effects, historically central to quantitative [chemistry](chemistry-split.md). Material accounting requires including all relevant substances, not merely weighing a visible solid.
