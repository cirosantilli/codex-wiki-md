<h1 id="1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

For $V\gg|t|$, the leading objective is to avoid adjacent electrons of the same [spin](../../../../spin.md). The unpolarized [Fermi sea](../../../../fermi-sea.md), although optimal within the preceding restricted family, has interaction [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) per site $V(1/2-2/\pi^2)>0$. Its cost grows linearly with $V$, while states with no forbidden neighbouring pairs have zero interaction [energy](../../../../energy-split.md).

There are two independent [spinless fermion t-V chains](../../../../spinless-fermion-t-v-chain.md), one for each [spin](../../../../spin.md). On an even ring, avoiding all neighbouring pairs allows at most $M/2$ particles in each chain. Since the total is $M$, both bounds must be saturated, and each chain must alternate occupied and empty sites. Their two independent choices give **four limiting occupation patterns**:

$$
\boxed{\begin{gathered}
\uparrow,\downarrow,\uparrow,\downarrow,\ldots;\qquad
\downarrow,\uparrow,\downarrow,\uparrow,\ldots;\\
\uparrow\downarrow,0,\uparrow\downarrow,0,\ldots;\qquad
0,\uparrow\downarrow,0,\uparrow\downarrow,\ldots.
\end{gathered}}
$$

The first two are [Néel states](../../../../neel-state.md) with one electron per site; the last two are [charge density waves](../../../../charge-density-wave.md) alternating doubly occupied and empty sites. Opposite-[spin](../../../../spin.md) double occupation costs nothing in this particular [Hamiltonian](../../../../hamiltonian.md). Each species has alternating occupation in all four patterns.

These are the exact zero-interaction [ground states](../../../../ground-state.md) at $t=0$, and the limiting ordered configurations as $V/|t|\to\infty$. At large finite $V/|t|$, [virtual hopping](../../../../virtual-hopping.md) admixes configurations with forbidden pairs, producing an [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) correction of order $-M|t|^2/V$. A finite ring can have [superpositions](../../../../superposition-principle.md) of the ordered configurations, with possible [quantum tunnelling](../../../../quantum-tunnelling.md) splittings; four exactly degenerate product [ground states](../../../../ground-state.md) are not implied at finite hopping. The four ordered choices describe the strong-coupling [thermodynamic limit](../../../../thermodynamic-limit.md), with [spontaneous symmetry breaking](../../../../spontaneous-symmetry-breaking.md) understood.
