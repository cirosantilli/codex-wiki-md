The [Hartree-Fock method](../../../../hartree-fock-method.md) approximates the [ground state](../../../../ground-state.md) [wavefunction](../../../../wave-function-split.md) by a single [Slater determinant](../../../../slater-determinant.md), minimizing the [expectation value](../../../../expectation-value.md) of the [Hamiltonian](../../../../hamiltonian.md) over its occupied [orbitals](../../../../single-particle-orbital.md). It retains [fermionic exchange symmetry](../../../../fermi-dirac-statistics.md) exactly within that determinant but omits [electron correlation](../../../../electron-correlation.md) beyond a single [Slater determinant](../../../../slater-determinant.md).
