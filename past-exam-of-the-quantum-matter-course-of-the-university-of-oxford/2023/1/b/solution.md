<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [quantized field](../../../../quantized-field.md) expansion in the complete [orthonormal basis](../../../../orthonormal-basis.md) of [orbitals](../../../../single-particle-orbital.md) is

$$
\boxed{\hat\psi(\mathbf r)=\sum_n\phi_n(\mathbf r)\hat a_n,\qquad
\hat\psi^\dagger(\mathbf r)=\sum_n\phi_n^*(\mathbf r)\hat a_n^\dagger.}
$$

The complex conjugation in the [creation operator](../../../../creation-operator.md) expansion is essential for complex [orbitals](../../../../single-particle-orbital.md). Indeed, $\hat a_n^\dagger=\int d^3r\,\phi_n(\mathbf r)\hat\psi^\dagger(\mathbf r)$ creates the [wavefunction](../../../../wave-function-split.md) $\phi_n$. The [resolution of the identity in an orthonormal basis](../../../../resolution-of-the-identity-in-an-orthonormal-basis.md) gives

$$
[\hat\psi(\mathbf r),\hat\psi^\dagger(\mathbf r')]
=\sum_n\phi_n(\mathbf r)\phi_n^*(\mathbf r')
=\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf r-\mathbf r'),
$$

recovering the [canonical commutation relation](../../../../canonical-commutation-relation.md) and the spatial [Dirac delta function](../../../../dirac-delta-function.md).
