<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write $h_0=-\hbar^2\nabla^2/(2m)+V(\mathbf r)$ for the one-particle [Hamiltonian operator](../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md). The [second quantization](../../../../second-quantization.md) of the [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md), external [potential energy](../../../../potential-energy.md) and repulsive [contact interaction](../../../../contact-interaction-quantum-mechanics.md) is

$$
\boxed{\hat H=\int d^3r\,\hat\psi^\dagger(\mathbf r)h_0\hat\psi(\mathbf r)
+\frac{U_0}{2}\int d^3r\,\hat\psi^\dagger(\mathbf r)^2\hat\psi(\mathbf r)^2.}
$$

The factor $1/2$ counts each unordered particle pair once. [Normal ordering](../../../../normal-ordering.md) excludes self-interaction: the interaction acts on two distinct [bosons](../../../../boson.md). The fixed [particle number](../../../../particle-number.md) sector has $\hat N=\int d^3r\,\hat\psi^\dagger\hat\psi=N$. In three dimensions, this [contact interaction](../../../../contact-interaction-quantum-mechanics.md) is understood as a regulated low-[energy](../../../../energy-split.md) interaction with a matched coupling, as in [renormalization of a three-dimensional dilute-gas contact coupling](../../../../renormalization-of-a-three-dimensional-dilute-gas-contact-coupling.md); the finite-mode calculation below uses its projected matrix elements.
