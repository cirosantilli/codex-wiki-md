<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $A$ be the real-space area and use $\sum_{\mathbf p}\to C\int d^2p$, with $C=A/(2\pi\hbar)^2$. There is one state per [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) cell, since the [fermions](../../../../fermion.md) are spinless. An annulus between $p$ and $p+dp$ contains $C\,2\pi p\,dp$ states. Near the [Fermi surface](../../../../fermi-surface.md), the [quasiparticle](../../../../quasiparticle.md) [dispersion relation](../../../../dispersion-relation-split.md) has $d\epsilon/dp=v_F^*=p_F/m^*$, so the total [density of states](../../../../density-of-states.md) is

$$
\boxed{D^*(\mu)=C\frac{2\pi p_F}{v_F^*}=\frac{Am^*}{2\pi\hbar^2}.}
$$

The [density of states](../../../../density-of-states.md) per unit area and the occupied-state count are, respectively,

$$
\boxed{g^*(\mu)=\frac{D^*(\mu)}A=\frac{m^*}{2\pi\hbar^2},\qquad
N=C\pi p_F^2=\frac{Ap_F^2}{4\pi\hbar^2}.}
$$

In particular, $D^*(\mu)=2Nm^*/p_F^2$. No factor of two for [spin degeneracy](../../../../spin-degeneracy.md) is present. This counts [Landau quasiparticle](../../../../landau-quasiparticle.md) levels, so it is not multiplied by the [quasiparticle residue](../../../../quasiparticle-residue.md) of the microscopic particle [Green's function](../../../../green-s-function-split.md).
