<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a uniform continuum gas with pair interactions depending on relative positions, a [Galilean boost](../../../../galilean-transformation.md) changes every particle [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) by $p_0\hat{\mathbf x}$ and leaves the interaction [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) unchanged. Write $\hat P_x=\sum_j\hat p_{j,x}$. The transformed many-body [Hamiltonian operator](../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) is

$$
\hat H'=\sum_{j=1}^N\frac{(\hat{\mathbf p}_j+p_0\hat{\mathbf x})^2}{2m}+\hat U
=\hat H+\frac{p_0}{m}\hat P_x+\frac{Np_0^2}{2m}.
$$

The resting [ground state](../../../../ground-state.md) has $\langle\hat P_x\rangle=0$, so the [Galilean boost energy of an interacting gas](../../../../galilean-boost-energy-of-an-interacting-gas.md) is

$$
\boxed{\Delta E=\frac{Np_0^2}{2m}.}
$$

The [mass](../../../../mass.md) here is the bare particle [mass](../../../../mass.md), because this is an exact transformation of the microscopic [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md). Substituting the [Fermi-liquid effective mass](../../../../fermi-liquid-effective-mass.md) $m^*$ would omit the change in interaction [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) encoded by the [Landau quasiparticle energy functional](../../../../landau-quasiparticle-energy-functional.md). This conclusion requires the [Principle of Galilean relativity](../../../../principle-of-galilean-relativity.md); a gas in a lattice or a fixed external [potential energy](../../../../potential-energy.md) landscape need not obey it.
