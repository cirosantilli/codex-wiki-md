<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Within a stable normal [Fermi liquid](../../../../fermi-liquid-split.md), the low-[energy](../../../../energy-split.md) states are described by long-lived [Landau quasiparticles](../../../../landau-quasiparticle.md) and [holes](../../../../electron-hole.md) in a thin shell about the [Fermi surface](../../../../fermi-surface.md); the [Pauli exclusion principle](../../../../pauli-exclusion-principle.md) suppresses their decay. Expanding the [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) about the equilibrium occupations gives the one-[quasiparticle](../../../../quasiparticle.md) [dispersion relation](../../../../dispersion-relation-split.md) at first order and the interaction between occupation changes at second order, namely the [Landau quasiparticle energy functional](../../../../landau-quasiparticle-energy-functional.md). For a smooth interaction, radial deviations in that interaction may be neglected at leading order, leaving its angular dependence; higher occupation terms contribute higher powers of the small shell thickness. Collective occupation deformations are included in the same description. The sufficiency claim is conditional on the normal [Fermi liquid](../../../../fermi-liquid-split.md) regime, and on supplementing this static [energy functional](../../../../energy-functional.md) with dynamics when required: it is not a description of arbitrary low-[energy](../../../../energy-split.md) phases such as a [superconductor](../../../../superconductor.md) or [non-Fermi liquid](../../../../non-fermi-liquid.md).
