# Adiabatic buoyancy frequency of an ideal gas

↑ **Parent:** [Buoyancy frequency](buoyancy-frequency.md)

For a gas of fixed composition in [hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md), with $z$ upward and downward [gravitational acceleration](gravitational-acceleration.md) of magnitude $g$, an [adiabatic process](adiabatic-process.md) gives the displaced element density change $\delta\rho_{\rm element}=\rho\gamma^{-1}(d\ln p/dz)\xi$. Comparing with the ambient change $(d\rho/dz)\xi$ yields

$$
\ddot\xi=-N^2\xi,\qquad N^2=g\left(\frac1\gamma\frac{d\ln p}{dz}-\frac{d\ln\rho}{dz}\right).
$$

The [ideal gas law](ideal-gas-law.md) $p=\rho\mathcal RT$ and $dp/dz=-\rho g$ give the title expression, where $c_p=\gamma\mathcal R/(\gamma-1)$ is the constant-pressure [specific heat capacity](specific-heat-capacity.md). A temperature gradient less negative than the adiabatic lapse rate gives positive $N^2$; a steeper decline gives [convective instability](stellar-convective-instability.md).
