# Weak-star fixed point theorem for an adjoint operator

↑ **Parent:** [Weak-star topology](weak-star-topology.md)

If $T=S^*$ for a [bounded operator](continuous-linear-operator.md) on a separable [Banach space](banach-space-split.md), and $C$ is a nonempty weak-star closed convex subset of the dual [unit ball](unit-ball.md) with $T(C)\subseteq C$, then $T$ has a fixed point in $C$. The [Cesaro averages](cesaro-mean.md) of the orbit remain in $C$, and $T$ minus the identity sends those averages to [vectors](vector.md) of [norm](norm.md) at most $2/n$. Weak-star [compactness](compact-space.md) supplies a cluster point, and the adjoint action is weak-star continuous. The corresponding assertion with only weak closedness is false for the right shift on the probability simplex in $\ell^1$, the dual of $c_0$.
