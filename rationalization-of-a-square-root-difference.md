# Rationalization of a square-root difference

↑ **Parent:** [Square root](square-root.md)

For $u,v\ge0$ with $u+v>0$, multiplication by the sum of the [square roots](square-root.md) gives

$$
\sqrt u-\sqrt v=\frac{u-v}{\sqrt u+\sqrt v}.
$$

This identity exposes small differences hidden by cancellation. For example, $\sqrt{n^2+n}-n=1/(\sqrt{1+1/n}+1)\to1/2$. It also converts normalized square-root increments into terms comparable with the [harmonic series](harmonic-series.md).
