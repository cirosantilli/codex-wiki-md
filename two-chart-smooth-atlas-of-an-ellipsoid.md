# Two-chart smooth atlas of an ellipsoid

↑ **Parent:** [Ellipsoid](ellipsoid.md)

If $D=\operatorname{diag}(a_1,\ldots,a_{n+1})$ is invertible, $x\mapsto Dx$ sends the [ellipsoid](ellipsoid.md) $\sum_i a_i^2x_i^2=1$ to the unit [sphere](sphere.md). On $u=(v,s)\in S^n$, the two [stereographic projections](stereographic-projection.md) $v/(1-s)$ and $v/(1+s)$ omit opposite poles and have inverses $(2w/(1+|w|^2),\pm(|w|^2-1)/(1+|w|^2))$, with the sign chosen for the appropriate pole. Their overlap transition is $w\mapsto w/|w|^2$, smooth and involutive on $\mathbb R^n\setminus\{0\}$. Composing their inverses with $D^{-1}$ gives a two-chart [smooth atlas](smooth-atlas.md) of the ellipsoid. Its [subspace topology](subspace-topology.md) is [Hausdorff](hausdorff-space.md) and has a [second-countable space](second-countable-space.md) topology, so it is a [smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md). For $n=0$ the overlap is empty and each chart is a point.
