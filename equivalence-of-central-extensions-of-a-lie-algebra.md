# Equivalence of central extensions of a Lie algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Central extension of a Lie algebra](central-extension-of-a-lie-algebra.md)

Two [central extensions of a Lie algebra](central-extension-of-a-lie-algebra.md) $0\to\mathfrak z\to E\to\mathfrak g\to0$ and $0\to\mathfrak z\to E'\to\mathfrak g\to0$ are equivalent if a [Lie algebra isomorphism](lie-algebra-isomorphism.md) $E\to E'$ commutes with both maps in the [short exact sequences](short-exact-sequence.md), inducing the identity on the specified $\mathfrak z$ and $\mathfrak g$. This is finer than asking only that $E$ and $E'$ be isomorphic as [Lie algebras](lie-algebra-split.md).

For $\mathfrak z=kz$, choose linear sections and identify the two [vector spaces](vector-space-split.md) with $\mathfrak g\oplus kz$. A [linear map](linear-map.md) compatible with both ends necessarily has the form $(x,uz)\mapsto(x,(u+t(x))z)$ for a [linear functional](linear-functional.md) $t$. If the two [Lie algebra two-cocycles](lie-algebra-two-cocycle.md) are $\alpha,\alpha'$, preservation of the [Lie bracket](lie-bracket.md) is exactly $\alpha'(x,y)=\alpha(x,y)+t([x,y])$. Hence equivalence means that their difference is a [Lie algebra two-coboundary](lie-algebra-two-coboundary.md). If changes of the identifications at the ends are allowed, an [automorphism of a Lie algebra](automorphism-of-a-lie-algebra.md) at either end can identify further classes. For a two-dimensional [abelian Lie algebra](abelian-lie-algebra.md) and a one-dimensional central line, the finer classes are parametrized by $k$, whereas the middle [Lie algebras](lie-algebra-split.md) have only the zero and nonzero classes, represented by the [abelian Lie algebra](abelian-lie-algebra.md) $k^3$ and the [Heisenberg Lie algebra](heisenberg-lie-algebra.md).
