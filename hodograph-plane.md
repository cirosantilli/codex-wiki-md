# Hodograph plane

↑ **Parent:** [Complex velocity](complex-velocity.md)

The hodograph plane records [velocity](velocity.md) rather than position. For a two-dimensional [potential flow](potential-flow.md), the complex coordinate $q=u-iv$ is the [complex velocity](complex-velocity.md). A straight impermeable wall fixes the [velocity](velocity.md) direction and hence maps to a radial line in this plane. A constant-[speed](speed.md) [free streamline](free-streamline.md) maps to a circle centered at zero. Thus a free-[boundary](boundary-of-a-set.md) problem in physical space can become a fixed geometric domain in [velocity](velocity.md) space. Recovering the physical map then uses $dz/dw=1/q(w)$, wherever $q$ is nonzero.
