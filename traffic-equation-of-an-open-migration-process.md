# Traffic equation of an open migration process

↑ **Parent:** [Open migration process](open-migration-process.md)

With row vectors, external arrival rates $\nu$ and transient routing [matrix](matrix.md) $P$, the total arrival rates satisfy $\alpha=\nu+\alpha P=\nu(I-P)^{-1}$. These include all internal migrations and do not depend on colony service speeds.
