# Flexes of a Hesse cubic

↑ **Parent:** [Hessian criterion for flexes of a plane cubic](hessian-criterion-for-flexes-of-a-plane-cubic.md)

For $c\ne0$ and $d^3\ne27c$ in characteristic zero, the displayed [plane cubic](plane-cubic.md) is smooth. Its Hessian determinant is $-6d^2(x^3+y^3+cz^3)+(216c-2d^3)xyz$. On the cubic this becomes $8(27c-d^3)xyz$. Hence all nine [plane cubic flexes](inflection-point-of-a-plane-cubic.md) lie on the coordinate axes: $(1:-\zeta:0)$, $(-\sqrt[3]c\,\zeta:0:1)$ and $(0:-\sqrt[3]c\,\zeta:1)$ for $\zeta^3=1$.
