# R and S configuration

↑ **Parent:** [Absolute configuration](absolute-configuration.md)

For a tetrahedral [stereocenter](stereocenter.md), rank its four [substituents](substituent.md) using [stereochemical sequence rules](stereochemical-sequence-rule.md). With lowest-priority group pointing away, a clockwise $1\to2\to3$ sequence is $R$ and a counterclockwise sequence is $S$. Looking from the other side reverses the apparent sequence, so the viewing condition is essential. The geometric letters A/B/C/D in the [tetrahedral mirror-image test](tetrahedral-mirror-image-test.md) carry no assigned priority. Neither $R$ nor $S$ determines positive or negative [optical rotation](optical-rotation.md).[McMurry, Organic Chemistry, §5.5, Figure 5.8 and discussion after Figure 5.10](organic-chemistry-by-john-mcmurry/5-5-sequence-rules-for-specifying-configuration.md)
