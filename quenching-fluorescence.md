# Quenching (fluorescence)

↑ **Parent:** [Fluorescence](fluorescence-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quenching_(fluorescence))

A reduction in [fluorescence](fluorescence-split.md) caused by a process competing with [photon emission](photon-emission.md), for example a collision that removes excitation [energy](energy-split.md) without emitting a [photon](photon.md). Measuring less [light](light.md) because fewer excitation [photons](photon.md) reach the sample does not by itself establish fluorescence quenching.
