# Length of a curve

↑ **Parent:** [Arc length](arc-length.md)

The length of a piecewise smooth [curve](curve.md) is its [arc length](arc-length.md): $L(\alpha)=\int\|\alpha'(t)\|\,dt$, using either the [Euclidean space](euclidean-norm.md) [norm](norm.md) or the [norm](norm.md) from a specified [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric-split.md). A straight segment from $p$ to $q$ in [Euclidean space](euclidean-norm.md) has [length](length.md) $\|q-p\|$, while a route that bends or retraces can be longer. On a [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md), comparing these [lengths](length.md) over all routes defines [Riemannian distance](riemannian-distance.md).
