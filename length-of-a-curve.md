# Length of a curve

↑ **Parent:** [Arc length](arc-length.md)

For a piecewise smooth [curve](curve.md), $L(\alpha)=\int\|\alpha^{\prime}(t)\|dt$ in an ambient Euclidean space, or the corresponding [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md) norm on a manifold. This is invariant under regular monotone reparametrization.
