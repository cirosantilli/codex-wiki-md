# Fully symmetric representation

↑ **Parent:** [Trivial representation](trivial-representation.md)

The [trivial representation](trivial-representation.md) of a molecular point group: every symmetry operation is represented by $1$. Labels such as $A_1$ or $A_{1g}$ depend on the group and its labelling convention.
