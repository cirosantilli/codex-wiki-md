<h1 id="integral-bounds-for-euler-s-constant">Integral bounds for Euler's constant</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Euler's constant](euler-s-constant.md)

For the [harmonic number](harmonic-number.md) $H_N$, the upper sequence $u_N=H_N-\ln N$ decreases and the lower sequence $v_N=H_N-\ln(N+1)$ increases. The inequalities $\ln(1+1/N)>1/(N+1)$ and $\ln(1+1/(N+1))<1/(N+1)$ follow by integrating the decreasing function $1/x$ over a unit interval. They give the two monotonicities, while $u_N-v_N=\ln(1+1/N)\to0$. The [monotone convergence theorem for sequences](bounded-monotone-sequence-theorem.md) therefore gives the common limit [Euler's constant](euler-s-constant.md) $\gamma$. Strict monotonicity makes each finite-index bound strict. At $N=1$, this gives $0<1-\ln2<\gamma<1$, since $\ln2<1$.
