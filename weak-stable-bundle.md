# Weak stable bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Stable bundle of an Anosov flow](stable-bundle-of-an-anosov-flow.md)

The weak stable bundle adjoins the flow direction to the strong stable bundle. It is tangent to the weak stable [foliation](foliation.md). For a [geodesic flow](geodesic-flow.md) on a [hyperbolic surface](hyperbolic-surface.md), the canonical frame gives $E^{ws}=\operatorname{span}\{X,H-V\}$.
