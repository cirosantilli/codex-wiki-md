# Injectivity of sums on two distinct rays

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean ray](euclidean-ray.md)

If finite point sets $P,Q$ lie on two distinct [Euclidean rays](euclidean-ray.md) in a plane, the map $(p,q)\mapsto p+q$ is [injective](injective-function.md) because the two direction vectors are [linearly independent](linear-independence.md). Thus $|P+Q|=|P||Q|$. If all points are nonzero and the rays have positive slope, every sum lies in the [open planar sector](open-planar-sector.md) between those rays.
