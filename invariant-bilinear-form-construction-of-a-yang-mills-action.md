# Invariant bilinear-form construction of a Yang-Mills action

↑ **Parent:** [Yang-Mills theory](yang-mills-theory.md)

If a [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) has an invariant symmetric nondegenerate form $h$, its curvature $F$ transforms in the [Adjoint representation](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md) and $\mathcal L=-h(F_{\mu\nu},F^{\mu\nu})/4$ is gauge invariant. Infinitesimal invariance follows from $h([\theta,X],Y)+h(X,[\theta,Y])=0$. For compact generators with $\operatorname{tr}(T^aT^b)=\delta^{ab}/2$, this gives $-\operatorname{tr}(F_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu})/2=-F^a_{\mu\nu}F^{a\mu\nu}/4$. An arbitrary index contraction is not gauge invariant for an arbitrary [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md).
