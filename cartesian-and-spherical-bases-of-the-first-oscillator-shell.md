# Cartesian and spherical bases of the first oscillator shell

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md)

In the isotropic three-dimensional [quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md), the first excited Cartesian [energy eigenstates](energy-eigenstate.md) are proportional to $x,y,z$ times the common Gaussian $e^{-r^2/(2a_0^2)}$. If $\phi_x,\phi_y,\phi_z$ are normalized with the same real phase convention, their combinations in the title are normalized [angular momentum eigenstates](angular-momentum-eigenstate.md) with $l=1$ and $m=1,0,-1$.

Indeed, $z=r\cos\theta$ and $x\pm iy=r\sin\theta e^{\pm i\varphi}$ identify a common radial factor times the [spherical harmonics](spherical-harmonic.md) $Y_{10},Y_{1,\pm1}$. The transformation is unitary, so it changes the basis rather than the threefold orbital degeneracy.
