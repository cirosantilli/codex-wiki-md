# Algebraic closure of the p-adic numbers is not complete

↑ **Parent:** [Completion of a valued field](completion-of-a-valued-field.md)

Choose compatible primitive [roots of unity](root-of-unity.md) $\zeta_{p^{n+1}}$. The partial sums $A_N=\sum_{n=0}^Np^{2n}\zeta_{p^{n+1}}$ form a [Cauchy sequence](cauchy-sequence.md). Each $A_N$ generates $\mathbb Q_p(\zeta_{p^{N+1}})$: for a nonidentity cyclotomic automorphism with $r=v_p(a-1)$, the first changed term has valuation $2r+1/(p-1)$, strictly below every later changed term. Thus the conjugate distance is at least $p^{-2N-1/(p-1)}$. The tail bound is $p^{-2N-2}$, strictly smaller. If the limit were algebraic, [Krasner's lemma](krasner-s-lemma.md) would place every one of these unbounded-degree cyclotomic fields in one finite extension, a contradiction.
