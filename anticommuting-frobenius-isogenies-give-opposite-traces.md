# Anticommuting Frobenius isogenies give opposite traces

↑ **Parent:** [Frobenius isogeny of an elliptic curve](frobenius-isogeny-of-an-elliptic-curve.md)

If an [isogeny of elliptic curves](isogeny-of-elliptic-curves.md) $\pi:E_1\to E_2$ satisfies $\pi\phi_1=-\phi_2\pi$ for their [Frobenius isogenies](frobenius-isogeny-of-an-elliptic-curve.md), then $\pi(1-\phi_1)=(1+\phi_2)\pi$. Multiplicativity of the [degree of an isogeny](degree-of-an-isogeny.md) gives $\deg(1-\phi_1)=\deg(1+\phi_2)$. The [degree parallelogram law](divisor-proof-of-the-degree-parallelogram-law.md) then gives the displayed point-count identity, since $\deg(1-\phi_i)=\#E_i(\mathbb F_q)$ and $\deg\phi_i=q$.
