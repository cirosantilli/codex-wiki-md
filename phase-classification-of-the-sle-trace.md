# Phase classification of the SLE trace

↑ **Parent:** [Schramm–Loewner evolution](schramm-loewner-evolution.md)

The trace of $\operatorname{SLE}_\kappa$ is [simple](simple-curve.md) for $0<\kappa\leq4$, has self-intersections without being [space-filling](space-filling-curve.md) for $4<\kappa<8$, and is space-filling for $\kappa\geq8$. The thresholds arise from the [Boundary-point Bessel flow for SLE](boundary-point-bessel-flow-for-sle.md) and estimates for the [Loewner chain](loewner-chain.md) near its driving point.
