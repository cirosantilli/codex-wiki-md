# Thin-film transistor

↑ **Parent:** [Field-effect transistor](field-effect-transistor.md)

A transistor whose active semiconductor channel is a thin film on a supporting substrate. Semiconductor purity, channel continuity, gate [dielectric](dielectric.md) and contacts jointly determine switching; a high intrinsic [charge carrier mobility](charge-carrier-mobility.md) alone does not guarantee a uniform transistor array.
