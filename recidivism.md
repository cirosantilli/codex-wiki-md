# Recidivism

↑ **Parent:** [Criminal behavior](criminal-behavior.md)

Repetition of offending after an earlier offence or intervention, operationalised over a specified follow-up period. Rearrest is one imperfect indicator affected by detection and policing as well as behaviour.
