# Lambda model

↑ **Parent:** [Lambda calculus](lambda-calculus.md)

A lambda model interprets variables, [lambda application](lambda-application.md) and [lambda abstraction](lambda-abstraction.md) so that [capture-avoiding substitution](capture-avoiding-substitution.md) agrees with environments and all [beta equivalence](beta-equivalence.md) equations hold. A [reflexive domain](reflexive-domain.md) gives such an interpretation. Validity of [eta conversion](eta-conversion.md) requires an extra inverse law and does not follow merely from a retraction.
