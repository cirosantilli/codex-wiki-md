# Cyclotron dee

↑ **Parent:** [Cyclotron](cyclotron.md)

A hollow D-shaped electrode that shields a circulating [ion](ion.md) from the accelerating [electric field](electric-field-split.md) during one half-turn. Two opposed cyclotron dees provide two shielded regions separated by a gap; their [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) alternates. The [vacuum](vacuum.md) reduces collisions with residual gas. An [ion source](ion-source.md) supplies particles near the center, and an extraction system directs an outer orbit toward a [particle detector](particle-detector.md) or target. Useful [electric current](electric-current.md) depends on retaining particles near the median plane and delivering them to the outlet, as well as on increasing their [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md).
