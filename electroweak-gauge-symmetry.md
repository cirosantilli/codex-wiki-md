# Electroweak gauge symmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Electroweak interaction](electroweak-interaction.md)

The local gauge structure of the [Standard Model](standard-model-split.md) electroweak sector. Left-chiral [fermions](fermion.md) form weak-isospin doublets and hypercharge assignments determine the electromagnetic generator $Q=T_3+Y$ in one common normalization. A chosen [Higgs field](higgs-field.md) vacuum leaves the electromagnetic subgroup manifest. Gauge transformations remain a redundancy of the description; physical vector masses are gauge-invariant observables.
