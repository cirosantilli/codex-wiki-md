# Exactly two composition series for a simple group times a prime cyclic group

↑ **Parent:** [Composition series](composition-series.md)

The only nontrivial proper [normal subgroups](normal-subgroup.md) of this [direct product of groups](direct-product-of-groups.md) are its two factors. Indeed a [normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md) meeting $S\times1$ nontrivially contains it by the [simple group](simple-group.md) property. Otherwise each of its commutators with $S\times1$ lies in that trivial intersection. Its first coordinates therefore lie in $Z(S)=1$, so it is contained in $1\times C_p$. Both factors are simple and each quotient by one factor is isomorphic to the other. Hence precisely two [composition series](composition-series.md) occur, one through each factor. This is compatible with the [Jordan–Hölder theorem](jordan-holder-theorem.md), since the two series have the same simple factors in opposite orders. For $S=A_5,p=2$, the [group](group-split.md) has order $120$.
