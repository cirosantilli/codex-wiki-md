# Temperature-linear pressure and volume-independent heat capacity

↑ **Parent:** [Heat capacity at constant volume](heat-capacity-at-constant-volume.md)

For a smooth simple compressible system, $C_V=T(\partial S/\partial T)_V$ and the [Maxwell relation](maxwell-relations.md) $(\partial S/\partial V)_T=(\partial p/\partial T)_V$ give $(\partial C_V/\partial V)_T=T(\partial^2p/\partial T^2)_V$. Thus an [equation of state](equation-of-state.md) $p=a(V)T+b(V)$ implies that $C_V$ depends on [temperature](temperature-split.md) alone within a smooth [phase](phase-waves.md). It does not require $C_V$ to be [temperature](temperature-split.md) [independent](independent-random-variables.md).
