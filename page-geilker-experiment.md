<h1 id="page-geilker-experiment">Page–Geilker experiment</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Semiclassical Einstein equation](semiclassical-einstein-equation.md)

The Page–Geilker experiment used a quantum random decision to choose a macroscopic source-mass configuration and measured its field with a torsion balance. The observed field followed the configuration in the observed branch rather than the nearly cancelling expectation-value average predicted by the simplest no-collapse [semiclassical Einstein equation](semiclassical-einstein-equation.md).

[https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.47.979](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.47.979)
