# Finite-cofinite Boolean algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Boolean algebra](boolean-algebra.md)

On an [infinite set](infinite-set.md) $D$, take its finite and cofinite [subsets](subset.md) with ordinary [union](set-union.md), [intersection](set-intersection.md) and complement. [Singletons](singleton-mathematics.md) are its [atoms](atom-of-a-boolean-algebra.md). Its Stone space consists of the isolated points of $D$ together with one nonisolated point whose [ultrafilter](ultrafilter.md) contains all cofinite [sets](set-split.md). For countably infinite $D$ this is a [countable](countable-set.md) [Boolean algebra](boolean-algebra.md) with countably many [atoms](atom-of-a-boolean-algebra.md), not the uncountable full [power set](power-set.md). It describes two-vector definability over a countably infinite [field](field.md): dependent directions are isolated [atoms](atom-of-a-boolean-algebra.md) and the independent-pair type is the additional nonisolated point.
