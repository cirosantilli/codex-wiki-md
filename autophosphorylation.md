# Autophosphorylation

↑ **Parent:** [Protein kinase](protein-kinase.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autophosphorylation)

[Autophosphorylation](autophosphorylation.md) is [phosphorylation](phosphorylation.md) of a [protein kinase](protein-kinase.md) by its own catalytic activity or that of another member of the same [protein kinase](protein-kinase.md) assembly. Distinguish modification within one chain from [trans-autophosphorylation](trans-autophosphorylation.md) between partners.
