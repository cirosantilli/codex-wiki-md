# Matrix pencil

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix](matrix.md)

A matrix pencil is an affine one-parameter family of [matrices](matrix.md). For square matrices of size $m$, its determinant is a [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) of degree at most $m$. If $B$ is invertible, its leading coefficient is $\det B\ne0$, so at most $m$ distinct parameters give a singular matrix. This translates linear dependence in a family of bases into polynomial root counting.
