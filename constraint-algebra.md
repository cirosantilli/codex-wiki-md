# Constraint algebra

↑ **Parent:** [First-class constraint](first-class-constraint.md)

The [Poisson brackets](poisson-bracket.md) of [first-class constraints](first-class-constraint.md) close on the constraints. When the constraints are independent and the coefficients are constant, the [Jacobi identity for the Poisson bracket](jacobi-identity-for-the-poisson-bracket.md) makes the coefficients [structure constants](structure-constant.md) of a [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md).
