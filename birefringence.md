# Birefringence

↑ **Parent:** [Linear anisotropic dielectric](linear-anisotropic-dielectric.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birefringence)

[Birefringence](birefringence.md) is the existence of different optical propagation constants for different eigenpolarizations in an anisotropic material. It provides the propagation-mode separation needed for [adiabatic optical following in a twisted nematic](adiabatic-optical-following-in-a-twisted-nematic.md).

Two different [refractive indices](refractive-index.md) can produce two displaced images, as in the [calcite double-image experiment](calcite-double-image-experiment.md), or a [phase retardation](phase-retardation.md) between overlapping components, as in a [waveplate](waveplate.md). Spatial separation concerns where [energy flux](energy-flux.md) travels; [phase retardation](phase-retardation.md) concerns the relative [wave phase](phase-waves.md). A specimen need not show two visibly separated images to have [birefringence](birefringence.md).
