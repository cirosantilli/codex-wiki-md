# Orientation of a smooth manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Orientable smooth manifold](orientable-smooth-manifold.md)

An orientation of a [smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md) is a continuous choice of [orientation of a vector space](orientation-of-a-vector-space.md) in every [tangent space](tangent-space.md), equivalently an [orientation of a vector bundle](orientation-of-a-vector-bundle.md) on its [tangent bundle](tangent-bundle.md). It can be specified by an atlas with positive [Jacobian determinants](jacobian-determinant.md) on all overlaps or by a nowhere-vanishing top-degree [differential form](differential-form-split.md). On a connected orientable manifold there are two choices, interchanged by reversing every tangent-space orientation.
