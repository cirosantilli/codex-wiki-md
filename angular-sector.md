# Angular sector

↑ **Parent:** [Complex plane](complex-plane.md)

The region between two rays with a common vertex, with no upper bound on the [modulus](modulus.md). In the displayed convention the vertex and boundary rays are excluded. A [holomorphic logarithm](holomorphic-logarithm.md) with $\alpha<\arg z<\beta$ defines powers $z^p=e^{p\operatorname{Log}z}$ there. For real $p>0$ with $p(\beta-\alpha)\leq2\pi$, the power is a [conformal bijection](biholomorphism.md) onto the [sector](angular-sector.md) $p\alpha<\arg w<p\beta$: it multiplies [arguments](argument-complex-analysis.md) by $p$ and sends [moduli](modulus.md) bijectively by $r\mapsto r^p$.
