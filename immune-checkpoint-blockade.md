# Immune checkpoint blockade

↑ **Parent:** [Immunology](immunology.md)

Treatment that interferes with inhibitory immune regulatory interactions, such as [PD-1](pd-1.md)/[PD-L1](pd-l1.md) or CTLA-4-associated restraint. It requires a usable antitumour recognition and effector system.
