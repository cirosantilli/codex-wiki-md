# Product topology

↑ **Parent:** [Geometry and topology](geometry-and-topology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Product_topology)

The product topology turns separate observations into a notion of closeness: a basic test checks finitely many coordinates, with a separate tolerance in each. For an [index set](index-set.md) $I$ and [topological spaces](topological-space.md) $X_i$, the underlying [Cartesian product](cartesian-product.md) and its coordinate [projection maps](projection-map.md) are

$$
X=\prod_{i\in I}X_i=\{(x_i)_{i\in I}:x_i\in X_i\},\qquad
\pi_i:X\to X_i,\quad\pi_i(x)=x_i.
$$

The product topology is the coarsest [topology](topology-split.md) making every $\pi_i$ a [continuous map](continuous-map.md).[Definition 2.2.4 and Proposition 2.2.5: coordinate projections and the coarsest topology making them continuous](point-set-topology-by-romyar-sharifi/point-set-topology-chapter-2-continuous-functions.md)

The [Cartesian product](cartesian-product.md) specifies which points exist; the [product topology](product-topology.md) specifies their [neighborhoods](neighbourhood-mathematics.md). Finite observations motivate its [finite-coordinate basis for the product topology](finite-coordinate-basis-for-the-product-topology.md), while [convergence in a product space](convergence-in-a-product-space.md) and [continuity into a product space](continuity-into-a-product-space.md) explain why it is useful. The [compactness](compact-space.md) result for this construction is [Tychonoff's theorem](tychonoff-s-theorem.md).
