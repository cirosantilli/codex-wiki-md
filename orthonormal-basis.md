# Orthonormal basis

↑ **Parent:** [Orthonormal set](orthonormal-set.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orthonormal_basis)

An orthonormal basis of a [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) is an [orthonormal set](orthonormal-set.md) whose [linear span](linear-span.md) has the whole space as its [closure](closure-topology.md). This [completeness of an orthonormal system](completeness-of-an-orthonormal-system.md) allows every vector to be expanded in the basis vectors, with convergence in norm. In finite dimension it is also a [basis](basis.md) in the finite-linear-combination sense.
