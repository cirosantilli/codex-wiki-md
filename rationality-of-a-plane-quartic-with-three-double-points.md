# Rationality of a plane quartic with three double points

↑ **Parent:** [Plane curve singularity multiplicity bound](plane-curve-singularity-multiplicity-bound.md)

Three distinct points of multiplicity two use the entire [arithmetic genus](arithmetic-genus.md) three of an [integral projective curve](integral-projective-curve.md) of degree four. One point blowup at each gives [arithmetic genus](arithmetic-genus.md) zero. The [normalization of an algebraic curve](normalization-of-an-algebraic-curve-split.md) then has genus zero and is the [projective line](projective-line.md) over an [algebraically closed field](algebraically-closed-field.md), by [rationality of a smooth projective genus-zero curve](rationality-of-a-smooth-projective-genus-zero-curve.md). This allows double points with one branch as well as ordinary nodes.
