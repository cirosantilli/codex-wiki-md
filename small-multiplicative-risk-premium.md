# Small multiplicative risk premium

↑ **Parent:** [Relative risk aversion coefficient](relative-risk-aversion-coefficient.md)

Compare expected utility of $W(1+\phi)$, where $\mathbb E\phi=0$, with the certainty equivalent $W(1-\psi)$. A second-order [Taylor expansion](taylor-expansion.md) gives $U(W)+W^2U''(W)\mathbb E\phi^2/2$ for the risky side and $U(W)-WU'(W)\psi$ for the sure side. Equality gives the displayed proportional [risk premium](risk-premium.md). This requires a genuinely small-shock family with controlled remainders; small variance alone does not control rare large wealth shocks.
