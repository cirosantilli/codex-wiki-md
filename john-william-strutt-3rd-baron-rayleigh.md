# John William Strutt, 3rd Baron Rayleigh

↑ **Parent:** [List of physicists](list-of-physicists.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_William_Strutt,_3rd_Baron_Rayleigh)

A [physicist](physicist-split.md) whose research joined [waves](wave-split.md), [optics](optics-split.md), [acoustics](acoustics.md) and precise measurement. He maintained a laboratory at Terling and served as Cavendish professor of experimental [physics](physics-split.md) from 1879 to 1884. At Cambridge he developed laboratory courses; later he donated his Nobel award proceeds toward a Cavendish laboratory extension.[O'Connor and Robertson, biography: Terling laboratory; Cavendish professorship and laboratory courses; donation of Nobel proceeds](john-william-strutt-lord-rayleigh-by-john-joseph-o-connor-and-edmund-frederick-robertson.md)

The [Rayleigh-Ramsay argon density experiment](rayleigh-ramsay-argon-density-experiment.md), pursued with [William Ramsay](william-ramsay.md), connects a small discrepancy in gas weighing to chemical separation. Its lesson is practical: agreement within one preparation method does not ensure that a second method produces the same [chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md). The [gas density](gas-density.md) model explains how composition and filling conditions both affect the readout.
