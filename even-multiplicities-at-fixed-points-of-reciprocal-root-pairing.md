# Even multiplicities at fixed points of reciprocal root pairing

↑ **Parent:** [Reciprocal polynomial](reciprocal-polynomial.md)

Let $P$ be a nonzero [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) of even degree $2m$ over a [field](field.md) of [characteristic](characteristic-of-a-field.md) different from two, satisfying the displayed reciprocal identity. If $P(x)=(x-1)^rA(x)$ with $A(1)\ne0$, cancellation gives $A(x)=(-1)^r x^{2m-r}A(1/x)$. Substitution of $x=1$ forces $r$ even. If $P(x)=(x+1)^sB(x)$ with $B(-1)\ne0$, cancellation gives $B(x)=x^{2m-s}B(1/x)$; substitution of $x=-1$ forces $s$ even. Zero multiplicities are included. Pairing nonfixed reciprocal roots alone cannot prove this parity, because $1$ and $-1$ are their own inverses.
