# Clamped second-order Sobolev space

↑ **Parent:** [Sobolev space](sobolev-space-split.md)

This is the closure of the compactly supported [test functions](test-function.md) $C_c^\infty(U)$ in the $H^2(U)$ [norm](norm.md). On a smooth bounded domain it consists precisely of the $H^2$ functions with zero value and zero [normal derivative](normal-derivative.md) traces. Tangential first derivatives then vanish as well. The [clamped Hessian identity](clamped-hessian-identity.md) makes the [Laplacian](laplacian.md) [norm](norm.md) equivalent to the full $H^2$ [norm](norm.md) on this space, enabling a [Lax-Milgram theorem](lax-milgram-theorem.md) treatment of the [clamped biharmonic problem](clamped-biharmonic-problem.md).
