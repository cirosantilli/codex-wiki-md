# In-line holography

↑ **Parent:** [Holography](holography.md)

[In-line holography](in-line-holography.md) uses nearly the same direction for the [holographic reference wave](holographic-reference-wave.md) and [holographic object wave](holographic-object-wave.md). In a transmission geometry, the undiffracted illumination supplies $R$ while the object supplies the scattered component $O$. This makes small [optical path differences](optical-path-difference.md) possible, but leaves the desired image, its [twin image in holography](twin-image-in-holography.md) and the bright background sharing an axis.

[Dennis Gabor](dennis-gabor.md)'s electron-microscopy motivation led to optical tests: one line of a high-pressure [mercury-vapor lamp](mercury-vapor-lamp.md) illuminated a 3 μm [pinhole](optical-pinhole.md), then approximately 1 mm [microphotographs](microphotograph.md), recording approximately 1 cm [holograms](hologram.md) on sensitive [photographic emulsions](photographic-emulsion.md) with minute-scale exposures. The roughly 0.1 mm [optical coherence length](optical-coherence-length.md) favoured an in-line arrangement. Reillumination reconstructed images; focusing separated their depths incompletely. Distorted letters and random optical disturbances limited the results. These were optical demonstrations, not an operating [electron microscope](electron-microscope.md).[Gabor, Holography, 1948-1971, Nobel lecture, Figs. 2–6 and accompanying optical-experiment account; authored transcription inspected at [https://studylib.net/doc/28317456/dennis-gabor-nobel-lecture](https://studylib.net/doc/28317456/dennis-gabor-nobel-lecture)](holography-1948-1971-by-dennis-gabor.md)

The recorded [microphotograph](microphotograph.md) and its reconstructed lettering provide an object-to-image comparison in that account. The blocked-reference control described above is an explanatory control for the model; it is not a claim about an additional historical measurement.
