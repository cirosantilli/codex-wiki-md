# Tensor-product operator

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor product of linear maps](tensor-product-of-linear-maps.md)

A tensor-product operator factors as $A\otimes B$ on a [tensor product](tensor-product.md) of two spaces. If both factors are [positive semidefinite operators](positive-operator.md), their tensor-product operator is positive: a [tensor-product basis](tensor-product-basis.md) of eigenvectors has eigenvalues $a_ib_j\geq0$. This positivity underlies the necessary direction of the [positive partial transpose criterion](positive-partial-transpose-criterion.md).
