# Incommensurate harmonic-oscillator orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Quasiperiodic motion](quasiperiodic-motion.md)

For two uncoupled [harmonic oscillators](simple-harmonic-motion.md) with nonzero amplitudes and irrational frequency ratio $\omega_1/\omega_2$, the orbit is bounded but not [periodic](periodic-orbit.md). It winds quasiperiodically on a two-dimensional [invariant torus](invariant-torus.md); a common period would force the frequency ratio to be rational.
