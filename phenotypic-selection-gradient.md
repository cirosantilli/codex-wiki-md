# Phenotypic selection gradient

↑ **Parent:** [Quantitative genetics](quantitative-genetics.md)

A [vector](vector.md) describing direct selection on measured [phenotypic traits](phenotypic-trait.md), conditional on the other measured traits. In a local deterministic fitness model it can be written $\boldsymbol\beta=\nabla_{\mathbf z}\log w$; population regression estimates require their own sampling and fitness assumptions. This differs in context from a rare-mutant invasion [selection gradient](selection-gradient.md).
