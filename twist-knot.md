# Twist knot

↑ **Parent:** [Knot](knot.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twist_knot)

A twist knot is obtained by closing two strands with a clasp after inserting a row of half-twists. Every nontrivial twist knot has [Seifert genus](seifert-genus.md) one; a standard reduced alternating diagram with $m$ crossings in the twist region has $m+2$ crossings.
