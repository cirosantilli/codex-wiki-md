<h1 id="stone-cech-compactification">Stone-Čech compactification</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Compactification (physics)](compactification-physics.md)

For a [completely regular Hausdorff space](completely-regular-hausdorff-space.md), the Stone-Čech compactification is the weak-star closure of the evaluation functionals in the dual unit ball of $C_b(X)$. The [Banach-Alaoglu theorem](banach-alaoglu-theorem.md) gives compactness; complete regularity makes evaluation a [topological embedding](topological-embedding.md). Every bounded real [continuous function](continuous-function.md) extends by $\widetilde f(p)=p(f)$, yielding an isometric isomorphism $C_b(X)\cong C(\beta X)$. For a continuous map into a [compact Hausdorff space](compact-hausdorff-space.md) $K$, the adjoint of pullback sends this closure into the closed evaluation copy of $K$, yielding the unique continuous extension. Uniqueness follows from density. It is a universal compactification, not a homeomorphism between $X$ and the whole dual space.
