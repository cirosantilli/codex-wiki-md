# Electric double layer

↑ **Parent:** [Electrostatics](electrostatics-split.md)

An [electric double layer](electric-double-layer.md) consists of an interfacial [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) and the compensating distribution of mobile [ions](ion.md) in the adjacent [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md). [Counterions](counterion.md) are enriched and [coions](coion.md) depleted near a simple charged interface. A diffuse layer is described approximately by the [Poisson-Boltzmann equation](poisson-boltzmann-equation.md); a compact specifically adsorbed layer requires additional surface chemistry. Overlap of diffuse layers can produce a screened repulsion between like-charged [colloids](colloid.md).
