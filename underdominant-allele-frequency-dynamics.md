# Underdominant allele-frequency dynamics

↑ **Parent:** [Underdominance](underdominance.md)

With [homozygote](homozygote.md) fitness advantages $a,b>0$ over the [heterozygote](heterozygote.md), the [allele frequency](allele-frequency.md) $u$ of the first [allele](allele.md) has an unstable threshold $b/(a+b)$. Below it that [allele](allele.md) decreases; above it it increases. In a discrete viability-selection generation the same numerator gives $u'-u$ after division by [mean](expected-value.md) [genotype fitness](genotype-fitness.md). Equal [homozygote](homozygote.md) advantages give $\dot u=su(1-u)(2u-1)$, with stable endpoints and unstable midpoint.
