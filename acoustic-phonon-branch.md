# Acoustic phonon branch

↑ **Parent:** [Phonon dispersion relation](phonon-dispersion-relation.md)

An acoustic phonon branch has $\omega(k)\to0$ as $k\to0$. Its long-wavelength [group velocity](group-velocity.md) is the [speed of sound](speed-of-sound.md).
