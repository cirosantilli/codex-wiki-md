# Matrix trace

↑ **Parent:** [Linear algebra](linear-algebra-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trace (linear algebra))

For an $n$-by-$n$ [square matrix](square-matrix.md) $A=(a_{ij})$ over a [field](field.md), its matrix trace is the sum of its diagonal entries:

$$
\operatorname{tr}A=\sum_{i=1}^n a_{ii}.
$$

For example, $A=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix}$ has [trace](matrix-trace.md) $2+1=3$. Adding corresponding diagonal entries immediately proves [linearity](linearity.md):

$$
\operatorname{tr}(\alpha A+\beta B)=\alpha\operatorname{tr}A+\beta\operatorname{tr}B.
$$

Thus [matrix trace](matrix-trace.md) is a [linear functional](linear-functional.md) on the [vector space](vector-space-split.md) of $n$-by-$n$ [matrices](matrix.md).[Axler, Linear Algebra Done Right, fourth edition, §8D, definition 8.47 and result 8.56](linear-algebra-done-right-by-sheldon-axler-4th-edition.md)

The surprising point is that the sum is intrinsic to a [linear operator](linear-operator.md), even though individual diagonal entries depend on the [basis](basis.md). The [cyclic property of the trace](cyclic-property-of-the-trace.md) gives, for any [invertible matrix](invertible-matrix.md) $P$,

$$
\operatorname{tr}(P^{-1}AP)=\operatorname{tr}(APP^{-1})=\operatorname{tr}A.
$$

Hence [matrix similarity](matrix-similarity.md) preserves [trace](matrix-trace.md). For the example above, choose the [change-of-basis matrix](change-of-basis-matrix.md) $P=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$. Then

$$
P^{-1}AP=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&2\end{pmatrix}:
\qquad 1+2=2+1=3.
$$

The diagonal entries have changed but their sum has not. This makes [trace](matrix-trace.md) useful for extracting a feature of the underlying [linear operator](linear-operator.md) without finding a preferred [basis](basis.md).

Over the [complex numbers](complex-number.md), or over the [real numbers](real-number.md) after allowing complex [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md), the [trace](matrix-trace.md) equals their sum with [algebraic multiplicity](algebraic-multiplicity.md):

$$
\operatorname{tr}A=\lambda_1+\cdots+\lambda_n.
$$

No [diagonalizable matrix](diagonalizable-matrix.md) hypothesis is needed.[Axler, Linear Algebra Done Right, fourth edition, §8D, results 8.50–8.52](linear-algebra-done-right-by-sheldon-axler-4th-edition.md) To see why, use [triangularization over an algebraically closed field](triangularization-over-an-algebraically-closed-field.md): an [upper triangular matrix](upper-triangular-matrix.md) similar to $A$ has the [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) on its diagonal, counted with [algebraic multiplicity](algebraic-multiplicity.md), because its [characteristic polynomial](characteristic-polynomial.md) is $\prod_i(z-\lambda_i)$. [Similarity](matrix-similarity.md) preserves [trace](matrix-trace.md), so summing that diagonal proves the formula.

For instance,

$$
J=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\0&2\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\det(zI-J)=(z-2)^2,\qquad\operatorname{tr}J=2+2=4.
$$

There is only one independent [eigenvector](eigenvector.md), since $(J-2I)(x,y)^T=(y,0)^T$, so $J$ is not a [diagonalizable matrix](diagonalizable-matrix.md). Nevertheless its repeated [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) contributes twice. For our earlier $A$, the [characteristic polynomial](characteristic-polynomial.md) is $z^2-3z+1$, its [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are $(3+\sqrt5)/2$ and $(3-\sqrt5)/2$, and their sum is $3$. An inexpensive diagonal sum therefore gives spectral information before solving the [characteristic polynomial](characteristic-polynomial.md).

The [trace](matrix-trace.md) of a real [matrix](matrix.md) does not give that [linear map](linear-map.md)'s [volume](volume.md) factor: that factor is the [absolute value](absolute-value.md) of its [determinant](determinant.md). For example, $2I_2$ has [trace](matrix-trace.md) $4$ and [area](surface-area.md) factor $4$, but $2I_3$ has [trace](matrix-trace.md) $6$ and [volume](volume.md) factor $8$. Their occasional numerical agreement in two dimensions is a coincidence. The connection between [trace and local volume change](trace-and-local-volume-change.md) gives a geometric interpretation near the [identity matrix](identity-matrix.md), and describes the [volume](volume.md) rate of a [flow map](flow-map.md) generated by a [linear system of differential equations](linear-system-of-differential-equations.md).
