# Near-wall radiative heating layer

↑ **Parent:** [Plane Poiseuille flow](plane-poiseuille-flow.md)

For a wall shear $u\simeq sz$, heating over streamwise length $L$ gives an outer [temperature](temperature-split.md) rise proportional to $L/(sz)$. Its transverse [thermal conduction](thermal-conduction.md) grows as $z^{-3}$ relative to the local source. If $\chi$ is [thermal diffusivity](thermal-diffusivity.md), comparing conduction with heating gives $\delta\sim(\chi L/s)^{1/3}$, up to a numerical constant depending on the precise comparison. The same scale follows by comparing transverse diffusion time $z^2/\chi$ with residence time $L/(sz)$.
