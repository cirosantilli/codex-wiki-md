# Perpendicular plasma pressure

↑ **Parent:** [Pressure tensor](pressure-tensor.md)

The equal normal [stresses](stress.md) in the two directions perpendicular to the local [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) for a gyrotropic [phase-space distribution function](phase-space-distribution-function.md). The factor one half averages the two perpendicular [velocity](velocity.md) components. With [first adiabatic invariant](first-adiabatic-invariant.md) $\mu=v_\perp^2/(2B)$, the integrand is $m\mu Bf$.
