# Cartesian product

↑ **Parent:** [Set theory](set-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_product)

The Cartesian product $X\times Y$ is the set of ordered pairs $(x,y)$ with $x\in X$ and $y\in Y$.
