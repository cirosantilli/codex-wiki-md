# Relative atomic mass

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic mass](atomic-mass.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Relative_atomic_mass)

For a [chemical element](chemical-element.md) in a specified population, its [relative atomic mass](relative-atomic-mass.md) or [atomic weight](relative-atomic-mass.md) is the [dimensionless](dimensionless-quantity.md) ratio $A_{\rm r}=\bar m/\mathrm u$, with $\bar m$ the mean neutral [atomic mass](atomic-mass.md) weighted by [isotopic abundance](isotopic-abundance.md). For the illustrative [neon](neon.md) population, $A_{\rm r}\approx20.1800$, whereas $\bar m\approx20.1800\,\mathrm u$ has [mass](mass.md) units.[Column Descriptions for Atomic Weights and Isotopic Compositions](column-descriptions-for-atomic-weights-and-isotopic-compositions.md)
