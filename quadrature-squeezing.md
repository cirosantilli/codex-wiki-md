# Quadrature squeezing

↑ **Parent:** [Optical quadrature](optical-quadrature.md)

With $X_\vartheta=(ae^{-i\vartheta}+a^\dagger e^{i\vartheta})/2$, the coherent-state and vacuum [variances](variance-split.md) are $1/4$. A state is squeezed in the chosen quadrature when its [variance](variance-split.md) about its own mean is smaller. A second moment about zero is not the [variance](variance-split.md) when that mean is nonzero. [balanced homodyne detection](balanced-homodyne-detection.md) measures a phase-selected quadrature distribution.
