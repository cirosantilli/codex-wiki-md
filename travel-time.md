# Travel time

↑ **Parent:** [Ray tracing](ray-tracing.md)

A wave's travel time along a [elastic ray](elastic-ray.md) is the integral of local [slowness](slowness.md) against [arc length](arc-length.md). Its variation with source and receiver position describes the arrival branches, including transmitted and reflected paths. Different paths can have different travel times between the same endpoints.
