# Schizosaccharomyces pombe

↑ **Parent:** [Yeast](yeast.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schizosaccharomyces_pombe)

[Schizosaccharomyces pombe](schizosaccharomyces-pombe.md) is a [yeast](yeast.md) [species](species.md) used as a [model organism](model-organism.md). It supplies the candidate [genes](gene.md) in the [yeast cross-species complementation experiment](yeast-cross-species-complementation-experiment.md); the recipient remains [Saccharomyces cerevisiae](saccharomyces-cerevisiae.md).
