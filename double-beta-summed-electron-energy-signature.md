# Double-beta summed-electron-energy signature

↑ **Parent:** [Neutrinoless double beta decay](neutrinoless-double-beta-decay.md)

A contained neutrinoless event deposits approximately the nuclear Q-value in the two-electron sum, apart from recoil and losses. Ordinary two-neutrino [double beta decay](double-beta-decay.md) has a continuous electron-sum [radiation spectrum](radiation-spectrum.md). Resolution, escape losses and backgrounds broaden or mimic features, so a peak assignment requires calibrated response, sidebands and appropriate event topology. A null peak search is a limit on a [decay rate](decay-width.md) under an efficiency/background model, not proof of Dirac [neutrinos](neutrino.md).
