# Molecular marker

↑ **Parent:** [Biology laboratory technique](biology-laboratory-technique.md)

A measured molecular feature used to identify a [cell state](cell-state.md), tissue or experimental condition. Marker loss can reflect altered expression without loss of the [cells](biological-cell-split.md) themselves.
