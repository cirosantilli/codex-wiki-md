# Hemiaminal

↑ **Parent:** [Organic chemistry](organic-chemistry.md)

A carbon bearing both a hydroxyl group and an amino substituent, commonly formed by amine addition to a [carbonyl](carbonyl-group.md). Cyclic [hemiaminals](hemiaminal.md) can equilibrate with open-chain [carbonyl](carbonyl-group.md) compounds.
