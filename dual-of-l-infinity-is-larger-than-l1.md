# Dual of L infinity is larger than L1

↑ **Parent:** [Duality of Lp spaces](duality-of-lp-spaces.md)

On $\mathbb R^n$, integration embeds $L^1$ isometrically into $(L^\infty)^*$, but this is not surjective. For example, the functional taking the limit at infinity on $\mathbb C1\oplus C_0(\mathbb R^n)$ extends to $L^\infty$ by the [Hahn-Banach theorem](hahn-banach-theorem.md); it vanishes on compactly supported functions and is one on the constant function, so no $L^1$ density represents it.
