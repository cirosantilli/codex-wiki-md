# Open ball

↑ **Parent:** [Open set](open-set.md)

In a [metric space](metric-space.md), the [open ball](open-ball.md) of radius $r>0$ about $x$ is $\{y:d(x,y)<r\}$; it is the open-boundary convention for a [ball](ball-mathematics.md).
