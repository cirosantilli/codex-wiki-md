# Principal strain rates

↑ **Parent:** [Strain-rate tensor](strain-rate-tensor.md)

[Principal strain rates](principal-strain-rates.md) are the [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $a,b,c$ of the symmetric [rate-of-strain tensor](strain-rate-tensor.md). Positive values are local extension and negative values local compression. In [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md) $a+b+c=0$, implying $a^3+b^3+c^3=3abc$. Their [eigenvectors](eigenvector.md) describe the principal deformation directions.
