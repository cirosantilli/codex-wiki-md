# Centered quadratic Gaussian transformation

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian random field](gaussian-random-field.md)

If $G$ is a centered [Gaussian random field](gaussian-random-field.md), the transformation $G+\alpha(G^2-\langle G^2\rangle)$ retains zero mean and introduces a three-point [connected correlation function](connected-correlation-function.md) at first order in $\alpha$. [Wick contractions](wick-contraction.md) give $2\alpha$ times the sum of products of two covariances. The corresponding local cosmological model uses $\alpha=3f_{\mathrm{NL}}/5$.
