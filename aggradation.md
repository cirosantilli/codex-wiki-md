# Aggradation

↑ **Parent:** [Sedimentology](sedimentology.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aggradation)

Rise of a depositional surface through net [sediment](sediment.md) accumulation. It can fill a hanging-wall basin and conceal an active [fault scarp](fault-scarp.md).
