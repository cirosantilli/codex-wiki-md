# Normalizer-centralizer equality for a toral abelian subalgebra

↑ **Parent:** [Normalizer of a Lie subalgebra](normalizer-of-a-lie-subalgebra.md)

If $\mathfrak a$ is a [toral subalgebra](toral-subalgebra.md) and an [abelian Lie algebra](abelian-lie-algebra.md), its [normalizer of a Lie subalgebra](normalizer-of-a-lie-subalgebra.md) equals its [centralizer of a Lie subalgebra](centralizer-of-a-lie-subalgebra.md). For $x$ in the former and $a\in\mathfrak a$, one has $[a,x]\in\mathfrak a$, hence $[a,[a,x]]=0$. A [diagonalizable](diagonalizable-matrix.md) [linear map](linear-map.md) has the same [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) as its square, by expansion in an [eigenbasis](eigenbasis.md). Applying this to $\operatorname{ad}a$ gives $[a,x]=0$ for every $a$. The reverse containment follows directly from the definitions. No assumption that $\mathfrak g$ is a [semisimple Lie algebra](semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md) is needed.
