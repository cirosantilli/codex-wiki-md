# Band insulator

↑ **Parent:** [Band filling](band-filling.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Band_insulator)

A band insulator has an integer number of completely filled [energy bands](energy-band.md), with the [Fermi energy](fermi-energy.md) inside a positive [band gap](band-gap.md). At zero temperature, no arbitrarily low-energy charged excitation is then available.
