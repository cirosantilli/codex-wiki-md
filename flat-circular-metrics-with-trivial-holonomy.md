# Flat circular metrics with trivial holonomy

↑ **Parent:** [Holonomy around circular fibres](holonomy-around-circular-fibres.md)

On a connected regular radial interval, trivial [holonomy around circular fibres](holonomy-around-circular-fibres.md) forces the continuous function $f'$ to be integer-valued, hence constant. The resulting [Riemannian metrics](riemannian-metric.md) have $f(r)=nr+b$, with $n\in\mathbb Z$ and $f>0$ on the interval. Their [Gaussian curvature](gaussian-curvature.md) is $-f''/f=0$. For $n\ne0$, $\rho=f/|n|$ and $\theta=|n|\phi$ give a [local isometry](local-isometry.md) to the [Euclidean plane](euclidean-plane.md); for $n=0$, $r,b\phi$ give a flat cylindrical chart. The polar [developing map](developing-map.md) is injective on the regular domain when $|n|=1$, and is a multiple covering when $|n|>1$. Trivial [holonomy](holonomy.md) therefore does not imply a global Euclidean chart. The slope is a discrete parameter, not an unrestricted real one.
