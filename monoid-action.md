# Monoid action

↑ **Parent:** [Monoid](monoid.md)

A left monoid action assigns each $m\in M$ a map $a\mapsto m\cdot a$ with $1\cdot a=a$ and $(mn)\cdot a=m\cdot(n\cdot a)$. It need not be invertible or injective. A left [M-set](m-set.md) is equivalently a covariant functor from the one-object category determined by $M$.
