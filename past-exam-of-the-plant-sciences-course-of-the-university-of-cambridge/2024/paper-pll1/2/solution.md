**Epigenetic modification could improve a [crop](../../../../crop.md) by selecting useful stable regulatory variants, deliberately altering a validated locus, preserving favorable states during propagation, or exploiting temporary physiological memory when permanent inheritance is unnecessary.** The appropriate strategy depends on the trait, the [crop](../../../../crop.md)'s mode of propagation and the stability of the state. A useful methylation marker is not automatically a causal target, and a demonstration in [Arabidopsis thaliana](../../../../arabidopsis-thaliana.md) is not yet an improved field variety.

A sensible starting point is natural variation. A breeder can relate a [phenotype](../../../../phenotype.md) to [differentially methylated regions](../../../../differentially-methylated-region.md), while measuring [genotype](../../../../genotype.md), expression and relevant environmental history. [epigenome-wide association studies](../../../../epigenome-wide-association-study.md) can prioritize candidate loci, but local sequence differences, trans-acting regulators and differences in tissue composition can all produce methylation associations. Common-environment comparisons, reciprocal crosses where appropriate and evidence that changing the state changes the trait help establish causation. The desired product is a regulatory state that remains useful in the target genetic background, not simply the largest statistical methylation difference.

A concrete nutritional example is [VTE3(1)](../../../../vte3-1.md) in [tomato](../../../../tomato.md). Differential methylation associated with a promoter [retrotransposon](../../../../retrotransposon.md) affects expression of a vitamin E biosynthetic gene and fruit tocopherol accumulation. The reported [epialleles](../../../../epiallele.md) connect a regulatory state with an agronomic quality trait, rather than merely correlating bulk methylation with vigorous growth.[Abstract; promoter methylation, expression and fruit tocopherol analyses](../../../../natural-occurring-epialleles-determine-vitamin-e-accumulation-in-tomato-fruits-by-leandro-quadrana-and-others.md) Selecting a favorable state could therefore complement conventional selection, provided its stability, the underlying promoter sequence and any correlated quality effects are characterized. The existence of this example does not imply that removing methylation generally raises [crop](../../../../crop.md) quality.

A second route is to create and select broader epigenetic variation. Transient disruption of methylation maintenance can generate [epigenetic recombinant inbred lines](../../../../epigenetic-recombinant-inbred-line.md) after the initiating genetic defect is removed. Such populations have produced selectable variation in quantitative traits in [Arabidopsis thaliana](../../../../arabidopsis-thaliana.md).[epiRIL trait mapping and response to selection](../../../../mapping-the-epigenetic-basis-of-complex-traits-by-sandra-cortijo-and-others.md) They can reveal regulatory states not readily encountered in an existing breeding pool. However, broad hypomethylation can also activate transposons or destabilize development. Genome sequencing is needed to distinguish genuine state effects from new insertions or other mutations, and restoring a maintenance gene does not guarantee that every induced [epiallele](../../../../epiallele.md) will persist.

[somaclonal variation](../../../../somaclonal-variation.md) during tissue culture is another source of altered states, but a good [crop](../../../../crop.md) strategy can involve preventing harmful epigenetic change rather than generating more. In [African oil palm](../../../../african-oil-palm.md), loss of methylation at the [Karma epiallele](../../../../karma-epiallele.md) in an intronic [retrotransposon](../../../../retrotransposon.md) is associated with abnormal splicing, mantled flowers and reduced yield. Methylation near the relevant splice region provided a predictor of the damaging variant.[Abstract and relation between methylation, transcript processing and fruit phenotype](../../../../loss-of-karma-transposon-methylation-underlies-the-mantled-somaclonal-variant-of-oil-palm-by-meilina-ong-abdullah-and-others.md) Screening propagated material for the favorable state can protect the value of elite clones; it is not evidence that arbitrary stress-induced methylation will improve yield.

More directed approaches use [epigenome editing](../../../../epigenome-editing.md). A sequence-targeting protein such as [dCas9](../../../../dcas9.md) can recruit an activity that changes [DNA methylation](../../../../dna-methylation.md) or a [chromatin](../../../../chromatin.md) regulator, rather than cutting the target sequence. One can in principle tune a validated promoter's expression while retaining its coding sequence. Targeted demethylation of the [FWA](../../../../fwa.md) locus in [Arabidopsis thaliana](../../../../arabidopsis-thaliana.md) demonstrated that a changed methylation state can alter expression and flowering behavior, with inheritance of the state at that particular locus.[FWA-targeted demethylation, expression, flowering and inheritance tests](../../../../targeted-dna-demethylation-of-the-arabidopsis-genome-using-the-human-tet1-catalytic-domain-by-javier-gallego-bartolome-and-others.md) This is a proof of a mechanism, not a recommendation that delayed flowering is universally favorable or that every edited locus will retain its state after the targeting factor disappears.

The causal chain should guide target choice: a defined regulatory state changes expression in the relevant tissue, which changes a biochemical or developmental process, which improves the intended [crop](../../../../crop.md) trait. Possible objectives include modifying flowering schedules, improving nutritional composition or reducing an unfavorable expression state during clonal propagation. Persistent effects require a mechanism for maintaining the new state. If local DNA repeats or small-RNA pathways continually restore the original state, a transient edit may be erased. Conversely, a state that is too persistent may impair later developmental transitions. Specificity also matters because broad changes to a [chromatin](../../../../chromatin.md) enzyme affect many loci simultaneously.

Not every useful application requires an inherited [epiallele](../../../../epiallele.md). [Seed priming](../../../../seed-priming.md) or a prior acclimating exposure can change later stress responses within the same [crop](../../../../crop.md) generation. If the treatment reproducibly improves establishment under the intended conditions, it can be agronomically useful even when offspring return to the original state. Nevertheless, [stress priming](../../../../stress-priming.md) may be mediated by stored metabolites, hormones or proteins rather than inherited [chromatin](../../../../chromatin.md). Benefits can disappear in another environment or be offset by reduced growth, altered maturation or shorter seed storage life. Such treatments should be evaluated as defined management interventions, rather than being advertised as proven transgenerational breeding.

Any deployment needs tests matched to how the [crop](../../../../crop.md) is propagated. A seed-propagated line must transmit the favorable effect through reproduction; a clone must retain it through the relevant number of propagation cycles. Multi-environment trials should measure the intended endpoint and relevant costs, including fertility, developmental normality and yield stability. Comparing treated and untreated material of the same [genotype](../../../../genotype.md), checking independent derivations and monitoring sequence as well as [epigenetic state](../../../../epigenetic-state.md) reduce the risk of attributing an unrelated change to the proposed mechanism. Molecular differences alone do not establish an economic benefit.

**The strongest strategy is therefore to treat [epigenetic state](../../../../epigenetic-state.md) as an additional, testable breeding or management variable.** Natural [epialleles](../../../../epiallele.md), directed editing and propagation control offer different opportunities, but none removes the need to establish causality, persistence and benefit in the actual [crop](../../../../crop.md) and production environment.
