**Resistance-gene evolution reflects recurrent pathogen selection acting within ecological and physiological constraints, while duplication, sequence exchange and gene loss generate changing receptor repertoires.** The result is not one universal genome organization: some receptors occur in variable clusters or linked functional pairs, others are conserved or dispersed, and some lineages lose parts of an immune pathway.

The relevant genes should first be distinguished. [Pattern recognition receptors](../../../../pattern-recognition-receptor.md) at the cell surface detect conserved microbial or damage-associated features, whereas many intracellular [nucleotide-binding leucine-rich repeat receptors](../../../../nucleotide-binding-leucine-rich-repeat-receptor.md) detect pathogen-derived molecules or disturbances of host targets. [NLRs](../../../../nucleotide-binding-leucine-rich-repeat-receptor.md) are a major class of [resistance genes](../../../../resistance-gene.md), but they are not all [resistance genes](../../../../resistance-gene.md): transport, metabolism, cell-wall properties and quantitative regulatory differences can also alter disease. Thus counts of [NLRs](../../../../nucleotide-binding-leucine-rich-repeat-receptor.md) describe one important part of the defense system rather than a complete measure of immunity.

Pathogens impose changing selection because recognition can reduce infection while successful pathogen variants evade or suppress the recognized response. A new recognition specificity can rise when it is useful; its advantage can decline when the corresponding pathogen population changes. Spatial and temporal variation in pathogen communities, and [negative frequency-dependent selection](../../../../negative-frequency-dependent-selection.md) under appropriate host–pathogen dynamics, can help maintain alternative resistance alleles through [balancing selection](../../../../balancing-selection.md). These processes need not produce the same signal as repeated directional selection. Conserved signaling functions may remain under purifying constraint while recognition-associated regions diversify more rapidly.

Resistance also has costs. Constitutive or inappropriate immune activity can divert resources, damage tissues or reduce reproduction in the absence of the relevant disease. This [growth-defense trade-off](../../../../growth-defense-trade-off.md) makes loss or reduced expression of an unused receptor potentially advantageous, and means that greater gene number is not automatically better. [Hybrid necrosis](../../../../hybrid-necrosis.md) supplies a particularly clear constraint: combining alleles from otherwise viable parents can inappropriately activate immunity. A characterized [Arabidopsis thaliana](../../../../arabidopsis-thaliana.md) incompatibility involved an NLR-associated immune response, illustrating how combinations beneficial or harmless in their native backgrounds can become deleterious in hybrids.[immune activation and genetic analysis of hybrid necrosis](../../../../autoimmune-response-as-a-mechanism-for-a-dobzhansky-muller-type-incompatibility-syndrome-in-plants-by-kirsten-bomblies-and-others.md)

The genomic raw material is generated by [gene duplication](../../../../gene-duplication.md), including [tandem duplication](../../../../tandem-duplication.md), followed by divergence, deletion or pseudogenization: [birth-and-death evolution](../../../../birth-and-death-evolution.md). Unequal exchange and [gene conversion](../../../../gene-conversion.md) can create copy-number differences and mosaic receptor sequences. A cluster therefore provides opportunities for new recognition combinations, while linked copies can also recombine into nonfunctional or incompatible products. Gene content may differ enough between accessions that a single reference genome omits relevant resistance loci altogether. Selection acts on the resulting haplotypes as well as on individual point substitutions.

Long-read, multi-accession analyses make that organization visible. The species-wide [Arabidopsis thaliana](../../../../arabidopsis-thaliana.md) [NLR](../../../../nucleotide-binding-leucine-rich-repeat-receptor.md) inventory found substantial [presence-absence variation](../../../../presence-absence-variation.md) and architectural diversity, establishing the importance of a [pan-NLRome](../../../../pan-nlrome.md) rather than one reference receptor list.[species-wide inventory, presence-absence variation and integrated-domain diversity](../../../../a-species-wide-inventory-of-nlr-genes-and-alleles-in-arabidopsis-thaliana-by-anna-lena-van-de-weyer-and-others.md) Such variation is consistent with duplication, turnover and selection, but differences in assembly quality or annotation can also alter apparent counts. Comparative conclusions therefore require sufficiently complete genomes and consistent identification of intact genes and fragments.

Physical linkage can preserve a functional combination as well as generate diversity. In a [paired NLR](../../../../paired-nlr.md) system, recognition and downstream activation can be partitioned between partners. Selection can favor inheritance and coordinated expression of a compatible pair; indiscriminate recombination is then potentially harmful. The [Arabidopsis thaliana](../../../../arabidopsis-thaliana.md) [RRS1-RPS4 receptor pair](../../../../rrs1-rps4-receptor-pair.md) illustrates an [integrated decoy](../../../../integrated-decoy.md) mechanism, where a receptor-associated WRKY domain links perturbation of a target-like domain to immunity.[paired receptor function and the integrated WRKY-domain mechanism](../../../../a-receptor-pair-with-an-integrated-decoy-converts-pathogen-disabling-of-transcription-factors-to-immunity-by-clementine-le-roux-and-others.md) This is an explanation for one type of organization, not a rule that every cluster is a functional pair or every integrated domain has the same role.

[plant](../../../../plant.md) lifestyle changes the selection regime. A long-lived perennial can experience many pathogen seasons, making maintenance of effective defense across time important; an annual may sometimes escape a disease through seasonal timing. These are conditional expectations, not deterministic predictions of [NLR](../../../../nucleotide-binding-leucine-rich-repeat-receptor.md) counts. [selfing](../../../../self-fertilization.md) changes heterozygosity and the persistence of linked combinations, while [outcrossing](../../../../outcrossing.md) exposes alleles to new partners and can favor different balances between recombination and linkage. Clonal propagation preserves whole immune haplotypes but can also produce large areas of similar susceptible hosts. [crop](../../../../crop.md) domestication, breeding bottlenecks and uniform deployment consequently alter the evolutionary setting relative to a diverse wild population.

Ecological adaptation can also change which components are retained. Aquatic or otherwise specialized habitats differ in microbial exposure and in the physiological demands placed on a [plant](../../../../plant.md); symbiotic associations add selection against indiscriminate defense activation. Comparative work identified independent losses of [NLRs](../../../../nucleotide-binding-leucine-rich-repeat-receptor.md) together with [EDS1](../../../../eds1.md)/[PAD4](../../../../pad4.md)-associated signaling components in several [plant](../../../../plant.md) lineages, with links to other immune and drought-response functions.[comparative genome analyses and coordinated pathway loss](../../../../convergent-loss-of-an-eds1-pad4-signaling-pathway-in-several-plant-lineages-reveals-coevolved-components-of-plant-immunity-and-drought-response-by-erin-l-baggs-and-others.md) This supports pathway coevolution and rewiring. It does not prove that an aquatic lifestyle alone caused each loss, or that those species have no remaining defenses; phylogeny and additional ecological changes must be considered.

Genomic organization therefore records both opportunity and constraint. Clusters can accelerate receptor-family turnover, linked pairs can preserve coadapted functions, and distributed signaling partners can limit which new sensors are useful. Changes in receptor expression, copy number and regulation may matter as much as coding-sequence diversity. Interactions with abiotic stress and beneficial microbes make pathogen pressure only part of the fitness landscape. Testing an evolutionary explanation requires connecting a genomic pattern to [phenotype](../../../../phenotype.md) and ecology, rather than inferring selective benefit from a large cluster alone.

**The resulting resistance genome is a dynamic, population-level repertoire shaped by pathogen variation, the costs of immune activation and the [plant](../../../../plant.md)'s ecological and reproductive context.** This explains simultaneous rapid diversification, retention of compatible gene combinations and repeated gene loss, and why a resistance allele that is useful in one [crop](../../../../crop.md) background or environment may not remain useful everywhere.
