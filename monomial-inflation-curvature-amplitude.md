# Monomial-inflation curvature amplitude

↑ **Parent:** [Monomial inflation potential](monomial-inflation-potential.md)

For $V=C_p(\Phi/M_{\rm Pl})^p$ and the leading endpoint estimate $\epsilon_V=1$, the [slow-roll approximation](slow-roll-approximation.md) gives $\Phi_N^2/M_{\rm Pl}^2=2pN+p^2/2$ and $\epsilon_V=p/(4N+p)$. Thus $\mathcal P_{\mathcal R}=C_p(2pN+p^2/2)^{p/2}(4N+p)/(24\pi^2M_{\rm Pl}^4p)$ and the [scalar spectral index](scalar-spectral-index.md) obeys $n_s-1=-2(p+2)/(4N+p)$. The coefficient sets the amplitude, whereas the monomial power and exit e-fold count set the leading tilt. Changing the power without specifying the coefficient leaves the overall normalization undetermined.
