# Cartesian streamfunction

↑ **Parent:** [Incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md)

For a two-dimensional [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md), a Cartesian streamfunction $\psi$ defines $u=\partial_y\psi$ and $v=-\partial_x\psi$. The continuity equation $u_x+v_y=0$ then holds identically.
