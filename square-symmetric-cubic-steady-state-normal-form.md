# Square-symmetric cubic steady-state normal form

↑ **Parent:** [Dihedral steady-state normal form](dihedral-steady-state-normal-form.md)

Independent sign changes and interchange of two coordinates in the natural [dihedral group](dihedral-group.md) action force the displayed cubic [normal form](normal-form-dynamical-systems.md). Apart from the origin, its nondegenerate small [equilibria](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) lie on coordinate axes or on the diagonals. An axial branch has squared amplitude $\mu/a$ and [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $-2\mu,\mu(1-b/a)$. A diagonal branch has component squared amplitude $\mu/(a+b)$ and [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $-2\mu,2\mu(b-a)/(a+b)$. Subtracting the two nonzero equilibrium equations gives $(a-b)(A^2-B^2)=0$, excluding other cubic branches when $a\ne b$.
