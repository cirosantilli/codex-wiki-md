# Hyperbolic space

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic geometry](hyperbolic-geometry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperbolic_space)

The complete simply connected [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md) of dimension $n$ with constant sectional curvature $-1$ is hyperbolic space. In the upper half-space model its metric is $ds^2=(dx_1^2+\cdots+dx_n^2)/x_n^2$ for $x_n>0$. Its ideal boundary in dimension three is the [Riemann sphere](riemann-sphere.md).
