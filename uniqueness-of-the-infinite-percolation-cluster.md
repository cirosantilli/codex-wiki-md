# Uniqueness of the infinite percolation cluster

↑ **Parent:** [Percolation cluster](percolation-cluster.md)

For independent [site percolation](site-percolation-split.md) or [bond percolation](bond-percolation-split.md) on the [cubic lattice](cubic-lattice.md) $\mathbb Z^d$, there is [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md) at most one [infinite percolation cluster](infinite-percolation-cluster.md) at every fixed parameter. Above the [percolation critical probability](percolation-critical-probability.md), there is exactly one [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md). This theorem is called the [Burton-Keane theorem](uniqueness-of-the-infinite-percolation-cluster.md). Existence uses translation ergodicity together with positive [percolation probability](percolation-probability.md); uniqueness is a separate conclusion. At $p=1$ uniqueness is deterministic.
