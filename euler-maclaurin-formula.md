# Euler-Maclaurin formula

↑ **Parent:** [Summation](summation.md)

The Euler-Maclaurin formula relates a sum to an [integral](integral-split.md), endpoint corrections, and terms involving [Bernoulli numbers](bernoulli-number-split.md) and endpoint [derivatives](derivative.md). Truncating after finitely many derivative corrections gives a remainder term that must be controlled; this supports accurate estimates of long sums.
