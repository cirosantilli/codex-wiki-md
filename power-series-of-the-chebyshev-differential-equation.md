# Power series of the Chebyshev differential equation

↑ **Parent:** [Chebyshev differential equation](chebyshev-differential-equation.md)

For the [Chebyshev differential equation](chebyshev-differential-equation.md) $(1-x^2)y''-xy'+k^2y=0$, a [power series](power-series.md) about the [ordinary point](ordinary-point-criterion-for-a-second-order-equation.md) zero splits into even and odd solutions with the displayed [recurrence relation](recurrence-relation.md). Every nonterminating parity series has [radius of convergence](radius-of-convergence.md) one. If $k^2=m^2$ for a nonnegative integer $m$, the parity series containing $x^m$ terminates, giving a [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) with infinite [radius of convergence](radius-of-convergence.md); the other independent series still has radius one. Normalized solutions are $\cos(k\arcsin x)$ and $\sin(k\arcsin x)/k$, with the latter replaced by $\arcsin x$ when $k=0$.
