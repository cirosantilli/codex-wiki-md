# Hot-bottom burning

↑ **Parent:** [Asymptotic giant branch](asymptotic-giant-branch.md)

When the bottom of an [Asymptotic giant branch](asymptotic-giant-branch.md) star's convective envelope becomes hot enough for [hydrogen burning](hydrogen-burning.md), the [CNO cycle](cno-cycle.md) can convert newly dredged-up [carbon](carbon.md) into [nitrogen](nitrogen.md). Its onset and strength depend on mass, composition and envelope structure; it is not a universal property of all stars at one initial mass.
