# Local factorization of X3 plus 25X2 minus 50X plus 40

↑ **Parent:** [Local factorization and extended absolute values](local-factorization-and-extended-absolute-values.md)

The polynomial

$$
F(X)=X^3+25X^2-50X+40
$$

splits into three linear factors over $\mathbb Q_2$. Over $\mathbb Q_5$ it is an [Eisenstein polynomial](eisenstein-polynomial.md), so adjoining a root gives a totally ramified cubic extension. Its splitting field over $\mathbb Q_5$ has [decomposition group](decomposition-group.md) $S_3$ and [inertia group](inertia-group.md) $A_3\cong C_3$.
