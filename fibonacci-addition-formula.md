# Fibonacci addition formula

↑ **Parent:** [Fibonacci number](fibonacci-number.md)

With the standard [Fibonacci numbers](fibonacci-number.md) $F_0=0$, $F_1=1$, this identity holds for $n\ge0$, $k\ge1$. At $k=1$ it is immediate. If it holds for $k$ at every $n$, apply it at $n+1$ and use $F_{n+2}=F_{n+1}+F_n$ to obtain $F_{n+k+1}=F_{k+1}F_{n+1}+F_kF_n$, proving the assertion by [mathematical induction](mathematical-induction.md). Taking $k=n$ gives $F_{2n}=F_n(F_{n+1}+F_{n-1})$ for $n\ge1$, linking the doubling formula to the [Lucas numbers](lucas-number.md).
