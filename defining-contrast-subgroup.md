# Defining contrast subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Fractional factorial design](fractional-factorial-design.md)

Factor subsets multiply by [symmetric difference](symmetric-difference.md); their sign columns multiply pointwise. The subgroup generated by independent defining words has $2^k$ elements. On a selected [fractional factorial design](fractional-factorial-design.md), every word in this subgroup is constant, with sign determined by the chosen generator signs. The cosets classify identical or opposite contrast columns.
