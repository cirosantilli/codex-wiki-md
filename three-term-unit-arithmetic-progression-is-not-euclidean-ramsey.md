# Three-term unit arithmetic progression is not Euclidean Ramsey

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean Ramsey set](euclidean-ramsey-set.md)

The collinear set $\{0,1,2\}$ is not a [Euclidean Ramsey set](euclidean-ramsey-set.md). In every dimension, coloring $x$ by $\lfloor2\|x\|^2\rfloor$ modulo ten prevents a monochromatic congruent copy; the [parallelogram law](parallelogram-law.md) would force the corresponding three integer parts to have a second difference strictly between two and six, which cannot be divisible by ten.
