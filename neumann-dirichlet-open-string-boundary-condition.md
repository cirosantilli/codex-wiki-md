# Neumann-Dirichlet open-string boundary condition

↑ **Parent:** [Open-string mode expansion](open-string-mode-expansion.md)

A transverse [open string](open-string.md) coordinate has an ND boundary condition when one endpoint obeys a [Neumann boundary condition](neumann-boundary-condition.md) and the other a [Dirichlet boundary condition](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md). On a strip of width $\pi$, its nonconstant spatial [eigenfunctions](eigenfunction.md) are $\cos((n+1/2)\sigma)$ when the free endpoint is at zero. They solve the endpoint equations because their derivatives vanish at zero and their values vanish at $\pi$. Reversing the endpoints gives sine [eigenfunctions](eigenfunction.md) with the same frequencies. The fixed endpoint removes the dynamical [worldsheet zero mode](worldsheet-zero-mode.md).
