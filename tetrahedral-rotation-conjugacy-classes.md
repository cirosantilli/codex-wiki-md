# Tetrahedral rotation conjugacy classes

↑ **Parent:** [Tetrahedral symmetry](tetrahedral-symmetry.md)

The proper rotational [tetrahedral symmetry](tetrahedral-symmetry.md) group is $A_4$, acting by even [permutations](permutation.md) of the vertices. Its [conjugacy classes](conjugacy-class.md) consist of the identity, the three half-turns about opposite-edge midpoint axes, four rotations through $+2\pi/3$ about outward-pointing vertex axes, and their four inverses. A 3-cycle has a three-element [centralizer](centralizer.md), so its class has size $12/3=4$. Conjugating it to its inverse would require an odd permutation on its three moved vertices, so the two orientations form different classes in $A_4$ even though they lie in one class in $S_4$.
