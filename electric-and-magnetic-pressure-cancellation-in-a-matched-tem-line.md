# Electric and magnetic pressure cancellation in a matched TEM line

↑ **Parent:** [Transverse electromagnetic mode](transverse-electromagnetic-mode.md)

In a matched lossless [parallel-plate transmission line](parallel-plate-transmission-line.md), a single travelling wave obeys $V/I=Z_0=(d/a)\sqrt{\mu_0/\varepsilon}$. The electric attraction per unit length is $f_E=\varepsilon aV_{\rm rms}^2/(2d^2)$; each plate feels the field of the other plate, excluding its self-field. Opposite sheet currents give magnetic repulsion $f_B=\mu_0I_{\rm rms}^2/(2a)$. Substituting the travelling-wave impedance proves their equality, so the ideal net transverse electromagnetic force is zero at every phase. A standing wave or independent voltage and current need not have this cancellation.
