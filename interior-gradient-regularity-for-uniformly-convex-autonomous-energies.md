# Interior gradient regularity for uniformly convex autonomous energies

↑ **Parent:** [Uniformly convex variational integrand](uniformly-convex-variational-integrand.md)

For scalar minimizers of $\int F(Du)$ with $F\in C^2(\mathbb R^n)$ and globally bounded uniformly positive [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md), no higher differentiability of $F$ is needed for interior gradient Hölder continuity. The weak Euler-Lagrange equation is $\operatorname{div}DF(Du)=0$. Difference quotients solve uniformly elliptic equations with coefficient matrices obtained by averaging $D^2F$ along gradient segments. The [Caccioppoli inequality](caccioppoli-inequality.md) and difference quotient characterization give $u\in H^2_{\mathrm{loc}}$. The [Sobolev chain rule](sobolev-chain-rule.md) then gives $\operatorname{div}(D^2F(Du)D(D_ku))=0$. The [De Giorgi-Nash-Moser theorem](de-giorgi-nash-moser-theorem.md) makes each derivative Hölder continuous, with exponent depending only on dimension and the ellipticity ratio. This scalar conclusion should not be assumed for vector-valued elliptic systems.
