# Finite-inventory directional salt rejection

↑ **Parent:** [Directional solidification](directional-solidification.md)

For pure solid rejecting all solute, pulling speed $V>0$, solutal diffusivity $D$ and finite solute inventory per unit area $N$, a stationary planar front has $C(z)=C_Ie^{-Vz/D}$ in its liquid half-space. The [advection-diffusion equation](advection-diffusion-equation.md) gives this profile, and $\int_0^\infty C\,dz=N$ fixes $C_I=VN/D$. A [liquidus](liquidus.md) $T_I=-mC_I$ fixes its position in an imposed [temperature gradient](temperature-gradient.md). The pure-solid branch requires $C_I<C_E$, where $C_E$ is the [eutectic composition](eutectic-composition.md).
