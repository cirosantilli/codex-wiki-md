# Field-effect transistor

↑ **Parent:** [Transistor](transistor.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Field-effect_transistor)

A [transistor](transistor.md) whose gate controls an [electric field](electric-field-split.md) that changes conduction through a channel between source and drain. Changing the gate's [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) controls the channel [electric current](electric-current.md).
