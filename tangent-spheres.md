# Tangent spheres

↑ **Parent:** [Sphere](sphere.md)

Two distinct [spheres](sphere.md) are externally tangent when their center distance equals the sum of their [radii](radius.md). They are internally tangent when it equals the absolute difference of their [radii](radius.md). At the point of contact they have the same tangent plane.
