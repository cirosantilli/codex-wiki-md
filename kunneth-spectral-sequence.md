<h1 id="kunneth-spectral-sequence">Künneth spectral sequence</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral sequence](spectral-sequence.md)

For a bounded complex $P$ of projective right modules and a left module $M$ of finite [projective dimension](projective-dimension.md), a finite projective resolution of $M$ yields a bounded [double cochain complex](double-cochain-complex.md). Its two [spectral sequences](spectral-sequence.md) give the displayed abutment. Over a noncommutative ring, the handedness of the two tensor factors is essential.
