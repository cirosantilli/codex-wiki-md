# Blasius equation

↑ **Parent:** [Prandtl boundary-layer equation](prandtl-boundary-layer-equation.md)

For a steady incompressible [viscous boundary layer](viscous-boundary-layer.md) over a flat plate with constant outer speed $U$, the [stream function](stream-function.md) $\psi=\sqrt{\nu Ux}\,f(y\sqrt{U/(\nu x)})$ reduces the [Prandtl boundary-layer equation](prandtl-boundary-layer-equation.md) to the displayed ordinary differential equation. The wall and outer conditions are $f(0)=f'(0)=0$ and $f'(\infty)=1$. The wall shear is $\rho U\sqrt{\nu U/x}\,f''(0)$, so its integral on one plate face of length $L$ is $2\rho U^2Lf''(0)/\sqrt{UL/\nu}$. Both equally exposed faces contribute twice this value.
