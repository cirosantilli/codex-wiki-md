# Zernike polynomials

↑ **Parent:** [Orthogonal polynomial](orthogonal-polynomial.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zernike_polynomials)

The real Zernike polynomials form an [orthogonal polynomial](orthogonal-polynomial.md) basis on the unit [Euclidean disk](disk-mathematics.md), with radial factor $R_n^m(\rho)$ and angular factor $\cos m\phi$ or $\sin m\phi$. Here $0\leq m\leq n$ and $n-m$ is [even](even-function.md). They decompose [wavefront errors](wavefront-error.md) into modes useful for optical correction. Normalizations differ, so coefficients should specify the chosen convention.
