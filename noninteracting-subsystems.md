# Noninteracting subsystems

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical ensemble](canonical-ensemble-split.md)

Two independently available parts of a [thermodynamic system](thermodynamic-system.md) are noninteracting in the canonical description when their [state space](state-space.md) is a [Cartesian product](cartesian-product.md) and their [energy](energy-split.md) is additive, $E(a,b)=E_A(a)+E_B(b)$. Both conditions are needed for the [canonical partition function](canonical-partition-function.md) to factor:

$$
Z=\sum_{a,b}e^{-\beta[E_A(a)+E_B(b)]}
=\left(\sum_a e^{-\beta E_A(a)}\right)\left(\sum_b e^{-\beta E_B(b)}\right)=Z_AZ_B.
$$

An additive expression on a constrained set of allowed state pairs need not give this factorization. Relabeling the states to make their allowed labels independent can instead introduce an interaction into the [energy](energy-split.md). For two two-state labels, an additive [energy](energy-split.md) necessarily satisfies the rectangular identity $E_{11}+E_{22}=E_{12}+E_{21}$.
