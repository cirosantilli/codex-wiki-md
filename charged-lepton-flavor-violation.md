# Charged-lepton flavor violation

↑ **Parent:** [Weak interaction](weak-interaction.md)

Processes that change individual charged-lepton family numbers are absent perturbatively in the minimal massless-neutrino [Standard Model](standard-model-split.md). A flavour-misaligned Higgs coupling can permit decays such as $h\to\mu\tau$. Neutrino masses and mixing permit extremely suppressed loop effects, while additional flavour structures can give larger rates.
