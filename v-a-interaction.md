# V-A interaction

↑ **Parent:** [Charged current](charged-current.md)

The vector-minus-axial-vector charged weak current contains the left-chiral projector $P_L=(1-\gamma^5)/2$. It violates [parity](parity.md) because [parity](parity.md) interchanges left and right chiral fields. For a massless particle [chirality](chirality-physics.md) fixes [helicity](helicity.md), but a massive charged [fermion](fermion.md)'s [helicity](helicity.md) need not be pure even when the vertex selects a chiral field. Nuclear [beta decay](beta-decay.md) uses the nonrelativistic reduction of vector and axial nucleon currents, giving Fermi and Gamow-Teller operators in the allowed limit.
