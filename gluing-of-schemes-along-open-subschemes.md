# Gluing of schemes along open subschemes

↑ **Parent:** [Scheme](scheme.md)

Given [schemes](scheme.md) $X_i$ and [isomorphisms](isomorphism.md) between designated [open subschemes](open-subscheme.md) satisfying inverse and cocycle conditions, identify the underlying open spaces and glue their structure sheaves. The resulting [locally ringed space](locally-ringed-space.md) is locally one of the $X_i$, hence a [scheme](scheme.md). The same construction works for [formal schemes](formal-scheme.md) and topological structure sheaves. Compatible morphisms out of the charts glue uniquely.
