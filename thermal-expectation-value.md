# Thermal expectation value

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical ensemble](canonical-ensemble-split.md)

The [canonical ensemble](canonical-ensemble-split.md) assigns the [density matrix](density-matrix-split.md) $e^{-\beta H}/Z$, with $Z=\operatorname{Tr}e^{-\beta H}$. Its [expectation value](expectation-value.md) of an observable is the displayed normalized trace. In imaginary time, cyclicity of this trace gives periodic bosonic [correlation functions](correlation-function.md); it does not make the imaginary-time Heisenberg operator itself periodic.
