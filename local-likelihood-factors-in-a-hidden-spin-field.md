# Local likelihood factors in a hidden spin field

↑ **Parent:** [Gibbs sampling for a finite hidden spin field](gibbs-sampling-for-a-finite-hidden-spin-field.md)

When an observation mean depends on the neighboring spins, changing a spin affects observations centered at its neighbors. Its own observation need not involve that spin. Selecting precisely the affected factors gives a correct local [full conditional distribution](full-conditional-distribution.md) without recomputing the entire posterior.
