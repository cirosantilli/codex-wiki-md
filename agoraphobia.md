# Agoraphobia

↑ **Parent:** [Anxiety disorder](anxiety-disorder.md)

Marked fear and avoidance of situations where escape or obtaining help is perceived as difficult. It can occur with or without [panic disorder](panic-disorder.md); a specific feared consequence and its maintaining responses should be assessed.
