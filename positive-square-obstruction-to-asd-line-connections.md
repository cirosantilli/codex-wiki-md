# Positive-square obstruction to ASD line connections

↑ **Parent:** [Anti-self-dual connection](anti-self-dual-connection.md)

An [anti-self-dual connection](anti-self-dual-connection.md) on a line bundle has $c_1(L)^2[X]=-\|iF_A/(2\pi)\|_2^2\leq0$. A line bundle with positive square therefore admits no such connection for any metric of the chosen orientation. The splitting $L\oplus L^{-1}$ has $c_2=-c_1(L)^2$, giving the equivalent nonnegative-instanton-charge obstruction.
