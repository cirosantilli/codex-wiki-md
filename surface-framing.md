# Surface framing

↑ **Parent:** [Framed link](framed-link.md)

A surface framing pushes a curve in the direction tangent to an oriented [topological surface](topological-surface.md) containing it and normal to the curve. For a [two-handle](two-handle.md) attached along a curve on a boundary [topological surface](topological-surface.md), this specifies the attaching annulus.
