# Power-law spectral K correction

↑ **Parent:** [K correction](k-correction.md)

For emitted spectral power per steradian $P_\nu\propto\nu^{-\alpha}$, the [luminosity distance](luminosity-distance.md) gives $S_{\nu_o}=(1+z)P_{(1+z)\nu_o}/d_L^2$. Photon energy and arrival-rate redshifts supply two factors of $1+z$, while the observed frequency interval is reduced by one. Substitution of the power-law spectrum gives the displayed [K correction](k-correction.md). This convention uses spectral flux per unit frequency and power per steradian; using per unit wavelength or total spectral luminosity changes the corresponding factors.
