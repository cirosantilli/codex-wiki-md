# Density of a composite system

↑ **Parent:** [Normalizing extensive quantities](normalizing-extensive-quantities.md)

Consider two hypothetical parcels at the same [temperature](temperature-split.md) $300\,\mathrm K$, kept separated with negligible partition [mass](mass.md) and excluded container [volume](volume.md). Parcel A has [mass](mass.md) $2\,\mathrm{kg}$ and [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) $0.002\,\mathrm{m^3}$; parcel B has [mass](mass.md) $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ and [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) $0.006\,\mathrm{m^3}$. They may be different materials. Their individual [mass densities](density.md) and the composite [mass density](density.md) are

$$
\rho_A=1000\,\mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}},\qquad
\rho_B=500\,\mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}},\qquad
\rho_{\rm total}=\frac{5\,\mathrm{kg}}{0.008\,\mathrm{m^3}}
=625\,\mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}.
$$

The composite [mass density](density.md) is total material [mass](mass.md) divided by total occupied [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md); it need not be the local [mass density](density.md) anywhere. Equal [temperature](temperature-split.md) does not make these parcels replicas. In particular, the unweighted average $750\,\mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$ is wrong. The [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) fractions are $1/4$ and $3/4$, giving

$$
\rho_{\rm total}=\tfrac14\rho_A+\tfrac34\rho_B
=625\,\mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}.
$$

With [mass](mass.md) fractions $2/5$ and $3/5$, the equivalent rule is a [weighted harmonic mean](weighted-harmonic-mean.md):

$$
\frac1{\rho_{\rm total}}
=\frac{2/5}{\rho_A}+\frac{3/5}{\rho_B}
=0.0016\,\mathrm{m^3\,kg^{-1}}.
$$

This follows directly by writing $V_i=m_i/\rho_i$ and dividing total [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) by total [mass](mass.md); it is also the [mass](mass.md)-weighted average of [specific volumes](specific-volume.md). An unweighted average of [mass densities](density.md) is valid when the parcel [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) values are equal, not merely when their [temperatures](temperature-split.md) are equal.

If a partition is removed and different substances mix, the final [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) need not equal the sum of initial [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) values. A new [temperature](temperature-split.md), [thermodynamic phase](thermodynamic-phase.md) or composition may also change the local [mass density](density.md). Use the actual final [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) in $\rho=m/V$; the pre-mixing weighted identity cannot supply it. Even unchanged [temperature](temperature-split.md) alone does not guarantee unchanged [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md).
