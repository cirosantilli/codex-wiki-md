# Standardized regression residual

↑ **Parent:** [Regression residual](regression-residual.md)

In a [normal linear model](normal-linear-model.md), the $i$th [regression residual](regression-residual.md) has [variance](variance-split.md) $\sigma^2(1-h_{ii})$, where $h_{ii}$ is a diagonal entry of the [hat matrix](hat-matrix.md). Replacing $\sigma$ by the residual standard deviation $s$ gives the standardized residual used in the normal [Q-Q plot](q-q-plot.md) of R's linear-model diagnostic plots.
