# Supersymmetric Liouville equation

↑ **Parent:** [Two-dimensional N=(1,1) superspace](two-dimensional-n-1-1-superspace.md)

In [two-dimensional N=(1,1) superspace](two-dimensional-n-1-1-superspace.md), one convention for the supersymmetric Liouville equation is $iD_-D_+\Phi=e^\Phi$. With the ordered expansion $\Phi=\phi+i\theta^-\psi_++i\theta^+\psi_-+i\theta^-\theta^+F$ and [left Grassmann derivatives](left-grassmann-derivative.md), its left side is

$$
F+i\theta^-\partial_-\psi_- -i\theta^+\partial_+\psi_+ -i\theta^-\theta^+\partial_-\partial_+\phi.
$$

Writing $N=\Phi-\phi$, [Grassmann parity](grassmann-parity.md) gives $N^2=2\theta^-\theta^+\psi_+\psi_-$ and $N^3=0$. Hence

$$
e^\Phi=e^\phi[1+i\theta^-\psi_++i\theta^+\psi_-+\theta^-\theta^+(iF+\psi_+\psi_-)].
$$

Comparing coefficients eliminates the [auxiliary field](auxiliary-field.md) as $F=e^\phi$ and gives $\partial_-\psi_-=e^\phi\psi_+$, $\partial_+\psi_+=-e^\phi\psi_-$, $\partial_-\partial_+\phi=-e^{2\phi}+ie^\phi\psi_+\psi_-$. The fermion bilinear sign depends on the stated ordering and derivative convention.
