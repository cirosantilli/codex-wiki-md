# Attaching sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Handle decomposition](handle-decomposition.md)

The attaching sphere of an index-$h$ handle $D^h\times D^{n-h}$ is $S^{h-1}\times\{0\}$ in the previous boundary. Its [framing of an embedded sphere](framing-of-an-embedded-sphere.md) identifies a neighbourhood with $S^{h-1}\times D^{n-h}$ and specifies the [handle attachment](handle-attachment.md).
