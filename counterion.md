# Counterion

↑ **Parent:** [Ion](ion.md)

A mobile [ion](ion.md) whose charge has the opposite sign to a charged macromolecule, surface or other fixed species. Its local [concentration](concentration.md) can increase close to that fixed charge; [counterion condensation](counterion-condensation.md) distinguishes a bound fraction that remains localized in an infinite-dilution limit.
