# Red supergiant

↑ **Parent:** [Supergiant](supergiant.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_supergiant)

A cool, highly luminous evolved massive [star](star.md) with an extended envelope, generally later than its initial hot [main sequence](main-sequence.md) stage. Its eventual core-collapse outcome depends on the core, winds, mixing and binary history, not on its red colour alone.
