# Pi-1 soundness

↑ **Parent:** [Soundness of an arithmetic theory](soundness-of-an-arithmetic-theory.md)

[Pi-1 soundness](pi-1-soundness.md) means that every closed [Pi-1](pi-1-formula.md) theorem is true in the standard [natural numbers](natural-number.md). [Consistency](consistent-first-order-theory.md) plus [bounded completeness](bounded-completeness-of-an-arithmetic-theory.md) implies it, since a false universal bounded statement has a standard counterexample whose arithmetic verification is provable. It is weaker than full soundness and does not prevent a [consistent](consistent-first-order-theory.md) [arithmetic theory](first-order-theory.md) from proving a false [Sigma-1](sigma-1-formula.md) sentence.
