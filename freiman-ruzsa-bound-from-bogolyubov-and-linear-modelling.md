# Freiman-Ruzsa bound from Bogolyubov and linear modelling

↑ **Parent:** [Freiman-Ruzsa theorem over a finite field](freiman-ruzsa-theorem-over-a-finite-field.md)

Let $A$ be a nonempty subset of $\mathbb F_2^N$ with [doubling constant](doubling-constant.md) at most $C\ge1$. Translate to $B=A+a$ containing zero. The [Petridis minimal-growth lemma](petridis-minimal-growth-lemma.md) and [Ruzsa triangle inequality](ruzsa-triangle-inequality.md) bound $|kB|\le C^k|B|$. In [injective linear modelling of a small sumset](injective-linear-modelling-of-a-small-sumset.md), the image has density at least $1/(4C^{12})$. The [Finite-field Bogolyubov lemma](finite-field-bogolyubov-lemma.md) finds a subspace of codimension at most $\lceil32C^{24}\rceil$ in its fourfold sumset.

After [lifting a subspace through a Freiman model](lifting-a-subspace-through-a-freiman-model.md), obtain $\widetilde W\subseteq4B$ with $|\widetilde W|\ge2^{-\lceil32C^{24}\rceil}|B|$. The set $B+\widetilde W\subseteq5B$ uses at most $C^5 2^{\lceil32C^{24}\rceil}$ cosets. Their representatives generate a quotient group of size at most two to this number. Since $|\widetilde W|\le C^4|B|$, adjoining those representatives and $a$ proves the displayed bound. Its constants are deliberately coarse, but depend only on $C$. The nonempty assumption is necessary: no vector subspace has cardinality at most a constant times the size of the empty set.
