# Focusing nonlinear Schrodinger equation

↑ **Parent:** [Integrable systems](integrable-systems-split.md)

The focusing cubic [nonlinear Schrödinger equation](nonlinear-schrodinger-equation.md) has the normalization

$$
i\psi_t+\psi_{xx}+2|\psi|^2\psi=0,
$$

the positive cubic term focuses the field and permits bright solitons on zero background.
