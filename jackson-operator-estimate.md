# Jackson operator estimate

↑ **Parent:** [Jackson kernel](jackson-kernel.md)

For $j_nf(x)=\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}f(x-t)J_n(t)\,dt$, evenness and unit mass reduce the error to an integral of second differences. The [Jackson kernel](jackson-kernel.md) bound and the scaling inequality for the [second modulus of smoothness](second-modulus-of-smoothness.md) yield $\|j_nf-f\|_\infty\leq C\omega_2(f,1/n)$. Since the degree is $2(n-1)$, a degree-$N$ estimate uses $n=\lfloor N/2\rfloor+1$. For $f\in C^2$ this gives $E_N(f)\leq C'N^{-2}\|f''\|_\infty$.
