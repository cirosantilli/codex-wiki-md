# Timelike curve

↑ **Parent:** [World line](world-line.md)

A [timelike curve](timelike-curve.md) has a nonzero [tangent vector](tangent-vector-split.md) with negative [Minkowski norm](minkowski-norm.md) in [metric signature](metric-signature.md) $(-,+,+,+)$ at every regular point. A physical massive observer's [world line](world-line.md) is also future-directed. Parametrizing it by coordinate time gives $-c^2+|\mathbf v|^2<0$, or $|\mathbf v|<c$: the local tangent condition is exactly the [subluminal speed](subluminal-speed.md) condition. It is stronger than checking only that two endpoints have [timelike separation](timelike-separation.md).
