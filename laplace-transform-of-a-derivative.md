# Laplace transform of a derivative

↑ **Parent:** [Laplace transform](laplace-transform.md)

Integration by parts gives

$$
\mathcal L\{f'\}(s)=s\widehat f(s)-f(0)
$$

whenever the boundary term at infinity vanishes.
