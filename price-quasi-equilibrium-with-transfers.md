# Price quasi-equilibrium with transfers

↑ **Parent:** [Exchange economy](exchange-economy.md)

A [price quasi-equilibrium with transfers](price-quasi-equilibrium-with-transfers.md) is an exactly feasible [consumption allocation](consumption-allocation.md) $f$ and a nonzero price vector $p$ for which, almost everywhere, every strictly preferred bundle has cost at least $p\cdot f(a)$. The transfer-adjusted wealth is $p\cdot f(a)$; transfers $p\cdot(f(a)-\omega(a))$ sum to zero. Equal-cost strict improvements may remain, which distinguishes a quasi-equilibrium from a utility-maximizing equilibrium. Nonnegative prices need extra assumptions if goods may be undesirable.
