# Geometrizable three-manifold

↑ **Parent:** [3-manifold](3-manifold.md)

A closed orientable [three-manifold](3-manifold.md) is geometrizable if its prime summands can be split along finitely many disjoint essential [tori](torus.md) into pieces whose interiors carry complete geometric structures modeled on the eight [Thurston geometries](thurston-geometry.md). Prime decomposition and capping sphere boundaries precede the torus cuts; a single homogeneous geometry on the original connected sum is not required.
