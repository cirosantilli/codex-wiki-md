# Intermodal dispersion

↑ **Parent:** [Wave dispersion](wave-dispersion.md)

[Intermodal dispersion](intermodal-dispersion.md) is the temporal separation of components in different propagating [normal modes](normal-mode.md) because their [group velocities](group-velocity.md) differ. A pulse exciting several modes can therefore broaden even when its individual modal components have negligible bandwidth. It is distinct from [group velocity dispersion](group-velocity-dispersion.md) within a single mode.
