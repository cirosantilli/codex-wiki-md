# Quasilinear diffusion in action space

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar dynamics](stellar-dynamics.md)

The second-order, angle-averaged evolution of a [galactic distribution function](galactic-distribution-function.md) caused by weak fluctuating gravitational perturbations. In [action-angle variables](action-angle-variables.md), the first-order response to a [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md) harmonic feeds a second-order flux proportional to $\mathbf k(\mathbf k\cdot\partial_{\mathbf J}f_0)$ and to the perturbation's power. Orbital [resonances](resonance.md) select where transport is strongest; a finite pulse produces [Gaussian resonance broadening](gaussian-resonance-broadening.md) when its time envelope is a [Gaussian function](gaussian-function.md). The general flux retains time memory until a further short-memory or pulse-integrated approximation is made.
