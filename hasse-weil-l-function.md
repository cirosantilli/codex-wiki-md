# Hasse-Weil L-function

↑ **Parent:** [L-function](l-function.md)

For an [elliptic curve](elliptic-curve.md) over a [number field](number-field.md), its Hasse-Weil L-function is the product of the local factors determined by its [Tate module](tate-module.md). At a good finite place, the factor is $(1-a_vNv^{-s}+Nv^{1-2s})^{-1}$, with $a_v=Nv+1-\#E(\kappa(v))$. At a bad place use the inertia-invariant subspace and an auxiliary prime different from the residue characteristic. For a [CM elliptic curve](cm-elliptic-curve.md) with all endomorphisms defined over the base, this factors as the product of the two conjugate [Hecke L-functions](hecke-l-function.md).
