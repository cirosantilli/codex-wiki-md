# Lorentz covariance of the Dirac operator

↑ **Parent:** [Dirac equation](dirac-equation.md)

A spinor Lorentz matrix satisfies $S^{-1}\gamma^\mu S=\Lambda^\mu{}_{\nu}\gamma^\nu$. Combining this identity with the transformed derivative shows that $(i\gamma^\mu\partial'_\mu-m)\Psi'=S(i\gamma^\mu\partial_\mu-m)\Psi$. Thus a [Lorentz transformation](lorentz-transformation.md) carries every solution of the [Dirac equation](dirac-equation.md) to another.
