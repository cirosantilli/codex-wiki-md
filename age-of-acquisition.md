# Age of acquisition

↑ **Parent:** [Language acquisition](language-acquisition.md)

The age at which [learning](learning.md) of a [language](language.md) begins, distinguished from current proficiency and duration of use. Delayed access to any first [language](language.md) can affect later [language processing](language-processing.md) and is not equivalent to [deafness](deafness.md).
