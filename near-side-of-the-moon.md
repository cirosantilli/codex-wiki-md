# Near side of the Moon

↑ **Parent:** [Moon](moon.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Near_side_of_the_Moon)

The [near side of the Moon](near-side-of-the-moon.md) is the hemisphere directed approximately toward [Earth](earth.md). [Libration](libration.md) changes the exact visible boundary, so [synchronous rotation](synchronous-rotation.md) does not mean that an observer always sees precisely the same hemisphere.
