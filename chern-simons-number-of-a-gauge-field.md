# Chern-Simons number of a gauge field

↑ **Parent:** [Chern-Simons 3-form](chern-simons-3-form.md)

For an oriented three-dimensional slice $\Sigma$, use the [Skew-Hermitian](skew-hermitian-matrix.md) trace convention

$$
N_{\rm CS}[A]= -\frac1{8\pi^2}\int_\Sigma\operatorname{tr}\left(A\wedge dA+\frac23A\wedge A\wedge A\right).
$$

Since the differential of the integrand is $\operatorname{tr}(F\wedge F)$, a space-time slab oriented by $dt\wedge\operatorname{vol}_\Sigma$ has [instanton](instanton.md) charge $k=N_{\rm CS}(t_+)-N_{\rm CS}(t_-)$ when the side boundary contributes no flux. For a pure gauge $A=h^{-1}dh$, this reduces to the winding integral $(24\pi^2)^{-1}\int_\Sigma\operatorname{tr}(h^{-1}dh)^3$. [Gauge transformations](gauge-transformation.md) of nonzero winding can shift it by an [integer](integer.md).
