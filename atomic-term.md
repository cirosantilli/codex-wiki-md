# Atomic term

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic physics](atomic-physics-split.md)

An [LS coupling](ls-coupling.md) multiplet with specified total orbital [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) $L$ and total [spin](spin.md) $S$, usually within a specified [electron configuration](electron-configuration.md). Its magnetic states have $M_L=-L,\ldots,L$ and $M_S=-S,\ldots,S$. [Spin-orbit coupling](spin-orbit-interaction.md) splits it into [atomic fine-structure levels](atomic-fine-structure-level.md).
