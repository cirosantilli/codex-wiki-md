# Sensible-heat polynya

↑ **Parent:** [Polynya](polynya.md)

A sensible-heat polynya is maintained by ocean heat delivered through currents, mixing or upwelling, sufficient to inhibit local [sea ice](sea-ice.md) growth. Mechanical export and sensible heating may act together, so this classification identifies a dominant mechanism rather than an exclusive geographical class.
