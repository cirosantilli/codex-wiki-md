# Curvature drift

↑ **Parent:** [Guiding-centre drift](guiding-centre-drift.md)

For a curved field direction $\mathbf b=\mathbf B/B$, define the curvature vector $\boldsymbol\kappa=(\mathbf b\cdot\nabla)\mathbf b$. Motion along curved field lines produces a gyroaveraged drift perpendicular to both field and curvature. In a purely toroidal field this and [magnetic gradient drift](magnetic-gradient-drift.md) cause opposite vertical drifts for opposite charge signs.
