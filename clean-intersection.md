# Clean intersection

↑ **Parent:** [Embedded submanifold](embedded-submanifold.md)

Submanifolds $A,B\subseteq M$ intersect cleanly when $A\cap B$ is an [embedded submanifold](embedded-submanifold.md) and

$$
T_x(A\cap B)=T_xA\cap T_xB
$$

at every $x\in A\cap B$. A [transverse intersection](transverse-intersection.md) is the special case in which $T_xA+T_xB=T_xM$.
