# Noninjective surjective shift on a product of fields

↑ **Parent:** [Direct product of rings](direct-product-of-rings.md)

For a [field](field.md) $k$, the shift on the [direct product of rings](direct-product-of-rings.md) $\prod_{n\geq0}k$ preserves coordinatewise addition, multiplication and the unit. It is a [surjective](surjective-function.md) [ring endomorphism](ring-endomorphism.md) because an arbitrary first coordinate can be prepended to a desired output. Its [kernel of a ring homomorphism](kernel-of-a-ring-homomorphism.md) consists of the sequences supported in coordinate zero, so it is not [injective](injective-function.md). The [ring](ring.md) is not [Noetherian](noetherian-ring.md): the [ideals](ideal.md) generated by the first $n+1$ coordinate [idempotents](idempotent.md) form a strictly ascending chain.
