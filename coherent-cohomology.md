# Coherent cohomology

↑ **Parent:** [Sheaf cohomology](sheaf-cohomology.md)

Coherent cohomology means [sheaf cohomology](sheaf-cohomology.md) with coefficients in a [coherent sheaf](coherent-sheaf.md). On a separated [Noetherian scheme](noetherian-scheme.md), a finite affine cover computes it using the [Čech cochain complex](cech-cochain-complex.md), because intersections of affine opens are affine and positive-degree [quasi-coherent sheaf](quasi-coherent-sheaf.md) cohomology vanishes there. Short exact sequences give [long exact sequences in sheaf cohomology](long-exact-sequence-in-sheaf-cohomology.md).
