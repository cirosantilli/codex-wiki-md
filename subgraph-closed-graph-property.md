# Subgraph-closed graph property

↑ **Parent:** [Graph property](graph-property.md)

Membership is preserved under deleting [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) and [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md). This is the decreasing meaning of monotonicity in extremal [graph](graph-split.md) enumeration; it differs from an increasing [monotone graph property](monotone-graph-property.md) used for random-graph thresholds. For a proper unbounded class, its least excluded chromatic number determines the leading quadratic logarithm of its labelled speed.
