# Bollobas set-pairs inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Extremal set theory](extremal-set-theory-split.md)

For disjoint pairs $A_i,B_i$ such that $A_i\cap B_j\ne\varnothing$ whenever $i\ne j$, the displayed sum is at most one. In a uniformly random [permutation](permutation.md), the events that all of $A_i$ precedes all of $B_i$ are [disjoint events](disjoint-events.md), and their probabilities are the summands. Taking $B_i$ to be the complement of the members $A_i$ of an [antichain](antichain.md) recovers the [LYM inequality](lubell-yamamoto-meshalkin-inequality.md).
