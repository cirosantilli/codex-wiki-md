# Maximal nine-dimensional supergravity

↑ **Parent:** [Supergravity](supergravity.md)

The ungauged maximal nine-dimensional [supergravity](supergravity.md) zero-mode multiplet has one metric, three vectors, two two-forms, one three-form and three scalars, giving $128$ bosonic states. Two Majorana [gravitini](gravitino.md) and four Majorana spin-one-half fields give $128$ fermionic states. Flat-circle reductions of massless type IIA and type IIB theories, and a flat-two-torus reduction of eleven-dimensional [supergravity](supergravity.md), give this same nonchiral spectrum.
