# Complex beam parameter

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian beam](gaussian-beam.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_beam_parameter)

The [complex beam parameter](complex-beam-parameter.md) combines a [Gaussian beam](gaussian-beam.md)'s [wavefront](wavefront.md) curvature $R_b$ and spot radius $w$: $1/q=1/R_b-i\lambda/(\pi w^2)$. With $q=z+iz_R$, the [beam waist](beam-waist.md) is $q=iz_R$. A real paraxial [ray transfer matrix](ray-transfer-matrix-analysis.md) maps it by $q'=(Aq+B)/(Cq+D)$. Positive imaginary part selects the physical finite-width branch in this convention.
