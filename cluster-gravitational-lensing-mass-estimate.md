# Cluster gravitational-lensing mass estimate

↑ **Parent:** [Galaxy cluster](galaxy-cluster.md)

[Gravitational lensing](gravitational-lensing.md) constrains a [galaxy cluster](galaxy-cluster.md)'s projected mass independently of its light output or dynamical equilibrium. The [lensing convergence](lensing-convergence.md) is projected [surface mass density](projected-surface-mass-density.md) divided by $\Sigma_{\rm crit}=c^2D_s/(4\pi GD_dD_{ds})$, using the appropriate [angular diameter distances](angular-diameter-distance.md). [Strong gravitational lensing](strong-gravitational-lensing.md) constrains the central mass through multiple-image geometry, and [weak gravitational lensing](weak-gravitational-lensing.md) constrains larger-scale projected mass through coherent image distortions. Converting these observations into a three-dimensional mass needs geometrical modelling and the relevant lensing degeneracies.
