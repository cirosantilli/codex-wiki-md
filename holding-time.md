# Holding time

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous-time Markov chain](continuous-time-markov-chain.md)

The holding time of a [continuous-time Markov chain](continuous-time-markov-chain.md) is the time spent in its current state before the next jump. In state $i$ it has an [exponential distribution](exponential-distribution.md) of rate equal to the total outgoing rate $q_i$.
