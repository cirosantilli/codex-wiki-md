# Helicity decomposition of a transverse Fourier mode

↑ **Parent:** [Helicity](helicity.md)

For $\mathbf k=k\hat z$, these complex vectors form an orthonormal basis of the transverse plane using the Hermitian [inner product](inner-product.md). They have opposite phases under rotations about $\hat z$, so they describe the two [helicity](helicity.md) components. Since $\mathbf e\cdot m^{(\pm)}=\sin\theta e^{\pm i\varphi}/\sqrt2$, transverse-vector sources carry only the angular sectors $m=\pm1$ of [spherical harmonics](spherical-harmonic.md). Streaming by $ik\cos\theta$ preserves $m$ and couples neighboring angular degrees.
