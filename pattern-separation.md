# Pattern separation

↑ **Parent:** [Associative memory in neural networks](associative-memory-in-neural-networks.md)

A transformation that makes representations of similar inputs less overlapping, helping reduce interference between stored events. It must retain enough information for useful downstream learning and retrieval. The [dentate gyrus](dentate-gyrus.md) is a proposed substrate, with quantitative input–output evidence from spatial cue-conflict experiments.[Abstract; simultaneous DG/[CA3](hippocampal-ca3.md) recordings during local/global cue conflict](ca3-retrieves-coherent-representations-from-degraded-input-direct-evidence-for-ca3-pattern-completion-and-dentate-gyrus-pattern-separation-by-joshua-p-neunuebel-and-others.md)
