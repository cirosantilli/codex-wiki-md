# Kan extension

↑ **Parent:** [Functor](functor.md)

A [left Kan extension](left-kan-extension.md) is [left adjoint](adjoint-functors.md) to precomposition, when it exists; a [Right Kan extension](right-kan-extension.md) is [right adjoint](adjoint-functors.md). For small indexing categories and set-valued [functors](functor.md), both exist by comma-category [colimit](colimit.md) and limit formulas. These two adjoints give the [essential geometric morphism](essential-geometric-morphism.md) induced by a [functor](functor.md) between presheaf indexing categories.
