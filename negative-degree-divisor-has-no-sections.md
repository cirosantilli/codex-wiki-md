# Negative-degree divisor has no sections

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann-Roch space](riemann-roch-space.md)

If a [divisor on an algebraic curve](divisor-on-an-algebraic-curve.md) $D$ on a [smooth projective curve](smooth-projective-curve.md) has negative [degree of a divisor](degree-of-a-divisor.md), then its [Riemann-Roch space](riemann-roch-space.md) is zero. Otherwise a nonzero $f\in L(D)$ would make $(f)+D$ an [effective divisor](effective-cartier-divisor.md) of degree $\deg D<0$, since a [principal divisor](principal-divisor-on-an-algebraic-curve.md) has degree zero. This is impossible. In [genus](genus-of-a-surface.md) one the [Riemann-Roch theorem](riemann-roch-theorem.md) consequently gives $\ell(D)=\deg D$ for every divisor of positive degree.
