# Eventown theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Extremal set theory](extremal-set-theory-split.md)

If every member of a [set family](set-family.md) on $[n]$ and every distinct pairwise [intersection](set-intersection.md) has even size, its [characteristic vectors](characteristic-vector-of-a-set.md) span a [self-orthogonal binary subspace](self-orthogonal-binary-subspace.md). The standard [dot product](dot-product.md) is [nondegenerate](nondegenerate-bilinear-form.md), so $W\subseteq W^\perp$ implies $2\dim W\leq n$. Thus the [set family](set-family.md) has at most $2^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}$ members. All unions of $\lfloor n/2\rfloor$ disjoint pairs of points attain this bound.
