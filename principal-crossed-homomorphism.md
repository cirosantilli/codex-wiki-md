# Principal crossed homomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Crossed homomorphism](crossed-homomorphism.md)

For $m\in M$, the map $g\mapsto g\cdot m-m$ is a principal crossed homomorphism. It is the [group coboundary](group-coboundary.md) of the zero-cochain $m$, so first [group cohomology](group-cohomology.md) is the quotient of crossed homomorphisms by principal crossed homomorphisms.
