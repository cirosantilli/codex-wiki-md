# Fractal

↑ **Parent:** [Fractal geometry](fractal-geometry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fractal)

A [fractal](fractal.md) is a set or pattern with structure at successively finer scales, often exhibiting [self-similarity](self-similarity.md) and a noninteger [Hausdorff dimension](hausdorff-dimension.md). [Fractal geometry](fractal-geometry.md) studies such objects.
