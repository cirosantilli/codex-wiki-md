# Kakutani solution of the Dirichlet problem

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian representation of the Dirichlet problem](brownian-representation-of-the-dirichlet-problem.md)

For a bounded [domain](domain-mathematical-analysis.md) whose boundary points are all [regular boundary points](regular-boundary-point.md), continuous [Dirichlet boundary data](dirichlet-boundary-data.md) $f$ have a unique solution

$$
u(x)=\mathbb E_x[f(B_{\tau_D})],\qquad\Delta u=0\text{ in }D,\quad u|_{\partial D}=f.
$$

Here $\tau_D$ is the [Brownian exit time](brownian-exit-time.md). The [Strong Markov property](strong-markov-property.md) gives the [mean value property for harmonic functions](mean-value-property-for-harmonic-functions.md); a [barrier for the Dirichlet problem](barrier-for-the-dirichlet-problem.md) gives continuity at the boundary. This is an existence theorem as well as the [Brownian representation of the Dirichlet problem](brownian-representation-of-the-dirichlet-problem.md) for an already known solution.
