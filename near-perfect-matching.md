# Near-perfect matching

↑ **Parent:** [Matching (graph theory)](matching-graph-theory.md)

In a [graph](graph-split.md) with $2n$ [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md), a near-perfect [matching in a graph](matching-graph-theory.md) has $n-1$ [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) and exactly two unmatched [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md).
