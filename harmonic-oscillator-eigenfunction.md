# Harmonic oscillator eigenfunction

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md)

With oscillator length $\ell=\sqrt{\hbar/(m\omega)}$, the eigenfunctions are proportional to $H_n(x/\ell)e^{-x^2/(2\ell^2)}$. They have $n$ real nodes, [parity](parity.md) $(-1)^n$, and energy $\hbar\omega(n+1/2)$.
