# Power objects turn coreflexive equalizers into coequalizers

↑ **Parent:** [Contravariant power-object functor](contravariant-power-object-functor.md)

For a [coreflexive pair](coreflexive-pair.md) $f,g:B\rightrightarrows A$ with equalizer $e:E\hookrightarrow B$, direct image along the mono $f$ satisfies $Pf\,\exists_f=1$ and $Pg\,\exists_f=\exists_e Pe$. Thus any map $h:PB\to Z$ equalizing $Pf,Pg$ obeys $h=h\exists_e Pe$. Since $Pe\exists_e=1$, the map $Pe$ is their [coequalizer](coequalizer.md). These identities hold for parameterized subobjects in any [elementary topos](elementary-topos.md).
