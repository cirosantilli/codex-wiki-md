# Thermal insulation

↑ **Parent:** [Heat transfer](heat-transfer.md)

[thermal insulation](thermal-insulation.md) reduces unwanted heat exchange. Conduction through supports, [convection](convection-split.md) in gas and [thermal radiation](thermal-radiation.md) require different treatments. [vacuum](vacuum.md) suppresses gas transport but leaves solid conduction and radiation; lowering [thermal conductance](thermal-conductance.md) also increases the [temperature](temperature-split.md) rise caused by a fixed internal heat load.
