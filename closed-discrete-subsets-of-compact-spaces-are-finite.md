# Closed discrete subsets of compact spaces are finite

↑ **Parent:** [Closed discrete subset](closed-discrete-subset.md)

Let $D$ be a [closed discrete subset](closed-discrete-subset.md) of a [compact space](compact-space.md) $X$. Closedness makes $D$ compact. Discreteness makes its singletons open in its [subspace topology](subspace-topology.md), so the singleton cover has a finite subcover. Thus $D$ is finite. Closedness is essential: the discrete set $\{1/n:n\geq1\}$ inside the compact interval $[0,1]$ is infinite and has the missing accumulation point zero.
