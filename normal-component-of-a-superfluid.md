# Normal component of a superfluid

↑ **Parent:** [Superfluid](superfluid.md)

The normal component of a [superfluid](superfluid.md) describes the momentum and entropy carried by excitations rather than the phase-gradient condensate current. Its [velocity](velocity.md) need not equal the [superfluid velocity](superfluid-velocity.md). In phenomenological condensate equations, prescribing its motion supplies a reference flow for dissipation; a full two-component fluid theory would also evolve its momentum and energy.
