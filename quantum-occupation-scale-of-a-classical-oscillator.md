# Quantum occupation scale of a classical oscillator

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md)

A classical oscillator of amplitude $A$ has [energy](energy-split.md) $m\omega^2A^2/2$. Comparing with the oscillator spacing $\hbar\omega$ gives the large-occupation scale displayed. A [coherent state](coherent-state.md) with this classical amplitude has exactly this [mean](expected-value-split.md) excitation occupation and a Poisson number distribution; its total [mean](expected-value-split.md) [energy](energy-split.md) also includes the zero-point term. An observed macroscopic amplitude does not, by itself, establish a pure [coherent state](coherent-state.md).
