# Faithful left adjoint criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Unit and counit of an adjunction](unit-and-counit-of-an-adjunction.md)

For $F\dashv G$, the left adjoint $F$ is [faithful](faithful-functor.md) exactly when every unit component $\eta_A:A\to GFA$ is a [monomorphism](monomorphism.md). Under the adjunction, equality after $\eta_A$ corresponds precisely to equality after applying $F$.
