# Reduced osmotic-pressure intercept

↑ **Parent:** [Osmometry](osmometry.md)

For [polymer](polymer-split.md) [mass](mass.md) [concentration](concentration.md) $c$, the leading ideal chain [pressure](pressure-split.md) is $RTc/M_n$. Plotting $\Pi/c$ against $c$ and extrapolating removes the leading virial correction. The intercept has units of energy per [mass](mass.md); without the measurement [temperature](temperature-split.md) it determines $M_n/T$, not an absolute molar [mass](mass.md).
