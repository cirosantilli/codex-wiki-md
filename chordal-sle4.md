# Chordal SLE4

↑ **Parent:** [Schramm–Loewner evolution](schramm-loewner-evolution.md)

Chordal [SLE](schramm-loewner-evolution.md) at parameter four has driver $2\beta_t$ in half-plane-capacity time. The [Itô formula](ito-s-lemma.md) makes the imaginary part of $\log(g_t(z)-W_t)$ drift-free, a feature used in the [SLE4 coupling with a Gaussian free field](sle4-coupling-with-a-gaussian-free-field.md). The trace is simple and avoids fixed nonzero boundary points.
