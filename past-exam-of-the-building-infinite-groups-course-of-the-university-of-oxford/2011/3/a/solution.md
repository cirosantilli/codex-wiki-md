<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $B=H_1\times\cdots\times H_m$, with each $H_i$ a copy of $H$. The [permutation](../../../../permutation.md) [group](../../../../group-split.md) $F\le S_m$ acts on $B$ by

$$
\alpha_\sigma(h_1,\ldots,h_m)_j=h_{\sigma^{-1}(j)}.
$$

Define the [permutation wreath product](../../../../permutation-wreath-product.md) to be the [semidirect product](../../../../semidirect-product.md)

$$
\boxed{H\operatorname{wr}F=H^m\rtimes_\alpha F.}
$$

Explicitly,

$$
(b,\sigma)(c,\tau)=\bigl(b\,\alpha_\sigma(c),\sigma\tau\bigr).
$$

Writing $h_i$ for the element with entry $h$ in coordinate $i$ and identity elsewhere, and writing $\sigma$ for $(1,\sigma)$, [conjugation](../../../../conjugation.md) satisfies

$$
\sigma h_i\sigma^{-1}=h_{\sigma(i)}.
$$

Thus the top [permutation](../../../../permutation.md) [group](../../../../group-split.md) permutes the coordinate subgroups in exactly its given action. Distinct coordinate subgroups commute, including when $H$ itself is nonabelian.
