# Confirmatory analysis

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical inference](statistical-inference-split.md)

Analysis evaluating a hypothesis or model whose relevant specification was fixed independently of the data used for its evaluation. The evidential interpretation depends on the complete analysis procedure, including selection and stopping rules.
