# Fluxoid

↑ **Parent:** [Fluxoid and flux quantization in a superconductor](fluxoid-and-flux-quantization-in-a-superconductor.md)

For uniform superconducting carrier density, the displayed combination of enclosed [magnetic flux](magnetic-flux.md) and the circulating [supercurrent](supercurrent.md) is quantized on a closed contour where the [order parameter](order-parameter.md) does not vanish. It follows by integrating the gauge-invariant phase gradient. Magnetic flux alone is quantized only when the current contribution on that contour is negligible.
