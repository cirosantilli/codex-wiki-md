# Hopf trace identity

↑ **Parent:** [Chain complex](chain-complex.md)

For an endomorphism of a finite-dimensional bounded [chain complex](chain-complex.md) over a [field](field.md), the alternating chain trace equals the alternating trace on [homology](homology-split.md). Split each chain space into boundaries, representatives for [homology](homology-split.md), and a complement mapping isomorphically to the preceding boundaries. The chain-map relation makes the traces on the first and third summands cancel in successive degrees, leaving the [homology](homology-split.md) traces. Over a [field](field.md) the induced [cohomology](cohomology-split.md) maps are dual and have the same traces. This identity turns the absence of diagonal simplex contributions into a vanishing [Lefschetz number](lefschetz-number.md).
