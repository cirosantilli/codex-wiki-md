# Subalgebra

↑ **Parent:** [Algebra over a field](algebra-over-a-field.md)

A [linear subspace](vector-subspace.md) of an [algebra over a field](algebra-over-a-field.md) closed under multiplication is a subalgebra. If a unital subalgebra is intended, it must also contain the ambient identity. A closed subalgebra of a normed algebra is closed in the [norm topology](norm-topology.md).
