# Compact real form of a complex semisimple Lie algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Semisimple Lie algebra](semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md)

A compact real form of a complex [semisimple Lie algebra](semisimple-lie-algebra-split.md) $\mathfrak g$ is a real [Lie subalgebra](lie-subalgebra.md) $\mathfrak u$ whose [complexification of a Lie algebra](complexification-of-a-lie-algebra.md) is $\mathfrak g$ and whose [Killing form](killing-form.md) is negative definite. Its simply connected [Lie group](lie-group.md) is compact. The positive internal [inner product](inner-product.md) used in unitary gauge theory is therefore $-\kappa$. For $\mathfrak{sl}_2(\mathbb C)$ the [SU(2) Lie algebra](su-2-lie-algebra.md) is a compact real form, whereas $\mathfrak{sl}_2(\mathbb R)$ is a different real form with indefinite Killing form.
