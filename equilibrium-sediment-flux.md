# Equilibrium sediment flux

↑ **Parent:** [Sediment transport saturation](sediment-transport-saturation.md)

The [equilibrium sediment flux](equilibrium-sediment-flux.md) is the transport supported locally by a prescribed bed [shear stress](shear-stress.md). A common idealization is $q_{\mathrm{sat}}=\phi_b\chi(\tau-\tau_{\mathrm{th}})_+^\gamma$. Linearization above the [sediment entrainment threshold](sediment-entrainment-threshold.md) requires perturbations small compared with the positive excess stress.
