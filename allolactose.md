# Allolactose

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical substance](chemical-substance-split.md)

A lactose-derived disaccharide that induces the [lac operon](lac-operon.md) by binding [LacI](laci.md). It links uptake and metabolism of the substrate to transcriptional control.
