# Chain in a partial order

↑ **Parent:** [Partially ordered set](partially-ordered-set.md)

A subset of a [partially ordered set](partially-ordered-set.md) on which the inherited order is total. The upper-bound hypothesis in [Zorn lemma](zorn-s-lemma.md) concerns all such subsets, not just countable increasing sequences.
