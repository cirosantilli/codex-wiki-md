# Dyadic transport of material line elements

↑ **Parent:** [Upper-convected derivative](upper-convected-derivative.md)

For a [material line](material-curve.md) tangent $\boldsymbol\ell$, $D_t\boldsymbol\ell=L\boldsymbol\ell$ with [velocity gradient](velocity-gradient.md) $L_{ij}=\partial_j u_i$. The [product rule](product-rule.md) gives $D_t(\boldsymbol\ell\boldsymbol\ell^T)=L\boldsymbol\ell\boldsymbol\ell^T+\boldsymbol\ell\boldsymbol\ell^TL^T$. Consequently the [upper-convected derivative](upper-convected-derivative.md) vanishes for any transported sum of these [outer products](outer-product.md). Under the derivative-index-first convention $G=L^T$ the same formula is $D_tA=G^TA+AG$. The distinction prevents transposition errors in [constitutive equations](constitutive-equation.md).
