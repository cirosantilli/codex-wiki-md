# Thermal cycler

↑ **Parent:** [Polymerase chain reaction](polymerase-chain-reaction.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thermal_cycler)

A [thermal cycler](thermal-cycler.md) changes the [temperature](temperature-split.md) of reaction tubes through a programmed sequence. A heated lid can reduce evaporation and condensation above the reaction. Instrument timing, sample [temperature](temperature-split.md) and nominal block [temperature](temperature-split.md) need not be identical.
