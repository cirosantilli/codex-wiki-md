# Inertial-wave dispersion relation

↑ **Parent:** [Inertial wave](inertial-wave.md)

Insert a transverse [plane wave](plane-wave.md) $\mathbf u=\mathbf u_0e^{i(\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf r-\omega t)}$ into the [inertial-wave vorticity equation](inertial-wave-vorticity-equation.md), with $\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf u_0=0$. It gives $\omega\mathbf k\times\mathbf u_0=2i(\boldsymbol\Omega\cdot\mathbf k)\mathbf u_0$. Applying $\mathbf k\times$ again and using transversality gives the title [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation-split.md). If $\theta$ is the angle to the rotation axis, $|\omega|=2|\Omega\cos\theta|$. At fixed direction the [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) does not depend on [wavelength](wavelength.md). Parallel wavevectors give the maximum $2|\Omega|$; perpendicular ones have zero frequency and belong to the nonoscillating balanced limit.
