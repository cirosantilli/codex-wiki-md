# Leibniz rule

↑ **Parent:** [Product rule](product-rule.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leibniz_rule)

The higher-order product rule is

$$
(fg)^{(n)}=\sum_{k=0}^n\binom nk f^{(k)}g^{(n-k)}.
$$
