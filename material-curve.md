# Material curve

↑ **Parent:** [Fluid mechanics](fluid-mechanics-split.md)

A material curve consists of the same [fluid elements](fluid-element.md) at all times. Differentiating their flow map gives $D\boldsymbol\ell/Dt=(\boldsymbol\ell\cdot\nabla)\mathbf u$ for its tangent. In [ideal magnetohydrodynamics](ideal-magnetohydrodynamics.md) a magnetic-field tangent obeys this same transport law after division by [mass density](density.md).
