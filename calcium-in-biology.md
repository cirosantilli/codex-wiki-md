# Calcium in biology

↑ **Parent:** [Biology](biology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calcium_in_biology)

Calcium has roles in cell signalling and skeletal structure. Dietary [calcium intake](calcium-intake.md) and [serum calcium](serum-calcium.md) are distinct quantities: physiological regulation means a change in one need not correspond to an equivalent change in the other.
