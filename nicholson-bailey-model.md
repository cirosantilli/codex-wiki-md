# Nicholson-Bailey model

↑ **Parent:** [Population dynamics](population-dynamics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicholson–Bailey_model)

The [Nicholson-Bailey model](nicholson-bailey-model.md) describes seasonal host-parasitoid interaction. [Hosts](host-biology.md) surviving random attacks reproduce, while [parasitoid](parasitoid.md) offspring are proportional to attacked [hosts](host-biology.md). For $r>1$, its positive [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) is $Y_*=(\log r)/a$, $X_*=rY_*/[c(r-1)]$. Counting attacked rather than surviving [hosts](host-biology.md) makes its [predator](predator.md) reproduction law different from the [annual pulse-breeding predator-prey model](annual-pulse-breeding-predator-prey-model.md).
