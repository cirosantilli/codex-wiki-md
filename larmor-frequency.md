# Larmor frequency

↑ **Parent:** [Larmor precession](larmor-precession.md)

The [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) of free [Larmor precession](larmor-precession.md) in a uniform [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) of magnitude $B$, with [gyromagnetic ratio](gyromagnetic-ratio.md) $\gamma$. Interactions can shift a collective resonance away from this free-spin scale.
