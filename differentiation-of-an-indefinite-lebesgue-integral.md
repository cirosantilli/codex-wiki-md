# Differentiation of an indefinite Lebesgue integral

↑ **Parent:** [Lebesgue differentiation theorem](lebesgue-differentiation-theorem.md)

If $g$ is locally [Lebesgue integrable](lebesgue-integrable-function.md) and

$$
G(x)=\int_a^x g(t)\,dt,
$$

then $G'(x)=g(x)$ at every [Lebesgue point](lebesgue-point.md) of $g$, and hence [almost everywhere](almost-everywhere.md).
