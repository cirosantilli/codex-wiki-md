# Allosteric modulator

↑ **Parent:** [Allostery](allostery.md)

A molecule binding away from the principal [ligand](ligand-biochemistry.md) site and changing its affinity or efficacy. The direction and size of modulation can depend on the orthosteric [ligand](ligand-biochemistry.md) and measured response.
