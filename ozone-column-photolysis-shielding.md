# Ozone-column photolysis shielding

↑ **Parent:** [Photolysis rate coefficient](photolysis-rate-coefficient.md)

In a direct-beam model with an effective [ozone](ozone.md) cross section $\sigma$, removal of an overlying column $\Delta N$ multiplies the transmitted [photodissociation](photodissociation.md) flux by $e^{\sigma\Delta N}$. A real [photodissociation](photodissociation.md) coefficient integrates wavelength-dependent cross section, [quantum yield](quantum-yield.md) and [actinic flux](actinic-flux.md), including scattering and the local radiation field.
