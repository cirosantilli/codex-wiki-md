# Incompressible limit of isotropic linear elasticity

↑ **Parent:** [Linear elasticity](linear-elasticity.md)

Fix a positive [shear modulus](shear-modulus.md) $\mu$ and let the other [Lamé parameter](lame-parameter.md) tend to infinity in $S_\lambda=\lambda\operatorname{tr}(e_\lambda)I+2\mu e_\lambda$. If [strain](strain.md) and [stress](stress.md) converge to finite limits and differentiation commutes with the limit, taking the [trace](matrix-trace.md) shows $\operatorname{tr}e_\lambda=O(\lambda^{-1})$. Thus $\operatorname{div}u=0$ and $S=-pI+2\mu e$ for $p=-\lim\lambda\operatorname{tr}e_\lambda$. Force balance becomes the displayed [Stokes equation](stokes-equation.md), with an elastic [shear modulus](shear-modulus.md) in place of fluid [dynamic viscosity](dynamic-viscosity-split.md). The vanishing [volumetric strain](volumetric-strain.md) is the linearization of [elastic incompressibility](elastic-incompressibility.md) $\det Dy=1$.
