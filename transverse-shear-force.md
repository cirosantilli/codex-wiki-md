# Transverse shear force

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic beam](elastic-beam.md)

The component of the internal resultant [force](force-split.md) perpendicular to an [elastic beam](elastic-beam.md)'s tangent. For a planar [elastic beam](elastic-beam.md), one consistent sign convention has [bending moment](bending-moment.md) $M=-Bw''$ and $M'=N$, so $N=-Bw'''$. Reversing the section or [bending moment](bending-moment.md) convention reverses the corresponding signs.
