# Orthogonal level curves of harmonic conjugates

↑ **Parent:** [Harmonic conjugate](harmonic-conjugate.md)

For a [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function-split.md) $f=u+iv$, the [Cauchy-Riemann equations](cauchy-riemann-equations.md) imply $\nabla u\cdot\nabla v=0$ and $|\nabla u|=|\nabla v|=|f'|$. At a point where $f'\ne0$, the two regular level curves therefore meet at right angles, since each tangent is perpendicular to its nonzero gradient. The noncritical condition is necessary: for $f(z)=z^2$, the zero level sets are the coordinate axes and the two diagonals, which meet at angles of $\pi/4$ at the critical point zero. Orthogonality at regular points is one geometric expression of conformality.
