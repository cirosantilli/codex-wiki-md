# Prequential log score identity

↑ **Parent:** [Logarithmic scoring rule](logarithmic-scoring-rule.md)

The probability chain rule makes a sum of sequential predictive log scores equal the log [Bayesian model evidence](bayesian-model-evidence.md) when the forecasts are coherent Bayesian one-step predictions under proper priors. Differences of total scores are therefore log [Bayes factors](bayes-factor.md).
