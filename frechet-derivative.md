<h1 id="frechet-derivative">Fréchet derivative</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Differentiable map](differentiable-map.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fréchet_derivative)

The Frechet derivative of $f$ at $a$ is the unique [linear map](linear-map.md) $Df_a$ such that

$$
\frac{\lVert f(a+h)-f(a)-Df_a(h)\rVert}{\lVert h\rVert}\to0.
$$
