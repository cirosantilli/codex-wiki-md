# Wall-metric properness criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Cubulation of a group](cubulation-of-a-group.md)

If $G$ acts on $(S,\mathcal W)$ and $x\in S$, then its action on the dual cube complex is metrically proper whenever

$$
d_{\mathcal W}(x,gx)\longrightarrow\infty
$$

as $g$ leaves every finite subset of $G$.
