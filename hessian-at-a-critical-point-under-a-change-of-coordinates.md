# Hessian at a critical point under a change of coordinates

↑ **Parent:** [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md)

Let $\phi$ be a twice continuously differentiable local [diffeomorphism](diffeomorphism.md), and suppose $\nabla f(\phi(u_*))=0$. The [chain rule](chain-rule.md) gives the displayed transformation of the [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md): terms containing second [partial derivatives](partial-derivative.md) of $\phi$ are multiplied by first [partial derivatives](partial-derivative.md) of $f$ and vanish at the [critical point](critical-point.md). By [Sylvester's law of inertia](sylvester-s-law-of-inertia.md), the numbers of positive, negative and zero [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are unchanged. Independently of nondegeneracy, the local [diffeomorphism](diffeomorphism.md) preserves the [critical point](critical-point.md)'s status as a [local minimum](local-minimum.md), [local maximum](local-maximum.md) or [saddle point of a scalar function](saddle-point-of-a-scalar-function.md), because it maps neighbourhoods to neighbourhoods.
