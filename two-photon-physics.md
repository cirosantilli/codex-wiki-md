# Two-photon physics

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum electrodynamics](quantum-electrodynamics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Two-photon_physics)

The study of interactions involving two incoming [photons](photon.md). Channels include elastic [light-by-light scattering](light-by-light-scattering.md) and [pair production](pair-production.md). A nonlinear response in a material and the direct vacuum [QED](quantum-electrodynamics.md) process have different physical mechanisms.
