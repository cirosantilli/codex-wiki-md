# Viscous-convective scalar range

↑ **Parent:** [Obukhov-Corrsin theory](obukhov-corrsin-theory.md)

For scalar diffusivity much smaller than [kinematic viscosity](kinematic-viscosity.md), a scalar has structure below the [Kolmogorov length scale](kolmogorov-length-scale.md) while the velocity is smooth. The strain rate $s\sim(\epsilon/\nu)^{1/2}$ transfers scalar variance, giving $dS_C/dr\sim\epsilon_c/(sr)$. Integration produces a logarithm plus an additive matching constant.
