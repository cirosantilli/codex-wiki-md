# Epicyclic motion

↑ **Parent:** [Radial epicyclic frequency](radial-epicyclic-frequency.md)

Epicyclic motion is the small oscillation of a displaced fluid element or particle about a circular orbit. Its natural radial frequency is the [radial epicyclic frequency](radial-epicyclic-frequency.md).
