# Shock wave

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar conservation law](scalar-conservation-law.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shock_wave)

A shock wave is a moving discontinuity of a [weak solution](weak-solution.md) to a conservation law. Its speed is fixed by the [Rankine-Hugoniot condition](rankine-hugoniot-conditions.md), while an entropy condition selects the physically admissible direction of compression.
