# Isobar (nuclide)

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic nucleus](atomic-nucleus-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isobar_(nuclide))

[Nuclear isobars](isobar-nuclide.md) are different [nuclides](nuclide.md) with the same [mass number](mass-number.md) $A$ and different [atomic numbers](atomic-number.md) $Z$. They differ from [isotopes](isotope.md), which have fixed $Z$. [Beta decay](beta-decay.md) connects neighboring [nuclear isobars](isobar-nuclide.md).
