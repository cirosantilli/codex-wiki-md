# Compression of an entropy sum

↑ **Parent:** [Entropy submodularity](entropy-submodularity.md)

A [union-intersection compression](union-intersection-compression.md) can only decrease the sum of [information entropies](information-entropy.md) $\sum_{A\in\mathcal A}H(X_A)$ for a finite-valued [random vector](random-vector.md) $X$. Each elementary step follows from [entropy submodularity](entropy-submodularity.md); iteration proves the assertion with [multiset](multiset.md) multiplicities. Uniform coordinate multiplicity gives [Shearer inequality](shearer-s-inequality.md) by compressing to repeated full sets and empty sets.
