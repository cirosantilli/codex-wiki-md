# Glacial lake outburst flood

↑ **Parent:** [Subglacial drainage](subglacial-drainage.md)

A [glacial lake outburst flood](glacial-lake-outburst-flood.md) is rapid release of [water](water.md) stored in or behind glacial [ice](ice.md) or its associated dam. Melt-driven enlargement of a subglacial channel can create [positive feedback](positive-feedback.md) between discharge, [frictional heating](frictional-heating.md) and further channel opening. Lake depletion and pressure-dependent [material creep](creep-deformation.md) closure then alter the event. Channel models explain one mechanism and should not be taken as a universal description of every glacial flood.
