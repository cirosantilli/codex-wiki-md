# Flat conformal Killing integrability

↑ **Parent:** [Conformal Killing equation](conformal-killing-equation.md)

For a flat [Conformal Killing vector field](conformal-killing-vector-field.md), let $\rho=\partial_\mu\omega^\mu$. Differentiate the [Conformal Killing equation](conformal-killing-equation.md) in three index orders, add two equations and subtract the third to obtain the displayed identity. Tracing gives $\Delta\omega_\alpha=(2-d)\partial_\alpha\rho/d$, whose divergence implies $\Delta\rho=0$ for $d>1$. Taking the divergence of the original identity gives $(d-2)\partial_\beta\partial_\gamma\rho=-\delta_{\beta\gamma}\Delta\rho$. Thus $\rho$ is affine and $\omega$ is at most quadratic when $d>2$.
