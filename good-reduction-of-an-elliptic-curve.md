# Good reduction of an elliptic curve

↑ **Parent:** [Reduction of an elliptic curve](reduction-of-an-elliptic-curve.md)

An elliptic curve has good reduction at a finite place when its reduced minimal Weierstrass equation is nonsingular, equivalently when its minimal discriminant has valuation zero.
