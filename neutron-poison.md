# Neutron poison

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear reactor](nuclear-reactor.md)

A constituent that absorbs [neutrons](neutron-split.md) and thereby reduces their availability for a [nuclear fission](nuclear-fission.md) chain reaction. Effectiveness depends on [isotope](isotope.md), [neutron](neutron-split.md) spectrum and spatial distribution, not just one thermal cross section.
