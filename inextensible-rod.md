# Inextensible rod

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic rod](elastic-rod.md)

An [inextensible rod](inextensible-rod.md) preserves material [arc length](arc-length.md), so its [stretch ratio](stretch-ratio.md) is one. In an unshearable [elastic rod](elastic-rod.md), $d\mathbf r/dS=\mathbf d_3$ for reference [arc length](arc-length.md) $S$.
