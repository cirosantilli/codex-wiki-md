# Translation covariance of a quantum field

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum field](quantum-field.md)

For a [quantum field](quantum-field.md) with [four-momentum operator](four-momentum-operator.md) $P^\mu$, spacetime dependence is implemented by the displayed conjugation. Taking a [matrix element](matrix-element.md) between [momentum eigenstates](momentum-eigenstate.md) gives $\langle q|\Phi(x)|p\rangle=e^{i(q-p)\cdot x}\langle q|\Phi(0)|p\rangle$. Differentiating gives $i\partial_t\Phi=[\Phi,H]$ and $-i\nabla\Phi=[\Phi,\mathbf P]$, connecting the [Heisenberg equation of motion](heisenberg-equation-of-motion.md) to spatial translation covariance.
