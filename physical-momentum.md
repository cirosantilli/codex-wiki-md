# Physical momentum

↑ **Parent:** [Four-momentum](four-momentum.md)

An observer with future-directed unit velocity $u^\mu$ measures particle energy $E=-u_\mu p^\mu$ and spatial [physical momentum](physical-momentum.md) given by the orthogonal projection of its [four-momentum](four-momentum.md) in the displayed formula. With signature $(-+++)$ and $p_\mu p^\mu=-m^2$, its squared magnitude is $p_{\perp\mu}p_\perp^\mu=E^2-m^2$. Components in the observer's orthonormal spatial frame agree with $\gamma m\mathbf v$. They differ in general from coordinate [canonical momentum](canonical-momentum.md) components. For a comoving observer in flat FRW, a conserved conventional spatial comoving covector has locally measured magnitude divided by the scale factor.
