# Complete lottery preferences

↑ **Parent:** [Preference relation](preference-relation.md)

A [preference relation](preference-relation.md) on [lotteries](lottery.md) is complete when every pair is comparable, allowing an [indifference relation](indifference-relation.md). Completeness does not require strict ranking of distinct [lotteries](lottery.md). Together with a [transitive relation](transitive-relation.md), it gives the ordering assumption in the [Von Neumann-Morgenstern utility theorem](von-neumann-morgenstern-utility-theorem.md).
