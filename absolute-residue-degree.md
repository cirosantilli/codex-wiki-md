# Absolute residue degree

↑ **Parent:** [Residue degree](residue-degree.md)

The absolute [residue degree](residue-degree.md) is the degree of the [residue field](residue-field.md) over its prime field. For a tower above $p$, degrees multiply: $[k_{\mathfrak q}:\mathbb F_p]=[k_{\mathfrak q}:k_{\mathfrak p}][k_{\mathfrak p}:\mathbb F_p]$. In a [Galois extension](finite-galois-extension.md) over $\mathbb Q$, the [decomposition group](decomposition-group.md) has a cyclic [quotient group](quotient-group.md) of this order by its [inertia group](inertia-group.md). This can exclude [residue degrees](residue-degree.md) even when the whole extension has that degree: $S_3$ has no cyclic [quotient group](quotient-group.md) of order six.
