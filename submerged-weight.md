# Submerged weight

↑ **Parent:** [Buoyancy](buoyancy.md)

For a grain of [mass density](density.md) $\rho_p$ and volume $\mathcal V$ in a fluid of [mass density](density.md) $\rho_f$, its effective downward [force](force.md) is $(\rho_p-\rho_f)g\mathcal V$, after subtracting [buoyancy](buoyancy.md) from weight.
