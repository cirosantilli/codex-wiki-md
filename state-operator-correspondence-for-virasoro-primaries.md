# State-operator correspondence for Virasoro primaries

↑ **Parent:** [State–operator correspondence](state-operator-correspondence.md)

For a [primary operator](primary-field.md), $T(z)\mathcal O(0)\sim h\mathcal O(0)/z^2+\partial\mathcal O(0)/z$. Taking the residue against $z^{n+1}$ gives the weight for $n=0$, the derivative descendant for $n=-1$, and zero for every $n>0$. Conversely the absence of positive-mode actions excludes higher poles in this operator product and gives the primary transformation law. The antiholomorphic argument is identical. Thus [primary states](primary-state.md) and [primary operators](primary-field.md) correspond under [radial quantization](radial-quantization.md).
