# Compilation of Thermal Properties of Hydrogen in Its Various Isotopic and Ortho-Para Modifications by Harold W. Woolley and others

↑ **Parent:** [Chemistry bibliography](chemistry-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by Harold W. Woolley](work-by-harold-w-woolley.md)

[https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/jres/041/5/v41.n05.a03.pdf](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/jres/041/5/v41.n05.a03.pdf)

Journal of Research of the National Bureau of Standards 41, 379–475 (1948), RP1932. DOI: [https://doi.org/10.6028/jres.041.037](https://doi.org/10.6028/jres.041.037)

Section III.2 and table 12 give ortho–para equilibrium composition and compare state sums with spectral and thermal evidence. The compilation distinguishes fixed-composition [normal hydrogen](normal-hydrogen.md) from equilibrium [spin isomers of hydrogen](spin-isomers-of-hydrogen.md) mixtures. It supplies institutional thermodynamic data and theory, rather than answers to a particular examination.
