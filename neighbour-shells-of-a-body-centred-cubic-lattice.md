# Neighbour shells of a body-centred cubic lattice

↑ **Parent:** [Body-centered cubic lattice](body-centered-cubic-lattice.md)

In a [body-centred cubic lattice](body-centered-cubic-lattice.md) of conventional edge $a$, a lattice site has eight nearest neighbours along body half-diagonals at distance $\sqrt3a/2$, then six next-nearest neighbours along the coordinate axes at distance $a$. There are two sites per conventional cell, so identical atoms of mass $m$ and [mass density](density.md) $\rho$ obey $\rho a^3=2m$. Specifying a neighbour shell matters: the nearest and next-nearest distances differ.
