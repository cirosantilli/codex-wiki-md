# Zonal flow

↑ **Parent:** [Geophysical fluid dynamics](geophysical-fluid-dynamics-split.md)

A flow along latitude circles, conventionally positive eastward. Its speed [Doppler shifts](doppler-effect.md) the intrinsic frequency of a disturbance. A stationary [Rossby wave](rossby-wave.md) in a uniform current requires the eastward [zonal flow](zonal-flow.md) to balance its westward intrinsic [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md).
