# The Mathematical Principles of Natural Philosophy by Isaac Newton (1846 edition)

[https://sites.pitt.edu/~jdnorton/teaching/2682_confirmation/2017/pdf/Principia_fragments/Principia_Bk3_to_Prop_15.pdf](https://sites.pitt.edu/~jdnorton/teaching/2682_confirmation/2017/pdf/Principia_fragments/Principia_Bk3_to_Prop_15.pdf)  
[https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Newton%27s_Principia_(1846)](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Newton%27s_Principia_(1846))

An English edition of the [Philosophiae Naturalis Principia Mathematica by Isaac Newton](philosophiae-naturalis-principia-mathematica-by-isaac-newton.md). The cited opening Book III propositions are available in the linked facsimile extract; the full edition permits verification of the Laws of Motion and Book I. Later additions must not be silently dated to the first edition: the General Scholium was added in 1713.
