# Commutator with powers of an eigenoperator

↑ **Parent:** [Commutator](commutator.md)

Suppose [linear operators](linear-operator.md) $A,B$ obey $[A,B]=cB$, where $c$ is a scalar, on a common invariant [operator domain](operator-domain.md). The [commutator product rule](commutator-product-rule.md) gives

$$
[A,B^n]=\sum_{r=0}^{n-1}B^r[A,B]B^{n-1-r}=ncB^n,\qquad n\geq1.
$$

For $n=0$ both sides vanish. This identity explains how powers of a [creation operator](creation-operator.md) shift the [number operator](number-operator.md) [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md).
