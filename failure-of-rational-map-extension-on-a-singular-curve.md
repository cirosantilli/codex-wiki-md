# Failure of rational-map extension on a singular curve

↑ **Parent:** [Extension of a rational map from a smooth projective curve](extension-of-a-rational-map-from-a-smooth-projective-curve.md)

Let $\nu:\widetilde C\to C$ be the [normalization of a nodal curve](normalization-of-a-nodal-curve.md). Its rational inverse $C\dashrightarrow\widetilde C$ cannot extend over the node: the two branches give two distinct points of $\widetilde C$, whereas a morphism can assign only one image to the node.
