# Cluster hydrostatic mass estimator

↑ **Parent:** [Intracluster medium](intracluster-medium.md)

For spherical thermal [hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md), $dP/dr=-\rho_gGM(<r)/r^2$. Inserting the [ideal gas law](ideal-gas-law.md) $P=\rho_gk_BT/(\mu m_p)$ with constant mean molecular weight gives the displayed estimator. The density $\rho_g$ is gas density, while $M$ includes every gravitating component. A varying $\mu$ adds $-d\log\mu/d\log r$ inside the logarithmic bracket. Nonthermal pressure requires differentiating the total supporting pressure rather than thermal pressure alone.
