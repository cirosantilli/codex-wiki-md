# Stein-Chen method

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md)

The Stein-Chen method compares an integer-valued count with a [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md) by solving the [Poisson Stein equation](poisson-stein-equation.md) and estimating its [expectation](expected-value.md) at the count. For sums of [indicator random variables](indicator-random-variable.md), dependency neighborhoods expose local overlap errors. This gives [total variation distance](total-variation-distance.md) bounds without having to compute the entire count distribution.
