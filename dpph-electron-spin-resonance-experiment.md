# DPPH electron spin resonance experiment

↑ **Parent:** [2,2-Diphenyl-1-picrylhydrazyl](2-2-diphenyl-1-picrylhydrazyl.md)

The University of Iowa's [Electron Spin Resonance, University of Iowa laboratory protocol](electron-spin-resonance-university-of-iowa-laboratory-protocol.md) (revised July 2007, Experiment and Resonance Data Analysis, printed pp. 2–6) specifies [DPPH](2-2-diphenyl-1-picrylhydrazyl.md) on cotton in a tube inside an RF coil, between [Helmholtz coils](helmholtz-coil.md). A bridge monitors RF absorption; an [oscilloscope](oscilloscope.md) records this output and the voltage across a $1\,\Omega$ current-monitor resistor. Sweeping the coil current passes through the resonant [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md). The protocol describes two dips when the sweep revisits resonance; these are repeated crossings, not two distinct spectral lines.

Its calibration replaces the sample with a [Hall effect](hall-effect.md) probe, maps the [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) in $2\,\mathrm{mm}$ steps, and accounts for sample dimensions. At least four RF [frequencies](frequency.md) test the resonance relation. Sweep speed is adjusted to obtain comparable passages. Only a local peak-plus-background fit is prescribed, because the full shape is not Gaussian. The protocol also flags unexplained trailing spikes and possible electronics contributions. These controls constrain field and sweep errors; an empty-tube/cotton blank would be an additional background control, not a reported measurement in this protocol.[Electron Spin Resonance, revised July 2007: printed pp. 4–7, sweep behavior, field mapping, local fitting and anomalous spikes](electron-spin-resonance-university-of-iowa-laboratory-protocol.md)

For an isolated effective [spin one-half](spin-one-half.md) with dimensionless positive factor $g$, the basic resonance model is

$$
\Delta E=g\mu_{\rm B}|B|=h\nu,\qquad
g=\frac{h\nu}{\mu_{\rm B}|B_{\rm res}|}.
$$

Here $\mu_{\rm B}$ is the [Bohr magneton](bohr-magneton.md), $h$ is the [Planck constant](planck-constant.md), $\nu$ is the applied [frequency](frequency.md), and $B_{\rm res}$ is the calibrated resonant [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md). A change in [frequency](frequency.md) should shift the resonance field proportionally in this model. The factor $g$ describes the electronic [magnetic moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md) coupling, not the species' net [electric charge](electric-charge.md).

A resonance supports the presence of detectable [paramagnetism](paramagnetism.md). It does not, by itself, identify an unknown [radical](radical-chemistry.md): other species exhibiting [paramagnetism](paramagnetism.md), impurities, overlapping responses, and interactions with [atomic nuclei](atomic-nucleus.md) can complicate interpretation. Conversely, absence of a detectable resonance need not mean absence of [free radicals](radical-chemistry.md); a low [concentration](concentration.md), a short lifetime, or an unsuitable measurement range can conceal them. The occupancy and chain diagrams are original bookkeeping illustrations; neither is a measured or simulated [DPPH](2-2-diphenyl-1-picrylhydrazyl.md) spectrum.
