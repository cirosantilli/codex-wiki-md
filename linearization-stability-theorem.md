# Linearization stability theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic equilibrium point](hyperbolic-equilibrium-point.md)

If every Jacobian eigenvalue has negative real part, the equilibrium is locally asymptotically stable.

More generally, if the [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) is hyperbolic, the [Hartman-Grobman theorem](hartman-grobman-theorem.md) transfers the local attracting, repelling or saddle behavior of the linearized [vector field](vector-field.md) to the nonlinear flow. Two real [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) with opposite signs give a [saddle equilibrium](saddle-equilibrium.md); all real parts positive give a source. Purely imaginary or zero [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) require a separate nonlinear analysis. Merely having distinct [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) does not remove this exception.
