# Scalar variance

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar transport](scalar-transport.md)

[Scalar variance](scalar-variance.md) measures spatial departure from the conserved mean. For zero mean, [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md) bounded by impermeable walls and homogeneous scalar [Neumann boundary conditions](neumann-boundary-condition.md), [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) gives $d\int\Theta^2/dt=-2\kappa\int|\nabla\Theta|^2$. [Advection](advection.md) preserves the instantaneous quadratic integral but can sharpen [gradients](gradient.md), allowing the [diffusion equation](diffusion-equation-split.md) to remove [scalar variance](scalar-variance.md) faster.
