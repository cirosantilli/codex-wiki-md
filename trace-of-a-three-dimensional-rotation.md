# Trace of a three-dimensional rotation

↑ **Parent:** [Rotation matrix](rotation-matrix.md)

A proper three-dimensional [rotation matrix](rotation-matrix.md) has an axis eigenvalue one and perpendicular-plane eigenvalues $e^{i\theta},e^{-i\theta}$. Their sum gives the displayed [trace](matrix-trace.md) identity. It determines the angle cosine but does not by itself choose the oriented axis or angle sign.
