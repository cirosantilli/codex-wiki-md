# Gas giant

↑ **Parent:** [Planet](planet.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gas_giant)

A large planet whose bulk mass includes a substantial [hydrogen](hydrogen.md)–[helium](helium.md) envelope, as in [Jupiter](jupiter.md) and [Saturn](saturn.md). The deep envelope is fluid rather than a dilute atmosphere with a sharp solid surface. Its cooling, compression and possible [electron degeneracy pressure](electron-degeneracy-pressure.md) influence the [mass-radius relation](mass-radius-relation.md).
