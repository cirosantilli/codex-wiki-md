<h1 id="focusing-schrodinger-lens-ansatz">Focusing Schrödinger lens ansatz</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Trapped focusing cubic ground-state equation](trapped-focusing-cubic-ground-state-equation.md)

If $P_\eta$ solves the [trapped focusing cubic ground-state equation](trapped-focusing-cubic-ground-state-equation.md), the ansatz

$$
u(t,x)=\lambda(t)^{-1}P_\eta(x/\lambda(t))
\exp\left(-\frac{i}{4}b(t)|x/\lambda(t)|^2+i\gamma(t)\right)
$$

solves the two-dimensional [mass-critical focusing nonlinear Schrödinger equation](mass-critical-focusing-nonlinear-schrodinger-equation.md) when

$$
b=-\lambda\lambda_t,
\qquad
\lambda^2b_t+b^2=-\eta,
\qquad
\gamma_t=\lambda^{-2}.
$$
