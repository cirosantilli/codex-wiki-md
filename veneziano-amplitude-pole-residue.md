# Veneziano amplitude pole residue

↑ **Parent:** [Veneziano amplitude](veneziano-amplitude.md)

In units $\alpha'=1$, generic fixed $t$ gives [simple poles](simple-pole.md) at $s=n-1$, $n\geq0$, with [residue](residue.md) $-\prod_{j=2}^{n+1}(t+j)/n!$. The empty product is one. A [residue](residue.md) zero removes that pole, and fixed $t$ must be away from its own channel singularities.
