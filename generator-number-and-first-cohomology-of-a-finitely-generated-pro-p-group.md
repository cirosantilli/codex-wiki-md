# Generator number and first cohomology of a finitely generated pro-p group

↑ **Parent:** [Pro-p group](pro-p-group.md)

For a finitely generated [pro-p group](pro-p-group.md), its [profinite Frattini subgroup](profinite-frattini-subgroup.md) has finite [elementary abelian](elementary-abelian-group.md) quotient. Continuous [group homomorphisms](group-homomorphism.md) to $\mathbb F_p$ are precisely the linear functionals on this quotient. The topological generation criterion identifies a minimal generating set with a basis, giving the displayed equality. Finite generation is essential when generator number means the least cardinality of an unrestricted dense generating family.
