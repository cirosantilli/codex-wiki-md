# Isobaric thermal instability

↑ **Parent:** [Isobaric thermal stability of a heated ideal gas](isobaric-thermal-stability-of-a-heated-ideal-gas.md)

At [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium-split.md), let $\mathcal L(\rho,T)$ be net cooling per unit [mass](mass.md), including a specified heating law. A gas perturbation that stays at constant [pressure](pressure-split.md) obeys $c_p\,d\delta T/dt=-(\partial\mathcal L/\partial T)_p\delta T$. It grows when this derivative is negative. The isobaric approximation requires [pressure](pressure-split.md) communication faster than thermal evolution; a volumetric cooling function instead introduces a background [mass density](density.md) in the denominator. [Thermal conduction](thermal-conduction.md) alters the short-wavelength criterion.
