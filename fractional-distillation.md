# Fractional distillation

↑ **Parent:** [Distillation](distillation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fractional_distillation)

[Fractional distillation](fractional-distillation.md) brings rising [vapor](vapor.md) into contact with descending [liquid](liquid.md) in a [fractionating column](fractionating-column.md). Packing supplies contact area; it does not act as a sieve for individual [molecules](molecule-split.md). Repeated exchange can enrich the rising [vapor](vapor.md) in the more volatile component. Some condensed [liquid](liquid.md) returns downward as [reflux](reflux.md), rather than all being withdrawn as distillate.[Introduction, paragraphs on reflux and plates, trays or packing](distillation-by-hong-kong-university-of-science-and-technology.md)

An [equilibrium stage](equilibrium-stage.md) is an idealization of this contact. A real [fractionating column](fractionating-column.md) has finite contact time and imperfect wetting and mixing; its length is not automatically a count of [equilibrium stages](equilibrium-stage.md). Product withdrawal and [reflux](reflux.md) affect the separation together. Thus the [repeated ideal-stage enrichment](repeated-ideal-stage-enrichment.md) example illustrates the composition advantage of repeated equilibration, but supplies neither a column design nor a specified [reflux](reflux.md) requirement.[Hyper-TVT by ETH Zurich; §5.6 Efficiency, equilibrium versus actual plates and flow-dependent efficiency](hyper-tvt-by-eth-zurich/distillation-efficiency.md)
