# Knot exterior

↑ **Parent:** [Knot](knot.md)

The knot exterior is $E_K=S^3\setminus\operatorname{int}\nu K$, where $\nu K$ is an open tubular neighborhood of the knot. It is homotopy equivalent to the [knot complement](knot-exterior.md) $S^3\setminus K$.
