# Reduced Brillouin zone

↑ **Parent:** [Brillouin zone](brillouin-zone.md)

A [reduced Brillouin zone](reduced-brillouin-zone.md) is the reciprocal primitive cell for an enlarged real-space unit cell. Doubling a one-dimensional unit cell halves the [momentum](momentum-split.md) interval but adds a second internal sublattice degree of freedom. Band folding therefore preserves the number of [normal modes](normal-mode.md).
