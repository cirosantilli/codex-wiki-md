# Larmor precession

↑ **Parent:** [Spin](spin.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Larmor_precession)

[Larmor precession](larmor-precession.md) is the precession of a magnetic moment about an applied magnetic field. For a spin with gyromagnetic ratio $\gamma$ and Hamiltonian $H=-\gamma\mathbf B\cdot\mathbf S$, its expectation obeys $d\langle\mathbf S
angle/dt=\gamma\langle\mathbf S
angle	imes\mathbf B$. A spin coherent state retains its coherent form during this rotation.
