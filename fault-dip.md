# Fault dip

↑ **Parent:** [Fault plane](fault-plane.md)

The [fault dip](fault-dip.md) is the angle between a [fault plane](fault-plane.md) and a horizontal plane, measured perpendicular to [fault strike](fault-strike.md) towards the descending side. It lies between $0$ and $90^\circ$.
