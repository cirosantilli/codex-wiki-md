# Lax pair for the Bogomolny equations

↑ **Parent:** [Lax pair](lax-pair.md)

For $D_i=\partial_i+A_i$ and Higgs multiplication $\Phi$, set

$$
L_0=D_1+iD_2-\lambda(D_3+i\Phi),\qquad L_1=D_3-i\Phi+\lambda(D_1-iD_2).
$$

Write $a=F_{12}-D_3\Phi$, $b=F_{31}-D_2\Phi$, $c=F_{23}-D_1\Phi$, where $D_i\Phi=\partial_i\Phi+[A_i,\Phi]$. Direct expansion gives $[L_0,L_1]=-b+ic-2i\lambda a-\lambda^2(b+ic)$. Its vanishing for every complex $\lambda$ is therefore equivalent to $a=b=c=0$, the [Bogomolny equations](bogomolny-equations.md). It is a parameter-dependent [zero-curvature representation](zero-curvature-condition.md).
