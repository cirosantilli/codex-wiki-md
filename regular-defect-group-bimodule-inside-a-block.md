# Regular defect-group bimodule inside a block

↑ **Parent:** [Defect group of a block](defect-group-of-a-block.md)

For a block idempotent $b$ with defect $D$, the [Brauer quotient of a module](brauer-quotient-of-a-module.md) of its [modular block](block-of-a-group-algebra.md) [bimodule](bimodule.md) at $\Delta D$ is nonzero: on the fixed basis $C_G(D)$, the block projection acts by $\operatorname{Br}_D(b)$. Restriction to $D\times D$ splits into transitive [permutation modules](permutation-module.md); their stabilizers have order at most $|D|$. A nonzero quotient at $\Delta D$ therefore forces a summand with stabilizer exactly $\Delta D$, which is the regular $kD$ [bimodule](bimodule.md). Tensoring on the right with any $kD$-module $U$ gives $U\mid\operatorname{Res}_D^G(b\operatorname{Ind}_D^G U)$. Consequently [finite representation type](finite-representation-type.md) of the [modular block](block-of-a-group-algebra.md) forces finite representation type of $kD$.
