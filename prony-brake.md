# Prony brake

↑ **Parent:** [Stirling engine](stirling-engine.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prony_brake)

A Prony brake measures shaft [power](power.md) using a resisting band: its [force](force-split.md) difference gives [torque](torque.md) $\tau=r(F_1-F_2)$, and rotation gives $P=\tau\omega$, where $\omega$ is [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md). The [Stirling engine](stirling-engine.md) experiment above uses this independent load measurement to distinguish shaft output from gas-cycle [work](work.md).
