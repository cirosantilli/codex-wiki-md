# Thermal relic

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmological particle freeze-out](cosmological-particle-freeze-out.md)

A [thermal relic](thermal-relic.md) is a surviving particle population whose earlier abundance was set by chemical [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium.md) with the cosmic plasma. After number-changing reactions cease and cosmic [entropy](entropy.md) is conserved, its comoving abundance is described by the yield $Y=n/s$. A relativistically decoupled species can retain an appreciable yield, whereas a species remaining in [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium.md) after becoming nonrelativistic is subject to [Boltzmann suppression](boltzmann-suppression.md). Its later mass density, lifetime and momentum distribution determine its cosmological effects.
