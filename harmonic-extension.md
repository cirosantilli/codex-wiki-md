# Harmonic extension

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet problem](dirichlet-problem.md)

A harmonic extension of boundary data $f$ is a [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md) satisfying those [Dirichlet boundary data](dirichlet-boundary-data.md) in a specified solution class. Existence and uniqueness depend on the domain, the regularity of the data and, on unbounded domains, growth conditions. A [Poisson kernel](poisson-kernel.md) constructs such an extension on standard domains.
