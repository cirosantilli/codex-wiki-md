# Randomized rounding

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical optimization](mathematical-optimization-split.md)

A method turning a solution of a continuous optimization relaxation into a random feasible solution of a discrete problem. An expected objective bound can give an approximation guarantee. [Gaussian hyperplane rounding](gaussian-hyperplane-rounding.md) and [Rademacher rounding for a semidefinite relaxation](rademacher-rounding-for-a-semidefinite-relaxation.md) are different examples.
