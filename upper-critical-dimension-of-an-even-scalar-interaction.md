# Upper critical dimension of an even scalar interaction

↑ **Parent:** [Upper critical dimension](upper-critical-dimension.md)

At the [Gaussian fixed point](gaussian-fixed-point.md), the [engineering dimension](engineering-dimension.md) of the coupling of $\phi^{2n}$ is $2n-(n-1)d$. It vanishes at the [upper critical dimension](upper-critical-dimension.md) $d_c=2n/(n-1)$.
