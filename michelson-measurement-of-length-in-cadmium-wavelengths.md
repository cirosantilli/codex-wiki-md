# Michelson measurement of length in cadmium wavelengths

↑ **Parent:** [Michelson interferometer](michelson-interferometer.md)

[Albert Abraham Michelson](albert-abraham-michelson.md)'s 1907 lecture, [Recent Advances in Spectroscopy](https://www.nobelprize.org/uploads/2018/06/michelson-lecture.pdf), pp. 171–172, connects counting [interference fringes](interference-fringe.md) with a reproducible length standard. Broad or multiple [spectral lines](spectral-line.md) obscured fringes at large path differences; red [cadmium](cadmium.md) emission supplied a suitable source. His 1907 [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics-split.md) recognized optical precision instruments and their [spectroscopy](spectroscopy.md) and [metrology](metrology.md) applications.[Nobel Lectures, Physics 1901–1921; Michelson, Nobel lecture, printed pp. 171–172; official prize motivation, printed p. 158](nobel-lectures-physics-1901-1921/recent-advances-in-spectroscopy-by-albert-abraham-michelson.md)

His authored account of the metre comparison at Sèvres describes a heated, evacuated [cadmium](cadmium.md) discharge tube and a [spectrometer](spectrometer.md) selecting one [spectral line](spectral-line.md). A movable reference mirror was compared with pairs of silvered parallel surfaces defining intermediate standards. Counted [interference fringes](interference-fringe.md) established a short interval; repeated comparisons extended it to a decimetre and then a metre. White-light fringes located surface coincidence, while monochromatic fringes supplied counts and fractional orders. A thermally shielding box, firm pier, calibrated translation screw and tested [thermometers](thermometer.md) controlled drafts, vibration, travel and [temperature](temperature-split.md). Uniform [temperature](temperature-split.md) mattered in comparisons; the final standard required an absolute [temperature](temperature-split.md) reading. [Optical microscopes](optical-microscope.md) made the final comparison to the marked metre bar, limiting the whole result.[Michelson, Light Waves and Their Uses (1903), Lecture V, Figs. 70–77: intermediate standards, counting, temperature controls and final microscope comparison](light-waves-and-their-uses-by-albert-abraham-michelson/lecture-v-light-waves-as-standards-of-length.md)

This is a measurement chain, rather than one enormous unchecked fringe count: short intervals establish whole orders; fractional-order readings refine each longer comparison. The [optical coherence](optical-coherence.md) envelope helps locate white-light coincidence, whereas the carrier [phase difference](phase-difference.md) measures small departures from it. Repetition checks counting mistakes, but agreement alone cannot exclude shared [systematic errors](systematic-error.md). The general lesson of [metrology](metrology.md) is to preserve the link from the optical ruler through each transfer and control to the final length.
