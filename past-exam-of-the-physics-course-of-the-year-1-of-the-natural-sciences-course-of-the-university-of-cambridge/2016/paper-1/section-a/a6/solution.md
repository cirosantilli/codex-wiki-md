<h1 id="section-a/a6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A6](../a6.md)

The threshold must conserve both [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) and [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md). For head-on [photons](../../../../../photon.md), the total [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) is $E_1+E_2$, while the total [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) has magnitude $|E_2-E_1|/c$. Their combined [invariant mass](../../../../../invariant-mass.md) $M$ satisfies

$$
M^2c^4=(E_1+E_2)^2-c^2\left(\frac{E_2-E_1}{c}\right)^2=4E_1E_2.
$$

At the threshold for [photon-photon pair production](../../../../../photon-photon-pair-production.md), the [electron](../../../../../electron.md) and [Positron](../../../../../positron.md) have no relative [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) in their [centre-of-momentum frame](../../../../../center-of-momentum-frame.md), so $M=2m_e$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{E_{2,\min}=\frac{(m_ec^2)^2}{E_1}=\frac{(5.110\times10^5\ \mathrm{eV})^2}{10^{-3}\ \mathrm{eV}}\simeq2.61\times10^{14}\ \mathrm{eV}=261\ \mathrm{TeV}.}
$$

Simply equating the laboratory [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) to $2m_ec^2$ would miss the very large laboratory [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md): the newly produced pair must carry it away.
