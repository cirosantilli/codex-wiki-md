<h1 id="section-a/a1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A1](../a1.md)

**The [ground-state energies](../../../../../ground-state-energy.md) are $1.50\ \mathrm{eV}$ and $0.376\ \mathrm{eV}$, respectively.** For a [particle in a box](../../../../../infinite-square-well.md) of width $L$, the [wave function](../../../../../wave-function-split.md) must vanish at both infinite walls. The lowest nonzero [standing wave](../../../../../standing-wave.md) therefore has [wavelength](../../../../../wavelength.md) $2L$, giving the [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) eigenvalue

$$
E_1=\frac{\pi^2\hbar^2}{2m_eL^2}=\frac{h^2}{8m_eL^2}.
$$

Using the [electron mass](../../../../../electron-mass.md) and converting [joules](../../../../../joule.md) to [electronvolts](../../../../../electronvolt.md) gives

$$
\boxed{E_1(0.5\ \mathrm{nm})\simeq1.504\ \mathrm{eV},\qquad E_1(1\ \mathrm{nm})\simeq0.3760\ \mathrm{eV}.}
$$

Doubling the confinement length divides this [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) by four. A zero [potential energy](../../../../../potential-energy.md) inside the box does not imply zero total [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md): a constant, zero-[momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) [wave function](../../../../../wave-function-split.md) cannot satisfy both wall conditions unless it vanishes everywhere. This is a concrete manifestation of [quantum confinement](../../../../../quantum-confinement.md) and the [Heisenberg uncertainty principle](../../../../../heisenberg-uncertainty-relation.md).
