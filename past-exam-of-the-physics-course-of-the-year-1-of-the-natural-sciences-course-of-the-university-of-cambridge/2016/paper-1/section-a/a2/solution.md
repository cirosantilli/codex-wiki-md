<h1 id="section-a/a2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A2](../a2.md)

Write the proposed [Jeans mass](../../../../../jeans-mass.md) as $M_J=K v^aG^bP^c$, with dimensionless $K$. The dimensions of [speed](../../../../../speed.md), the [gravitational constant](../../../../../gravitational-constant.md) and [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) are

$$
[v]=\mathsf L\mathsf T^{-1},\qquad [G]=\mathsf L^3\mathsf M^{-1}\mathsf T^{-2},\qquad [P]=\mathsf M\mathsf L^{-1}\mathsf T^{-2}.
$$

Equating the exponents of [mass](../../../../../mass.md), length and time gives

$$
-b+c=1,\qquad a+3b-c=0,\qquad -a-2b-2c=0.
$$

Thus $a=4$, $b=-3/2$ and $c=-1/2$, so the [pressure-confined Jeans mass](../../../../../pressure-confined-jeans-mass.md) scales as

$$
\boxed{M_J=K\frac{v^4}{G^{3/2}P^{1/2}}.}
$$

A larger [speed of sound](../../../../../speed-of-sound.md) gives stronger support against [gravitational collapse](../../../../../gravitational-collapse.md); stronger confinement at fixed [speed of sound](../../../../../speed-of-sound.md) lowers the characteristic unstable [mass](../../../../../mass.md). **[Dimensional analysis](../../../../../dimensional-analysis.md) determines the scaling, not $K$**, which requires a particular cloud model and instability criterion.
