<h1 id="section-c/c10/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The two stored [energies](../../../../../../energy-split.md) follow directly by squaring the expressions for [electric charge](../../../../../../electric-charge-split.md) and [electric current](../../../../../../electric-current.md):

$$
E_C=\frac12CV_0^2e^{-2\gamma t}\left(\cos\omega_dt+\frac\gamma{\omega_d}\sin\omega_dt\right)^2,\qquad E_I=\frac{V_0^2}{2L\omega_d^2}e^{-2\gamma t}\sin^2\omega_dt.
$$

Both are nonnegative, with alternating maxima. Initially all stored [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) is in the [capacitor](../../../../../../capacitor.md); at a zero of $q$ it is entirely in the [inductor](../../../../../../inductor.md). The total is

$$
E=\frac{q^2}{2C}+\frac12L\dot q^2,\qquad \boxed{\frac{dE}{dt}=\dot q\left(\frac qC+L\ddot q\right)=-R I^2\leq0.}
$$

**The total [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) decreases monotonically, with a horizontal tangent whenever the [electric current](../../../../../../electric-current.md) is zero.** Its envelope decays on the $1/(2\gamma)=20\ \mathrm{ms}$ scale, but the exact total is not a featureless exponential: the resistor's instantaneous loss varies within each oscillation. The figure in part (a) shows this gradual modulation of the downward slope as well as the component [energies](../../../../../../energy-split.md).
