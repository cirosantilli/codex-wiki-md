<h1 id="section-c/c10/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [capacitor](../../../../../../capacitor.md) stores [electrostatic energy](../../../../../../electrostatic-energy.md), the [inductor](../../../../../../inductor.md) stores [magnetic energy](../../../../../../magnetic-energy.md), and the [resistor](../../../../../../resistor.md) converts [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) into heat. Their instantaneous values are

$$
\boxed{E_C=\frac{q^2}{2C}=\frac12CV_C^2,\qquad E_I=\frac12LI^2,\qquad P_R=RI^2.}
$$

The sign convention for [electric current](../../../../../../electric-current.md) does not change these expressions. With the initial [voltage](../../../../../../voltage.md), $E_C(0)=CV_0^2/2=90\ \mu\mathrm J$ and $E_I(0)=P_R(0)=0$.
