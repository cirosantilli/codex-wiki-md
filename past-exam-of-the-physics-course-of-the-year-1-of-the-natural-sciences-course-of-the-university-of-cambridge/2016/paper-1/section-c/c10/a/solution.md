<h1 id="section-c/c10/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Put $\omega_0^2=1/(LC)$. The initial conditions give

$$
q(t)=Qe^{-\gamma t}\left(\cos\omega_dt+\frac\gamma{\omega_d}\sin\omega_dt\right),\qquad V_C(t)=V_0e^{-\gamma t}\left(\cos\omega_dt+\frac\gamma{\omega_d}\sin\omega_dt\right).
$$

The printed [electric current](../../../../../../electric-current.md) arrow points toward the positively labelled [capacitor](../../../../../../capacitor.md) plate, so its signed [electric current](../../../../../../electric-current.md) is $I=\dot q$, not the positive discharge magnitude. Hence

$$
\boxed{I(t)=-\frac{V_0}{L\omega_d}e^{-\gamma t}\sin\omega_dt.}
$$

For the stated [electrical resistance](../../../../../../electrical-resistance.md),

$$
\gamma=25\ \mathrm{s^{-1}},\quad \omega_0\simeq447.21\ \mathrm{s^{-1}},\quad \omega_d\simeq446.51\ \mathrm{s^{-1}},\quad T_d\simeq14.07\ \mathrm{ms}.
$$

**The [voltage](../../../../../../voltage.md) starts at $6\ \mathrm V$ with zero slope; the signed [electric current](../../../../../../electric-current.md) starts at zero and initially becomes negative.** Its initial slope is $\dot I(0)=-V_0/L=-6\ \mathrm{A\,s^{-1}}$. Both oscillate with an $e^{-25t}$ envelope and a $40\ \mathrm{ms}$ amplitude [time constant](../../../../../../time-constant.md). The phase difference is close to a quarter period, with the small damping correction displayed explicitly in $V_C$.
