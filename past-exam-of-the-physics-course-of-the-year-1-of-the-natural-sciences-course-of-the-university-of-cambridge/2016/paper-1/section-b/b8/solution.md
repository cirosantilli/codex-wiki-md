<h1 id="section-b/b8/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B8](../b8.md)

[Conservation of energy](../../../../../conservation-of-energy.md) states that the total [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) of an isolated system is constant. In a mechanical problem with conservative [forces](../../../../../force-split.md) and no dissipation, it gives $K+U=\text{constant}$. It often determines a [speed](../../../../../speed.md) or turning height directly, without solving the full time-dependent [equation of motion](../../../../../equation-of-motion.md). With external work or dissipation, use the corresponding [energy balance](../../../../../energy-balance.md) instead.

After a drop leaves the wheel, neglecting air resistance, the conservative [force](../../../../../force-split.md) of the [Newtonian gravitational field](../../../../../newtonian-gravitational-field.md) acts. Put the origin at the wheel axis and choose positive horizontal displacement to the right. In the printed diagram $\theta$ is measured upward from the leftward horizontal radius, and rotation is clockwise. The release position and [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) are therefore

$$
(x_0,y_0)=(-r\cos\theta,r\sin\theta),\qquad (v_{x0},v_{y0})=(r\omega\sin\theta,r\omega\cos\theta).
$$

For upward release, $\cos\theta>0$. These conventions fix the signs used below.
