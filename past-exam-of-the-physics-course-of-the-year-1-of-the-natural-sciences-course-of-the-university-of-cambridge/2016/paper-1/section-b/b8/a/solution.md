<h1 id="section-b/b8/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

With zero [gravitational potential energy](../../../../../../gravitational-energy.md) at the wheel axis, the release [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) and the [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) at the apex of the [projectile motion](../../../../../../projectile-motion.md) are

$$
E_{\rm release}=\frac12mr^2\omega^2+mgr\sin\theta,\qquad E_{\rm apex}=\frac12mr^2\omega^2\sin^2\theta+mgy_{\max}.
$$

Only the vertical [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) vanishes at the apex: the horizontal [kinetic energy](../../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) remains. Equating the two [energies](../../../../../../energy-split.md) gives

$$
\boxed{y_{\max}(\theta)=r\sin\theta+\frac{r^2\omega^2}{2g}\cos^2\theta.}
$$
