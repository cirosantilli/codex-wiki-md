<h1 id="section-e/e15/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [Lorentz force](../../../../../../lorentz-force.md) determines the motion of a [charged particle](../../../../../../charged-particle.md):

$$
\boxed{\frac{d\mathbf p}{dt}=q(\mathbf E+\mathbf v\times\mathbf B).}
$$

At nonrelativistic [speeds](../../../../../../speed.md), $\mathbf p=m\mathbf v$. The [electric field](../../../../../../electric-field-split.md) does work at rate $q\mathbf E\cdot\mathbf v$, while the magnetic part of the [Lorentz force](../../../../../../lorentz-force.md) is perpendicular to [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) and does no work. A uniform [electric field](../../../../../../electric-field-split.md) gives constant [acceleration](../../../../../../acceleration.md) $q\mathbf E/m$; with a transverse initial [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md), the trajectory is a [parabola](../../../../../../parabola.md).

In a uniform [magnetic field](../../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md), the parallel [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) is constant, while the perpendicular component undergoes [circular motion](../../../../../../circular-motion.md). The angular [cyclotron frequency](../../../../../../cyclotron-frequency.md), orbit radius and helical pitch are

$$
\boxed{\Omega_c=\frac{|q|B}{m},\qquad r_L=\frac{mv_\perp}{|q|B},\qquad p_{\rm helix}=\frac{2\pi v_\parallel}{\Omega_c}.}
$$

The sign of $q$ determines the sense of rotation. For perpendicular uniform [electric fields](../../../../../../electric-field-split.md) and [magnetic fields](../../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md), the guiding centre has the [electric drift](../../../../../../electric-drift-in-crossed-uniform-fields.md) velocity

$$
\boxed{\mathbf v_D=\frac{\mathbf E\times\mathbf B}{B^2}.}
$$

Indeed, $\mathbf E+\mathbf v_D\times\mathbf B=0$, so the remaining motion is cyclotron rotation about that drifting centre, with possible motion along the [magnetic field](../../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md). The drift is independent of [electric charge](../../../../../../electric-charge-split.md) sign and [mass](../../../../../../mass.md). At relativistic [energies](../../../../../../energy-split.md), retain $\mathbf p=\Gamma m\mathbf v$ and the corresponding relativistic orbit radius rather than assuming constant nonrelativistic [acceleration](../../../../../../acceleration.md).
