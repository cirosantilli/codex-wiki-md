<h1 id="section-e/e15/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

[Electromagnetic induction](../../../../../../electromagnetic-induction.md) is the production of an [electromotive force](../../../../../../electromotive-force.md) by changing [magnetic flux](../../../../../../magnetic-flux.md) or by moving a conductor through a [magnetic field](../../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md). With the loop and surface orientations related by the [right-hand rule](../../../../../../right-hand-rule.md), [Faraday's law of induction](../../../../../../faraday-s-law-of-induction.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathcal E=\oint(\mathbf E+\mathbf v\times\mathbf B)\cdot d\boldsymbol\ell=-\frac{d}{dt}\int\mathbf B\cdot d\mathbf S.}
$$

Here $\mathbf v$ is the local wire velocity. For a stationary loop, the induced [electric field](../../../../../../electric-field-split.md) can have a nonzero closed-path integral; its differential form is $\nabla\times\mathbf E=-\partial\mathbf B/\partial t$. For a straight rod moving perpendicularly to itself and to a uniform [magnetic field](../../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md), the motional [electromotive force](../../../../../../electromotive-force.md) has magnitude $B\ell v$.

The minus sign expresses [Lenz's law](../../../../../../lenz-s-law.md): the induced [electric current](../../../../../../electric-current.md) produces a [magnetic field](../../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) that opposes the change in linked [magnetic flux](../../../../../../magnetic-flux.md). Pulling a conducting loop through a [magnetic field](../../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) therefore requires work against an electromagnetic resisting [force](../../../../../../force-split.md); that work supplies the resulting heat or stored [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md).

For an [inductor](../../../../../../inductor.md) with constant self-[inductance](../../../../../../inductance.md), $\mathcal E=-L\,dI/dt$ and its stored [magnetic energy](../../../../../../magnetic-energy.md) is $LI^2/2$. A changing [electric current](../../../../../../electric-current.md) in one coil can induce an [electromotive force](../../../../../../electromotive-force.md) in another through [mutual inductance](../../../../../../mutual-inductance.md), the operating principle of a [electrical transformer](../../../../../../transformer-electrical-engineering.md). These examples connect the flux law, the direction of the response and [conservation of energy](../../../../../../conservation-of-energy.md).
