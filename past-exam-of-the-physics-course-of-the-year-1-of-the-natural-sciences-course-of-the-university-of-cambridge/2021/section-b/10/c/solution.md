<h1 id="section-b/10/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Keep $+x$ along the upper-sheet current and $+z$ upward, putting the lower sheet at $z=0$ and upper sheet at $z=d$. Between them, the upper sheet's lower-side [magnetic field](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) is $+\mu_0I/(2w)\widehat{\mathbf y}$. The lower sheet carries current in $-x$, so its upper-side [magnetic field](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) is also $+\mu_0I/(2w)\widehat{\mathbf y}$. The [superposition principle](../../../../../superposition-principle.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\mathbf B=\frac{\mu_0I}{w}\widehat{\mathbf y}.}
$$

Each perfect conductor is an [equipotential](../../../../../equipotential.md). The circuit's [resistor](../../../../../resistor.md) carries current from upper to lower, so [Ohm's law](../../../../../ohm-s-law.md) fixes their [electric potential difference](../../../../../electric-potential-difference.md):

$$
V_{\rm upper}-V_{\rm lower}=IR.
$$

The broad parallel faces and their constant potentials make the [electric field](../../../../../electric-field-split.md) perpendicular to them and independent of the coordinates along them, away from edges. There is no volume [electric charge](../../../../../electric-charge-split.md) in the gap. Apply [Gauss's law](../../../../../gauss-s-law.md) to a small pillbox wholly within the gap, with equal face [areas](../../../../../surface-area.md) $A$ at heights $z_1,z_2$. Its side flux is zero, so

$$
[E_z(z_2)-E_z(z_1)]A=\frac{Q_{\rm enclosed}}{\varepsilon_0}=0.
$$

Thus $E_z$ is independent of height as well. Since $V_{\rm upper}-V_{\rm lower}=-\int_0^dE_z\,dz$,

$$
\boxed{\mathbf E=-\frac{IR}{d}\widehat{\mathbf z},\qquad E=\frac{IR}{d}.}
$$

It points from the higher-potential upper sheet to the lower sheet. [Gauss's law](../../../../../gauss-s-law.md) requires the planar [symmetry](../../../../../symmetry-physics.md) argument: zero enclosed charge alone would not prove a uniform [electric field](../../../../../electric-field-split.md) in a general geometry. These combined fields characterize [parallel current sheets with a resistive termination](../../../../../parallel-current-sheets-with-a-resistive-termination.md).
