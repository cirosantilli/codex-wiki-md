<h1 id="section-b/10/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For injected [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) $\mathbf v=v\widehat{\mathbf x}$, the [Lorentz force](../../../../../lorentz-force.md) is

$$
\mathbf F=q(\mathbf E+\mathbf v\times\mathbf B)=q\left(-\frac{IR}{d}+v\frac{\mu_0I}{w}\right)\widehat{\mathbf z}.
$$

For nonzero [electric charge](../../../../../electric-charge-split.md) and nonzero operating current, the [velocity selector](../../../../../velocity-selector.md) condition is

$$
\boxed{v=\frac EB=\frac{Rw}{\mu_0d}.}
$$

The requested nonrelativistic [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) is consequently

$$
\boxed{T=\frac m2\left(\frac{Rw}{\mu_0d}\right)^2.}
$$

Both [electric current](../../../../../electric-current.md) and [electric charge](../../../../../electric-charge-split.md) cancel. Reversing the sign of $q$ reverses both component [forces](../../../../../force-split.md) and preserves their cancellation. The dimensions are consistent because $R/\mu_0$ has units of [speed](../../../../../speed.md), while $w/d$ is dimensionless. The assumed [nonrelativistic particle](../../../../../nonrelativistic-particle.md) regime requires $Rw/(\mu_0d)\ll c$; the problem supplies no values with which to evaluate a numerical [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md).
