<h1 id="section-b/10/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Choose $+x$ along the [electric current](../../../../../electric-current.md), $+z$ upward normal to the sheet, and $+y$ to complete a right-handed frame. In the interior region far from its edges, treat the sheet as infinite in its plane. Its [surface current density](../../../../../surface-current-density.md) is

$$
\mathbf K=\frac Iw\widehat{\mathbf x}.
$$

Reflection and translational [symmetry](../../../../../symmetry-physics.md) make the [magnetic field](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) parallel to the sheet, perpendicular to its current, and equal in magnitude with opposite direction above and below. Choose an [Ampère's circuital law](../../../../../ampere-s-circuital-law.md) rectangle with length $s$ along $y$, straddling the full thickness. The two long sides each contribute $Bs$ with the same sign; on the vertical sides $\mathbf B\cdot d\mathbf l=0$ since $\mathbf B$ is horizontal. The enclosed [electric current](../../../../../electric-current.md) is $Ks=Is/w$. Thus

$$
2Bs=\mu_0\frac Iw s,\qquad\boxed{B=\frac{\mu_0I}{2w}.}
$$

The [right-hand rule](../../../../../right-hand-rule.md) fixes the directions:

$$
\boxed{\mathbf B_{\rm above}=-\frac{\mu_0I}{2w}\widehat{\mathbf y},\qquad\mathbf B_{\rm below}=+\frac{\mu_0I}{2w}\widehat{\mathbf y}.}
$$

The [current density](../../../../../current-density.md) inside the finite-thickness conductor is $\mathbf J=I/(wt)\,\widehat{\mathbf x}$, so integrating through thickness $t$ gives the same $\mathbf K$. These are exterior fields in the broad-sheet approximation, not exact finite-width edge fields.
