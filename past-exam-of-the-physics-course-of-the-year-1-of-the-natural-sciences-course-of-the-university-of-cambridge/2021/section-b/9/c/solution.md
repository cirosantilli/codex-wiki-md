<h1 id="section-b/9/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Use positive object distance $u=20\,\mathrm{cm}$ and [focal length](../../../../../focal-length.md) $f=4\,\mathrm{cm}$. The [thin lens](../../../../../thin-lens.md) equation gives

$$
\frac1v=\frac14-\frac1{20}=\frac15\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}},\qquad\boxed{v=5.0\,\mathrm{cm}.}
$$

The [transverse magnification](../../../../../transverse-magnification.md) is $M=-v/u=-1/4$, so the signed height is

$$
\boxed{h_i=Mh_o=-\frac14(2\,\mathrm{cm})=-0.50\,\mathrm{cm}.}
$$

The [real image](../../../../../real-image.md) lies **$5.0\,\mathrm{cm}$ beyond the lens**, is **inverted**, and has positive height magnitude **$0.50\,\mathrm{cm}$**.
