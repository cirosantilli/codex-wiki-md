<h1 id="section-b/8/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [force](../../../../../force-split.md) is conservative, with $\mathbf F=-\nabla U$ and [potential energy](../../../../../potential-energy.md) zero at the origin:

$$
\boxed{U(t)=\frac k2[x(t)^2+y(t)^2]=\frac k2\left[x_0^2\cos^2(\omega t+\phi_1)+y_0^2\cos^2(\omega t+\phi_2)\right].}
$$

Released from rest at $(c,c)$, the trajectory has $x=y=c\cos\omega t$, giving

$$
\boxed{U(t)=kc^2\cos^2\omega t=\frac{kc^2}{2}(1+\cos2\omega t).}
$$

The right panel of the figure shows this nonnegative [oscillation](../../../../../oscillation.md): it starts at a maximum $kc^2$, reaches zero at $t=\pi/(2\omega)$, and repeats after $\pi/\omega$. Its [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md) is twice the coordinate [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md). [Conservation of mechanical energy](../../../../../conservation-of-mechanical-energy.md) gives $K(t)=kc^2\sin^2\omega t$, so the missing [potential energy](../../../../../potential-energy.md) is [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md), not an energy loss.
