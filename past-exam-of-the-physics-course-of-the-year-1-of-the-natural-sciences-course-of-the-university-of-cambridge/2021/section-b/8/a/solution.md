<h1 id="section-b/8/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [force](../../../../../force-split.md) law separates into two identical [harmonic oscillator equations](../../../../../simple-harmonic-motion.md):

$$
m\ddot x=-kx,\qquad m\ddot y=-ky.
$$

For positive $m,k$, the general real solution is

$$
\boxed{x(t)=x_0\cos(\omega t+\phi_1),\qquad y(t)=y_0\cos(\omega t+\phi_2),\qquad\omega=\sqrt{k/m}.}
$$

Since $\operatorname{Re}(e^{i\alpha})=\cos\alpha$ by [Euler's formula](../../../../../euler-s-formula.md), these are exactly the stated [real parts](../../../../../real-part.md) of [complex exponentials](../../../../../complex-exponential-function.md). The [amplitudes](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) $x_0,y_0$ and [phases](../../../../../phase-waves.md) $\phi_1,\phi_2$ are fixed by the four initial position and [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) components. This is two-dimensional [isotropic harmonic oscillator flow](../../../../../isotropic-harmonic-oscillator-flow.md): the same [angular frequency](../../../../../angular-frequency.md) acts in every direction.
