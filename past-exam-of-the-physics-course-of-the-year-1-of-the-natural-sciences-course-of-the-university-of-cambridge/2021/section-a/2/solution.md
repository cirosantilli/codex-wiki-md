<h1 id="section-a/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

The original diagram shows two equal balls, each of [mass](../../../../mass.md) $m$, attached to the fixed upper pivot and light sliding collar by four equal rods. Each ball describes a horizontal circle of radius $r=L\sin\theta$. The pivot-to-collar distance is $2L\cos\theta$, so the upward collar displacement compresses the [spring](../../../../spring.md) by

$$
\delta=2L(1-\cos\theta).
$$

Thus [Hooke's law](../../../../hooke-s-law.md) gives a downward spring [force](../../../../force-split.md) $F_s=k\delta$ on the collar. Let $T_u,T_l$ be the [tensions](../../../../tension.md) in the upper and lower rods attached to one ball. Vertical [force](../../../../force-split.md) balance on the light collar and then on either ball gives

$$
2T_l\cos\theta=F_s,\qquad T_u\cos\theta-T_l\cos\theta=mg.
$$

The radial [force](../../../../force-split.md) on one ball supplies its [centripetal acceleration](../../../../centripetal-acceleration.md):

$$
(T_u+T_l)\sin\theta=m\omega^2L\sin\theta.
$$

For $\theta\ne0$, adding the vertical-balance results and eliminating the rod [tensions](../../../../tension.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\omega^2=\frac{mg+F_s}{mL\cos\theta}=\frac{g}{L\cos\theta}+\frac{2k}{m}\frac{1-\cos\theta}{\cos\theta}.}
$$

For the given [centrifugal governor](../../../../centrifugal-governor.md), $\cos30^\circ=\sqrt3/2$ and

$$
\delta=0.0535898\,\mathrm m,\qquad F_s=26.7949\,\mathrm N,\qquad \omega^2=133.988\,\mathrm{s^{-2}}.
$$

Hence

$$
\boxed{\omega=11.58\,\mathrm{rad\,s^{-1}}\simeq110.5\text{ revolutions per minute}.}
$$

The factor of two in the collar balance matters: both lower rods support the same light collar. The spring force is not an extra downward force of this full magnitude on each ball; its effect on each ball is transmitted through its own lower rod. The formula is the steady [spring-loaded centrifugal governor](../../../../spring-loaded-centrifugal-governor.md) balance.
