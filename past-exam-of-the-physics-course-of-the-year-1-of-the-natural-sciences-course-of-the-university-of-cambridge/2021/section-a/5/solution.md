<h1 id="section-a/5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

The tunnel is taken thin enough that removal of its material does not significantly disturb spherical [symmetry](../../../../symmetry-physics.md). For $a\le r\le2a$, the enclosed [mass](../../../../mass.md) is

$$
M(r)=\frac{4\pi\rho}{3}(r^3-a^3).
$$

By the [spherical shell theorem](../../../../spherical-shell-theorem.md), the inward [gravitational acceleration](../../../../gravitational-acceleration.md) has magnitude

$$
g(r)=\frac{GM(r)}{r^2}=\frac{4\pi G\rho}{3}\left(r-\frac{a^3}{r^2}\right).
$$

The released [gravitational potential energy](../../../../gravitational-energy.md) is the positive [work](../../../../work.md) done by gravity while the [mass](../../../../mass.md) falls from radius $2a$ to $a$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
U_A-U_B&=m\int_a^{2a}g(r)\,dr\\
&=\frac{4\pi G\rho m}{3}\left[\frac{r^2}{2}+\frac{a^3}{r}\right]_a^{2a}\\
&=\boxed{\frac{4\pi G\rho ma^2}{3}.}
\end{aligned}
$$

By [conservation of mechanical energy](../../../../conservation-of-mechanical-energy.md),

$$
\frac12mv_B^2=U_A-U_B,\qquad\boxed{v_B=a\sqrt{\frac{8\pi G\rho}{3}}.}
$$

Inside the cavity, the [spherical shell theorem](../../../../spherical-shell-theorem.md) gives zero [gravitational field](../../../../gravitational-field.md), so the [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) stays constant. The original figure places B and C at opposite ends of a cavity diameter, a distance $2a$ apart. Therefore

$$
\boxed{t_{BC}=\frac{2a}{v_B}=\sqrt{\frac3{2\pi G\rho}}.}
$$

The quantity $G\rho$ has units $\mathrm{s^{-2}}$, so this is a [time](../../../../time-in-physics.md). There are no numerical values for $a$ or $\rho$ in the paper. The transit [time](../../../../time-in-physics.md) is independent of $a$ and the falling [mass](../../../../mass.md) $m$. The cavity's [gravitational potential](../../../../newtonian-potential-of-a-point-mass.md) is constant, not necessarily zero; only its [gradient](../../../../gradient.md) vanishes. The calculation is a [hollow-sphere gravitational transit](../../../../hollow-sphere-gravitational-transit.md).
