<h1 id="section-a/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

Let the [cylinder](../../../../cylinder-geometry.md) length be $\ell=0.020\,\mathrm m$, cross-sectional [area](../../../../surface-area.md) $A$, and the fluid [mass density](../../../../density.md) $\rho_f$. At [floating equilibrium](../../../../floating-equilibrium.md), [Archimedes' principle](../../../../archimedes-principle.md) gives

$$
mg=\rho_f gA\frac\ell2,\qquad m=\rho_f A\frac\ell2.
$$

For a small downward displacement $z$ from this [equilibrium](../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md), the displaced fluid [volume](../../../../volume.md) increases by $Az$. The additional upward [buoyant force](../../../../buoyancy.md) is $\rho_fgAz$, and [Newton's second law](../../../../newton-s-second-law.md) becomes

$$
m\ddot z=-\rho_fgAz,\qquad\ddot z+\frac{2g}{\ell}z=0.
$$

This is [simple harmonic motion](../../../../simple-harmonic-motion.md), with [angular frequency](../../../../angular-frequency.md) $\omega=\sqrt{2g/\ell}$ and [oscillation period](../../../../period-of-an-oscillation.md)

$$
\boxed{T=2\pi\sqrt{\frac\ell{2g}}=2\pi\sqrt{\frac{0.020\,\mathrm m}{2(9.81\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}})}}=0.2006\,\mathrm s\simeq0.20\,\mathrm s.}
$$

The result for [vertical oscillations of a floating cylinder](../../../../vertical-oscillations-of-a-floating-cylinder.md) is independent of its cross-sectional [area](../../../../surface-area.md) and fluid [mass density](../../../../density.md) because those quantities cancel using the known immersion fraction. It neglects [drag](../../../../drag-physics.md), surface effects and the extra fluid inertia from the motion; the [cylinder](../../../../cylinder-geometry.md) must remain partly immersed throughout the small motion.
