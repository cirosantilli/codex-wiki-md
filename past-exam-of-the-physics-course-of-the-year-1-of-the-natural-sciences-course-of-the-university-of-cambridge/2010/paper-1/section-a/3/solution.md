<h1 id="section-a/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

Apply [Ampère's law](../../../../../ampere-s-circuital-law.md) on a concentric circle. Uniform [current density](../../../../../current-density.md) is $J=I/(\pi a^2)$, so the enclosed current is $Ir^2/a^2$ inside and $I$ outside. With permeability $\mu_0$,

$$
\boxed{B(r)=\begin{cases}\mu_0Ir/(2\pi a^2),&0\le r\le a,\\\mu_0I/(2\pi r),&r\ge a.\end{cases}}
$$

The field circulates azimuthally by the [right-hand rule](../../../../../right-hand-rule.md). Its magnitude rises linearly from zero, is continuous and maximal at the surface, then falls as $1/r$. End effects are neglected.
