<h1 id="section-d/d13/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D13](../d13.md)

Direct differentiation gives $\psi''/\psi=x^2/a^4-1/a^2$. Inserting this into the [Schrödinger equation](../../../../../schrodinger-equation.md) and separately matching the $x^2$ and constant terms yields

$$
\boxed{a^4=\frac{\hbar^2}{2mV_0},\qquad a^2=\frac{\hbar}{\sqrt{2mV_0}},\qquad E=\frac{\hbar^2}{2ma^2}.}
$$

For $V_0=m\omega_0^2/2$, **$a^2=\hbar/(m\omega_0)$ and $E=\hbar\omega_0/2$**. The [normalizable](../../../../../normalizable-wavefunction.md) [Gaussian](../../../../../normal-distribution.md) has no [nodes](../../../../../node-dynamical-systems.md), as expected for the nondegenerate one-dimensional [ground state](../../../../../ground-state.md) of this confining potential.

The normalization integral is $|C|^2a\sqrt\pi=1$. Choose its irrelevant [global phase](../../../../../global-phase.md) so that

$$
\boxed{C=(\pi a^2)^{-1/4},\qquad dP=\frac1{\sqrt\pi a}e^{-x^2/a^2}\,dx.}
$$

For the [oscillator ground-state density in a comparison wavelength](../../../../../oscillator-ground-state-density-in-a-comparison-wavelength.md), choose a free comparison particle with the same total [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md). It has $p=\sqrt{2mE}=\hbar/a$, hence comparison [wavelength](../../../../../wavelength.md) $\lambda=h/p=2\pi a$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{dP=\frac{2\sqrt\pi}{\lambda}\exp\!\left(-\frac{4\pi^2x^2}{\lambda^2}\right)dx.}
$$

The bound density is localized on scale $a=\lambda/(2\pi)$, with [standard deviation](../../../../../standard-deviation.md) $\lambda/(2\pi\sqrt2)$, rather than being spatially uniform like the density of a single free [plane wave](../../../../../plane-wave.md). The comparison does not assign constant free-particle [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) to the [bound state](../../../../../bound-state.md); its total [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) also includes [potential energy](../../../../../potential-energy.md).
