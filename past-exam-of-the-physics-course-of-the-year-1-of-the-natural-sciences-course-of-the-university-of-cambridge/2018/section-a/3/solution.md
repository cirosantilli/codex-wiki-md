<h1 id="section-a/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

Use dimensions $\mathsf M,\mathsf L,\mathsf T$ for [mass](../../../../mass.md), [length](../../../../length.md) and [time](../../../../time-in-physics.md). The [reduced Planck constant](../../../../reduced-planck-constant.md), [gravitational constant](../../../../gravitational-constant.md) and [speed of light](../../../../speed-of-light.md) have dimensions

$$
[\hbar]=\mathsf M\mathsf L^2\mathsf T^{-1},\qquad
[G]=\mathsf L^3\mathsf M^{-1}\mathsf T^{-2},\qquad
[c]=\mathsf L\mathsf T^{-1}.
$$

For $l_p=\hbar^\alpha G^\beta c^\gamma$ to have the dimensions of a [length](../../../../length.md), [dimensional analysis](../../../../dimensional-analysis.md) requires

$$
\alpha-\beta=0,\qquad
2\alpha+3\beta+\gamma=1,\qquad
-\alpha-2\beta-\gamma=0.
$$

The first equation gives $\beta=\alpha$; the third gives $\gamma=-3\alpha$. Substitution in the second gives $2\alpha=1$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\alpha=\frac12,\qquad\beta=\frac12,\qquad\gamma=-\frac32,
\qquad l_p=\sqrt{\frac{\hbar G}{c^3}}.}
$$

With $\hbar=1.054571817\times10^{-34}\,\mathrm{J\,s}$, $G=6.67430\times10^{-11}\,\mathrm{m^3\,kg^{-1}\,s^{-2}}$ and $c=2.99792458\times10^8\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$, the [Planck length](../../../../planck-length.md) is

$$
\boxed{l_p\simeq1.6163\times10^{-35}\,\mathrm m.}
$$

The proposed monomial fixes the dimensionless prefactor to one; [dimensional analysis](../../../../dimensional-analysis.md) on its own cannot select an arbitrary dimensionless multiplier.
