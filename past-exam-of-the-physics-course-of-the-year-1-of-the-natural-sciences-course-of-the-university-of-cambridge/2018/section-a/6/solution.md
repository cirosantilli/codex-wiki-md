<h1 id="section-a/6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

Put the charged sheet at $z=0$ and the grounded plates at $z=\pm d/2$. By symmetry the [electric field](../../../../electric-field-split.md) points away from the positive sheet with equal magnitude in the two gaps. A [Gaussian pillbox](../../../../gaussian-pillbox.md) crossing the sheet gives

$$
E_z(0^+)-E_z(0^-)=\frac\sigma{\varepsilon_0},\qquad
|E|=\frac\sigma{2\varepsilon_0}.
$$

The plate boundary conditions fix their [electric potentials](../../../../electric-potential.md) to zero. For the [charged sheet between grounded parallel plates](../../../../charged-sheet-between-grounded-parallel-plates.md), the central potential is

$$
V_s=|E|\frac d2=\frac{\sigma d}{4\varepsilon_0},\qquad
\sigma=1.0\times10^{-7}\,\mathrm{C\,m^{-2}},\quad d=1.0\times10^{-2}\,\mathrm m.
$$

With [vacuum permittivity](../../../../vacuum-permittivity.md) $\varepsilon_0=8.85419\times10^{-12}\,\mathrm{F\,m^{-1}}$, $V_s\simeq28.24\,\mathrm V$. An [electron](../../../../electron.md) has [electric charge](../../../../electric-charge-split.md) $-e$, so moving from a grounded plate to this positive potential decreases its [potential energy](../../../../potential-energy.md) by $eV_s$. [Conservation of energy](../../../../conservation-of-energy.md) then gives

$$
\frac12m_ev^2=eV_s=\frac{e\sigma d}{4\varepsilon_0},
\qquad
\boxed{v=\sqrt{\frac{e\sigma d}{2\varepsilon_0m_e}}
\simeq3.2\times10^6\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}.}
$$

The unrounded value is $3.15\times10^6\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$, about $0.0105c$. Either plate gives the same [speed](../../../../speed.md). The ideal calculation neglects the electron's disturbance of the prescribed field and image-force effects at its release point, consistently with treating it as a [test particle](../../../../test-particle.md).
