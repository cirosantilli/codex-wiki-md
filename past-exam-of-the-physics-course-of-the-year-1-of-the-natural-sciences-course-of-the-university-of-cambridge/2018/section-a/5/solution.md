<h1 id="section-a/5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

For a [photon](../../../../photon.md), the [photon energy](../../../../photon-energy.md) formula and the vacuum relation $\nu=c/\lambda$ give $E_\gamma=hc/\lambda$. For an [electron](../../../../electron.md), the [de Broglie wavelength](../../../../de-broglie-wavelength.md) gives the same [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) magnitude $p=h/\lambda$, but the nonrelativistic [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) is $K_e=p^2/(2m_e)$. Using $h=6.62607015\times10^{-34}\,\mathrm{J\,s}$, $m_e=9.10938\times10^{-31}\,\mathrm{kg}$ and $1\,\mathrm{eV}=1.602176634\times10^{-19}\,\mathrm J$,

$$
E_\gamma=\frac{(6.62607015\times10^{-34})(2.99792458\times10^8)}{4.0\times10^{-9}}
=4.96611\times10^{-17}\,\mathrm J,
$$



$$
K_e=\frac{(6.62607015\times10^{-34})^2}{2(9.10938\times10^{-31})(4.0\times10^{-9})^2}
=1.50617\times10^{-20}\,\mathrm J.
$$

Thus, to the precision of the [wavelength](../../../../wavelength.md),

$$
\boxed{E_\gamma=3.1\times10^2\,\mathrm{eV},\qquad K_e=9.4\times10^{-2}\,\mathrm{eV}.}
$$

The electron answer is its [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md), as required for the diffraction experiment; its [relativistic energy](../../../../relativistic-energy.md) would additionally include the approximately $511\,\mathrm{keV}$ [rest energy](../../../../rest-energy.md). Since $K_e\ll m_ec^2$, the nonrelativistic approximation is well justified. Equal [wavelengths](../../../../wavelength.md) imply equal [momenta](../../../../momentum-split.md), not equal energies for particles with different [energy–momentum relations](../../../../energy-momentum-relation.md).
