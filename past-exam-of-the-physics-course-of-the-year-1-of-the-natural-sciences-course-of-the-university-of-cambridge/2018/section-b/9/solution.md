<h1 id="section-b/9/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [9](../9.md)

**Momentum identity.** From the [Lorentz factor](../../../../lorentz-factor.md), $v^2/c^2=1-\gamma^{-2}$. The relativistic [three-momentum](../../../../three-momentum.md) therefore satisfies

$$
p^2=\gamma^2m^2v^2=m^2c^2\gamma^2(1-\gamma^{-2}),
\qquad
\boxed{p^2=m^2c^2(\gamma^2-1).}
$$

Together with [relativistic energy](../../../../relativistic-energy.md) $E=\gamma mc^2$, this gives the [energy–momentum relation](../../../../energy-momentum-relation.md) $E^2-p^2c^2=m^2c^4$.

**Parent in the laboratory.** The [rest energy](../../../../rest-energy.md) is $m_0c^2=1\,\mathrm{GeV}$, while the total [relativistic energy](../../../../relativistic-energy.md) is $3\,\mathrm{GeV}$. Consequently

$$
\gamma_0=3,\qquad
\boxed{\frac{v_0}{c}=\sqrt{1-\frac19}=\frac{\sqrt8}{3}=0.942809.}
$$

Its [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) magnitude is $p_0c=\sqrt{3^2-1^2}\,\mathrm{GeV}=\sqrt8\,\mathrm{GeV}$.

**Daughters in the zero-momentum frame.** The [centre-of-momentum frame](../../../../center-of-momentum-frame.md) is initially the parent's rest frame. [Conservation of momentum](../../../../momentum-conservation.md) gives $\mathbf p_1^*=-\mathbf p_2^*$. Equal daughter [rest masses](../../../../invariant-mass.md) then imply equal [relativistic energies](../../../../relativistic-energy.md). [Conservation of relativistic energy](../../../../conservation-of-relativistic-energy.md) gives

$$
E_X^*=\frac{m_0c^2}{2}=\frac12\,\mathrm{GeV},\qquad
\gamma_X=\frac{E_X^*}{m_Xc^2}
=\frac{m_0/2}{m_0/3}=\frac32.
$$

Their [speeds](../../../../speed.md) are therefore

$$
\boxed{\gamma_X=\frac32,\qquad \frac{v_X^*}{c}
=\sqrt{1-\frac49}=\frac{\sqrt5}{3}=0.745356.}
$$

The two [velocities](../../../../velocity.md) are opposite, and $p_X^*c=\sqrt{(1/2)^2-(1/3)^2}\,\mathrm{GeV}=\sqrt5\,\mathrm{GeV}/6$. Their total [kinetic energy](../../../../kinetic-energy.md) is $m_0c^2-2m_Xc^2=m_0c^2/3$.

**Invariant of the complete system.** Before the decay, in the [centre-of-momentum frame](../../../../center-of-momentum-frame.md),

$$
\mathcal I_{\rm before}=(m_0c^2)^2-0=m_0^2c^4.
$$

After the [relativistic two-body decay](../../../../relativistic-two-body-decay.md),

$$
\mathcal I_{\rm after}=(E_X^*+E_X^*)^2
-c^2|\mathbf p_X^*+(-\mathbf p_X^*)|^2
=(m_0c^2)^2.
$$

In the laboratory, [four-momentum conservation](../../../../four-momentum-conservation.md) fixes the summed daughter energy to $3\,\mathrm{GeV}$ and their summed [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) to the parent's $\mathbf p_0$. Thus the same calculation is

$$
\boxed{\mathcal I_{\rm before}=\mathcal I_{\rm after}
=(3\,\mathrm{GeV})^2-(\sqrt8\,\mathrm{GeV})^2
=1\,\mathrm{GeV}^2=m_0^2c^4.}
$$

The invariant is formed after summing the daughter [four-momenta](../../../../four-momentum.md); adding their separate squared rest energies would omit the relative-motion contribution to the invariant [mass](../../../../mass.md) of the pair.
