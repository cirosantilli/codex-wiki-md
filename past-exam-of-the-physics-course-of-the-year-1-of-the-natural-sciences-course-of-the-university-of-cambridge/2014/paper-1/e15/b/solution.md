<h1 id="e15/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

[Faraday's law](../../../../../faraday-s-law-of-induction.md) relates induced [electromotive force](../../../../../electromotive-force.md) to the rate of change of [magnetic flux linkage](../../../../../magnetic-flux-linkage.md). For a coil with $N$ turns and a consistent right-hand orientation,

$$
\boxed{\mathcal E=-\frac{d(N\Phi_B)}{dt}.}
$$

[Flux](../../../../../flux.md) can change through field variation or circuit motion. [Lenz's law](../../../../../lenz-s-law.md) is the minus sign: the induced current's field opposes the [flux](../../../../../flux.md) change, consistent with [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) conservation. It opposes the change rather than necessarily opposing the original field.

For a linear fixed-geometry [inductor](../../../../../inductor.md), $N\Phi_B=LI$ and the self-induced [electromotive force](../../../../../electromotive-force.md) is $-L\,dI/dt$. The passive terminal [voltage](../../../../../voltage.md) drop is instead $V=L\,dI/dt$. The stored magnetic [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) is

$$
\boxed{U_B=\int_0^I LI'\,dI'=\tfrac12LI^2.}
$$

[Inductance](../../../../../inductance.md) is measured in henries. Transformers use [mutual inductance](../../../../../mutual-inductance.md): changing one winding's current induces an [electromotive force](../../../../../electromotive-force.md) in another.
