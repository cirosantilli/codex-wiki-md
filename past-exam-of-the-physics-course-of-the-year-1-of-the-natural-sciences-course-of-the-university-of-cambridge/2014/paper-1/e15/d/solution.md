<h1 id="e15/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For a test [mass](../../../../../mass.md) moving around a fixed central [mass](../../../../../mass.md) $M$, [gravity](../../../../../gravity.md) is central and conservative. [Specific angular momentum](../../../../../specific-angular-momentum.md) $h=|\mathbf r\times\mathbf v|$ and [specific orbital energy](../../../../../specific-orbital-energy.md) $\epsilon=v^2/2-GM/r$ are constant. The motion is planar and sweeps equal areas in equal times, $dA/dt=h/2$.

For a nonradial orbit, the inverse-square [force](../../../../../force-split.md) gives a [conic section](../../../../../conic-section.md),

$$
\boxed{r=\frac{h^2/(GM)}{1+e\cos\theta},\qquad
 e^2=1+\frac{2\epsilon h^2}{(GM)^2}.}
$$

Negative [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) gives an [ellipse](../../../../../ellipse.md), zero [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) a [parabola](../../../../../parabola.md) and positive [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) a [hyperbola](../../../../../hyperbola.md). For an [ellipse](../../../../../ellipse.md) of [semimajor axis](../../../../../semi-major-axis.md) $a$,

$$
\boxed{\epsilon=-\frac{GM}{2a},\qquad
P^2=\frac{4\pi^2a^3}{GM},\qquad
v^2=GM\left(\frac2r-\frac1a\right).}
$$

A [circular orbit](../../../../../circular-orbit.md) is the $e=0$ case, with $v=\sqrt{GM/r}$. Escape from radius $r$ requires $v\ge\sqrt{2GM/r}$ in the absence of further [forces](../../../../../force-split.md). For two comparable [masses](../../../../../mass.md), apply the relative two-body equation with $GM$ replaced by $G(M+m)$, rather than treating one body as fixed.
