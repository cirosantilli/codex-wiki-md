<h1 id="a6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A6](../a6.md)

The total ring [mass](../../../../mass.md) is $M=2\pi r\lambda_m=1.257\times10^7\,\mathrm{kg}$. For the [axial gravitational field of a uniform ring](../../../../axial-gravitational-field-of-a-uniform-ring.md), transverse [force](../../../../force-split.md) components cancel by symmetry; each axial component has projection factor $z/\sqrt{r^2+z^2}$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\mathbf F=-\frac{GMm z}{(r^2+z^2)^{3/2}}\,\hat{\mathbf z},
\qquad |\mathbf F|=3.04\times10^{-10}\,\mathrm N.}
$$

The [force](../../../../force-split.md) points towards the ring's centre along its axis.
