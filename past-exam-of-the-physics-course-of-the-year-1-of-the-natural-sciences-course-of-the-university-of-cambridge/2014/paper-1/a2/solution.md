<h1 id="a2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A2](../a2.md)

For this [equal-mass Coulomb circular orbit](../../../../equal-mass-coulomb-circular-orbit.md), let $s$ be the separation and $k_e=1/(4\pi\epsilon_0)$. Each particle describes a circle of radius $s/2$ about the [centre of mass](../../../../center-of-mass.md). [Coulomb's law](../../../../coulomb-s-law.md) and [centripetal acceleration](../../../../centripetal-acceleration.md) give

$$
\frac{k_ee^2}{s^2}=m_e\omega^2\frac s2,
\qquad
\boxed{T=2\pi\sqrt{\frac{m_es^3}{2k_ee^2}}=2.79\times10^{-10}\,\mathrm s.}
$$

Using $s$ as each particle's orbital radius would miss the equal-mass two-body motion.
