<h1 id="a4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A4](../a4.md)

Converting all lengths to metres gives $d=3.7276\times10^{-5}\,\mathrm m$. [Logarithmic differentiation](../../../../logarithmic-differentiation.md) gives

$$
\frac{\delta d}{d}=\frac{\delta F}{F}+3\frac{\delta L}{L}-\frac{\delta E}{E}-\frac{\delta w}{w}-3\frac{\delta t}{t}.
$$

If the quoted uncertainties are [independent](../../../../independent-random-variables.md) [standard uncertainties](../../../../standard-uncertainty.md), [monomial uncertainty propagation](../../../../monomial-uncertainty-propagation.md) gives

$$
\frac{\sigma_d}{d}=\sqrt{0.01^2+0.06^2+0.005^2+(0.1/9)^2+0.025^2}=0.0669,
\qquad
\boxed{d=(37.3\pm2.5)\,\mathrm{\mu m}.}
$$

If the quoted uncertainties instead mean bounds, the first-order conservative fractional error is $0.111$, giving **approximately $37.3\pm4.1\,\mathrm{\mu m}$**. The question does not specify their statistical meaning; these two error conventions should not be combined.
