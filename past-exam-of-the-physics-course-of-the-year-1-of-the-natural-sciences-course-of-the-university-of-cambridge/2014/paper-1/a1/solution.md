<h1 id="a1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A1](../a1.md)

Each [photon](../../../../photon.md) has [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) $hf$, giving the [photon emission rate from monochromatic power](../../../../photon-emission-rate-from-monochromatic-power.md) and [wavelength](../../../../wavelength.md)

$$
\boxed{\dot N=\frac{P}{hf}=8.38\times10^{22}\,\mathrm{s^{-1}},\qquad
\lambda=\frac cf=0.167\,\mathrm m.}
$$

Here $P$ is radiated signal [power](../../../../power.md), not the phone's total electrical consumption.
