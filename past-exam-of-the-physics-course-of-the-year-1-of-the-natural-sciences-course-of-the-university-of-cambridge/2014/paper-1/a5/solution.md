<h1 id="a5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A5](../a5.md)

For the real object and [real image](../../../../real-image.md), take positive distances $u=f+u'$ and $v=f+v'$. The [thin lens](../../../../thin-lens.md) equation yields

$$
uv=f(u+v)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
(f+u')(f+v')=f(2f+u'+v')\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\boxed{u'v'=f^2.}
$$

This is [Newton's lens formula](../../../../newton-s-lens-formula.md). The distances $u',v'$ refer to the adjacent [focal points](../../../../focal-point-of-a-hypersurface.md), not the lens plane.
