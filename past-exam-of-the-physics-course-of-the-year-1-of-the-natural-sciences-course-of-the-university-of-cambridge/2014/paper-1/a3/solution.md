<h1 id="a3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A3](../a3.md)

Use $[\eta]=ML^{-1}T^{-1}$ and $[dp/dx]=ML^{-2}T^{-2}$. Equating [mass](../../../../mass.md), length and time exponents gives

$$
\alpha+\gamma=0,\qquad-\alpha+\beta-2\gamma=3,\qquad-\alpha-2\gamma=-1,
$$

hence

$$
\boxed{\alpha=-1,\quad\beta=4,\quad\gamma=1.}
$$

[Dimensional analysis](../../../../dimensional-analysis.md) leaves $k$ undetermined. If positive $Q$ denotes flow along increasing $x$, the signed pressure derivative is negative and its minus sign belongs in the proportionality constant; alternatively use the pressure-drop magnitude.
