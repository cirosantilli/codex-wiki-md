<h1 id="section-e/15/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A charge $q$ moving nonrelativistically in prescribed fields obeys the [Lorentz force law](../../../../../lorentz-force.md)

$$
\boxed{m\frac{d\mathbf v}{dt}=q(\mathbf E+\mathbf v\times\mathbf B).}
$$

The magnetic part is perpendicular to [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md), so $d(mv^2/2)/dt=q\mathbf E\cdot\mathbf v$: the [electric field](../../../../../electric-field-split.md) can change [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md), while the [magnetic field](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) alone changes direction without doing [work](../../../../../work.md).

In a uniform [electric field](../../../../../electric-field-split.md) with no [magnetic field](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md), [acceleration](../../../../../acceleration.md) is constant: $\mathbf v=\mathbf v_0+(q\mathbf E/m)t$ and $\mathbf r=\mathbf r_0+\mathbf v_0t+q\mathbf E t^2/(2m)$. The trajectory is a parabola when there is initial motion perpendicular to the field, and a line when motion is purely along it. Reversing the charge reverses the electric [acceleration](../../../../../acceleration.md).

In a uniform [magnetic field](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) with no [electric field](../../../../../electric-field-split.md), resolve [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) into parallel and perpendicular components. The parallel component is constant; the perpendicular component rotates at the [cyclotron frequency](../../../../../cyclotron-frequency.md)

$$
\boxed{\omega_c=\frac{|q|B}{m},\qquad r_L=\frac{mv_\perp}{|q|B}.}
$$

The resulting path is a helix, or a circle if $v_\parallel=0$, with pitch $2\pi v_\parallel/\omega_c$. Charge sign determines its sense of rotation. These relations underlie magnetic bending and [mass](../../../../../mass.md)/charge analysis. At relativistic energies, use [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) $\mathbf p=\gamma m\mathbf v$; at fixed [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) the [cyclotron frequency](../../../../../cyclotron-frequency.md) becomes $|q|B/(\gamma m)$.

For uniform crossed fields $\mathbf E\perp\mathbf B$, the [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) $\mathbf v_D=\mathbf E\times\mathbf B/B^2$ satisfies $\mathbf E+\mathbf v_D\times\mathbf B=0$. The orbit is a gyromotion about a [guiding center](../../../../../guiding-center.md) drifting with

$$
\boxed{\mathbf v_D=\frac{\mathbf E\times\mathbf B}{B^2},}
$$

independent of [mass](../../../../../mass.md) and charge sign. A [velocity selector](../../../../../velocity-selector.md) transmits particles with the appropriate direction and [speed](../../../../../speed.md) $E/B$ without deflection. If the [electric field](../../../../../electric-field-split.md) has a component along the [magnetic field](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md), that component accelerates the parallel motion instead. Nonuniform fields can add other drifts; the simple helix and uniform crossed-field expressions require the stated uniform-field assumptions.
