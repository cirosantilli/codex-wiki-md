<h1 id="section-a/4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

Use base dimensions $\mathsf M,\mathsf L,\mathsf T$ for [mass](../../../../mass.md), [length](../../../../length.md) and [time](../../../../time-in-physics.md). The proposed [dimensional analysis](../../../../dimensional-analysis.md) gives

$$
[v]=\mathsf L\mathsf T^{-1},\quad
[\omega]=\mathsf T^{-1},\quad
[\rho]=\mathsf M\mathsf L^{-3},\quad
[g]=\mathsf L\mathsf T^{-2},
$$

so

$$
[\omega^a\rho^bg^c]
=\mathsf M^b\mathsf L^{-3b+c}\mathsf T^{-a-2c}.
$$

Equating the independent dimensional powers requires

$$
b=0,\qquad -3b+c=1,\qquad -a-2c=-1.
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{a=-1,\qquad b=0,\qquad c=1,\qquad v\propto\frac g\omega.}
$$

The [mass density](../../../../density.md) drops out because there is no other mass-bearing quantity with which to combine it under the stated assumptions.

[Dimensional analysis](../../../../dimensional-analysis.md) fixes the powers but leaves a dimensionless coefficient undetermined. For a linear [deep-water gravity wave](../../../../deep-water-gravity-wave.md), the [dispersion relation](../../../../dispersion-relation-split.md) is $\omega^2=gk$, giving [phase velocity](../../../../phase-velocity.md) $\omega/k=g/\omega$ and [group velocity](../../../../group-velocity.md) $d\omega/dk=g/(2\omega)$. Both obey the inferred scaling. The phrase wave speed alone does not distinguish these two velocities or fix the coefficient.
