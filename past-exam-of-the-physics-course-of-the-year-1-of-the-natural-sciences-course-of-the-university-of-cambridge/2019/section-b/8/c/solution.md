<h1 id="section-b/8/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For $E\gg V_0$, the two [wavenumbers](../../../../../wavenumber.md) nearly coincide:

$$
k_2=k_1\sqrt{1-\frac{V_0}{E}}
\simeq k_1\left(1-\frac{V_0}{2E}\right).
$$

Consequently $B/A\simeq V_0/(4E)\to0$ and $C/A\to1$. To leading order the complete [wavefunction](../../../../../wave-function-split.md) is

$$
\boxed{\Psi(x,t)\simeq A\exp\!\left[i\left(k_1x-\frac{Et}{\hbar}\right)\right].}
$$

The small fractional change in kinetic [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) scarcely changes the propagation [wavenumber](../../../../../wavenumber.md), so reflection vanishes and the particle behaves approximately as a free right-moving [plane wave](../../../../../plane-wave.md). A more accurate phase at large positive $x$ retains $k_2x$ on the transmitted side; even a small wavenumber difference accumulates over a sufficiently long distance.
