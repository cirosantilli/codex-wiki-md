<h1 id="section-b/8/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use a stationary [wavefunction](../../../../../wave-function-split.md) $\Psi(x,t)=\psi(x)e^{-iEt/\hbar}$ solving the [time-independent Schrödinger equation](../../../../../time-independent-schrodinger-equation.md). For [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) above the positive [step potential](../../../../../step-potential.md), the positive [wavenumbers](../../../../../wavenumber.md) are

$$
\boxed{k_1=\frac{\sqrt{2mE}}{\hbar},\qquad
k_2=\frac{\sqrt{2m(E-V_0)}}{\hbar}.}
$$

Incidence only from the left permits a right-moving incident [plane wave](../../../../../plane-wave.md) and a left-moving reflected [plane wave](../../../../../plane-wave.md) for $x\leq0$, but only a right-moving transmitted [plane wave](../../../../../plane-wave.md) for $x>0$:

$$
\psi(x)=\begin{cases}
Ae^{ik_1x}+Be^{-ik_1x},&x\leq0,\\
Ce^{ik_2x},&x>0.
\end{cases}
$$

The omitted right-incident component would describe a different scattering experiment. [Wavefunction matching at a finite potential step](../../../../../wavefunction-matching-at-a-finite-potential-step.md) requires continuity of both $\psi$ and $d\psi/dx$ for a finite jump and the same [mass](../../../../../mass.md) on either side:

$$
A+B=C,\qquad ik_1(A-B)=ik_2C.
$$

Eliminate $B=C-A$ from the second condition:

$$
k_1(2A-C)=k_2C,
\qquad
\boxed{\frac CA=\frac{2k_1}{k_1+k_2},\qquad
\frac BA=\frac{k_1-k_2}{k_1+k_2}.}
$$

The first ratio is a [transmission amplitude](../../../../../transmission-amplitude.md); a transmitted [probability current](../../../../../probability-current.md) also depends on its different propagation [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md).
