<h1 id="section-b/7/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For the lander, $u/c=35/37$, so

$$
\gamma_u=\frac1{\sqrt{1-(35/37)^2}}=\frac{37}{12}.
$$

The [relativistic kinetic energy](../../../../../../relativistic-kinetic-energy.md) is

$$
\boxed{K=(\gamma_u-1)mc^2=\frac{25}{12}(4\times10^5)c^2=7.49\times10^{22}\,\mathrm J.}
$$

The rest energy $mc^2$ is excluded from [kinetic energy](../../../../../../kinetic-energy.md).
