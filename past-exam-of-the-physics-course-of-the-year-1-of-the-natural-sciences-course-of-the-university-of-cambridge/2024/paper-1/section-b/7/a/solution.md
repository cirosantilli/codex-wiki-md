<h1 id="section-b/7/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The collinear [relativistic velocity addition](../../../../../../velocity-addition-formula.md) formula is

$$
u=\frac{v+u'}{1+vu'/c^2}.
$$

With both [velocities](../../../../../../velocity.md) directed toward Earth,

$$
\boxed{u=\frac{0.6c+0.8c}{1+0.48}=\frac{35}{37}c=0.946c.}
$$

The denominator keeps the result below $c$. Adding the two speeds by the Galilean rule would give an inadmissible $1.4c$.
