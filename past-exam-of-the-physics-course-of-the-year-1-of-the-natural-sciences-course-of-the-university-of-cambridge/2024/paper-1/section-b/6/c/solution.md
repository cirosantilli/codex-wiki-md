<h1 id="section-b/6/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let $m_p=0.250\,\mathrm{kg}$ and let $m_w$ be the hanging [mass](../../../../../../mass.md). Taking up-slope and downward respectively as positive,

$$
T-m_pg\sin\theta-\mu m_pg\cos\theta=m_pa,\qquad m_wg-T=m_wa.
$$

Eliminate the common [tension](../../../../../../tension.md) to obtain

$$
\boxed{m_w=\frac{m_p\{a+g(\sin\theta+\mu\cos\theta)\}}{g-a}=0.130\,\mathrm{kg}.}
$$

Here $\theta=10^\circ$, $\mu=0.1$ and $a=1.60\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$. The pulley changes the direction of the string [force](../../../../../../force-split.md); in the ideal model it does not change its magnitude.
