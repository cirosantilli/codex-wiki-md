<h1 id="section-b/6/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

Immediately after the parcel leaves, the [spring](../../../../../../spring.md) still stores $\tfrac12kx^2$, but only the $m_s=0.300\,\mathrm{kg}$ sledge is launched. Choose [gravitational potential](../../../../../../newtonian-potential-of-a-point-mass.md) zero at the ramp base. Its initial position is $-x$, so [conservation of energy](../../../../../../conservation-of-energy.md) to the apex gives

$$
\frac12kx^2-m_sgx=m_sgH_s.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{H_s=\frac{kx^2}{2m_sg}-x=2.17\,\mathrm m}
$$

above the ramp base. Its upward travel from the compressed position is $H_s+x=2.56882\,\mathrm m$, a different distance from the height above the base.
