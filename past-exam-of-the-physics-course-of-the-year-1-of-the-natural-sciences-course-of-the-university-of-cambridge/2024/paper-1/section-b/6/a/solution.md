<h1 id="section-b/6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

After the push, the [forces](../../../../../../force-split.md) are weight $mg$ downward, [normal reaction](../../../../../../normal-force.md) $N$ upward and sliding [friction](../../../../../../friction.md) $F=\mu N$ opposite the [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md). There is no continuing applied push. The table has constant slope

$$
a=\frac{1.02-1.20}{1.50}=-0.120\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}.
$$

Vertical balance gives $N=mg$, and horizontal motion gives $ma=-\mu mg$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\mu=\frac{0.120}{9.81}=0.0122.}
$$

The [friction](../../../../../../friction.md) magnitude is $0.0300\,\mathrm N$.
