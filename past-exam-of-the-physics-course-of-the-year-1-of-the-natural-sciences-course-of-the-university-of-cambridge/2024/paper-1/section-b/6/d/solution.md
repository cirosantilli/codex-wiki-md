<h1 id="section-b/6/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The required constant deceleration follows from $v^2=u^2+2as$:

$$
a=-\frac{4.8^2}{2(2)}=-5.76\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}.
$$

Both gravity and sliding [friction](../../../../../../friction.md) oppose ascent, so $g(\sin\theta+0.1\cos\theta)=5.76$. Write

$$
\sin\theta+0.1\cos\theta=\sqrt{1.01}\sin(\theta+\arctan0.1).
$$

The physical incline solution is **$\theta=30.0^\circ$** more precisely $30.0389^\circ$. The combined [mass](../../../../../../mass.md) cancels from the equation. The top of the track is consequently $H=2\sin\theta=1.00117\,\mathrm m$ above its base.
