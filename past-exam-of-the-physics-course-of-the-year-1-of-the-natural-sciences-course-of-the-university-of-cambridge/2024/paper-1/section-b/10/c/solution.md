<h1 id="section-b/10/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For a regular hexagon of side $a$, the perpendicular centre-to-side distance is $d=\sqrt3a/2$. Each side subtends endpoint [angles](../../../../../../angle.md) with $\cos\beta=1/2$ and $\cos\alpha=-1/2$. Its centre field is consequently $\mu_0I/(4\pi d)$.

All six contributions have the same normal direction, so

$$
\boxed{B_{\rm centre}=6\frac{\mu_0I}{4\pi d}=\frac{\sqrt3\,\mu_0I}{\pi a}.}
$$

The direction follows the current's [right-hand rule](../../../../../../right-hand-rule.md). Equal side lengths alone would not fix an arbitrary distorted hexagon's field; the usual regular-coil geometry is used here.
