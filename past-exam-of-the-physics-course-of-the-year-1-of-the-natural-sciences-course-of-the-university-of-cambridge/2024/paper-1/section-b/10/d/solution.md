<h1 id="section-b/10/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The regular hexagon has area $A=6(\sqrt3a^2/4)=3\sqrt3a^2/2$. Choose $t=0$ with its positive normal parallel to the imposed field. The external [magnetic flux](../../../../../../magnetic-flux.md) is $\Phi=B_0A\cos\omega t$, so [Faraday's law of induction](../../../../../../faraday-s-law-of-induction.md) gives

$$
\mathcal E=-\frac{d\Phi}{dt}=B_0A\omega\sin\omega t.
$$

Taking the stated resistance to determine the loop response,

$$
\boxed{I(t)=\frac{3\sqrt3B_0a^2\omega}{2R}\sin\omega t.}
$$

A different initial orientation adds a [phase](../../../../../../phase-waves.md). The sign is with respect to the chosen loop orientation and obeys [Lenz's law](../../../../../../lenz-s-law.md); reversal of that orientation reverses the signed current. This expression neglects [self-inductance](../../../../../../self-inductance.md), as in the resistive-loop model.
