<h1 id="section-b/9/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Use the dimensionless coordinate $u=x/L$:

$$
P=\int_{0.45}^{0.55}2\sin^2(2\pi u)\,du=\left[u-\frac{\sin4\pi u}{4\pi}\right]_{0.45}^{0.55}.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{P=0.1-\frac{\sin(\pi/5)}{2\pi}=0.00645\simeq0.645\%.}
$$

The central interval surrounds a node, which explains why the [probability](../../../../../../probability.md) is much smaller than its ten-percent share of the well's length.
