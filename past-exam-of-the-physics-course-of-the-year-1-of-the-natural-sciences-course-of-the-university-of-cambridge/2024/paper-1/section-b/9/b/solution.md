<h1 id="section-b/9/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a nonzero integer mode, normalize the [wavefunction](../../../../../../wave-function-split.md):

$$
1=|A|^2\int_0^L\sin^2\frac{n\pi x}{L}\,dx=|A|^2\frac L2.
$$

Thus **$|A|=\sqrt{2/L}$**. Choosing an arbitrary overall [phase](../../../../../../phase-waves.md) makes $A=\sqrt{2/L}$ real and positive. The physical modes are conventionally indexed $n=1,2,\ldots$; $n=0$ would be the zero function and cannot be normalized.
