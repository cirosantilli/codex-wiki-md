<h1 id="section-a/4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

The slit spacing is $d=(600\times10^3)^{-1}\,\mathrm m=1.6667\,\mu\mathrm m$. At normal incidence, the [diffraction grating](../../../../../diffraction-grating.md) maxima satisfy $d\sin\theta_m=m\lambda$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\theta_1=\pm22.3^\circ,\qquad\theta_2=\pm49.4^\circ.}
$$

The third order would require $\sin\theta_3=3\lambda/d=1.1394>1$, so it cannot propagate. At fixed order and spacing, a longer [wavelength](../../../../../wavelength.md) requires larger $|\theta|$. **Green light has a larger first-order [diffraction](../../../../../diffraction.md) [angle](../../../../../angle.md) than blue light.**
