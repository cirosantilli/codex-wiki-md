<h1 id="section-a/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

Using the convention $e^{i\omega t}$, the series [RLC circuit](../../../../../rlc-circuit.md) has [complex impedance](../../../../../electrical-impedance.md)

$$
Z=R+i\left(\omega L-\frac1{\omega C}\right),\qquad\omega=2\pi f.
$$

At the stated [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md), $\omega L=180.956\,\Omega$ and $1/(\omega C)=88.4194\,\Omega$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Im}Z=+92.5\,\Omega,\quad |Z|=533\,\Omega,\quad\arg Z=+10.0^\circ.}
$$

The vector is $(525,92.5)$ in the resistance–[reactance](../../../../../electrical-reactance.md) plane. Positive [reactance](../../../../../electrical-reactance.md) means that the net circuit is inductive and current lags voltage. With the alternative $e^{-i\omega t}$ convention the imaginary signs reverse, while the magnitude and physical [phase](../../../../../phase-waves.md) relationship agree.
