<h1 id="section-a/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

The [surface tension](../../../../../surface-tension-split.md) has dimensions $[\gamma]=MT^{-2}$, while $[\rho]=ML^{-3}$ and $[g]=LT^{-2}$. Consequently

$$
\left[\frac{\gamma}{\rho gd^n}\right]=\frac{MT^{-2}}{(ML^{-3})(LT^{-2})L^n}=L^{2-n}.
$$

Matching the dimension of height gives $2-n=1$, so **$n=1$**. [Dimensional analysis](../../../../../dimensional-analysis.md) fixes this exponent but cannot determine a dimensionless prefactor or a contact-angle dependence.
