<h1 id="section-a/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

The string's no-slip condition gives $v=R\omega$. Conservation of [mechanical energy](../../../../../mechanical-energy.md) from release to the stated height drop yields

$$
mgh=\frac12mv^2+\frac12I\frac{v^2}{R^2}.
$$

Solving for the spool's [moment of inertia](../../../../../moment-of-inertia.md),

$$
\boxed{I=\frac{2mghR^2}{v^2}-mR^2,\qquad A=2mghR^2,\quad B=mR^2.}
$$

The axle does no work because its point of application is fixed, and the ideal string and axle introduce no losses. The spool [mass](../../../../../mass.md) $M$ is not needed to infer $I$ from this energy measurement.
