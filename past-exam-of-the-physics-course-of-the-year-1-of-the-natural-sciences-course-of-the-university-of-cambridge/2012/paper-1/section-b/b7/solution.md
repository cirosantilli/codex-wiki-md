<h1 id="section-b/b7/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B7](../b7.md)

Let $s$ increase down the ramp. On a plane, [static friction](../../../../../static-friction.md) $F$ acts uphill. Translation and rotation give

$$
m\ddot s=mg\sin\theta-F,\qquad
Fa=I\dot\omega,\qquad \dot\omega=\ddot s/a.
$$

With $I=2ma^2/5$, elimination yields **$\ddot s=(5/7)g\sin\theta$**. [Static friction](../../../../../static-friction.md) does no work at the stationary contact point.

For a [sphere rolling in a symmetric groove](../../../../../sphere-rolling-in-a-symmetric-groove.md), the distance from the centre to the instantaneous contact axis AB is $h=a\sin(\phi/2)$, so $v=h\omega$. The [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) and work-energy equation are

$$
K=\frac12\left(m+\frac I{h^2}\right)\dot s^2,\qquad
\left(m+\frac I{h^2}\right)\ddot s=mg\sin\theta.
$$

Hence

$$
\boxed{\ddot s=\frac{5g\sin\theta}{5+2/\sin^2(\phi/2)}.}
$$

For the stated angles this is $5g/18$. Using $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$ and a one-second descent,

$$
\boxed{v_g=2.725\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}},\qquad
K_g=\frac12mv_g^2\left(1+\frac{2}{5\sin^2 45^\circ}\right)=33.4\,\mathrm J.}
$$

On the level floor the rolling [radius](../../../../../radius.md) becomes $a$, so an [energy-preserving change of rolling radius](../../../../../energy-preserving-change-of-rolling-radius.md) gives $K_g=\tfrac7{10}mv_f^2$. Therefore

$$
v_f=\sqrt{\frac97}\,v_g=3.090\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}},\qquad
\boxed{t_{\rm pins}=\frac{20\,\mathrm m}{v_f}=6.47\,\mathrm s.}
$$

The floor [speed](../../../../../speed.md) exceeds the groove [speed](../../../../../speed.md) because less of the same total [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) is rotational. Holding the translational [speed](../../../../../speed.md) fixed at the transition would violate the stipulated [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) balance with the new no-slip constraint.
