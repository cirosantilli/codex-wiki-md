<h1 id="section-b/b8/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B8](../b8.md)

For $m>0$, $E=\gamma_u mc^2$ and $\mathbf p=\gamma_um\mathbf u$, with $\gamma_u=(1-u^2/c^2)^{-1/2}$. Thus

$$
E^2-p^2c^2=\gamma_u^2m^2c^4(1-u^2/c^2)=m^2c^4.
$$

A [photon](../../../../../photon.md) obeys **$E=pc$**; $E=hf$ and $p=h/\lambda$ agree because $f\lambda=c$ in vacuum.

Take the incident [photon](../../../../../photon.md) along $+x$, and write $\beta=v/c$ for the boost. In the boosted frame the initial momenta are

$$
p'_{\gamma x}=mc\sqrt{\frac{1-\beta}{1+\beta}}=\gamma_vmc(1-\beta),\qquad
p'_{ex}=-\gamma_vm\beta c.
$$

Their sum vanishes when $1-2\beta=0$, giving **$v_{\rm ZM}=c/2$** and $\gamma_v=2/\sqrt3$. The initial [photon energy](../../../../../photon-energy.md) there is $mc^2/\sqrt3$, and total [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) is $\sqrt3mc^2$.

For [right-angle Compton scattering in the centre-of-momentum frame](../../../../../right-angle-compton-scattering-in-the-centre-of-momentum-frame.md), choose the outgoing ZM [photon](../../../../../photon.md) along $+y$. The [electron](../../../../../electron.md) has the opposite [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) $-p'\widehat{\mathbf y}$. [Conservation of energy](../../../../../conservation-of-energy.md) determines its magnitude:

$$
p'c+\sqrt{m^2c^4+p'^2c^2}=\sqrt3mc^2
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
p'=\frac{mc}{\sqrt3},\qquad E'_e=\frac{2mc^2}{\sqrt3}.
$$

Thus **$\mathbf u'_e=-(c/2)\widehat{\mathbf y}$**. Inverting the [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) transformation gives

$$
u_{ex}=\frac{u'_{ex}+v}{1+u'_{ex}v/c^2}=\frac c2,\qquad
u_{ey}=\frac{u'_{ey}}{\gamma_v(1+u'_{ex}v/c^2)}=-\frac{\sqrt3c}{4}.
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{u_e=\frac{\sqrt7}{4}c=0.6614c,\qquad
\theta_e=-\tan^{-1}\!\left(\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\right)=-40.9^\circ.}
$$

The [electron](../../../../../electron.md) recoils below the incident direction for our choice of [photon](../../../../../photon.md) scattering above it; the reflected configuration reverses both transverse signs. Finally,

$$
\boxed{E_{\gamma,\rm lab}=\gamma_v(E'_\gamma+vp'_{\gamma x})=\frac23mc^2\simeq341\,\mathrm{keV}.}
$$

As a check, $E_{e,\rm lab}=4mc^2/3$, so the final energies sum to the initial $2mc^2$. The outgoing laboratory [photon](../../../../../photon.md) makes $60^\circ$ with $+x$.
