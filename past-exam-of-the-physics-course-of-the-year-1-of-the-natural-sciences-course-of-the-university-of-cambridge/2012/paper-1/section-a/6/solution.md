<h1 id="section-a/6/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [6](../6.md)

The [breakdown-limited parallel-plate charge](../../../../../breakdown-limited-parallel-plate-charge.md) follows from the ideal [parallel-plate capacitor](../../../../../parallel-plate-capacitor.md) relation $Q=CV=(\epsilon_0A/d)(Ed)=\epsilon_0AE$. [Dielectric breakdown](../../../../../dielectric-breakdown.md) limits the [electric field](../../../../../electric-field-split.md) rather than the [voltage](../../../../../voltage.md) independently of separation:

$$
\boxed{Q_{\max}=\epsilon_0(4.0\times10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^2})(3.0\times10^6\,\mathrm{V\,m^{-1}})
=1.06\times10^{-10}\,\mathrm C\simeq106\,\mathrm{pC}.}
$$

This is the magnitude on either plate, not the sum of both magnitudes. Edge enhancement and fringing are neglected, as in the stated parallel-plate model.
