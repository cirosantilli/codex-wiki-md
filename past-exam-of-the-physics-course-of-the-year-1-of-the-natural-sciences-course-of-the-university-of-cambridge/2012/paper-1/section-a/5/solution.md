<h1 id="section-a/5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

The [phase](../../../../../phase-waves.md) step between adjacent slits is

$$
\delta=\frac{2\pi d\sin\theta}{\lambda}\simeq\frac\pi3.
$$

For equal [amplitudes](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) $a_0$, the six unmodified contributions form a closed [regular hexagon](../../../../../regular-hexagon.md) in the [complex plane](../../../../../complex-plane.md):

$$
S_0=a_0\sum_{j=0}^{5}e^{ij\pi/3}=0.
$$

[Reversing one slit at an array minimum](../../../../../reversing-one-slit-at-an-array-minimum.md) replaces slit $j$'s contribution by its negative, so $S=S_0-2a_0e^{ij\pi/3}$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{I_{\rm screen}=|S|^2=4|a_0|^2=4I.}
$$

This is independent of which slit is reversed. The result uses the far-field, narrow-slit and [small-angle approximations](../../../../../small-angle-approximation.md); retaining $\sin\theta$ changes the [phase](../../../../../phase-waves.md) step only by about $7\times10^{-7}$ radians.
