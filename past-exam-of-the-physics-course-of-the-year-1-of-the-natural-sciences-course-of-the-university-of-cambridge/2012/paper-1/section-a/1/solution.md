<h1 id="section-a/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

The [shell theorem](../../../../../spherical-shell-theorem.md) gives the [gravity of a concentric hollow sphere](../../../../../gravity-of-a-concentric-hollow-sphere.md) as $\mathbf g(r)=-GM(<r)\widehat{\mathbf r}/r^2$. There is no enclosed [mass](../../../../../mass.md) at the cavity boundary, whereas the entire shell has [mass](../../../../../mass.md) $M=\tfrac{4\pi\rho}{3}[(2a)^3-a^3]=\tfrac{28\pi}{3}\rho a^3$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\mathbf g(a)=0,\qquad
\mathbf g(2a)=-\frac{7\pi G\rho a}{3}\widehat{\mathbf r},\qquad
\mathbf g(4a)=-\frac{7\pi G\rho a}{12}\widehat{\mathbf r}.}
$$

Both nonzero fields point towards the centre. The field is continuous at each boundary; there is no surface [mass](../../../../../mass.md) sheet.
