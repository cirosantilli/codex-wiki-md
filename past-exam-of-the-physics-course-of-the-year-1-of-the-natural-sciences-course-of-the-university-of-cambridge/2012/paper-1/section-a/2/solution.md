<h1 id="section-a/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

For [drum-frequency dimensional scaling](../../../../../drum-frequency-dimensional-scaling.md), let $f\propto r^p\sigma^qT^s$, where $\sigma$ is [surface mass density](../../../../../projected-surface-mass-density.md). The dimensions are $[\sigma]=ML^{-2}$ and $[T]=MT^{-2}$, so matching [powers](../../../../../power.md) gives $s=1/2$, $q=-1/2$ and $p=-1$:

$$
f=C\frac1r\sqrt{\frac T\sigma},\qquad
\boxed{\frac{f_{\rm new}}f=\frac{\sqrt2}{2}=\frac1{\sqrt2}.}
$$

The dimensionless [normal mode](../../../../../normal-mode.md) constant $C$ is unchanged for the same circular shape and [boundary condition](../../../../../boundary-condition.md). The doubled [tension](../../../../../tension.md) does not offset the doubled [radius](../../../../../radius.md).
