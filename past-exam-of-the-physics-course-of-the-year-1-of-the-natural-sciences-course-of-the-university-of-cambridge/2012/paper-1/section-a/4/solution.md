<h1 id="section-a/4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

To find the [magnitude uncertainty of a series RL impedance](../../../../../magnitude-uncertainty-of-a-series-rl-impedance.md), put $X=\omega L$. Then $z=|Z|=\sqrt{R^2+X^2}=168.003\,\Omega$ and

$$
dz=\frac Rz\,dR+\frac Xz\,dX.
$$

If the quoted errors are [independent](../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) [standard uncertainties](../../../../../standard-uncertainty.md), $\sigma_R=1\,\Omega$ and $\sigma_X=6\,\Omega$ give

$$
\sigma_z=\frac{\sqrt{R^2\sigma_R^2+X^2\sigma_X^2}}z=4.86\,\Omega,\qquad
\boxed{|Z|\simeq(168\pm5)\,\Omega.}
$$

If they denote strict bounds instead, the first-order bound is $(R\Delta R+X\Delta X)/z=5.42\,\Omega$. The source does not specify their statistical interpretation, so these two error conventions should not be conflated.
