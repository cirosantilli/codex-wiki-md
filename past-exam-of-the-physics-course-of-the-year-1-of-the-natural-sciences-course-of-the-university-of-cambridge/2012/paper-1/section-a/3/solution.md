<h1 id="section-a/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

For a [mass change in a viscously damped spring oscillator](../../../../../mass-change-in-a-viscously-damped-spring-oscillator.md), use linear [viscous damping](../../../../../viscous-damping.md) $m\ddot x+b\dot x+kx=mg$, holding the [spring constant](../../../../../spring-constant.md) $k$ and [damping](../../../../../damping.md) coefficient $b$ fixed. Measuring $x$ from $mg/k$ removes the constant [force](../../../../../force-split.md). The envelope is $e^{-\gamma t}$, where

$$
\gamma=\frac b{2m}=\frac{\ln100}{30\,\mathrm s}=0.15351\,\mathrm{s^{-1}},\qquad
\omega_d=\sqrt{\frac km-\gamma^2}=\pi\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}.
$$

Doubling $m$ halves both $k/m$ and $\gamma$. Therefore

$$
\omega_{d,\rm new}^2=\frac{\omega_d^2}{2}+\frac{\gamma^2}{4},\qquad
\boxed{f_{\rm new}=0.35376\,\mathrm{Hz}\simeq0.354\,\mathrm{Hz},\qquad t_{1\%,\rm new}=60\,\mathrm s.}
$$

Neglecting [damping](../../../../../damping.md) in the [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md) gives $0.5/\sqrt2=0.35355\,\mathrm{Hz}$, indistinguishable at the supplied precision. The release condition sets the [phase](../../../../../phase-waves.md) and new initial [amplitude](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md), not these envelope ratios. A different mass-dependent drag law would require its [damping](../../../../../damping.md) coefficient to be specified.
