<h1 id="section-e/e15/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

In vacuum, [Maxwell's equations](../../../../../../maxwell-equations-split.md) in SI units are

$$
\nabla\cdot\mathbf E=\rho_q/\epsilon_0,\qquad
\nabla\cdot\mathbf B=0,\qquad
\nabla\times\mathbf E=-\partial_t\mathbf B,\qquad
\nabla\times\mathbf B=\mu_0\mathbf J+\mu_0\epsilon_0\partial_t\mathbf E.
$$

The [displacement current](../../../../../../displacement-current.md) term makes the last equation consistent with the [charge continuity equation](../../../../../../charge-continuity-equation.md). In a source-free region, take the curl of [Faraday's law](../../../../../../faraday-s-law-of-induction.md) and use $\nabla\times(\nabla\times\mathbf E)=\nabla(\nabla\cdot\mathbf E)-\nabla^2\mathbf E$. This gives

$$
\nabla^2\mathbf E=\mu_0\epsilon_0\partial_t^2\mathbf E,
\qquad \nabla^2\mathbf B=\mu_0\epsilon_0\partial_t^2\mathbf B.
$$

A [plane electromagnetic wave](../../../../../../plane-electromagnetic-wave.md) therefore propagates at **$c=1/\sqrt{\mu_0\epsilon_0}$**. The divergence equations make both fields transverse; the curl equations give

$$
\mathbf B=\frac1c\widehat{\mathbf k}\times\mathbf E.
$$

The [electric fields](../../../../../../electric-field-split.md) and [magnetic fields](../../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) are in [phase](../../../../../../phase-waves.md) and perpendicular to one another and to the propagation direction. Their [energy densities](../../../../../../energy-density.md) are equal:

$$
u=\frac12\epsilon_0E^2+\frac{B^2}{2\mu_0}=\epsilon_0E^2,\qquad
\mathbf S=\frac1{\mu_0}\mathbf E\times\mathbf B=uc\widehat{\mathbf k}.
$$

For electric-field peak [amplitude](../../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) $E_a$, the time-averaged [intensity](../../../../../../intensity-physics.md) is $\langle S\rangle=\epsilon_0cE_a^2/2$. **Mutually coupled changing [electric fields](../../../../../../electric-field-split.md) and [magnetic fields](../../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) transport [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) through vacuum**, even where no charges or conduction currents are present.
