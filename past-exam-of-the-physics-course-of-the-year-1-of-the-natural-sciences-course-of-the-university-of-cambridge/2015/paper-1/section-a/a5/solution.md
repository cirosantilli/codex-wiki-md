<h1 id="section-a/a5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A5](../a5.md)

Using metres and SI [viscosity](../../../../../dynamic-viscosity-split.md), the pressure difference is $\Delta P=200$ Pa and the central flow estimate is

$$
Q=\frac{\pi(2.3\times10^{-3})^4(200)}{8(8.9\times10^{-4})(0.50)}=4.939\times10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^3\,s^{-1}}.
$$

If the quoted uncertainties are independent [standard uncertainties](../../../../../standard-uncertainty.md), the pressure-difference uncertainty is $\sigma_{\Delta P}=\sqrt{10^2+10^2}=14.14$ Pa. First-order propagation gives

$$
\left(\frac{\sigma_Q}{Q}\right)^2=\left(4\frac{0.1}{2.3}\right)^2+\left(\frac{0.3}{8.9}\right)^2+\left(\frac{0.01}{0.50}\right)^2+\frac{10^2+10^2}{200^2}=0.03678.
$$

Hence

$$
\boxed{Q=(4.94\pm0.95)\times10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^3\,s^{-1}}=(4.94\pm0.95)\,\mathrm{mL\,s^{-1}}.}
$$

The [radius sensitivity of Poiseuille-flow uncertainty](../../../../../radius-sensitivity-of-poiseuille-flow-uncertainty.md) comes from the fourth-power dependence on radius. If the quoted errors instead mean independent worst-case bounds, the first-order absolute fractional bound is $4(0.1/2.3)+0.3/8.9+0.01/0.50+20/200=0.3276$, giving approximately $\pm1.62\times10^{-6}$ m³/s. That conservative bound and the [standard uncertainty](../../../../../standard-uncertainty.md) result are different conventions, not interchangeable errors. The supplied flow model presumes a [Newtonian fluid](../../../../../newtonian-fluid.md) and [laminar flow](../../../../../laminar-flow.md) that is fully developed; no [density](../../../../../density.md) is given for an independent Reynolds-number check.
