<h1 id="section-a/a1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A1](../a1.md)

Measure [displacement](../../../../../displacement.md) from each arrangement's gravitational [equilibrium](../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md). The [series and parallel spring stiffness](../../../../../series-and-parallel-spring-stiffness.md) follows by adding [forces](../../../../../force-split.md) or extensions. In the parallel arrangement the two [spring](../../../../../spring.md) [forces](../../../../../force-split.md) change by $kx$ each, giving equivalent stiffness $2k$. In series the common [force](../../../../../force-split.md) $F$ extends each [spring](../../../../../spring.md) by $F/k$, so total extension is $2F/k$ and the equivalent stiffness is $k/2$. Hence

$$
\boxed{f_{\parallel}=\frac1{2\pi}\sqrt{\frac{2k}{m}},\qquad f_{\rm series}=\frac1{2\pi}\sqrt{\frac{k}{2m}},\qquad\frac{f_{\parallel}}{f_{\rm series}}=2.}
$$

The corresponding [angular frequencies](../../../../../angular-frequency.md) are $2\pi f$. [gravity](../../../../../gravity.md) shifts the [equilibrium](../../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) extensions but does not alter the small-oscillation stiffness of these ideal linear, massless [springs](../../../../../spring.md).
