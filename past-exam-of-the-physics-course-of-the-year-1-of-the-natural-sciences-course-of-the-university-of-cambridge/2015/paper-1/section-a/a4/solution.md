<h1 id="section-a/a4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A4](../a4.md)

Adjacent slit contributions have [phase difference](../../../../../phase-difference.md) $\delta=2\pi d\sin\theta/\lambda$. The six-slit [amplitude](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) is a [geometric sum](../../../../../geometric-series.md) $\sum_{j=0}^{5}e^{ij\delta}=e^{5i\delta/2}\sin(3\delta)/\sin(\delta/2)$. The first zero away from the central maximum occurs at $\delta=2\pi/6$, where the denominator is nonzero. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\theta_1=\arcsin\frac{\lambda}{6d}=\arcsin\frac{550\times10^{-9}}{6\times100\times10^{-6}}\simeq9.17\times10^{-4}\,\mathrm{rad}=0.0525^\circ.}
$$

There is a matching zero at the negative [angle](../../../../../angle.md). This is the [first minimum of a finite slit array](../../../../../first-minimum-of-a-finite-slit-array.md), rather than the much larger principal-order [angle](../../../../../angle.md) or a single-slit envelope minimum. The slits are stipulated sufficiently narrow for that envelope not to set the first zero.
