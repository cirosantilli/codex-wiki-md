<h1 id="section-b/b9/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In the [laboratory frame](../../../../../../laboratory-frame.md) the incoming particle and the composite move along $+x$, while the target is initially at rest. In the [equal-energy two-photon decay of a moving particle](../../../../../../equal-energy-two-photon-decay-of-a-moving-particle.md), zero total transverse [momentum](../../../../../../momentum-split.md) makes the two [photon](../../../../../../photon.md) directions symmetric about $+x$. Each [photon](../../../../../../photon.md) has [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) $2mc^2$ and [momentum](../../../../../../momentum-split.md) magnitude $2mc$, so longitudinal [momentum conservation](../../../../../../momentum-conservation.md) gives $2(2mc)\cos\theta=2.4mc$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\theta=\arccos0.6=53.130^\circ,\qquad\text{photon opening angle}=2\theta=106.260^\circ.}
$$

Their momenta can be written $(1.2,\pm1.6)mc$. The opening [angle](../../../../../../angle.md) is not $180^\circ$ in this frame, because the parent carried nonzero total [momentum](../../../../../../momentum-split.md).
