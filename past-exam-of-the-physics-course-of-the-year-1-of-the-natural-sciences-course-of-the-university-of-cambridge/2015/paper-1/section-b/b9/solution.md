<h1 id="section-b/b9/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B9](../b9.md)

For a particle of [rest mass](../../../../../invariant-mass.md) $m_r$, $E=\gamma m_rc^2$ and $p=\gamma m_rv$, with $\gamma=(1-v^2/c^2)^{-1/2}$. The low-speed expansion is $E=m_rc^2+\tfrac12m_rv^2+O(m_rv^4/c^2)$, separating invariant [rest energy](../../../../../rest-energy.md) from the nonrelativistic [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) term.

Conserve the total laboratory [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) and [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) at coalescence: $E_{\rm tot}=4mc^2$ and $p_{\rm tot}=2.4mc$. Since $V=c^2p_{\rm tot}/E_{\rm tot}$, **the combined particle moves at $0.6c$**. Its [rest mass](../../../../../invariant-mass.md) is $M=\sqrt{4^2-2.4^2}\,m=3.2m$. The incident particle is not of [mass](../../../../../mass.md) $m$: its stated [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) and [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) imply $m_{\rm incident}=\sqrt{3^2-2.4^2}\,m=1.8m$. Thus

$$
\boxed{M=3.2m>m_{\rm incident}+m_{\rm target}=2.8m.}
$$

The extra $0.4mc^2$ is internal [rest energy](../../../../../rest-energy.md) retained by the composite after a [perfectly inelastic collision](../../../../../perfectly-inelastic-collision.md); total [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) and [four-momentum](../../../../../four-momentum.md) are conserved, while the sum of individual [rest masses](../../../../../invariant-mass.md) need not be.

The composite's [Lorentz factor](../../../../../lorentz-factor.md) is $1.25$. For a rest-frame lifetime $\tau$, the corresponding laboratory time is $1.25\tau$ and its travel [distance](../../../../../distance.md) is

$$
\boxed{\ell=V\gamma\tau=0.75c\tau.}
$$

If $\tau$ denotes a mean radioactive lifetime, this is the mean flight [distance](../../../../../distance.md), not a fixed [distance](../../../../../distance.md) for every decay. The separate frame diagrams below use the common positive collision axis.
