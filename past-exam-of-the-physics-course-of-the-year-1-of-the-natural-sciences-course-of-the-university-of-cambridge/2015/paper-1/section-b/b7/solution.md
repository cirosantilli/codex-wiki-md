<h1 id="section-b/b7/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B7](../b7.md)

An [impulse](../../../../../impulse.md) is $\mathbf J=\int\mathbf F\,dt$, giving $\Delta\mathbf P=\mathbf J$. For a short localized [impulse](../../../../../impulse.md) at [displacement](../../../../../displacement.md) $\mathbf r$ from the [centre of mass](../../../../../center-of-mass.md), the [angular impulse](../../../../../angular-impulse.md) is $\Delta\mathbf L_{\rm CM}=\mathbf r\times\mathbf J$; for a changing lever arm use its instantaneous value inside the [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md). For a uniform solid sphere with [volume](../../../../../volume.md) [density](../../../../../density.md) $\rho_V$, direct integration about the $z$ axis gives

$$
I=\rho_V\int_0^a r^4\,dr\int_0^\pi\sin^3\theta\,d\theta\int_0^{2\pi}d\phi=\frac{8\pi}{15}\rho_Va^5=\boxed{\frac25Ma^2}.
$$

Take forward translation $u$ and forward-rolling clockwise spin $\Omega$ as positive. Neglect the table's tangential [impulse](../../../../../impulse.md) during the much shorter cue impact. Immediately afterwards,

$$
\boxed{u_0=\frac{I_p}{M},\qquad\Omega_0=\frac{dI_p}{I}=\frac{5dI_p}{2Ma^2}.}
$$

The bottom contact point has slip [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) $s_0=u_0-a\Omega_0$. The [cue height for immediate rolling](../../../../../cue-height-for-immediate-rolling.md) is therefore **$d=2a/5$**.

For the [impulse-driven slipping-to-rolling transition of a sphere](../../../../../impulse-driven-slipping-to-rolling-transition-of-a-sphere.md) at an arbitrary admissible impact height, let $J_f$ be the signed [friction](../../../../../friction.md) [impulse](../../../../../impulse.md) accumulated before slipping ends. Translation and spin then obey $u_f=u_0+J_f/M$ and $\Omega_f=\Omega_0-aJ_f/I$. Impose $u_f=a\Omega_f$ and eliminate $J_f$:

$$
\boxed{u_f=\frac{u_0+(I/(Ma))\Omega_0}{1+I/(Ma^2)}=\frac{5I_p}{7M}\left(1+\frac da\right).}
$$

This expression handles both sliding directions. If $d<2a/5$, the contact point slips forward and [friction](../../../../../friction.md) slows the centre; if $d>2a/5$, excess spin makes it slip backward and [friction](../../../../../friction.md) accelerates the centre. [friction](../../../../../friction.md) still dissipates total [mechanical energy](../../../../../mechanical-energy.md). For $\mu_d>0$, $\dot s=-(7/2)\mu_dg\,\operatorname{sgn}s$, so the slipping time is $2|s_0|/(7\mu_dg)$. Its duration depends on [friction](../../../../../friction.md), while the final [speed](../../../../../speed.md) does not. With zero [friction](../../../../../friction.md) no slipping-to-rolling transition occurs unless rolling was present from the outset.
