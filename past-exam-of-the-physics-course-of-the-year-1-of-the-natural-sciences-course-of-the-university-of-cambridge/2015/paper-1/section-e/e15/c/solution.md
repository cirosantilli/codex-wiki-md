<h1 id="section-e/e15/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For a [test particle](../../../../../../test-particle.md) outside a spherical central [mass](../../../../../../mass.md), the gravitational [force](../../../../../../force-split.md) is central, so [angular momentum](../../../../../../angular-momentum.md) is conserved and the orbit is planar. With [specific angular momentum](../../../../../../specific-angular-momentum.md) $h=r^2\dot\theta$ and [specific orbital energy](../../../../../../specific-orbital-energy.md) $\epsilon=\dot r^2/2+h^2/(2r^2)-GM/r$, the radial [effective potential](../../../../../../effective-potential.md) combines attraction with the [centrifugal barrier](../../../../../../centrifugal-barrier.md). Its nonradial solutions are [conic sections](../../../../../../conic-section.md):

$$
\boxed{r(\theta)=\frac{p}{1+e\cos(\theta-\theta_0)},\qquad p=\frac{h^2}{GM},\quad e^2=1+\frac{2\epsilon h^2}{(GM)^2}.}
$$

For nonzero $h$, negative [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) gives an [ellipse](../../../../../../ellipse.md) with $0\le e<1$, zero [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) a [parabolic Kepler orbit](../../../../../../parabolic-trajectory.md) and positive [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) a [hyperbolic Kepler orbit](../../../../../../hyperbolic-kepler-orbit.md). The [circle](../../../../../../circle.md) is the special case $e=0$. Exactly radial motion is a separate zero-angular-momentum case and may hit the central body; a mathematical point-mass trajectory is not automatically a physically noncolliding orbit.

Conservation of $h$ gives constant [areal velocity](../../../../../../areal-velocity.md) $h/2$, so equal [areas](../../../../../../surface-area.md) are swept in equal times. For a bound [ellipse](../../../../../../ellipse.md) of [semimajor axis](../../../../../../semi-major-axis.md) $a$, $\epsilon=-GM/(2a)$ and $T^2=4\pi^2a^3/(GM)$. A [circular orbit](../../../../../../circular-orbit.md) has [speed](../../../../../../speed.md) $\sqrt{GM/r}$, while the minimum local [escape speed](../../../../../../escape-velocity.md) is $\sqrt{2GM/r}$, obtained by setting the total [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) to zero. These are the test-mass two-body results: a comparable second [mass](../../../../../../mass.md) requires the relative-coordinate [gravitational parameter](../../../../../../standard-gravitational-parameter.md) $G(M+m)$, and nonspherical structure or drag can perturb the conic and its conserved quantities.
