<h1 id="section-e/e15/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A time-independent [conservative force](../../../../../../conservative-force.md) defines a [conservative vector field](../../../../../../conservative-vector-field.md) on a domain when the [work](../../../../../../work.md) between two points is independent of path, equivalently when every closed-path [work](../../../../../../work.md) [integral](../../../../../../integral-split.md) vanishes. Define a [potential energy](../../../../../../potential-energy.md) $U$ by $\boxed{\mathbf F=-\nabla U}$ and $U(B)-U(A)=-\int_A^B\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r$. The additive constant is arbitrary; only differences affect [work](../../../../../../work.md). A vanishing [curl](../../../../../../curl.md) is necessary, and is sufficient on a [simply connected domain](../../../../../../simply-connected-domain.md) under the usual smoothness assumptions. Local zero [curl](../../../../../../curl.md) by itself need not guarantee a single-valued global potential on a domain with holes.

For a test [mass](../../../../../../mass.md) outside a point or spherical [mass](../../../../../../mass.md), $U=-GMm/r$ with zero at infinity reproduces the inward gravitational [force](../../../../../../force-split.md). Near a surface with approximately uniform downward [gravity](../../../../../../gravity.md), $U=mgz$ when $z$ increases upward. A linear [spring](../../../../../../spring.md) has $U=kx^2/2$. In [electrostatics](../../../../../../electrostatics-split.md) the [electric potential](../../../../../../electric-potential.md) is [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) per unit charge, so $U=qV$ and $\mathbf E=-\nabla V$; potential and [potential energy](../../../../../../potential-energy.md) have different units and roles.

For motion under a time-independent [conservative force](../../../../../../conservative-force.md), $d(K+U)/dt=0$. [nonconservative forces](../../../../../../nonconservative-force.md) change the [mechanical energy](../../../../../../mechanical-energy.md) by their [work](../../../../../../work.md), and an explicitly time-dependent potential can exchange [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) with the external agency changing it. A time-varying [magnetic field](../../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) can give a circulating [electric field](../../../../../../electric-field-split.md), so not every [electric field](../../../../../../electric-field-split.md) is electrostatic or conservative. Conversely, zero magnetic [work](../../../../../../work.md) on a charge does not turn a velocity-dependent magnetic [force](../../../../../../force-split.md) into an ordinary position-potential [force](../../../../../../force-split.md).
