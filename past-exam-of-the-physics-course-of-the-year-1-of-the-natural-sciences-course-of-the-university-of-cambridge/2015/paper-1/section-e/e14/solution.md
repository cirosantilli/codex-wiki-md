<h1 id="section-e/e14/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E14](../e14.md)

In the Lorentz law, $q$ is signed electric charge, $\mathbf E$ and $\mathbf B$ are the electric and [magnetic fields](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) at the bead, and the [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) in $q\mathbf V\times\mathbf B$ is its full laboratory [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md). Here $\mathbf V=v\hat{\mathbf r}+w\hat{\boldsymbol\theta}$. Since $\hat r\times\hat z=-\hat\theta$ and $\hat\theta\times\hat z=\hat r$,

$$
\boxed{F_{B,r}=qBw,\qquad F_{B,\theta}=-qBv,\qquad F_{B,z}=0.}
$$

The tangential component integrated around an instantaneous [circle](../../../../../circle.md) of nearly fixed radius gives

$$
\boxed{W_\theta=\int_0^{2\pi}F_{B,\theta}R\,d\theta=-2\pi RqvB.}
$$

This is the stated tangential [circulation](../../../../../circulation-physics.md) [work](../../../../../work.md) expression in the $w\gg v$ limit. **It is tangential [circulation](../../../../../circulation-physics.md) [work](../../../../../work.md).** The [tangential circulation work versus particle work](../../../../../tangential-circulation-work-versus-particle-work.md) distinction matters because the bead actually follows an expanding spiral. The radial [displacement](../../../../../displacement.md) may be small, but its [force](../../../../../force-split.md) is proportional to $w$, so its [power](../../../../../power.md) is of the same order as the tangential [power](../../../../../power.md). In fact

$$
P_{B,r}=qBwv,\qquad P_{B,\theta}=-qBvw,\qquad\boxed{\mathbf F_B\cdot\mathbf V=0.}
$$

The cancellation expresses the fact that [magnetic forces do no work](../../../../../magnetic-forces-do-no-work.md). Ignoring radial [work](../../../../../work.md) while retaining the first-order tangential effect would incorrectly conclude that a [magnetic field](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) supplies net particle [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md).

The [electric field](../../../../../electric-field-split.md) giving the same tangential [force](../../../../../force-split.md) would be $\boxed{\mathbf E_{\rm eq}=-vB\hat\theta}$. This is a force-equivalent field for the moving guide, not evidence that a real circulating [electric field](../../../../../electric-field-split.md) has appeared in the laboratory merely because the loop expands in a static [magnetic field](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md). An [electromotive force](../../../../../electromotive-force.md) is the tangential driving-force [circulation](../../../../../circulation-physics.md) per unit charge, with units of [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) per charge. For a material circuit moving with local [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) $\mathbf v_{\rm loop}$,

$$
\mathcal E=\oint(\mathbf E+\mathbf v_{\rm loop}\times\mathbf B)\cdot d\mathbf l.
$$

Orient the ring counterclockwise with positive normal $+\hat z$. Its flux is $\Phi=B\pi R^2$, so $\dot\Phi=2\pi BRv$. The tangential [circulation](../../../../../circulation-physics.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\mathcal E=W_\theta/q=-2\pi BRv=-\frac{d\Phi}{dt}.}
$$

It is the motional term that matters here. For a fixed loop, the [circulation](../../../../../circulation-physics.md) of the actual [electric field](../../../../../electric-field-split.md) instead follows the partial time [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) of the [magnetic field](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md). The distinction between moving-wire magnetic [forces](../../../../../force-split.md) and induction in a stationary loop is essential to the flux rule.[II.17–1, equations (17.1)–(17.3) and the distinction between moving circuits and changing fields; II.17–2, limits on applying the flux rule](../../../../../the-feynman-lectures-on-physics-volume-ii-by-richard-feynman-and-others.md)

For the [magnetic power balance on an expanding circular guide](../../../../../magnetic-power-balance-on-an-expanding-circular-guide.md), the guide must supply an additional inward reaction $-qBw\hat r$ opposing the radial magnetic [force](../../../../../force-split.md). With radial guide [speed](../../../../../speed.md) $v$, this contribution to its [power](../../../../../power.md) on the bead is **$P_{\rm guide}^{(B)}=-qBvw$**, equal to the tangential magnetic [power](../../../../../power.md). The radial magnetic [power](../../../../../power.md) is positive by the same amount, so the total magnetic [power](../../../../../power.md) still vanishes; the mechanical exchange is associated with the [moving mechanical constraint](../../../../../moving-mechanical-constraint.md).

If “the ring's [work](../../../../../work.md)” is meant as its full [work](../../../../../work.md) on a finite-mass bead, the source statement needs a further qualification. For a frictionless bead of [mass](../../../../../mass.md) $m_b$ with constant imposed radial [speed](../../../../../speed.md), its radial acceleration is $-w^2/R$, so the complete radial guide reaction and [power](../../../../../power.md) are

$$
N_r=-\frac{m_bw^2}{R}-qBw,\qquad P_{\rm guide}=N_rv=-\frac{m_bvw^2}{R}-qBvw.
$$

The first term is present even when $B=0$ and describes the familiar change of tangential [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) on an expanding guide. The requested $-qBvw$ is exactly the additional magnetic-field-dependent term, not that full reaction [power](../../../../../power.md). No bead [mass](../../../../../mass.md) or extra agency fixing $w$ is supplied, so it would be unjustified to assign a numerical total mechanical [power](../../../../../power.md) or silently discard its centripetal contribution.
