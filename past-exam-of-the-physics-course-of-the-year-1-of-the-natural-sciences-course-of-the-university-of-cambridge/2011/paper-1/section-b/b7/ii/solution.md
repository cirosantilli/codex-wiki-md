↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [work-energy theorem](../../../../../../work-energy-theorem.md) gives $dK=\mathbf F_{\rm net}\cdot d\mathbf r$ for a point particle. For a [conservative force](../../../../../../conservative-force.md), $\mathbf F=-\nabla U$ and its work is $-\Delta U$. Force is not generally a scalar derivative of total energy without specifying the displacement and the relevant energy.
