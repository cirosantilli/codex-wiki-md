↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The net [force](../../../../../../force-split.md) equals the time derivative of [momentum](../../../../../../momentum-split.md) in an inertial frame:

$$
\boxed{\mathbf F_{\rm net}=\frac{d\mathbf p}{dt}.}
$$
