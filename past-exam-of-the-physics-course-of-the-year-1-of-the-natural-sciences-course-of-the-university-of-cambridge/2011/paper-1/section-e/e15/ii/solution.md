<h1 id="section-e/e15/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A noncircular **ellipse with $r_0$ at pericentre has $\boxed{1<k<\sqrt2}$**. Its energy is negative. Merely specifying $k<1$ would make the stated closest-approach point an apocentre, so it is excluded by the geometry; the circular case is listed separately.
