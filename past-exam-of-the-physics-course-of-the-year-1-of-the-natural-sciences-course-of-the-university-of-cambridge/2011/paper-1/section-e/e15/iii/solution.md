<h1 id="section-e/e15/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The orbit is **unbound for $\boxed{k\ge\sqrt2}$**: parabolic at equality and hyperbolic above it. The pericentre speed has reached or exceeded the local [escape velocity](../../../../../../escape-velocity.md).
