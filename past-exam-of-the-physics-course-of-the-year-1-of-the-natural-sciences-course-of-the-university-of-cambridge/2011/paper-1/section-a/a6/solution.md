↑ **Parent:** [A6](../a6.md)

The central estimate is $c=4.15/1.93-1=1.15026$. With independent input standard errors, the [ratio-minus-one uncertainty](../../../../../ratio-minus-one-uncertainty.md) from first-order [uncertainty propagation](../../../../../propagation-of-uncertainty.md) is

$$
s_c^2=\left(\frac1b\right)^2s_a^2+\left(\frac a{b^2}\right)^2s_b^2,
\qquad\boxed{c=1.150\pm0.063\quad\text{(standard error)}.}
$$

The four independent reported results cluster near1.21, within about one quoted standard error of this estimate, so **they are consistent at this precision**. Their tight clustering is not evidence that this measurement has the same precision. If the measurements of a and b were correlated, a covariance term $-2a\operatorname{Cov}(a,b)/b^3$ would be needed.
