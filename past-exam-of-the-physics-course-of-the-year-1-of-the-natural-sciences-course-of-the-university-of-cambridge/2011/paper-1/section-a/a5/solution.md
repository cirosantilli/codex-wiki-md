<h1 id="section-a/a5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A5](../a5.md)

For the [double-slit experiment](../../../../../double-slit-experiment.md) with oblique incidence, the [optical path difference](../../../../../optical-path-difference.md) has an unknown incident contribution plus $a\sin\theta$ in the outgoing direction. It cancels between adjacent angular features. From a minimum to the next maximum, the path difference changes by $\lambda/2$:

$$
\boxed{\lambda=2a(\sin43^\circ-\sin17^\circ)
=4.68\times10^{-7}\,\mathrm m\simeq468\,\mathrm{nm}.}
$$

This [incidence-independent adjacent-fringe wavelength](../../../../../incidence-independent-adjacent-fringe-wavelength.md) uses the statement that intensity rises directly from the stated zero to the following peak. No absolute interference order or incidence angle is needed; nonadjacent extrema would require the number of intervening fringes.
