<h1 id="section-a/4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

Take the [thin lens](../../../../thin-lens.md) at $x=0$, with the object on the left and [light rays](../../../../light-ray.md) travelling to the right. The positive object distance is $u=12\,\mathrm{cm}$ and the [focal length](../../../../focal-length.md) is $f=8\,\mathrm{cm}$. The [thin lens](../../../../thin-lens.md) equation gives

$$
\frac1v=\frac1f-\frac1u=\left(\frac18-\frac1{12}\right)\mathrm{cm^{-1}}=\frac1{24}\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}},\qquad\boxed{v=24\,\mathrm{cm}.}
$$

The [transverse magnification](../../../../transverse-magnification.md) and signed image height are

$$
M=-\frac vu=-2,\qquad h_i=Mh_o=-2(5\,\mathrm{cm})=\boxed{-10\,\mathrm{cm}}.
$$

Thus the [real image](../../../../real-image.md) is **$24\,\mathrm{cm}$ beyond the lens, inverted and $10\,\mathrm{cm}$ high**. For the [ray tracing](../../../../ray-tracing.md), a [light ray](../../../../light-ray.md) parallel to the [optical axis](../../../../optical-axis.md) emerges through the far [optical focus](../../../../focus-optics.md); a [light ray](../../../../light-ray.md) through the optical centre continues straight in the ideal [thin lens](../../../../thin-lens.md) model. They intersect at $(24,-10)\,\mathrm{cm}$, independently reading off both answers from the diagram.

The calculation and diagram use the ideal [paraxial approximation](../../../../paraxial-approximation.md) implicit in the [thin lens](../../../../thin-lens.md) equation. The comparatively large object height does not supply enough information to calculate [optical aberrations](../../../../optical-aberration.md) of a real lens.
