<h1 id="section-a/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

Let $x$ be signed displacement along the rails, measured from the centre. The diagram in the original paper shows a guide extending across the centre, so the signed coordinate can pass through zero. [Hooke's law](../../../../hooke-s-law.md) gives the radial [force](../../../../force-split.md) $-kx$. In the [rotating reference frame](../../../../rotating-reference-frame.md), the radial [centrifugal force](../../../../centrifugal-force.md) is $+m\Omega^2x$. The [Coriolis force](../../../../coriolis-force.md) is perpendicular to the rails and is balanced by their reaction; it has no radial component. Therefore [Newton's second law](../../../../newton-s-second-law.md) gives

$$
m\ddot x=-kx+m\Omega^2x,\qquad\ddot x+\left(\frac km-\Omega^2\right)x=0.
$$

Equivalently, the radial component of [acceleration in polar coordinates](../../../../acceleration-in-polar-coordinates.md) in the [inertial frame](../../../../inertial-frame.md) is $\ddot x-\Omega^2x$. The [radial spring oscillator on a rotating guide](../../../../radial-spring-oscillator-on-a-rotating-guide.md) has [simple harmonic motion](../../../../simple-harmonic-motion.md) only when $k/m-\Omega^2>0$, with

$$
\boxed{\omega_{\rm osc}=\sqrt{\frac km-\Omega^2},\qquad f_{\rm osc}=\frac1{2\pi}\sqrt{\frac km-\Omega^2}.}
$$

The [angular frequency](../../../../angular-frequency.md) is in $\mathrm{rad\,s^{-1}}$ and the [frequency](../../../../frequency.md) in $\mathrm{Hz}$. The critical [angular speed](../../../../angular-speed.md) is

$$
\boxed{\Omega_{\rm crit}=\sqrt{k/m},\qquad 0\le\Omega<\Omega_{\rm crit}.}
$$

Strictly this is an upper bound, not an attained maximum for [oscillations](../../../../oscillation.md): at equality $\ddot x=0$, so a displaced object released from rest stays at its displaced position relative to the rails; above it, the central [equilibrium](../../../../equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) is unstable.
