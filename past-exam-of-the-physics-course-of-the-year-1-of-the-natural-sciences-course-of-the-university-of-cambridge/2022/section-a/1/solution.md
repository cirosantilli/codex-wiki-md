<h1 id="section-a/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

By [dimensional analysis](../../../../dimensional-analysis.md), [power](../../../../power.md) has dimensions $\mathsf{M L^2 T^{-3}}$, [mass density](../../../../density.md) has dimensions $\mathsf{M L^{-3}}$, and [angular velocity](../../../../angular-velocity.md) has dimensions $\mathsf{T^{-1}}$; [radians](../../../../radian.md) are dimensionless. Thus

$$
\mathsf{M L^2 T^{-3}}=\mathsf{M^\alpha L^{\gamma-3\alpha}T^{-\beta}}.
$$

Matching the three independent dimensions gives $\alpha=1$, $\beta=3$ and $\gamma-3\alpha=2$, hence

$$
\boxed{\alpha=1,\quad\beta=3,\quad\gamma=5,\qquad P\propto\rho\omega^3D^5.}
$$

With unchanged [mass density](../../../../density.md), [angular velocity](../../../../angular-velocity.md) and the same performance coefficient (a [dimensionless quantity](../../../../dimensionless-quantity.md)), the [pump power affinity law](../../../../pump-power-affinity-law.md) gives

$$
\frac{P_{\rm new}}{P_{\rm old}}=\left(\frac{D_{\rm new}}{D_{\rm old}}\right)^5=1.1^5=1.61051.
$$

The required [power](../../../../power.md) therefore **increases by $61.1\%$**. This uses the stipulated dependence on only these three quantities, with comparable pump geometry and performance; [dimensional analysis](../../../../dimensional-analysis.md) alone does not guarantee that unrelated pumps have the same performance coefficient.
