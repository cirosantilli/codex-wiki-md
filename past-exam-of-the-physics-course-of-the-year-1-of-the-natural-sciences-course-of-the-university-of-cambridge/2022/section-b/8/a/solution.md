<h1 id="section-b/8/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For [electric potential difference](../../../../../electric-potential-difference.md) and [electric current](../../../../../electric-current.md) varying as [sinusoids](../../../../../sinusoid.md), choose the [phasor](../../../../../phasor.md) convention

$$
V(t)=\operatorname{Re}(\widetilde V e^{i\omega t}),\qquad I(t)=\operatorname{Re}(\widetilde I e^{i\omega t}).
$$

The [electrical impedance](../../../../../electrical-impedance.md) is

$$
\boxed{Z(\omega)=\frac{\widetilde V}{\widetilde I},\qquad [Z]=\Omega.}
$$

Its [modulus](../../../../../modulus.md) gives the voltage-to-current [amplitude](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) ratio, and its [complex argument](../../../../../argument-complex-analysis.md) gives the voltage-minus-current [phase difference](../../../../../phase-difference.md). For ideal components,

$$
\boxed{Z_R=R,\qquad Z_C=\frac1{i\omega C}=-\frac{i}{\omega C},\qquad Z_L=i\omega L.}
$$

These follow from [Ohm's law](../../../../../ohm-s-law.md) $V=RI$, the [capacitor](../../../../../capacitor.md) law $I=C\,dV/dt$, and the [inductor](../../../../../inductor.md) law $V=L\,dI/dt$. In [phasor](../../../../../phasor.md) notation a [time derivative](../../../../../time-derivative.md) becomes multiplication by $i\omega$, turning the [linear ordinary differential equations](../../../../../linear-ordinary-differential-equation-split.md) of the [electrical circuit](../../../../../electrical-circuit-split.md) into algebraic equations. [Electrical impedances](../../../../../electrical-impedance.md) add in a [series circuit](../../../../../series-circuit.md); their reciprocals, the [electrical admittances](../../../../../electrical-admittance.md), add in a [parallel circuit](../../../../../parallel-circuit.md). This computes both [amplitudes](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) and [phase differences](../../../../../phase-difference.md) in the steady response to [sinusoids](../../../../../sinusoid.md).
