<h1 id="section-b/8/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let $C_T=C+C_1$ be the total parallel [capacitance](../../../../../capacitance.md), since [capacitors](../../../../../capacitor.md) in a [parallel circuit](../../../../../parallel-circuit.md) have additive [capacitances](../../../../../capacitance.md). Put $\omega_*=2\pi(50\,\mathrm{Hz})$ into the [lossy parallel coil resonance](../../../../../lossy-parallel-coil-resonance.md) condition:

$$
C_T=\frac{L}{R^2+\omega_*^2L^2}=\frac{0.150}{10^2+[2\pi(50)(0.150)]^2}\,\mathrm F=64.6368\,\mu\mathrm F.
$$

The additional [capacitance](../../../../../capacitance.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{C_1=C_T-C=64.6368-10.0000=54.6368\,\mu\mathrm F\simeq54.6\,\mu\mathrm F.}
$$

This is the added [capacitance](../../../../../capacitance.md), rather than the new total, and the conversion from [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md) to [angular frequency](../../../../../angular-frequency.md) is essential.
