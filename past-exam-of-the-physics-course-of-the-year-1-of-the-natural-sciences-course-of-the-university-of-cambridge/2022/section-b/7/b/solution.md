<h1 id="section-b/7/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Choose downward [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) $v$ positive, and assume the collected material is initially at rest in the cloud's [inertial frame](../../../../../inertial-frame.md). This assumption is required for the equation in the question. During $dt$, [momentum conservation](../../../../../momentum-conservation.md) for the droplet plus the material it collects gives

$$
(m+dm)(v+dv)=mv+mg\,dt,
$$

up to second-order increments. Thus the [variable-mass system](../../../../../variable-mass-system.md) satisfies

$$
m\dot v+v\dot m=mg,\qquad\dot v+\frac{\dot m}{m}v=g.
$$

Since $m\propto r^3$, $\dot m/m=3\dot r/r=3\mu/(r_0+\mu t)$, giving

$$
\boxed{\frac{dv}{dt}+\frac{3\mu}{r_0+\mu t}v=g.}
$$

The notation $dv/dt$ is appropriate because $v$ here depends only on [time](../../../../../time-in-physics.md). The extra term accounts for the [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) needed to accelerate newly collected [mass](../../../../../mass.md); using $m\dot v=mg$ would omit it. For incoming material with downward [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) $u$, the more general equation is $m\dot v=mg+(u-v)\dot m$.
