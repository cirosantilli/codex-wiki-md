<h1 id="section-b/7/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

An upward [drag force](../../../../../drag-physics.md) $F_D\ge0$ changes the [variable-mass system](../../../../../variable-mass-system.md) equation to

$$
\dot v+\frac{3\mu}{r_0+\mu t}v=g-\frac{F_D}{m}.
$$

For an ordinary [drag force](../../../../../drag-physics.md) increasing with positive [speed](../../../../../speed.md), the new curve lies below the no-drag curve. It still starts with slope $g$ if $F_D=0$ at rest. The dashed curve illustrates [quadratic drag on a sphere with constant radial growth](../../../../../quadratic-drag-on-a-sphere-with-constant-radial-growth.md), using $F_D=\tfrac12 C_D\rho_{\rm air}\pi r^2v^2$ with constant [drag coefficient](../../../../../drag-coefficient.md) $C_D$ and air [mass density](../../../../../density.md) $\rho_{\rm air}$. The equation for these [dimensionless quantities](../../../../../dimensionless-quantity.md) is

$$
\frac{dw}{d\tau}=1-\frac{3w}{1+\tau}-\eta\frac{w^2}{1+\tau},\qquad\eta=\frac{3C_D\rho_{\rm air}gr_0}{8\rho\mu^2}>0.
$$

The figure uses $\eta=2$ solely to show the shape; it is not an additional datum of the paper.

There is a limitation to the usual [terminal velocity](../../../../../terminal-velocity.md) sketch here. The examiner's commentary describes a curve approaching [terminal velocity](../../../../../terminal-velocity.md), but the paper maintains unbounded radius growth and specifies no drag law. A horizontal limit is justified if one additionally treats the radius as approximately fixed, or otherwise assumes a drag-to-mass ratio with a fixed limiting balance. With the growing radius and constant-$C_D$ [quadratic drag](../../../../../quadratic-drag.md) just written, the late balance instead gives

$$
\boxed{w\sim\sqrt{\tau/\eta},\qquad v\sim\sqrt{\frac{8\rho g\mu}{3C_D\rho_{\rm air}}\,t}.}
$$

This curve flattens in the sense that its slope tends to zero, but its [speed](../../../../../speed.md) remains unbounded. Thus **a lower, more strongly flattening curve is warranted; a finite terminal speed requires an additional assumption**. The no-growth approximation gives the familiar finite value $v_T=\sqrt{8\rho gr/(3C_D\rho_{\rm air})}$ for a specified fixed radius.[Examiner commentary, B7, released PDF page 62: the stated terminal-velocity expectation](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/natural_science_tripos_physics_e/response/2127325/attach/3/FOI%202022%20639%20Sullivan%20materials.pdf?cookie_passthrough=1#page=62)
