<h1 id="section-b/7/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Introduce the [dimensionless quantities](../../../../../dimensionless-quantity.md) $\tau=\mu t/r_0$ and $w=\mu v/(gr_0)$. Then

$$
w(\tau)=\frac14\left[(1+\tau)-(1+\tau)^{-3}\right].
$$

The [Taylor series](../../../../../taylor-series.md) at $t=0$ gives

$$
\boxed{v(t)=gt-\frac{3g\mu}{2r_0}t^2+\frac{5g\mu^2}{2r_0^2}t^3+O(t^4),\qquad t\ll r_0/\mu.}
$$

Thus the initial slope is $g$, as for [free fall](../../../../../free-fall.md). At late [times](../../../../../time-in-physics.md),

$$
\boxed{v(t)=\frac{gt}{4}+\frac{gr_0}{4\mu}-\frac{gr_0^4}{4\mu(r_0+\mu t)^3}\sim\frac{gt}{4},\qquad t\gg r_0/\mu.}
$$

The oblique [asymptote](../../../../../asymptote.md) is $v=gt/4+gr_0/(4\mu)$, approached from below. Direct differentiation gives

$$
\dot v=\frac g4\left[1+3(1+\tau)^{-4}\right]>0,\qquad\ddot v=-\frac{3g\mu}{r_0}(1+\tau)^{-5}<0.
$$

The [speed](../../../../../speed.md) increases monotonically with decreasing slope, from $g$ towards $g/4$. It does not reach a [terminal velocity](../../../../../terminal-velocity.md) in this drag-free model. Without [mass](../../../../../mass.md) accumulation, and still without [drag](../../../../../drag-physics.md), a drop released from rest has $v=gt$: its [acceleration](../../../../../acceleration.md) remains $g$, whereas the growing drop continually shares its [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) with newly acquired material.
