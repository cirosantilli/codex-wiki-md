<h1 id="section-b/10/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For an infinite straight wire in [vacuum](../../../../../vacuum.md), [Ampère's circuital law](../../../../../ampere-s-circuital-law.md) around a circle of radius $R$ gives $B(2\pi R)=\mu_0I$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\mathbf B=\frac{\mu_0I}{2\pi R}\widehat{\boldsymbol\phi}.}
$$

Here $\mu_0$ is the [vacuum permeability](../../../../../vacuum-permeability.md) and the azimuthal direction follows the [right-hand rule](../../../../../right-hand-rule.md) around the [electric current](../../../../../electric-current.md). The [magnetic field](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) is measured in $\mathrm T$.
