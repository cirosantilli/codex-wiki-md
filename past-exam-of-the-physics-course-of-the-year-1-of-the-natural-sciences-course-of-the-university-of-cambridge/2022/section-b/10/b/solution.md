<h1 id="section-b/10/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Choose the [electric current](../../../../../electric-current.md) counterclockwise as viewed from $+z$; reversing it reverses the [magnetic field](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md). Parameterize the circular wire by

$$
\mathbf r'(\phi)=R(\cos\phi,\sin\phi,0),\quad d\mathbf l=R(-\sin\phi,\cos\phi,0)\,d\phi,\quad\mathbf s=(0,0,z)-\mathbf r'.
$$

The [cross product](../../../../../cross-product.md) in the [Biot-Savart law](../../../../../biot-savart-law.md) is

$$
d\mathbf l\times\mathbf s=(Rz\cos\phi,Rz\sin\phi,R^2)\,d\phi,\qquad |\mathbf s|=\sqrt{R^2+z^2}.
$$

Consequently the transverse terms integrate to zero over $0\le\phi\le2\pi$, while the axial term is constant:

$$
\mathbf B(0,0,z)=\frac{\mu_0I}{4\pi(R^2+z^2)^{3/2}}\int_0^{2\pi}(Rz\cos\phi,Rz\sin\phi,R^2)\,d\phi.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{\mathbf B(0,0,z)=\frac{\mu_0IR^2}{2(R^2+z^2)^{3/2}}\widehat{\mathbf z}.}
$$

The direction is $+z$ on both halves of the axis for this [electric current](../../../../../electric-current.md) orientation, including $z<0$; it is not “away from the plane” on both sides. The [right-hand rule](../../../../../right-hand-rule.md) gives the same direction.
