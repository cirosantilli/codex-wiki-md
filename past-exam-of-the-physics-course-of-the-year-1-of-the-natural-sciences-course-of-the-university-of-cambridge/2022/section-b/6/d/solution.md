<h1 id="section-b/6/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Each ship needs [centripetal acceleration](../../../../../centripetal-acceleration.md) $\omega^2(l/2)$, supplied entirely by the [tension](../../../../../tension.md). Thus

$$
\boxed{T=m\omega^2\frac l2=\frac{m(v_1+v_2)^2}{2l}=\frac{2L^2}{ml^3}.}
$$

The result has units of [force](../../../../../force-split.md), $\mathrm N$.
