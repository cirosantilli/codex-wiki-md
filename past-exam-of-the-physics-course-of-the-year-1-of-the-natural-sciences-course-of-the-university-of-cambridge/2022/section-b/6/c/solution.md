<h1 id="section-b/6/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Take $+x$ along the upper ship's initial [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md), and take $+y$ from the lower ship to the upper ship at closest approach. The upper and lower ships then have initial [velocities](../../../../../velocity.md) $v_1\widehat{\mathbf x}$ and $-v_2\widehat{\mathbf x}$. Their equal [masses](../../../../../mass.md) put each at distance $l/2$ from the [centre of mass](../../../../../center-of-mass.md), so

$$
\boxed{I_{\rm CM}=2m(l/2)^2=\frac12ml^2.}
$$

By [momentum conservation](../../../../../momentum-conservation.md), the [centre of mass](../../../../../center-of-mass.md) keeps moving with constant [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md)

$$
\boxed{\mathbf V_{\rm CM}=\frac{v_1-v_2}{2}\widehat{\mathbf x}.}
$$

In that frame the ships have opposite [velocities](../../../../../velocity.md) of magnitude $u=(v_1+v_2)/2$. Their initial [angular momentum about the centre of mass](../../../../../angular-momentum-about-the-centre-of-mass.md) is nonzero even though their trajectories were straight:

$$
\mathbf L=-ml\frac{v_1+v_2}{2}\widehat{\mathbf z},\qquad L=\frac{ml(v_1+v_2)}2.
$$

The tether exerts central internal [forces](../../../../../force-split.md), so [conservation of angular momentum](../../../../../conservation-of-angular-momentum.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\omega=\frac L{I_{\rm CM}}=\frac{v_1+v_2}{l}.}
$$

Each ship follows uniform [circular motion](../../../../../circular-motion.md) of radius $l/2$ around the translating [centre of mass](../../../../../center-of-mass.md), clockwise viewed from $+z$. More explicitly, put the initial centre at the origin and define

$$
\mathbf n(t)=\sin(\omega t)\widehat{\mathbf x}+\cos(\omega t)\widehat{\mathbf y},\qquad\mathbf r_{1,2}(t)=\mathbf V_{\rm CM}t\pm\frac l2\mathbf n(t).
$$

The [kinetic-energy decomposition about the centre of mass](../../../../../kinetic-energy-decomposition-about-the-centre-of-mass.md) verifies [conservation of mechanical energy](../../../../../conservation-of-mechanical-energy.md) directly:

$$
\begin{aligned}
E_{\rm after}&=\frac12(2m)V_{\rm CM}^2+\frac12I_{\rm CM}\omega^2\\
&=\frac m4(v_1-v_2)^2+\frac m4(v_1+v_2)^2\\
&=\boxed{\frac12mv_1^2+\frac12mv_2^2=E_{\rm before}.}
\end{aligned}
$$

At closest approach the relative radial [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) is already zero, so connecting the tether requires no dissipative radial impulse. Subsequently its [tension](../../../../../tension.md) is perpendicular to each ship's [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) relative to the [centre of mass](../../../../../center-of-mass.md) and does no net [work](../../../../../work.md) on the pair at fixed $l$.
