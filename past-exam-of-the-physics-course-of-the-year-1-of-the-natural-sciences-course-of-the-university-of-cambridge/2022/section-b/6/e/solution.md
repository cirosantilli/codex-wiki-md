<h1 id="section-b/6/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

During [quasi-static tether contraction](../../../../../quasi-static-tether-contraction.md), [momentum conservation](../../../../../momentum-conservation.md) keeps $\mathbf V_{\rm CM}$ constant and [conservation of angular momentum](../../../../../conservation-of-angular-momentum.md) keeps $L$ constant. To leading order in the slow radial motion, the [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) is

$$
E=mV_{\rm CM}^2+\frac{L^2}{2I_{\rm CM}}=mV_{\rm CM}^2+\frac{L^2}{ml^2}.
$$

Its [derivative](../../../../../derivative.md) at fixed $L$, not fixed $\omega$, is

$$
\boxed{\frac{dE}{dt}=-\frac{2L^2}{ml^3}\dot l>0\quad(\dot l<0).}
$$

To compute the [power](../../../../../power.md) supplied by the [tension](../../../../../tension.md), use the instantaneous unit vector $\mathbf n$ from the lower to the upper ship. Their radial [velocities](../../../../../velocity.md) relative to the [centre of mass](../../../../../center-of-mass.md) are $\pm(\dot l/2)\mathbf n$, and the [tension](../../../../../tension.md) forces are $\mp T\mathbf n$. Consequently

$$
\dot W=(-T\mathbf n)\cdot\left(\frac{\dot l}{2}\mathbf n\right)+(T\mathbf n)\cdot\left(-\frac{\dot l}{2}\mathbf n\right)=-T\dot l=\boxed{-\frac{2L^2}{ml^3}\dot l=\frac{dE}{dt}.}
$$

The translational contributions cancel between the two ships and the tangential contributions are zero. The contraction mechanism supplies positive [work](../../../../../work.md), increasing the rotational [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md).

The slow-contraction approximation matters: for a prescribed smooth $l(t)$ with a taut tether, the exact [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) and [tension](../../../../../tension.md) include radial terms,

$$
E_{\rm exact}=mV_{\rm CM}^2+\frac{L^2}{ml^2}+\frac m4\dot l^2,\qquad T_{\rm exact}=\frac{2L^2}{ml^3}-\frac m2\ddot l.
$$

These still satisfy $dE_{\rm exact}/dt=-T_{\rm exact}\dot l$. Thus the paper's displayed formula is the leading result for slow, smooth contraction, or is exact when the radial [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) is constant; it is not an identity for every possible shrinking schedule.
