<h1 id="section-b/6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $I_\perp$ denote the [moment of inertia](../../../../../moment-of-inertia.md) normal to the disc and $I_d$ that about any central diameter. [Rotational symmetry](../../../../../rotational-symmetry.md) makes the two perpendicular diameter values equal. By the [perpendicular axis theorem](../../../../../perpendicular-axis-theorem.md), $I_\perp=2I_d$. The intended [angular impulse](../../../../../angular-impulse.md) has magnitude $J=I_\perp\omega_0$; delivering that magnitude about a diameter instead gives $J=I_d\omega$. Thus

$$
I_d\omega=2I_d\omega_0,\qquad\boxed{\omega=2\omega_0.}
$$

These are [principal axes](../../../../../principal-axis.md), so [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) is parallel to [angular velocity](../../../../../angular-velocity.md) in both cases. Equal delivered [angular impulse](../../../../../angular-impulse.md) means equal magnitude of the change in [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md), not equal rotational [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md); the actual rotational [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) is twice the intended value.
