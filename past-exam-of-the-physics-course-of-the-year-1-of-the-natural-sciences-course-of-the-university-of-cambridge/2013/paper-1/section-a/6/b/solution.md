<h1 id="section-a/6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The laboratory [mean lifetime](../../../../../../mean-lifetime-physics.md) is dilated to $\gamma\tau_0=44.7\,\mathrm{ns}$. Multiplying by the laboratory [speed](../../../../../../speed.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\langle\ell\rangle=v\gamma\tau_0\simeq10.9\,\mathrm m.}
$$

This is the mean of the exponential decay-distance distribution, not a fixed distance travelled by every [pion](../../../../../../pion.md).
