<h1 id="section-e/e15/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

In vacuum the electric and [magnetic field](../../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) [densities](../../../../../../density.md) are

$$
\boxed{u_E=\frac{\epsilon_0E^2}{2},\qquad u_B=\frac{B^2}{2\mu_0}.}
$$

For example, charging a [capacitor](../../../../../../capacitor.md) requires $U=\int_0^Q(q/C)\,dq=Q^2/(2C)=CV^2/2$. A [parallel-plate capacitor](../../../../../../parallel-plate-capacitor.md) with $C=\epsilon_0A/d$ and $E=V/d$ then gives $U=\epsilon_0E^2Ad/2$, identifying the [electric field](../../../../../../electric-field-split.md) [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) [density](../../../../../../density.md). Similarly an [inductor](../../../../../../inductor.md) stores $LI^2/2$; applying this to a long [solenoid](../../../../../../solenoid.md) yields $B^2/(2\mu_0)$ times its interior [volume](../../../../../../volume.md).

Electromagnetic [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) is transported by the [Poynting vector](../../../../../../poynting-vector.md) $\mathbf S=\mathbf E\times\mathbf B/\mu_0$. Poynting's theorem,

$$
\frac{\partial(u_E+u_B)}{\partial t}+\nabla\cdot\mathbf S=-\mathbf J\cdot\mathbf E,
$$

expresses local [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) conservation and transfer to matter. In a vacuum [plane wave](../../../../../../plane-wave.md) $E=cB$, so the two [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) [densities](../../../../../../density.md) are equal and the [energy](../../../../../../energy-split.md) [flux](../../../../../../flux.md) has magnitude $c(u_E+u_B)$. For nondispersive linear media the corresponding reversible expressions are $\mathbf E\cdot\mathbf D/2$ and $\mathbf B\cdot\mathbf H/2$; they must not be applied unchanged to arbitrary dispersive or hysteretic materials.
