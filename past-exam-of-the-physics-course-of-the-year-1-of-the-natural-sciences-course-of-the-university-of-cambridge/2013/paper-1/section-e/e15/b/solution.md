<h1 id="section-e/e15/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [Lorentz force](../../../../../../lorentz-force.md) determines the trajectory:

$$
\boxed{\frac{d\mathbf p}{dt}=q(\mathbf E+\mathbf v\times\mathbf B),\qquad
\frac{dE}{dt}=q\mathbf E\cdot\mathbf v.}
$$

Thus a [magnetic field](../../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) changes [momentum](../../../../../../momentum-split.md) direction but does no work; an [electric field](../../../../../../electric-field-split.md) can change the particle's [kinetic energy](../../../../../../kinetic-energy.md). In a uniform [electric field](../../../../../../electric-field-split.md) with negligible [magnetic field](../../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) and nonrelativistic [speed](../../../../../../speed.md), $\mathbf a=q\mathbf E/m$ is constant. A transverse initial [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) then gives a [parabola](../../../../../../parabola.md).

In a uniform [magnetic field](../../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md), the parallel [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) is unchanged and the perpendicular motion is circular. Nonrelativistically,

$$
r_L=\frac{mv_\perp}{|q|B},\qquad\omega_c=\frac{|q|B}{m};
$$

the combined trajectory is a helix, with circulation reversed when the charge sign reverses. Relativistically $r_L=p_\perp/(|q|B)$ and the gyrofrequency is $|q|B/(\gamma m)$.

For perpendicular uniform fields in the magnetic-dominated case, the drift [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) is $\mathbf v_d=\mathbf E\times\mathbf B/B^2$, which obeys $\mathbf E+\mathbf v_d\times\mathbf B=0$. A particle at this [velocity](../../../../../../velocity.md) is undeflected: the crossed-field [velocity selector](../../../../../../velocity-selector.md) selects [speed](../../../../../../speed.md) $E/B$ in the appropriate direction, [independent](../../../../../../independent-random-variables.md) of [mass](../../../../../../mass.md) and charge. Radiation reaction and collisions are neglected in these ideal trajectories.
