<h1 id="section-d/d12/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

At zero angle every slit element is in [phase](../../../../../../phase-waves.md) for every [wavelength](../../../../../../wavelength.md). Their central maxima coincide, so **the centre is bright white**. Shorter [wavelengths](../../../../../../wavelength.md) have narrower central maxima; farther from the axis their contributions fade sooner, giving the central band reddish outer edges for a smooth white spectrum.
