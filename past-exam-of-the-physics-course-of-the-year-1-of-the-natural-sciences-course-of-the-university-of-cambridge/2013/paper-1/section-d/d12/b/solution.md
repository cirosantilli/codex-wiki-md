<h1 id="section-d/d12/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The first zero of each colour is at $\theta=\lambda/a$. Blue therefore reaches its first zero closer to the axis than the reference green [wavelength](../../../../../../wavelength.md), and red reaches it farther away. At $\theta=\lambda_0/a$ the reference colour is absent, while other [wavelengths](../../../../../../wavelength.md) remain: **there is no common black first minimum in white [light](../../../../../../light.md)**. Adjacent regions change hue as different colour minima pass through them; [wavelengths](../../../../../../wavelength.md) longer than the reference are still in their central maxima and tend to dominate near its first zero. The exact hue depends on the incident spectrum and spectral response.
