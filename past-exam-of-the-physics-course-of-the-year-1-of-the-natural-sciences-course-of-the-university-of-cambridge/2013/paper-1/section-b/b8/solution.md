<h1 id="section-b/b8/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B8](../b8.md)

Take inertial axes parallel, with frame $S'$ moving at $+v\widehat{\mathbf x}$ relative to $S$ and origins coincident at zero time. The [Lorentz factor](../../../../../lorentz-factor.md) is $\gamma_v=(1-v^2/c^2)^{-1/2}$; $\Delta x,\Delta y,\Delta t$ are separations of the same pair of events in $S$, and primed quantities are their separations in $S'$. The transverse coordinates are unchanged. For two events on an inertial clock's worldline,

$$
c^2\Delta\tau^2=c^2\Delta t^2-|\Delta\mathbf r|^2.
$$

The [proper time](../../../../../proper-time.md) $\Delta\tau$ is that clock's elapsed time; uniform [speed](../../../../../speed.md) $w$ gives $\Delta t=\gamma_w\Delta\tau$. Coordinate intervals between spatially separated events must instead be Lorentz-transformed with their spatial separation included.

For the round trip in the station frame, the rendezvous distance is $ut_{\rm out}$, and the return has the same [speed](../../../../../speed.md) and distance. Thus $t_{\rm round}=2t_{\rm out}=10a/(\sqrt7c)$. Its departure and arrival both occur at the station, so $\Delta x=0$ for these endpoints:

$$
\boxed{\Delta t_{S,\rm round}=\frac{10}{\sqrt7}\,\mathrm{days}=3.780\,\mathrm{days},\qquad
\Delta t_{B,\rm round}=\gamma_v\Delta t_{S,\rm round}
=\frac{25}{2\sqrt7}\,\mathrm{days}=4.725\,\mathrm{days}.}
$$

This uses the return [speed](../../../../../speed.md) as measured by the station. The elapsed time aboard A, apart from an ideal instantaneous turn, would be $\Delta t_{S,\rm round}/\gamma_u$.

For the crash, A's station-frame [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) components are $(3c/5,\sqrt7c/5)$, while B's are $(3c/5,0)$. Here $\gamma_u=5/3$ and $\gamma_v=5/4$. [Four-momentum conservation](../../../../../four-momentum-conservation.md), with no separately escaping radiation, gives the wreckage totals in the station frame:

$$
\boxed{\mathbf P_S=mc\left(\frac74\widehat{\mathbf x}+\frac{\sqrt7}{3}\widehat{\mathbf y}\right),\qquad
E_S=\frac{35}{12}mc^2.}
$$

In B's original [inertial frame](../../../../../inertial-frame.md) the same totals are

$$
\boxed{\mathbf P_B=\frac{\sqrt7}{3}mc\,\widehat{\mathbf y},\qquad E_B=\frac73mc^2.}
$$

The invariant wreckage [mass](../../../../../mass.md) is $m\sqrt{14/3}$, exceeding $2m$ because [internal energy](../../../../../internal-energy-split.md) remains in the wreckage. Its [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) cannot be specified without naming a frame; both natural frames have been given.
