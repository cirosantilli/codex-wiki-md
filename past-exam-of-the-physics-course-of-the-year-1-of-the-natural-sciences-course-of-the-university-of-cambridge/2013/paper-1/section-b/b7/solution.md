<h1 id="section-b/b7/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B7](../b7.md)

In the [centre-of-momentum frame](../../../../../center-of-momentum-frame.md) the two equal [masses](../../../../../mass.md) have opposite [velocities](../../../../../velocity.md). Conservation of [kinetic energy](../../../../../kinetic-energy.md) in an elastic collision fixes their common [speed](../../../../../speed.md), so scattering changes their directions only. For incoming laboratory [velocities](../../../../../velocity.md) $\mathbf v$ and $\mathbf0$, the frame [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) is $\mathbf V=\mathbf v/2$ and the incoming relative [velocities](../../../../../velocity.md) are $\pm\mathbf v/2$. If the outgoing relative [velocities](../../../../../velocity.md) are $\pm\mathbf w$, then $|\mathbf w|=v/2$ and the laboratory [velocities](../../../../../velocity.md) are $\mathbf V\pm\mathbf w$. Their dot product is zero: **nonzero outgoing laboratory [velocities](../../../../../velocity.md) are perpendicular**.

For this [smooth equal-disc oblique collision](../../../../../smooth-equal-disc-oblique-collision.md), let $\mathbf n$ point from A to B at contact. The [impulse](../../../../../impulse.md) acts only along $\mathbf n$; the tangential [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) of A is unchanged. With $\mathbf v\cdot\mathbf n=v\cos\theta$, equal [masses](../../../../../mass.md) exchange their normal [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) components:

$$
\mathbf v_{Af}=\mathbf v-v\cos\theta\,\mathbf n,\qquad
\mathbf v_{Bf}=v\cos\theta\,\mathbf n.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{v_{Af}=v\sin\theta,\qquad\angle(\mathbf v,\mathbf v_{Af})=\frac\pi2-\theta.}
$$

No rotation about a disc centre is generated by these central [impulses](../../../../../impulse.md). At exactly head-on impact A stops, so its outgoing direction is undefined; the angle formula then denotes a limiting direction.

For this [adhesive collision of two equal discs](../../../../../adhesive-collision-of-two-equal-discs.md), use the [parallel axis theorem](../../../../../parallel-axis-theorem.md) about the midpoint of the touching centres:

$$
I_{\rm pair}=2(\tfrac12ma^2+ma^2)=3ma^2,\qquad
\mathbf L_{\rm CM}=(-a\mathbf n)\mathbin\times m\mathbf v.
$$

Its magnitude is $mav\sin\theta$. Linear and [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) conservation give

$$
\boxed{\mathbf V_{\rm CM}=\frac{\mathbf v}{2},\qquad
|\Omega|=\frac{v\sin\theta}{3a},\qquad
K_f=mv^2\left(\frac14+\frac{\sin^2\theta}{6}\right).}
$$

The rotation has the sign of $\mathbf L_{\rm CM}$, clockwise for the printed geometry. The [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) loss is $mv^2(1/4-\sin^2\theta/6)>0$. After impact the midpoint translates uniformly and the rigid pair rotates uniformly about it: there are no external horizontal [forces](../../../../../force-split.md) or axial [torques](../../../../../torque.md).

Rough unglued edges can transmit a tangential [impulse](../../../../../impulse.md) and generate [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) about the individual disc centres. The centre-of-mass [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) and total [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) still have the values above, but individual translational [speeds](../../../../../speed.md), [angular velocities](../../../../../angular-velocity.md) and [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) loss require a [friction](../../../../../friction.md)/contact law and normal restitution. **Roughness alone does not imply permanent sticking or the adhesive result.**
