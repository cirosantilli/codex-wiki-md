# Power-law Press-Schechter stellar mass function

↑ **Parent:** [Press-Schechter halo mass function](press-schechter-halo-mass-function.md)

For a power-law matter spectrum, smoothed variance scales as $\sigma\propto M^{-\beta}$ with $\beta=(n+3)/6$. Differentiating the Press-Schechter collapsed fraction gives a halo mass function proportional to $M^{\beta-2}\exp[-(M/M_{h,\rm ch})^{2\beta}]$. A constant [stellar-to-halo mass ratio](stellar-to-halo-mass-ratio.md) preserves both exponents and rescales characteristic mass and normalization.
