# Thick disk

↑ **Parent:** [Galactic disk](galactic-disk.md)

A stellar [galactic disk](galactic-disk.md) component with greater vertical extent and typically larger random [velocity dispersion](velocity-dispersion.md) than a [thin galactic disk](thin-galactic-disk.md). In the [Milky Way](milky-way.md) it contains an important old population. Its stars are not defined simply by being in a geometrically sharp slab or by all sharing one age.
