# Extension rule for Poincare duality groups

↑ **Parent:** [Poincare duality group](poincare-duality-group.md)

For an extension with kernel a dimension-$n$ [Poincare duality group](poincare-duality-group.md) and quotient a dimension-$q$ [Poincare duality group](poincare-duality-group.md), apply the [Lyndon–Hochschild–Serre spectral sequence](lyndon-hochschild-serre-spectral-sequence.md) to the ambient group ring. Kernel cohomology is concentrated in degree $n$ and is a regular quotient module with a rank-one orientation twist. Quotient cohomology is then concentrated in degree $q$ with underlying group $\mathbb Z$. The extension resolution supplies finiteness and gives cohomological dimension at most $n+q$, proving duality in that dimension. Orientation actions need not be trivial.
