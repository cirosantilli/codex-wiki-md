# Ultraviolet and unitary-gauge limits of the Proca propagator

↑ **Parent:** [Proca propagator](proca-propagator.md)

In mostly-plus signature, the [unitary gauge](unitary-gauge.md) limit $\xi\to\infty$ leaves the longitudinal [Proca propagator](proca-propagator.md) term $-iP_L/M^2$. Its ultraviolet scaling is constant in the [momentum](momentum-split.md) magnitude, whereas finite $\xi$ gives $1/k^2$ scaling. Thus taking the gauge-parameter limit before the ultraviolet limit obscures ordinary power-counting [renormalizability](renormalizable-quantum-field-theory.md). A conserved external current annihilates the longitudinal term, so the exchange between two such currents still falls as $1/k^2$. The issue does not make a free massive-vector [Gaussian integral](gaussian-integral.md) inconsistent.
