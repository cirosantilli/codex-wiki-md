# Screw motion

↑ **Parent:** [Proper Euclidean motion of Euclidean three-space](proper-euclidean-motion-of-euclidean-three-space.md)

A screw motion combines a [rotation in three dimensions](rotation-in-three-dimensions.md) about a fixed line with a translation parallel to that line. With unit axis direction $e$, axial [speed](speed.md) $v$, and [rotation matrix](rotation-matrix.md) $R_t$ fixing $e$, each map $\Phi_t(x)=R_tx+tve$ is a [proper Euclidean motion of Euclidean three-space](proper-euclidean-motion-of-euclidean-three-space.md), and together these maps form a [one-parameter subgroup](one-parameter-subgroup.md). Each off-axis [group orbit](orbit-group-theory.md) is a [circular helix](circular-helix.md) when both rotational [speed](speed.md) and axial [speed](speed.md) are nonzero. A pure rotation or translation is a limiting case.
