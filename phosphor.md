# Phosphor

↑ **Parent:** [Luminescence](luminescence.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phosphor)

A luminescent material, for example one that converts some of a blue [light-emitting diode](light-emitting-diode.md)'s [light](light.md) into longer [wavelengths](wavelength.md). The name does not require long-lived [phosphorescence](phosphorescence.md).
