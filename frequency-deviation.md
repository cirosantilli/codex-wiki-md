# Frequency deviation

↑ **Parent:** [Frequency modulation](frequency-modulation.md)

The peak frequency deviation is the largest [absolute value](absolute-value.md) of the [instantaneous frequency](instantaneous-frequency.md) offset from the chosen [carrier wave](carrier-wave.md) [frequency](frequency.md) $f_c$. For $f_i=f_c+K_fM\cos(2\pi f_mt)$, it is $\Delta f=|K_f|M$. It is a [frequency](frequency.md), measured in [hertz](hertz.md), rather than a [wave phase](phase-waves.md) or an exact [signal bandwidth](signal-bandwidth.md).
