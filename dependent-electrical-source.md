# Dependent electrical source

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical engineering](electrical-engineering-split.md)

An ideal source whose [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) or [electric current](electric-current.md) is controlled by a specified voltage or [electric current](electric-current.md) in the [electrical circuit](electrical-circuit-split.md). [Thévenin source suppression](thevenin-source-suppression.md) retains its linear control relation.
