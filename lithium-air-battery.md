# Lithium-air battery

↑ **Parent:** [Electric battery](electric-battery.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithium–air_battery)

A [battery](electric-battery.md) using lithium [oxidation](oxidation.md) and oxygen [reduction](reduction-chemistry.md), often producing lithium peroxide in a nonaqueous discharge. Specific-energy claims depend strongly on whether oxygen, [electrolyte](electrolyte.md) and inactive components are included in the mass.
