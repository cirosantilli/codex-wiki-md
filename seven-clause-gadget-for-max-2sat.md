# Seven-clause gadget for MAX-2SAT

↑ **Parent:** [Maximum 2-satisfiability](maximum-2-satisfiability.md)

A three-[literal](boolean-literal.md) disjunction can be represented by ten unit or two-[literal](boolean-literal.md) [clause](clause-of-a-boolean-formula.md) occurrences with one fresh auxiliary variable. If the number $r$ of true input [literals](boolean-literal.md) is zero, the best auxiliary choice satisfies six; for $r=1,2,3$, the best count is seven. Applying separate gadgets to all [clauses](clause-of-a-boolean-formula.md) of a [3-SAT](3-sat.md) instance gives target seven times the original [clause](clause-of-a-boolean-formula.md) count, proving [NP-completeness](np-completeness.md) of the decision form of [MAX-2SAT](maximum-2-satisfiability.md). The local counting argument is essential: no gadget may exceed seven and compensate for an unsatisfied input [clause](clause-of-a-boolean-formula.md).
