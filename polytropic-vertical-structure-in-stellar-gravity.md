# Polytropic vertical structure in stellar gravity

↑ **Parent:** [Vertical structure of an astrophysical disk](vertical-structure-of-an-astrophysical-disk.md)

In harmonic stellar gravity, an [ideal gas](ideal-gas.md) with a [polytropic equation of state](polytropic-equation-of-state.md) of index $n>0$ has a quadratic [temperature](temperature.md) profile and finite semi-thickness $H^2=2(n+1)\mathcal R T_0/\Omega^2$. This differs from the [polytropic vertical structure of a self-gravitating disk](polytropic-vertical-structure-of-a-self-gravitating-disk.md).
