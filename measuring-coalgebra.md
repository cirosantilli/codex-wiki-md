# Measuring coalgebra

↑ **Parent:** [Coalgebra](coalgebra.md)

For unital [algebras over a field](algebra-over-a-field.md) $A,B$, a [coalgebra](coalgebra.md) $C$ measures $A$ into $B$ when a linear map $p:C\to\operatorname{Hom}_k(A,B)$ satisfies the displayed identity and $p(c)(1_A)=\varepsilon(c)1_B$. [Group-like elements](group-like-element.md) therefore act by unital [algebra homomorphisms over a field](algebra-homomorphism-over-a-field.md).
