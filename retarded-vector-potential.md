# Retarded vector potential

↑ **Parent:** [Retarded potential](retarded-potential.md)

The causal [magnetic vector potential](magnetic-vector-potential.md) generated by a [current density](current-density.md) in the [Lorenz gauge](lorenz-gauge-condition.md), with no additional incoming homogeneous field. Each source contribution is evaluated at its [retarded time](retarded-time.md). The integral is a convolution with the retarded wave-equation [Green function](green-s-function-split.md); its validity includes the stated boundary and source conditions.
