# Derivative avoidance criterion for chordal restriction

↑ **Parent:** [Chordal restriction property](chordal-restriction-property.md)

Suppose a random proper [simple curve](simple-curve.md) from zero to infinity has the displayed avoidance probability for every admissible [compact H-hull](compact-h-hull.md) $A$, with fixed $\alpha>0$. Put $\psi_A=g_A-g_A(0)$. For another admissible hull $B$ in the image [complex upper half-plane](upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md), map composition and the [chain rule](chain-rule.md) give $g_C'(0)=g_B'(0)g_A'(0)$ for $C=A\cup\psi_A^{-1}(B)$. The conditional image curve therefore has avoidance probabilities $g_B'(0)^\alpha$, exactly those of the original. Since [avoidance probabilities determine a simple chordal curve law](avoidance-probabilities-determine-a-simple-chordal-curve-law.md), the conditional image law agrees with the original. This is the [chordal restriction property](chordal-restriction-property.md).
