# Complementary areas obstruct symplectic equivalence of separating curves

↑ **Parent:** [Classification of compact symplectic surfaces](classification-of-compact-symplectic-surfaces.md)

A [symplectomorphism](symplectomorphism.md) of a closed [symplectic surface](symplectic-surface.md) carries the complementary components of a separating simple closed curve to those of its image, preserving their unordered [symplectic areas](symplectic-area.md). On the unit [sphere](sphere.md) a latitude at height $h$ has complementary areas $2\pi(1-h)$ and $2\pi(1+h)$. Thus the equator cannot be sent to any nonequatorial latitude.
