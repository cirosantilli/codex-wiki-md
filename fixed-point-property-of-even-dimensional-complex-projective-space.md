# Fixed-point property of even-dimensional complex projective space

↑ **Parent:** [Complex projective space](complex-projective-space.md)

Every continuous self-map of $\mathbb{CP}^{2r}$ has a fixed point. If its pullback sends the integral degree-two generator to $d$ times itself, multiplicativity gives trace $d^j$ in degree $2j$. Thus its [Lefschetz number](lefschetz-number.md) is $1+d+\cdots+d^{2r}$, which is positive: for $d\ne1$ it is $(1-d^{2r+1})/(1-d)>0$, and at $d=1$ it is $2r+1$. The [Lefschetz fixed-point theorem](lefschetz-fixed-point-theorem.md) applies. In particular no nontrivial [finite group](finite-group.md) acts freely on this simply connected space.
