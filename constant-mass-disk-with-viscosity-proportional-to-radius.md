# Constant-mass disk with viscosity proportional to radius

↑ **Parent:** [Heat-equation transform for linear-radius disk viscosity](heat-equation-transform-for-linear-radius-disk-viscosity.md)

With $x=2\sqrt{r/(3A)}$ and $g=Ar^{3/2}\Sigma$, the half-line [heat equation](heat-equation.md) solution $g=Ct^{-1/2}e^{-x^2/(4t)}$ has $g_x(0)=0$ but $g(0)>0$. It gives $\Sigma\propto r^{-3/2}t^{-1/2}e^{-r/(3At)}$, constant disk mass and [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) proportional to $t^{1/2}$. Its radial [velocity](velocity.md) is $r/t$, and a central [torque](torque.md) proportional to $t^{-1/2}$ supplies the increasing [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md). It is an outward-spreading, torque-driven disk, not a zero-torque accreting solution.
