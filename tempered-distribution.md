# Tempered distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Schwartz space](schwartz-space.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tempered_distribution)

A tempered distribution is a continuous linear functional on the [Schwartz space](schwartz-space.md). Tempered distributions include functions of at most polynomial growth and admit a [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) by duality.
