# Singleton truth-preservation obstruction to expressive completeness

↑ **Parent:** [Functional completeness](functional-completeness.md)

Suppose a [first-order language](first-order-language.md) has [logical implication](logical-implication.md) as its only connective and has either or both of the quantifiers, but no primitive falsity. In a singleton structure with every atomic predicate true, all atomic equalities are also true, and [structural induction](structural-induction.md) makes every formula true. No formula of this language can therefore express a negated equality. Quantifiers cannot repair this failure of expressive completeness, even though they add syntactic operations beyond propositional connectives.
