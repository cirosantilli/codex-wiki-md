# Generalized delta-system lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Delta-system lemma](delta-system-lemma.md)

If $\mu<\theta=\operatorname{cf}(\theta)$ are infinite [cardinals](cardinal-number.md) and $|[\alpha]^{<\mu}|<\theta$ for every $\alpha<\theta$, every $\theta$-sized family of sets of size less than $\mu$ has a $\theta$-sized [delta-system](delta-system.md) subfamily.
