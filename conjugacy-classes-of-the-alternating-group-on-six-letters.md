# Conjugacy classes of the alternating group on six letters

↑ **Parent:** [Alternating group](alternating-group.md)

The [alternating conjugacy class splitting criterion](alternating-conjugacy-class-splitting-criterion.md) gives seven [conjugacy classes](conjugacy-class.md) in $A_6$. Their cycle types and sizes are $1^6:1$, $2^2 1^2:45$, $3 1^3:40$, $3^2:40$, $4\,2:90$, and two $5\,1$ classes of size 72. Representatives of the split classes are a 5-cycle and its square. Their sizes sum to 360. A [normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md) must be a union of these classes containing the identity, so these explicit sizes can prove simplicity using [Lagrange's theorem](lagrange-s-theorem.md).
