# Quantum decoherence

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum theory](quantum-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_decoherence)

Quantum decoherence is the suppression of observable phase coherence when a system becomes entangled with unobserved environmental degrees of freedom. In a preferred basis, tracing out the environment damps off-diagonal entries of the system's [reduced density matrix](reduced-density-matrix.md) while leaving the corresponding populations unchanged.
