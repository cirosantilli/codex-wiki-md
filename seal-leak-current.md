# Seal leak current

↑ **Parent:** [Gigaohm seal](gigaohm-seal.md)

[Seal leak current](seal-leak-current.md) bypasses the membrane patch through an imperfect seal. For an ideal ohmic seal to a zero-voltage bath, $I_{\mathrm{seal}}=V_p/R_{\mathrm{seal}}$, positive from pipette to bath. At $|V_p|=50\,\mathrm{mV}$ and $R_{\mathrm{seal}}=1\,\mathrm{G\Omega}$ its magnitude is $50\,\mathrm{pA}$, far larger than the synthetic $0.8\,\mathrm{pA}$ single-channel step. A stable baseline can be subtracted, but changing leakage can mimic a biological response. Intrinsic membrane leak through [ion channels](ion-channel.md) is distinct from this bypass: interpreting both as the same [electrical conductance](electrical-conductance.md) obscures whether the [biological cell](biological-cell.md) or the seal changed.
