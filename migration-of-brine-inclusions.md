# Migration of brine inclusions

↑ **Parent:** [Brine rejection](brine-rejection.md)

A temperature gradient can move a brine inclusion by melting its warmer wall and freezing its colder wall. This slow process can redistribute salt, but is generally much less effective at net desalination than [gravity drainage of sea-ice brine](gravity-drainage-of-sea-ice-brine.md) or [meltwater flushing of sea ice](meltwater-flushing-of-sea-ice.md).
