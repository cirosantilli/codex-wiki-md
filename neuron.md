# Neuron

↑ **Parent:** [Neuroscience](neuroscience.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neuron)

A specialized [biological cell](biological-cell.md) that receives and transmits signals. Many neurons integrate graded inputs and communicate through [action potentials](action-potential.md) and [synapses](synapse.md), although neuronal signaling is not limited to spikes.
