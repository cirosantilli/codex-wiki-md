<h1 id="cycle-sum-identity-for-young-jucys-murphy-elements">Cycle-sum identity for Young–Jucys–Murphy elements</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Jucys–Murphy element](jucys-murphy-element.md)

In the [group algebra](group-algebra.md) of $S_n$, the product of the [Young–Jucys–Murphy elements](jucys-murphy-element.md) $X_2,\ldots,X_n$ is the sum of all $n$-cycles, each with coefficient one. To prove it, multiply the sum of all $m$-cycles by $X_{m+1}$. Right multiplication by $(j\ m+1)$ inserts $m+1$ immediately after $j$ in the cycle. Every $(m+1)$-cycle has a unique predecessor of $m+1$, so deletion inverts this insertion bijectively. Induction starts at $X_2=(1\ 2)$. The identity turns a product of cell contents into a [central character value of a conjugacy-class sum](central-character-value-of-a-conjugacy-class-sum.md).
