# Magnetic field of a rotating charged solid cylinder

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetostatics](magnetostatics.md)

An infinite solid cylinder with radius $R$, prescribed uniform volume [charge density](charge-density.md) $\rho$, and [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md) $\omega\hat{\mathbf z}$ has [current density](current-density.md) $\mathbf J=\rho\omega r\hat{\boldsymbol\phi}$. Treat a shell at radius $a$ and thickness $da$ as a [solenoid](solenoid.md) carrying surface current $\rho\omega a\,da$. With no applied background [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) and permeability $\mu$,

$$
\mathbf B(r)=\begin{cases}\dfrac{\mu\rho\omega}{2}(R^2-r^2)\hat{\mathbf z},&r<R,\\0,&r>R.\end{cases}
$$

The same result follows from [Ampère's circuital law](ampere-s-circuital-law.md), since $-dB_z/dr=\mu\rho\omega r$ inside.
