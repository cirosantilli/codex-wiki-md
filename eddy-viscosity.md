# Eddy viscosity

↑ **Parent:** [Reynolds stress](reynolds-stress.md)

An eddy viscosity parametrizes turbulent momentum transport by $\overline{u'v'}=-\nu_TU_y$. The [mixing length](mixing-length.md) model gives $\nu_T=l^2|U_y|\geq0$. This coefficient depends on the mean flow and position; it is distinct from molecular [kinematic viscosity](kinematic-viscosity.md). It describes momentum transport rather than molecular energy dissipation.
