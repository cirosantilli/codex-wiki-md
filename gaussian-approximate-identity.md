# Gaussian approximate identity

↑ **Parent:** [Heat kernel](heat-kernel.md)

The centered normal densities

$$
g_t(x)=\frac1{\sqrt{2\pi t}}e^{-x^2/(2t)}
$$

form an [approximate identity](approximate-identity.md): for every $f\in L^1(\mathbb R)$, $f*g_t\to f$ at almost every point where the [Lebesgue differentiation theorem](lebesgue-differentiation-theorem.md) applies. Their Fourier transforms are $e^{-t\xi^2/2}$ in the angular-frequency convention.
