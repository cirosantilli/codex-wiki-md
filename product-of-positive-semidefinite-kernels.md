# Product of positive-semidefinite kernels

↑ **Parent:** [Closure property of positive-semidefinite kernels](closure-property-of-positive-semidefinite-kernels.md)

The pointwise product of two [positive-semidefinite kernels](positive-semidefinite-kernel.md) is positive semidefinite because its finite [kernel matrix](kernel-matrix.md) is the entrywise product of two positive-semidefinite matrices, to which the [Schur product theorem](schur-product-theorem.md) applies.
