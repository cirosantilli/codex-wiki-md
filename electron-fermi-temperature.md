# Electron Fermi temperature

↑ **Parent:** [Fermi energy](fermi-energy.md)

The kinetic [electron Fermi temperature](electron-fermi-temperature.md) is $T_F=[\sqrt{m_e^2c^4+p_F^2c^2}-m_ec^2]/k_B$, where the [Fermi momentum](fermi-momentum.md) $p_F=\hbar(3\pi^2n_e)^{1/3}$ counts the net [Electrons](electron.md). Strong degeneracy requires $T\ll T_F$. With [mean molecular weight per electron](mean-molecular-weight-per-electron.md) $\mu_e$ and $n_e=\rho/(\mu_em_u)$, $T_F=(m_ec^2/k_B)[\sqrt{1+(\rho/\rho_*)^{2/3}}-1]$. Its nonrelativistic and ultrarelativistic limits scale as $\rho^{2/3}$ and $\rho^{1/3}$, respectively.
