# Migdal-Kadanoff approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Real-space renormalization group](real-space-renormalization-group.md)

A real-space approximation that moves or combines lattice bonds so that selected intermediate spins can be summed through a chain recursion. On a one-dimensional nearest-neighbour [Ising model](ising-model.md), no transverse [bond moving](bond-moving.md) is needed and [exact zero-field Ising-chain decimation](exact-zero-field-ising-chain-decimation.md) closes exactly. In higher dimensions, neglected geometric [correlations](pearson-correlation-coefficient.md) generally make the transformed model approximate on the original lattice. The order of [bond moving](bond-moving.md) and decimation must be stated; the two operations need not commute. The bond-moving-first convention is described in [Position Space Renormalization Group by Mehran Kardar](position-space-renormalization-group-by-mehran-kardar.md) lecture 14, section V.D.
