# Homogeneous element of a graded algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Graded algebra](graded-algebra.md)

An element of a single graded component $A_n$ of a [graded algebra](graded-algebra.md) is homogeneous of degree $n$. The degree sign rules for an [exterior product](exterior-product.md) or a [graded Lie bracket](graded-lie-bracket.md) apply first to homogeneous elements, then extend by [linearity](linearity.md).
