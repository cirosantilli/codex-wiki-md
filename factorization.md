# Factorization

↑ **Parent:** [Algebra](algebra-split.md)

Factorization expresses an object as a product of simpler factors. [Integer factorization](integer-factorization.md) decomposes an [integer](integer-split.md); [polynomial factorization](factorization-of-polynomials.md) decomposes a [polynomial](polynomial-split.md). The permitted factors depend on the ambient algebraic structure.
