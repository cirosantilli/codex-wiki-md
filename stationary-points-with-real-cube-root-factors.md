# Stationary points with real cube-root factors

↑ **Parent:** [Stationary point](stationary-point.md)

Where $q(x)\ne0$, the [product rule](product-rule.md) and [chain rule](chain-rule.md) give the displayed real derivative. Its stationary points therefore satisfy $3q+(x-a)q'=0$. The denominator uses the square of the real [cube root](cube-root.md), which stays positive for negative $q$. Points with $q=0$ must be checked in the original function: a simple zero of $q$ with $x\ne a$ creates a vertical tangent, not a stationary point. If the prefactor also vanishes, a cancellation can change differentiability and must be analyzed separately.
