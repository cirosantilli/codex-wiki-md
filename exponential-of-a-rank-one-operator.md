# Exponential of a rank-one operator

↑ **Parent:** [Powers of a rank-one operator](powers-of-a-rank-one-operator.md)

For the bounded [rank-one operator](rank-one-operator.md) $Kx=a\varphi(x)$, insert the [powers of a rank-one operator](powers-of-a-rank-one-operator.md) into the convergent [operator exponential](operator-exponential.md) [power series](power-series.md). Writing $c=\varphi(a)$ gives the displayed formula for $c\ne0$. At $c=0$ the quotient has [limit](limit-of-a-function.md) $t$, so $e^{tK}=I+tK$. This includes the zero operator and the nonzero [nilpotent operator](nilpotent-linear-map.md) cases without dividing by zero.
