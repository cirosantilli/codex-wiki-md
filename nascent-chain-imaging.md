# Nascent-chain imaging

↑ **Parent:** [Translational regulation](translational-regulation.md)

Imaging newly emerging peptide chains at individual [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) molecules. Release and run-off controls help distinguish translation-dependent signals from aggregates or completed [proteins](protein-split.md).
