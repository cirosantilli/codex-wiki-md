# Mechanical stability

↑ **Parent:** [Thermodynamic stability](thermodynamic-stability.md)

For a mechanical coordinate $x$ at fixed [temperature](temperature-split.md), total [volume](volume.md) and [particle](particle-physics.md) numbers, the relevant [thermodynamic potential](thermodynamic-potential-split.md) is the [Helmholtz free energy](helmholtz-free-energy.md) $F(x)$. Its generalized [force](force-split.md) is $f=-F'(x)$. At [mechanical equilibrium](mechanical-equilibrium.md), $f(x_*)=0$, and a linearly restoring [force](force-split.md) has $f'(x_*)<0$, equivalently $F''(x_*)>0$. A [Taylor expansion](taylor-expansion.md) then proves a strict local free-energy minimum. If $F$ is a [strictly convex function](strictly-convex-function.md) throughout the accessible domain, the stationary state is the unique global minimum.
