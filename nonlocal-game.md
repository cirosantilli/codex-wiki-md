# Nonlocal game

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum information theory](quantum-information-theory-split.md)

A task in which separated players receive local questions and return local answers, with a joint winning condition and no communication after questions are received. A strategy may use pre-shared randomness, or pre-shared [quantum entanglement](entangled-state.md) when quantum resources are allowed. An average winning [probability](probability.md) requires a specified input distribution; winning with certainty on every promised input is a separate worst-case requirement. The [GHZ game](ghz-game.md) gives a finite example with a perfect quantum strategy and no perfect classical strategy.
