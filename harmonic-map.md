# Harmonic map

↑ **Parent:** [Differential geometry](differential-geometry-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harmonic_map)

A harmonic map between Riemannian manifolds is a critical point of the Dirichlet [energy](energy.md) $\frac12\int|dQ|^2$. For a Euclidean domain and target unit [sphere](sphere.md), the equation is $\Delta Q+|\nabla Q|^2Q=0$. [Stationary wave maps](stationary-wave-map.md) are exactly harmonic maps of their spatial domain.
