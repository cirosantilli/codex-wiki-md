# Weak-star separability of the entire dual

↑ **Parent:** [Weak-star topology](weak-star-topology.md)

The continuous dual of a separable [Banach space](banach-space-split.md) is separable in its [weak-star topology](weak-star-topology.md), even when it is not norm separable. [Banach-Alaoglu theorem](banach-alaoglu-theorem.md) and [weak-star metrizability of the dual ball](weak-star-metrizability-of-the-dual-ball.md) make the dual unit ball a separable compact metric space. The countable union of positive integer multiples of a countable dense subset of that ball is dense in the whole dual. This does not imply global weak-star metrizability.
