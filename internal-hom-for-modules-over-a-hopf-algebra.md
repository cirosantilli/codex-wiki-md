# Internal hom for modules over a Hopf algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Hopf algebra](hopf-algebra.md)

The full $\operatorname{Hom}_k(M,N)$ carries the action $(hf)(u)=\sum h_{(1)}f(S(h_{(2)})u)$, making it the internal hom right adjoint to $-\otimes M$. No finite-dimensionality or inverse [antipode](antipode.md) is needed.
