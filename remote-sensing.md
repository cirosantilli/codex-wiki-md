# Remote sensing

↑ **Parent:** [Radiometry](radiometry-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Remote_sensing)

Inferring properties of a distant object from measured signals, often [electromagnetic radiation](electromagnetic-radiation-split.md). Spatial sampling, spectral information and observation cadence must jointly resolve the physical process of interest. Received [radiance](radiance.md) alone need not uniquely separate [temperature](temperature-split.md), [emissivity](emissivity.md) and atmospheric attenuation.
