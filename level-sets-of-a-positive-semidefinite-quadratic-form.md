# Level sets of a positive semidefinite quadratic form

↑ **Parent:** [Quadratic form](quadratic-form-split.md)

For a real [symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md) $A$ with nonnegative [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md), the [finite-dimensional spectral theorem](finite-dimensional-spectral-theorem.md) changes the equation to $\sum_i\lambda_iq_i^2=E$ in an [orthonormal eigenbasis](orthonormal-eigenbasis.md). If $E>0$ and $A$ has positive rank, the positive-eigenvalue coordinates form an [ellipsoid](ellipsoid.md), while the zero-eigenvalue coordinates are arbitrary. The level set is therefore a cylinder over that [ellipsoid](ellipsoid.md), with axis directions given by the [kernel of a linear map](kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) of $A$. For rank two in three dimensions, it is an [elliptic cylinder](elliptic-cylinder.md). At $E=0$ the level set is exactly the [kernel of a linear map](kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md); for $E<0$ it is empty. This distinguishes a [positive semidefinite matrix](positive-semidefinite-matrix.md) from a [positive-definite matrix](positive-definite-matrix.md), whose positive level sets are bounded [ellipsoids](ellipsoid.md).
