# Third-turn flat three-manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Holonomy groups of closed orientable flat three-manifolds](holonomy-groups-of-closed-orientable-flat-three-manifolds.md)

The [mapping torus](mapping-torus.md) of an order-three rotation of a hexagonal [flat torus](flat-torus.md) is a closed orientable [flat Riemannian manifold](flat-manifold.md) with holonomy $C_3$. A screw rotation through $2\pi/3$ with axial displacement $1/3$, together with the planar lattice, describes its deck group. Its first [Betti number](betti-number.md) is one.
