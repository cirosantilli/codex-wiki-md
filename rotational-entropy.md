# Rotational entropy

↑ **Parent:** [Rotational partition function](rotational-partition-function.md)

The [entropy](entropy-split.md) contribution from populated molecular rotational states. A nonlinear [rigid rotor](rigid-rotor.md) has $q_{\rm rot}\propto T^{3/2}$ at high [temperature](temperature-split.md), giving $S_{\rm rot,m}=R(\ln q_{\rm rot}+3/2)$. Its [rotational symmetry number](rotational-symmetry-number.md) removes redundant orientations.
