# Interaural level difference

↑ **Parent:** [Hearing](hearing.md)

A difference in sound level between the ears, often influenced by the head's direction- and frequency-dependent acoustic shadow. It complements [interaural time differences](interaural-time-difference.md), rather than serving as an exact independent direction label in every environment.
