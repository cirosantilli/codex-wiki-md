# Lagrangian leaf

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrangian foliation](lagrangian-foliation.md)

A leaf of a [Lagrangian foliation](lagrangian-foliation.md) is an immersed [Lagrangian submanifold](lagrangian-submanifold.md) tangent to its integrable distribution. A [prequantum line bundle](prequantum-line-bundle.md) restricts to a flat connection along such a leaf, because its curvature is proportional to the [symplectic form](symplectic-form.md). Nonzero global parallel sections require trivial [holonomy](holonomy.md) along every leaf loop.
