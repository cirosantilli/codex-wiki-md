<h1 id="cusp-core-problem">Cusp--core problem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Navarro--Frenk--White profile](navarro-frenk-white-profile.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cusp--core_problem)

The cusp--core problem is the tension between the central cusps of collisionless cold-dark-matter halo profiles and the approximately constant-density cores inferred for many dwarf and low-surface-brightness galaxies.
