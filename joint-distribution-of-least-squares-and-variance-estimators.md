# Joint distribution of least-squares and variance estimators

↑ **Parent:** [Normal linear model](normal-linear-model.md)

With a full-column-rank $n\times p$ design and isotropic normal errors, [ordinary least squares](ordinary-least-squares.md) gives $\widehat\beta\sim N_p(\beta,\sigma^2(X^TX)^{-1})$, independent of the residual squared length. Orthogonal design and residual projections of the [multivariate normal distribution](multivariate-normal-distribution.md) are independent, and $\operatorname{RSS}/\sigma^2\sim\chi^2_{n-p}$. The maximum likelihood variance estimate is $\operatorname{RSS}/n$, not the unbiased estimate $\operatorname{RSS}/(n-p)$.
