# Euclidean invariance of the shape operator

↑ **Parent:** [Shape operator](shape-operator.md)

For a [proper Euclidean motion of Euclidean three-space](proper-euclidean-motion-of-euclidean-three-space.md) $E(x)=Ax+b$, transport the [unit normal](unit-normal.md) by $\widetilde N(E(p))=AN(p)$. The corresponding shape operators are orthogonally conjugate:

$$
\widetilde S_{E(p)}=A S_p A^{-1}.
$$

Their [determinant](determinant.md) and [trace](matrix-trace.md), hence [Gaussian curvature](gaussian-curvature.md) and [mean curvature](mean-curvature.md), are unchanged.
