# Compound nucleus

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear reaction](nuclear-reaction.md)

An intermediate excited [atomic nucleus](atomic-nucleus-split.md) formed when the reactants' available [energy](energy-split.md) is redistributed among nuclear degrees of freedom before decay. Its resolved levels can appear as resonances. An isolated level's formation and decay into particular channels are described by [partial decay widths](partial-decay-width.md) in the [Breit-Wigner resonance cross section](breit-wigner-resonance-cross-section.md); direct reactions and overlapping resonances need not obey that simple one-level description.
