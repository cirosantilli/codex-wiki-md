# Structure-from-motion photogrammetry

↑ **Parent:** [Photogrammetry](photogrammetry.md)

A [photogrammetry](photogrammetry.md) workflow jointly estimating camera geometry and scene structure from many overlapping images, commonly followed by dense surface reconstruction. [Ground control points](ground-control-point.md) or accurately surveyed camera positions establish scale and georeferencing. Independent check points test accuracy rather than participating in the fit. A fine image ground-sampling interval does not alone establish equal vertical accuracy.
