# Coordinate axis

↑ **Parent:** [Cartesian coordinate system](cartesian-coordinate-system.md)

One of the [straight lines](straight-line.md) through the origin parallel to a [standard basis](standard-basis.md) [vector](vector.md) in a [Cartesian coordinate system](cartesian-coordinate-system.md). The positive direction corresponds to positive values of that coordinate.
