# Free group

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric group theory](geometric-group-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_group)

The free group $F(S)$ on a set $S$ consists of reduced words in letters $s^{\pm1}$, with multiplication followed by cancellation. Every map from $S$ to a group extends uniquely to a homomorphism from $F(S)$.
