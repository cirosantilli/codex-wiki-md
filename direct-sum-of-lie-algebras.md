# Direct sum of Lie algebras

↑ **Parent:** [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md)

The direct sum $\mathfrak g\oplus\mathfrak h$ has componentwise bracket

$$
[(x,y),(x',y')]=([x,x'],[y,y']).
$$

The two summands are commuting [ideals](ideal-of-a-lie-algebra.md).
