# Nonstationary spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [Stationary spacetime](stationary-spacetime.md)

A [nonstationary spacetime](nonstationary-spacetime.md) lacks the stationary time-translation symmetry in the domain being considered. Without a preferred suitable timelike [Killing vector field](killing-vector-field.md), the classical geometry alone does not determine a unique [positive-frequency solution](positive-frequency-solution.md) subspace for quantization.
