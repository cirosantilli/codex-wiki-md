# Complex stress potential for antiplane shear

↑ **Parent:** [Antiplane shear](antiplane-shear.md)

A [harmonic conjugate](harmonic-conjugate.md) $\phi$ satisfying $\phi_{,1}=w_{,2}$ and $\phi_{,2}=-w_{,1}$ makes $F=\phi+iw$ a [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function.md). Its derivative packages the two [antiplane shear](antiplane-shear.md) stresses into the displayed complex field. On multiply connected domains a global potential additionally requires the appropriate periods to vanish; prescribed displacement jumps can instead be represented by branches of the potential.
