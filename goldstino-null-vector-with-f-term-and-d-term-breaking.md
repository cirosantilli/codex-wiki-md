# Goldstino null vector with F-term and D-term breaking

↑ **Parent:** [Goldstino](goldstino.md)

For canonical global [four-dimensional N=1 supersymmetry](four-dimensional-n-1-supersymmetry.md), define $F^i=\overline{W_i}$, $H_{ij}=W_{ij}$ and $G_{ia}=g^a\bar\phi_j(T^a)^j{}_i$ at a stationary vacuum. [Gauge invariance](gauge-invariance.md) of the [superpotential](superpotential.md) gives $G^TF=0$, while stationarity of $V=\sum_i|W_i|^2+\frac12\sum_a(D^a)^2$ gives $HF-GD=0$. Therefore the canonically normalized [fermion mass matrix](fermion-mass-matrix.md), after a harmless [gaugino](gaugino.md) phase choice, obeys

$$
\begin{pmatrix}H&-\sqrt2G\\-\sqrt2G^T&0\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}F\\D/\sqrt2\end{pmatrix}=0.
$$

If [supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md) is broken, this vector is nonzero and specifies a massless [Goldstino](goldstino.md). The matrix with off-diagonal blocks $-G$ instead acts on $(F,D)$; it is related to the canonical matrix by a change of normalization, not by equality of canonical entries. Additional zero modes can coexist with the [Goldstino](goldstino.md).
