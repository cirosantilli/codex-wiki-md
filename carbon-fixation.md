# Carbon fixation

↑ **Parent:** [Metabolism](metabolism.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carbon_fixation)

[Carbon fixation](carbon-fixation.md) incorporates [carbon](carbon.md) from [carbon dioxide](carbon-dioxide.md) into organic [chemical compounds](chemical-compound.md). It identifies a material transformation, rather than a particular source of [energy](energy.md): [carbon fixation](carbon-fixation.md) can be supported by [photosynthesis](photosynthesis.md) or by other [metabolism](metabolism.md). The [Calvin cycle](calvin-cycle.md) is one pathway, not the definition of all [carbon fixation](carbon-fixation.md).
