# Union of equivalence relations

↑ **Parent:** [Equivalence relation](equivalence-relation.md)

A union of [equivalence relations](equivalence-relation.md) on the same [set](set-split.md) is reflexive and symmetric, but can fail [transitivity](transitive-relation.md). On a three-point [set](set-split.md), partitions into blocks $\{1,2\},\{3\}$ and $\{1\},\{2,3\}$ make the union relate $1$ to $2$ and $2$ to $3$ without relating $1$ to $3$. An equivalence relation containing both requires transitive closure as well.
