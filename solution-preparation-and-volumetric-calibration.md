# Solution preparation and volumetric calibration

↑ **Parent:** [Solution (chemistry)](solution-chemistry.md)

A calibration mark can be tested against an independent [mass](mass.md) measurement. The Buffalo State-hosted Laboratory Experiment 1. Calibration of Volumetric Glassware uses a $25\,\mathrm{mL}$ [pipette](pipette.md), filler, [conical flask](erlenmeyer-flask.md), weighing bottle, balance, and thermometer. It specifies clean, freely draining glassware, bubble-free filling, an eye-level [liquid meniscus](meniscus-liquid-surface.md) reading, gravity drainage, and retaining the residual liquid allowed for by the [pipette](pipette.md)'s calibration. Degassed [water](water.md) is delivered to a tared bottle; ten weighings and initial/final [temperature](temperature-split.md) readings give repeated delivered [volumes](volume.md) using the [water](water.md) [mass density](density.md). Their [mean](expected-value-split.md) and [standard deviation](standard-deviation.md) assess delivery and repeatability. The balance and thermometer must themselves be checked before claiming accuracy. The same handout describes weighing an empty [volumetric flask](volumetric-flask.md) and then weighing it filled to its mark.[Laboratory Experiment 1. Calibration of Volumetric Glassware, pp. 1–3: flask principle; Calibrating 25 mL pipette; Calibration Procedure, steps 1–8](calibration-of-volumetric-glassware-buffalo-state-laboratory-handout.md)

The independent inference is $V=m_{\mathrm{water}}/\rho_{\mathrm{water}}(T)$. Agreement between repeats constrains scatter; agreement with the nominal delivery additionally needs trustworthy [mass density](density.md), [temperature](temperature-split.md), and balance readings. This experiment tests delivered or contained [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) under its conditions. It does not prove that a subsequent [solute](solute.md) transfer is complete or a prepared [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md) is homogeneous.

The University of Wisconsin–Madison teaching module supplies the complementary preparation demonstration. Dissolved [solute](solute.md) and vessel rinsings reach the [volumetric flask](volumetric-flask.md); the contents reach room [temperature](temperature-split.md) before a dropper adjusts the eye-level [liquid meniscus](meniscus-liquid-surface.md) to the calibration mark. The module excludes a stirring bar during filling because its displaced [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md) is absent from the calibration, and requires mixing the neck liquid with the bulk [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md). Its self-check identifies incomplete [dissolution](dissolution.md) and transfer as a cause of lower-than-intended [molar concentration](molar-concentration.md).[Volumetric Flask: Dissolving a Solute, transfer paragraph and incomplete-dissolution self-check](volumetric-flask-dissolving-a-solute-wisconsin-chemistry-teaching-module.md)[Volumetric Flask: Filling a Volumetric Flask, temperature, displaced volume, and eye-level mark reading](volumetric-flask-filling-a-volumetric-flask-wisconsin-chemistry-teaching-module.md)[Volumetric Flask: Mixing the Solution in a Volumetric Flask, neck/bulk concentration difference](volumetric-flask-mixing-the-solution-in-a-volumetric-flask-wisconsin-chemistry-teaching-module.md)

The observable endpoints are a liquid boundary at a mark and a delivered [mass](mass.md), not a direct count of [solute](solute.md) particles. An overfilled flask has excessive final [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md); removing some well-mixed [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md) also removes [solute](solute.md), so it cannot recover the intended inventory merely by restoring the mark. [Measurement uncertainty](measurement-uncertainty.md), contamination, incomplete transfer, and sampling before full mixing limit the inferred [molar concentration](molar-concentration.md). The apparatus demonstrates why the words “dilute to” refer to final [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md) [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md).
