# Cyclotomic integer

↑ **Parent:** [Cyclotomic field](cyclotomic-field.md)

A cyclotomic integer is an element of $\mathbb Z[\zeta_n]$, where $\zeta_n$ is a [root of unity](root-of-unity.md). For a primitive $n$-th root, this is the full [ring of integers](ring-of-integers.md) of the [cyclotomic field](cyclotomic-field.md) $\mathbb Q(\zeta_n)$.
