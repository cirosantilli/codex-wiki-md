# Thin spherical charge layer limit

↑ **Parent:** [Electrostatics](electrostatics-split.md)

For $a<r<a(1+\delta)$, a [charge density](charge-density.md) $\rho_0a^2h'(r/a)/(\delta r^2)$ has total [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) $4\pi\rho_0a^3[h(1+\delta)-h(1)]/\delta$. As $\delta\to0$, it tends to uniform [surface charge](surface-charge.md) on $r=a$ with [surface charge density](surface-charge-density.md) $\sigma=\rho_0a h'(1)$. The limiting normal [electric field](electric-field-split.md) has jump $\sigma/\epsilon_0$, although every finite-thickness layer has continuous [electric field](electric-field-split.md) at its inner edge. Taking the thin-layer limit and taking a one-sided spatial limit need not commute.
