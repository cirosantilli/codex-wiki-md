<h1 id="hermitian-matrix-double-cover-of-so0-3-1">Hermitian-matrix double cover of SO0(3,1)</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Spin group](spin-group.md)

On two-by-two [Hermitian matrices](hermitian-operator.md), the [determinant](determinant.md) of $X=tI+x\cdot\sigma$ is $t^2-|x|^2$. The action $X\mapsto AXA^\dagger$ preserves it for $A\in SL(2,\mathbb C)$. A kernel element is unitary and commutes with every Hermitian matrix, hence is $\pm I$. The derivative is injective and both real dimensions are six, giving the full proper orthochronous [Lorentz group](lorentz-group.md) as the connected image.
