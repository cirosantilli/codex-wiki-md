# Brownian moment recursion

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md)

For standard one-dimensional Brownian motion, the Itô formula for $B^k$ gives this recursion. Stop at exits from $[-n,n]$ to make the stochastic integral a true martingale. Gaussian tail integrability and the [Doob Lp maximal inequality](doob-lp-maximal-inequality.md) dominate all stopped powers on a fixed horizon, justifying the limit. Odd moments vanish and $\mathbb EB_t^{2k}=(2k)!t^k/(2^kk!)$.
