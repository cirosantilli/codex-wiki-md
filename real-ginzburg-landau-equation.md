<h1 id="real-ginzburg-landau-equation">Real Ginzburg–Landau equation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Amplitude equation](amplitude-equation.md)

The real-coefficient Ginzburg–Landau [amplitude equation](amplitude-equation.md) has complex $A$ and real parameters, usually $\xi,g>0$ near a supercritical stationary pattern onset. A detuned plane wave $A=\rho e^{iQX}$ has $\rho^2=(r-\xi Q^2)/g$. Its phase and amplitude sidebands determine the narrower [Eckhaus instability](eckhaus-instability.md) stability band. It is a local envelope model; additional conserved fields or mean flows may need their own equations.
