# Voltameter

↑ **Parent:** [Electrolysis](electrolysis.md)

An instrument estimating transported [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) through an electrolysis product. For a specified reaction and current efficiency, deposited mass or collected gas is proportional to charge. Dividing by elapsed [time](time-in-physics.md) estimates mean [electric current](electric-current.md).
