# Spin phase in the Dirac time-reversal matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Time reversal of a Dirac field](time-reversal-of-a-dirac-field.md)

The matrix in [time reversal of a Dirac field](time-reversal-of-a-dirac-field.md) depends on the convention for the spin-flip phases and on the sign of the spinor identity used when relabeling the [mode expansion of a Dirac field](mode-expansion-of-a-dirac-field.md). If $\epsilon_s=(-1)^{1/2-s}$ and $\epsilon_su^{-s*}(p_T)=-\gamma^5Cu^s(p)$, the relabelled coefficient is $\epsilon_{-s}u^{-s*}(p_T)=\gamma^5Cu^s(p)$, giving $B=\gamma^5C$. A common phase changes $B$ but leaves its [gamma matrix](gamma-matrices.md) conjugation property and the induced [fermion bilinear](fermion-bilinear.md) transformation unchanged. The [antiunitary operator](antiunitary-operator.md) acts on both numerical coefficients and creation or annihilation operators.
