# Hydroxy group

↑ **Parent:** [Chemistry](chemistry-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydroxy_group)

A [hydroxy group](hydroxy-group.md) is an $\mathrm{OH}$ group attached to the rest of a [molecule](molecule.md). In the illustrated [sugars](sugar.md), its [oxygen](oxygen.md) is bonded to [carbon](carbon.md) and to [hydrogen](hydrogen.md).
