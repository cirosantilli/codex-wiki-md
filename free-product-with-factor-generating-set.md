# Free product with factor generating set

↑ **Parent:** [Free product](free-product.md)

For nontrivial groups $A,B$, the [Cayley graph](cayley-graph.md) of $A*B$ with $S=(A\setminus\{1\})\cup(B\setminus\{1\})$ has the edges of the [Bass-Serre tree](bass-serre-tree.md) as its vertices. Two such vertices are adjacent exactly when the tree edges share an endpoint. Mapping each group element to its tree-edge midpoint is an isometry on the vertex metrics and has image within distance $1/2$ of every tree point, hence gives a [quasi-isometry](quasi-isometry.md) of metric graphs. The [normal form theorem for a free product](normal-form-theorem-for-a-free-product.md) identifies $|g|_S$ with reduced syllable length. The factor subgroups have diameter one in this metric.
