# Singular integral operator

↑ **Parent:** [Integral operator](integral-operator.md)

A singular [integral](integral-split.md) operator has a kernel whose ordinary [integral](integral-split.md) can fail at points on the integration domain. A specified limiting prescription, such as a [Cauchy principal value](cauchy-principal-value.md), can still define the operator by cancellation. For the [Hilbert transform](hilbert-transform.md) kernel $1/(t-x)$, subtracting $f(x)$ from a locally Hölder-[continuous](continuous-function.md) density leaves $[f(t)-f(x)]/(t-x)$ locally integrable; the constant part is interpreted by symmetric deletion. The limiting prescription and the class of allowed densities are part of the operator's definition, not optional details.
