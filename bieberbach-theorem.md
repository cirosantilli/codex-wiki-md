# Bieberbach theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Flat manifold](flat-manifold.md)

The theorem classifies compact [flat manifolds](flat-manifold.md) through their crystallographic covering groups.

For a compact [flat Riemannian manifold](flat-manifold.md) $\mathbb R^n/\Gamma$, the translations in $\Gamma$ form a normal full-rank [Euclidean lattice](euclidean-lattice.md) of finite index. The quotient is its finite linear holonomy. Affine conjugacy of the torsion-free crystallographic groups classifies these manifolds up to affine diffeomorphism. An abelian $\Gamma$ must consist entirely of translations: commuting with every lattice translation forces each linear part to fix a spanning set of $\mathbb R^n$.
