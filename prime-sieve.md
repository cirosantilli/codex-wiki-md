# Prime sieve

↑ **Parent:** [Number-theoretic sieve](number-theoretic-sieve.md)

A [number-theoretic sieve](number-theoretic-sieve.md) used to enumerate [prime numbers](prime-number-split.md) by excluding [composite numbers](composite-number.md). The [sieve of Eratosthenes](sieve-of-eratosthenes.md) marks multiples of each discovered prime, beginning at its square.
