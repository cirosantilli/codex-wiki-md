# VC dimension of Euclidean balls

↑ **Parent:** [VC dimension](vc-dimension.md)

The positive-radius closed [Euclidean balls](euclidean-ball.md) in $\mathbb R^d$ have [VC dimension](vc-dimension.md) $d+1$ for $d\geq1$. Vertices of a [regular simplex](regular-simplex.md) are shattered: interpolate any desired signs by an [affine function](affine-function.md) $a\cdot x+b$, then perturb a sphere through the vertices to the score $1+\eta b+\eta a\cdot x-\|x\|^2$. For the upper bound, take an [affine dependence](affine-dependence.md) $\lambda$ among $d+2$ points and orient it so that $Q=\sum_i\lambda_i\|x_i\|^2\geq0$. Labelling the positive coefficients inside and the negative coefficients outside would force $\sum_i\lambda_i(r^2-\|x_i-c\|^2)>0$, while the [affine dependence](affine-dependence.md) makes this sum equal $-Q\leq0$. The weaker $d+2$ upper bound from unrestricted lifted [affine linear classifiers](affine-linear-classifier.md) loses their fixed quadratic coefficient.
