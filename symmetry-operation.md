# Symmetry operation

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular symmetry](molecular-symmetry.md)

A transformation that preserves a specified structure, including the chemical identity of sites in a [molecule](molecule-split.md). Rotations, reflections, inversion and improper rotations can occur in a [molecular point group](molecular-point-group.md). The associated action on [atomic orbitals](atomic-orbital.md) also changes their orientations and signs, which must be included when computing a [character of a representation](character-of-a-representation.md).
