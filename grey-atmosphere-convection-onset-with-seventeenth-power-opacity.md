# Grey-atmosphere convection onset with seventeenth-power opacity

↑ **Parent:** [Grey-atmosphere pressure with power-law opacity](grey-atmosphere-pressure-with-power-law-opacity.md)

For a [grey atmosphere](grey-atmosphere.md) with $T^4=T_e^4(2+3\tau)/4$, an [ideal gas](ideal-gas.md) and $\kappa=\kappa_0\rho T^{17}$, [hydrostatic equilibrium in optical depth](hydrostatic-equilibrium-in-optical-depth.md) integrates to $P^2=512\mathcal Rg[1/8-(2+3\tau)^{-3}]/(9\mu\kappa_0T_e^{16})$, taking $P(0)=0$. The logarithmic radiative gradient is $[(2+3\tau)^3-8]/48$. Equating it to the monatomic [adiabatic temperature gradient](adiabatic-temperature-gradient.md) $2/5$ gives $(2+3\tau_b)^3=136/5$ and the displayed onset temperature. Beyond this point the radiative profile must be replaced by convective transport.
