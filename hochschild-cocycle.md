# Hochschild cocycle

↑ **Parent:** [Hochschild cochain complex](hochschild-cochain-complex.md)

A Hochschild cocycle is a cochain $f$ with $\delta f=0$ in the [Hochschild cochain complex](hochschild-cochain-complex.md). For degree two, this is $a\mu(b,c)-\mu(ab,c)+\mu(a,bc)-\mu(a,b)c=0$. It is the condition for [associativity](associative-property.md) of a [square-zero extension of an algebra](square-zero-extension-of-an-algebra.md).
