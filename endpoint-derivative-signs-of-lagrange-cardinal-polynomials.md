# Endpoint derivative signs of Lagrange cardinal polynomials

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrange cardinal polynomial](lagrange-cardinal-polynomial.md)

At decreasing distinct [interpolation nodes](interpolation-node.md) $1\ge t_0>\cdots>t_n\ge-1$, the [Lagrange cardinal polynomial](lagrange-cardinal-polynomial.md) $L_i(x)=\prod_{j\ne i}(x-t_j)/\prod_{j\ne i}(t_i-t_j)$ has denominator sign $(-1)^i$. For $1\le k\le n$, its numerator's $k$th [derivative](derivative.md) at one is $k!$ times a sum of products of $n-k$ nonnegative factors $1-t_j$. At most one factor vanishes, and at least one product avoids it, so that sum is strictly positive. The same alternating nodal signs therefore maximize every endpoint derivative simultaneously.
