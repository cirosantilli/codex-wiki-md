# Quintic polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial](polynomial-split.md)

A quintic polynomial is a [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) of [polynomial degree](degree-of-a-polynomial.md) five. Its [polynomial roots](root-of-a-polynomial.md) can be found as the [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) of a $5\times5$ [companion matrix](companion-matrix.md).
