<h1 id="sobolev-gallagher-inequality">Sobolev–Gallagher inequality</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Sobolev inequality](sobolev-inequality.md)

For a [continuously differentiable function](continuously-differentiable-function.md) $F$ on an interval $I=[t-\delta/2,t+\delta/2]$,

$$
|F(t)|^2\leq\frac1\delta\int_I|F(u)|^2\,du+\int_I|F(u)F\prime(u)|\,du.
$$

To prove it, average $|F(t)|^2-|F(u)|^2$ over $u\in I$ using the [fundamental theorem of calculus](fundamental-theorem-of-calculus.md). On either half of $I$, the resulting derivative kernel has [absolute value](absolute-value.md) at most $1/2$. Since $|(|F|^2)\prime|\leq2|FF\prime|$, the displayed bound follows.
