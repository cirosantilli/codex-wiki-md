# p-deficiency

↑ **Parent:** [Group presentation](group-presentation.md)

For a [group presentation](group-presentation.md) $\mathcal P=\langle X\mid R\rangle$ with finitely many generators and a [prime number](prime-number.md) $p$, define

$$
\nu_p(r)=\max\{k\geq0:r=w^{p^k}\text{ in }F(X)\},\qquad\operatorname{def}_p(\mathcal P)=|X|-\sum_{r\in R}p^{-\nu_p(r)}.
$$

This uses the unshifted convention. Identity relators have zero weight, and a divergent sum gives value $-\infty$. Some literature subtracts one instead; inequalities must be shifted accordingly. Taking roots in the [free group](free-group.md) matters: roots appearing only after passing to the quotient do not change the relator weight. The [p-rank of a group](p-rank-of-a-group.md) bounds this quantity above, and the [index-p rewriting bound for p-deficiency](index-p-rewriting-bound-for-p-deficiency.md) gives the infinitude criterion [p-deficiency at least one implies infinitude](p-deficiency-at-least-one-implies-infinitude.md).
