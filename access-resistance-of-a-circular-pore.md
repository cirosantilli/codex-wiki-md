# Access resistance of a circular pore

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md)

The continuum spreading resistance for current entering a circular aperture of radius $a$ from one uniform semi-infinite reservoir of [electrical resistivity](electrical-resistivity.md) $\rho$ is $\rho/(4a)$. Two symmetric reservoirs contribute $\rho/(2a)$ in series with a cylindrical pore resistance $\rho\ell/(\pi a^2)$. Unobstructed access is not the same as zero spreading resistance; molecular-scale pores also require non-continuum transport physics.
