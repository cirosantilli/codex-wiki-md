# Shallow-water shock into stationary unit-depth water

↑ **Parent:** [Shallow water equations](shallow-water-equations.md)

For the dimensionless [shallow water equations](shallow-water-equations.md) with gravity coefficient one, let the left state be $(h,u)$, $h>0$, and the right state $(1,0)$. The [Rankine-Hugoniot conditions](rankine-hugoniot-conditions.md) are $s(h-1)=hu$ and $shu=hu^2+(h^2-1)/2$. For $h\ne1$ and $s>0$, eliminating $u$ gives the displayed formulas. The [characteristic speeds](characteristic-speed.md) are $u\pm\sqrt h$ and $\pm1$. Because $u-\sqrt h<s$ always, the positive branch can only be an admissible family-plus shock. Its remaining [Lax shock admissibility conditions](lax-shock-admissibility-conditions.md), $1<s<u+\sqrt h$, both reduce to $h>1$. At $h=1$ there is no jump and the labelled interface has no determined shock speed.
