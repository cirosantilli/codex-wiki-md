# Galerkin orthogonality

↑ **Parent:** [Finite element method](finite-element-method.md)

If $u$ solves a variational problem and $u_h$ is its conforming [Galerkin method](galerkin-method.md) approximation in $V_h$, then

$$
a(u-u_h,v_h)=0
$$

for every $v_h\in V_h$.
