# Range condition in variational regularization

↑ **Parent:** [Source condition in variational regularization](source-condition-in-variational-regularization.md)

The range condition asks whether a prescribed exact solution is also a minimizer of a penalized problem with suitably chosen artificial data. The [subgradient optimality condition](subgradient-optimality-condition.md) makes it equivalent to a source subgradient lying in the range of the adjoint forward operator.
