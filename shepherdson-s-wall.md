<h1 id="shepherdson-s-wall">Shepherdson's wall</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Constructible universe](constructible-universe.md)

The obstruction to proving the consistency of nonconstructibility by a uniform [inner model](inner-model.md) construction is that, inside a universe satisfying $V=L$, every transitive inner model containing all ordinals has $L$ as its own constructible universe and therefore is the entire ambient universe. The [absoluteness of constructible levels](absoluteness-of-constructible-levels.md) proves this by induction over successor definability and limit unions. Thus a construction valid in every model of [ZF](zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory.md) cannot always produce such an inner model satisfying $V\ne L$, and in particular cannot always produce one violating [axiom of choice](axiom-of-choice.md).
