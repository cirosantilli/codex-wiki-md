# Cayley-Bacharach theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Projective plane curve](projective-plane-curve.md)

In its classical nine-point cubic form, the [Cayley-Bacharach theorem](cayley-bacharach-theorem.md) says that a cubic through eight of the nine distinct intersections of two plane cubics must contain the ninth. The [eight-point cubic completion for two triples of lines](eight-point-cubic-completion-for-two-triples-of-lines.md) is the elementary case needed for triangular-strip propagation in [incidence geometry](incidence-geometry.md). The phenomenon explains why interpolation conditions at such a complete intersection are dependent.
