# Relativistic motion in parallel electric and magnetic fields

↑ **Parent:** [Constant-field relativistic Lorentz-force solution](constant-field-relativistic-lorentz-force-solution.md)

When uniform $\mathbf E$ and $\mathbf B$ are parallel, the [relativistic Lorentz force](relativistic-lorentz-force.md) splits into independent hyperbolic motion along the fields and circular motion in the perpendicular plane. The circle is periodic in [proper time](proper-time.md), while electric acceleration makes the helix's longitudinal pitch grow exponentially from one proper-time cyclotron period to the next.
