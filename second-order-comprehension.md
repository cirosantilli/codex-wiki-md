# Second-order comprehension

↑ **Parent:** [Second-order logic](second-order-logic.md)

[Second-order comprehension](second-order-comprehension.md) is a scheme asserting the existence of a [second-order relation variable](second-order-relation-variable.md) whose members are exactly the [tuples](finite-tuple.md) satisfying a permitted formula:

$$
\exists R\,\forall x_1\cdots\forall x_k\,
\bigl(R(x_1,\ldots,x_k)\leftrightarrow\varphi(x_1,\ldots,x_k,\bar a,\bar S)\bigr).
$$

Here $R$ is not free in $\varphi$, and the displayed individual and relation parameters are universally closed in each instance. Under [full semantics for second-order logic](full-semantics-for-second-order-logic.md), the selected external [subset](subset.md) of $D^k$ is always available. Under [Henkin semantics](henkin-semantics.md), the selected [subset](subset.md) must lie in $\mathcal H_k$ for every allowed assignment of parameters. Different systems permit different formula classes: for example, [arithmetical comprehension theory](arithmetical-comprehension-theory.md) restricts comprehension to formulas without bound set variables, while still permitting set parameters. Consequently, the exact formula and parameter policy must accompany any claim that a restricted range satisfies [second-order comprehension](second-order-comprehension.md).
