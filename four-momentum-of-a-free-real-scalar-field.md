# Four-momentum of a free real scalar field

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical stress-energy tensor](canonical-stress-energy-tensor.md)

For the free [real scalar field](real-scalar-field.md) with signature $(+,-,-,-)$,

$$
E=\frac12\int(\dot\phi^2+|\nabla\phi|^2+m^2\phi^2)d^3x,
\qquad \mathbf P=-\int\dot\phi\,\nabla\phi\,d^3x.
$$

The [canonical stress-energy tensor](canonical-stress-energy-tensor.md) is $T^{\mu\nu}=\partial^\mu\phi\partial^\nu\phi-\eta^{\mu\nu}\mathcal L$. Its divergence vanishes by the [Klein-Gordon equation](klein-gordon-equation.md), giving constant integrated charges when integrals and boundary flux are well behaved. The minus sign in contravariant spatial [momentum](momentum.md) is from $\partial^i=-\partial_i$; lower-index spatial charges have the opposite sign. A [plane wave](plane-wave.md) $\cos(Et-\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf x)$ has [momentum](momentum.md) density pointing along $\mathbf k$.
