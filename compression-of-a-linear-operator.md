# Compression of a linear operator

↑ **Parent:** [Operator theory](linear-operator-theory-split.md)

If $V$ is a subspace of a space with an [inner product](inner-product.md) and $P_V$ is its [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection-onto-a-finite-dimensional-subspace.md), the compression of an operator $A$ to $V$ is

$$
P_VA|_V:V\longrightarrow V.
$$

If the columns of $Z$ are an [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) of $V$, its matrix in that basis is $Z^*AZ$.
