# Marginal straight-line launch at thirty degrees

↑ **Parent:** [Thirty-degree magnetocentrifugal launching criterion](thirty-degree-magnetocentrifugal-launching-criterion.md)

At exactly thirty degrees the quadratic curvature vanishes, but the specified straight-line point-mass [effective potential](effective-potential.md) is downhill for small positive displacement at cubic order. Thus the strict negative-curvature criterion does not exclude every nonlinear one-sided marginal launch. An exactly stationary bead remains at equilibrium until displaced.
