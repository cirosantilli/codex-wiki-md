# Disjoint union

↑ **Parent:** [Set theory](set-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disjoint_union)

The disjoint union tags the elements of its summands, for example $X\sqcup Y=(X\times\{0\})\cup(Y\times\{1\})$.
