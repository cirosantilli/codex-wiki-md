# Regular clique counting lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Regular pair of vertex sets](regular-pair-of-vertex-sets.md)

For fixed $r$ and positive density threshold $\lambda$, sufficiently regular pairwise dense bipartite graphs between $r$ disjoint equal-sized vertex classes contain at least $\delta n^r$ transversal [cliques](clique-graph-theory.md), for some $\delta>0$. A greedy embedding maintains common neighbourhoods and discards the small set of vertices with atypical degrees into them. The constants depend only on $r,\lambda$.
