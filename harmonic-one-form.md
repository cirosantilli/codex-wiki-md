# Harmonic one-form

↑ **Parent:** [Harmonic differential form](harmonic-differential-form.md)

A harmonic one-form is a [differential one-form](one-form.md) $\alpha$ satisfying $\Delta\alpha=0$ for the [Hodge Laplacian](hodge-laplacian.md). On a [closed manifold](closed-manifold.md), the [Hodge decomposition theorem](hodge-decomposition-theorem.md) identifies these forms with degree-one [de Rham cohomology](de-rham-cohomology.md). The [Bochner-Weitzenbock formula for one-forms](bochner-weitzenbock-formula-for-one-forms.md) implies that [harmonic one-forms are parallel under nonnegative Ricci curvature](harmonic-one-forms-are-parallel-under-nonnegative-ricci-curvature.md).
