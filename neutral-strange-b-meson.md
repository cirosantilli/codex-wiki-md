# Neutral strange B meson

↑ **Parent:** [B meson](b-meson.md)

A [neutral strange B meson](neutral-strange-b-meson.md) has valence content $\bar bs$ in the conventional $B_s^0$ assignment; its antiparticle has $b\bar s$. In its charged-current spectator amplitude for $J/\psi\,\phi$, $\bar b\to\bar cW^+$ and $W^+\to c\bar s$, while the original [strange quark](strange-quark.md) is carried into the final strange pair. This is one Standard-Model contribution, not a complete treatment of mixing, loop amplitudes or [hadronization](hadronization.md).
