# Charge sharing in a strip detector

↑ **Parent:** [Silicon microstrip detector](silicon-microstrip-detector.md)

A localized ionization signal can be collected or induced on adjacent strips through [diffusion](diffusion.md) and the weighting fields of the readout electrodes. Combining their analogue signals estimates the impact position within a strip pitch, improving on [binary strip position resolution](binary-strip-position-resolution.md) when noise and calibration permit.
