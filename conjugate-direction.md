# Conjugate direction

↑ **Parent:** [Conjugate gradient method](conjugate-gradient-method.md)

Nonzero [vectors](vector.md) are conjugate directions with respect to a [symmetric positive-definite matrix](symmetric-positive-definite-matrix.md) $A$ when they are pairwise [orthogonal](orthogonal-vectors.md) in the [inner product](inner-product-split.md) $\langle u,v\rangle_A=u^TAv$. They need not be [orthogonal](orthogonal-vectors.md) in the ordinary Euclidean [inner product](inner-product-split.md). They are [linearly independent](linear-independence.md): multiplying a vanishing [linear combination](linear-combination.md) by $p_j^TA$ isolates its $j$th coefficient times $p_j^TAp_j>0$.
