# Lyapunov quantum control

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum control](quantum-control.md)

For $\dot\rho=-i[H_0+fH_1,\rho]$ and $\dot\rho_d=-i[H_0,\rho_d]$, the [Hilbert-Schmidt distance](hilbert-schmidt-distance.md) gives $V=\|\rho-\rho_d\|^2/2$. The choice $f=\operatorname{Tr}(\rho_d[-iH_1,\rho])$ yields $\dot V=-f^2$. Decrease alone does not prove convergence: orthogonal pure eigenstates of a diagonal $H_0$ give zero input for a transverse Pauli control even though their distance remains nonzero. Such a law can be simulated to produce an [open-loop control](open-loop-control.md).
