<h1 id="section-b/b6/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Use the inverse coordinate transformation,

$$
\mathbf r_\perp=\mathbf r'_\perp,\qquad
\mathbf r_\parallel=\gamma(\mathbf r'_\parallel+\mathbf vt'),\qquad
t=\gamma\left(t'+\frac{\mathbf v\cdot\mathbf r'}{c^2}\right).
$$

Substitution into the [wave phase](../../../../../phase-waves.md) $\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf r-\omega t$ and comparison of the coefficients of $\mathbf r'$ and $t'$ gives

$$
\boxed{\omega'=\gamma(\omega-\mathbf v\cdot\mathbf k),\qquad
\mathbf k'_\parallel=\gamma\left(\mathbf k_\parallel-\frac\omega{c^2}\mathbf v\right),\qquad
\mathbf k'_\perp=\mathbf k_\perp.}
$$

The first is the [relativistic Doppler effect](../../../../../relativistic-doppler-effect.md); the changed direction of the [wavevector](../../../../../wavevector.md) describes [relativistic aberration](../../../../../relativistic-aberration.md). Equivalently,

$$
\mathbf k'=\mathbf k+\left[(\gamma-1)\frac{\mathbf v\cdot\mathbf k}{v^2}-\gamma\frac\omega{c^2}\right]\mathbf v.
$$

The electric-field [wave amplitude](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) follows from part (b), after rotating the boost axis to $\mathbf v$:

$$
\boxed{\mathbf E'_0=\mathbf E_{0,\parallel}+\gamma(\mathbf E_{0,\perp}+\mathbf v\times\mathbf B_0).}
$$

For a vacuum [plane wave](../../../../../plane-wave.md), [Maxwell equations](../../../../../maxwell-equations-split.md) give $\mathbf B_0=\mathbf k\times\mathbf E_0/\omega$ and $\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf E_0=0$. Thus an expression using only the given electric [wave amplitude](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) and [wavevector](../../../../../wavevector.md) is

$$
\boxed{\mathbf E'_0=\mathbf E_{0,\parallel}+\gamma\left[\mathbf E_{0,\perp}
+\frac{\mathbf v\times(\mathbf k\times\mathbf E_0)}\omega\right].}
$$

Equivalently, $\mathbf E'_0=\gamma(\mathbf E_0+\mathbf v\times\mathbf B_0)-[\gamma^2/(\gamma+1)]\mathbf v(\mathbf v\cdot\mathbf E_0)/c^2$. The [wave amplitude](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) is not a scalar carried unchanged by the [wave phase](../../../../../phase-waves.md) transformation; its direction must remain transverse to the transformed [wavevector](../../../../../wavevector.md).
