<h1 id="section-b/b6/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

A uniform [Lorentz boost](../../../../../lorentz-boost.md) preserves the [plane wave](../../../../../plane-wave.md) form, while changing both its [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md) and its vector [wave amplitude](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md). Decompose vectors parallel and perpendicular to $\mathbf v$, so $\mathbf k=\mathbf k_\parallel+\mathbf k_\perp$. In the observer's primed coordinates,

$$
\boxed{\mathbf E'(\mathbf r',t')=\mathbf E'_0
\exp\!\left\{i\left[\mathbf k_\perp\cdot\mathbf r'_\perp+
\gamma\left(\mathbf k_\parallel-\frac\omega{c^2}\mathbf v\right)\cdot\mathbf r'_\parallel
-\gamma(\omega-\mathbf v\cdot\mathbf k)t'\right]\right\}.}
$$

The transformed [wave amplitude](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) is specified in part (d). The unprimed “[rest frame](../../../../../rest-frame.md)” in this question is the original coordinate frame, not a [rest frame](../../../../../rest-frame.md) of a vacuum light wave, which has no inertial [rest frame](../../../../../rest-frame.md).
