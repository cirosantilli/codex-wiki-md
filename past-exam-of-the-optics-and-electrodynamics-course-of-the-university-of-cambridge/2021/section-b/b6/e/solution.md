<h1 id="section-b/b6/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md) and longitudinal [wavevector](../../../../../wavevector.md) formulas are exactly the boost of a contravariant [Lorentz four-vector](../../../../../four-vector.md):

$$
\begin{pmatrix}\omega'/c\\\mathbf k'_\parallel\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}\gamma&-\gamma\beta\\-\gamma\beta&\gamma\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}\omega/c\\\mathbf k_\parallel\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\mathbf k'_\perp=\mathbf k_\perp,
$$

where the matrix is understood on the time component and the signed scalar component along the boost direction. Hence the [four-wavevector](../../../../../four-wavevector.md) obeys

$$
\boxed{K'^\alpha=\Lambda^\alpha{}_{\beta}K^\beta,\qquad K^\alpha=(\omega/c,\mathbf k).}
$$

For the metric signature $(+,-,-,-)$, lowering the position index gives $R_\alpha=(ct,-\mathbf r)$, so

$$
\boxed{R_\alpha K^\alpha=\omega t-\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf r.}
$$

It is a dimensionless [Lorentz scalar](../../../../../lorentz-scalar.md), equal to minus the [wave phase](../../../../../phase-waves.md) used in the stated complex wave. Invariance follows by contracting the mutually inverse position and [wavevector](../../../../../wavevector.md) transformations. Reversing metric signature reverses this displayed scalar's sign, but not its invariance or any observable [phase difference](../../../../../phase-difference.md). In vacuum, $K_\alpha K^\alpha=0$ is an additional null-vector check; the wave remains lightlike in every inertial frame.
