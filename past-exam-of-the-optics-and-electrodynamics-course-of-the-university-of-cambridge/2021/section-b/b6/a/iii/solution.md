<h1 id="section-b/b6/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [chain rule](../../../../../../chain-rule.md) gives

$$
\frac\partial{\partial x'^\alpha}
=\frac{\partial x^\beta}{\partial x'^\alpha}\frac\partial{\partial x^\beta}.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{\partial_\alpha\equiv\frac\partial{\partial x^\alpha}\text{ transforms covariantly}.}
$$

Differentiation with respect to a contravariant coordinate has a lower transformation index. In inertial Cartesian coordinates in [Minkowski spacetime](../../../../../../minkowski-spacetime.md), no connection term is needed for this coordinate transformation of a derivative acting on a scalar; Lorentz transformations have constant Jacobians.
