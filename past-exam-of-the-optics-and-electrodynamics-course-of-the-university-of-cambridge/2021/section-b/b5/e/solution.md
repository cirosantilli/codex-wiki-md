<h1 id="section-b/b5/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

On the $x$ axis the two geometric delays are identical:

$$
t_1=t_2=\frac{kx}{\sqrt2\omega}.
$$

Their field [wave amplitudes](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) therefore add as equal copies at zero relative delay. Stationarity removes dependence on the common delay, giving

$$
\boxed{I(x)=I_0+\operatorname{Re}\Gamma(0)=2I_0,}
$$

independent of $x$ in the ideal overlapping plane-wave region. The corresponding mean normal [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) flux through a transverse plane is $\sqrt2I_0$. There is no increasing path difference along the bisector, so finite [temporal optical coherence](../../../../../temporal-optical-coherence.md) does not produce a longitudinal visibility envelope on this axis.
