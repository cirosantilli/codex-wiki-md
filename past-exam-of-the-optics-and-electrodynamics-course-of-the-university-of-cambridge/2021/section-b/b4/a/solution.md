<h1 id="section-b/b4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [characteristic polynomial](../../../../../characteristic-polynomial.md) of the given [Jones matrix](../../../../../jones-matrix.md) is

$$
\det(J-\lambda I)=(a-\lambda)^2-b^2,
$$

so a convenient choice of [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) labels is

$$
\boxed{\lambda_+=a+b,\quad \mathbf u_+=\frac1{\sqrt2}\begin{pmatrix}1\\-i\end{pmatrix};\qquad
\lambda_-=a-b,\quad\mathbf u_-=\frac1{\sqrt2}\begin{pmatrix}1\\i\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

Direct multiplication gives $J\mathbf u_\pm=\lambda_\pm\mathbf u_\pm$, and $\mathbf u_+^\dagger\mathbf u_-=0$. They represent opposite [circular polarizations](../../../../../circular-polarization.md). Since $a,b$ are real, $J$ is a [Hermitian matrix](../../../../../hermitian-operator.md) and both [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) are real, but they need not be positive. A negative [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) adds a [wave phase](../../../../../phase-waves.md) reversal to that mode. The labels are algebraic: $\lambda_+$ is the larger numerical [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) only when $b\geq0$. If $b=0$, the [eigenvalue](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) is degenerate and every nonzero [Jones vector](../../../../../jones-vector.md) is an [eigenvector](../../../../../eigenvector.md); the circular pair remains a valid basis.
