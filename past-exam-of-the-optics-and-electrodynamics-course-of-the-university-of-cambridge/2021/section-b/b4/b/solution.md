<h1 id="section-b/b4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Take an incident field along $\widehat{\mathbf x}$, without loss of generality because this component has the same circular [eigenmodes](../../../../../normal-mode.md) for every direction of input [linear polarization](../../../../../linear-polarization.md). Resolve its [Jones vector](../../../../../jones-vector.md) into equal circular components:

$$
E_0\widehat{\mathbf x}=\frac{E_0}{\sqrt2}(\mathbf u_++\mathbf u_-),\qquad
\mathbf E_{\rm out}=\frac{E_0}{\sqrt2}(\lambda_+\mathbf u_++\lambda_-\mathbf u_-)
=E_0\begin{pmatrix}a\\-ib\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Using the [complex amplitude](../../../../../complex-amplitude.md) convention $\operatorname{Re}[\mathbf E e^{-i\omega t}]$, the real circular contributions are

$$
\mathbf E_+(t)=\frac{E_0\lambda_+}{2}(\widehat{\mathbf x}\cos\omega t-\widehat{\mathbf y}\sin\omega t),\qquad
\mathbf E_-(t)=\frac{E_0\lambda_-}{2}(\widehat{\mathbf x}\cos\omega t+\widehat{\mathbf y}\sin\omega t).
$$

They counter-rotate and add to

$$
\boxed{\mathbf E_{\rm out}(t)=E_0(a\widehat{\mathbf x}\cos\omega t-b\widehat{\mathbf y}\sin\omega t).}
$$

In general the tip traces an [ellipse](../../../../../ellipse.md) with semiaxis lengths $|a|E_0$ and $|b|E_0$. Equal transmitted circular [wave amplitudes](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) produce a line; removal of one circular mode, $\lambda_+=0$ or $\lambda_-=0$, produces a [circle](../../../../../circle.md). The special zero-output case has no defined [polarization of waves](../../../../../polarization-waves.md).
