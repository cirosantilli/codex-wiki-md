<h1 id="section-b/b4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The normalized circular [eigenvectors](../../../../../eigenvector.md) from part (a) define the [orthogonal projection](../../../../../orthogonal-projection.md) matrices

$$
P_+=\mathbf u_+\mathbf u_+^\dagger=\frac12\begin{pmatrix}1&i\\-i&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
P_-=\mathbf u_-\mathbf u_-^\dagger=\frac12\begin{pmatrix}1&-i\\i&1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Thus the [spectral decomposition](../../../../../spectral-decomposition.md) of the [Jones matrix](../../../../../jones-matrix.md) is

$$
\boxed{J=\lambda_+P_++\lambda_-P_-
=\frac12\begin{pmatrix}
\lambda_++\lambda_-&i(\lambda_+-\lambda_-)\\
-i(\lambda_+-\lambda_-)&\lambda_++\lambda_-
\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

This simply substitutes $a=(\lambda_++\lambda_-)/2$ and $b=(\lambda_+-\lambda_-)/2$ and makes each [eigenmode](../../../../../normal-mode.md)'s [wave amplitude](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) transmission explicit.
