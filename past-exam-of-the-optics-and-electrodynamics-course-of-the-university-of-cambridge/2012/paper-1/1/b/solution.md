<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a [Hertzian dipole](../../../../../hertzian-dipole.md) of [length](../../../../../length.md) $\ell$ driven at peak [electric current](../../../../../electric-current.md) $I_0$, the [electric dipole moment](../../../../../electric-dipole-moment.md) [amplitude](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) is $p_0=I_0\ell/\omega$. Consequently

$$
\overline P=\frac{\omega^4p_0^2}{12\pi\epsilon_0c^3}
=\frac{I_0^2\ell^2\omega^2}{12\pi\epsilon_0c^3}.
$$

The [Hertzian dipole fixed-current power scaling](../../../../../hertzian-dipole-fixed-current-power-scaling.md) is thus $\overline P\propto I_0^2\ell^2/\lambda^2$, giving

$$
\boxed{\frac{P_1}{P_2}=\left(\frac{1}{0.5}\right)^2\left(\frac{25}{20}\right)^2=6.25.}
$$

The inverse-fourth-power [wavelength](../../../../../wavelength.md) scaling would hold at fixed [electric dipole moment](../../../../../electric-dipole-moment.md), not at the fixed [electric current](../../../../../electric-current.md) specified here.
