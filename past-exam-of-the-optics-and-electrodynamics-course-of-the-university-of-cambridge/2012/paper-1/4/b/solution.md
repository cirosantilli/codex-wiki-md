<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A material segment of the line with rest [length](../../../../../length.md) $dx'$ has laboratory [length](../../../../../length.md) $dx=dx'/\gamma$, while its charge $dQ$ is unchanged. Thus

$$
\boxed{\lambda=\gamma\lambda',\qquad I=\lambda u=\gamma\lambda'u.}
$$

Equivalently the line-integrated [four-current](../../../../../four-current.md) transforms from $(c\lambda',0)$ to $(\gamma c\lambda',\gamma u\lambda')$. Gauss's and [Ampère's laws](../../../../../ampere-s-circuital-law.md) in $S$ now give $E_r=\lambda/(2\pi\epsilon_0r)$ and $B_\varphi=\mu_0I/(2\pi r)$, exactly the fields found in part (a). [Charge density](../../../../../charge-density.md) is frame-dependent even though the charge of the same set of particles is invariant.
