<h1 id="4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

Take $x^\mu=(ct,x,y,z)$ and [metric signature](../../../../metric-signature.md) $(+,-,-,-)$. The [four-current](../../../../four-current.md), [four-potential](../../../../electromagnetic-four-potential.md) and [four-gradient](../../../../four-gradient.md) are

$$
J^\mu=(c\rho,\mathbf j),\qquad
A^\mu=(\phi/c,\mathbf A),\qquad
\partial_\mu=(c^{-1}\partial_t,\nabla),\qquad
\partial^\mu=(c^{-1}\partial_t,-\nabla).
$$

The first two transform as contravariant [four-vectors](../../../../four-vector.md) under an inertial-frame [Lorentz transformation](../../../../lorentz-transformation.md), within a consistently transformed electromagnetic gauge. The lowered-index [gradient](../../../../gradient.md) is a [covector](../../../../covector.md). Their contraction is a [Lorentz scalar](../../../../lorentz-scalar.md):

$$
\partial_\mu J^\mu=\partial_t\rho+\nabla\cdot\mathbf j=0.
$$

Indeed, $J'^\mu=\Lambda^\mu{}_{\nu}J^\nu$ and $\partial'_\mu=(\Lambda^{-1})^\alpha{}_{\mu}\partial_\alpha$, so the two matrices cancel in the contraction. **Local [charge conservation](../../../../charge-conservation.md) in one [inertial frame](../../../../inertial-frame.md) implies local [charge conservation](../../../../charge-conservation.md) in every [inertial frame](../../../../inertial-frame.md).** Integrating over a region gives the charge change equal to minus its outward current [flux](../../../../flux.md); global conserved charge requires no [flux](../../../../flux.md) through the system boundary.
