<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The longitudinal [Faraday effect](../../../../../faraday-effect.md) produces [circular birefringence](../../../../../circular-birefringence.md): right- and left-circular components have different [phase velocities](../../../../../phase-velocity.md). Linear input contains equal [amplitudes](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) of both circular eigenpolarizations. A relative [phase](../../../../../phase-waves.md) $\delta_F$ rotates its [linear polarization](../../../../../linear-polarization.md) through $\delta_F/2$, preserving linearity in an ideal transparent sample. Conventionally the signed rotation is $\vartheta_F=VB_\parallel d_F$, with [Verdet constant](../../../../../verdet-constant.md) $V$.

**A pure Faraday rotator cannot serve as the required switchable [quarter-wave plate](../../../../../quarter-wave-plate.md).** It rotates a line into another line rather than producing the required relative [phase](../../../../../phase-waves.md) between fixed [orthogonal](../../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) linear components. Applying a field that gives a nominal quarter-cycle circular-eigenmode retardation simply rotates [linear polarization](../../../../../linear-polarization.md) by $45^\circ$. Circular dichroism or a separate linear retarder would change the device, and is not the stated pure Faraday mechanism.
