<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

[Spatial coherence](../../../../../spatial-optical-coherence.md) compares the [electric fields](../../../../../electric-field-split.md) at different positions at a common time; [temporal coherence](../../../../../temporal-optical-coherence.md) compares the field with itself at a delay. Experimentally, they control whether separated apertures or unequal optical paths can form stable [interference](../../../../../interference-wave-propagation.md) fringes. They are correlation properties, not a requirement that every emitted wave have a deterministic [phase](../../../../../phase-waves.md).

The source diameter is $\alpha=40/206265=1.94\times10^{-4}\,\mathrm{rad}$. At a representative visible [wavelength](../../../../../wavelength.md) $\lambda=550\,\mathrm{nm}$,

$$
\boxed{\ell_\perp\sim\frac{\lambda}{\alpha}=2.8\,\mathrm{mm}.}
$$

For a uniformly bright circular disc, the first visibility zero is at $1.22\lambda/\alpha=3.5\,\mathrm{mm}$; the coefficient depends on the chosen coherence criterion and brightness distribution. Atmospheric [phase](../../../../../phase-waves.md) fluctuations can further spoil measured coherence.

For an explicitly assumed broad visible band from $400$ to $700\,\mathrm{nm}$, the [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md) span is $\Delta\nu=c(1/\lambda_{\min}-1/\lambda_{\max})=3.21\times10^{14}\,\mathrm{Hz}$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\ell_\parallel\sim\frac c{\Delta\nu}\simeq0.93\,\mathrm{\mu m},\qquad
\tau_c\sim\frac1{\Delta\nu}\simeq3.1\,\mathrm{fs}.}
$$

Thus broadband planetary [light](../../../../../light.md) has millimetre-scale transverse and micrometre-scale longitudinal coherence. The receiver band is not supplied: a narrow filter would increase the [temporal coherence](../../../../../temporal-optical-coherence.md) length, so this is a stated broadband estimate, not a unique source-only constant.
