<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

With B nearest the observer, [light](../../../../../light.md) from the reflecting target near the observer first crosses Q and then P on its way to the mirror. [Unpolarized light](../../../../../unpolarized-light.md) remains unpolarized through Q, then becomes x linear after P. Mirror [reflection](../../../../../reflection-mathematics.md) preserves that linear orientation. On return it passes the same P without further ideal rejection and then Q, becoming circular toward the observer. Thus **the reflected target remains visible**.

Algebraically, with input and output at the observer side,

$$
J_{\rm return}=QPM P Q=-QPQ,\qquad
\operatorname{tr}(J_{\rm return}C_{\rm in}J_{\rm return}^\dagger)=\frac{I_0}{2}.
$$

The target here is used only as a source/reflector of ordinary [unpolarized light](../../../../../unpolarized-light.md); no physiological model is involved.
