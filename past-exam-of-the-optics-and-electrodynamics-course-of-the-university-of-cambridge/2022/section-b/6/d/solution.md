<h1 id="section-b/6/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

[Spin-orbit coupling](../../../../../spin-orbit-interaction.md) is a relativistic interaction between a particle's [spin](../../../../../spin.md) and its [orbital angular momentum](../../../../../orbital-angular-momentum.md) or, more generally, its motion through an [electric field](../../../../../electric-field-split.md). A moving [electron](../../../../../electron.md) experiences a transformed [magnetic field](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md), which couples to its [magnetic dipole moment](../../../../../magnetic-dipole-moment.md). For an electron bound in a central electrostatic potential energy $U(r)$, the leading interaction, including the [Thomas precession](../../../../../thomas-precession.md) correction, is

$$
\boxed{H_{\rm so}=\frac1{2m_e^2c^2r}\frac{dU}{dr}\,\mathbf L\cdot\mathbf S.}
$$

Here $\mathbf L$ is [orbital angular momentum](../../../../../orbital-angular-momentum.md) and $\mathbf S$ is [spin](../../../../../spin.md). The factor of one-half relative to the simplest rest-field argument comes from [Thomas precession](../../../../../thomas-precession.md) of the accelerated [electron](../../../../../electron.md) frame.[§3.1.3, printed pp. 71–75: rest-frame magnetic interaction and Thomas correction](../../../../../topics-in-quantum-mechanics-by-david-tong.md)

For example, in the [hydrogen atom](../../../../../hydrogen-atom.md), the $2p$ states with total [angular momentum](../../../../../angular-momentum.md) $j=1/2$ and $j=3/2$ have different $\mathbf L\cdot\mathbf S$ [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md). Their splitting is part of the atomic [fine structure](../../../../../fine-structure.md). The interaction redistributes and splits spin-dependent energies; it is not the same physical effect as the spin-independent convective magnetic force between charged filaments.
