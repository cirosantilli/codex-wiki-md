<h1 id="section-b/6/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The infinitely long filaments have no finite total [force](../../../../../force-split.md); the meaningful result is [force per unit length](../../../../../force-per-unit-length.md) in the laboratory. Evaluate only A's field at B, excluding B's singular self-field. B is at $y=0,z=-d/2$, so define

$$
E_*\equiv\frac{\eta}{2\pi\epsilon_0d},\qquad
a_y=0,\quad a_z=-E_*.
$$

In both cases A moves at $+v\widehat{\mathbf x}$, hence at B

$$
\mathbf E_A=-\gamma E_*\widehat{\mathbf z},\qquad
\mathbf B_A=+\frac{\gamma vE_*}{c^2}\widehat{\mathbf y}.
$$

For a filament with [linear charge density](../../../../../linear-charge-density.md) $\lambda_B$ and convective [electric current](../../../../../electric-current.md) $I_B$, the [Lorentz force](../../../../../lorentz-force.md) per laboratory length is

$$
\mathbf f_B=\lambda_B\mathbf E_A+I_B\widehat{\mathbf x}\times\mathbf B_A.
$$

In Case 1, $\lambda_B=\gamma\eta$ and $I_B=\gamma\eta v$. Electric repulsion and magnetic attraction give

$$
\mathbf f_{B,1}=-\gamma^2\eta E_*\widehat{\mathbf z}
+\gamma^2\beta^2\eta E_*\widehat{\mathbf z},
$$

so

$$
\boxed{\mathbf f_{B,1}=-\frac{\eta^2}{2\pi\epsilon_0d}\widehat{\mathbf z}.}
$$

The cancellation uses $\gamma^2(1-\beta^2)=1$. In Case 2, B is stationary, $\lambda_B=\eta$ and $I_B=0$, giving

$$
\boxed{\mathbf f_{B,2}=-\frac{\gamma\eta^2}{2\pi\epsilon_0d}\widehat{\mathbf z}.}
$$

Both results have units $\mathrm{N\,m^{-1}}$ and are repulsive, away from A, irrespective of the common sign of $\eta$. In Case 1 the velocity independence of force per laboratory length does not violate the transverse [force transformation](../../../../../relativistic-force-transformation.md): a material segment of proper length $\ell_0$ occupies laboratory length $\ell_0/\gamma$, so its force is the rest-frame force divided by $\gamma$. The calculation interprets the stated $\eta$ as each filament's final proper density; holding a laboratory density fixed during acceleration would be a different preparation.
