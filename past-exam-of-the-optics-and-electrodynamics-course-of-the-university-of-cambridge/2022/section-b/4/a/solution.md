<h1 id="section-b/4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Birefringence](../../../../../birefringence.md) means that two independent polarization eigenmodes have different [refractive indices](../../../../../refractive-index.md) at the same [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md) and propagation direction. The rotatory-power formula relevant here requires [circular birefringence](../../../../../circular-birefringence.md): the eigenmodes are opposite [circular polarizations](../../../../../circular-polarization.md). General linear [birefringence](../../../../../birefringence.md) instead introduces [phase retardation](../../../../../phase-retardation.md) between linearly polarized components and need not rotate every incident [linear polarization](../../../../../linear-polarization.md) through one common angle.

Use the [complex amplitude](../../../../../complex-amplitude.md) convention $\operatorname{Re}[\mathbf E e^{-i\omega t}]$ and transverse [unit vectors](../../../../../unit-vector.md) $\mathbf e_1,\mathbf e_2$. Define

$$
\mathbf u_\pm=\frac{\mathbf e_1\mp i\mathbf e_2}{\sqrt2},\qquad
\mathbf e_1=\frac{\mathbf u_++\mathbf u_-}{\sqrt2}.
$$

Propagation through thickness $d$ adds [phases](../../../../../phase-waves.md) $\phi_\pm=(2\pi/\lambda_0)n_\pm d$, where $\lambda_0$ is the [vacuum](../../../../../vacuum.md) [wavelength](../../../../../wavelength.md). Recombining the two components gives

$$
\frac{e^{i\phi_+}\mathbf u_++e^{i\phi_-}\mathbf u_-}{\sqrt2}
=e^{i\bar\phi}(\mathbf e_1\cos\theta+\mathbf e_2\sin\theta),\qquad
\theta=\frac{\phi_+-\phi_-}{2}.
$$

Thus the rotation and the specific [rotatory power](../../../../../rotatory-power.md) per unit length are

$$
\boxed{\theta=\frac{\pi d}{\lambda_0}(n_+-n_-),\qquad
\rho\equiv\frac\theta d=\frac\pi{\lambda_0}(n_+-n_-)=\frac\omega{2c}(n_+-n_-).}
$$

The units of $\rho$ are $\mathrm{rad\,m^{-1}}$. The sign follows the specified circular basis and viewing convention. Equal attenuation of the two eigenmodes has been assumed; unequal attenuation also produces [elliptical polarization](../../../../../elliptical-polarization.md).
