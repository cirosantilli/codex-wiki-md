The transverse and longitudinal quantities characterize different correlations: [spatial optical coherence](../../../../spatial-optical-coherence.md) across a wavefront and [temporal optical coherence](../../../../temporal-optical-coherence.md) between delayed fields. Their numerical values require a convention for how much correlation counts as coherent.
