<h1 id="section-a/a2/retardation-mechanism">Retardation mechanism</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Solution](solution.md)

[Birefringence](../../../../birefringence.md) gives two orthogonal linear eigenpolarizations with different [refractive indices](../../../../refractive-index.md), $n_f$ and $n_s$. They accumulate different [wave phases](../../../../phase-waves.md) through the same physical thickness $d$. Their relative [phase retardation](../../../../phase-retardation.md) is

$$
\delta=\frac{\omega d}{c}(n_s-n_f)=\frac{2\pi d}{\lambda_0}(n_s-n_f),\qquad
\boxed{\delta=\pm\frac\pi2\pmod{2\pi}}.
$$

Here $\lambda_0$ is the [vacuum](../../../../vacuum.md) [wavelength](../../../../wavelength.md). For the thinnest positive-retardance plate, $d=\lambda_0/[4(n_s-n_f)]$. The plate uses a difference in [phase velocity](../../../../phase-velocity.md), rather than differential [wave absorption](../../../../wave-absorption.md). Equal [wave amplitudes](../../../../wave-amplitude.md) along the two axes, obtained from input [linear polarization](../../../../linear-polarization.md) at $45^\circ$ to them, acquire [phase quadrature](../../../../phase-quadrature.md) and become [circular polarization](../../../../circular-polarization.md). Reversing that conversion gives [linear polarization](../../../../linear-polarization.md) from the corresponding circular input. A different input angle generally gives [elliptical polarization](../../../../elliptical-polarization.md) on one pass.
