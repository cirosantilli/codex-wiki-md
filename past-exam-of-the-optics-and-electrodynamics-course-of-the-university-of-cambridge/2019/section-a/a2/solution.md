↑ **Parent:** [A2](../a2.md)

Use a transparent reciprocal [wave plate](../../../../waveplate.md) at its design [wavelength](../../../../wavelength.md). For the return calculation, assume a mirror at [normal incidence](../../../../normal-incidence.md) with the same reflection coefficient for both transverse components; this is the usual ideal [double-pass quarter-wave plate](../../../../double-pass-quarter-wave-plate.md) arrangement.
