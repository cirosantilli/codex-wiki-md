<h1 id="section-b/b5/binding-scale-and-recoil">Binding scale and recoil</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Solution](solution.md)

A distinct classical [Thomson scattering](../../../../thomson-scattering.md) window requires two separated scales:

$$
\omega_0\ll\omega\ll\omega_C,\qquad \omega_C=\frac{mc^2}{\hbar}.
$$

To remove that window, the binding frequency must be comparable with or greater than the [Compton scattering](../../../../compton-scattering.md) recoil scale. Thus the formal condition for [bound-electron scattering without a Thomson frequency window](../../../../bound-electron-scattering-without-a-thomson-frequency-window.md) is

$$
\sqrt{\frac km}\gtrsim\frac{mc^2}{\hbar},\qquad
\boxed{k\gtrsim\frac{m^3c^4}{\hbar^2}.}
$$

Using the stipulated approximate [electron mass](../../../../electron-mass.md) $m=10^{-30}\,\mathrm{kg}$, $c=2.998\times10^8\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ and $\hbar=1.055\times10^{-34}\,\mathrm{J\,s}$ gives

$$
\boxed{k_{\rm scale}\simeq7.3\times10^{11}\,\mathrm{N\,m^{-1}},\qquad
\omega_C\simeq8.5\times10^{20}\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}.}
$$

The dimensions are $[m^3c^4/\hbar^2]=\mathrm{kg\,s^{-2}}=\mathrm{N\,m^{-1}}$. The inequality denotes a scale comparison, not a uniquely specified spring constant or a sharp transition.

There is also a physical limitation to the question's premise. [Compton scattering](../../../../compton-scattering.md) does not begin discontinuously at $\hbar\omega=mc^2$; recoil corrections grow as photon [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) approaches the rest [energy](../../../../energy-split.md). If $\hbar\omega_0\gtrsim mc^2$, the assumed nonrelativistic binding model is itself no longer reliable. The calculation therefore locates where no well-separated classical [Thomson scattering](../../../../thomson-scattering.md) range exists; it cannot establish a literal direct transition of a real strongly bound system using this oscillator equation alone.
