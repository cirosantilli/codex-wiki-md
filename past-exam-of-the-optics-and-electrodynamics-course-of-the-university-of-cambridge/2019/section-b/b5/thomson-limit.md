<h1 id="section-b/b5/thomson-limit">Thomson limit</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Solution](solution.md)

[Thomson scattering](../../../../thomson-scattering.md) describes the low-recoil scattering of [electromagnetic radiation](../../../../electromagnetic-radiation-split.md) by a free charge. For charge $q$ and [mass](../../../../mass.md) $m$, the weak incident field gives peak [acceleration](../../../../acceleration.md) $|q|E_0/m$. The mean [Larmor formula](../../../../larmor-formula.md) [power](../../../../power.md) is therefore

$$
\langle P\rangle=\frac{q^4|E_0|^2}{12\pi\epsilon_0m^2c^3}.
$$

There is no binding force to introduce a characteristic [frequency](../../../../frequency.md). Dividing by the same incident [light intensity](../../../../light-intensity.md) gives the [Thomson cross-section](../../../../thomson-cross-section.md):

$$
\boxed{\sigma_T=\frac{q^4}{6\pi\epsilon_0^2m^2c^4}
=\frac{8\pi}{3}\left(\frac{q^2}{4\pi\epsilon_0mc^2}\right)^2.}
$$

For an [electron](../../../../electron.md), the length inside parentheses is the [classical electron radius](../../../../classical-electron-radius.md). The [cross section](../../../../cross-section-physics.md) has units $\mathrm m^2$ and is independent of [wavelength](../../../../wavelength.md) in this classical limit. The driven [velocity](../../../../velocity.md) must remain small compared with $c$, and $\hbar\omega\ll mc^2$ must make [Compton recoil kinematics](../../../../compton-recoil-kinematics.md) negligible.
