<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For [two-element polarization identification](../../../../../two-element-polarization-identification.md), assume ideal elements and use an [intensity](../../../../../intensity-physics.md) detector. For each source, test both element orders while rotating their relative axes. **The circular source followed by the [quarter-wave plate](../../../../../quarter-wave-plate.md) and then the [polarizer](../../../../../polarizer.md) is the combination that gives full [intensity](../../../../../intensity-physics.md) modulation.** The plate converts circular [light](../../../../../light.md) to linear [light](../../../../../light.md), and the downstream [polarizer](../../../../../polarizer.md) then obeys [Malus's law](../../../../../malus-s-law.md).

If the [polarizer](../../../../../polarizer.md) comes first, it transmits half the circular source's [intensity](../../../../../intensity-physics.md) and the downstream lossless plate cannot change that [intensity](../../../../../intensity-physics.md). [Unpolarized light](../../../../../unpolarized-light.md) also gives constant half-intensity in either order: a wave plate leaves it unpolarized, while a [polarizer](../../../../../polarizer.md) followed by a wave plate has no final analyzer. Thus the modulating source is circular, its first element is the [quarter-wave plate](../../../../../quarter-wave-plate.md), and its second is the [polarizer](../../../../../polarizer.md); the remaining source is unpolarized. Testing both orders avoids needing to know either source's original brightness.
