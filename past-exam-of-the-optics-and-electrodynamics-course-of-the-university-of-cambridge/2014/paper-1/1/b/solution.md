<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For the [oblique coupling of parallel Hertzian dipoles](../../../../../oblique-coupling-of-parallel-hertzian-dipoles.md), a lossless [Hertzian dipole](../../../../../hertzian-dipole.md) has directional gain $G(\theta)=\tfrac32\sin^2\theta$. The separation is $r=\sqrt2d$, and both transmitting and receiving patterns have $\sin^2\theta=1/2$, so $G_t=G_r=3/4$. [Reciprocity](../../../../../reciprocity-electromagnetism.md) gives matched receiving effective area $A_e=\lambda^2G_r/(4\pi)$. The emitted polarization matches the transverse projection of the receiving dipole axis, so there is no additional polarization mismatch. Consequently

$$
\boxed{P_L=\frac{PG_t}{4\pi r^2}\frac{\lambda^2G_r}{4\pi}
=\frac{9P\lambda^2}{512\pi^2d^2}.}
$$

Both the off-axis transmitting pattern and the off-axis receiving aperture matter. This assumes the stated far field, conjugate matching and negligible dissipative antenna losses.
