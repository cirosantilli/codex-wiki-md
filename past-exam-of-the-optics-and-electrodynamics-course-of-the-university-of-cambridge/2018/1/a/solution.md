<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [London penetration depth](../../../../london-penetration-depth.md) measures the density-to-mass ratio of carriers participating in superconducting screening. With electron-counted density $n_s$ and the stipulated bare [electron mass](../../../../electron-mass.md) $m_e$,

$$
\lambda_L^2=\frac{m_e}{\mu_0n_se^2},\qquad
n_s=\frac{m_e}{\mu_0e^2\lambda_L^2}.
$$

Using $m_e=9.109\times10^{-31}\,\mathrm{kg}$, $e=1.602\times10^{-19}\,\mathrm C$, $\mu_0\simeq4\pi\times10^{-7}\,\mathrm{H\,m^{-1}}$ and $\lambda_L=22\times10^{-9}\,\mathrm m$ gives

$$
\boxed{n_s\simeq5.8\times10^{28}\,\mathrm{m^{-3}}.}
$$

This is the requested conduction-electron density within the assumed local [London equation](../../../../london-equations.md) model. More precisely, it is the [superconducting carrier density from London screening](../../../../superconducting-carrier-density-from-london-screening.md); identifying it with all conduction [electrons](../../../../electron.md) assumes that they participate in screening at the measurement [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md). No temperature-dependent normal-carrier fraction is supplied.

If the condensate is instead counted as [Cooper pairs](../../../../cooper-pair.md), the pair mass is $2m_e$ and charge magnitude is $2e$, giving pair density $n_{\rm pair}=n_s/2\simeq2.9\times10^{28}\,\mathrm{m^{-3}}$. Keeping [electron](../../../../electron.md) and pair counting consistent avoids a spurious factor of two.
