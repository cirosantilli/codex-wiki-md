<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

At [normal incidence](../../../../normal-incidence.md), continuity of tangential [wave phase](../../../../phase-waves.md) makes both internal [wavevectors](../../../../wavevector.md) normal to the parallel faces. For the [ordinary wave](../../../../ordinary-wave.md) in a [uniaxial optical medium](../../../../uniaxial-optical-medium.md), the [electric field](../../../../electric-field-split.md) is an eigenpolarization of the [permittivity tensor](../../../../permittivity-tensor.md), so $\mathbf D\parallel\mathbf E$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\alpha_o=0.}
$$

Its [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) path is also normal to the faces. The [extraordinary wave](../../../../extraordinary-wave.md) differs because $\mathbf D\perp\mathbf k$ but $\mathbf E$ need not be perpendicular to $\mathbf k$. In a nonmagnetic crystal, $\mathbf H\parallel\mathbf B$ and $\mathbf S=\mathbf E\times\mathbf H$ is perpendicular to $\mathbf E$. The vectors $\mathbf D,\mathbf E,\mathbf k,\mathbf S$ lie in the same meridional plane, with $\mathbf D\perp\mathbf k$ and $\mathbf E\perp\mathbf S$. Therefore the [electric-displacement angle from birefringent walk-off](../../../../electric-displacement-angle-from-birefringent-walk-off.md) equals the angular separation between $\mathbf S$ and $\mathbf k$.

The observed lateral separation is accumulated over thickness $L$, so $\Delta=L\tan\alpha_e$. With $L=10\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $\Delta=0.1\,\mathrm{mm}$,

$$
\boxed{\alpha_e=\arctan(0.1/10)=\arctan(0.010)\simeq0.0100\,\mathrm{rad}=0.573^\circ.}
$$

The two emergent rays are parallel because their outgoing wavevectors are both normal to the faces in air; their internal [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) paths can nevertheless be displaced by [birefringent walk-off](../../../../birefringent-walk-off.md). The angle quoted is the positive angle between the field vectors, so the unspecified lateral direction does not affect its value. The [unpolarized light](../../../../unpolarized-light.md) excites both eigenpolarizations; it does not require their [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) paths to coincide.
