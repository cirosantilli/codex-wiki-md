<h1 id="section-b/b3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B3](../b3.md)

Use the ideal electrically short [Hertzian dipole](../../../../hertzian-dipole.md), $d\ll\lambda$, with negligible ohmic loss. Far-field statements additionally require an observation distance large compared with the [wavelength](../../../../wavelength.md) and source dimensions. These assumptions distinguish its radiation from near-field coupling and from scattering by a general extended antenna structure.
