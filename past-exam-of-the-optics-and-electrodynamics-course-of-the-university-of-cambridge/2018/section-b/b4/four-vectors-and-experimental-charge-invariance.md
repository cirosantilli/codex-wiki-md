<h1 id="section-b/b4/four-vectors-and-experimental-charge-invariance">Four-vectors and experimental charge invariance</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Solution](solution.md)

A [Lorentz four-vector](../../../../four-vector.md) is a set of four components transforming as $A'^\mu=\Lambda^\mu{}_{\nu}A^\nu$ under a [Lorentz transformation](../../../../lorentz-transformation.md). Examples include the four-position and four-velocity. A [Lorentz invariant](../../../../lorentz-invariance.md) is a quantity unchanged by that transformation. With metric signature $(+---)$, the scalar product $A_\mu B^\mu=A^0B^0-\mathbf A\cdot\mathbf B$ is invariant. The components of a four-vector are generally not individually invariant.

The proton's total [electric charge](../../../../electric-charge-split.md) is taken to be a scalar: it does not acquire a factor $\gamma$ when the proton moves. Experimental evidence comes from sensitive [charge neutrality](../../../../charge-neutrality.md) tests of matter. Hydrogen molecules and helium atoms remain neutral despite different binding and internal [electron](../../../../electron.md) and nuclear motions. If constituent charge scaled with kinetic [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) as relativistic mass does, the same cancellation of fixed numbers of proton and [electron](../../../../electron.md) charges would generally fail in different bound systems.

In a [gas-efflux test of charge neutrality](../../../../gas-efflux-test-of-charge-neutrality.md), gas escapes from an electrically isolated vessel connected to an [electrometer](../../../../electrometer.md). Any net charge per escaping molecule would leave a measurable opposite vessel charge, after residual ions and charged contaminants are removed. The hydrogen/helium efflux experiment supports velocity-independent additive charge. [Search for a Small Charge Carried by Molecules (1960), hydrogen and helium efflux measurements](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.5.562)

This is evidence for [charge invariance](../../../../charge-invariance.md), not merely [conservation of electric charge](../../../../charge-conservation.md): the latter concerns constancy during a process, while invariance compares descriptions or internal motions without rescaling charge by a boost factor. Net-matter neutrality constrains combinations of constituent charges; it should not be described as an exact proof covering every possible velocity-dependent microscopic model.
