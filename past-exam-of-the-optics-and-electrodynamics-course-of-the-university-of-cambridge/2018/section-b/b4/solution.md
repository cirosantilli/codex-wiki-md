↑ **Parent:** [B4](../b4.md)

Use a boost along the wire axis when comparing frames. The current variable in a [four-current](../../../../four-current.md) must be [current density](../../../../current-density.md), in $\mathrm{A\,m^{-2}}$, rather than a scalar total current in amperes. For the wire-force calculation it is convenient to integrate charge and current over each cross-section and work with line quantities.
