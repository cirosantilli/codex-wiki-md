<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Using the needle definitions in the question-level solution, $\mathbf u\cdot\mathbf e=u_z=\cos\theta$. The scattered field is parallel to $\mathbf v=u_x\hat{\mathbf x}+u_z\hat{\mathbf z}$, and

$$
\boxed{\frac{I_a}{KI_0}=u_z^2(1-u_y^2)=\cos^2\theta\left(1-\sin^2\theta\sin^2\phi\right).}
$$

The light is **linearly polarized** along the needle's projection into the $xz$ observation plane. Its [Jones vector](../../../../../jones-vector.md) in the $(x,z)$ basis is $(u_x,u_z)^{\mathsf T}/\sqrt{1-u_y^2}$ whenever the intensity is nonzero. A needle perpendicular to the incident [electric field](../../../../../electric-field-split.md), or pointing directly at the observer, has zero leading-order signal.
