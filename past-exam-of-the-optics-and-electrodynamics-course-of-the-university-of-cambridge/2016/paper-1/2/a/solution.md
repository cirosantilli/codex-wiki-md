**[Birefringence](../../../../../birefringence.md) is polarization-dependent propagation in an optically anisotropic medium.** Its [permittivity tensor](../../../../../permittivity-tensor.md) relates $\mathbf D$ to $\mathbf E$. In a lossless uniaxial [dielectric](../../../../../dielectric.md), take the [optic axis](../../../../../optic-axis.md) along $z$ and the principal relative permittivities as $(n_o^2,n_o^2,n_e^2)$. For a [wave vector](../../../../../wavevector.md) making angle $\vartheta$ with that axis, the ordinary mode has index $n_o$, while the [extraordinary phase-index angular dependence](../../../../../extraordinary-phase-index-angular-dependence.md) is

$$
\boxed{\frac1{n_{\rm ex}(\vartheta)^2}=\frac{\cos^2\vartheta}{n_o^2}+\frac{\sin^2\vartheta}{n_e^2}.}
$$

The ordinary [electric field](../../../../../electric-field-split.md) is perpendicular to the plane containing the [wave vector](../../../../../wavevector.md) and [optic axis](../../../../../optic-axis.md). The extraordinary [electric displacement field](../../../../../electric-displacement-field.md) lies in that plane and is perpendicular to the [wave vector](../../../../../wavevector.md), but its [electric field](../../../../../electric-field-split.md) need not be. Its energy-flow direction can therefore differ from the wave-normal direction: [birefringent walk-off](../../../../../birefringent-walk-off.md). Along the [optic axis](../../../../../optic-axis.md) the two indices coincide. [Refraction](../../../../../refraction.md) generally splits an incident beam into two differently polarized rays, producing double images, as in calcite.

A [waveplate](../../../../../waveplate.md) instead uses the relative phase of two eigenpolarizations. With the axes chosen for the propagation direction, the [retardance](../../../../../retardance.md) is $\Delta=2\pi L(n_2-n_1)/\lambda_0$. A [quarter-wave plate](../../../../../quarter-wave-plate.md) gives $|\Delta|=\pi/2$ modulo $2\pi$ and converts equal-amplitude orthogonal components between linear and [circular polarization](../../../../../circular-polarization.md). A [half-wave plate](../../../../../half-wave-plate.md) gives $|\Delta|=\pi$ and rotates a [linear polarization](../../../../../linear-polarization.md). This is a phase effect; unequal absorption is a separate property.[Creating Different Polarisation States; wave plates and relative phase](../../../../../polarisation-by-sander-konijnenberg-aurele-j-l-adam-and-h-paul-urbach.md)
