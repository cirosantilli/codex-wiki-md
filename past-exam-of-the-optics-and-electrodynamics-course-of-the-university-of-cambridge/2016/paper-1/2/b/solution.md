**[Synchrotron radiation](../../../../../synchrotron-radiation.md) is radiation from relativistic charges undergoing transverse [acceleration](../../../../../acceleration.md)**, for example electrons bent by a [magnetic field](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md). [Relativistic aberration](../../../../../relativistic-aberration.md) concentrates the radiation within an angle of order $1/\gamma$ about the instantaneous velocity. An observer sees short pulses as this cone sweeps past; [synchrotron pulse compression](../../../../../synchrotron-pulse-compression.md) produces a broad spectrum extending far above the orbital [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md).

For an ultrarelativistic circular trajectory of curvature radius $\rho$, the characteristic scales are

$$
\boxed{\omega_c\simeq\frac{3\gamma^3c}{2\rho},\qquad\Delta t_{\rm pulse}\sim\frac{\rho}{\gamma^3c}.}
$$

[Critical energy and critical mode number](../../../../../synchrotron-radiation-in-the-geant4-physics-reference-manual-by-geant4-collaboration.md) The [synchrotron critical frequency](../../../../../synchrotron-critical-frequency.md) sets the spectral turnover; it is not a sharp upper cutoff. The transverse-acceleration [Lienard formula](../../../../../lienard-radiated-power-formula.md) gives

$$
P=\frac{q^2\gamma^4v^4}{6\pi\epsilon_0c^3\rho^2}\simeq\frac{q^2c\gamma^4}{6\pi\epsilon_0\rho^2}.
$$

The strong energy dependence is a major loss mechanism in electron storage rings. Radiation observed in the orbital plane is linearly polarized in that plane; away from it the two components generally produce [elliptical polarization](../../../../../elliptical-polarization.md), with opposite handedness on opposite sides. Accelerator light sources exploit its brightness, broad tunable spectrum and pulsed timing. At low speeds the relativistic beaming and high-frequency extension disappear, approaching ordinary cyclotron radiation.[Relativistic radiation cone, pulse compression and synchrotron spectrum](../../../../../synchrotron-radiation-by-richard-fitzpatrick.md)
