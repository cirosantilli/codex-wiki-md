**The transverse [spatial coherence](../../../../../spatial-optical-coherence.md) width is much larger than the longitudinal [coherence length](../../../../../optical-coherence-length.md).** The longitudinal scale is controlled by spectral [bandwidth](../../../../../bandwidth-signal-processing.md):

$$
\ell_c\sim\frac{c}{\Delta\nu}\sim\frac{\lambda^2}{\Delta\lambda}.
$$

For broad, unfiltered visible sunlight it is of order a [wavelength](../../../../../wavelength.md) to a few wavelengths, roughly $10^{-6}\,\mathrm m$. This is an order-of-magnitude scale, not a uniquely defined solar constant; the precise value depends on spectral response and the coherence-width convention.

The [Sun](../../../../../sun.md) subtends angular diameter $\delta\simeq1919''\simeq9.3\times10^{-3}\,\mathrm{rad}$ at one astronomical unit.[Sun Observational Parameters: apparent diameter at 1 A.U.](../../../../../sun-fact-sheet-by-national-aeronautics-and-space-administration.md) Consequently the [sunlight coherence comparison](../../../../../sunlight-coherence-comparison.md) gives a transverse scale

$$
\boxed{w_c\sim\frac{\lambda}{\delta}\sim 6\times10^{-5}\,\mathrm m\gg\ell_c,}
$$

using $\lambda=550\,\mathrm{nm}$ only as an illustrative visible [wavelength](../../../../../wavelength.md). The [uniform-disk spatial coherence](../../../../../uniform-disk-spatial-coherence.md) model has first coherence zero at $1.22\lambda/\delta$, about $72\,\mathrm{\mu m}$ at that [wavelength](../../../../../wavelength.md). The small angular size permits coherence across tens of micrometres, while the broad spectrum destroys coherence over much shorter path differences. Spectral filtering increases $\ell_c$; spatial filtering changes the transverse scale.[Coherence of Light Sources; More on Spatial Coherence](../../../../../interference-and-coherence-by-sander-konijnenberg-aurele-j-l-adam-and-h-paul-urbach.md)
