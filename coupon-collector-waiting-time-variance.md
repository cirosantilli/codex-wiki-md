# Coupon collector waiting-time variance

↑ **Parent:** [Coupon collector problem](coupon-collector-problem.md)

For uniform independent draws of $n$ coupon types, the waiting times with $r$ unseen types remaining are independent positive-support [geometric distributions](geometric-distribution.md) of parameter $r/n$. Summing their means gives $n\sum_{r=1}^n1/r$, and summing their variances gives the displayed formula. [Independence](independent-random-variables.md) follows from fresh draws after each discovery and the fact that the next stage's conditional distribution depends only on the count of remaining types.
