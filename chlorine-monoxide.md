# Chlorine monoxide

↑ **Parent:** [Radical (chemistry)](radical-chemistry.md)

The [radical](radical-chemistry.md) $\mathrm{ClO}$ formed when a [chlorine](chlorine.md) atom reacts with [ozone](ozone.md). Its reaction partners and reservoirs depend on altitude and [temperature](temperature-split.md); the [chlorine monoxide dimer cycle](chlorine-monoxide-dimer-cycle.md) is important under cold, activated polar conditions.
