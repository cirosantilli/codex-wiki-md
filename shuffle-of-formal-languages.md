# Shuffle of formal languages

↑ **Parent:** [Formal language theory](formal-language-theory.md)

The shuffle of two [formal languages](formal-language.md) consists of [words](string.md) whose positions can be partitioned into two subsequences, each preserving its order, with the two resulting [words](string.md) belonging to the respective languages. For [regular languages](regular-language.md), a product-state [nondeterministic finite automaton](nondeterministic-finite-automaton.md) advances exactly one coordinate for each letter. Common letters allow either choice; the [powerset construction](powerset-construction.md) proves closure.
