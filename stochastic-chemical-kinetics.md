# Stochastic chemical kinetics

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical reaction network](chemical-reaction-network.md)

Stochastic chemical kinetics models molecular copy numbers as a [continuous-time Markov chain](continuous-time-markov-chain.md). Each reaction has a state-dependent [reaction propensity function](reaction-propensity-function.md) and changes the state by its [stoichiometric vector](stoichiometric-vector.md) when it fires.
