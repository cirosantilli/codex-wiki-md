# Lederberg replica plating experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Replica plating](replica-plating.md)

[Joshua Lederberg](joshua-lederberg.md) and [Esther Lederberg](esther-lederberg.md) reported [replica plating](replica-plating.md) in 1952. They [autoclave](autoclave.md)-sterilized velveteen and secured it pile-up on a wood or cork cylinder with a metal hoop. Inverted [agar](agar.md) plates touched the fabric lightly. Pencil marks or pin indentations fixed orientation. Complete-medium and minimal-medium replicas compared growth of [biological colonies](colony-biology.md).[pp. 399–402, Methods: Replica plating; Figure 1 caption; Results: plate orientation](replica-plating-and-indirect-selection-of-bacterial-mutants-by-joshua-lederberg-and-esther-lederberg.md)

[Escherichia coli](escherichia-coli.md) W-1 grew on plain [agar](agar.md); serial T-1 [bacteriophage](bacteriophage.md) replicas showed resistant [biological colonies](colony-biology.md) at matching sites. Corresponding unexposed master regions seeded repeated broth-and-plain-[agar](agar.md) enrichment, yielding resistant isolates. Stray-[bacteriophage](bacteriophage.md) checks were negative; resistance persisted without challenge. This supported preexisting heritable resistance. The authors noted variable transfer and moisture-dependent resolution.[pp. 401–403: transfer variability, Clonal occurrence of phage resistant mutants, Indirect selection and stray-phage checks; p. 404, Discussion](replica-plating-and-indirect-selection-of-bacterial-mutants-by-joshua-lederberg-and-esther-lederberg.md)

The [Luria-Delbrück experiment](luria-delbruck-experiment.md) uses variation between independently grown [cell cultures](cell-culture.md) to investigate the timing of resistant [mutations](mutation.md). The [Lederberg replica plating experiment](lederberg-replica-plating-experiment.md) adds a spatial route to a recoverable source: the tested samples identify an unexposed lineage. These are complementary arguments about [mutation](mutation.md) and [natural selection](natural-selection.md). Growth readouts establish a tested [phenotype](phenotype.md); identifying the responsible [nucleotide sequence](nucleic-acid-sequence.md) requires other evidence, such as [DNA sequencing](dna-sequencing.md).
