# Norm detects proper closed subsets under finite normalization

↑ **Parent:** [Noether normalization](noether-normalization.md)

For a finite integral inclusion $A=k[t_1,\ldots,t_r]\subset B$ of domains, a nonzero $b\in B$ has a monic [minimal polynomial](minimal-polynomial.md) over the fraction field with nonzero constant term $a_0\in A$. Its equation gives $a_0\in bB\cap A$. Consequently the image of $V_B(b)$ under the [finite morphism](finite-morphism.md) to $\mathbb A^r$ is contained in the proper [Zariski-closed set](zariski-closed-set.md) $V_A(a_0)$. Finite morphisms preserve the dimension of a closed subvariety and its image, so this proves dimension drops for proper closed subsets after [Noether normalization](noether-normalization.md). A power of $a_0$, up to sign, is the [field norm](field-norm.md) of $b$.
