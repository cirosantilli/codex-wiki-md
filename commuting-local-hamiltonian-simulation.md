# Commuting local Hamiltonian simulation

↑ **Parent:** [Hamiltonian simulation](hamiltonian-simulation.md)

If $H=\sum_jh_j$ and all local terms commute, then

$$
e^{-itH}=\prod_je^{-ith_j}
$$

exactly. Approximating each fixed-size factor to error at most $\varepsilon/m$ and applying the [telescoping bound for products of operators](telescoping-bound-for-products-of-operators.md) gives total [operator norm](operator-norm.md) error at most $\varepsilon$.
