# Two-section criterion for holomorphic triviality

↑ **Parent:** [Holomorphic line bundle](holomorphic-line-bundle.md)

On a compact connected [complex manifold](complex-manifold.md), pair nonzero [holomorphic sections](holomorphic-section.md) of a [holomorphic line bundle](holomorphic-line-bundle.md) and its [holomorphic dual vector bundle](holomorphic-dual-vector-bundle.md). Their product is a global [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function.md), hence constant by the [maximum modulus principle](maximum-modulus-principle.md). The [identity theorem](identity-theorem.md) makes their nonvanishing loci dense, so this constant is nonzero. Both sections are consequently nowhere zero and trivialize their bundles. Conversely, the constant section of a trivial bundle and its dual provide the two sections.
