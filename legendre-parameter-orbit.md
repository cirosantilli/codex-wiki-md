# Legendre parameter orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Legendre form of an elliptic curve](legendre-form-of-an-elliptic-curve.md)

For $Y^2Z=X(X-Z)(X-\lambda Z)$ over $\mathbb C$, order its three finite roots as $(r_0,r_1,r_2)$ and put $h=r_1-r_0$. The [projective transformation](projective-linear-transformation.md) $X'=(X-r_0Z)/h$, $Y'=Y/s$, $Z'=Z$, with $s^2=h^3$, gives [Legendre form of an elliptic curve](legendre-form-of-an-elliptic-curve.md) with parameter $(r_2-r_0)/(r_1-r_0)$. The six root orderings give the six displayed values, all different from zero and one. These exhaust changes that act affinely on $X/Z$ and scale $Y/Z$, because such a change must send the finite root set onto the new finite root set. There are six distinct values except that $\lambda\in\{-1,2,1/2\}$ gives three and $\lambda^2-\lambda+1=0$ gives two.
