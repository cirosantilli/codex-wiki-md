# Edwards-Sokal coupling

↑ **Parent:** [Random-cluster model](random-cluster-model.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edwards-Sokal_coupling)

The Edwards-Sokal coupling assigns one common random spin to every random-cluster component. For $q=2$ it couples the random-cluster model to the [Ising model](ising-model.md).
