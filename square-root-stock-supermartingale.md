# Square-root stock supermartingale

↑ **Parent:** [Supermartingale](supermartingale.md)

For a positive stock satisfying $dS=S\sigma\,dW$, the [Itô formula](ito-s-lemma.md) gives $d\sqrt S=\tfrac12\sqrt S\sigma dW-\tfrac18\sqrt S\sigma^2dt$. Localization and the conditional [Fatou lemma](fatou-s-lemma.md) make this nonnegative local supermartingale a true [supermartingale](supermartingale.md).
