# Photopion threshold on a photon background

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmic ray](cosmic-ray.md)

For an ultrarelativistic [proton](proton.md) of [energy](energy-split.md) $E$ colliding head-on with a [photon](photon.md) of [energy](energy-split.md) $\epsilon$, the invariant squared collision [energy](energy-split.md) is approximately $(m_pc^2)^2+4E\epsilon$. Producing a final [proton](proton.md) and [neutral pion](neutral-pion.md) requires at least $(m_pc^2+m_\pi c^2)^2$. Thus $E_{\rm th}=m_\pi c^2(2m_pc^2+m_\pi c^2)/(4\epsilon)$. A broad isotropic [photon](photon.md) background turns this threshold into an energy-loss feature depending on the [photon](photon.md) distribution and source distance, not an absolute ceiling for all arriving [protons](proton.md).
