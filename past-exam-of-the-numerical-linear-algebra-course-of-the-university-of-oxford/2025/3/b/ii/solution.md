<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For any nonzero [vector](../../../../../vector.md) $y\in\mathbb R^k$, [orthonormality](../../../../../orthonormal-set.md) gives $\|Q_ky\|_2=\|y\|_2>0$. Since $A$ is a [symmetric positive-definite matrix](../../../../../symmetric-positive-definite-matrix.md),

$$
\boxed{y^TT_ky=(Q_ky)^TA(Q_ky)>0}.
$$

The preceding part already gives symmetry, so $T_k$ is a [symmetric positive-definite matrix](../../../../../symmetric-positive-definite-matrix.md). In fact the [spectral theorem](../../../../../spectral-theorem.md) gives the useful bounds

$$
\lambda_{\min}(A)\|y\|_2^2
\leq y^TT_ky\leq\lambda_{\max}(A)\|y\|_2^2,
$$

which place its [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) between the smallest and largest [eigenvalues](../../../../../eigenvalue.md) of $A$.
