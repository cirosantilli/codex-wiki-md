<h1 id="2/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Choose the first $r$ [left singular vectors](../../../../../left-singular-vector.md):

$$
\boxed{Q_*=[u_1,\ldots,u_r]}.
$$

Their [orthonormality](../../../../../orthonormal-set.md) gives $Q_*^TQ_*=I_r$. The [orthogonal projection](../../../../../orthogonal-projection.md) $Q_*Q_*^T$ fixes $u_j$ for $j\leq r$ and annihilates $u_j$ for $j>r$. Applying it to the [singular value decomposition](../../../../../singular-value-decomposition.md) therefore gives

$$
Q_*Q_*^TA=\sum_{j=1}^r\sigma_j u_jv_j^T=A_r.
$$
