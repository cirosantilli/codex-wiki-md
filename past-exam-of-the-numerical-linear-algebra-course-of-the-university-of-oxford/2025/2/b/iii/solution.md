<h1 id="2/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The thin [QR decomposition](../../../../../qr-decomposition.md) gives $AX=QR$. Its columns already belong to the [column space](../../../../../column-space.md) of $Q$, so the [orthogonal projection](../../../../../orthogonal-projection.md) $QQ^T$ leaves them fixed:

$$
\boxed{\widehat AX=QQ^TAX=QQ^TQR=QR=AX}.
$$

This identity uses only $AX=QR$ and $Q^TQ=I_r$; it remains valid if $R$ is singular, provided such a factorization with [orthonormal](../../../../../orthonormal-set.md) $Q$ is used.
