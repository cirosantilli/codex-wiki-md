<h1 id="1/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Using the [orthonormal](../../../../../orthonormal-set.md) columns of $U_1,U_2$ in the factorization gives

$$
I_n=A^TA=V(\Sigma_1^2+\Sigma_2^2)V^T.
$$

Multiplying by the [orthogonal matrices](../../../../../orthogonal-matrix.md) $V^T$ and $V$ gives

$$
\boxed{\Sigma_1^2+\Sigma_2^2=I_n}.
$$
