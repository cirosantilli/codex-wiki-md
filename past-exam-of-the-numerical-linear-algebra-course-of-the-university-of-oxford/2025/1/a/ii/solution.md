<h1 id="1/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For every [vector](../../../../../vector.md) $z\in\mathbb R^n$, splitting the rows gives

$$
\|A_1z\|_2^2+\|A_2z\|_2^2=\|Az\|_2^2=z^TA^TAz=\|z\|_2^2.
$$

Both summands are nonnegative. Taking the supremum over [unit vectors](../../../../../unit-vector.md) in the definition of the [spectral norm](../../../../../matrix-2-norm.md) proves

$$
\boxed{\|A_1\|_2\leq1,\qquad \|A_2\|_2\leq1}.
$$
