<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [induced matrix norm](../../../../induced-matrix-norm.md) associated with the given [energy norm](../../../../energy-norm.md) is a supremum of length ratios. The invertible [change of variables](../../../../change-of-variables-formula.md) $y=A^{1/2}x$ gives

$$
\|B\|_A=\sup_{x\ne0}\frac{\|Bx\|_A}{\|x\|_A}
=\sup_{y\ne0}\frac{\|A^{1/2}BA^{-1/2}y\|_2}{\|y\|_2}
=\boxed{\|A^{1/2}BA^{-1/2}\|_2}.
$$

The last expression is the Euclidean [operator norm](../../../../operator-norm.md). This is the [matrix norm induced by an energy norm](../../../../matrix-norm-induced-by-an-energy-norm.md): transforming to coordinates with Euclidean lengths transforms the operator by the corresponding [matrix similarity](../../../../matrix-similarity.md).
