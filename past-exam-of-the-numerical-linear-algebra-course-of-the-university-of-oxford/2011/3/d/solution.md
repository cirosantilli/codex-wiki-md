<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Since $Ap_1=(5,2)^T$, an [A-conjugate direction](../../../../conjugate-direction.md) $p_2=(a,b)^T$ must satisfy $5a+2b=0$. One nonzero choice is

$$
\boxed{p_2=(2,-5)^T.}
$$

Direct [matrix multiplication](../../../../matrix-multiplication.md) gives $Ap_2=(3,-3)^T$, and $p_1^TAp_2=0$. All nonzero scalar multiples of this [vector](../../../../vector.md) also qualify.
