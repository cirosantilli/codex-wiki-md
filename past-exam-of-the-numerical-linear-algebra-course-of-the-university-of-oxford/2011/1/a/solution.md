<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A real [singular value decomposition](../../../../singular-value-decomposition.md) has the form $A=U\Sigma V^T$, where $U\in\mathbb R^{m\times m}$ and $V\in\mathbb R^{n\times n}$ are [orthogonal matrices](../../../../orthogonal-matrix.md), and $\Sigma$ is a rectangular [diagonal matrix](../../../../diagonal-matrix.md) with entries $\sigma_1\geq\cdots\geq\sigma_{\min(m,n)}\geq0$. These entries are the [singular values](../../../../singular-value.md). Their squares are the [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md) of $A^TA$, omitting the additional zero [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md) when $n>m$. The positive [singular values](../../../../singular-value.md) number exactly the [matrix rank](../../../../matrix-rank.md).
