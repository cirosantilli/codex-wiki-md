# 2025

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the numerical linear algebra course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Solutions to all three questions of the [University of Oxford](../university-of-oxford-split.md) [numerical linear algebra](../numerical-linear-algebra-split.md) paper, sat on 2 June 2025. Source: [released examination PDF, parent PDF pages 1–3](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/past_papers_omms/response/3521346/attach/3/FOI%20202607%20785%20Exam%20Papers%20OMMS.pdf#page=1). The question statements are omitted. The Gaussian-matrix estimates in parts 2(b)(v) and 3(b)(iv) are used as the paper explicitly permits; their limitations are explained in those solutions.
