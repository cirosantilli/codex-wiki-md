# Uranium-239

↑ **Parent:** [Uranium](uranium.md)

An unstable [uranium](uranium.md) [isotope](isotope.md) produced by [neutron capture](neutron-capture.md) on [uranium-238](uranium-238.md). Two successive [beta-minus decays](beta-minus-decay.md), through [neptunium-239](neptunium-239.md), lead to [plutonium-239](plutonium-239.md).
