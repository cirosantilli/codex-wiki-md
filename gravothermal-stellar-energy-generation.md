# Gravothermal stellar energy generation

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar energy balance equation](stellar-energy-balance-equation.md)

This term accounts for thermal storage and compression or expansion in the [stellar energy balance equation](stellar-energy-balance-equation.md). At fixed composition the [first law of thermodynamics](first-law-of-thermodynamics.md) makes it $-TDS/Dt$. [Kelvin-Helmholtz contraction](kelvin-helmholtz-mechanism.md) can supply positive [luminosity](luminosity.md), while thermal adjustment may locally absorb energy. It is not simply the full change in gravitational potential energy: the [stellar virial theorem](stellar-virial-theorem.md) also requires changes in internal energy.
