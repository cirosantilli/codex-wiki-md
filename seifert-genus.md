# Seifert genus

↑ **Parent:** [Seifert surface](seifert-surface.md)

The Seifert genus is the smallest genus of a [Seifert surface](seifert-surface.md) for the knot.

It minimizes the genus over all choices of [Seifert surface](seifert-surface.md).
