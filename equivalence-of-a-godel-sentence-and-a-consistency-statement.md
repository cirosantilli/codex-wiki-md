<h1 id="equivalence-of-a-godel-sentence-and-a-consistency-statement">Equivalence of a Gödel sentence and a consistency statement</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Gödel sentence](godel-sentence.md)

For a [Gödel sentence](godel-sentence.md) $G\leftrightarrow\neg\Box G$ with the [Hilbert-Bernays-Löb derivability conditions](hilbert-bernays-lob-derivability-conditions.md), fix a refutable contradiction sentence $\bot$. Since $\bot\to G$ is provable, $\Box\bot\to\Box G$, giving $G\to\neg\Box\bot$. For the reverse direction, $\Box G$ implies both $\Box\Box G$ and $\Box\neg\Box G$, hence $\Box\bot$. Contraposition gives $\neg\Box\bot\to\neg\Box G\to G$. This is an internal equivalence of sentences, not a rule asserting the theory's own consistency.
