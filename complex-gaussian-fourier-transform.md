# Complex Gaussian Fourier transform

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier transform of a Gaussian](fourier-transform-of-a-gaussian.md)

With kernel $e^{-2\pi i\langle u,x\rangle}$ and $\operatorname{Im}\tau>0$, the [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) of $e^{\pi i\tau|x|^2}$ is $(-i\tau)^{-n/2}e^{-\pi i|u|^2/\tau}$. Choose the logarithm of $-i\tau$ on the right half-plane. The ordinary real [Gaussian Fourier transform](fourier-transform-of-a-gaussian.md) proves the identity at $\tau=it$; holomorphic continuation proves the complex formula.
