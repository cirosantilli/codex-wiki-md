# Knudsen number

↑ **Parent:** [Kinetic theory](kinetic-theory.md)

The ratio of the [mean free path](mean-free-path.md) to a macroscopic length compares collision and transport scales. Small Knudsen number motivates the [hydrodynamic limit of the Boltzmann equation](hydrodynamic-limit-of-the-boltzmann-equation.md); large values favor a direct kinetic description. The scaling of time, velocity and space must also be stated when using it as a coefficient in a kinetic equation.
