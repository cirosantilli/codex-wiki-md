# Ion-acoustic wave

↑ **Parent:** [Plasma oscillation](plasma-oscillation.md)

A low-[frequency](frequency.md) electrostatic [plasma](plasma-physics.md) wave with ion inertia and a pressure restoring force, usually dominated by electron [pressure](pressure-split.md). Weak ion [Landau damping](landau-damping.md) requires the [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md) to exceed typical ion [speeds](speed.md). Electron [Landau damping](landau-damping.md) depends on the electron parallel [marginal distribution](marginal-distribution.md) and need not vanish even when ion damping is absent.
