# Ville inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Martingale](martingale-split.md)

For a nonnegative [martingale](martingale-split.md), the displayed maximal probability bound follows by stopping at the first crossing of $a$. The stopped expectation is at most its initial expectation, and on the crossing event its value is at least $a$. Bound the stopping time first and pass to the limit using nonnegativity. This form is useful for the [Poisson maximal concentration bound](poisson-maximal-concentration-bound.md).
