# Conjugate acid-base pair

↑ **Parent:** [Conjugate base](conjugate-base.md)

A [conjugate acid-base pair](conjugate-acid-base-pair.md) consists of two [chemical species](chemical-species.md) related by transfer of one [proton](proton.md). The [conjugate acid](conjugate-acid.md) has one more [proton](proton.md) and one more positive unit of [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) than the [conjugate base](conjugate-base.md).
