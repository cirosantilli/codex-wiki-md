# Minkowski interval

↑ **Parent:** [Minkowski metric](minkowski-metric.md)

The squared interval between two events in [Minkowski spacetime](minkowski-spacetime.md) is the quadratic form of their coordinate difference under the [Minkowski metric](minkowski-metric.md). In signature $(-,+,+,+)$ it is $-c^2\Delta t^2+|\Delta\mathbf x|^2$. [Lorentz transformations](lorentz-transformation.md) preserve this quadratic form; timelike, spacelike and null separation correspond to negative, positive and zero values.

The opposite [metric signature](metric-signature.md) $(+,-,-,-)$ reverses the sign of the [Minkowski interval](minkowski-interval.md), giving $c^2\Delta t^2-|\Delta\mathbf x|^2$; the physical classification and [proper time](proper-time.md) stay the same. State the convention before using a sign to identify a separation. With the mostly-plus convention here, a straight clock segment has $s^2=-c^2\Delta\tau^2$, while a light signal has $s^2=0$. [Richard Fitzpatrick, University of Texas: Spacetime Interval, using the opposite sign convention](https://farside.ph.utexas.edu/teaching/355/Surveyhtml/node136.html).
