# Reduced Hessian

↑ **Parent:** [Optimization Lagrangian](optimization-lagrangian.md)

For equality-constrained optimization, restrict the [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md) $H$ of the [optimization Lagrangian](optimization-lagrangian.md) to feasible tangent directions. If the columns of $Z$ form a [basis](basis.md) of the nullspace of the constraint [Jacobian matrix](jacobian-matrix.md) $A$, the resulting quadratic form has matrix $Z^THZ$. Positive definiteness gives positive quadratic curvature in every nonzero tangent direction. Nonsingularity of this reduced matrix, together with full row rank of $A$, characterizes nonsingularity of the corresponding equality-constrained [saddle-point matrix](saddle-point-matrix.md).
