<h1 id="representations-of-so-4-from-two-su2-spins">Representations of SO(4) from two SU2 spins</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [SO(4) group](so-4-group.md)

Tensor products $V_{j_L}\otimes V_{j_R}$ give the [irreducible representations](irreducible-representation.md) of the [Spin(4) double cover](spin-4-double-cover.md). The simultaneous central sign acts by $(-1)^{2j_L+2j_R}$, so exactly the integer-sum pairs descend to [SO(4)](so-4-group.md). Their dimensions are $(2j_L+1)(2j_R+1)$. Under the diagonal spatial rotation subgroup, the [Clebsch-Gordan decomposition for SU2](clebsch-gordan-decomposition-for-su2.md) gives spins from $|j_L-j_R|$ through $j_L+j_R$ in steps of one.
