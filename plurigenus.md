# Plurigenus

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical divisor of a smooth variety](canonical-divisor-of-a-smooth-variety.md)

For a smooth [projective variety](projective-variety.md), its $m$th plurigenus is $P_m(X)=h^0(X,\omega_X^{\otimes m})$, for $m\ge1$. These dimensions describe pluricanonical linear systems. [Blowup invariance of plurigenera](blowup-invariance-of-plurigenera.md) proves their invariance under a point blowup on a smooth surface.
