# Polychromatic hypergraph colouring

↑ **Parent:** [Hypergraph](hypergraph-split.md)

A colouring is polychromatic with $r$ colours if every [hypergraph edge](edge-of-a-hypergraph.md) contains a vertex of every colour. This is stronger than requiring every edge to be nonmonochromatic. Under independent uniform colours, a $k$-element edge misses at least one of three colours with probability $3(2/3)^k-3(1/3)^k$. If each edge meets at most $m$ other edges, the edge-intersection [dependency graph of events](dependency-graph-of-events.md) and the [Lovász local lemma](lovasz-local-lemma.md) therefore give a polychromatic three-colouring whenever $e(m+1)[3(2/3)^k-3(1/3)^k]\leq1$.
