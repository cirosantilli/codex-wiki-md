# Cis-regulatory evolution

↑ **Parent:** [Gene expression](gene-expression.md)

Evolutionary change in [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) sequences that regulate [gene expression](gene-expression.md) on the same molecule. A tissue-specific [enhancer](enhancer.md) can change one deployment of a [gene](gene.md) while preserving others, although regulatory changes can themselves have [pleiotropy](pleiotropy.md).
