<h1 id="two-triangle-disc-and-mobius-band-model-of-the-projective-plane">Two-triangle disc and Möbius-band model of the projective plane</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Real projective plane](real-projective-plane.md)

In a triangle whose two sides at an apex are glued with that apex fixed, cross-sections become circles and the apex becomes their [cone](cone-geometry.md) point. This [quotient](quotient.md) is a [disc](topological-disc.md). A second triangle with boundary word $aa b^{-1}$ becomes a [Möbius band](mobius-band.md) after the $a$ sides are identified. Gluing the remaining $b$ boundary to the first [disc](topological-disc.md) caps the band and gives the [real projective plane](real-projective-plane.md). The resulting two-face cell structure has two vertices, three edges and [Euler characteristic](euler-characteristic.md) one.
