# Initial acceleration of three stacked cylinders

↑ **Parent:** [Rolling without slipping](rolling-without-slipping.md)

Three identical cylinders initially form an equilateral triangle on a horizontal floor and are released from rest. If each has [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) $\beta mR^2$, the initial top downward [acceleration](acceleration.md) is $g/(7+6\beta)$ for no-slip rough ground and smooth cylinder contacts, or $g/(7+8\beta)$ for smooth ground and no-slip rough cylinder contacts. In both cases the lower cylinders separate with outward [acceleration](acceleration.md) $u=\sqrt3a_{\mathrm{top}}$. The corresponding lower-cylinder [angular acceleration](angular-acceleration.md) magnitudes are $u/R$ and $2a_{\mathrm{top}}/R$, with opposite rotation senses between the cases. Sufficient [static friction](static-friction.md) is required; a finite coefficient allowing sliding changes the problem.
