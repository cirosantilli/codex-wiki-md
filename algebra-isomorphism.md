# Algebra isomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Algebra over a field](algebra-over-a-field.md)

An algebra isomorphism is a bijective [linear map](linear-map.md) preserving multiplication and identity between two [algebras](algebra-split.md) over the same [field](field.md). In classification of a [square-zero extension of an algebra](square-zero-extension-of-an-algebra.md), an equivalence must additionally induce the identity on the prescribed quotient and kernel.
