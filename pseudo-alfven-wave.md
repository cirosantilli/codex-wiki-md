<h1 id="pseudo-alfven-wave">Pseudo-Alfvén wave</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [High-pressure limit of magnetosonic waves](high-pressure-limit-of-magnetosonic-waves.md)

The high-[pressure](pressure.md) limit of the [slow magnetosonic wave](slow-magnetosonic-wave.md) approaches an incompressible polarization in the plane of the background [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) and [wavevector](wavevector.md), perpendicular to the latter. It has the displayed leading [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation.md), the same as the out-of-plane [Alfvén wave](alfven-wave.md). [Magnetic tension](magnetic-tension.md) is the restoring force, and [pressure](pressure.md) balances the compressive component of the magnetic force. In exactly incompressible homogeneous [ideal magnetohydrodynamics](ideal-magnetohydrodynamics.md) it is one of the two transverse magnetic polarizations.
