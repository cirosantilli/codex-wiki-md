# Boundary-forced internal gravity wave

↑ **Parent:** [Internal wave](internal-wave.md)

A moving boundary excites an [internal gravity wave](internal-wave.md) through its [kinematic boundary condition](kinematic-boundary-condition.md). For [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) $k$ and [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) $\omega$, the vertical [velocity field](velocity-field.md) satisfies $W''+k^2(N^2/\omega^2-1)W=0$. The [radiation condition](radiation-condition.md) selects outward [group velocity](group-velocity.md); when $\omega>N$, the bounded response is an [evanescent wave](evanescent-wave.md).
