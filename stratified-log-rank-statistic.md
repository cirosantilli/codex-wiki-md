# Stratified log-rank statistic

↑ **Parent:** [Log-rank statistic](log-rank-statistic.md)

Compute observed-minus-expected event contributions within each stratum's [risk set](risk-set.md), then add them across independent strata. Under the null, the corresponding conditional [variances](variance-split.md) add. Standardizing gives an approximate [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md) test, or its square gives a one-degree-of-freedom [chi-squared distribution](chi-squared-distribution.md) test. The comparison requires the usual exchangeability and [independent censoring](independent-censoring.md) assumptions within strata.
