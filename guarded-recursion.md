# Guarded recursion

↑ **Parent:** [Recursion (computer science)](recursion-computer-science.md)

A recursive definition in which returning around a recursive dependency requires progress of the kind relevant to its semantics. In [communicating sequential processes](communicating-sequential-processes.md), the visible guard must survive [CSP hiding](csp-hiding.md); a loop whose apparent guard becomes a [CSP internal action](csp-internal-action.md) is not visibly guarded.
