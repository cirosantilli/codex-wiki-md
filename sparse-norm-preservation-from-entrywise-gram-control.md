# Sparse norm preservation from entrywise Gram control

↑ **Parent:** [Gram matrix](gram-matrix.md)

If $E=A^TA/d-I$ has [entrywise maximum norm](entrywise-maximum-norm.md) at most $t$, every vector $z$ supported on at most $s$ coordinates satisfies $|z^TEz|\leq t\|z\|_1^2\leq st\|z\|_2^2$. Thus the squared norm distortion is at most $st$ on all such sparse vectors. Use the quadratic inequality at $z=0$, where a ratio would be undefined.
