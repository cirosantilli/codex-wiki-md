# Structure sheaf of a complex manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Complex manifold](complex-manifold.md)

The structure sheaf assigns to an open set its ring of [holomorphic functions](holomorphic-function.md). Its invertible sections form the sheaf $\mathcal O_M^*$ of nowhere-zero [holomorphic functions](holomorphic-function.md).
