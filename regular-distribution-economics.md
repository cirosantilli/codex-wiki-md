# Regular distribution (economics)

↑ **Parent:** [Virtual valuation](virtual-valuation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_distribution_(economics))

A valuation distribution is regular when its [virtual valuation](virtual-valuation.md) is a [nondecreasing function](nondecreasing-function.md) of value. This is an auction-theory condition, not the unrelated regularity notions used elsewhere in mathematics.
