# Capacitance diaphragm gauge

↑ **Parent:** [Thermal transpiration experiment with a glass microtube](thermal-transpiration-experiment-with-a-glass-microtube.md)

A [capacitance diaphragm gauge](capacitance-diaphragm-gauge.md) senses [pressure](pressure-split.md) through deflection of a diaphragm that changes a [capacitor](capacitor.md)'s [capacitance](capacitance.md). Its head can have a different [temperature](temperature-split.md) from the vessel being measured, allowing [thermal transpiration](thermal-transpiration.md) in the connecting tube to change the head [pressure](pressure-split.md). [Daudé, Elandaloussi and Janssen, §I, diaphragm-gauge principle and thermal-transpiration effect](https://arxiv.org/html/1308.6794)
