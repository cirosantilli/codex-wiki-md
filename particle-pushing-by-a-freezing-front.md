# Particle pushing by a freezing front

↑ **Parent:** [Phase boundary](phase-boundary.md)

A planar freezing front can push a spherical particle steadily when repulsive [sphere-plane dispersion force](sphere-plane-dispersion-force.md) balances [lubrication resistance](lubrication-resistance.md). The thin parabolic gap gives hydraulic drag $6\pi\mu Va^2/d$, and balancing it against $Aa/(6d^2)$ gives the displayed speed. This balance assumes a nonretarded interaction, small gap and negligible inertial and other particle forces.
