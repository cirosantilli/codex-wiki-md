# Coequalizer of a reflexive pair from a pushout

↑ **Parent:** [Reflexive pair](reflexive-pair.md)

For a [reflexive pair](reflexive-pair.md) $f,g:A\rightrightarrows B$ with common section $r$, form the [pushout in a category](pushout-in-a-category.md) of $f$ and $g$, with maps $j_1,j_2:B\to P$. The equation $j_1f=j_2g$, composed with $r$, gives $j_1=j_2=q$. This common map is a [coequalizer](coequalizer.md) of $f,g$: every map equalizing them defines a commuting pair into the pushout and hence factors uniquely through $q$.
