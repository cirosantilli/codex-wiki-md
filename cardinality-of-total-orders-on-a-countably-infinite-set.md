# Cardinality of total orders on a countably infinite set

↑ **Parent:** [Total order](total-order.md)

There are at most continuum many [binary relations](binary-relation.md) on a [countable set](countable-set.md). For the matching lower bound, order the blocks $\{2n,2n+1\}$ by their index and independently choose the internal comparison in each block. A [binary sequence](bitstream.md) determines those choices and distinct [sequences](sequence.md) give distinct labelled [total orders](total-order.md). The resulting [order types](order-type.md) can all coincide; the count concerns relations, not [isomorphism](isomorphism.md) classes.
