# Gowers U3 norm on an interval

↑ **Parent:** [Gowers U3 norm](gowers-u3-norm.md)

Normalize the conjugated cube average by the number of integer cubes whose vertices all lie in the interval. Equivalently, extend $f$ by zero to a cyclic group of order greater than $8N$ and divide its [Gowers U3 norm](gowers-u3-norm.md) by that of the interval's [indicator function](indicator-function.md). A [quadratic phase](quadratic-phase.md) then has norm one. Omitting the denominator gives a different but uniformly equivalent interval normalization.
