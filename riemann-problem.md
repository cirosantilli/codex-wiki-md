# Riemann problem

↑ **Parent:** [System of conservation laws](system-of-conservation-laws.md)

A Riemann problem for a one-dimensional [system of conservation laws](system-of-conservation-laws.md) $\partial_tU+\partial_xf(U)=0$ has two constant initial states, $U_L$ for $x<0$ and $U_R$ for $x>0$. The data and equation are invariant under $(x,t)\mapsto(\lambda x,\lambda t)$. When the physically admissible [entropy solution](entropy-solution.md) is unique, this invariance makes it a [self-similar solution](similarity-solution.md) depending on $x/t$. For ideal-gas [compressible flow](compressible-flow-split.md), the characteristic speeds are $u-c,u,u+c$. The two acoustic families carry [shock waves](shock-wave.md) or [rarefaction waves](rarefaction-wave.md), while the middle family carries a [contact discontinuity](contact-discontinuity.md). Sufficiently strong separating velocities can instead create a vacuum region.
