# Bounded Dirichlet uniqueness in a quadrant

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet Poisson integral in a quadrant](dirichlet-poisson-integral-in-a-quadrant.md)

Two bounded [harmonic functions](harmonic-function.md) continuous to the quadrant edges with the same [Dirichlet boundary conditions](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) are equal. Their difference is bounded and zero on both axes. [Odd reflection](odd-reflection.md) across each axis gives a bounded harmonic extension away from the corner; the [removable singularity for a bounded harmonic function](removable-singularity-for-a-bounded-harmonic-function.md) fills in that corner. The [harmonic Liouville theorem](harmonic-liouville-theorem.md) makes that extension constant, and oddness makes the constant zero. This does not exclude unbounded examples such as the [zero-boundary harmonic growth in a quadrant](zero-boundary-harmonic-growth-in-a-quadrant.md).
