# Cyclotomic class of a finite field

↑ **Parent:** [Finite field](finite-field.md)

The [cosets](coset.md) of the index-$m$ [subgroup](subgroup.md) of the [multiplicative group of a finite field](multiplicative-group-of-a-finite-field.md) are its cyclotomic classes of index $m$. For a [character](character-of-a-representation.md) with $\chi(\gamma)=\zeta_m$, their indicators are $\mathbf1_{C_j}(x)=m^{-1}\sum_{h=0}^{m-1}\zeta_m^{-jh}\chi^h(x)$, with every [character](character-of-a-representation.md) extended by zero at zero. [Jacobi sums of finite-field characters](jacobi-sum-of-finite-field-characters.md) imply $|C_i\cap(t+C_j)|=q/m^2+O_m(\sqrt q)$ uniformly for $t\ne0$.
