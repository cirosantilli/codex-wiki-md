# Magnetic-energy injection by differential boundary rotation

↑ **Parent:** [Force-free magnetic field](force-free-magnetic-field.md)

The general ideal magnetic-energy balance has outward-surface contribution $\mu_0^{-1}\oint[(u\cdot B)B-B^2u]\cdot dS$ and volume contribution $-\int u\cdot(J\times B)dV$. The latter vanishes for a [force-free magnetic field](force-free-magnetic-field.md). For an axisymmetric boundary rotating tangentially, $u\cdot B=\Omega f(\psi)$ and the directed flux of a thin tube is $2\pi d\psi$. Pair its exit and entry endpoints to obtain the displayed energy injection, counting each boundary endpoint once. Equal endpoint angular [velocities](velocity.md) inject no [magnetic energy](magnetic-energy.md).
