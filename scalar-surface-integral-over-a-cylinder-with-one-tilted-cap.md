# Scalar surface integral over a cylinder with one tilted cap

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar surface integral](scalar-surface-integral.md)

Let $S$ be the full boundary of $x^2+y^2\leq R^2$, $0\leq z\leq H-\alpha y$, with $H>|\alpha|R$. The bottom contribution is zero by symmetry. On the top, the integrand is the constant $H$ and the [surface area element](surface-area-element.md) is $\sqrt{1+\alpha^2}\,dx\,dy$. On the side, set $y=R\sin\phi$ and integrate in $z$ first: the result is $R[H^2-\alpha^2R^2\sin^2\phi]/2$. Integrating from zero to $2\pi$ gives the displayed [scalar surface integral](scalar-surface-integral.md). No orientation signs enter; a scalar area integral is different from a [flux](flux.md).
