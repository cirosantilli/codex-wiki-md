# Angular momentum decay under uniform linear drag

↑ **Parent:** [Angular momentum](angular-momentum.md)

Consider particles of the same constant mass $m$, interacting through equal-and-opposite central internal forces, with external forces $F_i=-k\dot r_i$ for constant $k$. About a fixed point $a$, internal pair [torques](torque.md) cancel and the external [torque](torque.md) is $G_a=-k\sum_i(r_i-a)\times\dot r_i=-(k/m)L_a$. Hence $\dot L_a=-(k/m)L_a$ and the displayed exponential decay follows. The same result holds for [angular momentum about the centre of mass](angular-momentum-about-the-centre-of-mass.md), because the moving-origin term $-\dot R\times P=-\dot R\times M\dot R$ vanishes. Unequal masses do not generally permit factoring out a single $k/m$.
