# Universal property of the Jacobian variety

↑ **Parent:** [Jacobian variety](jacobian-variety.md)

For a smooth connected projective curve $C$ with chosen basepoint $p$, the [Abel map of an algebraic curve](abel-map-of-an-algebraic-curve.md) $i_p:C\to J(C)$ sends $q$ to $[q-p]$. Every morphism $g:C\to A$ to an [abelian variety](abelian-variety-split.md) with $g(p)=0$ factors uniquely through a homomorphism $J(C)\to A$. This universal property converts a curve embedded in an abelian variety into a homomorphism from its Jacobian.
