# Height-evaluation gravitational softening

↑ **Parent:** [Gravitational softening](gravitational-softening.md)

Evaluating the potential of a [razor-thin disk](razor-thin-disk-approximation.md) at height $\epsilon$ gives a softened horizontal force. Its Fourier multiplier is $e^{-k\epsilon}$ times the [razor-thin disk Poisson kernel](razor-thin-disk-poisson-kernel.md). This suppresses short [wavelengths](wavelength.md) while retaining the long-wave potential. A real finite-thickness profile generally has a different full reduction factor.
