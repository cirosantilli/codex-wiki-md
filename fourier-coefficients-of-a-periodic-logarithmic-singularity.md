# Fourier coefficients of a periodic logarithmic singularity

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md)

For the period-one continuation of $\log|x-1/2|$, symmetry and [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) give $c_m=-(-1)^m\operatorname{Si}(\pi m)/(\pi m)$ for $m\ne0$. Thus the [sine integral](sine-integral.md) asymptotic gives $c_m=-(-1)^m/(2m)+1/(\pi^2m^2)+O(m^{-4})$ as $m\to+\infty$. Translation of the logarithmic singularity causes the alternating phase; the endpoint derivative mismatch causes the next term.
