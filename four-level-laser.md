# Four-level laser

↑ **Parent:** [Laser](laser-split.md)

A pumping scheme with an auxiliary pump level, an upper laser level, a rapidly emptied lower laser level and a ground reservoir. Efficient lower-level removal reduces the population cost of inversion compared with a ground-state-terminated [three-level laser](three-level-laser.md), while cavity loss still imposes a finite [laser threshold](laser-threshold.md).
