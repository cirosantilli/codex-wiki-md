# Aromatic oxazolination

↑ **Parent:** [Electrophilic aromatic substitution](electrophilic-aromatic-substitution.md)

[Aromatic oxazolination](aromatic-oxazolination.md) introduces an oxazolinyl group at an aromatic C–H position. Activating [2-Oxazolidinone](2-oxazolidinone.md) with [triflic anhydride](triflic-anhydride.md) supplies an electrophilic [carbon](carbon.md) that an [electron](electron.md)-rich arene can attack.
