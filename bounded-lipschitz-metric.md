# Bounded-Lipschitz metric

↑ **Parent:** [Weak topology of probability measures](weak-topology-of-probability-measures.md)

The bounded-Lipschitz [metric](metric.md) tests [probability measures](probability-measure.md) against a uniformly bounded Lipschitz class. It metrizes [weak convergence of probability measures](weak-convergence-of-probability-measures.md) on separable [metric](metric.md) spaces. With a complete compatible base [metric](metric.md), it is complete on the [probability measures](probability-measure.md) of a [Polish space](polish-space.md). Replacing the sum norm by the maximum norm changes the [metric](metric.md) only by uniform constant factors.
