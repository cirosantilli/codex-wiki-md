# Population bottleneck

↑ **Parent:** [Population genetics](population-genetics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population_bottleneck)

A population bottleneck is a temporary large reduction in population size. It changes ancestral coalescence rates and can leave fewer ancestral [haplotypes](haplotype.md) and increased [linkage disequilibrium](linkage-disequilibrium.md) after the population expands.
