# Amino acid alpha carbon

↑ **Parent:** [Amino acid residue](amino-acid-residue.md)

The [amino acid alpha carbon](amino-acid-alpha-carbon.md) is the backbone [carbon](carbon.md) carrying the [amino acid side chain](amino-acid-side-chain.md) in the usual [protein](protein.md) [amino acid residue](amino-acid-residue.md). Marking successive [amino acid alpha carbons](amino-acid-alpha-carbon.md) is a useful way to trace a [polypeptide chain](polypeptide.md), but omits intervening backbone [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md). See [Clark, Douglas and Choi: the central alpha carbon](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/3-4-proteins).
