# Luminance histogram

↑ **Parent:** [Natural image statistics](natural-image-statistics.md)

The distribution of pixel [luminance](luminance.md) values without their spatial arrangement. Moments such as mean, [variance](variance-split.md) and skewness describe aspects of that distribution, while losing relationships among locations.
