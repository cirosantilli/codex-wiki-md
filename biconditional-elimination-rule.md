# Biconditional elimination rule

↑ **Parent:** [Natural deduction](natural-deduction.md)

From $A\leftrightarrow B$, infer $A\to B$ or $B\to A$. Applying the resulting implication requires a further [modus ponens rule](implication-elimination-rule.md).
