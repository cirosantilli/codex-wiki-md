# Delocalization energy

↑ **Parent:** [Hückel method](huckel-method.md)

The change in [energy](energy-split.md) on allowing a specified localized orbital reference to delocalize across a [conjugated system](conjugated-system.md). In a [Hückel method](huckel-method.md) calculation, the reference must use the same one-centre parameters and bond types as the delocalized [molecule](molecule-split.md). With the signed convention $E_\pi-E_{\rm localized}$, stabilization is negative; its positive magnitude is often also called the [delocalization energy](delocalization-energy.md), so the sign convention must be stated.
