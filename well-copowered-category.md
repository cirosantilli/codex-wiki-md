# Well-copowered category

↑ **Parent:** [Subobject](subobject.md)

A category is well-copowered if, for each object $C$, the [epimorphisms](epimorphism.md) $C\to Q$ have only a [set](set-split.md) of equivalence classes, where two are equivalent through an [isomorphism](isomorphism.md) of their codomains compatible with the quotient maps. Equivalently, its [opposite category](opposite-category.md) is [well-powered](well-powered-category.md).
