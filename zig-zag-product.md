# Zig-zag product

↑ **Parent:** [Expander graph](expander-graph.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zig-zag_product)

For a port-labeled $D$-regular [graph](graph-split.md) $G$ and a $d$-regular [graph](graph-split.md) $H$ on its $D$ port labels, replace every [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) by a copy of $H$. An [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) makes a step in $H$, crosses the corresponding [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) of $G$, then makes another step in $H$. The result has $|G|D$ [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) and degree $d^2$. Its normalized operator is $BRB$, where $B=I\otimes P_H$ and $R$ is the involutive [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) rotation of $G$. Decomposing a mean-zero vector into cloud-constant and cloud-orthogonal parts proves the bound $\rho(G\mathbin{\mathrm{zigzag}}H)\leq\rho(G)+2\rho(H)+\rho(H)^2$.
