# Energy form of a half-well sine state

↑ **Parent:** [Infinite square well](infinite-square-well.md)

In the [infinite square well](infinite-square-well.md) on $(-a,a)$, extend $\sqrt{2/a}\sin(\pi x/a)$ from $(0,a)$ by zero. It is normalized and continuous, but its [derivative](derivative.md) jumps at zero. Its energy [expectation value](expectation-value.md) is the [quadratic form of a positive quantum Hamiltonian](quadratic-form-of-a-positive-quantum-hamiltonian.md) $\hbar^2\int|\psi'|^2/(2m)$, giving the displayed value. It is not in the operator domain of the Dirichlet [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md): its distributional second [derivative](derivative.md) contains a [Dirac delta](dirac-delta-function.md). Its sine-basis coefficients are $a_2=-1/\sqrt2$, all other even coefficients zero, and $a_{2p+1}=4\sqrt2(-1)^p/[\pi(4-(2p+1)^2)]$. These make $\sum_nE_n|a_n|^2$ finite but $\sum_nE_n^2|a_n|^2$ divergent, independently distinguishing the form domain from the operator domain.
