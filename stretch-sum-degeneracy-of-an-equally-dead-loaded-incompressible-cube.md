# Stretch-sum degeneracy of an equally dead-loaded incompressible cube

↑ **Parent:** [Dead loading](dead-loading.md)

For $\Phi=\mu[\sum_i v_i^2/2+\sum_i v_i^{-1}]$ and positive stretch product one, the energy simplifies to $\mu S^2/2$, where $S=\sum_i v_i$. Equal nominal tensile loading adds potential $-\tau S$. The homogeneous diagonal [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) equations reduce to $(\mu S-\tau)(v_i-v_j)=0$. Thus the unit triple exists for every load, while the other [equilibria](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) form the displayed sum-product family. Since $S\geq3$, only the unit triple exists for $\tau\leq3\mu$. Above this load, a continuous family is obtained by choosing $v_1=t$ near one and taking $v_2,v_3$ as the two positive roots with sum $\tau/\mu-t$ and product $1/t$.
