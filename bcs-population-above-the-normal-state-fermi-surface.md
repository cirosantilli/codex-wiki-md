# BCS population above the normal-state Fermi surface

↑ **Parent:** [BCS coherence factors](bcs-coherence-factors.md)

For a uniform isotropic weak-gap BCS state, the zero-temperature [electron](electron.md) occupation is $n(\xi)=(1-\xi/\sqrt{\xi^2+\Delta^2})/2$. The number above the normal-state [Fermi surface](fermi-surface.md) is approximately $D(\mu)\int_0^\infty n(\xi)d\xi$. The integral equals $\Delta/2$, while a three-dimensional parabolic band including both spins has $D(\mu)=3N/(2\mu)$. This gives the displayed leading fraction.

The result measures redistribution relative to a normal [Fermi sea](fermi-sea.md), not bosonic single-mode condensate depletion or [quasiparticle](quasiparticle.md) occupation. A different band dimension or anisotropic gap requires its actual density-of-states or [momentum](momentum-split.md) integral. Material estimates need specified gap and chemical-potential scales.
