# Extent of reaction

↑ **Parent:** [Stoichiometry](stoichiometry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extent_of_reaction)

For a closed system with one specified [chemical reaction](chemical-reaction.md), the [extent of reaction](extent-of-reaction.md) $\xi$ records progress from $\xi=0$ through $n_i=n_{i,0}+\nu_i\xi$. Here $n_i$ is the [amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md) of species $i$, and the signed [stoichiometric vector](stoichiometric-vector.md) has negative entries for [reactants](reactant.md) and positive entries for [reaction products](product-chemistry.md). For the [water](water.md) [chemical equation](chemical-equation.md) in the order $\mathrm{H}_2,\mathrm{O}_2,\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O}$, it is $(-2,-1,2)$. The normalization of $\xi$ depends on the chosen coefficients. See [IUPAC Gold Book, extent of reaction](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/E02283).
