# Kondo insulator

↑ **Parent:** [Band insulator](band-insulator.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kondo_insulator)

A [Kondo insulator](kondo-insulator.md) has a bulk insulating state associated with mixing between relatively localized correlated electronic states and itinerant conduction states. A renormalized two-band picture captures a narrow [hybridization gap](hybridization-gap.md), although an uncorrelated orbital-mixing model does not explain the formation and temperature dependence of the coherent low-energy state. [Samarium hexaboride](samarium-hexaboride.md) is an example.
