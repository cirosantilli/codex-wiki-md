# Ordered right cosets of a convex lattice subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Convex lattice subgroup](convex-lattice-subgroup.md)

The displayed relation makes the right cosets into a [lattice](lattice.md), with $Pg\vee Ph=P(g\vee h)$ and $Pg\wedge Ph=P(g\wedge h)$. Antisymmetry uses convexity: $g\le ph$ and $h\le qg$ give $1\le phg^{-1}\le pq\in P$, hence $hg^{-1}\in P$. A [prime convex lattice subgroup](prime-convex-lattice-subgroup.md) makes this order total even if the subgroup is not normal.
