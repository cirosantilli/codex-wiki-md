# Hertzian contact

↑ **Parent:** [Contact mechanics](contact-mechanics.md)

The elastic contact of smooth nonadhesive bodies with locally quadratic surfaces. For a sphere on a flat surface, the load is proportional to the [reduced elastic modulus](reduced-elastic-modulus.md), square root of sphere radius and indentation to the three-halves power.
