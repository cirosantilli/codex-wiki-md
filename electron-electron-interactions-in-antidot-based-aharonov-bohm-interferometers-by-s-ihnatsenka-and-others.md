# Electron-electron interactions in antidot-based Aharonov-Bohm interferometers by S. Ihnatsenka and others

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by S. Ihnatsenka](work-by-s-ihnatsenka.md)

Physical Review B 80, 115303. [https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.80.115303](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.80.115303)

Primary preprint: [https://arxiv.org/abs/0905.1338](https://arxiv.org/abs/0905.1338)

Section I discusses edge-channel connectivity and the distinction between fixed-area [flux](flux.md) counting and charging-modified [periods](period-of-a-function.md). Section III.C, Fig. 7, discusses scattering-induced transmission via antidot-bound states. The calculation treats interaction effects and should not be read as a universal prediction of isolated Lorentzian peaks.
