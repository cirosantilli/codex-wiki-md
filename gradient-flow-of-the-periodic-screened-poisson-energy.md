# Gradient flow of the periodic screened Poisson energy

↑ **Parent:** [Gradient flow](gradient-flow.md)

For smooth $2\pi$-periodic $f$, the equation $u_t-u_{xx}+u=f$ is the negative $L^2$ [gradient flow](gradient-flow.md) of $I_f[u]=\frac12\int(u_x^2+u^2-2fu)\,dx$. Periodic [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) gives $dI_f/dt=-\int u_t^2\,dx$. Its unique equilibrium has [Fourier coefficients](fourier-coefficient.md) $f_n/(1+n^2)$, and every transient coefficient decays as $e^{-(1+n^2)t}$. Thus the flow converges to the unique minimizer of the energy.
