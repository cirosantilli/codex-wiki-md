# Observed flux of an unresolved spherical emitter

↑ **Parent:** [Radiative flux](radiative-flux.md)

For a uniformly emitting spherical body of [radius](radius.md) $R$ at [Euclidean distance](euclidean-distance.md) $d\gg R$, integration of its [specific intensity](specific-intensity.md) over the apparent [solid angle](solid-angle.md) gives $f_\lambda=\pi I_\lambda(R/d)^2$. For a [blackbody](blackbody.md), $I_\lambda=B_\lambda(T)$ and the bolometric result is $f=\sigma_{\rm SB}T^4(R/d)^2=L/(4\pi d^2)$. A grey [emissivity](emissivity.md) factor multiplies this expression; isothermality alone does not imply blackbody emission.
