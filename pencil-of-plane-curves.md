# Pencil of plane curves

↑ **Parent:** [Projective hypersurface](projective-hypersurface.md)

Given homogeneous polynomials $F,G$ of the same degree, their pencil is the one-parameter family

$$
V(sF+tG)\subseteq\mathbb P^2,
\qquad [s:t]\in\mathbb P^1.
$$

Its total space is the [projective hypersurface](projective-hypersurface.md) $V(sF+tG)\subseteq\mathbb P^2\times\mathbb P^1$, and projection to $[s:t]$ has these plane curves as its [fibres](fiber-of-a-morphism.md).

It is a [pencil](pencil-geometry.md) whose members are plane curves.
