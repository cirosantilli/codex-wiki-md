# Product fan

↑ **Parent:** [Fan in toric geometry](fan-in-toric-geometry.md)

The product of fans $\Sigma_1\subseteq N_{1,\mathbb R}$ and $\Sigma_2\subseteq N_{2,\mathbb R}$ consists of the cones $\sigma_1\times\sigma_2$. Its [toric variety](toric-variety.md) is $X_{\Sigma_1}\times X_{\Sigma_2}$.
