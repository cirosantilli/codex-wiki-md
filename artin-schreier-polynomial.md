<h1 id="artin-schreier-polynomial">Artin–Schreier polynomial</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Artin–Schreier theory](artin-schreier-theory.md)

An Artin–Schreier polynomial over a field of characteristic $p$ has the form

$$
X^p-X-a.
$$

If it has one root $\alpha$, all its roots are $\alpha+c$ for $c\in\mathbb F_p$. It either has a root in the base field and splits completely, or is an [irreducible polynomial](irreducible-polynomial.md) of degree $p$.
