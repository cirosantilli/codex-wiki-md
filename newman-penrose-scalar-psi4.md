<h1 id="newman-penrose-scalar-psi4">Newman--Penrose scalar Psi4</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Newman-Penrose formalism](newman-penrose-formalism.md)

For a null tetrad $(\ell,k,m,\bar m)$, one common curvature-sign convention defines $\Psi_4=C_{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}k^\mu\bar m^\nu k^\rho\bar m^\sigma$. In an outgoing wave zone it encodes the two transverse [gravitational wave polarizations](gravitational-wave-polarization.md) as one complex scalar.
