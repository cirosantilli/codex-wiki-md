# Komar angular momentum with inner boundaries

↑ **Parent:** [Komar angular momentum](komar-angular-momentum.md)

With zero [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md) and the component [Hodge star operator](hodge-star-operator.md) convention $(\star j)_{abc}=\epsilon_{abcd}j^d$, the [Einstein field equations](einstein-field-equations.md) and [Killing equation](killing-equation.md) give $d\star dm=-16\pi\star(T_{ab}m^b-\tfrac12Tm_a)$. If $m$ is tangent to a spacelike [hypersurface](hypersurface.md), the trace term pulls back to zero. For $\partial\Sigma=S_\infty-\bigcup_iS_i$, [Stokes theorem](stokes-theorem.md) yields

$$
J_\infty-\sum_iJ_i=-\int_\Sigma\star(T_{ab}m^b\,dx^a).
$$

The inner [Komar charges](komar-charge.md) cannot be dropped for a vacuum [Kerr black hole](kerr-black-hole.md), whose [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) is nonzero although its [stress-energy tensor](stress-energy-tensor.md) vanishes. A matter-only volume formula requires a regular filling with no inner boundaries.
