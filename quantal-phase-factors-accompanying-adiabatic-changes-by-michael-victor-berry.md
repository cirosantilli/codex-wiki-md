# Quantal phase factors accompanying adiabatic changes by Michael Victor Berry

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by Michael Victor Berry](work-by-michael-victor-berry.md)

The primary paper derives the [geometric phase](geometric-phase.md) accompanying adiabatic transport around a closed parameter loop. Section 4 treats a spinning particle in a slowly changing magnetic field, with [quantum phase](quantum-phase.md) proportional to its magnetic quantum number and the enclosed [solid angle](solid-angle.md).
