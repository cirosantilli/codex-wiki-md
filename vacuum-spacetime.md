# Vacuum spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [Vacuum Einstein equations](vacuum-einstein-equations.md)

A [vacuum spacetime](vacuum-spacetime.md) has vanishing matter [stress-energy tensor](stress-energy-tensor.md). In $D>2$ dimensions, tracing the [Vacuum Einstein equations](vacuum-einstein-equations.md) gives $R=2D\Lambda/(D-2)$ and hence $R_{ab}=2\Lambda g_{ab}/(D-2)$; in four dimensions this reduces to $R_{ab}=\Lambda g_{ab}$. With zero [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md) it is [Ricci flat](ricci-flat-manifold.md), although its full [Riemann curvature tensor](riemann-curvature-tensor.md) can be nonzero: a vacuum [Kerr black hole](kerr-black-hole.md) and vacuum [gravitational waves](gravitational-wave.md) illustrate that distinction.
