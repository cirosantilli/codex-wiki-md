# Internal variable

↑ **Parent:** [Continuum thermodynamics](continuum-thermodynamics.md)

An internal variable records a material state beyond its instantaneous [deformation gradient](deformation-gradient.md) and [temperature](temperature.md), such as a relaxing microstructural state. A [constitutive equation](constitutive-equation.md) specifies its evolution and its contribution to the [free energy](thermodynamic-free-energy.md).
