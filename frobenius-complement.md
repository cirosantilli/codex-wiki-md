# Frobenius complement

↑ **Parent:** [Frobenius group](frobenius-group.md)

A Frobenius complement is the nontrivial proper subgroup $H$ in a [Frobenius group](frobenius-group.md) with the displayed trivial-intersection property. In the action on cosets of $H$, it is a point stabilizer; no nonidentity element fixes two distinct points.
