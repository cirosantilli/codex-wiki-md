# Sobolev conjugate exponent

↑ **Parent:** [Sobolev inequality](sobolev-inequality.md)

For $1\leq p<n$, the Sobolev conjugate exponent $p^*$ is defined by

$$
\frac1{p^*}=\frac1p-\frac1n,
\qquad
p^*=\frac{np}{n-p}.
$$

The first-order [Sobolev inequality](sobolev-inequality.md) maps one derivative in $L^p(\mathbb R^n)$ to the function in $L^{p^*}(\mathbb R^n)$.
