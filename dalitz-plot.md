# Dalitz plot

↑ **Parent:** [Lorentz-invariant phase space](lorentz-invariant-phase-space.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dalitz_plot)

A display of three-body final states in two independent pairwise squared [invariant masses](invariant-mass.md). For a parent of [mass](mass.md) $M$ decaying into daughters of [masses](mass.md) $m_1,m_2,m_3$, [four-momentum conservation](four-momentum-conservation.md) gives $s_{12}+s_{13}+s_{23}=M^2+m_1^2+m_2^2+m_3^2$. A narrow intermediate two-body resonance fixes one pairwise [invariant mass](invariant-mass.md) and appears as a band.
