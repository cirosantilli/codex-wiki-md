# Viscosity window for an interfacial plug current

↑ **Parent:** [Ambient-controlled interfacial plug current](ambient-controlled-interfacial-plug-current.md)

The lower bound makes internal vertical velocity variation negligible; the upper bound makes internal in-plane extensional stress negligible compared with ambient [traction](traction.md). Both are needed for an [ambient-controlled interfacial plug current](ambient-controlled-interfacial-plug-current.md).
