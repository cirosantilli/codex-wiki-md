# Constructible line

↑ **Parent:** [Constructible point](constructible-point.md)

A [constructible line](constructible-line.md) is a [line](straight-line.md) through two distinct [constructible points](constructible-point.md). If the available coordinates lie in a real [field](field.md) $F$, a [line](straight-line.md) through $(a,b)$ and $(c,d)$ has equation $(d-b)(x-a)-(c-a)(y-b)=0$, with [coefficients](coefficient.md) in $F$ and a nonzero [normal vector](normal-vector.md). Intersecting two distinct such [lines](straight-line.md) introduces no new [field](field.md) elements, because solving a nonsingular pair of [linear equations](linear-equation.md) uses only [field](field.md) operations.
