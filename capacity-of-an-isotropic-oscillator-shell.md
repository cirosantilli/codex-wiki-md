# Capacity of an isotropic oscillator shell

↑ **Parent:** [Anisotropic quantum harmonic oscillator](anisotropic-quantum-harmonic-oscillator.md)

The number of nonnegative triples with $n_x+n_y+n_z=N$ is the stars-and-bars count $\binom{N+2}{2}$. Including a two-state [electron](electron.md) [spin](spin.md) gives capacity $(N+1)(N+2)$. Filling non-interacting shells therefore uses capacities $2,6,12,\ldots$, with shell [energy](energy-split.md) $(N+3/2)\hbar\omega$ per [electron](electron.md). A partly filled shell has degenerate many-body [ground states](ground-state.md); rearranging its occupants is not a positive excitation [energy](energy-split.md).
