# Nondegenerate one-dimensional elastic material

↑ **Parent:** [Linear elasticity](linear-elasticity.md)

For the one-dimensional reduction of isotropic [linear elasticity](linear-elasticity.md), nondegeneracy means that the effective longitudinal modulus $\lambda+2\mu$ is nonzero. The static [Navier-Cauchy equation](navier-cauchy-equation.md) then forces the displacement to be affine.
