# Bigness under finite normalization

↑ **Parent:** [Big divisor](big-divisor.md)

Let $\nu:X^\nu\to X$ be the finite normalization of an integral projective variety. The [coherent sheaf](coherent-sheaf.md) $\mathcal Q=\nu_*\mathcal O_{X^\nu}/\mathcal O_X$ is supported in dimension at most $n-1$. For a [Cartier divisor](cartier-divisor-split.md) $D$, the [projection formula for sheaves](projection-formula.md) and the resulting [long exact sequence in sheaf cohomology](long-exact-sequence-in-sheaf-cohomology.md) give

$$
0\leq h^0(X^\nu,m\nu^*D)-h^0(X,mD)\leq h^0(X,\mathcal Q\otimes\mathcal O_X(mD))=O(m^{n-1}).
$$

The last step uses the [polynomial bound for sections of a fixed divisor](polynomial-bound-for-sections-of-a-fixed-divisor.md). Therefore the leading order $m^n$ growth, and hence bigness, is preserved in both directions. This handles nonnormal varieties without assuming a resolution of singularities in positive characteristic.
