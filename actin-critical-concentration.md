# Actin critical concentration

↑ **Parent:** [Actin polymerization](actin-polymerization.md)

The free assembly-competent [actin monomer](actin-monomer.md) [concentration](concentration.md) at which net addition at a specified [actin filament](actin-filament.md) end is zero in a specified model: $C_c=k_-/k_+$. Different ends and [nucleotide](nucleotide.md) conditions can have different values.
