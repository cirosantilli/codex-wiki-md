# Stock-numeraire measure in the Black-Scholes model

↑ **Parent:** [Change of numeraire](change-of-numeraire.md)

For a non-dividend-paying [stock](stock.md) in the [Black-Scholes model](black-scholes-model.md), use density $dQ^S/dQ=e^{-\rho T}S_T/S_0$. Under $Q^S$, $W_t^S=W_t^Q-\sigma t$ is [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md), and $\log S_t=\log S_0+(\rho+\sigma^2/2)t+\sigma W_t^S$. Thus $e^{-\rho T}\mathbb E_Q[S_TH]=S_0\mathbb E_{Q^S}H$. This [change of numeraire](change-of-numeraire.md) is particularly useful for payments delivered in [stock](stock.md) units.
