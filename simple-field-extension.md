# Simple field extension

↑ **Parent:** [Field extension](field-extension.md)

A [simple field extension](simple-field-extension.md) is a [field extension](field-extension.md) generated by one element: $F(\alpha)$ is the smallest [subfield](subfield.md) of an ambient field containing $F$ and $\alpha$. Its elements are rational expressions in $\alpha$ with coefficients in $F$ and nonzero denominators. If $\alpha$ is algebraic, its [minimal polynomial](minimal-polynomial.md) $m_\alpha$ identifies $F(\alpha)\cong F[t]/(m_\alpha)$, so $[F(\alpha):F]=\deg m_\alpha$. The classes of $1,t,\ldots,t^{d-1}$ give a basis when $d=\deg m_\alpha$.

In particular, $\alpha^2\in F$ but $\alpha\notin F$ implies $[F(\alpha):F]=2$. Conversely, in characteristic different from two, any degree-two extension is of this form: for $\beta\notin F$, express $\beta^2=a+b\beta$ in the basis $1,\beta$, then $\alpha=\beta-b/2$ has $\alpha^2=a+b^2/4\in F$ and generates the same extension. The restriction on the characteristic makes division by two possible.
