# Astigmatism (eye)

↑ **Parent:** [Refractive error](refractive-error.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astigmatism)

An ocular [refractive error](refractive-error.md) in which a point object cannot be brought to one sharp retinal focus by a single spherical correction. [Regular ocular astigmatism](regular-ocular-astigmatism.md) has perpendicular principal meridians of unequal power; irregular departures require a more general description.[Overview and correction](astigmatism-by-the-national-eye-institute.md)
