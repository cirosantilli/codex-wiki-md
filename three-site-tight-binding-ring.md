# Three-site tight-binding ring

↑ **Parent:** [Tight binding](tight-binding.md)

In an [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) of localized [atomic orbitals](atomic-orbital.md), a ring of three equivalent sites with real hopping $t$ has [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) $H=(E_0-t)I+tJ$, where $J$ is the three-by-three [all-ones matrix](all-ones-matrix.md). The normalized symmetric state $(|1\rangle+|2\rangle+|3\rangle)/\sqrt3$ has energy $E_0+2t$. Its two-dimensional [orthogonal complement](orthogonal-complement.md) has energy $E_0-t$. Thus the symmetric state is the unique spatial [ground state](ground-state.md) for $t<0$, with an [energy](energy-split.md) difference $3|t|$ to the excited [eigenstates](eigenstate.md). Equivalently, the three discrete [Bloch states](bloch-state.md) have energies $E_0+2t\cos(2\pi l/3)$, $l=0,\pm1$. Nonorthogonal [atomic orbitals](atomic-orbital.md) instead require a [generalized eigenvalue problem](generalized-eigenvalue-problem.md).
