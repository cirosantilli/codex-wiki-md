# D9-brane

↑ **Parent:** [D-brane](d-brane.md)

A space-filling type-IIB [D-brane](d-brane.md). The associated ten-form potential is nonpropagating; its charge is not an additional local massless polarization. A consistent compact configuration must cancel any net space-filling RR charge, for example through appropriate other charged objects or projections.
