# 2-Oxazolidinone

↑ **Parent:** [Carbamate](carbamate.md)

[2-Oxazolidinone](2-oxazolidinone.md) is the five-membered cyclic [carbamate](carbamate.md) with ring path $\mathrm{O{-}C(O){-}NH{-}CH_2{-}CH_2}$. Electrophilic activation of its [carbonyl](carbonyl-group.md) [oxygen](oxygen.md) can generate an imidoyl-type [electrophile](electrophile.md).
