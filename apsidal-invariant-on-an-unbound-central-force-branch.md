# Apsidal invariant on an unbound central-force branch

↑ **Parent:** [Orbit equation for combined inverse-square and inverse-cube attraction](orbit-equation-for-combined-inverse-square-and-inverse-cube-attraction.md)

For $u=u_c[1+e\cos\nu(\psi-\psi_0)]$ with $\nu>0$ and $e\geq1$, the positive-radius scattering branch has $|\nu(\psi-\psi_0)|<\arccos(-1/e)\leq\pi$. Thus the principal complex argument in the displayed expression is continuous throughout that branch, unlike at an apocentre of a bound orbit. Its derivative is $\nu$, so the angle modulo $2\pi$, or the associated unit apsidal vector in the orbital plane, is a stationary [integral of motion](integral-of-motion.md) fixing the periapsis direction. Together with energy and three components of [specific angular momentum](specific-angular-momentum.md), it gives five regular scattering labels even for irrational $\nu$. Periodicity is therefore necessary for five orbit labels in recurrent bound motion, not in general for escaping trajectories.
