# Palindrome test

↑ **Parent:** [Palindrome](palindrome.md)

Decide whether a finite [string](string.md) is a [palindrome](palindrome.md) by comparing corresponding symbols from opposite ends. The test needs at most half as many symbol comparisons as the [string](string.md) length and can use constant auxiliary space.
