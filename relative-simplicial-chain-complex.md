# Relative simplicial chain complex

↑ **Parent:** [Relative chain complex](relative-chain-complex.md)

If $L$ is a [simplicial subcomplex](simplicial-subcomplex.md) of $K$, then

$$
C_k(K,L)=C_k(K)/C_k(L).
$$

Its homology is the simplicial [relative homology](relative-homology.md) $H_k(K,L)$.
