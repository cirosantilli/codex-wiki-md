# Prime-square point-count formula

↑ **Parent:** [Elliptic-curve point count over a finite field](elliptic-curve-point-count-over-a-finite-field.md)

For an [elliptic curve](elliptic-curve.md) over $\mathbb F_p$, put $N=\#E(\mathbb F_p)$ and $a=p+1-N$. The [trace of the square of an elliptic-curve endomorphism](trace-of-the-square-of-an-elliptic-curve-endomorphism.md) gives $\operatorname{tr}(\pi^2)=a^2-2p$ for the [Frobenius isogeny](frobenius-isogeny-of-an-elliptic-curve.md). Therefore $\#E(\mathbb F_{p^2})=p^2+1-a^2+2p=N(2p+2-N)$.
