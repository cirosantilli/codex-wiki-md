# Sheaf of abelian groups

↑ **Parent:** [Sheaf (mathematics)](sheaf-mathematics.md)

A sheaf of abelian groups is a [sheaf of sets](sheaf-mathematics.md) whose sections on each open set form an [abelian group](abelian-group.md) and whose restriction maps are group homomorphisms. Its [stalk of a sheaf](stalk-of-a-sheaf.md) is the corresponding group of germs. Kernels are computed on sections, whereas cokernels are obtained by [sheafification](sheafification.md) of the sectionwise cokernel. An [exact sequence](exact-sequence.md) can therefore be checked on stalks.
