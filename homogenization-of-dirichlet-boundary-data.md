# Homogenization of Dirichlet boundary data

↑ **Parent:** [Affine Sobolev space](affine-sobolev-space.md)

To solve a linear [Dirichlet problem](dirichlet-problem.md) with boundary value $g$, choose a sufficiently regular extension $G$ whose [trace](sobolev-trace-theorem.md) is $g$ and write $v=w+G$. Then $w$ has zero trace, while moving the known term $LG$ to the right-hand side turns the equation $Lv=f$ into the homogeneous-boundary problem $Lw=f-LG$.
