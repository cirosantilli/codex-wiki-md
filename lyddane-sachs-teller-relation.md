# Lyddane-Sachs-Teller relation

↑ **Parent:** [Lorentz oscillator model](lorentz-oscillator-model.md)

For a single lossless polar optical oscillator with frequency-independent electronic background, $\epsilon_r(\omega)=\epsilon_\infty(\omega_{\rm LO}^2-\omega^2)/(\omega_{\rm TO}^2-\omega^2)$. Setting frequency to zero yields the displayed static-to-background ratio. Multiple infrared-active branches require the corresponding product relation, rather than selecting an arbitrary pair of modes.
