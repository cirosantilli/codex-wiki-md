# Quartic oscillator

↑ **Parent:** [Simple harmonic motion](simple-harmonic-motion.md)

A quartic oscillator adds an anharmonic term to the quadratic potential, for example $H=(p^2+q^2)/2+\epsilon q^4$.

The quartic term is a specific [anharmonicity](anharmonicity.md) of the oscillator potential.
