# Central charge in supersymmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Super-Poincaré algebra](super-poincare-algebra.md)

A central charge in four-dimensional [extended supersymmetry](extended-supersymmetry.md) is a [Lorentz scalar](lorentz-scalar.md) even generator appearing in $\{Q_\alpha^A,Q_\beta^B\}=\epsilon_{\alpha\beta}Z^{AB}$. It commutes with the displayed translation, [Lorentz algebra](lorentz-algebra.md), and [supercharge](supersymmetry-generator.md) generators. An added [R-symmetry](r-symmetry.md) automorphism can act on the central charges.
