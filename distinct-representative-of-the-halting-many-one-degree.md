# Distinct representative of the halting many-one degree

↑ **Parent:** [Many-one completeness of the halting problem](many-one-completeness-of-the-halting-problem.md)

If $0\notin K$, then

$$
S=\{0\}\cup\{2n+1:n\in K\}
$$

differs from $K$, while the computable map $n\mapsto2n+1$ proves $K\leq_mS$.
