# Reduced-zone counting of bipartite spin waves

↑ **Parent:** [Antiferromagnetic spin wave](antiferromagnetic-spin-wave.md)

Two sublattices give two [bosonic](boson.md) modes for each of N/2 wavevectors in a magnetic [Brillouin zone](brillouin-zone.md). For a symmetric quadratic pairing block with number coefficient A and anomalous coefficient B, a bosonic [Bogoliubov transformation](bogoliubov-transformation.md) gives two equal positive energies $E_k=\sqrt{A^2-B_k^2}$ and the displayed vacuum shift. There are N modes overall. The [quantum depletion of Néel order](quantum-depletion-of-neel-order.md) per site is $(2/N)\sum_{\mathrm{MBZ}}v_k^2$, where $v_k^2=(A/E_k-1)/2$. Equivalently one can use one branch over a full zone with the corresponding factors of one half. Mixing these conventions doubles the vacuum energy or depletion.
