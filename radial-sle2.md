# Radial SLE2

↑ **Parent:** [Radial Schramm–Loewner evolution](radial-schramm-loewner-evolution.md)

For radial [SLE](schramm-loewner-evolution.md) at parameter two, the normalized slit-domain [Poisson kernel](poisson-kernel-for-the-upper-half-plane.md) at the growing tip is a [local martingale](local-martingale.md). This observable provides the coupling to [loop-erasure of planar Brownian motion](loop-erasure-of-planar-brownian-motion.md), oriented from the boundary point to the interior target. Its discrete counterpart is a [loop-erased random walk](loop-erased-random-walk.md).
