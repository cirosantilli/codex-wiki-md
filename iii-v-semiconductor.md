# III-V semiconductor

↑ **Parent:** [Semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md)

A compound semiconductor built from group-III and group-V elements, such as [gallium arsenide](gallium-arsenide.md). Many common members have a [zinc blende structure](zinc-blende-structure.md), while other materials or growth conditions give a [wurtzite structure](wurtzite.md). The chemical family alone does not fix a direct rather than indirect gap or a particular band ordering.
