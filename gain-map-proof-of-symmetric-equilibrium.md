# Gain-map proof of symmetric equilibrium

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetric Nash gain map](symmetric-nash-gain-map.md)

For a [symmetric finite game](symmetric-finite-game.md), pure-action expected payoffs $e(i,p)$ against independent identical opposing [mixed strategies](mixed-strategy.md) are continuous. The displayed map takes the [probability simplex](probability-simplex.md) continuously into itself. At a [Brouwer fixed-point theorem](brouwer-fixed-point-theorem.md) fixed point, put $r_i=[e(i,p)-e(p)]_+$ and $S=\sum_i r_i$. The fixed-point equations give $r_i=p_iS$. If $S>0$, every supported action has strictly positive excess payoff, contradicting $\sum_i p_i(e(i,p)-e(p))=0$. Hence $S=0$ and every supported action is a [best response](best-response.md), proving a [symmetric Nash equilibrium](symmetric-nash-equilibrium.md).
