# Higgs mechanism history

↑ **Parent:** [Higgs mechanism](higgs-mechanism.md)

In their 1964 paper, [François Englert](francois-englert.md) and [Robert Brout](robert-brout.md) showed that [gauge bosons](gauge-boson.md) associated with generators that rotate the chosen vacuum acquire masses while [gauge invariance](gauge-invariance.md) is maintained. Their discussion included both scalar and fermionic models.[pp. 321–323, especially Eq. (6)](broken-symmetry-and-the-mass-of-gauge-vector-mesons-by-francois-englert-and-robert-brout.md)

Independently, [Peter Higgs](peter-higgs.md) exhibited a massive vector and a surviving massive [scalar field](scalar-field.md) by linearizing a simple [gauge theory](gauge-theory.md). His paper explicitly distinguishes this classical calculation from a proof for the full quantized theory. These 1964 mechanisms supplied an ingredient for the later [electroweak interaction](electroweak-interaction.md), rather than the complete [Standard Model](standard-model-split.md).[p. 508, Eqs. (2b)–(4); p. 509, note 4](broken-symmetries-and-the-masses-of-gauge-bosons-by-peter-higgs.md)
