# Complete omega-consistent arithmetic theories are sound

↑ **Parent:** [Omega-consistency of an arithmetic theory](omega-consistency-of-an-arithmetic-theory.md)

A bounded-complete complete omega-consistent [arithmetic](arithmetic-split.md) [first-order theory](first-order-theory.md) is correct on every [arithmetic](arithmetic-split.md) [first-order sentence](first-order-sentence.md). Completeness and [first-order consistency](first-order-consistency.md) make its theoremhood obey the Boolean [truth tables](truth-table.md). [Omega-consistency](omega-consistency-of-an-arithmetic-theory.md) ensures that every proved [existential sentence](existential-sentence.md) has a provable standard numeral instance, since otherwise completeness refutes all such instances. [Induction](mathematical-induction.md) on [logical formulas](logical-formula.md) now identifies theoremhood with standard truth.
