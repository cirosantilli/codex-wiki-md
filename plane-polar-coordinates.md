# Plane polar coordinates

↑ **Parent:** [Position](position.md)

Plane polar coordinates describe a point by its radial distance $r$ and polar angle $\theta$.

These are the planar [polar coordinates](polar-coordinates.md); the general coordinate-system definition and transformations belong to that canonical concept.
