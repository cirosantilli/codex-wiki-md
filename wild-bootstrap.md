# Wild bootstrap

↑ **Parent:** [Bootstrap sample](bootstrap-sample.md)

For [independent](independent-random-variables.md) mean-zero errors in a fixed-design regression, form $y_i^*=x_i^\top\widehat\beta+\widehat e_i W_i$, where the [independent](independent-random-variables.md) multipliers have [mean](expected-value.md) zero and [variance](variance-split.md) one, and refit. Rademacher multipliers taking the values $-1$ and $1$ with equal [probability](probability.md) are a simple choice. Conditional on the data, the [bootstrap](bootstrapping-statistics.md) errors have [mean](expected-value.md) zero and preserve observation-specific residual [variances](variance-split.md), supporting [heteroscedasticity](heteroscedastic.md) rather than imposing a common residual distribution. Appropriate leverage corrections and regularity assumptions are required for accurate studentized inference.
