# Extended-zone representation of a phonon dispersion

↑ **Parent:** [Phonon dispersion relation](phonon-dispersion-relation.md)

The [crystal momentum](crystal-momentum.md) of a [phonon](phonon.md) is defined modulo a [reciprocal lattice vector](reciprocal-lattice-vector.md). Extending its [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation-split.md) periodically beyond the [first Brillouin zone](first-brillouin-zone.md) labels equivalent excitations by different extended [wavevectors](wavevector.md). A scattering transfer $Q=G+q$ can be outside that zone while its reduced [phonon](phonon.md) [wavevector](wavevector.md) $q$ is inside. The measured [neutron](neutron-split.md) recoil depends on the actual transfer $Q$, not on the reduced representative alone.
