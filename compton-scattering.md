↑ **Parent:** [Compton wavelength](compton-wavelength.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compton_scattering)

$$
\lambda'-\lambda=\frac h{mc}(1-\cos\theta).
$$
