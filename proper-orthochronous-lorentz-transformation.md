# Proper orthochronous Lorentz transformation

↑ **Parent:** [Proper orthochronous Lorentz group](proper-orthochronous-lorentz-group.md)

A [Lorentz transformation](lorentz-transformation.md) with [determinant](determinant.md) $+1$ that preserves future-directed timelike [four-vectors](four-vector.md). Equivalently, it belongs to the identity component of the [Lorentz group](lorentz-group.md). For the [Minkowski metric](minkowski-metric.md) $(-,+,+,+)$, its [matrix](matrix.md) satisfies $\Lambda^T\eta\Lambda=\eta$ and $\Lambda^0{}_0\ge1$. Such transformations preserve the future [light cone](light-cone.md), so the [retarded Green function](retarded-green-function.md) of the [d'Alembert operator](d-alembert-operator.md) transforms as a [Lorentz scalar](lorentz-scalar.md).
