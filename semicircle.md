# Semicircle

↑ **Parent:** [Circle](circle.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semicircle)

Half of a [circle](circle.md), with endpoints at opposite ends of a diameter. For radius $R>0$, the upper semicircle in the [complex plane](complex-plane.md) is parametrized by $z=Re^{it}$, $0\leq t\leq\pi$, going counterclockwise from $R$ to $-R$.
