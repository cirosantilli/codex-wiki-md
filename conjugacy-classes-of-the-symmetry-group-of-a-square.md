# Conjugacy classes of the symmetry group of a square

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetry group of a square](symmetry-group-of-a-square.md)

The displayed [conjugacy classes](conjugacy-class.md) have sizes one, one, two, two and two. The identity and half-turn are [central elements](central-element-of-a-group.md). Reflection sends $r$ to $r^{-1}$, pairing the two quarter-turns. Conjugating a reflection by $r$ sends $r^ks$ to $r^{k+2}s$; conjugating by $s$ sends it to $r^{-k}s$. Thus the parity of $k$ is invariant under [conjugation](conjugation.md), separating the axis reflections from the diagonal reflections, and each displayed pair is one [conjugacy class](conjugacy-class.md).
