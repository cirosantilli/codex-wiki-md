# Group antihomomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md)

A group antihomomorphism between [groups](group-split.md) reverses multiplication: $f(gh)=f(h)f(g)$. Inversion $g\mapsto g^{-1}$ is an example. An antihomomorphism is a [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md) when the multiplication in the target is replaced by its reverse. In a nonabelian target the reversal matters: the [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md) and antihomomorphism identities generally differ.
