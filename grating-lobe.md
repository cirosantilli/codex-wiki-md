# Grating lobe

↑ **Parent:** [Phased array](phased-array.md)

A [grating lobe](grating-lobe.md) is an additional principal maximum caused by spatial sampling in a [phased array](phased-array.md). A regularly spaced linear array steered towards $\theta_0$ also has maxima wherever $\sin\theta=\sin\theta_0+m\lambda/d$ lies in $[-1,1]$. Taking $d<\lambda/2$ excludes such maxima for all steering angles; $d=\lambda/2$ is the usual limiting choice, with an endfire degeneracy at the extreme scan directions.
