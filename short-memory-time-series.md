# Short-memory time series

↑ **Parent:** [Time series](time-series-split.md)

In a second-order sense a [weakly stationary process](weakly-stationary-process.md) has short memory when its [autocovariance](autocovariance.md) is absolutely summable. Then its [spectral density of a stationary process](spectral-density-of-a-stationary-process.md) is continuous and its sample-mean variance has the usual finite [long-run variance of a stationary process](long-run-variance-of-a-stationary-process.md). A [central limit theorem](central-limit-theorem.md) still needs additional conditions.
