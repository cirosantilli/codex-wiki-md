# Ocean basin equation with bottom drag and lateral viscosity

↑ **Parent:** [Sverdrup balance](sverdrup-balance.md)

With depth transport $(-\psi_y,\psi_x)$ and a depth-mean bottom-drag closure, the wind-driven equation is $\beta\psi_x=S-r\nabla^2\psi+\nu\nabla^4\psi$, where $S$ is wind-stress curl divided by density and $r=\gamma/(\rho_0H)$ is the drag rate. Its zonal boundary modes solve $\nu\lambda^3-r\lambda-\beta=0$. The [Munk boundary layer](munk-boundary-layer.md) and [Stommel boundary layer](stommel-boundary-layer.md) are different limits of this combined equation.
