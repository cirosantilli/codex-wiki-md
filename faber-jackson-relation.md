# Faber-Jackson relation

↑ **Parent:** [Elliptical galaxy](elliptical-galaxy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faber-Jackson_relation)

The [Faber-Jackson relation](faber-jackson-relation.md) is an empirical correlation between [luminosity](luminosity.md) and stellar [velocity dispersion](velocity-dispersion.md) for [elliptical galaxies](elliptical-galaxy.md), approximately $L\propto\sigma^4$ in its classical form. Slope, scatter, sample and band matter. A heuristic combines $M\propto\sigma^2R$, common [mass-to-light ratio](mass-to-light-ratio.md), and common characteristic [surface brightness](surface-brightness.md) $L/R^2$. The luminosity-dispersion correlation alone does not imply those auxiliary assumptions, and extrapolation to a [dwarf spheroidal galaxy](dwarf-spheroidal-galaxy.md) is not guaranteed.
