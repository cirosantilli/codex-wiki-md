# Fresnel diffraction by an opaque disk

↑ **Parent:** [Fresnel diffraction](fresnel-diffraction.md)

Under the same scalar paraxial assumptions as [Fresnel diffraction by a circular aperture](fresnel-diffraction-by-a-circular-aperture.md), [Babinet's principle](babinet-s-principle.md) gives the complementary opaque-disk field $U_d/U_0=e^{i\pi N_F}$ on axis. Its axial intensity equals the unobstructed intensity, producing the [Arago spot](arago-spot.md) inside the geometric shadow. The off-axis pattern depends on the full Fresnel integral; it is not obtained by subtracting aperture intensities from the incident intensity. Finite source size, misalignment and imperfect circular edges reduce the ideal spot contrast.
