# Character of an algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Banach algebra](banach-algebra-split.md)

A character on a complex algebra is a nonzero multiplicative linear functional $\varphi:A\to\mathbb C$.

Algebra characters form the domain of the [Gelfand representation](gelfand-representation.md); they are distinct from traces of group representations.
