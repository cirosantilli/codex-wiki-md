# Dropsy

↑ **Parent:** [History of medicine](history-of-medicine.md)

A historical term for abnormal accumulation of bodily fluid, overlapping with [oedema](oedema.md). It describes an appearance or syndrome, not a single specific modern [disease](disease.md).
