# Periodic resistive loading of a transmission line

↑ **Parent:** [Transmission line](transmission-line.md)

A shunt [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) $R$ inserted between the conductors of a uniform lossless [transmission line](transmission-line.md) of [characteristic impedance](characteristic-impedance.md) $Z$ has voltage [reflection coefficient](reflection-coefficient.md) $r=-Z/(2R+Z)$ and transmission amplitude $t=1+r$. Equally spaced shunts produce coherent reflected waves. The round-trip phase between neighbours is $2kL$, so interference repeats in [frequency](frequency.md) with spacing $v_p/(2L)$ for nondispersive phase [speed](speed.md) $v_p$.
