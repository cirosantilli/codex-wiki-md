# High-resolution quantization approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Uniform quantization error](uniform-quantization-error.md)

A smooth input [probability density](probability-density.md) varying slowly on the scale of one conversion interval makes the fractional [uniform quantization error](uniform-quantization-error.md) approximately uniform. Large overall [variance](variance-split.md) is not sufficient: discrete inputs aligned with conversion levels provide a counterexample. Clipping, coarse sampling and correlations between error and [signal](signal.md) require separate analysis.
