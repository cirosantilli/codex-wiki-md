<h1 id="brown-s-representability-theorem">Brown's representability theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Topological half-exact functor](topological-half-exact-functor.md)

A reduced contravariant [homotopy](homotopy.md) [functor](functor.md) on based [CW complexes](cw-complex.md) satisfying the wedge-to-product axiom and the Mayer-Vietoris weak-pullback gluing axiom is represented by $[X,Y]_*$ for a [CW complex](cw-complex.md) $Y$. Half-exactness alone is not the full hypothesis. A reduced [generalized cohomology theory](generalized-cohomology-theory.md) supplies these axioms; suspension compatibility assembles its representing spaces into an [Omega-spectrum](omega-spectrum.md).
