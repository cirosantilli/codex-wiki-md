# Ill-posed inverse problem

↑ **Parent:** [Inverse problem](inverse-problem-split.md)

An [inverse problem](inverse-problem-split.md) is an [ill-posed problem](ill-posed-problem.md) when its specified data do not give existence, uniqueness and continuous dependence of the recovered object in the chosen spaces. Missing Fourier modes can cause nonuniqueness, while small singular values can cause instability even when a unique solution exists. [Regularization of an inverse problem](regularization-of-an-inverse-problem.md) adds information or a stability preference to the reconstruction.
