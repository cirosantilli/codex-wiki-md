# Linear response in action-angle variables

↑ **Parent:** [Action-angle variables](action-angle-variables.md)

Linearizing the [Collisionless Boltzmann equation](collisionless-boltzmann-equation.md) around $f_0(\mathbf J)$ in an integrable background [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) $H_0(\mathbf J)$ gives $(\partial_t+i\mathbf k\cdot\boldsymbol\Omega)\delta f_{\mathbf k}=i\mathbf k\cdot\partial_{\mathbf J}f_0\Phi_{\mathbf k}$, with $\boldsymbol\Omega=\partial_{\mathbf J}H_0$. An [integrating factor](integrating-factor.md) gives the displayed zero-initial-perturbation solution. A nonzero initial perturbation adds the freely evolving term $e^{-i\mathbf k\cdot\boldsymbol\Omega t}\delta f_{\mathbf k}(0)$.
