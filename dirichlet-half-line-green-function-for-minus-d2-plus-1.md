# Dirichlet half-line Green function for minus d2 plus 1

↑ **Parent:** [One-dimensional modified Helmholtz Green function](one-dimensional-modified-helmholtz-green-function.md)

On the positive half-line, the [Green function](green-s-function-split.md) for $-d^2/dx^2+1$ with a zero [Dirichlet boundary condition](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) at the origin and decay at infinity is

$$
G(x,\xi)=\sinh(\min(x,\xi))e^{-\max(x,\xi)}=\frac12\left(e^{-|x-\xi|}-e^{-(x+\xi)}\right).
$$

It is continuous, has [derivative](derivative.md) jump $G_x(\xi+,\xi)-G_x(\xi-,\xi)=-1$, and is nonnegative. It is the negative of the [Dirichlet half-line Green function for d2 minus 1](dirichlet-half-line-green-function-for-d2-minus-1.md); the sign of the leading differential term matters.
