# Intrinsic-semiconductor chemical potential

↑ **Parent:** [Intrinsic semiconductor](intrinsic-semiconductor.md)

For nondegenerate [conduction bands](conduction-band.md) and [valence bands](valence-band.md), [charge neutrality](charge-neutrality.md) gives $N_c e^{-(E_c-\mu_i)/(k_BT)}=N_v e^{-(\mu_i-E_v)/(k_BT)}$. Taking logarithms yields

$$
\mu_i=\frac{E_c+E_v}{2}+\frac{k_BT}{2}\ln\frac{N_v}{N_c}.
$$

For single spin-degenerate parabolic extrema this becomes $(E_c+E_v)/2+(3k_BT/4)\ln(m_h^*/m_e^*)$. Valley multiplicities or additional hole bands belong in $N_c,N_v$ and change the logarithm. The intrinsic [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) lies exactly at midgap only when the two [effective density of states](effective-density-of-states.md) values agree.
