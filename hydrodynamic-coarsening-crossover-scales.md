# Hydrodynamic coarsening crossover scales

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamical scaling of binary-fluid coarsening](dynamical-scaling-of-binary-fluid-coarsening.md)

Under single-length [dynamical scaling of binary-fluid coarsening](dynamical-scaling-of-binary-fluid-coarsening.md), [mass density](density.md) $\rho$, [dynamic viscosity](dynamic-viscosity.md) $\eta$ and [surface tension](surface-tension.md) $\sigma$ determine the displayed unique length and time scales by [dimensional analysis](dimensional-analysis.md). Writing $L=L_0f(t/t_0)$ reduces the force-density estimate to $\alpha f''+\beta f'^2/f=\gamma f'/f^2+\delta/f^2$. Resistive and driving terms need opposite signed geometric coefficients. [Viscous hydrodynamic coarsening](viscous-hydrodynamic-coarsening.md) has $f\sim u$ and [inertial hydrodynamic coarsening](inertial-hydrodynamic-coarsening.md) has $f\sim u^{2/3}$. These follow respectively from density-independent and viscosity-independent powers in $L\propto\eta^{2-3p}\rho^{p-1}\sigma^{2p-1}t^p$. They require thin interfaces, statistically self-similar bicontinuous domains, dominant advection and no additional relevant macroscopic scales. A degeneracy in the signed inertial coefficients can invalidate the pure-power force balance without invalidating the dimensional calculation.
