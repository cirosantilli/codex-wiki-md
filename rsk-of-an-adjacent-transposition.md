# RSK of an adjacent transposition

↑ **Parent:** [Robinson-Schensted-Knuth correspondence](robinson-schensted-knuth-correspondence.md)

If a [standard Young tableau](standard-young-tableau.md) of shape $(n-1,1)$ has second-row entry $a$, then its self-pair under [RSK](robinson-schensted-knuth-correspondence.md) corresponds to the [adjacent transposition](adjacent-transposition.md) $(a-1,a)$. Inserting the word $1,\ldots,a-2,a,a-1,a+1,\ldots,n$ first appends increasing entries to the first row. At time $a$, inserting $a-1$ bumps $a$ into the unique second-row cell. Every later entry appends to the first row. Thus the insertion tableau is the stated tableau, and the recording tableau has its second-row label $a$ as well. This proves the correspondence directly, including $n=2$, without invoking a separate theorem about [involutions](involution.md).
