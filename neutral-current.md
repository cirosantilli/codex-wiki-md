# Neutral current

↑ **Parent:** [Weak interaction](weak-interaction.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neutral_current)

The weak neutral current couples the [Z boson](z-boson.md) to a fermion without changing its electric charge. Its vector and axial-vector coefficients depend on weak isospin, electric charge, and the weak mixing angle.
