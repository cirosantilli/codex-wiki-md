# Levine-Tristram signature

↑ **Parent:** [Seifert matrix](seifert-matrix.md)

For $\omega\in S^1\setminus\{1\}$, the Levine-Tristram signature is the signature of the Hermitian matrix

$$
H_\omega=(1-\omega)A+(1-\overline\omega)A^T.
$$

It is locally constant away from unit roots of the [Alexander polynomial of a knot](alexander-polynomial.md).

At $\omega=-1$, this becomes the [signature of a knot](signature-of-a-knot.md).
