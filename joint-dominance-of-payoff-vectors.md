# Joint dominance of payoff vectors

↑ **Parent:** [Nash bargaining problem](nash-bargaining-problem.md)

A feasible [payoff](payoff.md) [vector](vector.md) $u'$ jointly dominates $u$ if every coordinate is at least as large and at least one is strictly larger. A [Pareto optimal](pareto-efficiency.md) [vector](vector.md) has no feasible joint dominator. Strict increase of every coordinate is not required. This compares each player's own utilities, not the numerical size of utilities across different players.
