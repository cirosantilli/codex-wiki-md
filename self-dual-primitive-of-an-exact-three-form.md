# Self-dual primitive of an exact three-form

↑ **Parent:** [Hodge decomposition theorem](hodge-decomposition-theorem.md)

On a compact oriented [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md) of dimension four without boundary, every exact three-form has a [self-dual two-form](self-dual-differential-form.md) primitive. Write $\beta=d\omega$ and use the [Hodge decomposition theorem](hodge-decomposition-theorem.md) to split $\omega=h+d\xi+\delta\psi$. Then $\beta=d\delta\psi$. For $a=\delta\psi$, the [codifferential](codifferential.md) identity $\delta=-*d*$ and the fact $*^2=1$ on two-forms give $*a=-d*\psi$, so $d*a=0$. Thus $\sigma=a+*a$ satisfies $*\sigma=\sigma$ and $d\sigma=\beta$. It is twice the self-dual projection of $a$, rather than the projection itself.
