# Minimal polynomial of an affine function of a projection

↑ **Parent:** [Projection (linear algebra)](projection-linear-algebra.md)

For a [linear projection](projection-linear-algebra.md) $P$ on a nonzero finite-dimensional [vector space](vector-space-split.md), $V=\ker P\oplus\operatorname{im}P$. The [linear map](linear-map.md) $\alpha I+\beta P$ acts by the [scalar](scalar.md) $\alpha$ on the first summand and $\alpha+\beta$ on the second. Its [minimal polynomial](minimal-polynomial.md) is the product of the distinct corresponding [linear polynomials](linear-polynomial.md) whose summands are nonzero. If $\beta=0$ this is $x-\alpha$; if $P=0$ or $P=I$ only the associated single factor remains. Otherwise, for $\beta\ne0$, it is $(x-\alpha)(x-\alpha-\beta)$. This follows directly from an adapted [basis](basis.md), so it works in every [characteristic](characteristic-of-a-field.md).
