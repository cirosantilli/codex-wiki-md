# Primary quality

↑ **Parent:** [Primary-secondary quality distinction](primary-secondary-quality-distinction.md)

In [John Locke](john-locke.md)'s account, a quality such as extension, solidity, shape, number or [motion](motion.md), attributed to bodies independently of whether they are perceived. This historical list should not be treated as an unrevisable inventory of fundamental physical properties.
