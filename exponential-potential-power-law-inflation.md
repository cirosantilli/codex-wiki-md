# Exponential-potential power-law inflation

↑ **Parent:** [Power-law inflation](power-law-inflation.md)

Use [natural units](natural-units.md), the unreduced [Planck mass](planck-mass.md) $m_P=G^{-1/2}$, $V_0>0$ and $c\ne0$. In a flat universe dominated by a canonical [inflaton](inflaton.md), $\phi=(2m_P/c)\log(\beta t)$ and $a\propto t^p$ solve the [inflaton equation of motion](inflaton-equation-of-motion.md) and [Friedmann equation](friedmann-equations.md) exactly when $p=16\pi/c^2$ and $\beta^2=V_0c^2/[2m_P^2(3p-1)]$. The positive-potential scaling solution requires $p>1/3$, while [cosmic inflation](cosmic-inflation-split.md) requires $p>1$. Its constant [equation-of-state parameter](equation-of-state-parameter.md) is $w=-1+2/(3p)$. In terms of the [reduced Planck mass](reduced-planck-mass.md) $M=m_P/\sqrt{8\pi}$, the exponential slope is $\lambda=c/\sqrt{8\pi}$ and the same exponent is $p=2/\lambda^2$.
