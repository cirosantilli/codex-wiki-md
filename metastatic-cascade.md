# Metastatic cascade

↑ **Parent:** [Metastasis](metastasis.md)

A sequence of conditional transitions from a primary [tumour](tumour.md) to a distant growing lesion. A bypass assay tests only selected transitions, and failure at one stage need not identify the slowest step in every setting.
