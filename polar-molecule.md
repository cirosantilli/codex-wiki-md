# Polar molecule

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical polarity](chemical-polarity.md)

A [polar molecule](polar-molecule.md) has a nonzero permanent [electric dipole moment](electric-dipole-moment.md) because of its internal [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) distribution. It can still have zero total [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md). [Water](water.md) is polar; a [nonpolar molecule](nonpolar-molecule.md) has no permanent [electric dipole moment](electric-dipole-moment.md).
