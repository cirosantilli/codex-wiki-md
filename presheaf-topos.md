# Presheaf topos

↑ **Parent:** [Presheaf category](presheaf-category.md)

Every [presheaf category](presheaf-category.md) is a [topos](elementary-topos.md). Finite limits are pointwise; the exponential is

$$
(G^F)(C)=\operatorname{Nat}(yC\times F,G),
$$

and the [subobject classifier](subobject-classifier.md) assigns to $C$ the set of sieves on $C$.
