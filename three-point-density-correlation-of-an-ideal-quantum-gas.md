# Three-point density correlation of an ideal quantum gas

↑ **Parent:** [Normally ordered density correlation](normally-ordered-density-correlation.md)

For a number-conserving Gaussian pairing state, six [permutations](permutation.md) give this expression. Each [transposition](transposition-permutation.md) has sign $\epsilon$, positive for [bosons](boson.md) and negative for [fermions](fermion.md); the two three-cycles have positive sign for either statistics. Equivalently it is the permanent or [determinant](determinant.md) of the three-by-three [one-body density matrix](one-body-density-matrix.md). For bosonic [number states](number-state.md), repeated-mode [factorial](factorial.md) corrections must be included instead of assuming that [Wick theorem](wick-s-theorem.md).
