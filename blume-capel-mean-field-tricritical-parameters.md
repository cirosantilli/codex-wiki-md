<h1 id="blume-capel-mean-field-tricritical-parameters">Blume–Capel mean-field tricritical parameters</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Blume–Capel model](blume-capel-model.md)

For ferromagnetic exchange $J$ and coordination $z$, the [mean-field approximation](mean-field-approximation.md) has $f=zJM^2/2-k_BT\log[1+2e^{-g/(k_BT)}\cosh(zJM/(k_BT))]$. With $K=zJ/(k_BT)$ and $q=2e^{-g/(k_BT)}/[1+2e^{-g/(k_BT)}]$, its quadratic and quartic coefficients vanish at $q=1/3$, $K=3$. The sextic coefficient of $f/(k_BT)$ is $9/40>0$. Hence $T_{\mathrm{TCP}}=zJ/(3k_B)$ and $g_{\mathrm{TCP}}=k_BT_{\mathrm{TCP}}\log4$; these are not exact finite-dimensional lattice values.
