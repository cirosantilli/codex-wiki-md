<h1 id="perturbative-renormalization-of-a-fayet-iliopoulos-term">Perturbative renormalization of a Fayet–Iliopoulos term</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Fayet–Iliopoulos term](fayet-iliopoulos-term.md)

In an exactly supersymmetric Abelian theory a one-loop auxiliary-field tadpole can generate a cutoff-dependent [Fayet–Iliopoulos term](fayet-iliopoulos-term.md) proportional to the trace of the matter charges. Gauge invariance with arbitrary background [spurions](spurion.md) forbids a general coupling-dependent full-superspace coefficient multiplying the vector superfield. In the supersymmetry-preserving Wilsonian setting the additive perturbative correction is exhausted by the one-loop term; it vanishes for a traceless charge generator. Canonical vector normalization and soft breaking require separate treatment. For a simple non-Abelian group the term itself is forbidden.
