# Arctangent transform of a two-noise affine diffusion

↑ **Parent:** [Scale function (stochastic processes)](scale-function-stochastic-processes.md)

For independent [Brownian motions](brownian-motion-split.md) $W,B$, the [Itô formula](ito-s-lemma.md) cancels the drift after the arctangent transform. Normalizing the two noise coefficients gives a [continuous local martingale](continuous-local-martingale.md) $Z$ with [quadratic variation](quadratic-variation.md) $t$, hence another [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) by the [Lévy characterization of Brownian motion](levy-characterization-of-brownian-motion.md).
