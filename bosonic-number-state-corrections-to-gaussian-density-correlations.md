# Bosonic number-state corrections to Gaussian density correlations

↑ **Parent:** [Normally ordered density correlation](normally-ordered-density-correlation.md)

For a product of bosonic [number states](number-state.md), direct and exchange contractions correctly count distinct modes. They overcount repeated-mode terms because $\langle a^{\dagger2}a^2\rangle=N(N-1)$, while [Wick theorem](wick-s-theorem.md) would give $2N^2$. Subtracting the excess yields the displayed exact two-point correction. At higher order the occupation decrements carry [falling factorials](falling-factorial.md). This distinction is essential when comparing a fixed-number condensate with independent thermal modes.
