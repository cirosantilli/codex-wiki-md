# Metatheria

↑ **Parent:** [Theria](theria.md)

The lineage comprising [marsupials](marsupial.md) and extinct relatives closer to them than to [placental mammals](placental-mammal.md). A metatherian fossil need not fall within the living marsupial [crown group](crown-group.md).
