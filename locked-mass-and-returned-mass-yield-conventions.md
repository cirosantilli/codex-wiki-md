# Locked-mass and returned-mass yield conventions

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar yield](stellar-yield.md)

Let a fraction $R$ of gross stellar birth [mass](mass.md) return immediately, and let the new metal source be $bR\Psi$. The yield per permanently locked [mass](mass.md) is then $y=bR/(1-R)$. Metal conservation in an initially pristine closed box gives $Z=y\ln(M_0/M_g)$. A coefficient defined by the metal source must not be confused with a yield normalized per locked stellar [mass](mass.md); the source convention must be stated, especially when taking $R\to0$ or $R\to1$ limits.
