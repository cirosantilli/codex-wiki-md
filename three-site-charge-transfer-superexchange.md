# Three-site charge-transfer superexchange

↑ **Parent:** [Superexchange](superexchange.md)

In a minimal [hole](electron-hole.md) description of a metal–ligand–metal path, two [holes](electron-hole.md) initially occupy separate metal orbitals and the ligand has no [hole](electron-hole.md). Creating a virtual ligand [hole](electron-hole.md) costs a charge-transfer [energy](energy-split.md) $\Delta$. Two hops generate an effective metal–metal hopping of order $t_{pd}^2/\Delta$; two more hops through metal [double occupancy](double-occupancy.md) generate the antiferromagnetic scale in the title. Ligand [double occupancy](double-occupancy.md) adds a channel with denominator $2\Delta+U_p$. Orbital geometry and intra-atomic exchange determine when the antiferromagnetic channel dominates.
