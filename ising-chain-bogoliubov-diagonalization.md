# Ising-chain Bogoliubov diagonalization

↑ **Parent:** [Transverse-field Ising model](transverse-field-ising-model.md)

For a paired non-self-conjugate [momentum](momentum.md), rotate the [Ising-chain Nambu spinor](ising-chain-nambu-spinor.md) by an angle with $\cos\theta=(g-\cos k)/r_k$, $\sin\theta=-\sin k/r_k$ and $r_k=\sqrt{1+g^2-2g\cos k}$. Opposite angles at $k,-k$ preserve the [canonical anticommutation relations](canonical-anticommutation-relations.md). The result is $H=\sum_k\epsilon_k(\gamma_k^\dagger\gamma_k-1/2)$. Self-paired [momenta](momentum.md) have no pairing and are handled directly as number levels. Periodic spin-chain sectors additionally constrain [fermion parity](fermion-parity.md) and allowed mode quantization.
