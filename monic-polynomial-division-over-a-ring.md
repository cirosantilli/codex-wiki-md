# Monic polynomial division over a ring

↑ **Parent:** [Monic polynomial](monic-polynomial.md)

If $f(T)$ is monic of degree $k$ over a commutative [ring](ring.md), every polynomial has a unique expression $g=qf+r$ with $\deg r<k$. Cancel the highest remaining term successively; the leading coefficient one needs no inversion. Uniqueness follows because multiplication by a monic polynomial raises the degree of every nonzero polynomial by $k$, even when the ring has [zero divisors](zero-divisor.md). The same argument applies to an even central variable and central coefficients in a [graded commutative algebra](graded-commutative-algebra.md).
