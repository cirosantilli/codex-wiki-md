# Generalized homology theory

↑ **Parent:** [Homology (mathematics)](homology-split.md)

A [generalized homology theory](generalized-homology-theory.md) obeys the homotopy, exactness, suspension and wedge axioms, without imposing the ordinary dimension axiom. A [represented homology theory](represented-homology-theory.md) is obtained by taking [stable homotopy groups](stable-homotopy-group.md) after a [smash product of spectra](smash-product-of-spectra.md).
