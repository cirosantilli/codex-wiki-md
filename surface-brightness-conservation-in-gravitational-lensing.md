# Surface-brightness conservation in gravitational lensing

↑ **Parent:** [Gravitational lensing](gravitational-lensing-split.md)

In [geometric optics](geometrical-optics-split.md) without absorption, phase-space conservation preserves $I_\nu/\nu^3$ along a ray. Relative images of the same source at a common [redshift](redshift.md) therefore preserve [surface brightness](surface-brightness.md) while changing apparent angular area and integrated flux. Source structure and propagation effects can introduce observed wavelength dependence.
