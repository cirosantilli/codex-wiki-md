# Flat matter-radiation conformal solution

↑ **Parent:** [Friedmann equations](friedmann-equations.md)

For a spatially flat [FLRW metric](friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md) containing conserved [pressureless matter](pressureless-matter.md) and [radiation in cosmology](radiation-in-cosmology.md), with $a_0=1$ and [conformal time](conformal-time.md) zero at the [Big Bang](big-bang.md), the [Friedmann equation](friedmann-equations.md) gives $a'^2=H_0^2(\Omega_{M,0}a+\Omega_{R,0})$. Separating the positive square root gives the displayed [scale factor](scale-factor-cosmology.md). At [matter-radiation equality](matter-radiation-equality.md), $a_{\rm eq}=\Omega_{R,0}/\Omega_{M,0}$ and $\eta_{\rm eq}=2(\sqrt2-1)\sqrt{\Omega_{R,0}}/(H_0\Omega_{M,0})$. Here the prime denotes differentiation with respect to $\eta$, not $c\eta$.
