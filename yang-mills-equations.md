# Yang-Mills equations

↑ **Parent:** [Yang-Mills theory](yang-mills-theory.md)

Stationarity of the [Yang-Mills action](yang-mills-action.md) in the absence of sources gives $D_\mu F^{\mu\nu}=0$, using the adjoint [gauge covariant derivative](gauge-covariant-derivative.md). This dynamical equation differs from the geometric [gauge-theory Bianchi identity](gauge-theory-bianchi-identity.md).
