# Directed acyclic graph factorization

↑ **Parent:** [Causal directed acyclic graph](causal-directed-acyclic-graph.md)

A distribution factorizes according to a [Directed acyclic graph](directed-acyclic-graph.md) when its joint density is the product of the conditional density of each variable given its graphical parents. This factorization implies the graph's [global Markov property of a directed acyclic graph](global-markov-property-of-a-directed-acyclic-graph.md).
