# Right-inverse characterization of the axiom of choice

↑ **Parent:** [Right inverse](right-inverse.md)

The assertion that every [surjective function](surjective-function.md) has a [right inverse](right-inverse.md) is equivalent to the [axiom of choice](axiom-of-choice.md). Choice selects one member of each nonempty [fiber of a function](fiber-of-a-function.md), giving a right inverse. Conversely, for a family $(X_i)_{i\in I}$ of nonempty sets, the projection from the disjoint union $\{(i,x):i\in I,\ x\in X_i\}$ onto $I$ is surjective. A right inverse selects one element from each $X_i$, giving a choice function.
