# Reverse faulting

↑ **Parent:** [Dip-slip faulting](dip-slip-faulting.md)

Shortening accommodated by a hanging wall moving up relative to the footwall. The centre of its dip-slip [focal mechanism](focal-mechanism.md) is compressional under the usual lower-hemisphere convention. Low-angle [reverse faulting](reverse-faulting.md) is commonly called thrust faulting.
