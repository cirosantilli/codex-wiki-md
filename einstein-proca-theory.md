# Einstein-Proca theory

↑ **Parent:** [Einstein-Hilbert action](einstein-hilbert-action.md)

Einstein-Proca theory couples [general relativity](general-relativity-split.md) to a massive vector field governed by the [Proca action](proca-action.md). Its matter stress-energy tensor is

$$
T_{ab}=F_{ac}F_b{}^c-\frac14g_{ab}F_{cd}F^{cd}
+m^2\left(A_aA_b-\frac12g_{ab}A_cA^c\right).
$$

The [contracted Bianchi identity](contracted-bianchi-identity.md) makes the Einstein equation compatible with the covariant [Proca equation](proca-equation.md).
