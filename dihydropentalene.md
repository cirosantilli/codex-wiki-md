# Dihydropentalene

↑ **Parent:** [Pentalene](pentalene.md)

A $\mathrm{C_8H_8}$ hydrocarbon with two more hydrogen atoms than [pentalene](pentalene.md); double-bond isomers are possible. One and two [deprotonations](deprotonation.md) give chemically distinct hydropentalenide and [pentalenide](pentalenide.md) charge states.
