# Finite intersection witness for cross-intersecting families

↑ **Parent:** [Cross-intersecting family](cross-intersecting-family.md)

For [cross-intersecting families](cross-intersecting-family.md) $\mathcal A,\mathcal B$ with member sizes at most $r,s$ respectively, there is a finite [set](set-split.md) $X$ with $|X|\leq r\sum_{j=0}^s r^j$ such that $A\cap B\cap X\ne\varnothing$ for every $A\in\mathcal A,B\in\mathcal B$. Take a [bounded-size transversal kernel](bounded-size-transversal-kernel.md) $\mathcal A_0$ preserving [hitting sets](hitting-set.md) of size at most $s$ and set $X=\bigcup_{A\in\mathcal A_0}A$. For each $B$, the [set](set-split.md) $B\cap X$ is a [hitting set](hitting-set.md) for $\mathcal A_0$, hence for all of $\mathcal A$. This proves the assertion even for infinite [set families](set-family.md); if either family is empty, take $X=\varnothing$.
