# Dirac spinor

↑ **Parent:** [Dirac field](dirac-field.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dirac_spinor)

A Dirac spinor transforms as $(1/2,0)\oplus(0,1/2)$, so it combines the two [Weyl chiralities](weyl-spinor.md) into a parity-invariant representation.
