# Term symbol

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic physics](atomic-physics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Term_symbol)

In [LS coupling](ls-coupling.md), an atomic term symbol specifies total electronic [spin](spin.md) $S$, total [orbital angular momentum](orbital-angular-momentum.md) $L$, and total [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) $J$. The superscript $2S+1$ is the spin multiplicity; the letters $S,P,D,F,\ldots$ denote $L=0,1,2,3,\ldots$. Coupling gives $J=|L-S|,\ldots,L+S$, and a fixed-$J$ level has $2J+1$ magnetic substates before an external field resolves them. For example, $^3P$ has $S=L=1$ and levels $J=0,1,2$, containing $1+3+5=9$ states altogether. A term symbol does not itself specify the radial [electron configuration](electron-configuration.md).
