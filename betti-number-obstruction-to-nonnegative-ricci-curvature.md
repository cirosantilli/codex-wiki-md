# Betti-number obstruction to nonnegative Ricci curvature

↑ **Parent:** [Dimension bound for harmonic one-forms under nonnegative Ricci curvature](dimension-bound-for-harmonic-one-forms-under-nonnegative-ricci-curvature.md)

On a closed connected [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md) with nonnegative [Ricci curvature](ricci-curvature.md), [harmonic one-forms are parallel under nonnegative Ricci curvature](harmonic-one-forms-are-parallel-under-nonnegative-ricci-curvature.md). Evaluation at one point injects their space into the [cotangent space](cotangent-space.md). The [Hodge decomposition theorem](hodge-decomposition-theorem.md) identifies its dimension with the first [Betti number](betti-number.md), giving $b_1(M)\le\dim M$. For example, a genus-two closed orientable surface times a [circle](circle.md) has dimension three and first [Betti number](betti-number.md) five, so no [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md) on that product has nonnegative [Ricci curvature](ricci-curvature.md).
