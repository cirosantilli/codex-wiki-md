# Topological disjoint union

↑ **Parent:** [Topology](topology-split.md)

The topological disjoint union of [topological spaces](topological-space.md) $X_i$ has the labelled [disjoint union](disjoint-union.md) as its underlying [set](set-split.md). A [subset](subset.md) is [open](open-set.md) exactly when its [intersection](set-intersection.md) with each labelled copy $X_i$ is [open](open-set.md) in that copy. Each copy is a [clopen set](clopen-set.md), and a [function](function-split.md) from the topological disjoint union is [continuous](continuous-function.md) exactly when its [restriction](restriction-mathematics.md) to every copy is [continuous](continuous-function.md). This follows immediately by testing [preimages](preimage.md) of [open sets](open-set.md) on each copy. A [topological space](topological-space.md) is [homeomorphic](homeomorphism.md) to the topological disjoint union of its [connected components](connected-component.md) exactly when all those [connected components](connected-component.md) are [open](open-set.md).
