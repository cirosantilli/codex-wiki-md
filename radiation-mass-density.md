# Radiation mass density

↑ **Parent:** [Effective number of relativistic energy degrees of freedom](effective-number-of-relativistic-energy-degrees-of-freedom.md)

For relativistic species at a common temperature, the mass-equivalent energy density is $\rho=\pi^2g_*(k_BT)^4/(30\hbar^3c^5)$. Using mass rather than energy density accounts for the powers of $c$ in the [Friedmann equation](friedmann-equations.md).
