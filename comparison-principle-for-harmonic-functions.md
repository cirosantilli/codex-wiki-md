# Comparison principle for harmonic functions

↑ **Parent:** [Maximum principle for harmonic functions](maximum-principle-for-harmonic-functions.md)

If $u,v$ are [harmonic functions](harmonic-function.md) on a bounded domain and continuous on its closure, their difference is a [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md). Its minimum is attained on the boundary by the [maximum principle for harmonic functions](maximum-principle-for-harmonic-functions.md) applied to its negative. Nonnegative boundary difference therefore implies nonnegative interior difference. Applying the comparison in both directions proves uniqueness for the [Dirichlet problem](dirichlet-problem.md) whenever a classical solution exists. This is an order statement and does not itself establish existence.
