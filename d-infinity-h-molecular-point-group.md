# D infinity h molecular point group

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular symmetry](molecular-symmetry.md)

The [molecular point group](molecular-point-group.md) of a centrosymmetric linear [molecule](molecule-split.md). Longitudinal coordinates have $\Sigma$ symmetry; their behaviour under inversion about the [inversion centre](inversion-centre.md) supplies the $g$ or $u$ label, and a vertical reflection supplies the sign on a $\Sigma$ representation.
