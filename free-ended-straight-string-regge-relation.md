# Free-ended straight-string Regge relation

↑ **Parent:** [Rotating Nambu–Goto string](rotating-nambu-goto-string.md)

With [string tension](string-tension.md) $T=1/(2\pi\alpha')$, the free-ended [open string](open-string.md) $X^0=a\tau$, $X^1+iX^2=a\cos\sigma\,e^{i\tau}$, $0\leq\sigma\leq\pi$, is straight at every target time. Its [Nambu–Goto equations of motion](nambu-goto-equations-of-motion.md) and [Virasoro constraints](virasoro-constraint.md) hold, and its ends move at light speed. Integrating the [target-space Noether charges of a string](target-space-noether-charges-of-a-string.md) gives $M=\pi Ta$ and $J=\pi Ta^2/2$. Thus $J=\alpha'M^2$. Extending the same map to $0\leq\sigma\leq2\pi$ describes a double-covered folded [closed string](closed-string.md), with twice the mass and twice the [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md), hence $J=\alpha'M^2/2$. The different slopes record the different boundary topology.
