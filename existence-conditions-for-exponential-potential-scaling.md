# Existence conditions for exponential-potential scaling

↑ **Parent:** [Exponential-potential scalar-dominated power-law solution](exponential-potential-scalar-dominated-power-law-solution.md)

For $V_0\ne0$, real nonzero $\lambda$, and a finite positive logarithmic time scale $t_0$, the [exponential-potential scalar-dominated power-law solution](exponential-potential-scalar-dominated-power-law-solution.md) requires the displayed strict sign condition. Thus positive $V_0$ permits $0<\lambda^2<6$, while negative $V_0$ permits $\lambda^2>6$. The boundary $\lambda^2=6$ cannot occur with nonzero $V_0$ in this ansatz. Setting $\lambda=0$ gives a constant potential and a separate branch, including constant-field [de Sitter spacetime](de-sitter-spacetime.md) for $V_0>0$.
