# Neon-22 neutron excess from nitrogen-14

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar nucleosynthesis](stellar-nucleosynthesis.md)

Two alpha captures and an intervening [beta-plus decay](beta-plus-decay.md) turn each nitrogen-14 nucleus into neon-22, incorporating eight baryons from [helium](helium.md) and converting one [proton](proton.md) to a [neutron](neutron.md). With no loss or dilution and nuclear [mass](mass.md) defects neglected, a nitrogen-14 [mass](mass.md) fraction $X_{14}$ therefore yields neon-22 [mass](mass.md) fraction $(22/14)X_{14}$, not $X_{14}$. In otherwise symmetric carbon-oxygen matter this gives the displayed [electron fraction in stellar matter](electron-fraction-in-stellar-matter.md). A final [neon](neon.md) [mass](mass.md) fraction and a pre-helium-burning metallicity must not silently be treated as identical.
