<h1 id="time-independent-schrodinger-equation">Time-independent Schrödinger equation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Schrödinger equation](schrodinger-equation.md)

For a time-independent [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) $H$, an [energy eigenstate](energy-eigenstate.md) satisfies

$$
H\psi=E\psi.
$$

For a one-dimensional particle of [mass](mass.md) $m$ in a real [potential energy](potential-energy.md) $U(x)$,

$$
-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\psi''+U(x)\psi=E\psi.
$$

Its full time-dependent [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) is $\Psi(x,t)=\psi(x)e^{-iEt/\hbar}$: substitution in the [Schrödinger equation](schrodinger-equation.md) checks both the minus sign and the factor $\hbar$. Thus an [energy eigenstate](energy-eigenstate.md) has stationary position [probability density](probability-density.md), even though its [quantum phase](quantum-phase.md) evolves. The [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md) and [operator domain](operator-domain.md) are part of this [eigenvalue problem](eigenvalue-problem.md), selecting physically allowed solutions; the [infinite square well](infinite-square-well.md) and [Radial Schrodinger equation for the hydrogen atom](radial-schrodinger-equation-for-the-hydrogen-atom.md) give complementary confined examples.

For a complete discrete [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) of [energy eigenstates](energy-eigenstate.md), [linearity](linearity.md) gives $\Psi(x,t)=\sum_n c_n\psi_n(x)e^{-iE_nt/\hbar}$, with $\sum_n|c_n|^2=1$. Cross terms in $|\Psi|^2$ oscillate at [angular frequencies](angular-frequency.md) $(E_n-E_m)/\hbar$. A [continuous spectrum](continuous-spectrum.md) requires the corresponding spectral integrals; for a [free particle](free-particle.md) on the whole line, individual [plane waves](plane-wave.md) are generalized [eigenfunctions](eigenfunction.md), and a normalized [Gaussian wave packet](gaussian-wave-packet.md) is their integral [quantum superposition](quantum-superposition.md). Adding a spatially constant real $C$ to $U$ shifts every [energy eigenvalue](energy-eigenvalue-split.md) by $C$ and multiplies the full state by $e^{-iCt/\hbar}$, a [global phase](global-phase.md); [energy](energy-split.md) differences, position [probabilities](probability.md) and [probability current](probability-current.md) are unchanged.
