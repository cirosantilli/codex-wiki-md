# Secretory protein routing

↑ **Parent:** [Endoplasmic reticulum](endoplasmic-reticulum.md)

[Secretory protein routing](secretory-protein-routing.md) connects entry into the [endoplasmic reticulum](endoplasmic-reticulum.md), [protein folding](protein-folding.md) and sorting, transfer to the [Golgi apparatus](golgi-apparatus.md), and delivery onward. In the conventional soluble-cargo route, [transport vesicles](transport-vesicle.md) carry lumenal contents between compartments; [exocytosis](exocytosis.md) releases cargo outside the [cell membrane](cell-membrane-split.md). Resident [endoplasmic reticulum](endoplasmic-reticulum.md) [proteins](protein-split.md) can be retained or retrieved, and defective cargo can be held back. Many [cytosol](cytosol.md) [proteins](protein-split.md) never enter this route.[The Cell: A Molecular Approach, 2nd edition by Geoffrey M. Cooper; Protein Folding and Processing in the ER, Figure 9.14; Export of Proteins and Lipids from the ER, Figures 9.20 and 9.21](the-cell-a-molecular-approach-2nd-edition-by-geoffrey-m-cooper/the-endoplasmic-reticulum.md)

**[Figure 8](#image-secretory-protein-routing-preserves-membrane-sides). Secretory protein routing preserves membrane sides**. Original conceptual route for soluble cargo and a [membrane protein](membrane-protein.md). Intermediate compartments and return routes are omitted. Arrows show connectivity, not measured timing, fluorescence or [electron microscopy](electron-microscopy.md).

The useful geometric rule is preservation of membrane sides: during budding and fusion, a lumen-facing region of a [membrane protein](membrane-protein.md) stays lumen-facing, then faces the exterior after [exocytosis](exocytosis.md); its [cytosol](cytosol.md)-facing region stays cytosolic.[The Cell: A Molecular Approach, 2nd edition by Geoffrey M. Cooper; Insertion of Proteins into the ER Membrane, Figure 9.10; Export of Proteins and Lipids from the ER: preservation of orientation](the-cell-a-molecular-approach-2nd-edition-by-geoffrey-m-cooper/the-endoplasmic-reticulum.md)

Work through the schematic using two distinguishable labels A and B on a stipulated [membrane protein](membrane-protein.md). Start with A facing the [ER lumen](er-lumen.md) and B facing the [cytosol](cytosol.md). At each budding or fusion step, carry the surrounding membrane together with the labels; do not turn the [protein](protein-split.md) over. The resulting pair is A facing the [Golgi apparatus](golgi-apparatus.md) interior and B facing the [cytosol](cytosol.md), then A facing a [transport vesicle](transport-vesicle.md) interior and B facing the [cytosol](cytosol.md), and finally A facing the exterior and B facing the [cytosol](cytosol.md). This is a stepwise argument about the depicted operations, not a claim that the compartments have identical chemical contents. Likewise, once soluble cargo is inside the [ER lumen](er-lumen.md), this schematic reaches the exterior with zero further crossings through a [lipid bilayer](lipid-bilayer.md): enclosed spaces join when membranes fuse. Delivery therefore need not mean repeatedly pushing a folded [protein](protein-split.md) through a membrane.
