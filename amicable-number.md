# Amicable number

↑ **Parent:** [Divisor function](divisor-function.md)

Two distinct [positive integers](positive-integer.md) $a,b$ are amicable when the [sum](sum.md) of the [proper divisors](proper-divisor.md) of $a$ is $b$ and the [sum](sum.md) of the [proper divisors](proper-divisor.md) of $b$ is $a$. The pair $220,284$ is an example. Iterating the proper-divisor [sum](sum.md) can also produce longer [directed cycles](directed-cycle.md).
