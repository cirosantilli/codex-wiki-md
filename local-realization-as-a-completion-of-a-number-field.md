# Local realization as a completion of a number field

↑ **Parent:** [Krasner's lemma](krasner-s-lemma.md)

Every finite extension of $\mathbb Q_p$ is isomorphic to the completion $F_{\mathfrak p}$ of some number field $F$ at a prime $\mathfrak p$ above $p$. Approximate a primitive element's minimal polynomial by a polynomial over $\mathbb Q$ and use [Krasner's lemma](krasner-s-lemma.md) to preserve the generated local field.
