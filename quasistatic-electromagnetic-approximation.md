# Quasistatic electromagnetic approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Electromagnetism](electromagnetism-split.md)

A [quasistatic electromagnetic approximation](quasistatic-electromagnetic-approximation.md) neglects propagation delay over a vacuum device of characteristic size $L$ when $\omega L/c\ll1$. It does not automatically discard every time [derivative](derivative.md): an [Ampere-Maxwell law](ampere-s-circuital-law.md) calculation in a capacitor gap must retain [displacement current](displacement-current.md). In a medium use the relevant propagation and response scales. The dominant approximately static [electric field](electric-field-split.md) can be used to calculate a smaller induced [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md), provided the corrections to the dominant field remain small. Near a resonance or for a large distributed circuit, the size criterion alone may not justify an instantaneous lumped model.
