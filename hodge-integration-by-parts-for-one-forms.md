# Hodge integration by parts for one-forms

↑ **Parent:** [Codifferential](codifferential.md)

On an oriented boundaryless [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md), apply [Stokes theorem](stokes-theorem.md) to the compactly supported form $f*\alpha$. The [graded Leibniz rule](graded-leibniz-rule.md) gives $\int df\wedge*\alpha=-\int f\,d*\alpha$. The [Hodge star operator](hodge-star-operator.md) identifies these top forms with the scalar integrands displayed above. This proves that the [codifferential](codifferential.md) on one-forms is $\delta=-*d*$, the [formal adjoint](formal-adjoint.md) of the [exterior derivative](exterior-derivative.md). Compact support of $f$ suffices.
