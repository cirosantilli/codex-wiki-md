# Insulin receptor substrate

↑ **Parent:** [Insulin receptor](insulin-receptor.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Insulin_receptor_substrate)

An [insulin receptor substrate](insulin-receptor-substrate.md) is a docking [protein](protein.md) phosphorylated by the activated [insulin receptor](insulin-receptor.md). Its [phosphotyrosine residues](phosphotyrosine-residue.md) recruit downstream signaling [proteins](protein.md), including [SH2 domain](sh2-domain.md)-containing components of [phosphoinositide 3-kinase](phosphoinositide-3-kinase.md). This connects reception to the pathway described under [insulin](insulin.md), rather than making the [insulin receptor substrate](insulin-receptor-substrate.md) another extracellular [ligand](ligand-biochemistry.md).[“Linking RTK activation to cell signaling”: IRS docking proteins and Figure 3A](cell-signaling-by-receptor-tyrosine-kinases-by-mark-a-lemmon-and-joseph-schlessinger.md)
