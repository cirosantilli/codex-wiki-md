# Right singular vector

↑ **Parent:** [Singular value](singular-value.md)

A unit right singular vector of a [matrix](matrix.md) or [compact operator](compact-operator-split.md) $A$ is an [eigenvector](eigenvector.md) of $A^*A$ with [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) $\sigma_j^2$. For $\sigma_j>0$, $u_j=Av_j/\sigma_j$ is the corresponding [left singular vector](left-singular-vector.md). The largest [singular value](singular-value.md) is the [operator norm](operator-norm.md) of $A$, attained on its corresponding [right singular vectors](right-singular-vector.md).
