# Hyperbolic attracting limit cycle

↑ **Parent:** [Limit cycle](limit-cycle.md)

For an autonomous [ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md), the tangent to a [periodic orbit](periodic-orbit.md) gives a unit [Floquet multiplier](floquet-multiplier.md). A hyperbolic attracting cycle has that multiplier simple and all transverse multipliers strictly inside the unit disk. Thus the periodic variational operator has a one-dimensional [null space](kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) spanned by the tangent. Nonlinear asymptotic attraction without [hyperbolicity](hyperbolicity.md) does not suffice: $\dot r=-(r-1)^3$, $\dot\varphi=2\pi$ attracts to a unit-period circle but its radial [variational equation](variational-equation.md) is neutral.
