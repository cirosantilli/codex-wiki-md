# Lac operon

↑ **Parent:** [Regulation of gene expression](regulation-of-gene-expression.md)

An [Escherichia coli](escherichia-coli.md) transcription unit encoding lactose-use proteins. Expression integrates [LacI](laci.md)-dependent repression, [allolactose](allolactose.md) induction, [catabolite activator protein](catabolite-activator-protein.md) activation and [inducer exclusion](inducer-exclusion.md).
