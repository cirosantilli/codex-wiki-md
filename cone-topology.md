# Cone (topology)

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic topology](algebraic-topology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cone_(topology))

The cone of a [topological space](topological-space.md) $X$ is $CX=(X\times[0,1])/(X\times\{0\})$, with one end collapsed to a vertex. Realization of a [simplicial cone](simplicial-cone.md) gives this construction for the realized base complex.
