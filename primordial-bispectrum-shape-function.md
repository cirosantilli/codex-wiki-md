# Primordial bispectrum shape function

↑ **Parent:** [Primordial bispectrum](primordial-bispectrum.md)

For a scale-invariant [primordial bispectrum](primordial-bispectrum.md), the displayed shape function is invariant under common rescaling of all three wavenumbers. It isolates triangle dependence from the overall [power spectrum](power-spectrum.md) amplitude. It indicates intrinsic shape preference but alone does not determine observational signal-to-noise, which also depends on transfer functions, [covariance](covariance.md) and survey weights.
