<h1 id="evans-saksena-reduction">Evans–Saksena reduction</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Sodium borohydride](sodium-borohydride.md)

A hydroxyl-directed reduction of a β-hydroxy [ketone](ketone.md) with an acetoxyborohydride reagent, favouring an anti-1,3-diol through intramolecular hydride delivery.
