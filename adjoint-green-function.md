# Adjoint Green function

↑ **Parent:** [Green's function](green-s-function-split.md)

An adjoint [Green's function](green-s-function-split.md) solves the corresponding adjoint point-source problem, with boundary or terminal conditions chosen so that [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) gives a representation for a solution of the original operator. For $L=\partial_t-\partial_x^2+c$ on the real line, the formal adjoint is $L^*=-\partial_t-\partial_x^2+c$. The backward kernel with terminal [Dirac delta](dirac-delta-function.md) at $(\xi,\tau)$ is the translated [heat kernel with constant absorption](heat-kernel-with-constant-absorption.md), defined for $t<\tau$. Its pairing with a homogeneous forward solution is constant in $t$; taking the terminal limit evaluates that solution at $(\xi,\tau)$.
