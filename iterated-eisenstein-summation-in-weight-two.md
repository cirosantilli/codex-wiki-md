# Iterated Eisenstein summation in weight two

↑ **Parent:** [Eisenstein series of weight two](eisenstein-series-of-weight-two.md)

In the displayed iterated order, with the $n$ sum evaluated first, $G_2(z)=(\pi^2/3)E_2(z)$. The [cosecant partial-fraction identity](cosecant-partial-fraction-identity.md) $\sum_n(w+n)^{-2}=\pi^2\csc^2(\pi w)$ gives the positive-$m$ contribution $-4\pi^2\sum_{r\geq1}r q^{mr}$; negative $m$ gives the same contribution. Including the $m=0$ term yields $\pi^2/3-8\pi^2\sum_{n\geq1}\sigma_1(n)q^n$. The full lattice series does not have [absolute convergence](absolute-convergence.md), so this order must not be replaced by arbitrary rearrangement.
