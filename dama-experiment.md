# DAMA experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Dark-matter direct detection](dark-matter-direct-detection.md)

An underground NaI(Tl) [particle scintillation](scintillation-physics.md) experiment seeking annual variation in low-energy single-hit rates. Its reported modulation is an observable; mapping it to a [WIMP](weakly-interacting-massive-particle.md) [mass](mass.md) and [cross section](cross-section-physics.md) requires assumptions about the interaction, halo distribution and detector recoil response. Multiple-hit and other-energy samples test some backgrounds, but [particle scintillation](scintillation-physics.md) alone does not label every interaction as a [nuclear recoil](nuclear-recoil.md).[single-hit and multiple-hit annual-modulation comparison](new-results-from-dama-libra-by-r-bernabei-and-others.md)
