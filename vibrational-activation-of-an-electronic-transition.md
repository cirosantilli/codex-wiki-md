# Vibrational activation of an electronic transition

↑ **Parent:** [Vibronic coupling](vibronic-coupling.md)

An otherwise forbidden [electronic transition](electronic-transition.md) gains intensity when accompanying [molecular vibration](molecular-vibration.md) supplies a symmetry factor that permits a nonzero [electric dipole transition moment](electric-dipole-transition-moment.md). A symmetry-allowed total [vibronic state](vibronic-state.md) specifies a possible transition, while the coupling determines its actual strength.
