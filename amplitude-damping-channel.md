# Amplitude damping channel

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum channel](quantum-channel.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amplitude_damping_channel)

A zero-temperature energy-relaxation channel transfers the excited population of a [qubit](qubit.md) to its ground state. Its [Kraus operators](kraus-operator.md) are $A_0=\operatorname{diag}(1,\sqrt{1-p})$ and $A_1=\sqrt p|0\rangle\langle1|$ for $0\leq p\leq1$. They satisfy $\sum_jA_j^\dagger A_j=I$. The excited population is multiplied by $1-p$ and coherences by $\sqrt{1-p}$. For nonzero $p$, it is not a [unital quantum channel](unital-quantum-channel.md).
