<h1 id="cech-complete-spaces-are-baire-spaces">Čech-complete spaces are Baire spaces</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Čech-complete space](cech-complete-space.md)

Write the dense space $X$ as $\bigcap_nG_n$ in compact Hausdorff $K$. Every $G_n$ is dense and open. A dense open subset $U_n$ of $X$ has the form $X\cap O_n$ with $O_n$ dense and open in $K$. The compact [Baire category theorem](baire-category-theorem.md) makes $\bigcap_n(G_n\cap O_n)=\bigcap_nU_n$ dense in $K$, hence in $X$. Therefore every [Čech-complete space](cech-complete-space.md) is a [Baire space](baire-space.md). The rationals are not a [Baire space](baire-space.md), as deleting their points one at a time gives dense open sets with empty intersection.
