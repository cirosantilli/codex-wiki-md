# Mean-temperature correction in weakly nonlinear Darcy convection

↑ **Parent:** [Supercritical saturation of Darcy convection rolls](supercritical-saturation-of-darcy-convection-rolls.md)

For $\psi_1=\cos(\pi x)\sin(\pi z)$, the first temperature mode is $\theta_1=(2\pi)^{-1}\sin(\pi x)\sin(\pi z)$. The [streamfunction advection bracket](streamfunction-advection-bracket.md) is $J(\psi_1,\theta_1)=(\pi/4)\sin(2\pi z)$, independent of $x$. The second-order equations therefore have $\psi_2=0$ and

$$
\theta_2=-\frac1{16\pi}\sin(2\pi z).
$$

This modifies the vertical temperature gradient. Its interaction with the critical roll produces the cubic term in the [amplitude equation](amplitude-equation.md).
