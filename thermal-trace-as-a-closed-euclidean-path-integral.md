# Thermal trace as a closed Euclidean path integral

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean path integral](euclidean-path-integral.md)

Taking the [trace](matrix-trace.md) of the position-space imaginary-time kernel identifies its endpoints and integrates over the common [position](position.md). The resulting closed paths have imaginary-time [length](length.md) $\beta\hbar$. Bosonic coherent fields are periodic, while fermionic coherent fields are antiperiodic. This temporal condition follows from the [trace](matrix-trace.md), independently of the spatial [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md).
