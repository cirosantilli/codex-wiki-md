# Lava lake

↑ **Parent:** [Magma](magma.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lava_lake)

A lava lake is a persistent exposed body of molten or partly molten [magma](magma.md). Surface [radiative cooling](radiative-cooling.md), [thermal convection](thermal-convection.md), [latent heat](latent-heat.md), and internal [radiogenic heating](radiogenic-heating.md) can jointly control formation and melting of its crust.
