# Type-II seesaw mechanism

↑ **Parent:** [Seesaw mechanism](seesaw-mechanism.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Type-II_seesaw_mechanism)

The type-II seesaw mechanism couples two lepton doublets to an [electroweak scalar triplet](electroweak-scalar-triplet.md). A small neutral-triplet vacuum expectation value directly generates a left-handed neutrino [Majorana mass term](majorana-mass-term.md).
