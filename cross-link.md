# Cross-link

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer](polymer-split.md)

A cross-link connects different segments of a [polymer](polymer-split.md) network and restricts their independent motion. Permanent chemical cross-links commonly use [covalent bonds](covalent-bond-split.md); physical associations can instead have finite lifetimes. A connected permanent network can sustain long-time shear [stress](stress.md), whereas an uncrosslinked melt eventually flows. Cross-link density and network structure influence [elastomer](elastomer.md) stiffness.
