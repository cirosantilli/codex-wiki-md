# Kolmogorov equation for structure functions

↑ **Parent:** [Kármán-Howarth equation](karman-howarth-equation.md)

For unforced [homogeneous turbulence](homogeneous-turbulence.md) with [isotropic turbulence](isotropic-turbulence.md) statistics, put $F=u^2f=u^2-S_2/2$ and $S_3=6u^3K$. The longitudinal [Kármán-Howarth equation](karman-howarth-equation.md) is $F_t=r^{-4}\partial_r[r^4(S_3/6-\nu S_2')]$. Since $\partial_tu^2=-2\epsilon/3$, regularity at zero gives

$$
S_3-6\nu S_2'=-\frac45\epsilon r-\frac3{r^4}\int_0^r s^4\partial_tS_2(s,t)\,ds.
$$

Neglecting the last term in a locally equilibrated small-scale range gives the displayed equation. The [Kolmogorov four-fifths law](kolmogorov-four-fifths-law.md) follows where the viscous term is negligible as well.
