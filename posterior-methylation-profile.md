# Posterior methylation profile

↑ **Parent:** [DNA methylation](dna-methylation.md)

A [posterior methylation profile](posterior-methylation-profile.md) gives the [posterior probability](posterior-probability.md) of [DNA methylation](dna-methylation.md) at each modeled site. It differs from one sampled binary pattern and from a maximum-probability joint pattern.
