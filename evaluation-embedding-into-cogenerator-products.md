# Evaluation embedding into cogenerator products

↑ **Parent:** [Cogenerating set](cogenerating-set.md)

For a [small cogenerating family](cogenerating-set.md) $(Q_i)$ in a [locally small category](locally-small-category.md), the evaluation arrow $C\to\prod_{i,q\in\mathcal C(C,Q_i)}Q_i$ is a [monomorphism](monomorphism.md) whenever that small [product in a category](product-category-theory.md) exists. Equality after all its projections is equality after every map to a cogenerator, hence equality of the original parallel [morphisms](morphism.md).
