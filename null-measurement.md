# Null measurement

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical measurement](electrical-measurement.md)

A null measurement adjusts a known comparison until a detector indicates zero difference. In a [resistance bridge](resistance-bridge.md), the unknown [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) follows from the settings at balance rather than from a calibrated nonzero detector reading. A working detector must respond on both sides of the null: a disconnected [galvanometer](galvanometer.md) also reads zero, but supplies no evidence of balance.
