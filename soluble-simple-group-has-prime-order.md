# Soluble simple group has prime order

↑ **Parent:** [Simple group](simple-group.md)

For a nontrivial simple [soluble group](solvable-group.md), the [commutator subgroup](commutator-subgroup.md) is either the identity or the whole group. It cannot be the whole group because the [derived series](derived-series.md) would never terminate. The group is therefore [Abelian](abelian-group.md). Every nonidentity element then generates a normal cyclic subgroup, which must be the whole group. An infinite cyclic group has proper nontrivial subgroups; a finite cyclic group is simple exactly when its order is prime. Thus the soluble simple groups are precisely the cyclic groups of prime order.
