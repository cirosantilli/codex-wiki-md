# Sorting

↑ **Parent:** [Algorithm](algorithm-split.md)

Rearranging a finite [sequence](sequence.md) according to a specified order while preserving its entries and their multiplicities. A [sorting algorithm](sorting-algorithm.md) performs this operation; [lexicographic order](lexicographic-order.md) supplies a common order for tuples or strings.
