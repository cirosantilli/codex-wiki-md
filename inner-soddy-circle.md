# Inner Soddy circle

↑ **Parent:** [Soddy circle](soddy-circle.md)

Given three mutually externally tangent [circles](circle.md), the inner Soddy circle lies in their curvilinear triangular gap and is tangent to all three. The [Descartes' circle theorem](descartes-circle-theorem.md) determines its radius from the three given radii.
