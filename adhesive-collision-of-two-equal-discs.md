# Adhesive collision of two equal discs

↑ **Parent:** [Inelastic collision](inelastic-collision.md)

Two uniform discs of [mass](mass.md) m and radius a stick at first edge contact. One initially translates at [speed](speed.md) v and the other rests, with neither initially spinning. If the incoming [velocity](velocity.md) makes angle $\theta$ to the line of centres, the composite axial [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) about its [centre of mass](center-of-mass.md) is $3ma^2$. Conserved angular-momentum magnitude is $mav|\sin\theta|$, so the [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md) magnitude is $v|\sin\theta|/(3a)$. The centre-of-mass [speed](speed.md) is v/2. Splitting [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) into translation and rotation gives $K_f=mv^2(1/4+\sin^2\theta/6)$. The difference from $mv^2/2$ is dissipated internally; it cannot be removed from angular-momentum conservation.
