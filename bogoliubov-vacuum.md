# Bogoliubov vacuum

↑ **Parent:** [Bogoliubov transformation](bogoliubov-transformation.md)

A Bogoliubov vacuum is annihilated by the [quasiparticle](quasiparticle.md) [linear operators](linear-operator.md) that diagonalize a stable [quadratic bosonic Hamiltonian](quadratic-bosonic-hamiltonian.md). If $a_k=u_kb_k-v_kb_{-k}^\dagger$, its original-mode [occupation number](occupation-number.md) is $|v_k|^2$. It is generally a [multimode squeezed vacuum](multimode-squeezed-vacuum.md) rather than the original [Fock vacuum](fock-vacuum.md). In infinitely many modes, existence in the original [Fock space](fock-space.md) requires a separate convergence analysis.
