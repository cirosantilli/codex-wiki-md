# Augmentation ideal

↑ **Parent:** [Group ring](group-ring.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augmentation_ideal)

The augmentation homomorphism $\varepsilon:R[G]\to R$ sends $\sum_gr_gg$ to $\sum_gr_g$. Its kernel is the augmentation ideal.
