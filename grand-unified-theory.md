# Grand unified theory

↑ **Parent:** [Gauge theory](gauge-theory.md)

A theory embedding the [Standard Model](standard-model-split.md) gauge factors in a larger gauge group. Particular embeddings can relate couplings and matter multiplets or predict [proton decay](proton-decay.md). The unification hypothesis alone does not specify a unique [mass](mass.md) spectrum or an experimentally established extension.
