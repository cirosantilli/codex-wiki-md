# Divisibility of Lebesgue measure

↑ **Parent:** [Lebesgue measure](lebesgue-measure.md)

If $E\subseteq[0,1]$ is a [Lebesgue measurable set](lebesgue-measurable-set.md) and $0\leq b\leq\lambda(E)$, some measurable [subset](subset.md) $B\subseteq E$ has [Lebesgue measure](lebesgue-measure.md) $b$. The function $F(t)=\lambda(E\cap[0,t])$ has [Lipschitz continuity](lipschitz-continuity.md) with constant $1$, starts at $0$ and ends at $\lambda(E)$; the [intermediate value theorem](intermediate-value-theorem.md) proves the assertion. Applying the assertion successively to remaining portions of $E$ gives pairwise disjoint measurable pieces with any finite list of nonnegative measures whose sum is at most $\lambda(E)$. This is a concrete consequence of [non-atomic measure](non-atomic-measure.md) structure, which fails for general [measures](measure.md) containing an [atom of a measure](atom-measure-theory.md).
