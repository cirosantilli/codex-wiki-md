# Top-to-random card shuffle

↑ **Parent:** [Random-to-top card shuffle](random-to-top-card-shuffle.md)

The top-to-random shuffle removes the top card and inserts it in a uniformly random position. Under the uniform measure on permutations it is the time reversal of the [random-to-top card shuffle](random-to-top-card-shuffle.md), so the two shuffles have the same total-variation mixing profile.
