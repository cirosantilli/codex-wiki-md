# Scalar spectral measure

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral measure of a normal operator](spectral-measure-of-a-normal-operator.md)

For a [projection-valued measure](projection-valued-measure.md) $E$ and vectors $v,w$, the scalar spectral measure is the complex measure $\mu_{v,w}(B)=\langle E(B)v,w\rangle$. The positive measure $\mu_v=\mu_{v,v}$ has total mass $\|v\|^2$.
