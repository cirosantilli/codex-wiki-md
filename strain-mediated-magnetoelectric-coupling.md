# Strain-mediated magnetoelectric coupling

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetostriction](magnetostriction.md)

Coupling in which a magnetic material's preferred [strain](strain.md) transfers mechanical [stress](stress.md) to a piezoelectric constituent, producing an electrical response. It depends on interfaces, elastic load sharing and electrical boundary conditions; a composite's measured [voltage](voltage.md) is not by itself an intrinsic single-phase magnetoelectric coefficient.
