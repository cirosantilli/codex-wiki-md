# Potential slow-roll parameter

↑ **Parent:** [Slow-roll approximation](slow-roll-approximation.md)

The potential slow-roll parameter is

$$
\epsilon_V=\frac{M_{\rm Pl}^2}{2}
\left(\frac{V'}V\right)^2.
$$

Under the slow-roll equations, $\dot\phi^2/(2V)\simeq\epsilon_V/3$.
