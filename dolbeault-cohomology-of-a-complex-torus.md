# Dolbeault cohomology of a complex torus

↑ **Parent:** [Dolbeault cohomology](dolbeault-cohomology.md)

For a [complex torus](complex-torus.md) $X=V/\Lambda$ of complex dimension $g$, constant forms give a natural isomorphism $H^{p,q}_{\bar\partial}(X)\cong\bigwedge^pV^*\otimes\bigwedge^q\overline{V^*}$. A flat [Kähler metric](kahler-metric.md) and the [Dolbeault Hodge decomposition](dolbeault-hodge-decomposition-on-a-compact-hermitian-manifold.md) prove that these constant forms are precisely the harmonic representatives. Thus $h^{p,q}=\binom gp\binom gq$ for $0\leq p,q\leq g$, and zero otherwise.
