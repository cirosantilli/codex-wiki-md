# Principal stretch

↑ **Parent:** [Stretch tensor](stretch-tensor.md)

The principal stretches are the [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) of a [stretch tensor](stretch-tensor.md), or equivalently the [singular values](singular-value.md) of the [deformation gradient](deformation-gradient.md). They measure local length ratios along the principal reference directions.
