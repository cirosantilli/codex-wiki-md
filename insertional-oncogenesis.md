# Insertional oncogenesis

↑ **Parent:** [Mutation](mutation.md)

[Insertional oncogenesis](insertional-oncogenesis.md) is oncogenic change caused by insertion of genetic material at a site affecting cellular gene expression or function. Recurrent sites in independent tumours and functional validation help distinguish a causal driver from an incidental insertion.
