# Large solitary pulse on a cylindrical film

↑ **Parent:** [Thin liquid film on a vertical cylinder](thin-liquid-film-on-a-vertical-cylinder.md)

For a [travelling wave](travelling-wave.md) of large positive speed in the cylindrical film equation, the leading core is proportional to $1-\cos x$ over an axial interval of length $2\pi$. Edge coordinates $X=(3c)^{1/3}(x-x_e)$ reduce the transition equation to $H^3H_{XXX}=H-1$. Its real positive exponent gives a monotone trailing approach and its complex stable exponents give damped [capillary waves](capillary-wave.md) ahead, with physical wavelength $4\pi a/(3^{5/6}c^{1/3})$. Matching the two quadratic edge expansions selects the uniform film thickness; this is a [matched asymptotic expansion](matched-asymptotic-expansion.md), valid while the pulse itself remains thin relative to the cylinder.
