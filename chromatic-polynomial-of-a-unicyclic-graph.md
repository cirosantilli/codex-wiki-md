# Chromatic polynomial of a unicyclic graph

↑ **Parent:** [Chromatic polynomial](chromatic-polynomial.md)

If a [unicyclic graph](unicyclic-graph.md) has $n$ [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) and its unique [graph cycle](cycle-in-a-graph.md) has length $\ell$, each of its $n-\ell$ off-[graph cycle](cycle-in-a-graph.md) [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) contributes a factor $x-1$ after its [parent in a rooted tree](parent-in-a-rooted-tree.md) is coloured. Thus $p_G(x)=(x-1)^{n-\ell}p_{C_\ell}(x)$. Substituting the [chromatic polynomial of a cycle](chromatic-polynomial-of-a-cycle.md) yields the displayed formula, equivalently by repeated application of the [chromatic polynomial after attaching a leaf](chromatic-polynomial-after-attaching-a-leaf.md).
