# Iterated extrema of an array

↑ **Parent:** [Infimum and supremum](infimum-and-supremum.md)

For a nonempty bounded array of [real numbers](real-number.md), taking the [supremum](supremum.md) of each row and then the [supremum](supremum.md) of those values gives the [supremum](supremum.md) of all entries. Every row [supremum](supremum.md) is bounded by the global [supremum](supremum.md); conversely, every entry is bounded by the iterated [supremum](supremum.md). The same argument with [lower bounds](lower-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md) proves $\inf_m\inf_n x_{m,n}=\inf_{m,n}x_{m,n}$. Mixed [infima](infimum.md) and [suprema](supremum.md) satisfy the [weak minimax inequality](weak-minimax-inequality.md) rather than an equality in general.
