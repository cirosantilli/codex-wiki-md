# Ball (mathematics)

↑ **Parent:** [Metric space](metric-space.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ball_(mathematics))

In a [metric space](metric-space.md), an open ball $B(a,r)$ consists of points whose distance from $a$ is less than $r$. The closed ball uses a nonstrict inequality. A [Euclidean ball](euclidean-ball.md) uses Euclidean distance, while a [unit ball](unit-ball.md) in a normed space uses its norm and radius one.
