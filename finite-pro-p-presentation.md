# Finite pro-p presentation

↑ **Parent:** [Free pro-p group](free-pro-p-group.md)

A finite generating set and finitely many relators in a [free pro-p group](free-pro-p-group.md), divided out by their closed [normal closure](normal-closure.md). Relators are elements of the [free pro-p group](free-pro-p-group.md); they need not be finite ordinary words. A [pro-p group](pro-p-group.md) with such a presentation is finitely presented in the pro-$p$ category.
