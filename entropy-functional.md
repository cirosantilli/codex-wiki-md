# Entropy functional

↑ **Parent:** [Concentration inequality](concentration-inequality.md)

For a nonnegative integrable random variable $Z$, the entropy functional is

$$
\operatorname{Ent}(Z)
=\mathbb E[Z\log Z]-\mathbb EZ\log\mathbb EZ.
$$

It is the unnormalized [relative entropy](kullback-leibler-divergence.md) of the measure with density proportional to $Z$ relative to the original probability measure.
