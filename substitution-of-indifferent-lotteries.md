# Substitution of indifferent lotteries

↑ **Parent:** [Independence axiom for lottery preferences](independence-axiom-for-lottery-preferences.md)

Apply the [independence axiom for lottery preferences](independence-axiom-for-lottery-preferences.md) in both weak directions of the [indifference relation](indifference-relation.md). At $p=0$ the two mixtures are identical. Repeating this substitution and using [transitivity](transitive-relation.md) lets one replace any finite collection of indifferent component [lotteries](lottery.md), after which [reduction of compound lotteries](reduction-of-compound-lotteries.md) combines their outcome [probabilities](probability.md).
