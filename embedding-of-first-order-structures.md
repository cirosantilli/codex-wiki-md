# Embedding of first-order structures

↑ **Parent:** [First-order structure](first-order-structure.md)

An [embedding of first-order structures](embedding-of-first-order-structures.md) is an [injective](injective-function.md) [function](function-split.md) preserving [function symbols](function-symbol.md) and constants and both preserving and reflecting every relation symbol. Its image is a [substructure](substructure-of-a-first-order-structure.md). Satisfaction of an atomic [diagram of a structure](diagram-mathematical-logic.md) in an expansion gives such an [embedding of first-order structures](embedding-of-first-order-structures.md) by sending each named element to its new interpretation; literal inclusion requires fixing the interpretations of those names.
