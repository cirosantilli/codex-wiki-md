# Triple integral

↑ **Parent:** [Multiple integral](multiple-integral.md)

A triple integral is a [multiple integral](multiple-integral.md) over a three-dimensional region. For a [mass density](density.md) $\rho$, $\iiint_D\rho\,dV$ gives the total mass, while $\iiint_D1\,dV$ gives its [volume](volume.md). Iterated one-dimensional integration is justified by [Fubini's theorem](fubini-s-theorem.md) under absolute integrability, and a [change of variables](change-of-variables-formula.md) includes the absolute [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md) in the volume element.
