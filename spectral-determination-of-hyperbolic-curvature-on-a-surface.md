# Spectral determination of hyperbolic curvature on a surface

↑ **Parent:** [Heat invariants](heat-invariants.md)

On a closed two-dimensional [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md), the first three integrated scalar [heat invariants](heat-invariants.md) are $A$, $\tfrac13\int K\,dA$, and $\tfrac1{15}\int K^2\,dA$, where $K$ is [Gaussian curvature](gaussian-curvature.md). If its scalar spectrum agrees with that of a [hyperbolic surface](hyperbolic-surface.md) of area $A$, these integrals are $A,-A/3,A/15$. Expanding the displayed square then forces $K=-1$ everywhere. Thus constant negative curvature is spectrally determined in this setting, although the global hyperbolic metric need not be determined up to [isometry](isometry.md).
