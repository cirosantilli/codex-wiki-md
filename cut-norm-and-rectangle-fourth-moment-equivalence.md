# Cut norm and rectangle fourth-moment equivalence

↑ **Parent:** [Box norm](box-norm.md)

For a real function $f$ bounded by one on a finite Cartesian product, the fourth power of its [box norm](box-norm.md) is the mean of $f(x,y)f(x,y')f(x',y)f(x',y')$. Two applications of the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) bound every rectangular discrepancy by the [box norm](box-norm.md). Conversely, decompose each bounded real test function into positive and negative parts and use the [layer cake representation](layer-cake-representation.md). Its bilinear pairing with $f$ is at most four times the [cut norm](cut-norm.md). Applying this to the row and column tests furnished by fixed $x',y'$ bounds the fourth moment by four times the [cut norm](cut-norm.md). These dimension-independent estimates make the two notions of small discrepancy equivalent.
