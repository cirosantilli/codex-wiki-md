# One-bit heralding of a remote state preparation block

↑ **Parent:** [Heralded remote preparation of an arbitrary qubit](heralded-remote-preparation-of-an-arbitrary-qubit.md)

Apply [heralded remote preparation of an arbitrary qubit](heralded-remote-preparation-of-an-arbitrary-qubit.md) independently to $n$ shared [singlet states](singlet-state.md). The sender reports one [bit](bit.md) indicating whether every local outcome was successful. A positive flag certifies that the receiver's block is the desired product of $n$ [pure states](pure-state.md); its [probability](probability.md) is $2^{-n}$. A negative flag need not identify which states failed, since the whole block can be discarded.
