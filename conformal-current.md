# Conformal current

↑ **Parent:** [Noether current](noether-current.md)

For a symmetric conserved [stress-energy tensor](stress-energy-tensor.md) $\Theta$ and a [Conformal Killing vector field](conformal-killing-vector-field.md) $\omega$ in dimension $d$, the displayed [conformal current](conformal-current.md) has divergence $(\partial\cdot\omega)\Theta^\mu{}_{\mu}/d$. This follows by contracting the [Conformal Killing equation](conformal-killing-equation.md) with $\Theta$. A traceless tensor therefore gives a conserved current for every conformal generator.
