# Congruence of squares

↑ **Parent:** [Integer factorization](integer-factorization.md)

A congruence of squares is useful for [integer factorization](integer-factorization.md) because $N$ divides $(a-b)(a+b)$. If $a$ is not congruent to either $b$ or $-b$ modulo an odd semiprime, [greatest common divisor](greatest-common-divisor.md) computations with $a-b$ and $a+b$ recover proper factors. Smooth relations combined to make all prime exponents even produce such congruences in sieve-based factorization methods.
