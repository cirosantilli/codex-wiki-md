# Synapomorphy

↑ **Parent:** [Phylogenetics](phylogenetics.md)

A shared derived character supporting a particular branch of a [phylogenetic tree](phylogenetic-tree.md). Its level depends on inferred character polarity; a trait shared ancestrally across a wider group need not diagnose a smaller [clade](clade.md).
