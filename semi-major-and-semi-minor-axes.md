# Semi-major and semi-minor axes

↑ **Parent:** [Ellipse](ellipse.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semi-major_and_semi-minor_axes)

The [semi-major and semi-minor axes](semi-major-and-semi-minor-axes.md) are the larger and smaller half-axis lengths of an [ellipse](ellipse.md). In principal-axis coordinates the [ellipse](ellipse.md) has equation $x^2/a^2+y^2/b^2=1$ with $a\ge b>0$. The same terminology is used for a [hyperbola](hyperbola.md) $x^2/a^2-y^2/b^2=1$, where $a$ is half its transverse axis and $b$ half its conjugate axis. Orbital semiaxes apply these geometric quantities to [conic sections](conic-section.md).
