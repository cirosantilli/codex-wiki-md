# Phenol

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical compound](chemical-compound.md)

[Phenol](phenol.md) is [benzene](benzene.md) with one ring [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) replaced by a [hydroxy group](hydroxy-group.md). That O–H group supplies the [hydrogen bond donor](hydrogen-bond-donor.md) in the experiment under [hydrogen bond acceptor](hydrogen-bond-acceptor.md).
