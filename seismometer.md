# Seismometer

↑ **Parent:** [Seismology](seismology.md)

A [seismometer](seismometer.md) compares ground motion with the motion of an inertial test [mass](mass.md). A mechanical [seismograph](seismometer.md) records their relative displacement. Its [frequency response](frequency-response.md) depends on the suspension and on which relative or absolute velocity enters the damping law.
