# Bloodletting

↑ **Parent:** [History of medicine](history-of-medicine.md)

Deliberate removal of [blood](blood.md) for a therapeutic purpose. Historical practices include [venesection](venesection.md), [wet cupping](wet-cupping.md) and [medicinal leeches](medicinal-leech.md); their historical rationale must be separated from present-day indications for [venesection](venesection.md).
