# Continuity of lottery preferences

↑ **Parent:** [Lottery](lottery.md)

One standard mixture formulation says that if $A\succeq B\succeq C$, some $p\in[0,1]$ satisfies $B\sim pA+(1-p)C$. Under the other [Von Neumann-Morgenstern utility theorem](von-neumann-morgenstern-utility-theorem.md) assumptions, this excludes lexicographic comparisons and permits numerical [utility function](utility-function-split.md) scales. It concerns mixtures of [lotteries](lottery.md), rather than continuity of a monetary [utility function](utility-function-split.md) on a prespecified consumption domain.
