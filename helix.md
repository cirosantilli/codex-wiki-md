# Helix

↑ **Parent:** [Space curve](space-curve.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helix)

A helix is a [space curve](space-curve.md) that winds around an axis while advancing along it at a constant rate. A circular helix can be parametrized by $(R\cos t,R\sin t,at)$.
