# Punctured complex plane

↑ **Parent:** [Punctured Riemann surface](punctured-riemann-surface.md)

The punctured plane $\mathbb C^*$ is a connected Riemann surface homeomorphic to a cylinder.

It is the complex plane with a point removed, a special [punctured Riemann surface](punctured-riemann-surface.md).
