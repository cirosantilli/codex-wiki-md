# Inada conditions

↑ **Parent:** [Utility function](utility-function-split.md)

The Inada conditions specify infinite marginal utility at zero consumption or wealth and zero marginal utility at infinity. For an increasing strictly [concave](concave-function.md) [utility function](utility-function-split.md), they make the inverse marginal utility map cover all positive shadow prices, facilitating the [state-price budget constraint](state-price-budget-constraint.md) method.
