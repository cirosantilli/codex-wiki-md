# Genotype fitness

↑ **Parent:** [Population genetics](population-genetics.md)

[Genotype fitness](genotype-fitness.md) measures the reproductive or viability success assigned to a [genotype](genotype.md) in a specified selection model. Relative fitnesses weight [Hardy-Weinberg proportions](hardy-weinberg-principle.md) after viability selection; continuous-time fitness differences instead act as selection-rate differences. The convention and time units must be specified before comparing selection with migration.
