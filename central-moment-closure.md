# Central-moment closure

↑ **Parent:** [Moment closure](moment-closure.md)

A central-moment closure sets selected high-order [central moments](central-moment.md) to zero. For example, setting the third central moment of $X$ to zero gives

$$
\mathbb E[X^3]=3\mathbb E[X]\mathbb E[X^2]-2(\mathbb E[X])^3.
$$
