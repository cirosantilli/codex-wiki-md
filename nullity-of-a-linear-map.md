# Nullity of a linear map

↑ **Parent:** [Kernel of a linear map](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md)

The [nullity](nullity-of-a-linear-map.md) is the [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) of the [kernel of a linear map](kernel-of-a-linear-map.md). It counts independent directions annihilated by that map. For a finite-dimensional domain, the [rank-nullity theorem](rank-nullity-theorem.md) gives $n(T)+r(T)=\dim V$.
