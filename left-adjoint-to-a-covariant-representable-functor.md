# Left adjoint to a covariant representable functor

↑ **Parent:** [Representable functor](representable-functor.md)

If $\mathcal C$ has small [coproducts in a category](coproduct.md) and $U\cong\mathcal C(R,-)$, then $F(S)=\coprod_{s\in S}R$ is a [left adjoint](adjoint-functors.md) to $U$. Maps from this coproduct to $X$ are families of maps $R\to X$, naturally equivalent to functions $S\to UX$. Conversely, a set-valued [right adjoint](adjoint-functors.md) $U$ is represented by $F(1)$, because maps from a singleton evaluate to elements.
