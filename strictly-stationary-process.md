# Strictly stationary process

↑ **Parent:** [Stationary process](stationary-process.md)

Every finite-dimensional distribution of a strictly stationary process is invariant under a common shift of its time indices. If second moments are finite, strict stationarity implies a [weakly stationary process](weakly-stationary-process.md). A constant mean and lag-dependent covariance alone do not imply strict stationarity.
