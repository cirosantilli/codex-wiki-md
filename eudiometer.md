# Eudiometer

↑ **Parent:** [Gas volume](gas-volume.md)

A graduated tube for confining a [gas](gas.md), measuring its [volume](volume.md), and often examining changes after a [chemical reaction](chemical-reaction-split.md). The reading requires [temperature](temperature-split.md), [pressure](pressure-split.md) and [vapor pressure](vapor-pressure.md) corrections.
