# Palatini identity

↑ **Parent:** [Metric variation](metric-variation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palatini_identity)

For the [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md), the difference of two connections is a tensor. Varying the coordinate expression for the [Ricci tensor](ricci-tensor.md) then gives the displayed covariant identity. It is the starting point for the [metric variation of scalar curvature](metric-variation-of-scalar-curvature.md).
