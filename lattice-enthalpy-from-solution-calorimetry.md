# Lattice enthalpy from solution calorimetry

↑ **Parent:** [Born-Haber cycle](born-haber-cycle.md)

A second indirect route makes the experimental distinction especially clear: a [calorimeter](calorimeter.md) can measure dissolution of a weighed [salt](salt-chemistry.md) into a known amount of [water](water.md), but its final [ions](ion.md) are hydrated, not separated gaseous [ions](ion.md). At compatible [standard states](standard-state.md) and the same [temperature](temperature-split.md),

$$
\Delta H_{\rm sol}^{\circ}=L_{\rm diss}+\Delta H_{\rm hyd,pair}^{\circ},\qquad
L_{\rm diss}=\Delta H_{\rm sol}^{\circ}-\Delta H_{\rm hyd,pair}^{\circ}.
$$

Here the combined [hydration of ions](hydration-of-ions.md) term takes one [mole](mole-unit.md) each of gaseous $\mathrm{Na}^+$ and $\mathrm{Cl}^-$ to the specified aqueous reference state. Its sum suffices: dissolution [calorimetry](calorimetry-split.md) alone cannot independently assign single-ion [hydration of ions](hydration-of-ions.md) [enthalpies](enthalpy-split.md), or determine $L_{\rm diss}$ without another input. A finite-[concentration](concentration.md) measurement also requires dilution corrections before it can supply $\Delta H_{\rm sol}^{\circ}$.

In a simple constant-[pressure](pressure-split.md) solution [calorimeter](calorimeter.md), an insulated vessel, stirrer and [thermometer](thermometer.md) follow the [temperature](temperature-split.md) change after mixing. A calibrated effective [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) $C_{\rm eff}$ converts the corrected [temperature](temperature-split.md) change to [heat](heat-split.md). With no applied heating, negligible external [heat transfer](heat-transfer.md), and only pressure-volume [work](work.md), the approximate molar result is

$$
\Delta H_{\rm sol}\simeq-\frac{C_{\rm eff}\Delta T}{n}.
$$

Here $n$ is the [amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md) dissolved and $C_{\rm eff}$ includes the [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md) and the vessel. Cooling means positive dissolution [enthalpy](enthalpy-split.md), because the dissolving sample takes [heat](heat-split.md) from its surroundings.[§5.2, Figures 5.11–5.13 and discussion of including solution and calorimeter heat capacities](chemistry-2e-by-paul-flowers-and-others.md)

An actual reference measurement used [potassium chloride](potassium-chloride.md), not [sodium chloride](sodium-chloride.md): a platinum-lined [calorimeter](calorimeter.md) with an adiabatic shield and a quartz-oscillator [thermometer](thermometer.md). Because dissolution absorbed [heat](heat-split.md), measured electrical heating prevented loss of shield control; the readouts were electrical input and the vessel/shield [temperature](temperature-split.md) record. Stirring was checked visually in a dummy vessel. The [thermometer](thermometer.md) was compared with a calibrated resistance [thermometer](thermometer.md), and electrical calibration bracketed the reaction. The reported result was $+(17.584\pm0.017)\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$ at $298.15\,\mathrm K$, for one [mole](mole-unit.md) of [potassium chloride](potassium-chloride.md) in $500$ [moles](mole-unit.md) of [water](water.md).[§3 and §4: apparatus, calibration, readouts and specified dilution; inspected through indexed article text](the-enthalpy-of-solution-of-srm-1655-kcl-in-h2o-by-marthada-v-kilday.md)

Checks on residual [water](water.md), particle size and final [concentration](concentration.md), and agreement with a second [calorimeter](calorimeter.md), constrained systematic errors. Imperfect shield tracking required [heat transfer](heat-transfer.md) corrections. This experiment measures an [enthalpy of solution](enthalpy-change-of-solution.md), not a direct [lattice energy](lattice-energy.md); the hydration and dilution bookkeeping still has to be supplied.
