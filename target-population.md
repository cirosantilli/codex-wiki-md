# Target population

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical inference](statistical-inference-split.md)

The set of units or situations to which a study intends its claims to apply. A recruitment pool, the observed sample and the [target population](target-population.md) can differ. [External validity](external-validity.md) concerns whether the evidence transports to the intended target.
