# Indefinite Klein-Gordon charge density

↑ **Parent:** [Klein-Gordon inner product](klein-gordon-inner-product.md)

For a complex [Klein-Gordon field](klein-gordon-field.md), $j^\mu=i(\phi^*\partial^\mu\phi-\phi\partial^\mu\phi^*)$ is conserved. A mode $\phi=N e^{-iEt+i\mathbf p\cdot\mathbf x}$ has $j^0=2E|N|^2$, so unrestricted positive- and negative-frequency solutions do not have a positive probability norm under this current. In the quantized complex field, the associated conserved quantity is charge: particles and [antiparticles](antiparticle.md) contribute with opposite signs while both have positive excitation energy and positive [Fock space](fock-space.md) norm.
