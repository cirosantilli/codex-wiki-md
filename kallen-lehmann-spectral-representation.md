<h1 id="kallen-lehmann-spectral-representation">Källén–Lehmann spectral representation</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar propagator](scalar-propagator.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Källén–Lehmann_spectral_representation)

For a Hermitian [scalar field](scalar-field.md) in a positive-norm [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) with a Lorentz-invariant vacuum, the [spectrum condition](spectrum-condition.md) and completeness give the connected vacuum two-point function as a superposition of free mass-shell functions. Define the [Källén–Lehmann spectral density](kallen-lehmann-spectral-density.md) by $\widetilde W(p)=2\pi\theta(p^0)\rho(p^2)$, where $W(x)=\langle0|\phi(x)\phi(0)|0\rangle$ and its [vacuum expectation value](vacuum-expectation-value.md) has been subtracted if nonzero. Squared intermediate-state matrix elements make $\rho$ a nonnegative measure supported on nonnegative mass squared. Applying [time ordering](time-ordering.md) to each free mass shell gives the displayed [Feynman propagator](feynman-propagator.md) representation, using $i\Delta=\langle T\phi\phi\rangle$ and signature $(+---)$. Its positivity does not automatically extend to gauge-variant fields in an indefinite auxiliary space.
