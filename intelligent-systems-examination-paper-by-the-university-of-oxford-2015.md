# Intelligent Systems examination paper by the University of Oxford (2015)

↑ **Parent:** [Computer science bibliography](computer-science-bibliography-split.md)

Four-page [University of Oxford](university-of-oxford-split.md) Finals examination, Trinity Term 2015, dated Tuesday 16 June 2015, 9:30 am–11:00 am. The original marks Questions 1 and 3 as corrected. Printed p. 2 concerns [Horn clause](horn-clause.md) proof search and [admissible heuristics](admissible-heuristic.md); p. 3 concerns a [STRIPS](strips.md) encoding of a multi-stack [Tower of Hanoi](tower-of-hanoi.md) puzzle; p. 4 concerns [constraint satisfaction problems](constraint-satisfaction-problem.md), [tree decomposition](tree-decomposition.md), and [all-different constraints](all-different-constraint.md).

Public archive: [Lawrence Attachment.zip](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/computer_science_past_exam_paper/response/956037/attach/3/Lawrence%20Attachment.zip?cookie_passthrough=1), member `2015-is.pdf`.
