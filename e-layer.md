# E layer

↑ **Parent:** [Ionosphere](ionosphere.md)

The E layer is a main region of the [ionosphere](ionosphere.md) below the [F layer](f-layer.md). The 1924 [Appleton-Barnett radio interference experiment](appleton-barnett-radio-interference-experiment.md) concerns this lower region.[Edward V. Appleton, The ionosphere, Nobel lecture, 12 December 1947, printed p. 81, final three paragraphs](the-ionosphere-by-edward-victor-appleton.md)
