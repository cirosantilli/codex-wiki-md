# Nuclear energy level

↑ **Parent:** [Energy level](energy-level.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Nuclear structure](nuclear-structure.md)

A [nuclear energy level](nuclear-energy-level.md) is an [energy eigenvalue](energy-eigenvalue-split.md) of an [atomic nucleus](atomic-nucleus-split.md). Differences between levels give nuclear [excitation energies](excitation-energy.md) and the approximate [gamma ray](gamma-ray.md) energies of transitions.
