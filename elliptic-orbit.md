# Elliptic orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Kepler orbit](kepler-orbit.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliptic_orbit)

An elliptic orbit is a bound orbit shaped as an ellipse. A Kepler ellipse has negative [specific orbital energy](specific-orbital-energy.md) and eccentricity $0\leq e<1$.
