# Hard Lefschetz theorem on de Rham cohomology

↑ **Parent:** [Lefschetz operator of a Kähler manifold](lefschetz-operator-of-a-kahler-manifold.md)

For a compact [Kähler manifold](kahler-manifold.md) of complex dimension $n$, wedging with the $(n-k)$th power of its [Kähler class](kahler-class.md) is the displayed [isomorphism](isomorphism.md) for $0\leq k\leq n$. The [Hard Lefschetz isomorphism on Dolbeault cohomology](hard-lefschetz-isomorphism-on-dolbeault-cohomology.md) and [Hodge decomposition theorem for compact Kähler manifolds](hodge-decomposition-theorem-for-compact-kahler-manifolds.md) give the result by summing the bidegrees. In particular, $L:H^k\to H^{k+2}$ is [injective](injective-function.md) when $k<n$: if $L\alpha=0$, then $L^{n-k}\alpha=0$. Hence $b_k\leq b_{k+2}$ below the middle dimension.
