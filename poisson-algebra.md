# Poisson algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Algebra over a field](algebra-over-a-field.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poisson_algebra)

A [Poisson algebra](poisson-algebra.md) is a commutative associative algebra with a [Lie bracket](lie-bracket.md) that is a derivation in each argument. Smooth functions on a [symplectic manifold](symplectic-manifold.md) are its standard example. The linear span of moment-map components may form a finite-dimensional Lie subalgebra, while closing those components under multiplication normally produces a much larger [Poisson algebra](poisson-algebra.md).
