# Negative-diffusion criterion for viscous disk instability

↑ **Parent:** [Viscous instability of an accretion disk](viscous-instability-of-an-accretion-disk.md)

Freezing coefficients locally in the [Keplerian viscous diffusion equation](keplerian-viscous-diffusion-equation.md) gives $\Sigma_{1,t}\simeq3\partial_\Sigma(\bar\nu\Sigma)\Sigma_{1,rr}$. A radial [Fourier mode](fourier-mode.md) grows at rate $-3\partial_\Sigma(\bar\nu\Sigma)k_r^2$. Negative response reverses ordinary smoothing and gives a [backward heat equation](backward-heat-equation.md), within the [wavelength](wavelength.md) range where the [thin disc](thin-disk.md) transport closure applies.
