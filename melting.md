# Melting

↑ **Parent:** [Phase transition](phase-transition.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melting)

[Melting](melting.md) is a solid-to-liquid [phase transition](phase-transition.md) that absorbs [latent heat](latent-heat.md). It can be driven by heating, changing phase pressures, or adding solute that causes [freezing-point depression](freezing-point-depression.md). The [ice layer between freshwater and cold brine](ice-layer-between-freshwater-and-cold-brine.md) illustrates [melting](melting.md) into a cold liquid through dilution and a composition-dependent [liquidus](liquidus.md).
