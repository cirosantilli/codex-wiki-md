# Scientific control

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical modelling](statistical-modelling-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_control)

A scientific control supplies a comparison or procedure that distinguishes an intended effect from other influences on an experiment or observational measurement. Negative controls should show no target effect; positive controls should show a known effect. A [negative control outcome](negative-control-outcome.md) applies this logic to an outcome that the exposure cannot causally affect.
