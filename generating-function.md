# Generating function

↑ **Parent:** [Power series](power-series.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generating_function)

A [generating function](generating-function.md) encodes a [sequence](sequence.md) as a [series](series-mathematics.md), whose coefficients or prescribed weighted coefficients recover the [sequence](sequence.md). An [ordinary generating function](ordinary-generating-function.md) uses the [power series](power-series.md) $A(x)=\sum_{n\geq0}a_nx^n$; an [exponential generating function](exponential-generating-function.md) uses $\sum_{n\geq0}a_nx^n/n!$. A [Dirichlet series](dirichlet-series.md) $\sum_{n\geq1}a_nn^{-s}$ gives another generating-function encoding. The displayed [power series](power-series.md) is the ordinary specialization, rather than a restriction on all [generating functions](generating-function.md). These encodings turn combinatorial splitting and concatenation rules into algebraic identities. A counting [generating function](generating-function.md) is not necessarily a [probability generating function](probability-generating-function.md): its coefficients need not sum to one.
