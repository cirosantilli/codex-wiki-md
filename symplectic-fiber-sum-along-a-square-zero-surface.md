# Symplectic fiber sum along a square-zero surface

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic manifold](symplectic-manifold.md)

If symplectic four-manifolds contain symplectic copies of the same closed surface with self-intersection zero, their trivial symplectic normal bundles may be identified with reversed circle fibers. Removing the two tubular neighborhoods and gluing their boundaries gives a [symplectic sum](symplectic-sum.md) carrying a natural symplectic form.
