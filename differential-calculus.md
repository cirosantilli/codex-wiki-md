# Differential calculus

↑ **Parent:** [Calculus](calculus-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differential_calculus)

[Differential calculus](differential-calculus.md) studies [derivatives](derivative.md): how a [function](function-split.md) changes near an input and how that local information describes its graph or evolution. Its tools include [linear approximation](linear-approximation.md), higher [derivatives](derivative.md), and [Taylor series](taylor-series.md); applications include finding [velocity](velocity.md) from [position](position.md) and studying [optimization](mathematical-optimization-split.md) through [critical points](critical-point.md).
