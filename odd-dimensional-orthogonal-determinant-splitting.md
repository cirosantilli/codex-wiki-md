# Odd-dimensional orthogonal determinant splitting

↑ **Parent:** [Orthogonal group](orthogonal-group.md)

In odd dimension, the map $A\mapsto(\det A)A$ is a surjective [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md) from the [orthogonal group](orthogonal-group.md) to the [special orthogonal group](special-orthogonal-group.md), with [kernel of a group homomorphism](kernel-of-a-group-homomorphism.md) $\{I,-I\}$. Scalar matrices commute, and $\det[(\det A)A]=(\det A)^{2m+2}=1$. Together with the [determinant](determinant.md) sign, it identifies $O(2m+1)$ with the [direct product of groups](direct-product-of-groups.md) $SO(2m+1)\times C_2$. In even dimension, scalar multiplication by $\det A$ does not repair the determinant, so this construction does not give the same splitting.
