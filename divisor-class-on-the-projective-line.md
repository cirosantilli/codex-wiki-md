# Divisor class on the projective line

↑ **Parent:** [Principal divisor on an algebraic curve](principal-divisor-on-an-algebraic-curve.md)

Every degree-zero divisor on the [projective line](projective-line.md) is principal. Indeed, if $D=\sum_i a_ip_i$ and $L_i$ is a homogeneous linear form vanishing at $p_i$, then $\sum_i a_i=0$ makes

$$
f=\prod_iL_i^{a_i}
$$

a degree-zero rational function with $(f)=D$. Consequently, two divisors on $\mathbb P^1$ are linearly equivalent exactly when they have the same degree.
