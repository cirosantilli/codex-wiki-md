# Types of pairs of vectors over a finite field

↑ **Parent:** [Vector space over a finite field](vector-space-over-a-finite-field.md)

For infinite spaces over a [field](field.md) of size $q$, two-variable types are determined by the kernel of the [linear map](linear-map.md) sending $(a,b)$ to $ax+by$. Its ranks give the zero pair, $q+1$ one-dimensional directions and the independent-pair type. All $q+3$ types are isolated by finite formulas. [Quantifier elimination](quantifier-elimination.md) makes the [Lindenbaum algebra](lindenbaum-algebra.md) their full power-set [Boolean algebra](boolean-algebra.md). The one-variable algebra has just the zero and nonzero [atoms](atom-of-a-boolean-algebra.md), hence four [Elements](euclid-s-elements.md).
