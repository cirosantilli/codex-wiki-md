# Stable characteristic equation for free-free bending modes

↑ **Parent:** [Free-free bending spectrum](free-free-bending-spectrum.md)

Writing the characteristic equation with reciprocal [hyperbolic cosine](hyperbolic-cosine.md) avoids exponentially large products. For the positive roots indexed from one, $q_n=(n+1/2)\pi+2(-1)^{n+1}e^{-(n+1/2)\pi}+O(e^{-2(n+1/2)\pi})$. The apparent root at zero represents the separate affine kernel, not another positive bending mode. Evaluating hyperbolic pieces of high modes with decaying exponential forms also avoids cancellation.
