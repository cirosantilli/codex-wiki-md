# Resolvent of an element

↑ **Parent:** [Spectrum of an element](spectrum-of-an-element.md)

The resolvent is $R(\lambda,a)=(\lambda1-a)^{-1}$ on the complement of the [spectrum of an element](spectrum-of-an-element.md). In a Banach algebra it is analytic there.
