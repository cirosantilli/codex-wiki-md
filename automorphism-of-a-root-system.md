# Automorphism of a root system

↑ **Parent:** [Root system](root-system.md)

An automorphism of a [root system](root-system.md) $\Phi\subset E$ is an [orthogonal transformation](orthogonal-transformation.md) of $E$ mapping $\Phi$ onto itself. The [Weyl group](weyl-group.md) is a subgroup of this automorphism group; additional automorphisms can act nontrivially on a [Dynkin diagram](dynkin-diagram.md) while preserving a [root basis](fundamental-system-of-a-root-system.md).
