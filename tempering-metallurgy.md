# Tempering (metallurgy)

↑ **Parent:** [Steel microstructure](steel-microstructure.md)

Heating quenched steel below the austenitizing range to adjust its microstructure and relieve stresses. Carbon redistribution and carbide formation can improve [toughness](toughness.md) at a cost in [hardness](hardness.md).
