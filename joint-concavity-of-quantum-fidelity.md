# Joint concavity of quantum fidelity

↑ **Parent:** [Fidelity of quantum states](fidelity-of-quantum-states.md)

In the unsquared convention, $F(\sum_xp_x\rho_x,\sum_xp_x\sigma_x)\geq\sum_xp_xF(\rho_x,\sigma_x)$. Use [Uhlmann's theorem](uhlmann-s-theorem.md) to choose component purifications with nonnegative maximizing overlaps, and then construct matching [flagged purifications of a quantum ensemble](flagged-purification-of-a-quantum-ensemble.md). Their overlap is the weighted sum on the right; the fidelity of the mixtures maximizes over all such overlaps.
