# Wave ray

↑ **Parent:** [Ray tracing](ray-tracing.md)

A path followed by a slowly modulated [wave packet](wave-packet.md) under a local [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation-split.md). Its [velocity](velocity.md) is the [group velocity](group-velocity.md), while its [wavevector](wavevector.md) evolves as $d\mathbf k/dt=-\nabla_{\mathbf x}\omega$. In a uniform stationary medium both are constant, so the ray is straight even when [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md) and [group velocity](group-velocity.md) differ.
