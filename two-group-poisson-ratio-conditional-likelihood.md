# Two-group Poisson ratio conditional likelihood

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson regression](poisson-regression.md)

For independent aggregate [Poisson distributions](poisson-distribution.md) with means $n\lambda$ and $n\lambda e^\beta$, conditioning on the total eliminates $\lambda$. [Independent Poisson conditioning](independent-poisson-conditioning.md) gives the displayed [binomial distribution](binomial-distribution.md). Its [conditional likelihood](conditional-likelihood.md) is proportional to $e^{\beta Y_1}(1+e^\beta)^{-m}$, also the [profile likelihood](profile-likelihood.md) after maximizing over the unrestricted positive baseline rate. Unequal exposures replace the binomial success probability by $n_1e^\beta/(n_0+n_1e^\beta)$.
