# Quantum resonance of the kicked rotor

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum kicked rotor](quantum-kicked-rotor.md)

When the free angular-momentum [phases](phase-waves.md) all recur at the driving period, successive identical kicks add coherently. For the full revival $\hbar T/(2I)=2\pi$ and an initial zero-angular-momentum state, the [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) after $N$ kicks is $\hbar^2\eta^2N^2/(4I)$. This ballistic law is specific to the stated [resonance](resonance.md) and [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md).
