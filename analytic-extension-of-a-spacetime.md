# Analytic extension of a spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [General relativity](general-relativity-split.md)

An analytic extension embeds a spacetime isometrically as a proper open region in a larger analytic [Lorentzian manifold](lorentzian-manifold.md) with a nondegenerate [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md). A divergence of coordinate components does not obstruct extension if a new chart makes all metric coefficients analytic and the determinant nonzero. For a static spherical metric with an analytic lapse $f$ and a positive simple horizon radius, ingoing coordinates replace $-f\,dt^2+dr^2/f$ by $-f\,dv^2+2\,dv\,dr$. This removes the [coordinate singularity](coordinate-singularity.md) at $f=0$; transverse metric coefficients must also remain regular. An extension across such a horizon is distinct from extending through a [curvature singularity](curvature-singularity.md).
