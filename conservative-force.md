# Conservative force

↑ **Parent:** [Potential energy](potential-energy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conservative_force)

A force is conservative when its work depends only on the endpoints. On a simply connected domain this is equivalent to $F=-\nabla V$ for a [potential energy](potential-energy.md) $V$.
