# Fermi wavevector

↑ **Parent:** [Fermi momentum](fermi-momentum.md)

The Fermi wavevector is the radius of the occupied [sphere](sphere.md) in [wavevector](wavevector.md) coordinates for an isotropic [Fermi gas](fermi-gas.md). In the uniform two-component [electron](electron.md) model, $k_F=(3\pi^2n)^{1/3}$ and $E_F=\hbar^2k_F^2/(2m_e)$ for [nonrelativistic particles](nonrelativistic-particle.md). Its units are inverse [length](length.md), whereas the [Fermi momentum](fermi-momentum.md) has units of [momentum](momentum-split.md).
