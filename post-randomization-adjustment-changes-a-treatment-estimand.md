# Post-randomization adjustment changes a treatment estimand

↑ **Parent:** [Intention-to-treat analysis](intention-to-treat-analysis.md)

A treatment consequence is not interchangeable with a [baseline covariate](baseline-covariate.md) in an [intention-to-treat analysis](intention-to-treat-analysis.md). Let randomized $Z$ be independent of $U,\varepsilon$, and let actual intervention use be $D=Z+U$, with $Y=D+\varepsilon$ and independent centered errors. The assignment changes the mean outcome by one. But the [conditional expectation](conditional-expectation.md) $\mathbb E[Y\mid Z,D]=D$ has coefficient zero on $Z$. Thus adjustment for the post-randomization variable $D$ changes the [estimand](estimand.md) even in a simple identified [linear regression](linear-regression-split.md).
