# Relative effective Cartier divisor on a curve

↑ **Parent:** [Effective Cartier divisor](effective-cartier-divisor.md)

A relative effective divisor of degree $m$ on a [smooth projective curve](smooth-projective-curve.md) is an [effective Cartier divisor](effective-cartier-divisor.md) in $C\times T$ that is finite and flat of degree $m$ over $T$. Its associated [line bundle](line-bundle.md) has degree $m$ on every fibre. The [symmetric product of a curve](symmetric-product-of-a-curve.md) $C^{(m)}$ represents these families and carries their universal divisor. Locally on a smooth coordinate chart, the divisor is described by a monic polynomial, whose coefficients are elementary symmetric functions of its points; this description includes collisions and works in positive characteristic.
