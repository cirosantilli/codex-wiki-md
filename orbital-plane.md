# Orbital plane

↑ **Parent:** [Orbital element](orbital-element.md)

The plane containing a central-force [Kepler orbit](kepler-orbit.md), perpendicular to its conserved [specific angular momentum](specific-angular-momentum.md). Its orientation can be specified by an [orbital inclination](orbital-inclination.md) and an [orbital node](orbital-node.md). Perturbing [forces](force.md) can change that orientation.
