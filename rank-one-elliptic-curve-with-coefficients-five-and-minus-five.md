# Rank-one elliptic curve with coefficients five and minus five

↑ **Parent:** [Two-isogeny descent](two-isogeny-descent.md)

The [two-torsion square-class homomorphism](two-torsion-square-class-homomorphism.md) image on $E$ is all of $\{1,-1,5,-5\}$, realized by the identity, $(0,0)$, $(-1,3)$ and multiplication of classes. Its [two-isogeny formula](two-isogeny-formula.md) companion is $E':Y^2=X^3-10X^2+45X$. Positivity and [prime](prime-number-split.md) support allow only $1,3,5,15$. Class five is realized by $(0,0)$, whereas class three would require $N^2=3U^4-10U^2V^2+15V^4$. Reduction modulo three forces one of [coprime](coprime-integers.md) $U,V$ to be divisible by three; either choice gives odd [valuation](valuation.md) one on the right, contradicting a square. Multiplication by class five also excludes fifteen. The [two-isogeny rank formula](square-class-index-formula-for-two-isogeny-descent.md) gives $2^r=4\cdot2/4=2$.
