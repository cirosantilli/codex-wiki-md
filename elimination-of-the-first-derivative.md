# Elimination of the first derivative

↑ **Parent:** [Second-order linear differential equation](second-order-linear-differential-equation.md)

For a [second-order linear differential equation](second-order-linear-differential-equation.md)

$$
y''+p(x)y'+q(x)y=0,
$$

the substitution $y=u\exp[-\tfrac12\int p(x)\,dx]$ eliminates the first-derivative term and gives

$$
u''+\left(q-\frac12p'-\frac14p^2\right)u=0.
$$
