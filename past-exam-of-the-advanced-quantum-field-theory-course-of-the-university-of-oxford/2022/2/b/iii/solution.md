<h1 id="2/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Both $s$-channel annihilation and $t$-channel [gluon](../../../../../gluon.md) exchange are now allowed. Their [spin sum](../../../../../spin-sum.md) factors are the [Bhabha scattering](../../../../../bhabha-scattering.md) factors with $e\mapsto g$. Their [color charge](../../../../../color-charge.md) tensors, with the external indices of [Solution](../i/solution.md), are

$$
c_s=(T^a)_{ji}(T^a)_{kl},\qquad
c_t=(T^a)_{ki}(T^a)_{jl}.
$$

Each squared term has $K_{ss}=K_{tt}=(N_c^2-1)/(4N_c^2)$. The cross term instead has the [color average](../../../../../color-average.md)

$$
K_{st}=\frac1{N_c^2}\sum_{a,b}\operatorname{tr}(T^aT^bT^aT^b).
$$

To evaluate it, use the [SU(N) fundamental completeness relation](../../../../../su-n-fundamental-completeness-relation.md)

$$
\sum_a(T^a)_{ij}(T^a)_{kl}=\frac12\left(\delta_{il}\delta_{jk}-\frac1{N_c}\delta_{ij}\delta_{kl}\right).
$$

This follows by completing the orthogonal basis of traceless [Hermitian matrices](../../../../../hermitian-operator.md) with the identity. Since $\operatorname{tr}T^b=0$, it gives $\sum_aT^aT^bT^a=-T^b/(2N_c)$, and hence

$$
\boxed{K_{st}=-\frac{N_c^2-1}{4N_c^3}=-\frac1{N_c}K_{ss}.}
$$

Multiplying the two squared [Bhabha scattering](../../../../../bhabha-scattering.md) terms by $K_{ss}$ and its cross term by $K_{st}$ gives

$$
\boxed{\overline{|\mathcal M|^2}=g^4\frac{N_c^2-1}{2N_c^2}
\left[\frac{t^2+u^2}{s^2}+\frac{s^2+u^2}{t^2}-\frac2{N_c}\frac{u^2}{st}\right].}
$$

For the [SU(3)](../../../../../su-3-group.md) [gauge group](../../../../../gauge-group.md), this is

$$
\boxed{\overline{|\mathcal M|^2}=\frac{4g^4}{9}\left[\frac{t^2+u^2}{s^2}+\frac{s^2+u^2}{t^2}\right]
-\frac{8g^4}{27}\frac{u^2}{st}.}
$$

The negative [color charge](../../../../../color-charge.md) overlap reverses the sign of the [QED](../../../../../quantum-electrodynamics.md) [interference](../../../../../interference-wave-propagation.md). Since $st<0$, this cross contribution is positive in the physical region. No additional [spinor](../../../../../spinor.md) calculation is needed; all the new information is in the [SU(N) fundamental completeness relation](../../../../../su-n-fundamental-completeness-relation.md).
