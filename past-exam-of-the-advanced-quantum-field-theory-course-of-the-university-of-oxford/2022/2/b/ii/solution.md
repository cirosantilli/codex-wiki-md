<h1 id="2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The two [Bhabha scattering](../../../../../bhabha-scattering.md) [tree-level Feynman diagrams](../../../../../tree-level-feynman-diagram.md) are the two panels of [Solution](../i/solution.md) with a [photon](../../../../../photon.md) mediator: $s$-channel annihilation with internal [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) $p_1+p_2$, and $t$-channel exchange with internal [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) $p_1-p_3$. Each [interaction vertex](../../../../../interaction-vertex.md) preserves the [electron](../../../../../electron.md) species. There is no independent $u$-channel [Feynman diagram](../../../../../feynman-diagram.md), since the outgoing [electron](../../../../../electron.md) and [Positron](../../../../../positron.md) are distinguishable.

Removing the [color charge](../../../../../color-charge.md) factor from [Solution](../i/solution.md) and replacing $g$ by $e$ gives

$$
\overline{|\mathcal M_s|^2}=2e^4\frac{t^2+u^2}{s^2}.
$$

[Crossing symmetry](../../../../../crossing-symmetry.md) interchanges $s$ and $t$ for the exchange [Feynman diagram](../../../../../feynman-diagram.md), giving

$$
\overline{|\mathcal M_t|^2}=2e^4\frac{s^2+u^2}{t^2}.
$$

Squaring the sum of the two [scattering amplitudes](../../../../../scattering-amplitude.md) also produces the [interference](../../../../../interference-wave-propagation.md) term $2\operatorname{Re}\overline{\mathcal M_s\mathcal M_t^*}$. The given third term must be this cross term: it has both [propagator](../../../../../propagator.md) denominators $st$, whereas a squared individual [Feynman diagram](../../../../../feynman-diagram.md) has $s^2$ or $t^2$. With the correct relative [fermionic sign](../../../../../fermionic-sign.md), its value is $4e^4u^2/(st)$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\overline{|\mathcal M|^2}=2e^4\left[\frac{t^2+u^2}{s^2}+\frac{s^2+u^2}{t^2}+\frac{2u^2}{st}\right].}
$$

The coefficient of the last displayed fraction is positive, but $s>0$ and $t<0$ in the physical scattering region, so this [interference](../../../../../interference-wave-propagation.md) is destructive. The relative sign originates in the two possible external [fermion](../../../../../fermion.md) contractions; changing an external-state phase changes both [scattering amplitudes](../../../../../scattering-amplitude.md) together.[§6.6, Electron Positron Scattering: annihilation and exchange spinor contractions and their relative sign](../../../../../quantum-field-theory-by-david-tong.md)
