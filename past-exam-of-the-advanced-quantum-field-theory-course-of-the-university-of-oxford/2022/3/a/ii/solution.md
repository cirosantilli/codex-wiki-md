<h1 id="3/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Put $r^2=\phi_i\phi_i$. Completing the square in the [scalar potential](../../../../../scalar-potential.md) gives

$$
V=\frac\lambda4\left(r^2-\frac{\mu^2}{\lambda}\right)^2-\frac{\mu^4}{4\lambda}.
$$

Since $\lambda>0$, its global minima form the [vacuum manifold](../../../../../vacuum-manifold.md)

$$
\boxed{r=v,\qquad v=\frac{\mu}{\sqrt\lambda}.}
$$

Here $\mu$ denotes the positive square root of the parameter $\mu^2$. The origin is a local maximum, not a [ground state](../../../../../ground-state.md). Choose $\langle\boldsymbol\phi\rangle=(v,0,0)$. Its [stabilizer subgroup](../../../../../stabilizer-subgroup.md) is the [SO(2)](../../../../../special-orthogonal-group-in-two-dimensions.md) subgroup of rotations in the $(2,3)$ plane, so the stated [SO(3)](../../../../../so-3-group.md) [global symmetry in field theory](../../../../../global-symmetry-in-field-theory.md) is spontaneously broken to [SO(2)](../../../../../special-orthogonal-group-in-two-dimensions.md).

Write $\phi_1=v+h$, $\phi_2=\pi_2$, $\phi_3=\pi_3$. The [kinetic terms](../../../../../kinetic-term.md) stay canonically normalized, while the [scalar mass matrix](../../../../../scalar-mass-matrix.md) is

$$
(M^2)_{ij}=(-\mu^2+\lambda\phi^2)\delta_{ij}+2\lambda\phi_i\phi_j,
\qquad
M^2\big|_{(v,0,0)}=\operatorname{diag}(2\mu^2,0,0).
$$

Equivalently, the quadratic [scalar potential](../../../../../scalar-potential.md) is $\mu^2h^2$. Therefore the particle [masses](../../../../../mass.md) are

$$
\boxed{m_h=\sqrt2\mu,\qquad m_{\pi_2}=m_{\pi_3}=0.}
$$

The two angular [Goldstone bosons](../../../../../goldstone-boson.md) match $\dim SO(3)-\dim SO(2)=3-1=2$. The radial displacement changes $r$ and costs [potential energy](../../../../../potential-energy.md), giving the massive [Higgs mode](../../../../../higgs-mode.md). These are tree-level [masses](../../../../../mass.md); the [Goldstone theorem](../../../../../goldstone-theorem.md) protects the angular zero-[mass](../../../../../mass.md) modes in the exact global theory.
