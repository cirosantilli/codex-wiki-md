<h1 id="3/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The [Goldstone theorem](../../../../../goldstone-theorem.md) states that spontaneous breaking of a continuous internal [global symmetry in field theory](../../../../../global-symmetry-in-field-theory.md) in a local relativistic [quantum field theory](../../../../../quantum-field-theory-split.md) produces a zero-[mass](../../../../../mass.md) [Goldstone boson](../../../../../goldstone-boson.md) for each independent broken generator, under the usual positive-state-space and conserved-current hypotheses. For $G\to H$ there are $\dim G-\dim H$ symmetry-required [Goldstone bosons](../../../../../goldstone-boson.md). This count assumes a Lorentz-invariant [vacuum state](../../../../../vacuum-state.md); nonrelativistic counting can differ.

At the classical level, invariance of the [scalar potential](../../../../../scalar-potential.md) gives $\partial_iV\,(T^a\phi)_i=0$. Differentiating at a stationary [vacuum expectation value](../../../../../vacuum-expectation-value.md) $\phi_0$ yields

$$
\boxed{(M^2)_{ij}(T^a\phi_0)_j=0,\qquad (M^2)_{ij}=\left.\partial_i\partial_jV\right|_{\phi_0}.}
$$

These are the [Goldstone directions in the scalar mass matrix](../../../../../goldstone-directions-in-the-scalar-mass-matrix.md). Broken generators have $T^a\phi_0\ne0$, so their independent directions have zero quadratic [mass](../../../../../mass.md) with a canonical [kinetic term](../../../../../kinetic-term.md). The theorem guarantees these modes; it does not exclude further accidental zero-[mass](../../../../../mass.md) modes. A local [gauge symmetry](../../../../../gauge-invariance.md) is a redundancy and does not meet the physical global-symmetry hypothesis: the [Higgs mechanism](../../../../../higgs-mechanism.md) can instead supply longitudinal [particle polarizations](../../../../../particle-polarization.md) of massive [gauge bosons](../../../../../gauge-boson.md).
