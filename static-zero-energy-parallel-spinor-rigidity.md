# Static zero-energy parallel-spinor rigidity

↑ **Parent:** [Positive energy theorem](positive-energy-theorem.md)

The vacuum [Witten-Nester identity](witten-nester-identity.md) makes a zero-energy Witten spinor parallel under the [Sen spinor connection](sen-spinor-connection.md) on a complete asymptotically flat slice. In an orthogonal static slicing, extrinsic curvature vanishes, so this spinor is intrinsically parallel. Contracting its spin-curvature integrability condition gives zero spatial [Ricci tensor](ricci-tensor.md); in three dimensions this implies flatness. The static vacuum equation $D_iD_jN=N R_{ij}(h)$ then gives a parallel lapse gradient, which asymptotic normalization forces to vanish. The time-independent spinor consequently extends to a spacetime [parallel spinor](parallel-spinor.md), with [Minkowski spacetime](minkowski-spacetime.md) the complete regular asymptotically flat example.
