# Alvarez hydrogen-bubble-chamber reconstruction and resonances

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrogen bubble chamber](hydrogen-bubble-chamber.md)

[Luis Walter Alvarez](luis-walter-alvarez.md)'s program extended the [Glaser bubble-chamber experiment](glaser-bubble-chamber-experiment.md) into large [hydrogen bubble chambers](hydrogen-bubble-chamber.md) and systematic analysis. His lecture credits [John G. Wood](john-g-wood.md), an accelerator technician, with early hydrogen tracks, and emphasizes technicians' responsibility for chamber development.[Recent Developments in Particle Physics, lecture 11 December 1968; institutional report UCRL-18696, printed pp. 12–16, Fig. 2](recent-developments-in-particle-physics-by-luis-walter-alvarez.md)

Alvarez's 1956 report describes a ten-inch [hydrogen bubble chamber](hydrogen-bubble-chamber.md) in a steady [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md), exposed to a $700\,\mathrm{MeV}$ negative-[pion](pion.md) beam for [proton](proton.md) scattering. [Liquid](liquid.md) [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) was the target. Horizontal windows admitted illumination and stereo viewing; a shielded light source let bubble-scattered [light](light.md) reach the lenses. A [temperature gradient](temperature-gradient.md) initially restricted sensitivity near the top. Tilting and fast recompression improved uniformity.[Liquid Hydrogen Bubble Chambers, UCRL-3367, 4 April 1956, PDF pp. 5–7: ten-inch chamber, optics, recompression and scattering exposure](liquid-hydrogen-bubble-chambers-by-luis-walter-alvarez.md)

The same report compares tracks without a [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) against straightness expectations, testing spurious curvature. [Multiple Coulomb scattering](multiple-coulomb-scattering.md) remained a limitation. This separates an instrumental check from the physical [momentum](momentum-split.md) inferred with the field on.[UCRL-3367, PDF p. 7, paragraph beginning “The no field tracks”](liquid-hydrogen-bubble-chambers-by-luis-walter-alvarez.md)

The scientific opportunity is to turn a visible geometry into a test of an invisible intermediate state. The [bubble-chamber pressure cycle](bubble-chamber-pressure-cycle.md) prepares the sensitive target; [bubble-chamber track readout](bubble-chamber-track-readout.md) supplies geometric measurements. Neither step alone identifies a [particle resonance](particle-resonance.md).
