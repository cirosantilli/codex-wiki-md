# Matching minimal covering nets of a compact group

↑ **Parent:** [Minimal left covering net of a compact group](minimal-left-covering-net-of-a-compact-group.md)

Two minimal left covering nets for the same identity neighbourhood $V$ can be matched bijectively so that paired translates intersect. Join $f\in F$ to $f'\in F'$ when $fV\cap f'V\ne\varnothing$. For $S\subseteq F$, its neighbours $N(S)$ cover every point covered by $S$, so $(F\setminus S)\cup N(S)$ still covers the group. Minimality forces $|N(S)|\geq|S|$. [Hall's marriage theorem](hall-s-marriage-theorem.md) supplies the matching, and an intersection $fv=f'v'$ gives $f^{-1}f'=vv'^{-1}\in VV^{-1}$. Averaging a continuous function over matched nets therefore gives values differing by at most its uniform oscillation on right displacements in $VV^{-1}$. This is the finite combinatorial step in constructing [Haar measure](haar-measure.md) on a compact group.
