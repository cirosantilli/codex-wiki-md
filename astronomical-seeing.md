# Astronomical seeing

↑ **Parent:** [Atmospheric turbulence](atmospheric-turbulence.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomical_seeing)

Astronomical seeing is the atmospheric blurring of a stellar image, conventionally expressed as the angular full width at half maximum of its long-exposure [point spread function](point-spread-function.md), usually in arcseconds. For a large aperture in the ideal Kolmogorov model, it is approximately $0.98\lambda/r_0$ radians, with [Fried parameter](fried-parameter.md) $r_0$.
