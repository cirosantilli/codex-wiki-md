# Small-study effect

↑ **Parent:** [Funnel plot](funnel-plot.md)

A [small-study effect](small-study-effect.md) is a systematic relationship between study precision or size and estimated effect. It may result from selective publication, design differences, effect modification or other heterogeneity. A regression of effect on its [standard error](standard-error.md) tests one such relationship; it does not by itself determine which mechanism generated it.
