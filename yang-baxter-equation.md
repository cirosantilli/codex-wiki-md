# Yang-Baxter equation

↑ **Parent:** [Factorized scattering](factorized-scattering.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yang–Baxter_equation)

For a two-body operator $R(u)$ acting on a [tensor product](tensor-product.md) of particle species spaces, the spectral equation is $R_{12}(u)R_{13}(u+v)R_{23}(v)=R_{23}(v)R_{13}(u+v)R_{12}(u)$. It guarantees agreement between the two ways to reorder three particles in [factorized scattering](factorized-scattering.md). A [Yang–Baxter operator](yang-baxter-operator.md) is a related braid-form operator; multiplying by the permutation operator converts between the braided and unbraided conventions.
