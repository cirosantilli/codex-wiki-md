# Rational comb with a connecting spine

↑ **Parent:** [Path-connected space](path-connected-space.md)

The union of $\{0\}\times[0,1]$ with horizontal segments $[0,1]\times\{q\}$ for rational $q\in[0,1]$ is [path-connected](path-connected-space.md). Each point lies either on the vertical spine or on a segment meeting it. A path travels horizontally to the spine, vertically along it, and horizontally to its destination. This contrasts with disconnected rational-slope rays whose common origin has been excluded.
