# Conditional Jensen inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Conditional expectation](conditional-expectation.md)

For an integrable [random variable](random-variable-split.md) $X$, a sub-[sigma-algebra](sigma-algebra.md) $\mathcal G$, and a convex function $\phi$ with suitable integrability, $\phi(\mathbb E[X\mid\mathcal G])\le\mathbb E[\phi(X)\mid\mathcal G]$ almost surely. One proof writes $\phi$ as a supremum of supporting affine functions and applies monotonicity and [linearity](linearity.md) of [conditional expectation](conditional-expectation.md) to each. This proves the convex-loss form of the [Rao-Blackwell theorem](rao-blackwell-theorem.md).
