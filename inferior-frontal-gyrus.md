# Inferior frontal gyrus

↑ **Parent:** [Frontal lobe](frontal-lobe.md)

A frontal cortical gyrus containing anatomically and functionally distinguishable subregions. Its left [pars opercularis](pars-opercularis.md) and [pars triangularis](pars-triangularis.md) are commonly associated with [Broca's area](broca-s-area.md).
