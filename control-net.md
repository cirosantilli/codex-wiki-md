# Control net

↑ **Parent:** [Control point](control-point.md)

A mesh connecting a surface's [control points](control-point.md) according to the representation's indexing topology. A [tensor-product surface basis](tensor-product-surface-basis.md) gives a rectangular index grid and quadrilateral cells, even when its points in space are neither planar nor rectangular. The drawn mesh is a geometric skeleton, not generally the exact limit surface or an enclosing solid. Positive [partition of unity](partition-of-unity.md) coefficients give a [convex hull](convex-hull.md) bound on the relevant controls.
