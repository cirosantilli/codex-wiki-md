<h1 id="neron-tate-height">Néron–Tate height</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Height function](height-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Néron–Tate_height)

For a symmetric ample line bundle $L$ on an [abelian variety](abelian-variety-split.md), the canonical height is $\widehat h_L(P)=\lim_{n\to\infty}4^{-n}h_L([2^n]P)$. It is quadratic and differs from the associated logarithmic Weil height by a bounded function. The [canonical height of an elliptic curve](canonical-height-of-an-elliptic-curve.md) specializes this construction to a one-dimensional abelian variety.
