# Kinematic plume fluxes

↑ **Parent:** [Top-hat plume model](top-hat-plume-model.md)

Dividing the [mass flux](mass-flux.md), [momentum flux](momentum-flux.md), and density-weighted [buoyancy flux](buoyancy-flux.md) by ambient [mass density](density.md) gives the kinematic fluxes $q=\pi b^2V$, $m=\pi b^2V^2$, and $f=\pi b^2Vg(\rho_0-\rho)/\rho_0$. This convention makes dimensional [similarity solutions](similarity-solution.md) independent of the arbitrary ambient density unit.
