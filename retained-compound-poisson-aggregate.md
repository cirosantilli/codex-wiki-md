# Retained compound Poisson aggregate

↑ **Parent:** [Compound Poisson distribution](compound-poisson-distribution.md)

Applying a measurable per-claim retention $g$ to independent claim sizes preserves the [compound Poisson distribution](compound-poisson-distribution.md) form. The transformed severity is the [pushforward measure](pushforward-measure.md) of the original severity law. With count parameter $\lambda$, the retained aggregate has [expected value](expected-value.md) $\lambda\mathbb E g(X)$ and [variance](variance-split.md) $\lambda\mathbb E[g(X)^2]$. Zero retained marks may be removed by [Poisson thinning](poisson-thinning.md).
