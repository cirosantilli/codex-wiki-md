# Kramers radiative-zero envelope around a stellar core

↑ **Parent:** [Radiative envelope](radiative-envelope.md)

For a negligible-mass ideal-gas envelope above a core of [mass](mass.md) $M_c$, with constant [luminosity](luminosity.md) $L$ and [Kramers' opacity law](kramers-opacity-law.md), division of [hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) by radiative diffusion gives $P\,dP/dT=16\pi acGM_c\mathcal R\,T^{15/2}/(3\kappa_0L\mu)$. Taking the outer [pressure](pressure.md) constant to be negligible yields $P=CT^{17/4}$, where $C^2=64\pi acGM_c\mathcal R/(51\kappa_0L\mu)$, and $\rho=(\mu C/\mathcal R)T^{13/4}$. [Hydrostatic equilibrium](hydrostatic-equilibrium.md) then gives $dT/dr=-4\mu GM_c/(17\mathcal Rr^2)$, so $T=B/r+T_0$ with $B=4\mu GM_c/(17\mathcal R)$. The radiative-zero choice $T_0=0$ is exact for a vanishing-temperature boundary at infinity and a deep-envelope approximation for a very extended finite star. It is not implied by small surface [temperature](temperature.md) alone at a specified finite [radius](radius.md).
