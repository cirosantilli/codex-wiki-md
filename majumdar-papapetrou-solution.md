<h1 id="majumdar-papapetrou-solution">Majumdar–Papapetrou solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Einstein-Maxwell theory](einstein-maxwell-theory.md)

The four-dimensional [Majumdar–Papapetrou solution](majumdar-papapetrou-solution.md) is a static multi-black-hole Einstein-Maxwell solution with $H=1+\sum_iM_i/|\mathbf x-\mathbf x_i|$ and positive geometrized masses $M_i$. The electromagnetic potential can be written $A=\pm(H^{-1}-1)dt$, with a common sign of charge. Its mass at infinity is $\sum_iM_i$. Each distinct centre is an extremal horizon of zero surface gravity rather than a pointlike naked metric singularity.
