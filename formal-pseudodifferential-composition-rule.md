# Formal pseudodifferential composition rule

↑ **Parent:** [Formal pseudodifferential operator](formal-pseudodifferential-operator.md)

The generalized [Leibniz rule](leibniz-rule.md) extends composition of [differential operators](differential-operator.md) to all [integer](integer.md) $n$. Define $\binom nr=n(n-1)\cdots(n-r+1)/r!$. For nonnegative $n$ the sum stops at $r=n$. For $n=-s<0$, its [coefficients](coefficient.md) are $(-1)^r\binom{s+r-1}{r}$; in particular $\partial^{-1}f=f\partial^{-1}-f'\partial^{-2}+f''\partial^{-3}-\cdots$. Applying $\partial$ on the left cancels consecutive [derivative](derivative.md) terms and recovers multiplication by $f$, which explains the inverse expansion.
