# Symmetry of a cooperative-game value

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetry of players in a cooperative game](symmetry-of-players-in-a-cooperative-game.md)

A value has [cooperative-game symmetry](symmetry-of-a-cooperative-game-value.md) when it assigns equal [payoffs](payoff.md) to players satisfying [symmetry of players in a cooperative game](symmetry-of-players-in-a-cooperative-game.md). Thus equality of their [marginal contributions](marginal-contribution.md) is reflected in the allocation, rather than merely being a property of the underlying game.
