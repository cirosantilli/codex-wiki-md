# Superintegrable Hamiltonian system

↑ **Parent:** [Integrable Hamiltonian system](integrable-hamiltonian-system.md)

A [superintegrable Hamiltonian system](superintegrable-hamiltonian-system.md) with $n$ degrees of freedom has more than $n$ functionally independent [integrals of motion](integral-of-motion.md), including an integrable set of $n$ in involution. At a regular nonzero [Hamiltonian vector field](hamiltonian-vector-field.md), at most $2n-1$ independent integral differentials can annihilate the flow. A maximally superintegrable system attains that bound. The three-dimensional [Kepler orbit](kepler-orbit.md) has five independent integrals through energy, angular momentum and the [Laplace-Runge-Lenz vector](laplace-runge-lenz-vector.md). A closed orbit on one isolated resonance is weaker than maximal superintegrability on an open phase-space region.
