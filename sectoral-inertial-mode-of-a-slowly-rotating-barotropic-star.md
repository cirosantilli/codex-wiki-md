# Sectoral inertial mode of a slowly rotating barotropic star

↑ **Parent:** [Pressure equation for inertial oscillations of a barotropic star](pressure-equation-for-inertial-oscillations-of-a-barotropic-star.md)

For spherical [mass density](density.md) in the [anelastic approximation for a rotating barotropic star](anelastic-approximation-for-a-rotating-barotropic-star.md), the displayed [pressure](pressure.md) amplitude gives [velocity](velocity.md) proportional to $(-izR^{m-1},zR^{m-1},iR^m)$. This [velocity](velocity.md) is divergence free and tangent to spherical shells, so its mass-weighted divergence vanishes for every spherical [mass density](density.md) profile. It is an inertial r-mode driven by [Coriolis acceleration](coriolis-acceleration.md); with phase $e^{i\omega t+im\phi}$ its inertial frequency is $\omega=-(m-1)(m+2)\Omega/(m+1)$.
