# Deacon cell

↑ **Parent:** [Geophysical fluid dynamics](geophysical-fluid-dynamics-split.md)

A zonal-mean meridional overturning circulation associated with Southern Ocean wind forcing. In a southern-hemisphere channel with eastward wind stress, the mean circulation has northward surface transport and a southward deep return. Its Eulerian strength need not equal the residual water-mass transport because [eddy-induced overturning](eddy-induced-overturning.md) can oppose it.
