# Homomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic structure](algebraic-structure.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homomorphism)

A homomorphism is a map between [algebraic structures](algebraic-structure.md) preserving their specified operations. A [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md) preserves multiplication; a [ring homomorphism](ring-homomorphism.md) preserves addition and multiplication, with the identity convention specified; a [module homomorphism](module-homomorphism.md) preserves addition and scalar multiplication.
