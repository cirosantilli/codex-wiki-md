# Selberg sieve diagonalization

↑ **Parent:** [Selberg sieve weights](selberg-sieve-weights.md)

Put $L=\sqrt D$, $h(t)=\prod_{p\mid t}(g(p)^{-1}-1)$ and $y_t=\sum_{\substack{m\leq L\\t\mid m}}g(m)\rho_m$, where $g$ is a [multiplicative arithmetic function](multiplicative-function.md) supported on [squarefree integers](squarefree-integer.md). For [squarefree integers](squarefree-integer.md) $m,n$,

$$
g([m,n])=g(m)g(n)\sum_{t\mid(m,n)}h(t).
$$

Thus the quadratic form is $\Sigma=\sum_{t\leq L}h(t)y_t^2$. [Möbius inversion](mobius-inversion-formula.md) gives $g(d)\rho_d=\sum_{d\mid t\leq L}\mu(t/d)y_t$. On the permitted [squarefree integers](squarefree-integer.md), the constraint $\rho_1=1$ becomes $\sum_t\mu(t)y_t=1$. The [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) yields $\Sigma\geq1/J$, where $J=\sum_t1/h(t)$ over those integers; equality holds at $y_t=\mu(t)/(Jh(t))$.
