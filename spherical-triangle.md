# Spherical triangle

↑ **Parent:** [Spherical geometry](spherical-geometry.md)

A [spherical triangle](spherical-triangle.md) is bounded by three [great circle](great-circle.md) arcs. For the usual convex minor [spherical triangle](spherical-triangle.md), the vertices are neither antipodal nor on one [great circle](great-circle.md), and its angular sides and interior [angles](angle.md) lie in $(0,\pi)$. An interior [angle](angle.md) is measured between outgoing [tangent vectors](tangent-vector-split.md) to the two sides at a vertex, rather than between straight chords inside the [sphere](sphere.md). The [spherical law of sines](spherical-law-of-sines.md) and [spherical law of cosines](spherical-law-of-cosines.md) determine its side-angle relations; the [spherical excess formula](spherical-excess-formula.md) determines its [area](surface-area.md).
