# Spherical triangle

↑ **Parent:** [Spherical geometry](spherical-geometry.md)

A [spherical triangle](spherical-triangle.md) is bounded by three [great circle](great-circle.md) arcs. For the usual convex minor triangle, the vertices are neither antipodal nor on one [great circle](great-circle.md), and its sides and interior angles lie in $(0,\pi)$.
