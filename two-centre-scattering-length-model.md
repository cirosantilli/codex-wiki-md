# Two-centre scattering-length model

↑ **Parent:** [Bethe-Peierls boundary condition](bethe-peierls-boundary-condition.md)

Two separated short-range scatterers are coupled because the outgoing wave from either centre supplies part of the regular incoming field at the other. Applying the [Bethe-Peierls boundary condition](bethe-peierls-boundary-condition.md) at both centres gives the displayed two-by-two linear system, where $s_j$ is the incident plane-wave phase. [matrix](matrix.md) inversion sums repeated scattering between the centres. Near a small [determinant](determinant.md), effective-range corrections may matter even if the nominal microscopic range is short.
