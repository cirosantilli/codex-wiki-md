# Magnetic force

↑ **Parent:** [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md)

The magnetic contribution to the [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md) on a [point charge](point-charge.md). It is perpendicular to that charge's instantaneous [velocity](velocity.md), so its direct work on the charge is zero. Summing contributions in a conductor leads to the [magnetic force on a current-carrying wire](magnetic-force-on-a-current-carrying-wire.md); mechanical work in moving circuits must also account for the electric fields and constraints that maintain the current.
