# Parallel vector field

↑ **Parent:** [Connection (vector bundle)](connection-vector-bundle.md)

A [vector field](vector-field.md) is parallel for a specified [connection on a vector bundle](connection-vector-bundle.md) on the [tangent bundle](tangent-bundle.md) when its [covariant derivative](covariant-derivative.md) in every direction vanishes. Its values along every [smooth curve](smooth-curve.md) are related by [parallel transport](parallel-transport.md). For a [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md), its length is constant on each [connected component](connected-component.md), since the connection preserves the [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md).
