<h1 id="buhlmann-credibility-premium">Bühlmann credibility premium</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Bühlmann model](buhlmann-model.md)

The Bühlmann credibility premium is the best affine [mean squared error](mean-squared-error.md) estimate of a risk’s conditional claim [expected value](expected-value.md). In the [Bühlmann model](buhlmann-model.md) it is $Z\overline X+(1-Z)m$, where $Z=na/(na+v)$. The [linear least-squares projection](linear-least-squares-projection.md) equations use $\operatorname{Var}(X_j)=a+v$ and $\operatorname{Cov}(X_i,X_j)=a$ for distinct years.
