# Decorated-spin occupation insertion

↑ **Parent:** [Decimation of an alternating mixed-spin chain](decimation-of-an-alternating-mixed-spin-chain.md)

An [observable](observable.md) on a [spin](spin.md) removed by [decimation](decimation-of-an-alternating-mixed-spin-chain.md) is inserted before its local sum. For $\tau^2$, replace $V_{st}=\sum_\tau e^{\beta\tau(J_1s+J_2t)}$ by $Q_{st}=\sum_\tau\tau^2e^{\beta\tau(J_1s+J_2t)}=2\cosh[\beta(J_1s+J_2t)]$. Contracting the unchanged ring gives the displayed [thermal expectation value](expected-value-split.md). In the infinite chain it becomes $v_+^{\mathsf T}Qv_+/\lambda_+$ for [normalized](normalization.md) dominant [eigenvector](eigenvector.md) $v_+$.
