# Viscously damped monatomic-chain phonon

↑ **Parent:** [Monatomic-chain dispersion relation](monatomic-chain-dispersion-relation.md)

With real wave number and time convention $e^{-i\omega t}$, an on-site friction force $-\Gamma\dot u_j$ shifts the frequencies as displayed. Underdamped oscillations have amplitude envelope $e^{-\Gamma t/(2m)}$, while overdamped modes have two nonoscillatory rates. At small wave number a stable acoustic chain has slow decay $\omega\simeq-imc_s^2k^2/\Gamma$. Exactly uniform displacement remains a zero-restoring-force mode, so a constant displacement is not forced to vanish.
