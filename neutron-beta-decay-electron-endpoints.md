# Neutron beta-decay electron endpoints

↑ **Parent:** [Neutron](neutron-split.md)

In the [neutron](neutron-split.md) rest frame with a massless antineutrino, the smallest electron total energy is $m_ec^2$. At the upper endpoint the neutrino becomes soft and the electron and proton recoil with opposite equal momenta. The proton is not at rest there.
