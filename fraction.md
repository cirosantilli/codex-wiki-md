# Fraction

↑ **Parent:** [Rational number](rational-number.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fraction)

A fraction $a/b$ represents the quotient of its [numerator](numerator.md) $a$ by its nonzero [denominator](denominator.md) $b$. For [integers](integer-split.md) $a,b$ this is a [rational number](rational-number.md). Different fractions can represent the same number: $252/198=14/11$. Dividing both entries by their [greatest common divisor](greatest-common-divisor.md) gives a [reduced fraction](reduced-fraction.md), with the sign convention that the [denominator](denominator.md) is positive.
