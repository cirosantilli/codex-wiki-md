# Gravitational-electrostatic balance of equal charged spheres

↑ **Parent:** [Newton's law of universal gravitation](newton-s-law-of-universal-gravitation-split.md)

For two identical spheres sufficiently far apart, [Coulomb's law](coulomb-s-law.md) and [Newton's law of universal gravitation](newton-s-law-of-universal-gravitation-split.md) both give inverse-square forces. Equal-sign charges therefore balance attraction when the displayed charge-to-mass relation holds. For uniform mass density $\rho$, substituting $M=4\pi\rho a^3/3$ fixes the radius. Changing the separation does not restore balance if the charge-to-mass ratio is wrong. Spherically symmetric charge makes the monopole result exact outside each sphere; polarization can require a far-field approximation.
