# Powers of a rank-one operator

↑ **Parent:** [Rank-one operator](rank-one-operator.md)

Let $Kx=a\varphi(x)$ on a [vector space](vector-space-split.md), where $\varphi$ is a [linear functional](linear-functional.md). Its [image of a linear map](image-of-a-linear-map.md) lies in the span of $a$. Applying $K$ twice gives $K^2x=a\varphi(a)\varphi(x)=\varphi(a)Kx$. [Mathematical induction](mathematical-induction.md) then proves the displayed power formula. If $\varphi(a)=0$, $K^2=0$, even when $K\ne0$; this is a [nilpotent operator](nilpotent-linear-map.md). If $\varphi(a)\ne0$, then $K/\varphi(a)$ is an [idempotent linear map](projection-linear-algebra.md). In a real [inner product space](inner-product-space.md), the special case $\varphi(x)=b\cdot x$ gives $\varphi(a)=a\cdot b$.
