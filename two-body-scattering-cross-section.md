# Two-body scattering cross section

↑ **Parent:** [Cross section (physics)](cross-section-physics.md)

For distinguishable final particles, the [centre-of-momentum frame](center-of-momentum-frame.md) cross section is the displayed product of the invariant amplitude, incoming flux and [relativistic two-body phase space](relativistic-two-body-phase-space.md). Initial unpolarized states are averaged; unobserved final polarizations are summed. An isotropic amplitude integrates to $\sigma=(|\mathbf k_f|/|\mathbf p_i|)\overline{|\mathcal M|^2}/(16\pi s)$. A full-solid-angle integral for identical final particles additionally needs its symmetry factor.
