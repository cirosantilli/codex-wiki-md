# Real boundary bounds for a unit-disc H-hull

↑ **Parent:** [Mapping-out function of a compact H-hull](mapping-out-function-of-a-compact-h-hull.md)

If a [compact H-hull](compact-h-hull.md) lies in the unit disc, compare its tail [harmonic measure](harmonic-measure.md) with that of the empty hull and filled half-disc. The [Poisson kernel for the upper half-plane](poisson-kernel-for-the-upper-half-plane.md) turns the inclusion of Brownian exit events into $x\leq g_K(x)\leq x+1/x$ for $x>1$. Reflection gives $x+1/x\leq g_K(x)\leq x$ for $x<-1$.
