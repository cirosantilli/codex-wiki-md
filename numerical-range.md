# Numerical range

↑ **Parent:** [Unitary gate discrimination](unitary-gate-discrimination.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Numerical_range)

The numerical range of an operator $A$ is

$$
N(A)=\{\langle\psi|A|\psi\rangle:\|\psi\|=1\}.
$$
