# Szpilrajn extension theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Partially ordered set](partially-ordered-set.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Szpilrajn_extension_theorem)

Every [partial order](partially-ordered-set.md) has a [total order](total-order.md) extension. Apply [Zorn's lemma](zorn-s-lemma.md) to extensions ordered by inclusion: the union of a chain is a [partial order](partially-ordered-set.md). If a maximal extension leaves two elements incomparable, adjoining one comparison and taking its [transitive closure of a relation](transitive-closure-relation.md) creates no cycle, contradicting maximality. Quotienting a [preorder](preorder.md) by its [indifference relation](indifference-relation.md) and lifting a [total order](total-order.md) gives a complete weak-order extension preserving its strict part.
