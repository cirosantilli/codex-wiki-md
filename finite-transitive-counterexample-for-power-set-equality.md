# Finite transitive counterexample for power-set equality

↑ **Parent:** [Power-set equality is not upward absolute](power-set-equality-is-not-upward-absolute.md)

Let $M=4$ be a [Finite von Neumann ordinal](finite-von-neumann-ordinal.md) and $N=V_4$. With parameters $x=3$, $y=2$, the transitive structure $M$ satisfies the first-order power-set specification, since its available [subsets](subset.md) of $2$ are exactly $0,1,2$. In $N$, the additional [subset](subset.md) $\{1\}$ makes the same specification false. Bounded [logical formulas](logical-formula.md) are absolute between [transitive sets](transitive-set.md) by induction, so existential [logical formulas](logical-formula.md) with bounded matrices are upward absolute. This counterexample therefore rules out an unrestricted logical equivalence of power-set equality with a $\Sigma_1$ [logical formula](logical-formula.md).
