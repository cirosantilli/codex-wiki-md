# Half-harmonic oscillator

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md)

A [half-harmonic oscillator](half-harmonic-oscillator.md) has $V=m\omega^2x^2/2$ on $x>0$ and an infinite wall at $x=0$. Its [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) vanishes at the wall. Only the odd full-oscillator [energy eigenstates](energy-eigenstate.md) satisfy this condition; restricting them to the positive half-line and multiplying by $\sqrt2$ restores normalization. Thus $E_r=\hbar\omega(2r+3/2)$ for $r=0,1,\ldots$. With $\ell=\sqrt{\hbar/(m\omega)}$, its normalized [ground state](ground-state.md) is

$$
\psi_h(x)=\frac{2x}{\pi^{1/4}\ell^{3/2}}e^{-x^2/(2\ell^2)}\quad(x>0),\qquad \psi_h(x)=0\quad(x<0).
$$

It has no interior node on its allowed half-line, and energy $3\hbar\omega/2$.
