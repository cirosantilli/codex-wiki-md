# Limiting reagent

↑ **Parent:** [Stoichiometry](stoichiometry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limiting_reagent)

The [limiting reagent](limiting-reagent.md) is the [reactant](reactant.md) exhausted first if the specified [chemical reaction](chemical-reaction.md) proceeds to the maximum conversion permitted by [stoichiometry](stoichiometry.md). With initial values of [amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md) $n_{i,0}$ for the [reactants](reactant.md) and positive consumption coefficients $a_i$, the largest forward [extent of reaction](extent-of-reaction.md) is $\xi_{\max}=\min_i(n_{i,0}/a_i)$. Compare these quotients, rather than simply choosing the smallest initial quantity. This calculation predicts the maximum [reaction product](product-chemistry.md) quantity and identifies which [reactant](reactant.md) supply must increase to raise it. See [Flowers et al., Chemistry 2e, limiting reactants](https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/4-4-reaction-yields).

Start with $3\,\mathrm{mol}$ of [molecular hydrogen](molecular-hydrogen.md), $1\,\mathrm{mol}$ of [molecular oxygen](molecular-oxygen.md) and no [water](water.md). Assume complete conversion by the displayed [chemical reaction](chemical-reaction.md), without competing reactions. Then

$$
\xi_{\max}=\min\left(\frac{3\,\mathrm{mol}}2,\frac{1\,\mathrm{mol}}1\right)=1\,\mathrm{mol}.
$$

The [molecular oxygen](molecular-oxygen.md) is the [limiting reagent](limiting-reagent.md). Consumption of $2\,\mathrm{mol}$ of [molecular hydrogen](molecular-hydrogen.md) and $1\,\mathrm{mol}$ of [molecular oxygen](molecular-oxygen.md) forms $2\,\mathrm{mol}$ of [water](water.md), leaving $1\,\mathrm{mol}$ of [molecular hydrogen](molecular-hydrogen.md). Initially there are $1.806642228\times10^{24}$ [molecular hydrogen](molecular-hydrogen.md) [molecules](molecule.md) and $6.02214076\times10^{23}$ [molecular oxygen](molecular-oxygen.md) [molecules](molecule.md); after conversion there are $1.204428152\times10^{24}$ [water](water.md) [molecules](molecule.md) and $6.02214076\times10^{23}$ leftover [molecular hydrogen](molecular-hydrogen.md) [molecules](molecule.md).

As a check, the initial inventory has $6\,\mathrm{mol}$ of [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md) and $2\,\mathrm{mol}$ of [oxygen](oxygen.md) [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md). The final [water](water.md) contains $4\,\mathrm{mol}$ of [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) [chemical atoms](atom-physics.md), and leftover [molecular hydrogen](molecular-hydrogen.md) supplies the remaining $2\,\mathrm{mol}$. The atom totals balance even though the total [amount of substance](amount-of-substance.md) of [molecules](molecule.md) falls from four to three [moles](mole-unit.md).
