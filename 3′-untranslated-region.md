# 3′ untranslated region

↑ **Parent:** [Messenger RNA](messenger-rna.md)

The segment of a [messenger RNA](messenger-rna.md) downstream of its translated coding region. It can contain [cis-regulatory elements](cis-regulatory-element.md) affecting localization, stability and [biological translation](translation-biology.md).
