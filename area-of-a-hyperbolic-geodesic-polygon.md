# Area of a hyperbolic geodesic polygon

↑ **Parent:** [Gauss-Bonnet theorem](gauss-bonnet-theorem.md)

In the curvature-minus-one [Poincaré half-plane model](poincare-half-plane-model.md), a geodesic polygon with $n$ sides and interior angles $\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_n$ has area

$$
(n-2)\pi-\sum_{j=1}^n\alpha_j.
$$

This is the polygonal [Gauss-Bonnet theorem](gauss-bonnet-theorem.md).
