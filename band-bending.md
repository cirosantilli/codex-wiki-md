# Band bending

↑ **Parent:** [Surface state](surface-state.md)

Spatial variation of the [valence band](valence-band.md) and [conduction band](conduction-band.md) edges associated with a varying [electric potential](electric-potential.md). Near a [semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md) boundary it can deplete mobile [charge carriers](charge-carrier.md) or create a [semiconductor inversion layer](semiconductor-inversion-layer.md).
