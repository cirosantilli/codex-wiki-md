# PI3K regulatory subunit p85

↑ **Parent:** [Phosphoinositide 3-kinase](phosphoinositide-3-kinase.md)

A regulatory subunit associated with a class I [phosphoinositide 3-kinase](phosphoinositide-3-kinase.md) complex. A band of its approximate mass does not establish its identity or whether a detected phosphorylation is activating.
