<h1 id="cramer-lundberg-ruin-asymptotic">Cramér–Lundberg ruin asymptotic</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Lundberg inequality](lundberg-inequality.md)

In the [classical risk model](classical-risk-model.md) with positive [relative safety loading](relative-safety-loading.md) $\rho$ and [adjustment coefficient](adjustment-coefficient.md) $R$, tilting the ruin [defective renewal equation](defective-renewal-equation.md) gives a proper [renewal equation](renewal-equation.md). The [key renewal theorem](key-renewal-theorem.md) yields $e^{Ru}\psi(u)\to \rho/[R\int_0^\infty xe^{Rx}f_I(x)\,dx]$. The constant is positive if the denominator is finite and zero if it is infinite; the claim-size density provides the nonarithmetic hypothesis.
