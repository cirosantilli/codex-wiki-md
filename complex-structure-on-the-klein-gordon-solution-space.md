# Complex structure on the Klein-Gordon solution space

↑ **Parent:** [Klein-Gordon field](klein-gordon-field.md)

On the real [Klein-Gordon field](klein-gordon-field.md) solution space with conserved [symplectic form](symplectic-form.md) $\Omega$, a compatible real-linear map $J$ obeys $J^2=-I$, preserves $\Omega$, and makes $\mu(f,g)=\Omega(f,Jg)$ positive definite. Its $+i$ eigenspace in the complexified space has positive [Klein-Gordon inner product](klein-gordon-inner-product.md) with the convention $(u,v)_{KG}=i\Omega(\bar u,v)$. It defines the one-particle [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) and a free-field [Fock vacuum](fock-vacuum.md).
