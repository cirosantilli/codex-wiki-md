# Compact-core completeness for a Euclidean end

↑ **Parent:** [Geodesic completeness](geodesic-completeness.md)

Suppose these sets exhaust a manifold, and its [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md) is Euclidean beyond some radius in the end coordinates. A finite-time [geodesic](geodesic.md) has fixed speed and cannot increase its exterior radius faster than that speed. It remains in one [compact](compact-space.md) core enlargement, with velocity in a [compact](compact-space.md) disk bundle. The [geodesic](geodesic.md) equation therefore continues beyond every finite parameter endpoint. The [compactness](compact-space.md) hypothesis is substantive: the open exterior of a closed Euclidean ball, with empty core, is incomplete at its inner boundary despite having a [Euclidean metric](euclidean-metric.md) at infinity.
