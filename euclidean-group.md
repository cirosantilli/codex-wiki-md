# Euclidean group

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean geometry](euclidean-geometry.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euclidean_group)

The [Euclidean group](euclidean-group.md) $E(n)=O(n)\ltimes\mathbb R^n$ is the [group](group-split.md) of all [isometries](isometry.md) $x\mapsto Ax+b$ of [Euclidean space](euclidean-norm.md), with $A$ an [orthogonal matrix](orthogonal-matrix.md). Its orientation-preserving [subgroup](subgroup.md) is $SE(n)=SO(n)\ltimes\mathbb R^n$, the [semidirect product](semidirect-product.md) of the [special orthogonal group](special-orthogonal-group.md) with translations. The [Euclidean motion of Euclidean three-space](euclidean-motion-of-euclidean-three-space.md) and the [proper Euclidean motion of Euclidean three-space](proper-euclidean-motion-of-euclidean-three-space.md) are the respective three-dimensional instances.
