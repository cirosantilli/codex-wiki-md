# Two-qubit state

↑ **Parent:** [Qubit](qubit.md)

A [quantum state](quantum-state.md) on the [tensor product](tensor-product.md) of two two-dimensional complex state spaces. A normalized pure state has squared coefficient moduli summing to one. It is a [product state](product-state.md) exactly when $\alpha\delta=\beta\gamma$, by the [two-qubit product-state determinant criterion](two-qubit-product-state-determinant-criterion.md). The weaker equality of moduli only characterizes [independence](independent-random-variables.md) of the computational-basis outcomes; phases can preserve [quantum entanglement](entangled-state.md) despite those independent statistics.
