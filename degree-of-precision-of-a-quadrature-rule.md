# Degree of precision of a quadrature rule

↑ **Parent:** [Quadrature rule](quadrature-rule.md)

The [degree of precision of a quadrature rule](degree-of-precision-of-a-quadrature-rule.md) is the largest integer $d$ such that $Q(p)=I_\rho(p)$ for every [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) $p$ of [polynomial degree](degree-of-a-polynomial.md) at most $d$. By [linearity](linearity.md), checking the [monomials](monomial.md) $1,x,\ldots,x^d$ suffices. Exactness for one higher-degree [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) alone does not raise this degree: every lower [polynomial degree](degree-of-a-polynomial.md) must also be covered.
