# Geometric sum of shape-two gamma variables

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric-sum moment-generating function](geometric-sum-moment-generating-function.md)

Let the summands have [gamma distribution](gamma-distribution.md) with shape two and rate $\beta$, and let the independent positive-support [geometric distribution](geometric-distribution.md) count have parameter $p$. Put $a=\beta(1-\sqrt{1-p})$ and $b=\beta(1+\sqrt{1-p})$. The aggregate [moment-generating function](moment-generating-function.md) factors as $ab/((a-t)(b-t))$, so it is the sum of two independent [exponential distributions](exponential-distribution.md) of rates $a,b$. Its [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) is $ab(e^{-as}-e^{-bs})/(b-a)$ for $s>0$, nonnegative and integrating to one. This contrasts with the [geometric sum of exponential variables](geometric-sum-of-exponential-variables.md), which is itself exponential.
