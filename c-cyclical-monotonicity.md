# c-cyclical monotonicity

↑ **Parent:** [Optimal transport](optimal-transport.md)

A set of source-destination pairs has this property if every finite cyclic reassignment of destinations cannot lower its total cost. A [transport plan](transport-plan.md) has the property if it is concentrated on such a set. For a general cost, this is stronger than a two-point monotonicity test.
