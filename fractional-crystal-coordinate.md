# Fractional crystal coordinate

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic basis of a crystal](atomic-basis-of-a-crystal.md)

[Fractional crystal coordinates](fractional-crystal-coordinate.md) are the dimensionless coefficients in $r=ua_1+va_2+wa_3$ for specified [unit cell](unit-cell.md) vectors. They are not Cartesian lengths unless the vectors themselves are included. With primitive vectors, replacing $u,v,w$ by values differing by [integers](integer-split.md) selects an equivalent position in another [unit cell](unit-cell.md).

In the two-dimensional example under [crystal structure](crystal-structure.md), B has position $(0.65,0.55)$ in ordinary length coordinates, but [fractional crystal coordinates](fractional-crystal-coordinate.md) $(0.65/2,0.55/1.5)=(13/40,11/30)$. Multiplying these coefficients by the two translation vectors recovers the drawn position. Reporting the axes is therefore part of reporting a structure.
