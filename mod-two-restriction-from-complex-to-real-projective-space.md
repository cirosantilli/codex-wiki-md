# Mod-two restriction from complex to real projective space

↑ **Parent:** [Cohomology ring of complex projective space](cohomology-ring-of-complex-projective-space.md)

Let $i:\mathbb{RP}^d\hookrightarrow\mathbb{CP}^d$ be the standard inclusion. If $h=c_1(\mathcal O(1))\bmod2$ and $a=w_1(\gamma_d)$, then

$$
i^*h=a^2.
$$

The restricted complex [tautological bundle](tautological-bundle.md) is the [complexification of a real vector bundle](complexification-of-a-real-vector-bundle.md) $\gamma_d\otimes_{\mathbb R}\mathbb C$. Its underlying real bundle is $\gamma_d\oplus\gamma_d$. The [Whitney product formula for Stiefel–Whitney classes](whitney-product-formula-for-stiefel-whitney-classes.md) gives total class $(1+a)^2=1+a^2$, while the [Stiefel–Whitney class of the underlying real bundle of a complex line](stiefel-whitney-class-of-the-underlying-real-bundle-of-a-complex-line.md) identifies its second class with the reduced [First Chern class](first-chern-class.md). Dualizing changes the integral sign but has no effect modulo two. Thus the full [induced map on cohomology](induced-map-on-cohomology.md) sends $h^j$ to $a^{2j}$.
