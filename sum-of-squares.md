# Sum of squares

↑ **Parent:** [Arithmetic](arithmetic-split.md)

A sum of squares adds the [squares of numbers](square-of-a-number.md) $a_i^2$ for specified [real numbers](real-number.md) $a_i$. It is nonnegative and vanishes exactly when every term is zero. For the coordinates of a [vector](vector.md), it is the squared [Euclidean norm](euclidean-norm.md); for [regression residuals](regression-residual.md), it gives the [residual sum of squares](residual-sum-of-squares.md) minimized by [ordinary least squares](ordinary-least-squares.md).
