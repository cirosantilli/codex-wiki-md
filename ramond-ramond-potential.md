<h1 id="ramond-ramond-potential">Ramond–Ramond potential</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ramond–Ramond sector](ramond-ramond-sector.md)

A differential-form [gauge potential](gauge-field.md) from the [Ramond–Ramond sector](ramond-ramond-sector.md). A [D-brane](d-brane.md) carries electric or magnetic charge under the appropriate potential. Circle reduction of the M-theory metric gives the IIA one-form potential, while its three-form gives the IIA three-form potential.
