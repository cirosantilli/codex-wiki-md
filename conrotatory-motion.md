# Conrotatory motion

↑ **Parent:** [Electrocyclic reaction](electrocyclic-reaction.md)

Terminal rotation in the same rotational sense under a common viewing convention during an [electrocyclic reaction](electrocyclic-reaction.md). It is distinguished from [disrotatory motion](disrotatory-motion.md); looking from opposite ends without changing conventions can reverse an apparent clockwise label.
