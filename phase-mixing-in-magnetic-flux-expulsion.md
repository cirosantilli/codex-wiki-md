# Phase mixing in magnetic flux expulsion

↑ **Parent:** [Flux expulsion](flux-expulsion.md)

Differential circulation of a weak [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md) winds its flux function into progressively smaller radial scales. In a smooth eddy with angular-velocity gradient of order $U/L^2$, its radial wavenumber grows as $k_r\sim Ut/L^2$. Resistive damping becomes substantial when $\eta\int_0^t k_r^2dt\sim1$, giving $t\sim(L^4/(\eta U^2))^{1/3}=(L/U)\mathrm{Rm}^{1/3}$. This estimate presumes differential rotation and negligible magnetic feedback; a rigidly rotating eddy does not create this phase mixing. Finite [magnetic diffusivity](magnetic-diffusivity.md) then permits weak interior field and boundary concentration, unlike exact ideal flux freezing.
