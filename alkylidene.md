# Alkylidene

↑ **Parent:** [Organometallic compound](organometallic-compound.md)

A carbon fragment doubly bonded to a metal in the present organometallic usage. Metal–alkylidene exchange with an [alkene](alkene.md) underlies [olefin metathesis](olefin-metathesis.md).
