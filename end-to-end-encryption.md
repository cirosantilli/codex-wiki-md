# End-to-end encryption

↑ **Parent:** [Encryption](encryption.md)

[End-to-end encryption](end-to-end-encryption.md) protects content between specified ultimate application [endpoints](communication-endpoint.md), rather than merely protecting transport to an intermediary that may read it. A claimed provider-excluding secrecy property requires that provider to lack the relevant decryption capability. [Endpoint](communication-endpoint.md) compromise and communication metadata remain separate issues.
