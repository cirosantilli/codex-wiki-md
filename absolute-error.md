# Absolute error

↑ **Parent:** [Approximation error](approximation-error.md)

The [absolute error](absolute-error.md) is the [absolute value](absolute-value.md) of the [approximation error](approximation-error.md), $|x_{\mathrm{approx}}-x|$, measured in the same units as the numerical reference $x$. For example, approximating $1/3$ by $0.33$ gives [absolute error](absolute-error.md) $|0.33-1/3|=1/300$.
