# Power-decay characterization of the spectral radius

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral radius formula](spectral-radius-formula.md)

In a complex unital [Banach algebra](banach-algebra-split.md), the [spectral radius formula](spectral-radius-formula.md) makes the root norms of $(x/t)^n$ tend to $r(x)/t$. If $t>r(x)$, those norms are eventually bounded above by a geometric sequence and the powers vanish. Conversely, vanishing powers eventually have norm less than one; the infimum version of the radius formula then forces $r(x)/t<1$. Consequently $r(x)=\inf\{t>0:(x/t)^n\to0\}$, including the case $r(x)=0$.
