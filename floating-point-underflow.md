# Floating-point underflow

↑ **Parent:** [Floating-point arithmetic](floating-point-arithmetic-split.md)

[Floating-point underflow](floating-point-underflow.md) occurs when a nonzero numerical result is smaller in magnitude than the representable range or loses precision in the subnormal range. A long product of probabilities can become zero despite a mathematically positive result. Adding [logarithms](logarithm.md) avoids forming the product; adding alternative path probabilities then requires [log-sum-exp](log-sum-exp-function.md) or controlled rescaling.
