# Second-order density Laplacian counterterm

↑ **Parent:** [Effective field theory of large-scale structure](effective-field-theory-of-large-scale-structure.md)

For the one-dimensional [B411 contribution to the one-loop matter bispectrum](b411-contribution-to-the-one-loop-matter-bispectrum.md), contracting two hard inputs in the fourth-order field gives $-(I_\Lambda/2)k^2\delta^{(2)}$, where $I_\Lambda=\int_{q_h<|q|<\Lambda}dq\,P_L(q)/(2\pi q^2)$. A [counterterm](counterterm.md) $C(\Lambda)\partial_x^2\delta^{(2)}$ cancels this cutoff-dependent part when $C(\Lambda)=-I_\Lambda/2+C_{\rm finite}$. This sign follows from $\partial_x^2\leftrightarrow-k^2$; the finite coefficient describes unresolved short-scale dynamics.
