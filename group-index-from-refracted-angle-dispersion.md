# Group index from refracted-angle dispersion

↑ **Parent:** [Group refractive index](group-refractive-index.md)

For vacuum incidence at fixed angle $\theta_i$ into a transparent [isotropic](isotropy.md) medium, [Snell's law](snell-s-law.md) gives $n(\lambda_0)=\sin\theta_i/\sin\theta_t(\lambda_0)$. Thus $dn/d\lambda_0=-n\cot\theta_t\,d\theta_t/d\lambda_0$, and

$$
n_g=n\left[1+\lambda_0\cot\theta_t\frac{d\theta_t}{d\lambda_0}\right],\qquad v_g=c/n_g.
$$

Angles must be differentiated in radians. This determines the local [group velocity](group-velocity.md) from a first derivative of measured refraction angles; pulse broadening needs further information about [group velocity dispersion](group-velocity-dispersion.md) and absorption.
