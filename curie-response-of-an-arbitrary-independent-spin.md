# Curie response of an arbitrary independent spin

↑ **Parent:** [Paramagnetism](paramagnetism.md)

At fixed positive [temperature](temperature-split.md) and weak field, independent spin-$J$ moments have total moment $M=N(g\mu_B)^2J(J+1)B/(3k_BT)$. This is the [Curie law](curie-s-law.md); the corresponding [density](density.md) divides by the sample volume.
