<h1 id="prisoner-s-dilemma">Prisoner's dilemma</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Game theory](game-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prisoner's_dilemma)

The [prisoner's dilemma](prisoner-s-dilemma.md) has cooperation and defection actions, with mutual cooperation reward $R$, mutual defection [payoff](payoff.md) $P$, unilateral temptation $T$ and exploited-cooperator [payoff](payoff.md) $S$. Defection dominates in the one-shot game. The extra inequality $2R\ge T+S$ expresses cooperative efficiency against alternating asymmetric outcomes; it is a further assumption, not implied by the basic ordering.
