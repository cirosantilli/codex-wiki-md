# Full-row-rank matrix manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md)

The real $m$ by $n$ matrices of [matrix rank](matrix-rank.md) $m\leq n$ form an open [smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md) $X_{m,n}\subseteq\mathbb R^{mn}$. Indeed, it is the union of the open sets on which a chosen $m$ by $m$ minor has nonzero [determinant](determinant.md), so its dimension is $mn$.
