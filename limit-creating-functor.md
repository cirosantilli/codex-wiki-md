# Limit-creating functor

↑ **Parent:** [Limit-reflecting functor](limit-reflecting-functor.md)

A [functor](functor.md) creates [categorical limits](categorical-limit.md) of a specified shape when every given limiting [categorical cone](cone-over-a-diagram.md) over an image diagram uniquely lifts to a [categorical cone](cone-over-a-diagram.md) over the original diagram, and the lift is limiting. This is stronger than just being a [limit-reflecting functor](limit-reflecting-functor.md). Forgetting arrows from a [functor category](functor-category.md) creates its [pointwise limits in a functor category](pointwise-limits-in-a-functor-category.md).
