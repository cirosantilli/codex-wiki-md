# First integral of a cubic-quadratic reversible flow

↑ **Parent:** [First integral of an ordinary differential equation](first-integral-of-an-ordinary-differential-equation.md)

For $\dot x=y^3-y$, $\dot y=x-y^2$, set $a_\pm=(1\pm\sqrt3)/2$ and $F_\pm=x-1-a_\pm(y^2-1)$. Direct differentiation gives $\dot F_\pm=-2a_\pm yF_\pm$, proving the displayed first integral. The invariant parabolas meet at saddles $(1,\pm1)$ and bound a [heteroclinic cycle](heteroclinic-cycle.md). Inside that lens, the logarithm of $I$ has a strict nondegenerate maximum at the origin, proving a nonlinear [center equilibrium](center-equilibrium.md), rather than inferring one solely from imaginary Jacobian eigenvalues.
