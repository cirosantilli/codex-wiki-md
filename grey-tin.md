# Grey tin

↑ **Parent:** [Tin](tin.md)

The diamond-cubic form of [tin](tin.md) favoured below about $13\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ at ambient pressure. Its lower density than [white tin](white-tin.md) contributes to the damage associated with [tin pest](tin-pest.md).
