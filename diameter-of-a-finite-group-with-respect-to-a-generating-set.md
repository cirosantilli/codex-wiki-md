# Diameter of a finite group with respect to a generating set

↑ **Parent:** [Cayley graph](cayley-graph.md)

For a [finite group](finite-group.md) $G$ with a symmetric [generating set](generating-set.md) $S$, its diameter is

$$
\operatorname{diam}_S(G)=\min\{n:S^n=G\}.
$$

Equivalently, it is the diameter of the [Cayley graph](cayley-graph.md) $\operatorname{Cay}_S(G)$.
