# Isolated fibre point forces dominance in equal dimensions

↑ **Parent:** [Dimension of image of a morphism](dimension-of-image-of-a-morphism.md)

Let $f:X\to Y$ be a [proper morphism](proper-morphism.md) of irreducible [algebraic varieties](algebraic-variety.md) with equal dimension. If some fibre has an isolated point, its corresponding irreducible component has dimension zero. Apply the [fiber dimension theorem](fiber-dimension-theorem.md) to $X\to f(X)$: $0\ge\dim X-\dim f(X)$. Thus the closed image has dimension $\dim Y$ and equals $Y$. The [incidence proof that a cubic surface contains a line](incidence-proof-that-a-cubic-surface-contains-a-line.md) is an application; the entire witness fibre need not be finite.
