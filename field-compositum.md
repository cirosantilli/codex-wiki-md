# Field compositum

↑ **Parent:** [Field extension](field-extension.md)

Inside a common overfield, the compositum $L_1L_2$ is the smallest field containing both $L_1$ and $L_2$.

The multiplication image of the [tensor product of fields](tensor-product-of-fields.md) in a common overfield generates the compositum as a field. For algebraic extensions that image is already a field; in general it need not be. The compositum is therefore distinct from the tensor product algebra.
