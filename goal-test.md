# Goal test

↑ **Parent:** [Search problem](search-problem.md)

A condition deciding whether a state successfully completes a [search problem](search-problem.md). Its timing must agree with the [search transition model](search-transition-model.md): arriving at a destination before an adversary's compulsory response need not be a successful terminal state.
