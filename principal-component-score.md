# Principal component score

↑ **Parent:** [Principal component](principal-component.md)

A score is an observation's coordinate along a [principal component](principal-component.md) direction. Center an observation by the fitted [sample mean](sample-mean.md), apply the fitted variable scaling when using [principal component analysis on a correlation matrix](principal-component-analysis-on-a-correlation-matrix.md), then take its [inner product](inner-product.md) with the unit [principal component loading](principal-component-loading.md) vector. New observations must use the training center and scaling. The score [variance](variance-split.md) equals the corresponding [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) when the covariance normalization is consistent.
