# Electrometer

↑ **Parent:** [Electric charge](electric-charge-split.md)

An [electrometer](electrometer.md) measures small [electric potential differences](electric-potential-difference.md) or [electric charges](electric-charge-split.md) while drawing very little [electric current](electric-current.md). The conducting-pail experiment in [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) uses a voltage response to infer [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) with a fixed effective [capacitance](capacitance.md).
