# Isentropic process

↑ **Parent:** [Adiabatic process](adiabatic-process.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isentropic_process)

An isentropic process keeps the [entropy](entropy.md) constant. Every reversible [adiabatic process](adiabatic-process.md) is isentropic, while an irreversible adiabatic process can increase entropy.
