# All-pay effort identity

↑ **Parent:** [Rank-order contest](rank-order-contest.md)

Suppose values are nonnegative, the [rank-order expected prize allocation](rank-order-expected-prize-allocation.md) $a$ is increasing and differentiable, and $a(\underline v)=0$. With unit effort cost and zero effort at the lowest type, the symmetric equilibrium effort is $b(v)=v a(v)-\int_{\underline v}^v a(t)dt$. A true type $v$ imitating type $z$ receives utility $v a(z)-b(z)$, whose derivative in $z$ is $(v-z)a'(z)$. It increases up to $v$ and decreases afterwards, proving the [best response](best-response.md) property. This is the [interim payment identity](interim-payment-identity.md) specialized to an [all-pay contest](all-pay-auction.md).
