# Simple random walk on the hypercube

↑ **Parent:** [Simple random walk](simple-random-walk.md)

On the $n$-dimensional [hypercube graph](hypercube-graph.md), simple random walk flips one uniformly chosen coordinate at each step. Its stationary distribution is uniform on the $2^n$ vertices, so its mean return time is $2^n$.
