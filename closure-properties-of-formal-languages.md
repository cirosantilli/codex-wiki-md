# Closure properties of formal languages

↑ **Parent:** [Abstract family of languages](abstract-family-of-languages.md)

The axioms of an [abstract family of languages](abstract-family-of-languages.md) require particular [closure properties of formal languages](closure-properties-of-formal-languages.md). A language class is closed under an operation when applying that operation to members always produces another member of the class.
