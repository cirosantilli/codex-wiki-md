# Projectibility

↑ **Parent:** [New riddle of induction](new-riddle-of-induction.md)

The eligibility of a predicate or generalization for extending an inference beyond examined cases. Agreement with positive instances alone does not establish projectibility: [grue](grue.md) and green can fit the same past examples while supporting incompatible predictions.
