# Classical equations from Ehrenfest theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Ehrenfest theorem](ehrenfest-theorem.md)

For $H=p^2/(2m)+U(x)$, the [canonical commutation relation](canonical-commutation-relation.md) gives

$$
m\frac d{dt}\langle x\rangle=\langle p\rangle,
\qquad
\frac d{dt}\langle p\rangle=-\langle U'(x)\rangle,
\qquad
\frac d{dt}\langle H\rangle=0.
$$

These are the expectation-value analogues of [Newton's second law](newton-s-second-law.md) and conservation of energy.
