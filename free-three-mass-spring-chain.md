# Free three-mass spring chain

↑ **Parent:** [Normal mode](normal-mode.md)

For free-end [masses](mass.md) $m,\alpha m,m$ joined by two identical [springs](spring.md), the displayed squared [frequencies](frequency.md) have displacement vectors $(1,1,1)$, $(1,0,-1)$ and $(1,-2/\alpha,1)$. Distinct [eigenvectors](eigenvector.md) are [orthogonal](orthogonal-vectors.md) in the [mass](mass.md) [inner product](inner-product-split.md). The zero mode carries translation, while the middle [mass](mass.md) does not move in the antisymmetric internal mode. Fixed end [springs](spring.md) would give a different spectrum.
