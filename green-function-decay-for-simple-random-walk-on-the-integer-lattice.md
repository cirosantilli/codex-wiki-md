# Green-function decay for simple random walk on the integer lattice

↑ **Parent:** [Green function of a transient weighted graph](green-function-of-a-transient-weighted-graph.md)

For [simple random walk](simple-random-walk.md) on $\mathbb Z^d$ with $d\geq3$,

$$
g(0,x)\asymp |x|^{2-d}.
$$

Consequently the probability of ever hitting $x$ from the origin has the same order.
