# Number of homomorphisms between finite cyclic groups

↑ **Parent:** [Homomorphism between cyclic groups](homomorphism-between-cyclic-groups.md)

A [group homomorphism](group-homomorphism.md) from $\mathbb Z_m$ to the additive [cyclic group](cyclic-group.md) $\mathbb Z_n$ is determined by the image $a$ of $1$, subject to $ma\equiv0\pmod n$. Conversely every such $a$ gives a well-defined map $k\mapsto ka$. If $d=\gcd(m,n)$, the condition is equivalent to $n/d$ dividing $a$. There are exactly $d$ such residues modulo $n$, proving the formula. More generally, homomorphisms from $\mathbb Z_m$ to any [group](group-split.md) correspond to target elements whose [order of a group element](order-of-a-group-element.md) divides $m$.
