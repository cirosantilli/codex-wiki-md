# Closed differential one-form

↑ **Parent:** [Closed differential form](closed-differential-form.md)

A [differential one-form](one-form.md) is closed when its [exterior derivative](exterior-derivative.md) vanishes. In coordinates, $\eta=\eta_i dx^i$ is closed exactly when $\partial_i\eta_j=\partial_j\eta_i$. The [Poincaré lemma](poincare-lemma.md) makes it locally an [exact differential form](exact-differential-form.md), but global exactness can fail through nonzero integrals around closed curves.
