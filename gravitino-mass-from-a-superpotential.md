# Gravitino mass from a superpotential

↑ **Parent:** [Supergravity F-term potential](supergravity-f-term-potential.md)

The [gravitino](gravitino.md) mass parameter in four-dimensional [supergravity](supergravity.md) is the displayed expression. In a zero-energy [supersymmetry breaking](supersymmetry-breaking.md) vacuum with only [F-term](f-term.md) breaking, $K_{i\bar j}F^i\overline{F^j}=3m_{3/2}^2/\kappa^2$. Writing this norm as $\Lambda_{\mathrm{SUSY}}^4$ gives $m_{3/2}=\Lambda_{\mathrm{SUSY}}^2/(\sqrt3m_p)$. It is small relative to $\Lambda_{\mathrm{SUSY}}$ when the breaking scale is below the [Planck mass](planck-mass.md). The [super-Higgs mechanism](super-higgs-mechanism.md) supplies the longitudinal [gravitino](gravitino.md) states.
