# Cosmological bolometric background from conserved sources

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmological spectral background](cosmological-spectral-background.md)

Normalize the [scale factor](scale-factor-cosmology.md) to $a_0=1$. Identical isotropic sources of [bolometric luminosity](luminosity.md) $L$ and conserved [comoving number density](comoving-number-density.md) $n_0=a^3n$ produce all-sky [radiative flux](radiative-flux.md) $\mathcal F_0=cLn_0\int a(t)\,dt$ in a transparent [FLRW metric](friedmann-lemaitre-robertson-walker-metric.md). The [source counts on an FLRW past light cone](source-counts-on-an-flrw-past-light-cone.md) and [cosmological flux dimming](cosmological-flux-dimming.md) cancel the transverse shell-area factor. For arbitrary proper source density the expression is instead $cL\int n(t)a(t)^4dt$. The intensity per [solid angle](solid-angle.md) is $\mathcal F_0/(4\pi)$. A detector of collecting area $A$ receives power $A\mathcal F_0$ under an all-sky effective-area convention.
