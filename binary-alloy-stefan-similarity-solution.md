# Binary alloy Stefan similarity solution

↑ **Parent:** [Stefan problem](stefan-problem.md)

A two-phase diffusion model with constant properties has [similarity solutions](similarity-solution.md) built from the [complementary error function](complementary-error-function.md) in each half-line. Equilibrium gives $T_a=-mC_a$ and $C_s(a)=k_D C_a$. The [Stefan condition](stefan-condition.md) and interfacial solute balance determine $\lambda$ and $C_a$; finite diffusion on both sides must be retained when constructing the narrow transition near $\lambda=0$.
