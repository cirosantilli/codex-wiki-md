# 2-Oxazoline

↑ **Parent:** [Heterocyclic compound](heterocyclic-compound.md)

A [2-Oxazoline](2-oxazoline.md) has the five-membered ring $\mathrm{O{-}C{=}N{-}CH_2{-}CH_2}$. A substituent at its imine [carbon](carbon.md) can be introduced by aromatic capture of an activated cyclic [carbamate](carbamate.md).
