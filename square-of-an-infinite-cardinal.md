# Square of an infinite cardinal

↑ **Parent:** [Infinite cardinal arithmetic](infinite-cardinal-arithmetic.md)

Assuming the [axiom of choice](axiom-of-choice.md), every infinite cardinal satisfies

$$
\kappa\cdot\kappa=\kappa.
$$

One proof takes a least counterexample $\kappa$, identifies cardinals with [initial ordinals](initial-ordinal.md), and well-orders $\kappa\times\kappa$ first by $\max\{\alpha,\beta\}$. Every proper initial segment then has cardinality below $\kappa$ by minimality, so the whole order has cardinality at most $\kappa$, contradicting the choice of $\kappa$.
