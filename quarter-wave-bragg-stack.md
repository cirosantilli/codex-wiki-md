# Quarter-wave Bragg stack

↑ **Parent:** [Distributed Bragg reflector](distributed-bragg-reflector.md)

At normal incidence, alternating layers with indices $n_H,n_L$ and optical thicknesses $n_Hd_H=n_Ld_L=\lambda_0/4$ give reinforcing reflected waves near design [wavelength](wavelength.md) $\lambda_0$. Their periodic transfer matrix has a stop band when its Bloch [phase](phase-waves.md) is complex. A finite stack has nonzero transmission and its exterior impedances matter. Introducing a suitably phased central defect can support a transmission [resonance](resonance.md) within the stop band; a one-dimensional stop band is not a complete three-dimensional photonic gap.
