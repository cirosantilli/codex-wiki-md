# Capacitor current

↑ **Parent:** [Capacitor](capacitor.md)

With current entering the positive-voltage terminal, differentiating the [capacitor charge](capacitor-charge.md) relation at fixed [capacitance](capacitance.md) gives $I=C\,dV/dt$. A changing voltage drives current through the external circuit while a changing [electric field](electric-field-split.md) supplies [displacement current](displacement-current.md) across an ideal vacuum gap.
