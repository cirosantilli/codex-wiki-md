# Kelvin

↑ **Parent:** [Temperature](temperature.md)

The kelvin is the SI unit of [thermodynamic temperature](thermodynamic-temperature.md), with symbol $\mathrm K$. Its definition fixes the numerical value of the [Boltzmann constant](boltzmann-constant.md); it is written without a degree sign.[BIPM, SI unit kelvin](https://www.bipm.org/en/si-base-units/kelvin)
