# Units congruent to one modulo 3 plus square root two

↑ **Parent:** [Units of Q of square root two](units-of-q-of-square-root-two.md)

Reduction modulo $(3+\sqrt2)$ identifies $\mathbb Z[\sqrt2]/(3+\sqrt2)$ with $\mathbb F_7$, sends $\sqrt2$ to $4$, and sends $\varepsilon=1+\sqrt2$ to $5$. Since $5$ has order six and $5^3=-1$ in $\mathbb F_7^\times$, the kernel on units is the infinite cyclic group

$$
\left\langle-(1+\sqrt2)^3\right\rangle
=\langle-7-5\sqrt2\rangle.
$$
