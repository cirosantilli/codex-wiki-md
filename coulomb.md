# Coulomb

↑ **Parent:** [International System of Units](international-system-of-units.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coulomb)

The [coulomb](coulomb.md) is the [SI](international-system-of-units.md) unit of [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md). A constant [electric current](electric-current.md) of $1\,\mathrm A$ transports $1\,\mathrm C$ in a [time](time-in-physics.md) of $1\,\mathrm s$; a constant current of $2\,\mathrm A$ for $3\,\mathrm s$ transports $6\,\mathrm C$. The prefix in $1\,\mathrm{nC}=10^{-9}\,\mathrm C$ explains the much smaller charge scale used in the worked [Coulomb's law](coulomb-s-law.md) example. [The International System of Units (SI), ninth edition, SI Brochure](the-international-system-of-units-si-ninth-edition-si-brochure.md)
