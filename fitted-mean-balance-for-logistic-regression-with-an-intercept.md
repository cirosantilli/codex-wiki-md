# Fitted-mean balance for logistic regression with an intercept

↑ **Parent:** [Bernoulli logistic-regression model](bernoulli-logistic-regression-model.md)

At an interior maximum-likelihood fit of a logistic-regression model containing an intercept, the intercept [score function](informant-function.md) is

$$
\frac{\partial\ell}{\partial\beta_0}=\sum_i(y_i-\widehat p_i)=0.
$$

Consequently the sum of the fitted probabilities equals the number of observed successes exactly.
