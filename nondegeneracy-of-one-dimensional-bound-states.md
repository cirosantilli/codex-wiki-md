# Nondegeneracy of one-dimensional bound states

↑ **Parent:** [Schrödinger equation](schrodinger-equation.md)

Two bound-state solutions of the same one-dimensional time-independent [Schrödinger equation](schrodinger-equation.md) have constant [Wronskian](wronskian.md). Their decay at infinity makes this constant zero, so the two solutions are linearly dependent. Every bound-state energy is therefore [nondegenerate](nondegenerate-energy-eigenvalue.md).
