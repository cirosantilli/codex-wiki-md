# Scheduling response time

↑ **Parent:** [Process scheduling](process-scheduling.md)

The delay from a task's arrival $a_i$ until its first execution $f_i$: $R_i=f_i-a_i$. This measures access to initial [CPU](central-processing-unit.md) service, rather than completion of an entire user request. For C under [round-robin scheduling](round-robin-scheduling.md) in [process scheduling](process-scheduling.md), $R_C=4-2=2$; its [scheduling turnaround time](scheduling-turnaround-time.md) is instead $5-2=3$.
