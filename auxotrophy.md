# Auxotrophy

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular genetics](molecular-genetics-split.md)

[Auxotrophy](auxotrophy.md) is a [phenotype](phenotype.md) in which growth requires an externally supplied substance that a comparison [biological strain](strain-biology.md) can make for itself. Growth on supplemented [cell culture medium](cell-culture-medium.md) formulations can reveal the requirement without identifying the responsible [gene](gene.md).
