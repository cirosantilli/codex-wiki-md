# Beltrami identity for a second-derivative functional

↑ **Parent:** [Euler-Lagrange equation for a second-derivative functional](euler-lagrange-equation-for-a-second-derivative-functional.md)

For a smooth [functional](functional.md) $\int F(x,y,y',y'')\,dx$, let $P=F_{y'}$ and $Q=F_{y''}$. Along an [extremal](extremal.md), the [Euler-Lagrange equation for a second-derivative functional](euler-lagrange-equation-for-a-second-derivative-functional.md) is $F_y-P'+Q''=0$. Differentiating $H=y''Q+y'(P-Q')-F$ by the [chain rule](chain-rule.md) gives

$$
H'=y'(P'-Q''-F_y)-F_x=-F_x.
$$

Consequently $H$ is constant whenever $F$ has no explicit dependence on $x$. This extends the [Beltrami identity](beltrami-identity.md) to a second-derivative variational problem. If $F$ is independent of $y''$, the expression reduces to $y'F_{y'}-F$.
