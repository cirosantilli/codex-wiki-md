# Kinetic magnetohydrodynamics

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetohydrodynamics](magnetohydrodynamics-split.md)

A magnetized long-[wavelength](wavelength.md) [plasma](plasma-physics.md) description with a common leading perpendicular flow, [magnetic flux freezing](magnetic-flux-freezing.md) and a gyrotropic [pressure tensor](pressure-tensor.md). The [kinetic magnetohydrodynamic momentum equation](kinetic-magnetohydrodynamic-momentum-equation.md) couples to a [drift-kinetic equation](drift-kinetic-equation.md) for each species; [parallel plasma pressure](parallel-plasma-pressure.md) and [perpendicular plasma pressure](perpendicular-plasma-pressure.md) are calculated as [velocity](velocity.md) [moments](moment.md), rather than prescribed by an isotropic adiabatic closure. Relative species drifts enter at higher order in the ratio of [gyroradius](gyroradius.md) to macroscopic length.
