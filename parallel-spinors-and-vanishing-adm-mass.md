# Parallel spinors and vanishing ADM mass

↑ **Parent:** [Positive energy theorem](positive-energy-theorem.md)

A nonzero spacetime [parallel spinor](parallel-spinor.md) makes the [Nester two-form](nester-two-form.md) vanish, so its ADM boundary charge vanishes. For complete regular spin initial data in an [asymptotically flat spacetime](asymptotically-flat-spacetime.md) under the [dominant energy condition](dominant-energy-condition.md), the ADM momentum is future causal. Its contraction with the [spinor](spinor.md)'s nonzero future causal [Dirac current](dirac-current.md) cannot vanish if the momentum is timelike. Thus the invariant ADM [mass](mass.md) is zero; in a rest frame this gives zero energy. Local parallel-spinor existence without asymptotic/global hypotheses does not establish this [mass](mass.md) conclusion.
