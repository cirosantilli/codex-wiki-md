# Universal coefficient theorem for cohomology

↑ **Parent:** [Cohomology](cohomology-split.md)

This is the cohomological form of the [universal coefficient theorem](universal-coefficient-theorem.md), with Ext and Hom terms rather than the homological tensor and Tor terms.

For an abelian coefficient group $G$, cohomology fits into a split short exact sequence

$$
0\to\operatorname{Ext}(H_{n-1}(X;\mathbb Z),G)
\to H^n(X;G)
\to\operatorname{Hom}(H_n(X;\mathbb Z),G)\to0.
$$
