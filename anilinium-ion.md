# Anilinium ion

↑ **Parent:** [Ion](ion.md)

An [anilinium ion](anilinium-ion.md) is an aniline protonated at [nitrogen](nitrogen.md). [nitrogen](nitrogen.md) [protonation](protonation.md) removes its [lone pair](lone-pair.md) from donation to the [aromatic ring](aromatic-ring.md) and usually gives strong deactivation and meta direction in [electrophilic substitution](electrophilic-substitution.md).
