# Ricker population map

↑ **Parent:** [Mathematical biology](mathematical-biology-split.md)

The Ricker population map models density-dependent reproduction with a declining per-capita growth factor. Its nonzero equilibrium is $K$, with multiplier $1-r$. It loses stability through a [period-doubling bifurcation](period-doubling-bifurcation.md) at $r=2$. Every population after the first update is bounded by $K e^{r-1}/r$, the maximum of the update function on nonnegative populations.
