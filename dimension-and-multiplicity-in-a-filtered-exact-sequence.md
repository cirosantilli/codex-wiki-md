# Dimension and multiplicity in a filtered exact sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Multiplicity of a filtered module](multiplicity-of-a-filtered-module.md)

The [subspace filtration](subspace-filtration.md) and [quotient filtration](quotient-filtration.md) in a [short exact sequence](short-exact-sequence.md) are good when the associated graded algebra is [Noetherian](noetherian-ring.md). Their cumulative Hilbert functions add in every degree. Positive leading coefficients make the degree of the sum the larger degree; when both degrees agree, factorial times the leading coefficients add. Thus equal-dimensional nonzero factors consume positive [multiplicity of a filtered module](multiplicity-of-a-filtered-module.md). With a [standard graded algebra](standard-graded-algebra.md) normalization these multiplicities are integral.
