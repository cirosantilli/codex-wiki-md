# Zone plate

↑ **Parent:** [Fresnel diffraction](fresnel-diffraction.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zone_plate)

A concentric diffractive focusing element with successive zone boundaries chosen to differ by half a [wavelength](wavelength.md) of optical path to a focus. Alternating transmitting and absorbing zones suppress cancellation between neighboring half-period zones. For an incident [plane wave](plane-wave.md), the paraxial boundaries satisfy $r_j^2=j\lambda f$. The outermost zone width controls the finest transverse scale, and the [focal length](focal-length.md) varies with [wavelength](wavelength.md).
