# Vacuum permeability

↑ **Parent:** [Permeability (electromagnetism)](permeability-electromagnetism.md)

Vacuum permeability is the [magnetic permeability](permeability-electromagnetism.md) appearing in the vacuum [Maxwell equations](maxwell-equations.md). In [SI](international-system-of-units.md), it relates vacuum magnetic field strength to [magnetic flux density](magnetic-field.md) through $\mathbf B=\mu_0\mathbf H$ and obeys $\mu_0\varepsilon_0c^2=1$, where $c$ is the [speed of light](speed-of-light.md) and $\varepsilon_0$ the [vacuum permittivity](vacuum-permittivity.md).
