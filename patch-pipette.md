# Patch pipette

↑ **Parent:** [Patch clamp](patch-clamp.md)

A [patch pipette](patch-pipette.md) is the tapered glass, solution-filled probe used in [patch clamp](patch-clamp.md). Its [electrode](electrode.md) connects the solution to an amplifier, with another [electrode](electrode.md) referencing the bath. The open-tip [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) differs from the much larger [gigaohm seal](gigaohm-seal.md) [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md). After break-in, a narrow or partly obstructed tip contributes to [patch-clamp series resistance](patch-clamp-series-resistance.md).
