# Circular topographic anticyclone

↑ **Parent:** [Shallow-water potential vorticity](shallow-water-potential-vorticity.md)

For an upstream irrotational current on an [f-plane](f-plane.md) in a rigid layer of height $h$, a circular region of height $h(1-\epsilon)$ has the displayed relative [vorticity](vorticity.md) if its fluid retains upstream [potential vorticity](potential-vorticity.md) $f/h$. To leading order in the height contrast, take $u=-\Psi_y$, $v=\Psi_x$ and $\Psi=-Uy+\psi$. Solving the [Poisson equation](poisson-equation.md) for $\psi$ gives $\psi=-Cr^2/2$ inside and $\psi=Ca^2[\log(a/r)-1/2]$ outside, where $C=f\epsilon/2$. The disturbance is clockwise for positive $f\epsilon$. Variable-depth transport and the transition across the rim introduce higher-order corrections to this leading columnar inversion.
