# Radiopharmaceutical

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear medicine](nuclear-medicine.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radiopharmaceutical)

A pharmaceutical containing a [radionuclide](radionuclide.md), with uptake and clearance chosen for imaging or treatment. Biological targeting and [radioactive decay](radioactive-decay.md) jointly determine its useful signal and tissue dose.
