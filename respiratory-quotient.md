# Respiratory quotient

↑ **Parent:** [Respirometer gas balance](respirometer-gas-balance.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Respiratory_quotient)

The [respiratory quotient](respiratory-quotient.md) compares [carbon dioxide](carbon-dioxide.md) production with [molecular oxygen](molecular-oxygen.md) consumption over the same interval. Complete [glucose](glucose.md) [oxidation](oxidation.md) in the net equation above gives $\mathrm{RQ}=6/6=1$. In the ideal [respirometer gas balance](respirometer-gas-balance.md) with no absorber, $f=0$, that conversion gives $D=0$: substantial [cellular respiration](cellular-respiration.md) can occur without any net gas contraction. A zero contraction therefore does not by itself demonstrate zero [metabolism](metabolism.md).

Other fuels, incomplete conversion and concurrent synthesis can alter the ratio. Absorber-based [respirometry](respirometry.md) isolates [molecular oxygen](molecular-oxygen.md) uptake by deliberately removing the other gas signal; it cannot independently determine the [respiratory quotient](respiratory-quotient.md) or identify [glucose](glucose.md) as the consumed fuel. Separate [carbon dioxide](carbon-dioxide.md) measurement would supply the missing account.
