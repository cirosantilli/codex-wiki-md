# Entringer triangle

↑ **Parent:** [Entringer number](entringer-number.md)

The Entringer triangle arranges [Entringer numbers](entringer-number.md) into rows. Each row is obtained from reversed-row [prefix sums](prefix-sum.md), with the boundary convention specified by the recurrence.
