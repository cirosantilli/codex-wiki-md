<h1 id="buhlmann-straub-model">Bühlmann–Straub model</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Bühlmann model](buhlmann-model.md)

Conditional on a common [latent variable](latent-variable.md) $\Theta$, annual averages $X_j$ are independent, have common [conditional expectation](conditional-expectation.md) $\mu(\Theta)$, and have [conditional variance](conditional-variance.md) $\sigma^2(\Theta)/m_j$ for known positive exposures $m_j$. Its structural parameters are $m_0=\mathbb E\mu(\Theta)$, [expected process variance](expected-process-variance.md) $v=\mathbb E\sigma^2(\Theta)$ and [variance of hypothetical means](variance-of-hypothetical-means.md) $a=\operatorname{Var}(\mu(\Theta))$. It generalizes the equal-exposure [Bühlmann model](buhlmann-model.md).
