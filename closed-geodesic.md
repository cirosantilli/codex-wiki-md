# Closed geodesic

↑ **Parent:** [Geodesic](geodesic.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Closed_geodesic)

A nonconstant [geodesic](geodesic.md) whose position and tangent velocity repeat after a positive time. On a [flat torus](flat-torus.md) it is a straight line modulo the lattice, closing when its displacement is a [Euclidean lattice](euclidean-lattice.md) vector. A primitive closed geodesic traverses its image without being an iterate of a shorter periodic geodesic.
