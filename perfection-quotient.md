# Perfection quotient

↑ **Parent:** [Divisor function](divisor-function.md)

The ratio of the sum of the positive [divisors](divisor.md) of a [positive integer](positive-integer.md) to that [integer](integer-split.md). For [coprime](coprime-integers.md) $a,b$, the ratio is multiplicative because the [divisor function](divisor-function.md) $\sigma$ is multiplicative. The contribution of a [prime power](prime-power.md) $p^e$ is $(1+p+\cdots+p^e)/p^e$, so a prescribed quotient can be searched through its remaining [numerator](numerator.md) and [denominator](denominator.md).
