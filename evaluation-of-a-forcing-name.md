# Evaluation of a forcing name

↑ **Parent:** [Forcing name](forcing-name.md)

Interpret a [forcing name](forcing-name.md) recursively by $\operatorname{val}(\tau,G)=\{\operatorname{val}(\sigma,G):\exists p\in G\ ((\sigma,p)\in\tau)\}$. The collection of interpreted ground-model names is the [generic extension](generic-extension.md).
