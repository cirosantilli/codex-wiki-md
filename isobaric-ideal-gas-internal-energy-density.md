# Isobaric ideal-gas internal-energy density

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal gas](ideal-gas-split.md)

An [ideal gas](ideal-gas-split.md) with constant [heat capacity ratio](heat-capacity-ratio.md) $\gamma$ has $U=n c_{V,m}T$ up to a consistent thermal-energy reference, and $c_{V,m}=R/(\gamma-1)$. Combining this with $pV=nRT$ gives the displayed thermal internal-energy [density](density.md). At fixed [pressure](pressure-split.md) it does not grow with [temperature](temperature-split.md): the particle [density](density.md) falls as $1/T$. A fixed-volume space exchanging gas with the atmosphere therefore differs from a sealed fixed-particle system. Composition changes, activated internal modes or a [pressure](pressure-split.md) change require a modified comparison.
