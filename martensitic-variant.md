# Martensitic variant

↑ **Parent:** [Martensite](martensite.md)

A [martensitic variant](martensitic-variant.md) is one of the lattice deformations corresponding to the low-temperature [crystal](crystal.md) phase. The parent [crystal](crystal.md)'s discrete symmetries generate different variants. In a nonlinear [elasticity](elasticity-physics-split.md) model each representative $U_i$ supplies a [hyperelastic energy well](hyperelastic-energy-well.md) $SO(3)U_i$. A representative need not itself be a [symmetric](symmetric-relation.md) [stretch tensor](stretch-tensor.md); multiplication by a [proper rotation](proper-rotation.md) leaves the same well.
