# Magnetron motion

↑ **Parent:** [Penning trap normal modes](penning-trap-normal-modes.md)

The slow radial [normal mode](normal-mode.md) of a [Penning trap](penning-trap.md). Its [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) arises from the combined static [electric field](electric-field-split.md) and [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md); it is distinct from the driven [Paul trap micromotion](paul-trap-micromotion.md).
