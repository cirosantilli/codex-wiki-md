# Inhibitor in a reaction-diffusion system

↑ **Parent:** [Reaction–diffusion system](reaction-diffusion-system.md)

An inhibitor in an activator-inhibitor [reaction–diffusion system](reaction-diffusion-system.md) suppresses the [activator](activator-in-a-reaction-diffusion-system.md). Its diffusion and reaction feedback can stabilize a spatially uniform state while allowing some nonzero [wavenumbers](wavenumber.md) to grow through a [Turing instability](turing-instability.md).
