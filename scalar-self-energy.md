# Scalar self-energy

↑ **Parent:** [Self-energy](self-energy.md)

The two-point, one-particle-irreducible correction for a [scalar field](scalar-field.md). With an insertion denoted $-i\Sigma(p^2)$, the full [scalar propagator](scalar-propagator.md) is $i/[p^2-m^2-\Sigma(p^2)+i0]$. A divergent coefficient of $p^2$ requires [wave-function renormalization](wave-function-renormalization.md); a momentum-independent divergent term requires a [mass counterterm](mass-counterterm.md).
