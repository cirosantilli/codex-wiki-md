# Hebel-Slichter coherence peak

↑ **Parent:** [BCS coherence factors](bcs-coherence-factors.md)

In a conventional isotropic spin-singlet [superconductor](superconductor.md), the nuclear spin-lattice relaxation rate can rise just below the transition before falling at lower [temperature](temperature-split.md). The [BCS coherence factors](bcs-coherence-factors.md) and enhanced near-gap [density of states](density-of-states.md) produce the coherence enhancement. Nuclear relaxation and ultrasonic attenuation have different coherence factors, so their contrasting [temperature](temperature-split.md) dependences test pairing structure rather than only the existence of a gap.[Abstract, physical system and comparison with [BCS theory](bcs-theory.md)](nuclear-spin-relaxation-in-normal-and-superconducting-aluminum-by-l-c-hebel-and-charles-pence-slichter.md)
