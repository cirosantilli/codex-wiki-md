# Unit quaternion

↑ **Parent:** [Quaternion](quaternion.md)

A [quaternion](quaternion.md) of norm $1$ is a unit quaternion. They form the [topological group](topological-group-split.md) $\operatorname{Sp}(1)$ and identify with the sphere $S^3\subset\mathbb R^4$. Multiplication and inversion preserve the norm. The [Hurewicz theorem](hurewicz-theorem.md) identifies $\pi_3(S^3)$ with $H_3(S^3;\mathbb Z)$; hence [homotopy-group addition in a topological group](homotopy-group-addition-in-a-topological-group.md) shows that $q\mapsto q^m$ has [mapping degree](degree-of-a-continuous-mapping.md) $m$, rather than $m^2$.
