# Ornstein-Uhlenbeck stochastic differential equation

↑ **Parent:** [Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process](ornstein-uhlenbeck-process.md)

The linear [stochastic differential equation](stochastic-differential-equation.md) has the explicit solution $Y_t=e^{-\lambda t}Y_0+\sigma\int_0^te^{-\lambda(t-s)}dW_s$. Its integrating factor is $e^{\lambda t}$. For $\lambda>0$, an independent initial normal variable of variance $\sigma^2/(2\lambda)$ makes its zero-mean [Gaussian process](gaussian-process.md) stationary.
