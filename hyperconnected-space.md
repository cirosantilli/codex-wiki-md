# Hyperconnected space

↑ **Parent:** [Topology](topology-split.md)

A [topological space](topological-space.md) is hyperconnected if any two nonempty [open sets](open-set.md) intersect. Every [continuous map](continuous-map.md) from a nonempty hyperconnected space to a [Hausdorff space](hausdorff-space.md) is constant: two distinct image points would have disjoint open neighbourhoods whose nonempty inverse images contradict hyperconnectedness. Every infinite set with the [cofinite topology](cofinite-topology.md) is hyperconnected.
