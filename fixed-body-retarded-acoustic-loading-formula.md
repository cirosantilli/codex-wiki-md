# Fixed-body retarded acoustic loading formula

↑ **Parent:** [Ffowcs Williams-Hawkings equation](ffowcs-williams-hawkings-equation.md)

For a fixed impermeable object, the [Ffowcs Williams-Hawkings equation](ffowcs-williams-hawkings-equation.md) has a volume [acoustic quadrupole](acoustic-quadrupole.md) and a surface [acoustic dipole](acoustic-dipole.md), with no mass-injection term. If $F$ is the total force exerted on the fluid and $Q=\int T\,dV$ the integrated [Lighthill stress tensor](lighthill-stress-tensor.md), the [acoustic compact-source approximation](acoustic-compact-source-approximation.md) in the [acoustic far field](acoustic-far-field.md) gives the displayed pressure at retarded time. A force convention directed onto the body reverses the loading sign. The [retarded acoustic Green function](retarded-acoustic-green-function.md) carries $1/c_0^2$ when the wave operator is $\partial_t^2-c_0^2\Delta$.
