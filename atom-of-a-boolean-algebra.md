# Atom of a Boolean algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Boolean algebra](boolean-algebra.md)

An [atom](atom-of-a-boolean-algebra.md) is a minimal nonzero Boolean-algebra element. In a [Lindenbaum algebra](lindenbaum-algebra.md), its nonempty formula class isolates a [complete type](complete-type.md). A finite [Boolean algebra](boolean-algebra.md) is the [power set](power-set.md) of its finite [atom](atom-of-a-boolean-algebra.md) [set](set-split.md). An infinite atomic [Boolean algebra](boolean-algebra.md) need not contain every [union](set-union.md) of [atoms](atom-of-a-boolean-algebra.md): the [finite-cofinite algebra](finite-cofinite-boolean-algebra.md) is a simple counterexample.
