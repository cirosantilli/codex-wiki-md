# Flat matter-coasting-fluid Friedmann solution

↑ **Parent:** [Friedmann equations](friedmann-equations.md)

A flat universe with separately conserved [pressureless matter](pressureless-matter.md) and a [coasting fluid](coasting-fluid.md) satisfies $a'^2=H_0^2[\Omega_m a+(1-\Omega_m)a^2]$. For both densities positive, choosing the [Big Bang](big-bang.md) at zero [conformal time](conformal-time.md) gives $a=\Omega_m[\cosh(\alpha\tau)-1]/[2(1-\Omega_m)]$, with $\alpha=H_0\sqrt{1-\Omega_m}$. Physical time follows by integrating $dt=a\,d\tau$. The hyperbolic parametrization is formally like the open dust solution, but the spatial geometry here is flat.
