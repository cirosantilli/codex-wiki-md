# Closed unbounded class of ordinals

↑ **Parent:** [Club set](club-set.md)

An unbounded [class in set theory](class-set-theory.md) of [ordinals](ordinal.md) is closed if every nonempty set-sized increasing sequence of its members has its supremum in the class. This is the proper-class analogue of a [club set](club-set.md). The [reflection theorem for definable hierarchies](reflection-theorem-for-definable-hierarchies.md) gives such a class for every finite collection of [first-order formulas](first-order-formula.md).
