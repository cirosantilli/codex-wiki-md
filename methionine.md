# Methionine

↑ **Parent:** [Amino acid](amino-acid.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Methionine)

Methionine is an [amino acid](amino-acid.md) represented by Met or M in [protein](protein-split.md) sequence notation. In the standard [genetic code](genetic-code.md), AUG encodes it, as in the worked [gene expression](gene-expression.md) example.
