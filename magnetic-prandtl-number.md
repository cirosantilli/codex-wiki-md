# Magnetic Prandtl number

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic diffusivity](magnetic-diffusivity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnetic_Prandtl_number)

The magnetic Prandtl number compares [kinematic viscosity](kinematic-viscosity.md) with [magnetic diffusivity](magnetic-diffusivity.md). It is the ratio of the viscous and magnetic diffusion coefficients; equivalently it is the [magnetic Reynolds number](magnetic-reynolds-number.md) divided by the [Reynolds number](reynolds-number.md) when the same speed and length scale are used.
