# Infinite cardinal powers under GCH

↑ **Parent:** [Cardinal exponentiation](cardinal-exponentiation.md)

Assume the [Generalized continuum hypothesis](generalized-continuum-hypothesis.md). For necessity, [König theorem for cardinal numbers](konig-s-theorem-set-theory.md) gives $\rho^{\operatorname{cf}(\rho)}>\rho$ for every infinite [cardinal](cardinal-number-split.md). But $\lambda=\kappa^\mu$ implies $\lambda^\mu=\lambda$ by the [currying law for cardinal exponentiation](currying-law-for-cardinal-exponentiation.md), so $\operatorname{cf}(\lambda)>\mu$. This direction uses only [ZFC](zermelo-fraenkel-set-theory-with-choice.md). Conversely, uncountable [cofinality](cofinality.md) bounds the range of every [countable](countable-set.md) sequence in $\lambda$. Under [GCH](generalized-continuum-hypothesis.md), each smaller infinite [cardinal](cardinal-number-split.md) $\nu$ satisfies $\nu^{\aleph_0}\leq2^\nu=\nu^+\leq\lambda$. There are only $\lambda$ [ordinal](ordinal-split.md) bounds to consider, giving $\lambda^{\aleph_0}=\lambda$ and the representation with base $\lambda$ and exponent $\aleph_0$.
