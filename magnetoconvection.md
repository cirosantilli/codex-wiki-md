# Magnetoconvection

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetohydrodynamics](magnetohydrodynamics.md)

Magnetoconvection is thermal [convection](convection.md) coupled to the evolution and forces of a [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md). Conserved [magnetic flux](magnetic-flux.md) can supply a slow field that must be retained along with the convective [complex amplitude](complex-amplitude.md), rather than being eliminated into a single local [amplitude equation](amplitude-equation.md).
