# String density ratio for prescribed reflected power

↑ **Parent:** [Characteristic impedance of a stretched string](characteristic-impedance-of-a-stretched-string.md)

For two ideal strings under a common [tension](tension.md), the positive [mechanical impedances](mechanical-impedance.md) satisfy $Z_j=\sqrt{T\mu_j}$, where $\mu_j$ are their [linear mass densities](linear-mass-density.md). The reflected fraction of incident [power](power.md) is $\mathcal R=[(Z_1-Z_2)/(Z_1+Z_2)]^2$. Solving for the positive ratio $z=Z_2/Z_1$ gives $z=(1+\sqrt{\mathcal R})/(1-\sqrt{\mathcal R})$ or its reciprocal. Squaring yields the displayed [linear mass density](linear-mass-density.md) ratios. For $\mathcal R=1/2$, the heavier string has $17+12\sqrt2\simeq33.97$ times the lighter string's [linear mass density](linear-mass-density.md); the impedance ratio is only $3+2\sqrt2$.
