# Bioenergy

↑ **Parent:** [Renewable energy](renewable-energy.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bioenergy)

Energy recovered from biologically produced material, for example by combustion or conversion into fuels. Sustainable regrowth can recycle biogenic carbon, but land-use change, cultivation, processing, transport and regrowth time belong in its [life-cycle assessment](life-cycle-assessment.md). Harvested energy is incident solar energy times area, elapsed time and annual conversion efficiency; dry and wet mass yields require different heating-value conventions.
