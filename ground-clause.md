# Ground clause

↑ **Parent:** [First-order clause](first-order-clause.md)

A [first-order clause](first-order-clause.md) containing no variables. Its [first-order terms](first-order-term.md) are [ground terms](ground-term.md), so [resolution](resolution.md) between such [first-order clauses](first-order-clause.md) reduces to [propositional logic](propositional-logic.md) on the ground atoms.
