# Paul Dirac

↑ **Parent:** [List of physicists](list-of-physicists.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Dirac)

A [physicist](physicist-split.md) who developed a relativistic theory of the [electron](electron.md). He shared the [Nobel Prize in Physics in 1933](nobel-prize-in-physics-in-1933.md) with [Erwin Schrödinger](erwin-schrodinger.md).[Facts: life dates, joint prize and the 1928 relativistic electron theory](paul-a-m-dirac-facts-by-nobel-prize-outreach.md)
