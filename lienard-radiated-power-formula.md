<h1 id="lienard-radiated-power-formula">Liénard radiated-power formula</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Larmor formula](larmor-formula.md)

The relativistic generalization of the [Larmor formula](larmor-formula.md) uses the invariant proper-acceleration magnitude: $P=-\mu_0q^2 A^aA_a/(6\pi)$ in signature $(+---)$. In coordinate time this is the displayed expression. For perpendicular magnetic circular motion, $\gamma m|a|=|q|vB$, giving $P=\mu_0q^4(\gamma^2-1)B^2/(6\pi m^2)$. The statement concerns total instantaneous emitted power; directional radiation flux is not itself invariant.
