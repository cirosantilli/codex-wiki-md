# Particle horizon in a power-law universe

↑ **Parent:** [Particle horizon](particle-horizon.md)

The radial null integral from the [Big Bang](big-bang.md) is $\chi_p=c\int_0^tdt'/R(t')=ct^{1-p}/[A(1-p)]$. Multiplication by the [scale factor](scale-factor-cosmology.md) gives the proper radial horizon distance. It is $3ct$ for a flat matter-only model and $2ct$ for a flat radiation-only model. A finite initial [time](time-in-physics.md) different from zero or an earlier expansion era changes the result.
