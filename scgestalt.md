# scGESTALT

↑ **Parent:** [CRISPR lineage recording](crispr-lineage-recording.md)

A lineage-recording approach coupling inherited CRISPR barcode edits with single-[cell](biological-cell-split.md) transcriptomes. It can relate [cell](biological-cell-split.md) identity to developmental ancestry while retaining the limitations of incomplete barcode recovery and edit [homoplasy](homoplasy.md).[2018, Figures 1 and 3–6, recording pulses and lineage/cell-type recovery](simultaneous-single-cell-profiling-of-lineages-and-cell-types-in-the-vertebrate-brain-by-bushra-raj-and-others.md)
