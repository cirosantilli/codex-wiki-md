# Archean

↑ **Parent:** [Geological time scale](geological-time-scale.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archean)

The geological eon from about 4.0 to 2.5 billion years ago. [Continental crust](continental-crust.md) composition and tectonic processes varied during this interval.
