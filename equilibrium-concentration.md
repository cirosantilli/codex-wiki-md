# Equilibrium concentration

↑ **Parent:** [Concentration](concentration.md)

An [equilibrium concentration](equilibrium-concentration.md) is a constant [concentration](concentration.md) satisfying the [fixed point](fixed-point.md) equations of a specified dynamical model. Balance between production and loss can maintain such a value without implying thermodynamic [chemical equilibrium](chemical-equilibrium.md).
