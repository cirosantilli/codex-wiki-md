# Hidden supersymmetry-breaking sector

↑ **Parent:** [Supersymmetry breaking](supersymmetry-breaking.md)

A hidden sector contains fields whose [auxiliary fields](auxiliary-field.md) acquire expectation values that break [supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md). Loop effects or suppressed interactions communicate the breaking to visible fields as effective [soft supersymmetry breaking](soft-supersymmetry-breaking.md). This can avoid the canonical visible-sector [tree-level supertrace mass sum rule](tree-level-supertrace-mass-sum-rule.md).
