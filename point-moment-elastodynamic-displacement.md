# Point-moment elastodynamic displacement

↑ **Parent:** [Elastodynamic Green tensor](elastodynamic-green-tensor.md)

Integrating the equivalent dipole [body force](body-force.md) against the [elastodynamic Green tensor](elastodynamic-green-tensor.md) by parts gives the displayed time convolution. For a fixed [seismic moment tensor](seismic-moment-tensor.md) shape $M_{kj}=K_{kj}V(t)$, its $R^{-1}$ terms are $[\mathbf e(\mathbf e\cdot K\mathbf e)\dot V(t-R/\alpha)/\alpha^3+(K\mathbf e-\mathbf e(\mathbf e\cdot K\mathbf e))\dot V(t-R/\beta)/\beta^3]/(4\pi\rho R)$. Derivatives of the angular projectors and the retarded near-field integral additionally produce $R^{-2}$ and $R^{-4}$ coefficients, yielding the final static field after the source stops.
