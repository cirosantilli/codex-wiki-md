# Slab suction

↑ **Parent:** [Subduction](subduction.md)

The effect of [slab](slab.md)-induced [mantle flow](mantle-flow.md), [pressure](pressure-split.md) and [traction](traction.md) on the motion of neighbouring plates and their boundary. It is part of the coupled response to a sinking [slab](slab.md), rather than an unrelated energy source that can always be added independently to [slab pull](slab-pull.md).
