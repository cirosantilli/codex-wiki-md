# Stability matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Linear stability](linear-stability.md)

For $\dot x=f(x)$ near an [equilibrium point](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md), the stability matrix is the [Jacobian matrix](jacobian-matrix.md) of the vector field evaluated at that point. Perturbations obey $\dot\eta=J\eta$ to first order. Its [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) determine linear growth and decay: strictly negative real parts imply local [asymptotic stability](asymptotic-stability.md), while a positive real part gives instability. Zero real parts require further analysis. The [stability matrix of a renormalization-group fixed point](stability-matrix-of-a-renormalization-group-fixed-point.md) applies this construction to beta-function flows.
