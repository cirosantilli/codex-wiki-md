# Period lattice of the Jacobi elliptic sine

↑ **Parent:** [Jacobi elliptic sine](jacobi-elliptic-sine.md)

For real $0<k<1$, the [period lattice](period-lattice.md) of $\operatorname{sn}(u,k)$ is generated by

$$
4K(k)
\quad\hbox{and}\quad
2iK'(k).
$$

The periods arise by composing the [monodromy reflections](monodromy-reflection-at-a-square-root-branch-point.md) of its inverse [elliptic integral of the first kind](elliptic-integral-of-the-first-kind.md).
