# Two-dimensional stationary-phase formula

↑ **Parent:** [Stationary phase method](stationary-phase-method.md)

For a smooth real phase with an isolated nondegenerate interior critical point $q$, smooth amplitude $a$, and no competing boundary contribution, the leading contribution is $2\pi\lambda^{-1}a(q)|\det H|^{-1/2}e^{i\lambda\phi(q)+i\pi\operatorname{sgn}(H)/4}$, where $H$ is the [Hessian matrix](hessian-matrix.md). Orthogonal diagonalization gives two one-dimensional [Fresnel integrals](fresnel-integral.md). A degenerate critical point or a stationary boundary needs its own local analysis.
