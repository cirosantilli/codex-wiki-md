# 2021

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the introduction to schemes course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Solutions to all three questions of the [Introduction to schemes course of the University of Oxford](../introduction-to-schemes-course-of-the-university-of-oxford.md), Part C, sat on 1 June 2021. Source: [Oxford's released examination PDF, compilation pages 103–106; paper pages 1–4](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/oxford_physics_past_papers_2/response/2340696/attach/9/Physics%20Papers%202020.pdf?cookie_passthrough=1#page=103). The original question and letter labels are retained; the paper has no Roman subparts. The correction to the printed closed-subset description in 1(b), and the meaning of the comparison of [structure sheaves](../structure-sheaf-of-a-scheme.md) in 1(c), are explained where they arise.
