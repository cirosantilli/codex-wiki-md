<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [localization of a ring](../../../../localization-of-a-ring.md) $R_r$ is generated over $R$ by the single element $1/r$, since every fraction can be written as

$$
\frac{a}{r^n}=\phi(a)(1/r)^n.
$$

The [presentation of a localization at one element](../../../../presentation-of-a-localization-at-one-element.md) is

$$
\boxed{R_r\cong R[t]/(rt-1),\qquad t\longmapsto1/r.}
$$

To verify the presentation even when $R$ has [zero divisors](../../../../zero-divisor.md), observe that $r$ is invertible in $R[t]/(rt-1)$ with inverse $t$. Hence the map from $R$ to this [quotient ring](../../../../quotient-ring.md) extends uniquely to $R_r$. This extension and the displayed map are inverse [ring homomorphisms](../../../../ring-homomorphism.md): they agree on $R$ and send the designated inverse of $r$ to the designated inverse of $r$.

Thus $R_r$ is a [finitely generated algebra over a ring](../../../../finitely-generated-algebra-over-a-ring.md), and the criterion for a [morphism of finite type](../../../../morphism-of-finite-type.md) between [affine schemes](../../../../affine-scheme.md) applies to $u:\operatorname{Spec}R_r\to\operatorname{Spec}R$. There is just one [affine scheme](../../../../affine-scheme.md) in the required cover of the source, with one algebra generator. Therefore **$u$ is of finite type**. If $r$ is [nilpotent](../../../../nilpotent.md), the same presentation is the [zero ring](../../../../zero-ring.md), and the source is the empty [scheme](../../../../scheme.md); the conclusion still holds.
