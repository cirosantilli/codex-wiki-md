# 2023

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the introduction to schemes course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Solutions to all three questions of the [Introduction to schemes course of the University of Oxford](../introduction-to-schemes-course-of-the-university-of-oxford.md), Part C, sat on 8 June 2023. Source: [Oxford's released examination PDF, compilation pages 346–348; paper pages 1–3](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/request_for_mathematics_examinat/response/3129786/attach/3/FOI%20202507%20740%20Data.pdf#page=346). The original numeric, letter and Roman labels are retained. The compactness hypotheses in the [valuative criterion](../valuative-criterion.md) and the interpretation of the removed section in 3(e) are made explicit below.
