<h1 id="1/c/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Starting from $(r,e)$, the construction in (iii) preserves the composite to the [affine line](../../../../../affine-line.md) and sends $Z_A$ to the $A$ zero section and $Z_B$ to the $B$ zero section. Part (ii) therefore recovers $(r,e)$.

Conversely, start with $u:T\to X$ and construct $(r,e)$. The only points of $X$ outside $A$ lie on $B_0$, and the only points outside $B$ lie on $A_0$. Consequently $u^{-1}(A)=T\setminus Z_B=T_A$ and $u^{-1}(B)=T\setminus Z_A=T_B$. On each of these [open subsets](../../../../../open-set.md), $u$ is the unique [morphism of schemes](../../../../../morphism-of-schemes.md) to the appropriate [affine line](../../../../../affine-line.md) with coordinate $r$. Thus part (iii) recovers $u$.

Finally, a [ring homomorphism](../../../../../ring-homomorphism.md) $R\to R'$ sends $r$ to $r'$ and induces $R/(r)\to R'/(r')$, sending $e$ to an [idempotent](../../../../../idempotent.md) $e'$. Inverse images of the [clopen sets](../../../../../clopen-set.md) $Z_A,Z_B$ are precisely those determined by $e'$. The constructions therefore commute with this [base change](../../../../../base-change-of-a-morphism-of-schemes.md). We have a [natural bijection](../../../../../natural-bijection.md)

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathsf{Sch}}(\operatorname{Spec}R,X)\cong F(R),}
$$

so $F$ is a [functor represented by a scheme](../../../../../functor-represented-by-a-scheme.md), represented by the [affine line with doubled origin](../../../../../affine-line-with-doubled-origin.md). The representing [scheme](../../../../../scheme.md) is not required to be an [affine scheme](../../../../../affine-scheme.md).
