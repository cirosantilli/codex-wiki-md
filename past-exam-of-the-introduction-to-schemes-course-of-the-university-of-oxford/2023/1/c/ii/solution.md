<h1 id="1/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Label the two charts of the [affine line with doubled origin](../../../../../affine-line-with-doubled-origin.md) by $A,B$, and use $t$ for their common coordinate. Let $u:T=\operatorname{Spec}R\to X$ be an $R$-valued element of the [functor of points](../../../../../functor-represented-by-a-scheme.md). Its composite with $\phi$ corresponds to a unique $r\in R$ under the [affine-target adjunction for schemes](../../../../../affine-target-adjunction-for-schemes.md):

$$
\phi u:T\longrightarrow\mathbb A^1_{\mathbb Z},\qquad t\longmapsto r.
$$

The [closed subscheme](../../../../../closed-subscheme.md) $Z=V(r)=\operatorname{Spec}(R/(r))$ maps into the disjoint union of the two zero sections,

$$
X\times_{\mathbb A^1_{\mathbb Z}}\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Z
=A_0\sqcup B_0,\qquad A_0\cong B_0\cong\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Z.
$$

The inverse image $Z_A$ of $A_0$ is a [clopen set](../../../../../clopen-set.md) in $Z$. By the [idempotent–clopen correspondence](../../../../../idempotent-clopen-correspondence.md), it determines a unique [idempotent](../../../../../idempotent.md) $e\in R/(r)$, equal to $1$ on $Z_A$ and $0$ on the inverse image $Z_B$ of $B_0$. Hence

$$
\boxed{u\longmapsto(r,e)\in F(R),\qquad D_Z(e)=Z_A.}
$$

Over $\mathbb Z$, the two “origins” are entire zero sections, rather than single closed points; this construction uses their full [scheme-theoretic fibers](../../../../../scheme-theoretic-fiber.md).
