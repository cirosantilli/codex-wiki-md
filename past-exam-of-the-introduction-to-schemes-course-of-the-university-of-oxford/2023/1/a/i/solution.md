<h1 id="1/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Write $h_T(S)=\operatorname{Hom}(S,T)$ for the [functor of points](../../../../../functor-represented-by-a-scheme.md) of a [scheme](../../../../../scheme.md) $T$. A [subfunctor](../../../../../subfunctor.md) $H\subseteq F$ is an [open subfunctor](../../../../../open-subfunctor.md) if every [natural transformation](../../../../../natural-transformation.md) $h_T\to F$ has a [pullback](../../../../../pullback-category-theory.md) represented by an [open subscheme](../../../../../open-subscheme.md) $U\subseteq T$:

$$
\boxed{h_T\times_F H\cong h_U,\qquad U\hookrightarrow T\text{ an open immersion}.}
$$

The displayed [natural isomorphism](../../../../../natural-isomorphism.md) must identify the projection to $h_T$ with the [natural transformation](../../../../../natural-transformation.md) induced by $U\hookrightarrow T$. Equivalently, for each $\xi\in F(T)$, there is an [open subscheme](../../../../../open-subscheme.md) $U\subseteq T$ such that a [morphism of schemes](../../../../../morphism-of-schemes.md) $a:S\to T$ factors through $U$ exactly when $F(a)(\xi)\in H(S)$. The [pullback](../../../../../pullback-category-theory.md) is taken in the [functor category](../../../../../functor-category.md), so this tests arbitrary families, rather than just underlying points.
