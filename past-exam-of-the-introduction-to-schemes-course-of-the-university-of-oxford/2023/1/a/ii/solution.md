A [Zariski cover](../../../../../zariski-cover.md) of a [scheme](../../../../../scheme.md) $T$ is a family of [open immersions](../../../../../open-immersion.md) $(j_i:U_i\hookrightarrow T)_{i\in I}$ whose images cover its underlying [topological space](../../../../../topological-space.md):

$$
\boxed{T=\bigcup_{i\in I}j_i(U_i).}
$$

There is no finiteness requirement. Intersections used in the [sheaf on a site](../../../../../sheaf-on-a-site.md) condition are the [pullbacks](../../../../../pullback-category-theory.md) $U_i\times_TU_j$, identified with the corresponding [open subschemes](../../../../../open-subscheme.md) $U_i\cap U_j$.
