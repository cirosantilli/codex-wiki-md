<h1 id="1/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The [open affine representability criterion](../../../../../open-affine-representability-criterion.md) says that $F$ is a [functor represented by a scheme](../../../../../functor-represented-by-a-scheme.md) exactly when $F$ is a [Zariski sheaf](../../../../../zariski-sheaf.md) and admits a family of [open subfunctors](../../../../../open-subfunctor.md) $F_i\subseteq F$, each represented by an [affine scheme](../../../../../affine-scheme.md), which covers $F$ in the following sense: for every [scheme](../../../../../scheme.md) $T$ and every $\xi\in F(T)$, the [open subschemes](../../../../../open-subscheme.md) representing $h_T\times_FF_i$ cover $T$. In particular, the covering condition does not demand that every $\xi$ lie in one $F_i(T)$.

In symbols, for [affine schemes](../../../../../affine-scheme.md) $X_i$,

$$
\boxed{F_i\cong h_{X_i},\quad F_i\subseteq F\text{ open},\quad \bigcup_i(T\times_FF_i)=T,\quad F\text{ a Zariski sheaf}.}
$$

The family is indexed by a set. For necessity, take an [affine open cover](../../../../../affine-open-cover.md) of a representing [scheme](../../../../../scheme.md); [morphisms of schemes](../../../../../morphism-of-schemes.md) glue over a [Zariski cover](../../../../../zariski-cover.md). For sufficiency, $F_i\times_FF_j$ is represented by an [open subscheme](../../../../../open-subscheme.md) of each $X_i,X_j$. The two descriptions identify these overlaps and satisfy the compatibility on triple overlaps because all maps come from the inclusions into $F$. Apply [gluing of schemes along open subschemes](../../../../../gluing-of-schemes-along-open-subschemes.md) to obtain $X$. Local identification gives $h_X\to F$, and the [Zariski sheaf](../../../../../zariski-sheaf.md) condition makes this a [natural isomorphism](../../../../../natural-isomorphism.md): its elements can be constructed and compared on the pulled-back [open cover](../../../../../open-cover.md).
