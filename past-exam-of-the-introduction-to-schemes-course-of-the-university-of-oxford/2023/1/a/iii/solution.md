<h1 id="1/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

A [presheaf](../../../../../presheaf-of-sets-on-a-topological-space.md) $F:\mathsf{Sch}^{\mathrm{op}}\to\mathsf{Set}$ is a [Zariski sheaf](../../../../../zariski-sheaf.md) when every [Zariski cover](../../../../../zariski-cover.md) $(U_i\to T)$ gives an [equalizer](../../../../../equaliser.md) diagram

$$
\boxed{F(T)\longrightarrow\prod_iF(U_i)\rightrightarrows\prod_{i,j}F(U_i\times_TU_j).}
$$

Thus compatible local elements glue to a unique element of $F(T)$. Compatibility means that the two restrictions agree on every intersection. This includes the empty [Zariski cover](../../../../../zariski-cover.md) of the empty [scheme](../../../../../scheme.md), so $F(\varnothing)$ is a singleton.
