<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let $x\in\operatorname{Frac}(D)$ satisfy a [monic polynomial](../../../../monic-polynomial.md) equation

$$
x^n+a_{n-1}x^{n-1}+\cdots+a_0=0,\qquad a_i\in D.
$$

If $x=0$, there is nothing to prove. Suppose $x\notin D$. The defining property of a [valuation ring](../../../../valuation-ring.md) gives $y=x^{-1}\in D$. Multiplying the equation by $y^{n-1}$ yields

$$
x=-a_{n-1}-a_{n-2}y-\cdots-a_0y^{n-1}\in D,
$$

a contradiction. Therefore

$$
\boxed{D\text{ is an integrally closed domain}.}
$$

This proof uses only the comparison of an element with its inverse; no discreteness or finite generation of the [valuation](../../../../valuation.md) is needed.
