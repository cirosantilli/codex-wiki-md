<h1 id="2/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For every [valuation ring](../../../../../valuation-ring.md) $D$ with [fraction field](../../../../../field-of-fractions.md) $K$, consider compatible [morphisms of schemes](../../../../../morphism-of-schemes.md)

$$
u:\operatorname{Spec}K\to X,\qquad v:\operatorname{Spec}D\to S,\qquad fu=v|_{\operatorname{Spec}K}.
$$

An extension is a [morphism of schemes](../../../../../morphism-of-schemes.md) $\ell:\operatorname{Spec}D\to X$ satisfying $\ell|_{\operatorname{Spec}K}=u$ and $f\ell=v$. The [valuative criterion for universal closedness](../../../../../valuative-criterion-for-universal-closedness.md) states

$$
\boxed{f\text{ universally closed}\iff f\text{ quasi-compact and every such pair admits an extension}.}
$$

Equivalently, among [quasi-compact morphisms](../../../../../quasi-compact-morphism.md), the existence condition characterizes [universal closedness](../../../../../universally-closed-morphism.md). A [universally closed morphism](../../../../../universally-closed-morphism.md) is automatically a [quasi-compact morphism](../../../../../quasi-compact-morphism.md); the converse direction of the criterion needs this compactness condition. All [valuation rings](../../../../../valuation-ring.md) are allowed, with arbitrary [fraction fields](../../../../../field-of-fractions.md); there is no [Noetherian ring](../../../../../noetherian-ring.md) hypothesis and one cannot replace them by [discrete valuation rings](../../../../../discrete-valuation-ring.md) without additional assumptions.[Stacks Project, valuative criterion of universal closedness](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/01KF)[Stacks Project, universally closed morphisms are quasi-compact](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/04XU)
