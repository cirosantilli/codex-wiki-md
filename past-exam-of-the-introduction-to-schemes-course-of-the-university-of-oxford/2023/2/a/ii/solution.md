↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Use the compatible pair $u,v$ and the notion of extension from (i). The [valuative criterion for separatedness](../../../../../valuative-criterion-for-separatedness.md) is

$$
\boxed{f\text{ separated}\iff f\text{ quasi-separated and every such pair has at most one extension}.}
$$

A [quasi-separated morphism](../../../../../quasi-separated-morphism.md) means one whose [diagonal morphism](../../../../../diagonal-morphism.md) is a [quasi-compact morphism](../../../../../quasi-compact-morphism.md). A [separated morphism](../../../../../separated-morphism.md) automatically has this property because its [diagonal morphism](../../../../../diagonal-morphism.md) is a [closed immersion](../../../../../closed-immersion.md). The [quasi-separated morphism](../../../../../quasi-separated-morphism.md) hypothesis is needed in the converse valuative test for arbitrary [schemes](../../../../../scheme.md). Existence is not demanded.[Stacks Project, valuative criterion of separatedness](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/01KY)
