<h1 id="2/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

With the same compatible pair $u,v$, the [valuative criterion for properness](../../../../../valuative-criterion-for-properness.md) is

$$
\boxed{f\text{ proper}\iff f\text{ is of finite type and quasi-separated, and every pair has exactly one extension}.}
$$

Equivalently, one can replace [finite type](../../../../../morphism-of-finite-type.md) by being a [quasi-compact morphism](../../../../../quasi-compact-morphism.md) and [locally of finite type](../../../../../locally-of-finite-type.md). Necessity follows from the definition of a [proper morphism](../../../../../proper-morphism.md) and the existence and uniqueness criteria in (i) and (ii). Conversely, [finite type](../../../../../morphism-of-finite-type.md) supplies the compactness needed in (i), while being a [quasi-separated morphism](../../../../../quasi-separated-morphism.md) supplies the compactness of the [diagonal morphism](../../../../../diagonal-morphism.md) needed in (ii). Thus the extension condition gives both [universal closedness](../../../../../universally-closed-morphism.md) and [separatedness](../../../../../separated-morphism.md), and these together with [finite type](../../../../../morphism-of-finite-type.md) give a [proper morphism](../../../../../proper-morphism.md). These hypotheses are part of the criteria, even though the question is phrased for unrestricted [morphisms of schemes](../../../../../morphism-of-schemes.md).[Stacks Project, valuative criterion for properness](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0BX5)
