<h1 id="2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Let $S'\to S$ be a [morphism of schemes](../../../../../morphism-of-schemes.md) and set $X'=X\times_SS'$, with projection $f':X'\to S'$. Given compatible [morphisms of schemes](../../../../../morphism-of-schemes.md) $u':\operatorname{Spec}K\to X'$ and $v':\operatorname{Spec}D\to S'$, compose them with the projections to obtain a valuative problem for $f$. An extension $\ell:\operatorname{Spec}D\to X$ for that problem and the prescribed $v'$ determine a unique [morphism of schemes](../../../../../morphism-of-schemes.md)

$$
\ell'=(\ell,v'):\operatorname{Spec}D\longrightarrow X\times_SS'.
$$

The [universal property of a fibre product](../../../../../universal-property-of-a-fibre-product.md) shows that $\ell'$ extends $u'$ and lies over $v'$. Conversely, every extension $\ell'$ has such a first component $\ell$. Thus the sets of extensions in the two problems are in [bijection](../../../../../bijection.md).

If $f$ is a [universally closed morphism](../../../../../universally-closed-morphism.md), it is a [quasi-compact morphism](../../../../../quasi-compact-morphism.md) and satisfies existence. The allowed stability of [quasi-compact morphisms](../../../../../quasi-compact-morphism.md) under [base change](../../../../../base-change-of-a-morphism-of-schemes.md) and (a)(i) prove that $f'$ is a [universally closed morphism](../../../../../universally-closed-morphism.md). If $f$ is a [separated morphism](../../../../../separated-morphism.md), it is a [quasi-separated morphism](../../../../../quasi-separated-morphism.md) and satisfies uniqueness. Stability of [quasi-separated morphisms](../../../../../quasi-separated-morphism.md) under [base change](../../../../../base-change-of-a-morphism-of-schemes.md) and (a)(ii) prove that $f'$ is a [separated morphism](../../../../../separated-morphism.md).

If $f$ is a [proper morphism](../../../../../proper-morphism.md), the same [bijection](../../../../../bijection.md) supplies existence and uniqueness. The permitted stability under [base change](../../../../../base-change-of-a-morphism-of-schemes.md) of [quasi-compact morphisms](../../../../../quasi-compact-morphism.md), [quasi-separated morphisms](../../../../../quasi-separated-morphism.md) and [locally of finite type](../../../../../locally-of-finite-type.md) supplies all remaining hypotheses of (a)(iii). Hence

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
f\text{ universally closed}&\Longrightarrow f'\text{ universally closed},\\
f\text{ separated}&\Longrightarrow f'\text{ separated},\\
f\text{ proper}&\Longrightarrow f'\text{ proper}.
\end{aligned}}
$$
