<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Let $U=\operatorname{Spec}A$ and $V=\operatorname{Spec}B$ be [affine open subschemes](../../../../affine-open-subscheme.md) of the [separated scheme](../../../../separated-scheme.md) $X$. Its [diagonal morphism](../../../../diagonal-morphism.md) is a [closed immersion](../../../../closed-immersion.md)

$$
\Delta:X\hookrightarrow X\times_{\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Z}X.
$$

Restricting this [closed immersion](../../../../closed-immersion.md) to the [open subscheme](../../../../open-subscheme.md) $U\times_{\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Z}V$ identifies its inverse image with $U\cap V$. Hence

$$
U\cap V\hookrightarrow U\times_{\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Z}V
=\operatorname{Spec}(A\otimes_{\mathbb Z}B)
$$

is a [closed immersion](../../../../closed-immersion.md). A [closed subscheme](../../../../closed-subscheme.md) of an [affine scheme](../../../../affine-scheme.md) is an [affine scheme](../../../../affine-scheme.md), so

$$
\boxed{U\cap V\text{ is an affine open subscheme of }X.}
$$

The argument iterates to every finite intersection of [affine open subschemes](../../../../affine-open-subscheme.md). The empty intersection of two disjoint charts causes no exception: the empty [scheme](../../../../scheme.md) is $\operatorname{Spec}(0)$.
