<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a [quasi-compact scheme](../../../../quasi-compact-scheme.md) $X$, the [Serre criterion for affineness](../../../../cohomological-criterion-for-affineness.md) gives the equivalences

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
X\text{ affine}
&\iff H^1(X,\mathcal F)=0\text{ for every quasi-coherent }\mathcal O_X\text{-module }\mathcal F\\
&\iff H^q(X,\mathcal F)=0\text{ for every such }\mathcal F\text{ and every }q>0.
\end{aligned}}
$$

Indeed, an [affine scheme](../../../../affine-scheme.md) has [vanishing of quasi-coherent cohomology on an affine scheme](../../../../vanishing-of-quasi-coherent-cohomology-on-an-affine-scheme.md). Conversely, vanishing of the first [sheaf cohomology](../../../../sheaf-cohomology.md) for every [quasi-coherent sheaf](../../../../quasi-coherent-sheaf.md) includes every [quasi-coherent sheaf](../../../../quasi-coherent-sheaf.md) of ideals, which suffices for the criterion. No [separated scheme](../../../../separated-scheme.md) hypothesis is needed in this formulation. Nor may one test only the [structure sheaf](../../../../structure-sheaf-of-a-scheme.md).[Stacks Project, Serre's criterion for a quasi-compact scheme, using quasi-coherent ideals](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/01XF)
