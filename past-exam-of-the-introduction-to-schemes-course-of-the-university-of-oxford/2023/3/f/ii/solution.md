<h1 id="3/f/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $i:X_{\mathrm{red}}\hookrightarrow X$ be the [reduced induced subscheme](../../../../../reduced-induced-subscheme.md), with [structure sheaf](../../../../../structure-sheaf-of-a-scheme.md) $\mathcal O_{X_{\mathrm{red}}}=\mathcal O_X/\mathcal N$. Its underlying map of [topological spaces](../../../../../topological-space.md) is a [homeomorphism](../../../../../homeomorphism.md), since every [prime ideal](../../../../../prime-ideal.md) contains the [nilradical](../../../../../nilradical.md).

Put $\mathcal G=\mathcal F/\mathcal N\mathcal F$. Multiplication by $\mathcal N$ annihilates $\mathcal G$, so its action by $\mathcal O_X$ factors through $\mathcal O_X/\mathcal N$. Thus $\mathcal G$ is naturally a [sheaf of modules](../../../../../sheaf-of-modules-split.md) over $\mathcal O_{X_{\mathrm{red}}}$. On an [affine open subscheme](../../../../../affine-open-subscheme.md) $\operatorname{Spec}A$, with $\mathcal F=\widetilde M$, it corresponds to $M/\operatorname{Nil}(A)M$ over $A/\operatorname{Nil}(A)$, so it remains a [quasi-coherent sheaf](../../../../../quasi-coherent-sheaf.md).

The [sheaf cohomology](../../../../../sheaf-cohomology.md) of a [sheaf of modules](../../../../../sheaf-of-modules-split.md) is the [sheaf cohomology](../../../../../sheaf-cohomology.md) of its underlying [sheaf of abelian groups](../../../../../sheaf-of-abelian-groups.md). Under the above [homeomorphism](../../../../../homeomorphism.md), that underlying sheaf is identical on the two spaces: the [global sections functor](../../../../../global-sections-functor.md) and its [flasque resolutions](../../../../../flasque-resolution.md) are identical. Equivalently, use [sheaf cohomology under a closed inclusion](../../../../../sheaf-cohomology-under-a-closed-inclusion.md) with $i_*\mathcal G$. Therefore there are canonical [isomorphisms](../../../../../isomorphism.md), in every degree,

$$
\boxed{H^q(X,\mathcal F/\mathcal N\mathcal F)\cong H^q(X_{\mathrm{red}},\mathcal F/\mathcal N\mathcal F)\qquad(q\geq0).}
$$

In particular, this gives the requested equality in degree one. Equality of the underlying [topological spaces](../../../../../topological-space.md) alone would not compare arbitrary coefficient sheaves; the factorization of the action explains why the indicated coefficients are the same.
