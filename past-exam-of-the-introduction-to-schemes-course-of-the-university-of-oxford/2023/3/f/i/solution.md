<h1 id="3/f/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

A [Noetherian scheme](../../../../../noetherian-scheme.md) is a [quasi-compact scheme](../../../../../quasi-compact-scheme.md), so choose a finite [affine open cover](../../../../../affine-open-cover.md) $X=\bigcup_{i=1}^s\operatorname{Spec}A_i$ with all $A_i$ [Noetherian rings](../../../../../noetherian-ring.md). The [nilradical sheaf](../../../../../nilradical-sheaf.md) restricts on this chart to the [quasi-coherent sheaf](../../../../../quasi-coherent-sheaf.md) associated to $\operatorname{Nil}(A_i)$: the [nilradical](../../../../../nilradical.md) commutes with [localization](../../../../../localization-of-a-ring.md).

By the allowed fact, choose $d_i\geq1$ such that $\operatorname{Nil}(A_i)^{d_i}=0$ and put $m=\max_i d_i$. On every chart, the [ideal sheaf](../../../../../ideal-sheaf-of-a-closed-subscheme.md) $\mathcal N^m$ is zero, and hence it is zero globally. Higher powers are also zero. Thus

$$
\boxed{\mathcal N^d=0\qquad\text{for every }d\geq m.}
$$

If $X$ is empty, take $m=1$. The finite [affine open cover](../../../../../affine-open-cover.md) is essential for choosing one bound valid everywhere.
