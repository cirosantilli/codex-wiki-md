↑ **Parent:** [Biology bibliography](biology-bibliography-split.md)

[https://wellcomecollection.org/works/ebja53uq](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/ebja53uq)

Institutional catalogue for the primary compilation Sepulchretum sive anatomia practica ex cadaveribus morbo denatis. Identifies author, two volumes, Geneva and 1679. The observation numbers in the Oxford extract are those invoked by [Morgagni](giovanni-battista-morgagni.md); no unsupported claim is made to have independently verified every observation or the exact later edition he consulted.
