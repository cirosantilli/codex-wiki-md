# Boundary layer with exponentially varying outer velocity

↑ **Parent:** [Prandtl boundary-layer equation](prandtl-boundary-layer-equation.md)

For outer tangential speed $U_s=Ae^{\alpha x}$ in steady [Prandtl boundary-layer equations](prandtl-boundary-layer-equation.md), the displayed [similarity solution](similarity-solution.md) makes the streamwise convection, [pressure gradient](pressure-gradient.md) and transverse viscous diffusion have the same exponential factor. Its profile equation is $f'''+(\alpha/2)ff''+\alpha(A^2-(f')^2)=0$, with $f(0)=f'(0)=0$ and $f'(\infty)=A$. Attached far-field matching requires $\alpha A>0$: the outer speed increases along the direction of motion. Linearizing $g=f'-A$ at large $\eta$ gives $g''+(\alpha A/2)\eta g'-2\alpha A g\simeq0$. For positive $\alpha A$ there is a Gaussian-decaying mode, while negative $\alpha A$ leaves only growing nontrivial asymptotic modes. Local thinness additionally requires a large [Reynolds number](reynolds-number.md) based on the speed and its streamwise variation length.
