# Relative frequency

↑ **Parent:** [Statistics](statistics.md)

For a sample or finite [biological population](population-biology.md) of positive size $n$, the [relative frequency](relative-frequency.md) of a category represented $n_i$ times is $n_i/n$. It is a proportion and differs from an oscillation [frequency](frequency.md).
