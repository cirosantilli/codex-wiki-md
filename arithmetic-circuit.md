# Arithmetic circuit

↑ **Parent:** [Circuit complexity](circuit-complexity.md)

An arithmetic circuit is a [Directed acyclic graph](directed-acyclic-graph.md) whose input nodes are [variables](variable-mathematics.md) or constants in a [field](field.md), and whose internal gates perform [addition](addition.md), [subtraction](subtraction.md), or [multiplication](multiplication.md). Gates may reuse earlier outputs, so the circuit can represent a [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) without listing all its [monomials](monomial.md).
