# Hyperbolicity is invariant under quasi-isometry

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic metric space](hyperbolic-metric-space.md)

For [geodesic metric spaces](geodesic-metric-space.md), a [quasi-isometry](quasi-isometry.md) preserves the property of being a [Gromov-hyperbolic metric space](hyperbolic-metric-space.md). Images of [metric geodesic](metric-geodesic.md) sides are uniform [quasigeodesics](quasigeodesic.md). The [Morse lemma for quasi-geodesics](morse-lemma-for-quasi-geodesics.md) keeps them uniformly close to [metric geodesic](metric-geodesic.md) sides in the hyperbolic target. Thinness there and the coarse lower distance bound pull a uniform triangle-thinness constant back to the source. Coarse surjectivity supplies a quasi-inverse for the reverse implication. Consequently a [metric geodesic](metric-geodesic.md) space quasi-isometric to a tree is hyperbolic, with no local finiteness assumption on the tree.
