# Fluorescence emission filter

↑ **Parent:** [Fluorescence microscope](fluorescence-microscope.md)

A [fluorescence emission filter](fluorescence-emission-filter.md) transmits the chosen emitted [wavelength](wavelength.md) band while rejecting excitation leakage. Finite rejection, overlap of the [fluorescence emission spectrum](fluorescence-emission-spectrum.md) with excitation, and the [photodetector](photodetector.md)'s wavelength-dependent response can all affect the count. A darker filtered image alone does not establish a changed [fluorescence lifetime](fluorescence-lifetime.md) or increased [photobleaching](photobleaching.md).
