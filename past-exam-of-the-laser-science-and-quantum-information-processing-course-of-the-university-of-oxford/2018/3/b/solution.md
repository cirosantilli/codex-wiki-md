<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let the initial field be the normalized [coherent state](../../../../coherent-state.md) $|z\rangle=\sum_{n=0}^\infty c_n|n\rangle$, where $c_n=e^{-|z|^2/2}z^n/\sqrt{n!}$. The atom is again initially excited. Linearity of the [Schrödinger equation](../../../../schrodinger-equation.md) gives

$$
\boxed{|\Psi(t)\rangle=\sum_{n=0}^\infty c_ne^{-i\omega(n+1/2)t}\left[\cos(g\sqrt{n+1}\,t)|e,n\rangle-i\sin(g\sqrt{n+1}\,t)|g,n+1\rangle\right].}
$$

The different [photon numbers](../../../../photon-number.md) have different [Rabi frequencies](../../../../rabi-frequency.md), so the state generally becomes entangled between atom and field. It cannot in general be replaced by one Rabi-oscillating atomic state multiplied by an unchanged [coherent state](../../../../coherent-state.md).
