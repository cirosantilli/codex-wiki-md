<h1 id="3/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

Four complementary classical features of a large-amplitude [coherent state](../../../../coherent-state.md) are:

- It is an eigenstate of the [annihilation operator](../../../../annihilation-operator.md), $a|z\rangle=z|z\rangle$. Normally ordered field [amplitudes](../../../../wave-amplitude.md) therefore behave like a classical complex [amplitude](../../../../wave-amplitude.md); normalized [photon subtraction](../../../../photon-subtraction.md) leaves the same state.
- Free harmonic evolution preserves its form, $|z\rangle\to|ze^{-i\omega t}\rangle$. The mean [electric field](../../../../electric-field-split.md) follows a classical sinusoidal wave with an [amplitude](../../../../wave-amplitude.md) and [phase](../../../../phase-waves.md) set by $z$.
- Its [photon number](../../../../photon-number.md) has [Poisson distribution](../../../../poisson-distribution.md), with $\Delta n/\bar n=1/\sqrt{\bar n}$ and $g^{(2)}(0)=1$. Relative [intensity](../../../../intensity-physics.md) fluctuations become small at large [amplitude](../../../../wave-amplitude.md).
- Its [optical quadratures](../../../../optical-quadrature.md) $X=(a+a^\dagger)/2$ and $P=(a-a^\dagger)/(2i)$ have minimum, balanced uncertainties, $\Delta X=\Delta P=1/2$ and $\Delta X\Delta P=1/4$. The fixed vacuum-scale fluctuation becomes small relative to the large phase-space displacement.

These properties identify a particular classical oscillation, rather than a state of definite [photon number](../../../../photon-number.md). Absolute quantum fluctuations remain even when their relative size is small.
