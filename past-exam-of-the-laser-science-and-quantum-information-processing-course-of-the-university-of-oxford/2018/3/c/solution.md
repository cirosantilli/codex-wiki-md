<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Project the field onto the five-photon [number state](../../../../number-state.md). The excited-atom contribution comes from the initial $n=5$ block, while the ground-atom contribution comes from the initial $n=4$ block. The unnormalized conditional atomic state is

$$
|\widetilde\chi_5\rangle=c_5e^{-i11\omega t/2}\cos(g\sqrt6\,t)|e\rangle-i c_4e^{-i9\omega t/2}\sin(g\sqrt5\,t)|g\rangle.
$$

Thus the five-count [probability](../../../../probability.md) and normalized post-measurement state are

$$
\boxed{P_5=|c_5|^2\cos^2(g\sqrt6\,t)+|c_4|^2\sin^2(g\sqrt5\,t),\qquad
|\chi_5\rangle=\frac{|\widetilde\chi_5\rangle}{\sqrt{P_5}}.}
$$

The relative free-evolution [phase](../../../../phase-waves.md) is physically retained; it is absent only in an explicitly rotating atomic frame. The conditional state is defined when $P_5>0$. In particular, an initial vacuum cannot produce five photons in this one-atom model, so that conditioning event has [probability](../../../../probability.md) zero when $z=0$.
