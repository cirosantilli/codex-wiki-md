<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The conditional [probability](../../../../probability.md) of the atomic ground state is the squared ground-state [amplitude](../../../../wave-amplitude.md) divided by the five-photon event [probability](../../../../probability.md):

$$
\boxed{P(g\mid5)=\frac{|c_4|^2\sin^2(g\sqrt5\,t)}{|c_5|^2\cos^2(g\sqrt6\,t)+|c_4|^2\sin^2(g\sqrt5\,t)}.}
$$

For $z\ne0$, $|c_5|^2=|z|^2|c_4|^2/5$, giving the equivalent expression

$$
\boxed{P(g\mid5)=\frac{5\sin^2(g\sqrt5\,t)}{|z|^2\cos^2(g\sqrt6\,t)+5\sin^2(g\sqrt5\,t)}.}
$$

It applies only when the conditioning denominator is nonzero. The distinct $\sqrt5$ and $\sqrt6$ factors reflect the two different initial photon-number blocks.
