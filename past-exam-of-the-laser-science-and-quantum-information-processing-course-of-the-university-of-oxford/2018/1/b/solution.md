<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The temporal [intensity](../../../../intensity-physics.md) is proportional to the squared field modulus:

$$
I(t)=I_0e^{-2at^2}.
$$

Its half-maximum points satisfy $2at^2=\ln2$, so the [intensity](../../../../intensity-physics.md) [full width at half maximum](../../../../full-width-at-half-maximum.md) is

$$
\boxed{\tau=\sqrt{\frac{2\ln2}{a}}.}
$$

With the chosen $e^{-i\omega t}$ convention, the instantaneous [angular frequency](../../../../angular-frequency.md) is minus the time derivative of the field's argument. Hence

$$
\boxed{\omega(t)=\omega_0+2bt.}
$$

A localized Gaussian requires $\operatorname{Re}\Gamma=a>0$. It is positively chirped precisely when $d\omega/dt=2\operatorname{Im}\Gamma>0$, or $b>0$; $b=0$ gives no chirp and $b<0$ gives negative chirp.
