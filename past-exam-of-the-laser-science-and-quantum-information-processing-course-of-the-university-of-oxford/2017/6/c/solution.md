<h1 id="6/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Subtract the common sector [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) and call the remaining [eigenvalue](../../../../eigenvalue.md) $\hbar\lambda$. Its characteristic equation is

$$
\lambda^2-\frac{\Delta^2}{4}-g^2(n+1)=0.
$$

The dressed-state energies are consequently

$$
\boxed{E_{\pm n}=\hbar\omega\left(n+\frac12\right)\pm\frac\hbar2\Omega_n,
\qquad\Omega_n=\sqrt{\Delta^2+4g^2(n+1)}.}
$$

On resonance their splitting is $2\hbar|g|\sqrt{n+1}$; far off resonance the [eigenstates](../../../../eigenstate.md) approach the uncoupled [quantum states](../../../../quantum-state.md) with small dispersive [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) shifts. The [eigenvalue](../../../../eigenvalue.md) splitting is an angular-frequency difference $\Omega_n$, so an ordinary [frequency](../../../../frequency.md) would be $\Omega_n/(2\pi)$.
