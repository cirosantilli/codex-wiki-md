<h1 id="6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The total excitation operator $\mathcal N=a^\dagger a+(\sigma_z+1)/2$ commutes with the [Hamiltonian](../../../../hamiltonian.md). Each sector with $\mathcal N=n+1\geq1$ is therefore spanned by $|e,n\rangle,|g,n+1\rangle$, and no term connects it to a sector of different [excitation number](../../../../total-excitation-number.md).

In that ordered basis, with the source's [Rabi detuning](../../../../rabi-detuning.md) $\Delta=\omega-\omega_0$,

$$
\boxed{\mathcal H_n=\hbar\omega\left(n+\frac12\right)I
+\hbar\begin{pmatrix}-\Delta/2&g\sqrt{n+1}\\g\sqrt{n+1}&\Delta/2\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

The first term is the common sector [energy](../../../../energy-split.md); the remaining diagonal entries record the bare [Rabi detuning](../../../../rabi-detuning.md) and the off-diagonal terms exchange the excitation.

The [isolated ground sector of the Jaynes-Cummings model](../../../../isolated-ground-sector-of-the-jaynes-cummings-model.md) is an important exception to a statement that the _entire_ Hilbert space consists of such pairs: $|g,0\rangle$ has no partner and is annihilated by both interaction terms. It is a one-dimensional invariant sector with [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) $-\hbar\omega_0/2$. The full decomposition is this one-dimensional sector plus all the two-dimensional sectors above.
