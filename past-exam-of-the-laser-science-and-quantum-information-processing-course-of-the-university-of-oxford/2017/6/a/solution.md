<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use $a|n\rangle=\sqrt n|n-1\rangle$, $a^\dagger|n\rangle=\sqrt{n+1}|n+1\rangle$, $\sigma_z|e\rangle=|e\rangle$ and $\sigma_z|g\rangle=-|g\rangle$. Only one interaction term acts on each of the two [quantum states](../../../../quantum-state.md). Hence

$$
\boxed{\mathcal H|e,n\rangle=\hbar\left(n\omega+\frac{\omega_0}{2}\right)|e,n\rangle
+\hbar g\sqrt{n+1}|g,n+1\rangle,}
$$



$$
\boxed{\mathcal H|g,n+1\rangle=\hbar\left((n+1)\omega-\frac{\omega_0}{2}\right)|g,n+1\rangle
+\hbar g\sqrt{n+1}|e,n\rangle.}
$$

The interaction exchanges one atomic excitation with one field quantum. The square-root factor is the photon-number enhancement of the transition [matrix](../../../../matrix.md) element.
