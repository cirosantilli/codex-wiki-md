<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Use state-dependent [fluorescence](../../../../fluorescence-split.md). With 397 nm light on $S_{1/2}\leftrightarrow P_{1/2}$ and an 866 nm repumper on $D_{3/2}\leftrightarrow P_{1/2}$, an ion initially in $D_{3/2}$ is brought into a bright cycling manifold. The repumper prevents population trapping in $D_{3/2}$. An ion in $D_{5/2}$ remains dark while the 854 nm light is off, since it has no allowed link to this cycle. A detected bright or dark record therefore measures the [qubit](../../../../qubit.md) in its logical basis by [electron shelving](../../../../electron-shelving.md).

To reset, apply 854 nm light to empty the shelved state, and use 397 nm plus repumping for [Doppler cooling](../../../../doppler-cooling.md) and internal-state preparation. [Doppler cooling](../../../../doppler-cooling.md) alone generally does not reach the [ground state](../../../../ground-state.md). In the resolved-sideband and [Lamb-Dicke regime](../../../../lamb-dicke-regime.md), drive the [red motional Raman sideband](../../../../motional-sideband.md)

$$
|D_{3/2},n\rangle\longrightarrow|D_{5/2},n-1\rangle
$$

and then optically repump to $D_{3/2}$ with little change in motional occupation. Each cycle removes approximately one vibrational quantum. The $n=0$ state is dark to this red sideband because its [matrix](../../../../matrix.md) element contains $\sqrt n$. Repeat with appropriate [wavevector](../../../../wavevector.md) projections and [frequency](../../../../frequency.md) differences for all modes to be cooled, then optically pump into the selected $D_{3/2}$ sublevel. Small recoil during repumping is necessary for a high ground-state [probability](../../../../probability.md).
