<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The two $D$ states are metastable, so a [quantum superposition](../../../../quantum-superposition.md) can retain its relative [phase](../../../../phase-waves.md) much longer than one involving a rapidly decaying $P$ state. In the stipulated transition diagram they have no allowed downward [electric-dipole transition](../../../../electric-dipole-transition.md); real weaker decay channels limit, rather than eliminate, this lifetime advantage. Their distinct energies permit resolved state-selective optical control.

Both $D$ levels couple to $P_{3/2}$. Apply coherent beams near 850 nm and 854 nm, detuned from that common intermediate level by a large [Rabi detuning](../../../../rabi-detuning.md) $\Delta_R$. Absorption from one beam and [stimulated emission](../../../../stimulated-emission.md) into the other transfer population between the two $D$ states without appreciably populating the short-lived intermediate level. Tune the [frequency](../../../../frequency.md) difference to their splitting, including differential AC Stark shifts. In the far-detuned limit,

$$
\boxed{\Omega_{\mathrm{eff}}\simeq\frac{\Omega_{850}\Omega_{854}^*}{2\Delta_R},\qquad
P_{D_{5/2}}(t)=\sin^2\!\left(\frac{|\Omega_{\mathrm{eff}}|t}{2}\right)}
$$

for an initially pure $D_{3/2}$ state on two-photon resonance. The controllable relative optical [phase](../../../../phase-waves.md) sets the rotation axis; the large [Rabi detuning](../../../../rabi-detuning.md) suppresses spontaneous-scattering errors. Suitable [polarizations](../../../../polarization-waves.md) and a chosen pair of magnetic sublevels are required in an actual experiment.
