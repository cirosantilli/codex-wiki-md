<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Wave plates](../../../../waveplate.md) and [polarization](../../../../polarization-waves.md) [phase](../../../../phase-waves.md) shifters implement deterministic single-qubit [unitaries](../../../../unitary-connection.md): a suitable quarter-wave/half-wave/quarter-wave sequence realizes an arbitrary [polarization](../../../../polarization-waves.md) rotation. A deterministic entangling gate on two independently encoded [photons](../../../../photon.md) needs an effective interaction or additional resources that make one [photon](../../../../photon.md)'s logical action conditional on the other. For example, a sufficiently strong conditional [phase](../../../../phase-waves.md) followed by Hadamards on the target implements [CNOT gate](../../../../controlled-not-gate.md). Ordinary passive [linear optics](../../../../linear-optics.md) alone does not provide this deterministic interaction on independent [polarization](../../../../polarization-waves.md) [qubits](../../../../qubit.md). The practical difficulty is that optical nonlinearities at the single-photon level are weak and commonly accompanied by loss or noise; auxiliary [photons](../../../../photon.md), measurements and feed-forward trade determinism for resource overhead.

The [polarizing beam splitter](../../../../polarizing-beam-splitter.md) eigenpolarizations at angle $\alpha$ are the orthonormal states

$$
\boxed{|T_\alpha\rangle=\cos\alpha|H\rangle+\sin\alpha|V\rangle,\qquad
|R_\alpha\rangle=-\sin\alpha|H\rangle+\cos\alpha|V\rangle.}
$$

The first is perfectly transmitted and the second perfectly reflected, from either input port. Port-dependent reflection [phases](../../../../phase-waves.md) can be fixed by a consistent optical [phase](../../../../phase-waves.md) convention or compensated locally; they do not alter the transmitted/reflected basis.
