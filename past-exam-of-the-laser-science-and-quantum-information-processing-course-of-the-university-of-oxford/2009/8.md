## 1

↑ **Parent:** [8](8.md)

<h3 id="1/solution">Solution</h3>

↑ **Parent:** [1](#1)

Normalize the supplied [Bell state](../../bell-state.md) as $|\Psi^+\rangle=(|01\rangle+|10\rangle)/\sqrt2$, with $|V\rangle=|0\rangle$ and $|H\rangle=|1\rangle$. For a [measurement of linear photon polarization](../../quantum-measurement.md#measurement-of-linear-photon-polarization), the two orthogonal analyzer states can be taken as

$$
|\phi,+\rangle=\cos\phi|0\rangle+\sin\phi|1\rangle,\qquad
|\phi,-\rangle=-\sin\phi|0\rangle+\cos\phi|1\rangle.
$$

Assigning outcomes $+1,-1$ gives the [observable](../../quantum-system.md#observable)

$$
M(\phi)=|\phi,+\rangle\langle\phi,+|-|\phi,-\rangle\langle\phi,-|
=\begin{pmatrix}\cos2\phi&\sin2\phi\\\sin2\phi&-\cos2\phi\end{pmatrix}
=\cos2\phi\,Z+\sin2\phi\,X,
$$

where $X,Z$ are the [Pauli operators](../../quantum-circuit.md#pauli-operator). The angle of the [Bloch vector](../../density-matrix.md#bloch-vector) is twice the physical analyzer angle. Hence

$$
\boxed{A_1=Z,\quad A_2=X,\quad A_3=\frac{Z+X}{\sqrt2};\qquad
B_1=Z,\quad B_2=\frac{Z-X}{\sqrt2},\quad B_3=\frac{Z+X}{\sqrt2}.}
$$

Each has [eigenvalues](../../linear-operator-theory.md#eigenvalue) $\pm1$ and square equal to the [identity operator](../../vector-space.md#identity-operator).

## 2

↑ **Parent:** [8](8.md)

<h3 id="2/solution">Solution</h3>

↑ **Parent:** [2](#2)

Use an [Ekert protocol](../../computer-science.md#ekert-protocol). Alice and Bob choose their analyzer settings independently and randomly for each received pair, record the outcomes, and confirm reception before announcing settings over an [authenticated public classical channel](../../computer-science.md#authenticated-public-classical-channel). The authentication prevents an adversary from impersonating either party; it does not conceal the setting announcements. Announcing settings after reception also avoids the [premature basis announcement in quantum key distribution](../../computer-science.md#premature-basis-announcement-in-quantum-key-distribution) attack.

The [polarization correlations of the Psi-plus Bell state](../../bell-state.md#polarization-correlations-of-the-psi-plus-bell-state) satisfy

$$
E_{ij}=\langle\Psi^+|A_i\otimes B_j|\Psi^+\rangle,
\qquad E(\alpha,\beta)=-\cos 2(\alpha+\beta).
$$

Indeed, $\langle Z\otimes Z\rangle=-1$, $\langle X\otimes X\rangle=1$, and the cross terms vanish. In the specified setting order,

$$
(E_{ij})=\begin{pmatrix}
-1&-1/\sqrt2&-1/\sqrt2\\
0&-1/\sqrt2&1/\sqrt2\\
-1/\sqrt2&-1&0
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

For [key sifting](../../computer-science.md#key-sifting), retain the setting pairs **$(i,j)=(1,1)$ and $(3,2)$**. Their outcomes are perfectly anticorrelated. Alice assigns bit $0$ to outcome $+1$ and bit $1$ to outcome $-1$; Bob uses the opposite assignment. Their [raw keys](../../computer-science.md#raw-key) then agree. With uniform independent choices of the three settings, these pairs supply a raw-key fraction **$2/9$**, before sacrificing a test sample. They must keep the remaining raw-key outcomes secret. The four pairs $(1,2),(1,3),(2,2),(2,3)$ supply Bell-test data, and the other three setting pairs can be discarded.

Reveal the outcomes of those Bell-test rounds and estimate

$$
E_{ij}=\frac{N_{++}+N_{--}-N_{+-}-N_{-+}}{N_{++}+N_{--}+N_{+-}+N_{-+}},
\qquad
\boxed{S=-E_{12}-E_{13}-E_{22}+E_{23}.}
$$

The ideal entries give **$S=2\sqrt2$**. The [CHSH inequality](../../foundations-of-quantum-mechanics.md#chsh-inequality) gives $|S|\le2$ for [local hidden-variable theories](../../foundations-of-quantum-mechanics.md#local-hidden-variable-theory): for predetermined outcomes $a_1,a_2,b_2,b_3\in\{\pm1\}$, the signed combination is $-a_1(b_2+b_3)-a_2(b_2-b_3)$. One bracket is zero and the other is $\pm2$, so averaging cannot exceed two in magnitude. The [Tsirelson bound](../../foundations-of-quantum-mechanics.md#tsirelson-s-bound) limits quantum correlations to $2\sqrt2$.

For the specified, trusted [Pauli operators](../../quantum-circuit.md#pauli-operator), the [maximal CHSH witness for a fixed Bell state](../../foundations-of-quantum-mechanics.md#maximal-chsh-witness-for-a-fixed-bell-state) makes the ideal security statement precise. Its operator is

$$
\mathcal B=-A_1\otimes(B_2+B_3)-A_2\otimes(B_2-B_3)
=\sqrt2(X\otimes X-Z\otimes Z).
$$

Its largest [eigenvalue](../../linear-operator-theory.md#eigenvalue) $2\sqrt2$ has the unique [eigenvector](../../linear-operator-theory.md#eigenvector) $|\Psi^+\rangle$. Exact attainment therefore fixes the shared [density matrix](../../density-matrix.md) to this [pure state](../../quantum-theory.md#pure-state). Since [purity decouples a subsystem from its purification](../../density-matrix.md#purity-decouples-a-subsystem-from-its-purification), Eve cannot hold information correlated with that pair. This rules out information-gaining eavesdropping in the ideal model, rather than proving that no observer exists. More generally, [CHSH self-testing](../../foundations-of-quantum-mechanics.md#chsh-self-testing) identifies the entangled qubit pair up to local changes of representation under its own assumptions.

A [quantum measurement](../../quantum-measurement.md) that reads Eve's chosen polarization generally disturbs correlations in an incompatible basis. The [no-cloning theorem](../../quantum-theory.md#no-cloning-theorem) also prevents copying arbitrary unknown photon polarizations and retaining a perfect copy while forwarding an unchanged original. The Bell test checks several incompatible settings, so observing perfect correlation in only one key basis is insufficient.

Alice and Bob also reveal a randomly chosen sample of their raw keys to estimate the [quantum bit error rate](../../computer-science.md#quantum-bit-error-rate), then discard that sample. [Information reconciliation](../../computer-science.md#information-reconciliation) corrects discrepancies, and [privacy amplification](../../computer-science.md#privacy-amplification) removes residual information, including leakage from the public transcript. With finite data they need statistical bounds and a security analysis appropriate to their devices and noise. A measured violation $S>2$ alone does not assert zero information for Eve; the exact ideal argument above uses the maximal value.[Original 1991 proposal connecting Bell-correlation tests to quantum key distribution](../../physics-bibliography.md#quantum-cryptography-based-on-bell-s-theorem-by-artur-k-ekert)

## 3

↑ **Parent:** [8](8.md)

<h3 id="3/solution">Solution</h3>

↑ **Parent:** [3](#3)

Eve's [computational-basis intercept-resend attack](../../computer-science.md#computational-basis-intercept-resend-attack) applies a [projective measurement](../../quantum-measurement.md#projective-measurement) of $Z$ to Bob's photon and discards her outcome from Alice and Bob's description. Their resulting [density matrix](../../density-matrix.md) is

$$
\rho_{AB}^{\rm Eve}=\frac12|01\rangle\langle01|+\frac12|10\rangle\langle10|.
$$

This [separable state](../../quantum-information-theory.md#separable-quantum-state) keeps $\langle Z\otimes Z\rangle=-1$ but removes $\langle X\otimes X\rangle$. Hence

$$
E_{\rm Eve}(\alpha,\beta)=-\cos2\alpha\cos2\beta,
\qquad
(E_{ij}^{\rm Eve})=\begin{pmatrix}
-1&-1/\sqrt2&-1/\sqrt2\\
0&0&0\\
-1/\sqrt2&-1/2&-1/2
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Using the same signed Bell-test statistic gives

$$
\boxed{S_{\rm Eve}=\sqrt2,\qquad S_{\rm ideal}=2\sqrt2.}
$$

The ideal value is halved, and the attack no longer violates the [CHSH inequality](../../foundations-of-quantum-mechanics.md#chsh-inequality).

The key data show why several settings matter. For $(1,1)$, Eve's measurement leaves perfect anticorrelation, so this subset has **zero bit errors** after Bob flips his assignment. For $(3,2)$, $E=-1/2$, so correct anticorrelation has probability $(1-E)/2=3/4$: its [quantum bit error rate](../../computer-science.md#quantum-bit-error-rate) is **$1/4$**. If both key-setting pairs occur equally often, their combined raw-key error rate is **$1/8$**. The Bell-test change detects this attack even though the vertical/horizontal key subset alone would not.
