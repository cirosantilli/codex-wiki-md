<h1 id="1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Normalize the supplied [Bell state](../../../../bell-state-split.md) as $|\Psi^+\rangle=(|01\rangle+|10\rangle)/\sqrt2$, with $|V\rangle=|0\rangle$ and $|H\rangle=|1\rangle$. For a [measurement of linear photon polarization](../../../../measurement-of-linear-photon-polarization.md), the two orthogonal analyzer states can be taken as

$$
|\phi,+\rangle=\cos\phi|0\rangle+\sin\phi|1\rangle,\qquad
|\phi,-\rangle=-\sin\phi|0\rangle+\cos\phi|1\rangle.
$$

Assigning outcomes $+1,-1$ gives the [observable](../../../../observable.md)

$$
M(\phi)=|\phi,+\rangle\langle\phi,+|-|\phi,-\rangle\langle\phi,-|
=\begin{pmatrix}\cos2\phi&\sin2\phi\\\sin2\phi&-\cos2\phi\end{pmatrix}
=\cos2\phi\,Z+\sin2\phi\,X,
$$

where $X,Z$ are the [Pauli operators](../../../../pauli-operator.md). The angle of the [Bloch vector](../../../../bloch-vector.md) is twice the physical analyzer angle. Hence

$$
\boxed{A_1=Z,\quad A_2=X,\quad A_3=\frac{Z+X}{\sqrt2};\qquad
B_1=Z,\quad B_2=\frac{Z-X}{\sqrt2},\quad B_3=\frac{Z+X}{\sqrt2}.}
$$

Each has [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md) $\pm1$ and square equal to the [identity operator](../../../../identity-operator.md).
