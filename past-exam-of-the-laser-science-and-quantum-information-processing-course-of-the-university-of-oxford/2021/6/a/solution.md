<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Quantize a single electromagnetic [normal mode](../../../../normal-mode.md) of [angular frequency](../../../../angular-frequency.md) $\omega_c$ and represent one isolated atomic transition as a [two-level system](../../../../two-level-system.md) with raising operator $\sigma_+=|e\rangle\langle g|$. The bare [Hamiltonian operator](../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) is

$$
H_0=\hbar\omega_c a^\dagger a+\frac{\hbar\omega_a}{2}\sigma_z.
$$

The first main approximation is the [electric-dipole approximation](../../../../electric-dipole-approximation.md): the field varies negligibly across an atom, $ka_{\rm atom}\ll1$, and the leading coupling is $H_d=-\mathbf d\cdot\mathbf E(\mathbf r_0)$. For states of definite opposite parity, the relevant dipole part is off-diagonal. After choosing operator [phases](../../../../phase-waves.md), this gives $H_d=\hbar g(a+a^\dagger)(\sigma_++\sigma_-)$, with $g$ fixed by the transition dipole and the one-[photon](../../../../photon.md) field [wave amplitude](../../../../wave-amplitude.md).

In the [interaction picture](../../../../interaction-picture.md), $a\sigma_+$ and $a^\dagger\sigma_-$ oscillate at the difference $\omega_a-\omega_c$, whereas $a\sigma_-$ and $a^\dagger\sigma_+$ oscillate at the sum $\omega_a+\omega_c$. The second main approximation is the [rotating-wave approximation](../../../../rotating-wave-approximation.md): near [resonance](../../../../resonance.md) and for $g\sqrt{n+1}\ll\omega_a+\omega_c$, the latter terms average away on the interaction time scale. The resulting [Jaynes-Cummings model](../../../../jaynes-cummings-model.md) has

$$
\boxed{H_I=\hbar g(a\sigma_++a^\dagger\sigma_-).}
$$

The [two-level system](../../../../two-level-system.md) and single-[normal mode](../../../../normal-mode.md) truncations are additional assumptions: other atomic transitions and optical [normal modes](../../../../normal-mode.md) must be sufficiently far off [resonance](../../../../resonance.md) or weakly coupled. Dissipation is neglected over the interaction time. The retained terms exchange one atomic excitation for one [photon](../../../../photon.md), conserving $a^\dagger a+\sigma_+\sigma_-$.
