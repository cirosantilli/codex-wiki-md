<h1 id="6/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

An ideal [projective measurement](../../../../projective-measurement.md) of the field's [photon number](../../../../photon-number.md) selects the coefficient of $|1\rangle$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{P_1(t)=\frac13\sin^2(\sqrt6gt).}
$$

Whenever this probability is nonzero, normalization of the projected joint [quantum state](../../../../quantum-state.md) gives the atomic [Bell state](../../../../bell-state-split.md)

$$
\boxed{|\psi_{\rm atoms}\rangle=\frac{|eg\rangle+|ge\rangle}{\sqrt2},}
$$

up to a common [global phase](../../../../global-phase.md). The maximum heralding probability is $1/3$, attained at $\sqrt6gt=\pi/2+n\pi$. At a time with $P_1=0$, that conditioning event is impossible, so a conditional state is not defined there. The interpretation is a [photon number](../../../../photon-number.md) measurement at the selected time; continuous monitoring of escaping [photons](../../../../photon.md) would instead change the dynamics and require a different model.
