<h1 id="3/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

At [resonance](../../../../../resonance.md), the [Hamiltonian operator](../../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) becomes $\hbar V\sigma_x/2$. Therefore

$$
U(\tau)=e^{-iV\tau\sigma_x/2}=R_x(V\tau),\qquad
\boxed{\delta=0,\qquad \tau=\frac{\pi}{2V}.}
$$

It gives the requested positive [rotation about the x-axis](../../../../../rotation-about-the-x-axis.md). In the ordered [basis](../../../../../basis.md) $(|1\rangle,|0\rangle)$, $|1\rangle$ becomes $(|1\rangle-i|0\rangle)/\sqrt2$, whose [Bloch vector](../../../../../bloch-vector.md) is $(0,-1,0)$.
