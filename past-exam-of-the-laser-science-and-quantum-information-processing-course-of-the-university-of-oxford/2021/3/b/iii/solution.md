<h1 id="3/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

A resonant [Rabi pi pulse](../../../../../rabi-pi-pulse.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\delta=0,\qquad \tau=\frac{\pi}{V},\qquad U(\tau)=-i\sigma_x.}
$$

It swaps $|0\rangle$ and $|1\rangle$ with a common [global phase](../../../../../global-phase.md), implementing the [Pauli gate](../../../../../pauli-gate.md) $X$, or NOT. Its [Bloch sphere](../../../../../bloch-sphere.md) action is a half-turn about the $x$-axis.
