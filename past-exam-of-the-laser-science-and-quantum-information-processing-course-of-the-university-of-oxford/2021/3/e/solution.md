<h1 id="3/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

An ideal instantaneous NOT about $x$ reverses each atom's accumulated [phase](../../../../phase-waves.md) relative to its subsequent free precession. Algebraically, $X\sigma_zX=-\sigma_z$, so for a static [Rabi detuning](../../../../rabi-detuning.md) and $F=e^{i\delta T\sigma_z/4}$,

$$
FXF=X.
$$

The entire [Ramsey interferometry](../../../../ramsey-interferometry.md) sequence is consequently $PXP=-iI$, up to the additional common [global phase](../../../../global-phase.md) if NOT is implemented as $-iX$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\rho_{11}(T)=1\quad\hbox{for every }T.}
$$

This [spin echo](../../../../spin-echo.md) restores the initial population even for the distribution of static [Rabi detunings](../../../../rabi-detuning.md) in part (d). It removes reversible inhomogeneous [dephasing](../../../../dephasing-channel.md); fluctuating [Rabi detuning](../../../../rabi-detuning.md), finite pulse errors and irreversible loss of [quantum coherence](../../../../quantum-coherence-in-a-specified-basis.md) need not refocus. With an opposite final pulse, the return population would instead be zero; this distinguishes that alternative from the sequence actually specified.
