↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Without interaction, the two-[qubit](../../../../qubit.md) [Hamiltonian operator](../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) is $H_a\otimes I+I\otimes H_b$, so its evolution factors as $U_a\otimes U_b$. It maps each [product state](../../../../product-state.md) to another [product state](../../../../product-state.md), and cannot implement an entangling [two-qubit gate](../../../../two-qubit-gate.md) such as a [CNOT gate](../../../../controlled-not-gate.md).

In an [ion trap](../../../../ion-trap.md), the [Coulomb interaction](../../../../coulomb-interaction.md) couples the ions' displacements into collective vibrational [normal modes](../../../../normal-mode.md). The [qubit](../../../../qubit.md) states are internal atomic states; lasers coupling internal excitation to creation or annihilation of a shared vibrational quantum allow that motion to carry conditional information between ions. Thus the physical mediator is a collective motional [normal mode](../../../../normal-mode.md) of the [Coulomb](../../../../coulomb.md)-coupled chain, rather than a direct resonant exchange between isolated internal [qubit](../../../../qubit.md) transitions. Ground-state cooling and resolved motional sidebands support the ideal pulse model used here.
