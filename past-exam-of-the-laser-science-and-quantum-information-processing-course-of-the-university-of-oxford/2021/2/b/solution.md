<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

A [quasi-Fermi level](../../../../quasi-fermi-level.md) is the [chemical potential](../../../../chemical-potential-split.md) parameter of an approximately thermalized carrier population within one [energy band](../../../../energy-band.md). Rapid intraband scattering can establish a [Fermi-Dirac distribution](../../../../fermi-dirac-distribution.md) in each [energy band](../../../../energy-band.md) before slower interband recombination equilibrates their populations. Thus separate $E_{\rm Fc}$ and $E_{\rm Fv}$ describe a pumped [population inversion](../../../../population-inversion.md) even though the entire [semiconductor](../../../../semiconductor-split.md) is far from [thermal equilibrium](../../../../thermal-equilibrium-split.md).

At a common positive [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md), the [Fermi-Dirac distribution](../../../../fermi-dirac-distribution.md) is strictly decreasing in $E-E_F$. Consequently

$$
\begin{aligned}
f_c(E_2)>f_v(E_1)
&\iff E_2-E_{\rm Fc}<E_1-E_{\rm Fv}\\
&\iff \hbar\omega<E_{\rm Fc}-E_{\rm Fv}.
\end{aligned}
$$

The [joint density of states](../../../../joint-density-of-states.md) is nonzero only above the [band gap](../../../../band-gap.md), so

$$
\boxed{E_g<\hbar\omega<E_{\rm Fc}-E_{\rm Fv}.}
$$

In particular the [quasi-Fermi-level splitting](../../../../quasi-fermi-level-splitting.md) must exceed $E_g$. The upper boundary is transparency, where [stimulated emission](../../../../stimulated-emission.md) balances [absorption](../../../../absorption-electromagnetic-radiation.md); the lower boundary has zero [joint density of states](../../../../joint-density-of-states.md) in this ideal three-dimensional parabolic model. The strict occupation comparison uses $T>0$; at exactly zero [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) occupations become step functions, and their supports should be checked explicitly, as in the next part.
