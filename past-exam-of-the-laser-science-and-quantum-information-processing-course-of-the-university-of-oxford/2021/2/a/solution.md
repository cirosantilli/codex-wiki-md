<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In a [direct band gap](../../../../direct-band-gap.md) [semiconductor](../../../../semiconductor-split.md), the small [photon momentum](../../../../photon-momentum.md) makes optical transitions approximately vertical in [wave vector](../../../../wavevector.md). Their [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) is

$$
\mathcal E(k)=E_c(k)-E_v(k)=E_g+\frac{\hbar^2k^2}{2m_r},
\qquad \boxed{\frac1{m_r}=\frac1{m_c^*}+\frac1{m_v^*}}.
$$

The [joint density of states](../../../../joint-density-of-states.md) per unit volume per transition [energy](../../../../energy-split.md), including the supplied spin counting, is

$$
D_J(\mathcal E)=\frac{k^2}{\pi^2}\frac{dk}{d\mathcal E}
=\frac{m_r k}{\pi^2\hbar^2}
=\frac{\sqrt2\,m_r^{3/2}}{\pi^2\hbar^3}\sqrt{\mathcal E-E_g},\qquad \mathcal E>E_g.
$$

For an upper [electron](../../../../electron.md) state of occupation $f_c$ and a lower [electron](../../../../electron.md) state of occupation $f_v$, [stimulated emission](../../../../stimulated-emission.md) has occupation factor $f_c(1-f_v)$; [absorption](../../../../absorption-electromagnetic-radiation.md) has factor $f_v(1-f_c)$. Their difference is exactly $f_c-f_v$. Multiplication by the [joint density of states](../../../../joint-density-of-states.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{g(\omega)\ \propto\ m_r^{3/2}\sqrt{\hbar\omega-E_g}\,[f_c(E_2)-f_v(E_1)].}
$$

The proportionality suppresses the optical transition [matrix element](../../../../matrix-element.md) and other smoothly varying optical prefactors. It assumes parabolic isotropic [energy bands](../../../../energy-band.md), an allowed direct transition, and negligible linewidth and many-body corrections. Here $f_v$ counts [electrons](../../../../electron.md), not [electron holes](../../../../electron-hole.md).
