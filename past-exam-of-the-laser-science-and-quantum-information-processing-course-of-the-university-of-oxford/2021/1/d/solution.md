<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Place the [optical lens](../../../../lens-optics.md) at $z=0$. For a sufficiently broad incident [Gaussian beam](../../../../gaussian-beam.md), the [beam waist](../../../../beam-waist.md) positions approach the [focal lengths](../../../../focal-length.md) frequency by frequency:

$$
z_\omega\simeq f_0+\beta(\omega-\omega_0).
$$

Add the [group delay](../../../../group-delay.md) at the [optical lens](../../../../lens-optics.md) to propagation through [vacuum](../../../../vacuum.md):

$$
\boxed{t_\omega=2\eta(\omega-\omega_0)+\frac{z_\omega}{c}
=\frac{f_0}{c}+\left(2\eta+\frac\beta c\right)(\omega-\omega_0).}
$$

Here an arbitrary common time origin has been omitted. Differentiating both equations with respect to [angular frequency](../../../../angular-frequency.md) yields

$$
\boxed{\frac{v_f}{c}=\frac{\beta}{\beta+2\eta c}
=\left(1+\frac{2\eta c}{\beta}\right)^{-1}.}
$$

For $\beta>0$, define $u=2\eta c/\beta$. The velocity graph in the preceding figure has horizontal asymptote zero and a vertical asymptote at $u=-1$. For $\eta>0$, $0<v_f<c$: the higher-[frequency](../../../../frequency.md) components both enter later and travel farther. At $\eta=0$, $v_f=c$. For $-\beta/(2c)<\eta<0$, $v_f>c$: the farther-focusing components leave earlier, partly compensating their extra travel. At $\eta=-\beta/(2c)$ every component focuses at the same time, so the trajectory is not a finite-velocity function of time. For $\eta<-\beta/(2c)$ the farther positions focus first, giving $v_f<0$; it tends to zero from below as $\eta\to-\infty$.

A [flying focus](../../../../flying-focus.md) is a moving pattern assembled from different portions of an [optical pulse](../../../../optical-pulse.md). Each portion propagates causally at $c$ in this model. The superluminal or backward motion of the focus therefore does not describe a material object or information overtaking [light](../../../../light.md). The calculation assumes negligible additional frequency-dependent [group delay](../../../../group-delay.md) from the [optical lens](../../../../lens-optics.md); any such delay must be included in $\psi$.
