<h1 id="5/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A random [Pauli Z gate](../../../../../pauli-z-gate.md) with equal application and nonapplication probabilities gives

$$
\boxed{\rho'=\tfrac12\rho+\tfrac12Z\rho Z
=\begin{pmatrix}|\alpha|^2&0\\0&|\beta|^2\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

This is exactly the same [dephasing channel](../../../../../dephasing-channel.md) as the unrecorded measurement, for every input [density operator](../../../../../density-matrix-split.md), including one entangled with an auxiliary system. Individual trials differ: conditioned on the classical random choice, the state is the [pure state](../../../../../pure-state.md) $|\psi\rangle$ or $Z|\psi\rangle$, generally neither a computational [basis vector](../../../../../basis-vector.md). Knowledge of that choice permits recovery by the same [Pauli Z gate](../../../../../pauli-z-gate.md); a measurement outcome does not restore an unknown destroyed superposition.
