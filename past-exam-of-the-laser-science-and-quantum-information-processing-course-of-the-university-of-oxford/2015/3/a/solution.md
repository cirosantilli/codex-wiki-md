<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use [mode locking](../../../../mode-locking.md) to phase-lock many [longitudinal cavity modes](../../../../longitudinal-cavity-mode.md); their repeated constructive interference produces short [optical pulses](../../../../optical-pulse.md). A saturable absorber can select this pulsed operation. A $2\,\mathrm{MHz}$ fundamental repetition rate corresponds to a $500\,\mathrm{ns}$ round trip, or an optical round-trip length $c/R\simeq150\,\mathrm m$; a linear air-filled cavity would therefore be about $75\,\mathrm m$ long. A compact alternative is a faster mode-locked oscillator followed by pulse selection and, if required, amplification. Ordinary [Q-switching](../../../../q-switching.md) alone generally does not provide $350\,\mathrm{fs}$ pulses.[Chapter 7, mode locking: phase-related resonator modes and ultrashort pulses](../../../../fundamentals-of-photonics-and-quantum-electronics-lecture-notes-by-franz-x-kartner.md)
