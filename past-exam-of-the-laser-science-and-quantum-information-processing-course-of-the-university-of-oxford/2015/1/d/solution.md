↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

A [double heterostructure laser](../../../../double-heterostructure-laser.md) places a lower-[band gap](../../../../band-gap.md) active layer between wider-[band gap](../../../../band-gap.md) layers with suitable [type-I band alignment](../../../../type-i-band-alignment.md). The [band offsets](../../../../band-offset.md) confine injected [electrons](../../../../electron.md) and [electron holes](../../../../electron-hole.md) in the same small volume, increasing their overlap and reducing escape. Usually the active layer also has larger [refractive index](../../../../refractive-index.md), so the same sandwich forms an optical [waveguide](../../../../waveguide.md). Optical confinement increases the gain-overlap factor $\Gamma$, while carrier confinement lowers the injection needed to reach the required [quasi-Fermi-level splitting](../../../../quasi-fermi-level-splitting.md). These two distinct mechanisms reduce threshold current and heating. A [band gap](../../../../band-gap.md) difference alone does not establish the sign of both confinement barriers; suitable [band offsets](../../../../band-offset.md) are essential.
