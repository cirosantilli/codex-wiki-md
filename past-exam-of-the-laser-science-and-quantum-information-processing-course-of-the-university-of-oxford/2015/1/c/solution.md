<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Adjacent [longitudinal cavity modes](../../../../longitudinal-cavity-mode.md) obey $2nL=m\lambda_0$. Neglecting [optical dispersion](../../../../dispersion-optics.md) in estimating their spacing,

$$
\Delta\nu=\frac{c}{2nL}=8.33\times10^{10}\,\mathrm{Hz},\qquad
\Delta\lambda_0\simeq\frac{\lambda_0^2}{2nL}=0.1778\,\mathrm{nm}.
$$

Hence the gain interval accommodates

$$
\boxed{N\simeq\frac{20}{0.1778}=112.5\simeq113}
$$

possible [longitudinal cavity modes](../../../../longitudinal-cavity-mode.md). The exact integer depends on the positions of the interval endpoints and resonances. This counts available resonances; mode competition and actual [laser threshold](../../../../laser-threshold.md) can reduce the number that oscillates. More accurately, the spacing uses the [group refractive index](../../../../group-refractive-index.md) instead of $n$.

A broad multimode spectrum suffers differential delay through [optical fiber](../../../../optical-fiber.md) [group velocity dispersion](../../../../group-velocity-dispersion.md), broadening transmitted pulses. Near $800\,\mathrm{nm}$, ordinary silica [optical fiber attenuation](../../../../optical-fiber-attenuation.md) is also larger than in the long-distance windows near $1.3$ and $1.55\,\mu\mathrm m$. These limitations concern this short-wavelength, simple multimode device: suitably engineered single-frequency [semiconductor lasers](../../../../semiconductor-laser.md) at telecommunications [wavelengths](../../../../wavelength.md) are useful communication sources.
