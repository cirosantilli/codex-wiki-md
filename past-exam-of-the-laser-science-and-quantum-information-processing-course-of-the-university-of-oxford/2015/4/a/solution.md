<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For bulk electric-dipole [second-harmonic generation](../../../../second-harmonic-generation.md), the [crystal](../../../../crystal.md) must lack inversion [symmetry](../../../../symmetry-physics.md) and have a nonzero relevant component of its second-order [nonlinear optical susceptibility](../../../../nonlinear-optical-susceptibility.md). It should transmit both pump and harmonic, tolerate the required intensity, and allow [phase matching](../../../../phase-matching.md) by suitable [birefringence](../../../../birefringence.md) or another phase-compensation mechanism. A large nonlinear coefficient, low optical loss and good optical quality are desirable. Noncentrosymmetry permits a response but does not guarantee one in every chosen geometry.

In the undepleted [plane wave](../../../../plane-wave.md) limit, contributions from successive layers give a harmonic amplitude proportional to $\int_0^L e^{i\Delta k z}dz$, where $\Delta k=k_{2\omega}-2k_\omega$. Hence

$$
I_{2\omega}\propto d_{\rm eff}^2 I_\omega^2L^2\operatorname{sinc}^2(\Delta kL/2).
$$

Tune orientation, [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) and pump [polarization](../../../../polarization-waves.md) to maximize the nonlinear coupling and cancel [phase mismatch](../../../../phase-mismatch.md). For continuous operation, an impedance-matched enhancement [optical resonator](../../../../optical-cavity.md), or intracavity doubling, raises the circulating intensity. Focus the [Gaussian beam](../../../../gaussian-beam.md) to balance intensity against [diffraction](../../../../diffraction.md) and [spatial walk-off](../../../../spatial-walk-off.md); arbitrarily tight focusing shortens the useful overlap. Antireflection surfaces, stable resonance locking and efficient heat removal reduce avoidable loss and drift.
