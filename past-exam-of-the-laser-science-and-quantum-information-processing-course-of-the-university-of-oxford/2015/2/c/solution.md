<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [photon number](../../../../photon-number.md) probabilities of a [coherent state](../../../../coherent-state.md) form a [Poisson distribution](../../../../poisson-distribution.md) with mean $\mu=|\alpha|^2$. Alternatively normal ordering gives $\langle\hat n(\hat n-1)\rangle=\langle\hat a^{\dagger2}\hat a^2\rangle=|\alpha|^4$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\langle\hat n\rangle=|\alpha|^2,\qquad
\langle\hat n^2\rangle=|\alpha|^4+|\alpha|^2,\qquad
\Delta n=|\alpha|,\qquad
\frac{\Delta n}{\langle\hat n\rangle}=\frac1{|\alpha|}.}
$$

The last ratio is defined only for nonzero $\alpha$. At large $|\alpha|$ the absolute fluctuation grows but its size relative to the mean becomes negligible. This is the deterministic intensity limit within [quantum optics](../../../../quantum-optics-split.md); it does not assert the disappearance of vacuum-scale quadrature noise.
