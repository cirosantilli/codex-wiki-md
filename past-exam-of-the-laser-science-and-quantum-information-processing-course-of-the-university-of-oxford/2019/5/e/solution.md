<h1 id="5/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

As a single trapped-ion chain grows, it has more collective vibrational modes with increasingly crowded frequencies. A drive intended to address one [motional sideband](../../../../motional-sideband.md) can excite nearby modes, leaving unwanted spin-motion entanglement and crosstalk. Resolving the modes requires longer or more carefully shaped gates, while heating and [frequency](../../../../frequency.md) drift become more consequential. Thus the shared motional bus that is simple for a few [ions](../../../../ion.md) becomes difficult to isolate and return cleanly to its initial state for large registers. This is a control and scaling limitation, rather than a failure of [qubits](../../../../qubit.md) or gates to exist.
