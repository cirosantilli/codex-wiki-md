<h1 id="5/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

In a small [trapped-ion quantum computer](../../../../trapped-ion-quantum-computer.md), use two long-lived internal states of each [ion](../../../../ion.md), for example a pair of [hyperfine](../../../../hyperfine-structure.md) ground states or a narrow optical transition, as $|0\rangle,|1\rangle$. Electric trapping fields confine a short [ion](../../../../ion.md) string, [laser cooling](../../../../laser-cooling.md) reduces its motion, and [optical pumping](../../../../optical-pumping.md) initializes the internal states. State-dependent [fluorescence](../../../../fluorescence-split.md) supplies readout: one [qubit](../../../../qubit.md) level participates in a bright cycling transition and the other is dark.

A resonant microwave, Raman or optical drive performs a single-qubit [Rabi rotation](../../../../rabi-cycle.md). The drive phase fixes a rotation axis in the Bloch equatorial plane and its pulse area fixes the angle; detuning or phase-frame control supplies Z rotations. Individual spatial or [frequency](../../../../frequency.md) addressing selects the intended [ion](../../../../ion.md).

For a two-qubit gate, couple internal states to shared quantized vibrational modes. Resolved [motional sidebands](../../../../motional-sideband.md) let one [ion](../../../../ion.md)'s spin influence the collective oscillator and then the second [ion](../../../../ion.md)'s spin. For example, bichromatic red- and blue-sideband drives produce a spin-dependent force; a closed motional phase-space trajectory returns the oscillator to its initial state while accumulating an [entangling](../../../../entangled-state.md) interaction proportional to $X_iX_j$. Together with single-qubit rotations this implements a [CNOT](../../../../controlled-not-gate.md). The short chain has well-separated [normal-mode frequencies](../../../../normal-mode.md), and [sideband cooling](../../../../resolved-sideband-cooling.md) and the [Lamb-Dicke regime](../../../../lamb-dicke-regime.md) suppress unwanted motion and make coherent bus control practical.
