↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

There are four one-bit [Boolean functions](../../../../../boolean-function.md), fully specified by the ordered pair $(f(0),f(1))$:

- $(0,0)$: $f(x)=0$, constant.
- $(1,1)$: $f(x)=1$, constant.
- $(0,1)$: $f(x)=x$, balanced.
- $(1,0)$: $f(x)=1\oplus x$, balanced.

Writing $a=f(0)$ and $b=f(0)\oplus f(1)$ gives $f(x)=a\oplus bx$. Constant functions have $b=0$ and balanced functions have $b=1$.
