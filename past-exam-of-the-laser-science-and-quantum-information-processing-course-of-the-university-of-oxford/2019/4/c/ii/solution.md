<h1 id="4/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Since $F=1-2p(1-p)$ with $0\leq p\leq1$, its maximum occurs at $p=0$ or $p=1$:

$$
\boxed{F_{\max}=1,\qquad |\psi\rangle=|0\rangle\ \text{or}\ |1\rangle}
$$

up to a [global phase](../../../../../global-phase.md). These are the [eigenstates](../../../../../eigenstate.md) of the measured observable, so their ideal [measurement](../../../../../quantum-measurement-split.md) does not disturb them.
