<h1 id="4/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use the [fidelity](../../../../../fidelity-of-quantum-states.md) between the original [pure state](../../../../../pure-state.md) and the [measurement](../../../../../quantum-measurement-split.md) output averaged over Calvin's results. Put $p=|\alpha|^2$, so $1-p=|\beta|^2$. The output is $\rho_Z=\operatorname{diag}(p,1-p)$, and

$$
\boxed{F=\langle\psi|\rho_Z|\psi\rangle
=p^2+(1-p)^2=\frac{1+r_z^2}{2}.}
$$

Here $r_z=2p-1$ is the input Bloch component. If a particular result is specified instead, the conditional fidelities are $F_0=p$ and $F_1=1-p$; their outcome-weighted mean is precisely the displayed $F$. This distinction fixes what is meant by the [fidelity](../../../../../fidelity-of-quantum-states.md) of the [measurement](../../../../../quantum-measurement-split.md) procedure.
