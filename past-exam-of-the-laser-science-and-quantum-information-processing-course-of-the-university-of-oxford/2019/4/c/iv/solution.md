<h1 id="4/c/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

For a uniformly distributed pure [qubit](../../../../../qubit.md) on the [Bloch sphere](../../../../../bloch-sphere.md), rotational symmetry gives $\langle r_z^2\rangle=1/3$. Thus

$$
\boxed{\overline F=\frac12\left(1+\frac13\right)=\frac23.}
$$

Equivalently, $p=|\alpha|^2$ is uniform on $[0,1]$ under this measure and $\int_0^1[p^2+(1-p)^2]dp=2/3$. The three positive coordinate-axis states mentioned in the source give $F_Z=1$, $F_X=F_Y=1/2$, whose average is the same because this [fidelity](../../../../../fidelity-of-quantum-states.md) depends only on the quadratic component $r_z^2$. This is a specified uniform-state average, not a statement about every possible prior ensemble.
