<h1 id="4/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Complete the square: $F=2(p-1/2)^2+1/2$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{F_{\min}=\frac12,\qquad
|\psi\rangle=\frac{|0\rangle+e^{i\varphi}|1\rangle}{\sqrt2}.}
$$

Every equatorial Bloch-sphere state attains the minimum, not just $|+\rangle$ and $|-\rangle$. Its unread Z [measurement](../../../../../quantum-measurement-split.md) gives $I/2$, having removed all information contained in its [relative phase](../../../../../relative-quantum-phase.md).
