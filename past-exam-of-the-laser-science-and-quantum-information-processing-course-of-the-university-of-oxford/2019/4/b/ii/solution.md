<h1 id="4/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Bob must discard both results. An unread X [measurement](../../../../../quantum-measurement-split.md) gives $\rho_X=p_+|+\rangle\langle+|+p_-|-\rangle\langle-|$. Each of these two X [eigenstates](../../../../../eigenstate.md) gives Z outcomes with [probabilities](../../../../../probability.md) $1/2,1/2$. The final unread Z [measurement](../../../../../quantum-measurement-split.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\rho_{B,\rm final}=\frac12|0\rangle\langle0|+\frac12|1\rangle\langle1|=I/2.}
$$

Equivalently, in the [Bloch-vector](../../../../../bloch-vector.md) description the first [measurement](../../../../../quantum-measurement-split.md) retains only the X component, and the second retains only the Z component, leaving none. The final state is independent of the original $\alpha,\beta$.
