<h1 id="4/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Alice conditions on her observed [computational-basis](../../../../../computational-basis.md) result. For outcome $0$, occurring with [probability](../../../../../probability.md) $|\alpha|^2$, her post-measurement [density matrix](../../../../../density-matrix-split.md) is $|0\rangle\langle0|$; for outcome $1$, occurring with [probability](../../../../../probability.md) $|\beta|^2$, it is $|1\rangle\langle1|$. Explicitly,

$$
\boxed{\rho_{A\mid0}=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\rho_{A\mid1}=\begin{pmatrix}0&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

These are the conditional [Lüders rule](../../../../../luders-rule.md) updates for the two rank-one [projectors](../../../../../projection-linear-algebra.md). They assume the corresponding outcome has nonzero [probability](../../../../../probability.md) and Alice knows which one occurred.
