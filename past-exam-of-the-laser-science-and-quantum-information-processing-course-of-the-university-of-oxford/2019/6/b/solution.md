<h1 id="6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In ZZZ order, the only nonzero amplitudes are those of HHH and VVV. Thus

$$
\boxed{P(HHH)=P(VVV)=\frac12,\qquad
P(HHV)=P(HVH)=P(VHH)=P(HVV)=P(VHV)=P(VVH)=0.}
$$

For an equatorial outcome $a=\pm1$, use the X and Y [eigenvectors](../../../../eigenvector.md) in [a](../a.md). For a product basis containing $k$ Y [measurements](../../../../quantum-measurement-split.md), the bra acting on VVV contributes $(-i)^kabc/\sqrt8$, while that acting on HHH contributes $1/\sqrt8$. Hence the amplitude of outcome $(a,b,c)$ in the supplied state is

$$
\mathcal A_k(a,b,c)=\frac14[1+(-i)^{k+1}abc].
$$

Each of XXY, XYX and YXX has $k=1$, giving $\mathcal A=(1-abc)/4$. In each of the three respective product bases,

$$
\boxed{|\mathrm{GHZ}\rangle=\frac12\left(
|++-\rangle+|+-+\rangle+|-++\rangle+|---\rangle\right).}
$$

The [eigenvector](../../../../eigenvector.md) in each position belongs to the observable named in that position. All four outcomes with product $abc=-1$ have [probability](../../../../probability.md) $1/4$; the four with $abc=+1$ have [probability](../../../../probability.md) zero. This also specifies all eight [probabilities](../../../../probability.md) in all three bases, and all single-site equatorial marginals are unbiased.
