<h1 id="1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The intended quantity is $\boxed{N_{\rm th}^*}$, the threshold [population inversion](../../../../../population-inversion.md) density of the [cavity](../../../../../optical-cavity.md) after switching to low loss. At this value stimulated gain exactly balances the photon-loss rate:

$$
\left.\frac{dn}{dt}\right|_{N^*=N_{\rm th}^*}=0.
$$

Above threshold a nonzero seed grows; below threshold it decays. The unstarred $N_{\rm th}$ in the subpart is shorthand for the starred threshold quantity in the displayed equations, rather than a different total atomic population. The [photon lifetime](../../../../../photon-lifetime.md) $\tau_c$ fixes the [photon-density](../../../../../photon-number-density.md) loss rate $n/\tau_c$.
