<h1 id="1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Write $N=N^*$, $N_i=N_i^*$ and $N_f=N_f^*$ during this derivation. Eliminating the stimulated term between the two [laser rate equations](../../../../../laser-rate-equation.md) gives the [photon](../../../../../photon.md) balance

$$
\dot n=-\frac{f_c}{\beta}\dot N-\frac n{\tau_c}.
$$

Integrate over the pulse, taking negligible initial and final stored [photon](../../../../../photon.md) densities:

$$
\int_0^\infty\frac{n(t)}{\tau_c}\,dt
=\frac{f_c}{\beta}(N_i-N_f).
$$

The [photons](../../../../../photon.md) lost from the [cavity](../../../../../optical-cavity.md) carry total [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) $\hbar\omega_LV_c\int n/\tau_c\,dt$. Since $f_cV_c=V_g$,

$$
\boxed{E=\frac{(N_i^*-N_f^*)V_g\hbar\omega_L}{\beta}
=\eta N_i^*V_g\hbar\omega_L.}
$$

This identifies the supplied [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) formula under the ideal extraction convention that all [photon](../../../../../photon.md) loss is useful output. If only a fraction $\zeta=\kappa_{\rm out}/\kappa_{\rm total}$ of the loss exits the output coupler, the measured output [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) is $\zeta E$. The rate equations by themselves do not distinguish useful output from parasitic loss.
