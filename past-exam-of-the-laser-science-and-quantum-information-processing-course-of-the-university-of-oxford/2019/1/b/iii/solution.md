↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

The [Q-switched energy extraction efficiency](../../../../../q-switched-energy-extraction-efficiency.md) in the ideal loss convention of [ii](../ii.md) is

$$
\boxed{\eta=\frac1\beta\left(1-\frac{N_f^*}{N_i^*}\right),\qquad
\eta_{\max}=\frac1\beta.}
$$

The first factor reflects the definition of inversion: one stimulated transition reduces the upper population and increases the lower population, changing $N^*=N_2-(g_2/g_1)N_1$ by $1+g_2/g_1=\beta$. Thus one emitted [photon](../../../../../photon.md) consumes $\beta$ units of inversion. The second factor accounts for the inversion left after the pulse; finite pumping above threshold does not generally extract all the initially stored inversion. This $\eta$ is not the wall-plug efficiency and does not include the [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) cost of pumping or the factor $\zeta$ for imperfect output extraction.
