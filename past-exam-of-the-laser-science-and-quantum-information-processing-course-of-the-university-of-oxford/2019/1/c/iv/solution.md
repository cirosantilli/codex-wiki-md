<h1 id="1/c/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Extracting 95 percent of the maximum available inversion [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) means $\eta=0.95/\beta$, or $x=\beta\eta=0.95$. Substitution in [ii](../ii.md) gives

$$
\boxed{r=\frac{-\ln(0.05)}{0.95}=3.1534\ldots.}
$$

The degeneracy factor cancels because the target is a fraction of the maximum [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) $N_i^*V_g\hbar\omega_L/\beta$, rather than a fraction of $N_i^*V_g\hbar\omega_L$ without that factor.
