<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

There is at most one field [photon](../../../../photon.md) in [b](../b.md). Projection onto its one-photon sector gives

$$
\boxed{P(n=1;t)=\sin^2(gt),\qquad
\rho_{\rm atom\mid n=1}=|g\rangle\langle g|.}
$$

Before detector absorption, the normalized conditional joint state is $|g,1\rangle$ up to an overall phase. A destructive [photon](../../../../photon.md) count changes the field to its [vacuum](../../../../vacuum-state.md) but leaves the [atom](../../../../atom-physics.md) in $|g\rangle$. This conditional state is meaningful whenever the one-photon outcome has nonzero [probability](../../../../probability.md).
