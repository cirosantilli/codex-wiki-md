<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Conservation of $\mathcal N$ confines evolution from $|e,0\rangle$ to $|e,0\rangle,|g,1\rangle$. On this subspace,

$$
H=\hbar\omega I+\hbar g\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The interaction [matrix](../../../../matrix.md) squares to the identity, so its exponential separates into cosine and sine terms. The state is

$$
\boxed{|\Psi(t)\rangle=e^{-i\omega t}
\left[\cos(gt)|e,0\rangle-i\sin(gt)|g,1\rangle\right].}
$$

The overall phase can be discarded or removed by the [interaction picture](../../../../interaction-picture.md). The [relative phase](../../../../relative-quantum-phase.md) $-i$ follows from [Schrödinger evolution](../../../../schrodinger-equation.md) with the positive coupling in [a](../a.md). The [atom](../../../../atom-physics.md) and field are generally [entangled](../../../../entangled-state.md); complete excitation transfer occurs at $gt=\pi/2$.
