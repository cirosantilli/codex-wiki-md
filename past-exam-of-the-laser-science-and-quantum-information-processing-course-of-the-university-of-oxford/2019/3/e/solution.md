<h1 id="3/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Discarding the [photon-number](../../../../photon-number.md) result gives a classical mixture of the two outcome branches. The atomic state, in the basis $(|e\rangle,|g\rangle)$, is

$$
\boxed{\rho_A(t)=\cos^2(gt)|e\rangle\langle e|
+\sin^2(gt)|g\rangle\langle g|
=\begin{pmatrix}\cos^2(gt)&0\\0&\sin^2(gt)\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

It is also the reduced state before measuring the field; a nonselective operation on the field does not change the atomic [reduced density matrix](../../../../reduced-density-matrix.md). The [atom](../../../../atom-physics.md) is not in a [coherent superposition](../../../../quantum-superposition.md) with these populations.

For an atomic [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) gap $\hbar\omega$, the thermal population ratio is $p_e/p_g=e^{-\hbar\omega/(k_BT)}$. Matching it gives

$$
\boxed{T_{\rm eff}(t)=\frac{\hbar\omega}{k_B\ln[\tan^2(gt)]}.}
$$

A finite positive [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) exists when $0<p_e<p_g$, with $T\to0^+$ at $p_e=0$ and $T\to+\infty$ as $p_e\to1/2$ from below. Equal populations correspond to zero inverse [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md). If $p_e>1/2$, the expression is a negative effective [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md) for a bounded [two-level system](../../../../two-level-system.md), not an equilibrium state at any ordinary positive [temperature](../../../../temperature-split.md). For a concrete counterexample to an unrestricted positive-temperature interpretation, at $t=0$ the [atom](../../../../atom-physics.md) is certainly excited, whereas every positive-temperature [Gibbs distribution](../../../../quantum-gibbs-state.md) has $p_e\leq1/2$. The fully excited endpoint is the formal limit $T\to0^-$. Matching one [density matrix](../../../../density-matrix-split.md) does not establish genuine [thermalization](../../../../thermalization.md) with a bath.
