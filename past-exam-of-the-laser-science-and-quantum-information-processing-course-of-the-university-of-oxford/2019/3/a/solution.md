<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Let $|g\rangle,|e\rangle$ be the atomic levels, $\sigma_+=|e\rangle\langle g|$, and $a$ the [annihilation operator](../../../../annihilation-operator.md) of the single field mode. Choose the joint ground [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) to be zero. The resonant [Jaynes-Cummings model](../../../../jaynes-cummings-model.md) is

$$
\boxed{H=\hbar\omega a^\dagger a+\hbar\omega|e\rangle\langle e|
+\hbar g(a\sigma_++a^\dagger\sigma_-).}
$$

The first term counts field [photons](../../../../photon.md), the second the atomic excitation, and the interaction exchanges one [photon](../../../../photon.md) for one atomic excitation. A phase redefinition makes $g$ real and positive. Resonance means $\omega=\omega_{eg}$; the total excitation $\mathcal N=a^\dagger a+|e\rangle\langle e|$ is conserved. Adding an overall constant gives the equivalent convention using $\sigma_z/2$ and a zero-point field term.

The model follows from the [electric-dipole interaction](../../../../electric-dipole-approximation.md) and the [rotating-wave approximation](../../../../rotating-wave-approximation.md), retaining the energy-conserving terms while dropping $a\sigma_-$ and $a^\dagger\sigma_+$. We assume a single quantized monochromatic mode and negligible [cavity](../../../../optical-cavity.md) leakage and atomic relaxation over the time considered. With this convention, the resonant population [Rabi angular frequency](../../../../rabi-frequency.md) in the one-excitation subspace is $2g$.
