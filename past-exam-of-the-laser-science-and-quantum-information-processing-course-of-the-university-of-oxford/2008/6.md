## 1

↑ **Parent:** [6](6.md)

<h3 id="1/solution">Solution</h3>

↑ **Parent:** [1](#1)

Use the ordered [computational basis](../../quantum-theory.md#computational-basis) $(|00\rangle,|01\rangle,|10\rangle,|11\rangle)$, with $Z|0\rangle=|0\rangle$ and $Z|1\rangle=-|1\rangle$. Write $X,Y,Z$ for the [Pauli matrices](../../algebra.md#pauli-matrices), $I$ for the [identity operator](../../vector-space.md#identity-operator), $J=\omega_{12}$, $\Sigma=\omega_1+\omega_2$, and $\Delta=\omega_1-\omega_2$. As in the question, the [Hamiltonian operators](../../quantum-system.md#hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics) have [angular frequency](../../lagrangian-mechanics.md#angular-frequency) units, so the [time-evolution operator](../../quantum-system.md#time-evolution-operator) is $e^{-iHt}$. The [Heisenberg coupling matrix for two spins](../../angular-momentum-operator.md#heisenberg-coupling-matrix-for-two-spins) is

$$
C=X\otimes X+Y\otimes Y+Z\otimes Z
=\begin{pmatrix}
1&0&0&0\\
0&-1&2&0\\
0&2&-1&0\\
0&0&0&1
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Consequently the full [Heisenberg model](../../angular-momentum-operator.md#heisenberg-model) [Hamiltonian operator](../../quantum-system.md#hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics) and its proposed [Ising coupling](../../control-theory.md#ising-coupling-of-two-qubits) approximation are

$$
H_{\mathrm H}=\begin{pmatrix}
(\Sigma+J)/2&0&0&0\\
0&(\Delta-J)/2&J&0\\
0&J&(-\Delta-J)/2&0\\
0&0&0&(-\Sigma+J)/2
\end{pmatrix},\qquad
H_{\mathrm I}=\operatorname{diag}\!\left(
\frac{\Sigma+J}{2},\frac{\Delta-J}{2},
\frac{-\Delta-J}{2},\frac{-\Sigma+J}{2}\right).
$$

For [nondegenerate perturbation theory](../../quantum-mechanics.md#nondegenerate-perturbation-theory), choose $H_{\mathrm I}$ as the unperturbed [Hamiltonian operator](../../quantum-system.md#hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics) and

$$
V=H_{\mathrm H}-H_{\mathrm I}
=J\bigl(|01\rangle\langle10|+|10\rangle\langle01|\bigr)
$$

as the perturbation. Only $|01\rangle$ and $|10\rangle$ are coupled, and their unperturbed [energy levels](../../quantum-mechanics.md#energy-level) differ by $\Delta$. All first-order [energy](../../energy.md) corrections vanish. The first-order [eigenvectors](../../linear-operator-theory.md#eigenvector) and second-order [energy](../../energy.md) corrections are

$$
|\widetilde{01}\rangle=|01\rangle+\frac{J}{\Delta}|10\rangle
+O\!\left(\frac{J^2}{\Delta^2}\right),\qquad
|\widetilde{10}\rangle=|10\rangle-\frac{J}{\Delta}|01\rangle
+O\!\left(\frac{J^2}{\Delta^2}\right),
$$



$$
\delta E_{01}^{(2)}=\frac{J^2}{\Delta},\qquad
\delta E_{10}^{(2)}=-\frac{J^2}{\Delta}.
$$

The states $|00\rangle,|11\rangle$ are exact [eigenvectors](../../linear-operator-theory.md#eigenvector) throughout. Hence the [weak coupling condition for two NMR spins](../../nuclear-magnetic-resonance.md#weak-coupling-condition-for-two-nmr-spins) is

$$
\boxed{|\omega_1-\omega_2|\gg |\omega_{12}|.}
$$

A large common [Zeeman energy](../../electromagnetism.md#zeeman-energy) alone does not ensure this condition. Crossings involving the uncoupled parallel-spin states do not invalidate the argument: the relevant denominator is the separation of the two states connected by $V$.

As an exact check, the middle $2\times2$ [matrix](../../vector-space.md#matrix) has [eigenvalues](../../linear-operator-theory.md#eigenvalue)

$$
E_\pm=-\frac J2\pm\sqrt{\frac{\Delta^2}{4}+J^2}.
$$

For $\Delta\ne0$, labeling by the limiting [computational basis](../../quantum-theory.md#computational-basis) states reproduces the shifts above. The [quantum transition probability](../../wave-function.md#quantum-transition-probability) from $|01\rangle$ to $|10\rangle$ is

$$
P_{01\to10}(t)=\frac{4J^2}{\Delta^2+4J^2}
\sin^2\!\left(\frac{t}{2}\sqrt{\Delta^2+4J^2}\right).
$$

Thus unwanted exchange has a uniformly small amplitude when $|J/\Delta|\ll1$. If accumulated phases must also be approximated over a duration $t$, neglecting the second-order shifts additionally requires $J^2t/|\Delta|\ll1$, or those shifts must be retained in an improved effective [Hamiltonian operator](../../quantum-system.md#hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics).

The same result is the [secular approximation for scalar-coupled spins](../../nuclear-magnetic-resonance.md#secular-approximation-for-scalar-coupled-spins). In the [interaction picture](../../quantum-system.md#interaction-picture) of the [Zeeman energy](../../electromagnetism.md#zeeman-energy) terms,

$$
V_{\mathrm{int}}(t)=J\left(e^{i\Delta t}|01\rangle\langle10|
+e^{-i\Delta t}|10\rangle\langle01|\right).
$$

These terms average rapidly on the coupling timescale $1/|J|$ when $|\Delta|\gg|J|$, while $JZ\otimes Z/2$ survives.

For fixed nuclei and electronic environments, the individual [NMR](../../nuclear-magnetic-resonance.md) angular frequencies scale with the static [magnetic field](../../magnetic-field.md) $B_0$: schematically $\omega_i=\kappa_i B_0$, where $\kappa_i$ includes the [gyromagnetic ratio](../../electromagnetism.md#gyromagnetic-ratio) and magnetic shielding. The [NMR scalar coupling](../../nuclear-magnetic-resonance.md#nmr-scalar-coupling) $J$ in [angular frequency](../../lagrangian-mechanics.md#angular-frequency) units is approximately independent of $B_0$. Therefore a nonzero separation $|\Delta|=|\kappa_1-\kappa_2|B_0$ increases with [magnetic field](../../magnetic-field.md), improving the approximation. In particular, a fixed difference of [chemical shifts](../../nuclear-magnetic-resonance.md#chemical-shift) gives a larger separation in hertz at higher [magnetic field](../../magnetic-field.md). If the two resonances remain exactly equal, increasing their common frequency does **not** suppress their exchange.

<h3 id="2/solution">Solution</h3>

↑ **Parent:** [2](#2)

Set $\omega_1=\omega_2=\omega$. Define the [quantum rotating frame](../../quantum-system.md#quantum-rotating-frame) by

$$
H_Z=\frac{\omega}{2}(Z\otimes I+I\otimes Z),\qquad
R(t)=e^{-itH_Z},\qquad
|\psi_{\mathrm r}(t)\rangle=R(t)^\dagger|\psi(t)\rangle.
$$

The isotropic [Heisenberg model](../../angular-momentum-operator.md#heisenberg-model) coupling commutes with the total $z$ [spin angular momentum](../../angular-momentum-operator.md#spin), so $[H_Z,C]=0$. The transformed [Hamiltonian operator](../../quantum-system.md#hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics) is therefore

$$
H_{\mathrm r}=R^\dagger H_{\mathrm H}R-iR^\dagger\dot R
=R^\dagger H_{\mathrm H}R-H_Z=\frac J2C.
$$

Here removal of the common [Zeeman energy](../../electromagnetism.md#zeeman-energy) is exact; the exchange is resonant and cannot be discarded using the [weak coupling condition for two NMR spins](../../nuclear-magnetic-resonance.md#weak-coupling-condition-for-two-nmr-spins).

Put $a=Jt$, $c=\cos a$, and $s=\sin a$. Exponentiating the parallel-spin entries and the middle $2\times2$ [matrix](../../vector-space.md#matrix) gives the [time-evolution operator](../../quantum-system.md#time-evolution-operator)

$$
\boxed{U(t)=e^{-itH_{\mathrm r}}=
\begin{pmatrix}
e^{-ia/2}&0&0&0\\
0&e^{ia/2}c&-ie^{ia/2}s&0\\
0&-ie^{ia/2}s&e^{ia/2}c&0\\
0&0&0&e^{-ia/2}
\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

For example, at $a=\pi/2$ this equals the [SWAP gate](../../quantum-theory.md#swap-gate) times $e^{-i\pi/4}$, which checks both the exchange angle and the overall phase.

The printed network reads from left to right as $U$, a [Pauli Z gate](../../quantum-theory.md#pauli-z-gate) on the **upper spin only**, $U$, and another upper-spin [Pauli Z gate](../../quantum-theory.md#pauli-z-gate). Let

$$
P=Z\otimes I=\operatorname{diag}(1,1,-1,-1).
$$

Its total [time-evolution operator](../../quantum-system.md#time-evolution-operator), with the first operation on the right, is $W=PUPU$. For this [Heisenberg exchange echo](../../control-theory.md#heisenberg-exchange-echo),

$$
PH_{\mathrm r}P=\frac J2(-X\otimes X-Y\otimes Y+Z\otimes Z).
$$

The three paired [Pauli operators](../../quantum-circuit.md#pauli-operator) $X\otimes X$, $Y\otimes Y$, $Z\otimes Z$ commute pairwise: for example both products $(X\otimes X)(Y\otimes Y)$ and $(Y\otimes Y)(X\otimes X)$ equal $-Z\otimes Z$. Thus $PH_{\mathrm r}P$ commutes with $H_{\mathrm r}$, and

$$
\boxed{W=e^{-itPH_{\mathrm r}P}e^{-itH_{\mathrm r}}
=e^{-iJtZ\otimes Z}
=\begin{pmatrix}
e^{-ia}&0&0&0\\
0&e^{ia}&0&0\\
0&0&e^{ia}&0\\
0&0&0&e^{-ia}
\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

The [spin echo](../../nuclear-magnetic-resonance.md#spin-echo) cancels the $XX+YY$ exchange terms and retains the [Ising coupling](../../control-theory.md#ising-coupling-of-two-qubits). Equivalently, over the total free-evolution time $2t$ it produces the effective [Hamiltonian operator](../../quantum-system.md#hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics) $JZ\otimes Z/2$ exactly for ideal instantaneous gates. It is generally **not the identity**. A physical $\pi$ rotation about $z$ is $e^{-i\pi Z/2}=-iZ$; using two such pulses instead of the ideal $Z$ gates changes $W$ only by a physically irrelevant [global phase](../../quantum-system.md#global-phase) of minus one.

The implementation requires [frequency-selective spin control](../../control-theory.md#frequency-selective-spin-control). Under the usual common-[radio frequency](../../wave.md#radio-frequency) [NMR](../../nuclear-magnetic-resonance.md) assumptions, equal resonance frequencies give no way to apply a pulse to the first spin while leaving the second untouched. For equally coupled spins in a spatially uniform control field, the transverse control [Hamiltonian operator](../../quantum-system.md#hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics) has the form

$$
H_c(t)=\frac{u_x(t)}2(X\otimes I+I\otimes X)
+\frac{u_y(t)}2(Y\otimes I+I\otimes Y).
$$

Both this control and the drift [Hamiltonian operator](../../quantum-system.md#hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics) commute with the [SWAP gate](../../quantum-theory.md#swap-gate) $S$. Every reachable [time-evolution operator](../../quantum-system.md#time-evolution-operator) consequently commutes with $S$. But $SPS=I\otimes Z\ne P$: the individual pulse required by the diagram fails this symmetry test. This is the [exchange symmetry obstruction to individual spin control](../../control-theory.md#exchange-symmetry-obstruction-to-individual-spin-control). Applying simultaneous $Z$ gates gives $Q=Z\otimes Z$, which commutes with $H_{\mathrm r}$ and instead yields $QUQU=U^2$; it does not refocus the exchange. A common change of the RF phase reference also supplies only a collective rotation.

This obstruction assumes the usual common-RF control with no extra handle distinguishing the spins. Equal resonance frequencies do not universally forbid individual addressing: a spatially selective field, unequal control couplings, or another symmetry-breaking interaction could alter [controllability](../../control-theory.md#controllability). Such resources are absent from the specified equivalent-spin [NMR](../../nuclear-magnetic-resonance.md) model.

<h3 id="3/solution">Solution</h3>

↑ **Parent:** [3](#3)

[Quantum error correction](../../quantum-error-correction.md) encodes a [logical qubit](../../quantum-error-correction.md#logical-qubit) redundantly, obtains an [error syndrome](../../quantum-error-correction.md#error-syndrome) without measuring its unknown logical amplitudes, and applies a recovery that reverses the specified errors. A [decoherence-free subspace](../../quantum-error-correction.md#decoherence-free-subspace) instead uses a symmetry of a specified noise model: each noise operator acts as a scalar on the entire encoded space, so no syndrome-dependent recovery is required. This is passive protection and can also be viewed as a special case of [quantum error correction](../../quantum-error-correction.md).

For the [two-spin decoherence-free encoding](../../quantum-error-correction.md#two-spin-decoherence-free-encoding), choose

$$
|0_L\rangle=|01\rangle,\qquad |1_L\rangle=|10\rangle,\qquad
|\psi_L\rangle=\alpha|01\rangle+\beta|10\rangle,
\qquad |\alpha|^2+|\beta|^2=1.
$$

Simultaneous [Pauli Z gates](../../quantum-theory.md#pauli-z-gate) give

$$
(Z\otimes Z)|01\rangle=-|01\rangle,\qquad
(Z\otimes Z)|10\rangle=-|10\rangle,
$$

so

$$
\boxed{(Z\otimes Z)|\psi_L\rangle=-|\psi_L\rangle.}
$$

Both logical components acquire the same [global phase](../../quantum-system.md#global-phase); their [relative phase](../../quantum-system.md#relative-quantum-phase) and amplitudes survive. The [density matrix](../../density-matrix.md) is exactly unchanged, so even an unknown random choice between applying $I$ and $Z\otimes Z$ causes no logical [dephasing](../../quantum-information-theory.md#dephasing-channel).

More strongly, the encoding protects against arbitrary [collective dephasing](../../quantum-information-theory.md#collective-dephasing):

$$
[R_z(\phi)\otimes R_z(\phi)]|\psi_L\rangle
=e^{-i\phi(Z\otimes I+I\otimes Z)/2}|\psi_L\rangle
=|\psi_L\rangle,
$$

because both basis states have total $Z$ [eigenvalue](../../linear-operator-theory.md#eigenvalue) zero. At $\phi=\pi$, $R_z(\pi)\otimes R_z(\pi)=-Z\otimes Z$, consistent with the previous overall phase. The protection relies on correlated noise: independent $Z$ rotations generally change the logical relative phase, and this two-spin code does not protect a whole [logical qubit](../../quantum-error-correction.md#logical-qubit) against arbitrary collective rotations about all three axes or against arbitrary single-spin errors.
