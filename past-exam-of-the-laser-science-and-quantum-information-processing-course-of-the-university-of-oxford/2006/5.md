## 1

↑ **Parent:** [5](5.md)

<h3 id="1/solution">Solution</h3>

↑ **Parent:** [1](#1)

For one input [bit](../../information-theory.md#bit), a [Boolean function](../../analysis-of-boolean-functions.md#boolean-function) is specified by the pair $(f(0),f(1))$, and $k=f(0)+f(1)$ counts its [satisfying assignments](../../theoretical-computer-science.md#satisfying-assignment). In the ordered [computational basis](../../quantum-theory.md#computational-basis) $(|0\rangle,|1\rangle)$, the supplied [phase oracle](../../theoretical-computer-science.md#phase-oracle) convention gives

$$
U_f|x\rangle=(-1)^{f(x)\oplus1}|x\rangle,\qquad
U_f=\operatorname{diag}\bigl(2f(0)-1,2f(1)-1\bigr).
$$

Here $\oplus$ is [exclusive or](../../theoretical-computer-science.md#exclusive-or): **the oracle changes the sign of the inputs with $f(x)=0$**. With $I$ the [identity matrix](../../vector-space.md#identity-matrix) and $Z$ the [Pauli Z gate](../../quantum-theory.md#pauli-z-gate), all four cases are

$$
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c}
f(x)&(f(0),f(1))&k&U_f\\\hline
0&(0,0)&0&\begin{pmatrix}-1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix}=-I\\[3pt]
x&(0,1)&1&\begin{pmatrix}-1&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix}=-Z\\[3pt]
1-x&(1,0)&1&\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix}=Z\\[3pt]
1&(1,1)&2&\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix}=I
\end{array}
$$

The two middle cases are [balanced Boolean functions](../../analysis-of-boolean-functions.md#balanced-boolean-function); the others are [constant functions](../../function.md#constant-function). The specified zero-state sign change and the [Hadamard gate](../../quantum-theory.md#hadamard-gate) are

$$
\boxed{U_0=\begin{pmatrix}-1&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix}=-Z,\qquad
H=\frac1{\sqrt2}\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

Use the top [qubit](../../quantum-system.md#qubit) as the first [tensor product](../../multilinear-algebra.md#tensor-product) factor. Write the [controlled unitary gate](../../quantum-theory.md#controlled-unitary-gate) as $C(V)=|0\rangle\langle0|\otimes I+|1\rangle\langle1|\otimes V$. Reading the bracketed [quantum circuit](../../quantum-circuit.md) from left to right, its four operations are $C(U_f)$, $C(H)$, $C(U_0)$, $C(H)$. Thus the target operation in its control-one branch is

$$
G=H U_0 H U_f,
\qquad C(G)=C(H)C(U_0)C(H)C(U_f),
$$

where the rightmost [matrix](../../vector-space.md#matrix) acts first. The control-zero branch is the [identity operator](../../vector-space.md#identity-operator). The [Hadamard conjugation of a controlled gate](../../quantum-theory.md#hadamard-conjugation-of-a-controlled-gate) identity gives

$$
\boxed{C(H)C(U_0)C(H)=(I\otimes H)C(U_0)(I\otimes H).}
$$

Indeed, on control zero the replacement applies $H I H=I$ to the target, while on control one both sides apply $H U_0 H$. By [linearity](../../vector-space.md#linearity), the identity holds on every [quantum superposition](../../quantum-system.md#quantum-superposition) and therefore in every repetition of the bracket. This replaces the two internal controlled [Hadamard gates](../../quantum-theory.md#hadamard-gate) as a pair.

The [global phase of a controlled quantum gate](../../quantum-theory.md#global-phase-of-a-controlled-quantum-gate) must be preserved: $C(-I)=Z\otimes I$, whereas $C(I)=I\otimes I$. Consequently the minus signs in $U_0=-Z$ and the special [phase oracles](../../theoretical-computer-science.md#phase-oracle) $U_f=-I,-Z$ cannot be discarded as an unobservable [global phase](../../quantum-system.md#global-phase) of the complete [quantum circuit](../../quantum-circuit.md).

## 2

↑ **Parent:** [5](5.md)

<h3 id="2/solution">Solution</h3>

↑ **Parent:** [2](#2)

Define the [Hadamard basis](../../quantum-theory.md#hadamard-basis) states $|\pm\rangle=(|0\rangle\pm|1\rangle)/\sqrt2$. The initial two [Hadamard gates](../../quantum-theory.md#hadamard-gate) prepare $|+\rangle|+\rangle$. For one bracket, the two control branches give

$$
|+\rangle|+\rangle\ \longmapsto
\frac{|0\rangle|+\rangle+|1\rangle G|+\rangle}{\sqrt2}.
$$

Using the [Pauli X gate](../../quantum-theory.md#pauli-x-gate) $X=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$, [unitary conjugation](../../vector-space.md#unitary-conjugation) by the [Hadamard gate](../../quantum-theory.md#hadamard-gate) gives $H U_0 H=-X$, so $G=-XU_f$. Keeping every sign, the target states along the control-one branch, in the exact gate order, are

$$
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c|c|c}
f&U_f|+\rangle&H U_f|+\rangle&U_0 H U_f|+\rangle&G|+\rangle&H G|+\rangle\\\hline
0&-|+\rangle&-|0\rangle&|0\rangle&|+\rangle&|0\rangle\\
x&-| -\rangle&-|1\rangle&-|1\rangle&-| -\rangle&-|1\rangle\\
1-x&| -\rangle&|1\rangle&|1\rangle&| -\rangle&|1\rangle\\
1&|+\rangle&|0\rangle&-|0\rangle&-|+\rangle&-|0\rangle
\end{array}
$$

The last column also includes the final target [Hadamard gate](../../quantum-theory.md#hadamard-gate), which lies outside the bracket. Writing $|\chi_f\rangle=HG|+\rangle$, applying the final top [Hadamard gate](../../quantum-theory.md#hadamard-gate) gives the exact normalized two-[qubit](../../quantum-system.md#qubit) [quantum state](../../quantum-system.md#quantum-state)

$$
|\Psi_f\rangle=\frac12\left[|0\rangle\bigl(|0\rangle+|\chi_f\rangle\bigr)+|1\rangle\bigl(|0\rangle-|\chi_f\rangle\bigr)\right].
$$

Thus the complete outputs, in top-then-bottom [computational basis](../../quantum-theory.md#computational-basis) order, are

$$
\boxed{\begin{array}{c|c|c}
f&|\Psi_f\rangle&\langle Z\otimes I\rangle\\\hline
0&|00\rangle&+1\\[2pt]
x&\tfrac12\bigl(|00\rangle-|01\rangle+|10\rangle+|11\rangle\bigr)&0\\[2pt]
1-x&\tfrac12\bigl(|00\rangle+|01\rangle+|10\rangle-|11\rangle\bigr)&0\\[2pt]
1&|10\rangle&-1
\end{array}}
$$

For the first [qubit](../../quantum-system.md#qubit), the [Pauli matrix](../../algebra.md#pauli-matrices) $\sigma_z=Z$ has [expectation value](../../quantum-system.md#expectation-value) $P(0)-P(1)$ by the [Born rule](../../wave-function.md#born-rule). The two $k=1$ cases each have $P(0)=P(1)=1/2$, while the $k=0$ and $k=2$ cases are deterministic. Equivalently, this [Hadamard test](../../quantum-theory.md#hadamard-test) measures

$$
\langle Z\otimes I\rangle=\operatorname{Re}\langle+|G|+\rangle=1-k
\qquad(r=1,\ n=1).
$$

This explains the [approximate quantum counting](../../algorithm.md#approximate-quantum-counting) interpretation: repeated preparations estimate the [expectation value](../../quantum-system.md#expectation-value), and hence $k$. A single top-[qubit](../../quantum-system.md#qubit) [computational-basis measurement](../../quantum-theory.md#quantum-measurement-in-the-computational-basis) need not identify $k$.

## 3

↑ **Parent:** [5](5.md)

<h3 id="3/solution">Solution</h3>

↑ **Parent:** [3](#3)

For $r$ brackets the control-zero branch still does nothing, and the control-one branch applies $G^r$ to the target. Immediately before the final two [Hadamard gates](../../quantum-theory.md#hadamard-gate), the [quantum state](../../quantum-system.md#quantum-state) is

$$
\frac{|0\rangle|+\rangle+|1\rangle G^r|+\rangle}{\sqrt2}.
$$

For the [constant function](../../function.md#constant-function) with $k=0$, the [phase oracle](../../theoretical-computer-science.md#phase-oracle) is $U_f=-I$, so $G=(-X)(-I)=X$. Since $|+\rangle$ is an [eigenstate](../../energy-eigenvalue.md#eigenstate) of the [Pauli X gate](../../quantum-theory.md#pauli-x-gate) with [eigenvalue](../../linear-operator-theory.md#eigenvalue) $+1$, $G^r|+\rangle=|+\rangle$ for every integer $r$. The final two [Hadamard gates](../../quantum-theory.md#hadamard-gate) therefore give

$$
\boxed{|\Psi_{k=0}(r)\rangle=|00\rangle,\qquad \langle Z\otimes I\rangle=+1.}
$$

In particular, the first [qubit](../../quantum-system.md#qubit) always ends in $|0\rangle$.

For the [constant function](../../function.md#constant-function) with $k=2$, $U_f=I$ and $G=-X$, so $G^r|+\rangle=(-1)^r|+\rangle$. Thus the target remains in $|+\rangle$, and the top [qubit](../../quantum-system.md#qubit) before its final [Hadamard gate](../../quantum-theory.md#hadamard-gate) is $(|0\rangle+(-1)^r|1\rangle)/\sqrt2$. The sign is a [relative quantum phase](../../quantum-system.md#relative-quantum-phase) between the control branches. The [Hadamard gate](../../quantum-theory.md#hadamard-gate) converts it into the [integer parity](../../integer.md#parity-mathematics) of $r$:

$$
\boxed{|\Psi_{k=2}(r)\rangle=|r\bmod2\rangle\otimes|0\rangle,\qquad
\langle Z\otimes I\rangle=(-1)^r.}
$$

The first [qubit](../../quantum-system.md#qubit) is therefore $|0\rangle$ for even $r$ and $|1\rangle$ for odd $r$, including all requested $r>1$.
