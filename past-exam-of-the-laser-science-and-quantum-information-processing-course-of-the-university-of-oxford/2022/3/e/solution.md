<h1 id="3/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

[Zero-point fields](../../../../vacuum-fluctuation.md) are the irreducible [electromagnetic field](../../../../electromagnetic-field.md) fluctuations in the [vacuum state](../../../../vacuum-state.md). Each [normal mode](../../../../normal-mode.md) has ground [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) $\hbar\omega/2$. Although $\langle\mathcal E\rangle=\langle\mathcal B\rangle=0$ in the [vacuum state](../../../../vacuum-state.md), [d](../d.md) gives $\langle\mathcal E^2\rangle=\langle\mathcal B^2\rangle=1$. These are quantum fluctuations, not a classical random coherent field with a known instantaneous value.

Two consequences are the [Lamb shift](../../../../lamb-shift.md), where coupling to the quantized [electromagnetic field](../../../../electromagnetic-field.md) changes atomic [energy levels](../../../../energy-level.md), and [spontaneous emission](../../../../spontaneous-emission.md), where an excited [chemical atom](../../../../atom-physics.md) can emit a [photon](../../../../photon.md) into an initially empty [normal mode](../../../../normal-mode.md). In the latter process the [creation operator](../../../../creation-operator.md) has a nonzero [matrix element](../../../../matrix-element.md) $\langle1|a^\dagger|0\rangle=1$; stimulated-plus-spontaneous factors have the form $n+1$, which does not vanish at $n=0$. Descriptions splitting [spontaneous emission](../../../../spontaneous-emission.md) into [vacuum fluctuations](../../../../vacuum-fluctuation.md) and radiation reaction depend on operator-ordering conventions; the measurable total rate does not. [Zero-point fields](../../../../vacuum-fluctuation.md) do not mean that the [vacuum state](../../../../vacuum-state.md) contains a nonzero mean [photon number](../../../../photon-number.md).
