<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For one electromagnetic [normal mode](../../../../normal-mode.md), $a$ and $a^\dagger$ are the [annihilation operator](../../../../annihilation-operator.md) and [creation operator](../../../../creation-operator.md), with [commutator](../../../../commutator.md) $[a,a^\dagger]=I$. Their action on normalized [number states](../../../../number-state.md) is

$$
a|n\rangle=\sqrt n\,|n-1\rangle,\qquad
a^\dagger|n\rangle=\sqrt{n+1}\,|n+1\rangle.
$$

They remove or add one [photon](../../../../photon.md), whose [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) is $\hbar\omega$. The [vacuum state](../../../../vacuum-state.md) $|0\rangle$ is normalized and obeys $a|0\rangle=0$. It has no [photons](../../../../photon.md), but retains [zero-point energy](../../../../zero-point-energy.md) $\hbar\omega/2$ for the [quantum harmonic oscillator](../../../../quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md) [Hamiltonian](../../../../hamiltonian.md) $H=\hbar\omega(a^\dagger a+1/2)$. The [coherent state](../../../../coherent-state.md) $|\alpha\rangle=D(\alpha)|0\rangle$ is a displaced [vacuum state](../../../../vacuum-state.md).
