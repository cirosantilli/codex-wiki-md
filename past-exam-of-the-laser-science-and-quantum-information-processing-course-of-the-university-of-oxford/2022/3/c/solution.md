<h1 id="3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [number operator](../../../../number-operator.md) is $\hat N=a^\dagger a$. The [number state](../../../../number-state.md) expansion gives $a|\alpha\rangle=\alpha|\alpha\rangle$ by shifting the summation index; taking the adjoint gives $\langle\alpha|a^\dagger=\alpha^*\langle\alpha|$. Thus the [expectation value](../../../../expectation-value.md) is

$$
\overline N=\langle a^\dagger a\rangle=|\alpha|^2=\mu.
$$

For the second moment, use $aa^\dagger=a^\dagger a+I$:

$$
\hat N^2=a^\dagger aa^\dagger a=(a^\dagger)^2a^2+a^\dagger a.
$$

Its [expectation value](../../../../expectation-value.md) in the [coherent state](../../../../coherent-state.md) is $\langle\hat N^2\rangle=|\alpha|^4+|\alpha|^2$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\overline N=|\alpha|^2,\qquad
\operatorname{Var}(N)=\langle\hat N^2\rangle-\overline N^2=|\alpha|^2,\qquad
\sigma_N=|\alpha|.}
$$

The [variance](../../../../variance-split.md) equals the mean, exactly as for the [Poisson distribution](../../../../poisson-distribution.md) found in [b](../b.md). For $\mu>0$ the fractional [standard deviation](../../../../standard-deviation.md) is $1/\sqrt\mu$, the usual [photon shot noise](../../../../photon-shot-noise.md) scaling. For the [vacuum state](../../../../vacuum-state.md), both the mean [photon number](../../../../photon-number.md) and its [variance](../../../../variance-split.md) vanish; this does not imply zero [optical quadrature](../../../../optical-quadrature.md) fluctuations. At $\mu=0$ the fractional fluctuation is undefined.
