<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For the normalized [coherent state](../../../../coherent-state.md), $\langle a\rangle=\alpha$ and $\langle a^\dagger\rangle=\alpha^*$. The dimensionless [electric field](../../../../electric-field-split.md) and [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) [optical quadratures](../../../../optical-quadrature.md) consequently have [expectation values](../../../../expectation-value.md)

$$
\boxed{\langle\mathcal E\rangle=\alpha+\alpha^*=2\operatorname{Re}\alpha,\qquad
\langle\mathcal B\rangle=-i(\alpha-\alpha^*)=2\operatorname{Im}\alpha.}
$$

The physical [electric field](../../../../electric-field-split.md) and [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) strengths have the corresponding proportionality factors and mode functions. In free evolution replace $\alpha$ by $\alpha e^{-i\omega t}$ to recover the classical oscillation.

Expanding the squared operators cancels the $a^2$ and $(a^\dagger)^2$ terms:

$$
\mathcal E^2+\mathcal B^2=2(aa^\dagger+a^\dagger a)=4\hat N+2I.
$$

On the other hand, $(\alpha+\alpha^*)^2+[-i(\alpha-\alpha^*)]^2=4|\alpha|^2$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\langle\mathcal E^2+\mathcal B^2\rangle=4|\alpha|^2+2,\qquad
\langle\mathcal E\rangle^2+\langle\mathcal B\rangle^2=4|\alpha|^2.}
$$

The difference is the sum of the [variances](../../../../variance-split.md), not an extra mean field. Indeed $\operatorname{Var}(\mathcal E)=\operatorname{Var}(\mathcal B)=1$, independent of $\alpha$. The [commutator](../../../../commutator.md) $[\mathcal E,\mathcal B]=2iI$ requires $\sigma_{\mathcal E}\sigma_{\mathcal B}\geq1$, and the [coherent state](../../../../coherent-state.md) attains equality. The residual two units are the [vacuum fluctuations](../../../../vacuum-fluctuation.md) of the two [optical quadratures](../../../../optical-quadrature.md).
