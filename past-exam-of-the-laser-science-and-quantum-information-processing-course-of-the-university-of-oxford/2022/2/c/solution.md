<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The source [optical resonator](../../../../optical-cavity.md) has [Rayleigh range](../../../../rayleigh-length.md)

$$
z_A=\sqrt{L_1(R_1-L_1)}=\sqrt{1\times0.010}\,\mathrm m=0.100\,\mathrm m.
$$

The symmetric target [optical resonator](../../../../optical-cavity.md) has its fundamental [Gaussian beam](../../../../gaussian-beam.md) [beam waist](../../../../beam-waist.md) halfway between the mirrors. At distance $\ell=L_2/2$ from that [beam waist](../../../../beam-waist.md), [wavefront](../../../../wavefront.md) curvature is $R_2=\ell+z_B^2/\ell$. Thus

$$
z_B=\sqrt{\frac{L_2}{2}\left(R_2-\frac{L_2}{2}\right)}
=\sqrt{75}\,\mathrm m\simeq8.66025\,\mathrm m.
$$

Here $f^2=2.25\,\mathrm{m^2}>z_Az_B=0.866025\,\mathrm{m^2}$, so [b](../b.md) gives two real branches:

$$
\boxed{(u,v)\simeq(1.62642,12.44786)\,\mathrm m
\quad\hbox{or}\quad(1.37358,-9.44786)\,\mathrm m.}
$$

Only the first branch has the target [beam waist](../../../../beam-waist.md) downstream of the [optical lens](../../../../lens-optics.md). It is also geometrically accessible: the [optical lens](../../../../lens-optics.md) is $u-L_1\simeq0.62642\,\mathrm m$ beyond the source output mirror, and the target input mirror is $v-L_2/2\simeq7.44786\,\mathrm m$ beyond the [optical lens](../../../../lens-optics.md). The total separation between [beam waists](../../../../beam-waist.md) is $u+v\simeq14.07428\,\mathrm m$.
