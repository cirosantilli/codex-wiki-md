<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

In [second-harmonic generation](../../../../second-harmonic-generation.md), the quadratic [nonlinear polarization](../../../../nonlinear-polarization.md) contains products of pairs of comb components. Their [angular frequencies](../../../../angular-frequency.md) are

$$
\omega_p+\omega_q=2\omega_{\rm ce}+(p+q)\Delta\omega,
$$

including $2\omega_p$ when $p=q$. Original comb components have [angular frequencies](../../../../angular-frequency.md) $\omega_r=\omega_{\rm ce}+r\Delta\omega$, hence

$$
(\omega_p+\omega_q)-\omega_r
=\omega_{\rm ce}+(p+q-r)\Delta\omega.
$$

When the doubled low-[frequency](../../../../frequency.md) region overlaps the original high-[frequency](../../../../frequency.md) region, choosing $r=p+q$ gives the [beat](../../../../beat.md) at $|\omega_{\rm ce}|$. More generally the nearest positive [beat](../../../../beat.md) has [angular frequency](../../../../angular-frequency.md)

$$
\boxed{\omega_{\rm beat,min}
=\min_{k\in\mathbb Z}|\omega_{\rm ce}+k\Delta\omega|,}
$$

which is $|\omega_{\rm ce}|$ for the stipulated small offset. For $0\leq\omega_{\rm ce}<\Delta\omega$ it is $\min(\omega_{\rm ce},\Delta\omega-\omega_{\rm ce})$, provided the corresponding teeth are in the overlap.

In [comb self-referencing](../../../../comb-self-referencing.md), overlap the doubled red part and the original blue part on a [photodiode](../../../../photodiode.md). The [beat](../../../../beat.md) measures the [carrier-envelope offset frequency](../../../../carrier-envelope-offset-frequency.md). Feedback on an intracavity dispersion control or pump setting can set $\omega_{\rm ce}=0$ modulo $\Delta\omega$, giving $\boxed{\phi_{\rm slip}=0\pmod{2\pi}}$. A phase-sensitive lock or calibrated approach to zero resolves the sign ambiguity of an unsigned [beat](../../../../beat.md) measurement.[2000, comb self-referencing and stabilization of the pulse-to-pulse carrier phase](../../../../carrier-envelope-phase-control-of-femtosecond-mode-locked-lasers-and-direct-optical-frequency-synthesis-by-david-j-jones-and-others.md)

The coherent [optical spectrum](../../../../optical-spectrum.md) must span an octave, $\omega_{\max}\gtrsim2\omega_{\min}$, with sufficient overlapping spectral strength after [second-harmonic generation](../../../../second-harmonic-generation.md). This calls for very short broadband [optical pulses](../../../../optical-pulse.md), or coherent external spectral broadening. The two branches must preserve [optical coherence](../../../../optical-coherence.md), overlap in time and [polarization](../../../../polarization-waves.md), and have adequate [phase matching](../../../../phase-matching.md); bandwidth alone does not guarantee a detectable [beat](../../../../beat.md).
