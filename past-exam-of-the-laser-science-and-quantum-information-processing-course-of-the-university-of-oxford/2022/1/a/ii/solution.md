<h1 id="1/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

In [mode locking](../../../../../mode-locking.md), fixed relative [phases](../../../../../phase-waves.md) between many longitudinal [normal modes](../../../../../normal-mode.md) produce constructive [interference](../../../../../interference-wave-propagation.md) during a short part of each [optical resonator](../../../../../optical-cavity.md) round trip. The round-trip time sets the repetition period. The available coherent [gain bandwidth](../../../../../gain-bandwidth.md), the number of locked [normal modes](../../../../../normal-mode.md), the [spectral phase](../../../../../spectral-phase.md), and residual [group velocity dispersion](../../../../../group-velocity-dispersion.md) determine the [optical pulse](../../../../../optical-pulse.md) duration.

For $M$ consecutive equally weighted [normal modes](../../../../../normal-mode.md) of [angular frequency](../../../../../angular-frequency.md) spacing $\Delta\omega$, their [wave amplitude](../../../../../wave-amplitude.md) sum is proportional to

$$
\frac{\sin(M\Delta\omega t/2)}{\sin(\Delta\omega t/2)}.
$$

Its central peak has width of order $1/(M\Delta\omega)$, while its repetition period is $2\pi/\Delta\omega$. A [transform-limited pulse](../../../../../transform-limited-pulse.md) therefore becomes shorter as more coherent [gain bandwidth](../../../../../gain-bandwidth.md) is used. This mechanism can give picosecond or femtosecond [optical pulses](../../../../../optical-pulse.md): it does not require depletion of the whole stored [population inversion](../../../../../population-inversion.md) during each peak, as [Q-switching](../../../../../q-switching.md) does. [Mode locking](../../../../../mode-locking.md) generally accesses a much shorter [time-bandwidth product](../../../../../time-bandwidth-product.md) scale than the gain-discharge time of [Q-switching](../../../../../q-switching.md).
