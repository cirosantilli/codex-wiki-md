# Equatorial circular Kerr photon orbit

↑ **Parent:** [Circular null geodesic](circular-null-geodesic.md)

For $0<a<M$, the two exterior equatorial [circular null geodesics](circular-null-geodesic.md) of the [Kerr metric](kerr-metric.md) have radii solving the signed equation above. The plus sign gives the prograde branch and the minus sign gives the retrograde branch. To derive it put $b=L/E$ in the double-root conditions of the [equatorial Kerr null geodesic radial equation](equatorial-kerr-null-geodesic-radial-equation.md): $r^2=(b^2-a^2)/3$ and $r^3=M(b-a)^2$. Both exterior radii exceed $r_+=M+\sqrt{M^2-a^2}$ and approach $3M$ when $a\to0$. The horizon generators are a separate case with $E=L=0$; dividing by $E$ excludes them. Constant-radius null directions at other exterior radii generally have nonzero acceleration and are not [geodesics](geodesic.md).
