# Partial trace positivity from product vectors

↑ **Parent:** [Partial trace](partial-trace.md)

The [partial trace](partial-trace.md) of a [positive operator](positive-operator.md) is positive by this sum of nonnegative expectations in an orthonormal basis of the traced factor. Its trace equals the original trace, using the product orthonormal basis. Basis independence follows from $\operatorname{Tr}[(\operatorname{Tr}_B\rho)A]=\operatorname{Tr}[\rho(A\otimes I)]$, which uniquely determines the reduced operator. Thus reductions of [density matrices](density-matrix.md) remain density matrices. For a positive trace-class operator the same argument uses convergent nonnegative basis sums.
