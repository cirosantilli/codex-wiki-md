# Probability continuity equation

↑ **Parent:** [Probability current](probability-current.md)

For a [wavefunction](wave-function.md) satisfying the [Schrödinger equation](schrodinger-equation.md) with a real potential,

$$
\frac{\partial|\psi|^2}{\partial t}+\nabla\cdot J=0.
$$

This [continuity equation](continuity-equation.md) expresses local conservation of probability.
