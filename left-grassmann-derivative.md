# Left Grassmann derivative

↑ **Parent:** [Grassmann algebra](grassmann-algebra.md)

Left differentiation with respect to an odd [Grassmann variable](grassmann-variable.md) is the odd linear operation $\partial_\theta^L\theta=1$, with zero derivative on every other generator. For a homogeneous element $a$ of parity $|a|$, it obeys the graded product rule

$$
\partial_\theta^L(ab)=(\partial_\theta^La)b+(-1)^{|a|}a(\partial_\theta^Lb).
$$

For independent odd generators, $\partial_{\bar\theta}^L(\theta\bar\theta)=-\theta$. This sign comes from moving the derivative through the first odd factor in the [Grassmann algebra](grassmann-algebra.md). It fixes chirality in the [chiral-superfield component expansion](chiral-superfield-component-expansion.md); an ordinary commuting-variable product rule would give the wrong sign.
