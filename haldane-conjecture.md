# Haldane conjecture

↑ **Parent:** [Topological quantum matter](topological-quantum-matter-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haldane_conjecture)

For the uniform, nearest-neighbour, isotropic [Heisenberg antiferromagnet](heisenberg-antiferromagnet.md) chain, the [Haldane conjecture](haldane-conjecture.md) distinguishes integer [spin](spin.md) $S$, with a nonzero bulk excitation [spectral gap](spectral-gap.md), from half-odd-integer $S$, with gapless excitations in the thermodynamic limit. The continuum [nonlinear sigma model](nonlinear-sigma-model.md) contains a topological angle $\theta=2\pi S$: integer and half-odd-integer [spins](spin.md) give $0$ and $\pi$ modulo $2\pi$. This is a different use of topology from the two-dimensional occupied-band [Chern number](chern-number.md). The $S=1$ chain belongs to the [Haldane phase](haldane-phase.md).[Haldane, individual Nobel lecture delivered 8 December 2016, published 2017, pp. 040502-8–9: integer versus half-odd-integer chains and topological angle](nobel-lecture-topological-quantum-matter-by-f-duncan-m-haldane.md)
