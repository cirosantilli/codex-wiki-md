# Lens-shaped intersection of two disks

↑ **Parent:** [Disk (mathematics)](disk-mathematics.md)

For two [disks](disk-mathematics.md) of equal radius $r$ whose centres are separated by $0\leq d\leq2r$, their intersection consists of two circular segments. Each segment is a sector of angle $2\theta$ minus an isosceles triangle, where $\cos\theta=d/(2r)$. Hence its total [area](surface-area.md) is

$$
A=2r^2\theta-r^2\sin(2\theta)
=2r^2\arccos\frac{d}{2r}-\frac d2\sqrt{4r^2-d^2}.
$$

The limiting cases are $A=\pi r^2$ at $d=0$ and $A=0$ at $d=2r$. This geometric region is distinct from an [optical lens](lens-optics.md).
