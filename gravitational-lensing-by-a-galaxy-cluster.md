# Gravitational lensing by a galaxy cluster

↑ **Parent:** [Gravitational lensing](gravitational-lensing-split.md)

The projected gravitational field of a [galaxy cluster](galaxy-cluster.md) bends light from background sources, producing strong-lensing arcs and multiple images near critical curves, and weak coherent shape distortions farther out. Joint analysis probes total projected [mass](mass.md) rather than just luminous matter. Source [distances](distance.md), shape calibration, line-of-sight structure and degeneracies in the [mass](mass.md) model limit inference; lensing alone does not identify a dark-matter particle.
