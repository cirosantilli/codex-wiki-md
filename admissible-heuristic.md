# Admissible heuristic

↑ **Parent:** [Search problem](search-problem.md)

A [heuristic function](heuristic-function.md) bounded above by the minimum remaining goal cost $h^*(s)$. When no goal is reachable, take $h^*(s)=\infty$. Admissibility is a lower-bound guarantee, rather than a statement that the estimate is usually numerically accurate. Ignoring constraints can produce a [relaxation heuristic](relaxation-heuristic.md) because it enlarges the available solution set.
