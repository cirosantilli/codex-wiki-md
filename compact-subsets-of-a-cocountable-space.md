# Compact subsets of a cocountable space

↑ **Parent:** [Countable complement topology](countable-complement-topology.md)

Only finite subsets are compact in the [cocountable topology](countable-complement-topology.md). An infinite subset contains distinct $c_1,c_2,\ldots$. The open sets obtained by removing the tails $\{c_j:j\geq n\}$ form an increasing [open cover](open-cover.md), but every finite selection misses a tail point. Finite sets are compact in every topology.
