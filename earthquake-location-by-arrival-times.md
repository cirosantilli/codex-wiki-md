# Earthquake location by arrival times

↑ **Parent:** [Seismology](seismology.md)

Arrival-time location estimates a [hypocenter](hypocenter.md) and origin time by comparing picked phase arrivals with predicted [travel times](travel-time.md). [Linearization](linearization.md) gives residual changes $\delta t_i=\delta t_0+\nabla T_i\cdot\delta\mathbf x$. Poorly distinct [derivative](derivative.md) columns cause tradeoffs, while incorrect [wave speeds](wave-speed.md) or phase labels cause systematic bias even with many stations.
