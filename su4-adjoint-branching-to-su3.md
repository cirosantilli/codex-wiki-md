# SU4 adjoint branching to SU3

↑ **Parent:** [Branching of a representation](branching-of-a-representation.md)

For the upper-left [SU(3)](su-3-group.md) [subgroup](subgroup.md) of [SU(4)](su-4-group.md), the defining [representation](group-representation.md) restricts as $\mathbf4=\mathbf3\oplus\mathbf1$. Write a [traceless matrix](traceless-matrix.md) in three-plus-one block form. Its upper-left traceless block is the [SU(3)](su-3-group.md) [Adjoint representation](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md), its upper-right column is $\mathbf3$, its lower-left row is $\overline{\mathbf3}$, and the remaining diagonal direction $\operatorname{diag}(1,1,1,-3)$ is fixed. These [invariant subspaces](invariant-subspace.md) have [dimensions](dimension-vector-space.md) $8,3,3,1$ and exhaust the [SU(4)](su-4-group.md) [Adjoint representation](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md). This also proves the formula by restricting $\mathbf4\otimes\overline{\mathbf4}-\mathbf1$.
