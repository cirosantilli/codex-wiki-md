# Vanishing of weight-fourteen level-one cusp forms

↑ **Parent:** [Valence formula for the modular group](valence-formula-for-the-modular-group.md)

A nonzero weight-fourteen [cusp form](cusp-form.md) would have order at infinity at least one and order at $i$ at least one, since $i^{14}=-1$. The [valence formula for the modular group](valence-formula-for-the-modular-group.md) would then give $1+1/2\leq14/12$, which is impossible.
