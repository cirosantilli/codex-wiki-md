<h1 id="feit-thompson-theorem">Feit–Thompson theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Finite group theory](finite-group-theory-split.md)

Every finite [group](group-split.md) of odd order is a [solvable group](solvable-group.md). In particular, a nonabelian [simple group](simple-group.md) has even order and therefore has an [involution](involution.md) by [Cauchy's theorem for finite groups](cauchy-theorem-for-groups.md). This is a deep theorem, not an elementary consequence of the [Sylow theorems](sylow-theorems.md).
