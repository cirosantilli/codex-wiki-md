# Gauss-Legendre quadrature

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian quadrature](gaussian-quadrature.md)

Gauss-Legendre quadrature approximates an [integral](integral-split.md) on a finite interval using nodes obtained from roots of a [Legendre polynomial](legendre-polynomial.md). With $n$ nodes it integrates every [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) of degree at most $2n-1$ exactly.
