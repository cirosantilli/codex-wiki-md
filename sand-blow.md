# Sand blow

↑ **Parent:** [Soil liquefaction](soil-liquefaction.md)

A surface deposit of sand and fluid expelled during [soil liquefaction](soil-liquefaction.md). It can indicate past strong shaking but does not by itself identify rupture on the local [fault](geological-fault.md).
