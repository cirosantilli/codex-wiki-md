# Schwarzschild horizon collision energy

↑ **Parent:** [Schwarzschild event horizon](schwarzschild-event-horizon.md)

For two equal-mass inward [timelike geodesics](timelike-geodesic.md) of unit specific [Killing energy](killing-energy.md), their invariant [center-of-mass energy](center-of-mass-energy.md) has this finite horizon limit. If both are captured from infinity, [Schwarzschild marginally bound capture](schwarzschild-marginally-bound-capture.md) gives $E_{\rm com}<m\sqrt{20}$, with $m\sqrt{20}$ a supremum. No such universal bound follows for particles prepared inside the angular-momentum barrier.
