# Prismatic triangular-channel shallow water equations

↑ **Parent:** [Particle-laden gravity current](particle-laden-gravity-current.md)

For fixed channel width $b(z)=\beta z$, cross-sectional area is $A=\beta h^2/2$. With [reduced gravity](reduced-gravity-split.md) $G\phi$, uniform cross-sectional fields and downward [settling velocity](settling-velocity.md) magnitude $w$, [volume conservation](volume-conservation.md), [momentum](momentum.md) and [particle deposition flux](particle-deposition-flux.md) give

$$
h_t+uh_x+(h/2)u_x=0,\qquad u_t+uu_x+G\phi h_x+(Gh/3)\phi_x=0,\qquad\phi_t+u\phi_x=-2w\phi/h.
$$

The [hydrostatic pressure](hydrostatic-pressure.md) force contains $\int_0^h(h-z)\beta z\,dz=\beta h^3/6$. No streamwise widening term occurs in a prismatic channel. Deposition uses the projected boundary width $\beta h$ under the vertical-settling model.
