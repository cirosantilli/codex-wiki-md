# Symbolic Model Checking without BDDs by Armin Biere and others

↑ **Parent:** [Computer science bibliography](computer-science-bibliography-split.md)

By [Armin Biere](armin-biere.md), [Alessandro Cimatti](alessandro-cimatti.md), [Edmund Clarke](edmund-clarke.md) and [Yunshan Zhu](yunshan-zhu.md). Published in Tools and Algorithms for the Construction and Analysis of Systems, volume 1579, pp. 193–207.

Author-hosted text: [https://cca.informatik.uni-freiburg.de/papers/BiereCimattiClarkeZhu-TACAS99.pdf](https://cca.informatik.uni-freiburg.de/papers/BiereCimattiClarkeZhu-TACAS99.pdf)

Introduces [bounded model checking](bounded-model-checking.md) by translating a search for a bounded [counterexample](counterexample.md) into a [Boolean satisfiability problem](boolean-satisfiability-problem.md). Section 2 explains unrolling and loop closure through a worked example; §3 states infinite-path semantics; §4 gives the encoding; §5 discusses bounds.
