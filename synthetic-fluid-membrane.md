# Synthetic fluid membrane

↑ **Parent:** [Soft matter](soft-matter.md)

A [synthetic fluid membrane](synthetic-fluid-membrane.md) is a thin self-assembled sheet of synthetic [amphiphiles](amphiphile.md), fluid under static in-plane shear while resisting [area](surface-area.md) changes and bending. It provides a nonbiological realization of a two-dimensional fluid interface with [curvature](curvature.md) [elasticity](elasticity-physics-split.md). A [Helfrich energy](helfrich-energy.md) describes long-wavelength shape costs; its material parameters and any [spontaneous curvature](spontaneous-curvature.md) depend on composition and conditions. Lateral fluidity does not eliminate [membrane bending rigidity](membrane-bending-rigidity.md) or [membrane tension](membrane-tension.md). The [membrane fluctuation spectrum under tension](membrane-fluctuation-spectrum-under-tension.md) separates bending-dominated and tension-dominated [length](length.md) scales.
