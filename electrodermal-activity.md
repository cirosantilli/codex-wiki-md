# Electrodermal activity

↑ **Parent:** [Psychophysiology](psychophysiology.md)

Changes in the electrical properties of skin associated with [sweat gland](sweat-gland.md) activity. Recording electrodes can measure tonic levels and event-related changes in [skin conductance](skin-conductance.md). Such changes inform [psychophysiology](psychophysiology.md) but do not uniquely identify an [emotion](emotion.md).
