# Drag on a two-dimensional circular bubble from outer-flow dissipation

↑ **Parent:** [Dissipation estimate for high-Reynolds-number bubble drag](dissipation-estimate-for-high-reynolds-number-bubble-drag.md)

For the zero-circulation flow around a circle, $e:e=8U^2a^4/r^6$. Integration over the exterior plane gives dissipation $8\pi\mu U^2$ per unit axial length and hence drag $D=8\pi\mu U$ per unit length.
