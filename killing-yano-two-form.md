# Killing-Yano two-form

↑ **Parent:** [Killing-Yano tensor](killing-yano-tensor.md)

For a Killing-Yano two-form, $\nabla_aY_{bc}$ is a [differential three-form](differential-three-form.md). Along a [geodesic](geodesic.md) with velocity $v$, the covector $w_c=Y_{bc}v^b$ is carried by [parallel transport](parallel-transport.md), since $v^av^b\nabla_aY_{bc}=0$.
