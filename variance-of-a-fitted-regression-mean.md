# Variance of a fitted regression mean

↑ **Parent:** [Normal linear model](normal-linear-model.md)

For a full-column-rank [design matrix](design-matrix.md) in a [normal linear model](normal-linear-model.md), a fitted mean at covariate vector $v$ is $v^T\widehat\beta$. The [covariance matrix](covariance-matrix.md) of the [least-squares estimator](ordinary-least-squares-estimators.md) immediately gives the displayed [variance](variance-split.md). Replacing $\sigma^2$ by the independent unbiased residual [variance](variance-split.md) gives its squared [standard error](standard-error.md) and a [Student t confidence interval](student-t-confidence-interval.md) on $n-p$ [statistical degrees of freedom](statistical-degrees-of-freedom.md). A future response instead contributes an additional error [variance](variance-split.md) $\sigma^2$ when its error is independent of the fitted data.
