# Metabolic pathway

↑ **Parent:** [Biochemistry](biochemistry-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metabolic_pathway)

A [metabolic pathway](metabolic-pathway.md) is a connected sequence of [enzyme](enzyme-split.md)-catalyzed [chemical reactions](chemical-reaction-split.md) in [metabolism](metabolism.md), with a [reaction product](product-chemistry.md) of one step serving as a [substrate](substrate-biochemistry.md) for another. A pathway boundary is a choice about which transformations to count: [glycolysis](glycolysis.md) ends at [pyruvate](pyruvate.md), whereas subsequent [fermentation](fermentation.md) or [cellular respiration](cellular-respiration.md) changes the final material balance. Adding the individual [chemical equations](chemical-equation.md) cancels internal intermediates and exposes the net [stoichiometry](stoichiometry.md); it does not determine the [reaction rate](reaction-rate.md).
