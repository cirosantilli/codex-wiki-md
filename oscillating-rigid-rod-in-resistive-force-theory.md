# Oscillating rigid rod in resistive-force theory

↑ **Parent:** [Resistive-force theory](resistive-force-theory.md)

A rigid rod with moving centre and changing orientation has material [velocity](velocity.md) $\dot{\mathbf X}+s\dot\theta\mathbf n$. Its centred rotational [velocity](velocity.md) is odd in arclength and has no resultant [force](force.md), while its orientation-dependent resistance tensor couples translation to transverse [force](force.md). [Resistive-force theory](resistive-force-theory.md) uses per-length coefficients $c_\parallel,c_\perp$.
