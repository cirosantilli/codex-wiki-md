# Categorical thinning of a Poisson claim count

↑ **Parent:** [Poisson thinning](poisson-thinning.md)

Assign each of $N\sim\operatorname{Pois}(\lambda)$ claims with [independent](independent-random-variables.md) category labels to one of finitely many categories with [probabilities](probability.md) $p_i$. Conditional counts have a [multinomial distribution](multinomial-distribution.md) with [probability generating function](probability-generating-function.md) $(\sum_i p_i z_i)^N$. Averaging over $N$ gives $\exp(\lambda(\sum_i p_i z_i-1))=\prod_i\exp(\lambda p_i(z_i-1))$. This proves [independent](independent-random-variables.md) category counts and supports discrete-severity [compound Poisson distribution](compound-poisson-distribution.md) representations.
