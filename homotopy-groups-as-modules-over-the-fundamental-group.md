# Homotopy groups as modules over the fundamental group

↑ **Parent:** [Homotopy group](homotopy-group.md)

Moving the basepoint around a loop acts on a higher [homotopy group](homotopy-group.md). Since $\pi_n(X)$ is an [abelian group](abelian-group.md) for $n\geq2$, this action extends linearly to its integral [group ring](group-ring.md), making $\pi_n(X)$ a module. For a connected [CW complex](cw-complex.md), its [universal cover](universal-cover.md) gives an equivalent description by [deck transformations](deck-transformation.md), together with change of basepoint upstairs. Left and right conventions are converted by inversion of the loop. The [Hurewicz theorem](hurewicz-theorem.md) respects this action whenever it identifies a higher [homotopy group](homotopy-group.md) with the corresponding homology of the cover.
