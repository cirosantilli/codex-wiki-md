# Dirichlet Green function for a three-dimensional quarter-space

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet Green function](dirichlet-green-function.md)

For $D=\{x>0,z>0\}\subset\mathbb R^3$, take the [Dirichlet Green function](dirichlet-green-function.md) convention $-\Delta_PG(P,Q)=\delta_Q(P)$. Reflect $Q=(\xi,\eta,\zeta)$ to $Q_{ij}=((-1)^i\xi,\eta,(-1)^j\zeta)$. The displayed image sum vanishes on both faces, has exactly one positive unit source inside $D$, and decays at infinity. Pairwise cancellation proves the boundary conditions, while the three image terms are [harmonic](harmonic-function.md) inside $D$. Its inward source-normal derivative gives the [Poisson kernel of a three-dimensional quarter-space](poisson-kernel-of-a-three-dimensional-quarter-space.md). The source sign must be kept consistent with [Green's second identity](green-second-identity.md).
