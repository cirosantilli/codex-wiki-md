# Spectral permanence for Hermitian Banach algebras

↑ **Parent:** [Hermitian Banach algebra](hermitian-banach-algebra.md)

Let $B$ be a [closed](closed-set.md) [star-subalgebra](star-subalgebra.md) of a unital [Hermitian Banach algebra](hermitian-banach-algebra.md) $A$, containing the same identity. For Hermitian $h\in B$, the complement of $\sigma_A(h)\subseteq\mathbb R$ is [connected](connected-space.md). The resolvent belongs to $B$ at large modulus by the [Neumann series](neumann-series.md); its membership in $B$ is both open and [closed](closed-set.md) in that complement, so $\sigma_B(h)=\sigma_A(h)$. If $b$ is invertible in $A$, both $bb^*$ and $b^*b$ are invertible there and [Hermitian algebra elements](hermitian-element-of-a-star-algebra.md), hence invertible in $B$. Their inverses supply respectively a right and a left inverse for $b$ in $B$, proving invertibility there. Applying this to $b-\lambda1$ proves the spectrum equality.
