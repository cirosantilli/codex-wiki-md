# Ci field

↑ **Parent:** [Field](field.md)

A field is $C_i$ if every [homogeneous polynomial](homogeneous-polynomial.md) of degree $d\ge1$ in more than $d^i$ variables has a nonzero zero. Algebraically closed fields are $C_0$, [finite fields](finite-field.md) are $C_1$, and the [Lang-Nagata theorem for Ci fields](lang-nagata-theorem-for-ci-fields.md) controls function-[field extensions](field-extension.md).
