# Thermal winding instability of a closed bosonic string

↑ **Parent:** [Hagedorn temperature](hagedorn-temperature.md)

Euclidean finite [temperature](temperature.md) compactifies time to a circle of radius $R_\beta=\beta/(2\pi)$. The [string oscillator](string-oscillator.md) ground states with zero [momentum](momentum.md) number and winding $w=\pm1$ have the displayed mass from the [bosonic string mass spectrum](bosonic-string-mass-spectrum.md). They become massless at $\beta_H=4\pi\sqrt{\alpha'}$ and tachyonic at higher [temperature](temperature.md). This thermal winding mode diagnoses breakdown of the perturbative low-temperature string gas at the [Hagedorn temperature](hagedorn-temperature.md). The endpoint and order of a transition require interactions and a suitable stable vacuum; the ordinary zero-winding bosonic [tachyon](tachyon.md) is already unstable independently of this thermal threshold.
