# Convolution of p-adic measures

↑ **Parent:** [p-adic measure](p-adic-measure.md)

On a [profinite group](profinite-group.md), convolution is first defined for functions on a finite quotient by summing products of the two measures' coset coefficients, then extended by uniform approximation. Finite-quotient associativity proves associativity. For an abelian [group](group-split.md) it is commutative; the Dirac measure at the identity is the identity element. Under the [measure realization of an Iwasawa algebra](measure-realization-of-an-iwasawa-algebra.md), it becomes group-algebra multiplication.
