# Inelastic collision

↑ **Parent:** [Collision mechanics](collision-mechanics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inelastic_collision)

An inelastic collision conserves the total [momentum](momentum.md) of the isolated colliding bodies but converts some of their kinetic energy into internal energy. The decrease in translational [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) drives [dissipative spreading of a planetary ring](dissipative-spreading-of-a-planetary-ring.md).
