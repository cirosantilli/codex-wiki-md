# Inelastic collision

↑ **Parent:** [Collision mechanics](collision-mechanics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inelastic_collision)

An [inelastic collision](inelastic-collision.md) changes total [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md). In the dissipative case it decreases, transferring [energy](energy-split.md) to [internal energy](internal-energy-split.md), including [deformation](deformation-mechanics.md) and microscopic motion. Isolated colliding bodies still obey [momentum conservation](momentum-conservation.md); [conservation of energy](conservation-of-energy.md) requires accounting for the transferred [energy](energy-split.md). Bodies need not stick in every [inelastic collision](inelastic-collision.md). The decrease in translational [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) drives [dissipative spreading of a planetary ring](dissipative-spreading-of-a-planetary-ring.md).

For the incoming [masses](mass.md) and [velocities](velocity.md) in the [elastic collision](elastic-collision.md) example, stipulate that the bodies stick. This [perfectly inelastic collision](perfectly-inelastic-collision.md) has a shared outgoing [velocity](velocity.md)

$$
v=\frac{m_1u_1+m_2u_2}{m_1+m_2}
=\frac{6\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}}{3\,\mathrm{kg}}
=2\,\mathrm{m/s}.
$$

The outgoing [momenta](momentum-split.md) are $4$ and $2\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$, totaling $6\,\mathrm{kg\,m/s}$. The outgoing [kinetic energies](kinetic-energy.md) are $4$ and $2\,\mathrm J$, totaling $6\,\mathrm J$. Thus $9-6=3\,\mathrm J$ of [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) becomes [internal energy](internal-energy-split.md) within the isolated pair. Nothing in [momentum conservation](momentum-conservation.md) requires this $3\,\mathrm J$ to remain as [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md). [OpenStax University Physics Volume 1, section 9.4: elastic and perfectly inelastic outcomes](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/9-4-types-of-collisions)
