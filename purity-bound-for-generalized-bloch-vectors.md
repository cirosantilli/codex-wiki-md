# Purity bound for generalized Bloch vectors

↑ **Parent:** [Generalized Bloch representation](generalized-bloch-representation.md)

For a Hilbert-Schmidt orthonormal traceless Hermitian basis, the [Generalized Bloch representation](generalized-bloch-representation.md) gives the displayed identity by orthogonality. Nonnegative [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) of a [density operator](density-matrix.md) sum to one, so their squared sum is at most one, with equality precisely for a rank-one projector. For $N>2$, the radius bound alone does not guarantee positivity: $(2/N)I-P$ for a rank-one projector $P$ has [trace](matrix-trace.md) one and squared norm one, but one negative [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md).
