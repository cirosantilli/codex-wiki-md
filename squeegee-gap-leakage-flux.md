# Squeegee gap leakage flux

↑ **Parent:** [Squeegee lubrication model](squeegee-lubrication-model.md)

For a rounded blade with local [parabolic lubrication gap](parabolic-lubrication-gap.md) $d=\epsilon+x^2/(2\delta)$, $\epsilon\ll\delta$, the [Couette-Poiseuille flow in a thin gap](couette-poiseuille-flow-in-a-thin-gap.md) yields a floor-driven flux $2\epsilon/3$ and pressure-driven leakage proportional to $\epsilon^{5/2}\Delta p/\sqrt\delta$. Positive $J$ is from the upstream pile towards the rear.
