# Bacterial infection

↑ **Parent:** [Infection](infection.md)

An [infection](infection.md) caused by [bacteria](bacteria.md).

An [infection](infection.md) involves invasion and multiplication of an infectious agent in a [host](host-biology.md). [Bacteria](bacteria.md) are one possible cause; an agent capable of causing [disease](disease.md) is a [pathogen](pathogen.md). The National Cancer Institute defines infection through invasion and growth in the body. [NCI Dictionary: infection](https://www.cancer.gov/publications/dictionaries/cancer-terms/def/infection).

In a [SIR model](sir-model.md), acquiring [infection](infection.md) moves a member of the [susceptible compartment](susceptible-compartment.md) into the [infectious compartment](infectious-compartment.md). That immediate transition is a model assumption: the [SEIR model](seir-model.md) instead includes an exposed stage before infectiousness. Specify these states when translating a biological process into [compartmental models](compartmental-models-epidemiology.md).
