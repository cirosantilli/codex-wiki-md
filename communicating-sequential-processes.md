# Communicating sequential processes

↑ **Parent:** [Process algebra](process-algebra.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communicating_sequential_processes)

A [process algebra](process-algebra.md) in which components communicate through synchronized [CSP events](csp-event.md). Its operational description uses a [labelled transition system](labelled-transition-system.md); standard observations include [CSP traces](csp-trace.md), [CSP stable failures](csp-stable-failure.md) and [CSP divergences](csp-divergence.md).[§1.2, The CSP View of the World, opening paragraphs and list of semantic models](fdr2-user-manual.md)
