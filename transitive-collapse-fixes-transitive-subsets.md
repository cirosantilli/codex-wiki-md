# Transitive collapse fixes transitive subsets

↑ **Parent:** [Mostowski collapse theorem](mostowski-collapse-theorem.md)

If $X$ has a well-founded extensional inherited membership relation and $T\subseteq X$ is a [transitive set](transitive-set.md), its [Mostowski collapse theorem](mostowski-collapse-theorem.md) map $\pi$ fixes every member of $T$. The recursion $\pi(x)=\{\pi(y):y\in x\cap X\}$ and [well-founded relation](well-founded-relation.md) induction give this pointwise. If also $T\in X$, then $\pi(T)=T$.
