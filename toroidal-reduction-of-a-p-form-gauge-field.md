# Toroidal reduction of a p-form gauge field

↑ **Parent:** [Dimensional reduction](dimensional-reduction.md)

The zero-mode [dimensional reduction](dimensional-reduction.md) on a flat $(D-4)$-torus splits a [massless p-form gauge field](massless-p-form-gauge-field.md) by the number $r$ of spacetime indices. Each sector has $\binom{D-4}{p-r}$ copies and $\binom2r$ local polarizations per copy. Comparing coefficients in $(1+t)^{D-4}(1+t)^2$ gives $\sum_r\binom{D-4}{p-r}\binom2r=\binom{D-2}{p}$, proving polarization matching. Four-dimensional three- and four-form potentials contribute no local propagating states.
