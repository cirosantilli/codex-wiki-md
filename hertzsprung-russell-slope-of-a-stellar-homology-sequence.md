# Hertzsprung-Russell slope of a stellar homology sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Homology relations for radiative stars](homology-relations-for-radiative-stars.md)

For a [stellar homology](stellar-homology.md) sequence with $R\propto M^X$ and $L\propto M^Y$, the [Stefan–Boltzmann law](stefan-boltzmann-law.md) gives $T_{\rm eff}\propto M^{(Y-2X)/4}$. Eliminating mass gives the displayed [Hertzsprung-Russell diagram](hertzsprung-russell-diagram.md) slope when $Y\ne2X$. The usual horizontal axis decreases in [temperature](temperature.md) toward the right, reversing the visual left-to-right sign. The relation is between logarithmic variables and depends on the specified structural assumptions.
