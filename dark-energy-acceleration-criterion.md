# Dark-energy acceleration criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Dark energy](dark-energy.md)

Neglect [radiation in cosmology](radiation-in-cosmology.md) and take [pressureless matter](pressureless-matter.md) plus a separately conserved [dark energy](dark-energy.md) component with $p_{\rm DE}=w\rho_{\rm DE}c^2$, where $\rho$ here denotes mass-equivalent [energy density](energy-density.md). The [Friedmann acceleration equation](friedmann-acceleration-equation.md) gives

$$
\frac{\ddot a}{a}=-\frac{4\pi G}{3}\left[\rho_m+\rho_{\rm DE}(1+3w)\right].
$$

Thus $w<-1/3$ for a positive-density component is necessary for that component to accelerate expansion, but the total must also overcome [pressureless matter](pressureless-matter.md). For a [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md), $w=-1$ and $q_0=\Omega_{m,0}/2-\Omega_{\Lambda,0}$, so acceleration requires $\Omega_{\Lambda,0}>\Omega_{m,0}/2$. [Spatial curvature of an FLRW universe](spatial-curvature-of-an-flrw-universe.md) enters the distance relation but has no separate term in this acceleration criterion. A [Hubble diagram](hubble-diagram.md) alone does not identify a microscopic [dark energy](dark-energy.md) mechanism.
