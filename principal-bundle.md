# Principal bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Fiber bundle](fiber-bundle-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principal_bundle)

A principal $G$-bundle is a [fiber bundle](fiber-bundle-split.md) $\pi:P\to B$ with a free right action of a [Lie group](lie-group.md) $G$ that is locally equivariantly isomorphic to $U\times G\to U$. Each fiber is a single $G$-orbit.
