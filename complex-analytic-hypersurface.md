# Complex analytic hypersurface

↑ **Parent:** [Complex submanifold](complex-submanifold.md)

A complex analytic hypersurface in an $n$-dimensional [complex manifold](complex-manifold.md) is a closed analytic subset of pure complex codimension one. It is irreducible when it is not the union of two proper closed analytic subsets.
