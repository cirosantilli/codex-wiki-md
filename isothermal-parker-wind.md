# Isothermal Parker wind

↑ **Parent:** [Parker wind](parker-wind.md)

A steady spherical [Parker wind](parker-wind.md) with constant [isothermal sound speed](isothermal-sound-speed.md) $c$ and negligible wind [self-gravity](self-gravity.md) obeys $4\pi r^2\rho u=\dot M$ and $(u-c^2/u)u'=2c^2/r-GM/r^2$. A regular [sonic point](sonic-point.md) has $u=c$ at $r_s=GM/(2c^2)$. With $x=r/r_s$ and $w=u^2/c^2$, integration gives the displayed equation for the smooth accelerating [transonic branch](transonic-branch.md). Its slope at the sonic point is $u'(r_s)=c/r_s$. Maintaining constant gas [temperature](temperature-split.md) during expansion generally requires heat input, so this model is distinct from an adiabatic wind with $\gamma>1$.
