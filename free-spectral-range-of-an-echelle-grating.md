# Free spectral range of an echelle grating

↑ **Parent:** [Free spectral range](free-spectral-range.md)

At fixed incident and central outgoing angles, the [grating equation](grating-equation.md) gives $m\lambda_m=K=d(\sin\alpha+\sin\beta_0)$. Adjacent central wavelengths are separated by

$$
\lambda_m-\lambda_{m+1}=\frac K{m(m+1)}\sim\frac{\lambda_m}m.
$$

This is the usual adjacent-order [free spectral range](free-spectral-range.md). A detector width of approximately $q\Delta\lambda_{\rm FSR}$, where $q$ is the [grating dispersion](grating-dispersion.md), covers one such interval.
