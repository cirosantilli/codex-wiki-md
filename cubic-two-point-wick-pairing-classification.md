# Cubic two-point Wick-pairing classification

↑ **Parent:** [Wick contraction](wick-contraction.md)

At second order in the [coupling constant](coupling-constant-physics.md) of a real [phi cubed theory](phi-cubed-theory.md), the two-point numerator has eight [fields](field.md), hence $7!!=105$ [Wick contractions](wick-contraction.md). Of these, six give a free external line times a vacuum theta graph, nine give a free line times a vacuum dumbbell, 36 give a two-line bubble, 36 give a connected tadpole attachment, and 18 give two separate one-point tadpoles. Dividing by $2!(3!)^2=72$ gives weights $1/12,1/8,1/2,1/2,1/4$. The [normalized vacuum generating functional](normalized-vacuum-generating-functional.md) cancels the first two contributions, while the last remains in the full two-point [correlation function](correlation-function.md) as the product of one-point functions. Thus [cancellation of vacuum bubbles](cancellation-of-vacuum-bubbles.md) does not, by itself, take the connected part.
