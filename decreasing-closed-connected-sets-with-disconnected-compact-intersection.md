# Decreasing closed connected sets with disconnected compact intersection

↑ **Parent:** [Connectedness of an intersection from connected finite intersections](connectedness-of-an-intersection-from-connected-finite-intersections.md)

Use the arcs and points of the [noncompact counterexample to the Šura-Bura lemma](noncompact-counterexample-to-the-sura-bura-lemma.md), and add the central ray $\{(1/2,t):t\geq1\}$ to form $X$. Define $C_n=\{p,q\}\cup\bigcup_{m\geq n}A_m\cup\{(1/2,t):t\geq n\}$. Each graph tail is [connected](connected-space.md) because its arcs meet the ray. Its relative [closure](closure-topology.md) in $X$ adds just $p,q$, so each $C_n$ is closed and [connected](connected-space.md). These sets decrease and have [intersection](set-intersection.md) $\{p,q\}$, which is [compact](compact-space.md) and disconnected. The ambient $X=C_1$ is [connected](connected-space.md) and [Hausdorff](hausdorff-space.md) but noncompact.
