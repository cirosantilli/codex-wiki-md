<h1 id="lusternik-schnirelmann-theorem">Lusternik–Schnirelmann theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Borsuk-Ulam theorem](borsuk-ulam-theorem.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lusternik–Schnirelmann_theorem)

A cover of $S^d$ by $d+1$ [closed sets](closed-set.md) has a member containing an [antipodal pair](antipodal-pair.md). The equivalent version for [open sets](open-set.md) follows by shrinking finite [open covers](open-cover.md) on a [compact metric space](compact-metric-space.md); the reverse implication follows by enlarging antipodal-pair-free [closed sets](closed-set.md) slightly. This is a covering formulation of the [Borsuk-Ulam theorem](borsuk-ulam-theorem.md).
