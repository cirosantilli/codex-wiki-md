# Thomson escape layer of an index-one polytrope

↑ **Parent:** [Polytrope of index one](polytrope-of-index-one.md)

For a [polytrope of index one](polytrope-of-index-one.md) with $\rho(r)=\rho_c\sin(\pi r/R)/(\pi r/R)$ and fully ionized [hydrogen](hydrogen.md), [Thomson scattering](thomson-scattering.md) gives the central [mean free path](mean-free-path.md) $L=m_p/(\sigma_T\rho_c)$. At small depth $\Delta=R-r$, $\ell\simeq LR/\Delta$ and the radial [optical depth](optical-depth.md) is $\tau\simeq\Delta^2/(2LR)$. Thus $\ell=\Delta$ gives $\Delta=\sqrt{LR}$ whereas $\tau=1$ gives $\Delta=\sqrt{2LR}$. Neither condition by itself guarantees thermalization.
