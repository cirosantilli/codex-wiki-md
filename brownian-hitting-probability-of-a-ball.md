# Brownian hitting probability of a ball

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian exit time](brownian-exit-time.md)

For $d\geq3$ and $|x|>\varepsilon$, stop the [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md) $u(x)=|x|^{2-d}$ at the two boundaries of $\{\varepsilon<|x|<R\}$. Bounded [optional stopping](optional-sampling-theorem-for-a-supermartingale.md) gives the probability of hitting the inner sphere first as $(u(x)-u(R))/(u(\varepsilon)-u(R))$. Letting $R\to\infty$ gives the displayed formula; a finite hitting path is contained in some finite-radius ball.
