# Central and averaged Compton y parameters of a uniform sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Compton y parameter](compton-y-parameter.md)

In a uniform spherical [plasma](plasma-physics.md) of [radius](radius.md) $R$, [electron](electron.md) [number density](number-density.md) $n_e$ and [temperature](temperature-split.md) $T$, the [thermal Sunyaev-Zeldovich effect](thermal-sunyaev-zeldovich-effect.md) gives $y(0)=2\sigma_TRn_ek_BT/(m_ec^2)$ along the central ray. A chord at impact parameter $b$ is shorter by $\sqrt{1-b^2/R^2}$. Averaging $y(b)$ over the projected disk with area element $2\pi b\,db$ gives $\bar y=2y(0)/3$. Using an averaged measurement as though it were a central ray therefore biases any joint [X-ray](x-ray.md) and [Sunyaev-Zeldovich effect](sunyaev-zeldovich-effect.md) inference.
