# Riemannian gradient

↑ **Parent:** [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md)

For a smooth function $f$ on a [Riemannian manifold](riemannian-manifold.md), its Riemannian gradient is the unique [vector field](vector-field.md) satisfying $g(\nabla_gf,V)=df(V)$ for every [vector field](vector-field.md) $V$. It depends on the [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md), and its negative generates the downward [gradient flow](gradient-flow.md).
