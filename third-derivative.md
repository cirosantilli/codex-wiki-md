# Third derivative

↑ **Parent:** [Second derivative](second-derivative.md)

The [third derivative](third-derivative.md) is the [derivative](derivative.md) of the [second derivative](second-derivative.md). On the interior of a cubic [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) piece it is constant. A [cubic spline](cubic-spline.md) can have different constants on adjacent pieces, so its [third derivative](third-derivative.md) need not be continuous across a [spline knot](spline-knot.md).
