# Galilean invariance of collision elasticity

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic collision](elastic-collision.md)

For fixed particle [masses](mass.md), a [Galilean boost](galilean-transformation.md) by $\mathbf U$ changes total [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) to $K'=K-\mathbf U\cdot\mathbf P+M_TU^2/2$. An isolated collision conserves total [momentum](momentum-split.md) $\mathbf P$ and total nonrelativistic [mass](mass.md) $M_T$, so its final-minus-initial [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) change is independent of the [inertial frame](inertial-frame.md). An [elastic collision](elastic-collision.md) in one such frame is elastic in all of them. Conservation of [momentum](momentum-split.md), rather than invariance of the numerical value of K, is essential to the argument.
