# Dense torus of an affine toric variety

↑ **Parent:** [Affine toric variety](affine-toric-variety.md)

For a strongly convex rational cone $\sigma$, the group generated by $S=\sigma^\vee\cap M$ is $M$. Inverting its finitely many monomial generators gives $k[M]$, so $\operatorname{Spec}k[M]$ is a dense open [algebraic torus](algebraic-torus.md) in $\operatorname{Spec}k[S]$. The map $\chi^m\mapsto\chi^m\otimes\chi^m$ extends torus multiplication to the entire variety.
