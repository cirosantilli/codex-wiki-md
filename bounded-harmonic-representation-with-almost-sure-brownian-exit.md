# Bounded harmonic representation with almost sure Brownian exit

↑ **Parent:** [Brownian representation of the Dirichlet problem](brownian-representation-of-the-dirichlet-problem.md)

If $u$ is a bounded [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md) on a domain $D$, continuous on its closure, and the [Brownian exit time](brownian-exit-time.md) $T_D$ is almost surely finite from $z$, then $u(z)=\mathbb E_z[u(B_{T_D})]$. The [Itô formula](ito-s-lemma.md) and compact localization make $u(B_{t\wedge T_D})$ a bounded [martingale](martingale-split.md). [bounded convergence theorem](bounded-convergence-theorem.md) at the exit time proves the representation even if the domain is unbounded. Removing boundedness of $u$ can invalidate this conclusion through loss of [uniform integrability](uniform-integrability.md).
