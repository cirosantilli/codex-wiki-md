# Inflation and extension of an incompressible tube

↑ **Parent:** [Finite elasticity](finite-elasticity.md)

For reference radii $a_0,b_0$, current inner radius $a$ and uniform axial stretch $z>0$, [incompressibility](incompressible-flow.md) integrates to $r^2=a^2+(R^2-a_0^2)/z$. The [principal stretches](principal-stretch.md) are $r_{,R}=1/(z\lambda)$, $\lambda=r/R$ and $z$. Write $\widehat W(\lambda,z)=W((z\lambda)^{-1},\lambda,z)$ and $\mathcal E=2\pi\int_{a_0}^{b_0}\widehat W R\,dR$ per reference length. [Virtual work](virtual-work.md) gives $p=\mathcal E_{,a}/(2\pi az)$ and the total axial wall load $N_{\rm wall}=\mathcal E_{,z}$, both derivatives holding the other displayed parameter fixed. For pressurized closed ends the independently applied axial load is $N_{\rm ext}=N_{\rm wall}-\pi a^2p$. The distinction follows from the axial [pressure](pressure.md) power $\pi a^2p\dot z$.
