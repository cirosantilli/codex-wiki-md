# Locality loophole

↑ **Parent:** [Bell test](bell-test.md)

The [locality loophole](locality-loophole.md) permits a station's outcome to be influenced by the distant setting through an ordinary signal arriving before readout. [Spacelike separation](spacelike-separation.md) excludes such communication at or below the [speed of light](speed-of-light.md). Fast changes only help if the relevant choice and detection events have the required timing. Predictable switching also leaves open correlations with earlier settings; closing a communication channel does not prove [measurement independence](measurement-independence.md).
