# Norm-one idele group

↑ **Parent:** [Idelic modulus](idelic-modulus.md)

The norm-one idele group is the kernel of the [idelic modulus](idelic-modulus.md). Its quotient by the diagonal $K^\times$ is [compact](compact-space.md) for a [number field](number-field.md) and surjects onto the [ideal class group](ideal-class-group.md).
