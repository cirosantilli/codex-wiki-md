# Determinant of a complex block representation

↑ **Parent:** [Determinant](determinant.md)

The block matrix $M=\begin{pmatrix}A&-B\\B&A\end{pmatrix}$ is similar over $\mathbb C$ to $\operatorname{diag}(A+iB,A-iB)$ via $T=\begin{pmatrix}I&I\\-iI&iI\end{pmatrix}$. Therefore its [determinant](determinant.md) is $\det(A+iB)\det(A-iB)$ for arbitrary square matrices $A,B$. The argument uses a constant [change-of-basis matrix](change-of-basis-matrix.md) and [multiplicativity of the determinant](multiplicativity-of-the-determinant.md).
