# 2023

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the finite element methods for partial differential equations course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Solutions to all three questions of the [finite element methods for partial differential equations course of the University of Oxford](../finite-element-methods-for-partial-differential-equations-course-of-the-university-of-oxford.md) Part C examination, sat on 6 June 2023. Source: [original released PDF, bundle pages 325–328; paper pages 1–4](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/request_for_mathematics_examinat/response/3129786/attach/3/FOI%20202507%20740%20Data.pdf#page=325). The original numbering is retained; question statements are not reproduced.

All [function spaces](../function-space.md) and [bilinear forms](../bilinear-form-split.md) below are real. Write $(p,q)_0=\int_\Omega pq$ for the [L2 inner product](../l2-inner-product.md), and $\|p\|_1^2=\|p\|_0^2+\|\nabla p\|_0^2$ for the first-order [Sobolev norm](../sobolev-norm.md); the notation applies componentwise to [vector fields](../vector-field.md). We use the [Poincaré inequality](../poincare-inequality.md) explicitly allowed by the paper. On an arbitrary unbounded [Lipschitz domain](../lipschitz-domain.md) that inequality would need an additional assumption. The convergence assertions use the usual full [conforming finite element spaces](../conforming-finite-element-space.md), or [vector subspaces](../vector-subspace.md) containing the indicated [interpolants](../interpolant.md); merely being a [vector subspace](../vector-subspace.md) of the stated [piecewise polynomial functions](../piecewise-polynomial-function.md) is insufficient. Part 1(d) explains the intended meaning of positivity for its [nonsymmetric matrix](../nonsymmetric-matrix.md).
