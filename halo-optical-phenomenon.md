# Halo (optical phenomenon)

↑ **Parent:** [Meteorology](meteorology.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halo_(optical_phenomenon))

An atmospheric optical feature produced by interactions with ice crystals. A common $22^\circ$ halo comes from a $60^\circ$ prism path in hexagonal crystals; [minimum deviation](minimum-deviation.md) concentrates rays near its inner angular boundary. This differs from the liquid-water path of a [rainbow](rainbow.md).[Haloes](optical-effects-nature-s-light-show-by-the-met-office.md)
