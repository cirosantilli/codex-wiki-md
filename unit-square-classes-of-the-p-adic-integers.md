# Unit square classes of the p-adic integers

↑ **Parent:** [P-adic unit](p-adic-unit.md)

For odd $p$, a [p-adic unit](p-adic-unit.md) is a square exactly when its residue is a square, by the [Hensel lemma](hensel-s-lemma.md). Its unit square-class group is $C_2$. For $p=2$, a unit is a square exactly when it is congruent to one modulo eight, by the [strong form of Hensel lemma](strong-form-of-hensel-lemma.md). The four [square classes](square-class.md) are represented by $1,3,5,7$, giving $C_2\times C_2$.
