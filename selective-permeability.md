# Selective permeability

↑ **Parent:** [Cell membrane](cell-membrane-split.md)

[Selective permeability](selective-permeability.md) means that a [cell membrane](cell-membrane-split.md) permits different substances to pass at different rates under comparable conditions. Both the [lipid bilayer](lipid-bilayer.md) and the available [membrane proteins](membrane-protein.md) contribute to this selection.

Artificial boundaries can also have [selective permeability](selective-permeability.md). For [osmosis](osmosis.md), specify which [solutes](solute.md) cross and over what observation interval: a boundary that retains one [solute](solute.md) need not retain another. A [semipermeable membrane](semipermeable-membrane.md) is the ideal solvent-only boundary used in the [osmotic pressure](osmotic-pressure.md) calculation.
