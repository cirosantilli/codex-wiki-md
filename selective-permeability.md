# Selective permeability

↑ **Parent:** [Cell membrane](cell-membrane.md)

[Selective permeability](selective-permeability.md) means that a [cell membrane](cell-membrane.md) permits different substances to pass at different rates under comparable conditions. Both the [lipid bilayer](lipid-bilayer.md) and the available [membrane proteins](membrane-protein.md) contribute to this selection.
