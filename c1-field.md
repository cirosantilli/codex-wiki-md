# C1 field

↑ **Parent:** [Ci field](ci-field.md)

A $C_1$ field is a [Ci field](ci-field.md) with the bound $N>d$. The [Chevalley-Warning theorem](chevalley-warning-theorem.md) gives finite-field examples; the [Tsen theorem](tsen-theorem.md) gives function fields of curves over algebraically closed fields. A $C_1$ field has trivial [Brauer group](brauer-group.md).
