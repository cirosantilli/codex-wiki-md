# Adjustment-coefficient lower bound for NBUE claims

↑ **Parent:** [New better than used in expectation](new-better-than-used-in-expectation.md)

For [NBUE](new-better-than-used-in-expectation.md) claims in the [classical risk model](classical-risk-model.md), the equilibrium claim size $Y$ is stochastically bounded by $X$. The positive exponential [tail integral formula for moments](tail-integral-formula-for-moments.md) gives $M_Y(R)\le M_X(R)$. Since $M_Y(R)=1+\theta$ and $M_X(R)=1+(1+\theta)\mu R$, rearrangement proves the displayed bound.
