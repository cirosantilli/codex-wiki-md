# Small oscillation of a planar gas bubble in an annular liquid

↑ **Parent:** [Pressure in radially symmetric annular potential flow](pressure-in-radially-symmetric-annular-potential-flow.md)

For $a=a_0(1+\epsilon)$, equilibrium outer radius $b_0$, and a bubble gas obeying $p_0\pi a^2=\text{constant}$, the [linearization](linearization.md) is

$$
\rho a_0^2\log\frac{b_0}{a_0}\,\ddot\epsilon
=-2p_\infty\epsilon.
$$

The bubble therefore undergoes [simple harmonic motion](simple-harmonic-motion.md) with

$$
\omega^2=\frac{2p_\infty}{\rho a_0^2\log(b_0/a_0)}.
$$
