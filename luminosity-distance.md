# Luminosity distance

↑ **Parent:** [Cosmology](cosmology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luminosity_distance)

Luminosity distance is defined by $F=L/(4\pi d_L^2)$ and includes geometric dilution, photon redshift, and arrival-rate dilation.
