# Complete measure

↑ **Parent:** [Measure](measure.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complete_measure)

A [measure](measure.md) is complete when every [subset](subset.md) of each [null set](null-set.md) is a [measurable set](measurable-set.md). Such a subset also has measure zero by monotonicity. The [completion of a measure](completion-of-a-measure.md) extends it to a complete measure by adjoining all subsets of measurable null sets to its [sigma-algebra](sigma-algebra.md).
