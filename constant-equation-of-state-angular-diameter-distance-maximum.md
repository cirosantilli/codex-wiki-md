# Constant-equation-of-state angular diameter distance maximum

↑ **Parent:** [Angular diameter distance turnover](angular-diameter-distance-turnover.md)

For a flat universe with a single constant [equation-of-state parameter](equation-of-state-parameter.md) $w>-1$, $H(z)=H_0(1+z)^\alpha$. Its [angular diameter distance](angular-diameter-distance.md) is $H_0^{-1}[(1+z)^{-\alpha}-(1+z)^{-1}]/(1-\alpha)$, with logarithmic limit $H_0^{-1}\log(1+z)/(1+z)$ at $\alpha=1$. Differentiation gives the displayed unique maximum, interpreted as $z_m=e-1$ at $\alpha=1$. The distance vanishes at both endpoints $z=0$ and $z\to\infty$. As $w\downarrow-1$, the maximum moves to infinite [cosmological redshift](cosmological-redshift.md).
