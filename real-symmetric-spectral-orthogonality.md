# Real symmetric spectral orthogonality

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md)

A real [symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md) is a [Hermitian matrix](hermitian-operator.md), so $u^*Au$ is real for every complex vector $u$. If $u$ is an [eigenvector](eigenvector.md), its [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) is $(u^*Au)/(u^*u)$ and is real. Then $u^*Av=(Au)^*v$ gives $(\mu-\lambda)u^*v=0$. Reality is used in changing conjugate transpose to transpose; complex symmetry alone does not imply real eigenvalues.
