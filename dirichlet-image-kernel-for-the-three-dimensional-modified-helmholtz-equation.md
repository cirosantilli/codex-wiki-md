# Dirichlet image kernel for the three-dimensional modified Helmholtz equation

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet Green function](dirichlet-green-function.md)

For $\kappa\geq0$ in the upper half-space, let $r'^*$ be the reflection of the interior source $r'$ across its boundary plane. The displayed decaying [Green function](green-s-function-split.md) solves $(\Delta-\kappa^2)G_D=\delta(r-r')$ with zero [Dirichlet boundary condition](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md). The direct term supplies the source; the image source lies outside the domain and its opposite sign makes the two terms cancel on the plane. The minus sign relative to the positive [static Yukawa Green function](static-yukawa-green-function.md) comes from the operator convention. The case $\kappa=0$ is the Coulomb limit. An infinity condition is needed for uniqueness on an unbounded domain.
