# Sphere surgery preserving nontrivial complementary pieces

↑ **Parent:** [Punctured three-sphere](punctured-three-sphere.md)

Suppose disjoint separating [spheres](sphere.md) cut a [3-manifold](3-manifold.md) into pieces none of which is a [punctured three-sphere](punctured-three-sphere.md). Compress one [sphere](sphere.md) along a [disk](disk-mathematics.md) disjoint from the others. The old [sphere](sphere.md) and the two new [spheres](sphere.md) enclose a three-times punctured [sphere](sphere.md). At least one of the two pieces on the compressed side is not a punctured [sphere](sphere.md); retain that new [sphere](sphere.md) and discard the other. The piece on its opposite side cannot become a punctured [sphere](sphere.md), since capping its boundaries and applying the [Three-dimensional Schoenflies theorem](three-dimensional-schoenflies-theorem.md) would make the original opposite piece a punctured [sphere](sphere.md) as well. Hence the number of [spheres](sphere.md) and the nontriviality condition can be preserved.
