# Distribution (differential geometry)

↑ **Parent:** [Differential geometry](differential-geometry-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distribution_(differential_geometry))

A rank-$k$ distribution on a [smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md) is a rank-$k$ [vector subbundle](vector-subbundle.md) of its [tangent bundle](tangent-bundle.md). It is involutive when the [Lie bracket of vector fields](lie-bracket-of-vector-fields.md) of any two local sections is again a section.
