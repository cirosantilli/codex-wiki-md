# Exponential-rate maximum-likelihood estimator

↑ **Parent:** [Maximum-likelihood estimator](maximum-likelihood-estimator.md)

For independent observations from the [exponential distribution](exponential-distribution.md) with rate $\theta$, the log-likelihood is $n\log\theta-\theta\sum_iX_i$, so

$$
\widehat\theta=\frac n{\sum_iX_i}=\frac1{\overline X}.
$$

The [central limit theorem](central-limit-theorem.md) and [delta method](delta-method.md) give

$$
\sqrt n(\widehat\theta-\theta)\xrightarrow dN(0,\theta^2).
$$
