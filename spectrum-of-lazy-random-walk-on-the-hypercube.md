# Spectrum of lazy random walk on the hypercube

↑ **Parent:** [Simple random walk on the hypercube](simple-random-walk-on-the-hypercube.md)

For [random walk](random-walk.md) on $\{-1,1\}^n$ that chooses a coordinate uniformly and flips it with probability $1/2$, the [Walsh characters](walsh-character.md) $f_J(x)=\prod_{j\in J}x_j$ form an [orthonormal eigenbasis](orthonormal-eigenbasis.md) under the [uniform distribution on a finite set](discrete-uniform-distribution.md). Flipping a coordinate in $J$ negates $f_J$, so $Pf_J=(1-|J|/n)f_J$. Hence $1-k/n$ has [multiplicity](multiplicity-mathematics.md) $\binom nk$, and the ordinary and absolute [spectral gaps](spectral-gap.md) both equal $1/n$.
