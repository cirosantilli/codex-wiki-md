# Gravitational-wave energy flux

↑ **Parent:** [Gravitational wave](gravitational-wave.md)

In the radiation zone, the time-averaged energy flux of a weak wave in [transverse-traceless gauge](transverse-traceless-gauge.md) is

$$
\frac{dP}{d\Omega}
=\frac{c^3r^2}{32\pi G}
\left\langle\dot h^{\rm TT}_{ij}\dot h^{\rm TT}_{ij}\right\rangle.
$$

Combining this with the far-zone quadrupole field and integrating over directions gives the [quadrupole formula](quadrupole-formula.md).
