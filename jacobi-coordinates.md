# Jacobi coordinates

↑ **Parent:** [Center-of-mass reduction](center-of-mass-reduction.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacobi_coordinates)

For particles with masses $m_j$ and positions $r_j$, [Jacobi coordinates](jacobi-coordinates.md) consist of the total [center of mass](center-of-mass.md) and successive relative coordinates $\rho_j=r_{j+1}-(\sum_{l=1}^j m_l r_l)/(\sum_{l=1}^j m_l)$. They separate translation from internal motion and diagonalize the [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) with the corresponding reduced masses.
