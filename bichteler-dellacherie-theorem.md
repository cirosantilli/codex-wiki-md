# Bichteler-Dellacherie theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Semimartingale](semimartingale.md)

An adapted [càdlàg process](cadlag-process.md) is a [semimartingale](semimartingale.md) exactly when it is a good integrator: on each finite horizon, for every sequence of bounded [elementary predictable processes with stopping-time intervals](elementary-predictable-process-with-stopping-time-intervals.md) $H^n$ whose deterministic uniform bounds tend to zero, the elementary terminal integrals $(H^n\mathbin\cdot X)_T$ tend to zero in [probability](probability.md). The elementary integral is a finite sum of measurable coefficients times subsequent increments; the characterization says precisely when this operation extends continuously to stochastic integration. The theorem is used here as a standard characterization, not proved from first principles.
