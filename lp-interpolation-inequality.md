# Lp interpolation inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Lp space](lp-space.md)

If $1\leq p,r\leq\infty$, $0\leq\theta\leq1$ and $1/q=\theta/p+(1-\theta)/r$, then the displayed bound follows by applying the [Holder inequality](holder-inequality.md) to $|u|^{\theta q}|u|^{(1-\theta)q}$. Interpret the endpoint cases using the essential supremum. In particular convergence in [Lp space](lp-space.md) and boundedness in a larger-exponent [Lp space](lp-space.md) imply convergence at intermediate exponents.
