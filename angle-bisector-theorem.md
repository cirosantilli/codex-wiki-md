# Angle bisector theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Triangle](triangle.md)

An internal [angle bisector](angle-bisector.md) of a [triangle](triangle.md) divides the opposite side in the ratio of the two adjacent sides. If $AD$ bisects the [angle](angle.md) at $A$ in [triangle](triangle.md) $ABC$, then $BD/DC=AB/AC$. Comparing the [areas](surface-area.md) of [triangles](triangle.md) $ABD$ and $ACD$ proves this: their shared altitude gives the ratio $BD/DC$, while their equal included [angles](angle.md) give $AB/AC$.
