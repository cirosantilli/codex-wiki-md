# Smooth periodic partition of unity

↑ **Parent:** [Partition of unity](partition-of-unity.md)

For each $T>0$ there is a nonnegative [test function](test-function.md) $\Psi$ with the displayed identity. Choose a nonnegative [bump function](bump-function.md) $\eta$ supported in $[-T,T]$ and positive on $[-T/2,T/2]$. The sum $S(x)=\sum_k\eta(x+kT)$ is a positive, [smooth](smooth-function.md), $T$ [periodic function](periodic-function.md). Setting $\Psi=\eta/S$ preserves [compact support](compact-support.md) and smoothness, and periodicity of $S$ proves $\sum_k\Psi(x+kT)=S(x)/S(x)=1$. The translates form a locally finite [partition of unity](partition-of-unity.md) on the real line.
