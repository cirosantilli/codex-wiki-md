# Tree scattering with pseudoscalar exchange

↑ **Parent:** [Tree scattering in Yukawa theory](tree-scattering-in-yukawa-theory.md)

The [pseudoscalar Yukawa interaction](pseudoscalar-yukawa-interaction.md) with coefficient $-g$ has the displayed [Feynman vertex](interaction-vertex.md). Its two-fermion [tree-level Feynman diagrams](tree-level-feynman-diagram.md) are the $t$ and $u$ exchanges; antisymmetry of the final identical fermions requires their relative minus. Fermion-antifermion scattering has $t$ exchange and $s$ annihilation, again with a relative minus. With initial and final states ordered as $b_1^\dagger d_2^\dagger|0\rangle$ and $b_3^\dagger d_4^\dagger|0\rangle$, the normal-ordered current contributes $-d^\dagger d\,\bar v\gamma^5v$ in the exchange channel but $db$ and $b^\dagger d^\dagger$ in the annihilation channel. These [fermionic signs](fermionic-sign.md) fix the overall sign as well as their interference; changing an external-state phase changes both terms together.
