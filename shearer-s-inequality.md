<h1 id="shearer-s-inequality">Shearer's inequality</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Entropy submodularity](entropy-submodularity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shearer's_inequality)

Let $X=(X_1,\ldots,X_n)$ be a discrete [random vector](random-vector.md), and let $\mathcal S$ be a collection of subsets of $[n]$ in which every index occurs at least $r$ times. Then

$$
rH(X)\leq\sum_{S\in\mathcal S}H(X_S).
$$

Apply the [chain rule for information entropy](chain-rule-for-information-entropy.md) to each projection $X_S$ and use [conditioning reduces entropy](conditioning-reduces-entropy.md) to compare every term with the corresponding conditional entropy in the full chain rule.
