# Spin connection of a conformally flat metric

↑ **Parent:** [Spin connection](spin-connection.md)

For $g_{ab}=\Omega^2\eta_{ab}$, use the [vierbein](orthonormal-coframe-in-spacetime.md) $e^\mu{}_a=\Omega\delta^\mu{}_a$ and constant flat [gamma matrices](gamma-matrices.md). The [tetrad postulate](tetrad-postulate.md) and $\Gamma^c{}_{ab}=\delta^c_a\partial_b\log\Omega+\delta^c_b\partial_a\log\Omega-\eta_{ab}\eta^{cd}\partial_d\log\Omega$ give the displayed lowered [spin connection](spin-connection.md). With $L=\widehat\gamma^c\partial_c\log\Omega$, its spinor action is $\nabla_a=\partial_a+[\widehat\gamma_a,L]/4$. The Lorentz index lowering matters in indefinite signature.
