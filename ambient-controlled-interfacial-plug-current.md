# Ambient-controlled interfacial plug current

↑ **Parent:** [Interfacial viscous gravity current](interfacial-viscous-gravity-current.md)

The gravity force per interfacial area is $\Delta\rho gH^2/R$, while ambient viscous [traction](traction.md) is $\mu u/R$. Their balance gives the radial velocity scale independently of current [viscosity](dynamic-viscosity.md) within the appropriate plug-flow window.
