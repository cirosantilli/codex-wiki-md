# Translational symmetry parallel to a plane

↑ **Parent:** [Translational symmetry](translational-symmetry.md)

A scalar field unchanged by every [translation](translation-geometry.md) parallel to a specified [plane](plane.md) depends only on the coordinate normal to that plane. For [electrostatics](electrostatics-split.md) between ideal infinite parallel plates, this reduces [Laplace's equation](laplace-equation.md) to $d^2V/dz^2=0$. Finite plate edges break the exact symmetry and produce [fringing field](fringing-field.md).
