# Graph transpose

↑ **Parent:** [Directed graph](directed-graph.md)

The [directed graph](directed-graph.md) obtained by reversing every [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) while retaining the same [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md). Scanning [adjacency lists](adjacency-list.md) constructs the [graph transpose](graph-transpose.md) in $O(|V|+|E|)$ time. A path reverses direction, while mutual reachability and therefore [strongly connected components](strongly-connected-component.md) are unchanged.
