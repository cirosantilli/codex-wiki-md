# Griffiths transversality

↑ **Parent:** [Variation of Hodge structure](variation-of-hodge-structure.md)

In a [variation of Hodge structure](variation-of-hodge-structure.md), differentiation lowers the filtration index by at most one. In the relative de Rham construction, an absolute form of degree at least $p$ contributes, after extracting one base differential, a relative form of degree at least $p-1$. This filtration shift proves the condition for geometric variations.
