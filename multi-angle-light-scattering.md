# Multi-angle light scattering

↑ **Parent:** [Scattering of light](scattering-of-light.md)

[multi-angle light scattering](multi-angle-light-scattering.md) measures scattered light at multiple angles to infer molecular size and, with appropriate [concentration](concentration.md) and optical-contrast information, molar mass. Coupling it to [size-exclusion chromatography](size-exclusion-chromatography.md) helps distinguish eluting assemblies, although unresolved mixtures complicate inference.
