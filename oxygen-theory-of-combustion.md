# Oxygen theory of combustion

↑ **Parent:** [Combustion](combustion.md)

The historical explanation that ordinary [combustion](combustion.md) and metallic calcination involve combination with [oxygen](oxygen.md), rather than release of [phlogiston](phlogiston.md). It should not be extended into the false claim that every [chemical acid](acid-chemistry.md) contains [oxygen](oxygen.md).
