# Fault strike

↑ **Parent:** [Fault plane](fault-plane.md)

The [fault strike](fault-strike.md) is the azimuth of the intersection of a [fault plane](fault-plane.md) with a horizontal plane. A right-hand convention chooses the along-strike direction for which the fault dips to the observer's right.
