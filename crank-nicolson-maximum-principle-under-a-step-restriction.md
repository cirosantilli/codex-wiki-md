# Crank-Nicolson maximum principle under a step restriction

↑ **Parent:** [Crank-Nicolson diffusion scheme](crank-nicolson-diffusion-scheme.md)

For the two-dimensional [heat equation](heat-equation.md) with zero [Dirichlet boundary conditions](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md), the [Crank-Nicolson diffusion scheme](crank-nicolson-diffusion-scheme.md) preserves the interval between the minimum and maximum of the initial nodal values and zero under the displayed restriction. The old-time coefficients form a [convex combination](convex-combination.md), while the new-time matrix has positive diagonal, nonpositive off-diagonal entries and row diagonal-dominance margin one. A hypothetical new value above the old maximum makes the new-time row at least that value while its right side is at most the old maximum, a contradiction; apply the reversed inequality at a new minimum. In particular initial nonnegative or nonpositive values preserve their sign. The step restriction guarantees these value bounds even though scalar [A-stability](a-stability.md) holds without it.
