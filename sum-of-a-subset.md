# Sum of a subset

↑ **Parent:** [Sumset](sumset.md)

Given numbers $s_1,\ldots,s_n$, the sum associated with a subset $A$ of indices is $\sum_{i\in A}s_i$. The empty subset gives zero. The set of all these values is the [sumset](sumset.md) $\{0,s_1\}+\cdots+\{0,s_n\}$.
