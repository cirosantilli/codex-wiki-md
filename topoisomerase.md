# Topoisomerase

↑ **Parent:** [Enzyme](enzyme-split.md)

An [enzyme](enzyme-split.md) that changes nucleic-acid topology by transient strand cleavage and resealing. Activity on [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) can change [DNA supercoiling](dna-supercoiling.md).
