# Proton nuclear magnetic resonance

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear magnetic resonance](nuclear-magnetic-resonance-split.md)

[Proton nuclear magnetic resonance](proton-nuclear-magnetic-resonance.md) measures resonances of hydrogen-1 nuclei. Structural signal counts require an explicit [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md) and exchange-timescale model; exchangeable N–H or O–H signals can be broadened or lost.
