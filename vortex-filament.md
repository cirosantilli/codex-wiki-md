# Vortex filament

↑ **Parent:** [Vortex tube](vortex-tube.md)

A [vortex filament](vortex-filament.md) is the ideal thin-core limit of a [vortex tube](vortex-tube.md), concentrating a finite [circulation](circulation-physics.md) on a curve. In an unbounded incompressible [fluid](fluid.md) with [velocity](velocity.md) tending to zero at infinity, its induced [velocity field](velocity-field.md) follows the [vorticity](vorticity-split.md) version of the [Biot-Savart law](biot-savart-law.md). The ideal field is singular on the filament; finite-core models are needed there. A closed circular filament is a thin [vortex ring](vortex-ring.md).
