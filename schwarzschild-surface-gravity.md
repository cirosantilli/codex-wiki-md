# Schwarzschild surface gravity

↑ **Parent:** [Surface gravity](surface-gravity.md)

For a [Schwarzschild black hole](schwarzschild-spacetime.md) of [mass](mass.md) $M$ in [geometrized units](geometrized-units.md), $f=1-2M/r$ gives $\kappa=f\prime(2M)/2=1/(4M)$. The [Killing vector field](killing-vector-field.md) is normalized at infinity; changing that normalization changes $\kappa$.
