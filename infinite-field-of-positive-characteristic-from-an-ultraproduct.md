# Infinite field of positive characteristic from an ultraproduct

↑ **Parent:** [Łoś theorem](los-theorem.md)

For a [prime number](prime-number.md) $p$, a [nonprincipal ultrafilter](nonprincipal-ultrafilter.md) on $\mathbb N$ gives

$$
K=\prod_{n\geq1}\mathbb F_{p^n}/\mathcal U.
$$

The [Łoś theorem](los-theorem.md) makes $K$ a [field](field.md) of [characteristic](characteristic-of-a-field.md) $p$. For every natural number $m$, almost all factors have at least $m$ elements, so $K$ is infinite.
