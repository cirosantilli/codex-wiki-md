# Galois closure of a field extension

↑ **Parent:** [Galois theory](galois-theory-split.md)

The Galois closure of a finite separable extension $L/K$ is the smallest [Galois extension](finite-galois-extension.md) in a fixed separable closure containing $L$. It is generated by all the conjugates of $L$. If $G=\operatorname{Gal}(M/K)$ and $H=\operatorname{Gal}(M/L)$, their [subgroup core](core-group-theory.md) is trivial. This is distinct from the existing group-theoretic [normal closure](normal-closure.md).
