# Area formula (geometric measure theory)

↑ **Parent:** [Change of variables formula](change-of-variables-formula.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Area_formula_(geometric_measure_theory))

For a [locally Lipschitz function](locally-lipschitz-function.md) $T$ between Euclidean spaces of the same dimension, the area formula integrates its absolute [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md) against the multiplicity of its fibers. In particular, for an injective $C^1$ map $T:X\to\mathbb R^d$ and nonnegative measurable $a$,

$$
\int_X a(T(x))|\det DT(x)|\,dx=\int_{T(X)}a(y)\,dy.
$$

This injective version extends the [change of variables formula](change-of-variables-formula.md) to maps with a possibly singular derivative.
