# Proportional allocation contest

↑ **Parent:** [Contest theory](contest-theory.md)

For positive total effort, a proportional allocation contest awards player $i$ the prize with [probability](probability.md) $b_i/\sum_j b_j$, or gives it that proportion of a divisible prize. A convention is needed at zero total effort. This allocation is different from the discontinuous highest-effort rule of an [all-pay auction](all-pay-auction.md).
