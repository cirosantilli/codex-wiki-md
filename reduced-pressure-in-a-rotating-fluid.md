# Reduced pressure in a rotating fluid

↑ **Parent:** [Equation of motion in a rotating frame](equation-of-motion-in-a-rotating-frame.md)

For constant [density](density.md) and a [conservative force](conservative-force.md) per mass $-\nabla\Phi$, gravity and centrifugal acceleration can be incorporated into the displayed reduced pressure. The resulting [Euler equations](euler-equations-for-an-inviscid-fluid-split.md) in rotating axes are $\mathbf u_t+(\mathbf u\cdot\nabla)\mathbf u+2\boldsymbol\Omega\times\mathbf u=-\nabla P/\rho$. The sign of the centrifugal contribution follows from $\boldsymbol\Omega\times(\boldsymbol\Omega\times\mathbf x)=-\nabla|\boldsymbol\Omega\times\mathbf x|^2/2$. Constant density is what permits combination into one pressure gradient without extra density-gradient terms.
