# Axisymmetric vertical mode of a shearing sheet

↑ **Parent:** [Keplerian shearing sheet](keplerian-shearing-sheet.md)

A horizontal-velocity perturbation depending only on height has zero vertical velocity for nonzero [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) under [incompressibility](incompressible-flow.md). Its [pressure](pressure.md) amplitude then vanishes in the unstratified vertical momentum equation.
