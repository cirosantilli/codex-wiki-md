# H-infinity space

↑ **Parent:** [Hardy space](hardy-space.md)

This [Hardy space](hardy-space.md) consists of bounded [holomorphic functions](holomorphic-function.md) on the [unit disc](unit-disc.md), with norm $\|f\|_\infty=\sup_{z\in\mathbb D}|f(z)|$. Its almost-everywhere boundary realization follows from the [Fatou theorem for bounded holomorphic functions](fatou-theorem-for-bounded-holomorphic-functions.md).
