# Conformal boundary

↑ **Parent:** [Conformal compactification](conformal-compactification.md)

A boundary obtained by extending the rescaled metric $\widetilde g=\Omega^2g$ smoothly and nondegenerately to a hypersurface on which $\Omega$ vanishes with nonzero derivative. Its causal character is measured using the extended [Lorentzian metric](lorentzian-metric.md), not the divergent physical metric. In signature $(+---)$, a timelike normal makes the boundary spacelike, a spacelike normal makes it timelike, and a null normal makes it null. These cases lead to different causal and initial-value properties. The [conformal cylinder of two-dimensional de Sitter spacetime](conformal-cylinder-of-two-dimensional-de-sitter-spacetime.md) has spacelike past/future boundaries; the [conformal strip of two-dimensional anti-de Sitter spacetime](conformal-strip-of-two-dimensional-anti-de-sitter-spacetime.md) has two timelike boundaries.
