# A superficially convergent graph can have a divergent subgraph

↑ **Parent:** [Superficial degree of divergence](superficial-degree-of-divergence.md)

In massive four-dimensional [phi-fourth theory](quartic-interaction.md), a six-point triangle is [one-particle irreducible](one-particle-irreducible-feynman-diagram.md) and has superficial degree $-2$. Insert a [tadpole diagram](tadpole-diagram.md) self-energy into one of its internal lines. The resulting graph is still one-particle irreducible and still has degree $-2$, but its tadpole loop diverges. Recursive local [counterterms](counterterm.md) remove the subgraph divergence before overall power counting is applied. Convergence requires attention to all ultraviolet subgraphs, not merely to simultaneous scaling of all loop momenta.
