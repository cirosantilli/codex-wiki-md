# Full ternary tree

↑ **Parent:** [Rooted tree](rooted-tree.md)

A [rooted tree](rooted-tree.md) in which every internal [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) has exactly three [tree children](child-in-a-rooted-tree.md). For a finite [rooted tree](rooted-tree.md) with $m$ [tree leaves](leaf-of-a-tree.md) and internal [outdegrees](outdegree.md) $d_v$, counting [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) in two ways gives

$$
m=1+\sum_{v\text{ internal}}(d_v-1).
$$

Indeed, the number of [edges](edge-of-a-graph.md) is the total number of [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) minus one and also the sum of the internal [outdegrees](outdegree.md). Thus a full ternary tree with $N$ internal [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) has $m=1+2N$ [tree leaves](leaf-of-a-tree.md), always an [odd number](odd-number.md). More generally, if every internal [vertex](vertex-graph-theory.md) has either two or three [tree children](child-in-a-rooted-tree.md), $m=1+N_2+2N_3$. This [parity](parity.md) controls the initial merge in [ternary Huffman coding](ternary-huffman-coding.md).
