# Arc complex

↑ **Parent:** [Mapping class group](mapping-class-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arc_complex)

The vertices of the arc complex of a punctured surface $S$ are isotopy classes of unoriented essential simple proper arcs. A finite set spans a simplex when its classes have representatives with pairwise disjoint interiors. The [mapping class group](mapping-class-group.md) acts on the complex by simplicial automorphisms.
