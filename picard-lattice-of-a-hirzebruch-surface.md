# Picard lattice of a Hirzebruch surface

↑ **Parent:** [Intersection pairing on the Picard group of a surface](intersection-pairing-on-the-picard-group-of-a-surface.md)

For the [Hirzebruch surface](hirzebruch-surface.md) $\mathbb F_n$, the [negative section of a Hirzebruch surface](negative-section-of-a-hirzebruch-surface.md) $S$ and a [fiber class of a Hirzebruch surface](fiber-class-of-a-hirzebruch-surface.md) $F$ freely generate $\operatorname{Pic}(\mathbb F_n)$ and satisfy

$$
S^2=-n,\qquad S\cdot F=1,\qquad F^2=0.
$$
