# Slab dehydration

↑ **Parent:** [Subduction](subduction.md)

Release of [water](water.md)-rich fluid by reactions that destabilize hydrated [minerals](mineral.md) in a descending [slab](slab.md). It can promote [flux melting](flux-melting.md) of the [mantle wedge](mantle-wedge.md) and contribute to [dehydration embrittlement](dehydration-embrittlement.md). The released [water](water.md) is not evidence that the whole [slab](slab.md) has melted.
