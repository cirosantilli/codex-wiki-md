# Z-channel

↑ **Parent:** [Discrete memoryless channel](discrete-memoryless-channel.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Z-channel)

A Z-channel transmits one binary input without error while the other can change into the first. Unlike a [binary symmetric channel](binary-symmetric-channel.md), its errors are one-sided.
