# Symmetric double-delta potential

↑ **Parent:** [Multiple delta potential](multiple-delta-potential.md)

The symmetric double-delta potential

$$
V(x)=\frac{\hbar^2U_0}{2m}\bigl(\delta(x-a)+\delta(x+a)\bigr)
$$

separates into even and odd [parity](parity.md) channels. At either interaction, the [wavefunction](wave-function.md) is continuous and its derivative has the jump

$$
\psi'(a^+)-\psi'(a^-)=U_0\psi(a).
$$
