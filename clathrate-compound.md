# Clathrate compound

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical compound](chemical-compound.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clathrate_compound)

A clathrate compound has a host structure with cavities containing guest atoms or molecules. The host and guest motions can have very different stiffnesses and characteristic frequencies. Cage-like germanium frameworks with metal guests provide examples with low-energy [rattling modes](rattling-mode.md).
