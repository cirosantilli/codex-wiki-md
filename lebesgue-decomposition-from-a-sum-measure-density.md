# Lebesgue decomposition from a sum-measure density

↑ **Parent:** [Lebesgue decomposition theorem](lebesgue-decomposition-theorem.md)

Use the [Hilbert-space construction of dominated measure densities](hilbert-space-construction-of-dominated-measure-densities.md) with $\rho=\mu+\nu$. The set $B=\{h=1\}$ is a [null set](null-set.md) for $\mu$. Put $f=0$ on $B$ and $f=h/(1-h)$ elsewhere. Then $\int f\,d\mu=\nu(B^c)<\infty$ and $\nu(A)=\int_Af\,d\mu+\nu(A\cap B)$. This is the [Lebesgue decomposition](lebesgue-decomposition-theorem.md) into a density part and a part concentrated on a $\mu$-[null set](null-set.md).
