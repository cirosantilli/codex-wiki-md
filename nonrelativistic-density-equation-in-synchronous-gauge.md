# Nonrelativistic density equation in synchronous gauge

↑ **Parent:** [Jeans instability](jeans-instability.md)

For a matter-dominated fluid with constant $c_s^2=w\ll1$, combine [stress-energy conservation](stress-energy-conservation.md) in synchronous gauge with the scalar trace Einstein equation. Discard pressure corrections to the background gravitational and velocity-damping terms while retaining the potentially large pressure-gradient term $wk^2\delta$. This produces the displayed leading nonrelativistic equation. It is not an exact equation at finite $w$. At negligible pressure the growing and decaying matter-era solutions are $\tau^2$ and $\tau^{-3}$.
