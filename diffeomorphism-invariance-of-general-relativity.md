# Diffeomorphism invariance of general relativity

↑ **Parent:** [General relativity](general-relativity-split.md)

Transforming a [metric tensor](metric-tensor.md) and all matter fields together by the pullback of a [diffeomorphism](diffeomorphism.md) preserves the [Einstein field equations](einstein-field-equations.md) and covariant matter equations. Passive coordinate changes only change the description of the fields; active pullbacks produce another representative of the geometry. The treatment of transformations acting on a boundary depends on the boundary conditions and possible charges. A [diffeomorphism](diffeomorphism.md) is not necessarily an [isometry](isometry.md) of a fixed metric.
