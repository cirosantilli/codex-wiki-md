# p-adic unit

↑ **Parent:** [Local field](local-field.md)

A [p-adic unit](p-adic-unit.md) is an invertible element of the [ring](ring.md) $\mathbb Z_p$. For a [p-adic integer](p-adic-integer.md) $x$, this is equivalent to $v_p(x)=0$, $|x|_p=1$, or a nonzero initial [p-adic digit](p-adic-digit.md) $b_0$. A [rational number](rational-number.md) $a/b$ is a [p-adic unit](p-adic-unit.md) when neither its reduced [numerator](numerator.md) nor its [denominator](denominator.md) is divisible by $p$.

Thus $3$ and $1/3$ are [p-adic units](p-adic-unit.md) at $p=2$, while $2$ is a [p-adic integer](p-adic-integer.md) with no inverse inside $\mathbb Z_2$. The [modular inversion converges to a p-adic inverse](modular-inversion-converges-to-a-p-adic-inverse.md) construction computes the inverse of an integer unit to arbitrary finite precision.
