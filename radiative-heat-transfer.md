# Radiative heat transfer

↑ **Parent:** [Heat transfer](heat-transfer.md)

Transfer of [energy](energy-split.md) by [thermal radiation](thermal-radiation.md). A diffuse grey surface of [area](surface-area.md) $A$ and [emissivity](emissivity.md) $\epsilon$ completely enclosed by isothermal black surroundings has net outward [power](power.md) $\epsilon\sigma A(T_s^4-T_e^4)$. This subtracts absorbed environmental radiation from emitted radiation. Other geometries require exchange factors; a gross emitted [power](power.md) is not a net cooling rate.
