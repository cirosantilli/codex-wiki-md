# Covariant derivative of a covariant tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Covariant derivative](covariant-derivative.md)

For $W_{\mu\nu}$,

$$
\nabla_\alpha W_{\mu\nu}
=\partial_\alpha W_{\mu\nu}
-\Gamma^\rho_{\alpha\mu}W_{\rho\nu}
-\Gamma^\rho_{\alpha\nu}W_{\mu\rho}.
$$
