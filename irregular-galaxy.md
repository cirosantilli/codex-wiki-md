# Irregular galaxy

↑ **Parent:** [Galaxy](galaxy-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irregular_galaxy)

An [irregular galaxy](irregular-galaxy.md) lacks a regular spiral or elliptical outline. Many contain substantial gas and recent [star formation](star-formation.md); irregular appearance alone does not determine a [stellar population](stellar-population.md)'s age.
