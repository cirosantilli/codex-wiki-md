# Relativistic collision mass threshold

↑ **Parent:** [Four-momentum conservation](four-momentum-conservation.md)

If the total incoming [four-momentum](four-momentum.md) is timelike and has [centre-of-momentum energy](centre-of-momentum-energy.md) $W$, production of particles with [rest masses](invariant-mass.md) $\mu_i$ requires $W\geq\sum_i\mu_ic^2$. In the [centre-of-momentum frame](center-of-momentum-frame.md), total final energy is a sum of $\sqrt{\mu_i^2c^4+c^2|\mathbf p_i|^2}$, each at least its rest energy. For massive products equality occurs with all at rest in that frame. A single product requires equality of the entire invariant mass, not just an inequality; a massless product cannot have nonzero energy and be at rest.
