# Order of integration

↑ **Parent:** [Iterated integral](iterated-integral.md)

The sequence in which variables are integrated in an [iterated integral](iterated-integral.md). Reversing the order changes the slicing of the [domain of integration](domain-of-integration.md), often requiring more than one integral. [Fubini's theorem](fubini-s-theorem.md) permits interchange for absolutely integrable functions, while [Tonelli's theorem](tonelli-theorem.md) permits nonnegative measurable integrands even when the integral is infinite. Conditional convergence requires additional care; geometric reversal alone is not a general justification for interchanging improper integrals.
