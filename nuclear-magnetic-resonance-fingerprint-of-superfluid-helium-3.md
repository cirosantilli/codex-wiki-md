# Nuclear magnetic resonance fingerprint of superfluid helium-3

↑ **Parent:** [Helium-3 superfluidity](helium-3-superfluidity.md)

Transverse [nuclear magnetic resonance](nuclear-magnetic-resonance-split.md) measures absorption from a radio-frequency [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) perpendicular to the static field. In the A phase, the resonance moved above the [Larmor frequency](larmor-frequency.md); in the B phase it returned to that frequency despite the changed state. [Anthony James Leggett](anthony-james-leggett.md) connected the shift to collective spin-orbit structure. A predicted longitudinal resonance, driven parallel to the static field, supplied a further B-phase test.[pp.1002–1003 and1009, transverse geometry and longitudinal test](nobel-lecture-superfluid-3he-the-early-days-as-seen-by-a-theorist-by-anthony-james-leggett.md)

The [helium-3 superfluid discovery experiment](helium-3-superfluid-discovery-experiment.md) establishes why a new phase was inferred; comparing response channels then tests its internal structure. An unchanged peak in one geometry cannot, by itself, establish that nothing ordered.
