# EF-G

↑ **Parent:** [Translation (biology)](translation-biology.md)

A bacterial GTPase promoting [ribosomal translocation](ribosomal-translocation.md). Its nucleotide-exchange kinetics differ from those of [EF-Tu](ef-tu.md), so it does not require [EF-Ts](ef-ts.md) for recycling.
