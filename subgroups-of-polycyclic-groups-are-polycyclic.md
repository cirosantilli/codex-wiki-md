# Subgroups of polycyclic groups are polycyclic

↑ **Parent:** [Polycyclic group](polycyclic-group.md)

Given a cyclic-factor [subnormal series](subnormal-series.md) $1=G_0\trianglelefteq\cdots\trianglelefteq G_n=G$ and $H\leq G$, intersect each term with $H$. The induced map $(H\cap G_{i+1})/(H\cap G_i)\to G_{i+1}/G_i$ is [injective](injective-function.md). Since every subgroup of a [cyclic group](cyclic-group.md) is cyclic, the intersection chain is again a cyclic-factor [subnormal series](subnormal-series.md), after removing repetitions. Hence $H$ is a [polycyclic group](polycyclic-group.md). In particular, every subgroup is finitely generated.
