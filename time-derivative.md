# Time derivative

↑ **Parent:** [Partial derivative](partial-derivative.md)

A time derivative is the [partial derivative](partial-derivative.md) of an evolving field with respect to time while holding its spatial coordinates fixed. For a quantity depending only on time, it is an ordinary [derivative](derivative.md).
