# Action integral

↑ **Parent:** [Action-angle variables](action-angle-variables.md)

Near a regular [Lagrangian torus](lagrangian-torus.md), choose a one-form $\lambda$ with $d\lambda=-\omega$ and a basis of fibre cycles $\gamma_j$. The action $I_j=(2\pi)^{-1}\int_{\gamma_j}\lambda$ has [differential of a smooth map](differential-of-a-smooth-map.md) $dI_j=(2\pi)^{-1}\sum_i t_i^{(j)}dF_i$, where $t^{(j)}$ is the corresponding joint-flow period. Nonsingularity of the period matrix makes these actions local coordinates transverse to the tori.
