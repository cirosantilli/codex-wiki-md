# Johnson-Claisen rearrangement

↑ **Parent:** [Claisen rearrangement](claisen-rearrangement.md)

An allylic alcohol reacts with an orthoester under weak-acid catalysis to give a [ketene acetal](ketene-acetal.md), which rearranges to an unsaturated [ester](ester.md).
