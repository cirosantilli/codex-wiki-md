# Tetrahedron membership via scalar triple products

↑ **Parent:** [Tetrahedron](tetrahedron.md)

Let $D=\mathbf a\cdot(\mathbf b\times\mathbf c)>0$. The [reciprocal basis](reciprocal-basis.md) gives the coordinates of any [position vector](position-vector.md) as

$$
\alpha=\frac{\mathbf r\cdot(\mathbf b\times\mathbf c)}D,\qquad
\beta=\frac{\mathbf r\cdot(\mathbf c\times\mathbf a)}D,\qquad
\gamma=\frac{\mathbf r\cdot(\mathbf a\times\mathbf b)}D.
$$

The point lies in the closed [tetrahedron](tetrahedron.md) with vertices $0,\mathbf a,\mathbf b,\mathbf c$ exactly when $\alpha,\beta,\gamma\geq0$ and $\alpha+\beta+\gamma\leq1$. Indeed, these conditions express it as a [convex combination](convex-combination.md) of the four vertices, with fourth weight $1-\alpha-\beta-\gamma$. They also describe the intersection of the four inward [half-spaces](half-space.md) bounded by its faces. Strict inequalities characterize its interior.
