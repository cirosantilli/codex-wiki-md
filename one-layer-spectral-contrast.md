# One-layer spectral contrast

↑ **Parent:** [Formal solution of the radiative transfer equation](formal-solution-of-the-radiative-transfer-equation.md)

A non-scattering isothermal atmospheric layer in [local thermodynamic equilibrium](local-thermodynamic-equilibrium.md), of [temperature](temperature.md) $T_a$ and slant [optical depth](optical-depth.md) $t_\lambda$, lies above a [blackbody](blackbody.md) background at $T_b$. The [specific intensity](specific-intensity.md) is

$$
I_\lambda=B_\lambda(T_b)e^{-t_\lambda}+B_\lambda(T_a)(1-e^{-t_\lambda}).
$$

Thus an increase in [opacity](opacity.md) shifts the [specific intensity](specific-intensity.md) toward the layer's [Planck function](planck-function.md). If a line is more opaque than its continuum, it appears in absorption for $T_a<T_b$, in emission for $T_a>T_b$, and has no contrast for $T_a=T_b$. This is a local explanation, not a claim that all features diagnose one uniform layer.
