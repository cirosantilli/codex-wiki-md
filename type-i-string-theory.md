# Type I string theory

↑ **Parent:** [String theory](string-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Type_I_string_theory)

An unoriented theory containing open and closed superstrings. Consistent gauge and gravitational anomaly cancellation yields the familiar $\mathfrak{so}(32)$ gauge algebra. Open-string [Chan-Paton factors](chan-paton-factor.md) supply the gauge degrees of freedom, while closed strings contain gravity. It is related by strong/weak duality to the corresponding heterotic theory.
