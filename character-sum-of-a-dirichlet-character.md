# Character sum of a Dirichlet character

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet character](dirichlet-character.md)

A character sum of a [Dirichlet character](dirichlet-character.md) $\chi$ is a finite [sum](sum.md) such as $\sum_{n\leq x}\chi(n)$ or the weighted [sum](sum.md) $\sum_{n\leq x}a_n\chi(n)$. [Orthogonality of Dirichlet characters](orthogonality-of-dirichlet-characters.md) converts [residue class](residue-class.md) restrictions to these sums.
