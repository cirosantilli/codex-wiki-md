# Zurek spin-bath model

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum decoherence](quantum-decoherence.md)

A device qubit couples diagonally to a finite bath of spins. Conditional bath states acquire opposite phase rotations, and their [conditional environment overlap](conditional-environment-overlap.md) multiplies the device coherence. An initially aligned bath leaves the overlap's modulus at one. Initially equatorial spins yield $z_N(t)=\prod_k\cos(2g_kt)$. Global [unitary time evolution](unitary-time-evolution.md) remains reversible, and a [finite spin-bath coherence recurrence](finite-spin-bath-coherence-recurrence.md) prevents a strict zero long-time limit at finite bath size.
