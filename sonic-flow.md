# Sonic flow

↑ **Parent:** [Mach number](mach-number.md)

A [sonic flow](sonic-flow.md) has [Mach number](mach-number.md) exactly one: the local [fluid](fluid.md) speed relative to the body equals the local [sound speed](speed-of-sound.md). In uniform [gas](gas.md) flowing at $U=c$ past a resting body, an upstream-directed [acoustic wave](acoustic-wave.md) has zero body-frame [velocity](velocity.md). A [sonic point](sonic-point.md) is a location where this equality holds. In the [moving-point acoustic wavefronts](moving-point-acoustic-wavefronts.md) construction, old [spheres](sphere.md) touch the source at the origin; the [Mach angle](mach-angle.md) tends to $90^\circ$ as the [supersonic flow](supersonic-flow.md) approaches this threshold.
