<h1 id="powers-of-a-compact-kahler-form-have-nonzero-cohomology-classes">Powers of a compact Kähler form have nonzero cohomology classes</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Kähler form](kahler-form.md)

On a nonempty compact [Kähler manifold](kahler-manifold.md) of complex dimension $n$, the [Kähler form](kahler-form.md) is closed and $\int_X\omega^n>0$. If $\omega^k=d\eta$ for $1\leq k\leq n$, then $\omega^n=d(\eta\wedge\omega^{n-k})$; [Stokes theorem](stokes-theorem.md) contradicts the positive integral. Thus the [de Rham cohomology](de-rham-cohomology.md) is nonzero in every even degree through $2n$.
