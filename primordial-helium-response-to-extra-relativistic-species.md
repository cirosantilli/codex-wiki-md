# Primordial helium response to extra relativistic species

↑ **Parent:** [Primordial helium mass fraction](primordial-helium-mass-fraction.md)

With a weak conversion time proportional to $T^{-5}$ and a radiation-era age proportional to $g_*^{-1/2}T^{-2}$, [cosmological weak freeze-out](cosmological-weak-freeze-out.md) occurs at $T_f\propto g_*^{1/6}$. Extra radiation therefore raises the freeze-out temperature and the [neutron-to-proton ratio](neutron-to-proton-ratio.md) $r_f=e^{-Q/T_f}$. Before decay, the [primordial helium mass fraction](primordial-helium-mass-fraction.md) is $Y_f=2r_f/(1+r_f)$, giving $\delta Y_f/Y_f=Q\delta\log T_f/[T_f(1+r_f)]$. A shorter delay through the [deuterium bottleneck](deuterium-bottleneck.md) also leaves more neutrons. After freeze-out, conserve baryon number and evolve the neutron fraction to obtain $Y_p\simeq Y_f e^{-(t_N-t_f)/\tau_n}$. A precise abundance requires the weak rates and nuclear-burning history, not merely the number of relativistic species.
