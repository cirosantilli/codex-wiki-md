# Depolarization block

↑ **Parent:** [Action potential](action-potential.md)

Sustained [membrane potential](membrane-potential-split.md) depolarization can prevent repeated [action potentials](action-potential.md) because voltage-dependent [ion-channel inactivation](ion-channel-inactivation.md) leaves too few available sodium channels. This differs from a reduced response to a passive current step: active [Hodgkin-Huxley model](hodgkin-huxley-model.md) dynamics and recovery from inactivation are essential to the mechanism.
