# Basis invariance of an isonormal Gaussian process

↑ **Parent:** [Isonormal Gaussian process](isonormal-gaussian-process.md)

For any real separable [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) $H$, any [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) $(e_j)$ and [independent](independent-random-variables.md) [standard normal random variables](standard-normal-random-variable.md) $(Z_j)$, the displayed series converges [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md) for each fixed $h\in H$ and in [L2 space](l2-space-is-a-hilbert-space.md). Indeed, the [Parseval identity for a Hilbertian basis](parseval-identity-for-a-hilbertian-basis.md) gives $\sum_j\langle h,e_j\rangle^2=\|h\|^2$, so the partial sums form an [L2 space](l2-space-is-a-hilbert-space.md)-bounded [martingale](martingale-split.md). The [martingale convergence theorem](martingale-convergence-theorem.md) supplies both limits. Every finite linear combination has the [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md) with [variance](variance-split.md) $\|\sum_ju_jh_j\|^2$. Thus its finite-dimensional [characteristic function](characteristic-function-split.md) is $\exp(-\|\sum_ju_jh_j\|^2/2)$, independent of the chosen [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md). This proves equality of the [finite-dimensional distributions](finite-dimensional-distribution.md) of the resulting [isonormal Gaussian processes](isonormal-gaussian-process.md); pathwise equality needs a coupling.
