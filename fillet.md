# Fillet

↑ **Parent:** [Computer aided geometric design](computer-aided-geometric-design-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fillet_(mechanics))

A [fillet](fillet.md) rounds a corner or edge by inserting a tangent transition. In a planar circular construction, intersect [normal lines](normal-line.md) through the prescribed tangent [points](point-geometry.md) to find the [circle](circle.md) centre. Choosing the retained side and local clipping region is part of the design: intersecting the entire original solid with the fillet disk generally removes unrelated geometry. Tangent joining need not make [curvature](curvature.md) continuous.
