# Prime ring

↑ **Parent:** [Prime ideal of a noncommutative ring](prime-ideal-of-a-noncommutative-ring.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_ring)

A prime ring is a nonzero [ring](ring.md) whose zero ideal is a [prime ideal of a noncommutative ring](prime-ideal-of-a-noncommutative-ring.md). Equivalently, the product of any two nonzero two-sided [ideals](ideal.md) is nonzero. This condition is weaker than absence of zero divisors: a full matrix ring over a [division ring](division-ring.md) is prime even when its size is greater than one. For a commutative [ring](ring.md), primeness is exactly being an [integral domain](integral-domain.md).
