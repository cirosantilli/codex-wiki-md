# Kadec-Snobar projection bound

↑ **Parent:** [Projection (linear algebra)](projection-linear-algebra.md)

Every finite-dimensional [vector subspace](vector-subspace.md) $E$ of a [Banach space](banach-space-split.md) $X$ is the range of a bounded [linear projection](projection-linear-algebra.md) with the displayed norm bound. In maximal inscribed Euclidean or Hermitian [ellipsoid](ellipsoid.md) coordinates, write the [John contact decomposition](john-contact-decomposition.md) as $\sum_rc_ru_ru_r^*=I_E$. Its contact functionals $\phi_r(x)=u_r^*x$ have norm one on $E$. Extend them to $X$ by the [Hahn-Banach theorem](hahn-banach-theorem.md) and set $Px=\sum_rc_r\widetilde\phi_r(x)u_r$. This restricts to the identity on $E$. The contact synthesis operator has Euclidean norm one, the weights sum to $\dim E$, and the Euclidean unit ball is contained in the norm unit ball; these facts give $\|Px\|_E\le\sqrt{\dim E}\|x\|_X$.
