# Nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide phosphate

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical compound](chemical-compound.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicotinamide_adenine_dinucleotide_phosphate)

[Nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide phosphate](nicotinamide-adenine-dinucleotide-phosphate.md) is a [cofactor](cofactor.md) used in [metabolism](metabolism.md). Its oxidized and reduced forms, [NADP+](nadp-plus.md) and [NADPH](nadph.md), carry [electrons](electron.md) between coupled [chemical reactions](chemical-reaction-split.md). In [oxygenic photosynthesis](oxygenic-photosynthesis.md), [light-dependent reactions](light-dependent-reactions.md) produce [NADPH](nadph.md) for the [Calvin cycle](calvin-cycle.md), which returns [NADP+](nadp-plus.md) for reuse. [OpenStax Biology 2e, 8.3: recycling NADP+ and NADPH](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/8-3-using-light-energy-to-make-organic-molecules).
