# Radiation buildup factor

↑ **Parent:** [Radiation shielding](radiation-shielding.md)

A factor accounting for scattered radiation that contributes to a specified response beyond the uncollided [exponential attenuation](exponential-attenuation.md). It depends on material, geometry, spectrum and the response being counted; $B(x)=1+x$ is a stipulated simple model, not a universal law.
