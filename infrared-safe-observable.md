# Infrared-safe observable

↑ **Parent:** [Infrared divergence](infrared-divergence.md)

An observable unchanged by adding a particle with vanishing energy or replacing one massless particle by exactly collinear particles with the same total momentum. This property permits cancellation of real and virtual [infrared divergences](infrared-divergence.md) and [collinear divergences](collinear-divergence.md) in its perturbative cross-section. Fully inclusive electron-positron hadronic rates and suitably defined jet observables are examples.
