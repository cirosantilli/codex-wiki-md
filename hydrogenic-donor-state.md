# Hydrogenic donor state

↑ **Parent:** [Donor impurity](donor-impurity.md)

A shallow [donor impurity](donor-impurity.md) in an isotropic [semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md) can be approximated by an electron of [effective mass](band-effective-mass.md) $m^*$ in a Coulomb potential screened by relative [permittivity](permittivity-split.md) $\epsilon_r$. Its ground-state binding energy and radius are

$$
E_B=13.6057\,\mathrm{eV}\,\frac{m^*/m_e}{\epsilon_r^2},\qquad
a_B^*=a_0\frac{\epsilon_r}{m^*/m_e}.
$$

The donor level lies $E_B$ below the [conduction band](conduction-band.md). These are magnitudes: the bound electron's energy relative to the band edge is negative. Band anisotropy, short-distance impurity structure and screening by mobile carriers can invalidate the simple [Bohr model](bohr-model.md) rescaling.
