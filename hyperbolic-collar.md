# Hyperbolic collar

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic surface](hyperbolic-surface.md)

A collar around a simple closed [geodesic](geodesic.md) of length $\ell$ is an embedded annulus with metric $dr^2+\ell^2\cosh^2r\,dt^2$, where $t\in\mathbb R/\mathbb Z$. The [collar lemma](collar-lemma.md) gives half-width $w=\operatorname{arcsinh}(1/\sinh(\ell/2))$. As $\ell$ tends to zero the collar gets wide, forcing crossing [geodesics](geodesic.md) to become long.
