# Stokes shift

↑ **Parent:** [Fluorescence](fluorescence-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stokes_shift)

The [Stokes shift](stokes-shift.md) compares the lowest absorption-band maximum with the [fluorescence emission spectrum](fluorescence-emission-spectrum.md) maximum. Relaxation often makes $\Delta\lambda>0$. This comparison differs from using an arbitrary excitation [wavelength](wavelength.md). A spectral-density maximum can move when changing from per unit [wavelength](wavelength.md) to per unit [spectroscopic wavenumber](spectroscopic-wavenumber.md); specify the spectral convention.[Stokes Shift in Fluorescence Spectroscopy, definition, spectral-coordinate caveat, Figures 2 and 4](what-is-the-stokes-shift-by-edinburgh-instruments.md)

An original illustrative [energy](energy-split.md) budget makes the distinction concrete. Excitation at $450\,\mathrm{nm}$ and emission at $520\,\mathrm{nm}$ correspond to about $2.76$ and $2.38\,\mathrm{eV}$ per [photon](photon.md), using $E=h c/\lambda$ with [Planck constant](planck-constant.md) $h$ and [speed of light](speed-of-light.md) $c$ in [vacuum](vacuum.md). The difference, about $0.37\,\mathrm{eV}$, must go elsewhere in the complete [energy](energy-split.md) balance. These chosen [wavelengths](wavelength.md) are not measured band maxima for a particular [fluorophore](fluorophore.md).

Anti-Stokes emission has shorter [wavelength](wavelength.md) than excitation. Additional [energy](energy-split.md), for example from a thermally populated level of [molecular vibration](molecular-vibration.md) or multiple excitation [photons](photon.md), makes it possible. The one-photon, initially relaxed drawing does not cover these cases.[§1.1.1, final paragraphs: anti-Stokes emission and multiphoton excitation](advanced-fluorescence-microscopy-techniques-frap-flip-flap-fret-and-flim-by-hellen-c-ishikawa-ankerhold-and-others.md)
