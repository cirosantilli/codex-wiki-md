<h1 id="neumann-s-mutual-inductance-formula">Neumann's mutual-inductance formula</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic vector potential](magnetic-vector-potential.md)

For thin closed wire loops $C_1,C_2$, the mutual inductance is

$$
L_{12}=L_{21}
=\frac{\mu_0}{4\pi}
\oint_{C_1}\oint_{C_2}
\frac{dx_1\cdot dx_2}{|x_1-x_2|}.
$$
