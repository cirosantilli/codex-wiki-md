↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by H. Wagner](work-by-h-wagner.md), [Work by N. D. Mermin](work-by-n-d-mermin.md)

Physical Review Letters 17, 1133 (1966), DOI 10.1103/PhysRevLett.17.1133. The abstract states the absence of ferromagnetic or antiferromagnetic order at nonzero temperature in finite-range one- or two-dimensional isotropic spin-$S$ [Heisenberg models](heisenberg-model.md). This is a primary source for the low-dimensional limitation of mean-field magnetic ordering.
