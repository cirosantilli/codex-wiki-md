# Tricritical wing

↑ **Parent:** [Tricritical point](tricritical-point.md)

For an even sextic [Landau free energy](landau-free-energy.md) perturbed by a conjugate field, a tricritical wing is a [phase coexistence](phase-coexistence.md) surface at nonzero field between ordered minima of different magnitudes. Two symmetry-related wings emerge from the three-phase line at zero field and terminate at ordinary critical edges. They meet the zero-field ordered coexistence sheet at the [tricritical point](tricritical-point.md).
