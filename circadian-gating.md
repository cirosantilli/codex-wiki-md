# Circadian gating

↑ **Parent:** [Circadian rhythm](circadian-rhythm.md)

Dependence of a stimulus-response relationship on internal [circadian phase](circadian-phase.md). A rhythmic baseline or a daily change in stimulus availability alone does not demonstrate gating.
