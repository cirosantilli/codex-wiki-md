# Cross product

↑ **Parent:** [Vector](vector.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cross_product)

The cross product $a\times b$ of two three-dimensional [vectors](vector.md) is perpendicular to both, has magnitude $|a||b|\sin\theta$, and is oriented by the right-hand rule.
