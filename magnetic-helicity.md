# Magnetic helicity

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic vector potential](magnetic-vector-potential.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnetic_helicity)

The [magnetic helicity](magnetic-helicity.md) integrates the [dot product](dot-product.md) of a [magnetic vector potential](magnetic-vector-potential.md) and its [magnetic field](magnetic-field.md). It measures linkage and twisting of magnetic flux. For a [solenoidal vector field](solenoidal-vector-field.md) $\mathbf B$ tangent to the boundary, it is unchanged by the gauge transformation $\mathbf A\mapsto\mathbf A+\nabla\chi$, since the change is $\int_V\nabla\cdot(\chi\mathbf B)\,dV=\int_{\partial V}\chi\mathbf B\cdot d\mathbf S=0$. If its density satisfies $\partial_t h=\nabla\cdot(f\mathbf B-h\mathbf u)$ with both vector fields tangent to the boundary of a fixed volume, the [divergence theorem](divergence-theorem.md) gives conservation of its integral. This is an instance of [helicity conservation by tangent boundary conditions](helicity-conservation-by-tangent-boundary-conditions.md).
