# Interior dihedral angle of a convex polyhedron

↑ **Parent:** [Polyhedron](polyhedron.md)

At an edge of a convex [polyhedron](polyhedron.md), its interior dihedral angle is the angle of the wedge inside the solid, measured in a [plane](plane.md) perpendicular to the edge. If the two incident faces have outward [unit normal vectors](unit-normal.md) $\hat{\mathbf n}_1,\hat{\mathbf n}_2$, then their normal angle is supplementary to the interior angle. Thus $\cos\theta=-\hat{\mathbf n}_1\cdot\hat{\mathbf n}_2$. Equivalently, subtract from each vector pointing into one face its [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md) onto the common edge; the angle between the resulting inward face directions is the interior angle. This distinguishes an interior angle from the acute angle convention for two unoriented [planes](plane.md).
