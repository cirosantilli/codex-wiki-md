# Cold-plasma left-handed wave

↑ **Parent:** [Cold magnetized plasma](cold-magnetized-plasma.md)

With the same conventions as the [cold-plasma right-handed wave](cold-plasma-right-handed-wave.md), this transverse [electromagnetic wave](electromagnetic-wave-split.md) has $E_y=-iE_x$ and [refractive index](refractive-index.md) $n^2=S+g$. With immobile [ions](ion.md), $L=1-\omega_{pe}^2/[\omega(\omega+\Omega_e)]$.
