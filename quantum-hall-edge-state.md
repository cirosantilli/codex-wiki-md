# Quantum Hall edge state

↑ **Parent:** [Integer quantum Hall effect](integer-quantum-hall-effect.md)

A [quantum Hall edge state](quantum-hall-edge-state.md) is confined across a boundary but can extend along it and carry [electric current](electric-current.md). [B. I. Halperin](b-i-halperin.md) related these current-carrying boundary states to quantized Hall transport in a disordered two-dimensional system.[Publisher abstract: transverse confinement, perimeter extension and current](quantized-hall-conductance-current-carrying-edge-states-and-the-existence-of-extended-states-in-a-two-dimensional-disordered-potential-by-b-i-halperin.md)
