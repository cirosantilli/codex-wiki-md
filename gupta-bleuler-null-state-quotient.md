# Gupta-Bleuler null-state quotient

↑ **Parent:** [Gupta-Bleuler formalism](gupta-bleuler-formalism.md)

Choose $C_{\mathbf p}=a^0_{\mathbf p}-a^3_{\mathbf p}$ with the standard temporal and longitudinal [polarization vectors](polarization-vector.md). The physical pre-space is the common kernel of all $C_{\mathbf p}$. In each regulated mode, $a^0$ acts on creator [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) as $-\partial_{a^{0\dagger}}$ and $a^3$ as $\partial_{a^{3\dagger}}$, so the constraint kernel consists of transverse creator [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) and [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) in $C^\dagger=a^{0\dagger}-a^{3\dagger}$. Since $[C,C^\dagger]=0$, these latter excitations remain constrained. They are orthogonal to every constrained state because $\langle\Phi|C^\dagger=\langle C\Phi|=0$. Quotienting this [radical of a Hermitian form](radical-of-a-hermitian-form.md) leaves only the transverse [bosonic Fock space](bosonic-fock-space.md) with a positive [inner product](inner-product.md). The constraint alone gives a [positive semidefinite Hermitian form](positive-semidefinite-hermitian-form.md); quotienting removes its null directions. If starting from finite-particle creator [polynomials](polynomial-split.md), take the [Hilbert space completion](hilbert-space-completion.md) of this positive quotient to obtain the physical [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md).
