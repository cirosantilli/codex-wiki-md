# Space-charge-limited thermionic current

↑ **Parent:** [Thermionic emission](thermionic-emission-split.md)

A [space-charge-limited thermionic current](space-charge-limited-thermionic-current.md) occurs when the negative [space charge](space-charge.md) of emitted [electrons](electron.md) creates an [electric field](electric-field-split.md) that obstructs further transport across the gap. Raising the emitter [temperature](temperature-split.md) may then increase the surface supply without producing the corresponding collector [electric current](electric-current.md). The gap geometry and collecting [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) matter even when the surface is stable.

To distinguish this from an [emission-limited thermionic current](emission-limited-thermionic-current.md), vary the collecting [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) at fixed emitter [temperature](temperature-split.md), then repeat at another [temperature](temperature-split.md). A voltage-dependent transport region followed by a collection plateau separates a gap constraint from a supply constraint. A weak collector [electric current](electric-current.md) alone cannot establish a high [work function](work-function.md). Nor does simply choosing a very large collecting [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) guarantee a valid zero-field measurement: the surface barrier may then change.
