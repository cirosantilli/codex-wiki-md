# Half-dimensional interval sieve

↑ **Parent:** [Selberg upper-bound sieve](selberg-upper-bound-sieve.md)

Sieve an interval of length $z$ by the [prime](prime-number.md) two and the [primes](prime-number.md) congruent to three modulo four, with local densities $g(p)=1/p$. Take $D=z^{1/2}$ and $L=\sqrt D$. The [truncated Euler-product lower bound](truncated-euler-product-lower-bound.md), the [Mertens first theorem](mertens-first-theorem.md) and the [reciprocal-prime sum in residue class one modulo four](reciprocal-prime-sum-in-residue-class-one-modulo-four.md) give $J\gg\sqrt{\log z}$. The interval counts have bounded remainders, whose sum is $O(D\log^2D)$ by the [summatory bound for three to the prime omega](summatory-bound-for-three-to-the-prime-omega.md). Hence the number surviving is $O(z/\sqrt{\log z})$, uniformly in the location of the interval.
