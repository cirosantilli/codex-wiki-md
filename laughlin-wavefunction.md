# Laughlin wavefunction

↑ **Parent:** [Fractional quantum Hall effect](fractional-quantum-hall-effect.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laughlin_wavefunction)

[Robert Laughlin](robert-laughlin.md)'s 1983 [variational method](variational-method.md) uses pairwise zeros to suppress close [electron](electron.md) encounters within the [lowest Landau level](lowest-landau-level.md). For aligned [spin](spin.md), odd $m$ gives [fermionic exchange symmetry](fermi-dirac-statistics.md).[pp. 1395–1396, Eqs. (5)–(7)](anomalous-quantum-hall-effect-an-incompressible-quantum-fluid-with-fractionally-charged-excitations-by-robert-laughlin.md)

Here $\mathcal N$ enforces [wavefunction normalization](wavefunction-normalization.md), $\ell_B$ is the [magnetic length](magnetic-length.md), and [complex coordinates](complex-coordinate.md) $z_j=x_j-iy_j$ describe [electrons](electron.md) in a [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) $B\widehat{\mathbf z}$, $B>0$, with the [symmetric gauge](symmetric-gauge.md). Reversing the orientation conjugates the [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) without changing [probability densities](probability-density.md). The [Gaussian function](gaussian-function.md) confines the [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md).

The exchange check is especially simple for two [electrons](electron.md). Writing $w=z_1-z_2$, exchange sends $w\mapsto-w$, so $w^m\mapsto(-1)^mw^m$. An aligned two-[electron](electron.md) [spin](spin.md) state is symmetric, requiring the spatial [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) to change sign. Hence $m=1,3,5,\ldots$ works, whereas $m=2$ would give the wrong sign. At a close encounter, the [probability density](probability-density.md) vanishes as $|w|^{2m}$: $m=3$ imposes a sixth-order zero, stronger than the quadratic zero at $m=1$. This is the local geometric mechanism for avoiding a large repulsive [Coulomb potential energy](coulomb-potential-energy.md).

A useful large-$N$ counting outline follows directly from the [polynomial](polynomial-split.md). Each [complex coordinate](complex-coordinate.md) has maximum degree $m(N-1)$. A [lowest Landau level](lowest-landau-level.md) orbital proportional to $z^k e^{-|z|^2/(4\ell_B^2)}$ has its areal [probability density](probability-density.md) maximum at $r^2=2k\ell_B^2$, by differentiating its logarithm. This suggests droplet [area](surface-area.md) $A\simeq2\pi mN\ell_B^2$, neglecting the boundary correction. Dividing $N/A$ by the [degeneracy of a Landau level](degeneracy-of-a-landau-level.md) per [area](surface-area.md), $1/(2\pi\ell_B^2)$, gives [filling factor](filling-factor-quantum-hall-effect.md) $\nu\simeq1/m$. This counting is a motivation for the fraction, not an independent proof that the [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) minimizes the interacting [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md).
