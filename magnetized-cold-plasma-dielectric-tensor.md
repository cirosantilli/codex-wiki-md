# Magnetized cold-plasma dielectric tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Cold magnetized plasma](cold-magnetized-plasma.md)

For $e^{-i\omega t}$, [Gaussian units](gaussian-units.md) give $\boldsymbol\epsilon=\mathbf I+4\pi i\boldsymbol\sigma/\omega$, where $\boldsymbol\sigma$ is the summed [conductivity tensor](conductivity-tensor.md). With the equilibrium [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) along $z$, write $\epsilon_{xx}=\epsilon_{yy}=S$, $\epsilon_{zz}=P$, and $\epsilon_{xy}=-\epsilon_{yx}=ig$. For signed [cyclotron frequencies](cyclotron-frequency.md) $\Omega_s=q_sB/(m_sc)$,

$$
S=1-\sum_s\frac{\omega_{ps}^2}{\omega^2-\Omega_s^2},\quad P=1-\sum_s\frac{\omega_{ps}^2}{\omega^2},\quad g=-\sum_s\frac{\omega_{ps}^2\Omega_s}{\omega(\omega^2-\Omega_s^2)}.
$$

Here $\omega_{ps}$ is the species [plasma frequency](plasma-frequency.md). The two circular eigenvalues are $S\mp g$; defining the sign convention before naming their [polarizations](polarization-waves.md) prevents ambiguity.
