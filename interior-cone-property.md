# Interior cone property

↑ **Parent:** [Sobolev space](sobolev-space-split.md)

A domain has the uniform interior cone property if fixed-height, fixed-opening cones can be placed inside the domain at its points, with an allowed orientation depending on the point. This geometric regularity permits the [Sobolev embedding theorem](sobolev-embedding-theorem.md) and [Rellich-Kondrachov compactness theorem](rellich-kondrachov-theorem.md). It is an interior condition, distinct from the [exterior cone condition](exterior-cone-condition.md) used in boundary regularity.
