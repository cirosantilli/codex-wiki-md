# Inertial-convective scalar range

↑ **Parent:** [Obukhov-Corrsin theory](obukhov-corrsin-theory.md)

This range lies above both the [Kolmogorov length scale](kolmogorov-length-scale.md) and the scalar diffusion cutoff, but below the forcing scale. Molecular viscosity and scalar diffusion have negligible direct influence on transfer at those scales. The [Obukhov-Corrsin theory](obukhov-corrsin-theory.md) predicts a two-thirds [scalar structure function](scalar-structure-function.md) there.
