# Duane-Hunt law

↑ **Parent:** [Bremsstrahlung](bremsstrahlung.md)

For an [electron](electron.md) accelerated through a [electric potential difference](electric-potential-difference.md) $V$ and stopped at a massive target, the largest single [photon energy](photon-energy.md) is approximately $eV$. [Conservation of energy](conservation-of-energy.md) and $E_\gamma=hc/\lambda$ give the short-[wavelength](wavelength.md) cutoff $\lambda_{\min}=hc/(eV)$. Target recoil is neglected in this limiting relation.
