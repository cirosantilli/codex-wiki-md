# N-myristoylation

↑ **Parent:** [Protein](protein-split.md)

[N-myristoylation](n-myristoylation.md) attaches a myristoyl group through an amide linkage to an N-terminal residue, usually [glycine](glycine.md). It can cooperate with other targeting features rather than by itself specifying a unique membrane.
