# Transverse intersection

↑ **Parent:** [Clean intersection](clean-intersection.md)

Submanifolds $A,B\subseteq M$ intersect transversely when $T_xA+T_xB=T_xM$ at every intersection point. The [transverse intersection theorem](transverse-intersection-theorem.md) then determines the dimension and smoothness of $A\cap B$.
