# Logarithm tangent bound

↑ **Parent:** [Logarithm](logarithm.md)

For the natural [logarithm](logarithm.md), define $F(u)=u-1-\log u$. Its [derivative](derivative.md) is $1-1/u$, negative below one and positive above one. Thus its global minimum is $F(1)=0$, proving the bound, with equality only at one. Equivalently $\log(1+x)\leq x$ for $x>-1$; this also follows from [concavity](concave-function.md) and the tangent line at one.
