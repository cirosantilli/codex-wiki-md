# Likelihood-ratio test statistic

↑ **Parent:** [Likelihood-ratio test](likelihood-ratio-test.md)

For nested null and alternative parameter spaces, the likelihood-ratio test statistic is

$$
-2\log\Lambda
=2\left\{\sup_{\theta\in\Theta_1}\ell(\theta)
-\sup_{\theta\in\Theta_0}\ell(\theta)\right\}.
$$

Under the regularity conditions of [Wilks theorem](wilks-theorem.md), its null limit is a [chi-squared distribution](chi-squared-distribution.md) whose degrees of freedom equal the difference in model dimensions.
