<h1 id="kaplan-meier-telescoping-across-a-censor-free-interval">Kaplan–Meier telescoping across a censor-free interval</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Kaplan–Meier estimator](kaplan-meier-estimator.md)

For a fixed cohort with $r$ individuals at risk just after $a$, no censoring or entry inside $(a,b)$, and $d$ events in $(a,b]$, the [Kaplan–Meier estimator](kaplan-meier-estimator.md) factors telescope to the displayed ratio. A tied group of $d_j$ events contributes $(r_j-d_j)/r_j$, the same product as any sequential ordering without intervening censoring. Events at $b$ are counted before censoring at the same time under the usual convention.
