# Autoregressive integrated moving average

↑ **Parent:** [Autoregressive moving-average model](autoregressive-moving-average-model.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autoregressive_integrated_moving_average)

An [ARIMA](autoregressive-integrated-moving-average.md) model applies an [autoregressive moving-average model](autoregressive-moving-average-model.md) to the differenced process $(1-B)^dX_t$, where $B$ is the [backshift operator](backshift-operator.md). Differencing permits stochastic trends whose levels are not [weakly stationary processes](weakly-stationary-process.md). A local random-walk level observed with [independent](independent-random-variables.md) measurement [white noise](white-noise.md) has an ARIMA(0,1,1) representation, or a formal [ARMA](autoregressive-moving-average-model.md)(1,1) equation with autoregressive coefficient one.
