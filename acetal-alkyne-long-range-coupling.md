# Acetal-alkyne long-range coupling

↑ **Parent:** [Scalar spin-spin coupling](scalar-spin-spin-coupling.md)

[Proton](proton.md) coupling across H-C-C≡C-H can resolve a small four-bond splitting. In a propargyl [acetal](acetal.md) this connects the [acetal](acetal.md) methine and terminal [alkyne](alkyne.md) [proton](proton.md).
