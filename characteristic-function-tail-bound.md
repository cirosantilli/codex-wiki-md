# Characteristic-function tail bound

↑ **Parent:** [Characteristic function](characteristic-function.md)

Average $1-\cos(uy)$ over $0\le u\le1/\lambda$. Multiplication by $\lambda$ gives $1-\sin(y/\lambda)/(y/\lambda)$, with value zero at $y=0$. This is at least $1-\sin1$ when $|y|\ge\lambda$. [Tonelli's theorem](tonelli-theorem.md) then gives the displayed bound for a [probability measure](probability-measure.md) with [characteristic function](characteristic-function.md) $\phi$. The bound converts control of the [characteristic function](characteristic-function.md) near zero into [tightness of probability measures](uniform-tightness.md) and is useful in proving the [Lévy continuity theorem](levy-continuity-theorem.md).
