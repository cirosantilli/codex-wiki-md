# Limited proteolysis

↑ **Parent:** [Protein](protein-split.md)

[Limited proteolysis](limited-proteolysis.md) cleaves a protein under controlled conditions to probe exposed regions or recover stable domains. Resistance can reflect accessibility and dynamics as well as sequence specificity of the chosen protease.
