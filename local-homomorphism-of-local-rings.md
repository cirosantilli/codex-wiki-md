# Local homomorphism of local rings

↑ **Parent:** [Local ring](local-ring.md)

A homomorphism $\varphi:(A,\mathfrak m)\to(B,\mathfrak n)$ of [local rings](local-ring.md) is local if $\varphi(\mathfrak m)\subseteq\mathfrak n$, equivalently $\varphi^{-1}(\mathfrak n)=\mathfrak m$. The equivalence uses the fact that all elements outside $\mathfrak m$ are [units](unit-in-a-ring.md). It induces an embedding of [residue fields](residue-field.md) $A/\mathfrak m\to B/\mathfrak n$.
