# Geometric means of two summable positive sequences

↑ **Parent:** [Absolute convergence](absolute-convergence.md)

The [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) gives

$$
\sum_n\sqrt{a_nb_n}
\leq\left(\sum_na_n\right)^{1/2}
\left(\sum_nb_n\right)^{1/2}.
$$
