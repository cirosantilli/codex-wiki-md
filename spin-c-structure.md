# Spin-c structure

↑ **Parent:** [Spin structure](spin-structure.md)

A Spin-c structure on an oriented rank-$n$ real [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) is a lift of its oriented frame bundle to $\operatorname{Spin}^c(n)=(\operatorname{Spin}(n)\times U(1))/\{(1,1),(-1,-1)\}$. Its [determinant line bundle](determinant-line-bundle.md) is associated with $[s,z]\mapsto z^2$; its [First Chern class](first-chern-class.md) reduces modulo two to the second [Stiefel–Whitney class](stiefel-whitney-class.md). When such structures exist, their isomorphism classes form an affine space over $H^2$; tensoring by a complex line bundle of first Chern class $h$ changes the determinant class by $2h$. On a closed oriented three-manifold they always exist. On a four-manifold, the determinant class is characteristic: $\langle c_1(\mathfrak s),v\rangle\equiv v\cdot v\pmod2$.
