# Feigenbaum constants

↑ **Parent:** [Period-doubling cascade](period-doubling-cascade.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feigenbaum_constants)

For a generic quadratic [period-doubling cascade](period-doubling-cascade.md), the Feigenbaum constants are the limiting parameter ratio $\delta$ and spatial ratio $\alpha$. With $s_n$ the successive superstable period-$2^n$ parameters and $d_n=f_{s_n}^{2^{n-1}}(0)$ the signed central-orbit spacing,

$$
\delta=\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{s_n-s_{n-1}}{s_{n+1}-s_n},\qquad
\alpha=-\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{d_n}{d_{n+1}}.
$$

The positive convention gives $\delta\simeq4.669201609$ and $\alpha\simeq2.502907875$. The orientation-reversing spatial scale at the [Feigenbaum renormalization fixed point](feigenbaum-renormalization-fixed-point.md) is $a=-1/\alpha$.
