# Vacuum-preserving symmetry of the Skyrme model

↑ **Parent:** [Skyrme model](skyrme-model.md)

The massless derivative [Skyrme model](skyrme-model.md) has [chiral symmetry](chiral-symmetry.md) $U\mapsto LUR^\dagger$. For the fixed finite-energy boundary condition $U(\infty)=1$, a transformation preserves the same vacuum exactly when $LR^\dagger=1$, or $L=R$. This leaves conjugation $U\mapsto AUA^\dagger$, the [isospin](isospin.md) action. Since $A$ and $-A$ act identically, its effective group is $SO(3)$. Together with spatial translations and rotations it generates the localized zero-mode orbit of a [Skyrmion](skyrmion.md). Independent axial rotations change the massless vacuum and are not additional normalizable rigid orientation modes in that fixed-vacuum sector. A usual positive pion-mass term preserves only conjugation even before the boundary condition is imposed.
