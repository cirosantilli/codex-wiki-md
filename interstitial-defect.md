# Interstitial defect

↑ **Parent:** [Crystallographic defect](crystallographic-defect.md)

An interstitial defect is an atom occupying a position between the regular atomic positions in a [crystal structure](crystal-structure.md). Small solute atoms or an extra host atom can occupy such positions. Different interstitial orientations can induce anisotropic local strain; an applied [stress](stress.md) changes their relative energies. Thermally activated hopping among those positions can then produce delayed elastic strain and the [Snoek effect](snoek-effect.md).
