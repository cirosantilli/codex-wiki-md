# Exponential convergence of Gaussian quadrature

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian quadrature](gaussian-quadrature.md)

For positive [quadrature weights](quadrature-weight.md) summing to the total mass $M$, a [quadrature rule](quadrature-rule.md) exact on [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) through [polynomial degree](degree-of-a-polynomial.md) $d$ satisfies $|I(f)-Q(f)|\leq2M\|f-p_d\|_\infty$ for every such [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) $p_d$. This follows by subtracting the exactly integrated $p_d$ and applying the [triangle inequality](triangle-inequality.md) to the integral and weighted sum. For [Gauss-Legendre quadrature](gauss-legendre-quadrature.md) with $n+1$ nodes, $M=2$ and $d=2n+1$. Exponentially decaying [Chebyshev series](chebyshev-series.md) coefficients therefore yield a bound proportional to $e^{-2Kn}$ when $|c_j|\leq e^{-Kj}$.
