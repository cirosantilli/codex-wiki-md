# Cofinality of a linear order

↑ **Parent:** [Total order](total-order.md)

The [cofinality of a linear order](cofinality-of-a-linear-order.md) is the least [cardinality](cardinality.md) of a [cofinal subset](cofinal-subset.md). It is preserved by order [isomorphisms](isomorphism.md). The [order sum](order-sum.md) of $\omega_1$ copies of the rational order has [cofinality](cofinality.md) $\aleph_1$, whereas appending one final rational block changes its [cofinality](cofinality.md) to $\aleph_0$ without changing its [cardinality](cardinality.md) or the dense-linear-order axioms.
