# Intersection and join of benign subgroups

↑ **Parent:** [Benign subgroup](benign-subgroup.md)

For [benign subgroups](benign-subgroup.md) $H,K\le G$, amalgamate their finite-presentation witnesses over the finitely generated $G$, then adjoin independent [stable letters](stable-letter.md) $t,s$ centralizing their finite-generation witnesses. [Britton's lemma](britton-s-lemma.md) identifies the subgroup generated by $G,t,s$ with the extension centralizing $H,K$. In it, $G\cap G^{ts}=H\cap K$ and $G\cap\langle G^t,G^s\rangle=\langle H,K\rangle$. Both conjugate witnesses are finitely generated, proving benignity. For the second identity, the subgroup generated by $G,G^t,G^s$ is the iterated [amalgamated free product](amalgamated-free-product.md) $G*_H G^t *_K G^s$; its normal forms show that adjoining each outer copy intersects the central copy only in the subgroup already generated by $H,K$.
