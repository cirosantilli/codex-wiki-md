# Jacobi positivity criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Conjugate point](conjugate-point.md)

Assume the [Strengthened Legendre condition](strengthened-legendre-condition.md) $P>0$ and let $q(\varphi)=\int_a^b(P\varphi'^2+Q\varphi^2)$. Then $q$ is strictly positive on all nonzero fixed-endpoint [Lipschitz](lipschitz-continuity.md) variations exactly when the normalized [Jacobi equation](jacobi-equation.md) solution with $w(a)=0$, $P(a)w'(a)=1$ has no zero in $(a,b]$. A zero at $c$ gives a nonzero variation equal to $w$ up to $c$ and zero thereafter, with $q=0$. Conversely, if no zero occurs, the [Jacobi identity for the second variation](jacobi-identity-for-the-second-variation.md) writes $q$ as a nonnegative square. Equality forces $\varphi=Cw$, and the endpoint at $b$ forces $C=0$.
