# Change of variables for probability densities

↑ **Parent:** [Probability density function](probability-density-function.md)

For a [random variable](random-variable-split.md) $X$ with [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) $f_X$ on an [interval](interval-mathematics.md), let $Y=h(X)$, where $h$ is a [diffeomorphism](diffeomorphism.md) onto another [interval](interval-mathematics.md). The [change of variables formula](change-of-variables-formula.md) gives

$$
f_Y(y)=f_X(h^{-1}(y))\left|\frac{d h^{-1}(y)}{dy}\right|.
$$

Indeed, integrating the right-hand side over an outcome interval gives the [probability](probability.md) that $X$ lies in its inverse image. The absolute [derivative](derivative.md) preserves positive probability when $h$ reverses orientation. A [gravity deflection of an atomic beam](gravity-deflection-of-an-atomic-beam.md) is a physical example: its speed-to-height transformation changes the [probability density](probability-density.md) and its [mode](mode-statistics.md).
