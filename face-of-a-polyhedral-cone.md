# Face of a polyhedral cone

↑ **Parent:** [Cone in toric geometry](cone-in-toric-geometry.md)

A face of a polyhedral cone $C$ is $C\cap\ker\ell$ for a linear functional nonnegative on $C$. Every [convex face](face-of-a-convex-set.md) of a polyhedral cone is exposed in this way. Faces include the whole cone and its lineality space, and are closed under intersection.
