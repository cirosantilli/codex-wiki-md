# Logistic growth equation

↑ **Parent:** [Population dynamics](population-dynamics.md)

The [logistic growth equation](logistic-growth-equation.md) applies the [logistic differential equation](logistic-differential-equation.md) to [population dynamics](population-dynamics.md). For positive growth rate $r$ and [carrying capacity](carrying-capacity.md) $K$, an [initial condition](initial-condition.md) $0<N(0)<K$ gives $N(t)=K/[1+be^{-rt}]$, where $b=K/N(0)-1$. Its per-capita growth falls linearly with abundance, and a trajectory starting below $K/2$ reaches an [inflection point](inflection-point.md) when $N=K/2$. The exact [solutions of a differential equation](solution-of-a-differential-equation.md) and their diagrams are developed under [logistic differential equation](logistic-differential-equation.md). A translated and scaled [logistic function](logistic-function.md) has the same form. In [nonlinear regression](nonlinear-regression.md) for biological size, an analogous saturation curve need not imply that circumference itself obeys a mechanistic population law.
