# Logistic growth equation

↑ **Parent:** [Population dynamics](population-dynamics.md)

For positive growth rate $r$ and carrying capacity $K$, the logistic growth equation has solutions $N(t)=K/[1+be^{-rt}]$ for an initial value between zero and $K$. Its per-capita growth falls linearly with population size; the curve's inflection point occurs at $N=K/2$. A translated and scaled [logistic function](logistic-function.md) has the same form. In [nonlinear regression](nonlinear-regression.md) for biological size, an analogous saturation curve need not imply that circumference itself obeys a mechanistic population law.
