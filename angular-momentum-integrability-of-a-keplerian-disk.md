# Angular-momentum integrability of a Keplerian disk

↑ **Parent:** [Viscous evolution of an accretion disk](viscous-evolution-of-an-accretion-disk.md)

A [Keplerian disk](keplerian-disk.md) with [surface density](surface-density-of-a-disk.md) $\Sigma\sim r^{-p}$ at infinity has finite outer [mass](mass.md) for $p>2$ and finite outer [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) for $p>5/2$, since the relevant integrals are $\int\Sigma r\,dr$ and $\int\Sigma r^{3/2}dr$. The borderline $p=5/2$ has logarithmically divergent angular momentum even though its mass converges.
