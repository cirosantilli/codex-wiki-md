# Character theta transformation

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet character theta function](dirichlet-character-theta-function.md)

For a [primitive Dirichlet character](primitive-dirichlet-character.md) modulo $N>1$ of parity $a\in\{0,1\}$, put $\Theta_\chi(\tau)=\sum_n n^a\chi(n)e^{\pi i n^2\tau}$, extending the character by zero on nonunits. The square root is analytic with positive value when $-i\tau$ is positive real. Apply [Poisson summation](poisson-summation-formula.md) separately in each residue class modulo $N$, and use the [finite Fourier transform of a primitive Dirichlet character](finite-fourier-transform-of-a-primitive-dirichlet-character.md). The [Fourier transforms](fourier-transform.md) of $e^{\pi i\tau x^2}$ and $x e^{\pi i\tau x^2}$ are $(-i\tau)^{-1/2}e^{-\pi i t^2/\tau}$ and $(t/\tau)(-i\tau)^{-1/2}e^{-\pi i t^2/\tau}$. These give the formula, including its odd-character phase.
