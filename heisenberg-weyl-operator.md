# Heisenberg-Weyl operator

↑ **Parent:** [Random unitary channel](random-unitary-channel.md)

On a $d$-dimensional [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md), let $X|j\rangle=|j+1\bmod d\rangle$ and $Z|j\rangle=e^{2\pi ij/d}|j\rangle$. The $d^2$ unitaries $W_{k,m}=X^kZ^m$ form an orthogonal operator basis under the [Hilbert-Schmidt inner product](hilbert-schmidt-inner-product.md).
