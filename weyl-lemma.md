# Weyl lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Harmonic function](harmonic-function.md)

The Weyl lemma says that every locally integrable distributional solution of $\Delta u=0$ agrees almost everywhere with a smooth harmonic function. Convolution with a [mollifier](mollifier.md) and the harmonic mean value property provide a standard proof.
