# Assignment problem

↑ **Parent:** [Transportation problem](transportation-problem.md)

The assignment problem chooses a one-to-one pairing of jobs and agents minimizing their total cost. Its [minimum-cost flow](minimum-cost-flow-problem.md) formulation puts unit supplies on job vertices and unit demands on agent vertices of a [bipartite graph](bipartite-graph.md). Integral feasible flows are [perfect matchings](perfect-matching.md).
