# Noninverting amplifier

↑ **Parent:** [Operational amplifier](operational-amplifier.md)

A voltage-feedback [operational amplifier](operational-amplifier.md) driven at its noninverting input, with a feedback divider $R_f,R_g$ at its inverting input, has ideal gain $1+R_f/R_g$ under stable [negative feedback](negative-feedback.md).
