# Volume

↑ **Parent:** [Geometry and topology](geometry-and-topology-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volume)

Volume measures the size of a three-dimensional region. In Euclidean space it is the [Lebesgue measure](lebesgue-measure.md) $V=\int_\Omega dx\,dy\,dz$; a layer of thickness $h$ over a planar region has $V=\int h\,dA$.
