# Discrete Sobolev norm

↑ **Parent:** [Sobolev space](sobolev-space-split.md)

A discrete Sobolev norm combines weighted [norms](norm.md) of nodal values and their [finite differences](finite-difference-split.md). For a real sequence $W_0,\ldots,W_N$ with zero endpoints, the displayed expression is a [norm](norm.md): it is the Euclidean [norm](norm.md) of one vector containing both weighted lists, and zero [norm](norm.md) forces all nodal values to vanish. It approximates the [Sobolev space](sobolev-space-split.md) $H^1$ [norm](norm.md). Endpoint weights or prescribed zero endpoints must be specified when calling a discrete expression a [norm](norm.md).
