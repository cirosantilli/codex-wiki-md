# Surjectivity from injectivity modulo the Jacobson radical

↑ **Parent:** [Semi-local ring](semi-local-ring.md)

For a [finitely generated module](finitely-generated-module.md) $V$ on the right over a [semilocal ring](semi-local-ring.md), the displayed condition makes the induced [module endomorphism](module-endomorphism.md) on $V/VJ$ [injective](injective-function.md). This quotient is an [Artinian module](artinian-module.md), so [Artinian modules are co-Hopfian](artinian-modules-are-co-hopfian.md) makes that map [surjective](surjective-function.md). The remaining [quotient module](quotient-module.md) $V/\alpha(V)$ equals its product with $J$, and [Nakayama lemma](nakayama-lemma.md) makes it zero.
