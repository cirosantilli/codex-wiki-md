# Periapsis angular speed

↑ **Parent:** [Specific angular momentum](specific-angular-momentum.md)

The [specific angular momentum](specific-angular-momentum.md) of an elliptic [Kepler orbit](kepler-orbit.md) is $h=\sqrt{\mu a(1-e^2)}$. At [periapsis](periapsis.md), $r=a(1-e)$, so

$$
\dot\theta_{\rm peri}=\frac h{r^2}=n\frac{\sqrt{1+e}}{(1-e)^{3/2}}.
$$

The [mean motion](mean-motion.md) understates instantaneous angular speed near [periapsis](periapsis.md). A particle in an [exterior mean-motion resonance](exterior-mean-motion-resonance.md) can briefly overtake the planet in angle despite completing full orbits more slowly.
