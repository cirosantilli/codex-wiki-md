# Verdier duality

↑ **Parent:** [Derived category of sheaves](derived-category-of-sheaves.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verdier_duality)

On bounded constructible complexes over locally compact stratified spaces, [Verdier duality](verdier-duality.md) is $D_XE=R\mathcal Hom(E,\omega_X^\bullet)$, where $\omega_X^\bullet$ is the dualizing complex. For an oriented real $N$-manifold, $D_X\mathbb Q_X\cong\mathbb Q_X[N]$. Proper direct image commutes with this duality, and $i_x^!D_XE\cong D_{\{x\}}i_x^*E$. These properties exchange stalk support conditions with [costalk](costalk.md) conditions.
