# Bell state analyzer

↑ **Parent:** [Bell-basis measurement](bell-basis-measurement.md)

A device implementing a [Bell-basis measurement](bell-basis-measurement.md) on two [qubits](qubit.md). A complete analyzer distinguishes all four [Bell states](bell-state-split.md). A basic passive [linear optics](linear-optics.md) analyzer for two polarization [qubits](qubit.md) uses a [balanced beam splitter](balanced-beam-splitter.md), [polarizing beam splitters](polarizing-beam-splitter.md) and [single-photon detectors](single-photon-detector.md). Its ideal unambiguous success probability is $1/2$: the two opposite-polarization [Bell states](bell-state-split.md) have distinct click patterns, while the two same-polarization [Bell states](bell-state-split.md) remain mutually ambiguous. Additional physical resources can change this restriction. A destructive analyzer absorbs the measured [photons](photon.md); it need not preserve the detected [Bell state](bell-state-split.md).
