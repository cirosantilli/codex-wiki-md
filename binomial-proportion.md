# Binomial proportion

↑ **Parent:** [Binomial distribution](binomial-distribution.md)

For $D\sim\operatorname{Bin}(n,p)$ with $n>0$, the sample binomial proportion is $D/n$. Its [expectation](expected-value.md) is p and its [variance](variance-split.md) is $p(1-p)/n$. The [central limit theorem](central-limit-theorem.md) gives a [normal approximation](normal-approximation.md) when both expected outcome counts are sufficiently large. These moments determine a [standard error](standard-error.md) and model-based [control limits](control-limits.md).
