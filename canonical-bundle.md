# Canonical bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Complex manifold](complex-manifold.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canonical_bundle)

The canonical bundle of a complex $n$-manifold is the holomorphic line bundle $K_X=\bigwedge^n(T^{1,0}X)^*$.
