# Velocity moment

↑ **Parent:** [Kinetic theory](kinetic-theory-split.md)

Integrating a [phase-space distribution function](phase-space-distribution-function.md) against products of particle [velocity](velocity.md) components produces its velocity moments. The zeroth gives number density, the first gives number flux, and, after multiplying by particle [mass](mass.md), the second gives [momentum flux](momentum-flux.md). Replacing $\mathbf v$ by $\mathbf v-\mathbf u$, where $\mathbf u$ is the [mean velocity](mean-velocity-of-a-kinetic-distribution.md), defines central velocity moments; the second is the [pressure tensor](pressure-tensor.md) divided by particle [mass](mass.md).
