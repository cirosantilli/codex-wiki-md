# Silyl ether

↑ **Parent:** [Ether](ether.md)

A [silyl ether](silyl-ether.md) contains an O–Si bond, commonly protecting an alcohol or [phenol](phenol.md). Cleavage at electrophilic [silicon](silicon.md) releases an [oxygen](oxygen.md)-centered anion under suitable conditions.
