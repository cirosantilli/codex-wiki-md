# Uniaxial nematic order

↑ **Parent:** [Nematic order parameter](nematic-order-parameter.md)

A uniaxial [nematic order parameter](nematic-order-parameter.md) has one distinguished [nematic director](nematic-director.md) and equal [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) in its perpendicular plane. In the displayed normalization the [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are $\lambda,-\lambda/2,-\lambda/2$, giving $\operatorname{Tr}Q^2=3\lambda^2/2$ and $\operatorname{Tr}Q^3=3\lambda^3/4$. Positive and negative $\lambda$ describe prolate and oblate orientational anisotropy, respectively; reversing the [nematic director](nematic-director.md) leaves the tensor unchanged.
