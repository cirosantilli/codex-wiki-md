# Diagonalizable restriction lemma

↑ **Parent:** [Diagonalizable matrix](diagonalizable-matrix.md)

If $W$ is an [invariant subspace](invariant-subspace.md) for a finite-dimensional [diagonalizable](diagonalizable-matrix.md) [linear map](linear-map.md) $T$, its restriction is [diagonalizable](diagonalizable-matrix.md) over the same [field](field.md). The [minimal polynomial](minimal-polynomial.md) of $T|_W$ divides that of $T$: an annihilating [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) for $T$ still annihilates the restriction, and [polynomial division](polynomial-division.md) by its [minimal polynomial](minimal-polynomial.md) proves divisibility. A divisor of a product of distinct [linear polynomials](linear-polynomial.md) remains a product of distinct [linear polynomials](linear-polynomial.md), proving the assertion. This is the key step in [simultaneous diagonalization](simultaneous-diagonalization.md) of commuting [linear maps](linear-map.md).
