# Proper-time acceleration by reflected laser light

↑ **Parent:** [Radiation pressure](radiation-pressure.md)

An ideal receding mirror reflecting a source beam of constant [power](power.md) $P$ receives [force](force-split.md) $(2P/c)(1-\beta)/(1+\beta)$ after including both Doppler-shifted reflected [momentum](momentum-split.md) and reduced impact rate. For fixed [rest mass](invariant-mass.md) $M$, use $dt/d\tau=\gamma$ in the [momentum](momentum-split.md) equation. It reduces to $d\beta/d\tau=(1-\beta)^2/\tau_0$, where $\tau_0=Mc^2/(2P)$. Starting from rest gives $\beta=\tau/(\tau+\tau_0)$. This continuum mirror model neglects individual-photon recoil and finite beam interception.
