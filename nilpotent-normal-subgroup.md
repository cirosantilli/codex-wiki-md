# Nilpotent normal subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Fitting subgroup](fitting-subgroup.md)

A [normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md) which is a [nilpotent group](nilpotent-group.md). In a [finite group](finite-group.md) its unique [Sylow subgroups](sylow-subgroup.md) are [characteristic subgroups](characteristic-subgroup.md) and hence normal in the ambient group. Products of [nilpotent normal subgroups](nilpotent-normal-subgroup.md) are [nilpotent groups](nilpotent-group.md): their normal Sylow subgroups of different prime characteristics commute.
