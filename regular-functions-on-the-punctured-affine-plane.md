# Regular functions on the punctured affine plane

↑ **Parent:** [Global regular function](global-regular-function.md)

For $W=\mathbb A_k^2\setminus\{0\}$, restriction to $D(x)$ and $D(y)$ gives

$$
\Gamma(W,\mathcal O_W)=k[x,y]_x\cap k[x,y]_y=k[x,y].
$$

The intersection is taken in $k(x,y)$ and the equality follows from [unique factorization](unique-factorization-in-an-integral-domain.md): a reduced denominator dividing powers of both $x$ and $y$ is a unit. The inclusion $W\hookrightarrow\mathbb A^2$ induces an isomorphism on global functions but is not an isomorphism, so $W$ is not an [affine variety](affine-algebraic-set.md).
