# Glutamic acid residue

↑ **Parent:** [Amino acid residue](amino-acid-residue.md)

A [glutamic acid residue](glutamic-acid-residue.md) is an [amino acid residue](amino-acid-residue.md) represented by Glu or E in [polypeptide](polypeptide.md) sequence notation. GAA and GAG specify [glutamic acid residues](glutamic-acid-residue.md) in the standard [genetic code](genetic-code.md); the [ribosome](ribosome.md) example uses GAA as its third [codon](codon.md). See [Elzanowski and Ostell, NCBI: standard genetic code, table 1](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/Taxonomy/Utils/wprintgc.cgi#SG1). The name denotes an incorporated chain position, as distinguished from a free [amino acid](amino-acid.md) [molecule](molecule-split.md).
