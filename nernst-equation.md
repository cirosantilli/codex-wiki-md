# Nernst equation

↑ **Parent:** [Electrochemistry](electrochemistry-split.md)

For a cell reaction transferring $n$ electrons, $E=E^\circ-(RT/nF)\ln Q$, with dimensionless [reaction quotient](reaction-quotient.md) $Q$ and consistently chosen [standard states](standard-state.md).
