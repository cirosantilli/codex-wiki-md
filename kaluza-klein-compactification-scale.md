# Kaluza-Klein compactification scale

↑ **Parent:** [Kaluza-Klein tower](kaluza-klein-tower.md)

The compactification scale is the first nonzero internal-mode mass. On a circle it equals $1/R$ for a higher-dimensional massless field. A zero-mode [dimensional reduction](dimensional-reduction.md) applies at energies well below it. Warping, nontrivial geometry and boundary conditions can change the relation between the first mass and a geometric radius.
