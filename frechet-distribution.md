<h1 id="frechet-distribution">Fréchet distribution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Extreme value theory](extreme-value-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fréchet_distribution)

For $\alpha>0$, the standard [Fréchet distribution](frechet-distribution.md) has [distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md) $\Phi_\alpha(x)=e^{-x^{-\alpha}}$ for $x>0$ and zero for $x\leq0$. Its [maximum domain of attraction](maximum-domain-of-attraction.md) consists of [distribution functions](cumulative-distribution-function.md) with an infinite [right endpoint of a distribution](right-endpoint-of-a-distribution.md) and a [survival function](survival-function.md) with [regular variation](regular-variation.md) of index $-\alpha$.
