# Newform

↑ **Parent:** [New subspace of cusp forms](new-subspace-of-cusp-forms.md)

A newform is a normalized [Hecke eigenform](hecke-eigenform.md) in the [new subspace of cusp forms](new-subspace-of-cusp-forms.md). Its weight, level and [Dirichlet character](dirichlet-character.md) are part of its specification. For a [newform](newform.md), its [Fourier coefficients](fourier-coefficient.md) equal its [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) of [Hecke operators](hecke-operator.md).
