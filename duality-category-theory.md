# Duality (category theory)

↑ **Parent:** [Category](category-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duality_(category_theory))

Reversing the arrows in a categorical statement produces its dual statement. A theorem holding for all [categories](category-split.md) therefore has a dual theorem obtained by applying it to the [opposite category](opposite-category.md); for example, [categorical limits](categorical-limit.md) are exchanged with [colimits](colimit.md).
