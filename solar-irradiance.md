# Solar irradiance

↑ **Parent:** [Radiometry](radiometry-split.md)

Solar radiant [power](power.md) incident per unit area. Horizontal, beam-normal and tilted-plane irradiances are different quantities: for a direct beam on a horizontal plane, $I_h=I_n\sin\alpha$, where $\alpha$ is solar elevation.
