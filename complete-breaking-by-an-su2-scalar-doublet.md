# Complete breaking by an SU2 scalar doublet

↑ **Parent:** [SU(2) gauge theory](su-2-gauge-theory.md)

A nonzero complex fundamental doublet has trivial stabilizer in the gauged $SU(2)$. Its three angular directions are gauge directions, absorbed by the [Higgs mechanism](higgs-mechanism.md). For vacuum $\phi_0=(0,v/\sqrt2)^T$, the three gauge bosons have equal mass $gv/2$; one radial scalar remains. There is no unbroken photon-like gauge generator in this theory.
