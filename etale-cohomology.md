<h1 id="etale-cohomology">Étale cohomology</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic geometry](algebraic-geometry-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Étale_cohomology)

Étale cohomology is [sheaf cohomology](sheaf-cohomology.md) on the site whose objects are [étale morphisms](etale-morphism.md) $Y\to X$ and whose coverings are jointly surjective families of such morphisms. It provides cohomological invariants for [algebraic varieties](algebraic-variety.md) over fields of positive characteristic. In [Deligne-Lusztig theory](deligne-lusztig-theory.md), compactly supported groups with $\ell$-adic coefficients, for $\ell$ different from the field characteristic, give finite-dimensional representations of the finite groups acting on the varieties.
