# Cubic endpoint-to-saddle transition

↑ **Parent:** [Degenerate endpoint in Laplace's method](degenerate-endpoint-in-laplace-s-method.md)

For a [Laplace integral](laplace-integral.md) with local phase $\phi(t;\delta)=\delta t+bt^3/3+\cdots$, $b>0$, an interior minimum approaches the endpoint as $\delta\uparrow0$. The [distinguished limit](distinguished-limit.md) is $t=O(\nu^{-1/3})$, $\delta=O(\nu^{-2/3})$. With $s=(\nu b)^{1/3}t$, its leading local integral is

$$
(\nu b)^{-1/3}I(-\delta\nu^{2/3}b^{-1/3}),\qquad I(x)=\int_0^\infty e^{xs-s^3/3}\,ds.
$$

The [cubic Laplace transition integral](cubic-laplace-transition-integral.md) joins the ordinary endpoint estimate for positive $\delta$, the cubic endpoint estimate at zero, and the interior [Laplace's method](laplace-s-method.md) estimate for negative $\delta$. Separate fixed-parameter formulas fail to be uniform when the minimum is within its own width of the endpoint.
