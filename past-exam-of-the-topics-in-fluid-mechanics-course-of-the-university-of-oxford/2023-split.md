# 2023

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the topics in fluid mechanics course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Solutions to all three questions of the [topics in fluid mechanics course of the University of Oxford](../topics-in-fluid-mechanics-course-of-the-university-of-oxford.md) examination, sat on 9 June 2023. Source: [original released PDF, inclusive bundle pages 329–334; paper pages 1–6](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/request_for_mathematics_examinat/response/3129786/attach/3/FOI%20202507%20740%20Data.pdf#page=329). All 24 lettered or Roman-numbered leaves across the three questions are covered. The original PDF labels the final part of Question 1 as (c), which is the hierarchy used here.
