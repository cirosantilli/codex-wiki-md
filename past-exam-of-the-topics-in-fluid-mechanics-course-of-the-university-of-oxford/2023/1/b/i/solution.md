<h1 id="1/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For an [inverted falling film](../../../../../inverted-falling-film.md), keep $z$ directed from the solid into the liquid. The downslope component of [gravity](../../../../../gravity.md) is unchanged, but its normal component is now positive. Thus the [hydrostatic pressure](../../../../../hydrostatic-pressure.md) changes from $(h-z)\cot\alpha$ to $-(h-z)\cot\alpha$. The resulting [Benney equation](../../../../../benney-equation.md) is

$$
\boxed{h_t+2h^2h_x+\varepsilon\partial_x\left[
h^3\left(+\frac23h_x\cot\alpha+S h_{xxx}\right)
+\frac8{15}\mathrm{Re}\,h^6h_x\right]=0.}
$$

Only the normal-[gravity](../../../../../gravity.md) contribution changes sign; the [surface tension](../../../../../surface-tension-split.md) and inertial contributions retain their signs.
