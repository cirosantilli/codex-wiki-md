<h1 id="1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Define $A=2\cot\alpha/3+8\mathrm{Re}/15$. The [linearization](../../../../../linearization.md) about $h=1$ gives

$$
\eta_t+2\eta_x+\varepsilon(A\eta_{xx}+S\eta_{xxxx})=0.
$$

The [Galilean transformation](../../../../../galilean-transformation.md) $\xi=x-2t$, $\tau=t$ has $\partial_t=\partial_\tau-2\partial_\xi$ and $\partial_x=\partial_\xi$. It removes the leading [advection](../../../../../advection.md), leaving

$$
\eta_\tau+\varepsilon(A\eta_{\xi\xi}+S\eta_{\xi\xi\xi\xi})=0.
$$

For a [normal mode](../../../../../normal-mode.md) proportional to $e^{\sigma\tau+ik\xi}$, the second and fourth [derivatives](../../../../../derivative.md) contribute $-k^2$ and $k^4$. The [dispersion relation](../../../../../dispersion-relation-split.md) is therefore

$$
\boxed{\sigma=\varepsilon k^2\left(\frac23\cot\alpha+\frac8{15}\mathrm{Re}-Sk^2\right)
=\varepsilon k^2(A-Sk^2).}
$$

For $A>0$ and $S>0$, the unstable [wavenumbers](../../../../../wavenumber.md) satisfy $0<|k|<\sqrt{A/S}$. In the laboratory coordinates, the temporal exponent is $\sigma-2ik$, so this [linear stability](../../../../../linear-stability.md) calculation predicts growth together with downslope transport at dimensionless speed $2$.
