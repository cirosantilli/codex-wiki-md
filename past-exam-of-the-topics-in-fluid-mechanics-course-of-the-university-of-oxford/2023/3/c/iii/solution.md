<h1 id="3/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For the approximate [similarity profile](../../../../../similarity-profile.md), $F'=A\operatorname{erf}(\xi/\sqrt2)$, and therefore

$$
\boxed{V=-\Delta U\sqrt{CS}\,[Be^{-\xi^2/2}+D],\qquad
V(\pm\infty)=-\Delta U\sqrt{CS}\,D.}
$$

For the prescribed undisturbed horizontal streams, a suitable choice is **$D=0$**, which gives zero transverse [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) at both far-field limits. Then $V=-\Delta U\sqrt{CS}\,B e^{-\xi^2/2}$ inside the layer. The nonzero internal crossflow is consistent with the streamwise widening of the [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) transition.

Another possible convention is to make the line $y=0$ a [streamline](../../../../../streamline.md), imposing $V(0)=0$. This requires $D=-B$, equivalently $F(0)=0$, and gives $V(\pm\infty)=\Delta U\sqrt{CS}\,B$. It therefore requires a uniform outer crossflow relative to the coordinates used for the centred [error function](../../../../../error-function.md) profile. A single constant cannot impose both zero far-field crossflow and $V(0)=0$ unless $\Delta U=0$. The physical lateral [boundary conditions](../../../../../boundary-condition.md), or the chosen material centreline, determine which convention is appropriate. Since $B,D=O(r)$, these transverse [velocities](../../../../../velocity.md) are of order $\Delta U^2/U_c$ for fixed $C,S$.
