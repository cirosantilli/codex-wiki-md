<h1 id="2/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Return to dimensional variables. The river at base level imposes the drained [boundary condition](../../../../../boundary-condition.md), while the right-hand end is impermeable:

$$
\boxed{h(0,t)=0,\qquad q(L,t)=-Kh(L,t)h_x(L,t)=0.}
$$

As long as the right-hand end remains wet, the latter is $h_x(L,t)=0$. The [initial condition](../../../../../initial-condition.md) for the drought is the height profile inherited at $t=t^*$; stopping rainfall changes the forcing but does not instantaneously remove stored water.
