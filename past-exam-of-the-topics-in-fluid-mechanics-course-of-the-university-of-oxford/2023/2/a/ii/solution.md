<h1 id="2/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $H\ll L$ be the characteristic saturated thickness and let $\delta=H/L$. Scale [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) by $\rho gH$. The horizontal part of [Darcy's law](../../../../../darcy-law.md) gives the scale

$$
u\sim\frac{k\rho g}{\mu}\frac HL=K\delta,\qquad
K=\frac{k\rho g}{\mu}.
$$

By [incompressibility](../../../../../incompressible-flow.md), $w\sim\delta u\sim K\delta^2$. The vertical part of [Darcy's law](../../../../../darcy-law.md) is

$$
w=-\frac{k}{\mu}(p_z+\rho g).
$$

Its left side is smaller than the scale $K$ by $\delta^2$. Therefore $p_z+\rho g=O(\rho g\delta^2)$, and the leading [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) is [hydrostatic pressure](../../../../../hydrostatic-pressure.md):

$$
\boxed{p=p_a+\rho g[h(x,t)-z]+O(\rho gH\delta^2).}
$$

Here the [water table](../../../../../water-table.md) has [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) $p_a$. This is the [Dupuit approximation](../../../../../dupuit-approximation.md), valid away from the small region near an outlet where the surface slope need not remain small.
