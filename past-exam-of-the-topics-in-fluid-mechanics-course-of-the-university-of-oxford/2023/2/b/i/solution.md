<h1 id="2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Use the dimensionless equation from part (a). For the [similarity ansatz](../../../../../similarity-ansatz.md) $h=t^af(\eta)$, $\eta=x/t^b$, its three terms have time factors

$$
h_t=t^{a-1}(af-b\eta f'),\qquad
(hh_x)_x=t^{2a-2b}(ff')',\qquad 1=t^0.
$$

A nontrivial [similarity solution](../../../../../similarity-solution.md) retaining storage, lateral drainage and nonzero rainfall requires the exponents to agree:

$$
a-1=2a-2b=0,\qquad \boxed{a=b=1.}
$$

Unlike a source-free [diffusion equation](../../../../../diffusion-equation-split.md), the nonzero prescribed rainfall fixes an amplitude-versus-time scale directly, so no additional conserved quantity or [boundary condition](../../../../../boundary-condition.md) is required to determine these exponents. Degenerate spatially uniform solutions are an exception to this all-terms balance. The [rain-fed aquifer similarity solution](../../../../../rain-fed-aquifer-similarity-solution.md) satisfies

$$
\boxed{(ff')'+\eta f'-f+1=0,\qquad h=t f(x/t).}
$$

If the dimensional equation is used directly without scaling out coefficients, its [similarity profile](../../../../../similarity-profile.md) instead obeys $\phi(f-\eta f')=K(ff')'+R$. The normalization above is the one in which the subsequent condition $f(\infty)=1$ applies.
