<h1 id="2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The condition $f(0)=0$ means the river maintains the [water table](../../../../../water-table.md) at the impermeable base level: $h(0,t)=0$. The liquid is exposed to [atmospheric pressure](../../../../../atmospheric-pressure.md) there. It does not imply zero drainage, because $h_x$ may be singular at the outlet.

Far from the river, lateral drainage has not significantly affected storage. Rainfall simply fills pore volume, so the dimensional height is $h\sim Rt/\phi$. In the dimensionless variables this is $h\sim t$, giving $f(\infty)=1$.

Although the physical [aquifer](../../../../../aquifer.md) is finite, these [boundary conditions](../../../../../boundary-condition.md) describe its early [similarity solution](../../../../../similarity-solution.md) before the drainage-affected region reaches $x=L$. In dimensional variables its horizontal extent is of order $\sqrt{KR}\,t/\phi$, so the semi-infinite approximation requires $t\ll T=\phi L/\sqrt{KR}$.
