# Global binding integral of the hypervirial model

↑ **Parent:** [Hypervirial model](hypervirial-model.md)

In unit scales $I=\int\rho\psi d^3x=(p+1)\Gamma(1+1/p)^2/[p\Gamma(2+2/p)]$. The [local virial relation of the hypervirial model](local-virial-relation-of-the-hypervirial-model.md) gives global energies $T=I/4$ and $W=-I/2$. Restoring physical scales multiplies each integral by $GM^2/a$.
