# Minimal total family in a normed space

↑ **Parent:** [Normed vector space](normed-vector-space-split.md)

A total family has dense [linear span](linear-span.md); it is minimal when no [vector](vector.md) lies in the closed span of all the others. Equivalently, no proper subfamily remains total. Such a family has unique bounded coordinate functionals making a [biorthogonal system](biorthogonal-system.md). Indeed put $M_i=\overline{\operatorname{span}}\{x_j:j\ne i\}$ and $d_i=\operatorname{dist}(x_i,M_i)>0$. The functional $m+tx_i\mapsto t$ on $M_i+\mathbb Rx_i$ is bounded by $1/d_i$, so the [Hahn-Banach theorem](hahn-banach-theorem.md) extends it to $X$. Its [norm](norm.md) is exactly $1/d_i$, by the distance infimum. Uniqueness follows because its values on the total family determine it on a dense [subspace](vector-subspace.md). For a countable family, minimality and totality alone do not ensure norm-convergent expansions; the [uniform partial-sum criterion for a Schauder basis](uniform-partial-sum-criterion-for-a-schauder-basis.md) supplies the additional condition.
