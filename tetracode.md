# Tetracode

↑ **Parent:** [Linear code](linear-code.md)

The two-dimensional ternary [linear code](linear-code.md) generated by $(0,1,1,1)$ and $(1,1,-1,0)$. Its eight nonzero words have exactly three nonzero entries, with precisely two opposite words for each position of the zero coordinate.
