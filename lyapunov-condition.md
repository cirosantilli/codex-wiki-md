# Lyapunov condition

↑ **Parent:** [Lindeberg-Feller central limit theorem](lindeberg-feller-central-limit-theorem.md)

For independent centered [random variables](random-variable-split.md) with total [variance](variance-split.md) $s_n^2$, the Lyapunov condition requires the displayed expression to tend to zero for some $\delta>0$. The inequality $z^2\mathbf1_{\{|z|>\varepsilon s_n\}}\le |z|^{2+\delta}/(\varepsilon s_n)^\delta$ implies the [Lindeberg condition](lindeberg-condition.md) after normalization. The [Lindeberg-Feller central limit theorem](lindeberg-feller-central-limit-theorem.md) then makes the normalized sum converge to the standard [normal distribution](normal-distribution.md).
