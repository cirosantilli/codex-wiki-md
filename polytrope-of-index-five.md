# Polytrope of index five

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar polytrope](stellar-polytrope.md)

The regular $n=5$ [Lane-Emden equation](lane-emden-equation.md) solution has no finite first zero. Its [Lane-Emden mass formula](lane-emden-mass-formula.md) gives $m(\xi)=4\pi\rho_c\alpha^3\xi^3/[3(1+\xi^2/3)^{3/2}]$, so its infinite-radius total [mass](mass.md) is $4\pi\sqrt3\rho_c\alpha^3$. Its enclosed mean [mass density](density.md) is $\rho_c(1+\xi^2/3)^{-3/2}$ and tends to zero. A finite externally confined core instead requires a specified truncation pressure.
