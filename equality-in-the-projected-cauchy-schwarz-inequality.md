# Equality in the projected Cauchy-Schwarz inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md)

For an [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md) $P$ onto $W$ in an [inner product space](inner-product-space.md), apply the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) to $Pu,Pv$ and then use $\|Pu\|\leq\|u\|$, $\|Pv\|\leq\|v\|$. If either original [vector](vector.md) is [zero](zero-of-a-function.md), equality holds. Otherwise equality holds exactly when both [vectors](vector.md) belong to $W$ and are [linearly dependent](linear-dependence.md). Necessity follows because equality in the product of the two [norm](norm.md) bounds forces equality in each, while equality in [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) forces dependence. This retains the [zero](zero-of-a-function.md) cases that a division by the original [norms](norm.md) would lose.
