# Main-sequence turnoff

↑ **Parent:** [Main sequence](main-sequence.md)

The main-sequence turnoff is the location in a coeval population's [Hertzsprung-Russell diagram](hertzsprung-russell-diagram.md) where stars are leaving the [main sequence](main-sequence.md) after exhausting their core hydrogen. More massive stars generally have larger [luminosity](luminosity.md) per unit [mass](mass.md) and shorter nuclear lifetimes, so the most massive stars still on the main sequence constrain the population's age. In a simple model, equating the [stellar nuclear timescale](stellar-nuclear-timescale.md) $t\sim qM/L(M)$ to the common age determines a turnoff mass; detailed calibration requires composition and stellar models.
