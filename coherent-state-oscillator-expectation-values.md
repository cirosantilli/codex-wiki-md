# Coherent-state oscillator expectation values

↑ **Parent:** [Coherent state](coherent-state.md)

A normalized oscillator [coherent state](coherent-state.md) obeys $a|\alpha\rangle=\alpha|\alpha\rangle$, so its mean [occupation number](occupation-number.md) is $N=|\alpha|^2$. Under the [quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md) [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) $\hbar\omega(a^\dagger a+1/2)$, its evolution is $|\alpha(t)\rangle=e^{-i\omega t/2}|\alpha e^{-i\omega t}\rangle$. Inserting $x=\sqrt{\hbar/(2m\omega)}(a+a^\dagger)$ and $p=-i\sqrt{m\hbar\omega/2}(a-a^\dagger)$ gives

$$
\langle x\rangle_t=\sqrt{\frac{2\hbar}{m\omega}}\operatorname{Re}(\alpha e^{-i\omega t}),\qquad
\langle p\rangle_t=\sqrt{2m\hbar\omega}\operatorname{Im}(\alpha e^{-i\omega t}).
$$

These mean values obey the classical [harmonic oscillator](simple-harmonic-motion.md) equations. The displacement amplitude is $\sqrt{2\hbar N/(m\omega)}$, whose classical orbit has [energy](energy-split.md) $\hbar\omega N$. The full quantum mean [energy](energy-split.md) has the additional [zero-point energy](zero-point-energy.md) $\hbar\omega/2$. Indeed the variances are $\hbar/(2m\omega)$ and $m\hbar\omega/2$, and their kinetic and potential contributions sum to $\hbar\omega/2$.
