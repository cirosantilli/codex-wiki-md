# Optical soliton

↑ **Parent:** [Nonlinear optics](nonlinear-optics.md)

An optical [wave envelope](envelope-waves.md) whose spreading is balanced by nonlinear refraction. A [temporal optical soliton](temporal-optical-soliton.md) balances dispersion, while a [spatial optical soliton](spatial-optical-soliton.md) balances [diffraction](diffraction.md). Persistence of a profile in an ideal propagation model does not by itself establish robustness in a lossy, higher-dimensional or delayed-response medium.
