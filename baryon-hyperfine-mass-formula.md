# Baryon hyperfine mass formula

↑ **Parent:** [Constituent-quark model](constituent-quark-model.md)

This effective [baryon](baryon.md) mass model adds a colour-magnetic [spin-spin interaction](spin-spin-interaction.md) to a sum of [constituent quark masses](constituent-quark-mass.md). The common coefficient $A'$ absorbs a spatial contact density, colour factors, and the strong coupling. $A'$ has dimensions of [energy](energy-split.md) cubed in [natural units](natural-units.md). A shared coefficient is an approximation because different flavour configurations can have different spatial [wavefunctions](wave-function-split.md).
