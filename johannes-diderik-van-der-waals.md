The [Van der Waals equation](van-der-waals-equation.md) connects a dilute [gas](gas.md) model to the possibility of a [liquid-gas critical point](liquid-gas-critical-point.md). Its usefulness includes showing how attractions and [excluded volume](excluded-volume.md) can change an [equation of state](equation-of-state.md); its simple constants do not make it an exact model for every fluid.
