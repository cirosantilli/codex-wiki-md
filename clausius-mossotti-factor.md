# Clausius-Mossotti factor

↑ **Parent:** [Dielectrophoresis](dielectrophoresis.md)

The spherical-particle polarization-contrast factor $\widetilde K=(\widetilde\epsilon_p-\widetilde\epsilon_m)/(\widetilde\epsilon_p+2\widetilde\epsilon_m)$. Its real part controls ordinary time-averaged [dielectrophoresis](dielectrophoresis.md) in the quasistatic dipole approximation.
