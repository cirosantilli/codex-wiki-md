# Inductance of a toroidal winding

↑ **Parent:** [Inductance](inductance.md)

For a slender winding around a [toroid](toroid.md) with $N$ turns, core area $A$, mean magnetic path length $\ell$ and approximately constant [magnetic permeability](permeability-electromagnetism.md) $\mu$, [Ampère's law](ampere-s-circuital-law.md) gives $H\ell=NI$. The linked magnetic flux is $N\Phi=N\mu HA$, so $L=N\Phi/I=\mu N^2A/\ell$. This approximation neglects flux leakage and variation across the core and assumes operation below [magnetic saturation](magnetic-saturation.md). With a nonlinear magnetic core, an incremental inductance instead depends on differential permeability at the operating point.
