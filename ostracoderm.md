# Ostracoderm

↑ **Parent:** [Vertebrate](vertebrate.md)

A traditional assemblage of armoured jawless [fossil](fossil.md) [vertebrates](vertebrate.md). It is not a complete [clade](clade.md), and many members are stem relatives of [gnathostomes](gnathostome.md), rather than early [cyclostomes](cyclostome.md).
