# First Tor of a cyclic quotient

↑ **Parent:** [Tor functor](tor-functor.md)

Start a [free resolution](free-resolution.md) of $A/I$ with $P_0=A$ and a free module $P_1$ surjecting onto $I$. The next differential presents the relations in $I$. Right exactness of the [tensor product of modules](tensor-product-of-modules.md) identifies $\operatorname{coker}(P_2\otimes_AM\to P_1\otimes_AM)$ with $I\otimes_AM$. Taking degree-one homology then gives the displayed kernel formula. Hence vanishing for every ideal is equivalent to the [flatness criterion using ideals](flatness-criterion-using-ideals.md).
