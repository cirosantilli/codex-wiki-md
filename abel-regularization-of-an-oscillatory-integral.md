# Abel regularization of an oscillatory integral

↑ **Parent:** [Oscillatory integral](oscillatory-integral.md)

An exponential damping factor makes many otherwise conditionally convergent or divergent [oscillatory integrals](oscillatory-integral.md) absolutely convergent. Removing the damping after integration defines an Abel limit when it exists. For the [Bessel function](bessel-function.md) $J_1$, $\int_0^\infty e^{-ak}J_1(kR)dk=R^{-1}(1-a/\sqrt{a^2+R^2})$, giving the [Mestel disk](mestel-disk.md) radial field as $a\downarrow0$. This does not remove an unrelated additive divergence of the potential at low wavenumber.
