# Macrauchenia

↑ **Parent:** [Placental mammal](placental-mammal.md)

An extinct South American mammal whose unusual anatomy made its relationships difficult to resolve. Ancient protein sequences support affinities with the perissodactyl lineage. [Nature 522, pp. 81–84; collagen sequences from Toxodon and Macrauchenia](ancient-proteins-resolve-the-evolutionary-history-of-darwin-s-south-american-ungulates-by-frido-welker-and-others.md)
