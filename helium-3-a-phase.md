# Helium-3 A phase

↑ **Parent:** [Helium-3 pair order parameter](helium-3-pair-order-parameter.md)

Take a real [unit vector](unit-vector.md) in spin space $\widehat{\mathbf d}$ and choose orthogonal orbital axes. The model has

$$
A_A=\Delta\widehat{\mathbf d}\,(1,i,0),\qquad
|\mathbf d_A|=\Delta\sqrt{\widehat k_x^2+\widehat k_y^2}
=\Delta|\sin\theta|.
$$

It vanishes at the two polar directions and is largest at the equator. The orbital factor changes sign when $\widehat{\mathbf k}$ is reversed, as required for the odd orbital part of [spin-triplet p-wave pairing](spin-triplet-p-wave-pairing.md). Calling the phase A does not mean its [order parameter](order-parameter.md) is one real number.
