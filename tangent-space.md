# Tangent space

↑ **Parent:** [Tangent vector](tangent-vector.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tangent_space)

The tangent space $T_pM$ is the [vector space](vector-space-split.md) of tangent vectors to a [smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md) $M$ at $p$. For an embedded manifold $M\subseteq\mathbb R^N$, it is

$$
T_pM=\{\gamma'(0):\gamma:(-\varepsilon,\varepsilon)\to M
\text{ is smooth and }\gamma(0)=p\}.
$$

For a regular parametrized surface $X(u,v)$, it is spanned by $X_u$ and $X_v$.
