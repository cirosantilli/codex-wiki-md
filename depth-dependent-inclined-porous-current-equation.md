# Depth-dependent inclined porous-current equation

↑ **Parent:** [Inclined porous gravity current](inclined-porous-gravity-current.md)

For [porosity](porosity.md) $Pz^{\alpha-1}$ and [permeability of a porous medium](permeability-of-a-porous-medium.md) $Kz^{\beta-1}$ above an inclined impermeable plane, stored liquid volume per area is $Ph^\alpha/\alpha$. The depth-integrated [Darcy flux](darcy-velocity.md) is $\rho gKh^\beta(\sin\theta-\cos\theta\,h_x,-\cos\theta\,h_y)/(\beta\mu)$. [Conservation of mass](mass-conservation.md) yields the displayed equation with $X=x\tan\theta$, $Y=y\tan\theta$ and $T=t\alpha\rho gK\sin\theta\tan\theta/(\beta\mu P)$.
