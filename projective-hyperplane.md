# Projective hyperplane

↑ **Parent:** [Complex projective space](complex-projective-space.md)

A projective hyperplane in $\mathbb {CP}^n$ is the zero set of a nonzero complex-linear functional on $\mathbb C^{n+1}$. It is isomorphic to $\mathbb {CP}^{n-1}$ and determines a [hyperplane divisor](hyperplane-divisor.md).
