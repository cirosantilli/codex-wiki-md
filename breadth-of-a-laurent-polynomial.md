# Breadth of a Laurent polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Laurent polynomial](laurent-polynomial.md)

For a nonzero [Laurent polynomial](laurent-polynomial.md), its breadth is the largest exponent with nonzero coefficient minus the smallest such exponent. Unlike its highest exponent, it is unchanged by multiplying by a Laurent monomial [unit](unit-in-a-ring.md). The breadth of a nonzero constant is zero.
