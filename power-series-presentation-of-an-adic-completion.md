# Power-series presentation of an adic completion

↑ **Parent:** [Adic completion of a module](adic-completion-of-a-module.md)

If $I=(a_1,\ldots,a_s)$, evaluation $X_i\mapsto a_i$ gives a ring map to the $I$-adic completion, with each series evaluated modulo $I^r$ using only terms of total degree below $r$. It is surjective: successively represent the residual class in $I^r/I^{r+1}$ by a homogeneous degree-$r$ polynomial in the $a_i$. If $R$ is a commutative [Noetherian ring](noetherian-ring.md), [Noetherianity of formal power series rings](noetherianity-of-formal-power-series-rings.md) therefore makes its completion Noetherian.
