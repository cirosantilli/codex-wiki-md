# One-dimensional freely jointed chain

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical physics](statistical-physics-split.md)

This is the one-dimensional version of a [freely jointed chain](ideal-chain.md), an [ideal chain](ideal-chain.md). A chain of $n$ links of length $a$, each pointing independently right or left, has extension

$$
l=(n_+-n_-)a=(2n_+-n)a
$$

and multiplicity $n!/(n_+!n_-!)$.
