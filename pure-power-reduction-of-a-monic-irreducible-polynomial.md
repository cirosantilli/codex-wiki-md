# Pure-power reduction of a monic irreducible polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Unique extension of an absolute value to a finite extension](unique-extension-of-an-absolute-value-to-a-finite-extension.md)

Suppose a [Non-Archimedean absolute value](non-archimedean-absolute-value.md) extends uniquely to every finite extension. If $f\in\mathcal O_K[X]$ is [monic](monic-polynomial.md) and irreducible, every root has absolute value at most one: otherwise its leading power dominates all lower terms, contradicting $f(\alpha)=0$ by the [ultrametric inequality](ultrametric-inequality.md). In a [splitting field](splitting-field.md), let $\varphi$ be the [minimal polynomial of an algebraic element](minimal-polynomial-of-an-algebraic-element.md) of the residue of one root. Lift its coefficients to $\mathcal O_K$. The lift has absolute value less than one at that root and hence, by [equal absolute values of algebraic conjugates](equal-absolute-values-of-algebraic-conjugates.md), at every root. Consequently every root residue is a zero of $\varphi$. Since reduction preserves the product of the linear factors with multiplicities, every irreducible factor of $\overline f$ is $\varphi$, proving the displayed identity for some $m\geq1$.
