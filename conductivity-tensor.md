# Conductivity tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Electrical conductivity](electrical-conductivity.md)

The [tensor](tensor.md) relating local [current density](current-density.md) to [electric field](electric-field-split.md). Its inverse, when it exists, is the [electrical resistivity](electrical-resistivity.md) [tensor](tensor.md); [sheet conductivity](surface-conductivity.md) uses [surface current density](surface-current-density.md) instead.
