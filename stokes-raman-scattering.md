# Stokes Raman scattering

↑ **Parent:** [Raman scattering](raman-scattering.md)

In [Stokes Raman scattering](stokes-raman-scattering.md), matter gains internal [energy](energy-split.md) and the [photon](photon.md) loses the same amount. For a [quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md) representing one [molecular vibration](molecular-vibration.md), $E_v=h\nu_v(v+1/2)$ with $v=0,1,\ldots$. The transition $v\to v+1$ gives $\nu_s=\nu_0-\nu_v$, requiring $\nu_0>\nu_v$ for an outgoing [photon](photon.md). Even a molecule initially in its vibrational [ground state](ground-state.md) can undergo this process. The shared name does not turn it into the absorption/emission-band [Stokes shift](stokes-shift.md) used for [fluorescence](fluorescence-split.md).
