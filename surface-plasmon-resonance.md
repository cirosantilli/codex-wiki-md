# Surface plasmon resonance

↑ **Parent:** [Biophysical measurement](biophysical-measurement.md)

[Surface plasmon resonance](surface-plasmon-resonance.md) can monitor binding near an optical sensor surface in real time through refractive-index changes. Concentration-dependent association and dissociation can constrain kinetics, subject to controls for nonspecific binding, transport limitation and multivalency.
