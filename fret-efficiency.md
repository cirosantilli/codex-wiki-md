# FRET efficiency

↑ **Parent:** [Förster resonance energy transfer](forster-resonance-energy-transfer.md)

[FRET efficiency](fret-efficiency.md) is the [probability](probability.md) that an excitation of the [FRET donor](fret-donor.md) ends in transfer to the [FRET acceptor](fret-acceptor.md). Write $k_D=k_{\rm r}+k_{\rm nr}$ for the total decay rate without a [FRET acceptor](fret-acceptor.md), including radiative [fluorescence](fluorescence.md) and other decay, and $k_T$ for the transfer rate. For constant competing rates,

$$
k_T=k_D\left(\frac{R_0}{r}\right)^6,\qquad E=\frac{k_T}{k_D+k_T}=\int_0^\infty k_T e^{-(k_D+k_T)t}\,dt.
$$

The [integral](integral.md) counts transfer events among all excitations in a population with [exponential decay](exponential-decay.md). Substitution gives the separation law above. This short rate argument assumes one [FRET donor](fret-donor.md), one [FRET acceptor](fret-acceptor.md), weak coupling, an available [FRET acceptor](fret-acceptor.md), and fixed geometry during the decay; it is not a model for arbitrary interacting labels.[§3.1, equations 14–16 and discussion before §3.3: single-pair assumptions and transfer efficiency](advanced-fluorescence-microscopy-techniques-frap-flip-flap-fret-and-flim-by-hellen-c-ishikawa-ankerhold-and-others.md)
