# Conducting-wedge potential expansion

↑ **Parent:** [Electrostatics](electrostatics-split.md)

In a charge-free wedge $0<\theta<\alpha$ bounded by equal-potential conductor faces, separation of Laplace's equation gives the displayed local harmonic expansion. Bounded potential at a finite corner removes negative powers. Outer [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md) determine the remaining coefficients; the conductor potential alone does not determine a nonconstant solution in an unbounded domain.
