# Branching of a representation

↑ **Parent:** [Restriction of a representation](restriction-of-a-representation.md)

The branching of a [group representation](group-representation.md) $V$ to a [subgroup](subgroup.md) $H$ is its decomposition into [irreducible representations](irreducible-representation.md) of $H$, when that [restriction of a representation](restriction-of-a-representation.md) is a [semisimple representation](semisimple-representation.md): $V|_H=\bigoplus_\lambda m_\lambda W_\lambda$. The nonnegative integers $m_\lambda$ are the branching multiplicities. For [finite groups](finite-group.md) over the [complex numbers](complex-number.md), they are the [character inner products](character-inner-product.md) of the restricted [character](character-of-a-representation.md) with the [irreducible characters](irreducible-character.md) of $H$. Restriction to an arbitrary [subgroup](subgroup.md) need not be completely reducible.
