# Spectrum of the Laplacian on a sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral geometry](spectral-geometry.md)

For the unit $S^d$ and the nonnegative [Laplace-Beltrami operator](laplace-beltrami-operator.md), the [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) are $\ell(\ell+d-1)$, with multiplicities $\binom{\ell+d}{d}-\binom{\ell+d-2}{d}$ for $\ell\geq0$. The [eigenfunctions](eigenfunction.md) are restrictions of degree-$\ell$ [harmonic polynomials](harmonic-polynomial.md). The [harmonic decomposition of homogeneous polynomials](harmonic-decomposition-of-homogeneous-polynomials.md) and the [Stone-Weierstrass theorem](stone-weierstrass-theorem.md) prove completeness.
