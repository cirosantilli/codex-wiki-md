# Matrix Lie group

↑ **Parent:** [Lie group](lie-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matrix_Lie_group)

A matrix Lie group is a subgroup of a [general linear group](general-linear-group.md) that is also a [Lie group](lie-group.md) for its matrix topology. Near the identity, the [matrix exponential](matrix-exponential.md) and [matrix logarithm](matrix-logarithm.md) give inverse coordinate charts whenever they are restricted to sufficiently small neighborhoods in the group and its tangent space.
