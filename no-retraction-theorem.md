# No-retraction theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Topological analysis](topological-analysis-split.md)

No closed disc retracts continuously onto its boundary. This is equivalent to [Brouwer fixed-point theorem](brouwer-fixed-point-theorem.md) by the standard ray construction and the antipodal map.
