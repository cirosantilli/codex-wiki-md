<h1 id="alfven-mach-number">Alfvén Mach number</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Alfvén speed](alfven-speed.md)

The Alfvén Mach number compares a characteristic flow speed with the [Alfvén speed](alfven-speed.md) $v_A=B/\sqrt{\mu_0\rho}$. In an [axisymmetric magnetohydrodynamic wind](axisymmetric-magnetohydrodynamic-wind.md), the relevant poloidal quantity is $M_{Ap}=|\mathbf u_p|\sqrt{\mu_0\rho}/|\mathbf B_p|$. With [magnetohydrodynamic mass loading](magnetohydrodynamic-mass-loading.md) $\rho\mathbf u_p=\kappa\mathbf B_p$, its square is $\mu_0\kappa^2/\rho$. The [Alfvén surface](alfven-surface.md) is the locus $M_{Ap}=1$, and a smooth crossing requires the corresponding [Alfvén-surface regularity condition for an axisymmetric wind](alfven-surface-regularity-condition-for-an-axisymmetric-wind.md). The total-speed and poloidal-speed definitions should be distinguished when there is substantial rotation or a strong [toroidal magnetic field](toroidal-magnetic-field.md).
