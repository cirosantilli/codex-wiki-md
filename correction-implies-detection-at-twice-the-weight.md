# Correction implies detection at twice the weight

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum error detection](quantum-error-detection.md)

Split the support of a [Pauli operator](pauli-operator.md) of weight at most $2t$ into two sets of size at most $t$. It is, up to a scalar phase, $E_a^\dagger E_b$ for two errors in the correctable family. The [Knill--Laflamme condition](knill-laflamme-condition.md) makes its compression to the code scalar, which is [quantum error detection](quantum-error-detection.md). Expansion in the Pauli basis extends the assertion to every operator supported on at most $2t$ [qubits](qubit.md). The number $t$ counts affected qubits, not the cardinality of an arbitrary selected family of errors.
