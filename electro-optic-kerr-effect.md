# Electro-optic Kerr effect

↑ **Parent:** [Kerr effect](kerr-effect.md)

The [electro-optic Kerr effect](electro-optic-kerr-effect.md) produces [linear birefringence](linear-birefringence.md) proportional to the square of a static applied [electric field](electric-field-split.md). Defining the [Kerr effect](kerr-effect.md) coefficient $K$ by $\Delta n=\lambda_0 K E^2$, a length $L$ produces [phase retardation](phase-retardation.md) $\delta=2\pi KLE^2$. A switchable [quarter-wave plate](quarter-wave-plate.md) requires $|\delta|=\pi/2$ modulo $2\pi$, with fixed principal axes. This differs from the [Faraday effect](faraday-effect.md), whose eigenpolarizations are [circular polarizations](circular-polarization.md).
