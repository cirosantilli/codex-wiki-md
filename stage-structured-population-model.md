# Stage-structured population model

↑ **Parent:** [Population dynamics](population-dynamics.md)

A [stage-structured population model](stage-structured-population-model.md) tracks distinct life stages, such as juveniles and adults, with separate reproduction, survival and transition terms. A [matrix](matrix.md) update describes the linear case; density-dependent reproduction gives a nonlinear [discrete dynamical system](discrete-dynamical-system-split.md).
