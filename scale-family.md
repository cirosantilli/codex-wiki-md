# Scale family

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical model](statistical-model-split.md)

A scale family rescales a fixed distribution by a positive parameter. The standardized variable $Y/\sigma$ has density $f$, independent of $\sigma$. This simplifies moments, simulations and the [Jeffreys prior for a scale parameter](jeffreys-prior-for-a-scale-parameter.md).
