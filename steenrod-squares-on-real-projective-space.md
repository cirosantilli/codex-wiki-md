# Steenrod squares on real projective space

↑ **Parent:** [Real projective space](real-projective-space.md)

For the degree-one mod-two generator, $\operatorname{Sq}^1x=x^2$ and higher squares of $x$ vanish by instability. The Cartan formula gives the displayed binomial identity. Naturality applies it to relative classes of a [stunted real projective space](stunted-real-projective-space.md).
