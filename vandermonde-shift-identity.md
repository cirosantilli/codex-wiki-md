# Vandermonde shift identity

↑ **Parent:** [Vandermonde determinant](vandermonde-determinant.md)

The left side is an [alternating polynomial](alternating-polynomial.md) of total degree one more than $\Delta(x)$. Dividing by the [Vandermonde determinant](vandermonde-determinant.md) gives a symmetric homogeneous [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) of degree one, necessarily $a\sum_i x_i+bt$. At $t=0$, $a=1$; differentiation in $t$ and the [Euler theorem for homogeneous functions](euler-theorem-for-homogeneous-functions.md) give $b=\binom m2$. Thus the identity holds as a [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) identity, even at repeated coordinates. At shifted partition coordinates with $t=-1$, it proves the removable-corner recurrence in the [hook-length formula](hook-length-formula.md).
