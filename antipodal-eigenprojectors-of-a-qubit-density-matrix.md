# Antipodal eigenprojectors of a qubit density matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Bloch ball](bloch-ball.md)

For a qubit [density matrix](density-matrix.md) $\rho=(I+n\cdot\sigma)/2$ with nonzero [Bloch vector](bloch-vector.md), $\widehat n=n/\|n\|$ and the multiplication rule for the [Pauli matrices](pauli-matrices.md) give these orthogonal rank-one [spectral projectors](spectral-projector.md), with eigenvalues $(1\pm\|n\|)/2$. Their [Bloch vectors](bloch-vector.md) are opposite unit vectors $\pm\widehat n$. At $n=0$ the state is maximally mixed and the direction is undefined; any orthonormal eigenbasis gives an antipodal pair, but no pair is distinguished by the state.
