# Cyclopentadienyl ligand

↑ **Parent:** [Ligand in coordination chemistry](ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md)

The [cyclopentadienyl ligand](cyclopentadienyl-ligand.md) is based on $\mathrm{C_5H_5^-}$. In its common $\eta^5$ mode it binds through five ring carbons and donates six [electrons](electron.md) in ionic [electron counting](electron-counting.md). Ring substitution can create distinct NMR environments.
