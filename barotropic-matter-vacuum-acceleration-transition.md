# Barotropic matter-vacuum acceleration transition

↑ **Parent:** [Flat barotropic matter-vacuum solution](flat-barotropic-matter-vacuum-solution.md)

For a constant-$w$ material fluid and positive vacuum [energy](energy-split.md), the [Friedmann acceleration equation](friedmann-acceleration-equation.md) gives $\ddot a/(aH_0^2)=\Omega_{\Lambda0}-(1+3w)\Omega_{m0}(1+z)^{3(1+w)}/2$. If $w>-1/3$, its zero determines the displayed transition. A past transition additionally requires the right-hand side of the displayed equation to exceed one. The equation does not imply the same transition for a fluid that already has sufficiently negative [pressure](pressure-split.md).
