# Photon polarization sum

↑ **Parent:** [Polarization vector](polarization-vector.md)

The sum over the two physical transverse photon [polarization vectors](polarization-vector.md) is not the full tensor $-\eta_{\mu\nu}$. It differs by gauge-dependent terms proportional to the photon [four-momentum](four-momentum.md). When the amplitude satisfies the [Ward identity](ward-identity.md), those terms vanish on contraction and one may use $-\eta_{\mu\nu}$ as a replacement inside the squared amplitude. This replacement must not be mistaken for a free-standing equality of physical polarization tensors.
