# Correlator

↑ **Parent:** [Cross-correlation](cross-correlation.md)

A device or algorithm that estimates the [cross-correlation](cross-correlation.md) of input signals, often by multiplying one signal by a delayed or complex-conjugated copy of another and averaging. In [radio interferometry](radio-interferometry.md), calibrated complex correlations supply [complex visibilities](complex-visibility.md). Delay tracking, channel width and integration duration determine which field information survives that averaging.
