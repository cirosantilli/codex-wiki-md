<h1 id="reich-strebel-inequality">Reich–Strebel inequality</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Teichmüller map](teichmuller-map.md)

On closed [Riemann surfaces](riemann-surfaces.md) of [genus](genus-of-a-surface.md) at least two, if a [Teichmüller map](teichmuller-map.md) $f_0:X\to Y$ has unit-area source differential $q$ and dilatation $K_0$, any [quasiconformal map](quasiconformal-mapping.md) $f:X\to Y$ in the same [homotopy class](homotopy-class.md) satisfies $K_0\leq\int_X|q|\,|1+\mu_fq/|q||^2/(1-|\mu_f|^2)$. The plus sign corresponds to a [Teichmüller map](teichmuller-map.md) with $\mu_{f_0}=k_0|q|/q$ and $K_0=(1+k_0)/(1-k_0)$. This fundamental inequality yields [Teichmüller's uniqueness theorem](teichmuller-s-uniqueness-theorem.md) by the pointwise [triangle inequality](triangle-inequality.md) and its equality case. See [Gardiner and Hu, §5, equation (11)](https://userhome.brooklyn.cuny.edu/gardiner/A%20short%20course%20on%20Teichmuller%27s%20theorem.pdf).
