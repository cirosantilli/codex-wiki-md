# Nutation

↑ **Parent:** [Heavy symmetric top](heavy-symmetric-top.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nutation)

Variation in the inclination of a rotating body's axis, distinguished from its azimuthal [precession](precession.md). For a [heavy symmetric top](heavy-symmetric-top.md), the cyclic angular momenta reduce the motion to $\dot\mu^2=F(\mu)$ with $\mu=\cos\theta$ and $F$ cubic. Its admissible interval and turning points describe the nutation.
