# Galvanometer radial field

↑ **Parent:** [Moving-coil galvanometer](moving-coil-galvanometer.md)

A [galvanometer radial field](galvanometer-radial-field.md) is approximately radial about the rotation axis within each narrow working gap. Its direction follows the moving sides of the coil over the useful sweep. Here the [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) points from the left pole toward the core and from the core toward the right pole; it is not outward everywhere. This geometry keeps the [magnetic force on a current-carrying wire](magnetic-force-on-a-current-carrying-wire.md) tangential, with nearly constant [lever arm](lever-arm.md). A spatially uniform [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) in a fixed direction would instead give an [angle](angle.md)-dependent [torque](torque.md) as the coil turns.
