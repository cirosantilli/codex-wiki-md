# Random element

↑ **Parent:** [Random variable](random-variable-split.md)

A random element is a [measurable function](measurable-function.md) from a [probability space](probability-space.md) $(\Omega,\mathcal F,\mathbb P)$ to a [measurable space](measurable-space.md) $(E,\mathcal E)$. Its law is the [pushforward measure](pushforward-measure.md) $\mathbb P_X(A)=\mathbb P(X^{-1}(A))$ for $A\in\mathcal E$. Real-valued [random variables](random-variable-split.md) and vector-valued random variables are examples; a stochastic sample path can also be a random element when its path-space sigma-algebra is specified. For a [Polish space](polish-space.md) $E$, the usual choice is its [Borel sigma-algebra](borel-sigma-algebra.md). A [large deviation principle](large-deviation-principle.md) concerns the laws of a family of such random elements.
