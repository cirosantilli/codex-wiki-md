# Circular orbit through an inverse-fifth-power singularity

↑ **Parent:** [Binet equation](binet-equation.md)

For an attractive [central force](central-force.md) of magnitude $c/r^5$, a circle of radius $R$ through the force center has polar equation $r=2R\cos\theta$. With [specific angular momentum](specific-angular-momentum.md) $h$, substitution into the [Binet equation](binet-equation.md) gives $c=8mh^2R^2$. Consequently $R$ is inversely proportional to the magnitude of the [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md), and the angular-time integral gives $T=2\pi R^2/|h|\propto R^3$. This is the finite travel time along the punctured circle from one approach to the singular center to the next; the force law alone does not specify continuation through the collision.
