# Subexponential distribution (light-tailed)

↑ **Parent:** [Probability and statistics](probability-and-statistics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subexponential_distribution_(light-tailed))

A centered random variable is sub-exponential when its [moment-generating function](moment-generating-function.md) is bounded by a Gaussian moment-generating function in a neighbourhood of zero. Equivalently up to universal constants, its tail is bounded by $2e^{-ct/K}$ beyond scale $K$.
