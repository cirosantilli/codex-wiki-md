# Pion beta decay

↑ **Parent:** [Semileptonic decay](semileptonic-decay.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Pion](pion.md)

[Pion beta decay](pion-beta-decay.md) changes a charged [pion](pion.md) into a [neutral pion](neutral-pion.md) and a lepton pair through the [weak charged current](charged-current.md). The small charged-neutral pion mass gap severely restricts [phase space](phase-space.md), giving an approximately fifth-power dependence on that gap. It does not have the charged-lepton mass suppression of direct [leptonic pion decay](leptonic-pion-decay.md).
