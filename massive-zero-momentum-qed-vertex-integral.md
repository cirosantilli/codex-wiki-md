# Massive zero-momentum QED vertex integral

↑ **Parent:** [Ultraviolet pole of the QED vertex](ultraviolet-pole-of-the-qed-vertex.md)

For the [QED](quantum-electrodynamics.md) proper [Feynman vertex](interaction-vertex.md) with both external [four-momenta](four-momentum.md) zero, a massive internal [fermion](fermion.md) and a massless internal [photon](photon.md) in [Feynman gauge](feynman-gauge.md), the even numerator reduces under [Lorentz covariance](lorentz-covariance.md) to $\gamma^\mu[\alpha k^2+\beta m^2]$. The [gamma matrices](gamma-matrices.md) identity $\gamma^\nu\gamma^\mu\gamma_\nu=(2-D)\gamma^\mu$ and the integrated replacement $k_\rho k_\sigma\mapsto g_{\rho\sigma}k^2/D$ prove the coefficients. The term $m^2/[k^2(k^2-m^2+i0)^2]$ has neither an [infrared divergence](infrared-divergence.md) nor an [ultraviolet divergence](ultraviolet-divergence.md) for $m>0$; its integral in four dimensions is $-i/(16\pi^2)$. Only the remaining squared massive denominator supplies the [ultraviolet pole](ultraviolet-pole.md).
