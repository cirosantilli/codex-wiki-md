# Magnetic flux spectrum of a quantum ring

↑ **Parent:** [Aharonov-Bohm effect](aharonov-bohm-effect.md)

For circumference $L$ and [Hamiltonian operator](hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) $(p-a)^2/(2m)$ with a [periodic boundary condition](periodic-boundary-conditions.md), the [plane waves](plane-wave.md) $L^{-1/2}e^{2\pi inx/L}$ have energies $E_n=E_L(n-\alpha)^2$, where $E_L=2\pi^2\hbar^2/(mL^2)$ and $\alpha=aL/(2\pi\hbar)$. [Minimal coupling](minimal-coupling.md) gives $aL=q\Phi_C$, where $\Phi_C$ is the [magnetic flux](magnetic-flux.md) oriented consistently with increasing position around the ring. The ordered spectrum is periodic in $\alpha$ with period one, corresponding to [magnetic flux quantum](magnetic-flux-quantum.md) $h/|q|$.

On $|\alpha|\leq1/2$, its lowest two energies are $E_L\alpha^2$ and $E_L(1-|\alpha|)^2$. Their crossings at half-integer $\alpha$ can be opened by a potential that couples the relevant momentum states.
