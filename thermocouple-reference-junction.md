# Thermocouple reference junction

↑ **Parent:** [Thermocouple](thermocouple.md)

The [thermocouple reference junction](thermocouple-reference-junction.md) is the connection region where the two thermoelements meet the measurement leads. In ordinary [copper](copper.md)-lead wiring there are two material transitions, A–Cu and B–Cu, rather than a second welded A–B junction. They must have a common known [temperature](temperature-split.md) $T_c$ for the simple expression $E(T_h,T_c)$ to apply. An ice-water [heat bath](heat-bath.md) can establish a nominal $0\,{}^\circ\mathrm C$ reference; [cold-junction compensation](cold-junction-compensation.md) instead measures $T_c$ locally. The word “cold” describes the reference role and imposes no requirement that $T_c<T_h$.
