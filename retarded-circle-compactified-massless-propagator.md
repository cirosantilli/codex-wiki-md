# Retarded circle-compactified massless propagator

↑ **Parent:** [Kaluza-Klein theory](kaluza-klein-theory.md)

For a massless field on Minkowski space times a circle of circumference $R$, the compact delta distribution expands as $R^{-1}\sum_ne^{2\pi in\Delta w/R}$. The noncompact [retarded Green function](retarded-green-function.md) in each Fourier sector is that of a four-dimensional field with mass $m_n$. Equivalently, the time-frequency denominator is $\mathbf k^2+m_n^2-(\omega+i0)^2$, putting both poles below the real axis. At equal compact positions the sum has one zero mode and a doubly counted nonzero mass tower.
