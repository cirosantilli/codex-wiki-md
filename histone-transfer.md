# Histone transfer

↑ **Parent:** [Nucleosome](nucleosome.md)

Movement of [histones](histone.md) from one [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) substrate or complex to another. In competition experiments, loss of [histones](histone.md) from a labelled complex need not release free [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) if another [DNA-binding protein](dna-binding-protein.md) remains attached.
