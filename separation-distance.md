# Separation distance

↑ **Parent:** [Strong stationary time](strong-stationary-time.md)

For a finite [Markov chain](markov-chain.md) with [stationary distribution](stationary-distribution.md) $\pi$, the separation distance from an initial state $x$ is

$$
s_x(t)=\max_y\left(1-\frac{P^t(x,y)}{\pi(y)}\right).
$$

If $\tau$ is a [strong stationary time](strong-stationary-time.md), then $s_x(t)\leq\mathbb P_x(\tau>t)$, and [total variation distance](total-variation-distance.md) is at most separation distance.
