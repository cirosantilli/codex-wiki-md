# Stoner-enhanced magnetic susceptibility

↑ **Parent:** [Stoner criterion](stoner-criterion.md)

Let $g_s(E_F)$ be the [single-spin density of states](single-spin-density-of-states.md) per site per energy, and let the on-site [Hubbard model](hubbard-model.md) repulsion have [energy](energy-split.md) $U$. For a weak uniform magnetic field $H$, the half-splitting of the two spin bands is $\Delta=U\delta n/2+\mu_0\mu_BH$, where $\delta n=n_\uparrow-n_\downarrow$. Constant density of states gives $\delta n=2g_s\Delta$. Therefore

$$
\frac{\mu_B\delta n}{H}=\frac{2\mu_0\mu_B^2g_s}{1-Ug_s}.
$$

This is the response per site; multiplying by the site [number density](number-density.md) gives the dimensionless SI volume [magnetic susceptibility](magnetic-susceptibility.md). With [spin-summed density of states](spin-summed-density-of-states.md) $g=2g_s$, the denominator is $1-Ug/2$. Its vanishing is the mean-field [Stoner criterion](stoner-criterion.md). The unpolarized linear-response expression is stable only for $Ug_s<1$.
