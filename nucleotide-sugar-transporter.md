# Nucleotide-sugar transporter

↑ **Parent:** [Membrane transport protein](membrane-transport-protein.md)

A [nucleotide-sugar transporter](nucleotide-sugar-transporter.md) supplies an activated donor across a membrane to a compartment containing glycosyltransferases. Whole-cell donor abundance does not establish donor availability at a lumen-facing [enzyme](enzyme-split.md).
