# Fault displacement-length scaling

↑ **Parent:** [Geological fault](geological-fault.md)

The relationship between characteristic accumulated [fault slip](fault-slip.md) and fault-system length. Approximately $D\propto L$ follows in self-similar elastic models at comparable driving stress, but natural faults can accumulate displacement at nearly constant length over part of their history.
