# Bit rotation

↑ **Parent:** [Bit shift](bit-shift.md)

A [bit rotation](bit-rotation.md) cyclically moves the [bits](bit.md) of a fixed-width [machine word](machine-word.md): positions discarded at one end reappear at the other. A logical shift instead fills vacated positions with zero and discards outgoing [bits](bit.md). Consequently they have different wiring at the edge of a [barrel shifter](barrel-shifter.md).
