# Normal force

↑ **Parent:** [Force](force-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Normal_force)

A normal force is a contact [force](force-split.md) perpendicular to the constraining surface. It supplies support without being identical to [gravitational weight](weight-physics.md): its magnitude depends on the other [forces](force-split.md) and the body's [acceleration](acceleration.md). For a body at rest on a horizontal scale with no other vertical forces, it equals $mg$. A freely falling body has no supporting normal force, despite its nonzero [gravitational weight](weight-physics.md); this distinction defines the scale's [apparent weight](apparent-weight.md).
