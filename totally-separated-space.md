# Totally separated space

↑ **Parent:** [Separation axiom](separation-axiom.md)

A [topological space](topological-space.md) is totally separated when any two distinct [points](point-geometry.md) are separated by a [clopen set](clopen-set.md). Such a space has no nontrivial [connected subset](connected-subset.md). A [zero-dimensional space](zero-dimensional-space.md) with its usual [clopen](clopen-set.md)-base definition and $T_1$ is totally separated. Conversely these conditions agree for [compact Hausdorff spaces](compact-hausdorff-space.md) by the [Šura-Bura lemma](sura-bura-lemma.md) and [compactness](compact-space.md), although the definitions should not be conflated for arbitrary spaces. Any closed totally separated subset of the unit square is [nowhere dense](nowhere-dense-set.md), since every nonempty relatively open subset of the square contains a nontrivial [connected](connected-space.md) [line segment](line-segment.md). Restricting a [clopen set](clopen-set.md) separating the segment's endpoints would disconnect that segment.
