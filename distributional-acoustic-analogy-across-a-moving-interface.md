# Distributional acoustic analogy across a moving interface

↑ **Parent:** [Lighthill acoustic analogy](lighthill-acoustic-analogy.md)

Mass and momentum flux defects at a moving interface add surface [acoustic monopole](acoustic-monopole.md) and [acoustic dipole](acoustic-dipole.md) terms to [Lighthill acoustic analogy](lighthill-acoustic-analogy.md). For a physical conservative fluid [shock wave](shock-wave.md), the [Rankine-Hugoniot conditions](rankine-hugoniot-conditions.md) set both defects to zero; the shock still affects distributional derivatives of the [Lighthill stress tensor](lighthill-stress-tensor.md). A discontinuity alone is not independent mass or force injection.
