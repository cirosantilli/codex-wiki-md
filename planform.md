# Planform

↑ **Parent:** [Pattern formation](pattern-formation.md)

A planform is the horizontal spatial structure of a pattern, often described by the [Fourier modes](fourier-mode.md) active at a [bifurcation](bifurcation.md). For example, one pair of opposite [wavevectors](wavevector.md) gives parallel [convection rolls](convection-roll.md), while two equal-amplitude perpendicular pairs give squares. A planform includes the relative [amplitudes](wave-amplitude.md) and [phases](phase-waves.md) needed to distinguish spatial [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md) types.
