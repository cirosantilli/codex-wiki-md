# Minor (linear algebra)

↑ **Parent:** [Submatrix](submatrix.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minor_(linear_algebra))

A matrix minor is the [determinant](determinant.md) of a square [submatrix](submatrix.md) obtained by selecting rows and columns of a [matrix](matrix.md). Over a [field](field.md), the [matrix rank](matrix-rank.md) is less than $r$ exactly when every $r$-by-$r$ [matrix minor](minor-linear-algebra.md) vanishes. In a [matrix](matrix.md) of [regular functions](regular-function.md), these equations define a closed rank-defect locus, explaining the [determinantal variety](determinantal-variety.md) construction.
