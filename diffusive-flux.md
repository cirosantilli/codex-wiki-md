# Diffusive flux

↑ **Parent:** [Flux](flux.md)

A diffusive flux transports a density $u$ down its [gradient](gradient.md) according to [Fick's first law](fick-s-first-law.md). For scalar [diffusion coefficient](diffusion-coefficient.md) $D>0$, $\mathbf j=-D\nabla u$ and the [conservation law](conservation-law.md) $u_t+\operatorname{div}\mathbf j=0$ gives a [diffusion equation](diffusion-equation-split.md). If $D$ depends on $u$, this becomes a [nonlinear diffusion equation](nonlinear-diffusion-equation.md).
