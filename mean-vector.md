# Mean vector

↑ **Parent:** [Random vector](random-vector.md)

The mean vector of an integrable [random vector](random-vector.md) $X=(X_1,\ldots,X_d)$ is its coordinatewise [expected value](expected-value-split.md): $\mathbb EX=(\mathbb EX_1,\ldots,\mathbb EX_d)$. Integrability of every coordinate makes this a finite vector.
