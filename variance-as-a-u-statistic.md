# Variance as a U-statistic

↑ **Parent:** [U-statistic](u-statistic.md)

The symmetric kernel $h(x,y)=(x-y)^2/2$ has expectation equal to the population variance for independent identically distributed observations with finite second moment. Its [U-statistic](u-statistic.md) reduces to the displayed unbiased [sample variance](sample-variance.md), using $\sum_{i<j}(X_i-X_j)^2=n\sum_i(X_i-\overline X)^2$.
