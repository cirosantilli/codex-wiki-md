<h1 id="fejer-kernel-differentiation-identity">Fejér-kernel differentiation identity</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Bernstein inequality for trigonometric polynomials](bernstein-inequality-for-trigonometric-polynomials.md)

Use the convention $K_m=\sum_{|j|\leq m}(1-|j|/(m+1))e^{ijt}$. Multiplying $K_{n-1}$ by $2n\sin(nt)$ gives [Fourier coefficients](fourier-coefficient.md) $-ik$ for $|k|\leq n$. A [trigonometric polynomial](trigonometric-polynomial.md) of degree at most $n$ has no other frequencies, so [convolution](convolution.md) proves the displayed identity. [Positivity](positivity-linear-maps.md) and integral one of the [Fejér kernel](fejer-kernel.md) then give $\|P'\|_\infty\leq2n\|P\|_\infty$. This is a useful elementary estimate; the sharp [Bernstein inequality for trigonometric polynomials](bernstein-inequality-for-trigonometric-polynomials.md) improves $2n$ to $n$.
