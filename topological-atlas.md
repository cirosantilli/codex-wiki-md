# Topological atlas

↑ **Parent:** [Topological manifold](topological-manifold-split.md)

A topological atlas is a family $\mathcal A=\{(U_i,\varphi_i)\}$ of [coordinate charts](manifold-chart.md) whose domains form an [open cover](open-cover.md) of a [topological manifold](topological-manifold-split.md). Each point must belong to at least one chart domain; no individual domain needs to cover the entire [topological manifold](topological-manifold-split.md). On overlaps, [topological transition maps](topological-transition-map.md) describe the same points in two coordinate systems.
