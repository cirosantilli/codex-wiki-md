# Collisionless black-hole capture

↑ **Parent:** [Black-hole accretion](black-hole-accretion.md)

Collisionless matter approaching a [black hole](black-hole.md) generally cannot dissipate enough orbital energy to remain bound, so direct capture requires sufficiently small [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md). For nonrelativistic incident speed $v$, a [Schwarzschild black hole](schwarzschild-spacetime.md) has direct test-particle capture cross-section approximately $16\pi G^2M^2/(c^2v^2)$. A Newtonian pericentre estimate using the [Schwarzschild radius](schwarzschild-radius.md) instead gives $4\pi G^2M^2/(c^2v^2)$. Both show that capture at a given mass density is suppressed by order $(v/c)^2$ relative to supersonic [Bondi--Hoyle--Lyttleton accretion](bondi-hoyle-accretion.md). [Tidal disruption events](tidal-disruption-event.md) provide a distinct way to accrete some stellar debris.
