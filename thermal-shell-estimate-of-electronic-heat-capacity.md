# Thermal-shell estimate of electronic heat capacity

↑ **Parent:** [Electronic heat capacity](electronic-heat-capacity.md)

For a three-dimensional [free electron model](free-electron-model.md), the spin-summed [density of states](density-of-states.md) at the [Fermi energy](fermi-energy.md) is $g(E_F)=3N/(2E_F)$. A shell estimate treats $N_{\rm act}\sim g(E_F)k_BT$ electrons as thermally active and assigns each an excitation energy of order $k_BT$. For the illustrative choice $3k_BT/2$, $\Delta U\sim(9/4)Nk_B^2T^2/E_F$ and differentiation gives $C_V\sim(9/2)Nk_B T/T_F$. The numerical coefficient is heuristic: the controlled [Sommerfeld expansion](sommerfeld-expansion.md) instead gives

$$
C_V=\frac{\pi^2}{2}Nk_B\frac{T}{T_F}+O\!\left(Nk_B(T/T_F)^3\right).
$$

Both estimates express [Pauli exclusion principle](pauli-exclusion-principle.md) suppression of the classical [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md) when $T\ll T_F$. A shell argument establishes scaling rather than a universal prefactor.
