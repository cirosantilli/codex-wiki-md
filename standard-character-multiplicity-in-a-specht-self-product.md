# Standard-character multiplicity in a Specht self-product

↑ **Parent:** [Restriction branching rule for a symmetric group](restriction-branching-rule-for-a-symmetric-group.md)

For $\lambda\vdash n$ with $n\geq2$,

$$
\langle\chi^\lambda\chi^\lambda,\chi^{(n-1,1)}\rangle=|\lambda^-|-1.
$$

Indeed, adjoining the trivial character to the standard character gives the point-permutation character, and [Frobenius reciprocity](frobenius-reciprocity.md) turns its multiplicity in the self-product into the norm of the multiplicity-free restriction.
