# Clock period

↑ **Parent:** [Clock signal](clock-signal.md)

The time between equivalent [clock edges](clock-edge.md) in consecutive cycles of a periodic [clock signal](clock-signal.md). It is the reciprocal of the clock's [frequency](frequency.md).
