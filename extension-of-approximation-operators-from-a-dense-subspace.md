# Extension of approximation operators from a dense subspace

↑ **Parent:** [Uniform approximation](uniform-approximation-split.md)

Suppose [linear operators](linear-operator.md) $T_n$ on a [normed vector space](normed-vector-space.md) satisfy $\|T_n\|\le C$ uniformly and $T_np\to p$ on a [dense subspace](dense-subspace.md). The displayed [triangle inequality](triangle-inequality.md) for any element $p$ of that subspace first permits $p$ to approximate $f$, then lets $n$ increase, proving $T_nf\to f$ for every $f$. Applied to the [Bernstein polynomial](bernstein-polynomial.md), the [Weierstrass approximation theorem](weierstrass-approximation-theorem.md) supplies the dense polynomial subspace and $C=1$.
