# Cameron-Martin space of Wiener measure

↑ **Parent:** [Wiener measure](wiener-measure.md)

On $C_0([0,T])$, the Cameron-Martin space consists of the [absolutely continuous functions](absolutely-continuous-function.md) $h$ with $h(0)=0$ and $h'\in L^2[0,T]$. Its norm is

$$
\lVert h\rVert_H^2=\int_0^T|h'(s)|^2ds.
$$
