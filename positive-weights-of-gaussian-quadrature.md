# Positive weights of Gaussian quadrature

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian quadrature](gaussian-quadrature.md)

For an $n$-node [Gaussian quadrature](gaussian-quadrature.md) rule exact through degree $2n-1$, the cardinal [Lagrange interpolation polynomial](lagrange-polynomial.md) $\ell_j$ has square of degree $2n-2$. Exactness gives $A_j=\int\ell_j^2>0$ for a positive integration weight. This proof turns [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) exactness directly into positivity.
