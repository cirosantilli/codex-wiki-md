# Cotangent lift of a diffeomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Cotangent bundle](cotangent-bundle.md)

For a diffeomorphism $f:X\to X$, its cotangent lift is

$$
f_\#(x,\xi)=\bigl(f(x),(df_x^{-1})^*\xi\bigr).
$$

It preserves the canonical one-form and therefore the canonical symplectic form. Cotangent lift is functorial under composition.
