# Sulfide

↑ **Parent:** [Ion](ion.md)

A [sulfide](sulfide.md) ion is formally $\mathrm{S^{2-}}$. Relative to [oxide](oxide.md) it is larger, more polarizable and more readily oxidized; the ability of a [metal](metal.md) to support a high [oxidation state](oxidation-state.md) need not survive replacement of [oxygen](oxygen.md) by [sulfur](sulfur.md).
