# Howea belmoreana

↑ **Parent:** [Angiosperm](angiosperm.md)

A palm native to Lord Howe Island and closely related to [Howea forsteriana](howea-forsteriana.md). Divergence in ecology and reproductive timing has been studied as a candidate for [sympatric speciation](sympatric-speciation.md).
