<h1 id="m-m-∞-queue">M/M/∞ queue</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Birth-death process](birth-death-process.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M/M/∞_queue)

An $M/M/\infty$ queue has [Poisson](poisson-process.md) arrivals of rate $\lambda$, [independent](independent-random-variables.md) exponential service times of rate $\mu$, and infinitely many servers. Its occupancy is a [birth-death process](birth-death-process.md) with birth rate $\lambda$ and death rate $n\mu$ in state $n$.
