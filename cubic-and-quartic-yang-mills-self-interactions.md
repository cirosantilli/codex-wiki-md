# Cubic and quartic Yang-Mills self-interactions

↑ **Parent:** [Yang-Mills theory](yang-mills-theory.md)

With $D_\mu=\partial_\mu+igA_\mu$ and $[T^a,T^b]=if_{abc}T^c$, the [Yang-Mills field strength](gauge-field-strength.md) has the sign $F_{\mu\nu}^a=G_{\mu\nu}^a-gf_{abc}A_\mu^bA_\nu^c$. Expanding $-F^a_{\mu\nu}F^{a\mu\nu}/4$ gives a cross term $(g/2)G^a_{\mu\nu}f_{abc}A^{b\mu}A^{c\nu}$; antisymmetry makes its two derivative terms equal, proving the cubic expression. Squaring the nonlinear term gives the quartic expression. Both vertices arise from the same [gauge coupling](gauge-coupling.md), rather than independent free parameters.
