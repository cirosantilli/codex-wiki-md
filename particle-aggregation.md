# Particle aggregation

↑ **Parent:** [Colloid](colloid.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Particle_aggregation)

Particle aggregation is association of initially separate particles into larger clusters. For [colloids](colloid.md), both the rate of [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) encounters and the probability that an encounter produces attachment matter. Strong attraction with negligible repulsive barriers can give [diffusion-limited cluster aggregation](diffusion-limited-cluster-aggregation.md); a barrier that makes attachment unlikely can give [reaction-limited cluster aggregation](reaction-limited-cluster-aggregation.md). Reversible association and bond rearrangement require additional kinetic assumptions.
