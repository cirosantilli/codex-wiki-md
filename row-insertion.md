# Row insertion

↑ **Parent:** [Young tableau](young-tableau.md)

Insert $x$ into the first row of a [Young tableau](young-tableau.md) by replacing its leftmost entry strictly greater than $x$, and carry the displaced entry into the next row. If no entry is greater, append $x$ and stop. The procedure preserves a [semistandard Young tableau](semistandard-young-tableau.md); when all entries are distinct it preserves a [near Young tableau](near-young-tableau.md). It underlies the [Robinson–Schensted correspondence](robinson-schensted-correspondence.md).
