# Instantaneous covariance rank in a one-factor rate model

↑ **Parent:** [One-factor short-rate model](one-factor-short-rate-model.md)

If all traded [zero-coupon bonds](zero-coupon-bond.md) depend on one scalar rate driven by one [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md), their diffusion exposures form one vector $v_i=\eta P_{i,r}$. Their instantaneous [covariance matrix](covariance-matrix.md) is $vv^T$, of rank at most one. Nondegenerate maturity changes are therefore instantaneously perfectly correlated, with correlation $+1$ or $-1$, though finite-horizon bond-return correlations need not be one.
