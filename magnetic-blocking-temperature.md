# Magnetic blocking temperature

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetism](magnetism.md)

The observation-timescale-dependent [temperature](temperature-split.md) below which a magnetic configuration no longer relaxes readily during a measurement. For [exchange bias](exchange-bias.md) it can mark stable pinning of antiferromagnetic interfacial domains and can be below the bulk [Néel temperature](neel-temperature.md). It is not identical to the thermodynamic onset of magnetic order.
