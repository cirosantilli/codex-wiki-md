# Phonon dispersion and Kohn anomaly in tetrathiafulvalene-tetracyanoquinodimethane (TTF-TCNQ) by Gen Shirane and others

↑ **Parent:** [Physics bibliography](physics-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by Gen Shirane](work-by-gen-shirane.md)

An [inelastic neutron scattering](inelastic-neutron-scattering.md) measurement of phonon dispersion in deuterated [TTF-TCNQ](ttf-tcnq.md), including the temperature-dependent anomaly near $2k_F$. The abstract specifies the $60$–$300\,\mathrm K$ measurement range and the transition at $54\,\mathrm K$.

Publisher: [https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.14.2325](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.14.2325)
