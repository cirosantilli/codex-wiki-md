# Minimum of a subset of a total order

↑ **Parent:** [Total order](total-order.md)

A minimum of $A$ is a member of $A$ less than or equal to every member of $A$. It equals the [infimum](infimum.md) when that lower bound is attained. A nonempty finite subset of a [totally ordered set](totally-ordered-set.md) has a minimum; a nonempty open real interval does not.
