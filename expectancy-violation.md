# Expectancy violation

↑ **Parent:** [Exposure therapy](exposure-therapy.md)

A discrepancy between a predicted outcome and the outcome experienced. During exposure, explicit predictions can make such discrepancies informative; a [safety behavior](safety-behavior.md) may prevent revision if the person attributes the benign outcome to that behavior.
