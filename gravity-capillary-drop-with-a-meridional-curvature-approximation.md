# Gravity-capillary drop with a meridional curvature approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Thin-film equation](thin-film-equation.md)

If an axisymmetric [thin-film equation](thin-film-equation.md) is deliberately closed using only the meridional [curvature](curvature.md) $\kappa=-h''$, its regular equilibrium obeys $h'''=h'/l^2$, with [capillary length](capillary-length.md) $l=\sqrt{\gamma/(\rho g)}$. The [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md) $h'(0)=0$, $h(R)=0$, $h'(R)=-\tan\theta_c$ give the displayed profile and $\alpha=\tan\theta_c/\sinh(R/l)$. Its [volume](volume.md) is $2\pi\alpha l^3[s^2\cosh s/2-s\sinh s+\cosh s-1]$, $s=R/l$. This model omits the azimuthal contribution of [axisymmetric free-surface curvature](axisymmetric-free-surface-curvature.md). Retaining that term instead gives $h=l\tan\theta_c[I_0(R/l)-I_0(r/l)]/I_1(R/l)$ in terms of [modified Bessel functions](modified-bessel-function.md).
