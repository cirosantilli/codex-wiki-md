# Electric double-layer capacitor

↑ **Parent:** [Supercapacitor](supercapacitor.md)

A [supercapacitor](supercapacitor.md) whose principal storage mechanism is non-Faradaic charge separation at electrode–electrolyte interfaces. The two electrode interfaces contribute series capacitances.
