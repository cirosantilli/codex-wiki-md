# Soundness of an arithmetic theory

↑ **Parent:** [First-order theory](first-order-theory.md)

An [arithmetic](arithmetic-split.md) theory is sound if all its theorems hold in the standard [natural numbers](natural-number.md). [Arithmetic soundness](soundness-of-an-arithmetic-theory.md) implies consistency and rules out a proof of the negation of a true sentence. It is a semantic property, distinct from effective axiomatizability.
