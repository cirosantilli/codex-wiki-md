# Conservative mass-transfer critical mass ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamical stability of binary mass transfer](dynamical-stability-of-binary-mass-transfer.md)

With fixed total mass and orbital [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) and $R_L\propto a(M_d/M)^{1/3}$, the [Roche-lobe radius response exponent](roche-lobe-radius-response-exponent.md) is $2q-5/3$. A donor with [stellar radius response exponent](stellar-radius-response-exponent.md) $\zeta_*$ is stable to infinitesimal mass loss when $\zeta_*>2q-5/3$, giving $q<(\zeta_*+5/3)/2$. The threshold depends on the donor response.
