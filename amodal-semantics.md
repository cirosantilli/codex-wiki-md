# Amodal semantics

↑ **Parent:** [Amodal representation](amodal-representation.md)

Conceptual information accessible through different forms such as words, signs and pictures. Shared conceptual content can coexist with modality-specific input and output pathways; an anatomical overlap alone does not establish identical [neural codes](neural-code.md).
