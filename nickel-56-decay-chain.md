# Nickel-56 decay chain

↑ **Parent:** [Nickel](nickel.md)

The radioactive chain converts nickel-56 through cobalt-56 into iron-56, releasing energy that can power supernova light curves. [Electron capture](electron-capture.md) and, where allowed, [beta-plus decay](beta-plus-decay.md) convert protons into neutrons; gamma rays and charged decay products deposit some of the released energy. Ionization and escape of decay radiation affect the observed heating.
