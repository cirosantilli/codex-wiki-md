<h1 id="1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For a profile depending only on $y$, the [Poisson equation](../../../../../poisson-s-equation.md) becomes $w_1''=-G/\mu$. Integrating twice gives

$$
w_1(y)=-\frac{G}{2\mu}y^2+C_1y+C_2.
$$

The [no-slip boundary conditions](../../../../../no-slip-boundary-condition.md) $w_1(b)=w_1(-b)=0$ imply $C_1=0$ and $C_2=Gb^2/(2\mu)$. Thus

$$
\boxed{w_1(y)=\frac{G}{2\mu}(b^2-y^2).}
$$

This is [plane Poiseuille flow](../../../../../plane-poiseuille-flow.md) between the two horizontal walls. It is positive in the interior for $G>0$, so the [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) points down the [pressure gradient](../../../../../pressure-gradient.md). It does not satisfy the side-wall [no-slip boundary conditions](../../../../../no-slip-boundary-condition.md), which is precisely what the correction in part (iii) repairs.
