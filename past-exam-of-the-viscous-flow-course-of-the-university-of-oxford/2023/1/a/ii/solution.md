<h1 id="1/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

At an oriented internal surface with unit [normal vector](../../../../../normal-vector.md) $\mathbf n$, the [traction](../../../../../traction.md) $\mathbf t(\mathbf x,t;\mathbf n)$ is the limiting contact [force](../../../../../force-split.md) per unit [surface area](../../../../../surface-area.md) exerted by the material on the positive-$\mathbf n$ side on the material on the negative-$\mathbf n$ side:

$$
\mathbf t(\mathbf n)=\lim_{\Delta A\to0}\frac{\Delta\mathbf F}{\Delta A}.
$$

For a boundary of a chosen [material volume](../../../../../material-volume.md), this convention gives the external [traction](../../../../../traction.md) when $\mathbf n$ is outward. The [Cauchy stress tensor](../../../../../cauchy-stress-tensor.md) has components

$$
\sigma_{ij}=\mathbf e_i\cdot\mathbf t(\mathbf e_j),
$$

so the second index specifies the surface orientation and the first specifies the [force](../../../../../force-split.md) component. The vectors $\mathbf e_i$ form the standard [Cartesian coordinate system](../../../../../cartesian-coordinate-system.md) basis of [unit vectors](../../../../../unit-vector.md).

[Cauchy's stress theorem](../../../../../cauchy-s-stress-theorem.md) states that the dependence of [traction](../../../../../traction.md) on the [normal vector](../../../../../normal-vector.md) is linear:

$$
\boxed{\mathbf t(\mathbf n)=\boldsymbol\sigma\mathbf n,
\qquad t_i=\sigma_{ij}n_j.}
$$

Repeated indices are summed according to [Einstein notation](../../../../../einstein-notation.md). In particular, reversing the [normal vector](../../../../../normal-vector.md) reverses the [traction](../../../../../traction.md), and knowing the [tractions](../../../../../traction.md) on three mutually perpendicular coordinate planes determines the [traction](../../../../../traction.md) on every plane. Symmetry of the [Cauchy stress tensor](../../../../../cauchy-stress-tensor.md), for an ordinary fluid without body couples, follows separately from [angular momentum conservation](../../../../../conservation-of-angular-momentum.md).
