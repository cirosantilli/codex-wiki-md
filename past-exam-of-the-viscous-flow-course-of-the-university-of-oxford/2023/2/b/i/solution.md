<h1 id="2/b/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The held-flat interface imposes the [no-penetration boundary condition](../../../../../no-penetration-boundary-condition.md), while its specified tangential [traction](../../../../../traction.md) gives a [shear stress](../../../../../shear-stress.md) condition:

$$
\boxed{w(x,0)=0,\qquad u_z(x,0)+w_x(x,0)=\beta x.}
$$

A differentiable boundary value $w(x,0)\equiv0$ implies $w_x(x,0)=0$, so the second [boundary condition](../../../../../boundary-condition.md) is also $u_z(x,0)=\beta x$. There is no imposed [no-slip boundary condition](../../../../../no-slip-boundary-condition.md) at this interface; tangential [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) is determined by the [shear stress](../../../../../shear-stress.md) and the fluid equations.

The solution in part (a) has zero tangential [traction](../../../../../traction.md), and so remains valid for the whole interface **if and only if $\beta=0$**. At the single point $x=0$ the imposed [shear stress](../../../../../shear-stress.md) vanishes for any $\beta$, but this does not satisfy a [boundary condition](../../../../../boundary-condition.md) that must hold for every $x$.
