# 2023

↑ **Parent:** [Past exam of the viscous flow course of the University of Oxford](split.md)

Solutions to all three questions of the [viscous flow course of the University of Oxford](../viscous-flow-course-of-the-university-of-oxford.md) Part B examination, sat on 15 June 2023. Source: [original released PDF, bundle pages 228–231; paper pages 1–4](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/request_for_mathematics_examinat/response/3129786/attach/3/FOI%20202507%20740%20Data.pdf#page=228). The solutions follow every lettered and Roman-numbered part. In Question 1(b), the requested equation implicitly selects [steady flow](../steady-flow.md). In Question 2(c), the printed far-field sign must be corrected to describe the specified lower half-space; Question 3(a) also has a harmless component-name typo. These qualifications are explained at the affected steps.
