# Closed-loop gain

↑ **Parent:** [Operational-amplifier negative feedback](operational-amplifier-negative-feedback.md)

The [closed-loop gain](closed-loop-gain.md) is the input-to-output transfer after closing the feedback connection. In the elementary noninverting voltage model it tends to $1/\beta$ for large stable [loop gain](loop-gain.md), rather than retaining the uncertain [open-loop gain](open-loop-gain.md) $A$. A frequency-dependent transfer must be checked for stability before that approximation is used.
