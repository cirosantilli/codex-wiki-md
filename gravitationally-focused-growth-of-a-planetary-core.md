# Gravitationally focused growth of a planetary core

↑ **Parent:** [Gravitational focusing](gravitational-focusing.md)

Conservation of orbital energy and [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) in a two-body encounter gives $\sigma=\pi R_c^2(1+v_{\rm esc}^2/v_\infty^2)$. At fixed [density](density.md) and encounter speed in the strong-focusing limit, $R_c\propto M_c^{1/3}$ and $\sigma\propto M_c^{4/3}$. This yields runaway fractional growth until stirring or supply changes the assumptions.
