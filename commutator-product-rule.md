# Commutator product rule

↑ **Parent:** [Commutator](commutator.md)

For [linear operators](linear-operator.md) on a common invariant domain, $[A,BC]=[A,B]C+B[A,C]$. The identity follows by adding and subtracting $BAC$ in the [commutator](commutator.md).
