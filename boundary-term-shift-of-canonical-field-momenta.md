# Boundary-term shift of canonical field momenta

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical momentum](canonical-momentum.md)

If the spatially integrated [Lagrangian](lagrangian.md) changes by $dB[\Phi]/dt$, where $B$ depends on fields and their spatial derivatives but not velocities, the [canonical momentum](canonical-momentum.md) components change by $\pi_a\mapsto\pi_a+\delta B/\delta\Phi_a$. This follows by varying $dB/dt$ and using spatial [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md). The canonical one-form changes by the exact variation $\delta B$, so the [symplectic form](symplectic-form.md) and [canonical commutation relations](canonical-commutation-relation.md) are preserved. For the two [Feynman gauge](feynman-gauge.md) Maxwell densities, $B=\int A_0\partial_iA_i\,d^3x$ gives shifts $\Delta\pi^0=\partial_iA_i$ and $\Delta\pi^i=-\partial_iA_0$. Boundary flux must vanish for this spatial functional calculation.
