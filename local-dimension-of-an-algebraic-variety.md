# Local dimension of an algebraic variety

↑ **Parent:** [Dimension of an algebraic set](dimension-of-an-algebraic-set.md)

The [Krull dimension](krull-dimension.md) of $\mathcal O_{X,x}$ at a point $x$. For a closed point of an irreducible [affine variety](affine-algebraic-set.md) it equals $\dim X$, by the [closed-point dimension lemma for affine domains](closed-point-dimension-lemma-for-affine-domains.md). At a generic point of a codimension-one subvariety the local dimension is one.
