# Electronic state correlation diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Potential energy surface](potential-energy-surface.md)

A correlation between many-electron states classified by total spin and spatial [symmetry](symmetry-physics.md) along a nuclear path. Orbital occupations, spin adaptation and [configuration interaction](configuration-interaction.md) are needed; one-electron energy curves alone are not a state diagram.
