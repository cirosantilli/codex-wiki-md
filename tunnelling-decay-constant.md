# Tunnelling decay constant

↑ **Parent:** [Finite rectangular barrier matching](finite-rectangular-barrier-matching.md)

The [tunnelling decay constant](tunnelling-decay-constant.md) is the inverse length controlling the [exponential decay](exponential-decay.md) of an [evanescent wave](evanescent-wave.md) in a constant [potential barrier](potential-barrier.md) with $E<V_0$. The [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) amplitude decay length is $1/\kappa$; the squared-amplitude decay length is $1/(2\kappa)$.
