# Additivity of independently driven squared Bessel processes

↑ **Parent:** [Squared Bessel process](squared-bessel-process.md)

For independently driven nonnegative [squared Bessel processes](squared-bessel-process.md) $X,Y$, put $Z=X+Y$. Where $Z>0$, use Brownian weights $\sqrt{X/Z}$ and $\sqrt{Y/Z}$. Where $Z=0$, fill the weights with $(1,0)$. Their squared norm is always one and their cross-variation is zero, so their stochastic integral is [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) by the [Lévy characterization of Brownian motion](levy-characterization-of-brownian-motion.md). Multiplying by $2\sqrt Z$ recovers the sum's martingale part even at zero, and the drifts add to $\alpha+\beta$.
