# Extension and contraction of ideals

↑ **Parent:** [Ring homomorphism](ring-homomorphism.md)

For a [ring homomorphism](ring-homomorphism.md) $f:R\to S$, extension sends an [ideal](ideal.md) $I\subseteq R$ to the ideal $S f(I)$ generated by its image. Contraction sends an ideal $J\subseteq S$ to $f^{-1}(J)$. These are distinct forward and inverse-image operations.
