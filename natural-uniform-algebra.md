# Natural uniform algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Uniform algebra](uniform-algebra.md)

A [uniform algebra](uniform-algebra.md) on $K$ is natural if every [character of an algebra](character-of-an-algebra.md) is evaluation at a point of $K$. The evaluation embedding then identifies its character space homeomorphically with $K$. A [natural uniform algebra](natural-uniform-algebra.md) is characterized by the [finite-generator criterion for a natural uniform algebra](finite-generator-criterion-for-a-natural-uniform-algebra.md). In particular, $C(K)$ is natural; the [disk algebra](disk-algebra.md) is natural when its underlying space is the closed disk.
