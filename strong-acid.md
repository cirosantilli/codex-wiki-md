# Strong acid

↑ **Parent:** [Acid (chemistry)](acid-chemistry.md)

In [water](water.md), a [strong acid](strong-acid.md) transfers its available [proton](proton.md) essentially completely to [water](water.md) under the dilute conditions considered here. For the [pH](ph.md) example, each initial [chemical acid](acid-chemistry.md) unit supplies one [hydronium](hydronium.md) [ion](ion.md). “Strong” describes this conversion, rather than a high [concentration](concentration.md). See [14.3 Relative Strengths of Acids and Bases](chemistry-2e-by-paul-flowers-and-others/14-3-relative-strengths-of-acids-and-bases.md).
