# Unbroken neutral gauge group of a zero-hypercharge real triplet

↑ **Parent:** [SU(2) gauge theory with an adjoint Higgs field](su-2-gauge-theory-with-an-adjoint-higgs-field.md)

A [real scalar triplet](real-scalar-triplet.md) of [weak hypercharge](hypercharge.md) zero with [vacuum expectation value](vacuum-expectation-value.md) $(0,0,v)$ breaks $T^1,T^2$ but preserves both $T^3$ and the independent [weak hypercharge](hypercharge.md) generator. With canonical kinetic terms its [gauge-boson mass matrix](gauge-boson-mass-matrix.md) in $(W^1,W^2,W^3,B)$ is $g^2v^2\operatorname{diag}(1,1,0,0)$. The two massless neutral [gauge bosons](gauge-boson.md) prevent this field alone from reproducing [Standard Model](standard-model-split.md) [electroweak symmetry breaking](electroweak-symmetry-breaking.md).
