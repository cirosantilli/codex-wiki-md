# Disk warp

↑ **Parent:** [Astrophysical disk](astrophysical-disk.md)

A disk warp is a radial change of the orbital plane of successive disk annuli. In the small-tilt approximation, a complex tilt $W$ records its magnitude and azimuth. Pressure and internal torques communicate the tilt through [bending waves of a Keplerian disk](bending-wave-of-a-keplerian-disk.md) in the inviscid thin-disk regime. A flat tilted disk has constant $W$ even though its absolute orientation differs from the reference equatorial plane.
