# Weighted graph Laplacian

↑ **Parent:** [Laplacian matrix](laplacian-matrix.md)

For symmetric edge weights $w_{ij}\geq0$, the weighted graph Laplacian has quadratic form

$$
x^TL_wx=\sum_{\{i,j\}\in E}w_{ij}(x_i-x_j)^2.
$$
