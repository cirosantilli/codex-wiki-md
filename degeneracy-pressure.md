# Degeneracy pressure

↑ **Parent:** [Fermi gas](fermi-gas.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Degeneracy_pressure)

Degeneracy pressure is the pressure of a fermionic system that remains at zero temperature because the [Pauli exclusion principle](pauli-exclusion-principle.md) forces particles to occupy states with nonzero momentum.
