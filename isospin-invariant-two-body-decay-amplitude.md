# Isospin-invariant two-body decay amplitude

↑ **Parent:** [Isospin multiplet](isospin-multiplet.md)

An [isospin](isospin.md)-invariant [decay amplitude](decay-amplitude.md) is an intertwining map from the initial [isospin multiplet](isospin-multiplet.md) into the final tensor product. The [Clebsch-Gordan decomposition for SU2](clebsch-gordan-decomposition-for-su2.md) contains each allowed final isospin once. Commutation with the third generator and the [quadratic Casimir operator](quadratic-casimir-operator.md) places the image in the matching coupled state. Commutation with the [ladder operators](ladder-operator.md) makes its scalar coefficient independent of the magnetic component. Orthogonality of the [Clebsch-Gordan coefficients](clebsch-gordan-coefficients.md) then gives a summed squared amplitude $|a|^2$. A physical [decay rate](decay-width.md) additionally includes phase space and spin sums, which can be absorbed into a common reduced coefficient when exact isospin symmetry makes them identical between channels.
