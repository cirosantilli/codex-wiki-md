# Hippocampal remapping

↑ **Parent:** [Place cell](place-cell.md)

A change in a [hippocampal](hippocampus.md) spatial representation across environments or contexts. Models distinguish changes in firing rates, field locations and the relationships among simultaneously recorded units; visual cue changes need not produce one universal kind of response.
