# Iwasawa algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Completed group algebra](completed-group-algebra.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iwasawa_algebra)

An Iwasawa algebra is a [completed group algebra](completed-group-algebra.md) such as $\mathbb Z_p[[G]]$ or $\mathbb F_p[[G]]$ for a compact p-adic analytic group $G$.
