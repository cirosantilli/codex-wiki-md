# Eight-point isogenous elliptic curves over F5

↑ **Parent:** [Isogenous elliptic curves can have different rational point groups](isogenous-elliptic-curves-can-have-different-rational-point-groups.md)

The [elliptic curves](elliptic-curve.md) $E_1:y^2=x^3-x$ and $E_2:y^2=x^3-x+1$ over $\mathbb F_5$ both have eight rational points. The first has four rational [2-torsion](2-torsion.md) points and the second has two, so their point groups are not isomorphic. They are nevertheless degree-two isogenous via $X=x+2/(x-1)$, $Y=y(1-2/(x-1)^2)$. The map sends $O$ and $(1,0)$ to $O$; its pole divisor for $X$ is $2O+2(1,0)$, giving degree two. Their [j-invariant of an elliptic curve](j-invariant-of-an-elliptic-curve.md) values are respectively three and one, so the curves are not geometrically isomorphic either.
