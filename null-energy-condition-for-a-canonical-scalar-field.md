# Null energy condition for a canonical scalar field

↑ **Parent:** [Null energy condition](null-energy-condition.md)

The classical [stress-energy tensor of a canonical scalar field](stress-energy-tensor-of-a-canonical-scalar-field.md) has a gradient-product term and a metric-proportional term. Contracting with a real [null vector](null-vector.md) removes the latter, leaving the displayed square for a real field. This proves the [null energy condition](null-energy-condition.md) for a minimally coupled canonical scalar, including the massless case. It is not a statement about arbitrary curvature couplings, wrong-sign kinetic terms or a renormalized quantum [stress-energy tensor](stress-energy-tensor.md).
