# Carboxylate

↑ **Parent:** [Carboxylic acid](carboxylic-acid.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carboxylate)

A [carboxylate](carboxylate.md) is the [conjugate base](conjugate-base.md) of a [carboxylic acid](carboxylic-acid.md); an [acetate ion](acetate-ion.md) is one example.
