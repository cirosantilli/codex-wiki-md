<h1 id="poly-3-hexylthiophene">Poly(3-hexylthiophene)</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Organic semiconductor](organic-semiconductor.md)

A conjugated polymer with hexyl-substituted thiophene repeat units, often used as a model electron-donor [semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md). Regioregularity and packing influence its optical and transport response.
