# Sine-Gordon kink fluctuation operator

↑ **Parent:** [Sine-Gordon kink](sine-gordon-kink.md)

The [second variation](second-variation.md) of the [Sine-Gordon theory](sine-gordon-theory.md) action about its static [kink](scalar-field-kink.md) gives $\Delta_x=-\partial_x^2+1-2\operatorname{sech}^2x$. Let $A=\partial_x+\tanh x$. Then $\Delta_x=A^\dagger A$ and $AA^\dagger=-\partial_x^2+1$. Thus $\Delta_x$ is nonnegative, with its [translational zero mode of a sine-Gordon kink](translational-zero-mode-of-a-sine-gordon-kink.md) and a continuum at $\omega^2=k^2+1$. The construction is the [supersymmetric factorization of the one-soliton potential](supersymmetric-factorization-of-the-one-soliton-potential.md).
