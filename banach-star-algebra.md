# Banach star-algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Banach algebra](banach-algebra-split.md)

A [Banach star-algebra](banach-star-algebra.md) is a complex [Banach algebra](banach-algebra-split.md) equipped with a continuous [algebra involution](algebra-involution.md). Thus the involution is conjugate-linear, reverses products, and squares to the identity. Some conventions additionally require $\|a^*\|=\|a\|$; this is the isometric convention. In the unital case the involution fixes the unit. The [C-star identity](c-star-identity.md) is a stronger additional condition. The algebra of continuous complex-valued functions on a [compact Hausdorff space](compact-hausdorff-space.md), with pointwise operations, [complex conjugation](complex-conjugation.md) and the [supremum norm](supremum-norm.md), satisfies both the isometric condition and the C-star identity.
