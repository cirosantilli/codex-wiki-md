# Nucleic acid complementarity

↑ **Parent:** [Base pairing](base-pairing.md)

[nucleic acid complementarity](nucleic-acid-complementarity.md) is the relation between sequences that permits specified [base pairs](base-pair.md) to form when aligned with appropriate strand orientation. In [homologous recombination](homologous-recombination.md), structural organization helps test pairing rather than reading one fixed base sequence.
