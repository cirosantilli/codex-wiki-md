# Stellar crossing time

↑ **Parent:** [Stellar dynamics](stellar-dynamics.md)

The [stellar crossing time](stellar-crossing-time.md) is a characteristic [time](time-in-physics.md) to traverse a system, $t_{\rm dyn}\sim R/v$. In a virialized self-gravitating system, $v^2\sim GM/R$, so $t_{\rm dyn}\sim\sqrt{R^3/(GM)}$. It is distinct from the much longer spherical [stellar relaxation time](stellar-relaxation-time.md). Radius versus diameter and characteristic-speed conventions change order-one factors.
