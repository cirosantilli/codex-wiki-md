# Cell ablation

↑ **Parent:** [Biology laboratory technique](biology-laboratory-technique.md)

Selective destruction of [cells](biological-cell-split.md) to test their necessity or the response of surrounding tissues. Compensation and collateral injury complicate interpretation; ablation alone is not a record of ancestry.
