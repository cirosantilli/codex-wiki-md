<h1 id="plane-cusp-of-type-2-5">Plane cusp of type (2,5)</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Plane curve](plane-curve.md)

This irreducible plane branch has parametrization $x=t^5$, $y=t^2$. Its only singular point is the origin. The [resolution of the (2,5) cusp by two blowups](resolution-of-the-2-5-cusp-by-two-blowups.md) makes its [strict transform](strict-transform.md) smooth in every characteristic.
