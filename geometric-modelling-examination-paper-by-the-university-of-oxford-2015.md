# Geometric Modelling examination paper by the University of Oxford (2015)

↑ **Parent:** [Computer science bibliography](computer-science-bibliography-split.md)

The four-page [University of Oxford](university-of-oxford-split.md) [computer science](computer-science-split.md) finals paper dated Wednesday 10 June 2015, 9:30 am–11:00 am. The public [archive released by the University of Oxford](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/computer_science_past_exam_paper/response/956037/attach/3/Lawrence%20Attachment.zip?cookie_passthrough=1) contains it as `2015-gmod.pdf`. Printed pp. 2–4 cover [constructive solid geometry](constructive-solid-geometry.md), a circular [fillet](fillet.md), quadratic [Bézier curves](bezier-curve.md) and [B-splines](b-spline.md), and the [incremental three-dimensional convex hull algorithm](incremental-three-dimensional-convex-hull-algorithm.md).
