# Rank of a vector bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Vector bundle](vector-bundle.md)

The rank of a [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) at a point is the [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) of its fiber as a [vector space](vector-space-split.md). A [vector bundle trivialization](vector-bundle-trivialization.md) identifies nearby fibers with the same [vector space](vector-space-split.md), so this rank is locally constant. A rank-$r$ bundle has fibers of dimension $r$ everywhere.
