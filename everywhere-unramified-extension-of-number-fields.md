# Everywhere unramified extension of number fields

↑ **Parent:** [Ramification of a prime](ramification-of-a-prime.md)

A finite extension of [number fields](number-field.md) is unramified everywhere when every finite [prime ideal](prime-ideal.md) is unramified. If infinite places are included in the convention, one also requires every real place to remain real.
