# Open-chain endpoint corrections for an exponential Ising kernel

↑ **Parent:** [Exponentially coupled Ising inverse kernel](exponentially-coupled-ising-inverse-kernel.md)

For $N\ge2$, the open-chain [Ising model](ising-model.md) coupling $[J_N]_{ij}=J\rho^{|i-j|}$, $0<\rho<1$, has a tridiagonal inverse. Its endpoint diagonal entries are $1/[J(1-\rho^2)]$, its interior diagonal entries are $(1+\rho^2)/[J(1-\rho^2)]$, and adjacent off-diagonal entries are $-\rho/[J(1-\rho^2)]$. Direct [matrix multiplication](matrix-multiplication.md) verifies the inverse because the geometric sequences cancel in every interior row. The displayed [quadratic form](quadratic-form.md) follows with $D=\rho/[J(1-\rho^2)]$, $A=(1-\rho)/[J(1+\rho)]$ and $B=\rho/[J(1+\rho)]$. For $\rho=e^{-\kappa}$, $D=(2J\sinh\kappa)^{-1}$ and $A=\tanh(\kappa/2)/J$. Omitting the endpoint term requires an infinite chain, a correctly periodized coupling, or a bulk [thermodynamic limit](thermodynamic-limit.md).
