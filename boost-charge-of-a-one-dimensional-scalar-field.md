# Boost charge of a one-dimensional scalar field

↑ **Parent:** [Lorentz current](lorentz-current.md)

For a free massless real [scalar field](scalar-field.md) with [wave speed](wave-speed.md) set to one and metric $(+,-)$, define $\mathcal E=(\phi_t^2+\phi_x^2)/2$ and $p=-\phi_t\phi_x$. Its symmetric [stress-energy tensor](stress-energy-tensor-split.md) is $T^{\mu\nu}=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}\mathcal E&p\\p&\mathcal E\end{smallmatrix}\right)$. The [Lorentz current](lorentz-current.md) has density $t p-x\mathcal E$ and flux $t\mathcal E-xp$. Its conserved charge is the displayed expression when boundary flux vanishes. In one spatial dimension it generates boosts, not spatial rotations; there is no independent purely spatial angular-momentum component. Its conservation says that the center of energy moves uniformly. This is a symmetry of the wave-field model and need not survive boundaries or additional material effects.
