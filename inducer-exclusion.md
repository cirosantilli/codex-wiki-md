# Inducer exclusion

↑ **Parent:** [Regulation of gene expression](regulation-of-gene-expression.md)

Inhibition of uptake of an inducing substrate by a preferred-carbon-source regulatory system. Glucose-dependent inhibition of [lactose permease](lactose-permease.md) reduces formation of [allolactose](allolactose.md) and reinforces [lac operon](lac-operon.md) repression.
