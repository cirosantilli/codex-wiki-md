# Density of bounded centered scores

↑ **Parent:** [Bounded density tilt](bounded-density-tilt.md)

For $h\in L^2_0(P)$, truncate $h$ to $h_m=\max(-m,\min(h,m))$ and subtract $Ph_m$. [Dominated convergence](dominated-convergence-theorem.md) gives $h_m\to h$ in [L2 space](l2-space-is-a-hilbert-space.md), and the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) gives $Ph_m\to0$. Thus bounded centered directions are dense in the [mean-zero L2 space](mean-zero-l2-space.md). The measure defining the [L2 norm](l2-norm.md) is essential: density-weighted [L2 space](l2-space-is-a-hilbert-space.md) can contain [functions](function-split.md) outside the unweighted Lebesgue space.
