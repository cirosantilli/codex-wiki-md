# Magnetic-pressure equality radius under Keplerian winding

↑ **Parent:** [Axisymmetric magnetic winding](axisymmetric-magnetic-winding.md)

For [Keplerian rotation](keplerian-disk.md), initial $B_R\propto R^{-1}$ and thermal pressure $p\propto R^{-2}$, [axisymmetric magnetic winding](axisymmetric-magnetic-winding.md) gives $B_\phi=-(3/2)\Omega tB_R$. If the initial [plasma beta](plasma-beta.md) is $\beta_p>1$, the formal equality of [magnetic pressure](magnetic-pressure.md) and gas pressure occurs at

$$
R_{\rm eq}^3=\frac{9GMt^2}{4(\beta_p-1)}.
$$

The magnetic-to-thermal pressure ratio decreases with radius, so magnetic pressure dominates inside this radius. In a disk with a finite radial range, an equality radius exists in the disk only when this formal radius lies within that range.
