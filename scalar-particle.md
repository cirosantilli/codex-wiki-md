# Scalar particle

↑ **Parent:** [Particle (physics)](particle-physics.md)

A [scalar](scalar-field.md) particle has zero intrinsic [spin](spin.md). A scalar-field [excitation](elementary-excitation.md) provides its relativistic description. [Spin](spin.md) zero alone does not force its decay [matrix element](matrix-element.md) to be independent of internal [invariant masses](invariant-mass.md); [resonance](resonance.md) dynamics can still produce a nonuniform three-body distribution.
