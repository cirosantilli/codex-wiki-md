# Concentric hemispherical electron analyser

↑ **Parent:** [Photoemission spectroscopy](photoemission-spectroscopy.md)

An electrostatic energy analyser using trajectories between two concentric hemispheres. [electrons](electron.md) near a selected pass energy reach the exit aperture; entrance optics can retard faster [electrons](electron.md) before analysis.
