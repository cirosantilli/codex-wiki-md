# Crossmodal plasticity

↑ **Parent:** [Neuroplasticity](neuroplasticity.md)

Changes in the contribution of one sensory system to processing information from another modality, often investigated after altered sensory input. Imaging can identify recruitment; causal perturbation can test whether the recruited network contributes to a task.
