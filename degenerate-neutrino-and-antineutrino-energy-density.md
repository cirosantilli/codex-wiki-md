# Degenerate neutrino and antineutrino energy density

↑ **Parent:** [Neutrino degeneracy parameter](neutrino-degeneracy-parameter.md)

For one massless [neutrino](neutrino.md) flavor with one helicity per member and $|\mu_\nu|\gg T_\nu$, the [Fermi-Dirac distribution](fermi-dirac-distribution.md) fills momentum states up to the positive [chemical potential](chemical-potential.md). If $\mu_\nu>0$, the [neutrino](neutrino.md) density is $\mu_\nu^4/(8\pi^2)$ and the [antineutrino](antineutrino.md) contribution is approximately $3T_\nu^4e^{-\mu_\nu/T_\nu}/\pi^2$. For negative [chemical potential](chemical-potential.md) the roles reverse. Thus the leading total [energy density](energy-density.md) is even in $\mu_\nu$ and has no factor of two from the two species. Extra populated internal degrees of freedom do multiply it. This distinction prevents using a positive-degeneracy approximation for an exponentially dilute negative-chemical-potential distribution.
