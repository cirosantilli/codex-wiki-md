# Three-point Riemann-Roch construction of a plane cubic

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann-Roch space](riemann-roch-space.md)

On a [genus](genus-of-a-surface.md)-one curve, for distinct $p,q,r$, the [Riemann-Roch theorem](riemann-roch-theorem.md) gives $\ell(p+q+r)=3$. Choose $f$ with simple poles exactly at $p,q$ and $g$ with simple poles exactly at $q,r$. Pole comparison proves $1,f,g$ independent. Multiplying these coordinates by a vanishing local parameter extends $F$ across every pole, with images $[0:1:0]$, $[0:b:c]$, $[0:0:1]$, where $bc\ne0$. The ten homogeneous cubic monomials evaluate in $L(3p+3q+3r)$, of [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) nine, so a nonzero homogeneous cubic vanishes on the image. The three chosen points map to the line with first coordinate zero. This connects linear systems to [projective plane](projective-plane.md) equations by a [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) count.
