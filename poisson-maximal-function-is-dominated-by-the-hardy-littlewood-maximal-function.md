# Poisson maximal function is dominated by the Hardy-Littlewood maximal function

↑ **Parent:** [Non-tangential maximal function](non-tangential-maximal-function.md)

For integrable boundary data $g$ on the [unit circle](complex-unit-circle.md), this follows from the [Poisson kernel](poisson-kernel-for-the-upper-half-plane.md) bound $C_A\delta/(\delta^2+d^2)$ in a fixed [Stolz region](stolz-region.md). The arc of radius $\delta$ and successive dyadic annuli contribute at most $C_A2^{-k}Mg(\xi)$, which sum to the displayed estimate. This links harmonic extension to the [Hardy-Littlewood maximal function](hardy-littlewood-maximal-function.md) and its strong $L^p$ bounds for $p>1$.
