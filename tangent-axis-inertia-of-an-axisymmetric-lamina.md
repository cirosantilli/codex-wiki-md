# Tangent-axis inertia of an axisymmetric lamina

↑ **Parent:** [Perpendicular axis theorem](perpendicular-axis-theorem.md)

For an axisymmetric [planar lamina](planar-lamina.md) of total [mass](mass.md) $M$, let $I_z$ be its [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) about the central axis normal to its plane. The [perpendicular axis theorem](perpendicular-axis-theorem.md) and [rotational symmetry](rotational-symmetry.md) give $I_x=I_y=I_z/2$ about central diameters. An in-plane tangent line a perpendicular distance $R$ from a parallel central diameter therefore has [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) $I_{\mathrm{tan}}=I_z/2+MR^2$ by the [parallel axis theorem](parallel-axis-theorem.md). An axis normal to the lamina through the same rim point instead has $I_z+MR^2$; specifying the point without the axis direction is insufficient.
