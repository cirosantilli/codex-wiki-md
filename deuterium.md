# Deuterium

↑ **Parent:** [Hydrogen](hydrogen.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deuterium)

This stable [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) isotope has one [proton](proton.md) and one [neutron](neutron.md). In the [Proton–proton chain](proton-proton-chain.md) it is produced by the slow weak proton-pair reaction and is rapidly consumed by [proton](proton.md) capture to make [helium-3](helium-3.md). Its short nuclear lifetime inside a stellar core allows a [deuterium quasi-equilibrium in proton-proton burning](deuterium-quasi-equilibrium-in-proton-proton-burning.md) approximation.
