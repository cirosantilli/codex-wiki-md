<h1 id="2/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For four categories the number of [statistical degrees of freedom](../../../../../statistical-degrees-of-freedom.md) is $4-1=3$. The supplied 5% critical value is therefore $q_3=7.82$, and $7.3<7.82$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\text{Do not reject equal category probabilities at the 5\% level.}}
$$

The approximate [p-value](../../../../../p-value.md) is $\Pr(\chi_3^2\geq7.3)\approx0.063$. This conclusion assumes the large-sample multinomial approximation is adequate, in particular sufficiently large expected category counts. It means there is insufficient evidence against the uniform [null hypothesis](../../../../../null-hypothesis.md), rather than establishing that the probabilities are equal.
