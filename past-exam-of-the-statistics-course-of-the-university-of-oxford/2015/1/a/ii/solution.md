<h1 id="1/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

For odd sample size, the [sample median](../../../../../sample-median.md) is the central [order statistic](../../../../../order-statistic.md), $M_U=U_{(m+1)}$. Since $n=2m+1$, its index is $r=(n+1)/2$ and $n-r+1=(n+1)/2$. Substitute these into the [expectation](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) and [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) formulas from part (i):

$$
\boxed{\mathbb E[M_U]=\frac12,\qquad
\operatorname{Var}(M_U)=\frac1{4(n+2)}.}
$$

In fact $M_U$ has the symmetric [beta distribution](../../../../../beta-distribution.md) $\operatorname{Beta}(m+1,m+1)$, explaining why its [expectation](../../../../../expected-value-split.md) is exactly the centre of the interval.
