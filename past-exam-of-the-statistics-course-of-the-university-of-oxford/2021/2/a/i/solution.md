↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

**False.** A [test size](../../../../../size-of-a-statistical-test.md) constrains the [probability](../../../../../probability.md) of rejection conditional on the [null hypothesis](../../../../../null-hypothesis.md) being true:

$$
\mathbb P_{H_0}(\text{reject }H_0)=\alpha
$$

for an exact size-$\alpha$ test of a [simple hypothesis](../../../../../simple-hypothesis.md). It does not assign a [posterior probability](../../../../../posterior-probability.md) to that [statistical hypothesis](../../../../../statistical-hypothesis.md) after observing nonrejection. In the frequentist interpretation, the [statistical parameter](../../../../../statistical-parameter.md) is fixed and the data are random. Nonrejection can occur even when the [alternative hypothesis](../../../../../alternative-hypothesis.md) is true; for example, it is particularly likely for an alternative against which the test has low [statistical power](../../../../../statistical-power.md). A [posterior probability](../../../../../posterior-probability.md) would require a [prior distribution](../../../../../prior-probability.md) and a Bayesian model.
