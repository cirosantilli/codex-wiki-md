↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

**True, with the test's rejection rule fixed.** If its [rejection region](../../../../../rejection-region.md) in the space of the [test statistic](../../../../../test-statistic.md) is $C$, its [statistical power](../../../../../statistical-power.md) against the specified [simple hypothesis](../../../../../simple-hypothesis.md) $H_1$ is

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P_{H_1}(T\in C)}.
$$

Thus the [probability distribution](../../../../../probability-distribution-split.md) of $T$ under the [alternative hypothesis](../../../../../alternative-hypothesis.md) determines the [statistical power](../../../../../statistical-power.md) of this test. The [null distribution](../../../../../null-distribution.md) is used to choose a rule with the desired [test size](../../../../../size-of-a-statistical-test.md), but [statistical power](../../../../../statistical-power.md) is evaluated under the [alternative hypothesis](../../../../../alternative-hypothesis.md). Different rejection rules can have different [statistical power](../../../../../statistical-power.md) even for the same [probability distribution](../../../../../probability-distribution-split.md) of $T$.
