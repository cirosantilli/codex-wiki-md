**False.** For the test associated with a [p-value](../../../../../p-value.md), reject at [significance level](../../../../../significance-level.md) $\alpha$ when $p\leq\alpha$. Here $0.1>0.05$, so **do not reject at level 0.05**. The [p-value](../../../../../p-value.md) is neither the [posterior probability](../../../../../posterior-probability.md) of the [null hypothesis](../../../../../null-hypothesis.md) nor the [probability](../../../../../probability.md) of a [Type I error](../../../../../type-i-and-type-ii-errors.md) conditional on these observed data.
