<h1 id="1/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The equal-[variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) two-sample [Student's t-test](../../../../../student-s-t-test.md) pools the within-group sums of squares. If $s_A^2$ and $s_B^2$ are the two unbiased sample [variances](../../../../../variance-split.md), the required positive square root is

$$
\boxed{S=\sqrt{\frac{\sum_{i=1}^m(A_i-\overline A)^2+\sum_{j=1}^n(B_j-\overline B)^2}{m+n-2}}
=\sqrt{\frac{(m-1)s_A^2+(n-1)s_B^2}{m+n-2}}.}
$$

For ten observations in each group, $S^2=(s_A^2+s_B^2)/2$ and the usual unpaired reference [Student's t-distribution](../../../../../student-s-t-distribution.md) has $18$ [statistical degrees of freedom](../../../../../statistical-degrees-of-freedom.md), provided the assumed independence between groups is justified.
