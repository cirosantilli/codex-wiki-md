<h1 id="3/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

An [equal-tailed credible interval](../../../../../equal-tailed-credible-interval.md) allocates equal posterior [probability](../../../../../probability.md) to its two excluded tails:

$$
\Pr(\theta<L\mid x)=\Pr(\theta>U\mid x)=\alpha/2.
$$

For a continuous strictly increasing posterior [cumulative distribution function](../../../../../cumulative-distribution-function.md) $F$, the endpoints are $F^{-1}(\alpha/2)$ and $F^{-1}(1-\alpha/2)$.

A [highest posterior density interval](../../../../../highest-posterior-density-interval.md) instead includes the parameter values of greatest posterior density: there is a cutoff $c$ such that the retained region is a superlevel set of $\pi(\theta\mid x)$, of posterior mass $1-\alpha$. Every interior retained value has density at least as large as every excluded value, apart from possible ties. For a unimodal continuous density this is an interval; when both endpoints lie inside the support they have equal posterior density. For a multimodal [posterior distribution](../../../../../bayesian-posterior.md), a highest-density region may be a union of intervals, rather than one interval. **Equal tail probabilities and highest density are different criteria.**
