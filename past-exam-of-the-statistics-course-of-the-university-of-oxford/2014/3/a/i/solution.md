<h1 id="3/a/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Given observed data $x$, a [credible interval](../../../../../credible-interval.md) $[L(x),U(x)]$ has posterior [probability](../../../../../probability.md) $1-\alpha$:

$$
\boxed{\Pr\{L(x)\leq\theta\leq U(x)\mid x\}=1-\alpha.}
$$

For a continuous [posterior distribution](../../../../../bayesian-posterior.md) with [probability density function](../../../../../probability-density-function.md) $\pi(\theta\mid x)$, this is $\int_{L(x)}^{U(x)}\pi(\theta\mid x)\,d\theta=1-\alpha$. The probability is conditional on the observed data and concerns the [statistical parameter](../../../../../statistical-parameter.md) under its [posterior distribution](../../../../../bayesian-posterior.md); it does not assert frequentist [coverage probability](../../../../../coverage-probability.md).
