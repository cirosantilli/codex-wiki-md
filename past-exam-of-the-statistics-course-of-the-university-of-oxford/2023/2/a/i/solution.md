↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For a [critical region](../../../../../rejection-region.md) $C$, the [size of a statistical test](../../../../../size-of-a-statistical-test.md) of the simple [null hypothesis](../../../../../null-hypothesis.md) $P_0$ is $\alpha=P_0(\boldsymbol X\in C)$, the [Type I error](../../../../../type-i-and-type-ii-errors.md) probability. Its [statistical power](../../../../../statistical-power.md) against the simple [alternative hypothesis](../../../../../alternative-hypothesis.md) $P_1$ is $P_1(\boldsymbol X\in C)$, the probability of correctly rejecting that null. For a randomized test function $\varphi(\boldsymbol x)\in[0,1]$, replace these by $\mathbb E_0\varphi$ and $\mathbb E_1\varphi$.
