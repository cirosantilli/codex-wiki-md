↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The estimated [standard error](../../../../../standard-error.md) is $\widehat\theta^{3/2}/\sqrt n$. Using the central 95% [standard normal quantiles](../../../../../standard-normal-quantile.md) $\pm1.96$, a [Wald confidence interval](../../../../../wald-confidence-interval.md) is

$$
\boxed{\left[\widehat\theta-1.96\frac{\widehat\theta^{3/2}}{\sqrt n},\quad\widehat\theta+1.96\frac{\widehat\theta^{3/2}}{\sqrt n}\right]\cap(0,\infty)}.
$$

Consistency of $\widehat\theta$ and [Slutsky's theorem](../../../../../slutsky-theorem.md) justify replacing the unknown scale $\theta^{3/2}$ by its estimate. The [coverage probability](../../../../../coverage-probability.md) is asymptotic, not exact at finite $n$. Intersecting with the positive parameter space removes impossible values without changing whether the true positive parameter is covered.
