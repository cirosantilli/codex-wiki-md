<h1 id="1/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The full parameter space has dimension two and the null space dimension one. The regular [Wilks theorem](../../../../../wilks-theorem.md) therefore gives $W\xrightarrow d\chi_1^2$ under $H_0$ as $n\to\infty$. If $F_1$ denotes the [cumulative distribution function](../../../../../cumulative-distribution-function.md) of the [chi-squared distribution](../../../../../chi-squared-distribution.md) with one degree of freedom, the approximate size-$\alpha$ [rejection region](../../../../../rejection-region.md) is

$$
\boxed{q-n-n\log(q/n)\geq F_1^{-1}(1-\alpha)}.
$$

The corresponding approximate [p-value](../../../../../p-value.md) is

$$
\boxed{1-F_1\!\left(q_{\mathrm{obs}}-n-n\log(q_{\mathrm{obs}}/n)\right)}.
$$

The one degree of freedom here describes the large-sample [likelihood-ratio test statistic](../../../../../likelihood-ratio-test-statistic.md); it is different from the exact residual degrees of freedom $n-1$ in the next part.
