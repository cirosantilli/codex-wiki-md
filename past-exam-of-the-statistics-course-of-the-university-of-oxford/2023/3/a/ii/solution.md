An [equal-tailed credible interval](../../../../../equal-tailed-credible-interval.md) for a continuous [posterior distribution](../../../../../bayesian-posterior.md) has probability $\alpha/2$ below its lower endpoint and $\alpha/2$ above its upper endpoint. If $F$ is the posterior [cumulative distribution function](../../../../../cumulative-distribution-function.md), the endpoints are $F^{-1}(\alpha/2)$ and $F^{-1}(1-\alpha/2)$.

A [highest posterior density interval](../../../../../highest-posterior-density-interval.md) is a [credible interval](../../../../../credible-interval.md) consisting of points of highest [posterior density](../../../../../posterior-density.md): every included point has density at least as high as every excluded point, apart from null-set and boundary choices. It is a posterior [highest density region](../../../../../highest-density-region.md) when that region is an interval. For a continuous unimodal density with two interior endpoints, their densities agree. A mode at a support boundary can instead force one endpoint to that boundary; a multimodal posterior can have a disconnected highest-density region rather than one interval. Equal tails and highest density need not give the same interval.
