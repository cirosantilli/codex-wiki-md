↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Given the observed data, a $100(1-\alpha)\%$ [credible interval](../../../../../credible-interval.md) $[a,b]$ for the scalar parameter satisfies

$$
\boxed{\mathbb P(a\leq\theta\leq b\mid\boldsymbol x)=1-\alpha}.
$$

The probability refers to the [posterior distribution](../../../../../bayesian-posterior.md), proportional to $\pi(\theta)\prod_i f(x_i\mid\theta)$ on the parameter space. It conditions on the observations; it is not the repeated-sampling [coverage probability](../../../../../coverage-probability.md) definition of a [confidence interval](../../../../../confidence-interval.md).
