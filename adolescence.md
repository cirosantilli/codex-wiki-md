# Adolescence

↑ **Parent:** [Human](human.md)

A developmental period spanning changes in physical maturation, social roles and increasing autonomy. Clinical research should specify sampled ages and relevant developmental characteristics rather than treating an age label as a complete explanation of treatment response.
