# Maximally entangled overlap bound from Schmidt rank

↑ **Parent:** [Schmidt rank](schmidt-rank.md)

A normalized bipartite [pure state](pure-state.md) of [Schmidt rank](schmidt-rank.md) $r$ has [quantum fidelity](fidelity-of-quantum-states.md) at most $\sqrt{r/d}$ with a fixed [maximally entangled state](maximally-entangled-state.md) on $d\otimes d$. Its coefficient [matrix](matrix.md) has [matrix rank](matrix-rank.md) $r$ and [Hilbert-Schmidt norm](hilbert-schmidt-norm.md) one. Its overlap is the [trace](matrix-trace.md) of that [matrix](matrix.md) divided by $\sqrt d$; [trace duality](trace-duality.md) and the [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) bound this by $\sqrt{r/d}$. Uniform [Schmidt coefficients](schmidt-coefficient.md) on $r$ matching basis pairs attain equality.
