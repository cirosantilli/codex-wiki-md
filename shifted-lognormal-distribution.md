# Shifted lognormal distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Log-normal distribution](log-normal-distribution.md)

The support is $x>\theta$ and the [probability density function](probability-density-function.md) is

$$
f(x)=\frac{\exp[-(\log(x-\theta)-m)^2/(2\sigma^2)]}{(x-\theta)\sigma\sqrt{2\pi}}.
$$

The [change of variables](change-of-variables-formula.md) $z=\log(x-\theta)$ proves normalization. The threshold $\theta$ is a lower endpoint, distinct from the log-mean $m$ and log-standard-deviation $\sigma$. In measured [sea-ice pressure ridge](sea-ice-pressure-ridge.md) spacings, finite ridge widths and detection rules can create such a lower endpoint.
