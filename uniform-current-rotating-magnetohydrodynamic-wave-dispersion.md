# Uniform-current rotating magnetohydrodynamic wave dispersion

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetohydrodynamic wave](magnetohydrodynamic-wave.md)

For an ideal [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md) rotating about the axis of the field $\mathbf B=(J/2)s\widehat{\boldsymbol\phi}$, use cylindrical [Fourier modes](fourier-mode.md) $e^{\sigma t+im\phi}$. The [azimuthal derivative of a cylindrical vector Fourier mode](azimuthal-derivative-of-a-cylindrical-vector-fourier-mode.md) reduces induction to $\sigma\mathbf b=imJ\mathbf u/2$. If the boundary eigenproblem gives $\lambda=i\delta\nu$, with real $|\delta|<1$, eliminating the perturbation gives the displayed [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation.md). Here $J=\nabla\times\mathbf B\cdot\widehat{\mathbf z}=\mu_0J_{\rm phys}$ for SI [current density](current-density.md); the formula is conditional on that boundary eigenrelation.
