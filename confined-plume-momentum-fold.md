# Confined-plume momentum fold

↑ **Parent:** [Confined plume with compensating return flow](confined-plume-with-compensating-return-flow.md)

For the pressure-neglecting [confined plume with compensating return flow](confined-plume-with-compensating-return-flow.md), define specific plume [momentum flux](momentum-flux.md) $M=b^2w^2$ and area fraction $s=Q^2/(R^2M)$. Then $J=M/(1-s)$ and

$$
(1-2s)M'=\frac{B_sQ}{M}(1-s)^2-\frac{2Q}{R^2}Q'.
$$

The branch issuing from a [pure plume](pure-plume.md) reaches a fold at $s=1/2$, where plume and ambient have equal areas, opposite [velocities](velocity.md) and equal specific [momentum fluxes](momentum-flux.md). The numerator is nonzero on this branch, so $M'$ diverges although $M,Q,w$ remain finite. This signals failure of the steady uniform-profile description, not infinite physical velocity.
