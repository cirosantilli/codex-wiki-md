# Backward Euler method

↑ **Parent:** [A-stability](a-stability.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Backward_Euler_method)

Backward Euler advances $y'=f(t,y)$ by $y_{n+1}=y_n+h f(t_{n+1},y_{n+1})$. Its stability function $R(z)=(1-z)^{-1}$ makes it [L-stable](l-stability.md).
