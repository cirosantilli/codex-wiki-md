# Maximal Cauchy development

↑ **Parent:** [Initial data in general relativity](initial-data-in-general-relativity.md)

A [maximal Cauchy development](maximal-cauchy-development.md) is the largest globally hyperbolic solution, unique up to an appropriate [isometry](isometry.md), determined by admissible [initial data](initial-data-in-general-relativity.md), with the initial hypersurface a [Cauchy hypersurface](cauchy-surface.md). It can sometimes be extended as a spacetime across a [Cauchy horizon](cauchy-horizon.md) even though that extension is no longer globally determined by the initial hypersurface.
