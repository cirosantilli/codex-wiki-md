# Reduced BCS pairing Hamiltonian

↑ **Parent:** [BCS theory](bcs-theory.md)

The [reduced BCS pairing Hamiltonian](reduced-bcs-pairing-hamiltonian.md) retains scattering of opposite-momentum, opposite-spin [Cooper pairs](cooper-pair.md):

$$
H=\sum_{k,\sigma}\epsilon_kc_{k\sigma}^\dagger c_{k\sigma}+\sum_{k,k'}V_{k,k'}b_k^\dagger b_{k'},\qquad b_k^\dagger=c_{k\uparrow}^\dagger c_{-k\downarrow}^\dagger.
$$

A [mean-field approximation](mean-field-approximation.md) introduces the [BCS anomalous average](bcs-anomalous-average.md) and pairing field $\Delta_k=-\sum_{k'}V_{k,k'}\langle b_{k'}\rangle$. The subtraction $-\sum V_{k,k'}\langle b_k^\dagger\rangle\langle b_{k'}\rangle$ corrects the double counting of the mean-field interaction energy.
