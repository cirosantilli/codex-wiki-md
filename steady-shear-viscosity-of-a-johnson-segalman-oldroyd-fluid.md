<h1 id="steady-shear-viscosity-of-a-johnson-segalman-oldroyd-fluid">Steady shear viscosity of a Johnson--Segalman--Oldroyd fluid</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Johnson--Segalman--Oldroyd model](johnson-segalman-oldroyd-model.md)

For Gordon--Schowalter parameter $0<a<2$, the steady shear viscosity is

$$
\eta_{\rm sh}=\eta
\frac{1+a(2-a)\lambda_1\lambda_2\dot\gamma^2}
{1+a(2-a)\lambda_1^2\dot\gamma^2}.
$$

It is shear thinning when $\lambda_1>\lambda_2$.
