# Elastic-sheet tension

↑ **Parent:** [Acoustic wave on a tensioned massive membrane](acoustic-wave-on-a-tensioned-massive-membrane.md)

The in-plane tensile [force](force.md) per unit transverse length in a thin elastic sheet produces the linear transverse restoring [force](force.md) $T\eta_{xx}$ per unit area. A spatially uniform displacement has zero curvature and hence no [elastic-sheet tension](elastic-sheet-tension.md) [force](force.md). This explains why infinite [elastic-sheet tension](elastic-sheet-tension.md) blocks fixed nonzero-wavenumber [acoustic membrane waves](acoustic-wave-on-a-tensioned-massive-membrane.md) but does not make the uniform mode rigid. The sheet's inertia, by contrast, acts also on uniform oscillations.
