# Free-fall proper time in Schwarzschild spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [Painlevé–Gullstrand coordinates](painleve-gullstrand-coordinates.md)

The inward radial [geodesic congruence](geodesic-congruence-split.md) with unit [Killing energy](killing-energy.md) has $U=f^{-1}\partial_t-\sqrt{2M/r}\,\partial_r$, where $f=1-2M/r$. Its metric dual is $U^\flat=-d\tau$, so $\tau$ is a synchronized [proper time](proper-time.md) for the congruence. Locally $\tau=t+2\sqrt{2Mr}+2M\log| (\sqrt r-\sqrt{2M})/(\sqrt r+\sqrt{2M}) |$. This yields [Painlevé–Gullstrand coordinates](painleve-gullstrand-coordinates.md) with flat spatial slices and a regular future horizon.
