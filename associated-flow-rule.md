# Associated flow rule

↑ **Parent:** [Plasticity (physics)](plasticity-physics.md)

Plastic strain rate is normal to the [yield surface](yield-surface.md) in the work-conjugate stress variables. With symmetric [stress](stress.md) variations, the pairing is $D:\delta\sigma$; in two dimensions its off-diagonal contribution is $2D_{12}\delta\sigma_{12}$. This factor is essential when changing to reduced stress coordinates.
