# Translational and two-level energy variance

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical ensemble](canonical-ensemble-split.md)

For a dilute classical [ideal gas](ideal-gas-split.md) whose atoms carry independent nondegenerate internal levels $0,\epsilon$, the [canonical partition function](canonical-partition-function.md) factorizes into translation and $(1+e^{-\beta\epsilon})^N$. The upper-level [probability](probability.md) is $p=(1+e^{\beta\epsilon})^{-1}$. Taking two derivatives of its logarithm gives the title sum of independent [variances](variance-split.md). The internal contribution freezes exponentially at low [temperature](temperature-split.md) and tends to $N\epsilon^2/4$ at high [temperature](temperature-split.md). The translational classical result cannot be continued through [quantum degeneracy](degenerate-energy-levels.md) without changing the model.
