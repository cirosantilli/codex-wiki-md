# Divisor-square summatory bound

↑ **Parent:** [Divisor function](divisor-function.md)

The [divisor function](divisor-function.md) satisfies $\sum_{n\le X}d(n)^2\ll X\log^3(2X)$. [Partial summation](abel-s-summation-formula.md) consequently gives $\sum_{n\le X}d(n)^2/n\ll\log^4(2X)$.
