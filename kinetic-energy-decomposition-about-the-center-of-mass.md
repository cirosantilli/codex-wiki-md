# Kinetic energy decomposition about the center of mass

↑ **Parent:** [Kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md)

Writing particle positions relative to the [center of mass](center-of-mass.md) as $\rho_i=r_i-R$ makes $\sum_i m_i\rho_i=0$, so the cross term in the squared [velocities](velocity.md) vanishes. The total [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) splits into center-of-mass translation and relative motion as displayed. For a [rigid body](rigid-body-dynamics.md), the latter is $\tfrac12 I\omega^2$, where $I$ is the [moment of inertia](moment-of-inertia.md) about the instantaneous rotation axis through the [center of mass](center-of-mass.md).
