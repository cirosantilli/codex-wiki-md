# Broadband radiation

↑ **Parent:** [Electromagnetic radiation](electromagnetic-radiation-split.md)

Radiation with appreciable spectral content over a range of [frequencies](frequency.md). A broad spectrum alone does not specify the relative phases: incoherent radiation and a coherent broadband pulse can have different transition probabilities despite similar spectral intensities.
