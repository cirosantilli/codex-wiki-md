# Bad sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Well-quasi-ordering](well-quasi-ordering.md)

A bad sequence in a [preorder](preorder.md) is an infinite [sequence](sequence.md) with no indices $i<j$ satisfying $x_i\le x_j$. A [preorder](preorder.md) is a [well-quasi-ordering](well-quasi-ordering.md) precisely when no such sequence exists.
