# Dual of a rational Schur module

↑ **Parent:** [Rational Schur module](rational-schur-module.md)

The dual of $D_\lambda$ has dominant label $-w_0\lambda=(-\lambda_m,\ldots,-\lambda_1)$. Substitution $x_i\mapsto x_i^{-1}$ in the [alternant character formula for the general linear group](alternant-character-formula-for-the-general-linear-group.md) and reversal of determinant columns gives that [character](character-of-a-representation.md). The numerator and denominator reversal signs and common [monomial](monomial.md) factors cancel.
