# Euclidean accessible-volume luminosity weighting

↑ **Parent:** [Malmquist bias](malmquist-bias.md)

For a [flux](flux.md) threshold $f_{\min}$ and isotropic point-source [luminosity](luminosity.md) $L$, the detection radius is $r_{\max}=\sqrt{L/(4\pi f_{\min})}$. In a homogeneous Euclidean population the accessible volume is proportional to $L^{3/2}$, so the selected [luminosity](luminosity.md) distribution is proportional to $\varphi(L)L^{3/2}$. This assumes [flux](flux.md) selection and inverse-square propagation, not an arbitrary surface-brightness-selected diffuse field.
