# Phlegmasia alba dolens

↑ **Parent:** [Deep vein thrombosis](deep-vein-thrombosis.md)

A historical clinical term for a pale, painful, swollen limb, especially after childbirth, associated with extensive [deep vein thrombosis](deep-vein-thrombosis.md). It is distinct from blue, severely congested [phlegmasia cerulea dolens](phlegmasia-cerulea-dolens.md).
