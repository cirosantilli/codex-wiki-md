# New subspace of cusp forms

↑ **Parent:** [Cusp form](cusp-form.md)

The new subspace is the [orthogonal complement](orthogonal-complement.md) of the [old subspace of cusp forms](old-subspace-of-cusp-forms.md) for the [Petersson inner product](petersson-inner-product.md). A form belongs to it precisely when the adjoints of all lower-level [oldform by argument dilation](oldform-by-argument-dilation.md) maps annihilate it.
