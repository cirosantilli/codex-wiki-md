# BPS state

↑ **Parent:** [BPS bound in supersymmetry](bps-bound-in-supersymmetry.md)

A [BPS state](bps-state.md) saturates a [BPS bound in supersymmetry](bps-bound-in-supersymmetry.md). The zero-norm combinations of [supercharges](supersymmetry-generator.md) and their adjoints annihilate its entire irreducible [supermultiplet](supermultiplet.md). Thus part of the [supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md) is preserved. For a positive-mass representation, its [massive supermultiplet](massive-supermultiplet.md) is shortened; [massless supermultiplets](massless-supermultiplet.md) require the separate null-momentum construction. Charge conjugation can require a separate conjugate multiplet in the physical spectrum.
