# Indecomposable count for a cyclic defect block

↑ **Parent:** [Brauer tree algebra](brauer-tree-algebra.md)

For a split [block of a group algebra](block-of-a-group-algebra.md) with cyclic [defect group of a block](defect-group-of-a-block.md) of order $q=p^n$ and [inertial index of a block](inertial-index-of-a-block.md) $e$, its [Brauer tree algebra](brauer-tree-algebra.md) has $e$ simple modules and exceptional multiplicity $(q-1)/e$. Its [stable Auslander–Reiten quiver](stable-auslander-reiten-quiver.md) is $\mathbb ZA_{q-1}/\langle\tau^e\rangle$, giving $e(q-1)$ nonprojective indecomposables. Adding the $e$ indecomposable projectives gives $eq$ indecomposables in total.
