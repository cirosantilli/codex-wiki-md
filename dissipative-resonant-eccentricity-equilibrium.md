# Dissipative resonant eccentricity equilibrium

↑ **Parent:** [Resonant trapping of dust](resonant-trapping-of-dust.md)

For [resonant trapping of dust](resonant-trapping-of-dust.md) in an exterior $(p+q):p$ [mean-motion resonance](mean-motion-resonance.md), let [Poynting–Robertson drag](poynting-robertson-drag.md) have parameter $A$. Holding [semi-major axis](semi-major-axis.md) $a$ fixed and retaining secular rates through second order in [orbital eccentricity](orbital-eccentricity.md) gives

$$
\frac{d(e^2)}{dt}=\frac A{a^2}\left[\frac{2q}{p+q}-\frac{5p-q}{p+q}e^2\right],
\qquad e_{\rm eq}^2=\frac{2q}{5p-q},\qquad
\tau=\frac{a^2(p+q)}{A(5p-q)}.
$$

For $5p>q$, squared [orbital eccentricity](orbital-eccentricity.md) approaches this equilibrium exponentially in the truncated model. If the predicted value is not small, higher-order terms are needed for quantitative dynamics. Finite planetary torque also imposes a minimum eccentricity for balance, which alone does not guarantee capture during migration.
