# Branching-depth antichain of rooted trees

↑ **Parent:** [Adjacency-preserving rooted-tree embedding](adjacency-preserving-rooted-tree-embedding.md)

Let $T_m$ consist of a rooted path of $m\geq1$ edges followed by two leaves at its far end. Its only vertex with two children has depth $m$. An [adjacency-preserving rooted-tree embedding](adjacency-preserving-rooted-tree-embedding.md) must preserve this depth, so $T_m$ embeds into $T_n$ only when $m=n$. This gives an infinite [bad sequence](bad-sequence.md), while [rooted-tree homeomorphic embeddings](homeomorphic-embedding-of-a-rooted-tree.md) can stretch the stems.
