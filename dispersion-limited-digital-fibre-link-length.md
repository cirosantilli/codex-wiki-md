# Dispersion-limited digital-fibre link length

↑ **Parent:** [Group velocity dispersion](group-velocity-dispersion.md)

A pulse's [frequency](frequency.md) components accumulate a differential [group delay](group-delay.md) $\Delta t=L|\partial_f(1/v_g)|\Delta f$. If that spread approaches the pulse repetition period $T_r$, neighbouring symbols overlap. The resulting length estimate depends on [bandwidth](bandwidth-signal-processing.md), initial pulse width, chirp and the receiver's allowable intersymbol interference. For $n(f)=n_0+\tau(f-f_0)$, the group index is $n_0+\tau(2f-f_0)$ and $\partial_f(1/v_g)=2\tau/c$: differentiating the phase index alone loses a factor of two.
