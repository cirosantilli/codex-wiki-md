# Sideband coupling

↑ **Parent:** [Fibre modulational instability](fibre-modulational-instability.md)

Linearization around a pumped background couples the [perturbation](perturbation.md) at positive [frequency](frequency.md) offset to the conjugate of the negative-offset [perturbation](perturbation.md). Their matrix [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) determine amplification; assuming two unrelated scalar phase shifts misses the instability.
