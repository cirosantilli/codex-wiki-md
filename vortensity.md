# Vortensity

↑ **Parent:** [Shearing sheet](shearing-sheet.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vortensity)

Vortensity is [absolute vorticity](absolute-vorticity.md) divided by surface density. In a two-dimensional inviscid barotropic flow, it is conserved along fluid trajectories.
