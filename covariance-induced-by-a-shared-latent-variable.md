# Covariance induced by a shared latent variable

↑ **Parent:** [Conditional independence](conditional-independence.md)

Let $A,B$ be [indicator random variables](indicator-random-variable.md) with [conditional independence](conditional-independence.md) given a [random variable](random-variable-split.md) $T$, and suppose $\mathbb E[A\mid T]=\mathbb E[B\mid T]=g(T)$. Then the [law of total expectation](law-of-total-expectation.md) gives $\mathbb E[AB]=\mathbb E[g(T)^2]$ and $\mathbb E[A]=\mathbb E[B]=\mathbb E[g(T)]$. Thus their [covariance](covariance.md) is the [variance](variance-split.md) of $g(T)$ and is nonnegative. The result explains correlated component evolutionary states in a [coeval binary population](coeval-binary-population.md) without correlation between initial masses.
