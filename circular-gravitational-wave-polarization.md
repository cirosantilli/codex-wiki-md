# Circular gravitational-wave polarization

↑ **Parent:** [Gravitational wave polarization](gravitational-wave-polarization.md)

Equal-amplitude [plus polarization](plus-polarization.md) and [cross polarization](cross-polarization.md) with a quarter-cycle phase difference form circularly polarized [gravitational radiation](gravitational-wave-split.md). The principal transverse strain axes rotate by half the phase angle. An observer on the rotation axis of a circular binary sees this polarization; the sign distinguishes the handedness relative to a stated propagation direction.
