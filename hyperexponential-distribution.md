# Hyperexponential distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Mixture distribution](mixture-distribution.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperexponential_distribution)

A finite [mixture distribution](mixture-distribution.md) of [exponential distributions](exponential-distribution.md) with rates $r_i>0$ and nonnegative weights summing to one. Its [expected value](expected-value.md) is $\sum_iw_i/r_i$ and its [moment-generating function](moment-generating-function.md) is $\sum_i w_i r_i/(r_i-t)$ on the common finite domain. The mixture chooses one component for each observation; it is not a sum of independent exponential variables.
