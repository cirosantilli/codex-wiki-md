# Test-function pairing with a Gaussian free field

↑ **Parent:** [Zero-boundary Gaussian free field](zero-boundary-gaussian-free-field.md)

For a [test function](test-function.md) $\phi$ on $D$, let $f_\phi=-2\pi\Delta^{-1}\phi\in H_0^1(D)$. The pairing

$$
(h,\phi):=(h,f_\phi)_\nabla
$$

is a centered [Gaussian random variable](gaussian-random-variable.md) with variance

$$
\iint_{D\times D}\phi(x)G_D(x,y)\phi(y)\,dx\,dy.
$$
