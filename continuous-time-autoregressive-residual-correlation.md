# Continuous-time autoregressive residual correlation

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian linear mixed model](gaussian-linear-mixed-model.md)

For $\kappa>0$, this error correlation decreases exponentially with elapsed time and permits irregular observation times. It is the stationary correlation of an [Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process](ornstein-uhlenbeck-process.md). Within a [Gaussian linear mixed model](gaussian-linear-mixed-model.md), apply it to the errors conditional on [random effects](random-effect.md); the marginal correlation also includes those effects. `nlme::corCAR1` parametrizes the same correlation by $\rho=e^{-\kappa}\in(0,1)$ and permits separate grouped time series.
